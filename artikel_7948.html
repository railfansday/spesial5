Holiday gear for handy people is selling fast this year. The Home Depot’s viral sweater sold out in minutes (twice), and Craftsman’s 12-door advent calendar has already vanished from most retailers. It’s easy to see the appeal: Instead of a square of chocolate, each door hides something that deserves its spot in a tool box, whether that’s a ratchet or a pair of pliers.

We rounded up our top five calendars for DIYers, from a premium Makita set to a budget-friendly GreatNeck. If you’re still building your list, our guide to the best gifts for tool lovers has plenty more ideas.

Makita’s calendar is the splurge of the group, an obvious choice for the DIYer who already trusts the brand’s tools. Its 24 doors open to reveal a 38-piece drill and bit set, a ratcheting screwdriver, a tape measure, a hex key set, a ¼-inch ratchet with four sockets, and combination pliers, plus Makita swag like a beanie and a 2027 desk calendar. Everything lives in a holiday-themed Makpac case you can keep using after December.

Get the Makita advent calendar at Amazon.

The headliner behind the 24 doors of Wera’s advent calendar is the Flashdriver, a compact flashlight with a built-in bit holder that lets you light up a dark cabinet while tightening a screw. Built for working in cramped situations, it includes nine BiTorsion bits, seven hex keys, and a Kraftform handle. Everything builds into four modules that snap onto a fabric dock, with adapters for Wera’s modular storage.

Bosch’s advent calendar is a practical choice for the person who is always fixing something around the house. Its 24 doors hide 44 pieces, including 7-inch pliers, a ¼-inch ratchet, a snap-off knife, a precision bit screwdriver, and an assortment of bits, plus a zippered bag to hold it all. It’s a good match for a new homeowner or a DIYer who likes having the right bit within reach.

Right now this one is sold out, but we’re including it because we absolutely love it and are crossing our fingers for a restock. Behind this Craftsman calendar’s 12 doors are a 3/8-inch ratchet, extension bar, magnetic nut driver, 11-piece bit set, assorted sockets, a magnetic pickup tool, tape-measure key chain, and a Versastack case. If you can manage to get your hands on one, it’s a great way to give a DIYer a useful starting collection or an experienced handy person a few helpful additions. GreatNeck’s multi-bit screwdriver advent calendar is an approachable, budget-friendly option for someone just getting into DIY. Instead of requiring a big investment upfront, it delivers useful screwdriver bits and accessories over the course of the countdown, helping build a practical starter kit one day at a time. This is a good pick for a college student, first-time homeowner, or casual fixer who needs a reliable set of basics.

Get the GreatNeck advent calendar at Amazon.
