Viral retro gaming creator Jacob Payne dies as family warns fans of impersonators

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Viral retro gaming creator Jacob Payne, better known as Retro Jake, has died, his family announced on Instagram on November 7.

His family said on Instagram that Payne was “in heaven now” and had “fought until the very end,” while asking followers to keep his loved ones in their thoughts and prayers as they take time to mourn.

Payne’s death came after he had been largely absent from regular uploads. His last listed YouTube video, titled “Life Update. Long Video Incoming,” was posted on October 25, while a local report said his family had announced he was hospitalized with severe heart failure the following week. His family has not publicly provided a formal cause of death.

The Retro Jake account later issued a warning after the family said scammers were already attempting to exploit the situation. Followers were told not to send money or answer questions from anyone claiming to represent Payne, his relatives or his friends.

The family said all legitimate information would be shared through Retro Jake’s official Facebook page. It also warned that no financial support would be requested unless it was announced through that account.

Payne was known for buying, selling and trading retro games while showcasing collections and customer interactions at his stores. He also supported local causes, including a “Console For Kids” initiative with the Richmond Police Department that delivered video game systems to Girls Inc. last Christmas.
