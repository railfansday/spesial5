Here is Steve Carden’s match report, including reaction from boss Sam Cornish...

A Marley Amber brace saw off a tough and physical challenge from visitors Loxwood as Oak recovered from last week’s disappointing display at Guildford.

In a first half of minimal chances, two penalties gave some life to the proceedings.

The first came when Jack Jefferies handled and Pat Sessions conjured up yet another superb save. The second came when Chris Whitington was fouled in the opposition box and Ambler finished emphatically, so the Oak went into the break with a lead.

The Oak came out full of purpose in the second 45, dominating the early stages and were eventually rewarded when Alex Masters headed a ball across the box and Ambler nodded home.

Comfortably in charge, the coaching team decided to blood some of the club’s youth products, Aziz Mahmoud and debutant Bobby Bryne both excelling. A goal from Loxwood five minutes from time caused a few flutters for the home crowd but Oak held firm to take a welcome three points.

A pleased Sam Cornish said: “After last weekend’s disappointing result we needed to bounce back, so a home game was a perfectly opportunity for that albeit against a tricky Loxwood side.

"I thought we started really brightly and thanks to another brilliant penalty save from Pat we were able to continue to dominate the opening exchanges.

"We had a plan which we spoke about before the game, despite going in ahead we hadn’t really managed to implement that game plan in the first half.

"The second half was a different story and once we got the well worked second goal we were in cruise control for a large part of that half. We knew at some stage Loxwood would throw everything at us, and we stood tall to that challenge.”
