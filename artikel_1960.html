Arsenal transfer news: Why Kevin Schade could be a smart January signing

Arsenal are looking to strengthen their attacking options in January. One player they are reportedly keeping an eye on is Brentford winger Kevin Schade. Mikel Arteta will be keen to add competition in attack, and Schade may be a good option.

Schade will give additional pace to the offence. He is capable of playing in multiple attacking positions to provide Arteta with extra depth in the squad.

However, it may be difficult to get Schade. He is on loan at Brentford, and may want to keep him in the long term. If Schade is seen as a key player in the future, they may be difficult to negotiate with.

Schade’s versatility is another big plus. His speed and straightforward method make him hard to deal with for defenders. In addition, he can be used as a left-sided attacker, giving Arteta more flexibility in his front line. As the club are playing in a number of competitions, this can be very useful.

Schade has already scored 3 times in the Premier League this season. He has quickly adapted to playing in England after moving from Freiburg to Brentford in 2023.

Despite only being 24 years old, Schade still has a lot to improve on. He has lots of experience in the Premier League but has not quite reached the peak of his ability yet. If he moves to Arsenal, he may be able to elevate his performance.

Brentford has already started negotiations with Schade regarding a contract extension that would keep him tied to the club for a long time. He is already contracted until the summer of 2028.

Arsenal’s interest could prove problematic. Other clubs are also keeping their eyes on this player. Tottenham in particular are not seen as a major threat at this stage.

It is going to be very challenging for Brentford to let go of Schade because he has played a key part in their offensive setup, and losing Schade could prove disastrous.

Arsenal will have to pay Brentford whatever they ask for, Schade, and since he has a decent amount of time left on his contract, Brentford has an edge in negotiations.

Arsenal’s interest in Schade is logical. His versatility will provide Arteta with additional flexibility because of his prior experience in the Premier League.

It can be considered positive to sign new strikers. Arsenal must decide if they believe that Schade will fit in well with their current players and whether the asking price from Brentford is reasonable.

As of now, there are no signs of a transfer on the horizon, but it is clear that Schade is a player worth monitoring as the winter transfer window approaches.
