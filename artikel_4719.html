15 Airbnbs With Indoor Pools & Jacuzzis (2026): Top Summer Escapes

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Airbnbs with indoor pools (especially the heated kind) are the kinds of “needle in a haystack” rentals that can save a vacation from feeling completely miserable in the coldest months of the year, or heighten the excitement of the enclave you’ve secured for a week of summer bliss. And though many of your standard Airbnb rentals will offer guests some generous amenities—a plush bed, a functional kitchen, and a high-pressure shower immediately come to mind—only a very select few allow you to book a pool that’s all yours (not shared, or fought over, by a bunch of resort guests). Better yet: There’s no “pool closes at 9 p.m.” signs.

But here’s the catch: The gap between a great indoor-pool rental and a truly tragic one is vast. We’re talking sleek, glass-wrapped sanctuaries on one end—and, on the other, a sad, chlorinated afterthought hiding in a basement with fluorescent lighting and 1994 motel energy. The good ones, though, have the power to allow you to lock in your vacation and tune everything else out. From a cedar-lined pool room with views of the surrounding forest to modern desert escapes where you can watch the sunset turn the surrounding mountains pink at sunset from your floaty, these pools make for an incredible retreat year-round, weather be damned.

Perks: Turkish steam bath, sauna, massage room, private chef, butler service, private backyard, security system

If your ideal vacation involves frescoed ceilings, crystal chandeliers, and a butler to fetch you juice that’s been freshly squeezed from the garden's citrus trees, Villa Dorata in Positano delivers. This five-bedroom Baroque-style villa is gloriously over the top, with jewel-toned interiors, gilded art, elaborate tilework, and terraces overlooking the Amalfi Coast. The private spa is an attraction in its own right, complete with an indoor swimming pool, Turkish steam bath, and massage room. Outside, lush gardens, fountains, and citrus groves provide plenty of places to enjoy the Mediterranean sunshine. There are even two traditional Italian pizza ovens for alfresco dinners. Daily breakfast, housekeeping, and butler service are included, so guests can spend less time managing the logistics of a group vacation and more time enjoying the villa's considerable extravagance. From $5,078 per night.

Swimming season never ends at this three-bedroom home outside Binghamton, New York. The heated indoor pool occupies its own glass-lined space, supplying views of the surrounding trees and simulating the feeling of being outdoors. The rest of the house features vaulted ceilings, bamboo floors, and a contemporary mix of furnishings. Floor-to-ceiling windows let in plenty of natural light into the main living areas, and an expansive deck extends the entertaining space outdoors. There's also a balcony for morning coffee, a propane fire pit for chilly evenings, and a jetted soaking tub in the primary suite. With room for eight guests and a pet-friendly policy, it's a practical choice for a group getaway, particularly when the weather makes an outdoor pool considerably less appealing. From $425 per night.

The Catskills have no shortage of cabins with fireplaces and woodland views, but few come with their own indoor swimming pool. This four-bedroom getaway near Callicoon has all three, plus an infrared sauna for the ultimate wellness-centric stay. Cathedral ceilings, exposed timber, and two wood-burning fireplaces give the interiors a classic mountain house look, while oversized windows keep the surrounding three acres of forest in view. Outside, a path leads down to Callicoon Creek, and a generously-sized deck and fire pit make the most of the secluded setting. The house sleeps 10, so there's room to bring a crowd without having to share a hotel pool with strangers. From $410 per night.

The Invisible House, with its mirror-clad façade, is a well-known landmark in its area of the Joshua Tree Desert. Staying here ensures a prime place for trekking out on hikes, while indoors you can take in wraparound views of the landscape while you swim laps in the deep end. From $2,333 per night.
