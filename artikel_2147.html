Romano: Milan ‘agree terms’ with Krösche after derby win – Cardinale gets his man

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Through the summer, Gerry Cardinale wanted to hire one man to help lead the AC Milan project and he’s now got him.

There have been several names linked to Milan and their vacant Head of Football role in recent months, yet one man has always seemed to be the priority: Markus Krösche. The German didn’t leave Frankfurt in the summer, despite things looking likely at several points, but the American never lost hope.

So, with Krösche admitting his desire to move on to a new challenge, Cardinale pushed harder and has now gotten his man.

Talks of a deal being reached soon have been prominent recently, but after the win against Inter, a positive night was made even better by a report from Fabrizio Romano.

“AC Milan have agreed terms with Markus Krösche to become new Head of Football, wanted by Gerry Cardinale.

“Advanced talks for weeks and verbal agreement now done for the man wanted since last June. Despite denials, deal agreed in principle.”

It’s a huge deal for the Rossoneri and Cardinale, who’s got his man after months of hard work, and now the work begins to continue improving the squad.
