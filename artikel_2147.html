Update emerges on future of Manchester City’s goalkeepers – it could impact QPR and Pierce Charles

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

QPR signed Pierce Charles on loan from Manchester City in the summer, following his switch from Sheffield Wednesday to the Premier League giants. The 20-year-old was viewed as a big talent before this season, but his starring performances for the Hoops have put the wider footballing world on notice.

As Charles impresses at Loftus Road, change could await in the goalkeeping department back at his parent club. The Athletic tips Manchester City to make changes in between the sticks by moving out both Gianluigi Donnarumma and backup Geronimo Rulli in 2027.

Rulli, 34, is only cover, but Donnarumma has drawn criticism for his shortcomings in possession, given that City need a goalkeeper who is comfortable in helping in build-up phases. This is undoubtedly Charles’ standout attribute, and it may well see him accelerated into first-team contention at the Etihad Stadium.

To make it clear, there is nothing suggested about a Charles recall from QPR in The Athletic’s new report. In October, it was confirmed that Manchester City have an option to bring him back in January if they wish, but there were no plans at the time.

Regular EFL viewers have known about Charles’ potential for some time. He emerged on the first-team stage at Sheffield Wednesday and quickly impressed with his distribution, showing composure beyond his years and a passing range that wouldn’t look out of place in midfield.

Those same attributes in possession have been on show with the Hoops, but Charles is growing in confidence in commanding his box and claiming crosses too. A save percentage of 67.8% shows there is still room for improvement with regard to shot-stopping (FotMob).

However, Charles’ passing abilities make him an ideal fit for Manchester City’s style, and he seems bound to get a chance in the future. Accuracy is one thing, but the Northern Irishman’s technique sees means he can play flat balls into the attackers too, allowing them to bring it down on their chest or with their feet rather than challenging for headers with big Championship centre-backs.

This allows QPR to get the ball forward quickly and keep it there. It is a rare ability in goalkeepers, and one that will not go unnoticed at Manchester City.

Manchester City need a goalkeeper comfortable in build-up, and Charles is just that. However, at this moment in time, it would be a big risk to pull him from his QPR loan and throw him into the starting lineup.

As touched on before, his save percentage shows he still needs to improve as a shot-stopper. The progress he is making in West London suggests he can grow and become a starting option for City further down the line, but at present, the areas he still has to improve could be exposed at an elite level.

It means the Hoops should be confident of keeping Charles for the season – it would be best for him to stay for the full campaign too.

However, in the interest of staying prepared, it might be wise for the R’s to eye potential replacements. That way, if Manchester City do make a bold move to recall Charles, they are ready to act swiftly when January comes around.
