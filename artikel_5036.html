XRP’s Price Fails To Hold $1 After a Brief Spike, But..

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Home » Ripple (XRP) » XRP’s Price Fails To Hold $1 After a Brief Spike, But..

Institutional progress continues, but the development of Ripple’s custody service doesn’t spark up a rally.

XRP’s price briefly climbed above $1 this week, reaching roughly $1.03 before sellers pushed it back below the level within hours. The move followed a rebound from near $0.91, leaving the token trading around $0.93 to $0.96.

The push above $1 was helped in part by traders closing bearish futures positions. XRP short liquidations made up a substantial share of recent XRP futures liquidations, a dynamic that can produce sharp rallies without durable buying.

Spot-market data has shown sellers retaining the upper hand in recent weeks, though the pace of net selling has eased. Large crypto holders accumulated an estimated $72 million in XRP over several days, standing apart from reported selling in Bitcoin and Ether (ETH).

That activity has not yet turned $1 from resistance into a reliable floor. XRP was rejected around the level more than once during the latest stretch of trading.

The price weakness has continued even as Ripple expands its institutional footprint. Yapı Kredi, one of Türkiye’s largest private banks, plans to use Ripple Custody for digital-asset services connected to its crypto platform, with the infrastructure operated on the bank’s own premises.

Ripple’s RLUSD stablecoin has also grown. Its circulating supply recently reached a high near $2.87 billion, briefly overtaking USD1 before subsequent redemptions put the rival token marginally back ahead.

💵 Ripple's RLUSD has overtaken Trump-linked USD1 by circulating supply, hitting a record $2.87B. Ripple's stablecoin keeps growing, even in a bear market. The onchain dollar keeps growing. This is where real adoption is happening.#RLUSD #Ripple #Stablecoins

About $761 million of RLUSD supply is held on the XRP Ledger, where XRP is used to pay transaction fees.

Those developments strengthen Ripple’s broader payments, custody and stablecoin business, but they do not automatically create immediate buying pressure for XRP.

Turkish XRP trading against the lira, for example, has fallen sharply from year-earlier levels despite the bank custody announcement.

XRP’s price has lost about 26% over the past month and remains well below its longer-term trend averages. The altcoin now trades just one cent above the low-tier Bollinger Band (BOLL).

A break above the recent high near $1.03 could bring the $1.04 to $1.08 area into view, while failure to hold the $0.91 to $0.92 support zone could expose $0.86.

Ripple’s institutional progress may matter over a longer horizon, but XRP investors are still waiting for that momentum to show up decisively in the market.
