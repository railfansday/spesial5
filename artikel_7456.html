YouTube expands AI-powered creative chatbot to all mobile users

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

Ask Studio offers creators guidance on project management and ideation and is now available for Android and iOS.

YouTube has expanded its Ask Studio artificial intelligence-powered chatbot to all mobile users, while it's also added visual prompts for Ask Studio queries, and added a new auto-clipping option for podcast creators.

First, YouTube is bringing Ask Studio to the YouTube Studio app for both Android and iOS.

YouTube has been gradually expanding access to its Ask Studio creative assistant, which provides AI-generated guidance. Earlier this month, YouTube also began testing an integrated Ask Studio and YouTube Help chat element in order to streamline assistance.

Expanded access on mobile will make it easier for creators to manage their projects at any time, and any changes made in the mobile app will be reflected on the desktop version.

As explained by YouTube Head of Editorial Rene Ritchie: “Analyze how recent uploads are performing with quick summaries of audience sentiment and top comments, capture ideas, outline scripts and draft titles on the fly, without waiting to go back to your computer.”

In addition to announcing the expanded launch, Ritchie said creators can prompt Ask Studio via voice-to-text, or upload visuals to Ask Studio for expanded context.

Ritchie said creators can use this feature for thumbnail ideation and also upload sketches, photos or graphics to generate new concepts.

“You can also upload still frames from your work in progress edit to get targeted notes on framing, composition before exporting,” Ritchie said.

YouTube also updated its analytics element to provide more plain language explainers.

As shown in this example, the updated YouTube analytics presentation will include AI-generated explainers of specific elements, as well as additional guidance on key points of note.

Ritchie added that the updated analytics are designed to create less on-screen clutter and put more focus on key metrics.

Finally, YouTube wants to make it easier for podcasters to share segments of their conversations by offering a new Shorts generation feature that will recommend highlights from longer videos to clip.

As shown in this image, podcasters can access a list of recommended Shorts within their longer uploads, which they can review and share as previews in order to generate more interest in their content.

YouTube said these suggested clips will be identified by AI, and are designed to help creators select the best moments from each video.

As per YouTube: “For Shorts, your 16:9 podcast episode will be transformed into a verticalized video, keeping the most important parts of the video in the frame. You’ll also see captions automatically added to your video, and you’ll be able to customize the fonts, styling, and positioning.”

Streamlining the process of sharing specific highlights could be a faster, easier way to maximize podcast content.

YouTube said its AI-powered suggestions and outlines are currently available only to creators in select countries and languages, although the platform said it is “actively working to expand this to more creators soon.
