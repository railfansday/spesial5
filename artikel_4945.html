“The thing that happens, especially in long-term relationships, is people tend to revert to a default. Now, there’s nothing wrong with a position that consistently helps you orgasm. But “our nervous systems adapt and become less excited by something that feels routine, or like the same thing over and over again,” she explains. “Sometimes, excitement can come from something not working most effectively—or getting to orgasm—but from something being new.”

While trying out new sex positions can certainly help you climax, novelty also produces feel-good hormones that benefit your overall health. And novelty isn’t just good for your physical health—it can also boost your emotional connection with your partner.
