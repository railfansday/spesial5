7 Glitches That Have Already Been Discovered in The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake

The original Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is one of the most speedrun games ever, with numerous people making new record attempts on a yearly basis. These many attempts have led to countless glitches and exploits being found throughout the game.

With the release of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake, it was only a matter of time speedrunners discovered new glitches and exploits to break the game wide open. Well, it only took them a few days to find ways to get items early, infinitely jump, and more.

It will only be a matter of time before the newer version of the game becomes just as broken as the original. Maybe Speedrunners will be able to teleport to the end of the game as well.

You end up getting the Hylian Shield very early on in the Ocarina of Time, and the same can be said in the remake. Thanks to this, you can perform a very simple super jump that takes advantage of the interaction between objects in the game.

Don’t worry, there are plenty of other jump glitches that have been found, but this is the most simple. For this glitch, you need to be child Link. Stand on or in some type of interactable object. This can be a box or standing inside a chest.

Hold down ZL with the Hylian Shield equipped, angle your camera to look down at Link, and then press X. This will give you a high jump that you can use to reach ledges and get places early. Of course, there are other jumps that are more effective, but this one is very easy to use.

Whenever you are a child in Ocarina of Time, you can purchase Magic Beans. These Magic Beans are an item that you can plant in dirt mounds, and when you come back as an adult, find plants that will float you to new areas.

Well, it was discovered that you can use the game’s physics against it to achieve an impossible jump. When standing on one of the flowers, if you jump at the right time after using the Hookshot on the ground in front of you, it will launch you into the air.

This has allowed speedrunnners to skip portions of the game, with the most notable being that they can get the Silver Gauntlets early.

The speedrunning community in Breath of the Wild is thriving in the game's freedom.

There are a lot of times where jumping is what will break the game for you, and this is the case after you get the Silver Gauntlets. Once you have obtained the Silver Gauntlets, jump into the chest as adult Link.

Once you are in the chest, run forward against the back wall, then pull out your shield and quickly swap to the ocarina while still holding the shield button. While performing this action, hold the angle of your walk the entire way to the right.

Now, cancel out the ocarina and jump immediately. When performed correctly, this glitch sends you flying up onto the giant statue outside of the Spirit Temple. You can then walk around the statue and drop onto the other side, allowing you to get the Mirror Shield early.

I swear this is the last jump I will talk about, but this one matters a lot, as it lets you reach areas early. Essentially, you are trying to get the game’s frames to buffer and send you higher and higher into the air. This allows you to jump very high, reaching new areas early where Magic Bean flowers don’t exist.

What you want to do is perform a forward jump attack with a Deku Stick. As soon as you start the jump, press the ZR button to open the item menu. This pauses the screen, and rapidly swapping to another item will keep your momentum going up.

Throughout the process, you will need to continuously tap ZR rapidly to get the menu to open so you can swap items back and forth between something and the Deku Stick. This will keep your momentum going up, allowing you to jump over high walls and even get to areas like the Shadow Temple early.

It is nice that Nintendo decided to give Link a sprint button in the Ocarina of Time Remake. However, speedrunners have already found a way to make that button obsolete, as you can move faster by roll canceling.

What is roll canceling? Glad you asked! It is when you cancel a roll midway through and start a new one. This makes the roll happen very quickly, allowing you to get places at a faster rate. It is also easy to do.

Basically, you just want to start a roll and attack midway through, and then roll again immediately, and attack again midway through. Keep doing this and you get a roll that is much faster than sprinting. You just look silly doing it.

Tears of the Kingdom is a fertile ground for experimentation, and fans are already breaking the game's rules as we speak.

This skip uses the same idea as the infinite jump with the Deku Stick, but you can use it early to skip part of the Deku Tree dungeon. In the basement of the Deku Tree dungeon, you will find a ledge that spawns a Deku Baba.

Here, you can hug the wall and use a small jump to get onto a tiny ledge. From here, you can jump with the Deku Stick and quickly swap over to another item to get a tiny bit of extra height out of the jump.

This allows Link to grab the ledge, pulling himself up to the area. Normally, you need to go through the entire dungeon, but using this method, you can push a box down early, giving you access to the end of the dungeon in a much quicker way. Better yet, this is also a glitch from the original game, but it has become easier thanks to item swapping.

The Hover Boots are an interesting item in Ocarina of Time. When equipped, they allow Link to walk a short distance in the air after walking off a ledge. However, this use is momentary, and you fall shortly after doing so.

Speedrunners have discovered a means of letting the boots last an infinite amount of time, and all you need is a bottle. When you walk off a ledge, you just need to spam the use of a bottle.

Each time you spam the bottle, it refills your hover boot time, allowing you to take a few extra steps in the air. Keep repeating using the bottle every couple of steps, and you can keep walking across the air infinitely. This has already been used to skip the fire trial, and people are trying to figure out other uses for it.
