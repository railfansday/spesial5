Svilar partially returns to group training

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Roma continues preparations for the away match against Parma, scheduled for Saturday, November 21st at 6:00 PM at the Ennio Tardini Stadium.

There are three days to go until the match against Alberto Gilardino’s team, an important fixture for the Giallorossi, who are intent on staying close to Lazio and Inter at the top of the table.

Gian Piero Gasperini is carefully monitoring the condition of his players, especially those recovering from injury, to ensure they have as many players available as possible when the season resumes.

The most encouraging signs are from Paulo Dybala and Gianluca Mancini, who, like in the previous session, trained regularly with the rest of the team.

This is a positive sign for both, who are increasingly close to full fitness for the trip to Emilia. The situation is different for Mile Svilar and Pierluigi Gollini, who only participated in part of the training session.

Svilar’s recovery is progressing positively, although he has not yet fully returned to normal activity.

After a stress fracture in his fifth toe, the goalkeeper is gradually regaining confidence in kicking the ball, a crucial step towards his full availability.

Meanwhile, the Giallorossi squad is nearing completion after the international break.

Soulé, Balerdi, and Castro will be available again starting Thursday, once their commitments with Argentina are concluded.

Their return will allow Gasperini to work with the full squad in the final phase of preparation. The Grugliasco coach is scheduled for a press conference on Friday, where he will be able to assess the condition of the unavailable players and discuss possible lineup options. The team will then depart for Parma in the afternoon.
