Opinion: Liverpool have found their perfect Alisson successor, but it's not who you think

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Alisson is one of Liverpool's biggest-named players to be linked with an exit this summer.

The Brazilian's contract is set to expire in June 2027 and, right now, it doesn't feel like the Reds are going to offer him a new deal.

Indeed, rumours about both ends of the deal have been emerging and, even though the former Roma man has been an important asset for Liverpool this term, his age coupled with the sheer number of goalkeepers the Reds have on their books make a deal difficult.

Obviously, Alisson's time at the club has been well regarded. He's won two Premier League titles and one Champions League trophy.

But, since he's now in the twilight stages of his career, Liverpool need to approach this deal with caution and weigh up their options before making a final decision.

Lucas Chevalier wants to leave Paris Saint-Germain, Liverpool could make a move

Having a decent goalkeeper is one of the most important things for any football team.

Even if you have one of the best teams in the world in terms of outfielders, if your goalkeeper is poor, they're more than likely going to let you down.

This was the case when Giorgi Mamardashvili played last season with fans demanding that a new 'keeper was signed.

Initially, the Georgian was expected to take over from the Brazilian but, thanks to how poorly he performed, that didn't end up being the case.

Mamardashvili's poor form led to fans wondering who their next goalkeeper could be with a fresh update from L'Equipe now making that more than clear.

"Lucas Chevalier is ready to leave Paris Saint-Germain this winter. But there are many conditions for a departure. PSG will notably have to find a replacement before considering letting him go," they wrote.

Lucas Chevalier would be an out of the box signing for Liverpool

When Chevalier was brought to PSG by Luis Enrique, fans, pundits and journalists all thought that the Frenchman was destined to become their first choice.

Obviously he had some big shoes to fill after Gianluigi Donnarumma exited for Manchester City but, for Enrique's style of play, the Frenchman was the better option.

But, for one reason or another, the 25-year-old didn't hit the right notes and has been downgraded to their back-up by Matvey Safonov.

Neither of them are considered among the world's best but, Chevalier is almost certainly a better option for Liverpool than Mamardashvili would be.

Others such as Bart Verbruggen and Diogo Costa have also been linked with Liverpool but, since Chevalier actually wants to exit, a deal for him might be easier for Liverpool to agree on with PSG.

Nobody knows what the future has in store for any of Liverpool's first team squad but, if Chevalier is a player welcomed through the Anfield doors, the Reds will be in a decent place, even if he is replacing Alisson.
