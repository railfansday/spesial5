Tuttosport: Stuttgart midfielder back on Milan’s radar amid imminent Krösche arrival

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

AC Milan are evaluating several players for the midfield in the hope of finding Luka Modric’s heir, with a previous target re-emerging on their radar.

Modric renewed his contract with Milan in the summer and has been performing well so far, certainly showing that age still isn’t an issue for him. In fact, he has even said that as long as he feels like he does right now, there is no reason to retire.

That said, Milan are obviously thinking about what life could be without the veteran. Ardon Jashari was perhaps initially identified as his heir, but his struggles so far and characteristics suggest someone else might arrive for that role.

According to today’s edition of Tuttosport, as transcribed by MilanNews, the likes of Yasin Ayari (Brighton) and Alex Scott (Bournemouth) are two players on Milan’s radar. However, with the arrival of Markus Krösche as the new Head of Football, another name could go to the top of the list.

We are talking about Stuttgart’s Angelo Stiller, who was linked with a move to the Rossoneri at the end of 2024 as well. The 25-year-old has a €40m release clause in his contract and several big clubs are keeping an eye on him.

It’s still very early, of course, as Krösche hasn’t even officially joined the club yet, but the German midfielder at least appears to be back in the fold.
