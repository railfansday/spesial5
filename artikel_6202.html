17 Best Airbnbs in Bangkok for Your Next Trip to Thailand's Capital

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

A vast metropolis where modern skyscrapers mix with antique wooden houses, Bangkok is, in my opinion, one of the best cities in the world. Whether you’re on the hunt for boat noodles or Thai massages, there’s so much to do and no bad time of year to visit. And when it comes to where to stay, travelers will find just as much variety among the excellent lodging options. While this Thai city delivers a sensory overload of epic proportions, there are plenty of peaceful pockets and quiet corners perfect for catching your breath after a long day of exploring. One of the best ways to get a bit of solitude is by renting a dreamy Airbnb in the area.

Over the years, I’ve spent months in Thailand’s capital city. I've visited for weekend girls trips, concerts, and trips with family, staying everywhere from Siam to Chinatown. I've experienced the city's endless ways to fill your days, from world-class museums to addictive street food to some of Asia’s best cocktail bars. If you're planning a stay and want a little more elbow room than the city's hotels might allow, there are plenty of charming rentals to lay your head down at, from studios with incredible river views to massive homes for the whole family to shophouses in Chinatown. Read on for some of the best Airbnbs in Bangkok.

What are the best neighborhoods to stay in Bangkok?

Chinatown and Talat Noi are always my go-to neighborhoods when I'm in Bangkok. They are full of fun, trendy bars, restaurants, and cafés worth spending some time wandering around. Sukhumvit is a great neighborhood for first-timers, with plenty of restaurant options, rooftop bars, and access to the MRT.

When is the best season to visit Bangkok?

Many people suggest that the best time to visit Bangkok is at the end of the rainy season—October through December—before the smog gets bad in January. March to May is the hottest time to visit, so it may be best to avoid if you don't do well in the heat, but I highly recommend planning a trip around Songkran (April 13–15), a.k.a. Thai New Year. It's known for hosting the world's largest water fight, and in my opinion, is one of Asia's best festivals.
