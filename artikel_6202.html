Unexpected Upgrade? Tell Us What Happened

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

We’re looking to share readers’ stories of unexpected upgrades, the moments a regular ticket turned into something far better than planned.

Have you been moved to a better cabin on a flight, or received a surprise perk at the airport? We’d love to hear from you.

This could be anything from being bumped from economy to business class to a surprise upgrade at the gate. We’re also interested in how it happened, whether it was a kind gesture from the crew or an oversold cabin.

If you’re a passenger or work in aviation and have a story to share, please get in touch with a brief outline of what happened and when it occurred. Include the airline and route if you can. Photos and videos are welcome.

If you would like to take part in this series, send us a message at admin@simpleflying.com!

Selected stories may be featured in an upcoming Simple Flying article, and interview quotes may also appear on our sister site, TheTravel.com.
