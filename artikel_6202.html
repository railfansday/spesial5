Delta Air Lines and American Express offer seven cobranded cards for everyone from occasional Delta flyers to frequent travelers.

Depending on the card and the offer you qualify for, you could earn up to 125,000 bonus SkyMiles.

Here’s how the current offers compare and what to consider before applying.

*Bonus value is based on TPG’s November 2026 valuations and is not provided or reviewed by the issuer.

American Express has a few eligibility rules to keep in mind before applying for one of these cards. For starters, Amex typically only allows a person to earn one welcome bonus per card in their lifetime. That means you'll want to prioritize applying when a card offers an elevated, limited-time offer, such as the ones all but one of these cards have right now.

You also may not be eligible for a welcome bonus on a personal Delta Amex card if you have or previously had a higher-tier Delta Amex card. For example, if you have or previously had the Delta Reserve Amex, you may not be eligible for the welcome offers on the Delta Blue, Delta Gold or Delta Platinum.

Delta's business Amex cards are not subject to this same "family rule."

Amex may consider other factors when determining your eligibility, including your income, credit score and history with the issuer. Fortunately, you'll generally find out whether you're eligible for a welcome offer before accepting the card.

Delta offers four personal cards. The card that is right for you depends on how frequently you fly with Delta and how much you’re willing to pay for an annual fee.

Let’s take a look at what differentiates each of the Delta SkyMiles personal cards.

Best for: Delta loyalists or those who want Sky Club access (subject to limitations)

The Delta SkyMiles Reserve is ideal for hardcore Delta fans and frequent flyers.

Current welcome offer: Find out your offer and see if you are eligible for as high as 125,000 bonus miles after spending $5,000 on purchases in the first six months of card membership. (Welcome offers may vary, and you may not be eligible for an offer.)

This bonus is worth up to $1,500 per our valuations.

If you qualify for the full 125,000 miles, this is an excellent time to apply. It matches the highest mileage bonus we’ve seen on the card and requires $1,000 less spending than the previous 125,000-mile offer with the lowest spending requirement.

Key benefits: Delta’s premium consumer credit card offers a range of valuable perks. For loyal Delta flyers who enjoy visiting the Sky Club, this card can pay for itself through lounge access, spending boosts and other benefits.

If you fly frequently with Delta but can’t justify the pricier Delta SkyMiles Reserve, the Delta SkyMiles Platinum is worth considering.

Current welcome offer: Find out your offer and see if you are eligible for as high as 90,000 bonus miles after spending $3,000 on purchases in the first six months of card membership. (Welcome offers may vary, and you may not be eligible for an offer.)

Per TPG's valuations, this bonus is worth up to $1,080.

If you qualify for the full 90,000 miles, this is a strong offer for $3,000 in spending. We’ve seen offers with more miles or a statement credit, though they required more spending.

Key benefits: This card is a good option if you fly Delta regularly and want to earn miles on purchases and reach elite status. However, even if you aren’t pursuing status, the companion certificate alone can make the annual fee for this card worth it.

Best for: Occasional Delta flyers or those test-driving a Delta card

If you want to take advantage of a welcome bonus but don’t fly Delta frequently, the Delta SkyMiles Gold is a strong option.

TPG's valuations place this bonus at up to $960.

If you qualify for the full 80,000 miles, now is a good time to apply. However, we have seen up to 90,000 miles — as well as up to 80,000 miles plus a statement credit — offered before.

Annual fee: $0 introductory annual fee for the first year, then $150

Key benefits: This card is ideal for occasional Delta flyers seeking a few extra perks when flying with the airline.

With this card, you’ll earn 2 miles per dollar spent on Delta purchases made directly with the airline.

If you want an entry-level card to start earning SkyMiles and fly less frequently with Delta, then Delta SkyMiles Blue is the card for you.

Current welcome offer: Earn 10,000 bonus miles after spending $1,000 on purchases in the first six months of card membership.

According to TPG’s valuations, these miles are worth $120. The SkyMiles Blue typically doesn't receive an elevated offer, so we recommend applying whenever you're ready.

Key benefits: As a no-annual-fee card, it offers fewer benefits than the others. Still, there are some simple perks for cardmembers.
