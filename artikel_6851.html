Dakota Johnson and MGK Are Already Outfit Coordinating

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Dakota Johnson and Machine Gun Kelly may be one of the newest couples in our Hollywood circles, but it seems they’re already totally in sync—sartorially, at least.

Last night, the actor and the musician were seen on a dinner date at Mediterranean restaurant Casaléna in Calabasas, California, and in coordinating leather looks, no less.

Johnson was cool and fabulous as per usual, keeping warm with the help of a black leather jacket with a tan shearling collar. The sleek, zip piece hails from Dôen and retails for a hot $1,850.

A lover of high-low style, the Materialists star paired her jacket with baggy, ripped blue jeans and a statement bag from Prada. This was a vintage leopard-print pony-hair tote with black leather top handles.

Black ballet flats, some classic gold jewelry, and a messy top knot held up with a red claw clip pulled it all together.

MGK—born Colson Baker—matched her vibe in his own black leather biker jacket: the textured, studded Sp5der debossed web leather jacket by Spider Worldwide (Sp5der). He styled the outerwear over a slouchy black hoodie. As for bottoms, the “I Think I’m OKAY” singer opted for baggy orange sweatpants that pooled around his white Vans sneakers. A bubblegum-pink bandana (that matched the lettering on his jacket) added an additional pop of color.

Johnson and MGK have not publicly commented on their relationship, but they have been spotted out together several times since July, after they reportedly hit it off at Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce’s starry Madison Square Garden wedding.
