Everything I Know About ‘Selling Sunset’ Season 10 So Far, Including the Major Changes to the Cast

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Chrishell is out, Christine is back, and the O Group is getting a major shake-up.

I’m going to be honest: I wasn’t sure there was a future for Selling Sunset after Chrishell Stause announced her departure from the series following season 9, but it seems as though Selling Sunset season 10 is, in fact, happening. What’s more: Chrishell’s absence won’t be the only cast change fans can anticipate.

After a four-year hiatus from the series, Christine Quinn will be returning to Selling Sunset following her season 5 exit, as will Heather Rae El Moussa, who exited the series after having a reduced role in season 7. And since only four cast members from season 9 are returning, you can expect a whole lot of new faces, too.

What other shake-ups can you expect from this upcoming season? Here’s everything that has been revealed about Selling Sunset season 10 so far. Starting with…

At long last, we have a release date! Netflix confirmed that season 10 will hit Netflix on December 4, over a year after the release of season 9 in October 2025.

Why the delay? An Us Weekly source previously revealed back in March that the cast was “supposed to start filming and it has been delayed because they are waiting for [Christine Quinn] to finish negotiations.”

In addition to confirming Christine Quinn and Heather Rae El Moussa’s return, Us Weekly first reported that newbie Tara Burns would be joining the cast, with a source saying, “She will be full time and has worked with Jason [Oppenheim] before.”

Now the other new cast members have been revealed as well: Jordyn Braff (who previously appeared on Selling the City and also famously dated Trevor Noah), Sonni Pacheco (who was briefly married to Jeremy Renner and shares a daughter with him), and Sasha Lance (best known as Breonna Strong in All American).

Returning from season 9 to round out the crew are Amanza Smith, Bre Tiesi, and Brett and Jason Oppenheim—and you can expect brief appearances from Selling the OC star Alex Hall as well.

With several new(ish) folks joining the cast, many others will be departing. Along with Chrishell, People reported in March that controversial cast member Nicole Young would not be returning, with a rep saying it was “entirely Nicole’s own decision to step away from the show” and move to Nashville, as “she felt she was no longer being represented authentically.” Mmkay!

Following season 9, it was widely believed that Chelsea Lazkani would also be exiting—and while she initially shut down those rumors, taking to Threads in March to write, “If you plan on firing me, do it loudly and proudly and state the reason. If I decide to leave on my own accord, I’ll make that clear as well,” it seems things have changed.

In May, the Daily Mail reported that—in addition to Chelsea—four other cast members were being cut from the show: Emma Hernan, Mary Bonnet, Alanna Gold, and Sandra Vergara.
