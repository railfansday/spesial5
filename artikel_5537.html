Nearly half of employees exaggerate their AI use, while companies pay a premium to rehire laid-off talent, highlighting the growing gap between AI ambitions and workforce readiness

VANCOUVER, BC, Nov. 10, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Visier, the award-winning industry leader and creator of Visier Workforce AI, today released its annual workforce trends report, Beyond the AI Hype: The Workforce Trends That Will Define 2027, revealing six critical shifts that will reshape how organizations manage talent, deploy AI, and make workforce decisions in the year ahead.

As organizations accelerate AI investments, the challenge of proving the technology's business value increases. Visier's prior research, AI Usage at Work: "Performative AI" Stems from Lack of Confidence in Leaders, revealed a growing disconnect between executive AI ambitions and workforce realities. Nearly half (48%) of employees admit to exaggerating their AI usage or expertise, while 59% say their employer either lacks a clear plan for how AI will affect jobs, or they're unsure whether one exists. Meanwhile, organizations are dealing with hidden costs of workforce decisions made in anticipation of AI-driven efficiencies, including rehiring previously laid-off employees at a 3% salary increase.

The Six Workforce Trends That Will Define 2027

This latest research from Visier, which examines more than 10 million live employee records from across the globe, signals a turning point for HR leaders. In 2027, success will depend less on how quickly organizations adopt AI and more on how effectively they redesign work, preserve critical human expertise, and use workforce intelligence to connect technology investments to measurable business outcomes.

"The conversation around AI fundamentally changed in 2026. Organizations are moving beyond asking how quickly they can implement AI to asking what it's actually delivering for their business," said Dr. Andrea Derler, Principal, Research and Customer Value at Visier. "You can't make informed decisions about AI without first understanding your workforce, and because of this, the businesses that use workforce intelligence to understand where AI creates value, where human expertise remains essential, and how to bring the two together will be the ones to drive top business outcomes."

Across all six trends, it's apparent that organizations cannot realize AI's full potential without a clear understanding of their people, skills, workflows, and business needs. HR leaders will play a central role in determining where automation adds value and which workforce investments deliver measurable returns.

Rather than treating AI adoption as a technology initiative in and of itself, Visier predicts that leading organizations will use workforce intelligence to guide decisions across HR, finance, and operations, creating a stronger foundation for sustainable business growth that connects people and talent to bottom-line results.
