Jonathan Scott’s Trick for Making a Fake Christmas Tree Look Real Is Brilliant

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Jonathan Scott shares his favorite Christmas decorating tricks, from making artificial trees look real to refreshing decor without spending more.

There’s no place like home for the holidays. As it gets closer to the most magical time of the year, stores are stocked with decorations big and small, and figuring out how you want your home to look can be a little overwhelming. Thankfully, experts in the home decor space are sharing their seasonal tips to help you get ready.

HGTV’s Jonathan Scott shared a video with a few Christmas decorating hacks he and his partner, Zooey Deschanel, use in their home.

“I like it to be a little bit nostalgic, a little bit fun, and a whole lot of holiday spirit,” Scott says.

There’s no one right way to decorate for Christmas, and even if so that doesn’t mean you should stick to the conventional script, according to Scott. “There are lots of different rules when decorating for the holidays, but of course, as we know, rules are meant to be broken,” Scott says. “So it really depends on your comfort level.”

Even the nicest artificial trees can look a little too perfect. Scott’s trick is to stuff a few extra branches into the tree, leaving some sticking out at random. He also adds more lights, including larger bulbs, even though his tree is already pre-lit.

Scott has been collecting nutcrackers for years, and yes, he even names them. Rather than display them the same way every Christmas, he changes things up with different garlands, beads, and branches. It’s a way to get a new look out of decorations he already owns.

Those giant stacked presents in Scott’s house look like they’d be a pain to store, but they’re not. Each box fits inside the next, so the whole display takes up a fraction of the space once Christmas is over.

At the top of everyone’s mind around the holidays should be safety.

Scott recommends being mindful of fragile decorations, especially if you have young children or large pets. Glass ornaments and other breakable decorations can easily get knocked over, so consider using sturdier pieces in areas where kids and pets spend a lot of time.

The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) also recommends looking for a “fire resistant” label when purchasing an artificial Christmas tree and regularly watering live trees to reduce fire risks.

Whether you’re adding a few new touches to your favorite decorations or going all out with a Christmas tree in every room, the most important thing is creating a space that feels festive to you.
