Erling Haaland crowned Golden Player 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

This Monday, the 24th edition of the Golden Boy Awards crowned Pau Cubarsí as the Golden Boy 2026. A great reward for the Spanish central defender who notably won the World Cup with La Roja. The 19-year-old succeeded Jude Bellingham, Lamine Yamal and Désiré Doué for this trophy which rewards the best player under 21 playing in Europe.

Italian media Tuttosport which organizes this ceremony has also awarded another category for several years with the Golden Player. As in 2023, Erling Haaland won the title for this year 2026. He succeeds Diogo Jota honored posthumously and Toni Kroos rewarded in 2024. Karim Benzema won in 2022 while Robert Lewandowski had gleaned the trophies for 2020 and 2021.
