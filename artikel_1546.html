Opinion: Julian Ward masterstroke could hand Liverpool a new Virgil van Dijk

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Virgil van Dijk has been a constant part of Liverpool's starting XI ever since he joined the club from Southampton back in 2017.

At the time, most people thought that the Reds had massively overpaid for the Dutchman but, after what he's done during his nine years at the club, he's more than repaid their faith.

However, he's now in the final year of his contract and, with him able to speak to foreign clubs about a free transfer by 1 January, a decision needs to be made on his future soon.

Indeed, negotiations were left until the last minute a couple of years ago but, if Andoni Iraola wants to get Liverpool competing for titles again, he needs to be prepared for whatever is thrown his way.

That's why, this latest transfer masterstroke from Julian Ward could be essential to their progress.

Nico Schlotterbeck is going to be Liverpool's next Virgil van Dijk

One of the main things that Van Dijk has is his physical presence.

The 6'4 Dutchman has been the rock at the back for Liverpool for as long as anyone can remember.

Without him, the Reds would have won a lot less trophies than they have.

But, since he'll be 36 years old by the start of the 2026-27 season, Liverpool must figure out if he can continue performing to this level for another two years.

If he can't, a succession plan needs to be put in place with this summer potentially the best time for an amicable parting of ways.

For this to happen, a replacement needs to be sourced because, as good as Giovanni Leoni is likely to be, he's still not played any football for over a year and, since he's never played a minute of Premier League football, nobody knows how good he's actually going to be.

That's why someone such as Borussia Dortmund's Nico Schlotterbeck needs to be targeted.

Nico Schlotterbeck would be a superb transfer for Andoni Iraola

The fact that Van Dijk plays as the left-sided centre-back in Iraola's back four is also a bit of a problem.

Among their ranks at the moment, the Reds have no senior left-footed centre-backs and, most of the defenders they've been linked to are all also right-footed.

Obviously, this hasn't been a problem for Van Dijk but, that doesn't mean it won't be for another defender.

That's why Schlotterbeck, who is left-footed and can also be used as a left-back is perfect.

This, coupled with his Champions League experience, his age (26 years old) and his ability on the ball all make him the perfect successor for the Liverpoool captain.

The German also has a release clause embedded within his contract that can only be activated by certain clubs (Liverpool are rumoured to be one of them) that could play in their favour.

Nobody knows what Iraola's team will look like at the start of the 2027-28 season but, if it includes a team with Schlotterbeck in their defence, they'll be onto a winner.
