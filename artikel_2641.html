Kyle Shanahan on Brandon Aiyuk signing with Commanders: &quot;They got a good player&quot;

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Aaron Rodgers rigmarole has pushed plenty of other stories out of the spotlight. Including the sudden second act for Brandon Aiyuk.

Aiyuk visited the Commanders on Wednesday, two days after being released by the 49ers and one day after clearing waivers. He promptly signed with the team's practice squad.

49ers coach Kyle Shanahan was asked for his thoughts on Aiyuk landing with the Commanders.

"None," Shanahan told reporters. "It was just a transactional thing and it's good for Washington. They got a good player."

Indeed they did. That's why the 49ers signed him to a four-year, $120 million contract only 27 months ago. They could have traded him to the Steelers. They paid him.

It all fell apart after that, perhaps fueled by the lingering rancor of the protracted and contentious contract talks. Everyone hopefully learned a lesson.

Now, the Commanders have a much-needed piece to the offense. A player who can help fill the void created when Terry McLaurin was lost for the season.

And the Commanders — despite being 4-5-1 — remain in solid position to make a late-season run. With seven games to play, they're two games behind the Giants. The Commanders hold the tiebreaker, thanks to the 1-0-1 record in their 2026 head-to-head competition.

The Commanders face the Cardinals, Titans, Texans, Falcons, Vikings, Jaguars, and Cowboys. The Giants have the Colts, 49ers, Seahawks, Browns, Lions, Cowboys, and Eagles.

Along the way, the Commanders will also need to leapfrog the 5-6 Cowboys.

Still, it's far from crazy to think the Commanders can pull it off.

However it plays out, the Commanders have added a player who will potentially help them navigate the remaining schedule in a way that vaults them into the postseason.

Who knows? Maybe Aiyuk will get a chance to return to Santa Clara in January. And maybe the Commanders will get a chance to do what they nearly did in Week 6.

That's still weeks away. But it will be here quickly. And Commanders-49ers is one of the various potential permutations that could play out as the playoffs approach and the postseason commences.
