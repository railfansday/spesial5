Browns, Texans injuries: 8 players sit out practice, over a dozen limited

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Now that the Cleveland Browns have made their decision that Deshaun Watson will return as the starting quarterback, preparations for Week 10 versus the Houston Texans are underway. According to reports from the ground, both teams had a number of players sitting out practice to start the week. Injuries are always a story as the NFL season goes on, with 11 Cleveland players not practicing or limited and another 11 with the same designations from Houston.

Wednesday afternoon, both teams submitted their first injury reports for the week, providing all the details about their injured players;

LB Carson Schwesinger – Wrist, Ankle – Limited

LB Azeez Al-Shaair – Groin, Not Injury Related – Personal – DNP

OL Keylan Rutledge – Neck, Shoulder – DNP

S Reed Blankenship – Concussion, Hand – Limited

WR Marvin Harrison Jr. – Hamstring – Limited
