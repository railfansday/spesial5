Match review: RSL falls despite Cabral heroics

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Real Salt Lake Captain Rafael Cabral had another brilliant night as the Brazilian goalkeeper made five crucial saves that kept the Wasatch side from falling behind early on.

"We have to get more rotations, we have to move the ball across the back line quicker and we have to get the ball to the outside backs so we can move up the field faster to attack." — Head coach Pablo Mastroeni on what he thought the back line could do better.

The contest started out slowly as both sides looked for an opening in the opposition defense to find an early opening goal. Vancouver created the best chance of the opening 15' as Whitecaps striker Bryan White found himself in a pocket of space inside the Real Salt Lake penalty area. Experienced defender Lukas Engel slid over from the left side to make a crucial block to deny the home team an early lead. The Canadian side came close to scoring the first goal of the match after a beautiful bicycle kick that almost found the top corner of the net if not for a headed clearance made by Philip Quinton.

RSL had a golden opportunity to take the lead following a breakaway from Rookie striker Sergi Solans, the Spanish-born attacker saw his effort parried away by the Vancouver goalkeeper. The initial clearance was smashed into the feet of Saba Lobjanidze, whose shot forced Yohei Takaoka into making another important save.

A minute before the end of the first half, a deep cross found the foot of Édier Ocampo, who cannoned a one-time strike into the roof of the net for the opening goal of the game.

Vancouver doubled its lead following chaos in the Real Salt Lake penalty box, multiple rebounds that found the feet of the Whitecaps players. The Utah defenders threw their bodies at the ball before a big deflection save made by Cabral, clawing the ball away from the mouth of his goal. The Canadian-based team was able to dig out a dangerous cross that found the head of Bruno Caicado, who was wide open in the middle of the six-yard box to make it 2-0.

The Whitecaps came close to making it three after Caicado had a curling effort that was denied by the strong left hand of Cabral. On the ensuing corner, Caicado found himself wide out in the middle of the six-yard box for a second time. His header was powered toward the ground but another brilliant save from Cabral using the right side of his body to keep the game within striking distance.
