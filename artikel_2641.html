Bonkers Cutter Gauthier stats prove he is untouchable right now

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Anaheim Ducks had a messy offseason that threatened to end their contention window before it began. So far this season, though, they have remained a competitive team. One major reason for this is star forward Cutter Gauthier.

Gauthier may be the hottest player in the league right now. On Tuesday, he scored his fourth hat trick in nine games in a chaotic loss to the St. Louis Blues. He made history as a result, becoming the first player in league history with four hat tricks in a calendar month, via Sportsnet Stats. But the extent of his run has gone even further than that.

"Cutter Gauthier has scored 17 goals in November. That's more than the Boston Bruins, Detroit Red Wings, Columbus Blue Jackets, Buffalo Sabres, Calgary Flames, Pittsburgh Penguins, Toronto Maple Leafs or Seattle Kraken have scored in November," Associated Press reporter Greg Beacham noted on Tuesday night.

Gauthier has scored in each of his last 10 games. The last time he went without a goal was October 27 against the Vancouver Canucks. He now has 21 goals and 29 points on the season following the loss to the Blues.

The Ducks star began his incredible run on November 1st. He scored back-to-back hat tricks to begin the month. His third hat trick of the month came on November 8 against the Pittsburgh Penguins.

The Ducks still have six games remaining in the month of November. There is a chance the 22-year-old Gauthier can improve on his already incredible performance. We'll see if he can make it five hat tricks on the month when Anaheim takes on the Colorado Avalanche on Thursday.
