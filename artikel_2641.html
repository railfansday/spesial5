Instant analysis after Bengals destroy Steelers in primetime

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Cincinnati Bengals certainly didn't look like the team with the major rest disadvantage on Sunday night in primetime while walloping the Pittsburgh Steelers, 38-18.

Joe Burrow and the Bengals won the coin toss, took the ball and cruised to a 24-3 halftime lead before continuing to simply shred a deflated-looking rival.

The win puts the Bengals at 7-2 and tops in the AFC North and indeed will create more whispers about playoffs and a Burrow MVP.

Here are some quick notes, stats to know and takeaways from the game.

Just a total evisceration of the Steelers from the jump. It was 24-3 at halftime and the only Steelers points were on a drive assisted down the field by a taunting penalty on Jordan Battle.

The Bengals outgained the Steelers 337-40 in the first half, of the many impressive numbers:

The Bengals' pass-rusher depth chart is still super deep even with Cashius Howell on injured reserve. The likes of Cedric Johnson were getting in on the action while taking down Aaron Rodgers. There was a Jonathan Allen third-down sack in there as one of the many highlights.

Dalton Risner left with a knee injury after battling an illness that had him questionable earlier in the day.

It can't be stressed enough that Amarius Mims is a massive reason for this Bengals offense. The fact he can sit out there on an island with the likes of TJ Watt on cruise control is dramatically opening up what the attack can do.

Waves hand at everything: Jokes aside, the Bengals won the first downs (30-17), total yardage (484-221), yards-per-play (7.4-3.8) and all the other battles one could come up with, really. One of the most lopsided showings in the rivalry, ever.

AFC North's top dog: So much for those concerns about playing an international game last week while the Steelers were on a bye, creating a big rest disparity. The Bengals were the better team from the opening whistle, casually trouncing a rival that wanted to play the usual bully ball. Those saying the Bengals are the best team in football right now would be hard to dispute.

Now 7-2, the Bengals hit the road again for another primetime game, this time a Week 11 cross-conference game against the Washington Commanders on a Monday night.
