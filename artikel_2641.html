Rams lose offensive starter for extended amount of time

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Los Angeles Rams' injury problems along the offensive line continue to mount, with starting center Coleman Shelton reportedly expected to miss 4-6 weeks due to a hamstring strain. It's another significant blow for a team that seemingly loses another player to injury every week. At this point in the season, the Rams can hardly afford another setback, particularly at a position group that has already taken its share of hits.

Shelton becomes the second Rams starting offensive lineman to suffer an injury, putting additional pressure on an offensive line that needs consistency to keep Matthew Stafford upright and create running lanes. Losing one starter is difficult enough, but losing multiple contributors along the same unit can quickly disrupt an offense's rhythm.

It is a weekly ritual for the Rams at this point. With the Rams fighting to stay in the NFC playoff picture, their depth across the entire roster is being tested. But the offensive line needs continuity, especially with the team already navigating injuries at several other positions. And continuity along the front matters when the team is about to play playoff caliber competition.
