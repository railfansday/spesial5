Around the West: Lakers, Spurs continue reigns atop conference

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

As Thanksgiving approaches, I'm here for my weekly update about the rest of the Western Conference. Both the Los Angeles Lakers and San Antonio Spurs saw their lengthy winning streaks snapped last week but continue to sit atop the West at 14-3 with the third-seeded, 12-5 Minnesota Timberwolves two games back (and tied with two others). While two games separate second from third out West, two games also separate third from ninth, so the separation up top hasn't pervaded throughout the entire playoff landscape.

Net ratings are courtesy of Cleaning the Glass and exclude garbage time.

The Spurs dropped their first game of the month Tuesday against the Detroit Pistons but rebounded with a pair of wins over the Los Angeles Clippers and New Orleans Pelicans, including a 50-point explosion from Victor Wembanyama. That loss to Detroit did, however, provide a double whammy as Dylan Harper suffered a left hip rotator strain and will miss 1-to-3 weeks.

Despite losing a crunch-time affair on Thursday to the streaking New York Knicks (winners of four in a row), the Timberwolves authored an impressive week with three victories in four tries. They nabbed both legs of a back-to-back against the good and feisty Portland Trail Blazers then discarded the Memphis Grizzlies, 104-95, on Saturday for a shiny 3-1 record against three legitimate playoff contenders. That'll do, especially as Anthony Edwards continues to recover from knee surgery.

Among the early surprises of the NBA season, the Grizzlies are playing at 57-win pace through five weeks, despite their five-game winning streak being snapped Saturday by Minnesota. They're top eight in both offensive and defensive rating and are riding a rotation full of good, versatile NBA players to success thus far. It's a treat to watch.

A lean, two-game week saw the Rockets split their contests by beating the Indiana Pacers and dropping a tight one to the Denver Nuggets, which look increasingly potent as the year progresses. Fred VanVleet has played quite well this season in his return from a torn ACL, averaging 14.4 points, 5.7 assists (1.1 turnovers) and 1.1 steals while shooting 39.2% beyond the arc.

After losing to the Phoenix Suns to open the week, Denver notched a couple of mighty fine wins over the Rockets and Boston Celtics before surviving in overtime against the Chicago Bulls on Sunday. They've won seven of their past nine games following a 3-2 start.

Clutch time was rather unkind to these Blazers this past week. Portland lost both legs of a back-to-back set against the Timberwolves — one of which occurred in the final seconds — then handled business against the Milwaukee Bucks before losing to the New York Knicks at Madison Square Garden Saturday night courtesy of a Mikal Bridges game-winning three.

A forgiving stretch of games against the Utah Jazz, Los Angeles Clippers and Stephen Curry-less Golden State Warriors helped Dallas wash away the nasty taste of a brutal three-game losing streak a week and a half ago. The competition ramps up Monday night, though, when the Mavs travel to Denver to face a Nuggets team starting to find its way again.

Not much has come easy for the injury riddled Thunder this season and that was certainly the case during a 1-2 week. They endured close losses to the Atlanta Hawks and Miami Heat sandwiched around a crunch-time victory over the Pelicans on Friday night featuring a 40-point, eight-assist performance from Shai Gilgeous-Alexander.

The Suns scored their niftiest victory of the year last Monday when they defeated the Nuggets in Denver. They backed that up with two more wins to move above .500 for the first time this season and will look for a fourth consecutive victory Monday night against New Orleans.

With an 88-87 victory over the Celtics on Sunday night, led by 18 points, seven rebounds and two assists from Brandin Podziemski, Golden State extinguished its five-game losing streak — in which it lost by an average of 23.2 points — to secure its first win since Stephen Curry went down.

The drop-off from 11th to 12th in the West is severe. There are already 4.5 games separating the Warriors and Pelicans, which have lost nine of their past 10 games after a 4-4 start.

Despite going winless last week, the Clippers did receive some good news when it was announced Brandon Ingram will make his season debut Monday against the Houston Rockets. They'll hope he can invigorate their 24th-ranked offense, which is pretty starved for shot creation beyond Darius Garland.

The Jazz broke their 12-game losing streak with back-to-back wins over the Clippers and Lakers last week. The joy of success appears short-lived, though, as they ended the week with two more losses. They'll try to return to the win column Monday against the Lakers.

Speaking of ending droughts, the Kings finally secured a home win Saturday night — their first of the year — over the Boston Celtics as every starter scored 13 or more points. Watching rookie marvel Darius Acuff Jr. get to light the beam was pretty fun. Look at that smile!
