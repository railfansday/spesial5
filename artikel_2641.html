Florida Atlantic expected to fly to Tulsa, football game moved to 7:15

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Florida Atlantic's football game at Tulsa on Saturday was pushed back to a 7:15 start in the aftermath of Friday's FAA-ordered grounding of all flights in the United States.

According to a press release from the university, the Owls were scheduled to depart on Saturday morning. Kickoff originally was scheduled for 3:30 p.m.

"We are very appreciative of the American Conference and our colleagues at Tulsa for their communication and accommodations during these unique circumstances," said Josh McCowan, FAU's Vice President and Director of Athletics.

The FAA cited extraordinary circumstances and safety concerns related to an air traffic control issue when announcing the grounding. The directive ordered all departing aircraft to remain grounded until further notice and the ground stop remained in effect until around 8 p.m.

The Owls, who were waiting on buses on the tarmac when the order came down, eventually returned to campus.

Florida Atlantic was not the only Florida college football team affected by the grounding. Central Florida's home game against Arizona State was pushed back to a 5:30 p.m. start (from 3:30) due to the Sun Devils flight also being grounded on Friday. Other sports events around the nation also were affected.

The FAU-Tulsa game will air live on ESPN+ and can also be heard on Fox Sports South Florida 640 a.m.
