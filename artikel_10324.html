If you're a fan of Warhammer 40K and want to invite a SpaceMarine chapter or even a Chaos God to adorn your setup, then take a look at some of our favorite product picks for the perfect gift for yourself, a friend, or a loved one.

From Warhammer-inspired furniture that includes desks, chairs, and mouse pads, to keycaps, plushies, and even cool backpacks, there's something for every budget, and representation from a variety of the creatures and characters from the 40K universe.

Once your setup is complete, maybe even pick up the latest Warhammer 40K-inspired PC games to really jump into the fray. Below is our compiled list of some of the best Warhammer 40K furniture, collectibles, and PC games.

If you're looking for Warhammer furniture, then Secretlab is a good first stop and has a range of officially licensed Warhammer 40K furniture that includes both desks and chairs.

The Titan Evo chairs are fully decorated with Space Marine chapter livery and standards, with chair skins also available to convert existing chairs to the same designs.

How about converting one of Secretlab's Magnus Pro electric sit-stand desks into the base of your setup with a 40K-themed MAGPAD that can easily attach and change the look of your desk in an instant?

Decorate your gaming setup with Warhammer 40K-themed items. Some of our favorite spotted products include mouse pads, keycaps, Nurgle plushies, Death Korps rucksacks, and Space Marine figures.

Take a break from painting your miniatures and relax with some of the best Warhammer 40K PC games. Bring the grimdark future straight to your gaming PC and grab some great deals on the latest titles. Combat the hive mind as a Space Marine, or jump on board the upcoming Dawn of War IV RTS and command the Space Marines, Orks, Necrons, and the Adeptus Mechanicus. You can even go retro with the latest Boltgun sequel.
