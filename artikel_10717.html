Winter Tokyo City region treasure hunt location in Forza Horizon 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The winter Tokyo City region treasure hunt is one of the weekly activities in Forza Horizon 6 Series 7, "City Life." Whenever a new hunt begins, a box appears somewhere on the map and, to locate it, the only hints you have are the region's name and a picture. Completing this challenge helps you get closer to claiming the weekly and the Series cars.

If you need help locating the box, here's where to find the "City Life" treasure hunt box in Forza Horizon 6.

Update (Nov. 19): We've added the Series 7 winter Tokyo City treasure hunt location.

The Series 7 winter treasure hunt is once again making you investigate the maze-like streets of Tokyo City to find the treasure box. This "City Life" weekly challenge will reward you with 100,00 credits and three seasonal points if you smash the box.

The biggest challenge whenever there's a treasure hunt in the Tokyo City region is recognizing the elements in the photo clue. This week's photo clue shows some traffic cones, the treasure box, and a building in the background. Sadly, the building is the best clue we have to locate this treasure box.

You can find the Series 7 winter treasure box on the southwestern side of the Tokyo City region, in a small area that you can only access via a narrow street near the large park you find around this part of the city. The fastest way to reach the place is to fast travel to somewhere you have previously explored by using Square/X on controller or X on keyboard. If you haven't checked this side of the city yet, travel south of the Tokyo City Food Delivery job or head northwest from the Daikoku area.
