Solana Hits $5 Billion in RWA Value

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

As tokenized assets on Solana continue to expand, the leading blockchain network retains sustained demand for tokenized products and has just achieved a new milestone.

Over the past months, Solana has continued to position itself as a major network in tokenization as it continues to see rising activity even when the market is facing a volatile moment.

Per the latest data shared today, Solana has set a new record for its network as its RWA value crossed $5.19 billion for the first time ever on October 29.

The milestone has drawn attention across the crypto market as it represents a major increase from the levels recorded earlier in the year.

This comes as the blockchain network continues to see rapid expansion of tokenized assets, causing it to reach a new all-time high in real-world asset value.

Notably, the new milestone in the Solana RWA ecosystem has proven its efficiency in the issuance, transfer, and management of tokenized assets.

According to the chart showcased through its X handle, it appears that Solana's RWA value remained relatively low throughout 2024 before it began to surge around mid-2025.

Furthermore, the Solana RWA market continued to gain bigger momentum in 2026 when its total value rose above $2 billion around March of this year before recording sharper increases in subsequent months.

While it has seen a more significant increase within a space of seven months, it has added over $2 billion within that period.

Further data showed that the Solana RWA value had moved beyond $4 billion earlier this month, before making another major jump to $5.19 billion as of October 29.
