How to Go 2x Long Anthropic (ANT): Leverage Shares by Themes Explains ANUU, the 2x Leveraged Anthropic ETF Launching Monday

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Following Anthropic's $83-per-share IPO pricing and Nasdaq trading debut at $111.50-per-share on November 19, 2026, Leverage Shares by Themes outlines how investors can gain long exposure to Anthropic stock (ANT), and where its planned 2X Long Anthropic ETF (ANUU) fits into the investment landscape.

GREENWICH, Conn., Nov. 21, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Leverage Shares by Themes today published a step-by-step guide for investors looking to invest in Anthropic stock following the company's highly anticipated initial public offering. Anthropic, PBC priced its IPO at $83.00 per share on November 18, 2026, above its initially proposed range of $70 to $78, with the offering totaling approximately $81.3 billion. The company's Class A common stock began trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on November 19, opening at $111.50 per share. With Anthropic now publicly traded, investors can purchase shares through brokerage accounts, while those seeking leveraged exposure can explore the planned Leverage Shares 2X Long Anthropic Daily ETF (ANUU).

How to Buy Anthropic Stock (ANT) Now That It Is Trading on NasdaqFollowing its IPO on November 19, 2026, investors can buy Anthropic (ANT) shares through a brokerage account that offers Nasdaq-listed stocks.

Step 1: Find Anthropic Stock (ANT) Through Your Brokerage

Search for Anthropic under the ticker ANT in your brokerage account. Investors no longer need to request an IPO allocation, as shares are now available for purchase on the open market during regular trading hours.

Anthropic's market price may differ significantly from its $83.00 IPO price and can fluctuate throughout the trading day. Review the latest ANT share price before placing an order.

Investors can place a market order to buy at the prevailing market price or use a limit order to specify the maximum price they are willing to pay. Given the potential volatility of newly listed stocks, investors should consider the risks of each order type before trading.

Can You Get 2X Long Exposure to Anthropic Stock?Yes, once it launches: the Leverage Shares 2X Long Anthropic Daily ETF (ANUU) will seek daily investment results, before fees and expenses, corresponding to 200% (2X) of the daily performance of Anthropic common stock.

ANUU is not a way to buy into the IPO itself. The Fund does not take part in the offering and does not hold Anthropic shares. It is designed for knowledgeable investors who want to take a bullish daily view on Anthropic and who monitor their positions actively. Investors with a bearish daily view can use ANDD (2X short) or ANSS (1X short) once they launch.

ANUU, ANDD and ANSS are not yet trading. Their first trading date on Cboe is this coming Monday, November 23, 2026.
