Microsoft’s Global Outage and Fixes: What We Know

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The company described “encouraging” progress on efforts to overcome a second day of disruptions to its email, messaging and other services.

Microsoft is reporting widespread improvements to its services after a global outage took them offline on Thursday, disrupting communications and work at schools, government agencies and offices around the world. The company, headquartered in Redmond, Wash., said on Friday that it was seeing “encouraging recovery progress, with service availability and email delivery performance improving across a growing portion of affected infrastructure.”

“We remain focused on addressing residual impact and ensuring reliable service restoration,” the company added.

Here’s what to know as of Friday afternoon.

Outages started disrupting Microsoft tools and apps around noon Eastern time on Thursday and quickly spread to more than 70 countries and territories, affecting millions of users, according to Downdetector, an outage-tracking site.

Some of Microsoft’s most popular products, including the email manager Outlook, the workplace messaging and video calling app Teams and the artificial intelligence assistant Copilot, stopped working.

Microsoft said in a statement on Friday that “for now, we can confirm that there is no indication this is the result of malicious or cyber activity.” It added that the problem was “a technical issue not related to cyber or A.I.” agents.

The company said it had no indication that a Federal Aviation Administration ground stop issued on late Friday afternoon and affecting air travel nationwide was related to the outage.
