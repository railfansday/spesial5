'We show class on the way out': Terry Bradshaw rips Aaron Rodgers over Steelers exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Terry Bradshaw spent 14 seasons with the Pittsburgh Steelers, and he wasn't a fan of how Aaron Rodgers left the organization.

The Steelers benched Rodgers on Tuesday, and less than 24 hours later, he went scorched earth on head coach Mike McCarthy during a surprise appearance on The Pat McAfee Show, prompting the team to release him and igniting one of the biggest stories in the league this year.

Rodgers has already been getting a lot of criticism, with former Packers teammates James Jones and T.J. Lang both ripping the quarterback, and CBS' Nate Burleson calling him out for dodging accountability. On Fox's Thanksgiving pregame show, Bradshaw piled on to the criticism as he defended McCarthy and the Steelers' organization.

"They're a classy bunch, classy organization. Mike McCarthy and I have been friends for years. Classy man. I don't know all the details," Bradshaw said of the Rooney family and the Steelers.

Terry Bradshaw then made it clear that Aaron Rodgers' outburst didn't surprise him.

"You know what? I'm just gonna tell you how I feel right now. I'm sorry that Aaron Rodgers had to do this. I'm sorry that he had to go public with all his gripes and complaints," Bradshaw said. "Am I surprised? Absolutely not. To my opinion, he's a strange guy anyway."

The four-time Super Bowl champion argued that players should never turn on their own organization on the way out.

"And the one thing you don't do, all of us athletes up here, including you, none of us turn on our teammates. We don't turn on our coaching staff. We don't turn on our ownership. We show class on the way out," Bradshaw said. "Aaron Rodgers didn't do that."

Terry Bradshaw also took a shot at Rodgers' retirement plans. Rodgers has said that once his playing career is over, "it's Keyser Söze, and you won't see me."

"And one thing I'm reminded of, he said, 'When I'm through playing here in Pittsburgh, I'll disappear, and you'll never hear or see from me again,'" Bradshaw said. "And I hope this is one time he's telling the truth."
