Back-end starter continues to build on underrated San Diego tenure

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Welcome to our series grading every member of the San Diego Padres' 40-man roster. The roster being evaluated is current as of Oct. 18, prior to the end of the World Series. Because of that, it will include players who will be free agents once the postseason concludes. Each player will be graded on their performance in the 2026 season, including the playoffs. That performance will be weighted based on how much time the player spent with the team (availability is the best ability).

Vásquez was an under-the-radar piece in the blockbuster trade between the Padres and the New York Yankees for superstar Juan Soto. Soto and Trent Grisham were sent to the Bronx, with Michael King being the headliner of the return. At the time, Vásquez had pitched quite well in the big leagues, with a 2.87 ERA in 37 2/3 innings. He blossomed into a back-end rotation piece in San Diego, breaking out in 2025 with a 3.84 ERA over 133 2/3 innings of work.

He started off 2026 even better. Vásquez pitched to a 1.88 ERA through his first five starts and a 2.96 ERA through his first 10 outings. He regressed in June (7.84 ERA, 20.2 IP) before turning in a phenomenal August and September (2.76 ERA, 45.2 IP). Vásquez ended the season with career highs in innings pitched (140.2) and strikeouts (87) along with a career-low 1.27 WHIP.

Overall, Vásquez had an incredible 2026 campaign. He built on his breakout in 2025 and pushed himself to become a better pitcher. His underlying metrics in 2025 suggested that most of his success was luck. 2026 proved that it wasn't all coincidence. The underlying stats still aren't kind to Vásquez (5.54 xERA, .288 xBA), but he's managed to prove those stats wrong.

There is always a question of whether that success will continue. However, with Vásquez as the only viable starter on the Padres' current rotation, San Diego seems to trust the right-hander to fill out the back end of their rotation in 2027 and beyond.
