NFC Playoff Picture: Why Packers vs. Rams could have major Seahawks ramifications

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The NFL is getting Thanksgiving week started earlier than ever before, with the Los Angeles Rams hosting the Green Bay Packers on Wednesday night on Netflix. Both teams are 6-4, just like the Seattle Seahawks, who will have their own huge clash with the San Francisco 49ers to focus on this Sunday. The outcome of Wednesday's matchup is therefore very relevant to Seattle.

Here's the relevant portion of the NFC playoff picture for Seattle:

8. Seattle Seahawks (6-4, 2-1 NFCW, 2-3 NFC)

Seattle is once again tussling with the Packers for a Wild Card spot, just as it did in 2022 (successfully) and 2023 (unsuccessfully). Green Bay currently holds the No. 7 seed over the Seahawks based on the conference record tiebreaker (4-4 for the Packers vs. 2-3 for the Seahawks). Meanwhile, the Seahawks have a superior division record over the Rams (2-1 vs. 1-2).

If the Rams defeat the Packers, they would leapfrog over both Seattle and Green Bay into the last playoff spot. However, the Seahawks could overtake the Rams again by beating the 49ers, therefore assuming the No. 7 seed on the aforementioned division tiebreaker over Los Angeles.

Should the Packers prevail, the Rams would fall to 6-5 and the Seahawks would be guaranteed to stay in the eighth spot in the NFC at the end of Week 12. Either they beat the 49ers and stay behind the Packers on the conference record tiebreaker, or they lose to the 49ers and stay ahead of the Rams on division record tiebreaker.

If you go through The Athletic/New York Times Playoff Machine, Seattle currently has a 52% chance to make the playoffs. Outside of the Seahawks' own game, the game on the Week 12 schedule that has the widest playoff probability range is (by a percentage point) Packers-Rams. Folks… you're not going to like the answer for which team to "root" for.

As an aside, I assume the Chicago Bears over the Detroit Lions is preferred for the Seahawks because the Bears getting sucked into the Wild Card chase would hurt Seattle, who don't hold the head-to-head tiebreaker.

I'm not saying "Go Rams!" or anything so sacrilegious, I'm just saying that a Rams win is not the worst thing in the world for the Seahawks. Of course, a Seahawks loss would put them below 50 percent probability regardless of the Packers-Rams result, but a win puts them in the driver's seat for a Wild Card berth entering December.

And if the Rams lose and the Seahawks win this week? Well, there's a not-so-unrealistic scenario in which the Seahawks could be looking to mathematically eliminate the Rams from postseason contention on Christmas night.
