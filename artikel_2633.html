Tom Brady breaks his silence with scathing Aaron Rodgers verdict live on Fox

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Just about everyone has had their say on Aaron Rodgers' blockbuster interview on The Pat McAfee Show. Now, Tom Brady has broken his silence on the saga.

Rodgers cleared waivers on Thursday afternoon, with the Pittsburgh Steelers preparing for their game on Friday against the Denver Broncos. Whether a team will even look to bring him into their building on the back of his explosive interview remains a huge unknown.

The interview on McAfee's show has certainly split opinions, with some people welcoming the honesty from the future Hall of Famer. Brady does not seem to fall on that side of the divide, with the former quarterback sharing his thoughts on Fox Sports' coverage of the Dallas Cowboys vs Philadelphia Eagles.

No one needs reminding of what Rodgers said on The Pat McAfee Show, with his comments being the major talking point across the NFL over the last 24 hours. Brady was left far from impressed, with the 49-year-old finally speaking out on the matter.

"Yeah, and that's one thing I just think in response to that: the sanctity of an NFL locker room is a very sacred place, and we shouldn't ever lose track of that as players. It's a privilege to be in that locker room. You're fighting with your teammates, you're fighting with your coaches, and you're always acting as a team together. It shouldn't be a place for private things to become public. That's what I'll say," Brady said on Fox Sports.

This has been a running theme with a lot of former players and coaches, with Rob Gronkowski also unimpressed with Rodgers. For some, the 42-year-old has only spoken the harsh truths, but for others, he has broken the trust of an NFL locker room.

"Certainly a Hall of Fame career, and he's a tremendous player. And that's not going to detract from what he plays for, which is a fortunate lot of teammates over the years. What made him a great teammate was that he cared about his teammates," Brady added.

Given the view of Brady and some other former players and coaches, it is difficult to see how Rodgers would be accepted by another team before the end of the season. It really does look like this is how he is going to bow out of his incredible NFL career.
