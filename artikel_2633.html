Former Lakota West guard Josh Tyson officially signs with UC Bearcats

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Jerrod Calhoun officially has another local recruit coming his way for 2027-28 as former Lakota West guard Josh Tyson signed with the Cincinnati Bearcats Nov. 18 from his current school, La Lumiere in La Porte, Indiana.

The 6-foot-2, 180-pound four-star guard with the 6-foot-10 wingspan posted a photo of the ceremony on Instagram.

Tyson transferred to the Indiana school in early July after averaging 16.3 points, 3.8 assists and two steals per game while shooting nearly 63% last season for coach Kelven Moss at Lakota West. Moss was Coach of the Year in the Greater Miami Conference and Tyson was GMC Player of the Year.

Tyson shot 34.4% from the perimeter as the Firebirds went 24-2. His top game of 28 points came against his current school, La Lumiere, last season in a 72-71 defeat.

He committed to Jerrod Calhoun and the Bearcats less than two months ago, coming back to Lakota West for the ceremony.

"Being from Cincinnati, it was an easy choice," Tyson said. "It (Calhoun's system) is positionless, and it's flow. It's very similar to (Lakota West) and La Lumiere's (current school) offense. I thrive in the split-action, and it's super easy."

Current Princeton High School guard Kam Mercer, who also just signed with Calhoun and the Bearcats, attended the ceremony for Moss in late September.

"I just wanted to play with him," Mercer said. "Most people don't get the opportunity, but it's two of us at one school, and it's in our backyard. We're looking forward to turning Bearcat Nation up!"
