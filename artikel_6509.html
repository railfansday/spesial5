Apple Fries

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Here’s how to make Legoland’s legendary apple fries, complete with the cinnamon sugar coating, caramel drizzle and vanilla whipped cream dip.

Legoland’s most popular food isn’t the usual lacy funnel cake or plate of chicken tenders. Look around for the longest line, and you’ll find it at Granny’s Apple Fries, where deep-fried apple sticks are coated in cinnamon-sugar, drizzled with caramel sauce and served with vanilla whipped cream. Thankfully you don’t need to wait in line (or buy a ticket to the park) to enjoy these legendary apple fries. Here’s how to make a copycat version of Legoland’s apple fries right at home, down to the homemade whipped cream and caramel sauce drizzle.

Peel and core the apples. Using a sharp knife, carefully cut the apples into 1/2-inch sticks.

Place the cornstarch in a large bowl. Add the apple sticks, a few at a time, tossing to thoroughly coat them. Shake off the excess.

In an electric skillet or deep-fat fryer, heat the oil to 375°. Fry the apple fries, a few at a time, until they’re golden brown. Drain the oil from the apple fries by placing them on paper towels.

In a small bowl, stir together the granulated sugar and cinnamon. Immediately sprinkle the mixture over the hot fries. The sooner you sprinkle, the better the mixture sticks.

Editor’s Tip: Never, ever overcrowd your skillet or deep-fat fryer. Doing so may cause a dangerous boil-over. Always work in small batches.

In a small, chilled glass bowl, use chilled beaters to beat the heavy whipping cream until it begins to thicken. Add the confectioners’ sugar and vanilla extract, and beat until soft peaks form.

Serve the apple fries warm with the vanilla whipped cream. If desired, drizzle the apple fries with warmed caramel sauce.

Allow leftover apple fries to cool completely to room temperature, then store them in an airtight container. They can last in the fridge for up to four days.

Yes, you can freeze apple fries. Allow them to cool to room temperature, then transfer them to an airtight container with pieces of parchment or waxed paper between them. Freeze them for up to two months. Reheat them in a 400° oven until they’re crispy and warmed through.

To cut apples for apple fries, peel and core the apples, then cut them into fourths. From there, cut the apple into 1/2-inch slices lengthwise, then turn each slice on its side and cut it in half lengthwise. You should be left with apple matchsticks.

If your apple fries are soggy, the oil temperature was likely too low. When the oil isn’t hot enough, it will soak into the cornstarch and make it soggy rather than crisping the fries. It’s a common deep-frying mistake, so make sure to keep a close watch on the oil’s temperature.

Serve apple fries immediately while they’re still hot. We love to finish them with salted caramel sauce and dip them in the vanilla whipped cream. Serve your apple fries with hot apple cider or homemade hot chocolate to make your treat extra decadent and sweet.
