Here's How To Make Delish Appear First In Your Google Searches

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Stop scrolling through AI-generated recipes and get straight to the good stuff.

We know you love Delish, but these days, finding the recipes our team works so hard to create can feel harder than ever. A LOT of AI is clogging search results pages (psst... those recipes are pretty useless), and it can feel impossible to slog through it all.

Luckily, there’s an easy fix! You can select Delish as your preferred source on Google, making it easier for our recipes to show up when you’re searching for your next weeknight meal, holiday dessert, or anytime snack.

You'll need to be signed in to your Google account for this to work.

The next time you Google something food-related, you'll start seeing Delish recipes, food news, and more at the top of your search results.
