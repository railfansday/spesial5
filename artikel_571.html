Early Black Friday deal: LGs most popular OLED TV is the cheapest its been all year

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

SAVE $1,000: Ahead of Black Friday, you can get the LG 55-inch C6 OLED 4K TV for its best price to date — just $999.99 — at both Amazon and Best Buy. That's 50% off its original list price. Several other sizes are also on sale for all-time lows.

One of the best things about this time of year is that all of our favorite TV models start dropping to record-low prices. LG's most popular OLED TV for 2026, in particular, has hit an all-time low well ahead of Black Friday. If your TV has taken its last breath and you can't wait a few more weeks, the LG C6 OLED is definitely worth a look.

As of Nov. 9, both Amazon and Best Buy have the LG 55-inch C6 OLED 4K TV for only $999.99. Originally listed at $1,999.99, that's 50% off its actual price. It's currently down to the same sale price as the 48-inch model, which basically means you'll get seven extra inches of screen real estate for free (girl math, duh). The LG C6 is our favorite OLED TV for most people. OLED TVs are expensive, so any chance you have to save a significant amount of cash on one, you should take. With a 50% price drop, this deal on the LG C6 is an outstanding value.
