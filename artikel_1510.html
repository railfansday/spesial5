The 8 players Leeds United let go in the summer – & how they’re faring elsewhere

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Leeds United made some significant additions to their squad over the summer, but there was plenty of activity in the opposite direction too.

Daniel Farke oversaw a considerable clear-out at Elland Road, with several players departing permanently as Leeds looked to further consolidate their top-flight status.

But have those exits been vindicated? Here’s how the eight players Leeds let go in the summer have been performing at their new clubs.

“As our accounts reflect, we spent every penny possible, as promised, to earn promotion and keep the club in the Premier League,” Leeds chairman Paraag Marathe wrote to supporters at the end of last season.

“Our approach to the transfer market will once again be strategic and disciplined to comply with regulations while striving further to improve, and players will both arrive and depart as a result.”

That hint was met with a bit of a gulp, as it suggested that Leeds would have to let go of a big-name player in order to comply with the league’s new Squad Cost Ratio (SCR) rules.

Sure enough, Struijk was a controversial departure. An academy graduate, almost 200 appearances and the best season of his Leeds career fresh in the memory. There were sound financial reasons for selling, given his age, contract and resale value, but the decision raised some debate within the fanbase.
