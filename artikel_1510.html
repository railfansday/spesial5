Two PSG Titis in discussion to extend

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

A decisive passer against Olympique Lyonnais on Monday (2-0), Quentin Ndjantou has been playing with the PSG first team for several months. Appearing 16 times in Parisian colors, the 19-year-old attacking midfielder notably played several Champions League matches at Barcelona and against Tottenham last season, before being stopped following a hamstring injury at the end of 2025. An injury which had slowed down his progress, but which does not seem to have disrupted Luis Enrique’s plans for him. Indeed, Ndjantou – linked to PSG until 2028 – is in discussions with sports management for an extension until 2031, according to RMC Sport.

At the same time, Paris Saint-Germain wants to sign his first professional contract for young right-back David Boly. The 17-year-old played 29 minutes with the first team in the Coupe de France against Vendée Fontenay Foot (December 20, 0-4), before leaving due to injury. Present during the Parisian club’s pre-season this summer, Boly is under an aspiring contract until next June.
