'All the force!' - Mercedes open to prioritising Russell in final races

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Mercedes hoping for a one-two in the Drivers' Championship to go with dual title triumph; Kimi Antonelli already champion but George Russell currently third; watch the Las Vegas GP weekend on Sky Sports F1, with qualifying at 4am on Saturday and the race at 4am on Sunday

Toto Wolff says Mercedes could throw their weight behind George Russell over the final three races of the season to help him finish second in the Drivers' Championship.

Mercedes have already secured a world championship double this year, with Kimi Antonelli clinching the drivers' title in Mexico before the team wrapped up the constructors' crown a week later in Brazil.

But a difficult run of results for Russell since his last win at the Azerbaijan Grand Prix in September has seen the Briton slip out of the top two in the Drivers' Championship. He is third, 17 points behind Red Bull's Max Verstappen in second and only one point ahead of Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton in fourth.

Speaking after the Sao Paulo Grand Prix, Wolff was asked whether, with both titles now won, Mercedes would prioritise Russell over the final three rounds to help get him back into second place behind Antonelli.

"I haven't thought about the driver championship yet, but we absolutely want him to score the maximum and hopefully finish second," said the Mercedes team principal.

"And we need to think about what we're going to do about it.

"But it could be a possibility that we're giving it all the force."

Pre-season title favourite Russell has endured a disappointing season in which under-performance relative to Antonelli and misfortune have combined to leave him more than 100 points behind his team-mate.

But Wolff is expecting the 28-year-old to bounce back strongly in 2027.

"He will recover," said Wolff. "I have no doubt about that for next year.

"And all the very best sports people have gone through difficult periods and that's going to be a part of his continued development."

Russell's hitherto best F1 championship finish is fourth, which he achieved in 2022 and 2025.

While Mercedes have become world champions again with room to spare this year, the early-season dominance that laid the significant groundwork for their title triumphs in the first season of the sport's latest ruleset appears a long time ago now.

Antonelli's win at the United States Grand Prix last month is their only victory in the last five races amid ever-increasing competitiveness between the grid's four teams at the front of the field.

Wolff believes all three of Mercedes chief rivals will be in next year's world title fight.

"Look at the last few races, we haven't won," said Wolff.

"At the beginning of the race [in Brazil], Kimi was just storming away, it looked like this could be ours. But the competition has been getting tougher between Ferrari, McLaren, Red Bull and ourselves.

"It's four teams that can win races and will be challenging for a world championship.

"And I have no doubt that it's going to be very difficult for us next year. For me, the 2027 season has started now.

What is it we can learn from [race day in Brazil] and attack these last three races full blast, while also making sure that we have the learning for next year's power unit and next year's car?"
