Kristen Doute to Host Sports and Pop Culture Podcast ‘Run It Back’ in Partnership With PODGO (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

'The Valley' star will co-host the show with Katie DiMento.

Reality television veteran Kristen Doute is channeling her passion for sports and knowledge of pop culture in the brand new podcast Run It Back, in partnership with PODGO.

Co-hosted with Katie DiMento, the video-first show will release new episodes weekly. Run It Back will blend together the intersection of sports and pop culture, creating a space for conversations in sports, entertainment, celebrity culture and everyday life to meet.

“From the NFL, Fantasy drafts and the biggest moments in the worlds of sports to Bravo drama, pop culture’s most viral moments, relationships, style and everything happening online, the hosts bring different perspectives, genuine expertise and plenty of personality to the conversation,” a description for the podcast further reads. “The result is an entertaining, accessible and opinionated take on the stories women are talking about, without assuming that sports knowledge and pop culture knowledge have to exist in separate worlds.” Related Stories Movies Lance Broadway, Pitcher Turned 'Olympus Has Fallen' Actor, Dies at 43 Business New York Knicks Star Josh Hart Inks Two-Book Deal With HarperCollins (Exclusive)

Doute is a seasoned reality TV star, first appearing in Bravo’s acclaimed Vanderpump Rules. A founding cast member of the series, she currently stars in its spinoff The Valley, which centers on former Vanderpump Rules stars and their new friend group tackling adulthood while living in the San Fernando Valley.

She is also the co-host of the Balancing Act podcast, which was originally branded as Sex, Love…and What Else Matters and launched in 2022.

“I’ve always loved sports, and growing up a Detroit football fan, Sundays have always been about the game, the passion and, of course, having a lot to say about it,” Doute said in a statement. “I’ve also always loved a good conversation, a little friendly debate and, let’s be honest, a little bit of drama. What makes Run It Back so fun is that we get to bring together two worlds that people don’t always think belong in the same conversation. Sports and pop culture are such a huge part of what we’re talking about every day, and women have plenty to say about both. Katie brings her sports knowledge and perspective, I bring my love of pop culture and my own sports fandom, and together we’re creating a space where we can be opinionated, laugh, disagree and say what everyone else is thinking.”

DiMento, a Boston native and sports authority, added, “Sports have always been such a big part of my life and for my family, and I’ve always loved the conversations, debates and personalities that come with being a fan. What I love about Run It Back is that we’re not putting sports and pop culture into separate boxes. They’re connected, they’re constantly influencing each other, and women are just as invested in those conversations as anyone else.”

“Kristen and I bring completely different perspectives, which makes for some really fun, honest and unexpected conversations,” she added. “This show is about having a voice, knowing your stuff, being curious and never feeling like you need permission to join the conversation.”

The partnership was brokered in collaboration with Maxx Lepselter of Maxx Mgmt, who represents the talent; Maxx Mgmt will identify and develop brand collaborations, sponsorships and marketing partnerships for Run It Back.

“We’re excited to have played a role in bringing this partnership together and helping Kristen and Katie launch Run It Back with PODGO,” Lepselter said in a statement. “Representing talent is about more than negotiating deals. It’s about identifying opportunities that align with their personalities, passions and long-term goals. This podcast brings together two dynamic voices with a unique perspective on sports and pop culture, and we see tremendous potential to build on that through strategic brand collaborations, sponsorships and marketing partnerships that extend well beyond the podcast itself.”

PODGO will handle distribution across major podcast platforms and manage advertising representation and sponsorship opportunities for the series.

“There’s such a natural connection between sports and pop culture, and women are already part of both conversations,” said Tristan Capes-Davis, founder of PODGO. “Kristen and Katie bring two distinct audiences and perspectives together in a way that feels authentic, entertaining and highly relevant. Kristen has an established relationship with a passionate reality-TV and pop-culture audience, while Katie brings the sports authority that gives the show a real point of difference. Run It Back has the personality, format and audience appeal to build something special, and PODGO is excited to help bring it to audiences everywhere.”

Run It Back will release new episodes weekly. Full video episodes will be available on YouTube and video podcast distribution across Spotify and Apple Podcasts, while short-form content and highlights will be distributed across social media at @runitbackwithKD across platforms.
