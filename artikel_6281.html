11 Best Airbnbs Near SoFi Stadium to Make the Most of Your Super Bowl Weekend

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

With its translucent roof, cutting-edge technology, and $5.5 billion price tag, SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles ranks among the most ambitious sports venues in the world. Since opening in 2020, it has made headlines for its indoor-outdoor design and massive dual-sided video board suspended above the field. It's played host to some spectacular events, from Super Bowl LVI in 2022, when the Los Angeles Rams won on their home turf, to FIFA World Cup matches in 2026. As the official host of the Super Bowl LXI in 2027, SoFi continues to attract fans from around the world. But getting there—and finding the right Airbnb to stay at nearby—can be quite the challenge.

LA traffic is notorious for turning a short trip into an hours-long commute, especially during a big event like the Super Bowl. Add expensive rideshares and post-event congestion, and transportation to SoFi Stadium can quickly become a headache. The solution? The Airbnbs below, which let you stay closer to the action to make the most of your trip.

Every property on this list is less than a 25-minute drive from the stadium (sans traffic), and some are just steps away, so you can skip the transportation stress and spend more time enjoying the big game. Read on for the best Airbnbs near SoFi Stadium, whether you’re headed there for the Super Bowl, the next buzzy concert, or to take a behind-the-scenes stadium tour.
