Harry Kane ends England’s 25-year wait for Ballon d’Or winner

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

“Wow, wow, wow,” Harry Kane said as he took to the stage at the London Palladium after winning the 2026 Ballon d’Or. Wow indeed. “I am speechless,” Kane added before launching into a heartfelt speech, the wobble in his voice showing just how much this meant to him.

In truth it would have been a travesty had the 33-year-old England captain – and Bayern Munich striker – not won with his family, friends and coaches all watching from the stalls at this famous old theatre. “All those years of sacrifice,” he said of his parents, Pat and Kim, his voice faltering more than ever at that moment. It was from the heart. And faltering again as he spoke about his wife and “best friend” Katie Goodland. “This is a trophy we all celebrate,” he said, talking to his children back in Bavaria. “I bring a few others [trophies] home but this one is a special one.”

Kane’s speech was as outstanding as his football. “Lastly for me it is the journey,” he said. “Learn through resilience, determination and self-belief. My journey was not easy and I had to fight my way to be the very best right now. I hope that can give you inspiration.”

So much for the “one-season wonder”. So much for the doubters who poured scorn on him. So much for those who believed he would never make it after loan spells at Leyton Orient, Millwall, Norwich City and Leicester City. With varying degrees of success.

Kate Scott, the presenter, perfectly detected the emotion and said: “You held it together. You got through it.” It showed how much it meant to Kane, who simply and quietly replied: “Yes”.

What a winner. What a player. What a role model. The award given in the city where he has scored most of his goals. But he would not have been in the running for this accolade had he not been brave enough to quit Tottenham Hotspur after 19 years and move to Germany.

Kane was the favourite. But, despite its prestige, the Ballon d’Or process is an odd one. One journalist from each of the top-100-ranked Fifa countries is given a vote, with the organisers, France Football, laying out the criteria: individual performance, collective performance and fair play. In that order. They often go for the biggest name. Not the best.

Kane’s numbers are incredible. They speak for themselves. Those numbers show he deserved to win the prize: 65 games for club and country and 73 goals. Plus eight assists (for the judging period – August 3, 2025 to July 19, 2026). That is 61 goals for Bayern and 12 for England. That is a goal less than every 72 minutes. That is beyond elite. That is world class and legendary.

Bayern Munich and England's Ballon d'Or winner doing what he does best 🎯 https://t.co/yWNburqkpg

For his club, that is 19 goals more than any other player in the top five European leagues. And the most since Cristiano Ronaldo scored 61 for Real Madrid in 2014-15. Kane won last season’s Golden Shoe by nine goals from Erling Haaland. He reached his century of Bundesliga goals last month in just 98 games, destroying Dieter Müller’s record set 49 years ago – he took 129 matches.

Kane became one of only two players in history (the other is Ronaldo) to score 20-plus goals across World Cups and European Championships and was the only one to score in two major club cup finals, including a hat-trick. Neither Kylian Mbappé nor Lamine Yamal, his closest rivals, scored in any finals.

Both gave interviews declaring why they should win the Ballon d’Or. Kane? “I don’t like talking about myself,” he said. “The 73 goals speak for themselves.” They do.

Those are the facts, but there is also the football and, yes, the conduct. Kane has scored some superb goals during that period, not least for England. Take his two goals against the Democratic Republic of Congo at the World Cup that saved his team and Thomas Tuchel from an embarrassing early exit.

The Prince of Wales, who has been FA president since 2006, congratulated Kane on “a fantastic achievement”.

“A brilliant captain, a remarkable player and someone who has given football fans across the world so many unforgettable moments,” Prince William wrote on Instagram.

Kane, the first English player since Michael Owen in 2001 to win the award, is also the ultimate team player – so fulfilling that “collective performance” criteria – and a captain who leads by example on and off the pitch. Fair play? He was last booked in January and was cautioned just three times in 68 games and not at all in any of his 13 appearances this season.

And he works hard, so very hard. Kane was in the top 10 for total distance covered at the World Cup, despite playing a game fewer than most of the others on the list. And he is often seen winning as many defensive headers in his own penalty area as attacking ones in the opposition’s.

That work extends to off the pitch, to try to maximise his ability, to do everything to be the best version of himself and to be the best. Which he has now been recognised as. And it stems from his winning mentality. Some scoffed when Kane walked into England’s press conference before the 2018 World Cup and said he could be top-scorer – but he proved them wrong.

They probably also scoffed at the notion that he would one day be declared the best player in the world and win the Ballon d’Or. But he has done all that. It confirms England’s all-time cap holder (for now jointly with Peter Shilton) and goalscorer as one of the greats. And we are privileged to witness that greatness. Wow, what a night.

By Christian FalkGermany do not have a single player among the 30 in the running to win the Ballon d’Or in London on Monday night – but Bayern Munich do have England captain Harry Kane.

For the 35-time German champions, the award is perhaps even more important than it is for the peerless striker himself. The Bayern hierarchy are planning to travel to the Palladium Theatre en masse to support Kane.

Kane is not only Bayern’s top striker, he is their international figurehead and, internally, their poster-boy professional. Club patron and honorary president Uli Hoeness described the England captain as the best signing in the club’s history. Hoeness even went so far as to say he loves Kane, and he is not alone in this.

There's only one Harry Kane. https://t.co/NfZEFmjaUN

Hoeness is one of many in the club’s leadership who have been endlessly promoting Kane to help the 33-year-old fulfil his lifelong dream of winning the trophy. Bayern officials have been campaigning on his behalf for weeks.

First up was manager Vincent Kompany, who remains in hospital for tests after collapsing at Bayern’s training ground on Friday. Kompany said: “If he can now crown his career or his season with the Ballon d’Or, that would be fitting given the way he plays for the team.”

The plaudits kept coming. Whether it was sporting director Max Eberl (“He has more than earned the Ballon d’Or. Anyone who loves football loves characters like Harry Kane”), chief executive Jan-Christian Dreesen (“One thing is clear – anyone talking about this year’s greatest individual honours cannot overlook Harry Kane”) or president Herbert Hainer (“Harry Kane embodies the idea that sporting greatness is incomplete without greatness of character. He would be a worthy World Footballer of the Year”), everyone at the club is fighting for Kane.

The official Ballon d’Or website has announced Harry Kane, Kylian Mbappe and Lamine Yamal as winners of the main prize in an embarrassing blunder for the organisation.

Three separate articles were published before quickly being taken down.

The headline on Kane’s article read: ‘Harry Kane wins 2026 Ballon d’Or: Here are his best and most meaningful strikes from a 61-goal season’.

The 70th Ballon d’Or ceremony takes place at the London Palladium as England and Bayern Munich striker Harry Kane aims to become the first Englishman in more than 20 years to win the award. Michael Owen was the last Englishman to win the award back in 2001.

The choice of London as the host city pays tribute to the award’s inaugural winner, England’s Stanley Matthews, who won it in 1956. This also marks the first time the ceremony will be organised outside France. Kane, who scored over 70 goals for club and country in the relevant year, is among the favourites for this year’s award alongside the likes of Barcelona and Spain’s Lamine Yamal and Real Madrid and France’s Kylian Mbappe.

Kane won the Bundesliga with Bayern Munich whereas Yamal won both La Liga and the World Cup. Yamal won the Kopa Trophy at last year’s ceremony as the best men’s young player.

Last year’s winners were Spain and Barcelona midfielder Aitana Bonmati and France and Barcelona forward Ousmane Dembele. Dembele is aiming to become just the 11th man to win the top prize at least twice, although it would appear to be very unlikely that the Frenchman will be winning the award this evening.

Selected international journalists cast their votes based on the performances of players throughout the season. The panel consists of one representative per country from the top 100 nations in the Fifa men’s world ranking and the top 50 in the women’s world ranking. Each voter ranks their top 10 candidates, with points distributed on a descending scale. They vote based on three factors: individual performance, team achievements, and class combined with fair play.

In total, 13 trophies will be presented across the men’s and women’s game, including the two man Ballon d’Or awards, the best young players and best teams. The awards take into account achievements from August 3, 2025, to July 19, 2026 (August 16 for the women).
