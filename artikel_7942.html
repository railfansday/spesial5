A garage can become the default home for everything that doesn’t fit elsewhere. Yet even when clutter crowds the floor, your walls and ceiling may be going underused. Finding a logical home for everything is the first step toward reclaiming your space.

This list of garage organization solutions covers everything from a mobile workbench and overhead rack to wall-mounted storage for lumber, bikes, and brooms. It mixes budget-friendly organizers with a few heavy-duty upgrades, and it includes several products from BobVila.com’s best-of guides. Whatever is causing the biggest clutter problem, there’s a fix here to match.
