Premier League table predicted after Leeds United, Crystal Palace & Sunderland blows and Hull City draw

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Opta have forecasted Premier League finishes for Leeds United, Hull City, Sunderland, Crystal Palace and more

It was another busy weekend of action in the Premier League.

Leeds United visited AFC Bournemouth, suffering their second league defeat of the season as goals from Evanilson and Amine Adli put the Whites to the sword.

Hull City played out a 2-2 draw with fellow newly-promoted side Ipswich Town, with Tim Iroegbunam and Oli McBurnie getting on the scoresheet.

Away from Yorkshire, Brentford saw off Nottingham Forest and Coventry City claimed three points at Sunderland’s expense. Tottenham Hotspur were 2-0 winners over Crystal Palace, Manchester City lost to Brighton & Hove Albion and Aston Villa were held to a draw by Fulham.

Chelsea came out on top against Manchester United, Arsenal edged past Liverpool and Everton defeated Newcastle United to round off the weekend.
