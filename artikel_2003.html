Crystal Palace 'surprised' by Thomas Frank demands as agreement reached

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Crystal Palace have been surprised by Thomas Frank's demands despite an agreement now having been reached in principle.

The former Tottenham and Brentford manager quickly emerged as the leading target to replace Pierre Sage at Selhurst Park, following the Frenchman's dismissal last week.

However, talks have gone on for longer than the club had hoped, with less than a week now before their next Premier League game away from home against Coventry City.

This comes amid reports that the boss would have to take a pay cut from his Tottenham wages, with potential bonuses also having led to delays in reaching an agreement.

Having now seemingly overcome those concerns, Frank looks set to be named as the next Crystal Palace manager, but at a later date than many had initially hoped.

Football Insider revealed on 13 November that Crystal Palace were closing in on an agreement with Frank to become their next manager after talks in recent days.

That agreement has now been reached in principle, with the boss having always been seen as the first-choice target despite other options on their radar.

Frank now looks set to take over at Selhurst Park in the coming days, but the delays have raised concerns about Palace's decision-making and the time taken to reach an agreement.

"I'm told the delays around Frank's appointment are to do with the money he's asking for," Brown told Football Insider.

"It seems Crystal Palace have been surprised by his demands, the wage he wants and the bonuses that would be included, and have wanted to negotiate over it.

"To be honest, it's a ridiculous situation for them to be in, because they should have known his demands before they sacked Pierre Sage.

"They shouldn't be at a stage where, a week after they decided to change their manager, they're still haggling over money and so they're still without a manager in charge.

"The work should already have been done to figure these things out, and eventually an agreement has been reached, but it should not have taken anywhere near this long.

"Frank has always been the one Palace wanted, so the money shouldn't really have been a problem, they were always going to pay it.

"But it's led to these delays which are only going to hurt them, because these are days when they could've had him in and working with the players, but they've been wasted."

The financial incentives behind Frank's appointment have not been the only reason for the delays, as negotiations are ongoing despite an agreement with the boss.

Football Insider revealed on 15 November that talks are ongoing to bring Frank's coaching staff to the club, and are taking longer than expected, sparking concerns.

Crystal Palace fans will remember the summer of 2021, when Nuno Espirito Santo looked set to take over, before the club pulled the plug due to his overinflated backroom team.

This situation is believed to be different, with optimism on both sides that a deal will be agreed before too long, but concerns are starting to creep in at Selhurst Park.
