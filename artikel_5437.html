Crypto futures are implying Anthropic is worth more than its IPO price range

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Contracts tied to Anthropic's Nasdaq debut on Thursday imply about $1.7 trillion, against $1.4 trillion at the top of the range

Anthropic is being priced at roughly $1.7 trillion in crypto futures markets, exceeding the $1.4 trillion value tied to the high end of its planned IPO range, according to Bloomberg.

Anthropic is expected to set its IPO price higher than the $70 to $78 a share it is currently marketing, according to CNBC, citing unnamed sources. CNBC also said the company plans to list on the Nasdaq $NDAQ under the ticker ANT.

The contracts are called perpetual futures since they have no expiration date. They give traders a way to speculate on Anthropic's share price after listing without owning any shares, according to Bloomberg. Holding them gives a trader no stake in the company and no vote in its affairs.

Trade.xyz, a derivatives issuer built on the Hyperliquid blockchain, has seen its contracts drop by as much as 21% since Nov. 4, reaching about $97 in Asia trading on Wednesday. That puts the price about 24% higher than the $78 ceiling of the proposed range, according to Bloomberg.

The AI trade has been under strain since voters went to the polls in the U.S. midterms, as fears of a bubble and outsized spending pressure shares, according to Bloomberg. Nvidia $NVDA stock has also been hit by the exit of CEO Jensen Huang.

"The perps market is still positioned for a pop, just a smaller one than it was pricing a week ago," said Kelly Ye, head of research at Talos, according to Bloomberg.

The futures have not always matched debut prices. SpaceX stock began trading in June at $150, which topped its $135 offer price yet fell short of the $176 its futures had reached earlier the same morning, according to Bloomberg. Futures for Cerebras Systems were priced no more than 3% away from its opening-day price one hour ahead of the first trades. "Pre-IPO perps aren't a crystal ball," Ye said.

Anthropic perpetuals on Binance and Hyperliquid had about $422 million in open interest two days ahead of the anticipated debut, versus $251 million for SpaceX contracts at a similar point, according to Bloomberg. It said 20 wallets hold more than two-thirds of the positions in the Trade.xyz contract, and each $1 million in net purchases or sales shifts that market by about 2%.

Nvidia announced last week that Huang had resigned as president, CEO and board member days before the offering. Nvidia has committed about $10 billion to buy Anthropic stock in a $26 billion private placement set to close alongside the IPO.

Anthropic’s offering could bring in as much as $64.4 billion at the top of the range, split between the company and its shareholders, according to Bloomberg. Goldman Sachs $GS and Morgan Stanley $MS are leading the offering, and Altimeter Capital plans to commit $5 billion to it.
