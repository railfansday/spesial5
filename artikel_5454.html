United Is Banning AI Agents From Booking Flights — But Here’s What They Can Still Do

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

You're viewing the US website. Looking for the Site edition?

Anyone hoping to have an AI agent book a holiday flight on United will have to do it the old-fashioned way.

United says AI agents haven’t driven many bookings so far, so the ban is mostly about where air travel is headed. It also sets United apart from Delta and American, which have both signaled that agents can book flights under certain circumstances.

Agents such as Muse and Dots can still browse United’s website to compare fares and lay out options for users to choose from. They just can’t complete the purchase.
