Zcash Price Fate: ZEC Crypto to Bounce or Fall Further From Critical $1,000 Support?

The breakdown puts a major psychological level in view, with traders watching if bulls can reclaim $1,000 before another wave of selling hits. Zcash is also approaching its 0.5 Fibonacci retracement level, an area that could attract buyers looking for a potential relief rally.

Strangely enough, the decline comes despite Grayscale committing $1 million to support the Zcash ecosystem. Its investment campaign and development funding have failed to reverse the selling pressure. A technical bounce will depend heavily on Bitcoin stabilizing after its own recent weakness. If BTC continues falling, ZEC could struggle to find enough demand to establish a lasting bottom.

Zcash price latest decline has erased much of its September rally, when ZEC climbed toward $1,700 before momentum started fading. The coin fell 13% to 15% during the latest major sell-off, briefly touching $960. Its weekly losses have now exceeded 26%, putting it among the weaker performers in the large-cap market.

Not just that, Grayscale has also launched a nationwide Zcash marketing campaign, starting with a front-page advertising wrap and a full-page ad inside The Wall Street Journal promoting its Zcash ETF (ZCSH).

The first $400,000 has already been distributed among four organizations, including the Zcash Foundation, Shielded Labs, Project Tachyon, and Pretty Good Policy for Zcash. Grayscale also intends to reinvest 100% of its Zcash ETF sponsor fees into network development, marketing, and education for up to one year.

Grayscale’s Zcash ETF, trading under the ticker ZCSH, recorded $46.25 million in outflows during October. November withdrawals have added further pressure, with nearly $30 million leaving the fund through November 11.

Derivatives traders have also faced significant losses. ZEC liquidations reached approximately $18 million over 24 hours, with leveraged long positions accounting for around 72% of the total.

Technically, Zcash is approaching an important decision point after losing the $1,000 psychological level. The 0.5 Fibonacci retracement is now attracting attention as a potential area for buyers to defend. This midpoint could decide whether a correction is becoming exhausted or another leg lower is playing out.

A successful defense could give ZEC room for a short-term recovery, particularly if Bitcoin manages to stabilize and restore confidence across the altcoin market. The first challenge is reclaiming $1,000. ZEC needs to turn that former support into resistance-turned-support again before bulls can build a convincing recovery case.

A sustained move above $1,000 could open the way toward $1,100, followed by the $1,200 region. The latter also aligns with an important technical recovery area after the recent breakdown.

But the bearish scenario remains firmly on the table. If ZEC loses its 0.5 Fibonacci support, traders could start looking toward $900 and potentially the $800 region. That would extend the correction and give September’s rally further room to unwind.

The Relative Strength Index is also approaching oversold territory, with recent readings near 34. This shows that selling momentum has become stretched, although it does not guarantee an immediate reversal.

$1,000 remains the level bulls need to reclaim, while the 0.5 Fibonacci retracement could determine whether the latest correction is nearing exhaustion or has another painful chapter ahead.
