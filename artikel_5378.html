TransPerfect San Juan Bowl Announces Partnership with CBS Sports

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

First-Ever NCAA Football Game in Puerto Rico Will Air on CBS Sports Network December 16

NEW YORK and SAN JUAN, Puerto Rico, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- TransPerfect, the title sponsor and owner of the inaugural TransPerfect San Juan Bowl, today announced that the game will be televised nationally on CBS Sports Network and streamed on the CBS Sports app. Scheduled for Wednesday, December 16, 2026, at Hiram Bithorn Stadium in San Juan, the historic matchup will mark the first NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision (FBS) bowl game played in Puerto Rico.

The game is scheduled to feature teams from the Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC) and the Sun Belt Conference, with kickoff at 7:30 PM ET.

TransPerfect President and Co-CEO Phil Shawe stated, "We're thrilled to partner with CBS Sports to air the inaugural TransPerfect San Juan Bowl. The importance of bringing American football to the Latino community is underscored by this past weekend's NFL game in Mexico Cityâand we are proud to play a small role in that endeavor. Further, we expect to showcase Puerto Rico's unique appeal with cultural events surrounding the game, including musical performances by well-known local artists."

The TransPerfect San Juan Bowl will bring NCAA college football to Puerto Rico for the first time. Hiram Bithorn Stadium has previously hosted major sporting events, including the World Baseball Classic and Major League Baseball games.

Tickets are available now through Ticketera. Participating teams will be announced following college football's Selection Sunday on December 6, 2026. For information and updates, visit www.sanjuanbowl.com and follow @sanjuanbowl on Instagram, Facebook, and X.

About the TransPerfect San Juan BowlThe TransPerfect San Juan Bowl is a postseason NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision (FBS) game featuring teams from the Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC) and the Sun Belt Conference. Debuting December 16, 2026, at Hiram Bithorn Stadium in San Juan, Puerto Rico, it brings FBS postseason football to the island for the first time. For more information, visit www.sanjuanbowl.com.
