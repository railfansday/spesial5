After 15 years explaining benefits, Jellyvision now runs them: ALEX Home benefits administration is live

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Built by industry veterans and shaped by brokers who've trusted ALEX for 15 years, Jellyvision's benefits administration software now finishes the conversation ALEX starts with millions of employees each open enrollment.

CHICAGO, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Jellyvision, the company behind ALEX, is now a benefits administration company. Normally, this is where we'd say, "We're thrilled to announce this groundbreaking new solution." But it's open enrollment, and HR teams have enough to deal with.

So here's the short version: ALEX Home benefits administration is live, employees are finishing early, and HR is spending less time chasing them. Imagine that.

At one of the first organizations to use it, nearly one in three employees (30%) enrolled within 48 hours. The last-minute scramble was cut by more than half: employees enrolling on the final day fell from 20% last year to 8% this year.

The move came from two places. For 15 years, ALEX has guided millions through benefits decisions, and brokers kept asking to finish the conversation: from choosing a plan to enrolling, all in one place. The team behind ALEX Home also brought decades of industry experience fixing what ben admin gets wrong. They knew they could do better.

Naturally, the team put its own ben admin to the test. For CEO Amanda Lannert, that meant breaking a long-held habit.

"I've spent 25 years at a company that helps millions of people understand their benefits, and I've always enrolled in mine on the last possible day," said Lannert. "Understanding was never my problem. Enrolling was. This year, I finished on day one, and I didn't furrow a single brow."

"Turns out, I was experiencing exactly what our brokers had been telling us for years: employees can understand their benefits perfectly but still get tripped up by the system they enroll in," Lannert added. "Fixing half the problem wasn't good enough anymore."

We're done normalizing mediocre benefits software.

Benefits administration software sits between people and healthcare coverage. Yet only 18% of organizations surveyed by Sapient Insights say their benefits applications always meet their business needs.

Employees wrestle with confusing choices. HR teams chase people who think they've enrolled but haven't. Brokers moonlight as tech support. And when something breaks, it's a newborn waiting for coverage or a paycheck that's suddenly wrong.

Anyone can enroll people. That's not enough.

ALEX helps employees make sense of decisions affecting their health, finances, and families, and 85% say they understand their benefits better as a result.

Now ALEX Home puts guidance and enrollment in one place, with year-round support. Implementation doesn't have to take months, either: customers have gone live in as few as 14 days, with Jellyvision handling configuration. Renewing for the next plan year can take as little as a day.

MyALEXHealth brings the same plain-language approach to Medicare, helping employees approaching 65 learn their options before a costly mistake follows them into retirement.

Through it all, ALEX's guidance has never been for sale. No commissions. No pay-to-play. Just honest support, even when the answer is, "You don't need that."

Open enrollment will never be fun. But it can finally be uneventful, in the best possible way.
