Milan priority is to lock Rabiot down to new improved contract

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Milan patron Gerry Cardinale held a meeting at the club headquarters today to discuss the transfer strategy, but the priority is to lock down Adrien Rabiot to a new and improved contract.

The RedBird chief has been very hands-on with the Serie A side this season, having vented his frustration at missing out on a Champions League spot last term by cleaning house.

According to Sky Sport Italia, Cardinale was present at the club headquarters this afternoon for a series of meetings with various directors, as they begin to set out the plans for January.

The main priority, however, is to lock down the talent in the squad right now, and that means ensuring France international Rabiot signs a new contract.

The 31-year-old is already tied to the San Siro giants until June 2028, with an option to extend, but that is considered insufficient for his importance to Ruben Amorim’s team.

Rabiot contributed five goals and as many assists in 13 competitive appearances for Milan so far this season, and his absence was sorely felt whenever there was rotation to let him rest.

It is believed the new contract will run to June 2030, with an improved salary of €5m per season plus performance-related bonuses, compared to his current €3.5m.

Meanwhile, the Rossoneri are also ready to announce the arrival of new Head of Football Markus Krosche over the next few days, once the final details have been resolved.
