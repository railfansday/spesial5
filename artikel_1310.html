Care Leavers Tour Hill Dickinson Stadium

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Hill Dickinson Stadium welcomed some of Liverpool’s care experienced young people for an exclusive behind-the-scenes tour to mark National Care Leavers Month.

A group of 25 care leavers, who have either left or are preparing to leave local authority care, were given the opportunity to explore the Club’s stunning waterfront home.

Observed every November, National Care Leavers Month is a UK-wide campaign that celebrates care experienced young people, amplifies their voices and promotes positive change in policy and practice.

The youngsters, many of whom were visiting a football stadium for the first time, enjoyed an informative tour of the state-of-the-art venue, including the impressive home dressing room, the Media Centre, pitchside areas and learning about the heritage of the historic site.

Jenny Glennard, Corporate Director of Children & Young People’s Services at Liverpool City Council, said: "On behalf of Liverpool Children's Social Care, I would like to again extend my gratitude to Everton for providing our care experienced young people with the fantastic opportunity to visit Hill Dickinson Stadium as part of National Care Leavers Month.

"To be given a personal group tour was an experience and memory they will never forget. All of our young people have expressed how much they enjoyed the day and how impressed they were with the stadium, as well as the staff who made them feel so welcome.

"We value our relationship with Everton and look forward to continuing to work with the Club in the future."

Liverpool City Council's Leaving Care Services provide a wide range of support for young people transitioning from local authority care.

Using a person-centred approach to independent living skills, planning and assessment, the service aims to improve outcomes across education, training, employment, health and accommodation.
