Arteta's Big Mistake Exposed as Arsenal Draw 2-2 vs Newcastle

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Arsenal saw their chance to extend their lead at the top of the Premier League table fall short as they were held to a 2-2 draw with Newcastle at St James' Park on Saturday night.

The Gunners took the lead in the first half thanks to Eberechi Eze's powerful strike, before goals from Harvey Barnes and Malick Thiaw gave the hosts the lead in the second half, as they turned in a resurgent performance. But Noni Madueke equalised late on to get the north London club a vital point, after Man City drew with Fulham earlier in the day.

But the performance of the reigning champions in the second half in particular was a concern for manager Mikel Arteta, who saw the intensity drop off, and chances become hard to come by.

"No, my feeling is that we have lost two points today. And I say we because we were exceptional in the first half, from the beginning to the end. The intensity, the level of aggression, the intensity that we put on the defensive aspects, the quality that we showed.

"And right at the beginning [of the second half], we aren’t at the races. And then we start to lose duels, we start to lose second balls, we give the ball away and have really poor circulation goals against their press and their waves. And when that happens, you give them belief. One thing is to give them belief, and another thing is to give them a goal. And then we both give them the lead first, and then we give them a goal from a throw, and that is obviously very, very, very disappointing"

The boss opted for a strong starting lineup, with William Saliba remaining on the bench despite being fit again, while Bruno Guimaraes started in midfield against his old club alongside Declan Rice and Martin Odegaard.

But the Brazilian didn't have the greatest game at his old stomping ground, and was substituted in the 70th minute and replaced by Myles Lewis-Skelly. Guimaraes has started nine games for Arsenal since joining in a £70m deal from the Magpies, and this was the seventh time he was substituted in the 70th minute or earlier. His performance was relatively poor too, with The Sun handing him a 5/10 rating.

After winning the Premier League last season, Arteta prioritised strengthening his squad in the summer to retain the title for the first time in the club's history. Guimaraes joined alongside Ezri Konsa and Christos Tzolis, while Gabriel Martinelli and Leandro Trossard departed.

But with the way the season has gone so far, there is a fair argument to say that the club could have spent their money better, and that would start with not signing the Brazil international.

On paper, there is no denying that Guimaraes is an excellent signing. He adds steel and quality to midfield, and makes Arsenal stronger. But it's also fair to say that the Gunners needed a striker far more than they needed midfield reinforcements.

Rice is the star of the show for the north London club and starts any game he is fit for, while Martin Zubimendi, Myles Lewis-Skelly, and Mikel Merino were able to help Arsenal reach the Champions League final last season.

But a striker was and is a huge miss for this Arsenal side. Viktor Gyokeres has fallen out of favour despite being the top scorer last season, while Kai Havertz just doesn't have the killer edge and isn't reliable enough in front of goal to be a first-choice striker for a club with the aspirations of Arsenal.

Considering moves for Morgan Rogers, Vini Jr, and Julian Alvarez all fell by the wayside in the summer, the extra £70m transfer fee plus the wages being spent on Bruno could have gone a long way towards bringing in a truly elite forward to take the team to the next level.

Arsenal's midfield options are more than enough to win the vast majority of games they play even without Guimaraes, and by bringing a world-class striker in instead, they would have had the perfect chance to achieve world domination and become the top team around.

Instead, they continue to struggle to score goals and create enough to really put teams to the sword, and have splashed a huge chunk of any budget on a player they didn't really need.

The Gunners were held to a shock draw at St James' Park.
