He Played With Jamie Carragher and Emile Heskey, But is Now in Jail After £15m Scam

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Sadly, there are many examples of talented footballers who end up in prison. Success in football is no guarantee, but even those who make it at a high level don't always stay on the right path.

El-Hadji Diouf, Joey Barton and Duncan Ferguson are just some of the ex-Premier League stars who ended up behind bars for various reasons. Even past Ballon d'Or winner Ronaldinho spent time in prison after using a fake passport back in March 2020 – former Barcelona teammate Lionel Messi supposedly helped bail the Brazilian out.

Richard Rufus is a lesser-known footballer who was sent to prison and remains in jail to this day.

For a number of years, Richard Rufus was a mainstay in the Charlton Athletic defence. He came through the club's youth system and was just 19 when he made his debut in 1994. One season later, he was named in the PFA Team of the Year for the Football League First Division (now Championship).

The talented centre-back was also capped for England at the U21 level, sharing game time with future Three Lions senior stars such as Emile Heskey and Jamie Carragher. Rufus, though, never got a first-team call-up, and spent his entire career with Charlton.

He won promotion to the Premier League on two occasions under Alan Curbishley. With a total of 254 games for the Addicks, the Englishman was even voted by fans as the club's greatest defender in 2005, just one year after he retired.

In the years since hanging up his boots, however, Rufus has unfortunately taken a dark path away from football and into crime.

While Olise was attending the 2026 Ballon d’Or ceremony, his old teammate was in court.

In December 2013, Rufus was declared bankrupt after a £6m failed investment, which cost his church £5m. But the issues did not stop there.

Just two years later, he was branded a 'fraudster' by a specialist civil court judge following an £8m loss to investors relating to a Ponzi scheme. Then in 2023, a press release from the City of London Police confirmed that the former footballer had been jailed for over seven years for his £15m investment scam:

"A former footballer who convinced up to 100 friends and members of his family to invest a total of more than £15 million in a foreign currency exchange scheme has been jailed for seven and a half years."

Rufus was found guilty of four counts of fraud by false representation, money laundering and one count of carrying out a regulated activity without authorisation. Detective Constable Claire Sandford-Day, from the fraud operations team at the City of London Police, said:

“Rufus deliberately deceived those who were close to him and those who trusted him to scam them out of substantial sums of money.

“He used his status as a former footballer to make it appear that he was leading a life of wealth as a result of his former career and investments, when the reality was, he was not making profits from his trading activities and was using the money from his victims to pay for his lifestyle.”

It was said that of the £15m paid to accounts controlled by Rufus, investors received back a total of around just £7.6 million. He was found to have used the money to fund his own lifestyle, spending almost £300,000 on shopping, car finance, travel and restaurants

As a result of the scam, friendships and loyalties between friends were said to be 'shattered', with many suffering from huge financial and mental health difficulties as a result of the scheme.
