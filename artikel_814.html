SLOMW’s Jen Affleck Calls Out Zac Affleck’s Behavior in Unseen Videos

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Secret Lives of Mormon Wives’ Jen Affleck shared never-before-seen videos with Zac Affleck’s behavior on display during their marriage in the hours after he addressed their divorce.

In the wake of news that the Secret Lives of Mormon Wives star filed for divorce from her husband Zac Affleck after seven years of marriage—and after he shared a five-minute video responding to their split—Jen is sharing a look at past moments from their relationship.

In the first never-before-seen video, Jen wrote over the clip, “POV: your [sic] two weeks postpartum and trying to become an influencer.”

While standing in a kitchen in her bathrobe, an off-camera Zac tells Jen, “I hope you’re not making a TikTok looking like that. Your boobs are literally—your tits are sagging all over.”

As Jen laughs in response, he adds, “You don’t want to post that, baby, I’ll tell you that right now.”

She then asks her husband, “Am I embarrassing myself?” To which Zac responds, “A little bit.”

Jen—who shares three kids with Zac and is currently pregnant with their fourth baby—captioned the TikTok, “He was never my biggest supporter.”

In a second video, filmed in their bathroom, Zac asks Jen why she left a towel on the ground, and she notes it was dirty.

“Well, what am I supposed to shower with?” he asks his wife. “Is there no other towels?”

As he walks out of the bathroom, she responds, “I’ve been doing all your laundry for the past—forever. If I got a little bit more help, maybe it’d be nice. Maybe we’d have some towels.”

As she chuckles into the mirror, Zac glares at her from the doorway before moving out of frame.

The latest videos from Jen were posted shortly after Zac shared a video addressing his and Jen’s split. In it, he alleges that while she first brought up parting ways a year ago, and he’d encouraged her to give their marriage another shot, he learned of her divorce filing via TMZ.

"I don't want anyone speaking bad about Jen," he said in the video. "I'll be the first to say I've made plenty of mistakes in our marriage, and it just really was time for us to go our own ways. We got married so young, and it sucks because I really did feel like we had something pretty special when we got married."

He added, “When I first met Jen, she had this light about her, unlike anyone I've ever met. And she really does have such a good heart, and I will continue to support her and be her biggest fan.”

Within the comments of his TikTok, one user referenced Jen’s videos, calling Zac out for calling Jen’s boobs “saggy” postpartum.

“This was a video from 2022. Obviously horrible and should have never been said,” he replied. “When I saw the video today I was absolutely shocked I had said that. I said a lot of horrible and hurtful things but I have tried my best to learn and grow.”

When another fan referenced the same moment, he added, “I think that world already knows I made a lot of mistakes and said a lot of absolutely horrible things.”

Nevertheless, he hopes he and Jen can find their footing as coparents, especially as they prepare to welcome their fourth baby.

“I feel like probably the thing that's eating me alive the most is I feel like I failed my kids, and I'm worried that I'm going to cause them damage,” he said in his video. “I just hope that we can still give them a normal childhood, but as far as that goes, Jen and I are definitely on the same page of wanting to give them the best the best life we can."
