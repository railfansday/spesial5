Bukayo Saka may not return for Arsenal before Christmas, Mikel Arteta admits

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Zubimendi also out of Saturday’s game at Newcastle

Arteta imposes Fantasy Premier League ban after leak

Mikel Arteta has said Bukayo Saka may not return before Christmas as the Arsenal forward continues his rehabilitation from a thigh injury.

Saka has been out since limping off against Bayern Munich at the end of last month, and it is understood he has yet to resume light training. Arteta revealed more tests during the international break confirmed the England forward did not need surgery and that he was expected to be out for another four weeks.

When asked whether Saka could be fit for the game against Manchester United on 20 December, Arteta was unsure. “I cannot be that specific,” he said. “In four weeks’ time a lot of things can happen. Maybe it is three and then it is five.

“At the end, there are a few elements. One is the healing of that tissue – and we cannot predict exactly when that tissue is going to be in the best condition to push it. Then there is the rehab plan that we have now, what he needs to do in the gym, what he needs to do in terms of treatment and on the field.”

Arsenal will also be without Martín Zubimendi for Saturday’s trip to Newcastle after the Spain midfielder picked up a hamstring injury against England last Sunday. Otherwise Arteta has a clean bill of health, with William Saliba poised to return after a long-term back injury, although Max Dowman remains suspended after his red card against Fleetwood.

Christos Tzolis is expected to start but Arteta revealed Arsenal’s players were now banned from selecting themselves or any teammates in the Fantasy Premier League game after the Greece forward’s team was leaked before the win over Liverpool on 1 November. Tzolis had been out injured for several weeks but picked himself for the trip to Anfield, alerting their opponents. He is believed to have played his wildcard to remove all the Arsenal players from his squad.

“We haven’t dealt with it [before] and I wanted to put new things in place,” said Arteta. “But obviously confidentiality is something critical because it’s about trust and about what you are trying to do. That should be kept as a huge secret and we’ll take measures to make sure it doesn’t happen.”
