Michelle Pfeiffer Shares Rare Insight on David E. Kelley Marriage

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Michelle Pfeiffer celebrated her 33rd wedding anniversary with husband David E. Kelley, calling herself "the luckiest girl in the room" in a sweet tribute.

Michelle Pfeiffer's marriage to David E. Kelley is rich with love.

The Margot's Got Money Troubles star celebrated her 33rd wedding anniversary with the TV producer on Nov. 13, sharing a selfie from their recent date night at the Museum of Modern Art Film Benefit in Los Angeles.

"33 years around the sun with this beautiful man," Pfeiffer captioned the photo, which showed her in a black Chanel gown and emerald earrings as she sat next to Kelley, who donned a dark suit with matching tie. "And 33 years of being the luckiest girl in the room."

The couple's love story clearly struck a chord with Pfeiffer's costar Nick Offerman, who wrote in the comments, "Thank you for setting the bar so high for us mortals."

Their onscreen daughter Elle Fanning added, "Favorite lovebirds."

Although Pfeiffer and Kelley—who share kids Claudia Pfeiffer, 33, and John Kelley, 32—have been married for over three decades, they've largely kept their relationship out of the spotlight. In fact, Pfeiffer avoided working with Kelley for the longest time.

“I’ve seen a lot of couples where they seem to have a really great marriage," she told The New Yorker in 2021, "and then they work together and next year they’re filing for divorce."

That is, until Kelley—who previously produced Ally McBeal, Boston Legal and Big Little Lies—created Margot's Got Money Troubles, in which Pfeiffer plays a mom whose daughter turns to OnlyFans to financially support her newborn baby.

“All these years I sat on the sidelines,” Pfeiffer said in a June interview with IndieWire in June. “I was in envy of all of these other actors getting to speak his words, because boy can he write dialogue. I would turn to him every now and then: ‘I hope these actors realize how lucky they are to be saying these words.’”

And while setting boundaries with work keeps their marriage in mint condition, Pfeiffer also credits a good foundation as the secret to a long-lasting relationship.

"It's starting with good materials, like wrapping a gift," the Batman Returns alum told Extra last year. "If you don't have good material, well...I picked well, so you have to pick well. If you don't pick well, you're kinda screwed."

You don't reach the 50-year mark in any relationship by letting disagreements drag on. Any time there's a blowup, noted the Grace and Frankie star, "Usually, I'm the one who apologizes.
