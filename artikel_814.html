Kourtney Kardashian Celebrates Son Rocky, 3, With Nostalgic B-Day Bash

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Kourtney Kardashian and Travis Barker celebrated their son Rocky’s 3rd birthday by throwing him a larger, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles-themed birthday party.

Kourtney Kardashian and Travis Barker’s son Rocky enjoyed a shell-abration.

After all, the Lemme founder and the Blink-182 drummer threw their youngest son a Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles-themed party Nov. 8 for his milestone birthday.

Kourtney showed off the extravagant bash in a video shared to her Instagram Stories, where she held Rocky on her hip while approaching an elaborate tunnel that read “Turtle Power” on the top.

Meanwhile, characters from the franchise, including turtles Leonardo and Donatello as well as their confidante April O’Neil, stood outside to greet their guests.

“Cowabunga, dudes!” the woman dressed as April O’Neil said as Kourtney, Travis and Rocky walked up to admire the decorations. “Let’s go have fun in the sewer. Welcome!”

Amazed at the replicated sewer, The Kardashians star noted, “Thank you, Look at this!”

After the trio was instructed to grab glow sticks and flashlights before entering the turtles’ home, they walked inside the structure to find something truly amazing, sweetie.

As they made their way through the tunnel, Michelangelo’s catchphrase “Cowabunga” could be seen spray painted on the walls before Kourtney captured green slime oozing from all around.

The tunnel then opened up to reveal the backyard where Rocky’s pool party would take place, which was decked out with balloons as characters Michelangelo and Raphael stood nearby.

And while the party was meant to celebrate her little guy, Kourtney—also mom to kids Mason, 16, Penelope, 14, and Reign, 11, with ex Scott Disick—couldn’t help but feel giddy about the theme, writing over the clip how the party was satisfying her “TMNT Childhood Dreams.”

But the event marks just one soirée that Kourtney and Travis have had to keep up with for their little guy. For his actual birthday on Nov. 1, Kourtney shared a snap on Instagram Stories of her son’s toys wearing party hats as they gathered around his birthday cake.

Also in attendance was Rocky’s big sister Alabama Barker—whom Travis shares with ex Shanna Moakler along with kids Atiana de la Hoya, 27 and Landon Barker, 22—who shared a snap of the toddler getting ready to enjoy his birthday cake as Kourtney crouched down next to him.

“Happy birthday to my little man,” the 20-year-old wrote over the snap shared to her Instagram Stories. “I love you rock rock.”
