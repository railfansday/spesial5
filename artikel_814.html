Pete Davidson Says Ex Elsie Hewitt “Crushed” This Part of Halloween

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Pete Davidson shared insight into his Halloween reunion with ex Elsie Hewitt, confirming she played an important role in their daughter Scottie’s Sopranos-themed costume.

You don’t got to love this costume choice, but you will respect it.

When it came to celebrating his daughter Scottie’s first Halloween, Pete Davidson knew he had to dress her up as the protagonist of his favorite TV show, Tony Soprano. As for their Sopranos-themed costumes, the SNL alum said it was his ex Elsie Hewitt who best steered that ship.

“We had a great time,” Davidson told Jimmy Fallon during his Nov. 9 appearance on The Tonight Show. “And I’ve got to give Elsie credit, she did the whole wardrobe. She crushed it.”

After all, not only was Scottie outfitted in a bathrobe, gold watch and fake cigar plus slicked back hair to complete her Tony costume, but Davidson and Hewitt rounded out the Soprano clan as Paulie Walnuts and Christopher Moltisanti, respectively.

As for why the pair landed on the James Gandolfini character for the 10-month-old’s Halloween debut?

In addition to being the “biggest Sopranos guy,” Davidson revealed, “Before she was even born me and Elsie were like, ‘We gotta.’ Because her hair’s gonna grow. She’s got the perfect Tony hair, because she has no hair. It’s the only year we could.”

And though the How to Rob a Bank star admitted Scottie “hated” dressing up as the head of the Soprano family, he’s still hoping to instill a love of the mafia show from an early age—without exposing Scottie to the bloodier aspects of the series.

“Sometimes when she's playing, I'll play The Sopranos in the background so she gets used to Tony's voice,” Davidson revealed. “Because my sick head thinks, ‘Well, when she's older, she'll be like, “Oh.’” It'll be familiar.”

But in the meantime, even though Scottie is years away from being able to tune into the drama of the DiMeo crime family, there’s nowhere that Davidson would rather report live from than at his daughter’s side.

As he said of fatherhood, “It’s the best.”

For Hewitt and Davidson, Halloween fell about five months after they parted ways as a couple. Despite the breakup, however, the pair reunited to give Scottie a seamless holiday experience.

In fact, Hewitt gave an up-close look at their night out on Oct. 31, which saw the pair parade Scottie around in a Sopranos-decorated wagon.

As a nod to a beloved episode of the series, Hewitt captioned the carousel, “Smells like Halloween.”
