Fantasy Bundesliga: Scout squads for Matchday 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Bulinews' Fantasy Bundesliga writers have each put together a squad of 10 outfield players for Matchday 9 of the 2026/27 season.

Managers have full freedom with formations, but squads can’t exceed 110M, can only include up to three players from the same club, and must be built around low-ownership picks (under 20%).

On top of that, every manager can choose one captain, whose points will be doubled.

With the rules set, let’s take a look at the squads our writers have assembled for Matchday 9:

Konstantinos Karetsas, Borussia Dortmund (15.58M, 4%)

Can Uzun, Eintracht Frankfurt (15.94M, 2%)
