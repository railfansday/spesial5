How to get the Clockwork SMG in The Division 2: Echoes of Central Park

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The new map in Echoes of Central Park has a fair bit of collectibles to unlock. You can improve your arsenal in The Division 2 with the addition of the Clockwork SMG. However, you’ll need to find the secret Guardian cards to unlock the weapon.

In this guide, I will discuss the location of every secret Guardian card you can find.

The Guardian cards are spread all around Central Park. Some are easy to get, while few of them will require you to do a fair bit of traveling.

The first one can be found east from the Mire’s lair. Head to the spot marked on the image above, and you’ll find two trucks. Look for the one that’s tipped to its side. Climb on the truck to get to the elevated area, and then drop to continue forward.

Keep heading straight, and you’ll come to the spot where the Toad is available.

To get the Owl, go east from the Central Park North checkpoint. Once you spot a truck, go left through the bush to enter the maze. To get your collectible, you’ll need to reach the end of the maze. Make sure to complete this in time. If you take too long, you’ll die.

The collectible will be available for you at the end of the maze.

To find this one, you have to go south from the Thorn’s lair all the way to the spot marked on the image above. You’ll find four Emerald signs, and shooting at them releases a red smoke. You need to shoot all four of them.

Enemies will be waiting for you when you try to shoot the signs, so be careful. The four signs are located all around the map. I have marked their locations to make it easy for you. You can’t miss the signs since they have “Emerald” written on them.

You also need to do this task at night after it’s 9pm (in-game time).

To find the Spider, head to the marked spot on the image above. You’ll find two broken-down taxis. Take the left from the front of the taxi shown in the image below.

There’s reportedly an invisible wall; if you get stuck, use the vault function to proceed through.

To get this collectible, you need to extinguish all four candles located around the region. You can put them out in any order you want. Once again, this task has to be done at night.

Once you extinguish all four candles, head to the location marked in the image to find the secret cave that leads you to the Porcupine.

For the Stag, you don’t need to go anywhere. Simply completing the Central Park campaign mission will add the collectible to your collection.

Once you find all six secrets, you can finally proceed towards unlocking the Clockwork SMG.

Head to the Central Park East checkpoint and go to the location marked on the image above. You’ll be able to activate an ECHO. Wait for it to be over, which will automatically drop the Clockwork SMG for you to obtain.
