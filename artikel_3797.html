There's another awesome feature in this issue of PC Gamer, too. On the long-running World War II RTS series' 20th anniversary, we speak directly to the developers who are currently looking after it and planning for its future, Relic Entertainment. And, with plenty of insider stories from the series past and present, this is a must-read for fans.

This issue is also loaded with great previews. First up we have our lead BlizzCon 2026 show report, which covers both Diablo 5 as well as the exciting new StarCraft FPS. This is followed by hands-on previews of Masters of Albion, Gundam Rogue Orbit, Guns of Eschaton, Warhammer Blood Bowl, Muramasa: Revenant Blades, and Crown of Hispania, among others.

Then, in terms of reviews, we pass our official judgement on the adrenaline-inducing Star Wars Galactic Racer, as well as Control Resonant, Endless Legend 2, Nighthawks, Valor Mortis, and Silent Hill: Townfall, among others.

What's in this month's cover story: We get world-exclusive access to 4A Games and its gripping new FPS, Metro 2039. And we're pleased to say that, after lengthy interviews with the game's key developers, as well as behind-the-scenes access, we discover what looks set to be the next great single-player FPS campaign, one that combines the best bits of all previous Metro games into a hyper-dark, visually stunning adventure.

What's in this feature: From its first foray back in 2006, Company of Heroes has developed into one of the genre's most beloved series, with a trilogy of games and various expansions letting gamers live out some explosive, tactically satisfying World War II campaigns. So, on the series landmark 20th anniversary, we speak to Relic Entertainment about Company of Heroes' past, present, and future.

Our lead preview this issue sees PC Gamer report in on the two biggest reveals from BlizzCon 2026. First, we dig into the dramatic reveal of a new single-player StarCraft FPS game and then, secondly, we explore everything we now know about Diablo 5. This is a great first look at what will no doubt be two highly anticipated games.

Our lead review this issue sees PC Gamer buckle up and switch on the afterburner to keep pace with the adrenaline-inducing Star Wars Galactic Racer. And, with a fresh run-based roguelite campaign structure, we find there's plenty to enjoy at breakneck speed, even if the whole racing package is a little rough around the edges.

Just when you'd think you had seen it all, some talented modder goes and splices slick first-person action-platformer Mirror's Edge into the wet-your-pants-scary horror game, Amnesia: The Bunker. The result is Bunker's Edge, and it is as great/crazy as you imagine.

This month's special report explores the talented, dedicated modders who are releasing fan English-language translations of classic, formerly Japanese-exclusive, Mystery Dungeon games such as Torneko's Great Adventure.

If you're a PC gamer who prefers to keep the cooling in your rig as simple and cheap as possible, eschewing water cooling solutions, then this month's group test is for you, with the PC Gamer hardware team testing out six of the best air coolers on the market today, from budget to premium.
