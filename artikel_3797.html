Spyro: A Realm Beyond teased for The Game Awards 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Publisher Activision and developer Toys for Bob will share more information on Spyro: A Realm Beyond at The Game Awards 2026 on December 10, the companies teased.

“[The Game Awards 2026] are around the corner, and Spyro is getting fired up,” Activision said in an email. “Before we get there, here’s a look at some of the Spyro: A Realm Beyond assets [of the Painted Plains area] you might have sneaked a peek at in [Xbox’s 25th anniversary] commemorative magazine. Who is ready to see more of the first all-new Spyro adventure in nearly 20 years? Sheep beware.”

Spyro: A Realm Beyond is due out for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, and PC via Steam and Microsoft Store in spring 2027.
