Disney Dreamlight Valley The Keepsake Sea release countdown: Exact start time and date

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Disney Dreamlight Valley’s big, annual Expansion Pass is almost here, and it’s looking like it could be the biggest one yet. There’s quite a lot to look forward to in this one, so you’ll want to be ready to dive in and start exploring The Keepsake Sea as soon as possible.

Expansion Passes are the biggest form of content drops we get in the valley, so they’re quite an exciting time for the game. If you’re eager to unlock your mermaid form and start exploring underwater, here’s the exact time and date when The Keepsake Sea Expansion Pass will be released for Disney Dreamlight Valley.

The Keepsake Sea Expansion Pass for DDV officially launches on Nov. 18. A precise launch time hasn’t been revealed, but luckily, DDV sticks to a very reliable and consistent schedule. This means that as long as everything stays on track like it usually does, we can expect the DLC to go live at precisely 8am CT.

This is the same time other DLC releases, like Honeyglow Woods and Wishblossom Ranch, went live. It’s also the time the vast majority of updates have been released. The update will be deployed at the same time for everyone, but some platforms, like Nintendo Switch, may take a bit of extra time for it to appear.

If any unexpected delays or changes to the launch time occur, we’ll provide an update here. Otherwise, though, you can check back here leading up to release to see how much longer is left to wait.
