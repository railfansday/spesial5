Chase Ink Business Preferred Card Bonus: 100K Points Welcome Offer

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Ink Business Preferred® Credit Card has a phenomenal sign-up bonus of 100,000 points after spending $8,000 within three first three months, and there’s lots of value to this card in the long term, especially thanks to its reasonable $95 annual fee — read my Ink Preferred review here. If you’re eligible for the card, there’s no reason not to apply.

This is part of the Chase Ink Business card portfolio, and it’s arguably Chase’s most rewarding and best value business credit card. In this post I’d like to go over the Ink Business Preferred bonus details, including eligibility requirements.

Link: Learn more about the Ink Business Preferred® Credit Card with 100K bonus points

The Chase Ink Preferred Card currently has a welcome offer of 100,000 Ultimate Rewards points after spending $8,000 within the first three months.

Based on my latest miles & points valuations, I consider Chase Ultimate Rewards points to be worth 1.7 cents each, meaning the 100,000 points are worth $1,700 to me, which is among the best bonuses out there. There are lots of great uses of Chase points, as they can be transferred to all kinds of useful airline and hotel partners.

Chase Ultimate Rewards partners with programs like Air Canada Aeroplan, Air France-KLM Flying Blue, British Airways Club, United MileagePlus, Virgin Atlantic Flying Club, World of Hyatt, and more. If you want to cash out Chase points (which I wouldn’t recommend), you can get one cent of value per point, meaning the 100,000 points would be worth $1,000.

Just to give some examples of possible uses of the points, you could transfer 34,000 points to Iberia Club for a one-way Iberia business class ticket from Boston to Madrid, or 60,000 points to Flying Blue for a one-way Air France or KLM business class ticket from Miami to Paris. You can stretch your points even further by taking advantage of transfer bonuses.

Let me emphasize that the Chase Ink Preferred 100K bonus is the best publicly available sign-up bonus that’s currently available on the card, and it has been the standard offer since November 2025. How do the previous Chase Ink Preferred offers compare, though?

As you can see, the Ink Business Preferred sign-up bonus on the card has varied from 80,000 points to 120,000 points, and the current bonus of 100,000 points is 10,000 points better than the previous offer, which was for up to 90,000 points.

Everyone has to decide for themselves if it’s worth waiting for the possibility of another welcome bonus of over 100,000 points. Personally I probably wouldn’t wait, since we have no reason to believe such an offer is returning soon. Furthermore, with the card’s useful points categories for spending, there’s potentially quite an opportunity cost to waiting. But still, if you’re in no rush, it could be worth waiting.

When it comes to getting approved for the Chase Ink Preferred Card and earning the welcome bonus, what rules should you be aware of? Well, there are the card specific policies, and then also the more generic Chase policies. When you pull up the application, you’ll see the following terms:

The new cardmember bonus may not be available to you if you have ever had this card. We may also consider factors pertinent to your business in determining your bonus eligibility.

As you can see, officially the welcome bonus may not be available to you if you currently have this card, or if you’ve had it in the past. However, eligibility for the card (including the bonus) is unrelated to whether you have any other Chase business card.

Let me acknowledge that the Ink Preferred isn’t right for everyone. As I see it, the Ink Preferred is all about the rewards structure and the strong purchase and travel protection, while also having a reasonable cost. If you’re looking for airport lounge access or a card with all kinds of premium perks and credits, then consider the Sapphire Reserve Business, which many people will find to be worthwhile for the Chase Sapphire Lounge access, Priority Pass membership, travel credits, and more.

The Chase Ink Preferred Card is one of the best business cards out there, and if you’re eligible for the card, there’s lots of merit to applying.

Not only does the card have a big bonus, but it’s worth holding onto for the great rewards structure, excellent protection benefits, and the ability to maximize other cards in the Chase ecosystem. With the changes we’ve seen to other Chase cards in recent times, this card is arguably even more compelling than in the past.
