This Halloween, Hailey and Justin Bieber Are the Angel and Devil on Your Shoulder

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Their 2026 couples costume was to die for

Hailey Bieber has always been in support of going all out when it comes to Halloween. Whether dressing up as a Victorian vampire with blood-stained pearls and a lace-covered corset (2023) or pairing up with Kendall Jenner to channel Nicole Richie and Paris Hilton from The Simple Life (2024), she’s committed to the holiday year after year. Last October, she got her growing family involved, planning red-and-black Incredibles costumes that turned her into Elastigirl, her pop star husband into Mr. Incredible, and their son, Jack Blues, into baby Jack-Jack.

This year, the Biebers opted for good old fashioned couples costume. In photos shared from a dreamy Halloween shoot, the parents were captured embracing their inner angel (Hailey) and devil (Justin). The Rhode founder, who teased the look hours before with her own Instagram story, was dressed in a feathery white bra, a white button-down, and little black shorts. She accessorized with a classic halo and massive white wings that gave the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show a run for its money. She slipped into black heels and finished things off with a glistening glam, adding an extra touch of ethereality.

As for Justin, the Swag artist put together an all-red fit, composed of an oversize jacket, baggy trousers with a tail, and red sneakers from his own brand, Skylrk. Of course, every good devil costume needs a set of horns, and here, he found a cozy beanie with some built in. He added matching sunglasses to top it all off.

In addition to their fiery couples costume, Hailey also shared a little glimpse into their son’s Halloween. On her Instagram story, she shared a photo of Jack trick-or-treating in what appeared to be a ghost costume.
