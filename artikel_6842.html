In Case You Missed It, Everyone Is Wearing Novelty Jackets Right Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The season doesn’t last forever—get yours before it’s too late.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

We’re in the Goldilocks of jacket season. It’s been a consistent 55 to 60 degrees (knock on wood)—a temperature that doesn’t subject you to puffers and parkas, but rather transitional outerwear. And our favorite piece in this category is the novelty jacket.

Maybe you already have one. You know, that gold-buttoned, swanky blazer that you bought from the thrift store because you knew one day you would find the right occasion for it. Or maybe you’ve never had the courage to buy that fringed shacket because you didn’t think you could pull it off. Well, we’re here to tell you that you can, and that now’s the time to do it. Lately, there’s been a loud call to action for the novelty jacket on social media. TikTok influencer Tinx is one of the novelty jacket’s biggest advocates, promising that she’s going to be wearing a new one every day while the weather holds.

So listen to Tinx, and get yourself a novelty jacket. These are our 11 favorites on the market right now that will bring you endless cheer—and compliments.

This sheeny bomber is the result of that decision—and there’s a whole other host of options if they don’t have your team’s colors available in this specific piece.

Yves Salomon’s jacket brings the drama with its voluminous fur number. Pare it down with light-washed jeans or use it to add the glamour to an LBD. It does it all.
