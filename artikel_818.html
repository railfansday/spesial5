Milla Jovovich Proves Daughter Ever, 19, Is Her Resident Twin

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Milla Jovovich shared a new photo of her daughter Ever in honor of her 19th birthday, celebrating the person the teen is becoming: “You, in truth, have been a gift in every way.”

Any time Milla Jovovich gets to dote on her daughter Ever, she’s in her element.

The Resident Evil actress paid tribute to her and husband Paul W.S. Anderson’s eldest child in honor of her 19th birthday, sharing a new glimpse of the teen.

“Happy 19th bday to my little queen Ever!!!” Milla wrote on Instagram Nov. 3. “What an incredible journey you’ve created for yourself in this life! It’s so amazing to watch you use all the tools you were exposed to so purposefully and expertly.”

“It’s gratifying to any parent who wants their kids to appreciate the world around them,” she continued. “Then, see you add all that to your experiences everyday, learning, growing and evolving into the capable, resourceful and inspiring person you are today? Nothing could make me more confident of your present and future choices which ease the fear everyone has when their children grow up and go out into the world.”

Alongside the sweet message, the 50-year-old—who is also mom to kids Dashiel, 11, and Osian, 6, with Paul—shared a striking photo of her daughter in which she looked eerily like her mom as she gave the camera a pout.

But as The Fifth Element actress put it, Ever’s looks don’t even begin to make up the most special parts of her.

“Your beauty is of course impossible to ignore,” Milla wrote, “but your interest in becoming a compelling, independent, passionate, cultured and disciplined human is a gift beyond comparison.”

“You, in truth, have been a gift in every way. As a mother, you have brought me love in ways I couldn’t imagine before you,” she added. “As a friend, you’ve been loyal and thoughtful even when I know it was difficult for you. As another soul I share this life journey with, you have both been my student and my teacher until now I can just admire you for who you have become, separate and unique, a powerful force of nature.”

And while Milla can hardly believe her little girl has grown up so fast, it seems Ever is just as shocked. After all, she shared a slew of baby pictures on Instagram, writing, “I feel like i blinked and turned 19.”

Milla and Paul welcomed Ever in 2007, two years before they tied the knot at their home in Beverly Hills, Calif.

“I remember looking at my newborn, my first baby and wondering what kind of a teenager she would be,” Milla wrote of Ever on Instagram in 2024. “Not even being able to imagine how this tiny, adorable, screaming being could develop into a rational, articulate human.”

“What an honor it’s been raising you,” she continued. “What a privilege to share this life together.”
