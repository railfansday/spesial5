Gordon Ramsay is only getting better with thyme.

The Hell’s Kitchen star celebrated the day after his 60th birthday with a steamy shirtless picture that has so much heat you nearly have to stay out of the kitchen.

In the Nov. 9 Instagram picture, Ramsay proved that, unlike his vegetables, he is not chopped as he stood nearly-naked in his workout gear and flexed for the camera, showing off his toned physique.

The chef—who trains for his triathlons with cardio and daily pushups—paired his post with a side of gratitude, writing in the caption, “Thank you everyone for the incredible birthday messages! Day 2 of being 60……LFG!!!!”

The day before, Ramsay commemorated his birthday milestone by celebrating his daughter—and birthday twin—Tilly’s 25th stir around the sun.

“Happy birthday to this incredible young lady,” he wrote on Instagram alongside throwback pictures with his (tater) tot. “Sharing such a special day with you is a Dads dream, there’s not enough words to explain how proud you make me feel.”

Ramsay added, “Have a fantastic 25th birthday and I can’t believe I’m bloody 60 wtf.”

But while he may be known for his strict personality on TV, the MasterChef host—also dad to kids Megan, 28, Jack, 26, Holly, 26, Oscar, 7, and Jesse, 2, with wife Tana Ramsay—has explained that his harsh demeanor is boiled down to a reduction when he’s at home with his family.

“The kids have brought the most emotion out of me,” he told People in 2023. “It’s funny, isn’t it? Because everyone thinks, ‘God, you must be an absolute ass to be at home with.’ [But] Tana’s super fierce, an ex-Montessori school teacher. So I’m the softie.”

In fact, Ramsay’s daughter Holly—who tied the knot with Olympic swimmer Adam Peaty in December—quipped that he would need more than a dish towel on her wedding day.

“The day he walks me down the aisle, we are going to need so many tissues,” she added to People. “My veil will have to be [made of] tissues so he can walk behind me and mop up his tears.”
