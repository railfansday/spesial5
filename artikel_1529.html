Brazil, Italy: Robinho Jr impresses people while his father is in prison

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Quickly considered one of the great talents of Brazilian football, Robinho (42) has had a solid career. Although he has not necessarily reached the level expected of him, he played for Real Madrid, Manchester City and AC Milan while obtaining 100 caps (28 goals) with Brazil. The striker who was champion in Brazil, Spain, Italy, China and Turkey hung up his boots in 2020 with Santos. However, his fate has taken a much less pleasant turn since then. Found guilty in a story of gang rape committed in 2013 in Milan, the former attacker has been serving a nine-year prison sentence since March 2024 at Tremembé prison in the São Paulo region. Almost two years later, his situation has not really changed except that he changed penitentiary establishment to continue his sentence in Limeira.

On the other hand, his son is talked about thanks to football. Trained in Santos, Robinho Junior follows in his father’s footsteps when it comes to the green rectangle. Considered an excellent dribbler, the young winger debuted on July 17, 2025 in the first team with the Paulista club. Playing 27 matches with Santos, however, he saw the situation worsen with a clash with Neymar, the star of the team. Not yet established as a starter, he was one year from the end of his contract last spring before finally extending his lease until June 2031. However, he made a bold choice by being loaned to Italy. Genoa bet on him and have a purchase option.

And for the moment, learning is going well with the Grifoni. With the U20 team, he already has three goals and an assist in five matches, scoring a double against Atalanta and another achievement against Juventus. Solid performances which even allowed him to be called up for four Serie A matches. Better, he entered the field on October 17 against AS Roma. His coach Daniele De Rossi praised him during an interview for Gazzetta dello Sport : “he made an excellent impression on me. I didn’t know him, it’s an operation more for the future, but he is very interesting. He does useful and intelligent things, he needs to develop physically, but he knows how to hold his position on the pitch.

Binational Brazilian, but also Italian, Robinho Junior is also monitored by the Nazionale and coach Roberto Mancini. However, it is on the Brazilian side that he shines with the selection. On the night of Monday to Tuesday with the Brazil U20 team, he stood out in a match against Peru. While the Auriverdes won 9-0, the right winger was a major player in this festival by scoring a double in two minutes. After this performance, however, he left the door open to the Nazionale: “Every child dreams of representing their national team. I have two. Either one would suit me. Of course, Paulo Victor said he was counting on me. So, I’m available.” Far from his father’s prison, Robinho Junior restores the shine his name needed.
