Dybala-Mora favored over Soule in Parma clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The countdown has begun for Paulo Dybala’s return to action.

The Argentine striker was forced to sit out the match against Real Madrid due to a calf injury that kept him sidelined longer than expected.

The number 21 has returned to training and is a candidate for a starting spot for Parma-Roma, scheduled for Saturday at 6:00 PM.

Rodrigo Mora could be in the starting lineup alongside Dybala. The 2007-born player didn’t play a single minute for Portugal, so, aside from the travel, he hasn’t suffered any fatigue.
