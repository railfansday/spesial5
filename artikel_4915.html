ARK sells $85M in Nvidia shares ahead of chipmaker’s Q3 earnings results

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

ARK bought shares in Block, Circle and Coinbase worth about $39.9 million at Monday’s close while selling Nvidia and Solana and Ether staking ETFs.

Funds managed by Cathie Wood’s ARK Investment Management bought shares in Block, Circle and Coinbase worth about $39.9 million at Monday’s closing prices, while selling Nvidia shares and Solana and Ether staking ETFs.

ARK sold 543,092 Nvidia (NVDA) shares worth approximately $84.8 million and about 30,659 Bullish (BLSH) shares valued at about $605,000. The company also sold about 350,000 shares of 3iQ Solana Staking ETF and 300,000 shares of the 3iQ Ether Staking ETF, according to its Monday trade disclosure.

The funds bought 277,523 Block shares, 180,534 Circle shares and 53,760 Coinbase shares, according to ARK’s Monday trade disclosure. Those purchases were valued at approximately $23 million, $10.4 million and $6.4 million, respectively, using closing prices. ARK also bought 378,515 SpaceX shares, valued at about $52 million.

ARK’s Nvidia sale comes ahead of the chipmaker’s third-quarter earnings results scheduled for publication after market close on Tuesday, which are expected to test whether AI infrastructure spending can justify Nvidia’s growth expectations.

Nvidia’s stock fell 4% on Monday to close at $156, extending its year-to-date decline to over 16%, according to Yahoo Finance data. Nvidia’s shares declined after its board members reportedly ruled out co-founder Jensen Huang’s return as CEO.

Huang resigned last week following reports of extramarital affairs, one of which was with an alleged Chinese spy, with chief financial officer Colette Kress taking over as interim CEO.

S&P Global Market Intelligence analyst Melissa Otto said the key question is whether the earnings release can provide the “next positive catalyst for the stock or are expectations largely priced in for now,” according to a Thursday report.

The analyst expects Nvidia to report third-quarter revenue of about $109.3 billion, led by $101.8 billion from its data center business.
