Draymond Green defends Rockets from ‘dirty' label after Steph Curry injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Draymond Green defends Rockets from 'dirty' label after Steph Curry injury originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

Draymond Green does not think the Houston Rockets are a dirty team. He does, however, believe they are a very physical one.

Speaking on "The Draymond Green Show" as he discussed Steph Curry's injury and playing without him, Green addressed the label that followed the Warriors' 115-108 loss in Houston on Nov. 11. Curry grimaced and reached for his right side after tangling with Rockets center Alperen Sengun on a screen early in the first quarter, leaving the game minutes later.

An MRI confirmed an oblique abdominal tear, and Curry will be re-evaluated in two weeks, with ESPN's Shams Charania reporting the team expects him to miss closer to a month. Some fans blamed Sengun, yet Green explains the team is just outright physical, not dirty.

"I know a lot of people are saying, 'Oh man, the Houston Rockets are a dirty team.' I don't get off into that dirty thing," Green said. "Every now and then you see some people that just do things that are downright dirty, and it happens, and when it does, I'll speak on it."

The matchup has been physical before. Curry left a Warriors loss to Houston last November with a right quad contusion after a collision with Amen Thompson and a screen by Sengun.

Wednesday's game saw Rockets guard Marcus Smart ejected for throwing a punch that grazed the nose of Warriors center Charles Bassey, and the NBA suspended Smart three games. Bassey said Smart was playing dirty "the whole game" and called the punch "fake toughness."

Green drew a different line. He said the thing that bothers him most about Houston is not rough play.

"Do they do some bulls— when we play them? Absolutely," Green said. "I think the thing that may irritate me the most is, like, they play super physical, but then they start flopping sometimes, you know. And so I would say that irritates me more, but I'm not going to sit here and call them a dirty team. They're a physical team."

"When we were watching film pregame, you can see on the film they are physical as hell, and so give them credit," Green said.

Green will speak up when something truly is dirty. In his eyes, the Rockets are just physical
