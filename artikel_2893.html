Chargers QB Justin Herbert (concussion) to miss Week 12, DJ Uiagalelei will make first NFL start vs. Patriots

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Los Angeles Chargers have ruled out quarterback Justin Herbert (concussion) for their Week 12 matchup against the New England Patriots, the team announced Friday.

Herbert was placed in concussion protocol following last week's 27-24 loss to the New York Jets. He did not practice this week before the team confirmed that the 28-year-old will not play.

DJ Uiagalelei will make his first NFL start in Herbert's place. Jake Browning, who was signed to the Chargers' active roster this week, will serve as the backup.

"This isn't my first rodeo with regard to starting quarterbacks having an injury you don't know at the beginning of the week when we start game planning," said Chargers offensive coordinator Mike McDaniel, who experienced similar concussion issues at the quarterback position when he coached the Miami Dolphins and Tua Tagovailoa.

Uiagalelei is a former elite high school prospect who took a circuitous route to his first NFL start. One of the top quarterback recruits in his class, he appeared destined to follow in Trevor Lawrence's footsteps at Clemson, but struggled to replicate that success once given the starting job. He wound up spending the last two years of his college career at Oregon State and Florida State.

He went unselected in the 2025 NFL Draft and has spent most of his professional career on the Chargers' practice squad, but moved to the backup role in October after an injury to Trey Lance. Now, he will be under center against the defending AFC champions.

The 1-9 Chargers are tied for the worst record in the NFL entering Week 12. Following Sunday's game against the Patriots, Jim Harbaugh's team goes on the road back-to-back weeks to take on the Tampa Bay Buccaneers and Las Vegas Raiders before welcoming the San Francisco 49ers at home in Week 15.
