Week 5 Look-Ahead: Stephen Curry and Caleb Wilson go down, and toss Jeremiah Fears the keys

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

By the end of Sunday's game, four weeks will have passed in the 2026-27 season. Unfortunately, we've got more injuries to key players to deal with, and there has also been a trade request. Most NBA players won't become trade-eligible until December 15, so it remains to be seen whether there's significant movement before then. Let's look at some of the key storylines from Week 4 before moving on to Week 5.

- Warriors lose Stephen Curry: After a 0-3 start, the Golden State Warriors looked nothing like a team capable of being a factor in the Western Conference. They then ripped off eight straight wins, thanks to Stephen Curry's continued greatness, rookie Yaxel Lendeborg being ready for the moment and a supporting cast offering consistent contributions. Unfortunately, the momentum came to a screeching halt during the Warriors' November 11 loss to the Rockets, as Curry suffered an oblique abdominal tear in the first quarter.

He'll be re-evaluated in a little under two weeks, but the injury could keep Curry sidelined for close to a month. The November 13 game against the Spurs was Golden State's first since Curry's injury, and it was not good. In the 123-85 defeat, Lendeborg shot 1-of-13 from the field, scoring three points, and Brandin Podziemski (eight points) wasn't much better, making two of his ten attempts. Those two will need to step up in Curry's absence, with the Warriors beginning a four-game Week 5 on Monday in New Orleans.

- Caleb Wilson injury shifts the ROY race: While the Bulls haven't started the season well, losing nine of their first 12 games, Wilson's play has been a bright spot. The rookie forward is averaging 18.9 points, 6.2 rebounds, 1.9 assists, 1.8 steals, 0.9 blocks and 2.3 three-pointers per game. Unfortunately, he fractured his right hand during the Bulls' November 11 loss to the Spurs and is expected to miss four to six weeks.

In their first game since Wilson's hand injury, the Bulls beat the Hawks in overtime on November 13. Isaac Okoro (15/3/2/1 with two three-pointers) moved into the starting lineup, while Norman Powell (33/5/2/1 with four three-pointers) and Josh Giddey (15/10/14 with three blocks and three three-pointers) had highly productive nights. Two players to watch after Wilson's injury are Matas Buzelis and Patrick Williams. The former appeared to have trouble finding his place in the Bulls' starting lineup with Wilson on the floor, while the latter has moved into the rotation after racking up DNP-CDs early in the season.

- Cade Cunningham historic night: Cunningham had a career night in the Pistons' November 12 double-overtime win over the Wizards, going off for 52 points, five rebounds, 11 assists, one steal, one block, three three-pointers and zero turnovers. Not only was this his first 50-point game, but this was also the first time since the NBA began tracking turnovers in 1977-78 that a player finished with at least 50 points, 10 assists and no turnovers.

After averaging 4.4 turnovers per game in 2024-25, Cunningham's average has decreased each of the last two seasons, down to 3.2 per game in 2026-27. His play is a big reason the Pistons enter Week 5 with an 8-4 record, good for third in the Eastern Conference.

- Dejounte Murray requests a trade: The Pelicans' roster felt awkward when the team opened training camp, due to the mixture of veterans whose long-term outlooks with the franchise were a bit cloudy and young players who needed room to figure out who they can be as potential building blocks. That led to Murray and second-year guard Jeremiah Fears starting together, and New Orleans began the season with four straight defeats.

Murray was away from the team for personal reasons in late October, and New Orleans went on a three-game win streak with Fears running the show by himself. The Pelicans went 1-4 after Murray's return, and on Thursday it was announced that his representation requested a trade. Pinning the Pelicans' struggles solely on Murray would be unfair, but it was clear something needed to change. Officially, Murray has missed the last two games with a sprained ankle, but it's difficult to envision him playing another game for the Pelicans. Not only does this boost Fears' fantasy value, but it does the same for Herb Jones, who has returned to the starting lineup.

- Paul George steals the show in Jayson Tatum/Jaylen Brown duel: Tuesday's game between the Celtics and 76ers was the first regular-season meeting between the teams since the offseason trade that sent Jaylen Brown to Philadelphia and Paul George to Boston. Of course, the main storyline was the relationship between Brown and Jayson Tatum, but PG-13 stole the show. In 30 minutes, George dropped 36 points, five rebounds, one assist and six three-pointers, shooting 14-of-22 from the field.

He did not play against Toronto the following night, but the only two games George has missed so far were for injury management. George played 78 games total in two seasons with the 76ers; if he stays relatively healthy, that would be a game-changer for the Celtics and for fantasy managers who grabbed him with a middle-round pick in drafts.

- The Lakers could be without Austin Reaves: Reaves suffered a contusion and bone bruise in his left heel during a November 11 win over the Bucks. Despite the Lakers not having played a game since, the guard was ruled out for their November 15 matchup with the Suns. The good news for the Lakers is that Quentin Grimes is available after missing the Milwaukee win with a finger injury; he could be the player asked to fill the void in the starting lineup.

Ziaire Williams, who began the season as a starter, may also see his opportunities increase after Jake LaRavia jumped ahead of him in the pecking order. The Lakers play three games in Week 5, beginning with a home back-to-back against the Hornets and Jazz on Tuesday and Wednesday. Between Reaves and Luka Dončić, fantasy managers rostering either player may not get full value because of injury or injury management.

- Franz Wagner carries a four-game streak of 30 points or more: The Magic forward has been outstanding recently, scoring at least 30 points in four straight games. In his most recent appearance, Wagner recorded 35 points, nine rebounds, 12 assists, one steal, one block and three three-pointers in a November 13 win over the Bucks.

While the schedule has been forgiving, with Orlando following up an overtime loss to the Warriors with wins over Sacramento, Utah and Milwaukee, that shouldn't take away from Wagner's play. And with Paolo Banchero (ankle) not due to be re-evaluated until late November, Franz has more room to take control of things offensively.

- The Celtics, Mavericks and Knicks have the busiest weekends: Boston, Dallas and New York will play games on both Saturday and Sunday to end Week 5. From an injury-management standpoint, Boston's Tatum, George, and Dallas' Kyrie Irving are the players most likely to be affected. Players like Payton Pritchard (Boston), Naji Marshall, and Zaccharie Risacher (Dallas) may find themselves in positions where they can take on more responsibility than usual.

The Knicks may have the most forgiving back-to-back of the three, as both of their games will be at home. Boston ends its west coast trip with games against the Kings (prime resting spot?) and Warriors, while Dallas hosts the Clippers on Saturday before visiting the Jazz the following day.

- Seven teams will have the weekend off: Among the teams that won't play on either Saturday or Sunday to end Week 5 are the Cavaliers, Rockets and Lakers. Due to a back-to-back, made worse by Friday's travel issues caused by the FAA, Cleveland didn't play any of its starters on Saturday against Dallas. In one of the most impressive performances of the season, the Cavs won. Tyrese Proctor recorded a triple-double, Meleek Thomas scored 20 points, Mario Hezonja scored 22 and Thomas Bryant recorded a double-double.

Kenny Atkinson's team plays games on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, so the stars will get a little more rest in a good schedule week for them. As noted above, the Lakers could also use a weekend to recuperate from recent injury issues. Each of the seven teams that don't play on Saturday or Sunday will be active on Friday, so the end of those games is when fantasy managers should move on from players who aren't considered "must-roster."

- The Trail Blazers get to play another back-to-back against the same team: After hosting the Thunder for games on Tuesday and Wednesday of Week 4, winning both, Portland begins Week 5 with games in Minnesota against the Timberwolves on Monday and Tuesday. For the Blazers, Damian Lillard played both games of the Thunder back-to-back, logging 24 minutes in a 20-point win on Tuesday and 32 in a 10-point win the following night. His ability to play back-to-backs coming off of the Achilles tendon rupture is encouraging, but the nature of the first win over Oklahoma City likely helped, as Lillard didn't play at all in the fourth quarter.
