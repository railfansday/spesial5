Exclusive: Matt Jarvis backs ‘exceptional’ Scott Parker in verdict on West Ham’s Nuno sacking

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

West Ham parted ways with Nuno Espirito Santo on Tuesday, and swiftly named Scott Parker as his replacement. The new boss led WHUFC for Wednesday’s 3-3 draw at Cardiff City, and many are backing him for success.

Among the supporters of Parker is former Hammers winger Matt Jarvis, who believes inconsistency ultimately cost Nuno his job. Speaking to The72 via Midnite, Jarvis said: “The fans’ perspective flipped from last year’s misery to expecting to dominate this league and win promotion. Visiting the London Stadium in front of 55,000 to 60,000 supporters is every opposition team’s cup final, and adapting to that takes time.

“With Nuno, people forget he had been there for a significant period. It took him time to establish himself in the Premier League, then they had a good spell before fading towards the end. The board backed him in the transfer market after relegation, but results remained too inconsistent. The supporters couldn’t see a clear playing identity, and the home defeat to West Brom proved the final straw.”

Jarvis went on to talk up Parker’s chances of success as West Ham boss. He picked out his communication skills, experience and passion as key traits as he looks to take the Hammers back to the Premier League.

“People will debate whether the decision came too early or whether they should have waited for the international break, but the club felt a change was needed. They have four upcoming fixtures where they should realistically target three points from every game.

“Scott Parker has a fantastic Championship track record and an existing connection to the club.

“He’s an exceptional communicator, which is vital for translating training-ground instruction onto the pitch on matchdays. He’s passionate, knows what it takes to get out of this division, and his arrival should have an immediate impact.”

Parker has won three promotions from the Championship, achieving the feat at Fulham, Bournemouth and Burnley. In his most recent triumph, he led the Clarets to 2nd place in 2024/25 and broke the league record for the fewest goals conceded in an entire campaign at just 16.

The switch from Nuno to Parker has split some opinions. There are justified concerns that their play styles are too similar given the new boss’ recent teams have been built on the foundation of a strong defence.

This was the case in his last job at Burnley, but the 46-year-old has shown he has the ability to switch up his approach. At Fulham in the 2020/21 season, his side had the second-highest average possession in the Championship with 61.4% (FotMob), as well 85 big chances created and 1,101 touches in the opposition box.

Over time, it will become clear whether Parker plans on emulating his Burnley tactics at WHUFC, or his Fulham style. Alternatively, he could switch things up completely, and if he does, ex-Hammer Jarvis believes he has the know-how and communication skills to get this across to his new squad.

After taking charge of the Cardiff City draw a matter of hours after his appointment on Wednesday, Parker leads his side for the second time against Blackburn Rovers this Saturday.
