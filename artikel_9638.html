Traffic Without Signups: Diagnosing an AI Product Landing Page

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Posted on Nov 26 • Originally published at okaihub.com

Traffic that does not convert is the most common growth problem and the most misdiagnosed. The instinct is to get more traffic — more channels, more posts, more listings. But if the page loses most visitors, more traffic just loses more people faster, and the real signal is being drowned out.

Fix the page first. This is the diagnostic order, from the thing that loses the most people to the thing that loses the fewest.

Before touching the page, check that the visitors are the ones who could want the product. A conversion problem and a targeting problem look identical in the numbers.

Most visitors decide in a few seconds whether the page is about them, and that decision is made almost entirely from the headline and the first sentence. This is where most traffic is lost.

The test: show only your headline and first sentence to someone who has not seen the site, and ask what the product does. If they cannot tell you, that sentence is losing most of your traffic before they scroll.
