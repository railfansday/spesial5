Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: Schwab Investor Savings Account is now a HYSA at 3.5% APY

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Just talked to two banking specialist at Schwab and I think I can summarize where we are at:
1) only recent change was the increase in savings APY and the ability to open an account online. In all other ways this is the same Investor Savings account already offered.
2) You CAN setup checking to overdraft from Savings for everything except Zelle and wires.
3) There is not an outgoing transaction limit from Savings, however, you cannot ACH pull from savings. Direct deposits are fine, no bill pay or credit card payment pulls.
4) You can use an ATM/debit card against your Savings account.

So I think the setup would be to keep checking at zero, except for when Zelle/wire is required. Have all deposits directed to Savings but have all spending from checking which then autodrafts out of checking.

I still intend to only keep about a months of income in Savings and move money out monthly to our brokerage account for T-Bills and the like. I experimented with the autodraft from brokerage (ala margin) a few months ago and didn't like the mechanism. This new option is a way to upgrade from nearly zero to 3.5% on only monthly slush.
