GTA 6’s PS5 Pro upgrade is bigger than you might expect

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

GTA 6 targets 30fps on both PS5 and PS5 Pro, but Digital Foundry’s first side-by-side comparison shows a sharper image and steadier performance on Sony’s upgraded console.

Reviews for GTA 6 are in, and it’s already the joint best-reviewed game of all time, sitting right alongside Nintendo’s The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.

One outlet that has been incredibly impressed is video game tech analysis YouTube channel Digital Foundry, which has now given a deep dive into the differences between the base PS5 and PS5 Pro performance when it comes to playing GTA 6.

Digital Foundry’s John Linneman played GTA 6 for more than 50 hours on PS5 Pro without noticing a frame rate dip during normal gameplay. Well, besides a special case when he blew up multiple cars with a grenade launcher, which sent the Pro’s FPS down to 24 frames per second.

Linneman did note that this is a special case and the Pro frame rate stays smooth for the majority of gameplay, while the base PS5 showed occasional drops during normal gameplay.

“They’re really very minor, but I noticed them, and it does slightly detract from the experience. It’s largely fine, and as I said earlier, it’s smoother than any GTA game at launch up till now, but I’d still like to have seen this cleaned up for release,” continued Linneman.

The Pro also produces a noticeably clearer image, despite both versions appearing to render internally at 1440p. In its tech review, Linneman credits PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution, or PSSR, for the Pro’s sharper presentation. He says the technology reconstructs an image that “convincingly passes for native 4K,” while the standard PS5 more clearly resembles a 1440p image that has been upscaled.

“The difference in image quality is substantial,” says Linneman, with the comparison footage also showing more precise shadows on PS5 Pro. Both versions use ray-traced lighting and reflections, but the Pro adds ray-traced shadows that remove some of the rougher edges visible in the base PS5’s shadow maps.

The performance gap is less dramatic during ordinary play, though Linneman says the Pro delivers the definitive way to play GTA 6.

“I spent most of my time on the PS5 Pro, which is the best way to play this game by far. You basically get a near-flawless 30fps update. The frame pacing is perfect, cutscenes are consistent and fluid, unlike older Rockstar titles, and everything moves without hiccups or stutters. It’s extremely solid, as you would hope, given that it’s only 30FPS.”

Speaking to Wccftech, Rockstar’s developers also revealed that GTA 6 renders at 1440p on Xbox Series X before being upscaled to 4K using the studio’s own TAA solution, while the Xbox Series S version starts at 720p and uses AMD FSR Upscaling 4 to reach 1080p.
