Hunter Schafer Gives Daryl Hannah’s Iconic ‘Blade Runner’ Beauty a 2099 Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

For the premiere of a show set in 2099, Hunter Schafer went all the way back to 1982. Attending the Los Angeles premiere of Blade Runner 2099 last night, the actor delivered a striking, menacing android beauty homage to Ridley Scott’s original sci-fi classic.

“The concept was a nod to Daryl Hannah’s character Pris in the original Blade Runner movie,” makeup artist Sandy Ganzer confirms to Vogue. “Her makeup is so iconic and we wanted to pay homage with a modern and more red carpet-friendly version of it.”

Hannah’s punkish replicant is responsible for one of cinema’s most enduring beauty looks. We first got to know Pris with a shock of peroxide-blonde hair and a dramatic, amask-like strip of shadow across the eyes. For Schafer, Ganzer reimagined that graphic eye makeup in metallic gunmetal and smoky taupe, extending the shadow horizontally toward the temples and across the bridge of the nose for that cyborg look.

To create the shape, Ganzer began with the matte grayish-brown crease shade from the Natasha Denona Glam palette, layering its deepest metallic gunmetal shadow over the top for depth and dimension. She then applied the white highlighter from Dior’s Universal Glow palette along the bridge of Schafer’s nose, fading it into the inner corners of her eyes for a futuristic, silvered glow. Prada Beauty eyeliner and mascara created more precise definition. The secret to that razor-sharp upper edge? “I cleaned up the edges on top with a Q-tip dipped in micellar water to make sure it was ultra sharp and left the bottom edges diffused and blurry,” Ganzer explains.

The rest of the face was kept deliberately soft, with a little Haus Labs foundation and concealer, the lightest touch of Saie blush in Mia, and Lancôme’s Hush Hush lip color, dabbed on with a finger for a romantic and blurred finish. It was a necessary contrast to the severity of the eyes.

Daryl Hannah as Pris in Ridley Scott’s Blade Runner.

“We really wanted the eyes to have their own moment, so with the hair, I wanted to keep it feeling a bit more restrained against all the glamour,” hairstylist Rena Calhoun explains. Schafer’s hair was pulled back into a deliberately disheveled updo, with flyaways and loose pieces escaping at the back. “The idea was for it to feel a bit rough and undone, with little pieces falling out in the back that gave it a slightly messy, frazzled quality, almost like Pris’s texture in the film.”

Styled by Dara, Schafer kept up the dystopian femmebot look in a burnt brown-and-black leather Prada corset dress with a sculptural neckline, layered beneath a voluminous chocolate-brown fur coat, with textured black tights and sparkling earrings.

Method-glamming has very much been amped up across the last year, thanks in part to Schafer’s Euphoria co-star Zendaya. Out on the press tour for The Odyssey, Zendaya swapped her bixie for ethereal, Greek goddess lengths care of hairstylist Coree Moreno. They had lots of fun with Grecian half-up half-down styles and braided crowns.

Schafer stars in the forthcoming Prime Video series, which picks up 50 years after the events of Blade Runner 2049. She plays Cora, a human fugitive who assumes the identity of a Blade Runner within the LAPD to protect her younger brother and secure a future for humanity. Alongside Michelle Yeoh, who plays a replicant nearing the end of her life, Schafer finds herself navigating a dystopian Los Angeles in which the boundaries between human and machine have become increasingly precarious.

And if this premiere is any indication, the Blade Runner 2099 press tour is shaping up to be a particularly thrilling beauty and fashion ride. Schafer and Allen have already been exploring the possibilities of sci-fi dressing, from a graphic, color-blocked Prada look to a neon, crystal-embellished NIHL ensemble. There is certainly plenty of aesthetic territory to explore in the Blade Runner universe itself, oscillating from the cyberpunk heroines to more noir glamour. With Hunter Schafer at the controls, 2099 is already looking rather good.
