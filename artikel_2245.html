Premiership star bemoans 'unlucky' red card picked up against Rangers

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

St Johnstone winger Kai Fotheringham has claimed he did not deserve to be sent off against Rangers.

The wide man picked up a red card during his side's heavy defeat at Ibrox after clattering into Nico Raskin. He made contact with the ball but followed through and caught the Belgian on his shin with his studs.

After a VAR review, he was ordered off, but it was a decision which sparked lots of debate in the aftermath.

After serving his suspension and getting back into the team, Fotheringham has opened up with his thoughts on it.

“The red card was unlucky I felt," he said.

“I don’t think I deserved it – it’s one of those that looks worse when you see it with a still image.

“There was nothing I could do about it, though.

“All I could focus on was getting back into training, working hard and being ready when I was next called upon.

“I did extra with the sports scientist to really make sure.

“It’s easy to feel sorry for yourself but I didn’t do that."
