They say that all good things must come to an end, but that doesn't soften the blow when it finally happens, and the Spurs are shut down by one of the league's best defenses along with a scary injury in the last minute of the game to one of the only players who was able to make an offensive impact against the Pistons. The Spurs were maybe a little complacent after winning 12 in a row, and the physicality of Cade Cunningham and their entire roster of scrappy fighters disrupted the Spurs' offense for the entire game, and Harper slipping on the court and losing the ball when the Spurs had a shot to cut the lead to 5 points with a minute left was the coup de grace for the Silver and Black's slim chances of extending the win streak.

Dylan Harper is listed as out tonight with 'hip soreness' which is actually a bit encouraging, since soreness could be an injury where could be returning within a couple of weeks or less, and when he fell, it looked a lot more serious than that. Obviously, we are going to have to wait for a more precise timeline, but I feel like it could have been a lot worse, and the lack of terrible news feels like good news to me.

Tonight the Spurs face the 4-9 Clippers, who are dangerous despite their losing record. They're 2-1 in their last three beating the Thunder in an NBA Cup game by one point, defeating the Hornets, and then inexplicably being blown out 132-107 by the Jazz behind Brice Sensabaugh's 40 point offensive explosion. I feel lucky that the Spurs are done playing the Jazz this season, because they seem to be putting things together under Will Hardy with their talented young roster.

Will we see the Clippers who beat the Thunder, or the team that couldn't hang with the Jazz? I think that isn't what the team needs to worry about, they need to get back to themselves after the losing effort against Detroit, and work on more efficient offensive execution and freeing up Victor in the paint. The bench needs to improve their play, and with Julian Champagnie moving into the starting lineup, they'll be at even more of a disadvantage. Players like Carter Bryant need to step up and not just occasionally make spectacular plays but become consistent assets on both ends of the court. A game against a mid-level team like the Clippers is a good opportunity to work on that.

GO SPURS GO!! Carter must dominate Rui. Let's have some fun and hopefully we will see early JMac minutes. Actually with Harper out, that's pretty much guaranteed to happen.
