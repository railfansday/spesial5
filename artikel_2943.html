Cohu: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Software analytics revenue surges 85% sequentially, potentially creating a cushion against hardware cycle swings.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 24, 2026 at 11:00AM EST

On its third-quarter 026 earnings call, held on Oct. 28, 2026, Cohu (COHU -10.22%) announced that revenue grew 37% year over year to $173 million. High-performance computing, or HPC, infrastructure demand is driving a turnaround in test equipment requirements. The market is shifting toward higher thermal-control needs, positioning the business for growth.

Three takeaways from the call stand out for long-term investors.

HPC applications require precise thermal management during chip testing. The expansion of customer qualifications gives the company visibility into multiyear system deliveries.

Software analytics and factory maintenance tools create predictable revenue streams. Expanding deployments across high-bandwidth memory, or HBM, chipmakers help insulate total sales from hardware cycle volatility.

Management expects 2027 revenue to grow 30% to 40% over 2026. Because operating expenses will stay mostly flat, incremental sales yield high profit margins.

Surging demand for thermal testing creates near-term manufacturing bottlenecks and cost pressures. Management is expanding factory capacity in Malaysia and the Philippines while securing key parts to protect delivery schedules.

Growth hinges on executing capacity expansion and converting evaluation systems into commercial orders across artificial intelligence, or AI, and memory customers.
