Obsessed With ‘Obsession’ Star Inde Navarrette? Time to Meet Her Boyfriend Nick Aiello

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Greetings to everyone who’s still fully obsessed with Obsession (as well as with the film’s star, Inde Navarrette). Inde is totally captivating in her role as Nikki in the indie horror film from YouTuber Curry Barke—and while she may not be an obsessive girlfriend in real life, the scream queen is taken, as evidenced by her SNL hard-launch. So, without further ado: Time to meet her boyfriend, musician Nick Aiello.

It’s unclear how long the pair has been together, since both keep their personal lives pretty under wraps. But it seems like the two have been an item since at least early 2026. For instance: Earlier this spring, both Inde and Nick posted videos of themselves riding motorbikes in Thailand, and a few weeks later, both Inde and Nick included close-up shots of each other as the last slide in their respective photo dumps from Coachella.

Though they didn’t take any red carpet snaps together, Nick even attended the L.A. special screening of Obsession alongside Inde.

The maybe-couple kept the rumor mill turning when they were photographed grabbing late-night tacos together in May, and they’ve been spotted together several times since—though it wasn’t until Inde referred to Nick as her “boyfriend” during her SNL monologue on October 31 that dating rumors were finally confirmed. Cuuuute!

So, who is Nick Aiello, you ask? He’s a singer-songwriter from San Diego who studied musical theater at the University of Michigan and happens to be very good friends with High School Musical: The Musical: The Series star Joshua Bassett. The two even performed together at Belly Up Tavern in San Diego.

Back in April, Josh also posted a photo of himself, Nick, Inde, and a couple of other friends casually hanging in a Goodyear Blimp.

Nick released his first EP, ILO, back in February 2026 and has been performing nonstop ever since. Here’s hoping we see more of him and Inde in the future!!
