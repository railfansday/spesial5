For the week nine matchup, two prime NFL teams, the Bengals and Falcons, play against each other for domination on the field. Meanwhile, there might be one or two celebrity sightings during the game too.

The NFL Madrid Game happens on Sunday (Nov. 8) with a matchup featuring the Cincinnati Bengals (5-2) taking on the Atlanta Falcons (3-5) at Santiago Bernabéu in Madrid, Spain, with a start time of 9:30 a.m. ET/6:30 a.m. PT (3:30 p.m. CEST).

The Bengals-Falcons game broadcasts on NFL Network, while the NFL game is available to livestream on ESPN Unlimited.

NFL games are known for celebrity sightings, so it’s likely there may be a number of famous actors, actresses and recording artists in attendance. Artists have been known to appear at games in the past, include Spanish NFL fans Bizarrap, Daddy Yankee, Madrid’s Mayor José Luis Martínez Almeida, former-Real Madrid goalie Iker Casillas and others.

Colombian singer and Latin Grammy Awards-nominee Manuel Turizo performs at halftime, which starts around 11 a.m. ET/8 a.m. PT (5 p.m. CEST).

Plus, Spanish classical guitarist Pablo Sáinz-Villegas is also scheduled to perform the National Anthem of Spain, “La Marcha Granadera,” while Taylor Momsen, lead singer of The Pretty Reckless, is set to perform the American National Anthem, “The Star-Spangled Banner.”
