Taylor Swift Presents Brooks & Dunn With Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award at 2026 CMAs

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The boot-scootin' duo helped mentor a young Swift when she got her start in country music.

The Country Music Association has given out the Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award since 2012. Aside from Nelson earning the inaugural trophy, Kenny Rogers, Johnny Cash (posthumously), Dolly Parton, Kris Kristofferson, Charley Pride, Loretta Lynn, Alan Jackson, George Strait and Vince Gill have received the honor. During Wednesday (Nov. 18) night’s CMA Awards, Brooks & Dunn became the latest recipients of the career-spanning honor. After a surprisingly metal-leaning medley of their hits from Brothers Osborne, Luke Bryan, Halestorm, HARDY and Marcus King, Taylor Swift took the stage to present Brooks & Dunn with the award. “This is given to an artist or group that ascended to incomparable heights in country music,” Swift — whose performance of “I Knew It, I Knew You” earlier in the evening boasted a Brooks & Dunn cameo — explained. “Given that it’s named after Willie Nelson, evidently to win it you also have to have a massive amount of aura points.”

Swift paid tribute to the duo’s outlook on music making, mentoring and life. “They still approach their music with the mischief and excitement of little kids just discovering their love for this. They’ve mentored and supported so many over artists over the years, like me,” she said. “Ronnie [Dunn] will provide you with sage advice and Kix [Brooks] will mercilessly tease you. That’s the thing about Brooks & Dunn — they don’t take very much too seriously. Except for all the important stuff: Their families, their fans and their country music.”

During his acceptance speech, Dunn said that receiving a lifetime achievement award named after Willie Nelson had special resonance for them, as Nelson was a major part of what inspired them to start making their own music.

“Red Headed Stranger, the outlaw album [Wanted! The Outlaws featuring Nelson], it inspired all of us to take off – we were trying to get away with not having to do what our parents were making us do,” Dunn said. He thanked the CMA “from the bottom of our hearts. We don’t take this stuff for granted.”

As if to prove Swift’s point about his merciless teasing, Brooks started his thank-you speech by ribbing Dunn for eating up all the time. “I probably have 15 seconds left, thanks!” he joked. “Willie and Waylon [Jennings], they’re the ones who turned me when I was in college,” he shared of his early inspirations, adding Merle Haggard to the list as well. Listing off some of the award’s past recipients, he ended his speech on a heartfelt note. “Willie and Cash and Kristofferson and Dolly,” he said, sounding incredulous. “These names will never be my peers, our peers. They’re our heroes, y’all.”

Brooks & Dunn have an astonishing 20 No. 1 songs on Billboard‘s Hot Country Songs chart, from “Brand New Man” in 1991 to “Play Something Country” in 2005.
