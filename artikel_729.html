BIGBANG Adds World Tour Finale at L.A.’s SoFi Stadium

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The K-pop veterans will complete their 20th anniversary tour with a one-off show at SoFi Stadium next March.

BIGBANG will bring their world tour to a close with a newly-announced concert next March in Los Angeles.

The K-pop veterans add a one-off show at SoFi Stadium on Saturday, March 13, 2027, for what will be the final date on their extensive 20th anniversary run.

Since getting underway in August, BIGBANG 2026-2027 World Tour ‘XX : COSMOS’ has visited stadiums across Asia, North America, Europe, the United Kingdom and Australia, and is now winding its way through another pan-Asian route. This tour sees G-Dragon, Taeyang and Daesung reunite for the first time since 2017, and for the first time as a three-piece. When the group played MetLife Stadium in September, the setlist ran through 30 songs across about three hours, Billboard’s Jeff Benjamin reported, and included “BiiiG,” BIGBANG’s first new single in four years. “BiiiG” dropped on Aug. 19 to mark 20 years to the day since the group’s debut. Launched by YG Entertainment, BIGBANG arrived in August 2006 and broke new ground for the Korean-pop scene by winning the inaugural best worldwide act trophy onstage at the 2011 MTV Europe Music Awards. The following year, they became the first K-pop group to send an album into the Billboard 200 chart, when 2012’s ALIVE reached No. 150, and strut their live stuff with two world tours (hitting multiple U.S. arenas in 2012 and 2015).

In those 20 years, BIGBANG have landed five No. 1s on Billboard‘s U.S.-based World Digital Song Sales chart, and bagged their first Top 10 hit on the Billboard Global 200 with the special 2022 reunion single “Still Life” that marked T.O.P’s final release with the group.

The general onsale for the SoFi Stadium show opens Friday, Nov. 13 at 10am PT via Ticketmaster.
