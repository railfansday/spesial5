Five names on Juventus’s January striker shortlist: Scamacca back in fashion

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Juventus are on the hunt for a new centre-forward heading into the January transfer window, and Tuesday’s reports indicate that there are four leading candidates, including Atalanta’s Gianluca Scamacca.

Juventus could make several moves in January, including for a new central midfielder and full-back, but a new, physically dominant centre-forward is one of the biggest priorities.

Luciano Spalletti has recently spoken about his need for a traditional no.9 capable of playing with their back to goal, which neither Randal Kolo Muani nor Nick Woltemade really offer.

Tuesday’s reports from both La Gazzetta dello Sport and Tuttosport suggest that Italy international Scamacca is becoming an increasingly viable option heading into the January transfer window.

Scamacca possesses the height and physical characteristics that Spalletti is after, and given the form of Nikola Krstovic this season, his opportunities with Atalanta have been limited in 2026-27. Coupled with a minor thigh injury last month, Scamacca has only played for 37 minutes in Serie A since the beginning of October.

Scamacca is also well known to Juventus CEO Giovanni Carnevali, who worked with him at Sassuolo.

La Gazzetta dello Sport suggests that Scamacca is still a valuable asset to Atalanta, but Tuttosport claim that La Dea have not ruled out the possibility of his departure and that they could even entertain offers of a loan with a conditional obligation to buy in January. An asking price in the region of €30m has been mentioned.

Both outlets suggest that Atletico Madrid’s Alexander Sorloth is also an option. The Corriere dello Sport, meanwhile, claims that the Norwegian is Juventus’s preferred option ahead of the January transfer window.

Juventus have been linked with a move for the Atleti striker on-and-off since the summer, and like Scamacca, he also boasts the physical and positional characteristics that could help to bring the best out of Kolo Muani and Woltemade.

Tuesday’s reports suggest that Atletico are not interested in initial loan offers for Sorloth, though, especially if other sides, such as VfB Stuttgart, are willing to offer a permanent deal this winter.

Though Juventus could offer the permanent services of Jonathan David, currently on loan in Madrid, or Nico Gonzalez, who spent last season on loan under Diego Simeone, a swap deal will be difficult to pull off in January.

Among the other names mentioned in Tuesday’s reports are the in-form Varela, who is likely to fetch a transfer fee in excess of €40m and is attracting interest from some of the biggest clubs all over Europe.

Crystal Palace’s Jean-Phillipe Mateta, who had been linked with a move to Juventus last winter, has also been mentioned as a potential option by La Gazzetta dello Sport.

There have also been suggestions that Joshua Zirkzee could be made available by Manchester United, but there are concerns that the Dutchman is not the traditional no.9 that Spalletti is after.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
