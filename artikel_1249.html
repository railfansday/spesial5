Europa League: Juventus vs. Omonoia – Probable line-ups and where to watch on TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Jeff Ekhator is set to earn his first start for Juventus at centre-forward when a rotated Bianconeri side take on Omonoia in the Europa League on Thursday evening.

Luciano Spalletti’s side remain unbeaten after four matches in the Europa League in 2026-27 with three victories and one draw on their record heading into Thursday’s tie. The Bianconeri find themselves in fifth place on 10 points at the start of the day.

Juventus are coming into Thursday’s game off the back of a 4-0 victory over Venezia in Serie A over the weekend.

Spalletti confirmed during his pre-match press conference on Wednesday that Ekhator is set to play. Back-up goalkeeper Neto joined Spalletti for the pre-match media duties and is expected to start over Guglielmo Vicario, his first outing since rejoining the Bianconeri over the summer.

Francisco Conceicao is back from injuru and could feature on the right wing. Pre-match reports suggest that Jeremie Boga will complete the attacking line-up.

Juventus are still without long-term absentees Kenan Yildiz, Khephren Thuram and Manuel Locatelli. Nico Gonzalez and Pierre Kalulu are closer to returns but are also unavailable on Thursday.

Juventus (3-4-3): Neto; Gatti, Bremer, Lucumi; McKennie, D. Luiz, Koopmeiners, Cambiaso; Conceivable, Ekhator, Boga.

Omonoia (4-3-3): Kaminski; Mayambela, Coulibaly, Šatka, Duverne; Andreou, Tănase, Marić; Ewandro, Diony, Montnor.

Tonight’s game will be available to watch live in the UK on TNT Sports 4 and online through HBO Max. Supporters in the USA are able to tune in via Paramount+.
