Sources: Man Utd Make Adrien Truffert 'Dream Target' for January Transfer

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Manchester United have made Bournemouth star Adrien Truffert their 'dream target' for a January transfer, GIVEMESPORT sources understand.

The Red Devils have had their worst start to a season since 1972 and currently sit 19th in the Premier League table following a 4-0 defeat to Liverpool this past weekend, in Mauricio Pochettino's first game in charge since replacing Michael Carrick as manager.

The team have now turned their attention towards a Champions League clash with Sporting CP on Wednesday night, before a home clash with Brentford at the weekend as they look to turn their campaign around, but there is also an understanding that business needs to be done in the January window.

A striker, centre-back and left-back signing will all be looked at during the window, with limited funds available to the Argentine boss to strengthen his squad in the new year.

Luke Shaw has been absent with injury since the 1-1 draw with Tottenham on October 10th, and Patrick Dorgu has been preferred as a left-wing option this season. Youngster Harry Amass has only featured for 18 minutes, despite the lack of options at left-back.

And while speaking on the latest episode of the Market Madness podcast, GIVEMESPORT senior correspondent Ben Jacobs revealed that there are a number of names on their shortlist, but Cherries star Truffert is someone they consider to be a 'dream' target.

“They will be looking at a striker and then either some form of hybrid defender who can play full-back and centre-back or a full-back and a centre-back.

“Direction of travel at the moment seems to be Calvin Bassey if they can get him, but that’s not going to be easy with Fulham hoping to tie him down to a new contract. There is some appreciation of Nathan Collins, Balde could be available in the winter market.

"One dream target is Truffert, but Bournemouth are not going to want to let many of their stars go.

While a defender is expected to be the priority for Man Utd in January, the club are also keen to bring in a new striker after Benjamin Sesko's injury troubles and the future of Joshua Zirkzee being unclear.

Leeds star Dominic Calvert-Lewin had emerged as a target for the club, but GIVEMESPORT sources understand that the England international is now in advanced talks over signing a new long-term contract at Elland Road.

A move to Old Trafford was always seen as unlikely due to the rivalries between the clubs, but the new contract is expected to formally end Man Utd's interest in the player. Former Brentford star Ivan Toney is a potential alternative should he decide to leave Saudi Arabia, but it's understood that Crystal Palace and Tottenham would likely provide competition for his signature.

United will look to be busy in the window as they look to avoid a relegation battle, and with a defensive rebuild planned for 2027, this window may be seen as a chance to get a headstart on their summer business.
