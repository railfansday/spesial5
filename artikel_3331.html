Lauren Neustadter Exiting Reese Witherspoon’s Hello Sunshine

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Lauren Neustadter, President of Film & Television at Hello Sunshine, is leaving Reese Witherspoon’s company after nearly a decade.

It comes just over a month after French group Mediawan, which backs Chernin Entertainment, Slow Horses producer See-Saw Films and Brad Pitt’s Plan B, acquired the company alongside the Legally Blonde star.

Hello Sunshine also just laid off around half its staff, thought to be around 30 employees, after the deal.

Neustadter, who will work through the end of the day, joined Hello Sunshine shortly after its launch in 2017 and has exec produces series including The Morning Show, which is heading into its fifth and final season, Big Little Lies, which is returning for its third season, Little Fires Everywhere, Elle, From Scratch, Lucky, Surface, The Last Thing He Told Me and Truth Be Told. She also produced Hello Sunshine films including Something from Tiffany’s, Your Place or Mine, You’re Cordially Invited, Where the Crawdads Sing and the upcoming The Nightingale. Watch on Deadline

She joined Hello Sunshine from Fox, where she was SVP, Programming and worked on series including Brooklyn Nine-Nine, The Mindy Project and Lethal Weapon. Prior to that, she worked as a film executive at Miramax and Twentieth Century Fox.

Witherspoon said that she was “proud” of what the pair accomplished together.

“Building Hello Sunshine alongside Reese and our incredible team has been the privilege of a lifetime,” added Neustadter.
