‘SNL’ Cold Open: Jensen Huang, Tech CEOs Get a Crash Course in Female Spies

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Saturday Night Live opened with host John Mulaney instructing a naive group of tech leaders how to tell whether a woman is genuinely interested in them or is a spy trying to extract valuable information from them, as may be the case with ousted Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang.

Mulaney, as a CIA officer, summons Peter Thiel (James Austin Johnson), Sam Altman (Ben Marshall), Dario Amodei (Jane Wickline), Mark Zuckerberg (Andrew Dismukes) and Elon Musk (Jeremy Culhane) to agency headquarters to brief them on a matter of national security.

“America needs you to learn the difference between a woman who would actually want to have sex with you, and a woman who is clearly a spy,” Mulaney says. He then asks the group what the lesson in Huang’s downfall is.

“Never date a Chinese woman,” Amodei says.

“Never date a human woman,” Zuckerberg answers.

“Never date anyone, lest they become the antichrist,” Thiel says.

Mulaney then tries using a Venn diagram to get his point across. The diagram shows two circles that do not overlap one bit: “beautiful women” and “your interests and opinions.”

“So,” he asks the group, “if someone is both of these things, then they are a what?”

When informed that this person would be a spy, Musk goes on one of his unfunny tangents about James Bond, prompting Mulaney to squirt him with a water bottle to get him to shut up.

A second Venn diagram gets introduced after Zuckerberg says that if a woman approached him, he would interpret it as a sign that she “wanted to sleep with the man who created the Metaverse, as all women do.” This diagram also has “beautiful women” in one circle.
