‘Yellowjackets’ Co-Creator Bart Nickerson Hints That Unreleased Bonus Episode Didn’t Fit “Canon Of The Show”

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

As Yellowjackets reaches its final destination after four seasons, many fans are still curious about an episode that never made it to air.

While discussing Season 4 of the supernatural survival thriller, which premieres Nov. 20, co-creators Bart Nickerson and Ashley Lyle shed some light on the unreleased bonus episode for Deadline after it was intended to air following Season 2.

“I mean, ultimately, the canon of the show is sort of what’s aired, and for us, the fourth season is like the final installment of a complete story,” explained Nickerson. “And so, along the way of the making of it, there were lots of paths considered and things like that were explored, but I think ultimately, the final version of the show is what it was supposed to be.”

Lyle first confirmed the bonus episode’s existence on social media in June 2023, days after the Season 2 finale, responding to a fan’s post stating they’re “staying up a couple hours late” in the off chance the then-rumored episode would make a surprise midnight drop. Watch on Deadline

“Noooo. Go to bed! Get some rest!” wrote Lyle on X at the time. “There will be a bonus episode between seasons, but I love you guys and I don’t want y’all to lose sleep thinking it’s tonight.”

Although photos circulated on social media of series star Melanie Lynskey’s husband Jason Ritter filming on set for Season 2 after his casting was announced, the episode never aired, leaving the season with only nine episodes instead of the 10 every other season has received.

Behind-the-scenes images of Ritter led fans to believe his character would be the mysterious “Cabin Guy,” whose skeleton the team discovers in the cabin, along with his plane in the woods. The cabin ultimately burns down in the Season 2 finale episode “Storytelling” after serving as shelter for the winter.

In Yellowjackets, a 1996 New Jersey soccer team’s plane crashes in the Canadian wilderness, where they struggle to survive while they’re stranded for 19 months as a dark supernatural force seemingly lurks in the woods. Meanwhile, their adult counterparts cope with the memories of what went down in the wilderness.

Premiering Nov. 20 on Paramount+ with Premium Plan, the fourth and final season sees the Yellowjackets rescued and returned home, where they must adjust to civilization and take their secrets to the grave.
