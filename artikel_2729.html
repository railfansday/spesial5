Jayden Daniels tosses three TD passes in road win

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Washington Commanders QB Jayden Daniels completed 23 of 33 passes for 249 yards, three touchdowns, and two interceptions against the New York Giants in Week 10. He also ran 10 times for 28 yards.

Why It Matters: The first time Daniels faced the Giants, he hurt them with his legs. On Thursday night, he did the damage with his arm. It wasn't all positives for the mobile quarterback as he once again did little as a runner and tossed his first two interceptions of the season. Still, it was a net positive performance from Daniels, who has proven capable of producing modest QB1 value even without extensive running.
