NFL flexes Raiders-Browns to NBC's Sunday Night Football, Patriots-Chargers out

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

NFL flexes Raiders-Browns to NBC's Sunday Night Football, Patriots-Chargers out originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The league on Tuesday flexed the Las Vegas Raiders-Cleveland Browns Week 12 matchup into NBC's Sunday Night Football slot. It was initially slated for a 1 p.m. ET start.

The New England Patriots–Los Angeles Chargers game that was initially set for primetime will move up to a 4:05 p.m. ET kickoff and will be on CBS.

The NFL can flex games up to 12 days before they are scheduled, though Week 12 caused rarer challenges due to Thanksgiving week featuring more primetime games than usual.

Despite making the Super Bowl last season, the Patriots have regressed after a 5-5 start. Quarterback Drake Maye is also now set for an extended spell on the sidelines, causing further issues for an ailing team.

The Chargers have been one of the surprises of the season, but for all the wrong reasons. Justin Herbert and Co. went winless for their first eight games. They just finally took down a Jacoby Brissett-led Baltimore team to get to 1-8.

Las Vegas will enter the matchup with No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza taking over from Kirk Cousins, who was traded to Minnesota on deadline day. The 4-6 Raiders will be on primetime for the first time this season. Their last SNF game was in 2023.

Cleveland is 6-4 and currently occupies the final AFC playoff spot as Deshaun Watson returns to form. The Browns are 4-0 at home for the first time since 1963, though they've eked out several close wins. Cleveland last hosted an SNF game in 2019.

Its most recent SNF game was in 2021 at Baltimore, and its only other primetime game this season was a Thursday night Week 4 home win vs. Pittsburgh.

The league also announced the Tennessee Titans-Jacksonville Jaguars game will move up to 1 p.m. ET.
