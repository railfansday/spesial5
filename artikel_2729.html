Weston girls swimming continues Class S dominance with its 13th straight championship

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

WEST HARTFORD - Weston girls swimming head coach Brandon Barone felt a little nervous heading into Wednesday afternoon's CIAC Class S championship meet at the Cornerstone Aquatics Center.

That may seem peculiar to those who know about the Trojans' long run of state postseason success. Weston, however, had just gone all-out to win its first South-West Conference championship since 2021. That meet was Nov. 7, and the Class S trials were Nov. 14.

"I was a little bit worried coming into this meet. I was thinking, with the energy, excitement, and the girls wanted to beat Pomperaug. That's one of our rivals," Barone said. "They wanted to get that (SWC crown) back, so the fact that they were able to match it here with such intensity, especially this afternoon. I think, minus the medley relay, we won every heat that we were in for the first (three races)."

Weston won its 13th straight Class S title with 775 points, well ahead of second-place Joel Barlow (403) and third-place New Fairfield (353). It was a terrific couple of days for the South-West Conference: Pomperaug and Brookfield tied for the Class M crown Tuesday.

Weston won five individual events. Senior Ashlynn Fudale won diving with 380.95 points on Nov. 13.

Senior McKenzie Parsons won two individual events and broke the meet record in the 100-yard butterfly with a first-place time of 55.95 seconds. The previous record, 56.02, was set by Seymour's Sierra Cripps in 2018. Parsons also won the 200 freestyle in 1:53.37 and was part of the winning 200 freestyle relay (1:40.09) and 400 freestyle relay (3:36.15).

The 200 freestyle relay consisted of Parsons, junior Elizabeth Akolzina, junior Asha Douglass and senior Meghan Leahy. The 400 freestyle relay team consisted of Parsons, Akolzina, senior Abigaile Mais and Leahy.

Mais won the 200 IM in 2:14.06, and Leahy won the 100 breaststroke in 1:05.80. Weston earned eight individual medals.

Leahy finished second in the 50 freestyle. Akolzina placed third in the 100 freestyle and fifth in the 50 freestyle, while freshman Avery Mihelic finished third in the 100 breaststroke. Senior Lily Fournier placed fourth in diving.

When asked what keeps Weston at the top, Barone said: "It's really the culture year in and year out. The girls know that we're looking to win this meet. Everything is for championships going into the season. That cohesion across them. They may not all be best friends, but they're all going to come in and do their job, day in and day out, for each other, and just keeping that going all through the years."

Lauralton Hall sophomore Elle Massey was a double winner, taking the 50 freestyle in 24.04 and the 100 freestyle in 52.58. Windsor Locks Co-op junior Brianna Laforest won the 500 freestyle in 5:17.59, and Joel Barlow junior Makayla Pedersen won the 100 backstroke in 58.34. Pedersen also was part of Barlow's winning 200 medley relay (1:52.86), along with freshman Savanna Jensen, freshman Addison Sopko and sophomore Lilly Friedman.
