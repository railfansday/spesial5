WATCH: Kurt Busch’s Emotional Ad For Coverland Seat Covers

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Kurt Busch has a new commercial that was released on Monday, and it is one NASCAR fans do not want to miss. The NASCAR Hall of Famer partnered up with Coverland, an automotive accessory brand, to promote the company's seat covers.

The commercial was an emotional one as it tapped into Kurt's on-track success and made references to the loss of his brother, two-time NASCAR Cup Series champion Kyle Busch.

On Monday, Kurt Busch took to social media to share his newest commercial promoting Coverland seat covers.

"The road isn't always a straight line, and I've never been one to stop chasing what's next. More laps ahead.

The advertisement sees Busch walking on an unknown race course and strapping into a sports car with Coverland seat covers. The 2004 Cup Series champion is seen taking laps around the track. During the advertisement, a voiceover of Busch can be heard talking about his competitiveness behind the wheel.

Here is everything Busch said in the commercial.

"Speed isn't something you find. It's something you earn. I knew that from the first lap I ever ran. All I knew was fast. It wasn't a straight line to get here. Some people, I'd give every trophy to have one more lap with. But you don't give up. You keep going and you get back out there."

"I never stopped chasing greatness. No matter what you do in life, make every moment matter. Because everyone is capable of something great. Greatness isn't about winning. It's about a legacy worth protecting. Coverland seat covers, protect what matters."

Busch saying "some people, I'd give every trophy to have one more lap with" was a reference to his brother, Kyle Busch. On May 21 of this year, Kyle Busch passed away suddenly at age 41 due to complications of pneumonia.

Kyle Busch died three days before the Coca-Cola 600 at the Charlotte Motor Speedway, NASCAR's longest race of the year.

Prior to the green flag for the race, the sport took a moment to recognize one of the greatest drivers in history. Older brother Kurt, who was in attendance, laid eight flowers down on a painted No. 8 on the infield grass honoring his younger brother.

Kurt also appeared on "Today" and in an exclusive interview with CNN's Anderson Cooper to discuss the tragic moments of his younger brother's death.
