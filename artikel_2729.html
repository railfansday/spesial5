Investors led by U.S. billionaire Todd Boehly to buy Lukoil's oil and gas assets outside Russia, sources say

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Investors led by U.S. billionaire Todd Boehly's asset management company Eldridge have struck a deal to buy Lukoil's assets outside of Russia, sources confirmed to CNBC.

The U.S. International Development Finance Corporation and Catena Investments, based in the United Arab Emirates, are also parties to the deal, the sources said.

The deal transfers Lukoil's international refineries and other production assets in Bulgaria, Romania, Africa and the Middle East to the U.S.-led group, the sources said. It also includes retail gas stations in the U.S., Europe and Asia, they said.

The U.S. Office of Foreign Assets Control is expected to sign off on the deal but final approval is still needed, the sources said. The deal would give the U.S.-led group about 350,000 barrels per day of production.

The agreement would allow Europe to buy fuel such as diesel previously produced by Lukoil through the U.S.-led group rather than Russia.
