What Missouri coach Eli Drinkwitz said after another loss to Georgia

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Georgia Bulldogs locked up with the Missouri Tigers during SEC play in Week 11 and the Bulldogs dominated the Tigers from start to finish in all facets of the game. The Missouri Tigers haven't defeated the Georgia Bulldogs since 2013 and that win came in Athens at Sanford Stadium.

Missouri ranks 57th in the country when it comes to points per game at 29.9 and they are tied-55th for points against at 22.9 per game. The Tigers are averaging 249.3 yards per game which is ranked 62nd. The rushing offense ranks at 73rd with 156.6 rushing yards per game.

Missouri head football coach Eli Drinkwitz had a lot of praise for the Georgia Bulldogs and what they have been able to do in the SEC. Missouri has had a great season so far despite playing with a bit of inconsistency. The Bulldogs reminded them of just how far they are from being an elite at a consistent basis in the SEC. Eli Drinkwitz has done an outstanding job as head coach for the Tigers but still has a lot of work to do in order to get his team where it needs to be to compete with the likes of the Georgia Bulldogs.

Drinkwitz knows that if his team has any chance of beating a team like Georgia, they have to be able to put together drives and not allow their defense to stay on the field for long periods of time. Especially being on the road, you have to be able to move the ball and allow your defense to breathe in order to make plays at a consistent rate.

"You're on the road, you got to have some sustained drives early to give yourself a chance...defense on the field the entire first quarter," Drinkwitz said.

Since the Florida game, Georgia has turned up it's offense several notches. The Bulldogs showcased against the Missouri Tigers just how good their offense really is. The Bulldogs got creative offensively at times which threw the Tigers off defensively.

"They got us a couple of times on one on ones. I thought that was a great back shoulder catch on Sione and obviously the sale route, they got us on the third down across the middle, they got us on a tight end wheel. I mean, they did a great job of scheming us up," Drinkwitz said.

Drinkwitz stated, "Giving up 286 yards passing is never going to be a recipe for success."

The Bulldogs had their way offensively and with how the Georgia defense has been playing, it makes it extremely difficult to defend and contain a team like Georgia when they are clicking on all cylinders.

The Georgia Bulldogs have been dominant for most of the season up to this point and everyone in the country is looking to Georgia as the measuring stick for their own team's success. Drinkwitz praised Kirby Smart and his Georgia Bulldogs for how they have been able to win at a high level on a consistent basis.

"They are a good football team. They're the number one team in the country. They've done this to a lot of people that come in here," Drinkwitz said.

Drinkwitz made it clear that Georgia is the standard in the SEC and in all of the country when it comes to the college football landscape by giving credit to Kirby Smart and his program.

Drinkwitz stated, "The benchmark for the league right now in the way that coach Smart runs his program, the philosophical identity that he has in offense, defense, and special teams."
