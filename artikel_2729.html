NFL predictions: New York Jets vs Los Angeles Chargers - Battle of the Bottom Dwellers

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The New York Jets are on the road in Los Angeles, California to take on the Los Angeles Chargers in the Jets' 11th game of the 2026 season this Sunday. The Jets will be looking to get back on the winning track after dropping eight of their last nine games.

These two teams last met in a 27 – 6 Chargers victory in 2023. The Chargers have won five straight against the Jets and seven of the last nine. The Chargers lead the overall series 24 – 14 – 1.

The Chargers come into this game fresh off their first victory of the season, a 23 – 20 squeaker over the Baltimore Ravens on Monday Night Football. The Chargers are one of three one win teams in the NFL this season, along with the Miami Dolphins and the Tennessee Titans. They have been weighed down by atrocious quarterback play from the usually reliable Justin Herbert. Herbert has not meshed with the offense brought in by Mike McDaniel. The Chargers rank last in the NFL in points scored, last in the NFL in offensive turnovers, and bottom five in passing yards, passing touchdowns and interceptions. It has been a rough year for the Chargers offense, but they have had some success running the ball, ranking 10th in the NFL in rushing yards.

On defense the Chargers have struggled against the pass while doing a decent job against the run.

Let's make a few predictions for the game on Sunday.

Omarion Hampton rushes for 90+ yards and a touchdown against a Jets defense that struggles mightily against the run.

Justin Herbert is held under 200 yards passing.

The Jets sack Herbert 4+ times and force at least one Herbert turnover.

Geno Smith puts up 250+ passing yards and 2+ touchdowns.

The Jets beat the Chargers in a tight game, the victory sealed by a late Chargers turnover.
