Eagles News: Where does Joe Milton fit in Philadelphia’s future?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Welcome to The Linc, the daily Philadelphia Eagles news and links roundup at Bleeding Green Nation!

We'll learn more about Joe Milton and how the Eagles value him (or not) over the rest of the season. He's set to be a restricted free agent after the season. If they're of the mind he can legitimately contend for QB2, they could offer him a right of first refusal tender valued around $3.8 million. Other teams would then have the ability to beat that price to sign him to an offer sheet, which the Eagles could then decide to match or not. Or they could try to sign him to an extension to avoid the RFA process altogether. What do you make of how he's played thus far and do you have interest in him being back in 2027? A strong showing in a win over the Dallas Cowboys today would certainly help his case.

Let's get to the Philadelphia Eagles news and links …

In Roob's Eagles Observations: Why tanking doesn't make sense in the NFL – NBCSP

JOE MILTON PART I: Joe Milton has not been great so far, but considering his lack of practice time in this offense and with these receivers and behind this offensive line and also considering his lack of NFL experience, I think he's done as well as you could expect. What's interesting to me is where he fits into the Eagles' future. He'll be a restricted free agent after the season and I would expect the Eagles to sign him to a bargain contract extension to make sure he's here next year, get him a full offseason in whatever offense whoever the head coach and offensive coordinator are running and begin 2027 with him as Jalen Hurts' backup … assuming Hurts is healthy and ready to go. Obviously, the Eagles like Milton more than Tanner McKee, and McKee will be unrestricted anyway and will likely be somewhere else in 2027. It doesn't feel like Cole Payton is going to be ready to be No. 2 next year so Milton makes the most sense. And I'd like to see what that looks like with OTAs, summer throwing with the WRs, training camp and a preseason. The kid clearly has talent. He just needs time on task. Will be interesting to see what he looks like when he's not coming in cold in the middle of a season with a bunch of guys he doesn't know.

Eagles' offensive changes produced less than encouraging results – BGN

There were significant changes on how the Eagles ran their offense, from pre-snap motion, to when they snapped the ball, and the formations they used. Since Joe Milton became the starting quarterback, the Eagles were trending in this direction. The results, unfortunately, have been more of the same. The Eagles can't control the clock, they've been unable to stay on the field, and struggle to score points. This was with Saquon Barkley healthier (for a half at least) and DeVonta Smith back. Sean Mannion's offense still failed to score 20 points and gain 250 yards. This was with the changes made — and how Philadelphia has been trending in that direction — over the last two weeks. There have been some positives with the changes, but the results have been what we're accustomed to.

NFL Week 12 picks: Upset and score predictions, matchup breakdowns for every game – NFL.com

Why Bobby picked the Cowboys: The Eagles mercifully got off the schneid in Week 11 against the Steelers, ending a seven-game losing streak, but the way they won doesn't seem replicable against a scorching Cowboys squad. Philadelphia downed Pittsburgh by scoring 13 first-quarter points and then holding on for dear life for a 19-16 win. That likely won't cut it against Dallas, considering Dak Prescott has accounted for 10 touchdowns over the past two games while leading the offense to 91 points. Joe Milton III hasn't performed consistently enough to keep up with any scoring output remotely similar to that, and the Cowboys already proved capable of such an onslaught while carving up the Eagles earlier this season for 38 points and 416 yards (204 of which came on the ground) in a 17-point win. That emphatic victory took place in Philadelphia. Now, the Cowboys get their NFC East rivals at home.

Eagles-Cowboys predictions: Dallas scored 91 points in two games. Why are our writers split? – The Athletic

Berman: The Eagles defense demonstrated improvement in recent games. The Cowboys offense is scorching — 91 points in two games! — and I'm focused less on the Eagles defense in this matchup and more on the offense. Can the Eagles score points? They haven't scored more than 24 points this season. They've averaged 15 points in five games without Jalen Hurts. I don't see them winning 19-16, and I'm not sold on this team scoring into the high 20s and 30s. That makes me lean toward the Cowboys, who are 8.5-point home favorites. Is there a chance Milton has a career day on national television and Saquon Barkley explodes for 100-plus yards, a la LeSean McCoy on Thanksgiving in 2014? Sure. But I'm not predicting it. Cowboys 28, Eagles 20 […] Kubena: I put more stock in the Cowboys putting up 40 points on the San Francisco 49ers than I do in the 51 they hung on the Tennessee Titans, who are reaching new lows after trading All-Pro defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons (who played against the Eagles in Week 2). I also thought the Eagles showed progress offensively against the Steelers. They scored on their first three drives, and Milton's second-half misses were the main reasons that game didn't become a blowout. I expect the Eagles to build on Milton's third start (and second with DeVonta Smith in the lineup) and field their first game with a positive offensive EPA since — you guessed it — they played the Cowboys in Week 7. Thanksgiving Game Preview at Cowboys: Six storylines to follow – PhiladelphiaEagles.com

4) The Joe Milton Show. The young quarterback makes his third NFL start after beating the Steelers and again making positive strides. What is the next step for Milton, who has used his legs to make plays as well as his impressive arm? Could the Eagles design some running plays for Milton to break down the Dallas defense? "I think Joe's skill set is unique in terms of his athletic ability. I think both within truly schemed plays, and then he made a nice off-schedule play on a third-and-long," Offensive Coordinator Sean Mannion said. "So, I think it's an asset for us. I think he can be additive that way, but each week we're always going to try to build the best plan possible. Some of it might be with the QB run game and some of it might look a little bit differently. So, it's a weekly challenge each week. He's got an athletic skill set both in the scripted opportunities with true QB runs and then also with the off-schedule."

NY Giants injury news: Abdul Carter should be 'fine' despite missing practice – Big Blue View

Defensive Player of the Week Abdul Carter did not practice Wednesday for the New York Giants due to what the team reported as an ankle/calf issue. Giants head coach John Harbaugh said that Carter is "day-to-day" and expects him to be "fine" for Sunday's game against the Indianapolis Colts. Carter is coming off the best game of his career, with 2.0 sacks and 12 pressures against the Jacksonville Jaguars.
