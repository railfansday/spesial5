Player Grades: Lakers vs. Jazz

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

After stringing together a series of great nights and victories, the Lakers finally saw their winning streak come to an end on Wednesday.

Not every night can be perfect and, unfortunately for the purple and gold, nearly every Laker had an off night at the same time. The result was a loss to a two-win Jazz team, making it the ugliest loss of the season — albeit a short list — right after one of the best wins of the year.

These types of performances happen in an 82-game season. The challenge will be learning and bouncing back from it. But it doesn't make it any less frustrating in the moment.

So, let's dive into the loss. As always, grades are based on expectations for each player. A "B" grade represents the average performance for that player.

21 minutes, 2 points, 1 rebound, 3 assists, 4 steals, 1 block, 1 turnover, 1-4 FG, 0-2 3PT, -12

Another great night defensively for Thybulle, but he's really testing the limits of how little he can do offensively and still be seen as a positive.

18 minutes, 7 points, 2 rebounds, 4 fouls, 1 turnover, 2-3 FG, 1-2 3PT, 2-2 FT, -23

Another night where he gets into foul trouble. It's not clear why he was moved back into the starting lineup but he didn't make a great impression to stay there.

30 minutes, 10 points, 11 rebounds, 1 assist, 2 fouls, 1 turnover, 3-7 FG, 4-10 FT, -7

In his first game against the Jazz, Kessler said he let his emotions get the best of him. It felt like that moment was in the rearview mirror and it wouldn't be a big thing playing them moving forward. Well, tonight was another bad game against them. Good thing the Lakers play them one more time next week and no more the rest of the season.

35 minutes, 20 points, 7 rebounds, 4 assists, 2 fouls, 1 turnover, 8-20 FG, 4-10 3PT, -10

It wasn't an efficient game from Grimes, but he was still one of the best players in purple and gold. If you're looking for a positive takeaway, though, the hesitation on what to do when the ball is swung to him is gone. Now he just has to make the shots.

36 minutes, 29 points, 5 rebounds, 7 assists, 2 steals, 3 fouls, 9 turnovers, 10-28 FG, 2-12 3PT, 7-7 FT, -18

I don't feel nearly as bad about giving Luka a bad grade here. Those audacious threes he made earlier in the year aren't falling and on a night when the Lakers needed some kind of life offensively, they felt especially costly when they missed.

20 minutes, 6 points, 3 rebounds, 1 foul, 1-10 FG, 0-4 3PT, 4-4 FT, -4

Mamu is in quite the nasty slump at this point, which is especially unfortunate considering it comes on the heels really seeming to have figured things out.

30 minutes, 4 points, 6 rebounds, 2 assists, 3 steals, 2 fouls, 1 turnover, 2-3 FG, -3

This was a fairly standard LaRavia game. He stuffed the stat sheet, turned down an open three and hustled around the floor.

12 minutes, 5 rebounds, 3 assists, 1 steal, 1 foul, 0-1 FG, 0-2 FT, -9

When the rest of the offense isn't working, Vando's lack of offense feels a bit more noticeable. It's unfair to Vando because he's just not going to provide much on that end, but the Lakers just had a host of wings who couldn't score on Wednesday.

It's also harder to have him on the court when you're playing from behind and need points.

19 minutes, 12 points, 1 assist, 2 fouls, 3 turnovers, 4-8 FG, 1-2 3PT, 3-3 FT, -9

This is becoming something of the norm for Sexton. He'll give you low double-digit points, get to the line a couple times and make a couple loud errors.

Thiero got one shift in the first half where he made a nice play on a steal and assist but did little else. The rest of the players only got in during garbage time.

The move from LaRavia to Williams in the starting lineup is a real head-scratcher. Williams played well in that role to start the year but nothing about LaRavia's recent play felt like it warranted being removed from the starting lineup.

It didn't lose the team the game as the players did that themselves. But it set them back.

Wednesday's inactives: Austin Reaves, Arthur Kaluma, Chris Mañon
