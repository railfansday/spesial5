What NFL games are on today? Week 10 TV channels, Sunday schedule, results

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The NFL Week 10 schedule is filled with the sort of Sunday matchups that could transform the league's playoff picture.

It starts with the first game of the day in the morning when the Detroit Lions face the New England Patriots in Munich, Germany and continues all the way through to a rematch between the Pittsburgh Steelers and Cincinnati Bengals with first place in the AFC North on the line.

Between those two games, the Minnesota Vikings and Green Bay Packers will play in a crucial NFC North matchup during the early afternoon window, and the San Francisco 49ers and Dallas Cowboys get a national broadcast spotlight as part of Sunday's late afternoon kickoff.
