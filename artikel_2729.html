Broncos vs. Raiders score predictions for Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

After a long two weeks, the Denver Broncos will be back on the field and at home to host the Las Vegas Raiders in Week 11. After a fast start, the Raiders season faded rapidly where they ended up trading away Kirk Cousins to finish the year with the number one overall pick from this year's draft, Fernando Mendoza. He'll have his first road start in one of the most hostile environments in the NFL.

This is a game the Broncos should win by double-digits, but this team also tends to play up or down to their opponent that day. However, coming off a bye week, I think they will be ready to roll on Sunday and finally give us a comfortable margin of victory.
