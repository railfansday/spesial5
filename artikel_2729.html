Skip Bayless pours cold water on Troy Aikman-Cowboys reunion: ex-QB ‘would be a threat’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Skip Bayless isn't buying the idea that Jerry Jones could bring in Troy Aikman to help him run the Cowboys.

On the "Double Coverage Podcast," Bayless commented on host Zach Hirsch's suggestion that Jones might consider bringing on Aikman, Deion Sanders or Jason Witten to help lead the franchise.

"The problem with everything you just said is all three of those names would be threats to Jerry's stature," Bayless said. "He's the face of the franchise. He's the most famous or infamous owner in all of sports. And he loves to sit up there on his throne peering down on his Cowboys and all the TV close-ups, and he just doesn't want a threat. Troy, Troy would be a threat."

ESPN journalist Skip Bayless attends the IAVA 7th Annual Heroes Gala at Cipriani 42nd Street in New York City, Nov. 12, 2013. Michael Loccisano

The rumblings come as Aikman's broadcasting future remains in question. He remains without an extension with ESPN to call "Monday Night Football," though longtime partner Joe Buck signed a massive deal earlier this year, which stirred reports of a feud between the pair.

ESPN seems willing to move on from Aikman after this season, which will end after the network airs the Super Bowl for the first time in its history.

Aikman has often expressed interest in taking on a front office role and an eagerness to go back to the Cowboys. Jones recently said that interest in working with his former quarterback is reciprocated.

However, Jones' and Aikman's clashing philosophies on running a team have shown in the past. Jones has minimized the importance of a big investment in a head coach, while Aikman recently said that should he become an executive, he would immediately offer the 49ers anything they wanted to get Kyle Shanahan.

Joe Buck, left, and Troy Aikman look on before an NFC Wild Card playoff game between the Dallas Cowboys and Tampa Bay Buccaneers in Tampa, Fla., Jan. 16, 2023. Getty Images

It wouldn't be Aikman's first experience in a front office, as he's worked for the Dolphins as a consultant since January. Aikman assisted in the search for Miami's new general manager and head coach.

Rumors of a rift between Aikman and Bayless have swirled in the past, especially following the release of Bayless's book and his hiring by Fox Sports in 2016, but in 2024, Bayless said their relationship was "great" and the two spoke regularly.
