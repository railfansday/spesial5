Patriots currently expect QB Drake Maye to be out "a while"

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Patriots quarterback Drake Maye left Sunday’s game against the Lions in Germany after the first drive, due to an ankle injury. And while the specific nature of the injury has yet to be diagnosed via the available imaging technologies, the organization is bracing for not-great news.

Per a source with knowledge of the situation, the current expectation is that Maye will be out “a while.”

They have a bye in Week 11, before facing the Chargers in L.A. on Sunday night in Week 12. (That game could be flexed out of prime time in the coming days.)

After that, the Patriots host the Bills in Week 13 and the Vikings in Week 14. In Week 15, they face the Chiefs on Monday night in Kansas City.

The season ends with a game at the Jets, followed by home games against the Broncos and Dolphins.

The Patriots are currently 5-5. They’d miss the playoffs, if the playoffs started today.

Without Maye, the starter is Tommy DeVito and the backup is Behren Morton.
