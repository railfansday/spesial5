Shannon Sharpe gives his verdict on SEC potentially kicking LSU out of the conference

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Shannon Sharpe believes the SEC's decision to kick LSU out of the Conference is intended to send a statement for breaching rules.

It was announced on Monday that the SEC is threatening to hold a vote among the Chancellors and Presidents to remove LSU from the Conference after they violated a rule.

LSU broke the Conference rule by allowing RJ Luis Jr. to play for the Tigers' basketball team in the game against the UL Monroe. Luis played for the Boston Celtics in the NBA preseason last season, and according to the SEC's policies, teams are not allowed to roster players from professional leagues, including the NBA.

This is the second violation by LSU, as they previously broke it when Lane Kiffin tried to recruit tight end Dae'Quan Wright and defensive lineman Zxavian Harris, who went undrafted in the NFL to the Tigers. Both of these players spent time on the training camp rosters of NFL teams, which is why the SEC was against the decision to allow them to compete in the College Football season.

Sharpe believes the SEC's decision to potentially remove LSU from the Conference is to try and set a precedent.

SEC Commissioner Greg Sankey has reportedly announced a special meeting on December 3, to discuss the possible termination of LSU from the Conference.

To move ahead with the motion, the Conference needs the votes of two-thirds of the other 15 Presidents.

"Well, I understand that part. The man shouldn't have played. He played in the NBA. You shouldn't be able to come to college and play after you've played in the league. I agree with that. I think they deserve punishment, but to kick them out of the conference, that's a little too much." Joe Johnson said on The Nightcap Show.

The SEC put out a statement to announce the official date of the meeting and what led to the Conference to decide to take an extreme step as such.

According to a Louisiana State Court, the SEC was barred from penalizing LSU for allowing Luis Jr. to play. However, the Conference took the bold step, which has now led them to face a suit from him.

Shannon Sharpe believes, the Conference is standing its ground to send a statement, as it wasn't the first time LSU was trying something like this.

"I think this is something to get their attention. It'll get all the members' attention because, remember, they said, 'Oh, y'all can't.' They wanted to play those football guys. Remember those football guys who went to the NFL? They didn't make the team, but they were on the roster during the pre-season and came back," said Sharpe.

"They had to threaten them. The team said, "Look, we're not playing y'all. We're just going to say, 'Hey, we're not playing y'all.'" And then LSU backed down. And I'll be damned, lo and behold, you get a basketball player and you do the same thing. So clearly the SEC threatening y'all is going in one ear and out the other."

A meeting was expected to take place to consider removing LSU in September as well, but after Lane Kiffin decided to remove them from his roster, the Conference stopped pushing hard. However, this time, the Tigers went ahead and played him in a game, where he contributed 11 points. Although he has been removed from the team, the SEC has a decision to make if they want to make themselves known, as removing LSU would be a bold step, considering they are founding members of the Conference.
