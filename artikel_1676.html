Opinion: Liverpool ready to sign their next Jeremy Jacquet, he's perfect

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Jeremy Jacquet has been a revelation at Liverpool.

The young Frenchman was brought in for around £55 million with plenty of fans, pundits and journalists all saying that Liverpool had spent too much on a player who'd never played outside of France.

The fact that he was only 20 years old didn't help matters much either.

However, to the surprise of many, he took becoming a Liverpool player in his stride and has arguably been one of their best performers this term.

He's far better than Ibrahima Konate ever was and, he's still got another decade left as a top-level player.

So, the fact that Liverpool are ready to sign their next version of him is rather exciting.

Liverpool have entered the transfer race for Giorgio Scalvini

Liverpool's defence may have had a decent chunk of change spent on it over the last couple of years but, there are still a couple of problem areas that they need to fix.

For example, both Kostas Tsimikas and Milos Kerkez have massively underperformed this season.

Every time one of them is played, fans want the other to come back in and take their place.

Obviously, this isn't ideal considering Kerkez was expected to be the Reds' next Andy Robertson.

However, the centre of their defence also has a couple of question marks hanging over it thanks to the undecided futures of Virgil van Dijk and Joe Gomez.

So, Liverpool being linked with Giorgio Scalvini is hardly a surprised, especially with how much potential he has to fulfil.

"Manchester United, Liverpool and Chelsea are positioning themselves to make a possible move for Atalanta defensive star Giorgio Scalvini, with the highly-rated Italian emerging as a major potential transfer target for 2027, TEAMtalk can exclusively reveal," TEAMtalk wrote.

"TEAMtalk understands that a number of English sides are now carrying out detailed work on Scalvini as the possibility of him finally leaving Atalanta gathers momentum.

"Newcastle United are long-term admirers of the Italy international, but the latest interest is coming from Manchester United, Liverpool and Chelsea, who have all been monitoring his development.

"United and Liverpool are both assessing their central defensive options as they consider potential reinforcements, with Scalvini increasingly emerging as a player of interest to their recruitment departments."

Giorgio Scalvini could become Liverpool's next Jeremy Jacquet

Scalvini has been linked with an exit from Atalanta for a fair few years now.

Ever since he broke into their first team back in the 2021/22 season, the Italian has been of interest to many different clubs around the globe.

However, now that Liverpool have proven that they're giving youngsters a go in their starting XI with their use of Jacquet and others such as Trey Nyoni, they have more ground to stand on than other clubs.

Of course, Scalvini is a tad older than Jacquet at 22 years old but, since they're both young and have a lot of their careers left to go, it's clear to see why the Italian could become Liverpool's next Jacquet.

He also plays as the right-sided centre-back in a back four so, he'd fit straight into Andoni Iraola's plans if they chose to bring him in.
