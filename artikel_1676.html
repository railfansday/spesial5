Kompany and Bayern relishing ‘tough’ Bundesliga title race

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Vincent Kompany is ready for a "tough title race" with Bayern Munich, who are looking to leapfrog Borussia Dortmund when they travel to Hamburg in the Bundesliga.

After 10 games, it is Dortmund who are setting the pace at the top of the table, sitting two points ahead of Bayern, who are in action before their rivals this weekend.

Bayern remain unbeaten in the top flight this term (W6 D4) but have alternated between wins and draws in their last four outings, drawing 1-1 with Koln last weekend.

They also come into this match on the back of a 2-1 loss to Lille in the Champions League, having thrown away a lead in the second half.

But Kompany’s focus is firmly back on the Bundesliga, as he knows it is important to get back to winning ways quickly.

"I'm always ready for a tough title race – from the very beginning," Kompany told a press conference.

"Leverkusen got 90 points [in 2023-24]. That's a tough title race for any generation of Bayern team.

"Only once would we have won the title in that scenario. We set the record with 91 points.

"If another team is that good, then so be it. But our thoughts are still only about what we can influence."

Bayern are struggling with injuries at the moment, with Harry Kane and Michael Olise two of their biggest absentees.

They face a Hamburg side who are 12th in the Bundesliga and coming into this game on the back of a crushing 4-0 defeat to Werder Bremen.

But it is a special match for Kompany, who spent two seasons at Hamburg between 2006 and 2008 before joining Manchester City.

"It'll always be a special game for me," Kompany said. "I don't know why. I wasn't there that long, but the city and the people do something to you.

"But we've got extra motivation to get three points, because we've got our challenges at the moment. So, the match isn't just emotional because of my history, but also for the history of our team.

"We want to go to Hamburg with our fans and represent something that makes us feel we're doing everything we can.

"And as for the things over which we have no control, we won't be able to influence them."

Last time out at home in the Bundesliga, Hamburg won 3-1 against Borussia Monchengladbach, with Gronbeak getting a goal and an assist in that match.

He has a team-high four assists in the top flight this term, while only Miro Muheim (15) has created more chances than the Dane (14).

Upamecano has not lost any of his last 54 Bundesliga matches – only Jerome Boateng has had a longer such run in the competition's history, with 56 between 2012 and 2014.

Among all centre-backs, he has also recorded the second-most ball recoveries in the Bundesliga this season (61, behind Tjark Scheller's 65).

Hamburg drew 2-2 in their last Bundesliga home match against Bayern in January, but they are winless in their last 19 Bundesliga matches against them (D4 L15).

It is the longest winless run against any opponent in Hamburg's Bundesliga history. Their last victory against Bayern was a 1-0 home win in September 2009.

Hamburg have won three consecutive Bundesliga home games for the first time since returning to the top flight in 2025; only RB Leipzig currently have a longer home winning streak in the league (10).

But Bayern are unbeaten in their last 31 Bundesliga away matches (W23 D8) – they have only had one longer unbeaten run on the road in the competition, a 33-game streak between 2012 and 2014.

With 22 points from 10 matches, Bayern are having their weakest Bundesliga season in four years after 2022-23 (19 points). They have kept just two clean sheets in their first 10 Bundesliga matches this season; the last time they had as few at this stage was in 2020-21 under Hansi Flick.
