Aston Villa injury, suspension news and return dates for Ipswich Town clash: Amadou Onana, Matty Cash, Matteo Ruggeri latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

By Matt Law | 25 Nov 2026 09:36, Last updated: 25 Nov 2026 09:36

Aston Villa are back in Premier League action on Saturday away to Ipswich Town.

Unai Emery's side are currently on a six-game winless run in all competitions, and they sit 12th in the Premier League table on 12 points.

Here, Sports Mole rounds up Villa’s latest injury and suspension news ahead of their clash with Ipswich, who are down in 16th spot in the division.

Onana ruptured his anterior cruciate ligament while representing Belgium at the 2026 World Cup, and the midfielder remains a long-term absentee for Villa.

Possible return date: November 28 (vs. Ipswich Town)

Cash has recently been absent after suffering a concussion while representing Portugal during the international break, but the defender could be back in the squad this weekend.

Possible return date: December 2 (vs. Everton)

Ruggeri remains sidelined for Villa due to a hamstring injury, but the defender could be back in the squad for the game against Everton on December 2.

Villa have no suspended players for this match.
