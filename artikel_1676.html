Liverpool XI vs Everton: Predicted lineup, confirmed team news, injury latest for Premier League

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Liverpool are full of confidence as they travel to face rivals Everton in the latest Merseyside derby on Sunday.

Andoni Iraola’s surging Reds have been piling up the goals since the November international break - scoring seven times across their last two fixtures alone.

A hugely satisfying 4-0 dismantling of Manchester United at Anfield last weekend in what was Mauricio Pochettino’s first match in charge of the visitors was quickly followed by a 3-0 victory in the Champions League away at Club Brugge.

Their two statement signings from last summer - Florian Wirtz and Alexander Isak - are both hitting top form, which bodes well for this weekend’s derby showdown.

Iraola will likely already know his starting XI for the short trip to Everton, based on the strong form of his team.

Stalwart Alisson will start in goal, while Virgil van Dijk will return to captain the side after being rested in Belgium on Wednesday night.

Ronald Araujo will be shifted to right-back as it is unlikely that Jeremie Frimpong will be asked to play twice in the span of five days following injury.

Jeremy Jacquet will partner Van Dijk in central defence, while Milos Kerkez looks set to make his 90th appearance under Iraola - the left-back having been trusted by his head coach at both Liverpool and Bournemouth.

Dominik Szoboszlai seems to have put his injury woes in the past, and while young Trey Nyoni has impressed when given opportunities this term, Alexis Mac Allister will surely get the nod alongside him in the midfield engine room as Ryan Gravenberch remains a doubt with ongoing heel pain.

The aforementioned Wirtz is playing the best football of his Liverpool career to date; Rio Ngumoha and Bradley Barcola look set to line up either side of the German, with Victor Munoz forced off injured against Brugge to join Cody Gakpo in the treatment room.
