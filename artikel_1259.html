Leeds Need to Tie Farke Down After Tanaka and Calvert-Lewin Agreements

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Leeds United are closing in on two important agreements as they look to progress in their second season back in the Premier League.

GIVEMESPORT exclusively revealed on Tuesday evening that Dominic Calvert-Lewin is close to signing a new deal to stay at Elland Road, having been a huge Leeds success following his free transfer move from Everton in the summer of 2025.

The 29-year-old has cemented his spot in Thomas Tuchel's England squad in recent months, and he has come off of the substitutes' bench in three of the Three Lions' previous six fixtures, including the famous win in Spain during the last international break.

Calvert-Lewin has been in fine goalscoring form for the Whites, which has led to interest from rivals Manchester United, as previously revealed by GMS sources.

The Red Devils are looking to sign a new centre-forward to compete with Benjamin Sesko following injury issues for the Slovenia international this term, but they won't be getting the Englishman with talks advanced over a new deal.

Meanwhile, Leeds are also in advanced discussions over extending the contract of Japan international Ao Tanaka, who has become one of their most important players following their return to England's top flight.

While the front two of Calvert-Lewin and Noah Okafor were brilliant in Saturday's 4-1 win against Chelsea at Stamford Bridge, Tanaka ran the show in midfield behind them and is fully deserving of a new deal in Yorkshire.

Tying the duo down is a big boost as Leeds look to secure a top-half finish this term, having comfortably escaped a relegation battle in the final weeks of last season.

Next, they need to finalise an even more important agreement with manager Daniel Farke.

Farke has sometimes been a bit of a divisive manager for the Elland Road faithful, especially after losing the 2024 Championship playoff final, and at points last season following their promotion to the Premier League.

There would have been some supporters at times last season who would have believed that the German wouldn't remain in charge of them for the long-term in Yorkshire, but he has ultimately done an outstanding job this term.

The 49ers backed the 50-year-old with some big summer signings like James Trafford, Tarik Muharemovic and Harry Wilson, and they currently lie in seventh position in the table.

As we approach December, they have only been beaten twice in the league, and their highlight of the season so far came on Saturday when they thrashed Chelsea 4-1 at Stamford Bridge.

Only the reigning champions Arsenal have conceded fewer goals than Leeds, and while their goalscoring had been a concern prior to their thumping win against Xabi Alonso's side, that game will give them confidence in their scoring abilities.

With Farke's contract currently due to expire at the end of the season, he is continuing to show that he is capable of taking the team forward to even better things if he has the right backing.

The Athletic reported last month that an agreement in principle had been reached over a new deal, but there is still no sign of the confirmation.

There could be more signings and other contract extensions to come in 2027, but tying Farke down has to be the urgent priority for the 49ers now, to remove any uncertainty around Elland Road.
