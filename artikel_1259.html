Tottenham Injury News: Savio and Bergvall Update as 5 Ruled Out for Sunderland

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Tottenham will be looking to maintain their impressive run of form and make it five wins in six matches when they travel to Sunderland on Sunday. Roberto De Zerbi's side have propped up the Premier League table in recent weeks, having picked up 12 points from their last 15 available and beat Ipswich Town 2-0 last time out.

Indeed, Tottenham, who were rock bottom up until mid-October, now sit 10th and are just three points behind fifth-place Bournemouth. But Spurs will now be desperate to record another win against a Sunderland team that are struggling and were booed off during their 1-1 draw against Jagiellonia Bialystok in the Europa League on Thursday.

Sunderland sit inside the Premier League relegation zone having picked up nine points from their first 11 matches and Tottenham will fancy their chances at the Stadium of Light, despite losing there in De Zerbi's first game in charge back in April. But the Italian does have a number of injury concerns to deal with going into the trip to Wearside.
