Ben Jacobs Names 3 Right-Backs Arsenal Are Targeting In January Transfer Window

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Arsenal do not traditionally use the January transfer window to do lots of business, but this season may be different.

The Gunners sit pretty at the top of the Premier League table, six points clear of Brighton and Manchester City who sit second and third currently.

But they may look to strengthen their squad when the winter window opens and manager Mikel Arteta is targeting a right-back and a central midfielder.

GIVEMESPORT senior football correspondent Ben Jacobs believes the Gunners will look in the market in January, but may wait until the summer of 2027 to add significantly to their ranks.

Arsenal have suffered with a number of injuries already this season, with William Saliba and Jurrien Timber missing the start of the campaign, while Bukayo Saka, Martin Zubimendi and more recently David Raya are now sidelined.

Timber has had to cover at centre-back on occasions this season, with Ben White playing at right-back and it is therefore no surprise that they are potentially looking at reinforcements in the full-back area.

And Jacobs has named three potential targets in his exclusive GMS newsletter.

He said: "My information is that Arsenal will be looking for another midfielder, but before we talk about that particular area, we have to think about other pressing positions that Arsenal may focus on.

"As far as the winter is concerned, right-back is relatively high in Arsenal’s thinking, so maybe in the winter, they explore that, if not, it’ll be summer 2027. So yes, midfielder is important, and that is even after the arrival of Bruno Guimaraes, but right-back is another area.

"Arsenal are one of the clubs that have looked historically at Malo Gusto, along with Manchester City and even Manchester United in weeks gone by, but the challenge for all three of these clubs is price. £75million was what Chelsea wanted over the summer. If that drops, and Gusto becomes a market opportunity, then there’s several clubs, including from within England, that might decide to advance on Gusto. City were maybe the most concrete on Gusto over the summer, but nothing developed, as I say, because Chelsea’s asking price was just a little bit too high.

"You’ve got David Boly and also Ivan Fresneda, two players that are on Arsenal’s radar too, so they’ll be in the market for some form of right-back, for sure.”
