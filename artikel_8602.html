OfferUp Review for Sellers – No Selling Fees, but Local Pickup Only

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Post on OfferUp as a free second listing beside Facebook Marketplace when you’re selling furniture, appliances, tools, or other things buyers pick up locally. It takes no cut of any sale, but it has been local pickup only since shipping ended in 2025, so take cash at the meetup before you hand anything over.

Copying an item you’ve listed on Facebook Marketplace to OfferUp costs only your time, up to a monthly limit on free listings. Promoting it near the top of buyers’ feeds with Promote Plus costs $19.99 a month, which by our math is about 7% of a $300 dresser’s price but half of a $40 lamp’s.

Sellers clearing out furniture, appliances, or tools in a metro where OfferUp is busy should list there beside Facebook Marketplace, since they keep the full price of every sale. Sellers of small items they’d rather mail do better on Mercari, where they ship on prepaid labels for a 10% selling fee.
