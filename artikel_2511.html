Germany U21 forced into three injury replacements ahead of Belgium clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Following injuries to Borussia Mönchengladbach's Lukas Ullrich, Celtic's Mika Baur and Augsburg's Arijon Ibrahimovic, Germany U21 coach Antonio Di Salvo has had to make three changes to his squad for the upcoming games against Belgium and Croatia.

Elversberg's Maurice Krattenmacher and Hamburger SV's Kofi Amoako could both make their debuts, while 1. FC Union Berlin's Tom Rothe has also received a late call-up.

Rothe has previously made nine appearances for Germany's U21 side, scoring twice.

Already assured of a place at next summer's European Championship, Di Salvo's men will test themselves in friendlies against Belgium (November 13) and Croatia (November 17).
