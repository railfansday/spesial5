Drake Maye, Aidan Hutchinson injured on same play: Injury updates for Patriots QB, Lions DE

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

Two of the game's biggest stars went down on the same play in Germany, as New England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye and Detroit Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson headed to the locker room following injuries on a third-down sack on the first drive.

The Patriots announced that Maye was downgraded from questionable to out with an ankle injury, and the Lions announced that Hutchinson was downgraded to out with a knee injury. Patriots backup QB Tommy DeVito took over for Maye and played the remainder of the 35-10 loss for New England. He finished 12-for-24 with 81 yards, one touchdown and one interception.

Patriots head coach Mike Vrabel said he didn't have an update on Maye after the game but did say they would run more tests back in the United States to determine the severity of the injury. He called it a "lower leg" injury.

When asked why Maye was ruled out despite not knowing the full extent of the injury, Vrabel said, "He couldn't function, couldn't play, couldn't run, couldn't do whatever he needed to do to play QB to protect himself to execute, or function at that level."

Vrabel initially said there were a lot of issues he didn't believe Maye's absence affected, but then said it "may have, but I highly doubt it" when asked what his concern is without his QB1 on the offense.

The head coach said the team as a whole needs to do the little things better on all three phases and work on fundamentals. He listed tackling, creating turnovers, preventing turnovers on offense, running the football, getting open, protecting the quarterback, and special teams needing to create some momentum as areas the team needs to work on, regardless of who is at QB.

According to reports, Hutchinson is feared to have suffered a significant knee injury, and there "does not appear hope that he returns this season." The DE immediately had an MRI and will get a second opinion, per the report.

After the game, Lions coach Dan Campbell said he had "nothing new" in terms of injury news surrounding Hutchinson, and that the team will "know more when we get back in the States."

"Any time you lose a player of his caliber, the type of teammate he is, he's a captain of ours, that doesn't make things easy. I thought guys stepped up in his wake and showed up for us today and helped us out ... but knowing Hutch for who he is, he doesn't stay down long," Campbell said. "... but certainly you don't want to know that a player like that is down."

He emphasized how well the team adjusted and made up for a big defensive loss.

On third-and-goal, Hutchinson got to the quarterback, who was limping after the play. Maye was able to walk under his own power to the sideline, but was hunched over, seemingly in pain, and eventually went to the locker room.

The Patriots' opening drive ended in a successful field goal attempt. The Lions immediately responded with an Amon-Ra St. Brown touchdown on their first offensive drive of the game.

Fortunately for the Patriots, they have a bye in Week 11, so they will have time to nurse these injuries before they take the field again in Week 12, when they visit the Los Angeles Chargers. The Lions will return to Detroit to host the Tampa Bay Bucanneers in Week 11.
