Ella Langley & More Record-Setters at the 2026 CMA Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Ella didn't win everything. It just seemed like it.

Ella Langley set a new, all-time record for most CMA Awards wins in one night on Wednesday (Nov. 18). Langley won six awards, breaking the old record of five wins in one night first set by Johnny Cash in 1969 and then tied by Vince Gill in 1993, Alan Jackson in 2002 and Lainey Wilson in 2023.

Langley has won 10 CMA Awards in just three years. Only two other female solo artists and one all-female group have won 10 or more CMA Awards. Miranda Lambert upped her total of wins to 17 this year. Wilson has won 12 (though she didn’t add to her tally this year). The Chicks have won 10.

Langley, 27, became the second-youngest entertainer of the year winner in CMA history. Taylor Swift was even younger, 19, when she won in that category for the first time in 2009. (She was 21 when she won for a second time two years later.)

Langley won entertainer one year after Wilson won in the category for a second time. This is the second time in CMA history that female acts (solo artists or all-female groups) have won back-to-back awards in this category. The first was when Shania Twain won in 1999, followed by The Chicks in 2000.

Langley won single, song and video of the year for her smash “Choosin’ Texas.” She won in all three categories last year in tandem with Riley Green for their sexy duet “You Look Like You Love Me.” She’s the first artist to achieve this three-award sweep twice. The Brooks & Dunn classic “Believe” won all three awards in 2006.

Langley is just the second artist in CMA history to win single of the year two years running. Lady A, then known as Lady Antebellum, won in 2009 with “I Run to You” and in 2010 with “Need You Now.” (“Need You Now” went on to win Grammys for record and song of the year — and no one would be surprised if “Choosin’ Texas” did the same at the 69th annual Grammy Awards next Feb. 7.)

In the CMA competition for song of the year, Langley is the first songwriter to win back-to-back awards since Lori McKenna, who won in 2015 for co-writing “Girl Crush” and in 2016 for writing “Humble and Kind.”

Just two other songwriters (or teams of songwriters) have won song of the year in back-to-back years with different songs. Vince Gill won three years in a row, 1991-93, for co-writing “When I Call Your Name,” “Look at Us” and “I Still Believe in You.” Paul Overstreet and Don Schlitz won in 1986 and 1987 for co-writing “On the Other Hand” and “Forever and Ever, Amen.”

Langley’s other awards are female vocalist of the year, breaking Lainey Wilson’s four-year hold on the award, and album of the year for Dandelion.

Here are five artists not named Ella Langley who set records at the 2026 CMA Awards.

Stephen Wilson Jr. won the award for new artist of the year at age 47. That makes him the oldest winner in the history of the category, which originated in 1981 as the Horizon Award. The previous record holder was Darius Rucker, who was 43 when he won in 2009.

The youngest winner in the category, if you’re curious, was LeAnn Rimes, who was just 15 when she won in 1997. Taylor Swift was 17 when she won in 2007.

This was Wilson’s second try at the CMA Award for new artist of the year. He lost last year to Zach Top. He is the third artist in a row to win on his or her second try in this category, following Megan Moroney and Zach Top.

Wilson is having a good week. On Monday, he received his first two Grammy nominations – best country song for “Hardwired” and best country solo performance for “Gary.” He was entered in the 2027 Grammy competition for best new artist but wasn’t nominated. This historic CMA win more than makes up for that.

The duo won vocal duo of the year for a record-extending 17th time. This ups their career total of awards to 21, extending their lead as the act with the most awards overall.

They also took home the 2026 Willie Nelson lifetime achievement award. (For the record, the CMA counts only awards won in competition in their official tally of artists with the most wins.)

Stapleton won male vocalist of the year for a record-extending ninth time. This ups his career total of awards to 20, extending his lead as the male solo artist with the most wins.

The only disappointing note: Stapleton has gone 0-10 in the entertainer of the year category, a record for the most nods without a win. He has lost the big one to Garth Brooks (three times), Luke Combs and Lainey Wilson (twice each) and Keith Urban, Morgan Wallen and Ella Langley (once each).

If Stapleton finally win the big one, the title of artist with the most nominations without winning will pass to Carrie Underwood and Miranda Lambert, who have each gone 0-6 in the category.

Lambert won three awards, upping her career total to 17 – more than any other female artist (or all-female group) in CMA history. Details above.

McGraw won musical event of the year for the fifth time for “McArthur,” a collab with HARDY, Eric Church and Morgan Wallen. McGraw had previously won in the category for “It’s Your Love” (with Faith Hill); “Find Out Who Your Friends Are” (with Tracy Lawrence and Kenny Chesney); “Feel Like a Rock Star” (with Chesney); and “Highway Don’t Care” (with Taylor Swift and Keith Urban).

McGraw’s fifth win enabled him to break out of a tie with Brad Paisley, Urban, Chesney and Vince Gill, who have won four times in the category.
