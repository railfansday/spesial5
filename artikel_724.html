Slegers sacked by Arsenal

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Renee Slegers leaves Arsenal after less than two years in charge; Slegers led Gunners to Women's Champions League title in May 2025, but exits after poor start to 2026/27 campaign; the Gunners sit sixth in the WSL, 12 points off the top and have not won at home in the league all season

Renee Slegers has been sacked as Arsenal head coach after a poor start to the Women's Super League season.

Slegers led Arsenal to Women's Champions League glory in May 2025, having initially stepped up from assistant to Jonas Eidevall on an interim basis, then narrowly missed out on the WSL title to Manchester City in 2025/26.

Such was her success at the time, the Dutchwoman sign a new contract with the club in January 2026, running until 2029.

But a poor start to the 2026/27 campaign, in which Arsenal went on a five-game winless run in the WSL for the first time in their history, has culminated in Slegers' departure just 10 months into her new deal.

The Gunners remain winless at home in the WSL this season having drawn a sixth consecutive game against West Ham on Sunday.

Arsenal sit sixth in the WSL this season, 12 points off leaders Chelsea and eight points behind London City Lionesses in the third and final Champions League position.

Slegers' side have also only scored 11 goals in 11 WSL matches this season - only Everton and bottom club Charlton have managed fewer this term.

Her assistant coach Aaron D'Antino - who has been with the Gunners since 2018 - also leaves the club, with former Brighton boss and current Arenal head of analytics Melissa Clarke-Reed will take interim charge.

"It was an extremely difficult decision to part ways with Renée, who has led the team with dedication and commitment throughout her time here," said director of women's football Clare Wheatley.

"In leading us to glory in the Champions League and the inaugural FIFA Champions Cup, Renee has made a significant and lasting contribution to the history of Arsenal Football Club, with close support from Aaron.

"After careful reflection, it is clear that results have been below the expected level, and change is necessary."

Renee Slegers speaking after Arsenal's 0-0 draw with West Ham on if she's considering her future:

"I love the club. I'm very passionate about this group of players, we're going through a very, very difficult time, and I take my responsibilities, because the results haven't been good enough.

"In the second half we are chasing again, but we're not creating good enough chances to the final third, from all phases, and the way we get there, it's not the Arsenal standard and we need to do that better. But I take my responsibilities in my role.

"That [fans booing] is on us and that's our responsibility. That it's so quiet in the stadium because we don't inspire enough. We don't bring enough energy.

"I think, about the fans, because it's cold and they want to see good football and get inspired today and that's not what we did. And at the end of the day, that's my responsibility.

"And so that doesn't feel good. The fans booing at the end they care and they want more from this team which the group is determined to turn things around.

"It's very tough for the players and the players that have been at Arsenal for longer, or even the players that came last year, no one has gone through this ever with Arsenal.

"So, of course, I think the group has been great. They've been very good in the situation, but, of course, it's the last one of the block and frustration is expressed, which I don't think is a bad thing, because we shouldn't be accepting this."
