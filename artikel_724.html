‘The Day of the Jackal’ Season 2: Eddie Redmayne Back as Globe-Trotting Assassin in First Look

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Sky and Peacock Original will return to our screens in March 2027.

Our first look at season two of The Day of the Jackal is here.

Eddie Redmayne returns as the elusive assassin in a first-look teaser for the second installment of the Sky and Peacock Original, coming in March 2027. Taken from the opening of the new series, the teaser offers a glimpse of the “globe-trotting scale and high-stakes action to come.”

Oscar winner Redmayne (The Good Nurse, The Trial of the Chicago Seven, The Theory of Everything), who also serves as an executive producer, earned Golden Globe, Critics’ Choice and SAG Award nominations for his performance in the first series, opposite Lashana Lynch. That first season was the biggest new series to ever launch on Sky, THR revealed exclusively. Episode one reached a U.K. audience of 4.5 million after 26 viewing days. On Peacock, the show ranked among the top five original streaming series in the U.S. for its opening weekend, based on preliminary Nielsen data.

The series followed a cat-and-mouse game between a chameleonic assassin (Redmayne) and an MI6 agent (Lynch) who’s determined to catch him.

Returning cast members for season two include Úrsula Corberó (Money Heist), Charles Dance (Game of Thrones), Chukwudi Iwuji (Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3), Eleanor Matsuura (The Walking Dead), Adam James (Mr Bates vs The Post Office), Christy Meyer (The Amateur) and Corey Johnson (The Bourne Ultimatum).

Joining the cast for series two are Pablo Schreiber (Halo), Weruche Opia (Genius MLK/X), Matt Bomer (White Collar) and Pia Tjelta (Jo Nesbø’s Detective Hole).

The Day of the Jackal is produced by multi-award-winning Carnival Films, which is part of Universal Global Television, a division of Universal Studio Group.
