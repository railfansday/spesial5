Hollywood Celebrates Democrats’ Midterm Sweep: “The Trump Regime Is Over”

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Stars took to Instagram, Bluesky and X after Democrats won back both the House and Senate.

Hollywood woke up happy after the 2026 midterm elections delivered a blue flood, with Democrats not only retaking both chambers of Congress but also scoring a number of notable and historic wins. Two years after Republicans walloped the opposition and Donald Trump made his historic political comeback, many celebrities felt it was a good time to gloat. They took to social media to do just that. Here’s a look.

Rosie O’Donnell, a longtime foe of President Trump, was recorded by Really American’s Marco Foster knocking her decades-long nemesis. The actor and comic, who moved to Ireland with her family, recently returned to the New York stage with a critically acclaimed one-woman show, Common Knowledge, and received rave reviews for her subsequent stint filling in on late night for Jimmy Kimmel. We gave a very loud and clear message to Trump and his sycophants, his criminal friends: We’ve had enough. I’m so proud of our country. Let the investigations begin, people. This is a glorious day. You don’t get to define us, you madman. I love you, America.”

John Cusack was briefer in one of his election-related Bluesky posts, declaring, “Texas South Carolina Say fuck you to trump.” In a second post, he exclaimed, “Trump and musk getting the shit kicked out of them in Texas and all over the country.” Cusack added in separate posts that Trump’s impeachment and removal were non-negotiable, and praised Michigan Senate race winner Abdul El-Sayed as a smart progressive who overcame a smear campaign.

“You did it, we did it, this is redemption time,” John Leguizamo said on Instagram. The Odyssey supporting star told fans it’s now time to fix the mess.

Bette Midler, a longtime outspoken Democrat, expressed her delight in a simple text post on Instagram. “I am OVERJOYED,” she wrote. “What a great, great night! Thank you America! You’ve saved your beloved country!!”

James Woods, one of Hollywood’s rare staunch Trump supporters, surprised by congratulating Democrats and admonish Republicans for their massive losses across the country on Tuesday, which saw the GOP lose the governor’s office and a Senate seat, essentially turning blue — which has been a dream for the Democratic Party for decades and has finally become a reality.
