Round Up: The Reviews Are In For The Legend Of Zelda: Ocarina Of Time On Switch 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The week is finally upon us, folks! We are less than 24 hours away from the launch of Ocarina of Time on Switch 2, and to say that we're all excited would be an understatement.

Our excitement is made all the bigger by the fact that the game's reviews are now in, so we can start to get an idea of what the full experience has in store for us.

Here at Nintendo Life, the sublime Ollie Reynolds awarded Link's latest a 9/10, calling it a "very safe remake," but "comfortably the most visually stunning work Nintendo has ever put out, and it’s unquestionably the best way to play one of the most seminal games of all time". Here's a brief snippet followed by a link to the full thing:

The visuals, the gameplay, the audio – everything has been thoughtfully upgraded to such a degree that this will completely replace the original for many. While the lack of Master Quest means it falls short of being definitive, it's still a remarkable remake, and a must-buy for Switch 2.

Review The Legend Of Zelda: Ocarina Of Time (Switch 2) - A Stunning Remake, But Not Quite Definitive

But of course, we're not alone in our thoughts. Reviews have now gone live across many outlets, so we have assembled the following roundup to give you an idea of the spread of critical opinions as it stands (spoiler: there's not all that much of a spread).

We'll start with a few full-beans scores. The first comes from GamesRadar, with a 5/5 review that calls it "a reimagining bigger and bolder than any I've ever played before it":

It's as timeless as it ever was, managing to recreate one of the most beautiful games to date without compromising the magic of its 1998 predecessor – only adding to it

VGC also came in with a 5/5, praising "how little has been lost in translation" between the remake and the original:

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time was already one of gaming’s most essential experiences, and this wonderful restoration makes it the definitive way to discover – or rediscover – a masterpiece

And Eurogamer followed suit with another 5/5:

This is Ocarina as it's always been, as timeless and intricate, but it also has a vibrancy and clarity that makes it feel like it was just created last Wednesday

Things drop ever so slightly (perhaps thanks only to the bump to a bigger review score range) in Game Informer's 9.25 review:

I was rarely surprised during my playthrough, but I was incredibly excited to see old friends and I happily share without embarrassment that playing through its final hour made me cry

By approaching this remake with a degree of reverence and respect for the source material, Nintendo has shown that a true masterpiece can stand the test of time

This remake proves there’s still so much value in the traditional Zelda formula: the dense, intimate world of Hyrule is stuffed with charming characters and fascinating secrets

And finally, just in the interest of showing the odd lower score out there, RPG Site awarded the remake an 8/10:

While some decisions and directions in this remake may keep me from reaching for it again anytime soon, Ocarina of Time is a classic tale and remains a standout among the pantheon of the Zelda titles, and this remake is a great showing of why, even with its blemishes

What a stellar showing, eh? As it stands, the game sits at an eye-watering score of 96 on Metacritic based on 81 critic reviews. Good grief.
