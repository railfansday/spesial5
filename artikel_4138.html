Zelda Switch 2 Console Is Back In Stock Soon If You Missed Out (US)

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

If you live in the US and happened to miss out on the Zelda 40th Anniversary Switch 2 console, then good news – more is on the way.

This was already kind of confirmed in a recent interview with Nintendo's Latin America Vice President and General Manager Bill van Zyll, in which he claimed Nintendo "underestimated" how popular the hardware would be, but promised that more would come at a later date.

Well, we can now narrow down that date a little bit thanks to the official Nintendo Store in the US. The page for the Switch 2 console states that "more inventory is on the way in January 2027", providing an option to add the item to your Wish List to receive a notification when it's back in stock.

So while you won't be able to pick one up in time for Christmas, you won't have to wait much longer to get your hands on one. Hey, it's good way to banish those 'January Blues', right? Unfortunately, we don't have any similar information regarding stock in other regions right now.

Nintendo reiterated plans to celebrate Zelda's 40th anniversary at its latest financial briefing, with one of its biggest initiatives being a huge concert tour kicking off in 2027. Of course, Zelda: Ocarina of Time has made a massive splash this month, earning universal critical acclaim and selling over 5 million copies in the first four days of sale.
