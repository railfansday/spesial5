Since the start of Q4, the crypto market has continued to weaken amid volatility. Major asset prices have pulled back from their highs, and market participants have become more cautious. As the market cools, HTX has focused its efforts on solidifying its trading fundamentals and offering diverse yield generation pathways. In October, HTX saw a significant overall improvement in the market depth of its core spot trading pairs, with the 0.05% depth for the BTC/USDT pair increasing by 78%. Meanwhile, the platform’s total count  of stock trading assets grew to 307, with cumulative TradFi trading volume surpassing 7.5 billion USDT. At the end of October, HTX Earn launched the APY Boost Promotion as part of the HTX Winter Campaign, delivering stable yield options for users amid fluctuating market conditions.

Overall Spot Depth Enhancement, Ensuring Smoother Execution for Large-Volume Trades

In October, HTX continued to strengthen its collaboration with professional market makers, improving market-making support, order book depth, and the overall trading environment, which contributed to an overall improvement in the market depth of its core spot trading pairs. Taking BTC/USDT as an example, the pair’s 0.01% depth increased by 42% in October, while its 0.05% depth jumped by 78%. Deeper market depth ensures that orders of the same size have a smaller price impact, allowing large-volume trades to be executed closer to market price. For core assets like BTC, this means users can easily execute trades even during periods of severe market volatility.

Centered around the two core assets of BTC and ETH, HTX also rolled out a series of trading rewards in October, including trading challenges, fee rebates, trading lucky draws, and iPhone Duo theme activities, with the flagship trading challenge attracting over 10,000 participants.

Asset Matrix Continues to Expand, Stock Trading Assets Reach 307 Types

In October, HTX Futures launched 40 new assets, including 29 selected stocks and ETFs alongside 11 crypto assets. To date, the platform’s number of stock trading assets has grown to 307, covering core US stocks, major global indices and ETFs, biopharmaceuticals, energy infrastructure, and Asia-Pacific market leaders. Overall platform liquidity and user activity have both reached new highs.

This month’s new listings focused on sectors including AI computing power and hardware innovation, biopharmaceuticals and healthcare, mass consumption and high-dividend blue chips, as well as Asia-Pacific core markets and frontier technologies. Targets such as STMicroelectronics (STM), United Microelectronics (UMC), Synopsys (SNPS), Constellation Energy (CEG), Pfizer (PFE), Boeing (BA), Procter & Gamble (PG), McDonald’s (MCD), Starbucks (SBUX), Indonesia Index ETF (EIDO), and Taiyo Yuden (TAIYOYUDEN) were rolled out in succession, offering a balanced mix of growth-oriented tech stocks and defensive assets like consumer goods and pharmaceuticals.

As HTX continues to expand its asset offerings, cumulative TradFi trading volume has surpassed $7.5 billion, with $1.5 billion added in October alone.

In the spot asset category, new listings introduced by HTX in October primarily centered around the AI sector, featuring projects such as POD, AXIS, DNA, and NMR. Notably, Axis Robotics (AXIS), which focuses on physical AI and robotics training data, was jointly launched as a primary listing by HTX, surging by up to 37% following its debut.

HTX Earn Offers APY Boost on 7 Products, USDT Loan Rates Maintain Strong Competitiveness

In October, user interest in HTX Earn continued to rise. SVIP Flexible subscriptions among users with large holdings remained exceptionally popular, with participating users increasing by 16.1% month-on-month and subscription volume surging by over 20% month-on-month.

In terms of borrowing, HTX’s USDT loan interest rates maintained robust competitiveness across the industry. In October, the platform’s annualized USDT loan interest rate ranged from 4.75% to 5.3%, with discounted rates for large-volume borrowers as low as 3.84%, empowering users to unlock the capital potential of their holdings at lower costs. During the HTX Margin Autumn Craze, USDT loan scale grew by 18.6% month-on-month, and margin trading volume surged by over 128%. Currently, the HTX Margin Trading & Borrowing Bonanza Month is underway and will run through November 25, offering 7 tiers of rewards for USDT loans alongside up to a 50% fee rebate on trades during the Margin Trading Contest.

In the OTC sector, HTX has continued to expand payment channels for its OTC Desk, partnering with multiple payment providers to further optimize the user trading experience and reduce transaction costs.

$HTX Q3 Burn Performance Shines, TOKEN2049 Spotlights Brand Influence

On October 15, HTX DAO completed its $HTX token burn for Q3 2026. A total of approximately 9.39 trillion $HTX tokens were burned, valued at roughly $15.48 million, representing a quarter-over-quarter burn volume increase of approximately 25.6%. This brings the cumulative total of $HTX burned and pledged to 127.17 trillion tokens to date. Under HTX DAO’s established mechanism, HTX acts as an ecosystem partner, dedicating 50% of its quarterly revenues to $HTX buybacks and burns.

From October 6 to 8, global crypto attention converged on TOKEN2049 in Singapore. HTX Ventures, the global investment arm of HTX, made its presence felt at the Sands Expo and Convention Center in Singapore, where Unitree robots and Huobao (HTX mascot) IP parades, and multiple targeted lucky draws attracted large crowds of visitors . Meanwhile, HTX DAO hosted multiple sideline events during the summit. At HTX DAO Future Night, Justin Sun, Advisor to HTX and a core supporter of HTX DAO, delivered a video address sharing perspectives on crypto industry trends and the future development of the digital economy, highlighting the free flow of value within the digital world as a major trend worth watching over the coming decade. At the VIP private dinner, the HTX team engaged in deep dialogues with industry representatives, ecosystem contributors, and partners, exchanging viewpoints on industry evolution, market shifts, and product experiences to foster mutual understanding while optimizing services and expanding collaborations.

Standing by Users Through Every Market Cycle

Markets fluctuate. Facing market ups and downs, what the platform can do for users is deliver a better trading experience with each transaction, introduce valuable innovative products and practical rewards, and firmly safeguard user asset security. Compliance is the foundation of all these efforts. In October, HTX continued to deepen constructive communication with relevant regulatory authorities and work toward the proper resolution of relevant matters, while further strengthening its compliance measures. These efforts create more favorable conditions for focusing on user services and pursuing long-term development. Over the past 13 years, HTX has weathered market cycles one after another alongside users worldwide. This winter, HTX will continue to uphold a user-first philosophy, accompanying every user through the cold season and awaiting the arrival of the next cycle together.

Founded in 2013, HTX has evolved from a virtual asset exchange into a comprehensive ecosystem of blockchain businesses that span digital asset trading, derivatives, research, investments, incubation, and other businesses.

Guided by its vision to “Achieve Financial Freedom for 8 Billion People Worldwide,” HTX works to make the digital asset space more accessible to a broader audience. The platform continues to develop its products and services in response to user needs and an evolving industry landscape.
