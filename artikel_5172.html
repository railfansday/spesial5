Lucky Trader Banks 66,000% Profit on GTA 6 Meme Coin Ticker Confusion

One lucky meme coin trader made bank as Grand Theft Auto 6 launched worldwide, turning $300 into over $200,000 after early footage of the game sent degens flocking from one unofficial token to another.

The trader bought $307 worth of the DCTY token shortly after it launched in October and has sold their bag for $202,278, according to DEX Screener, a profit of almost 66,000%.

Early indications had pointed to GTA 6 featuring an in-game meme coin—with, it's worth stressing, no actual blockchain element—called "DacityCoin," which appeared in the official launch trailer on a plane with a wing walker setting off a smoke bomb on top of it.

It was later linked to leaked gameplay of an mission which appeared to show game protagonist Lucia parachuting as a stunt to attract investment to DacityCoin.

Amid this hype, per DEX Screener, a real-world and unofficial DacityCoin launched using the Solana launchpad Pump.fun. It first peaked at a $4.95 million market capitalization, following the launch trailer's release, before tanking 93% to $343,620 as hype faded.

With GTA 6 set to launch within a matter of hours of publication, the token drew renewed attention, propelling it to a new all-time high of $10.8 million Wednesday. Unfortunately for investors, GTA 6's staggered global release means that some gamers are already playing it—and footage started to spread which suggested the Solana token's ticker was wrong.

The result was a rollercoaster of volatility, with the DacityCoin token now sitting at a market cap of around $800,000.

Gameplay footage from Brazilian streamer Combo Infinito suggests that the in-game token is called DacityCoin, with the ticker DCTY.

That propelled another Solana token with a seemingly accurate name and ticker sprang into action. DCTY's previous all-time high market cap was just $54,000 before the ticker was revealed, and it set a new high of over $12 million after footage of the game appeared to confirm that its ticker matched the in-game token. It is now trading at a market cap of around $10 million.

The game itself appears to be somewhat inconsistent in its naming of the in-game token. During the trailer, it appeared to be spelled "DacityCoin" as one word, however, the subtitles in the leak show it spelled as "Dacity coin," two words. Similarly, the company behind the in-game token is AudaCity, but leaks show three different spellings: Auda City, AudaCity, and Auda-City.

It is worth stressing: Rockstar has not explicitly confirmed that a meme coin will be featured in-game, and any real-world token has no official links to GTA 6 or its creators. Footage is still being released, and absolute confirmation of the in-game token's involvement is still being established.

Those still holding the now-tanking DacityCoin are hoping things will turn around. Mike Lerro, the community takeover lead for DacityCoin, shrugged off any chance that the unofficial token's name and ticker are wrong.

"No, bro. DacityCoin and AudaCity are in the game. Yes or no?" When Decrypt suggested the ticker might be wrong, he replied, "It doesn't matter… Okay and ? The person who creates it will dump on you then it goes to zero. Who's gonna [do the community takeover]? No one."

"DACITYCOIN AND AUDACITY ARE IN THE GAME," Lerro wrote in the meme coin's Telegram. "NOTHING ELSE MATTERS."

"It's so crazy, it'll correct itself," Lerro said in an emergency Telegram group chat call. "I hope so," an investor replied.
