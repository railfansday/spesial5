Missing Teen Found Alive After 6 Days Alone in Australian Bushland

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A 17-year-old boy was found alive in the Australian wilderness and has been reunited with his mother after going missing for 6 days.

The boy went missing on Friday, November 6 after his mother dropped him and a friend off to go camping. William had reportedly walked a short distance away to retrieve more camping equipment and did not return, and the friend was unable to find him despite searching the area.

An extensive search was launched, and William was ultimately located when an aerial search unit spotted a tackle box in the bushland. Honing in on the area, they soon spotted William lying near a creek nearby and were able to retrieve him. After an emotional reunion between William and his mother, he was brought to the hospital for monitoring.

“Amazingly, William is in good spirits and for the most part, in good health,” Victoria police said in a press release.
