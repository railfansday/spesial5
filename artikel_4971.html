New mixing benchmark could help industry reduce energy use in stirred tanks

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Solid–liquid mixing, where particles are dispersed in a liquid, is essential in many industrial processes such as slurry preparation, crystallization, precipitation, adsorption and dissolution. In such cases, effective mixing is critical for contact between particles and liquid, enabling mass transfer. Floating particles often pose a challenge in mixing because they are lighter than the liquid and tend to float at its surface. While impeller agitation does draw them into the liquid, the minimum mixing intensity needed for effective drawdown has been difficult to define.
