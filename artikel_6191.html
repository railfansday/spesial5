Away Is Offering Its Biggest Discount of the Year for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Deals and pricing updated as of 4:18 a.m. on November 27, 2026.

When plotting out your Black Friday purchases, it’s always a good idea to prioritize those higher-priced necessities you’ve been putting off for far too long—and for many of us, that means a new set of luggage. One of our favorite brands for years on end, Away is a great place to start. Now through December 1, you can save 25% on almost everything, plus an extra 10% when you spend $750 (recent holiday drops and the Series 3 collection are excluded from the sale).

From the Bigger Carry-On to the Medium checked bag, Away’s pieces are classic, functional, and made to last. For a brand that runs on simplicity, it’s pretty incredible that it keeps innovating, each suitcase or travel accessory better than the last—whether that’s a carry-on that opens from the top, or a sunglasses case that stores your eyewear like a filing cabinet. While the sale will run through Cyber Monday, some popular colors and suitcases might sell out, so don't delay adding something you're eyeing to your cart. To help you find your new perfect suitcase, I’ve gathered the best Black Friday deals to shop now during Away’s biggest sale of the year.

The Bigger Carry-On is Away’s best-selling suitcase, sized to fit in the overhead bin of most major US airlines. Available in a range of colors from Olive Green to Cherry Red, this is what to get for your loved one who absolutely insists on not checking a bag. Perfect for a weeklong trip, it can fit between six and nine outfits.

Soft suitcase apologists, this one’s for you. Shannon McMahon, senior destinations editor, is a fan of this suitcase’s ability to swivel and roll smoothly when packed full, crediting the effective stabilization technology that works so well that not even a laptop in the front pocket can take it down.

Launched earlier this year, the Away Topside collection is designed for easy access in tight spaces, plus it comes with brand-new wheel brake technology, which works to anchor the bag on slanted surfaces. Think how easy it’ll be to open this suitcase in a pinch at the airport or in the plane aisle.

Whether it's a Rimowa or a Tumi, aluminum suitcases tend to cost a pretty penny, but Away’s sits in the mid-range (add the Black Friday discount, and the price gets even nicer). The TSA-accepted locks are a total game changer, allowing you to seamlessly snap everything in place.

When you’ve decided a carry-on will not, in fact, cut it for that two-week trip to Tokyo, it’s time to cave and get The Medium, one of Away’s best-selling hard-shell suitcases. It’s got all the makings of a stand-out piece of luggage: 360-degree wheels, a compression pad, and three mesh pockets.

The Aluminum Edge Large offers the durability of an aluminum suitcase without the dents, featuring a shiny frame that’s as decorative as it is functional. The shell on this one has a pebbled finish, so it looks like leather, while the inside boasts Away's signature compression system.

Ideal for a one- or two-week trip, the Softside Medium is designed to expand comfortably with your belongings—without tipping over. The extra-strength nylon is water-repellent, so even though it’s made of fabric, it can handle whatever weather comes its way.

Sometimes large just isn’t large enough. When you’re about to embark on a two-week trip and just know you’ll get some shopping done while you’re away, this suitcase offers the luxury of space. The zipper’s Flex feature allows for an additional 1.75 inches of packing space.

For those weekend trips requiring a simple outfit change or two, you can’t go wrong with Away's Featherlight Weekender. With its barely-there, ultralight functionality, the Featherlight collection has your back (literally) with some seriously smart organization, like a bottom compartment to keep your extra pair of shoes separated.

This versatile personal item can also double as a gym or overnight bag. It’s made from a comfortable, water-repellent nylon, so it’ll stay in shape wherever you go, and it has all the compartments you could ever want—from a laptop sleeve to a water bottle pocket. It's a favorite of commerce editor Meaghan Kenny's for its durable construction and roomy interior.

If you're a frequent traveler (and overpacker), it's likely you've found yourself in a sitting-on-suitcase situation. Sold in a set of four, Away’s best-selling Insider Packing Cubes are made from water-repellent nylon to protect your belongings.

Resembling a mini Away suitcase, this glasses case fits up to three pairs of eyewear, with a padded microsuede lining to keep them from scratching each other. Get it for the friend who changes their sunnies with each look.
