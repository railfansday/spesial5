Athleta Promo Codes: 40-60% Off | Black Friday 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

First thing’s first: I am a big Gap Inc. fan. I’m of the opinion that you can fill out nearly an entire wardrobe with clothing from the company’s four brands: Gap, Banana Republic, Old Navy, and Athleta. Today though, we’re here to talk about Athleta, known for its higher-quality athleisure and workout wear. It’s a great place to shop for travel day clothing, no matter where you sit on the pajamas-to-business-casual spectrum of dressing for a flight.

If you’re a sweats kind of traveler, it's got you covered (I love the Forever Fleece high rise joggers). For something a little more put together and on trend, look to the wide-leg Seasoft mid rise pants. For something you can wear from the place into an office or work meeting, consider the Brooklyn mid-rise ankle pants. Then of course there’s the workout wear—soft, stretchy leggings and sports bras well suited to yoga or Pilates classes; T-shirts and lightweight jackets to wear on your way to and from the studio; and more.

Athleta does tend to be the most expensive athleisure option in the family (compared to Old Navy and Gap Body), so if you’re looking to save, you’re in the right place—keep reading for the latest Athleta promo codes and sale items available this Black Friday.

Those newer to the brand, or just not on the email list yet, can score a 15% off Athleta promo code by signing up for emails. Once you’re on the list, you’ll gain access to exclusive offers, new arrivals, private sales, and free in-store fitness classes. (If a pop-up doesn't appear, scroll to the bottom of the website homepage to sign up.)

As part of Athleta's Black Friday sale, everything on the site and in store (excluding outlets) is 40% off through Saturday, November 28 at 11:59 p.m. PT. Select Daily Spotlights bring savings as high as 60% during Cyber Week. This is a fantastic time to stock up on favorite items in new colors, or to try out some new styles. Today's steepest discounts include 60% off the comfortable Favorite Cotton Relaxed Top, Foundation Cap Sleeve Tee, and the Elemental Puffer Jacket (bringing the winter coat's cost down to under $100!).

I recommend the Brooklyn mid-rise ankle pant, a great work-travel hybrid pant; most colors are 40% off, with a pretty brown shade 50% off. The Seasoft mid-rise straight pant (up to 50% off) is another favorite—I just flew in them the other day. Fall and winter-friendly pieces are marked down too, like the Seasoft classic quarter zip (40% off) and Elation ultra high-rise leggings (these are SO soft and comfortable, and as much as 50% off in certain colors).

Of note, some pieces may be final sale, meaning no returns are allowed, so keep an eye on the fine print when checking out.

Join the Athleta rewards program to access even more perks and ways of saving. The program has recently adjusted its tiers slightly to now include:

It's an easy yes to sign up for Core. For all three tiers, perks of the program include members only exclusive discounts; birthday discounts; and free shipping on all orders of $50 or more at Gap, Banana Republic, Old Navy, and Athleta. All-Access Cardmembers also gain early access to sales.

Premier members (hit once your annual spend reaches $350) get a coupon every year for 15% off their purchase, and can choose what day to use it. A shopping spree might be in order, as this coupon can be used one time at each of the family of brands on the sale day, either online or in-store. They'll also earn an extended return window—up to 45 days to make returns (take note that returns after 30 days receive merchandise credit).

Those who open an Athleta Rewards Credit Card will become an All-Access Cardmember and get an extra 30% off their first purchase, valid the first time you use your Athleta Rewards credit card so long as it’s within 14 days of account opening. The card has no annual fee and card users earn 25 points for every $1 spent across the family of brands (Gap, Banana Republic, Old Navy, and Athleta). Core and Premier members earn 5 points for each $1 spent.

Of note, if you have a separate Mastercard, you can earn 15 points for each $1 spent in Athleta's family of retail brands, and 5 points for each $1 spent everywhere else Mastercard is accepted.

If an Athleta promo code isn’t working, there are a few things you can do. Start by double-checking for typos, extra spaces, or incorrect capitalization, all of which can prevent a code from applying correctly. You'll also want to confirm that the code is still valid, as expiration dates, minimum spend requirements, and product exclusions can apply, and some offers are limited to first-time shoppers or are single-use only.
