The Best Airbnbs in Colorado, Including a Ski-In Chalet and a Cliffside Cabin

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

I’ve always found Colorado a little hard to resist. After a handful of trips, I’ve explored everything from Aspen’s glitzy streets to Denver’s spirited food and cocktail scene to the many smaller towns in between—and somehow, there’s still more to discover. With four national parks; miles of hiking and biking trails; world-class ski resorts; and cities brimming with independent shops, restaurants, and bars, Colorado is a year-round playground with plenty to keep you busy (and plenty of reasons to come back).

Finding the right Colorado Airbnb is part of the fun. Think tucked-away mountain cabins with front-row seats to the snow, sprawling homes made for group getaways, and character-filled apartments that put you right in the middle of the action. While there are plenty of gorgeous Colorado hotels, there's something pretty wonderful about having your own Airbnb to return to after a day of chasing fresh powder, hiking, or exploring a new town. Whether you're plotting a winter ski escape, a summer adventure, or a fall foliage getaway, these are the 13 best Airbnbs in Colorado to add to your wish list.

We've selected these listings based on Superhost and Guest Favorite status, ratings, amenities, location, decor, editor stays, and previous guest reviews.
