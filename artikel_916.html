Serbia will be relieved to reach their final two games of 2026 after a nightmare 12 months for Veljko Paunovic's side.

The Eagles have lost seven of their eight matches this year, and each of their last six games - including all four in Nations League Group A2. However, they will retain a faint hope of avoiding relegation to the second tier if they can beat Germany in Belgrade.

Sasa Lukic and Nemanja Maksimovic are unavailable through suspension, while former Fulham and Newcastle United striker Aleksandar Mitrovic looks set to lead the line.

Serbia are the only team Germany have beaten in their four matches under Jurgen Klopp, after goals by Tom Bischof and Florian Wirtz secured a 2-0 win in Munich last month.

A draw and a defeat against Greece, plus another draw with the Netherlands, means Die Mannschaft are likely to need at least four points from tonight's game and Monday's home clash with the Dutch if they are to reach the Nations League quarter-finals.

Klopp named a 24-man squad for this international window but can include only 23 players across his starting XI and substitutes' bench, so Borussia Monchengladbach teenager Wael Mohya misses out in Belgrade.
