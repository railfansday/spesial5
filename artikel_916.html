Jennifer Lawrence Reacts to Connor Wood Comment Going Viral

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

After Jennifer Lawrence playfully called out DWTS season 35 contestant Connor Wood for paying her “no mind,” despite naming her as his celebrity crush, she reacted to the quip going viral.

Jennifer Lawrence was paying no mind to what the internet would think of this quip.

After The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping actress playfully called out Connor Wood for ignoring her when they once crossed paths, she admitted she didn’t think it would get so much attention.

“I don’t really know how to feel about it,” Lawrence told Andy Cohen during a Nov. 19 appearance on Watch What Happens Live alongside costar Josh Hutcherson. “I did not expect that to go viral. I don’t really get it.”

Lawrence—who joined Instagram in September—dropped the now-viral comment last month on a clip of Wood sharing she was his celebrity crush, and he wanted her to notice him.

“I just want a little acknowledgment from Jennifer Lawrence that I’m funny,” he said in an Oct. 20 Instagram video. “And that’s all I need.”

He joked, “Jennifer, I really need something good to happen to me.”

To which Lawrence revealed he’d already had his chance in a comment that now has over 100,000 likes, “I saw you on 8th avenue drinking a smoothie and you paid me no mind.”

Wood later reacted to the comment in a video shared by his Dancing With the Stars season 35 dance partner Rylee Arnold.

“‘I saw you on Eighth Avenue drinking a smoothie and you paid me no mind,’” Wood read over and over in the video. “It’s rolling around in my head.”

As Wood neared tears in the video, Arnold assured, “Don’t be upset! She’s doing that to be funny.”

Later, the 31-year-old embraced the comment, stepping out in a T-shirt with the quote emblazoned on the front, as well as striking up a brand deal with Smoothie King.

While the moment has inspired memes and even merchandise, it didn’t go viral for everyone. As Hutcherson joked during WWHL of the ordeal, “I don’t know what any of those words mean.”

Lawrence, meanwhile, had one correction to make. It wasn’t Eighth Avenue after all. As she noted, “It was also Greenwich.”
