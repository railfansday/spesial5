The next generation of workers is taking its talent and spending power to this Southern state

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Gen Z is moving to South Carolina for affordability, job opportunities and access to Charlotte’s booming metro area, LendingTree’s chief consumer finance analyst says.

The next generation of workers is on the move, and South Carolina is drawing more young adults than any other state.

The Palmetto State recorded a net gain of 22,843 Gen Z adults through interstate migration in 2024, according to a new LendingTree analysis. The study defines Gen Z as adults ages 18 to 27, the generation that follows millennials.

LendingTree chief consumer finance analyst Matt Schulz told Fox News Digital that affordability and job prospects may help explain the state’s appeal. South Carolina’s proximity to Charlotte, he said, gives young adults access to a growing job market while potentially offering a less expensive place to live.

"Gen Z is at the most mobile time of their lives, and they’re looking for a place where they can live affordably, where there are decent jobs and good opportunity," Schulz said.

RED STATES HAVE SPENT DECADES DOMINATING A KEY CORNER OF THE ECONOMY

South Carolina is drawing young adults seeking new opportunities and a lower cost of living. (iStock)

California had the largest net loss of Gen Z residents, at 58,109. Texas was the most popular destination for those leaving, drawing 19,287 Californians.

Schulz said California, New Jersey and New York had some of the largest net losses, while South Carolina, Missouri, Tennessee and Texas posted net gains. But he said young adults are also looking for career opportunities and a lifestyle they can afford.

"Ultimately, it is about looking for job opportunity and looking for the best place to get started with your career and being able to afford the things that you want to do and have the life you want to live," he said.

AMERICANS KEEP MOVING TO TEXAS AND FLORIDA — BUT ONE OTHER RED STATE IS GROWING EVEN FASTER

The figures build on a broader migration trend.

South Carolina added more than 59,000 residents from other states between 2022 and 2023, gaining roughly $4.1 billion in income, according to IRS data cited in an earlier Fox News Digital analysis.

The state had the nation’s largest per-capita net gain, while Texas and Florida drew more newcomers overall.

Relative to its size, South Carolina grew faster than any other state in the nation, according to Census Bureau data. (Jeffrey Greenberg/Universal Images Group/Getty Images)

Schulz said young adults’ moves can have broader economic implications as they enter the workforce, start families and make major purchases.

"These are the future leaders of your country and the future business owners and parents," he said. "The future of our economy depends a lot on what Gen Z does, whether it’s the housing market or auto loans or starting small businesses."
