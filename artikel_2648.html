Lakers rookie flexes his gains after coaches challenge: ‘I’ve been lifting’

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

From the moment Cameron Carr was selected with the No. 24 pick in June's NBA draft, the Lakers and the rookie wing have openly discussed the need for Carr, like most rookies, to adjust to the league's physicality.

And for Carr, part of that adjustment includes adding muscle to his 6-foot-5, 175-pound frame.

Cameron Carr of the Los Angeles Lakers prepares to shoot a free throw. NBAE via Getty Images

The former Baylor Bear has made progress in this area over the last six months.

And he wasn't shy with letting it be known after the Lakers' home win over the Jazz on Monday.

Cameron Carr #43 of the Los Angeles Lakers speaks to the media. NBAE via Getty Images

During his postgame media availability, Carr took a moment to ask the reporters in the interview room a question.

"Can y'all tell I've been in the weight room? I've been lifting," Carr said. "I hope y'all are getting that on camera too."

When asked what his personal records are, Carr appeared to be willing to share some numbers.

"I can't say too much," Carr said. "All of the above. I've been getting stronger."

Cameron Carr #43 of the Los Angeles Lakers drives to the basket. NBAE via Getty Images

Carr's gotten more playing time over the last two games as the Lakers await the return of Austin Reaves, who's missed five consecutive games because of a bruised left heel.

The Lakers' first-round draft pick scored nine points in 10 minutes during the win over the Jazz after having five points in 12 minutes during Friday's win over the Kings.

"We have to look for opportunities to give Cam rotation minutes with Austin out," coach JJ Redick said. "The last two games were opportunities for that. When you're on a good team, it's hard to get good reps as a young player, it just is. We've got to get him opportunities to get minutes, and with that, minutes where he can play through mistakes.

"The same thing with Adou [Thiero]. With Austin coming back, maybe Adou fits in more when he's back. So again, we'll just continue to look for that. But [Carr is] a rookie. He's not going to be perfect. You have to allow him to play through some stuff."
