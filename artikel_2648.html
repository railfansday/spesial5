Lights went out on Monday night because routine maintenance schedule wasn't changed

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

As unexpected (and unwanted) developments at Northwest Stadium go, worse things than the lights going out can happen — and have happened.

Still, the lights went out last night during the game. And the explanation speaks to a basic level of background incompetence.

Via Tyler Lauletta of Awful Announcing, ESPN’s Joe Buck eventually provided the explanation.

“If you want to know the reason why the lights went out earlier, the official word is the lighting vendor never disabled the regularly scheduled maintenance for 10 p.m.,” Buck said. “It’s a weekday thing, and they forgot it was a Monday night game.”

Last October, they remembered. During a Week 6 Monday night game between the Bears and Commanders in the same venue, the lights didn’t go off at 10:00 p.m. ET.

Here’s hoping they come up with a better system for remembering to shut off the automatic shutoff for future Monday night games played there.
