George Pickens' TD has Cowboys with a 10-7 lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

George Pickens is back and has his first touchdown since Oct. 26.

The Cowboys wide receiver caught a 35-yard touchdown from Dak Prescott, a play after Javonte Williams recovered his own fumble.

Pickens, who missed Week 9 with a hamstring injury, has the Cowboys leading 10-7. He has four catches for 51 yards after two possessions.

Dak Prescott is 4-for-9 for 51 yards and a touchdown.

The Cowboys went 71 yards in 11 plays for their touchdown after an eight-play, 35-yard field goal drive.

The Cowboys list cornerback DaRon Bland as questionable to return with an ankle injury.
