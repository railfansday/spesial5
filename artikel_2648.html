Week 11 Fantasy Football Waiver Wire Pickups: Kaelon Black among top adds with 6 teams on bye, rash of injuries

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Every Monday during the season, Justin Boone breaks down the recommended waiver-wire pickups.

Roster percentages are generated from Yahoo leagues. Free-Agent Budget (FAB) amounts are based on a $100 salary cap. Only players rostered in less than 50% of leagues are considered.

The Titans are pushing the pace on offense and are using a no-huddle approach at one of the highest rates in the league. That's helped Ward to his two best fantasy results of the season in the last two games, finishing as the QB4 in Week 8 and coming off the bye he's sitting as the QB12 with only Monday Night Football left to be played.

Ward has also done this without his top receiver Carnell Tate, who's missed the last two outings but is expected to return for their Week 11 matchup with the Cowboys. It's the right time to get back since Tennessee is third in neutral pace of play and Dallas leads the league in that category — setting up a massive opportunity for fantasy scoring.

It's also worth noting that Ward has one of the five easiest fantasy schedules for QBs the rest of the way, including matchups with the Commanders (Week 13), Lions (Week 14), Colts (Week 15) and Raiders (Week 16). Though I'm still only valuing Ward as a fantasy QB2 streamer, don't be surprised if we see the former first-round pick finish hot down the stretch.

Desperation streaming options (if starter is out)

Black has been one of the stars of this column all season long and the fact that he's still available in over 60% of fantasy leagues is a massive mistake by managers.

Just look at what happened with Emmett Johnson last week, when Kenneth Walker III got hurt in practice. The Chiefs' rookie stepped into the starting role and delivered 171 scrimmage yards and two touchdowns. That's the kind of upside Black offers if Christian McCaffrey were ever to miss time.

Even with CMC active, Black's role has grown in recent weeks resulting in 59 yards and TD in Week 7, a bye in Week 8, 70 yards in Week 9 and 68 yards and a TD last week. Most importantly, he's been given at least 11 touches in each of those games. When you're playing in one of the best offenses in the NFL, that kind of volume makes you a strong RB3/flex option with weekly RB2 upside when you find the end zone.

Don't let Black remain on the waiver wire any longer, he should be rostered in 100% of leagues.

Baker Mayfield is banged up and might sit out this week, which would put Jalon Daniels back under center. The rookie performed well in his three starts earlier in the season, but be prepared for the Bucs offense to lean even heavier on the run.

Gainwell has displayed weekly RB3/flex value over the last three games with 88 yards in Week 7, 140 yards in Week 8 and 76 yards in Week 9 before the team went on bye.

Bucky Irving is the unquestioned starter in Tampa Bay's backfield, but Gainwell needs to be rostered everywhere as a flex option with RB1 upside if injuries strike.

Even after a potential Jonathan Taylor trade failed to materialize, the Colts continued to split RB volume with the veteran turning his 18 touches into 103 scrimmage yards and McGowan chipping in with 77 yards and a TD on 12 opportunities.

It's the second consecutive game where the rookie reached the end zone, making him a legitimate RB3/flex option in fantasy, even as Taylor's running mate.

We could also see the team shut Taylor down at some point in the final two months, if he gets even the slightest injury. There's no reason to risk your star on a team that's going nowhere. If that occurs, McGowan would be a fantasy RB2 in whatever games he starts.

Woody Marks suffered a concussion and is unlikely to be cleared before Thursday's game against the Colts. David Montgomery, who hasn't suited up since Week 5 due to a groin injury, returned to practice last week and put in a full session on Friday, but was ultimately ruled out for the game. That puts Montgomery on track to potentially play against the Colts, unless he suffered a setback.

If Montgomery can't go, then Demercado would be the next man up. He's earned 15 touches while playing behind Marks the last two games, only managing 39 scoreless scrimmage yards. In that scenario, Demercado would be a volume-based fantasy RB3.

Jacory Croskey-Merritt is 52% rostered but would be the No. 3 RB add, if available.

Chris Rodriguez Jr. at NYG (37% rostered)

It's easy to forget, but Bryant had a growing role in the Broncos' passing attack during the opening month before being placed on injured reserve with an ankle injury. In those first four games, Bryant topped 40 yards three times.

So, it was great to see him post a season-high 56 yards and a touchdown on four catches in his first game back since the injury. Jaylen Waddle also suffered a concussion, but he should be cleared coming off Denver's Week 10 bye.

Regardless, Bryant was a borderline flex option early in the season and has returned with an even better performance. Given Waddle's struggles and Courtland Sutton's age, there's a non-zero chance Bryant becomes the most valuable fantasy receiver in Denver for the rest of the season.

While CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens get all the headlines, Flournoy has become a very consistent contributor in the Cowboys' passing attack.

The 27-year-old has at least 50 yards and/or a touchdown in six straight games, finishing as a top-24 fantasy WR in five of those outings.

Even as the third pass-catcher in Dallas, Flournoy can be started in fantasy as a WR3/flex option, especially in an uptempo contest against a Titans defense allowing the most points to wideouts.

Despite not earning the kind of volume we'd like to see from a full-time receiver, Williams has been productive most weeks. The rookie has at least 50 yards or a touchdown in three of his last four games since Terry McLaurin was lost for the season.

Until he sees more consistent targets, it's hard to trust Williams as anything more than a flex option. However, his schedule is favorable in the coming weeks with the Bengals, Cardinals, Titans, Texans and Falcons. With Stefon Diggs drawing the attention of top corners, Williams could earn more volume during that span.

Mitchell's outlook is complicated. Omar Cooper Jr. is dealing with an ankle injury that could sideline him for a few games. That would condense the crowded receiver room down to Mitchell and Garrett Wilson, making Mitchell a possible WR3/flex option in fantasy.

Unfortunately, Geno Smith also suffered an ankle injury that might sideline him. Rookie backup Cade Klubnik represents a significant downgrade that would lower the ceiling of all the skill positions players in New York's offense.

So, Mitchell is a tentative add who could be started if Cooper misses time and if Smith doesn't. The fact that I'm writing about him in the waiver wire column is a clear sign we've reached Week 11 and the pickups are getting thin.

Keenan Allen is 59% rostered but would be the No. 2 WR add behind Tyson, if available.

Tank Dell is 51% rostered but would be the No. 6 WR add behind Tyson, Allen, Flournoy, Williams and Mitchell, if available.

Khalil Shakir is 59% rostered but would be the No. 7 WR add behind Tyson, Allen, Flournoy, Williams, Mitchell and Dell, if available.

Dart-throw flex options (if starter is out)

Brock Bowers hurt his ankle on Sunday, but thankfully the team believes he avoided a major injury and should return "sooner rather than later."

Whether that means he'll be ready for their Week 11 date with the Broncos, remains to be seen.

Denver is one of the five easiest matchups for fantasy tight ends, so Mayer would immediately be in the streamer mix if he's called upon to start.

Since McLaurin's season-ending injury, Okonkwo has averaged the ninth-most targets among tight ends and turned in three games with over 50 yards in five outings.

Okonkwo was well on his way to a much bigger day this past week, but his ongoing back injury caused him to miss part of the contest. With Okonkwo not playing his usual snaps, backup Ben Sinnott turned in four catches, 42 yards and a touchdown.

As long as he stays healthy, Okonkwo has moved into the high-end TE2 streamer conversation, with a chance to crack the low-end TE1 range moving forward.

Chiefs defense is 58% rostered but would be the No. 1 DEF add, if available.

Quarterbacks: Lamar Jackson, Aaron Rodgers

Running Backs: Tyler Allgeier, J.K. Dobbins, TreVeyon Henderson, Corey Kiner

Wide Receivers: A.J. Brown, Germie Bernard, DeMario Douglas, Xavier Worthy

Aside from taking advantage of the Cowboys defense in Week 8, Allgeier has struggled to make his mark with Jeremiyah Love on IR. Allgeier only managed 46 yards on 19 touches in Week 9 and turned his five carries into 14 yards on Sunday. With so many injuries to the Cardinals offense, Allgeier isn't likely to provide much value moving forward.

The Patriots have lost Drake Maye for an extended stretch and possibly the rest of the season. That's why you see so many Pats in the drop section, including Brown (who's hurt as well), Douglas, Henderson, Kiner and Henry. There's very little value in New England with Tommy DeVito slated to start when they return from their Week 11 bye.

Since his back to back 80-yard games a month ago, Bernard has failed to top 30 yards in any of his past three outings. With Michael Pittman Jr. and Roman Wilson eventually returning, Bernard can be dropped in favor of a pickup who offers more upside.
