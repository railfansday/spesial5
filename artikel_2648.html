Another Fast Start Dooms Mizzou

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Georgia flexes once again, whipping the Missouri Tigers 48-21.

I have to admit I felt a little bit of "home field" trepidation considering the last time the Red 'n Black graced Dooley Field at Sanford Stadium the outcome wasn't very pleasant. It took all of 1 play to set the 'ol mind at ease when Raylen Wilson picked off Austin Simmons on a beautiful defensive play that set up a Nate Frazier touchdown run and Georgia was off and running.

Georgia forced Missouri 4 early "three-n-outs" while scoring on 4 of its first five possessions and essentially putting the game on ice before the half.

Gunner Stockton was once again typically efficient, spreading the ball around all evening to 7 different receivers before being rested in the 4th quarter. His impressive stat line: 18/24, for 252 yards and 1 passing touchdown, 1 running touchdown.

Craig Dandridge led all receivers with 6 receptions for 85 yards while Talyn Taylor tacked on 3 for 63 yards, including a spectacular twisting fingertip grab from Ryan Puglisi late that will surely make some highlight reels.

Isiah Canion, in his first meaningful return since the Arkansas game, hauled in 3 catches for 63 yards and a touchdown. Having his big-bodied presence back bodes well for the stretch run and beyond.

Nate Frazier finished with 72 yards of 14 carries and 3 touchdowns. Georgia ran the ball 34 times for 172 on a team that was allowing less than a hundred yards per game.

Defensively, Georgia was very stout. No sacks were recorded, but the defensive racked up 5 TFL's and were nasty until "The Renegades" entered the game late and gave up some scores in garbage time. Justin Williams led the way the 8 solo tackles. The secondary played well, highlighted by Ellis Robinson's excellent pass breakup.

Special teams – outside of a muffed punt late by Tyriq Green who has returned since a high-ankle sprain in week 2 – were special. Peyton Woodring was 2/2 in field goals, and Sacovie White-Helton had solid game with a 30 yard punt return and a 58 kickoff return to open the 2nd half.

This team will only lose if it beats itself. Georgia had some lapses in concentration, particularly in the 2nd quarter, that Kirby Smart was quite "vocal" about much to the delight of the broadcast booth, the crowd in the stadium (hot mic moment) and the nation. That kind of ammunition for Kirby to use going into a tough game in Columbia, SC next Saturday night is alright by me.
