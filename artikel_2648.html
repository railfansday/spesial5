Ravens Snap Count Analysis, Week 11: Mike Green shines again, T.J. Tampa steps into larger role

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Ravens played 65 total offensive snaps and, in what has been a rare occasion this season, the starting quarterback and all five offensive linemen played all of them. The five-man group up front had a better performance than in recent weeks, particularly in run blocking.

Justice Hill led the backfield in snaps as the Ravens played up-tempo a good amount. Derrick Henry also exited the game late with a possible concussion, which left Hill as the lone option. Both players were effective, although they each had costly lost fumbles. Henry had over 100 scrimmage yards and a touchdown, while Hill also scored and averaged 11.5 yards per carry.

Baltimore's defense was on the field for 71 plays as the Panthers put together six total scoring drives. On the defensive line, Travis Jones played 44 snaps and Broderick Washington took on a season-high snap share with Nnamdi Madubuike sidelined. David Olagija was a game-day elevation and played 21 snaps, ahead of both Aeneas Peebles and John Jenkins.

Mike Green had another big performance and led all edge rushers with 51 snaps, racking up two sacks and two tackles for loss with a safety forced. Trey Hendrickson closely trailed him in snap count (47) and had a pass breakup and quarterback hit, but is still searching for his third sack of the year. Zion Young and Ethan Burke combined to play just over 40 snaps.

Roquan Smith was the only defender to play every down, and Trenton Simpson was not far behind him with 60 snaps at linebacker. At safety, Jaylinn Hawkins' continued absence is allowing both K'Von Wallace (27 snaps) and Keondre Jackson (14) to see expanded playing time. Kyle Hamilton and Malaki Starks played their usual near every-down roles.

The biggest riser on defense was T.J. Tampa, who took advantage of his opportunity to play more with Marlon Humphrey absent. In 40 snaps, Tampa had two passes defended with an interception in the end zone to prevent a touchdown in the second half. Tampa out-snapped veteran Chidobe Awuzie (31 snaps), while Keyon Martin maintained his usual workload as the nickel corner.
