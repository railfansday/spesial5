Fred VanVleet is quietly having his most efficient season for Rockets

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Despite the tough loss to the Denver Nuggets on Friday, the Houston Rockets are off to a good start to the season. The Rockets are 12-5, even though they have had one of the toughest schedules to start the season. The Rockets are getting ready for a new week after a weekend off, before starting a four-game homestand on Monday as they welcome the Los Angeles Clippers.

The Rockets, as expected, are led by Alperen Sengun and Kevin Durant, who have built great chemistry over their first two seasons together. Sengun, in particular, is off to a great start as the only player in the league leading his team in points, rebounds, and assists. Sengun is also having the best season of his career from the 3-point range, shooting 50 percent and attempting a career-high two 3-pointers a game.

Durant continues, even two decades into his career, to be one of the best players in the NBA, remaining one of the league's most efficient players even while taking a career-low number of shots per game. Another player, however, whose return is one of the biggest reasons for the Rockets' good start to the season is Fred VanVleet.

When it was announced that VanVleet would be ready for the start of the season, everyone expected him to have a positive impact as a leader on and off the court, but so far he has been much more than a distributor; he is having the most efficient season of his career.

When VanVleet first arrived in Houston, the Rockets were a team in transition. They had been through several years of rebuilding, finishing at the bottom of the standings each season as they looked to bring in high draft picks. The plan worked, as they landed multiple top-five picks, but it didn't lead to wins immediately. The Rockets knew it was time to pivot from rebuilding to winning, and that started with bringing in players like Steven Adams, Jeff Green, Dillon Brooks, and, more importantly, VanVleet.

VanVleet came in as a former All-Star and NBA champion, which is exactly what the Rockets needed. The veteran point guard's calming presence paid immediate dividends: the Rockets went on a 19-win turnaround in his first season, the best in the NBA that season.

The Rockets realized how important VanVleet is to the team as he became the Rockets' on- and off-court leader from day one. If anyone outside the organization doubted his importance, last season confirmed it: VanVleet missed the entire season, and the Rockets at times struggled without his guiding hand, especially on offense.

With VanVleet back leading the team, the Rockets have a top-five offense and are much better organized to start the season. However, it isn't just his leadership that has shone this season. VanVleet's offense, especially his efficiency, has been on another level.

In VanVleet's second season, his offensive production dipped. VanVleet had his second-worst 3-point and field-goal percentages of his career in 2024-25, and some people started to question whether he had lost a step on the offensive end of the court.

That notion was amplified as many believed he would struggle again this season, coming off a major leg injury at 32. However, that hasn't been the case, as VanVleet is not only bouncing back offensively; he's also having the most efficient season of his career.

VanVleet has career highs in effective field goal percentage, true shooting percentage, and overall field goal percentage, and his .392 from the 3-point range is the second-best of his career. This is actually more impressive than his career high of .414 in his second season because it is coming on almost four more attempts per game. Probably the most impressive part of his game this season has been his shooting inside the arc, as for the first time in his career, he is shooting over 50 percent from the 2-point range at .517.

The original theory when Durant first arrived in Houston was that players like VanVleet would have an easier time getting good shots with all the attention Durant draws from the defense. That plan was put on hold when VanVleet missed the entire 2025-26 season, but it is bearing fruit this season, as VanVleet has taken full advantage of the double teams Durant and Sengun see every game.

Just having VanVleet back as the leader of the team is a big deal for the Rockets, but he's not only brought his calming presence back; he has also brought the most efficient offense of his career and a big reason for the Rockets' strong start to the season.
