Tony Romo re-emerges, as an employee of a gambling company

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The unofficial path for a sports-media second act is becoming more and more clear.

Tony Romo, whose career with CBS ended earlier this year in the aftermath of a DUI arrest that would have been overlooked if the company was otherwise happy with his work, will be the “chief predictions officer” at Betr.

The press release leans in to Romo’s reputation for being able to predict plays before they happen. This overlooks the fact that he got away from doing that after his first year or so in the CBS booth. It also glosses over the reality that his access to practice and production meetings equipped him with the ability to make “predictions” that (based on his inside information) also could be called “spoilers.”

It’s not entirely clear what he’ll be doing. The release says that Romo will “serve as a prominent face of the Betr brand, appearing in original content, delivering sports predictions and engaging fans across the company’s expanding ecosystem of gaming products.”

Of course, Betr won’t want his predictions to be too accurate — as it relates to its sportsbook. The sportsbooks only win when the customers lose. If the customers win too much, the sportsbook will land in one of two chapters: 11 or 7.

Prediction markets are different. The companies make money on the transactions, with the participants balancing out wins and losses in the zero-sum game of who’s right and who’s wrong.

The next question is whether this is the first step for Romo, or whether it’s his primary post-CBS play. With the NFL and its broadcast partners aligned with the Coke and Pepsi of sports gambling, Romo’s relationship with one of the various companies that hopes to establish itself as the RC Cola won’t improve his shot at getting back into a prominent position in a broadcast booth.

Regardless, Romo is back. At least he’s not working for an illegal offshore sportsbook.

Either way, his message will be the same to the masses as it is to those who are looking for a fresh foothold in sports media.
