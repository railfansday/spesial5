The U.S. Justice Department is seeking to recover about $1.17 billion in USDC tied to the Oct. 29 Binance hack, according to a civil forfeiture complaint seen by The Block.

Strategy bought another 457 BTC for $29.6 million last week, bringing its total holdings to 851,305 BTC, according to an SEC filing.

The CFTC's proposed prediction market rules have reached the White House for review, marking another step in the agency's effort to clarify its oversight of event contracts.

Sky Protocol cofounder Rune Christensen said he may sell part of his SKY holdings to institutional investors to repay roughly 77 million USDS in outstanding loans.

A solo bitcoin miner with just 11.1 TH/s of computing power solved a block on Sunday, earning 3.129 BTC worth nearly $191,000 before fees.
