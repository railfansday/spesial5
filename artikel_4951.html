<b><strong>Eadem Le Chouchou Lip Balm Is My Holy Grail for Chapped Lips</strong></b>

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

It moisturizes like a lip mask, buffs away dry flakes without a scrub, and comes in six shades.

Chapped lips are a real bummer this time of year. They’re uncomfy and unsightly—and often feel inevitable thanks to cold weather and dry indoor air. After trying what feels like a seemingly infinite number of lip balms, I finally found my dream solution: Eadem Le Chouchou Lip Softening Balm. Over the past two years, it has quickly become one of my beauty MVPs. TBH, that’s a distinction that I don’t take lightly since I’m incredibly picky about what I use in my daily life and what I can confidently suggest for you to buy. (I fell for the hype with a certain mascara in a pink and green tube in middle school and refuse to waste my money again.) But Eadem’s combination of nourishing and exfoliating ingredients, variety of buildable shades, and a glossy sheen have kept it in the top spot of my daily routine, especially during the fall and winter.

I recently crowned Eadem Le Chouchou as the best overall product in our guide to the best lip balms. It ticks everything off my wish list and helped me recognize that I deserve more from a lip balm.

What I love most about Le Chouchou is that I no longer need to splurge on a lip balm and scrub. Eadem combines the two products into one. A mix of lactic acid and hibiscus enzymes gently buff away dry, flaky skin. By morning, my parched, cracked lips are pillowy soft and smooth again. It’s a must for cool weather and for makeup prep to ensure my pout remains baby soft before applying my Charlotte Tilbury Pillow Talk Medium Lipstick.

Le Chouchou is also packed with an incredible lineup of ingredients that keep my pout hydrated with each swipe, including a nourishing and antioxidant-rich blend of Brazilian açaí and African mongongo butters. Meanwhile, moringa oil provides non-greasy, deep hydration that supports my skin barrier. It even contains a collagen-boosting peptide that smooths the look of my natural lip lines, creating a fuller-looking pout without stinging. Together, these ingredients keep my lips soft and moisturized for hours.

After a few applications, I can go a full day or two without using this balm because the formula is that nourishing. The texture is also impressive—it’s similar to an intense lip mask without the thick, hard-to-spread texture. My lips feel protected without feeling overly thick and goopy. Plus, it comes in a mess-free tube that tucks away neatly in my purse.

I talk about Eadem so often that it has become the hot topic of my group chats. Even the Women’s Health and Men’s Health team, including digital designer Taryn Colbert and style editor Joseph Cheatham, have become big fans after my recommendations. “It has the qualities I look for in a lip product which are to keep my lips looking glossy while actually moisturizing them at the same time,” says Colbert. Meanwhile Cheatam appreciates the clear version for daily wear. He can get away with a light, thin layer for moisture without looking overly shiny.

Need another reason to snag this lip balm? It’s available in six colors, plus a clear option on Amazon. My silver tube of Butter Mochi—a classic clear—is on its last few squeezes and I plan on restocking it, stat. Other gorgeous hues range from seasonal shades, like Boba Bounce, a rich brown, and Bissap Glaze, a deep plum berry to Fig Sauce, a milky rosy brown, and Burnt Malai, a milky desert rose. They all look beautiful on their own or layered with lip liner and lipstick.

Eadem is my ride-or-die lip balm. It’s honestly held me down for two years and it has quickly become the hot topic of my group chats. However, if you’re curious what other lip balms I’ve tried and enjoyed, consider these selections. They range from a $5 Amazon find to a multipurpose powerhouse that can even be used as a hand cream and face primer too.
