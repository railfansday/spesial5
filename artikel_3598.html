Sarah Paulson Explains Why She Doesn’t Live With Partner Holland Taylor

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Sarah Paulson is pulling back the curtain on her living arrangement with longtime partner Holland Taylor.

“We met when I was already 40 and she was already 72,” Paulson, 51, said on the Monday, November 16, episode of “The Run-Through With Vogue” podcast. “Stories had been written and ways in which we moved through the world had been decided and she’s a really independent person.”

Paulson and Taylor, 83, first crossed paths in 2005 at a party and later reconnected, confirming their romance one decade later. During Monday’s episode, Paulson recalled a moment “early on” in their relationship where she bought a house and “worried” whether it was atypical for her and Taylor not to be residing together.

“When I was sort of like … ‘Is it weird that we are not living together?’” Paulson said. “She was like, ‘Well, you did just buy a house, so what are you asking me about it for as if it’s something I’m doing? You made that choice.’ I was like, ‘Oh, right. Well, I never owned a home. And I thought it would be good to do so.’”

She continued, “I don’t even think about like, ‘Oh absence makes the heart go fonder’ — although I do think it does, and it can — but I also just think there is something about the respect for each other’s individual space. Where it’s like you don’t have to force it upon one another in order to be intimate and connected and together.”

Paulson explained that her living situation with the Morning Show actress “feels really good” to her, while noting that it’s “not necessarily a traditional way of doing something.”

“But neither is being with a person 32 years older than you, is not traditional either,” she added. “There’s something about the story that just is what it is. I’m not here to convince anybody why it works, why it doesn’t work, what works for me, why I like it, you know. But it’s great.”

Paulson noted that she has currently been “cohabitating more” while dealing with “drama” surrounding coyotes on her property and navigating construction on her home.

“She’s often in New York working or she’s in Texas shooting that show Brothers with Matthew McConaughey and Woody Harrelson. I was doing [American] Horror Story and traveling back and forth with All’s Fair,” she said. “So we are together far more than it sounds like we are.”
