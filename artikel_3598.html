Miley Cyrus Reacts to 2027 Grammy Nomination With Godmother Dolly Parton

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Miley Cyrus is reflecting on her lifelong bond with her late godmother and mentor, Dolly Parton, following their joint 2027 Grammy Award nomination.

“My Aunt Dolly is one of the greatest songwriters the world has ever known,” Cyrus, 33, said via a Monday, November 16 tweet. “She never made music for recognition but to make the world a happier place through her songs.”

Cyrus and Parton received a Best Country Duo/Group Performance Grammy nomination on Monday for their remake of “Light of a Clear Blue Morning” alongside Lainey Wilson, Queen Latifah and Reba McEntire. Parton was also nominated a second time in that same category for her and the Zac Brown Band’s duet, “Butterfly.”

The 2026 reinterpretation of Parton’s inspirational 1977 single “Light of a Clear Blue Morning” was released to raise money for pediatric cancer research at Nashville’s Monroe Carell Jr. Children’s Hospital at Vanderbilt.

Reflecting on her latest Grammy nomination, Cyrus shared that it was particularly meaningful since Parton gifted her a vinyl copy of her 1977 album New Harvest…First Gathering — featuring the original version of “Light of a Clear Blue Morning” — “a couple Christmases ago.”

“To be nominated alongside her, Lainey Wilson, Queen Latifah and Reba McEntire for Light of a Clear Blue Morning is a beautiful way to honor her enormous heart,” Cyrus tweeted on Monday. “This cover supported pediatric cancer research at Vanderbilt’s Monroe Carell Jr. Children’s Hospital, a cause close to her heart.”

She added, “I miss her calling on big days like this but feel her in every moment. Love you Aunt Dolly. Congratulations to these beautiful women and all the other nominees.”

Cyrus publicly mourned her mentor when Parton’s rep announced the legendary country music singer-songwriter’s death at age 80 following “a brief battle with cancer” in August.

“The feeling of losing my Aunt Dolly is beyond what feels right to share. Our most sacred moments were private and they’ll stay there behind the stage, beneath the glamour & between our hearts,” Cyrus wrote via Instagram on August 25. “What I KNOW is Dolly would want me to feel it, write a song about it, and then continue on and be strong. I have an angel by my side. I always have. One of our last conversations was about music and metamorphosis. 🦋.”

The “Wrecking Ball” singer continued, “I will always love her and want to make her proud. My heart breaks for the world, her family and friends who will miss her deeply. We’re in this loss together and we will see her in all of the most beautiful things.”

Cyrus broke down on stage during an October show at the Hollywood Bowl, revealing she’d only ever seen Parton perform “live in concert one time in my entire life” — and it was at the very same historic Los Angeles area venue.

“Now, I spent a lot of time backstage and … if she was playing the show, I’d be standing right beside her in a matching costume,” she beamed.

The 69th Annual Grammy Awards will air on ABC and Disney+ Sunday, February 7, 2027, from Los Angeles’ Crypto.com Arena.
