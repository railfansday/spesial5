‘It’s great to be Norwegian’, says Haaland after ‘craziest’ year

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Erling Haaland quipped it is "great to be Norwegian", after a "crazy" 2026 was capped off in dramatic fashion.

Haaland scored a 96th-minute penalty on Tuesday as Norway overcame Denmark 3-2 in the Nations League.

That victory saw Norway finish above Denmark in Nations League Group A4 and progress into the quarter-finals.

Rasmus Hojlund and Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg gave Denmark a 2-0 lead inside 14 minutes at Parken Stadium.

But Haaland halved the deficit before the break and, after Martin Odegaard had restored parity, Norway's star striker struck the decisive blow from the spot deep in stoppage time.

"This has been one of the craziest years in football I've ever experienced," Haaland said after the game. "It's great to be Norwegian!"

Norway reached the quarter-finals of this year's World Cup, losing to England but beating Brazil en route.

Stale Solbakken's team won eight of their 16 matches in 2026, scoring 30 goals.

Their final goal of the year was netted at 95 minutes and 45 seconds, marking the latest Haaland has scored for his country.

"[Coach] Stale said before the match: 'Let's just round off what has been an absolutely incredible year of football on a high note,'" Haaland said.

"We completely dominated them in the second half."

Haaland's view is backed up by the metrics, with Norway creating chances worth 2.31 expected goals in the second half, to Denmark's 0.11 xG.

Norway had 11 shots after the break, with Denmark managing just three.
