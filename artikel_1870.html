Christos Tzolis: Arsenal handed huge injury boost as star overcomes MRI scare

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Christos Tzolis starts for Greece this evening in what will come as a relief to Arsenal.

The winger was a fitness concern for both his club and his country on Monday evening, having felt some muscular discomfort in his side’s Nations League clash with the Netherlands last week.

The forward underwent an MRI scan over the weekend in a bid to uncover the issue, but results came back positive.

As such, it meant that he could feature for Greece on Monday against Serbia, and he was named from the off on the left flank.

The former Norwich City winger missed four weeks with a hamstring injury recently in a blow to the Premier League champions, but he returned in the surprise 1-0 win over Liverpool at Anfield, where Kai Havertz netted the only goal of the game on Merseyside.

He has failed to score a single goal so far for his new club since his summer move to Club Brugge, and he has provided just one assist in seven Premier League matches so far this term.

The Gunners take on Newcastle United this weekend at St James’ Park before a Champions League clash with Borussia Dortmund.

Manchester City visit the Emirates Stadium next weekend in what could prove to be an enthralling encounter.
