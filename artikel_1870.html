‘I’d rather go to the Champions League than win the derbies and finish 15th’, says Pellegrini

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Real Betis boss Manuel Pellegrini revealed he "would rather go to the Champions League and not win than win the derbies and finish 15th".

Betis take on rivals Sevilla in LaLiga on Saturday, with both sides looking to make a push for the Champions League places this season.

Betis are fourth after 10 games, seven points behind leaders Barcelona following a 2-0 win over Elche before the international break.

Sevilla, meanwhile, are eight points behind their rivals in sixth ahead of their clash at Estadio Ramon Sanchez Pizjuan.

Pellegrini has won two of his last three LaLiga games against Sevilla (D1), after failing to win any of his previous 10 (D6 L4). Away from home, he has only won three of his 15 matches against Sevilla in the competition (D3 L9).

The former Manchester City boss acknowledged the importance of the rivalry, but has bigger goals in mind for his Betis side.

"I expect Sevilla to have a clear, very direct style of football, they play in their own half and will be a very dangerous team," said Pellegrini.

"The approaches are different every year because the squads are different. Rivalry is always positive as long as it stays on the pitch and not off the field. We will try again to overcome them through our style of play.

"For the fans, winning all the derbies would be important. But if I had to choose, I'd rather go to the Champions League and not win than win the derbies and finish 15th.

"The most important thing is to get the three points. The best way to win is to maintain the same style of play we showed against other opponents. Playing away from home doesn't change our fundamental approach."

Lucien Agoume has regained possession 65 times in the 2026-27 LaLiga season; only Barcelona's Pedri (69) has completed more recoveries among outfield players in the competition this term.

Marc Bartra has scored three goals in eight LaLiga games this season; only Atletico Madrid's Alejandro Grimaldo (four) has scored more among all defenders in the competition this term.

Sevilla have lost two of their last three LaLiga matches against Betis (D1) after not losing any of their previous 12 against them in the competition (W7 D5).

Sevilla have also picked up points in 11 of their last 13 home LaLiga games against Betis (W9 D2 L2), although they lost the most recent meeting 2-0 in November 2025.

Betis are unbeaten in their last four away LaLiga matches (W2 D2), keeping clean sheets in their last two. They have not recorded three consecutive away clean sheets in the competition since October 2022 (W1 D2).

Sevilla have won just one of their last five LaLiga derbies against Andalusian teams (D1 L3). Betis, however, have lost only one of their last 14 derbies in the competition (W6 D7), but that defeat was against Sevilla (1-0 in October 2024).

Sevilla boss Luis Garcia has faced Pellegrini 11 times in LaLiga, more than any other coach (P11 W2 D4 L5), failing to win any of his last six meetings with him (D4 L2).
