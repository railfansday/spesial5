Report: Liverpool and Arsenal fighting for €60m rated defender

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Left-back has been a problem for some time now at Anfield.

Andrew Robertson was unquestionably one of the best defenders in world football during his peak years, but the legendary fullback had started to decline before his Anfield exit.

His replacement Milos Kerkez has impressed in spells, showing glimpses of greatness, but has been disappointing overall and has failed to make the position his own.

Liverpool are now being linked with several left-backs ahead of the winter window, but they will face heavy competition for one signature with Arsenal also interested.

The Reds are ready to take on the Gunners, but it won't be easy.

Maxim De Cuyper has been one of the standout players in the Premier League this season.

The Belgium international has already recorded four goals and four assists in 16 appearances for Brighton in all competitions, and his impressive form is attracting interest.

Although he has played in a more advanced position at times for the Seagulls this season, De Cuyper is a left-back that could be the long-term solution at Anfield.

According to a report from German outlet Fussball News, Liverpool and Arsenal and PSG are leading the race for the 25-year-old, who Brighton value at €60 million (~£50m).

AC Milan, Juventus and Roma are all also reportedly in the chase for De Cuyper, who moved to the Premier League from Club Brugge in 2025.

Liverpool have been interested in De Cuyper since before his move to Brighton, and the dynamic fullback was considered as an option before the club signed Milos Kerkez.

With so many top teams interested, it is not hard to imagine a world in which De Cuyper's final price ends up significantly higher than the €60 million Brighton are reportedly asking for.

With Liverpool's spend since winning the league title in 2025 already over £650 million, the club may be reluctant to enter a bidding war with the likes of PSG and Arsenal.

De Cuyper would be a great addition at Anfield, but a deal won't be straight forward.
