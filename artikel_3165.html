Here's What a $1,000 Investment in Broadcom Stock Could Be Worth By 2035

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Broadcom's AI chip business is growing at an extraordinary pace. Here's how much a $1,000 investment could be worth by 2035 if the company delivers on its growth potential.

By Matt DiLallo – Nov 22, 2026 at 2:02PM EST

Broadcom (AVGO -12.19%) has become one of the biggest winners of the artificial intelligence (AI) infrastructure boom. Demand for its custom AI chips and networking gear is surging as tech giants pour billions of dollars into data centers. Last quarter, its AI semiconductor revenue rocketed 221% to $16.7 billion, and management expects that business to double in fiscal 2027 and again in fiscal 2028.

The stock, however, already prices in a lot of that success. While it currently trades about 30% below its 52-week high, it's still up nearly 250% over the past three years. Here's a look at what a $1,000 investment in Broadcom could be worth by 2035.

Broadcom's fiscal 2026 third quarter was its best yet. Total revenue rose 86% to $29.6 billion, and AI semiconductor revenue climbed 221% to $16.7 billion. For the fourth quarter, the company guided to $21.7 billion in AI revenue, up 236%. Management has also laid out a path to roughly $58 billion of AI semiconductor revenue in fiscal 2026, $115 billion in fiscal 2027, and $230 billion in fiscal 2028. Custom accelerators for customers such as Anthropic and OpenAI drive that growth, along with the Ethernet switching and optical connections that link those chips together.

Broadcom isn't just an AI chip story. Its infrastructure software business, anchored by VMware, recorded nearly $8.8 billion in revenue last quarter, up 29%. That high-margin business generates growing recurring revenue for the company.

Investor expectations are as big as the AI opportunity. So are the risks. Broadcom depends on a small group of AI infrastructure customers, so a spending pullback by even one could hurt. AI spending could also cool once the current build-out peaks, and some customers may struggle to finance or monetize their data centers. Worries about AI financing and customer concentration have already driven a sell-off in chip stocks.

Margins are another watch item. As lower-margin custom accelerators account for a larger share of sales, margins could shrink. Competition from rivals and in-house chip designs also adds margin risk.

The question isn't whether AI infrastructure spending will grow over time. It's whether Broadcom can deliver the profits that investors already assume.

Broadcom recently traded around $325 per share. Analysts expect about $19.39 in adjusted earnings per share (EPS) for fiscal 2027, which puts the stock at roughly 17 times that estimate. Wall Street is bullish: none of the 50 analysts covering the company rate it a sell, and the average price target is over $525 a share. That estimate already assumes more than 60% upside from the recent price.

However, even in a conservative base case, Broadcom could deliver a nearly 150% return. Meanwhile, the bull case is even stronger.

Broadcom has a compelling opportunity in custom AI chips and networking, and its software business adds another source of profitable growth. Under my base case, a $1,000 investment in the semiconductor stock could grow to about $2,500 by 2035, a nearly 11% annualized total return. That's an attractive return, and it doesn't require Broadcom to sustain its extraordinary near-term growth indefinitely. While the outcome depends on the company meeting very ambitious earnings expectations, Broadcom looks like a compelling AI stock to buy right now.
