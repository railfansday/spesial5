Vista Gold Corp. (VGZ -3.30%) announced a definitive agreement to be acquired by Artemis Gold through a court-approved plan of arrangement. Management reported that the transaction follows a multiyear evaluation of strategic alternatives intended to reduce the execution risks associated with developing the Mt Todd project independently. The company transitioned its focus toward finalizing technical optimization programs and securing environmental permit variations in Australia while maintaining its cash position of $43.5 million. Additionally, the project team increased water management investments at the Mt Todd site to prepare for potential construction starting in 2028.
