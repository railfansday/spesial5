Loar Holdings Inc. (LOAR +4.69%) reported record third-quarter sales and adjusted EBITDA, leading to an upward revision of its 2026 financial outlook and the initiation of 2027 guidance. Management reported that growth was driven by organic performance across commercial and defense end markets, alongside contributions from recent acquisitions. The company indicated it is targeting further margin expansion through operating leverage, value-based pricing, and productivity initiatives. Management stated that the integration of acquired businesses like LMB and Harper Engineering is progressing as expected while the company maintains its strategy of acquiring one to two niche aerospace brands annually.
