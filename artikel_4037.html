Hyperspace Neptunia: Unlimited coming west in 2027 for PS5, Switch 2, PS4, Switch, and PC

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Idea Factory International will release Compile Heart-developed Hyperspace Neptunia: Unlimited for PlayStation 5, Switch 2, PlayStation 4, Switch, and PC via Steam in 2027 in the west, the publisher announced. The physical edition will be available for PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Switch 2 (full game on card).

Hyperspace Neptunia: Unlimited first launched for PlayStation 5, Switch 2, PlayStation 4, and Switch on August 27 in Japan. It is the first mainline Neptunia game since Megadimension Neptunia VII in 2015.

Here is an overview of the game, via Idea Factory:

When the first asteroid struck, the end of the world followed. As despair consumed all, only the Goddess Neptune was left behind in a white void.

A mysterious voice began to guide her, and she discovered numbers etched upon her cheek.

This mark signaled Neptune’s new challenge against Doomsday.

She activated a “retry,” and thus began her loop of endings and beginnings.

Neptune encounters new allies and enemies and must overcome countless defeats as she and her friends confront the true Doomsday.
