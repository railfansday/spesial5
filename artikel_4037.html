It's Official, Zelda: Ocarina Of Time Has Sold Over 5 Million Copies In 4 Days

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Nintendo has announced that The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time has sold a total of 5.2 million units in just four days.

This number includes 810,000 copies sold in Japan alone, and makes Ocarina of Time the fastest-selling Nintendo-published Switch 2 game since the console's launch. For added context, the N64 original sold an overall total of 7.6 million units, according to Video Game Sales wiki.

This also makes it the second highest-selling Switch 2 game, with initial sales putting it above Donkey Kong Bananza's lifetime sales at 5.05 million (as of 30th September 2026). The best-selling Switch 2 game remains Mario Kart World at 16.48 million.

It's not clear what the digital/physical split is at the time of writing, though we should have a slightly better picture of this once Famitsu releases the Japanese boxed charts later this week.

Nintendo Co., Ltd. (HQ: Kyoto, Minami-ku, Japan; President and Representative Director: Shuntaro Furukawa, “Nintendo” hereafter) announced that The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for Nintendo Switch 2 sold over 5.2 million units worldwide (including 810,000 units sold in Japan) in the first four days following its launch on November 5, 2026.

This is the highest global sales for any Nintendo-published Nintendo Switch 2 game in its first four days.

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for Nintendo Switch 2, released worldwide on November 5, is a full remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, originally released for Nintendo 64 in 1998. Link travels between two time periods, as Young Link and Adult Link, making use of different abilities and items available in each period to progress through his adventure. Developed for Nintendo Switch 2, the game features reimagined visuals and enhanced playability, while preserving the experience of the original game.

The game also supports ZELDA NOTES, a service available within the Nintendo Switch App for compatible smart devices. By using ZELDA NOTES, players can access a variety of features that support gameplay.

In the UK, it was confirmed that Ocarina of Time was the second biggest physical launch in series history, coming in just behind Tears of the Kingdom. It stormed to the top of the charts to become the biggest physical video game launch of 2026 (so far – hi, GTA).

As for our review, we awarded Ocarina of Time a 9/10, stating it to be "the best way to play one of the most seminal games of all time":

"Ocarina of Time on Switch 2 is a stellar reimagining of the original, one that completely revamps the experience with stunning visuals, modernised gameplay, and vastly improved audio. Despite some minor blemishes, this is comfortably the most visually stunning work Nintendo has ever put out, and it’s unquestionably the best way to play one of the most seminal games of all time."
