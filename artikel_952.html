Anyone who watched Unai Emery's post-match interview after Aston Villa's 4-4 draw with Manchester United 13 days ago may have noticed that the Spaniard was not in the best of moods.

While conceding two last-gasp goals at Old Trafford was infuriating, it was also the latest in a string of frustrating results that have seen Villa drop six points from winning positions since the start of October.

They head into today's visit of Sunderland on a four-match winless run in all competitions and Emery will be desperate to change that as he reaches 150 Premier League games in charge of the club.

Matty Cash is sidelined after he was concussed on international duty with Poland, while Johan Manzambi's availability is in doubt having picked up an injury while away with Switzerland. Aaron Wan-Bissaka could return to boost Emery's defensive options.

Sunderland manager Regis Le Bris also has to deal with some key absentees, including striker Brian Brobbey and captain Granit Xhaka.

Those injuries are a major blow for the Black Cats, who secured only their second league win of the season against Chelsea a fortnight ago and will hope that result can be a springboard to climb out of the relegation zone.

They start the weekend just one point behind 17th-place Coventry City and now would be the perfect time to claim their first away victory of the Premier League campaign, having taken a single point from five games on their travels.

Le Bris will be boosted by the return of centre back Omar Alderete, who served a one-match suspension against Chelsea.

Villa's home form has been strong recently, while the absence of Brobbey limits the Black Cats' attacking threat.
