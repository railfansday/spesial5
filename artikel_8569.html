Use Ibotta if you buy name-brand groceries from a list, since you add its offers before each trip and collect cash back afterward, by scanning the receipt or, at stores like Walmart, through a linked account. If you mostly buy store brands, expect fewer of its offers to match your cart.

Ibotta is a free app that pays you cash back on products you pick from its offers before you shop, in store or online, and it suits shoppers who buy name brands. Brands pay Ibotta for each sale their offers bring, and only its Any Brand and Any Receipt offers let you choose any brand.

Ibotta pays best for shoppers who plan their list around name brands and shop where they can link an account, like Walmart. Shoppers who mostly buy store brands do better with receipt scanning apps, which pay on any receipt, than with Ibotta.
