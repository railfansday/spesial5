Michael Carrick sack news LIVE: Manchester United latest as shock twist emerges

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Manchester United have decided to sack Michael Carrick as manager following the chaotic 4-4 draw with Aston Villa at Old Trafford. The under-fire boss was given Sunday’s draw, and the visit of Roma earlier in the week, which saw Man Utd overturn a 2-0 half-time deficit to win in the Champions League, but it hasn’t proven enough to keep his job.

Widespread reports overnight claim that a decision has finally been made, with Carrick set to depart and a new manager be appointed - though a decision may be delayed until Wednesday or Thursday as Sir Jim Ratcliffe has summoned club chiefs to Monaco. There also remain plenty of questions regarding who the new man could be. Eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann were considered early frontrunners, and Antonio Conte is said to be keen, but it is Mauricio Pochettino who is now the favourite to land the job.

United head into the November international break sitting inside the bottom three amid genuine fears over a potential relegation battle this season, having won just one of their first 10 matches - at home to newly-promoted Ipswich back in August - and collected only eight points with a tough trip to Liverpool to come next. Whoever come in has an almighty task on their hands. Follow the latest on Michael Carrick’s Manchester United future live below!

Antonio Conte will not become the new Manchester United manager.

That is according to Fabrizio Romano, who reports that the speculation around the former Chelsea and Tottenham head coach moving to Old Trafford has “gone cold”.

The Italian had been heavily linked with a switch to the north west in recent weeks as pressure piled on Michael Carrick after a dismal run of form saw the Red Devils win just once in 11 matches, as they lie in the relegation zone of the Premier League table.

BBC Sport claims that instead of sacking Michael Carrick, Manchester United could turn to strengthening his coaching set-up.

Aaron Danks, the Bayern Munich assistant manager, has been linked with a switch to Old Trafford should that happen, having worked with Carrick at Middlesbrough.

Cole Palmer has previously heaped praise on Mauricio Pochettino, amid a transfer theory involving the Chelsea star.

Pochettino was at Chelsea when Palmer was signed in a deal worth £45million from Manchester City on transfer Deadline Day in September 2023.

To this day, there is a strong argument that Palmer played his best club football under the Argentine.

During their one season together, Palmer was the stand-out player in a youthful Chelsea side, leading the scoring charts with 25 goals in 45 appearances across all competitions.

That form led to Palmer being named Men's Young Player of the Year at the 2024 London Football Awards and it was during the ceremony where he spoke about his relationship with Pochettino.

"He speaks to you about anything, like family issues,” Palmer said. “I think if he knows what's going on off the pitch, it can affect how he is with you on the pitch. He's really great at that.

"He's given me the freedom to play my stuff and I like it when he frees you because I feel I can express my stuff more.

"I'm loving it. Every day he's talking to you about different things, not just football things but life things as well. He's good at making you feel good and trying to get the best out of you."

Mauricio Pochettino is expected to address the media this week, though he is likely to be quizzed about links to Manchester United rather than the USMNT’s upcoming fixture.

Pochettino, who signed a contract extension with the national team back in August - taking him through to the 2030 World Cup, is busy preparing for this weekend’s Concacaf Nations League quarter-final against Haiti.

However, the build-up to the game has been somewhat disrupted by talk of the Argentine becoming the next Man United manager.

Following United’s 4-4 draw with Aston Villa, Pochettino - out of nowhere - emerged as the new leading contender to replace Michael Carrick in the Old Trafford hotseat.

The former Tottenham and Chelsea boss is now the favourite among bookmakers for the job amid reports Man Utd chief executive Omar Berrada and director of football Jason Wilcox will recommend a managerial change during a scheduled meeting with the Glazer family and minority owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe.

While the possibility of Carrick staying on has not yet been ruled out, a final decision may not come until Thursday - around 24 hours before Pochettino’s pre-Haiti press conference.

Unless more clarity is provided before Pochettino sits down in front of the media to fulfil his usual pre-matchday duties for the USMNT, he will almost certainly be asked about the Man Utd job, thus forcing him to break his silence on the matter.

Manchester United could well be moving towards appointing a new manager.

The Red Devils look set to part company with under-fire head coach Michael Carrick this week, according to widespread reports, with a crisis meeting set to be held in Monaco on Wednesday with club executives including Sir Jim Ratcliffe, Omar Berrada and Jason Wilcox.

Carrick’s position has come under severe scrutiny after just one win in 11 games in all competitions, and United were almost embarrassed at home against Aston Villa at the weekend, falling behind by two goals twice inside the opening minutes of either half.

It was a game that they roared back to draw, with Patrick Dorgu notching an injury-time equaliser, but the level of the overall performance was again cause for concern, with the 20-time Premier League champions mired in the relegation zone after 10 matches.

Michael Carrick is done for the day, with reports he left the training ground an hour ago after overseeing a session with the players not called up for international duty.

Michael Carrick has taken charge of Manchester United first-team training, with the final international break of 2026 officially underway.

Carrick’s presence at Carrington on Tuesday morning came as somewhat of a surprise given widespread reports overnight that he is set to depart as manager.

Instead, an announcement regarding the future of Carrick has been delayed, with BBC Sport reporting that a meeting has been scheduled for Wednesday in Monaco has been scheduled for owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe and executives Omar Berrada and Jason Wilcox.

Carrick will have been working with a limited number of senior players during training on Tuesday as several members of the Man United first-team squad have been selected for international duty.

Interesting developments regarding Jason Wilcox and Omar Berrada.

Despite fierce scrutiny of their positions at Manchester United, Wilcox and Berrada are expected to retain Sir Jim Ratcliffe’s backing .

According to the Telegraph, they still have Ratcliffe’s trust, and a change to their positions is considered unlikely as things stand.

Furthermore, it is understood that Ratcliffe placing his faith in the pair to navigate the club through its current troubles.

This does not bode well for Michael Carrick ahead of Wednesday’s meeting in Monaco as Wilcox and Berrada are expected to recommend a change of manager.

Michael Carrick has already issued a warning to Manchester United chief executive Omar Berrada over his "Project 150" plan.

In 2028, Man United will celebrate its 150th anniversary and Berrada has previously gone on record stating the plan to mark the landmark achievement is by winning both the men's and women's league titles.

In an interview with the United We Stand fanzine, he said: "It's establishing a series of targets within a timeframe so we can focus our efforts and energy on that goal.”

Michael Carrick's future as Manchester United head coach is little closer to being resolved.

Despite widespread reports on Monday night claiming a decision has been made to sack the under-pressure manager, Carrick arrived at the club’s Carrington training ground on Tuesday and oversaw training with a depleted group due to international commitments.

It was expected that Carrick would be sacked on Tuesday, with an official announcement to come soon after, but BBC Sport report that it will be at least another day until their is concrete clarity.

Carrick’s future is set to be discussed at a meeting involving Sir Jim Ratcliffe and senior club executives in Monaco on Wednesday.

Chief executive Omar Berrada and director of football Jason Wilcox on Monday flew out to Monaco for the pre-planned meeting, during which Carrick’s future will be discussed.

BBC Sport add that Berrada and Wilcox are expected to will recommend on Wednesday to minority owner Ratcliffe and the Glazer family that Carrick be dismissed and a new manager be appointed.

A final decision may then not come until Thursday, with Carrick and the club in limbo until official confirmation. Carrick staying has not yet been ruled out.
