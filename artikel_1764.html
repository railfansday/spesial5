Premier League Team of the Week: Dominik Szoboszlai, Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

By Matt Law | 23 Nov 2026 10:01, Last updated: 23 Nov 2026 10:04

Manchester United's struggles continued on Sunday afternoon, as Mauricio Pochettino's first game in charge of the Red Devils ended in a 4-0 defeat to Liverpool at Anfield.

The Red Devils sit 19th in the Premier League table, boasting only eight points from 11 matches, and it has been a disastrous opening to the campaign for the club.

Elsewhere, title favourites Arsenal and Manchester City were held to draws by Newcastle United and Fulham respectively, while Leeds United recorded a standout 4-1 win at Chelsea.

There were also victories for Tottenham Hotspur, Coventry City, Bournemouth and Hull City, while Sunderland held Aston Villa to a goalless draw at Villa Park.

Here, Sports Mole presents its Premier League Team of the Week for Gameweek 11 of the 2026-27 campaign, according to the data.

Sunderland goalkeeper Roefs was in excellent form in his side's goalless draw against Villa, coming up with eight saves, playing a vital role in the Black Cats claiming a point.

Porro provided the assist for Luca Williams-Barnett to send Tottenham ahead in the clash with Ipswich Town, and his use of the ball throughout the contest was excellent, with the right-back showing his Spain performance level in this contest.

Silva was exceptional in the heart of the Bournemouth defence against Nottingham Forest, winning seven aerial duels and making four tackles, while his pass success rate was 85%.

In just his third Premier League appearance of the season, Senesi was excellent against Ipswich, winning two aerial duels, while his pass success rate was up at 91%.

Kerkez has found it difficult to show his best form since arriving at Liverpool, but he put in a dominant performance against Man United on Sunday to help Andoni Iraola's side claim a clean sheet, while he was also a threat going the other way.
