Kane lauds Fulham star King after first senior England call-up

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Harry Kane is sure Josh King has a bright future with England after impressing the Ballon d'Or winner during his time with the Three Lions.

King was called up to Thomas Tuchel's squad for the first time for the November international break, with England facing Croatia and Spain in the Nations League.

The 19-year-old did not feature in either of the victories that secured England's spot at the top of Group A3, but the rising Fulham talent has certainly left an impression on Kane.

King, alongside Liverpool's Rio Ngumoha and Bournemouth's Alex Scott, were called up to England’s training squad ahead of the World Cup.

The Fulham attacker spoke glowingly of Kane, saying his first memories of England came at the World Cup in 2018, when England were beaten in the semi-finals by Croatia.

And Kane has been impressed by what he has seen from King.

"It's crazy. I remember talking to Myles [Lewis-Skelly] a few camps ago, and I was asking him about his early England memories, and he said 2018, and it was crazy," Kane said.

"It makes me feel a bit older, but it's a reminder of how young some of these guys are, and we have given this younger generation a lot of good moments and reasons to cheer.

"It just motivates me to help some of these younger players and newer members in the squad to experience what we have had.

"Josh has been great; he was great in the summer in the prep camp, he's been really good here, and he is having a great season. Everyone fits in pretty nicely into the squad."

King has been Fulham's standout performer this season, registering two goals and an assist in the Premier League so far, with Manchester City up next for the Cottagers.

The Fulham youngster was picked ahead of City's Phil Foden, who was also omitted from the England squad for the World Cup this summer.

Foden has managed eight goal involvements in all competitions this term (four goals, four assists), totals only bettered by Rayan Cherki (10) and Erling Haaland (14) for City.

And despite Foden not being picked for his country, City boss Enzo Maresca has been pleased with his performances, saying: "He's doing well. He's doing well, to be honest.

"The reason why he's been left out of the international team, I don't know, but we're happy with Phil. We know that he can give much more, we know that.

"It's my duty to try to help him slowly to get back and be one of the best."

Only Haaland, Evanilson (both nine), Joao Pedro, and Pascal Gross (both eight) have been involved in more Premier League goals this season than Cherki (seven – five goals, two assists).

Cherki has done so while having the league's highest differential (+3.1) between his goals and expected goals this term (1.9 xG).

He has also second for defensive line-breaking passes (39), while only three players have more line-breaking passes leading to shots than his 20.

City have won their last 17 Premier League games against Fulham, scoring at least twice each time. It’s the longest winning streak one team has had against another in English league history.

The Citizens have also won their last 20 games against Fulham in all competitions, the longest winning streak one team has had against another in English football.

However, Maresca’s side have lost each of their last three Premier League games, as many as in their previous 36 (W25 D8). Since the start of 2007-08, they’ve only lost four in a row twice, doing so in August 2008 and December 2024.

Fulham, meanwhile, are unbeaten in their last four Premier League games (W2 D2), and have lost just one of their last seven (W2 D4). There have been as many goals in their previous seven games (11 – F6 A5) as in their opening three fixtures of the campaign (F4 A7).

But they have conceded at least once in their last 32 Premier League games against City (80 goals in total). Their last clean sheet came in their first visit to the Etihad Stadium in March 2004 with Edwin van der Sar in goal.
