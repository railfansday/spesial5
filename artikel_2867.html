Rams’ Sean McVay Admits One Puka Nacua Decision Has Him ‘Kicking Himself’ After Costly Injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Los Angeles Rams beat the Washington Commanders 20–13 on November 8. However, a late-game injury shifted attention to head coach Sean McVay's decision on the final offensive play.

McVay has since questioned the call as the Rams prepare to play without Puka Nacua.

The Los Angeles Rams defeated the Washington Commanders, earning their third consecutive win. However, wide receiver Puka Nacua injured his right knee on the final offensive play.

Oct 25, 2026; Paradise, Nevada, USA; Los Angeles Rams wide receiver Puka Nacua (12) reacts in the first half against the Las Vegas Raiders at Allegiant Stadium. Mandatory Credit: David Brytus-Imagn Images

Before leaving the game, Nacua caught 14 of 19 targets for 150 yards and a touchdown. He also added two carries for 20 yards. In the fourth quarter, his seven-yard touchdown reception from Matthew Stafford gave the Rams a 20–13 lead. His 150 receiving yards marked his highest single-game total of the 2026 season.

The Rams selected Nacua in the fifth round of the 2023 NFL Draft with the 177th overall pick. He emerged as the team's leading wide receiver during his rookie season and became one of Stafford's most reliable targets.

Speaking to reporters on November 9, McVay said he had revisited the call several times after the game.

"I'm kicking myself about those kinds of things," McVay said. "I'd be lying to you if I said I haven't questioned the decision-making on that last play over and over."

The Rams faced third-and-11 on their final offensive play. Looking to run down the clock, McVay called a jet sweep for Nacua. He gained five yards before Commanders defenders Jeremy Reaves and Sonny Styles tackled him, suffering the injury on the play.

Medical examinations revealed MCL and PCL sprains in Nacua's right knee, along with a turf toe injury. He is expected to miss at least four weeks. The Rams are considering placing him on injured reserve, which would sideline him for at least four games.

Nacua's injury adds to the Rams' receiving concerns. Davante Adams has been sidelined since suffering a hamstring injury against the Arizona Cardinals on October 18. Before the injury, he recorded 30 receptions for 480 yards and four touchdowns in six games. His recovery could take six to 10 weeks.

With both receivers expected to be unavailable, Stafford may rely more on Tutu Atwell, Jordan Whittington, Konata Mumpfield and C.J. Daniels. Tight ends Tyler Higbee, Colby Parkinson and Davis Allen also provide additional options in the passing game.

Through seven games, Nacua had 70 receptions for 715 yards and four touchdowns. The Rams face the Arizona Cardinals on November 15 before their Week 11 bye. They will then play the Green Bay Packers and Kansas City Chiefs.
