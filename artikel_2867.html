Colts officially place Daniel Jones on waivers

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Daniel Jones is officially on the waiver wire.

The NFL transaction report has gone out this afternoon, and Jones appears as a waiver from the Colts. After the trade deadline, every player who gets released by his team has to go on waivers before he becomes a free agent.

That means any team that wants Jones on his current contract can claim him, but that is extraordinarily unlikely to happen. As the waiver transaction notes, Jones is a vested veteran with a fully guaranteed salary for the rest of this season. No team is going to want to claim that salary.

Once he clears waivers, however, he'll be an unrestricted free agent, free to sign with any team, and it won't be surprising if some team wants to add him for veteran depth. Jones has been terrible this season, but he's been a competent starter at times in his career, and a team that wants an experienced backup may be interested in signing him for a minimal salary.

Jones was also waived almost exactly two years ago, on November 22, 2024. He cleared waivers and signed with the Vikings' practice squad before signing with the Colts after that season.
