Bengals News: Salute to Landon Robinson

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

It's November, which means two things when the Cincinnati Bengals are concerned.

One: Joe Burrow and the offense have woken up from their annual offseason slumber (that extended a little too far into the season, as usual).

With Veterans Day just a few days away, the NFL is in its military-appreciation month, and the Bengals happen to have a defensive tackle from the Navy and a Marine on the roster. After being named the Bengals' Salute to Service Award nominee, Landon Robinson spoke with Bengals.com's Geoff Hobson about his time at Navy, his service, and his future before the team left for Spain.

The Bengals selected Robinson with the No. 226 pick in the 2026 NFL Draft. That's the same pick the Arizona Cardinals spent on former NFL safety and former Army Ranger Pat Tillman.

"I took a class where we did a case study on him [Tillman] and learned about his story and what he did in the NFL and made the decision to go serve. When I saw I got drafted 226, 'Wow, that is crazy,'" he said when he was asked about the coincidence by Hobson.
