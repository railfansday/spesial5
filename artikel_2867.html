More tickets available for the home match against Leverkusen

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The visiting club has not taken up its full ticket allocation for the Bundesliga home match against Bayer Leverkusen (29 November, 19:30). Additional Category 5 (€50) and Category 6 (€35) tickets are therefore available in Block 57.

Tickets are available from the online ticket shop. BVB fans can purchase up to six tickets.
