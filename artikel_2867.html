Open Thread: Sean Elliott introduces “shunk” into the Spurs vernacular

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Was it a dunk? Was it a shot? Jared Weiss of The Athletic asked fans that very question. Either way it was Victor Wembanyama doing Victor Wembanyama things.

Jacob Tobey posted a conversation with Sean Elliott in which the Spurs legend officially locked in on "shunk."
