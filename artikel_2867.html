Giannis Antetokounmpo on facing Milwaukee: 'It’s going to be just another game'

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Is anyone believing this? Including Giannis Antetokounmpo?

Wednesday night, the Miami Heat will host Milwaukee and face the Bucks, the first time that Antetokounmpo will play in another uniform against the team where he spent 13 seasons and won an NBA title. It's a game everyone had circled on the calendar, although the big one is Jan. 28 when Miami plays in Milwaukee.

However, when asked about it after the Heat's impressive win against the Rockets Monday night, Antetokounmpo tried to play it down as just another game.

"It's going to be just another game. I'm going to do the same thing. I'm going to eat the same meal. I'm going to kiss my kids before they go to bed, get ready, wake up, do my routine and get ready for the game. It's the same game. It's the same thing. It doesn't change. It doesn't matter the jersey...

"I haven't found myself being emotional. I think the only time I've been emotional was when we won the championship. I think I played my whole career, I haven't like found myself like putting emotion to the game. I feel like emotions are for movies, and this is just basketball."

He can say that because the game is in Miami, but Bucks fans are not going to feel the same way — this is emotional.

Antetokounmpo is the greatest Bucks player of all time and he put together a Hall of Fame resume while in Milwaukee: NBA champion and Finals MVP in 2021, two-time MVP, Defensive Player of the Year, nine All-NBA teams and 10 All-Star teams. He won the team its second NBA title, the first since the 1970s. Beyond the on-court success, he built a life in Milwaukee and invested in the city, doing charity work and more.

The exit got a little messy because of that investment — while there may have been basketball reasons for Antetokounmpo to ask for a trade earlier, he didn't want to be the bad guy forcing his way out of the only city and away from the only franchise he had known. Eventually, he felt he had to if he was going to win another ring, and he landed in Miami for a package of picks and players that will help the Bucks on the long road to rebuilding.

Antetokounmpo and Miami come to Milwaukee having found a groove and winning five straight, including beating a good Houston team to improve to 8-6 (good enough for the No. 4 seed in the East right now). Milwaukee is headed the opposite direction, having lost 10 straight.

It is going to be emotional on Wednesday, even if Antetokounmpo won't admit it.
