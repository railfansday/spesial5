Ravens activate RB Adam Randall from injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Baltimore Ravens activated running back Adam Randall from injured reserve on Wednesday, possibly positioning the sixth-round pick to make his NFL debut on Sunday against the Panthers.

The Ravens now have four IR activations remaining with two more available if they miraculously make the playoff. One is reserved for quarterback Lamar Jackson, and another could be bookmarked for kicker Tyler Loop. When center Jovaughn Gwyn went down in Week 3 with knee injury, there was some hope that he could come back at some point but no updates since. The same is true of second-year cornerback Bilhol Kone, who was designated for return when he was placed on IR during roster cuts but has yet to return to practice. Baltimore also needs to consider the possibility of key players going down in the next few weeks, which could require IR stints before late-season returns.

Randall, 22, landed on injured reserve at the end of the preseason with a foot injury. What was initially suspected to be an IR stash for a young player turned out to be a more serious issue that sidelined him until Week 8. Randall returned to practice on October 28 as a full participant, but the Ravens used his full three-week practice window to preserve roster flexibility as they navigated injuries and the trade deadline.

Baltimore's run game has been stagnant for weeks, so Randall could prove some juice as a change-of-pace back. However, he is unlikely to do much better than Derrick Henry behind a struggling offensive line. Randall's ability to contribute in the pass catching game is unlikely to be utilized given the presence of Justice Hill. However, he is no guarantee to be part of the game day picture with Rasheen Ali entrenched on special teams.

As the Ravens' season slips further and further away, they should consider giving Randall more time. His age, size, and athleticism offer an appealing developmental trajectory that could elevate him to at least a No. 2 role in the backfield while on his rookie contract.

The Ravens also re-signed guard Michael Jordan to the practice squad as additional offensive line depth. He fills the spot opened by kicker Harrison Mevis' release on Tuesday. Riley Patterson hit all four of his kicks – two short field goals and two extra points – in his team debut on Monday night, likely securing him the kicking job until Tyler Loop comes off IR.
