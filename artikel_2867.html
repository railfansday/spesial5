What changed? Rams make stunning Puka Nacua reversal just one day after McVay’s assurances

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

On Wednesday, Los Angeles Rams head coach Sean McVay insisted the team had no plans to place star wide receiver Puka Nacua on injured reserve, citing what he called "roster semantics."

By Thursday afternoon, those semantics apparently went out the window.

The Rams announced they were placing Nacua on IR after all, guaranteeing their leading receiver will miss at least four games as he recovers from knee and toe injuries suffered during Sunday's 20-13 victory over the Washington Commanders.

"Puka [Nacua] with his knee/toe. We're not going to put him on IR because of some semantics with the roster and things like that, but he still is going to be out for an extended period of time," McVay said Wednesday. "It wasn't like any sort of new information."

Apparently, the roster math changed overnight.

Nacua will now be sidelined through the Rams' Dec. 13 showdown with the San Francisco 49ers, making their Dec. 20 matchup against the Dallas Cowboys his earliest possible return.

To fill the vacancy, Los Angeles promoted wide receiver Brennan Presley to the active roster and signed Tyler Scott to the practice squad.

But the decision carries consequences beyond losing Stafford's favorite target for the next month.

Under NFL rules, teams are limited to eight injured reserve return designations during the regular season. The Rams have already used three and are expected to need four more for Davante Adams, Kobie Turner, Terrance Ferguson and Nacua.

That would leave just one available designation for the remainder of the season.

Justin Dedich, Dean Connors and Jordan Whittington are also on injured reserve, meaning the Rams could eventually face some uncomfortable decisions about who gets another opportunity to play.

They could also preserve that final designation as insurance against another injury.

For a team already battered by injuries and desperately trying to keep its Super Bowl aspirations alive, every roster spot has now become precious.
