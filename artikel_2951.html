LeBron James struggles with eight points and six turnovers as Cavaliers blow out 76ers 119-95

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

LeBron James can apparently always come home, where the fans are still nice to him but the rims have gotten quite mean. For the second consecutive game in the arena where he was, and in some respects still is, king, LeBron suffered through one of the worst performances of his career — this time as a Philadelphia 76er. He managed just 8 points on 3-of-12 shooting with six turnovers in the Sixers' 119-95 loss to the Cleveland Cavaliers in an NBA Cup game, the first contest for James here since picking Philadelphia over Cleveland in free agency.
