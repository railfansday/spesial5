Why BellRing Brands Stock Surged Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

People are striving to get more protein in their diets.

By Joe Tenebruso – Nov 18, 2026 at 8:52PM EST

10 stocks we like better than BellRing Brands ›

Shares of BellRing Brands (BRBR +24.07%) jumped 24% on Wednesday after the nutritional supplements maker issued an upbeat earnings forecast for the year ahead.

BellRing's net sales rose 7% year over year to $692 million in its fiscal 2026 fourth quarter, which ended on Sept. 30. The gains were fueled by higher sales of the wellness company's ready-to-drink Premier Protein shakes.

Fitness trends have led more people to seek to increase their protein intake. New offerings, such as Ultimate Protein Shakes that contain 42 grams of protein per bottle, are helping BellRing meet the rising demand for healthier drinks.

Additionally, BellRing recently entered the carbonated beverage market with the launch of its Sparkling Protein Soda, which contains 15 grams of whey protein isolate and only 90 calories and 3 grams of sugar per can.

Distribution gains also contributed to BellRing's sales growth. A new partnership, announced on Nov. 16, will bring BellRing's Ultimate 42g Protein Shakes to 7‑Eleven stores across the U.S.

It should be noted that higher commodity, freight, and tariff-related costs have weighed on BellRing's profits. Yet new CEO Mike Axelrod has a plan to offset these expenses.

"We are advancing a strategic supply chain optimization program targeting at least $100 million in gross savings over the next three years," Axelrod said during a conference call with analysts. "These opportunities span manufacturing, procurement, packaging, and logistics."

BellRing, in turn, sees its earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) rising to between $310 million and $350 million in fiscal 2027, up from $289 million in fiscal 2026.
