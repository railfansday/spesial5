Cowboys injuries: Caleb Downs clears concussion protocol

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Dallas cowboys will play the Indianapolis Colts on Sunday game in a game they must win. Can they win it? Well, that depends on what type of team the Cowboys field. Injuries have torn through the roster, making it uncertain as to who will be in uniform Sunday.

After Friday's injury report, we have more insight. Here is the final report of the week.

Malachi Lawrence (Ankle) and KaVontae Turpin (Ribs) did not practice today and have been ruled out. Lawrence's absence could be prolonged so we'll see if IR is an option.

DeMarvion Overshown (Hamstring/Back), George Pickens (Hamstring), Jake Ferguson (Ankle) and Joey Porter Jr. (Ankle) got in limited work today and were designated as questionable to play Sunday.

In important news for the secondary, Caleb Downs (Concussion/Ankle) and P.J. Locke (Concussion) have both cleared concussion protocol and should play.

Linebacker Marist Liufau looks ready to return so they may open up a spot for him on the roster.
