MobileX Unveils Black Friday Bargain: 10GB for Just $10 a Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

New Customers Get Unlimited Talk and Text, Plus 10GB of High-Speed Data, with Six Months of Service for $60 Upfront

IRVINE, Calif., Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- MobileX, the most customizable wireless service designed to save consumers money, today announced its Black Friday promotion offering new customers 10GB of high-speed data, plus unlimited talk and text, for just $10 a month when they purchase six months of service upfront for $60*.

"Black Friday is about getting more of what you want for less. That's exactly what we're delivering," said Peter Adderton, Founder and CEO of MobileX. "10GB of high-speed data, unlimited talk and text, all for 10 bucks a month. No gimmicks. Just great wireless at a great price."

The Black Friday promotion coincides with the launch of MobileX's redesigned website and updated app, making it easier for customers to explore plans, sign up, and activate service. New customers can take advantage of the limited-time offer through November 30 at mobilex.com or in the MobileX app.

* Prices do not include applicable taxes and government surcharges. Additional terms and conditions apply. Visit mobilex.com for full offer details.

About MobileXHeadquartered in Orange County, California, MobileX is the world's most customizable mobile carrier, delivering the ultimate in choice and cost control. MobileX is a unique service that uses artificial intelligence to predict how much data customers need, delivering a dramatic reduction in cost while ensuring reliable speed and service. MobileX was founded by Peter Adderton, who also founded both Boost Mobile and Digital Turbine. For more information, please visit mobilex.com.
