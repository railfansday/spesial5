Why United States Antimony Stock Is Tumbling Lower Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Analysts continue to see lower upside for this mineral stock.

By Scott Levine – Nov 20, 2026 at 3:04PM EST

10 stocks we like better than United States Antimony ›

Positioned to end the week on a bearish note, United States Antimony (UAMY -6.30%) stock is falling today after an analyst lowered his price target.

As of 3:00 p.m. ET, shares of the mineral production company are down 6.5%.

Maintaining a buy rating, Matthew Key, a Texas Capital analyst, cut his price target on United States Antimony stock to $8 from $9.50. Based on shares closing at $2.23 yesterday, the new price target implies 259% upside.

Key's downwardly revised price target is the third such adjustment this week. On Monday, Alliance Global cut its price target on United States Antimony to $9 from $13.50, and on Wednesday, B. Riley analyst Nick Giles dropped his price target to $6 from $10.

United States Antimony operates an antimony smelter and a precious metal refinery in Montana as well as a smelter and three mills in Mexico.

With investors learning of several lowered price targets this week, it's unsurprising that they're motivated to click the sell button. Prospective investors, however, should recognize that United States Antimony hasn't reported anything disastrous that suggests the materials stock's growth prospects have been hindered.

United States Antimony stock is trading at 9.1 times trailing sales; however, this is a premium to its five-year average sales multiple of 5.9. At this point, investors may want to wait for a further pullback before deciding to load up on shares.
