Barnsley FC captain's 'dogfight' admission and warning to team-mates ahead of Sheffield Wednesday derby

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The beleaguered Oakwell outfit find themselves second from bottom in League One ahead of Friday night's televised home derby with Sheffield Wednesday.

Winless in nine league matches since September 2, the Reds are only kept off the foot of the table by goal difference. They have taken just two points from the last available 27.

Barnsley must make do without McGeehan on Friday after he picked up his fifth league booking of the season.

The return from international duty of Northern Ireland's Patrick Kelly will at least soften the blow.

But the goalscoring nous of McGeehan (pictured), who netted his ninth goal of the season in Saturday's reverse at fellow strugglers Wigan Athletic in a crunch Roses fixture, will be an undoubted miss.

McGeehan's derby role will revolve around rallying a young group of players and he has stressed that the Reds squad must start showing more appetite for the survival fight.

On the task ahead for Barnsley, who last played in the old fourth division in 1978-79, said: "We've got to fight.

"We're in a dogfight. We've got to be prepared every time we go out now to fight more than we have been because we're letting ourselves down at the minute.

"We've got to fight more and more. That's all we can do.

"Fight for the shirt and we'll turn it around."

Part of a Northampton Town side who were relegated last term, McGeehan, 31, is personally motivated to avoid that fate for a second successive year.

"It's our jobs. It's our life. We live for it.

"We come in every day. We're always thinking about it. We take it home with us every night.

"We understand the fans have every right to be frustrated.

"We've got a huge game at home against our local rivals.

"If there's ever a game to turn it around, it's that game."

Wednesday are set to be backed by a travelling contingent of 5,310 supporters at Oakwell.

The Owls quickly sold out their initial allocation and a further 500 tickets were made available from 2pm on Tuesday.
