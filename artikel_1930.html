Manchester United fans 'in agreement' over Michael Carrick sack decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Manchester United fans are "in agreement" at Michael Carrick should be sacked.

The manager looks, once again, at significant risk of dismissal on Sunday evening despite another spirited fightback against Aston Villa to earn a 4-4-4 draw to leave them in the Premier League relegation zone for the international break.

Carrick is the first manager in 40 years to guide Man Utd into the bottom three of the top-flight standings.

The former midfielder was given a reprieve by club bosses two weeks ago following a 3-0 home defeat at the hands of Bournemouth, and again last week after a 2-0 loss at Chelsea.

It was decided following the loss at Stamford Bridge that Carrick would get at least two more games - against Roma and Villa - before the situation was assessed during the international break.

It remains to be seen if that sentiment at Man Utd has changes, but the fans now appears largely in agreement that a change of manager is needed.

One X user wrote during the game against Aston Villa: "I don’t think the fanbase has ever been so in agreement that a manager needs to be sacked.
