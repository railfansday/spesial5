Deshaun Watson clears concussion protocol, remains questionable for Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Browns quarterback Deshaun Watson won't be starting Sunday's game against the Saints, but he could be active as Shedeur Sanders's backup.

The Browns announced on Saturday that Watson, who returned to practice Friday, has cleared concussion protocol. He is still listed as questionable to be available on Sunday, however.

Browns head coach Todd Monken announced Sanders will start on Friday, but declined to say whether Watson will be active as the No. 2. Dillon Gabriel and Taylen Green are both on the 53-man roster and could serve in that role.

The Browns also announced that they have signed defensive lineman Sam Kamara to the active roster and that they have elevated tight end Carsen Ryan from the practice squad for the game in New Orleans.
