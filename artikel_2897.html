Jerry Jones makes announcement on Cowboys’ coach after team blows 21-point lead to Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Dallas Cowboys owner Jerry Jones weighed in on the fate of embattled head coach Brian Schottenheimer after the team blew a 21-point lead in a 39-38 loss to the Eagles on Thanksgiving

The loss was the fifth in eight games for the Cowboys (5-7) and helped the Giants (6-3-1) stay in firm control of the woeful NFC East. Dallas has not won back-to-back games in more than a year.

"It's a bitter disappointment....All three teams, ownership, coaching, everybody is responsible for what we saw at the end of that game," Jones told reporters after the Cowboys blew a 31-10 third-quarter lead and ultimately lost on Jake Elliott's 59-yard field goal with two seconds left.

The loss had to be especially galling for Jones considering they were lit up by quarterback Joe Milton III, whom the Eagles signed off the Cowboys practice squad last month. Milton threw for 331 yards and three touchdowns while adding 15 rushing yards and a rushing TD.

"These are setbacks, and they're are damning setbacks, there's no question about it," Jones said of the loss.

Jones was asked if Schottenheimer. who is now 12-16-1 as head coach, is "still the guy" for the Cowboys.

"Nothing we've seen out here tonight [gives him confidence in Schottenheimer], but you gotta look at everything and that's certainly not the thought on my mind at this particular time. We've got several more games to go, and we've gotta have our mind on that," he said, adding that he he "would not anticipate" staff changes during the team's 11-day break before its next game.

Jones' only in-season coaching change came when he replaced Wade Phillips with Jason Garrett during the 2010 season.

The Cowboys already made one significant change this season after a 46-40 loss to the San Francisco 49ers on Nov. 15, demoting defensive coordinator Christian Parker to senior assistant and having senior defensive assistant Mike Pettine call the defense.
