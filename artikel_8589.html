Choose Vanguard Personal Advisor if you have $50,000 or more to invest and will call its advisors before big money decisions. About 0.30% a year buys a managed index portfolio plus those calls with a team of advisors. If you won’t call, Vanguard’s Digital Advisor runs the same funds for about half as much.

Vanguard Personal Advisor turns your goals and how much risk you’ll take into a portfolio of Vanguard funds and keeps it rebalanced, for investors who’d rather hand off their investing. It costs about 0.30% a year, under a third of the 1% to 1.2% that Kitces Research finds common among advisors on portfolios under $1 million, though a team takes your calls rather than one advisor.

Personal Advisor fits investors with $50,000 to $500,000 who’ll book a call before big moves, such as starting retirement withdrawals. Investors who just want the portfolio managed pay about half as much at Digital Advisor, while a flat-fee planner suits those whose money sits mostly outside Vanguard.
