Bybit WSOT 2026 Raises the Stakes with Anthropic (ANT)-Linked Listings and New Trading Challenges

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

DUBAI, UAE, Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Bybit, the New Financial Platform trusted by more than 90 million registered users worldwide, is excited to announce four new Anthropic (ANT) trading tasks for the World Series of Trading (WSOT) 2026, its flagship global trading competition with up to 10 million USDT in prizes. Following Anthropic's public listing, eligible Bybit users can trade ANT through Bybit Stocks and the ANTUSDT pair on Bybit perpetual contracts to participate in WSOT 2026, earning lucky draw entries and airdrop rewards, while eligible trading volume also counts toward WSOT leaderboard rewards.

Under this year's theme, The World Trades Here, WSOT 2026 has evolved from a crypto competition into a trading tournament across asset classes. Crypto, stocks and commodities now share one account and one competition, with AI built into the full experience. The competition runs until 10:00 UTC on December 2, 2026.

To mark the historic Anthropic IPO, WSOT 2026 has introduced four new tasks for participants. The segment covers both ways to trade ANT exposure, accessible from the TradFi arena in the WSOT Preheat Quest from now until December 2, 2026.

Together, the two volume challenges add an extra 50,000 USDT in airdrops to WSOT 2026. Eligible ANT trading volume also contributes to the WSOT Spot Volume and TradFi Perps leaderboards, so a single trade can count toward both task rewards and leaderboard rankings.

Bybit Stocks debuted on October 12, 2026, and now gives eligible users access to more than 1,000 U.S.-listed stocks and ETFs through a single platform. By adding ANT to WSOT, Bybit gives its trading community more ways to access assets across crypto and TradFi, use Bybit AI and compete for rewards in one place.
