<a href="https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/health-tech/photon-pharmacy-marketplace-integrated-chatgpt-clinicians" hreflang="en">Photon pharmacy marketplace integrated with ChatGPT for clinicians</a>

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Photon pharmacy marketplace integrated with ChatGPT for clinicians" style="max-width:100%; height:auto; border-radius:8px;" loading="lazy">

Photon, a prescription infrastructure company, is now available as a plugin for ChatGPT to streamline prescribing for clinicians within a tool they already use.

The option is available to eligible U.S. licensed clinicians using ChatGPT for Clinicians (free for verified clinicians) or ChatGPT for Healthcare. Just as clinicians must accept OpenAI’s BAA before sharing protected patient information, they must accept Photon’s BAA to maintain HIPAA compliance when using Photon in ChatGPT. Controlled substances are not supported. The feature is free for clinicians through Dec. 31, 2026.

The patient experience remains the same through Photon. What is new is where the clinician can write a prescription. Clinicians with a Photon account can draft, review and send prescriptions without leaving ChatGPT. They just need to add the Photon plugin into ChatGPT, and then they can draft prescriptions. A patient will get a text to Photon’s prescription marketplace outside of ChatGPT. Patients can compare prices and request to change a pharmacy as needed.

“AI is moving from note-taking and reference into treatment workflows. Clinicians are already using ChatGPT to understand treatment options. Once they’ve decided what to prescribe, they should be able to act on that decision in the same conversation,” Otto Sipe, co-founder and CEO, wrote to Fierce Healthcare in an emailed comment. “Photon provides the infrastructure behind that step.”

In the new interface, clinicians begin a prompt with the name of the patient and medication. Photon uses authorized patient information and treatment context shared with ChatGPT to help prepare a prescription for a clinician’s review. They can also add new patient details during the process. Once saved in Photon, that patient can be found by name for future prescriptions.

From there, clinicians review the prescribing details, including the dosing, instructions and quantity. Clinicians can ask about dosing options in the chat. Patient records are saved in Photon, and draft prescriptions are checked against the patient’s recorded allergies and medications. Finally, clinicians review and authorize the prescription to be sent, remaining responsible for the treatment. Clinicians can check a prescription’s status in ChatGPT.
