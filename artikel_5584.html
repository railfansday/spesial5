Robert Pattinson Named the Next James Bond—Months After Joking His Beverly Hills Move Made Him ‘Bougie’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Robert Pattinson is taking over as James Bond—months after the British actor revealed that he and Suki Waterhouse had traded Los Angeles’ east side for a rental in Beverly Hills.

The 40-year-old will succeed Daniel Craig in the 26th Bond film, becoming the seventh actor to play 007 in the official series.

Denis Villeneuve will direct the still-untitled movie, with Steven Knight writing the screenplay and Amy Pascal and David Heyman producing. Craig last appeared as the British spy in 2021’s "No Time to Die."

Five months before the casting announcement, Pattinson told GQ that he, Waterhouse, and their young daughter were renting a house in Beverly Hills.

The move took him to a part of town where he said he’d never lived. In his earlier years in Los Angeles, he owned a large home in Los Feliz, which he eventually sold.

“I always thought I was an Eastside hipster guy. I would only ever come to Beverly Hills for my agency and stuff. Now I'm like, everything's kind of nice. Maybe I've turned bougie," he told the outlet.

One feature of the rental had a practical purpose: its home gym. Pattinson told GQ he was using it to train for "The Batman: Part II," the sequel to his 2022 debut as the superhero.

He recalled working out twice a day during preparations for his first turn as Batman and said that comments he’d made in interviews had given some people the wrong impression about how much he trained.

“[People said,] ‘You didn't work out at all.' I worked out every f—ing day. Even after that, I still look like I didn't work out. I worked out twice a day at, like, three o'clock in the morning," he told the outlet.

Pattinson has lived in Los Angeles on and off since his "'Twilight' years" in a "giant home that he wandered like a ghost," but his work still takes the family far from Beverly Hills.

He said his 2-year-old daughter had already joined Waterhouse on tour and accompanied him to film sets.

He also joked that fatherhood had changed his routine at home, where he now found himself going to bed far earlier than he used to.

The Batman sequel was set to shoot in London, and Pattinson said he had already looked into a possible school for his daughter while there.

The one that caught his attention was the school set up for the young actors on Warner Bros.’ new Harry Potter television series.

Pattinson has his own history with the franchise, having played Cedric Diggory before his breakout as Edward Cullen in "Twilight."

Bond will also reunite Pattinson with Villeneuve, who directed him in "Dune: Part Three." Before taking on 007, Pattinson had moved between major franchises and smaller films such as "Good Time" and "The Lighthouse."

His casting follows years of speculation about who would take over from Craig and marks the first Bond film since Amazon MGM Studios gained creative control of the franchise.
