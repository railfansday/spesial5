How to get full Tactical Effectiveness in Football Manager 27

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Managing your way to trophies in Football Manager 27 is dependent on how many games you win, and at its heart lies the Tactical Effectiveness system. This is a brand new addition to this year’s title, and as of writing, it has the biggest impact when it comes to deciding match outcomes.

Let’s find out how you can maximize the meter and take it to green.

Tactical Effectiveness is a combination of four different factors, and each one plays a key role. To improve your Tactical Effectiveness, you have to enhance all four contributing factors.

To improve Tactical Understanding, you have to focus seriously on the team’s training. There are eight different training modules available under Tactical Understanding. You’ll want to focus as many schedules as possible on these eight modules.

Until your meter appears in the green, sacrifice all other types of training. Once it reaches the green zone, go for Tactical+Match modules.
