Dinghai: The Ocean Pillar launches December 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Dinghai: The Ocean Pillar, a roguelike action RPG set in the mythic world of the Classic of Mountains and Seas, will launch for PC via Steam on December 3, developer Justdan International announced. It will support English, Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese language options. A demo is currently available.

Here is an overview of the game, via Justdan International:

Set against the backdrop of the Classic of Mountains and Seas and the myth of Yu the Great taming the floods, Dinghai: The Ocean Pillar blends Action RPG mechanics with a Roguelike structure. Featuring a fluid dual-weapon combat system and an epic narrative, the game invites players on a grand mythological adventure.

Cultivate Your Strength and Brave the Mythological World of the Classic of Mountains and Seas

Combining Action RPG gameplay with Roguelike elements, Dinghai: The Ocean Pillar lets players craft their preferred builds using a diverse arsenal of weapons and skills acquired throughout each stage. Between runs, players must also visit their home base—the Ruyi Jingu Bang—to continuously train and strengthen their capabilities through multiple progression systems, preparing themselves to overcome formidable foes across a world teeming with gods and monsters!

Beyond unlocking Talents to learn new abilities and boost your stats, you can also unlock practical perks that aid your journey—such as expanding the selection of items available for purchase.

The world of the Classic of Mountains and Seas is home to numerous Spirit Beasts. Defeating them grants Divine Skills, offering a wide variety of abilities ranging from healing and defense-breaking to enhanced mobility. Mastering how to combine and deploy these Divine Skills is key to surviving your adventure!

Through the Forge, players can craft accessories imbued with various special effects, modify weapons to increase their attack power and defense, and upgrade the levels of both weapons and accessories.

Battling monsters across different stages will occasionally reward you with Spirit Jade. By socketing Spirit Jade into the Astral Array, you can unlock a wide array of passive enhancements that provide crucial advantages in combat!
