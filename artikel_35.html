'Maintain control': UN chief calls for creation of global AI safety framework within a year

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

UN chief Antonio Guterres on Monday urged countries to agree on the creation of a global framework for managing the dangers posed by artificial intelligence within one year. Speaking at a summit in Barbados, he warned that taming the technology is vital if humanity is to stay in charge of its own future.
