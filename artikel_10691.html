Woman forced to jump in stranger’s car after being chased by “agressive” turkeys

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A woman has gone viral after being forced to jump into a stranger’s car by a pack of wild turkeys, with the helpful driver calling them “bullies” as well.

Getting chased by animals when you’re going about your day can, at times, be a pretty traumatic and scary experience. This is especially the case when it’s a bigger animal or scarier breed.

However, on November 21, a woman went viral after she was caught being chased down a street by a pack of wild turkeys. Taryn Laprade told WHDH that she and her pastor had taken a wrong turn and spotted the woman being chased in Newton, Massachusetts.

“Are they trying to get you?” the pastor shouted out. “They’re gobbling at her!” a surprised Laprade added. The woman begged for the pair’s help as more and more turkeys kept on following her as well.

“Were you feeding them?” the man asked as the unnamed woman jumped in the car. Laprade and her pastor offered to give her a short ride away from the “aggressive” animals as well.

“It was bizarre. I’ve never seen anything like it before. I see turkeys all the time, but I’ve never seen turkeys aggressive like that, I mean, they were right on her,” Laprade told WHDH.

“Afterwards she thanked us and kept telling us, ‘You saved my life.’ She was very, very grateful that we pulled up.”

According to the report, the woman did not sustain any injuries during the ordeal, and neither did any of the turkeys. It was just a scary moment for her.
