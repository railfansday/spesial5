From equipment to ferries to locomotives, Illinois ports are getting a ZEV makeover

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Illinois Clean Ports Program is putting $104 million behind efforts to help clean up its working waterfronts, funds earmarked for heavy-duty electric equipment assets that includes forklifts, giant reach stackers, switcher locomotives, and the charging equipment needed to support them. There’s even a passenger ferry!

Procurement is now underway for zero-emission vehicles, equipment, and supporting infrastructure at four sites across the state. Administered by the Illinois Environmental Protection Agency (Illinois EPA), Illinois’ Clean Ports Program aims to modernize operations at ports and waterfront facilities in Chicago, the Quad Cities, and southern Illinois’ river port districts.

“Illinois’ waterways are a competitive advantage for the state,” said Illinois Governor JB Pritzker, who was elected to a historic third term on 03NOV. “By modernizing port infrastructure, advancing zero-emission technology, and improving air quality, we are strengthening our economy while building a cleaner future for Illinois.”

Illinois’ Clean Ports Program isn’t just replacing diesel-powered equipment with electric alternatives – instead, the plan also includes Level 2 and DC fast charging, shore power and charging for electrified ships, and comprehensive grid upgrades designed to modernize existing port operations and support the deployment of new electric equipment.

The program takes a holistic approach to port electrification at Chicago’s Illinois International Port District (IIPD), treating energy generation, storage, and deployment as parts of a single connected system. On-site solar helps meet the port’s growing electricity needs while excess power is stored for peak hours, when grid electricity is most expensive. This setup reduces strain on the local grid, and acts as a hedge against rising energy costs.

For smaller public port districts, replacing aging equipment and upgrading electrical infrastructure can be an enormous financial undertaking. The Illinois Clean Ports Program helps make those investments possible, bringing cleaner, healthier, zero-emission technology within reach of terminal operators while preparing the state’s ports for a more competitive freight future.
