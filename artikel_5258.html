Aster (ASTER) is the native utility and governance token powering Aster DEX, a prominent multi-chain decentralized exchange (DEX) specialized in high-leverage perpetual futures and spot trading.

Emerging in late 2024 from a high-profile merger between APX Finance and Astherus, the platform operates across major Layer-1 and Layer-2 blockchains, including BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Ethereum, and Solana. It is designed to bridge the user experience gap between centralized exchanges and on-chain trading
