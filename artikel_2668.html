The two top teams in the AFC North will face off tonight on Sunday Night Football. The Pittsburgh Steelers and the Cincinnati Bengals are both 6-2 heading into Week 10, and the winner of this week's game will claim a definitive lead in the division. When the teams met in Week 3, the Steelers narrowly won 30-27. With Pittsburgh coming off a bye week in Week 9 and the Bengals traveling back from their international game in Spain last weekend, it seems like the well-rested Steelers may have the advantage, but the odds are ever so slightly in the Bengals' favor.

Tonight's Sunday Night Football game kicks off at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock from Paycor Stadium in Cincinnati. Here's everything you need to know about where to tune in to the Week 10 game.

That's a lot of savings and perks rolled into one subscription.
