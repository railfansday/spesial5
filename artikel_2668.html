Jesse Minter: "Unacceptable" that we haven't been able to run the ball consistently

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Ravens have lost five straight games since a 4-1 start to the season and a look at the team’s results running the ball during the two halves of their season presents a big reason why their fortunes have changed.

Baltimore ran for an average of 164.4 yards per game in their first five contests and they’ve averaged 86.8 yards per game on the ground in the last five. The latter number would be even less impressive without a 65-yard touchdown by Justice Hill late in a blowout loss to the Jaguars and Derrick Henry’s current average of 3.8 yards per carry is almost two yards less than he managed during his first two seasons in Baltimore.

Things hit a new low on Monday night when the Ravens had 16 rushing yards in their loss to the previously winless Chargers. On Tuesday, head coach Jesse Minter didn’t point to one specific reason why the team has lost its way but he made it clear that it needs to change.

“There’s to me just a combination of a lot of factors that we need to figure out how to be able to run the football,” Minter said, via the team’s website. “There’s been spurts of it, but the last few games, it’s obviously not where it needs to be. It starts with me, starts with the coaches, goes down to the players. [It’s] unacceptable, honestly, that we’re not able to. [In] the first half, to have no yards rushing, it’s embarrassing. And so, we’ll keep trying to find a better way to do it.”

One obvious reason for the change is that Lamar Jackson was the quarterback for four of the first five games, which Minter referenced and rejected on Tuesday before saying he still believes the team has players who can get the job done. He followed that up by noting “talk is cheap” and the Ravens will have to find their way if they’re going to have any chance of reversing their playoff fortunes in the final weeks of the season.
