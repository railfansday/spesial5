Game Preview: Phoenix Suns continue road trip against Sacramento Kings

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Who: Phoenix Suns (6-7) vs. Sacramento Kings (1-12)

Where: Golden 1 Center — Sacramento, California

Watch: Arizona's Family 3TV, Arizona's Family Sports, NBATV

The Phoenix Suns roll into Sacramento with five wins in their last seven games, while Sacramento comes in on a six-game losing streak. For the Suns, this game is all about building off of their best performance of the season against the Denver Nuggets Monday night. In that game, the Suns' 4 main starters all scored above 15 points, which is just the second time they have done that this season. Even more importantly, the second-half energy and defensive intensity were the best minutes they have played together. Dillon Brooks and Jalen Green both looked as confident and aggressive as they have all season, without disrupting the flow of everyone else on the floor.

Jordan Ott made some rotation adjustments against Denver that worked well, and against a Kings team that is the bottom of the barrel in the NBA, look for him to tinker with lineups even more. Do we see more Koa Peat after he only played one minute against the Nuggets?

The Suns can start this five-game road swing 2-0 and take another positive step in establishing the team identity for the rest of the season, regardless of whether it is the starter or bench units on the floor.

Precious Achiuwa — QUESTIONABLE (Lower Leg Contusion)

De'Andre Hunter — QUESTIONABLE (Left Hip Soreness)

Maxime Raynaud — OUT (Right Patella Subluxation)

Ben Simmons — OUT (Right Achilles Tendon)

The Phoenix Suns are going up against a rookie point guard in Darius Acuff Jr., who loves to shoot and is averaging over four turnovers per game. He is the head of the snake, and the Suns' commitment to not letting him get going and forcing him into difficult situations to create easy opportunities going the other way is what I will be watching for defensively.

These are the two worst rebounding teams in the NBA, so it is an opportunity for the Suns to show they are a steadily improving rebounding team against a team that does not rebound well. When the Suns defend and rebound with energy, it unlocks the version of this team that can be better than last year's team because it is healthier and more talented. Do we see that against the Kings, or do the Suns try to sleepwalk against the Kings squad that has not played well this season?

If the Suns starters do try and sleepwalk their way through this game, the bench is more than capable of picking up the slack. The Kings bench is 29th in bench scoring this season while the Suns are seventh and only getting better. Luke Kennard, Collin Gillespie, and Jordan Goodwin are shooting the ball at an elite level so far this season, and if they continue to play well, the Suns can rely on them to put this game out of reach by the fourth quarter.

Against a team the Suns are far more talented than, the key is to play with the same energy and defensive effort they showed against the Nuggets. That level of play set the new standard for how this starting group should play, and if both the starters and the bench show up and take care of business, this game will be over long before the 48 minutes of game time are done.
