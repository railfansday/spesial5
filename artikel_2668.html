MLB Awards chat and discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Happy Veterans Day to those who have served and are currently serving. Thank you for your service.

There will be a slew of awards handing out tonight, to include potentially some players from the Atlanta Braves. At 7 p.m. ET the Cy Young awards will be announced. Chris Sale is a finalist for Cy Young, although he is not the favorite to win.

There is also the MLB Awards Show at 9 p.m. ET where the Hank Aaron Award, Reliever of the Year Award, Outstanding DH Award, and All-MLB teams will all be anounced.

Follow along in the comments below with your predictions on who on the Braves will be named to the All-MLB team, and how many votes Chris Sale will receive for Cy Young. It will also be interesting to see if Fuentes, eighth place in ROTY votes, gets mentioned in the Reliever of the Year conversation on the MLB show at all.
