Nick Bosa Hit with Boos During Mexico City Game After He Criticized Mexican Fans for Their Cheering

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Nick Bosa was booed in Mexico City on Sunday night

The San Francisco 49ers star gave the crowd a thumbs-up after the reception

Bosa made comments before the game about how Mexican fans needed to be educated on how to cheer for football

Nick Bosa got an earful from fans in Mexico City on Sunday night, days after he said he wanted to educate them on how to cheer for football.

As the San Francisco 49ers star warmed up before the game against the Minnesota Vikings on Sunday, Nov. 22, fans booed the defensive end.

In a video shown from the city's Estadio Azteca, Bosa seemingly acknowledged the greeting with a thumbs up.

Bosa, 29, spoke out ahead of the game, and reminisced about the atmosphere in 2022 when the 49ers played the Arizona Cardinals at the same venue.

"Yeah, I kind of want … I think we need some information given to the fans, so they know when to cheer," Bosa said earlier last week. "Because they just chant the whole time. If we can kind of teach them — no offense to them and their soccer cheering techniques — but if they can cheer when we're on defense, that would be great."

American football has been played for more than 100 years in Mexico and NFL games have been broadcast in Mexico for 61 years, according to the Associated Press.

The first NFL game occurred in September 1978 when the New Orleans Saints played the Philadelphia Eagles in an exhibition game.

Bosa has had a history of making news — and advocating for his point of view — during Sunday Night Football games.

In 2024, he was fined by the NFL for wearing a Make America Great Again hat after a game against the Dallas Cowboys.

The Florida native was billed $11,255 after he wore the hat while crashing an interview with NBC's Melissa Stark following the 49ers' Oct. 27 win, which came days before the 2024 Presidential election.

Stark was in the process of interviewing Bosa's fellow teammates Brock Purdy and George Kittle when the moment occurred.

"I'm not going to talk too much about it, but I think it's an important time," Bosa said at the time.

The athlete also shared a photo of the moment on his Instagram the following day.
