Heat, Celtics deep dive + WNBA Lottery questions & Aces offseason outlook

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

We have a jam-packed episode of The Dunker Spot coming your way!

On the NBA side, Nekias Duncan and Steve Jones talk about the streaking Miami Heat. They discuss the growing cohesion on both ends of the floor, the Giannis/Bam partnership, Davion Mitchell's growth, the importance of Klay Thompson and more. From there, the guys check in on the Boston Celtics -- elite on both ends of the floor, with potentially more buttons to press as the year goes on -- and salute the recent play of Luka Doncic and Franz Wagner.

On the WNBA side, the guys salute the lottery-winning Toronto Tempo before diving into how they should handle their guard/wing room in the event that they land Juju Watkins. They also look at other key decisions other lottery teams (WAS, SEA, LA, CHI) may have to sort through.

To close, the Offseason Outlook series continues with the Las Vegas Aces. With plenty of unrestricted free agents on the docket, headlined by star wing Jackie Young, the guys discuss the importance of bringing her back, as well as potential contingency plans if she goes elsewhere.
