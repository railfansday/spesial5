Vikes Views: Sunday King: Week 10 – Vikings at Packers

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Minnesota Vikings strolled into Green Bay and destroyed the Packers, 30-10. It was another dominant performance by the Viking defense, so much so it's hard not to pick nearly every player in the front 7. The Vikings offense looked much better under Kirk Cousins despite missing T.J. Hockenson and Jordan Addison. A win is always fun, a dismantling of Green Bay in Wisconsin is electrifying. Let's take a look at Sunday's best Vikings.

The defensive front was overwhelming on Sunday. Levi led the team with 2 sacks and 4 hits on Jordan Love.

Turner showed off his versatile skill set on Sunday. He was in the backfield all day wreaking havoc. He recorded a sack on a bull rush straight through to Jordan Love and recovered the errant snap Love launched in the third quarter. Turner nearly added an interception as well, diving in coverage to break up a pass. Turner has become exactly the player the Vikings hoped they were getting when they drafted him in 2024.

Wilson was the signal caller (green dot) Sunday, filling the shoes of Blake Cashman. He also led the team with 7 tackles, including a sack of his own.

Van Ginkel made an immediate impact on the Packers' 2nd offensive play of the game. He just stole the ball from TE Mark Redman. After the Vikings started with a three and out, he stepped up and gave them the ball back on the Green Bay 10. The Vikings were unable to punch it in, but it started them off on the right foot.

Cousins wasn't perfect on Sunday, but it was the best quarterbacked game by the Vikings this season. He made the throws neither of the previous quarterbacks could. The touchdown pass to Myles Price was a reprieve from the QB incompetence we've endured as fans. There's no guarantee it will continue, but it was fun to enjoy yesterday.

Nolan Teasley – I originally had Vikings GM Nolan Teasley on my list, but the system only allows me six voting options. Trading for a starting QB mid-year is a bold move and it paid immediate dividends on Sunday.

Jakobe Thomas – Thomas played his first game on Sunday. He took advantage of his window on special teams with a beautiful hit on the Packers kick returner to force a fumble.

Harrison Smith, Elijah Williams, Kyle Van Noy, Jalen Redmond – they all had impact plays and were part of an elite defensive performance. There were too many to list.

The Vikings have now beaten Green Bay twice this season by a combined score of 69-32.
