Patriots margin of error ‘basically at zero’ after loss to Lions

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Even though they are still 5-5 and only one spot outside of the playoff picture, the New England Patriots' season is at a crossroads. Injury questions with none more consequential than Drake Maye's are one part of it, but so is the performance on the field.

The Patriots are heading into their Week 11 bye off a two-game losing streak, with neither of the contests particularly competitive. The message coming out of the locker room is therefore clear: get it together, or else.

"We have a seven-game stretch that we have to – the margin of error is basically at zero," said safety Kevin Byard after the Patriots' 35-10 defeat at the hands of the Detroit Lions on Sunday. "We have to find a way to string some wins together. We have to start playing together as a team, and it's going to start in practice. Honestly, I feel like we had a great week of preparation this week. We're just searching for answers right now."

The Patriots did not stand a chance against the Lions in Munich over the weekend. While one could play the "what if" game in regards to Maye exiting the contest after one series, fact is that neither the offense nor the defense looked particularly capable of, or at times even interested in, putting up a fight.

The end result was jarring. Not only were the Patriots out-scored by 25 points, they also were out-gained by a whopping 343 yards. Lions running back Jahmyr Gibbs by himself put up more yards (252) and touchdowns (2) than all of New England's offense did — despite playing just 69% of Detroit's offensive snaps.

Long story short, this was a total collapse. It was also one the team needs to quickly put behind itself in order to move forward.

Unsurprisingly, head coach Mike Vrabel was trying to instill a fresh mindset right after the game.

"We're 0-0," he said during his post-game presser. "We're heading into a bye. We have to get some things fixed and changed and corrected, and figure out ways to win as opposed to what we're doing right now, which is not giving ourselves enough of a change."

The first step in that process is making sure the locker room stays united, even though Maye reportedly missing extensive time will make for a challenging environment. For Byard, one of the team's captains, doing so will not start by any raw-raw gestures but with the performance put forward every day.

"We have to perform better," he said. "Bottom line in this league, talking, motivational speeches, getting guys up on the sideline, it means nothing if we're not playing well. We're not playing connected as a team. We're not playing complementary football. I would have bet my paycheck today that we wouldn't put that performance out there that we did today. It just sucks right now. We are in a bit of a slump, injuries are piling up. It's all type of things. But, obviously, going to a bye week, I'm going to take some time off, take my mind off of things and try to recharge."

The Patriots as a whole are in need of that. Whether they come out improved on the other side will be up to them, even though the circumstances are difficult.
