Madison Beer all smiles over Justin Herbert TD days after burglary scare

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

It's been a rough start to the 2026 NFL campaign for Justin Herbert and the Los Angeles Chargers, but after getting their first win of the season last week, momentum could be building in their favor.

During Sunday's game against the New York Jets, Herbert found the end zone in the second quarter, which left fiancée Madison Beer swooning in the crowd.

After Herbert rushed in for the score, cameras cut to Beer in her suite cheering on her man.

Beer, who was all smiles, was sporting a casual look with a Chargers Pro Live sweatshirt and a touch of luxury with some Chanel sunglasses.

It's great to see Beer enjoying the game.

Leading up to Sunday's contest at SoFi Stadium, there were some questions about whether Beer would be in attendance after a burglary scare at her home last week. LAPD swarmed Beer's home in Los Angeles, after a report of a break-in at a home.

The incident occurred during a recent string of break-ins in the Sherman Oaks area, so police were being cautious. Ultimately, it was determined that no crime took place, while Beer and Herbert were spotted outside chatting with authorities.

Hopefully Herbert and the Chargers will be able to continue giving Beer something to smile about as the night goes on.
