Top 5 college football games to watch in Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

There are just two weeks left in the 2026 college football regular season, and just three weeks remaining until the College Football Playoff bracket and the rest of the bowl slate get revealed. That means, even with a lighter slate than we've gotten used to this week, there are still plenty of important games on the docket in Week 12.

This year has seen no shortage of marquee matchups from week to week, but this Saturday isn't as full as recent weeks have been. However, many college football fans will tell you that weeks like these often bring results you weren't expecting, and there's a lot on the line as we head into the stretch run of the season.

Here at Sooners Wire, we'll have our eyes on the home finale for the Oklahoma Sooners. That comes in the form of a battle against the Texas A&M Aggies in Norman, as OU looks to grab a third-straight win. That game will kick off at 2:45 p.m. CT.

Here are the top five games you need to be watching in Week 12.

It's been two years since Georgia and South Carolina last met, after facing off every year from 1992 to 2023. The Bulldogs are playing extremely good football right now, with impressive wins over Florida, Ole Miss, and Missouri following their only loss of the season against Auburn. Head coach Kirby Smart has his team primed and ready for another SEC title, but they'll have to handle the primetime environment in Columbia to make it happen. Quarterback Gunner Stockton will look to keep the offense rolling on the road on Saturday night.

The Gamecocks suffered a crushing loss to Arkansas last week that knocked head coach Shane Beamer's team out of the CFP conversation. Star quarterback LaNorris Sellers missed that game due to injury, which doomed the team. Backup quarterback Cutter Woods started in his place but it looks like Sellers should be back for Week 12. If Sellers does play and South Carolina gets a vintage Williams-Brice stadium atmosphere under the lights, don't count out the Gamecocks this week.

LSU and Tennessee have gone in opposite directions over the past few weeks, but they'll meet in a very important late-season SEC contest. The Tigers are out of the playoff race after consecutive losses to Alabama and Texas, and they're fighting to close a disappointing season on a high note. Quarterback Sam Leavitt will have to take care of the ball much better on the road against a stingy defense, and Knoxville will be revved up for head coach Lane Kiffin's return to Neyland Stadium on Saturday.

The Volunteers are in the thick of both the SEC and CFP races, but they face one more major test in the regular season. Head coach Josh Heupel has another quarterback conversation on his hands, as starter George MacIntyre suffered an injury in last week's win over Texas A&M. If he cannot play this week, it would be original starter Faizon Brandon getting back under center instead. A win this week could propel the Vols to much bigger things, but they'll have to play a great game to take care of business at home.

Kentucky and Missouri looked like two of the SEC's surprise teams earlier this year, but the month of November has hit hard for both programs. The Wildcats are still clinging to slim playoff hopes, but consecutive losses to Tennessee and Florida have put those chances on life support. New head coach Will Stein has still done a very impressive job this season, but a bounce-back win is needed after two rough weeks. Quarterback Kenny Minchey will look to get the offense back on track on the road.

The Tigers, meanwhile, are out of the running in the SEC and the playoff, with two straight losses to Texas and Georgia doing them in. However, there's still bowl placement hierarchy for head coach Eliah Drinkwitz's team to play for, and back-to-back home games in Columbia to close the year could help them finish strong. Quarterback Austin Simmons will look to get his offense rolling again this week, after a tough outing last Saturday.

SMU and Notre Dame have not played each other since 1989, but that will change on Saturday night. The Mustangs were dominated at home by Wake Forest last week, knocking them out of the playoff picture in emphatic fashion. Head coach Rhett Lashlee now takes his team on the road to South Bend to try and pull off the upset, with quarterback Kevin Jennings needing a big game if they're going to get it done this week.

The Fighting Irish have looked strong since suffering their only loss to BYU, and it would take a lot to knock head coach Marcus Freeman's team out of the CFP picture this year. This is the biggest remaining test for quarterback C.J. Carr and the Irish, and the offense has been putting up a ton of points so far this season. They'll have the benefit of a primetime crowd on their hands against an upset-minded team this weekend.

San Diego State and Boise State will play in one of the Pac-12's biggest games of the season, in what could end up being a preview of the league title game in two weeks. The Aztecs are in a tie for second place in the conference standings right now with a Texas State team they defeated head-to-head earlier this season. Head coach Sean Lewis had to change quarterbacks when starter Jayden Denegal got injured midseason, and Stone Saunders will again get the call this week on the road.

The Broncos are already guaranteed to host the conference championship game in two weeks, but they need this win to continue to sit in the driver's seat for the Group of Six spot in the playoff. Head coach Spencer Danielson has an excellent team again this year, and if they continue winning, they'll be in the CFP for the second time in three years. Quarterback Maddux Madsen returned from injury to have a great performance in last week's win over Oregon State, and he'll look to help fend off the upset attempt on the blue turf on Saturday night.
