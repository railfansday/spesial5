Brian Kilmeade and Dr. Kevin Roberts on the Stories That Forged a Nation

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

As the United States commemorates 250 years since its founding, Americans have an opportunity not only to celebrate the nation’s remarkable history, but also to rediscover the people, principles, and defining moments that made the American experiment possible.

In his new book, Uniting the States: Six Crucial Moments That Forged the American Miracle, Brian Kilmeade brings the early years of the U.S. to life through the stories of six Americans whose courage and conviction helped shape a fledgling republic. From John Adams’s defense of the rule of law and George Washington’s leadership in the Revolution to Thomas Jefferson’s Louisiana Purchase and James Madison’s stand against Great Britain, these pivotal moments reveal how a young nation confronted division, uncertainty, and extraordinary challenges.

Brian Kilmeade and Heritage Foundation President Kevin Roberts bring these lessons of great American leaders to the new generation. They explore the character, ideas, and leadership that forged the American miracle and consider what it will take to preserve that inheritance for generations to come.
