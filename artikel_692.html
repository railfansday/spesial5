CAA and Hybe Launch Strategic Partnership

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Entertainment and sports agency Creative Artists Agency and music behemoth/lifestyle platform Hybe launched a partnership to grow Hybe’s creative and commercial opportunities.

Through Hybe’s artist network and CAA’s global reach, the companies will collaborate across content, brand partnerships, sports, live experiences and licensing and merchandising to expand Hybe’s growth in its content, brands and global IP. “This partnership with CAA marks an important step in expanding creative possibilities for Hybe’s artists,” said Hybe CEO Jason Jaesang Lee in a statement. “By bringing together our complementary strengths and global networks, we aim to extend the reach and value of our artists and their IP across content, brands and live experiences. Together, we will explore new creative opportunities that transcend borders and formats, bringing audiences closer to our artists through more immersive and meaningful experiences.”

The partnership also aims to accelerate strategic and commercial initiatives across Hybe’s key markets, which includes Korea, Japan, the Americas, China and India.

“Hybe has created a cultural movement,” said Bryan Lourd, the CEO and co-chairman of CAA. “Hybe artists do not just entertain; they connect with audiences in extraordinary and innovative ways that define the future of our industry and the intersection of art, influence and fandom. We are honored to help carry that forward, building on CAA’s experience and relationships across the region, to show the world what is possible when two organizations that believe in creative talent come together.”

Earlier this month, BTS’ world tour pushed Hybe to its highest quarterly revenue and operating profit in its history. Revenue for the third quarter totaled $1.13 billion with an operating profit of $140.2 million.
