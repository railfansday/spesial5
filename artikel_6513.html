Ferrero Rocher Blossom Cookies

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

In a large bowl, cream the butter and sugar with a handheld mixer until they’re light and fluffy, five to seven minutes. Beat in the egg yolk, milk and vanilla.

In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, cocoa powder and salt. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the creamed mixture, mixing until a soft dough forms. Cover and refrigerate the dough for about one hour, or until it’s firm enough to handle.

Preheat the oven to 350°F. In a small bowl, whisk the egg white until it’s foamy. Shape the chilled dough into 1-1/2-inch balls. Dip the balls into the egg white, letting any excess drip off, then roll them in the chopped hazelnuts to coat.

Editor’s Tip: A piping bag or small zip-top bag with one corner snipped off makes it easy to add the Nutella neatly to the centers.

If the dough starts to feel sticky when you’re shaping it, pop it back in the refrigerator for another 15 to 20 minutes. You want it firm enough to handle without sticking to your fingers.
