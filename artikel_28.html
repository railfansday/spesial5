Dhaka bursting at the seams: Bangladeshi capital faces public health, housing crisis

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Bangladeshi capital Dhaka is one of the most densely populated places on Earth, with over 35,000 people per square kilometre, compared with 20,000 in Paris. Every year, 400,000 people from across Bangladesh move to the city. Its rapid and unplanned growth is triggering a crisis in housing, transport and public health. FRANCE 24's Gabrielle Gohin and Navodita Kumari report.
