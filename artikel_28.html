Ukraine claims it sank Russian warship off Libya

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Ukraine on Monday claimed it was behind strikes on two Russian vessels off Libya, including one that sank a modern warship, in the latest spillover from Moscow's invasion. The attacks on Sunday near the Libyan port of Benghazi come at a time when Ukraine has stepped up its daring strikes against Russia far from the frontlines in retaliation for Moscow's deadly invasion. FRANCE 24's Gulliver Cragg reports from Kyiv.
