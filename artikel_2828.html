Lakers’ Austin Reaves gets crucial injury upgrade ahead of Spurs game

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Austin Reaves moved one step closer to making his return to the court on Thursday.

The Lakers star was upgraded to questionable on the team's injury report for Friday's road game against the Spurs in San Antonio.

Reaves was sidelined for the Lakers' previous five games because of a bruised left heel injury he suffered during the third quarter of their Nov. 11 road win over the Bucks in Milwaukee.

Austin Reaves (15) of the Los Angeles Lakers shoots a free throw. NBAE via Getty Images

The team deemed his status day-to-day, but Thursday was the first time he was listed as questionable on one of the team's injury reports since he's been sidelined.

The 6-foot-5 star guard was a full participant in the team's practice on Wednesday.

"It felt good to play basketball again," Reaves said in his first public comments since suffering the injury. "Wind was a little bad, but kind of to be expected being out two weeks. But felt good."

Austin Reaves has been sidelined for five games because of his heel injury. NBAE via Getty Images

He wasn't sure what happened on the play against the Bucks to cause the injury.

"I had no idea," he said. "Tried to play through it, and then I drove baseline, planted on it, and was like, I can't do this. So walked to the back and was in some pretty bad pain. I didn't think I broke my foot, but it sure felt like I broke my foot."

The Lakers went 4-1 after Reaves suffered the injury, but the team clearly missed his offensive production.

They had a 112.9 offensive rating (tied for No. 18) in the five games Reaves missed compared to the 121.6 (No. 1) offensive rating in their first 13 games.

"Who would have thought that not having an elite offensive player, an All-Star level player would hurt your offense," coach JJ Redick quipped. "That's crazy."

Los Angeles Lakers' Austin Reaves suffered his heel injury against the Bucks. AP Photo/Aaron Gash

Reaves is averaging 24.7 points (51.3% shooting from the field, 40.9% on 3-pointers), 4.7 assists and 3.3 rebounds on the season.

"One of those things that just, it sucks," Reaves said. "I was rolling, playing well. We were rolling as a team, wanted to continue to be out there and play games. Hate missing games. But it's one of those things, like I said, there wasn't much that we could really do. But we took care of business."
