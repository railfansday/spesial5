Cincinnati schedules home-and-home with Missouri for 2027 and 2032

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

While the 2026 regular season is in its twilight stages, some teams are already looking ahead to 2027.

On Tuesday, Cincinnati and Missouri announced a home-and-home non-conference series. Missouri will host the Bearcats at Faurot Field on Sept. 18, 2027, while Cincinnati welcomes the Tigers to Nippert Stadium on Sept. 11, 2032.

Although the campuses are separated by fewer than 500 miles, the 2027 matchup will mark the first-ever meeting between the Big 12 and SEC opponents.

Cincinnati has now finalized its non-conference schedule for the 2027 season. The Bearcats will host Central Connecticut of the FCS on Aug. 28, remain at home against Missouri State on Sept. 4, and complete their non-conference slate at Missouri on Sept. 18. Cincinnati does not have a game scheduled for the weekend of Sept. 11, when it will either play its Big 12 opener or take a bye.

Both Missouri and Cincinnati experienced recent cancelations in their 2027 non-conference slates, making them a perfect match for each other. The Tigers saw their 2027 home contest against Illinois get axed, while Cincinnati's looming road trip to Boston College was also removed from the schedule.

The Bearcats have won five consecutive regular season non-conference matchups, sweeping all three opponents during the 2026 season. The 2027 trip to Missouri will mark the program's first true road non-conference game since Sept. 14, 2024 when it traveled to crosstown rival Miami (OH). Also, it will be the Bearcats' first out-of-state non-conference road game since Sept. 9, 2023 at Pittsburgh and first SEC matchup since Sept. 3, 2022 at Arkansas.

Cincinnati traditionally had one non-conference vacancy reserved for Miami, as the two competed in one of the longest running series in college football. The rivals played their 129th and final scheduled meeting this past September, making it the fourth-most played FBS matchup behind Minnesota-Wisconsin, Auburn-Georgia, and North Carolina-Virginia.

Although an iconic rivalry has been shelved, the Bearcats can look forward to securing their first victory over SEC competition on Sept. 18, 2027 in Columbia.
