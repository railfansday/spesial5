See all Gadsden-area AHSAA Round 2 games on the Alabama high school football schedule

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

High school football is rolling along in the Gadsden area as Round two of the playoffs take center stage.

Nine Gadsden teams are left in the postseason, after six teams saw their season come to a close. Just two of those teams are in Etowah County with Etowah traveling to Plainview and Westbrook Christian hosting Whitesburg Christian, with a chance to advance to the Private A semifinals on the line.

The regular season ran through October before the playoffs start in November. The AHSAA football championships are Dec. 1-5 in Mobile, one of many changes this season. The biggest shakeup was the public-private split and ensuing fallout that created two classes and got rid of 7A.

Most of the Gadsden area football programs are available to stream on the NFHS Network. It is a subscription service that cost $11.99 but gives access to all content on the site across all sports, so you can keep up with volleyball as well this fall.

It is up to the schools to provide the stream, so it's not a guarantee all games in all sports are available but most area schools look to have at least some football games on the site.
