Bill Belichick, Tom Coughlin among five coaches to advance in Hall of Fame selection process

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Bill Belichick fell short of the necessary votes to be elected to the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 2026, but he may have another chance in 2027.

The Hall of Fame announced the five coaches who have advanced to the next round of consideration in the current selection process. One of those coaches will be selected for consideration by the full selection committee ahead of the Super Bowl.

Belichick is joined by Tom Coughlin, Mike Holmgren, Dan Reeves, and Mike Shanahan. All five coaches also advanced to this point in the selection process last year. This is the fourth time that Holmgren has advanced to this point.

Don Coryell was the last coach selected for the Hall of Fame. He was part of the Class of 2023.
