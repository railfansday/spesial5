Brandon Ingram expected to make season debut soon with Clippers

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Brandon Ingram is set to make his season debut with the Los Angeles Clippers on Monday, Nov. 23, according to ESPN's Shams Charania.

The Clippers will play the Houston Rockets at the Toyota Center in Houston on Monday.

Ingram had been working his way back from right heel surgery he had in May. The two-time All-Star also had a partially torn Achilles tendon.

Ingram was initially supposed to have a bone removed from his heel, but the doctors found the partially torn Achilles during that procedure.

Earlier this month, Ingram told reporters that he was "pretty close" to returning and had been on the court a bit more.

He also stated that he had not played any 5-on-5 during the summer but has since been "gaining that confidence."

The Clippers acquired Ingram as one of the primary trade assets in the package sent over by the Toronto Raptors for Kawhi Leonard during the offseason.

Ingram has remain close with the team since his arrival.

He took some time to praise his teammates despite the Clippers' slow start to the season. L.A. has a 4-10 record through the first 14 games.

"I actually want to commend those guys because everybody is in new positions and asked to do more," Ingram told reporters earlier this month. "So the guys are trying. They're playing really, really good defense."

The Clippers fell short in a 124-112 loss to the San Antonio Spurs, in what was a competitive game considering both teams are on opposite sides of the standings.

Ingram return would be welcomed, especially on the offensive end of the floor. The All-Star led Toronto with 21.5 points and was third in rebounds (5.6) during the 2025-26 season. He started 77 games for the Raptors.

"They're trying to figure it out on the offensive end," Ingram told reporters. "When I come in, I want to play make, make it a little bit easier for guys to catch and shoot, and not have to make so many decisions. And be able to, when it gets down to it, be able to score."

Ingram already had a level of familiarity with head coach Tyronn Lue and assistant coach Jeff Van Gundy from playing for Team USA during the 2023 FIBA World Cup.

The Clippers had several players injured to start the month, including Ingram, center Yanic Konan Niederhäuser and Max Strus.

The trio was recalled on Nov. 2 and participated in a weekend assignment with the team's G-League affiliate.

Strus returned to the team after recovering from a partial tear to his right plantar fascia. Konan Niederhäuser made his season debut on Nov. 19 against the Clippers, after recovering from right Lisfranc surgery.

Bradley Beal (right knee injury management) and Jordan Miller (left rotator cuff surgery) will remain the only Clippers without a timetable to return.
