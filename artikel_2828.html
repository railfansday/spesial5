Confirmed lineups: FC Augsburg vs. Mainz 05

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Augsburg coach Manuel Baum makes five changes from the heavy defeat in Leipzig before the international break. Juma Bah, Marius Wolf, Tim Schnitzer, Alexis Claude-Maurice and Michael Gregoritsch come into the starting lineup for Jeffrey Gouweleeuw, Chrislain Matsima, Han-Noah Massengo, Arijon Ibrahimovic and Rodrigo Ribeiro.

Schnitzer is making his first start for the club, while team top-scorer with five Gregoritsch returns to the lineup to lead the line once more.

Urs Fischer has made four changes from the narrow 2-1 home defeat to Bayern last time out. Stefan Posch, Philipp Mwene, Paul Nebel and Philipp Tietz come in for Fabio Gruber, Jae-Sung Lee, Silvan Widmer and Ransford Königsdörffer.

FC Augsburg (3-4-2-1): Dahmen; Keitel, Bah, Behrens; Fellhauer, Schnitzer, Rieder, Wolf; Kade, Claude-Maurice; Gregoritsch

Mainz 05 (3-5-2): Zentner; da Costa, Posch, Potulski; Caci, Nebel, Sano, Amiri, Mwene; Tietz, Becker
