CSC Reacts Results: Will the Saints defeat the Bears on Sunday?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Ib this week's reacts poll, we asked our readers: Will the Saints defeat the Bears on Sunday?

An overwhelming 90% of voters said New Orleans will not leave the Windy City victorious.

Only 9% of fans said they have confidence that the team is headed in the right direction.

These results are not surprising given the Saints 3-6 record. Fans are frustrated and disappointed in how the season is turning out. Injuries, penalties, and slow starts have put up huge roadblocks in what looked to be such a promising year with a favorable schedule and easier division to win.

While there are still plenty of games left in the season and no clear frontrunner has emerged in the NFC South, the Saints still have an opportunity to turn things around.

However, as we approach this week's matchup against the 7-2 Chicago Bears, New Orleans will need to bring its A-game against a formidable opponent that could add another loss to the Saints' record.
