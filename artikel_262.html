Apple M5 MacBook Air Black Friday deals: See the best sales still in stock

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Black Friday is here again — one of the great holiday shopping traditions. We have our own tradition here at Mashable: making a beeline for the MacBook deals in Amazon's Black Friday sale. For most of the year, Apple prices are what they are, so it's always exciting to see just how heavily MacBooks are discounted for Black Friday.

If you're in the market for the lighter, slimmer, more portable MacBook Air, you're in luck. Amazon has slashed prices across the 2026 range, with savings of up to $300. These models are powered by the mighty M5 chip, which was until very recently the most powerful processor in Apple's MacBook arsenal. (You can also find a deal on the M6 MacBook Pro if you need something heftier.)

The 15-inch, 512GB version of the MacBook Air is the best model for most users. It combines Apple's M5 chip with a stunning Liquid Retina display and the fluid user experience you expect for Apple devices. If you need MacBook Pro-level power and memory but you still want the portability, this is the best option. Still powered by the M5, it cranks everything up — double the RAM, double the storage. It's also more expensive, which is why a $150 savings is very welcome for Black Friday.
