Netflix Tunes Into Music-Driven Chinese Fantasy Series ‘The Melody Of Love’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Netflix has snapped up The Melody Of Love and is launching the music-driven Chinese fantasy drama in the U.S. and internationally on its platform on 29 Nov.

The show hails from Chinese production company Stellar Pictures, which made fantasy series Love Between Fairy and Devil and Love Game in Eastern Fantasy, both of which are also available on Netflix.

The Melody Of Love is a 40-part series starring Li Yitong and Chen Xinhai. Set in a fantasy world shaped by the power of music, the drama follows a legendary musician living under a concealed identity and a mysterious musical spirit who has lost his memory. Forced into an unlikely alliance to protect their kingdom, the two gradually fall in love. Zhao Yilong directs the series. It bows on Netflix on the same day it has its domestic premiere in China, on Hunan Satellite TV, Tencent Video and Mango TV. There has been strong interest locally, with the release announcement generating hundreds of millions of views on Douyin, the Chinese counterpart to TikTok. Watch on Deadline

“Over the past few years, we have seen our stories find audiences in markets that were once unfamiliar with Chinese fantasy,” said Wang Yixu, Founder and Chairman of Stellar Pictures.

“With The Melody of Love, we wanted to explore a different creative direction through Chinese musical traditions. We hope more Chinese stories like this can travel beyond borders and be seen and loved by audiences around the world.”

As well as Netflix, The Melody Of Love will also be on Tencent’s international streaming service WeTV internationally, as well as Rakuten-owned streamer Viki in selected territories.
