Glass House Brands (GLAS -2.48%) reported third quarter revenue of $56.8 million and record biomass production of 319,000 pounds while navigating an unfavorable product mix and wholesale price compression. Management reported that the company completed the deconsolidation of its retail operations and successfully uplisted to the New York Stock Exchange during the period. Operational focus remains on optimizing Greenhouse 2 and Greenhouse 5 to improve the ratio of high-value flower and smalls in the production mix. The company reported a cash balance of $39.7 million, which management stated is sufficient to fund its $20 million capital expenditure plan and reach positive cash flow. Strategic initiatives include preparing for potential interstate and international commerce following the transition of medical cannabis to Schedule III and obtaining international quality certifications for existing farms.
