‘Love Is Blind’ Reunion Spoilers Reveal Michaella Started Dating This Rejected Contestant After Breaking Off Her Engagement

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

As anyone keeping up with the chaos that is Love Is Blind’s Boston era knows, Rob and Michaella did...not work out. Basically, Rob had a meltdown during a cast reunion at a local bar, told everyone he didn’t care about their relationship, pointed out they’d only known each other for a few weeks, flipped out over Michaella talking to beekeeper Matt, and then ditched Michaella in the middle of the night.

However! If your takeaway from the bar night was that Michaella and Matt still had unresolved chemistry, you are not alone. Thanks to reunion spoilers from Reality Receipts podcaster Rikkii Wise (@storytimewithrikkii), we know that Michaella and Matt started dating after filming wrapped on Love Is Blind. However...looks like they didn’t last, and eventually split up.

As for where Michaella and Rob stand, they are absolutely not together. Michaella recently told Tudum, “Him behaving that way just felt like such a betrayal to me.” She added, “While he’s doing it, I’m sitting there thinking to myself, ‘What are you doing? You just met my parents. I have shown you the most I’ve ever shown anybody about myself and been so patient and kind. How could you be saying these things in front of your friends and on camera?’”

Michaella added that she wishes they’d left the show together, saying, “It all just got to a really dark place. I think it would’ve been better for his mental state. He was so stressed, and I don’t want anyone to be stressed.”
