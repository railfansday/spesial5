Source Reveals ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Producers Wanted This Celebrity to Win Ahead of the Finale

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Dancing With the Stars just aired one of its best finales everrrrr, resulting in Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach being crowned winners! And apparently this was a best-case scenario for producers, who—per an insider—were hoping Ezra would win.

“Producers are mildly panicking because in a best case scenario world, Ezra would win,” a source told The Sun ahead of the live finale on Tuesday night. “He is in producers’ mind the perfect representation of a winner for this season.”

Per the outlet, producers believe Ezra could be the show’s “golden boy,” but were worried that his live scores wouldn’t be high enough to net a win after noticing engagement on his social media had “dropped significantly.” But clearly fans came out in droves voting for Ezra and Daniella, which—combined with their perfect scores all night—resulted in them winning!

In case you missed it, Ezra reflected on his beautiful freestyle routine in the skybox, and talked about his prosthetic unexpectedly coming off mid-performance. “It’s crazy. You do something so many times,” he said. “The leg un-suctioned, and it came off in the middle of the dance. But listen, we put our heart, our blood, our sweat, our tears into this. If one person with a disability feels more confident, holds their head up high because of this show, then I’ve done my job.”
