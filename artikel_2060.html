Crawley Town boss makes Cardiff City comparison as he takes on huge relegation challenge

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The club has confirmed the 67-year-old will remain in charge of Reds until the end of the season following a month as interim head coach following the sacking of Colin Kazim-Richards in October.

And he has a tough task on his hand turning the season around after a poor start which has seen them rooted to the bottom of the League Two table, six points adrift of safety, albeit with three games in hand on some teams around them.

But the former Leeds United boss is no stranger to firefighting and and helping teams get out of trouble – and he believes he can do that again as long as they ‘really, really’ pull their finger out on the pitch.

"I think certainly towards the last five or six years, myself and Neil [Warnock] were going firefighting at Rotherham, Middlesbrough, Cardiff and we'd take them out the bottom three and Cardiff into the Premier League.

“This here would be a great achievement as well. As I say, there's a lot of good stuff here. It's not like going into a club where a lot of it is in a bad shape.

“A lot of stuff is good here, but the one thing that matters is what happens on the pitch and that is where we have to really, really pull our finger out.”

Reds face Fleetwood and Barnet in the space of four days before they head into a busy December where they have six fixtures.

Blackwell said: “Well, the season is a marathon, it's not a sprint. It's always nice to be in and around the pack when you need to be and you don't want to be too far adrift, but it's still too early to say that these are defining games.

“But my word, they are very, very important going into the Christmas period.”

Blackwell also added that he had to do a bit of sweet-talking with his wife to carry on as boss at the Broadfield Stadium. “I've had to do a little bit of crawling, there's no doubt about that. But Julie's been brilliant about it. She's saying, ‘when are you going to stop?’ But I love football and while you're still good at your job, and I think I'm good at my job, we'll keep going for a little while longer.”
