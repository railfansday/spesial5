Moretto: Cagliari talent could emerge in Camarda talks with Milan’s interest confirmed

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It has been confirmed by a reliable source that Paul Mendy is someone AC Milan are watching closely.

In the past few days, the idea of Francesco Camarda leaving on loan in January has turned from a suggestion into something that seems like a concrete possibility. Given his talent, there has already been interest from a number of clubs.

So, the Milan management must carefully pick the right landing spot for him, and Cagliari have emerged as a suitor. The reason this is something to keep an eye on is because the Rossoneri in turn are keen on one of their forwards, so a mutually beneficial arrangement could arise.

According to Matteo Moretto’s latest update on Fabrizio Romano’s YouTube channel, an explorative contact took place a couple of weeks ago between Camarda’s entourage and Cagliari. They are an option for him, but there are also other options for the Italian in Italy and abroad as well.

Meanwhile, the journalist also confirmed that Cagliari forward Paul Mendy appeals to Milan just like he appeals to several other clubs. Milan are interested, but there is nothing advanced to talk about at the moment.

Mendy is 19 years of age and he burst onto the scene last season, scoring his first two Serie A goals for the Sardinian side. He has one goal and two assists in the league so far this season.
