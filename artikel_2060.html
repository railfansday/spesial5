Major Bradford City takeover update emerges in fresh twist

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Bradford City are being targeted by a Thai-based consortium over a potential takeover bid, which could make for an interesting few weeks ahead.

Journalist Alan Nixon revealed that the Bantams are subject to interest from a consortium from Thailand. Bradford are currently in League One, and will be looking to win promotion into the Championship this term under the management of newly-appointed boss, Michael Duff.

Nixon revealed that the group interested in taking over Bradford have held previous interest in other EFL clubs, and so it remains to be seen as to whether they'll make a breakthrough this time around with the third-tier club.

In the same report, the reputable journalist also stated that Bradford could be open to a sale, although it remains to be seen as to what their asking price could be.

"Bradford City are a surprise target for a Thai-based group who are thinking of a takeover bid. The Bantams are flying high in League One and come backed by a big support.

"That has caught the eye of a group of Far East speculators who have had previous interest in buying EFL clubs.

"Bradford could be for sale at the right price and the Thais, who have an intermediary with inside knowledge, are considering an official offer."

Bradford are currently owned by Stefan Rupp, who has been in charge since May 2016, as they completed a takeover of the club alongside fellow German investor Edin Rahic from joint chairmen Julian Rhodes and Mark Lawn.

In a previous interview in 2025, Rupp revealed his stance on any potential interest in the Bantams, revealing that he wouldn't ever hand the club over to someone that can't take Bradford further.

"Why should I sell it? You get requests from time to time but they are tyre kickers, people without money, wannabe football club owners, or people you simply don't like.

“I'm the keeper of the keys and I would never hand it over to somebody where I have the impression he can't do any better.

“At the moment, I'm really enjoying it. It's a good time and we have a lot of momentum. We have a lot of motivation and we have a vision as a club where we want to be. Maybe you'll ask me in four or five years and I’ll still be here.”

Bradford are currently in their second successive season in League One, and have made plenty of progress in that time, as they're now challenging for promotion into the second-tier of English football.

They won promotion from League Two in the 2024/25 season, as they finished third, winning promotion automatically whilst under the management of Graham Alexander.

Alexander recently departed in favour of a move to Preston North End in the Championship, and Bantams supporters will be hoping that his successor can hit the ground running heading towards the potentially season-defining festive period.

Bradford are currently eighth in the League One table, but have a couple of games in had on some of the teams above them in the table, which gives them the ideal opportunity to move back into the play-off positions.

They only dropped out due to teams around them playing over the international break, with their match against Leicester City being postponed due to international call-ups.

They're set to return to action on Saturday, as they take on struggling Doncaster Rovers, in a game they'll be expected to pick up a much-needed win to fire themselves back into the top-six.
