The Launch: November’s Must-Know Fashion News

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Fall is in full swing, and with it, so is the fashion industry. As the leaves continue to turn and preparations for the holiday season get serious, brands are finding new ways to celebrate with exciting new capsules, collaborations, and stocking-worthy releases. Look no further than J.J. Martin, whose new book, Table Energy, explores the art of Italian entertaining. Below, all the fashion news to know this month.

Why: Just in time for the holidays, La DoubleJ founder J.J. Martin has released Table Energy, a new book about the art of Italian entertaining, with a foreword by fashion writer Leandra Medine Cohen. Its pages move from alfresco lunches to candlelit dinners, all in the colorful, convivial spirit of La DoubleJ. The book made its debut at the brand’s Upper East Side boutique, The Lighthouse, where Martin, ELLE Decor’s head of editorial content Elisa Lipsky-Karasz, and ELLE editor-in-chief Nina Garcia gathered to celebrate l’art de la table.

Where: Online at tecovas.com, aninebing.com, and select retail locations.

Why: Anine Bing has a new way to kick up a look. The designer has teamed up with Tecovas on a capsule that reworks the label’s Loretta and Jamie boots with star embroidery and polished goat leather. Cowboy hats and belts round out the lineup, which launches alongside Anine Bing’s spring/summer 2027 collection.
