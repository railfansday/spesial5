What Kind of Power Player Are You, According to Your Zodiac Sign?

The cutthroat lawyers of All's Fair provide more than just fashion inspiration and captivating plot twists. Each character also embodies one or more zodiac signs. Together, the power players of Grant, Ronson, & Greene represent the 12 signs’ best traits—and some of their not-so-nice ones, too. As the legal drama returns November 4 on Hulu for another season of scandals, secrets, and stilettos, we’ll get to see cunning worthy of a Scorpio, ambition that would impress a Capricorn, and diplomacy that any Libra would applaud. Find your legal avatar below, and as Carr would say, “See you in court.”

Aries is assertive, ambitious, and impulsive. They go after what they want, even if it means being the bad guy. And if nobody wants to join them, they’ll do their own thing. The first sign of the zodiac, Aries is ruled by Mars, the planet of war—a fitting influence for their ability to turn any courtroom into a battlefield.

A born romantic who yearns for a life of luxury—that’s the earth sign Taurus. But it’s not all red roses and dreamy silk gowns. Taurus also shows determination and a serious work ethic; the sign is known for its stubborn pursuit of goal.

Gemini is the hyper-intelligent sign of communication. They not only find out information, but strategically choose how and when to reveal it. Gemini loves to make a statement, whether with what they say or what they wear.

Cancer feels everything fully, and their big heart can often lead them to form bonds with unexpected people. At the same time, their mood can also cause drama. Like Emerald, this water sign’s empathetic nature makes it easy to relate to others and understand where they’re coming from—even if their lives and personalities are totally different.

Creative and charismatic, Leo is the natural leader of any group. In fact, they command every room they’re in. Leo’s confidence means they find it easy to finalize a deal and they don’t second-guess themselves. Leo also loves a bold look and has a bit of a vain side.

Though quieter than others at the firm, Liberty is just as intelligent. Her strategic mind is what makes her an excellent Virgo. Liberty finds loopholes in the law and notices details others miss, making winning cases a breeze. Virgo is one of the hardest-working signs of the zodiac, and Liberty is one of the hardest-working lawyers in LA. Virgo is also one of the most caring signs, as exemplified by Liberty’s commitment to empowering women.

Represented by the scales of justice, Libra is the sign of equality and fairness. They’re literally the sign of the legal field. As a veteran lawyer, Dina knows the legal field like the back of her hand. Her passion for law drives her work, and her diplomacy helps her connect with both her coworkers and their clients.

Scorpio is all about power—who has it, who wants it, and how to use it. We can see this in both Allura and Carr, two ambitious Scorpios who often find themselves on opposite sides of a power struggle. Scorpios treat clothing like armor, and both women do just that by cultivating a glamorous, intimidating wardrobe.

Sagittarius thinks outside the box. Like Milan, they’re willing to go off script and try wild ideas that might seem out-there to others. To them, the end result justifies the means, and if an eccentric tactic accomplishes their goals, that’s enough of a reason to try it out.

The most ambitious sign of the zodiac, Capricorn will stop at nothing to get what they want. Hard-working and intelligent, they’re more motivated by material gains than intangible ideals. And their intensity can be a little scary. But as any Capricorn knows, whatever it takes to succeed, it’s worth it.

As the sign of innovation, Aquarius loves nothing more than breaking with tradition and setting out independently. When the old ways don’t work, they create a new way. Aquarius is also quite independent; they’re often more comfortable alone rather than relying on others.

Pisces is the last sign of the zodiac, a placement that gives it wisdom. They often find themselves in a mentor role, inspiring the younger people who follow in their career footsteps. Pisces is always ready to share advice or listen without judgment. They have a strong sense of creativity and a willingness to think outside the box.
