Donnie's Sunken Boat Location In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

In Grand Theft Auto 6, there are plenty of tasks that Jason gets sent to do in the early game. He gets sent around by and for Brian, oftentimes cleaning up Donnie's messes. One such mess is a boat that Donnie sank off the coast of Corsair Key that had some illicit substances left in it.

However, the game only gives you a search radius and doesn't show the boat's exact location. While it's not so large that it's impossible to find, it can be tough to spot on the ocean floor. This mission is one that's definitely best left for the daytime.

To find where Donnie sunk his boat, you'll have to make your way off the east coast of Corsair Key. You'll have the search area marked on the map once the quest unlocks, and you can take a boat or jetski out to the area and try to find it. Luckily, there's one of each at Jason's house that you can take.

It's helpful to have scuba gear with you, but it isn't necessary.

Once you're in the circle, make your way to the spot on the map that's shown in the first image above. You'll be able to tell that you're in the right place by all the floating debris that's in the area. There's a lot of it scattered around, but the highest concentration of debris is right above where the boat sank.

Press the right bumper or R1 to dive below the surface and swim all the way down to the ocean floor. When you spot the boat, it'll be on its side, with contents spilled all over. The bricks won't be randomly strewn across the ocean floor, and there's no reason to hang around because there's no other loot in the area.

To find the contents of the boat that you're searching for, you'll have to get right up close to the boat itself. There are three compartments that you can interact with: one behind the driver's seat, and two along what would be the floor of the boat.

When you open the compartment behind the seat, some fish will swim out and there won't be anything you can collect in there. Instead, go to the floor compartment towards the rear of the boat, and that's where you'll find the bricks that you're looking for. If you search the forward compartment, you won't find anything.

After you've got the bricks, simply swim back up to the surface and take them to Brian's lockup to get some money back for them. Luckily, you can do this whole mission without attracting any attention from the police, so it should be smooth sailing the whole way. At least, smoother than that boat ride was for Donnie.
