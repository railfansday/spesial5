Everything to Know About Nicholas Hoult’s Wife, Bryana Holly

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

They just made their red carpet debut at the premiere of Nosferatu.

Nicholas Hoult’s latest flick How to Rob a Bank is about to hit theaters, which means the actor is in the thick of a press tour for the project and making rounds on red carpets.

He made a stop at Jake Shane’s podcast, Therapuss, during his press tour on November 11 and made rare comments about his wife—yes, wife—Bryana Holly.

I know what you’re thinking: How long has it been since Nicholas Hoult and Jennifer Lawrence called it quits, and when did he start dating Bryana? Ahead, find everything we know about Nicholas Hoult’s wife, Bryana Holly.

Bryana is signed to Wilhemina Models and occasionally poses for lingerie, swimsuit, and clothing brands. She also starred in Hoodie Allen’s music video for “Movie” in 2015:

If Bryana looks a little familiar, you may recognize her from her past relationships. The model was romantically linked to Brody Jenner in 2013 before she dated 5 Seconds of Summer drummer Ashton Irwin from 2015 to 2016.

The pair has been together since 2017, and in 2024, Nicholas confirmed they secretly tied the knot.

At the Governors Awards in November 2024, Nicholas called Bryana his “wife” while talking to E! News. “Afterwards, I remember I had my wife’s nails imprinted on my hand,” he said of her reaction to Nosferatu.

A few days later, he confirmed they were married to People, saying, “I feel like it’s fairly clear from my comments.”

Remember that Therapuss interview I mentioned earlier? Well, Nicholas shared the story of how he met his wife in 2016.

“I was shooting a movie,” he said during his convo with Jake. “We were kind of filming and shooting a Halloween party at the same time. And then we had actually a really fun…it was kind of an overcommitment first date.”

He shared that he and Bryana started chatting about their music taste and decided to go to the Desert Trip music festival (which he claimed is “affectionately known” as “Oldchella). They apparently bonded over listening to artists like The Rolling Stones, Paul McCartney, Bob Dylan, and The Who.

“We didn't really know each other, but we both wanted to go,” he explained. “We went to dinner once before just to be like, ‘Let’s make sure neither of us are complete lunatics.’”

Nicholas brought Bryana as his date to the Nosferatu premiere in L.A. back in 2024. And, if you were wondering, this marks their first red carpet appearance after being together for seven years.

Bryana and Nicholas welcomed their first child, Joaquin, in 2018. He’s been seen out and about with the Skins alum and has even made a rare appearance on Bryana’s Instagram (which is now private, womp).

The couple then quietly welcomed their second child in 2022, and according to Nicholas’ How to Rob a Bank co-star, Pete Davidson, they also gave birth to twins at some point.

“He’s a great dad, father of four,” Pete told Entertainment Tonight. “Four under what? 7 [years old] ... which is nuts.”

He also praised Nicholas for how much he catered to his family while filming.

“Elsie was pregnant while we were filming and he just had his twins,” Pete continued. “He would fly back every weekend to go see his family, and that was very inspiring and awesome.”
