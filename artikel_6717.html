The T3 Aire 360 Is The Best Multi-Styler for Jet Setters

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The upgraded dual-voltage design ensures great hair days all over the world.

We may earn commission from links on this page, but we only recommend products we love. Promise.

I’ve gotten compliments on my hair my entire life. (I mean, I am a redhead.) But I noticed a significant uptick in flattery once I mastered the art of the bouncy at-home blowout. As a beauty editor, I’ve tried nearly every blow-dry brush and multi-styler on the market—yes, even the pricey Dyson Airwrap and beloved Shark Flexstyle. Neither of those viral tools comes close to my beloved T3 Aire 360, though.

I’m not the only one obsessed with this hair tool. When the Aire 360 first launched in 2024, my colleagues raved about how it expertly smoothed their hair, how easy it is to use, and the fact that it comes in a cute, convenient carrying pouch. T3 has since come out with an upgraded dual-voltage model that can be used all over the world. (As an avid traveler, that detail is essential!)

Could the upgraded T3 Aire 360 be the right multi-styler for you? Keep reading to discover everything you need to know about the tool and get first-person intel from two editors who can’t live without it.

T3 has all the bells and whistles you could want in a multi-styler. The tool comes with four attachments: A blow dryer, two curling wands (for each direction), and a smoothing round brush. The barrels stand out from other models because they’re ceramic, meaning they conduct heat beautifully, smooth strands, and produce impressive style longevity. (Just be careful because these can burn your hands if you touch them while using.)

Another cool feature? It’s designed with “SoftAire,” which is the dryer’s unique mechanism to help speed up drying time. The brand boasts “zero” heat damage, but obviously, there is some heat. However, it’s much cooler than a normal curling iron or hair straightener, so using this tool can protect your hair in the long run.

My hair is very fine, but I have a lot of it. It has also changed texture and become more curly over the past two years (thanks, birth control!), so I’ve had to relearn how to style and take care of it. Overall, though, I don’t have many issues with it. Stylists often say my mane is very “obedient” because it responds well to heat and is easily styled. I try to wash it two times a week and will always blow it out with a round brush.

This is a much smaller kit of attachments than I’m used to, but I actually prefer it. I don’t need all that. One of my favorite aspects is the chic leather carrying case that holds everything inside, making storage a lot easier. (So many multi-stylers don’t come with a storage bag, FYI!) Also, it’s pink. Love that.

Let me preface this by saying I’ve religiously used the T3 Aire 360 for the past two years, and the upgraded dual-voltage model functions just as beautifully. When I wash my hair at home, I blow it out with the round brush attachment. The tool is so easy and intuitive to use, and it makes my hair look incredibly smooth and sleek. I’ve perfected my at-home blowout routine with it, and most days I don’t feel the need to use a separate device—like a curling iron or flat iron—to zhuzh my hair.

When it comes to the curling wand attachments, these produce the best results out of all of the multi-stylers I’ve tested, probably because they’re ceramic. Do they get a little hot? Sure, but not at temperatures as high as your standard curling iron. The conductive ceramic material ensures my curls last for days. They might not be as defined on day two or three, but I don’t feel the need to touch them up either.

“I don’t say this lightly: My hair has never looked so good. I walked into the office with a level of confidence that should probably be studied. Who knew a good blowout could make you have a good day? The curls are so bouncy, but my ends still feel soft and not fried like a curling iron or straightener would do. And it really only took 15 minutes or so from start to finish. My Airwrap officially has steep competition. I think I’ll stick with it for my bangs, but the curling powers on the T3 Aire are unmatched.”—Beth Gillette, Beauty Editor

When it comes to multi-stylers, the T3 Aire 360 is one of the best. The price and attachments are comparable to other options, but this has one major differentiator: It’s designed for international use. The dual-voltage upgrade, along with the chic pink leather carrying case, make this the ideal choice for avid travelers. If you’re a jet setter (like me), this is the multi-styler to purchase because you’ll never experience a bad hair day, no matter where you are in the world.
