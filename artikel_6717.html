Charlize Theron Brings the Exposed-Bra Trend to Incredible New Heights at the 2026 Governors Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

She brought the drama in a plunging Tom Ford fit

Last night, the 2026 Governors Awards served as the kickoff to award season, with some of the biggest names in Hollywood gathering at the Dolby Theatre in Los Angeles for the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences’s annual film gala. There, the stars gave us a taste of what to expect fashion-wise when ceremonies like the Oscars roll around in the coming months. Zendaya stunned in an archival Giles Deacon dress with a waterfall of golden fringe. Florence Pugh shimmered in bedazzled Valentino. Timothée Chalamet brought back his Call Me By Your Name-era curls.

One of the most show-stopping looks of the night though came courtesy of former Oscar winner Charlize Theron, who brought some heat to the high-profile ceremony in a straight-off-the-runway ensemble from the Tom Ford Spring/Summer 2027 ready-to-wear collection.

The crimson fit consisted of a flowy maxi dress with a draping bodice that was constructed out of wide, slightly off-the-shoulder swathes. These diverging pieces created a plunging neckline so deep it nearly left Theron’s entire torso bare. Underneath, the actor wore a matching red leather bra, embracing the ongoing exposed-underwear trend.

A slight slit running up the side of the skirt revealed Theron’s pair of strappy black sandal heels that tied up her ankles, and she accessorized with crystal drop earrings from Anita Ko that dangled alongside her neck like daggers. To complete the look, she matched her dress with a bright red lip, while pulling her hair back into a slicked bun.
