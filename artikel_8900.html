Why You Keep Falling Off Your Fitness Routine (and the 2-Minute Plan That Fixes It)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Expecting yourself to stick to the same fitness routine, no matter what, is why it fails.

Because the only predictable thing about life is that it’s unpredictable.

A kid gets sick. Sleep falls apart. The to-do list explodes.

You can’t know which one is coming or when, but you can know something is, and you can build your routine to expect it.

That’s the whole trick: take two minutes and decide, in advance, exactly what you’ll do the next time life goes sideways.
