You can build (or neglect) the romance and relationship between Jason and Lucia in GTA 6

Romance in GTA 6 isn't something you should shy away from. Rockstar Games' latest open-world crime epic follows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos as the pair reunite in Leonida. Whether they stick to being partners in crime or develop a healthy relationship status in GTA 6 is completely up-to you, with the connection between the two playable characters strengthening or weakening depending on your approach to the world. Jason and Lucia's relationship status isn't determined by completing certain missions or ticking off a few optional objectives, but rather it can change based on your actions, choices, and the time you spend together. Small acts of everyday affection and kindness will grow a healthier relationship in GTA 6, while ignoring your partner will slowly drive them apart.

There are four relationship statuses in GTA 6. You can move between each status at any time, organically depending on how you choose to play. Tip: Jason and Lucia will remain connected as criminal partners throughout all of the GTA 6 missions, regardless of how their romantic relationship develops. However, certain moments in the story and interactions with characters may change depending on their relationship status at the time.

The process of building up your GTA 6 relationship status is organic – it happens gradually, over time through daily acts of affection and small acts of kindness. It's the smallest things that can make a difference, like greeting your partner when you meet up, buying them a snack, restocking their ammunition at an Ammu-Nation, or by holding hands while you explore the city together.

The bond between Jason and Lucia can also improve if you take time to do different activities together. Completing robberies and hitting gang hideouts together will strengthen their relationship, as will doing workouts together at the gym, and enjoying sports and leisure activities as a couple.

To have the strongest relationship in GTA 6, both Jason and Lucia need to contribute to it. You can switch characters by holding right on the d-pad and then pressing R1. If you prioritize playing as one character over the other while you're playing, don't be surprised if the relationship naturally begins to degrade – this will impact the story and interactions between the two in a myriad of ways.

If you'd like to take more deliberate actions to lower your relationship status in GTA 6, then you certainly can do so. If you start ignoring your partner's calls and messages, don't be surprised if they start getting a little short with you on WhatUp. Spending lots of time apart, abandoning your partner while traveling together, or otherwise failing to engage with them on a daily basis will drive the two apart.

Tip: Small acts of affection will always help to improve the bond between Jason and Lucia, but if one of them has a GTA 6 Criminal Profile of 'Irredeemable' the relationship may never recover.

No, you cannot romance other characters beyond Lucia and Jason, at least through my experiences. I played through GTA 6 for over 100 hours and have yet to encounter any real opportunity to form a relationship or romance with any characters beyond those two, suggesting that their relationship is not an open one.
