PSA: GTA 6 Does Not Have Online Multiplayer at Launch

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

GTA 6 is now less than a week away from its official launch, coming to PS5 on 19th November, 2026.

It's likely to become the biggest entertainment launch of all time, with excitement at a fever pitch coming off the back of GTA 5, which is the third best-selling game ever at a staggering 235 million copies.

Something that not everyone may know, however, is that GTA 6 will be a single-player game only at first; the online component will not be available initially.

This won't come as a shock to those in the know, as Rockstar Games has described it as a solo experience and has not yet referred to any form of online play.

However, it seems there are many expectant fans that are excited to hop straight into multiplayer with their buddies. In case you didn't know, you won't be able to do this when GTA 6 launches next week.

I suspect this confusion comes from just how long GTA 5 has been around. The game was first released on PS3 13 years ago, and GTA Online is almost as old, launching a couple of weeks after the main game.

Re-releases of GTA 5 on PS4 and PS5 have always arrived with GTA Online as part of the package, so even though the original game didn't have online multiplayer to begin with, it's been a constant presence for millions of players for well over a decade.

It makes sense that people have become accustomed to GTA 5 providing GTA Online, and took it for granted that its sequel would include the feature from day one. This is not the case.

For GTA 6, it's expected that an updated version of GTA Online will launch sometime in 2027, though an exact date has yet to be confirmed. It won't take a full year to appear, despite what this recent Bloomberg video inadvertently suggests.

It's believed that GTA Online will come to GTA 6 in a matter of a few months, possibly launching in the first quarter of 2027.

Rumour has it that the PC port of GTA 6 will launch in a similar timeframe, and it would make sense for these two things to be aligned.

So, to summarise: GTA 6 does not feature online multiplayer at launch, but it likely won't be too far off.
