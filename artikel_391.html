What to wear to a holiday party (without overthinking it)

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Holiday party invitations have a way of raising more questions than they answer. What exactly counts as "festive attire"? Is a sweater enough for the office party? And does "cocktail attire" mean you need to dig out a suit and tie?

The trick is to match your outfit to the occasion, then build around a few versatile pieces you can wear long after the decorations come down. A merino sweater and collared shirt can work for a family dinner, while adding a sport coat gives you a more polished look for the office. For dressier gatherings, a dark suit is a reliable choice.

From comfortable sweaters to tailored suits, Men’s Wearhouse has the pieces to help you dress for any holiday gathering without overthinking it.

It depends on the occasion, so start by checking the invitation for a dress code. An office party usually calls for business casual: Try a sport coat over a dress shirt with chinos and leather shoes. For dinner with friends and loved ones, a merino sweater layered over a collared shirt and dark trousers strikes a comfortable balance.

If the invitation specifies cocktail attire, play it safe with a dark suit, dress shirt, and dress shoes. A tie is a good idea if you’re unsure how formal the gathering will be.

For a casual get-together with friends, dark jeans, a quarter-zip sweater, and clean leather sneakers should do the trick.

For men, cocktail attire at a holiday party generally means a dark suit, dress shirt, and leather dress shoes. A tie isn’t always required, but it’s a good idea if you’re unsure how formal the event will be. (Worst-case scenario, you take it off and put it in your jacket pocket, no biggie.)

Cocktail attire falls somewhere between business casual and black tie. A navy or charcoal suit is a reliable choice, while a patterned tie or pocket square can add a little holiday flair without going overboard. You don’t need a tuxedo, but jeans and sneakers are usually too casual. For colder evenings, a wool overcoat looks more appropriate over a suit than a casual puffer jacket, and a scarf adds warmth without sacrificing the dressier look.

Other dress codes can be harder to decipher. "Business casual" typically means a sport coat or sweater with a collared shirt, chinos or dress trousers and leather shoes. According to the Emily Post Institute’s dress-code guide, “festive attire” generally means cocktail-party clothing with a seasonal touch, such as a colorful tie or pocket square. "Festive casual" is more relaxed, allowing for dark jeans, sweaters, and seasonal colors.

When the invitation is vague, consider the venue and who’s hosting. A New Year’s Eve party at an upscale restaurant may call for a dark suit, while a casual Christmas gathering at a friend’s house might require nothing dressier than a sweater and chinos. When in doubt, it’s usually better to be slightly overdressed than underdressed.

A merino wool sweater is dressy enough for most office Christmas parties, family holiday dinners, and casual New Year’s gatherings, especially when paired with a collared shirt and tailored trousers. It’s a comfortable alternative to a suit that still looks polished enough for the occasion.

Merino wool comes from merino sheep and is prized for its soft feel, breathability, and ability to help regulate body temperature. A fine-knit merino sweater looks more refined than a chunky knit, making it easy to dress up or down. A crewneck layered over a dress shirt is a reliable choice, while a quarter-zip (a sweater with a short zipper at the neck) offers a slightly sportier look.

If you’re wondering whether to wear a sweater or a blazer, consider the setting. A sweater on its own works well for family gatherings and casual office parties, while adding a sports coat makes the outfit more appropriate for dressier events. For cocktail attire, however, a suit is generally the safer choice.
