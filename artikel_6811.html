Another ‘Secret Lives of Mormon Wives’ Star Has Quietly Filed for Divorce, Per Court Documents

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Just days after Jen Affleck filed for divorce from Zac Affleck, it looks like another Secret Lives of Mormon Wives cast member is officially pulling the plug on her marriage.

The Sun reports that Miranda Hope has filed for divorce from her estranged husband Chase McWhorter, news that comes a full two years after their separation. Per the outlet, which accessed court docs, she “opted to keep the filing and other documents related to the case private” but “a child support worksheet and a notice of required classes were also filed.”

Miranda and Chase got married in 2017, and they’ve had a lot of ups and downs navigating their split and coparenting their kids. It’s unclear what triggered Miranda to formally file for divorce after two years (note: she’s been referring to herself as “divorced” for some time now), but she’s moved on and is dating Kaleb Winterburn.

Back in July, Miranda spoke about her relationship with Chase to Page Six, saying, “It’s best case scenario for being divorced and coparenting. It’s not always seamless, we still argue and there’s ebbs and flows. I think that at the end of the day, something that Chase and I both pride ourselves on is the fact that we always put our kids first. So even when there are those ebbs and flows and those lows, our number one priority is the kids. Being able to have this type of relationship where we still have a family dynamic, we still consider ourselves and refer to ourselves as a family. We’re still a family, it just looks different. You have two homes.”

Miranda also added that she doesn’t have expectations for Chase as a partner, she just expects him to be a teammate: “Our relationship took a different form and we’re both so much better for it.”
