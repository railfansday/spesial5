Bluemercury’s Holiday Shopping Party Has 2026’s Most-Wanted Beauty on Sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Last night, I was scrolling through my inbox when an invite to the opening of “Ruloph’s Rooftop”—a holiday pop-up at Chicago’s LondonHouse Hotel, replete with private, twinkling igloos—caught my eye. The event is tomorrow, which serves as a jarring reminder that it’s the season to have all party-ready beauty essentials on standby. After all, at this time of year you never know when Rudolph (or something equally festive) will call.

Just in time, Bluemercury’s Holiday Shopping Party is here, offering tiered savings for up to 30 percent off your final haul. The sale runs from November 19 to 25, offering discounts on coveted items for hair, skin, body, and holiday glam. From Oribe’s cult-status texturizing spray and Dyson’s coveted multi-styler to a Victoria Beckham Beauty blush that will make you look sun-kissed in the dead of winter and Tronque’s firming, plumping body butter, some of the most in-demand beauty products of 2026 are on sale.

As you stock your cart, just keep a few caveats in mind: First, discounts are not valid on gift cards, spa services, or products from Aesop, Baobob, Bluemercury exclusive sets, Dr. Diamond’s Metacine Instafacial Collection Plasma Duo or Dr. Diamond’s Metacine Plasma + Hydr/O Duo, LYMA, or Vintner’s Daughter. For SkinCeuticals products, there’s a limit of three per customer and a maximum discount of 15 percent. For goods from Charlotte Tilbury, Dyson, Elta MD, Maison Francis Kurkdjian, Oribe, PCA Skin, and Victoria Beckham Beauty, there’s a maximum 20% discount. Finally, offers cannot be combined with any other discounts, coupons, or promotions; nor can they be redeemed for cash or used toward prior purchases.

With that cleared up, there are no rules for how to use the following products to give yourself—or anyone on your gift list—the ultimate holiday glow-up. Party, indeed.

Get glass skin for the holidays with up to 15% off SkinCeuticals’ P-Tiox, a “Botox in a bottle”-type serum that smooths, softens, and has won the heart of many a Vogue editor (you can read our full review here). Dermatologists are also quick to recommend anything from SkinMedica, including its TNS Advanced+ Serum, which experts count on for tightening, lifting, and making skin (merry and) bright. For a touch of post-party pampering, the Black Rose Cream Mask from Sisley-Paris smells like fresh petals and makes skin feel just as soft. And while Augustinus Bader’s mineral sunscreen may not be as economical as your average SPF, it’s on sale now—making this the right time to elevate your fun in the sun.

Why not knock out some of your holiday shopping while you’re at it? Decked with a limited-edition charm from French jewelry designer Lauren Rubinski, Hourglass’s limited-edition Ambient Lighting Editor Unlocked palette has already sold out at Sephora—someone on your gift list would be delighted indeed to get their hands on the coveted launch. Meanwhile, Chantecaille’s compact wraps powder highlighter in gold and pearls, turning a quick cheek touch-up into a luxury experience. La Mer’s soothing concentrate is a gift that will keep on giving all winter long, keeping skin soft and calm, while Byredo’s Gypsy Water Hand Cream—which smells of pine needles and juniper berries wrapped in incense—will be the hit of the White Elephant swap.

No holiday-party blowout is complete without a touch of shine, and Moroccanoil’s treatment delivers one with nourishing argan oil and a touch of irresistible, spicy amber scent. For a festive coif that’s both textured and touchable, Oribe’s cult-classic spray is always in style—and rarely on sale—while R+Co’s Picture This Dry Texture Balm tames frizz, boosts volume, and fits in your chicest holiday clutch. Between parties, give your lengths a little TLC with Maria Nila’s Head & Hair Heal Shampoo. The Swedish concoction soothes strands and scalp with aloe vera extract, and it smells like jasmine and linden blossoms. Just the in-shower reset the chaotic season calls for.

‘Tis the season to plug it in and light up—and that goes for red lights, as well as twinkling lights. Take advantage of the Bluemercury sale to snap up Higherdose’s Red Light Hat, which promotes blood circulation in the scalp to increase scalp health and (possibly) reduce hair loss. For face, the Light Salon’s Boost LED Mask uses professional-grade wavelengths of red and near-infrared light to brighten, plump, and firm the skin, while The Skincare Tools’ Contour Sculptor offers a more analog solution for a just-left-the-spa-level glow. Finally, if it’s that post-salon look you’re after, Dyson’s Airwrap multi-styler and dryer has yet to be replaced as the coveted tool to beat.

It’s rare to have a natural-looking, sun-kissed glow on one’s cheeks during the holiday season, but Victoria Beckham’s watercolor-like blush makes it easy to achieve. It’s also rare to find a setting powder that comes in seven shades (and comes with a heart-shaped powder puff), as Charlotte Tilbury’s does. Rarer yet is the chance to score up to 20 percent off items from both coveted brands, but it would seem that makeup lovers’ dreams come true during the Bluemercury Holiday Shopping Party. Save room in your makeup pouch for Tom Ford’s sculpting highlighter duo and Trish McEvoy’s intensely pigmented gel eyeliner—both versatile enough for everyday wear or holiday glam.

Why should fragrant spruces have all the fun this season? Bluemercury’s best fragrance deals extend to a Diptyque candle worth gifting—or saving just for yourself—to scent your space with a woodsy-floral blend of blackcurrant leaves and moss. Or, keep the scents closer to your skin (or his!) with Parfums de Marly’s new Crécy Extrait de Parfum, also studded with blackcurrant as well as lavender, rose, woods, and benzoin. For something slightly spicier—and just a touch boozier—Jo Malone’s limited-edition Vanilla Scotch cologne is all warming vanilla and scotch accord. And for a head start on 2027’s smoky-fragrances trend, may we suggest a spritz of Bō’s Casa Blanca Parfum? Its mix of creamy minerals, white tea, suede, oud, and tobacco places it firmly in on-trend (and terribly alluring) ground.

Polish, tighten, and tone with discounted goods that fit neatly into a stocking—or on a bathroom shelf. Tronque’s Firming Butter is the New Zealander’s answer to lax or dull winter skin, with its intensely hydrating mix of biotech ceramides and natural oils, plus ocean kelp and red seaweed to plump and tighten. For more skin-softening courtesy of the finest ingredients from the sea, Osea’s Undaria Cleansing Body Polish packs antioxidant-rich seaweed into a gel-to-milk formula that leaves skin glowy and soft as a mermaid’s mane. Meanwhile, Nécessaire’s Body Retinol is a dermatologist-approved secret for smoother skin head-to-toe, while U Beauty’s Super Body Hydrator puts a high-tech spin on beneath-the-chin skincare with Siren capsule technology that strategically delivers hydration to the areas that need it most.
