Test the export before you accept a managed-service handover

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The provider handed over a CSV. Before treating it as a migration backup, open one row you know should exist but rarely see in the default view: a closed request, an archived item, or a record owned by a different team. Use the role that will actually perform the export.

This matters because exporting a view and recovering a system are different jobs. Microsoft’s Lists documentation describes export from the current view and notes a 30,000-row limit for CSV export. The Excel option creates a one-way connection back to the list. Confirm which export options are enabled in your environment and how the documented limit affects your handover.
