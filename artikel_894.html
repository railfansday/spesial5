Days of Our Lives' James Reynolds Shares Parkinson’s Disease Diagnosis

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Days of Our Lives star James Reynolds shared he was diagnosed with Parkinson’s disease, detailing why he wanted his personal journey being incorporated into long-running soap.

There’s one way the days of James Reynolds’ life offscreen mirrors the one he has on TV.

The Days of Our Lives star shared that over the last few years he has been navigating life with his Parkinson’s disease, and that ultimately it was a health diagnosis he requested be written into the long-running soap.

“With Parkinson’s, I realize something different every day,” Reynolds told Entertainment Tonight in an interview published Nov. 10. “When I first went and got it diagnosed, I had the tremor that didn’t want to go away. A little bit off balance.”

Though the 80-year-old kept his diagnosis private for a “few years” before sharing it publicly, he believes his symptoms began many years, or even decades, before he knew he had the disease.

“I realized just a couple of weeks ago that I may have had this 15 or 20 years ago because you get the hallucinations,” Reynolds explained. “My wife would say, ‘You were hitting me last night in bed.’ I was having these really horrible dreams, and I didn’t know there was a physical component. Sometimes I’d fall out of bed.”

Looking back, the Daytime Emmy winner realized how he was experiencing dreams was an “early symptom” of Parkinson’s disease, a neurodegenerative “disorder of the nervous system that affects movement,” according to the National Institute of Health. The condition, which does not have a cure, can lead to tremors and shaking, struggles with balance and movement and stiffness in muscles.

As for why Reynolds decided to share his diagnosis now—and for it to be reflected on the small screen through his role as Abe Carver—he noted “there are certain things that eventually you just can't hide,” pointing to his tremors, his balance issues and apathy.

“The writers also wanted to incorporate it because it was obvious, and we play life here,” he continued. “There are many other characters who have gone through real-life issues that are then mirrored on the show, and this is an issue that is going to be mirrored on the show. There’s nothing any of us can really do about it.”

Reynolds—who has starred as the Salem police commissioner turned mayor since 1981—added, “I feel like the value of Abe as a character who overcomes a lot, has a tremendous amount of strength, deals with a lot of things with humor as well as understanding, and then I realized I’m shaking all over the place. What am I doing?”

With the support of his loved ones and his castmates, including his onscreen wife Jackée Harry, the beloved soap star is maintaining a positive outlook, despite his diagnoses.

“You can still work,” Reynolds, dad to son Jed Reynolds, 47, with ex Laura Toffenetti, emphasized. “You can live your life. You can participate with your family. You can raise your children.”

“You don’t have to be lost,” he added. “You can arm yourself. You can fight back.”
