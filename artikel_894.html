Scotland draw Germany, Netherlands & Sweden in Nations League

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Scotland face a tough series of Nations League matches in 2027

Scotland will meet Germany, Netherlands and Sweden in the next edition of the women's Nations League.

Melissa Andreatta's side are preparing for a World Cup play-off with the Swedes in December.

Scotland, who were in the lowest tier of seeds in the A division, met Germany and the Dutch in the 2025 competition.

They lost 6-1 and 4-0 to Germany, but the games against Netherlands were much closer, with a 2-1 home defeat and a 1-1 draw in Tilburg.

Scotland lost 4-0 and 1-0 to the Dutch in 2023.

Sweden and Scotland last met in a 2021 friendly in Paisley, with the visitors prevailing 2-0.

On the impending showdown with Sweden, Andreatta said: "I'm glad we can narrow in on what's ahead of us immediately and that's performing in these next two games.

"The last two games against the Czech Republic was exactly the kind of preparation we needed."

Captain Caroline Weir has not played for Lyon since pulling up in the warm-up before the second leg at home to the Czechs but Andreatta said the star midfielder "is progressing well and to plan".
