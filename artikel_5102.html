Chainflip users face frozen withdrawals after $4.86 million vanishes from Ethereum vault

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The pause also hit routes such as Bitcoin to Solana, where deposits awaiting confirmation have to wait until Chainflip lifts Safe Mode.

Chainflip, a cross-chain exchange, halted swaps across its protocol on Nov. 21 after nearly $5 million in ether and stablecoins left its Ethereum vault, according to blockchain data firm Bitquery. The shutdown also suspended deposits, withdrawals and quotes.

The exchange announced full safe mode on its @Chainflip account at 09:14 UTC. It described the suspension as a precaution while it investigated a potential exploit affecting its vaults.

Bitquery traced the Ethereum vault loss to a single transaction at 07:10:59 UTC. It reported that about 520.2 ETH, 3 million USDC and 758,141 USDT left the vault. Bitquery valued the ether at $1,635 at the time of the transfer and counted both stablecoins at face value. The Ethereum vault switched to a new signing key at 08:53 UTC. The key contract accepted no further signatures through Bitquery’s 11:23 UTC cutoff.

Bitquery said the contract accepted the signature on the withdrawal as valid. How the signature was produced remains unresolved. The loss estimate covers the Ethereum vault. Bitquery said the Arbitrum payouts it reviewed that morning appeared ordinary. Its review also covered one Bitcoin vault address, with Solana outside its scope.

CryptoSlate has previously covered how DeFi hacks can tie up liquidity and leave users waiting for withdrawals even when they did not use the affected contract.

Chainflip normally lets users swap Bitcoin for Solana. To complete that trade, Chainflip has to recognize a deposit on Bitcoin and send the payout on Solana. Those steps depend on the same protocol that Chainflip put into safe mode after the Ethereum vault loss.

Chainflip’s governance documentation says full Red Safe Mode stops outgoing transactions, including swap payouts and withdrawals by liquidity providers. The State Chain, which coordinates those operations, keeps producing blocks so governance can act. Users whose swap deposits were awaiting final confirmation have to wait until Safe Mode ends. Chainflip’s documentation says those deposits will then be rescanned and processed.

Around 18:15 UTC Saturday, Chainflip’s swap app and liquidity-provider app still displayed maintenance notices and pointed users to Discord for updates. The notices offered no specific restart time.

The mainnet status page showed some resources under maintenance. Its public overview gave no route-by-route reopening information.

Chainflip’s documentation says governance can lift Safe Mode once the incident is resolved. The team promised updates in its Nov. 21 announcement and gave no reimbursement details.
