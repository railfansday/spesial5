Nvidia shares climbed about 5% in after-hours trading on Tuesday after the chipmaker posted record third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion and forecast stronger-than-expected sales for the current quarter. The stock had closed the regular session 0.44% lower at $155.46. Revenue topped the Bloomberg consensus of $109.15 billion, while data center sales reached a record $107.6 billion against forecasts of $101.85 billion. Net income came in at a record $65.3 billion, The Kobeissi Letter reported, and adjusted earnings of $2.68 per share beat the expected $2.47. Nvidia guided fourth-quarter revenue to about $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, above the $125.25 billion Wall Street had projected, and announced a new $150 billion stock buyback. The rally offers some relief to a stock that had fallen about 35% from its October 6 high, a slide that deepened after Jensen Huang resigned as CEO and CFO Colette Kress stepped in as interim chief.
