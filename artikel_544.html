The best Black Friday grooming deals for anyone whos tired of fighting body hair

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

As a woman of Italian descent (who also happens to have hormonal issues), I’m no stranger to unwanted hair growth. I’ve tried hair removal creams, wax strips, the fanciest razors money could buy, and, most recently, IPL (intense pulsed light) devices. And despite my best efforts to find one thing that does it all, keeping my skin smooth and hairless takes a combination of tools. Apparently, this is a group project.

It’s also an expensive one. Between replacing razors and buying a device that promises less shaving in the future, the cost of grooming adds up. Throw in the pink tax, and I’d rather wait for a sale than pay full price for the privilege of removing hair I didn’t ask for.

Luckily, Amazon has been dropping Black Friday deals since Nov. 19, so there’s no need to wait until after Thanksgiving to see what’s on sale. Whether you’re trying to get ahead of ingrown hairs, keep a beard in check, or finally commit to IPL, these are the best Black Friday grooming deals to shop right now.
