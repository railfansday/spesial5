Willy Chavarria Debuts His Dream “Bitchy Little Bags”

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Are you a woman chasing the Willy Chavarria look? You’ll want to channel a Hitchcock heroine—Tippi Hedren is his favorite. Elongating skirt? Check. Strong-shouldered blouse or jacket? Done. Swooping french twist? Looking up Youtube tutorials now. But what about the classic handbag hitched up in the crook of your arm? Now, that can be yours, too.

Launched this morning on willychavarria.com, two new styles—the Bronca and the W Slouch—mark the New York designer’s official debut in the handbag space. Despite the pretty regular appearance of bags at his runway shows (both the Bronca and the W Slouch were seen at his spring 2027 menswear outing), introducing the category was a long time coming for Chavarria. “I’ve just been really timid about launching bags because it’s such a big thing,” he tells Vogue. “I never wanted to have a bag that ends up at the back of the store, doesn’t get the attention it needs—we just kind of pulled back and we said, okay, we’re going to launch this very soft bag that feels good.” Hence, only two styles, and direct-to-consumer only.

The Bronca is “sassy,” “a bitchy little bag,” even, and that’s just how Chavarria likes it. Available in pink, blue, black, and chocolate brown, it features a rolled leather handle with a set of keys dangling from the clasp. His dream girl to sport it is his forever muse Bianca Jagger; he’s fantasized about her pairing it with the iconic Yves Saint Laurent white wedding suit she wore to marry Mick in 1971.

In contrast, Chavarria says the W Slouch, available in green, chocolate brown, and black, is geared more towards his menswear customers. It comes with a detachable padded long strap, making it ideal for trekking across town with half of your belongings. To style it, he suggests following the wrong shoe theory. “I think it’s really cool with a tough look for a guy to have a bag that throws it off,” he says. “I always love those looks that just make you do a double take.”

Prior to today’s release, the designer has produced a W-embossed leather tote, some clutches, and a few coin purses, but he doesn’t look at those offerings in the same light. “This is more of me looking at [the category] as a business opportunity and building a plan and thinking about how I can grow this business,” he says.

It helps that with every collection, he’s dabbling in womenswear more and more. For his women customers, he sees a handbag as the perfect way in. “The brand is still small enough that I’m very careful about adding new categories, but as I’ve seen my womenswear business grow, it makes sense from a strategic standpoint to add a bag to the collection that actually enhances the full collection. And maybe she just buys the bag, maybe that’s all she wants.”

Though Chavarria is personally partial to a razor-sharp evening clutch, he doesn’t have plans to expand into additional silhouettes anytime soon. “I want to sit with these for a while,” he says. “I want to get these in the market and have people wearing them and get comfortable with them, and then maybe add on some little offspring, some little babies that go inside.” A nesting doll of handbag options await.
