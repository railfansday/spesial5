"Joe Low's had the same this week as well. But he's on the mend from that point of view.

Meanwhile, Drury has also revealed that defenders Murray Wallace and Jack Whatmough are both 'on track’ in their recoveries from injury.

Wallace has been out since injuring his knee in the home derby with Sheffield Wednesday last month.

Whatmough has been plagued by calf problems and has been out since late August. He has made just two appearances this term.

Drury commented: "They are both are on track. Murray's out of his brace and he's increasing his workload, so he's well on track with his. It's still a little bit of time away for Murray because he was a little bit longer.

"Jack's probably closer than Murray. Jack's on the grass and been running, so without putting a definitive time frame on it, I'd like to think Jack will be available in the next three or four weeks.

"Anything earlier than that would be a bonus, but he's working hard every day on the grass.
