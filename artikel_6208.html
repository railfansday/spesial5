British Airways Unveils 8 New Boeing 787 Heathrow Routes: See Full List Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Robin Guess | Shutterstock, Simple Flying

British Airways is one of only a tiny number of operators to fly all three variants of the Boeing 787. Ch-aviation shows that the oneworld member has 44 Dreamliners, which is the Boeing moniker. It has 18 787-9s (215 and 216 seats), 14 787-10s (256 seats), and 12 787-8s (204 seats).

The subfleet is entirely deployed from London Heathrow. However, BA eventually plans to replace its so-called Gatwick-configured 777-200ERs at the UK's second-busiest airport, which have fewer premium seats and higher overall capacity, with the 787-10. In time, Gatwick's widebodies will be all-Dreamliner.

OAG data was used to examine BA's 787 network from Heathrow. This looked at the routes available between January and September 2026, and then compared that to what's scheduled to operate from October 2026 through next year. This reflects what is known as of November 12 and certainly may change. Recent analysis shows that BA has pulled the 787 from six routes.

Some of the routes are highly intriguing. This includes service to San José, the capital of Costa Rica and an increasingly important gateway for higher-end nature tourism. While the long route to Central America was flown by BA from Gatwick for the past decade, it is the first time that Heathrow has had flights there. The switch will help achieve higher yields and, somewhat paradoxically, a greater focus on connecting traffic.

On October 28, 2026, BA reintroduced the 787 on the short hop from Heathrow to Madrid, which links two critical IAG hubs. BA has flown widebody aircraft to Barajas for a long time. Iberia has too, and continues to do so on the A330 and A350 (I have flown the A350 on this route). The primary reason is to provide additional belly hold freight capacity to South America.

BA deployed the 767-300ER to Madrid for years, followed by the 777-200ER and the 787. The A380 appeared for crew familiarization and training. The so-called Dreamliner was used to Spain between 2017 and 2022, so the return after four years was notable. The type more or less replaced the Triple Seven, although that type is still going to appear occasionally this winter. The 777-200ER will replace the 787 next summer.

The 787-8, 787-9, and 787-10 are all scheduled to operate this route. This might reflect the lack of aircraft availability and 'slotting in' variants based on their availability. It is one of only six routes in the current month to see all three variants, although it's usually the case that one of the variants has very few flights, but they do exist.
