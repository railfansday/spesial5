Thanksgiving Jungle Juice

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

While I love individual Thanksgiving cocktails, if you're serving a big crowd, a punch like this one is the way to go. If you need to save your energy for cooking and serving up the food this turkey day, then whip up this shareable Thanksgiving jungle juice, and let your guests serve themselves all day long. Made with two bottles of red wine, whiskey, and Fireball, this punch is the drink to serve if you're looking to party this holiday.

I think it's perfect as is, but feel free to experiment with it—punch is extremely versatile. Use your favorite red wine that you enjoy drinking, and consult our list of the best whiskey brands if you need some help deciding which bottle to pick. Add in as much seasonal fruit as you like, and get to serving!
