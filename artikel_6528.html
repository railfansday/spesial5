The Garment Pre-Fall 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Interiors, and interiority, are recurring subjects of Charlotte Eskildsen’s collections for The Garment. For pre-fall, the designer imagined a woman living in a townhouse in central Copenhagen near the Marble Church, a very chic area, who wears clothes in “her own really private way... Nothing in this collection is too tied up,” the designer said. “Even if it’s suiting, it will stretch, and it’s comfortable to wear.”

Some of the garments have a feeling of intimacy that comes from their origin in the boudoir. Lingerie dressing is a trend that Eskildsen isn’t ready to give up as she sees it as an expression of femininity that counters the outward strength often expected of women. So next to a lace-trimmed skirt with an asymmetrically inserted godet and a collarless jacket, the many slip dresses in the collection looked somewhat saccharine and less street-ready than the more structured looks. She’s “dressed, but she doesn’t know if she wants to go out or not,” the designer said of her muse. (This cozy, hazy should-I-or-shouldn’t-I feeling assumes win-win options.)

In betweenness is at the heart of all pre-fall collections which need to adapt to snow and sun. For cooler temps there is a smart cropped black leather jacket shown with tailored white pants. An injection of romance comes in the form of a flowy Daisy Buchanan-ish dress the color of a snowflake with bias cut lines which combines silk chiffon, jacquard, and silk wool. There are “a lot of cut lines to be able to mix all the fabrics so it’s like a blanket of different fabrics,” as Eskildsen put it. Hygge, in other words, but make it haute.
