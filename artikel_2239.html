Why Jota was subbed at half-time for Celtic as O'Neill explains decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

After subbing Jota at half-time, Celtic boss Martin O'Neill has explained that decision was down to tiredness and he had never expected the winger to last much longer than that.

For the 1-0 win over Kilmarnock yesterday, the Portuguese wide man started his first game since returning from his year-and-a-half long absence with an ACL injury.

He was subbed off at half-time, with Yang replacing him. However, that was not down to any injury concerns.

O'Neill explained that he had hoped to try and give the 27-year-old 60 minutes but always planned to assess that after 45. The decision was then taken to replace the tiring winger with Yang, who was raring to go.

The Celtic manager said: "Jota, that was his first start. I didn't expect him to last.

"I maybe thought he might get 60 minutes in the game, but he was really tiring in the last 10 minutes before half-time.

"I said to him before the game, "I don't expect you to go the whole way, I want to see how it is at half-time."

"But I just thought he was getting tired the last 10 minutes, particularly chasing back, and Yang was ready to go. Yang has been brilliant for us this season."
