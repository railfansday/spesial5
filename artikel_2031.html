Dominic Solanke and Omar Marmoush have no excuses, Tottenham teammate has elite creative numbers

Dominic Solanke and Omar Marmoush have been a frustrating pair to watch at Tottenham.

In the Premier League this season, the striker duo have just three goals between them.

Which, over 10 games, is far from the output you would want considering the money that has been tabled for them.

Most strikers may claim they are getting few chances, and are not being platformed correctly.

However, in N17, that is far from the case, particularly with Mohammed Kudus coming into the fold.

The Ghanaian is proving to be an elite-level creator in the early stages of this season.

Tottenham are struggling up front, with just 10 goals scored in 10 games this season.

It is a problem that manager Roberto De Zerbi will want to solve as goals will push his side up the table.

However, much of that is not down to winger Kudus, who has been trying his best to put the ball on a plate for his teammates.

According to Data.MB, Kudus has been one of the better crossers, or at least the most active crosser in the Premier League.

He ranks third for crosses per 90 with 5.21, while his successful crosses rank him fourth among all wingers, with 1.74.

For any striker, a winger who routinely looks to put the ball into the box is a dream.

Kudus is also ranked third compared to every English top-flight winger for passes into the opposition box, with 5.84.

He also ranks fifth for successful attacking actions with 6.32 per 90 this season.

Solanke and Marmoush must start making the most of the service being provided, as Kudus is doing his part to create opportunities in dangerous areas.

While putting the ball into dangerous areas is key, Kudus is also creating big chances for his team, showing it is not just hopeless passing.

Among wingers, he is placed in the 76th percentile for big chances created per 90, with 0.5.

Solanke and Marmoush are putting shots on goal, highlighted by the Englishman's 2.94 per 90 and the Egyptian's 2.46.

Yet, they are failing to make the most of the chances that teammates such as Kudus are providing them with this season.
