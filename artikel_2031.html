Fair to say that Matthias Jaissle hasn’t had the best of luck when it comes to injuries with his Newcastle United squad.

Having taken over at NUFC only 18 days ahead of the 2026-27 season, the last thing the new German Head Coach needed was a ridiculous injury list.

Yet this is what Matthias Jaissle has had.

Injuries blighting this Newcastle United squad and that reached a peak (hopefully!) two months ago, when nine first team squad players were unavailable for the win over Hull City at St James’ Park.

Hopefully now though there is light at the end of the tunnel.

This two week international break helping to get recovering players back to full fitness, plus no new injuries (everything crossed!) reported.

Now a new media update has reported that the understanding is that Matthias Jaissle should have an almost entire Newcastle United first team squad available for the Arsenal match on Saturday.

The Mail report that (apart from back up keeper Ewen Jaouen), Joelinton appears to be the only senior player set to beunavailable.

Even with Joelinton the update is positive.

The media update saying that whilst it was feared he could be out a lot longer, Joelinton could even be back in time for the Sunderland match in 17 days time. If not then, very soon after.
