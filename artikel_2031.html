Bruno Fernandes breaks silence on Man Utd contract offer

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Shumail Sajid writes about Manchester United for Read Man Utd and Crystal Palace for Read Crystal Palace, with occasional pieces on other Premier League clubs. He has also written for FanSided's Red Devils Dispatch, Yardbarker, Anfield Central and The Sports Side.

Bruno Fernandes has been Manchester United’s most creative player since his arrival to the club in 2020.

The 32-year-old is approaching the final months of his current deal, which expires at the end of the season. United do have the option to extend his contract by another 12 months, keeping him at Old Trafford until 2028.

While they can extend his contract for a year, Manchester United are actively pursuing a deal to tie Bruno Fernandes beyond 2028.

Bruno Fernandes himself has now confirmed that the club have offered him a new contract, but the midfielder has made it clear that he does not want discussions about his future to become a priority right now.

Fernandes confirmed that an offer had been made but dismissed suggestions that he was holding out for a better salary or a longer agreement.

“The club offered me a new contract, so I think that, from their side, shows that they want me to stay,” he told Sky Sports.

He also insisted that money has never been the reason behind his decision to remain at United, despite having opportunities to move elsewhere in previous transfer windows.

“If I wanted more money, I wouldn’t be here,” Fernandes said.

The Portugal international added that he could have earned considerably more by accepting offers from other clubs, but always chose to continue his career in Manchester.

Fernandes revealed that discussions initially took place towards the end of last season and continued when the players returned for pre-season.

However, the United captain believes the current situation at the club makes it the wrong time to focus on his personal future.

“We came in at the beginning of this season speaking about the contract, but about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to a side,” he explained.

“I don’t think it’s the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations.”

Fernandes has been one of United’s most influential players since arriving from Sporting in 2020. He has scored 75 Premier League goals for the club and set a new competition record for assists in a single season last term.

Bruno’s comments don’t sound like he is holding United hostage demanding more pay or refusing to sign a new one. The intention as told by Bruno Fernandes just show his leadership trait about putting the team first. He has no immediate intention of making his contract situation the centre of attention. That will be a sign of encouragement for the fans as well.

Manchester United need to start winning games again. When they do and surely Bruno Fernandes will be at the centre of it, the contract talks can resume and take priority.
