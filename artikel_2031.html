Newcastle senior duo told to 'come forward' as dressing room mutiny intensifies - expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Newcastle United's senior players including the likes of Dan Brun and Sven Botman have been told to step up amid growing concerns.

Reports emerging in recent weeks have claimed Newcatle's players have been left unhappy with the board's summer recruitment and the manager's tactical instruction.

After the likes of Bruno Guimaraes and Sandro Tonali were sold during the summer, it has been suggested they were not adequateley replaced, with the squad lacking leaders.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that the senior players inside the Newcastle dressing room must step up to help turn the negative mood around.

Whispers of discontent from St James' Park have raised alarm bells about what could prove to be a difficult season alongside their recent poor run of form in the Premier League.

Jaissle's side have won just one of their last six games across all competitons, but rather than leaking their unhappyiness publicly, the players have been told to step up.

Brown told Football Insider on 9 November that Newcastle aer worried about the leaks coming out of the dressing room and are asking questions about their origin.

Claims that the players may not be fully behind Jaissle, and also that they have been questioning the club's decisions, make for what could become a toxic atmosphere behind the scenes.

Newcastle's recent form is unlikely to improve without the players on side, and their senior stars are now being urged to step up and take control of the situation.

"It's never a good sign when you start hearing rumours of discontent from the dressing room," Brown told Football Insider.

"But whether they're unhappy with the manager, unhappy with the board, the transfers, any of that sort of things, it's up to the players to go out and perform on the pitch.

"All of that stuff plays a part, of course, but ultimately it's the players who get the results, and it's on the senior players to step up and get a hold of the situation.

"Yes they've lost some big influences, and it's difficult when they're being replaced with young players who don't have the same impact.

"But 'd be looking at the likes of Dan Burn, Sven Botman, other senior players to come forward now, get on with it, and show that they're still prepared to fight to turn things around on the pitch.

"It's the senior players' responsibility to resolve any dressing room issues and make sure they don't impact things on the pitch, because that's when things can go badly wrong."

The recent rumours of discontent from within the Newcastle dressing room have added to the growing pressure on the manager's position at St James' Park.

The Magpies have dropped to 12th in the Premier League table, with a recent lack of wins becoming a problem and fans beginning to grow frustrated with the manager.

However, Football Insider revealed on 9 November that Jaissle still has the full backing of the Newcastle board despite the recent whispers of discontent.

Sources say that the Newcastle board were aware that there would be "bumps in the road" when they appointed the German, and have no plans to sack him at the moment.
