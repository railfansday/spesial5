Ref Watch: Three Chris Kavanagh blunders in Man United vs Aston Villa 4-4 thriller

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Chris Kavanagh had a mixed evening at Old Trafford as Aston Villa drew 4-4 with Man United.

When Matheus Cunha was fouled in the 14th minute, the referee got it absolutely spot on by awarding a penalty.

And then he was correct not to rule out Man United's equalising goal at 2-2, when Mbuemo was in fact onside while blocking Zion Suzuki's view.

The foul by Diogo Dalot on Alysson in the second half was also spot-on, with the away side getting a penalty of their own.

However, not all of his decisions were correct at Old Trafford, with Kavanagh coming away with a mixed record.

With many contentious decisions still to discuss throughout the 90 minutes, Football Insider takes a look at three errors the Englishman made while officiating the clash.

In the eighth minute of the match, Kavanagh had his positioning all wrong, leading to Man United missing out on an open player to pass to.

The referee found himself right at the centre of a United attack, and as he took steps back to get out of the way, he managed to step on Fernandes.

The Portuguese midfielder's lane to receive the ball was then blocked by the official, al and he hopped in pain, as Villa would then regain the ball.

Not a great start for the referee, who had a number of important decisions set to come up.

Just before half-time, Fernandes had gone in high on Villa winger Alysson, with the Brazilian ending up on the floor inside the box.

It was quite reckless from the Portuguese midfielder, and on replay, it showed he did not get the ball.

VAR did check over the incident, but no penalty was awarded to the away side.

Fernandes is lucky to have escaped giving a penalty away on that occasion, although many could argue there was limited contact.

In the 72nd minute, United defender Leny Yoro went in quite strongly on Johan Manzambi, leaving the Swiss international in pen and requiring treatment.

Replays showed that the Villa man was struck first before the ball, but Kavanagh instead gave a throw-in.

The tackle was one that should have been awarded a booking and a free-kick to the away side.
