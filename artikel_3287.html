iPhone Air 2 dummy unit shows off new dual-camera design

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Apple is expected to release the iPhone 18 and iPhone Air 2 sometime next spring. Ahead of that launch, new dummy units posted to social media today give us a closer look at the devices, including a big update to the iPhone Air 2.

In a post on X, Sonny Dickson shared dummy units of the iPhone Air 2, giving us a glimpse of the new dual-camera system. Meanwhile, the base iPhone 18 looks largely unchanged compared to the iPhone 17.

The first-generation iPhone Air has a single 48MP Main camera. The second-generation model, however, will reportedly add an Ultra Wide camera.

First look at the iPhone 18 Air and iPhone 18 dummy units. The biggest change is the iPhone 18 Air, which now features two rear cameras instead of one, while the standard iPhone 18 retains a design similar to the iPhone 17. pic.twitter.com/MdOVuwPlm3

As expected, the iPhone Air 2 cameras are arranged horizontally across the pill-shaped camera plateau, similar to the iPhone Duo’s arrangement.

In addition to the dual-camera system, the iPhone Air 2 is expected to be powered by the A20 Pro chip. Apple is also reportedly working to improve the battery life. The Information has reported the device will feature a vapor chamber cooling system, which Apple debuted with the iPhone 17 Pro. Another report suggested the device will have a smaller Dynamic Island.

The base model iPhone 18, meanwhile, will reportedly use Apple’s A20 chip. Ming-Chi Kuo has reported it will have 9GB of RAM, up from the iPhone 17’s 8GB. It’s expected to be a modest year-over-year update, with Apple focused on cutting costs.
