U2 Will Bring Their ‘Greatest Show on Earth’ Stadium Tour to North America in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

U2 have finally announced a North American leg for their “Greatest Show on Earth World Tour” in support of their new LP, Carnaval De Luz.

The stadium tour is set to start Aug. 3 at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami and wrap Oct. 18 at BC Place in Vancouver. During the first half of the tour, U2 will receive support from Stephen Wilson Jr., while the War on Drugs will open the second half of the tour. A full itinerary is below.

U2 will spend much of 2027 on the road, marking their first major outing since the 2019 leg of their Joshua Tree anniversary tour. The Greatest Show on Earth Tour will kick off in February with a run of shows in Mexico City, followed by dates in South America. A European and U.K. tour will launch in April and wrap in July.

U2 released Carnaval de Luz, their 16th studio album, earlier this month.
