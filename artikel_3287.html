‘Grand Theft Auto VI’ Speeds Away With Eight Game Awards Nominations For 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Grand Theft Auto VI already has racked up more than $1B in presales ahead of its release on Thursday, and now the hit title is being showered with awards glory as well. The Rockstar Games release notched eight Game Awards nominations when that list was announced today, including Game of The Year.

The other Game of the Year nominees for 2026 are 007 First Light from IO Interactive, Control Resonant from Remedy and Annapurna, Phantom Blade Zero from S-Game, Resident Evil Requiem from Capcom and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time from Nintendo. See the full list of nominated games and individuals recognized below.

Nintendo is the Game Awards’ most nominated publisher in 2026 with 12 across its combined portfolio, followed by Capcom with 11 nominations and Annapurna Interactive and PlayStation with nine each. Watch on Deadline
