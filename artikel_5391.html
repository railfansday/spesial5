Dr. Tomy Sebastian of HL Mechatronics, HL Mando's North American R&D Hub, Elected to the U.S. National Academy of Engineering for 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Recognized for contributions to the design and development of automotive electrification systems, one of the highest honors in U.S. engineering

AUBURN, Mich., Nov. 26, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Dr. Tomy Sebastian of HL Mechatronics, HL Mando's North American R&D hub for intelligent mobility technologies, has been elected to the U.S. National Academy of Engineering (NAE) as a member of its 2026 class. His election recognizes his contributions to the design and development of automotive electrification systems.

Election to the NAE is considered one of the highest honors in the U.S. engineering community. Members are selected based on the significance and impact of their engineering achievements, with a limited number elected each year. A founding member of HL Mechatronics, Dr. Sebastian has led advances in automotive motor and control technologies. One of his major achievements was the application of permanent-magnet motors to electric power steering (EPS) systems. By addressing vibration and noise caused by torque ripple, and reducing costs, his work helped lay the foundation for wider adoption of the technology. His expertise has also been recognized by the international engineering community. In 2003, he was elected a Fellow of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE), a distinction held by no more than 0.1 percent of IEEE members and awarded in recognition of exceptional technical achievements. He served as president of the IEEE Industry Applications Society (IAS) from 2017 to 2018 and received the IEEE Nikola Tesla Award in 2019 for his achievements in the fields of power electronics and motors. Over several decades, Dr. Sebastian has contributed to the advancement of automotive motor technology. He has published more than 60 technical papers and holds approximately 30 U.S. patents.

Meanwhile, Dr. Mohammad Islam, Chief Scientist at HL Mechatronics, has received the IEEE IAS Gerald Kliman Innovator Award. Dr. Islam has helped drive a paradigm shift in steering systems by designing motors essential to the electrification of hydraulic steering systems. The motor and sensor technologies he developed are now used in tens of millions of vehicles worldwide.

Commenting on the achievements, the two engineers said, "We are deeply honored by these meaningful achievements. They would not have been possible without the outstanding colleagues we worked with." They added, "We will continue to pursue advanced technology development with an unwavering spirit of inquiry and contribute to strengthening HL Mechatronics' competitiveness."

HL Mechatronics is HL Mando's research hub for intelligent mobility technologies. Established in 2013 in Detroit, a center of the North American automotive industry, the company designs future motion technologies by integrating hardware and software. It has integrated engineering capabilities spanning electrical, mechanical and control engineering, as well as system safety and validation, supported by core technologies including electronic systems, embedded software, motors and sensors. Building on its expertise in mobility, HL Mechatronics is advancing next-generation precision motion control technologies and expanding into new industries and markets.
