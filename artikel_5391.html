Ellucian Student Wins Best Use of AI in Education & Learning at the 2026 A.I. Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

RESTON, Va., Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Ellucian, the leading higher education technology solutions provider, announced that Ellucian Student has won the Best Use of AI in Education & Learning category of The 2026 A.I. Awards. Operated by global cloud computing awards body The Cloud Awards, The A.I. Awards celebrates international leaders driving the next generation of artificial intelligence and machine learning.

Ellucian Student is higher education's complete solution that powers the end-to-end student lifecycle. Powered by the Ellucian Higher Ed Knowledge Graph, a living catalog of nearly 10,000 unique higher education processes informed by 60 years of expertise, Ellucian Student embeds AI directly into mission-critical campus workflows to enable faster, more informed action.

Advancing Higher Education with Purpose-Built AI

"We're honored to receive this recognition from The 2026 A.I. Awards, reflecting our commitment to delivering AI that makes a meaningful difference for higher education," said Mike Wulff, Chief Product and Technology Officer, Ellucian. "The real promise of AI lies in turning information into action. Ellucian Student moves beyond generating answers to reducing manual work and handoffs, removing barriers that slow students down, and giving faculty and staff more time to focus on what matters most. That's how institutions move from AI experimentation to measurable results."

Unlike generic AI tools, Ellucian AI is grounded in the business logic, policies and regulations unique to higher education, enabling institutions to move from insight to action with greater confidence.

WHAT ARE THE CLOUD AWARDS The Cloud Awards is an international program which has been recognizing and honoring industry leaders, innovators and organizational transformation in cloud computing since 2011. The Cloud Awards comprises five awards programs, each uniquely celebrating success across cloud computing, software-as-a-service (SaaS), cloud security, artificial intelligence (AI), and financial technologies (FinTech).

Winners are selected by a judging panel of international industry experts. For more information about the Cloud Awards, please visit https://www.cloud-awards.com/.

WHAT IS ELLUCIANEllucian powers innovation for higher education, partnering with approximately 3,000 customers across 50 countries, serving more than 21 million students.
