Best Version Media Relocates Headquarters to Expanded Wisconsin Facility to Support Rapid Growth

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

New Brookfield, Wisconsin location accommodates recent new hires and provides the foundation for BVM's next chapter in omnichannel local marketing

BROOKFIELD, Wis., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Best Version Media (BVM), a leading neighborhood magazine publisher and omnichannel marketing solutions provider operating across the United States and Canada, today announced that it has relocated its corporate headquarters to 15800 W. Bluemound Road, Suite 400, Brookfield, WI 53005. The new, expanded facility replaces BVM's previous headquarters at 19355 Janacek Ct, maintaining its strong roots in Brookfield while providing the necessary space for its expanding operations.

The move follows a period of accelerated growth for BVM as it successfully scaled its integrated digital marketing solutions alongside its core hyperlocal print publications. With BVM surpassing 82,000 digital campaigns managed to date and publishing over 1,300 print magazines each month, the company has experienced a significant increase in internal demand. This transition to a larger headquarters supports the growth trajectory BVM outlined following its 2025 acquisition by H.I.G. Capital, giving BVM the footprint required for recent new hires, ongoing recruitment, and expanded service offerings.

"As we continue our rapid expansion and welcome top-tier talent to our team, we needed a headquarters that reflects our next chapter while keeping us rooted in the local community," said Chris Laurence, CEO of Best Version Media. "This new space gives us a highly collaborative, flexible footprint that supports our new hires, nurtures our award-winning company culture, and positions us perfectly for the added opportunities that lie ahead as we help local businesses grow across North America."

Best Version Media is a leading neighborhood magazine publisher and omnichannel marketing solutions provider that operates across the United States and Canada. Founded in 2007 in Wisconsin, BVM specializes in creating hyperlocal print publications and integrated digital marketing services, fostering powerful community connections, brand storytelling, and local impact. Today, BVM produces over 1,300 print publications and manages tens of thousands of digital campaigns. A multi-year winner of the USA TODAY Top Workplaces and Glassdoor Employees' Choice Awards, BVM is dedicated to bringing communities closer together by sharing positive, local stories. To learn more, visit www.bestversionmedia.com.
