Chase's hotel and resort program, The Edit, gives some cardholders the chance to book stays at spectacular hotels.

Many premium credit cards have their own travel portals where you can earn and redeem your points. They also have a curated list of hotels with which they partner to offer elevated benefits to their cardholders who book directly through their program.

The Edit is Chase’s program, and similar to the Fine Hotels & Resorts program from American Express, travelers need to book a prepaid reservation directly through a Chase Travel channel and pay with their Chase card to receive the benefits.

What benefits do you receive when you book through the program? Well, they can be substantial.

At nearly 1,400 hotels and resorts, The Edit customers will enjoy:

If the hotel is an all-inclusive property, guests would instead receive a $200 property credit to use for experiences like spa treatments. While stays at hotels in The Edit Boutique Collection do include the other perks, those stays are not eligible for a room upgrade or early check-in/late checkout considerations.

The tradeoff of using The Edit program is that it does not necessarily always have the lowest rate. Sometimes you can find a lower rate directly with the hotel or another website. But, when you consider the list of benefits above, any differential in price can more than outweigh the extra cost.

While it belongs to Chase, The Edit is not open to all Chase cardholders. It is a dedicated perk for primary cardholders and authorized users of the Chase Sapphire Reserve® and J.P. Morgan Reserve cards, as well as primary Sapphire Reserve for Business℠ cardholders.

Even better is that these eligible Chase cards come with a total of $500 in credits that can be used at The Edit hotels each year. They are divided into two $250 credits that each must be used for at least a two-night stay. And when paying for the rest of your stay through The Edit, cardholders earn 8x Chase Ultimate Rewards® points per dollar spent. In addition, hotel stays booked through The Edit and labeled as “Loyalty Plus” are eligible to also earn points with any associated hotel loyalty program.

These are some of our favorite hotels in the program where you can take advantage of your annual $500 credits as well as earn significant Chase points for a prepaid stay.

It also has a full-service spa, kids club and access to Al Maaden Golf course. Its Moroccan restaurant is worth a visit and offers cuisine from all of Morocco and the North African region (with many ingredients coming from the chef’s onsite garden). Guests can add a stash of World of Hyatt points to their account after a stay here, but be sure to look to see if it is “eligible” in the search results (as shown below).

Guests can arrive at this luxury resort by its own fleet of two sea planes that serve the three properties in the Four Seasons portfolio. (Two of the properties are in The Edit, the third is a private island: Four Seasons Maldives Kuda Huraa, Four Seasons Resort Maldives at Landaa Giraavaru and Four Seasons Maldives Private Island at Voavah, respectively.)

Overwater villas with their own pools and hammocks dangling over the sea, beach bungalows with private access pools and excellent dining (from Asian to Lebanese) are hallmarks of the resort. Its newest element is the AyurMa wellness program at the spa with onsite practitioners who guide guests on personalized, multiday journeys covering everything from nutrition to physical therapy.

This resort is designed around 64 pool suites and villas with access to a beautiful beachfront, swimming pools and a jungle spa. Its island location lies within the Gulf of Thailand near Koh Samui, and its beach is well-known for its famous Full Moon parties. Other activities, besides relaxing, include Muay Thai boxing, scuba diving, snorkeling, kayaking, stand-up paddle boarding and yoga sessions.

Anantara is part of Minor Hotels that are associated with the Discovery program, which means travelers can earn Discovery Dollars, even when booking via The Edit.

Along the Southbank Promenade, this luxury hotel has great views of the Melbourne skyline and is within walking distance of the city’s great shopping, museums and dining options. When you enter the lobby, the first thing you notice is the grand Waterford crystal chandelier, one of the largest in the Southern Hemisphere.

This is also one of the city’s biggest hotels, and it is highly regarded as a top hotel in town. Be sure to request one of the rooms with a balcony or terrace to take in the views.

Known by many as Dublin’s grande dame, this Autograph Collection member is fresh off the heels of a top-to-bottom renovation that renewed its interiors, but didn’t change its historic spirit. While renovation work will linger into 2027, the refreshed rooms are worth experiencing.

The hotel is where the drafting of the Irish Constitution took place, but today it serves as a hub for visiting elite and locals to mix and mingle. When booking through The Edit, members can earn Marriott Bonvoy points in addition to Chase Ultimate Rewards®.

This beachfront hotel is a favorite destination for European travelers who want a Dalmatian coastline view while also being in close proximity to the historic center of town. There are multiple pools, a beach club and spa for onsite entertainment. Radisson Rewards members would also be able to earn points for their stay.

Radisson is growing its portfolio of high-end hotels, resorts and luxury properties, which makes the loyalty program one not to overlook for Americans who may not be as familiar with it.

This city-center hotel is ideal for business travelers, but also attracts attention for its rooftop bar and restaurant. Just remember you need to book through The Edit with a prepaid, eligible rate.

In a historic bank building, the hotel’s Bauhaus-inspired design with colorful art make this more than just a standard business property. And like Kimpton properties worldwide, guests here can take advantage of morning coffee and refreshments in the lobby, an evening happy hour, free bikes and yoga mats to use, and of course the secret password. Mention that password (which changes seasonally) at reception to be eligible for a prize that could include drink vouchers or room upgrades among other things.

Because it's an independent resort, booking through The Edit is a great way to still earn points for your stay since it does not have a loyalty program of its own.

Celebrating 100 years, the hotel has unveiled renovations across the hotel, which sits in midtown Manhattan, and they will continue for the next year. Guests who book a Club rate or have the annual lounge benefit they can select as a Milestone Reward can visit the lobby-level club lounge here.

The building was built as part of the Grand Central Terminal design plan and now belongs to the Historic Hotels of America group. Guests who book through The Edit will receive IHG One Rewards for their stay and any eligible elite status perks.

The “Pink Palace of the Pacific” will celebrate its 100th anniversary in 2027 and is a famous hotel along one of Hawaii’s most famous beaches. This is one of many Marriott Bonvoy family hotels in Honolulu, and it is surrounded by lush gardens and has stunning ocean views. Guests can walk to nearby shopping, take the onsite history tour or snap photos with Diamond Head in the background. Guests can earn Marriott Bonvoy points for their stay, and elite status members can take advantage of any eligible perks.

This all-inclusive, family hotel is a great option for those looking for great dining, sunshine and oceanfront accommodations. Parents can enjoy the spa while kids are having fun in the kids club. Other activities include movie nights outside, painting classes, beach barbecues and lawn games. The resort is part of the Preferred Hotels & Resorts program, and earning Ultimate Rewards points for booking a stay through The Edit is a nice bonus.

Prepare for a new experience at one of the Chilean capital’s most posh hotels, and you can book your entire stay through The Edit. As a result, you would receive the perks of the program as well as Marriott Bonvoy points at the only Ritz-Carlton in South America.

Big changes at the hotel include new menus that refocus the restaurant cuisine on modern takes of Chilean recipes. The chef partners with a local farmer for produce, but goes one step further in being able to have first dibs on the best produce and herbs available each season. On the top floor, guests can enjoy a panoramic indoor swimming pool beneath a glass atrium, panoramic rooftop terrace and spa offering traditional Andean healing treatments.

Booking a hotel via The Edit leads to instant benefits like free breakfast, property credits, and early check-in and late checkout. While the rates may not always be the lowest you’ll find online, the perks that come with The Edit may more than outweigh any cost differential.

The Edit’s curated collection of hotels and resorts covers a broad range of high-end properties, and the program is only open to select Chase credit cardholders. When making a prepaid, eligible reservation through The Edit, the cardholder also receives 8x Ultimate Rewards points on top of the onsite perks that make this a great offer. Even better, if the property participates in its own loyalty program, Chase has worked out that members can double dip and earn Chase Ultimate Rewards® and the relevant hotel points and potential elite status perks, too (as long as it is labeled as “eligible” in the search function).

Don’t forget about the $500 in built-in credits that come with your card, which renew each year and only further the value with Chase’s The Edit program.
