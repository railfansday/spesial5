TV deals are the rockstar of any Black Friday sale. Whether you're shopping for your first OLED TV or upgrading to Mini-LED — Black Friday TV deals offer the lowest prices of the year. I should know because I've been tracking Black Friday TV deals for 19 years.

Yes, you can find TV deals year-round on just about any retail holiday, but Black Friday is hands-down when you'll find the lowest prices. And I'm not just saying that. Two years ago I purchased my first OLED TV on Black Friday. (I got the still-amazing the LG C2). It's one of my favorite tech purchases.

On this live blog I'll be highlighting the best Black Friday TV deals on the TVs the experts at Tom's Guide have reviewed, recommended, and in some cases — purchased for themselves. One of the best deals you can still get is the 65-inch Roku OLED Pro for just $799, which is the lowest price I'be ever seen for an OLED TV this size.

Keep on scrolling to find the best Black Friday TV deals you can still get at Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, and beyond.

Starting from just $199, if you're on a serious budget, the Amazon Ember 4-Series could be a great starter TV. Naturally, it's not as bright as OLEDs or QLEDs, but the 4K panel is capable of displaying accurate colors and has a helpful Alexa+ interface.

The TCL QM6K has been a Tom's Guide go-to recommendation for some time now, especially if you're on a budget and looking to spend less than $1,000. The 4K Mini-LED TV offers better brightness and backlight control than pricier competitors, supports 4K gaming at 120Hz, and if you hook up a gaming PC, the QM6K supports 4K at 144Hz. It's an all-around crowd pleaser.

In our Hisense U6SF Pro review, we called the U6 Pro one of the best budget Mini-LED TVs out there. All models are on sale, but the 75-inch model is such an epic bargain at this price point. Two of its four HDMI inputs feature 2.1 functionality (like support for 4K games up to 144Hz), and the screen is outfitted with a glare-free finish that actually works. If you're on a budget, enjoy mostly daytime viewing, and are an avid gamer, this TV is for you.

The QM7L is a fantastic way to experience TCL's SQD Mini-LED technology for much less than what you'll spend on a higher-end model. We recently awarded this TV a 4.5-star rating for its sensational color, its thorough collection of features, and its fantastically bright picture.

The C6 is LG's flagship OLED TV for 2026. In our LG C6 OLED TV review, we said the Editor's Choice TV performs brilliantly, offering top-tier brightness and colors. We're also fans of its gameplay-enhancing features, including a new Game Optimizer mode that obliterates input lag and boosts FPS on both consoles and PCs. Whether you're a gamer, sports fan, or movie buff — you won't be disappointed with the C6.

The Sony Bravia 6 OLED is up to $1,600 off for the 83-inch panel. This premium TV has Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos, 4 HDMI 2.1 ports, is capable of displaying over a billion colors, and has bass reflex speakers.

The B6 is LG's midrange OLED set, and while it may not offer the same brightness or colors as the flagship C6, it delivers tremendous value for the price — especially now that it's 55% off. OLED TV for 2026. In our LG B6 OLED review, we said it was nearly as good as the C6 for a fraction of the price.

If you just want the cheapest 4K TV possible, this is an easy recommendation. The 43-inch Amazon Ember 4-Series is only $199 at Amazon, down from $329. It's not going to wow you with brightness like an OLED or QLED set would, but at this price, can you really complain?

If you care how your TV looks on the wall even when you're not using it, this one is for you. The 65-inch Frame Pro from Samsung's 2025 lineup is just $1,499 at Best Buy for Black Friday, down from $1,999. That's a record-low price, and gets you a gorgeous TV that doubles as décor when it's off.

Sony TV deals like this don't come around often. The 65-inch Bravia 7 II is $1,499 at Best Buy, down from $2,599. That's about the same price as the LG C6 OLED, which is $1,399 at Best Buy right now. This is Sony's most affordable True RGB TV, and our review found it excellent for HDR content (and more). If you want something bright and punchy but don't want to go OLED, check this one out.

Out of all the budget Mini-LED sets you can get this Black Friday, this is the one I'd tell anyone to grab. The 75-inch Hisense U6SF Pro is just $699 at Amazon, down from $1,399. That's half off for a huge TV that looks great, holds up in a bright room, and works just as well for gaming. It won't match an OLED in dark scenes, but at this price, that might not bother you.

We recently reviewed the TCL QM64L and gave it a glowing 4-star recommendation, praising its detailed color and compact design, delightful gaming experience, and improved interface.

Although you'll still need to pick up one of the best cheap soundbars to get a truly immersive experience, the QM64L was vibrant and bright while watching 4K Blu-rays and Netflix alike. The 50-inch is at its lowest price ever and so is the 65-inch (if you want a bigger screen).

One of the best soundbars is a quick and easy way to upgrade your audio, and our current top pick, the Sonos Arc Ultra, is 23% off at Amazon for Black Friday. This soundbar supports Dolby Atmos, and can be paired with a subwoofer and additional speakers to create a truly immersive home theater experience. Yes, Sonos has had its troubles in recent years, but it still puts out a pretty great soundbar.

Roku doesn't make a lot of TVs, and frankly, not all of them are great, but the Roku Pro Series OLED is an exception. This budget-friendly set won't outshine high-end models, but for the price, it performs very well, which is why we gave the Roku Pro Series a 4-star rating in our review. And right now, the 65-inch model is on sale for $799, down from $1,199.

Here's another Samsung OLED set we really like. In our Samsung S90H review, we awarded it four out of five stars, and praised its color reproduction and brightness — among the highest among its peers.

It also has really good speakers, something you don't often see (or hear) for a modern-day TV set. And, it's good for gamers, as it boasts four HDMI 2.1 ports, has a 165Hz refresh rate, and low input lag of just 9.3 ms.

Here's another big screen value. The 75-inch Hisense U7SG is now just $999 at Best Buy, a discount of 50%. In our review, we praised its great color reproduction, its four HDMI 2.1 ports and 165Hz native refresh rate, which will make it great if you just got GTA VI. Yes, we only gave it 3.5 stars, but at this lower price, it's a lot more attractive.

One of the biggest questions we got, at least when it comes to TVs, is if you should spring for the LG C6, or go for the less expensive B6. Well, we compared both in our LG B6 vs. the LG C6 story, and and yes, the C6 is the better pick if you want the best-looking picture out of an OLED set, but if you're on a budget, then the B6 is no slouch. And right now, you can get the 48-inch LG B6 for just $599 at Best Buy — that's 50% off its regular price.

If you want a giant — and I mean giant —TV, look no further than the TCL 98" QM7L Series 4K Smart TV. This screen is absolutely gargantuan. The price is equally large, but Best Buy has dropped it substantially for Black Friday, taking half off the regular price.

Now, don't let the size fool you into thinking this TV lacks features. It's absolutely loaded with just about everything you could want from a high-end TV. It supports Dolby Vision, Hybrid Log-Gamma (HLG), HDR10+ and HDR10, along with a 144Hz refresh rate for smooth motion.

And with four HDMI ports, you can hook up anything you want to it.

The Samsung S95H is one of our favorite TVs of the year — our very own Kate Kozuch gave this OLED set a rare 5-star rating in her review, which means it's practically perfect. And now that 65-inch model is on sale for $2,299 at Best Buy, down from $3,399, it's even easier to recommend.

On her review, she said that the "Samsung S95H sets a new benchmark for OLED performance, combining best-in-class brightness with an incredibly effective Glare Free screen." About the only knock on it was lack of Dolby Vision, but nobody's perfect.

LG makes some of the best OLED TVs, and the 2026 flagship is no exception. We gave the LG C6 4.5 stars in our review, because it offers excellent picture quality, 165Hz refresh rate, a range of game-optimizing features, support for every HDR standard you can think of, and all with a whopping $1,000 off the usual price — which is the biggest discount we've seen over Black Friday so far.
