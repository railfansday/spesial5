The best carbon plate running shoes all have a few common features. They’re all lightweight shoes with efficiency-boosting designs that rely on springy foams and stiff plates. And they’re all incredibly expensive.

Fortunately, Black Friday deals have arrived and with them has come a cavalcade of discounts on carbon plate racing shoes, which makes them a far more accessible option for runners of all levels.

I’ve been testing super-shoes ever since the original Nike Vaporfly 4% and I’ve picked out three deals on carbon racers worth checking out in the sales right now, with the pick of them being the huge $160 saving on the Saucony Endorphin Elite 2 on Amazon.

Note that with all of the deals below the price will vary depending on the size and color you pick, so do click around to find the best deal for you.

The Endorphin Elite 2 is one of the softest and bounciest carbon shoes I've tested, which means it's both fun and fast to race and train in. Several colors of the shoe have been reduced by over 50% in the Amazon sale, with a wide range of sizes available. It's a generation old, but still worth the investment.

The best deal I've seen on the New Balance SC Elite v5 brings it down to just $130, but even if this price doesn't apply to your size the discounts available on Amazon are worth considering. I found the New Balance SC Elite v5 to be a nimble racer that's great for races of any distance.

Is the best deal on the speedy Hyperion Elite 5 only available on the worst, yellow color? Yes, but don't hold that against this lightning fast carbon racer, because you'll learn to love your mustard super-shoes once you've racked up a few PRs in them. I know I did when I ran in the Brooks Hyperion Elite 5.
