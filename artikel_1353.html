Como consider new striker after Kean injury problem

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The unexpected Moise Kean setback could prompt a change of plan for Como in January, as they might need to invest in another centre-forward with Sergio Camello of Rayo Vallecano mentioned.

The Lariani already spent a lot of money convincing Fiorentina to sell Kean in the summer for €35m plus up to another €5m in add-ons.

However, after just four competitive appearances, he has been struggling with a recurring leg injury and might be out for a while longer.

Como released a statement confirming that Kean is undergoing further medical assessment following an issue in training, but the fear is he will be out until at least 2027.

That leaves Tasos Douvikas as the only available centre-forward, with Nico Paz potentially moving into a False 9 role on occasion.

It simply isn’t enough for a team in Serie A, the Champions League and Coppa Italia, so they are likely to push for another striker in the January transfer window.

According to Tuttomercatoweb, the primary target is Rayo Vallecano forward Sergio Camello.

A product of the Atletico Madrid youth academy, he was sold to Rayo in 2023 for €5m and his contract is due to expire at the end of the season, so he would be available relatively cheap.

Camello scored seven goals with nine assists in 11 LaLiga games so far this season.
