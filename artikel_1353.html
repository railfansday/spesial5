Video: Milan star Pulisic scores bicycle-kick goal in Pochettino’s final game as USMNT coach

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Milan star Christian Pulisic left his mark in Mauricio Pochettino’s final game as USMNT coach by scoring a stunning bicycle-kick goal against Haiti.

Pulisic netted a fantastic goal for his national team on Tuesday night, helping secure a 2-0 win over Haiti in the CONCACAF Nations League.

The Rossoneri winger opened the account in the 25th minute, scoring his 36th goal in 92 appearances with the USMNT.

The 28-year-old had already scored a brace in the reverse fixture on November 14, which was won 6-0. The USMNT qualified for the semi-finals thanks to an 8-0 aggregate win.

Last night’s game against Haiti also marked Pochettino’s final game in charge of the USMNT, as the Argentine will now start his tenure at Manchester United.
