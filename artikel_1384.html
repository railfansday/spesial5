Erling Haaland sparks fear among fans after concerning TV interview as he admits he is 'questioning everything' after Man City's draw with Napoli

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Published: 04:06 EST, 25 November 2026 | Updated: 08:33 EST, 25 November 2026

Manchester City fans have expressed concern over Erling Haaland's despondent interview following their Champions League draw with Napoli, with the star admitting he is 'questioning everything' amid their slump in form.

Enzo Maresca's side rescued a late draw at the Etihad on Tuesday night, with Rayan Cherki equalising in the 86th minute to secure a 2-2 draw with Napoli.

The result, however, continued an underwhelming run of form with Man City having won just twice in their last seven matches – one of which was achieved in a penalty shoot-out against Brighton in the Carabao Cup.

Man City have suffered defeats to Aston Villa, Brighton and Nottingham Forest in the Premier League in the run, as well as drawing with Fulham on the weekend.

Haaland, who captained Man City against Napoli, admitted post-match to TNT Sports that their underwhelming form has left the squad questioning themselves.

'We were not able to control and attack the game in the way we want,' Haaland said. 'Football is easy and difficult. When you win it's easy and you know what to do. When you don't you start questioning everything.

'The perfect thing would be to not think but it's not so easy.

'We have so much quality so it's about getting the quality out in every player. We have to regroup, stay positive and bring energy on the pitch and try to do our best.'

Haaland admitted in another interview with Bein Sports that the run of form 'hurts', with the star having offered a resigned laugh when asked whether the draw against Napoli should be considered a 'good point'.

The 26-year-old was seen looking down throughout the minute and a half interview, while he also rubbed his face with his hand as he looked to find answers for Man City's recent struggles.

'How the game was we managed to come back two times, but we demand more,' Haaland said.

'We have to do better and find some ways to control the game more and perform in a better way.

'I have no idea what we were missing. You could feel it in the last few games that it is not clicking fully and we have to find out, stay positive and get the best out of every single day.

'With and without the armband I feel huge responsibility because all I want to do is perform for this club and win games for Man City and right now we are not doing that. It hurts.'

Fans quickly noted Haaland's downbeat responses to questions and despondent body language during the interview.

Man City fans have expressed concerns over Haaland's downbeat responses and body language in the interview

Fans claimed they have never seen Haaland 'looking this flat' and suggested the star 'looks distraught' amid their underwhelming run

'I've never seen Haaland looking this flat. Not even close to it,' one commented.

'At least he’s being honest with himself. Looks distraught,' a second said, with a third writing 'Extremely concerning.'

'Never seen Erling Haaland look like this before,' another added, while a Man City fan wrote 'You can really tell how confused Haaland is from his expression. They don’t have confidence in their own football.'

One went as far as claiming 'Haaland looks like he’s done with Manchester City…' while sharing footage of his reaction to one of Napoli's goals on the night.

Other fans questioned whether Haaland and his Man City team-mates' recent slump may be related to concerns over the potential punishment the club could face for breaching financial rules.

Man City were found guilty of the vast majority of charges brought against them by the Premier League for financial wrongdoing when the verdict was released in September. The club have since appealed.

An independent commission ruled Man City had disguised payments worth £900million in a nine-year period with ‘sham’ deals.

Man City were found to have artificially inflated sponsorship agreements and pumped money into the club from its Abu Dhabi owners via disguised payments in a bid to swerve Premier League spending rules.

Man City have continued to insist they had 'irrefutable evidence' proving their innocence, but if the club loses its appeal, the Premier League have a range of sanctions at their disposal – including relegation or expulsion.

Man City face Arsenal in a crucial match on Sunday as they look to make inroads in the Gunners six point lead at the top of the Premier League

Haaland had pushed back against suggestions the case had affected their form during the international break, while stressing he was committed long-term to the club.

The forward's first answer to Norwegian broadcaster TV2 had spanned two minutes and 14 seconds, as he reeled off the teams he had represented from 2009 to 2018, the years covered by the charges after he was asked about the club's situation.

'In 2009, I played for Bryne U9s. In 2010, the U10s. In 2011, the U11s. 2012, the U12s, 2013, the U13s. Shall we continue? We don't need the whole list. 2014, the U14s. 2015, the U15s and the national team,' Haaland said.

'Then the U16s, I think. In 2016, I moved up to Bryne's first team, second team and youth team. In 2017, I went to Molde. In 2018, I was with Molde. The first team, Molde 2 and a bit with the youth team.

'So this is a question I struggle to answer because it's a legal battle…

'It's a case between the Premier League and Manchester City that a panel will deal with. Am I supposed to answer that? I'm not really sure.'

He continued: 'It's a difficult question to answer when I don't really know that much about it.

'I assume the answers will come… I don't know when. I haven't read much about it.

'To be completely honest, I'm focused on trying to perform for Manchester City.

The Man City star, who signed a nine-and-a-half-year contract in late 2024, told Norwegian television earlier this month that his focus was on helping the team to turn their form around.

The draw against Napoli leaves Man City lying sixth in the Champions League table - which would be enough to see Maresca's side to qualify for the last-16.

Man City face a crucial clash on the weekend when they face reigning champions Arsenal in the Premier League, as they look to halve the six-point gap to the leaders at the top of the table.
