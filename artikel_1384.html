Chiellini: ‘Italy assessment is positive, light at the end of the tunnel’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Giorgio Chiellini says that his overall assessment of the Italy national team over the last two months is positive despite ending the calendar year on a sour note with a 4-1 defeat against Belgium, and insists that the light at the end of the tunnel is starting to become visible.

Juventus’s Chief Club Affairs Officer spoke to reporters at the inaugural event for the Juventus Official Fan Club European Union in Belgium on Wednesday.

Chiellini shared his thoughts on the Italy national team since the return of Roberto Mancini as head coach and said that it is the nation’s ‘duty’ to support the Azzurri through their transitional period ‘and the people who are making it happen’.

Italy have won three, drawn one and lost twice since Mancini returned as national team head coach over the summer. Both defeats came against Belgium, in their first and final matches of the Nations League group stage campaign.

Italy had sat at the top of their Nations League group heading into the final round of group stage fixtures thanks to a 3-1 win at home against France last week, but a 4-1 defeat in Brussels saw them overtaken by both Belgium and Les Bleus on Sunday night.

“Of course I wanted the last few matches. I think it’s the start of a process, and one that has moved quicker than expected,” Chiellini told reporters on Wednesday (via Mediaset).

“Since the first defeat in Rome, there has been significant growth. It’s not easy to get all games right at this level when they’re just a few days apart.

“So, I believe the assessment of these last two months in this tournament is positive and the light at the end of the tunnel is visible.”

Italy have picked up 10 points from their last five matches against Belgium, France (twice) and Turkey (twice). That was not quite enough for a spot in the Nations League quarter-finals, but was enough to avoid relegation to Nations League League B.

“There’s still a long road ahead and it won’t be easy, it takes patience, strength and a lot of willpower, but I believe it is our duty to support this restart and the people who are making it happen,” Chiellini concluded.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
