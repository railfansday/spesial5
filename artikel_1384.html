Hutchinson: ‘My dream to get Milan contract’ and leave Nottingham Forest for good

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Omari Hutchinson insists Milan have ‘got the players’ to challenge for the Scudetto and Europa League, while his dream is to ‘get a contract’ and make the loan move from Nottingham Forest permanent.

The 23-year-old made the transfer on deadline day for €4.2m, with an option to buy at the end of the season for a further €40m.

In case that option is activated, then it would also automatically lead to a contract with the Rossoneri until June 2031.

“It’s going really well, I’m getting my minutes, I still want to push to get into the first team, now we need to start winning more and seeing out games,” Hutchinson told Milan Press as he attended a fan meet and greet in the city.

He is often used as a winger, but was signed on paper to act as a second striker, so where does he prefer?

“I like to play anywhere, ideally right wing, but wherever the manager puts me, I try to adapt and do my job. He knows what I can do on the ball, he just says to create and do your best.”

There have been some bizarre ups and downs for Milan this season, lurching from success in the Derby della Madonnina to defeat in the Europa League with Ferencvaros in the space of a few days.

“It’s difficult, because we’ve had games where we’re doing well and make silly mistakes. We’ve got to keep pushing on in the Europa League, we’ve got the squad to do it.”

Hutchinson is certainly enjoying his time at Milan and hopes that it will be a permanent step away from Nottingham Forest.

“I see how passionate everyone is about the club, the fans are always behind you, so it’s amazing to see, because I’ve never had this experience before,” he added.

He has so far contributed one goal and one assist in 11 games between Serie A and the Europa League, but has lofty ambitions for both tournaments.

“We’ve got the players, a good squad, there’s always a small detail that we’re missing, but we’re building on that, the manager and staff are working on it, and once it clicks properly, I think we’ll do well.”

Hutchinson is only on loan with an option to buy at the end of the season from Nottingham Forest, so he was asked what his dream is.

“My dream is to do well this season, hopefully get a contract here. I see myself playing here for a little while. The stadium is crazy, you see all the fans, even the away fans, and you want to create special moments to celebrate in front of everyone.”

Does he see a big difference between playing in Serie A and the Premier League?

“I think it’s tough, because when teams play against AC Milan, they want to show their best qualities. Tactically, it’s more defensive in Serie A, maybe you get a little bit more space in the Premier League, but I don’t think there’s a big gap. The leagues are quite close.”
