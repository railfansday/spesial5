International Blues: When And Where Are They In Action?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Several of Everton’s Men’s squad have been called up by their nations for upcoming fixtures during the international break.

Jordan Pickford, Jarrad Branthwaite and James Garner – all in the England squad – face UEFA Nations League matches against Croatia and Spain.

Read below to see when and where the Club’s players will represent their countries. Regular updates will be provided on evertonfc.com and our social media channels.
