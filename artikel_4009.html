K-pop Royalty Recruited for PlayStation Korea's Glitzy GTA 6 Ad

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

One thing I always appreciate about PlayStation is its ability to appeal to localised audiences, despite its message being unified globally.

The company’s current marketing strategy revolves around GTA 6, and it’s using the slogan ‘Plays Best on PS5’. But it’s promoting this in a variety of ways around the world.

Its Korean commercial is particularly glitzy, featuring K-pop royalty like Jay Park and members from girl group QWER.

The cinematic trailer revolves around the 13-year wait since GTA 5.

In the video, the city lights go down and the aforementioned celebs all rush home to power up the game – on the PS5, of course.

It’s a fun ad, and it’s interesting to see Sony invest so much into a bespoke commercial like this.

It’s particularly amusing seeing as the Rockstar open world was almost denied release in South Korea, due to its inclusion of “out of context” nudity.

Of course, it’s targeted marketing like this that’s made PlayStation a powerhouse around the globe, and will help ensure GTA 6 sells best on Sony’s box by some margin.
