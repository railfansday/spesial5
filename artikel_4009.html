Xbox Announces Black Friday Sale 2026, Reveals Nine Included Games

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Earlier this month, we mentioned that Xbox's Black Friday Sale for 2026 was set to begin on November 19th according to a leak, and now Microsoft has officially announced that it will indeed start showing up as of this Thursday.

The leak mentioned it had a start time of 3pm EST, but Xbox actually says that it'll be 9pm PST / 12pm EST / 5am GMT on November 19th going into November 20th, so you might need to hang around a bit longer until the deals appear.

"The best time to grab a gift for gamers is launching on November 19 at 9 PM PST. During our Black Friday sale, save up to 80% on select XBOX and PC games, and check out discounts on select XBOX accessories."

We also have nine confirmed games for this Xbox Black Friday Sale, which are as follows:

If you're a bit underwhelmed by what you're seeing here, don't worry - over 2,000 games will be included in the full Xbox Black Friday Sale, and despite Xbox suggesting they'll be "up to 80% off", we know from past experience that the sale will include discounts of 90% off and even 95% off for some games.

We'll obviously have a whole bunch of Xbox Black Friday roundups for you beginning later this week on Pure Xbox, but we should also mention that Microsoft is promoting discounts on various Xbox accessories (like controllers and Xbox Design Lab purchases) for the Black Friday period, along with a special Samsung TV deal that includes a free Xbox Nano controller along with two months of Xbox Game Pass Ultimate.

Sit tight, and we'll be back with all the big highlights from the Xbox Black Friday game sale in a couple of days' time!

Main reason a publisher would avoid making deep discounts after only a month or two is because they can accidentally create the expectation of such quick discounts and that in turn can hurt day one sales.
