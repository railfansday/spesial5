GTA 6 Preload Is Now Available for All Digital Pre-Orders, Download to Your PS5 Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Publisher and developer Rockstar Games has announced that preloading for the highly anticipated GTA 6 is now available to everyone who pre-ordered digitally.

In other words, if you pre-ordered the game via the PS Store, you can now begin downloading and installing the game on your PS5 to ensure it's ready to go come launch day.

The news comes alongside a brief new trailer, which includes a few new snippets of GTA 6 in action as well as the song Blood In Your Eyes by Tove Lo, which is part of the game's licensed soundtrack.

Those who have pre-ordered the code-in-a-box version of the game will need to wait a little longer to begin preloading, and you can read more about that in our GTA 6 Release Times and Preload guide through the link.

It'll definitely be worth preloading the game as soon as possible, because the install size is pretty enormous, so it may take a while for you to get the download done.
