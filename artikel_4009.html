Sea of Remnants ‘Dawnbreaker’ closed beta test begins December 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Publisher NetEase Games and developer Joker Studio will host the “Dawnbreaker” closed beta test for free-to-play oceanic adventure RPG Sea of Remnants starting December 3, the companies announced. The test will be available for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, PC via a dedicated launcher, iOS via TestFlight, and Android. Pre-download will begin on December 1, with access being limited and granted on a first-come, first-served basis.

21 Playable Characters, More Ways to Build Your Team

The Dawnbreaker closed beta test will feature 21 playable characters: two limited-time Black Spot characters, six permanent Black Spot characters, twelve permanent Purple Spot characters, and the Traveler, the game’s protagonist. Players can experiment with different team compositions and turn-based combat strategies, from pursuit-focused combinations to area-of-effect (AoE) damage builds. Character development and build customization offer further opportunities to refine tactics and tackle increasingly challenging encounters.

Players can explore two newly opened regions, the White Sea and Bridge Sea, alongside new Journey and Roaming Quests scattered throughout these waters.

The test also introduces two starting ship options. Players will receive the Fireworks Wanderer and can choose between it and the Drift Bottle before their first voyage, each offering a distinct naval combat style. For those seeking a tougher test of their strategies, the new high-difficulty challenge Peril Theatre will put team compositions and tactical skills to the test.

Embers: A World of Fallen Kingdoms and New Ambitions

The story concept PV, Embers, looks back on the fall of the Mad King and the end of an era. The Oathblade once witnessed the Five Great Houses swear their loyalty, but their ancient alliance crumbled with the Mad King’s fall. As the old generation’s ambitions fade, a new struggle for power is quietly beginning. Young Baron Michael is determined to restore the order his family lost, while his old enemy, Torino, never stays far behind. Meanwhile, Ravenscar—the Empress—has accepted the new king’s commission, becoming the strongest blade in House Hegney’s ranks.

But what does any of this have to do with you?

You are the new captain of the Drift Bottle. Will you cut through the shadows of the past, or set sail with sunshine, sea breeze, and a few friends for a carefree adventure full of mischief?

Multiplayer: Casual Minigames and Future Plans

Previously introduced casual minigames, including Lucky Duel and Goose Chess, will remain available during the closed beta test. Matchmaking is limited to players on the same server.

The fully shared-world multiplayer adventure is planned for progressive implementation after launch, bringing immersive solo adventures and a lively multiplayer pirate playground where friends can meet, compete, and explore together.

Build your crew, shape your strategy, and chart your own adventure in the Dawnbreaker closed beta test!

Sea of Remnants is due out for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, PC via Steam, iOS, and Android in early 2027.
