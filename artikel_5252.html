Anthony Pompliano has suggested that Elon Musk should consider merging SpaceX with Nvidia (NASDAQ: NVDA) if Jensen Huang is pushed out as CEO. He believes the move could accelerate Musk’s ambition to build a leading AI company. In a post on X, Pompliano proposed combining SpaceX, Tesla (NASDAQ: TSLA) and Nvidia to create a vertically integrated AI business covering everything from chip manufacturing to AI applications. He argued that Musk’s experience in building hardware and managing global supply chains could help lead Nvidia through its next phase of growth. Pompliano described the potential deal as one of the largest mergers in history, while acknowledging that regulatory and political challenges could make it extremely difficult. He also said the next few weeks could bring major developments for the AI industry.
