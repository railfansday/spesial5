Bitcoin broke below $70,000 on Friday for the first time in months, sliding from a 24-hour high of $73,866 to a low of $68,943 on Binance before falling further to around $68,770, according to CoinGecko. That leaves BTC down 6.6% over 24 hours and 9.5% over the past week. The drop triggered a wave of forced selling. CoinGlass data showed $622 million in liquidations across exchanges in four hours, with $549 million coming from traders betting on higher prices and $72.46 million from short sellers. Ether fell 6% to $1,925.57, extending its weekly loss to 13.7%, while BNB dropped 6.9% to $631.89. Tether held its dollar peg. The selloff comes as markets reel from Jensen Huang’s resignation at Nvidia, rising odds of a December Fed rate hike and fresh tensions after President Trump ruled out a deal with Iran.
