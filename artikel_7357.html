New video interviews from creators highlight issues including access to physical creative spaces and the need for more governmental recognition.

YouTube has shared more insights into the concerns of EU content creators, while also highlighting how regulators can work with the video platform to help maximize opportunities.

Last month, YouTube published its European creator consultation report, for which it surveyed 20,000 creators across 27 EU member states in order to get a better understanding of the key benefits and challenges of creating online.

YouTube shared the initial findings in its Creative Capital report, a 17-page document that outlines the various concerns of its EU creator cohort.
