NikeSKIMS Goes Vintage In New Retro Sport Collection

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

Some of the coolest activewear brands are bucking the default futuristic, technical look and instead turning to sportswear’s past for inspiration. NikeSKIMS just threw its hat in the throwback ring with its latest capsule, the Retro Sport apparel collection.

The new Retro Sport collection includes everything from outerwear and hoodies to bras, leggings, and socks. All pieces inject SKIMS minimalist aesthetics with some vintage-inspired silhouettes and materials, like fleece and nylon. The new NikeSKIMS Retro Sport collection is available to shop now at Nike.com.

Some of our favorite pieces from the Retro Sport collection are the jackets, like the half-zip Filled Puffer, which features Nike Therma-FIT technology with a water-repellent shell. There’s also a Crunchy Nylon hooded parka that’s great for rainy days, and a bold Filled Iridescent wrap.

At the heart of the NikeSKIMS collection is the duo’s Waffle Racer shoe, which launched earlier this month ahead of the apparel capsule. You can still shop the NikeSKIMS Waffle Racer at Nike.com here for $130. The shoe features a low-profile, 1970s-inspired silhouette with a nylon upper and leather and suede overlays. It’s available in three colors — Baroque Brown, Thunder Blue, and Light Bone — and looks great with athletic fits or everyday clothing like jeans and button-ups.

Another new shoe from NikeSKIMS is the Rift, a split-toe ballet shoe offered in a striking Metallic Silver color.
