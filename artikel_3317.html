‘Fatherland’ Takes Top Prize At ToruńCamerimage; ‘Wild Horse Nine’ Wins Audience Award

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The 34th ToruńCamerimage Film Festival wrapped this weekend and Fatherland and Wild Horse Nine were among the winners at the prestigious event focused on celebrating cinematographers.

Fatherland cinematographer Łukasz Żal was awarded the Golden Frog for its main competition while director Paweł Pawlikowski earned a Golden Frog Special Mention. The film premiered in Cannes earlier this year and is Poland’s submission for the Best International Feature Film at the Oscars. Set in 1949, the film follows Nobel Prize-winning author Thomas Mann (Hanns Zischler) and his daughter Erika (Sandra Hüller) as they travel across a post-war divided Germany from Frankfurt to Weimar. Cinematographer Jasper J. Spanning won the Silver Frog for Woman Unknown from May El-Toukhy while Mikhail Krichman won the Bronze Frog for Minotaur from director Andrey Zvyagintsev. To Hold A Mountain, shot by cinematographer Eva Kraljević, won the Golden Frog for Documentary Features. Watch on Deadline

Martin McDonagh’s Wild Horse Nine earned the Audience Award. The spy comedy, which is the fourth collaboration between McDonagh and cinematographer Ben Davis, stars John Malkovich and Sam Rockwell as two CIA agents dispatched to Easter Island just before the 1973 Chilean coup.

Golden Frog Special Mention for Director: Paweł Pawlikowski

Silver Frog Special Mention for Director: May El-Toukhy

Bronze Frog Special Mention for Director: Andrey Zvyagintsev

Golden Frog Special Mention for Directors: Eliza Godlewska and Alan Ruczyński

Golden Frog for Best Director’s Debut: Kaifeng Zhong

Golden Frog for Best Cinematographer’s Debut: Eira Wyn Jones

Golden Frog for Best Documentary Feature: Eva Kraljević

Golden Frog for Best Documentary Short: Maxim Arbugaev and Vladimir Egorov

Golden Frog for Best TV Episode: Maciej Sobieraj

Golden Frog Special Mention for Director: Jan Holoubek
