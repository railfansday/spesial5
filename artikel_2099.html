Carlos Vicente exit twist emerges amid Birmingham City, Wolves transfer tussle

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

La Liga side, Sevilla, have reportedly set their sights on a January transfer move for Birmingham City winger, Carlos Vicente, less than a year on from the Spaniard's initial move to St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park.

That is according to Vamos Mi Sevilla, with the Spanish giants currently sixth in their top-flight, having taken 19 points from 13 matches.

Birmingham endured a mixed 2025/26 Championship campaign, but have shown signs that they can potentially mount a sustained promotion bid this term, with Wednesday night's 2-0 success over Burnley seeing them remain fourth.

Vicente was one of two high-profile signings made by the Second City outfit in January, with his move from Deportivo Alaves coming days after seven-goal forward, August Priske, was recruited from Djurgardens IF.

It hasn't been the smoothest of rides for the 27-year-old at St. Andrew's, and he could be on the move once again if his former boss, Luis Garcia Plaza, sees his transfer wish granted.

Vicente was heavily courted by Blues having shone for Deportivo Alaves under Plaza's management, scoring 17 times and posting 13 assists in 82 appearances for his former club.

Such was their desire to retain the winger's services, it was suggested the Championship side needed to pay £17.3m for his signature, before the initial fee was whittled down to £7m as Vicente penned a three-and-a-half year contract in the West Midlands.

Davies has since utilised the wide man's services on 36 occasions, with Vicente scoring five times and posting two assists, but it has emerged that Plaza is wishing to reunite with his former star at the Ramon Sanchez-Pizjuan Stadium.

Reports state that Vicente also has a desire to return to his home country, and that Sevilla are exploring the possibility of an initial loan swoop, which could be made permanent in the summer, by which time the winger will have 24 months' left on his Birmingham contract.

Vicente made 17 Championship appearances in the second half of last season, with his best form coming in the final weeks of the campaign.

In a run of six successive starts to round off the season, the Spaniard netted his first goals against Ipswich Town and Wrexham, and continued to keep Patrick Roberts out of the starting lineup in the current campaign's early weeks.

Despite goals, again, in back-to-back outings against Norwich City and Derby County in September, a strong run of form from Roberts saw Vicente dropped to the bench, before he made a strong impact in his first start in a month in the victory against Burnley in midweek.

During the encounter, the winger created three chances and set Jay Stansfield free for the club-record signing to provide Priske with his seventh-minute opener.

It remains to be seen what Vicente's immediate future holds considering his impact from the get-go against the Clarets, as well as the sheer number of fixtures Birmingham have to contest in the coming weeks.

What does make such exit discourse particularly interesting, though, is that it has coincided with reports suggesting that Blues are, again, plotting a move for Liel Abada, formerly of Celtic, alongside local and promotion rivals, Wolves.
