GTA 6 Officially Unveils New Batch Of Screenshots

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

With the game launching in just two weeks and no delay in sight, we're officially in the month of GTA 6.

To celebrate, Rockstar has uploaded a brand-new batch of screenshots, giving us yet another glimpse into Jason and Lucia's crime spree across Leonida before we get to explore Vice City for ourselves.

There's a lot to unpack here; we see Lucia at the strip club, Jason lifting weights topless, densely-packed bars and clubs, a character scuba diving with some incredibly photorealistic fish, a group crowded on the beach after presumably hunting snakes and alligators, an understated, quieter look at the pair sitting together on a hill, enjoying the tranquility of nature, and even a snap of rapper Kodak Black, confirming his appearance in the game.

The close-up screenshots of NPC faces are uncannily realistic, while glimpses at the docks and the birds-eye-view of the pool could easily be mistaken for actual photography.

There are dozens to look through here that I'm sure GTA 6 fans will unpack in even more detail, and some that appear to have been snapped during the story, like Lucia holding a pistol to her chest while a police officer approaches. Though it could also just be Rockstar showing off how good the game looks even in the open world. Time will tell.

We really are in the endgame for GTA 6, as Rockstar has surprised us with a sudden new trailer.

We can at least see some of the gameplay on display here, like Jason refueling his car, the pair holding up a bank, and a few motorcycle chases. But it's the smaller details that really sell the world Rockstar is building, from the densely packed nightlife to the dog that makes Chop look like a San Andreas character by comparison.

GTA 6 finally arrives on November 19, so we'll be able to snap our own screenshots soon enough. Just be careful to share any spoilers, since Rockstar has warned that it will take down any major story beats, even after the game has launched.

GTA 6 looks set to be the best-looking game on consoles.
