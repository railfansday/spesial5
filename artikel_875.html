SNL Alum Heidi Gardner’s Boyfriend Ray Tintori Dead at 43

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Saturday Night Live alum Heidi Gardner’s boyfriend Ray Tintori died at age 43. The filmmaker was known for directing MGMT's "Time to Pretend," "Kids" and "Electric Feel" music videos.

Heidi Gardner is navigating an unimaginable tragedy.

The Saturday Night Live alum's boyfriend Ray Tintori has died, according to TMZ. He was 43.

A spokesperson for the New York City Office of Chief Medical Examiner confirmed to E! News their department is investigating Tintori's death, adding in a Nov. 16 statement that a "cause and manner of death are pending further study.”

E! has reached out to Gardner's reps for comment but hasn't heard back.

Tintori was a director known for his work with MGMT, helming music videos for the rock band's 2008 smash hits "Time to Pretend," "Kids" and "Electric Feel" as well as 2024's "Dancing in Babylon." He also directed The Killers' 2009 "Spaceman" music video and the visuals for Arcade Fire's 2017 single "Chemistry."

The son of film editor John Tintori and late script supervisor Mary Cybulski, the filmmaker was a founding member of Court 13, a New Orleans-based, non-profit arts collective that produced the Oscar-nominated 2012 movie Beasts of the Southern Wild.

While Tintori largely kept his personal life out of the spotlight, he did pen a lengthy birthday tribute to Gardner in 2025, describing the Shrinking actress "prettier and more graceful than the moon and by far the coolest person I’ve ever met."

"My favorite song in the world is you singing softly in my ear," he captioned photos of the couple together on Instagram. "My favorite color is the gold at the center of your eyes. I remember the first time we saw each other and how you laughed with your whole body the second our eyes met, and I immediately fell so fast."

"You are the sexiest woman who ever lived, in every single manner of clothing, hair, and old age makeup," he continued. "I’m crushing on you so deep, endlessly. You hold my heart perfectly and love me so proper."

Calling himself Gardener's "boy forever," Tintori added, "You carry the party in your eyes and move like a modern dancer everywhere you go. You are the most genius at making art and surfing the wave with the purest flow I’ve ever seen. The world is a love letter to you."

Prior to her relationship with Tintori, Gardner, 43, was married to Marvel Comics writer Zeb Wells for 13 years before their 2023 split.

"Sadly I went through the end of a relationship, which it feels weird to say end because I also know that person will always be in my life and it will transform in a different way," she shared in a 2024 episode of the The Liz Moody Podcast. "Sending them all the love and respect in the world that I still have for them, but it was just a couple-year period of transition and grief and hope and loss and all of those things."

However, the comedian noted that the breakup also allowed her to get to know herself better.

"During that time, I feel like I never wasted a day in getting to know myself," she added, "becoming a better version of myself and becoming a better friend and future partner."
