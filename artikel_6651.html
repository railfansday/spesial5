Jennifer Lopez’s “Spiced Apple Cider” Manicure Is the Coziest Winter Nail Trend

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The perfect manicure for minimalists this season

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

There's something so comforting about a rich autumnal manicure that embraces the colors that make cozy season so magical.

Deep, earthy tones and foliage-inspired hues come into their own in the colder months, and no one knows this better than Jennifer Lopez, who appeared on The Drew Barrymore Show yesterday with her fingertips wrapped in a warm, cozy shade of “spice apple cider,” according to her nail artist, Tom Bachik.

Lopez’s manicure, a sophisticated smorgasbord of brown, burnt orange, and burgundy, is made to go with pillow-soft cashmere knits, but as she showed this week, it can also be dressed up for party season.

For her appearance on Barrymore’s show, the star looked typically stylish in a form-fitting black dress complete with buttoned detailing and a faux fur collar. Lopez added height in pointed black slingbacks, but her terra-cotta manicure tied the look together and looked intentional against the dress’s jet-black hue.

For anyone looking to up the ante slightly this party season, warm, orange-tinged manicures like Lopez’s are the perfect base for a metallic cat-eye finish (a technique that uses swipes of glossy magnetic polish with minuscule metal particles).

As Daisy Kalnina, founder of The Gel Bottle, previously told Bazaar, cat-eye finishes applied to a burnt orange shade “catch the light in such a striking way.” Kalnina added: “It’s daring but incredibly flattering. It’s the kind of shade that instantly elevates a beige knit or camel coat.”
