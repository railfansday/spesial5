GTA 6 Has A Metacritic Score So High It’s Only Happened Once Before

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grand Theft Auto 6 has officially launched, and unsurprisingly, it's earning rave reviews. It's arriving hot on the heels of several critically acclaimed games this year, but does it have enough momentum to knock the current best-reviewed game of the year, The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake, off its throne? The answer is a resounding yes: not only is GTA 6 being reviewed higher than Nintendo's little hero in green, but it's also emerging as one of the highest-rated games of all time.

When Ocarina of Time launched in early November, it quickly shot to the top of the Metacritic charts, earning a metascore of 96 from GameSpot's sister site. That score came from 128 reviews, and Metacritic rates it as "Universal Acclaim" on its scale. For GTA 6, the game is sitting on a review aggregate of 99 from 147 reviews at the time of writing, with just a single point keeping it away from a perfect score.

GTA 6 is only the second game to earn a metascore of 98, and hilariously, it's tied with the original Ocarina of Time from 1998. Historically, GTA games have always had some of the highest review aggregates, especially after the series moved to the third dimension. Starting with GTA 3, mainline entries have all earned metascores in the high 90s, with Grand Theft Auto 4 being the highest-reviewed game in the franchise until GTA 6 came along. Here's a closer look at those metascores over the years.:

  • Grand Theft Auto — No metascore
  • Grand Theft Auto 2 — 70
  • Grand Theft Auto 3 — 97
  • Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas — 95
  • Grand Theft Auto 4 — 98
  • Grand Theft Auto 5 — 97

Don't feel too bad for Ocarina of Time losing its crown, because the only thing better than critical success is commercial success. The remake has sold more than 5 million copies globally in its first four days, and those numbers will keep growing as we enter the festive season. Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick recently reacted to the game's release, saying it's "terrific" and that Nintendo has every right to be proud of it.

"This game is the culmination of everything the developer has previously achieved in the open-world space that pushes the genre to heights that, in all likelihood, no other developer will be able to reach. GTA 6 is an astonishing open-world action game that I’d wager won’t be outdone until Grand Theft Auto 7," GameSpot editor-at-large Tamoor Hussain wrote in GameSpot's GTA 6 review.
