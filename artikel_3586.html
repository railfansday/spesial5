The Best Star Wars Racing Game In Years Is Going To Add Split-Screen Multiplayer

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Co-opSplit-ScreenStar Wars: Galactic Racer

Star Wars: Galactic Racer is a fantastic arcade racer that doubles as a neat Star Wars game. There’s currently no way to play local split-screen, but that’s changing next year.

Developer Fuse Games confirmed that one of the recent release’s big updates for 2027 will add a “Free Play” mode that lets people choose characters, vehicles, and the planets they want to race on. Most importantly, it’ll let them group up with a friend and race against one another in local multiplayer.

Galactic Racer didn’t launch with a split-screen option and I’m not surprised. The game is beautiful to look at and moves very, very fast. I’ll settle for one-off versus races against friends.

Galactic Racer is in a dead heat with Star Wars Zero Company for best Star Wars game of 2026, and depending on who you ask, it narrowly edges out its XCOM-like rival. But if you’re any sort of Star Wars fan, you should really play both.
