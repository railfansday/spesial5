‘Survivor’ Alum Sophi Balerdi Marries Gabriel Gonzalez in ‘Timeless’ Wedding

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Former Survivor contestant Sophi Balerdi is officially off the market.

Us can exclusively confirm the reality star, 28, married Gabriel Gonzalez on Saturday, November 14, at St. Patrick Cathedral in Miami Beach, Florida.

“We wanted our wedding to feel timeless and classic, but also warm, personal and very us,” Sophi exclusively shared with Us before the big day captured by Tori Wise. “We chose ivory and champagne tones, and loved the idea of celebrating in Miami where our families built their lives and where so much of our story belongs. As a Cuban-American, having our families and traditions woven into the day meant everything to me.”

Amanda Graham Events helped plan the celebration aimed to make every guest feel loved.

“I wrote personal letters to our guests because I wanted them to know that their presence mattered and that they weren’t just filling a seat, but were part of the story that brought us here,” Sophi explained. “We wanted a meaningful ceremony centered on our faith, followed by a celebration where everyone could let loose, dance and feel like family.”

For the wedding ceremony, Sophi stunned in a breathtaking white wedding gown from KYHA Studios.

As for the party, guests included Survivor alumni Nate Moore, Rizo Velovic, Savannah Louie, Michelle “MC” Chukwujekwu, Alex Moore, Jeremiah Ing and Kristina Mills. WWE stars Valerie Loureda and Damian Priest were also in attendance.

Fans met Sophi when she competed on Survivor 49 in 2025. After a surprise win at the final immunity challenge, Sophi made it to the final tribal council.

Although the jury respected her social game and perseverance, her close friend (and wedding guest) Savannah, 32, ultimately won the season.

Away from Fiji, where Survivor was filmed, Sophi was able to build an unbreakable bond with her now-husband.

When asked when she knew Gabriel was The One, Sophi confidently said the first date.

“It was one of those fireworks moments you see in the movies,” she said. “There was a connection that’s hard to explain, but neither of us has forgotten it. That first feeling only grew stronger through all the little moments that followed. … What started as a spark became the certainty that I couldn’t picture my future without him.”

As she looks towards the future, Sophi is more than excited for her new relationship status and celebrating with a honeymoon.

“We’re ready to eat our way through Japan, explore and probably get a little lost before slowing down in Hawaii,” she told Us. “After months of wedding planning, quality time without a seating chart or a vendor email sounds pretty perfect.”
