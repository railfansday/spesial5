You will own recruitment for China-market roles, bring local market insight to our global recruitment team, and build strong pipelines for specialist and hard-to-fill positions.

Focused on impact. You understand the 80/20 rule and focus on activities that matter.

Partner with the recruitment team, hiring managers and hiring teams to calibrate role requirements and business needs

Build and maintain talent maps and pipelines across China's gaming ecosystem

Stay up to date on the latest recruitment trends, tools, and best practices.

Experiment with AI and new sourcing approaches to improve our hiring quality and efficiency.

Proven experience managing the full recruitment cycle, from sourcing to closing, ideally in a fast-paced environment with a demonstrated ability to hire across various roles.

Strong knowledge of the Chinese gaming market (or adjacent technology) talent ecosystem

Experience recruiting across mobile games, LiveOps, game publishing, monetization or gaming business development.
