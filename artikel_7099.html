Own the full sales cycle, including outbound prospecting, cold calls, email, conferences, product demonstrations, and contract negotiations.

Sell to partners and practice leaders at large accounting firms, managing deals in the mid-five- to high-six-figure range with sales cycles of three to five months.

Two to five years of experience as a quota-carrying account executive at an early-stage B2B SaaS startup.

A record of closing six-figure B2B software deals through multi-month enterprise sales cycles.

Experience building and self-sourcing pipeline through cold calling, email, and industry events.

Entrepreneurial experience in an early-stage startup environment.

Base salary is $150,000 to $200,000 annually. Visa sponsorship is not available.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
