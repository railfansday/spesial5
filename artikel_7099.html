Taylor Swift Hints at Her Debut Era With Easter Eggs in Her 2026 CMA Awards Performance

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Taylor Swift is back at the CMA Awards this evening—and fans, as always, were ready to hunt for possible Easter eggs the moment she stepped out. The singer delivered when she performed her original song for Toy Story 5, “I Knew It, I Knew You,” which was nominated for Single of the Year. During the show, Swifties honed in on the details, with some theorizing that Swift was hinting at the incoming “Taylor’s Version” of her self-titled debut album, which turned 20 this year.

The first clue was obvious: Swift mashed up “I Knew It, I Knew You” with “Our Song,” the smash hit from her first album.

The other Easter eggs were sneakier. Swifties noticed that at the very beginning of her performance, she starts in a dressing room surrounded by a number of dresses from her debut era, including ones from a previous CMA appearance, her “Teardrops on My Guitar” video, and more.

She donned a cowboy hat and cowboy boots during the performance, too.

Swift’s whole performance was a tribute to country music and her own country roots. While making her way to the stage, stars of the genre made cameos with her, including Sugarland, Brooks and Dunn, Eric Church, and Lainey Wilson. Keith Urban played guitar with Swift onstage. Altogether, it felt like a celebration of where she came from, and maybe a hint that a re-recording of her 2006 album, Taylor Swift, is on the way.

When Swift announced in May 2025 that she bought back her masters, she also mentioned, “I’ve already completely re-recorded my entire debut album, and I really love how it sounds now.” She added that the Taylor’s Versions of that album and Reputation “can still have their moments to re-emerge when the time is right.” Maybe that time for Taylor Swift has finally come.
