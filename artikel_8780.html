Hoka just launched its Black Friday Sale, and the brand’s sale section is packed with major bargains, with discounts up to 40 percent. Plenty of shoes are on sale, but here’s one you shouldn’t miss: the acclaimed Arahi 8 trainer is over 30 percent off.

The Arahi 8 is down to just $99, a hefty 34% discount, at Hoka. This deal applies to all colorways and sizes, both men’s and women’s, but this model is selling fast, and several are already sold out. Hoka’s price beats the prices at other retailers like REI, Fleet Feet, and Bloomingdale’s.

The Arahi 8 is a core part of Hoka’s lineup and a great choice if you’re searching for a training shoe with a predictable, cushioned ride. It’s built with a dual-density midsole setup made from Hoka’s tried-and-true compression-molded EVA foam (Hoka added 3mm of foam to the heel compared to the Arahi 7), and it gets a stability boost from an H-frame platform—essentially an H-shaped layer of firm foam that cradles and supports the bottom of the foot. It even earned the APMA Seal of Acceptance, which means it was independently assessed and found to benefit foot health.

The Arahi 8 delivers a comfortable, peppy ride that impressed us in testing and helped the shoe earn a 2026 Gear of the Year Award. While Hoka has since released a successor, the Arahi 9, the 8 is still a worthy pickup. It’s slightly lighter than the 9, and both shoes share the same basic midsole setup: EVA foam and a supportive H-frame.

The only problem? You need to act fast, because these discounted Arahi 8s are flying out the door at Hoka. Snag your pair now before they’re all gone.
