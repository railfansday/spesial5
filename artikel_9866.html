What are you doing this week?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

What are you doing this week? Feel free to share!

Keep in mind it’s OK to do nothing at all, too.

Live streaming a personal research project to explore reimplementing the terminal architecture from scratch: no kernel TTY driver, no PTY, no escape codes. I've actually managed to carve out time to focus on this almost full time for the next year. Of course it would be great to have something groundbreaking and popular at the end of it, but I'm also making sure to let myself dive deep into the history of current terminals. There's so much lore there that explains and contextualises modern computing as a whole. I've been describing this project to friends as a kind of pilgrimage, that it's about the journey as much as the destination. It's such a privilege to have this opportunity so I want to make sure I try to make the most of it.

I'm organising a Half-Life game jam like they do in the Quake community, the theme is Snow & Explosions. I'm making levels and assets for the intro/hub, and writing the tooling required to merge all entries into one mod.

I picked up Material Maker (FOSS, Godot-based) along the way to make textures and it turned out to be very capable, it's easy to get something usable out of it.

I also had to resurrect Terragen 0.9 to generate skyboxes, it's been 20 years since I last looked at it and it still works fine with Wine.

Continue working on https://juju.bi to address the feedback I got from some early testers.

I am also working on designing a WASM-based plugin system for Jujutsu (https://jj-vcs.dev).

It requires some money upfront (at a minimum €5k for 50k images for a year, I'm thinking) mainly because of the storage, memory, network, and VLM costs, and I've got most of the search crawl + ranking tech built with Uruky Site Search (it'd need tweaks), though that's focused on indie/smallweb, but this would aim to be a global index, focused in Europe initially.

I hope to launch a campaign and the website still this week and if anyone would be willing to promote/share in their socials (I don't have any), I'd greatly appreciate it!

I picked up an Erlang book from eBay for £3.63, so I've been digging through that. I'm somewhat interested in targeting BEAM in some toy compilers (or investigating reviving an "Erlang on Xen" effort). For now, though, I'm trying to see if its concurrency model would help us rewrite various daemons at work (the XenAPI toolstack).

In case it helps: from experience, the best way to target the BEAM is to generate the Erlang abstract format; afaict its effectively replaced "core Erlang" which was always a compiler implementation detail, and the BEAM bytecode is not stable between major versions (though also apparently not very unstable).

I'm finally building my own mobile OS/linux distro. Installing it on my phone was easier than I thought (using claude code, ofc). If anyone wants to try, I have been writing the findings here: https://github.com/android2linux/android2linux
