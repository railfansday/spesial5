Scotland boss hails 'buy-in' as senior trio embrace Under-21 drop

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Scotland Under-21s head coach Tony Docherty is delighted with the buy-in from the three players who have dropped down from the senior squad.

Colby Donovan, Lennon Miller and James Wilson have all been included in a new-look Scotland Under-21 squad for friendlies against Ukraine and United States in Spain this month.

The three players were all in Sebastian Pocogonoli’s first full Scotland squad and join Tyler Fletcher, who went to the World Cup before joining Docherty’s group in September.

“I’m really, really delighted with the introduction of the ones that have had that A squad experience, and they’re really the drivers,” Docherty said. “They become your leadership group that drives standards, create that mentality, get that identity.

“You’ve seen that in the last camp when I was out at the A squad camp and I returned with James and Findlay (Curtis), and then Findlay goes back up again as a result of his performance.

“Lennon and Colby Donovan, I’ve had dialogue with both of them quite a bit over the last couple of days, just to outline what their pathway is.

“If they’re not going to be getting minutes for this camp with the A squad, it’s important they come in to my squad and it will be a mirror image in terms of structure, fundamentals. And they just come in and get an opportunity to get game time.

“So, really pleased that Lennon, Colby and you can include Fletch, James in that, they don’t just come to the 21s, they really participate.

“I had a particularly long conversation with Lennon. You’ve seen the example, the last camp there with Barney Stewart. He comes in, he scores three goals, he’s in a good place when he gets back, gets into West Brom’s first team, scores a couple of goals, they win a couple of games.

“I had a good conversation with Colby and he just wants to play. So it’s important that we’ve got the buy-in. It’s not us telling Colby, ‘you’re playing with the 21s’, it’s him in dialogue with us saying, ‘I want to do that, I want to play and I want to impress’.

“And that’s very much where we are now, and that’s a relationship I think is really healthy.”

St Mirren striker Eseosa Sule has been called up as Docherty prepares for a new European qualifying campaign after 16 of the 27 players from his first squad went beyond the age level.

Rangers youth player Kyle Glasgow drops to the Scotland Under-19 squad for three European qualifying games. The 16-year-old on-loan Alloa player joins elder brother Euan Glasgow, who plays for Edinburgh City on loan from Hearts.
