Lakers’ incoming owners sit with Jeanie Buss at first game together since deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Lakers' soon-to-be co-owners were in attendance for the team's home matchup against the Jazz Monday night, marking the first time they were at a game together since agreeing to purchase the majority ownership of the franchise since the summer.

Josh Kushner and Bob Iger, who are purchasing the majority ownership of the Lakers from Mark Walter at a record-setting valuation of $12.5 billion, were at Crypto.com Arena for Lakers-Jazz.

Bob Iger attends the PAC NYC Icon Of Culture Gala in New York. Gregory Pace/Shutterstock

They sat with current franchise Governor Jeanie Buss, with there being continued discussions about Buss' role with the organization after the sale is complete.

Kushner and Iger agreed to purchase ownership of the franchise from Walter in mid-August, just 10 months after he officially purchased the team from the Buss family at $10 billion.

Joshua Kushner, left, and former Disney CEO Bob Iger, right. JASON SZENES/ NY POST

Walter and Kushner met at Walter's home in Florida last month to finalize the terms of the sale.

Once the agreement of the sale is officially signed, the transaction will need approval from the NBA's Board of Governors.

Any NBA ownership sale requires approval from at least three-quarters of the Board of Governors.
