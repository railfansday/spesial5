Red Sox's Biggest Offseason Addition Might Already Be on Their Roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Red Sox's Biggest Offseason Addition Might Already Be on Their Roster originally appeared on NESN. Add NESN as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

With the Red Sox already making one of the biggest moves in Major League Baseball free agency by signing Jake Bauers, there's been some hope within the fan base that they'll go out and make another blockbuster deal.

However, while the Red Sox are always in the business of getting better and will continue to try to do so, it's important to remember some of the injuries they dealt with a year ago.

Specifically, not having left-hander Garrett Crochet in that rotation all year was a major loss for a Red Sox team that was already one of the better teams in the American League without him for much of last season.

That's part of the reason why the Red Sox can feel very good about what 2027 and beyond might look like.

Crochet, at his best in 2025, finished second in American League Cy Young voting, was named an All-Star, and finished in the top 10 in American League MVP voting.

He posted a 2.59 ERA and struck out 255 hitters in 205.1 innings.

Again, while the Red Sox will always be looking for ways to improve, and there are ways to do so offensively, sometimes the best additions come from the players you already have.

Crochet is undoubtedly one of the best five pitchers in Major League Baseball when he's healthy, and that's going to give the Red Sox another look they barely had last year, after he pitched only six games during the regular season.

There are always questions about guys coming back from injuries and how they'll look, but Crochet seems to be healthy and has a whole offseason to get even healthier.
