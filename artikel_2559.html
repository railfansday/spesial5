Confirmed lineups: SC Freiburg vs. 1. FC Union Berlin

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Julian Schuster makes four  changes to the team that won away at Trabzonspor in Europa League action on Thursday evening. Phillip Treu, Niklas Beste, Derry Scherhant and Yuito Suzuki replace Lukas Kübler, Cyriaque Irie, Vincenzo Grifo and Keisuke Goto.

Mauro Lustrinelli has named an unchanged side from last week’s 1-1 draw with Köln.

SC Freiburg (4-2-3-1): Backhaus; Treu, Ginter, Lienhart, Makengo; Eggestein, Engelhardt; Beste, Suzuki, Scherhant; Matanović

1. FC Union Berlin (3-4-1-2): Rønnow; Van den Bosch, Friedrich, Khedira; Haberer, Aebischer, Schäfer, Köhn; Imeri; Skarke, Burcu
