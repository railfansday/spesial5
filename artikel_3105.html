Disney+ and Hulu’s ‘fully unified’ app is coming next month

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Disney has announced a major milestone coming next month in its plans to merge the Disney+ and Hulu apps, which will be “fully unified” into Disney+ soon.

Today Disney reported its quarterly earnings and gave an update on plans to merge Disney+ and Hulu into one app.

Live TV and premium add-ons were the final Hulu features that hadn’t yet been imported to the Disney+ app. But by adding those capabilities into Disney+, the app unification will be “complete.”

The ultimate goal of unifying everything in Disney+, though, will be accomplished soon.

Also in the earnings report, Disney said that creator content will start appearing in the Disney+ app in December. This is related to the company’s deal with TikTok that debuted after its previous OpenAI deal fell apart when Sora was killed off. The Disney+ app will also get “more vertical video, live sports, and podcasts” soon too, Disney said.
