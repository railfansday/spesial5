Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Asset sales and mining margin pressure shape the quarter's deleveraging push.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 18, 2026 at 4:23PM EST

Siderúrgica Nacional (SID -5.89%) reported a decrease in quarterly earnings as margin compression in the mining segment and unfavorable freight conditions offset gains in its other divisions. Management indicated that the company is executing a deleveraging plan through the divestment of noncore assets, including the sale of its cement subsidiary and a German steel unit. The company intends to rebalance its capital structure by extending debt maturities and reducing inventory levels while focusing capital expenditures on the P15 iron ore expansion project. Officers stated that structural improvements in mining profitability depend on the completion of this infrastructure initiative to reduce dependence on freight cycles.
