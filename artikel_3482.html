Forget ‘Dept Q,’ The Best New Scottish Mystery Series Is Officially Here

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

On October 24, filming came to an end on the second season of one of Netflix's best recent breakout shows. Matthew Goode's Detective Chief Inspector Carl Morck will be back with many of his trusted team, including Alexej Manvelov, Leah Byrne, Jamie Sives, Mark Bonnar, Kate Dickie, Aaron McVeigh, and Sanjeev Kohli. Speak No Evil's Aisling Franciosi and Gavin & Stacey star James Corden have joined the cast, alongside Greg Wise, Nicholas Rowe, Tony Curran, and more.

Dept. Q is expected to return sometime in 2027, but if you can't until then for your fill of Scottish-set crime-solving, then you needn't worry, as BritBox has you covered. BritBox and the BBC have officially announced a new series titled The Loch Ness Mysteries, set in the remote Highland district of Monaruadh, with filming starting later this year around Luss and Glasgow. Melissa George (Mosquito Coast, Mulholland Drive) will star as DCI Vanessa ‘Nessie’ Keen, with Dept. Q's own Jamie Sives and Ashley Storrie (Dinosaur, Up for It) also starring. George said in a statement:

"I’m incredibly excited to be part of The Loch Ness Mysteries. Rarely do stories like this come across, with a brilliant and developed character like Nessie. I was immediately drawn to this world and it has been a joy to have a hand in shaping it. I cannot wait to step into these shoes and get on set."

Four criminal masterminds. One of them was always going to be yours. Here is who comes for you, why they picked you out of everyone available, and what they want from the encounter.

Your nemesis does not want to beat you. He wants to know you completely, and he is patient enough to wait for it. The frightening part is how good the conversation is.

Your nemesis is not after money, territory or revenge. He is after a demonstration, and you are the person he has chosen to prove his point on.

Your nemesis respects you, which sounds flattering until you work out what it means. He chose you because nobody else was worth his time.

Your nemesis has no personal interest in you whatsoever, and that is precisely what makes him unsurvivable. You are not a rival. You are a stop on the way.

Writer Ed Sellek (Sister Boniface Mysteries, Money Gone) will tackle the six-part series. Scottish writers Ciara Conway (The Teacher 3, Whitstable Pearl) and Niki Rooney (Malory Towers, The Dumping Ground) will also write for the series, with Katherine Churcher (Grantchester, The Marlow Murder Club), Outlander's Brian Kelly, and Ryan Graham (River City, Coronation Street) directing. A synopsis for the series reads:

"DCI Vanessa ‘Nessie’ Keen (Melissa George), is a whip-smart, deeply provocative London detective whose life is thrown off course when a brush with death leaves her signed off work and persona non grata… until a peculiar personal mystery summons her to the shores of Loch Ness."
