Jennifer Lawrence Pokes Fun at Rumors She Had Bleph Plastic Surgery Procedure

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Jennifer Lawrence has a message for anyone speculating she underwent blepharoplasty surgery on her eyelids.

“Iykyk she’s the one who did my bleph,” Lawrence, 36, wrote via Instagram Stories on Monday, November 23, amid online speculation the famed actress underwent an eyelid lift, instead jokingly crediting her makeup artist, Shayna Goldberg, for her fresh-faced look.

Lawrence addressed the rumors while reposting Goldberg’s photo, showing a glimpse at some new products from her mes° haircare brand, which the No Hard Feelings star launched in October.

“Very exciting to see this printed,” she wrote.“Thanks for sending…”

Fans questioned if she underwent the cosmetic procedure, which removes or repositions excess skin, muscle or fat on the upper or lower eyelids after seeing Lawrence’s photo shoot for The Cut and her latest red carpet appearance for the New York City premiere of The Hunger Games: Sunrise On The Reaping on November 16.

Lawrence has been an open book about her thoughts on plastic surgery over the years, telling Andy Cohen on the November 19 episode of Watch What Happens Live that she recently got a breast augmentation.

“Yes, and I have implants. They’re new,” she said. “It’s a full C. I think! It’s the same size that they were,” Lawrence added, referring to her pre-pregnancy cup size.

Prior to that interview, Lawrence told The Cut about her breastlift and plans to get a facelift down the line.

“I want to be a sexy mom,” the producer, who shares two sons with husband Cooke Maroney, said. “At drop-off, I want to look like a woman that has her s*** together even when I’ve just woken up and don’t have my s*** together.”

In 2023, Lawrence talked candidly about enhancing her appearance with some makeup magic.

“It’s incredible what makeup can do because I work with Hung [Vanngo], who overlines the lip, and I call him a plastic surgeon because everybody in the last few months since I’ve been working with him is convinced that I had eye surgery,” she told Kylie Jenner for Interview magazine.
