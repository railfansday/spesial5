UEFA Nations League: Bremen duo and 2. Bundesliga stars shine in League C

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Holstein Kiel’s striker Adrián Kaprálik saved Slovakia’s unbeaten record in the 2026/27 UEFA Nations League with a 95th-minute equalizer against Kazakhstan. The 24-year-old scored his first goal for the senior national team as Slovakia win promotion to League B with 12 points.

After his debut appearance in the 2-2 draw with Faroe Islands, the Haarlem-born midfielder Ludovit Reis appeared off the bench as Vladimír Weiss’ side repeated that scoreline on Monday with the help of Kaprálik's strike. The Werder Bremen central midfielder decided to change his allegiance after "only a matter of one phone call", according to the head coach.

Meanwhile, Reis’ club teammate Karl Hein and VfL Bochum's center-back Karol Mets helped Estonia to a 2-1 win over Lukas Petkov's Bulgaria on Monday. Estonia finished second in their group despite also finishing with 12 points. Mets is the captain of the team, while the Arsenal loanee won back his #1 spot after his solid performances for Bremen.

Despite Estonia's unbeaten campaign, they finished two points behind Ísak Jóhannesson's Iceland, who automatically won promotion to League B alongside Albania, Montenegro and Slovakia. The Köln midfielder started all six games for Iceland. Estonia, Belarus, Cyprus and Faroe Islands will enter playoff with bottom-placed teams from League B.
