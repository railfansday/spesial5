Draymond Green has advice for heckling Rockets fan during heated discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Golden State Warriors had an opportunity to post a statement victory on the road against the Houston Rockets on Wednesday night. Houston came out on top in the battle of hot teams, 115-108, in a contentious contest that featured both a thrown punch and what looks like a significant Stephen Curry injury.

The home crowd felt even more embolden to needle Draymond Green late in the game with the outcome essentially decided. And as one might expect, the veteran Warriors forward did not let that be a one-sided conversation.

MORE: Warriors' Steve Kerr shares concerning injury update on Stephen Curry after early exit

Which led to this verbal confrontation during a trip to the foul line for Houston.

Green had some advice for the heckler's next move, which he shared without much of a filter. Then he took things a step further and made it his mission to get the fan booted by security. Or fans, as the ESPN broadcast reported that two individuals were removed following Green's request.

Predictably, Green is getting ripped for being soft following the incident.

Look, the point of having principles is so they can be followed even when it's difficult. Green is not a sympathetic figure. He's a future Hall of Famer and wildly entertaining, but he has plenty of stuff on his ledger that fans might not like. Or at the very least find annoying.

You still have to side with the player over the fan. Even if Green isn't your cup of team.

The amount of stupid stuff players have to hear every game would boggle the mind of anyone who experiences it. Most of it must be white noise at this point for players who are used to it so one needs to trust that if they're responding, then it's over the line.
