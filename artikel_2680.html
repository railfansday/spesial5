More Maxx Crosby appreciation

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It's Thanksgiving and we might as well show some thankfulness.

So, as we often use this space to show appreciate for the greatness of Las Vegas Raiders star defensive end Maxx Crosby, today seems like a perfect day tp throw Crosby some more love.

Crosby affects games in so many ways and Sunday at the Denver Broncos he showed his versatile value again. Crosby batted down two Bo Nix passes at the line of scrimmage in the Raiders' 38-24 defeat. He now has 32 career pass defensed since he entered the NFL in 2019.

It's the fifth most among NFL defensive linemen in that time span, according to the Raiders' public relations department.
