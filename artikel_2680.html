Chris Broussard gets 100% real on Cowboys’ defensive disaster vs. 49ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Chris Broussard gets 100% real on Cowboys' defensive disaster vs. 49ers appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Dak Prescott and the Dallas Cowboys suffered another loss this season, recently falling 46-40 in a shootout against the San Francisco 49ers. The offense did its job, but the defense failed once again as sports analyst Chris Broussard blasted them.

Dallas tried its best to keep up with the 49ers' offense, having Prescott fire touchdown passes to George Pickens, who had his best performance of the season. However, the defense's inability to contain Brock Purdy and Christian McCaffrey resulted in multiple lapses as it cost Dallas greatly.

Broussard reacted to the events of the game during his Monday appearance on First Things First. He talked about the mistakes the defense made, using the acronym Prescott had about the offense and flipped it towards the defense with how poor they have been this season.

"You're not the greatest offense there is, but good news! You are the worst DEFENSE there is! So you get a 'W' for weak, a 'W' for wretched, a 'W' for walked all over!" Broussard said.

The Cowboys have the offensive talent they need with Dak Prescott leading the way to be a fierce playoff contender. However, their defense will be the reason they miss the playoffs if they don't figure it out throughout the second half of the season.

Prescott was active with his throwing throughout the game. He completed 30 out of 51 passes for 343 yards and five touchdowns and one interception.

Javonte Williams led the run game with 16 carries for 63 yards. George Pickens commanded receivers with 11 catches for 145 yards and three touchdowns. Meanwhile, CeeDee Lamb caught seven passes for 72 yards and a touchdown.

The Cowboys will look to bounce back in their next matchup, remaining at home. They host the Tennessee Titans in Week 11 as kickoff commences on Nov. 22 at 1 p.m. ET.
