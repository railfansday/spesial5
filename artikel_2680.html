Orlando City roster update: Iván Angulo in talks to return

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Orlando City announced their year-end roster decisions on Tuesday, confirming discussions to bring wingback Iván Angulo back to the club.

The 27-year-old Colombian is out of contract following the 2026 season, but could return to the Lions in 2027. Angulo has played 181 games for Orlando across all competitions since joining the club in 2022. He just recorded his best year with the club, tallying career highs in goals (seven) and assists (13) during the regular season.

Orlando are also in talks to bring back Peru international midfielder Wilder Cartagena, whose contract option was declined.

Additionally, club captain Robin Jansson officially announced his retirement after eight seasons with Orlando. The center back made 263 appearances across all competitions.

The Lions exercised further contract options on four players, including goalkeeper Javier Otero, midfielder Joran Gerbet and defenders Nolan Miller and Zakaria Taifi.

In 2026, Orlando enjoyed a sensational second-half turnaround after a slow start to the season, buoyed by the arrival of the 2026 MLS Newcomer of the Year, Antoine Griezmann. They finished eighth in the East but were defeated by FC Cincinnati in the Wild Card match.
