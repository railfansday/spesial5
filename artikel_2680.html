WATCH: Raiders QB Fernando Mendoza gorgeous 68-yard 1st career TD pass

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

In the second start of Fernando Mendoza's career, he got his first ever touchdown pass. In the final minutes of the second quarter in Denver, with Las Vegas down 17-3, Mendoza saw a mismatch with a linebacker guarding Ashton Jeanty. He dropped back and threw a perfect dime ball right into Jeanty's bread basket. Jeanty caught the ball in stride and was gone 68 yards for the score.

This isn't the first touchdown of Mendoza's career. He got his first touchdown on the ground on the first drive of his first start last week. It went for ten yards to put the Raiders up 7-0

But as cool a moment as it must have been for Mendoza on that rushing TD, he's in the NFL for his arm first and foremost. He showed that off in spectacular fashion for the first TD pass of what the Raiders hope is a long career in Las Vegas.
