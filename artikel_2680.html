Amerks rally late, stun Laval on Kuntar's overtime winner

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Trevor Kuntar scored 2:13 into overtime after Jason Polin tied the game on a late two-man advantage, lifting the Rochester Americans to a 5-4 victory over the Laval Rocket on Saturday at Blue Cross Arena.

Kuntar's sixth goal of the season completed Rochester's comeback from a 4-3 deficit and snapped the Amerks' seven-game losing streak against Laval dating to last season. The win also gave Rochester a split of the weekend series after the Rocket won Friday's opener 3-1.

Riley Fiddler-Schultz had a hand in four of Rochester's five goals, finishing with a goal and three assists. Jason Polin scored twice, Jake Richard contributed a goal and an assist, and Caleb Jones set up two goals as the Amerks improved to 8-6-0-1.

Laval, the North Division leader, moved to 12-3-2-0 and earned three of a possible four standings points during the two-game visit. The Rocket finished the night tied with Grand Rapids atop the American Hockey League standings.

Rochester led 3-2 after two periods, but Sasha Pastujov turned the game around with two power-play goals in the third.

Pastujov tied the score 59 seconds into the period, then completed a natural hat trick at 13:59 to put Laval ahead 4-3. The three goals raised Pastujov's season total to 10.

The Amerks received their opening when Hunter McKown was assessed a high-sticking penalty at 16:50 and Michael Szuber was called for interference 36 seconds later. Rochester converted the ensuing five-on-three advantage when Polin put home the rebound of a Conor Sheary shot from near the crease with 1:17 remaining.

Polin's second goal of the night and ninth of the season sent the game to overtime. Six of Polin's nine goals have come on the power play, and Saturday marked his fourth multi-goal performance through 15 games with Rochester. He already has more multi-goal performances with Rochester than he recorded in 134 games with the Colorado Eagles.

In overtime, Jones and Fiddler-Schultz assisted on Kuntar's winner. Kuntar finished the game with three shots and a plus-3 rating.

Laval opened the scoring 5:37 into the first period when Alex Belzile converted a power play. Richard answered with a power-play goal at 16:20, sending the teams to the intermission tied at 1.

Pastujov gave Laval another lead 52 seconds into the second, but Rochester responded with two even-strength goals. Polin tied the game at 6:25, and Fiddler-Schultz put the Amerks ahead at 15:36.

Fiddler-Schultz's four points were a career high and raised his season totals to five goals and six assists in 15 games. All 11 of his points have come on home ice.

Luke Mittelstadt assisted on all four Laval goals, while Belzile added two assists to his opening goal. Laval's power play finished 4-for-6, accounting for every Rocket goal, while Rochester went 2-for-6.

Matt Villalta stopped 28 of 32 shots to earn the win and improve to 7-5-1. Kaapo Kähkönen made 20 saves on 25 shots for Laval.

The Rocket held a 32-25 advantage in shots, including 15-6 in the third period. Rochester generated only one shot in overtime, but Kuntar made it count before a crowd of 6,183.

The Amerks carried a 1-0 lead into the third period Friday before Laval struck three times to rally for a 3-1 victory at Blue Cross Arena.

Vsevolod Komarov gave Rochester the lead at 13:26 of the second period with his first goal of the season. Matt Villalta made the advantage stand through 40 minutes despite Laval holding a 25-14 edge in shots.

The Rocket finally broke through when Jared Davidson capitalized on a Rochester turnover to tie the game at 6:08 of the third. Samuel Poulin then scored the go-ahead power-play goal at 11:14 before adding an empty-netter with 1:14 remaining.

Villalta finished with 29 saves on 31 shots. Rochester went 0-for-6 on the power play, while Laval converted one of its three opportunities.

The Amerks next visit the Syracuse Crunch at 7 p.m. Wednesday.
