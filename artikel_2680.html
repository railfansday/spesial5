Ravens’ Jesse Minter puts entire team on notice with warning after ’embarrassing’ Chargers loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Baltimore Ravens are running out of answers after another disappointing loss, and head coach Jesse Minter is making it clear that nobody on the roster should feel comfortable with their current performance.

Minter faced his former team, the Los Angeles Chargers, for the first time as Baltimore's head coach on Monday night. Unfortunately for the Ravens, the reunion didn't go as planned. The Chargers earned their first win of the season with a 23-20 victory, handing Baltimore its fifth consecutive loss.

The Ravens opened the season 4-1, but have since fallen to 4-6. Following Monday's defeat, Minter didn't hide his frustration with what he saw from his team.

When asked about the offense producing no rushing yards by halftime, Minter had a blunt response.

"It's unacceptable," Minter said. "It's embarrassing."

Derrick Henry even expressed his frustration with the total as well. But the running game wasn't the only area drawing attention from the head coach. Minter was also asked whether he was considering other options at punt returner, but his answer made it clear that his concerns extend well beyond special teams.

"To me, every position is at that point," Minter said, per Jamison Hensley. "So it's not just punt returner. Nobody has played to a level where you're like, 'Man, this guy.'"

Minter continued by pointing to the lack of consistency across the roster and made it clear that the coaching staff is evaluating every position. The message is difficult to misunderstand. Baltimore has struggled to put together complete performances, and the five-game losing streak has put the team in a completely different position than it expected to be in after its 4-1 start.

Minter isn't singling out one player or one position. He's challenging the entire locker room to improve, and his comments suggest that changes could be coming if the Ravens don't start showing more consistency.
