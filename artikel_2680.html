Izzo thankful for MSU-Arkansas to join ‘great day’ of Detroit sports on Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

EAST LANSING — Michigan State is used to having basketball on the menu for Thanksgiving. This time, it'll eat in its own backyard.

Following the Detroit Lions' divisional game against the Chicago Bears, Michigan State and Arkansas will play across Woodward at Little Caesars Arena in a game that tips off at 4:30 p.m.

"You got the turkey trot that I used to run in when I was younger. You got the Thanksgiving Day parade, got the Lions game, and then of course you've got the Spartans," MSU coach Tom Izzo said. "It should be a great day for a lot of Spartans in Detroit, but (also) for the city of Detroit."

Thursday will mark the 13th time under Tom Izzo that the Spartans (5-1) will play a Thanksgiving Day game, as well as the 11th in the past 14 years. The Spartans are 8-4 in those previous 12 games, all played in distant locales. Alaska and the Bahamas, Oregon and California, Florida and Puerto Rico. Now, it is closer to home in Detroit, though plans for turkey dinners with families will have to wait until after the final buzzer.

This season, Michigan State was supposed to return Arkansas' trip to Breslin Center last year — MSU won the first game, 69-66, on Nov. 8, 2025 thanks to a double-double from Cam Ward. So how exactly did Izzo convince John Calipari to bring his team up here for a second straight season?

"I told Cal he had three tickets to the parade, and dress him up as some strange character," Izzo joked.

In all seriousness, Izzo commended Calipari for his flexibility for taking what he sees as a big opportunity. For years dating back to his tenure at Kentucky, Calipari wanted to play neutral site games in these big matchups. Then he started to lean toward the draw of home and homes. That played a part in MSU's original home-and-home deal with Arkansas.

"I give John credit. He jumped on it and he didn't have to, and I appreciate him doing that," Izzo said. The Spartans will travel to Fayetteville next season.

A good TV opportunity helped draw Arkansas. CBS stepped in with an idea to follow the Lions' Thanksgiving game with some college basketball. When MSU drew a high audience in 2023's Acrisure Classic against Arizona right after the Lions game, it piqued the interest of TV executives. Fox got the Spartans down to the Fort Myers Tip-Off last year to play North Carolina right after the Lions' loss to Green Bay.

According to TV ratings company Nielsen, Michigan State and Arkansas ranked first and second among all men's college basketball teams for viewership through the non-conference schedule last season. Duke, North Carolina, and Kentucky — all teams Michigan State played — followed.

"This was kind of a treat," Izzo said. "I think (Calipari) realized that the situation on the draw is probably good for his recruiting too, you know. When you look at playing after the Lions, it's been really good for us the past couple of years, and so hopefully Dan will do the job and get a win, and then pass the baton over to us and see what we can do."

It will be a big-time matchup for Michigan State, which had a strong win over Duke in the Champions Classic two weeks ago but has lost a bit of its footing with injury woes. A 61-46 loss at No. 11 Tennessee and a close-until-it-wasn't 91-70 win over Detroit Mercy slid Michigan State from down from No. 2 to No. 10 in the latest USA Today Coaches Poll. Arkansas (4-2), meanwhile, slid completely out of the polls after picking up its second loss to now-No. 24 Indiana. It also lost to No. 4 Virginia in the season opener.

Michigan State is expected to get forwards Jordan Scott (right shoulder) and Coen Carr (right knee) back in the lineup after sitting out the Detroit Mercy game to rest injuries. Meanwhile, Arkansas will get star freshman Jordan Smith Jr. back after a wrist injury.

Michigan State is 7-1 all-time at Little Caesars Arena, which opened Sept. 5, 2017. Its only loss was 2018's second round loss to Syracuse, 55–53. Five of those wins were against Oakland, one against Bucknell in the 2018 NCAA Tournament and one against Baylor in 2023.

Michigan State is 8-4 all-time under Tom Izzo in games played on Thanksgiving

2025: No. 11 Michigan State beat No. 16 North Carolina, 74-58, in the Fort Myers Tip-Off in Fort Myers, Fla.

2023: No. 21 Michigan State lost to No. 3 Arizona, 74-68, in the Acrisure Classic in Palm Springs, Calif.

2022: No. 12 Michigan State lost to No. 18 Alabama, 81-70, in the Phil Knight Invitational in Portland, Ore.

2021: Michigan State beat No. 22 UConn, 64-60, in the Battle 4 Atlantis in the Bahamas

2018: No. 11 Michigan State beat No. 17 UCLA, 87-67, in the Las Vegas Invitational in Las Vegas, Nev.

2017: No. 4 Michigan State beat DePaul, 73-51, in the PK80 Invitational in Portland, Ore.

2016: Michigan State lost to Baylor, 73-58, in the Battle 4 Atlantis in the Bahamas

2015: No. 3 Michigan State beat Boston College, 99-68, in the Wooden Legacy in Fullerton, Calif.

2014: No. 20 Michigan State beat Rider, 77-45, in the Orlando Classic in Lake Buena Vista, Fla.

2013: No. 5 Michigan State lost to Maryland, 80-62, in the Old Spice Classic in Lake Buena Vista, Fla.

2002: No. 9 Michigan State beat Montana, 80-60, in the Great Alaska Shootout in Anchorage, Alaska

1999: No. 3 Michigan State beat Providence, 82-58, in the Puerto Rico Shootout in San Juan, Puerto Rico
