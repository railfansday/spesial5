Sarina Wiegman has recalled Chelsea forward Aggie Beever-Jones and handed two players their first England call-ups ahead of the Lionesses’ World Cup play-off matches against Ukraine.

Beever-Jones, 23, was left out of England’s previous camp in October, when the Lionesses overcame Greece, but she has earned her place back in a 25-player squad named on Tuesday morning.

Injuries to regulars Alex Greenwood and Lauren James have forced them out of the team while forward Isobel Goodwin and goalkeeper Katie Cox have both been handed maiden senior call-ups.

On London City Lionesses star Goodwin, Wiegman said: “I think she has done well. We always know she’s a huge talent, high potential. She showed that over a couple of games. So we want to see her in our environment.

“She has some different qualities. She’s really fast too. She has length, she can head and she can score goals.”

Grace Fisk, Freya Godfrey, Erica Meg Parkinson and Lexi Potter all made their debuts in October’s 8-1 two-legged round one rout over Greece and have kept their places in the squad this time.

London City Lionesses left-back Poppy Pattinson, who made her debut against Ukraine in March, has been recalled.

Arsenal forward Chloe Kelly continues to miss out and there is no place for Lucia Kendall while Manchester United’s Ella Toone keeps her place in the squad after she originally missed out on being named in October’s internationals.

Speaking on Toone, Wiegman said: “She responded really well, working really hard.

“She came in our camp and was ready also straight away. Going back to her club, she’s done well too and that’s what has been fed back to me.”

Wiegman’s side are just one step away from securing their place at next summer’s World Cup in Brazil, needing to beat Ukraine in round two of the play-offs.

England will travel to Murcia on Tuesday 1 December for the away fixture before they host the second leg at Wembley Stadium four days later.

The upcoming fixtures will be England’s first since head coach Wiegman signed a contract extension until the conclusion of Euro 2029.
