"Abducting your own lover no longer gives you opinion of yourself" declare the notes for Crusader Kings 3's free Florin update, released alongside Silk and Silver

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Funky feudal family frolic Crusader Kings 3 got its second major expansion of the year this week. It's called Silk and Silver (read my impressions), is all about merchant republics, and shipped alongside a beefy free update to the base game which - while largely focused on overhauling economics - also has some funny patch notes and features worth being aware of.

The main features of the free 'Florin' update are outlined by Paradox in the video above, and unless you're really into the nitty gritty details of realm and wealth management, there's one bit of it which stands out. The option to play as a landless adventurer has finally made its way into the base game, as Paradox promised earlier this year.

This means you can kick off a playthrough as a lowborn leader of a travelling band or a wandering noble with claims on realms ruled by others. Originally locked behind the Roads to Power DLC, the option to start off this way or end up living if you end up deposed from your throne of choice is a great addition to the story arc of any CK3 run. No longer are you glued to the throne from day one to the day you quit for good - power's a privilege which can be earned and revoked depending on what you get up to. Though, it's worth noting that only three ways of operating as a landless adventurer are being made free to access with this update. You can be an aimless wanderers, sword-toting mercenaries, or scholars. If you want your band to earn their living as explorers, free booters, or legitimists, you'll still need Roads to Power.
