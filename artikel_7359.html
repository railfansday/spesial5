Mozilla: Staff Software Engineer, OS Integration

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Mozilla Corporation is wholly owned by the non-profit 501(c) Mozilla Foundation. This means we aren’t beholden to any shareholders — only to our mission. Along with thousands of volunteer contributors and collaborators all over the world, Mozillians design, build and distribute open-source software that enables people to enjoy the internet on their terms.

The OS Integration team ensures Firefox works well with the host operating system and environment. We’re a distributed team with expertise in Windows, macOS, and Linux-based operating systems. Our responsibilities include developing fixes and improvements for Firefox related to Windows, macOS, and Linux environments. This might include integration with a new OS feature or fixing an OS-specific user interface bug. Other responsibilities include native crash reporting, OS abstraction layers, sandboxing, hardening, installer, updater, device APIs, and enterprise DLP support.

As a member of the OS Integration team, you’ll develop fixes, improvements, and new features for Firefox. The role will lean on your Windows expertise, but projects may have a cross-platform component. This role will include development of features for Firefox in enterprise environments especially implementation of Data Loss Prevention (DLP) features. As a Staff Software Engineer, you’re a clear communicator and expert in your domain, ready to work on multi-month projects. You’ll contribute to the team through code review, mentoring, and discussion.

Mozilla exists to build the Internet as a public resource accessible to all because we believe that open and free is better than closed and controlled. When you work at Mozilla, you give yourself a chance to make a difference in the lives of Web users everywhere. And you give us a chance to make a difference in your life every single day. Join us to work on the Web as the platform and help create more opportunity and innovation for everyone online.
