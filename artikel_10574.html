Indie game will pay $5,000 towards your wedding or divorce if you finish it fastest

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

An upcoming Steam game is offering up to $5,000 towards a wedding or divorce for the player who can remove 100,000 padlocks from a bridge in Paris faster than anyone else.

Love Locks is a cleanup sim from Collision Games that is based on the tradition of couples fastening padlocks to a bridge and throwing the keys into the river. Your job is to undo all that work, starting with a magnet to fish those discarded keys out of the Seine River.

Every lock you remove adds to your scrap pile, which unlocks better tools as it grows. You can start by hunting down the right key for an individual lock, then work your way up to bolt cutters, blowtorches, and lasers that can clear larger sections of the bridge.

And there’s an extra incentive to clear the bridge quickly, as the devs are willing to give the person who completes the game fastest up to $5,000 towards their wedding…or divorce.

The contest opens when the game launches on December 3 and runs through December 10. You’ll need to register and clear all 100,000 locks on a fresh save before the deadline.

You can try as many times as you like during the week, with your fastest valid run counting. The winner is decided by verified in-game time, so someone who finishes later in the week can still take the prize.

Collision Games plans to announce the winner on December 17 once the results have been checked.

The $5,000 is the maximum the studio will put towards expenses, and the winner will have two years to submit invoices for their wedding or divorce. If they agree, their name will also be displayed on the Eiffel Tower flag in-game.

Of course, this is an optional challenge. If you just want to see how long it takes to clear a bridge, Love Locks also lets you play at your own pace or invite friends to keep you company.
