Hidden Valley Ranch will pay for your wedding if you can prove your love for ranch

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Hidden Valley Ranch is giving one lucky couple the chance to get married for free, with an all-expenses-paid wedding that brings together football, Valentine’s Day, and plenty of ranch dressing.

The company announced its “A Match Made in Hidden Valley” contest on November 10, offering engaged couples across the US the opportunity to win a wedding in Los Angeles for themselves and 50 guests.

The giveaway comes as Valentine’s Day and the Super Bowl fall on the same day in 2027, prompting Hidden Valley Ranch to combine the two occasions into one celebration.

Couples interested in winning the wedding can enter the contest between November 19 and December 4, 2026, through the official Hidden Valley Wedding website.

To qualify, eligible engaged couples must complete an entry form, answer five short essay questions, and submit a video lasting no longer than 90 seconds through Google Drive or an unlisted YouTube link.

Applicants are encouraged to share their relationship stories and explain why they want to celebrate Valentine’s Day and the Super Bowl together. Hidden Valley Ranch is also looking for couples who can demonstrate how the brand has played a role in their relationship or game-day traditions.

The winning couple will be notified on or around December 14, giving them approximately two months to prepare for their February 14, 2027 wedding.

The prize includes a wedding celebration in Los Angeles for 50 guests, with travel, accommodations, and wedding attire provided for the couple. Hidden Valley Ranch will also cover the venue, catering, drinks, and event arrangements.

The ceremony will take place before kickoff, followed by a traditional wedding reception and a selection of game-day foods paired with Hidden Valley Ranch.

“You shouldn’t have to choose between romance and game day,” said Daniel Schear, Marketing Director at Hidden Valley Ranch. “We created this contest to celebrate love without compromise, bringing together two classic American obsessions—football and ranch—for an unforgettable wedding provided by Hidden Valley Ranch.”
