The 10 Best Aldi Finds of November Include the Cutest Stocking Stuffer for Little Shoppers

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

From flavor mash-up snacks and holiday decor to a very special Barbie doll, these November Aldi Finds deserve a spot in your shopping cart.

November is one of my favorite months to go grocery shopping. First, no one can deny that it is peak soup season, so I love stocking up on ingredients for creamy butternut squash soup or a classic lentil stew. I never seem to mind that the sun sets earlier when I have a truly cozy lineup of fall dinner recipes.

And of course, Thanksgiving preparations take up a bulk of the month as well. In my family, I am tasked with two things: bread and Thanksgiving desserts. This year I am going to try my hand at making a sourdough pecan pie! Aldi is definitely the first place I go for my Thanksgiving shopping, because it’s guaranteed to have all my ingredients in stock. And, of course, there’s no shortage of seasonal goodies to look forward to.

Every month, Aldi drops a slew of new items and brings back some customer favorites. Since the grocery store is famous for its motto of “Here today, gone tomorrow,” I’ve turned my passion into an inside scoop so you can stock up while things last. Here are the most exciting November Aldi Finds that will make the holiday season even better.

Nothing says “welcome to the holidays” quite like a big wreath on the front door. This wreath has tiny pine cones and lights, which makes it even more magical. I love that it’s available with either a red or green velvet ribbon, so you can choose which suits your personal aesthetic best.

Of course, if you prefer savory treats over sweet ones, then you might really enjoy these turkey stuffing-flavored potato chips. All the classic Thanksgiving flavors you love, but in chip form. The wavy chip makes it perfect for dipping—say, in some homemade cranberry sauce?

This board is such a great idea! The snowman outline gives you plenty of opportunities to arrange your favorite meats and cheeses in a cute way. The snap-top lid ensures everything stays in place during transit from your house to wherever the party is. It’s so festive, I might just be giving these as gifts this year.

I’m a sucker for any kind of fancy bread, and this loaf in particular caught my eye. Imagine the pillowy sweet softness of a classic brioche, but with added maple nut flavor. It sounds like a breakfast dream come true! I’ll be using this for Thanksgiving leftover sandwiches and French toast.

If there’s one thing Aldi does well, it’s providing exceptionally budget-friendly dupes of much fancier brands. Like this adorable cherry red braiser pan, which has a leaf design on the lid. It might cost upward of $300 from some brands, but Aldi gives it to you at a mere fraction of the price.

OK, this is so cool! A special Aldi-branded Barbie doll is a perfect stocking stuffer for any of the grocery-obsessed kids (or adults!) in your life. This Barbie comes with her own shopping cart and reusable tote, so she is ready to stock up on all her favorite Aldi finds.

I have several holiday runners from Aldi, and they are some of my favorite pieces of decor. This beautiful floral version has big poinsettia flowers and holly leaves, so it’s sure to make a statement on your table for the holidays. It’s machine washable, too, which is very convenient—you don’t need to stress if someone spills red wine on it (speaking from experience!)

This has been my absolute favorite seasonal Aldi find over the last few years. Adding a few of these fancy flavored marshmallows to a simple cup of hot cocoa transforms it into an irresistible treat. I am partial to the peppermint flavor, but the salted caramel marshmallows are also incredible. And again, they are perfect for gifting!

Which Aldi Find are you most excited for this month? Let us know in the comments below!
