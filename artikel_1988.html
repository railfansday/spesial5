'I have to put my hands up....' - Ex-chief scout wowed by Arsenal star Ben White, makes bold claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Arsenal defender Ben White has been impressive on his return to the England team and should have nearly 100 caps for his country rather than his meagre haul.

That is according to experienced former Everton, Tottenham and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 29-year-old had not appeared in an England shirt for four years when Thomas Tuchel brought him back into the fold in March.

White's performances for Arsenal over the years have been more than good enough to warrant call-ups, even though he is the second choice right-back behind Jurrien Timber, but he refused to return to Gareth Southgate's squad after a reported fall-out with assistant manager Steve Holland during the 2022 World Cup.

Southgate's departure left the door open for the Englishman to return and he has done, though the manner of his previous refusal has seen him booed by some fans at Wembley Stadium.

But those same fans may well have been cheering on Sunday, with White's goal against Spain in Madrid ultimately proving the winner in a famous 3-2 victory.

Tuchel has recently called on England fans to accept White back onto the international stage.

The defender, too, has insisted that fans do not understand the full story behind his previous withdrawal.

It would certainly appear a positive if White were to be a regular feature, with the defender finishing brilliantly on Sunday.

"The thing with Ben White is that he's 29 years old and he's only got eight caps," King told Football Insider. "He should have 80.

"Against Spain, he contained a very good player. He showed that he could push on. He got forward. His goal was a classic. I mean, what a great finish.

"It was a great finish and a great run. The combination play up to that, and the way he came into the box on the right-hand side and a great finish.

"The finish of a centre-forward. He had a very good performance. I have to put my hands up and say that that's as well as I've seen England play.

"They've beaten teams before, and they've played all right. But here, they were better than Spain all over the pitch."

On the balance of Sunday's display, it appears that White is not going anywhere anytime soon.

"I don't want to make it bigger than it is. I don't think so. I don't hope so. He seems to be always committed and always dedicated and excited to come to camp and get the call-up," Tuchel said last week when asked if the defender could withdraw again.

"He had too many injuries. No, I don't hope so. He is a high-quality player and I honestly don't even know what happened and what were the deep reasons and the situation that he left camp.

"Since I am in charge, there is no reason to doubt his commitment, his passion. I don't want to think in these extreme terms at the moment. He overcame the boos the last time.

"I didn't hear them this time. He can play on a high level. He proves it week in, week out for Arsenal. That's why I don't want to think about it at the moment."
