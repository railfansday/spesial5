What channel is St Mirren v Rangers? TV and live stream plus team news, referee and VAR

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Rangers will look to apply pressure on Celtic at the top of the Scottish Premiership when they visit St Mirren on Saturday night.

Derek McInnes’ side are currently four points behind their Old Firm rivals but have a game in hand due to their involvement in last weekend’s Premier Sports Cup semi-finals, where a 1-0 win over Aberdeen booked a Hampden final against Hibs next month.

Celtic missed the chance to move seven points clear with a 2-0 defeat at Falkirk and Rangers will now look to capitalise on the opportunity by closing the gap at the top of the table before the leaders host Kilmarnock on Sunday. Rangers are unbeaten in their last 11 matches but have but have drawn their past two away games in the league, both 2-2 stalemates at Dundee and Hearts.

St Mirren picked up three wins and a draw in their opening four Premiership matches but were unable to sustain their strong start with only one win in their following eight games. The Buddies are eighth in the table on 14 points.

The recent head-to-head record between the teams is weighted heavily in favour of Rangers, who have won 11, drawn four and lost just one of their past 16 league visits to Paisley, a 2-1 defeat in December 2024 under Philippe Clement.

St Mirren have also lost four successive meetings with Rangers in all competitions, including twice already this season - 5-1 in the League Cup and 1-0 in the league.

The Scottish Premiership match between St Mirren and Rangers takes place at Marbill Coaches Stadium, Paisley, on Saturday, November 7, 2026. Kick-off is 8pm.

The match will be broadcast live on Sky Sports Football with coverage starting at 7.30pm. Highlights will be shown on Sportscene on BBC One Scotland at 10.55pm.

St Mirren’s Ryan Carr is suspended and fellow midfielder Paul Nsio cannot play against his parent club. Ismeal Kabia (calf), Nikolas Agrafiotis (calf), Keanu Baccus (Achilles), Malik Dijksteel (hamstring) and Jonah Ayunga (knee) remain out.

Bedredine Bouanani (illness) and Emmanuel Fernandez (ankle) are expected to shake off fitness concerns. Ross McCrorie, Cammy Devlin, Lawrence Shankland (all hamstring), Daisuke Yokota (groin) and Youssef Chermiti (knee) remain out.

The match will be refereed by Nick Walsh, with Greg Aitken overseeing VAR duties. The full Scottish FA officiating team also features assistant referees Frank Connor and Daniel McFarlane, fourth official Gary Hanvidge, and assistant VAR Peter Stuart.
