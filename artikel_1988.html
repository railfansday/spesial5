“Pablo Price Drop” West Ham Stunned By Striker’s Dramatic Drop In Value

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The true extent of West Ham striker Pablo Felipe’s depreciation in value has been laid bare following a transfer offer from a Portuguese club. The Brazilian striker moved to the London Stadium from Gil Vicente for over €20 million less than a year ago and has struggled to make his mark in East London.

As we covered extensively at the time, the selling club received only €8 million of the transfer fee, but the cost to the Hammers was considerably higher. It had been hoped that Pablo would be able to hit some goalscoring form in the Championship, but he has scored only twice in all competitions this campaign and has not featured in Scott Parker’s matchday squads.

His omission has been noted back in Portugal, with loan approaches made for the player. However, it is the valuation which has really taken the club aback.

Speaking to a transfer insider yesterday, Claret & Hugh were told: “There’s been practically no interest in Pablo. A couple of loan offers, one of which had an option to buy for €3m.”

Pablo price drop: West Ham have been left stunned by the market valuation of Pablo

That’s right, the highest offer West Ham have received for the 22-year-old includes a purchase option of just €3 million, suggesting his value has dropped by a whopping €17 million or more in under a year.

Of course, poor old Pablo was never worth that much to begin with. Gil Vicente paid his former Portuguese club Famalicão just €240,000 to sign the striker permanently six months before he was sold to the Hammers.

With West Ham looking to have something of a striker clearout in January, the scale of the potential losses from the big-money transfers of both Pablo and Taty Castellanos is beginning to be felt by the bean counters at Hammers HQ.
