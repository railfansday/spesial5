'The word is out...' Kieran McKenna to Man United update shared by ex-manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Kieran McKenna hopes to manoeuvre himself into position the Manchester United manager's job.

That is according to former manager and experienced Tottenham, Everton and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The Red Devils intend to dismiss Michael Carrick in the coming hours, despite a slight uptick in performances before the international break.

Man United came from behind to beat Roma last Tuesday and fought back to a dramatic 4-4 draw with Villa on Sunday, but Carrick's team remain without a Premier League win since August.

The Englishman was backed following a successful five months in charge as an interim manager last season, but has overseen the worst start to a Premier League season ever at Old Trafford.

United States international manager Mauricio Pochettino has emerged as a leading contender to replace him, but McKenna is available and has experience of working at Man United.

McKenna joined Man United as the manager of their Under-18 side in 2016, having performed the same role at Tottenham.

In 2018, he was promoted alongside Carrick to join Jose Mourinho's backroom staff, and he would stay with Ole Gunnar Solskjaer until he was sacked.

In 2021, McKenna left for his first manager's role at Ipswich Town, who he guided from League One to promotion to the Premier League on two different occasions before stepping down in the summer of 2026.

"It'll be interesting now, because Thomas Frank's available. I don't know whether [Eddie] Howe will think it's not a big enough club for him," King told Football Insider, when asked about the soon-to-be vacant Crystal Palace job.

"McKenna could be a good option, but the word is out that he's been looking to get back to United.

"He served under a lot of managers there. Knows the club. Was pretty successful at Ipswich, and it looks as though he could be somebody to be brought into a club like that.

"But at the moment, I think Manchester United are in turmoil as it is. Look at the game on Sunday."

Should Carrick have a say in his replacement, he may well provide McKenna with his support.

"I must say Kieran has been a big help. He's experienced in coaching for a long time now," the former midfielder said in 2019.

"He's helped me out. As you expect, for me, it's quite new so I am learning on the job a little bit but he's been fantastic. He's a terrific coach and he's passed on a lot of things to me.

"Even though he's younger than me, he's a lot more experienced on this side of things."
