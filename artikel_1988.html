Arsenal and Manchester City have are both tracking Newcastle United right-back Tino Livramento at the moment, sources have told Football Insider.

The pair are both long-term admirers of the 23-year-old, and they have continued to "monitor" him this season.

Livramento's long-term future at Newcastle is beginning to come under scrutiny, with his current contract due to expire in the summer of 2028.

Sources say that the Magpies will reluctantly look to sell the England international next summer, if they cannot progress towards a new deal between now and then.

Arsenal and City have both made attempts to sign Livramento in recent years, but have seen their efforts blocked by Newcastle.

However, with the defender out injured once again at the moment, the Tynesiders are approaching decision time in terms of his future.

Livramento's promising career has been regularly hampered by injuries already, and it has been no different for him of late.

The former Southampton ace was only fit enough to start 14 Premier League games last term, and his involvement has been hugely limited so far in 2026-27.

The youngster has only played 33 minutes of Premier League action, and hasn't even featured in a matchday squad since 14 September.

However, Football Insider revealed on Wednesday that Newcastle "would love" to tie Livramento down to a new deal, as they have no doubt about his quality.

The Chelsea academy graduate is widely regarded as one of the best young full-backs in the country when he is fit and firing, and he has regularly been subject to interest from some of the Premier League's elite clubs.

Livramento's stock has dropped at all amid his latest injury lay-off, and Newcastle are only prepared to sell if they absolutely have to.

Football Insider revealed last week that Newcastle sporting director Ross Wilson is fighting for his job at St James' Park right now.

The recruitment boss oversaw a huge overhaul of the first-team squad in the summer, with several key players replaced by younger, up-and-coming stars.

Sources say that the Magpies' hierarchy are less than impressed by his summer signings, with a few not even featuring in Matthias Jaissle's plans at the moment.

It looks set to be a challenging season for the North East outfit, with Wilson already walking the tightrope behind the scenes.
