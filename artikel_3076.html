Intercorp Financial Services Inc. (IFS +1.38%) reported third-quarter results characterized by growth in higher-yielding loan segments and the continued scaling of its digital payments ecosystem. Management maintained its full-year profitability guidance while taking a proactive approach to risk management through extraordinary provisioning for climate-related contingencies. The company reported a recovery in net interest margins, driven by a shift toward consumer and small business lending and a reduction in funding costs. Operations were supported by a resilient macroeconomic environment in Peru, where private investment and domestic demand reached multiyear highs. The company continues to prioritize its digital transformation, reporting high penetration across its banking, insurance, and wealth management platforms.
