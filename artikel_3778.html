A total of 61 GTA 6 cheats are available for PS5 and Xbox Series X, but you won't be able to use any of them right from the beginning of your adventures in Vice City. You need to fully complete Jason and Lucia's main story for cheat codes in GTA 6 to even activate, and from there you have to complete a series of challenges to unlock the codes themselves. It's a lot of work to go through in GTA 6, but trust me, it's worth it in the end.

As expected, once you have activated a cheat in GTA 6 all Trophies or Achievements are disabled for the duration of the play session – the ability to save the game will be disabled too. But with all of that out of the way, here are the GTA 6 cheats we have found so far.This guide is in-progress and will be updated as more GTA 6 cheat codes are unlocked.

GTA 6 cheats are only accessible after you complete all of the main missions. Once you've wrapped up Jason and Lucia's story, open the main menu with the touchpad or menu button and then press R1/RB to navigate to the Progress tab. In there you'll find a new option for Cheats in GTA 6 that wasn't there before.

GTA 6 cheats will not work until you have completed Jason and Lucia's story, regardless of whether you know the code already.

Using Grand Theft Auto 6 cheats is easy enough. While you're in free-roam, all you have to do is press Up on the D-pad to pull out your phone. From that main phone screen, you then need to hold L1/LB and R1/RB together at the same time, before inputting the relevant GTA 6 cheat code with the D-pad to activate it.
