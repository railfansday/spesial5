KJ Apa Returns to Romance in a New Love-Triangle Movie Tomorrow

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Over the last decade, KJ Apa has become one of the brightest rising stars in Hollywood. The Jimmy star is having quite the time this year; after bringing icon Jimmy Stewart to life, Apa is set to take on the romantic comedy genre once more with the new film Falling, directed by James Krisel and Colin Krisel. Falling stars Apa, Isabel May, Archie Renaux, Emma Laird, Alex Jarrett, and Sophie Simnett. Ahead of the film's home release on Digital and Demand, Collider is thrilled to bring you an exclusive first look at the film that features Jack (Apa) talking with his friends about a new girl that he's falling for.

The new film is a fun return to the rom-com genre as well as a new entry in Apa's romantic lead filmography. Apa is best known for playing the charming and lovable Archie Andrews on the hit show Riverdale, and fans getting to experience Apa in a new kind of romantic comedy is going to be exciting. Besides, who doesn't love a good old-fashioned love triangle? Falling is described as follows: "Jack is a quick-witted but commitment-phobic guy who finds himself caught in a love triangle when he falls for Jillian, unaware that his best friend Johnny also harbors feelings for her."

In our exclusive sneak peek from Falling, we get to see Jack talking with his friends about a girl that he has feelings for. His friends are giving him some sage advice, like, do not stalk the girl you're crushing on by standing outside of her home. But the question is: Will Jack listen to his friends as they try to help him get the girl, or is this love triangle going to be harder to navigate? That's the joy of a romantic comedy!

Four love stories, four completely different ideas of what it means to be somebody's person. This is the one that matches how you actually fall, and what it would ask of you.

You do not fall for strangers. You fall for the person you have already been talking to for years, which is the slowest and by some distance the most durable way anybody has found to do this.

You want a partner, not an audience. Somebody who wants their own life as badly as you want yours, and who would never ask you to be smaller to make the relationship easier.

The intensity was never the problem. The secrecy is. You are drawn to a love that lives entirely in private, and to the particular ache of wanting somebody you are not supposed to want.

You do not do this cautiously. When it happens for you it is immediate and total and it rearranges everything, and you would not accept a smaller version of it.

Falling comes right on the heels of the theatrical release of Jimmy. In Jimmy, Apa plays the beloved It's a Wonderful Life actor Jimmy Stewart, mastering Stewart's unique accent and telling an emotional story about his life off-camera. In Falling, fans can hear Apa use his natural New Zealand accent instead of bringing a good ol' American boy to life, as he did for years on Riverdale.

You can watch Apa's return to romance when Falling hits VOD tomorrow, November 10, when the film will be available for purchase or rent on platforms like Prime Video, Apple TV, and more. Jimmy is now playing in theaters. Stay tuned at Collider for more.
