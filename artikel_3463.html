Miley Cyrus & Maxx Morando Arrive at Rehearsal Dinner With Mom Tish & Sister Noah

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Miley Cyrus and fiancé Maxx Morando were spotted arriving at their wedding rehearsal dinner hand in hand ... and we have the pics.

In photos obtained by TMZ ... Miley and Maxx showed up at a restaurant near their SoCal wedding venue Friday night, with the "Flowers" singer sporting black pants and a jacket while Maxx looked sharp in a suit.

Joining them were Miley's mom Tish Cyrus -- who donned a cheetah print jacket -- and her sister Noah Cyrus -- who wore a black floral dress.

A source tells TMZ ... Miley and her crew wrapped up the wedding rehearsal around 6 PM before arriving at the restaurant just 15 minutes later.

Everyone ended up leaving the dinner at 8:45 PM ... with Miley departing last.

We reported earlier ... Miley and Maxx's rehearsal dinner was full of delicious menu offerings, including filet mignon and Maine lobster ravioli.

Now, it's only a matter of time before Miley and Maxx say their "I do's" and officially become husband and wife!
