3 Elite Netflix Series to Binge This Week (Nov 9-13)

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

True crime is back at the American streaming summit on Netflix. For the past couple of weeks, the return of Adam Brody and Kristen Bell's rabbi and sex podcaster in Nobody Wants This Season 3 has dominated the streaming charts, with Erin Foster​​​​​​'s return to form currently ranking in second place. However, the indulgent pull of true crime docuseries on Netflix has hit once again, as the newest must-watch on the streamer, Taken: Natalee Holloway, places first in the U.S. top 10. But what else should you be streaming at the moment? Here's a look at three shows you should binge-watch on Netflix this weekend.

For more recommendations, check out our list of the best shows and movies on Netflix.

Disclaimer: These titles are available on US Netflix.

Of all the shows in this November's streaming rotation, the most exciting arrival on Netflix is Power, Starz's flagship crime series that spawned four spin-offs (Power Book II: Ghost, Book III: Raising Kanan, and Book IV: Force). The series follows Omari Hardwick's ambitious James "Ghost" St. Patrick, who lives a double life as a wealthy nightclub owner and a drug kingpin in high-flying New York.

For six years, six seasons, and 63 episodes, Power remained one of the most consistent and grittiest shows of the 2010s. A suspenseful and unapologetically violent series packed with iconic performances, Power's addition to the Netflix catalog cannot be overstated. With two more spin-offs on the way, Power: Origins and Power: Legacy, there's been no better time to finally indulge in this crime drama gem.

Are there many more hotly anticipated TV events this November than the return of Yellowjackets? A recent trailer for the final installment showcased the epic nature of what's to come on November 20, making a binge-watch on Netflix right now a must, as fans remind themselves of what came before, and the uninitiated catch up just in time.

This dual-timeline tale of a high school girls' soccer team stranded in the remote Canadian wilderness after a plane crash and the trauma they face 25 years later has delighted and dazzled audiences for the past five years, earning a remarkable 10 Primetime Emmy nominations along the way. Melanie Lynskey, Christina Ricci, Tawny Cypress, and Hilary Swank all star, with the upcoming fourth season featuring guest stars such as June Squibb, Elijah Wood, Molly Ringwald, Devon Sawa, Sam Braun, and Dulé Hill.

With 2026 coming to an end in the next few weeks, it's time to revisit some of the best shows of the year so far. On Netflix, this includes the genius sci-fi mystery series The Boroughs, executive produced by Stranger Things' creators, the Duffer Brothers. Set in an unassuming retirement community, the series follows a ragtag band of unlikely heroes who must save the world from a strange threat.

Created by Jeffrey Addiss and Will Matthews, The Boroughs hugely impressed both critics and audiences with its heartfelt story and excellent performances. These performances included plenty of stars, such as Alfred Molina, Bill Pullman, Geena Davis, Alfre Woodard, Clarke Peters, and more. Sadly, due to high production costs and viewing figures that, although impressive, didn't reach the required heights, The Boroughs was cancelled after just one season.
