HBO’s ‘Harry Potter’ Officially Changes a Classic Book Moment in First Full Scene Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

For millions of Harry Potter fans, that iconic at Hogwarts is one of those moments that's practically impossible to forget. It's a sensory overload with the singing Sorting Hat, the Great Hall, hundreds of floating candles, and nothing short of a mountain of food per person. Chris Columbus brought the scene to life in 2001's Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone, and it's remained one of the most recognizable moments in the franchise ever since. Now, with HBO preparing to take audiences back to Hogwarts this Christmas, we're finally getting a proper look at how the new version compares.

HBO has officially released the first full scene from Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone, and despite the fact it's recognizable, it's also distinctly different from what's come before. The approximately 90-second clip shows Harry arriving in the Great Hall alongside Ron, with Albus Dumbledore preparing to begin the feast. One of the first differences comes when the students begin chanting Dumbledore's name, something that doesn't happen in J.K. Rowling's original novel. Harry then asks Ron whether Dumbledore is a little mad, whereas in the book he directs that question to Ron's older brother Percy.

However, the biggest addition comes when Dumbledore and Professor McGonagall share a private conversation about Harry, with McGonagall commenting on his resemblance to his father, James Potter. The exchange doesn't appear in the novel, which tells the banquet scene from Harry's perspective. It's the first look at how the series will start showing us different perspectives than that of the titular character.

The series is led by Dominic McLaughlin as Harry Potter, Arabella Stanton as Hermione Granger and Alastair Stout as Ron Weasley, alongside John Lithgow (Conclave) as Albus Dumbledore, Janet McTeer (Ozark) as Minerva McGonagall, Paapa Essiedu (I May Destroy You) as Severus Snape, Nick Frost (Shaun of the Dead) as Rubeus Hagrid, Lox Pratt (Lord of the Flies) as Draco Malfoy and Warwick Davis (Willow) as Filius Flitwick. Season 2 is already building its own impressive cast too, with Kit Harington (Game of Thrones) playing Gilderoy Lockhart, Lenny Rush (Doctor Who) voicing Dobby, Billy Barratt (Invasion) as Tom Riddle and Rafe Spall (Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom) as Arthur Weasley.

The series is written and executive produced by Francesca Gardiner (Succession), with Mark Mylod (The Last of Us) directing multiple episodes. Rowling also serves as an executive producer alongside David Heyman, who produced the original film franchise.
