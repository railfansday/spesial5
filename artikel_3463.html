The director of Focker-in-Law has addressed the comedy's ending and whether it teases another sequel for the long-standing franchise.

The latest sequel to the 2000 movie Meet the Parents introduces Skyler Gisondo as Greg and Pam's son and Ariana Grande as his girlfriend, Olivia Jones. After the Focker and Byrnes family's shenanigans cause chaos, the couple gets engaged, and Olivia seemingly teases a Focker-in-Law sequel. The movie's ending memorably features Olivia declaring that Greg and Pam need to meet her parents. Now, the director of the 2026 comedy has addressed whether that line was truly part of a major plan or just a throwaway gag.

On November 25, Variety published a transcript of its interview with Focker-in-Law's director and co-writer, John Hamburg. During that discussion, the creative mind behind the comedy was asked whether the film was designed to set up a forthcoming sequel. In response, he said another Fockers movie could happen if it feels "organic" and even revealed he has ideas about who could play Olivia's parents if it does. Check out Hamburg's comments below:

Variety: The movie ends with a teaser. Do you plan to make another "Focker" movie?

John Hamburg: Look, I have no one to blame but myself. I wrote that line. Maybe there’s more to mine, but only if it’s organic and feels like there’s a reason for it to exist. Talk to me after Thanksgiving weekend.

Variety: Do you have actors in mind to play the parents of Ariana’s character?

John Hamburg: I have some people in mind, but there’s no way I could talk about this right now.

Based on early indications, there are a lot of reasons to think that producing another movie in the highly successful film series could make sense for everyone involved. For example, even though Focker-in-Law's reviews are underwhelming, it currently holds an impressive 87% Popcornmeter score on Rotten Tomatoes based on more than 250 user ratings.

In addition, it seems like the franchise's main cast members still enjoy returning to their characters long after Meet the Parents became a hit that is often regarded as one of Ben Stiller's best movies. After all, Stiller, Robert De Niro, Blythe Danner, Teri Polo, and Owen Wilson have all appeared in four films in the series over a span of roughly 26 years.

That said, any potential plans to make a sequel to Focker-in-Law could fall apart if the movie fails at the box office, and it has a massive stumbling block to overcome in that regard. According to The Hollywood Reporter, it was produced on an estimated $90 million budget. Given the all-star cast that would be involved in any sequel, especially if stars were cast as Olivia's parents, a fifth film would also almost certainly cost a hefty sum to make.

While it is still unclear if there will be another movie in the Fockers film franchise, viewers who want to see more will no doubt be happy to know that the co-writer and director of Focker-in-Law seems open to the prospect. Whether the movie happens or not, it is certain that he will eventually have to reveal who he had in mind to portray Olivia's parents.
