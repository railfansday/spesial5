Anna Paulina Luna Says Dave Chappelle's Jokes About Charlie Kirk Aren't Funny

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Anna Paulina Luna says Dave Chappelle's jokes about Charlie Kirk after he was murdered on a college campus are in bad taste and just straight up not funny ... even though she admits she usually likes his stand-up.

Jacob caught up with the Republican congresswoman from Florida -- who recently won her third term in the House of Representatives -- on Tuesday ... and he asked her about Chappelle joking that he feels better since Kirk was killed and making a comment about Kirk "looking up" at people from the afterlife.
