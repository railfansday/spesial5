Ridley Scott’s 10/10 Sci-Fi Masterpiece Officially Won’t Return in 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

2026 will be remembered as a bumpy year for the great Ridley Scott, who finally returned to sci-fi after nearly 10 years away from the genre. Scott tapped widely known stars, including Josh Brolin, Jacob Elordi, and even Margaret Qualley, to feature in The Dog Stars, but the film eked out only $39 million at the box office. The budget for the film was never publicly disclosed, but it was reported to be anywhere between $75 million and $100 million, which makes it a total box office disaster. Scott isn’t taking some time to reevaluate after the woeful performance of The Dog Stars, either – he’s returning to the screen next November for a Treasure Island reboot starring Hugh Jackman as the titular pirate. The movie is expected to begin shooting early next year, which will leave a tight window to hit its release date.

In this later stage of his career, Ridley Scott isn’t directing as many movies as he once was, but he remains involved in projects across the board through his Scott Free production banner. For example, it’s been almost 10 years since Scott directed his last Alien project, Alien: Covenant, but he produced the last few franchise installments, such as Alien: Romulus and Alien: Earth. The latter project, written and created by Fargo scribe Noah Hawley, premiered back in 2024 and was a massive hit with critics and audiences, prompting FX to bring the show back for Season 2. Production on Alien: Earth Season 2 kicked off earlier this year, and while there was an initial wave of optimism that the show would return before the end of 2026, it can now be safely assumed that it won’t be released until 2027.

Most of the stars whose characters survived the events of Season 1 are expected back in Season 2, but there are plenty of exciting new additions to the cast as well. Both Peter Dinklage and Jerome Flynn are among the new stars joining the Alien: Earth Season 2 cast, but this won’t be their first time working together. The duo spent years playing the pair of Tyrion Lannister and Bronn in Game of Thrones, the hit HBO fantasy series that was on the air from 2010 to 2019.
