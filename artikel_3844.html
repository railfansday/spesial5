We got GTA 6 before Winds of Winter, and now Rockstar's new game hides two references to George R.R. Martin's Game of Thrones universe in its massive amount of background detail.

One keen-eyed player has spotted that one of Grand Theft Auto 6's protagonists, Jason, has a copy of a familiar looking book on his bedside table. The book is titled The Battle for Zantoroth, and it's penned by T.D.D. Ewell.

The cover design is in the exact same style as Martin's novels: an object in the middle against a single color background, and white, all-caps font for the title and author name. Check it out below.

That's not the only reference, either, with a ticker on the game's news channel reading "man who drank gas to 'become dragon' sadly dies in ER." This is the fate of Aerion Targaryen, the brutish prince who gets Dunk in so much trouble in the first Dunk and Egg novella, adapted into A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms season 1.

Aerion drank wildfire in the hopes of turning into a dragon himself, however, all the stunt ended up doing was causing his painful death. RIP. Dragons wouldn't return to Westeros until much later, when Daenerys Targaryen hatched her three eggs in the first novel (as seen in the Game of Thrones season 1 finale). Perhaps someone in Leonida is working on a bonfire to hatch their own dragons as we speak?

It's been a big year for the world of A Song of Ice and Fire with the release of A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms season 1, House of the Dragon season 3, and the Game of Thrones: The Mad King stage play. Next year brings with it A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms season 2, while House of the Dragon season 4 and a movie based on Aegon's Conquest are also in the works.

As for The Winds of Winter, though, the wait goes on. It has now surpassed the amount of time it took for all the main novels so far to be released, and there's no update on when the sixth book might arrive.
