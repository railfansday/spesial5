Monster Hunter Stories 3: Twisted Reflection sales top one million

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Total sales for Monster Hunter Stories 3: Twisted Reflection have surpassed one million units worldwide, Capcom announced.

Monster Hunter Stories 3: Twisted Reflection launched for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, and PC via Steam on March 13. A demo is also available.
