Dunkin' Is Making 3 Childhood Cereal Favorites A Little More Grown-Up

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

I didn't think my childhood breakfast needed a makeover, but I'm willing to hear them out.

I'm not that convinced Cinnamon Toast Crunch, Reese's Puffs, or Golden Grahams need much improving. All three have been breakfast staples for decades, and based on how long they’ve been on store shelves, it’s clear they've done pretty well for themselves.

But if we're going to mess with the cereals we grew up eating, borrowing a few flavors from Dunkin' isn't the worst idea.

The chain is teaming up with General Mills on three limited-edition varieties arriving this December, each taking its own approach to the coffee-influenced variation.

The new Peanut Butter Mocha flavor sounds like a pretty natural fit, considering chocolate and peanut butter are already involved. Meanwhile, Cinnamon Toast Crunch Cinnamon Caramel Latte adds a little caramel to its familiar cinnamon flavor, while Golden Grahams Vanilla Graham Latte gives those honey-graham squares a vanilla coffee-shop makeover.

And before any parents start worrying about their kids bouncing off the walls after breakfast, there's no actual coffee or caffeine in these boxes. The flavors are inspired by Dunkin's drinks, so unfortunately, you'll still need the real thing if you're looking for a morning pick-me-up.

Brian Gilbert, vice president of retail business development at Inspire Brands, said in a statement that the collaboration allows Dunkin' to “bring the flavors of some fan-favorite Dunkin’ beverages beyond the cup and into the cereal bowl.”

All three are scheduled to arrive in December, although you'll have to visit Walmart specifically for the Golden Grahams version, while the other two will be available at retailers nationwide.

And judging by early reactions online, plenty of people are just as interested as I am in giving them a shot.

"Those Golden Grahams finna slap 👋🏽" one person predicted, while another wrote, "I'm definitely going try all of them 💯". General Mills' previous cereal experiments seem to have earned it some goodwill, too. Its Reese's Puffs PB&J variety got a shoutout from one fan: "The pb&j Reese's cereal is really good. Excited for these!"

And someone else decided to get to the point and address Dunkin' directly, writing, "let me go head and send you my address NOW!!!"

As someone who loves the taste of coffee, I have a feeling these are going to be right up my alley. My morning iced coffee, though? That's never getting replaced.
