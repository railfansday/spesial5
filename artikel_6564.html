Chatpata Potato Chip Tower

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Cookbook author Rachel Gurjar doesn’t usually jump on food trends, but the potato chip tower called to her. “Chips are already great as is, but I knew a version inspired by chana chor—a fiery street snack made from flattened, fried black chickpeas tossed with onions, chiles, lemon, and chaat masala—would kick it up a notch,” she writes in her debut title, East to East. Chatpata is a Hindi word that means salty, sour, spicy, and sweet at the same time, and this chatpata potato chip tower absolutely lives up to its name. While you can use any potato chips you like—or even substitute tortilla chips, pita chips, or fried pita bread—Gurjar recommends thick kettle-cooked chips, as they stay crispy longer.
