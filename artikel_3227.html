Chris Pratt To Star In Paramount’s Feature Take Of Joseph Eckert Novel ‘The Traveler’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Chris Pratt is set to headline Paramount Pictures’ adaptation of The Traveler, based on the novel by Joseph Eckert.

The project is part of the Guardians of the Galaxy star’s first-look three-year producing deal with the Melrose Ave lot through his Indivisible production banner, with the potential to star in select projects.

Zach Lipovsky and Adam Stein are helming the feature off Justin Rhodes’ screenplay. The film will be produced by Adam Rosenberg and Jules Daly.

Additional projects under Pratt’s deal include Miami Heat, written by Brad Copeland (Jerry & Marge Go Large), with Pratt set to star and produce alongside Hutch Parker (Venom: The Last Dance).

Pratt’s movie canon counts more than $15 billion at the global box office. He can be seen this fall in Apple Original Films’ Way of the Warrior Kid, an adaptation of Jocko Willink’s bestselling children’s novel. It begins streaming November 25. He also stars in one of the five $1 billion-plus grossing movies of the year in Universal/Illumination’s Super Mario Galaxy Movie. Watch on Deadline

Pratt also starred in TV’s The Terminal List, which premiered in July 2022. Based on the Jack Carr novel and produced through Indivisible Productions, the series debuted at No. 1 on Prime Video. The series was renewed for a second season and debuted October 21. A prequel series, Dark Wolf, led by Taylor Kitsch, premiered in August 2025.
