Celtic & Southampton must be feeling fresh Stoke City transfer regret

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Stoke City headed into the international break level on points with Birmingham City and Middlesbrough at the top of the Championship table, as Mark Robins will be looking to ensure his side can maintain this form upon their return to action.

Indeed, only Birmingham’s and Middlesbrough’s superior goal difference keeps Stoke off the top of the Championship table right now as things stand, with the Potters enjoying another excellent start to the campaign.

It was around this time last year when Stoke suffered a calamitous dip in form, as Robins must be doing everything he can to avoid a similar fate this time around.

A strong summer window will give the former Coventry City boss the belief that this year is different, and when they do return to action after the international break, he will be looking to one player in particular for a sizeable impact.

This most recent summer transfer window was Robins’ second in charge at the bet365 Stadium, and he will certainly be pleased with the business his side was able to conduct.

Lewis Baker, Steven Nzonzi, Tatsuki Seko, and Sorba Thomas left through the exit door, as the likes of Luke Graham, Ethan Galbraith, Djibril Soumare, George Hirst, and Jack McGlynn headed in the opposite direction.

McGlynn, alongside compatriot Luca Bombino, arrived in the Potteries late in the window, but he wouldn’t quite be the final addition, as Stoke completed a deal worth around £5 million for Northern Ireland international Justin Devenny.

Devenny, 23, began his career in the lower reaches of Scotland, predominantly plying his trade with Airdrieonians before being spotted by Crystal Palace in the summer of 2023.

The versatile midfielder made 60 appearances across all competitions for the Eagles during his two-year stay in South London, helping the club lift the FA Cup, UEFA Europa Conference League, and Community Shield, and before his move to Stoke, had numerous other admirers as well.

According to Alan Nixon via Patreon, the likes of Southampton, Preston North End, and Leicester City joined the race for Devenny back in July, but neither of those teams were willing to stump up the funds to meet Crystal Palace’s asking price.

Middlesbrough, as per Nixon, also joined the hunt for the Northern Irishman the following month, and sounded out Devenny as a serious option as part of their incredible summer window, before Nixon reported on Celtic’s interest late into the window.

The Bhoys were only seemingly keen on a loan deal, as Stoke sensed an opportunity following the departure of Sorba Thomas to Hull City and completed a deal which saw the 23-year-old head to North Staffordshire.

During the international break, Devenny linked back up with the Northern Ireland squad for games against Ukraine and Hungary, joining the likes of Galbraith, Isaac Price, Pierce Charles, and Ruari McConville in the squad.

Starting as the left wing-back in both games, Devenny thrived under former Stoke and Blackburn Rovers boss Michael O’Neill, as the Green and White Army beat Ukraine 1-0 in the first game before earning a 1-1 draw with Hungary in the second, as the 23-year-old took centre stage.

Following a long throw from Trai Hume, the ball was cleared to Devenny on the edge of the area, who took one touch to set himself up before firing a rocket into the top corner, securing his nation a place in the top flight of the UEFA Nations League on a historic night.

Watching on, the likes of Southampton, Celtic, and Middlesbrough, in particular, must be feeling some fresh transfer regret right now, having opted against the purchase of Devenny, as Stoke will surely feel they have found a bargain.

On his Stoke debut against Charlton Athletic in the Championship, Devenny was thrust straight into the starting XI, and just days after his arrival, assumed penalty duties right away, giving his new side the lead before deflecting Djibril Soumare’s effort beyond Arthur Okonkwo to make it 4-0 before half-time.

Since then, the former Palace star has failed to grab a single goal or assist for the Potters, but has consistently impressed, as Robins will be looking to him to have an increased impact on games following his exploits for his country.

If Stoke are to achieve anything special this season, then Devenny will almost certainly be a crucial cog in that, as Southampton, Celtic, and Middlesbrough must be scratching their heads now.
