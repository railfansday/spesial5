Philippe Clement thinks Norwich City man can follow in Sunderland star’s footsteps

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Norwich City boss Philippe Clement has compared Lucien Mahovo to former PSG and Borussia Dortmund player Thomas Meunier, now a Sunderland star, as the 21-year-old has been deployed in a more attacking role than expected since returning from injury.

Mahovo's emergence into the Norwich fold since he arrived from Notts County back in 2024 has been stunted by various setbacks, most recently an ankle injury during pre-season, which ruled him out for the first couple of months of the campaign.

Since returning to action, though, the 21-year-old has been substituted on as a right-sided attacker in league action, much to the surprise of Canaries fans, as he'd broken through as an attacking left-back.

Clement is honing in on his qualities going forward, while the likes of Ben Chrisene and Darlin Yongwa both battle for the starting left-back spot, and the Norwich boss feels he could thrive in this new role, just like a young Meunier did while he was on the coaching staff at Club Brugge.

It's safe to say that Philippe Clement rates Mahovo highly; he wouldn't be looking for ways to include him while the left-back spot remains pretty saturated otherwise.

Speaking to the press following their recent 5-0 demolition at Bristol City, he explained that, back in Bruges, a young Thomas Meunier was breaking through as an attacker before finding his feet further back.

The 35-year-old, off the back of that, has 83 Belgian caps and many years at the top of the game in Europe with Borussia Dortmund and PSG, before joining Sunderland this past summer and making his bow in the Premier League.

"I spoke with Lucien about the plan in training sessions to put him higher, because I've seen good things with him in four-against-four, five-against-five, six-against-six in the small-sided games about dribbling, shooting, making decisive actions, and like this he has more chance to be in the selection and to come on the pitch, and that's his ambition," Clement said.

"Will it be his position for the rest of his life? I don't know, to be honest. Sometimes those things can happen, and suddenly you see something that changes a career.

"We had Thomas Meunier in Bruges, when I was assistant, as a striker. He became a winger with the manager there, and he did that job defensively well. And later on, he became a full back, and he made an international career, going to the best teams in the world as a right full back.

"He would never have been a Paris (Saint-Germain) player for years as a striker or a right winger, but as a right full back, yes."

Meunier has a career to be proud of, and if Clement is tipping Mahovo to have a similar impact as the 35-year-old has had following a slight positional tweak, then that's high praise.

It's not like Norwich aren't deprived of wingers, either. If the 21-year-old isn't playing at left-back, he'll be competing with Andre Brooks for that right-wing role, whom the Canaries signed for £8 million from Sheffield United in the summer.

The Belgian boss is evidently a big fan, though, and his defensive nous as a winger could help craft a well-rounded player, which would likely put him ahead in the pecking order for the majority of the coaches he'd play under.

"He has so much potential, the boy, and I believe so hard in his potential,” Clement continued. “Also offensively, he does his defensive duty. So I really think he can, in both positions, make an amazing career.

"I believe really hard in Lucien’s quality, not only as a full back but also in those positions to hurt opponents with this playing style, so those are things also to develop.”

Norwich are starting to hit their stride this season, with four successive wins lifting them into the top half at the November international break. Having everyone fit and healthy will be key if the Canaries are to continue to overcome yet another slow start and challenge for the play-offs.

Mahovo has the support and belief from his head coach. Now, he needs to remain available for him to maintain that, something which he's struggled to do now for a couple of years.

In the 2024/25 campaign under Johannes Hoff Thorup, the then-teenage full-back started six consecutive league games between January and February, and looked to be breaking through, before injury curtailed his year.

Then, last season, he looked to be getting back into contention under Clement over the Christmas period, but more muscular issues saw him miss almost the entire second half of the term.

Coming off the bench in each of his last three is a good sign that he may be able to make a sustained run in the side and, if he can, he'll be able to nail down a more important role in this exciting Norwich squad.

Mahovo has the potential, both defensively and going forward, but only time will tell whether he can stay healthy enough to reach it.
