Southampton face losing Premier League loan man previously eyed by Millwall, Preston North End

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Southampton signed Nottingham Forest defender Zach Abbott in the summer, bringing him in on loan for added depth at the back. The 20-year-old mainly plays at centre-back but is capable of covering at right-back, however his versatility has not helped him find regular minutes this season.

Questions over his future at St Mary’s Stadium have arisen as a result, and The Telegraph now reports that the Saints could lose Abbott in January. Nottingham Forest are set to consider recalling the highly-rated defender, who has made just two appearances for Tonda Eckert‘s side.

It is added that Championship pair Millwall and Preston North End were interested in Abbott. He did not play for Forest this season before his loan switch, so he would be able to move to another team in the bid to find game time over the second half of the 2026/27 campaign.

20-year-old Abbott is a prospect held in high regard. He has been capped at all England youth levels from U16 to U20 and found a place in Nottingham Forest’s first-team squad last season, with his 10 appearances including seven outings in their run to the semi-finals of the UEFA Europa League.

It was hoped that a loan to the Championship with Southampton would give him the game time needed to make meaningful progress. There is still value to take from training in a new environment, but put simply, two appearances is nowhere near what Abbott will have hoped for.

With a winter relocation, Abbott can move to a club where he will find more game time. Such an opportunity could still come at Championship level, but opportunities towards the top end of League One should also be considered.

Things could yet change if injuries hit and Abbott gets a chance to impress with the Saints. But, as things stand, it is hard to argue against the notion that he should be recalled and sent elsewhere.

It remains to be seen what decision Nottingham Forest come to, but if Abbott is to be recalled from Southampton and loaned out, a switch to previous admirers Preston North End could be ideal.

The Lilywhites are at the wrong end of the Championship table but could do with some youthful exuberance at the back and Abbott would provide just that. He most certainly has the ability to displace any of Andrew Hughes, Liam Lindsay or Odel Offiah in Graham Alexander’s recent back three.

Furthermore, he would have plenty of defending to do and would gain experience in dealing with strong attacking pressure. Learning in a relegation-battling environment can help harden Abbott for the years to come in his exciting career too.
