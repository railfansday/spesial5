Gracie Abrams Sets 2027 Australian Arena Shows for ‘Look at My Life Tour’

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The singer-songwriter will play three nights each in Melbourne and Sydney next July and August, with Arlo Parks as a special guest

Gracie Abrams is bringing The Look at My Life Tour to Australia next year.

Abrams will play six arena shows across two cities in July and August 2027: three nights at Melbourne’s Rod Laver Arena on July 27, 28 and 30, followed by three at Sydney’s Afterpay Arena on Aug. 3, 4 and 6. British singer-songwriter Arlo Parks will open all six dates.

“Australia! I’ve missed you so much and I cannot express just how excited I am to return to Melbourne and Sydney next July/August for ‘The Look at My Life Tour,’” Abrams said in a statement. “A show that we’ve been working very hard to put together, a show that we hope you love as much as we do.” Related Daniel Hall, Co-Founder of Australian Rock Band Taxiride, Dies at 48 U2 Opens 2026 Rock & Roll Hall of Fame Ceremony With Surprise Performance Phil Collins Tells Rock Hall Crowd: 'You Haven't Heard the Last of Me' Explore Arlo Parks Gracie Abrams See latest videos, charts and news

“I’ve just missed you so much more than I can tell you… as soon as I heard for sure we were coming back my heart began to sing,” she added. “I will see you soon, I love you so much… we’re gonna make it great.”

The tour supports Daughter From Hell, Abrams’ third studio album, which debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 in July with 124,000 equivalent album units, her first chart-topping album. Produced with longtime collaborator Aaron Dessner, it also reached No. 1 in Australia. A deluxe edition arrives Friday (Nov. 20) with five additional tracks, including “Out of Nowhere,” which she first performed at Glastonbury in 2025, and “Crazy Girl,” previously available only on vinyl. The Look at My Life Tour launches Dec. 2 in Denver.

The run marks Abrams’ third visit to Australia in four years. She sold out six theater shows on her first trip in January 2024, then returned in 2025 for 11 arena dates across Sydney, Brisbane, Melbourne, Adelaide and Perth on The Secret of Us Tour. This time she is concentrating on two markets.

The Secret of Us (2024) debuted at No. 2 on the Billboard 200, and its deluxe edition produced “That’s So True,” her first top 10 hit on the Billboard Hot 100. Her Taylor Swift collaboration “us.” earned a Grammy nomination for best pop duo/group performance. Abrams will also make her acting debut in the upcoming A24 film Please.

An artist presale begins Wednesday (Nov. 18) at 10 a.m. local time, followed by Frontier Touring and Live Nation presales Thursday (Nov. 19). General sale opens Friday (Nov. 20) at 10 a.m. local time.
