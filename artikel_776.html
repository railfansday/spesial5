OURBIRTHDAY Is Billboard’s K-Pop Up & Comer of the Month for November: Exclusive Photos

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

From the exact moment their debut finally felt real to the sonic territories they're eager to explore next, Billboard Korea caught up with OURBIRTHDAY.

OURBIRTHDAY wants every day to feel like a birthday.

Driven by bright energy, diverse personalities and an eagerness to turn ordinary moments into something worth celebrating, the rookie act steps onto the K-pop scene with a clear sense of identity.

The seven-member girl group made their official debut this summer with their single “Our Birthday” and its upbeat title track “SQUEEZY,” immediately showcasing a fresh, free-spirited dynamic. Blending distinct vocal tones and performance styles into one cohesive sound, the septet is carving out a lane of their own with “WHIP-HOP,” a self-coined genre where “whip” embodies each member’s individuality.

From the exact moment their debut finally felt real to the sonic territories they’re eager to explore next, Billboard Korea caught up with OURBIRTHDAY.

You’ve naturally been described as “the first new girl group in four years since NMIXX from JYP, the house of girl groups.” You must have worked hard right up until debut to live up to that. Was there a post-debut moment you dreamed about as trainees?

KUK CHOROK: As a trainee, I imagined so many times what it would be like to stand on stage and hear fans call my name. After debut, when I actually heard fans chanting our names, I thought, “The moment I dreamed of is really here.” It felt surreal, and I was so grateful. I never want to forget how I felt the first time I stepped on stage!

ACHIRAYA: The moment I’d dreamed of since my trainee days was dancing and singing my heart out on stage while looking right at our fans!

While preparing your debut single ‘Our Birthday,’ what musical strengths did each of you want to bring to it, things you’ve been honing and wanted to show? Feel free to answer from any angle, from the songs to the performance.

CHO HYEJIN: As rookies, we wanted our characters to stand out clearly, so I was determined that we prepare thoroughly and not show any weak spots on stage. Since I used to work as a dancer, I felt I had to dance even better than I had before. Singing still doesn’t feel as natural to me as dancing, so I practiced hard to feel confident on any stage!

U: Through this single, I wanted to show the appeal of my voice and my rap. I wanted to bring a twist, where the mood of the song shifts a little when I come in, so I spent a lot of time working on my rap, my facial expressions and how to make the most of my tone. During that process, one phrase stuck with me: “Small details people barely notice can be what wins their hearts.” So I paid attention to every little expression and gesture as I prepared.

Is there a Korean or international artist you’d like to collaborate with?

ACHIRAYA: If I ever get the chance, I’d love to collaborate with KATSEYE!

KUK CHOROK: I’d love to work with BIBI! She has such a distinct musical identity, and I’ve always loved her music. I think if we blended our voices, we could make something a little unusual and full of atmosphere, so I’d really love to work with her.

Before debut, you released a hero animation featuring each member’s warrior character. Is there anything about yourself that makes you think, “Even I’d say that’s pretty heroic”? Small things count too.

CHO HYEJIN: When I finish a stage I’ve prepared for and dreamed about for so long, together with the members and with no regrets, that’s when I feel like a hero!

SHIN HYEWON: When I notice what the people around me are feeling or what they need, and take care of it.

U: About 70%, I’d say! I remember talking about this character with my dad on the phone. He said the character’s calm, steady vibe and hip outfits reminded him of me, since I’m thoughtful but also passionate. The headset the character loves is also something I never go to a schedule without, so I feel even more attached to it.

Are there habits or things about your fellow members that feel like a gift, or a birthday present?

KILALA: Maybe because I’m the youngest, the older members dote on me a lot, and I really love that. We’re all so close that every day feels as fun as a birthday.

BABY: My roommate right now is our youngest, KILALA, so we see a lot of each other’s habits and everyday moments. If one of us falls asleep first at night, the other puts in earphones and switches to a softer light without being asked. Little considerate things like that feel comforting, like a gift.

You previously released “HUNGRY” in two versions, Side A and Side B. How do they differ, and what does each bring? When you heard the other unit’s version or watched their performance, what felt fresh to you?

KILALA: I think our Side B has a high-energy, clean performance style, while the older members in Side A show more of a hip-hop feel and personality. When we first learned “HUNGRY,” there were a lot of freestyle sections, and since I’d never done that style before, it felt really new.

BABY: I think Side A and Side B each have their own charm. Side A is all about freedom and swagger, and Side B is about energy and precision. It’s the same song, but because the units sing it separately, you get to feel different charms! The part that felt freshest to me was the intro. Each member freestyles it, so everyone’s personality really comes through, and when those personalities come together, it feels different again.

You debuted with your own genre, “WHIP-HOP,” and “whip” also stands for the individuality of the seven members. What makes each of you distinct? What personal style and musical strengths do you want to show music fans going forward? It’d be great to hear from each member.

KUK CHOROK: The seven of us are completely different in personality, voice and how we come across on stage. For me, I think what sets me apart is the contrast in my voice and vibe! I’m usually bright and playful, but when I sing, a husky low register comes out and the whole mood changes. I hope that contrast comes through in our music too!

SHIN HYEWON: I love languid or rhythmic R&B! My musical strength is that I pick up on the feel or vibe of a song well. Of course, I still have a lot to learn about actually expressing it!

CHO HYEJIN: As the leader, when I look at the members, each of us has such a clear personality that I feel we’re a team people can stay with for a long time! As for me, I think my strength is that I can show many different sides through a range of genres.

U: I’m still very much learning, but I’m really interested in finding my own tone and way of expressing myself by listening to all kinds of music. I’ve been especially inspired by artists who rap over an R&B base, and someday I’d like to take part in production myself and make music that carries my own sensibility. I’ve long been interested in video production, songwriting and composing, so I’ve been collecting ideas bit by bit as they come to me. I want to keep learning, turn those ideas into music or videos, and build a color that’s my own.

BABY: I love dancing in a hip, free and relaxed way, so I think my own mix of hip and calm is what makes me distinct. I want to show fans more of my hip side, and show more range musically as well.

ACHIRAYA: I think I have my own style in both dancing and singing. What I’m most confident about in singing is putting my emotions into it. So I want to keep giving you songs full of feeling! If I get the chance, I’d love to write a song myself with my own emotions and stories in it!

KILALA: I think my strength is my dance lines, especially how my hands and body move. I also think there’s something special about my voice, so I’ll keep showing you my own tone and skills!

Is there anything you’d like to achieve before the year ends? It can be something small.

KILALA: On a day off, I’d love for all seven of us to get together and share a meal!

CHO HYEJIN: Twenty is such a symbolic age, and now that it’s coming to an end, I want to throw myself into everything without fear and try everything I want to do! And since winter, my favorite season, is on its way, I’d love to spend Christmas with the members.

What moments in your life right now feel like a birthday present?

U: The joys and sorrows, the happiness and pain that come each year build up one by one into special feelings only the me of that moment can have. When a slightly more grown-up me looks back on those moments later, I think all of those experiences stay with me like gifts.

SHIN HYEWON: When I read warm messages or letters from fans, when I see a pretty sky, and when I get to have a slow, relaxed evening.

Is there a genre or concept you’d like to try in the future?

BABY: The vibe I’m always going for is hip and cool, so someday I’d like to try a genre that’s full-on hip-hop.

KUK CHOROK: I’d like to try a more mature, hip concept that shows a different side from the OURBIRTHDAY you see now. I especially want to try R&B or soul, genres that can bring out the members’ voices and vibes even more! It’d also be fun to look like a completely different person on stage.

KUK CHOROK: I think I’m someone who shares bright energy with the people around me! I want to be someone who’s easy to be around and keeps you laughing, and at the same time, I take responsibility for whatever I’m given and see it through.

SHIN HYEWON: I always look laid-back, but I might actually be the most impatient one! When I do something, I want to do it properly. Normally I don’t overthink things, but when I’m interested in something or want to be good at it, no hassle is too much.

CHO HYEJIN: I think I’m someone who knows how to fall and how to get back up. Rather than someone perfect who nails everything, I want to be transparent and honest, someone who keeps learning and growing even when I fail sometimes, and I feel like that’s who I am.

U: I think I’m like a set of paints. There’s so much I want to try, take on and express, even emotions I want to feel, and I’m curious about anything new. So rather than staying in one color, I want to turn everything I feel and experience into colors of my own. Someday I want to spread all my colors freely across a canvas called “U.” That’s my dream, and I think it’s why I want to keep standing in front of all of you.

BABY: I’m introverted, but I have strong convictions. I work hard to stick to my own standards. My MBTI is INTJ, and true to type, I’m a total planner. I like to put things in order and make plans when I work, so when a plan falls through, I come up with a Plan B right away. I’m shy around people I’ve just met, but once we’re close, I’m a lot of fun!

ACHIRAYA: I’m a positive person with lots of bright energy! I love talking and hanging out with friends and other people. I’d describe my personality as fun and funny!

KILALA: I’m someone with a very clear color of my own. I also think more than people expect, and I’m the type who grows even more when I get praised!

KUK CHOROK: It was when we went out for the full-cast ending after our first music show! Doing the ending shot on the same stage as seniors I’d only ever seen on TV, looking out at so many fans, I thought, “I’ve really debuted.”

SHIN HYEWON: It hit me most when my parents told me their friends had seen me somewhere.

CHO HYEJIN: I think it felt most real when I met our fans for the first time and received the precious letters they wrote. Reading every word about how they love our music and support us, and holding it all close to my heart, that’s when it sank in.

U: It started to sink in when our title track was nominated for first place! It reminded me of all the time I spent preparing for debut with the members, and I felt so grateful. Thank you so much to our fans who always cheer us on. I love you!

BABY: It felt most real when I searched my name on social media and when I headed out to music show schedules.

ACHIRAYA: Right before going up for our debut showcase, I was waiting below the stage looking up at it, and that’s when I thought, “I’m really debuting.” I was so happy and proud that I got choked up.

KILALA: When we did our first music show for “HUNGRY.”

Which part of your debut single’s title track or B-sides shows off your charm best?

KUK CHOROK: I have the opening rap in the B-side “Our Birthday.” In the other songs I mostly got bright-sounding parts, but here I used a really low tone, so I think it shows a different side of me.

SHIN HYEWON: The bridge in “Our Birthday” with the line “I don’t need candles”!

CHO HYEJIN: In our title track “SQUEEZY,” I have the line “with my sneaker laces pulled tight.” I have a superstition about tying my laces tight before every battle or performance, and the line felt like it expressed how firmly resolved I am on our debut album, so I chose it.

U: The part in my first-verse rap in the B-side “Our Birthday” with the line “Imagination come true, bursting confetti piled high on our heads.” The choreography there, where I step forward with free gestures, captures the excitement of debut and the freedom I feel on stage. I remember putting extra effort into it during practice.

BABY: In our title track “SQUEEZY,” the “Squeezy easy squeezy easy ya ya” part and the narration in the bridge. I’ve always loved and wanted parts that change the mood of a song, and narration parts, so getting exactly the parts I love makes me think they suit me best.

ACHIRAYA: I think my charm comes through best in the “HUNGRY” part “My name is Kick, yeah I’m looking for fun.” I loved it because I get to express myself and show off some cool dancing too!

KILALA: I do a lot of ad-libs, and I think those parts that stand out so prettily represent me best.

What words are carved in your heart? (Your motto)

KUK CHOROK: “Just go for it!” When I overthink, it gets harder to start, so when something’s on my mind, I try to just dive in. I believe you can try first and fill in what’s missing later, and I want to keep being someone who isn’t afraid of new things and experiences as much as possible!

SHIN HYEWON: “The more I wander, the more ground I claim!”

CHO HYEJIN: “By my own standards, without any fuss!”

U: My motto is “I have me.” Whenever I’m scared to take on a challenge, I tell myself, “I’ve gotten through every hurt and every hard time so far, so even if I fall or fail, I’ll be the one to pick myself back up.” With that positive mindset, I try to push past my limits one by one.

BABY: “Live as if today were your last.” It means giving my best at everything, as if there’s no next time.

KILALA: “Cherish the expressions only I can make” and “Give it my all so I never regret my choices”!

What kind of presence do you hope OURBIRTHDAY will grow into in the global music market?

ACHIRAYA: I want us to grow into artists with cool performances, where each member’s individual charm stands out through OURBIRTHDAY’s own WHIP-HOP.

CHO HYEJIN: I want us to be artists whose songs hold people’s memories, music anyone can enjoy, and always as special as a birthday!
