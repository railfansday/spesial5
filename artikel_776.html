Carra: Chelsea must sign centre-back or achieve nothing

Jamie Carragher believes Chelsea must sign a dominant centre-back or they will have "no chance of achieving anything".

Chelsea have conceded 20 goals in 11 Premier League games this season and were thrashed 4-1 at home to Leeds on Saturday, which has put pressure on new manager Xabi Alonso, with his side sitting eighth and already 12 points behind leaders Arsenal.

Leeds overpowered Chelsea with their physicality, as Dominic Calvert-Lewin bullied centre-backs Levi Colwill and Wesley Fofana, exposing their weak defence the same way Sunderland's Brian Brobbey did in their 1-0 win before the international break.

Chelsea are five points behind fourth-placed Liverpool and are already without European football this season - but Carragher feels qualifying for the Champions League will be a difficult task with their current centre-back pairing.

"Chelsea have got a massive problem at centre-back and if they don't change it in January, they're going to have a big problem in terms of where they feel they should be this season with the Champions League," he said on Monday Night Football.

"Colwill at Sunderland against Brobbey gave the penalty away, and the manager took him off at half-time. Fofana's performance against Man United, especially in the first half, was one of the worst you'll see from a centre-back this season.

"As a partnership, this will never work because they're not physical enough and there's no real leader there.

"It almost feels like you've got two novices playing, but one cost £75m, and Colwill has been talked up a lot not just at Chelsea but at international level as well."

Chelsea signed centre-back Maxence Lacroix from Crystal Palace for £51m in the summer, but he has missed the last three matches with a hamstring injury.

"Those two (Colwill and Fofana) can't play in a back four as a two, but it reminds me of myself at Liverpool," said Carragher. "We didn't fix it defensively and we were seen as weak, conceding from set pieces and long throws. It wasn't until we signed Sami Hyypia, 6ft 4in, dominant centre-back, leader, great player, that it changed for us.

"This is not going to change with what they have. Lacroix is a decent player, that's a sticking plaster until the next transfer window in January or the end of the season. Until they sign that dominant centre-back and leader, they've got absolutely no chance of achieving anything."

Chelsea have kept the fourth fewest clean sheets in Europe this calendar year, which largely predates Alonso's tenure, although Carragher has been surprised by the former Bayer Leverkusen and Real Madrid head coach's style.

"There's a lot of talk now about Xabi Alonso and I think watching Chelsea this season, they haven't been that impressive even when they've won games in terms of how I think we all felt they would play," he added.

"Everybody had this idea, myself included, who had watched Leverkusen play, you think of the player Xabi Alonso would play, the Spanish influence and managed by Pep Guardiola. You're expecting Chelsea to be more of a possession-based team and when you watch them the football isn't amazing and they rely on the front three a lot.

"Cole Palmer has dropped off in the last few weeks and Joao Pedro has been injured as well. When they've all been there, it always felt like there was an overreliance on that front three. The goal on the weekend was a direct play; you can get goals in different ways, but there hasn't been that intricate football that we were expecting."

In his latest Sky Sports column, Paul Merson has raised doubts about whether Alonso is good enough as Chelsea manager after Saturday's home loss to Leeds.

Merson believes Liverpool may have snubbed Alonso for the head coach role - eventually taken by Andoni Iraola - due to concerns about his pedigree.

He told Sky Sports: "My biggest worry is that Liverpool could have had Xabi Alonso and they chose not to. Obviously, Liverpool didn't think he was good enough.

"Alonso took the Chelsea job, and Liverpool sacked Arne Slot two weeks later. You can't tell me that Liverpool couldn't have spoken to Alonso and say: 'Don't take the Chelsea job, we're going to take you'.

"He was out of a job as well, Liverpool could have had him and he'd have gone there all day long. So why didn't they take him? Did they know something that we're seeing now?

"It just always sits in my mind. That was my worry - and now it's playing out.

"If this was Liam Rosenior in charge at Chelsea and getting these results, he'd be in major trouble. One hundred per cent, he'd be in trouble. And remember, Rosenior didn't have Morgan Rogers in his Chelsea team, bought for £116m.

"But it's because of the unbelievable job he did at Bayer Leverkusen that everybody is thinking: it will be alright."
