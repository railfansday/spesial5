Anyma Will Return to the Great Pyramids of Giza In 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Jean-Michel Jarre will share the stage for both shows, for what will mark the French electronic music pioneer's own return to Giza.

Anyma will return next spring to the Great Pyramids of Giza, Egypt, the setting of his record-setting shows in 2025. Only this time he’s bringing with him one of the great wonders of the electronic music world — Jean-Michel Jarre. Explore Anyma Jean-Michel Jarre See latest videos, charts and news

The Italian-American producer and DJ (real name: Matteo Milleri) will stage two spectacular electronic performances at the Pyramids, titled ÆDEN MILLENNIUM, on March 19 and 26, 2027. It’s a touch of history repeating for Anyma, who last October used the canvas of the Pyramids for two sold-out shows, which drew fans from 92 countries, reps say. “I’ve been fortunate enough to bring my show to some incredible venues around the world in the past few years, but the two-part show at the Pyramids of Giza has been by far one of the most memorable,” he explains in a statement.

“I’m working on a new, entirely unique production for our return in 2027, especially paying tribute to the magnitude of the area’s beauty and history,” he continues. “I can’t wait to share ÆDEN MILLENNIUM with you in Egypt next year.”

Jarre will share the stage for both dates, for what will mark the French electronic music pioneer’s own return to Giza after ringing in the Millennium with his 12-hour production, The Twelve Dreams Of The Sun.

“Delighted to accept Anyma’s invitation to join forces,” Jarre remarks. “When I welcomed the new Millennium at the Great Pyramids of Egypt, my ambition was to create a dialogue between this ancient setting and the future through music, light and technology. It’s exciting to return with Anyma, who shares that same spirit of exploration, and imagine a new chapter together.”

Jarre, now aged 78, is 22 albums deep in a career that places him at the head of the table for electronic music’s greatest and most influential artists.

Among his masterpieces is debut Oxygene, which this year celebrates its 50th anniversary. To celebrate its milestone, Jarre embarked on a pan-European run of summer concerts, including a performance at Amnesia Ibiza, for the club’s own half-century celebrations, and he appeared as special guest of honor at the 30th edition of Amsterdam Dance Event in October, where he presented the sold-out opening concert, “Oxygene & Beyond: A Sonic Journey Celebrating 30 Years of ADE.”

Born in Lyon, France, Jarre has clocked up career album sales north of 85 million, and was awarded the Commander of the Légion d’Honneur in 2021, France’s highest order of merit. President Emmanuel Macron was on hand for the presentation.

He’s readying the release of a special edition of Oxygene on Nov. 20 through an expanded new partnership with BMG, and the U.S. publication of Machines: A History Of Electronic Music on Nov. 24 via Thames & Hudson.

Anyma launched his all-new live show ÆDEN at his headline set at Coachella 2026, and has since presented it across the globe, including a performance to 65,000 people in Mexico City, and at China’s largest ever electronic headline concert, May 2 in Shanghai.

Tickets for ÆDEN MILLENNIUM will be available on artist presale from Nov. 18 at 2pm GMT (9am ET), with general sale from Nov. 19 at 2pm GMT. More acts for both dates will be announced in due course.
