‘KPop Demon Hunters’ Criterion Collection Blu-ray On Sale for 50% Off on Heels of New Tour Announcement

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

“KPop Demon Hunters” is getting the Criterion treatment. Netflix’s breakout musical fantasy film is now available as a physical release, thanks to a special Blu-ray set available to buy online now.

The film is available in Blu-ray and 4K UHD editions, priced at $39.95 and $49.95, respectively but Amazon currently has the Blu-ray version discounted by 50% off ahead of Black Friday and on the heels of the new “KPop Demon Hunters” tour announcement. The Criterion Collection release features the Oscar-winning animated film remastered in 4K with Dolby Atmos soundtrack. Behind-the-scenes content includes a “making of” documentary, plus lyric videos and other featurettes. The set also comes with collectible postcards with original illustrations from animators who worked on the film. The anime-inspired cover art was also created exclusively for the physical release.

The release caps a major run for “KPop Demon Hunters,” which became one of Netflix’s biggest original film breakouts after releasing last June. Directed by Maggie Kang and Chris Appelhans and produced in partnership with Sony Pictures Animation, the film follows K-pop superstars Rumi, Mira and Zoey — voiced by Arden Cho, May Hong and Ji-young Yoo — who lead double lives as demon hunters when they aren’t selling out stadiums.

The film, which won the Oscar for best animated feature, broke multiple records over the past year. It became the most streamed movie of 2025 while its corresponding soundtrack hit No. 1 on the Billboard 200. The lead single “Golden” remained at No. 1 on the Hot 100, with the film becoming the first soundtrack to place four songs in the chart’s top 10 at the same time.

The film has also spawned dozens of lucrative partnerships and licensing deals. In fact, Mattel and Hasbro, fierce rivals, were named a global “co-master” toy licensee for “KPop Demon Hunters — a dual license structure believed to be unprecedented in the industry. Themed products such as Uno, Monopoly, and dolls have begun rolling out this spring.

A sequel is currently in development at Netflix and Sony Pictures Animation, with Kang and Appelhans returning. The just-announced tour, meantime, puts the film’s animated K-pop trio Huntr/x in front of live audiences. Dates kick off in June.
