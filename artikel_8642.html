Choose the American Express card built for your biggest spending. Households wanting cash back should get a Blue Cash card, while the Gold and Platinum pay off only if you’ll use their credits and travel with the points. Business owners should start with a no-fee Blue Business card.

The cards here pay you in cash back, in American Express’s Membership Rewards points, or in Delta miles and Hilton points. American Express’s own examples, figured on its Platinum Card, value a point at 1 cent toward flights booked through its Amex Travel site and 0.6 cent when you use it to pay a charge on your statement. So the points are worth most to people who travel. The Delta and Hilton cards serve regulars of those brands, and if you’re loyal to another airline or hotel chain, choose among all airline credit cards or hotel credit cards instead.

The Gold and Platinum come with statement credits, money back on your bill, but most arrive as monthly, quarterly, or twice-yearly amounts you collect only by making the purchases they name in that period. American Express says its cards can be used at 99% of U.S. places that take credit cards. Abroad, it says it is still working to get more merchants to take its cards, so pack a Visa or Mastercard for trips overseas.
