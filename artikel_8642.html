BridgeBio Pharma, Inc. (BBIO) Discusses FDA Approval of Welgemda for LGMD2I November 23, 2026 4:30 PM EST

Neil Kumar - Co-Founder, CEO & DirectorChristine Siu - Chief Executive Officer of BridgeBio NeuromuscularAnna Wade - BD & Operations

Thank you for joining us. Today, the FDA approved Welgemda, formerly known as BBP-418 for the treatment of LGMDR9, also known as LGMD2I in adult and pediatric patients weighing 30 kilograms or more. Welgemda is the first and only FDA-approved therapy for individuals living with LGMD2I and represents the first approved treatment for any form of limb-girdle muscular dystrophy. For patients and their families, this is a day that many hope for, and we're thrilled to share it with you. To the patients and families who participated in our clinical trials, including the Phase III/IV to V study, you gave your time, your trust and your courage with no guarantee of the outcome. This approval would not have happened without you. To the investigators, physicians, nurses, clinical evaluators, coordinators and care teams running these studies around the world, you held the work to an extraordinary standard and cared for every participant along the way.

To the advocates and patient organizations who built this community, supported the early science and helped us understand which outcomes matter most, you have been integral to this program from the beginning. To the McColl and Lockwood families who more than 2 decades ago, set out to change what a diagnosis of LGMD2I could mean, and to the scientists of the McColl Lockwood laboratory, you turned a family search for answers into a medicine for an entire community. And to our colleagues of BridgeBio Neuromuscular and a cross BridgeBio through clinical development, regulatory review, manufacturing and launch preparation, you brought rigor and urgency to the work of getting this medicine
