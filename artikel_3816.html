MLB The Show 27 announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Publisher Sony Interactive Entertainment and developer San Diego Studio have officially announced MLB The Show 27.

“So, what lies ahead? We’re eager to share the exciting new features and improvements coming to MLB The Show 27, but it’s still a little too early to reveal what’s in store,” said San Diego Studio product development communications and brand strategist Ramone Russell in a PlayStation Blog post. “However, there’s one announcement we can’t wait to make: The Tech Test is officially returning for MLB The Show 27.

“Tech Tests are invaluable to the development process. They help our team identify bugs, evaluate performance, and gather meaningful feedback from the community before the game officially launches.

“Now, you might be wondering: If Tech Tests are so important, why haven’t we held one since MLB The Show 23? That’s a fair question.

“Game development is challenging, and each year brings its own set of difficult decisions and competing priorities. Some of those decisions resulted in us not holding Tech Tests for the past few years. We appreciate your patience, passion, and continued support, and we’re looking forward to having you be part of the process again. We will provide more details on the Tech Test in the new year.”
