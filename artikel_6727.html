These Quick &amp; Easy Comfort Food Recipes Are Here To Make Your Weeknights Even Cozier

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Mash-ups, one-pan dinners, lazy meals, and more.

It stands to reason that comfort food shouldn’t cause you any discomfort. I don’t know about you, but time- and effort-intensive dishes, having to wash every pot, pan, and utensil in your kitchen, and dinners that require making every ingredient from scratch can certainly pull me out of a cozy mood. With this in mind, I tracked down 15 of our easiest comfort food recipes, made simple by some of our favorite kitchen shortcuts (i.e., one-pan wonders, mashup recipes, and store-bought swaps). Don’t worry, cutting down on the number of pans involved, hours in the kitchen, and homemade pastas won’t sacrifice any of the comfort in these warm, filling meals. In fact, I think you just may find your night getting a little more cozy.

Chicken pot pie is a warm, hearty crowd-pleaser. But for all the comfort it delivers, it can be a difficult dinner dish, with hours of work required to make the perfect pie crust. So, we came up with a solution: chicken pot pie casserole. Classic chicken pot pie filling is baked into a stress-free casserole, topped with buttery biscuits for that tantalizing golden-brown finish.

Saucy, cheesy, and extremely satisfying, chicken Parmesan is the dish that the Delish kitchen can’t get enough of. When we don’t have time for frying but need chicken Parm, this 30-minute dinner is the one we turn to. Cheesy, flavorful chicken meatballs are baked in a skillet with a homemade tomato sauce and plenty of cheese, until bubbling and delicious.

Cabbage rolls just became a viable weeknight dinner with this quicker to make skillet dinner, which skips the cabbage rolling and gets dinner directly on the table. It’s less work, yet the payoff of a comforting home-cooked meal all the same.

Sloppy Joe casseroles are where. It’s. AT. Cozy? Check! Meaty? Check! Buttery carbs and cheese? Check and check! This hearty casserole has plenty of peppers and onions, lots of sauce, and so much flavor too. More than just delicious, this casserole is flexible, freezable, and ready to serve on a busy weeknight for dinner.

Chicken pot pie is a go-to comfort food, but when we’re feeling lazy, we just don’t have the patience to deal with a homemade pie crust. Enter our favorite cozy shortcut: chicken pot pie noodle bake. This recipe combines the classic pot pie filling with egg noodles for a baked pasta dinner that is every bit as satisfying.

Let’s be honest—nothing sounds better after a long day than the classic, cheesy, saucy comfort food, but it’s also the last thing we want to take the time to prepare. Enter: this lazy lasagna. With some store-bought hacks, you can achieve a weeknight-friendly lasagna that’s every bit as comforting.

Take all the wonderful ingredients of chicken noodle soup and bake them into a hearty casserole? You have a hearty dinner that leads to full bellies and happy hearts.

It honestly doesn’t matter if you’re from Madison, Minneapolis, or Miami; this classic Midwestern tater tot casserole is about to become a weekly staple meal in your home. It’s easy, reliable, and delicious.

Pot roast is already a classic comfort food, but we have to say, this French onion version takes the meaning of cozy winter dinner to new heights. Taking inspo from our onion soup rib roast, the beef is seasoned with onion soup mix to add depth and an extra umami boost.

Featuring tender pieces of dark meat chicken, creamy white beans, and tender veggies, this cozy Greek-inspired chili is the perfect filling-but-not-too-heavy dinner for those extra-chilly nights. Flavored with a bouquet of hearty herbs, this stew is easy to throw together in one pot for minimal mess and maximum flavor.

In this sheet-pan recipe, the paprika-dusted chicken is roasted on a bed of herby, sliced red onions and whole garlic cloves until the onions are jammy sweet with a little char and the chicken is cooked through and juicy.

When we’re craving pulled pork for a game day or a comforting weeknight dinner, if we have the time, we'll always turn to the slow cooker. This method makes it easier than ever to prepare pulled pork for a crowd with maximum flavor and minimum effort.

This recipe combines the rich smoky flavors of traditional barbacoa with a cheesy topping and buttery biscuits for an irresistible meal. At the heart of this dish is succulent beef chuck roast, slow cooked in a blend of fire-roasted tomatoes, chipotle chiles, and a medley of spices.

Tortellini makes any soup better, but this Tuscan tortellini soup is one of our favorite examples. Cheese tortellini works perfectly in this hearty soup filled with chicken sausage, spinach, and white beans. It’s filling, cozy, and ready in under an hour, making it a perfect option when you need something warm and hearty for dinner in a pinch.

When you combine chili and mac and cheese, incredible things happen. We started with white chicken chili and added in all the creamy, cheesy goodness of mac and cheese for the perfect weeknight dinner for indecisive families. The best part? After preparing the pasta, everything comes together in one skillet—minimal dishes, maximum comfort.
