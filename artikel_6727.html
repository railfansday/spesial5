Jennifer Lawrence Swaps Her Pops of Lemon for a Polished Dark Monochrome Look

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Complete with a glossy Hermès crocodile Kelly

Jennifer Lawrence can just as easily pull off a bright lemon-yellow Colleen Allen coat or a pair of funky sequined pink Siedrés pants as she can a polished, monochromatic business look.

Earlier this week, the actor looked so chic in an outfit composed of timeless navy and black staples. The base of her look was made up of an elongated, buttoned blazer, and matching trousers. To continue the color scheme, she wrapped a navy scarf around her neck.

Then came the luxurious black accents: square sunglasses and a rare Hermès Kelly Sellier in shiny crocodile leather and gold hardware. And because Lawrence cannot live without her pops of color, the actor slipped on a pair of burgundy velvet slippers.

Lawrence is often one to go minimalist with her jewelry and glam, and she did that here, opting for polish-free nails, minimal makeup, and a few gold rings (one of them being her engagement ring from husband Cooke Maroney).

As you may have guessed, the cherry on top of the look was Lawrence’s dirty-blonde hair, which was styled down in easy, breezy long waves. The Hunger Games alum recently introduced her haircare brand, Mes, and has since been promoting her products around town.

“I feel like if my hair looks good, no one will notice anything else about my face, my clothes, or my body,” the actor joked in a recent interview with The Cut.

Of course, we disagree. While we do love a good hair day, we’ll never miss a J.Law street-style moment.
