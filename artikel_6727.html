Before You Shop Glossier’s Black Friday Sale, Here’s Our Ranking of the 15 Best Products

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Once the new kid on the block, Glossier is one of the biggest and most well-known (and loved) makeup and skincare brands out there. “Cloud Paint” and “Boy Brow” aren’t just product names now; they’re trends that developed whole product categories, igniting the launch of a zillion dupes. It’s iconic, to say the least.

Glossier became known for its minimalist approach, both in packaging and product formula. Every makeup product is filled with skin-loving ingredients (like hyaluronic acid and glycerin), the skincare is gentle and suitable for all skin types, and their approach to beauty is simple and fresh-faced. From the best-selling Balm Dotcom to makeup artist-favorite Stretch Concealer to the hidden-gem bodycare—choosing a favorite child is hard. That’s why I ranked and reviewed all of the best Glossier products below.

While we’re here... Glossier is running its annual Black Friday sale right now. FWIW, Glossier rarely goes on sale. The brand really only does discounts during its annual Black Friday sale, meaning you won’t see prices this low for likely another year. I know 25% off doesn’t feel all that buzzy or exciting (it’s not the same shock as all those TVs and gaming consoles you stood in line for as a kid), but it’s one of the highest I’ve ever seen Glossier offer. And if you spend more than $100, your discount shoots up to 30% off. That, my friends, is a steal in the Glossier world.

Did you too pluck your eyebrows just a little (read: a lot) when you were younger? Unforch, the sentiment is v relatable, but this brow gel helps give that fluffy appearance even when you don’t have too much going on above your eyelids. “It's the only brow gel that visibly thickens my brows upon application,” editor Corinne Sullivan notes. “My favorite thing is how it keeps my brows conditioned rather than crunchy like other products do.”

When it comes to blush, Glossier’s beginner-friendly Cloud Paint will forever remain a cult-favorite pick among beauty lovers. “Sorry, but Cloud Paint is still the GOAT of all cream blushes,” beauty editor Beth Gillette shares. “Yup, I said it!!! It blends better than any other formula I've used, especially with my fingers, making it practically the simplest blush to apply ever. The shades are timeless and trendy and I'll never get tired of 'em.”

Beauty director Lauren Balsamo is obsessed with these blushes (she even said it was her favorite makeup launch last year). “The original Cloud Paint provides a nice wash of color that lasts for an hour or two, but Plush Blush lasts all day without fading, even on my extremely oily skin,” she says. “I’m not someone who wears primer or sets my makeup with a setting spray, so I fully expect most makeup to fade after a few hours, but Plush Blush survived a 12-hour office day on my cheeks.”

If dewy glasslike skin is what you’re after, dewy glasslike is what you’ll get! As the name suggests, Futuredew is the oil-serum hybrid that will make future you dewy as hell. Packed with nourishing oils and plant-based extracts, you’ll be looking as radiant as ever in no time. Wear it alone, under makeup, or as a highlighter on the high points of your face.

Makeup artist Katie Jane Hughes got me obsessed with this brow pencil, and now it’s virtually the only one I’ll use. It has a unique, straight tip (versus the usual fine, pointy one) that helps create hairlike strokes and fill in my brows with the same tool. My brows look natural and soft every time.

Glossier might not have been the first to launch a lip oil, but it made a pretty damn good one. It makes my lips look plush and glossy and hydrates like a lip treatment. The tints are extremely sheer too, so it works with basically every lip liner I pair it with.

Where skincare and makeup come to play, this foundation feels like a smooth gel cream that melts into the skin like butter. Available in 32 shades, you can build the coverage of this foundation up or keep it as minimal as you’d like. As for its ingredients, hydration is the name of the game with a mix of squalane and glycerin.

Lip Line is one of my favorite lip liners ever, and I own more than 50 (sorry, I’m a lip liner fiend). I love how easily the formula glides onto my lips, but the best part is how long it lasts. I don’t always want a stain, but I do often want a liner I don’t have to reapply right away. These stay on for hours without needing to pull it out of my bag and re-do, and they do so without drying out my lips.

As a lipstick lover, Ultralip remains a top-tier product. The main reason is the super hydrating formula that still gives a stunning color payoff. “For long-lasting color and tons of moisture, this is my go-to balm!” editor Corinne Sullivan shares. “It provides a nice, subtle tint while keeping my lips smooth and shiny (even in the winter!).”

Looking in the mirror and seeing your under-eyes creased is a top-five woe of makeup users all around. Stretch Balm concealer is flexible, lightweight, and hydrating. It covers up all the dark circles and redness you might wake up with after a long night of watching Eras Tour livestreams (I see you, I hear you, and I am you).

Let’s be real: Not all cleansers easily take off all of your makeup in one go. This gentle solution has a pH-balanced formula that actually removes your mascara and eye makeup without excess scrubbing. It’s creamy and nourishing so you don’t have to worry about your face feeling stripped and dry after.

What can we say, we’re fans of the classics! Balm Dotcom is one of the brand’s best-selling products, and for good reason. “Let the record show that Balm Dot Calm is still one of the best lip balms out there, despite having spawned hundreds of copycats since it first took over our feeds,” says commerce writer Sarah Maberry. “Old-school Glossier fans will be happy to hear they returned to the OG formula but kept the new and improved packaging. For years, I've kept a few of the birthday cake flavor on hand for an easy last-minute gift that everyone loves. I've even had friends tell me they look forward to their annual restock.”

If you wish you could bathe in Glossier You, you’ll enjoy dousing yourself in this body butter. It’s rich and decadent as it hydrates skin with shea butter and jojoba oil. I keep the chic jar on my nightstand and coat my skin with this cream before bed, and it makes my skin soft and supple (and smell amazing).

If I could lather myself in this lotion at all times, I would. As someone with oily skin, the idea of feeling sticky/greasy after lotioning is a constant fear. But this one absorbs into the skin so well while also giving off a subtle glowing finish. It literally feels like soft silk and smells so good with its orange blossom neroli complex.

A dab of this underneath my eyes is like a primer for my concealer. It immediately plumps my skin, making my under-eyes look brighter, firmer, and more awake.
