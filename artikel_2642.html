Giants announce good news before Commanders showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Giants announce good news before Commanders showdown originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The New York Giants are coming up on an important game against the Washington Commanders on Thursday under the bright lights of Thursday Night Football. At this point, the Giants are already having a stellar season, despite franchise quarterback Jaxson Dart being out all season due to injury. But they have to keep winning to go all the way, of course.

One of the most important factors for any team being ready to go at this point in the season and win games is simply health. The healthiest teams are often the ones that win. Now, the Giants have some good news on two of their most important players.

On Wednesday, the Giants released their latest injury report. As Adam Schefter of ESPN notes, running back Cam Skattebo, who has been dealing with a hamstring injury, and new quarterback J.J. McCarthy, who has been nursing a hip injury, have no injury status designation for Thursday night. They're "good to go vs. the Commanders," he notes.

This is big for the Giants. Having a healthy quarterback and running back is key to keeping the team's winning streak alive. They're 5-2-1 heading into the game against the Commanders, who are 2-5-1.

The game is set for MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey, and it will air on Amazon Prime. It's fairly incredible what first-year head coach John Harbaugh has done with his team. To go from the bottom of their division to the top, and without their franchise quarterback, is special.
