Rams’ Myles Garrett vows to make the Fearsome Foursome proud vs. Packers

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Los Angeles Rams take on the Green Bay Packers on Thanksgiving Eve. The Rams will be wearing their "Fearsome White" uniform for the only time this season during that game. The special uni is in honor of the legendary "Fearsome Four" defensive line that featured Deacon Jones, Merlin Olsen, Lamar Lundy and Rosey Grier during the '60s.

Myles Garrett is well aware of the history behind those great teams and is ready to do the uniform justice on Wednesday.

"I have a lot of respect for the history of the game and especially that Rams team. It means a lot to be able to wear that uniform and wear those colors and hopefully make them proud," Garrett said.

After a rough start, Myles Garrett and the Rams are on the rise with four wins in a row. The much-hyped combination of Garrett and Aaron Donald is finally healthy and starting to show its true potential. After Garrett's breakout three-sack performance against the Washington Commanders, Aaron Donald also got his first sacks of the season with 1.5 in the dominant 34-6 win over the Arizona Cardinals on Sunday.

The Rams will need Garrett and the defense to carry the team the rest of the way amid key injuries on offense. Star wide receiver Puka Nacua is already lost for the season after MCL surgery, while Davante Adams is still not ready to return from his hamstring injury. Garrett and co. will also get their first big test in a while as they take on Jordan Love and the explosive Packers passing attack.
