Vote for the Register female Athlete of the Week for Nov. 8-14

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The week of Nov. 8-14 saw the IGHSAU girls state swimming and diving meet take place at the University of Iowa Campus Recreation and Wellness Center in Iowa City.

Dowling Catholic won the team championship for the third year in a row with 294 points. Waukee Northwest tied Bettendorf for third with 181 points, finishing just behind Cedar Falls runner-up score of 209.

There were several standout individual performances by girls swimmers and divers from the Des Moines metro area during the girls state swimming and diving meet.

Here are the Des Moines Register's female Athlete of the Week candidates from the Des Moines metro area for the week of Nov. 8-14. DesMoinesRegister.com readers vote to determine the winners, who will be announced at the end of the week.

Weinheimer earned one gold and silver medal apiece for Des Moines North at the IGHSAU girls swimming and diving meet on Nov. 13 in Iowa City.

Weinheimer won the 100-yard backstroke after swimming a time of 55.20 seconds. Weinheimer took second in the 50 freestyle, finishing the race in 23.45.

Corwin played a big role in Dowling Catholic winning its third state swimming and diving team championship in a row by winning two events in the finals competition at the state girls swimming and diving meet on Nov. 13 in Iowa City.

Corwin placed first in the 200-yard individual medley with a time of two minutes, 2.68 seconds and the 100 buttlerfly in 54.32. She also anchored the Maroons to victory in the 400 freestyle relay with a time of 3:25.06.

Braunschweig-Norris claimed her first state diving title during the IGHSAU state swimming and diving meet on Nov. 12 at Iowa City.

Braunschweig-Norris won the 1-meter diving competition with a score of 471.85 points on 11 dives. She beat Dowling Catholic's Mylee Dirks by 19.65 points for the championship.

Qualley picked up four medals for Dowling at the girls state swimming and diving meet on Nov. 13 in Iowa City.

Qualley teammed with Corwin, Taylor Hall and Gabby Freeseman to win the 400 freestyle relay in 3:25.06. Individually, she placed second in the 200 freestyle (1:49.63).

Hall helped the Maroon 200 medley relay take third in 1:45.60, and she picked up an individual bronze in the 500 freestyle after touching the wall in 4:59.54.

Gojovich won an individual silver medal, and she helped Waukee Northwest place third in the 400 freestyle relay at the girls state swimming and diving meet on Nov. 13 in Iowa City.

Gojovich took second to Weinheimer in the 100 backstroke with a time of 55.59. She joined forces with Kate Trosper, Addie DeYoung and Sasha Bykova to swim the 400 freestyle relay in 3:30.92.
