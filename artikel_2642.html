Live score updates, highlights for Alabama basketball vs Kansas State

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Alabama basketball is looking to get back in the win column before leaving Las Vegas.

After suffering its first loss of the 2026-27 season against Baylor, No. 15 Alabama (5-1) returns to T-Mobile Arena to face Kansas State (3-3) in the consolation bracket of the Players Era Festival on Thursday, Nov. 26.

The Crimson Tide nearly erased a 16-point second-half deficit against the Bears before falling 68-63 on Tuesday. Meanwhile, the Wildcats are looking to snap a two-game losing streak after suffering a 101-61 loss to Gonzaga in their tournament opener.

Follow along for live score updates, highlights and more as coach Nate Oats and Alabama look to reset on Thanksgiving.

Alabama's final opponent in Las Vegas hinges on the outcome of its matchup against Kansas State.

No. 17 Miami defeated Michigan 97-70 earlier Thursday, advancing to the fifth-place game. The winner between Alabama and Kansas State will face the Hurricanes at 7:30 p.m. CT on Friday, Nov. 27.

The loser will face the Wolverines at 4:30 p.m. CT. Both games will be televised on ESPNU.

After eclipsing 100 points three times in its first five games, Alabama was held to a season-low 63 points against Baylor.

The Crimson Tide shot 34.5% from the field and 23.3% from beyond the arc against the Bears, recording just two assists in the first half.
