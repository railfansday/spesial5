Bears-Lions is first Thanksgiving game ever with both teams on 5+ game winning streaks

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

One of the best Thanksgiving games ever will take place Thursday in Detroit.

When the Lions host the Bears, it will be the first time in NFL history that two teams on winning streaks of five games or longer meet on Thanksgiving. The Lions are on a six-game winning streak and the Bears are on a five-game winning streak.

The Lions and Bears are both 8-2 and tied for first place in the NFC North, so the game has major playoff implications. The winner will be all alone in first place in the best division in football.

This will be the 21st time the Bears and Lions have met on Thanksgiving. The Bears have an 11-9 edge in the previous 20 games.
