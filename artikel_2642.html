Kevin Porter Jr. reportedly to sign two-year deal with Orlando Magic

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Orlando has been looking for some scoring punch at the guard spot and has turned to free agent Kevin Porter Jr., who has agreed to sign a two-year deal with the team, his agents told ESPN's Shams Charania.

While the terms of the deal were not announced, this is undoubtedly for the veteran minimum, and the second year is very likely non-guaranteed.

Orlando was one of two teams reportedly interested in Porter earlier in the week. The other was Denver, but it didn't want to make a move right now.

While the Magic play Jalen Suggs and Anthony Black for most of the guard minutes, they want some scoring off the bench, and Porter can give them that. Last season in 38 games with the Bucks he averaged 17.4 points, 7.4 assists and 5.2 rebounds per game. He can be a streaky shooter and is not much of a defender, but in a backup role this can work.

Orlando had an open roster spot, so no other moves are needed. However, they are already over the luxury tax line, meaning this veteran minimum contract will cost them closer to $10 million total.
