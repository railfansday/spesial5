The clock is ticking on Mike McCarthy's time with the Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

When it comes to the nuclear war of words that Aaron Rodgers started on Wednesday, the end result is the same as it would be if the world’s superpowers ever started hunting and pecking the launch codes.

Rodgers, as multiple former Green Bay Packers are making clear, is destroying his reputation and legacy as a teammate by breaking the fundamental code of the locker room. But he’s also destroying Mike McCarthy’s reputation and legacy as a coach.

For a change, Rodgers’s rant was centered in reality. ESPNFL reports that the things Rodgers said about McCarthy are true.

Which could make it impossible for the Steelers to keep McCarthy beyond the 2026 season.

His only path to salvation is to win. Starting on Friday, when the 8-2 Broncos come to town for a game against a Steelers team that, despite the sudden dysfunction, is 6-4.

It’s really that simple. The next seven games will become the litmus test as to whether McCarthy’s now-documented snoring-and-ice-cream-sandwiches flaws are outweighed by his capabilities.

Of course, the fact that Rodgers rolled a grenade into the locker room on his way out won’t make that any easier. Which could be one of the reasons why Rodgers did it.

Maximum chaos will make it harder for McCarthy to win. Which will make it harder for McCarthy to make it to Year 2. Which will create the impression that Rodgers has been vindicated.

Sure, the initial word is that the situation doesn’t impact McCarthy’s standing. But the most important aspect of Wednesday’s report from ESPNFL that McCarthy “has the full support of the organization” carries an important qualifier.

The Steelers are “not planning to make any immediate changes.”

So they’re not firing McCarthy or asking him to resign before Friday’s game. What happens if the Steelers lose by double digits to the Broncos? What happens if the current two-game losing streak becomes an extended skid that sweeps them out of the AFC postseason field?

The answer will become obvious based on how the final seven games unfold. In the immediate aftermath of Aaron Rodgers dumping Agent Orange on the organization, it’s hard to imagine McCarthy surviving this.

Ultimately, the decision will be made by owner Art Rooney II. And the outcome could show where the Steelers reside on the line that divides the functional from dysfunctional franchises.

Functional teams admit mistakes and commence rectifying them. Dysfunctional teams deny the facts and double down.
