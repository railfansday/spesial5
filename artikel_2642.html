Jalen Suggs delivers ‘point blank’ message on Magic’s struggles at home

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Orlando Magic have slowly but surely been turning their season around. With Paolo Banchero still on the mend due to an ankle injury, Franz Wagner has taken it upon himself to lead the way for the Magic. They entered their Wednesday night contest against the Philadelphia 76ers having won four consecutive games, including a huge victory over the Boston Celtics back on Monday.

However, for the Magic an unstoppable force met an immovable object. And no, it wasn't the 76ers that was immovable. It was Orlando's continued woes at home. Heading into their home matchup against Philly, they only won one of their five outings in Kia Center, and they found no reprieve in their own floor on Wednesday as they suffered a 121-116 defeat.

Now 1-5 at home, Magic guard Jalen Suggs, who recently returned from a knee injury, said that they simply have to be better at defending homecourt.

"We've got to be better in here. Point blank, period. We're better in here, the energy will pick up in here. So, we've got to do our part first," Suggs said, per Jason Beede of The Orlando Sentinel.

"We come back home off of this trip, understand that we've got to do what needs to be done, execute, sustain it for a full 48 – that energy and like I said, understand that everybody will run with us if we do that. But until we do that, it's kind of hard to ask (for) any change."

Magic fans have grown restless over their team's performance at home, and Suggs knows that it's up to them to change the mood at Kia Center.

The Magic's only home win this season came against the putrid Milwaukee Bucks. To be fair to them, their five losses at home came against good teams (Atlanta Hawks, Minnesota Timberwolves, Celtics, and 76ers), although they also did lose to the struggling Washington Wizards.

Nonetheless, there is plenty of time left in the season to make Kia Center the fortress the Magic believe it can be.
