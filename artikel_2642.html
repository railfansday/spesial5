Bryce Young's 14 game-winning drives tied for most by any player 25 or younger since at least 1950

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Panthers quarterback Bryce Young showed a knack for leading game-winning drives while leading the Panthers to a division title last season and he’s gotten back into the groove the last couple of weeks.

Young led the Panthers to a game-winning field goal in the final 30 seconds against the Saints in Week 10 and then got them into position for another Ryan Fitzgerald kick with 1:41 to play against the Ravens on Sunday. The defense closed out the 35-33 win from there, which means that Young has now led 14 game-winning drives since entering the league in 2023.

That’s the second-most by any quarterback since Young entered the league and, per the NFL, he is tied with Dak Prescott and Justin Herbert for the most game-winning drives by any player 25 years of age or younger since at least 1950.

“Game on the line, nobody else I want with the ball,” Panthers head coach Dave Canales said Sunday, via the team’s website. “Bryce Young’s going to go there. He’s going to be, he’s going to be in control. He’s going to be focused, locked into the moment, and that’s what he looked like on that drive.”

Young was 39-of-60 for 454 yards, two touchdowns and an interception over the last two games and the wins have given the Panthers a two-game lead in the division heading into Week 12. If they can keep rolling against the Buccaneers next Sunday, another playoff berth will feel even likelier in Carolina.
