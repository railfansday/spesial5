Don’t be shocked if LSU actually gets kicked out of SEC

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

LSU may have finally pushed the SEC too far. Will Wade, the Tigers basketball coach, played RJ Luis Jr. in a game last week after a court ruled that the school could play him despite an earlier ruling by the conference. The SEC had ruled Luis ineligible because he signed a contract with an NBA team last year. Now Luis is planning to sue the SEC and the SEC is threatening to vote LSU out of the conference. Two very high-profile media members believe that the SEC could really do it this time.

Former LSU coach Brian Kelly appeared on SiriusXM this week and said that he believes they might do it this time. The SEC previously threatened to vote LSU out two months ago when Lane Kiffin and the Tigers football program had a contentious and public showdown about whether or not they would use Dae'Quan Wright and Zxavian Harris, two players who had been in training camp with NFL teams.

Kelly coached at LSU from 2022 to 2025 before he was fired. He's working for CBS as an analyst this season, along with some other odd jobs. There's plenty of reason to believe he is hearing things from people around the conference.

Another person who would definitely be hearing things is ESPN's Paul Finebaum who also says the threat to LSU is very real right now saying things had risen to the level of DEFCON-1.

"It violated egregious SEC policy," said Finebaum. "The commissioner and the presidents have talked about this before. Remember, they considered it during Kiffin. But this time there are enough votes. You need 11 out of 16. They have the votes to kick LSU out. There's a lot of murmur in this room to go ahead and do it. The consequences are massive. We have never seen anything quite like this before. Most people don't think it will happen, but something serious is going to happen."

LSU was one of the 13 founding members of the SEC back in 1932. Nearly a century later the conference will meet on Dec. 3 for a vote. It's starting to sound like they might chose the nuclear option.
