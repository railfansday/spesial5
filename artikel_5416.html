HMP Global Enters into Definitive Agreement to Be Acquired by TJC

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Investment partnership intended to support the next phase of growth in existing businesses, new offerings and acquisitions

MALVERN, Pa., Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- HMP Global, a healthcare events, education and engagement company dedicated to improving patient care, today announced that it has entered into a definitive agreement under which investment funds managed by TJC, L.P. ("TJC") will acquire a majority interest in the company. Susquehanna Growth Equity ("SGE"), HMP Global's partner since 2008, will retain a minority interest. The transaction is expected to close during the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to customary closing conditions and any required regulatory approvals. Financial terms of the transaction were not disclosed.

For more than four decades, HMP Global has connected healthcare professionals with the clinical knowledge, research and communities that help advance care. From its origins in medical publishing, the company has grown into a portfolio of established conferences, educational programs, clinical content and year-round engagement opportunities. Its brands include Psych Congress, NEI Congress, the Symposium on Advanced Wound Care (SAWC), Advances in IBD (AIBD), EMS World Expo and others. Through HMP Collective, the company delivers custom content, education and community engagement programs that connect healthcare companies with clinical audiences throughout the year.

"We have built HMP Global through trusted brands, strong clinical relationships and a team committed to improving patient care," said Jeff Hennessy Sr., Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of HMP Global. "We thank Scott Feldman, Amir Goldman, Sam Major, and the SGE team for helping us build the business we have today. TJC shares our ambition for what comes next. Our next chapter will include investment in our existing businesses, new offerings and acquisitions that expand our influence in healthcare education and communications."

"Jeff and his team have grown HMP Global and integrated acquisitions while keeping the mission of advancing patient care at the center of their focus," said Dan Williams, Partner and Head of Healthcare at TJC. "We are proud to partner with them to invest in new event launches, the continued growth of HMP Collective and acquisitions that extend HMP Global's reach to more healthcare professionals and the patients they serve."

"It has been a privilege to partner with Jeff and the HMP Global team as they have transformed the company into a leading healthcare events and education platform," said Scott Feldman, Managing Director, Susquehanna Growth Equity. "Their vision, commitment to the healthcare communities they serve, and ability to continually evolve the business have been central to that success. We believe there is tremendous opportunity ahead and look forward to continuing our partnership alongside TJC."

Following completion of the transaction, HMP Global will continue to be led by Jeff Hennessy Sr., Chairman and CEO, and its management team. The company will remain headquartered in Malvern, Pennsylvania.

Solomon Partners served as exclusive financial advisor to HMP Global in connection with the transaction. Troutman Pepper Locke served as legal counsel to HMP Global. Kirkland & Ellis LLP is serving as legal counsel to TJC.

About HMP GlobalHMP Global is the trusted leader in healthcare events, education and omnichannel engagement, with a mission to improve patient care by bringing the healthcare community together. For more than 40 years, the company has built trusted brands that healthcare professionals rely on throughout their careers, including Psych Congress, the nation's leading conference on practical psychopharmacology; the Symposium on Advanced Wound Care (SAWC), the world's largest wound care meeting; EMS World Expo; NEI; LINC; and Advances in IBD. Today, HMP Global's portfolio includes 27 brands and 83 events that connect more than 2 million healthcare professionals with more than 6,000 leading experts through live experiences, accredited education, year-round engagement through HMP Collective, and first-party clinician intelligence powered by Viia, creating one of the largest healthcare engagement ecosystems.
