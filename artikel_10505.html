Thanksgiving gas prices: See a map that shows how much you will pay at the pump in all 50 states

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Thanksgiving travel season has arrived. Though most people won’t be checking routes and traffic conditions until tomorrow—typically the busiest travel day of the holiday week—the question of how much it will cost to fill up their tank for the trip has likely already crossed their mind.

That’s where some good news and bad news come in. The good news is that gas prices have been falling recently. The bad news is that this Thanksgiving period may be the most expensive for gas prices on record. Here’s what you need to know.

The most expensive Thanksgiving on record?

Anyone with a car knows gas prices have been one of the biggest economic burdens of 2026. Thanks mostly to the continuing war in Iran, oil prices have surged, which has directly correlated with skyrocketing prices at the pump.

In May, the national average topped $4.50 per gallon of regular unleaded. Thankfully, prices have fallen since then, and today’s national average price for a gallon of regular unleaded is down to $3.61.

That’s 17 cents lower than the average of $3.78 a week ago, and 69 cents lower than the national average of $4.30 a month ago.

That’s all good news. But unfortunately, there’s some bad news too.

People drive a lot during Thanksgiving week as they go to and from their festive destinations. That means they fill up at the pump a lot. And this Thanksgiving week, gas prices are expected to exceed or be near Thanksgiving records.

In a November 19 post, the American Automobile Association (AAA) noted that national average gas prices have been dropping as Thanksgiving approaches. On November 19, the national average was $3.72—eleven cents higher than today’s average.

However, the organization also noted that unless gas prices dropped another 15 cents from that average, this Thanksgiving Day would see the highest national average gas prices on record.

That milestone is currently held by Thanksgiving Day 2022 (November 24). On that day, the national average was $3.58 per gallon.

If today’s national average of $3.61 does not drop another 3 cents before Thursday, this Thanksgiving will have the highest gas prices ever.

And even if it drops below the 2022 record, it will still be significantly higher than Thanksgiving Day gas prices last year, when the national average hovered around $3.10 per gallon for regular unleaded.

Map shows gas price average in all 50 states

Of course, the national average is just that—an average of gas prices across the country. The price you will actually pay at the pump depends heavily on the state and area in which you live.

Currently, AAA says national retail prices for a gallon of gas range from $2.97 in some lucky states to highs of more than $5.58 in others.

To quickly find your state’s average price for a gallon of regular unleaded, you can check out AAA’s interactive fuel price map. 

Hover your cursor over a state to see its statewide average. States are shaded from deep blue to dark red. The deeper the blue, the lower the statewide average. The darker the red, the higher the statewide average.

Which states have the highest and lowest gas prices?

The price at the pump fluctuates by the hour, but according to AAA’s most recent data for November 24, some of the states with the highest average gas prices include:

  • California: $5.43
  • Nevada: $4.67
  • Washington: $4.63

States that can boast having among the lowest average prices are:

  • Indiana: $2.97
  • North Carolina: $2.97
  • Ohio: $3.00
