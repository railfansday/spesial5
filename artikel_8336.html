Winter Hiking Gift Guide (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Winter is a great time to gear up and head out for cold-weather hiking, mountaineering, or snowshoeing. The bugs are gone, and so are the crowds, leaving the mountains and the hills to those who can’t live without their weekly dose of sun and snow. Here are 20 expert gift recommendations for the winter hiker and backpacker in your life.

Drinking water will freeze in winter unless you do something to prevent it. The best way to carry water in winter is to fill wide-mouth Nalgene bottles with boiling water and carry them upside down (so the caps don’t freeze) in an external insulated water bottle pocket, like the Hyperlite Mountain Gear Insulator, so they stay warm longer.

Glove leashes keep your winter gloves or mittens safely attached to your wrists. When you take them off to use your camera, check your GPS app, or adjust gear, the gloves dangle by the strap instead of falling into the snow or getting lost.

The Black Diamond Alpine Start Hoody is a highly breathable, lightweight softshell jacket that works well as a wind shirt and protects against light precipitation. Ideal for cold-weather pursuits, it offers excellent breathability without the weight of a heavier technical shell. Made with Schoeller stretch-woven fabric, it’s ideal for dynamic activities like winter hiking, mountaineering, ice climbing, or XC skiing. You want a highly breathable wind layer with an adjustable helmet-compatible hood, stretch cuffs, and a zippered chest pocket. Available for men and women.

You can wear the Polar Buff as a helmet liner, balaclava, mask, scarf, hood, or hat for warmth where you need it. One side features an original Buff, while the other adds a layer of Polartec fleece to help reduce heat loss. Super warm and comfortable, this item’s value comes from its versatility in winter conditions.
