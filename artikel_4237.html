Cadillac’s luxury 3-row EV just got more expensive, now starting at over $80,000

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Cadillac’s three-row electric SUV just got a little more expensive. The 2027 Cadillac Vistiq now starts at over $80,000.

The Vistiq sits between the massive Escalade IQ and Lyriq as a “mini Escalade,” if you will, offering three rows of seating, but in a smaller, more affordable package.

Just a month ago, the 2027 Cadillac Vistiq was available starting at $79,495, but GM quietly raised prices.

As GM Authority uncovered on Cadillac’s website, prices for the base 2027 Vistiq Luxury are now listed from $80,395, or $900 more than last month. In fact, the Luxury, Premium Luxury, and Platinum models are all $900 more, while the Sport trim got a $1,000 price increase.

While $900 won’t break the bank on a luxury three-row SUV, the cheapest 2027 Cadillac Vistiq Luxury trim now starts at over $80,000.

Like all 2027 GM electric vehicles, the Vistiq now has a built-in NACS charge port, so you don’t need a separate adapter to use at Tesla Superchargers.

The 2027 Cadillac Vistiq is available in four trims: Luxury, Sport, Premium Luxury, and Platinum. All are powered by a dual-motor all-wheel-drive (AWD) powertrain that delivers a combined 615 hp and 650 lb-ft of torque.

Cadillac’s three-row electric SUV uses a 102 kWh battery, offering an EPA-estimated driving range of up to 305 miles on a single charge.

All trims come with a 33″ Horizon Display, Power driver and front passenger massage seats, a premium 23-speaker AKG Studio audio system, and a Power SkyGlass roof as standard.
