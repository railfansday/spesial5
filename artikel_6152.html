17 Aircraft Spotted In GTA 6’s Trailers, From Boeing 747s To Biplanes

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

13 years after the launch of its predecessor, Grand Theft Auto VI launches on November 19, cementing its place in history as one of the most eagerly awaited video games of all time. Rockstar's open-world extravaganza is set in a fictionalized version of the US state of Florida, and since the first trailers dropped, eagle-eyed fans have been trying to find whatever small details they can.

For an avgeek, one of the most interesting aspects of the trailers is the wide range of aircraft on show. Planes and helicopters have long played a key role in video games in the Grand Theft Auto series, with some even letting players fly them: who could forget the near-impossible challenge of stealing a fighter jet in GTA V!? Across the trailers, there are 17 aircraft sightings (including some duplicates).

Based on what is seen in the trailers, GTA VI looks set to include an interesting range of fixed-wing jets. On the airliner side of things, GTA Base highlights the fact that a double-decker quadjet that is clearly based on the Boeing 747-400 and/or 747-8 can be seen. The site lists this as being simply known as 'Jet' in the game's files. A separate clip shows an unidentified large airliner or freighter during a heist.

Private jets, however, are more detailed and numerous. GTA Base notes that the game will feature three 'Buckingham' private jets: the Luxor, the Shamal (both based on the Learjet 45), and the Nimbus (based on the Cessna Citation X).

Regardless of (or perhaps thanks to) its aviation diversity, GTA VI already tops Metacritic's 'best games of all time list with a score of 99, according to a critic review.

"Grand Theft Auto VI was worth waiting 13 years for, and will no doubt be one of the most influential games over the next decade."

There is also a considerable degree of diversity in the light aircraft and floatplanes visible in GTA VI's trailers. Once again, these are fictionalized versions of the aircraft rather than the real thing, but they draw inspiration from the kinds of aircraft you can see in Florida today. As seen above, one is a floatplane with similar proportions to the Cessna Caravan or De Havilland DHC-3 Otter.

Another trailer shows a floatplane that is reminiscent of the De Havilland DHC-2 Beaver. This is known in the game, per GTA Base, as the Mammoth Dodo. Among the wheeled planes, a light aircraft reminiscent of the Cessna 173 Skyhawk is also briefly visible in the trailers, with GTA Base noting that it is known in the game as the JoBuilt Mammatus. A banner-towing plane also overflies the beach.

The latter of these is a classic aviation scene in Florida (and, indeed, coastal cities in the US more generally). Similarly, a wingwalker can be seen on a biplane that is known in the game as the Western Duster, but seems to be derived from the real-life Stearman Model 75. GTA Base also highlights the Western Mallard (based on the Extra EA-300) and the Mammoth Streamer 216 (based on the DHC-6 Twin Otter).

The lounge also has options for food and beverages.

Alongside the classic blimp, based on the Goodyear Aerospace GZ-20, several helicopters also appear in GTA VI's trailers. As seen above, one resembles an MD 500 or OH-6 'Little Bird.' Elsewhere, Airbus AS350 and Bell 206-type light helicopters are also visible, as well as one that looks like an Airbus EC135 or Bo 105 with twin skids. There are also the classic generic police-style helicopters.

With earlier video games in the Grand Theft Auto series having also had flyable aircraft, avgeeks and gamers alike certainly have a lot to look forward to on this front. There are likely more to be discovered, as we haven't even scratched the surface on the 27-minute 'Extended Look' video. Either way, as GTA VI goes live on November 19, it's fair to say that, in the skies and on the ground, it promises a lot.
