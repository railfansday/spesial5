Alaska Atmos Rewards Business Card Bonus: 90K Points Welcome Offer

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The $195 annual fee Atmos™ Rewards Visa Signature® Business Card currently has a huge bonus of 90,000 Atmos Rewards points after spending $6,000 within the first 90 days. For context, this is the business card of the Atmos Rewards portfolio, the loyalty program of Alaska and Hawaiian. This is a potentially lucrative card that has just undergone a refresh, and you can read my Atmos Rewards Business Card review here.

In this post I’d like to go over the details of the Atmos Rewards Business Card bonus, for anyone who may be considering applying. There are lots of reasons to get this card, particularly the incredible bonus.

Link: Learn more about the Atmos™ Rewards Visa Signature® Business Card with 90K bonus points

The Atmos Rewards Business Card currently has a welcome offer of 90,000 Atmos Rewards bonus points after spending $6,000 within the first 90 days.

Based on my latest miles & points valuations, I consider Atmos Rewards points to be worth 1.5 cents each, meaning the 90,000 points are worth $1,350 to me, which is among the best bonuses out there. There are lots of great uses of Atmos Rewards points, for travel on Alaska, Hawaiian, oneworld partners, and non-alliance partners.

Atmos Rewards points have all kinds of uses, ranging from booking short haul one-way economy awards on American for 4,500 points, to booking Starlux business class one-way awards from the US to Asia for 75,000 points. Of course capacity controls apply, so patience and flexibility will be required to get the best deal. Still, I find this to be one of the most useful points currencies out there.

The Atmos Rewards Business Card 90,000-point offer is the best publicly available sign-up bonus that we’ve seen on the card since it launched in August 2025 (which is when the Atmos Rewards program formally launched). How do the previous Atmos Rewards Business Card bonuses compare?

As you can see, the Atmos Rewards Business Card sign-up bonus has varied from 70,000 points to 90,000 points, and the current bonus of 90,000 points is the best publicly available offer we’ve seen. So if there has ever been a time to apply for this card, this is it.

When it comes to getting approved for the Atmos Rewards Business Card, what rules should you be aware of? There are the card specific policies, and then also the more generic Bank of America policies. When you pull up the application, you’ll see the following terms:

This one-time promotion is limited to company level Business Card clients opening a new account in response to this offer and will not apply to requests to convert existing accounts.

The good news is that eligibility for the Atmos Rewards Business Card is considered completely independently of any other cards in the portfolio, so you’re eligible if you have either of the personal Atmos Rewards credit cards. Many people even report being eligible for the Atmos Rewards Business Card (including the bonus) if they’ve had it in the past.

Beyond that, Bank of America doesn’t have many consistent restrictions when it comes to getting approved for business cards. While Bank of America has the 2/3/4 rule and 3/12 rules, those apply to personal cards, and not business cards. Generally those with a Bank of America banking relationship also have the best odds of approval (though it’s by no means a requirement).

There’s not a consistent credit score you need to have to be approved for this card, though personally I’d hope to have a score in the “good” to “excellent” range, ideally 700+ points. However, people with lower scores have been approved, and people with higher scores have been rejected.

Applying for this card won’t count as a further card toward Chase’s 5/24 rule, in case that’s a limit you’re trying to stay under.

Beyond the great bonus, there are multiple reasons to consider the $195 annual fee Atmos Rewards Business Card:

If you fly Alaska or Hawaiian with any frequency, then having this card should be a no-brainer. The benefits really add up, between the lounge passes, the first checked bag free, preferred boarding, savings on inflight purchases, and more.

The Atmos Rewards Business Card has introduced a best-ever welcome offer, whereby you can earn 90,000 Atmos Rewards points upon completing minimum spending.

This is an exceptional bonus, and this card is absolutely worth picking up for the points, especially given that eligibility isn’t mutually exclusive with other Atmos Rewards cards. In the long run, the card can be worth it for the perks for travel on Alaska and Hawaiian.

Anecdotally I find Bank of America business cards to be quite easy to get approved for, including if you’ve had the card in the past. Applying for this card also shouldn’t count as a further card toward the Chase 5/24 limit, if that’s of concern to you.
