It's a fair question OP. Concentration risk is pretty high these days, even when the CEO isn't keeping company with a Chinese spy. I think we also have this dynamic of "superstar CEOs" where they tend to make their own headlines and manufacture their own hype. When things go pear shaped they tend to do so spectacularly.

But to me equal weighting is a cure that's worse than the disease. In the S&P we'd have every company with a 1/500th of the capitalization so for Apple that's perhaps 40 times less with a similar-ish multiple the other way for the smallest. To me that's a MASSIVE active decision. My guess is it'll work well in certain periods and poorly a bit more often.

But to me, I try to ignore factors and market timing and a lot of other things that may have their time in the sun. I'm just not good at predicting what's gonna happen so I don't try. Good luck!
