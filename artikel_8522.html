Upwork Review – Pitch for Jobs, Budget for Fees & Connects

Join Upwork if you have in-demand skills and will write a tailored proposal for each job, since its clients post work and freelancers bid for it. Budget for the 0% to 15% fee on each contract and for Connects, the credits every proposal costs whether or not you’re hired.

Upwork is a freelance marketplace, one of the quickest ways to earn from a skill you already have, where clients post jobs and freelancers win them with proposals. If a $1,000 project’s fee is 10% and winning it took 10 proposals at 16 Connects each, you keep $876 (our math) after the $100 fee and $24 in Connects, the 15-cent credits you spend on proposals.

Upwork pays best for freelancers with in-demand skills who write a tailored proposal for each job. Freelancers who’d rather list a packaged service for buyers to order should look at Fiverr, which is built around those listings and keeps 20% of each order.
