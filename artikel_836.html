Who was back to his best? Player ratings for Crystal Palace v Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Wirtz scored his first Premier League goal of the season

Alexis Mac Allister, Florian Wirtz and Alexander Isak scored as Liverpool won at Crystal Palace and in doing so, increased the pressure on Pierre Sage, the Palace boss.

Here's how I rated the players and how you rated the players.

Liverpool win at Palace to pile pressure on Sage

Dean Henderson: Made a couple of good saves though was well beaten for all three goals. 5

Jefferson Lerma: Initially battled well against the visitors frontline but Liverpool's quality was too much. 4

Chris Richards: Part of a backline that had their work cut out and ultimately conceded three goals that were far too easy. 4

Axel Disasi: Like Lerma and Richards, kept Alexander Isak quiet for most the game but punished in the end. 4

Oscar Mingueza: The Spaniard was good on the ball but perhaps didn't push forward enough. 5

Adam Wharton: Tidy in possession and had a glancing header saved by Alisson though wasn't able to dictate the tempo of the game. 5

Quinten Timber: Lost Mac Allister for the first goal. Played some good deliveries but his decision-making was poor. Replaced on the hour mark. 4

Tyrick Mitchell: Was really good going forward and found Pino who hit the bar, before teeing up Kamada, who forced a save from Alisson. 6

Daichi Kamada: Nearly scored with a solid volley but on the whole, didn't do enough to challenge Kerkez down Liverpool's left. 5

Yeremy Pino: One of the few positives for Palace. Hit the woodwork in the first half and constantly posed Liverpool problems with his quality. Unlucky not to score. 7

Jorgen Strand Larsen: Had a couple of openings in the first-half and did ask questions of Liverpool's backline. 5

Osorio (on 60, Timber): Failed to really have an impact. 5

Nketiah (on 72, Strand Larsen): Missed a good chance but scored from the spot. 6

Subs not used: Benitez, Canvot, Chilwell, Doucoure, Hughes.

Alisson: Made a stunning reaction save to deny Kamada, shortly before Liverpool took the lead, and plenty of crucial saves after. Back to his best. 9

Ronald Araujo: Stuggled against Tyrick Mitchell and Yeremy Pino and picked up an early booking but was involved in the second goal. 6

Virgil van Dijk: Made a solid block to deny Jorgen Strand Larsen and was solid but will be annoyed at Liverpool's failure to keep a clean sheet. 8

Jeremy Jacquet: Much better defensively after having a poor game against Fenerbahce. Nearly scored just before half-time. 7

Milos Kerkez: Constantly pushed up but his final delivery wasn't good enough. Dealt well with the threat posed by Kamada and Mingueza. Conceded the penalty. 6

Alexis Mac Allister: Came close with a header early on and shortly after, scored his first Premier League goal of the season with a great header. 7

Dominik Szoboszlai: Got the assist for Mac Allister and was influential, particularly with his passing range. Could have had three assists in the first-half alone. 7

Cody Gakpo: Unlucky with his pull-back early on and always ensured Liverpool had an outlet down the flanks. 6

Florian Wirtz: Pressed really well and after an average first-half, finished nicely for his first Premier League goal of the season. Got the assist for Isak too. 8

Bradley Barcola: Looked dangerous but missed a sitter when he blazed over from close range. Got the assist for Wirtz after a good run down the right. 7

Alexander Isak: Looked on it from the outset. Made a smart run for Wirtz's goal and persisted well to get his goal late on. Now has seven in the league this season 7

Nyoni (on 69, Szoboszlai): Didn't put a foot wrong. 6

Munoz (on 69, Barcola): Booked for diving. 5

Subs not used: Mamardashvili, Endo, Tsimikas, McConnell.

After the opportunity to rate players has closed, the score displayed represents the average from all the submissions by BBC Sport users.
