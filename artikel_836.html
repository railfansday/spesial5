Rob Kardashian Gives Glimpse Into Bond With Daughter Dream

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Rob Kardashian celebrated his daughter Dream—who he shares with ex Blac Chyna—turning 10 years old on Nov. 10 with a sweet pajama party.

Experiencing fatherhood is Rob Kardashian's dream.

As such, the Keeping Up alum made sure his daughter Dream Kardashian, shared with ex Blac Chyna, woke up on the right side of the bed for her 10th birthday with a cozy pajama party Nov. 10.

"Happy golden Birthday to my Dream girl," Rob wrote on Instagram, referencing the one year when a person's age and date of birth align. "I can’t believe 10 years has gone by already goodness Lord please slow down time."

And the 39-year-old kicked off his pre-teen's day with a sweet gesture: a donut cake that perfectly matched the birthday girl's sleepwear, covered in colorful candles.

In addition to the festive feast, Rob pampered his 10-year-old with flowers and giant gold balloons for the occasion he coined "DREAM Day."

"Being your dad is the greatest gift of my life!" the reality star continued. "You make me proud every single day! I love you more than you’ll ever know!!"

And Dream's celebration was a big deal for both of her parents. Joining in on cherishing the milestone, Chyna replied to Rob's tribute, "Happy birthday to our beautiful girl. Thank you Robert."

In addition, Khloe Kardashian chimed in to compliment her niece, writing, "She’s the best girl ever," while grandmother Kris Jenner sent her love through three blue heart emojis.

The family affair comes as Rob and Chyna—who is also mom to 14-year-old son King Cairo Stevenson with ex Tyga—continue to prioritize co-parenting nearly a decade after their contentious split.

The Rob & Chyna stars shared a look at their cordial relationship on an Oct. 24 livestream where Rob revealed his plans to take their daughter pumpkin painting at Khloe's Halloween party during his weekend with Dream.

"Paint me one," Chyna, 38, joked about making her youngest's art project a self-portrait. "Put some piercings in the face for me and put a bang on it and some long nails."
