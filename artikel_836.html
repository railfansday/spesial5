Tiësto Co-Founded Unlimited Productions Opens First U.S. Offices

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The company launched its New York offices and announced plans to open an L.A.-based operation in 2027.

Unlimited Productions, the production company originally co-founded by DJ and music producer Tiësto, opened its first U.S. offices, it announced on Monday (Nov. 9). Located in New York, the new office is overseen by managing director and partner Jan Maarten Luijnenburg.

The company also announced plans to open an office on the West Coast in early 2027.

The expansion follows Unlimited Productions’ recent work in the U.S., where the company serves as the production director for Elements Music & Arts Festival in Long Pond, Penn. Unlimited built two stage designs for the festival, the Fire and Earth stages, and also redesigned the mainstage at HiJinx Festival in Philadelphia.

“For 20 years we’ve worked one way: we own every discipline, we draw every bolt before we arrive on site, and we treat our vendors as partners, not suppliers,” Luijnenburg said in a statement. “This year in the U.S. proved that model travels, from building Martin Garrix’s touring rig, designing the new HiJinx shows to bringing brand-new stages to Elements. No is not an answer for us. That’s where we do our best work.”

The launch of the New York office expands Unlimited Production’s footprint in the U.S., while the company’s European headquarters are located in Breda, Netherlands.

Unlimited Productions also built the production for Tiësto’s performance at the Pyramids of Giza in December 2025.

Jack van Deursen and Olivier Monod de Froideville first began the company, which was then named Livetime Productions, in 2005. It expanded into Unlimited Productions in 2009 with Bart Roelen and Tijs Verwest (better known under his stage name, Tiësto), and now serves as an “architect, general contractor and site supervisor” for shows and festivals, according to a press release.
