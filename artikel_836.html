Life With Derek Star Says Actors “Leaned Into” Stepsibling Romance

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Life With Derek’s Michael Seater revealed that he and costar Ashley Leggat were not only aware of romance rumors between their stepsibling characters, but they “leaned into” it on screen.

Nearly 15 years after Life With Derek concluded, the actor behind the titular Derek Venturi in show admitted he and onscreen stepsister Ashley Leggat—a.k.a. Casey McDonald—leaned into their characters’ flirty tension while filming the blended family comedy.

“We had an undeniable chemistry that the fans loved,” Seater recalled during a recent appearance on the Back to the Best podcast. “Enemies to lovers? Whatever the trope is? So, fans wanted that and we were like, ‘Well they’re step siblings so technically…’”

Seater and Leggat played not only stepsiblings, but characters who were always at odds with each other. However, while the Canadian sitcom was airing—it aired from 2005 to 2009 on the Family Channel in Canada in the Disney Channel in the U.S. before ending with a 2010 movie—many fans picked up on how the constant fire between their characters could easily burn into a romance.

And the actors didn’t let that speculation go unnoticed.

“In these early days of the internet, our producers don’t know there’s like forums online where people are talking but we do,” the 39-year-old explained. “So, we saw that the fans were talking about it. We knew that the fans liked it, so we leaned into it without letting the writers or producers know that we were leaning into it.”

And while Seater and Leggat, 40, were never actually romantically involved on the show, the showrunners and writers had to step in to keep them apart. As he shared of one take, “We’d be like sitting on a couch and they’d be like, ‘Can you maybe separate yourselves a little on the couch?’”

Of course, Seater stressed that while he loved the ‘will they, won’t they?’ that existed between Derek and Casey, the duo didn’t want the stepsiblings to canonically be together.

“The thing is, won’t they,” Seater continued, “but we’d tease them a little.”

Case in point: Remember that final scene in the finale where Derek appears to seduce Casey by emphasizing their stepsibling status? Seater teased, “I know what I’m doing there.”

Behind the camera, however, Seater and Leggat—who reprised their roles in the 2023 TV movie Life with Luca—have never been anything but great friends and scene partners.

“Ashley’s always my favorite scene partner honestly because I could always do anything,” he mused. “You felt so safe. In comedy, you want to be able to try stuff and play around and it’s not always going to land and if you have a scene partner that’s like, ‘Ew I don’t know about that. That wasn’t great.’ It makes you afraid to try things. It makes you self-conscious.”

As he put it, “We just always had each other’s backs and we were always just going with it.”
