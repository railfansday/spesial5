Open Western State Bank Online Savings if you have at least $1,000 to start and won’t move money often. It pays 4.20% on every balance, with no promotional rate to run out. The catches are possible fees past 10 withdrawals a statement cycle and a $20,000 limit on online transfers every 30 days.

Western State Bank is a North Dakota community bank founded in 1902, and this savings account is the only one it opens online, for U.S. citizens 18 or older. The $1,000 to open isn’t a fee but your first deposit, and it earns the same rate as every dollar you add, insured by the FDIC to at least $250,000, the same as at a big bank.

Savers with $1,000 or more who move money a few times a year get a top rate here and a banker they can call. Anyone starting smaller, or withdrawing more than 10 times a statement cycle, is better off with an account that needs nothing to open and charges nothing per withdrawal.
