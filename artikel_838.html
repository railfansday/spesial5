Dakota Johnson, Machine Gun Kelly Hit a High Note With Dinner Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Dakota Johnson and Machine Gun Kelly stepped out for a dinner date in Calabasas, Calif., on Nov. 3, continuing to fuel romance rumors with another public outing.

There’s nothing grey about Dakota Johnson and Machine Gun Kelly’s chemistry

That’s because the Madame Web actress and “blood valentine” artist added more fuel to relationship speculation when they enjoyed another joint outing together at a restaurant in Calabasas, Calif., on Nov. 3.

The pair—who first sparked romance rumors in September—were all smiles, grinning at one another as they waited for their car at valet outside the establishment.

For their dinner date, Johnson kept her look effortlessly chic, wearing a black leather jacket with a fur collar with a pair of light washed jeans. She styled her dark locks in a loose updo with a claw clip. To accessorize, the Fifty Shades of Grey star made a statement with an animal-print handbag.

As for MGK (real name Colson Baker), he rocked a casual ensemble, sporting salmon pink sweatpants and a dark gray shirt, with a hoodie and black bomber jacket on top.

Although she’s created a web of conversation with her love life, Johnson—who previously dated Coldplay’s Chris Martin from 2017 until 2025—jokingly alluded that she may have a type when it comes to musicians.

While Vogue pointed out in a Sept. 18 interview that the Suspiria star does “know a lot of musicians,” she quipped, “They like me. I don’t know what to say.”

After Johnson noted that she “like[s] them” too, the interviewer asked, “What’s not to like?” to which the Bigger Splash actress had a cheeky remark ready to go.

“A lot! A lot,” she joked. “Tricky motherf--kers.”
