SFA leads tributes to former president Peat

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Former Scottish FA president George Peat has died at the age of 86.

The former Airdrieonians and Stenhousemuir director spent four years in the role, taking over from John McBeth in 2007.

He oversaw the appointments of Scotland managers George Burley and Craig Levein and commissioned former first minister Henry McLeish to conduct a review of Scottish football.

Peat was also at the helm when Scottish referees opted to take strike action in November 2010.

"George loved his football and was renowned as a forthright personality and formidable negotiator but behind that imposing exterior lay a sharp wit and a glint in his eye," said current SFA president Mike Mulraney.

"He cared deeply about the national game and his beloved Airdrieonians.

"George was also passionate about the modernisation of the association and commissioned the McLeish Review of Scottish football as one of his first acts as president.

"He was a regular visitor to Hampden, even as his health deteriorated, and will be sadly missed by his friends and former colleagues at the Scottish FA and across the game."
