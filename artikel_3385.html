ScreenRant’s 2026/27 Winter TV Preview: An Exclusive Look At Over 50 Upcoming Shows

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

There's no shortage of notable TV shows arriving during this winter. Check out ScreenRant's official preview, featuring exclusive images of 50+ shows.

Saying goodbye to 2026 also means the winter season is almost here. In the past, the fall used to be the most important season for TV, ushering in the year's biggest series, but that's no longer the case. Major TV shows release throughout the year, including the winter months, and ScreenRant is here with exclusive looks at more than 50 of those new and returning titles coming in December, January, and February.

While not all series releasing between December 2026 and February 2027 are included, we have previews for many of the biggest titles set to release in the coming months. You will find titles linked to massive franchises like Star Wars, Marvel, One Piece, and even Garfield, as well as intriguing new series, spinoffs of past hits, long-awaited reboots, and a few Christmas specials.

Though streaming platforms such as Netflix, Prime Video, and Paramount+ will be frequently mentioned in this winter preview, we also have plenty to feature for fans of network TV. In addition to shows making their debut in early 2027, we also have exclusive looks at shows making their midseason returns this January.

Kicking off the December slate of new releases on Netflix is a new thriller titled Trinity. The series centers on a naval commander (Gugu Mbatha-Raw) who gets pulled into a dangerous conspiracy tied to a top-secret program after becoming close to the Secretary of Defense (Richard Madden). Trinity comes from Jed Mercurio, the creator of Bodyguard, which also featured Madden in a lead role. The Netflix series also stars Marcia Gay Harden, JD Pardo, Bruce Greenwood, and James Remar in key roles.

Apple TV's comedy crime drama, Bad Monkey, is back for its sophomore season this December. Vince Vaughn returns as Andrew Yancy, the former detective who finds himself involved in another dangerous case between rival criminal organizations. In addition to dealing with a high-profile murder and a new crime boss played by John Malkovich, Vaughn's character is also facing personal challenges with his father and romantic life. While the first season was based on Carl Hiaasen's novel, Bad Monkey season 2 is an original story.

Jane Austen's 1813 classic novel is getting a new adaptation on Netflix with Pride and Prejudice. Arriving within the first few days of December, the limited series comes from writer Dolly Alderton and features a star-studded ensemble cast. While Emma Corrin and Jack Lowden will play leads, Elizabeth Bennet and Mr. Darcy, the rest of the cast includes names such as Olivia Colman, Rufus Sewell​​​, Louis Partridge, Freya Mavor, Fiona Shaw, and Daryl McCormack​​​​.

Fraggle Rock was first rebooted on Apple TV in 2022, and the series is getting its third Christmas special to date with the release of Fraggle Rock: A Holiday Gift this December. When the holiday spirit starts to diminish in Fraggle Rock after a beloved snow globe is taken, Wembley, who is serving as the Fraggle in charge of holiday cheer, must step up to return it to its rightful owner. Fraggle Rock: A Holiday Gift will also feature a guest appearance by singer/actress Nicole Scherzinger​​​​.

Katori Hall's award-winning series P-Valley is finally back this December with nine episodes that will serve as the series' final installment. The Starz drama that last aired in 2022 is gearing up to say goodbye, but there's plenty of story left to tell for the residents of Chucalissa ​​​​​​and the drama that comes when a new casino opens in the area. While the casino will bring new faces, P-Valley season 3 will still be heavily focused on what's next for Uncle Clifford (Nicco Annan), Lil’ Murda (J. Alphonse Nicholson), Roulette (Gail Bean), Mercedes (Brandee Evans), and Keyshawn (Shannon Thornton).

Only Murders in the Building season 6 is going international, with Cinda Canning's (Tina Fey) murder sending Charles (Steve Martin), Oliver (Martin Short), and Mabel (Selena Gomez) to London for the investigation. Under the title of Only Murders in London for the upcoming installment, the hit Hulu series is embracing its new setting. Among season 6's guest stars are well-known UK figures, such as Olivia Colman, Simone Ashley, Nicola Coughlan, David Tennant, Jim Broadbent, Martin Freeman, Jodie Whittaker, and Geri Halliwell-Horner.

Maxton Hall - The World Between Us returns for its third and final season in early December with six brand-new episodes. The upcoming installment will follow Ruby (Harriet Herbig-Matten) dealing with the fallout of her suspension and affair scandal as her boyfriend James (Damian Hardung) helps set the record straight. The German romance series, which is based on Mona Kasten's novel Save Me, has been a global hit for Prime Video, and it looks like there will be plenty of drama for the show's concluding chapter.

Following the release of a Halloween special at the end of October, the supernatural sitcom is releasing another holiday-themed hour-long episode this December. The Ghosts Christmas special, titled Ghostmas: A Died Hard Special, releases on December 10, and it will be inspired by Die Hard, the 1988 action film that is still a topic of debate about whether it should be considered a Christmas movie. The episode will specifically see Utkarsh Ambudkar's Jay transform into the sitcom's version of John McClane after several ghosts are held hostage.

Traditional episodes for Ghosts season 6 will return January 21, 2027.

Based on the beloved comic strip by Jim Davis, Garfield is coming in the form of a new 2-D animated series on Paramount+. The show, co-produced by Nickelodeon Animation Studio, marks the first animated project for the franchise since Garfield and Friends concluded in 1994. In this new iteration, Lamorne Morris is voicing the sarcastic feline, with Joe Brogie taking on the voice of Jon Arbuckle. Aside from appearances by notable Garfield characters, like Odie, Arlene, and Nermal, the Paramount+ series will introduce plenty of newcomers.

A new coming-of-age series titled Sunshine Girls is making its debut in mid-December on Netflix. The show will center on Alma Marian​​​​'s Olivia, a teen who makes the cross-country move from New York City to a California suburb following her parents' divorce. During her freshman year at a new school, Olivia quickly connects with classmates Taylor (Isabella Fowler) and Riley (Erin Rose Park), who happens to be an influencer. Speaking of influencers, Sunshine Girls will feature appearances by several real-life influencers, including Déjà Clark, PresLee Faith, Kaido Roberts, Kylie Castillo, and Nina Castillo.

The reality competition series from Jimmy Donaldson, aka MrBeast, is returning for a third season this December on Prime Video. The season, titled Beast Games: World Champions, will feature competitors from around the globe, representing 196 nations, taking part in various challenges, giving one team a chance to win $5,000,000. Along with new challenges, Prime Video has also teased the Roman Colosseum global stage that was used for live audience members.

Freida Pinto stars in Netflix's upcoming family drama titled Unaccustomed Earth that follows a woman who moves her family from India to Cambridge, Massachusetts following a major cancer diagnosis. Not only does Parul Chaudhury (Pinto) return to a life she once sacrificed, but she also reconnects with a past lover, a relationship that ends up impacting her entire family. Unaccustomed Earth is notably based on a short story collection of the same name by author Jhumpa Lahiri and will also star Indraneil Sengupta, Iyla Sundarsingh McKaig, Siddharth​​​​​​, and Justin Bartha.

The next entry in the Ted franchise is coming this December with Ted: The Animated Series. The upcoming series serves as a sequel to the movies, taking place 12 years after the events of Ted 2. Though Ted and John both have their own families, they are still close friends and even live next door to each other. In addition to Seth MacFarlane returning to voice Ted, several members of the movies are back to voice their animated counterparts, including Mark Wahlberg (John), Amanda Seyfried (Sam), and Jessica Barth (Tami-Lynn). Peacock has also confirmed more episodes will debut in 2027.

After making his debut in Tulsa King, Samuel L. Jackson's Russell Lee Washington Jr. is set to lead his own spinoff in Frisco King. While Taylor Sheridan has a history of developing spinoffs, Tulsa King is the first show outside the Yellowstone universe to get an expansion. The series, which also stars Asa Germann​​​, Kai Caster, Lilah Pate, and Savanna Gann, focuses on Russell trying to build a new criminal empire after relocating to Frisco, Texas. Sylvester Stallone's Tulsa King character, Dwight Manfredi, will also make an appearance.

Emily in Paris will bid adieu with the upcoming release of its sixth season, which will also be its last. The romantic comedy-drama first debuted in 2020 and turned into a lasting hit for the streamer. The final chapter slated to drop on Christmas Eve will see Lily Collins' Emily Cooper facing decisions that will shape her future from both a professional and personal standpoint after a trip to Greece results in a surprise run-in with Gabriel (Lucas Bravo). Emily in Paris season 6 will also see the return of major characters Mindy (Ashley Park), Sylvia (Philippine Leroy-Beaulieu), Lucien (Alfie), Princess Jane (Minnie Driver), Luc (Bruno Gouery), Julien (Samuel Arnold), and Antoine (William Abadie).

Prime Video's acclaimed docuseries One Night in Idaho is getting a three-part follow-up with the release of One Night in Idaho: Catching a Killer this December. Whereas the original docuseries focused on the four victims of the 2022 killings of University of Idaho students–Madison Mogen, Kaylee Goncalves, Ethan Chapin, and Xana Kernodle–the new episodes will follow the law enforcement officers who investigated the case, including Lead Detectives Brett Payne and Darren Gilbertson​​​​​.

The third season of Mayfair Witches, officially titled Anne Rice’s Mayfair Witches: Salem Coven, is making its debut by the end of the year. The latest installment shifts the setting to Salem, Massachusetts, after Alexandra Daddario Rowan Fielding finds herself stuck in the town best known for its long historical connection to witches. Salem Coven will also feature a few new cast members, including Betsy Brandt, Michiel Huisman, Eliza Scanlen, James Frain, and Omar Maskati.

David Boreanaz is stepping into the role of James Rockford for NBC's The Rockford Files, coming this January. The reboot of the '70s crime drama follows a man tied to a crime he didn't commit, attempting to return to his job as a private investigator in Los Angeles. However, he's now a target of both the police and organized crime in the city. The series also stars Michaela McManus, Felix Solis, and Jacki Weaver.

Following the special preview on Saturday, January 2, The Rockford Files will return to its regular timeslot on January 7, 2027.

Will Trent is officially making its return as part of ABC's midseason schedule, beginning the first week of January. Season 5 picks back up following the deaths of Amanda Wagner (Sonja Sohn) and Dr. Seth McDale (Scott Foley), who also happens to be Angie's (Erika Christensen) husband. With a new baby, Will (Ramón Rodríguez) steps up to help the grieving mother, while newcomer Alexandra Metz joins the crime drama as Birdie Palmer, Amanda's successor as GBI Deputy Director.

Even though High Potential has a new showrunner, Kaitlin Olson's Morgan Gillory will still be at the front and center of ABC's hit comedy crime drama when it returns for season 3 in January. Though Steve Howey won't be returning as a lead character in the new installment, High Potential will still need to reveal Captain Wagner's fate following season 2's cliffhanger. That said, there will be plenty of familiar faces sticking around, including Adam Karadec (Daniel Sunjata)​​​​​, Daphne Forrester (Javicia Leslie), Lev "Oz" Özdil (Deniz Akdeniz), and Lieutenant Selena Soto (Judy Reyes).

A special episode of High Potential season 3 will air following Super Bowl LXI on Sunday, February 14, 2027.

Shifting Gears is another popular ABC series making its grand return as part of the midseason slate. When season 2 ended, Kat Dennings' Riley found herself in a love triangle with Andy (Jesse Williams) and Gabe (Seann William Scott), something that will need to be resolved when the show picks back up with season 3. As for Riley's dad, Matt (Tim Allen), he's back together with Eve (Jenna Elfman), who will be appearing throughout the new installment. As for newcomers, Shifting Gears season 3 will feature a special 2 Broke Girls reunion when Beth Behrs makes a guest appearance as an ER doctor.

ABC's midseason schedule includes several of the network's most popular series, including The Rookie, which is set to return for its ninth season the first week of 2027. Nathan Fillion still leads the police procedural as John Nolan, but a lot of the attention coming into season 9 will be on the fates of Lucy Chen (Melissa O'Neil) and Tim Bradford (Eric Winter). As for newcomers in The Rookie season 9, it has been announced that Troian Bellisario, Dulé Hill, and Molly Kunz will serve as guest stars.

2027 will notably see a double dose of The Rookie, with The Rookie: North making its official debut on January 6. The second spinoff of the original series follows Alex Holland (Jay Ellis), a middle-aged man who decides to join the police force after a life-altering home invasion. Though The Rookie: North is set in the Pacific Northwest, there's potential for future crossovers with the main series, especially with Nathan Fillion's character set to appear in the pilot.

Baywatch is getting the reboot-sequel ("requel") treatment at Fox, and the series is kicking off the first week of 2027. This new iteration is set to focus on original character Hobie Buchannon, who is now played by Stephen Amell, as he leads a group of LA lifeguards as the official Baywatch Captain. Hobie must also deal with the unexpected arrival of his biological daughter, Charlie (Jessica Belkin). Baywatch's cast also includes Hassie Harrison, Shay Mitchell, Brooks Nader, Noah Beck, Thaddeus LaGrone, and Livvy Dunn. Original Baywatch actors David Chokachi​​​​, Michael Bergin, Kelly Packard​​​, and Erika Eleniak will also appear.

Hugh Laurie stars in Apple TV's new crime thriller The Wanted Man as Felix Carmichael, a crime boss who finds himself in prison after being betrayed by a member of his own organization. In addition to seeking revenge on those who turned on him, Felix will also be looking to make a grand escape from prison to save his criminal empire. Debuting this January, The Wanted Man also stars Thandiwe Newton and is created by George Kay, who is also known for developing Lupin, Hijack, and HBO's recent War series.

9-1-1's midseason finale airs in November, but fans can already get ready for season 10's return. While we won't spoil what happens in "Et Tu?," the episode that serves as the fall finale, we can share a look at the show's return as part of ABC's big winter premiere week during the first week of January. The exclusive look features Ravi (Anirudh Pisharody) visiting May (Corinne Massiah) at the hospital where she works as part of her nursing program.

Following the midseason premiere of 9-1-1, the show's spinoff, 9-1-1: Nashville, will also return with a new episode as part of the sophomore season. Like the case with the main series, there's not much we can share regarding 9-1-1: Nashville season 2's return without spoiling the events of the fall finale, "Tears of the Nile." That said, we can provide an exclusive look at the future of season 2, specifically a new image of firefighters Blue (Hunter McVey) and Ryan (Michael Provost).

November will also see the fall finale of Grey's Anatomy season 23, but that means it will join many other ABC titles in celebrating 2027 by returning the first week of January. The fall finale, "Till I Collapse," saw tension among Grey Sloan leadership when they did not agree on how to deal with a sudden increase in patients. As for what to expect when Grey's Anatomy season 23 returns, we have sneak peeks at Miranda Bailey (Chandra Wilson), Ben Warren (Jason George), Jules Millin (Adelaide Kane)​​​​​, Jo Wilson (Camilla Luddington), and Link (Chris Carmack).

The Bosch franchise is growing yet again with a prequel series titled Bosch: Start of Watch. The series, which was first announced in late 2025, will star Cameron Monaghan as young Harry Bosch when he's still a rookie LA cop in 1992. Start of Watch will also introduce key figures that helped Harry's life earlier in his career, including a fellow rookie cop and potential love interest, Rosa (Ariana Guerra), and Harry's surrogate brother, Cory (JD Pardo). The MGM+ series has already been renewed for a second season.

Following on the heels of VisionQuest and Avengers: Doomsday, 2027 will see the arrival of Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man season 2 on Disney+. Though the animated series follows an alternate version of Peter Parker and Spider-Man, it's set within the multiverse, with season 2 specifically part of Phase 6 of the MCU. The new season will see Peter stuck between Norman Osborn and Doctor Ock's growing rivalry, as well as a bigger focus on the symbiote after the Venom story was teased in season 1.

Based on Ed Brubaker's comic book series of the same name, Criminal follows several families connected through generations of crime and secrets. The Prime Video series will release in two parts, "Coward" on January 13 and "Lawless" on January 27, with each part comprised of four episodes. "Coward" will center on Leo Patterson (Charlie Hunnam) and Ricky Lawless (Gus Halper), lifelong friends who can't run from a childhood murder and botched heist that pulls them right into their hidden past. Criminal's cast also includes Adria Arjona, Luke Evans, Emilia Clarke, Michael Mando, Garrett Hedlund, and Patrick J. Adams.

Following its debut in 2024, 3 Body Problem is back for season 2 in January. The sci-fi series based on Liu Cixin's novels confirmed the existence of an extraterrestrial civilization known as the San-Ti. In season 2, Earth is preparing for the San-Ti invasion with the plan to carry out a special space fleet mission that could be the only way to save the planet from being taken over. In addition to the return of cast members like Jovan Adepo, Liam Cunningham, Eiza González, Jess Hong, Benedict Wong, Sea Shimooka, Marlo Kelly, and Saamer Usmani, season 2 will see the arrival of Claudia Doumit​​​​​, Ellie de Lange​​​​​, and Alfie Allen, to name a few.

Taylor Sheridan's Mayor of Kingstown is nearing its end with the arrival of the show's fifth and final season on January 15. Led by Jeremy Renner in his role as Mike McLusky, Mayor of Kingstown season 5 will follow Mike and his brother Kyle looking for vengeance after the murder of Kyle's wife, Tracy. The death also brings FBI agent Russell Hardy (David Morse) to town, which complicates matters for the McLusky family. Aside from another newcomer played by Nestor Carbonell, season 5 will feature the return of Edie Falco, Lennie James, and, of course, Tobi Bamtefa as Deverin “Bunny” Washington​​​​​.

Miss Scarlet is another series coming to an end this winter after seven seasons. Debuting in mid-January, Miss Scarlet season 7 will feature Kate Phillips' Eliza take on her role as Victorian London's first female private detective as she balances a new romance with Alexander Blake (Tom Durant-Pritchard) and a crime boss causing trouble in the city. Inspector Blake, on the other hand, will be dealing with his own career complications with a new superior officer at Scotland Yard.

All Creatures Great and Small will also be making its seventh season debut this January on Masterpiece on PBS. The British series based on James Herriot’s novels is now focusing on Skeldale House and its team of veterinarians after the war in 1946. In addition to advancements in veterinary medicine, James (Nicholas Ralph), Siegfried (Samuel West), and Tristan (Callum Woodhouse) will experience a new rival vet in the Yorkshire Dales. In addition to the upcoming season 7, the show was already renewed for an eighth season.

Sakamoto Days, the anime series based on Yuto Suzuki's manga, is returning in mid-January for its second season. The action comedy produced by TMS Entertainment will jump back into the life of former hitman Taro Sakamoto as he joins forces with Shin Asakura to go undercover to gather intel at Japan's assassin training institution known as the JCC. Elsewhere, ORDER members Nagumo, Shishiba, and Osaragi ​​​​​​get pulled into a conspiracy after their mission in Kyoto is ambushed.

The award-winning historical drama Blue Eye Samurai is finally making its return to Netflix after making its debut in 2023. Mizu (Maya Erksine), the mixed-race samurai, continues her quest to London in search of the man who could be her father. Back in Japan, Princess Tokunobu Akemi (Brenda Strong) and Taigen (Darren Barnet) attempt to deal with a new Shogun. The voice cast is also led by Kenneth Branagh, George Takei, Masi Oka, Cary-Hiroyuki Tagawa, and Randall Park. Blue Eye Samurai is already renewed for a third and final season.

The first major Star Wars live-action release of 2027 arrives in January with Ahsoka season 2. The highly anticipated return of the Disney+ series will see titular character Ahsoka Tano (Rosario Dawson) stranded in another galaxy with her apprentice Sabine Wren (Natasha Liu Bordizzo). Meanwhile, their allies, Ezra Bridger (Eman Esfandi) and Hera Syndulla (Mary Elizabeth Winstead), are preparing the rebels to face off against Grand Admiral Thrawn (Lars Mikkelsen) and the existing remnants of the Galactic Empire. Ahsoka season 2 will also notably feature Hayden Christensen reprising his role as Anakin Skywalker.

The creator of Vikings will soon be debuting another series full of fierce Viking warriors, including Erik Bloodaxe. In fact, the Prime Video series titled Bloodaxe is set to center on the warrior who eventually rose to claim the Norwegian throne. Xavier Molyneux will portray the legendary Norse figure, with Jessica Madsen playing his wife, Gunnhild. While fighting for the throne, the couple will get pulled into dangerous rivalries, surprising betrayals, and other rulers that will look to block Erik Bloodaxe's path to the throne. Bloodaxe is already renewed for season 2.

Apple TV's sci-fi domination will look to grow in 2027, especially with the debut of Neuromancer in January. Serving as an adaptation of William Gibson's cyberpunk novel from 1984, Neuromancer follows a super-hacker named Case (Callum Turner) who is partnered with an assassin named Molly (Briana Middleton) to carry out a dangerous digital heist. Blurring the concept of man and machine, the futuristic sci-fi show also features a cast that includes Mark Strong, Peter Sarsgaard, Clémence Poésy, Joseph Lee, and Dane DeHann.

The award-winning animated series Common Side Effects is back with brand new episodes in January. Season 2 picks up with Marshall (Dave King) and Frances (Emily Pendergast) finding themselves facing the consequences after discovering a mushroom that can heal seemingly anything. Now fugitives because of the discovery, the two friends have no choice but to go on the run, which takes them on a journey across the globe. Aside from the DEA, Marshall and Frances will encounter a new villain in season 2.

MGM+'s 2027 slate also features the political thriller Embassy, a six-part series set in London. Anna Kendrick stars as an American diplomat who has no choice but to turn to her ex-fiancé and British SAS soldier (Sam Heughan) after mercenaries target the U.S. Embassy in London. Kendrick's character, Layla, must decide if it's worth giving up a valuable asset even if it means putting the life of the U.S. Ambassador (J.K. Simmons) in serious danger.

Serving as the second TV series in the franchise, Barbershop follows Travis “Trav” Walker (Jermaine Fowler) as he returns to Chicago to take over Calvin's Barbershop in honor of his grandfather, Eddie (Cedric the Entertainer). Upon arriving, Trav is tasked with keeping up the shop's legacy while finding a place among the employees and community. Barbershop's supporting cast includes Bretty Gray, Roy Wood Jr., E.J. Bonilla​​​​​, and Punkie Johnson.

Will Sharpe and Ayo Edebiri co-lead Apple TV's upcoming British romantic-comedy series, Prodigies. The duo portrays former child prodigies who've been side-by-side since they were nine years old. Now in their thirties, Didi (Edebiri) and Ren (Sharpe) contemplate whether their lives and relationship status fulfilled their childhood hopes and dreams. Prodigies' supporting cast includes John Malkovich, Tobias Menzies, Rina Sawayama, and Lolly Adefope​​​​​.

From Dan Fogelman comes Hulu's new sports/family drama, The Land. The series debuting in February centers on a fictionalized version of the Cleveland Browns and the multi-generational families tied to the NFL team. William H. Macy portrays team owner Hal Durkin as he tries to pick which of his daughters will eventually take over the franchise. There's also a familial struggle between the coach, Danny Roarke (Christopher Meloni), and his new quarterback, who is also his son, Connor (Sam Corlett). The Land also stars Mandy Moore, Chloe Bennet, Chace Crawford, and Omar Epps.

Jon Favreau is expanding his presence on Disney+ with the release of Oswald in 2027. The three-part miniseries, which Favreau created and wrote, will be a mix of 2-D animation and live-action, featuring Disney's original cartoon character, Oswald the Lucky Rabbit, as he adapts to the modern world. What's more, the 2027 debut of the series marks the 100th anniversary of the character's first-ever appearance. The cast includes Ravi Cabot-Conyers​​​​​, Ryder Allen, Amy Sedaris, Kathryn Hahn, and Steve Martin.

Overcompensating, the Prime Video series from Benito Skinner, who also stars as lead character Benny, will be back for a second season in February. Season 2 picks up directly after the first season's cliffhanger, where Benny was seemingly outed by Carmen (Wally Baram), possibly revealing the secret to Benny's sister, Grace (Mary Beth Barone). Covering Benny's second semester in college, Overcompensating season 2 promises plenty of drama, hook-ups, hilarious encounters, and coming-of-age conflicts. New cast members will include Tom Francis, Aisha Dee, and AnnaSophia Robb.

Edith Wharton's 1920 novel is getting a new adaptation from Emma Frost with the arrival of The Age of Innocence on Netflix. The limited series examines what it's like growing up in the Gilded Age, focusing on high society in New York City during the late 1800s. In the middle of the story is a love triangle between characters Ellen Olenska (Camila Morrone), May Welland (Kristine Froseth), and Newland Archer (Ben Radcliffe). Other notable cast members include Margo Martindale, Fiona Glascott, Emma Shipp, Belinda Bromilow, and Will Tudor.

The One Piece is going back to the very beginning with a new adaptation of Eiichiro Oda's manga. The series from Wit Studio will follow the East Blue saga, centering on a 17-year-old Monkey D. Luffy and his newly formed crew as he searches for the "One Piece" treasure. While an exact release date for The One Piece hasn't been announced, we know the series will release in February 2027 with seven episodes that will cover the first 50 chapters of Oda's beloved series through a fresh take on the adventurous tale.

Another era of The Walking Dead is coming to an end in 2027 with Daryl Dixon season 4. The upcoming season will be the spinoff's last, and it's set to follow Daryl (Norman Reedus) and Carol (Melissa McBride) as they travel across the dangerous badlands in Spain in the hopes of making it back home. Jimmy Smits notably joins the final season as Captain Alejandro Perkins, a former U.S. Navy officer and doctor who uses unique methods to survive, which an injured Daryl will soon find out.

Rogue Heroes, the WW2 historical drama from Steven Knight, will return on MGM+ at some point in early 2027. Season 3 will specifically take place in the summer of 1944, following members of the British SAS unit behind enemy lines in France. During the Allied liberation of Western Europe, the SAS will find new recruits while also fighting against members of the French Resistance. Notable cast members in Rogue Heroes season 3 include Jacob McCarthy, Leïla Muse, Nick Hargrove, Theo Barklem-Biggs, Jack Barton, and Jack O'Connell.

Acorn TV's Irish Blood is set to return for season 2 in early 2027. The second installment will once again feature Alicia Silverstone's Fiona Fox, but this time around, she's trying to save her mother, Mary (Wendy Crewson), from a grifter while still dealing with the aftermath of her father's death. The lingering mysteries surrounding his passing send Fiona, Mary, and Róisín (Ruth Codd) on an adventurous investigation across Ireland.
