Taylor Sheridan’s ‘Dutton Ranch’ Season 2 Has To Escape This ‘Yellowstone’ Trap

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Dutton Ranch might bid farewell to the bittersweet Montana ranch, but trouble still awaits Beth Dutton (Kelly Reilly) and Rip Wheeler (Cole Hauser) in Rio Paloma, Texas. The couple has seen their fair share of extreme highs and lows in Yellowstone, and after its tragic finale, they've had enough violence to last a lifetime. In Taylor Sheridan’s spin-off, Beth, Rip, and their adopted son, Carter (Finn Little), pack their bags and head south in search of a fresh start.

Unfortunately, leaving Montana doesn't mean they can leave their family legacy behind. Their reputation is bound to follow them to Texas, bringing a new set of problems that Beth and Rip just can't seem to shake off. No matter how much they've changed or how far they get from the Yellowstone ranch, the skeletons in their closets always find a way to catch up with them.

Roughly six months after the Yellowstone finale, Beth, Rip, and Carter leave Montana for good after a wildfire strikes the property. Heading all the way south, the family settles on a 7,000-acre homestead near Rio Paloma in South Texas, determined to build their ranch from scratch. However, the local 10-Petal Ranch, led by its hardened matriarch Beulah Jackson (Annette Bening), doesn't take kindly to their arrival, sensing new competition in the area.

But just like the Duttons, the Jackson family has problems of its own. Between an alcoholic son and a granddaughter whose resentment toward the ranch is driving a wedge between her and her father, Beulah is struggling to keep the 10-Petal legacy intact. And just when the Duttons begin settling into their new home, hoping to give Carter a safer future and finally find some much-needed peace themselves, things take a turn when Rip discovers a corpse on their property in the middle of the night.

Like everyone in Yellowstone, Beth and Rip are notoriously prone to aggression, whether they're dishing it out or on the receiving end. Beth has learned to mask her trauma by making herself untouchable. Anyone who messes with her, be it Jamie (Wes Bentley), Summer (Piper Perabo), or a random woman hitting on her husband at a bar, is bound to get a beating. The same goes for Rip. As the de facto enforcer of the Dutton Ranch, it's his job to protect the property at all costs. That means unleashing a raging bull into a bar or confronting a motorcycle gang with nothing but a branding iron.

Things are different in Dutton Ranch. Beth and Rip now have Carter to look after, and the kind of life they led in Montana could put their son's future at risk. Beth is particularly aware of this, refusing to let Carter follow in their footsteps as a rancher and pushing him to finish school instead. Although they're still knee-deep in the ranching business, their priorities have shifted. Rather than getting caught up in blind ambition that defined Yellowstone, Beth and Rip are more concerned with keeping their family afloat and building a home where Carter can have a better future. Local competition still threatens their livelihood, but they're no longer looking to seize everything in sight.

Unfortunately, trouble never stays away for long in Dutton Ranch. Ironically enough, Beth and Rip aren't even the ones starting it. Expanding their modest ranch while competing against a local cattle dynasty is already hard enough, but no amount of ranching experience can prepare them for the messes that keep landing on their doorstep. A dead body turns up on their property, and their cattle contract foot-and-mouth disease (FMD), forcing them to lose their entire herd overnight.

But the real danger starts when Beth and Rip get caught up in the 10-Petal Ranch drama. They initially step in to help the struggling Jackson family, only to stumble upon a much bigger operation involving cattle smuggling and fentanyl trafficking. That's a whole different level of criminal activity, even by Dutton standards. And if there's one sign that Dutton Ranch is heading back toward Yellowstone's bloody roots in Season 2, it's the Season 1 finale. After Beth and Rip expose the operation, cartel associates retaliate by kidnapping Carter. Beth and Rip wanted a fresh start in Texas, but with Carter taken, their turbulent past is catching up with them. Come Season 2, Dutton Ranch looks ready to go down the same bloody path as Yellowstone.
