2027 marks Star Wars' 50th anniversary, celebrating the release of the original 1977 sci-fi blockbuster. Not only will the original film receive a theatrical re-release, but two other highly anticipated Star Wars projects are also scheduled to arrive that year. It's shaping up to be a huge year for Star Wars fans, and merchandise companies are preparing to join the year-long celebration.

One of these companies is LEGO, the toy brand known for its colorful building bricks. Aside from its original products, the company has partnered with several major franchises, including Star Wars. The partnership has even produced LEGO's most expensive set, the Death Star, priced at $999.99. LEGO has released numerous sets based on the iconic sci-fi universe, and with the 50th anniversary approaching, the company has unveiled another addition to its collection.

LEGO has revealed its newest addition to its Art Collection: A New Hope Movie Poster. This 3,209-piece LEGO set lets fans recreate the original 1977 poster, featuring Luke Skywalker, Princess Leia, C-3PO, and R2-D2, with Darth Vader and the Death Star in the background. The completed LEGO poster measures 59 cm tall, 40 cm wide, and 5.1 cm deep and costs $299.99. As of this writing, LEGO's A New Hope Movie Poster is not available for pre-order but is scheduled for release on January 4, 2027.

Lucasfilm and Disney have unveiled their plans for Star Wars' year-long celebration. The first is a theatrical re-release of the original 1977 Star Wars film, which will be available in IMAX 70mm and other IMAX formats, as well as D-BOX. The companies have also confirmed that the next Star Wars movie, Star Wars: Starfighter, starring Ryan Gosling and directed by Shawn Levy, is scheduled for release on May 28, 2027. Season 2 of Ahsoka is also set to premiere in January 2027, while a new documentary titled The Hero Awakens will explore the making of Star Wars, featuring archival footage and audio from the 1977 film's production. Lastly, three major Star Wars events are scheduled for next year. The first is Star Wars: A New Hope at Cosm in Shared Reality, which will immerse audiences in "iconic Star Wars locations and moments." The second is the upcoming Star Wars Celebration, scheduled for the Los Angeles Convention Center from April 1 to 4. The third is Star Wars in Concert: The Skywalker Saga, a concert featuring a live orchestra performing music from all nine films.
