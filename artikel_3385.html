Skip Hinnant, Peanuts Star, Dies At 86

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Skip Hinnant, the beloved actor behind one of Peanuts' classic characters, has sadly passed away.

Hinnant, who played Schroeder in the Off-Broadway musical You're a Good Man, Charlie Brown and the voice behind Fritz the Cat, died on Sunday, November 8, 2026, in Denver, Colorado, according to a statement published by the New York Times. His family say that the actor was surrounded by loved ones at the time of his death. The cause of death has not been publicly disclosed at the time of this article's publication.
