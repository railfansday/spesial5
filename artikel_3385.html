Denzel Washington’s New Crime Thriller Officially Casts a Major ‘Sinners’ Star

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Denzel Washington may not be done with the Equalizer franchise, but he's still looking towards the future and other thriller projects to apply his Academy Award-winning talents to. Early next year, he'll appear in The Take, a heist flick on Netflix that has him at the center of a star-studded cast, including Robert Pattinson and Daisy Edgar-Jones. News also broke last month confirming a new team-up with another action veteran, Mission: Impossible director Christopher McQuarrie, for a yet-untitled crime thriller under Amazon MGM Studios. With a release date of October 1, 2027, progress is moving quickly on the project, and the cast is coming together in short order.

The film already has one key role filled by The Marvels star Teyonah Parris, who is set to play Washington's estranged daughter. The story revolves around a former police detective in prison on a life sentence until it's discovered he only has six months to live with terminal brain cancer. He's granted compassionate release and chooses to use that time to reconnect with his daughter and make up for lost time before it's too late. However, what he's not prepared for is the fact that he has a grandson he never knew about and that his dark past hasn't left him behind. Washington's on-screen family is now officially being rounded out with the breakout star of Ryan Coogler's Oscar-winning horror flick, Sinners.

Miles Caton, who earned a Critics' Choice Award and dozens of nominations for his acting debut as Sammie Moore in the Michael B. Jordan vampire joint, will play Washington's grandson in the unnamed thriller. It officially marks the musician's first role booked since opening eyes as the young blues guitarist cousin of the Smokestack Twins. The son of gospel legend Timiney Figueroa, Caton came virtually out of nowhere as a 21-year-old star, having spent his entire career to this point on music, between playing background for H.E.R. and launching his bid as a solo artist with singles like "This Ain't It" and "Lean On" and, as of this year, a deal with Columbia Records. He was critical to Sinners' Grammy-winning soundtrack, even performing "I Lied to You" at the Oscars earlier this year.

For McQuarrie, working with Washington will bring him back to his crime thriller roots that launched him to stardom. Before shooting some of Tom Cruise's most daring stunt work for the big screen, he earned an Academy Award for his work penning the acclaimed 1995 mob movie The Usual Suspects. Scott Barkan and Gregg Zehentner wrote his latest effort, having previously worked together on Into the Dark and Edge of Fear. The production will be a collaboration between Amazon MGM and Scott Stuber's United Artists banner.

Washington and McQuarrie's new crime thriller releases in theaters on October 1, 2027. Stay tuned here at Collider for more on the film as it comes together.
