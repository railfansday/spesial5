Death metal band is so addicted to WoW Forever they’re playing it during shows

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A death metal guitarist turned side-stage into his own gaming den, grinding World of Warcraft: Forever on a Steam Deck mid-show.

Blizzard’s hotly anticipated MMO arrived on November 4, capping weeks in which fans had been toiling through beta queues and a name-reservation bug that locked out paying players. For a band touring Europe, though, the servers flung open at midnight on a show day, so waiting for load-out was never really an option.

Canadian tech-death band Archspire shared the footage on Instagram, and it showed Justin McKinney, guitarist for their support act The Zenith Passage, playing Forever on his Steam Deck at the side of the stage while the headliners came barreling through their set.

A post shared by Archspire (@archspireofficial)

“POV: World of Warcraft: Forever comes out on the same day as the show,” the clip read, before the caption explained that his set started at 21:00, “but you just zoned into Undercity to pick up your Voidwalker quest so you can pet-tank your way through Fenris Isle.”

Getting Forever onto Valve’s handheld takes some fiddling, given it lives on Battle.net rather than Steam, but McKinney had plenty of company in Azeroth either way, with Blizzard calling it the biggest launch in WoW history by peak concurrent players.

Nor was he the first metal musician to haul Azeroth on the road, as Cannibal Corpse frontman George “Corpsegrinder” Fisher’s Horde devotion once earned him his own Wrath of the Lich King NPC, later renamed in 2021. McKinney still has a few nights left to level, too, with Archspire’s European Vacation tour wrapping up in Hamburg on November 16.

And if a Steam Deck beside a tech-death headliner sounds like an odd setup, it looks downright sensible next to the YouTuber who cleared a Mythic Keystone dungeon using hot dogs as buttons earlier this year.
