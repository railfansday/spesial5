GTA 6 players who used PS5 New Zealand trick are missing out on a stat buff

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

GTA 6 players who used the New Zealand trick to play early on PS5 may have inadvertently purchased a censored version of the game that’s missing a useful stamina buff.

With GTA 6 launching at midnight local time on November 19, impatient fans around the world used the New Zealand trick to get a head start on their adventures in Leonida.

While Xbox players could simply change their console’s location, PS5 owners had to create a New Zealand PlayStation account and purchase GTA 6 through the country’s digital storefront.

Unfortunately, getting into Vice City a few hours early appears to have come with an unexpected drawback.

As reported by IGN, Rockstar left out the ability to consume cocaine from your inventory in the Australian version of GTA 6, with New Zealand reportedly receiving the same version.

In the international release, players can collect baggies of cocaine or skim some off larger bricks. Consuming cocaine through the item wheel gives players a supercharged stamina bar for roughly 30 seconds.

Australia’s video game classification rules allow depictions of illicit drug use but prohibit players from receiving gameplay benefits for taking them, even in games rated R18+.

Interestingly, New Zealand doesn’t have this same restriction, but testing by GTA BOOM found that the mechanic was also absent from its New Zealand playthroughs.

Players had already suspected GTA 6 was censored in certain regions before launch when dataminers discovered a roughly 700KB discrepancy in the Australian and New Zealand game files.

Unlike Xbox users who temporarily changed their console’s location, PS5 players who bought the New Zealand edition still own that regional copy even when playing on their regular account.

Luckily, those affected aren’t missing out on too much. Adrenaline can still be used to boost stamina and other stats, while scripted sequences involving cocaine remain intact.

Still, it’s a rather amusing twist that some of GTA 6‘s most impatient fans ended up with less content than those who waited a few extra hours.
