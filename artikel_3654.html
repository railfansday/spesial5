Tom Arnold, 67, Calls People His Age 'Disgusting,' Admits He Hasn't Dated

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Tom Arnold is opening up about his dating mindset amid his ongoing hiatus from the romance scene.

“I used to say, people are like — because I haven’t had a date for nine and a half years — ‘You should date someone your own age,’” Arnold, 67, told Page Six in an interview published on Friday, November 13. “And I’m like, ‘Dude, have you seen people my age? We are freaking disgusting.’”

“But here’s the thing,” he continued, “I have learned there’s a lot of funny, smart, sexy, 60-something women. … But I’ll tell you what they’re not looking for. They’re not looking for a guy that used to have a lot of money and now has two little kids. They are like, ‘Forget that. That is not what I’m looking for.’”

Arnold, who confirmed he was serious about wanting to find love again, shared that “it would be good to love somebody and be loved.”

“I love my kids so much. There’s a lot of love there. And my buddies,” he said. “But I’ll be OK. You know, I’ve had four marriages.”

Arnold was previously married to Roseanne Barr from 1990 to 1994, Julie Armstrong from 1995 to 1999, Shelby Ross from 2002 to 2008 and Ashley Groussman from 2009 to 2020. Arnold and Groussman share two children: son Jax, 13, and daughter Quinn, 10.

In 2025, Arnold got candid about his marriage to Barr while promoting his My Crazy X-Wife comedy tour.

“People say ‘My Crazy X-Wife, oh, I know which one that is. It’s Roseanne,’” he told Us. “And I’m like, ‘She’s not even close to my craziest ex-wife because we didn’t have kids together.’ The worst thing she did was go on Saturday Night Live and say I have a three-inch penis, which is confidential information. You take that to the grave.”

“Even a 747 looks small when it flies over the Grand Canyon,” he quipped.

The next year, Barr came to her ex’s defense when producer Chuck Lorre — who worked alongside the pair during her show — claimed in his book that the Roseanne writers put Arnold’s name on scripts he didn’t write.

“Unbelievably — I don’t think you’ll hear me say this often — but Tom Arnold is telling the truth for once,” she told The Hollywood Reporter in October. “Talk about the paradigm switch here. Tom Arnold is defending me. I have to thank him for that. That’s the first time he’s done something right by me in his f***ing life. Thanks, Tom.”

Arnold then reacted to Barr’s comments, writing via X, “Don’t give up. Don’t ever give up. 33 years ago I predicted I would do something so cool my 1st X Wife Roseanne would be forced to say something almost nice about me. Hell finally froze over today.”
