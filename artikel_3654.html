We cover fashion daily, so it’s hard to get Us excited about a particular trend. But the ‘grandpa’ sweater craze sweeping rich girl wardrobes? We’re thrilled. Even British Vogue is calling the broader ‘posh grandpa’ aesthetic a major fashion mood for 2026, with vintage-inspired knits, relaxed tailoring and old-school textures leading the charge. Not only is it an excuse to ditch tight sweaters, but it’s also an easy way to dress expensive. These timeless knits are the ticket to a cool, laid-back, effortlessly chic aesthetic, and the trendiest picks start at only $5!

Oddly stylish and totally nostalgic, grandpa sweaters are defined by oversized silhouettes, timeless prints, lived-in fabrics and classic knit textures — so yes, they give your wardrobe a charming, vintage flair.
