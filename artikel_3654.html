If you think you’re seeing a ton of plush brown fur lately, that’s because you are! Wealthy ladies are wearing ‘heirloom’ teddy coats nonstop this fall. Heirloom styles are similar to regular teddy coats, but they’re classier, cozier and much more luxe. Basically, they’re so elegant that they look passed down through generations. . .  so, heirlooms.

These chic teddy coats are sleeker than puffers, warmer than trenches and much more interesting than peacoats, making them both pretty and practical. The best part? It doesn’t matter what you wear underneath. The coat totally steals the show, taking any dress or lounge set to polished heights. It’s never been easier to dress rich — especially since the most timeless styles start at only $11!
