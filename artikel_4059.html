GTA 6's Map Sparks Family Guy Character Comparisons With Peter Griffin

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The absolutely massive map of Grand Theft Auto 6 is one that players have been theorizing about for years and have still only discovered a small portion of, despite players having already racked up 80,000 years' worth of playtime in the game on PlayStation 5 alone. While these numbers are certainly impressive, the more interesting part of Leonida is how many Easter eggs and hidden details are scattered throughout the map.

Although some of these references are more obvious, like callbacks to Miami's Dexter compared to others that could take years before being fully pieced together, one comparison to a character from one other than Family Guy made by a player online has completely changed how we see the map.

While you might need to squint to see it at first, after being compared side by side from X user gardeninqueen, GTA 6's map of Leonida state really does bear a striking resemblance to Peter Griffon from Family Guy, but more specifically his standard hands-at-the-side pose.

Although it's hard to imagine Rockstar spending over a decade working on a game just to make its entire map a Family Guy gag, the post has since gone viral with countless players backing up how similar they look and saying, "It's genuinely him" or "Oh my god I see it" when overlaying the image over top of Leonida.

The funniest part is that this isn't even the first time this has happened to Rockstar either, as players also said the same thing about Grand Theft Auto 5's map looking like Homer Simpson's head. While many players also questioned how either of these comparisons were noticed in the first place, it isn't too surprising given how rabid fans have been over map speculation since GTA 6 was revealed.
