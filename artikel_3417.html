GTA 6 Sets New Record As Highest-Rated Video Game of All Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The 13-year wait for Grand Theft Auto 6 pays off

After more than a decade of waiting, Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here. Rockstar Games' new open-world crime adventure game is playable by the masses — at least in some parts of the world — and has been playable by critics for some time. GTA 6's review embargo lifted on Wednesday, Nov. 18, and more than a hundred reviews have been tabulated by Metacritic. The consensus is clear: GTA 6 is "monumental," "unbelievably fun," and "truly remarkable," critics say.

The overwhelmingly positive critical response has helped GTA 6 set a new record: It's now the highest-rated game of all time on Metacritic, where it currently sits above the previous title-holder, the original The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. GTA 6 currently has a 99 Metascore (out of 100), the same score that Metacritic displays for Ocarina of Time. The latter score is based on just 22 critic reviews of the Nintendo 64 game. GTA 6's Metascore is based on more than 150.

Both the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X versions of Grand Theft Auto 6 have a 99 Metascore, signaling "universal acclaim," according to Metacritic. The achievement puts GTA 6 atop a very strong top 10 on Metacritic — and knocks another Rockstar Games release out of the site's top 10 list.

Rockstar's Red Dead Redemption 2 held the No. 10 position until today, with its 97 Metascore.

In Polygon's review of GTA 6, critic Giovanni Colantonio lauded the game for its immersion, storytelling, and depth. "GTA once again earns its title as public enemy #1 — only this time, Rockstar accomplishes that less through bloodshed and more with an empathetic love story built for an era of stark economic uncertainty," Colantonio wrote. "It’s a bold choice, considering the series’ cold-blooded reputation, but it’s also what makes GTA 6 one of Rockstar’s finest games to date."

Grand Theft Auto 6 was nominated for eight awards, including Game of the Year, at this year's The Game Awards on Tuesday, making it an early frontrunner for multiple GOTY awards. Ahead of launch, the game has reportedly sold more than 17 million copies, according to Sensor Tower, earning $1.65 billion in revenue for Rockstar Games and publisher Take-Two Interactive.

Grand Theft Auto 6 officially launches on Nov. 19 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X.
