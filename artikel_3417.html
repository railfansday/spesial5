Here’s How to Score Deals on Travel, Concerts, and Other Experiences With Sam’s Club

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

Most shoppers know about Sam’s Club’s benefits like savings on bulk groceries and essentials, fast shipping, and access to special deals. But the warehouse club also offers a host of travel and entertainment benefits, including savings on everything from hotels and cruises to rental cars to amusement parks and basketball games. That means members can score significant savings on the overall cost of their next vacation, or they can nab deals on nights out closer to home. Check out the dedicated travel hub here, or read on for some of the highlights.

If you’re planning a vacation, a Sam’s Club membership can help you save on all the big purchases. The retailer’s travel hub lets you browse options in one place and compare deals. You’ll find hotel deals including up to 40% off in top-visited cities like Las Vegas, Miami, Orlando, and San Francisco. Booking hotels through Sam’s Club also gets you a dedicated customer service line if your plans change. Once you’ve found accommodations, you can also find rental car deals of up to 25% off at participating providers on Sam’s Club.

Sam’s Club’s entertainment section is also chock-full of savings opportunities. Members can browse discounts on tickets to major theme parks, including Walt Disney World, Disneyland Resort, Universal parks, LEGOLAND, and SeaWorld. These discounts include special multi-day ticket offers that significantly lower the overall price.

The entertainment savings on Sam’s Club also extend to events like Broadway productions, concerts, sports games, comedy shows, and movie tickets. In many cases, you’ll find special deals not available directly through theaters or ticketing services. Use these savings for a special night at home or while traveling.

One of the best parts of the Sam’s Club entertainment deals is that they’re not limited to the card-holding member. If you’re signed up, you can purchase tickets for the whole family or any friends joining the fun — even if they don’t have a Sam’s Club membership.
