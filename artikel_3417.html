‘The Batman: Part II’ Set Video Officially Sparks Major Character Death Theories

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

One of the more effective scenes in Matt Reeves' The Batman involved Robert Pattinson's Bruce Wayne attending a funeral without the protection of his Batsuit. What made the scene particularly interesting was watching Bruce trying to save somebody's life while surrounded by people who had absolutely no idea what he spent his evenings doing. Of course, there was another moment later in the movie when we were briefly led to believe that Bruce might have to attend a rather more personal funeral, having discovered that the Riddler had sent a package to Wayne Tower that was subsequently opened by Alfred. And it turns out that somebody else in Gotham City is getting a funeral in the sequel.

As reported by ComicBook, a new video from the set of The Batman: Part II has revealed that a funeral scene is being filmed for the upcoming DC sequel, although there's nothing in the footage to indicate whose funeral it actually is. Among the theories being floated by fans are the deaths of Andy Serkis' Alfred Pennyworth and Colin Farrell's Oswald Cobb, while some have even suggested that we could be looking at the funeral of Bruce Wayne himself. There's also every possibility that what we're seeing is a flashback to the funeral of Thomas and Martha Wayne.

Of course, we didn't actually know a great deal about Don Mitchell Jr. when we attended his funeral in the first movie, and the most interesting thing about that scene was what the Riddler had planned for the people attending it. It would also be a little strange to kill off Alfred in the sequel, given that Reeves had already used the prospect of Bruce losing him as a means of getting the two men to open up to one another in the first movie.

Had this footage emerged six months ago, there probably wouldn't have been quite so many people entertaining the idea that it could be Bruce Wayne's funeral. Since then, though, Pattinson has landed the role of James Bond, we're about to get a new man in charge of DC Studios, and there's now a different director helping to finish the movie that Reeves started, with Steven Soderbergh having stepped in to help complete production.

By the time we get to see Pattinson in the Batsuit again, it'll have been almost six years since his first outing as the character, and we still don't know whether we'll be getting a third movie. That said, if you're Warner Bros. and you've made the decision to kill off Batman, would you really film his funeral somewhere that anybody with a phone could record it?
