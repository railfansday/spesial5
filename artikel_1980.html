West Ham United spared their blushes against Cardiff City for the most part, but it wasn't an ideal first game for Scott Parker.

Jack Moylan fired home a first-half hat-trick at the Cardiff City Stadium on Wednesday, 4 November, prompting boos and jeers from the away end.

Parker clearly got to his players during the break, though, with goals from Divin Mukasa, Arne Engels and Max Kilman securing a 3-3 draw.

The Hammers remain eighth in the Championship table, the same position they were in under Nuno Espirito Santo before his demise.

Despite a new manager in charge, the squad of players is still the same, and that could be a big problem for Parker for the rest of 2026-27.

Players like Morato, Mads Hermansen and Joel Veltman simply aren't good enough, but they weren't West Ham's biggest problem against Cardiff.

The first 45 minutes was an embarrassing half of football for everyone involved, but no more so than midfielder Soungoutou Magassa.

In that time, he completed just 77 per cent of his passes, made no tackles, won zero per cent of either ground or aerial duels, and committed two fouls [FotMob].

Having also picked up a yellow card for a challenge on Alex Robertson, it was no surprise to see him replaced by Mohamadou Kante during the break.

He's been stuck on the bench for the entire season, sparking serious questions about Nuno's decisions, but it's become incredibly obvious now.

West Ham's best decision would be to sell Magassa during the January transfer window. The main problem would be finding an interested suitor.

Magassa did cost a lot of money, especially for a team relegated to the Championship last season, but he needs to be sacrificed already.

Not only has he failed to impress, he has also stopped Kante's progression at the London Stadium, a player who deserves to be starting much more.

Despite boasting three goals and two assists, he's only started eight of the Hammers' 14 Championship fixtures.

Of course, Tomas Soucek is a much more experienced option, but Kante's raw talent carries him through games, and he gets fans on their feet.
