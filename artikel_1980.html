Scott McTominay nets Napoli winner days after major announcement as Man Utd and Real Madrid rumours emerge

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Scotland’s Scott McTominay capped off a big week by netting the winner in a Serie A showdown with Torino on Saturday.

The goalscoring midfielder journeyed back to his club side after a mixed November for the national team that included Nations League victory over North Macedonia before defeat against Switzerland. There was exciting news at home with the public announcement he is due to become a father, declared on social media before Saturday’s home encounter against a squad that features Scotland teammates Che Adams and Nathan Patterson.

McTominay scored the game’s only goal in a 1-0 win for Napoli, picking up the ball on the edge of the Torino box, before a piledriver that flew into the bottom corner. It comes as more rumours suggest his stunning stint in Naples could be heading for a close, possibly through a major transfer to AC Milan or Real Madrid, or returning to Manchester United.

Speaking after his latest moment of importance in light blue, McTominay smiled: "I am feeling much better. It's an important goal, but more importantly we won, we had to win. I'm happy for the fans, we need to get a bit of momentum going, keep winning. In football it's about the next game, and the next game, you have to do your best. We have a team that wants to work, wants to push, and we want to win more games."

Manchester United sold the 29-year-old who turns 30 next month to the Serie A side in 2024 and that is the only other senior club side he has represented. McTominay’s ties with Adidas are said to have their part in AC Milan keeping an eye on the Scotland star, according to Italian claims, as the Italian giants are left ‘waiting’ alongside Manchester United and La Liga’s Real Madrid.

Contract talks between Napoli and one of their key players are currently on hold for ‘financial reasons.’ Speaking after McTominay’s goal sealed a much-needed three points for his side, boss Massimiliano Allegri said: “The mood wasn’t right because we had so many chances, I told them at half-time to try smiling and we might get a goal.

“They did really well to win this game, while suffering against a strong opponent. Perhaps it is positive that we won under pressure, but we also should’ve done better controlling the tempo, moving the ball from one side to the other. The lads did very well in defending on set plays and corner kicks.

“I just told McTominay not to be already in the position right behind Rasmus Hojlund, but to run into that space, as it would make him harder to mark. If he is already there and not mobile, then obviously he’s going to be easier to control, but he started to move between the lines and did better.”
