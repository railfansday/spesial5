Sources: Rangers make Vanja Dragojevic January transfer decision

Rangers are not planning to send midfielder Vanja Dragojevic out on loan in January, sources have told Football Insider.

The 20-year-old has suffered from a severe lack of football of late, but is still viewed as an important part of the project at Ibrox.

The Gers spent around £4.6million to sign the Serbian from Partizan Belgrade in the summer, and believe he has the potential to become a key player for them in future.

Sources say that Rangers were always expecting Dragojevic to take time to settle in at the club, given that he is a young player moving to a new league and a new country.

They have no plans to loan him out this winter, and believe that he can compete for a starting spot in the middle of the park.

Dragojevic has played just 48 minutes in the Scottish Premiership so far this season, and has hardly featured since the UEFA Conference League defeat to Jablonec at the end of August.

Derek McInnes' side have enjoyed great success so far this season, and are now on a 13-game unbeaten run in domestic action.

The boss has largely kept a very settled side in recent weeks, as he and his team try and fight their way to the top of the Premiership table.

However, that undoubtedly hasn't helped players like Dragojevic, who have found themselves on the fringes of the squad as a result.

Nicolas Raskin and Dan Neil have cemented themselves as McInnes' starting midfield pairing of late, and that has shown no signs of changing.

Dragojevic enjoyed a brief cameo from the bench in last weekend's win over St Mirren, but will be hoping for more regular action moving forward.

Rangers feel they can provide that for him long-term, rather than turning towards a loan move to try and boost his development.

Football Insider revealed on Tuesday that Rangers are not planning to sell left-back Tuur Rommens any time soon, despite his lack of game time of late.

The Belgian has only recently returned to fitness, but is firmly below James Penrice in the pecking order right now.

Sources say that Rommens still has a long-term future at Ibrox, regardless of the fact that Rangers have every expectation of turning Penrice's loan deal into a permanent stay.

Rommens excelled last season after arriving in January, but has struggled to make an impact for Rangers of late.
