Sources: Celtic can sign Simon Ebonog with sub-£5m offer, new midfield shortlist revealed

Celtic target Simon Ebonog will be available to sign for less than £5million in January, sources have told Football Insider.

The Le Havre midfielder has emerged on the Bhoys' latest shortlist of potential midfield targets.

Martin O'Neill is keen to bolster his options in the middle of the park this winter, and the recruitment team are "compiling" a list of options as a result.

Sources say that Ebonog is likely to be available for a cheap fee this winter, with his Le Havre side currently sitting bottom of the table in Ligue 1.

Celtic are keen to bring in a new midfielder, as there is still a feeling that they have not replaced Arne Engels following his summer exit.

Ebonog has started all 10 league games for Le Havre so far this season, but could soon be plying his trade at Celtic Park.

Celtic's centre-midfield depth was one of the strongest aspects of their squad last season, but they have struggled in that position in the new campaign.

Engels' £22million departure to West Ham back in August has left them short of quality options at the heart of the team, and they are trying to rectify that issue in January.

Ebonog is one of several names who are currently under consideration for a move to Parkhead, with Celtic looking to exploit Le Havre's difficult situation.

There is no doubt that O'Neill's side are capable of paying the necessary fee to secure the 22-year-old's signature, after some lucrative summer sales.

Whether they choose to is another matter entirely, with the recruitment team currently trying to whittle down their list of midfield targets.

Former Aberdeen chief executive Keith Wyness told Football Insider last week that the Celtic board are working overtime to defuse the "toxic" situation with the fans ahead of the club's upcoming AGM.

Lats season's AGM was abandoned after just 25 minutes, with fans continually heckling the club hierarchy present at the event.

Wyness believes that the board must try and restore peace at Parkhead before the meeting, or it could suffer a similar fate to 2025's edition.

He also pointed out that it may even take a change of ownership for the fanbase to get back on board with the club.
