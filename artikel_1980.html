West Ham’s January Transfer Plans Face Argentine Complication

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

West Ham United could be forced to wait until the summer if they are to sign River Plate goalkeeper Santiago Beltrán, according to the latest reports from Argentina. The Hammers have been linked with the highly rated goalkeeper for several weeks, but there appears to be a potential complication regarding the timing of any transfer.

Argentine journalist Juan Cortese has reported that River Plate could agree to sell Beltrán on the condition that he remains with the club until June. As reported this morning by The72, Aston Villa and Fiorentina are also showing interest in the goalkeeper.

West Ham’s interest in Beltrán comes as little surprise given Scott Parker’s ongoing goalkeeping problems. However, there is no suggestion that the Hammers have submitted an offer, and Cortese reports that West Ham are in no rush.

Personally, if West Ham have to wait until the summer for a goalkeeper, I see little point. By that time, dreams of a return to the Premier League will likely be over because I don’t believe there’s any chance of promotion with our current goalkeeping line-up.

The two immediate improvements Nils Koppen can make to the team in January are at goalkeeper and striker. With the Hammers already looking to offload Taty Castellanos, it’ll be interesting to see what business the club can get done.

Get it right and it’s not beyond the realms of possibility that West Ham can become a force in the division. But wait until June and we could find ourselves buying a goalkeeper for League One.
