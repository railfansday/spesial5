Sam Darnold injury: Seahawks get worrisome update in Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Seattle Seahawks could face another setback at quarterback as they prepare for their next game against the Arizona Cardinals. Sam Darnold's injury situation remains a concern after the veteran was ruled out of the Seahawks' Week 9 matchup against the Arizona Cardinals.

Seattle had hoped to have its starting quarterback available again soon. However, the latest update suggests his absence could extend beyond Week 9.

Darnold is in jeopardy of missing the Seahawks' Week 10 game against the Raiders (h/t ESPN's Adam Schefter).

The update raises further concerns about the quarterback's recovery after he suffered a quadriceps injury during Seattle's Week 8 loss to the Chicago Bears. Head coach Mike Macdonald previously described the injury as week-to-week and ruled out placing Darnold on injured reserve.

Darnold also suffered a cracked rib early in the Bears game but continued playing. Macdonald revealed that the quarterback sustained the injury on the second play before completing the contest.

Darnold's potential absence against Las Vegas would force Seattle to continue without its first-choice quarterback.

This is concerning because Drew Lock has also been managing an elbow injury. Macdonald had previously indicated that the coaching staff would manage Lock's throwing workload while preparing both him and Milroe.

Lock has experience starting for Seattle and previously led the team to a win over Arizona in Week 2. Milroe, meanwhile, remains another option if Lock cannot handle the full workload.
