Patrick Mahomes’ telling 3-word reaction to 49ers gruesome injury that’s hard to watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

One of the NFL's biggest stars showed support for an injured 49ers player after he was carted off the field on Sunday night with an ugly injury.

Kansas City Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes took to "X" to show support for offensive lineman Colton McKivitz after he left the game when his left foot was facing the wrong way after a running play during the first half of San Francisco's game against the Minnesota Vikings.

San Francisco 49ers offensive tackle Colton McKivitz (68) goes down with an injury during an NFL football game against the Minnesota Vikings, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Eduardo Verdugo

Mahomes, of course, has been the 49ers' nemesis. He's beaten them in two Super Bowls with the Chiefs including a heartbreaker in overtime after the 2023 season.

With the 49ers and Chiefs both rolling this year, there's a chance they meet in Los Angeles for a rubber match come Valentine's Day.

San Francisco 49ers offensive tackle Colton McKivitz (68) is injured during an NFL football game against the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City, Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Eduardo Verdugo

The 49ers are a favorite to earn the No. 1 seed in the NFC while the Chiefs improved to 8-2 with their win over the Arizona Cardinals on Sunday.

Mahomes is 5-0 against the 49ers, with three regular-season wins and two Super Bowl victories.

Patrick Mahomes #15 of the Kansas City Chiefs reacts against the San Francisco 49ers during Super Bowl LIV in Miami, Sunday, Feb. 2, 2020. Getty Images

San Francisco gets another shot at him in Week 16, when it visits the Chiefs at Arrowhead Stadium on Sunday, Dec. 27, at 1:25 p.m. PT (3:25 p.m. CT).

In the meantime, Mahomes is showing class with his support of McKivitz despite the rivalry.
