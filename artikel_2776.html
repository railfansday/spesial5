Eagles News: ESPN predicts a Philadelphia upset

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Welcome to The Linc, the daily Philadelphia Eagles links roundup at Bleeding Green Nation!

Might the Philadelphia Eagles actually win a football game today?! I'm not counting on it. But I do think it's more possible after seeing the Pittsburgh Steelers drop from being 4.5-point road favorites to 3.5-point road favorites.

Let's get to the Philadelphia Eagles news and links …

NFL Week 11 predictions: Upsets, fantasy sleepers, stat lines, TD scorers, more – ESPN

Philadelphia Eagles +4.5 (+180 moneyline) vs. Pittsburgh Steelers. Quarterback Aaron Rodgers hasn't given much reason to trust Pittsburgh laying points on the road Sunday. Philadelphia's specialty is playing more man coverage and sending extra rushers, both of which have bothered Rodgers in 2026. Plus, defensive tackle Jalen Carter can attack an offensive line that just lost guard Mason McCormick (ankle). Eagles backup QB Joe Milton taking sacks is the main concern for their offense, but he can still get positive yards as a scrambler. With wide receiver DeVonta Smith potentially returning from a hamstring injury, the Eagles have enough to win this by a field goal.

Should the Eagles want Shane Steichen back? – BGN

The Eagles have been so bad this season that they've forced me to change what I write about. I really enjoy studying opposing teams for the weekly previews, but I'm already more interested in what the Eagles might look like next year. So, over the remaining weeks of the season, I'm going to examine potential coaching candidates and some offenses and defenses that interest me. Obviously, this depends on the Eagles making changes, and in this particular case, the Colts letting Shane Steichen go. Neither is guaranteed. However, I thought it would be interesting to look beyond the record and examine whether Steichen is still someone Eagles fans should want back.

Steelers activate WR Roman Wilson ahead of Eagles matchup – Behind The Steel Curtain

The Steelers are getting one of their top wide receivers back ahead of Week 11's matchup against the Philadelphia Eagles. Pittsburgh announced Saturday that wide receiver Roman Wilson has been activated to the 53-man roster ahead of Sunday's game. In addition, the team announced the signing of edge rusher K.J. Henry from the practice squad to the roster. The two signings fill the open spots on the Steelers' roster. The Henry signing follows the Steelers announcing outside linebacker Nick Herbig as out for Sunday's game. Now, Pittsburgh has an open slot on its practice squad.

Eagles locker room notebook: Taking stock of Makai Lemon ahead of full-circle matchup vs. Pittsburgh Steelers – PHLY Eagles

Expect to see Ty Robinson take some of the nose-tackle snaps previously reserved for Jordan Davis at the heart of the Eagles defense this season. He's an unconventional fit for the position at 6-foot-5, 288 pounds, but Eagles defensive coordinator Vic Fangio said the second-year lineman out of Nebraska has gotten extensive work in practice playing as a zero technique and believes he can hold up even with a relatively smaller frame for the role. "The nose tackle isn't totally the position that is viewed as it was in the past," Fangio said. "When there was a two back running game and there was a lot of [isolation runs] where he would get a lot, a lot of double teams. It still happens, but not as much as it used to." Robinson said it would be "big shoes" to fill in for Davis, but wasn't concerned about his size in the new role. "All last year I was learning all three positions," Robinson said. "The playbook is the easiest part now. It's just about going out there and executing and trying to fill the shoes of Jordan Davis. And those are some big shoes to fill. … It's just about the heart of the dog in the fight. I go against Jordan Mailata every day. That dude's massive. We go against massive dudes. It's nothing discouraging, just going out there and playing the way I know how to play."

Eagles notebook: Nolan Smith reacts to the Jordan Davis trade – NBCSP

Nolan Smith and Jordan Davis were teammates at Georgia and then became teammates again in the NFL. They have been friends for a long time. But there's no time to be sad. "Man, he's a great guy," Smith said on Friday. "That's part of the business. That's how it goes. I'm pretty sure Jacksonville is picking up a good player. But we have to focus on us. We have leaders in this locker room. We've got guys who can lead and push guys." Davis was traded to the Jaguars ahead of the trade deadline last week and this was the first chance to catch up with Smith since the trade. So it has been over a week since it all went down. Jalen Carter, who also played with Davis at Georgia before becoming teammates in the NFL, was again not available to reporters this week. The return on the Davis trade for the Eagles was undeniable. Getting a first-round pick and second-round pick for Davis represented incredible value. But it doesn't mean they won't miss Davis. "Man, it was special," Smith said of their time together in Philly. "But that time come and go, man. I'm still playing with guys that I played with at Georgia. But that was a real special moment, though."

Game Preview vs. Steelers: Six storylines to follow Sunday – PhiladelphiaEagles.com

Handling the Steelers' blitz game. There are individual stars here – T.J. Watt has 7.5 sacks and 9 tackles for loss – but it's the collective that makes the Steelers so good on defense. They bring pressure and they aren't afraid to expose their back end to one-on-one matchups and that's why having Smith is so critical. They can win those matchups. But the Eagles first have to handle pressure. Quarterback Joe Milton III said he has confidence in how much he worked to advance his understanding of the scheme and of what Pittsburgh does, so we will see how the Eagles react. Milton ran for 65 yards against the Giants two weeks ago. He can gash the blitz with his legs, but the Eagles also need to make the passing game go and avoid negative plays.

Oren Burks placed on IR by Bengals – Cincy Jungle

The Cincinnati Bengals have placed [Eagles Super Bowl LIX champion] linebacker Oren Burks on the injured reserve list with a groin injury, the team announced on Saturday. Burks, a ninth-year player, has played in all nine games this season, totaling seven tackles and one tackle for loss on defense to go with nine tackles on special teams. He suffered his injury during the team's Week 10 win over the Pittsburgh Steelers.

NY Giants' pass defense has been much better than expected – Big Blue View

Fast-forward nine games, and the Giants' secondary has been much, much, better than anticipated under [former Eagles assistant] Dennard Wilson, Donald D'Alesio, and Addison Lynch. The Giants are currently fielding the NFL's fourth-best defense in total yardage allowed (2,751 total yards). They're third-best in passing yardage allowed per game (184.1 yards per game), second in yards per completion allowed (9.8 yards per completion), and 2nd in yards per attempt allowed (6.2 yards per attempt). The Giants are accomplishing this feat with smothering coverage from their starting corners. Both Deonte Banks and Greg Newsome II have been incredibly sticky in man coverage, allowing little separation for receivers.

Cowboys passing attack should shine, pass rush could struggle – Blogging The Boys

The Dallas Cowboys desperately need a win, and Sunday's matchup against the Tennessee Titans offers a chance to get something positive going after all the Christian Parker drama. Tennessee is 1-8 and has one of the league's worst defenses, particularly in the secondary. Dallas should have plenty of opportunities to move the ball through the air, especially with CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens coming off productive performances. There are still reasons to be cautious. The Cowboys' defense has been a disaster this year, and Tennessee quarterback Cam Ward has improved over the past few weeks. With Dallas also changing its defensive play-calling, this matchup could tell us a lot about the team's direction. Here are two players to buy and one area to sell ahead of Sunday's game.

Why Daronte Jones has Washington Commanders defense trending up – Hogs Haven

Washington's defense will be tested by the Cincinnati Bengals offense, which has scored 30-plus points in their last five games. Quarterback Joe Burrow might be this defense's kryptonite, considering how well he and the Bengals coaching staff handles the blitz. Burrow has the third-highest success rate when facing five rushers or more and has the NFL's highest completion rate at 73.4% against the blitz. Also, Jones said that Cincinnati prioritizes protection and does a good job disguising their work in pass pro. Does all that impact how Jones will call Monday night's game? He isn't volunteering that. He did provide the primary objective, to keep Burrow off balance. "We have to, in some way shape or form, provide mental stress," Jones said. "He can't be sitting back there knowing our coverage. We have to find a way to change the picture. It's going to become a chess match."

This Deshaun Watson reference in GTA 6 is wild – SB Nation

Grand Theft Auto VI is the highest-budget entertainment product in history, and if you've spent any time with the game, you'd understand why. The attention to detail is alarming; the depth of Rockstar's version of Florida is staggering — and the in-game radio has some of the most stunning celebrity DJs imaginable. One of these is comedian Stavros Halkias, who hosts a sports talk radio show that is completely off the rails. Stavros rants about bad performances by Vice City's football teams, claims that every other sport isn't manly — and even has an extended take on why Deshaun Watson did nothing wrong. Watson is never mentioned by name, but listen to this for just a few seconds and it's OBVIOUS.
