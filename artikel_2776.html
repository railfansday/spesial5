Christian McCaffrey on career-high receiving yards: You just catch it and run as fast as you can

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The 49ers running back had never had more than 121 receiving yards in a single game. He had 161 receiving yards on four catches Sunday against the Cowboys, scoring on catch-and-runs of 74 and 66 in the 49ers’ 46-40 win.

“You never know,” McCaffrey said. “You go into each game. You don’t really predict anything. Again, you just have to have the right mindset on a play that’s going to break. You trust your instincts and play fast.”

His first receiving touchdown came when Brock Purdy rolled to his right to buy time and found McCaffrey running free on the scramble drill.

“It really is backyard football at that point,” McCaffrey said. “We all played it as kids at recess, and you draw up plays on your hand, and it never happens. You ever tell a 5-year-old to run a post route, and he just runs a corner and something else, but he’s open? That’s kind of what that was. Again, I think those little decisions that you make in games, from Brock to scramble, not to just surrender the play but to keep it alive. That’s a big deal. There’s no planning that goes into that. It’s just him doing a great job playing ball.”

What turned out to be the game-winning touchdown came with 3:23 remaining when Purdy found McCaffrey, who was matched up against Von Miller, over the middle.

“They brought pressure,” McCaffrey said. “They dropped a D-end on me, so that’s a perfect look for that play. Again, it was a great job by Brock getting the ball where it needed to be. It made it easy for me.

“You just catch it and run as fast as you can to try to score. Don’t overthink it. You hope that nobody catches you, and you hope that you’re really fast so your teammates don’t catch up to you to celebrate, because it makes you look slow. We’ll have to check the tape.”

McCaffrey had a 16-yard touchdown run and 62 yards rushing. He is the first player in the Super Bowl era with at least 150 receiving yards, two receiving touchdowns, 50-plus rushing yards and at least one rushing touchdown.
