Ratliff, Arenas help USC trounce UAPB to stay undefeated in historic victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

LOS ANGELES -- It was a fun afternoon for the No. 21 USC Trojans on Sunday at the Galen Center. Trouncing the Arkansas-Pine Bluff Golden Lions 122-46, the squad improved to 4-0 on the season to open up a three-game homestand. The 76-point victory marked the largest margin of victory in program history.

The victory also marks the seventh time in head coach Eric Musselman's career that his teams have started the season with four consecutive wins. Sunday showcased a wire-to-wire, well-rounded effort led by a dominant performance from forward Adonis Ratliff.

"I feel like today it was easy to show off my playmaking," Ratliff said. "I feel like I'm a pretty good passer. I don't get to show it much, but I feel like I was able to do that today."

It was an Adonis avalanche from the beginning, as the freshman got the Trojans out to an 8-0 advantage all by himself. He put back a missed attempt from his brother Darius before drilling back-to-back triples and forcing an early timeout from the Golden Lions.

Adonis continued to fill up the stat sheet in more ways than one, making his presence felt just as strongly on the defensive end of the floor. By the end of the afternoon, he compiled 17 points to go along with eight rebounds, five assists and four blocks.

Darius, meanwhile, tallied only nine points. He made his impact in other ways, grabbing seven rebounds and a steal.

USC's defensive effort as a whole had Arkansas-Pine Bluff struggling to find any sort of stride. The Trojans were suffocating on the glass, winning the rebounding battle 43-22. They also forced 19 turnovers while committing just nine.

The Golden Lions were held to their second-lowest point total this season, falling to 0-5 in the process. Guards Jai-chaunn Hayes and Jahmari Hamilton-Brown both scored 11 to lead the team.

In the backcourt, guards Alijah Arenas and Rodney Rice continued their hot starts to the season with a pair of impressive displays on the offensive end. The duo, who both notched career highs in scoring just two games ago against Wichita State, were lights-out from distance on Sunday (7-of-11 combined).

Arenas finished with 21 points, six assists and a steal, shooting 4-of-5 from distance. Rice nearly kept pace with 18 points of his own, adding two rebounds and an assist. Both are averaging over 20 points in the young season, and Arenas headed into Sunday's contest with his 23.3 points per game leading the Big Ten in scoring.

"I think we have one of the most dynamic backcourts in the country," said Musselman. "I'm super impressed with how unselfish they are for the amount of points they're scoring. I thought Alijah tonight, his efficiency, you couldn't draw it up that well."

Rice also had high praise for the sophomore guard, who is looking to string together his first fully healthy season.

"It's fun playing with Alijah," he said. "He runs the break well, scores the ball incredibly well, so I feel confident when he has the ball, and I trust in his decision-making and shot-making. I love playing with Alijah."

Jacob Cofie provided solid starting minutes at the forward position alongside Adonis, scoring 10 points and adding two assists. The junior had his first double-double of the season on Wednesday at Cal, playing all 40 minutes in the contest.

Guard Jalen Cox gave USC its biggest boost off the bench, scoring 17 points on an 8-of-10 showing from the field. It was a season high for the senior.

Freshman forward Christian Collins, who missed the first three games of the season for the Trojans, suited up for the first time on Sunday to make his collegiate debut. He checked into the game with just over 12 minutes remaining in the first half, logging 12 minutes total in the contest.

Collins scored just two points on 1-of-2 shooting, but the five-star recruit was on a minutes restriction and could see his role increase as the season progresses. The St. John Bosco product is one of five former McDonald's All-Americans on USC's roster.

Off to a 4-0 start for the second straight year, USC will host the Tarleton State Texans next Thursday evening, looking to keep their momentum rolling. The Texans are 3-1 this season and have not faced the Trojans before.
