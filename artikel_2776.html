Pat Bryant off IR; to play in Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Denver Broncos WR Pat Bryant (ankle) was activated from the Reserve/Injured list on Saturday, Nov. 7.

Why it Matters: Head coach Sean Payton said Bryant is expected to come off the reserve/injured list to play Sunday in Week 9 and that's exactly what is happening. Prior to Bryant's injury, he had at least two receptions and 44 yards in his two games prior to injury, while posting a TD in Week 3. After missing four games, Bryant is a merely a bench stash in fantasy but has some contingent value and upside as a WR4.
