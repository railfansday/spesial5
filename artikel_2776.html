Spurs drop NBA Cup game to Trail Blazers despite Wembanyama’s big game

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

It felt like a playoff game in the Moda Center on the night before Thanksgiving. Maybe it was the drive to win an NBA Cup game, or maybe it was the Portland Trail Blazers wanting revenge on the San Antonio Spurs from last year's playoffs, and a thumping earlier this season. Either way, the Blazers were motivated, and the crowd was raucous. The Spurs kept pace for most of the game, but faltered late in a 116-111 loss.

It looked like Victor Wembanyama would will the Spurs to victory without De'Aaron Fox and Dylan Harper, who missed the game with injuries. Wembanyama had it going in the post, scoring 44 points on 17-for-30 shooting. Immediately, he took Blazers center Donovan Clingan into the low post, scoring over him or forcing him to foul, eventually leading to Clingan fouling out.

However, Wembanyama's effort wasn't enough to overcome Portland getting stellar performances from two of their stars. Damian Lillard looked like prime Dame, hitting three after three over the Spurs defenders. Lillard had 29 points and 6 assists while shooting 5-of-12 from three. He was outshined by his teammate, Deni Avdija, who hit the big bank three at the end of the game to seal the win for Portland. He had 33 points, 12 rebounds, and 7 assists on an efficient 11-of-21 shooting from the field.

San Antonio came into the fourth quarter seemingly taking control. A Devin Vassell fadeaway three-pointer to beat the third-quarter buzzer put the team up three points. After struggling to shoot the ball in the first half, the team got it going in the third quarter, shooting 6-of-10 from three-point range. With Portland out of challenges and its big man in foul trouble, the Spurs had several advantages to steal a win on the road.

Immediately, the Blazers went on a 12-2 run to take a 7-point lead early in the fourth quarter. They played with a lot of force on both ends. That was particularly clear when Sidy Cissoko drove right into Wembanyama and dunked over the top of him. The former Spur had 16 points, knocking down four of his ten three-pointers.

All game long, but especially down the stretch, the Blazers were able to get into the Spurs' ball-handlers' space and force mistakes. San Antonio turned the ball over 14 times, giving up 14 points off turnovers. That defensive pressure especially hurt the Spurs in the final two minutes of the game, when San Antonio turned the ball over two possessions in a row with the chance to tie the game.

Stephon Castle took on the majority of the ball-handling responsibilities and had an up-and-down game. Castle finished with 20 points, 9 assists, 6 rebounds, and 3 turnovers while shooting 6-of-18 from the field. He was stopped in the backcourt by Toumani Camara for an 8-second violation late in the game that gave the Blazers a shot of momentum heading into the end of the game.

Overall, it was hard for the Spurs to find an offensive rhythm outside of forcing the ball to Wembanyama in the high or low post. San Antonio shot just 43% from the field and 28% from three.

With the loss, the Spurs fall to 2-1 in the Western Conference Group C. They are in a four-way tie for first with Portland, the Golden State Warriors, and the Los Angeles Lakers. The Spurs play the Lakers, and the Blazers play the Warriors on Friday. San Antonio needs to win that game, and Portland to lose, to win the group outright. San Antonio may have a shot at the wild card spot if the Blazers and Spurs win.
