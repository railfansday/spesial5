NFL Week 11 odds and predictions: Dolphins vs. Bills, full schedule

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Miami Dolphins fell to the Indianapolis Colts 24-22 in Week 10, and face a much tougher opponent in Week 11. The Dolphins face their AFC East rivals, the Buffalo Bills, on the road this weekend, a game that features a team tied for the worst record in the AFC visiting the team with the best record in the conference. Division games are typically closer than expected, but this one might be the exception that proves the rule.

Miami is relying on rookies and first-year players across the roster this year, including quarterback Kyle McCord. An undrafted rookie in 2025, McCord spent his entire rookie season on the Philadelphia Eagles' practice squad. He looked composed last week against the Colts and he will look to carry that into Week 11. The good news for McCord and the Dolphins' offense is Buffalo's pass defense ranks just 28th in the league, allowing 241.9 passing yards per game.

Miami faces a tough battle this week, but they are also a team that has played tough all season. They may have only won one of their first nine games, but they have competed in most of them, and they are clearly growing as the complete franchise rebuild progresses into the second half of the year.

The Dolphins are 16.5-point underdogs for Sunday, a line that has held fairly steady throughout the week. The moneyline has Buffalo at -1,600 to win straight up, while the Dolphins are +900. The combined point total is 44.5.
