NFL Week 11 Survivor/Eliminator Picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

NFL Survivor has been a (slightly) easier game the last few weeks. Favorites have been winning a lot of games, which means that you have probably stayed alive in your contests. That said, you have also likely been burning through some of the key usable teams, which makes every subsequent week a little tougher. That brings us to this week's picks. Check out my Week 11 pick for Survivor contests. And while we're here, you can use our NFL Betting Model and our Bet Tracker to make a pick for yourself.

Every week, we have the same process. We eliminate teams in groups until we narrow it down to our real considerations. Start with all 32, whittle it down. And remember — this game is "once you use a team, they're off the board," so it's not always just a matter of taking the team you think will win. There has to be some strategy. I can't pick a team I've picked before, but you might not have tailed me, so previously used teams are eligible until the end.

My favorite category — picks I can't get wrong! If you don't play, you can't win, but also I won't pick you, so woohoo for easy. Atlanta Falcons Cleveland Browns Green Bay Packers Los Angeles Rams New England Patriots Seattle Seahawks That's six off the board.

Maybe these are good teams, maybe not, but it's too hard to trust them given who they are facing. This is a 10-team list every week (fewer when there are byes); we are eliminating anyone facing a top-10 team in our FTN NFL Power Rankings. Arizona Cardinals (@ Kansas City) Las Vegas Raiders (@ Denver) Miami Dolphins (@ Buffalo) Minnesota Vikings (vs. San Francisco in Mexico City) New Orleans Saints (@ Chicago) New York Giants (vs. Jacksonville) San Francisco 49ers (vs. Minnesota in Mexico City) Washington Commanders (vs. Cincinnati) Some of these teams will win but you can't confidently say so, considering who they're facing. That's eight more off the board. 18 left.

You can face the worst team in all of football, but if you're the second-worst team, well, I'm still not going to be too excited about selecting you. We'll return to the power rankings here and cross off any team in the bottom 12. Baltimore Ravens (@ Carolina) Indianapolis Colts (@ Houston) Los Angeles Chargers (vs. NY Jets) New York Jets (@ LA Chargers) Philadelphia Eagles (vs. Pittsburgh) Tennessee Titans (@ Dallas) Six more eliminations leaves us with 12 remaining.

Island games just get weird. I would need some very convincing evidence to pick anyone on a Thursday, or a Monday, or an overseas game. There aren't many left this week, but I still want to note them. Cincinnati Bengals (@ Washington) Houston Texans (vs. Indianapolis) Two more gone, 10 left. There's no good way to eliminate a chunk from here, so this week, we have a 10-team list of contenders. That's longer than I'd like, but oh well.
