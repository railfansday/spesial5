Rams get devastating Puka Nacua update as playoff hopes take hit

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The last sliver of hope that Puka Nacua might return for a Rams playoff run has officially disappeared.

The Rams' star wide receiver is expected to miss four to five months recovering from surgery to repair the MCL in his right knee, ruling him out for the remainder of the 2026 NFL season, including the playoffs, according to ESPN's Adam Schefter.

The updated recovery timeline is significantly longer than the initial estimate Rams coach Sean McVay provided Friday, when he suggested Nacua would be sidelined for "a couple of months" but wouldn't completely rule out a postseason return.

Los Angeles Rams wide receiver Puka Nacua reacts after winning an NFL football game against the Washington Commanders in Landover, Md., Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026. AP Photo/Stephanie Scarbrough

According to Schefter, three doctors recommended that Nacua undergo surgery, which was performed Tuesday by renowned orthopedic surgeon and Rams team physician Dr. Neal ElAttrache.

The lone silver lining? Medical evaluations reportedly found no additional damage beyond the MCL, and Nacua is expected to make a full recovery in time for the 2027 season.

"I thought when the initial injury occurred that this was more likely the direction that this would go," McVay said Friday. "This isn't anything necessarily new on our end."

Nacua suffered the knee injury, along with turf toe, on the Rams' final offensive snap of their Week 9 victory over Washington. He was placed on injured reserve Nov. 12.

The two-time Pro Bowler finishes his fourth NFL season with 70 receptions for 715 yards and four touchdowns in just seven games.

Nacua was suspended for three games by the NFL on Thursday.

According to Schefter, the league was unaware Nacua had already undergone surgery when it handed down the suspension.

For Nacua, 25, the stakes extend beyond this season. He's scheduled to become an unrestricted free agent after playing out the final year of his rookie contract.

McVay acknowledged that the decision to undergo surgery wasn't simply about salvaging the Rams' season but protecting Nacua's future.

"There's a lot of thought and conversations that go into whether you're going to do that or not and what's the best plan of action for return to performance for him," McVay said. "Not exclusive to this year but for his career moving forward."

Whether that career continues with the Rams is going to be a huge storyline this offseason.
