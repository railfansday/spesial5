RSM Classic FedEx Cup Fall bubble: Who’s in, who’s out for 2027 PGA Tour cards after 36 holes

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Michael Thorbjornsen won the Rocket Classic in July and has already locked up his playing privileges for next season on the PGA Tour, but he detailed what the players at the RSM Classic endured on a cold, windy day on St. Simons Island, Georgia, that was better suited for indoor activities.

"Some of the toughest stretch of golf I've ever had to pay," said Thorbjornsen, who played alongside Brooks Koepka and Blades Brown. "You'd pay money on every tee box for a par."

"On hole 1, I think Brooks, Blades and I hit our drives around 240 (yards) straight into the wind. That kind of shows what it was like out there," he said. "I don't think I've ever hit a drive that short in my life, condition-wise."

Well, the good news is there's only 36 holes to go in the RSM Classic and Thorbjornsen managed a 3-under 69, his 10th round of 69 or better at either the Seaside or Plantation courses at Sea Island Resort. That lifted him to 9-under 133 and a share of the lead at the midway point with Marco Penge and Rasmus Neergaard-Petersen.

The bad news? It's probably going to be raining sideways with wind forecasted to gust up to 40 miles per hour on Saturday.

"It is what it is," Thorbjornsen said of the third round weather forecast. "Everyone has to play in those conditions and we'll just try our best to get the ball in the fairway and hit some greens."

There's still a lot at stake besides the trophy this week as players jockey for position in the tournament within the tournament.

The top 100 in the FedEx Cup Fall standings following the RSM Classic become exempt on the PGA Tour for the 2027 season. The FedEx Cup Fall also determines eligibility for early-season signature events, with Nos. 51-60 in the final standings earning spots in the 2027 AT&T Pebble Beach Pro-Am and 2027 Genesis Invitational.

Nos. 101-110 earn conditional status for the 2027 season via the 101-110 Prior Season FedEx Cup Fall Points List category, which is not subject to reshuffle

Nos. 111-125 earn conditional status for the 2027 season via the 111-125 Prior Season FedEx Cup Fall Points List category, which is subject to reshuffle

Nos. 126-150 earn conditional status for the 2027 season via the 126-150 Prior Season FedEx Cup Fall Points List category, which is subject to reshuffle. This category will earn limited access to PGA Tour events.

Three players are projected to move into the top 100. Neergaard-Peterson would leap from No. 106 to No. 57 and also gain two signature event starts with a win; Penge would vault from No. 120 to No. 65 should he claim the title; and Lee Hodges, who is one back and T-4, is protected to jump from No. 105 to No. 94.

Three players are projected to drop out of the top 100. John Parry and David Lipsky fell to Nos. 101 and 102 and both missed the cut and no longer control their own destiny. Christiaan Bezuidenhout rallied to make the cut and sits T-41. He entered the week as the 'Bubble Boy' at No. 100 and is currently projected at No. 103.
