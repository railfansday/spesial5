Eagles Injury Report: Saquon Barkley upgraded to limited

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Philadelphia Eagles issued their second official injury report of the week on Thursday in advance of their Week 9 game against the New York Giants.

The Eagles listed seven players under DID NOT PARTICIPATE: DeVonta Smith, Dontayvion Wicks, Jihaad Campbell, Michael Carter II, Britain Covey, and Jonathan Jones.

Smith and Wicks are both tracking to miss their third straight game. With Covey also looking unlikely to play, the Eagles will be down to Darius Cooper, Makai Lemon, Hollywood Brown, and Elijah Moore at wide receiver.

Campbell is tracking to miss his second game in a row. Jeremiah Trotter Jr. will make another start at off-ball linebacker.

MC2 will likely be out after aggravating an abdomen injury in Week 8.

If Covey can't play, look for Cooper DeJean to return punts.

Jones' absence will leave the Eagles in need of a new top backup cornerback.

The Eagles listed five players under LIMITED PARTICIPATION: Saquon Barkley, Tanner McKee, Jake Elliott, Jalyx Hunt, and Jonathan Greenard.

Barkley was upgraded to limited after not practicing on Wednesday. It doesn't seem great to me that Barkley is playing despite clearly being hampered and in pain. Especially when the Eagles have a chance to sit him down for this game ahead of a Week 10 bye, giving him a two-week break. Nevertheless, here we are.

McKee was downgraded from full participation with a chest injury on Wednesday to limited with a rib injury on Thursday. Conspiracy theory: Maybe an injury is being embellished here to stealth bench McKee to being the emergency third quarterback? Maybe the Eagles want Cole Payton to be the No. 2. now. We'll see.

Elliott, Hunt, and Greenard were limited for the second day in a row. They'll probably be able to play, though Hunt and Greenard will likely be on snap limits.

The Eagles listed eight players under FULL PARTICIPATION: Jordan Mailata, Landon Dickerson, Dallas Goedert, Zack Baun, Jordan Davis, Moro Ojomo, Markel Bell, and Kenny Moore II.

Mailata is a new addition to the report with an elbow injury.

Dickerson, Goedert, Baun, Davis, Ojomo, Bell, and Moore were all full go for the second day in a row.

Giants leading receiver Malik Nabers missed practice on Thursday but told reporters he'll be good for Sunday.

Giants leading rusher Cam Skattebo missed practice for the second day in a row. He's tracking to miss Sunday's game. With Tyrone Tracy recently placed on injured reserve, New York could be down to Devin Singletary and Gregory Desrosiers.

Starting defensive lineman Darius Alexander in a new addition to the report. He apparently suffered a foot injury in Thursday's practice. Something to monitor.

Giants co-sack leader Abdul Carter was upgraded to full after missing Wednesday due to illness.
