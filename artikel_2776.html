MMA Junkie rankings, Nov. 3: New faces emerge

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

UFC Fight Night 292 featured very few ranked names, but that meant an opportunity for unranked fighters to knock on the door of the top 15.

In the main event, Tom Nolan picked up a TKO stoppage win over Renato Moicano. The fight result was extremely controversial due to the referee's decision to stand up the fight while Moicano had back control with a body triangle locked in, but Nolan did his part after by displaying excellent striking to end the fight. Nolan was unranked before the fight, and now settles among the honorable mentions at lightweight. Moicano falls out.

The only ranked vs. ranked matchup on the card was a women's bantamweight bout between fighters sharing the same last name. Luana Santos won a decision over Yana Santos, causing movement for each. Luana moves up to No. 6, while Yana falls to No. 10.
