Strangers Raise $800,000 for Red Baron Worker Filmed Being Harassed by Prankster

A Red Baron pizza worker who was filmed being harassed by a prankster pretending to steal $20 from him has paused donations on a GoFundMe page set up on his behalf after it neared $800,000.

The prank severely backfired on the young man filming the video after the Red Baron worker lost his temper with him.

Red Baron pizza stocker goes viral for going off on kid trying to prank him for a social media video. $400k has now been raised for him online & Red Baron says he’s employee of the month. Crazy this “pranker” uploaded this video at all. https://t.co/DYjhP4JvlQ

— Clay Travis (@ClayTravis) October 30, 2026

The wannabe influencer approaches the Red Baron worker, known only as Walt Z, as he was stacking shelves in a grocery store. In the video, he asks for the time, and when Walt gets out his phone, he plays the Apple Pay sound and says, “thanks for the $10.”

It appears the prankster is filming on Meta Ray-Bans and has at least one accomplice recording nearby.

Walt initially walks away, but the prankster comes back for more and says, “Thank you for the $20.” This causes Walt to snap.

“I don’t have time for this cr*p in my life; my life is hard enough as it is,” says Walt, visibly upset. “I got kids at home who went to bed without me… and I was supposed to be at home an hour ago, and I’m still out here working.”

“I’ve got to get up at five in the morning and do 10 more stores,” Walt continues. “So this is not a joke to me.”

“You’re probably making money off this, and I can’t even pay my goddamn mortgage,” Walt adds. “And I’ve got four kids at home.”

It’s a powerful tirade that caught many people’s attention. Fortune reports that the GoFundMe page was set up by a man called Luke Oakenshield who has never met Walt but wanted to track him down after watching the video, which was filmed back in August but only published recently.

The campaign raised $100,000 in three days, with former Republican presidential hopeful Vivek Ramaswamy donating $38,000. After it came close to $800,000, Walt posted a message on GoFundMe thanking people for their support and announcing that he is pausing donations and urging people to support other families in need. He adds that he won’t be giving any interviews.

Call them rage-bait pranks or clout-chasing harassment; these types of videos are a modern phenomenon that has its roots firmly in photography.

Meta’s smart glasses, which were apparently used to film the video of Walt, are just the latest tool in a long line of clandestine cameras. But with this kind of camera technology being made available to every Tom, Dick, and Harry, it’s becoming clear that society is losing its tolerance for such cruel pranksters.
