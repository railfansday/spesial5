Mourinho stands in the way of Endrick’s move

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Endrick is Roma’s rumored top target for January, and, eager to find more playing time, he’d welcome a possible future at Gian Piero Gasperini’s Roma.

Mourinho, however, is ready to nip the issue in the bud.

According to Il Corriere Dello Sport, the Portuguese coach is firmly convinced that the boy’s immense potential must be nurtured directly within his own four walls, blindly betting that, between now and the end of the season, Endrick’s contribution will prove to be an absolutely decisive factor in the team’s fortunes.

Furthermore, the possibility of a loan with a simple option to buy, an option that the Giallorossi management would strongly like to pursue to safeguard their position, is not seen by the other side as a real added value.
