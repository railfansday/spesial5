Gittens on loan to Roma remains a possibility

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Tony D’Amico is seeking opportunities in the Premier League to strengthen Gian Piero Gasperini’s attack.

The Grugliasco-born coach needs a capable left-back who can provide additional options. Roma are hoping for another Malen-style signing.

Immediately following the dream of Endrick (whose presence remains a constant threat from Mourinho), Jamie Gittens appears on the Roma sporting director’s list.

The 2004-born player could be the breakthrough winger missing from the squad, giving Gasperini a different weapon to continue his record-breaking attack in Serie A.

Roma is closely monitoring the future of Jamie Gittens. As reported by La Gazzetta dello Sport, the move is being made possible by the Englishman’s desire to join the Giallorossi project, given that he has no playing time at Chelsea.

The 2004-born player would also be reunited with Malen in the capital, with whom he shared his time at Borussia Dortmund.
