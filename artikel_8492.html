Choose Truist One Checking if you live near a Truist branch and qualify for a fee waiver, such as $500 a month in direct deposits or $500 across your Truist accounts. You’ll pay no monthly fee and no overdraft fees. Pick an online bank instead if you live outside Truist’s 17 states and D.C. or want interest.

Truist One Checking is Truist’s everyday checking account. Its $12 monthly fee, $144 a year, is also waived when the primary owner is a student, under 25, or 62 or older, and for anyone with a Truist mortgage, consumer loan, or personal credit card.

Customers near a Truist branch who already meet a fee waiver get branch banking with no monthly or overdraft fees. Savers who want interest, and anyone far from a Truist branch, should look to an online bank, while anyone who’d rather have a purchase declined than overdraw can open the checkless Truist Confidence Account.
