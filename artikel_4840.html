Our recent research (Crawford and colleagues, 2026) argues that despite recognizing the importance of feeling included and engaged, we have largely ignored the psychological need to belong in the workplace. In short, it is not enough to be included in meetings and in decision-making. Workers need to feel like they truly belong.
