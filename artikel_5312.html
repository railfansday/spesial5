The KAS price prediction hinges on one immediate question: can buyers defend the $0.03 to $0.032 demand zone? Kaspa is testing that area after a pullback, and a convincing rebound hasn’t been confirmed yet. Traders looking for a recovery have several resistance levels to watch before any $0.065 target becomes realistic.

The current price is around $0.03186, placing KAS inside its marked local demand range. A clear rejection of lower prices could suggest buyers are returning. But hoping for a bounce and confirming one are two different things.

The next requirement is a higher low. Without that, the recovery remains a chart scenario rather than an established trend reversal.

The first hurdle sits at $0.035 to $0.037. A successful reclaim, followed by a pullback that holds, would improve the short-term structure.

Above that, attention shifts to $0.042 to $0.045, identified as key supply. Clearing this zone could bring descending resistance and the recent swing area around $0.050 into focus. Daily confirmation matters; a brief move above resistance isn’t enough.

If KAS clears $0.050 and establishes support, the next major supply zone sits at $0.058 to $0.060. Beyond that, the chart outlines $0.065 as a potential objective.

That’s a substantial move from current levels, but the route isn’t automatic. Each reclaimed resistance area needs to become support before the next advance looks convincing.

The downside is equally clear. Daily acceptance below $0.030 would weaken the KAS price prediction and put deeper support at $0.027 to $0.0285 in focus. For now, the demand reaction matters more than the ambitious target.
