Malik Nabers on sideline moment: I was just trying to watch the game

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Late in Sunday’s win over the Jaguars, video of the Giants’ sideline showed quarterback Jameis Winston trying to restrain receiver Malik Nabers. Nabers eventually worked his way past Winston and moved toward the edge of the field.

“I vaguely remember it,” Nabers told reporters, via Charlotte Carroll of The Athletic. “But, I mean, I just remember me saying, ‘I’m good. I’m good. I wanna get back to watching the defense play.’ And that was really all, I think. Everything was going on, I was just telling the offense, ‘Let’s go, let’s go,’ I think. Jameis kind of [was] trying to calm me down, but I was fine. So I was just telling him, ‘I’m good. I’m good. I just wanna watch the game.”

The fact that Nabers navigated past Winston and got into position to watch the game seems to confirm Nabers’s explanation. He just wanted to watch the game.

The problem is that Winston’s reaction to Nabers’s initial movement created the impression that something else was happening. No quotes on the situation have emerged from Winston. It would be interesting to know why he thought Nabers needed to be restrained at a time when restraint ultimately wasn’t necessary.

Either way, the moment went viral — and more people will see that video and draw possibly inaccurate conclusions than will hear Nabers’s explanation.
