AFC North’s changing of the guard from Steelers/Ravens to Bengals/Browns happening fast

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

For years- really, decades- the AFC North has seen the Cleveland Browns in the basement, with the Baltimore Ravens and Pittsburgh Steelers running the show. The Cincinnati Bengals have had windows of time when they join the top two, but they haven't had the kind of consistency that the others have.

The Browns have been consistently bad, with just a couple of playoff appearances and no AFC North titles.

Things have started to change during the 2026 NFL season, with Cincinnati and Cleveland at the top of the division, even if Pittsburgh had the same 6-4 record as the second-place team. Baltimore has fallen off without Lamar Jackson.

Now, with the news of the Steelers benching Aaron Rodgers late on Tuesday night, after he practiced as the team's starter the first two days of the week, the bloom seems to be off in Mike McCarthy's first year. As our Producer at Behind the Steel Curtain said, Pittsburgh needs to reset everything in order to rebuild:

The Ravens may be looking at a similar turnover with Jackson's contract at a point where the team either needs to try to extend him (something they've tried and failed to do), trade him, or release him this offseason. Given Jackson's price tag, injury history, and limited playoff success, Baltimore's future may not include their MVP-winning quarterback.

There are many around Baltimore and Pittsburgh's fanbases who are hoping that Jesse Minter and McCarthy are one-and-done coaches, whereas Cincinnati's Zac Taylor was the one holdover in the division, and Todd Monken has had a positive start to his tenure in Cleveland.

With the Bengals having stability at quarterback, an offensive line coming together well, great weapons on offense, and a defense coming together well, Cincinnati might be the cream of the crop in the AFC North for a few seasons. The Browns could be close behind, with a young, exciting roster of talented players all over the roster, extra draft picks over the next couple of years, and a coach who is making everything work ahead of schedule.

By the time the next decade starts, we could be looking back at the 2026 NFL season as the time that the changing of the guard in the AFC North was fast-tracked, with the State of Ohio on top for a number of years.
