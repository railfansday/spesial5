Real Madrid: serious accusations against Bellingham and Rudiger

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Real Madrid had a particularly eventful evening on the Valencia pitch. If the Merengues displayed their superiority on the field (3-0), the meeting was also marked by numerous tensions between the two teams. In a particularly hostile Mestalla venue, the Valencian players tried to thwart José Mourinho’s men by multiplying duels and provocations. Jude Bellingham notably found himself at the heart of several heated exchanges with Pepelu and Pablo Maffeo, even forcing Federico Valverde to intervene to calm his teammate. But it was when returning to the locker room after the break that the situation really degenerated. According to information revealed by A Punt Esportsthen detailed by ABC And Marcaa violent altercation broke out in the Mestalla tunnel, involving several Madrid players and members of security.

It all started with the protests of Braulio Vázquez, Valencia’s sporting director, who was particularly upset with the refereeing, particularly after a foul preceding Madrid’s first goal. “It’s not a foul, it’s not a foul!” Amazing ! »he would have repeated vehemently to one of the assistants. An attitude which required the intervention of the Spanish national police, as specified in the official report of the referee. But according to MarcaJude Bellingham then intervened by grabbing the Valencian leader’s suit to pull him away from the official, causing a new rise in tension. Several players from both teams then found themselves at the heart of a scuffle, forcing three security agents to intervene to try to separate the protagonists and push the Madrilenians back to their locker room.

And the accusations are particularly serious. According to the first revelations of the journalist Nacho Sanchis, of the À Punt channel, Jude Bellingham would have hit a security agent, while Antonio Rüdiger would have slapped another member of the system responsible for calming the spirits. Information also reported by ABC, which mentions the presence of Éder Militão at the time of the incidents. For its part, Marca provides new details on the progress of this altercation. The Spanish daily claims that Rüdiger allegedly punched an agent in the face while the latter was trying to separate the players. Ibrahima Konaté also allegedly tried to calm the situation by repeatedly asking the protagonists to calm down. According to the Madrid media, Valencia even has video images showing the incidents, in particular the gesture of the German defender. Accusations that could place the two Real Madrid players in a delicate situation.

But one element is particularly noteworthy in Spain: the absence of these incidents in the referee’s official report. Although informed by the security agents, José Francisco Hernández Maeso did not mention any altercation involving the Madrid players. Only Braulio Vázquez appears in the report, the Valencian sporting director having had to be restrained and removed by security after his protests. A situation that Valencia CF does not understand, especially since the club has video evidence of the scuffle. Despite Real Madrid’s victory (3-0), this evening at Mestalla could therefore leave its mark. It remains to be seen whether the images evoked by the Spanish press will give rise to an examination by the disciplinary authorities and possible sanctions against the players concerned.
