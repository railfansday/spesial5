Real Madrid confirms Arda Guler contract extension until 2032

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Real Madrid has confirmed that Arda Guler has signed a contract extension until June 30, 2032, securing the Turkish playmaker’s long-term future at the Bernabeu.

The club announced the agreement on Wednesday, having brought Guler to Spain in July 2023 at the age of 18. Now in his fourth season, he has made 126 appearances for Madrid.

His time at the club has brought five trophies: the Champions League, Club World Cup, UEFA Super Cup, La Liga and Spanish Super Cup. Madrid also highlighted the Champions League breakthrough player award he received in September for his performances during the 2025-26 campaign. Guler has earned 38 caps for Turkey, establishing himself on the international stage alongside his development in Madrid

The announcement follows reports of an agreement in October, when Arsenal and Manchester City were credited with earlier interest in the midfielder. Those reports suggested a deal running until at least 2031; Madrid has now confirmed the expiry date as 2032. The club has not disclosed the financial terms.
