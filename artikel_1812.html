Liverpool: Andoni Iraola predicts how Manchester United will change under Mauricio Pochettino

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola expects a much tougher test from Manchester United following the appointment of Mauricio Pochettino.

It has been all change for today's visitors to Anfield with Michael Carrick sacked and Pochettino hired, though the Argentine only arrived on Wednesday and did not oversee training until Thursday.

But Iraola expects the new face in the opposition dugout to have his charges firing on all cyclinders, despite limited time implementing new tactics.

Writing in his programme notes for Sunday's game, Iraola wrote: “Liverpool versus Manchester United can only ever create a sense of anticipation, no matter when and where the game is played and no matter who is involved.

“For us it is a privilege and it is one that comes with a lot of responsibility because of what this rivalry means. The eyes of the world will be on us and we should use this as a motivation.

“We want – and will almost certainly need – to be as near to our best as we can. That is the standard of this fixture.

“What people think the league table tells us in this moment is not something that we can afford to be distracted by. This is a day when this kind of thing does not matter in the same way that it might otherwise. The history of the rivalry between our two clubs tells us that.

“We should expect and prepare for the best version of United because they will definitely see this fixture as an opportunity to start showing the best of themselves, particularly under new management.

“For this reason, we have to be focused only on delivering the strongest performance that we possibly can and I can tell you that the players have been incredibly focused on this in training from the moment they returned from the international period."

Despite enduring a tumultuous few seasons, Man Utd have continued to enjoy success against Liverpool. They beat them home and away last season and have lost just one of the last seven meetings.

And Iraola feels the fans will play a crucial part in the teams' performance and end result.

He continued: “Making the most of Anfield is something that we do not just want to do, we absolutely have to. The support has been there – at times it has been next level – so it is up to us to build on this by getting more positive results here.

“Up to now I have only experienced this fixture from a distance but I know the atmosphere will be pretty special. Again, using this is vital. In the games against Manchester City and Arsenal there were times when we were close to doing this but for one reason or another we didn’t quite manage it, so we need this to change.

“That means being all in from the very start and in every single moment from then on. It also means making the most of each situation. If we can do this I have no doubt that our chances of getting the kind of result that our fans and ourselves want will only increase.

“As always, the responsibility is with us so hopefully on and off the pitch we can show the best face of Liverpool by being together from the first whistle to the last.”
