Crawley Town 0, Barnet 0: Reds hold high-flying Bees in Broadfield stalemate to pick up another vital point

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Reds made one change from the weekend’s win over Fleetwood, as captain Jude Arthurs served a one-match ban due to accumulating too many yellow cards. Lewis Richards came in for the suspended Arthurs, and Charlie Barker picked up the captain’s armband.

In a fairly uneventful first half, neither side really troubled the respective goalkeepers. Kellan Gordon had a couple of shots early in the first half, one was well over, and the other was palmed away for a corner by former Reds’ loanee Matthew Cox.

The first real chance of the game fell to Barnet on 40 minutes, when Tom Knowles received a short corner, and somehow managed to get to the edge of the box, and his shot curled past Mannone but back off the post and was eventually cleared.

Crawley’s box was under a lot of pressure in the second half. However, it was the Reds who had the first effort of the half. Dylan Morgan got hold of a half clearance, laid it off to Lewis Richards, who fired a shot goalward, but it was well saved by Cox for a corner.

It was then the Bees who had the better chances. Firstly, Kellan Gordon gave away a free kick on the edge of the box by Zac Williams, which bobbles around the box and eventually finds a way past the post with Mannone beaten.

A few minutes later, Idris Kanu had a shot parried by Mannone, but only to Charlie Lakin, who hit the bar and then dribbled a shot past the post. Mannone was having a great game, and he was called into action again when Crichlow-Noble latched onto a free kick from just outside the box, and Mannone parried away through a crowd of players.

Lakin had another shot from just inside the box but saved by Mannone from a tight angle. Adam Senior also had a great chance, as his header down bounced back to him and he fired over the bar from close range.

The game finally settled down in the final 20 minutes, and Crawley managed to see the game out for a valuable point, which sees the Reds level on points with Rotherham in 23rd, with a game in hand.

Crawley’s next game is away to Exeter City for a 12:30pm kick off this Saturday, and then at home to Port Vale the following Tuesday (1st Dec).
