Fabian Hürzeler gives brilliant answer to Gary Lineker's Brighton title race theory

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Gary Lineker argued that the Seagulls could become genuine title contenders – and be ‘very difficult to stop’ – if they had a striker in the mould of former Leicester City forward Jamie Vardy.

All-time leading Premier League goalscorer Alan Shearer agreed but also praised the quality of recent performances, including the surprise defeat at Hull City.

Hürzeler insists he is happy with the attacking options already at his disposal, pointing to Brighton’s goalscoring record and the number of players capable of finding the net. Albion lead the Premier League goals scored table, finding the net 26 times in 11 games.

Asked about Lineker and Shearer’s praise for Brighton as one of the most entertaining teams in the league, Hürzeler welcomed the recognition but stressed that performances, rather than outside opinion, must remain the priority.

“If they say this, of course we should listen to it,” he said.

“On the other side, being the best football team doesn't mean you automatically win games.

“We really have to keep focusing on our performances, that’s what we can control. We can't control what others think about us.

“We can only control how well we prepare for a game and how high our levels are on matchday.

“The truth is always on the pitch, and therefore we have to get back to our highest level.”

Hürzeler was more emphatic when asked whether he agreed with Lineker’s suggestion that Brighton were missing a clinical, traditional centre-forward.

“An Alan Shearer?” the German replied, tongue-in-cheek.

“No, no. I think we have a lot of good strikers. When you look at how many goals we've scored in the season so far, and how many goals we scored today.

"We might not have one striker but we have plenty of players who are able to score goals.

“That's what has made us strong in the past and what will make us strong in the future, hopefully on Sunday as well.”

The Brighton head coach was speaking after his side responded to their disappointing 2-0 defeat at Hull City with a 4-0 victory over Craiova in the UEFA Conference League.

Next up is a home match against Newcastle United, in the Premier League, this weekend.

Hürzeler specifically named the attacking players he believes can provide the goals, making clear that he has no concerns about the options available to him.

He added: “We're very happy with Kostoulas. We're very happy with [Georginio] Rutter.

"We're also happy with Tobi [Promise David], who came onto the pitch today against Craiova.”

The head coach also appeared keen to ensure that the defeat at Hull did not define the assessment of his team's attacking play.

Brighton created opportunities at the MKM Stadium but were unable to find a way past an inspired goalkeeper, in Konstantinos Tzolakis, prompting Lineker to suggest that a striker with Vardy's instincts could make them much harder to stop.

Hürzeler, however, would rather focus on the wider picture than one frustrating afternoon in front of goal.

“We're focusing on the positives,” he said.

"We are not focusing on one game where we missed a few chances.”

The 4-0 victory over Craiova offered an immediate reminder of Brighton's goalscoring capabilities, although Hürzeler will now want his players to carry that performance into Sunday's match.
