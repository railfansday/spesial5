Steven Pressley answers Dundee resignation question after Hearts thrashing - but no Hibs appeal planned

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Steven Pressley did not consider quitting in the wake of Dundee suffering their biggest-ever home defeat against Hearts on Saturday night.

The 8-0 loss led the manager to take full blame afterwards. He said he wanted to “crawl under a rock and hide” following such a landmark reversal.

The 53-year-old has had little time to ponder his future. Dundee are back in action as soon as Tuesday night at Easter Road against Hibs, who are themselves hurting following a 4-0 home defeat against Dundee United in their last outing. "It has been a tough 24, 48 hours," said Pressley. "There’s a lot of embarrassment, a lot of disappointment."

He stressed there was also a determination to learn from the most challenging experience of his managerial career. Although he has spoken before of coming close to walking away on at least two occasions last season, resigning had not crossed his mind this time.

“Not this weekend, no,” he said. “I've had to find strength from I don't know where, but not this weekend. Not this weekend. But it was hugely embarrassing.

“It probably ranks as the most embarrassing moment of my managerial career thus far.

“I'm 17, 18 years into my coaching career and it's why, in football, you can never become complacent.

“Because nothing ever surprises you. You think you've lived through everything, and then something else comes along. And very much that was Saturday.”

Pressley has admitted considering quitting after disappointing results in his first season at Dundee, one of which was a 4-0 defeat at Pittodrie against Aberdeen. Dundee recovered from a poor start to the campaign and Pressley succeeded in getting his critics firmly on side.

However, the historic nature of Saturday’s result meant he found himself under fire again from some quarters. Pressley has resolved to find the answers. Dundee will be without key defender Owen Bevan, who was sent off on Saturday night, against Hibs.

The club have decided against appealing the decision. “We accept the punishment,” said Pressley. “It is not ideal but I don’t think there was a real chance of it being overturned.”

Pressley suggested there would not be a radical overhaul of the side or system. “It is difficult to change a lot in two days so we have to be very careful," he said. "But we have thought a lot about what we can do. There has to be a change in (the suspended) Owen Bevan. There is a lot to consider. But the most important thing is we give a response and try to get some level of pride back.”
