Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Chelsea could well have two key men back when they welcome Leeds this weekend.

The Blues were beaten 1-0 by Sunderland just before the international break, and they have been without a number of stars, which has left Xabi Alonso with a headache.

The west Londoners have dropped to fifth in the Premier League table as a result of the defeat, but they are hoping to have some of their big names back on Saturday.

With that in mind, here is the latest Chelsea injury news and return dates…

Acheampong was left out of the squad at the Stadium of Light, which was a surprise having started all five games before that.

Alonso revealed, however, that the youngster was dealing with a minor knock, and he withdrew from international duty with England’s under-21 side.

“He is recovering from a minor thing,” Alonso said after Sunderland.

“He missed the fitness to be ready for today but we are expecting that he will be back after the international break,” Alonso said of Acheampong before the defeat to Sunderland. “It’s a pity because he was in a good moment.

“There are situations. Yesterday Josh felt something and he was not fit to play. So we are shorter in options and we need to go through this time.

“Even when we win, or when we lose, we miss some players that we have injured and for sure we are waiting to have them back because they are important.

“Now we need to use the time to recover them, but with the players we have, we have to get ready for the next one.”

Potential return date: Saturday November 21, vs Leeds (H)

No1 Martinez has been out for four matches after he broke his jaw.

He was clattered by Jarrad Branthwaite against Everton, and was forced to have surgery.

The Argentine was scheduled to be sidelined for six weeks, but he has already returned to training, albeit not being risked against Sunderland.

"He has made a great effort to be available but probably it was too rash, too risky [to be selected],” Alonso said.

“So he will use one more week, 10 days, to keep on training and get used to the mask. I’m sure after the break he will be available.”
