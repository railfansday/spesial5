GdS: Milan enjoying the ‘best ever’ version of Rabiot – renewal imminent as figures emerge

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Adrien Rabiot continues to dazzle with his performances and goal contributions, which AC Milan intend to reward him for.

La Gazzetta dello Sport confirm that the discussions between Rabiot and Milan have been underway for some time regarding an ‘update’ to the terms of his contract. Gerry Cardinale has very clear ideas about his priorities, and the Frenchman is next on the list.

In recent months, several players have ascended to the fourth floor of Casa Milan to sign extensions. There have been the young talents (Camarda and Comotto, 2031), veterans (Modric, 2027) and core pieces within the squad (Pavlovic, Pulisic, 2031).

Rabiot clearly belongs to the latter category (but also to a certain extent the same one as Modric) and has become the most urgent task to complete in terms of contracts.

The parties are in fact talking based on a shared desire to move forward together. For Cardinale, Rabiot represents one of the pillars on which the team’s rebuilding will continue.

Rabiot is seen as a player with the wisdom of experience, a perfect example for the younger players, and someone with the qualities of a leader even without the captain’s armband. Milan want to get back to the top, by Cardinale’s admission, and they need players like Adrien to help the cause.

So, a contract extension until 2030 has been ready for him for some time now (his current one expires in 2028). After the summer Napoli rumours, Rabiot remained at Milan partly because of his existing deal, but also partly because Cardinale convinced him.

What happens now? The next crucial step will be with his mother. Indeed, it is Mrs. Véronique who manages the former PSG midfielder’s interests, and a meeting will take place shortly. A pay rise from €3.5m net per season to €5m per year – what Pulisic earns – seems reasonable to presume.

With Rabiot on nine goal contributions for the season (five goals, four assists) he needs just one more to match his total from the entirety of 2025-26. So, he seems to be ageing like a fine French wine. All that’s needed is a final push on the contract front to continue an undoubtedly happy marriage.
