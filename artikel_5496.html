Zillow Launches Four Tools to Narrow Down Homes, Agents and Affordability

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

To help renters and homebuyers answer questions, Zillow has announced the release of four new tools to help users rent or buy with more confidence.

According to a release, Zillow’s Home Loans’ BuyAbility℠ tool allows users to answer the question: How much can I afford? The Home Loans’ BuyAbility tool can help keep a buyer’s home budget more current and accurate.

Buyers can link to Experian—the credit reporting company—once to verify their credit and debt, and their target home price will update automatically each month based on any changes. BuyAbility is also refreshed daily with updated interest rates from Zillow Home Loans, Zillow notes.

Because the Experian integration is built on a verified credit score, the release notes that Zillow Home Loans can estimate what buyers can afford more accurately, so they can search with more confidence from the start.

Buyers can also plan for and track their progress toward homeownership as they pay down debt and improve their credit. The BuyAbility tool is free, has no impact on credit scores and doesn’t obligate buyers to use Zillow Home Loans.

“Moving is a series of important decisions, and each one gets easier when you have clear information in front of you,” said Cynthia Taylor, Zillow’s senior vice president of product. “These tools give both buyers and renters clarity at every step, and connect homeowners with local experts if they’re ready to sell.”

Zillow’s Regional Insights page addresses the questions of: Where should I live? The release says that it can also describe what it’s like to live in cities and neighborhoods across the U.S., so renters and buyers can weigh neighborhood tradeoffs and compare prices early in their search.

The “compare homes” module further narrows down lists of homes for buyers. Now, buyers and renters can view their top homes side by side, with photos, financial information and key details in one place. They can start a comparison from search results, listing or their “favorites,” and stop toggling between multiple tabs.

For the final new tool available, the release states that for off-market homes, Zillow now displays a curated list of Zillow Preferred℠ agent partners who recently sold similar homes nearby, allowing homeowners to compare and contact local listing agents who may be a good match.

All four tools are live on Zillow, with availability that varies according to the tool and the user’s platform, such as the web, iOS or Android.

For more information, visit https://www.zillow.com/product-releases/fall-2026/.
