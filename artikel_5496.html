Cigna beat profit expectations and raised its annual earnings forecast

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Adjusted earnings were $7.98 a share, up from $7.83 in the third quarter of 2025, and Cigna lifted its 2026 outlook by 15 cents

The Cigna $CI Group raised its 2026 profit forecast on Thursday after reporting third-quarter adjusted income from operations of $7.98 per share, compared with $7.83 in the third quarter of 2025.

The company expects full-year adjusted income from operations of at least $30.60 per share, up 15 cents from its prior projection. The third-quarter adjusted figure exceeded the average analyst estimate, according to Bloomberg. Revenue and profit at both Evernorth Health Services and Cigna Healthcare also exceeded analyst estimates.

Total revenues rose 6% to $73.7 billion. Shareholders' net income was $1.4 billion, or $5.47 per share, compared with $1.9 billion, or $6.98 per share, in the third quarter of 2025. The company said the decline mainly reflected the absence of an after-tax gain on a sale recognized in the 2025 quarter.

Adjusted pre-tax income at Evernorth Health Services fell 4%, while adjusted revenues rose 6%. Pharmacy Benefit Services adjusted pre-tax income dropped 22%, which the company attributed to large client contract renewals and strategic investments. Specialty and Care Services adjusted pre-tax income rose 15%, driven by organic growth in specialty businesses, including higher adoption of biosimilars and specialty generics.

Cigna Healthcare adjusted pre-tax income rose 15%, which the company attributed to higher net investment income and improved operating efficiency. The segment's medical care ratio, which measures medical costs as a percentage of premiums, was 85.0%, compared with 84.8% a year ago.

For 2026, Cigna Healthcare adjusted pre-tax income is projected at no less than $4.6 billion, up $50 million from the prior projection. The company narrowed its medical care ratio outlook to 84.2%, plus or minus 25 basis points. Evernorth adjusted pre-tax income is projected at no less than $6.9 billion.

Cigna raised its full-year forecast in July to at least $30.45 per share after second-quarter adjusted earnings of $7.78 per share.

Chief Executive Officer Brian Evanko said in a statement that the company is advancing its Accelerate to One initiative to drive productivity and reduce costs. The company hosted an Investor Day, where it set targets of 10% to 14% compound annual growth in adjusted earnings per share and about $50 billion in cumulative operating cash flow through 2030. It also announced a multiyear, $3 billion modernization and productivity initiative.

Management will host a conference call on the results at 8:30 a.m. ET on Thursday.
