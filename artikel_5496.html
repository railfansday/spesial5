StreetIQ Appoints Jake Schneider to Board of Directors

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Former Cartegraph CEO joins as independent director as the company expands nationally

INDIANAPOLIS, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Street Intelligence, Inc. ("StreetIQ"), a provider of capital planning software for local road networks, today announced the appointment of Jake Schneider to its board of directors as an independent director. The appointment was effective October 21, 2026.

Schneider brings more than two decades of experience working with local governments and building and scaling GovTech businesses, including 18 years in leadership roles at Cartegraph. He served as President and Chief Executive Officer of Cartegraph from 2013 to 2019, succeeding the company's founder. Cartegraph built operations and asset management software for cities, counties and public works departments, and under Schneider's leadership grew from its Dubuque, Iowa headquarters into a national provider, completing a growth investment from Pamlico Capital in 2017. He subsequently served as President and Chief Operating Officer of Buxton, a customer analytics firm based in Fort Worth, Texas.

StreetIQ helps cities, counties and their engineering partners turn pavement condition data into capital plans, using AI-powered recommendations to prioritize road maintenance and preservation within available budgets. The company is expanding beyond its home market in Indiana.

"Jake spent nearly two decades selling asset management software to public works directors and city engineers," said Joe Becker, Chief Executive Officer of StreetIQ. "He knows how those decisions actually get made, how long they take, and what earns trust with a public agency. We get that perspective in the room now instead of learning it the slow way."

"We looked at candidates across the country for this seat," said Mitch Black, board member and Venture Director at HG Ventures. "Jake is the one who had already done the thing we need to do next. He scaled a municipal software company out of Dubuque and sold it nationally. We are doing that from Indianapolis."

Schneider joins a board that includes Becker and Black. He will advise the company on go-to-market strategy, geographic expansion and partnerships with regional engineering firms.

Street Intelligence, Inc. (StreetIQ) develops capital planning software for local governments and their engineering partners. The platform combines pavement condition assessments with AI-powered recommendations to help agencies prioritize road maintenance and preservation and align multiyear plans with available budgets. The company is headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana. Learn more at streetiq.com.

HG Ventures is the corporate venture arm of The Heritage Group, a family-owned company with more than 100 years of operating experience across infrastructure, environmental services, specialty chemicals and refining. HG Ventures invests in and partners with companies building the technologies that will shape those industries. Learn more at hgventures.com.
