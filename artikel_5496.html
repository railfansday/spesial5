Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO amid affairs scandal, stock sinks 10%

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress will serve as interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent successor

Nvidia $NVDA CEO Jensen Huang resigned on Thursday, and the chipmaker's stock fell about 12% in premarket trading.

Huang stepped down as CEO and as a member of the board, Nvidia said in a statement. Colette Kress, the chief financial officer, will step in as interim CEO while a successor is found. Harvey C. Jones, who previously led Synopsys $SNPS, becomes Nvidia's first board chair and will direct the effort to find a permanent CEO, which will weigh candidates from inside and outside the company, the company said.

Nvidia stock traded 11.9% lower before the opening bell at 9:14 a.m. ET, while a separate premarket quote of $152.02 represented a 12.5% drop. The stock had slid 7% for the week through Wednesday's close and has fallen by more than a fifth in the past month.

Futures tracking the Nasdaq $NDAQ 100 were down 1.3%, and S&P 500 futures were down 0.6%. The iShares Semiconductor ETF had slipped 3.2%. Shares of AMD $AMD and Broadcom $AVGO gained ahead of the opening bell.

The decline put Nvidia on track to have lost nearly three-quarters of a trillion dollars in market value since the scandal broke, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Nvidia gave no reason for the resignation, according to Barron's. Huang confessed to an affair on Sunday, according to CNBC. U.S. lawmakers had called for his resignation before the announcement.

Huang has said he is opposed to succession planning, and no obvious successor is ready to step in, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993. The company's revenue for fiscal 2026 reached $215.9 billion, a 702% increase over the $26.9 billion it booked in fiscal 2022.

Nvidia said its fiscal third-quarter earnings report remains set for Tuesday, Nov. 17.
