Get Ready for the Great Asterix Film Festival and Three Restored ‘Asterix’ Animated Movies!

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

StudioCanal and Mediatoon Distribution have struck a distribution agreement for the popular European comic book franchise.

StudioCanal, the studio unit of Canal+ Group, and Mediatoon Distribution, the content distribution arm of Média-Participations, have struck a European distribution agreement covering three restored animated Asterix film classics.

The agreement covers three landmark titles from the franchise’s catalogue, namely Asterix the Gaul, Asterix and Cleopatra, and Asterix Conquers Rome, across several European territories, including France, Germany, Poland and the Netherlands. Financial terms weren’t disclosed.

“The festival will bring these three films back to the big screen in their restored versions, offering audiences a unique opportunity to rediscover some of the most iconic animated adventures of the famous Gaul,” the company said on Thursday. “Alongside the theatrical event, a collector’s DVD box set featuring the three films will be released in France and Germany during the 2026 holiday season and in the spring of 2027, respectively. In Poland and the Netherlands, the films will also become available on video-on-demand platforms, reaching new audiences across both territories.”

The initiative forms part of a broader effort to celebrate and promote the Asterix universe across Europe. “It will accompany several important milestones in the history of the franchise over the coming years, including the 50th anniversary of Asterix Conquers Rome and the 60th anniversary of Asterix the Gaul in 2028,” StudioCanal said.

With more than 400 million comic books sold worldwide and generations of readers and viewers captivated over the past six decades, Asterix, created by René Goscinny and Albert Uderzo, is one of Europe’s most successful and enduring cultural franchises. The collaboration also reflects StudioCanal’s long-standing commitment to the Asterix universe, owned by Les Editions Albert René, which holds all exploitation rights.

“Asterix is one of the most iconic works of European popular culture and continues to bring generations together,” said Pauline Saint-Hilaire, head of catalogue at StudioCanal. “Through this agreement with Mediatoon Distribution, we are delighted to help new audiences discover, or rediscover, these films in their restored versions and contribute to the lasting appeal of this unique universe.”

Added Frédéric Gentet, head of sales at Mediatoon Distribution: “For generations, Asterix has brought together audiences of all ages. We are thrilled to join forces with StudioCanal’s to give these animated classics new visibility across Europe. From the Asterix Kino Festival to new digital launches and collector editions, we are celebrating the extraordinary heritage and timeless appeal of one of Europe’s most beloved brands.”
