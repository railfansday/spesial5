French Court Rejects Depardieu Appeal in Rape Case

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The French actor will stand trial for the alleged rape of actress Charlotte Arnould in 2018.

French star Gérard Depardieu (Cyrano de Bergerac, Green Card) will stand trial for the alleged rape and sexual assault of actress Charlotte Arnould.

Arnould alleges that Depardieu, a friend of her father’s, raped her on two separate occasions in the summer of 2018 when she had visited his Parisian home to ask for advice. Arnould was 22 years old at the time, while Depardieu was 69.

On Thursday, a Parisian court dismissed Depardieu appeal to have the case thrown out.

Earlier this month, Depardieu dropped his appeal against a conviction in a separate case, accepting his guilt in the sexual assault of two women on the set of Jean Becker’s The Green Shutters in 2021. The court found the French actor had sexually groped two women, a production designer and an assistant director, on the set of the film and handed him an 18-month suspended sentence. The ruling also requires Depardieu to register on France’s national sex offender registry. The Paris prosecutor’s office on Saturday (November 21) said had opened a new judicial investigation into Depardieu in connection to a separate rape and sexual assault complaint filed against him. An actress has accused Depardieu of rape for an alleged incident that occurred on the set of Guillaume Nicloux’s film Thalasso in 2018. The actress alleges that during the shooting of an intimate scene, which was not in the original script, she was raped by Depardieu and sexually assaulted by his co-star, the French author Michel Houellebecq. Houellebecq, one of France’s most acclaimed novelists, is also under investigation.
