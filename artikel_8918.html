Louis Vuitton Launches Three Fragrances as Part of Expansive Keith Haring Collection

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Executive Editor, ARTnews & Art in America

As part of an expansive collection set to include ready-to-wear fashion, accessories, and a recently touted line of limited-edition Artycapucines handbags, Louis Vuitton announced three new fragrances inspired by the art and life of the late Keith Haring.

“Three fragrances, three ways to let the imagination speak,” reads a somewhat cryptic press release.

A scent named Attrape-Rêves is described as “a radiant, optimistic fragrance that carries the promise embodied by the ‘radiant baby,’ Keith Haring’s emblem.” In another scent named Imagination, “the artist’s celebrated characters come alive through the glass of the bottle and its case. The final fragrance, Ombre Nomade, is characterized as “a golden labyrinth, with no beginning or end, its lines echoing the swirls of oud wood.” (There’s nary a mention of the smells of a graffiti-strewn 1980s–era subway station, as one might expect.)

The fragrances will be up for sale at Louis Vuitton stores starting January 1, when the rest of the fashion house’s Keith Haring line is made available. The line was inspired in part by a garment case on which Haring painted his radiant baby decades before Nicolas Ghesquière, Louis Vuitton’s artistic director of women’s collections, included it in his Cruise 2027 Show staged at the Frick Collection in New York, as part of a three-year partnership between the brand and the museum.
