Use Splitwise’s free plan if you split rent and bills with roommates, since its limit of four new expenses a day leaves a household plenty of room. On a trip where you pay for most meals and rides, you’ll hit that limit fast, so buy a month of Splitwise Pro or use a free rival.

Splitwise keeps a running tally of who owes whom in a household, a couple, or a trip group, and lets each person settle up in the app. Every member can add expenses from the web, an iPhone, or an Android phone, so roommates and travel companions can each log what they paid.

Roommates who set rent, utilities, and internet to repeat monthly and add a few shared purchases a week rarely come close to four a day. The friend who covers most of a trip’s costs should buy a month of Pro for $4.99 and cancel it before it renews, or the group can use Tricount, which is free with no limit on expenses.
