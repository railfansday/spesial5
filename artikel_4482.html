Strawberries, cucumbers and lemon basil show promise for future moon and Mars menus

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

As NASA prepares for future missions to the moon and Mars, researchers are studying crops that could provide astronauts with fresh food, nutrition and variety far from Earth. Such crop variety can help with menus in deep space, something top of mind this week as the nation celebrates Thanksgiving.
