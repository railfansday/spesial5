FC Barcelona: Marc Bernal pays PSG and Real Madrid

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Marc Bernal has no doubt: FC Barcelona is the best team in Europe, ahead of Real Madrid and Paris Saint-Germain! Guest of the Spanish show El Partidazo de COPEthe young Barcelona midfielder showed immense confidence in the abilities of his team, despite the defeats conceded against some of his European competitors. Asked about the comments of Marc Cucurella, who had designated Real Madrid as the best team on the continent, the Spaniard immediately showed his disagreement. “ Cucurella said Real? I saw it, I talked to him about it. For me, it’s Barça “, he said. And when the name of Paris Saint-Germain was mentioned, Bernal did not hesitate either: “ PSG better than us? For me, no. If we play at home against PSG, do you think they will beat us? » An ambitious statement from the Catalan player, who refuses to consider the results of direct confrontations as definitive proof of the superiority of an opponent. “ We lost, but a match doesn’t determine who is better “, he insisted.

To defend his point of view, Marc Bernal relies above all on the collective potential of FC Barcelona and on its ability to compete with the biggest European teams when all the conditions are met. The Spanish midfielder believes in particular that injuries and the quality of the game offered can make all the difference in this type of confrontation. “ If we don’t have confidence, what are we going to do? Honestly, if we are good, we play our game and there are no injuries, we can beat them “, he explained about PSG. Without denying the difficulties encountered by his team, Bernal therefore refuses to place the Parisians or the Madrilenians above Barça. His statements also reflect the state of mind he wants to display in major events: not to be impressed by previous results and to remain convinced that the Blaugranas have the necessary qualities to beat their main rivals on the European scene.
