Opinion: Julian Ward will be forced to change Liverpool's transfer plans

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Julian Ward has a lot of decisions to make ahead of his first transfer window as Liverpool's new sporting director.

The Englishman took over from Richard Hughes as the Reds' new director after Hughes chose to seek pastures new and join Al-Hilal.

Hughes has spent the most money of any Liverpool sporting director ever and, the Reds still need to add more new players to their ranks.

It's unclear whether this is because of all the changes at Anfield or if it's because of another reason.

But, if Ward thought his job was going to be easy, he can think again as he may already be forced to change his winter transfer plans.

Victor Munoz's injury could massively hurt Liverpool

Liverpool's attack was one of the main areas that they focused on improving this summer.

Bradley Barcola and Victor Munoz were brought in for a combined total worth over £150 million.

And, while both players have had their moments, it still feels like Liverpool could do with adding another player or two to their ranks.

But, since there are also many other areas among their first-team squad that also need changing, it didn't really feel like any deals for attackers were going to occur in January.

However, Munoz was unfortunately forced off the pitch during the Reds' 3-0 win against Club Brugge on Wednesday evening.

So, depending on how long he's out for, the Reds may need to turn their transfer plans upside down and add another attacker to their ranks.

Liverpool will be hoping that Victor Munoz isn't out for too long

Recently, Munoz has been finding his feet at Anfield.

The attacker was clearly brought in during the summer transfer window to be a back-up.

He only cost the club around £35 million which, in this day and age, is a steal.

His initial performances weren't all that great but, over the last couple of weeks, he's got himself into a purple patch of form with his blistering pace being one of his best assets.

In addition to this, Munoz is only 23 years old so, he still has a lot of his career ahead of him which means that he's probably going to get better.

So, a long-term injury is definitely not what he needs right now.

Nobody knows exactly what has happened to the Spaniard yet but, the Reds will certainly be hoping that it's nothing too serious.
