Bills turn to two former first-round picks as injuries hit defense

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Buffalo Bills are calling in some veteran reinforcements.

They aren't your typical practice squad players.

Buffalo elevated former first-round draft picks Tre'Davious White and Shaq Thompson for Sunday's game against the Miami Dolphins after losing two defensive starters to injuries.

White and Thompson could see their first action this season for the Bills.

Cornerback Christian Benford (groin) and linebacker Dorian Williams (calf) were ruled out Friday after missing all three practices.

White, 31, was selected by Buffalo with the 27th overall pick in the 2017 NFL Draft. Thompson, 32, was selected by the Carolina Panthers with the 25th pick in 2015.

Both have experience in Buffalo's defense.

White spent his first seven NFL seasons with the Bills, becoming a two-time Pro Bowl selection and earning first-team All-Pro honors in 2019 after tying for the NFL lead with six interceptions.

Injuries eventually derailed his career in Buffalo, including a torn ACL in 2021 and a torn Achilles in 2023.

The Bills released him in March 2024, but he returned last season and started 16 regular-season games, recording 40 tackles, 10 passes defensed and an interception.

White became a free agent after the season and remained unsigned until Buffalo brought him back for a third stint Oct. 19.

Thompson has a shorter history with the Bills but also made an impact last season.

The former Panthers linebacker appeared in 12 games with six starts for Buffalo in 2025, recording 56 tackles, six tackles for loss, a sack and a forced fumble.

He also stepped up in the playoffs, intercepting Jacksonville Jaguars quarterback Trevor Lawrence during Buffalo's wild-card victory and leading the Bills with eight tackles.

Thompson returned to Buffalo's practice squad Sept. 1 after spending the offseason as a free agent.

Rookie Kaleb Elarms-Orr is expected to make his first NFL start in place of Williams. The Bills could start rookie Davison Igbinosun and second-year Maxwell Hairston at outside cornerback with Benford sidelined.

The Bills (8-1) have won five straight games and allowed just one touchdown over their last three.
