Cam Skattebo makes backflipping change in wake of Aidan Hutchinson’s devastating injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

After seeing Lions star Aidan Hutchinson tear his ACL during his sack celebration over the weekend, Skattebo barely let the question get off Wednesday in the Giants locker room before he said he is retiring his signature touchdown celebration.

New York Giants running back Cam Skattebo speaks to the media after practice in East Rutherford, N.J., Friday, Oct. 23, 2026. Charles Wenzelberg / New York Post

Cam Skattebo #44 of the New York Giants celebrates after he scores a touchdown. Charles Wenzelberg/NY Post

"It's hard to see," Skattebo told a couple of reporters after Wednesday's practice. "I'm saying yeah (no more). Mentally, I'm like, 'I'm not doing that.' That ain't going to happen to me."

Skattebo left open the possibility of bringing it back for the big moment "if we go to the playoffs or something." And, of course, in the heat of the moment instincts sometimes take over.

"He didn't think about (the risk) either," Skattebo said, "and it happened."

Head coach John Harbaugh and general manager Joe Schoen didn't pull Skattebo aside to ask him to stop.

Aidan Hutchinson of the Detroit Lions falls with an injury while celebrating a sack during an NFL game against the New England Patriots in Munich, Germany, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

But that conversation has been a back-and-forth between the Giants and Skattebo since he backflipped on his surgically repaired ankle over the summer at Fanatics Fest.

Skattebo typically holds a formal media briefing on Fridays of game week and could be asked to expand then.
