Indiana fans want to see Grant Wilson start the rest of the 2026 season

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Indiana football made a move at starting quarterback last week. Grant Wilson, who was depth in the Hoosiers' 2025 quarterbacks room before elevating to the No. 2 spot this season, earned his first start with the program after helping lead Indiana past Minnesota in relief two weeks prior.

The offense ended up showing some life for the first time in weeks, scoring 27 points as Indiana took down USC by multiple scores. Wilson himself finished with 305 passing yards, completing 17 of his 27 attempts through the air for two touchdowns and one interception.

After that performance, we asked Indiana fans if the change should be made permanent and Wilson should fully take over moving forward. Josh Hoover, who started the Hoosiers' first nine games, hasn't been the same since suffering an arm injury against Ohio State.
