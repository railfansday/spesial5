49ers waive Brandin Cooks, activate C.J. West

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The San Francisco 49ers made a pair of roster moves for their upcoming game against the Minnesota Vikings. They announced that they have activated defensive lineman C.J. West. To make room, they have waived wide receiver Brandin Cooks. Additionally, Cornerback Nate Hobbs has been downgraded to "out."

The Cooks waiver makes sense given that Mike Evans is expected to play and De'Zhaun Stribling could return next month. I know some may want the 49ers to put Jacob Cowing on the chopping block, but it looks like Cooks is the odd man out. The 49ers probably didn't want to make the decision after the Vikings game, as time was running out for West's IR designation.

West's return will also help the defensive line, which had sack issues last week against the Dallas Cowboys. Whether you think that was blown out of proportion is up to you. West started in two games before his knee injury and was largely quiet. Now that his knee is healed, hopefully he can help the 49ers generate some significant pressure.

With Hobbs being out, the 49ers have until Wednesday to make a decision. If they don't activate Hobbs by then, his season is over.

As for Cooks, he will need to clear waivers and then become a free agent. The 49ers could have a handshake agreement that, once he clears waivers, they'll pick him back up for the practice squad, where he started his brief time with the team. Given all the time spent to get him up to speed on the offense, holding onto him in some way may be a smart move.
