Draymond Green Gives Blunt ‘Spoiled Milk’ Take On Kristaps Porzingis’ Career-Ending Decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Golden State Warriors' forward Kristaps Porzingis has decided to step away from basketball over his health concerns. Draymond Green, Porzingis's teammate on the Warriors, spoke to senior NBA writer Sam Amick and shared his thoughts on the Latvian big man's decision to end his 10-year career abruptly.

"Since I've been here on this team, it's never been about bickering, never about 'Shame on this guy,' 'Shame on that guy,'" Draymond Green told Amick.

"We've never had that culture here. So the same goes for Porziņģis. No one's sitting around bashing this guy or even worried about him, for that matter. In our minds, we've moved forward with the players that we have here," he further explained.

"Everyone in here thinks he can help us? Of course. We know what type of player he is. But I don't think anyone in here is sitting around waiting on him to walk through that door. Everybody's moved on in their mindset."

"When we first heard about the morning before the Lakers game, it kind of jolted us a little bit," Green said.

Mike Dunleavy reportedly addressed the locker room about this one time, and then they collectively decided to move ahead instead of dwelling in the past.

"They said, 'Hey, guys, we're moving forward,'" Draymond Green added. "Whatever has to be done with that, we'll handle it. But y'all go play.'"

"As I said, everybody's moved on. It's a next-man-up mentality, right? That's an opportunity for Charles Bassey. That's an opportunity for Graham Ike to get more minutes and continue to try to elevate his career."

"So you embrace those guys, and you pour into those guys, to try to help them with all that they can be helped with so that they could ultimately help this team. But you can't sit and worry about spoiled milk," Green concluded.

While Draymond Green may simply be trying to highlight the silver lining for the Warriors' young players after his teammate effectively ended his NBA career, his choice of words drew heavy backlash from fans on social media.

Even though Porzingis and Green played only 15 games together, they should still show sensitivity when addressing a teammate's career-ending decision.

Porzingis has made only 15 appearances for the Warriors since being traded from the Hawks in February 2026. In those 15 games, he averaged 16.1 points, 5.3 rebounds, and 2.3 assists while shooting 43.3% from the field and 31.1% from beyond the arc.

While Green's comments reflect the Warriors' desire to move forward, his choice of words has understandably sparked controversy. Porzingis' health concerns deserve empathy, especially given the uncertainty surrounding the abrupt end to his NBA career.

After signing a two-year, $40 million contract extension in June, Porzingis is now on the trade block for teams looking to buy him out and clear cap space. His $3 million guaranteed clause makes him a unique trade chip for the Warriors.

It will be interesting to see whether the Warriors can find a valuable addition to their roster in what will essentially be a salary-dump trade for any team looking to negotiate with them.
