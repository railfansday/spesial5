Ben Roethlisberger, Eli Manning, Antonio Brown among players advancing in Pro Football Hall of Fame voting process for 2027 class

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

A pair of quarterbacks from the fabled 2004 draft class, Pittsburgh Steelers legend Ben Roethlisberger and New York Giants icon Eli Manning, plus embattled wide receiver Antonio Brown, are among the 52 players the Pro Football Hall of Fame revealed are advancing in the voting process for the 2027 class.

Per current Hall of Fame bylaws, at this stage of that process, selectors cast ballots for 50 players they believe should move forward in their candidacy, but those tied for the 50th position also remain eligible for induction.

The list the Hall announced on Tuesday will next be cut down to 25 players. That more exclusive group will be unveiled in about two weeks.

For now, here's a position-by-position breakdown of the players being considered for enshrinement:

Ben Roethlisberger, Eli Manning, Ken Anderson

Shaun Alexander, Frank Gore, Marshawn Lynch, LeSean McCoy, Adrian Peterson, Fred Taylor, Ricky Watters

Anquan Boldin, Antonio Brown, Rob Gronkowski, Torry Holt, Billy "White Shoes" Johnson, Steve Smith Sr., Otis Taylor, Hines Ward, Reggie Wayne, Jason Witten

Rodney Harrison, Lester Hayes, Albert Lewis, Eddie Meador, Richard Sherman, Earl Thomas III, Everson Walls, Darren Woodson

John Abraham, Geno Atkins, L.C. Greenwood, Jim Marshall, Robert Mathis, Haloti Ngata, Vince Wilfork, Kevin Williams

Maxie Baughan, London Fletcher, James Harrison, Karl Mecklenburg, Tommy Nobis, Terrell Suggs

Roethlisberger and Manning draw the eye, considering they each quarterbacked historic franchises to a pair of Super Bowl victories; however, this cohort is understandably littered with household names.

Running back Adrian Peterson is forever part of Minnesota Vikings lore. He's one of the nine NFL players to ever rush for 2,000 yards in a season. In fact, Peterson's 2,097 yards on the ground in 2012 were the second most in a single-season in league history, behind only the 2,105 rushing yards Eric Dickerson logged while starring for the Los Angeles Rams in 1984. Peterson earned NFL MVP honors that season.

Another RB in the running, Frank Gore, is the NFL's third all-time leading rusher. Gore spent the bulk of his career with the San Francisco 49ers. Even in his 16th and final campaign, he was still somewhat productive, amassing 653 rushing yards in his age-37 season with the New York Jets in 2020.

Rob Gronkowski and Jason Witten are both integral to the annals of tight end play. Gronkowski was a red-zone monster, finishing his 11-season career with a whopping 92 receiving touchdowns. Together, he and seven-time Super Bowl-winning quarterback Tom Brady authored one of the most electrifying duos in the sport, first with the New England Patriots and then with the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. As for Witten, he proved to be one of the NFL's most reliable security blankets to date, maxing out with 110 catches during his 2012 campaign with the Dallas Cowboys.

Interestingly, beleaguered wideout Antonio Brown was among the players included in 52-man list the Hall put forth on Tuesday. Brown was a superstar during the peak of his head-scratching 12-season run in the league. He built most of his résumé in Pittsburgh, where he stacked seven 1,000-yard receiving seasons. In his nine-season stay with the Steelers, he led the NFL in receiving yards twice: first in 2014 and again in 2017. He also led the league in touchdown catches in 2018.

But the end of Brown's career was marred by the trouble he got himself into, on and off the field. Earlier this month, in a Miami court, Brown pleaded guilty to aggravated assault following a 2025 shooting incident.

Brown still has long odds of being inducted next year. Ultimately, 13 players will make up most of the 15 finalists who will be presented at the selection committee's annual meeting. The other two candidates will be Bill Belichick (who emerged from the "coach" category) and Patriots owner Robert Kraft (who came out of the "contributor" category).

The Hall's 2027 class will be announced at "NFL Honors" in February.
