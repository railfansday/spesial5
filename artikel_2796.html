Texas RB Hollywood Smothers out for rest of season because of knee injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Texas RB Hollywood Smothers' 2026 season is over.

Smothers will miss the final month of the regular season and any postseason games because of a torn MCL, according to multiple reports. Smothers has dealt with multiple injuries in 2026 and has already missed two games.

He played in No. 7 Texas' win over Mississippi State on Halloween, but left the game early. He had four carries for minus-6 yards.

Overall, Smothers had 69 carries for 283 yards and two rushing touchdowns to go along with 10 catches for 79 yards and a receiving TD. Smothers scored the game-winning touchdown on the ground in the Longhorns' Week 2 comeback win over Ohio State.

Smothers transferred to Texas after a season at Oklahoma and two seasons at NC State. He rushed for 939 yards and six scores in 2025 for the Wolfpack and had originally committed to go to Alabama before choosing the Longhorns over the Crimson Tide.

With Smothers out for the season, Texas is going to rely more on Raleek Brown at running back. The Arizona State transfer is the team's leading rusher with 92 carries for 452 yards and five scores. Arch Manning has 70 carries — remember, college stats count sacks as rushing attempts — and no other player has more than 29 rushing attempts.

While Texas knows it will be without both Summers and tackle Trevor Goosby (who suffered a season-ending knee injury against Ole Miss) on Saturday against No. 18 Missouri, the Longhorns also have three key contributors whose status is up in the air. Edge rusher Colin Simmons and wide receivers Ryan Wingo and Cam Coleman are all listed as questionable. Simmons hasn't played since he suffered a high-ankle sprain against Florida, while both Wingo and Coleman are dealing with shoulder injuries.
