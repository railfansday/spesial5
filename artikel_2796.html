Lions' Aidan Hutchinson says he'll make big change after season-ending injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Aidan Hutchinson is keeping his feet on the ground going forward.

The Detroit Lions star edge rusher saw his season come to an end during the team's 35-10 victory over the New England Patriots in Germany on Nov. 15 – and it had nothing to do with a hit he took. Hutchinson sacked Drake Maye in the first quarter, jumped in the air to celebrate and suffered a torn ACL when he came back down.

It was unbelievable in the moment and the shocking nature of the injury remains that way for Hutchinson in the aftermath, which he spoke about in an Instagram post on Nov. 19:

"I honestly can't believe it," Hutchinson said. "I've jumped hundreds of times on a football field and never in a million years did I think this would happen to me. But even something like this won't hold me back.

"I can ask why over and over again, but it'll never make sense. All I can do is surrender to the path. Only God knows why and I trust in Him.

"Very grateful for all of your prayers and well wishes."

Hutchinson then explained that he knows two things for sure, writing that he "always" comes back stronger and "that's the last time I go airborne," before leaving a parting message for his teammates.

Detroit will now play the remainder of the season without its best defender, continuing a theme of significant losses to injury on that side of the ball this season.

On Nov. 1, the Lions lost linebacker Jack Campbell to a serious neck injury that left him motionless on the field. While Campbell announced that he has movement in his lower extremities and was making progress toward that in his upper body, the linebacker remains sidelined.

The Super Bowl hopeful Lions have seen the defense get picked apart by injuries and absences from some of their best players, leaving many to wonder about what could've been if everyone remained healthy.

Head coach Dan Campbell didn't want to dwell on the "what ifs" when he met with reporters on Nov. 17.

"Well, I think there's times where if you're not careful, you lay in bed at night and you start thinking about all the what-ifs, and at some point you just become a miserable piece of (expletive)," Dan Campbell said. "And I'm not gonna do it, man. We're not doing it. I refuse to look at it that way.

"Does it stink? Yeah, it sure did in the moment. It's time to move on. We have to."
