Dodgers’ All-Time Saves Leader Kenley Jansen Cast Off By New Team

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Los Angeles Dodgers have plenty of work ahead of them this offseason as they attempt to strengthen their roster and reclaim the World Series championship. After falling short of defending their title, the organization could look to make several significant additions before the 2027 campaign, with its bullpen once again a key area worth monitoring.

Eric Lauer, Kris Bubic, Brusdal Grateral, Evan Phillips, Blake Treinen and Alex Vesia are all free agents, giving the Dodgers some flexibility in reshaping a relief corps that appears less stable than the other roster units. And with the free agent and trade markets beginning to take shape ahead of the expected December lockout, the team has received an update involving one of its most accomplished former closers.

Kenley Jansen, the Dodgers' all-time saves leader who is approaching one of baseball's most exclusive career milestones, is suddenly available again after his latest team made a significant contract decision.

On Monday, the Detroit Tigers announced that they would not retain Jansen under his contract option for next season. The decision brings his one-year arrangement with Detroit to an end and gives the veteran closer another opportunity to find a new team.

"Kenley Jansen's quest to join the 500-saves club will have to continue with another team," Jason Beck wrote for MLB.com. "The Tigers declined their $12 million option on the legendary closer, paying a $2 million buyout and making the 39-year-old right-hander a free agent."

Jansen signed with Detroit last offseason after spending the 2025 campaign with the Los Angeles Angels. The move could put the Dodgers' former closer on the radar of organizations looking for experienced bullpen help, although there has been no indication that Los Angeles is pursuing a reunion.

Jansen established himself as one of the most accomplished relievers in Dodgers history after signing with the organization as an amateur free agent in 2004. He eventually converted from catcher to pitcher, becoming a fixture in Los Angeles' bullpen before departing after the 2021 season.

He recorded 350 saves with the Dodgers, comfortably establishing the franchise record ahead of second-place Eric Gagne's 161. Jansen was a key member of the 2020 World Series championship team that kicked off the ongoing golden age for the franchise.

"The future Hall of Famer enters his age-39 season with 498 career saves, third on the all-time list," Anthony Franco noted for MLB Trade Rumors. "While he's almost certainly not going to join Mariano Rivera and Trevor Hoffman in the 600 club, Jansen should celebrate his 500th save within the first month of next season."

Jansen finished the 2026 regular season with 22 saves and a 3.20 ERA across 45 innings.

Whether the Dodgers consider bringing back their former closer remains to be seen. But with Jansen now available and just two saves away from 500, his next destination will be worth watching as the offseason develops.
