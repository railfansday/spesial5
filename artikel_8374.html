Escaping the Amish Mennonite Church on the Appalachian Trail with Moxie Farr (BPR #382)

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

In today’s episode of Backpacker Radio presented by The Trek, brought to you by Topo Athletic, we are joined by Moxie Farr, an Appalachian Trail thru-hiker who grew up in a strict Amish-Mennonite community, funded her hike with a chicken business, and is now living off-grid while pursuing the education she was denied growing up.

In this one, Moxie walks us through how the AT became a lifeline when she felt trapped in her church, she explains how she researched backpacking gear by bribing hikers with homemade sandwiches, and shares the emotional moments of subtle church rebellion on trail.

We also cover how Hollywood gets Rumspringa completely wrong, what it felt like to be treated as an equal by men for the first time on trail, and why she wants a redemption AT thru-hike where she can finally hike without looking over her shoulder. This story is truly one-of-a-kind- Moxie’s life will be turned into a movie before long.

We wrap the show with a couple of PCT trail towns that are up for sale, the outdated trends we refuse to abandon, the Triple Crown of “they’re a 10, but…,” Sass gives us a one-minute gear review of Mountain Hardwear’s ALUULA ultralight pack, and we hear from a listener about the worst YouTube channel on the Internet.
