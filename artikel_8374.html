Personal Investments • Re: TIAA pre-tax and Roth contributions

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

I wonder if it might be illegal to prevent reallocation of customer funds in an account that has more than one "menu" item? I mean, after all, there is a positive obligation to provide customers with a menu that has adequate diversification. If you can't move your own money, how can you maintain diversification?

I worry about the many paternalistic employers who don't permit in-service rollovers. This is an employer choice, made when they initially contract with TIAA.

Someone lucky enough to have R4 CREF Account expense ratios in a 403 or 401, or perhaps two or three Vanguard funds with 0.04% ERs, would not want to roll over to a TIAA IRA with R3 CREF Accounts and zero Vanguard options!
