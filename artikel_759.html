P1Harmony Earns First No. 1 on Top Album Sales Chart With ‘BLOW UP’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Plus, Kings of Leon, Steven Wilson, A Charlie Brown Christmas and Ariana Grande make waves in the top 10.

P1Harmony earns its first No. 1 on Billboard’s Top Album Sales chart as BLOW UP bows atop the chart dated Nov. 21. It’s the seventh top 10-charted set for the pop group, which had previously topped out at No. 2, twice, with EX in 2025 and UNIQUE earlier in 2026. BLOW UP begins with 43,000 copies sold in the United States in the week ending Nov. 12, according to Luminate. The set’s first-week sales were powered by its availability across 25 CD variants and five vinyl variants (all containing collectible items such as photocards and posters, with some items randomized). Elsewhere in the top 10 of the latest Top Album Sales chart, Kings of Leon and Steven Wilson debut, while Vince Guaraldi Trio’s classic soundtrack to A Charlie Brown Christmas returns. Plus, Ariana Grande’s Christmas & Chill reaches the top 10 for the first time after a new vinyl variant was released.

Kings of Leon clock its seventh top 10 on Top Album Sales with the No. 6 debut of O My Beloved, selling 11,000 copies. Steven Wilson collects his second top 10 as Requiem for a Village arrives at No. 9 with 8,000 sold.

A pair of seasonal albums are in the top 10, as the A Charlie Brown Christmas soundtrack climbs 13-8 with 9,000 sold (up 48%) and Grande’s Christmas & Chill re-enters at No. 10 with 7,000 sold (up 1,451%) after it a new Target-exclusive vinyl variant was released.

Billboard’s Top Album Sales chart ranks the top-selling albums of the week based only on pure album sales (physical and digital download purchases combined). The chart’s history dates to May 25, 1991, the first week Billboard began tabulating charts with electronically monitored piece count information from SoundScan, now Luminate. Pure album sales were the sole measurement utilized by the Billboard 200 albums chart through the list dated Dec. 6, 2014, after which that chart switched to a methodology that blends album sales with track equivalent album (TEA) units and streaming equivalent album (SEA) units.
