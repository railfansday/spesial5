Vans Warped Tour Announces 2027 Stops in Washington, D.C.,Long Beach, Mexico City and Orlando

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The rebooted punk fest will kick off in the nation's capital on June 19.

The rebooted Vans Warped Tour will touch down in four cities next year in keeping with the 2.0 edition’s smaller footprint. The beloved festival that spotlights every variety of punk will kick things off in 2027 in Washington, D.C. on June 19, followed by a second show on the 20th. The traveling carnival will then move on to Long Beach, Calif. for a double-down in July on the 24th and 25th, followed by Mexico City on Sept. 11 and 12 and Orlando, Fla. on Nov. 13 and 14. All four cities hosted the Warped tour earlier this year; Montreal will not be on the list this year, however. While some fans were peeved that Montreal did not make the cut next year, tour founder Kevin Lyman told Billboard Canada that the gang might be back at a later date. “We have decided to focus on a potential return in 2028,” he said in October.

Tickets for all the U.S. cities will go on sale on Friday (Nov. 20) at 12 p.m. ET here.

The touring rock fest that kicked off in 1995 barnstormed North America for more than a decade until closing up shop in 2018 and then mounting a three-stop anniversary tour in 2019. It then returned in 2025 to celebrate its 30th anniversary with shows in D.C., Long Beach and Orlando. This year’s event featured sets from 3OH!3, All Time Low, Bowling For Soup, Escape the Fate, Flogging Molly, Hawthorne Heights, Jimmy Eat World, Madball, MXPX, Pennywise, Sublime, Taking Back Sunday, Jutes, Simple Plan, Agnostic Front, Drug Church, Chiodos and more.

At press time no lineups have been announced for the 2027 Vans Warped Tour dates.
