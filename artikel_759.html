Why TLC's Whitney Way Thore Began Taking Weight Loss Medication

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

As Whitney Way Thore revealed in the upcoming season of My Big Fat Fabulous Life, she began taking weight loss medications. The TLC star explained to E! News why she decided to give them a shot.

Years into her journey to welcome a baby, Whitney Way Thore is ready to give just about anything a shot.

And so when her fertility doctor suggested the My Big Fat Fabulous Life star consider using a weight loss medication, which can have an impact on ovulation and reproductive hormones, she listened.

"She said, 'Just take it, and then if you get pregnant, stop,'" Way Thore shared with E! News in an exclusive interview. "And so that's why I started it."

Ignoring any particularly loud outsider opinions, "I didn't really have any side effects or anything like that," detailed the 42-year-old, back with the 10th season of her TLC series (returning Nov. 10 at 10 p.m.). "I think physically it was easy."

But committing to a prescription was just a small piece of Way Thore's fertility journey. The bigger piece involved an admittedly terrifying conversation with now-fiancé Jake Beaudoin.

Though the pair are engaged now, they were dating more casually when Way Thore told him about her dreams of getting pregnant.

"I was obviously very nervous to tell him that that was my plan," said Way Thore who had found a sperm donor in the hopes of getting pregnant through intrauterine insemination. Though, she added, "I hadn't really expected that it would matter to him because I didn't expect that we would be in a serious relationship."

And she assumed any hope of that would be dashed when she filled him in on the details.

"I thought, 'Well, I'm going to tell him this and obviously, and understandably, you're not going to stay with me through this,'" Way Thore shared of her line of thought. Instead, she marveled, "He was so supportive and so excited, and he never wavered on that once and still hasn't. So, that was really amazing."

Which is an apt descriptor for their entire relationship.

Having been through the romantic wringer—"Obviously if you're still single at this age, you've been through some s--t," Way Thore reasoned—she had her eye out for any red flags the DJ may be throwing in the air.

"I've almost never dated a man that I leave the relationship having any shred of respect for," stressed Way Thore. "When you're this old and you've dated as much as I have, and been as disappointed in men as I have," she detailed, well, it can be hard to see the good in people.

"You want to protect yourself and you want to not be as stupid as you might have been," she explained. "Or, you might want to approach things in a smart way."

However, she acknowledged, "I think there's a balance between doing that and being hyper vigilant because I think that kind of hyper vigilance can definitely stop a relationship before it has a chance to really get off the ground."

Admittedly, the North Carolinian waved a scarlet-tinged banner when he ghosted Way Thore after they initially connected in 2018.

"Basically we met, we only hung out a couple times," she explained. Still, "I bring it up constantly. He, for the longest time, would try to defend it, and then it became this thing, and I just got so annoyed."

But when it comes to Beaudoin that's certainly not her dominant emotion.

"When you go from thinking you're just in a casual relationship to all of a sudden we're going to try to have a baby together, things move very quickly," Way Thore explained. "And that can expose a lot of weaknesses that you might feel. But I continue to be pleasantly surprised at every turn, even though I was really, really, really looking for reasons to believe that it wasn't right."

Basically, acknowledged Thore, she's grateful "he proved me wrong."

And, yes, she's pretty psyched to be rocking an aquamarine gemstone on her left hand. "I think when you're in love, you naturally want to share that," she explained of letting viewers into her engagement journey. She joins a long list of celebrities work their way down the aisle.
