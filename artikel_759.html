‘Concrete Land’ Leads IDA Documentary Award Nominations With Six; ‘To Hold a Mountain,’ ‘You Can See Everything’ Also Score

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

This year’s International Documentary Association Documentary Awards nominations have been announced.

Asmahan Bkerat’s “Concrete Land” leads the field with six nominations, followed by Biljana Tutorov and Petar Glomazić’s “To Hold a Mountain” with five.

Mehrdad Oskouei and Soraya Akhlaghi’s “A Fox Under a Pink Moon” and Pegah Ahangarani’s “Rehearsals for a Revolution” also scored strong showings, earning four nominations apiece. Related Stories Dua Lipa, Tame Impala and SZA to Headline Coachella 2027

Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim’s box office hit “You Can See Everything” earned three nominations, including best feature documentary, best director and best original music score, but was shut out of the cinematography and editing categories.

IDA received a record 650 submissions from 95 countries, with 32 countries represented among this year’s nominees.

“I couldn’t be more excited to celebrate these meaningful and creative documentaries. It’s a challenging time for documentary makers, but also a really inspiring moment for IDA and for the field,” says Ina Fichman, IDA board president. “This year we see nominees whose deeply personal stories share narratives of resistance and exile, and we see artful, innovative filmmaking that is capturing new and robust audiences.”

The awards ceremony will take place Dec. 4 at the Ebell of Los Angeles, with Tabitha Jackson returning as host for the second consecutive year.
