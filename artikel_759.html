Forest beat Chelsea to heap pressure on Alonso as Fofana is booed

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Morgan Gibbs-White scored two in Nottingham Forest’s 3-2 win over Chelsea at the City Ground as the crisis deepened for Xabi Alonso.

Igor Jesus volleyed Forest in front inside five minutes of what was a dismal first half for Chelsea and they never recovered parity. Wesley Fofana - booed by his own supporters throughout - conceded a penalty early in the second half that Gibbs-White converted.

Emmanuel Emegha did come off the bench to pull one back soon afterwards but another goal from the Forest captain better reflected the superiority of Oliver Glasner's side even if Emegha's second in the 89th minute made for a surprisingly nervy finale.

Forest began the evening beside the Trent with only goal difference keeping them out of the relegation zone, but victory lifts them clear of the bottom six. The spotlight now falls on Chelsea, who missed the chance to climb back into the top five.

It is four Premier League defeats in their last five games under Alonso who appears to be scrambling for solutions given the evidence of this team selection. It is not only Fofana under scrutiny at Chelsea. A club in disarray? Alonso needs to get a grip on it and fast.

Liam Delap had been the two-goal hero in the 3-1 win over Manchester City last time out at home and would have been desperate to score against another of his former clubs but it was Igor Jesus' stunning strike that silenced the Chelsea supporters early on.

"It is the first opportunity for them to play together, they have different strengths," Glasner had told Sky Sports beforehand. Igor Jesus' strengths include volleys beyond Emiliano Martinez, it turns out. It was his first goal at the City Ground since April.

Alonso had responded to the alarming defeat to Leeds by dropping Levi Colwill and playing Moises Caicedo in defence. The Chelsea boss changed to a back four as well in a bid to find some form after losing three of their previous four Premier League games.

"Today we need strong characters, big personalities," said Alonso beforehand. "Players that know what it takes to compete in the Premier League." That explained the decision to bring in Danny Welbeck up front, with Jordan Henderson holding midfield together.

But Welbeck was a passenger in the first half, having only seven touches of the ball - half as many as anyone else - while Henderson found himself overrun. Forest were at the back line time and again. Chelsea were fortunate to be only one down at the break.

Fofana had not endeared himself to supporters by giving an unauthorised interview in which he not only talked of being close to quitting football but appeared to joke about missing a penalty in the Carabao Cup defeat to Liverpool. This performance was worse.

He was culpable for Forest's second goal, bested by Delap before hastily fouling him inside the penalty box in a rash attempt to recover. Gibbs-White slotted home from the spot and Fofana was substituted to the sound obscene chants from the Chelsea fans.

Alonso's side did pull one back through the young substitute Emegha, when Pedro Neto's shot was deflected kindly into his path. But Xaver Schlager's sumptuous ball clipped in behind the Chelsea defence allowed Gibbs-White to hook home his second.

Emegha nodded in another late on to make the decision to omit him after scoring the first goal against Leeds look even more of an error, but it was not enough to earn an undeserved point. Forest held on and are on the up again. Chelsea have problems.

Nottingham Forest boss Oliver Glasner speaking in the press conference:

"It looks like we are progressing and this is what we all want to see. But also last time [against Man City] was the first swallow [that does not make a summer] and now it's the second swallow and I think everybody could feel it tonight.

"So stay humble, take this feeling of the win and the confidence and the belief but stay humble and prepare well for the three-game week before we face Brighton here.

"We are all feeling again that we are heading in the right direction. Still a lot to do, but every win helps in building confidence and trust. But still 12 points after 12 games is not enough."

Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso speaking in the press conference:

"We are working, trying to find solutions, trying to do our best, but things are not going the way we want. And I don't understand any other way but to work, to try again.

"Because if we want to get something, it will have to come from us. No one will come from outside to give us a solution. And the quality that we need, the mentality that we need, it needs to come from within the team.

"Overall, we can do better. I think that we are going through a difficult moment of confidence, things are not clicking, things are not going the way we want.

"We know that in football, this can change quickly, or this can continue. So it's down to us to work and to find solutions to have a better feeling on the pitch and to get better results, because that's what we need."

Asked if he was frustrated with the Chelsea fans booing Wesley Fofana:

"We need to support everyone because we need everyone. We need the fans, we need all the players, we need to stick together, because if not, it will get more complicated."
