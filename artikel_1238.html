Di Canio: ‘Milan would be mid-table with Leao, Cisse worth three times more’

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Former Italian striker Paolo Di Canio feels Milan would ‘be mid-table’ with Rafael Leao this season: ‘Cisse is worth three times more, Moreira five.’

Milan secured a 5-1 win over Frosinone on Sunday, a result that helped the Rossoneri climb to fourth in the Serie A table with four wins from their last five league matches.

Di Canio examined Milan’s victory during Sky Sport Italia’s Sunday Night Show, highlighting a big difference from the past.

The former Milan, Lazio, Juventus and West Ham striker has always been very critical of Rafael Leao, especially due to the Portuguese’s attitude and, last night, he reiterated (via TMW): “You massacred me for three years. This is a serious team; Cisse is already worth three times more than Leao.

“Moreira is worth five times more because of his seriousness, effectiveness, quality and empathy with his teammates. Milan are a serious team. With Leao, they’d be mid-table now, with doubts.”

Milan sold Leao to Galatasaray last summer for €38m plus a 20% sell-on clause.

They brought back Italy international Alphadjo Cisse from a loan spell at Serie B side Catanzaro and signed Diego Moreira from Strasbourg for €50m.

Di Canio went on to praise Adrien Rabiot, who has scored or assisted in each of his last seven Serie A matches, equalling Rossoneri legend Kaká.

Di Canio, however, also pointed out that Frosinone’s defence was hardly the toughest to test.

“He [Rabiot] is good, but he can play blindfolded here,” the ex-striker concluded.

“The last eight, ten teams in Serie A defend pathetically. You go straight in goal with a vertical pass; it’s ridiculous.”

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
