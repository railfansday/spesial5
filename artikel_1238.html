UCL | Manchester City 2-2 Napoli: Regret as Partenopei waste double advantage

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Napoli leave England with regrets after twice taking the lead through Rasmus Hojlund and Noa Lang, but Enzo Maresca’s Manchester City clawed their way back to a 2-2 draw.

The Partenopei had Rafa Marin suspended with Antonio Vergara, Benoit Badiashile, Alex Meret, Stanislav Lobotka, Giovanni Di Lorenzo, Alisson Santos and Alessandro Buongiorno injured. They lost here 2-0 last season with Di Lorenzo sent off after 21 minutes, prompting De Bruyne’s early substitution against his former club.

Matheus Nunes and Marc Guehi shook off knocks, leaving only Antoine Semenyo unavailable for coach Enzo Maresca, but top names like Ruben Dias, Jeremy Doku and Ilman Ndiaye were rested. Gianluigi Donnarumma was feeling pressure after one point from the last four Premier League rounds.

Vanja Milinkovic-Savic made a save in the first minute on Elliott Anderson, while Scott McTominay’s shot was deflected inches wide of the near post and Nico O’Reilly couldn’t keep his header down on the Matheus Nunes cross.

Allan Elias had a strike charged down after a corner, with Erling Haaland’s appeals for a tussle with Matias Olivera rejected by the referee, and Rasmus Hojlund could only drill into the arms of Donnarumma from a tight angle.

An unusual Haaland backwards header was plucked out of the air, but Donnarumma needed a sensational save to keep out Sam Beukema’s header on the Politano corner just before half-time.

Noa Lang wasted a golden opportunity at the dawn of the second half when sent down the left by Hojlund, as he went too wide and then slipped at the crucial moment.

Billy Gilmour threw himself in the path of a scorcher from Juventus transfer target Rayan Cherki, but Napoli broke the deadlock with a well-worked move.
