Three Italians ruled out for several months with serious injuries after Serie A Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Three Italian players are set for lengthy spells on the sidelines after the latest round of Serie A fixtures, including two members of Roberto Mancini’s most recent national team squad.

Napoli’s Antonio Vergara, Atalanta’s Marco Carnesecchi and Genoa’s Lorenzo Colombo are all expected to miss several months of action with serious injuries they suffered during the 12th round of 2026-27 Serie A fixtures.

Vergara was visibly distressed when he was forced out of Napoli’s 1-0 win over Torino, making way for Kevin De Bruyne after just 11 minutes.

Napoli confirmed in a statement on Monday that he has undergone medical tests that have revealed a high-grade tear to the biceps femoris muscle in his left thigh and according to reports from Sky Sport Italia, this will keep him out of action for a minimum of two months. Other outlets suggest that the timeline will be closer to three months.

Atalanta stopper Carnesecchi, meanwhile, is due to undergo surgery on his knee on Tuesday after suffering a tear of the medial meniscus in his left knee after pulling up injured in the warm-up of his side’s 4-4 draw against Inter on Sunday evening.

It remains unclear exactly how long the 26-year-old will miss, but initial reports suggest that he will be ruled out until January at least.

It was also confirmed on Monday that Genoa striker Colombo has suffered an injury to the anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) of his right knee. Again, an exact recovery time has not been specified, but his injury is the most serious of the three and some reports suggest that his 2026-27 season could be over.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
