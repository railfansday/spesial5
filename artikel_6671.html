The Best Black Friday Jewelry Deals 2026, Handpicked by Vogue Editors

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

We love a good Black Friday fashion deal as much as the next. A cashmere sweater half off? A pair of boots we’ve been eyeing all season, at a fraction of its original price? Yes, and yes. But while the clothing (and home and beauty!) discounts are certainly worth browsing, we find the best Black Friday sales in the jewelry department.

Black Friday is the perfect opportunity to invest in the pieces you’ve been dreaming about but haven’t quite been able to justify buying at full price. Maybe it’s a diamond tennis necklace you’ve had on your wishlist for months, a pair of gold hoops you imagine you’ll never take off, or a delicate bracelet that will look just as good stacked with existing favorites as it does on its own. And while there’s certainly moments indulge in an impulse, jewelry is one category where planning pays off. Fine jewelry, in particular, adds up—so when our favorite brands offer a discount, we pay attention.

With the holidays around the corner, we’re not exclusively shopping for ourselves. Jewelry is also one of the easiest places to start checking names off the gift lists. Unlike clothing, there’s no need to worry about sizing (at least not with necklaces, bracelets, and earrings), and we can’t think of many people who would object to adding another beautiful bauble to their jewelry box. Whether it's a personalized pendant for your best friend, a pair of sparkling earrings for your mom, or something special for your partner, a thoughtfully chosen piece of jewelry always feels personal.

Ahead, we’ve rounded up the best Black Friday jewelry deals from the brands we love and wear ourselves. You'll find a mix of everyday staples we reach for on repeat, fashion-forward designs from designer names, and fine and demi-fine classics worth investing in. So go ahead and treat yourself to that long-awaited splurge while getting a head start on holiday gifting. These are the jewelry sales you don’t want to miss.
