Chicken Spinach Artichoke Soup

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

If you love spinach and artichoke dip, you’re going to love this soup. We took the creamy perfection of the party dip and turned it into a hearty soup by adding chicken breasts, broth, and plenty of flavor. While we love to cozy up to this soup during the cold months, it's light (and delicious) enough to enjoy all year long.

Looking to switch up this soup? You've got plenty of options. Throw in some dry pasta after you add the shredded chicken back into the soup, then boil 8 minutes more before stirring in your spinach. Soup + pasta + classic dip flavors = our kind of dinner. Alternatively, switch up the chicken breasts for your favorite protein (or no protein at all!). Prefer less cheese? Feel free to decrease the mozz and add some more heavy cream.
