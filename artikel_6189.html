Southwest Airlines Launches 5 New Routes In 1 Day: See Full List Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Southwest Airlines is the USA's and the world's fourth-largest operator for flights in November 2026. OAG data indicates that the increasingly evolving carrier, which rightly calls itself a hybrid airline nowadays, has 4,052 services a day.

The carrier's activity has jumped by 4% year-over-year, with 168 additional daily movements added. However, Southwest remains nearly 3% smaller than it was in November 2023. Despite this, multiple new routes are coming, with five launching on one day this week, including new transcontinental operations.

The airline's entire schedule between November 16 and 22 was examined, which identified that five routes will begin in this period. Interestingly, all of them will take off on Saturday, November 21, in the run-up to Thanksgiving. They include a route each from both Denver — which is the airline's most-served airport for flights — and Fort Lauderdale.

On the upcoming Saturday, Southwest will begin flying from Denver to West Palm Beach. The airport's IATA code was recently changed from PBI to DJT because of the name change. At 1,459 nautical miles (2,702 km), it'll become Southwest's new longest-ever nonstop link from the Florida airport. Facing United Airlines, it'll be the first time the market has had two carriers since Frontier Airlines pulled out in 2023, having only returned the year before after a five-year absence.

Three weekly (flights run through November 30 for Thanksgiving, then return on December 18 for Christmas)

On the same day, transcontinental service will commence from Fort Lauderdale to San Diego. It'll be one of three carriers with nonstop service, alongside JetBlue (which takes off on November 19) and Alaska Airlines. It'll be the first time that three airlines have operated simultaneously. In fact, until 2016, the market did not have any nonstop flights at all.

On November 21, Southwest will also introduce service from Kansas City to San Francisco and Las Vegas to Jacksonville. The former route is brand-new to the carrier's map, although it really replaces the link to Oakland, which existed between 1997 and January this year. In contrast, Sin City to Jacksonville was previously served between 2008 and 2016, with a return after a decade notable.

Southwest will coexist with United to San Francisco, with that market last having two operators in 2019, when Alaska Airlines flew. Even more significantly, Las Vegas to Jacksonville will have two airlines for the first time, with Southwest joining Breeze, which has operated since 2022. In both cases, passengers will be pleased with the greater choice of nonstop service. But will the relatively thin market of Jacksonville sustain two airlines?

Finally, there's Orlando to San José (California, not Costa Rica). You've guessed it: Southwest service starts on November 21. The airline previously operated the long domestic link to California between 2018 and 2020. As it is the sole operator, the market regains flights after a six-year absence.

Weekly (flights exist until November 28 for Thanksgiving, then return on December 19 for Christmas through January 5. They return again on June 5. Only a weekly service is available)

In the 12 months to July 2026, US Department of Transportation data shows that the local market between Orlando and San José had 72,100 round-trip passengers. The City Beautiful was the largest unserved domestic market from San José. In the other direction, the Californian city ranked fourth, behind Honolulu (89,900), Boise (80,500), and Ontario (72,600). Like San José, one problem is that all these places have many different hubs at which passengers may connect.
