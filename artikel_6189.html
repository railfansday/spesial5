JetBlue Cancels All Flights To 6 Destinations: See Full List Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

JetBlue continues to undergo many changes. Despite this, OAG data shows that the carrier has 892 daily movements (takeoffs and landings combined) in November, up 4% compared to the same month last year.

Benefiting from the end of the now-defunct Spirit, JetBlue's domestic activity has risen by 8% in 12 months, especially at Fort Lauderdale (+57%). In contrast, international service has fallen by 9%. Like with most airlines, various airports have been dropped recently.

The carrier's network between January and October 2026 was compared to what's available from November 2026. This process identified that the following US airports have been removed. They were not served for long. None were significant, at least not to JetBlue. But the airline was important for them.

Initially between January 2016 and January 2019. Returned in December 2025 but pulled out in September 2026

Boston and New York JFK. Boston only had flights in 2025 and 2026

Fort Lauderdale, Fort Myers, New York JFK, Orlando

In the first nine months of 2026, JetBlue had 17% of the flights at Daytona Beach (18% of the seats due to higher-capacity equipment). It also provided the only service from Boston and JFK to the Florida destination. Delta still flies from LaGuardia, but that is it for the Big Apple.

JetBlue had a smaller role at Manchester-Boston Regional Airport, which is by far New Hampshire's busiest facility. Between January and July 2026, JetBlue had 6% of the services but 10% of the seats for sale. However, Manchester no longer has nonstop service to Fort Lauderdale or New York JFK. The short link to JFK was surely partly for slot-sitting purposes, although also for those transiting elsewhere.

All of these places saw the airline at some point between January and October this year but no longer do so. With JetBlue's exit, only one of the four destinations remains served from New York JFK. That's Antigua, which remains served by American Airlines. Delta Air Lines ended flights in May 2026. However, United Airlines flies from Newark to Antigua, Bonaire, Belize City, and Tulum, although all flights to Tulum will end in January 2027.

The US Department of Transportation shows that JetBlue transported a total of 91,226 round-trip passengers between New York and Tulum. The load factor was a paltry 70.4%, against 83.2% for the airline's overall network from JFK to Mexico. More than half of the months that had Tulum flights saw fewer than seven in ten seats filled. Fare data is unavailable.

Tulum is located on the eastern coast of the Yucatán Peninsula and has been a growing tourist destination for years. This is despite what seem to be clear problems, such as sewage and very high-priced taxis. Historically, people traveled there via Cancun. But despite the opening of Tulum's airport, ground transport from Cancun appears to be easier than ever.

Let's narrow the focus to November 2026. As mentioned earlier, JetBlue's activity at Fort Lauderdale has jumped by a considerable 57% year-on-year. This development played a critical role in the airline's domestic growth. Other major airports grew much more shallowly or declined, such as Boston (+4%) and JFK (-14%).

JetBlue really benefited from the exit of Spirit at the South Florida airport. Last November, Fort Lauderdale was JetBlue's third most-served airport. Now it is first, also influenced, of course, by the decline at JFK. The growth at Fort Lauderdale was driven by the number of routes jumping from 37 last November to 61 now. On November 19, the airline will begin flying to San Diego.
