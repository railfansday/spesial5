Amex Business Platinum Card Bonus: As High As 300K Points Welcome Offer

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Business Platinum Card® from American Express is one of the most perks-rich business cards out there, and is useful if you’re looking to earn Amex Membership Rewards points.

We’re currently seeing a massive elevated welcome offer on the Amex Business Platinum, making it an ideal time to apply. If you’re eligible for this card and the highest possible bonus, it’s absolutely worth taking advantage of, in my opinion.

Link: Learn more about The Business Platinum Card® from American Express

The Amex Business Platinum Card currently has a massive welcome offer that’s as high as 300,000 Membership Rewards points after spending $20,000 within the first three months. Welcome offers vary and you may not be eligible for an offer.

Based on my latest miles & points valuations, I consider Membership Rewards points to be worth 1.7 cents each. This means that to me, the 300,000 points are worth a staggering $5,100. As I see it, that’s basically the best welcome offer you’ll find on any card.

Let me emphasize that Amex uses a variety of factors when deciding what offer people are eligible for, including history of opening and closing cards with Amex, spending history with Amex, how many welcome offers people have earned, etc. So, how does this Amex Business Platinum Card bonus actually work?

You should find the highest possible Amex Business Platinum bonus through the above link. However, if you don’t, it can always be worth checking refer-a-friend links, opting in to targeted emails from Amex, or even using the CardMatch tool to see what you’re eligible for there.

That being said, don’t expect to get a higher offer through any of those sources, since offers are typically targeted based on the person, rather than the application method. However, it can always be worth checking back every so often, because the promotions people are targeted for can change over time.

The Amex Business Platinum bonus spending requirement is potentially significant, as it averages out to nearly $6,700 per month. However, there are lots of ways to be creative with reaching minimum spending, and to make a plan. You could time your application around some large upcoming business expenses (whether that’s an ad campaign or buying inventory), prepay for some items, and maybe even put some expenses on credit cards that you might otherwise pay for with cash. Of course make sure you pay off everything in full by the payment due date, so that you don’t get stuck paying high interest charges.

Upon completing minimum spending, bonus points will officially post after 8-12 weeks, though in practice, typically post much faster than that.

There are lots of great uses for Amex points, as they can be transferred to all kinds of useful airline and hotel partners. Also keep in mind that having this card lets you unlock the full value of Amex Pay With Points, potentially allowing you to get up to 1.54 cents of value per point toward the cost of airfare on a designated airline (this comes in the form of a 35% Pay with Points rebate, up to one million points per year). You could then use that redemption rate for all your linked Amex Membership Rewards cards.

Anecdotally, I find the Amex Business Platinum to be a pretty easy card to get approved for, and you can read about my experience applying here.

There’s not a consistent credit score you need to get approved for the Amex Business Platinum, though I’d hope to have a score in the “good” to “excellent” range, ideally 700+ points. However, people with lower scores have been approved, and people with higher scores have been rejected, as it’s one of only many factors that impacts card approval.
