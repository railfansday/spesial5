Nvidia posted third-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of reportedly $115.8 billion, ahead of the roughly $109.3 billion consensus, and guided January-quarter sales to $130 billion, plus or minus 3%. Data center revenue reportedly reached $107.6 billion, topping estimates of $101.87 billion and up about 110% from a year earlier, while adjusted earnings came in at $2.68 per share against expectations of $2.47. Adjusted gross margin was 74.9%, though the outlook points to a narrower 71% to 72% this quarter. The fourth-quarter forecast sits above the $124.4 billion to $125.2 billion range expected by the Street and implies sequential growth of about 12%. Nvidia also said Vera Rubin NVL72 systems have begun shipping, calling it the fastest product ramp in its history, according to the posts. The report is the first since Jensen Huang resigned as CEO on November 12.
