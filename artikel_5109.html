The 60 Best White Elephant Gift Ideas of 2026 Are Worth the Steal

A White Elephant gift exchange can go so many ways. First, you have to find the perfect present to delight your friends, horrify your family, and prank your colleagues. Then comes the gift-stealing chaos and the excitement of what you'll ultimately take home. If that makes you skeptical, trust that it's FUN.

For those who haven't had the pleasure, White Elephant is a holiday game where everyone brings a gift—usually with a set price limit. The gifts are usually a little bit off-color, though not always. But it's not another game of Secret Santa. Instead, White Elephant kicks off with someone picking a present at random, so whatever you gift has to have pretty wide appeal.

From there, everyone can either steal a gift that's already been opened or pick a new one. Whether you can steal gifts back is a key distinction before things get competitive and you end up with a bunch of Grinches on your hands.

Ready to steal your way to a happy holiday season? We curated a list of the 60 best White Elephant gifts that are all under $50, fun, unique, and—best of all!—in stock. While we don’t advise procrastinating, almost all our picks are available on Amazon, so they’ll be at your doorstep in no time. Honestly, some of these are so good, you’ll be tempted to snag ‘em for yourself.

Just snap, print, and stick them in journals, scrapbooks, cards, and more. Plus, this printer uses thermal technology and no ink, so you won't have to worry about pesky refills.
