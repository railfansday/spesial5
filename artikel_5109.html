Bullish recruits Wall Street heavyweights to solve tokenized stocks’ biggest trading problem

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

DRW, Flow Traders, SoFi and Superstate have joined a 12-member coalition seeking to make blockchain-based shares easier to trade across platforms.

Bullish has added four major financial firms to a coalition seeking to make tokenized stocks easier to trade across platforms.

The crypto exchange and transfer agent Equiniti announced on Nov. 17 that DRW, Flow Traders, SoFi and Superstate had joined the Issuer Token Coalition, bringing its membership to 12 companies.

The group, which also includes the New York Stock Exchange, is developing common standards for issuing, transferring and trading shares represented on blockchain networks.

Its latest expansion brings together institutional trading firms, liquidity providers, financial services companies and tokenization specialists to address a persistent obstacle in the emerging market: investors can hold real company shares onchain but cannot freely trade them across different platforms.

Bullish's own shares illustrate the problem. The company allows eligible shareholders to convert their BLSH holdings into tokens on Solana, where they can hold them in personal wallets or transfer them directly to other approved investors.

However, both parties must be registered with Equiniti, which maintains Bullish's official shareholder records, while their wallets must pass compliance screening.

Moving tokenized shares to another brokerage or trading venue also requires that platform to support the security, verify the investor, and recognize the ownership recorded by the transfer agent.

These restrictions have largely confined tokenized shares to arrangements established by their issuers and service providers.

The coalition intends to address those barriers through common technical standards, liquidity arrangements and infrastructure connecting traditional financial markets with blockchain networks.

Flow Traders, which provides liquidity across conventional and digital asset markets, said the initiative would help establish conditions for reliable trading, including outside traditional market hours.

Superstate already operates a model demonstrating how those connections can work.

In September 2025, Galaxy Digital introduced tokenized GLXY shares on Solana through an arrangement involving Equiniti and Superstate.

Under the structure, shareholders can move conventional Galaxy shares through Equiniti to Superstate, receive corresponding tokens in approved wallets and reverse the process to return their holdings to a brokerage account.

Bullish is pursuing a broader integration of these capabilities through its planned acquisition of Equiniti.

In its Nov. 18 earnings report, the company maintained its expectation that the transaction would close in January 2027, bringing the transfer agent responsible for shareholder records under common ownership with its exchange.

The acquisition would also give Bullish access to Equiniti's relationships with thousands of corporate issuers as it seeks to expand stock tokenization.

Meanwhile, the coalition's next phase will focus on liquidity mechanics, technical architecture and commercial arrangements needed to connect issuers, investors and trading venues.
