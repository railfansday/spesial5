Ripple’s XRP Ledger Lending Protocol Eyes Final Activation

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The XRP Ledger lending protocol proposed by leading blockchain firm Ripple is making the headlines as it appears to be rapidly moving closer to its final debut.

The latest reports on its progress show that the protocol is just one validator vote away from concluding its mandatory approval process on the network.

Barely a few weeks after Ripple proposed a lending protocol on the XRP Ledger, the lending protocol amendment has already secured 28 of the 35 validator votes required to begin the approval process.

This marks major progress for the development, as the XRPL amendment system requires that a proposal earn more than 80% support from trusted validators for two consecutive weeks before it becomes permanently enabled.

While the approval period is mandated to restart if support falls below that level during the countdown, it appears that the Ripple lending protocol is already set to wrap up this process, as it is just one more vote away from reaching the 29-vote milestone.

This means that the lending vaults may be on track for their activation on December 9 if the lending protocol amendment retains the ongoing support.

With the proposed protocol drawing closer to its final launch, the XRP community has continued to express excitement as the proposal aims to introduce fixed-term, fixed-rate lending directly on the XRP Ledger.

As such, users will be able to deposit assets including the Ripple stablecoin and earn interest from borrowers, providing additional ways to earn money.

It is important to note that the proposal aims to provide exclusive opportunities for both lenders and borrowers while further expanding Ripple’s $837 million stablecoin market.
