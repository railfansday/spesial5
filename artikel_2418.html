Dortmund unlikely to pursue Gladbach gem Mohya as focus is placed on Albert

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Borussia Dortmund are not expected to intensify their efforts to sign Wael Mohya, according to BILD podcast Dortmund Insider.

While BVB considers the Borussia Mönchengladbach talent a “super player”, signing Moyha is not an option as it stands.

The reason for this is that the Westphalians have a talented player in their ranks in Mathis Albert, whose playing style is very similar.

Albert, a US international, is considered to have "incredibly high potential" within the club, even if he hasn't been getting as much playing time.

Sky had linked Mohya with Borussia Dortmund at the end of October, but now it appears certain that BVB will not pursue a transfer.

For Mohya, whose contract at Gladbach runs until 2028, numerous other options like Bayern Munich and Bayer Leverkusen remain.
