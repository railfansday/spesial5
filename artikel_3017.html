Bam Adebayo leaves win over Rockets early with hip injury, Heat don't expect long-term issue

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Bam Adebayo left Monday night's game against the Houston Rockets early with an apparent hip injury.

The Miami Heat star did not take the court to start the second half of their 128-110 victory over the Rockets at the Kaseya Center on Monday, and was instead replaced by Andrew Wiggins to open the third quarter. It didn't appear as if anything happened to Adebayo in the first half, but he was not spotted on the bench as play resumed.

Eventually, the Heat said he was questionable to return with right hip tightness. They then ruled him out for good in the fourth quarter. Further specifics of the injury are not yet known.

Adebayo finished the night with just four points and two assists while shooting 1 of 3 from the field. He entered the night averaging 19.5 points and 8.5 rebounds per contest this season.

Heat coach Erik Spoelstra said after the game that the team was being "proactive" in holding Adebayo out, and that he didn't expect it to be a long-term issue, according to the Miami Herald's Anthony Chiang.

"I saw how he was grinding through it in the second quarter," Spoelstra said, via ESPN. "No matter what he says and no matter what anybody says, let's sit him down and evaluate him tomorrow."

While they don't have specifics yet, losing Adebayo for an extended amount of time would undoubtedly make a major impact on the Heat. Despite losing him, the Heat still managed to roll to a convincing win over the Rockets.

Miami led nearly the entire way on Monday night. The Heat carried a 16-point lead into the locker room at halftime and just cruised the rest of the way to secure the 18-point victory. Giannis Antetokounmpo led the way with 20 points, 16 rebounds and 6 assists. Klay Thompson added 17 points after going 5 of 10 from the 3-point line, and Wiggins had 17 points and five rebounds after stepping in for Adebayo in the second half. The Heat have now won five straight, and sit at 8-6 on the season.

Alperen Sengun led the Rockets with 23 points and seven rebounds. Kevin Durant finished with 22 points, and Jabari Smith Jr. had 12 points and 10 rebounds. The loss dropped them to 11-4 on the year.
