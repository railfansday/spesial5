Memory shortages weigh on consumer demand, yet a 2028 rebound looms.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 25, 2026 at 10:09AM EST

Ambarella (AMBA -19.89%) reported record edge AI revenue but issued fourth-quarter guidance below seasonal averages due to memory supply constraints. Management stated that rising prices and limited availability of DRAM caused customers to cancel, push out, or delay orders, specifically in the consumer IoT segment. The company maintained its long-term outlook for edge AI applications and reported that revenue growth is expected to return in fiscal 2028, driven by production ramps of 5-nanometer and 4-nanometer products with higher average selling prices.
