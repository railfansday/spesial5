Name any retailer and chances are they're offering Black Friday TV deals today. For years, TVs have been the rockstar of Black Friday deals and that remains true for 2026. But not every TV deal you'll find today is worth your time.

I've been covering Black Friday TV deals for 19 years. I even purchased my first OLED TV two Black Friday ago. So when it comes to deal hunting, consider me your expert guide. I'll help you navigate through the maze of TV deals and spotlight the discounts on the TVs the Tom's Guide staff has tested, recommends, or has purchased.

My top pick today is the Hisense 75-inch U6 Pro Mini-LED 4K TV for $699 at Amazon. In our Hisense U6SF Pro review we called the Editor's Choice TV one of this year's best budget TVs. The 55-, 65-, and 75-inch models are all at their lowest price of the year starting from $469.

Make sure to bookmark this page as we bring you more of the best Black Friday TV deals today. For a wider look at this week's sales, check out our Black Friday deals guide.

The Ember Artline is Amazon's take on the art TV competing with the likes of Samsung's The Frame. It features a matte 4K QLED display that, like The Frame, can act as artwork when you're not watching shows or movies. Instead of Tizen, however, the Artline comes with the new Fire TV operating system that received an overhaul this past February, as well as access to Alexa+.

The TCL QM6K has been a Tom's Guide go-to recommendation for some time now, especially if you're on a budget and looking to spend less than $1,000. The 4K Mini-LED TV offers better brightness and backlight control than pricier competitors, supports 4K gaming at 120Hz, and if you hook up a gaming PC, the QM6K supports 4K at 144Hz. It's an all-around crowd pleaser.

In our Hisense U6SF Pro review, we called the U6 Pro one of the best budget Mini-LED TVs out there. All models are on sale, but the 75-inch model is such an epic bargain at this price point. Two of its four HDMI inputs feature 2.1 functionality (like support for 4K games up to 144Hz), and the screen is outfitted with a glare-free finish that actually works. If you're on a budget, enjoy mostly daytime viewing, and are an avid gamer, this TV is for you.

The QM7L is a fantastic way to experience TCL's SQD Mini-LED technology for much less than what you'll spend on a higher-end model. We recently awarded this TV a 4.5-star rating for its sensational color, its thorough collection of features, and its fantastically bright picture.

The C6 is LG's flagship OLED TV for 2026. In our LG C6 OLED TV review we said the Editor's Choice TV performs brilliantly offering top-tier brightness and colors. We're also fans of its gameplay enhancing features including a new Game Optimizer mode that obliterates input lag and boosts FPS on both consoles and PCs. Whether you're a gamer, sports fan, or movie buff — you won't be disappointed with the C6.

Let me start by saying that we didn't give the 2026 Roku Select Series TV the best review. Yes, it's very affordable, but my colleague Sean Captain said its generally disappointing and you might be better off with the 2025 Roku Plus Series for $299 (if you can still find stock).

The Hisense UR8 is an RGB Mini-LED 4K TV. That's a mouthful, but in a nutshell — RGB LED TVs use an array of ultra-small LEDs arranged in dimmable zones. However, unlike Mini-LEDs — RGB LEDs use red, green and blue LEDs. That results in brighter, purer colors than what we’ve come to expect from even the best TVs.

The UR8 is a Best Buy exclusive — along with many other RGB Mini-LEDs — and in typical Hisense fashion — it's disrupting the market with an insanely low price point. Case in point — this TV debuted at $1,699 and it's already down to $849, an all-time price low.

You also get some great gaming features, a cool design, and impressive black levels. It's our top pick for value and a sick deal for anyone looking to go OLED this year.
