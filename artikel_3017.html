Christian Parker stripped of play-calling duties with Cowboys

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Christian Parker stripped of play-calling duties with Cowboys originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Cowboys have reportedly stripped play-calling duties from defensive coordinator Christian Parker after 10 games.

Parker, 34, spent the previous two seasons coaching defensive backs with the Eagles before taking the DC job in Dallas, but now veteran coach Mike Pettine will take over play-calling, ESPN reported.

The Eagles face the Cowboys on Thanksgiving in eight days.

In the first meeting of these two teams, the Cowboys won 38-21 and the Eagles were able to gain 321 yards — their second-highest total all season.

The Cowboys brought in the 60-year-old Pettine as a senior defensive assistant back in October and a little over a month later, he's now the play-caller. While Parker is remaining on staff as the Cowboys' DC, it doesn't seem like he's long for that position.

The Eagles tried a mid-season DC switch back in 2023 when they went from Sean Desai to Matt Patricia. That certainly didn't work; the defense got worse after the Week 13 change.

When the Cowboys hired Parker, their hope was that he could stabilize that defense to pair with their explosive offense. That hasn't gone to plan. The Cowboys' defense under Parker ranks 32 in yards allowed and 30th in points allowed. In the Cowboys 46-40 loss to the 49ers in Week 10, they gave up 584 total yards.

While Parker has not found success in Dallas, he was a very good defensive backs coach with the Eagles. He helped the Eagles win a Super Bowl in 2024 and then helped Quinyon Mitchell and Cooper DeJean become All-Pros in Year 2 of their careers.

There are plenty of similarities between Parker and Eagles' OC Sean Mannion. Both were exciting hires this offseason of young, up-and-coming coaches. Both were charged with trying to get their respective units to play at a league-average rate and both have been unable to do it.
