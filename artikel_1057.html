When will England play Norway and who could they play in Nations League semi-finals?

England will play Norway in the Nations League quarter-finals in March, in a rematch of this summer’s World Cup quarter-final.

Jude Bellingham scored twice in Miami as England came from behind to end Norway’s sensational World Cup run back in July. England’s World Cup came to an end following defeat to Argentina in the semi-finals but Thomas Tuchel’s side are now looking to build on their impressive victory over world champions Spain in Madrid, which saw them top Group A3, by winning the Nations League for the first time.

But Erling Haaland’s team will be out for revenge after their incredible comeback to beat Denmark secured progress to the two-legged Nations League quarter-finals. Norway needed to beat Denmark in Copenhagen to finish as runners-up in Group A4, but found themselves two goals down inside 15 minutes. They fought back, however, with Haaland’s last-minute penalty sending them through to the quarter-finals for the first time.

A place in the Nations League Finals, which will be staged in June and at the end of the season, is up for grabs for the winners. There have been reports of the Football Association expressing an interest in hosting the Nations League Finals, which feature the two semi-finals, a third-place match, and the final. Portugal, Italy, the Netherlands and Germany have previously hosted the Finals.

England will go to Norway for the first leg on Saturday 27 March 2027, with a kick-off time of 5pm GMT (UK time).

The return leg will then be played at home on Tuesday 30 March 2027, with a kick-off time of 7:45pm GMT.

The Nations League finals are held at the end of the club season and will be played at one venue between 9-13 June 2027. Portugal, Italy, the Netherlands and Germany have previously hosted the Finals, and there have been reports that England would be interested in staging them should Thomas Tuchel’s side reach the final four.

The host country of the Finals is typically involved in the final four, although the Nations League could also allow the FA a test event before hosting Euro 2028 the following summer, with Scotland, Wales and the Republic of Ireland also co-hosts.

Should England progress to the semi-finals, they will play either Jurgen Klopp’s Germany or Belgium, while the other semi-final will see either Spain or France play against Portugal or the Netherlands.

Germany impressively thashed Netherlands 6-0 on the final game of the group phase to leapfrog them in the table and top Group A2, while Belgium won 4-1 against Italy to finish runner-up in Group A2 behind France.
