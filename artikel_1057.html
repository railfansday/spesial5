Xabi Alonso tells angry Chelsea fans to back Wesley Fofana after they told him to 'f*** off' during desperate defeat by Nottingham Forest

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Published: 18:09 EST, 27 November 2026 | Updated: 18:33 EST, 27 November 2026

Xabi Alonso called on Chelsea’s fans to support Wesley Fofana after their away end spent their latest defeat chanting for their own player to get out of their club.

Fofana started against Nottingham Forest after apologising for the unauthorised interview he had conducted with a French YouTube channel. The content of that chat included him laughing off how he missed the penalty which condemned Chelsea to their Carabao Cup exit at Liverpool.

From the first minute at the City Ground, he was booed on the ball and subjected to abusive singing from Chelsea’s supporters, who chanted: ‘F*** off Fofana, you’re not wanted here.’

Fofana went on to give away a penalty by fouling his former team-mate Liam Delap and was then replaced by Levi Colwill, who had been dropped from the line-up instead of the Frenchman.

Alonso confirmed he had not yet spoken with Fofana after full-time and added at his post-match press conference on the fans: ‘We need to support everyone because we need everyone. We need the fans, we need the players, we need to stick together because if not, things will get more complicated. We need to help. We need to go through this together.’

Alonso also would not confirm if he will leave Fofana out of Chelsea’s next game at home to Crystal Palace on Wednesday despite what turned into an ugly night here for his team.

‘We are going through a difficult moment of confidence, things are not clicking, not going the way we want,’ Alonso added. ‘We know in football, this can change quickly or can continue. It's down to us to work to find those solutions to have a better feeling on the pitch and to get better results because that's what we need.

Forest manager Oliver Glasner added: ‘We were struggling at the beginning to score the goals, especially here at the City Ground. We could see the progress but we couldn't get the results in many other games and now it's of course to keep working. Just 12 games played with 12 points so it's still not satisfying for us. Many games to go and for us it's to stay humble and keep working.’
