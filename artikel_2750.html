Jaguars Injury Report: Tuten Downgraded Ahead of Giants Matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Jacksonville Jaguars' Week 11 Thursday injury report was almost identical to Wednesday's practice opener, with one major exception. Running back Bhayshul Tuten has been dealing with an ankle/foot issue since the November 1st injury against the Indianapolis Colts that just doesn't seem to be going away anytime soon.

Here is the latest on where everyone stands.

C Robert Hainsey (knee): Head coach Liam Coen ruled Hainsey out on Wednesday. He is reportedly week-to-week.

RB Bhayshul Tuten (ankle/foot): After being a limited participant on Wednesday, the second-year back, on Thursday, was downgraded as the Jaguars prepare to face the New York Giants on the road. Notably, Tuten was spotted stretching and lightly working off to the side of the field on Thursday. Head coach Liam Coen previously touched on Tuten's health coming out of Sunday's victory:

I don't know if he was feeling 100 percent yesterday, and some runs that I think he normally either breaks or gets extra yards on didn't quite occur yesterday, but I mean, 26 [carries] was his biggest workload that he's had over the year… He's got a lot of confidence. I just want him to hopefully feel a little bit better as we go."

DE BJ Green II (forearm): Green practiced fully again after doing so all of last week. He continues to work his way back from injured reserve after his 21-day practice window opened last week. As he continues stacking up full participation practices without a setback, the odds of the second-year defensive end returning increase. Could this be the Sunday we see BJ Green?

CB Nic Jones (knee): Jones didn't practice for two straight days. While he played just 7% of snaps for New York, his absence would be felt mostly on special teams, where he has played 83% of the Giants' snaps (188), according to Pro-Football-Reference.

Skattebo has been dealing with an intermittent hamstring issue throughout November, resting it through the bye week and missing the team's Week 9 matchup against the Philadelphia Eagles before returning last week against Washington. He initially injured it during a 42-yard run in Week 7 against the Houston Texans, but seems to be in full health at this time.
