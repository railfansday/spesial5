Jets beat Chargers 27-24 to end five-game losing streak

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Jets offense has struggled in the red zone throughout the 2026 season, but they were able to change their luck in Los Angeles on Sunday.

Geno Smith threw three touchdown passes and the Jets scored on their first five possessions of the game. Their sixth and final possession began after the Chargers cut their lead to 27-24 on the final play before the two minute warning and the Jets were able to run for a pair of first downs that allowed them to head home with their third win of the season.

The Jets’ five scoring drives were all long marches down the field that ate up clock and cut down on the Chargers’ margin for error during their own time with the ball. The Jets were 9-of-11 on third downs over the course of the afternoon and Smith’s efficient 17-of-21 passing for 176 yards was matched by a strong rushing attack.

Breece Hall had 20 carries for 86 yards and Braelon Allen chipped in 16 carries for 75 more yards. Hall caught one of Smith’s touchdowns and veteran Sterling Shepard caught the other two after coming up as a practice squad elevation to help cover for the absences of Omar Cooper and Isaiah Williams.

The Chargers were fairly efficient as well and they avoided the turnovers that plagued them during their 0-8 start to the season, but a three-and-out to open the third quarter was followed by a 10-minute Jets scoring drive and the defense’s inability to get off the field didn’t leave the Chargers with enough opportunities to pick up their second win of the season.

They’ll remain in Los Angeles in Week 12 to host the reeling Patriots while the 3-8 Jets will be in Miami to try to avoid a season sweep at the hands of the Dolphins.
