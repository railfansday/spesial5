What's the weather forecast for Giants vs. Commanders?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The New York Giants and Washington Commanders are playing an important NFC East divisional game on Thursday night. And just like last time, some inclement weather could make it a very sloppy affair. It seems Mother Nature cares not to have perfect conditions for a random professional football game. Who would have thought?

The current forecast for the Giants' and Commanders' game in New Jersey, per The Weather Channel, calls for heavy rain right around kickoff. The precipitation will gradually taper off, but it is expected to rain on some level until near the end of the game. Woof.

For what it's worth, this is what the conditions in New Jersey looked like around 5:30 Eastern Time. It's raining cats and dogs out there!

There's a good chance we'll see a lot of high-level athletes slipping and sliding all over on Thursday night. I, for one, can't wait to see the possible shenanigans that this sort of weather creates.
