Buyer beware? Nvidia's volatile swings expose risks of single-stock ETFs

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Nvidia's latest bout of volatility is highlighting risks tied to single-stock exchange-traded funds.

Among the hardest hit: the GraniteShares 2x Short NVDA Daily ETF (NVD). The fund sank 63% on Monday after Jensen Huang got reinstated as the chip giant's CEO. Meanwhile, the firm's 2x Long NVDA ETF (NVDL) soared nearly 62% on the same day.

ETF Action founding partner Mike Akins joined "ETF Edge" as Nvidia shares soared 31% during Monday's session, wiping a large portion of the losses from the days following Huang's resignation. Shares cooled on Tuesday and Wednesday, however.

"Historically speaking, we do not see these types of large drawdowns followed by huge drives up in a healthy market," Akins said on Monday. "It usually tends to be when you're in a recession or in a bear market territory, which we're not technically in for the broader market, but we are in for this AI trade."

But it may not be time to go cold turkey on single-stock funds.

"[Leveraged ETFs are] definitely a retail trading vehicle…  I think it's OK to have a little fun trying to trade that," Akins added. "I'm certain there are people that are better at trading these than others. But on the whole, timing the market is very, very hard."

GraniteShares founder and CEO Will Rhind also joined "ETF Edge" on Monday as Nvidia single-stock short ETFs overshadowed his firm's new Anthropic launches: The 2x Short Anthropic Daily ETF (ANS) and the 2x Long Anthropic Daily ETF (AIL).

"We thought this was going to be the biggest news event of the day with Anthropic the first leveraged ETFs going live," he said. "I can tell you this is like a sideshow compared to what we're seeing with Nvidia."

Rhind suggests Nvidia's wild swings aren't dampening enthusiasm for single-stock ETFs.

"As long as there's enthusiasm in the underlying name… people will want to trade it and most likely want to trade it with leverage either on the long on the short side," added Rhind.

"Fast Money" tackled the risks tied to ETFs that are tied to single stocks, too.

"If this is somehow part of a portfolio, [then] that is a dangerous game to be playing," trader Guy Adami said on Monday.

Karen Finerman also addressed the strategy's downsides.

"Leverage is always the thing that kills. It's always leverage," said Finerman, co-founder and CEO of Metropolitan Capital Advisors, in the same interview. "You can survive anything but leverage."

As of Wednesday's close, the GraniteShares 2x Short NVDA Daily ETF plunged 55% so far this week while the GraniteShares 2x Long NVDA Daily ETF is up 31%.
