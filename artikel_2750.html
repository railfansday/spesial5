Commanders DT Javon Kinlaw had MCL surgery, is expected to miss rest of season

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Commanders defensive tackle Javon Kinlaw’s season is reportedly over.

Kinlaw went on injured reserve this week due to a knee injury. Adam Schefter of ESPN reports on Wednesday that he had surgery to repair an MCL injury and that he will miss the rest of the season as a result.

It’s the second veteran defensive tackle that the Commanders have lost for the season in recent weeks. Daron Payne will be out for the rest of the year with a foot injury.

Kinlaw started six games before his injury and he had 13 tackles and a sack in those appearances. He is signed for 2027 with a non-guaranteed base salary of $12.9 million and a $16.9 million cap hit.
