Los Angeles Chargers news: Bolts’ commitment to Omarion Hampton apparent in 1st win

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Chargers began the season with Omarion Hampton as their lead back, but the second-year runner lost two fumbles in as many games to start the campaign and that quickly led the Bolts to a three-headed committee for the next month-plus.

In recent weeks, Hampton's been garnering a larger share of the backfield touches, but Monday night's performance was much more of what we all expected to be the norm week-in and week-out. Hampton carried the football more than 20 times and finished with 105 yards and a score, his first 100-yard game since Week 4 of last year. He looked strong, locked-in, and overall more comfortable as the game went on the more he touched the football.

This success on the ground or Hampton also coincided with a shift in the run game, going away from zone and more into gap and power schemes. Per James Koh of 97.1 The Fan in Los Angeles, the Chargers ran zone 53 percent of the time from Weeks One through Nine, but that number dropped to just 38 percent against the Ravens.

The shift away from zone for Hampton has been discussed at length this season, noting his success in gap schemes dating back to his time at North Carolina. On one of his best runs of the night, the Bolts pulled left tackle Joe Alt across the formation to kick out a defender in the hole and Hampton cut right off his backside for a gain of 19 to begin the second half.

Overall the Chargers ran for 164 yards on 34 carries, aided by Justin Herbert's 51 yards off scrambles, to end the night averaging a strong 4.7 yards per tote.

Is this the new recipe moving forward for the Chargers? I wouldn't be shocked in the slightest, if so.
