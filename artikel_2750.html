Vikings Week 10 Stock Market Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Minnesota Vikings righted the ship with a convincing 30-10 blowout of the Green Bay Packers at Lambeau Field, sweeping the season series and moving into a three-way tie for the division lead at 7-2 with the Chicago Bears and Detroit Lions. The Vikings have the tie-breaker over the Bears and the Lions have the tie-breaker over the Vikings. Kirk Cousins provided some key throws and generally provided competent quarterback play, allowing the Vikings to score 30 points. Meanwhile, the defense terrorized Jordan Love most of the game, generating 8 sacks and three turnovers. Special teams produced a fourth turnover. Overall, it was a bit of a statement game for the Vikings with Cousins now at the helm and the defense continuing to play absolutely lights out.

Kirk Cousins. It wasn't an outstanding stat line from Cousins in his first start since the Vikings traded for him, but it was more than competent with some key passes that turned into touchdowns while allowing the Vikings to win the time of possession battle with intelligent play despite just two full practices since arriving in Minnesota. He finished 21/31 for 207 yards, 2 TDs, 1 INT, a 94.4 passer rating and a 74.1 QBR. His game highlights below.

Myles Price. He had 4 receptions on 4 targets for 49 yards and the Vikings first touchdown on 3rd and 23. Great play to keep his balance and get into the end zone. Best performance by a WR3 this season and worthy of a more permanent role.

The Entire Defensive Front Seven Including Backups. You wouldn't know that Blake Cashman missed this game- which hasn't been the case in the past- or Tyrion Ingram-Dawkins. They produced 8 sacks on 27 quarterback pressures and 2 turnovers while allowing just 3.3 yards per rush attempt. Levi Drake-Rodriquez, Dallas Turner, Jalen Redmond, Andrew Van Ginkel, Caleb Banks (yes Caleb Banks), Elijah Williams, Domonique Orange, Kyle Van Noy, Ivan Pace Jr., Eric Wilson, and Jake Golday. It's great to see the backups getting worked in and doing well, which is encouraging should there be injuries and for future seasons.

Tai Felton. He had 3 receptions on 5 targets for 33 yards including an impressive back shoulder grab on 3rd and 15 that setup the Vikings' second touchdown.

Justin Jefferson. The return of Kirk Cousins was music to the ears of Jefferson, who had 9 receptions on 13 targets for 74 yards. Still below his career game average, but his most productive game in the last four.

Bret Thorson. He's had a couple nice games punting the ball lately. He had three against the Packers, two of which were inside the 20 including a 64-yarder. He's also done a great job as holder.

Will Reichard. He is back to his perfect self, making all three field goal attempts and all three extra points.

Joe Huber. He had a clean sheet in pass protection and a good preliminary PFF run blocking grade. Making a strong case to be a starter next year as well.

Blake Brandel. He's allowed just one hurry with a near elite preliminary grade as a run blocker. He's proving to be very solid at center.

Gavin Bartholomew. He did well in relief of T.J. Hockenson with 3 receptions on 4 targets for 36 yards and a touchdown (his first).

Jakobe Thomas. He got his first reps of the season on special teams against the Packers and forced a fumble which Jake Golday recovered and put the game out of reach.

Jay Ward. He had a personal foul early in the game on special teams after some extracurricular activity that wasn't smart. Can't give away cheap yards like that, especially on the road.

The Minnesota Vikings as legitimate Super Bowl contenders with Cousins at quarterback and perhaps the most dominant defense in recent years.

It being automatic that the Vikings will have a deep playoff run. There's still a lot of football left to play and key games against the 49ers next week in Mexico, the Lions, and Bears. The Vikings will need to stay healthy and keep their foot on the gas from now until February.
