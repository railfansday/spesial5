Steelers’ Nick Herbig still limited ahead of Broncos game

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

With Aaron Rodgers' televising his exposé on the Steelers and eventually being released Wednesday, it's easy to forget that Pittsburgh still held a team practice ahead of a Black Friday matchup with the Denver Broncos.

In the Steelers' Wednesday injury report, outside linebacker Nick Herbig remained limited in practice for the second straight day as he recovers from a hip/ribs injury that caused him to miss Pittsburgh's Week 11 game against the Philadelphia Eagles.

Notably, the Steelers had several new names join the availability report, with safety DeShon Elliott (knee) and linebacker Cole Holcomb (ankle) appearing as limited. Quarterback Aaron Rodgers, now off the team, was unsurprisingly a non-participant for "personal" reasons; veteran defender Cam Heyward was also given a rest day.

Outside linebacker Alex Highsmith (ankle) and wide receiver Ben Skowronek (calf) did not participate for the second straight practice on Wednesday.

Still in their 21-day return windows from injured reserve, wide receiver Michael Pittman Jr. and rookie guard Gennings Dunker continued to log full practices.

As for Denver, safety Taylor Rapp (limited; quadricep) and defensive end Sai'vion Jones (DNP; hamstring) were listed as out, but the rest of the team appears healthy. Running back Tyler Badie, who has logged full practices this week, is expected to be activated from IR at some point with the Broncos' JK Dobbins serving a one-game team suspension this week.

The Steelers have not yet released game designations for Black Friday's game.
