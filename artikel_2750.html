Shead: Approach every game the same…We have to grow up in that sense

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

After the Toronto Raptors lost 116-111 to the Milwaukee Bucks, backup point guard Jamal Shead took to the podium.

Shead finished with 11 points and six assists. But none of that mattered because of the outcome, and that was evident by Shead's post-game presser.

Here are five quotes from his sit-down with the media:

"Approach every game the same way we approached Detroit," said Shead. "I think we have to grow up in that sense. Just being prepared and be ready."

Shead started his media availability with the obvious. The Raptors have looked like two different teams at times. They'll take the defending champion New York Knicks to the absolute limit and defeat the Detroit Pistons on their home court, but then fall to the lowly Bucks.

"Last year is over. All this year, we referenced last year a lot, and we need to stop doing that."

There has been a considerable amount of discourse regarding this, especially when it comes to comparing the two different rosters. I've been guilty of it. It's impossible to avoid comparisons because the 2026-27 Raptors are basically the same team, outside of a swap between Kawhi Leonard and Brandon Ingram. However, the team that fans are watching now is basically the same squad that took the Cleveland Cavaliers to seven games in the playoffs.

"We missed a lot of open shots because they were doubling him [Scottie]…We make those shots and it's a different game."

This is key. It'll always be key for as long as Scottie Barnes is on the team. Toronto needs to surround its star with shooting, just like Leonard was in 2018-19.

"The pressure has been on since the season started…we keep saying the same thing over and over, we have to be better."

Shead was asked if they feel pressure the further they progress through the season. There's still some time, but this year's iteration of the Raptors needs to figure it out sooner rather than later.

"He just needs to slow himself down. We all saw what he's capable of last year…he just needs to find different ways to impact the game, he's on the scouting report now…people prepare for him."

While he may have entered the league as an older rookie, Shead has come a long way since his inaugural season in the NBA. He spoke about Collin Murray-Boyles and offered advice on how his teammate can navigate this tricky stretch.
