Grocery and general merchandise demand supported sales, while Walmart continued attracting shoppers seeking lower prices. Its digital business remained a standout. Global e-commerce sales increased 17%, reflecting continued growth in online shopping, marketplace activity and store-based fulfillment.

The results highlight Walmart’s ability to capture consumer demand in a price-sensitive environment. However, slower comparable-sales growth compared with last year suggests the company still faces pressure to maintain momentum.

Walmart raised its full-year outlook for the third time this year, signaling confidence in its operating performance. The retailer now expects full-year sales growth of 4.9% to 5.2% and adjusted earnings of $2.84 to $2.89 per share.

Investors are also watching operating income, as Walmart’s price investments and elevated costs could limit profit growth despite stronger sales. The guidance leaves the market with a mixed signal: revenue momentum remains resilient, but profitability may take longer to accelerate.

The holiday shopping season is now the biggest test for Walmart’s growth outlook. With consumers remaining cautious about discretionary spending, Walmart’s low-price positioning could help it gain market share across groceries, electronics, apparel and household products. Its expanding online marketplace and faster delivery services could also strengthen its competitive position during Black Friday and the broader holiday period.

Investors will closely monitor management’s commentary on holiday traffic, promotional intensity and consumer spending.Â  Strong demand could reinforce expectations for continued sales growth, while cautious commentary may raise concerns about margins and the sustainability of Walmart’s rally.

Walmart stock closed at $127.36 ahead of the earnings announcement, leaving it below its recent resistance zone and near the lower boundary of its short-term rising channel. The stock faces immediate resistance at $131.86. A sustained breakout above this level could open the path toward $135.89, near its previous record high, followed by $139.57.

On the downside, $125.10 remains a crucial support level. A break below it could weaken the bullish structure and expose WMT to $123.02, followed by $120.57. A sustained move above $131.86 could signal renewed buying momentum, while a breakdown below $125.10 may indicate that investors are selling the earnings reaction.

The next move in WMT stock may depend less on the earnings beat and more on whether Walmart can deliver stronger holiday demand without sacrificing profitability.
