XRP on Track to Post Worst ETF Month Ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The XRP ETF market might be bracing to close a record-breaking month of outflows as demand for the XRP-based investment product has remained on hold all through the month.

This comes as the prolonged and intense market volatility has extended to the ETF ecosystem, causing institutional investors to withdraw their capital.

With November just about three weeks gone, the latest data from SoSoValue shows that XRP ETFs have already recorded a total of $30.76 million in net outflows this November.

Previous ETF data shows that March remains the only month in which spot XRP ETFs have recorded net outflows since their launch.

While it still stands as the record outflow month for XRP ETFs, investors withdrew about $31.16 million from the funds during that month.

This means that the persistent withdrawals seen this month have brought the November outflow dangerously close to its previous monthly record seen in March.

Apparently, the spot XRP ETFs are on track to close November as their worst-performing month since launch, with just about $1 million left to hit the record.

Further data also shows that the spot XRP ETFs have yet to record a single day of net inflows throughout November, as the persistent price decline has caused investors to slow down their purchases.

As such, the influx of fresh capital into the spot XRP ETF market has remained on hold amid growing market uncertainty and a shift in investor sentiment.
