NFL Week 12 grades for Thanksgiving: Bears earn 'B' for stunning win over Lions, Packers leave L.A. with 'D'

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

Welcome to the Turkey edition of the Week 12 grades!

Playing on Thanksgiving is one of the NFL's longest running traditions, but this year, it's not just about Thanksgiving. The league is trying to take over nearly the entire week.

The holiday slate of games kicked off on Wednesday night with the Rams beating the Packers 26-13 in the NFL's first-ever Thanksgiving Eve game. Hopefully you enjoyed the NFL's newest tradition, because it will be sticking around. The league is planning on making this game an annual event.

The game between the Rams and Packers served as the appetizer for Thursday's feast of Thanksgiving games. The Turkey Day action started in Detroit, where the Bears pulled off a dramatic 39-35 win in a game where Caleb Williams threw a touchdown pass to Rome Odunze with just three seconds left to help Chicago stun Detroit. The two teams combined for 1,007 yards of offense, which is the second-most ever in a Thanksgiving game.

The second game of the day is being played in Dallas, where the Cowboys are looking to keep their NFC East title hopes alive against a struggling Eagles team that has lost seven of their past eight games.

The Thanksgiving nightcap will be taking place in Buffalo, and it's definitely the main event of the day: It's Patrick Mahomes facing off against Josh Allen for the 11th time. Although Mahomes has a perfect 4-0 postseason record, Allen has dominated the regular-season series with a 5-1 record against the Chiefs.
