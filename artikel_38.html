Burnham, 'Brejoin' & the Ghosts of Brexit

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

“The people of Britain have spoken, and the answer is: we are out.” The words of David Dimbleby at 4.40am on 24 June 2016, as the Brexit referendum result became clear. But nearly a decade on, is Britain changing its mind? Prime Minister Andy Burnham has reopened talks with Brussels, exploring options for a closer relationship with the EU. But the move has drawn criticism from key figures behind Brexit, including Theresa May and Boris Johnson. So what is driving this rethink, and where might it lead? France 24's Gavin Lee speaks to John McTernan, veteran Labour strategist and former adviser to Tony Blair.
