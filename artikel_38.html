Denmark accuses Russia of plotting to assassinate US-Ukrainian rapper

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Denmark has accused Russian intelligence services of plotting to assassinate a Ukrainian-American near Copenhagen, saying they had "planned" and "initiated" the attempt.

Denmark's security and intelligence agency said it had stopped individuals who were intending to kill the person, named by Danish media as rapper Kyivstoner, at a concert in September. Two people were charged with attempted murder and assisting a foreign intelligence service, and pleaded not guilty.

Denmark's foreign minister said Russia's defence attachÃ© will now be expelled.

Russia's Embassy in Denmark called the allegations "unsubstantiated" and said Copenhagen failed to provide evidence of Moscow's involvement.

In a statement posted on social media, the embassy claimed a "Danish model" had emerged, which it described as a "Russian trace" allegedly being discovered and "exploited for a propaganda spectacle". It accused Denmark of "provocations" intending to "frighten the Danish population in order to justify... a prolonged confrontation with Russia".

Russian state media cites the foreign ministry in Moscow as saying it will respond to the expulsion of its official "accordingly".

Danish media named the target of the assassination attempt as the rapper known as Kyivstoner, whose real name is Albert Vasiliev.

Denmark's security and intelligence agency (PET) said the individuals who allegedly tried to assassinate him were associated with a banned Danish gang named Loyal to Familia. The group is "known for extensive, serious and violent criminal activity", according to the agency.

PET, which worked with police on the case, said three individuals were stopped while travelling by car near the music venue where Kyivstoner was set to perform on 25 September.

Firearms and knives were found in the vehicle, PET said.

Since then, two people linked to the case have been charged with attempted murder and assisting a foreign intelligence service.

Both individuals pleaded not guilty to the charges, local media reported.

In July, Russia's FSB security service accused Vasiliev and the Ukrainian authorities of organising a "terrorist attack" on military facilities and personnel in Russia. The rapper denied this allegation on social media.
