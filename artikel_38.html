War photographer Patrick Chauvel: 'People have lost faith in the press'

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

One of France's top war photographers, who has a 50-year CV of taking pictures in and around conflicts across the planet, has spoken to FRANCE 24 about his concerns that people have lost faith in the press. He notes that journalists are only looking for the truth. Patrick Chauvel has produced nearly 380,000 photographs, from Vietnam to Cambodia, from Afghanistan to Palestine, from Lebanon to Ukraine. He says he wants to take people by the hand to show them how the press works and why.
