Trump's accounts traded Nvidia shares 44 times this year, records show

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Donald Trump's investment portfolios have bought or sold shares in chipmaker Nvidia at least 44 times this year, a review of the US president's financial disclosures shows.

Trump's most recent public declarations of his investment trading show that between January and September the portfolios purchased between $5.2m and $17m (Â£4m and Â£13m) in Nvidia stock - and sold between $4.4m and $12m. His portfolios held between $12.5m and $58m of Nvidia shares at the end of 2025.

Financial disclosures to the Office of Government Ethics report a low and high range of transaction values, so a more precise analysis of how much Nvidia stock was traded is not possible.

The period of recent turmoil at Nvidia and volatility in its share price is not covered by the disclosures. In the last two weeks, co-founder and chief executive Jensen Huang resigned and then Trump played a significant part in his reinstatement.

The White House has said that Trump's stock and bond portfolio is independently managed by third parties and neither he nor his family "has any ability to direct, influence or provide input regarding how the portfolio is invested or when investments are bought or sold".

Huang resigned on 12 November after reports of extramarital affairs, including with one woman who has alleged ties to Chinese intelligence. Nvidia's share price dived following news of his resignation.

But on Monday Nvidia's board announced the 63-year-old was being reinstated as CEO.

Trump told US media he had "made the deal" to "convince" the tech giant's board to bring Huang back. The board said it had "received input from senior levels of the United States government" that he posed "no national security risk".

Nvidia's share price surged 31% on Monday following Huang's return. About $1tn (Â£763bn) was added to Nvidia's market value, but the company's shares are still trading below their value before reports of Huang's alleged affairs began to circulate.

However, concerns remain among both Republican and Democratic politicians about Huang's actions and Nvidia's compliance with US controls over the export of advanced computer chips to China, as well as Trump's involvement in getting him reappointed as CEO.

Nvidia's chip technology and investments have been critical to the growth of the artificial intelligence (AI) industry, leading to it becoming one of the world's most valuable companies.

The firm now represents more than 6% of the S&P 500 share index, according to State Street financial services. This makes it the second most valuable company on the index after Apple, whose shares Trump's accounts also frequently trade.

Nvidia also ranks highly in many global indices, meaning that investment accounts held around the world, including pensions, are likely to include its shares.

Our analysis of Trump's Nvidia trades does not include other investment products held by him which may include its shares.

Trump made at least $2.2bn (Â£1.7bn) from a variety of investments and commercial activities, according to the financial disclosure report for the first year of Trump's second term.

Barbara Perry, a presidential historian at the University of Virginia's Miller Center, told the BBC there is "no precedent for this," adding: "It's beyond anything we've ever seen in the presidency."
