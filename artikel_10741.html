Burger King reveals 2026 Advent calendar that lets fans build their own mini restaurant

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Burger King is bringing back its popular Advent calendar for the 2026 holiday season, this time allowing fans to build their own miniature version of the fast food chain’s restaurants.

The limited-edition collectible returns for its third consecutive year, featuring 12 days of surprises that gradually come together to create a miniature Burger King location.

The company announced the calendar on November 5, revealing that this year’s release will include miniature versions of popular menu items, restaurant fixtures, and holiday decorations.

Unlike traditional Advent calendars filled with chocolate or candy, Burger King’s 2026 version features 12 miniature collectibles that can be assembled into a complete restaurant.

Each door reveals a different piece of the restaurant, including a miniature Whopper in its updated box packaging, chicken nuggets, a soda fountain, and the King’s Booth.

The calendar also includes holiday decorations, such as a Christmas tree and reindeer, allowing fans to create a festive version of the restaurant.

On Day 12, the individual pieces come together to form a complete miniature Burger King restaurant that can be displayed on its own or added to a holiday village.

The final door also contains a miniature $5 Burger King digital coupon for Royal Perks members.

“We know Guests will be excited that for the third year in a row, the Advent Calendar is back, and it’s the best one ever,” said Joel Yashinsky, Chief Marketing Officer of Burger King US&C.

“So this year, fans can build their very own BK restaurant, complete with a mini-Whopper®, nuggets and other items that make BK feel like BK.”

Burger King’s 2026 Advent calendar will cost $19.54, referencing the year the fast food chain was founded.

The collectible will be sold exclusively through BK’s website, with a limited number available each day.

Beginning Monday, November 16, Burger King will release just 110 calendars daily at 12 PM ET through Friday, November 20, while supplies last. That means only 550 calendars are scheduled to be available across the five-day release period.

According to Burger King, its previous two Advent calendars sold out in under five minutes, making this year’s limited release another opportunity for collectors to secure one of the holiday items.

The 2026 calendar will only be available online, rather than at participating Burger King restaurants.
