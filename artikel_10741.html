Razer reveals new Soma gaming chairs that let you feel explosions and other in-game action

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Razer has unveiled two new gaming chairs that use built-in haptic motors to let players physically feel what’s happening in their games, including explosions, impacts, and approaching threats.

The Razer Soma Pro and Soma Sensa HD Haptics were announced on November 13, expanding the company’s lineup of immersive gaming chairs with technology that responds to on-screen action.

Both models build on the Soma Chroma, which introduced reactive RGB lighting to Razer’s gaming chair lineup. However, the new additions go beyond lighting by incorporating physical feedback directly into the seat, while the more advanced Soma Pro also features built-in surround sound.

Both the Soma Pro and Soma Sensa HD Haptics feature six built-in haptic motor actuators powered by Razer Sensa HD Haptics technology.

The motors generate vibrations that respond to the intensity, direction, and distance of supported in-game events. According to Razer, this allows players to distinguish between different sensations, such as an explosion occurring to their left and an impact happening nearby.

The technology also works with content that doesn’t natively support haptic feedback. Through Razer Synapse, audio can be converted into vibrations, allowing users to experience physical feedback while playing unsupported games, watching movies, or listening to music.

Players can adjust the intensity of the vibrations, switch between profiles, and manage wireless connectivity using a control panel built directly into the chair.

Both models support Razer HyperSpeed Wireless connectivity for PC and Bluetooth LE for compatible mobile devices.

The Soma Pro takes the experience further by incorporating speakers with THX Spatial Audio, allowing users to hear game audio directly from their chair. The speakers can operate independently or alongside front speakers to create a wider surround sound setup.

It also features Razer Chroma RGB lighting that responds to in-game events across more than 300 integrated games. The lighting can synchronize with other compatible Razer devices through Synapse.

While the Soma Sensa HD Haptics focuses on physical feedback, the Soma Pro combines haptics, lighting, and audio into a single chair.

Both models share several ergonomic features, including a built-in lumbar arch, dual-density cold-cured foam cushioning, and a backrest that reclines up to 132 degrees.

The Soma Pro adds 4D adjustable armrests, a magnetic head cushion, and a tilt mechanism for additional customization.

Razer has also designed both chairs to operate without a permanent power cable running to a wall outlet. Users can power them through a compatible USB-C power bank or connect them directly to a wall outlet for continuous use.

The two chairs join the existing Soma Chroma in Razer’s growing lineup of immersive gaming furniture, giving players the option to add physical feedback to their gaming setups without purchasing the company’s more advanced model.
