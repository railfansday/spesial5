All Treasure Hunt Gold cars in Fortnite and how to get them for free

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Treasure Hunters mini-season has several Gold vehicles you can win in Fortnite, but finding them won’t be easy. They’re part of many goodies you can unlock throughout the course of the season.

Let’s find out all the available models, and more importantly, how you can get them all.

Gold cars are special versions of the ordinary vehicle skins that you can find in the game. They have a bright yellow glimmer, which also makes them pretty rare to find. The only way to find them during the Treasure Hunters season, as of writing, is to open chests.

Gold cars are one of the various rare items that have been added to the regular treasure chest. They are the second-rarest tier of rewards, so the only item that’s harder to find in comparison is the Pirate Shop access token.

Finding one will completely depend on your luck. If the odds are in your favor, you might open one chest and find a gold car. Realistically, however, you will need to open several at minimum to add one to your collection.

You can always increase the luck in your favor by visiting the vaults. Guarantees you four rare chase stoppers, which have enhanced rates for rarer rewards. Similarly, you can also use certain spirit abilities to raise the odds in your favor.

Ultimately, keep opening the chest until you turn out to be lucky. If you are playing in a group, only one of you needs to find the car.

There will be four different models available for you to collect. So far, we are only in Week 1, but there are three more weeks to follow after this one.
