Developing a Pilates mat routine that’s consistent is one of my major fitness goals for 2027, so I’ve been testing a lot of at-home pilates equipment lately. Mat Pilates appeals to me because it’s more-than-meets-the-eye challenging while at the same time being accessible.

And, if you’re doing your workouts in your living room like me, they’re also very convenient, not to mention extremely effective for building core strength. I’ll also admit that the fitness nerd in me appreciates that Joseph Pilates himself considered the mat exercises that bear his name to be the foundation of Pilates.

All week long, I’ve been hunting for the best Black Friday deals for Pilates equipment, and I found savings on gear I’ve been personally testing, as well as items that are on my at-home workout equipment wish list.
