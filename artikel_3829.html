This month's Replay theme is all about pleasing the big man: 'Captain Price approves' for Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4, in partnership with Hamilton, and its special edition Khaki Field Modern Warfare 4 watch. Whether it's DMZ, multiplayer, Warzone, or the campaign, if you can submit a clip that will earn Price's approval then you might be in with a chance of winning a £/$250 Amazon voucher!

Just to be clear, you can submit clips of any game - there are two prizes on offer: one for absolutely anything cool or interesting (in any game!) and a second Modern Warfare 4 specific prize. So dust off those loadouts, check all the corners, and see what you can do.

GamesRadar+ Replay is a community video series made by all of you: submit and share your amazing, funny, or wtf clips here and the world will see, bringing you fame, bragging rights, and the chance to win one of two $/£250 Amazon vouchers.
