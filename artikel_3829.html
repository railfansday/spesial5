ARIKA director Akira Kurabayashi dies

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Akira Kurabayashi, a company director and head of the software development division at ARIKA, passed away suddenly on November 14, 2026, due to heart failure, ARIKA president Akira Nishitani announced.

Kurabayashi previously worked as a game programmer at TAITO, where he served as main programmer on titles such as Darius Gaiden, Fighters’ Impact, and Vertexer. After joining ARIKA, he served as both a company director and the head of the software development division, contributing to numerous titles through major industry transitions like 2D to 3D, arcade to home consoles, and the introduction of online multiplayer.

A memorial service is being planned in accordance with his family’s wishes.
