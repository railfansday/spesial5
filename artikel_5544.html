Deloitte and SIFMA Release Whitepaper on Digital Cash and the Future of US Securities Settlement

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

NEW YORK, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Deloitte and SIFMA today released "Digital Cash and the future of U.S. Capital Markets: An Industry Perspective on Opportunities, Challenges, and the Path Ahead," a whitepaper that explores how US securities markets can adopt and integrate digital cash across the end-to-end settlement lifecycle.

The paper draws on a structured series of industry discussions conducted by SIFMA with participating institutions spanning banks and broker-dealers, asset managers, custodians, stablecoin issuers, digital asset infrastructure providers, and financial market infrastructures. It considers the major forms of digital cash, including tokenized deposits, payment stablecoins, and central bank money, and how each could support settlement, collateral mobility, repo and securities lending, and cross-border funds movement.

The paper also sets out an adoption path, pairing a readiness checklist and near-term steps firms can take under existing regulatory frameworks with the longer-term infrastructure the industry will need to build together.

"Digital cash has the potential to drive innovation across the securities lifecycle, including making payments and settlement faster, more precise, and more resilient, but realizing that potential depends on interoperability, legal clarity, and industry-wide coordination," said Charles DeSimone, SIFMA managing director, technology and operations. "This paper reflects highlights the value of digital cash, offers a practical starting point for firms considering how to engage, and provides a roadmap for the industry broadly as we build out this transformative new form of money."

"There is an opportunity to put digital cash to work where it can create measurable value, improving how firms manage liquidity, move collateral, and settle transactions. The best place to start is with specific uses where it can make a difference and a clear plan for expanding those uses over time," said Roy Ben-Hur, managing director and Digital Assets Financial Services leader, Deloitte.

The full whitepaper is available at Deloitte.com and at SIFMA.org.
