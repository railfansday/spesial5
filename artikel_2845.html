New York Giants vs. Washington Commanders Week 10 ‘TNF’ game chat

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Yuck. The New York Giants might be 5-2-1, but they're leading what many see as among the worst divisions in the NFL. Tonight, they take on the 2-5-1 Washington Commanders — that's right, a team they played to a tie earlier this season. Will there be similar "fireworks" for Thursday Night Football?

It would be great if this game was exciting — and even better if it wasn't in primetime. It's safe to say that football fans have had their fill of these teams, of the NFC East, and the NFC in general. At least Week 10 gives us two AFC showdowns. The trouble is that the one on Monday night is about as bad as it gets.

There aren't any rooting interests here as it relates to the Buffalo Bills. You might find yourself cheering for the Commanders just to hand the Giants a loss, or at least see them make good on their horrible decisions that led to the tie in the previous matchup. Depending on how far back your Bills fandom goes, you may find it impossible rooting for an NFC East team (except when they face the New England Patriots, and probably the Kansas City Chiefs).

There's a lot of bad football being trotted out to the masses this season. but this is what you get tonight and you're probably watching until the end. With that, here's your open thread for Week 10 of Thursday Night Football. As always, be kind to one another — and in all things… Go Bills!
