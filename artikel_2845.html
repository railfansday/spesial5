LIVE DISCUSSION: Brooklyn Nets vs. Milwaukee Bucks, 8 PM ET

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It would be an abomination if the Brooklyn Nets lose this game.

The Milwaukee Bucks finally got a win on Monday, snapping a 13-game losing streak against the Toronto Raptors. The Nets are still waiting for that feeling. They've lost five straight, including a 142-116 blowout against Minnesota, and get a crack at a 2-16 Milwaukee team that's somehow been even worse.

Mikel Brown Jr. is also getting a bigger opportunity with Nolan Traore sidelined, giving Nets fans something to watch for.

Who: Brooklyn Nets (3-13) vs. Milwaukee Bucks (2-16)

"By the numbers, these are the two worst defensive teams in the NBA. Brooklyn allows 120.3 points per 100 possessions, worst in the NBA. Milwaukee isn't that far behind at 119.8 points per 100 possessions — the second-worst in the NBA. These two teams allow easy buckets and bad defense makes games a lot harder to sit through if you're rooting for the team with the bad defense. Look for this one to be a high scoring affair and whoever can put together enough stops on defense should walk away with the win."
