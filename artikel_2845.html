Cardinals vs. Chiefs Week 11 TV Map

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Arizona Cardinals are not having a good season under new head coach Mike LaFleur. At 3-7-0, the team is most likely going to limp to the finish line. The Kansas City Chiefs are one of the best AFC clubs this year with a sterling 7-2-0 record and no signs of slowing down. The Cardinals are just a speed bump headed towards their Thanksgiving Day game against the Buffalo Bills, another AFC juggernaut.

The good news for Arizona is that the majority of players listed on the injury list are returning this weekend.

The Week 11 home game for the Cardinals against the Chiefs will air on CBS at 1:00 PM ET, 3:00 PM MT on Sunday. Also, NFL+

Announcers: Ian Eagle, Ross Tucker, and Evan Washburn (sideline)

Arizona Cardinals Radio: Arizona Sports 98.7 FM (English flagship for the Cardinals)
