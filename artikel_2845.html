Desmond Bane ruled out as Magic down another starter at Warriors

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Already without starters Paolo Banchero (right high ankle sprain) and Jalen Suggs (right knee bone bruise) entering Saturday's game at Golden State, the Magic received more negative news regarding player availability.

Roughly six hours ahead of tip-off against the Warriors inside Chase Center, the Magic added Desmond Bane to the team's injury report.

Bane, who appeared in and started all 82 regular-season games in his first season with Orlando last year, was ruled out of Saturday's contest because of an illness.

It marks the first regular-season game Bane will miss since April 13, 2025, when he was still with the Memphis Grizzlies. Last season with Orlando, Bane was one of only four players in the NBA to start all 82 regular-season games. He was the first Magic player to do that since Dwight Howard in the 2009-10 season.

Through the first eight games this season, his seventh year as a pro, Bane averaged 17.5 points, 3.9 rebounds and 2.3 assists during 34.5 minutes per night while shooting 36.8% from 3-point point range. Between last season and the start of this season, Bane had played in 90 consecutive regular-season games.

Entering Saturday's slate of games, Bane was tied for 17th league-wide in minutes played per contest, according to NBA.com. His teammate Franz Wagner, who averaged 36.1 minutes per night ahead of Saturday, was fifth in the NBA.

In addition to Bane, Suggs and Banchero, the Magic were also missing forwards Noah Penda (partially torn right hamstring), Jonathan Isaac (left knee injury management) and two-way rookie Izaiyah Nelson (left ankle injury recovery) at Golden State.

Those absences left Magic coach Sean Sweeney with only nine standard contract players available and a pair of two-way players as well against the Warriors, who entered Saturday on a six-game winning streak.

Orlando still has a standard contract roster spot open but Magic president of basketball operations Jeff Weltman said at the team's media day Sept. 28 that there were no immediate plans to fill it — largely due to the team's lack of financial flexibility.

The Magic continue their four-game road trip Tuesday night at Sacramento in the first contest of a back-to-back. Orlando travels to Utah the following night before returning to Kia Center to host the Bucks in NBA Cup action Friday.
