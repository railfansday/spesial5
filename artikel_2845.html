Jaylen Brown ruled OUT vs. Magic with hamstring strain

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

An impressive performance for Jaylen Brown on Monday night ended in a late exit from the contest due to hamstring tightness, Nick Nurse confirmed to reporters postgame after the Sixers' win over the Nets.

Now, Brown has been ruled out for Philadelphia's Wednesday game against the Orlando Magic, listed officially with a left hamstring strain.

Monday night had been a great one for Brown up to his exit — minus bonking heads with Julius Randle early in the fourth and ending up with a forehead cut, from which he returned to the contest. Brown tied his season-high with 31 points and grabbed a season-high 13 rebounds in the Sixers' victory. There was no clear moment on the broadcast of the game that showed when Brown may have suffered the injury.

Brown has been extremely durable across his career. Excluding the two NBA seasons shortened by COVID, 63 games played is the fewest total for him since he entered the league. He's gotten this season off to a great start with his new squad, averaging 22.6 points shooting 38.6% from long range with 6.6 rebounds, 3.6 assists and 1.3 steals per contest.

The best case scenario here would be that the Sixers are being extra cautious with Brown, especially so early in the season and against a weaker opponent like the Magic. After Wednesday night's game, the Sixers will get few full days off before returning to action on Saturday afternoon hosting the Milwaukee Bucks, so Brown will get at least three days off here. Hopefully the injury is minor and he is able to play again soon.
