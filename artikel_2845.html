Mikki Allen fired as Tennessee State athletic director

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Mikki Allen has been fired as Tennessee State's athletic director, the school announced on Nov. 13. Allen was placed on administrative leave on July 23 pending an investigation into a vote of no confidence in Allen signed by six of the school's head coaches and sent to TSU President Dwayne Tucker on July 21.

The school notified Allen that he would not be returning to as the school's AD.

Jennifer Bell will continue to serve as interim athletics director, according to the school.
