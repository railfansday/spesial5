Jake Ferguson, Tyler Booker remain out of practice for Cowboys

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Cowboys upgraded the practice participation of a couple of players on Tuesday's injury report, but two offensive starters remained out.

Right guard Tyler Booker (ankle) and tight end Jake Ferguson (knee, ankle) were listed as non-participants for the second straight day. Ferguson has missed the last two games and Booker was out against the Titans last Sunday, so it seems unlikely either will play against the Eagles on Thanksgiving.

Linebackers Jaishawn Barham (hip, groin) and Dee Winters (knee) both returned as limited participants. Barham did not play against the Titans, but Winters had two tackles.

Wide receiver Jonathan Mingo (ankle) was downgraded to out and safety P.J. Locke (concussion) was limited for the second straight day. Cornerback Cobie Durant (neck), linebacker Donovan Ezeiruaku (ankle), cornerback Devin Moore (hip), defensive tackle Otito Ogbonnia (knee), cornerback Joey Porter Jr. (foot), quarterback Dak Prescott (left hand), and safety Jalen Thompson (hamstring) remained full participants.
