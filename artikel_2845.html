Broncos cut cornerback to make room for new linebacker Daiyan Henley

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

After agreeing to terms on a trade to acquire linebacker Daiyan Henley from the Los Angeles Chargers on Tuesday, the Denver Broncos waived cornerback Sean Fresch to open up a spot on the 53-man roster. The Henley trade will become official after he passes a physical, and he will then take Fresch's former spot on the active roster.

Additionally, Denver cut defensive back Ugo Amadi from the practice squad, according to the NFL's transaction wire. That move was presumably made to create room for Fresch to return on the practice squad if he clears waivers.

Fresch, a former UFL star, has appeared in four games for the Broncos this season, totaling three tackles on 57 special teams snaps. He also returned six kickoffs for 153 yards, averaging 25.5 yards per return.

Denver's 53-man roster is now full, and they have one open spot on the 17-player practice squad that will likely be held for Fresch's potential return.
