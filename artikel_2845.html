Jaguars trade for former Pro Bowl S Jessie Bates III from Falcons

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Jacksonville Jaguars are loading up their defense for the stretch run.

The team on Monday agreed to acquire safety Jessie Bates III from the Atlanta Falcons in exchange for a 2027 second-round pick and 2028 fifth-round pick, according to multiple reports.

The move comes one day ahead of the Nov. 10 trade deadline.

Bates, 29, is a former Pro Bowl selection who is in the final year of a four-year, $64 million contract he signed in 2023.

He confirmed the move in a post on social media expressing his excitement for joining a top contender.

At 6-2, the Jaguars have won five of their last six games and lead the AFC South by three games. Bates, who has developed a reputation as one of the league's premier ballhawks and ranks third among active players with 28 career interceptions, could further boost a defense that currently stands third in takeaways with 15.

The Falcons, meanwhile, are 3-6 but have dropped their last two games.
