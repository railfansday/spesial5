Steelers waive veteran WR

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Pittsburgh has created another open spot on its 53-man roster, with the team announcing it has waived wide receiver Brandon Smith on Thursday.

Smith has appeared in five games for the Steelers this season, recording four catches for 39 yards. His release seems to indicate that at least one of Roman Wilson or Michael Pittman Jr. should return to the Steelers' active roster from injured reserve ahead of a Week 11 matchup against the Philadelphia Eagles. Both receivers were full participants in the Steelers' Thursday practice amid their 21-day return windows from IR.

After the Steelers placed starting left guard Mason McCormick on injured reserve on Wednesday, Pittsburgh currently has two open spots on the 53-man roster.

Smith opened the year on the Steelers' practice squad. He was elevated to Pittsburgh's active roster the maximum of three times in 2026 before being officially signed to the roster ahead of the Steelers' Week 8 clash with the Cleveland Browns. Smith has appeared in five of his eight career NFL games with the Steelers.
