Arch Manning gives strong hint on 2027 NFL Draft decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Texas is playing Arkansas in the school's final home game of the 2026 college football season on Saturday.

Texas is 8-2 and is trying to improve to 9-2 overall, while keeping its spot in the College Football Playoff. The program has won four straight after last Saturday's last-second 22-21 win over LSU.

Before this Saturday's game kicked off, Texas quarterback Arch Manning posed for a picture with his parents for Senior Day. While nothing is official, it could signal that Manning will declare for the 2027 NFL Draft after the season ends.

Manning's 2026 campaign has been up and down, with 2,198 yards, 19 touchdowns, and 10 interceptions. He's made some brutal decisions but has also been clutch at times.

Manning can still return to college for one more year because he redshirted his freshman season in 2023. He only played in three games that year. He could go back to Texas or transfer to another school for one season.

Everyone will wait for his final decision, but it definitely looks like he may be leaning toward the NFL.
