‘We don’t forget’: Steph Curry says he remembers what everyone was saying about the Warriors

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Golden State Warriors have turned their season around in magnificent fashion.

Steph Curry has once again been their driving force, and on Saturday night, he scored more than 40 points again to lead his side to a win against the Orlando Magic.

After the game, the 38-year-old had a few things to say.

The Golden State Warriors got off to a terrible start to the season.

Steve Kerr's side lost each of their first three games – against the Lakers, the Grizzlies and the Suns – and that led to a lot of criticism.

Some people went as far as ruling the Warriors out of the playoffs after just three games, and a few others urged Curry to leave them for another franchise.

Since then, the Warriors have won every single one of their seven games and are now 7-3 for the season. They are on an incredible run and look like they could beat anyone.

People now view Golden State as contenders and are heaping praise on them, but Curry says he and his teammates haven't forgotten about what was said.

"I just love the resilience, attitude and commitment from everybody. When you set foot on the floor, just play hard, and good things will happen," Curry said on NBCS.

"We don't forget where people thought we were at the beginning of the year. So, it's just a matter of continuing to build."

Even in his post-game press conference, Curry addressed everything that was said about his team and admitted that it's difficult not to pay attention to them.

He said: "It's hard not to hear it, but you don't ever play into it. I know I've seen myself with the towel over my head, staring into the distance, trying to figure out what we need to do.

"People like put an existential crisis on those looks. We hate losing. You want to respond to all of that. You hear it, but we've been around long enough to know we've got to be able to block it out and be who you are."

The Warriors deserve enormous praise for the way they have turned their season around. The heads could've easily dropped after going 0-3, but to win seven games in a row from that point shows elite mentality.

That is exactly what's needed to go all the way this season.
