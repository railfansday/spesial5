Depth carries New Canaan girls swimming to first Class L title since 2010

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

WEST HARTFORD - New Canaan girls swim coach Kat Munson had a big smile on her face when Grace Wang returned to the team's sitting area during Wednesday night's Class L championship meet at the Cornerstone Aquatics Center.

"That was such an amazing race," Munson told Wang after the freshman won the 500-yard freestyle in 5:08.84, knocking more than nine seconds off her preliminary time.

It was those kinds of efforts that led to the biggest smiles of all. Those smiles belonged to the members of the New Canaan program, and with good reason.

For the first time since 2010, New Canaan is a state champion. The Rams scored 698 points to outdistance Darien (615) and Cheshire (599). Wilton was fourth with 538 points.

"I think the thing that is most special about this team is we have one all-state swimmer individually, one all-state relay and one all-state diver, and we were still state champs," Munson said after a well-deserved jump into the pool with her team to celebrate. "What we talked about all season is it's all about everyone contributing. This is going to be a full team effort, a full team win, and our goal was to get as many back as possible, and we did that."

Besides Wang, the other all-state efforts came from second-place senior diver Julie Frangione (432.50) and the second-place 200 medley relay team (1:50.62), made up of Wang, senior Kaitlyn Knowles, freshman Caroline Santaniello and freshman Allison Santaniello.

For New Canaan, it was really about adding up points with its depth.

"It's a numbers game. That's really what states is," Munson said. "It's about getting those bodies back, obviously in the best positions possible, and we knew that we were going to have to basically win the meet on Saturday. We were going to have to put ourselves in position to do what we did tonight, and we did that."

New Canaan scored the most points of any team in four individual events Wednesday: 200 freestyle (73), 100 butterfly (74), 500 freestyle (78), and 100 breaststroke (80).

The Rams trailed Darien 391-373 after the 100 freestyle, but things turned around beginning with the 500 freestyle. They outscored the Blue Wave 78-48 in that event to claim a 451-439 lead. Darien clawed back to within eight points following the 200 freestyle relay, with New Canaan leading 499-491. New Canaan did well in the 100 backstroke (67 team points), then opened it up wide in the 100 breaststroke.

New Canaan's swimmers could sense the momentum shift, and they were getting excited.

"I feel like by the end, we were all super excited because we knew we had the chance of winning," Knowles said. "I feel like it's just unlike anything we've ever felt as a team, because it's been since 2010 that we've won this title. We were just so excited. It was insane."

New Canaan's lead over Darien grew from just eight points to 85, 646-561 after the breaststroke.

"I am so incredibly proud of this team. We've worked so hard this year, and just seeing everyone drop time in these past events, it's so exciting because I'm just so proud of every single one of these girls," New Canaan senior Cora Huang said.

The championship is New Canaan's 14th class-meet title. New Canaan was the runner-up to Ridgefield in Class L in each of the past two seasons, and the Rams were also the runner-up in 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014 and 2015 to Darien.

"It's my 20th season, so this is just the best way to celebrate 20 years of being part of this program," Munson said. "I'm just really so proud of them at the end of the day. They did it, they showed up, swam their hearts out."

Wilton had the meet's lone double-winners, seniors Megan Lussier and Vedha Kongettira. Lussier took gold in the 200 freestyle (1:53.07) and 100 freestyle (51.76), and Kongettira captured gold in the 50 freestyle (23.67) and 100 backstroke (55.87). They were also part of both winning relays for the Warriors (200 medley, 1:46.40; 200 freestyle, 1:36.90), along with junior Maryn Brooks and senior Maya Kreuzer.

Cheshire senior Annabelle Superchi won the 200 IM (2:06.25) and was part of the winning 400 freestyle relay team (3:33.64), along with junior Corinne Gleason, senior Gabrielle Williamson and junior Mackenzie Hearrin.

Darien's individual winners were senior Ilse Fedoronko (100 butterfly, 56.38) and sophomore Victoria Brupbacher (diving, 493.90 points). Shelton senior Ava Piscioniere won the 100 breaststroke (1:04.48) over Hearrin after finishing second to her in the same race at last year's meet.
