Gators Stay Perfect After Strong Performance in Players Era Opener

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Florida Gators continued their impressive start to the 2026-27 college basketball season Tuesday, defeating Notre Dame 86-78 in the opening round of the Players Era 8 tournament in Las Vegas. The No. 1 ranked Gators entered the matchup undefeated and faced one of their toughest tests of the young season against a Fighting Irish team that also had not lost.

Florida improved to 5-0 with the victory, while Notre Dame suffered its first defeat and dropped to 4-1. The Gators showed why they are considered one of the nation's top teams, using a strong first half and timely plays down the stretch to secure the win.

Florida established control during the opening half, putting together an impressive offensive performance. The Gators attacked the basket, moved the ball effectively and found ways to create opportunities against Notre Dame's defense.

Their efforts resulted in a 47-32 halftime advantage. Florida's ability to build a 15-point lead gave the team valuable breathing room heading into the second half.

Alex Condon and Thomas Haugh helped establish the offensive rhythm, while the Gators received important contributions from their supporting cast. Urban Klavzar also provided a major scoring boost, giving Florida another offensive weapon that Notre Dame struggled to contain.

The Fighting Irish refused to go away quietly. After struggling to keep pace during the first half, Notre Dame responded with a much better offensive performance after halftime.

The Irish scored 46 points in the second half, cutting into Florida's advantage and making the closing minutes more competitive. Their improved shooting helped create pressure, forcing the Gators to continue executing instead of simply protecting their lead.

However, Florida had enough answers to prevent the comeback. Whenever Notre Dame threatened to make the game even closer, the Gators found important baskets and defensive stops to maintain control.

Florida's balanced offensive attack proved to be one of the biggest differences. Alex Condon finished with 19 points, 10 rebounds and five assists, delivering an excellent all-around performance. Haugh added 14 points and nine rebounds, continuing to show why he is such an important piece of Florida's frontcourt.

Klavzar led the Gators with 22 points, providing a major scoring contribution off the bench. Denzel Aberdeen also reached double figures with 13 points.

Notre Dame showed plenty of fight, particularly after halftime, but the early deficit ultimately proved too difficult to overcome.

The victory gives Florida another important win as it continues its pursuit of the Players Era 8 championship. With the tournament featuring several strong programs, the Gators will need to maintain their level of play throughout the week.

Florida's ability to win despite Notre Dame's second half push should give the team confidence moving forward. The Gators are now 5-0 and have another opportunity to demonstrate why they entered the season among the favorites to compete for a national championship.
