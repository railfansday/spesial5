Kenneth Walker III reportedly won't play vs. Bills on Thanksgiving due to groin issue

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Kansas City Chiefs are looking at another game without star running back Kenneth Walker III. Despite returning to practice Tuesday, Walker will reportedly not play against the Buffalo Bills on Thanksgiving, according to multiple reports.

It will mark the third straight game Walker will miss due to the issue.

Prior to the injury, Walker was well on his way toward posting his finest season in the NFL. Through eight games, the running back already rushed for 872 yards and seven touchdowns. He also hauled in 26 catches for 172 yards and four receiving scores.
