The Rams should dominate Packers’ OL

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

While the Los Angeles Rams' offense is set to be short-handed for the remainder of the season, for at least this week, the defense can lead the way against a weak Green Bay Packers offensive line.

The Rams must win against the Packers. Wednesday night carries significant weight within the NFC playoff picture. Not taking care of business would be a massive blow in a grueling second-half schedule.

Fortunately, Kobie Turner is set to return for the first time since Week 6. He'll join Myles Garrett, Aaron Donald, Byron Young, and Braden Fiske on an aggressive defensive front.

No matter how you split it, Green Bay is inept at protecting its quarterback.

And Jordan Love's play has suffered as a result. He's been an inconsistent quarterback in 2026, mainly because the Packers are inconsistent in their ability to protect him.

Through Week 11, there are five games in which Love has been pressured 13 times or fewer and five to the contrary. The effect on his performance is obvious:

It's no surprise that quarterbacks play worse when under pressure. Still, this is a stark juxtaposition that one takeaway is clear: Love is a great quarterback when he is kept clean. The Rams have a duty to disrupt him. The Packers must try to protect him against a capable front.

It's an oversimplification to say that the defensive line can win the game Wednesday night for the Rams. However, if Love isn't his normal self and Los Angeles walks out with a win, it's likely the pass rush had a major role to play in the victory.

The Rams should dominate the Packers' offensive line on Wednesday.
