Patriots vs. Lions live updates, inactives, where to watch: Drake Maye, Jared Goff square off in Germany to kick off Sunday slate in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The New England Patriots are getting everyone back in Germany. After a week of speculation, both wideout A.J. Brown and running back Rhamondre Stevenson are active for the contest.

The Patriots will need them. On the other side, the Detroit Lions will also be fully operational, with star receiver Amon-Ra St. Brown also active for Detroit. St. Brown has quite a bit to play for during the contest, as he is German.

Having all three players active should result in plenty of points being scored early in Week 10 on Sunday.

The Patriots and Lions will kick off the Sunday slate in Week 10 from Munich, Germany. Kickoff is set for 9:30 a.m. ET from Allianz Arena.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for live updates, highlights and more as the Patriots and Lions face off in Munich to kick off the Sunday slate, airing on NFL Network.

Detroit Lions star Amon-Ra St. Brown won't let a rib issue hold him back in Germany. The Lions put out their inactives Sunday and St. Brown wasn't on them, meaning he will play in Week 10.

St. Brown should have a lot at stake, as he's German. With the game in Munich, he'll have an opportunity to show out in front of German fans.

St. Brown's mother was born in Germany. Because of that, he speaks fluent German and even has dual citizenship in the United States and Germany.

Patriots wide receiver A.J. Brown (ankle/knee) will return this week after a multi-week absence when the New England Patriots face the Detroit Lions in Munich. Brown traveled with the team to Germany and, along with running back Rhamondre Stevenson (knee), has looked "pretty good" in practice this week, according to head coach Mike Vrabel.

Turns out, both players are active for the contest.

Brown has been limited to three games this season and has been out since Week 7. He was listed as questionable for Sunday, along with running back Rhamondre Stevenson.

Meanwhile, running back TreVeyon Henderson remains out after issuing a statement last week that he's been experiencing unexplained neurological symptoms. It's unclear if there's a timetable for his return.
