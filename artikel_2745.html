NC State a 9.5-point underdog as it preps for FSU

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

It's the middle of November but the spirit of Halloween remains as the headless corpses of the NC State and Florida State football programs meet in Tallahassee on Saturday. FSU finally gave Mike Norvell the axe a couple of weeks ago, and all that cost was $50 million in buyout money.

The Noles are actually 2-0 since then, with one-score road victories over BC and Pitt to bring them back level at 5-5 and in pretty good position to get bowl eligible, what with their being a two-score favorite this week. They'll probably want to win this one to take some pressure off of the Florida game the week following.

NC State, for its part, must now win out after coughing up another fourth quarter lead, and I can't say I'm feeling super optimistic about that happening. I think there's a pretty strong chance that the Pack doesn't put forth much of an effort against the Seminoles.

It might be more helpful if NC State didn't go to a bowl game this year, anyway.
