Sean Mannion to continue calling offensive plays for Eagles, Joe Milton to remain at QB

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Eagles head coach Nick Sirianni said that the team would talk about changes to roles and responsibilities on the coaching staff during their bye week, but he said on Wednesday that they decided against any major ones.

That means offensive coordinator Sean Mannion will continue calling the team’s offensive plays when they face the Steelers this week. Sirianni explained why he’s staying the course at a Wednesday press conference.

“I think he’s really smart. I think he has a great football mind,” Sirianni said, via a transcript from the team. “He thinks through everything. I think one thing I always value about him is [that] anybody can come and give a play and say, ‘Hey, I want to do this, or I want to do that.’ I think he just has a really good monotonous approach to figuring out everything that could potentially happen in that situation, what the downfalls are, what the upside is and whether we move forward with some of those things. I think that just speaks to the detail that he has. It hasn’t gone well enough for any of us this year offensively. That’s coaches, that’s players. We all have things that we need to do better, but I do love that approach that he has with that. As we looked at some of our schemes that he’s brought in, some of those have been really, really good. It has to be better as a whole without getting too much into the specifics of what those are. We just need to build on that.”

Mannion will be sending the play calls in to quarterback Joe Milton for the second straight game. He was 18-of-34 for 169 yards while running nine times for 64 yards and losing a fumble in a Week 9 loss to the Giants.

“He works extremely hard with everything,” Sirianni said. “Really love his approach, like his personality, but I really appreciate his approach to how he goes through everything.”

The loss to the Giants was the seventh in a row for the Eagles. They’ll try to avoid their first eight-game slide since 2012 this week.
