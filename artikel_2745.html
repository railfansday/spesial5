Arch Manning Commits To Senior Day While Draft Future Lingers

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Arch Manning is taking his senior year experience to the fullest, but he refuses to lock in his NFL future just yet. The Texas quarterback confirmed Monday that he will participate in Senior Day festivities before the team's final home game against Arkansas on Saturday.

The pressure to leave early is real, but Manning insists his mind is on the present. Texas sits at 8-2 and is on a four-game win streak after a shaky start. The team has already beaten ranked opponents like Ole Miss and LSU, proving they can hang with the best. Manning has thrown 19 touchdowns and 10 interceptions while completing 64.7 percent of his passes. He told reporters that his career has "been great" as the team looks to march to a championship. "I feel like I've grown a lot as a person and a player," Manning said.

Participating in the pregame ceremony does not mean Manning is done with college football. He could easily return to Austin next season if he decides against the 2027 NFL Draft. The Longhorns might even host a College Football Playoff game, which would give him one last shot at glory on the biggest stage. Manning insists he is not looking past the upcoming matchup. "I've enjoyed my time, but I'm just focused on this week, winning this week. I'm not really thinking about the future right now," he said, according to the New York Post.

His grandfather Archie Manning and uncles Peyton and Eli all became NFL legends, adding weight to every decision he makes. Texas fans want to know if he will stay or go, but the quarterback is keeping his cards close. The team's recent success suggests they might not need him to leave early to compete. Manning's 20-5 record as a starter shows he has already made an impact.

The next game is Saturday night in Austin. The No. 6 ranked Longhorns will face Arkansas in what could be their final home game of the regular season.
