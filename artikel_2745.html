Los Angeles Chargers news: Bolts add back CB Avery Smith

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Chargers got back a familiar face on Wednesday, claiming cornerback Avery Smith off waivers from the Seahawks. Smith was traded by the Chargers to Seattle prior to the start of the regular season in return for a 2027 sixth-round pick.

Serendipitously, the Chargers and Smith have found their way back to each other while that extra draft pick remains in the hands of LA. Smith now adds to a cornerback room which has dealt with a number of consistent injuries this year, most recently with Deane Leonard being placed on injured/reserve this week.

After being a preseason standout for the Bolts, Smith gets another chance with the team that originally signed him in April, including a new opportunity to prove he can be a part of this team in the future, as well.
