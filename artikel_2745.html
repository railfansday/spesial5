Steelers defense scores two touchdowns in dominant win over Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

After a tumultuous week of horrible PR featuring Aaron Rodgers being released from the team, the Pittsburgh Steelers bounced back after two consecutive losses in the biggest way possible, scoring two defensive touchdowns en route to a 27-5 win over the Denver Broncos.

The Steelers' defense set the tone early on. After the offense punted following an eight-play drive, Bo Nix dropped the ball after a pump fake, which rolled into the end zone and was picked up by T.J. Watt for a touchdown to put the Steelers up 7-0. Denver then went three-and-out, and a Mason Rudolph hit Germie Bernard on a short crosser that the rookie took for a 58-yard catch-and-run. Three plays later, Rudolph connected with DK Metcalf for a touchdown on a one-on-one fade route over Pat Surtain. The 14-0 lead also marked the first time since 2019 that Pittsburgh scored 14 points in the first quarter.

Pittsburgh's defense wasn't done scoring, though. After a safety and a field goal made the game 14-5, Chris Boswell kicked a field goal with less than two minutes to play in the half, extending the lead to 17-5. On the ensuing drive, Nix threw a pass that looked as if it was intended for Jalen Ramsey, who took the interception 36 yards to the end zone to give the Steelers a 24-5 lead at the half. A Chris Boswell field goal in the fourth quarter would be the final points of the game, as Pittsburgh wins 27-5 and climbs to the No. 6 seed in the AFC.

The Steelers sacked Bo Nix three times and held him to just 169 yards passing on an astoundingly low 4.2 yards per attempt. Denver couldn't run the ball, either, totaling just 73 yards on 26 carries. Overall, Denver had just 205 yards on three yards per play.

Mason Rudolph didn't throw the ball a ton, but he made his throws count. He finished the day 9-of-15 for 142 yards and a touchdown. He had completions of 58, 37, and 22 yards, pushing the ball downfield and proving to be a steady hand to complement the run game.

The Steelers ran the ball often, totaling 117 yards on 31 carries. It wasn't an explosive run game, but it was effective to help Pittsburgh get a much-needed win.

Pittsburgh now prepares for a massive Week 13 Sunday night game against the Houston Texans, who are fighting for their playoff lives at 4-6.
