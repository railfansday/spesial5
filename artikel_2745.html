Do the Titans need to sign another big-money CB this offseason?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Tennessee Titans entered this past offseason with no starting cornerbacks on the roster. Mike Borgonzi inherited the L'Jarius Sneed nightmare from the previous regime, and released him when it became financially plausible. He also traded Roger McCreary and Jarvis Brownlee during the 2025 season. Both players haven't done much since leaving.

Marcus Harris was viewed as the nickelback, but the defense had zero starters on the boundary. When free agency opened, in collaboration with head coach Robert Saleh, Borgonzi signed Alontae Taylor and Cor'Dale Flott to three-year contracts. Taylor earns $19 million annually, while Flott is paid $15 million per year.

The results have been fairly disappointing. The Taylor acquisition has especially been a drastic mistake. The Titans signed him to play cornerback, but abandoned that mission after five weeks. They've since moved Taylor to nickel, his more natural position, while benching Harris, who was playing well. It's painfully obvious they are attempting to salvage an ill-advised contract.

Flott hasn't been as bad as this fanbase suggests. He's played quite decently in some games. If the Titans could field any semblance of a pass rush, you'd likely be surprised with how effective Flott is as this defense's CB2.

The issue is Taylor and his contract. There's no logical escape route next offseason. In all likelihood, the Titans will absorb the Taylor blow through next season, and release him the following offseason (2028).

The Taylor mistake likely puts the Titans back into the market for a starting cornerback this coming offseason. They cannot enter 2027 with Joshua Williams or Micah Robinson as a starter. Unless they draft Notre Dame cornerback Leonard Moore with a top-five selection in the 2027 NFL Draft, they'd be asking a lot of a late first-or-second-round cornerback to start immediately in Week 1, especially in Saleh's complex defense.

The Titans may have to consider signing another veteran cornerback to start opposite Taylor. Ideally, it would be a highly-drafted rookie, but free agency poses another avenue. Some of the known cornerbacks on expiring contracts include Joey Porter Jr., D.J. Turner, Tyrique Stevenson, Tariq Woolen, and Marlon Humphrey. Brandin Echoles, who played for Saleh in NY, is also a pending free agent. Saleh would probably love to get someone like D.J. Reed, who is under contract with the Detroit Lions (but may be available?).
