Eagles put LB Zack Baun, WR Dontayvion Wicks on IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Eagles won’t have linebacker Zack Baun and wide receiver Dontayvion Wicks for their next four games.

Baun and Wicks were both ruled out on Friday and placed on injured reserve on Saturday. Baun has a back injury while Wicks is dealing with ankle and foot injuries.

The Eagles filled one of their roster spots by activating Will Shipley from injured reserve. He’ll join Saquon Barkley and Jaydon Blue as backfield options for Sunday’s game against the Eagles.

Defensive tackle Gabe Hall and linebacker Anfernee Orji have been elevated from the practice squad on a temporary basis. They’ll revert back after the matchup with the Steelers.
