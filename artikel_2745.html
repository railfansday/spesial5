Seven Army hockey games to air on MSG Networks, Big Ten

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Army hockey, off to one of its best starts in years at 6-2, will get some regional television coverage with seven upcoming games.

MSG Networks will broadcast six games and Big Ten Network has another.

The Sacred Heart at Army series on Friday, Nov. 13, and Saturday, Nov. 14, will be carried live from West Point's Tate Rink. The Friday 7 p.m. game will air on MSG Sports Network 2 and the Saturday 7 p.m. contest on MSG Sports Network and MSG Sports Network 2.

RPI visits on Thanksgiving weekend with the Saturday, Nov. 28, game at 4 p.m. airing on MSG Network and MSG Network 2.

Army and Penn State will play twice in December. The Thursday, Dec. 10, game at the Prudential Center in Newark, New Jersey, will be carried by MSG Network at 7 p.m. Two nights later, the series shifts to University Park, Pennsylvania, with the 7 p.m. game aired on Big Ten Network.

Air Force visits Tate Rink on Friday, Feb. 19, at 7 p.m. and Saturday, Feb. 20, at 4 p.m., with both games airing on MSG Network.

As of now, the remainder of Army's Atlantic Hockey schedule will be webstreamed by subscription service FloHockey.com.

Army already had a game at Holy Cross aired by New England Sports Network and road game with Quinnipiac streamed on ESPN Plus.
