Tyler Goodson placed on Injured Reserve Saturday

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Dallas Cowboys RB Tyler Goodson (ankle, knee) was placed on the Reserve/Injured list Saturday, Nov. 14. The team also signed practice squad TE James Mitchell to their 53-man roster. Terms of the contract were not disclosed.
