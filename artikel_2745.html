Vaibhav Sooryavanshi creates history! Smashes fastest-ever hundred in List A cricket with 28-ball ton

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi produced a breathtaking display of power-hitting against the United Arab Emirates (UAE) in the ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026, rewriting the List A record books with a sensational century for India A in Doha on Tuesday.The teenage opener smashed a 16-ball half-century before racing to his hundred in just 28 deliveries, registering the fastest century in List A cricket. In the process, Sooryavanshi also became the first batter to score two List A half-centuries in 16 balls or fewer.Sooryavanshi made his intentions clear from the outset, smashing three sixes in the opening over bowled by Zahid Ali. Opening alongside Abhishek Sharma, he helped India A race to 68 runs in just 3.3 overs before Abhishek departed for 12 off five balls.The early dismissal did little to slow Sooryavanshi down, as he continued to attack the UAE bowling attack and brought up his half-century in just 16 deliveries.Fastest List A century (by balls)28 Vaibhav Sooryavanshi India A vs UAE 202629 Jake Fraser-McGurk South Aus vs Tasmania Adelaide 202331 AB de Villiers SA vs WI Joburg 201532 Sakibul Gani Bihar vs Arunachal Ranchi 202533 Ishan Kishan Jharkhand vs Karnataka Ahmedabad 2025The left-hander's assault also helped India A create history as a team. They raced to 100 in just five overs, registering the fastest team century in List A one-day cricket, where ball-by-ball data is available.The previous record belonged to New Zealand, who had reached 100 in 6.3 overs against Sri Lanka in Christchurch in 2015. India A's effort also bettered the 6.3-over mark achieved by India A against Sri Lanka A in Dambulla in 2026 and New Zealand's 6.4-over effort against England in Wellington in 2015.Sooryavanshi's extraordinary knock put him in a league of his own, with his 16-ball fifty adding another milestone to an already remarkable innings.Earlier, UAE had posted 249/8 after being asked to bat first. Former India domestic batter Harpreet Singh Bhatia led their recovery with a career-best 130 off 144 balls, including 12 fours and four sixes.UAE had slipped to 20/2 inside the first six overs after Harshit Rana and Arshad Khan struck early. Harpreet then stitched together important partnerships with Muhammad Shahdad (28) and Ethan D'Souza (25) to rebuild the innings.Arshad finished with four wickets for 43 runs, while Basil Hameed remained unbeaten on 36 off 36 balls to help UAE reach 249/8 in their 50 overs.

India A needed 250 runs to win the Group B encounter and secure a place in the semi-finals, but Sooryavanshi's extraordinary start quickly turned the chase into a record-breaking spectacle.
