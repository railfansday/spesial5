Billy Edwards Jr. recently given a solid CBS Sports power ranking

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

When Billy Edwards Jr. entered the NCAA Transfer Portal following a stagnant one-year stint at Wisconsin, fans of the North Carolina Tar Heels didn't quite know the impact the former three-star was going to have.

While Edwards hasn't been perfect, his 2,247 yards through the air on a 65.5% completion rate, along with 18 touchdowns and three interceptions, have steadied the ship on what could have been a tumultuous second season for Bill Belichick.

Earlier this week, CBS Sports' David Cobb released a quarterback power ranking that slotted Edwards into 35th in the nation, a four-spot drop from where he was after North Carolina's 17-point win over UConn the week prior.

Minutes into the second half against the Louisville Cardinals, Edwards went down with an undisclosed injury that Belichick has since evaded in-depth talk about.

If Edwards is out when UNC travels up to Scott Stadium to take on UVA this weekend, freshman Travis Burgess would likely get the start. By no means did Burgess perform poorly against the Cardinals in the 16-14 heartbreaker, but with bowl hopes hanging by a thread, most in Chapel Hill would have preferred the consistent arm of Edwards this late in the season.
