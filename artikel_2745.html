Geno Smith plans to play against Chargers despite lingering ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Geno Smith was limited in practice Wednesday with an ankle injury suffered against Buffalo, but the quarterback indicated he expected to be able to play Sunday in Los Angeles versus the Chargers.

Asked if he believed he'd be able to start, Smith said, "That's the plan. I've got to take it day by day."

Smith, who has started all 10 games for the 2-8 Jets, was limping in the locker room Wednesday and added the ankle was "a little bit better. I'm working through it."

Aaron Glenn said "We'll see how the week goes" regarding Smith's status for Sunday.

Rookie Cade Klubnik filled in when Smith was sidelined and could be in play this week if Smith is unable to go.

"Cade moved around well and moved the offense well," Smith said. "The moment was not too big for him. I'm proud of the way he played. He showed a lot of promise."

New York Jets quarterback Geno Smith speaks to reporters in Florham Park, N.J., Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026. Bill Kostroun for NY Post

"I think he delivered the ball where he needed to deliver it,'' Glenn said of Klubnik. "The thing that's impressive is his ability to make plays with his feet."

Even if Smith is able to go, the Jets will be looking for more points than they've been able to put up recently.

They've gone nine quarters without a touchdown and scored a total of 24 points over their last two games.

And their red zone offense has been the worst in the league.

"We have to score touchdowns, be better, execute better and get in the end zone,'' Smith said.

Garrett Wilson said the team would be working on its red zone offense this week.

New York Jets quarterback Cade Klubnik (10) runs with the ball against the Buffalo Bills on Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, N.J. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

"I'm not happy with my production down there,'' Wilson said. "I take that personal. That's on me. I'm not happy with that."

Asked if he had "confidence" in offensive coordinator Frank Reich amidst the team's struggles, Glenn — seemingly surprised by the question — said, "I have a lot of confidence in Frank."

Rookie WR Omar Cooper Jr. (ankle/hip), DL Harrison Phillips (ankle) and WR Isaiah Williams (ribs) all didn't practice Wednesday. Edge Joseph Ossai (foot/ankle), OL Dylan Parham (ankle) and LB Trevin Wallace (groin) were limited, as was DT David Onyemata (abdomen), who has been on the IR for eight weeks. He returned to practice Wednesday.

Andrew Beck has been the team's most versatile player, with the fullback not only kicking off, but also getting a first down on a tush push on Sunday against the Bills.

"He's a unique player who has done a little bit of everything throughout his career, from high school to college,'' Glenn said. "We want to utilize that as much as we can."

Glenn said Kenyon Sadiq was also considered for use on the running play, but Beck "was a prime candidate. He was up for it."
