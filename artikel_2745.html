Bucs sign QB Easton Stick from the practice squad to the active roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Buccaneers signed quarterback Easton Stick from the practice squad to the active roster on Monday, the team announced.

They filled the practice squad spot by signing Cooper Rush.

In addition, the Buccaneers signed wide receiver David Sills to the practice squad and released cornerback Brandon Johnson to make room on the 17-player unit.

The quarterback maneuvers come as Baker Mayfield deals with a lower-leg injury.

Rookie quarterback Jalon Daniels will make his fourth start of the season if Mayfield can’t play Sunday. Stick would serve as Daniels’ backup.

Stick was active for Daniels’ three starts in Weeks 4-6 as Mayfield worked his way back from a dislocated right thumb. Stick is now out of practice squad elevations.

The Bucs signed Stick to the practice squad on Aug. 31, but he has previous experience in offensive coordinator Zac Robinson’s system. The two were together in Atlanta last season.

Rush also has NFL starting experience, having opened 18 games with Dallas, Baltimore and Atlanta. That includes two starts for the Falcons this season.

Sills was on the Buccaneers’ active roster for three weeks before the team waived him Nov. 10.

Johnson was on the Buccaneers’ practice squad for five weeks. He previously saw regular-season action with the Eagles in 2025, appearing in three games with one start.
