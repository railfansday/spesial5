Sources: Chelsea lead Cesc Fabregas race after Xabi Alonso latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Arsenal and Chelsea both hold a long-term interest in Cesc Fabregas becoming their next manager, sources have told Football Insider.

The 39-year-old is proving his managerial credentials in Serie A with Como, and has also impressed with his side's performances in the Champions League this season.

An eventual move to the Premier League is seen as inevitable for the former midfield star, and his two former clubs are expected to be among the leading contenders.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Fabregas' growing reputation means both Arsenal and Chelsea will be keeping tabs on his progress.

Man United were also linked with a move to appoint the Spaniard before landing Mauricio Pochettino, with a Premier League switch becoming increasingly likely.

Arsenal's interest will be delayed, for now, with Mikel Arteta safe in his position after signing a new contract, but Xabi Alonso's situation at Chelsea is less secure.

Football Insider revealed earlier this year that Chelsea were considering Fabregas as a contender to replace Liam Rosenior after the former Stasbourg boss' dismissal.

Despite ultimately opting to go with Alonso, their faith in Fabregas is a sign that he will soon be ready to land one of the top jobs in the Premier League.

Having now also been considered by Arsenal, and with Barcelona also strong admirers, there is a feeling clubs could be racing to land him sooner rather than later.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Como are well aware of the growing interest from maybe some of the top European clubs in Cesc Fabregas.

"He's getting a lot of admirers after the last 18 months or so for what he's done with Como, and he's now adding Champions League experience to that as well.

"I'm sure if Arsenal needed a new manager, or Chelsea made a change, or even Barcelona where he's played before, I'm sure Fabregas would be pretty high up on the list.

"I think it's inevitable at some point that he will move on to bigger and better things in Como, and those top European and Premier League clubs will be ready to give him a shot."

While Arteta is arguably the most secure manager in world football at Arsenal, the same cannot be said for Alonso at Chelsea, adding a new element to their interest in Fabregas.

The former Real Madrid boss has come under some pressure at Stamford Bridge, with sections of the fanbase questioning his decisions after a difficult run of form.

Former Man United coach Mick Brown told Football Insider on 9 November that Alonso is safe in his position as Chelsea manager, with the club keen to back him.

However, with fans questioning his decisions and a recent downturn in form, doubts have begun to emerge about the manager's future in West London.

Following a huge 4-1 defeat at home against rivals Leeds last time out, Alonso has questions to answer, and must prove his side can turn around their recent form.
