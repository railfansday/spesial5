Reports: Milan watching on as Man Utd confirm renewal talks with Fernandes

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Bruno Fernandes has ended up on AC Milan’s radar ahead of the summer, according to reports, with a free transfer potentially on the cards.

Ever since the summer, when the BBC were the first to report on Milan’s interest, Fernandes has been brought up by various outlets as an option for the Rossoneri. Just a few days ago, Fabrizio Romano said he is ‘more than a dream’ for them and that Ruben Amorim could be key.

Having just turned 32, Fernandes is still at a good age and he’s showing that with Manchester United so far this season. In 14 appearances, he has bagged six goals and three assists, so it’s no surprise that many clubs are monitoring his situation given the expiring contract.

As reported by both Corriere dello Sport (via MilanNews) and Tuttosport (via MilanNews) this morning, Milan are seriously evaluating the idea of signing him. It’s still too early to talk about negotiations, but he’s definitely on their radar and will likely remain there.

Of course, if he does end up leaving on a free transfer, there will be no shortage of suitors so Milan would likely have to table a hefty contract offer. The club owner, Gerry Cardinale, seems more willing to do that now, though, than he did just a year ago.

One potential problem is that Manchester United have confirmed that they are discussing a renewal with the player. The CEO Omar Berrada showered the Portuguese star with praise, as cited by Fabrizio Romano, so it seems a stay in Manchester cannot be ruled out either.
