Man City remain certain that they were right to sell James Trafford and back Gianluigi Donnarumma as their number one goalkeeper, sources have told Football Insider.

Trafford spent last season behind Donnarumma in the goalkeeper pecking order at Man City following a return to the club.

He left the Etihad Stadium in the summer as Leeds completed a club-record £40million move for his services.

Trafford has since gone from strength to strength at Leeds, while Donnarumma has received criticism for regularly lacklustre performances this season.

Despite their differing fortunes, Man City chiefs do not regret letting Trafford leave, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast.

The 24-year-old was always expected to push for a move after a tough season back at the Etihad, and they were satisfied with the fee received.

Trafford has kept four clean sheets in 10 Premier League appearances and is the leading goalkeeper for save percentage (82.2) in the top flight so far this season.

Donnarumma is 13th in the same list, with 65.8 per cent, while he has kept three clean sheets in 10 league games and conceded five more goals than the Englishman

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I’m not sure if there’ll be regrets. I’m sure there’ll be some thoughts where maybe they feel they should have kept hold of Trafford long term, but it was the player's desire to be playing football that really forced their hand and they cashed in on him by selling him to Leeds for a big fee as well.

"The player wasn’t happy playing second fiddle to Donnarumma. He wanted to be playing, and has gone to Leeds and taken that opportunity with both hands.

"He's now really established himself in the England squad under Thomas Tuchel. So maybe no regrets for City, but it’s probably a missed opportunity in that respect."

Geronimo Rulli was signed as Trafford's replacement in the summer, and has impressed in his two Carabao Cup appearances so far this term.

Some fans and pundits have called for him to replace Donnarumma in the City starting eleven on a permanent basis, but Maresca is certain that the Italian deserves to keep his place.

In a press conference on Friday, he said: "He has been good. I think with the international team, he made a mistake in the build-up. But in Italy he was the man of the match in the game, he made many good saves.

"We made some mistakes in the last games. Gigi was part of that. He is aware. Hopefully he's not going to make any more mistakes. He is going to start against Fulham."
