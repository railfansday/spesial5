Ex-PGMOL chief: Connor Roberts 'should be banned' as Norway vs Wales footage re-analysed

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Connor Roberts should have been sent off when conceding a late penalty in Wales' 3-1 defeat to Norway and be facing a suspension.

That is according to former PGMOL chief and ex-FIFA referee, Keith Hackett, who exclusively told Football Insider that the Burnley defender made no legal attempt for the ball.

Craig Bellamy's side were condemned to their fifth defeat in six matches in Oslo on Saturday, 14 November, seeing them finish bottom of Group 4.

Antonio Nusa, Andreas Schjelderup and Erling Haaland were all on target, with David Brooks' strike 10 minutes from time simply a consolation.

Haaland's 88th-minute penalty only rubbed salt into the wounds, but it could have been even worse for Wales, who must now face a play-off relegation match.

Roberts, while tracking back, shoved Schjelderup in the back, giving away a penalty and picking up a yellow card as a result of his desperate actions.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Hackett has stated that, in line with the laws, Roberts should have been given his marching orders instead.

"The reality here is that he's fortunate not to have received a red card. What you have to look at is distance from goal. Well, he's a shot away," he said.

"It's not necessarily the ball heading for goal, and admittedly, that's probably the area where the referee's decided direction of play.

"But in reality, direction of play was towards goal when that foul took place. And the ball then took a different route.

"It's not just the ball direction; it's the direction of play, the law states. So in my opinion, he's fortunate not to have got a red card."

During the summer, Bellamy was heavily linked with a move to Burnley, but decided against joining the Clarets, sticking with Wales instead.

That decision split opinion amongst the fanbase, but a growing majority are now wishing that the Welsh FA had made the decision for him.

Wales have won just one of their last nine games, including friendlies, and that came in a surprise 2-1 win over Norway at the Cardiff City Stadium.

Bellamy's days currently look numbered, and unless he can find some form in the coming months, there might be a change in the dugout.
