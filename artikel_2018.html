Exclusive: Swansea City & Burnley in race to sign Huddersfield Town star Joe Taylor

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The January transfer window is just over a month away from opening, with EFL clubs already scouting new potential players ahead of the month-long chance to bolster their squads.

For clubs that have players that are starring in their leagues, they're going to face a fight to keep hold of them when it's all said and done, and then there are clubs at various points in the standings of any division that are going to look to strengthen their options.

And for high-flying Huddersfield Town in League One, they have a number of players who may be of interest to teams higher up in the footballing pyramid than they currently are - one player is certainly being looked at by Championship clubs.

Football League World has been exclusively informed that Terriers hot-shot Joe Taylor is a wanted man in the second tier ahead of the mid-season window.

Despite Dutch winger Derensili Sanches Fernandes taking most of the praise and plaudits for the West Yorkshire outfit since his summer arrival from Excelsior, Taylor has been quietly going about his business as part of their dangerous attacking unit, having forced his way back into contention at the Accu Stadium following his loan out to Wigan Athletic in 2025-26.

The 24-year-old was shipped out temporarily by previous manager Lee Grant to the Latics, where he found his spark again, scoring 11 goals in half a season for them before returning to Town in the summer under new head coach Martin Drury.

Alongside the aforementioned Sanches Fernandes in a supporting attacking role, where the pair play behind more of a focal point such as Ashley Fletcher or Bojan Radulovic more often than not, Taylor has found the back of the net 10 times in League One already in just 14 outings, proving to be one of League One's best once again.

And that goalscoring performance is not going unnoticed, with FLW exclusively being able to reveal that managerless Swansea City, as well as struggling Burnley are both weighing up a move for the Welsh centre-forward in January.

Taylor has only sparringly played at Championship level before for Luton Town before getting his move to Huddersfield, but there are clubs looking at trying to get the attacker a move up the pyramid.

Swansea of course lost head coach Vitor Matos to Strasbourg last week, but it will not stop them eyeing up targets ahead of January, and Taylor is on their radar, even with a strike-force of Adam Idah, Ross Stewart and Zan Vipotnik to choose from - the latter was the Championship's top scorer last season but has failed to fire in 2026-27, and theoretically could be cashed in on to open up the space for another new forward.

Burnley meanwhile are in the Championship relegation zone and haven't won in Fabio Celestini's four matches in charge - they have centre-forward options like Jamie Vardy, Zeki Amdouni and Andreas Hountondji, but with a lack of goals coming from that particular trio, Taylor is being considered as a January option.

Whether Huddersfield would cash in on Taylor mid-season with their promotion aspirations remains to be seen, but given he's under contract until 2029, it'll likely take a significant amount of cash to prise him away from Drury's squad.
