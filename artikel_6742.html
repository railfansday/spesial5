Finding your signature scent involves a lot of trial and error—and a tingling nose. Fragrance is powerful, and discovering a unique scent for daily or occasional wear can uplift your mood, evoke a memory, or make your presence known in any room before you even speak. Whether you’re hunting for a fragrance that feels distinctly you or building a perfume wardrobe to ensure you’re prepared for every event on your social calendar, shopping for perfume can feel overwhelming. One moment, everyone’s going for guava; the next, it’s all about sea salt.

Truth is, trends come and go. However, the journey to building a unique collection is about finding a fragrance that truly resonates—whether floral, gourmand, musky, or a distinct layered blend.

“Fragrance has the power to evoke memories of a person, a moment, an experience,” says DSM-Firmenich perfumer Bérénice Watteau. “We’ve all experienced it: walking down the street, catching a familiar scent on a stranger, and suddenly being brought back to someone we love or a beautiful memory shared with them. Perfume captures those fleeting feelings and makes them timeless.” Your scent should also feel like an extension of yourself, something that reflects your personality, style, and the way you want to be remembered. Don’t be afraid to layer a couple of scents for an irreplicable fragrance of your own creation.

As beauty editors, we’re lucky enough to spritz, sniff, and test hundreds of scents, so it takes a lot for one to impress us—that’s how you know the ones on the list below are extra special. We also consulted three experts and combed through numerous customer reviews to find 16 of the best perfumes for women that we can’t stop wearing.

What should you consider when choosing a perfume?

Understanding fragrance notes is one of the most effective ways to find your perfect scent. “Very similar to the fashion industry, there can be trends with fragrances as well,” Tanis says. “Choosing a fragrance isn’t always easy, but make the right choice by selecting one that reflects who you are and makes you feel good. My best advice is to let your personality, tastes, and age guide your choice.”

“The most important part of finding a fragrance is, of course, trying it on,” Tanis continues, adding that you can have multiple signature scents, rather than just one, for variety. “Nowadays, it is all about a collection of fragrances. Much like clothing, modern men and women desire a fragrance wardrobe, allowing them the freedom to choose what fragrance to wear daily.”

What is the difference between an eau de toilette and eau de parfum?

Many popular scents fall into these fragrance categories. The difference between the two lies in the concentration of perfume oil each contains, says Tanis. “Eau de toilettes (EDT) have a relatively lighter composition due to their low concentration of perfume oil— between five and 15 percent. On the other hand, Eau de parfums (EDP) have a higher concentration of perfume oils—typically between 12 and 18 percent—meaning they offer a significantly stronger scent that lasts between five and eight hours.”

Choosing between the two might come down to the specific occasion or the weather (i.e., you may lean towards a lighter scent for work and during the summer months, and a richer scent for nights out and during the winter). Check out our perfume terminology guide to learn more about fragrance terms.

Tanis suggests applying fragrance to the pulse points, where the skin is naturally warm, such as the wrists, neck, or inside the elbows. “This will allow the fragrance to warm up and reveal the perfume's sillage (trail) over time.”
