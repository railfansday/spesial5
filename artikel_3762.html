Best Stands in Stand Out for PVP and PVE!

There are a lot of different stands in Stand Out that have a wide array of applications for PVP, PVE or just roleplay and having fun. Any stand can be good depending on what your playstyle is but some are more mechanically effective at PVP or PVE so that’s what we’re ranking for today. Here’s my Stand Out stand tier list.

⇓ Strong Stands That Are More Situational ⇓

⇓ Niche Stands That Can Excel In Specific Situations But Are Less Powerful In General ⇓

⇓ Weak and Sub-Optimal Stands Apart From Roleplay and Personal Preference ⇓

The best stand in Stand Out largely depends on how much practice and playtime you put into it but S and A-Tier stands allow you to be strong in PVP and PVE more easily. B and C-Tier Stands are more situational so you might have a harder time playing them effectively without a lot of practice. I would avoid D-Tier stands unless you personally find them fun since they are ineffective for PVP and PVE.

How to Get a Stand: You will get a Stand Arrow to awaken your stand after the tutorial. You can also get Stand Arrows via Crafting, Bounties, the Train Raid and the Train Shop.

How to Reroll Stand: To reroll stands you simply need another Stand Arrow.

How to Remove a Stand: You remove stands by eating an Emerald Roka (Or mutated versions) which you can get from planting/Gardening. This fully upgrades your sub-ability but you need one like Hamon or Vampire to remove stands
