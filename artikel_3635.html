Your Criminal Profile in Grand Theft Auto 6 is your reputation, similar to an honor or morality system

Actions have consequences in Grand Theft Auto 6. Building on the honor system in Red Dead Redemption 2, fully functional Criminal Profiles in GTA 6 not only keep track of the way that you're playing the game, but help dictate how the world (and the characters who inhabit it) react to Jason and Lucia's presence.

GTA 6 Criminal Profiles are a really important metric that can change fundamental aspects of your play experience. I'm going to help you understand all of this with an overview of the Criminal Profiles in GTA 6, and offer up some tips on how you can change through the five tiers.

Your actions and behavior are captured in real-time by the Criminal Profile system. You are able to quickly check the status of your current criminal profile for both Jason and Lucia by holding right on the d-pad – look for the little icons next to their profile pictures for an update at a glance.

For a deeper dive navigate to the "Jason & Lucia" menu. These profiles change over time, and their status can have a lasting impact on each of the characters so you will want to check this frequently.

Jason and Lucia each have their own individual Criminal Profiles, but the actions of one character can influence the behavior of the other when you're out on the streets together.

There are five Criminal Profiles in GTA 6 that Lucia and Jason can move between, all of which are laid out below from least to most severe.

It's possible to move freely between Professional, Hardened, Violent, and Psycho tiers. However, should you create enough chaos to be considered Irredeemable this is an irreversible trait – and one which may permanently impact your relationship status in GTA 6.

Professional and Hardened Criminal Profiles are assigned to characters who do their best to avoid violence, and at least attempt to resolve situations without incident. Start pursuing extreme violence and cruelty to both animals and civilians and you'll become to etch into Violent and Psycho territories.

While you're on a mission, Criminal Profiles aren't affected by any criminal acts that the game requires you to do. It's only when you go beyond what is truly necessary or reasonable that action begins to have consequences in either direction.

Below your health bar is where the GTA 6 flashes up little icons which denote how your actions are impacting your criminal profile. The game will track low-casualty incursions and clean getaways, just as it will acts of extreme violence and destruction.

If your character is going in a direction you aren't happy with, here's what to be aware of with each Criminal Profile:

If you're after a Criminal Profile that lists you as a 'Professional' then you'll need to be considerate of your actions. Avoid casualties where possible, don't chase unnecessary aggression, and only do what could be considered reasonable to get the task at hand completed.

It's worth noting that making threats won't impact your Professional Criminal Profile, and that the best way to maintain this reputation is to avoid violence unless you're threatened and to try to remain undetected while committing crimes. This isn't always possible, just don't escalate for the sake of it.

Tip: Non-lethal incapacitations (aiming for joints rather than the head or heart), thoughtful actions, and petting every dog and cat you come across will increase your Focus in GTA 6 while you have a Professional Criminal Profile.

If you've found yourself with a Hardened Criminal Profile in GTA 6, it probably means that you've started to resort to violence to complete your objectives. Rushing to kill enemies and breaking detection for the sake of it will contribute to this profile – although it's still easy enough to get back to a Professional Criminal Profile if you take steps to avoid violence in a couple of robberies.

If you continue down this path, harming civilians and killing cops, then your Criminal Profile will eventually shift from Hardened to Violent. It's worth flagging that, like the Professional profile, non-lethal incapacitations will still contribute to an increase in your focus.

If you've gotten yourself a Violent Criminal Profile in GTA 6 then something has gone really wrong. This is the game reflecting that you've probably spent a fair few hours attacking cops and civilians for the smallest infractions. Brutally attacking cops and shooting fleeing civilians in the back will contribute to your Violent profile but acting in a self-defensive capacity will not.

If you find yourself pushing into Psycho territory, it is still possible to move back to Hardened or Professional. You'll just need to be more conscious about the way you are interacting with the world, and become more deliberate with your actions.

Tip: Headshots will increase your focus if you've gotten yourself a Violent profile, but each one of those will also make it more difficult to return to Hardened or Professional state.

You know what you're doing, and you don't care who knows about it. If you get assigned the Psycho Criminal Profile in GTA 6 then you've most likely spent a few hours mercilessly harming vulnerable animals (yes, hunting does count in certain instances), murdering defenseless civilians with abandon, and have been blasting through cops with no hesitation.

Something to note with the Psycho profile is that there is an additional icon you should keep an eye out for. If you see a line-scratch through the icon, it means you've reached a breaking point and are at risk of tipping into Irredeemable territory. Start shooting to incapacitate to get back to being a reasonable member of society.

If you continue acting with wild abandon once you reach the breaking point within the Psycho boundaries, eventually you will earn yourself an Irredeemable Criminal Profile in GTA 6. This is only given to the worst of the worst – this would have been Trevor's starting point in GTA 5 – and reflects your insistence on creating maximum chaos, regardless of the cost.

This level of excessive violence will make you irredeemable, which is permanently attributed to your character. This will impact the way civilians react to you on the street, it will drive a wedge between Jason and Lucia, and will change the way cops approach you too. If you've got a Psycho or Irredeemable Criminal Profile, expect cops to start open firing at you far faster than before.

Having a Psycho or Irredeemable profile will mean that headshots increase your focus, which is a great way to engage in gunplay. However, you'll also lose the ability to surrender to the cops – which can have a pretty big impact on your money in GTA 6.
