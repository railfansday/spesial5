Desire Doue will undergo surgery after suffering a torn ACL in his right knee while playing for Paris Saint-Germain.

The 21-year-old France international was carried off in ⁠tears towards the ​end of ⁠the first half of PSG's 3-2 victory at Nice on ⁠Saturday after suffering the serious injury, which will rule him out for the season.

The club have confirmed he will go under the knife “before the end of the month” but did not provide a timeline on his return.

Doue scored his second league goal of the season against Nice, but was injured when his fully extended leg buckled as he turned to shield the ball from Nice midfielder Sofiane Diop, with no contact between the players.

The injury is the latest setback for Doue, who missed parts of last season with calf and hamstring problems.

Doue's absence further compounds PSG's injury woes, with the European champions already missing influential playmaker Khvicha Kvaratskhelia.

The Georgian winger is likewise out with his own ACL tear, which he suffered against Barcelona in October, and is unlikely to feature again this term.

PSG return to action on Wednesday, when they host Roma in the Champions League.
