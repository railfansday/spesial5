Smartlook Alternative for WordPress: Self-Hosted Session Recordings and Heatmaps

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Posted on Nov 20 • Originally published at optiuser.com

We build Opti-Behavior, a WordPress plugin; this guide was first published on our blog. The export steps and the checklist are useful whatever tool you pick.

Smartlook stopped selling new subscriptions on 31 May 2026 and the platform will be switched off on 30 September 2027, with all remaining data deleted. If your recordings and heatmaps run on Smartlook, you need a Smartlook alternative before your paid plan ends, and you need to export what you want to keep first. This guide gives the official dates, the export route Smartlook documents, a checklist to compare replacements, and the steps to move a WordPress site to a self-hosted tool.

The short version: copy your data with the Smartlook APIs before your paid subscription expires, list what you really use (recordings, heatmaps, funnels, events), then test one or two replacements on a real page for a week before your plan runs out.

Between the end of your paid plan and 30 September 2027, Smartlook says you can still use its Free plan, with that plan's limits. Smartlook also states that Splunk will not migrate historical data from Smartlook to Digital Experience Analytics, and that your current subscription will not be transitioned automatically.

In practice, the date that matters for you is not 2027. It is the day your own paid plan ends: after it, you lose the paid features and work inside free-plan limits. Write that date down now and plan the switch a few weeks before it.

Old behavior data ages fast. A redesign, a new theme or a new checkout makes last year's heatmaps less useful. Your event and funnel definitions keep their value, so export those first.

Feature lists look alike, so compare replacements on the points that change your daily work and your costs. Ask each vendor these five questions and write the answers in one table.

2. What does the free quota really cover?

Check what is counted (sessions, page views, recordings), whether bots count, and what happens at the limit: does recording pause, does old data disappear, do features lock? A quota that sounds large can be used up by bot traffic on a small site.

3. How long is the trial, and does it need a card?

Two weeks rarely covers a full buying cycle. A longer trial lets you see weekend traffic, a campaign and a few real sales before you decide.

If you sell with WooCommerce, check that the tool sees the steps product, cart, checkout and order without custom code, and that it can show where buyers leave.

Do not trust a vendor's speed claim, ours included. Measure it: run PageSpeed Insights on the same page three times with the tool on, then three times with it off, and compare the median.

Keep Smartlook running in parallel until your export is done and the new tool shows data.

Then rebuild the funnels you exported from Smartlook. On a WooCommerce shop, the plugin suggests a purchase funnel (shop, product, cart, checkout, order received) that you create in one click.

Collecting behavior data is the easy part. The hard part is finding the one problem worth fixing this week. Opti-Behavior connects its modules so that the answer comes to you instead of the other way round.

Smart Insights reads these modules together and turns them into findings: a funnel step that loses most visitors, a button nobody clicks, a form field that blocks. The core findings are Free; Smart Insights Pro adds the evidence from recordings, forms, errors, journeys, funnels, Shop and A/B tests.

Is there a free Smartlook alternative on WordPress?

Yes. The Opti-Behavior Free plugin gives traffic analytics, click heatmaps, funnels, WooCommerce counters and Smart Insights core with no time limit. Recordings and the other Pro modules come with the Pro trial: 6 months of every Pro feature, no credit card.

With Opti-Behavior, recordings and analytics are stored on your own WordPress server, in your database and files. Hosted tools such as Hotjar, Clarity and Mouseflow keep data on their own servers.

After the 6-month trial, Pro stays free up to 1,000 sessions a month, bots and spam included. At the quota, new Pro recordings pause until the 1st of the next month; nothing is deleted, and the Free features keep collecting. Paid plans have no quota.
