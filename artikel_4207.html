Delightful Tidying Up Indie Gets A Surprise Switch 2 Edition Today, Complete With 120fps

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Admirers of 2022's delightful little 'tidy 'em up', A Little To The Left, will want to pay attention to this one. Developer Max Inferno and publisher Secret Mode have announced the game's Switch 2 Edition, and it's available today!

Launching to celebrate the game's fourth anniversary, A Little to the Left - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition boasts a smooth 120fps, Mouse Mode controls, faster load times, a visual boost, and USB mouse and keyboard support.

The best part is that the upgrade path is completely free for all those who own the base game on Switch 1. The NS2E store page isn't live at the time of writing, but considering that the original only set us back £12.99 / $14.99, we can't imagine this new version will be particularly bank-breaking.

Max Inferno also has a patch inbound for all versions of the game, which targets a few known issues in the Daily Tidy mode and Seeing Stars DLC puzzles.

The fiddly controls were one of our biggest hangups with the original release on Switch 1, which we otherwise said "provides an experience that is a weird mingling of the semi-passive and viscerally intrusive" in our 8/10 review. Let's hope that everything plays as it should in this new version.
