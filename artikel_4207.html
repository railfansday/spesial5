PS5 Closing in on 100 Million Sales as Gradual Drop-Off Continues

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

That's another 2.6 million PS5 consoles sold

Sony sold another 2.6 million PS5 consoles during the second quarter of its 2026 fiscal year, bringing the total number of systems sold to 97.9 million.

It should surpass the 100 million sales milestone by the end of the next fiscal report, but the downward trend in sales has continued. PlayStation sold four million PS5 machines during the same quarter of its last fiscal year, confirming 1.4 million fewer systems were sold in the quarter just gone.

The Q1 period of fiscal year 2026 also recorded a reduced number of PS5 consoles sold: 1.6 million compared to 2.5 million in the previous fiscal year.

Besides the gradual decrease in PS5 system sales, though, practically all of PlayStation's results for the quarter are on the up.

It sold 88.2 million PS5 and PS4 games, which is an increase of 7.9 million over the same time period last fiscal year. 8.2 million of those sales were first-party projects, which is 1.9 million more purchases than the same quarter in the previous fiscal year.

Once it hits the 100 million sales milestone, the PS5 will be the fourth PlayStation console to manage the feat, with PS1, PS2, and PS4 all managing the accomplishment. The PS3 topped out at around 87.4 million units sold.
