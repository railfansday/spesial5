Heartopia shows off My Little Pony collaboration, and there's a pre-launch event to hold you over

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Heartopia’s My Little Pony collaboration is close, and the pre-launch event has already kicked off.

Heartopia has officially shared initial details about its first-ever collaboration event. The game, which launched a month ago to great success on mobile, as well as Steam on PC, has already been a staple among fans of Animal Crossing and The Sims.

Things are kicking into gear, it seems, because the game is already about to host its first collaboration event, and it is with My Little Pony.

The Heartopia X My Little Pony event officially begins on February 14, but a pre-launch login event is now live through the actual collaboration’s start. The limited-time event offers players sign-in puzzles every day that will unlock time-limited Exhibition Passes.

The first actual piece of My Little Pony-themed content can be yours if you complete all puzzles, as that gets you a limited edition costume for your pet, based on the show’s Spike. The event itself, which will be available until March 15, brings My Little Pony’s Tree of Harmony into the world of Heartopia.

The ponies need your help to Wield Elements of Harmony so they can reawaken the tree, and bring it back to Equestria. In gameplay terms, you’re going to want to perform daily sensing at the tree in order to earn three of the six Elements of Harmony: Honesty, Generosity, Loyalty, Kindness, Laughter, and Magic.

The more you do this, the more the tree’s Energy goes up, and the more rewards and resources you stand to earn. Even more time-limited Exhibition Passes, and Moonlight Crystals can be earned as you water the tree.

Badge Pull Tokens are your big reward, which is what you’re actually going to use to get the special Pony Series badges. You’ll earn ten time-limited Exhibition Passes each time you complete one of the three sets of Pony Series badges. Collect all three and you’ll earn Town Collaboration Series badges.

Heartopia is a free-to-play game, so you know there is a Gacha component to this event. Expect six non-replacement Gacha Pools that each map to one member of My Little Pony’s Mane Six. Within each pool, you’ll be able to earn five outfit pieces, and one vehicle.

If any of that sounds interesting, definitely log in now to play the pre-launch event, which should hold you over until the proper show kicks off Saturday.
