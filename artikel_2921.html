NBA Trade Rumors: Jazz connected to superstar guard?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Utah Jazz have given internal consideration to Kyri Irving, according to Ian Begley.

It's also no surprise that the Jazz "want to win."

When I hear rumors like this, I try to consider where the rumor is potentially coming from, and what the purpose of the rumor is.

My first bet is potentially the agent. Irving would make a lot of sense for Utah as a third guard/super sub who could play huge minutes come playoff time. Utah could have reached out to Irving's agent to see if he'd be up for that kind of role.

This could also be from the Mavericks' side. Maybe there was a phone call, and the Mavericks want to drive up any potential offer for Irving.

Whatever the case, it's not surprising if the Jazz are looking for another high-level ball handler to come off the bench. Irving as a sixth man would be a cheat code and could help Utah take things to another level sooner rather than later. The question is: would Utah be willing to pay the price for Irving? And would Irving be happy to join the Jazz in a different role?

It's clear Utah expects to win more than they have, and they're talking about how to do it. We'll see if anything materializes because talk is just talk until someone gets desperate.
