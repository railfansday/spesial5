Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Atlanta Falcons have had a less than ideal season so far.

They are 3-7 through 10 games. Bijan Robinson is averaging 95.1 rushing yards per game and Atlanta is third in the NFL with 1,335 yards on the ground. But his workload is heavy with Brian Robinson Jr. on injured reserve.

Michael Penix Jr. has struggled to take control of the offense, throwing seven interceptions to go along with his 10 touchdowns. In Week 8, he threw four picks in a loss to the Tampa Bay Buccaneers.

The NFC South is one of the weaker divisions in the league and the Falcons aren't totally out of the playoff picture. But Week 11 is a chance for them to recalibrate to avoid missing the playoffs for the ninth straight year.
