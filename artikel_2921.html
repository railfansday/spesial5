Arnold Ebiketie: No hard feelings after trade to Bengals falls through

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Arnold Ebiketie: No hard feelings after trade to Bengals falls through originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

Arnold Ebiketie was at breakfast last Tuesday when he got the call.

The Eagles were trading him to the Bengals for a 2027 sixth-round draft pick. As Ebiketie drove home to pack his bags, his mind was racing. He was already thinking about how he was going to ship his car to Cincinnati, pack up his belongings and spend some time with his kids before heading to Ohio. His flight had already been booked.

But about two hours later, Ebiketie got another call. The deal fell through.

"It's the NFL, the business-side of it," Ebiketie said on Wednesday as the Eagles began their preparations to play the Steelers. "I felt like one call I was going to Cincinnati, another call I was staying here. I wasn't mad at the fact that I was staying so I got back to my bye week. That was the best part about it."

The Bengals were looking for some added edge rusher depth before the deadline when they agreed to the trade with the Eagles, but they somehow overlooked the fact that trading for Ebiketie would cancel out a projected third-round compensatory pick coming their way in next year's draft. Ebiketie might have been worth a sixth-round pick, but he wasn't worth losing a third-rounder too.

So the Bengals had to embarrassingly call off the deal and the Eagles had to welcome back a player they very obviously tried to trade.

You would understand if Ebiketie found the situation a little awkward, but he claims it isn't.

"Nah. A lot of people ask me but I wouldn't say it makes me feel awkward," he said. "It's football. Now I'm back at work, I've got to get ready to play Pittsburgh this weekend. It's been normal."

Ebiketie said the first thing he did when he learned about the trade was to look up the Bengals' schedule. He was hoping that Cincinnati hadn't yet had their bye week and was disappointed to learn they already had it in Week 6.

Had the trade gone through, Ebiketie would have had to forfeit a bye week and join the Bengals immediately.

"Getting traded on your bye week, that's not the best thing to happen," Ebiketie said. "But then I realized I was staying, I was like, 'You know what? I can get back to my plans,' which I wasn't doing much. Going to DC for the weekend. Got back to my plans."

The Eagles signed the 27-year-old Ebiketie to a one-year deal this offseason and he has played in all nine games this season. Ebiketie has played a total of 300 defensive snaps (48%) but has yet to register a sack and has just two quarterback hits.

After four years with the Falcons, Ebiketie had to take a one-year, prove-it deal in Philly and he hasn't been very successful. The team hasn't been very successful either.

But Ebiketie is happy to still be in Philly. He's especially happy to have more time with his teammates.

"That's the hardest part, you know," Ebiketie said. "When you make the connections with the guys and you get traded and you have to leave and that's the first thing that goes through your head. The people that you're around in the locker room, people that you're out there playing with, you're definitely going to miss them."
