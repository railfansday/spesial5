One of the greatest players in Bruins history will receive one of hockey's highest honors on Monday night.

Patrice Bergeron is set to be inducted into the Hockey Hall of Fame as part of the Class of 2026, recognizing a career defined by leadership, consistency and excellence on both ends of the ice.

Follow along as NESN provides live coverage of the induction ceremony, including highlights, memorable moments, and reactions as Bergeron takes his place among the sport's legends.

7:37 p.m.: There have been sightings of Ray Bourque, Bruins president Cam Neely, head coach Marco Sturm, Don Sweeney, and the man that can not be missed, Zdeno Chara.

6:49 p.m.: Looks like Brad Marchand is also in attendance.

Welcome to NESN's LIVE coverage of the 2026 Hockey Hall of Fame induction ceremony.

The 2026 Hockey Hall of Fame induction ceremony will air on NHL Network. The event will also be available to stream on Fubo and DIRECTV.

Coverage of the 2026 Hockey Hall of Fame induction ceremony will start at 8 p.m. ET on Monday, Nov. 9. The event will be held at the Hockey Hall of Fame in Toronto.
