Holafly Plans: The Best eSIM Plan for Long Term Travelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

There are a lot of eSIM companies in the world. They all work a similar way: you download their app, pick your package, install the eSIM into your smartphone, and off you go. There’s many I like.

But one I like more than the rest is Holafly and I’ve been using their eSIMs for years. I find their app to be easy to use and their rates really competitive.

So when Holafly launched Holafly Plans, a single eSIM that works as an ongoing subscription across 160+ destinations, I was excited. It’s a great option for those who are looking for more data and flexibility than a traditional eSIM. (An eSIM is a digital SIM built directly into your phone’s hardware, so there’s no physical card to insert or swap. Instead of visiting a shop for a plastic SIM, you activate a plan by scanning a QR code or installing it through an app, and you’re connected within minutes. Because it’s software-based, a single phone can hold multiple eSIMs, letting you switch between a home number and a local or global travel plan without touching your device. I have like 10 installed. lol)

In this post, I’m going to explain what the product is, how it’s different than a traditional eSIM, and who I think this option is for!

Holafly Plans is a single global eSIM that covers more than 160 destinations on one ongoing subscription. You buy it once, install it once, and it just keeps working as you move from country to country. You don’t reswap SIMs, you don’t redownload anything, there’s no re-purchasing, and no hunting for a new QR code every time you land somewhere new.

It automatically reconnects you when you land in a new place. Think of it as like a traditional mobile carrier – but a lot cheaper.

You can buy it monthly or annually and it comes in two tiers: Light and Unlimited. There’s no annual contract and you can cancel any time.

The Unlimited Plan includes no data caps and an unlimited hotspot so you can tether your laptop on the go. If you’re a digital nomad uploading video, doing video calls, or just someone who doesn’t want to think about data ever again, this is a great add-on. This tier also comes with a local phone number from the US, UK, or Canada, which lets you receive SMS messages.

The Light Plan gives you 25GB of data a month, still includes a hotspot, and still covers the same 160+ destination coverage. For someone who mostly uses maps, messaging apps, and the occasional Instagram scroll, this package is probably the best option.

Additionally, with both Holafly Plans, once your plan is active, you get their “Always On” feature which is 1GB of backup data every month, automatically for life, even if you eventually cancel your subscription entirely.

Pricing varies a bit by region and currency and depending on how long you purchase, so check out their current rates on the Holafly Plans page. However, as an example, their unlimited plan is only $55 USD per month for 1 year, which is a lot cheaper than any US based carrier charges for their base option that includes unlimited 5G data overseas.  You can get monthly, quarterly, or yearly plans. Quarterly plans save approximately 10% while yearly plans save 15%.

Classic Holafly eSIMs are built for a single trip. You pick a country or region (there are 200+ available), choose how many days of data you need, and it covers just that trip. If you’re flying to Portugal for two weeks, a classic eSIM is still the simpler, cheaper option. It’s a one-and-done purchase. If you only take a couple trips per year then this is still the best option.

On the other hand, if you’re a digital nomad who is gone from your home country most of the year and moving between different countries all the time, then the Holafly Plans fly is the much better option. It’s a standing global subscription designed for people who are more or less always somewhere. Instead of buying a new eSIM every time your itinerary changes, you have one number, one connection, one bill that follows you across borders indefinitely. Plus, you can get a phone number, unlimited data, and a hotspot. In the US, if you were to get T-Mobile’s plan, which caps data and is the closest thing we have to “affordable” international plans, you’re going to spend over $100 USD a month (I know because I used to use this plan!). Holafly Plans costs less and gives you more.

Is Holafly Plans different from a regular Holafly eSIM? Yes. Regular Holafly eSIMs are one-off purchases for a single trip or destination (200+ available). Holafly Plans is an ongoing global subscription for people who are more or less always traveling.

Does Always On cost extra? No. It’s included automatically with every Holafly Plans subscription and keeps giving you 1GB of backup data a month across 150+ destinations even after you cancel.

Can I switch between Light and Unlimited? You’d need to cancel your current plan and purchase the other tier — there’s no in-place upgrade yet.

What if I don’t like it? Holafly backs Plans with a 6-month refund policy, which is more generous than most eSIM companies offer.
