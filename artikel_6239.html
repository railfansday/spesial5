SeaWorld Parks Promo Codes November 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Marriott Coupon CodesYou never forget your first dolphin encounter—and for many kids across the country, that often happens at SeaWorld. The beloved oceanarium puts the “amusement” in amusement parks: orcas leaping in the air, floorless rollercoasters named after sea monsters, and even aquarium sleepovers. With parks in Orlando, San Diego, San Antonio, and Abu Dhabi, SeaWorld’s aquatic allure has no bounds. SeaWorld is a top choice when it comes to taking the family out for some educational fun.

Established in 1964 by four UCLA graduates, SeaWorld began in San Diego with 22 acres, six attractions, and a handful of sea lions and dolphins (beloved orca whale Shamu arrived at the party a year later). In the early 2000s, the parks expanded with thrill rides and rollercoasters, as well as Discovery Cove, allowing visitors to interact with marine animals like bottlenose dolphins. Over the course of its history, SeaWorld has rescued over 40,000 animals and, through the SeaWorld and Busch Gardens Conservation Fund, donated over $19 million to global projects, from restoring coral reefs to preventing coastal erosion. If you want your little ones to have a whale of a time, here are the best SeaWorld Parks promo codes, discounts, and deals.

Combine it with the All-Day Dining option for $40, and you won't have to worry about buying meals. SeaWorld expresses its gratitude for the service of military members through the Waves of Honor program, which grants any US active-duty military, activated or drilling reservist, or National Guardsman one complimentary admission per year, and tickets for as many as three direct dependents. Military personnel can also enjoy a 50% off discount on up to six SeaWorld Orlando single-day tickets, plus a Silver Annual Pass at SeaWorld Orlando (unlimited admission with no blockout dates for 12 months) for $161 instead of $279. Those who can take advantage of this offer will need to verify their identity via ID.Me.

For military personnel who have completed their service, SeaWorld is offering 50% off single-day tickets at SeaWorld Orlando, plus the same Silver Annual Pass discount that active-duty military members get to enjoy. To take advantage of this offer, verify with ID.Me.

If you’ve got a preschooler, give them the gift of maximum aquarium time. Preschool Cards are limited to Texas residents only.

Because SeaWorld is dedicated to research and education, the theme park offers certified K–12 teachers in Florida free admission. The SeaWorld Florida Teacher Card is valid for unlimited admission to SeaWorld Orlando through September 2, 2027. All teachers have to do is pre-register on ID.Me with a Department of Education (DOE) number. Plus, Florida Teacher Card holders have the option to add Aquatica Orlando for only $49.99.

If you find yourself visiting the dolphins often, consider investing in an annual pass for the lowest cost per visit. Annual passes often include perks like free guest tickets and discounts on food and merchandise. During the sale, prices for passes are reduced for new members: Save up to 15% on the Bronze Pass, 20% on Silver, 25% on Gold, and 30% on Platinum. To make sure your SeaWorld discount applies correctly, verify the expiration date; many SeaWorld coupons are time-sensitive. Check if the code is park-specific (for example, a SeaWorld Orlando promo code may not work for San Diego). And finally, ensure you are not trying to stack, let’s say, an AAA discount with a military offer, as most SeaWorld discounts cannot be combined.
