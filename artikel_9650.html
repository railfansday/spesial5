Spiced Cranberry Cherry Cobbler

This spiced cranberry cherry cobbler combines sweet cherries and tart cranberries with a warmly spiced buttery biscuit topping. Orange zest, cinnamon, and nutmeg add cozy flavor, while a little brown sugar gives the tender biscuits a hint of sweetness. The fruit filling thickens as it bakes, bubbling up around the golden fluffy topping. Serve it warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream for a comforting dessert that's especially welcome during the fall and winter months.
