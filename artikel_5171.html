Tokentus Investment CEO Oliver Michel expects XRP to enter another bullish phase around February or March 2027.

Michel says he could sell his entire XRP holdings if the anticipated rally reaches a major market peak.

XRP is trading near $0.95, with the $0.90–$0.92 demand zone emerging as a critical support area.

XRP is showing early signs of stabilization after an extended correction, but its recovery remains fragile below the $1 psychological level. A fresh outlook from Tokentus Investment CEO Oliver Michel has shifted attention toward the token’s longer-term prospects, with the executive expecting a potential bullish phase around February or March 2027.

Michel’s outlook comes as XRP trades near $0.95, leaving investors to assess whether the current consolidation is forming a base for the next rally or whether further downside remains before a meaningful recovery.

Oliver Michel, CEO of Tokentus Investment AG, believes XRP could experience another significant bullish phase around February or March next year. According to his recent comments, Michel expects the next market peak to create an opportunity to exit his XRP position. He indicated that he could sell his entire holdings if the anticipated rally reaches that stage.

Michel has previously identified XRP as his largest cryptocurrency investment. However, he has not disclosed a specific selling price or confirmed the exact size of his holdings.

His forecast places early 2027 on investors’ radar, although the timing remains an individual market expectation rather than confirmation of an approaching price reversal.

XRP has experienced considerable selling pressure during November, with the token losing the $1 psychological level and struggling to attract sustained buying interest.

Michel’s February–March forecast suggests he expects the current weakness to eventually give way to a stronger market cycle. However, several factors will determine whether that scenario develops.

Broader crypto-market liquidity, Bitcoin’s direction, institutional demand and developments across the XRP ecosystem will remain important catalysts. A sustained recovery in these areas could improve XRP’s prospects heading into 2027. Conversely, continued market weakness could delay the anticipated bullish phase.

XRP is trading near $0.95, with its daily chart showing early stabilization after a prolonged decline. The immediate demand zone sits around $0.90–$0.92, where buyers are attempting to establish support.

However, XRP remains below several important moving-average resistance levels, keeping the broader technical structure bearish. A sustained move above $1.00–$1.06 could provide the first meaningful indication of improving momentum. Further resistance appears around $1.13, $1.20 and $1.29.

A sustained recovery above $1.06 could strengthen the bullish structure and bring $1.13 into focus. However, a daily breakdown below $0.90 could expose XRP to another decline toward $0.85.

Michel’s forecast has introduced a potential recovery timeline, but XRP still needs to establish stronger price support before investors can consider the correction over.

The first important signal would be sustained buying around $0.90, followed by a recovery above $1.00. Improving institutional demand and a broader crypto-market rebound could provide additional support. For now, the technical picture remains cautious. XRP’s ability to defend $0.90 and reclaim $1 will be crucial in determining whether the token can build a stronger foundation ahead of the February–March 2027 rally anticipated by Michel.
