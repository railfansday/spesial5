XMR fell more than 20% on November 26, briefly touching around $430 after KuCoin announced its delisting.

KuCoin will close four XMR spot markets on November 30, with withdrawals scheduled to remain open until December 31.

MEXC has also removed XMR spot and futures markets, while Eigenwallet's atomic swaps offer another exchange route.

Monero took a sharp hit on November 26, with the KuCoin Monero delisting announcement landing as XMR suffered its biggest single-day decline since June 4.

The timing is hard to ignore. But one exchange announcement doesn’t explain every sell order.

KuCoin confirmed it will close four XMR spot markets “XMR/USDT, XMR/USDC, XMR/ETH and XMR/BTC” at 08:00 UTC on November 30.

Other services face separate cutoffs. XMR Flexible Savings ends November 27 at 12:00 UTC, while Convert closes November 30 at 03:00 UTC. Trading bots stop at 06:00 UTC, followed by spot trading at 08:00 UTC.

Withdrawals are scheduled to remain available until December 31 at 08:00 UTC. Deposits are already closed. KuCoin says open orders will be cancelled when trading ends, while Flexible Savings balances and accrued earnings will move to Funding Accounts.

The exchange also warns withdrawals could be suspended earlier under certain project-related circumstances. It hasn’t provided a detailed reason for the removal.

KuCoin isn’t the only venue cutting Monero access. MEXC previously announced the removal of XMR spot pairs and later issued a separate notice covering XMR and DASH perpetual futures.

Those are different products. Spot markets provide access to the underlying asset, while perpetual contracts offer derivatives exposure.

Earlier Monero research from TRM Labs described liquidity becoming concentrated across fewer venues following exchange restrictions. That context matters, but it doesn’t establish how much of Thursday’s sell-off came from KuCoin.

Monero’s official account also announced an Eigenwallet release supporting Bitcoin and Monero, including native atomic swaps. These swaps allow users to exchange the assets without depositing both with a conventional exchange.

Still, a new trading route doesn’t automatically replace the depth of removed order books. The KuCoin Monero delisting leaves XMR facing a practical liquidity question as the deadlines approach.
