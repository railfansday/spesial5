If you’re picking up any gifts that require AA batteries, it’s not a bad idea to buy a set you can keep using long after the holidays. Thankfully, you can currently get a 12-pack of Zepath’s rechargeable 3,000mWh AAs on Amazon with a charger for $23.99 ($8 off) for Black Friday, which is a new low. If you don’t need as many, Amazon is also selling an eight-pack under a different name for just $15.99 ($4 off).

My colleague Sean Hollister tested Zepath’s higher-capacity 3,600mWh batteries against competing lithium-ion AAs in April, and he picked them as his top choice. In his tests, they even outlasted Panasonic’s pricier Eneloop Pros in both a Game Boy Advance and a high-powered flashlight. Zepath also says the batteries deliver a steady 1.5V until they’re nearly empty and hold their charge well when not in use.

The best part about Zepath’s rechargeables, though, is how you charge them. Instead of relying on a multi-headed USB-C cable that plugs into each cell, you simply drop them into the included clamshell charging case, like a pair of AirPods or a similar set of wireless earbuds. The case also charges via USB-C, so you can top off eight batteries at once with a single connection in about two and a half hours.
