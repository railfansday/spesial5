TMW: Milan could replace Estupinan with Atalanta youngster Bernasconi – the details

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

AC Milan are looking for an upgrade on the left flank, and they have a rising Atalanta star in their sights as per a report.

According to TMW (via PianetaMilan), a target that Milan pursued last summer could become a hot topic again this winter. However, there is competition to overcome, and the pursuit hinges primarily on the future of Pervis Estupiñán.

Milan had a name already circled in red last summer: Lorenzo Bernasconi. The 22-year-old left-back has established himself as a starter with Atalanta, and the Rossoneri have been monitoring his development closely.

The management had already attempted to assess the possibilities of a deal during the last summer transfer window. The player’s possible arrival at Milanello, however, was tied to the sale of Estupiñán which did not happen.

The situation could change in January, when the directors – including the incoming Markus Krösche – will be called upon to reassess the squad composition and the left-back hierarchy. Estupiñán’s performances have so far been less than convincing, and a possible departure cannot be ruled out.

The decision to start Yunus Musah at left-back against Genoa shows Ruben Amorim doesn’t have full faith in the more natural options there, so for this reasons Bernasconi’s name could rise to the top of the list of priorities again.

The problem for Milan is the competition: Roma have also followed the Italian’s development with interest, identifying him as a potential reinforcement for the left flank. The Giallorossi bid €15m for him during the 2026 winter transfer window, but it was turned down.

Newcastle have registered made an interest in the full-back, having reportedly observed him several times through their scouts. With Bernasconi under contract until 2030, it will likely take a figure of €20-25m to convince La Dea to come to the negotiating table.
