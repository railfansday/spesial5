Broncos vs. Panthers: Live Fan Discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Denver Broncos are in first place in the AFC West for the first time this season after taking down the Kansas City Chiefs last week. Now they have to keep it. That starts today on the road against a Carolina Panthers team they should be able to beat, but nothing is a given in the NFL. They have to go out an execute.

Kickoff is set for 11:00 a.m. Mile High time on Sunday, November 8, 2026 at Bank of America Stadium in Charlotte, North Carolina. You can watch on CBS affiliates. The game will be called by Ian Eagle (play-by-play), Ross Tucker (analyst), and Evan Washburn (sideline). You can also check local Broncos radio network affiliate stations.

Download the SB Nation app on iOS or Android.

This could be a 'let down' game. I called it a 'trap' game at first, but realized that is used when a team is looking ahead and not focusing on the game right in front of them. That's not the case with the bye week, so I think the term I should have used was the 'let down' game. Coming off a huge, emotional home win against a bitter division rival to play a team everyone expects you to roll. That is the 'let down' to a T. While I expect the score to be close, I think Denver pulls away and keeps their win streak alive.
