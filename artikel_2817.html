Cardinals add QB Joey Aguilar off Jaguars' practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

After trading quarterback Jacoby Brissett, and then losing his replacement Gardner Minshew to a potentially season-ending elbow injury, the Cardinals are bringing in quarterback reinforcements.

Joey Aguilar will join the Cardinals’ 53-player roster from the Jaguars’ practice squad, according to Jeremy Fowler of ESPN.

The 25-year-old Aguilar was the starting quarterback for the Tennessee Volunteers last season and spent a total of seven years in college football, with two at City College of San Francisco, two at Diablo Valley College and two at Appalachian State before transferring to Tennessee.

The only healthy quarterback in Arizona right now is rookie Carson Beck. Josh Johnson is expected to join the Cardinals as well, and Aguilar will presumably be the third quarterback for the Cardinals.
