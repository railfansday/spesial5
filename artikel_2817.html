Kellen Moore has run out of excuses in New Orleans

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

How many more embarrassing losses do the New Orleans Saints have to suffer before it's time to move on from Kellen Moore? Sunday's 20-17 loss to the Carolina Panthers at the Caesars Superdome should have been the final straw.

This was a game the Saints had every opportunity to win. Carolina committed an incredible 17 penalties for 152 yards, compared to just three penalties for 46 yards by New Orleans. The Panthers repeatedly shot themselves in the foot, yet the Saints couldn't take advantage of the opportunities in front of them.

Instead, New Orleans dropped to 3-6 and watched Carolina take sole possession of first place in the NFC South.

The Saints managed just 222 total yards, couldn't establish a running game even with Travis Etienne Jr. back and scored zero points in the first half. The running backs combined for just 24 yards on 17 carries, leaving Tyler Shough to shoulder far too much of the offensive responsibility.

At some point, the excuses have to stop. Injuries, personnel issues and growing pains can only explain so much. Moore is in his second season, and the same problems that plagued this team last year continue to show up every Sunday.

And now, it's starting to regress his promising young quarterback.

The Saints have struggled with preparation, offensive consistency, overall execution and closing out games during Moore's tenure. Those are the habits of a poorly coached football team, and Moore has repeatedly failed to find answers.

New Orleans needs a change. Sunday's loss was another reminder that this current regime just isn't getting the job done. The Saints cannot keep wasting opportunities in a division that remains wide open.

It's time for the Saints to stop pretending things are going to get better without making a change.
