A.J. Brown, TreVeyon Henderson, Hunter Henry did not practice on Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Patriots have a lengthy injury report, with several key players remaining out of practice on Thursday.

Receiver A.J. Brown (ankle/knee), running back TreVeyon Henderson (illness), and tight end Hunter Henry (foot) were listed as non-participants for the second consecutive day.

But cornerback Marcus Jones (foot) was added to the injury report as another DNP.

Safety Brenden Schooler (hamstring) was also added to the report as a limited participant.

Defensive tackle Christian Barmore (illness) was upgraded from DNP to full.

Safety Kevin Byard (chest), cornerback Carlton Davis (neck), receiver Mack Hollins (calf), outside linebacker Gabe Jacas (knee), linebacker Dre’Mont Jones (shoulder/knee), offensive tackle Morgan Moses (foot), and offensive lineman Greg Van Roten (hamstring) all remained limited.

And quarterback Drake Maye (right shoulder) remained full.
