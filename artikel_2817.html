List: 2026 Mitchell-area all-conference high school volleyball selections

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Nov. 27—MITCHELL — For their outstanding seasons during the fall, area high school volleyball players were recognized by their local conferences.

A summary of all the area players selected is listed below, sorted by conference.

For the second season in a row, Bridgewater-Emery senior standout Oakley Weber was selected as the Cornbelt Conference's MVP for volleyball.

Weber leads the all-Cornbelt first team and is joined by teammate and senior Lily Hilton, Freeman seniors Rylee Peters and Claire Loofbourrow, Irene-Wakonda sophomore Reagan Rudd and Hanson senior Ava Doyle.

The Cornbelt second team selections included Freeman junior Libby Ammann and sophomore Cadence Hofer, Hanson senior Alivia Bender, Viborg-Hurley sophomores Kaisley Knudsen and Tatum Hauger, plus Irene-Wakonda sophomore Bella Healey.

Area third-team selections included Bridgewater-Emery senior Macie Weber and senior Summer Koch, Howard senior Reese Rudebusch and Hanson senior Jozlynn Tegethoff and junior Brooklyn Schroeder.

Area honorable mention selections included Bridgewater-Emery sophomore Tenley Jatczak, Freeman sophomore Landrei Baker, Howard sophomore Kennedy Charles, Hanson sophomore Avery Squires, Menno senior Kylie Guthmiller and Canistota senior Katelyn Tieszen.

B-E, Freeman and Irene-Wakonda all finished 6-1 in the Cornbelt standings at season's end.

Five schools were represented on the all-Southeast South Dakota Conference first team, with Wagner putting a pair of selections on the honor squad.

The Red Raiders' Ravyn Medricky and Hannah Holzbauer were both selected to the first team, along with Platte-Geddes' Kori VanDerWerff, Winner's Payton Sargent, Parkston's Keeara Oakley, and Miller's Hailey Werdel.

Members of the second team included Mount Vernon/Plankinton's Madalynn Lorang, Wagner's Braxton Nedved, Parkston's Berkley Ziebart, Burke's Emmy Spencer, Platte-Geddes' Kolbi Hoffman and Miller's Emma Pugh

Third team players included Winner's Alice Wilson, Gregory's Dani VanDerWerff, Bon Homme's Aubry Williams, Chamberlain's Jayde McQuirk, Burke's Emily Diekmann and Miller's Bailey Brueggeman.

Sanborn Central/Woonsocket senior Addison Bartholow led the all-281 Conference first-team selections after a standout season.

She was joined by Wolsey-Wessington senior Shae Uttecht, senior Maria Nelson and freshman Preslie Jensen, along with Highmore-Harrold junior Jada Baloun and Hitchcock-Tulare senior Olivia Knox on the first-team squad.

SCW also landed sophomore Kaidence Fridley on the all-281 second team. Additional selections to the second team included H-T senior Emmerie Arthurs and junior Keilee Schone, Highmore-Harrold senior Lainee Ping, W-W senior Marley Boomsma and Sunshine Bible Academy junior Ciera Mickelson.

Additional area selections to the honorable mention team include SCW senior Hudson Fouberg and junior Ellie Hofer, along with Wessington Springs senior Kendall Kolousek.

After strong seasons, Avon and Wagner led the way in the Little Missouri Valley Conference with four all-conference selections each.

Avon's contingent included seniors Rilyn Thury and Jordyn Voigt, junior Kaydence Metzger and sophomore Brielle Voigt. For Wagner, the all-conference selections included seniors Ravyn Medricky, Braxton Nedved and Hayden Knudsen, and junior Hannah Holzbauer.

Bon Homme and Tripp-Delmont/Armour each had three selections. The Cavaliers were represented by seniors Jersie Schonebaum and Cati Uecker and sophomore Aubry Williams. TDA was led by seniors Mia Reiner and Sara Bertram and junior Ryenn Loneman.

Additional selections included Scotland senior Braelyn Fischer and sophomore Campbell Mogck and Andes Central/Dakota Christian senior Audrey DeHaan and freshman Sophia Nelson.

Winner had four all-conference selections to lead the Big Dakota Conference honor team at the end of the 2026 season.

The Warriors' selections included senior libero Alice Wilson, junior outside hitter Payton Sargent, junior outside hitter Seventh Zeigler and sophomore setter Josie Corkle.

Chamberlain had one all-Big Dakota pick, with sophomore setter Jayde McQuirk being tabbed for the team.

Additional selections to the first team included McLaughlin junior Skye White Mountain, Mobridge-Pollock freshmen Bentley Bauer and Hayleigh Schlomer, Stanley County senior JoRayah Walker, and Todd County senior Bree White Hat, sophomore Caisley Brady and libero Destiny Macias.

In the Big East Conference, McCook Central/Montrose has three selections to the honor team, joining Chester for the most on the 14-member first team.

For MCM, senior Ella DeKnikker and juniors JoLynn Deters and Jaida Skyberg were each selected. MCM also had honorable mentions in senior Nora Gordon and junior Khloie Klinkhammer.

Big East Conference champion Chester had senior Jacy Wolf and sophomores Lizzie Peterson and Kaytee Wold selected to the first team. The Flyers were 7-0 in league play, while MCM was 6-1 and Sioux Valley was 5-2.

Additional selections included Sioux Valley sophomore Macy Licht and freshman Kinsley Sapp, Baltic senior Kaiya Lenderts and junior Issie Clark, Parker/Marion sophomore Maliyah Nankivel, Garretson senior Vivian Johnson, Flandreau senior Nora Hamilton and Beresford senior Kate Degen.

Avon and Corsica-Stickney each had three players named to the all-Great Plains Conference honor squad, plus one honorable mention apiece.

Seniors Rilyn Thury and Jordyn Voigt and sophomore Brielle Voigt were picked from the Pirates, while juniors Kinley Spaans and Hadynn Johnson and sophomore Remi Nelson made it from the Jaguars. Avon junior Kaydence Metzger and C-S sophomore Faith Plamp got the honorable mention nods.

Burke placed senior Emmy Spencer and junior Breckynn Pistulka on the all-GPC team, and sophomore Izaria Koenig was an honorable mention. For Tripp-Delmont/Armour, senior Mia Reiner and sophomore Ryenn Loneman were named all-conference, with senior Sara Bertram as an honorable mention. ACDC rounded out the area programs with two all-GPC picks in senior Audrey DeHaan and freshman Sophia Nelson, as senior Marin Bultje was an honorable mention.

Scotland sophomore Campbell Mogck was picked to the honor team, and senior Braelyn Fischer was an honorable mention.

Gayville-Volin, which placed third at the Class B state volleyball tournament, headlined the other all-conference selections. Maia Achen was the GPC MVP, while fellow senior Aliza Westrum and junior Paisley Dimmer joined Achen on the all-GPC team. Jolie Westrum, another Raider senior, received an honorable mention nod.
