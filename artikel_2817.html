High School Football Scores: USA TODAY Sports Super 15, Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The 2026 high school football postseason has arrived for many states, while others are just about to wrap up their regular seasons.

Regardless of where the seasons stand right now, all the games matter more as the stakes rise entering the month of November. Week 12 of the high school football season featured some big-time matchups for ranked teams, with higher stakes, as a loss could mean the end of the season.
