Heroes, zeroes from Jets’ win over Chargers: David Bailey has his way with fellow rookie

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Heroes, zeros and the full blitz from the Jets' 27-24 win over the Chargers on Sunday:

Jets quarterback Geno Smith was sharp in this one. He completed 17 of 21 passes for 176 yards and three touchdowns. At one point, he completed 14 straight passes.

Chargers rookie right tackle Travis Burke had a rough time trying to block Jets star David Bailey. He was not responsible for Bailey on every big play he had, but most of them. Bailey had two sacks, a forced fumble and hit Justin Herbert's arm to force an incompletion.

Los Angeles Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert is tackled by New York Jets safety Dane Belton, left, and linebacker David Bailey on Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. CHRIS TORRES/EPA/Shutterstock

Sterling Shepard scored two touchdowns in a game for the first time in his NFL career Sunday, and this is the first time he has scored multiple touchdowns in a season since 2020. Shepard was elevated off the practice squad for this one and made that move look smart.

The Jets went 3-for-4 in the red zone, an area where they have struggled all year but not Sunday.

"We've got a long flight home. Man, this flight is going to feel good. We're ready to get on to next week. We really are."
