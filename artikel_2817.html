Pochettino says he faced 'resistance' as USMNT coach for not being American

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

CINCINNATI — Mauricio Pochettino has admitted he felt "resistance" during his tenure as U.S. men's national team coach because he is not American.

Pochettino will coach his final game with the USMNT on Tuesday, Nov. 17, as his side faces Haiti in the second leg of the Concacaf Nations League quarterfinal.

The Argentine will depart after the game to take over Manchester United.

Pochettino arrived in the fall of 2024, taking over from former USMNT player Gregg Berhalter. With the exception of Jurgen Klinsmann, every USMNT head coach over the previous 30 years had been American.

Speaking at his final pre-match press conference as USMNT head coach, Pochettino admitted that it wasn't always easy coming into American soccer as an outsider.

"I know how difficult it is in that country to make soccer flourish when it's not the first sport in the country," he said on Monday, Nov. 16.

"Of course, maybe I didn't feel the support that, in my head, I was expecting when I arrived. Because it's true that there always was this type of thing, the ego, something that is bad for humanity. Because when I arrived, I wanted to meet these guys. I wanted to see and to listen. But OK, when this type of thing happens, it's like, 'Ah, this foreign guy that came from abroad, what is he going to teach us? He's going to hear what he wants to hear. He doesn't know the culture of soccer,' all these types of things.

"But no bad feelings, because I understand why they behave in this way. I promise, if I am in Argentina and someone comes there, I'm going to be very open and flexible to provide everything to be successful. The resistance to not being American, we suffered a little bit. That is true."

Pochettino did not elaborate on who exactly he felt resistance from. But he added that he and his staff will be more than happy to help whichever coaches takes his place.

"We are going to be open to help," he said. "We are going to be more than happy because with the people that you love, you want the best. And we really love this organization and the players that deserve the best."
