Man Utd crisis: Michael Carrick sack expected as club chief raises concerns over INEOS

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Michael Carrick's future is under threat at Manchester United. -Credit:Getty Images

Manchester United find themselves in full crisis mode as the international break arrives. The Red Devils head into the two-week hiatus rooted in 18th place in the Premier League table, enduring their worst-ever start to a season.

Michael Carrick's side salvaged a point in a thrilling 4-4 draw against Aston Villa on Sunday, courtesy of Patrick Dorgu's 96th-minute equalizer. However, the Dane's header has done little to quell the growing unrest at Old Trafford, with Carrick's position remaining very much up in the air.

Questions surrounding the Englishman are inevitable, with one potential strategy regarding his future role now emerging. Meanwhile, United supporters have been put on notice to brace themselves for further misery under Sir Jim Ratcliffe.

Here, Express Sport examines the two most recent developments coming out of the embattled club....

Manchester United are reportedly considering bringing in a new coach to strengthen Carrick's backroom staff at Old Trafford, should the head coach retain his position. The 45 year old is under significant pressure given United's historically dismal start to the campaign.

Talks among the club's hierarchy, led by director of football Jason Wilcox, are set for Monaco on Tuesday, with Carrick's future anticipated to be a key discussion point. Among the proposals to turn around United's fortunes is the appointment of Bayern Munich assistant coach Aaron Danks.

The 43 year old Englishman is a crucial part of Vincent Kompany's coaching staff in Germany and previously worked under Carrick at Middlesbrough. Danks' relationship with the former Manchester City captain dates back to his stint at Anderlecht, before he departed Belgium to join Aston Villa as an assistant under Dean Smith and Steven Gerrard.

Nevertheless, United are anticipated to dismiss Carrick in the coming days.

United fans have been cautioned to prepare for further difficulties under Sir Jim Ratcliffe's stewardship. The billionaire is approaching three years since purchasing a minority stake in the Premier League club.

While many supporters initially regarded the 74 year old's arrival as a positive step in the club's development, United have remained without silverware, with frustration becoming a constant presence at the Theatre of Dreams.

An unnamed former Premier League executive has suggested that Ratcliffe's reluctance to accept counsel will keep United struggling for years to come. They told ESPN: "I don't think Jim listens to many people.

"In my experience, it takes about five years for owners to learn their lessons and take some responsibility. That's when humility starts to land. Up until year three or four, they are still putting the blame on what they inherited."
