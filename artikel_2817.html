The Brooklyn Nets have had a poor start to the 2026-27 season, currently sitting at 3-10 about a month into the campaign. Despite this, Michael Porter Jr. has continued to put up some impressive stat lines and could potentially be a trade target down the line for contending teams.

Over the years, Porter Jr. has become known for a series of unfortunate remarks during various podcast appearances, and that trend continued with a recent episode of his "Curious Mike" podcast.

Porter Jr.'s guest was influencer Sommer Ray, and the two discussed whether or not they would stay with their significant others if the person was subjected to an extreme event, such as a fire or major car accident, that left them physically disabled.

"I always say, if that happened to me, my girl, something crazy happens to her, I'm going to be by her side in the moment, but there's going to be a slow (exit)," he said, per NBACentral on X, formerly Twitter.

Porter Jr. clarified that this would only apply if he were dating someone, not if he were married to them.

Still, fans in the comment section were not thrilled with the sentiment.

'I'm not saying he's dumb, dumb, but he got some layers of dumb dust on him. Functional basketball dumb," wrote one fan.

"Nobody is ever gonna trade for this dude unless he has a stay away from podcast in his contract extension clause," added another.

This is far from the first time that MPJ has caused a stir with comments said on a podcast microphone, and it doesn't look like he's planning on stopping anytime soon.

In any case, the Nets will next hit the floor on Wednesday evening at home vs the Chicago Bulls.
