The Browns have a 13-7 all-time record against the Saints.

The last time these two teams met was earlier this season in Cleveland on November 17, 2024, when the Saints destroyed the Browns 35-14. It was much closer than the final score indicated for much of the game. In fact, it was tied 14-14 heading to the final quarter, as Jameis Winston threw for 395 yards. Derek Carr was efficient for the Saints, but the killer was actually when Taysom Hill came in to run. Hill had a 33-yard touchdown run and a 75-yard touchdown run in the fourth quarter, making it three scores on the day for him.

The Browns have lost three in a row against the Saints.
