Panthers LB Bobby Okereke will miss a third consecutive game

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Panthers linebacker Bobby Okereke will miss his third consecutive game with a foot injury. The team ruled him out for Sunday’s game against the Ravens.

The Panthers also won’t have special teams player Isaiah Simmons (knee).

Tight end Darren Waller (knee), tight end Tommy Tremble (knee), running back Jonathon Brooks (groin), offensive lineman Brady Christensen (hamstring) and cornerback Mike Jackson (groin) are questionable.

Brooks, Christensen and Jackson are in their 21-day return-to-practice windows.

Cornerback Will Lee III, who missed the past two games with a hamstring injury, did not receive an injury designation.

Quarterback Bryce Young (foot) had three full practices this week. He, too, is good to go.
