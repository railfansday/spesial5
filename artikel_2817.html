Knicks Bulletin: ‘Sometimes, it just happens’

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Knicks face the Ja-less Blazers today.

New York will host this and four more games before the Knicks pack their bags again.

Here are a few quotes for you sickos sticking to the screen even on a sunny Saturday (as if I wasn't one).

On experimenting with Jose Alvarado alongside Jalen Brunson to give opposing defenses a different look:

"Just trying to give defenses a different look. Getting Jalen off the ball some, because if you think about it, Jose played with Jalen. Tyler was the backup, he was on the floor when Jalen nor Jose was on. Jose was on the floor with Jalen, and it allowed us still to match up and move Jalen around a little bit."

On why playing Alvarado alongside Brunson has reduced Miles McBride's minutes:

"The reality of it is, if Deuce plays and we still try to do that, then Jose is not playing and Jalen is back on the ball. We'll keep trying to mix it up to see what works best for us as we go forward. It could be that or it could be Jose, because it's been two games now I think that we did it, we did it that way a little bit in Toronto, too. We felt it worked well for us."

On the Knicks finishing their road trip 3-1 despite struggling to close out the Timberwolves:

"It's a win. Three and one on the road trip. So we'll take it."

On the Knicks finding their rhythm and improving their defensive execution:

"I feel like we're getting into a good rhythm offensively, defensively. I think you'll see some of the defensive rotations were on point. Obviously, we can be better. But we're continuing to learn, gain experience. Every year is different, every team is different. So I think we're doing that, and I think we're getting better. Whenever you win in the league, you continue to build confidence."

On the Knicks proving they can win games in different ways:

"It shows that we're able to win in different ways. And that's what you have to do in the league. Some days, you're gonna shoot the cover off the ball and you can win by 10, 15, 20. Other games, it might be a possession game and offensively it's not really clicking, not knocking down shots, but you're getting stops defensively and you're able to pull out stops like that. I think it shows that this team can win games in different fashions. And that's the most important thing in the league."

On the Knicks rebuilding confidence by winning close games in different ways:

"For us to have a great road trip like this and win in so many different ways, it [says] a lot about our team. It's great when you can find a way to win. At the end of the day, it's hard to win in the NBA. I've learned that. It's hard to win. And you just have to find a way to win every single night. For us on this road trip to continue to find ways to win, it speaks to this team, and it's the most important thing when you play."

On staying ready despite an unpredictable role in the Knicks' guard rotation:

"When the time comes, you can't really explain the situation sometimes. Sometimes, it just happens. When your number is called you just always gotta be ready, so that's what the NBA has been for me. I'm just glad to be in a situation where I could at least get an opportunity or at least make my voice heard and my game played."

On why Marlon Humphrey's criticism of Baltimore felt personal to him:

"I've been sitting with this one for a couple of days, because Baltimore isn't a talking point to me. It's my blood."

On witnessing the impact of crime and systemic problems while growing up in Baltimore:

"I saw what this city can take from you. I lost friends to the streets, and I watched people I loved get swallowed by a system that was built to swallow them. I know the reputation. I've heard every joke and every headline my whole life."

On accepting Humphrey's apology but demanding more meaningful action:

"Marlon, I see the apology. I'll take it, man to man. But Baltimore has been apologized to its whole life. Apologies don't build anything. Presence does… You don't get to rep Baltimore on Sundays and talk about it like an outsider on Wednesday. Baltimore doesn't make criminals."
