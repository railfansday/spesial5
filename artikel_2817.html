Fan Notes from the Patriots’ loss to the Lions

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

I wouldn't say that Sunday's game was the worst thing to ever happen to Patriots in Germany… but for a certain subset of the United States, it's certainly up there.

The Patriots got destroyed, Drake Maye is possibly out for the season, the defense has given up 80 points over a two-week span, and for all intents and purposes, the 2026 season is over. Maybe we'll get some good news over the bye week, but most of the reliable reporters are saying that Maye's ankle injury is fairly significant and we're looking at multiple weeks.

But Drake Maye getting hurt is just one of many things wrong with this team. Let's talk about them.

I'm going to start off with the posistives, because believe it or not, there are a few. None of them involve the product on the field, but in light of a game like this one some pretty great truths came to light.

The first truth is that any lingering doubts about Drake Maye being a superstar should fully be gone. The Patriots offense looked strong on their opening drive and then didn't do a damn thing for the rest of the game. The offense that Maye had moving was only able to generate two first downs until the fourth quarter. They had seven three and outs. Tommy Cutlets finished with 81 passing yards. Cory Kiner led the team in rushing with 25 yards. Jahmyr Gibbs single-handedly gained more yards than the entire Patriots team. This is an absolutely putrid unit that Drake Maye was able to run significantly well. I'm taking a lot of solace in that.

The second truth is that if your QB is going to suffer a "significant lower leg injury" a broken ankle is the one I want to hear about. Torn ligaments and ruined knees and shattered femurs take forever to heal and there's always a risk of reinjury. But if it's a broken bone, then Maye can just stay off it, have his beautiful wife ply him with her delicious baked goods, and he'll be just fine.

The third truth is that maybe this injury was kind of a blessing. I don't think a single game has gone by in 2026 where the Patriots didn't have at least one starter go down, and it was pretty obvious to me that this wasn't their year. I saw this team making the playoffs for sure, but I didn't have a ton of confidence that they'd be making it back to the Super Bowl. And even if they did, the NFC is light years stronger than the AFC this year, so I wasn't overly enthusiastic about having to go up against whatever wagon that conference sends to the championship game. So if the rumors are true and Maye is going to be out for a while, then you can just shut him down, shut A.J. Brown down, shut everybody down, evaluate the players that will be a big part of the team next year, and focus on a high pick in what promises to be a very strong 2027 NFL Draft class. That's not a horrible way to close out the season.

It especially sucks that the injury came on what I thought was a pretty clear hip-drop tackle, despite what the league says. Double sucks that the Lions lost one of their all-stars on the same play when Aiden Hutchinson Bill Grammatica'd himself celebrating. I don' think that Hutchinson is a dirty player and he was trying to hurt Maye in any way, so I don't see his injury as any kind of karma or anything like that. Tough day for the league losing two great players on one play.

But I did just get to use "Bill Grammatica" as a verb, which I never thought I'd do. So, that's something, I guess.

And you have to love the way the NFL stands behind the decisions the on-field refs make no matter what and only seem to apply the exact letter of the law when it doesn't make them look incompetent. There could be a scenario where a lineman pulled out a samurai sword and straight-up decapitated a player with no flag thrown, then when the commentators go to Gene Steratore for a reaction he'd say "if you take a look, guys, you'll see that there's still some tendons just barely keeping the head on the neck there. You'll also notice that the blood is gushing straight up instead of pouring out and over. That doesn't make it a clean decapitation, so great no-call by the refs."

There's no point discussing the offense. The offense is dead in the water. The only thing I wish I'd seen yesterday was Tommy DeVito paying homage to his home country's soccer team and taking a flop at some point, rolling around holding his ankle like someone just took a sledgehammer to it, Annie Wilkes style. I would have liked to see Behren Morton get activated yesterday. Because why the hell not.

Fun, random fact: though Annie Wilkes (played by the immortal Kathy Bates) shatters Paul Sheldon's legs with a hammer in the movie version of MISERY, in the novel she cuts off his foot with an axe. She also cuts off his thumb and puts it on top of a cake. #TheMoreYouKnow.

What makes absolutely no sense to me is how bad this defense has gotten. It's more or less the same unit from last year. They're all mostly healthy. And they can't rush the passer, cover, or tackle—three things I'd argue comprise an important part of the game. If the Lions had beaten the Patriots something like 16-6 yesterday, I would have been much more OK with this loss. But when Amon-Ra St. Brown, who weighs maybe 200 pounds at six feet tall, is carrying six Patriots defenders into the end zone with him, it speaks to a much larger issue.

And the larger issue parallels almost exactly the things that I have grown very, tired of.

I'm very tired of watching Christian Gonzalez get absolutely smoked at least once per game.

I'm very tired of the defense surrendering multiple 90-plus yard scoring drives.

I'm very tired of A.J. Brown dropping two out of every five passes thrown his way.

And I'm very tired of this pervasive feeling that, for some reason, this Patriots team doesn't really give a crap about this season.

And it's that last one that's most concerning. A team can be banged up and not all that good, but if I got the sense that they genuinely cared about the season, I could live with it. I just can't shake the feeling that the team has kind of punted on the year when they were all in and one of the most likable units ever in 2025.

Why? Is it really the Mike Vrabel offseason stuff? Is that really not sitting well with the team? Maybe. I find that a little hard to believe personally, but who knows. Whatever it is, I feel like the players who are still healthy enough to stand are on their tip toes glancing down south towards Cancun, where they have already booked a flight scheduled to depart on January 11th.

I used to live in Los Angeles. I spent three years there post-college working in all facets of the production industry, not because I had any grad dream of making it big as an actor or director or anything like that, but because I was young, single, and very stupid, and L.A. seems like a great place to be if you check all of those particular boxes. Sadly, I'm now only one of those three things, so my West Coast days are long over. I'm an East Coaster at heart, and I don't really miss LA. all that much, with two notable exceptions. The first is Southern California in February. The second is 10 a.m. football on Sundays. Waking up hungover as hell, stumbling to the kitchen for a Gatorade and a coffee, then crawling back into bed for a few hours to watch football while I assessed the previous night's poor decisions. I don't really miss the hangover parts, but it was nice to wake up to a football game.

So yesterday was a nice little throwback to that. I woke up, let the dog out, got some coffee, turned on the TV, and there were the Patriots, getting ready to dominate. Even better, I wasn't even hungover!

And by "getting ready to dominate," I mean "getting ready to get dominated." I think it's time we all sit down and have a serious talk.

Given the fact that Drake Maye left after the first drive and didn't return, there isn't really much to say about the game itself. So instead I'm going to treat today like Festivus come early and air a few grievances I currently have with the 2026 Patriots.

I feel like the respectful thing for both teams to do yesterday was just run it three times and punt it for the rest of the game to speed it up and get it over with. Not because the Lions owe any team any mercy or anything like that, but when a game is over by the first quarter, you just kind of want to get on with your day.

To that end, another nice thing about yesterday's game is it reminded me of the few years I spent living in Los Angeles post-college. Waking up on Sunday to a 10 a.m. football game was awesome, and then you were free from 1 p.m. on. Pats at 10, then head down to Hermosa Beach to watch the later slate of games. It's nice to have the Patriots game over by 12:30.

Especially when it's a game that represents the end of your season. The sooner you can attack the grieving process, the better.

They're 5-5 coming into the bye. Technically, the season isn't over. But with the Chargers, Bills, Vikings, Chiefs, Jets, Broncos, and Dolphins left to play, how many of those games do youy think they even have a chance of winning without Drake Maye? I think absolute best-case scenario there is three more wins to finish 8-9.

Which is also the absolute worst-case scenario. 8-9 is a great way to end up picking 16th or 17th overall as opposed to the Top 10 pick you'd get with a 5-12 season. So who knows. I'm kind of just talking out of my ass at this point. I guess all we can really do now is wait until we get the official word on Maye from the team and forge on as best we can.
