Rangers star saved by Man Utd man as team secure quarter-final place with stunning comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Rangers midfielder Nico Raskin breathed a sigh of relief after seeing the penalty he conceded just minutes after his introduction to the pitch saved by Manchester United goalkeeper Senne Lammens during Belgium’s thumping 4-1 win over Italy in the Nations League on Sunday night.

Despite starting Belgium’s 2-1 win away over Turkiye on Thursday night, the Rangers star had to be content with a place on the bench against Italy as Mark van Bommel’s side looked to seal a place in the quarter-finals of the Nations League at the Stade Roi Baudouin.

Two points ahead of their visitors ahead of the Group A1 clash on nine points, the game got off to a disastrous start for the hosts when Italy went into an 11th-minute lead when Brentford defender Michael Kayode's cross to Pio Esposito to score 10th international goal in 15 appearances.

However, the lead was short-lived, and Belgium found themselves back on level terms just four minutes later when Brighton wing-back Maxim de Cuyper slammed home to restore parity in Brussels, before centre-back Nathan Ngoy rifled home from close range after a scramble in the box on 22 minutes.

Jeremy Doku then completed the turnaround when he added a third for Belgium just four minutes later, dancing through the Italian defence with stunning footwork before firing a low drive beyond his Manchester City teammate Gianluigi Donnarumma in the visitor’s goal. The strike put his side temporarily top of Group A1, with Turkiye holding France to a 0-0 draw at half-time.

With 10 minutes to spare, Diego Moreira made sure of the win when he neatly placed home his side’s fourth of the game after being teed up by Kevin De Bruyne. However, it was not enough to seal top spot in the group, with Belgium forced to be content with qualification to the Nations League quarter-finals via second place as France ran out 2-1 winners in Paris.

Despite the Group standings being all but confirmed, the action did not cease, with Italy awarded a last-minute penalty after VAR ruled that Raskin had handled Alphadjo Cisse’s shot in the area. The Rangers man had replaced Romeo Lavia just over 10 minutes earlier, but needn’t have worried, as goalkeeper Lammens pulled off an excellent save to deny Esposito a brace.

“If Donnarumma had not been in goal, then we would've scored six,” said new Belgium head coach van Bommel. “I'll be honest, I didn't expect this when we started two months ago, but even before that first match with Italy, I could tell this was a really intelligent group of players. That makes it a pleasure for a coach to work with them.

“These players can anticipate an opponent, are flexible, and can adapt to different situations. Today you saw that reflected. Not only was the idea right, individually many players stepped up too. And ultimately we managed to dominate an opponent like Italy for long periods. That is the most important thing to me."

Belgium will discover their Nations League quarter-final opponents on Friday, November 20, 2026, with the draw set to take place at 11am UK time in Nyon, Switzerland.
