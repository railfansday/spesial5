This is the secret to Noni Madueke's 'immense' Arsenal transformation - expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Noni Madueke has undergone a transformation in his confidence in recent weeks at Arsenal.

That is the opinion of experienced former Tottenham, Aston Villa and Everton scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 24-year-old is enjoying arguably the best form of his Arsenal career so far, and appears to be finally proving the value in his £52million arrival from Chelsea in the summer of 2025.

Madueke had been performing well on the left of the attack before moving to the right following Bukayo Saka's quad injury in October.

There, the England international has looked to become Arsenal's most dangerous attacker, proving key to breaking the deadlock in Saturday's win over Hull City when the rebound of his shot was turned in by Martin Odegaard.

With Saka unlikely to return before December, Mikel Arteta will hope that Madueke can build on his form and become a key man for the Gunners.

Madueke's remarkable performance against Hull before the international break very much summed up the changes he has undergone.

The winger led every player on the pitch in nearly every attacking stat, taking on and beating his man over and over again.

But that was not all - Madueke was getting his defensive work, too, and could be seen repeatedly racing back to help out his defenders.

"I think we've hit on this before, that his form is helped by the players around him," King told Football Insider.

"And I think that's beginning to show that the players around him are starting to believe that he is a decent player.

"He cost a lot of money, but they've certainly got good confidence in him, which seems to be improving his game immensely.

"There's nothing better than a player with confidence, and players around him with confidence."

Though there have been doubts over Madueke's decision-making and execution, his ability has never been in question.

The 24-year-old undoubtedly has the physical and technical attributes to become a leading player for Arsenal.

Few others can match the forward's ability to create space to create chances and take shots himself.

If Madueke can add the final touch more often than not, he could easily become a superstar.
