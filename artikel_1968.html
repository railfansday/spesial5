Expert reveals who is Arsenal's first choice defender as William Saliba makes comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

William Saliba's return from injury will hand Arsenal boss Mikel Arteta a difficult but positive decision to make in his defence.

That is the opinion of experienced former Tottenham, Everton and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

Ezri Konsa made the £55million switch from Aston Villa in the summer transfer window, and has been simply superb.

The 29-year-old was bought to cover for Saliba, who has been missing since the World Cup with a back injury, and has paired with Gabriel seamlessly.

But the France international is now due to return to action after the international break, and will hope to break back into the team.

Saliba made his return for Arsenal's Under-21s on Sunday, and will spend the international break building his fitness.

Saliba's excellent last season will not be forgotten in a hurry. The Frenchman was vital to the season that finally saw Arsenal lift the Premier League.

But Konsa has not put a foot wrong since arriving, and has formed a rock-solid partnership with Gabriel that has kept seven clean sheets in 13 games across all competitions.

"He's Arsenal's first choice at the moment, and that's how football is," King told Football Insider when asked about the ex-Aston Villa man.

"The one thing that Arsenal have done well is that they've got cover on all positions. They've got quality in all positions, and what a lovely headache to have.

"You've got the England international, you've got Saliba coming back. You can't beat competition at a football club for places, and Arsenal have got that.

"There was talk when [Martin] Odegaard was out at one stage, 'what's going to happen to him?' He's worked hard and he's got himself back.

"And now he's the central part of the side again, and an important part of the side. It's a great headache to have 20 players of quality."

With both Gabriel and Saliba suffering injuries last season, Arteta will be grateful for the opportunity to rotate.

It remains to be seen who his first choice will be when Arsenal are faced with a key game, however.

Speaking last week, Arteta said: "“So far we haven’t had that luxury, I wouldn’t call it a problem!

"The moment that they are fit & available, we’re going to have the opportunity to give players [rest] or load the players & to manage congestion in a much better way.”

"Ezri Konsa can play as a full-back, but I think his main position is as a centre-back. He can play right or left & William Saliba is the same with the national team or here at Arsenal, that’s going to be something really positive."
