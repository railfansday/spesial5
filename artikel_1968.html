Eastbourne United fans find another win to talk about for years to come

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

For Eastbourne United it has to be between THAT FA Vase semi-final, beating Crawley Town in the Sussex Senior Cup, or, on the other side of that coin, the following Senior Cup game, where Bognor put seven past Simon Rowland’s men.

Spool forward 12 years, and United had a chance at restoring their good name in West Sussex, again in the Senior Cup.

After a deluge on the way to the game, the United faithful were pleasantly surprised to see a pleasant evening for this step 4 v step 5 encounter.

As the referee got the match underway, it became immediately obvious that Anthony Storey’s boys were giving no quarter at all to the higher league team – Joe Overy running head down at the defence time and again as Charlie Ball, Callum Barlow and James Hull all went close top opening the scoring, with no real penetration from Bognor until the 20th minute, when Zach Robitaille and Dan Hull combined to clear off the line.

On the half hour, Overy decided to go it alone, his shot going just over the bar. Jorge Nones was looking very lively indeed and was unfortunate not to score, the Bognor keeper again earning his corn with a point blank save.

As the teams went in 0-0 at half time, nerves were showing among the Bognor crowd – maybe the lower league team will run out of steam?

Had they done their homework, they would know that Eastbourne United are one of the fittest teams in the SCFL Premier, and so it was that as the teams came out for the second half, if anything United had stepped their game up.

James Hull had picked up a nasty cut in the first half and was replaced by the lively Freddie Jones on the hour. Almost straight away, Overy crossed for Barlow who made no mistake against his old club, to the absolute horror of the home support.

Bognor made a triple change in response, but to be honest did not really look to have an answer. James Stone replaced Barlow a few minutes later, and within minutes Ball broke down the right, his cross finding Stone for a great finish and 2-0. The upset was definitely on the cards.

Alieu Secka saved well from Cal Laycock, but with 5 minutes to go, the lifeline appeared and it was 2-1. United were not rattled – quite the opposite as Jones went straight up the other end and crossed for Overy to make it 3-1.

Alfie Gander replaced Ball as the game moved to 6 minutes of added time. Right at the end Bognor snuck a second goal, but it was too late, it made for an exciting last couple of minutes, but everyone knew that the game was up, indeed as the referee blew the final whistle there was generous applause from the Rocks supporters after a game played in a very good atmosphere.

Eastbourne United are in the quarter-finals and ready for another adventure in the next round.

On Saturday Guernsey are the visitors to the Oval for a 2pm kick-off, another match that will be vital in United’s promotion push.

Eastbourne Town’s Senior Cup interest is over. Sonny Walsh’s goal wasn’t enough to prevent a 2-1 defeat to Lewes at The Saffrons, also on Tuesday night.

But there was better news for Jude Macdonald’s team in the Isthmian South East last Saturday – they drew 1-1 at Sheppey, with Alfie Rogers their scorer. That ended a run of eight league defeats on the trot for Town and took them off bottom spot, now occupied by Horley.

Macdonald’s men host Whitstable tomorrow.
