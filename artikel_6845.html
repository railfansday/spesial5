Ryan Seacrest’s Gigantic ‘Wheel of Fortune’ Salary Was Revealed and It’s About to Floor You

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Today in celebrity paychecks that’ll make your jaw hit the floor, Ryan Seacrest’s alleged Wheel of Fortune salary was revealed and...dear god it’s a lot of money. According to a source who chatted to The Sun (so take this with a grain of salt), “Ryan makes more from Wheel than he does for American Idol. About $18 mil a Wheel season.”

I repeat, in all-caps: $18 MILLION A SEASON.

The source added that “The time commitment to Wheel of Fortune exceeds his schedule for filming Idol, which is why he is paid more for Wheel. He doesn’t make as much as Pat, but when he renews his contract, he’ll get a significant pay bump.”

And please be advised this is simply one of the many ways Ryan makes money. He also—per Forbes—takes home $1 million for Dick Clark’s New Year’s Rockin’ Eve, a rumored $10 million per season of American Idol, and signed a $25 million podcast deal for On Air With Ryan Seacrest.

Honestly, no idea how this man has time to juggle this many jobs, let alone time to deposit all his various paychecks. But either way, Ryan has amassed a gigantic net worth for himself! According to Celebrity Net Worth, he has $500 million to his name, making him one of THE wealthiest people in the industry.
