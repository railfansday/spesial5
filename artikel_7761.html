Former Sean ‘Diddy’ Combs prosecutor will lead Cornell investigation

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

A Cornell University alumna and former federal prosecutor who oversaw the criminal case against hip-hop mogul Sean “Diddy” Combs will serve as outside counsel for the investigation into an alleged gang rape on Cornell’s campus in 2024. (Photo by Shareif Ziyadat/Getty Images)

A Cornell University alumna and former federal prosecutor who oversaw the criminal case against hip-hop mogul Sean “Diddy” Combs will serve as outside counsel for the investigation into an alleged gang rape on Cornell’s campus in 2024.

New York Attorney General Letitia James announced Wednesday that Jacqueline Kelly, who joined Boies Schiller Flexner as a partner last year, will lead an investigative team of attorneys and staff from her law firm and the New York attorney general’s office.

Kelly began her career as an assistant U.S. attorney for the Southern District of New York in 2016 and then became the chief of the Criminal Division’s Civil Rights Unit in 2022, according to a story by Law360.

New York Gov. Kathy Hochul signed an executive order last month appointing James’ office to investigate the allegations by a former student who said she was sexually assaulted by seven men at a fraternity house.
