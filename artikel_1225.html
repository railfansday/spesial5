Confirmed: Juventus name 23-man squad for Omonoia including new Next Gen call-up

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Luciano Spalletti has named a squad of 23 Juventus players for Thursday evening’s Europa League clash against Omonoia, including Stefano Turco, who was recently added to the club’s UEFA squad list and has never previously been called up to the first team.

The Bianconeri will host Omonoia at the Allianz Stadium at 20.00 GMT on Thursday evening.

Their European campaign has got off to a promising start in 2026-27 with three wins and one draw on their record after the first four matches of the season. They will go up against the Cypriot side, who have won two and lost two of their four Europa League games thus far.

Spalletti’s matchday squad of 23 players has recently been confirmed. The absentee list still includes Kenan Yildiz, Khephren Thuram and Manuel Locatelli, as well as Nico Gonzalez and Pierre Kalulu. Edon Zhegrova is not on Juve’s UEFA squad list and is therefore ineligible to feature this evening.

There will be opportunities for a number of Juventus’s lesser-used players against Omonoia on Thursday: Luciano Spalletti confirmed on Wednesday that summer signing Jeff Ekhator is set to earn his first competitive start for the Bianconeri, and back-up goalkeeper Neto is also set to start in place of Guglielmo Vicario.

The Old Lady have also included three Next Gen representatives in their matchday squad on Thursday, namely Arman Durmisi, Stefano Merola and Stefano Turco.

Goalkeepers: 23. Carlo Pinsoglio, 25. Guglielmo Vicario, 33. Neto.

Defenders: 2. Zeki Celik, 3. Bremer, 4. Federico Gatti, 6. Lloyd Kelly, 20. Andrea Cambiaso, 26. Jhon Lucumi, 32. Juan Cabal, 52. Stefano Turco.

Midfielders: 8. Teun Koopmeiners, 12. Douglas Luiz, 22. Weston McKennie, 19. Pape Matar Sarr.

Forwards: 7. Francisco Conceicao, 9. Randal Kolo Muani, 13. Jeremie Boga, 17. Kerim Alajbegovic, 18. Jeff Ekhator, 27. Nick Woltemade, 47. Arman Durmisi, 51. Stefano Merola.
