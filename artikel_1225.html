8 Defenders Man Utd Can Sign For Under £50m in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Mauricio Pochettino's first match in charge of Manchester United couldn't have gone much worse. After spending the past week being reminded by the media of just how poisoned the chalice he was taking from Michael Carrick had become, with warnings about the fate of the seven managers who had tried and failed to turn the club's fortunes around, Sunday afternoon's action was somehow even worse than anyone could have envisaged.

After just eight minutes, the Red Devils were down to 10 men, with Leny Yoro sent off for denying Alexander Isak a clear goalscoring opportunity. And by the 15th minute, Dominik Szoboszlai and Florian Wirtz had taken full advantage of a worryingly fragile defence to put Liverpool two goals ahead. Second-half strikes from Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz then twisted the knife, condemning United to a chastening 4-0 defeat.

The result leaves Pochettino with plenty of fires to put out at his adopted club. United sit 19th in the Premier League with just one win from 11 matches, have suffered the worst defeat by a new manager in 100 years and, alarmingly, have conceded more goals than any other side in European football, with 34.

After spending much of last summer rebuilding their midfield and putting all their eggs in the attacking basket the year before, United are now desperately short of answers at the back. There is a clear need to strengthen their defensive ranks in January before their relegation battle spirals further out of control - and here are eight new faces they should consider, whose Transfermarkt market values have also been listed.

The defender on this list who has been linked with the Red Devils for the longest - and who would likely cost them the most money - is Newcastle left-back Lewis Hall.

Every transfer window seems to bring fresh links between the England international and Old Trafford, but, for one reason or another, no serious move has ever materialised.

With long-standing issues at full-back, Pochettino may want to keep the pressure on the club's top brass to finally get a deal over the line, particularly given that Diogo Dalot has increasingly become the fans' favourite scapegoat in that position.

A more versatile, cheaper alternative to the aforementioned Hall would be to raid Fulham for Antonee Robinson - a player that Pochettino knows well from his time as USMNT boss.

The 29-year-old can play on either flank, and he is just as good at getting forward a she is tracking back and helping out his central defensive partners. Transfermarkt have estimated his market value at just £18.4 million, too.

Several reports have already linked Alex Freeman to United, and interest appears to have ramped up since the Red Devils' humiliating trip to Anfield over the weekend. The 22-year-old is still something of an unknown quantity but, after cutting his teeth with La Liga side Villarreal this season, he's shown impressive traits.

In the 2026/27 La Liga season so far, Freeman - also a USMNT international - has completed 509 of 559 passes (91%) across 715 minutes, ranking in the 94th percentile for successful passes (65 per 90), 90th percentile for line-breaking passes (12.8 per 90), and 91st percentile for chances created (1.45 per 90) among La Liga full-backs.

He also ranks in the 94th percentile for duels won (7.55 per 90), 93rd percentile for aerial duels won (1.89 per 90), and 100th percentile for recoveries (6.97 per 90).

Moving on to some central defenders that Man United could take a look at this January, Antonio Rudiger could bring a level of experience to the backline that it currently lacks. The Real Madrid defender has been out of favour under Jose Mourinho this season, but brings a certain kind of presence not seen among many in his position.

He is out of contract in 2027, but with United needing reinforcements now, they could choose to bring the German in for a cut-price fee in the coming couple of months. He may not be as fast as other defenders on their radar, but he more than makes up for that in intelligience and experience.

Rumour has it that Calvin Bassey is nearing a contract extension at Fulham, and that instantly puts United on the back-foot if they were to negotiate a move for him. But a club of their stature shouldn't be completely put off by that.

A central defender known for his athleticism and ball-carrying abilities, he would fit Pochettino's system, which relies on a high-octane brand of football to succeed. Right now, the likes of Harry Maguire and Matthijs De Ligt struggle to keep up with play - something Bassey continues to prove to have no issue with.

It is believed that Manchester United have made fresh contact with the representatives of Everton defender Jarrad Branthwaite ahead of a possible move. The Red Devils have previously shown an interest in signing the England international, seeing multiple bids rejected back in 2024.

A series of hamstring issues last year may have put off potential suitors, but the Englishman has been abck to his best this season so far, and he will surely be in Thomas Tuchel's plans, should his form continue.

Yet another player United have previously taken a look at without actually making a concrete move is Murillo, who the Red Devils had eyed up a transfer for back in spring, before deciding they already had enough options in their backline.

The Brazilian is another athletic runner suitable for Pochettino's high-intensity tactics, and while he may not be the tallest, he has been a goal threat for fellow relegation battlers Nottingham Forest, and was part of the team that qualified for Europe just two seasons ago.

Italy is perhaps the greatest producer of defenders in football history, with Alessandro Bastoni the most recent example of a top centre-back they boast. If United want a cheaper alternative to the Inter Milan star, they may wish to turn to Atalanta's Giorgio Scalvini.
