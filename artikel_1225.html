He Wants to Join: Man Utd Must Sign Jean-Philippe Mateta Instead of Balogun

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Manchester United will prioritise signing a new striker for manager Mauricio Pochettino in the January transfer window, according to the Times, but they should look closer to home than Monaco striker Folarin Balogun.

Man United's drama-filled season continued against Sporting Lisbon in the Champions League on Wednesday night, in a match that was dominated by the performance of referee Szymon Marciniak.

Senne Lammens had only just saved a penalty when he then gave away another one in the first half, while his mistake late on allowed Luis Suarez to score the equaliser in a 2-2 draw in the Portuguese capital.

In between all of that, the Red Devils were left furious when Amad Diallo's equaliser was controversially ruled out following a foul by Youri Tielemans, before Bruno Fernandes scored a penalty early in the second half.

Fernandes then gave United the lead when his weak shot hit a divot and bounced over Kaique Pereira in the Sporting goal, and there was further drama after Suarez's 80th-minute leveller.

Pochettino's men had another goal disallowed when Patrick Dorgu was adjudged to have fouled Pereira, with Marciniak changing his mind having been sent to the monitor by VAR.

There was also still time for a red card for Youri Tielemans on a crazy night, as the Argentine head coach picked up his first point as United boss.

It was an improved performance from Sunday's 4-0 disaster against Liverpool, and now they must build upon it in the Premier League, starting against Brentford at Old Trafford on Saturday.

In the absence of the injured Benjamin Sesko, who is expected back in action in January, at the earliest, Pochettino decided to start Joshua Zirkzee in the centre-forward role against Sporting.

Zirkzee has found himself out of favour under Ruben Amorim and Michael Carrick previously, and while he may hope things can be different under Pochettino, the report that a new striker is the priority in January suggests it's an early change the former Spurs manager wants to make.

United have been mentioned alongside Balogun who, GMS understands, could be on his way out of Monaco in January, with another report suggesting that Pochettino could ask club chiefs to sign the USA international.

The Times claim that he wants to be reunited with Pochettino, but there is a better option right in front of their eyes, if they are able to land him, in Crystal Palace's Jean-Philippe Mateta.

TalkSPORT reported last month that Mateta is one of the more viable options for United in the winter market, and the player himself is keen on the move.

GMS understands that Pochettino has a clear idea of the type of striker he wants United chiefs to sign in January.

Speaking on the latest episode of our Market Madness podcast, GMS senior correspondent Ben Jacobs said:

Mauricio Pochettino would prefer a defined focal point, someone who is big and strong, someone old-fashioned in many ways.

If the ideal player is like that, and also needs to have Premier League experience, which you would think would be preferential, then there aren't many better options than Mateta.

Balogun has performed well under Pochettino at international level and for Monaco, but there is no evidence that he can score goals in the English top flight.

The Red Devils have also been linked with Al-Ahli's Ivan Toney, who could sign for Tottenham on one condition and is another big striker who has scored plenty of goals in England, but Mateta might be even better.

The striker is 6ft 4in tall, he's a France international, and he has scored 50 Premier League goals in 166 appearances for Palace.

The intriguing thing about the 29-year-old is that, not only has he long been linked with a move away from Selhurst Park, but his contract is also due to expire at the end of the season.

While Palace may have no interest in losing one of their best players as they battle relegation, it could also be their final chance to bring in some money for the Frenchman before he leaves them for free, which is the current direction of travel.

United should at least be trying their luck with Mateta ahead of January, who was described as being 'among Europe's best strikers' by the Opta analyst last year.

Nobody is talking about how bad one Man Utd star is because of Senne Lammens.
