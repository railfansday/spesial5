Where Does Brock Purdy Live? Exploring the Homes of the San Francisco 49ers Quarterback

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

He spends plenty of time at Levi Stadium as the quarterback of the San Francisco 49ers, but where does Brock Purdy live when he’s off the field? The football player was born and raised in Queen Creek, Arizona, and played football at Perry High School in nearby Gilbert. While Purdy left Arizona in 2018 to play at Iowa State University, he’s still fond of his home state. “I love the state of Arizona and everyone that’s been a part of my life,” he told reporters ahead of the 2024 Super Bowl. “And obviously, I’m trying to represent them as best as I can for as long as my career goes.”

At Iowa State, Purdy was a three-time All-Big 12 quarterback and was the winningest quarterback in the school’s history. The University’s Midwest environment suited Purdy. “My favorite part is the people, just because of how down-to-earth and real everyone is,” he told the Iowa Stater in 2024. “Everyone wants to help each other out. There’s no selfishness. I don’t know what it is about the culture in Iowa, but everybody just loves one another authentically. And when you go back, you feel that every time.” Purdy also met his future wife, Jenna, his freshman year at Iowa State. The couple got engaged in 2023 and tied the knot back in Iowa in 2024.

In 2022, the San Francisco 49ers selected Purdy as the final pick of the NFL Draft. This pick typically earns the harsh nickname Mr. Irrelevant, but Purdy quickly proved the moniker wrong, becoming starting quarterback in his rookie season. Now in his fifth season with the 49ers, Purdy is also building a successful life off the field. Read on to learn about Brock Purdy’s homes over the years.

Teammates—and onetime roommates—Alfredo Gutierrez, Nick Zakelj, and Purdy in the locker room in 2022

At the beginning of his NFL career, Purdy lived more like a recent college grad than a pro football player. During his rookie year, Purdy shared a house with offensive linemen Alfredo Gutierrez and Nick Zakelj. The trio even gave out a Christmas card from the household to teammates. In an interview with NBC Sports Bay Area, Gutierrez revealed that Purdy did most of the cooking out of the three and that they spent downtime watching football, movies, and playing Mario Super Slugger.

By 2023, Purdy had only one roommate: Zakelj. “So he and I are still splitting rent,” Purdy told the TODAY Show. “I still drive my Toyota Sequoia. Other than that, it’s pretty simple.”

Purdy with his wife, Jenna, and daughter, Millie Joleen, in 2025. They now have a second child on the way.

Purdy left his shared home in 2024 when he married his wife, Jenna. They now live in a rental home in the Bay Area with their daughter, Millie Joleen, who was born in July 2025. The couple announced in July 2026 that they would soon be welcoming a second child.

The couple focused on making the home family-friendly, adding plenty of storage for their daughter’s toys, and durable furniture for hosting. “Becoming new parents, we really wanted this house to feel like a home where our family is going to continue to grow,” Jenna said in a video unveiling the couple’s living room and bedroom makeovers, which they worked on with Pottery Barn.

In May 2025, Purdy signed a five-year, $265 million deal with the 49ers, making him the fifth-highest-paid quarterback in the league. The hefty pay raise led Purdy to start his real estate portfolio. His teammate, 49ers tight end George Kittle, revealed on a podcast in June of that year that Purdy had purchased a home in Nashville.

Purdy with teammate George Kittle in 2025

“He did purchase a house in Nashville, Tennessee,” Kittle said. “He will be kind of a neighbor to me at some point. That will be pretty fun, because I’ll have a quarterback that can throw to me all the time, that’s actually on my team.” That summer, Purdy was seen training at Kittle’s football compound in Nashville.
