Bayern Munich ready to refuse €300m from Real Madrid for Michael Olise

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Michael Olise case could well become one of the big soap operas of the next transfer windows. For several months, the French international has been on the shelves of Real Madrid, who dream of attracting him to strengthen their offensive sector. Casa Blanca particularly appreciates its technical profile, its ability to eliminate in small spaces and its efficiency in the last meters. A player capable of making a difference and being part of the Madrid project in the long term. Last summer, the Spanish club had already been strongly associated with the Bayern Munich winger. His name regularly circulated in discussions around Florentino Pérez’s potential future recruits. But the Bavarian leaders had never really opened the door to a departure. And for good reason, since his arrival from Crystal Palace, the Frenchman has established himself as one of the most important elements of the Munich squad.

However, Real Madrid has not abandoned this avenue. Michael Olise remains a dream for Madrid leaders, who know that such a recruitment would require considerable resources. Bayern Munich is perfectly aware of this interest and has absolutely no intention of making things easier for its suitor. According to our information, the German club has not responded, for the moment, to requests regarding its player. The situation therefore remains at a standstill, despite numerous speculations about a possible XXL offensive by the Spanish team. Bayern have no intention of parting ways with their French international, still under contract until June 2029. And even a proposal exceeding 250 million euros does not seem likely to shake up its management. The amount of 300 million euros is now mentioned in discussions around the winger’s future. A dizzying sum which obviously does not change the position of the German champions.

Herbert Hainer also provided several details on this matter during an interview given to T-Online. The president of Bayern Munich confirmed that he discussed the Olise case with Florentino Pérez during his trip to Madrid last summer. The two men have enjoyed a friendly relationship for more than 25 years, during Hainer’s time as CEO of Adidas. “Through my former role as CEO of Adidas, I have known Florentino Pérez for over 25 years, and we have a friendly relationship. That is why I was happy to accept his invitation to Madrid, where I gave the speech of praise for the graduates of Escuela Universitaria Real Madrid. Of course, we also talked about Michael Olise”he explained. The Bavarian leader then revealed the position held by his Madrid counterpart regarding the French international. “He assured me that Real Madrid had never had any discussions either with Michael Olise or with his agent. We are relaxed about the situation. Michael Olise’s contract runs until 2029. Our goal is that he stays at FC Bayern for many years to come – so that the question of Real Madrid does not even arise. » A particularly firm speech which reflects Bayern’s desire to cut short speculation.

But it was especially when he was questioned about a possible offer of 250 or 300 million euros that Herbert Hainer showed his club’s determination. The Bavarian president left very little room for doubt about the possibility of departure, even in exchange for record compensation. To support his point, he brought out a historical formula from Karl-Heinz Rummenigge, former manager of Bayern. “I would like to quote Karl-Heinz Rummenigge, who often said during his tenure as CEO: “We are not a selling club, but a buying club.” That hasn’t changed. » Hainer then insisted that financial considerations cannot dictate everything in the management of a workforce. “You can’t always look at things from a purely financial perspective. We also need to consider whether a player suits us – in terms of playing style, personality, character and, of course, on a personal level. Michael feels good here and knows what he has at FC Bayern. » Even with an envelope of 300 million euros, Bayern does not intend to behave like a selling club and intends to keep one of its jewels.
