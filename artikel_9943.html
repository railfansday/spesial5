Release: Roto v0.14.0

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Roto version 0.14.0 is now available on crates.io! This is mostly a bugfix release and most users should be able to upgrade without any problems. The only breaking change is around List::contains and List::index when they are used from Rust.

For this release, I’d like to thank sha90w and @algernon in particular for both reporting and fixing many issues!
