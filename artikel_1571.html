Opinion: Liverpool finally have world class Mo Salah replacement

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Rio Ngumoha is one of the most exciting young players in world football, and the teenager is already one of Liverpool's best players.

Mohamed Salah's departure left a clear gap in the Reds' forward line, and Sunday's win over Manchester United showed exactly why the club were right to trust Ngumoha to fill it.

The England international started on the left wing, with Bradley Barcola on the right and Alexander Isak through the middle.

Being the only one of the front three to cost less than £120 million, expectations on the youngster going into the massive clash were understandably lower, but Rio showed that he is every bit as dangerous as Isak and Barcola.

When Liverpool went 1-0 up against 10-man Manchester United after just 10 minutes on Sunday afternoon many believed that another massive result could be on the cards at Anfield.

Then when Ngumoha assisted Florian Wirtz just three minutes later, that belief grew ten-fold.

The teenager looked a complete player on the left against United, teeing up the German perfectly for his third goal of the season and opening his own account for the campaign.

When Wirtz volleyed home Ngumoha's cross, he made the four-time England international a record breaker.

Ngumoha became the youngest player to ever register a goal involvement in a match between Liverpool and Man United, at 18 years and 85 days old.

It was Rio's third assist of the season for Liverpool, and the youngster looks more than capable of being a regular starter for the Reds.

Ngumoha scoring Liverpool's third topped off the phenomenal performance, even leading Reds' legend Jamie Carragher to say the teenager was the "best player on the pitch" just after the goal.

Sunday's game against United was only Rio's second start in the Premier League this season, but it is very hard to argue the winger doesn't deserve to start more often from here on out.
