Malen sponsors Gittens’ potential move to Roma

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Roma continues to monitor several prospects to strengthen Gian Piero Gasperini’s attack.

Among the first names on the list is Chelsea’s Jamie Gittens: born in 2004, he made his mark with 17 goals and 12 assists between the ages of 18 and 21 at Borussia Dortmund, before falling through the cracks due to injuries and lackluster performances.

According to Corriere dello Sport, Roma is ready to gamble on his relaunch: a loan deal with an option to buy. Napoli are also interested in the Englishman, but Roma has an additional asset: Donyell Malen.

The Dutch striker is very familiar with Gittens, having played alongside him for two and a half years at Borussia Dortmund.

Gittens is going through a tough time at Chelsea, despite the English club’s €56 million investment about a year and a half ago. The 2004-born player has only played 55 minutes this season, which is why he could start elsewhere, far from London.

Roma have been considering him since last season, and Malen, his former teammate, had already spoken highly of the English winger. This detail could accelerate a deal that has been delayed for a few months.
