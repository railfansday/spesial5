Real Madrid rubs its hands for Jacobo Ramón

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Real Madrid could well carry out an excellent operation with Jacobo Ramón. According to newspaper information A.S.the Como central defender is already attracting the attention of several Premier League teams in view of the next summer transfer window. Manchester United are said to be the most interested club, while Chelsea and Liverpool are also following his development closely. Called up for the first time with the Spanish A selection, the 21-year-old player saw his rating rise after notable performances in Italy. A situation which does not leave Madrid leaders indifferent.

And for good reason, Casa Blanca has several levers in this matter. When he left Como for 2.5 million euros, Real Madrid retained 50% of the player’s rights, while negotiating a buyout option estimated at around 10 million euros. While his market value is now estimated at 45 million euros by Transfermarkt, the Merengues can consider a return at a lower cost or benefit from a possible resale. Enough to rub your hands in Madrid, where the Jacobo Ramón file could quickly become a very good financial transaction.
