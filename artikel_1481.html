Opposition insight for Milan-Frosinone: Form, injuries, predicted XI and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

AC Milan are looking to start a Europa League comeback, if you will, in Greece tomorrow against an Olympiacos side who are flying.

From their last trip to Olympiacos, Milan know that getting three points tomorrow night will not be easy. Unfortunately, it comes at a time when they really do need easy points.

In terms of qualifying for the knockout phase, their best hopes are currently finishing in the top 24 and getting through the Round of 16 knockout tie. However, a win on Thursday could open hopes of a top eight finish…

➤ Manager: Imanol Alguacil Barrenetxea ➤ Form (all comps): W-W-D-D-W (most recent) ➤ Top scorer (Europa League): Hezze, Yaremchuk, Gonzalez – 1 ➤ Most assists (Serie A): Freuler, Chiquinho – 1 ➤ Injuries & suspensions: El Kaabi, Yazici, Chiquinho, Puerta, Hezze.

Olympiacos came into the new season having had a mixed 2025-26 campaign under Jose Luis Mendilibar. They finished runners-up to city rivals AEK Athens in the Greek Super League and lost in the Greek Cup quarter-finals, but won the Super Cup and got to the knockout stage play-offs in the Champions League.

In the summer window, the Erythrolefkoi spent over €78m on signings. The headline additions were midfielder Gustavo Puerta from Racing Santander for €16m, centre-back David Carmo rom Nottingham Forest for €9m and striker Armando Gonzalez from Chivas for €8.6m.

They also spent sums between €5-7m on striker Marius Mouandilmadji, right-back Marcus Pedersen, winger Leon Bailey, left-back Nair Tiknizyan, winger Joel Roca and centre-back Zinedin Smajlovic.

The spending was almost totally offset by four big sales. Goalkeeper Konstantinos Tzolakis went to Hull City for €23.3m, winger Andre Luis headed to Kansas City for €15.5m, midfielder Christos Mouzakitis also joined Hill for €15, and full-back Costinha was bought by Brighton for €12.7m.

Mandilibar would only last until September 9 before being replaced by Imanol Alguacil as the new head coach. Faith had been waning since the 2-1 aggregate loss to NEC in the Champions League third qualifying round, and the change came after a 1-1 draw with Volos.

Olympiacos are currently second in the league table with 26 points from 11 games, via eight wins, two draws and one defeat. They have scored 22 goals and allowed only seven, trailing the current leaders Panathinaikos by four points. They are three ahead of AEK, who have a game in hand and a better goal difference.

In the Europa League, they have been exceptional so far. They beat Jagellonia Bialystok 2-1 at home, Marseille 1-0 away, Sparta Praha 1-0 at home and drew 0-0 away with Stade Rennais. That puts them sixth in the table, and within striking distance of top.

In terms of injury news, Olympiacos are expected to be without forwards Ayoub El Kaabi, Yusuf Yazici (who has a Europa League hat-trick against Milan, for Lille) and Chiquino. Midfielders Gustavo Puerta and Santiago Hezze are out, while Leon Bailey is the latest to join the treatment room as per reports.

In defence, a few changes are expected, especially after conceding twice at the weekend. So, Ortega, Saliakas and Restos keep their spots. However, Carmo and Onyemaechi are expected to start.

Moving into midfield, the pivot won’t be touched, with Scipioni and former Bologna man Remo Freuler keeping their spots with Silva starting ahead of them.

It seems Leon Bailey may be given the nod on the right, despite the medical issues, whilst Fortounis will continue on the left of the attacking quartet behind Gonzalez.

Predicted Olympiacos XI vs AC Milan (4-2-3-1): Ortega; Saliakas, Retsos, Carmo, Onyemaechi; Freuler, Scipioni; Bailey, Silva, Fortounis; Gonzalez.

Predicted bench: Stournaras, Georgakopoulos, Smajlovic, Pirola, Toufakis, Pedersen, Martins, Yaremchuk, Tiknizyan, Bruno, Sa.
