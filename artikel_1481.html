Endrick: “I don’t know what will happen in January. Madrid is great, but I want to grow.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Endrick remains one of Roma’s transfer targets ahead of the next transfer window.

Gian Piero Gasperini believes he is the perfect player to complete his team’s attack.

The Real Madrid striker, according to Fabrizio Romano, also spoke about his future: “I will always give everything for Real Madrid because the club comes before anyone else, but with the attention and affection Mourinho shows me, I’m even more motivated to give my all.”

Gian Piero Gasperini dreams of Endrick, and the player hasn’t closed the door on a January transfer. The Real Madrid striker has left all options open for the transfer window: “I’m only 20, so I’ll see how things unfold. I just want to play, be happy, and help my teammates. I don’t know what will happen in January; it’s in God’s hands. I only ask for what’s best for my career. Whatever is destined to happen, will happen.”

The 2006-born player concluded: “I’ll keep my feet on the ground and continue to give my absolute best. Madrid is a wonderful city, but I’ll always do whatever it takes to advance my career.”
