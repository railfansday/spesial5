Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Leading the lineup is the EVO-X5 Pro, GMKtec's flagship desktop AI supercomputer powered by the AMD Ryzen AI Max+ PRO 495 processor, with up to 192GB of memory. Jointly launched by GMKtec and Michael Nordquist, CVP, Client BU at AMD, at IFA 2026, it supports the deployment of large language models with up to 320 billion parameters under suitable configurations.
