Forgive me but sometimes I can't help myself but go back to root causes. Why have you looked at 80+ houses and not been able to buy one yet? Are you uncertain about what you want? It sounds like you put in one offer, do you know why it wasn't accepted? Were there multiple offers and yours was not the best or was your offer under the asking price and the seller declined to lower their price?

Realtors are happy to work with first time buyers, but looking at 80 houses (with a realtor and going to open houses on your own) is a lot. Realtors get paid when a sale closes so at some point they are going to spend less time with a buyer who appears less willing to make a purchase. That's human nature and how a market economy works. Yes you want to be educated but seems like you are past that. If you aren't willing to pay as much as other buyers for a given property then you probably won't be buying a house. What you are now seeing is that a rebating agent who in the end gets less commission isn't very responsive to you.

Maybe you need to step back and figure out what you really want in terms of the property and your budget? If a buyer wants to pay $500K for a house that other people are willing to pay $600K for you can see that sellers and realtors won't want to spend time with such a buyer? Everyone wants a bargain, but you can only get a bargain if you can find a seller willing to give you one. The realtor has nothing to do with that. The real estate market is changing in many areas now, but if in your area for the type of house you want to buy listings are still getting multiple offers over asking price then you have to play along with the game otherwise you won't be buying anything.
