What Will the Midterms Mean for Healthcare?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

— KFF's Larry Levitt breaks down the polling and what's at stake for health policy

In this 43-minute webinar, MedPage Today Editor-in-Chief Jeremy Faust, MD, speaks with Larry Levitt, executive vice president for health policy at KFF, about the issues shaping voters' views of healthcare heading into Election Day.

They discuss healthcare affordability, abortion policy, growing support for "Medicare for All," declining trust in government health agencies and vaccines, the future of Affordable Care Act (ACA) subsidies, and how much change Americans want to see in the healthcare system.
