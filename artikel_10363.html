American Express just made one of its most popular credit card perks even better

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

American Express is giving cardholders another reason to make their dinner reservations through Resy.

The company is launching Resy Rewards Tuesday, a new loyalty program that lets eligible Amex cardholders earn four additional Membership Rewards points per dollar they spend at participating restaurants. Those points are in addition to whatever rewards their credit card already earns, so that means some diners could earn as much as 8X points on a single meal.

The program marks the first time American Express has brought its Membership Rewards program directly into the Resy app, bringing together two of the company’s biggest offerings as competition for restaurant loyalty heats up.

How Resy Rewards works

Resy Rewards will initially be available in New York City, with plans to expand to additional U.S. markets in 2027.

Eligible American Express cardholders can enroll their cards in the program through the Resy iOS app (Android support is coming soon). Once enrolled, you’ll find a new Rewards tab where you can browse participating restaurants and make reservations.

To earn the bonus points, diners need to book through that Rewards tab, complete their reservation, and pay for their meal using their enrolled American Express card.

They’ll then earn four additional Membership Rewards points for every eligible dollar spent, on top of the points their card would normally earn.

For American Express Gold Card members, for instance, who already earn 4X points on qualifying restaurant purchases, that means earning up to 8X points per dollar spent. That means a $200 dinner that would normally earn 800 points could earn 1,600 points through Resy Rewards.

Eligible American Express Business Gold Card members can also earn the additional points. However, the card’s existing 4X restaurant rewards depend on restaurant spending being among the cardholder’s top two eligible spending categories during the billing cycle. A points cap also applies.

At launch, Resy Rewards will be available at a selection of New York City restaurants, including Carne Mare, Colonia Verde, Hearth, Kiko, OPTO and Le Pavillon.

The company plans to expand the program to additional U.S. cities in 2027, when select restaurants on Tock, another reservation platform owned by American Express, will also become eligible to participate.

Why American Express is betting on restaurant rewards

Restaurant loyalty has traditionally been a difficult business. Diners want flexibility in where they eat, while restaurants need to balance the cost of offering incentives against the additional business those programs generate.

American Express has an advantage because it doesn’t need to create an entirely new rewards system. Instead, Resy Rewards uses Membership Rewards, an established points currency that millions of American Express cardholders already collect through everyday purchases.

“For the first time, we’re bringing American Express’s industry-leading Rewards program into the Resy iOS app and connecting two things our Card Members value deeply: great dining experiences and meaningful rewards,” said Pablo Rivero, CEO of Resy and senior vice president of American Express Global Dining.

“As we’ve seen with the success of the Resy Credit, rewarding diners while driving demand and revenue to Resy restaurants is a win on both sides of the table.”

American Express points to the success of its existing Resy Credit as evidence that dining incentives can influence consumer spending. According to the company, spending on Resy Credit-eligible American Express card products increased 33% at U.S. Resy restaurants following the credit’s introduction. The credit has also been redeemed at 98% of U.S. Resy restaurants.
