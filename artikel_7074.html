Anthropic: Corporate Network Engineer

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

Corporate Network Engineering designs, builds, and operates the networks in Anthropic's offices around the world: campus switching, Wi-Fi, and edge. We think a good office network is no more complicated than its requirements demand, fast and reliable enough that users never think about it, secure by default, and tolerant of failures to enable a lean team to operate a global portfolio of offices.

Anthropic is growing quickly, and our team is small and runs with high autonomy, so we care a great deal about how the work gets done: configuration kept in version control and shipped through review and staged rollouts, problems that happen more than once fixed at the source, and incidents closed only once the cause is fixed. We use Claude heavily in this work and expect you to as well.

We're hiring an experienced network engineer who works this way already: someone who has designed, built, and operated campus networks at scale, wired and wireless, and has also built the code and tooling to run them. As a network engineer here you'll work across all of it: network design and standards, new office buildouts, infrastructure as code, monitoring and incident response, and network security, where Corporate Security and Detection & Response are close partners. This is a hands-on individual contributor role.

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
