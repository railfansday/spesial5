Oportun: Sr. Manager, Bank Partner Program Management (R14382)

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Oportun (Nasdaq: OPRT) is a mission-driven financial services company that puts its members' financial goals within reach. With intelligent borrowing, savings, and budgeting capabilities, Oportun empowers members with the confidence to build a better financial future. Since inception, Oportun has provided more than $22.7 billion in responsible and affordable credit, saved its members more than $2.5 billion in interest and fees, and helped its members set aside an average of more than $1,800 annually.

We’re looking for a Senior Manager, Bank Partner Program Management to help lead the day-to-day execution and ongoing management of Oportun’s bank partner programs. You’ll serve as a key point of coordination between Oportun and our bank partners, helping drive timely approvals, resolve issues, manage reporting and obligations, and keep critical cross-functional initiatives moving forward.

This role sits at the intersection of Product, Marketing, Risk, Finance, Operations, Legal, Compliance, and Technology. You’ll bring strong program management, consumer lending knowledge, regulatory awareness, and partner management skills to help strengthen our bank partnerships while supporting growth and a strong member experience.

At Oportun, we move fast and we hold each other to a high bar. If you care about doing your best work while actually moving the needle for real people, you'll fit right in.

You’re a highly organized and proactive program leader who knows how to keep complex work moving across multiple teams and external partners. You’re comfortable managing competing priorities, working through ambiguity, and escalating risks when needed.

You understand consumer lending operations and the regulatory environment well enough to recognize where issues, approvals, or control gaps require deeper attention. You communicate clearly, build trust with partners, and know how to balance urgency with accuracy and sound judgment.

You also take ownership. You follow through on commitments, keep stakeholders informed, and look for ways to improve how work gets done.
