United Airlines Plans Seatback Screens And Bigger Bins For Aging Boeing 757-300s—Retirement Can Wait

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

United Airlines is planning a major cabin upgrade for its 21 Boeing 757-300s, bringing seatback entertainment, larger overhead bins and refreshed interiors to planes that average roughly 24 years old. American Airlines retired its last 757s early in the pandemic. United apparently sees enough flying left in these stretched jets to spend money on them. According to aviation watchdog JonNYC, the work will bring the aircraft into the airline’s United Next cabin program. That’s a strong indication they’re sticking around for years, even as United replaces a different part of its 757 fleet with new Airbus aircraft. Jon reports that the 757-300 modification includes: Boeing Space Bins, providing more overhead storage. Panasonic on-demand entertainment at every seat. A full cabin in-seat power system. New cabin dividers, headers and United branding. Refreshed seats and cabin fixtures throughout the aircraft.
