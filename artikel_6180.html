Amex Gold Card Bonus: As High As 100K Points Welcome Offer (Check Yours)

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The American Express® Gold Card is one of Amex’s most compelling cards for earning Membership Rewards points, and it currently has a best-ever welcome offer that’s as high as 100,000 bonus points, making it a great time to apply. In this post, I’d like to go over all the details of the current Amex Gold Card bonus, including who is eligible, and why you might want to apply.

Link: Learn more about the American Express® Gold Card

The Amex Gold Card currently has a lucrative bonus, marketed as being as high as 100,000 Membership Rewards bonus points after spending $8,000 within the first six months. Welcome offers vary and you may not be eligible for an offer.

The way this works, you can apply for the Amex Gold Card, and then find out your offer amount. Then you can decide whether you want to accept the offer or not. That’s an unconventional approach for a welcome offer. At least Amex has the “apply with confidence” feature, so there won’t be a hard pull unless you accept the offer.

Based on my latest miles & points valuations, I consider Membership Rewards points to be worth 1.7 cents each. This means that to me, the 100,000 points are worth $1,700, making it among the best welcome offers out there. Of course that assumes you’re eligible for this offer, which not everyone will be.

As a point of comparison, previously the Amex Gold Card bonus offered up to 60,000 points, so this is a lot better. As a matter of fact, the current Amex Gold Card offer is potentially the best publicly available one that we’ve seen.

There are lots of great uses for Amex points, as they can be transferred to roughly 20 airline and hotel partners, ranging from Air Canada Aeroplan, to Air France-KLM Flying Blue, All Nippon Airways Mileage Club, to Virgin Atlantic Flying Club. Just to give a couple of quick examples:

In line with Amex’s “once in a lifetime” rule, the welcome offer on the Amex Gold Card isn’t available to those who currently have the card, or those who have had the card (or previous versions of the card) in the past. On top of that, you may not be eligible for the welcome offer on this card if you have or have had American Express Platinum Card® (learn more).

This is a reason to pick up the Amex Gold Card before you pick up the Amex Platinum Card. That’s because you’re potentially eligible for the Amex Platinum Card welcome offer if you have or have had the Amex Gold Card, while the inverse may not be true.

The Amex Gold Card welcome offer is currently as high as 100,000 points upon completing minimum spending. The way this works, you have to fill out the application, and then you’ll see exactly what offer you’re eligible for. If you are eligible for the full Amex Gold Card bonus, this card is absolutely worth picking up. Amex doesn’t have that many super lucrative cards in terms of the number of points you can earn, and this is among my favorites.
