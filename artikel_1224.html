Thomas Frank Must Unleash Gamechanger Mateta for Crystal Palace vs Sociedad

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Thomas Frank got off to a bad start as Crystal Palace manager against Coventry, but he has the chance to put it right on Thursday in the Europa League against Real Sociedad.

The La Liga outfit will travel to Selhurst Park for a European fixture, having won just one of their first four games in the competition, and they are desperate to get back to winning ways after a very slow start to the season.

Frank came into the club to replace Pierre Sage with the team sitting bottom of the Premier League and struggling to qualify for the knockout stages of the Europa League. But despite the team fighting back from losing positions twice in his first game in charge, he couldn't prevent the defeat.

One of the manager's biggest tasks is finding a way to stop leaking goals defensively, and a switch to a back four formation shows that he is willing to implement his own ideas and style in as quickly as possible.

But he also needs to get more goals in the team, and he started off well with getting Jorgen Strand Larsen and Yeremy Pino on the scoresheet in the defeat to Coventry. The Norwegian striker has scored just nine times since joining the club from Wolves in a £50m deal back in the January transfer window, and the strike was just his fifth in the Premier League since his arrival.

With Ismaila Sarr, who was the team's top goalscorer last season, still sidelined for a number of weeks and seemingly not that keen to be at the club, Frank must find alternative solutions, starting with the game against Sociedad.

One player who the boss didn't have available to him for his Premier League debut was Jean-Philippe Mateta, but the Frenchman is ready and available to return in the Europa League.

Mateta was suspended after his red card against Tottenham, but that only stands in domestic fixtures. Now Frank has the chance to put the phenomenal forward into his starting lineup and find a way to get him firing again, trying to get the goals he can offer up into the side as quickly as possible.

Larsen was initially signed as Mateta's replacement before his move to AC Milan collapsed in January, but there is now an opportunity for the manager to try and pair them together and be a real handful for the opposition.

While Pino and Larsen scored at Coventry, Dwight McNeil was handed a surprise start and had a terrible outing, scoring the own goal that eventually saw them leave empty-handed.

While McNeil and Mateta don't play in the same position, Frank has always been known for his tactical adaptability, and getting Mateta in the team is a no-brainer. He's fresh, strong, great in the box, and knows how to score goals. Alongside Larsen, they could form a formidable partnership, just like Ivan Toney and Bryan Mbeumo did at Brentford.

There are other attacking options in the side too, and if Frank wants to stick with his normal formation, then Larsen should be brutally axed despite his goal at the weekend because Mateta is by far the better player, and has proven it in the past.

Frank must unleash his best players to finally get back to winning ways and get off to a good start, while also helping the team in their chase to qualify in Europe.

Thomas Frank already has his Bryan Mbeumo at Crystal Palace in Yeremy Pino.
