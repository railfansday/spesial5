American Adds New Routes, Drops Others in Reshuffle at LAX

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

American Airlines will add new connections and cut others as it works to optimize its network at Los Angeles.

The carrier confirmed Friday that it will launch year-round service from LAX to Reno, Nevada, and seasonal service to Bozeman, Montana, and Grand Junction, Colorado.

LA-Reno will operate three times weekly, starting May 27, 2027, using Embraer E175 aircraft.

The routes to Bozeman and Grand Junction will operate from May 27 to Aug. 9, with Bozeman service using an E175 and Grand Junction flights using a Bombardier CRJ700.

An American official said Grand Junction is a “seasonal market addition” while service to Aspen/Pitkin County, Colorado, is temporarily suspended due to construction work there.
