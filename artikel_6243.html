Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The complimentary slippers at a luxury hotel are always tempting to take home, but if we're honest, they rarely last beyond a few wears. Fortunately, plenty of more durable—and more comfortable—options exist, from plush, shearling slides and classic wool slip-ons to lightweight travel slippers that barely take up any room in your suitcase. Some have cushioned footbeds and rubber soles sturdy enough for a quick trip outside; others feature soft, suede soles better suited to padding around the house.

What are the best brands for travel slippers?

Bombas and EverFoams design lightweight slippers that are conveniently packable, while Ugg and Quince offer cozy shearling options that take up a bit more suitcase space. If you love the slippers you usually find at luxury hotels, The Ritz-Carlton also sells its signature Frette spa slippers online, so you can bring five-star comfort right to your home.

Slippers can be a welcome change from sneakers or boots on a long-haul flight, especially if you’re hoping to get some sleep. Choose a lightweight, packable pair with nonslip soles, but switch back to shoes when walking around the cabin or using the bathroom.

What should you look for in the best indoor slippers?

When shopping for indoor slippers, fit and material matter most: Wool and shearling provide warmth without trapping too much heat, while memory foam adds cushioning. Look for a soft, flexible sole with enough grip to keep you from slipping on hardwood or tile floors.
