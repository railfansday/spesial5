Hermoso after Roma’s heroic 2-2 draw vs PSG: “A great result but we had opportunities to win it.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Mario Hermoso spoke to Amazon Prime Video to comment on the draw against Paris Saint-Germain.

“It also helps with how the team entered the match, how we played against a team that has won the last two Champions Leagues. That’s a big deal for a team like ours, who knows what to do and doesn’t give up on their style of play and mentality. It’s an important point; it’s a shame we didn’t take home three, but even one is good.”

“I had two chances to score and I didn’t take them. But we’re still there and fighting in this League Phase, as well as in Serie A. It’s a great moment for Roma, for the entire club, and for all the fans who supported us here. Let’s keep going like this.”

“We have to win the next two. They are two games we will play at home, we have to win to continue in the Champions League.”

“For us, the important thing is to continue growing; we have plenty of room for improvement. We want to fight for everything; we have the mentality and the quality to do so. Let’s continue like this, with what the coach wants. For us, it’s the right path.”
