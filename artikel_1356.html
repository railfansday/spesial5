Because of Farke: Wilfried Gnonto Has Played His Final Game For Leeds

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Leeds United look to have a good chance of finishing in the top half of the Premier League table this season after their opening 10 matches.

Leeds started the campaign positively, but there would have been a worry that they would slip towards the bottom three following a run of five top flight games without a win.

While it wasn't pretty and the best game they will play this term, the Yorkshire outfit did pick up their first three points since thrashing Newcastle United at Elland Road in September, as they beat Tottenham Hotspur thanks to Noah Okafor's late goal.

Leeds have the third-best defensive record in the Premier League, having conceded just eight times, but there will be concerns about an attack that has scored 10 times in the same number of fixtures.

Still, Farke would have been happy to see Okafor net his second of the season against Spurs, joining Jayden Bogle on that number, with Dominic Calvert-Lewin, who is attracting Man Utd interest amid talk of a new Leeds contract, ahead on three.

It would be no surprise to see the Yorkshire club dip into the transfer market in January to add to their attacking options, with the club already being linked with a number of players.

According to reports, Leeds hold an interest in Galatasaray playmaker Gabriel Sara, who has impressed in English football previously with Norwich City.

Jack Rudoni of Coventry City is another player catching the attention of scouts, while they have been interested in Lyon's Pavel Sulc for months, although any move for him is on the back-burner after he suffered an injury that is set to keep him on the sidelines until March.

The Whites have also been linked with a move for Harvey Elliott, whose career continues to stall as he struggles to find the right home away from Liverpool.

Should a new attacking signing arrive in January, or next summer, it would only further cement the fact that Wilfried Gnonto has no future at Elland Road.

Despite being something of a key attacking player for Leeds during their time in the Championship, Gnonto was able to replicate that sort of form in the Premier League.

It quickly became clear last season that Farke didn't fancy the Italy international as he looked to ensure the club stayed in the Premier League the year after they won promotion from the Championship.

In the summer, Gnonto ended up joining Serie A outfit Fiorentina on loan, in a deal which the BBC reported gave La Viola the option to buy the 23-year-old permanently for £18m, including the loan fee and future add-ons.

The winger has started life with the Italian club by scoring one goal in 10 appearances, and you would think he needs to significantly improve that number if there is any chance of a permanent deal happening.

Still, one change Farke has made in recent weeks means that Gnonto has no future at the club even if he does return to Yorkshire at the end of the season.

Much of the club's success this season has come from the German coach using a 3-5-2 formation, and it looks as though it's here to stay after delivering another three points against Tottenham.

Gnonto was already struggling to get into a team that used 4-3-3 and 3-4-3 systems last term, and he would need to play either as a wing-back, behind the strikers, or as a striker in Farke's trusted new set-up.

That simply isn't going to happen, and it would now be a big surprise if the Italian attacker ever wears a Leeds shirt again.
