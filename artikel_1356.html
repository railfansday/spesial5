Better Than Mudryk: Roberto De Zerbi Must Unleash Savio Against Ipswich

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Tottenham will be looking to get back to winning ways when they return to Premier League action against Ipswich Town on Saturday afternoon.

After moving out of the relegation zone with three wins from three in October, the Lilywhites crashed back down to earth with a slender 1-0 defeat at Leeds United just prior to November's international break.

The home encounter against Ipswich represents a chance to claim another three points for Roberto De Zerbi's men, although the match will be far from straightforward as the Tractor Boys have made a decent start to life back in the Premier League with three wins from their first ten league games under Gary O'Neil.

De Zerbi has some big calls to make in regards to his team, with Mathys Tel set to miss a period of action after suffering an injury at Leeds following a challenge by Jayden Bogle.

One player who could potentially make his return to the team is Savio following a month on the sidelines due to a muscle injury sustained in the draw at Manchester United.

The Brazilian winger started on the left at Old Trafford and he may quite well come straight into the team if fit and take Tel's place on the left flank for the home encounter against Ipswich.

De Zerbi may also have Mykhailo Mudryk at his disposal again following an ankle injury sustained in training.

The loan signing's only appearance in a Tottenham shirt came at the start of September with a 16-minute cameo off the bench against Nottingham Forest.

Mudryk will be desperate to make up for lost time and show everyone exactly why he was brought to Tottenham on loan in the first place.

Mudryk and Savio's 2026/27 season in numbers

Tottenham supporters are desperate to see more of Savio in the starting XI with injury restricting him to only six appearances following his big-money move from Man City.

The 22-year-old, who was labelled as "incredible" by Pep Guardiola during his time at Man City, had a Spurs debut to remember after registering one goal and one assist against Charlton Athletic in the Carabao Cup in August, thus seeing Roberto De Zerbi label it as the "perfect day" for his new signing.

Savio fared well in his last appearance in the draw at Manchester United, delivering a bright performance but unfortunately it was his end product that let him down at times.

It should be a rather straightforward call for De Zerbi to make between Savio and Mudryk, with the ex-City winger ahead of his competitor when it comes to starting for Tottenham.

If indeed Savio does feature against Ipswich, it is a huge opportunity for him to try and cement his place in De Zerbi's side with some winnable games coming up prior to next month's North London Derby.
