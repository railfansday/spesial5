Have your say: Should Juventus bring back Conte?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Antonio Conte has recently admitted that he would one day like to return to Juventus to ‘close the cycle’ and we want to know whether the Old Lady would be wise to bring the five-time Scudetto winner back to Turin.

In an interview with Cronache di Spogliatoio, which has been teased online over the last few days, Conte admitted that he would like to return to Juventus, where he spent over a decade as a player and won three consecutive league titles as a head coach.

To date, Juventus is the only club or national team where Conte has lasted any more than two years as a head coach.

“I think that sooner or later the circle will close,” Conte confessed in an interview with Cronache di Spogliatoio, which was teased on Wednesday.

“I know I left something behind and I know that eventually the circle will be closed with Juventus.

“There have been moments in the past where we could have reunited, but it’s been difficult because two things have to align. I have to be available and Juventus have to be looking for a coach who isn’t currently employed.

“There have been two instances. Once, when Juventus decided to hire Maurizio Sarri, I was available and later ended up at Inter. Then there was a second time when I was free, just as Giuntoli arrived. Giuntoli decided to go for Motta and I went to Napoli.”

As Conte explained, in order to facilitate a return to Juventus, he would need to be available, as he currently is, but Juve would also need to be on the search for a new head coach, which is not currently the case given that Luciano Spalletti remains in charge.

However, Spalletti did face growing pressure in Turin prior to the international break, and if things were to take a turn for the worse, would Conte be the right man to turn to? Have your say in the poll below and let us know your thoughts in the comments.
