Manchester United reportedly look to be finalising a deal to hire Mauricio Pochettino as their next manager, but it’s not 100% done yet.

According to the Athletic’s David Ornstein, the Red Devils have chosen Pochettino as their candidate to come in if they go ahead with the sacking of Michael Carrick.

However, it seems Man Utd have yet to contact Pochettino’s current employers, the USA national team, while it seems a final decision has also not yet been communicated to Carrick.

Talks will take place today to finalise the decision, with the Athletic‘s report suggesting the expectation remains that Carrick will be sacked…

Pochettino looks like an exciting potential appointment, having done great work during his time in the Premier League with Southampton and Tottenham.

The 54-year-old also had a recent spell in charge of Chelsea, which was more of a struggle, while he is also seen as having under-achieved slightly during a spell in charge of Paris Saint-Germain.

The United job would be a big challenge, with Carrick’s side currently sitting in the relegation zone with just one win in their first ten Premier League games of the season.

Even though MUFC finished third last season, there are numerous issues in this current squad that Pochettino would have to contend with, as well as a huge amount of external pressure due to the expectations of the fanbase.
