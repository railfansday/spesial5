Japanese Charts: Pikmin 4 Makes A Lukewarm Debut On Switch 2 As Zelda Dominates

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The latest Japanese charts data is in, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time has comfortably maintained its lead with another 86,097 sales. Next in line is Metaphor: ReFantazio as its hits the Switch 2, shifting a total of 11,167 units.

Pikmin 4 - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy also made its debut, selling just 2,854 copies to land at number 9. Now, it's important to note that many owners of the game on Switch 1 will have simply bought the upgrade pack, which won't count toward this total, however the game has also seen its fair share of controversy soon after release.

It current holds a user score of 6.4 over on Metacritic, though this was as low as 2.3 just the other day as players reported their disappointment at the lack of brand-new content, no 60fps support, and supposedly longer load times. Indeed, we gave it a score of 7/10 in our review – a relatively low verdict for such a consistently strong series – citing the lack of seemingly obvious features like mouse mode and higher frame rate.

All three PS5 SKUs this week total 11,136, while the Switch 1 managed 3,754, and the Xbox just 707. It really is all about the Switch 2 in Japan now, huh?
