World of Slime is coming to mobile, offering you cute creature-collecting MMORPG fun

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

It seems that nowadays creature-collecting has become more popular than ever, no doubt helped by the likes of Palworld really blowing the doors off the concept after Pokémon dominated it for so long. And one of the cutest, lightweight takes has now hit pre-registration on mobile with World of Slime!

World of Slime is, well, exactly as described. This cute top-down MMORPG is best described as a mix of Roco Kingdom with a RuneScape perspective, where you explore a world completing quests and tasks, while capturing cute slime creatures to join your roster.

There's also more straightforward MMORPG combat as you battle it out with huge bosses that stand in your way. It's not exactly World of Warcraft, but World of Slime undoubtedly has plenty in the way of potential. And that small scale makes it a perfect fit for mobile, alongside its adorable cast!

At the same time, it's not been received without controversy, and currently sits at a mixed reception on Steam. Hopefully, that lacklustre reception doesn't translate to a lacking mobile launch. But if you are interested, you can pre-register for World of Slime now on the official site!
