Round Up: Spare A Thought For These Games Launching On 'GTA 6 Day'

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

We interrupt your Grand Theft Auto 6 playtime to bring you the following announcement: there are more games launching today.

Yes, while 19th November 2026 will forever be known as 'GTA 6 Day ', a handful of other games are also braving the launch window and are releasing on Switch today.

We know that your adventures in Leonida will take priority, and that's okay, but we thought it was only right to highlight the other experiences that didn't back down from the big boy's launch plans. And no, we're not highlighting everything launching on Switch today, since, well, the eShop is always bombarded with questionable titles that probably aren't worth checking out.

If, however, you're on the hunt for something with fewer Vice City vibes, here are some other options launching today.

On this page: Round Up: Spare A Thought For These Games Launching On 'GTA 6 Day'

There's every chance that you have already played through Bedtime Digital's Figment and Figment 2: Creed Valley when they launched on Switch 1 a few years back, but the Switch 2 versions are also launching today with some neat performance bumps. We're talking 60fps at 1080p and "enhanced graphics". Sounds ripe for a replay, if you ask us.

Review Figment 2: Creed Valley - Charming Action Puzzling, But Sluggish On Switch

Published and developed by room6, this is a roguelike all about gathering cute pixel art plushies to your aid and using their powers to survive a nighttime onslaught of monsters. Need we say more? It seems to strike that balance of cute/creepy pretty well, and we can see it being quite the time sink, with 40 recruitable plushies, 100 items and 20 costumes available to mix up every run.

Those who frequent Japanese merch stalls will no doubt recognise the Sumikkogurashi gang. This one sees the team of adorable little blobs tasked with helping out at onsen inns to ensure their smooth running. There's plenty of minigames and scope to expand the inns further, but come on, the cuteness is the big draw here. Just look at those little guys!

It's The Binding of Isaac again (again). This re-release features all of the game's DLC content and the chance to play it with up to four players online.

Mini Review The Binding Of Isaac: Repentance (Switch) - A Welcome Rebalance For The Roguelite Classic

Desperate for more throwback shooting after breezing through Boltgun? Auroch Digital has got you covered. After launching on other consoles last month, Warhammer 40,000 Boltgun 2 finally arrives on Switch 2 today with even more strafing, shooting and scary monstrosities. The score features a surprise cameo track from composer Mick Gordon (DOOM, DOOM Eternal, Wolfenstein: The New Order), which is an added bonus for music fans.

Purge daemons and heretics wherever you go as Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun 2 comes to Nintendo Switch 2 on the 19th November! The critically-acclaimed Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun 2 is headed to Nintendo Switch so you can take the brutal FPS action on the move ⚔️
