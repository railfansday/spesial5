Chocolate Peppermint Rice Krispies Treats

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Recipe by Cori Cooper, flagstaff, Arizona

These chocolate peppermint Rice Krispies treats with chocolate chips look (and taste) very Christmasy, but you can serve them anytime, really! Plus, you can customize them for any occasion or palate: If you want more chocolate flavor, use Cocoa Krispies instead of regular Rice Krispies. If you want a green(ish) dessert for St. Patrick's Day, replace the crushed peppermint candies with mint chocolate chips. —Cori Cooper, Flagstaff, Arizona
