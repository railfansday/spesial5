Anthropic’s crypto futures traded 25% above its $81 billion IPO price as Bitcoin slumped 22%

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Anthropic-linked futures on Hyperliquid hit $186 million in open interest. Block Scholes found little evidence that money moved directly from Bitcoin into AI.

Crypto futures tied to Anthropic traded about 25% above the company’s IPO price on Thursday. The bets swelled during a Bitcoin selloff, but research from Block Scholes points to a broader retreat from risk rather than clear evidence that investors sold Bitcoin to buy Anthropic.

Anthropic priced 980 million shares at $83 on Nov. 18, above its initial $70 to $78 range. The base offering totaled $81 billion, including shares sold by existing holders, and valued the company at about $1.5 trillion. It surpassed SpaceX’s June IPO. Anthropic set Nov. 19 as the expected Nasdaq debut for its shares under the symbol ANT.

Across 19 venues, the median price for Anthropic-linked perpetual futures was about $104 at 14:18 UTC on Nov. 19, roughly 25% above the $83 offer price, OptimistFi reported. The contracts track a reference price and do not give holders ownership of Anthropic shares.

Bitcoin had tested support near $62,000 twice by Thursday as traders watched for signs of returning demand.

In research shared with CryptoSlate, crypto derivatives analytics firm Block Scholes found that Bitcoin’s selloff broadly tracked weakness across other markets.

The firm's research analyst, Thahbib Rahman, examined Bitcoin's relationship with the Nasdaq-100 between January and September and found that a 1% move in the technology-heavy index corresponded, on average, with a 1.03% move in BTC in the same direction.

Block Scholes estimated an 11% Bitcoin decline from the Nasdaq-100’s performance alone. Including changes in gold, the US dollar and Treasury yields raised the model’s estimate to 14%, still short of the 22% drop the firm used for comparison.

US spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded about $3 billion in net outflows in November through Nov. 18, according to daily fund-flow data from Farside Investors.

The firm's analysis of previous stock market listings also challenges the capital-flight narrative.

Among 17 US IPOs that raised at least $900 million this year, Bitcoin declined during the preceding 50 days in nine cases, showing no consistent pattern of selling ahead of major offerings.

While Bitcoin has struggled, cryptocurrency derivatives markets have experienced a surge in trading tied to Anthropic's valuation.

Token Terminal reported that traders had speculated on Anthropic through perpetual futures on Hyperliquid for more than a year.

These markets valued the AI company at approximately $339 billion a year ago. By Nov. 18, the implied valuation had climbed to between $1.7 trillion and $1.8 trillion, well above the $1.5 trillion valuation established by the IPO.

Token Terminal data showed that open interest in Anthropic-linked perpetuals reached an all-time high of $186 million on Nov. 18, with all the previous week's net growth concentrated on trade[XYZ], which launched its Anthropic market on Oct. 26.

Daily trading volume peaked at $261 million on Nov. 10, coinciding with the announcement of Anthropic's initial IPO pricing range.

Bullish participation also accelerated. On Nov. 18, Token Terminal recorded 5,567 traders holding long positions, compared with 1,996 holding short positions, a nearly three-to-one ratio.

On OKX, Anthropic’s pre-IPO perpetual initially traded near 130 USDC when the company’s roadshow began, about 67% above the top of the proposed range. The run-up followed earlier leveraged bets on Anthropic’s IPO in September.

The contract subsequently declined toward 98 USDC as bankers raised the offering price, before recovering above 103 USDC following Wednesday's final pricing announcement.

Erald Ghoos, CEO of OKX Europe, told CryptoSlate that the divergence reflected a disagreement between traditional financial institutions and crypto traders over the company's valuation.

“For 10 days, traders and bankers disagreed about what Anthropic is worth. The bankers moved up to $83. Traders still think that's too low, by about 24%.”

The executive pointed to SpaceX's June listing, when pre-IPO derivatives traded within 4% of the company's eventual first-day closing price, while the stock's IPO price was approximately 16% below that close.

Block Scholes also reported growing activity on Hyperliquid, where Anthropic-linked perpetuals averaged approximately $75 million in daily trading volume since Nov. 5, up from $7 million in September.

However, those contracts represented only about 1% of Hyperliquid's overall trading volume and roughly 3% of the increase in non-crypto trading since September.

The benchmarks for Anthropic’s first day on Nasdaq are the $83 IPO price and the roughly $104 futures quote. The gap is what traders bet on, not what public-market investors paid.
