FXStreet Surpasses One million YouTube Views in Less Than Two Months

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Barcelona, Spain, November 4th: FXStreet has surpassed 1 million views on its YouTube channel in less than two months, representing year-over-year growth of more than 1,500%, compared to the 61,000 views recorded during the same period last year. This milestone highlights the success of its video content strategy and reinforces the company’s efforts to diversify
