DK Metcalf speaks out after ejection from Eagles-Steelers game

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Pittsburgh Steelers wide receiver DK Metcalf has addressed the controversy surrounding his ejection in Sunday's NFL clash against the Philadelphia Eagles. The incident occurred during Sunday's 19-16 defeat, when the receiver became involved in two confrontations with Eagles cornerback Cooper DeJean.

Metcalf's actions attracted attention after officials disqualified him in the third quarter. He left the stadium before speaking to reporters, prompting questions about the confrontation and his decision to leave early.

However, the Steelers receiver returned to speak with the media on Monday. His explanation offered a different perspective on the incident and raised further questions about what happened between the two players.

Speaking to reporters on Monday, Metcalf denied attempting to poke DeJean in the eye. When a reporter asked about the accusation, he responded, "You said I tried to what?" He then explained that he was telling DeJean to stop pushing him.

Metcalf also refused to accept responsibility for the confrontation alone. Asked whether DeJean had provoked him, the receiver replied, "You've gotta ask him." He added that he would not be surprised if opponents deliberately tried to agitate him, saying such treatment was nothing new during his career.

The Steelers receiver also acknowledged that he should have handled the situation differently. When asked whether he regretted his actions, he gave a short response: "I just wish I would have been there for my team." He also admitted leaving the stadium early to avoid speaking with reporters.

Metcalf's ejection followed two separate confrontations with DeJean during the third quarter. Officials first penalized him for unnecessary roughness after he continued blocking beyond the whistle. A second incident resulted in an unsportsmanlike conduct penalty and his disqualification.

DeJean offered a different account after the game. He described the confrontation as two players competing and said Metcalf had nearly poked him in the eye. "Just missed," the Eagles cornerback said when asked about the incident. Metcalf finished with one reception for two yards on four targets.

The NFL will review the incident for possible additional discipline, including a suspension. That could affect Metcalf's availability when the Pittsburgh Steelers host the Denver Broncos on November 27.
