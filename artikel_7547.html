LA County could dodge liability in Palisades Fire lawsuit

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Thousands of plaintiffs are suing California and the city and county of Los Angeles for allowing a relatively small brush fire grow into the Palisades blaze.

In a tentative ruling that was not made public, Superior Court Judge Samantha Jessner indicated she would likely sustain a demurrer filed by the county and water agency. The plaintiffs have argued those two public entities had recently upgraded a water system to better withstand a large wildfire.

Following a 1993 fire in Malibu, the water district, which is managed by the county, installed emergency backup generators capable of pushing water uphill in the case of a power outage. However, the district decided to not bury the new power lines underground or otherwise protect them. As a result, the plaintiffs say, the lines melted during the 2025 blaze, rendering the recent improvements all but worthless.
