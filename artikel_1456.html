Mainoo looks primed for battle with Man City’s new-look midfield

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

This was Manchester United’s first foray back into the Champions League for 1,003 days, but even with hazy memories of the competition, they know full well they are unlikely to have it this easy again in Europe this season.

After an unconvincing beginning to their Premier League campaign and with Manchester City the visitors to Old Trafford on Sunday, Michael Carrick will have been grateful for a gentle start to life back among Europe’s elite.

Little-known Sabah of Azerbaijan have quite the story to tell, but the closest they got to making this an awkward evening for United came in the first half when, already trailing 1-0, Joy-Lance Mickels miscued an inviting header from close range. Otherwise, it was one-way traffic.

United’s biggest threat on the night was complacency, but there was none of that. This was a professional, unfussy display with a few nice flourishes. Kobbie Mainoo impressed again in central midfield and Carrick was able to substitute a number of his best players early with the derby in mind, as well as resting Luke Shaw entirely.

Goals from Matheus Cunha, Bruno Fernandes and Benjamin Sesko in the first half killed the game as a contest before Lisandro Martínez scored a fourth with just over 20 minutes remaining.

“It’s nice to keep a clean sheet and obviously win the game,” Carrick said. “Some of the football we played was really good – dangerous on the transition. The result looks comfortable, but it wasn’t always like that but we did a lot of good things.

United probably could not have asked for a softer return to Champions League football. Atlético Madrid at the Wanda Metropolitano next month will certainly be a very different beast, but it was a rewarding first 45 minutes all the same that paved the way for Carrick to ring the changes ahead of the derby.

Already leading 1-0, two goals shortly before the interval changed the complexion of the game and effectively extinguished any flickering hopes Sabah had of a gargantuan upset.

Founded just nine years ago, Sabah had navigated four qualifying rounds that began on July 7 just to reach this stage.

Throw in the fact Valdas Dambrauskas, their Lithuanian manager, got his break in coaching with the Manchester United Soccer Schools 20 years ago, and it is quite the heartwarming tale.

But there is not much sentiment at this level and, given time and space, United punished their inexperience and carelessness.

Carrick will not read too much into the performance. City will be an entirely different proposition and it will be interesting to see whether he goes for a two-man midfield with Bruno Fernandes deployed as an advanced No 10, or perhaps operates with a midfield three and adjusts his attack.

Either way, Mainoo will be central to the game plan.

It is hard to believe the England midfielder missed most of pre-season after reporting back late following the World Cup, because he has barely missed a beat in his last three outings, even if his team-mates have not always hummed to his tune.

There is so much to like about Mainoo. His manipulation of space, how he never looks under duress, even when hounded, and his vision on the ball. There was one lovely angled ball over the top here, after United had worked the space well, that released Fernandes in behind. His cross was cut out before Sesko could tap in.

Mainoo’s work off the ball has improved enormously, too. There were numerous instances here of him making a timely intervention or nicking the ball away at the right moment to recover possession.United have spent £155m reconfiguring their midfield, but Mainoo will be a constant. “He’s a fantastic player, it’s pretty obvious to see,” Carrick said. “He has a massive influence on our games. He is a real joy to have, he wants to improve, he wants to listen and it’s great to see.”

Kobbie Mainoo completed 81/83 passes, 6/6 accurate long balls and 10 passes into final third on his full Champions League debut ⭐️ pic.twitter.com/NK9BIGvc4m

It will certainly be interesting to see how Carrick’s new-look midfield shapes up against City’s own revamped one in the form of Elliot Anderson, Enzo Fernández and Ayyoub Bouaddi.

“There are some good connections and we have played some good football through the middle of the pitch,” Carrick added. “A lot of it is on wavelength and understanding of where each other wants the ball. We haven’t had the perfect start but there are really good signs.”

Carrick was suitably confident to withdraw Youri Tielemans at half-time and bring on Mason Mount, who was having a good pre-season before his familiar injury jinx hit, and he will have been pleased to see Sesko on the scoresheet again. “He definitely offers a different kind of threat,” Carrick said. “There are not many who can give us what Ben does with his physicality, playing on the last line. It’s great to have him back.”

Sesko’s goal, rounding the goalkeeper to slot home from Bryan Mbeumo’s pass after line-breaking balls from Mainoo and Fernandes, capped off a quick-fire three minutes when Sabah were unpicked. Before then, Cunha had picked out Tielemans’s burst into the penalty area, from where he pulled the ball back for Fernandes to coolly finish.

Carrick prefers Dorgu further up the pitch and it remains to be seen if he trusts the Dane at left-back in bigger games than this.

“Nice to have a clean sheet and obviously win the game. Some of our attacking play was fantastic. We were dangerous in transition, defended well in the most part. The confidence is there, like at Everton on Sunday for large spells, just took a hit there at the end with the result. It is always nice to win. Another big one on Sunday.”

Presenting your Champions League Reds 📋❤️‍🔥

Manchester United make their return to the Champions League as they host tournament debutants Sabah of Azerbaijan on matchday one at Old Trafford. It will be the first time that Manchester United have faced an Azerbaijani side in European competition.

Manchester United have one win, one loss and one draw to their name in their opening three Premier League games. They were seconds away from winning their second game of the season on Sunday but conceded a late equaliser at Everton. They last featured in the Champions League during the 2023/24 season, when they were eliminated in the group stage, and have won just one of their last nine games in the competition.

“It means an awful lot [to be back in the Champions League],” Manchester United boss Michael Carrick said. “Challenging yourself against the elite, the very best. This football club has been used to being the best. It is not something you can take for granted. We missed out on it for a number of years and now we are back. It is where we expect to be without getting ahead of ourselves. We have made huge steps to make it in, now it is about pushing into the next chapter and evolution if the squad and this is certainly where we want to be.

“I do not think it is a case of if we get to a certain stage it is a success and if we do not it is not. We have to embrace the new challenge that we are facing as a group and push to what we can push to. If we get further down the line in this competition then who knows? I am not writing anything off. But we are taking it step by step and we certainly are not getting ahead of ourselves. We have got a squad that is strong enough to compete.”

Sabah make their Champions League debut having been founded just eight years ago in September 2017. Back in April, they secured the Azerbaijan top-flight title for the first time in their history and also won the Azerbaijan Cup in May. They earned a spot in the Champions League group stages after beating Hapoel Be’er Sheva 6-4 on aggregate. In the previous three seasons, they have tried to reach the group stages of the Europa and Conference Leagues but failed to do so. They have won all three leagues thus far, including a 5-0 home win over Zira last Saturday.
