Bologna set on terminating Dovbyk’s loan

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Artem Dovbyk, so far, hasn’t found the redemption he hoped for in Bologna.

The Ukrainian striker joined the Rossoblu on loan this summer with an option to buy, hoping to rediscover his best self. Nothing of the sort. The 1997-born striker has played just 105 minutes, scoring a goal against Sassuolo.

The Felsinei are far from satisfied with the former Girona player. Dovbyk hasn’t found any playing time under either Domenico Tedesco or Raffaele Palladino; the player isn’t convincing anyone at Bologna.

Bologna, as reported by Tuttosport, are dissatisfied with Artem Dovbyk’s performance, so much so that they’re considering terminating his loan six months early.

The Rossoblu club is already considering a replacement for the Ukrainian striker: Lorenzo Lucca, who is leaving Napoli.

The former Udinese player is a target for Palladino, who wants a striker more suited to his style of play than Dovbyk.

The situation will need to be monitored, pending concrete developments. Roma is aware that the 1997-born striker will have little room at Bologna.
