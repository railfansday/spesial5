We've all been wrecked by the global memory crisis, aka RAMageddon, this year. Hard drives, microSD cards, portable SSDs, and other tech essentials have been rapidly rising in price, and there's no sign of that upward trajectory slowing any time soon. In fact, the supply chain experts Mashable has interviewed all say the memory crisis will get worse before it gets better. Yikes.

That's why you have to take advantage of discounts on memory products while you can. And so we've gathered up all of the best Black Friday deals on SSD drives, flash drives, internal hard drives, and microSD cards.

Whether you've been wanting to build out your PC, secure offline data backups, or give your console a little breathing space, Black Friday is the best time to take advantage of portable SSD and hard drive deals.
