Texas Pacific Land agreed to acquire EagleRock in a $3 billion all-stock deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

EagleRock investors would receive stock in a new holding company; Texas Pacific Land holders would own about 86% of the combined firm

Texas Pacific Land agreed to acquire EagleRock Land, the companies said on Monday, in an all-stock transaction that implies an equity value of about $3 billion.

For every EagleRock Class A share held, EagleRock shareholders will get 0.0831 share of New Titan Holdings, a newly formed holding company. Measured against Texas Pacific Land's average share price over the five trading days through Nov. 20, weighted by volume, that ratio puts a value of $23.56 on each EagleRock Class A share. The holding company is to be renamed Texas Pacific Land at closing.

Current Texas Pacific Land shareholders are expected to hold roughly 86% of the merged company on a fully diluted basis, with former EagleRock holders owning about 14%. Texas Pacific Land's board and executive team will lead the company, which will be based in Dallas. No vote of Texas Pacific Land shareholders is required.

The companies expect the deal to close in the first half of 2027. Closing is subject to approval by EagleRock shareholders, expiration or termination of the Hart-Scott-Rodino antitrust waiting period and other customary conditions. In a Form 8-K, Texas Pacific Land disclosed that EagleRock holders with about 63% of the voting power signed agreements to vote in favor.

EagleRock would owe Texas Pacific Land a $96.5 million termination fee under specified circumstances, including if EagleRock's board changes its recommendation, the filing said.

The deal adds about 59,000 fee-owned acres, primarily in Lea County, New Mexico. It raises Texas Pacific Land's Midland Basin surface acreage by nearly 40%. Water-solutions arrangements with Double Eagle, EagleRock's largest shareholder, are supported by about 105,000 dedicated acres.

Texas Pacific Land said it expects the transaction to boost what it projects it will generate in 2027 in free cash flow on a per-share basis, excluding synergies. It sees roughly $45 million in yearly run-rate benefits, consisting of $20 million from trimming corporate costs and $25 million from revenue opportunities.

Texas Pacific Land's board also raised its stock repurchase authorization by $500 million, leaving about $670.2 million available, the filing said.

"Joining TPL creates an opportunity to bring broader capabilities to our customers," EagleRock CEO Greg Pipkin said in a statement.

EagleRock stock rose more than 9% by midday Monday, while Texas Pacific Land stock fell about 4%. EagleRock went public in May, according to the Houston Chronicle.
