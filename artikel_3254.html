Julianne Hough’s Stylist Addresses Fan Backlash To Her ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Wardrobe This Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Fans had a lot to say about this season of Dancing with the Stars — and not just about who should or shouldn’t have gone home before the Nov. 24 finale.

Julianne Hough broke out of her comfort zone this fall by donning “super extravagant and wild and playful” gowns while emceeing with Alfonso Ribeiro, she told Glamour. But her body-hugging, attention-seeking frocks sometimes upstaged the celebrities, which didn’t land well with the show’s hyper-critical fans.

In an interview with the fashion and beauty outlet, Hough acknowledged that her collaboration with celebrity stylist Brad Goreski had her initially thinking his selections were “too colorful … too much.” But that doesn’t mean she jibed with the negative feedback. “Apathy is the enemy,” she told Glamour. “The reason things exist and have charge and stay visible [is] people either love it or hate it. They still want to talk about it.” Watch on Deadline

For his part, Goreski welcomes the feedback.

“Any conversation, good or bad, around fashion is great. I think people weighing in — they like the hair but not the dress; they thought it was a bad outfit; they think she should fire me — is totally fine, because we’re really excited by what we’re doing, and we put a lot of thought and energy into it,” he told the outlet.

“I think Jules ultimately brings the looks to life, and I’m glad that there’s another part of the show that people talk about,” he continued. “Not every person’s going to love every look. I know that from being a stylist for 15 years. And that’s OK.”

Hough, who previously competed and judged on the show, started co-hosting DWTS in the fall of 2023. She and Ribeiro just finished emceeing a successful and very emotional season of the ABC variety show; the Nov. 24 finale scored 9.2 million total live + same-day viewers across ABC and Disney+, making it the most-watched episode of the season.
