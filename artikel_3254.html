Bending Spoons: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Management ties AI-driven engineering gains to rising deal volume so that the efficiency story may matter as much as the acquisitions themselves.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 20, 2026, Bending Spoons (BSP -1.56%) announced an agreement to acquire email marketing platform Mailchimp from Intuit for an enterprise value, or total transaction value, of $3.68 billion. The acquisition underscores the software buyer's strategy of acquiring predictable cash flows to compound earnings per share over a multi-decade horizon. Three key takeaways from management's comments highlight how the company operates.

Management directs research and development (R&D) and marketing budgets toward maximizing long-term returns rather than defending legacy product lines that are slowing.

Proprietary development tools enable engineers to handle code update submissions without inflating corporate headcount as mergers and acquisitions (M&A) accelerate.

Moving from self-serve digital apps to enterprise software requires specialized sales tools to boost representative output with complex business clients.

Full-year 2026 revenue guidance climbed to between $3.07 billion and $3.09 billion as integration efforts accelerate across newly acquired platforms.

Expanding tech hubs and proprietary automation preserve operational leverage as leadership evaluates target availability and pipeline capacity.
