Anthony Boyle and Julia Garner Reunited at the Paris Theater as The Altruists Hits Netflix

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

“The first rule of acting is that you don’t judge your character,” Julia Garner told an enthralled audience on Thursday evening at Manhattan’s Paris Theater, moments after the credits rolled following the world premiere of The Altruists. The Netflix original series, available on the streaming platform now, sees Garner step into the shoes of Caroline Ellison, the disgraced co-CEO of Alameda Research and on-again, off-again girlfriend of FTX co-founder Sam Bankman-Fried. “I wasn’t necessarily thinking of the fraud. I just wanted people to be able to understand her,” she added.

After a screening of the surprisingly humorous pilot episode, Garner and her co-star, Anthony Boyle, joined the show’s creator, Graham Moore, for a panel discussion with actor-turned-journalist Ben McKenzie about the treachery, trickery, and turbulence that pervade the world of cryptocurrency. “You’re probably wondering what’s Ryan Atwood doing up here,” McKenzie joked from the stage, referring to his early-aughts turn as one of The O.C.’s leading teen heartthrobs. “For the last four years, I’ve been investigating and documenting fraud and other criminal activity… And having met and interviewed the real Sam Bankman-Fried, I would like to say that Anthony has a lot more charm [than him].”

The series doesn’t just chronicle the shocking fall of Bankman-Fried and FTX. Rather, it centers largely on the romantic trajectory of the two financial-industry protégés. “It’s the story of two people who I genuinely, to the core of my being, believe got into this for the right reasons and wanted to do the right thing,” Moore said. “But over the course of their tortuous relationship and those six years, they turned each other into people who did the exact opposite of the thing they initially set out to do.” (Drawing a laugh from the crowd, Northern Irish actor Boyle likened their dynamic to “something from an episode of Love on the Spectrum.”)

After the panel, Garner, in a custom ballet-slipper-pink jumpsuit by Haider Ackermann for Tom Ford and an earful of Lorraine Schwartz bling, and an all-black-clad Boyle joined their castmates, crew members, and press for an after-party in The Plaza Hotel’s Oak Room—making it one of the last industry events to be hosted in the storied venue before it closes for a four-year renovation.

Over mini-umbrella-adorned mai-tais and passed bites, guests discussed the show and compared notes on how much they actually understood crypto terminology, while twirling SBF-approved fidget spinners and branded poker chips around in their hands.
