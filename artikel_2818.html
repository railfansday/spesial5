Chiefs vs. Falcons odds: Kansas City opens as 3.5-point favorites in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

After snapping a two-game losing streak with a much-needed victory over the New York Jets, the Kansas City Chiefs (6-2) will travel to Mercedes-Benz Stadium to face the Atlanta Falcons (3-6) in Week 10.

Kansas City got back into the win column Sunday at Arrowhead Stadium after consecutive losses to the Seattle Seahawks and Denver Broncos. The victory helped the Chiefs regain some momentum as they continue their pursuit of the AFC West title and the conference's top playoff seed.

According to FanDuel Sportsbook, Kansas City opened as a 3.5-point road favorite against Atlanta. The game's opening over/under is set at 46.5 points.

The Falcons enter the matchup looking to turn their season around after a difficult first half. Despite offensive weapons like running back Bijan Robinson and wide receiver Drake London, Atlanta has struggled to turn its talent into consistent wins.

On defense, the Falcons will be down starting safety Jessie Bates, who has a track record of intercepting quarterback Patrick Mahomes. Bates is being traded to the Jacksonville Jaguars.

For Kansas City, the matchup presents another opportunity to build momentum after Sunday's win. The Chiefs' defense rediscovered its pass rush against the Jets, while the offense will look to be more consistent heading into the second half of the season.

Although Kansas City enters as the favorite, the relatively narrow spread suggests oddsmakers expect a competitive road contest.
