Former Packers taken in the UFL’s 2027 draft

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The United Football League now has a draft structure that fans of professional football are used to seeing. Previously, the spring league's drafts were by position (e.g., front seven draft, offensive line draft, etc.), but now the UFL has a full 15-round draft — giving fans a look at when players are taken, something previous drafts didn't disclose.

This week, seven former Green Bay Packers were selected by spring league teams, including some names that weren't listed among the league's top 120 prospects entering the draft.

Here are the former Packers to come off the board:

#23: Cornelius Johnson, WR – Rhode Island Breakers

#40: McCallan Castles, TE – Louisville Kings

#56: Tyron Herring, CB – Louisville Kings

#60: Damien Martinez, RB – Colorado Stampede

#83: M.J. Devonshire, CB – Dallas Renegades

#84: Brenden Rice, WR – Colorado Stampede

#100: Kyron Drones, RB – Dallas Renegades

Each of these players has been a member of the Packers' camp or practice squad rosters, but none saw regular-season action with Green Bay. Herring was let go when Packers players reported pre-draft, but five of the other six players were with the Packers for at least part of training camp in the 2026 season. Cornelius Johnson was in Green Bay in 2024.

Interestingly, former Packers quarterback Kyron Drones, who was waived by the team after the first preseason game in 2026, when Drones was 0-of-1 passing with three sacks taken, has been listed as a running back for the Dallas Renegades.

The Colorado Stampede will now have two former Green Bay running backs on the team, as Damien Martinez will join Eillis Merriweather in Colorado. For what it's worth, Martinez was just the second running back taken in the UFL draft, only behind Miles Davis, who went 49th.
