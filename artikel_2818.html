Marcus Smart suspended multiple games for punching Warriors player in Rockets win

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

A scuffle between the Houston Rockets and Golden State Warriors on Wednesday night has now led to a multi-game suspension for a key role player on a top team in the Western Conference to start the 2026-27 season. The NBA announced it's suspending Rockets guard Marcus Smart after he threw a punch at Warriors center Charles Bassey during a mid-game tussle that happened as Houston's Amen Thompson was taking the ball to the basket on a fastbreak.

Smart was hit with a three-game suspension by the NBA for throwing the punch. He was assessed with a Flagrant-2 foul and was subsequently ejected from the game after the play. He'll miss games against the Utah Jazz, Washington Wizards, and Miami Heat, with the last two being road games.
