Cardinals rookie QB in line to make NFL debut after Gardner Minshew injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

It took three days for the Arizona Cardinals' latest quarterback personnel decision to create more drama.

Rookie quarterback Carson Beck appears set to make his NFL debut in Week 10 after veteran Gardner Minshew sustained a potentially season-ending injury in the Cardinals' Week 9 loss to the Seattle Seahawks.

Beck spent the first eight weeks of the season as the Cardinals' No. 3 quarterback on the depth chart. Arizona's decision to trade Jacoby Brissett to the Baltimore Ravens on Friday, Nov. 6 moved the rookie to the backup role behind Minshew in Week 9. One week later, a window has opened for Beck to make his first start.

Minshew, who took over the starting job for the Cardinals after Brissett was benched last week, appeared to injure his arm when taking a sack before halftime on Sunday, Nov. 8. He managed to return and played the rest of the game, but reports emerged on Monday, Nov. 9 that he may need season-ending surgery to repair a torn UCL in his throwing arm.

ESPN's Adam Schefter reported Minshew "is seeking additional medical opinions" before undergoing the procedure.

The Cardinals do not have another quarterback on their active roster or on their practice squad.

Beck, the Cardinals' third-round pick in the 2026 NFL Draft, finished his collegiate career at Miami (Fla.) after spending two seasons as the starting quarterback at Georgia. The former Bulldog led the Hurricanes to an appearance in the CFP National Championship, where they fell to the Indiana Hoosiers and 2026 No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza.

Minshew "likely will need season-ending surgery on a torn UCL" in his throwing arm, according to ESPN reporting. The elbow injury, which is much more common in baseball pitchers, is similar to the one that 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy sustained in the NFC championship clash with the Eagles after the 2022 season.

The Cardinals' starter seemed to suffer his arm injury at the end of the first half of his team's Week 9 loss to the Seahawks. Edge rusher Dante Fowler managed to get a hand on Minshew's arm as the quarterback began his throwing motion, stripping the ball from Minshew's grip.

Minshew immediately grabbed at his right forearm after the contact from Fowler. When the play was over, he rolled on the ground in apparent pain before getting up and running off the field.

Despite the injury, which happened on the last play of the second quarter, the veteran managed to stay in the game after halftime. Minshew played out the rest of his first start of the season, attempting 18 more throws and completing 11 of them in the second half.

Further evaluation of Minshew's injury revealed the extent of the injury. Minshew is reportedly seeking alternate opinions before undergoing the season-ending procedure to repair the torn elbow ligament.
