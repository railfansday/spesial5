Burrow dons Pikachu-themed hoodie, neck pillow for trip to Spain

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Cincinnati Bengals shared video via Twitter/X of quarterback Joe Burrow boarding the team flight to Madrid wearing a yellow Pikachu-themed hoodie and neck pillow.

Burrow and the Bengals departed Tuesday night ahead of a Week 9 matchup with the Atlanta Falcons in order to arrive in Spain several days before the game and get acclimated.

Burrow has made it known previously that he is a big Pokémon fan. He discussed his collection of Pokémon cards as he talked to reporters in Dec. 2025.

Bengals head coach Zac Taylor was asked whether he and the team will do any sightseeing while they're in Spain. Taylor also talked about facing Jessie Bates III for the first time since Bates left the Bengals for the Falcons.
