Ukraine damages Putin’s prized Crimea bridge as Zelenskyy lauds ‘unprecedented operation’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Ukraine has damaged the Kerch Bridge linking Russia to the occupied Crimean Peninsula, delivering a symbolic blow to President Vladimir Putin as Kyiv reels from strikes on its own infrastructure.

Sergey Aksyonov, the Russia-installed governor of Crimea, confirmed that the strategically important bridge, which had been briefly closed on Monday, was attacked overnight for the second time in two days.

Videos and photos circulating on social media appeared to show the partial destruction of the bridge, with one section having collapsed into the water. CNBC was unable to independently verify the footage.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy described the attack as an "unprecedented operation" several weeks in the making, with Ukraine's defense forces, security services and weapons manufacturers involved.

"Russia strikes our infrastructure every day, including bridges in Zaporizhzhia, Dnipro, Kyiv, and the Odesa region," Zelenskyy said Tuesday on X.

"We are restoring our infrastructure. But we are also reminding Russia that war will always return to where it began. Ukraine's defensive capabilities are strong and will remain so, and we will never leave terror against our cities and people unanswered," he added.

Train and vehicle traffic was suspended due to the attack. No injuries have been reported.

Ukraine has repeatedly targeted the bridge since Russia launched its full-scale invasion in early 2022.

A 12-mile crossing of road and railways, the Kerch Bridge is one of Putin's signature projects. The crossing, sometimes referred to as the Crimean bridge, was built on his orders shortly after the Kremlin forcibly annexed Crimea in 2014 to support Moscow's claims to the territory.

Rail traffic on the bridge was opened on one track, Russia's Ministry of Transport said in a statement via Telegram on Tuesday, while restoration work continues on the second section.

The co-owner and chief engineer of Ukrainian missile and drone maker Fire Point, Denys Shtilierman, appeared to confirm the company's role in the attack, following reports that a Ukrainian Flamingo cruise missile was used in the strike.

"We dedicate this to the memory of our friends and colleagues Andriy and Yuriy, without whom this would not have been possible. R.I.P.," Shtilierman said via X on Tuesday. A spokesperson at Fire Point separately confirmed the company's role in the attack.

Analysts at the Institute for the Study of War, a Washington-based think tank, said Ukraine's attacks on the Kerch Bridge were likely part of Kyiv's ongoing and systematic strike campaign aimed at denying Russian forces the use of major ground lines of communication via Crimea.

Russian forces, meanwhile, struck two bridges crossing the Dnipro River in Kyiv.

Vitaliy Klitschko, the mayor of Ukraine's capital, said via Telegram on Tuesday that specialists were working to assess the extent of the damage to the North and South bridges.

Klitschko said Russian attacks on Kyiv overnight had also caused disruptions to water and heating supplies.

The strikes come ahead of Putin's trip to China this week for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum in Shenzhen, where a trilateral meeting with U.S. President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping is planned on Thursday.

Asked whether Ukraine's latest attacks might be connected to the upcoming trilateral summit, Kremlin spokesperson Dmitry Peskov said this could not be ruled out.

"Combat operations continue, and our military is striking targets that are somehow connected to Ukraine's military-industrial complex. And Ukraine is continuing its terrorist attacks," Peskov said, according to Russian state news outlet Ria Novosti.
