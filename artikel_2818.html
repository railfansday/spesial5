&#8220;I think he could be top&#8221; &#8211; Gary Lineker heaps praise on Liverpool star

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Liverpool have a habit of turning promise into production. This season, two teenagers have pushed that tradition forward, Rio Ngumoha with the flash and Jeremy Jacquet with the substance.

Ngumoha grabbed attention again with his display against Manchester United. That is what attackers do. They make noise, they fill clips, they drive headlines. He is rightly regarded as one of the best young talents in world football and his progress has been obvious for months.

Still, if the louder story is Ngumoha, the stronger case may belong to Jacquet. Centre-backs do not usually get the same oxygen, unless they make mistakes. Jacquet has done the opposite. He has looked calm, quick, progressive and, most importantly, dependable.

The clearest example came in Liverpool's 3-0 win over Club Brugge, when Jacquet led the defence in the absence of Virgil van Dijk. For a young defender, that is the sort of assignment that exposes you. Instead, he handled it and looked comfortable doing so.

There is no mystery as to why people are taking notice. "I think he's really good Jacquet," said the former England striker. "I think he could be top." That is blunt and accurate. The tools are obvious.

"His potential is huge. He's only young, isn't he? But he's quick, he's really good on the ball and he likes to look forward.

"That's what you want as a forward. You want your centre-halves to try and pick you out and break the lines. He's really good at it."

Jacquet's biggest strength may be the one modern elite sides value most in a central defender, his ability to move the game forward. He does not pass for the sake of statistics. He breaks lines, commits opponents and starts attacks before they properly form.

That matters for Liverpool. It gives the midfield a head start and gives forwards earlier service. It also explains why he has looked such a natural fit in this side.

Ngumoha brings the attacking edge, Jacquet brings the platform. Between them, Liverpool have two elite-level young players influencing games at both ends of the pitch.

There will be difficult moments. There always are with young players, especially defenders. But this has already gone beyond vague talk about upside. Jacquet is not playing like a prospect, he is playing like someone who belongs. Ngumoha is doing much the same in advanced areas.

Liverpool do not need to wait years to find out what they have. The evidence is already on the pitch. Two young players, two major talents, and one clear conclusion, the future has arrived early at Anfield.
