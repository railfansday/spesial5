J.J. McCarthy, Cam Skattebo return to full participation Tuesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Giants quarterback J.J. McCarthy and running back Cam Skattebo returned to full participation in Tuesday's practice. The two players were listed as limited in Monday's walk-through.

McCarthy has a hip injury that briefly forced him out of Sunday's 19-11 win over the Eagles, and Skattebo has a hamstring injury.

Wide receiver Darnell Mooney (chest) also returned to full participation on Tuesday's report.

Outside linebacker Abdul Carter (knee/back) and linebacker Malik Harrison (groin) remained limited, and cornerback Colton Hood (finger) was added to the report as limited. Coach John Harbaugh said Hood dislocated a finger.

Right tackle Jermaine Eluemunor (calf), tight end Theo Johnson (neck), wide receiver Charlie Jones (thumb), wide receiver Malik Nabers (knee) and left tackle Andrew Thomas (groin) were full participants again.
