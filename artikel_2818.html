AFC playoff picture: Browns get some good news on the bye week

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Cleveland Browns hit their bye week at the perfect time.

Cleveland picked up a much-needed win in Week 10 to ensure they did not spend their off week staring at a three-game losing streak.

The Browns, like every other team, are dealing with injuries to some key players, and a week off should do defensive tackles Mason Graham, Maliek Collins, and Mike Hall Jr., among others, some good.

Sitting at 6-4 and in the playoff race in the AFC also provided everyone with a break from the nonsensical and pointless discussion about the quarterback position.

While the Browns were relaxing, reading books, and playing Grand Theft Auto VI, they also received some good news as the Pittsburgh Steelers lost to the Philadelphia Eagles, the twelve consecutive loss by the Steelers in Philadelphia.

With the early games in the books, let's take a look at the current AFC North standings and the playoff picture in the AFC.

Cincinnati Bengals: 7-2 (play Monday night against Washington)

Cleveland Browns: 6-4 (2-1 in the division)

Pittsburgh Steelers: 6-4 (2-2 in the division)

Baltimore Ravens: 4-7 (0-2 in the division)

Los Angeles Chargers: 1-9 (playing at 4:05 pm)
