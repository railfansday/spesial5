Aaron Rodgers clears waivers, officially a free agent following Wednesday's release from Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Veteran quarterback Aaron Rodgers passed through waivers on Thursday and was not claimed, according to multiple reports.

Rodgers was released by the Pittsburgh Steelers on Wednesday and was subject to waivers. After going unclaimed, the quarterback is now free to sign with any team, though it is unclear which teams may be interested given how he conducted himself this week.

News broke after 11 p.m. ET Tuesday night that Rodgers was not only being benched for the team's Black Friday game against the Denver Broncos, but would also be inactive.

Rodgers responded by joining "The Pat McAfee Show" on Wednesday afternoon and going scorched-earth on head coach Mike McCarthy for about 35 minutes. The quarterback went off on McCarthy, accusing him of sleeping in meetings, not watching film, and saying, "1-2-3 Packers!" when breaking down a halftime huddle with players.

While the writing was on the wall that Rodgers could be headed for a release, his interview with McAfee certainly sealed it. During his appearance, the quarterback even said he was not going to return to Pittsburgh.

The soon-to-be 43-year-old is now a free agent. Rodgers could land with a team that has quarterback injury problems or is right on the playoff bubble, but his conduct on Wednesday reportedly means that his options might be even more limited.

Rodgers may have played the final game of his illustrious career, but his tenure with the Steelers will almost assuredly be remembered for his rant on Wednesday.
