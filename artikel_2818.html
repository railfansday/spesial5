Brock Purdy ruled out of Seahawks vs. 49ers rematch

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

In a divisional showdown with significant playoff implications, the San Francisco 49ers will be without quarterback Brock Purdy against the Seattle Seahawks after the team officially ruled him out with an ankle injury on Friday.

Former New England Patriots first-round pick Mac Jones will get the start in place of the injured Purdy in the second game between the Seahawks and 49ers this season. Jones has never made a start against the Seahawks, only briefly appearing in the NFC Divisional Round game last year at Lumen Field as the 49ers waved the white flag. In that garbage-time action, Jones completed two of his four passing attempts for 4 yards.

Jones has served valiantly as the 49ers backup, going 5-3 as a starter last year while Purdy was out. The backup got starting reps throughout the week, so head coach Kyle Shanahan will have had the time to get Jones up to speed before the game.

Purdy didn't practice all week, including on Friday, with an ankle injury suffered in Mexico City against the Minnesota Vikings last Sunday. The 49ers quarterback has struggled against Mike Macdonald-led defenses his entire career, a trend that the Seahawks will look to continue with Jones.

This game carries massive weight in the NFC playoff picture as the Seahawks, 6-4, are looking to chase down the 7th seed, while the 49ers, 9-1, hope to stay in the driver's seat for the top seed in the conference. Seattle is set to get their starting quarterback, Sam Darnold, and several key defensive players back before this matchup, which may flip the playoff picture on its head. That pivotal matchup will kick off at 1:25 p.m. PST on FOX from Santa Clara.
