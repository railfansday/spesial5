‘Dancing With the Stars’ Grammy Night Attracts Star-Studded Performances as a Frontrunner Is Eliminated: See the Scores, Who Went Home

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Couples also competed in the dance-off, which saw two duos perform the same dance style and song at the same time.

[This story contains spoilers for episode 10 of Dancing With the Stars season 35.]

Dancing With the Stars celebrated the best in music for its first-ever Grammy Night.

During the musical-themed night of competition, couples performed dances inspired by different Grammy-nominated and winning artists and songs. Plus, they also competed in the dance-off, which saw two duos perform the same dance style and song at the same time.

The judges were tasked with naming a winning duo in each round of the dance-off, who earned two additional points that were added to their overall scores. Each song performed in the dance-off round was performed live, with Brandy and Monica, Maren Morris and Sheryl Crow present in the ballroom. At the end of Grammy Night, Jenna Dewan and Val Chmerkovskiy were eliminated from DWTS season 35. It was a shocking elimination, after the couple finished as the second-best scoring couple of the night.

Celebrities still competing on season 35 include Tatyana Ali, Ezra Frech, Amber Glenn, Maura Higgins, Harry Shum Jr. and Connor Wood.

Harry and Jenna Johnson earned immunity from the dance-off round after last week’s Disney Night.

Grammy Night saw Jenna and her partner Val Chmerkovskiy perform a jazz to “All for You” by Janet Jackson, whom the Step Up star previously danced for. After they hit the ballroom floor, DWTS played a video sent in by Miss Jackson herself, wishing Jenna luck as she performed to her song. They scored a 38/30.

In more star-studded appearances, Tatyana and Jan Ravnik danced a tango to the Black Eyed Peas’ “Boom Boom Pow,” with will.i.am in the ballroom to support the duo. They scored a 27/30, earning their highest marks of the season thus far.

Notably, no couple earned a perfect 30/30 score on Grammy Night; Harry and Jenna came the closest with a 29/30 for their salsa to “Die With A Smile” by Lady Gaga and Bruno Mars.

During the dance-off round, Amber and Pasha; Jenna and Val; and Maura and Mark each won their respective rounds of competition, which added two points to their scores for the night.

Last week, Julia Stiles and Ezra Sosa were eliminated from season 35.

The couple with the lowest combined total of judges’ scores and viewer votes is eliminated at the end of each DWTS episode. Each viewer is allowed up to 10 votes per couple, per voting method (via ABC’s website and by text).

Live voting takes place during the East Coast airing of each show and ends shortly after the final performance; that means if you’re a West Coast viewer and want to vote, you must do so between 5 p.m. PT and roughly 6:45 p.m. PT, as voting ends after the final performance.
