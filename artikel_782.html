SiriusXM Names Tubi CEO Anjali Sud New Leader

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The exec will take over as CEO of the audio giant as Jennifer Witz steps down.

SiriusXM CEO Jennifer Witz is stepping down from the role with Tubi CEO Anjali Sud set to take over.

Sud, who has been a board member of SiriusXM since March 2025, will take over starting Jan. 1, 2027. Witz made the decision to step down after about 25 years at the company. She will remain in an advisory role through the end of 2027. Sud announced her departure from Tubi last week, after Fox’s acquisition of Roku was complete.

Sud has served as CEO of the Fox-owned Tubi since 2023, and has helped grow the free streaming business into a bigger contender in the entertainment marketplace, with more than 110 million monthly active users, and expanded the service globally. Sud joined Tubi after serving as CEO of Vimeo, where she took the company public and led its transformation into a profitable, global video platform with 300 million users. She previously held various positions in e-commerce, finance, and media at Amazon and Time Warner.

“Anjali has built some of the most successful consumer entertainment platforms. In her previous roles, she scaled audiences, expanded platforms, and led businesses supported by both advertising and subscription models. As a member of our Board, she has intimate knowledge of our business and strategy. With her digital media experience, CEO-level success, and Board perspective, we are confident Anjali will hit the ground running and build on our momentum as we continue to execute against our objectives. We believe she will be an outstanding CEO for SiriusXM,” said Greg Maffei, Chairman of the Board.

She joins SiriusXM after Witz’s long tenure, with the CEO saying that “now is the time for a transition.” Witz also did not take the stage at SiriusXM’s event for advertisers in early October.
