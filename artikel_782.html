From conceding seven to Hull heroics - how Tzolakis recovered to defy Brighton

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

McBurnie stoppage-time double earns Hull win over 10-man Brighton

Hull boss Sergej Jakirovic used one word to describe the performance of his goalkeeper Konstantinos Tzolakis in Sunday's 2-0 Premier League win against Brighton.

"Unbelievable," he told Match of the Day.

"Especially after the international break, which was very difficult for him."

Tzolakis' previous outing had been for Greece against the Netherlands in the Uefa Nations League, where he and his side were humbled 7-0 by the hosts in Amsterdam.

Nine days later, his contribution to the Tigers' first league victory since 29 August was every bit as important as that of striker Oli McBurnie, who netted both goals in second-half stoppage time against the second-placed Seagulls.

Tzolakis made a series of top saves to keep the highest scorers in the top flight at bay and, when Brighton full-back Costinha was sent off in the 89th minute for a second bookable offence, the hosts seized their opportunity to clinch only their third win following their Premier League return.

McBurnie said the 24-year-old stopper had "kept Hull in a few games" this season and the club were "lucky to have him", while Tzolakis told Sky Sports: "We are a unit and a team.

"We play for each other. We showed a good team effort and we deserved the three points."

With the opportunity to move three points behind leaders Arsenal, Brighton looked most likely to score for long spells of Sunday's game, but just could not find a way past former Olympiacos goalkeeper Tzolakis, who joined Hull in August for a reported Â£20m fee.

He got down to make a vital block early on to deny Diego Gomez, who had slid in to meet a low cross from the left wing. It was the first of several occasions the Paraguay international would be frustrated in front of goal.

Tzolakis then raced out spreadeagled to foil Georginio Rutter with an outstretched left hand - a stop that BBC Radio 5 Live pundit and former striker Clinton Morrison described as "Peter Schmeichel-esque", referring to the legendary Denmark keeper.

Second-half efforts from distance by Pascal Gross and Lewis Dunk were dealt with, too.

Yasin Ayari went close with a thumping strike against the bar and when Brighton substitute Charalampos Kostoulas pounced on a short backpass to go through one-on-one, Tzolakis got a hand on his Greece team-mate's shot and Regan Slater was back on the goalline to head the ball to safety.

Former Liverpool midfielder Danny Murphy, a pundit on Sunday's Match of the Day, said: "We saw again how important Tzolakis is for Hull. He had one of those days where he was always in the right place at the right time.

"When you are a Hull player and you see the kind of saves Tzolakis made when he spread himself and kept it out with his foot, you start thinking that it could be your day.

"It happens a lot in football and, on the flip side, Brighton will have been thinking, 'are we ever going to score here?'."

Hull made a superb start to the campaign after being promoted via the Championship play-offs in May.

They followed victories against Manchester United and Coventry with a draw against Aston Villa - all without conceding a goal.

The Tigers had not won since that 1-0 success at Coventry until beating Brighton - but they are one of four top-flight sides still unbeaten at home and have not lost any fixture by more than a one-goal margin.

They lost 1-0 to champions Arsenal before the international break and Scotland forward McBurnie told Match of the Day: "We know we can make it difficult for the top teams in the league and we've proved that in the past two games.

"We want to make it as difficult as we can for teams to come here and make the MKM Stadium a tough place to come. It's proving that way so far, so long may it continue."
