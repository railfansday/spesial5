The Best Tech Gifts to Shop at Best Buy This Holiday Season, From Bose Headphones to the Oura Ring

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Finding the perfect holiday gift for the tech enthusiast in your life can be tricky, especially when they’re already equipped with the latest smartphones, headphones and smart home gadgets. But this year’s most giftable electronics go beyond the usual upgrades, offering new ways to capture memories, stream entertainment and track wellness.

Best Buy is the best online retailer for holiday shopping because of its My Best Buy Plus, the retailer’s $29.99 annual membership, which offers exclusive member pricing on select products, 1% back in rewards on eligible purchases, free two-day shipping and an extended 60-day return and exchange window on most items. The membership also provides access to members-only sales and offers, making it particularly useful for anyone planning to check several names off their holiday shopping list. Below, find four tech gifts worth splurging on this holiday season:

For the aspiring filmmaker or content creator, Insta360’s X5 action camera offers a considerable upgrade from traditional smartphone footage. Equipped with dual sensors and 8K 360-degree video recording, the pocket-sized device captures everything happening around the user, allowing them to choose their framing after filming. Its AI-powered editing features make it easier to transform raw footage into polished videos, while an invisible selfie-stick effect creates cinematic, third-person perspectives without requiring an actual camera operator. It’s an especially clever gift for travelers, outdoor adventurers and anyone looking to elevate their TikTok or YouTube content.

For the urban commuter who’s tired of public transportation, Segway’s Max G3 electric scooter offers a faster, more flexible way to get around. The high-performance model boasts a top speed of up to 28 mph and a maximum advertised range of 50 miles on a single charge, along with a suspension system designed to smooth out uneven city streets. It also features built-in Apple Find My integration, making it easier to locate the scooter when it’s parked. It’s a practical splurge for the person who’s always on the move, provided local riding regulations allow it.

A reliable pair of noise-canceling headphones is a holiday gift that’s almost impossible to get wrong, particularly when they come from Bose. The second generation of the brand’s QuietComfort Ultra headphones combines premium sound quality with advanced noise cancellation and a lightweight, cushioned design that’s comfortable enough for long flights or marathon listening sessions. Bose’s Immersive Audio technology adds a spatial dimension to music, while the headphones’ new Cinema Mode is designed to make movies and television shows sound more immersive. With up to 30 hours of battery life under standard listening conditions, they’re a worthwhile upgrade for music lovers, frequent flyers and dedicated binge-watchers alike.

For the wellness enthusiast who already owns every fitness gadget imaginable, Oura‘s smart ring packs an impressive amount of health-tracking technology into a piece of everyday jewelry. The sleek titanium ring monitors sleep, activity, heart rate and recovery, translating the data into personalized insights through the Oura app. Unlike a bulky smartwatch, its discreet design makes it easy to wear around the clock, whether at the gym, in the office or in bed. Available in multiple metallic finishes, it’s an especially stylish option for anyone interested in understanding their daily habits. Keep in mind that a separate Oura membership is required to unlock the device’s full range of insights.
