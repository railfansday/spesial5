A 'wow' player - why Ngumoha is already a Liverpool game-changer

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Rio Ngumoha is going to be a 'wow' player for Liverpool - and he showed everyone exactly what he is already capable of in Sunday's 4-0 win over Manchester United.

He made an outstanding assist then added a fine finish, and there is much more to come. He plays without fear so he is going to score and make many more goals the same way.

Ngumoha has only just turned 18 but he is already a game-changer, someone who gets people excited because he is so hard to stop when he is one-on-one.

Of course he can frustrate you at times too, but that's what creative and explosive wingers like him do - and it's because he is so talented that everyone wants to see an end product from him every time he gets on the ball.

There were times against United when Ngumoha did not quite deliver, but he is still so young and I would much rather see players like him take too many touches - or try too many things and fail - than be afraid to try them in the first place.

So, if he runs out of play or runs into too many players at times, because teams are doubling up on him like United were, that just shows how scared they are of him.

His ability means he occupies opposition players - Youri Tielemans was always backing up Diogo Dalot on his wing to try to help him out, but that just meant United were leaving someone else free.

Sometimes it is actually harder when you are playing out wide against 10 men because you don't get the same amount of space - United were not attacking the same way they would have done with 11, and they were sitting in more.

Yes, Ngumoha ran up the odd blind alley or got outnumbered but that is always going to happen. As he learns, and gets even better, his decision-making will improve too. He will know when to try to go past people or when to find that free man instead.

But he still showed all the bravery you want from a winger against United and, when you keep trying things the way he did, something is going to come off for you.

I look at Bradley Barcola in a similar way to Ngumoha - he can be frustrating but he tries things, and can be brilliant.

For Liverpool right now, one of the biggest positives is the competition they have in wide areas, with those two plus Victor Munoz and Cody Gakpo.

There are other areas of their squad, like left-back or central midfield, where that competition needs to improve, but I don't really worry about the wide attacking areas in the same way.

Sunday was only Ngumoha's second Premier League start of the season, and his first since the opening day.

He maybe only got his chance because Gakpo has a calf problem but, when everyone is fit, it is really exciting that they will all be thinking they have to play their best to push the manager and get in the team.

There were plenty of other positives for Liverpool, too. All in all, Sunday could not have gone much better for them.

United made a woeful start with poor concentration and mistakes which cost them but, from there, Liverpool saw the game out well.

Just because the opposition has a man sent off early on does not mean any kind of victory is a given, let alone a 4-0 win.

Look at how United's 11 men lost to City's 10 in the Manchester derby earlier this season - we've seen many times how good teams can frustrate a side with an extra player like that.

It definitely does not mean you are going to steamroller them, but Liverpool did that and made an abundance of chances to get even more goals.

Days like this are so important for building belief in all their attacking players, especially because they were playing at home, and against their big rivals.

Up until now, it had felt like Liverpool's better days this season had come on the road, but now the pressure and expectation is off them a bit - particularly if, like Ngumoha or Florian Wirtz, you have scored and played well.

I know from experience that, the next time they go back to Anfield, they will be buzzing. You know that the fans are already with you after you've scored against United, and you feel the love a little bit more.

This is the start of a big week for Liverpool and Iraola will probably make changes against Club Brugge in Belgium on Wednesday, knowing how difficult Sunday's Merseyside derby will be.

Everton is always a tough game but I think Liverpool will enjoy their trip to Hill Dickinson Stadium, and the confidence they will have after beating United will mean they can go there with a really positive mindset.

Danny Murphy was speaking to BBC Sport's Chris Bevan.
