Zainab Johnson on ditching scripts, navigating double standards in stand-up & crafting Toxically Optimistic

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

On Mashable's Say More, hosts Kristy Puchko (Mashable's Entertainment Editor) and Mark Stetson (Senior Creative Producer) bring humor and their trusted insights to the biggest shows, films, digital trends, and cultural moments. From viral-worthy rants and passionate raves to smart recaps and first-look teasers, they cover what everyone is talking about. Celebrity guests join the conversation for real talk about their careers, upcoming projects, and what’s trending online.

Zainab Johnson is a commanding voice in stand-up, known for conversational warmth, sharp storytelling, and a refusal to let anyone box in her perspective. Over 16 years of grinding through open mics in Los Angeles, taping her debut special Hijabs Off, and releasing her Netflix hour Toxically Optimistic, executive produced by Barack and Michelle Obama’s Higher Ground, she has built a reputation for comedy that turns personal stories into universal truths.

In this interview, Johnson sits down with Mashable's Executive Producer, Mark Stetson, to discuss the evolution of her latest hour, Toxically Optimistic, navigating double standards in the industry, and why protecting her artistic process matters more than chasing viral moments.

Johnson looks back on trading a path as a math teacher for Los Angeles open mics, ditching written scripts to find her real voice on stage, and the unforgettable Delta flight attendant job interview that kept her on her comedy path.

Johnson then dives into the craft behind her specials: treating Hijabs Off like a comedy graduation and Toxically Optimistic like a celebration, weaving together her viral opossum bit (Eugene), and challenging the double standards Black and Muslim women face in comedy.

Johnson also addresses the pressure of streaming and social media algorithms: why comics today rush specials too early, why she refused to front-load her Netflix hour, and why nothing replaces experiencing stand-up live in the room.

Zainab Johnson's two hour-long specials are now streaming: Hijabs Off on Prime Video and Toxically Optimistic on Netflix.
