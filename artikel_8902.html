ruko f11pro 2 plus (rc4) drone reshapes entry-level flight to democratize aerial storytelling

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

ruko breaks the mold of entry-level drones with the F11PRO 2 plus (RC4), pairing a touchscreen controller and 48-megapixel camera to make steady aerial filmmaking accessible to travelers, families, and beginners.

mariana rodrigues November 24, 2026 4 min

With the F11PRO 2 Plus (RC4), Ruko breaks the mold of what an entry-level drone can do, removing the setup friction that usually slows down first-time flyers. From the moment it leaves the bag, the process takes seconds to unfold the compact frame, press a single power button, and send the aircraft skyward without ever reaching for a smartphone or tangled cable.

Its dedicated 5.5-inch touchscreen controller links automatically on startup, putting a 48-megapixel camera, a three-axis mechanical gimbal, and one-touch intelligent flight modes immediately at the pilot’s fingertips. Designed for beginners, travelers, and families, the folding drone turns spontaneous outings into cinema-grade perspectives to democratize high-end aerial storytelling.

Across a catalog spanning aerial drones, smart robots, remote-control vehicles, and charging gear, Ruko builds approachable electronics meant to bring families together through hands-on curiosity. Instead of chasing technical complexity for its own sake, the company pairs clean industrial forms with intuitive engineering that anyone can pick up and enjoy. With the F11PRO 2 Plus (RC4) drone, that focus on everyday usability puts framing landscapes and sharing moments ahead of troubleshooting hardware.

Central to the flight experience is the RC4 smart controller, a standalone unit built around a 5.5-inch 1080p touchscreen that reaches 1,200 nits of brightness. Even under direct midday sunlight, pilots can check live flight data and frame shots clearly without squinting at a dim phone screen. Automatic pairing links the drone upon startup, while 2K dual-screen mirroring lets friends and family follow the live aerial feed on a connected smartphone, turning a solo flight into a shared activity.

Above the lens, a 48-megapixel camera sensor records 8K still photographs at 7680 by 4320 resolution alongside smooth 4K video at 30 frames per second. When daylight fades over lakeside sunsets or evening campsites, an automated night mode balances brightness and shadow detail without requiring manual adjustments. Beneath the housing, a three-axis mechanical brushless gimbal counteracts wind gusts and sudden turns in real time, keeping footage steady without cropping into the frame.

Beyond manual control, automated flight modes handle complex camera movements with minimal pilot input. Subject tracking offers Focus, Follow, and Companion settings to keep hikers, boats, or family outings centered without constant stick corrections, while preset flight paths such as Rocket, Dronie, Orbit, Comet, and Helix join GPS waypoint planning and cruise control to capture sweeping perspectives at a single tap.

For extended outdoor trips, the Ruko drone delivers up to 37 minutes of flight per battery, Level 6 wind resistance, over-the-air firmware updates, and dual-antenna transmission reaching 10 kilometers. Multi-sensor positioning using GPS, VIO, TOF, and a barometer works alongside landing pad recognition, customizable return-to-home options, and a locating buzzer to keep every landing predictable and secure.
