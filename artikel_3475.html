Hoda Kotb Addresses Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade Being Postponed

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Hoda Kotb is speaking out after the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade was postponed for the first time in its 100-year history.

“It’s OK,” Kotb, 62, said on the Wednesday, November 25, episode of Late Night With Seth Meyers. “We waited 100 years for this parade… we’re gonna wait two more days. We’re gonna have it on Saturday. It’s gonna be a beautiful, sunsplash day.”

Kotb said the weather forecast for the parade’s original date on Thursday, November 26, “was not gonna be fun.”

“The winds were like tropical storm force. There are a bunch of kids who were marching and people with the balloons,” the former Today show cohost reasoned. “Safety first.”

“The people have been preparing this 100-year parade, [they] have been working tirelessly for a year-plus,” Kotb continued. “The musical acts are incredible. So imagine spending all that time painting every little bit of every float, wondering if the float is going to fly away somewhere.

“So the good news is, to me, I’m relieved. Saturday’s gonna be amazing. Everybody’s still hanging out. We’re gonna watch it anyway,” Kotb said of the parade’s new date on Saturday, November 28.

She added, “And during the parade piece that’s usually on tomorrow, guess what? We’re gonna watch an old parade and you won’t know the difference, OK? It’s gonna be spectacular.”

Macy’s announced the postponement of its annual Thanksgiving Day parade in New York City due to adverse weather conditions in a statement to Us on Wednesday.

“With severe weather expected on Thanksgiving Day, and out of an abundance of caution, in partnership with the City, the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade will be postponed,” the statement read.

It continued, “In close coordination with our City and agency partners, the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade will move to Saturday, Nov. 28, with an expected step-off at 8:30 a.m.”

“Our team is now working through the complex operational logistics required to bring the Parade to life on Saturday, and we will share additional details as they are finalized,” Macy’s added. “We are incredibly grateful to the thousands of volunteers, performers and partners who make this extraordinary tradition possible. We can’t wait to celebrate 100 years of Parade magic together on Saturday.”

The postponement marks the first time in the parade’s history that it has been delayed due to weather. The event was previously canceled during World War II between 1942 and 1944.
