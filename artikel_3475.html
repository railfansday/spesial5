This Terrifying GTA 6 Easter Egg Will Give You Nightmares

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Rockstar's GTA 6 features a massive open world to explore, and people continue to make new discoveries, including a terrifying killer clown Easter egg.

It's been discovered that the Haymarket building in Vice Beach County features an elevator filled with balloons. That's creepy, but not the worst. However, after entering the elevators, the doors close and the balloons fall to the ground. As this is happening, carnival music begins to play.

The balloons then mysteriously float back up to the ceiling of the elevator, as the lights flicker and the music takes on an eerie tone. Then the lights go out and a killer clown with blood on his face and a knife in his hands charges at you as the lights go out. You don't die in this interaction, but maybe that's the point: to instill even more horror that makes you question if what you saw was even real.

There are lots of Easter eggs in GTA 6, including an invincible NPC who teleports and a literal Easter egg. You can also travel through time via an elaborate Easter egg. For more, check out GameSpot's rundown of all the best Easter eggs in GTA 6.

You can also check out GameSpot’s GTA 6 review. If you’re just getting started with the game, be sure to read GameSpot’s top five best things to do first. The game's first patch is out now as well.

GTA 6 sold 32.5 million copies via pre-purchases alone, and the game has now reportedly reached 45 million units sold and $4 billion in revenue.
