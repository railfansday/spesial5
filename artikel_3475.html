The BBC has unveiled new details about an upcoming sci-fi thriller series, which is headlined by a former Marvel Cinematic Universe star and a memorable Game of Thrones actor.

Two talented performers who have impacted viewers with standout roles in landmark series are starring in an exciting upcoming project produced by the BBC. After one of them starred in what many consider the MCU's best show and the other left a massive mark on Game of Thrones history, they are teaming up to headline an ambitious new series.

On November 19, the BBC released new details and images from an upcoming sci-fi thriller series titled Sutherland that is set in the near future. The show will take place both in deep space, as it focuses on the crew of the U.K.'s first crewed space mission, and on Earth, where a missing biologist suddenly reappears, adding mystery to the story.

One of the BBC's freshly released Sutherland images shows members of the crew of astronauts appearing anxious as they are strapped to their chairs. Other photos spotlight the show's actors on Earth looking deeply concerned, with one of them featuring a police officer in the background.

Sutherland is produced by World Productions Scotland and is created and written by James Smythe, who has penned episodes of Vigil, The Last Kingdom, and All Her Fault. The upcoming show is set to launch on BBC iPlayer and BBC One sometime in 2027.

The update from the BBC revealed that Sutherland will star Gugu Mbatha-Raw and Stuart Martin. Many people know Mbatha-Raw as one of the stars of Loki, as she embodied Judge Ravonna Lexus Renslayer, a.k.a. Rebecca Tourminet, in nine episodes of that series. She has also starred in movies like Belle, Beyond the Lights, and The Cloverfield Paradox, as well as other shows like Black Mirror and Doctor Who.

A Geektown article about Sutherland stated that Michael McElhatton is also set to star in the BBC's upcoming show. Even though he is a veteran actor with an extremely long filmography, many people will always know him best for portraying Game of Thrones' Roose Bolton. After all, the lord of House Bolton is responsible for the Red Wedding, and he is the father of the vicious, fearsome, and cunning Ramsay Bolton.

Even though his character didn't orchestrate the most shocking Game of Thrones moment as McElhatton's did, Stuart Martin appeared in an episode of the series as a Lannister soldier. More notably, the Scottish actor has appeared in shows like Miss Scarlet and The Duke, Babylon, Medici: Masters of Florence, and Jamestown.

Sutherland's other confirmed cast members include Iain De Caestecker, Phyllis Logan, Ivana Miličević, Tibu Fortes, Amy Manson, Oli Fyne, Jacob Hauberg Lohmann, Nicholas Rowe, Sam Hazeldine, Ingrid Oliver, and James Mackenzie. Considering the series has an intriguing story and an impressive cast, including actors from the MCU and Game of Thrones, there is every reason to have high hopes that it will become a hit.
