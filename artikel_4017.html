These 7 PS5 Games Launched on the Same Day as GTA 6 Like Mad Men

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

What’s the definition of insanity? It’s got to be launching your game on the exact same day as GTA 6, which is likely to be the biggest release in history.

I do remember hearing, in the PS3 days, that some smaller publishers would specifically launch alongside Call of Duty titles, because they argued they could benefit from the increased store foot traffic and pick up secondary sales.

I wonder if that’s true in the era of the PS Store, where most players are preloading in advance and probably not exploring too far beyond the titles promoted on the homepage.

Nevertheless, I think it takes a brave and stupid soul to go head-to-head with Rockstar like this, so I respect that.

And y’know what, I’m going to give every game that competed with the seminal open world its flowers on this very page:

The House of Tesla: Definitive Edition probably looks like the best pick, depending on your penchant for puzzlers.

We won’t be reviewing any of these games for obvious reasons, but to be brutally honest, I don’t think we would have even if GTA 6 hadn’t come out.

Nevertheless, it’s interesting to see so many titles release on the exact same day as the biggest game of all time. A few major live services got big updates too, like Disney Dreamlight Valley.
