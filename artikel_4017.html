Katsuhiro Harada Reveals The Pokémon He Wanted To Add To Pokken Tournament

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Famous Japanese game director and producer Katsuhiro Harada, known for the Bandai Namco series Tekken, has revealed a Pokémon he wanted to add to the Pokémon fighting game Pokken Tournament but it didn't make the cut.

Harada, who worked as a producer on both versions of the game, wanted the fighting / dark-type Pokémon 'Pangoro' from Generation VI. As for why he wanted to include it, Harada thought it had a "really cool" design and almost looked like it was wearing a traditional Japanese school uniform.

There were many discussions before finalising the roster, and a lot of it came down to the gameplay and systems in place, along with The Pokémon Company's own requests. Here's what Harada had to say about this (via EventHubs):

Harada: "We had thorough discussions before finalizing the roster. There were Pokémon that the development team wanted to include from a gameplay and systems perspective, and of course, The Pokémon Company also had its own requests for which Pokémon they wanted to see in the game."
