GTA 6 features a manual save system. You can do this at practically any time, giving you the freedom to create your checkpoint in Leonida and come back to the game at a later date. Given that there are close to 100 GTA 6 Missions, this is going to come in very handy – particularly as there is no way to replay missions in GTA 6.

The save system will also disable if you activate any GTA 6 cheats, forcing you to quit out of the game and reload a save that wasn't revelling in pure chaos.
