The 28 Best Gifts for Boys Who Love to Build, Tinker, and Create

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Gear-obsessed editors choose every product we review. We may earn commission if you buy from a link. Why Trust Us?

Budding geniuses deserve gifts that match their geeky interests. Whether it's robotics or chemistry or the mechanics of remote-control cars, the educational toy market is chock full of kits that help reinforce STEM (and STEAM) skills learned in school. They also give kids an off-screen activity that they’ll actually like doing.

Yet in the sea of options, there are only a handful of exceptional toys that truly stand out. We surveyed the landscape and handpicked the best of the best, guided by our familiarity with top brands and with our love of tinkering—an enriching activity people of all ages and genders love.

Here are our favorite gifts for boys (and any kids, really) who want to become the next generation of engineers, mechanics, carpenters, or astronomers.

This interactive Lego set has a cool twist. Kids help Luigi solve a mystery through a garden that they build themselves. The set comes with four interactive figures that have a slew of reactions—it’s like an IRL video game.

Kids solve puzzle cards and design a 3D roller coaster around the solution. There are 60 different challenges and they can team up to play or stay entertained by themselves.

Space enthusiasts will love this Artemis build that mimics a rocket launch. The 632-piece set isn’t for the faint of heart but yields an amazing build that displays three separates stages of a real takeoff.

The Snap Circuit set introduces kids to the wonderful world of electrical currents that keeps us running everyday. They can power a helicopter or fan, and even make a doorbell ring. There’s over 100 projects to choose from where they can create and learn along the way.

This tiny robot has a big personality to teach kids the basics of programming, algorithms, geometry, and more.It responds to voice cues and commands using the integrated app. Its versatility means kid will never get bored: Configure mechanical builds with the included set or program new behaviors and skills into the robot through coding.

This fun intro to logic and and brain teaser puzzles is like a 3D version of Sudoku. Kids can play one of the 100 puzzle cards with each other or by themselves.

This kit gives anyone the satisfaction of assembling a wearable mechanical hand. Once built, it can pick up objects using levers and the power of hydraulics, teaching kids about green energy in the process.

Sticky boogers, squishy eyeballs, and a handful of stringy guts to hold? Yes, please! This chemistry set lets kids create more than 40 truly disgusting experiments using included supplies and common household items. When it comes to slime, you can't lose.

Kids can tinker with this robot kit endlessly, turning it into one of twelve different configurations, each of which offers unique play. It runs on solar power or replaceable batteries, and includes 190 pieces and thorough instructions for building different animals and motions. Customers especially like Sillbird’s exceptional service when it comes to replacing faulty or missing parts.

This 116-piece gear set from Learning Resources introduces young engineers to basic building skills. They can follow instructions to create pre-designed configurations, or have the freedom to make up their own. Most importantly, they’ll learn how gears and simple machines work—not bad for a toy designed for five-year-olds.

Kids learn basic automotive principals while building this iconic British race car, which features working wheels and doors, and moving pistons inside the V12 engine. It contains more than 700 pieces and there's the option of using printed instructions or Lego's building app for assembly.

Kids can create tons of fun electronic gadgets using this kit and have fun learning along the way. There are more than 300 projects to explore using the base grid and snap-together pieces, plus a helpful color manual for projects and instructions.

Get ready to launch objects up to 15 feet with a catapult, crossbow, and canon based on Leonardo Da Vinci's original designs. It comes with laser-cut pieces for three models that each take from 1 to 3 hours to build.

We like that this one is a logic puzzle and a fun marble run, all in one. Assemble the cubes so that when a marble is dropped from the top it makes it all the way to the bottom. Kids can create the pre-designed builds, or make their own mazes once they understand the concept.

Kids can choose to arrange this domino maze however they like in hundreds of different chain reactions or complete the increasingly difficult challenges laid out in the included cards. The goal is to build a chain from one point to another and hit the designated target. This gift features challenges that get harder as obstacles like stairs, walls, and blockers come into play.

This cardboard model kit adds some hands-on fun for fans of the Nintendo Switch. Kids build the customizable steering wheel, submarine, and airplane by following instructions on the Switch, then use their creations to play games with the console. Each model has built-in games and everything they need is included to build the sets, even things like tape.

Kids who love to tinker are sure to go bananas while unwrapping this gift. The 95-piece set comes with a wrench, screwdrivers, a hammer, and other tools, plus safety goggles and gloves. There’s also a tool belt and bag. The manufacturer recommends adult supervision for use.

A physics lesson, science experiment, and toy combined, the Water Rocket Kit can fly 90 feet into the air harnessing water and air pressure alone. Everything needed to construct it is included, except for a pump.

Forget about a boring day off from school. This book, compiled by Popular Mechanics editors, packs over 300 fun activities, interesting facts, and experiments in one place to always keep your kids entertained. It’s best for ages 8 to 12 and is a great book to keep on hand during long road trips and summer breaks.

Adults are sure to have a hard time keeping their hands off this awesome metal model airplane set. It comes with 258 pieces plus the tools they'll need to assemble it, and spans nearly 16 inches wide and 14 inches long when constructed. It’s recommended for kids ages 8 to 12.
