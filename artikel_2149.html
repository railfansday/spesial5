Notts County confirm TV decision and it's sparked backlash

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Notts County's League One struggles look to be over after their four matches in a row lost in September and October, with Martin Paterson finally finding the winning formula again.

Paterson guided the Magpies back into League One earlier this year with a resounding League Two play-off final victory over Salford City, and he was backed by the Reedtz Brothers with some exciting cash signings and eye-catching loanees.

County were in the relegation zone of the third tier in mid-October after four defeats on the spin, but wins over Sheffield Wednesday and Mansfield Town, as well as a respectable draw at home to Stockport County, has seen the mood lift at Meadow Lane, and the pressure somewhat rise from Paterson's shoulders.

Last weekend, Notts County stepped away from league action to take part in Round One of the FA Cup, where they were handed a potential banana skin in the form of National League side Altrincham.

In-front of over 4,000 fans at Moss Lane, County went 2-1 behind to the fifth tier outfit, only for Cal Roberts to rescue the game in the 84th-minute and send it to extra time.

And despite the deteriorating foggy conditions, Notts County won out in the resulting penalty shootout, despite the lack of visibility for their supporters at the opposite end of the ground.

One of 40 clubs that were in Monday's second round draw, Notts were handed a tie against a club even further down the footballing pyramid than their previous opponents, with a trip to Northern Premier League side Guiseley on the horizon.

The West Yorkshire outfit, based around nine miles north of the city of Leeds, made it through to round two by beating Sutton United from two divisions above 1-0, and they have now been rewarded with another home clash with a much bigger outfit.

They got over 1,500 fans through the gates of Nethermoor Park last weekend, and that may be even bigger with the visit of a League One outfit, which was scheduled to take place on the weekend of December 5.

There was always a high likelihood though that given the gap in divisions between the two clubs that this would be moved for television coverage, and that's exactly what has happened - but it's perhaps got one of the unwanted slots.

No fewer than eight matches will be televised out of the 20 drawn out, with Guiseley against Notts County saved for a 7:30pm kick-off on December 7 - a Monday night.

It's fair to say that the Guiseley game being moved to a Monday night hasn't exactly gone down well with the Notts County fanbase.

Another Notts fan is disappointed that their weekend plans have been tampered with by the TV broadcasters.

This County supporter meanwhile believes the Monday night kick-off is detrimental as it will already be known who either them or Guiseley will play in Round Three of the cup, with the draw likely to take place pre-match.

Another County fan has had his chances of attending scuppered by TNT's decision to show the game on a Monday night - there is a handful of social media fans happy enough with the change, but they're in the minority.

Whilst ideally it would be a 3pm kick-off on the Saturday, the fact this was always going to be moved for television means a Saturday night slot or Sunday afternoon may have been preferred for the majority of Notts fans.
