Nicky Hayen eyed by new club after Burnley exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Burnley cut ties with the Belgian at the end of last month after a poor start to the Championship season. He was replaced by Fabio Celestini at Turf Moor.

Hayen, 46, was appointed by the Clarets in the summer after their relegation from the Premier League but he struggled to adapt to life in Lancashire. He won only one of his 14 matches at the helm before he was eventually given the chop following a 4-1 away defeat at Lincoln City.

In this latest update regarding his situation, the Press and Journal claim he has emerged on the radar of Aberdeen up in Scotland as they look to bring in a new head coach. They have recently parted ways with ex-Oldham Athletic and Morecambe man Stephen Robinson and they are in the hunt for someone new.

Burnley drew with both Portsmouth and QPR after Hayen’s departure before their frantic 5-4 loss against Cardiff City in South Wales last time out before the international break. They are back in action next Sunday with an away trip to Ewood Park to play rivals Blackburn Rovers.

Hayen was the wrong man for Burnley and wasn’t able to get a tune out of their squad. He arrived in England as a bit of an unknown quantity back in July.

The Clarets handed him a three-year deal running until June 2029. Celestini has now been left to pick up the pieces though and faces a tough job.

Burnley are in the drop zone along with Preston North End and Derby County. They are five points from safety behind both Lincoln City and Watford after their disappointing collapse at the Cardiff City Stadium.

Prior to his switch, Hayen spent seven months at Genk. He has also had spells in the past in his native Belgium at Sint-Truidense VV, SK Beveren and Club Brugge, as well as having a stint in Wales at Haverfordwest County.

A change of scene to Aberdeen would be good for Hayen. It can provide him with a fresh start and a new opportunity to prove himself.

The Dons are 11th in the league and risk slipping into the Scottish Championship if they aren’t careful. They have won three of their opening 12 matches and are two points above Kilmarnock at the bottom who they play up next.
