Positive emerges for Swansea City in Vitor Matos exit saga

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Swansea City are facing another fight to retain the services of head coach, Vitor Matos, with it emerging that French top-flight side, RC Strasbourg, have approached the Welsh side for his services.

The Swans have enjoyed an impressive start to the Championship season, as they sit sixth in the table after 15 games on 26 points, despite losing three of their last five encounters.

Matos' side were narrowly defeated before the November international break by current leaders, Birmingham City, at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park despite being a man down for the vast majority of the contest, and it may transpire that the loss in the West Midlands could be the 38-year-old's final game in charge.

Speculation involving the former Liverpool coach is nothing new to supporters in this part of South Wales in recent weeks, but one positive has emerged should Matos decide to move on to a third managerial role within the space of 12 months.

Matos was appointed by Swansea on November 24th of last year, three weeks after the Welsh outfit parted company with Alan Sheehan.

Jürgen Klopp's former Anfield assistant was lured from Maritimo through a reported £880,000 compensation fee, and subsequently transformed the Swans' form for the remainder of the season as they finished 11th - nine points outside the play-offs whilst boasting Golden Boot winner, Zan Vipotnik.

Having signed a one-year contract extension until June 2030 on July 31st, a strong first pre-season and summer transfer window has been backed up by a positive start, but it emerged on Monday that Strasbourg have approached Swansea over the potential appointment of Matos, with his compatriot, Hugo Oliveira, under pressure after just three wins this season.

Strasbourg are 13th in Ligue 1 at present, but would be forced to part with a significant sum of cash if they were to instill the highly-rated head coach at the Stade de la Meinau, and it has since been suggested that the figure being negotiated is in the region of £4m as Matos braces for his latest managerial change.

Matos won 22 of his first 47 matches in charge of Swansea, boasting a win percentage of 47%, following on from a 54% total in just 13 matches at his previous employers, Maritimo.

It is a testament to the work put in by the Portuguese that clubs are looking to poach him away from Swansea, and that recently included the Premier League's bottom club, Crystal Palace.

The Eagles were recently credited with interest in the Portuguese and Middlesbrough head coach, Kim Hellberg, before opting to confirm Thomas Frank as Pierre Sage's replacement at Selhurst Park on Monday afternoon.

Despite losing Ethan Galbraith to Stoke City for £10m, Matos changed the outlook of his Swansea side with 11 new recruits, with the likes of Joseph Opoku, Moussa Yeo and Stephen Welsh, already earning rave reviews.

Such a strong recruitment drive came after the penning of Matos' contract extension, after which he explained his sudden connection towards all aspects of the club as he aimed to drive the Swans forward.

"From the moment I arrived here, I have sensed how powerful the connection between Swansea City and the fans and community is," Matos said.

"This is something very special, it is a feeling myself and the players love, and the desire to keep building that and sharing great moments together is one of the key motivations in extending my contract with the club."
