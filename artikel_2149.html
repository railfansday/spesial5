Middlesbrough man urged to stay in January by ex-football chief amid ‘best option’ claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Middlesbrough could face a battle to keep hold of the attacker down the line amid expected attention from elsewhere. He is under contract until June 2031 after he penned a long-term five year contract in the last transfer window.

Lankshear, 21, joined Boro from Tottenham Hotspur. He has since scored 15 goals in 16 games in all competitions under the guidance of Kim Hellberg.

Brown, who used to work for Manchester United, has delivered his verdict on the forward, as per a report by Football Insider: “Will Lankshear’s best option would be to stay doing what he’s doing with Middlesbrough. He’s showing what he can do in the Championship at the moment, and it’s been key to his development that he’s been willing to drop down into that league to become a regular.”

He added: “If he was to make a move to the Premier League, is that form going to continue? You’d like to think so, but it’s far from certain. It’s a big jump. At the moment, he’s better off sticking with Middlesbrough where he knows he can be successful, helping them achieve their goals, and then seeing what’s next.”

Middlesbrough are unlikely to sell Lankshear this winter, especially halfway through a campaign. He has been linked with an exit to Newcastle United in the Premier League but a departure would be a surprise.

If Boro fail to go up, they will find it hard to retain his services next summer. He is one of the most exciting talents in the EFL right now and has a bright future ahead of him in the game.

He was snapped up by his current club to bolster their attacking department. The England youth international has since established himself as their most prized asset.

Lankshear, who is from Hertfordshire, knows where the back of the net is at second tier level. He is on course to surpass 30 goals at his current rate.

Middlesbrough have been without Lankshear in their last two outings due to injury. He sat out of their 2-0 loss away at Wrexham.

He was also missing in their 0-0 draw at Watford last time out before the international break. Boro seriously miss him when he isn’t playing and they need other players to step up in his absence.

Kyle Joseph joined them from Hull City in July but he has struggled to make an impact and he hasn’t scored in the league yet. David Strelec is another option but he has been injured this term and is still finding his feet again.

Middlesbrough are sat joint-top of the table along with Birmingham City and Stoke City as they look to reach the top flight under Hellberg. They were beaten in the play-off final by Hull City at Wembley back in May and are eager to bounce back with promotion.
