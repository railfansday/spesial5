How can Mauricio Pochettino help Manchester United players to perform again?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Shumail Sajid writes about Manchester United for Read Man Utd and Crystal Palace for Read Crystal Palace, with occasional pieces on other Premier League clubs. He has also written for FanSided's Red Devils Dispatch, Yardbarker, Anfield Central and The Sports Side.

Mauricio Pochettino has called on Manchester United to rediscover their winning mentality after agreeing to become the club’s new head coach.

Manchester United announced Pochettino’s appointment just over 24 hours after confirming the departure of Michael Carrick. The former Chelsea and Tottenham manager is believed to have agreed a two-and-a-half-year deal at Old Trafford.

He takes charge of United who sit in an embarrassing 18th place in the Premier League table. They have managed just one win in their opening 10 league matches, coming against Ipswich Town.

There is little time for the new head coach to settle in, with a difficult first fixture against fierce rivals, Liverpool already awaiting him.

Speaking to Manchester United, Mauricio Pochettino made it clear that restoring confidence in the team and improving performances will be his immediate priorities.

“The most important thing for now is to start to work hard to recover that feeling about being a winner – to win games, to perform.”

The Argentine hinted at sticking with the current crop of players to help them rediscover their form.

“We need to love the players that we have. We need to support the players, we need to provide all the tools for them to perform.”

Pochettino’s first match in charge will be one of the toughest possible introductions to English football’s biggest rivalry. Manchester United travel to Anfield to face Liverpool on Sunday, giving Pochettino very little time to work with his squad before his first outing.

The new head coach faces a mountain ahead of him. He has walked into one of the most difficult jobs in world football. United have struggled so far into the season and they need results quickly to get them out of the relegation spots.

Pochettino has spoken about bringing back the winning feeling at Old Trafford, but his first challenge will be getting a response from his players who have managed only one league win in 10 games so far. That is far from Manchester United standards and Pochettino will be hoping he can instill some confidence back in the team.

The Liverpool game will offer an early indication of how his players respond to the change at United. A positive result would provide immediate relief for the under pressure United but the longer-term job of rebuilding United will take much more than one match.
