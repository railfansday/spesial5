Swansea City double transfer must be on Scott Parker's radar at West Ham

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

West Ham United headed into the international break not quite where they hoped they'd be, but the Hammers remain well and truly in automatic promotion contention, as new head coach Scott Parker sets his sights on improving the squad.

West Ham sit just outside of the play-off places as things stand after six wins, five draws, and four defeats in their opening 15 games upon their return to the Championship.

Amid the club's struggles, Nuno Espírito Santo was dismissed from his post in the London Stadium dugout, swiftly being replaced by former Fulham, Bournemouth, and Burnley boss Scott Parker.

Parker, who made 129 appearances for West Ham as a player once upon a time, knows exactly what the Hammers supporters demand, and having had the opportunity to assess his squad over the break, has outlined two key areas in need of improvement this winter.

According to Alan Nixon's Patreon, Parker has identified left-back and striker as the two areas West Ham must be targeting heading into the January window.

With Taty Castellanos, Pablo Felipe, and Joel Piroe as options up front right now, it's not exactly as if the Hammers are short on strength and depth in this area, but despite this, Parker is clearly not totally convinced by any of that trio as things stand.

At left-back, each of Ollie Scarles, Morato, Adam Boiro, and Ezra Mayers has covered this area of the pitch at some stage this season, though none have been able to successfully nail down a starting berth under either Nuno or Parker.

It would be no surprise to see West Ham splash the cash this winter once again to boost their promotion chances, and should look towards a fellow top-eight contender to strike a deal.

Between them, Castellanos, Pablo, and Piroe have six league goals so far this campaign, with the latter grabbing four of those, as neither Castellanos nor Pablo have been able to adapt to the Championship.

It would be no surprise to see either of those move on in the winter, either on loan or permanently, and in that event, West Ham ought to rekindle their interest in Slovenian centre-forward Zan Vipotnik.

Vipotnik, 24, finished as the Championship's top goalscorer last season, with 23 goals and three assists in 44 league appearances for Swansea City, as midway through the season, the Hammers registered an interest in the forward.

Swansea would stand firm on Vipotnik, who would eventually sign a fresh deal in South Wales, but this season, he has found himself battling for minutes with Adam Idah and Ross Stewart, as a move this winter could be on the cards.

West Ham should move again, and in the process, bring along star left-back Josh Tymon with them, with the former Hull City and Stoke City full-back a large reason for Vipotnik's goalscoring success last year.

Although Vipotnik has fallen down the pecking order at Swansea this season, his goalscoring ability at this level is undeniable, and West Ham must be looking to take advantage of his situation.

With Castellanos and Pablo likely to exit after their poor starts to the season, Vipotnik would offer a different option up top at the London Stadium, and a coach like Parker will know how to get the best out of him.

Tymon, since joining Swansea from Stoke in the summer of 2023, has cemented himself as one of the very best in his position in the entire division, especially going forward, with his excellent delivery proving incredibly difficult to defend against.

At 27 and with nearly 250 Championship appearances under his belt across his career, Tymon is in his prime years and knows the division better than most, as though neither will likely come especially cheap; if they can help guide the Hammers back to the Premier League, it would be difficult to deny they're worth it.

With a 3-3 draw away to Cardiff City and a 2-1 defeat away to Blackburn Rovers to begin his tenure as West Ham boss, Parker won't be particularly pleased with either of his sides' performances, despite battling back from 3-0 down against Cardiff.

Parker, with each of Fulham, Bournemouth, and Burnley, had the entire summer to prepare his squads for the upcoming campaign; having only ever taken charge of a club midway through the season once before with Club Brugge, this challenge poses different obstacles than what he is used to.

The Englishman will surely be aiming to stamp his authority on the West Ham side ASAP, and the additions of Vipotnik and Tymon would certainly show a statement of intent by strengthening his side and weakening a direct rival.
