Ramos ‘happy’ to be back on target for Milan: “We are building something beautiful”

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Goncalo Ramos got back on the scoresheet in AC Milan’s big win against Frosinone on Sunday evening, expressing his delight after the game.

The pressure has certainly been mounting on Ramos in the last few months. The high price tag paid in the summer came with certain expectations from the Milan fans and with just three goals to his name prior to today’s game, it’s easy to see why there was criticism.

As such, the Rossoneri will be hoping that the goal scored against Frosinone can be a turning point for the No.9. It wasn’t just that he scored the goal either; he got stuck in and played a part in the other goals as well, which we highlighted in the player ratings.

Speaking to Sky and DAZN after the game, as transcribed by MilanNews, Ramos shared his thoughts on the goal and also said that Milan are ‘building something beautiful’.

“I’m really happy to have scored. The most important thing is that we won and we played a good team game. The three points are always the most important thing.”

“Yes, everything is going very well here: the team, the spirit, the atmosphere. We’re getting to know each other better every day, every week, after every training session, and I think everyone can see it on the pitch like today.

“We’re building something beautiful, and we want to keep working and keep moving forward.”
