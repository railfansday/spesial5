Chelsea legend John Terry claims £27m Arsenal star is ‘best in the world’

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

John Terry believes that Arsenal star David Raya is ‘100 per cent’ the best goalkeeper in world football at present.

Raya joined Arsenal on an initial loan deal from Brentford in August 2023 before his move was made permanent for £27 million the following year.

The 31-year-old was an integral part in Arsenal’s success last season, in which the team won the Premier League and reached the Champions League final, where they were beaten on penalties by Paris Saint-Germain.

Raya has continued that form this season to help Arsenal open up a six-point lead at the top of the table after 11 games, and Terry believes the Spaniard is without doubt the world’s best shotstopper.

‘You look at Raya, who they brought in, who knew he was that good?’ Terry said in an interview with Rory Jennings.

‘He’s the best keeper in the world, 100 per cent. Catches crosses, dominates his box. Unbelievable with both feet. Really good.’

Terry was also keen to praise the rest of Arsenal’s defence, and believes their current backline could go down as one of the best in Premier League history.

‘I think they’re annoyingly good, and I think if they get to the levels that I think they will and win back-to-back-to-back [titles], I think they have to be in the debate,’ he added.

‘Now, you can argue individually who’s better, this one or that one, but I think if they’ve got titles underneath them, then I think they have to be in the conversation as a collective and individually as well.’

Arsenal will look to showcase their defensive prowess against Manchester City on Sunday to put more distance between themselves and one of their main title rivals.

The aforementioned Raya was absent in midweek against Borussia Dortmund after sustaining a shoulder injury in the 2-2 draw with Newcastle United last weekend, but Arteta did not rule the Spaniard returning to face City.

‘We still have a few hours before the match, and we’re still trying to get all the players fit,’ Arteta said.

Asked if Raya could still play, Arteta added: ‘Yes.’
