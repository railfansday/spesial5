Report: Liverpool stars thrilled, two ridiculously good players have finally joined them

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Liverpool's players are thrilled. Two ridiculously good players have finally joined up with them - and now the squad looks better for it.

Liverpool know full well that this is an imbalanced squad that needs some work. It's been abundantly clear since the opening fixture of the season and everyone expects something to happen about it. One way or another.

Transfers are, of course, one way of going about that. Liverpool are currently weighing up their options ahead of the winter window and it's entirely possible that they make some reasonably big moves.

That might not even mean big-money deals. It could be that Liverpool decide to restructure key areas of the squad - midfield being one that deserves a reconsideration right now.

But transfers aren't the only way. That's also very clear and the Reds know that this squad will naturally improve no matter what.

Liverpool hope to improve in a few ways this season. One is by getting used to Andoni Iraola's plans and gelling as a unit. Another is by the improvement of developing players, such as Trey Nyoni and Rio Ngumoha.

But one massive reason for excitement is the return of injured stars. Liverpool went into this campaign without some high-profile absentees and things were always going to look better once they got back into the fold.

This week saw two of them finally link up with their teammates: Conor Bradley and Giovanni Leoni. The pair of them have been out long-term, Leoni for over a year now, but they're back training.

Now, we can't be sure what that means for a return to playing. It may well be another month or so before Liverpool are confident with competitive football for the pair.

But The Times reports that their return to training has been enough to lift the players. Bradley and Leoni unquestionably improve this squad, quite significantly, in fact, and their returns just make life easier for everyone.

Bradley, in particular, should have a massive effect. Liverpool have been using Ronald Araujo at full-back but Bradley is undoubtedly the no.1 there when fully fit. It sounds as though he still holds onto that status, too.
