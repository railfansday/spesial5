Watch: ‘Best player on the pitch’ – Pulisic signs off Pochettino era with stunning bicycle kick

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Christian Pulisic’s excellent international break continued as he scored a remarkable bicycle kick for the USA overnight.

It was the end of an era at Cincinnati’s TQL Stadium on Tuesday, as Mauricio Pochettino took charge of his last game before heading off to become the new head coach of Manchester United. The United States signed off with a 2-0 win over Haiti, completing an 8-0 aggregate victory.

After scoring twice in the first leg a few days ago, Pulisic scored a stunning bicycle-kick goal in the 25th minute last night. The audacity and the confidence to even try an attempt like that shows the incredible form that the Milan forward is in.

According to Sofascore, Pulisic registered 30/33 passes completed, 48 touches, 17 carries and even had 2 tackles won, 2 recoveries and 4/6 ground duels won. His goal came from his only shot and carried an Expected Goals number of 0.25.

ESPN gave Pulisic a 9 out of 10 in their player ratings, writing: “A bicycle-kick goal, a hockey assist, and acting as a clear leader with the strings he was pulling in the build-up of plays. What more could you ask?”

Meanwhile, Sports Illustrated went with an 8.1: “When Pulisic is in this kind of form, there’s few players in all of Concacaf that can match his level – if any. The best player on the pitch, and it wasn’t close at all.”
