Reports: Milan’s predicted XI to face Frosinone – Musah in midfield; Cisse benched

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Ruben Amorim is expected to make one change to the starting eleven for Milan-Frosinone, according to the latest reports.

As the international break is over, Milan have used the last couple of days to prepare for Sunday’s clash against Frosinone. It’s a chance for the Rossoneri to make it three consecutive wins for the first time in the league this season, but as highlighted by Amorim, it’s a tough one.

The good news is that Mike Maignan is available despite the injury scare mid-week, while Strahinja Pavlovic has also been included in the squad. Amorim actually said that the Serbian wants to play, but it would be silly to rush him back.

According to MilanNews, it will be a 4-3-3 formation with Davide Bartesaghi returning as the starting left-back. Yunus Musah will move into the midfield, while Diego Moreira and Christian Pulisic will occupy the flanks in attack.

Since Pavlovic seemingly isn’t ready to start, Sankhoun Diawara will keep his spot alongside Koni De Winter in the heart of the defence. The biggest surprise is, of course, that Alphadjo Cisse is tipped to start on the bench. He has been in great form lately, so some might question that decision.

Sky Italia is suggesting that it could be more of a 4-4-1-1, with Rabiot having a free role to move as he pleases.

Predicted Milan XI (4-3-3): Maignan; Gila, De Winter, Diawara, Bartesaghi; Musah, Modric, Rabiot; Moreira, Ramos, Pulisic.

Predicted bench: Torriani, P. Terracciano, F. Terracciano, Pavlovic, Vladimirov, Estupinan, Jashari, Comotto, Hutchinson, Cisse, Camarda.
