The November 2026 archival release comes on the heels of Parke’s second collaboration with Stoney Clover Lane and the brand’s Halloween drop, which similarly revived sold-out favorites. Other recent campaigns include Parke’s Pink Aid partnership timed to Breast Cancer Awareness Month, its Fall ’26 Collection and Parke’s Collegiate Collection, the brand’s first licensed apparel launch, created in partnership with sports lifestyle brand ’47.

Since launching in 2022, Chelsea Parke’s direct-to-consumer clothing line has taken social media by storm. What started as reworked denim quickly turned into limited-release loungewear, most notably, Parke’s signature mockneck, known to sell out within minutes of online drops. By April 2026, the viral fashion brand made its way into Target stores nationwide.
