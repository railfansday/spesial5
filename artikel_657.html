‘Home Alone’ Director Chris Columbus Said Minutes Before New Sequel Was Announced That Another Movie ‘Can’t Happen’ After Catherine O’Hara’s Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Chris Columbus didn’t know a new “Home Alone” was coming.

The director of the 1990 original spoke with Variety minutes before news broke that Disney is developing a new installment with Macaulay Culkin. According to sources, Columbus had no knowledge that the project was in development or about to be announced. When asked about the franchise’s future, his answer was that any return faces a steep climb without Catherine O’Hara. “Catherine, as I’ve always said, is 50% of ‘Home Alone,’” Columbus says. “There’s the yin and yang of ‘Home Alone’: Catherine O’Hara and Macaulay Culkin. Without those two in the film, there is no film, because they create the emotional epicenter of that film.”

O’Hara, who played Kevin McCallister’s mother, Kate, died Jan. 30 at 71.

As reported Friday, Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg are shaping the script with Culkin and will produce through their Point Grey banner. The project is in early development at Disney, and plot details and casting remain under wraps. According to Deadline, the film grew out of a pitch Culkin made to Disney film chief David Greenbaum, told from the point of view of an older Kevin as a father.

When Variety followed up after the announcement, Columbus declined to comment further.

Columbus, who is set to receive a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in December, was particularly warm about his former star’s return to the role in a McDonald’s holiday commercial released Nov. 17. “I love Macaulay’s McDonald’s commercial,” he says. “Macaulay is a great actor, and I hope to work with him on something. Maybe it’ll be ‘Home Alone’-related, maybe it won’t.”

He leaves the door open to a return built around the original ensemble.

“I think you potentially could still entertain the idea of doing another ‘Home Alone’ with the cast,” Columbus explains. “Not any of these ridiculous follow-ups to the first. And I only say ridiculous because it’s weird with filmmakers. They don’t understand why that cast works so well. It’s always about the cast. It’s like ‘The Goonies.’ It’s about the cast.”

The franchise continued without Culkin and Columbus in “Home Alone 3” (1997), “Home Alone 4” (2002), “Home Alone: The Holiday Heist” (2012) and Disney+’s “Home Sweet Home Alone” (2021).

But Columbus says O’Hara’s death has changed what a reunion could be. “It was really about Catherine and Macaulay, if anyone ever wanted to do a sequel,” he says, “and unfortunately that can’t happen now.”

He also makes the case for the film’s burglars, Harry and Marv, played by Joe Pesci and Daniel Stern.

“Let’s not downsize Harry and Marv,” Columbus says. “Daniel Stern and Joe Pesci are the ultimate comic duo for that particular film. Everyone has tried in subsequent sequels to be as funny or as interesting as Dan and Joe, and they just had a chemistry that is undefinable. So you can’t do that.”

Columbus, who also directed 1992’s “Home Alone 2: Lost in New York,” has long been skeptical of revisiting the property. In 2020, he told Insider that Disney’s planned reboot was “a waste of time” and that “nobody got in touch with me about it.” That project became “Home Sweet Home Alone.” Last year, he told Entertainment Tonight that another “Home Alone” would be a “mistake.”

Rogen also has a personal connection to O’Hara. She played studio executive Patty Leigh in his Apple TV comedy “The Studio,” a role she still held when she died. O’Hara also spoke at Culkin’s own Walk of Fame ceremony in December 2023.

Columbus is next set to direct “Gremlins 3,” which is scheduled to begin shooting early next year. He executive produced Robert Eggers’ upcoming horror film “Werwulf,” which Focus Features opens Dec. 25.
