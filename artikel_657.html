Katseye Brings Out Camila Cabello for ‘Gabriela’ Dance at Los Angeles Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Roughly six songs into their performance on Saturday night, Katseye made a girl group connection with Fifth Harmony member Camila Cabello as Daniela Avanzini brought her out to be the collective’s “Gabriela” for the evening, dancing to the choreography that accompanies the song.

Katseye, who performed as a quartet consisting of Avanzini alongside Lara Raj, Yoonchae and Megan Skindiel, took the stage at around 8:30 p.m. at Los Angeles’ Crypto.com Arena, running through opener “Hootie Frutti” and “Touch” before the girls exited so that Avanzini could bring out Cabello. “OK, you look stunning,” said Avanzini as Cabello joined her. “Thank you Camila, I have one question for you: Do you know the ‘Gabriela’ dance?”

“I know a little bit, just a little bit,” responded Cabello. “We’re two Latina mamis, I feel like there’s a lot of Latina mamis in here. So even if we get steps wrong, it’s the sazon that counts.” They continued on to do the “Gabriela” dance, hitting the hand-ography before Cabello left the stage and the rest of Katseye joined back up to do a full performance of the single.

Throughout the show, Katseye gave a tour of their discography from the past few years, including hits like “Pinky Up,” “Gnarly” and “Mean Girls.” Katseye, who was recently announced as the recipient of the Group of the Year Award at Variety Hitmakers’ 2026, is currently on its “Wildworld” tour amid a year that saw several members take temporary hiatuses to improve their mental health. Katseye will perform again at Crypto.com Arena on Sunday night before a show in Phoenix and a tour closer at Fort Worth, Texas’ Ariat Arena on December 1.
