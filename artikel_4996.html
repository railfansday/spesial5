I Work Out for a Living—Here Are the Best Headphones for Everything From Runs to Gym Sessions

WHETHER YOU PREFER to listen to music, a podcast, or tune into sports while getting your sweat on, you'll need a solid pair of workout headphones to make it happen. Beyond a high-quality pre-workout, nothing elevates your training quite like the perfect pair of headphones. These devices not only drown out grunting gym-goers but also help you stay focused, find your rhythm, and push through those last few reps when your body is telling you to quit.

The key to a good pair of workout headphones is finding ones that fit your ears snugly and won't budge while you run or do kettlebell swings. Workout headphones need water resistance to handle sweat and 5-mile runs in the rain. We can recommend eleven pairs of workout headphones for a range of training environments.

Like our favorite running shoes, modern workout headphones are built for performance, combining durability with athletic-minded features. Sweat and water resistance are non-negotiable, while a secure, comfortable fit prevents them from falling out during burpees, sprints, or heavy lifts. Meanwhile, battery life has improved dramatically in recent years. Many models offer enough juice to power through a week’s worth of workouts on a single charge. Need to focus? Noise cancellation, intuitive controls, and quick-pairing technology keep you in the zone rather than fumbling with your tech.

Whether you’re just starting your fitness journey or a seasoned athlete, the best workout headphones can transform your training sessions. The Men’s Health fitness and tech editors tested a few dozen options to identify the eleven best models available. From true wireless earbuds to over-ear models to bone conduction headphones, these top-performing headphones deliver impressive durability, high-quality sound, and workout-specific features to help you push through the pain.

The AirPods Pro have improved significantly over the past few updates, and the latest version, the AirPods Pro 3, is what we believe are the best do-it-all workout headphones. These earbuds deliver excellent comfort, premium features, and high-quality sound. Apple has systematically addressed every pain point we experienced with earlier models, transforming these wireless earbuds from casual earbuds into essential fitness companions.

The fit is a major reason the AirPods Pro work so well during workouts, even surpassing the latest Apple release, the Apple AirPods 5. After dozens of reps, we found they don’t budge. The multiple silicone ear tips let our testing team find the perfect seal during burpees and box jumps. Despite wearing them for multiple training sessions (and in the office), we experienced no ear fatigue thanks to their lightweight design.

The improved ergonomics also contribute to better passive noise isolation, working with active noise cancellation to block out excessively loud gym music. For athletes who rely on music for motivation during workouts, the secure, comfortable fit eliminates the constant annoyance of loose earbuds that need readjusting. Their IP57 rating provides additional peace of mind, ensuring they can handle the sweat that comes with serious workouts.

MH Senior Fitness Editor Brett Williams, NASM, found the most exciting new feature of the AirPods Pro 3s to be their ability to monitor your heart rate through your ear canals, a capability more commonly associated with devices that wrap around your wrist or chest. They aren't the first headphones to do so, but having this added to Apple's already impressive offerings only strengthens their case.

Battery life exceeded our expectations, with up to eight hours of continuous playback and a charging case that extends total listening time to 24 hours. The case supports wireless charging, making it easy to recharge on a charging pad. Meanwhile, the AirPods Pro 3 support several advanced features like adaptive audio, conversation awareness, and transparency mode, the latter of which lets outside sound in, so you can be aware of what’s going on around you during a run.

The touch controls are intuitive during workouts—you can play/pause, skip tracks, answer a call, and adjust the volume by pressing the stem. For iPhone owners, the AirPods are a cinch to set up. Simply open the case next to your phone, tap Connect, and you’re ready to go. This combination of comfort, performance, and ease of use makes the AirPods Pro 3 the best overall workout headphones, setting a high bar for others to match.

While the Beats Studio Pro aren’t specifically designed for working out, they’re by no means out of place in a gym. That said, the Studio Pro omits some features that are standard on other premium models, like ear detection, so they don’t automatically pause the music when you remove them from your head. Still, the headphones provide impressive performance wrapped in the brand’s iconic style.

If you go hard during your workouts, you'll sweat—probably a lot. You need headphones that can keep up. Look for headphones or earbuds that are at least water-resistant or, even better, waterproof. Really, earbuds usually offer better sweat resistance than over-ear headphones. Over-ear headphones with sweat-resistant construction are a lot harder to find, although brands like Sony and Beats still hold up well to sweat. If most of your workouts are moderate, sweat resistance may not matter, and any pair of the workout headphones we've noted below will work for you.

Your favorite gym clothes aren't the only things drenched in sweat after a workout. So durability for workout headphones starts with water and sweat protection, measured by the standardized Ingress Protection (IP) rating system. The rating has two numbers: the first indicates dust protection (6 being the highest), and the second indicates water resistance (9 being the highest).

For serious athletes (and seriously sweaty workouts), we recommend headphones that offer water protection to survive intense training sessions. If you primarily work out indoors, you can get by without dust protection, but consider proper dust resistance if you’re trail running or doing HIIT on the beach. Beyond dust and water resistance, the best workout headphones boast impact-resistant materials, such as durable plastic, high-grade silicone or rubber, and reinforced hinges and headbands.

Comfort and fit are essential for peak performance. While these factors can be highly personal, certain features separate the headphones you’ll love wearing from those that’ll end up at the bottom of your gym bag.

Several types of workout headphones suit different preferences. In-ear models are the most popular for workouts thanks to their compact size, and they come in several variations. The first type features interchangeable silicone tips in small, medium, and large sizes, creating a customized seal in your ear canal. Another design incorporates flexible ear hooks that loop over your ears for added stability.

If you don’t like wearing earbuds, on-ear headphones provide cushioned pads that rest against your ears, while over-ear models create a complete seal around each ear. Bone conduction headphones offer a unique solution by conducting sound through your cheekbones while keeping your ear canals open. These are a good option for anyone who needs to maintain awareness of their surroundings, such as when running at night or in a busy city.

When you’re crushing a grueling rowing routine or leg day, the last thing you want is your headphones dying mid-workout. Look for wireless earbuds that provide at least six to eight hours of continuous playback, or on-ear/over-ear headphones that can go 20+ hours on a single charge. That should last through a full week of workouts.

The good news is most earbuds come with charging cases that can extend total battery life by several hours; it’s like having a portable battery with you at all times. Additionally, features like wireless charging and fast charging make it even more convenient to keep your headphones powered up, whether you’re dropping them on a charging pad before bed or need a quick boost before heading out the door.

Obtuse controls can throw off your entire rhythm, so look for earbuds and headphones with simple buttons or touch controls. The best workout headphones feature intuitive controls that let you skip tracks, adjust the volume, and answer phone calls—all without touching your phone. Some headphones even offer customizable controls through companion apps, allowing you to tailor the experience to your needs.

Connectivity should be equally seamless. That means pairing quickly with your smartphone without jumping through menus and Bluetooth settings. For instance, the AirPods Pro 3 and Beats Powerbeats Pro 2 are easy to pair with an iPhone—simply open the earbuds’ case near your device and tap to connect. Once paired, your AirPods can automatically connect to all your Apple devices through iCloud. Android users can take advantage of Fast Pair technology, which offers a similar one-tap connection experience with compatible headphones.

Additionally, many models now support multipoint pairing, allowing you to seamlessly switch between your phone and computer without manually disconnecting and reconnecting your headphones each time. Voice assistant integration adds another layer of hands-free convenience, letting you control your music, set a timer, or make a phone call using just your voice.

For those of us who work in busy offices, active noise cancellation (ANC) can be a game-changer for blocking out distractions. In gyms, this technology is just as useful, drowning out clanking weights, loud conversations, and blaring music.

However, the best workout headphones don’t just block out noise—they also let noise in. For outdoor workouts (or simply walking down the street), a transparency mode is an essential safety feature that uses the headphones’ microphones to pipe in ambient noise, such as approaching vehicles and emergency sirens. The most effective models let you switch between ANC and transparency modes with a simple tap or voice command.

Beyond noise cancellation, adaptive audio can automatically adjust the sound based on your environment, so you don’t have to constantly fiddle with volume controls.

The guide below highlights what we believe are the best workout headphones to know about right now.

Sweat causes a lot of wear and tear on over-ear headphones, especially the ear pads. We recommend using only over-ear headphones for moderate activity, such as lifting and light cardio. You don't really want to wear over-ear headphones for HIIT or other high-cardio workouts, because the more sweat you create, the more likely your headphones are to slip and slide around on your head.

Over-ear headphones do provide great sound quality, and if you are strategic about how you work out with them, you likely won't see sweat as an issue until much farther down the road. In one specific case, one of our editors was a big fan of his Beats Studio Pro headphones for weight lifting. It took about 3 years for his ear pads to wear down from sweat and regular use, so he then bought replacement ear pads for those headphones.

You won't have any problems with sweat and water resistance on the running headphones we curated above; however, some are rated higher than others—which means they're more durable.

When you see an "IP" rating, what you are really reading is the Ingress Protection rating. You can read a rating from each number: the first indicates dust protection, and the second indicates water protection. Some of the headphones above have an "X" as the first number. The X indicates that no data were collected on dust protection. Sometimes it helps to know the dust protection, especially if you're running on trails. If you're a trail runner, it's probably worth paying attention to the more rugged running headphones that also have a dust rating.

The second number indicates how waterproof the headphones are. Zero is the lowest, while 9K is the highest (you're unlikely to see a 9K rating on headphones because this level is reserved for materials that can handle direct, high-temp, high-pressure water).

So what's a good number range to look for? We recommend anything between four and six, but seven and higher is a premium rating and can withstand full submersion in water. Most of what you will see is "IPX4", which is solid, but if you're really wanting a pair of running headphones that are made to handle high activity, then look for a second number of seven or above.
