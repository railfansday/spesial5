Daily Shows Desi Lydic reacts to Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang allegedly having 3 affairs

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The story of (now former) Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang having an affair with an alleged Chinese spy was already dramatic enough, but there are now allegations that he actually had three affairs — and Daily Show host Desi Lydic has thoughts.

"A third affair? Cheating on your mistress you were cheating on your mistress with? This man is a nesting doll of adultery," Lydic says in the clip above. "It's just amazing that just a month ago, Huang was considered the most dignified of all the tech CEOs. Now the news has to bring on Steve Kornacki to break down every district Huang busted a nut in."
