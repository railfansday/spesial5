Tanggal: 2026-11-04

TL;DR: New users can get one year of Rosetta Stone Sapphire for just $127.20, including 25 languages, pronunciation feedback, conversation practice, and personalized activities.

Ordering dinner in another language is a useful skill. Keeping the conversation going after the waiter goes off-script? Even better. Rosetta Stone Sapphire’s 1-year subscription is $127.20 (reg. $159) for a limited time, giving new users access to 25 languages and tools built around everyday speaking confidence.

Drawing on more than 30 years of language-learning expertise, Sapphire uses Rosetta Stone’s Dynamic Immersion method. Rather than relying on direct translation or memorization drills, it teaches through imagery, context, listening, reading, and speaking. Bite-sized lessons build vocabulary and grammar while giving you opportunities to use what you learn.

Pronunciation gets its own attention through TruAccent speech recognition, which provides real-time feedback. Meanwhile, guided Chat Missions let you practice scenarios involving travel, social situations, and everyday interactions. Think of it as a rehearsal before you need the words in person.

The experience also leaves room for your interests. Sapphire Studio lets you create personalized learning activities on topics that matter to you, while more than 500 flashcard sets cover subjects such as business, hobbies, sports, art, and travel. Your next study session doesn’t have to revolve around someone else’s itinerary.

You can switch among available languages, including Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Mandarin, German, and Italian. Progress saves and syncs across supported desktop, mobile, and tablet devices, so you can pick up where you left off when your schedule allows.

For learners who want structured lessons alongside speaking practice, Sapphire brings both into one platform.

Get the Rosetta Stone Sapphire one-year subscription for just $127.20 (reg. $159) through Nov. 8.
