Service members offered time off to play GTA 6 — but theres a big catch

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Is a four-day weekend of playing Grand Theft Auto VI worth two to six more years of military service? For 43 members of the U.S. Army's 9th Brigade Engineer Battalion in Georgia, the answer is indeed "yes."

Lt. Col. Ryan Hodgson offered 130 of his soldiers "a special 4-day pass" to play the game in exchange for reenlisting in the military — and one-third agreed to the terms, the Washington Post reports.

While servicemembers already receive numerous perks for reenlisting — from financial bonuses to special ceremonies to events with professional sports teams — Hodgson’s offer was a "cherry on top," Angel Tomko, a spokesperson for the Army’s 3rd Infantry Division, told the Post.

For soldiers already planning to reenlist, the offer is essentially a cheat code for an automatic 4-day pass.

Four days will just scratch the surface of GTA 6’s immersive world, which is a thinly veiled take on Florida. According to IGN (which is owned by Ziff Davis, Mashable’s parent company), its fastest player finished the game in 42 hours, while it took 115 hours for its slowest player to reach the end. Of course, the game has numerous secrets and side missions beyond the main objective of helping protagonists Jason and Lucia achieve their happy ending.

Meanwhile, critics and the public can't get enough GTA 6 — pre-orders alone have already topped $1 billion.
