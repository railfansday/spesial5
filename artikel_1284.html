Real Madrid star Endrick opens up amid Milan and Roma links: ‘I don’t know what will happen’

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Endrick admits he doesn’t know what will happen in January, but he has made it clear he wants regular football as Milan and Roma monitor his situation at Real Madrid.

According to Calciomercato.com, the 20-year-old Brazilian looks set to leave the Bernabéu this winter. Real Madrid would prefer a loan, like his spell at Lyon last season, but both Serie A clubs want better terms: either a permanent deal or an option to buy with a high buy-back clause for Real.

Endrick is under contract until 2030 and earns a reported €6m gross per season. Real paid Palmeiras €35m plus €25m in bonuses for him in December 2022, and he’s now valued at about €60m.

“I’m still young, I’m only 20, I don’t have to take off and throw myself off a cliff,” he told Globo. “Let’s see what happens. I can only say that I want to play, be happy, help my teammates, score goals and provide assists, like I did at Lyon. That was one of the best things that’s ever happened to me and one of the happiest times of my life.

“I don’t know what will happen in January, it’s all in God’s hands. I’m only asking to be able to do what’s best for my career. Whatever happens, happens.”

Endrick has played only a few minutes with Kylian Mbappé ahead of him, although he did feature against Roma in the Champions League.

“You all know who we have at Real Madrid and who’s ahead of me. Mbappé is a great player, he came second in the Ballon d’Or. I try to take the opportunities I’m given and do my best.”

He also praised José Mourinho, who said before the Roma game that the forward wasn’t going anywhere.

“Mourinho is a great person. Every day he comes to me and asks how I am, but he never talks about football. He wants to know about my private life. I like his attitude, he cares. I’ll always give my all for Real Madrid, which comes first.”
