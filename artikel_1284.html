Allegri reveals possible cause of Vergara’s injury, warns Napoli fans about Man City clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Napoli coach Massimiliano Allegri says Antonio Vergara’s injury might be related to the ‘greater pressure’ the Italian talent is facing this season and insists the Partenopei must be ‘realistic’ in their next Champions League game against Manchester City.

Vergara suffered a new muscle injury on Saturday night, during Napoli’s 1-0 win over Torino.

The 23-year-old looked particularly frustrated as he left the pitch in tears.

“He got injured, and I don’t know how long we’ll be without him,” Allegri said at a post-match press conference via TuttoNapoli.

“It’s very unfortunate; he was very down. I think the injury may be related to what he’s going through: this year he has different responsibilities, the national team, and greater mental pressure. Sometimes these injuries are caused by those things too.”

Vergara, 23, has made ten appearances across all competitions this season with Napoli, scoring one goal and providing one assist.

However, he has already missed four club games due to a calf injury.

His latest injury is to the flexor of his left thigh, and according to Il Mattino, it’s unclear whether the versatile attacker will play for his club again in 2026.

During his post-match press conference, Allegri also spoke about the upcoming Champions League game against Manchester City, suggesting Napoli’s motivation might not be at its highest.

“It’s a very important match and a great one to play in,” Allegri said.

“We have to be realistic; our Champions League campaign will be decided against Club Brugge, Sabah and Viking. We had to beat Bodo, and we did. Tuesday will be very important and difficult, but we need to sort out the league, and the home match against Club Brugge will be crucial.”

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
