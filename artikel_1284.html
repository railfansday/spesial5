Footage Shows  Moment Usman Nurmagomedov Jumped UFC’s Merab Dvalishvili

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

In a post which has now been deleted, UFC star Ilia Topuria has shared footage of the shocking moment Usman Nurmagomedov jumped Merab Dvalishvili. He also slammed the incident, calling out the cowardly behaviour.

Dvalishvili revealed today (11 November) that he had been jumped by the Russian fighter while in a hotel lobby ahead of his upcoming RAF Abu Dhabi match. He is scheduled to take on Marlon Moraes this Saturday, but the fight is now in doubt. Indeed, the 35-year-old has been left bruised and bloodied after his unexpected clash with Nurmagomedov, which comes weeks after his UFC 333 title fight with Petr Yan.

Dvalishvili has spoken out on the incident.

Topuria has now shared footage of the shocking moment Nurmagomedov jumped Dvalishvili. He also added his own comment as he called out the incident before later deleting his post on X, which had read:

"You’re a bunch of cowards, and you’ll be held accountable for what you’ve done. You’re anything but real men."

The wild footage has emerged elsewhere online, as it shows Nurmagomedov, one of the greatest Russian MMA fighters in history, approaching Dvalishvili from behind before swinging at the Georgian. A mass brawl ensues – watch the full clip below:

Revealing what had happened, Dvalishvili took to social media to show the aftermath of the incident. First, a caption read: "Was doing an interview for RAF in hotel lobby and Usman and his buddies came up from behind and started punching me and my young friend. Security was holding me - They kept kicking us when we were down like losers."

But in the clip, the 35-year-old spoke with pure emotion, as blood dripped down his face. He called out Nurmagomedov, explaining what went down:

“What a sneaky way, guys, just two of us came here in Abu Dhabi, and Usman Nurmagomedov and his friends came like snakes from behind, what kind of fighters are you? What kind of men are you? You came from behind, there is just two of us and you guys were prepared, what the f***?”

While still receiving medical care, he continued: “We bleed, we fight, we work, you guys are just sneaky guys, you are not real men, what the f***, if you want to fight let’s fight, we are fighters, bro. You guys came from behind, all of your friends waiting for us in a sneaky way. I knew what kind of guys you are… We were fighting like real men, 20 guys vs just two guys, we are warriors, you are p******."
