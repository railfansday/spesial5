Banijay Reveals First Financials Following All3Media Merger, Says Synergies From Deal ‘Expected to Ramp Up in 2027’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

European giant Banijay has announced its first set of financial results since its closed the $8 billion merger with All3Media in July.

For the first nine months of 2026, the French conglomerate posted overall of $4.7 billion — a leap of 33.9% over 2025, taking into account both All3Media and its acquisition of betting company Tipico, which closed in April — but 6.6% up on a pro-forma basis. Meanwhile, adjusted EBITDA rose 37.3% to $901 million and 3% pro-forma. Banijay Entertainment, Banijay’s London-based content division into which All3Media was merged, saw production revenue slip 3% pro forma (including contributions from All3Media in 2025 and 2026) in the first nine months of the year to $1.81 billion. Distribution revenues fell 5% to $329. However, Banijay Entertainment’s overall revenue’s rose slightly due to a surge in its Live division, with revenues up nearly 46% to $417 — thanks to its work at the Olympic Winter Games and FIFA World Cup.

According to Banijay the fall in production reflected the “anticipated seasonality of deliveries” and a “tough comparison” to 2025, which saw the delivery of shows such as “The Buccaneers” and “House of Guinness.” However, it also highlighted shows from All3Media over the last 9 months including “The Wanted Man,” “War” and “The Grand Tour.”

“Our latest figures provide the first financial indication of the newly combined Banijay Entertainment — a group that, despite challenging market conditions, continues to demonstrate strength, stability, and a solid standing for the future,” said Banijay Entertainment CEO Marco Bassetti.

“The merger immediately bolstered this business, providing greater creative scale, strengthened English-language output and expanded digital and distribution capabilities. With financial discipline and a robust strategy for the road ahead, we remain positively focused on growth in our core activities and across our burgeoning diversification pillars – live, digital and sport.”
