Ben Affleck Negotiates With a Duck as Artists Equity Kicks Off Thanksgiving Campaign for Aflac

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The launch of the campaign on the NBC Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade follows Aflac and Affleck's Artists Equity teaming up earlier this year.

Ben Affleck’s Artists Equity and the insurance company Aflac are using Thanksgiving to launch a new installment of their campaign starring the actor and the namesake duck.

The new spot, dubbed “High Stakes,” is set to debut on the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade on NBC Thursday, and will see Affleck serving as a translator between a team of negotiators… and the Aflac Duck.

Artits Equity and Aflac unveiled their campaign in September via a viral marketing effort that saw Affleck vandalizing Aflac billboards. It’s expected to run through next summer, leaning on tentpole events (like, say this week’s parade) to move it along. Affleck himself frequently directs commercial spots from Artists Equity

“Sure, it’s funny to have Ben Affleck translating for the Aflac Duck. But the idea is rooted in a simple truth,” said Aflac senior VPand CMO Garth Knutson. “Every year, millions of Americans turn to Aflac agents and HR professionals to help them understand their benefits and make informed decisions about their coverage. In this commercial, Ben does it for 60 seconds. They do it every day.”“As we explored the bond forming between the Aflac Duck and the Affleck Human, it became clear we needed to show audiences that Ben was gaining a deeper understanding of Aflac and its brand icon,” added Brandon Pierce, co-president and ECD of Artists Equity Brand Studio. “And what better way to prove it than through a high-stakes negotiation where Ben could simply and boldly declare, ‘I speak Aflac.’”
