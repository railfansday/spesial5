Vertex Pharmaceuticals Has Turned $10,000 Into $61,660 Over 10 Years. Here's What Could Come Next

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Vertex's next growth wave is already in the pipeline.

By Prosper Junior Bakiny – Nov 25, 2026 at 7:12PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Vertex Pharmaceuticals ›

Vertex Pharmaceuticals (VRTX +0.04%), a biotech company, has been on an impressive run over the past decade. If you had invested $10,000 in the stock in 2016, you'd have about $61,660 today. By comparison, $10,000 invested in the S&P 500 10 years ago would be worth about $38,663 today (with dividends reinvested) -- that's not bad, but it pales in comparison to the returns you would have had with Vertex. That said, the past is the past. The important question is whether the drugmaker can deliver similarly impressive returns going forward.

Vertex has crushed broader equity markets thanks to its dominance in the cystic fibrosis (CF) market. The company has very little competition in this niche, and thanks to several new launches over the past 10 years that have allowed it to treat increasingly more CF patients, revenue and earnings growth have been strong. Vertex's CF franchise should remain an important growth driver over the next decade. After all, the company's most important medicines won't lose patent exclusivity until the late 2030s.

But in the meantime, Vertex should significantly diversify and expand its lineup. The company's work in kidney diseases looks particularly promising. Vertex Pharmaceuticals could earn approval for one product in its kidney disease portfolio, povetacicept -- a medicine for IgA nephropathy -- by the end of the month. This medicine has peak sales potential of around $4 billion across IgA nephropathy and other potential indications, according to some estimates.

Of course, that outcome isn't guaranteed yet, not by a long shot. But the point is that Vertex's kidney disease pipeline, with products like povetacicept and others, including inaxaplin and VX-407, looks promising. The biotech leader could, eventually, generate close to $10 billion in peak sales from these products if all goes well. Another area where Vertex could make progress is pain treatments.

It has already earned approval for Journavx, a medicine for acute pain and the first oral non-opioid pain signal inhibitor ever. Journavx is in several ongoing clinical trials and could earn key label expansions, while Vertex works on other pain treatments. Further, thanks to a recent acquisition, Vertex Pharmaceuticals has an interesting endocrine diseases portfolio that it believes could reach more than $5 billion in annual sales.

We haven't even mentioned other newer launches, including Casgevy, a gene-editing medicine for a pair of rare blood diseases. Vertex's potential across its entire pipeline is massive, and even after probability-adjusting sales projections, since pipeline products aren't guaranteed approval, my view is that the company's prospects look promising. That said, I wouldn't expect Vertex Pharmaceuticals to repeat its performance since 2016 over the next decade.

That's a tall order for a larger biotech that faces more competition than it did 10 years ago. Even so, from current levels, Vertex is well positioned to beat the market over the long run, making it a great stock to buy.
