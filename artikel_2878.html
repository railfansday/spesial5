Is Mitchell Robinson washed? (and other Mailbag answers)

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Ok, for full disclosure, the question in the title wasn't actually asked. But that is the kind of vibe this mailbag has, so I felt it was representative of the overall feel. And as a reminder, I did ask people to provide their hot takes and questions. So don't be mad that people delivered. Don't hate the playa, hate the game. Which, would honestly be fair in this case.

Hot take 1: Big Country looks out of shape. Over the first few games he's playing well below how he has performed in prior seasons. I will be interested to see if this continues or if this is a product of the team getting used to Big Mitch and him getting used to the team.

The boring/optimistic answer is to preach patience and give him time to acclimate to his new surroundings and role. The concern is that these things don't always work out as we would hope. He had a specific role in New York and thrived in that system. What if he doesn't work within the Celtics structure? We haven't even hit the first time missed due to injury that we all expected.

At the moment I'm not overly worried (once again, small sample size) but it is something to keep an eye on.

Hot take 2: Derrick White should be traded. There are many trade approaches that could be taken, his contract could be used to grab either another center or forward in an effort to lean into Peyton, Baylor, Hugo, and Ron stepping up to fill his role/shift the ball handler role more onto JT and PG13, the other would be to find a way to make a trade exception, have the aforementioned bench players absorb some of those minutes and also create minutes for Chris Cenac. Ultimately Derrick White is close to his athletic downturn. His playstyle, though not nearly as reliant on his athletic ability, does operate on the right side of a thin margin that contributes of his ability to stay in front, react defensively, and generally use what athletic ability he does have to keep up with the more athletic players he usually is tasked with guarding. Losing a half step, which I believe he is in the process of doing, will put him on the WRONG side of that margin and we need to deal him before he is on the wrong side of that margin.

I love, love, love Derrick White. I do not want to trade Derrick White. I will fight for Derrick White.

With all that said, it is hard to argue that he might be a logical option to move. Not necessarily this year, but perhaps by this coming offseason. Smart GM's tend to move on from players before they lose a step and White's shooting woes last year indicate that he might be close to that downturn you are talking about.

He has a large (but not max) contract that could be paired with picks or players to bring back another star or an impact long-term piece.

With all that said, I'd be very reluctant to move White until one or two of the younger players has firmly established themselves as consistent performers. Not just "shows us something occasionally" but contributing on both ends on a nightly basis. You need consistent, effective adults on the floor to win games.

Hot take 3: we should flip Garza and give his minutes to Chris Cenac. Right now Garza is the much better player by a decent margin. I agree that the current minute allocation where Garza is the third big in the rotation will lead to the most wins throughout the season. However, Chris and Luka fill very similar niches when they are on the floor. Giving Chris those minutes now will lead to more winning further down the line. The one drawback is that Cenac starts to click early which will increase his value when his rookie contract ends which may cause an unnecessary increase in his first contract negotiations.

I legitimately don't think either of those last two things will happen, but for the first one I am optimistic that we will see an improvement of Mitch's fit within the next few games…

I support the idea of finding minutes for Cenac, but I don't think we necessarily need to bench Garza to get them. First of all, I think he could use the reps in Maine. Secondly, I think there will be plenty of scheduled (or sometimes unscheduled) games off for Mitchell or Queta and there will be opportunities for Cenac to play on the 2nd night of a back-t0-back or in garbage time.
