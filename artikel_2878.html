Brazil U17s wrap up preparations at Granja Comary before Qatar trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Team held an intense training session this Friday (13)Credits: Nelson Terme /CBF

The Brazil Men's U-17 National Team held its final training session at Granja Comary in Teresópolis, Rio de Janeiro, this Friday (13), before departing for Qatar, where it will compete in the U-17 World Cup. The session, led by head coach Carlos Eduardo Patetuci, marked the end of the team's on-field preparations in Brazil.

Preparations in Teresópolis began on November 3 (look back here), with the arrival of the first players called up and the first training session. By the following day, the full squad had assembled to continue training. Throughout this period, the coaching staff worked on different aspects of the game, with drills focused on technique, possession, attacking movement, defensive organization and finishing.

The final training session began with a small-sided 4-on-4 game featuring three support players, aimed at maintaining possession and adjusting the team's response after losing the ball. Next, the players took part in a defensive balance drill, focusing on team organization, protecting the goal and preventing opponents from getting shots off.

To wrap up the session, Patetuci led an 11-a-side practice match with two 30-minute halves. The exercise allowed the team to apply the concepts covered in previous sessions and make final on-field adjustments before the trip.

The Brazilian delegation departs for Qatar this Saturday (14) and is scheduled to arrive in Doha on Sunday (15). Brazil is in Group I of the U-17 World Cup alongside Ireland, Tanzania and Costa Rica. The national team will play its opening match on November 21 against Ireland. The competition runs until December 13.
