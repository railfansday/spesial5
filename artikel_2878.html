Another week without Kenneth Walker III? It's looking that way for the Kansas City Chiefs.

After missing Week 10 due to groin tightness, the Chiefs running back has yet to practice this week and is in jeopardy of missing Sunday's game against the Arizona Cardinals. Should he not be able to go, rookie Emmett Johnson, who rushed for 101 yards and caught two receiving touchdowns last week, will once again take on the starter's role.

"It's getting better, but with those, you don't wanna do something stupid, right?" Chiefs head coach Andy Reid said of Walker. "Put him out there, and it's still bugging him a little bit, but he's feeling a lot better than he was. We'll just see. I mean, it's a day-to-day thing, so we'll just see how it goes."

San Francisco 49ers wide receiver Mike Evans (Achilles) has missed the past three games, but he took a big step this week returning to practice as the team prepares for its Mexico City game against the Minnesota Vikings.

Evans has not been ruled out for Week 11, but being on the field was an encouraging sign for his impending return.

"We just want to make sure that we're doing the right thing, and our health and performance team has done a great job leading the process," said 49ers general manager John Lynch. "Mike's been doing a great job. He's been frustrated. He wants out there on the field, but he's been a pro."

Luther Burden III (concussion) has cleared protocol after taking a helmet-to-helmet hit in Week 8 against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. The Chicago Bears wide receiver logged back-to-back full practices on Wednesday and Thursday, a good indication he'll be available against the New Orleans Saints. Defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons isn't concerned about a knee issue that kept him limited this week. "Like 99 problems, but this ain't one," he said.

Injuries that kept him limited in Wednesday's practice did not keep Las Vegas Raiders tight end Brock Bowers (ankle/quad) away from being on the field Thursday. The Raiders' leading receiver exited Sunday's loss to the Seattle Seahawks after just 11 snaps with an ankle issue.

DeVonta Smith (hamstring) has missed the past three games for the Philadelphia Eagles, but says he's 100% and has felt that way for a while. Smith said he was able to play last week against the Carolina Panthers, but some things are out of his control. "I didn't bust my ass this whole summer, I didn't continue to get up at 4:30 in the morning every day, to come out here and not play," he said. "My focus has been to getting where I was supposed to be."
