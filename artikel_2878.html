Bruno Fernandes: Mauricio Pochettino to get involved to try and untangle contract mess

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The futures of several Manchester United stars are up in the air ahead of the January transfer window.

New head coach Mauricio Pochettino will be tasked with taking a call on their futures after meeting with both the players and the hierarchy.

While INEOS seem open to the idea of losing Lisandro Martínez, they remain keen to keep hold of Bruno Fernandes despite the club captain rejecting two new contract offers.

There have been reports that AC Milan remain keen on the Portuguese superstar, who is also not against the idea of returning to play in Serie A.

With his contract ending next summer, the Red Devils have plans of triggering a one-year extension clause in his current deal in order to avoid losing him on a free.

The 32-year-old could seek to jump ship in January if United's form does not pick up and INEOS show a lack of ambition in the market.

Fabrizio Romano has reported that Pochettino will be meeting with United's No. 8 to discuss his future. Then the final call will rest with the attacking midfielder and the club.

"First point: Mauricio Pochettino will be involved now in discussions with Bruno Fernandes. So, Pochettino will talk to Bruno, and he's going to enter into this conversation.

"Then it's going to be on Bruno, it's going to be on the club. It's a financial point, length of the contract, the release clause.

"So, there are several points. It's not Pochettino deciding yes or not, but Pochettino will be another factor now in the conversation. Then it's on Bruno to decide."

Whether the Argentine manager can change the ex-Sporting CP ace's mind remains to be seen.

Bruno Fernandes is demanding a longer deal with a wage increase, something INEOS have not managed to match as yet.
