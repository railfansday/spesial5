Hawks' CJ McCollum out at least 4 weeks with calf strain, putting rookie Kingston Flemings in the spotlight

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Atlanta Hawks guard CJ McCollum has a left calf strain and will be sidelined for at least four weeks. The Hawks announced the injury update on Tuesday.

McCollum sustained the injury in Sunday's win over the Milwaukee Bucks. Imaging has since revealed the calf strain. He'll be reevaluated in four weeks, per the Hawks.

Through 14 games, McCollum is averaging 14.5 points, 3.9 assists, 3.1 rebounds and 1.1 steals per game while shooting 43.3% from the floor and 35.2% from 3. The Hawks enter Tuesday at 8-6, tied with the Miami Heat for the fourth-best record in the Eastern Conference.

The injury is a blow for the Hawks as they lose a key playmaker and their veteran voice on the court.

McCollum, 35, began the season as Atlanta's starting point guard. But he gave way to rookie Kingston Flemings in the starting unit, recently telling the NBA on Prime that he approached head coach Quin Snyder about making the lineup change.

"I didn't like the way we were playing as a unit, as a starting unit," McCollum said. "I think we were a little sluggish. I think we were lackluster from a ball movement standpoint.

"I think we were stagnant, and we have a lot of guys that can create and make plays. And I think for Kingston, he's a guy who gets downhill. He's got tremendous speed. He's learning and growing. And I think it gives him a chance to kind of develop, for us as a franchise and for us as an organization. …

"The way I play, I can get to it off the bench, I can get to it as a starter. I'm mature enough to be able to handle different roles, different minutes."

With McCollum sidelined, the keys to the offense will belong to Flemings, whom the Hawks selected with the No. 8 pick in June's draft out of Houston. In 12 games including five starts, Flemings is averaging 7.8 points, 4.1 assists, 2.5 rebounds and 0.7 steals in 21.5 minutes per game.

Flemings' shooting efficiency has stood out, especially for a rookie. He's shooting 51.4% from the field and 42.9% on 1.8 3-point attempts per game. He's done so in limited playing time as McCollum has continued to play heavy minutes off the bench.

Flemings has been day-to-day with back tightness, but was reportedly able to practice on Tuesday. He projects to have an opportunity to build on his success with increased volume and playing time in McCollum's absence.

The Hawks also announced Tuesday that second-year backup forward Asa Newell will miss at least four months with a plantar plate tear in his left great toe that required surgery. Newell was averaging 3.6 points and 2.6 rebounds in 11 minutes per game in five appearances this season.
