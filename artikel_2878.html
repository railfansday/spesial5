Cowboys’ Jerry Jones stands by Brian Schottenheimer amid firing speculation

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Cowboys' Jerry Jones stands by Brian Schottenheimer amid firing speculation appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Jerry Jones continues to show full commitment towards Brian Schottenheimer's ability to lead the Dallas Cowboys for the long term despite ups and downs within the first two years.

Schottenheimer started his head coaching career in the NFL with the Cowboys, looking to lead the Cowboys back to the playoffs. They came up short in Year 1, but even though they made improvements on both sides of the ball, they continue to be shaky with their overall form.

Jones spoke with media about Schottenheimer on Friday, per reporter Jon Machota. The longtime owner and president continues to have confidence in his hiring of the head coach, looking forward to his long-term growth with the franchise.

"Given the ability to live to tell about it, Brian will be a year from now, two years from now, three years from now, he'll be much more (prepared) for the kinds of challenges a head coach has. There's no question, you pay a price when you're doing it for the first time. … I still like (our) bet (on him)," Jones said.

Jerry Jones sees the potential with Brian Schottenheimer, seeing the Cowboys offense stand out as one of the best in the NFL. If they can figure out their issues on the defensive side of the ball, they can have an upward trajectory for the rest of the 2026 campaign.

Dallas has a 4-5 record this season, sitting at second place in the NFC East Division standings. They are above the Washington Commanders and the Philadelphia Eagles while trailing the New York Giants. In terms of the conference standings, they are 10th place. They lead over the Commanders and the New Orleans Saints while being under the Los Angeles Rams and the Seattle Seahawks.

The Cowboys will continue preparations for their next matchup, being at home. They host the San Francisco 49ers in Week 10 as kickoff commences on Nov. 15 at 4:25 p.m. ET.
