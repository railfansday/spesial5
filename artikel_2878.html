College volleyball fans, rejoice — the Big Ten is hosting their inaugural conference tournament this weekend in Fishers, Indiana.

As one of college volleyball's most elite conferences, this tournament is long overdue. Competition has been fierce this season, with several programs showing promise to make national campaigns come December.

15 of the conference's 18 teams qualified for the inaugural tournament, with Iowa, Northwestern and Ohio State ruled out of contention. As expected, Nebraska clinched the No. 1 overall seed, earning themselves a double-bye alongside Oregon, Penn State and Wisconsin. The Huskers already clinched the regular-season title with an impressive 28-0 record.

Who will conquer the first-ever Big Ten volleyball tournament?

Here's everything you need to know about the competition, including TV channel and streaming options for each match.

Each match in the inaugural Big Ten volleyball tournament will be broadcast live on Big Ten Network. Cord-cutters can also catch the action on B1G+, or Fubo, which offers a free trial for all new users.

The 2026 Big Ten volleyball tournament plays from Friday, Nov. 20 through Wednesday, Nov. 25. All matches will take place at Fishers Event Center in Fishers, IN.
