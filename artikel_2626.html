Mike Tomlin: DK Metcalf lacks "professional maturity"

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Current Steelers coach Mike McCarthy has some decisions to make regarding receiver DK Metcalf. His former coach likely wouldn’t hesitate to send an unmistakable message.

Mike Tomlin made his thoughts on Metcalf clear during halftime of Sunday night’s NBC game, after Metcalf was ejected from the Week 11 loss to the Eagles.

“It’s one thing to struggle in terms of production, and he certainly is struggling,” Tomlin said. “But it’s another thing to complicate it by showing lack of professional maturity. And that’s what he showed. He was frustrated and so he did some things that are outside the lines of the game, and certainly he paid the consequence today, but he’s probably gonna continue to pay the consequence, whether it’s from New York or whether it’s in-house.”

A league-imposed suspension is possible, and last year’s two-game suspension for an altercation with a fan in Detroit could be a factor in such an outcome. The team could choose to suspend Metcalf, or to make him a healthy scratch for Friday’s game against the Broncos.

The fact that Metcalf’s production has lagged this year makes it much easier to take a stand. And that’s how it always works. There’s a sliding scale, where in-house consequences become more likely the less impactful the player is.

Currently, Metcalf isn’t impactful. As the Steelers find themselves at 6-4 and staring at games against the Broncos, Texans, and Jaguars, taking an aggressive stance as to Metcalf could give the team a much-needed kick in the ass.

If, of course, the locker room will react positively to the team suspending or benching Metcalf. His “good friend” Aaron Rodgers will need to be on board. Even though Rodgers isn’t the player he once was, he has tremendous influence over his teammates.

And that’s one of the problems that flows from keeping Rodgers around for the first year of McCarthy’s tenure. A certain amount of the coach’s authority has been delegated to Rodgers.

If that authority is going to be exercised against Metcalf, Rodgers needs to approve it.
