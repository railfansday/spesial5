Dodgers suffer unexpected loss as reliever undergoes Tommy John surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Last week, Dodgers general manager Brandon Gomes said the team wasn't expecting any other players to have surgery this offseason.

But on Wednesday, some more unexpected injury news emerged.

Right-handed reliever Seth Halvorsen underwent a Tommy John procedure that will sideline him for all of 2027, the club announced. The operation marks the latest setback for Halvorsen, a hard-throwing 26-year-old who has a career ERA of 4.78 in 86 big-league appearances.

Seth Halvorsen of the Los Angeles Dodgers pitches against the Kansas City Royals during a game at Dodger Stadium on Aug. 11, 2026, in Los Angeles. Getty Images

A former seventh-round pick of the Rockies, Halvorsen was acquired by the Dodgers a few weeks before this year's trade deadline in exchange for prospects Nick Frasso and Landyn Vidourek. The hope was that he would be a diamond-in-the-rough addition, similar to Will Klein's emergence the season before.

Instead, Halvorsen struggled in 11 appearances with the team, giving up nine earned runs while striking out just six batters. While he pitched better for the team's Triple-A affiliate, going 10 outings without allowing an earned run, he wasn't in the picture for their playoff roster.

Halvorsen might have had a renewed pathway to big-league opportunities in 2027, with several key relievers –– including Evan Phillips, who has since signed with the Braves –– hitting the free agent market this winter.

But now, he will spend all of next year rehabbing instead.

Seth Halvorsen of the Los Angeles Dodgers pitches against the Cincinnati Reds in Cincinnati, Sept. 16, 2026. Getty Images

He remains under team control through 2031.

Halvorsen becomes the third Dodgers player to undergo surgery since the end of the season, joining Klein (who had loose bodies removed from his elbow) and catcher Dalton Rushing (who had an internal brace procedure in his elbow). Shohei Ohtani avoided surgery after his injury-plagued 2026, but his role as a pitcher is still to be determined for next season.
