Lakers’ red-hot start to season will strike fear in Celtics fans hearts

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Lakers are on a quest to become a championship-level team.

And if history can be an indicator, their hot start to the 2026-27 NBA season suggests they're on the right path.

Lakers star guards Luka Doncic (77) and Austin Reaves (15). NBAE via Getty Images

With Monday's home win over the Jazz, the Lakers improved to 15-3 on the season, which was not only tied with the Spurs for the league's best record, but also a positive sign for the Lakers' hopes of being title contenders.

This season is the sixth time this century the Lakers have started a season by winning at least 15 of their first 18 games.

All five of those seasons ended with the Lakers making the NBA Finals, including winning the title in four of them.

Los Angeles Lakers head coach JJ Redick watches his players. AP Photo/Kyusung Gong

The other five times since 2000 that the Lakers won at least 83 percent of their first 18 games were: 2001-2002 (16-2), 2003-04 (15-3), 2008-09 (16-2), 2009-10 (15-3) and 2019-20 (16-2).

They won the 2002, '09, '10 and '20 championships after their hot start to those seasons.

They made it to the Finals in 2004, which was their fourth championship series appearance in five years, before losing to the Pistons in five games.

Walker Kessler, left, and Luka Doncic high-five during a game. NBAE via Getty Images

The Lakers have won at least 15 of their first 18 games 13 times in franchise history.

Half (six of 13) of those seasons ended with them winning the NBA title, while three more ended with them at least making it to the NBA Finals.

Another commonality between those seasons is that the Lakers won at least 54 regular season games (or the equivalent of it in an 82-game season in the case of the pandemic-shortened 2019-20) each time they had such a hot start to the season.

For a team that was projected to be a borderline playoff contender, that's great company to be in — a high standard to live up to — but also a sign they're on the right path.
