Grizzlies rookie Cam Boozer out at least 4 weeks with ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Memphis Grizzlies rookie Cam Boozer is expected to miss at least four weeks with an ankle injury. The Grizzlies announced news of the injury on Monday. Imaging of the injury revealed a moderate-grade medial sprain of Boozer's right ankle.

Per the Grizzlies, testing also confirmed the structural stability of Boozer's ankle, and he's expected to make a full recovery. He'll be reevaluated in approximately four weeks.

Boozer suffered the injury during Thursday's win over the Warriors. He grabbed his ankle in pain after falling following a drive to the basket. He was sidelined for Saturday's loss to the Timberwolves.

The news is a tough blow for the Grizzlies and Boozer, who's off to a strong start to his NBA career.

Memphis selected Boozer with the No. 3 pick in June's NBA Draft following a freshman season at Duke that earned him consensus National Player of the Year honors. Boozer has so far delivered on his potential as a standout from a rookie class that's projected as one of the best in NBA history.

In 16 games, Boozer has averaged 16.6 points, 9.2 rebounds, 5.3 assists, 0.8 steals and 0.8 blocks per game. As with most rookies, Boozer has room to improve his efficiency with a 41.7% field-goal rate and a 32.2% success rate from 3. But his dynamic offensive game as a skilled, floor-stretching power forward has translated to the NBA early in his career.

Boozer's impact has contributed to winning on the floor for Memphis. A Grizzlies team that finished 25-57 last season is off to a 12-5 start, which was good for fifth place in the West going into Monday's games. Boozer has emerged a a leader on the team alongside breakout guard Ty Jerome (24.8 ppg, 5.3 apg) as the Grizzlies look like contenders to return to the postseason.

They'll look to stay afloat in the top half of the Western Conference standings over the next four weeks without their standout rookie.
