Joshua Kimmich blasts Bayern Munich board over Jamal Musiala health situation

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Joshua Kimmich has hit out at Bayern Munich’s hierarchy over their comments about Jamal Musiala’s health situation.

Musiala collapsed in two different games in the space of four days during Bayern’s pre-season campaign in the summer and revealed that he had been suffering from ‘absence seizures resulting from a neurological dysfunction’.

The 23-year-old’s start to the season was also disrupted due to a thigh injury and the midfielder has started just one game in the Bundesliga so far.

Last week, Bayern’s supervisory board member, Karl-Heinz Rummenigge, suggested that Musiala may be offered a pay cut due to his health situation and claims no club would buy the midfielder ‘under these conditions’.

Earlier this month, Bayern’s honorary president, Uli Hoeness, also raised questions about Musiala’s contract due to his health issue.

Speaking after Bayern’s 2-1 defeat away to Lille in the Champions League on Wednesday night, Kimmich described the club’s actions towards Musiala as ‘completely incomprehensible’.

‘Generally speaking, you have to look at the situation from different perspectives. I completely understand that this isn’t an easy situation for a club either,’ Kimmich said.

‘Of course, I’ve followed Jamal’s journey over the last two years. I mean, he carried us at the European Championship and was our standout player afterwards. Then he extended his contract, got the number 10 shirt here and for the German national team. Then came the first muscle injury. I can say from my own experience, when you extend your contract, especially at that age, it’s not about sitting back and enjoying the next five years. It’s an incentive and a challenge you want to live up to.

‘Then Jamal had the illness and the serious injury. We mustn’t forget that in the last year and a half, I don’t think he’s played four or five games in a row.

‘So, to a certain extent, it’s understandable that he’s not at that level. And it’s also understandable that the club is worried because of the illness. What I don’t understand is the way this is being communicated publicly. It should never happen that this is played out at the expense of a player.

‘Of course, the club is always the highest priority and is bigger than us as players but still not bigger than the individual. And that is an absolute no-go and not in the spirit of Bayern, especially in the current phase, where I myself have often emphasised how very, very special the club is.

‘Even in my own situation, the club was very, very special. You could really feel that this ‘Mia san mia’ spirit was truly lived. What has happened now should simply never happen. I find it completely incomprehensible.’

Asked if he has spoken with Musiala about the comments from Bayern’s hierarchy, Kimmich said: ‘Honestly, I get the impression that Jamal isn’t aware of everything that’s going on out there. I think that’s actually a good thing.

‘Of course, there was some discussion. For me, the most important thing is that he comes to Sabener Strasse every day and enjoys playing football. That’s the most important thing in his situation.

‘You also mustn’t forget that he had another operation in the summer and then suffered another injury. But I see him and experience him every day. I see how he works, how hard he works. That also adds to the situation. It’s not that he has problems because he’s unprofessional, rather, he’s there every day, naturally wants to get the most out of himself, works very, very hard every day, and I think he enjoys being with us in the team. I hope he feels that we all stand behind him 100 per cent – ​​the team, the management, and the club.’
