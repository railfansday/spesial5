Erling Haaland reveals Man City concerns ahead of ‘most difficult’ Arsenal trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Erling Haaland says Manchester City are ”not clicking fully” ahead of “the most difficult game of the season" against Arsenal.

City have won just two of their past seven matches in all competitions after twice coming from behind to draw 2-2 with Napoli in the Champions League on Tuesday.

Haaland endured a frustrating evening at the Etihad Stadium, failing to score and managing only one shot on target. The Norway international believes his side are not producing the performances expected of them and called for greater consistency from his team-mates.

"We managed to come back two times but we demand more, we have to do better, we have to find some ways to make us control the game more and perform in a better way," Haaland told beIN Sports.

"I have no idea what we were missing, but you could feel it in the last few games that there's something that's not clicking fully and we have to find out and we have to stay positive and we have to get the best out of every single player."

In a separate post-match interview, Haaland reflected on the impact of results on confidence and the need for City to remain positive.

"Football is so easy and so difficult. When you win games everything is easy and you know what to do, and when you don't win you start to question everything. The perfect thing to do is don't think.

"We have so much quality so it's about getting that quality out of every single player.

"We have to regroup, stay positive, bring all the energy on the pitch and try to do the best we can.

"We missed a goal. In the end we tried to push but we couldn't. It's not good enough. We let them in too easy and we were not able to control and attack the game as we want."

City now face a difficult test against Arsenal at Emirates Stadium, but he is looking forward to facing foe Gabriel.

"We go to the champions, it's the most difficult game of the season, the champions away," he said.

"We need a top performance from every single one of us. It's always good to play against the best teams.

"Arsenal have been the best team last year, and one of the best in the last five years now.

“It's always fun to play and it's always fun to play against Gabriel."
