3 ways Disney+ now sells Avengers: Doomsday tickets inside the app

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Disney+ offers a Tickets tab, post-trailer prompts and pause screens for Avengers: Doomsday seats. See how each of the 3 routes works.

Streaming services have started selling the moments when people stop watching. A pause, the last seconds of a trailer or the gap between episodes once held nothing but a viewer waiting for the next thing, and now those moments hold offers. TheDesk.net reports that Disney $DIS, Netflix $NFLX, Amazon $AMZN and Warner Bros Discovery $WBD are all putting more into ad-supported streaming. With that much money behind ads, the companies are rebuilding the viewing screen so it can show sales messages. Disney owns both Marvel and Disney+, according to ABC News. As Disney pushes further into ads, its own film studio can become a customer of its own streaming service, and the advertiser and the outlet can be the same company. For the viewer, the line between watching a show and being sold something gets thinner.

Disney is making this pitch for its next superhero film on a deadline. Avengers: Doomsday opens in theaters on Friday, Dec. 18, according to ABC News. Disney+ puts its ticket offers in front of fans before that date, while they are still deciding whether to go and when to buy seats. Disney did not build the tools behind these offers for a single movie. "Each release informed the next, enabling teams to achieve parity across platforms and scale rapidly while maintaining high creative and performance standards," Alex Combs, vice president of product management for ad platforms at Disney Entertainment and ESPN, said, as reported by TheDesk.net. That makes the ticket routes part of an ad system Disney designed to reuse, not a one-time Marvel promotion. A fan who enjoys the shortcut is also the target of that system.

What's on Disney+ gathered the Disney+ routes for buying seats to the upcoming Marvel movie, and a fan inside the app has more than one way to reach a ticket. Each route raises the same question for anyone planning to see the film: Is it a shortcut worth taking, or an ad that happens to end in a ticket?

Fans can now start buying seats for Avengers: Doomsday from a Tickets tab inside Disney $DIS+, according to What's on Disney+. A fan no longer has to leave the app and look up a theater chain's website to begin the purchase. Disney also controls the home screen around that tab, so the same company decides both what a viewer watches and where the ticket offer appears.

Disney is also filling its service with reasons to see the new film. Avengers: Endgame Encore is a new edit of the last Avengers film with four minutes of added footage that points toward Doomsday. It debuted on Disney+ on Nov. 13, 2026, according to ABC News. Fans can watch that footage weeks before the film reaches theaters, while they are still deciding about tickets. Disney also groups earlier Marvel shows and movies into a collection called "Countdown to Avengers: Doomsday," which presents the older titles as viewing to finish before the new release. By the time a viewer opens the Tickets tab, Disney may already have spent hours of that viewer's watching time pointing them toward it.

For a fan who has already decided to go, starting the purchase inside the app is a real convenience. It is less clear whether the tab persuades anyone else.

The tab is easy to use, but the urge to use it may have come from the same screen.

Disney $DIS+ puts its ticket pitch in front of Avengers fans at the moment a trailer finishes, when most viewers would otherwise move on to the next thing. What's on Disney+ reports that a fan who finishes the last Avengers: Doomsday trailer in the app is prompted with either a phone alert or a scan code for Fandango. Both prompts move the next step off the TV and onto the phone in the viewer's hand, so the fan doesn't have to go looking for showtimes.

Disney+ starts the sale, but it doesn't finish it. The prompt only links out to Fandango, and the purchase itself takes place on Fandango's side. In practice, the post-trailer prompt works much like an ad with a link attached. A fan who follows it ends up making the same Fandango purchase they could have made by opening the site on their own, only sooner.

The shortcut saves the fan a search, not a checkout.

Disney $DIS+ now answers the pause button with a sales pitch for Avengers: Doomsday. Viewers usually pause to grab a snack or answer the door, and it is the one moment when they aren't asking to be shown anything. When a viewer pauses any title, Disney+ shows a Doomsday screen with links to buy tickets, according to What's on Disney+. Someone partway through a show with no connection to the Avengers gets the same ticket pitch as a devoted Marvel fan.

The Doomsday screen is ad space that Disney already sells. TheDesk.net reports that Disney's Pause Ads format runs when a viewer pauses a program. The Marvel ticket offer uses that same trigger, which makes Doomsday one more advertiser in a slot that Disney also offers to outside brands. A viewer who finds the screen handy is still looking at an ad, and the convenience is simply the way Disney delivers it.

Disney has not shown that its pause screens sell movie tickets. The results Disney has shared for upgraded pause formats such as Pause Plus Trivia come from its own beta testing, according to TheDesk.net. Those are the company's own numbers, and none of them link a pause screen to a ticket purchase. Nobody yet knows whether a screen that shows up during a pause moves anyone from the couch to a theater seat.

For a viewer, the pause screen is an ad first and a shortcut second, and it is worth tapping only for someone who had already decided to go.
