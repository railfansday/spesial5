Taty Castellanos' performances have been under the microscope since West Ham United's relegation to the Championship.

The 28-year-old netted seven times after joining from Lazio in January of this year, but it wasn't enough to save in the Hammers' bid for survival.

After West Ham dropped down to the second tier of English football, Castellanos was expected to breeze through the division.

However, 14 games later, and he has just a single goal in the 6-0 win over Wrexham, though his four assists cannot be ignored.

The centre-forward's effort levels and body language has been scrutinised heavily, and the reason for his lack of goals may have just become clear.

It had already been suggested that Castellanos has no desire to play in the Championship, and that now appears to be true.

Reports from Argentina state that the player "wants to leave West Ham" and he is being heavily linked with River Plate ahead of January.

This is hardly a surprise. Nonetheless, fans are utterly furious.

If true, remove him from the squad and bring Pablo back, and also include Ajala.

Attitude was great when he joined, but he’s absolutely stunk the place out this season

Mick Brown, a former Hammers scout, has told Football Insider that Castellanos could be the first one out the door as Scott Parker plans a squad overhaul.

Joel Piroe has proven that he can lead the line, supported by Divine Mukasa or Pablo, so there's really no need to keep a player who doesn't want to be there.

Castellanos is far from the Irons' only problem right now, with the team currently sitting ninth in the Championship table after 15 games.

The goalkeeper spot is always a major talking point. Neither Mads Hermansen or Alphonse Areola are worthy of taking the No.1 shirt.

In defence, Max Kilman should be nowhere the pitch, while Adam Boiro, who joined on loan from Athletic Club, has made a single appearance.

Edson Alvarez's future, obviously, needs sorting out, as does Maxwel Cornet's, while Soungoutou Magassa could also be sold in January.
