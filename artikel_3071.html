Apple releasing Product(RED) versions of two products soon, per report

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Apple plans to launch new Product(RED) versions of AirPods Max and the HomePod mini 2, according to a new Bloomberg report. The launch is set for “on or around” December 1, which is World AIDS Day.

Apple has worked with the Product(RED) organization for years, though it hasn’t released hardware through that partnership in a while. The last Product(RED) iPhones that Apple sold were the iPhone 14 and iPhone 14 Plus in 2022. Currently, Apple doesn’t sell any Product(RED) hardware, cases, or other accessories.

The new HomePod mini will have a “red mesh exterior,” while the new AirPods Max will have a silver design with a red Digital Crown:

The company is gearing up to release the new HomePod mini 2 with a red mesh exterior and a new version of the AirPods Max 2 headphones, according to people familiar with the matter, who asked not to be identified because the announcements aren’t public.

The headphones will feature a silver finish, white mesh ear cups and a white headband, along with a red Digital Crown, the control on top of the device used to manage playback. Apple doesn’t currently offer the AirPods Max in silver.

Through its Product(RED) partnership, Apple typically donates a portion of proceeds to the organization, supporting the fight against AIDS and providing millions of people with lifesaving care.

Anecdotally, 9to5Mac has heard from a source that Apple has also developed a Product(RED) version of the AirTag item tracker. Whether it’s released alongside the AirPods Max 2 and HomePod mini remains to be seen.
