Marchex (MCHX) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Bundled AI actions are driving bigger customer spend, and management outlined a $100 million target.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Marchex, Inc. (MCHX +7.69%) reported its return to positive GAAP net income and year-over-year revenue growth following the acquisition and integration of Archenia. Management indicated that the company is transitioning from providing conversational analytics to a model that bundles insights with AI-powered automated actions to drive measurable revenue outcomes for enterprise customers. The company established a long-term annual revenue objective of more than $100 million while maintaining an annualized adjusted EBITDA run rate exceeding $10 million through expense efficiency and organizational realignment.
