PrivadoVPN’s 24-month subscription costs $1.11 per month ($30 upfront) and includes 3 FREE months. There's also a 30-day money-back guarantee, so you can try it risk-free before committing.

Admittedly, PrivadoVPN doesn’t rival the very best VPNs, but it has all the key ingredients of a good VPN. Starting with speed, PrivadoVPN clocked in at 2,334 Mbps on a 10 Gbps line during our testing, firmly securing its place among the fastest VPNs we’ve tested, alongside juggernauts Proton VPN, ExpressVPN, and NordVPN.

Not only will PrivadoVPN not slow you down, but its above-par unblocking capabilities bring the world’s content to your Android, iOS, Windows, or Mac device – PrivadoVPN has clean apps for all – from wherever you might be. It easily unblocked Netflix in all regions, plus Disney+ in the US and UK, as well as regional services, including BBC iPlayer, Channel 4, ITVX, 9Now, and 10Play.

PrivadoVPN is underwhelming in this aspect, with only “hundreds” of servers in 50 countries. It is, however, a great torrenting VPN, with P2P on all servers and support for the SOCKS5 proxy.

Elsewhere, you get the best and most up-to-date VPN protocols, an app-level kill switch, split tunneling for all major platforms, including iOS, VPN obfuscation (PrivadoVPN calls it Scrambler), parental controls, and an ad blocker that stopped 92% of ads in our tests – the highest of any VPN we’ve reviewed.

Overall, PrivadoVPN gets the basics right, and while it may lack a few advanced features, at just $1.11 a month, it’s hard to argue against – especially with its full antivirus thrown in for free.
