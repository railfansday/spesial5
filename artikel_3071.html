Sigma Lithium (SGML -5.38%) reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026, highlighted by a $125 million debt refinancing and the resolution of environmental regulatory challenges in Brazil. Management reported that the issuance of five-year convertible bonds enabled the full repayment of secured debt, significantly extending the company's debt maturity profile and lowering short-term liabilities. Despite temporary operational suspensions during the quarter that impacted sales volumes and revenue, the company maintained unit production costs below current market prices and resumed full operations in early October. The company stated it is now focused on its multiyear growth strategy, which includes expanding industrial capacity to 830,000 tons per year by 2028.
