Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Production ramp-up and deferred deliveries define its near-term outlook.

Ur-Energy Inc. (URG -16.10%) reported its transition to a multiasset production profile following the launch of operations at Shirley Basin and the first resin shipment to its Lost Creek processing hub. Management indicated that the company is currently focused on a production ramp-up across its Wyoming assets while advancing a district-scale expansion strategy. The company reported significant year-over-year increases in uranium sales and capture rates as it optimizes wellfield flow and processing capacity. Operational initiatives include the implementation of sand filtration systems and the construction of a permanent wastewater treatment facility to support higher throughput levels. Additionally, the company is progressing exploration at Lost Creek South and North Hadsell while preparing a technical report for the potential development of Lost Soldier as a satellite mine.
