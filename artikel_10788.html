Japan launches a ‘dumbphone’ that doesn’t connect to internet and looks like a Nokia brick

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A brand-new phone that doesn’t connect to the internet has been launched in Japan, and it looks like the classic Nokia phones that you might remember.

For years, smartphone users have been trying to find creative solutions to cut down their screen time, especially as phones have started doing weekly reviews of app usage and screen time.

Some have opted to set time limits on the apps they can use, while others have created heavyweight cases that will quite literally force you to work out in order to use your smartphone.

In other cases, some people have taken a step back in time, ditching their smartphones for flip phones and devices that don’t actually connect to the internet. These throwback phones have popped up across Europe and the United States, and now Japan is getting in on the act with a new device.

As per The Japan Times, Japan’s KDDI Corporation has introduced a mobile phone with “minimal functions.”

Named the Infobar Zero, it will be available to pick up on Christmas Day, and is limited to calls, text messages, and other basic features. Owners won’t have the ability to connect to the internet, but it will have a camera and a music player.

While it isn’t a complete recreation of Nokia’s iconic 3310 phone, the Infobar Zero does strike up plenty of comparisons to that classic device.

Additionally, the ‘dumbphone’ isn’t aimed at replacing your smartphone, but rather providing a break from the smartphone life.

It has been planned by KDDI for quite some time, just as a way of relieving the reliance to live life on apps.

Owners will be able to register their phone numbers on the Zero and a smartphone as part of KDDI’s “two numbers” system – sort of like using an eSIM in some parts of the world.
