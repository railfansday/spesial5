Cowboys vs Titans: Writer predictions for Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Dallas gets one of its best opportunities of the season to steady things after the 46-40 loss to San Francisco. Tennessee is 1-8 and ranks near the bottom of the league on both sides of the ball. Dallas should be the better team here, but after giving up 584 yards last week, the defense still has something to prove.

Tennessee should lean heavily on Tony Pollard. The former Cowboy has 628 rushing yards and six touchdowns, and Dallas has just been gashed for 214 yards on the ground by San Francisco. If the Titans can get Pollard moving early, it takes pressure off Cam Ward and gives Tennessee a chance to control the tempo.

Dallas has to make Ward win this game through the air. Tennessee ranks near the bottom of the league in scoring and total offense, while Ward has been inconsistent despite showing flashes. The Cowboys need to contain Pollard, keep Ward in the pocket and force long third downs. With Tennessee also dealing with injuries at receiver, Dallas should have the advantage if it avoids the missed tackles and busted assignments that hurt so badly last week.

This is where Dallas should take control. Tennessee owns the league's worst pass defense, while Dak Prescott is playing some of his best football and just threw five touchdowns against San Francisco. With CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens both capable of taking over a game, Dallas should attack downfield rather than becoming too conservative.

Javonte Williams still needs enough work to keep Tennessee honest, but this matchup belongs in Prescott's hands. The Titans can create turnovers, so Dallas cannot get careless, but if the offensive line protects well there should be favourable matchups across the field all afternoon.

The Cowboys should have too much firepower for Tennessee. If Dallas tackles better, limits Pollard and avoids gifting the Titans short fields, this is a game it should control.

Often a shakeup mid-season sees at least a temporary improvement in performance. I think that will be the case here. Cowboys 34 Titans 23

The Cowboys made a big shakeup this week by taking play-calling duties away from Christian Parker. I feel like the defense is going to try to win this game for their defensive coordinator, and it sets them up well with the Tennessee Titans coming to town.

The Titans should be a get-right game for the Cowboys on all fronts, for a team that's in desperate need of a win. Dallas should have their best performance of the season on defense and should have a strong performance on offense, leading to a 34-17 win before Thanksgiving.

The Cowboys have struggled against every team this season, good and bad. Considering the game day changes Dallas has made with the defensive play-calling duties, there is a chance Dallas finally takes care of things against a bad team early in a game instead of in the waning moments. But I won't trust it until I see it. My gut feeling tells me Dallas wins by a touchdown or less. Let's go with 31-27 Cowboys

The Cowboys are no doubt a mess, but this absolutely has to be a get-right matchup for them. With all the issues both on and off the field I don't have much confidence they'll do it, but I'm going to pick Dallas to get a win. Give me the Cowboys, 33-23.

To lose this one would bring shame to the game for Dallas. Sure, things are rough right now for the Cowboys after last week, and to lose this one would be a massive shock to all of Cowboys Nation. Confidence is best described as positive, but skidish. Cowboys win 31-17.
