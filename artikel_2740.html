Bills don't allow a touchdown for third straight game, beat Dolphins 26-9

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Bills defense flexed its muscles at home against the Dolphins on Sunday.

Miami picked up 185 yards over the entire game and never got the ball into the end zone, which makes it three straight games where the Bills have allowed nothing more than a field goal. The 26-9 win was their sixth in a row and their 9-1 record matches the best 10-game start in franchise history, but things should get tougher in Week 12.

Dolphins quarterback Kyle McCord was making his second NFL start, which fits in with recent trends for the AFC East frontrunner. They have also faced the likes of Joe Fagnano, Carson Wentz, and Cade Klubnik for all or large portions of their four wins after their Week Seven bye, but Thursday night’s game against the Chiefs will force the defense to tangle with Patrick Mahomes.

That will force the defense to clean up any loose ends and the Bills offense will be looking to do the same over the next few days. The Bills never punted on Sunday, but Josh Allen threw a pair of interceptions and five of their drives ended with field goal attempts rather than touchdowns. That is enough to outlast teams like the Dolphins, but finishing off their bid for the top seed in the AFC is going to take more emphatic efforts over the rest of the season.

Running back James Cook should play a leading role in that effort. Cook ran 22 times for 89 yards and a touchdown, which pushed him over 1,000 rushing yards for the season and gives him nine straight games with at least 75 rushing yards and a score. Per CBS, that’s the longest streak of its kind in NFL history.

Allen finished 27-of-45 for 304 yards and a touchdown that went to wide receiver DJ Moore. Moore had eight catches for 91 yards and Khalil Shakir added five catches for 101 yards. On the defensive side, Greg Rousseau had a pair of sacks to move him to 10 for the season and safety Damar Hamlin had his first interception of the season.

The Dolphins will be back in Miami for Week 12 and they’ll host the Jets in a bid to sweep the season series from their divisional rivals. That won’t have any impact on the playoff race, but both of those teams have at least one eye on the draft order as they play out the string on a losing season.
