Tired-looking Lakers can't make their shots in loss to Jazz

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The tank appeared to be empty, and there was nothing Luka Doncic or Quentin Grimes or Walker Kessler could do to help the Lakers this time.

Doncic and Grimes especially had been driving the Lakers during their nine-game winning streak, but their struggles Wednesday were a microcosm of what ailed the Lakers during a 109-97 loss to the Utah Jazz at Crypto.com Arena.

Doncic and Grimes combined to shoot 18 for 48 from the field and six for 22 from three-point range.

As a team, the Lakers shot 38.2% from the field and 27.3% from three-point range, their nine-game winning streak going down in the process.

After the Jazz opened an 18-point lead with 1:51 left, Lakers coach JJ Redick waved the white flag, pulling his starters after a rough night.

The Lakers were playing a back-to-back game and third game this week, and they looked fatigued from it all.

The 3-13 Jazz also was playing a back-to-back game, but won their second straight game despite turning the ball over 23 times.

"I asked the guys, 'Who in here feels like they played well tonight?' Nobody raised their hand," Redick said. "So, like everybody can be better. We can sit here and nitpick every single guy. Nitpick me. It's like whatever. Could my rotation's been different? Maybe gotten a little more offense. Maybe played a little more size at the five…Yeah, we'll do all that.

"But we didn't play well. Nobody played well. So, it's not Walker. It's not Luka. It's not Q. It's not Sandro (Mamukelashvili). Nobody had a great game."

Doncic had 29 points, but he was 10 for 28 from the field and two for 12 from three-point range, never able to get on track because of the constant double teams the Jazz sent his way.

"Honestly I've just got to do way better than this," Doncic, who was assessed a technical foul in the second quarter, said. "This is unacceptable for my part. I got to lead the team in better ways. So just, like I said, got to look at this game, got to move on and learn from this."

Doncic had to do a lot with his backcourt running mate, Austin Reaves (left heel bruise), out.

Doncic has been carrying the team, but he still took responsibility for the loss and explained what he has to do to be a better leader for the Lakers.

Lakers forward Sandro Mamukelashvili goes to the basket against Utah's Brice Sensabaugh and Svi Mykhailiuk. (Eric Thayer / Los Angeles Times)

"Well, just a little bit of everything," he said. "Obviously the shots, the turnovers. Those [are] two [of the] specifics. I think defensively, we're in a good space. Just offensively, I've got to lead the team better than this."

Grimes had 20 points, but he was eight for 20 from the field and four for 10 from three-point range.

He had just come off a career-high 47-point game Tuesday night against the Hornets.

"We had a lot of emotion in last night's game coming back as a team, as a whole, and it was a quick less-than-24-hour turnaround against a team that's better than their record (indicates)," Grimes said. "We tried to get as much energy as we could into that third, but it just wasn't enough. Not enough legs. We felt like none of us played well tonight and we were still in the game. So that's a positive takeaway, for sure."

Kessler spent his first four years in the NBA playing for the Jazz, making relationships and friendships that remain even though he now plays for the Lakers. But when the Lakers and Kessler played the Jazz last month in Utah, those same friends did all they could to make life difficult for the big man.

Lakers center Walker Kessler dunks over Jazz guard Ace Bailey in the second half on Wednesday. (Eric Thayer / Los Angeles Times)

Kessler had just two points on just two shots, to go along with seven rebounds. The Lakers won the game, so it was all good for Kessler.

He missed the game against the Charlotte Hornets on Tuesday night with right lower leg soreness but was back starting and looking forward to having a better outing tWednesday against the Jazz.

He finished the game with a double-double of 10 points and 11 rebounds, but was four-for-10 from the free-throw line and three-for-seven from the field.

"It was a tough night for me," Kessler said. "Tried to get it back with the rebounds on the defensive side. My touch was not what it should be tonight, from the free-throw line obviously and from the field. But, you got to move on, figure it out and respond. That's why I love this game so much because you're only as good as your last game. So, this one stings a little bit, but wake up tomorrow and go back to work and get another one Friday."
