NFL news, live updates: Steelers release Aaron Rodgers after he delivers rant on Mike McCarthy; 49ers QB Brock Purdy dealing with ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Week 12 of the NFL season kicks off with a rare Wednesday matchup between the Green Bay Packers and Los Angeles Rams, prepping us for a full Thanksgiving feast of football the following day, headlined by an MVP showcase between the Buffalo Bills and Kansas City Chiefs on Thursday night.

Ahead of that matchup, the Steelers made headlines by reportedly benching veteran quarterback and former MVP Aaron Rodgers in favor of backup Mason Rudolph for Week 12 after recent struggles on offense.

This storyline then reached a fever pitch on Wednesday when Rodgers appeared on "The Pat McAfee Show" and went scorched-earth on a rant about head coach Mike McCarthy. Rodgers accused McCarthy of repeatedly falling asleep in meetings and ending a team breakdown with "1-2-3 Let's go Packers."

Shortly after his appearance on the show, the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette's Gerry Dulac reported that Rodgers was being released. The quarterback had said on the show that he was "not going back to Pittsburgh." He was officially waived on Wednesday afternoon's transaction wire.

We're watching plenty of injuries ahead of the action, including Las Vegas Raiders All-Pro tight end Brock Bowers, who missed Week 11 with an ankle injury, though he was reportedly pushing to play in that matchup. The Kansas City Chiefs have already ruled out running back Kenneth Walker III for his third consecutive game with a groin injury, giving rookie Emmett Johnson the nod for a Thanksgiving contest with the Buffalo Bills.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for the latest NFL news, injury updates and more coming out of Week 11.

1. New York Jets (Trade with Tennessee Titans) — Drew Mestemaker, QB, Oklahoma State

In this deal, the Jets keep the No. 5 overall pick and package the Nos. 4 and 15 picks and a 2028 second-rounder to move up and tab Mestemaker as their QB of the future. Mestemaker has solid size (6-foot-3, 215 pounds) with a good but not overwhelming arm. He plays in a friendly offense that he maximizes with his aggressiveness. He's a loose athlete with a knack for changing arm slots and the arc on his throws. He's had some inconsistent play over the last month or so as his aggression leads to too many dicey decisions. Yet he's the exact type of gunslinger who would be fun to pair with the Jets' assortment of talented young skill players.
