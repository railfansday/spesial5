Broncos, under Bo Nix, are much better at home than on the road

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Lost in the ongoing drama in Pittsburgh is that they play the 8-2 Broncos today. Lost in that is that the Broncos, with Bo Nix at quarterback, are a much better team at home than they are on the road.

After playing 23 games in Denver, Nix has a 20-3 record with 43 touchdown passes and averaging 242.6 passing yards per game. Playing 23 games elsewhere, the Broncos are 13-10, with Nix throwing 27 touchdowns and averaging 195.9 passing yards per game.

The Steelers, in contrast, are 4-0 at home this year and 2-4 on the road.

With the forecast for the game similar to the conditions that played out in Buffalo on Thursday night, that could even things out, from a talent standpoint. And it could give the Steelers a chance to rebound from two days of total dysfunction with a season-altering win.

However it goes, the pressure is on Steelers coach Mike McCarthy. The best way to turn the page is to win. A loss (particularly an ugly one) will give even more credence to the things Aaron Rodgers said publicly on Wednesday.
