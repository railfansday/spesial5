Raiders vs. Broncos odds: Opening lines for Week 11 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Las Vegas Raiders have lost six out of their last seven games. Mendoza's first start didn't go as planned, and the Raiders lost 21-13 to the Seattle Seahawks.

Now the Raiders face the Denver Broncos on the road. It will be another challenge for Klint Kubiak and his young quarterback. The Broncos are one of the league's better defenses and are dominant on the defensive line. At 4-6, they will be pushing to keep their playoff dreams alive.

Fanduel Sportsbook posted odds for all the games of the 1oth week of the season. The Raiders are 9.5-point underdogs against the Denver Broncos, with an over/under (O/U) of 40.5.

With teams on a bye in Week 11, plenty of NFL action remains. FanDuel lists the Kansas City Chiefs as the biggest favorites in the upcoming games, at -15.5 over the Arizona Cardinals.
