Patriots LT Will Campbell provides update on QB Drake Maye's condition

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

New England Patriots left tackle Will Campbell provided an update on quarterback Drake Maye, as the team continues to monitor the star signal-caller's recovery following the leg injury that he sustained in Week 10 against the Detroit Lions.

Speaking to the media on Monday, Campbell expressed confidence that Maye will do all that is necessary to put himself in position to return, per the Boston Herald's Doug Kyed.

"I think he's very upbeat, very positive," Campbell said. "I know he's going to work his tail off to get back whenever he can, and we'll see what happens."

Maye fractured his fibula against Detroit, leaving the game after being sacked by Lions star edge rusher Aidan Hutchinson. The injury is expected to keep Maye sidelined for at least six weeks.

The sack occurred when Hutchinson attacked Maye from the blindside, wrapping up the third-year quarterback from his waist for the highlight play after getting past Campbell in the pass rush. Despite the play coming at the expense of Campbell's protective efforts, the Patriots' young tackle has enjoyed an improved season in 2026, establishing himself as an important part of New England's offensive line.

The Patriots remain hopeful that Maye might be able to return before the season concludes. The team will continue monitoring his rehabilitation with seven weeks remaining in the year and will determine whether he will be able to return in time to help New England wage a potential postseason run.
