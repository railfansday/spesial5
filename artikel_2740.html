Eagles-Commanders somehow breaks 19 million viewers

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

When George Costanza pitched the show about nothing to NBC president Russell Dalrymple, Dalrymple asked, “Well, why am I watching it?”

More than 30 years later, that sentiment still applies. As evidenced by the fact that, per Nielsen, 19.015 million (on average) watched Sunday night’s snoozefest between the Eagles and Commanders on the network that Dalrymple once ran.

It’s an amazing number, given that it was the 2-5 Eagles against the 1-4-1 Commanders. The game itself wasn’t particularly compelling, and it followed afternoon windows that culminated in a down-to-the-wire showdown between the Chiefs and the Broncos.

The league could have flexed the game out of the evening window. It chose not to. In the end, the not-ready-for-prime-time game managed to attract far more viewers than anyone would have expected.

It helped that there wasn’t a 9:30 a.m. ET game in Week 8. Fourteen straight hours of football is a lot, even for the most ardent fan.

Then again, Commanders-Eagles was a lot. Even for the most ardent fan. And more than 19 million nevertheless watched the game.
