Robert Griffin III wants unbeaten team to be ranked No. 1 in College Football rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Robert Griffin III has said that he wants the Utah Utes to be ranked No. 1 in college football after they secured their ninth win of the season.

In what was a game against BYU, the biggest rivals of the Utes, they came out on top with a 28-20 win to move to a 9-0 record, the best in college football.

They were ranked third going into the game against BYU, who had a 7-1 record before the matchup. However, Robert Griffin III wants that to change after their repeated brilliance this season.

"Utah should be the #1 team in the country," the former Heisman trophy winner wrote.

He confirmed his Top 5 in a YouTube video, where Griffin III ranked Utah and UCLA, the two unbeaten teams in the Top 2. Georgia, Notre Dame and Miami rounded out his Top 5.

"Utah and UCLA should #1 and #2 in the rankings because no Power 4 team with a loss should be ranked higher than an undefeated P4 team. Winning matters more than who you lost to or conference bias," wrote RG3 in another post.

AP placed Utah No. 2 in their official standings. This is their best position and with a few more games left to play, the program could also claim the top spot, given all the problems their counterparts have faced.

One of the standout stat for the Utes has been their first-quarter performance. They have outscored opponents 103-9 in the first quarter, which shows how quick they have been to start.

Led by quarterback Devon Dampier, their offence has been consistent alongside their defence. Utah will be hoping to maintain their winning record and claim the first spot in the rankings, something they have never achieved in their history.
