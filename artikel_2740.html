Jerry Jones on Mike McCarthy: "He was outstanding as the head coach"

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The recent remarks from former Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers regarding Steelers coach Mike McCarthy have raised plenty of questions, posed to plenty of people.

After Thursday’s 39-38 loss to the Eagles, Cowboys owner and G.M. Jerry Jones was asked about McCarthy, who coached Jones’s team for five seasons, from 2020 through 2024.

“I offered him to stay at least another year when he left,” Jones told reporters. “And so I thought he was outstanding as the head coach and had a great command of, frankly, quarterback, offense. Can’t say enough good things about him. He’s, frankly, one of the finest people that around football that I’ve been around. And I can’t say enough about him. I never had the type of thing that is being said about him. I never had that in consideration in any of my time with him, extending his contract. Any of that, that was never on the table. And we never had an issue within the organization.”

That conflicts, obviously, with the things Rodgers said on Wednesday.

Regardless, Jones no longer employs McCarthy. While Jones possibly chose to take the high road, a former employer vouching for a coach is fundamentally different than his current employer vouching for him.

McCarthy took the Cowboys to the playoffs three times in five years, advancing to the divisional round in 2022. The Cowboys failed to qualify for the postseason in the first year after McCarthy left. Thursday’s loss to the Eagles makes it harder for them to get there the second post-McCarthy season.
