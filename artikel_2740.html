Ryan Conwell Proves Heat Found Gold In Second-Round Pick

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Ryan Conwell just dropped 20 points on the Cleveland Cavaliers, and that performance changes the whole conversation about Miami's backcourt. This rookie is not just filling time; he is making plays when the team needs them most. Sun Sentinel's Ira Winderman notes that Conwell has emerged as a valuable second-round selection for the franchise.

The numbers back up the hype from Friday night. Conwell finished with five rebounds while shooting 6-for-12 from the field and 4-for-9 from deep against the Cavs, per All U Can Heat. His last four games show a player on the rise, averaging 11 points and hitting 40 percent of his threes. The Heat went 3-1 during this specific stretch, proving the rookie's impact on the win column.

Heat fans know how thin the roster gets when injuries pile up. Pelle Larsson and Klay Thompson sat out the Friday night victory, leaving a massive hole in the lineup. Conwell stepped in and logged 33 minutes, playing more than any Heat player aside from Bam Adebayo and Davion Mitchell, according to All U Can Heat. That heavy workload shows exactly how much the coaching staff trusts him right now.

But there is a clear difference between Conwell and other options. Sun Sentinel reporter Ira Winderman noted that comparing Conwell to Kasparas Jakucionis is somewhat apples to oranges. Jakucionis is more of a point guard, while Conwell operates as an undersized scorer who can create his own shot. This distinction matters because Miami needs someone who can score when the defense collapses.

Even with a strong showing, the team faces a tough reality about its backcourt. Ira Winderman highlighted that insufficient depth at the point guard spot continues to be a major concern for Miami. During Friday's matchup, Erik Spoelstra faced significant difficulty resting Davion Mitchell, while Dru Smith contributed little in his short appearance. The truth is that the Heat likely require point guard depth more than in any other area.

The story of how Conwell landed in Miami adds another layer to his value. Per Sun Sentinel, he resulted from a compensatory second-round pick the Heat obtained from the Hornets because they were not informed about the gambling investigation depth concerning Terry Rozier before the trade. That pick turned into a player who arrived exactly when needed.

Some fans might compare his future value to the first-round pick the Hornets still hold from the Rozier trade, which is top-14 protected in June and otherwise unprotected in 2028, according to reports. That potential future asset highlights just how valuable a current contributor like Conwell can be. A more fitting comparison for Ryan could be whatever asset the Hornets eventually acquire from that first-round selection.
