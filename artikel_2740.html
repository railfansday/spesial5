Bears-Lions thriller delivers fantasy fireworks as Jahmyr Gibbs posts season-best 45.7 points

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Bears-Lions 39-35 Thanksgiving thriller not only had major NFC North implications but also huge fantasy ramifications. While Detroit lost the game, Jahmyr Gibbs will likely lead several fantasy managers to victories in Week 12.

The star running back had 252 total yards and three touchdowns, posting 45.70 fantasy points in half-PPR scoring, which is the highest total by any player this season. It's also his second 40-plus-point game in 2026 as he continues to be the best player in fantasy.

Wide receiver Amon-Ra St. Brown caught seven balls for 146 yards (18.10 fantasy points) and tight end Sam LaPorta had a touchdown catch (12.40 points) as both posted solid lines.

On the other side, Chicago was paced by D'Andre Swift's 25.70 fantasy points, which included his first rushing touchdown since Week 8. He was also busy as a receiver, catching seven balls for 97 yards. Meanwhile, Kyle Monangai's 15.40 points came thanks to two short TD runs, but he struggled to get going otherwise, rushing eight times for 15 yards.

How about the QB-WR duo who connected for the game-winning touchdown? Well, Caleb Williams had his best fantasy day since Week 1, finishing with 25.30 points, while Rome Odunze easily had his best game of the year with 23.70 points. His nine targets also tied a season high, and he could be more of a focal point if TE Colston Loveland misses time after leaving the game early with a knee injury.

Not all the big names got home in the 74-point affair. Luther Burden III finished with 45 yards on four touches for six points. Jared Goff (16.82 points) and Jameson Williams (11 points) delivered decent stat lines, but their managers would have hoped for more with the fireworks show in Detroit.

These two teams will meet again in Week 17, which coincides with fantasy title matchups. Get your popcorn ready!
