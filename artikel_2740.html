Jaylen Brown on facing Celtics: 'I didn't have any emotion. I was locked in'

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Jaylen Brown finished the game with four fouls, which he hit by the 10:40 mark in the third quarter after a reaching call. "I'm a bit frustrated, honestly," Brown said. On the surface, it appeared that the emotions of this matchup had gotten to Brown, but he attempted to shut that idea down postgame. "I didn't have any emotion. I was locked in. I was focused, I was ready to go," Brown said. "Foul trouble kind of took me out of my rhythm a little bit. I had to sit for a while, and we didn't execute as well as they did."
