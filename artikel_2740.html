John Mateer reveals where Jason Witten has helped him the most

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Oklahoma Sooners hope to continue their winning streak on Saturday as they take on the Texas A&M Aggies in their final home game of the season. The Sooners are sitting at a disappointing 6-4 after having College Football Playoff and national championship aspirations before the season.

The offense has played the biggest role in the disappointing season. After making some improvement last season, the offense fell back to being one of the worst units in college football. Oklahoma ranks No. 116 in total offense, No. 117 in rushing offense, No. 81 in passing offense and No. 107 in scoring offense. That resulted in offensive coordinator Ben Arbuckle being let go. Jason Witten has been the interim offensive coordinator and the offense has played much more efficient the last two weeks, especially on the ground, averaging 166 yards per game.

Quarterback John Mateer said Witten has helped him over the last two games.

"I think he's just encouraged me to trust myself and to trust the guys around me and play free and have fun with the game," Mateer said via SoonerScoop. "He says that a lot, helping us enjoy it, and at the end of the day it's a kids' game and you play it together. I think you can see that. We are smiling a lot. We're enjoying it. Now, we're winning and doing really good things on offense, so it's obviously easier to smile, but it's a lot of fun. I think that he trusts my ability, and that helps me trust my ability."

Witten's impact has been noticeable in the way Oklahoma has approached the game over the past two weeks. The offense is playing with more confidence, Mateer is becoming a bigger part of the rushing attack, and the Sooners have shown more balance than they did earlier in the season. While two games do not erase the struggles that came before, the recent progress gives the offense something to build on as the schedule reaches its final stretch.
