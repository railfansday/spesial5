UFC Vegas 124: Johnny Walker stuns Waldo Cortes-Acosta for second straight win at heavyweight

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Johnny Walker is looking mighty comfortable at heavyweight.

The UFC veteran notched his second straight win since moving up from his old home of light heavyweight, taking out Waldo Cortes-Acosta via third-round KO on Saturday at UFC Vegas 124. The end came suddenly, with Walker leveling Cortes-Acosta with a hard right.
