Celtics blow 17-point lead to Nuggets, lose 105-101

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Celtics opened up a four-game road trip in Denver on Wednesday. While, it started well, it did not end well as Boston lost the game 105-101. There was a no call on what very easily could have been an illegal screen and then a technical foul on Jayson Tatum that was also a questionable call. It was a tough one for the C's to lose.

The Celtics started their usuals of Derrick White, Jordan Walsh, Paul George, Jayson Tatum and Neemias Queta. They had everyone active for the game. Denver also started their usuals of Jamal Murray, Christian Braun, Cam Johnson, Aaron Gordon and Nikola Jokic. Julian Strawther missed the game for Denver.

The Celtics first subs were Baylor Scheierman and Mitchell Robinson into the game. Joe Mazzulla likes to sub his bigs early so that was not a surprise, Walsh coming out early was notable because Joe probably knows that Walsh and Robinson sharing the floor has been a disaster for the Celtics.

Entering Wednesday's game, Boston was -15.3 per 100 possessions in 96 minutes with Robinson and Walsh sharing the court.

The Nuggets lost their challenge in the first quarter, on an offensive call on Trevon Brazile.

The Celtics led at the end of the first quarter, 30-24. Jayson Tatum lead the way with 14 points and 4 rebounds. Meanwhile, Payton Pritchard had 6 points off of the bench.

Mazzulla had a quick hook in the 2nd quarter, he pulled Luka Garza after he gave up an offensive rebound and Baylor Scheierman after he airmailed a floater. We also saw rookie Chris Cenac Jr. in the early stages of the 2nd quarter.

The Celtics lead ballooned to 15 in the 2nd quarter as David Adelman called a timeout with 4:44 in the quarter with the Nuggets down 49-34.

Boston went into the locker room with a 12 point lead, 61-49. Jayson Tatum was the leading scorer with 20 points and 6 rebounds while Payton Pritchard chipped in 11 and Derrick White chipped in 10.

Hugo Gonzalez replaced Jordan Walsh in the starting five to start the 2nd half. Walsh was pulled 4:22 into the first half and did not get back in the game.

It was an ice cold start to the quarter for the Celtics.

They were also sloppy with the ball, the Celtics went 6 possessions in a row with turnovers as Denver cut the Celtics lead to 2 and Mazzulla called a timeout.

Boston turned it over out of the timeout and Denver tied the game and then the Celtics turned it over with an offensive foul as I was typing out about the last turnover.

It was a nightmare quarter for the Celtics, however, the walked out of it tied at 80. They were outscored by 12, 31-19 as they had 9 turnovers in the quarter and Denver had 12 points off of those turnovers.

DeMar DeRozan started the 4th quarter out hot, he had 6 points and drew 4 Celtics fouls to have Denver in the bonus when Nikola Jokic and Jamal Murray returned to the game. However, the Celtics held a 4 point lead when David Adelman called a timeout with 7:43 to go in the 4th quarter.

It was a night to forget for Paul George, who fouled out with 6:14 to go in the game after playing 22:31 and scoring 10 points. The last foul was a silly one as he missed a shot and went for the ball when Jamal Murray clearly had the ball.

The fourth quarter was back and forth but a clutch jumper from Jamal Murray put Denver up 1 with 1:32 to go.

Neemias Queta got an offensive rebound to retake the lead for Boston.

Derrick White, meanwhile, fouled DeMar DeRozan on two possessions late in the game.

After White missed a wide open three that would have put the Celtics in front, Jamal Murray hit a shot to put the Nuggets up 3. Then Jayson Tatum picked up a technical foul and the Nuggets went up 4. That was that, the Celtics lost the game 105-101.

A brutal loss for Boston. Just 4 points in the 2nd half for Tatum to his 5 turnovers. He finished 24 points for the game, Neemias Queta had a double-double with 17 points and 10 rebounds and Chris Cenac Jr. was in the closing lineup for Joe Mazzulla.

The C's shot 41% from the field and 25% from three while Denver shot 44% from the field and 23% from three. The Celtics next game is Saturday in Sacramento at 10pm/EST.
