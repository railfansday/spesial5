Thursday Night Football: Commanders lead Giants 10-7 at halftime

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Giants and Commanders played a first half fitting the weather. On a cold, rainy night, the Commanders lead the Giants 10-7 at halftime.

Ben Sinnott caught a 1-yard touchdown pass from Jayden Daniels with 5 seconds remaining in the first half. It completed a nine-play, 67-yard drive.

Washington also had an 11-play, 49-yard drive that ended with Drew Stevens’ 46-yard field goal.

Daniels was 11-of-15 for 112 yards and a touchdown in the first half. Rachaad White had six carries for 20 yards, and Stefon Diggs caught two passes for 34 yards.

The Giants outgained the Commanders 152 to 143 and had 12 first downs and a 17:18 to 12:42 advantage in time of possession.

Cam Skattebo ran for a 1-yard touchdown with 2:35 remaining in the second quarter, giving New York its first lead at 7-3. It capped a 16-play, 72-yard drive.

The Giants punted after reaching the Washington 37 on their first drive, and Dominic Zvada missed a 33-yard field goal after the Giants stalled at the Washington 15 on the second drive.

Skattebo had 35 yards and a touchdown on eight carries and two catches for 37 yards. J.J. McCarthy went 12-of-19 for 92 yards and rushed for 30 yards on four carries. Isaiah Likely had five catches for 39 yards.

Commanders tight end Chig Okonkwo (back) is questionable to return.
