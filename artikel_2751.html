Chiefs overwhelm Cardinals, move to 8-2 with 34-7 victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

With a big matchup against the Bills on tap for Thanksgiving night, the Chiefs might have looked past the Cardinals, who were starting their fourth QB in four weeks.

But that’s not at all what Kansas City did, instead getting a dominant 34-7 victory over Arizona for the team’s eighth win of the season.

The Chiefs scored on each of their five first-half drives, putting up three touchdowns and a pair of Harrison Butker field goals to go into halftime with a 27-7 lead.

Patrick Mahomes’ 18-yard touchdown pass to Xavier Worthy early in the fourth quarter put the game away with a 27-point lead.

That was also Mahomes’ last pass, as Justin Fields came in to play the rest of the game.

Mahomes finished the contest 27-of-39 for 324 yards with three touchdowns and no interceptions. It’s the first time this season Mahomes has thrown for at least three touchdowns with no interceptions in consecutive games.

Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce moved past Rob Gronkowski for No. 3 all-time in touchdown receptions by a tight end, trailing only Antonio Gates and Tony Gonzalez. He finished the contest with six catches for 74 yards with a pair of touchdowns.

Once again starting in place of the injured Kenneth Walker, rookie Emmett Johnson had another strong performance with 105 yards rushing on 23 carries with a touchdown. He also caught four passes for 38 yards.

On the other side, quarterback Josh Johnson was able to get a little something going midway through the first half with a 10-play touchdown drive. But other than that, the Cardinals’ offense was overmatched and overwhelmed throughout the afternoon. Johnson finished 18-of-31 for 155 yards with a touchdown. Arizona did not have much of a running game, with Bam Knight gaining 35 yards on seven carries and Tyler Allgeier rushing for 23 yards on seven carries.

Trey McBride was limited to 54 yards on nine catches.

Now at 8-2, the Chiefs will have a quick turnaround as they’ll play the Bills in Buffalo on Thursday.

The 3-8 Cardinals will try to break their long home losing streak against the Commanders next Sunday afternoon.
