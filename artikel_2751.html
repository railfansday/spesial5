Open Thread: Giving thanks for Carter Bryant

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Today carter Bryant turns 21. Now in his second year, Bryant is finding his place on a Spurs team determined to return to the Finals. He's developing his three-point shot, taking more catch-and-shoot opportunities while on the floor.

While emerging as a three-point threat, he continues to play aggressive defense, often taking on some high-profile physical opponents. In addition to his 3-and-D growth, CB displays brutal slam dunk abilities.

This is a volatile era for Bryant as he seeks a larger role on the team. To make space and add minutes, Carter begun coming off the bench before Harrison Barnes and on occasion even Keldon Johnson.

This year, Bryant celebrates his birthday on Thanksgiving. Nothing like having a holiday overshadow your birthday. But like everything else involving Carter Bryant, he's quietly raising his profile without the need for a spotlight.

Happy birthday, Carter. Eat, drink (legally), and be merry, for tomorrow you take on the Los Angeles Lakers, one of the Spurs oldest rivals and the only team whose record mirrors the Spurs. There is a trip to the NBA Cup elimination rounds on the line, and this is now a must-win game if the Spurs want to keep their Cup dreams alive.
