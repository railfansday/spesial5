Seahawks LT Charles Cross practices for the first time since Week 7

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Seahawks had their starting left tackle back at practice on Thursday.

Charles Cross missed the last two games with an ankle injury, but he was able to work as a limited participant in his first practice since being injured in Week 7. The Seahawks will issue injury designations for their game in Las Vegas on Friday.

Wide receiver Rashid Shaheed (knee) also missed the last two games, but he has been a full participant in both practices this week. Wide receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba was also a full participant after resting on Wednesday.

Guard Quenton Nelson (knee), quarterback Sam Darnold (ribs, quad), defensive tackle Brandon Pili (back), defensive tackle Jarran Reed (personal), and defensive tackle Leonard Williams (stinger) missed practice for the second straight day. Guard Anthony Bradford (knee), linebacker Derick Hall (back), quarterback Drew Lock (right elbow, oblique), linebacker Uchenna Nwosu (ankle), linebacker Patrick O’Connell (toe), and linebacker Connor O’Toole (foot) remained limited participants.
