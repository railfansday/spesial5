Majority of Bills fans predict a win for Buffalo vs. Chiefs on Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

A major test awaits the Buffalo Bills this evening. Few games carry the weight like any matchup with the Kansas City Chiefs. Playing in primetime on Thanksgiving night only adds to the drama. It's a true feast for the eyes and a diabolical thief of one's resting heart rate.

Bills Mafia is well aware of the history between quarterbacks Josh Allen and Patrick Mahomes. Having played 10 times to this point between the regular season and playoffs, the all-time series is split down the middle between the two. Allen is 5-1 against Mahomes and the Chiefs in the regular season, while Mahomes is 4-0 in the postseason.

By and large, it's likely that Allen and Mahomes play key roles in the outcome of tonight's game. However, weather is going to be a factor — and that may play very well into an expanded rushing attack.

Bills fans know that may call for an enhanced featured role for running back James Cook III. It may also mean that short passes to running backs Ty Johnson and Ray Davis, as well wide receiver Khalil Shakir are the go-to for Allen while trying to combat the rain and wind.

Though some see the expected foul weather as a detriment, it may actually benefit the Bills. Buffalo is a powerful, run-first offense. Part of that game plan of course involves Allen as a rusher. Last weekend, Allen didn't attempt a single carry, but that shouldn't repeat against Kansas City.

It's anyone's guess as to how this game plays out.

Will the Bills success against the Chiefs with Brady now in charge, and Buffalo claiming regular-season bragging right over Kansas City? We asked Rumblers to weigh in on just that, and 78% believe the Bills will defeat the Chiefs in Week 12.

There's a lot at stake on Thanksgiving night. If Buffalo wins and maintains first place in the AFC playoff race, they'd claim a major head-to-head advantage over their biggest elimination game rivals. That would improve their chances of claiming the one seed and a bye week to start the postseason.

Something's run afoul, Rumblers. The 9-1 Bills, the team currently holding the top spot in the AFC playoff race, and one that's won six games in a row… well, they've somehow convinced a segment of Bills Mafia that things aren't headed in the right direction.

Buffalo has held their last four opponents to a combined nine field goals. Total. Zero touchdowns. The same defense that began the season ranked last in points allowed per game has surrendered just one touchdown since the bye week, that being a ground score early in action against running back Derrick Henry and the Baltimore Ravens.

Despite this, just 76% of fans polled gave their vote of confidence in the current direction of the Bills under head coach Joe Brady, defensive coordinator Jim Leonhard, and president of football operations/general manager Brandon Beane. Just? Yes, it's appropriate, considering the continued drop in fan confidence from a high of 94% two weeks ago, to 89% last week, and now seeing a further 13% loss in fan confidence

Expect a major jump in either direction after Buffalo's game against the Kansas City Chiefs this evening.

Let's quickly check in on the fan vote over at Arrowhead Pride. Just 62% of fans think the Chiefs will defeat the Bills on Thanksgiving night. However, right now, 91% of voters there are confident in the current direction of the team.

One of this week's national polls asked voters to pick the Thanksgiving game (Eve and Day) they believe will be the most entertaining. Would you be surprised to learn that Bills-Chiefs leads the way with 49% of the total vote?

The only other game to garner a fair bit of the vote was Detroit Lions vs. Chicago Bears (40%). However, with the recent Aaron Rodgers-Mike McCarthy soap opera, that Friday game between the Pittsburgh Steelers and Denver Broncos may pick up some viewers.
