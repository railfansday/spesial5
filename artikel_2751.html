Brown out again with hamstring injury for Sixers' last NBA Cup group game

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Brown out again with hamstring injury for Sixers' last NBA Cup group game originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

Jaylen Brown is on the sidelines again with a hamstring injury.

The Sixers on Thursday night listed Brown as out because of "left hamstring injury management" for their NBA Cup game Friday in Indiana against the Pacers. Joel Embiid (illness) was listed as questionable after missing the last two games.

Brown's hamstring has been problematic of late. He missed wins last week over the Magic and Bucks with a left hamstring strain. The star forward then returned Monday night and scored 20 points in the Sixers' loss to the Trail Blazers. He also played Wednesday in the team's victory against the Heat, but Brown was ruled out at halftime with an issue the Sixers described as "left hamstring tightness."

"He'll get some imaging and we'll get the update tomorrow," Sixers head coach Nick Nurse said after the game.

Through his first 16 appearances as a Sixer, Brown has averaged 21.4 points, 6.0 rebounds and 3.4 assists.

The 15-4 Sixers would win Eastern Conference Group B and advance to the NBA Cup quarterfinals for the first time with a win over the 6-12 Pacers. They'd be eliminated from NBA Cup play with a loss, according to the NBA's rundown of all potential Cup group scenarios.
