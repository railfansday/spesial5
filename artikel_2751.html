Consumer confidence shoots up after Democrats' midterm victories — though inflation worries persist

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Consumers grew significantly more optimistic in November in the wake of the midterm elections, with a growing number of Americans now seeing improvement in the labor market and expecting inflation to cool.

The Conference Board survey released Tuesday showed improvement across the board, with the headline confidence index jumping to 103.3, up 11.4 points from October and much better than the Dow Jones consensus forecast for 92.2.

Confidence among Democrats and independent voters improved sharply, the Conference Board said, while confidence among Republican respondents fell slightly. Still, confidence among Republicans remained the highest among the three groups.

While respondents "continued to express frustration with high prices and rising costs," they also "cited hope for the general economy," said Fergus McCormick, the board's senior economist for global indicators.

The survey comes with the Federal Reserve continuing to try to bring down inflation, which has run above the central bank's 2% target for some 5½ years. The Fed is expected to raise its benchmark interest rate by a quarter percentage point when it meets again in two weeks.

Despite the lingering worries about the cost of living — a centerpiece issue during the midterms — the survey's present situation and expectations indexes both posted strong gains. Inflation expectations for 12 months from now fell for the second straight month but remained high at 5.7%.

The survey's closely watched gap between jobs considered "plentiful" versus "hard to get" widened to 12.4 percentage points. The "plentiful" side rose to 30.8% from 26.2% while "hard to get" fell to 18.4%, down 3 percentage points.

The survey results are the second in the past week to reveal that Americans are growing more optimistic about the economy. The University of Michigan's November survey, released Friday, showed a 7.3% increase since October, with a similar partisan split.
