Rob Gronkowski hits signature Gronk Spike celebration during Patriots Hall of Fame induction ceremony

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

After becoming the latest player to be inducted into the New England Patriots Hall of Fame, Rob Gronkowski celebrated the only way he knows how. During a halftime ceremony, the legendary Patriots tight end ended his speech by doing his signature touchdown celebration, the Gronk Spike.

Gronkowski told the Gillette Stadium crowd that he wanted to do "one final Gronk Spike" before making the move.

"Watch out!" he said. "This is gonna be dangerous."

The induction itself took place Saturday, with Gronkowski being given a red jacket to represent his entrance into the Hall. On Sunday, with the Patriots hosting the Green Bay Packers at Gillette Stadium, Gronkowski was honored again in front of the fans.

Gronkowski was joined by a number of other members of the Hall of Fame at both ceremonies. Longtime teammate Tom Brady — who was inducted into the Patriots' HOF in 2024 after owner Robert Kraft waived the typical waiting period — was at Saturday's ceremony, but was in the booth calling the game as an analyst for Fox on Sunday.

Former Patriots head coach Bill Belichick, who had high praise for Gronkowski after UNC beat UConn in Connecticut, was also reportedly in the building. Rather than heading back to Chapel Hill, Belichick reportedly drove from Storrs to Foxborough on Saturday night to join Gronkowski at a steakhouse after the ceremony, per MassLive.

Before the game, the Patriots honored the 10-year anniversary of Super Bowl LI, where New England beat the Atlanta Falcons after erasing a 25-point deficit in the fourth quarter to win the game in overtime.

Sunday's game ended with the Packers beating New England 45-27.
