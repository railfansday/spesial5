Maxx Crosby drops NSFW reason why Raiders still believe in AFC playoff run this season

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

When the Raiders traded Kirk Cousins to the Vikings during the NFL trade deadline, the general consensus was that they were punting their season away — even though they were 4-5.

Raiders star defensive end Maxx Crosby would tell you otherwise.

Maxx Crosby walking off the field following their loss to the Seahawks on Sunday. Getty Images

Crosby spoke about the Raiders' decision to move on from Cousins and make way for rookie quarterback Fernando Mendoza on his podcast, "The Rush." He said the team's mindset has not changed.

"We don't look at it as 'Oh, yeah, we're playing for the future.' No, we're playing for now. And we're playing to win now," Crosby said. "That's all you can do. I know the locker room and everybody in that building feels the same way, and we believe in him, and we're gonna rock with him."

Crosby said the Raiders' defense will need to help Mendoza as much as it can, while the offense needs to step up to help him "play good ball."

In Mendoza's first NFL start on Sunday, the Raiders scored on their first offensive possession. Mendoza showed why the team selected him No. 1 overall in the 2026 NFL Draft. He dropped back to pass but decided to keep the ball and scramble up the middle for a 10-yard rushing touchdown.

Fernando Mendoza rushes up the field for a 10-yard touchdown run against the Seahawks on Sunday. AP Photo/John Locher

Coincidentally, the scramble featured shades of his fourth-quarter run in the national title game against Miami in January.

It was the only score the Raiders rookie had in their 21-13 loss to Seattle, but Mendoza showed a lot of promise, completing 17 of 29 passes for 143 yards.

Raiders quarterback Fernando Mendoza walking off the field following Sunday's loss to the Seahawks. AP Photo/John Locher

Crosby knows Mendoza has a chance to be special and also knows what he's capable of after going up against him during practice when Mendoza was the Raiders' scout team quarterback.

"He's a robot. He does everything the right way. He's a great f–king kid," Crosby said. "He's extremely talented, he's competitive, he's going to be a great quarterback for a long time."
