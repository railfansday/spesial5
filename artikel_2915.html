Former All-Star has created NBA’s biggest ‘f–king mess’ — and it just keeps getting worse

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Situations don't get much uglier than Dejounte Murray's in New Orleans.

The Pelicans guard wants a trade from the franchise, and his relationship with the organization appears beyond repair.

But worst of all, opposing teams reportedly aren't biting, asking for draft compensation just to take him off New Orleans' hands in a trade.

"What a f–king mess," one NBA executive told Yahoo Sports' Kelly Iko this week. "No one wants to touch him."

Dejounte Murray #5 of the New Orleans Pelicans reacts to a call during a game against the Denver Nuggets in New Orleans, Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026. Getty Images

It's a quick fall for the former All-Star, whom the Pelicans acquired from the Hawks in a 2024 blockbuster deal for a package that included Dyson Daniels and two first-round picks.

Now, just over two years later, the Pelicans could be forced to give up even more assets to get rid of him.

Two opposing team officials told Sports Illustrated's Chris Mannix they would demand a first-round pick to take on Murray's contract, which pays him $32.8 million this season and includes a $30.8 million player option for 2027-28.

Murray, 30, has appeared in just 54 games since arriving in New Orleans, having suffered a broken hand and a torn Achilles.

Dejounte Murray of the New Orleans Pelicans reacts during a game against the Golden State Warriors in San Francisco, Monday, Nov. 9, 2026. Getty Images

He's averaging 13.4 points and 4.4 assists through nine games this season, shooting just 29.6 percent from beyond the arc.

But his struggles on the court are only part of the problem.

Murray took to social media to lash out at critics earlier this month, suggesting someone within the organization was responsible for negative posts about him.

Dejounte Murray of the New Orleans Pelicans handles the ball against the LA Clippers on Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026, at Intuit Dome in Inglewood, Calif. NBAE via Getty Images

"The sabotage is real!" he wrote in a since-deleted post.

In another since-deleted tirade, Murray went after a Pelicans fan account that highlighted his mistakes during a blowout loss to the Nets.

"You have no f–king clue what we dealing with," Murray, who hasn't played since Nov. 11, wrote on the platform.

ESPN's Brian Windhorst subsequently claimed the Pelicans had sent Murray home following his trade request, while suggesting the guard had become difficult to keep around the team.

"They sent him home," Windhorst said on "The Hoop Collective" podcast.

"NEVER WAS SENT HOME," he wrote in the comments of a Bleacher Report Instagram post.

Murray has reportedly expressed interest in joining the Suns, with Phoenix guard Jalen Green floated as a potential trade piece.

But according to The Athletic's Sam Amick on Friday, recent discussions between the two teams lasted approximately five minutes and gained "zero traction."

The Suns reportedly don't want to take on Murray's contract without getting back draft compensation.

Iko added that Murray's unhappiness dates back to last season and was exacerbated by the Pelicans' drafting of young point guard Jeremiah Fears in 2025.

While the organization is seemingly ready to move forward without him, Murray could find himself stuck in New Orleans for the foreseeable future.
