Why Micron Stock Finally Popped Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

By Rich Smith – Nov 19, 2026 at 10:03AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Micron Technology ›

I guess I've been predicting this for a few days now, but for Micron (MU +5.98%) investors, it must still come as a relief: After five straight days of declines, shares of the NAND and DRAM memory chip specialist finally popped on Thursday, rising 6.5% through 9:45 a.m. ET.

And once again, they have Lynx Equity to thank for it.

You may recall Lynx Equity providing the catalyst that sent Micron stock roaring higher earlier this month? Back then, analyst KC Rajkumar tried to restore momentum to the AI trade when he reported that DRAM and NAND flash memory supplies remain tight and are showing no evidence of a drop in demand.

In a note carried on StreetInsider.com this morning, Rajkumar argues that even if data center construction slows in the U.S. (and it might), this doesn't necessarily mean demand for memory chips will weaken. AI data centers aren't the only users of memory chips, for one thing. For another, tighter regulations and higher costs to build data centers might soon be offset by a wave of money going to one of the bigger AI users, Anthropic (ANT +0.00%), which begins trading as a public stock today.

Could Lynx be wrong about this? Sure it could. But as Rajkumar points out, the chance that it is wrong has been largely de-risked by a steep plunge in Micron's stock price -- which is down 17% over the past month.

Selling for just $750 as I type these words, Rajkumar predicts Micron stock will cost $1,325 per share within one year. At less than 10x trailing earnings, and with a long-term growth rate estimated north of 43% on S&P Global Market Intelligence, Rajkumar calls Micron stock "ridiculously cheap."
