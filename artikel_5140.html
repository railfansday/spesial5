Anthropic is an artificial intelligence company founded in 2021 by former OpenAI employees. It develops AI models and tools, including Claude, which helps users with writing, coding, research, and business tasks. Anthropic focuses on building advanced and safer AI systems. The company has entered the public stock market through its IPO, raising approximately $81.3 billion at $83 per share. Its shares are listed on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, starting November 19, 2026. The IPO values Anthropic at approximately $1.5 trillion, making it one of the world’s most valuable AI companies.
