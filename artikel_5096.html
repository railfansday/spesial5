Grayscale Confirms Bitcoin’s Record Bear Market

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Although Bitcoin has recently reclaimed the $60,000 price mark after seeing a sudden shift in market sentiments that triggered increases of over 6% in its price during the last day, momentum has slowed again, further stretching the prolonged volatility seen over the past months.

With the Bitcoin bear market still persisting, Grayscale has sparked discussions across the crypto market after drawing attention toward a major bearish milestone for the asset.

Earlier today, Grayscale revealed that Bitcoin's current bear market has lasted more than 411 days and is becoming the longest on record.

While this marks an historic milestone in the Bitcoin bear market, Grayscale further disclosed an interesting twist that makes the current bear market pretty different from previous times.

The source confirmed that this bear market has been relatively mild in terms of price losses despite lasting longer than previous downturns.

All through the current bear market, Bitcoin has only posted a price decrease 56%, suggesting that it has suffered considerably smaller losses than several previous bear markets.

This means that even though Bitcoin has spent more than a year in a bear market, its decline has not been as severe as those seen in earlier cycles.

To back the data, Grayscale showed charts comparing Bitcoin's performance across historical bear markets that ended in June 2011, December 2013, December 2017, November 2021, and the ongoing October 2025 cycle.

While the 2011, 2017 and 2021 bear cycles suffered massive losses, especially during the former when its indexed performance fell below 10, the current 2025 bear market broke historical pattern by enduring a longer period yet a comparatively modest decline in Bitcoin’s price.
