XRP Ledger trial settled 213,000 paid AI requests in 6,324 transactions

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

t54 Labs reported an average network fee of 0.36 drops per paid request in its closed October beta. Two November checks paid the same merchant in RLUSD, with individual requests counted off-ledger.

Two checks cashed on the XRP Ledger on Nov. 18 paid the same AI service in RLUSD. Payments company t54 Labs identified both as batches. In its closed October beta, t54 reported that bundling requests cut the average network fee to 0.36 drops of XRP per paid call.

The checks delivered 0.31 RLUSD and 0.46 RLUSD to the same ledger address. The XRPL AI Hub, run by t54 Labs, identifies the merchant as Heurist Inference Router and classifies both transfers as batches.

The first check and second check came from different payer addresses. Each had a spending ceiling of 1 RLUSD. The ledger charged 12 drops of XRP to open each check and 12 to cash it, for a total of 24 drops per pair.

A drop is one millionth of an XRP. The Hub marks payment intent as unverified in its first receipt and its second receipt. The receipts give no call counts, so the November fee per request remains unknown.

t54 Labs reported that its mainnet beta settled 213,008 paid requests across 3,162 sessions using 6,324 ledger transactions from Oct. 14 to 17. It put the average network fee at about 0.36 drops per paid request. Its benchmark was a single transaction per request at the ledger's standard minimum fee of 10 drops, which can rise under load.

In t54's beta, the payer first opens a check on the ledger with a maximum amount. A session key authorizes each charge off-ledger as the agent makes paid requests. At the end of the session, the merchant cashes the check once for the amount used. Each completed session takes two ledger transactions, so adding requests to a session spreads those two fees over more charges.

The beta began on mainnet in October with RLUSD as its only settlement asset. t54 says it is limited to onboarded merchants and registered payers, and its developer guide still describes sessions as part of internal merchant testing. The public facilitator has yet to offer batch sessions, according to t54.

An open check adds 0.2 XRP to the payer's reserve requirement on top of the regular 1 XRP account reserve. That amount remains in the account while the check is open. A payer with several checks open at once needs the extra reserve for each one. The reserve requirement falls when the check is cashed or canceled. Expiration alone leaves the check on the ledger until someone cancels it.

The RLUSD principal moves at cashing. The ceiling caps what the merchant can collect, and the payer needs enough funds when the merchant submits the check.
