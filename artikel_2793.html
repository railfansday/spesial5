The Post's Boys Athlete of the Week: Vote on the best of Nov. 2 - 7

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Welcome to Athlete of the Week voting from The Palm Beach Post and Baptist Health Orthopedic Care!

Congrats to our last winner, Ladamien Bragdon of Forest Hill football. The junior tallied 14 tackles, including two for a loss, in a 43-8 victory over John I. Leonard.

Each week, The Post will highlight the best boys and girls high school student-athlete performances in Palm Beach County. The polls will be open until noon Friday.

Nominees are accepted via email or phone, but will be selected by staff in the absence of submission.

Here are top performances from boys athletes across the most recent week of competition.

Voting will be open until Friday, Nov. 13 at noon.

Soccer: Matthew Armwood, Sr., Dwyer — Recorded a goal and two assists in a 5-1 win over Jupiter Christian

Soccer: Noah Charleston, Jr., Seminole Ridge — Tallied four goals in a 4-3 victory over John I. Leonard

Cross country: Elijah Arterburn, Sr., Atlantic Christian — Ran a 16:26.59 to take first place at the District 7-1A district championship

Swimming and Diving: Charles Howard, Sr., Jupiter — Swam a 1:36.70 to win the boys 200 yard freestyle at the state meet, helping the Warriors to a Class 4A title

Golf: Charlie Sundermeier, So., St. John Paul II Academy — Shot a 64 to place second overall in the Class 1A Region 4 championship

Golf: Samuel Friday, Jr., Jupiter — Shot a 69 to place third overall in the Class 3A Region 2 championship

Alex Peterman is a high school sports reporter for The Palm Beach Post. He can be reached at apeterman@usatodayco.com.
