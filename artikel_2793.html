Miami Dolphins news roundup: Week 11 injury report includes Aaron Brewer update

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Miami Dolphins will be without punter Bradley Pinoin on Sunday as they face the Buffalo Bills. Pinion has a right groin injury and missed practice all week before being ruled out on Friday's injury report. Miami signed punter Jack Browning to the practice squad this week and is expected to elevate him to the active roster by today's 4 p.m. Eastern deadline.

The Dolphins are also making a change at kicker this week, waiving Zane Gonzalez after he missed three kicks last week and signing kicker Daniel Carlson to the practice squad. Long snapper Tucker Addington is the lone specialist on the roster and the only one of the three who has played a game for Miami prior to this weekend's contest.

Miami also ruled out wide receiver Jared Wayne for Sunday's game. He has a quadriceps injury.

The Dolphins injury report included cornerback Jaylin Simpson (knee) and safety Dante Trader Jr. (shoulder/groin), both doubtful for the game. Cornerback Darrell Baker Jr. (knee), center Aaron Brewer (concussion), and cornerback Marcellas Dial Jr. (toe) are all questionable. Brewer, who missed last week's game with the head injury, fully participated in practice on Friday and, as long as he has no symptoms on Saturday, should be cleared to play.

The Bills side of the reports includes cornerback Christian Benford (groin) and linebacker Dorian Williams (calf) having been ruled out of the game while tight end Dalton Kincaid (thumb/ankle) is doubtful. Safety Damar Hamlin (toe), defensive tackle T.J. Sanders (defensive tackle), and tight end Jackson Hawes (shoulder) are all questionable.
