Packers plan for Micah Parsons to do 11-on-11 work this week

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Micah Parsons is set to take another step back toward the Packers lineup this week.

Parsons did individual work in his return to practice last week and head coach Matt LaFleur said on Wednesday that the team expects him to take part in 11-on-11 work during practice this week.

“That is the plan,” LaFleur said at a press conference.

Thursday will be the first day that Parsons can get involved in team work. LaFleur spoke ahead of Wednesday’s practice, which was held at a jog-through pace and LaFleur said Parsons would not participate due to an illness.

Green Bay has a bye in Week 11, which has led to their Wednesday night game against the Rams in Week 12 being circled as the likely date of Parsons’s first game since tearing his ACL last December. A successful return to team drills will make that scenario even likelier.
