Bruins Under-The-Radar Prospect Is Suddenly Dominating The AHL

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Boston Bruins have a prospect that fans should starting paying more attention to.

This is because forward Riley Duran is starting to make some noise in the AHL with the Providence Bruins.

After posting just two points in his first eight games this season with Providence, Duran has been heating up immensely since.

Duran has two goals and seven points over his last four games alone for Providence. This included him putting together a three-assist game in Providence's most recent matchup against the Charlotte Checkers. With this, the 24-year-old is showing no signs of slowing down from his hot streak right now.

If Duran continues to produce so well offensively for Providence while also maintaining his gritty style of play, it could open the door him to get a look on Boston's roster again. He last played at the NHL level during the 2024-25 season, where he had zero points, two penalty minutes, and six hits in his first two career NHL games.

Nevertheless, it is encouraging to see Duran starting to find his offense at the AHL level. In 15 games now this season for Providence, he has three goals and 11 points. This is after he had seven goals and 19 points in 72 games for Providence in 2025-26.

Nine out of Duran's 11 points this season for Providence have come in his last seven games.
