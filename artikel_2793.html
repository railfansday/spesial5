NFL Week 11 Sunday Night Football Best Player Props—Vikings Vs. 49ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

NFL Week 11 is packed with games projected to be lopsided, but Sunday Night Football features two NFC powerhouse teams in an NFL International Series battle in Mexico City, Mexico, and the spread is tight, with the San Francisco 49ers favored by 3.5 points over the Minnesota Vikings.

Two player props stand out as the best bets on Sunday Night Football. My NFL player props have a 22-14 record this season, generating $574 profit for anyone who bet $100 on each suggested player prop at the listed odds, and the following two player props have an opportunity to add to the season's profit.

Kaelon Black Over 7.5 Rush Attempts (-108) at Caesars Sportsbook

Justin Jefferson Over 80.5 Receiving Yards (-125) at Fanatics Sportsbook

Kaelon Black isn't a threat to overtake Christian McCaffey's spot on San Francisco's depth chart. Still, the rookie running back has allowed Kyle Shanahan to ease CMC's workload by playing well in his own right.

McCaffrey has a backfield-high 115 rush attempts, but Black has 79 carries (8.8 per game) in nine games. Jordan James also has eight carries. Thus, Black has handled 39.1% of San Francisco's backfield's rush attempts this year.

He opened the year with a season-high 14 carries in a blowout win against the Rams in Australia in Week 1 before falling short of 7.5 rush attempts in four straight contests, logging seven, four, six and six from Week 2 through Week 5.

Black's workload has ticked up lately. In his last four contests, he's rattled off eight, 12, 11 and 11 carries. In that four-game stretch, he averaged 10.5 rush attempts per game.

Black could be busy again this week. Teams have largely opted to go after Minnesota's fearsome defense on the ground. The Vikings rank 32nd in pass rate over expected (PROE) faced (-5.1%) this season.

That could also be due to Minnesota's dreadful quarterback situation before Kirk Cousins started for them last week, but that remains to be seen. Still, the Vikings have faced 23.1 rush attempts per game by running backs, and 12 running backs have gone over 7.5 rush attempts against them, including teammates in three contests.

Black has an excellent chance to exceed 7.5 rush attempts if the game is close, and he'd be a near lock if the 49ers win handily and opt to salt the game away, making his over for rush attempts an outstanding bet.

Justin Jefferson can have a big effort on Sunday Night Football in Mexico City, Mexico.

Cousins' impact on the Vikings was felt immediately. Minnesota had a season-high 8.1% PROE last week. They had a 0.0% PROE through their first eight games.

Cousins is a competent passer, and no one on the offense was likely happier to see the organization trade for him than Justin Jefferson. In the team's first nine games, Jefferson had an 82.2% snap share, 79.8% route participation rate, 25.5% target share, 0.27 targets per route run (TPRR), 33.3% first-read rate, 35.5% air yard share, 4.4 receptions per game, 56.1 receiving yards per game and 2.21 yards per route run (Y/RR).

In his first game of the year with Cousins, Jefferson had a 90.8% snap share, 88.9% route participation rate, 41.9% target share, 0.41 TPRR, 50.0% first-read rate, 49.1% air yard share, nine receptions, 74 receiving yards and 2.31 Y/RR. Jefferson likely would have had a bigger effort if the Packers pushed them, but the Vikings stomped them 30-10.

The Vikings are underdogs this week, enhancing the likelihood that they'll need to keep their foot on the accelerator and lean on their best offensive weapon. Moreover, Jefferson has a track record of dominance with Cousins.

In 2,340 snaps with Cousins on the field with him since 2021, Jefferson has had a 27.4% target share, 0.28 TPRR and 2.75 Y/RR. Jefferson can have a vintage effort on Sunday Night Football and eclipse 80.5 receiving yards against the 49ers.
