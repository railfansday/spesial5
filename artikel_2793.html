Titans' Calvin Ridley involved in incident at Nashville International

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Tennessee Titans are on a bye this week, and no real news has trickled out of the team facility until Sunday evening, when the Titans released a statement concerning an incident at the Nashville International Airport.

WKRN News reports that Titans wide receiver Calvin Ridley was involved in an incident at Nashville International Airport over the weekend, although no details have been released. A Titans spokesperson confirmed the incident but offered no further comment beyond a brief statement.

"We are aware of the incident and are in communication with Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority," the team said in the statement. "We will not be able to comment any further at this time."

The timing is odd, with the NFL trade deadline fast approaching, and it could complicate any potential trade involving the veteran wideout.

Head coach Robert Saleh is expected to meet with the media on his scheduled availability on Monday, and this news will likely be one of the hot topics.
