Aidan Hutchinson breaks silence after brutal season-ending injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Professional athletes put so much time and dedication into their craft, so any major injury is heartbreaking to see. But Detroit Lions star Aidan Hutchinson suffered a torn ACL during the team's victory over the New England Patriots—just flat-out unlucky. Celebrating big plays is and always will be part of the NFL and any sport, for that matter, so when Hutchinson tore his ACL after celebrating a sack, it's impossible not to feel bad.

However, after what most would consider a "freak injury," Hutchinson sounds like he's focused on moving forward and returning to the high level of play fans see every week. The Lions pass rusher made that much known in his first public message since tearing his ACL.

Hutchinson's injury has also affected other players known for their unique celebrations. Specifically, New York Giants running back Cam Skattebo admitted that his backflip celebration is one fans may not see often, if at all, anymore. Skatteo spoke about it after hearing of Hutchinson's torn ACL.

"It's hard to see," Skattebo said, via the New York Post's Ryan Dunleavy. "I'm saying yeah [no more]. Mentally, I'm like, 'I'm not doing that.' That ain't going to happen to me."

The unfortunate setback came on a play where it felt like everything that could have gone wrong did, as Hutchinson's injury came moments after a sack on Patriots quarterback Drake Maye and resulted in an ankle injury that could sideline him for an extended period.

Hutchinson previously suffered a season-ending injury in 2024, when he broke his tibia and fibula, but he returned to play all 17 regular-season games in 2025. The 26-year-old rebounded after that setback with 14.5 sacks last year, and through nine games in 2026, he had totaled 24 combined tackles, six sacks and one forced fumble.
