The no-code platform gives every AI agent its own wallet and on-chain rules, so agents can act on their own without putting their owners’ funds at risk

JuvrixAI, a no-code platform for building AI agents on NEAR Protocol, has started the presale of its utility token, $JUX. The project tackles a central question of the AI agent era: how to let agents act autonomously, including spending money, while their owners stay in control.

AI agents become truly useful when they can finish tasks: finding the data, choosing the tool, paying for the service. But autonomy and payments create a trade-off.

Giving an agent direct access to a wallet or card means full spending power and full risk. Approving every payment manually keeps funds safe but removes the autonomy that makes agents valuable. And since agents can be manipulated through prompt injection, telling an agent to “be careful” is not a reliable safeguard.

Because the contract enforces the rules, a tricked agent still cannot exceed its limits or pay an unapproved recipient.
