Grok AI Predicts TRUMP Crypto to Zero as Democrats Turn the US Blue

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Elon Musk’s Grok AI predicts a price target for the TRUMP crypto memecoin that would see the President’s token utterly worthless

The Official TRUMP memecoin is positioned for a severe and sustained collapse, Elon Musk’s Grok AI predicts. In the 2026 midterms, Democrats delivered a sweeping victory, reclaiming control of both the House and the Senate. The party flipped multiple Republican-held Senate seats, including in traditionally red or competitive states, and secured a clear House majority.

This outcome sharply rebukes the current Republican administration and significantly weakens the party’s legislative influence for the remainder of the presidential term. With Democrats now holding both chambers, Republicans’ ability to advance their agenda is heavily constrained, and the balance of power in Washington has shifted decisively.

(SOURCE: Grok AI Predicts a Catastrophic TRUMP Crypto Crash)

Democratic majorities have historically favored stronger regulatory oversight of digital assets. A more assertive stance on crypto, ranging from expanded enforcement to potentially restrictive legislation, would remove much of the political tailwind that previously supported Trump-branded tokens.

Political memecoins like TRUMP are especially vulnerable: their value is tightly linked to narrative momentum and perceived political relevance. A cooler regulatory climate and diminished Republican influence could accelerate capital flight from these assets and pressure the more speculative corners of the broader crypto market.

TRUMP has already experienced a dramatic drawdown from its early 2025 peak near $75 and currently trades at a small fraction of that level, down -15% on the day and -98% from that $75 all-time high.

Price action shows persistent lower highs, weak follow-through on any short-term rallies, and elevated sensitivity to political headlines, sparking fear among the remaining TRUMP crypto holders.

Volume has tended to expand more on downside moves than on recoveries, a classic sign of distribution. With a large portion of the supply still subject to unlocks and concentrated holdings, the technical structure remains fragile.

A break of recent support levels would likely trigger further liquidation and accelerate the downtrend. With reduced political relevance, rising regulatory pressure, and deteriorating technicals, the path of least resistance points sharply lower.

A Democratic-led Congress is more likely to favor stricter oversight of digital assets. This environment reduces political support for crypto-friendly initiatives and raises the prospect of tighter regulation or enforcement.

Among all crypto assets, the Official TRUMP memecoin stands out as uniquely exposed. Its value is closely tied to political momentum and the current administration’s standing.

As the most visible Trump-branded token, it is expected to attract disproportionate attention from regulators, critics, and risk-averse investors. While the broader market may feel some pressure, TRUMP is positioned to suffer more severe and prolonged selling than most other tokens.

By January 1, 2027, Official TRUMP could trade in the $0.08 – $0.25 range, with a severe downside scenario testing levels below $0.05.
