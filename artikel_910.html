Mormon Wives' Aspyn Ovard Shares Daughter's Terminal Cancer Diagnosis

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Aspyn Ovard shared during the premiere episode of Hulu's Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County that her and ex-husband Parker Ferris' daughter was diagnosed with terminal cancer.

Aspyn Ovard's family is navigating an unimaginable health journey.

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County star, mom to daughters Cove, 7, Lola, 5, and Elle, 2, with ex-husband Parker Ferris, shared that one of their kids has been diagnosed with terminal cancer.

"Everything happened so, just out of nowhere," Ovard said through tears during a confessional interview for the Hulu show, which premiered Nov. 12. "One day she was OK, and then the next day she wasn't."

"At the hospital, you're like, 'It has to be fine,'" the 30-year-old continued. "You can't fathom that they're actually going to tell you that your baby has cancer, and that it's terminal. She could be gone in a month, we don't know. I've been living like that for over three years, just having no idea."

As Ovard explained, her relationship with Ferris had already reached a breaking point prior to receiving the devastating health news.

"My marriage was nowhere near in a place to be able to make it through something like this," the YouTuber, who split from Ferris in 2024, noted. "We weren't even going to make it through before this happened."

"It was just so toxic and hard," she added. "And through all of it, I feel like I still tried to take care of him because I know how hard he's grieving."

During a therapy session with Ferris, Ovard—now part of a throuple with Bri Davis and her husband Billy Davis—explained to her ex that she's trying to make money so that they won't have to work if they're mourning the loss of a child.

"You know that I will always take care of you because I always have!" Ovard told Ferris during a disagreement. "When everything goes to s--t and things get really hard, I don't want you to have to do anything."

The former couple, high school sweethearts from Utah, tied the knot in October 2015 when she was 19 and he was 20.

Ovard filed for divorce from Ferris nearly nine years later, on April 1, 2024, the same day she announced the birth of their third daughter.

In September 2024, months after filing for divorce, Ovard revealed whether she'd ever consider walking down the aisle again.

"I don't think that I really believe in the concept of marriage at this point," she said on the Weekly Trash podcast at the time. "I would have a long-term partner if I met someone, and I feel like this is it. But to get legally married, I wouldn't ever do again. It's just too hard to separate."

"I think just legally, it's just too much to it," Ovard added. "Even the concept of marriage, you just never know what life is going to throw at you so I think to make those vows, I don't feel like I can really commit to that even if I loved someone so much."
