Ella Langley, Morgan Wallen & Tim McGraw Among Early CMA Award Winners

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Ella Langley and her “Choosin’ Texas” took the CMA Music Video of the Year honor this morning as the Country Music Association revealed early winners in two categories ahead of the full ceremony tonight.

The other winner – for CMA Musical Event of the Year – went to “McArthur,” the song by Hardy, Eric Church, Morgan Wallen and Tim McGraw.

Both of the early awards were announced live today on ABC’s Good Morning America, with Lara Spencer announcing the winners from Nashville’s Honky Tonk Central.

The 60th Annual CMA Awards airs tonight, November 18, live from Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena (8 p.m. ET/7 p.m. CT) on ABC and Disney+ (next day on Hulu). Lainey Wilson hosts.

On GMA this morning, viewers got an exclusive look inside Bridgestone Arena with Wilson, who gave a behind-the-scenes look as final preparations got underway. Watch on Deadline

Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas” video was directed by Langley, Caylee Robillard and Wales Toney. McArthur” was produced by Jay Joyce.

The 60th Annual CMA Awards is a production of the Country Music Association. Robert Deaton serves as Executive Producer, Alan Carter is Director, and Jon Macks is Head Writer.
