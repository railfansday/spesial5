‘The Hunting Wives’ Pays Tribute To Lionsgate Exec Salli Newman

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Viewers of the second season of The Hunting Wives, currently streaming on Netflix, will find a poignant message at the end of the season finale: “In Loving Memory of Salli Newman.”

Newman was SVP Television Production at Lionsgate, producer of The Hunting Wives. She died of a heart attack on December 8, 2025, in her Burbank home at the age of 67, according to her family.

Hunting Wives showrunner Rebecca Cutter told Entertainment Weekly that Newman died during the filming of Season 2. “So we dedicated the final episode to her,” Cutter said. “And, yeah, that was really sad. She was such a hard worker. She so loved the show, and it was just really devastating.”

Newman was also included in the 2026 Primetime Emmy Awards In Memoriam broadcast on September 24.

Newman moved to Los Angeles after graduating from Montana State University and launched her show business career at 19, training as an actor and stuntwoman while working as a secretary/assistant at Consolidated Film Industries.

“Salli soaked up all the knowledge she could from industry veterans,” her family notes, “and worked hard to learn her craft. She created Firebrand Productions in 1990 and spent the next 30 years as an independent producer, working on both made-for-tv movies and feature films, plus several series for both networks and streaming services.”

Among her many producing, co-producing or executive producing credits were D2: The Mighty Ducks (1994), When a Man Loves a Woman (1994), Naomi & Wynonna: Love Can Build A Bridge (1995), Bunheads (2013) and 31 episodes of Greenleaf (2018-2020).

In 2020, Newman was hired as a Senior VP of Television Production at LionsGate Films in Santa Monica, where she worked until her death in December 2025.

Newman is survived by four sisters, two brothers, stepmother Joan Newman, five step-siblings and numerous nieces and nephews.

The second season of The Hunting Wives, starring Malin Akerman, Dermot Mulroney and Brittany Snow, is available to stream on Netflix.
