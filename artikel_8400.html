Authorities Kill 4 Bears Involved in Fatal British Columbia Hiker Attack

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Four grizzly bears — a sow and three cubs — have been euthanized after forensic testing revealed they were responsible for the attack that killed two hikers in British Columbia late last month. Investigators determined that the attack had likely involved predatory behavior, and the bears were killed to protect public safety.

Jennifer Balcarras and her daughter Selah Reed went for what a family member described as “a casual walk” on a familiar trail near Bella Coola, British Columbia on October 31. They were reported missing on November 1 when they did not return home. Balcarras’s remains were discovered on November 2 and Reed’s on November 3.

Following the discovery of the remains, more than a dozen conservation officers worked to investigate the attack and capture any bears involved.

Authorities said DNA testing confirmed that the four bears responsible for this attack were not connected to an attack from last November in which a grizzly mauled three children and an adult on a school trip. The bear in that incident was never captured.

The Bella Coola Conservation Officers Service (BCOS) has said that the investigation into last month’s attack has now concluded, but that “further analysis will examine the response and the circumstances that led to this tragic event.”
