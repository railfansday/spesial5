Sam Bankman-Fried Eyes Possible Return to Crypto, AI and Finance After Prison

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Former FTX CEO Sam Bankman-Fried would consider a return to crypto and philanthropy after his time in prison, while weighing the impact on his reputation. The embattled digital asset executive was sentenced after an infamous collapse that plunged the sector into murky waters.

In an interview with Eli Goldfine, SBF discussed possible options after his release amid the negative impact on his reputation. He might make a crypto return despite this past run with the collapsed crypto exchange, FTX.

SBF said he would consider it depending on when and how he got out, and he also commented on the criminal justice system. His return to finance and crypto is to resume earning money for charitable donations and other causes.

“I would strongly consider it. It would depend on when I got out and how. I’d strongly consider crypto, AI, and finance more generally… “I think Anthropic is clearly one of, if not the top, AI companies,” he said.

Previously, SBF was big on donations through the concept of earning to give, urging wealthy founders to direct a portion of their earnings to charity. Though in jail, SBF is keen on artificial intelligence and its impact on finance and technology.

Per the interview, he tipped small companies built around AI products scaling between $100 million and $1 billion within years. This follows the ongoing AI wave attracting billions from traditional investors, underscoring capital rotation from other sectors.

Anthropic’s IPO has led to funds circling the market, with whales targeting new highs in related stocks. Meanwhile, crypto products have suffered, with asset prices falling to multi-month lows.

Furthermore, SBF warned against companies positioning as pro-AI just to attract funding. Recall that SBF backed Anthropic through Alameda Research, investing $500 million before selling its entire holdings in 2024 to pay debts.

While he planned his return after his sentence, SBF admitted his role in public discourse might be limited because of his current reputation. “…looking at public discourse, politics, and media, I’m not sure what role I could play, if any. But earning to give is a default.”

FTX collapsed in 2022, fueling a massive selloff and billions in losses. The aftermath led to tighter regulations and a lengthy investigation of executives linked to the exchange.
