Brentford vs Everton – Match preview and team news

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Brentford host Everton on Monday night without the suspended Igor Thiago, as two European hopefuls close out Premier League gameweek 11.

Keith Andrews's side trailed Everton by two places and two points before the international break, and a 2-0 defeat at Brighton left them with one win from their last six matches in all tournaments, with 14 points from 10 games. Thiago was sent off for violent conduct in that game, and Brentford failed with an appeal, so he misses this match and the games against Manchester United and Arsenal. Thiago has been involved in four of Brentford's 16 Premier League goals this season. Home form has been their strength: 10 of their 14 points have come at the Gtech Community Stadium, where they have conceded just three league goals, with only Arsenal and Brighton conceding fewer in the first 10 rounds, and every home win has been to nil.

David Moyes's Everton arrive on a run of three straight wins in all competitions after beating Coventry City 3-1, Thierno Barry scoring twice and Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall once. They have won one of five league away games, at Newcastle United, but are unbeaten in their last four Premier League matches at Brentford, drawing three and winning one. The sides drew 2-2 at the Gtech Community Stadium on 11 April, when Dewsbury-Hall scored in added time.

Last meeting: Brentford 2-2 Everton, 11 April 2026, Premier League

Igor Thiago begins a three-match suspension, leaving Andrews to choose between the veteran Callum Wilson as a like-for-like replacement or Kevin Schade through the middle with two of Keane Lewis-Potter, Jaidon Anthony and Dango Ouattara in support. Wilson is expected to start, and Fabio Carvalho could return from a knock. Aaron Hickey (thigh), Nathan Collins (knee), Rico Henry (groin) and Kaye Furo (ankle) are out, as are Antoni Milambo and Josh Dasilva, both with fitness issues, though the pair are due to return to Under-21s action over the weekend.

Jack Grealish is back after Moyes confirmed he will be restored to the squad following his breach of club discipline, though a place on the bench is more likely than a start. Moyes has confirmed that Everton are injury-free heading into the game. Tyler Dibling and Tyrique George are expected to keep the wide roles, supporting Barry and Dewsbury-Hall.

Brentford's one win in their last six matches in all competitions came against Nottingham Forest, with draws against Aston Villa and Hull City and defeats against Liverpool, Sunderland and Brighton. Their three home wins have all been to nil, against Tottenham Hotspur, Chelsea and Forest, and they have scored in every home match.

Everton have won three, drawn one and lost one of their five league games at the Hill Dickinson Stadium, but only one of five away, a 1-0 win at Newcastle United in their most recent road trip. They had lost back-to-back league games to Chelsea and Arsenal before beating Newcastle in both the EFL Cup and the league, having begun the season unbeaten in six.

Brentford possible starting XI: Kelleher; Kayode, Van den Berg, Ajer, Diouf; Yarmoliuk, Janelt, Jensen; Schade, Wilson, Lewis-Potter

Everton possible starting XI: Pickford; Rohl, Tarkowski, Branthwaite, Mykolenko; Garner, Armstrong; Dibling, Dewsbury-Hall, George; Barry

The match will be broadcast live in the United Kingdom on Sky Sports.
