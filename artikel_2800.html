Bengals designate Andrei Iosivas, two others for return from IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Bengals will have three players back at practice on Thursday.

They announced that they have designated wide receiver Andrei Iosivas, cornerback Jalen Davis, and guard Brian Parker for return from injured reserve. All three players will be able to practice with the team for the next 21 days and they can return to the active roster at any point in that window.

Iosivas has been out since Week 2 with a thumb injury. He had three catches for 19 yards in the first two weeks of the season and had 69 catches over the previous two seasons.

Davis played in the first three games and has been out with a hamstring injury. He had five tackles, a tackle for loss and a pass defensed before his injury.

Parker was a sixth-round pick this year and landed on injured reserve ahead of Week 1.
