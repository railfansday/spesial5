Notre Dame football vs Boston College live updates, score, highlights

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

It's the game after THE GAME for No. 5 Notre Dame football Saturday as the welcome a struggling Boston College team into Notre Dame Stadium.

The Fighting Irish (8-1) are coming off a dramatic 31-24 victory over then-No. 1 Miami last Saturday night in South Bend. The victory seemingly set up a path for Notre Dame to make the College Football Playoff as long as it takes care of business in its final three games, starting today with the Eagles (4-5).

The South Bend Tribune is on site for the renewal of the "Holy War." Follow below for LIVE updates, scoring plays, highlights, stats and more as Notre Dame faces Boston College.

This section will be updated closer to the 3:30 p.m. kickoff time.

Boston College at Notre Dame football will televise on NBC today. Jason Benetti will handle play-by-play duties, Jason Garrett on color commentary and Caroline Pineda as the sideline reporter. Benetti is stepping in for the late Dan Hicks, who passed away Thursday at the age of 64. Hicks had been a broadcaster at NBC for almost four decades and had done Notre Dame games for parts of seven seasons.

Notre Dame is a more-than-four-touchdown favorite over Boston College today.
