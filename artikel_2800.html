Justin Herbert ruled out, DJ Uiagalelei to make first start vs. Patriots

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It is officially going to be DJ Uiagalelei vs. Tommy DeVito on Sunday.

Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert has been ruled out with a concussion suffered during last week's loss to the Jets.

Uiagalelei made his debut earlier this month against Houston, completing 2-of-7 passes for 24 yards with a touchdown.

Herbert has completed 62.4 percent of his passes for 1,851 yards with eight touchdowns and 11 interceptions so far this season.

Linebacker Daiyan Henley (knee) and running back Keaton Mitchell (knee) are also out.

Outside linebacker Akheem Mesidor (ankle) and receiver Brenen Thompson (hamstring) are doubtful.

Tight end David Njoku (shoulder), cornerback Tarheeb Still (ankle), defensive lineman Dalvin Tomlinson (knee), outside linebacker Tuli Tuipulotu (ankle), and safety Kendall Williamson (calf) are questionable.
