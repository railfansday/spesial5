David Montgomery credits the offensive line after his first big game as a Texan

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Texans traded for running back David Montgomery this offseason, and through the first 10 weeks of the season, he hadn’t contributed much of anything. That changed on Thursday night.

Montgomery had 26 carries for 107 yards, plus three catches for 17 yards, as the Texans beat the Colts, 26-3. Montgomery nearly doubled his previous season-high for yardage, and he averaged 4.1 yards per carry after having previously never averaged more than three yards per carry as a Texan.

Afterwards, Montgomery gave the credit to the offensive line, which has been subjected to quite a bit of criticism this season.

“Everybody on our offense, on the O-line, we should believe now because we can do it as long as we stick together and we stick to fundamentals,” Montgomery said, via the Houston Chronicle. “With them guys up front, they catch a lot of heat. They did a hell of a job.”

At 4-6 the Texans still have an uphill battle to make the playoffs. But they traded for Montgomery in the hopes that he could have big games late in the season while they’re fighting for a playoff berth, and on Thursday night, Montgomery and the Texans’ offensive line did that.
