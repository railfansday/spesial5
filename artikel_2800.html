Titans vs. Jaguars odds: Opening lines for Week 12 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

It gets harder and harder to write things about this team. The Tennessee Titans have opened as 14.5-point underdogs at the Jacksonville Jaguars in week 12. As I say every week, it's not enough. This game was originally scheduled for a 3:05 Central start, but the NFL didn't want more people to have to watch the Titans, so it was moved to noon Central last week.

At this point, we are just watching to see if Cam Ward can prove that he is good enough for the Titans to try to build around. He played fine yesterday, but it is tough to evaluate him when his offense has to score every time they have the ball or they are going to be down by multiple touchdowns before halftime. He made a crazy fun play yesterday, but those plays aren't sustainable over time.

The Titans will beat the Indianapolis Colts in a few weeks because they have the only quarterback in the league that can't put up points against this defense, but that is the only team they have a remote chance against remaining on the schedule. We are staring at 2-15, which is a step backward from the last two terrible years.
