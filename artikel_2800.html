Nick Wright gives encouraging update on if an LA Lakers vs. 76ers finals is possible

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Nick Wright explores the possibility of a Los Angeles Lakers vs. Philadelphia 76ers matchup in the NBA Finals.

LeBron James' decision to part ways with the Los Angeles Lakers in the off-season and join the Philadelphia 76ers has worked out well for both sides.

After all, the Lakers are currently tied for a conference-best 14-3 record. On the other hand, the 76ers hold the No.1 seed in the Eastern Conference.

Keeping that in mind, many are already contemplating whether a Lakers vs. 76ers battle in the 2027 NBA Finals is possible.

Theoretically, the two sides can only meet in the playoffs during the NBA Finals. But do the two teams' rosters have enough firepower to make it a reality?

Well, according to sports analyst Nick Wright, the possibility is increasing with each passing game during the 2026-27 season.

"There's a non-zero chance we get a Lakers-Sixers NBA Finals. LeBron against the team that he just left. Unbelievable theater.

"Philadelphia is clearly the favourites in the Eastern Conference. If the Sixers make the finals, the three most likely opponents are all just unreal storylines," Wright said.

He added, "Durant-LeBron, one more time in the finals, their fourth time against each other would be so sick. Wemby-LeBron, potentially the official passing of the torch.

"Or Luka-LeBron, where in theory, we could have in the NBA Finals for a possession or two, a father guarding his son, which seems pretty sick."

Previously, Wright was doubtful that the 76ers would be as good as they are in the East. But he now firmly believes that it's their race to lose.

So much so that Wright seemed rather confident in the 76ers reaching the 2027 NBA Finals; it's only a matter of which team they will face from the West.
