Bills news: Key matchups to know and players to watch vs. Buffalo

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Buffalo Bills (8-1) host the Miami Dolphins (1-8) on Sunday afternoon in a battle of teams sitting at opposite ends of the AFC East.

What can fans expect in Week 11, when the Bills and Dolphins meet up for the 125th time? Today's edition of Buffalo Rumblinks leads off by sharing the key matchups to watch and positional analysis as Josh Allen and the Bills look to beat the Dolphins for the 15th time in their last 18 meetings.

Check out the assessments below and then let us know your thoughts on the game in the comments section.

Linked below: Breaking down the key story lines to watch, including how, for the fourth straight week, Buffalo's defense will be tasked with facing a backup quarterback: former Syracuse University standout Kyle McCord, who will be making his second start. We examine what a McCord-offense could look like against an improved Bills defense that has excelled over the last three weeks, regardless of who was in at quarterback. Buffalo knocked out both Baltimore Ravens starting quarterback Lamar Jackson and New York Jets starting QB Geno Smith in wins in Weeks 8 and 10, while confounding Minnesota Vikings backup QB Carson Wentz in a Week 9 win on Monday Night Football.
