Rangers sign ex-Braves All-Star reliever who hasn't pitched since 2024

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Texas Rangers hope for a strong 2027 season.

They've missed the postseason the last three years after their 2023 World Series championship.

One area of need for the Rangers this offseason is their bullpen. Jakob Junis, Jalen Beeks, Tyler Alexander, and Trevor Williams are all free agents.

Texas added bullpen depth on Friday morning, signing a former All-Star reliever who hasn't pitched in the Majors since 2024 due to injuries.

The Athletic's Ken Rosenthal reports the Rangers have signed right-hander Joe Jimenez to a minor league contract.

"Free-agent right-handed reliever Joe Jiménez in agreement with Rangers on minor-league deal, sources tell me and @Jesus_Cano88. Jiménez, 31, last pitched in the majors in 2024. He has undergone three knee surgeries since," Rosenthal wrote.

Jimenez last appeared in the Majors in 2024 with the Atlanta Braves, posting a 2.62 ERA with 82 strikeouts in 68.2 innings.

He earned an All-Star selection with the Detroit Tigers in 2018, the team he spent the first six seasons of his career with. Jimenez then spent two seasons with the Braves from 2023-24.

Jimenez was one of the more effective relievers in the Majors before knee issues led to his season-ending surgeries in 2025 and '26.

With the Rangers, Jimenez hopes to prove he is healthy and capable of making an impact at the Major League level.
