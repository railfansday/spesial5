The AL Poll: Post Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The AP Poll is released at 1 p.m. on Sundays during the college football season and in my opinion, is a detriment to CFB society. The glaring issue with the poll is that it is flawed to ball watchers everywhere, the release time of updated rankings are the top piece of evidence in this claim. It is impossible for the voters to have enough time to fully digest the nearly 30 games from the weekend that must be taken into consideration. To save everyone from the AP Poll, I have very bravely taken it amongst myself to give you a more accurate take otherwise known as the Andrew Lotz Poll or AL Poll. I would love to hear your own thoughts in the comments below.

1) I watched either live or condensed game replays for every team that is ranked or has been removed. While I hope you comment with how wrong I am, my process is a definite step in the right direction that major polls should take into consideration.

2) I humbly can say that I am hardly wrong when it comes to these issues.

Imagine that – I was right. There is no movement in the Top 11 this week in the poll because there was no need for it. Somedays I wonder what I did in a past life to deserve such wisdom and knowledge, yet I am honored to carry this burden and share my thoughts with you. I will forewarn you this entry will be rather short as this was the least chaotic week we have seen since early this season.

With Indiana essentially locking up their spot in Indianapolis vs UCLA, the thing that remains clear to me is that if Oregon is able to sneak into the playoff this year it should be viewed as a crime. They will have not played in a conference championship nor beaten a real team this season while carrying losses to Oklahoma State and Illinois. Iowa is in everyway more deserving of that bid and will likely hold a better record at the end of the season. Only time will tell and each week in obviously unpredictable, but the Hawkeyes have every reason to be the most upset team in the field currently.

As much as I wanted to believe it wasn't going to happen, Boise St has punched their ticket to the CFP barring a collapse. I never saw the Broncos as the best team in the G6 field, but as the season went on others kept losing and they kept winning. My answer to the question of if they should be included would be no 100 out of 100 times, nonetheless they will be the 12th seed. I am however looking forward to there eventual round one exit vs whoever becomes the 5th seed as it will only add fuel to my "G6 should have its own playoff" fire.
