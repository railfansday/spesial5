Vikings announce return of Kirk Cousins

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Vikings have announced the trade that brings Cousins from the Raiders for a third-round pick in the 2027 draft. The deal hinges on Cousins passing a physical, which is usually (but not always) a formality.

The Vikings would be out of luck at quarterback if Cousins fails the physical. That will make them far more likely to give it a passing grade.

Cousins spent 2018 through 2023 with the Vikings. His return undoubtedly means he’ll become the instant starter, with Carson Wentz undoubtedly being released.

There’s no reason to part ways with Kyler Murray. At $1.3 million, he’s a low-cost insurance policy, in the event of a Cousins injury. It remains to be seen whether Murray or Max Brosmer will be the primary backup to Cousins.
