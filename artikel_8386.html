I am familiar with that market as my daughter spent much of this year looking there. She and her husband eventually found a house, but only after they had been outbid multiple times on other houses. It is a seller's market, so you are at a disadvantage as a buyer.

I bought my first house in 1987. Each time I bought a house, I was nervous before signing the contract and for probably the first few months after moving in. I asked myself was it the right house, was I paying too much, what if I have to replace / repair the roof, heater, windows, kitchen, bathroom, etc.....These thoughts you are having are normal. In the long run, it usually worked out for me. The downside was that I spent more money on repairs / upgrades than I originally thought.

You have been looking for 3 years and finally made an offer. What is your current living situation? If it is ok, but not as nice as you would like, you may want to back out. If it is sub-standard, such as way too small with 2 young children, or in a bad neighborhood, you may want to not back out.

If you back out of this contract, what is your plan B - will you continue looking for several more years?
