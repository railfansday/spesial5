Choose Google Fi Wireless if you travel abroad several times a year and Fi’s network is strong where you live. Its Unlimited Premium plan, $65 a month for one line plus taxes and fees, includes 50GB of high-speed data in more than 200 destinations, so you have data on arrival without buying a pass or a local SIM.

Fi is Google’s phone service, and only two of its four plans, Unlimited Premium and Flexible, include data across those destinations, where Flexible charges the same $10 per GB as at home. For one line before taxes, Flexible costs $50 in a month you use 3GB, against Premium’s flat $65, and Premium costs less once you use more than 4.5GB a month.

Fi suits travelers who take several trips abroad a year and get a strong signal at home, which the free trial lets you test before you move your number. For travelers who need 20GB or less abroad, US Mobile’s Unlimited Premium costs less than Fi’s, $44 a month with taxes included against $65 plus taxes, though it reaches 180-plus destinations at most, where Fi reaches 200-plus.
