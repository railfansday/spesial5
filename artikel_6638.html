Nostalgia Alert! 10 Decor Pieces That Bring Back the Magic of a ’90s Christmas

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The '90s are back! From kitschy collectibles to vintage-style Christmas trees, we found the best decor pieces to help you relive this beloved era.

As a child of the ’90s and lover of all things Christmas, I never really warmed to the minimalist, beige Christmas aesthetic. My childhood was filled with bright colors, big jolly Santas and plenty of tinsel. Now, that’s not to say modern decor isn’t lovely. The latest My Christmas House collection, for instance, is swoon-worthy. But my heart belongs to the colorful, often mismatched decor of Christmases past.

Much to my delight, the ’90s are coming back in all of their hokey glory. And now, it is my absolute pleasure to present you with 10 decor pieces that will transform your home into a ’90s Christmas wonderland.

The ’90s were a time of bold colors and patterns, big lights and collectibles galore. Burgundy, hunter green and gold were the colors of choice, and pops of sparkly silver tinsel adorned many a Christmas tree. Christmas villages sat nestled in fake snow atop mantles or credenzas while animated Santas stood in windows waving their candles. Blow molds adorned porches and homeowners scoured local stores in hopes of snagging a set of popular icicle lights.

Today’s 90s-inspired Christmas decor includes all of the above.  The items on our list are colorful, cheerful and bound to get you in the Christmas spirit.

This Vintage Pine Prelit Christmas tree screams ’90s. When she first saw this tree, tester Nancy Snyder was reminded of her own childhood decor. “Part of what makes the holidays so special is revisiting the traditions we loved as kids,” she writes in her full review. “If you’ve been looking for a tree that brings back the magic of childhood holidays, this one is worth a look.”

A holiday porch goose is sure to delight every visitor who comes over this holiday season. Deputy shopping editor Katie Bandurski dresses up her Porch Goose all year long, but the holidays are especially fun. “For Christmas, there are a bevy of fun costumes for your gaggle to choose from. Think—Santa and Mrs. Claus, a gingerbread man and even a penguin,” she writes in her holiday goose roundup.

I was gifted this Little Debbie Christmas Tree at our annual Christmas party last year and it was an instant favorite. This blow mold stands 22-inches tall and emits a warm glow when plugged in. Looking for something a bit more classic? Try this nostalgic snowman blow mold instead.

When I was a kid, this lighted ceramic Christmas tree was a staple in every home I visited. Now, I have one of my own. I opted for an 18-inch green tree, but there are three sizes and a variety of colors to choose from.

Trim up the tree with Old World Christmas. The brand, which is known for its hand-painted, blown glass Christmas ornaments, has an impressive selection of retro and vintage-style ornaments that will brighten up your room. Choose from fun designs like this I Love The 90s cassette tape ornament or VHS tape. If you prefer a more classic look, opt for a red peppermint reflection ornament and  retro tree top.

We were thrilled by the return of the nostalgic Lenox Spice Village but we are absolutely in love with the brand’s Mistletoe Park Village collection. This collection features charming figurines so you can create the ultimate Christmas village. Each figurine features the brand’s signature creamy porcelain exterior and 24k gold accents. Most of the larger buildings light up to really bring your village to life.

Put this animated Santa by your tree for a holly jolly Christmas. He climbs up and down his 42-inch ladder with a string of brightly colored Christmas lights. Rather than toys, his sack holds a speaker which plays 15 different Christmas carols.

Rifle Paper Co. makes some of the best throw blankets we’ve tested. We especially love this Beary Christmas blanket, which is made of 100% cotton and features an array of nostalgic teddy bears dressed in their cozy best.

Hang these needlepoint stockings by the chimney with care. Each stocking is made of 100% cotton with polyester batting and measures 9-inches wide and 19.5-inches long. That means there’s plenty of room for stocking stuffers! Better still, these stockings can be personalized for each member of your family.
