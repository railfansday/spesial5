Dave &amp; Buster's Is Becoming "Doom &amp; Buster's" With Five New Marvel-Inspired Drinks

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

New games, new drinks, and plenty of reasons for superhero lovers to stop by.

Dave & Buster's is getting a major makeover, and we have Doctor Doom to thank.

Ahead of Marvel Studios' Avengers: Doomsday, the restaurant and arcade chain is temporarily renaming itself Doom & Buster's as part of a nationwide takeover featuring themed games, exclusive merchandise, and five new drinks. And considering the movie doesn't hit theaters until December 18, fans will have plenty of time to get in on the action beforehand.

New Marvel games and experiences began rolling out November 4, ahead of the full transformation on November 16. That's when participating Dave & Buster's locations will become Doom & Buster's, complete with immersive Doomsday Zones, Marvel-inspired games, collectible Power Cards, and themed prizes. The takeover runs through January 31, 2027, although specific attractions will vary by location.

There's also DOOMHOUR, a surprise hour when D&B Rewards members can earn double tickets on eligible arcade games. Members will receive advance notice through the rewards app.

The chain is introducing three limited-time cocktails and two alcohol-free mocktails, all inspired by Marvel characters and the upcoming movie.

First up is Doomsday's Dominion, which combines Smirnoff vodka, melon liqueur, pineapple, and lemon-lime soda. It also comes with a Black Magic sidecar that guests can pour into the drink themselves.

There's also the Multiversal Mule, made with vodka, blue orange liqueur, dragon fruit, and ginger beer. It comes with edible shimmer and an LED glow cube, so it'll be hard to miss at the bar.

For anyone who can't decide on just one drink, the Fantastic Flight includes four miniature cocktails made with vodka, orange liqueur, and citrus sour, each featuring its own flavor.

The mocktails are getting just as much attention to detail.

Panther's Potion combines cherry, ginger beer, and citrus sour, and comes with a blue shimmer sidecar inspired by vibranium and a silver sugar rim. Meanwhile, Captain Punch mixes blue raspberry, coconut, pineapple, lemon-lime soda, and citrus sour, topped with a blue sugar rim, cherries, and a blue sour candy strip.

All five drinks will be available from November 16 through January 31, 2027.
