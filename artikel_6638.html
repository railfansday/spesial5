This Coffee Subscription Delivers Some of the Freshest Beans We’ve Tried

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

It really doesn't get fresher than this. A Storyville coffee subscription delivers delicious beans right to your doorstep.

We’ve all been there. Standing in the grocery store staring at dozens of coffee brands and trying to decide which one might perk us up, only to end up scowling at a subpar cup of sludge the next morning. Well, those days are over because Storyville, one of Seattle’s best coffee shops, now delivers freshly roasted beans straight to your door.

I tried a Storyville coffee subscription, and I assure you that you’ll never have to waste your time (or money) on stale beans again. Here’s everything you need to know about this caffeinated club.

Storyville is one of the highest-rated coffee shops in Seattle, with locations at Pike Place and at 1st and Madison. They also have a roasting studio you can tour to see exactly where your coffee beans are roasted before making their way to your door. But if you can’t make it to Seattle, they also offer coffee subscription services with nationwide shipping.

The brand sources premium-grade green coffees and roasts each bean varietal individually before blending. Beans are roasted on the darker end of medium and feature a rich, natural sweetness with notes of chocolate and caramel. Unlike many other dark roasts, this coffee has a smooth finish and no bitterness. Best of all, Storyville ships its coffee immediately after roasting.

Storyville subscriptions are highly customizable in order to fit your needs. First, choose between whole beans and single-serve pods. Then choose the quantity and delivery frequency that best suits your needs. You can pause, skip or cancel via text at any time. Moreover, you’ll receive 20% off, among other perks.

I am a coffee fiend. On any given day, I’m three cups in by 9 a.m. That’s why I couldn’t wait to get my hands on a bag of real Seattle coffee.

My order arrived on Wednesday, October 28. I received a bag of Prologue coffee beans, Sunrise espresso beans and Epilogue decaf coffee beans. I knew the roast dates would be stamped on the bags, but I was amazed to see that my beans had been roasted just days prior, on October 25 and October 27. Talk about fresh!

I opened the Prologue first and poured the entire bag into the bean hopper of my De’Longhi Rivelia. After brewing, I took a sip before adding my cream and sugar and was amazed by how smooth it was. It was fresh and flavorful without a hint of bitterness. I love a dark roast and could drink this all day long.

The next day, I opened the Sunrise espresso beans. I used it to make a regular cup of coffee followed by an Americano. Like the Prologue, the beans were clearly fresh, and the coffee was exceptionally smooth! I’ll be saving this bag along with the decaf for espresso martinis with my girlfriends.

As a dark roast gal, I’ve had my fair share of coffee that tastes bitter or burnt. I didn’t get any notes of bitterness from Storyville coffee. I’ll also point out that a bag of Prologue coffee lasted one week. Given how much coffee I drink plus the fact that my husband tends to make a cup here and there, I’m not surprised we went through it so quickly. Luckily, the subscription is completely customizable. If you drink as much coffee as I do, you’ll want to order two or three bags at a time. But if you just enjoy one cup per day, then one bag at a time will suffice.

That said, these beans are so fresh we wouldn’t be surprised if you start drinking more coffee than usual. And we certainly wouldn’t blame you!

It’s also easy to send a subscription as a gift. Simply head over to their gifting page and opt for three, five, eight or 12 prepaid shipments.
