Wolves face spell without 22-year-old after midweek warning leads to injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Wolves saw Jordan James forced off after 36 minutes in the 3-0 win over Cardiff City last Saturday, and he missed Wednesday’s victory at Bristol City as a result. The Express and Star has now confirmed the Welshman faces a short spell out with a hamstring injury.

James had complained of discomfort in his hamstring in the week leading up to the game, but he was cleared in scans and started. Further tests in the wake of his withdrawal ruled him out against the Robins, and the midfielder is set to sit out against Wrexham this Saturday too.

The 22-year-old, who had interest from Wrexham in the summer of 2025, will hope to return as soon as safely possible. James was not given a Wales call-up for the upcoming UEFA Nations League fixtures, so he will have the break to rest up and recover.

James has not played the starring role many backed him for at Wolves after his fine stint with Leicester City last season. He had to wait until October 13th for his first league start, but his back-to-back starting XI selections in the Watford and Cardiff City clashes suggested he was beginning to find his place.

In the midweek win over Bristol City, Jean-Ricner Bellegarde was brought into the side in attacking midfield. Marshall Munetsi, who James has also been battling with for a start, was in a deeper midfield role alongside Andre.

Bellegarde does have competition for his place though. Mateus Mane will hope to reclaim a starting berth having been benched for four of the last five matches, while Rodrigo Gomes could be reintroduced as well.

Tawanda Chirewa offers energy off the bench, so Cesar Peixoto is not short of options despite James’ injury.

As touched on before, James hasn’t made the impact quite hoped for at Molineux yet. He has had to battle for his starting spot and two goals in 13 games means he isn’t replicating the productive form he showed at Leicester City (34 games, 11 goals, four assists).

However, from his spell with the Foxes and in his Birmingham City breakthrough beforehand, there is no doubt that the Wales international can be a real standout at this level. He will hope to rediscover his previous best with the Old Gold yet.

Providing no further setbacks emerge, James can use the international break to recover and work closely with Peixoto and his coaches to help him have a bigger impact.

Wanderers face Charlton Athletic in the first game after the break on November 21st. It remains to be seen if the loan midfielder is fit and ready for the return to action.
