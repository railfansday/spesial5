Expedia to Buy Kiwi.com, Extending Its Acquisition Spree Into Flights

Expedia is buying its way toward becoming a one-stop shop for travel sellers and customers, even as the economics of that strategy come under pressure.

Expedia Group agreed Wednesday to acquire Czech online travel agency Kiwi.com, expanding its European flight offering for consumers and B2B partners.

The companies did not disclose the price, and Expedia declined to comment on it when contacted by Skift. Kiwi generated €227 million ($264 million) in 2025 revenue.

The deal is expected to close in the first half of 2027, subject to customary closing conditions. As Skift reported in September, Kiwi’s €100 million ($116 million) convertible bond matures June 28, 2027.

The deal could provide an exit for majority shareholder General Atlantic, which invested in 2019. Shareholder arrangements were not disclosed.

“Combining Kiwi.com's technology with Expedia Group’s scale will strengthen Brand Expedia and our B2B business’ position in air, giving travelers and partners more choice, confidence and value,” Expedia Group CEO Ariane Gorin said in the announcement.

Expedia Group agreed to acquire Czech online travel agency Kiwi.com, adding virtual interlining technology — the practice of combining flights from airlines without commercial agreements into single itineraries — to Brand Expedia and its B2B business. Neither company disclosed the price. Kiwi generated €227 million ($264 million) in 2025 revenue, down about 20% from its 2023 peak of €284 million, while operating profit rose fivefold to €15 million after personnel expenses fell 31% and customer support spending fell 32%. The deal is expected to close in the first half of 2027, ahead of the June 28, 2027 maturity of Kiwi's €100 million convertible bond, and could provide an exit for majority shareholder General Atlantic, which held 66.7% of ordinary shares by December 2025. Kiwi is the fourth Expedia acquisition in this stretch, following Tiqets, CarTrawler, and Layla, which carried an aggregate disclosed price of $676 million. The purchases land as Expedia's B2B margins tighten: third-quarter B2B revenue rose 23% to $1.71 billion, but adjusted EBITDA margin fell 134 basis points to 27.5%. Booking Holdings' €1.63 billion bid for flight specialist Etraveli was blocked by the European Commission in 2023, a decision upheld in September and now under appeal.
