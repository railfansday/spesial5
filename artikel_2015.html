Ex-Liverpool and Wolves star enters Blackpool FC managerial frame

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Former Premier League and England international defender, Conor Coady, has reportedly emerged as a leading contender to replace Ian Evatt at League One strugglers, Blackpool.

That is according to an update from Pete O'Rourke via X, with the Tangerines beginning third-tier life without their former defender with one point from a possible six.

A 2-1 loss to Lancashire rivals, Wigan Athletic, at Bloomfield Road on Saturday lunchtime was followed up by an encouraging draw against Russell Martin's in-form Leicester City on Tuesday night, with Niall Ennis equalising Will Alves' opener.

Blackpool are currently 20th in League One having taken just 13 points from 15 matches, and whilst the club still have a game in hand to play over many divisional rivals, only a two-goal swing separates them and Doncaster Rovers, who occupy the final relegation place.

Blackpool have endured mixed fortunes since their relegation from the Championship three-and-a-half years ago, with whoever replaces Evatt being the fifth head coach at Bloomfield Road since then.

Evatt was able to guide the Seasiders to a 13th-placed finish last season, ending the campaign with four wins on the bounce, but departed the club last week on his 45th birthday, and attention has now turned as to who will be his successor in the long run.

Steve Thompson led the fixtures against Wigan and Leicester, but as per O'Rourke, it has been claimed that Coady, 33, has emerged as a 'top contender' for the role, just weeks after confirming his retirement from playing.

The centre-back amassed over 300 combined appearances at Premier League and Championship level, and is best known for his spells at Liverpool, Wolves, Everton and Wrexham, which included a second-tier title win at Molineux, as well as earning 10 England caps.

It is stated that the England Under-21s assistant has already impressed higher-ups at the Football Association after landing said role two months' ago.

Coady terminated his Wrexham contract in August after making just six appearances under Phil Parkinson at the Racecourse Ground, and immediately turned his focus to coaching.

Indeed, he was offered a role within Liverpool's academy setup, working alongside former Blackpool midfielder, Jay Spearing, before replacing Ashley Cole as part of Lee Carsley's setup with the Young Lions, who have recently won successive UEFA Under-21 European Championships.

It remains to be seen if Coady does land his first full-time managerial role on the Fylde Coast, but when asked about what the future holds, he recently said: "I'm not massively in a rush because I want to make sure it's (the decision is) right."

There has, unsurprisingly, been plenty of discourse as to who the Blackpool hierarchy will turn to next in an attempt to improve the club's fortunes.

Coady, of course, would be a high-profile name based off his playing exploits, but the St Helens native isn't the only figure linked with the job.
