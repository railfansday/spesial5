Matheus Nunes wants to accelerate talks over signing a new contract with Man City, sources have told Football Insider.

Nunes has established himself as a reliable operator at the top level since joining Man City from Wolves in 2023.

The 28-year-old reinvented himself as a right-back under Pep Guardiola following a budding early career as a box-to-box midfielder.

He remains a key part of their squad under Enzo Maresca, but is approaching the final stages of his City contract, which expires in June 2028.

The club recently opened talks with his camp over fresh terms, and as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Nunes wants to sign an extension as soon as possible.

He is content with his standing under Maresca and keen to lift more silverware at the Etihad Stadium.

Nunes has made 10 appearances for City so far this term, but is currently sidelined after pulling out of Portugal duty last week.

City could explore the addition of a recognised right-back in 2027, but Nunes remains keen to stay at the club for the next few seasons.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I’m sure there’s a willingness from all parties to get a deal done, so I think everybody involved is pretty relaxed on this one. They are confident that Nunes will end up signing a new deal at City.

"He’s happy at the club as well and is closing in on 150 appearances for them, which just shows how much of a mainstay he has been in the side. Obviously, City want to keep hold of their best players."

Maresca is happy with Nunes, but the future of the right-back position remains a long-term question mark for the Citizens.

They were linked with a move for 23-year-old Chelsea star Gusto over the summer, but their top six rivals were not keen to do business.

He is open to exploring a move away from Stamford Bridge, however, and sources have revealed to Football Insider that City could make another move for Gusto in January

Chelsea remain keen to retain his services and will stand by their lofty price-tag of the French international.
