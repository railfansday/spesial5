Chickens come home to roost as the same pairing are exposed again.. and again.. West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

West Ham United will have to make do without one of their – ahem – key players when the Championship resumes after the international break. Following on from today’s 2-1 defeat against a modest Blackburn Rovers side who knew exactly what they were about this afternoon, the Hammers go into a two week period of ‘deep reflection’ before restarting against Preston North End in two weeks time.

And they’ll have to do so without £4 million – oops that should read £40 million man Maximilian Kilman who picked up a fifth yellow card of the campaign – this one apparently for simulation after being smacked cleanly in the head –  and he will therefore sit out the first game back after the merciful lull in West Ham’s shambolic descent from the top end  of the table..

In depressing echoes of a year ago, West Ham still have to set up repeatedly with Kilman and Mavropanos as the defensive pairing of doom – only a matter of time before one, or other of them is caught hopelessly out of position.

At least Dinos Mavropanos offers a goal threat – whilst when paired with Kilman is made to look defensively distinctly ordinary

In what can only be seen as a massive mistake, West Ham chose not to add to their centre back keystone cops double act in the summer window, letting Todibo leave on loan and therefore both committing and condemning themselves to the duo for the foreseeable future.

West Ham solved the problem last year – but not the number of points lost – by bringing in Axel Disasi in the January window and you’d expect them to make a similar move for an urgent upgrade this winter. Too little, too late, much as it was a year ago.

We can only hope that Airidas Golambeckis impresses enough in his outing alongside Dinos in two weeks’ time that he keeps Kilman out of the side.

To be honest, it is tough watching the same pairing year after year making the same mistakes and costing clean sheet after clean sheet. Having a goalkeeper with the arms of a five year old doesn’t help but until West Ham wake up, smell the coffee and realise that Mavropanos and Kilman will never be more than mediocre, the same results will flow, week in, week out.
