Sunderland will not entertain offers for Brian Brobbey in the January transfer window, sources have told Football Insider.

The 24-year-old put pen to paper on a new contract earlier this month, and while it has not ended the speculation, it has placed the club in a strong position to dictate his future.

He has played a crucial part in Regis Le Bris' plans so far this season, and will continue to do so, with no plans to see him leave the Stadium of Light any time soon.

However, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Sunderland may be forced to sell Brobbey if they are relegated from the Premier League.

That is seen as the "only way" the Netherlands international could become available, and with the Black Cats currently in the bottom three, it could be a real possibility.

Le Bris will hope to turn around his side's fortunes following the international break and ease any concerns, but the future of his star players could be on the line.

Football Insider revealed on 15 November that Sunderland have no plans to lose Brobbey mid-way through the season as he has a crucial role to play in their ambitions.

However, should their Premier League struggles continue, the Black Cats may have no choice but to listen to offers from the striker as they sink into the relegation zone.

A 1-0 win against Chelsea last time out has eased any immediate concerns, but contingency plans will have to be put in place in case the worst happens on Wearside.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Sunderland have shown how much they value Brian Brobbey by giving him that new bumper contract until 2031.

"So they won't be planning to lose Brian Brobbey anytime soon. His price tag has gone up probably within the last 12 months as well.

"But the only way that Brian Brobbey could maybe leave Sunderland anytime soon would be if Sunderland were to be relegated from their Premier League.

"Because I'm sure the player himself wouldn't want to be playing in the championship but he probably wants to be playing at the highest level possible."

Sunderland's determination to keep hold of Brobbey has come as a result of growing interest from a number of clubs both in the Premier League and across Europe.

The likes of Juventus and Inter Milan have been linked, while Tottenham and Chelsea are among his domestic suitors, with the striker's displays parking new interest.

Meanwhile, Football Insider revealed in September that Man United have been keeping a close eye on Brobbey and could move to lure him away from Sunderland.

The Red Devils have long been after a new striker, and the Netherlands international is said to tick a number of boxes at Old Trafford.

Since then, Brobbey has signed a new contract to place Sunderland in a strong position with regard to his future, but relegation would take the call out of their hands.
