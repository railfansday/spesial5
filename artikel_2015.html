Andrew Cavenagh ‘tempted’ to personally fund marquee Rangers signing

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Rangers chairman Andrew Cavenagh is fighting the urge to fund a permanent move for Rangers loanee Badredine Bouanani.

That is according to former Aberdeen chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the Ibrox chief will be “tempted” to trigger the club’s £15million purchase option for the winger.

Bouanani has broken into the starting XI in recent weeks and is catching the eye with his direct style and quick feet.

Wyness believes that Cavenagh’s impending deal to sell his ParetoHealth company could potentially be used to help fund the permanent acquisition of Bouanani.

The 21-year-old is on loan from Stuttgart at the moment and is quickly becoming a firm favourite among the Rangers fans.

The Algerian has already chipped in with a goal and an assist so far in Scottish Premiership action, and could be a crucial part of Derek McInnes’ plans moving forward.

Aberdeen’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Pittodrie between 2000 and 2004 and now runs a consultancy business advising elite clubs – believes that Cavenagh could personally fund a move for Bouanani if ParetoHealth are sold.

Recent reports in America have suggested that the company is worth an eye-watering £4.5billion, which could see Cavenagh net a huge chunk of cash in the coming months.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider’s Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how he may be tempted to invest part of that into Rangers’ transfer budget.

“The one other story that we talked about recently was Andrew Cavenagh, the chairman, selling his ParetoHealth and he's now cashed up personally,” he said.

“Would he be tempted now to think of that and have the football disease and put some of his personal wealth into a player situation like Bouanani?

“I'm sure the Rangers fans will be encouraging him to do that, of course. Hopefully his wife has got better sense and tells him to stop.

“But anyway, that’s are the sort of option it might need to fund this thing.”

Wyness also told Football Insider on Sunday that another option to help Rangers fund a move for Bouanani could be to sell striker Youssef Chermiti.

The 22-year-old is currently out of action with an ACL injury, and is expected to miss almost the entire remainder of the campaign.

Wyness believes that there is still a “market” for the Portuguese across Europe, which Rangers could look to exploit in order to keep Bouanani long-term.

The Ibrox outfit rejected a £22m bid for Chermiti from Besiktas in the summer, after outlining that it did not meet their valuation for the frontman.
