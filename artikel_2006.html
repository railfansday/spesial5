Arsenal transfer interest in Alphadjo Cisse: Arsenal face a £50m decision as AC Milan youngster attracts Premier League interest

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Arsenal transfer interest in Alphadjo Cisse is starting to take shape. Arsenal are monitoring the development of AC Milan starlet Alphadjo Cissé, although his sensational start to the season could prove to be costly for Mikel Arteta.

The 20-year-old has made a blistering start to his Serie A career and attracted interest from a number of Premier League clubs after his impressive displays so far. Arsenal are reported to be interested.

The Gunners are among several clubs, including Brighton, keeping tabs on the player, according to Italian football news website Tuttomercatoweb. The striker is rumoured to be worth up to 60 million euros. That’s an eye-watering sum for a player who is just starting out in his professional career.

Cissé was signed by Milan from Hellas Verona in February 2026 but was allowed to finish the season on loan at Catanzaro.

Prior to joining the San Siro, he had already showcased his potential with six goals in 22 games in Serie B. He has made a huge impact since moving up to the top division.

Cissé has found the net five times in nine Serie A games this season. He has also chipped in with three goals in four Europa League games, making a total of eight goals in 13 games.

This has made him an important addition to the Milan squad. He is also versatile, as he can play as an attacking midfielder or higher up the park.

Arsenal may find his versatility attractive. Arteta likes players who can play in different positions and with injuries, and with a busy fixture list, he will want to have players who can do that.

The main issue could be that Milan are keen to keep their young star.

It is reported that the Italian club have turned down a verbal offer from Brighton and are looking to extend Cissé’s contract, which currently runs until June 2030.

This indicates that Milan is considering his future, rather than looking to make a profit. It also demonstrates just how quickly his reputation has developed.

Arsenal have the funds to target exciting young talent, but would they want to spend so much on Cissé?

There is no indication that he would be an instant replacement for Arsenal’s current attacking options. However, Cissé’s age, goal-scoring ability and versatility make him an interesting prospect.

This could be one to keep an eye on beyond January. Milan have little motivation to sell, and Arsenal might want to see how he develops before committing themselves.

Cissé has already hit the ground running in Italy and now needs to prove that his initial success can turn into something more consistent.
