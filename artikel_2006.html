Huddersfield Town player wins League One award following derby stunner at Bradford City

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

It came after his brilliant early overhead kick in the 3-0 derby win at Valley Parade on Hallowe’en.

Forward for a long throw-in, Low bided his time in the Bradford area and stayed forward.

As the ball dropped to him, the central defender had his back to goal but hooked it spectacularly over his shoulder past Bantams keeper Jon McCracken in front of a stunned Kop.

It put the visitors on their way to a comprehensive victory over their local rivals.

Lead Sky Bet EFL Goal of the Month judge and Sky Sports Pundit Don Goodman said: "The ball was bouncing around the Bradford City box waiting for a bit of quality and Joe Low definitely delivered.

"He found himself some space and when it fell to him he beat the keeper with a terrific overhead kick.”

Low beat off competition from Burton Albion’s Gbemi Arubi, MK Don’s Alex Gilbey and Notts County’s Callum Roberts to win the accolade.
