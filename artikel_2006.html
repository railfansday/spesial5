Mauricio Pochettino 'will fail at Man United' because of one 'massive problem' - Insider

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Mauricio Pochettino has been tipped to struggle at Manchester United due to a lack of confidence in the board.

The 54-year-old was confirmed as the replacement for Michael Carrick following his dismissal last week, and has been tasked with turning around their nightmare start.

He was immediately identified as the top target at Old Trafford, despite other contenders, as they believe he will be the right man to get them back on track for success.

However, former Man United assistant manager Mick Brown told Football Insider that Pochettino could be hamstrung by the "incompetence" of the chief decision-makers.

There is a feeling that, regardless of which manager is brought in to steer the ship, they will face an uphill struggle to turn things around with the way the club has been run.

This comes after Pochettino became the fourth permanent manager to take charge at Man United in just four years, with each having suffered from the same issues.

Football Insider revealed on 15 November that Man United believe Pochettino is the man for the job, and his first task will be to shore up their porous defence.

That defence, however, has become an issue due to the club's transfer plans as they have not provided previous managers with backing to improve their options.

While Pochettino may be backed in the January transfer window, he has been thrust into a difficult position and must deal with a squad that has not been good enough.

"Unfortunately, it doesn't matter who the manager is when this group are making the decisions," Brown told Football Insider.

"It's not a coincidence that all of the managers they've had recently have failed somewhere down the line, it's all been for similar reasons, and it's becoming a massive problem.

"They want Pochettino to come in and improve the defence, because that has been a problem, but it's been made worse by the lack of investment in the position.

"They're not making decisions behind the scenes to set the club and the managers up for success, it's just poor decision after poor decision, and now look where we are.

"So Pochettino can come in, he can try to work some magic, but as long as the board aren't giving him the cirucmstances to succeed then he will fail at Man United.

"Any manager who comes in under these cirucmstances will continue to struggle just like those before him, and the blame can only be placed on those at the top."

Pochettino's appointment has come at a time when there is increasing pressure on the board at Old Trafford, with some chief decision-makers already facing backlash.

Sporting director Jason Wilcox has come under fire, with players having spoken out publicly against him and the club's plans, with major questions now being asked.

Football Insider revealed on 14 November that Pochettino already wants Wilcox to be replaced in his role and wants somebody he can trust to lead the club's recruitment.

Amid the growing pressure on the sporting director's position, there is a growing need for a clear sense of direction which has not been provided under the current crop.

Instead, changes from the top could be what is required to spark a revival at Old Trafford, with Pochettino keen to make changes that will benefit his side long-term.
