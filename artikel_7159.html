Parallel: Speech-Language Pathologist – Kentucky (Part-Time, Remote) – 2026–27

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Parallel is hiring a Kentucky-licensed Speech-Language Pathologist for a 17.5-hour-per-week remote assignment with a Kentucky school district partner. The role covers direct speech therapy for an elementary caseload, starts in November 2026, and runs through the end of the school year (May 2027).

Are you a dedicated clinician driven to make a meaningful difference in the lives of children with learning differences? Join a team where providers are treated as part of the school community, not as a stopgap, and help students build the communication skills they need to thrive.

Pay starts at $52/hour and varies depending on state and experience. This is a 1099 role only. A Clinical Recruiter can walk you through the full breakdown and answer any questions you may have about compensation.

If you’re seeking flexible, rewarding work with a team that truly supports its clinicians, we’d be excited to hear from you.

Parallel is the first tech-forward provider of care for learning and thinking differences across the United States. We believe learning differences are parallel ways of thinking that should be celebrated. Our mission is to provide students with the resources and encouragement to succeed in the classroom and beyond by helping them build confidence in their unique strengths and create strategies to work around their challenges.

Parallel simplifies the process of getting support for learning differences by consolidating providers and resources on a single platform. We connect students with qualified professionals while significantly reducing waiting times, costs, and confusion.
