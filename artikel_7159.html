Week In Review: What makes an attendance strategy successful?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

As schools work to reduce their student chronic absenteeism rates, two recent reports highlight which strategies are the most and least effective in improving attendance. For instance, the reports concluded that attendance efforts that strongly rely on truancy laws to get students back in classrooms are less effective than approaches that focus on other strategies like building family engagement and fostering student connectedness.

Additionally, school leaders using multiple evidence-based strategies at once to tackle chronic absenteeism saw more success than principals who only used one effective strategy, according to an analysis of principal surveys by the Everyone Graduates Center, Partnership for Student Success and the GRAD Partnership.
