Jameis Winston briefly takes over for injured J.J. McCarthy, leading Giants to 19-11 win over Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

J.J. McCarthy made his second start at quarterback for the New York Giants, but left the game with an injury in the third quarter. While he was being evaluated in the locker room, Jameis Winston led a touchdown drive to put the Giants ahead in an eventual 19-11 win over the Philadelphia Eagles on Sunday.

The victory improved the Giants' record to 5-2-1 and strengthened their first-place lead in the NFC East over the Dallas Cowboys.

New York's defense made Eagles QB Joe Milton III miserable, sacking him seven times. Making the first start of his NFL career, Milton completed 18 of 34 passes for 169 yards and no touchdowns. The Giants also held Philadephia to 92 rushing yards.

McCarthy left the game after taking a hit to his right shoulder on a 3-yard run in the third quarter. He left the field without assistance and Jameis Winston took over at quarterback for the Giants.

After being checked in the medical tent, McCarthy attempted to warm up for a return to the game but grabbed his right side in pain and went to a knee. He subsequently went to the locker room and the Giants announced he was questionable to return with a hip injury.

Winston led the Giants to a touchdown on their next series, completing 6 of 7 passes and connecting with tight end Isaiah Likely for a 5-yard score. The big play on the 76-yard series was a 27-yard completion from Winston to Darnell Mooney that put the Giants in the red zone.

Accuracy has always been an issue for Milton, whose physical attributes continue to make him an intriguing prospect for coaches. However, his inaccuracy may have cost the Eagles a touchdown on a third-and-8 from the Giants' 34-yard line with 2:25 remaining in regulation.

Milton threw a pass low to Elijah Mitchell, who had to go to the turf for the catch. Had the ball been thrown chest-high, allowing Mitchell to run, he likely would have run in for a touchdown. Instead, he was short of the line to gain and Philadelphia had to settle for a 45-yard field goal from Jake Elliott.

The Giants initially took a 6-0 lead, finishing their first two possessions with Dominic Zvada field goals after J.J. McCarthy was sacked to stall each drive.

Philadelphia scored the game's first touchdown with Saquon Barkley scoring on a 3-yard run. That finished off a 58-yard drive highlighted by a 19-yard scramble by Joe Milton and a pass interference penalty on Giants cornerback Deonte Banks that resulted in 30 yards.

The Giants had only 88 total yards at halftime, due largely to five sacks by the Eagles' defense, two by Zack Baun. New York also managed only 16 yards rushing, with 8 of those coming from McCarthy.

Philadelphia didn't fare much better in the first half with Milton throwing for 87 yards and getting sacked three times. Additionally, Barkley was held to 22 yards rushing in 10 carries. Milton outrushed him with 24 yards.
