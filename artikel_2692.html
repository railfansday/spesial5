Connecticut high school football Week 9 roundup: Enfield upsets Berlin in final seeconds

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Week 9 high school football roundup. Coaches and statisticians, send your summaries to info@gametimect.com.

AJ Siana connected with Levi Wozny on a 3rd and 7 play for 38 yards to set up a game-winning, 1-yard touchdown run from Sawyer Pequita with 19 seconds left to lead Enfield to its CCC upset road win over Berlin at Sage Park.

Pequita ended the game with 169 yards rushing and three touchdowns on 25 carries. His 73-yard touchdown put Enfield up 7-0. Siana completed 4 of 8 passes for 129 yards and a touchdown.

Joel Budd had six tackles, includi two for losses, for the Enfield defense. Grant Lyver added six tackles, Isaiah Lawrence five, including one for loss and Cam Trotta seven tackles.

Willie Gay rushed for 132 yards and three touchdowns on 10 carries, Kayden Boland added 64 yards and two touchdowns on 10 carries and Luca Houle was 6 of 12 for 106 yards to lead Rockville to its Pequot win.

Zach Crawford led Rockville's defense with 12 tackles, a sack, and a forced fumble. Dante Whitaker returned an interception 41 yards for a touchdown. Ayden Spagnolo added six tackles, Anthony Firetto five tackles with a sack and Demir Sanford six tackles, with two sacks.
