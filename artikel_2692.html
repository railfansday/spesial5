‘I don’t want to hear’: Donovan McNabb hates what people are saying about Eagles QB Joe Milton

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Joe Milton's passes have become a hot topic of conversation lately.

The quarterback started back-to-back games for the Philadelphia Eagles against the New York Giants and the Pittsburgh Steelers, and he impressed in both games.

However, one criticism of Milton has been that he throws the ball too hard. Eagles legend Donovan McNabb is having none of that.

The game between the Philadelphia Eagles and the Pittsburgh Steelers was full of drama.

DK Metcalf's ejection made all the headlines, but Milton's performance as the quarterback was very impressive too, especially his arm strength.

However, some felt that the 26-year-old is perhaps too strong, because one of his passes, in particular, flew over DeVonta Smith's head.

The question everyone started asking then was if Milton was throwing the ball too hard, but McNabb has claimed that he doesn't want to listen to that theory.

He said on Sports Radio 94 WIP: "I hate it when people always tried to use that label with me. And then somebody else says they throw a catchable ball.

"Hey, man, I get paid to throw it. You get paid to catch the ball. So I don't want to hear nothing about you throwing the ball too hard. I think for receivers, they'll tell you the same thing. They got to make the catch, and so that's just something that they have to get comfortable with him, and that's going to continue to grow as well.

"But yes, he does have a strong arm. That's kind of one of his strengths, his arm strength. So I just think for Joe, it's just getting more and more opportunities with these guys."

Smith claimed after the game that even he didn't think it was possible that he could get overthrown, but Milton did quite comfortably.

Of course, you would want your quarterback to be perfectly accurate with his deep passes, but having a very strong arm is never a bad thing.

It's now up to Milton and other receivers like Smith to form a relationship where both players understand each other. Once that happens, they could become a force.
