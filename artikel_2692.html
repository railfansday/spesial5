What’s next for the Detroit Lions’ defense after Aidan Hutchinson’s injury?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Detroit Lions are 7-2 and in the driver's seat of the NFC North. They're on a five-game winning streak; they've yet to lose a divisional game despite it being an extremely strong set of teams, and they are in the midst of playing their best football right now.

That's all the good news. The bad news is that Aidan Hutchinson likely suffered a season-ending injury on Sunday, and it comes two weeks after the Lions lost another star captain, Jack Campbell, to injury.

On this week's PODcast—where Erik Schlitt takes my place amongst the panel—we discuss everything that transpired during Sunday's Detroit Lions vs. New England Patriots game in Munich, Germany. We talk about Amon-Ra St. Brown magical weekend, Jahmyr Gibbs' continued path to MVP and Offensive Player of the Year consideration, and an inspired performance on the defense.

But for the second half of the show, the fellas mostly talk about the Hutchinson injury fallout. Who will need to step up on the defensive line? Who will get more opportunities? What kind of formations and schematic changes will defensive coordinator Kelvin Sheppard consider moving forward? And can the health and improvement of the secondary help mitigate some of the damage?

Check it all out on this week's show. I'll be back on Tuesday for the Midweek Mailbag podcast.
