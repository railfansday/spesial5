Barca fight back to beat Villarreal as Inter held to draw by Atalanta

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Barcelona remain top of LaLiga after they fought back from two goals down to beat Villarreal in a 3-2 win where both teams finished with 10 men.

Tajon Buchanan and Tani Oluwaseyi stunned the Nou Camp with two goals inside the opening second minutes to give the visitors a healthy half-time lead.

Gerard Martin reduced the deficit in the 57th minute and Fermin Lopez levelled four minutes later before the turnaround was completed by a Pau Navarro own goal, but Lopez received a red card soon after and Villarreal had Navarro sent off in stoppage time.

Atletico Madrid are five points behind leaders Barca after a 3-0 victory at Getafe.

Alex Baena's powerful long-range strike broke the deadlock after 10 minutes before Johnny Cardoso made it 2-0 in the 30th minute.

Alejandro Grimaldo wrapped up the points with a penalty in stoppage time to make it eight league wins in nine for Diego Simeone's men.

Francesco Pio Esposito's hat-trick could not help Inter Milan win at Atalanta to leave the Serie A champions three points off early pace-setters Lazio.

Esposito's treble put Inter 3-1 up before Petar Sucic's spot-kick made it 4-2, but late goals by Charles De Ketelaere and Franck Kessie earned Atalanta a point from the eight-goal thriller.

AC Milan thrashed Frosinone 5-1 with Alphadjo Cisse scoring a brace for Ruben Amorim's team at San Siro while Christian Pulisic was also on target.

Newcastle loanee Nick Woltemade scored in Juventus' 4-0 victory at home to Venezia.

Liam Rosenior's Paris FC are second and three points behind Paris St Germain after a 4-0 victory at home to Angers.

Patrick Zabi, Luca Koleosho, Maxime Lopez and Diego Coppola scored in a scintillating second-half display under the former Chelsea boss.

Marseille are down in fifth after they squandered a two-goal advantage to lose 3-2 at home to Le Mans with Jean Vercruysee scoring a stoppage-time winner.
