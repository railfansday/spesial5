SEC Week 12 power rankings: Georgia remains No. 1, Texas takes No. 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Multiple upsets across the Southeastern Conference cost many teams spots in this week's football power rankings, with just two weeks left in the regular season.

Georgia remains in the top spot as they continue to steamroll their remaining opponents. The Bulldogs secured another blowout victory, demolishing No. 22 Missouri, 48-21, in Athens on Saturday. Right behind the Bulldogs are the Texas Longhorns, coming off their thrilling 22-21 win over No. 21 LSU, notching their fifth AP Top 25 win of the 2026 season.

The Tennessee Volunteers were bumped down to No. 3 with their 22-7 win over Texas A&M, followed by Alabama at No. 4 with their 21-6 win at Vanderbilt. Despite their Week 11 loss to Florida at Kroger Field, the Kentucky Wildcats round out the top five.
