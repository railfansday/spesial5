Aaron Rodgers took his usual practice reps on Tuesday, did his usual midweek press conference

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

For the Steelers, Tuesday unfolded in the usual way that it would unfold with a game coming on Friday — even if late Tuesday night included a highly unexpected curveball.

With the sudden and stunning benching of quarterback Aaron Rodgers happening via a tweet posted by the Steelers at 11:10 p.m. ET, it’s fair to take a look at how the day before that unfolded.

The Steelers had what would have been their usual Wednesday practice on Tuesday. And while it was a walkthrough, Rodgers was not listed on the injury report — either with an injury or as a limited or non-participant due to rest. This means that he took the full amount of his normal reps as the team’s starter.

Rodgers also conducted the usual starting quarterback midweek press conference.

He arguably seemed to be wet-eyed early in the session, as if he already knew what was coming and that it could potentially be the last traditional starting-quarterback press conference of his career.

Near the end of the availability, Rodgers took a not-so-subtle jab at the organization’s assessment of receiver Max Hurleman, who was called up from the practice squad against the Eagles and had six catches for 61 yards and a touchdown in 22 of 71 offensive snaps in his regular-season debut.

“I think it’s as much for him as it is for everybody else upstairs making decisions,” Rodgers said regarding the impact of Hurleman’s performance. “You know, I think everybody felt before the injury in camp that he was a 53[-man roster] guy. I know the guys in our room did, for sure. So for him to go out there and finally get elevated and go out and play the way he played, was not surprising to any of us, but great for his confidence, and great reminder for the folks upstairs what kind of player he is.”

While Rodgers has in the past had far more pointed things to say about the front office (specifically when he played for the Packers), it’s another fact that gets added to the overall stew of circumstances that resulted in a normal workday becoming a very abnormal eleventh hour.
