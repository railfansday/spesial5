Kenneth Walker won't practice Wednesday with groin injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Kenneth Walker’s groin injury will keep him out for at least another day.

Head coach Andy Reid told reporters in his press conference that the running back is one of a few players who won’t practice on Wednesday.

“He’s got a sore groin, so it’s getting better. But with those … you don’t want to do something stupid, going out there,” Reid said. “It’s still bugging him a little bit. But he’s feeling a lot better than he was.

“We’ll just see. It’s a day-to-day thing, so we’ll see how it goes.”

Walker did not practice last Friday before missing Sunday’s victory over the Falcons. Rookie Emmett Johnson rushed for 101 yards and had six catches for 70 yards with two touchdowns in Walker’s stead.

Additionally, Reid said right tackle Jaylon Moore (ankle) and right guard Trey Smith (concussion protocol) will not practice on Wednesday.

The full first injury report of the week for Kansas City will be released later in the day.
