Rams move ahead 20-13 on Blake Corum TD

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Rams let Blake Corum do most of the heavy lifting on their opening drive of the second half.

Corum ran nine time for 35 yards during a 15-play Rams drive and the final yard he picked up was a touchdown that broke a tie and put the Rams up 13-10. The drive opened the third quarter and ate up eight minutes of playing time.

The Rams faced three third downs on the drive and had Matthew Stafford throw to wide receiver Konata Mumpfield each time. The first two were completions that picked up 30 yards and the third was initially ruled a touchdown on the field. Replays showed Mumpfield did not have control of the ball in the end zone, but the Rams still got a first down due to a defensive holding penalty on the day.

Corum has 16 carries for 58 yards and Mumpfield has four catches for 69 yards.
