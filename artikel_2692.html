Wednesday Cannon Fodder: Champions League match day

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Good morning, TSFers! I'm a bit tired from following election results last night. But we move. We've got Arsenal in the Champions League to attend to this afternoon / evening. The squad is in Prague to face Slavia Praha, kickoff at 3:00 pm ET.

Yesterday's matches went mostly as you'd expect. Stuttgart was disappointing in a 2-0 loss to Galatasaray, but as we always point out, it's tough to win away in the CL. Manchester United were trailing 2-0 at home to Roma at halftime but came roaring back with three goals in the second half. Matheus Cunha tallied what they're calling a Diego Costa hat trick — two goals and a red card (for two yellows).
