San Jose Sea Lions unveiled as CA's 1st pro women's hockey team

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Professional Women's Hockey League officially unveiled the identity of its newest Bay Area franchise Tuesday, introducing the San Jose Sea Lions as California's first PWHL team.

The Sea Lions are one of four expansion clubs joining the league for the 2026-27 season and will play their home games at SAP Center alongside the NHL's San Jose Sharks. San Jose leaders say the franchise arrives at a time of unprecedented growth for women's sports.

"Women's sports are having a huge, well-deserved moment," Mayor Matt Mahan said during Tuesday's unveiling event, pointing to the growing popularity of professional women's leagues across the country.

The announcement comes on the heels of the Golden State Valkyries' rapid rise in the Bay Area, including a championship run and sellout crowds at Chase Center, underscoring the region's appetite for women's professional sports.

With their inaugural season approaching, players say they're eager to build a connection with local fans.

"We can't wait to take the ice in the orange and blue as the San Jose Sea Lions and give you something all to cheer for," goaltender Tia Chan said.

Developed by women-led PWHL design teams, the Sea Lions logo reflects the Bay Area's coastal roots.

The name also carries historical significance, honoring the 1946 San Francisco Sea Lions of the West Coast Negro Baseball Association, a Negro League-era team that helped break barriers in professional sports by adding pioneering athlete Toni Stone to its roster.

The Sea Lions are scheduled to open their inaugural season at home on Dec. 5, with tickets already on sale.
