NFL predictions: Scores and picks for Week 11 between the Buccaneers and Lions

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Tampa Bay Buccaneers return from their bye week looking to get back into the win column as they travel to Detroit to take on the Lions in Week 11.

Tampa Bay (3-6) enters Sunday's matchup following a disappointing 27-24 loss to the Chicago Bears. The Buccaneers have struggled with consistency throughout the season, and questions surrounding quarterback Baker Mayfield's health have added another challenge heading into a critical stretch of games.

Detroit (7-2), meanwhile, is riding a five-game winning streak after defeating the New England Patriots 35-10 in Germany last week. The Lions will look to continue their momentum at home against a Buccaneers team desperate to keep pace in the NFC South.

Unlike the previous matchup against Chicago, the Bucs Nation staff is divided on this week's outcome. Two writers believe Tampa Bay can pull off the road upset, while the other two see Detroit extending its winning streak.

While oddsmakers over at FanDuel are giving Detroit a 3.5-point advantage, both Mike and Gil are predicting a three-point victory for Tampa Bay, while Evan expects a closely contested game that ultimately favors Detroit. Will, however, sees the Lions having a much easier time, predicting a 12-point victory over the Buccaneers.

One of the biggest questions heading into Sunday's matchup is Mayfield's availability. The veteran quarterback is listed as questionable with foot and right thumb injuries, although there is optimism he could take the field against Detroit.

Can the Buccaneers overcome their injury concerns and pull off the upset, or will the Lions continue their winning streak and hand Tampa Bay its seventh loss of the season?

The Buccaneers and Lions kick off Sunday at 1:00 p.m. ET on CBS.
