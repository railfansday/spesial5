Liquidia: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Its next-generation inhaler is being tested in pulmonary hypertension tied to COPD, a market beyond the core PAH franchise.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 27, 2026 at 4:06PM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 10, 2026, Liquidia (LQDA +2.36%) reported net product sales of $222.7 million for its inhaled hypertension treatment, YUTREPIA. Resolving litigation overhangs clears the pathway for expanding prescriber adoption and advancing clinical pipeline programs. Three things management said stand out.

Net product sales grew 31% quarter over quarter, anchored by $212.2 million in direct patient demand. Operational leverage improved as selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses fell to 26% of revenue.

Competitor communications led to short-term prescriber hesitation, but referral rates stabilized, and monthly patient shipments reached launch highs. Patient conversion rates remained steady because payers and specialty pharmacies maintained existing coverage without requiring product switches.

Liquidia is advancing L606, an extended-release liposomal treprostinil delivered twice daily through a breath-actuated nebulizer, a device that turns liquid medication into mist. Development targets pulmonary hypertension associated with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (PH-COPD), a progressive lung condition, alongside pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH), a high blood pressure condition in lung arteries.

Prescription volumes continue to build across expanding physician networks, and management isn't hiding its satisfaction with the momentum.

Dosing flexibility and 94% gross margins drive durable operational leverage. Management is strategically tracking referral recovery, conversion stability, and trial enrollment across new indications.

Liquidia ended the quarter with $396.1 million in cash before its settlement obligations, most of which are payable in cash through April 2027.
