This Under-the-Radar Crypto Stock Could Surprise Everyone

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Asset tokenization could provide a huge boost to the future growth prospects of Securitize.

By Dominic Basulto – Nov 21, 2026 at 8:30PM EST

If you only look at a stock chart for Securitize (SECZ -7.45%), you might miss the bigger picture.

Yes, Securitize is down 47% since it went public in July. But there's a fast-expanding financial trend that could significantly move the dial for Securitize in the years ahead: real-world asset tokenization.

Asset tokenization is taking off by leaps and bounds. The value of the tokenized equities market is now $3.8 billion, and Securitize is one of the key reasons why. The company is at the forefront of asset tokenization, the process of transforming real-world assets (such as stocks and bonds) into digital assets that reside on the blockchain.

By all accounts, asset tokenization could be a multitrillion-dollar market opportunity. There are simply too many cost savings and efficiency gains to be had from tokenization. And U.S. regulators, using a new "innovation exemption," have now cleared the way for tokenized equities to begin trading on exchanges and blockchain trading platforms.

At some point in the future, 24/7 trading of digital blockchain assets will become the norm rather than the exception. For that reason, analysts project strong future growth ahead for Securitize.

Moreover, leading investors are beginning to buy into Securitize for their portfolios and funds. One of those is Cathie Wood of Ark Invest. She has been buying Securitize for Ark Invest's ETFs and has also partnered with the company on tokenizing one of its venture funds.

That said, there are no guarantees that asset tokenization will become as big as some people think. But if this financial trend delivers even a modicum of the upside promised, Securitize could be a big winner going forward.
