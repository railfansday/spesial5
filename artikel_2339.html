Chelsea have trump card in race with Arsenal, Brighton and Aston Villa for Bayern wonderkid

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

As widely reported in recent months, Leonard Prescott is expected to leave Bayern Munich on a free transfer when his contract expires in the summer of 2027, with negotiations over a new deal having reached a stalemate.

According to Sky Germany, a number of English clubs are monitoring the situation, including Chelsea, Arsenal, Brighton & Hove Albion and Aston Villa.

The German broadcaster further reports that Chelsea have a potential trump card up their sleeve, as they could tempt the youngster with the prospect of regular playing time on loan at Racing Strasbourg, the London outfit's sister club.

Prescott, who is currently sidelined with a meniscus injury, has made a total of 20 appearances for Bayern's U19 team, according to Transfermarkt.He was included in the senior squad for several matches last season.

The 17-year-old is also a German youth international and has made nine appearances for the U17 national team.
