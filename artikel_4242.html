Bill & Ted Sidescroller to Launch Backerkit Campaign in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Daniel is a lifelong gamer with a love for various genres and gameplay styles who enjoys exploring games and playing from various angles.

For the uninitiated, Bill & Ted’s Excellent Adventure was a 1989 sci-fi comedy starring Alex Winter and Keanu Reeves as the titular duo of teenage weirdos who get a chance to travel through time, meet famous historical figures, and make mild nuisances of themselves. It also co-starred my personal hero and one of the greatest comedians of all time, the late George Carlin, as their host in a time-traveling phone booth, Rufus.

The film, as well as its two sequels, remains one of the hallmarks of late 80s, early 90s pop culture, with any proclamation of “excellent” being a great way to make millennials spontaneously start air guitar-ing. Outside its films, the IP pops up in mildly odd places every now and then, including, apparently, a brand-new video game.

Indie developer Art of Play Interactive announced today that it’s developing a brand-new Bill & Ted game, Bill & Ted’s Excellent Adventure Arcade, a hybrid sidescrolling beat ‘em up and minigame collection based on the first film. This game is slated to be bankrolled by a Backerkit campaign, which is planned to launch in February 2027 with a handful of physical rewards available for early backers, as well as presently unannounced other swag like collectibles and apparel.

According to the game’s Backerkit and Steam pages, Bill & Ted’s Excellent Adventure Arcade is meant to be a playable version of the original film, starting with Rufus whisking the boys away into history in the time-traveling phone booth and running them through 15 levels as they try to meet famous folks for their history report. The game will feature audio from Alex Winter, Keanu Reeves, and George Carlin ripped directly from the film’s original recordings. It’s all original stuff, by the way; the Backerkit page stresses that this project will not use any generative AI whatsoever.

The actual game seems to switch off between side-scrolling beat ‘em up and assorted minigames. Bill and Ted travel through the time periods, getting into scuffles or running away from them, and when they encounter a famous historical figure, you play minigames to earn their trust and get them to come with you, such as battling Beethoven in a music duel or beating Napoleon in an ice cream-eating contest.

The game is meant to be played in local co-op, one character per player, though you can also play it solo and assume control over both Bill and Ted simultaneously. After beating the game’s main story, you’ll unlock arcade mode for faster playthroughs, as well as the individual mini-games for piecemeal enjoyment.

As you often see for these kinds of campaigns, and especially those for popular IPs from the 80s, the Backerkit campaign for Bill & Ted’s Excellent Adventure Arcade is planned to be accompanied by assorted physical rewards for those who get in on the ground floor.

To start, everyone who backs the campaign will receive a free Rufus Metallic Phone Booth card; those who reserve their pledge for $5 will receive additional Bill and Ted metallic cards, and everyone who backs in the first 48 hours will receive a fourth metallic card featuring the “historical dudes.” These four cards can be built together with the included top and bottom pieces to create your own little phone booth model. According to the Backerkit page, there will be additional incentives during the campaign proper, including exclusive merch, apparel, and other goodies.

The game is aiming for digital release on Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, and PC via Steam. Additionally, the game will have a limited physical release, with boxes for the Switch and PS5 versions, and even a physical CD-ROM for the PC version. There also seems to be a knock-off SNES cartridge in the works, though I’m assuming it’s just a prop and won’t actually run on a SNES.
