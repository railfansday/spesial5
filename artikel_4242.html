How To Unlock All Achievements In Probably Stolen

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

There are a ton of ways to play Probably Stolen, with plenty of lore and secrets to explore throughout the Lower Levels. There's more than enough to keep anyone playing for more than one playthrough to find out about each faction, learn about the reasoning behind the Rebellion, and keep an eye on the mysterious Joe Wild and his associates.

If you're looking to 100% Probably Stolen, you'll have to face some of its hardest challenges including having the most beautiful store possible, earning a ridiculous number of credits, and making all the right (or wrong) friends. Here's everything you need to know about getting every achievement in the game.

There are 21 total achievements in Probably Stolen that you can unlock throughout the game. About 14 of these achievements can be unlocked through a normal playthrough of the game to the end of its story, while three of them require special starting conditions. The rest require either hitting a particular deadline (such as getting the deed to the shop before day 50) or a particularly high level of some in-game stat (level 10 reputation, 100,000 credits in the bank, etc.).

There are a few achievements that you'll need to restart the game to get, so it's not possible to 100% Probably Stolen in one playthrough. For example, you can (if you're very lucky or very determined) get the achievements for starting the game on the hardest scenario and the hardest difficulty at the same time as refusing to sell contraband or stolen items, but you can't do this and also finish the tutorial.

Minimum, you'll need to replay at least once to unlock all of the achievements, though it's probably easier to do multiple playthroughs. Consider breaking it down into a set of runs based on categories of achievements. Play through normally first to complete the tutorial, learn the mechanics, and get all of the basic achievements. Then play a run focused specifically on the shop's quality, then on reputation, etc. until you've gotten all of the more advanced achievements completed. You might then do specific challenge playthroughs for the most difficult achievements such as From Nothing and From Nothing, The Hard Way. How you break down your runs and their focuses will come down to your play style and how easy certain mechanics are for you to focus on simultaneously.

Here's how I'd recommend breaking down your playthroughs:

A Probably Stolen playthrough focused on the main storyline can take roughly 20 hours to complete if you're taking it at a normal pace. The game does also have some randomness to it, which can mean you might end up having to restart an achievement run or get an unexpected boon that helps you get something done faster. Due to this randomness, and the fact that you need to restart the game anyway multiple times to get all of the achievements, it may take anywhere from 50 to 160 hours to get all 21 achievements in Probably Stolen.

Unlock every achievement and show off while you Gamble With Your Friends

Most of the achievements for Probably Stolen can be done with a normally paced playthrough save for the challenge requirements, though your playthrough is probably going to be pretty long.

Automatically unlocks when you finish the tutorial on day 15.

Start a day with more than 10,000 credits in estimated total wealth.

Sell enough products and appraise enough items to make sure that you have 10,000 credits in your bank after rent and fees like insurance and cleaning.

Change the label of any item using the labeler.

Get the labeler from The Mentor during the tutorial and drag it over any counterfeit item or contraband substance to change its label (real versus counterfeit, street grade versus lab grade, etc.).

Unlock Appraisals on day eight and keep your certificate in the display case. Use the magnifying glass and appropriate handbooks from the Board to properly identify and value appraisal items. Follow the handbook exactly to get 10 perfect scores.

Buy the business permit from the Station Trade Board Official when he comes to your counter.

Own 10 machines simultaneously at the start of the day.

Get 10 machines, either from people at the counter or through Dr. Jackson's upgrade shop and put them in your inventory. A machine bay can help you manage inventory space for this.

Buy the deed to the store from The Landlord with 12,000 credits in Mark Up difficulty. You can either buy it outright or get a mortgage through The Loan Shark with a 2,500 credit down payment and pay the rest off slowly over time. The price of the property goes up after day 50 at a rate of 25 credits a day.

Reach maximum reputation with any faction.

Use bargaining, intel, and your network to boost your relationship with the Revolution, Security, the Lower Levels, the Upper Levels, the Black Market, or the Sector 13 Cartel. Security is fairly easy to keep on good terms with if you avoid the Black Market, the Revolution, and the Cartell, and vice versa.

Use the shotgun and identification guide you get on day 12 to shoot 10 different augmented people. The initial augs are fairly easy to spot — they'll have strange, wide-open eyes, will move erratically or be too still, and will talk strangely, often robotically. They'll also usually be smiling strangely.

You can get scratchers from people selling to the store or scavenging. Scratch through any you get for the chance at a jackpot.

Start a day with more than 100,000 credits in estimated total wealth.

Sell enough products and appraise enough items to make sure that you have 100,000 credits in your bank after rent and fees like insurance and cleaning.

Get the Retired Chemist perk from Joe Wild and acquire the machines and supplies necessary to make lab-grown items (Chemical Supplies, Composition Spectrometer, Chemical Equipment including the Blender, Heating Plate, and Chem Finisher, as well as various ingredients). Follow a recipe exactly to produce a Lab Pure narcotic — either Nomore, Dream Dust, or Phanta.

Start a new playthrough using the Rat Wrangler Scenario. This isn't actually hidden anymore, and is available right when you start the game.

Use Modules and the Module Bay Expansion Kit to upgrade any machine to +250 quality. You can do this with any of the standard machines (Alarm System, Cryptographic Desequencer, DNA Manipulator, Furnace, Hydroponic System, Moisture Farm, 3D Printer, Mirage Projector, Water Purifier, and AgeWell).

Make 12,000 credits and buy the deed from the Landlord, or pay off the mortgage from the Loan Shark, before day 50.

Start a day with 1,000 store attractiveness.

Use Joe Wild's network and the janitorial service to get your store to 1,000 attractiveness. You'll need to keep it clean with no trash, invest in the Store Renovations (I, II, and III), and get a Mirage Projector that's powered.

Produce a Huge Nutrifruit from the Hydroponic.

Set up a Hydroponic Farm with a Nutrifruit seed in it. Keep the plant at 100% health and feed it Pure Water with added mineral pills (around two should do it).

Start and subsequently win a new game with the Rock Bottom scenario, which starts you with no credits, no introduction (or tutorial), and none of the items you would normally receive in the tutorial (magnifier, labeler, bandage, and fanny pack).

Win the game without selling a stolen or contraband item.

On any difficulty or starting mode, play through the entire game until you hit an ending without selling any stolen or contraband goods; either throw them away or refuse to buy them in the first place. You can also choose to report anyone carrying contraband or counterfeit goods to Security, but this will make the Revolution and other factions dislike you.

Win the game starting from Rock Bottom on hard mode.

Start and subsequently win a new game with the Rock Bottom scenario, but this time you're also on the Thin Margins difficulty, which is hard mode. In this mode, you have no retail markup when selling items, your rent increases by 10% per week, the nightly service fees, property costs, and permit costs are higher, upgrades cost 50% more, suppliers charge you more, your reputation grows at only 75% of the normal rate while it can be lost at 125% the normal rate, and finally, Joe Wild is much harder to gain favor with. This is the second most challenging version of the game behind Red Ink difficulty.
