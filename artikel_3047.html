Management prioritized risk discipline and credit tightening, which resulted in reduced loan facilitation volumes and a significant reduction in user acquisition spending. While domestic operations faced headwinds from a national regulatory campaign targeting the collection industry, the company continued to invest in AI-driven efficiency tools and its overseas expansion strategy across Europe, Latin America, and Southeast Asia.
