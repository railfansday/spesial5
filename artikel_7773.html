Firm partners in Silicon Valley paid more than NY partners, new survey shows

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

In a reversal that could reflect the artificial intelligence-fueled technology industry boom, law firm partners in Silicon Valley earned more on average than partners in New York last year, according to a new survey. (Image from Shutterstock)

In a reversal that could reflect the artificial intelligence-fueled technology industry boom, law firm partners in Silicon Valley earned more on average than partners in New York last year, according to a new survey.

Compensation for Silicon Valley partners was $2.57 million on average in ​2025, 4% more than partners in New York, according to a survey of large firms.

The survey was released this week by legal recruiting agency Major, Lindsey & Africa and included more than 1,000 respondents, according to a story by Reuters.

Average partner compensation throughout the top 200 largest U.S. firms increased 10% to $1.55 million since 2024. In 2016, ​the figure was $877,000.

The survey also revealed that average hourly billing rate increased by 11%, from $1,114 in ⁠2024 to $1,242, according to Reuters.
