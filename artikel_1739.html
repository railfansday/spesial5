Liverpool boost as Andoni Iraola confirms Giovanni Leoni and Conor Bradley injury return dates

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Giovanni Leoni and Conor Bradley have both returned to Liverpool training, with potential for them both to return within weeks.

Bradley has been sidelined since suffering a serious knee injury against Arsenal back in January.

Leoni, meanwhile, was a victim of profoundly bad luck when he tore his ACL on his debut for the Reds in September 2025, just weeks after signing from Parma.

But after months of rehabilitation, Iraola has confirmed that both men resumed training with the Liverpool first team during the November international break and could soon be in contention for competitive play.

“Very good news from both sides,” Iraola confirmed on Friday.

“I think the international break was the best moment for us to try to introduce them slowly, slowly with the rest of the players. They have done this and now even in the last part of the week they have already started doing things with the team.

“Obviously they are still not available but it’s very good news that they are able to do things with the group.”

He continued, adding that Liverpool would be gentle with the pair’s reintegration as they look to avoid setbacks.

“It’s true that they have to be loaded carefully – they don’t train every day with us. One day, yes. The next day, not. The next day, yes. We are in the last part of this process, a long process.

“But both of them are looking good and I hope they can play in some weeks.”

It is said Bradley and Leoni have become close friends as they went through their rehabilitation together at Liverpool’s Kirkby training ground.

Iraola also confirmed Jeremie Frimpong, Ryan Gravenberch and Dominik Szoboszlai are all in line to play a role as Liverpool host Manchester United on Sunday.
