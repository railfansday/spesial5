Chris Wilder says, 'sometimes it's bigger than just the draw' as Sheffield United hold Southampton

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Saints comfortably outplayed the Blades in the first half-hour and deservedly took the lead through Lewis Dobbin. But from there, the hosts took the initiative and made far better use of the ball than Southampton did, even if it took until the 90th minute for Sydie Peck to find the equaliser.

But Wilder was proud of his team's efforts against a Championship side he greatly respects.

"I was proud of the players today for the way they really stood up to the challenge," he commented. "I thought when the bullets were flying, they were there.

"They took the ball in the first half, they just gave the ball away too cheaply in the first 20, 25 minutes.

"If you allow really good players the opportunity to get on the ball they'll dictate the game, which they did.

"But I was absolutely delighted with it. I'm proud of the players, the way they got themselves back in the game.

"Tactically, we got ourselves back in the game and sorted that situation out and really drove and dominated, and everybody stepped into the game."

Michael Cooper made a crucial save from substitute Samuel Edozie in stoppage time to preserve the point, and Wilder felt it was no less than his side deserved.

"I think it would have been the cruellest of cruel blows if they'd have nicked a winner, and I think we definitely deserve something from the game, with the attitude, the way we played, and how brave we were," said Wilder.

"It was a difficult (first) 25 minutes for the players to deal with, but I wanted them to show courage, and they all did, they all stepped up to it, and we had the performance second half that was worthy of a result against the top side.

"I don't think anybody's done that to them all season, I don't think anybody's done that since Tonda (Eckert) took over.

"Getting back on the coach, they might say they had an opportunity to win it, which they did, when the left-back just appeared from nowhere, coming off the pitch and we get caught up in the game.

"You're all caught up in the emotion of the game, and it was an emotional game.

"I want us to play with that emotion, and 100 per cent as soon as the ball went in the back of the net, with Sydie's finish it was, we want to go for a win.

"When the boy counter-attacks off the left, and he finds Finn Azaz, who's a top player, and he finds a pass, you're thinking really, 'Come on, we deserve a little bit better than that.'

"Sometimes, when you look back on draws, it's bigger than just the draw, with the performance against a side of the quality that we played against."
