Moretto claims Camarda’s ‘real exit door’ is abroad rather than in Italy: “Trying a different experience”

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

There’s been a lot said about Francesco Camarda’s future in recent months and it seems AC Milan are getting closer to a decision.

Put simply, Milan are in the same situation that they were a few years ago. Camarda isn’t playing and when he does, he’s not really getting the most out of games. So, another loan will be sought in the mercato.

His time with Lecce previously wasn’t exactly fruitful, but he did grab a first Serie A goal, which helped. However, he’s still without a competitive goal for the Rossoneri and his minutes aren’t regular. In other words, it’s looking like another six months wasted.

So, he’ll leave the nest in January once again.

Talk of a move abroad has been really common recently, and it seems increasingly likely that his next test will be abroad. However, there are no inklings about where it may be, even in Matteo Moretto’s latest update on Fabrizio Romano’s YouTube channel, as MilanNews relays.

“AC Milan ‘s decision has essentially already been made: they want the boy to be able to play consistently and consistently, trying a different experience.

“There’s been a lot of talk about Cagliari, there’s been a lot of talk about Genoa, there’s been a lot of talk about Italy, but as I’ve said in recent days, keep an eye on options that lead abroad.

“It’s true that there are Italian teams interested in Camarda, but as of today, I can’t see Genoa and Cagliari pursuing the player, despite preliminary discussions. Cagliari, in particular, had inquired about the terms, but nothing more and nothing less.

“This solution, from what I understand, is unlikely to go ahead. I might keep a small door open to Italy for you, but the real door is a door that leads abroad.

“Keep an eye on foreign championships, keep an eye on championships and teams that give confidence to young people, that let young people play and that give young people space. So, as of today, this is the line on Camarda.”
