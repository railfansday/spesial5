WhatsApp now lets you schedule messages in group and individual chats

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

WhatsApp on Wednesday started rolling out a much-sought-after feature: the ability to schedule messages in both individual and group chats.

The feature is rolling out globally to all users across iOS and Android. Once it becomes available, you can type a message and long-press the send button to schedule it. You can choose to send a message anywhere between 10 minutes and 14 days later.

Users will be able to see all their scheduled messages and delete them if needed. It’s not clear if there is a way to edit the schedule of a message.

Notably, scheduled messages will be sent even if your phone is off or outside network coverage. You can schedule messages containing text, photos, and videos.

The feature could prove useful for wishing someone a happy birthday on time or chatting with people across time zones. Businesses may also find it easier to schedule texts and updates to their customers, and people who use the app to coordinate work will likely find themselves using scheduled texts to update their teams.

WhatsApp rivals like Telegram and Signal have had the ability to schedule messages for a while now, so the Meta-owned chat app is late in rolling it out.
