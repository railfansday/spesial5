Playtronic Games is getting ready to launch Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair: Definitive Edition as a free update on PlayStation 5, Switch 2, and PC on December 15th. Unfortunately for Xbox owners, the developer says it “can’t commit to any additional platforms for the foreseeable future.” In a statement posted on X, Playtronic said that it understands the frustration some players may feel, but that bringing the Definitive Edition to Xbox just isn’t viable “given the technological challenges and costs,” and reminded people that they’re a small team.

Some, like The Game Business editor-in-chief Christopher Dring, have speculated that Xbox’s smaller install base has made it harder for some indie devs to “financially justify” launching on Xbox. Others, like John Linneman of Digital Foundry, have countered that Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair: Definitive Edition is ultimately a minor update and pointed out that the Xbox Series X version was already available in native 4K, unlike the PS4 and Switch versions.
