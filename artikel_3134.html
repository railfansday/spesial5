New Era Energy & Digital, Inc. (NUAI +16.74%) reported progress on its flagship Texas Critical Data Centers project, transitioning to a self-execution model for Phase 1 delivery. Management stated that the company has secured power outside the standard ERCOT Batch Zero process, providing a path to service by late 2027. The company entered into a nonbinding letter of intent with a global technology firm for both Phase 1 and Phase 2, while concurrently strengthening its balance sheet through a private placement. The development strategy emphasizes modular construction to reduce capital outlays and an islanded power solution for future phases to mitigate grid-related constraints.
