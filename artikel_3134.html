Hayward: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Pricing discipline and manufacturing shifts may help protect margins even as new pool construction remains depressed.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 28, 2026, Hayward (HAYW -0.48%) announced raised full-year guidance as net sales grew 6% year over year. An expanding installed pool base powers steady equipment replacement even during severe real estate contractions.

The aftermarket, which includes replacement parts and maintenance equipment for existing pools, generates more than 85% of net sales. Essential maintenance protects home values and keeps water safe, shielding revenue when homebuilders construct fewer pools.

Factory productivity improvements and targeted price adjustments helped offset rising input expenses for raw materials. Operating leverage from North American volume growth enabled the business to protect profitability while expanding investment in research and development.

After shifting U.S.-bound manufacturing back home, management directed its Wuxi, China facility toward European supply routes. This move repurposes a low-cost plant to improve operating margins in overseas markets.

Early buy order momentum and balanced distributor inventory positions support expanding market share entering the upcoming season. The company maintains structural advantages in proprietary automation technology and deep dealer relationships while managing input cost pressures.

Management relies on operational execution and market expansion to drive durable long-term performance.
