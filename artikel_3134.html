Ready Capital: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Management has stopped selling distressed commercial real estate loans at steep discounts and is now working to stabilize nonperforming loans and foreclosed properties first.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 6, 2026, Ready Capital (RC -2.88%) announced that it substantially completed its balance sheet repositioning and liquidity plan. The real estate finance company absorbed significant book value declines to eliminate debt and protect capital.

Generating $2.3 billion in liquidity required hard trade-offs, including dividend cuts and book value write-downs. With corporate debt paid down, the company is shifting from defensive cash conservation to earnings recovery.

The Small Business Administration 7(a) program, or SBA 7(a), provides government-guaranteed small business loans that generate gain-on-sale income while requiring minimal capital. Expanding origination volume offers immediate quarterly revenue gains to offset commercial real estate drags.

Management stopped selling distressed commercial real estate (CRE) loans at steep discounts to raise quick cash. The company is now actively managing nonperforming loans and real estate owned, or REO properties acquired through foreclosure, to stabilize net operating income before seeking exits.

Expense reductions take center stage as Ready Capital matches operational spending to a smaller balance sheet. The company leverages its high-yielding loan platforms and patient property management to rebuild cash flow while watching noncore business sales.

Recycling legacy assets and expanding high-return channels remain critical to bringing distributable earnings back to breakeven.
