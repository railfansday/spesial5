AST SpaceMobile Is 78% Below Its High and Pushed Its Launch Target to 2027. Here’s the Exact Price Where I’d Finally Buy.

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The space stock could sink to the low teens in the next market crash.

10 stocks we like better than AST SpaceMobile ›

AST SpaceMobile's (ASTS -2.72%) stock closed at a record high of $133.09 per share on May 28, 2026. But today, the satellite maker's stock trades at about $29 per share.

AST produces low-Earth orbit (LEO) satellites for cellular connectivity. It helps wireless carriers, including AT&T (T -0.61%) and Verizon (VZ -0.48%), expand their networks to remote areas.

AST has already launched 13 of its BlueBird satellites, but it won't generate stable recurring commercial revenue until it launches at least 45 satellites. AST originally planned to reach that milestone by the end of 2026, but it pushed that target back to 2027 earlier this year.

The second major shock was SpaceX's (SPCX +0.45%) $8 billion acquisition of Grain Management's nationwide 800 MHz low-band spectrum. That spectrum, which once belonged to T-Mobile (TMUS -1.07%), could turn Starlink into a major wireless carrier.

From 2026 to 2028, analysts expect AST's revenue to surge from $166 million to $1.65 billion as it expands its constellation toward its long-term goal of 248 satellites.

They also expect its adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) to turn positive at $1.20 billion in the final year. With an enterprise value of $10 billion, AST's stock already trades at seven times that bullish estimate.

Therefore, I wouldn't be surprised if it gets cut in half to the mid-teens in the next market crash. But if that happens, I'd buy some shares as a long-term play on space-based cellular networks.
