CTO Realty: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Acquisitions below replacement cost and double-digit structured yields show how CTO is working to extract value beyond traditional leasing.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 28, 2026, CTO Realty (CTO +0.10%) announced that it raised its full-year guidance after deploying $472 million into investments. For retail real estate investors, cash flow growth hinges on filling vacant spaces quickly and putting capital into high-yielding properties. The company's execution shows how active asset management turns vacant storefronts into durable rental income.

Three key takeaways from the call highlight its trajectory.

When major retail tenants close, landlords lose both rent and foot traffic. Management eliminated that drag by resolving all 11 vacated anchor spaces in its portfolio. It achieved a 70% cash-rent spread, which measures the percentage increase between previous and new lease rates.

CTO Realty entered the Kansas City market by purchasing Zona Rosa, an open-air shopping destination, for $63 million. Management bought the property at foreclosure for $82 per square foot. That is well below replacement cost, giving the company a clear path to boost earnings as it fills vacant space.

Beyond physical real estate, the company deploys capital into high-yield loans and preferred equity, which gives priority over common equity for dividends and asset recovery. These structured deals generate double-digit yields without requiring full asset ownership.

Leasing demand across the retail portfolio is expanding steadily, supported by consistent tenant interest and below-market rents. CTO Realty benefits from long-term leases with creditworthy anchors and a new $1 billion credit facility, which provides access to flexible corporate debt, lowers borrowing costs, and extends debt maturities.

Management is focusing on capital allocation, executing its signed-not-open pipeline, and unlocking value from newly acquired lifestyle centers. The company relies on its embedded growth drivers to fuel long-term shareholder returns.
