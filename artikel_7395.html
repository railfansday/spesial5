Weekday: Interview Coordination & No-Show Specialist

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

This is an internal role not for our client roleExperience: 0–2 yearsShifts: 8 AM–5 PM / 12 AM–9 AMLocation: RemoteCompensation : 10k - 20k (This includes fixed and variable component)

Are you the person who never lets an interview slip through the cracks?

We’re looking for a highly reliable coordinator to ensure every scheduled interview happens smoothly. You’ll be the go-to person between clients and candidates - confirming interviews, preventing no-shows, and keeping everything on track.

Confirm interviews with candidates and clients before they happen.

Send invites, reminders, and joining links.

Coordinate timings, reschedules, and last-minute changes.

Follow up quickly on delayed or missed interviews.

Maintain simple interview trackers and share daily updates.

Excellent English communication - confident on calls, email, and chat.

A proactive follow-up mindset: you don’t wait to be reminded.

Strong attention to detail with timings, names, links, and client instructions.

Recruitment, BPO, customer support, or client coordination experience is a plus.

Take ownership and follow through without supervision.

Stay calm and professional when plans change.

Enjoy organizing, tracking, and closing the loop.

Understand that one missed interview affects both a client and a candidate.

Directly improve client satisfaction and candidate experience.

Build strong skills in recruitment operations and client coordination.

Work in a structured team where reliability is recognized.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
