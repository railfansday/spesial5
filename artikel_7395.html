Deloitte Digital’s new agentic tools aim to fix messy marketing workflows

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

A new Converge by Front Office suite is designed to help brands better connect their existing technology stacks and wring greater value from them.

Marketers continue to wrestle with the gap between the promise of AI and the reality of the technology’s results, which for some haven’t lived up to the hype. Agencies and other marketing services providers are responding to persistent frustrations around AI fragmentation and execution with a wave of solutions themed around orchestration, or improving workflows.

Seventy-six percent of surveyed brand leaders believe their operating foundations do not meaningfully support realizing value from AI, which CMOs are under growing pressure to master, according to a recent Deloitte Digital report on personalization trends. Nearly all, 97%, of respondents say that the pace of AI development outstrips their ability to optimize AI for personalization purposes. Platform and workflow inefficiencies were also cited as hindrances.

The consultancy’s Converge Front Office suite seeks to address these shortcomings by bringing greater unity to marketers’ AI tech stacks. Agents will work to link existing tools and data, including from cloud-computing and software providers. Clients can also enlist Deloitte engineers to ensure Converge solutions cater to specific needs or adapt as their organization’s priorities evolve.

The Converge Marketing product is designed to break down silos and better integrate campaign strategy, planning, execution and measurement while ensuring marketers retain control over key decisions. Agent capabilities span research, strategy planning, asset building, campaign optimization and performance tracking. The goal is to get marketers from the pilot phase to production faster, Mark Singer, marketing service lead and CMO at Deloitte Digital, said in a statement.

Converge Sales boasts agents that can help identify prospects, engage inbound buyers and manage sales pipeline activity, among other functions. Deloitte Digital is aligning the new Converge brands with Converge Signals, a predictive intelligence platform that draws on data from partners including customer insights firm HundredX and Deloitte Digital’s own BrandWorth scoring system.

Other agencies are racing to hone their competitive edge on the agentic front. Publicis Groupe recently completed its acquisition of data-collaboration platform LiveRamp, a deal the ad-holding group says will enable it to produce more sophisticated agents on behalf of clients.

Deloitte has ramped up its agentic services as some of its consulting divisions experience a slowdown. The company’s technology and transformation unit grew 2.5% in the fiscal year 2026, a lower rate of growth than the prior year.
