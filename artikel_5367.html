Cowork Frederick Foundation Announces 2026 Gala Fundraiser

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

December 2nd event to raise funds for programs for Frederick County freelancers and micro-businesses.

FREDERICK, Md., Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The Cowork Frederick Foundation, the nonprofit arm of Frederick's first and longest-running coworking space, announces its fourth annual fundraiser gala. The "Winter Fairy Tale Gala" will be held at Steinhardt Brewing Co. on December 2nd from 6:30 to 9:30 pm.

The event raises funds for programs that educate, inform, and mentor Frederick County freelancers and micro-businesses, and creates opportunities for them to connect and collaborate.

Generous sponsors, local businesses themselves, help cover the cost of the event. Local retailers and restaurants provide gift cards which are bundled into raffle packages valued at between $100 and $1000 (e.g., "Magical Date Night": a meal at White Rabbit Gastropub + tickets to the Frederick Magic Theater). Thus, the event helps raise awareness of and bring business to these local businesses.

The annual event was launched in 2023, after the U.S. Surgeon General declared an epidemic of loneliness. Many in the Foundation's targeted demographic (local freelancers, remote workers, and micro-businesses) are not able to host or attend a company holiday party. The opportunity to address that was clear: a holiday party not just for a party's sake, but to help them connect with each other, and to raise funds for the Foundation's important programs.

Join us for an enchanting winter evening filled with magical bites, festive spirits, connections, music, dancing, and a raffle for some seriously great prize packages. All for a good cause. This event benefits local micro-businesses by raising funds for programs that educate, mentor, and create opportunities to connect and collaborate. Chill vibes from 6:30 to 8:00 pm, party vibes 8:00 to 9:30 pm.

Location: Steinhardt Brewing Co, 340 E Patrick St Suite 100-102, Frederick, MD 21701

More Information: https://coworkfrederickfoundation.org/2026-gala/
