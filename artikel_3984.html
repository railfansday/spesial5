God of War: Sons of Sparta Really Will Be Ported Backwards to PS4

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Out on 16th Nov, with a free update for PS5 owners

It's now been confirmed God of War: Sons of Sparta, the mostly forgotten Metroidvania game that launched earlier this year, will indeed be ported back to PS4.

Having debuted on PS5 in February, the PS4 version launches next week, on 16th November 2026.

For both current PS5 owners and any new players on PS4, the base title will be supported by two extra modes: Hermes Mode and Erebus Mode.

Hermes Mode is a speedrun challenge featuring a split-based timer and a scoreboard. You automatically unlock the mode by finishing the game, or you can enter the following cheat code on the main menu to access it straight away:

Hermes Mode Code: Up Up Down Down Left Right Left Right L1 R1 Touch Pad

Then there's Erebus Mode, which revolves around both you and the enemy dying from one hit. It's dubbed the "ultimate challenge" for God of War: Sons of Sparta.

When it launched on PS5 following a shadow drop reveal, Sons of Sparta didn't exactly set the world alight. We enjoyed the adventure, awarding it a 7/10 score in our God of War: Sons of Sparta PS5 review.

"It's a mostly successful experiment; the Metroidvania structure works well with the franchise's mechanics, and it looks and sounds the part. However, it takes too long to really come into its own, leaving the early parts of the game feeling a bit lacking compared to the richer experience found later on."

We were one of the few who did, though, as the overall critic rating on Metacritic sits at a 64.

Now on a less powerful system, it's not like a PS4 version can fix any of its problems. At least it gives those people still using a PS4 a chance to play the game.
