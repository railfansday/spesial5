GTA 6's In-Game Animated TV Series, Macca & Friends, Hints at the Game's Absurd Scope

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

I think this might be the one thing about GTA 6 that's really made me aware of the sheer scale of what Rockstar Games is cooking up.

Exclusively revealed via DiscussingFilm, an in-game, animated TV series named Macca & Friends has been revealed with the below teaser trailer:

What strikes me about this is that the show looks exactly like the sort of thing you would see on Adult Swim. It has similar vibes to other cartoons aimed at adults, like Aqua Teen Hunger Force or Rick & Morty.

What I mean is, this doesn't look like your typical throwaway gag that plays on a loop in the background. It looks like a full production on its own.

It's described as a "series" in the official blurb, so it seems this will have multiple episodes you can watch within GTA 6. The show "follows the adventures of Macca the Gator and his wetland friends across the state of Leonida."

This won't be the only thing you can tune into, either; according to Kinda Funny, there are multiple shows that have been created, including a "novela" and another animated show called The Johnsons.

They say Rockstar "hired an animation studio" to create these things, and that seemingly includes Macca & Friends.

If this is the sort of scope we can expect from just watching TV in GTA 6, it gets me excited about the level of detail and scope in the rest of the game.
