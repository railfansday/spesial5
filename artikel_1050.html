Manchester City’s Marc Guehi will be available for selection ahead of their return to Premier League action, despite a scare before the international break.

Guehi pulled out of Thomas Tuchel’s squad for last week’s Nations League games against Croatia and Spain, after coming off at half-time during City’s 3-1 defeat to Nottingham Forest in their last top-flight outing.

But manager Enzo Maresca confirmed he was back in training ahead of the Cottagers’ visit, although Matheus Nunes and Abdukodir Khusanov are likely to miss out.

Maresca said on Friday: “From the Forest game, we have still Matheus and Khusa that are still doubts for tomorrow because they are still with some problems. We will see in the next hours.

“[Guehi] didn’t join the national team because he had a small issue against Forest but it’s not because he needed a break.”

Maresca also backed under-fire keeper Gianluigi Donnarumma, who has endured a difficult spell including errors in goal both for City and for the Italian national team. Over the international window he made a major error in the 3-1 win over France and let in four goals in a heavy defeat to Belgium.

But Maresca said: “He has been good. I think with the international team, he made a mistake in the build-up. But in Italy he was the man of the match in the game, he made many good saves.

“We made some mistakes in the last games. Gigi was part of that. He is aware. Hopefully he's not going to make any more mistakes.”

He confirmed that the 27-year-old would start against Fulham.

City suffered a run of three straight defeats in the Premier League before the international break and sit third in the standings, six points off leaders Arsenal and one behind Brighton, who beat City 1-0.

The international break may have diverted some attention away from the club after it was found guilty of breaking the Premier League’s financial rules, but questions over the case are likely to resume along with domestic football.

Sanctions have not yet been handed down and matches have continued while the club awaits the verdict on its appeal, but Maresca denied that it was affecting his players.

He said: “I don't think the players are thinking too much about the noise.

“If we want to relate the noise with the defeats, then at Villa away we had so many chances. Brighton, the 'keeper was man of the match. At Forest, Erling Haaland missed a chance for 2-2. I don't think the players are worried or focused on that.”
