Burger King reveals wild Avengers: Doomsday menu with green Whopper, chicken and Sprite

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Burger King has joined the Avengers: Doomsday food frenzy, unveiling a bizarre new menu featuring green-covered Whoppers, superhero-themed chicken and a mysterious Sprite drink.

With Marvel’s next blockbuster just weeks away, fast-food chains are going all out with their promotional menus. However, Burger King’s latest creation might be one of the strangest yet.

The chain has launched its Avengers: Doomsday Collection in India, introducing a range of burgers, sides and drinks inspired by Doctor Doom and several Marvel superheroes.

The main attraction is the Doomsday Whopper, which features a black glazed bun and a bright-green spicy garlic sauce that gives the burger a rather radioactive appearance.

Customers can choose between two versions: the Extra Crunch Veg Whopper and the Flame-Grilled Chicken Whopper. Burger King says the black buns use natural, plant-derived food coloring.

The menu also includes a Loki Molten Cheese Burger featuring a massive fried cheese patty, a Cyclops Soft Paneer Burger and a Magneto Crunchy Chicken Burger.

Marvel fans can also order Thor Thunder Nuggets, Falcon Fearless Chicken Wings, Magneto Boneless Chicken and Shuri Vibranium Fries, with the green garlic sauce making an appearance across the lineup.

There’s even a drink called Fantastic Fizz, which food insider Snackolator describes as a Sprite mixture, although the exact ingredients have yet to be detailed.

The collaboration also features special Doctor Doom packaging, collectible crowns and limited-edition merchandise, including an Avengers: Doomsday tumbler.

Burger King’s collection launched in India on November 3, with burgers starting at ₹199 (around $2.10 USD). There has been no announcement about bringing the menu to the United States or other international markets.

It’s not the only chain getting in on the Marvel madness, either.

KFC has revealed its own Avengers: Doomsday menu, featuring black burgers, green mayo and something called Doom Dust. That collaboration launches in select markets on November 9.

Meanwhile, Dunkin’ has assembled a massive Marvel menu featuring character-inspired drinks, donuts and Doctor Doom collectibles.

The promotions arrive ahead of Avengers: Doomsday’s theatrical release on December 18, 2026, with Robert Downey Jr. taking on the role of Doctor Doom.
