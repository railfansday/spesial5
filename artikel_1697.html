Deal complete: Chelsea owners steal in before Liverpool's FSG

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Chelsea's owners, Blue Co., have swooped in to steal a deal ahead of Liverpool and FSG. This wasn't how things were supposed to go.

Everything changed at Liverpool in early 2024 as Jurgen Klopp got ready to leave the club. The German's exit provided an opportunity for an overhaul - and a move towards a new plan.

Liverpool's owners FSG welcomed back Michael Edwards and Julian Ward, installing Richard Hughes as sporting director. Edwards and Ward, however, weren't strictly working for Liverpool but instead headed up a new multi-club project.

Multi-club projects, while correctly controversial, are an increasingly common occurrence in football as elite clubs look to consolidate their position. FSG wanted a piece of that and instructed Edwards to help lead the search for more clubs to bring under their ownership.

The idea, however, fell flat. FSG consistently failed to find a club for a reasonable price and eventually cancelled the plan around two and half years after kicking it off.

Swansea City coach Vitor Matos has, per Fabrizio Romano, agreed to take over at Strasbourg. The French side are owned by Chelsea's owners Blue Co. and they'll now take the highly-rated coach under their wing.

Swansea have been enjoying life under Matos and it's no surprise that he's been given a 'bigger' gig. However, this was supposed to be FSG making the move.

Matos, after all, was one of Klopp's assistants at Liverpool and only left the Reds when the German did. He embarked on a career as a head coach but Sunday Times correspondent Jonathan Northcroft claims the plan was always to bring him 'back'.

'Back' meaning to a club under the FSG flag, in other words. Matos was viewed as one for the future and someone who could help lead the development at a different club. You'd imagine the potential for him to rejoin Liverpool as head coach was there, too.

Instead, it's the Chelsea group who've got him. Matos is heading there with Strasbourg and it feels as though the Reds have missed their chance.
