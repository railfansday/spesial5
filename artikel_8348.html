OP, appreciate the tool.

One thing to keep in mind is that all this info, its use to settle the estate depends on the executor and to some or large extent on the temperament of the surviving spouse or child/children.

As my therapist friend says all the info and insight from therapy is useless if the person ultimately does not understand that the purpose of the therapy is to free them to act. Without action, nothing happens.

Understand how the executor acts in a stressful situation. What do they avoid etc.

When possible, simplify!

Your idea of cash flow for paying expenses for home, bills etc won't be survivor's or beneficiaries' preference. Communication is essential to delegate these decisions. And to give them agency to make choices different from yours.
