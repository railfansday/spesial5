Channing Tatum doesn't think his Gambit voice has changed, reminding fans that Avengers: Doomsday "is not Deadpool tone"

Contrary to what Marvel fans may think, Gambit star Channing Tatum says he hasn't toned down his mutant's accent in Avengers: Doomsday, it's just less Cajun French.

"I think I have been quoted in the press as toning down the accent. That is not exactly what I'm doing. I'm just speaking less Cajun French," said Tatum to Associated Press. "It's hard to explain, but basically Cajun French is French that almost French people can't understand. So I'm speaking less of that, but the Cajun accent is still there."

Tatum first took to the screen as Gambit in Deadpool and Wolverine, where he sported a rather thick accent. In Marvel comics, the hero, real name Remy LeBeau, does indeed speak with an Acadiana twang, reflecting his Louisiana heritage. However, fans were quick to point out the difference in Gambit's accent in the first listen at his voice ahead of Doomsday, which arrived earlier this month in a Dunkin Donuts commercial.

Nevertheless, Tatum reminds fans that Doomsday is a very different movie from Deadpool 3, which might explain his change in cadence. "The movie and the tone in general is not Deadpool tone. Shawn Levy and Ryan Reynolds have their own tone that is specific to the Deadpool movies," explained Tatum. "The Russos, they have comedy and levity in their movies, but they definitely will punch you in the gut emotionally as well. So I think you're going to get a lot more drama. There's going to be a lot of drama in the movie, let me just say that."

Though he couldn't give too much away, Tatum said we can "expect a little more emotional moments" from his mutant. "The movie is going to take you on a ride. We get to see a little bit more of Gambit's fighting style," added the star. We have already seen Gambit flick some cards at Shang-Chi in the Doomsday trailer, but the hero will have to learn to work as a team if he wants to defeat Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom as he threatens to destroy multiple Marvel timelines.

Gambit will be joined by other X-Men in the Marvel Phase 6 movie, including Patrick Stewart's Charles Xavier, Ian McKellen's Magneto, James Marsden's Cyclops, Alan Cumming's Nightcrawler, and Rebecca Romijn's Mystique.

Avengers: Doomsday hits cinemas on December 18, and December 16 in the UK. For more, check out our guide to the Marvel timeline, and keep up with upcoming Marvel movies and shows on the way.
