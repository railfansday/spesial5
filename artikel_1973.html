Prediction: USA vs Haiti

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The United States head into Tuesday’s second leg of their CONCACAF Nations League quarter-final against Haiti with one foot already in the semi-finals.

After a dominant 6-0 victory in the opening meeting, it would take an extraordinary turnaround for the Americans to be eliminated, as Mauricio Pochettino prepares for his final match in charge before departing for Manchester United.

The USA have little left to do but finish the tie professionally after dismantling Haiti in the first leg.

Tuesday’s match will represent the final game of Mauricio Pochettino’s tenure, and the Argentine will be eager to leave on a high note. While he departs without delivering silverware, he has overseen significant progress since the World Cup, with the United States currently riding a five-match winning streak in all competitions.

The Americans have been particularly impressive in recent months, keeping three consecutive clean sheets and scoring at least three goals in four of their last five matches.

Home form has also been a major strength. The USA have a perfect record in Nations League quarter-final matches on home soil and remain unbeaten at TQL Stadium, where they have won two previous internationals.

History is also firmly on their side. The United States are unbeaten in their last 11 meetings with Haiti and have won the last four encounters by an aggregate score of 10-1.

Julian Hall was the youngest player in the American starting lineup during the first leg, while Santiago Castaneda and Reed Baker-Whiting are still waiting to make their senior international debuts.

Christian Pulisic and Weston McKennie both made their first appearances since the World Cup in the opening match of the tie and immediately made an impact. Pulisic scored twice, while McKennie also found the net.

Hall, Auston Trusty and Folarin Balogun were also on the scoresheet as the Americans ran riot.

Haiti travel to Cincinnati facing a seemingly impossible task after suffering a crushing defeat in the first leg.

David Badia’s side had entered the quarter-finals with momentum after winning their opening three Nations League group-stage matches, but back-to-back defeats have abruptly halted that progress. Their last two losses have come by a combined score of 8-0.

The 6-0 defeat to the USA was particularly painful, marking the first time since 2021 that Haiti had conceded six goals in a single match.

Despite the deficit, Haiti will still be determined to finish the campaign with a positive performance. Their last quarter-final victory in a CONCACAF competition came during the 2019 Gold Cup, when they staged a remarkable comeback from 2-0 down to beat Canada 3-2.

The visitors are also attempting to secure their first victory on American soil against the United States since a World Cup qualifier triumph in 1969.

Goalkeeper Jonah Mednard and midfielder Miguel Joseph remain uncapped at senior international level.

Fabrice Picault is pushing for a place in the starting lineup after beginning the first leg on the bench, while Ruben Providence and Yassin Fortune could also be considered for greater involvement.

With a six-goal advantage already established, the United States can approach this fixture with confidence and relatively little pressure. Pochettino’s side have been in excellent form, while Haiti appear to have lost momentum at exactly the wrong time.

Even if the Americans rotate heavily, the gulf in quality and confidence between the teams should still be evident. Haiti may offer greater resistance than in the first leg, but another comfortable home victory looks the most likely outcome.
