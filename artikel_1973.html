Alarming parallels should be heeded as Hammers hit the bottom half of the table | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Sure it couldn’t happen, could it? Only five times in the history of the Premier League have sides suffered successive relegations from the top tier to the third.

For anyone who witnessed the shambolic showing at Fratton Park on Tuesday night, that nightmare scenario suddenly doesn’t look so  ridiculous. West Ham in the third division, 62,500 seater stadium and all?

Alarming parallels exist between West Ham’s performance this season and that of their former competitors Leicester City who now find themselves playing their football in the third division.

The Hammers are now in the bottom half of the Championship, with just 24 points from 17 games. At the same stage last season, the Foxes themselves, after 17 games had amassed … Exactly the same number of points.

All of which seemed unimaginable when the Irons beat Swansea five weeks ago to go second, and Nuno Espirito Santo picked upthe September manager of the month award.

Fratton Park showing was a real wake up call to West Ham’s management and board. This ship is sinking.

The only positive is that a month is a long time in this league. A run of form could be just around the corner and any team – West Ham included – can win five or six games and transform their fortunes. But the Hammers’ form at present is most definitely that of a relegation candidate rather than a play-off hopeful.

Which hardly helps the recruitment in January.

For anyone living under the misconception that Leicester City’s case was an isolated example and that lightning couldn’t strike twice, Wolverhampton Wanderers, who were also relegated in 2012/13, had 22 points after 17 games.

Stoke City come calling on Saturday with a seriously impressive run of form, unbeaten in six whereas the Hammers are winless in their last half dozen. No time like the present to get back to winning ways and arrest the downhill slide. One relegation is enough, thank you very much.
