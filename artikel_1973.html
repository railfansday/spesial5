Sources: Celtic turn attention to completing Abdoulaye Doumbia deal ahead of January

Celtic have set their sights on a deal to sign BK Hacken star Abdoulaye Doumbia in January, sources have told Football Insider.

The 19-year-old has shone foer the Swedish club so far this season, where he has made 25 league appearances, scored two goals and provided three asissts from midfield.

Primarily deployed in a deeper role, the Ivory Coast-born star can play across the midfield, and his performances have caught the eye of watching scouts at Parkhead.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Celtic have joined a host of European club keeping tabs on Doumbia's progress in Sweden.

Hacken currently sit fourth in the Allsvenskan table, and are a way off challenging for the title, but the midfielder's displays have been key to their positive progress.

Doumbia is now firmly on Celtic's radar as Martin O'Neill aims to bolster his midfield option in January, but any deal may prove difficult for Celtic to pull off given the competition.

Football Insider revealed last month that Celtic are prioritising the addition of at least one new midfielder as they prepare to strengthen their squad in January.

O'Neill has become concerned about his current options, as well as a clear lack of quality depth in the position, and a number of potential targets are now being considered.

Doumbia has emerged as a leading target for Celtic thanks to his displays over in Sweden, where the Hoops have had scouts keeping a close eye on his progress.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Again, it's an area of the team that I think that Celtic are really lacking in, that defensive midfield position.

"Doumbia is probably somebody that is on their radar because he's done very well for Hacken since his arrival there last January.

"He's impressed a number of clubs with his performances in Sweden, and Celtic have been keeping a close eye on his progress.

"The problem for Celtic is the competition for his services, there's a lot of interest across Europe, so it might be difficult for Celtic to compete with these clubs for Doumbia's signature.

"For Celtic, they might not be top of the choices for Doumbia if all those clubs were to make a move for him."

Doumbia is not the only midfielder on Celtic's radar ahead of the January transfer window, as they put plans in place to strenghten the position as a matter of urgency.

As part of those plans, they could revisit a player who they missed out on during the summer, with Torino star Gvidas Gineitis still high on their list of targets.

Football Insider revealed on 23 September that Celtic may now be prepared to reignite their interest in Gineitis if Torino lower their asking price in January.

Gineitis' agent recently admitted that Celtic's coaching staff were keen on signing him, but the move broke down because of disagreements at board level.

The Serie A club had placed an asking price of £13million on the midfielder in the summer, but there is a feeling that could change after the turn of the year.
