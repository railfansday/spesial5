Chelsea 1 Leeds United 4: Whites avenge cup exits in style by tearing Xabi Alonso’s Blues apart

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Leeds United avenged the two cup defeats they suffered at Chelsea’s hands with a stunning 4-1 victory over their old rivals at Stamford Bridge.

Having been edged out by the Blues at the semi-final stage of the FA Cup in April, Leeds were subjected to a 6-3 Carabao Cup mauling by Xabi Alonso’s men in September.

On this occasion, however, it was Daniel Farke’s men inflicting the damage. Emmanuel Emegha fired the hosts ahead, but Noah Okafor goals either side of a Dominic Calvert-Lewin penalty and a late Brenden Aaronson finish gave Leeds a Premier League win that will go down in the history books.

Chelsea sought to assume authority from the get-go, with Cole Palmer driving at the heart of the Leeds midfield inside the opening minute. The hosts may have been straight on the offensive, but Leeds soon made it clear they would not be content with one-way traffic.

Jayden Bogle and Sean Longstaff both made headway down the right-hand side, sending in crosses that were not capitalised upon.

The opening exchanges played out in a fast and furious manner, with robust challenges stoking the fires of a historic rivalry. Chelsea had the early edge, although referee Michael Oliver had no interest in appeals for a penalty when Tarik Muharemovic challenged Emmanuel Emegha inside the box.

Leeds handled Chelsea’s ventures forward well, mostly limiting the Blues to speculative efforts. Josh Acheamping, Morgan Rogers and Estevao all pulled the trigger from distance, yet little was asked of James Trafford between the Leeds sticks.

At the other end, Leeds continued to make inroads on the right. James Justin whipped a cross over for Calvert-Lewin, who struggled to generate power but did hit the crossbar with his looped header.

Leeds’ focus soon shifted to the left, where Gudmundsson started to flex his attacking muscles. He made an incisive dart into the box and went to ground under close attention from Estevao, although the referee paid no attention to penalty appeals.

Leeds’ left wing-back then picked out Calvert-Lewin and saw the England international force a strong hand from Emiliano Martinez with a headed effort.

The visitors looked to be moving through the gears but just as the tide was turning, Chelsea drew first blood. Justin and Nico Elvedi both failed to stop the advancement of Rogers, who fed Emegha and saw the frontman beat Trafford at his near post with a blasted finish.

Leeds bit back almost immediately, levelling proceedings within three minutes of the opener. Calvert-Lewin contested a long ball with Wesley Fofana before picking up the scraps and releasing Okafor, who jinked his way into space and rifled across Martinez.

With the wind behind their sails, Leeds came close to overturning the deficit via their equaliser’s creator. Calvert-Lewin met a Muharemovic cross and his deft flick came agonisingly close to finding the bottom corner.

Farke’s men continued applying pressure in the closing stages of the first half and Ao Tanaka was unfortunate to see a rasping rocket of a shot deflected before it could cause Martinez any trouble.

Gudmundsson then danced his way into an opportunity before poking harmlessly wide of Martinez’s right-hand post.

The next chance fell to Tanaka, who pushed the ball out of his feet but could only force a straightforward save with a shot straight down Martinez’s throat.

Leeds did have the ball in the back of the net on the stroke of half-time, although Justin was clearly offside before he delivered for Muharemovic to finish. When the half-time whistle did blow, it was Leeds firmly in the driving seat.

The second half started as the first ended, with Leeds in the ascendency. There were early appeals for a penalty when Calvert-Lewin tangled with Martinez, but Oliver was again dismissive of protests.

Tanaka then stretched to get on the end of a cross from deep, steering just off target as pressure piled on Chelsea.

Having seen their protests ignored mere minutes earlier, Leeds saw Oliver point to the spot when Rogers was judged to have handled a Muharemovic shot. After a lengthy VAR check, the decision was stuck by and Calvert-Lewin buried the spot-kick.

Chelsea responded by putting their front foot forward and Rogers forced Trafford down to his left with a low drive from distance.

However, it was Leeds who struck next and sent the the travelling supporters into a frenzy. After Chelsea failed to clear their lines after a long throw, Ethan Ampadu’s deflected cross was hammered home by Okafor in emphatic fashion.

Somewhat predictably, Chelsea surged back into the driving seat as they looked for a way back into a game getting away from them. Much to the frustration of the home supporters, their spell of control yielded little more than occasional half-chances.

There were sharp inhales of breath when Rogers arrowed a free-kick goalwards from a tight angle, but it dipped over the crossbar with Trafford at full stretch.

Many of Chelsea’s supporters were already heading out to beat the traffic when Aaronson capped an astonishing afternoon, wriggling through a creaking defence before steering a fourth past Martinez.
