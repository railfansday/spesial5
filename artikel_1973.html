Trey Nyoni: Ex-chief scout reveals what he likes most about Liverpool sensation, names his best position

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Liverpool starlet Trey Nyoni has a remarkable composure about him despite being so young.

That is the opinion of experienced former Everton, Tottenham and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 19-year-old has broken through this season, becoming a key part of Andoni Iraola's midfield.

Nyoni featured last season under Arne Slot but has earned his new manager's trust, featuring in 13 games and starting three of Liverpool's last four.

Ryan Gravenberch has been among the Reds' most important players in recent years but he appears to have been leapfrogged in the pecking order by the teenager, who started in a key game against Arsenal earlier this month.

The Netherlands international now faces a battle to get back into the team after missing two games through injury.

Liverpool have often struggled for depth in midfield in recent years, but they appear to have unearthed a gem in Nyoni.

The teenager was signed from the Leicester City academy in 2023, and has taken to senior football with seemingly no problem at all.

"He's a he's a great prospect. What a breath of fresh air it's been watching him play," King told Football Insider when asked about Nyoni.

"I mean, he's 19 years old. He's got a great energy. He looks a very good prospect.  There's going to be a period here where you'll probably get left out for a little bit because everything is very intense when you're in the Premier League.

"And at that age, he might need a little rest or a breather. But he's certainly a player for the future, that is for sure. Very exciting player.

"I see him as a number six. He wins it. He can pass it. He's not clever on the ball and when I say that I mean he doesn't look to beat one, two or three men. He plays it simple, which is great for a young player."

Liverpool will hope that, in Nyoni, they have found the base of their midfield for the next decade.

The 19-year-old is just that good, and all focus will be on securing him to a new contract and protecting him from the building hype.

"I think he has to continue in that same place he's been since the start of the season," Iraola said earlier this month.

"We trust him, he's doing very well, he needs to continue, we have to encourage him.”
