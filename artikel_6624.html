If you’re shopping for a true foodie, nothing can top a gourmet gift basket. But if you go that route, you have to do your research. After all, not all food gifts are the same. The last thing you want to do is send your epicurean friend or family member yet another disappointing fruit basket or a block of shelf-stable, gummy cheese!

Instead, for something ready-to-eat, opt for beloved foods with an established fanbase—perhaps one of the editor-loved finds on Goldbelly. Or, if you want to really impress them, high-quality ingredients are sure to inspire their culinary creativity. That’s why my go-to food gift is an olive oil gift set from Enzo’s Table.

The Signature Selection gift set offers a diverse lineup of flavors to impress your favorite foodies.

Folks at Enzo’s Table have been farming their vineyard in California’s fertile San Joaquin Valley for nearly a century. This gives their gourmand products a farm-to-table feel that’s backed by decades of know-how. In fact, over the years, the brand has proudly received over 200 awards for its signature olive oils, jams and biscotti!

The Signature Selection olive oil gift set is a fleet consisting of four of Enzo’s famous olive oils, plus their signature balsamic vinegar. All five 100ml bottles are organic, having been produced in the San Joaquin Valley vineyard. The gift set contains the following:

The Delicate EVOO offers a buttery flavor and incredibly mild yet peppery finish, making it a perfectly smooth pick for dipping bread or incorporating into salad dressings. I love using it for roasting wintery vegetables and crafting homemade tomato sauce for spaghetti, since it doesn’t overpower acidic or umami flavors found in fresh tomatoes and mushrooms.

Enzo’s Bold EVOO is best for imparting lasting flavor. I love it for roasted meats in particular, like the rotisserie chicken my husband makes on his grill. It leaves quite the impression with its peppery, spicy profile, especially on milder meats like chicken breast.

The Clementine Crush EVOO is probably my favorite of the five. This one goes well in salad dressings, cakes—anything that benefits from a fruity, acidic punch. Because it’s milled with fresh organic California mandarins, it’s a good EVOO to add a little brightness to dishes. Personally, it’s also my favorite option for delicate fish and sautéing shrimp.

Enzo’s Basil Crush EVOO is, as you’d expect, herby and earthy. It’s another option that’s particularly good for roasts, especially red meats. I also love adding a splash on caprese salad.

The Traditional Balsamic Vinegar is incredibly versatile in all the ways you love to see with balsamic. Because it’s crafted from sweet, organic Lambrusco and Trebbiano grapes, it shines brightest on summery dishes like grilled fruits and balsamic Brussels sprouts. I’ve tried a lot of balsamic over the years, and this one might be the thickest, richest blend I’ve enjoyed.

Delight your favorite foodie with a full fleet of flavors when you gift them an Enzo Olive Oil gift set!

Folks seeking the perfect gift for foodies can’t go wrong with the highly versatile Signature Selection gift set. With so many unique flavors inside and generous portions of each offering, it’s a wonderfully simple way to spark inspiration in the kitchen!

If your recipient finds any fast favorites while they work through their discovery set, another great gift idea is one of their olive oil subscriptions. This is also a good way to save on Enzo olive oil if you’re a big fan yourself. Over the years, food subscriptions have become increasingly popular, and they remain some of our readers’ favorite holiday gifts by far!
