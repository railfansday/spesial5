Alphonso Davies injury diagnosis confirmed after Canada setback

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Bayern Munich were dealt an injury blow last week when Alphonso Davies was forced off in the first half of Canada's 3-2 win over Guatemala.

Canada Soccer has now confirmed the nature of the injury.

"Following a medical scan today in Toronto, it has been confirmed that Alphonso Davies sustained a minor lower-body muscle strain. He is travelling back to Germany, where he will continue to receive ongoing care," the Canadian Football Association was quoted as saying by The Athletic.

Meanwhile, Bild reports that Bayern's medical staff have also confirmed the muscle strain, with the positive news being that a more serious injury has been ruled out.

According to TZ, the injury is expected to keep Davies out for 7-10 days, with Bayern hoping to have the Canada captain available for nezt week's Champions League game against Lille.

Davies himself has opened up about the setback and his recurring muscle problems in an interview with OneSoccer.

“Having had too many hamstring injuries, I know what they feel like, you know? It's not the first time. I know what they feel like,” Davies said, adding:

“I knew I felt this feeling before. And me taking myself out of the game, for me, for the team, was better than me pushing through. Because the feeling I felt, I felt this before, and I've tried to push through last time, and it didn't end well. We don't know yet how severe it is. But for me I know for sure there's something.”
