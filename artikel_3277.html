Lainey Wilson Debuts New Song ‘Just a Woman’ with All-Female Band at 2026 CMA Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Though she has yet to announce a new album, Lainey Wilson has been steadily dropping singles over the past few months. But Wednesday night at the 2026 CMA Awards, Wilson took a break from hosting to debut one of her most powerful songs yet, the country-soul ballad “Just a Woman” — for which she appropriately performed with an all-female string band and gospel chorus. Strong and sultry, it was one of Wilson’s best award show moments to date.

The performance was the second of the evening for Wilson, following her touching tribute to Dolly Parton alongside Vince Gill, which opened the 60th annual awards show.

Written with Luke Dick and Shane McAnally, “Just a Woman” is both tender and to the point: “I ain’t an actress playing a part/damn sure ain’t a mattress/in case you forgot.” Wilson is known for her quirky, sometimes goofy sense of humor (see her recent video for the John Mayer-accompanied “Phone, Keys, Wallet,” where she quite literally looses her head), but “Just a Woman” proves that she can just as easily tackle something far more topical and lyrically potent. Wilson hasn’t been shy why it comes to speaking out about the double standards women are saddled with. At last year’s CMA awards, one of her acceptance speeches directly targeted those who seem to think there’s only enough room for one female artist in the room at a time. “The folks that are sitting at home in your mama’s basement eating your Cheetos,” she said, “trying to pit women against each other, y’all need to find something better to do.”

Wilson is nominated for three CMA Awards this year, including the coveted Entertainer of the Year, which she has previously won twice. If she takes the honor this year, she will make history as the only woman to do so three times.
