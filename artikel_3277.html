iOS 27.3 introduces design changes for these Apple apps

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

iOS 27.3 includes many new features for Apple’s built-in apps, but it also introduces design changes on the iPhone across Calendar, Safari, Music, and more. Here’s what’s new.

Apple is always iterating on the design of its software. While major overhauls like iOS 26’s Liquid Glass don’t come along often, the company makes smaller design changes all the time via software updates. In iOS 27.3, which will launch to all users next month, Apple has design changes coming to four popular apps: Health, Calendar, Safari, and Music.

The Health app gets the biggest design changes.

As seen above, Health introduces brand new Insights, Longevity, and Browse tabs that replace the previous options and include a variety of fresh UI elements like a top navigation row, personalized prompts and coaching, educational videos, and a lot more.

Apple Calendar’s design updates focus on making key buttons more reachable one-handed.

As the side-by-side above shows, the buttons for search and creating a new event have moved out of the top-right corner and into the bottom of the screen.

Calendar also now shows the year at the top of the screen next to the current month, and has updated the ‘Today’ button to show the current date instead.

Safari introduces an optional new design for tabs: Vertical Tabs. You can enable these inside Settings ⇾ Apps ⇾ Safari for a different approach to tabs across iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Here’s an example of vertical tabs on iPhone Duo.

Vertical Tabs won’t be for everyone, but they’ve proven popular in other browsers, so it’s great to see Safari add the option.

Apple Music has a subtle but beautiful design update in iOS 27.3 when viewing lyrics in landscape mode.

Song artwork is now able to break the constraints of a square and blend seamlessly into the scrolling lyrics view. Here are examples from Beta Profiles on X:

New in iOS 27.3 Beta: Animated artwork blends into the Apple Music player in landscape mode, and it looks so good. pic.twitter.com/k8rgSEALGo

This lyrics view was previously available on iPhone Duo, but now it’s coming to all iPhones.
