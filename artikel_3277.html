Taylor Swift Uggs Story Sparks Hilarious Memes: ‘She Wears High Heels, I Wear Ugg Boots’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Earlier this week, Vanity Fair published a polarizing commentary article about Taylor Swift that sent the internet into a frenzy. Some people didn’t understand the point of the piece, which didn’t name the artist, only referring to “The Star,” and included interviews with 100 people from different parts of her life. Others defended the need to share the “true” story of one of the biggest pop stars in the world. But one detail in particular has been the center of discussion: the fact that a tween Swift apparently loved wearing Uggs to her middle school in West Reading, Pennsylvania.

The anecdote came from one of the pop star’s former classmates, Arielle Peppers, who recalled the various colors Swift owned:”She had the brown one, the black one, the blue one.” The former peer later clarified that her observation was meant to explain Swift’s family’s financial status. “I shared a straightforward childhood memory with a reporter about school and what was affordable for different families at the time. It wasn’t intended as an attack on anyone,” Peppers told TMZ.

Regardless, fans online are turning the detail into hilarious memes. Apparently, there are dozens of ways to fit a mention of the popular lounge boots in Swift’s songs. “She wears high heels, I wear Ugg boots. She’s cheer captain, and I’m wearing my second pair of Ugg boots,” one fan wrote in a nod to Swift’s hit “You Belong With Me.” Another fan took the sentiment from Red (Taylor’s Version)‘s “I Bet You Think About Me” to come up with, “With your organic shoes and your million-dollar Uggs.”
