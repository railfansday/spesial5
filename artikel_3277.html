Gulfport Energy Corporation (GPOR -2.53%) reported a transition toward continuous operational programs in Appalachia to improve capital efficiency and lower development costs. Management stated the company reached a production inflection point in the third quarter, driven by the completion of liquids-rich pads in the Utica shale. The company indicated it is reallocating capital from the SCOOP asset in Oklahoma to Appalachia while assessing alternative drilling horizons in the Anadarko Basin. Furthermore, Gulfport expanded its shareholder return framework through an increased share repurchase authorization and secured long-term supply agreements to capture premium pricing from growing regional power demand.
