This California Tuning Shop Built an Insane Lexus GX–Toyota Tacoma Off-Road Pickup Truck

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The “Lexus TX1” is equal parts GX 550, Taco, and trophy truck.

The Toyota Tacoma is one of the most popular mid-size pickup trucks on sale in America today, with a wide range of variants. But what if there were a luxury version? What if it were built by Lexus? California tuning shop Meso Customs has brought an answer to that line of questioning with its 2026 SEMA Show build, and the results are surprisingly compelling. It’s called the Lexus TX1, and it is immediately recognizable as the forbidden love child of a Lexus GX 550 and a fourth-generation Toyota Tacoma.

As Toyota’s luxury wing, Lexus does not have a pickup in its lineup; premium trucks tend to sell best when they have non-luxury badges. That said, the TX1 features horizontal grille slats and a prominent silver Lexus badge that somehow all seem to blend in and work together well. On top of all that, a rally-style light bar on top of the windshield and a high-clearance metal front bumper show the TX1 means business.

The project started as a CGI rendering, according before evolving into a full build a year later, with Meso Customs using CAD designs and fabricated individual pieces to make the TX1 a reality. But this isn’t a simple body kit build, either. The Yuba City, California-based shop added a lift kit from King Off-Road Racing Shocks, 74Weld portal axles, and 38-inch Toyo Tires.

The rear end is equally stunning, with a straight-across LED light bar from the GX550 and a tucked bumper. The bed was actually custom-built to incorporate the shape of a GX hatch into the Tacoma shell, while Baja Designs lights were integrated underneath and to the side of the wing mirrors. Meso Customs even displayed the TX1 with a color-matched Honda Grom in the bed, to show just how practical a Lexus pickup truck could be.

Of course, the TX1 build was not officially sponsored by Toyota or Lexus, so don’t expect a luxury-style Tacoma derivative from the Japanese automaker anytime soon. Even so, the idea that Lexus could build a proper off-road pickup with a luxurious interior is one fun fantasy. And if you’re inclined to see it in person, Meso Customs currently has the Lexus TX1 on display at the Toyo Tires Treadpass exhibit at the 2026 SEMA Show.
