Fortune treasure hours schedule in the Fortnite Chapter 7 mini season

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Here's what time the Treasure Hours take place in your time zone

Fortnite is continuing the tradition of special Saturday hourly events during the Chapter 7 mini season, though this time they're treasure hours, centered around the pirate-themed "Treasure Hunters" season. During these hours, certain sprites will get increased drop rates, and you'll drop into the game with special items.

This week features Fortune treasure hours, increasing the drop rate for Fortune variant sprites. You'll also start the game with A$AP Rocky's Shock Rocket and a Self-Revive Kit. Fortune sprites will occasionally set off a radar-like effect, highlighting nearby chests — making them the perfect sprite to use to hunt down the Pirate Shop Access Token.
