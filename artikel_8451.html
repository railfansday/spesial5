If the concern is needing to cover elevated expenses that materialize when equity markets have tanked, then why not just liquidate the benefit max of $72K from the portfolio, and put it in a short term TIPS fund. Then add the LTC premium you would be willing to pay for LTCI were it available at that price to the ST TIPS portfolio so that it covers an increasing share if any LTC expenses. The assets could be used for a different purpose if that became appropriate.

Can split the selling of shares over two tax years if appropriate.
About 2-3 years ago I was thinking about earmarking a certain amount of the portfolio for her LTC and was going to open a separate Fidelity account just for that. But, after doing some research on this forum and elsewhere, it seemed the majority of folks seem to think it was simply better to just have the "one" portfolio and not separate things out so I never followed through on that plan.
