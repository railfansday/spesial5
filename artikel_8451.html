Personal Finance (Not Investing) • IRMAA surcharge increases for 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

I noticed that the Medicare IRMAA premium surcharges for 2027 were recently released -- See https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheet ... eductibles.

For those of us who file as single or HoH, MAGI (in tax year 2025) > $112,000 and <= $141,000, the Medicare monthly IRMAA surcharge will be $83.70, increasing the 2027 standard Medicare monthly premium from $209.40 to $293.10.

For the next tier up, the monthly IRMAA surcharge will be $209.40 -- that's an extra annual cost of $1515.60 for going just one dollar over the $141K MAGI cliff.

For single / HoH taxpayers with a 2025 MAGI > $177,000 and <= $212,000, the monthly IRMAA surcharge will be $335.00.

Does the IRMAA surcharge always increase, from year to year?
