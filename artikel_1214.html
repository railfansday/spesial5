Football Italia Show: Malen on fire, Inter’s old problem & Amorim learns from Man Utd mistakes

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Lorenzo Bettoni and Giancarlo Rinaldi are back with the first weekly episode of the Football Italia Show, breaking down all the talking points from the latest round of Serie A action.

Lazio sit top of the table, with city rivals Roma hot on their heels — and both sides are being driven by Dutch stars in sensational form. Inter, meanwhile, continue to struggle with the same old problems, while Ruben Amorim looks to have learned some valuable lessons from his Manchester United experience.

Juventus, on the other hand, are increasingly relying on Nick Woltemade to keep them afloat.

Who’s flying, who’s struggling, and what are the biggest stories emerging from Serie A? Watch the full breakdown in the video!
