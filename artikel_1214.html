15 Funniest Football Chants of All Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

When Liverpool fans chanted about Mauricio Pochettino's job security just 15 minutes into his first game in charge of Manchester United, who instantly found themselves 2-0 down at Anfield, it acted as a timely reminder of just how entertaining football supporters can be.

Even when watching their side lose every week, they get creative. Perhaps if they don't laugh, they will cry at the state of their team. Either way, it has made for some fairly hilarious moments over the years.

Whether it's because a player has shared a name with a shop or the thought of their feet sticking out the bed, there have been plenty of funny chants across the world of football and GiveMeSport has ranked them.

Sometimes, the best thing to do when watching your team lose is to join the mockery, and that's exactly what Norwich City fans have done in the past.

Fed up of waiting for a goal, the Canaries simply chanted about having possession, starting their chant with "we've won the ball", before singing "we've lost the ball" as soon as possession was conceded.

"We've won the ball, we've won the ball, we've won the ball,

"We've lost the ball, we've lost the ball, we've lost the ball"

Players can't get away with anything, can they? It's like fans are the flies on the wall in the dressing room at times and when Jason Puncheon left the pitch midway through the second-half of Southampton's game against Everton in 2013, they knew exactly what he was going to do.

Weeks later, the former Southampton man celebrates by pretending to sit on the toilet in his goal celebration. When a man's got to go, a man's got to go...

Who takes the banter football crown in a sport full of wind up merchants and clowns?

Jonjo Shelvey's so-called resemblance to infamous Harry Potter villain Voldemort is unfortunate, to say the least, and it didn't take long for West Ham fans to take notice.

They mocked the then-Liverpool midfielder during his warm-up by chanting all about Voldemort's biggest rival.

"He's coming for you, he's coming for you, Harry Potter, he's coming for you."

The song has followed Shelvey round for the rest of his career ever since. Awkward.

It could be the worst moment of a player's career, but that doesn't matter for football fans. Chelsea picked the perfect moment to rub salt in Steven Gerrard's wounds in 2014, when his slip allowed Demba Ba to all but end Liverpool's Premier League title hopes at Anfield of all places.

Fans instantly got creative, turning Liverpool's Gerrard song into a version of their own, mocking the Anfield captain for how he cost his side what would have been their first Premier League title at the time.

"Steve Gerrard, Gerrard, he slipped on his f****** a***,

"He passed the ball to Demba Ba, Steve Gerrard, Gerrard."

Give football fans a trendy song and they'll have a player's name involved within the hour. At Celtic, it was the Pussycat Dolls' 'Don't Cha' song which took their interest, as they turned it into a song for right-back Cha Du-Ri.

The Bhoys serenaded their defender with the new version of the song, and it remains one of the most catchy chants that Scottish football fans have ever produced.

"Don't Cha wish your full back was Cha Du-Ri? Don't Cha? Don't Cha?"

Poor Filip Sebo didn't go a second without a moment of humiliation in Scotland. The former Rangers forward quickly became known for his horror misses and ended his Ibrox career with just two goals to his name.

This initially handed Celtic fans plenty of ammunition. The Bhoys would chant his name whenever he missed a sitter in front of his own fans, much to the frustration of those at Ibrox.

The Gers soon flipped the script, however, and joined in the mocking of their transfer flop.

Anyone else struggle to even think about the original 'Freed From Desire' song these days? Whenever it is played, it instantly becomes the Will Grigg song all over again.

Wigan Athletic fans went viral after turning the song into a tune about their striker following his 28-goal season in 2016 and it has followed his career ever since. It is one of the most viral and funniest chants that football fans have ever come up with.

"Will Griggs on fire, your defence is terrified,

Will Griggs on fire, your defence is terrified,

Will Griggs on fire, your defence is terrified,

Will Griggs on fire Na, na, na, na, na, na, na, na, na, na, na, na, na, na..."

It feels fitting that Peter Crouch has one of the funniest chants in football. The towering striker was one of the biggest characters back in his playing days, quite literally and figuratively speaking.

Liverpool fans didn't always see the best of their striker, but that didn't stop them from singing his name. Dedicating a song to his absurd height, Anfield claimed that Crouch was so tall that his feet stuck out the bed.

"He's big, he's red, his feet stick out the bed, Peter Crouch, Peter Crouch."

Erling Haaland, Joao Pedro and Alexander Isak all feature as the 15 best strikers in the Premier League right now are ranked.

When Sunderland appointed Dick Advocacaat in 2015, Sunderland fans had an opportunity that they were never going to let pass them by. And so, when they travelled to face West Ham, they did so with a large inflatable object in reference to their manager's first name.

The fun didn't last long, however, as stewards soon confiscated that inflatable object, leading to a hilarious chant from the travelling Black Cats about getting it back.

"We want our d*** back, we want our d*** back, we want our d*** back."

There's not much worse than travelling to watch your team face Hull City and then hearing the 'mauled by the Tigers' song alongside the clawing hand gesture.

It has become a tool of mockery for the home fans whenever they find themselves on the winning side and the likes of Brighton and Man United have all faced a mauling.

It is arguably one of the best chants in the Premier League right now.
