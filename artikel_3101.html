Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Record orders and a sharp margin jump signal a shift toward professional referrals.

Myomo, Inc. (MYO +15.70%) reported record quarterly revenue and reached approximately breakeven adjusted EBITDA as it continues a strategic transition toward recurring professional referral sources. Management indicated that the MyoConnect program, which now spans more than 500 locations, is driving a higher percentage of revenue from clinical referrals while reducing the relative cost of customer acquisition. The company reported significant gross margin expansion driven by material cost reductions and higher manufacturing volumes. Management stated that new commercial leadership and expanded payer network access are expected to support continued scale across the United States and Germany.
