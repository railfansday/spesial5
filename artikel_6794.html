<em>The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping</em>’s<em> </em>Cast Is Totally Stacked

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

See Joseph Zada, Whitney Peak, Elle Fanning, Kieran Culkin, Jesse Plemons, and Glenn Close in the new movie

The Hunger Games franchise is welcoming a new installment, Sunrise on the Reaping, the sixth film adaptation of Suzanne Collins’s best-selling dystopian saga.

Releasing on November 20, the film boasts a star-studded cast of about 33 actors and counting, poised to bring a blend of familiar names and new faces to the world of Panem.

Ahead of the film, we’re reacquainting ourselves with beloved characters from the original trilogy, like Effie Trinket (Elle Fanning), Caesar Flickerman (Kieran Culkin), Beetee (Kelvin Harrison Jr.), and Wiress (Maya Hawke). Even the completely new characters, like Maysilee Donner and Lenore Dove, have family ties to well-known characters in the franchise.

Based on Collins’s second prequel novel, Sunrise on the Reaping delves into the brutal 50th Hunger Games, spotlighting a young Haymitch Abernathy 24 years before he became Katniss and Peeta’s mentor. We, as readers, thought we knew a detailed account of the second Quarter Quell from Mockingjay, but as we learn in the Sunrise on the Reaping novel, much of what we learned was manipulated by the Capitol to be used as propaganda.

This much is still true: With twice the tributes, the 50th Hunger Games doubles the bloodshed, trauma, and size of the cast. To prepare for the inevitably heart-wrenching film, get to know the extensive cast.

Joseph Zada is a 21-year-old Australian actor best known for playing Johnny Sinclair in the television series We Were Liars and Cal Trask in East of Eden.

Zada shows a very different side of Woody Harrelson’s character, before the biting sarcasm and heavy drinking. In the new film, we follow 16-year-old Haymitch’s harrowing journey through the 50th Hunger Games and, arguably, a more distressing journey after victory. We knew Zada was perfect for the role when he discussed re-creating Harrelson’s Mockingjay Part 2 red-carpet pajama moment.

Elle Fanning is a 28-year-old American actress best known for her roles as Aurora in Maleficent, Syvie Russo in A Complete Unknown, and Catherine in The Great, as well as being half of an iconic sister duo with Dakota Fanning.

While Trinket is only a side character in Sunrise on the Reaping, her casting was one of the most highly anticipated, with fans almost unanimously agreeing that Fanning was the only correct choice to reprise Elizabeth Banks’s character. In Sunrise on the Reaping, Trinket is a young member of the Capitol styling and beauty team for District 12.

It’s possible that Suzanne Collins got tired of the endless Coriolanus Snow thirst edits on TikTok and went ahead and cast Voldemort. No, really, 63-year-old English actor Ralph Fiennes is best known for his role as Voldemort in the Harry Potter film series.

Fiennes plays President Snow, a younger version of the late Donald Sutherland’s character in the original trilogy and an older version of Tom Blythe’s character in The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes. President Snow reaches his peak level of viciousness in Sunrise on the Reaping, and Fiennes is the perfect vessel to carry out Snow’s evil plans.

Kieran Culkin is a 44-year-old actor best known for his roles in A Real Pain and Succession. Despite recently winning an Oscar, Emmy, Golden Globe, and SAG Award, his brother Macaulay still has him beat in household name recognition for his childhood role in Home Alone. Reprising Stanley Tucci's role as Hunger Games host Caesar Flickerman may change that.

Jesse Plemons is a 38-year-old actor best known for his roles in Friday Night Lights and Breaking Bad (and for being married to Kirsten Dunst). In another impeccable casting choice, Plemons also played the son of the original Plutarch (the late Philip Seymour Hoffman) in The Master (2012).

In both Sunrise on the Reaping and the original trilogy, Plutarch Heavensbee is an old-money Capitol citizen whose morally gray philosophies make him one of the franchise’s most fascinating characters. In the new film, we see him as a young, ambitious cameraman, years before he became head gamemaker.

Lili Taylor is a 59-year-old American actress and author best known for her roles in Mystic Pizza and The Conjuring. She has the perfect sweet smile to play Mags Flanagan, the victor of the 11th Hunger Games from District 4.

Mags, previously played by the late Lynn Cohen, was introduced in Catching Fire when she volunteered to take Annie Cresta's place in the 75th Hunger Games. In Sunrise on the Reaping, Mags acts as District 12's mentor, as they have no surviving winners of their own.

Maya Hawke is the 28-year-old daughter of Uma Thurman and Ethan Hawke, best known for her roles as Robin in Stranger Things and Anxiety in Inside Out 2.

She plays Wiress, the District 3 victor fresh out of the 49th Hunger Games. Amanda Plummer played the character in Catching Fire, when she was reaped with Beetee as the 75th Hunger Games District 3 tributes.

Kelvin Harrison Jr. is a 32-year-old actor who previously played the son of the original Beetee (Jeffrey Wright) in Monsters (2018).

As the Hunger Games fan account Panem Propaganda said, “If I had a nickel for every time a Sunrise on the Reaping cast member played the son of their older Hunger Games counterpart in a completely unrelated movie, I’d have two nickels. Which isn’t a lot, but it’s weird that it happened twice,” referring to the castings of Beetee and Plutarch.

Beetee was the winner of the 34th Hunger Games from District 3 who was reaped for the 75th Hunger Games in Catching Fire. The new film delves into his heartbreaking backstory, involving the fate of his son, Ampert, as well as his secret plan with Haymitch to disrupt the games.

Iris Apatow is a 24-year-old actress who previously appeared in This Is 40, The Bubble, and Knocked Up. She is the daughter of actress Leslie Mann and producer Judd Apatow, the sister of Euphoria star Maude Apatow, and the girlfriend of White Lotus star Sam Nivola. She plays Proserpina Trinket, Effie’s younger sister and member of the District 12 prep team.

Edvin Ryding is a 23-year-old Swedish actor who previously appeared in 28 Years Later and Young Royals. He plays Vitus, Proserpina’s friend and sidekick on the District 12 prep team.

Whitney Peak is a 23-year-old Ugandan-Canadian actress best known for her role as Zoya in the 2021 Gossip Girl reboot. She plays Lenore Dove, the Covey singer and love of Haymitch's life.

Though her character is unique to Sunrise on the Reaping, Lenore Dove is rumored to be the daughter of either Maude Ivory or Lucy Gray Baird. Fans can’t help but notice the resemblance between Peak and Rachel Zegler.

McKenna Grace is a 20-year-old actress best known for her roles in The Handmaid's Tale, Ghostbusters: Afterlife, and Young Sheldon. While young, she has been acting since she was five, and she has had at least 34 film and television roles.

She plays Maysilee Donner, the spoiled mean girl who turns into a star when she is reaped for the 50th Hunger Games. She has a twin sister, Merrilee (whose daughter later gifts Katniss the Mockingjay pin), and is friends with Asterid March, Katniss's mother. She allies herself with Haymitch during the game, and the two share a sibling-like bond.

Ben Wang is a 29-year-old actor best known for his roles in Karate Kid: Legends and American Born Chinese.

He plays Wyatt Callow, Haymitch's fellow District 12 boy tribute. The Callow family runs the gambling scene in District 12, placing bets on the Hunger Games tributes each year.

Wang’s Instagram is @definitelynotbenwang.

Glenn Close is a 79-year-old Tony, Emmy, and Golden Globe winner best known for her roles in Fatal Attraction, 101 Dalmatians, and The Deliverance.

She plays Drusilla Sickle, the District 12 tributes’ escort. Drusilla is known for her many cosmetic surgeries that poorly attempt to disguise her age. She and Maysilee are the biggest divas in Panem, and their personalities clash disastrously.

Billy Porter is a 57-year-old Emmy, Tony, and Grammy Award–winning actor, singer, composer, director, and playwright.

He plays Magno Stift, the incompetent stylist for the District 12 tributes. His personal style involves wearing a plethora of live reptiles, and his hobbies include licking frogs for the psychedelic effects.

Molly McCann is a young Irish actress who previously appeared in Bad Sisters, Herself, and Rosie. She plays Louella McCoy, a young girl from District 12 who Haymitch views as a little sister and nicknamed “Sweetheart.” Fans were quick to notice McCann's resemblance to Katniss (Jennifer Laurence), the only other character who Haymitch refers to as Sweetheart.

McCann’s Instagram is @mollymccannactress.

Iona Bell is a young English actress who plays Louella’s uncanny lookalike, Lou Lou.

Her Instagram is @iona_bell_official_actor.

Kara Tointon is a 43-year-old English actress who previously appeared in EastEnders, The Teacher, and Too Good to Be True. She plays Willamae Abernathy, Haymitch's mother.
