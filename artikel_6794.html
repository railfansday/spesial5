Zimmermann Hosts a Star-Studded Soirée in Downtown New York City

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Katie Holmes, Uma Thurman, and Naomi Watts came out to celebrate the brand’s cruise 2027 collection.

Inside Indochine’s red-lit, retro-inspired French-Vietnamese dining room in New York City, Zimmermann gathered friends of the brand to celebrate its cruise 2027 collection. Presented on the sun-drenched Côte d’Azur in June, the label’s latest served as the perfect bit of vacation and occasion-ready dressing for November in New York. Now that the lineup has officially hit stores, fashion insiders and fans of the brand alike (including Uma Thurman, Eve Hewson, Katie Holmes, and Naomi Watts) showed up dripping in ruffled gowns, sailor-inspired stripes, chunky shearlings, and ethereal lace and silks from the collection. Against the dark, 1940s-inspired tropical salon interior of Indochine, it was the ideal mix of downtown energy and New York glamour.

As champagne flowed through the bar area, glasses clinked while miniature crab cakes and spring rolls were passed. Zimmermann’s sister duo Nicky and Simone Zimmermann could be spotted catching up with friends new and old. “We have a very, very close connection with New York,” Nicky told ELLE in between greeting guests. “It was pretty much when I started the business, one of the first places internationally that I went to in the ’90s, and it blew my mind. It’s been inspirational to us 100 percent.” It was a rare occasion for the brand; for years, Zimmermann has shown at Paris Fashion Week (after presenting in New York in the mid-2010s) and the duo oscillates between Sydney and Paris for most of the year.

Zimmermann’s painterly and intrinsically bohemian flourishes could be seen in the many outfits of guests, all highlighting the vivid cruise collection. Think: decadent silky draped drop-waisted gowns and paneled lace maxi dresses, or, for the more demure, sleek all-black looks that captured the New York essence. “For me, there’s a little bit of a sense of humor behind it—without insulting anybody—it was about the story when Australia actually beat America in the America’s Cup, which is a very famous sailing race,” Nicky told ELLE of the collection’s inspiration.

“I think it was in 1983, and America had not lost the America’s Cup in something like 173 years; it was a ridiculous winning streak, and Australia came along. The inspiration is more about a David and Goliath kind of story,” she continued. “We came along as the Aussies, not established in the sailing world. They were very cheeky, rogue, kind of Aussie young guys up against this amazing, sophisticated establishment of the New York Yacht Club. And they never lost—and we beat them. So in Australia, it’s a legendary story.” Nicky combined the sportiness of sailing gear with the sort of elite New York Yacht Club preppiness, resulting in colorful silk scarf embellishment or sweaters decked out in accessorized pins. “They would hold the most incredible parties and the women were so glamorous and sophisticated and beautiful and not anything that Australia could relate to at the time, so I loved the contrast.”

Conversation was flowing and blooming anthuriums covered the tables as guests took their seats. Nicky kicked off the dinner with a speech, musing on her early days in New York on the Lower East Side and the many parties she attended in Tribeca before sharing more inspiration behind the collection: “It’s about establishment, and then it’s about people, you know, working really hard, coming together as a team, defeating the undefeatable,” she said, with a laugh.

Next came an intimate performance by the singer and Bad Monkey actress Charlotte Lawrence, who sang two of her original songs, then led a folksy sing-along to “Dreams” by Fleetwood Mac. Even the most guarded of celebs and fashion’s elite couldn’t help but bob their heads and sing a tune. As guests dined on a family-style mix of kale salad, spicy shrimp, grilled salmon, barbecued chicken, and steak, the champagne glass clinks slowed as candles slowly flickered and the evening closed out with lemon tarts.

A little yacht-club polish, a little Aussie irreverence: Zimmermann celebrated cruise 2027 in high style.
