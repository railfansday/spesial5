US Army detonates lead explosives in Iowa despite contamination fears

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The US Army on Wednesday blew up 5 tons of lead-based explosives at an ammunition plant in Iowa despite community backlash over fears of environmental contamination. The detonation was postponed three times, but got the green light after a federal appeals court this week found the US Environmental Protection Agency adequately incorporated safeguards to minimise the dispersal of lead dust.
