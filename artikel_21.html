Duran Duran guitarist Andy Taylor dies at 65

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Andy Taylor first revealed he was battling cancer in 2022 when Duran Duran was inducted into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame. The band had numerous hits in the 1980s, including "Girls on Film" and "Hungry Like The Wolf."

Duran Duran guitarist Andy Taylor died after "a long and courageous battle with prostate cancer," his family announced on Wednesday. He was 65.

Taylor was responsible for 1980s new wave hits like "Girls on Film," "Hungry Like The Wolf" and "Rio."

The band's pioneering music videos helped drive early success, including through airtime on MTV.

"From the moment he arrived in Birmingham from the North East with a guitar and an unshakeable belief in what we could become, he brought an indelible fire and swagger to our music," the band said in a statement.

"His playing drove the songs that took us around the world, and with a dry wit that was matched only by his wonderfully unsentimental, no-nonsense outlook on life he kept us grounded and gave the band its edge through the extraordinary years that followed."

Duran Duran accumulated a slew of accolades including two Grammys, six platinum records, two Ivor Novello Awards and the MTV Video Visionary Award.

Taylor parted with the band in 1986 to pursue other projects.

The band was due to reunite when they were inducted into Rock & Roll Hall of Fame in 2022.

However, Taylor was unable to be present. He revealed that he had been battling stage 4 metastatic prostate cancer for several years.

"I would like to thank each of my brothers in this great band," he wrote at the time. "You can dream about what happened to us but to experience it, on one's own terms, as mates, was beyond incredible."
