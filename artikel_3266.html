Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade Postponed For Weather

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

While families across the nation gather to celebrate the Thanksgiving holiday, one tradition will look a little different this year. The 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade has been postponed for weather, according to NBC News. The parade is now expected to take place on Saturday.

Weather experts have been warning U.S. residents about a rapidly approaching storm system making its way up the east coast, bringing with it high winds, heavy rains, and flood warnings. According to CNN, winds in New York City are expected to reach around 25 to 35 mph — which would be dangerous for the parade’s famous floats and giant balloons.

“The safety of our participants and spectators is our top priority, and after close consultation with our City and agency partners, we have made the decision to move this year’s celebration,” Macy’s said in a statement. “Thank you to the thousands of volunteers, performers and partners who create the magic millions enjoy each year. We look forward to celebrating together on Saturday.”

This is the first time the Macy’s Thanksgiving Parade has been postponed because of inclimate weather. It was cancelled in 1942, 1943, and 1944 because the U.S. needed all excess helium and rubber for the World War II effort. But past windy parades might provide insight into the decision to postpone this year’s. During the 1997 parade, winds broke a Barney the Dinosaur balloon and pushed a Cat in the Hat balloon into buildings, injuring one bystander. In 1998, a “Where The Wild Things Are” balloon hit a lamppost during its debut parade and had to be removed. In 2006 and 2019, parade officials had to lower the height of the balloons to avoid aggressive wind and subsequent damage.

More than 3 million people were expected to watch the parade along its 2.5 mile route. Grammy-nominated singer Raye was expected to open the parade with a holiday performance featuring her sisters, Amma and Absolutely; followed by floats and performances by Wyclef Jean, Smash Mouth, Natasha Bedingfield, Icona Pop, JoJo, Hunger Games star McKenna Grace, and Queen Latifah. No additional changes to the parade programming or lineup have been announced.
