Comedians Leah Rudick & Jessica Michelle Singleton Launch ‘People Pleasers’ Podcast With PodcastOne

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: Comedian Leah Rudick and Jessica Michelle Singleton have announced the launch of People Pleasers, a new comedy podcast with a multi-year deal with PodcastOne, which will serve as its exclusive sales and distribution network.

With People Pleasers, Rudick and Singleton are set to offer an unfiltered weekly conversation about self-improvement, self-sabotage, and the never-ending quest to get everyone to like them. Each week, the duo tackles everything from woo-woo self-improvement and personal reinvention to blatant self-sabotage and the everyday absurdities of navigating life. Think of it as a conversation with your best (and possibly worst) millennial girlfriends. Kit Gray, PodcastOne’s President and Co-Founder, told us: “When we founded PodcastOne, one of our tentpole goals was to amplify women’s voices and we’re thrilled to continue that legacy with the launch of People Pleasers. Leah and Jessica are two of the most sought after women in comedy and we feel privileged to provide them the space to create content that audiences and advertisers seek.” Watch on Deadline

Rudick is a Los Angeles-based actor, writer, and comedian who has amassed nearly two million followers across social media with her viral original characters, including Wealthy Woman, Joannie, and Love Coach. Known for her signature offbeat, ‘weird girl’ comedy, she was named one of Deadline’s Stand-Ups to Keep an Eye On in 2026. Her credits include HBO’s High Maintenance, IFC’s Commuters and Dropout TV’s Crowd Control. She has performed at Moontower, Just for Laughs, SF Sketchfest, and Laughing Skull festivals, and her debut comedy hour, Spiraling, is available on both Amazon and Apple TV.

Also a comedian, writer, and actor, Singleton broke out in Hulu’s Iliza’s Locals, the stand-up series from Iliza Shlesinger. Her comedy specials and Don’t Tell Comedy performances have garnered millions of views, with her sophomore comedy album, Horny for Death, debuting at No. 1 on iTunes and Amazon. Singleton has toured internationally alongside some of comedy’s biggest names, including Tom Segura, Theo Von, Mark Normand, Bobby Lee and Hannah Berner. Her screen credits include Showtime’s The Comedy Store docuseries and writing for The Roast of Whitney Cummings. She released her debut hour special, Hi Y’all!, in 2025.

PodcastOne’s roster of podcasts spans news, comedy, true crime, sports and society and culture and includes shows such as Karma and Chaos, Off the Vine with Kaitlyn Bristowe, LadyGang, Court Junkie, The Prosecutors and Varnamtown. Their shows are available through PodcastOne, Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, iHeart, Amazon and wherever podcasts are heard.

Both Rudick and Singleton are managed by Nate Dixon at Mosaic.
