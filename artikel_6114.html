Saudia's Surprising New 8-Hour UK Route On 110-Seat Jet: See Map Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Saudia is the fourth-largest airline operating flights between the United Kingdom and the Middle East. The airline has been reviewing its UK network in recent years. In fact, earlier this year, it discontinued services to Birmingham and London Gatwick. With that being said, the SkyTeam member now appears to be considering a new UK destination.

This time, to the Scottish capital, Edinburgh. According to Airport Coordination Limited's (ACL) Edinburgh Summer 2027 Initial Coordination Report, Saudia has secured slots at Edinburgh Airport (EDI) for the upcoming summer season. If the airline proceeds with the planned service, it will become its third UK destination.

According to the ACL report, Saudia has requested slots for four weekly flights to Edinburgh, using its 110-seat Airbus A320 aircraft. However, securing slots does not necessarily mean that the airline will operate the flights as planned. The aircraft type could also change, as airlines can revise their planned equipment after slots have been allocated.

Given the distance involved and the range limitations of a typical A320, operating the route with this aircraft would be unusual. One possible alternative is the Airbus A321XLR, which airlines are increasingly using on long-haul routes with relatively limited passenger demand.

According to ch-aviation data, the Saudi flag carrier currently operates five A321XLRs and is awaiting deliveries of another ten aircraft. It could therefore consider deploying the extra-long-range narrowbody on the Edinburgh route instead of the A320. In fact, Saudia already uses the A321XLR on its service between Jeddah’s King Abdulaziz International Airport (JED) and Manchester Airport (MAN).

Interestingly, the 110-seat A320 is no stranger to the UK. Saudia previously deployed the aircraft on its service between Neom Bay Airport (NUM) and London Gatwick Airport (LGW). The airline currently has seven A320s in this configuration and regularly uses them on several European routes.

According to scheduled data from aviation analytics provider Cirium, Saudia will deploy the aircraft on nine of its European routes in December, most of which originate from Jeddah. These include services to Barcelona, Rome Fiumicino, Frankfurt, Geneva, Istanbul, Munich and Zurich.

The airline will also use the 110-seat A320 on its flights from Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport (RUH) to Rome Fiumicino and Geneva. Apart from this, the SkyTeam member also operates its 188-seat Airbus A321neo on some European routes and regularly deploys the A321XLR, which accommodates 144 passengers.

A new era of travel dawns as Saudia unveils plans for its cutting-edge fleet addition.

Nevertheless, it will be interesting to see whether Saudia goes ahead with its plans to serve Edinburgh. Currently, there are no nonstop flights between the Scottish capital and Saudi Arabia, meaning passengers have to travel via other airports, such as Doha, Dubai, Istanbul, or London Heathrow. Demand for travel between the UK and Saudi Arabia is strong. In 2025, more than 1.76 million passengers traveled between the two countries.

According to Cirium data, four airlines have scheduled flights between the two countries for 2027; Saudia is by far the largest operator in the market. The airline serves four routes, three of which connect London Heathrow Airport (LHR) with King Fahd International Airport (DMM) in Dammam, Riyadh, and Jeddah. The fourth connects Jeddah with MAN.

London Heathrow – Riyadh (Up to 3x daily)

London Heathrow – Jeddah (Up to 3x daily)

London Heathrow – Riyadh (Daily from Jan. 11)

London Heathrow – Red Sea International (3x weekly)**

**Depends on whether the carrier starts service in December 2026

Apart from Saudia, newcomer Riyadh Air offers flights from Riyadh to London Heathrow and Manchester. British Airways is also set to resume its Heathrow-Riyadh service in January 2027. In addition, the all-business-class carrier beOnd had planned to launch three weekly nonstop flights between Red Sea International Airport (RSI) and London Heathrow on December 15, 2026. However, it has recently paused its scheduled flights for December.
