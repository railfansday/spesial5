Delta SkyMiles & World Of Hyatt Partnership Live: Dual Earning, Milestone Rewards, & Linking Accounts

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

In September 2026, Delta SkyMiles and World of Hyatt unveiled plans to launch a new, long term strategic partnership. This announcement came just shortly after American AAdvantage and World of Hyatt announced that they will be ending their partnership.

At the time, we didn’t actually have any details as to what the partnership would entail, but that has now changed, as additional information has been revealed. Parts of the partnership are even now live, and members can already link their accounts.

Delta and Hyatt are launching a new loyalty program collaboration, which comes with a “shared ambition to create additional value, recognition and engagement opportunities across more of the travel journey.” This includes reciprocal opportunities to earn rewards, and also reciprocal opportunities to select perks with the partner brand.

Here’s how Laurie Blair, Hyatt’s SVP of Global Marketing and Loyalty, describes this:

“World of Hyatt and Delta SkyMiles were each built to make travel more meaningful and rewarding, which makes this collaboration feel meant to be. By connecting the Delta flight and Hyatt stay, we are helping members get more from the journeys they are already taking while opening the door to new experiences, destinations and memories. It is an exciting next step in our commitment to caring for members throughout the full travel journey, from takeoff to check out.”

Here’s how Dwight James, Delta’s SVP of Customer Engagement & Loyalty, describes this:

“At Delta, we know loyalty is something we earn by delivering meaningful value and experiences that make our Members want to keep choosing us. Our relationship with World of Hyatt gives us another way to do that by recognizing and rewarding Members across more of their travel, while creating new ways to earn, engage and experience both brands.”

Effective immediately (as of November 17, 2026), elite members of both loyalty programs can earn simultaneously with the new “Dual Earn” benefit. Members must link their accounts, and can then earn additional rewards.

Basically, Hyatt has dumped American in favor of Delta. Admittedly the partnership between American and Hyatt had already been scaled back over time. The two programs used to offer a dual accrual opportunity as well, but that was eventually cut.

After all, companies only want to offer perks like that if they’re driving incremental business, and I imagine at some point it’s hard to justify that expense, when you feel like there’s not much upside left:

Now the Delta and Hyatt partnership is starting out in a similar way to how the American one did, with one major exception. On the dual accrual front, SkyMiles elite members are lucky, as they earn rewards on all qualifying Hyatt spending. Meanwhile World of Hyatt members only earn rewards when booking premium tickets, which is a bit disappointing. And here I was thinking that all seats on Delta aircraft are premium!!

I do have to say, as much as I’m a World of Hyatt loyalist, the program has certainly become less differentiated over time. While Delta can argue all it wants about how premium it is, SkyMiles simply isn’t as good of a loyalty program as AAdvantage. So perhaps this swap reflects the direction Hyatt is trying to head on loyalty… I dunno.

Delta and Hyatt have launched an exclusive new partnership, which explains why American and Hyatt cut ties. The partnership is now live, and it includes dual points accrual opportunities, reciprocal milestone perks, and some limited time opportunities.

At a minimum, it’s definitely worth linking accounts. And if you’re a World of Hyatt member with a stay planned before the end of the year, be sure to register for an extra elite night, as there’s no reason not to take advantage of that.
