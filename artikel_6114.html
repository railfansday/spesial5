The Democratic Party Is at a Critical Crossroad

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Until 2026, the most important midterm year of the last quarter-century was very probably 2010. Barack Obama famously called the elections a "shellacking" for the Democratic party and he was right. Thanks to a sluggish economic recovery from the Great Recession and backlash to the passage of the Affordable Care Act, Republicans took control of the House with a 63-seat gain - the largest House sweep since the 1940s - and won six seats in the Senate.
