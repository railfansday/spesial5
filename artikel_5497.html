OneTrust’s Fall ’26 Release expands AI visibility, privacy and third-party risk tools

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Governance platform provider OneTrust LLC today announced its Fall ’26 Release, a set of platform updates said to bring governance into the workflows where business decisions are made.

Leading the list of updates is a revised AI inventory, which is designed to reflect changes across a customer’s environment on a continuous basis. According to OneTrust, the change is needed because many organizations running complex networks of AI systems and agents have only limited visibility into how those systems operate. Model associations, potential matches and deprecation indicators from providers are now pulled into the inventory from a curated library of foundation models.

Relationships among AI systems, models, agents, projects, risks, controls and workflows can be explored through an interactive AI lineage view. AI performance and operational dashboards, added in the release, bring usage, associated risk, evaluation coverage, outbound personally identifiable information discovery and token consumption into one summary.

On the third-party risk side, a workflow rule that launches a risk assessment can now trigger OneTrust’s Deep Research AI skill. The in-depth research report that the skill compiles from public data sources is then used to answer the assessment questionnaire. OneTrust claims the integration can cut the time needed to complete a third-party risk assessment from weeks to minutes.

Drawing on previous assessments and supplier documentation, the company’s expanded AI Document Scanning and Inventory Response Analysis features can now also answer questions about the business engagement tied to a third party.

In the company’s consent management platform, navigation, configuration, scanning, publishing and geolocation management have also been brought into a single workflow under a modernized interface.

OneTrust is sharing availability details for the Fall ’26 Release through an overview webinar.

OneTrust has raised $1.13 billion to date, including $150 million in 2023 at a valuation of $4.5 billion. Investors in the company include Insight Partners LP, Coatue Management, Technology Crossover Ventures LP, SoftBank Vision Fund 2 LP, Franklin Resources Inc., Generation Investment Management LLP and Sands Capital Management.
