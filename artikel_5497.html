CoreWeave stock jumps 11% as earnings beat eases AI slowdown fears

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The AI cloud provider lifted its full-year revenue guidance to $13.0 billion-$13.3 billion and reported a $132.5 billion backlog

CoreWeave raised its full-year 2026 revenue guidance to a range of $13.0 billion to $13.3 billion after it reported third-quarter results on Tuesday that included revenue of $3.66 billion.

CoreWeave stock was up 11% before the opening bell on Wednesday. Shares of Nebius, Applied Digital and IREN rose in premarket trading as well.

Third-quarter revenue compared with $1.365 billion a year earlier. The company lost $709 million, or $1.27 per share, compared with a $110 million loss, or 22 cents per share, in the third quarter of 2025.

Adjusted loss was 95 cents a share, according to CNBC. Analysts had expected a loss of $1.16 a share and revenue of $3.55 billion.

CoreWeave raised its full-year adjusted operating income guidance to a range of $1.275 billion to $1.375 billion, a 26% increase at the midpoint from prior guidance. Analysts had forecast adjusted operating income of $1.07 billion and 2026 revenue of $12.86 billion, according to CNBC. The company's August revenue guidance was $12.4 billion to $13.2 billion.

The company now expects capital expenditures of $37 billion to $39 billion for the year, up from a prior range of $35 billion to $39 billion. It cited an expectation of delivering infrastructure to customers on an earlier schedule than planned. It continues to expect more than 1.85 gigawatts of active power at year-end and raised its annualized run-rate revenue guidance to $20 billion to $21 billion to exit 2026.

Revenue backlog was about $132.5 billion as of Sept. 30. That compares with $104 billion at the end of June.

"Our growth is supported by multiple sources of funding, including financing backed by customer contracts, operating cash flow, and approximately $9 billion in customer prepayments and deposits expected in the next three months alone," CEO Michael Intrator said in a statement.

CoreWeave stock is down more than 40% from where it traded a month ago. Investors had questioned the company's heavy borrowing amid rising interest rates, according to Reuters.

Chief Financial Officer Nitin Agrawal said during the earnings call that CoreWeave has no plans to sell more unsecured bonds at the parent-company level through 2027 while yields remain where they are, according to CNBC. He projected that CoreWeave will raise less than $5 billion in debt at the parent level through 2027.

According to Reuters, CoreWeave expects its adjusted results to turn profitable during the opening six months of 2028.
