2026 BCDA Grand: Newfield

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Phase one of the Newfield master plan in Palm City, Florida, spans 500 acres and establishes the vision, planning principles, and character that will define the community at full buildout.

The community was envisioned around four planning objectives: preserving the site’s natural landscape, creating a walkable and connected community, integrating agriculture and recreation into daily life, and fostering a lasting sense of place.

Mattamy Homes brought this vision to life, offering an alternative to conventional suburban development by balancing thoughtfully designed neighborhoods with environmental stewardship.

As part of the development, 70% of the property’s 3,411 acres will be preserved as dedicated open space. Rather than relegating wetlands, forests, lakes, parks, conservation areas, and trails to the perimeter, Newfield weaves the natural assets throughout the development.

Rosette Park, the community’s first neighborhood, includes walkable streets, front porches, rear-loaded garages, public parks, village squares, town greens, and additional spaces for recreation. The first phase also includes Newfield Farm, the Barn, the Citrus House wellness amenity, and the trails within the Kiplinger Conservancy.

Each element highlights the blending of agricultural land, open space, and development. Rather than treating preserved areas as passive amenities, Mattamy Homes and its partners transformed these spaces into destinations for residents and visitors.
