DeepX plans to spend more than $100 million on a Samsung-made AI chip for robots

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The South Korean startup sent final DX-M2 designs to Samsung's foundry and expects first silicon by March 2027

DeepX, a South Korean startup, intends to commit upward of $100 million to a next-generation AI chip that will be fabricated on Samsung Electronics’ 2-nanometer process, according to Bloomberg.

DeepX has completed work on the chip, called the DX-M2, and handed the finished blueprints to Samsung's contract-manufacturing arm so that production can begin, according to Bloomberg. The company anticipates getting its first working chips by March 2027, followed by testing by customers and large-scale production in 2028.

The estimated cost of more than $100 million covers performance verification and production engineering expenses, according to Bloomberg.

DeepX is aiming the DX-M2 at three markets: robotics, factory and other industrial machinery, and PCs with built-in AI capabilities, according to Bloomberg. The chip is designed to handle generative-AI workloads while drawing only about 5 watts.

The project signals DeepX's wager that AI models are better run on the devices themselves than on servers in far-off data centers, according to Bloomberg. That approach can cut power consumption, expense and latency by avoiding round trips of data to the cloud.

DeepX's semiconductors are aimed at AI tasks and uses beyond the data center, a type of facility that is drawing growing pushback over how much electricity and water it uses, according to Bloomberg.
