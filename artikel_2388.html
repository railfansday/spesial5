Scally, Stöger and Machino all on the chopping block at Gladbach

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

According to a report in BILD, Joe Scally, Shuto Machino and Kevin Stöger are allowed to leave Borussia Mönchengladbach.

The Rhineland club have seemingly suggested they would not stand in the trio's way, provided a suitable offer is received.

Scally, featuring at right wing-back, has been one of the bright spots this season, contributing to six goals in total.

However, given his contract expires in the summer, this is Gladbach's last chance to receive a transfer fee.

This is also the case with creative midfielder Stöger, who is once again playing second fiddle this year since Alexander Blessin arrived.

Finally, Machino's contract with Gladbach runs until 2029. The striker, who joined from Holstein Kiel last year for €8million.

As Machino is one of the club's highest earners, Borussia would likely grant him a transfer in the winter – he hasn't lived up to expectations.
