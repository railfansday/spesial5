Ascenda Powers Australia's Largest Banking Loyalty Program, CommBank Yello

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Australia's largest bank elevates banking loyalty with a broadened rewards program powered by Ascenda's global content network and AI-driven marketing and personalisation.

SYDNEY, Nov. 22, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Ascenda, the global loyalty network that drives growth for the world's leading brands, is providing loyalty technology and capabilities supporting CommBank Yello, Commonwealth Bank of Australia's customer loyalty and recognition program.

CommBank Yello has recently expanded into a points-based program, with more than nine million CommBank customers able to earn points across home loans, term deposits, cards and insurance, and redeem them with some of Australia's best-known brands.

The program is supported by Ascenda's next-generation loyalty stack that fuses its global content network and AI-driven marketing and personalisation.

The program builds on Ascenda's track record of loyalty innovation in financial services, centered around brand-wide engagement and richer customer engagement and value.

"CommBank has taken a bold, forward-thinking step: to invest comprehensively in customer loyalty with a broad, unified proposition that rewards customers whole-of-bank relationship," said Alex Ramos, SVP & General Manager, Australia and New Zealand at Ascenda.

Ascenda's platform underpins the reimagined CommBank Yello program, announced by Commonwealth Bank in August 2026.

Commonwealth Bank (ASX:CBA) is one of Australia's leading providers of personal banking, business and institutional banking and share broking services. With more than 15 million customers and a history spanning more than a century, the Group's purpose is to build a brighter future for all. Commonwealth Bank is Australia's leader in digital banking and maintains the largest branch network across the country. Headquartered in Sydney, Australia, the Bank operates brands including Bankwest in Australia and ASB in New Zealand. For more information on Commonwealth Bank, visit www.commbank.com.au
