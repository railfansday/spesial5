BTC price surged just now after Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, reversing last week’s brutal sell-off across technology stocks. BTC climbed toward $63,800, while Nvidia shares price exploded by more than 25% and Nasdaq futures jumped as investors rushed back into risk assets.

The rally came after Bitcoin fell close to $55,000 over the weekend. The sudden recovery lifted crypto prices alongside stocks, suggesting traders were unwinding bearish positions as confidence returned to the AI sector.

The rebound offers some relief for crypto holders, but Bitcoin still has substantial ground to recover after its recent losses.

Nvidia’s board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO, and board director on Monday, effective immediately. The decision reverses his departure on November 12, which followed reports involving alleged personal relationships and a suspected foreign intelligence connection.

Investors responded immediately. Nvidia shares surged nearly 30% in premarket trading, while Nasdaq-100 futures jumped more than 6%. The broader rebound spread across semiconductor stocks and companies tied to AI computing infrastructure.

The move rescues the global market after Nvidia suffered a steep weekly decline. Its shares had fallen nearly 23% the previous week as the AI trade weakened and concerns over technology valuations intensified.

Huang’s return helped reverse that pressure in a single session, adding fresh momentum to a market that had been struggling to find buyers.

Bitcoin climbed as high as $63,800 before easing toward $63,000, leaving it roughly 4% higher on the day. Ethereum and other major crypto also recovered as traders moved back into riskier assets.

The timing suggests Bitcoin’s bounce was closely tied to the market reversal. Nvidia’s surge helped lift technology stocks, Nasdaq futures gained sharply, and crypto followed the same direction.

Short sellers also faced pressure as prices recovered from weekend lows. Bitcoin’s rapid climb forced bearish traders to reassess positions entered during the sell-off, adding another potential source of buying.

Today’s move is important for crypto traders because Bitcoin had been struggling to attract buyers during the recent downturn. A recovery above $63,000 puts the market back on firmer ground, but it does not erase the damage from the previous decline.

Jensen Huang’s return has delivered a fresh catalyst to markets that desperately needed one. BTC price is bouncing, Nvidia is roaring back, and traders are finally getting a session that looks different from last week’s relentless selling.
