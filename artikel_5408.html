Perplexity AI predicts that Binance’s Perp DEX, ASTER crypto, could be on the verge of significant developments. By January 1, 2027, and based on an intentionally extreme hypothetical scenario, the AI predicts that ASTER price could potentially surpass Hyperliquid.

Perplexity’s AI model hypothesizes that a consortium of major traditional brokers and fintech companies would officially designate ASTER as the primary decentralized platform for trading tokenized U.S. equities, commodities, and related perpetual contracts.

In this scenario, significant volumes of equity and commodity derivatives could transition to an on-chain system through ASTER’s multi-chain infrastructure. This would position ASTER as a vital component in the next wave of regulated on-chain trading for traditional assets.

On higher timeframes, ASTER has been consolidating in the $0.70 to $0.72 range after a broader pullback from earlier peaks around $2.40 to $3.00.

The price continues to hold above key intermediate support levels while facing resistance in the $0.75 to $0.85 zone. In a typical bull market, a sustained breakout above $1.00 to $1.20 would confirm intermediate strength and set up higher recovery targets.

In the extreme scenario of increased adoption of tokenized equities and commodities trading, breaking through significant resistance levels could trigger strong measured-move extensions and multi-cycle Fibonacci projections from the longer-term base.

Currently, momentum indicators are neutral, suggesting substantial room for price movement into the extreme overbought territory often seen during parabolic advances.

Key near-term support levels are around $0.68 to $0.70 and $0.60 to $0.65; maintaining these levels would help keep the broader market structure intact.

Several external factors could enhance this optimistic outlook. A sustainable resolution to major geopolitical conflicts, such as the Russia-Ukraine war or a lasting ceasefire involving Iran, along with the normalization of shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, could lower risk premiums and encourage capital to flow back into growth and risk assets.

On the economic front, a decisive shift by major central banks toward more accommodative monetary policy, coupled with high liquidity, would promote risk-taking across markets.

In the cryptocurrency space, Bitcoin and Ethereum ETF inflows would continue to grow, institutional adoption of decentralized derivatives would increase, trading volumes in tokenized real-world assets would rise, and on-chain stock trading would expand.

A renewed appetite for altcoins would also create a favorable liquidity environment. This environment would particularly benefit high-performance perpetual DEX platforms like Aster.

In this highly speculative scenario, assuming a full-blown crypto bull market returns and external conditions remain supportive, Aster (ASTER) could reach $12 to $22, with an ambitious target of $35 or more by January 1, 2027.

This is purely speculative and should be viewed as entertainment rather than a realistic outlook. Achieving this would require extraordinary coordination among traditional brokers, regulators, and the Aster ecosystem, which is currently highly unlikely.
