raumlaborberlin at IE School of Architecture & Design’s COMMON(S) Lecture Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

IE School of Architecture & Design welcomes raumlaborberlin, the internationally acclaimed German architecture collective, as part of its COMMON(S) 2026–27 Lecture Series. On November 25, 2026, Benjamin Foerster-Baldenius, founding member of raumlabor, will present the lecture "In the Flow of Making" at IE University's campus in Madrid.

IE School of Architecture & Design welcomes raumlaborberlin, the internationally acclaimed German architecture collective, as part of its COMMON(S) 2026–27 Lecture Series. On November 25, 2026, Benjamin Foerster-Baldenius, founding member of raumlabor, will present the lecture "In the Flow of Making" at IE University's campus in Madrid.

Founded in Berlin in 1999, raumlaborberlin is a group of nine members working at the intersection of architecture, urban planning, art and performance. Rather than considering architecture primarily as the production of buildings, raumlaborberlin understands it as an ongoing process shaped by people, places and their relationships. Its projects often engage with neglected, abandoned or transitional urban sites, transforming them into spaces of encounter, learning and collective imagination. Through what they describe as research-based design, raumlaborberlin develops experimental approaches that involve local communities and other urban actors in imagining and testing alternative futures.
