Cabeceiras do Aricanduva Municipal Natural Park / Natureza Urbana

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Read the original version in Portuguese here.

Text description provided by the architects. Located in Cidade Tiradentes, in the eastern zone of São Paulo, the Nascentes Hub of the Cabeceiras do Aricanduva Municipal Natural Park spans an area of approximately 96,000 m², featuring remnants of the Atlantic Forest and the headwaters of the Aricanduva River basin. Developed by Natureza Urbana in partnership with the Department of Implementation, Projects, and Works of the Municipal Secretariat for the Green and the Environment, the project brings the local community closer to this natural heritage while establishing a transition between the neighborhood and the preservation area.
