GTA 6 developer shares heartbreaking story behind baby daughter’s in-game memorial

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

A GTA 6 developer has thanked Rockstar Games for immortalizing his baby daughter in an in-game memorial after he and his wife lost her five months into the pregnancy.

Since GTA 6 launched on November 19, millions of players have been exploring Leonida, uncovering hidden secrets and Easter eggs scattered throughout Rockstar’s sprawling open world.

However, some of the game’s most meaningful discoveries have been deeply personal tributes to the loved ones of those who spent years developing it.

Rockstar lead game tools programmer Bradley Stone took to X to share footage of himself visiting a memorial bearing his daughter’s name, Evelyn Stone, alongside the date April 8, 2023.

“To our baby we lost at 5 months pregnancy, you have been forever memorialised and honoured,” he wrote.

“Thank you, Rockstar for giving us this place to remember our loved ones, to show this game was made by real people and thank you for all the support/care you gave me through my grief.”

Stone explained that he and his wife had already experienced multiple miscarriages when a scan in 2023 revealed their baby had a severe case of spina bifida.

After further examinations and consultations with doctors, the couple learned that their daughter would not survive. They were left facing a medical termination, with Stone’s wife having to endure nearly 24 hours of labor.

This was the darkest moment of our lives and I cannot put into words the strength and courage my wife showed and I can only love her more for that.A few years of ups and downs and more sadness we were finally blessed with a beautiful baby girl.

“We cried and held each other close as she delivered the baby,” he recalled. “The next hardest part was leaving the hospital without our baby. This still breaks me to this day.”

Stone described the experience as the “darkest moment” of their lives, praising his wife’s strength and courage. He also revealed that, following several more years of ups and downs, the couple had welcomed a baby girl.

“To the baby we lost, we named you Evelyn,” he wrote. “You will forever be in our hearts.”

Stone isn’t the only Rockstar employee whose loved ones have been immortalized in GTA 6. Fellow developer Matt Green previously revealed that his late mother, Bev, had been honored at the memorial just north of Hamlet.

While GTA 6 continues to shatter records around the world, the memorial serves as a touching reminder of the people behind the game and the loved ones they lost along the way.
