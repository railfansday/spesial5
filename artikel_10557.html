Anthropic stock closes 17% higher on its first trading day as Nasdaq sputters

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Update Thursday: 4:05 p.m.

Shares of Anthropic, PBC closed 17% above their offering price on Thursday in a sign that investor interest in the AI revolution has not waned, although the enthusiasm was not enough to lift other Big Tech companies in the space.

The Claude maker’s stock opened at $111.50 a share on their first trading day, well above its $83-per-share IPO price, but then it fell shortly afterward, closing the day at $97.17.

Much of the Nasdaq did not fare so well, with AI-adjacent companies including Alphabet, Meta Platforms, Amazon, and SpaceX all down between 2%-4%, despite seeing an early-morning lift during premarket trading as interest in Anthropic’s listing hit fever pitch.

Anthropic raised a record-breaking $81.3 billion in its IPO. Now all eyes will be on how it and its peers perform in the days and weeks ahead. Shares were down slighly in after-hours trading shortly after the closing bell.

Update Thursday, 12:35 p.m.

Shares of Anthropic, PBC opened at $111.50 on Thursday in the year’s most hyped stock market debut. That’s roughly 34% higher than the $83 share price set before trading began—itself an increase over the AI giant’s original target range.

However, the stock quickly began to fall in the first hour and was trading at around $99 a share as of this writing.

Much of the market also moved lower, with the Nasdaq down around 0.73% as of 12:40 p.m. ET.

Original story:

Today, the most hyped IPO of the year (and that’s saying something) is expected to finally take place.

Claude LLM maker Anthropic, PBC (Nasdaq: ANT) will debut on the Nasdaq to much fanfare and lofty expectations. But while the Nasdaq opens at 9:30 a.m. ET, it’s likely Anthropic’s shares will not begin trading to the general public until later.

Here’s what you need to know.

The largest IPO in history

Today is Anthropic’s market debut, and its shares will begin trading on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. The initial public offering is the largest in history, surpassing SpaceX’s $75 billion IPO in June. The AI giant raised $81.3 billion in the offering.

The forthcoming stock listing is also the most-hyped of the year.

As Fast Company previously noted, Anthropic is considered a pure-play AI company. That is, the company’s sole business is artificial intelligence, as opposed to, say, SpaceX, which is mainly a space transportation and telecommunications company first, and an AI company second.

As the largest pure-play AI IPO ever, there’s a lot riding on how the shares react. If investors scoop them up and the share price pops, it could suggest that Wall Street still has an appetite for riskier AI-related stocks, despite recent pullbacks in the broader tech market.

However, don’t expect to know how ANT shares are performing as soon as the Nasdaq opens at 9:30 a.m. ET. That’s because, despite the market open, the first buy and sell orders for ANT shares likely won’t be fulfilled until later in the day.

What time do Anthropic’s shares begin trading today?

If a stock is already listed on an exchange, its shares usually begin trading to the public as soon as that exchange opens. But for market debuts, things are a little different.

Stock exchanges know that there is frequently a flurry of orders (both buy and sell) when a hot stock debuts.

If the stock were to trade immediately when the market opened, it could cause extreme price volatility, sending the stock up or down significantly in a matter of seconds. Another reason is operational: Nasdaq simply needs time to match orders—buyers with sellers—and determine a price.

Because of this, markets like the Nasdaq have procedures in place that delay trading by a little bit so buyers and sellers can get a better idea of demand and price the stock accordingly. Nasdaq calls this a Halt Cross.

Only once Nasdaq has determined an opening clearing price based on all its buy and sell orders does it release the stock for trading.

Why does this sound familiar?

If this sounds familiar, it’s because it’s exactly the same process that happened with the SpaceX IPO.

While SPCX shares debuted on the Nasdaq on June 12, they did not begin trading immediately when the Nasdaq opened at 9:30 a.m. ET. Instead, SpaceX’s shares began trading about two to three hours after the morning bell.

So, what time, then, will Anthropic’s shares actually begin trading on the Nasdaq?

It’s impossible to say for sure. Nasdaq’s rules don’t restrict the Halt Cross to a certain defined period, such as two hours. The Halt Cross lasts as long as it needs to.

However, if history is any indication, the Halt Cross usually ends anywhere from 2-4 hours after markets open.

That rough estimate suggests that Anthropic shares will begin trading at some point roughly between 11:30 a.m. ET and 1:30 p.m. ET today.
