Fernandes: Man Utd must fight for our lives

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Bruno Fernandes has urged Manchester United to "stick together and fight for our lives" after their dramatic 2-2 draw with Sporting CP in the Champions League.

Senne Lammens saved a 31st-minute penalty from Luis Suarez, only to give away a second spot-kick less than 90 seconds later, and the Colombian made no mistake that time.

Amad Diallo then had an equaliser disallowed as Youri Tielemans – who was sent off late on – was adjudged to have committed a foul in the build-up, though it looked like he won the ball.

Fernandes returned to his former club with a quickfire second-half brace, netting from the spot before a low drive took a big deflection off a divot in the pitch to wrong-foot Kaique.

However, Lammens spilled a catch to gift Suarez an acrobatic equaliser before a United winner was ruled out at the other end – Patrick Dorgu was penalised for fouling the goalkeeper, who had flapped at the catch.

United have conceded two or more goals in seven consecutive games in all competitions for the first time since between October and November 1977 (a run of eight), while no side across Europe's big five leagues have conceded more than them this term (36).

"[At half-time, the manager] wanted more aggression, more bodies," Fernandes told TNT Sports. "The first goal comes from that.

"We need points to get through. We need to focus on the next one. Now the moment is to get back to the Premier League. We need to stick together and fight for our lives.

"Obviously, I think we started the game well. We gave a penalty away where Senne saved it. We gave one or two counters, but we were very good in recovery positions.

"They got the goal, but we were still in control of the game. We didn't have much people in the last third. Second half, we got the two goals. That was the moment we should push a bit more.

"Mistakes are part of football, and from that you get goals. Everyone has to take the blame for everything we do good and we do bad as well. We need to be together in the good and in the bad moments."

United have won just three out of 17 games in all competitions this season (D6 L8) – among Premier League sides, only Nottingham Forest have won fewer (two in 12 games).

The Red Devils have also received five red cards in all competitions this season – two more than any other Premier League side.

They are only the fourth English side to receive three red cards in one Champions League season, along with Arsenal in 2001-02, Tottenham in 2010-11 and Manchester City in 2014-15.

But Pochettino, who is still waiting for his first win in charge of United, was impressed by the performance and wants to carry the positives forward.

"It depends which way you prefer [to analyse the game]," he told TNT Sports. "So proud of the players.

"It was a very tough game. I like the team for the 90 minutes, the personality, the character. We showed the way we want to play. We've only been working together one week.

"Always we need to concentrate more and focus more. Try to recover better. We start to fix. With a performance like this, it's the best medicine to prepare the next game.

"The priority is next one. We need to believe more and more and more. Second half, we recovered a lot of balls in the opposition half. That's the way we want to play.

"We need to go to Old Trafford and show the commitment and play the way we want to play."
