Zirkzee warns Man Utd must look at themselves ahead of Brentford clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Joshua Zirkzee refused to blame the officials for Manchester United's failure to beat Sporting CP in the Champions League, instead urging his team-mates to find a response.

Mauricio Pochettino's wait for a first win in charge of United continued on Wednesday following a 2-2 draw with Sporting, though the encounter did not come without controversy.

Luis Suarez gave Sporting a 33rd-minute lead with a penalty, just three minutes after seeing a spot-kick saved by Senne Lammens at the Estadio Jose Alvalade.

United thought they had equalised through Amad Diallo before the break, but the goal was ruled out for a foul by Youri Tielemans when he appeared to win the ball cleanly.

The Red Devils then netted twice in the space of two minutes early in the second half, with Bruno Fernandes scoring a penalty before seeing his long-distance strike hit a divot and fly over the stunned Kaique.

But more drama would come, with Lammens failing to deal with a cross and allowing Suarez to score, but United were denied a late winner when Patrick Dorgu was adjudged to have fouled Kaique from a corner, with the decision confirmed by a VAR review.

Speaking to MUTV, Zirkzee said: "In my opinion, there were definitely some key decisions there that didn't really favour us.

"We shouldn't look at the referee, we should look at ourselves instead, but it definitely has an impact on us, you can't deny that.

"But the team that we have, we should come here and not look at the referee and make sure that we get the win, regardless of the decisions that he makes."

United have the chance to put things right against Brentford in Pochettino's first game at Old Trafford, and Zirkzee acknowledged the importance of getting all three points.

"It's a big test," Zirkzee said of Brentford. "The fans deserve a result and all we want is a result. We've got to dig ourselves out of the trenches."

Brentford, meanwhile, are flying high after thumping Everton 4-0 on Monday, with Keith Andrews' side moving up to sixth in the Premier League and ever closer to the top four.

The Bees enjoyed their joint-biggest home win in the Premier League, equalling their 4-0 thrashing of Man United in August 2022, with Keane Lewis-Potter scoring twice.

And Lewis-Potter's fine display, along with his performances this season, earned him a new five-and-a-half year deal with Brentford, which was announced earlier this week.

In 11 games this season, Lewis-Potter (four) has already beaten his best-ever scoring season in the Premier League (three in 36 apps in 2025-26 and three in 30 in 2023-24).

"Keane is constantly growing. He's become part of the fabric of the club, from a very young man coming from Hull to establishing himself in the group," Andrews said.

"Over the period I've been here, we've seen real progression in his game and his versatility in terms of where he plays it. His consistency has also been very good.

"We've lost some players that went on that initial journey, like Ethan Pinnock, Christian Norgaard and Bryan Mbeumo. The only four left from the promotion team are Mathias Jensen, Rico Henry, Josh Dasilva and Vitaly Janelt.

"You've then got Kristoffer Ajer, who came in for the first year of the Premier League, and Keane’s wrapped into that new breed of cultural carriers – it's really important to keep that. I like working with him. He's such a talented player, and I'm delighted he's going to be here until 2032."

After scoring only his second brace in a Champions League match (also November 2020 vs Istanbul Basaksehir) in midweek, Fernandes will be hoping to have a similar impact at Old Trafford to get his team back to winning ways.

He is on 149 goal involvements in the Premier League (75 goals, 74 assists), while his next assist will make him the eighth player to register at least 75 goals and 75 assists in the competition.

Anthony has four assists in his last five Premier League games, one more than he managed in his first 75 appearances in the competition (three).

Only Mikkel Damsgaard (four) created more chances than Anthony's three against Everton, and you feel he will be key once again at Old Trafford.

United have won all five of their Premier League home games against Brentford. They've only faced Wigan Athletic (eight), Birmingham City and Portsmouth (both seven) more at Old Trafford while maintaining a 100% win rate.

However, the Red Devils are winless in their last nine Premier League games (D5 L4), their longest ever run in the competition and longest in the league since an 11-game stretch between November 1989 and February 1990.

They started the day 18th in their 4-4 draw with Aston Villa, and 19th in their 4-0 defeat to Liverpool. This is the first time since between September and October 1986 (a run of six) that they have played 3+ consecutive league games when starting the day in the bottom three.

Having won just one of their first seven Premier League games against United (D1 L5), Brentford have now won two of their last three (L1). They've netted at least three goals in all three of their victories against the Red Devils (4-0, 4-3 and 3-1).

But Brentford are winless in their last 10 away league games (D7 L3), their longest run since a 15-game streak between April and December 2018 in the Championship.
