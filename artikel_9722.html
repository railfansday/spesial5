IronWallet Review: A Simple, Open Wallet That Is Growing Fast

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

IronWallet seems to have appeared out of nowhere, but according to Ermo Eero, the project has been around for four and a half years. The idea was to build a simple, easy-to-use crypto wallet that anyone can open, with no KYC, no ID checks, and no sign-ups. What started as a niche project has grown into a competitive product. Its most notable feature is gasless transfers, which let users move funds without holding the network's native coin to pay fees. IronWallet also offers gasless swaps, routed through SwapKit, one of the market's leading swap aggregators.

IronWallet comes across as a solid all-rounder. Everything runs inside the app: sending, receiving, swapping, and buying crypto. Everything seemed to work well. Buying requires some form of KYC, which is expected, but the choice of payment providers offered in the app was wider than I anticipated.

The biggest gap is network coverage. IronWallet supports 12 networks. They include many of the most popular chains, but established non-custodial wallets support considerably more, and IronWallet still has ground to make up against the market leaders.

The second gap is platform. There is no desktop or web version. Nearly every competitor offers one, and for users who manage funds from a computer, that is a real limitation.

IronWallet's answer to that gap is its MCP server. The MCP is open source, and it works somewhat like a desktop companion: it lets AI agents in desktop tools control the wallet, and it includes a local wallet manager that opens in your own browser for adding, importing, backing up, and deleting wallets. Experienced users of desktop AI agents will likely find it straightforward to set up. For newcomers, the advice is simple: read the FAQ first.

IronWallet is a clear, open, and easy-to-use non-custodial wallet, and it is improving quickly. Its gasless transfers and gasless swaps make everyday use simpler, and buying crypto inside the app is a practical bonus. Network count and platform reach are the areas to watch, and with a project that has been building steadily for years, the momentum suggests they will keep improving. For mobile users who value simplicity and no-KYC access, IronWallet is already a strong choice, and it is well worth a look.
