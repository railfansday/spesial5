The media in Spain have provided an update on FC Andorra goalkeeper Jesus Owono after the tragic death of Jesus de Miguel.

Earlier in the month, it was confirmed that CD Tenerife striker De Miguel had passed away at the age of just 30, on the back of a serious incident during a Spanish Segunda Division match against FC Andorra.

During stoppage time, the match was called off when De Miguel appeared to lose consciousness after a challenge from Owono, who was then sent off.

After De Miguel’s death, it was reported by the BBC that police in Spain have opened an investigation into possible involuntary manslaughter following the Spaniard’s death. The report stated: "Under Spanish law, any death that is deemed non-natural automatically triggers a judicial inquiry."

Now, according to Spanish news outlet Marca, Owono has made a request to reach out to De Miguel’s family.

It has been claimed that Owono has asked FC Andorra to try to set up a meeting, by letter, by phone or by travelling to Toledo, with De Miguel’s family.

The report stated that Owono wants to reach out to De Miguel’s family in order to ‘share in their grief and offer his condolences in person’.

Jesus Owono has broken his silence after the tragic passing of CD Tenerife forward Jesus de Miguel.

In addition, it has been claimed that Owono ‘wanted to call the family the day after the accident and even discussed the possibility of attending the funeral with those close to him’. However, it is believed that ‘both the club and his own inner circle advised him to wait a while longer’.

After Andorra reached out, it has been reported that DC Tenerife asked for more time out of respect for the De Miguel family. It is also unclear whether Owono will return to football.

Speaking recently, as reported by BBC Sport, FC Andorra manager Carles Manso stated that Owono is yet to decide on his own footballing future.
