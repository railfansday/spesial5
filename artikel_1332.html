Sky Sports: Man Utd Plotting £67m Move for Newcastle Star Malick Thiaw

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Manchester United are looking to bolster their defensive ranks in 2027, and a new target has emerged as a possibility for Mauricio Pochettino.

The Argentine has replaced Michael Carrick as the boss at Old Trafford this week, and will lead the team into a new era, with a defensive rebuild planned for the new year in both January and the summer.

Harry Maguire, Luke Shaw, and Lisandro Martinez are all out of contract at the end of the current campaign, and it seems unlikely that any of them will be staying at the club beyond this season. New arrivals are expected to take their place, and the club are once again looking at Premier League players who can come in to make an instant impact.

A number of central defenders have been linked with a January move to the club, including Brentford captain Nathan Collins and Fulham star Calvin Bassey.

But it seems United are in contact with a number of players at the Unique Sports Group agency, who represent Bassey, as a report by Sky Sports Germany states that the club are also showing a strong interest in Newcastle United star Malick Thiaw.

The 25-year-old joined the Magpies in the summer of 2025 from AC Milan in a deal worth around £30m, and immediately established himself as a key player. His status in the squad was cemented this summer, when he was appointed into the club's leadership group, and he has worn the captain's armband on a number of occasions this season.

Thiaw is now valued at £67m by Newcastle, but it's reported that a summer exit in 2027 has not been ruled out, with Man Utd among the most interested parties for his signature.

Regarded as the 'undisputed leader' of the defence at St James' Park, the club do not want to lose him, but accept that they may be forced to sell for a big fee if Champions League clubs come calling, and they are unable to turn their slow start to the season around.

The 6ft 4 star has also recently broken into the Germany first-team, with Jurgen Klopp understood to be a huge fan of his qualities. His current contract is due to expire in 2029.

Man Utd need defensive reinforcements, and so it's no surprise that a number of players are going to start being linked with a move to Old Trafford as a result.

INEOS have shown that they prefer to shop in the Premier League since taking over, and Thiaw would fit that. He is also just 25 and has his prime years ahead of him, while he has the experience of playing for a big club like AC Milan in his career too.

But with Newcastle likely to demand a huge fee to let him leave, the Red Devils could surely find a player of comparable quality for a much lesser fee, and free up funds to make other signings instead.

Thiaw has his qualities, but we have seen him exposed in the Premier League before. Until there is a leader and proven starter in the backline to help guide him along, United would be making a huge gamble by spending such big money on bringing him in at this stage of their rebuild under Pochettino.

United simply cannot afford to get their signings wrong at this stage, and with Newcastle certain to play hardball for any deal, they'd be better off looking elsewhere unless they are 100% convinced he is the right man for the role and the new manager.
