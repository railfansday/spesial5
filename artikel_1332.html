Inter receive double boost: Confidence over defender’s extension and injury return

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Sky Sport Italia reports that Inter are close to reaching an agreement over Carlos Augusto’s contract extension, while Luis Henrique returned to group training today.

Inter seem to have received some good news today, both on and off the pitch.

According to Sky Sport Italia, Nerazzurri defender Carlos Augusto is close to agreeing to a new deal with the Nerazzurri until June 2031.

The report claims both parties are confident they will secure and sign the deal in the coming days.

The versatile Brazilian defender has been among Inter’s best players this season, scoring four goals and providing three assists in 12 appearances across all competitions. His current contract runs out in June 2028.

Another Brazilian player, Luis Henrique, returned to group training on Thursday, Sky Sport adds.

The former Marseille star has recovered from an adductor injury that kept him on the sidelines since October 23.

Luis Henrique will be available for Inter’s next Serie A match against Atalanta on November 22.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
