French team: the winners and losers of this November gathering

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The November gathering was rich in lessons for the French team. With a victory against Turkey and a defeat against Italy, the Blues experienced two contrasting encounters, but ultimately achieved their objective by validating their qualification for the rest of the Nations League. This Sunday, the content left something to be desired, particularly on the defensive level and in the intensity of the pressing and which provoked the ire of Zinedine Zidane after the meeting. Obviously, several individuals scored points during this gathering, while others may have lost credit for the Blues’ upcoming games.

This gathering allowed several players to stand out and score points in the minds of the staff. This is particularly the case ofAndy Dioufwho took advantage of the playing time granted to show the extent of his qualities. Active on the left flank of the defense, the Inter Milan midfielder displayed great availability, with a real ability to project himself forward and support offensives. His activity in recovery, his investment in defensive work and his desire to play forward were also interesting. Everything was not perfect but in a position that was not very well provided, the Interist clearly showed that Zidane could count on him. Lucas Da Cunha is also one of the big winners of this gathering. After a good performance against Italy, the midfielder was particularly convincing against Turkey, delivering a successful match in midfield. Technically comfortable, he stood out for his quality of passing, his vision of the game and his ability to quickly find his partners in space.

Available, accurate in his transmissions and involved in defensive efforts, he displayed a particularly attractive face. For his second rally, Da Cunha has almost already established himself as an indisputable player in the midfield. Finally, Eli Junior Kroupiscorer against the Turks, is also one of the players who scored points during this international window. The young striker was able to take advantage of this experience with the Blues to get a little closer to the group and show his qualities. His offensive profile, his mobility and his willingness to provide solutions in the last meters constitute interesting arguments for the future. In a sector where competition is particularly high, each gathering represents an opportunity to stand out and gain credit with the staff. These three players took advantage of this period to send positive signals, with the hope of continuing this dynamic with a view to the Final 8 of the Nations League next March.

Among the successful firsts, we must necessarily mention Saël Kumbedi. A mystery guest on Zinédine Zidane’s list and summoned following Pierre Kalulu’s withdrawal, the former OL player was sharp for his first start this Sunday against Turkey. Not flamboyant, however, the Wolfsburg right-back showed that he was a credible candidate for the next gatherings even if the returns of Jules Koundé and Pierre Kalulu could obscure his international future in the long term. In any case, he scored points, unlike another player who we will talk about later, and showed that he can rejoin the group in the future in case of absences. In this line, Ibrahima Konate (re)applied to ZZ. Getting better and better with Real Madrid, the central defender was the author of a good rally and a reassuring tenure in central defense against Turkey. Despite increased competition from William Saliba, Dayot Upamecano, Maxence Lacroix and Jérémy Jacquet, the 27-year-old rock has shown that he is clearly fit to claim a place in the squad in the future.

And the happiness of Ibrahima Konaté causes the misfortune of Leny Yoro. While the numerous absences in defense could allow him to accumulate playing time and score points, the Manchester United defender ultimately did not play a single minute. The only outfield player in the group not to have played during this break, he could be the first fuse to blow with the returns of Lacroix and Saliba. Especially since Konaté seems to have taken a head start in the hierarchy. Yoro was therefore unable to seize the opportunity offered to him and could quickly see his chances of winning dwindle. On the other hand, the big loser of this gathering is undoubtedly Malo Gusto.

Wandered against Italy, the Chelsea full-back did not even have the opportunity to redeem himself against Turkey. Worse, Kumbedi’s encouraging tenure may suggest that the latter has now passed him in the hierarchy. A worrying situation for Gusto, who is already struggling to get playing time at club level with Chelsea. In front, Bradley Barcola didn’t score any points either. While he had a great opportunity to express himself with the absences of Désiré Doué and Ousmane Dembélé, the Parisian winger delivered two fairly average performances against Italy and Turkey. Interesting in sequences, he especially gave the impression of often being disconnected from his partners. Not to mention his finish and his last gesture are still frustrating. To hope to maintain an important place in the offensive hierarchy, he will have to show more regularity and influence, especially since Kroupi’s return from injury and promising debut could reshuffle the cards.

In the Lyonnais gang, Rayan Cherki is called to the stand. Even if he remains an important player in Zidane’s plans, the virtuoso must also remain vigilant. The Lyonnais has the full confidence of the coach and has once again demonstrated his ability to turn a match around thanks to his technical quality, by being involved in both goals against Turkey. But some of his choices and his body language can sometimes be annoying. His midfield positioning could also be called into question with the returns of Akliouche, Koné and Tchouaméni, especially if Zidane decides to reorganize his system. He nevertheless remains a real step ahead thanks to his valuable talent and his importance in offensive animation. Finally, in the goals, Robin Risser now appears officially relegated to third goalkeeper after Restes replaced Maignan against Turkey. A hierarchy which places him at the forefront among the players likely to leave the group if another goalkeeper were to perform and convince. In difficulty with RC Lens since the start of the season, the young Lensois goalkeeper, targeted by Bayern, will therefore have to remain efficient so as not to permanently lose his place at Clairefontaine.
