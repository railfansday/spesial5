Cranberry Pie

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

What People Are Saying:“I made 3 desserts this Thanksgiving and this was an absolute hit!! Everyone at the table raved about it including my super picky 7 year old who had herself seconds. Definitely a winner and will be making again.” - PurplePuzzle

Preheat your oven to 350° and grease a 9-inch pie plate with butter.

For the crust, grab a large bowl and mix together the Biscoff crumbs, melted butter, and a pinch of salt. Gently press this mixture into the bottom and up the sides of your greased pie plate.

Bake the crust until it turns golden and feels firm to the touch. Once done, set it aside on a wire rack to cool.

In a small bowl, mix the gelatin with some cold water and let it sit so it can bloom.

Next, for the filling, grab a medium saucepan, toss in the cranberries, brown sugar, orange juice, and zest, and bring it to a simmer over medium heat. Stir occasionally while the cranberries burst and the liquid has thickened slightly, which should take about 7 minutes.

Pour in the coconut milk, ginger, heavy cream, almond extract, and a little salt. Stir everything together and heat until it’s nice and steamy, but not quite simmering. Remove from the heat and stir in the bloomed gelatin until fully dissolved.

Now, blend the cranberry mixture until it’s super smooth—use whatever blender or food processor you have handy. Strain it into a bowl, toss out any solids, and let the mixture cool down to room temperature. Pour the cranberry filling into your crust and refrigerate until set, which should take at least 6 hours.

Serve with homemade whipped cream and enjoy!

This pie will keep for up to a week in the fridge and frozen for up to 5 months. Simply thaw it out in the fridge the next day and serve.
