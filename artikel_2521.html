Commanders QB Jayden Daniels injures hand vs Bengals Monday night

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Washington Commanders starting quarterback Jayden Daniels suffered an apparent hand injury during the team's NFL Week 11 game against the Bengals at Northwest Stadium on ESPN's Monday Night Football.

Daniels injured his non-throwing hand and was seen icing it on the sideline during the game.

Daniels entered the game as the Commanders' leading rusher this season with 280 yards on 42 carries through seven games.

During the Bengals-Commanders preseason game in 2025, MNF analyst Troy Aikman criticized Bengals defensive back Jordan Battle for not hitting Daniels on the QB's first-quarter touchdown run.

In a 2024 battle between former LSU quarterbacks who won Heisman Trophies, Daniels outdueled the Bengals' Joe Burrow. Burrow finished with more passing yards, but Daniels completed 21 of his 23 attempts and added 39 rushing yards and a touchdown on the ground.

It was the first NFL game since 1940 with no turnovers and no punts.
