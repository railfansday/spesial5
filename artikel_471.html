How to find your old Spotify Wrapped playlists and revisit your past music eras

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Music has a funny way of transporting you back to a specific moment in your life. Sometimes, all it takes is the opening notes of a song to bring back an entire summer, a person you haven't spoken to in years, or a version of yourself you barely recognize anymore.

And if you've been using Spotify for the better part of a decade, chances are you've inadvertently been keeping a musical diary.

Since Spotify Wrapped debuted in 2016, the streaming platform has compiled personalized playlists of users' most-played songs each year. Collectively, those playlists offer a fascinating snapshot of how your taste in music — and, perhaps, your life — has changed over time.

With Spotify Wrapped 2026 just around the corner, there's no better time to revisit your past musical eras. What were you listening to before the pandemic? Which songs got you through lockdown in 2020? (Thank you, "God's Menu" by Stray Kids.) And what happened to that artist you were completely obsessed with in 2018?

Here's how to find your old Spotify Wrapped playlists.

Even after the annual Wrapped experience disappears from the Spotify app, your personalized Top Songs playlists may still be accessible. The easiest way to find them is through Spotify's search function. You can do this on the mobile app, desktop app, or web player.

Type "Your Top Songs" followed by the year you're looking for (for example, "Your Top Songs 2020").

Filter your search results by Playlists, if necessary.

Look for the personalized playlist created by Spotify.

Once you've found it, tap the plus icon to save it to your library for easy access.

You can repeat this process for each year you'd like to revisit.

If you're having trouble finding a particular playlist, a Spotify moderator recommends trying a few different search queries. For example, if you're looking for your 2020 Wrapped playlist, try searching for "Wrapped 2020" or "year:2020" (without any spaces).

Keep in mind that not every old Wrapped playlist will necessarily be available. If you didn't listen to enough music on Spotify during a particular year, the platform may not have generated a personalized playlist for you. Older playlists can also be trickier to track down. And when browsing your search results, look for the "Spotify • Made for you" label to make sure you've found your own personalized playlist rather than someone else's with a similar name.

Unfortunately, revisiting your old Spotify Wrapped playlists isn't quite the same as revisiting your old Spotify Wrapped.

Spotify doesn't offer a permanent archive of its annual interactive Wrapped experiences. That means you generally can't go back and see how many minutes you listened, who your top five artists were, or what else Spotify told you about your listening habits.

Want to know which Sound Town Spotify assigned you in 2023? Or what your Listening Personality was in 2022? Unless you saved a screenshot, you may be out of luck.

As for this year's Wrapped, consider saving screenshots of your favorite slides when it arrives. Your future self might appreciate them.

If you've managed to track down several years' worth of Wrapped playlists, why not make an afternoon of it?

Listen to your oldest playlist and see how many songs you still know by heart. Compare your top songs from five years ago with the music you're listening to today. Look for the artists who've remained a constant in your life and the ones you'd almost forgotten existed. You could even create a new playlist combining your favorite songs from every year of Wrapped, essentially making your own decade-spanning musical retrospective.

Part of the fun is discovering the little things your memory has lost along the way. The song you used to listen to on your morning commute. The album that became the soundtrack to a new city. The artist you discovered through a friend you no longer talk to. Or that time you fell asleep on a five-hour flight with aespa's "Illusion" on repeat, inadvertently catapulting it to the top of your most-played songs of 2022. (Just me?)

We tend to think of Spotify Wrapped as a year-end ritual, something to share on social media and collectively obsess over for a few days in December. But revisiting those playlists years later gives them an entirely different kind of significance.

Years later, there's something lovely about being able to visit a former version of yourself simply by pressing play.
