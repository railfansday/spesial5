Can America’s Next Jobs Boom Build a Middle-Class Life?

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

America’s aging population is driving rapid growth in demand for home care workers, but many of those jobs still offer wages too low to support a family. Ai-jen Poo of the National Domestic Workers Alliance says the country will need hundreds of thousands of additional home care workers in the coming decades, while Harvard economist Lawrence Katz argues that expanding the workforce will not be enough if those jobs offer little opportunity for workers to move up. The challenge is whether care work can follow the path of manufacturing and evolve from low-wage labor into a stable route to the middle class. (Source: Bloomberg)
