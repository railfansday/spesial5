Kolibri Global (KGEI) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Higher oil prices lifted revenue as new wells came online later.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Kolibri Global Energy Inc. (KGEI -9.17%) reported a quarter defined by higher realized pricing and the strategic testing of new geological intervals. Management focused on the successful flowback of three Clifton Mack wells and the initial production results from the Lovina 5-8-1HF well, which represents the company's first test of the False Caney formation. While production during the quarter was affected by temporary shut-ins related to drilling activities, the company ended the period with an exit rate of approximately 5,700 barrels of oil equivalent per day. Financial performance remained stable with flat net income as increased revenue was balanced by unrealized losses on commodity hedges. Management expressed confidence in the company's debt position and current liquidity, noting that the credit facility was recently redetermined with a $75 million borrowing base.
