Enel expands U.S. renewables portfolio with 317 MW solar deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Enel (ENLAY) said Friday it agreed to acquire a portfolio of solar plants in the U.S. from clean energy infrastructure developer energyRe, boosting its installed renewable capacity in North America to 13.5 GW, including storage assets.
