Cavs' Jarrett Allen exits early vs. Thunder with right knee injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Cleveland Cavaliers center Jarrett Allen left Wednesday's matchup with the Oklahoma City Thunder after suffering a right knee injury late in the first quarter.

Allen suffered the injury while setting an off-ball screen just past half-court with the Cavs trailing the host Thunder by 19 points. When Jared McCain tried to get over the screen, the Oklahoma City guard's right knee collided with Allen's right knee, which appeared to jam as he stood rooted to his spot on the floor:

The 11th-year veteran fell to the deck in evident pain, grabbing for that right knee. He laid on the floor for a moment as teammate Mario Hezonja put back an offensive rebound, then raised one finger in the air to signal for a substitution.

After Cavs guard Sam Merrill committed a foul to stop the action, Allen slowly got to his feet and walked gingerly back to the locker room. Shortly thereafter, Cleveland announced that he was doubtful to return to the contest with a right knee injury. When the Cavs came out of the locker room to start the second half, Hezonja took Allen's place in Cleveland's starting lineup alongside Donovan Mitchell, James Harden, Evan Mobley and Tyrese Proctor, whom head coach Kenny Atkinson inserted in place of the injured Peyton Watson.

Allen did not return to Wednesday's game, finishing scoreless with one rebound in seven minutes of playing time. After trailing by as many as 25, the Cavs clawed back to within two possessions several times in the closing minutes. They couldn't get all the way over the hump, though, as the Thunder held on for a 124-116 win behind 42 points and eight assists from two-time NBA MVP Shai Gilgeous-Alexander.

Allen, 28, has been Cleveland's starting center since joining the team in a trade midway through the 2020-21 season. A one-time All-Star, he's averaging 15.1 points on 63.1% shooting to go with a team-high 8.7 rebounds, 1.7 blocks and 1.4 assists in 27.9 minutes per game for the Cavs, who fell to 4-4 with the loss.
