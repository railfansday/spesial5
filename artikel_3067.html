Is a Bloom Energy Stock Crash Coming? 1 Warning Sign Investors Shouldn’t Ignore.

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bloom Energy stock has nearly doubled in price this year.

By Ryan Vanzo – Nov 19, 2026 at 4:30PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Bloom Energy ›

Bloom Energy (BE -1.68%) stock is on an incredible run. In 2026 alone, shares have nearly doubled in value, pushing the fuel cell company's valuation above $50 billion.

Investors are very pleased with this year's performance. But there's one warning sign that could portend trouble ahead.

Even after a sharp decline, Bloom Energy shares still trade at roughly 120 times trailing earnings. Profits are growing rapidly, and shares trade at just 35 times forward earnings. But make no mistake: The market is betting big on profits continuing to expand aggressively.

High expectations leave less room for error. And yet concerns over the global data center build-out continue to rise. To be clear, AI adoption rates continue to impress. The pace and scale of the accompanying infrastructure build-out, however, is increasingly in question.

"AI demand remains strong," notes research from Barclays. "What the market is questioning is funding, as hyperscalers keep lifting capex while free cash flow evaporates."

I expect Bloom Energy's revenues and profits to continue rising for years to come, as its products are directly benefiting from the AI tailwinds. But that doesn't mean that the data center build-out won't experience hiccups. With so much priced into the stock, even short industry delays could have an undue impact on its stock price.

"Only about 50%-60% of data center capacity scheduled for the next one to two years is expected to come online on time amid delays and cancellations," warns research from Goldman Sachs. If that prediction comes true, there may be a buying opportunity ahead for Bloom Energy stock.
