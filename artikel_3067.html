Neemias Queta is flourishing

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

"I feel like his growth and his role has gone kind of under the radar," Joe Mazzulla said of Neemias Queta after the Celtics' 131-94 win over the Mavericks on Sunday night.

Coming into last season, we did not know what to expect from Neemias Queta as he entered his first season as the Celtics starting center. He had some good moments in his first two years as a Celtic, but nothing to show he was going to be the long-term center.

There were growing pains at the start. The Celtics lost games early on due to their lack off rebounding but since that point, Queta has surpassed even his biggest fans' expectations.

With Queta as the closest defender this year, teams are shooing 41.3% on shots less than 6 feet away from the rim. That is the best mark in the NBA among players who have faced 4 or more shots around the rim per game. The average mark is 63%.

According to databallr, teams shoot 20% worse in general when Queta is on the court.

Databallr has a stat called DDPM which "estimates how much a player improves expected team defense per 100 possessions. It rolls together rim deterrence, contests, rebounding, disruption, foul discipline, and context-adjusted defensive information." Neemias Queta's DDPM is +2.3, which ranks in the 98th percentile of the NBA.

That shows in Queta's on/off numbers as well. With Queta on the floor this year, the Celtics are giving up 102.5 points per 100 possessions (compared to 112.1 with him on the bench) and have a net rating of +23.8 when he is on the floor (compared to +0.2 with him off of it).

Perhaps there is no better example of Neemy's impact than at the end of the Raptors game when he blocked 2 shots at the rim late in the game to seal a Celtics win. One of them came up one with 17 seconds left and the other with the Celtics up 5 with 4 seconds left.

In what was a five-point win for the Celtics — Queta was a +27. His impact on both ends of the court cannot be overstated.

Queta is not a good defensive center; he is one of the best defenders in the NBA. The way he impacts the rim allows for the Celtics to play more aggressive on defense, which is something they wanted to do this season.

He is anchoring the 2nd best defense in the NBA right now. The Celtics defensive rating is 106.0, just 2nd in the NBA behind the Minnesota Timberwolves, at 104.0.

He has started to take and make corner threes this year. He doesn't shoot them a lot; he has only shot 6 to start the season but he has made 4 of them. He isn't taking contested threes or any threes above the break so we can't call him a three-point threat or a stretch big, but he has made the ones he takes so I can't complain about them.

The advanced numbers say his offense has been average. Databallr's ODPM has Queta at 0.0, in the 67th percentile and the Celtics only score 1.00 points per possession with Queta as the roll man in the pick and roll.

However, the on/off numbers say Boston is significantly better on offense with Queta on the court. The Celtics score 126.4 points with Queta on the floor and 112.1 with Queta off the floor. If we take Jayson Tatum off the court, the Celtics are scoring 131.4 points per 100 possessions with Queta on the court. Granted that is just 74 minutes, but it shows that these numbers are not skewed thanks to most of Queta's minutes happening with Tatum on the floor.

One reason for this is that he has the most screen assist points in the NBA per 36 minutes, with 15 points per 36 minutes. He has also been setting a ton of flare screens, which are off ball screens set and a player moves away from the ball. His screening creates a ton of opportunities for Boston in the half court.

The Celtics have gotten a lot of praise for their player development. They might not have a bigger win than Queta. Turning him into one of the best defensive centers in the NBA is not something many (including the Sacramento Kings who waived him) saw coming. Having Queta as a security blanket at the rim has been a big deal for the Celtics this year as their defense has become one of the best units in the NBA.
