Seeing Katahdin and Moose! (Day 165)

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Today is another early morning for Dreamcatcher and me, while SB slept in again. It’s nice we can just go at our own speed, doing our own thing and meeting up later.

We’ve got four mountains to climb today, not the least of which is White Cap. We actually got our first view of Katahdin today!

All the way in the distance in the middle between the clouds

Poet from Shaw’s Hiker Hostel told us this moment is very emotional and special for a lot of hikers. To be honest, I didn’t feel anything. I’m not too solemn about it ending and I’m also not trying to get it over with as soon as possible, as a lot of hikers are at this point. I’m just excited to finish the trail, thinking about what comes next.

As per usual, we took a long lunch break and enjoyed the terrain. I was listening to a Lord of the Rings audiobook, we turned the corner and out of nowhere I saw a moose! The moose was just beside the trail, maybe a 100 feet away. It saw us and got spooked. They’re enormous! I’m glad it decided to go into another direction. Maybe 10 seconds later, Dreamcatcher pointed out another one on the right side further away up the mountain. They were just too quick for us to take a picture though..

They’re majestic animals and I’m so glad I got to see not one, but two of them! Moose aren’t simply a big deer though. They can be very dangerous, way more dangerous than a black bear. Dreamcatcher and I decided to wait on Small Bites. Safety in numbers and all. We just felt bad for him. He wanted to see a moose so bad. Not long after, Small Bites joined us. Shouting: “I just saw a moose!”. Again, completely in another direction on another part along the trail at the same time we saw ours. Three moose encounters in one day. You don’t experience that everyday. I’m glad we all got to see one though! We’ve seen every animal we wanted to see and more. What a majestic place.

At the end of the day, we camped at another beautiful lake. Maine is in no shortage of those and they’re our favorite camping spots on trail for sure!
