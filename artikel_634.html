Anuel AA’s ‘RHLM2’ Album Is Finally Here: ‘I Made It With My Heart and Soul’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The long-awaited album is a sequel to his 2018 debut, Real Hasta La Muerte, and his first in four years.

Anuel AA is celebrating his 34th birthday on Thursday (Nov. 26) with the release of his long-awaited RHLM2 album.

The sequel to his debut studio album, Real Hasta La Muerte (2018), is home to 23 tracks that blend trap and reggaetón. It’s a return to the sound that made him a household name as “El Dios del Trap” (The God of Latin Trap).

Though it’s taken the Puerto Rican act nearly 10 years to release the sequel to RHLM — which debuted at No. 1 on Top Latin Albums in July 2018—he has released other promising albums in between, including Emmanuel (2020) and Las Leyendas Nunca Mueren (2021), both of which also hit No. 1 on the chart.

“I poured my heart and soul into this album for everyone who believes in me and stays loyal no matter what. This album means the world to me. There are no perfect words to explain how I feel and what I feel for this album,” he said in an Instagram post ahead of the set’s release on Thanksgiving night. He dedicated his new project to his fans, his family and those who have stuck by his side during the good and bad times.

He continued: “Thank you for never giving up on me, I love you all so much with all my heart!!!!!!! I made this album for you […] I will never forget you!!!!!”

From trapsoul to sensual reggaetón, RHLM2 includes fiery collaborations such as “Sexo Nama” with De La Rose, “Tal Ve Un Ignorante Fui” with ROA and “Si Me Matan a Balazos” with Fuerza Regida. Most notable is “Instagram,” a collaboration with Blessd and Wisin, where they cleverly sample Don Omar’s 2006 hit “Myspace” alongside Wisin y Yandel.

The set wraps with five previously released commercial hits—“Amanece” feat. Haze (2018), “Mas Rica Que Ayer” feat. Mambo Kingz & DJ Luian (2022), “¿Qué Nos Pasó?” (2022), “La Llevo al Cielo” feat. Chris Jedi, Chencho Corleone & Ñengo Flow (2022) and the Ovy on the Drums-produced “Deportivo” feat. Blessd (2024)—further reminding fans of his ongoing star power.
