Jason Kennedy and Karrueche Tran Celebrity Digital Series ‘The Pop Exchange’ Scores More Than 11 Million Views in First Month (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Guests on the show included Chris Pratt, Patrick Schwarzenegger and Blake Griffin.

“The Pop Exchange,” the live celebrity digital series hosted by “E! News” veteran Jason Kennedy and actress Karrueche Tran, had more than 11 million views in its first month.

The show launched on Oct. 12. Guests included Chris Pratt, Patrick Schwarzenegger, Blake Griffin, Heather McMahan, David Henrie and Dallas Jenkins, among others.

“The Pop Exchange” airs live every Monday and Friday on YouTube, with full episodes also available on Spotify and Apple Podcasts and additional clips shared across YouTube Shorts, Instagram, TikTok and X. The viewership is based on views across the show’s channels and third-party reposts.

“What makes this show so fun is that no two conversations are the same,” Kennedy said in a statement. “We wanted to create something that feels fresh and spontaneous, where we can bring our own personalities and experiences into the mix, hear different perspectives and respond to what’s happening in the moment. Having the audience there with us makes every show feel even more engaging.”

Tran said, “What’s been exciting to see is how the conversations are extending beyond the live show and finding an audience across social platforms. We’re creating moments that people want to watch, share and be part of and the response in our first month has been incredible.”

“The Pop Exchange” is co-founded and executive produced by Kennedy and Tran, alongside former Spotify executive Brandon Bauman and Emmy-nominated producer Ryan Holcomb.

“The world doesn’t need another podcast or another entertainment show built for a media landscape that no longer exists,” Bauman said. “We started with a different question: What would the definitive destination for pop culture look like if you built it today? You would need extraordinary talent with real credibility and firsthand experience, a format that brings audiences into the conversation and distribution designed for wherever people actually consume content. Jason and Karrueche bring something you can’t manufacture. They’ve lived the stories, they know the people and they understand the culture from the inside.”
