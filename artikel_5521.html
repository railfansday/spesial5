MSC Cruises is fighting a $1.8B U.S. trade secrets lawsuit in a U.K. court

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The cruise line wants a London judge to hear confidentiality claims from startup Dreamology Labs, which sued in Florida

MSC Cruises petitioned a High Court judge in London to rule that the trade secrets claims in a $1.8 billion U.S. lawsuit belong before British courts, according to Bloomberg.

Dreamology Labs, an immersive entertainment company, and founder Alessandra Maderni claim in the Florida suit that MSC Cruises copied their concepts without authorization during partnership talks. The suit also accuses MSC executive chairman Pierfrancesco Vago of coercing her and committing sexual misconduct, according to Bloomberg. MSC did not ask the London court to block those claims.

MSC argues that London is the proper venue since the confidentiality agreements between the two sides fall under English law, and that Miami was the wrong place to bring the claims, according to Bloomberg. The company has yet to file its defense in the Florida court.

"We strongly dispute the allegations of the U.S. legal proceedings and the defenses made before the High Court in London," MSC said in an emailed statement, according to Bloomberg. "We will defend these claims vigorously through the appropriate legal process, including by holding Ms. Maderni and Dreamology Labs responsible for their false and defamatory statements." The company called the sexual misconduct allegations "false and without merit."

Maderni said in a statement sent to Bloomberg that MSC's response in the U.K. "has only made me more determined to pursue accountability for both the commercial and sexual exploitation alleged."

Dreamology's lawyers say in the Florida complaint that Vago and Maderni were introduced in 2019. Vago later reached out to her himself, a meeting was arranged, and the two sides executed a nondisclosure agreement before they began swapping proprietary ideas, according to the complaint.

Maderni’s lawyers allege that Vago asked her up to his Miami hotel suite in early 2022, while the two were discussing “multi-million dollar collaborations,” and gave her a kiss on the cheek. They allege he summoned her to his room again the next day and started a sexual encounter with her. When she protested, he promised to back her but canceled meetings and pulled out of a potential deal, the lawyers allege. In filings drawn up ahead of a Nov. 3 hearing in a London court, they said “a coercive dynamic developed in which Mr. Vago would connect sexual advances with promises of business dealings.”

Dreamology says MSC reproduced ideas it had been given, such as giant fish sculptures, ship structures draped in the tentacles of sea monsters and games aimed at younger passengers, in designs and activities on its ships. It says that conduct led to financial losses and also deprived the company of openings to expand, secure funding and form partnerships.

MSC Cruises ranks as the largest closely held cruise operator globally, according to Bloomberg.
