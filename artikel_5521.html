Dow futures rally 500 points as oil drops and traders await inflation data and AI earnings

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

S&P 500 futures rose 0.7% and Nasdaq 100 futures gained 0.4%, while Nvidia stock fell about 5% in premarket trading

Dow Jones Industrial Average futures climbed 1.1%, or 538 points, on Monday as oil prices declined and traders anticipated an inflation reading plus profit reports from leading AI companies. S&P 500 futures gained 0.7%, and Nasdaq $NDAQ 100 futures rose 0.4%.

West Texas Intermediate futures dropped to $88.17 a barrel. Brent, the international benchmark, slid 3.4% to $94.94.

Nvidia $NVDA stock fell about 5% in premarket trading. The company’s board has launched a probe into the ties between CEO Jensen Huang and a businesswoman whom U.S. intelligence agencies suspect of spying for China, according to CNBC. According to The Wall Street Journal, a spokesperson for Nvidia said Huang intends to remain as chief executive.

According to The Wall Street Journal, President Donald Trump plans to meet with Qatar's prime minister, whose country is among those mediating between Washington and Tehran. Trump has said he considers it "highly unlikely" that he will order major new attacks against Iran.

The moves follow a rebound on Friday from a selloff on Thursday. The Nasdaq Composite rose 1.1% and the S&P 500 gained 0.3% as chip stocks recovered. The Dow lost 237 points, or 0.5%.

Investor sentiment remained cautious after the Democrats' strong midterm results, according to CNBC. Investors worry that stricter rules covering how data centers are built, how much power they consume and what they cost to develop could slow the expansion of AI infrastructure, CNBC said.

CoreWeave, Cisco $CSCO and Applied Materials $AMAT are scheduled to report earnings this week, according to CNBC. The consumer price index is due Tuesday, and the producer price index is due Friday. Investors are looking to both reports for clues on the Federal Reserve's next move.

On Monday, September wholesale inventories data is due at 10 a.m. ET. Take-Two Interactive $TTWO Software, Occidental $OXY Petroleum, KKR, Constellation Energy and Skydance are set to release their latest quarterly results, according to CNBC.
