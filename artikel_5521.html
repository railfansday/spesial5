Anthropic slid to just 5 cents above its IPO price, a stunning reversal of its first day pop

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Anthropic’s stock has come within a hair of falling below its initial public offering price on Friday, erasing the gains made one day earlier during the AI company’s blockbuster Wall Street debut.

Shares in the buzzy AI lab traded as low as $83.05 on Friday, down more than 14% on their second day of trading, before recovering some ground and climbing to roughly $87 in midday trading. The shares are down more than 25% from the intraday high of $115.10 they achieved on Thursday, the first day the stock traded.At its lowest point on Friday, Anthropic’s stock traded just a nickel above its IPO price of $83, at which Anthropic was valued at approximately $1.5 trillion.The retreat in the stock comes amid broader weakness in technology shares, as investors reassess valuations across the board. Problems at AI giant Nvidia and the results of the midterm elections have also worried the markets in recent weeks.

Anthropic’s decline marks a sharp reversal from Thursday’s opening session, when Anthropic shares opened at $111.50, 34% above the IPO price, and then surged even higher, briefly pushing the company’s market capitalization above $2 trillion. The stock quickly pared back those gains though to close at $97.17, still 17% above its offering price and an impressive first day.

Anthropic’s IPO was one of the largest in history, with the company raising approximately $91 billion through the share sale (including a 147 million-share over-allotment sold by the deal’s underwriters on Friday), plus an additional $26 billion private placement. All told, Anthropic raised more than $115 billion in the offering—capital that will be vital to its efforts to stay ahead in the AI race, regardless of how the stock trades in the coming days and weeks.
