Stock futures rise after Democrats sweep the midterm elections

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

One strategist said the rebound "was in part due to the president's initial tone and reaction to the results"

Democrats won control of both the House and the Senate in the midterm elections on Tuesday, and U.S. stock futures rose on Wednesday.

Dow Jones Industrial Average futures climbed 912 points, or 1.75%, while S&P 500 futures added 0.8%. Nasdaq $NDAQ-100 futures recovered from an early slide of up to 2.4% and were up 0.9%.

President Donald Trump said he was willing to accept the results and congratulated Democrats for doing "a good job." Jay Woods, chief market strategist at Freedom Capital Markets, said the rebound "was in part due to the president's initial tone and reaction to the results," according to CNBC.

Walmart $WMT stock added 2% and Bank of America $BAC stock rose 1.9%. Among companies that cover patients or run hospitals, UnitedHealth $UNH stock climbed 1.4%, Molina Healthcare stock was up 6.3%, Centene $CNC stock advanced 4.7% and HCA Healthcare $HCA stock gained 1.4%.

Renewable energy stocks climbed. The Invesco Solar ETF jumped 5%, and shares of Orsted and SMA Solar rose more than 8% each in Europe. Hanwha Solutions stock jumped 19% in South Korea. Investors believe a Democratic wave will benefit businesses that supply the clean energy industry, according to Bloomberg.

Shares of Oracle $ORCL and CoreWeave headed lower. Tesla $TSLA stock and SpaceX stock fell about 3.5% each before the open, and defense stocks declined.

Investors had worried that a Democratic victory might lead to tighter limits on data centers. Gina Hinojosa, who won the Texas governor’s race, has pledged an immediate moratorium on data centers in the state.

In other markets, the Stoxx 600 gained more than 1%, and the Kospi wiped out a slide that had reached 4%. The 10-year Treasury yield slid seven basis points to 4.89% at its low, then retraced some of the move. Gold rose as much as 3.1% to above $4,000 an ounce, and WTI crude oil traded 1% lower. Bitcoin dropped by up to 2.8%, sinking beneath $76,000 while the results were arriving, before it bounced back.
