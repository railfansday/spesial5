Greenway Health's Novare™, an AI-Native Platform That Carries Patient Context From First Appointment to Final Payment, Now Available

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Scheduling, the exam room, and the revenue cycle now share one platform, with coordinated AI agents that keep work moving alongside care

TAMPA, Fla., Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Greenway Health today announced the availability of Novare, an AI-native platform for ambulatory practices that brings patient engagement and scheduling, the clinical encounter, and the revenue cycle together on one data foundation. Coordinated agents built into the platform carry patient context from the first appointment through final payment, so documentation, coding, and authorization progress alongside care instead of becoming another administrative backlog.

In many practices, these steps live in separate systems. Clinicians and staff gather records, resolve coverage questions, and chase information for billing, and gaps often surface only in a denial queue, creating rework and delaying payment.

Novare takes a different approach. Scheduling, clinical record, and revenue cycle workflows share a single data foundation, and agents work natively inside the platform rather than being added on to separate tools. Each agent builds on information already gathered, shifting the model from an EHR that records what happened to a platform that helps determine what happens next.

Before the visit. When a patient books an appointment and completes digital intake, eligibility agents verify coverage, identify coordination-of-benefits gaps, and help find insurance when needed. Patient Story retrieves available documents from connected care settings, such as a recent urgent care visit or a specialist appointment, while AI Snapshot and AI Search help clinicians review relevant chart information. Staff spend less time chasing records by phone and fax.

During the visit. Ambient documentation drafts the note and assistive coding supports documentation and coding review as the encounter unfolds, so clinicians spend less time searching and typing and more time with the patient. If a clinician orders a procedure that requires prior authorization, Novare's authorization agent connects clinical evidence with coverage requirements to prepare and advance the request while the details are fresh. Staff review the request, and clinicians review and approve the record and remain responsible for care decisions.

After the visit. Documentation and coding context carry into billing, reducing repeated data entry and questions about the encounter. Because coverage and documentation gaps are addressed earlier, fewer reach a claim or denial queue, and revenue teams can focus their expertise on the exceptions that need attention.

"Healthcare doesn't need another AI tool that finishes one task and leaves people to connect everything around it," said David Cohen, Chief Product and Strategy Officer at Greenway Health. "Novare was designed for ambulatory healthcare practices, with agents that carry patient context from scheduling through the exam room and into the claim. We're not making the old workflow faster. We're changing what needs to happen, when it happens, and how much of that burden technology takes off clinicians and staff."

"I'm 71 years old and I've been in practice for 41 years," said Dr. Richard Cole, physician at Patrick County Family Practice. "I should be the last person that would adopt, but I'm very glad that I'm on the train, that I've adopted [Novare] and it's making my life better."
