We Tried Dolce Vita’s Fall Shoes to Find the Pairs Worth Wearing

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

From morning commutes to evening plans, we wanted to see if these shoes keep up.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

There’s a particular art to getting dressed this time of year. One minute, you’re heading into the office after navigating the morning’s unpredictable weather; the next, you’re rushing to make a dinner reservation or squeezing in a holiday party before the weekend. And while a great outfit can usually rise to the occasion, finding shoes that can do the same is another story.

That’s where Dolce Vita comes in. The brand’s fall collection makes a case for shoes you can wear just about anywhere. To bring that vision to life, the company opened The Dolce Vita Flat, a month-long Williamsburg residency inspired by the way personal style extends beyond our wardrobes.

Naturally, we decided to put that philosophy to the test. ELLE editors slipped into some of the collection’s standout styles to see which pairs could earn a permanent spot in our rotations. From polished office staples to shoes we’re excited to style for the holidays, here’s what made the cut.

“I’ve been searching for a suede boot for fall and the Jazmins were exactly what I wanted—they’re great to pair with dresses or knee-length skirts for a minimalist ’90s vibe. They have a chic pointed toe and a low kitten heel that makes them easy to walk in. The knee-high shaft is soft and not tight; plus it helps that there’s a little elastic panel for a bit of stretch. I hover between a size 7.5 and 8, so I opted up, which turned out to fit perfectly. It offers just enough wiggle room for socks without being too loose.”

“These shoes really exceeded my expectations. The leather is soft, lightweight, and supple straight out of the box. They don’t require any breaking in, and they even have room for my orthotic inserts. One note, though: definitely size down.”

“Every girl has a little black dress. The Kinzy boots are my little black boots—although, maybe not as little as they appear. Paired with sweater dresses, pencil skirts, and tucked under jeans, these knee-highs make me feel like a million bucks each time I wear them.”

“Not only do I own these boots in every color, but most of my shoe collection is actually from Dolce Vita. The brand makes some of the most comfortable heels I’ve ever worn, and these chic, surprisingly wearable Kinzy stiletto boots are no exception. They’ve become my fall go-to, pairing perfectly with all my favorite cold-weather looks, especially midi skirts and dresses. I can even walk across the city in them without a single complaint. I also adore the silver buckle detail, which adds a stylish touch and sets them apart from the other black boots in my lineup.”

“If there’s one thing I can’t resist, it’s a shoe that looks more expensive than it actually is. The Emani heels checked that box immediately. I’m obsessed with the neutral-colored suede, but it’s really the shape that sold me. It feels modern, a little fashion-forward, and just different enough from your standard heel.”

“The ballet flat is easily the most versatile shoe a girl can own, but the slightest variations in shape and style can determine whether it becomes a wardrobe staple or simply a shoe you wear until it wears out. The Andey quickly earned its place in my everyday rotation, so much so that I had to own it in both black and cream! The perfect low stacked heel, slightly squared toe, and sleek, high-cut vamp make it the ideal silhouette for any occasion. Whether peeking out from a pair of jeans or paired with a midi skirt, this style lends an effortlessly polished feel to any look. Size down half a size for a fit that feels like a glove.”

“If you’re anything like me and you’ve been searching for the perfect everyday black boot, look no further. With a low enough stiletto heel, this style gives me the look I’m going for without sacrificing comfort. My one note? If you’re planning to use thicker-than-mesh socks or would consider your foot anything other than narrow, go up by a half size.”
