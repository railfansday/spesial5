What GQ Editors Are Actually Buying Ahead of Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

When it comes to Black Friday, your local GQ Recommends editors operate from a fairly unique vantage point: we shop for a living, after all, so the chaotic nature of the weekend hardly phases us.

What they didn't tell us when we started here, though, is how easy it is to mistake professional life for play, shopping away the wee hours of the morning even when we're off the clock. And when you eyeball literally hundreds of items a day, you’re bound to encounter a few that you actually like—so much so that you’re willing to put your hard-earned salary towards 'em. That risk gets doubly dangerous around this time of year, when said Black Friday deals start popping up like moles in the digital tundra.

So hear’s a rare peek into the truly sick minds—and truly sickening carts—of us GQ editors. From plush corduroy shirts to red-carpet-ready shades, these are the pre-Black Friday deals weighing heaviest on our fragile hearts and long-suffering bank accounts. Scroll on if you dare.
