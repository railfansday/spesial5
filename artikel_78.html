Lamarre-Condon returns to the witness box

The former police officer has taken the witness box as he resumes giving evidence.

The 15-person jury has re-entered the court.

Welcome to Guardian Australia’s continuing coverage of the Beau Lamarre-Condon trial.

Lamarre-Condon started giving evidence yesterday and this morning returns to the witness box in his double murder trial in the New South Wales supreme court.

The 31-year-old has pleaded not guilty to murdering Jesse Baird and Luke Davies with his police service weapon in Baird’s Paddington home on 19 February 2024.

He has denied two counts of murder and one charge of aggravated break and enter.
