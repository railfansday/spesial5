Justin Herbert injury update: Chargers QB out vs Patriots, Uiagalelei to start

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Los Angeles Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert has officially been ruled out for the home game against the New England Patriots on Nov. 29.

Herbert had entered concussion protocol after the 27-24 loss to the New York Jets at SoFi Stadium on Sunday, Nov. 22, to fall to 1-9 this season.

DJ Uiagalelei will serve as the team's starting quarterback this week. Uiagalelei had his first regular-season playing experience come against the Houston Texans on Nov. 8. He completed 2 of 7 passes for 24 yards and a touchdown.

The Chargers signed Jake Browning to the active roster on Thursday and will serve as the backup.

Browning has not recorded a statistic this season, but he has NFL experience. The former Washington Husky has completed 252 of 268 passes for 2,707 yards, 18 passes and 15 touchdowns. He's also rushed for 164 yards and four touchdowns during his career.

The quarterback finished out the game, having led the Chargers down the field with a 16-play, 63-yard drive and concluded with a 6-yard touchdown pass to receiver Ladd McConkey with 1:58 left in the fourth quarter. Herbert was sacked six times during the game, including twice on the Chargers' final possession.

It was not made clear when a potential concussion might have taken place.
