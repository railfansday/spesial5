Putting Phoenix’s four-game winning streak into perspective

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Phoenix Suns have fought and clawed their way back above .500, and as they sit at 9-7 preparing to face the Houston Rockets in NBA Cup play tonight, one common question is being asked: Are they legit, or are they simply beating up on bad teams?

This is one of those situations where I feel like multiple things can be true at once. Yes, the Suns have gotten right during a valley in their competition's quality. And I would certainly take these results over dropping a game or two against subpar competition, something we've become all too familiar with in recent years. Many would find themselves stewing had they dropped a game to Utah or were defeated by the Kings.

How legit are they? Time will ultimately tell. I look at it through this lens: Where did you predict the Suns would finish the season? The majority of Bright Side readers had them falling somewhere in the 44 to 47 win range. At 9-7? They're on pace for 46 wins.

So, despite the interesting journey to get to this point, the Suns are kind of exactly who we thought they were. They're a team that most certainly has flaws, possesses the ability to be competitive, and can win games. They reside somewhere between the NBA's elite teams and the basement dwellers.

What we've witnessed thus far is a reminder that the journey to whatever the final record may be isn't linear. It's one defined by peaks and valleys, opportunities presented by the schedule, and those unfortunate back-to-backs against quality competition where it feels like you're being set up to lose. How many times did we play Oklahoma City or San Antonio last season on the second night of a back-to-back, am I right?

Tonight's game against Houston will be an interesting one, considering the Rockets enter at 13-5 and possess one of the best offenses in the league. And should the Suns lose? Many will sprint to their keyboards, overreact to the result, and begin questioning the current state of the Phoenix Suns. I'd advise against that. Remember that this journey is a long and winding road, and for a team that resides somewhere in the middle, those results are to be expected.

The Suns don't need to prove they're a championship contender tonight. They need to keep building, keep finding ways to win, and continue showing that the team we thought could compete is, in fact, capable of competing. A loss to Houston wouldn't erase the progress they've made. A win would certainly add another layer of intrigue to the conversation.

But let's be honest. If they beat the Rockets, I'm not interested in hearing about strength of schedule anymore. Clear the parade route. We're winning a championship.
