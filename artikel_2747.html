'Virat and Dhoni were a sight to see': Usman Khawaja recalls how India duo took T20 World Cup 2016 game away from Australia in Mohali

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Usman Khawaja recalled how Virat Kohli and MS Dhoni took the game away from Australia in the 2016 T20 World Cup.

Khawaja praised their running between the wickets and Kohli's remarkable back-foot six off James Faulkner.

Kohli's unbeaten 82 helped India chase down 160 and secure a place in the semi-finals.

In his autobiography 'Sabr: Finding My Way in Cricket and Life', Khawaja walked down memory lane as he recalled the 2016 T20 World Cup game against India as one of the most exciting games of his career.

The former Australia opener talked about the time when Virat Kohli and MS Dhoni played together and put Australia out of the competition in Mohali, 10 years back.

India were struggling at 49/3 at one point, while chasing 161 runs set by Australia. From there on, Kohli first build a partnership with Yuvraj Singh and then with MS Dhoni to guide the hosts home.

Kohli remained not out at 82 in the end, while Dhoni ably supported the India star with a resilient knock of 18 runs as India knocked Australia out of the tournament.

In his book, Khawaja wrote, "We were cruising to a win too, as he (Kohli) was batting with an injured Yuvraj Singh. He couldn't run two and Virat is the king of twos. It cooked his whole momentum.

"Then Shane Watson took a hanger to get rid of Yuvraj, which was the worst thing we could have done, because in came the king, Dhoni," Khawaja went on to explain how the dismissal was the costliest for Australia as Dhoni joined Kohli in the middle of the game.

Khawaja hailed Kohli's innings as one of the best knocks which he had seen, remembering the way Kohli had changed gears after Dhoni got out with him on the crease.

"We reached the knockout stage of the T20 World Cup, but then needed to beat India. Unfortunately, Virat Kohli played one of the best knocks I've ever seen," Khawaja wrote.

Adding to the effect that Yuvraj's injury had on India's running between the wicket, he added, "Virat and Dhoni were a sight to see. Running quick twos at will. You could sense the game disappearing, then Virat went into hyperdrive and started smacking boundaries everywhere."

What Khawaja remembered in particular was that incredible six of Kohli off James Faulkner, when Kohli hammered that slower ball over Faulkner's head even though the ball was pitched short of good length.

"Normally, people would hit across the line over midwicket to be able to generate enough power, but Virat had a different idea. He somehow hit it back over his head off the back foot for six – a truly amazing shot.

"I'd see him hit a similar one against Haris Rauf in the T20 World Cup in Australia at the MCG a few years later," Khawaja compared that shot to one during 2022 T20 World Cup.

India required 39 runs in their last three overs when the partnership between Kohli and Dhoni started. Kohli had scored 50 from 40 balls at the end of the 17th over, while Dhoni had scored just 12 runs. James Faulkner bowled the 18th over, but Kohli smashed him for 19 runs, putting India firmly in the driver's seat.

He continued his onslaught against Nathan Coulter-Nile, and India required just four runs from the final over. Kohli ended up unbeaten at 82 off 51 balls, including nine fours and two sixes. Dhoni made an unbeaten 18 from 10 balls, while the duo added 67 runs for the fifth wicket.

India finished with a score of 161/4 in 19.1 overs, winning by six wickets and making it through to the semi-finals. This match is counted as one of the highlights of Kohli's career in T20 international cricket.
