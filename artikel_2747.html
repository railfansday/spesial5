Brock Bowers doesn't know whether he will play Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Raiders tight end Brock Bowers is listed as questionable for Sunday's game against the Broncos. Even Bowers, who has quad and ankle injuries, doesn't know if he will play.

"We'll see," he said, via Ryan McFadden of ESPN.

Bowers didn't practice Wednesday but was limited Thursday and Friday. He said he was "feeling all right," but can't say whether he will play.

"I don't know. We still have some conversations to have," Bowers said.

Bowers injured his right ankle on the opening drive of the Raiders' 21-13 loss to the Seahawks. He tried to play through it before leaving for good.

Bowers played 11 snaps, and he caught two passes for 18 yards.

"I really wanted to play, so I was just doing everything I could to get back out there, like just trying a new tape job and stuff," Bowers said. "Felt it each time I went back out there. It was tough."

Bowers has 50 catches for 676 yards and six touchdowns this season.
