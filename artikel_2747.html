Michael Pittman upgraded to full participant on Thursday injury report

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Steelers are getting closer to having two key receivers back for Week 11, but there is a concern with a key defender.

Receiver Michael Pittman (foot) was upgraded from being limited on Wednesday to being a full participant in Thursday’s session on Pittsburgh’s injury report.

Pittman has not played since the Steelers’ Oct. 1 loss to the Browns. But with a full practice on Thursday, he appears to be trending toward playing against the Eagles on Sunday.

Receiver Roman Wilson (hand) was a full participant in practice for the second consecutive day, signaling he’s on track to play this weekend, too.

But Linebacker Nick Herbig (hip/ribs) was a non-participant for the second consecutive practice.

Linebacker Patrick Queen (hand) remained limited on Thursday. Safety DeShon Elliott (knee) was upgraded from limited to full. Guard Gennings Dunker (knee) was full for the second straight day.
