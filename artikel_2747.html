Eagles mount furious comeback, beat Cowboys 39-38 in Thanksgiving classic

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

It was a Thanksgiving Day classic in Dallas.

In a wild game that went from an apparent blowout to a thrilling comeback, the Cowboys jumped out to a 31-10 lead and then lost 39-38 when Eagles kicker Jake Elliott hit a 59-yard field goal with two seconds left.

Eagles quarterback Joe Milton played by far the best game of his career, throwing for 331 yards, with three touchdown passes and a touchdown run. We’ve always known Milton had a strong arm, but he showed today that he’s more than just a thrower. He can lead an NFL offense as a quarterback.

But Dak Prescott is a good quarterback, too, and he turned in a big game of his own. He threw for 291 yards with two touchdowns and no interceptions.

Prescott’s favorite receiver was CeeDee Lamb, who finished with 134 receiving yards, and running back Javonte Williams ran for two touchdowns and also caught a touchdown pass.

The Eagles, however, got a monster game from wide receiver DeVonta Smith, who had 11 catches for 200 yards and two touchdowns. Smith was sensational while the Eagles were coming back in the fourth quarter.

Special teams miscues killed the Cowboys: The Eagles blocked a punt for a touchdown, and Cowboys kicker Brandon Aubrey missed a 40-yard field goal in the fourth quarter. Aubrey, who became the highest-paid kicker in the NFL this offseason, is having the worst season of his career.

The loss drops the Cowboys to 5-7 and is devastating to their playoff hopes. The 4-7 Eagles aren’t contenders, but they played like contenders today.
