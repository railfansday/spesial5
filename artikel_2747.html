Cowboys put 51 on the board vs. Titans

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Last week, 40 points weren’t enough to get a win for the Cowboys. This week, 51 should do it.

After a long defensive return following a Tennessee fumble deep in the Dallas end, the Cowboys scored their seventh touchdown in eight possessions to get to 51 points against the Titans.

The three-yard touchdown pass to tight end Brevyn Spann-Ford from quarterback Dak Prescott was Prescott’s fifth total touchdown of the game (four passing, one rushing).

Now leading 51-23 late in the fourth, the victory will move the Cowboys to 5-6 on the season. They host the Eagles on Thanksgiving.

The only bad news is that the Cowboys extended their franchise-record streak of 20 or more points allowed, Dallas opponents have now hit at least 20 in 18 straight games.

Both receiver CeeDee Lamb and receiver George Pickens have broken the 100-yard mark on Sunday.
