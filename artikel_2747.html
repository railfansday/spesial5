Broncos eyeing the one seed

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Week 11 of the NFL slate is in the books, and we had some significant changes to the AFC Playoff picture. First, our Denver Broncos moved back into the top spot of the AFC West while both the Bengals and Jaguars hurt their chances of getting the No. 1 seed with losses to the Commanders and the Giants.

Meanwhile, the Chiefs fell back to the 5th seed while the Browns moved up a spot during their bye week. The Steelers' free fall continues, but due to the struggles of the AFC, they currently do not have much competition for that 7th seed, but that could change.

Looking ahead, the Broncos will be watching the outcome of the Thanksgiving Night game between the Buffalo Bills and the Kansas City Chiefs. Either outcome benefits the Broncos if they can defeat the Pittsburgh Steelers on Black Friday, but a Bills loss and a Broncos win would move them to the No. 1 seed in the AFC.

Let us take an in-depth team-by-team look at the AFC Playoff picture and the remaining schedule for each of the contenders.

8. New England Patriots (5-5)9. Houston Texans (4-6)10. Las Vegas Raiders (4-7)11. Baltimore Ravens (4-7)12. New York Jets (3-8, 3-5 AFC)13. Indianapolis Colts (3-8, 2-6 AFC)14. Los Angeles Chargers (1-9, 1-6 AFC)15. Tennessee Titans (1-9, 1-6 AFC)16. Miami Dolphins (1-9, 1-6 AFC)

The Bills defeated the Dolphins on Sunday to improve to 9-1 on the year and continue to be the top seed in the AFC as we head into the Thanksgiving week of games. This week, they will host their nemesis, Patrick Mahomes and the Kansas City Chiefs on Thanksgiving Night, which figures to be a cold, rainy, and windy night in Buffalo. The Mafia figures to be loud in their new stadium and will look to hold off the Chiefs. A loss would knock them out of the 1 seed and give that title to either the Broncos or Chiefs. So, this is a big game for the Bills and Chiefs and a potential postseason preview.

Looking ahead, the Bills host the Chiefs on Thursday Night and then will travel to Foxboro to take on the reeling New England Patriots. After that, they take on two NFC North teams in the Packers and Bears before a Christmas showdown vs. Bo Nix and the Denver Broncos. They will then finish out the year taking on the Dolphins and Jets

Looking ahead: vs. Chiefs, @ Patriots, @ Packers, vs. Bears, @ Broncos, @ Dolphins, vs. Jets

The Broncos keep on winning and appear to have found a running game. Rookie Jonah Coleman rushed for over 100 yards and had 3 touchdowns in their victory over Fernando Mendoza and the Las Vegas Raiders. This win allowed the Broncos to retake the AFC West lead as they eye winning the AFC and AFC West in back-to-back seasons. This week, the Broncos will head east on Black Friday to take on Aaron Rodgers and the reeling Pittsburgh Steelers, who are coming off back-to-back losses.

Looking ahead, the Broncos have a very favorable schedule. After the Steelers, they have games vs. the Dolphins and Jets before a rematch vs. the Raiders. Their biggest game remaining on the schedule is a rematch of the Divisional Round game on Christmas when they host Josh Allen and the Buffalo Bills. They will finish out the season facing the Patriots and Chargers.

Looking ahead: @ Steelers, vs. Dolphins, @ Jets, @ Raiders, vs. Bills, @ Patriots, and vs. Chargers

The Jags entered the week as one of the hottest teams in the NFL, but then they ran into J.J. McCarthy and the upstart New York Giants, who would pull off the upset. This loss hurts their chances of winning the one seed, as they will lose a tiebreaker with the Broncos and now trail them by one game in the loss column. They remain the three seed after the Bengals were also upset on Monday Night, but this was a tough loss for Liam Coen's squad.

Looking ahead, they host the Titans this week before another tough road game vs. the Chicago Bears. They will then face the Texans, Cowboys, and Commanders, who all could be fighting for their postseason lives before finishing out the year against the Colts, who will be eyeing the offseason at that point.

Looking ahead: Titans, @ Bears, vs. Steelers, @ Texans, @ Cowboys, vs. Commanders, and @ Colts

The Bengals entered the week having the hottest offense in the NFL. Quarterback Joe Burrow looks like an MVP frontrunner, and the combo of Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins looks unstoppable. However, you need more than an explosive offense to win in the NFL, and that was the case on Monday Night when their defense allowed Commanders QB Jayden Daniels to lead a comeback win in the final seconds of the 4th quarter. Daniels got them into makeable field goal range, and the Bengals would end up losing the game on a game-winning field goal as the clock hit zero. This loss hurts the Bengals' odds of winning the one seed, but their path to an AFC North title remains alive and well.

This coming week, they will host the Saints before a potential division lead-on-the-line game vs. the Browns the following week. They then have a big game vs. Patrick Mahomes and the Chiefs before games vs. the Panthers and Colts. They will finish out the season with back-to-back divisional games vs. the Ravens and the Browns.

Looking ahead: vs. Saints, @ Browns, vs. Chiefs, @ Panthers, @ Colts, vs. Ravens, and vs. Browns

The Chiefs have had a cupcake schedule on paper this season, and that continued this past week when they hosted fourth-string 40-year-old career journeyman Josh Johnson and the last-place Arizona Cardinals. The Chiefs easily marched to victory and improved their record to 8-2 but lost their AFC West lead to the Broncos due to a common opponents tie-breaker (the Broncos beat the Seahawks while the Chiefs lost to the Seahawks).

However, the Chiefs' schedule is about to get a lot tougher moving forward.

This week, they will head east to Buffalo on Thanksgiving Night to take on Josh Allen and the Bills and then travel west to take on Matthew Stafford and the Los Angeles Rams. They will then head east for a third straight road game vs. Joe Burrow and the explosive Bengals offense. They will finally return home and host the 1st-place 49ers. They will then finish out the year vs. the Chargers and Raiders.

Looking ahead: @ Bills, @Rams, @ Bengals, vs. Patriots, vs. 49ers, @ Chargers, vs. Raiders

The Browns enjoyed their bye week and saw themselves move from the 7th seed to the 6th seed after the Pittsburgh Steelers were upset by the reeling Philadelphia Eagles.

Looking ahead, they will host rookie quarterback Fernando Mendoza and the Las Vegas Raiders this Sunday before a big divisional matchup vs. the Bengals the following week. They will then face two NFC teams in the Falcons and Giants before games vs. the Ravens and Colts. They will finish out the season with a potentially big divisional game vs. the Bengals that may have postseason implications.

Looking ahead: vs. Raiders, vs. Bengals, vs. Falcons, @ Giants, @ Ravens, vs. Colts, and @ Bengals

The Steelers have now lost two games in a row after an upset loss to the Philadelphia Eagles this past Sunday. Now, they will host the Denver Broncos on Black Friday and will look to avoid their third-straight loss. They are now the 7th seed in the AFC but currently do not have an obvious team on their heels pushing for that final playoff spot.

Looking ahead, they will host the Broncos and Texans before a tough road game vs. the Jaguars. They will then have games vs. their rival Ravens and then the Panthers before finishing out the year against the Titans and their rival Ravens in the finale.

Looking ahead: vs. Broncos, vs. Texans, @Jaguars, vs. Ravens, vs. Panthers, @ Titans, and @ Ravens

8. New England Patriots (5-5): QB Drake Maye is sidelined for an extended period of time after he suffered a broken fibula, and the Patriots' postseason hopes appear to be sinking, and sinking fast.

9. Houston Texans (4-6): The Texans might have the best shot of any team on this list to make the playoffs, but they have played inconsistently this season and cannot afford many more losses if they want to make a push in the final month.

10. Las Vegas Raiders (4-7): After starting the season 3-0, they have gone 1-7 the rest of the way, with their lone victory coming vs. the Jets. Could they go on a run and push for a playoff spot? Maybe, but with a rookie quarterback and a 4-7 record currently, that seems unlikely.

11. Baltimore Ravens (4-7): The Ravens have lost 6 straight games and are quickly falling out of the postseason conversation. Lamar Jackson is sidelined, and Jacoby Brissett has not played well recently. It is possible that they can push the Steelers for the final spot, but they have dug themselves in quite the hole and have little room for error moving forward.
