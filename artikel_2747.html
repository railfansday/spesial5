Rex Ryan politicks for job with Cowboys

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Rex Ryan is plenty of things. Bashful is not one of them.

Days after being arrested for allegedly running over the feet of two people in Nashville with his pickup truck and fleeing the scene, Ryan is on the air — and he’s using the bully pulpit to politick for a job with the Cowboys.

“I could go there today and say, ‘Alright, we can have an average ass defense,’” Ryan said on ESPN New York, via Brandon Contes of Awful Announcing. “I’ll promise you they’d be average, I’d probably make them top-10. But I’ll take that group right now, in one week, I’d go in and turn this thing out.”

Ryan’s pitch came after he ripped the current defensive coaching staff in Dallas.

“It’s the worst coached defense I’ve ever seen,” Ryan said. “It really is. And I’ve seen some horrible defenses, this one’s awful, and it starts with coaching. . . . They make more mental mistakes than any defense I’ve seen, as well as the physical, they’re not a tough physical defense.”

As noted separately by Michael McCarthy of Front Office Sports, Ryan also criticized the Dallas defense on ESPN’s Get Up.

“It starts with the young guy they hired, Christian Parker,” Ryan said. “What on his resume told you he was going to be a good defensive coordinator candidate for a team like the Dallas Cowboys with Super Bowl aspirations? By the way, what told you anything about him that would make you think he could be a leader in this? Because you better hire a leader for that spot.”

(It would have been great if someone else on the show would have said, “Yeah, the Cowboys defense is extremely pedestrian.”)

“To me, absolute garbage,” Ryan said. “You can’t fire them all yet, but you damn sure better. To me, it’s the whole defensive staff. It’s not just Christian Parker. It’s not. It’s every one of those coaches on defense.”

Prior to the 2024 season, the Cowboys were considering hiring Ryan to be the defensive coordinator. Eventually, Dallas brought back former Cowboys defensive coordinator (and former Vikings coach) Mike Zimmer. In September 2024, Ryan said he would have had the job if owner and G.M. Jerry Jones had been willing to pay.

“I could’ve fixed [the Cowboys’ defense] in a New York minute,” Ryan said at the time. “But, that being said, they couldn’t pony up the money, or I would’ve been there.”

Jones obviously could have come up with the money. But he wouldn’t. Because he doesn’t. And it’s hard to imagine Jones cleaning house on the defensive side of the ball and giving the keys to Ryan, who hasn’t had a coaching job in more than a decade.

Especially if Jones would owe any of the defensive coaches a buyout.

Then again, if Ryan is willing to work for whatever Jerry will pay, that could be the key to getting Ryan a chance to put his feet where his mouth is.
