Charlie Jones out, Xavier Gipson in as Giants shake up return game

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The New York Giants cut wide receiver and kickoff/punt returner Charlie Jones on Monday amidst a flurry of roster moves. They also cut defensive back Nikko Reed. Added to the roster were defensive back Tavierre Thomas and wide receiver return man Xavier Gipson.

Jones signed with the Giants when he was cut by the Cincinnati Bengals when they trimmed their roster to 53 players. A four-year veteran, Jones averaged 10.0 yards on 16 punt returns and 23.8 yards on 14 kickoff returns.

Jones had a key fumble in a loss to the New Orleans Saints. He also muffed a punt against the Philadelphia Eagles, and has been dealing with a thumb injury.

Gipson is being signed off the Philadelphia Eagles' practice squad. This will actually be Gipson's third time as a Giant. He was claimed off waivers from the New York Jets in 2025, then lost to the Eagles. He was re-signed to the practice squad late last season, and was with the team throughout training camp before being released in final roster cuts.

Gipson, 25, has extensive experience as a returner. He has averaged 9.1 yards on 74 punt returns and 25.9 yards on 51 kickoff returns.

Gipson's issue has always been ball security. He has 12 fumbles in 163 career touches, one every 13.6 touches, and has lost jobs with the Jets and Eagles largely because of those issues. If the Giants are looking for added ball security, turning to Gipson is risky.

Reed, who was signed off the Los Angeles Chargers' practice squad earlier this season, appeared in three games for the Giants. He played entirely on special teams. He was cut to make room for Thomas, whose signing was reported earlier in the day.

The Giants also worked out running back Antonio Gibson, who spent four seasons with the Washington Commanders and two with the New England Patriots. Gibson had a 1,000-yard season in 2021 with Washington.
