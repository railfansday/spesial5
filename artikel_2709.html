San Antonio vs. Sacramento, Final Score: Short-handed Spurs scorch the nets in 123-107 win

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Victor Wembanyama, De'Aaron Fox, and Tobias Harris sat shoulder to shoulder, decked out in designer clothes, on the sidelines of the Golden 1 Center. Typically, the San Antonio Spurs would need them to don their uniforms to win a game, but that wasn't necessary on Monday night. Down four key rotation players, the Spurs took down the Sacramento Kings at home 123-107.

The Spurs played an analytical game, taking a lot of threes and frequently getting to the free-throw line. San Antonio knocked down 16 of their 46 three-pointers, and shot 23-for-25 from the stripe. The Spurs pulled out a double-digit victory despite being outscored in the paint 54-46.

Without a majority of the starting lineup, Stephon Castle led the Spurs with a near triple-double. Castle led all scorers with 28 points and added 8 rebounds and 11 assists, all while turning the ball over only twice. The Kings elected not to guard Castle from three, and he made them pay, shooting four-for-eight from deep.

Domantas Sabonis led the way for the Kings with 20 points, 12 rebounds and 8 assists on an efficient 8-for-9 shooting from the field. He carved the Spurs up inside, but wasn't able to provide enough defensively to keep Sacramento in the game.

San Antonio has now won three straight games. They'll look to keep their winning streak alive in an important NBA Cup game against the Portland Trail Blazers on Wednesday night.

Julian Champagnie's hot shooting in the second quarter opened up a big lead for the Spurs. He hit five three-pointers in the frame and then didn't make another for the rest of the game. Champagnie scored 20 of his 22 points in the first half.

Keldon Johnson's passing continues to be one of the best new wrinkles in San Antonio's offensive attack. He's found great touch on lobs, especially after forcefully getting downhill on a drive. He tallied five assists, including an alley-oop that led to a thunderous Luke Kornet jam late in the fourth quarter.

Kornet filled in admirably for Wembanyama, just as he did last season. Kornet had a double-double with 18 points and 11 rebounds. He served as a big target on lob attempts in the pick-and-roll and fought for position on the offensive glass. Maybe a game like this is what he needed to find his rhythm for the rest of the season.

Tarris Reed Jr. played substantial minutes for the first time this season and got better as the game went along. Early on, he looked out of position and a bit timid, but as he got used to the pace of the game, he settled in and looked a lot more comfortable. Reed Jr. had 7 points, 7 rebounds, and 2 blocks in 15 minutes. One area where Reed Jr. thrived was on the glass, where he used his big body to clear out space and gobble up rebounds. He picked up three offensive boards in his limited playing time.

The Spurs' best offensive play against the Kings was the pick-and-roll, particularly with Castle handling and Kornet screening. Castle made some great reads to find shooters wide open in the corner as the Kings overhelped to contest Kornet in the paint. When Sacramento adjusted, Castle was able to get ot the basket or find his big man on lobs.

Ja'Kobi Gillespie played some excellent on-ball defense against Malik Monk at the end of the third quarter. He stayed in front of him and forced a bad shot. Overall, the rookie guard looked solid in his first meaningful NBA action. He had 3 points (a catch-and-shoot three), 4 assists, and a steal with no turnovers in 15 minutes. San Antonio desperately needed another player who could handle the ball and run the pick-and-roll on Monday. Gillespie filled in admirably.

David Jones-Garcia checked in during the second quarter, air-balled a three, then was subbed out and didn't return until the final moment of garbage time, when he knocked down a mid-range jumper.

San Antonio checked in a super small-ball lineup in the fourth quarter with Harrison Barnes playing center. It did not work and allowed the Kings to cut the lead to single digits. The Spurs put the starters back in and extended the lead, and the game never got to single digits again.
