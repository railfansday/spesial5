Brewers draw hard line on William Contreras while opening door for Trevor Megill trade

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Milwaukee Brewers are standing firm on their plans for William Contreras, making it clear that the World Series MVP is not available for trade. While Milwaukee continues to shut down any possibility of moving its star catcher, the club appears more willing to entertain offers for closer Trevor Megill.

Bob Nightengale of USA Today reports that Milwaukee has informed other clubs that Contreras will remain with the Brewers. The team is also interested in working out a contract extension with its All-Star catcher. Megill, meanwhile, is available for trade discussions, highlighting the organization's different plans for keeping its World Series-winning roster intact.

These reports largely reaffirm what had already been expected from Milwaukee's front office. Contreras was previously viewed as unlikely to be dealt, while Megill had emerged as a possible trade candidate. The catcher remains a key part of the Brewers' future after playing a major role in the franchise's first World Series title. He also claimed World Series MVP honors after posting 18 home runs and 94 RBIs during the regular season.

Milwaukee picked up Contreras' $15.5 million option for the 2027 season, but he could enter free agency after that year if the two sides fail to agree on a longer deal. Megill is approaching a similar point in his contract. The veteran reliever finished the 2026 campaign with 30 saves and a 2.63 ERA over 63 appearances.

The Brewers have a history of dealing established pitchers nearing free agency, as seen with Corbin Burnes, Devin Williams and Freddy Peralta. That track record helps explain the interest surrounding Megill's availability. However, Milwaukee's willingness to hear offers does not mean a deal is inevitable. The team could keep its closer if the proposals it receives do not provide enough value to justify losing a key piece of its bullpen.

Milwaukee's current approach suggests it wants to keep Contreras as its starting catcher while remaining open to offers for Megill. The key issue now is whether the Brewers can agree to a long-term extension with Contreras before he gets closer to free agency.
