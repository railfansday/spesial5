Five key takeaways from Florida's road win at FSU during Rivalry Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Florida football saved one of its strongest defensive performances for its biggest rivalry, defeating Florida State 26-10 on Friday afternoon in Tallahassee to close out Jon Sumrall's first season as head coach.

The No. 20 Gators entered the matchup looking to extend their winning streak to three games, and although the offense struggled to capitalize on several scoring opportunities, the defense delivered enough timely plays to keep the Seminoles from mounting a comeback.

The victory improved Florida to 8-4, giving the Orange and Blue additional momentum heading into bowl season.

Here are five key takeaways from the Gators' 16-point victory over their in-state rivals.

Florida's secondary has experienced its share of inconsistencies this season, particularly when opposing receivers have found space beyond the first level of the defense. However, J'vari Flowers delivered arguably the unit's most impressive individual performance of the year against the Seminoles.

Flowers recorded two interceptions, providing the Gators with much-needed takeaways in a rivalry matchup where every possession mattered. His second interception essentially sealed the victory, preventing Florida State from generating any late momentum.

While the offense struggled at times, Florida's defense took control early by keeping the Seminoles out of the end zone. The Gators forced Florida State to work for scoring opportunities and prevented the home team from sustaining drives consistently.

Florida State eventually found the end zone after backup quarterback Kevin Sperry entered for the injured Ashton Daniels and connected with Landen Thomas on a scoring pass. Nevertheless, that proved to be the Seminoles' only touchdown of the afternoon.

Holding FSU to 10 points was the lowest margin the Gators held an opponent to since Week 2, when they faced Campbell.

Jadan Baugh added another milestone to his impressive collegiate career Friday, becoming the first player in Florida football history to record consecutive 1,000-yard rushing seasons. The third-year running back reached the historic mark on a 28-yard run in the second quarter against Florida State.

Baugh finished with 77 rushing yards and a touchdown on 19 carries, helping Florida secure its third straight win to close out the regular season.

Despite closing the regular season with three consecutive victories, Florida still faces questions surrounding its quarterback situation heading into the offseason.

Aaron Philo and Tramell Jones Jr. both got opportunities under center throughout the 2026 campaign, but neither popped off the screen or won games for the program the way you'd want.

Philo showed encouraging development late in the year, particularly in his mobility, and Jones demonstrated his potential earlier in the season when he helped lead Florida to a 27-24 upset victory over Texas in his first collegiate start.

However, with the transfer portal opening Jan. 4, the Gators could explore adding another veteran quarterback to compete for the starting job.

The Gators' regular season featured plenty of adversity, but finishing with three consecutive victories is an encouraging conclusion to Sumrall's inaugural campaign.

After defeating Kentucky and Vanderbilt in consecutive weeks, Florida completed the regular-season sweep of its final three opponents with Friday's rivalry victory.

The win improved the Gators to 8-4 overall, a positive finish for a team that struggled with offensive and defensive inconsistencies throughout the year.

Although questions remain about the team's consistency and overall execution, closing the regular season with a rivalry win gives the Gators a foundation to build on as they head into bowl preparations.
