Jordan Love, Christian Watson cut Rams lead to 13-10

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The points keep coming in the first half at SoFi Stadium.

A little more than three minutes after the Rams scored their first touchdown of the night, the Packers paid their first visit to the end zone. Jordan Love hooked up with Christian Watson for a six-yard score that cut the Rams’ lead to 13-10 with just over eight minutes to play in the half.

Watson also caught a pass on the first play of the drive and that completion went for 21 yards. Love hit Watson’s fellow wideout Matthew Golden for a 39-yard gain on the next play and the quarterback scrambled for the third first down of the possession a couple of plays after that big gain.

The Packers went three-and-out and turned the ball over on their first two possessions, but they are now outgaining the Rams thanks to back-to-back scoring drives.
