Woody Marks logs 19 touches against Chargers in Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Houston Texans RB Woody Marks rushed 18 times for 70 yards and caught one pass for four yards against the Los Angeles Chargers in Week 9.

Why it Matters: With David Montgomery still sidelined, Marks continues to lead the Houston backfield. He hasn't provided a ton of big plays, but Marks has logged at least 17 touches in four straight games. If Montgomery remains sidelined, Marks can be a volume-based flex option.
