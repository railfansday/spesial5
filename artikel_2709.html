Matt LaFleur confirms Packers plan to have Micah Parsons return Week 12 vs. Rams

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It was widely reported that edge rusher Micah Parsons was targeting the Week 12 game against the Rams for his return from reconstructive knee surgery.

Now that is getting closer to reality, as the Packers will be on their bye in Week 11 before playing in Los Angeles next Wednesday night.

Head coach Matt LaFleur told reporters on Monday that his plan is for Parsons to play, but that’s still not guaranteed.

“Well, that’s my plan, but I think my plan’s been different than everybody else’s the whole time,” LaFleur said, via Rob Demovsky of ESPN. “There’s still a couple more hurdles for him to clear before we can definitively say he’s a go. He’s shown progress, but we’ll see where he is by the end of the week.”

The Packers opened Parsons’ 21-day practice window earlier this month, setting him up for his return. He has been a limited participant in practice for all three days in each of the last two weeks.
