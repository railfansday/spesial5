St. Henry has added 15 new members into athletics hall of fame

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

St. Henry District High School inducted 15 new members into its athletic hall of fame in a ceremony on Oct. 17.

Ten new members were celebrated with the Athletic Excellence Award, two were given the Father Bach Community Award and three were given the Bill Toberget Service Award.

Also honored was St. Henry's 2010 girls soccer team as the Team of Distinction. In 2010, St. Henry 24-2-1, beating Simon Kenton in the 9th Region championship and Lexington Christian 2-1 in the Kentucky High School Athletic Association state championship.

Steve Rice, a 1975 graduate, and Denny Seibert and Jeff Schuler were awarded with the service award. Daniel (a 1970 graduate) and Karen Riegler were given the community award.

Chris Danks (2003), Gordon Dooley (2008), former head basketball coach Dave Faust, Ray Hoffman (1945), Krista Dowell Knapke (1993), Gerard Koop (1975), Ralph Landrum (1975), Libby Leedom Nields (2013), Stephanie Niemer (2007) and Abbey Bessler Schuster (2013) were inducted with the excellence award.
