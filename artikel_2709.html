Giants injuries: QB J.J. McCarthy, RB Cam Skattebo look ready to play

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Quarterback J.J. McCarthy and running back Cam Skattebo were both full practice participants for the New York Giants on Tuesday. That is a sign that both McCarthy (hip) and Skattebo (hamstring) should play Thursday against the Washington Commanders.

Head coach John Harbaugh said that McCarthy "looked fine" moving around during practice, and that Skattebo was "moving well."

McCarthy made it clear he plans on playing Thursday night.

"I feel really good," he said. "Obviously, Sunday, it's football, it's a physical game, but just credit to the athletic training staff, getting me back to feeling really good as quick as possible."

Cornerback Colton Hood is dealing with a dislocated finger suffered during practice.
