Chicago Bears beat New Orleans Saints to claim eighth win of season

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Chicago Bears beat New Orleans Saints 21-10 to rack up their eighth NFL victory of the season.

Bears put in a sluggish display but they emerged victorious thanks to touchdowns from Cairo Santos, D'Andre Swift, Colston Loveland and Julian Blackmon which put them into a 16-0 lead.

Saints then hit back and cut the arrears thanks to a score from Alvin Kamara.

The Bears now face a quick turnaround this week as they head to Detroit to face the Lions next week. The two teams will both go into the game at 8-2 overall in the NFC North division.

Tennessee Titans' poor run continued as they lost for the ninth time in 10 matches, going down 51-30 to the Dallas Cowboys, the first time that score had been recorded in NFL history.

Titans fell victim again to their defensive frailties, Dak Prescott leading the charge for Cowboys with CeeDee Lamb reaching the 1,000-yard receiving mark for the sixth consecutive season.

Javonte Williams also had an impactful afternoon, adding multiple touchdowns as Dallas repeatedly found ways through the Titans defence.

Detroit Lions made it six straight victories thanks to a 31-21 victory over Tampa Bay Buccaneers.

Jahmyr Gibbs made the difference for Lions with two touchdowns to edge a tight affair.

Buffalo Bills triumphed 26-9 over Miami Dolphins, Carolina Panthers edged out Baltimore Ravens 35-33 in a tight match and Kansas City Chiefs beat Arizona Cardinals 34-7.

Philadelphia Eagles were 19-16 winners against Pittsburgh Steelers.

Patrick Mahomes and Travis Kelce proved themselves once again a formidable team in the Kansas City Chiefs' 34-7 win over the Arizona Cardinals.

Mahomes was responsible for three touchdown passes, including two to Kelce, ahead of their Thanksgiving game against the Bills.

The Denver Broncos beat a spirited Las Vegas Raiders 38-24 thanks to rookie Jonah Coleman's career-best efforts including, rushing 24 times for 111 yards and three scores.

The New York Giants ended the Jacksonville Jaguars three-game winning streak with a 23-18 win in New Jersey, while the New York Jets held on for a nail-biting 27-24 victory over the Los Angeles Chargers.

The San Francisco 49ers demolished the Minnesota Vikings 35-10 in front of a rapturous crowd at Mexico City's Estadio Azteca.

Both teams had specialty training in the lead-up to the game to help them play at the high altitude of 7,300 feet above sea level.
