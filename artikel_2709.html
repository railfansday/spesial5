Blake Corum finds end zone in Week 9 vs. Commanders

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Los Angeles Rams running back Blake Corum rushed 11 times for 58 yards and a touchdown in Week 9 against the Washington Commanders, while he went without a catch on two targets.

Why it Matters: Corum has forced his way into a timeshare with Kyren Williams. While the latter had more carries (15), Williams averaged just 3.0 yards per carry to 5.3 YPC for Corum. Williams is a more superior pass catcher, so those roles aren't likely to change. But, Corum should get 10-12 carries per game, and he remains a solid RB3 or flex fantasy option in most leagues.
