8 trending teams + a fast NBA Draft riser with John Fanta

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Kevin O'Connor is joined by John Fanta to break down trending teams in the NBA. Which teams are trending up, down and holding steady?

The pair deep dives into Luka Dončić's MVP-worthy dominance for the Lakers, the Portland Trail Blazers' rise up the Western Confernce standings and the Dallas Mavericks' struggles on both ends of the floor. They also debate if the Miami Heat are finally on the right track, react to Cleveland's struggling backcourt and take a quick look at Victor Wembanyama's latest leap.

Plus, the duo touches on early NBA Draft buzz, highlighting rising college star Quentin Coleman of Illinois.
