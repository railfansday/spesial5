The ‘choice’ Ohio State doesn’t have for Michigan game after Nebraska debacle, per Ryan Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Ohio State football against Michigan is an iconic rivalry. But Nebraska's upset of the Buckeyes, plus Michigan being 6-5, sparked a Comedy Central joke from college football insider Brett McMurphy. OSU coach Ryan Day isn't in a joking mood, though.

Day sent out this message regarding the "choice" the Buckeyes don't have for this game.

"We don't have a choice. We've got to get this thing together next week and go win this game. That's important," Day told reporters.

Day, however, also lands in evaluation mode: Evaluating any potential changes to make on the Buckeyes.

"When you watch a game like this, you've got a lot of things that have to change," Day said postgame. "I'm not going to jump to anything right now because we have a game we've got to play in another week. Like always, we have to evaluate what's going on, and we'll do that."

Perhaps this signals staff changes moving forward involving Day. Ohio State's once stout defenses took steps backward under Matt Patricia, leaving many wondering if he's on his way out. Offensive coordinator Arthur Smith got met with mixed results too in his first season at Columbus.

Ohio State has taken its most regular season losses since 2011 when Luke Fickell coached on an interim basis. That OSU team finished 6-7 overall before eventually introducing Urban Meyer as head coach. But this Buckeyes team has their most single season losses in the Day era. He never lost more than two games until this season arrived.

A loss to Michigan certainly will ignite the "fire Day" crowd.
