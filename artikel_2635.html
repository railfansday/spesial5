Bia Mesquita keeps up Ailin Perez callout after taking Rousey’s record at UFC 334

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

NEW YORK – Bia Mesquita met the media Saturday after her win over Macy Chiasson at UFC 334.

Mesquita (9-0 MMA, 4-0 UFC) made easy work of Chiasson (10-7 MMA, 8-7 UFC) on the prelims at Madison Square Garden. The "Lady GOAT" jiu-jitsu expert submitted the Season 28 "Ultimate Fighter" winner with a first-round rear-naked choke.

"That's insane (to break Ronda Rousey's bantamweight submission record). I wasn't expecting that, for real. … Ronda was one of the names that opened a lot of doors for all the women to be in the UFC, to have the space we have right now. Being able to break her record is just like amazing because my background is a grappler. I'm a jiu-jitsu fighter, and being able to show that and prove to people that's who I am, that's why I'm the 'Lady GOAT' – I go there and I put on a show and I finish people."

"(A quick rise is) one of the things me and (American Top Team) have planned since I decided to go to MMA. We know I'm not going to have 20 years that I had in jiu-jitsu. I'd like to keep climbing. The thing is to push the pace, but not run through the steps we need to have like to be at the top."

"The (callout) is still the same: I want to face Ailin Perez for the reasons I've been talking about so many times. But more than that, now she's in the top five. I hope I finish the year as a top 10. I think after this performance, I will. Being in the top 10, the next step is to be in the top five – so nothing better than facing her, fighting her, climbing to the top five, and fighting for the title. (I want to fight for the bantamweight title) hopefully by the end of 2027."
