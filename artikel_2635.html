Tacko Fall reacts to receiving real minutes after Sixers beat Pacers

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

PHILADELPHIA -- The Philadelphia 76ers needed some big men on Sunday against the Indiana Pacers as they had to battle Ivica Zubac, Jay Huff, and Larry Nance Jr. without the services of Joel Embiid. The Sixers decided to rest the big fella on the front end of a back-to-back and turned to Jabari Walker to start at the 5.

Walker was terrific as he had 11 points and seven rebounds in the start, but the Sixers needed some size and coach Nick Nurse turned to 7-foot-6 Tacko Fall in the third quarter to give him his first real minutes of the season. He had played in two previous games, but those situations came in garbage time.

Fall played five minutes, pulled down three rebounds, deterred the Pacers from getting into the paint, and his size made a difference as Philadelphia earned a 131-124 win on Sunday afternoon.

"I think I came in and tried to do my job, bring energy," Fall said after the win. "Coach trusted me to go out there and do what he knows I'm capable of, and that's really what I focus on."

Nurse had stated before the game that he just hasn't had the opportunity to see Fall in a game setting and see what he can really bring to the table. After playing in 37 games for the Boston Celtics and the Cleveland Cavaliers from 2019 through 2022, Fall had been out of the league until the Sixers signed him to a two-way deal in the offseason. It was something of a surprise for Fall to hear his name, but the big man just wanted to stay calm and produce for the Sixers.

"I try not to think too much," Fall explained. "Like I said, I knew what I had to do coming in, making sure I played hard, get us some extra possession, especially defensively, make sure that I protect the basket."

It has been a long journey for Fall to get back to this level. He had been toiling overseas before the Sixers gave him a real opportunity to come back to the league and make a difference. To get back on the floor and hear the roar of the Xfinity Mobile Arena crowd is a big deal to him. Will he get another opportunity? Who knows? But Fall will be ready for whatever comes his way.

"We'll see," Fall finished. "Nobody knows what's going to happen tomorrow. For me, I'm really focused on today. Whenever my name is called, like you said, I've worked very hard, and God has brought me back here, and I'm thankful. And like I said, I'm ready. So whenever my name is called, I'm going to go out there and do my job."

The Sixers will take on the Brooklyn Nets on Monday on the second night of a back-to-back.
