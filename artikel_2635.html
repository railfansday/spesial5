Jevón Holland on controversial Parker Washington call: "Shoutout Vegas"

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Back when the NFL hated gambling, it feared that widespread legalized sports betting would prompt fans to think the fix is in. Although the league has done its best to ignore the fact that its fears were accurate, not everyone has gotten the memo.

Giants safety Jevón Holland went there on Sunday, regarding the decision that Jaguars receiver Parker Washington failed to catch what would have been a touchdown pass in the fourth quarter.

“The game’s over? That might have been a catch,” Holland said, via Ryan Dunleavy of the New York Post. “That was a hell of a play. When they called it out, I was like, ‘Alright.’ Shoutout Vegas, man. I don’t know.”

Holland was surely joke. On the topic of suggesting that something fishy was happening, however, the NFL may not have a sense of humor.

It’s precisely the kind of comment to which the tinfoil-hat crowd will cling. Even if Holland doesn’t mean it, those who want to think the fix is in will seize on the statement of evidence that the fix is in.

As we’ve said time and again, the fix isn’t in. However, the NFL needs to be far more concerned about that perception than it is.
