Former NBA lottery pick Rob Dillingham gets punched by Wizards rookie Felix Okpara during G League game

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Playing for the G League Westchester Knicks, former NBA first-round pick Rob Dillingham was involved in a scuffle during Friday's matchup with the Capital City Go-Go.

In the fourth quarter of Westchester's 125-104 win, Dillingham was punched by Capital City center Felix Okpara at the top of the 3-point line. Dillingham pushed Okpara on his way down the court, and the center looked at the official for a foul call.

When one wasn't made, Okpara clotheslined Dillingham across the neck and shoulders, knocking him to the floor.

Both teams then gathered in a scrum past the Westchester basket, going off the court as Okpara backpedaled toward the baseline. Coaches, officials and arena security personnel then separated each group of players.

Ermal Kuqo, the Knicks' senior director of minor league operations, and his 6-foot-10, 270-pound frame also got involved, which surely helped provide a significant obstacle to fisticuffs.

Dillingham and Okpara were subsequently both ejected from the game. Dillingham finished with 10 points and 8 assists, while Okpara scored 12 points with 9 rebounds for the Go-Go.

Okpara was the Washington Wizards' second-round pick in the 2026 NBA Draft, selected No. 46 overall out of Tennessee. In three games with the Wizards, he averaged one point and 0.7 rebounds in five minutes of play. In the G League, Okpara is scoring 11.4 points, 8.8 rebounds and 2.4 blocks per game.

Dillingham was the San Antonio Spurs' 2024 first-round pick, selected No. 8 overall out of Kentucky. He was traded to the Minnesota Timberwolves on draft night for a first-round pick swap in 2030 and first-rounder in 2031.

Last season, the T-Wolves dealt him midway through his second season in a trade involving Ayo Dosunmu going to the Chicago Bulls. Before training camp this season, he was moved to the Charlotte Hornets in exchange for Buddy Hield but was subsequently waived. In November, he signed with the New York Knicks' G League team.

In three games with Westchester, Dillingham is averaging 21 points and 6.3 assists per game while shooting 35% on 17 3-point attempts.
