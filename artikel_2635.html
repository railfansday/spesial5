Gardner Minshew had surgery to repair torn ligament in throwing elbow

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Cardinals quarterback Gardner Minshew has had surgery to repair the elbow injury that ended his season.

Minshew had the surgery on his right ulnar collateral ligament on Monday and is expected to make a full recovery, according to ESPN.

Although tearing the UCL is a famously brutal injury for baseball pitchers that necessitated the development of the Tommy John surgery procedure, it tends not to be as severe for the throwing motion of a quarterback. Brock Purdy tore his UCL in the 49ers’ playoff loss in January of 2023, had surgery to repair it in March, and was healthy enough to play the full season and lead the 49ers to the Super Bowl the next year.

Minshew, who will be a free agent in March, should be healthy enough to sign with some team and play in 2027. Where he’ll be playing remains a question.
