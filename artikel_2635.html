Brock Bowers did not practice Wednesday with ankle, quad injuries

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Raiders coach Klint Kubiak called tight end Brock Bowers "day-to-day" earlier this week, but Wednesday was not the day.

Bowers did not practice with ankle and quadriceps injuries.

He left Sunday's game after only 11 snaps with his ankle injury.

Bowers has been on the injury report most of the season with hamstring, calf and knee issues.

He leads the Raiders with 50 catches, 676 receiving yards and six receiving touchdowns this season.

Defensive tackle Tonka Hemingway (stinger) also didn't practice.

Defensive tackle Folorunso Fatukasi (toe) and kicker Matt Gay (hip) were limited.

Defensive tackle Thomas Booker (ankle) and tight end Ian Thomas (calf) were full participants.
