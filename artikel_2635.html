Pass the gravy — Rams turn Packers game into a Thanksgiving feast

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

SoFi Stadium will be home for the NFL's first-ever Thanksgiving Eve game to take place between the Rams and Packers on Wednesday night, and they're doing everything they can to keep the holiday spirit in the game.

According to Arash Markazi, the founder of The Sporting Tribune, the Rams will have a special Thanksgiving menu that will be sold to fans at SoFi Stadium and available to purchase three hours before kickoff.

The menu will include smoked turkey legs served with cranberry barbecue sauce, a turkey pot pie, and smoked turkey with mashed potatoes that includes gravy with herb dressing and cranberry sauce.

According to The Sporting Tribune, the fans will have a few different options to eat turkey on bread, as SoFi Stadium will be selling a smoked turkey burnt ends sandwich, as well as panes con pavo, pairing turkey with crisp vegetables and the tang of curtido.

Los Angeles Rams mascot Rampage handing out turkeys at SoFi Stadium last week. AFP via Getty Images

For desserts, fans will have a few options available as miniature pumpkin, pecan, and Dutch apple pies will be sold, and hot apple cider will be on the menu.

The Rams will be selling a few drink options in the stadium, which include the "Ramsgiving Ponche," a blend of guava, apple, hibiscus, tamarind, and warm spices. Fans will also be able to purchase it with tequila or as a mocktail.

Rampage is handing out turkeys to football fans ahead of Thanksgiving. AFP via Getty Images

According to Markazi's post, fans will be able to purchase a special souvenir sipper that is an imitation of a miniature Rams water cooler.

The Rams' Thanksgiving menu is one of the many highlights that will take place in Inglewood, as the rock and roll band U2 will be performing live at halftime.

Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford posing for a photo on the Los Angeles sideline. AP Photo/Caroline Brehman

Los Angeles will also be debuting their new "Fearsome White" uniforms to honor the legendary Fearsome Foursome defensive line that dominated the NFL in the 1960s.

The Rams and Packers will kick the game off at 5:00 p.m. PT and will stream live on Netflix.
