Aaron Rodgers clears waivers

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

It’s official. Aaron Rodgers is a free agent, again.

For the third time since March 2025, Rodgers can sign with any team at any time.

The soon-to-be 43-year-old cleared waivers on Thursday, per multiple reports.

He can sign whenever he chooses, either to a practice squad or a 53-man roster. He can wait until the postseason, if he chooses.

Of course, someone will have to want to sign him. In the 2026 offseason, only the Steelers did. At this point, an injury to a starter on a contending team will be the one thing that could create immediate interest in his services.

As long as that team is willing to risk Rodgers eventually dialing up McAfee for another elongated monologue of grievances. At this point, any other outcome would be a surprise.
