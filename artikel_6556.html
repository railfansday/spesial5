Chrissy Teigen Reveals What Her Four Kids Actually Eat

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Even cookbook authors can't escape chicken nuggets.

You’d think having Chrissy Teigen as your mom would mean enjoying some pretty incredible meals every day. After all, she’s a cookbook author with her own food brand, Cravings, and is constantly experimenting with new recipes. But apparently, even Teigen can’t get all four of her kids to agree on dinner.

During a recent episode of Déjà Food, Teigen opened up about mealtime with her husband, John Legend, and their four children, Luna, Miles, Esti, and Wren.

“We are in this stage right now where nobody really wants to eat the same meal, but we also aren’t a restaurant,” Teigen explains when asked about the last meal all four children ate together.

“I can almost guarantee your answer is chicken nuggets and Kraft macaroni and cheese,” she says. “And then I get whatever is left on the baking sheet.”

And in an exclusive interview with Delish, Teigen reveals just how different her children's eating habits really are.

Miles, 8, is apparently the pickiest of the bunch. “Miles is our ‘won't eat anything’ guy,” she admits, explaining that he’s been selective about food since he started eating solids. His preferred foods include chicken nuggets, fries, hamburgers, and pizza.

Esti, 3, is a little less predictable. According to Teigen, she might insist on having apples with every dinner one day, only to decide she doesn’t like them the next.

Wren, meanwhile, is the complete opposite of his older brother. “Wren is our tank. He’ll eat anything,” Teigen says.

And then there’s Luna, 10, who appears to have inherited her mom’s interest in cooking. Teigen explains that Luna knows how to improve dishes, whether that means cutting off a burnt corner or finding another way to make something taste better.

In fact, Luna and Esti are regulars in the kitchen. Teigen says her daughters are so involved in cooking that Esti worries she’ll miss part of a recipe if she leaves the room.

But the kids aren’t getting away with chicken nuggets and mac and cheese every single day.

On Déjà Food, Teigen reveals that when developing a Cravings Banana Foster Pancake Mix, her entire family became her unofficial testing panel.

“Every morning, everyone in the whole family has to try our new pancake,” she says, joking that they’re being forced to eat the recipe throughout the week.

Despite everyone’s strong opinions, Teigen isn’t particularly concerned about her children’s changing tastes. She recalls disliking raw tomatoes and avocados when she was younger, even though she loves them now.

“They're kids, and they’re told what to do all day,” she says. “Food can be one way they speak up and feel like their opinions matter.”

It’s a pretty refreshing perspective from someone who’s built a career around food. And considering Teigen grew up loving Hungry-Man dinners, Kid Cuisine, and Hot Pockets herself, perhaps her kids’ appreciation for convenient comfort food shouldn’t be all that surprising.

As long as everyone is fed and happy, she’s not interested in turning dinner into a battle.
