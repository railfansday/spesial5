Blue Origin is pushing its New Glenn rocket's return to flight to January 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Dave Limp told employees pad reconstruction delays, including bad summer weather, pushed back the launch after a May explosion

Blue Origin, the space company founded by Jeff Bezos, is delaying the return to flight of its New Glenn rocket to January 2027, according to CNBC. The company had targeted the end of this year.

CEO Dave Limp wrote to staff on Wednesday that the launch date moved because the rebuild of the company's Cape Canaveral pad ran behind schedule, a problem made worse by poor summer weather, according to CNBC.

"This small adjustment doesn't diminish what we've accomplished or the importance of the work still ahead," Limp wrote in the memo, according to CNBC. "Returning New Glenn to flight remains our top priority — it's worth it."

The January timeframe sits roughly 10 days past the date Blue Origin had previously aimed for, according to CNBC. Limp said the company has come a long way in its recovery effort but that "rebuilding at this pace hasn't been easy." He added: "In just six months, Team Blue has accomplished what many thought would take years."

A New Glenn explosion during a hot-fire test in May damaged the company's Florida launch pad, according to CNBC. Blue Origin was due to put 48 of Amazon $AMZN's Leo space-based internet satellites into orbit only days afterward. Bezos said last month that he expected New Glenn to be back in the air in December.

Amazon counts on Blue Origin to handle some of its Leo launches, and the setbacks have held up its work to build out the constellation, according to CNBC. Amazon has shifted to alternative providers, among them United Launch Alliance and Arianespace.

Blue Origin has announced a $330 million expansion of its Florida facilities, which includes building up to 150 New Glenn upper stages a year. The project is scheduled for completion in summer 2027. The company also plans two additional pads for launching and landing New Glenn.

Limp has said the pad repair effort employs 1,000 workers split across day and night shifts. Blue Origin competes with SpaceX for private-sector and government customers, and NASA has hired Blue Origin for several Artemis contracts, including a lunar lander, according to CNBC.
