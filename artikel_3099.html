The Best Gifts for Every Tech Nerd in Your Life

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

This series will help you pick the perfect presents, decor, and activities to make this Christmas the Best Christmas Ever.

Our grandparents could drop some treats in a sock and call it Christmas, secure in the knowledge that their children would be delighted by an orange. These days, though, everyone from your tech-obsessed granny to your cutting-edge teen wants to be impressed. Luckily, there's a mind-blowing high-tech gadget out there for everyone.

I don't know anyone who wouldn't appreciate finding these display glasses in their Christmas stocking. The Xbx a01s are sunglasses you can plug into any device with a USB-C port to create your own personal, private monitor. The resulting picture is the equivalent of a 142" TV. The refresh rate is a crisp 120Hz, and the glasses support HDR10 and real-time SDR-to-HDR upscaling and billions of colors, all in a package that weighs only 62 grams. In other words: It's an HD TV you can carry in your pocket and wear on your face. The $299 list price won't break your wallet either. They're the best smart glasses for most people. Check out my full review for more details.

It can be hard to buy a gift for a health-focused person. They usually don't want booze and they like putting in effort. But the Whoop Peak 5 fitness tracker will brighten the Christmas morning of the most diehard athlete. It's more versatile than a watch (you can wear it on your bicep and even in your underwear) and it monitors heart rate, respiratory rate, heart rate variability, skin temperature, blood oxygen levels, daily activity, and sleep—basically everything I don't worry about. Check out Lifehacker's senior health editor Beth Skwarecki's in-depth look at the Whoop Peak 5 for more details.

You cannot go wrong with a GoPro. The company's easy-to-use, hard-to-break cameras are synonymous with "action photography" and compact enough to fit in a stocking. If there's a snowboarder, skateboarder, kayaker, or BMX racer on your list, this is a must. Even if your giftee isn't an adventurer, the GoPro Hero 13 supports additional lenses and filters that let users go beyond the "gnarly fish-eye, dude" photography and videography of past generations. Check out associate tech editor Michelle Ehrhardt's GoPro Hero 13 review for more details.

Everyone wants their own droid. Beni from Mondo Robotics is a camera robot that takes 4K video while autonomously following its subject wherever they go. If you want to get action footage, there's no need to hassle with a tripod or another person: You can control Beni with the remote, or simply tell it to follow you. It can be set to track you from behind, from the side, or it can circle you, and you can adjust what it is doing from the app or remote on the fly, so there's no limit to the kind of elaborate shots it can pull off. Plus it's freakin' adorable. The $799 list price might be a bit steep for most gift-givers, but as far as how well it works and how much fun I've had using it, it's worth the money.

The R36 Max 2 retro handheld game console lets you take your gaming anywhere. It comes with over 20,000 games pre-installed, so you can play every title from classic consoles like the Atari 2600 or the PlayStation 1, as well as just about every arcade game anyone ever dropped a coin into, all for less than $100. I paid more than that in quarters just to beat Dragon's Lair!

I'm obsessed with the Yamaha Seqtrak. It crams a drum machine, sequencer, sampler, three separate synthesizers, and a speaker into a package small enough to put into a Christmas stocking. It's a complete mobile studio—everything you need to produce a song or beat—but it also integrates with your computer's DAW and other MIDI gear to become part of your home studio. All that for only $300!

The Flipper Zero is a must-have device for the hardcore, glasses-wearing, circuit-bender type of techie. Its ability to do evil deeds like hack ATMs is greatly exaggerated, but the Flipper is still a technological Swiss Army knife that lets you read and manipulate the invisible waves around you, from radio waves to RFID, NFC, Bluetooth, and wifi signals. It's educational, fascinating, and powerful; how you use that power is your business.

If you're giving a gift to a would-be Antonie van Leeuwenhoek, this miniature, wireless microscope will rock their holiday. It's easy to set up, works with PCs, Android, or iOS devices, magnifies things up to 1000x, and takes 1080p pictures of your discoveries.
