Laird Superfood: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Gross margin fell 7 points to 29.6% as acquired Terrasoul product lines and organic cacao costs weighed on profitability.

On its third quarter of 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 12, 2026, Laird Superfood (LSF -7.35%) announced net sales reached $42 million. The functional food maker is expanding its product footprint into major retail channels while working to turn acquired brands into a profitable scale platform. Three things management said stand out.

Management is choosing to step up brand spending to capture demand from consumers switching to powdered coffee and those using glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) weight-loss medications who seek nutrient-dense foods. To support this growth push, Laird Superfood is deploying top-of-funnel campaigns across online marketplaces and retail accounts.

Gross profit reached $12.4 million, but gross margin (the portion of revenue left after production costs) compressed by 7 percentage points to 29.6%. Commodity cost inflation and lower-margin product lines from Terrasoul drove the contraction, which management is countering through active cost containment.

E-commerce accounted for 54% of total net sales during the quarter, up from $20.5 million to $22.5 million as online marketplaces expanded. Laird Superfood is utilizing Terrasoul's fulfillment and advertising expertise to scale its broader brand portfolio across digital storefronts.

Net sales guidance for full-year 2026 stands between $135 million to $140 million as integration work continues across supply chain network facilities and enterprise resource planning (ERP) software systems. Platform scale gives the leadership team wider choice in strategic deal selection while maintaining pricing discipline across retail accounts.

Full-year adjusted EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, adjusted for non-recurring items) is expected to arrive between $5 million and $6 million, supported by an unlevered balance sheet holding $17.3 million in cash. Strategic capital backing allows management to execute its long-term strategy.
