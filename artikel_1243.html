Forget Williams-Barnett: Tottenham Have 2 Signings De Zerbi Must Prioritise

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Luca Williams-Barnett was the talk of Tottenham Hotspur supporters on Saturday, after his impressive full debut for the club.

The 18-year-old grabbed a goal and an assist on his full Premier League debut, becoming the second-youngest to do that in Premier League history after Michael Owen. He also became Spurs' youngest goalscorer in the Premier League, beating Gareth Bale's record.

Supporters have been clamouring for the teen star to be given a run in the team, and his breakthrough performance against Ipswich Town and Saturday may well see them get their wish.

Harry Kane, one of the greatest strikers in Premier League history, was the last player to come through the Tottenham Hotspur academy and become a fully-fledged star and Williams-Barnett could now follow in his footsteps.

And Spurs have rewarded the teenager for his work by offering him a lucrative new contract, which will ward off any potential suitors in the transfer window too.

Williams-Barnett's current deal with Spurs, which he signed last year when turning 17, only runs until 2028, but Tottenham have moved quickly to extend that, with reports suggesting he is set to put pen-to-paper on that extension in the coming days.

It will be a huge signing for the club, as they look to safeguard the future of one of their biggest stars, but Williams-Barnett is not the only one who is in need of a contract extension at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium. There are two other players whose contracts expire in 2028, who are arguably just as important to tie down as the teenager. And they are both players in attacking positions too, which is where Spurs have really struggled under Roberto De Zerbi so far this season.

Spurs have only scored 12 goals in their 11 Premier League games so far in 2026/27, with only three teams in the Premier League table scoring fewer. They have lacked a certain amount of creativity with key players out injured, but a number of them are now slowly getting back in action and working their way back to full fitness.

James Maddison and Dejan Kulusevski are key creative outlets for Spurs and both see their current deals expire in north London in 2028.

Kulusevski has hardly played since injuring his knee in May 2025 but is now back as part of the first-team squad. Given his time on the treatment table it may not be a huge pay rise that he gets when he is offered a new contract, but Spurs have stood by him and there is no doubt that when fully fit he can be a matchwinner, and offering him new terms is imperative.

Maddison on the other hand is a firm fans' favourite, who, like Kulusevski, has had his injury problems. The Englishman is now back playing regularly for the first team, however, and has a huge role to play, not just on the pitch but off it too with his leadership.

He will be 31 by the time his contract actually ends, but offering him a couple of years' extension is definitely worthwhile given the impact he can have on the dressing room.
