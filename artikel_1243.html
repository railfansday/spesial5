What Bruno Fernandes’ new Manchester United contract means for Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Manchester United have triggered a one-year extension on Bruno Fernandes’ contract, ending Milan’s hopes of signing him on a free transfer next summer.

According to The Athletic, the club captain is now tied to Old Trafford until June 2028. Fernandes had been entering the final year of his deal and recently confirmed that talks over a new contract had been put on hold, with both sides wanting to focus on improving results.

By exercising the option, United have stopped the 32-year-old from negotiating with foreign clubs from 1 January, when he would otherwise have been free to agree a pre-contract.

That doesn’t rule out a move next year, but any club wanting Fernandes will now have to pay a fee. Milan are among the European clubs monitoring him, along with interest from Saudi Arabia, and United have already identified possible replacements.

The Portuguese midfielder’s future had looked uncertain after Michael Carrick dropped him for a 3-0 defeat to Bournemouth. That followed his public criticism of the team’s tactics after a 4-1 Champions League loss to Como.

Since then, however, Fernandes has kept the captaincy under new coach Mauricio Pochettino and scored twice in Wednesday’s 2-2 draw at Sporting CP.
