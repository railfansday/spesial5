GTA 6 almost banned in Korea after government officials got to play it early

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Grand Theft Auto 6 was almost denied a rating in South Korea, as officials asked to play the game early and see some parts of the map.

With November 19 getting closer and closer, Grand Theft Auto fans from around the world will soon finally get to play GTA 6. However, if you’re in South Korea, that opportunity was almost taken away from players.

As per a report from Inven Global, Rockstar Games’ newest title “came close” to not receiving a rating classification from officials. According to the report, an initial review of the game ended in a three-three between giving GTA 6 an ‘adults only’ rating and not rating it at all.

This review took place in early September, with requests being made to Rockstar so that officials could see parts of the game early and then vote again.

Initially, there were concerns about sexual scenes being out of context and “far too much genital exposure,” rather than the violence or crime side of GTA.

On top of that, security concerns about leaks meant that a playable build was not available to officials until the end of September, hence a delay in handing out a rating to the game.

As per Inven, officials also noted that the map is “so vast” that they had to make multiple requests to Rockstar to point out specific areas around any exposed characters. However, they found that the player “could not engage in any sexual interaction.”

Upon a later review, one member of the committee noted that: “It appears that they intentionally covered genital exposure in explicitly sexual scenes, while allowing exposure in background settings.”

Officials added that it would be a “massive controversy” if they refused to rate the game – which would effectively ban it from being released in Korea – as they’d be the only country to do so.
