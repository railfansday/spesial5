GTA 7 could finally introduce a completely new city as Rockstar opens door to leaving America

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Rockstar Games has opened the door to taking Grand Theft Auto to a completely new city or even potentially leaving the United States for GTA 7 rather than revisiting familiar locations.

With GTA 6 launching on November 19, players are preparing to return to Vice City, Rockstar’s fictional version of Miami, alongside the wider state of Leonida.

However, the franchise has spent decades revisiting the same American cities, including Liberty City, Los Santos, and Vice City. That has left fans wondering whether a future installment could finally break the cycle.

Now, one of Rockstar’s top developers has suggested that an international setting isn’t entirely off the table.

Speaking to The Guardian, Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson was asked whether the company could ever envision a GTA game set outside the United States.

“Never say never, but there’s still so much to do in America,” Nelson responded.

While Nelson didn’t specifically mention GTA 7 or confirm plans to abandon American cities, his comments leave the possibility open for future installments. Even if the series continues taking place in the United States, it also sounds like we could finally see Grand Theft Auto tackle a fresh location.

Interestingly, Rockstar has already explored taking GTA overseas.

As Dexerto previously reported, former Rockstar North technical director Obbe Vermeij revealed that GTA: Tokyo was once seriously considered, with a Japanese developer potentially taking charge of the project.

Other international locations discussed included Rio de Janeiro, Moscow, Istanbul, London, and Paris. However, none of those concepts progressed into a completed game.

Vermeij was considerably less optimistic about the franchise leaving America when he discussed GTA’s future in January 2026.

He argued that Rockstar was effectively stuck revisiting American cities because their familiarity and cultural influence made them safer choices for games with enormous budgets.

Nelson’s latest comments suggest the company hasn’t completely closed the door on exploring somewhere different.

For now, Rockstar’s focus remains firmly on GTA 6, which has taken more than a decade to develop and features the series’ most detailed recreation of America yet.

And while a former Rockstar developer believes GTA 7 could already be in development, the studio has yet to officially announce the next installment or reveal where it might take place.
