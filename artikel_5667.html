Nominations Now Open for RISMedia’s 2nd Annual Tech Showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

After a successful inaugural competition, RISMedia is bringing back its 2nd Annual Tech Showdown to recognize emerging technology companies developing innovative solutions that could reshape the real estate industry.

Open to companies launched within the past one to three years, the competition seeks technologies designed to help brokers and agents improve efficiency, streamline operations and drive business growth.

Selected finalists will compete in a live, Shark Tank-style pitch battle before a panel of industry experts, showcasing their solutions and their potential impact on the industry.

The winning company will receive a promotional package valued at more than $80,000, including a dedicated RISMedia editorial feature and Product Showcase Webinar.
