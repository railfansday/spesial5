Geno Smith had a full practice Friday, has no injury designation

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Jets quarterback Geno Smith (ankle) was a full participant at Friday's practice and has no injury designation.

Smith will start against the Chargers on Sunday.

"He looks solid," Jets coach Aaron Glenn said before practice, via Amanda Vogt of the team website. "It's very encouraging."

The Jets ruled out wide receiver Omar Cooper Jr. (ankle/hip), wide receiver Isaiah Williams (ribs) and defensive lineman David Onyemata (abdomen). Onyemata is in his 21-day return-to-practice window.

Edge rusher Joseph Ossai (foot/ankle), offensive lineman Dylan Parham (knee), defensive lineman Harrison Phillips (ankle) and linebacker Trevin Wallace (groin) are questionable. Phillips was the only one of those four players who had a limited practice.

"We have two days to see how these guys operate, but it's looking promising for all these guys," Glenn said. "I feel good about that."
