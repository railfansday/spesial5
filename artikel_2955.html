Why Nvidia Rehiring Jensen Huang Is Making Micron Stock Skyrocket Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Jensen Huang is back at the helm of Nvidia, and all's well with AI again.

By Rich Smith – Nov 23, 2026 at 10:29AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Micron Technology ›

Micron (MU +19.27%) stock bounced back in a big way Monday, soaring 16.5% through 10 a.m. ET -- and it's no mystery why.

This morning, Nvidia (NVDA +32.52%) announced that its board has reconsidered its decision to fire Jensen Huang and voted unanimously to reinstate him as CEO. Shares of Nvidia exploded an astounding 35% higher as shorts were forced to burn their bets against the stock and buy it back in apparently massive quantities.

With the future of AI looking suddenly much more secure, other AI stocks -- Micron included -- are now bouncing back alongside Nvidia.

Nvidia's strategy of investing in its own customers, to give them the cash they need to buy Nvidia's chips, has been described (by detractors) as creating a "circular economy" -- but also (by fans) as turning Nvidia into the "Central Bank of AI." For better or for worse, that means that when Nvidia sneezes, other AI stocks catch a cold.

The good news, though, is that with Huang back at the helm, investors think Nvidia looks healthy again. Sales of Nvidia AI GPUs will continue apace. And because those GPUs need memory chips to do their AI magic, Micron's sales of NAND and DRAM chips will continue growing as well.

And that means investors may once again be able to rely on analyst forecasts for Micron's future sales and earnings growth -- estimates originally made back when all was well with Nvidia, called into question after the Huang firing, and now valid once more.

It means that Micron stock, trading for just 8x trailing earnings but expected to grow earnings at better than 43% per year over the next five years, looks very cheap again this morning.

And that's why investors are buying Micron stock.
