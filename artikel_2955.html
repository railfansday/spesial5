Robinhood Markets vs. Toast: Which Financial Stock Is a Better Buy for 2027?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Robinhood's revenue growth outpaced Toast's by a wide margin, yet Toast carries no debt and trades at a far lower sales multiple.

By Brendan Coffey – Updated Nov 25, 2026 at 11:01AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Robinhood Markets ›

Investors choosing between Robinhood Markets (HOOD -5.82%) and Toast Inc. (TOST -2.79%) must weigh high-growth retail finance against specialized restaurant technology. Both companies are disrupting traditional service models to capture massive digital markets.

Robinhood changed how individual investors access the stock market, while Toast provides the digital backbone for modern eateries. Both businesses benefit from the shift toward digital-first financial interactions. This comparison examines their growth trajectories and valuations to help you decide which stock fits your 2027 goals.

Robinhood Markets provides a global platform for retail brokerage, cryptocurrency, and digital banking. Its platforms allow 28.7 million funded customers to trade equities and options. Key partners include SIG for derivatives trading and Morningstar Inc. (MORN +0.75%) for investment research. As one of the more prominent financial stocks, the company is actively diversifying its revenue streams through acquisitions like Bitstamp. It also collaborates with liquidity providers such as Virtu Financial inc. (VIRT -2.82%) for trading execution. These strategic relationships help the firm provide a seamless experience for its growing user base.

In FY 2025, revenue reached nearly $4.5 billion, marking a significant growth rate of approximately 51.6% compared to the prior year. This follows a strong performance in FY 2024 where revenue was close to $3.0 billion. The company reported net income in 2025 of roughly $1.9 billion. This resulted in a net margin of around 42%, which measures the portion of revenue remaining as profit after all expenses. The upward trend in profitability highlights the company's focus on scaling its operations efficiently while adding new products like futures trading.

As of its December 2025 balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio is approximately 1.3x. This ratio shows how much debt a company uses to finance its assets relative to shareholder equity. The current ratio, which measures the ability to pay short-term debts, is also roughly 1.3x. Free cash flow, which is cash flow from operations minus capital expenditures, reached nearly $1.6 billion. This liquidity provides the firm with flexibility for future acquisitions or further research and development initiatives into automated advisory services.

Toast sells a specialized technology platform for the restaurant and retail sectors, combining software, payment processing, and hardware. The company serves approximately 189,000 locations, ranging from small cafes to large enterprise chains. It also provides payroll processing and working capital loans through strategic bank partnerships. Many tech stocks seek to dominate a single niche, and Toast has achieved this within the restaurant community. By integrating all aspects of a restaurant's digital needs, the company creates a high level of customer loyalty. This integrated approach makes it difficult for competitors to displace its services.

In FY 2025, revenue reached close to $6.2 billion, representing a year-over-year increase of approximately 24%. This is an increase from the almost $5.0 billion in revenue generated during FY 2024. The company reported a net income of roughly $342.0 million for the period. This produced a net margin of about 5.6%, which reflects the percentage of revenue kept as profit after accounting for all costs. This positive net income marks a significant improvement over the net loss reported in FY 2023, showing the business model is reaching a profitable scale.

As of its December 2025 balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio is 0.0x, indicating the company carries no debt. Its current ratio is approximately 2.8x, suggesting a strong ability to cover near-term obligations with liquid assets. Free cash flow reached about $608 million for the year. Note that stock-based compensation (SBC) represented roughly 36.6% of operating cash flow, which inflates reported cash generation since SBC is a non-cash expense added back in the cash flow statement. Even so, the lack of debt provides a clean financial profile for continued geographic expansion.

Robinhood Markets faces intense regulatory and litigation risks from agencies like the SEC and FINRA. Changes to rules regarding payment for order flow or the classification of cryptocurrencies could disrupt its core business model. Additionally, the company is highly sensitive to market volatility and interest rate changes. It also relies on third-party providers like Amazon.com Inc. (AMZN -5.75%) for cloud infrastructure. Cybersecurity vulnerabilities at these third-party vendors pose significant operational and reputational risks if customer data is compromised.

Toast is currently facing a lawsuit from the Federal Trade Commission alleging deceptive pricing in customer contracts. The business also depends heavily on a limited number of payment processors and a single bank partner for its lending programs. Furthermore, the company faces supply chain vulnerabilities for its hardware components from international manufacturers. Managing rapid growth has also placed strain on its internal accounting and IT systems. Failure to integrate acquired technologies effectively could lead to operational mistakes and a loss of customer satisfaction.

Toast appears to be the more conservatively priced option because its Forward P/E, based on future earnings estimates, and its P/S ratio, which measures market value against sales over the past twelve months, are lower than those for Robinhood.

Robinhood introduced no-cost stock trading to the masses when it debuted last decade, raking in retail clients and sparking a move industrywide to mostly $0 commission trading for equities. The key to its strength is a simple, intuitive app that makes trading accessible, avoiding the complexity of trading platforms designed for professionals. That has the average age of a customer in the mid-30s, well below other brokerages.

Its appeal to traders resulted in a record third quarter of fiscal 2026, with Robinhood posting its highest-ever revenue of $1.42 billion, up 12% from the year-ago period, and net income of $468 million, down $98 million from 2025. The company sees agentic trading, which only about 1% of customers utilize now, as a way to generate short-term growth, and the expected massive wealth transfer from the Baby Boom generation to younger adults as a long-term tailwind that should help establish its wealth management offerings.

For fiscal 2027, analysts foresee revenue of $6.88 billion, a rise of about 29% from 2026's expected final tally of $5.32 billion, with net income of around $2.7 billion, up 49%.

Toast, meanwhile, continues to add thousands of restaurants each quarter as that industry seeks a single platform to handle a myriad of tasks, like employee scheduling, point-of-sale transactions, tip automation, back-office needs, loyalty programs, handling takeout orders, and personalized marketing to customers.

All that should bring in 2027 revenue of $8.89 billion, which would be a rise of about 19% from 2026's expected full-year sales of $7.49 billion. Net income next year should touch $732 million, up about $140 million from 2026.

Longer term, Toast management sees opportunities to expand into gas station convenience stores, since most are small businesses, and into sports and entertainment facilities.

So which stock is the better buy for 2027? Robinhood trades at a premium, and while revenue forecasts are good, a bearish stock market always reduces retail traders' interest in the market and trading volumes. That is a potential risk. Toast, meanwhile, has a formula that is driving fast growth in the highly fragmented restaurant industry, from high-end eateries to takeout breakfast joints. At a cheaper forward PP/E and P/S than Robinhood, it's likely the more profitable position to build.
