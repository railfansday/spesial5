PepsiCo and LIV Golf Vet Adam Harter Takes His Shot on 3-on-3 at BIG3 League

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Adam Harter is among the most consequential CMOs around when it comes to sports sponsorships.

Last week, BIG3—the 3-on-3 basketball league founded by rapper Ice Cube and entertainment executive Jeff Kwatinetz nearly a decade ago—announced that Harter had signed on as president of business operations. His marketing-based remit includes growing the fanbase, expanding live events, and inking global brand partnerships.

The high-profile hire isn’t just significant for where Harter’s headed, but where he’s come from.

Harter was previously CMO of LIV Golf, the short-lived league that’s currently in Chapter 11 and headed into liquidation. Launched as a challenger to the PGA and backed by the Saudi Public Investment Fund, LIV Golf boasted Greg Norman as its founder and dangled nine-figure salaries to get star golfers to defect.

Robert Klara is the senior editor of brands at Adweek, specializing in the evolution and impact of brands.
