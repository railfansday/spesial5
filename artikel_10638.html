Free pigeon GTA game lets you terrorize Toronto on a massive map bigger than GTA 5

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

A developer has created a free web browser game where you control a robotic pigeon, causing chaos around an open world that’s nine times bigger than GTA 5.

Web browser games come in all shapes and sizes, from Poke Derby that recreates Umamusume-style races with your favorite monsters to those that let you destroy any website with a gun-wielding stick figure.

The latest addition to catch our attention is COO, a “bird GTA simulator” that lets you fly around a massive comic book-style Toronto that features first-person flying, car hijacking, and plenty of pedestrians to mess around with.

According to creator Yegor Sak, COO covers 630 square kilometers (roughly 243 square miles), which the dev says is around nine times the size of GTA 5’s map. Sak says players can take on missions or ignore their assignments and freely explore, with the ability to fly anywhere and walk every street.

The game also includes 438,819 real buildings, with heights taken from municipal 3D data, alongside around 5,700 kilometers of streets. Coo’s version of Toronto also includes traffic, pedestrians, boats, flocks of birds, changing weather, and a day-and-night cycle. COO even uses real-life flight data to show planes that are flying over the city.

I built a full 3D model of Toronto drawn as a living comic book, in bold ink lines, pastel cel shading and halftone dots, and turned it into bird GTA simulator (pun intended). You’ll also be able to learn about Toronto, with facts being delivered as you fly through various locations like parks, stations, squares, and major landmarks. Sak says the project was built in seven days using AI, but is currently asking Toronto residents to help assess how closely the sprawling recreation resembles their city.

“If you live in Toronto, fly to your neighborhood (Fast Deploy via map), and I’d love your feedback so I can improve realism. Given the massive scope of the map, I didn’t experience 99% of places in the game personally,” wrote the developer via X.
