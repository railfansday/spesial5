Delivery driver fired after homeowner catches him throwing package at cat

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

A delivery driver caught on camera throwing a package at a family’s pet cat has been permanently removed from the GOFO delivery network following an investigation into the viral incident.

The incident occurred on October 25 in Virginia Beach, Virginia, when a driver was filmed throwing a package at a 21-pound black cat named Opie while he was relaxing on his owner’s front porch.

As Dexerto previously reported, the footage sparked widespread backlash, with the driver eventually returning to the home to apologize after the video went viral.

“Cats make me uneasy,” he said through a translation app. “I came to apologize to the person. To show my face, because I’m telling you, I’m not someone who likes trouble, let alone cats.”

Now, delivery company GOFO has confirmed to Dexerto that the driver will no longer be permitted to make deliveries through its network.

In a statement provided to Dexerto, GOFO confirmed that an investigation carried out alongside its independent Delivery Service Partner (DSP) resulted in the driver’s permanent removal.

“Following an investigation conducted in coordination with the responsible Delivery Service Partner (DSP), the driver involved has been permanently removed from the GOFO delivery network,” the company said.

GOFO clarified that the driver was employed by an independent contractor rather than directly by the company.

The DSP has also received a formal warning and must introduce additional training on handling encounters with animals, alongside increased monitoring of driver behavior.

GOFO representatives and the delivery partner also visited Opie’s owner, Isabell Sump, in person to apologize and explain the measures taken. Photos shared by GOFO show they also provided Opie with a haul of gifts.

“The conversation was constructive, and the pet owner expressed understanding of GOFO’s efforts to address the incident,” the company explained.

“GOFO strongly condemns any form of animal mistreatment. The conduct shown in the video is unacceptable and does not reflect the standards we expect from those operating within our delivery network.”

The update comes after the driver previously returned to apologize, explaining through a translation app that cats made him uncomfortable.

Sump had questioned why the driver didn’t simply leave the package elsewhere rather than throwing it at her pet.

GOFO says it will continue monitoring its delivery partners to ensure the new safety measures are implemented and prevent similar incidents from happening again.
