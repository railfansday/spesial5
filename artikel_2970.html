Prediction: This Will Be Broadcom’s Stock Price by the End of 2028

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Broadcom has underperformed the semiconductor sector over the past year, but that could change as its growth accelerates.

The past year has been a forgettable one for Broadcom (AVGO +1.55%) investors, as the stock has significantly underperformed the broader semiconductor sector, posting paltry gains of 1%.

The PHLX Semiconductor Sector index, meanwhile, has shot up by 48% over the past year despite experiencing volatility in recent months. However, a closer look at Broadcom's financial performance makes it clear that the market has been unnecessarily harsh on the stock.

In fact, I won't be surprised to see Broadcom stock stepping on the gas and delivering terrific gains to investors over the next couple of years. Let's look at the reasons why.

Broadcom designs application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs) and networking components. These chips are in terrific demand from hyperscalers and AI companies, which explains why Broadcom has built a solid client base that includes Anthropic, OpenAI, Alphabet's Google, and Meta Platforms.

In all, Broadcom has six customers for its custom AI processors and networking chips, and they are poised to ramp up the deployment of its products. This explains why the company is on track to deliver significant acceleration in AI revenue over the next couple of fiscal years.

Broadcom's AI semiconductor revenue will nearly triple in fiscal 2026 (which ended earlier this month) to $58 billion. Even better, the company expects its AI revenue to double in each of the next two fiscal years, reaching $115 billion in fiscal 2027 and $230 billion in fiscal 2028. However, Broadcom's AI revenue growth could exceed expectations.

That's because the demand for optical networking components is poised to jump exponentially between 2026 and 2028. Goldman Sachs estimates that the optical component space could generate $154 billion in revenue in 2028, up from just $15 billion this year. Broadcom designs optical digital signal processors (DSPs), electro-absorption modulated (EML) lasers, and other components to enable optical networking in data centers.

What's more, Broadcom management noted on the September earnings call that the demand for its AI solutions exceeds its outlook. The company is focused on securing additional supply to meet growing demand, which I think gives it the potential to exceed its forecasts over the next couple of years.

Broadcom's underperformance this year and its exceptional growth explain why the stock can be bought at an incredibly cheap 18 times forward earnings. That's lower than the tech-laden Nasdaq-100 index's forward earnings multiple of almost 21.

Analysts are projecting a 71% increase in Broadcom's earnings per share (EPS) in fiscal 2026 to $11.67. Looking ahead, Broadcom's earnings are poised for a big lift-off over the next couple of fiscal years.

It is worth noting that the fiscal 2028 EPS estimate is almost in line with Broadcom's guidance. That implies potential gains of 124%, suggesting investors can buy a top AI stock at an attractive valuation right now.
