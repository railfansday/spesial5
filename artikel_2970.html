WATCH: Stefon Diggs has 32-yard game-winning TD on TNF vs Giants

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Washington Commanders got the ball back with 1:46 left on the clock and down by four points. After a shanked punt by the New York Giants and a 32-yard pass interference penalty, Washington was in Giants territory. It also took three plays after that for the Commanders to score.

On third and four, Washington desperately needed a first down to keep the chains moving. Instead, Commanders receiver Stefon Diggs broke a tackle and took it 32 yards for a touchdown to give Washington a 24-21 lead with under a minute left in the game.

Here is a look at Diggs' touchdown, courtesy of the NFL's official social media:

That was Diggs' longest touchdown since he had a 55-yard score with the Buffalo Bills back in 2023. It was also his sixth touchdown of the season and his fifth from quarterback Jayden Daniels. Daniels now has three passing touchdowns in this game; it is first game with at least three touchdowns this year.

It ended up being the game-winning touchdown after Washington's defense forced an interception from Giants quarterback J.J. McCarthy on New York's final drive of the game.
