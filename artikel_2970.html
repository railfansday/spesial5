Prediction market odds for Tesla-SpaceX merger by April 2027 top 60%

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Odds of a SpaceX and Tesla merger before March 2027 have climbed to 62% from 27% on Kalshi since Thursday.

Chances that Elon Musk's two companies, SpaceX, a satellite and rocket maker, and Tesla, an electric vehicle manufacturer, will soon integrate have jumped since late last week across multiple Kalshi markets.

The contract asks, "When will Tesla and SpaceX merge?" and would be verified by news outlets and the Securities and Exchange Commission's EDGAR database.

On Friday, Walter Isaacson, a CNBC contributor and author of a Musk biography, told CNBC's "Squawk Box" he's confident a merger is coming. He cited Terafab, the semiconductor project involving Tesla, SpaceX and xAI, as a catalyst.

"I think you have to have a merger. Makes no sense otherwise," Isaacson said.

Tesla's 2026 annual shareholder meeting, set for Dec. 3, may provide more insight into a potential linkup, Isaacson said.

While speculators are anticipating an announcement of a merger this year, a research note from Jefferies last week said a deal will likely face backlash after the Democrats won control of Congress and several key governorships in the midterm elections earlier this month.

"We understand there is higher risk of scrutiny post elections, but the vision of a combined SpaceX and Tesla as proxies for longer-term U.S. national strategies of independence and leadership in space, energy, manufacturing or semiconductors, in our view, remain highly relevant regardless of which party controls Congress," wrote Jefferies analyst Philippe Houchois.

Texas Attorney General-elect Nathan Johnson, a Democrat, said a possible merger between Musk's two companies would face "tremendous scrutiny," Bloomberg reported Thursday.
