The GTA 6 Police Don’t Have Equal Response Times For All Crimes

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

As with all Grand Theft Auto games, in GTA 6, the police will come after you if you commit a crime with witnesses. However, their response time will not always be the same, and you may be able to use this to your advantage, depending on the situation.

As we learned during our time with Grand Theft Auto 6 so far, the police will respond more slowly if you are assaulting a gang hideout. Presumably, this is because the police force believes you're doing them a favor, but regardless, they will still show up if you get into an all-out firefight and make a bunch of noise.

We also found that, for this reason, using silenced weapons is extremely helpful, as the police will only be called if you are spotted or heard committing a crime. Of course, this is GTA, so you're still naturally going to screw up at some point and have a massive wave of police cars after you, but it doesn't need to happen all the time.
