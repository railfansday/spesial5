Jordon Hudson Files Lawsuit Over ‘Egregious’ Video Amid Bill Belichick Split

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Jordon Hudson filed a lawsuit claiming her rights were “egregiously” violated over the leak of a video from an Airbnb she shared with her ex-boyfriend Bill Belichick.

The lawsuit, obtained by Us on Monday, November 9, stems from a Ring video taken in August 2023 at a rental property in Winthrop, Massachusetts, where Hudson, 25, was staying with Belichick, 74.

On the final day of the stay, Hudson claimed she was sent video footage from the property’s owners showing a shirtless Belichick outside of the house. The owner allegedly sent the clip via text message with a warning about parking rules.

“We didn’t [sic] find the video hilarious,” Hudson alleged one of the messages read. “As we discussed, I’m a huge cowboys fan, but don’t live under a rock! Wishing you the best! Please don’t forget to leave the parking pass.”

At the time the video was taken, Belichick was still head coach of the New England Patriots.

Hudson allegedly responded, “To be honest — I don’t quite understand your message,” Regardless, I hear you…No parking…”

“It’s clear who is on the video…I’ll leave it at that,” the owners countered.

Hudson expressed concern that the video would be leaked, stressing that it should “be deleted.”

Hudson and Belichick did not confirm their relationship until October 2024.

The owners eventually responded, “We will delete it from ring, but as you know, unfortunately, videos are never totally deleted.”

Months after the Airbnb stay, the shirtless footage of Belichick was leaked on social media and went “immediately viral,” Hudson claimed in the lawsuit.

“As a result of this leak, numerous false tabloid-like and click-bait stories have, and continue to, be published about her, which have held her up to intense ridicule and derision and caused immense damage,” the lawsuit alleged.

After the property owners initially denied they were responsible for leaking the footage, Hudson allegedly received an “out of blue” message from them in May 2025, in which they admitted to sharing the video with “a few people wondering is this him [Mr. Belichick], isn’t it him and I don’t know what happened from there, but we did delete it from the camera, as I assured you.”

“We were never happy that it was released and never commented that it was our home on any social media channels,” the owners allegedly added. “You guys should come back lol!”

“Now that you destroyed our livelihood, we are weighing our options about whom we will talk to and what we will discuss with them,” one of the texts allegedly said.

Another text allegedly read, “You’re not even famous now! You are infamous. Good for you!’”

When reached by the California Post on Sunday, November 8, the property owners said they had “no comment” about the lawsuit.

Hudson and Belichick broke off their relationship “late last year,” TMZ reported on October 30.

When asked about the split rumors during a press conference on November 2, Belichick said, “I’ll keep my personal comments and relationships to myself, if that’s all right with you.”
