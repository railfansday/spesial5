Death Penalty Sought for Man Accused of Killing Girl, 18, After Meeting Online

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Prosecutors are seeking the death penalty for a man charged in the murder of 18-year-old Yessenia Norman, who was found stabbed more than two dozen times in his Arizona home.

Norman’s body was discovered in Randal Basilio Santillan’s Tolleson apartment on January 28, nearly two weeks after she left a group home to meet a man believed to be Basilio Santillan and vanished, according to authorities.

The Maricopa County Attorney’s Office announced in a Tuesday, November 17, news release that prosecutors filed a notice of intent to seek the death penalty against Basilio Santillan, 28, following his February indictment in connection with Norman’s killing.

A grand jury indicted Basilio Santillan on charges of first-degree murder and kidnapping.

Investigators believe Basilio Santillan and Norman met on an online dating site before meeting in person on January 15, when Norman went missing, per NBC affiliate KPNX-TV.

Then, on January 28, officers were called to a welfare check at an apartment where Basilio Santillan and his wife lived after someone noticed flies by their door and an awful smell, according to police.

Inside the apartment, police found Norman dead, with blankets covering her body, in addition to maggots on the floor, per court documents obtained by the TV station.

Police learned Norman had been reporting missing and ultimately discovered surveillance footage and cellphone data linking Basilio Santillan to her disappearance, according to the county attorney’s office.

Authorities located and arrested Basilio Santillan in Mississippi before his extradition to Maricopa County, prosecutors said.

During the investigation, detectives found Basilio Santillan had child sexual abuse material on his phone, according to prosecutors.

He was separately indicted on 10 counts of sexual exploitation of a minor and dangerous crime against children in September in connection with the discovery, the attorney’s office said.

Court records indicate Basilio Santillan pleaded not guilty in the child sexual abuse material case.

It wasn’t immediately clear whether he entered a plea in the murder case.

Basilio Santillan is in custody on a $1 million cash bond in connection with Norman’s murder, as well as a $100,000 secured bond in his other case, according to prosecutors.

Norman was remembered as a “beautiful and bright” young woman “of deep compassion and strength for everyone she knew” in a GoFundMe created to support her mother, Jessica Nuñez.

“She was a very talented young woman who had a very bright future waiting for her,” the page read. “She recently signed up for community college and was very eager to start her adult life.”
