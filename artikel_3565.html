Tiffani Thiessen, 52, Gives Candid Update on Future Hip Replacement Surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Saved By the Bell alum Tiffani Thiessen is offering fans a candid update on her upcoming hip replacement surgery.

“As of right now, I am not getting it because of just the timing of travel, and I start season 2 very soon, so I’m looking at doing it next year after I finish season 2 of my show,” Thiessen, 52, said while speaking to Woman’s World in an interview published on Wednesday, November 25, referring to her Disney+ show Coven Academy.

“So right now, it’s kind of steroid shots to kind of help me through — which, my last one I just got last week helped tremendously,” she continued. “I even played pickleball for four hours and didn’t feel a thing, so we’re good.”

She added of her future surgery, “It is definitely something in my very near future that I need to get done. And that’s okay. It’s part of it, right? Part of getting old and being active.”

The actress revealed on a November 10 episode Tom Papa’s eponymous podcast that she needs to undergo a surgical hip replacement after learning she had “hip dysplasia” while seeking a second medical opinion.

“I have to get a hip replacement — I do,” she revealed at the time. “I was a dancer when I was younger, which was a good thing, [and] I have a torn labrum, that’s one issue I have. Then, I found out, going to my second opinion, … I have hip dysplasia.”

Hip dysplasia, according to the Cleveland Clinic, occurs when bones in the hip joint no longer properly fit together.

“So, when I was very flexible as a young child has now caused my issue now,” she continued. “I hear the hip replacements aren’t as bad as knee and shoulders [replacement procedures].”

According to Theissen, she was in so much pain at the time that she was unable to carry out certain tasks, including a friendly game of pickleball — an issue that, according to the actress, have since been remedied thanks to the shots she’s receiving until she can go under the knife.

“We play [as a family],” the actress, who shares two kids with husband Brady Smith, added at the time. “My doctor didn’t say it was pickleball, but it probably is not help[ful]. I have not been able to play in the last couple weeks ‘cause my hip’s been so bad.”

On Wednesday, Thiessen opened up about the pain she was feeling prior to her diagnosis and discovering her health issue would require surgery.

“I was having a lot of pain in my hip, and I was… my mobility had changed on the one side of my left hip,” she explained. “When you’re born with really shallow hips, being very athletic and like a dancer and a runner and all those things that I was, I have just worn down my hips so much that because I don’t have a ton of coverage, it just makes it 10 times worse.”

She added, “So, that’s what’s caused a lot of the pain that I’ve been in. That’s what a new hip’s gonna give me, new life again, so we’ll get there. I’m gonna thrive next year. Who knows, maybe I’ll do another marathon.”
