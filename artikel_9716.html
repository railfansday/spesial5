Matters.AI Lands India’s Largest Cybersecurity Pre-Series A Round to Fight AI Data Risks

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

News, news analysis, and commentary on the latest trends in cybersecurity technology.

The security startup consolidates four legacy security tools into an autonomous platform that uses context-aware AI to trace sensitive data lineage, analyze user intent, and automatically remediate threats across enterprise environments.

As companies rapidly adopt AI, autonomous AI agents are increasingly accessing, copying, and moving sensitive business data using standard employee permissions. Traditional security tools show defenders where the organization's data is stored, but cannot differentiate between a human or agent accessing it, or what is happening to that data. Cybersecurity teams are forced to manually cobble together a view based on conflicting alerts from multiple tools.This is the problem Indian security startup Matters.AI is tacking with its data detection and response platform. Security teams can use Matters.AI's platform to gain visibility over cloud and on-premises systems, SaaS applications, endpoints, and browsers and use artificial intelligence to investigate and stop threats. The company just announced a $12 million pre-series A round (₹115 crore), bringing its total funding to $18.25 million (₹175 crore). This funding round is India's largest cybersecurity pre-Series A funding round, the company said.

According to the company, Matters.AI combines four separate legacy security tools—data security posture management, activity monitoring, insider risk, and data loss prevention—into a single system to find and classify data; track movement of data across enterprise systems; and detect and fix issues. AI is used to find and organize the organization's sensitive data and then follow them as they get copied or shared. The platform combines data sensitivity and user access, and infers user intent to flag potential compliance risks and insider threats.

Matters.AI says the new funding will be used to drive research into automated investigations, endpoint intelligence, and safeguards against AI agents misusing data.

The company was co-founded by Keshava Murthy (CEO) and Harsh Sahu (CTO). Sahu was a founding machine learning team member at Observe.AI and brings AI/ML expertise to Matters.AI. This founding round was led by Tanglin Venture Partners, alongside IIFL Fintech Fund, Piper Serica's Bharat Tech Fund, Foster Ventures, and Tetration Ventures, with continued backing from Kalaari Capital and Endiya Partners. Endiya Partners has a deep information security portfolio, including threat intelligence company CloudSEK, Scrut Automation, ShieldSquare (acquired by Radware in 2019), and Shamrock AI. Kalaari Capital has backed NudgeBee and byteXL.
