Star Wars Galactic Racer will get local split-screen multiplayer in early 2027 with new 'Free Play' mode, finally letting racers yell at one another in a tangible space

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Split-screen multiplayer is often treated by developers as a forgotten vestige of a bygone age, something to be left behind as the standardisation of online play makes it redundant, especially following the pandemic. It's considered unlikely that you'll have another human being in your home to pick up a controller with you, and since you've inevitably got an internet connection, why not just use that to connect to anyone in the world? I find there's a unique beauty in playing something shoulder to shoulder on a couch with another person, and it seems Fuse Games sees that light too, with Star Wars: Galactic Racer set to be updated to add local splitscreen multiplayer sometime early next year.

This new mode, Free Play, not only allows for local multiplayer of up to four players (with either humans or CPUs racing together), but also for complete freedom of choice in vehicle, character and planet to race on. A simple versus mode like this is a far cry from the roguelite structure of the main game, and one that draws it closer to its arcade racing game roots. An embracing of that is always welcome, as Galactic Racer has been a reminder to many of just how wonderful the arcade racing genre can be, and how poorly served its been in the industry for a while. For example, while the Fuse Games team is partly made up of Criterion Games veterans, Criterion itself has recently been folded into being a Battlefield Studio by EA, making the future of Need for Speed and Burnout uncertain.

As for why this feature may not have been available at launch, other than the cynical view that it wasn't considered a moneymaking priority for Fuse Games and Secret Mode, CD Projekt Red senior technical producer Shayna Moon weighed in to theorise that it took some effort to get four separately-rendered views of the game running without making their machine explode like the Death Star. As Moon puts it: "To be overly simple about it, if the game is running at 30fps for each player, that means it’s actually rendering 120fps." Likely not an issue for a lot of PC players, but a potential issue for console players considering the game is built to run at a locked 60fps for one player on consoles. Past this upcoming update, it looks like the Star Wars franchise as a whole could be set for more smaller games ala Galactic Racer in general, per comments made by Lucasfilms' vice president.
