Sporting 2-2 Manchester United: A ridiculous Champions League clash finished all square after the visitors had two goals disallowed and conceded two penalties

Mauricio Pochettino was denied his first win as Manchester United manager against Sporting as his side saw a late winner disallowed by VAR in a bonkers Champions league clash in Lisbon.

Referee Szymon Marciniak was in the thick of the action throughout and in the 84th minute ruled that Patrick Dorgu had fouled Sporting goalkeeper Kaique Pereira, who let a corner from Bruno Fernandes slip through his fingers.

United had only just conceded a late equaliser themselves when goalkeeper Senne Lammens fumbled a high cross and allowed Sporting striker Luis Suarez to hook in his second of the night.

A freak Fernandes goal had looked to have given United a lucky win as the captain, playing against his former team, saw his long-range shot take a wicked deflection off a divot in the pitch to beat the stranded goalkeeper.

It was a second goal for Fernandes in the space of three minutes as he levelled from the penalty spot, after Lisandro Martinez was brought down in the box.

United had trailed after a chaotic first half where they conceded two penalties in the space of three minutes, with Lammens saving the first from Suarez before conceding another 75 seconds later, with Suarez scoring at the second attempt.

Pochettino’s side were also aggrieved when Amad Diallo saw an equaliser disallowed for a foul in the build up, with Youri Tielemans instead penalised. Tielemans was booked, and was then sent off in stoppage time for picking up his second yellow card.

Three penalties, two disallowed goals, one fluke, and a “main-character energy” referee: Manchester United’s 2-2 draw with Sporting in the Champions League had a bit of everything.

The Player of the Match award was given to Bruno Fernandes.

Former Man Utd star Ashley Young has also slated the referee and his decisions.

Speaking on Radio 5 Live, Young said: “The referee's performance was just simply wow.

“The decisions that he gave. Youri Tielemans getting booked to eventually getting sent off later because you booked him when it should never have been a booking and it should have been an equalising goal.

“The third [Man Utd] goal that should have stood because he's saying Patrick Dorgu fouled the keeper.

“The keeper comes out and flaps at it before he is even touched.

“How that has gone to VAR is beyond me. His decision making today is nothing more than a disgrace.”

Karen Carney, on punditry duty for TNT Sports, critiqued referee Szymon Marciniak and the decisions he made tonight.

“The referee had main character energy,” she said.

“Sometimes in life you earn your luck. The referee - main character energy.

“His decisions were poor, Man Utd should come away with more than just a draw.”

Bruno Fernandes also address the mistakes from goalkeeper Senne Lammens which led to both of Sporting’s goal.

Fernandes said: "Mistakes are part of football and from that you get goals.

“Everyone has to take the blame for everything we do good and we do bad as well. We need to be together in the good and in the bad moments."

It was the second goal United had disallowed after Amad Diallo saw a first-half header chalked off.

Here are some more thoughts from the Man Utd captain.

Bruno Fernandes said: "Obviously, I think we started the game well. We gave a penalty away where Senne [Lammens] saved it. We gave one or two counters, but we were very good in recovery positions.

"They got the goal, but we were still in control of the game. We didn't have much people in the last third. Second half we got the two goals. That was the moment we should push a bit more.”
