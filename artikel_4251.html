Resident Evil Requiem Crowned 'Ultimate Game Of The Year' At The Golden Joysticks Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Resident Evil Requiem has been crowned 'Ultimate Game of the Year' at this year's Golden Joystick Awards.

In fact, Capcom did remarkably well, scooping a total of six awards, including 'Studio of the Year'. The awards are mostly decided by public vote, with Requiem gaining 17% for its GOTY gong, ahead of Phantom Blade Zero at 16% and Crimson Desert at 15%.

Angela Sant'Albano, who played protagonist Grace Ashcroft in Requiem, won the award for 'Best Lead Performer', while the game also scooped up the 'Best Game Trailer' award for its short film starring Maika Monroe.

Requiem also won 'Console Game of the Year', while Onimusha: Way of the Sword won 'Best Audio Design'.

Unfortunately, no awards were handed out for first-party Nintendo games. Ocarina of Time, of course, launched too late to be eligible.
