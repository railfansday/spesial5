Four radiation belts surrounded Earth for weeks after 2024 solar superstorm

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Within Earth's magnetic field, energetic particles are trapped in two doughnut-shaped regions known as the Van Allen radiation belts—usually separated by a gap where particles are especially sparse. This structure can be dramatically rearranged during solar storms, but until now, astronomers have remained largely unsure as to how this happens.
