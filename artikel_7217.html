Lifecycle QA Specialist at Winona

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Winona is one of the leading telemedicine companies providing HRT for women in menopause. We’ve built all of our technology in-house including an EHR, patient care admin system, patient portal, website, marketing technology etc. Winona has two compounding pharmacies and a team of in-house physicians providing world-class care via a fully vertically integrated business model.

We are seeking a detail-oriented QA Specialist to ensure that every email and SMS campaign and journey is configured correctly and functions as intended prior to launch. This role spans campaign configuration and tracking, content and design review, and cross-platform QA — identifying and resolving issues before they reach our audience.

Conduct QA on all email and SMS campaigns and journeys in Iterable prior to launch to ensure every element is configured correctly and functions as intended.

Verify campaign and journey names against Winona’s naming conventions (name, year, event, medium and audience).

Review journey configuration, including start tiles, suppressions, scheduled send dates and times, tile logic and conditionals, entry limits, exit rules and journey event tracking.

Confirm that monthly and recurring journeys are refreshed with updated start tiles prior to each send.

Verify that campaign labels are applied correctly (audience, segments, category, goal and month/year).

Confirm that the appropriate conversion events are assigned for each campaign type: prospect, customer upsell, Shop, community and live event campaigns.

Review delivery settings, including quiet hours, send time optimization, frequency caps and performance alerts.

Verify that link tracking is configured correctly, including Google Analytics, UTM parameters and custom link parameters.

Review sender details: message type, from name, from email and reply-to address.

Review email templates against Winona’s brand and design standards, including fonts, sizing, padding, CTA button styling, link formatting and footers.

Test all links, CTA buttons and image links, and confirm that images include alt text.

Proofread copy for accuracy, consistency and formatting.

Send and review test proofs in light and dark mode on iOS and Android, across both Gmail and Apple Mail.

Coordinate SMS test sends with the appropriate team members prior to testing.

Conduct QA on email touchpoints that connect to Shopify, such as Shop campaigns and checkout-related flows, to ensure a seamless end-to-end experience.

Follow and help maintain internal templating documentation, QA checklists and the HTML template library.

Communicate issues, errors and inconsistencies to the lifecycle team clearly and promptly, and track them through resolution.

Recommend improvements to QA processes and standards on an ongoing basis.

At least 3 years of experience performing QA within an ESP like Iterable or similar platforms.

Working knowledge of HTML and CSS for email.

Exceptional attention to detail and consistent checklist execution.

Understanding of how emails render across clients, devices and dark mode.

Clear written communication for reporting issues.

Proficiency with Handlebars or dynamic content personalization.

Shopify experience, particularly with checkout and order flows.

Experience with email testing tools such as Litmus or Email on Acid.

Familiarity with Google Analytics and UTM tracking.

Experience with ClickUp or similar project management tools.

Knowledge of SMS best practices and compliance standards (CAN-SPAM, CASL).

As a full-time independent contractor, you’ll enjoy:

Paid non-working holidays per country of residence

Pro-rated 13th-month bonus in select regions

Employee engagement activities and virtual gatherings
