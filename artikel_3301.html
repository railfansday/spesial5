Connor Schell’s Full Day Scores Netflix Deal For FIFA Women’s World Cup Brazil 2027 Programming

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

EXCLUSIVE: Full Day Productions, the production company founded by former ESPN exec Conor Schell, has partnered with Netflix on a slate of programming around the FIFA Women’s World Cup Brazil 2027.

The deal will see the company, which is backed by Chernin Entertainment’s Words + Pictures, develop and produce original features, teasers, profiles, and promotional content that “capture the personalities, emotion and cultural impact of the tournament and its stars.”

It will tell these stories alongside Netflix’s live coverage of the tournament, which runs 24 June to 25 July 2027 and is the first Women’s World Cup held in South America. Netflix has the exclusive U.S. and Canadian rights to the tournament in 2027 and 2031. Alongside IMG, which is producing the live coverage, the two companies will also support live coverage of the final draw on December 11. Watch on Deadline

Elle Duncan, Rebecca Lowe and Andres Cantor are part of the broadcast team.Full Day, which is led by President David Chamberlain, is known for its work in the live entertainment space, producing the likes of the ESPYS, NFL Honors, the Oscars Red Carpet Show, the Met Gala, the NFL Pro Bowl Games, the Savage X Fenty Show, the Netflix Cup, and the Netflix Slam, as well as live college volleyball single-night tournament Spikes Under the Lights.

“The FIFA Women’s World Cup is one of the most powerful stages in all of sports,” said Connor Schell, CEO and Founder of Full Day, “Brazil 2027 represents a historic moment for the women’s game as the tournament comes to South America for the first time. Through our storytelling, we’re excited to work alongside Netflix to bring fans closer to the players, teams and culture surrounding this incredible event. We have an exceptionally talented and passionate team at Full Day committed to telling the best stories in the world in short and long form, and I couldn’t be more excited to bring that collective creativity and expertise to a stage of this magnitude.”
