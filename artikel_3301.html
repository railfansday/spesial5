Cody Johnson to Skip CMA Awards Due to ‘Mental and Physical Exhaustion’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Entertainer of the Year nominee Cody Johnson has announced that he will no longer be attending or performing at Wednesday’s 60th Annual CMA Awards. Johnson, through a post on his social media accounts, shared a vulnerable message that he is skipping the broadcast due to “mental and physical exhaustion.”

“I want to be open about this because I believe we help one another by revealing our truths, and this time my truth isn’t in the words of a song; it’s real life,” Johnson posted. “At the end of the day, I’m a human being, no matter how much I tell myself and others I’m Superman. I have everything to be thankful for, but sometimes I put too much pressure on myself. I’m going to spend some time recouping at home; being grounded by my family is what I need most right now.”

The artist added, “I’ve waited my whole life to be where I am in my career, and I wouldn’t have it any other way. It was well worth the wait and the hard work it took to get here. However, going 100 mph all the time isn’t healthy, and I see that now.” Johnson ended his note by thanking fans for their support during this time. Johnson is nominated for four awards at the upcoming CMA’s, including the coveted Entertainer of the Year. He also just received his first Grammy nomination, for Banks of the Trinity in the Traditional Country Album category.

The stigma around mental health in country music is changing thanks to frank posts like Johnson’s. He joins a group that includes Kelsea Ballerini, Luke Combs, Chase Rice, and many more who have shared their experiences in order to destigmatize and normalize conversations around self-care, therapy, and the importance of personal boundaries in a relentless industry.
