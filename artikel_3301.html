What to Expect From Best Buy's Black Friday and Cyber Monday Sales This Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

For all intents and purposes, Black Friday sales are already here. There are plenty of deals out already, even though we're weeks away from the actual event. One of the reasons for that is the competition retailers have with each other to be the first to get your money. One of the retailers that is making the biggest early splash is Best Buy, confirming their entire plan for the rest of the year. Here's what you need to know.

Best Buy has announced the following events:

DoorBoosters: A Halloween sale from Oct. 30 to Nov. 1.

Early Black Friday Deals and Doorbusters: The official start of early Black Friday deals on Nov. 6. New deals will drop every Friday.

Black Friday Doorbusters and Deals: Best Buy says this will be its main sale, starting on Thursday, Nov. 19 and running until Nov. 28 with deals on "computing, gaming, home theater, wearables, and more."

Cyber Sunday and Monday: Best Buy is extending Cyber Monday to five days and says you can find deals going up to 50% off select tech.

Last-minute savings: The Christmas sale will run from Dec. 18 until Dec. 24.

You don't need to be any kind of member to shop Best Buy's Black Friday in July sale; however, it is free to sign up for a My Best Buy membership, which will get you free shipping. If you join one of their paid subscriptions (My Best Buy Plus for $49.99 per year or My Best Buy Total for $179.99 per year), you'll get faster free shipping, exclusive prices and deals, an extended 60-day return window on most products, and access to select sales, like the Member Deals Days sale that is going on right now. Paid members will also earn extra rewards from both the current sale and the upcoming Black Friday in July sale.

You can find deals on TVs, laptops, headphones, video games, electric bikes, and more. There will be new deals every week in stores, on its website, and in the Best Buy mobile app. They'll offer price matching, meaning if the price drops further during the sale (Dec. 24), they will match it and offer a refund.
