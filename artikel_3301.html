Early Black Friday sales are ramping up, and I've been keeping an eye on all the best fitness-related deals. If you work out at home, adjustable dumbbells are a no-brainer. Where buying individual pairs of dumbbells in five-pound increments up to 80 pounds would cost well over $1,500 (and take up a bunch of space in your home gym), adjustable dumbbells deliver the same versatility for a fraction of that price.

Pretty much every "best home dumbbells" list worth its salt will feature the PowerBlock Elite Adjustable Dumbbells near the top. In fact, I named them the most gift-worthy piece of home gym equipment in Lifehacker's first-ever "gift of the year" awards.

The futuristic cubic look is stylish, but more importantly, it's a seriously economical, space-saving design—which is the whole point of going adjustable over a traditional rack of individual weights. The weights have flat edges so you can hold them on end on your lap, and unlike a lot of adjustable models, they're compact enough that they won’t bump into each other on shoulder or chest presses.

These dumbbells also come in 70-pound and 90-pound versions. If you go with one of the smaller sets, you can also buy expansion sets later to upgrade to the larger versions.
