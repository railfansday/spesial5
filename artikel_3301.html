Coachella Tickets Are Already Sold Out — Here’s What They’re Going for on Resale

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

The Coachella lineup was announced this week, with Dua Lipa, Tame Impala, and SZA topping the bill for the 2027 edition. Tickets sold out within hours of going on sale on Friday, including GA, VIP, and camping passes for both weekends. The festival falls across two weekends in spring: April 9-11 and 16-18.

Also on the bill are Madonna, Phoebe Bridgers, Don Toliver, Zara Larrson, Weezer, and Dom Dolla, with a special “In the Desert” slot for Daddy Yankee.

If you missed out on the general sale, here’s what to know about the waitlist and resale options.

Fans can join the waitlist for weekend one or two with a $20 deposit. Fans whose waitlist requests are fulfilled will be automatically charged the full ticket price. Unlike general sale, there is no payment plan option for the waitlist. If you aren’t able to secure a ticket, the payment will be refunded. GA tickets cost $779, while VIP tickets are $1,399.

Official resale is available through AXS. Fans can also check secondary resale sites like StubHub, SeatGeek, and VividSeats, which carries greater risk, although prices are sometimes lower than official resale.

Prices occasionally dip for events closer to the start date, so it can be worth taking a wait-and-see approach to buying tickets. But since you need a wristband to enter the festival, it’s not advised to wait too long, since the seller will need to physically mail them to you. It’s also worth noting that tickets are sold for all three days of either weekend, so fans should be wary of listings for a specific day.
