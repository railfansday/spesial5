‘NBC100: The Live Celebration’ Sets Amy Poehler, Conan O’Brien, Mariska Hargitay & More For Centennial Special Lineup; More Soon

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The onscreen home of the peacock is marking 100 years with a star-studded celebration as NBC100: The Live Celebration takes shape.

Set for Thursday, Dec. 10 at the Dolby Theatre, NBC has set stars from ER, Parks and Recreation, 30 Rock, Law & Order, The Office, This Is Us, Saturday Night Live and more for the centennial anniversary special, which will air live at 8pm ET/7pm CT on NBC and Peacock.

The first round of stars announced for the special include Adam Levine, Amy Poehler, Andy Buckley, Angela Kinsey, B.J. The three-hour live event will honor the stars, stories and moments spanning generations of NBC, featuring surprise reunions, musical performances, comedic tributes and heartwarming flashbacks.

Produced by Done + Dusted, executive producers for NBC100 include Raj Kapoor, Katy Mullan and Sarah Levine Hall, with co-EPs Rob Paine, Taryn Hurd and Patrick Menton.
