Comedian Peyton Ruddy Signs With Brillstein Entertainment Partners

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

EXCLUSIVE: Comedian Peyton Ruddy has signed with Brillstein Entertainment Partners for representation in all areas.

A rising star in the stand-up space, Ruddy was selected as a New Face in the stand-up category of this year’s Just For Laughs comedy festival in Montreal and was recently announced as one of The Breakouts for this year’s New York Comedy Festival.

Many are familiar with Ruddy through his video series My Best Work, with which he’s gathered hundreds of millions of views and just shy of a million followers across all social media platforms. The series has culminated in a tour of the same name, on which he’s currently hitting 16 different markets. Before social media virality, Ruddy started his career in comedy while in high school at 17 years old. After years spent coming up in the Chicago scene, he moved to Austin, Texas where he was hired as a door guy at Joe Rogan’s comedy club, The Comedy Mothership, and showcased as a viral bucket pull on Kill Tony. Watch on Deadline

While in Austin, Ruddy opened for some of stand-up’s biggest names, including Tim Dillon, Ron White, Joe List, Patton Oswalt, Ralph Barbosa, Chris Distefano, Craig Robinson, Harland Williams, Kam Patterson, Casey Rocket and Sam Tallent. Since leaving Austin, he has been consistently touring and selling out clubs all over the country — even headlining the main room at the Comedy Mothership, in a full-circle moment.

Brillstein’s roots in comedy extend back more than 45 years. The company has repped numerous original comedy luminaries, including assorted Saturday Night Live cast members, and currently works with talent including Nikki Glaser, Nate Bargatze, Jim Gaffigan, Whitney Cummings, Bill Maher, Cecily Strong, Jim Jefferies and more.

Ruddy continues to be repped by WME and Yorn Levine Barnes.
