Tuchel learns lesson as England clinch third place in 10-goal thriller

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Was this evidence of England head coach Thomas Tuchel already learning lessons from the Argentina collapse? Let’s hope so.

With England on the ropes and in danger of throwing away a lead against France, Tuchel resisted the temptation to flood his team with defenders and was rewarded with a World Cup bronze medal.

England looked on course to secure an emphatic victory to clinch the nation’s best World Cup finish since 1966 and yet Tuchel was left with the exact same scenario that had gone so badly against Argentina in the semi-final.

Leading by four goals at half-time, England somehow went into the final hydration break of this bronze medal game with a single-goal advantage. This time, Tuchel steered England over the line by getting his late substitutions right.

Jude Bellingham stepped off the bench to cap a wonderful tournament for him with the goal that made sure England finished the World Cup in third place.

It may have resembled an exhibition match at times, with France failing to turn up in the first half. But England’s thrilling 6-4 victory will send Tuchel home from the United States feeling a little bit better, even though he is not yet off the hook for what happened in Atlanta.

The fact that success against France was earned with a Bukayo Saka hat-trick, as well as goals from Declan Rice, Ezri Konsa and Bellingham, will prompt more debate over Tuchel’s decision-making when it really mattered. Not least why Saka watched the entire Argentina game from the substitutes’ bench.

Tuchel cut a relaxed figure, changing his touchline attire to a casual white T-shirt and tracksuit bottoms. But his team meant business, as they raced into a stunning four-goal first-half lead.

Saka played like a man with a point to prove. He scored twice and produced the shot from which England won the corner for Konsa’s goal in the opening 45 minutes. Was this a message to Tuchel or simply the winger taking out his frustration on France?

France looked uninterested from the start and it was a loose Désiré Doué pass that gifted possession to Rice. As England’s captain for the evening strode forwards, Doué did not bother to track him and Rice curled a lovely shot into the corner of the net to give Tuchel’s team a third-minute lead.

He leaves France having won a World Cup and reached the final in another. This team has arguably played the best football of the lot, but will finish empty-handed. The smart money is on Zinedine Zidane replacing him, who won three consecutive Champions Leagues while Real Madrid head coach. So he knows his way around knockout football.

Neither England nor France wanted to be playing this game in Miami, but they can have no complaints about the nature of their semi-final defeats.

Tournament favourites France were comprehensively outplayed by Spain, with their feted front four of Kylian Mbappe, Ousmane Dembele, Bradley Barcola and Michael Olise all turning in below-par performances.

England’s defeat by Argentina was perhaps not as much of a surprise, but the nature of Thomas Tuchel’s team’s second-half collapse was just as alarming as France’s no-show.

England’s manager was fiercely criticised for replacing Anthony Gordon with Ezri Konsa and switching to a back five in the 72nd minute, but Tuchel has since defended his decisions.

“If you are asking if I regret my decisions, if this is the question, then I say no. I don’t regret my ‌decisions because I ‌felt that we became ⁠too passive,” said Tuchel.

“I took several decisions, trusting my instinct, my intuition, my experience, trusting my competitiveness, and I took the decision in order to help the team and get the result.

“I would regret if I didn’t help. I would regret ⁠if we didn’t react, but I have no regrets over the decision itself.”

Quite how strong Didier Deschamps and Tuchel decide to go with their teams is anyone’s guess. There has been some suggestion that Mbappe will start to give him the chance to beat Lionel Messi to the Golden Boot.

Some of England’s regular starters, such as Declan Rice, look physically spent, so there could be some rotation, and even a chance for players such as Kobbie Mainoo who has not played a minute of football at the tournament.

The third/fourth-place play-off has been rebranded as the “Bronze Final” for this tournament, and Tuchel was keen to point out that finishing third would be England’s best World Cup result since 1966. They lost this fixture against Belgium in 2018, but the way that game has been widely forgotten speaks volumes.
