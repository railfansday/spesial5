Derek McInnes names Rangers starting line-up vs St Mirren, makes one change

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Derek McInnes has named his Rangers starting XI for tonight’s William Hill Premiership clash against St Mirren in Paisley.

The Ibrox side head into the game looking to continue their recent run of form and secure another important three points before the international break.

Ahead of tonight's game, McInnes has made one change from the side that started the 1-0 Premier Sports Cup semi-final victory over Aberdeen at Hampden last weekend.

Because of this, Dan Neil has replaced Tuur Rommens in the starting line-up.

Rangers XI: Pandur, Sterling, Fernandez, Makhanya, Penrice, Neil, Raskin, Minsu, Bouanani, Naderi, Kelsy

Subs: Kelly, Nedeljkovic, Godfrey, Rommens, Dragojevic, Chukwuani, Curtis, Gassama, Miovski
