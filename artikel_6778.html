All About Brittany Snow’s Fiancé, Hunter Moreno

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

In October 2024, The Hunting Wives actress Brittany Snow was seen with a mystery man in New York City. He was identified as cinematographer Hunter Moreno, and the pair looked very cozy, sharing a kiss while walking Snow’s dog. The actress was wearing a Yankees cap, a long black coat, and wide-legged pants for the outing with a pair of sunglasses. Moreno was in a baseball cap, jeans, and a big white hoodie.

A year earlier, Snow divorced ex-husband Tyler Stanaland, known for his role on Selling the OC, a spinoff of the popular Netflix reality show, Selling Sunset.

The former couple announced their split in September 2022 in a joint statement on Instagram that is now deleted, saying, “This decision was made with love and mutual respect for one another. We have realized we need to take some time and make sure we are each living our most fulfilling and authentic lives. We started this journey as best friends and our relationship will continue to be a priority not only for us but for our dog Charlie.”

Fast-forward to November 2025, reports surfaced that Snow and Moreno had gotten engaged. Snow has since posted tributes to him on Instagram, though she hasn’t discussed their relationship status herself.

Here’s what there is to know about Moreno and his relationship with Snow so far.

Moreno is a creative director, cinematographer, and photographer who has worked with celebrities like Selena Gomez, Cara Delevingne, Reneé Rapp, Machine Gun Kelly, Miley Cyrus, and brands like Vogue, Inkd Magazine, and Madewell. He started his career in Los Angeles, filming YouTube videos for growing influencers.

He seems to have been working on directing more music videos in 2024, and was nominated for a directing award at the People’s Choice Country Awards.

He also briefly dated Tana Mongeau in 2017.

Moreno is the co-founder and creative director of Make Sure Your Friends Are OK, or MSYFAO. The organization sells merchandise and then donates a portion of the profits to mental health charities. In an episode of Recovering From Reality with Alexis Haines, he says he was inspired by his own struggles with mental health issues.

Mental health is an important issue to Snow as well, who co-wrote a book titled September Letters about issues around mental well-being, partnering with the Mental Health Coalition.

In January 2025, Snow tagged Moreno in a series of photos taken of her for a Netflix press event, crediting him for photographing her. She wrote, “I was asked to hang out a little bit with my friends at @nightagentnetflix. I got to be an agent and run around the streets of Bangkok. Weirdly, I did not wear this dress. 🤍* press for season 2 *”

As of July 2025, the two still follow each other on Instagram, and Moreno has liked almost all her recent Instagrams. She also regularly likes his posts.

Yes. On March 28, 2026, Snow quietly tucked a photo of herself with Moreno into an Instagram carousel:

She shared a birthday tribute to him on Oct. 20, 2026. “Happy birthday to my favorite person on the planet,” she wrote. “I must have done something right to deserve someone like you. Everyone who knows you, knows you are sunshine in human form. This was the first picture we ever took together (we didn’t know it was being taken) and I’ve never stopped hugging you & laughing with you since. It’s my favorite place to be. I love u @huntermoreno.”

During her November 2026 appearance on Armchair Expert with Dax Shepard, Snow touched on her relationship with Moreno when discussing why she is thankful for her divorce from Tyler Stanaland. “I am so grateful that I went through that because the person that I am and the partner I’m with now, it’s just so different,” she said. “I can’t believe I’m grateful for my divorce. I was 37 and I was living in an Airbnb, just staring at my dog being like, ‘I don’t know what’s going to happen. It’s just you and me, kiddo, and we’re going to try to rebuild this.’”

“And I really appreciated so many women, friends of mine that were like, ‘That happened to me,’” she added. “And, you know, I would go on Reddit and look up stories from women going through things in their later 30s and finding people later in life. We have to make it what we make of it.”

On Nov. 10, 2025, a video surfaced on social media of Snow and Claire Danes talking on the red carpet of The Beast in Me’s premiere. At one point, Danes smiled and asked Snow, “You’re engaged? When did that happen?”

One day later, TMZ reported that Moreno did indeed pop the question, though Snow has not discussed their status herself.
