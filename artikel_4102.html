Halo 2 Is 22 Years Old Today, And I'm Gutted The Remake Isn't Happening

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Pure Xbox is listening to: Blow Me Away - Breaking Benjamin 🎶

It feels like only yesterday that Halo: Campaign Evolved teased a remake of Halo 2, something I've been hoping for more and more as time went on. After all, it's been 22 long years now. Yep, that's right - Halo 2 launched on November 9, 2004 for the original Xbox, and has often been credited as the game that put Xbox Live on the map.

As the years slip by, anniversaries for OG Xbox titles have a habit of making me feel my age. Still, what a privilege it was to be there for the inception of Xbox Live, to play both co-op and PvP with friends across the globe in the second entry of Bungie's incredible run of Halo games.

Admittedly, it's been a hot-minute since I last booted up Halo 2 in the Master Chief Collection. But visits to retro gaming bars like Manchester's NQ64 reminded me just how good this FPS still is. I recall sitting down for a few games of Slayer, only for the muscle memory to kick in and humble a good number of Hookshot staff - Fraser included. Oh boy, it never gets old.

In fact, just sit back and listen to 15 minutes of Halo 2's main menu music and ponder the good old days of dual-wielding SMGs in Beaver Creek.

Halo 2 took Master Chief's fight against the Covenant to Earth, selling more than 8.4 million copies before its servers shut down in 2010, following the discontinuation of the original Xbox Live services. The game was later ported to PC and remastered for Xbox One as part of Halo: The Master Chief Collection.

Xbox's 25th anniversary is just round the corner: in fact, it's this Sunday! We'll be here, keeping an eye out for any and all announcements from Xbox throughout the week. Understandably, we've been feeling pretty nostalgic, hence this Halo 2 feature. In fact, I'm reinstalling the MCC as we speak.

I was a big fan of Halo Studios' Campaign Evolved. Like, I really, really enjoyed it. So, it's bittersweet knowing that their teased Halo 2 remake in Unreal Engine will never happen.

Sure, the devs fumbled modern Halo multiplayer, but perhaps a Halo 2 remake could have been a case of history repeating itself. Y'know, a remade return of Halo 2's iconic online multiplayer?! Ah, well.
