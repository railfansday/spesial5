GTA 6 Won't Have Any Last Minute Delays, as It's Officially Gone Gold

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

In case there was any doubt remaining, GTA 6 will definitely be making its debut on 19th November, 2026, with no risk of another delay of some kind.

That's because it's finally been confirmed the game has gone gold, which essentially means the open world action title is ready for release.

This comes via Take-Two's most recent earnings call, during which CEO Strauss Zelnick assured shareholders that GTA 6 had gone gold.

Separately, in an interview with IGN, he also says there are to be no further delays to the release. While that was unlikely given how close we are to the game's arrival, it's never a complete certainty until the software's actually out, so it's nice to have confirmation.

GTA 6 has been in the works for a very long time — at least the eight years since the launch of Red Dead Redemption 2 — so to hear the game is ready to roll out will be an exciting milestone for fans.

I've no doubt there will be additional patches and updates to install as Rockstar further irons out bugs and such.

For those who have pre-ordered the game on PS Store, you can begin pre-loading it starting today, and given the enormous file size, I'd recommend getting that download started as soon as you can.
