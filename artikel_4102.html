Video: Zelda: Ocarina Of Time Side-By-Side Graphics Comparison (Switch 2, 3DS, N64)

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Zelda: Ocarina of Time returned this week on the Switch 2 and it looks better than ever. If you are wondering just much of a facelift it's had since the 2011 outing, and the original N64 hit, you might want to check out this side-by-side comparison video.

YouTube channel ElAnalistDeBits has now released a "final graphics comparison" which takes a closer look at the character models, environments, physics, the many locations in the game, and much more.

If you are wondering about the performance, the remake on Switch 2 runs at 60fps. In comparison, the original N64 outing ran at 20fps. And as for the resolution, this new remake ranges from 1080p to 1440p in docked, and 720p to 1080p in handheld (via Digital Foundry).

In our review of the Ocarina of Time remake, we noted it was the most visually stunning work Nintendo has ever released:

"Ocarina of Time on Switch 2 is a stellar reimagining of the original, one that completely revamps the experience with stunning visuals, modernised gameplay, and vastly improved audio. Despite some minor blemishes, this is comfortably the most visually stunning work Nintendo has ever put out, and it’s unquestionably the best way to play one of the most seminal games of all time."

“Despite some minor blemishes, this is comfortably the most visually stunning work Nintendo has ever put out”

More than that, it’s the most visually stunning game ever. Somehow they managed to undersell the richness of the environments, especially Kakariko Village.

3DS version's aesthetics are definitely my favorite of the three. The Switch 2 one's certainly the most technically impressive but still not really that fond of how glossy everyone is and think most of the areas are overdesigned. Game also can't really seem to make up its mind if it's trying to expand on the look of the original, trying to look like BotW, or trying to do its own thing and it feels inconsistent at times as a result.

Good example of that off the top of my head, Ruto's overall look - especially her face - being very clearly retooled to more closely resemble the BotW Zoras, while Nabooru has a giant square nose and round eyes that while they kinda align with BotW are a lot more stiff and puppet-like to the point she more closely resembles her N64 model. The two designs just do not look like they belong together.

I love how pulling back the camera in the remake gives you a top-down angle like 2D Zelda. I ran around in 2D for a good 15 minutes when I discovered it.

While I'm loving the Switch 2 remake, I still really like the 3DS version. It looks like what one remembers of the 64 version after many years without checking it because the mind makes it look better in memories, I don't know how to explain it.

Interesting how much smaller/more crowded some areas feel in the Switch 2 version. I definitely noticed not having as much room to run around while fighting Dead Hand, but it made the fight feel more exciting.

I have played the 3ds version but wasn't aware of how much better it looks than the OG. I thought it was just down to being on a smaller screen so looked better. To be fair tho, whatever console you play it on, what a belter of a game.

Yeah, quite the jump isn't it (pun intended considering the change to that as well) - still a sucker for the original and the 3DS versions in their own ways, but man am I loving the Switch 2 remake thanks to its additions and changes not only to the visuals!

It’s interesting how empty the original environments are. Not how I remember it in my mind at all but obviously makes total sense from a power point of view.

The remake is a visual masterpiece, and I can’t wait to play it many many times.

There are lots of things which are beautiful if looked at separately and from time to time I think "they really nailed this design here", but rarely on a whole unfortunately.
