Brad Pitt Officially Confirms 'World War Z 2' Status

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

13 years ago, Brad Pitt found himself running faster and harder than he ever had before, with the fate of humanity at stake from the fastest undead you've ever laid eyes upon. World War Z was a massive global success, earning more than $540 million worldwide and proving that audiences were more than happy to watch Pitt as the lead in a legitimate blockbuster, and as a result, a sequel seemed inevitable, right? Well, it's not quite that simple.

A previously announced follow-up with David Fincher at the helm injected some excitement into proceedings, but prior to the pandemic, the project fell apart due to budget concerns. Then, it all went quiet until earlier this year. Now, Pitt has offered some news that might get the blood pumping again. Speaking exclusively with Collider's Steve Weintraub at the premiere of The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, Pitt confirmed that World War Z 2 is still moving forward. When asked directly about the sequel's status, Pitt replied:

“Oh, it's coming. We're working on it. We're talking zombie logic even as we speak. If we can get the script together, the thing is, you know, it's well-worn territory, and so how do we take it [up], keep it fresh? How do we take it somewhere it hasn't gone before? So, we got some good ideas I'm pretty excited about. We just got to flesh them out now.”

Paramount revived plans for World War Z 2 earlier this year, with Edward Berger (Conclave) attached to direct and Dennis Kelly (Utopia) writing the screenplay. The original movie, directed by Marc Forster (Quantum of Solace), starred Pitt as Gerry Lane, a former United Nations investigator traveling around the world to find a way to stop a zombie pandemic. The cast included Mireille Enos (The Killing) as Karin Lane, Daniella Kertesz (AfterDeath) as Segen, and James Badge Dale (Iron Man 3) as Captain Speke.

Pitt also told Weintraub that he's recently been drawn toward smaller, more intimate dramas, explaining:

“Well, at the moment, I've been kind of focused on little intimate dramas. It's the thing that's intrigued me. A lot of actors I've been watching, studying, and it's drawn me back into that world a little bit. So that's kind of my jam at the moment this last year and this coming year.”
