‘A Lot of Fighting’: What Happened Before Courtney Clenney Killed Christian Obumseli?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

On the surface, Courney Clenney and Christian Obumseli seemed like they had a great relationship. She was a successful influencer and OnlyFans model, and he was her handsome, crypto-trading boyfriend, and together they’d just moved into a luxury apartment in Austin, Texas. But their neighbor, Aidan Nesvisky, had a different experience with them.

“At first they seemed like a normal couple, but soon it was obvious they weren’t,” Nesvisky says in a new episode of Dateline, which premieres tonight at 10 p.m. ET. “We could hear them all the time, and it was not pleasant. It was a lot of fighting, a lot of breaking glass, a lot of shouting.” He remembers a painting being thrown off their balcony and landing in his. “It was this crazy tiger painting. We called it the murder tiger.”

That nickname would become a lot darker a few months later, after Clenney and Obumseli had moved to Miami. On April 3, 2022, she would fatally stab him. Police questioned her, but soon let her go. Obumseli’s family took to social media, begging for justice. For their investigation, Dateline spoke with Obumseli’s brother, cousin, and mother, about the kindhearted young man they’d watched grow up; as well as Clenney’s parents about the young woman they saw starting her own business, and supporting her boyfriend. They spoke with a detective on the case in Florida, and neighbors who saw what their home life was like. Together, it paints a complicated picture of a troubled relationship, and the devastation that a violent death can leave on a family.

Clenney was eventually charged with second-degree murder, and pleaded guilty to manslaughter in April 2026; she was sentenced to six years in prison and five years of probation. She is expected to be released in 2028. Despite the legal case being closed, there’s still a mystery around the crime — what really happened that night in Miami that left Obumseli dead, and Clenney serving time?
