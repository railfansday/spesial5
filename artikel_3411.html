PlayStation Network Outage Hits On GTA 6 Release Day Eve

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

PS5 users are reporting widespread technical issues as GTA 6 rolls out

What happens when a potential 250 million people try logging into a server at the same time? Problems, that's what. Thursday, Nov. 19 is the official release day for Grand Theft Auto 6 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X, and it appears Sony's platform, commonly known as PlayStation Network, is already buckling the day before under enormous demand for the world's most hyped game.

Many PS5 users tried to plan ahead by preloading the game starting on Nov. 10. Sony surely anticipated potential issues — the company has publicly treated GTA 6's release as if it were a first-party game — but that did little to prevent outages. Players around the world are facing messages like "Sony services are busy," "Too many failed connection attempts," or a mishmash of different errors. Though Sony's official status tracker currently lists the network as healthy, websites like Down Detector are seeing a sudden jump in reports related to the service. Most PS5 players say they're experiencing login issues with the PlayStation network, but there are also many reports of PSN server connection issues and update and download hiccups.

Whether you've seen codes like WS-116449-5, CE-34861-2, or WS-37505-0, they all point to the same thing: PlayStation's servers are facing serious strain.

GTA 6 is the follow-up to one of the most successful video games of all time, so the number of people trying to log in at once and download the game is likely massive. In the United States, it's the early afternoon — so many GTA fans probably just got off work and are all trying to do the same thing.

Though GTA 6 is not a multiplayer game that requires an online connection, players must download the title and its mandatory updates before they can enjoy it. This would have been the case even if GTA 6 had been printed on a disc — seemingly all major games require a day-one download for new patches or security reasons, like the recent PS5 jailbreak situation. This means that millions of people are trying to download hundreds of gigabytes of data at the same time, which was never going to end well.

There's no timeline for when PlayStation's servers might stabilize — and by extension, GTA 6 — but given the importance of this release, Sony are undoubtedly working on it.

Grand Theft Auto 6 officially launches on Nov. 19 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X.
