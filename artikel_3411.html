‘Black Widow’ Meets ‘Killing Eve’ in HBO Max’s Official Smash Hit

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

One of the biggest fantasy films of the year is now just a couple of weeks away. On November 20, The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping continues the prequel stories set in the Hunger Games universe and follows The Ballad of Songbird and Snakes. Set on the morning of the reaping of the 50th Hunger Games, also known as the Second Quarter Quell, Sunrise on the Reaping will follow a young Haymitch Abernathy (Joseph Zada, previously played by Woody Harrelson) as he is selected from District 12.

Based on the latest entry in Suzanne Collins' dystopian book series, which broke a series record by selling over 1.5 million copies globally in its first week, Sunrise on the Reaping has assembled a typically stacked cast for the franchise, with Zada joined by the likes of Elle Fanning, Mckenna Grace, Jesse Plemons, Ralph Fiennes, Glenn Close, Whitney Peak, Kieran Culkin, and others. The most eye-catching casting for the film is that of Jennifer Lawrence, who is joining original co-star Josh Hutcherson in reprising their career-making roles for the film, having mentioned on a podcast that stepping back on set was emotional.

How have Hunger Games fans been passing the time before Katniss returns? By checking out one of Lawrence's other badass performances, of course. Released in 2018, three years after Lawrence's last Hunger Games appearance, Red Sparrow turned the actress into a mix between Marvel’s Black Widow and Villanelle from the hit series Killing Eve. Eight years later, ahead of Sunrise on the Reaping, Lawrence's spy thriller is a hit again. At the time of writing, Red Sparrow is one of the ten most-watched movies on HBO Max in the U.S.

Francis Lawrence, director of five Hunger Games movies, including the upcoming Sunrise on the Reaping, took to the director's chair for Red Sparrow, though the film faced much more critical backlash than he is used to. Red Sparrow also only grossed around $150 million worldwide, which barely turned its reported $70 million production budget into a profit. Charlotte Rampling, Joel Edgerton, Jeremy Irons, and Mary-Louise Parker also starred in a retrospectively underrated spy thriller.
