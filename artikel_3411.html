GTA 6: Easiest Ways To Earn Money

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

As in many open-world games, cash rules everything in GTA 6. You need it to properly prep for some of the tougher encounters in the game, and there are so many ways to earn money that it can be overwhelming. It is an open-world, expansive adventure, after all, but there are plenty of ways to simplify making money.

In addition to robbing places with Jason and Lucia together, you can string multiple robberies into one long crime session, or opt for more legitimate ways to earn money. No matter which option you choose, here are the easiest ways to earn cash in GTA 6.

It sounds obvious, but mainlining story missions is an easy way to earn cash and advance the campaign. Early on, Jason will get constant payments from jobs in his mailbox, so check it nearly every morning before driving off the property.

Some story missions can offer incredibly luxurious rewards, like gear, clothes, weapons, and of course, tons of cash. Some mid-to-late game missions offer tens of thousands of dollars in rewards, quickly boosting your bank account without doing much beyond playing the story.

Once you get to Chapter 2 with Lucia and meet Moses, you'll have the power to bring him cars, and either "register" them (so you own them above board), or sell them.

All you need to do is get in the car, then drive it to Moses (marked clearly on the map in the northwest portion of the game world), drive it into his garage, interact with him (L2/LT) and manually sell or register the car.

Selling nicer cars to Moses is a very easy way to get a ton of cash early in the game, and beyond.

Very early in GTA 6, you can knock off convenience stores and pawn shops, most of which have either one armored guard (who can easily be taken out as you enter) or no protection at all.

Walk into the store, take out the armed NPCs, and then point a gun at an employee. Hold L2 (LT) to bring up a prompt (typically confirmed with Square on PlayStation, X on Xbox), asking them to take money out of the cash register or let you know where valuables are kept.

Depending on where you rob, there might be an additional opportunity for extra cash:

Once you have the illegal goods (which are typically substances or marked bills), you'll need to deliver them to a specific NPC (marked in green on the world map) to "clean" them. This includes marked bills from banks, or illegal substances seized from enemy operations. Just bring the items (housed in a duffel bag) to the NPC, speak to them, and hand them over with a button prompt.

If you die or get caught by the police in GTA 6, you'll lose your goods in the duffel bag, so keep that in mind. If you're doing a huge job and have tons of cash and illegal goods in a duffel bag, you'll want to turn it in to a helpful NPC sooner rather than later.
