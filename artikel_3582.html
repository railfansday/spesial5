'SNL' Alum Heidi Gardner's Boyfriend Ray Tintori Dead at 43

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Saturday Night Live alum Heidi Gardner‘s boyfriend, filmmaker Ray Tintori, has died. He was 43.

The New York City Office of Chief Medical Examiner confirmed to Us on Monday, November 16, that Tintori’s death is currently under investigation, and the “cause and manner of death are pending further study.”

Us has reached out to Gardner’s rep for comment. TMZ was first to report the news.

Tintori was an accomplished music video director, having worked with major music acts such as Solange Knowles, The Killers, Arcade Fire and MGMT. He was credited as a special effects unit director on the 2012 fantasy movie Beasts of the Southern Wild, which received four Academy Award nominations and won the Grand Jury Prize at that year’s Sundance Film Festival.

Ray’s father, John Tintori, was a film editor who worked on Eight Men Out and Mr. Wonderful while his mother, Mary Cybulski, was as a script supervisor and photographer on Life of Pi, Syriana and The Ice Storm, among other projects. (Cybulski died from glioblastoma at age 70 in November 2025.)

It is not clear how long Gardner, 43, and Ray were together, though he posted a loving tribute to the comedian in honor of her 42nd birthday in July 2025.

“Prettier and more graceful than the moon and by far the coolest person I’ve ever met,” he wrote via Instagram alongside a carousel of sweet photos. “My favorite song in the world is you singing softly in my ear. My favorite color is the gold at the center of your eyes.”

Ray recalled “falling so fast” for Gardner the “second [their] eyes met” while she was “laughing with [her] whole body.”

“I’ve just been falling faster every day. You are the sexiest woman who ever lived, in every single manner of clothing, hair, and old age makeup,” he added. “I’m crushing on you so deep, endlessly. You hold my heart perfectly and love me so proper. You carry the party in your eyes and move like a modern dancer everywhere you go.”

The director concluded, “You are the most genius at making art and surfing the wave with the purest flow I’ve ever seen. The world is a love letter to you. I am your boy forever.”

Prior to her relationship with Ray, Gardner was married to Marvel Comics writer Zeb Wells from 2010 to 2023.

The former SNL cast member opened up about her divorce on “The Liz Moody Podcast” in 2024, indicating that Wells, 49, “will always be in [her] life and it will transform in a different way.” (She did not directly reference Wells by name.)

Acknowledging that their breakup was “very painful and uncomfortable,” she noted, “I will say the biggest thing I learned in that time is lean on the women in your life and your girlfriend.”

“I already had amazing friends and family, but I will just say that women showed up in such an incredible way and showed me that in future relationships, my romantic partner, I learned does not need to be my emotional rock,” Gardner added.
