Kirk Cousins Gets Emotional After Blowout Win in Triumphant Vikings Return

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Kirk Cousins was noticeably overcome by the moment following a dominant win over the Green Bay Packers in his return to the Minnesota Vikings on Sunday, November 15.

“It was emotional coming to the stadium,” Cousins, 38, told ESPN’s Lindsey Thiry on the field at Green Bay’s Lambeau Field after the Vikings’ 30-10 victory. “Three years ago, tearing my Achilles here. It’s been a tough three years. I was just grateful for the opportunity to play here today, and grateful for my teammates and what they did to make this outcome possible.”

Cousins was traded back to the Vikings from the Las Vegas Raiders on Wednesday, November 11.

The quarterback previously played for Minnesota for six seasons from 2018 to 2023. His final game as a member of the Vikings came in October 2023, when he tore his right Achilles tendon in a game against the Packers. Cousins spent the next two seasons with the Atlanta Falcons before signing with the Raiders in April.

While acknowledging the symbolism of the moment, Cousins urged, “There’s so much football up ahead.”

“Certainly it’s gratifying to be able to come back here with the purple on,” Cousins added. “I think to come back here with another team isn’t quite the same. To come back here with the purple on, similar time of year, three years later, it’s a blessing, to be able to have a win like we had today.”

The Vikings (7-2) are currently tied for first place in the NFC North with the Detroit Lions and Chicago Bears. With the loss on Sunday, the Packers sit alone in fourth place at 6-4.

Cousins and the Vikings return to action when they host the San Francisco 49ers on Sunday, November 22.

“He’s always been mature,” Minnesota head coach, Kevin O’Connell, said of Cousins’ performance. “He’s always been meticulous in his preparation. He’s always done everything in his power to prepare himself for games. But there was a level of poise. It never felt like at any point from the time we talked on the phone [before the trade] all the way through to giving him a game ball today, where it was anything other than just rock solid, consistent.”

Cousins finished 21-for-31 passing for 207 yards with two touchdowns and one interception.

Vikings wide receiver Justin Jefferson said Cousins “did what we needed him to do.”

“It wasn’t really too much of big shots down the field, but it was just dumping it off and getting those extra three, four yards after the catch,” Jefferson, 27, told reporters after the game. “So it was definitely great for us. And his composure, his confidence in the huddle, all of that, it definitely created a positive vibe in the huddle.”
