Mass Effect Spiritual Successor Exodus Has Revealed Its Version Of FemShep And I Have A Couple Of Questions

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It feels reductive to continually refer to Exodus, the new game from Archetype Entertainment developed by a team led by ex-BioWare developers, as a spiritual successor to Mass Effect. But it’s hard to stop calling it that when the game keeps reminding me of its now-deceased inspiration by calling the newly revealed female version of its main character FemJun.

Calling the female version of your default character “Fem+name” feels like an obvious throwback to Mass Effect and the way the community and developers alike referred to the female version of Commander ShepardÂ  as FemShep. It’s nice to be reminded of the good old days when FemShep, through the incredible performance of Jennifer Hale, became the community’s preferred version of the character (with no disrespect to the equally memorable performance of Mark Meer). But with Exodus so far removed in both time and development studio from Mass Effect, I wonder why such a distinction is even necessary. After all, the male version of Jun isn’t called MaleJun on Exodus‘ social channels, he’s just Jun.

Why are video games doing that in 2026? Why was the female version of Jun teased out like this anyway? I remember being mightily concerned that there wouldn’t be the option to play as a female character when I was first digging into Exodus. Though brief mentions of her were buried in text, she didn’t show up in any earlier videos discussing the game or its characters. Maybe the developers hadn’t finalized her look until now or were hoping that, by saving her appearance for a grand announcement like this, they could make some of the sheen of FemShep rub off on FemJun by association. Appending “Fem” to Jun feels necessary for differentiation, but it also sets up maleness as the default and everything else a deviation.

Speaking of default: Are video game developers aware that, when making a game with a customizable character, there are other ways for that default character to look besides white? Because I’m not sure they are. The default look for both Jun characters seems to be white, adding to a long and storied tradition of video games setting up white as the standard, and that’s really something we don’t need to be doing in 2026. Character creators, presumably including the one Exodus will feature, have gotten so sophisticated, with a wide variety of different hair textures, eye colors, and skin colors to choose from. Why not pay just a little bit of homage to that wealth of creative options by putting forth a character that actually makes interesting use of it? Players, when they get their hands on this game, will be able to create a character to their tastes anyway.

None of this surprises me, nor am I looking for Exodus to be some kind of “woke” standard bearer among video games. But these questions are worth asking, sad as it is that they have to keep being asked, in the hopes that maybe, perhaps by the time the events of Exodus are meant to take place, things will have changed.
