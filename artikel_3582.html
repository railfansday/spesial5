Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando’s Wedding Guest List Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Miley Cyrus and her husband, Maxx Morando, tied the knot in an intimate ceremony attended by some very familiar faces.

The “Let’s Get Married” singer and her musician beau reportedly exchanged vows in the presence of Miley’s mom, Tish Cyrus, and her husband, Dominic Purcell, as well her dad, Billy Ray Cyrus, and his partner, Elizabeth Hurley, in Palm Springs, California, on Saturday, November 21. Miley wore an all white, low-back dress whereas her groom chose a traditional tuxedo.

Miley and Morando pulled out all the stops for a memorable ceremony, reportedly enlisting her frequent collaborator Wayne Coyne, frontman of the Flaming Lips, to officiate the poolside ceremony at a private residence. Miley’s late godmother Dolly Parton‘s classic 1974 song “Love Is Like a Butterfly” was played during the wedding, per Entertainment Tonight.

“Before I met you I was lost, and, in finding you, I found myself,” Miley reportedly told Morando in her vows. “Creating with you is my all-time favorite thing, which is why I can’t wait to make a baby! I hope our baby has your hair, but mostly I hope that it has your sweet spirit.”⁠

Morando shared his own heartfelt sentiments, telling his bride, “I’m going to be the husband who makes you feel safe and supported. I want to show up for you and our family everyday… I’m a better version of myself because of you.”

Numerous outlets — including Entertainment Tonight, Just Jared and DeuxMoi — reported on which of the newlyweds’ friends and family made the cut on their wedding guest list.
