Ben Jacobs: Man Utd Want Igor Matanovic as January Transfer Chances Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

All eyes will be on Manchester United's dealings in the January transfer window as they look to give new boss Mauricio Pochettino a much-needed boost.

The former Tottenham and Chelsea boss has quite the task on his hands at Old Trafford as his new side currently sit 18th in the Premier League table after ten games.

Improving the team's backline is certainly a must and it would appear that the club are also looking at adding more firepower to their attack for the remainder of the season and beyond.

According to transfer expert Fabrizio Romano, the Red Devils "have already started exploring potential opportunities in the January transfer window for the striker position".

It comes as no surprise to see a number of names already linked with a move to Old Trafford, with The i Paper reporting that AS Monaco's Folarin Balogun and Freiburg's Igor Matanovic are of interest to the club.

Pochettino knows all about the former after working with him for the United States national team and he could possibly get the best out of Balogun if a move to Manchester United did materialise.

A Premier League move for Matanovic could well be given the green light at some point in the not too distant future with the Red Devils, Arsenal and Tottenham already linked with him.

Speaking about Matanovic earlier this year, GIVEMESPORT senior correspondent Ben Jacobs said:

He's basically like a basketball player. I think he's about six foot five, and he's done very well since joining Freiburg. He's been capped for Croatia.

The Croatia international has thrived in front of goal since moving to Freiburg from Eintracht Frankfurt in 2025.

The tall, powerful striker finished last season with 15 goals to his name in 50 appearances as he helped them reach the Europa League final against Aston Villa.

Matanovic already looks on course to beat his tally of 15 from last term as he has already netted eight times in 16 appearances in the 2026/27 season, with four of his strikes coming in the Bundesliga.

Igor Matanovic's 2026/27 season in numbers

So could Matanovic be the new striker to spearhead Pochettino's Manchester United side? He certainly has the height and physicality to be a major threat in both boxes for the Red Devils.

However, it would appear that he may not be the new striker to immediately come in and help the team in the winter market.

According to GiveMeSport transfer expert Ben Jacobs, Matanovic is unlikely to be available for a move come January.

"Igor Matanovic is on the list [for Manchester United] as well, and he is doing really well for Freiburg, but he is unlikely to be available in the winter market," said Jacobs in his latest newsletter video for GiveMeSport.

Manchester United, and other interested clubs for that matter, may well have to wait until the summer to prise Matanovic away from Freiburg.
