Trinity Rodman pulls out of USA roster for upcoming tournament just hours after shock split from Ben Shelton

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

By JAKE FENNER, US SPORTS WRITER and DAN MATTHEWS

Published: 22:24 EST, 24 November 2026 | Updated: 07:55 EST, 25 November 2026

United States women's soccer star Trinity Rodman has backed out of an upcoming tournament hours after reports revealed she is no longer in a relationship with tennis star Ben Shelton.

Rodman was set to take part in the 2026 CONCACAF W Championship window in Texas, but has now pulled out due to what US Soccer called 'a minor shoulder injury' in a statement announcing the roster move.

The statement says the injury occurred during the 2026 NWSL Championship Game last Saturday, where Rodman and the Washington Spirit fell 2-1 to the Kansas City Current. US Soccer has replaced Rodman with Gotham FC winger Jaedyn Shaw.

Rodman's removal comes hours after the Daily Mail was first to report on her split from the American tennis star.

The American sporting couple began dating around March 2025, frequently supporting one another at their various events, but have recently decided to call time on their relationship.

Their conflicting work schedules and constant travel made it too challenging for the couple to continue maintaining their relationship, the Daily Mail understands. However, the pair are believed to remain close, despite their split.
