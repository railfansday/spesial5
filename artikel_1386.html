Where Italy stand in FIFA World Rankings after latest international fixtures

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Italy will end the calendar year in 14th place in the FIFA World Rankings following a 3-1 victory at home against France and a 4-1 defeat away against Belgium in the Nations League during the November international break.

Italy began the November international break rated as the 15th best national team in the world.

The 3-1 victory at home against France temporarily saw the Azzurri rise up to 13th, but the latest update to the FIFA Men’s World Rankings, which arrived on Thursday, sees them drop back down to 14th following the completion of the latest round of international fixtures.

There have been no changes to the top four-ranked national teams since the start of the November international break: World Cup winners Spain remain top of the rankings despite their recent defeat against England, World Cup finalists Argentina remain in second, France are still in third despite their defeat against Italy and England remain in fourth.

Morocco have risen two places in the rankings up to fifth, Portugal have dropped down one place to sixth, Carlo Ancelotti’s Brazil have also dropped one place down to seventh, Belgium remain in eighth place, the Netherlands remain in ninth and Colombia have risen one place to complete the top 10.

Currently ahead of Italy in the rankings are Germany in 11th (up two places), Switzerland in 12th and Mexico in 13th (down three places).

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.

Immediately after the Win against France, Italy rose to 12th Rank (+3) and not 13th. After the Loss against Belgium, Italy dropped by two Ranks.
