The 8 highest-paid football pundits in 2026: Sky trio top the list

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Punditry work is the lucrative alternative to management for retired ex-footballers and some earn more than a pretty penny for their time.

TV companies want ex-pros to front their coverage and for some audiences, watching the after-match debate has become as entertaining as watching the actual football.

As was the case when they were playing, the best ones get paid the big bucks but who are the biggest earners in football?

We’ve done our best to put together an accurate list but it is worth noting a couple of things. First off, the BBC’s Royal Charter means that they have to declare anyone who receives over £178k so anyone earning below that will not feature in this list.

Another important point to note is that the salaries of TNT pundits like Peter Crouch and Joe Cole are far more secretive than those at Sky.

But with those caveats in mind, here are the top earners in the UK.
