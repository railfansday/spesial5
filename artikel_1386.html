Match Guide: Everton Women v Brighton and Hove Albion Women

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Everton Women host Brighton and Hove Albion in the Barclays Women’s Super League at Goodison Park on Sunday November 22 (2pm KO).

Fans attending this game have the opportunity to win a fantastic range of prizes - simply by tapping their phone! Prizes include a matchday upgrade to one of our VIP lounges, a chance to take place in the half time ‘Hit the Bar’ challenge, a post-match meet and greet with an Everton Women’s player and free tickets and upgrades for the festive fixture against Arsenal on Friday 11 December. More details HERE.

For this game, The Park End will not be used and supporters will be automatically reallocated a seat in one of the two operating stands; the Gwladys Street End and the Main Stand/Enclosure.

Supporters attending the game are also strongly advised to ensure that no valuables, cash, mobile devices or chargers are left on display in vehicles. Merseyside Police advise that car thieves are known to be targeting football fans, who are asked to take the precautionary measures outlined above.

Our comprehensive Goodison match guide, below, will help you plan your Goodison matchday and includes more on food and drink, digital tickets, travel and accessibility.

A reminder that Goodison now has a dedicated Buggy Park, to make attending games even easier for fans with young families. Buggies, strollers and prams can be booked into a safe space at the corner of the Park End and Bullens Road stands, accessed via the entrance on Goodison Road.
