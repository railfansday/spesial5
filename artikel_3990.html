What you need to know to beat Gu Wanyue, The Wailing Wraith, in Phantom Blade Zero

Fighting the Phantom Blade Zero Gu Wanyue boss is a real challenge, and she's definitely one of the hardest bosses up until this stage of the game. The main reason for this is because she's actually entirely immune to damage most of the time, with perfect parries and dodges being some of the only ways you can remove her invulnerability. In short, you need to lock in if you're to stand a chance against Gu Wanyue, and my tips below should help.

Gu Wanyue attacks hard and fast, with rapid combos and lightning-fast strikes that can shred through your health. It also doesn't help that she can partially blind you and that her damage immunity can be only temporarily removed in certain ways, so here are some pointers for dealing with her:

Gu Wanyue thankfully doesn't have a second phase, so once you've defeated her, you're done and your ears will be saved from more singing. For your victory, you'll get a Fanatic's Fragment: Fixation, which is part of an item that is needed to Soul Temper a weapon. Unfortunately, you need to find the other fragments to complete the item first.

And now you can proceed through the rest of Pangzhen by jumping down the well. Very shortly after this is where you'll find the Phantom Blade Zero Mandate Encampment Key, followed by learning how to upgrade medicine in Phantom Blade Zero. Later in Pangzhen, you can get the Phantom Blade Zero Flaming Mane and complete the Phantom Blade Zero Flesh Lily quest too.
