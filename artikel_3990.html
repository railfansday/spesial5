Witchbrook Has Officially Gone Gold

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The long-awaited cozy magical sim game Witchbrook has officially gone Gold, developer Chucklefish announced today. For those unfamiliar with the term “Gold," it generally means the game is complete and ready to be submitted to platforms for certification (and is less likely to be delayed).

Witchbrook is set to launch on January 28, ending a development cycle that stretches back nearly a decade. First teased in 2017, Witchbrook is a life-sim where players control a witch who begins life in the seaside town of Mossport. This includes making friends, finding love, and, most importantly, enrolling in Witchbrook College to master magic by taking classes and completing assignments.

We're delighted to announce that Witchbrook has GONE GOLD ALL PLATFORMS! 💛 We're can't for you all to join us on the journey on Jan 28th! ✨[image or embed]

Witchbrook is more or less Stardew Valley meets Hogwarts Legacy, as you can also start and run businesses, engage in seasonal events, and interact with fully fleshed-out citizens who have daily routines and schedules to consider. After a development cycle marked by long stretches of silence and several delays, those anticipating Witchbrook can breathe easier knowing the game will more than likely stick to its release date.

When Witchbrook launches on January 28, it will be available on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, Switch 2, Xbox One, Switch, and PC.
