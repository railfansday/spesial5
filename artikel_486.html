Black Friday 2026 at Walmart: See the ad and check out early deals

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Walmart's Black Friday sale is arriving in three waves this year, with the first big deals drop kicking off this week on Nov. 12 and running until Nov. 15. If you're eager to see what the retailer will have in store during this time, you don't even have to wait for the sale to start: a couple of the retailer's ad scans are already live, offering shoppers a little sneak peek at what's to come during its early Black Friday sale.

This three-day-long event is just the start, though. The retailer also has a main Black Friday sale planned for Nov. 23-29, plus a Cyber Monday sale running from Nov. 30 to Dec. 2. Walmart+ members can even get early access to the sale (plus exclusive online-only deals during the first 24 hours). If that's piqued your interest, check out our guide to signing up for Walmart+ to learn more.

There are quite a few early deals up that are worth a look before the sales kick off. As of the time of this writing, we've spotted discounts on AirPods Pro 3 and AirPods 4, a 65-inch LG C6 TV, and even the Lego Pokémon Pikachu and Poké Ball, which has hit an all-time low price. These are just a few highlights.

Check out more of our favorite finds below, or for a deeper dive into what's available, our rundown of early Black Friday deals at Walmart can help you out.
