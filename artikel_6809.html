The 25 Best Cocktails for Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

With a whiskey warmer, spiced sour, or comforting classic in your hand, you can get through any awkward family gathering.

Are you ready for your annual awkward family gathering? Thanksgiving is finally here, and if thinking about safe topics to discuss with all three generations of your family is making you break out in hives, then rest assured we’ve got some recipes to give you the liquid courage to face it.

You’ve got the turkey basted and roasting in the oven. You’ve got your grandmother’s perfect mac and cheese. You’ve got mashed potatoes—the best. And you’ve got store-bought pie from your slacker cousin. But, wait, do you know what you’ll be drinking for the most important feast of the year? We promise: There’s something for everyone at your dinner table on this curated list of cocktails.

Does Grandpa only abide by the saying, “If it’s brown, drink it down?” We’ve got a few whiskey drinks that will keep him on ice. Did your aunt who drinks only French reds run through the bottle she brought already? There’s a sangria recipe that’ll have her too busy sipping to snip. Need a warm cup of something strong while you “take the dog for a walk”? There’s an Irish coffee that’ll keep your wits about you. So hurry up and add these cocktail recipes to your Turkey Day menu.

This sumptuous hot cocktail makes a great Thanksgiving treat.

The margarita isn’t just a summer drink anymore.

In a cocktail shaker, combine all the ingredients with ice and shake. Rim a glass with cinnamon sugar and serve over rocks. Garnish with slices of lime and orange.

You need to keep the booze flowing to keep the mood light and the conversation going. So mix together this sangria recipe that’ll satiate any apple-cider lover.

Place apples, orange, and cloves in a large pitcher. Drizzle honey over the fruit, then add in apple cider, rum, ginger beer, and white wine. Stir to mix. Top with ice and let chill for 20 minutes before serving. Serve in glasses, including the chunks of fruit.

Whoever invented pouring bourbon into a cup of tea, we want to give you a big hug.

Boil water in a teapot. Steep star anise pod or tea bag inside. Add bourbon, honey, lemon, and Tiki bitters to a mug. Stir. Serve in glass mugs with a spoon and the teapot. To enjoy, pour tea over cocktail base to taste and stir.

This aperitivo is the perfect refreshment before you start serving those heavy Thanksgiving dishes.

A balanced spin on the Manhattan that uses Scotch whisky, this cocktail will impress any fan of brown liquor.

Simple enough to whip up in under five minutes, this cocktail has enough Scotch to knock Grandpa under the table.
