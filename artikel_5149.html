Variational’s $1.5 billion trading business has a catch even profitable traders cannot escape

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Variational’s $110 million raise targets higher trading limits and more markets on Omni as counterparty risks remain concentrated.

Variational raised $110 million and plans to use it to lift trading limits on Omni, its derivatives platform.

The company is recruiting more dealers as it expands into more stock, index, commodity, and currency markets. Omni traders will still rely on a single counterparty to pay their gains.

Bain Capital Crypto led the Series B at a $1.5 billion valuation, Variational announced on Nov. 18. CEO Lucas Schuermann said in the company’s release that stocks, indexes and commodities already accounted for more than half of Omni’s daily volume.

Variational said that an unnamed tier-one market maker already supplies liquidity for swaps. It is seeking institutions that can quote equities, commodities and currencies around the clock.

The Omni Liquidity Provider (OLP) is Variational’s in-house market maker, which quotes a price and takes the other side when a customer trades. It can then hedge that exposure with a traditional dealer or on another venue.

More dealers can help OLP quote more markets and take on more exposure, but the customer still settles with OLP.

At 20:10 UTC on Nov. 20, Omni’s public market table showed $94.6 million in open interest on the Nasdaq-100 swap and $57.1 million on the gold swap.

Variational had set the initial caps at about $10 million per market when the two swaps launched in September. It said then it planned to lift each cap to $50 million or more. The company put potential capacity under its initial dealer agreements at more than $1 billion across the arrangements.

Under the standard schedule, both swaps trade from midnight to 5 p.m. and from 6 p.m. through 11:59 p.m. Eastern time on weekdays. They close on weekends, and holidays can change trading hours.

During a closure, orders and liquidations wait until the market reopens, while around-the-clock swap trading remains a goal.

Variational reported over $1.3 billion in Omni open interest with the round. Entropy Advisors’ on-chain dashboard shows a series that includes OLP alongside one that counts only traders’ positions. On its traditional-markets chart, OLP’s side equals the traders’ side.

Omni’s public API counts both sides in the platform total and counts only traders in its per-market long and short figures. The Series B release leaves its counting basis unspecified, so lining it up with the dashboard would require matching the series and the observation time.

Each Omni trader and OLP post collateral to a separate on-chain settlement pool for that pair, and a vault holds USDC to supply OLP’s margin.

OLP uses its own capital when hedging outside the platform, leaving customers’ collateral in their pools.
