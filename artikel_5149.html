Apia, Samoa, November 20, 2026 – HTX Research, the dedicated research arm of HTX, has published a new report titled From a Price Rebound to a Recovery in Capital Flows: A Six-Month Outlook for Bitcoin and Crypto Markets.

The report addresses a central question for the months ahead: after a significant amount of leverage has been liquidated, who has the capital to absorb the remaining selling pressure? Its base case is that selling pressure will ease and BTC will attempt to stabilize first, with the durability of any recovery depending on whether sustained inflows return.

The report covers the current cycle position, macroeconomic and dollar liquidity constraints, ETF and digital asset treasury company flows, stablecoin market dynamics, on-chain cost ranges, derivatives and spot liquidity, and the divergence across crypto assets. It also sets out the key observation windows from late November 2026 through May 2027, along with conditional scenarios for the six-month period.
