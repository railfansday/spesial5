XRP rose 4.0% in the past 24 hours to $0.9664, according to data. The bounce has not erased the damage as the token is still down 14.1% over the week.

The rebound comes after XRP broke a support zone that had held since June. The question for traders now is whether this is the start of a recovery or just a pause in the decline.

On the daily chart, XRP broke clearly below the $0.99 to $1.02 zone. That area marked the lows the token set in June and August this year, and it was the last major support level left.

The daily candle closed well below that zone, which confirmed the breakdown. According to analyst Josh of Crypto World, the old floor will likely turn into a ceiling and any bounce in the coming days or weeks could stall between $0.99 and $1.02.

The selling has run into a major Fibonacci level on the weekly chart near $0.93. Whether it has held depends on the timeframe:

Today’s 4% gain has lifted the price back above $0.93. That keeps the weekly support intact for now.

XRP is now extremely oversold on the daily chart, meaning the selling has run far and fast in a short time. Conditions like this often lead to at least a short pause in the decline.

The weekly chart may also be forming a larger bullish divergence, where momentum indicators rise even as the price makes new lows. The signal is not confirmed yet. It only becomes meaningful if it keeps developing over the coming weeks.

XRP Price Prediction: A Pause, Not a Reversal

The base case is a short break in the crash rather than a full turnaround. XRP could hold its recent lows for a few days or even a few weeks, but a strong rally back toward its yearly highs looks unlikely in the near term.

The market remains in a cool-off phase with little bullish momentum.
