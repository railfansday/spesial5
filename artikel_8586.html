Get the Truly Simple® Credit Card if you bank with Fifth Third and can pay off a balance or a big purchase before its intro rate ends. Purchases and balance transfers both get 0% for 18 months, and the card has no annual fee. If you need more time, pick a card with a longer 0% period.

The Truly Simple is Fifth Third’s card for borrowing at 0% rather than earning rewards. If you move $5,000 from a card charging 22% and repay it in equal monthly payments over the intro period, you pay a $200 transfer fee instead of about $915 in interest, a saving of about $715.

It’s the pick for Fifth Third customers who can clear the balance before the 0% ends and want to keep the debt with the bank they already use. The Reflect, at 0% for 21 months, suits borrowers who need more time, and a cash back credit card suits those who pay in full each month.
