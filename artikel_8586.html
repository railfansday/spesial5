Crew Review: High-Yield Checking With Built-In Budgeting for Families

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Some products in this article are from our partners. Read our Advertiser Discloser.

Managing money as a household is harder than it looks. Most people use a regular checking account at a big bank, then juggle a separate budgeting app, maybe a savings account somewhere else, and possibly a kids’ debit card on top of that. It’s a lot to keep track of.

Crew is a fintech app that tries to put all of that in one place. It combines a high-yield checking account, built-in budgeting tools, and family banking features – all inside a single app. The goal is to help you organize your money, automate your budget, and earn more interest without switching between apps.

In this Crew review, we cover what it is, how it works, who it’s best for, the key features, pricing, and what real users are saying. If you’re looking for a smarter checking account that does more, this review gives you the full picture.

Crew is a financial technology (fintech) company based in Lehi, Utah. It was founded by Gentry Davies and Steve Domino, who previously worked at Divvy, a business expense platform acquired by Bill.com. The app launched publicly and has been growing steadily since 2023.

Crew is not a bank. Banking services are provided by Bangor Savings Bank, which is FDIC-insured. Your deposits are held at Bangor Savings Bank, so they are protected up to federal insurance limits – $250,000 for individual accounts and $500,000 for joint accounts.

The app is available on both iOS and Android. A web app is currently in beta.

Crew works well for a wide range of people, but it’s especially useful if you fall into one of these groups:

Crew offers a checking account that earns 2.95% APY on your full balance. This rate applies to every dollar you hold in the account – there’s no minimum deposit requirement and no tiered rate structure.

Why it matters: Most big bank checking accounts pay close to 0.01% APY. Earning nearly 3% on your everyday checking balance can add meaningful interest over time, especially for families keeping a few thousand dollars in their account at any given time.

Pockets are virtual “buckets” inside your single Crew checking account. You can create as many as you need – for example, one for bills, one for savings, one for a vacation fund, one for groceries.

Why it matters: You don’t need a separate savings account or multiple bank accounts. Pockets let you visually and mentally divide your money without moving it to another institution. You can also lock a pocket in “piggy bank mode” to avoid dipping into it.

Crew’s Autopilot feature lets you create rules that move money automatically based on triggers. Triggers can be events like a paycheck arriving or a balance dropping below a set level, or they can be time-based, like every Friday or the 1st of the month.

Why it matters: Automation removes the discipline requirement from budgeting. You set your rules once, and Crew handles the rest. For example, you can tell Crew to automatically send 10% of every paycheck to your giving pocket, $500 to bills, and keep the rest in checking — without touching the app every payday.

Crew supports joint accounts for couples and child accounts for kids. Each child gets their own Crew account with a debit card that has their name on it. Kids can log into the app with their own login to check their balance and track spending.

Why it matters: Most banking apps bolt on a kid’s account as an afterthought. Crew builds family banking into the core experience. Parents get real-time spending notifications, spend controls, and the ability to set automated allowances.

Parents can set up automatic recurring transfers to a child’s account. You define the amount and the schedule, and Crew handles the delivery.

Why it matters: Automated allowances teach kids about money while removing the “I forgot to pay your allowance” problem for parents. Kids learn what consistent saving looks like in practice.

Every account member gets a Mastercard debit card. You can switch which pocket your card draws from at any time inside the app – or update a transaction after the fact if you accidentally spent from the wrong pocket.

Why it matters: This setup means budgeting is “baked in” to your spending. Instead of tracking what you spent after the fact, you decide which budget category you’re spending from before or at the time of purchase.

You can use your Crew Mastercard at any ATM in the Mastercard, Maestro, or Cirrus networks. Crew does not have its own fee-free ATM network, but it reimburses ATM fees for up to 20 withdrawals per year.

Why it matters: The annual ATM fee reimbursement covers most casual cash users without adding much complexity.

Crew supports outgoing wire transfers. The fee depends on your total account balance.

Incoming wires and standard ACH transfers are processed as usual through the app.

Deposits held through Crew are insured through Bangor Savings Bank, a Member FDIC institution.

This is standard FDIC pass-through insurance. Crew’s website states that pass-through insurance is subject to certain conditions set forth in the deposit agreement.

Crew includes an AI-powered financial assistant called Penny. Penny can answer questions about your account, help you set up automations, and give you guidance on your financial situation.

Why it matters: Penny gives users a simple way to interact with their finances through natural language, without digging through menus.

User feedback for Crew is largely positive, particularly among families and people who value built-in budgeting features.

Crew’s iOS app carries consistently high ratings. Users describe it as well-designed and easy to navigate. Family features are frequently praised, with parents noting that kids enjoy having their own card and app login.

Reviewers across platforms repeatedly mention Crew as a genuine alternative to maintaining separate banking and budgeting apps. The combination of high-yield checking, automatic paycheck splitting, and child accounts in one place is the most-cited reason for switching from other banks or apps.

The most consistent piece of constructive feedback is that Crew is still a young product and some features – like the web app – are still catching up to the mobile experience.

Crew is a well-designed fintech product that combines several things that usually require multiple apps or accounts: a high-yield checking account, budgeting through pockets, spending automation, and family banking.

Overall, Crew fills a genuine gap. Most checking accounts don’t pay meaningful interest and don’t have built-in budgeting. Crew does both, and it adds family banking on top. For households looking to simplify their financial toolkit and earn more interest on everyday balances, it’s worth a serious look.
