All-inclusive resorts take the guess work out of a vacation, allowing travelers to focus on relaxing rather than feeling nickel-and-dimed at every turn. And thankfully, many of these dreamy properties are on sale for Black Friday and Cyber Monday.

From discounted stays on JW Marriott’s first all-inclusive in Costa Rica to spa and airfare credits on adults-only Sandals Resorts, kids-stay-free offers at a family-friendly Colorado dude ranch and more, there’s a deal for everyone.

This is a roundup of some of the best post-Thanksgiving deals for all-inclusive resorts across the globe.

Related: 15 best all-inclusive resorts in the US

Marriott operates all-inclusives across several of the hotel group’s brands from The Westin to JW Marriott, W Hotels and beyond. From Nov. 24 through Dec. 1, Marriott Bonvoy members can save 25 percent on stays when they book through the Marriott Bonvoy app. Both members and non-members who book on Marriott.com can save 15 percent off their stay. The deal is available for travel from Nov. 29 through May 2, 2027.

The sale is valid on participating properties, which include several popular options like the JW Marriott Costa Elena Resort & Spa, All-Inclusive in Costa Rica; Sanctuary Cap Cana, a Luxury Collection Resort, Dominican Republic, Adult All-Inclusive; Marriott Cancun, an All-Inclusive Resort; the W Punta Cana, Adult All-Inclusive; The Westin Playa Vallarta, an All-Inclusive Resort and more.

Through Nov. 22, 2026, families who book an all-inclusive Hard Rock Hotel can save up to 55 percent, bring kids and teens for free and score perks like early check-in, room upgrades, free round-trip transportation and express manicures or pedicures. Travelers who book a stay of seven nights or longer will also receive unlimited resort credits.

The deals are available for stays through Dec. 31, 2027, with perks varying by property, including for Hard Rock Hotel Riviera Maya, Hard Rock Hotel Cancun, Hard Rock Hotel Los Cabos, Hard Rock Hotel Punta Cana and Hard Rock Hotel Vallarta.

These adults-only all-inclusives, which operate properties in Jamaica and Mexico, are offering discounts and perks for Black Friday when travelers book by Nov. 22. At UNICO Montego Bay, travelers can save up to 50 percent off, receive a $200 inclusion resort credit, complimentary room upgrades, complimentary ground transportation, access to the hydrotherapy circuit and more for stays between March 1, 2027, and Dec. 31, 2027.

At UNICO Riviera Nayarit and UNICO Riviera Maya, travelers who stay through Dec. 31, 2027, can score up to 35 percent off their stay, plus receive a $200 daily inclusion resort credit, a complimentary beauty salon service, free room upgrades and more.

With more than a dozen adults-only properties throughout the Caribbean, Sandals is offering up to 65 percent off stays for travelers who book by Dec. 1. In addition, travelers can get up to  $2,000 in instant credit along with a $250 spa credit when they stay five or more nights by Dec. 31, 2028. The company is also offering up to a $750 air credit for stays between March 1, 2027, and Oct. 2, 2027. Blackout dates and minimum stay requirements apply for the sale.

From Sesame Street-themed programming to kids and teen camps, waterslides and lazy rivers and more, there is no shortage of fun in the sun at these family-friendly all-inclusives. And from now through Dec. 1, travelers who book can receive up to 65 percent off their stay, up to $1,400 in instant credit, a $250 spa credit on stays of five or more nights, and up to a $750 air credit. Minimum stay requirements and blackout dates vary based on the perk, but travelers hoping to take advantage of the instant credit must travel by Dec. 31, 2028.

These luxury all-inclusive properties are dotted across Mexico and boast amenities like Michelin-starred dining, concierge service at the pools and beaches, kids and teens’ clubs and more. And now, travelers can save up to 30 percent off nightly rates with free stays for kids at participating family-friendly properties.

Different locations are also offering property-specific perks, like a $50 spa credit per adult, per night and a complimentary lifestyle photography session at the Grand Velas Riviera Maya; and a $50 spa credit per night, a $50 experience credit per suite and half-off round-trip airport transportation at the Grand Velas Boutique Los Cabos.

To take advantage, travelers must register by Nov. 14. The main Black Friday sale then runs from Nov. 17 to Dec. 3.

With more than two dozen resorts spread across several of the Caribbean’s most popular destinations, Royalton Hotels & Resorts offers a Black Friday deal for everyone. The deals vary by property, and include perks like kids stay free in family suites. The deal is valid on bookings made by Nov. 22 for stays through Dec. 31, 2027.

This collection of independent hotels boasts stays across the globe, including several all-inclusives from the British Virgin Islands to South Africa. For Black Friday, the group is offering 35 percent off the best available rates when travelers book a minimum two-night stay at participating hotels. These include more than a dozen all-inclusive properties, including Mexico’s Haven Riviera Cancun, California’s The Clement Palo Alto and beyond. To take advantage, travelers must book between Nov. 23 and Dec. 3 and travel by June 30, 2027.

Set in Granby, Colorado, this all-inclusive dude ranch boasts everything from horseback riding, archery and fly fishing in the summer to snowshoeing and sleigh rides in the winter. And for travelers who book between Nov. 20 and Dec. 4, the property is welcoming kids to stay for free with one free child per paying adult. The deal is valid on stays from Jan. 21, 2027, through April 25, 2027.

These adults-only escapes are located across Mexico, Jamaica and the Dominican Republic, and are offering more than 50 percent off when travelers book by Dec. 7. Travelers who are part of The Excellence Collection Rewards loyalty program can also snag additional discounts and free suite upgrades. The sale is valid on stays between March 1, 2027, and Dec. 20, 2027.
