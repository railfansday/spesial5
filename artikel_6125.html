Nithya Raman is projected to defeat incumbent Karen Bass in the Los Angeles mayoral race, a result that complicates Airbnb's effort to loosen the city's short-term rental rules before the 2028 Olympics. Skift previously reported that Airbnb and its nonprofit spent more than $12 million on Los Angeles politics, including committees backing Bass and several council candidates. Los Angeles has restricted short-term rental listings to primary residences since 2018, and Bass's proposal to extend them to second homes was dropped from the budget adopted in May. Raman has accused Airbnb of "pay-to-play" politics and called for stricter enforcement. Bernstein senior analyst Richard Clarke said Raman is more likely to preserve the status quo on short-term rental restrictions, though he described the financial downside for Airbnb as probably fairly minimal — Airbnb CEO Brian Chesky said in 2023 that no single city accounted for more than 1.5% of revenue. The stakes are the roughly 2 million visitors the Games are expected to draw to the greater Los Angeles area, and as much as $4.3 billion in direct visitor spending, according to a study commissioned by LA28.
