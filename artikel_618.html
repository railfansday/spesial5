Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade Postponed as Storm Descends on Northeast

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The annual event is one of the most-watched TV events of the year for NBC.

In an unprecedented move, the 100th edition of the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York City will be postponed until Saturday Nov. 28, Macy’s and NBC announced Wednesday.

The decision comes as a powerful nor’easter storm is bearing down on the northeast, with potential wind gusts north of 60 miles per hour alongside heavy rain.

The parade is now expected to kick off on the Upper West Side at 8:30 a.m. on Saturday, with the floats, balloons and performers making their way down to Macy’s flagship store on 34th Street. NBC confirmed that it will broadcast the parade Saturday at 8:30 a.m. to all time zones; it will also stream on Peacock.

“Due to the extreme weather conditions, the city of New York, Macy’s and NBC have decided, in the interest of safety for both the participants and spectators, to move the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade to Saturday, Nov. 28,” an NBC spokesperson told The Hollywood Reporter. “More details to follow. In addition, NBC will telecast the 2025 Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade tomorrow at 8:30 a.m. in all time zones.”

It’s also a logistical nightmare, with many performance groups and musical artists coming into town for the parade, and which may need to find alternative travel plans.

The parade has never been canceled in its 100 years, though some of the iconic giant balloons have been grounded when there have been high winds. Balloons are supposed to be grounded if wind gusts top 34 miles per hour.

In 1993, a Sonic the Hedgehog balloon was pushed into a lamppost amid high winds, injuring multiple people, and a similar incident occurred in 1997 with a Cat in the Hat balloon.

This year’s postponement is also a first. New York City officials and Macy’s executives are expected to address the postponement later on Wednesday.
