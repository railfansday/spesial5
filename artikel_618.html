Denis Villeneuve Pledges Traditional, “Unapologetic” Bond: “Not Here to Change the DNA”

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The director teases a classic approach to his Amazon MGM film starring Robert Pattinson: "I love the way the character behaves."

Denis Villeneuve says his highly anticipated reboot of the James Bond franchise isn’t going to back away from the “unapologetic” traits of the iconic character.

The Dune: Part Three director teased his vision of 007 in a new interview and suggested that he’s a traditionalist when it comes to portraying Ian Fleming’s suave, seductive, killer spy.

“I love traditions,” Villeneuve told Time. “I love the unapologetic way the character behaves, the panache. I’m not here to change the DNA of James Bond. I’m here to embrace it, to bring it to the world of today. I’m a hardcore James Bond fan, and I want other fans to have fun watching my film. Of course, it will be tainted by my sensibility. But I wake up every morning with a stupid smile because it’s a world of pure cinematic pleasure.” Related Stories Movies 'Twilight' Director Reveals Robert Pattinson Inspired James Bond Line in 2008 Film: "We Were Seeing the Future" Movies 'Avengers: Doomsday' Eyes $270M Opening as 'Dune: Part Three' Also Heads for Big Start

That said, the director added, “I don’t want to talk too much about Bond because it’s still in the oven cooking. This stage of the process is my favorite part because everything is possible.”

The comments are interesting because of the cultural debate that’s surrounded Bond in recent years, and how the later Daniel Craig films attempted to wrestle with the question of how the often sexist cinematic character dating back to the 1960s should operate in the modern age.

How much Villeneuve was thinking of Pattinson during his comments is unclear — the interview was conducted one week before The Batman actor was publicly announced as the new Bond.

Meanwhile, speculation has shifted to who will play the film’s villain, with oddsmakers thinking Tom Hardy has a strong shot (indeed, given Pattinson’s slight frame, choosing a physically imposing villain would make for a nicely formidable antagonist). Director Danny Boyle — who was originally tapped to direct No Time to Die — has said that “beginning to talk about Jack O’Connell as a possible villain.” O’Connell was previously considered a contender to play Bond himself.

Boyle told The Alchemy podcast that both Pattinson and O’Connell have a similar quality. “They have that thing [where] you want to watch them,” he said. “And it’s impossible to quantify what it is; otherwise we’d all buy it for ourselves. But it’s just there and you want to follow them.”
