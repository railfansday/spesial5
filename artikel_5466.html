Anthropic's IPO filing shows Amazon and Google will own about 28% of its stock

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Amazon is set to hold nearly 15% and Google about 14% after the IPO and a roughly $26 billion private placement

Amazon $AMZN and Alphabet $GOOGL's Google are set to own about 28% of Anthropic stock in aggregate after the company's IPO and a concurrent private placement, according to Bloomberg.

Amazon would hold nearly 15% of Anthropic's outstanding capital stock and Google about 14%, according to Bloomberg. Google's stake takes in non-voting stock worth around $10 billion, which it committed to purchase in a private placement worth about $26 billion that is running in tandem with the IPO.

Google will hold 32% of Anthropic’s non-voting Class N stock, and Amazon will hold 34%, according to Bloomberg. Amazon’s holdings would climb to 24% of all outstanding stock if the company lifted its ownership cap and converted every one of its notes. The agreement allows Amazon to convert notes only if doing so wouldn’t push its overall stake above 12%, and Amazon has committed to leaving the cap in place.

Anthropic and its co-founders could raise as much as $64.4 billion through the IPO at the top of the range, according to Bloomberg. Counting the private placement as well, the sale would be the largest initial share offering on record, topping SpaceX's base offering of $75 billion in June.

The IPO is scheduled to be priced Wednesday, Nov. 18, with shares starting to trade a day later, according to Bloomberg. Goldman Sachs $GS and Morgan Stanley $MS are leading the offering alongside 20 other banks, and Anthropic shares are expected to list with the ticker ANT on the Nasdaq $NDAQ Global Select Market.

Anthropic launched the roadshow for its IPO on Nov. 10 with an expected price range of $70 to $78 a share. At the top of that range, the company could carry a fully diluted valuation as high as $1.405 trillion. Microsoft $MSFT and Google are among seven companies that agreed to buy $26 billion of Class N common stock in the private placement at the IPO price, a purchase that depends on the IPO being completed.

The filing says Amazon, Google and Microsoft each wear several hats for Anthropic, backing it financially, buying its services, helping distribute its products and competing against it, according to Bloomberg. Customers who buy access to Claude through accounts with the three cloud providers generated about 44% of Anthropic's revenue over the opening nine months of 2026.

Anthropic also disclosed lock-up agreements that restrict certain holders from selling or otherwise transferring stock in connection with the offering without written consent from the underwriters' representatives, with listed exceptions.
