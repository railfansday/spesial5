Oil prices slip as U.S. inventory build and supply glut fears outweigh Mideast risks

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Iran said its drones hit a U.S. radar base in Bahrain, while U.S. Central Command said they were intercepted without damage

Brent crude slid 1.9% to $91.38 a barrel and U.S. West Texas Intermediate futures lost 2.6% to $83.80 in early European trading on Wednesday, as a larger U.S. stockpile and bets on a market with too much supply outweighed ongoing strikes on tankers in the Middle East.

Figures from the American Petroleum Institute point to a 5.3-million-barrel increase in U.S. crude stockpiles, according to The Wall Street Journal.

"Further disruptions around the Strait of Hormuz remain an important upside risk, but rising OPEC+ output, resilient Russian exports and building U.S. inventories point to a more comfortable supply picture," analysts at ING said, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Saudi Arabia has lifted its output to 10.5 million barrels a day, citing the latest reports, Alex Kuptsikevich, chief market analyst at FxPro, told The Wall Street Journal in an email. Kuptsikevich added that there are reports of higher output in Iraq and Kuwait. He said Mideast tensions may limit price losses.

Iran said it struck a U.S. radar base in Bahrain with drones, according to The Wall Street Journal. U.S. Central Command posted on X $TWTR that the drones were intercepted "without any damage to U.S. equipment or injury to American personnel."

The International Energy Agency said on Nov. 13 that global oil supply exceeded demand in October, the first time since the war in Iran began. Global supply rose by 2.3 million barrels a day to 105.4 million barrels a day, and observed inventories rose by 52 million barrels, the first increase since June.

The agency expects fourth-quarter supply to average 105 million barrels a day, 1.7 million barrels a day above forecast demand. It said attacks on shipping threaten the recovery.

Crude had edged lower on Monday, with Brent at $95.32 a barrel and West Texas Intermediate at $88.29 as of 6:14 a.m. ET.
