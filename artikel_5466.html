Homeowners are tapping home equity lines of credit at the fastest pace in decades

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Total U.S. household debt rose 1.8% to $19.1 trillion in the quarter, and 5.0% of outstanding debt was in some stage of delinquency

The Federal Reserve Bank of New York reported that home equity line of credit balances rose $23 billion in the third quarter, the biggest quarterly jump since 2005. The figures come from the bank's Quarterly Report on Household Debt and Credit, released on Tuesday.

Home equity line of credit, or HELOC, balances totaled $481 billion at the end of the quarter, up $59 billion from a year earlier. HELOC limits rose $31 billion in the quarter, continuing an expansion that began in 2022.

The quarterly rise was the 18th in a row, according to CNBC.

Total household debt grew $334 billion, or 1.8%, to $19.1 trillion. Mortgage balances increased $257 billion to $13.4 trillion, and mortgage originations reached $595 billion.

"Household debt levels rose moderately, led by increases in mortgage and home equity line of credit originations," Donghoon Lee, economic research advisor at the New York Fed, said in a statement. "Although delinquency transition rates for housing debts have crept up, the overall credit quality of these loans remains strong."

Overall, 5.0% of outstanding debt was in some stage of delinquency, up 0.3 percentage point from the previous quarter. The share of HELOC balances moving into serious delinquency, meaning 90 days or more past due, was 1.17%, compared with 1.27% a year earlier.

The increase in HELOC balances is occurring while the housing market remains stagnant and numerous owners are unwilling to trade away their low mortgage rates, according to CNBC. HELOCs let borrowers draw on their equity while keeping those rates.

"HELOCs let them borrow against their equity without giving up the low interest rate on their home," certified financial planner Douglas Boneparth, president and founder of Bone Fide Wealth, told CNBC.

HELOCs generally carry variable interest rates, according to CNBC. The average rate on a $30,000 HELOC stood at 7.49% on Nov. 4. Many HELOCs have a draw period during which borrowers typically pay only interest, followed by a repayment period that adds principal.

"Payments potentially could double overnight, and you really want to know that before you sign anything," Boneparth said.

The housing market has weakened since mortgage rates returned to 7% in mid-September, and an index tracking contract signings for previously owned homes hit a record low in September. Thirty-year mortgage rates averaged 7.33% in October, 110 basis points above a year earlier.
