Bethell ruled out of South Africa tour

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Jacob Bethell has been ruled out of the tour of South Africa, with the England batter set to undergo knee surgery; Bethell has not featured since since July and suffered another setback with a flare-up after joining up with the white-ball squad in Australia

England batter Jacob Bethell has been ruled out of the tour of South Africa as he is set to undergo more surgery on a knee issue.

Bethell has not featured since July because of a right knee problem, which required an operation over the summer.

The 23-year-old had joined up with England's white-ball squad in Perth last week ahead of his planned comeback before the three-Test trip to South Africa in December.

However, following a flare-up in his knee, Bethell returned to England for assessment by a specialist and will now need another procedure.

An England and Wales Cricket Board spokesperson said: "Jacob Bethell will undergo surgery on his right knee this week and has been ruled out of the tour of South Africa."

There is understood to be no initial timeframe set on Bethell's return to action. A decision on a replacement will be made once England are in South Africa.

Left-hander Bethell had started 2026 with a fine century in the final Ashes Test in Sydney, but is now set to miss out on seven consecutive series across all formats.

With Bethell's continued absence, Tom Banton will have the chance to continue opening in the 50-over side while Emilio Gay and Jordan Cox remain in contention for the Test XI.
