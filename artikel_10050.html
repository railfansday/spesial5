The vast majority of what you're referring to are delta configuration, not tadpole configuration. They have radically different driving dynamics. A tadpole configuration has almost the exact same driving dynamics as a four-wheeled vehicle, because as you brake, your CG (already rather forward) shifts further forward, toward between the two front wheels. In a delta trike, however, that forward-shifted CG basically induces a pivot on a single point (the single front wheel), making them highly unstable. Also, modern EVs are inherently rollover to begin with, due to their low CG. A tadpole EV is well more rollover resistant than a four-wheeled ICE vehicle.

Losing the second rear wheel isn't simply regulatory, it has some pretty dramatic mass and aero benefits.
