Bills acquire playmaking safety Amani Hooker from Titans, send second-round pick in return

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

With their sights set on a Super Bowl title, the Buffalo Bills are adding a dynamic defensive playmaker in trading for Tennessee Titans safety Amani Hooker.

The Bills announced at Tuesday's NFL trade deadline that they are sending the Titans a 2027 second-round draft pick and will also receive a 2027 fifth-round selection in return to acquire Hooker. The Bills also inquired about eight-time Pro Bowl Cardinals safety Budda Baker before trading for Hooker, NFL Insider Jordan Schultz reports.

The move bolsters a defense that ranks 29th in the NFL in passing yards allowed per game. The Bills have one of the league's best offenses helmed by an MVP quarterback in Josh Allen, but their pass defense has been a liability throughout the season.

Trading a second-round pick to acquire Hooker is a steep price, but the move addresses a clear area of need for a Super Bowl contender.

Hooker, 28, is a difference-maker in the secondary. He joins the Bills tied for the league lead with five interceptions alongside six passes defended. The interception tally also ties a career high through just seven games. Hooker has 17 interceptions and 45 passes defended in his career.

Hooker is also an accomplished defender against the run. He ranks second on the Titans this season with 52 total tackles. He projects to plug in as an immediate starter for the Bills.

An eighth-year veteran, Hooker has played his entire NFL career for the Titans since they selected him in the fourth round of the 2019 draft.

For the Titans, they part with a long-term veteran leader and one of their best players. But Hooker is best suited to contribute to a team ready to win now, and at 1-7, the Titans are not that. A second-round pick is a valuable asset as they seek to continue to rebuild around second-year quarterback Cam Ward.
