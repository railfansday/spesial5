Detroit Lions plan to activate, play Kerby Joseph this week vs. Buccaneers

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Detroit Lions safety Kerby Joseph has not played in a football game in 401 days, but it appears that counter will drop to zero this upcoming weekend.

On his weekly radio appearance on 97.1 The Ticket, Lions coach Dan Campbell said the expectations—barring any setback—is for Joseph to be activated from the Physically Unable to Perform list this week and play in Sunday's game against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers.

"Barring anything else here negatively—which there hasn't been, he's progressed nicely here—the plan would be to get him going this week. Get him going and get him into Tampa Bay and let him play," Campbell said.

Joseph has been dealing with a knee injury that limited his play early in 2025 and was eventually painful enough to cause him to miss the final 11 games of the season. Since then, Joseph has been working tirelessly to build enough strength and stability in his knee for the Lions to be comfortable enough to put him back on the field. The Lions returned him to practice three weeks ago, and they've been encouraged by his progress.

"He looked a lot better than what I anticipated him looking (like)," Lions safety coach Jim O'Neil said a couple weeks ago. "So it was very encouraging. So now he's gotta stack multiple days. He's got to stack them, so today will be a big day for him, but he looked awesome yesterday at practice."

It's unclear how much of a role Joseph will have upon his return, as the Lions have a pretty deep bench at safety. Not only did Brian Branch return to the lineup a few weeks ago, but veteran Chuck Clark has started to play better, and Detroit did just trade for former Jets safety Andre Cisco—who saw the field on defense last week in Germany. Regardless, it sounds like Detroit will have their All-Pro safety back in some capacity.

On top of providing an update on Joseph, Campbell noted that center Cade Mays (ankle) is unlikely to play this week, but he's still very encouraged by how backup Juice Scruggs is playing.

"I don't see Cade being ready this week. There's a chance, but I'm not putting all my eggs in that basket," Campbell said. "And Juice has been doing a nice job for us. He's playing really good football. That whole o-line played dang good football the other day."
