The Inaugural Big Ten Volleyball Tournament Begins TOMORROW!

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The post season is here and is coming in hot as the top 15 teams in the Big Ten will converge on Fishers, Indiana starting on Friday, the 20th, and ending on Wednesday, the 25th, with the championship match. The bracket was just set after Tuesday nights slate of matches.

The Huskers will get the first two days off since they are the #1 seed with a triple bye. They will have to wait to find out who their opponent is until Friday afternoon when #8 Indiana and #9 UCLA face off. The Huskers will then get the winner of this match. Both teams started off the year pretty strong, but have had skids that unfortunately landed them in the lower seeded spots.

Rosters will be available below with links to team websites and stats. Links to articles from the match against each team will be linked as well for information since there will be multiple matches this weekend and not a lot of time for previews on each match.
