Nvidia to probe reports that Chinese intelligence targeted CEO Jensen Huang

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Nvidia’s board said Sunday it would investigate reports that CEO Jensen Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence operation. The New York Times reported Huang had an affair with an executive US intelligence believed was gathering information for Beijing. Huang will remain CEO during the inquiry and has pledged cooperation.
