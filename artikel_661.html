‘Being Heumann’ Producers Team With Marc Furmie, Rezistor Films on Cult Comic ‘Deadworld’ Film — Film News in Brief

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

“Being Heumann” producers Kevin Cleary and John W. Beach, of Gravity Squared Entertainment, are joining with Marc Furmie of Rezistor Films to adapt and produce “Deadworld.”

Furmie will direct from his own screenplay, an adaptation based on the original cult comic franchise from Caliber Entertainment. “Deadworld” is a post-apocalyptic road movie following a group of juvenile delinquents. The ragtag band accepts a mission to deliver a scientist and a child to a distant refuge but soon discover they are the last line of defense against a horde of demons intent on opening a dimensional gate to hell.

“This is a passion project for me that I have been developing with Caliber for several years now, and I am excited to work with Kevin and John to bring a very ambitious vision to life,” Furmie said. “We’ve designed an edgy and commercial epic, driven by a Gen-Z / Gen-Alpha cast, that is heavily inspired by Mad Max: Fury Road, Come and See and Seven Samurai. After years of grinding on smaller films in the US, Deadworld will allow me the opportunity to return home to Australia and work with my favorite collaborators to push the envelope on a larger canvas.”

Designed as a launch point for a wider franchise, the film adapts the first story arc of Caliber’s long-running series, which spans more than 100 issues and 20+ graphic novels. Cleary, Beach and Furmie produce. Jason Mavraidis and Ty Linegar of Rezistor Films and Eric Reichert of Caliber executive produce.

Tubi’s upcoming horror-comedy “Do You Like Me?” has cast Josh Server (“All That,” “The Neighborhood”), Yasmine Sahid (“Never Have I Spent the Winter with the Romano Brothers”), Tyler Gaca and Alexander Huntley.

Set to release in 2027, the film follows friends-with-benefits Owen (Gaca) and Sam (Huntley) whose camping trip takes a deadly turn when they become stranded in the woods and seek shelter in a stranger’s cabin.

The film also stars Curtis Edward Jackson, Narea Kang and Fadeke Oparinde with a screenplay by Gaca and Huntley. Alexander Haessner serves as director and director of photography. “Do You Like Me?” is a production of Bohemia Group Originals and Outshine Entertainment, with Justin Ross and Kiah Stern producing. Shawn Balentine serves as co-producer.

Newton Thomas Sigel will be honored with the Lifetime Achievement Award from the American Society of Cinematographers.

Sigel photographed “Bohemian Rhapsody,” which earned him a BAFTA nomination and went on to be one of the highest-grossing music biopics ever. His work with director Bryan Singer on “The Usual Suspects” kickstarted a partnership spanning 10 films, including “Superman Returns,” “Valkyrie” and four “X-Men” titles. Other credits include “Blood and Wine,” “Fallen,” “Confessions of a Dangerous Mind,” “Leatherheads,” “The Brothers Grimm,” “Towelhead,” “Da 5 Bloods” and “Marshall.”

“Tom Sigel has consistently elevated the art of cinematography through his bold visual storytelling and deep commitment to collaboration,” said ASC President Shelly Johnson. “His contributions span an extraordinary range of films and genres, each marked by a clarity of vision and a profound respect for the craft. This Lifetime Achievement Award celebrates not only his remarkable accomplishments, but the enduring legacy he has built within the cinematography community.”

The award will be presented at the 41st Annual Outstanding Achievement in Cinematography Gala on March 7, 2027, which will be held at The Beverly Hilton in Beverly Hills, California, and livestreamed globally. Other honorees include Robert McLachlan, Dejan Georgevich, Julianne Grosso and the Tiffen Company.

McLachlan will receive the Career Achievement in Television Award, Georgevich will be presented with the President’s Award, Grosso will receive an Award of Distinction and Tiffen will be recognized with the Curtis Clark Technology Award to spotlight its creative and cultural influence on cinematography.

The creative and technical team behind Christopher Nolan’s “The Odyssey,” along with Patricia and the late David Keighley, will be honored at the Hollywood Professional Association Awards on Dec. 3.

“The Odyssey” creative team will receive the HPA Judges Award for Creativity and Innovation. The award recognizes companies and individuals who have made contributions to creative storytelling and technical innovation. It is not awarded annually but reserved for groundbreaking work.

Producer Emma Thomas will accept the award on behalf of the team, led by Nolan and including cinematographer Hoyte van Hoytema, editor Jennifer Lame, production designer Ruth De Jong, costume designer Ellen Mirojnick, composer Ludwig Göransson, visual effects supervisor Andrew Jackson, special effects supervisor Scott R. Fisher, supervising sound editor Craig Henighan, production sound mixer Willie Burton, rerecording mixer Gary Rizzo, super supervising dialogue & ADR editor David Cutler, casting director John Papsidera, makeup designer Luisa Abel and hair designer Gloria Casny.

Imax Chief Quality Guru Patricia Keighley and the late David Keighley will receive the HPA Legacy Award, which recognizes leaders, mentors and influencers who contribute to the industry. The award was first presented in 2019 to veteran journalist Peter Caranicas.

For more than 50 years, the Keighleys have worked on post-production for over 500 large-format documentary and feature films. They created and oversaw the development of the next-generation Imax Film Camera used to shoot “The Odyssey.”

The HPA Awards will be held Dec. 3 at 6 p.m. at the ASC Clubhouse in Hollywood.

‘The Eyes of Ghana’ Reaches 1 Million Views on YouTube in One Day

Ben Proudfoot’s acclaimed documentary “The Eyes of Ghana” has reached 1 million views on IShowSpeed’s YouTube channel in a single day.

The Breakwater Studios, Higher Ground and IShowSpeed production was released on YouTube on Tuesday following a livestream announcement the previous day between filmmakers Ben Proudfoot and Nana Adwoa Frimpong, Ethan Lewis from Higher Ground, Yaw Amponsah Apenteng, subject Chris Hesse, President Barack Obama and IShowSpeed.

The documentary follows Ghanaian cameraman Chris Hesse’s mission to rescue a hidden film archive documenting the birth of African independence. It is produced by IShowSpeed under his name Darren Watkin, directed by Ben Proudfoot and executive produced by President Barack Obama and Michelle Obama.

“Ghana and Africa mean so much to me,” IShowSpeed said in a statement. “Chris Hesse’s story matters and we’re not waiting on anybody to tell us which stories matter. We’re bringing this film to the world, for free. Chris Hesse spent his life protecting this important history. He deserves this moment. Ghana deserves this moment.”

Jane Lynch will host the 2027 AARP Movies & TV for Grownups Awards Jan. 9, celebrating the year’s top films, television programs and performances for audiences over 50 years old.

“We wanted a host who could bring sharp wit, warmth and impeccable timing to this year’s celebration and Jane Lynch delivers on every front,” said Myechia Minter-Jordan, the CEO of AARP. “These awards honor the artists who are redefining what’s possible on screen at every age, and Jane is the perfect person to help us celebrate creative work that expands representation and reflects the richness of people’s lives.”

Lynch will also announce this year’s nominees live on AARP’s Instagram and Facebook channels Monday at 9 a.m. PT, spanning categories that include best actor, actress and TV series or limited series.

“I’m thrilled to join AARP Movies & TV for Grownups as host,” Lynch said. “The longer I’ve worked in this business, the more I’ve come to appreciate the depth, perspective and insight that experience brings to great storytelling. I’m looking forward to celebrating the artists and creators whose work has entertained us, surprised us and stayed with us long after the credits rolled.”

AARP Movies & TV for Grownups Awards will be broadcast Feb. 26 at 6 p.m. PT on PBS and will stream on the “Great Performances” YouTube channel the following day.

David Alan Grier and Sarah Finn to Be Honored at Yale in Hollywood Fest

The three-day virtual film festival Yale in Hollywood Fest will honor David Alan Grier with the Legacy Award and Sarah Finn with the Career Achievement Award.

Grier is a Tony Award-winning actor and comedian, currently starring in NBC’s “St. Denis Medical.” He is the first recipient of the Legacy Award.

Finn is an award-winning casting director. Previous recipients include actress Jennifer Beals, actor C.S. Lee and television creator Justin Noble.

The festival will also spotlight Yale School of Music alumnus and filmmaker Charles Kaufmann, presenting his feature documentary “Tenacity: The Story of Edwin and Elise Harleston” alongside three of his short films.
