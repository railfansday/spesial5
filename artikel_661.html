‘Michael’ Director Antoine Fuqua Accused of Assault, Battery by L.A. Bar Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

“Michael” director Antoine Fuqua has been accused of assault, battery and intentional infliction of emotional distress by a Los Angeles bar manager following an incident back in February during which Fuqua allegedly pulled a gun on and struck the employee in the head.

In a 10-page civil claim filed Friday in the Los Angeles Superior Court, William Rosenthal, an assistant manager at the bar Good Times at Davey Wayne’s, is said to have denied entry to Fuqua, his son, Brando, and his daughter because they were “visibly overly intoxicated.” Rosenthal was then spat on by a member of the group, to which Rosenthal told them they needed to leave the bar. An hour later, Rosenthal alleges that Fuqua and his party remained standing outside the bar’s line, and upon noticing their presence, was approached by Fuqua and accused of pepper-spraying his daughter. When the manager denied the allegation, Fuqua “pulled out a handgun” and “struck” Rosenthal in the face before directing “a racial and antisemitic slur” at Rosenthal and threatening “to kill him.”

“As Plaintiff staggered back in pain, Defendant Brando Fuqua appeared and approached Plaintiff and forcefully swung a handgun at Plaintiff’s face,” read the complaint. “Defendants Antoine Fuqua and Brandon Fuqua then pointed their handguns at Plaintiff, placing him in imminent fear for his life, before fleeing down the street.”

Rosenthal is seeking $50,000 as a result of his suffering “serious physical and psychological injuries” which include a nose fracture, head injury, skin lacerations, post-traumatic stress disorder, severe emotional distress and severe scarring, all of which continue to cause him “great physical and mental pain and suffering.” The claim noted that Rosenthal believes said injuries “will result in some permanent disability.”

Reps for Fuqua told Variety that “none of this is true.”
