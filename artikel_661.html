Dolly Parton Estate Reveals Creative Team That Will ‘Preserve and Carry Forward’ Her Legacy

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The team will be led by Steve Summers, who worked with the music icon for more than 30 years.

The Dolly Parton estate has announced the leadership of its creative team led by Steve Summers, who worked with Parton for more than 30 years and will serve as the estate’s creative director.

Summers will steer a team that also includes creative manager Riley Reed Hanratty, design specialist Vance Nichols and creative specialist Bradley Schnabel. Together, they will be responsible for the creative direction of the estate, “helping preserve and carry forward the unmistakable style, creativity and vision that defined Dolly’s legacy,” according to a press release.

Summers launched his professional career in 1991 as a singer and dancer at Dollywood, where he developed an interest in costume and set design before taking on additional creative responsibilities. In 2004, Parton brought on Summers to stage and choreograph her Hello, I’m Dolly Tour, and in 2006, he was named creative director of Dolly Parton Enterprises. In that role, he oversaw Parton’s personal wardrobe styling and visual creative direction encompassing costumes, album artwork, stage design, product development and other elements. Working closely with Parton, he and his team created hundreds of custom looks every year and preserved her existing wardrobe.

As creative director of Parton’s estate, Summers will bring his decades of experience working with the late star to a portfolio that includes the upcoming Broadway show DOLLY: A True Original Musical and other creative initiatives celebrating the icon. He will additionally work on projects with Dolly’s niece Danielle Parton, who was recently announced as an official brand ambassador for the estate.

Hanratty joined Parton’s team in 2018 and has held a number of roles. She started in alterations before joining the team tasked with digitizing Parton’s clothing, a process that included cataloging, photographing, describing and preserving garments. In 2022, she was named costume shop manager and head seamstress, working closely with Summers while overseeing Parton’s personal closet.

Nichols, a 30-year veteran of scenic and interior design, began freelancing for Parton in 2019 under Summers’ direction. He initially focused on wardrobe embellishments while also assisting with tours and other large events. Nichols joined the team full-time in 2018, with his role expanding to include set design and décor for photo shoots, TV appearances and commercials. He has also worked with Parton’s sister, Rachel Parton George, on redesigning and decorating some of her personal properties.

Schnabel spent several years in restaurant management before coming on to assist Dolly Parton Enterprises’ property and creative team. He later transitioned into being Parton’s personal assistant, a role he held for almost five years. In his new role, he will support the creative team with project logistics, planning and coordination.
