Ella Langley Wins Album of the Year Award at 2026 CMA Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

While the Album of the Year category at the 2026 CMA Awards was stacked, only one musician could take home the title.

Ella Langley‘s Dandelion was crowned the category’s winner at the Wednesday, November 18, awards ceremony held at Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena, airing live on ABC and Disney+. The musician was up against Zach Top for Ain’t In It For My Health, Kacey Musgraves for Middle of Nowhere, Parker McCollum for the album of the same name and Luke Combs for The Way I Am.

“I just want to say I have so much love and respect for every single woman in this category. Not only every single woman in this category but the women who have gone before me,” the “Choosin’ Texas” singer, 27, said after winning her fourth award. “This is what I have lived and breathed … genuinely my whole entire life.”

She closed her acceptance speech by teasing, “We’re just getting started. We really are!”

Langley nearly achieved a clean sweep of the nine nominations she received, picking up Female Vocalist of the Year as well as Single and Song of the Year for “Choosin’ Texas.”

By the time Langley closed the show by accepting Entertainer of the Year, she joked, “At this point, I’m sick of me. I have watched these awards since I was a little kid and the award I cared most about was Song of the Year and Entertainer of the Year … I’ve run out of things to say. I just don’t know.”

Langley led the nominations for the awards show, earning a whopping nine nods across categories including Entertainer of the Year, Single of the Year (“Be Her” and “Choosin’ Texas”), Album of the Year (Dandelion), Song of the Year (“Be Her” and “Choosin’ Texas”), Female Vocalist of the Year, Musical Event of the Year (“I Can’t Love You Anymore”) and Music Video of the Year (“Choosin’ Texas”).

Closely following Langley’s nods are Miranda Lambert with seven nominations, Musgraves with five nominations and Ben West with five nominations for the awards show, which was hosted by Lainey Wilson for her third consecutive year in a row. In 2025, Wilson took home the prestigious Album of the Year title for her LP Whirlwind.

“Mom and Daddy, look, check it out! I was absolutely not expecting this,” Wilson said during the awards show at the time, as she gave a shout-out to her fans, label, band and country radio. “I have the best fans in the world. They showed up every single night singing every single word off of this record.”

Wilson, who also served as the host of the CMAs in 2025, shared that she wrote Whirlwind when she felt like her life was “constantly changing” as she navigated her newfound fame.

“I just remember being that little 11-year-old girl who would come home from school and pick up my guitar and pour my heart and soul into that guitar, and that’s how I navigated and communicated,” she reflected at the time. “And that’s exactly what I did these last two years of my life when I felt like my life was changing but I still felt exactly the same.”
