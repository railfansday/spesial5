Why Did ABC Censor Jan Ravnik After 'DWTS' Freestyle Routine?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Dancing With the Stars pro Jan Ravnik was preemptively censored after his stunning freestyle with Tatyana Ali on finale night.

“No, I’m just extremely proud of her,” Ravnik, 31, told cohost Julianne Hough when asked about his emotions after their performance on Tuesday, November 24.

The audio then cut before Ali, 47, was heard screaming.

“I think you’re on somebody else’s bad list now,” Hough, 38, quipped, referring to Ravnik’s infamous “DTM List.” (Moments earlier, Hough asked Ravnik whether she was still on the “Dead to Me” list following their back-and-forth earlier this season.)

Fellow pro Alan Bersten revealed on the show’s official TikTok livestream that Ravnik had said the word “f***.” (The FCC has specific guidelines about what can be said live during primetime programs.)

Ravnik and Ali performed a showstopping freestyle set to “Minnie the Moocher” by Cab Calloway and “Over the Rainbow” by Judy Garland.

“Jan told me to tell him what my dream world was like, and he created this beautiful dance that’s a metaphor for my experience here,” Ali tearfully said from the skybox. “So it was fitting that it would be the last dance that we did.”

The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air alum is one of the five finalists closing out season 35, alongside Ezra Frech, Amber Glenn, Maura Higgins and Connor Wood. Each celeb danced three routines on Tuesday night with their pro partners.

Ali previously opened up to Us about her DWTS journey after the semifinals earlier this month. “I was a different size when I came here, but I loved my body then too,” she said on November 17. “I loved her. She helped me bring my babies into this world. She went through a lot of stuff [and] trauma. She got me through it.”

She continued, “I liked my body before I got here, but it feels like I have — I don’t know how to describe it — I have room in my body. I don’t know how else to say it.”

Ali has been praised by the judges for her growth throughout the season and went on to offer fellow moms advice.

“My advice for them is dance. Get your girls together and your mom friends because we’re not supposed to be momming by ourselves,” Ali stated. “I think that’s unnatural. We’re supposed to actually have a village and be doing it together. But y’all need to dance.”

The actress also noticed how far she’s come.

“I feel so free inside my body,” she told Us. “Freer than I felt in a long time. Like, there’s room in here. … It’s been transformative.”

Dancing With the Stars airs on ABC and Disney+ at 8 p.m. ET.
