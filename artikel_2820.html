Malik Willis going on IR can cost the Packers a round in the 2027 draft

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The NFL's compensatory pick formula is a little complicated, but to boil it down: Players are awarded points for their average salary over the life of the contract that they signed in free agency and receive playing-time bonuses based on the percentage of snaps they play during the regular season. If you lose players worth more points than the ones you sign, you get a draft pick from the NFL (which essentially mints an extra round of the draft, awarding 32 picks from the third to seventh rounds).

That's why the news of Malik Willis, a former Green Bay Packers quarterback, going on injured reserve impacts Green Bay in 2027. Already, Over The Cap has adjusted Willis' compensatory draft pick projection to be a fourth-round pick, rather than the third-round pick he was initially expected to earn for the Packers. Willis was already a borderline third-round case (there are only two players currently set to earn third-round compensatory picks: edge defenders Jaelen Phillips and Trey Hendrickson), but losing at least four games of action is essentially a nail in the coffin for that selection.

So instead of having what was projected to be the 98th pick in the draft for the loss of Willis, the Packers are now projected to receive the 133rd pick, according to Over The Cap.

Former Packers leaving Green Bay haven't had much luck this year. Offensive tackle Rasheed Walker, who was expected to net the Packers at least a fifth-round pick in free agency, overplayed his hand and signed late — leaving him with just a one-year deal. At the moment, the Packers are not expected to be awarded a pick for the loss of Walker. On top of that, receiver Romeo Doubs, who had a chance to earn Green Bay a fourth-round pick, is also on the injured reserve, which likely means that the Packers will only get a fifth-round pick for losing him.

They will also get the Cowboys' fourth-round pick (Rashan Gary trade) and the Eagles' sixth-round pick (Dontayvion Wicks trade) in 2027, bringing their overall total to 11 picks.
