Dolphins vs. Bills odds: Opening lines for Week 11 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Miami Dolphins fell to 1-8 earlier today after coming up just short of beating the Indianapolis Colts, 24-22. Miami's offense played well enough under quarterback Kyle McCord, making his NFL debut after spending his rookie season on the Philadelphia Eagles' practice squad last year, and the defense did its part to keep Miami in the game. The special teams let down the Dolphins – with kicker Zane Gonzalez missing two field goals and an extra point during the game.

The Dolphins' loss was yet another straight-up loss, but they did enough to cover the 3.5-point spread throughout the week, or the 4.5-point mark to which the spread ballooned on Sunday. Miami is being given an even bigger window for next week's game.

Miami visits the Buffalo Bills in Week 11, the first of two meetings between the AFC East rivals in 2026. According to FanDuel Sportsbook, the Dolphins have little chance against the division-leading Bills. The opening line has the Bills favored by 16.5 points.

Buffalo demolished the New York Jets, another AFC East division opponent, on Sunday, 31-12. The win moved the Bills to 8-1 for the year and keeps them in the top spot in the AFC playoff picture, a game ahead of the Kansas City Chiefs and Jacksonville Jaguars. The winner of tonight's Sunday Night Football game between the Pittsburgh Steelers and Cincinnati Bengals will also reach the 7-2 mark, a game behind Buffalo.

The Bills are -1800 on the moneyline for the straight-up victory, while the Dolphins are +980. The point total opened at 47.5.

McCord will start his second game in Week 11, looking to continue to grow in the Dolphins' offense as he replaces Malik Willis, who is on injured reserve with a groin injury. McCord finished the game 17-for-32 for 209 yards with no touchdown passes or interceptions; he did score a rushing touchdown.

The Dolphins and Bills will kickoff at 1 p.m. Eastern on Sunday, November 22.
