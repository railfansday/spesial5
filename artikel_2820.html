Detroit Lions officially place LB Jack Campbell on injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Detroit Lions placed linebacker Jack Campbell on injured reserve Saturday, Nov. 7, six days after he was carted off the field with a serious neck injury.

Campbell was injured on the first play of the fourth quarter in the Lions' win over the Minnesota Vikings last week, when his head hit tight end T.J. Hockenson's hip on a tackle.

Campbell remains hospitalized, though Lions coach Dan Campbell said Friday his condition is improving.

In a statement released by the Lions on Wednesday, Jack Campbell said he has regained full movement in his lower body and was "continuing to make progress with movement" in his upper body. He said in the statement he was "very optimistic" about his recovery.

The Lions (5-2) have taken a win-for-Campbell approach to this week's game against the Miami Dolphins.

Linebacker Derrick Barnes said a key to victory this week is to "prepare and play" like Campbell would, and defensive coordinator Kelvin Sheppard said he wakes up every day asking himself, "What would 46 want me to do today?"

"We're getting a better handle on this," Dan Campbell said. "Jack continues to improve, which is all great news and there again this is about, man, how would he prepare, how would he play? Those are the standards for us going into this game. We owe it to him."

In addition, the Lions signed defensive back Nick Whiteside to the active roster from the practice squad and elevated edge defender Anthony Lucas and running back Jabari Small from the practice squad to the active/inactive list
