Micah Parsons: Our play in second half Wednesday night was inexcusable

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Packers defensive end Micah Parsons made his return to the lineup against the Rams on Wednesday night, but he wasn't able to help his team to a win in Los Angeles.

Parsons picked up a sack late in the first half that sent the game to the break with the score tied 13-13, but he couldn't make an impact in the second half because the Rams barely threw the ball. After running 12 times for 46 yards in the first half, the Rams ran 35 times for 194 yards while outscoring the Packers 13-0 in the final 30 minutes.

After the game, Parsons said it is "just inexcusable" to allow opposing teams to "dictate games like that" and said the aftermath has to be an increased level of accountability for the entire defense.

"We need to figure out who's going to be a dog or not," Parsons said. "That's just the reality of it. To me, to be honest and this is around the league, if someone can line up 50 times and run down your mouth and you don't have an answer for it, that is player — that's accountability. That's just how I really feel about it. I wasn't able to get on the field because of it, because they didn't want to waste my plays on first and second down I think the whole thing is just bad and we've got to figure out who's willing to play with us or who's playing against us at this point."

The Packers don't have the luxury of having time to figure things out. The loss dropped them to 6-5 on the season and the makeup of the NFC means that they're going to have to win most of their remaining games to have any chance of advancing to the postseason.
