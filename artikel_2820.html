Andy Reid on Harrison Butker: I haven’t made any decisions yet

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Chiefs have a clear and obvious kicking problem.

Harrison Butker officially missed one more field goal on Sunday, sailing a 51-yard attempt wide right.

But everyone also saw him miss from 56 a snap before — a play that didn’t count because of a Jets penalty.

Butker has now missed 17 kicks over the last two seasons, which is a league-high.

Kansas City has Justin Tucker on its practice squad, and he would ostensibly be the next man up. But in his Monday news conference, Reid told reporters that he wasn’t ready to commit to any changes at kicker.

“With Butker, I haven’t made any decisions yet,” Reid said. “I’m going to evaluate everything and go forward from there. Again, I haven’t decided on reps during practice, I haven’t done any of that right now. Just going through it, evaluating. And we’ll go from there.”

Butker has made just 12-of-19 field goals so far this season. He’s also missed an extra point.

We’ll see if Reid has more to say about his kicker situation when the Chiefs start their practice week on Wednesday.
