Browns, Saints injury reports: 2 new important concerns for CLE

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The NFL is often a league of attrition and the Cleveland Browns are no exception. While the New Orleans Saints are hopeful that they can get two starters back on offense (WR Jordyn Tyson and OT Kelvin Banks Jr.), the Browns are expecting to be without starting QB Deshaun Watson, who missed his second straight practice, and could be without DT Maliek Collins as well.

The Saints are already the favorites in Week 9, with more and more picking the home team to come away with the victory. News that Cleveland added TE Harold Fannin Jr. and S Emmanuel McNeil-Warren to the injury report on Thursday doesn't help the visitor's cause.

The official injury reports for Week 9 in New Orleans, with any changes noted in bold:

TE Harold Fannin Jr. – Groin – Added as Limited

CB Myles Harden – Hamstring – Limited to Full

OL Elgton Jenkins – Knee – Limited to Full

S Emmanuel McNeil-Warren – Hip – Added as Limited

LB Carson Schwesinger – Wrist, Ankle – Limited

LB Quincy Williams – Hamstring – DNP to Limited

Given Fannin's groin injury last year, popping up on the injury report with the same designation is concerning:

RB CJ Donaldson – Shoulder, Concussion – Limited to DNP

RB Alvin Kamara – Rest, Back – DNP to Limited

S Justin Reid – Concussion – Full to No Injury Designation

DE Nathan Shepherd – Illness – Full to No Injury Designation
