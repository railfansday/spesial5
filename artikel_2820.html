NFL Draft 2027: Updated order after Jaguars-Eagles trade

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

We have reached the midpoint of the 2026 NFL season.

And the order for the first round of the 2027 NFL Draft is taking shape.

The Los Angeles Chargers remain in the driver's seat for the first-overall selection, as the Chargers limp into Week 10 still seeking their first win of the season. Right behind Los Angeles are. the Tennessee Titans and the Miami Dolphins, those teams each sitting at 1-7 on the year.

The New York Jets sit in the fourth spot for now, holding the rights to the Indianapolis Colts' first pick in the 2027 NFL Draft, thanks to the Sauce Gardner trade. The Jets are one of four teams with multiple picks in the first round next spring. New York holds their own selection, the Colts pick (at No. 4), and the Dallas Cowboys' selection (at No. 13) due to the Quinnen Williams trade.

Cleveland holds their own pick (at No. 16) and the Los Angeles Rams' pick (No. 18) due to the Myles Garrett trade.

The Titans have the No. 30 pick from the Chicago Bears, as part of the Jeffery Simmons deal.

And the Philadelphia Eagles joined this list on Monday, acquiring the Jacksonville Jaguars' selection as part of the Jordan Davis trade. Now the Eagles have their own pick (currently No. 5) and Jacksonville's, which at the moment slots in at No. 25.
