Puka Nacua's 14th catch goes for TD, Rams lead 20-13

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

With the Rams sporting a thin receiving corps, Nacua’s 14th catch of Sunday’s game against the Commanders went for a touchdown.Los Angeles now leads Washington 20-13.

The Commanders were able to tie the game at 13-13 after Matthew Stafford tossed an interception in the third quarter in L.A. territory. Stafford was looking for Nacua on the sideline, but safety Nick Cross stepped into the throw’s path to pick it off.

But on the ensuing possession, the Rams used a 12-play, 65-yard drive to take the lead back. The club was able to pick up its first third-down conversion of the day to move the chains on third-and-5, third-and-11, and third-and-1 in the red zone.

Nacua had to go up to get the touchdown on second-and-goal but nevertheless came down with it.

On the injury front, Washington center Lucas Patrick has been downgraded to out with a concussion.
