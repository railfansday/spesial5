Stanton scores 14, Princeton downs Hofstra 60-58

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

PRINCETON, N.J. (AP) — Jack Stanton's 14 points helped Princeton defeat Hofstra 60-58 on Thursday.

Stanton went 5 of 8 from the field (4 for 7 from 3-point range) for the Tigers (2-0). Jackson Hicke scored 10 points and added five assists. Ethan Sabec had nine points and shot 4 for 10, including 0 for 5 from beyond the arc.

Tanner Thomas led the way for the Pride (1-1) with 14 points and nine rebounds. Justin Moore added 12 points, eight rebounds and four assists for Hofstra. Nick Jefferson also had 10 points.

Princeton plays Sunday against Siena on the road, and Hofstra visits Arizona State on Tuesday.
