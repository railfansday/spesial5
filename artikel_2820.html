Matthew Stafford ready to go for Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Los Angeles Rams QB Matthew Stafford (elbow) was a full participant in practice Friday, Nov. 6, and he does not carry an injury designation for Week 9.

Why it Matters: Stafford will be looking to bounce back from a lackluster performance in Week 8, but with four games with 300-plus yards and three games with three-plus touchdowns this year, he still has QB1 upside. He should at least be a high-end QB2 against Washington in Week 9.
