‘The writing has been on the wall’: Brandon Aiyuk’s agent gives update on return

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Brandon Aiyuk saga seems to be nearing an end, but for now, the wide receiver and his agent, Drew Rosenhaus, still await word from the San Francisco 49ers.

The NFL granted Aiyuk reinstatement on November 9th, while giving the 49ers until November 24th to reinstate him to the 53-man roster. Amid the noise, the Niners have made it clear they don't expect the wide receiver to take another snap for San Francisco, but still have the decision to cut or trade Aiyuk, though this season's trade deadline has already passed.

Recently, Rosenhaus sat down with Dianna Russini on Wednesday's episode of The Russini Report and provided clarity on the pending situation, as Aiyuk is healthy and ready to go.

"He did pass all of the physicals that the 49ers gave him," Rosenhaus said. "He had MRIs, and X-rays, and a physical exam, and he did really well. There were no issues. He is 100 percent healthy right now, and in shape, and working out full-force, and he is absolutely ready to go back to play football. As soon as he gets the green light, he'll be practicing and playing."

Although Aiyuk is ready for action, Niners players and coaches don't see a path for Aiyuk back to the team. Both head coach Kyle Shanahan and starting tight end George Kittle voiced their opinions and indicated they have moved on from the situation in recent interviews, and Rosenhaus has taken note.

"We haven't discussed it, simply because the writing has been on the wall for a while now," Rosenhaus added. "The Niners have not been bashful in saying publicly that they're not planning on him coming back…Brandon and I have not seriously discussed him returning to the Niners just because we are not sure that that is an option."

Currently, Aiyuk is in football purgatory as he is not permitted to work out, negotiate, or speak contractually to any teams until the Niners make their final decision on his contract. But Rosenhaus has high hopes for his client's career.

"I'm not sure what's going to happen with waivers because of his current contract, but our instinct is that he'll become a free agent and we'll have options. I'm not permitted to talk to teams, but based on my 38 years of experience and reading the tea leaves, I expect that he'll have many suitors…but he would like to get out there and play as soon as possible," Rosenhaus said.

Russini asked Rosenhaus why any team would trust Aiyuk after all this time, to which he defended his client and spoke highly of him.

"When I talk to teams, when I'm allowed to do that, I will let them know….I was very impressed with him, where he's at, where his mind is at, what his goals are, and he's been nothing but professional," Rosenhaus told Russini.

It will be interesting to see if Aiyuk can make it back to the field, as Monday the 24th is the Niners deadline.
