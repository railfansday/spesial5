Sixers' Joel Embiid talks ambitious dunk attempt after win over Nets

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

NEW YORK -- Philadelphia 76ers star Joel Embiid sent a subtle reminder to everybody that he's still got it. The big fella went to work on Monday night to the tune of 37 points on 13-for-22 shooting, 2-for-4 from deep, and 10-for-13 from the free-throw line in a 135-123 win over the Brooklyn Nets.

"Well, I mean, obviously, the shots are going, and he scored quite a bit," said coach Nick Nurse. "So, that's nice to see. Made a couple perimeter shots, had some stretches of great 15-foot work, etc."

On top of it all, Embiid even attempted an off-the-backboard alley-oop dunk to himself. He wasn't able to convert, but this is the second straight game he tried to do that as he continues to work his way back to himself and what he knows he can do on the offensive end of the floor.

"I did it last game, too, but I think they counted it as a shot," Embiid said with his signature smirk. "Yeah, I don't know. That's part of I'm so used to playing hard that when I don't do that, it affects everything else. Just wanted to go back to playing hard. And yeah, that's the main thing. I think it's a long season, so we're all going to have these moments. It's all about being balanced and making sure you stay healthy."

It's no secret that Embiid has not had the same efficiency that he once had before all of the injuries slowed him, but he and trainer Drew Hanlen sat down and figured out a subtle adjustment that allowed him to get into a rhythm against the Nets on Monday.

"I think just the process," Embiid said of his offensive night. "One thing that me and Drew saw on film is that — not that I've been playing lazy, but yeah. I'm used to playing hard, putting everything into what I do. So it kind of feels like I've been going out there and just letting myself go a little bit. So that was just the one adjustment that we made, because I feel like we felt like that affected everything."

One cannot accuse Embiid of playing lazy to begin the 2026-27 season, but it's certainly been a weird start for the 2023 MVP winner. When he plays hard and is able to put his thumbprint on the game early, then it allows him to find his flow and give the Sixers the offensive force they need in order to move forward.

"The more force I play with, the more aggressive I am, the better the shot is going to be or is going to look," Embiid explained. "And then obviously them trapping Tyrese (Maxey) all game and getting the space to do a little bit out there."

The two 3-pointers made are big for Embiid. He is still shooting only 6-for-40 from deep, but him going 2-for-4, including nailing a clutch one late that put the Nets away, is a big sign for the big fella. He's always been able to shoot the ball, and he has kept confidence in that shot, but it's great to see the work finally pay off for him.

"Like I said, make or miss," Embiid finished. "I haven't felt anything about the misses, it sucks, especially when you know how good of a shooter you are, so I keep walking out there, sometimes they go in, sometimes they don't. They haven't been going in, tonight they did. So be it on that, and hope it gets better."

The Sixers will finish their quick 2-game road trip on Wednesday when they take on the Orlando Magic.
