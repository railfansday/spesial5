Puka Nacua won’t go on IR and Davante Adams could be back soon

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Los Angeles Rams will not put Puka Nacua on injury reserve with his knee injury according to head coach Sean McVay, but that does not mean that the receiver will miss fewer than four games.

ESPN's Sarah Barshop reports that the decision to keep Nacua on the 53-man roster is due to "semantics" and that Nacua missing IR is likely with regard to the Rams only having so many spots and too many players being injured.

There is also the chance that the Rams are thinking Nacua can suit up in Week 14 against the San Francisco 49ers, in which case he would only miss three games. This could be wishful thinking however.

Week 14 may be the earliest Nacua rejoins the Rams as they reportedly expect him to miss "extended time". Whatever the reason(s), Nacua may have avoided a mandatory missing of four games by staying off IR, and that could be a positive development for the Rams. On the bright side, McVay indicated that Davante Adams may be able to return after the bye week, which would bring him back in Week 12 against the Packers.

On the season, Nacua has in some ways been the Rams offense with over 700 receiving yards and four touchdowns. The veteran wide receiver has also hauled in 70 catches on 94 targets. Davante Adams is the only other receiving weapon "close" to Nacua with 30 receptions, 480 yards and matches Nacua with four touchdowns of his own.

The only other receiver on the Rams with 30 or more receptions is Kyren Williams and he's a running back.

There are real questions for what L.A.'s offense will look like without Nacua, and at the time of this reporting/tweeting from Barshop, the Rams may only have to live in that realm for a few weeks, with Nacua expected to be back sooner than initially projected.

Of course, if Terrance Ferguson can have an injury setback, so can Nacua. Nacua isn't out of the woods yet in terms of how much time he'll miss, but reports are more optimistic on Wednesday then they were Sunday/Monday after Nacua's injury was first reported.
