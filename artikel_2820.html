Is Heat star Bam Adebayo going home to play GTA 6 after beating Bucks? ‘Absolutely’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Miami Heat, in celebration of the wide release of the much-anticipated GTA 6, held a special night at Kaseya Center — embracing their identity as Vice City. They even wore special jerseys to match the occasion, and they hosted the Milwaukee Bucks in a game filled with subplots.

Not only was Giannis Antetokounmpo facing his former team for the first time since the blockbuster offseason trade, Bam Adebayo also had the opportunity to face Tyler Herro after their physical confrontation this offseason. In the end, it was the Heat that grabbed a 137-130 victory to cap what was a perfect night overall for the team.

Now, Adebayo can't wait to get home, as he said in his postgame interview with ESPN that he "absolutely" will be playing GTA 6 once he steps foot into the confines of his abode.

Fans of the "Grand Theft Auto" franchise had to wait over 13 years before the next installment of the renowned game was released. Adebayo would not be drafted in the NBA until four years had passed since GTA 5's initial release for the PS3 and Xbox 360 back in September 2013.

Adebayo must be in touch with his inner child while playing GTA 6, and what a way this is for him to blow off steam after a tiring night at work where emotions must have run high considering his history with his former teammate in Herro.

As for the Heat, they have now won six consecutive games, putting memories of their struggles to begin the new season in the rearview mirror. Adebayo put up 26 points on the night on 7-of-15 shooting from the field, and he seems to have adjusted to life playing alongside Antetokounmpo.

No one can discredit the Heat's six-game winning streak. Three of those wins came against teams that made the playoffs last year (Cleveland Cavaliers, Detroit Pistons, and Houston Rockets), and they are now on quite the roll.
