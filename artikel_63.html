Guinea-Bissau's military leaders delay return to civilian rule until 2028

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Transitional President Gen Horta N'Tam said the postponement was driven by factors including final reasons

Guinea-Bissau's military rulers have postponed elections scheduled for next week by two years, delaying the return to civilian rule.

Transitional President Gen Horta N'Tam announced in a decree that the presidential and parliamentary elections, originally due on 6 December, would now take place on 26 November 2028.

The announcement came exactly a year after the military seized power, promising to return the country to civilian rule within 12 months.

In a statement, the presidency said an assessment of electoral preparations had found that there were insufficient "technical, material and financial conditions" to hold the polls as planned.

It said the delay would provide more time to ensure the elections were credible, transparent and inclusive, with all candidates given equal opportunities.

The presidency also reiterated its commitment to holding the country's first-ever local government elections but gave no date for the vote.
