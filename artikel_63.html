It was also a big night for Puerto Rican reggaeton legend Daddy Yankee, who won the person of the year award.

He performed some of his best-loved tracks including Gasolina and Con Calma, and was joined on stage by rappers including LL Cool J and French Montana.

Punjabi singer Diljit Dosanjh, who collabed with Daddy Yankee on Pokito, even took a break from his Aura India tour for the set, making him the first Indian artist to perform at the Latin Grammys.

Dosanjh superfan Devanshi Arora described the singer's appearance as "a cultural revolution".

"It's really invigorating and motivating to see someone from my country has made it all around the world," she tells Newsbeat.

She sees a lot of similarities between South American and Punjabi communities and hopes this leads to more collaborations - with a link-up between Dosanjh and Bad Bunny top of her wishlist.

Daddy Yankee was presented with the lifetime achievement award by Janet Jackson, who told him in Spanish that his music "has inspired an entire generation".
