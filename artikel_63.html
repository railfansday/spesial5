Nine injured, three critically, after Bangkok oil refinery explosion

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Watch: Fire and smoke billows out of refinery owned by energy company Bangchak Corporation

Nine people have been injured after an explosion at an oil refinery in Bangkok, authorities have said.

Three of them are in critical condition, according to news agency AFP, which said the local hospital had revised its earlier assessment of four.

A fire broke out after the blast at the Phra Khanong facility run by energy giant Bangchak Corporation in the east of Thailand's capital at around 14:45 (07:45 GMT).

The company said the fire was brought under control and put out within 30 minutes, adding that the cause of the incident was being investigated.

Black smoke could be seen across Bangkok's skyline after the blast, as local officials told residents to leave the area.

An alert sent to mobile phones by Bangkok authorities confirmed that the fire was under control, but urged people to avoid the area.

Eyewitnesses shared video clips on social media of flames and smoke rising above the refinery.

On a visit to the scene, Thailand's energy minister Akanat Promphan said it appeared the impact of the explosion was limited because the fire started in a distillation tower about 100m (330ft) from where oil was being stored.

He said the incident was concerning as the refinery was in a residential area and the government was suspending production until it could be confident that operations would resume without any issues.

Bangchak Corporation said air quality monitoring in the immediate area around the refinery was within normal ranges.

It confirmed that production at the site, which can process up to 120,000 barrels of oil a day had been shut down. The company has a second refinery in Chonburi province, about 50 miles (80km) from Bangkok, handling a daily capacity of about 180,000 barrels.

An explosion occurred at the Bangkok refinery in July 2012, sparking a fire but no reported injuries.
