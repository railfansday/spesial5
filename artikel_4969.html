What Is It Supposed to Feel Like to Push Your Limits? 5-Time CrossFit Games Champ Mat Fraser Knows.

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

No pain, no gain? The GOAT shares his perspective.

MAT FRASER UNDERSTANDS what it’s like to push your limits. But the five-time CrossFit Games champ found the motivation to train to failure at the 2015 CrossFit Games, when he finished in second place. Fraser remembers feeling devastated—finishing as a runner-up felt like a personal offense to his purpose and identity.

“Once I made that commitment to get back in the gym and give it another shot, I kept that moment fresh,” Fraser says in the latest episode of Strong Talk. “Knowing that you don't get that beautiful moment in front of thousands of people without the thousands of moments by yourself in your gym when no one's patting you on the back and no one's cheering for you.”

What did it feel like to push his limits in his workouts? Fraser says he’s on the right track when he’s “tasting pennies,” the top of his head is tingling, and his heart rate is over 200 bpm. “Leave no doubt on the table, and be proud of myself. Then no matter what results I get, I know that's what I earned,” he explains.

But despite his preparation, Fraser is only human. Despite his impressive accolades, he never got rid of the pre-event jitters. Fraser tells MH fitness director Ebenezer Samuel, C.S.C.S. and exercise physiologist and strength coach Dr. Pat Davidson that he used to dry heave or grab a bucket as his breakfast came back up before every competition. In the episode, the trio delved more into the hardest workouts he did to prepare for the Games, what it's like to train recreationally versus for competition, and how to exercise and fuel your body when you only have 15 minutes.
