How LeBron James dissected Pacers defense down the stretch

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

How LeBron James dissected Pacers defense down the stretch originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

As he overtook Jason Kidd for the third-most assists in NBA history, LeBron James piloted the Sixers' offense down the stretch against the Indiana Pacers on Sunday.

With Joel Embiid sitting out and Tyrese Maxey getting a breather on the bench, James took over in the fourth quarter. He did so by spamming the same action over and over again.

The set consisted of James getting a ball screen from a guard – either VJ Edgecombe or Anfernee Simons – at the top of the key. After setting or ghosting the screen, the guard would short roll towards the basket.

When the Pacers blitzed or hedged the screen, James found the guard. The Sixers could then attack a 4-on-3 or 3-on-2 situation.

Twice, Edgecombe and Simons scored off the roll.

When the Pacers helped to the roller, that opened up the shooters in each corner.

James directed traffic on offense to ensure that the Sixers spaced the floor properly. Here, he waved Dean Wade out of the dunker spot and into the corner. Edgecombe promptly hit him for an open three off the short roll.

After all of the Sixers' success, the Pacers eventually switched the ball screen. That left the 6-foot-4, 173-pound Quenton Jackson on James, who has five inches and 77 pounds on him.

Jackson and James had already gone head to head on Sunday.

In the first quarter, James set a ball screen for Edgecombe which the Pacers switched. Edgecombe fed James in the post and the Pacers doubled him to support Jackson. James punished them for it.

They didn't double James after the switch in the fourth quarter. This time, James backed Jackson down, backed him down again and backed him down some more before scoring inside.

James' strength abusing matchups against smaller defenders after switches has been a safety valve for the Sixers' offense. Guards struggle to stop him from getting to the basket. When defenses send help, he picks them apart with his otherworldly passing ability.

That combination forced the Pacers to pick their poison. From the 9:55 mark in the fourth until the Pacers switched to a zone defense (after James bullied Jackson) with just over 6:00 left, the Sixers scored 13 points out of this action across seven possessions. That's a stellar 1.86 points per possession.

James, who's scoring a career-low 15.1 points per game so far this season, might not be the elite bucket-getter he was for most of his career. But he's still brilliant at conducting highly efficient offense that pressures a defense's pain points.
