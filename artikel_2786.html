Chiefs Week 11 snap count analysis: Hunter Nourzad emerges up front

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Welcome to Arrowhead Pride's weekly snap-count analysis!

Let's take a closer look at the Kansas City Chiefs' snap counts in the 34-7 win over the Arizona Cardinals in Week 11. The starting lineups are courtesy of NFL Game Stats and Information, and the snap-count data comes from Pro Football Focus' premium stats.

Starters (offensive): LT Kahlil Benson, LG Kingsley Suamataia, C Creed Humphrey, RG Hunter Nourzad, RT Joshua Ezeudu, QB Patrick Mahomes, WR Rashee Rice, WR Xavier Worthy, RB Emmett Johnson, TE Noah Gray and TE Travis Kelce

Starters (defensive): DE George Karlaftis, DT Chris Jones, NT Khyiris Tonga, DE Felix Anudike-Uzomah, LB Nick Bolton, LB Drue Tranquill, LB Jack Cochrane, CB Nohl Williams, CB Mansoor Delane, S Alohi Gilman and S Chamarri Conner

Inactive: QB Garrett Nussmeier (Emergency third QB), RB Kenneth Walker (groin), RG Trey Smith (concussion), DT Omar Norman-Lott (knee), DT Peter Woods (neck), CB Melvin Smith Jr. and RT Jaylon Moore (ankle)

Third-year lineman Hunter Nourzad was given the nod as the starting right guard against Arizona, marking his second career start — although last year's season finale against the Las Vegas Raiders hardly felt like a regular-season game.

He was one of two players (along with right tackle Joshua Ezeudu) to play every snap of the blowout win. He switched to center for the final five snaps of his day.

He moved defenders in the run game, but allowed just one pressure over 41 pass-blocking snaps. It was a strong first impression for Nourzad, who, after years of battling veteran Mike Caliendo, may have finally broken through in the rotation.

Last week, backup tight end Jared Wiley went down with a season-ending ankle injury, which shed light on a banged up position with the veteran Travis Kelce (ankle) appearing on the injury report over the last month.

On Sunday, tight end Noah Gray left the game — and even fullback Ben VanSumeren went out with an injury. It sets up a challenge for second-year tight end Jake Briningstool, who played a season-high 27 snaps against Arizona.

He didn't see a pass come his way, but he's coming off two games with multiple targets each; last week against the Atlanta Falcons, he gained 20 yards on a catch-and-run before Mahomes threw him a long-range pass. Get used to No. 88 in the Chiefs' offense; he may see a lot of work against the Buffalo Bills on a short week.

As Kansas City moves into playoff mode, it feels like the defensive line rotation is sharpening to maximize the strengths of its role players around edge rusher George Karlaftis and defensive tackle Chris Jones.

Second-year defensive lineman Ashton Gillotte continues to ascend as an every-down player, whether he lines up on the edge (24 snaps) or inside (23 snaps).

However, fourth-year defensive end Felix Anudike-Uzomah remains the first-down starter opposite Karlaftis and is versatile up and down the front depending on the situation as well. He registered two quarterback hits in 18 pass rushes against the Cardinals.

To round it out, rookie edge rusher R Mason Thomas is using his athleticism on pass downs, whether to get after the quarterback (1 pressure over 16 rushes on Sunday) or handle coverage (4 snaps).
