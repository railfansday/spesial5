Bayern Munich News: Brajan Gruda likely not in FC Bayern’s plans; Donyell Malen linked to Bayern; Real Madrid angling for Nico Paz; and MORE!

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Despite some reports to the contrary, Sport Bild journalist Christian Falk is reporting that while Bayern Munich likes Brajan Gruda, there is no concrete plan to pursue the RB Leipzig star for the Bavarians:

I think Brajan Gruda was the success story of Germany's last two international games. He scored, he assisted… and Bayern have had him on their list. He is a Brighton player, but he won't be with the Seagulls long, I suspect. That's because there is a release clause. Gruda must make at least three Bundesliga appearances for RB Leipzig during the 2027 calendar year for the mandatory purchase clause to take effect. At that point, if €25m [£20.9m] is put on the table for this player, he'll become an RB Leipzig player. From here, I can't see Leipzig selling the player on immediately.

From a Bayern perspective, they'll be thinking about what's happening with the future of Michael Olise in the summer. They do have Lennart Karl also to think about. So, I think Gruda is more of a plan for the future rather than a definitive target for the winter or summer transfer windows. He's playing very well at Leipzig, but Martin Demichelis was very clear in his latest press conference that the teenager has to stay grounded and keep consistent at Leipzig. Let's see how things go, but I think Leipzig are destined to sign Gruda on a permanent basis in the summer.

Surely, if Michael Olise decided that he wanted to leave Bayern Munich, the club would likely have Gruda in its pool of potential replacement, but Olise is not expected to talk about his future with Bayern Munich until the summer.

By that time, Gruda is expected to be a full-time member of Die Roten Bullen.

Given the recent mass of injuries sustained by Bayern Munich, reports are emerging with different names being linked to the Bavarians. One of them is AS Roma's Donyell Malen, who had a stint with Borussia Dortmund:

Bayern Munich have been linked with Roma striker Donyell Malen after Harry Kane suffered a long-term ankle injury, with Paris Saint-Germain also reportedly keen. Kane, 33, will be out for several months, a blow that could push Bayern to sign a goalscorer in the January transfer window.

According to Calciomercato, Bayern are expected to position themselves for Malen, the prolific Netherlands striker. The Italian outlet says the 27-year-old 'deserves the attention' of the Bavarian hierarchy. Bayern have not made an offer, and signing him in the coming weeks looks very complicated given his outstanding 2026-27 season with Roma.

Malen has scored 16 goals in 15 matches in all competitions. The former Borussia Dortmund player leads the Serie A scoring charts with 12 goals in 11 appearances. On Saturday he scored four goals in Roma's 5-1 win at Parma. His xG of 9.71, higher than Kane's, confirms that the number 14 is overperforming.

The statistics may impress several clubs more prestigious than Roma, but Bayern have no chance of convincing the Italian club to release their number one attacking weapon in the middle of the season. Malen is under contract until June 2030, with a market value of £47m, and will not be sold before the summer of 2027.

Malen is a nice talent, but he would likely carry too hefty a price even if AS Roma were willing to sell him.

Bayern Munich star Harry Kane recently spoke of his admiration for Karim Benzema as a player (maybe not so much for some of his antics off the pitch).

"I've always been a huge admirer of Karim Benzema. What he achieved at Real Madrid in 2022 was truly impressive. He has the ability to drop into midfield and play almost like a number 10, dictating the game while still arriving in the box at exactly the right moment. We have slightly different physical attributes, but in terms of football intelligence, our ability to find space and our desire to help the players around us, I clearly see a lot of similarities in the way we play. Both of us have shown that you don't always have to be inside the box to score goals," Kane said.

Paris Saint-Germain megastar Khvicha Kvaratskhelia might be out with a torn ACL, but that will not stop him from getting a new contract:

Khvicha Kvaratskhelia is closing in on a new contract with Paris Saint-Germain, which could extend his stay until 2032. Nicolo Schira reports that the Georgia winger is in advanced talks with the back-to-back European champions about new terms, with his current deal running until 2029. The new contract is set to be until 2031, but with the option of an extra year. Kvaratskhelia has made 91 appearances for PSG since joining from Napoli, but he is set for an extended period on the sidelines as he recovers from a serious knee injury.

Real Madrid might want to bring its old charge, Como's Nico Paz, back:

Real Madrid are expected to trigger the €80m release clause in Nico Paz's contract at Como, following another impressive campaign in Italy. Mundo Deportivo claims that Los Blancos have until next summer to activate the clause after recent uncertainty over his future. The 22-year-old playmaker first joined Como for €6m in 2024, but a restructured €60m deal was reached last summer that increased his release clause. Paz has scored six goals and assisted eight in 14 appearances across all competitions, giving Real Madrid a huge decision to make over his future in the coming months.

To clarify the report, Real Madrid holds an €80 million buy-back option on Paz.
