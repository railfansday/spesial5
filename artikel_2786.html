Denzel Boston remains out of Thursday's practice with toe injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

On Wednesday, Browns head coach Todd Monken said he was anticipating rookie receiver Denzel Boston would practice on Thursday.

Boston is not participating on Thursday as he continues to deal with a toe injury, the club announced in its pre-practice report.

Monken said on Wednesday that he didn’t think Boston’s injury was a concern, calling it “a little toe issue.”

Boston leads the Browns with 507 receiving yards. He’s made 32 receptions with three touchdowns.

Browns starting center Elgton Jenkins (knee) is returning to Thursday’s practice.

Safety Ronnie Hickman (concussion) and fullback Michael Burton (calf) are slated to remain sidelined.
