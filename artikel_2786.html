Iowa high school football state championship game schedule released

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The semifinals of the Iowa high school football playoffs are in the books after an exciting slew of games throughout the state last week.

By the end of the week, only two teams remained standing with a shot at the state title in each of Iowa's seven classes, and by the end of next week, there will just be seven teams worthy of calling themselves state champions.

Here's a look at the schedule for next week's IHSAA football state championship games.

All games will be played at the UNI-Dome between Thursday, Nov. 19 and Friday, Nov. 20.
