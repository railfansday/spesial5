Thomas Booker, Dont'e Thornton are inactive for the Raiders

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Raiders had a pair of players listed as questionable on their final injury report of the week and neither of them will be in the lineup for Fernando Mendoza’s first NFL start.

Defensive tackle Thomas Booker will miss the game with an ankle injury while wide receiver Dont’e Thornton will sit after a back injury that popped up late in the week.

Wide receiver Malik Benson, safety Tristin McCollum, safety Dalton Johnson, and guard Bryce Cabeldue are also inactive for the Raiders.

The Seahawks ruled quarterback Sam Darnold, guard Quenton Nelson, left tackle Charles Cross, and defensive lineman Leonard Williams out during the week. Running back Emanuel Wilson, cornerback Avery Smith, and defensive tackle Deven Eastern are also out.
