UFC ‘Beast’ suspended for drug testing violation just two fights removed from Noche title shot

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

UFC flyweight veteran Manon "The Beast" Fiorot, who failed to unseat Valentina Shevchenko from the 125-pound throne in their five-round title fight in the UFC 315 co-main event back in early 2025, recently tested positive for ostarine and was suspended three months by Combat Sports Anti-Doping (CSAD).

From the official announcement on UFC.com:

"Fiorot tested positive for low levels of ostarine, a prohibited non-Specified Substance in the class of Anabolic Agents that is prohibited at all times under the UFC ADP. Ostarine was detected in an in-competition sample collected on September 12, 2026, at UFC Noche, where Fiorot competed against Alexa Grasso. Fiorot lost the bout. Before being notified of the adverse analytical finding from her September 12 sample, Fiorot provided an additional sample on October 19, 2026, which returned a negative result."

Turns out a tainted supplement was to blame.

"[Fiorot] detailed her work with a nutritionist in preparation for UFC Noche, explaining that the nutritionist had provided her with several dietary supplements during fight week that were new to her supplementation routine. Fiorot reported that she stopped using said supplements after fight week. After reviewing the evidence, CSAD determined that the findings were consistent with inadvertent exposure resulting from low-level ostarine contamination of the supplements, rather than intentional use. CSAD also concluded that the extremely low levels of ostarine present in the supplements did not provide Fiorot with any significant performance advantage."

The 36-year-old Fiorot (13-3) rebounded from her Shevchenko loss to destroy Jasmine Jasudavicius at UFC Vancouver later that year, only to suffer another setback against Grasso. Her period of ineligibility began on Oct. 19, 2026 (the date she was notified of her suspension) and will expire on Jan. 19, 2027.
