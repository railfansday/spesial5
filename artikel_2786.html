Indianapolis Colts Injury Report: Week 9 Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Indianapolis Colts today released their Wednesday injury report ahead of week 9 of the NFL season against the Dallas Cowboys.

Wide receiver Keenan Allen and cornerback Charvarius Ward both missed practice today. Both were on scheduled rest days. Running back Jonathan Taylor was limited today as part of his scheduled rest days. All will likely be full participants at practice tomorrow and Friday.

Cornerback Justin Walley (knee) missed practice practice today. Walley missed last weeks practices and Sundays game with the knee injury. It doesn't look promising so far for his availability for this weeks game.

Linebacker Bryce Boettcher (groin) was a full participant at practice today. Boettcher had missed time due to the groin injury so it is positive to see him managing to practice this week. It will be interesting to see the linebacker rotation moving forward once he is fit enough to play.

Linebacker Akeem Davis-Gaither (knee) missed practice today. Davis-Gaithersburg has missed multiple practices over the past few weeks with different injuries but it is a knee injury that stops him from going today.

Wide receiver Anthony Gould (concussion) was a full participant as was right TD Joe Jalen Travis. Gould looks to work his way out if the concussion protocol and be available for Sunday.

Tight end Andrew Ogletree (knee) missed practice today. Ogletree missed Sundays game with the knee injury and it is clearly still causing him to miss practices also.
