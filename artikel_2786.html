Sixers Bell Ringer: Vintage LeBron, VJ-Maxx produce non-clutch win vs. Heat, keep Cup chances alive

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Sixers Bell Ringer Season Standings:VJ Edgecombe – 4Jaylen Brown – 3.5Tyrese Maxey – 3.5Joel Embiid – 3LeBron James – 2Anfernee Simons – 1Dean Wade – 1

Only fitting for LeBron James to have his best game as a Sixer against the Miami Heat.

The King looked like the Heatles version Wednesday, securing his first triple-double of the season as the Sixers took down Miami 111-104 (full recap here). The win keeps the team's NBA Cup chances alive as they continue group play Friday night against the Indiana Pacers.

This was also a rare non-clutch win for the Sixers, who basically led from the second quarter on. James and Tyrese Maxey carried the offense for much of the night, while VJ Edgecombe filled in the gaps. With Joel Embiid out of the lineup because of an illness, the trio of Jabari Walker, Dominick Barlow and Adem Bona all made an impact.

The only bad news from the night was Jaylen Brown being forced to leave the game at the end of the first half. The team ruled him out for the second half with left hamstring tightness, an injury that forced him to miss two games last week.

(Happy Thanksgiving to all who celebrate! We're all thankful for all of you!)

LeBron James: 25 points, 12 assists, 10 rebounds, , 4-of-6 3PT, 1 steal

LeBron was sensational Wednesday night. His second-quarter heater, which saw him bury three triples — including a heat check from the parking lot — helped put the Sixers in front. They really controlled the game from there.

The threes definitely helped, but it was a vintage James performance. He bullied his way to the rim. He found open teammates with gorgeous passes. He pulled down tough rebounds.

At 41 years old and in his 24th season, James was the best player on the floor for much of the night. Incredible.

Tyrese Maxey: 30 points, 6 assists, 4 rebounds, 1 steal

Maxey has set a high standard for himself. Even he'd agree his last two games didn't meet it.

While he seemed more inclined to pick his spots over the last two contests, he was aggressive from the jump tonight, sensing his team needed a bit more offense. We're at a point where Maxey is establishing himself as one of the best three-level scorers in the league.

He got into the paint, hit a couple middies and nailed 3-of-5 from deep. When he's rolling like he was Wednesday, he's nearly impossible to guard — and Davion Mitchell is no slouch defensively.

VJ Edgecombe: 18 points, 6 rebounds, 5 assists, 2 steals

Another game where it just felt like Edgecombe was everywhere. The box score shows it, but he was relentless, seemingly playing at a different speed than anyone on the Heat. With Brown out, Edgecombe scored 13 of his points after halftime.

While the LeBron pass was ridiculous, don't sleep on the Edgecombe finish here.

The Center Triumvirate (Walker, Barlow, Bona): 16 points, 16 rebounds, 3 blocks, 1 steal

Piecing together 48 minutes without Embiid has generally felt like an impossible task. This season, it's felt even more difficult.

Against a frontcourt featuring Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo, the trio of Jabari Walker, Dominick Barlow and Adem Bona did a sensational job.

Antetokounmpo is great and he's going to get his, but those three (and Dean Wade) made him work for everything. Adebayo had a tough night, going just 6-of-16 from the field.

Barlow in particular has been outstanding since coming back from his back injury. Since making his season debut last Saturday against the Milwaukee Bucks, Barlow has been incredibly impactful offensively, defensively and on the glass.
