Zeek Biggers suffers season-ending injury; Could Christian Wilkins be a consideration?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Miami Dolphins defensive line suffered a major loss last week when Zach Sieler was placed on injured reserve following a biceps rupture sustained in the team's game against the Detroit Lions. A week later, they lost another key defensive tackle with Zeek Biggers sustaining a knee injury that will end his season. NFL Network's Ian Rapoport was the first to report the update.

Biggers will undergo surgery to repair the lateral meniscus in his right knee. He is expected to fully recover in time to start the 2027 season.

The Dolphins selected Biggers in the seventh round of the 2025 NFL Draft. He has appeared in 18 games, including all nine for Miami this season, with two starts and has recorded 38 tackles, 2.5 sacks, and a pass defensed in his career.

With Sieler on injured reserve and Biggers headed there, Miami is left with defensive tackles Jordan Phillips, Kenneth Grant, and Keith Cooper, Jr., on the 53-man roster. They also have Jordan Jefferson on the practice squad.

Phillips also left last Sunday's game against the Indianapolis Colts and had his arm in a sling after the game. He was expected to get an MRI on Monday to determine the severity of his injury.

The Dolphins could be looking to bring in a veteran defensive tackle to provide depth with he loss of Biggers, and could be desperate for additional linemen if Phillips is also unavailable for an extended period of time.

Miami could consider former Dolphins defensive tackle Christian Wilkins, if the veteran is healthy after a foot injury cost him most of 2024 and all of 2025. The former 13th overall pick spent five years in Miami before joining the Las Vegas Raiders in 2024. There was drama between Wilkins and the Raiders following his injury, with reported disagreements about his recovery plan as well as allegation that he tried to kiss a teammate on the head. Wilkins has not played since being released.

Bringing Wilkins back to Miami could give the Dolphins the veteran they need who can immediately slide into the starting lineup. How his foot healed after the injury and his playing shape would have to be determined before Miami would make any moves.

The Dolphins could also consider bringing back defensive tackles Matthew Butler or Fatorma Mulbah, both of whom were with the team in training camp before being cut ahead of the 53-man roster limit to start the regular season. Besides Wilkins, there also veterans like Quinton Jefferson and Maurice Hurst who could be considerations.

Miami face the Buffalo Bills in Week 11. Buffalo is the second-ranked rushing team in the league, averaging 152.7 yards per game this season, and will pressure the Dolphins interior throughout Sunday's game. The defensive tackles, including any additions this week, have to be ready for the onslaught coming their way.
