Patriots vs. Packers Thursday injury report: Marcus Jones added to the list of non-participants

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The New England Patriots continued Green Bay Packers work without four.

While defensive tackle Christian Barmore returned to practice in full on Thursday, cornerback Marcus Jones joined running back TreVeyon Henderson, tight end Hunter Henry and wide receiver A.J. Brown on the list of non-participants in Foxborough.

The all-purpose captain was sidelined due to a foot issue after beginning the week without limitations.

Here's the second injury report ahead of Sunday's 4:25 p.m. ET kickoff at Gillette Stadium.

Jones has played 81.4 percent of the defensive snaps for the Patriots this fall while appearing in every game. Making a handful of starts in the secondary, the two-time All-Pro stands with 41 tackles, one sack and one interception while also averaging 10.9 yards per punt return.

As for the three veterans sidelined on the offensive side of the ball, Henderson has now missed four consecutive practices with an illness and was downgraded to out on the eve of last weekend's Miami Dolphins matchup along with Brown. Like the sophomore running back, the June trade acquisition at wide receiver has also missed four straight sessions after a knee concerns joined the high ankle sprain on the injury report.

Henry, meanwhile, now begins the work week with back-to-back absences. The tenured tight end and team captain was announced as questionable to return before halftime at Hard Rock Stadium because of a foot injury. He was downgraded to out in the final quarter of what became a 24-19 AFC East win, finishing with three catches for 30 yards through just eight offensive snaps.

Eight Patriots practiced in a partial capacity on Thursday. The lone addition to limited group would be Schooler.

Last year's special teams captain is dealing with a hamstring ailment after spending all of training camp and the initial four games of the campaign on the non-football injury list. The core-four safety has logged four tackles over four games back from a back issue.

Elsewhere, Hollins and Van Roten saw their 21-day practice windows open as Packers prep got underway. The eldest statesman out wide landed on injured reserve with a calf injury in early October, while the eldest statesman along the offensive line landed on injured reserve with a hamstring injury in late September.

Barmore's return from an illness sent him back to full participation alongside the reigning AFC Offensive Player of the Month.

While Maye's throwing shoulder remains listed, the Patriots quarterback finished October with a 67.2 completion percentage along with 1,199 yards, 12 touchdowns, two interceptions and a 119.0 passer rating. He added 129 yards and a pair of touchdowns as a rusher.

And as the calendar turned in Miami Gardens, Maye began November by completing 16-of-20 throws for 199 yards with two touchdowns via air and another touchdown via ground.
