Editor-in-chief mailbag: The good, the bad and the ugly

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Sixers are 14-4, good for a 2.5 game lead over the Atlanta Hawks, Boston Celtics and New York Knicks for the top spot in the East. They're also 12-0 in clutch games. It's one of their best starts in franchise history. These are irrefutably good things. If anything, the consensus seemed to be the Sixers would start off slow and find their way as they built chemistry, but here we are.

Despite the record, the Sixers are 12th in the NBA in net rating. While they've moved into the top-10 in offensive rating, their defense has dropped to 15th. Winning clutch games isn't a bad thing, but the Sixers are playing a lot of them — and many of their own creation.

Despite going 6-2 in their last eight games, the Sixers have the second-worst defensive rating in the NBA in that span, only better than the 3-13 Brooklyn Nets. They've run into a couple teams having hot nights, but the defensive results have been ugly.

So, how are you feeling right now? The record is objectively great, but there are fair underlying questions. Hit me with you questions and thoughts in the comments.
