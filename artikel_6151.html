Craving an Island Escape? These Bahamas Airbnbs Are Just What the Doctor Ordered

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Growing up in Fort Lauderdale, the Bahamas always felt like an extension of South Florida to me. Just a short flight or boat ride away, the archipelago was somewhere I returned to often, and still do. Yet what continues to surprise me is how different one island can feel from the next. I've always thought it was a shame that so many visitors to the Bahamas never make it beyond Nassau or Freeport. Eleuthera has some of the prettiest beaches I've seen, the water around the Exumas is almost unbelievably clear, and the Abacos have small waterfront towns that are worth exploring. Each island has its own personality, and even after years of visiting, there are so many I still want to discover.

Renting a home is one of my favorite ways to experience it all. Beautiful beachfront villas, colorful cottages, and contemporary homes with pools overlooking the sea are everywhere in the Bahamas. Having your own place makes it easy to settle in, explore a little, and enjoy the particular rhythm of whichever island you've chosen. After so many visits, I’m eager to step slightly off the typical tourist path (although the resorts and hotels here are also beautiful), and these Bahamas Airbnbs are a great way to do so. Read on for my most-recommended rentals in the Bahamas, from beachfront stays to island cottages.
