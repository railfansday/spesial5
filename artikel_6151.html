Lufthansa Does The Most German Thing Ever: Restricts Access To Online Check-in [Roundup]

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

News and notes from around the interweb: Lufthansa now requires an account to check-in online maybe the most German thing ever? The airline group is making a ‘Travel ID’ account mandatory for online check-in, with U.S. departures covered starting November 16 and the rollout completed November 30, though exceptions include tickets issued by other airlines and airport check-in.
