Prediction: Cagliari vs AC Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Turning their attention back to Serie A action on Monday night, AC Milan will travel to Sardinia when they meet Cagliari.

With Italy’s elite stealing centre stage on Monday, there are plenty of eye-catching Serie A betting promotions available.

Two camps who find the moods in their respective camps at complete polar opposites, you can find all of the key Cagliari vs AC Milan H2H stats here.

Verdict: Christian Pulisic over 0.5 shots on target

Starting with the hosts, while Cagliari might have claimed an essential Serie A boost prior to November’s international break as they clinched a hard-fought 1-0 victory at home against Frosinone, it appears that was just some minor consolation for Fabio Pisacane’s downbeat squad. Making an immediate return to losing ways last time out as they marked their return from November’s fortnight interval with a routine 2-0 defeat away at Como, the Islanders have come crashing back down to earth since their sensational start to proceedings. Signing off October with a rollercoaster 4-2 loss at the hands of Roma, Cagliari have now lost five of their previous six appearances across all competitions. Likewise, putting just a single domestic victory on the board since a 1-0 win away at Udinese on September 19th, Monday’s hosts should be bracing themselves for another stern examination in Sardinia.

Yet to feature over the past month and once again missing out against Como last weekend with a recent back issue, Cagliari will be without former Tottenham midfielder Harry Winks here. Likewise, dealing with a reported thigh injury, the Islanders are still sweating over the fitness of ex-Everton defender Yerry Mina.

Featuring for Uruguay over November’s international break and receiving plenty of admirers from across the continent, 21-year-old Juan Rodriguez could be handed an immediate full recall. Notching five Serie A strikes so far this season, Atalanta loanee Daniel Maldini will spearhead Cagliari’s attack.

As for the visitors, while there might have been plenty of raised eyebrows across the continent following Ruben Amorim’s arrival over the summer, AC Milan are clearly relishing life under the former Manchester United boss. Getting their Europa League charge back on track in midweek as they clinched a thrilling 4-2 victory away at Olympiacos, the Italian icons should find the mood in their camp at a red-hot high. Marking their return from November’s international break with a dominant display as they stormed their way to a 5-1 romp at home against Frosinone, AC Milan have won each of their previous three appearances across all competitions by an aggregate score of 11-4. Likewise, gaining a real reputation for their clinical performances in the final third, Amorim’s men have also suffered just a sole defeat from any of their first 12 Serie A showdowns. It should also be highlighted, Monday’s guests clinched a 1-0 victory when they last travelled to Sardinia for a top-flight battle back in January.

Suffering a recent thigh injury, AC Milan have already confirmed that veteran superstar and former Ballon d’Or winner Luka Modric will miss out on Monday night. Likewise, dealing with what has become a stacked injury list, Amorim’s camp will travel to Sardinia without the likes of Ruben Loftus-Cheek, Matteo Gabbia and Belgium international Alexis Saelemaekers.

Producing a Man of the Match display against Olympiacos in midweek and notching a maiden Europa League strike, Nottingham Forest loanee Omari Hutchinson will keep his spot from the start. Enjoying what has become a sensational flurry and now notching eight goals in his previous nine appearances for club and country, USA skipper Christian Pulisic could play a leading role in the final third.

Clearly relishing life under Amorim’s guidance, AC Milan are riding a serious wave of momentum. Mounting a Europa League comeback away at Olympiacos on Thursday evening as they clinched a thrilling 4-2 victory on foreign soil, Monday’s guests will be confident of laying down another marker away from the San Siro. Marking their return from November’s fortnight interval with a thumping 5-1 win against Frosinone, AC Milan will be confident of exploiting a Cagliari squad who find themselves at the heart of a public crisis. Tumbling down the Serie A standings and losing five of their last six top-flight matchups, the Islanders should be bracing themselves for another stern examination.
