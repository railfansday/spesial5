Manchester United handed huge transfer boost as left-back ‘wants £50m move’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Manchester United have been handed a boost in their transfer pursuit of Maxi Araujo.

That is according to Sport Witness, who claim that the Red Devils could seal a deal worth between €50million and €60m for the left-back.

The left-sided full-back position has been a source of real problems for United, and particularly for new manager Mauricio Pochettino, who is still awaiting his first win in charge since his arrival last week.

The Argentine took the reins at Old Trafford last Wednesday, leading his side to Anfield for a 4-0 debut demolition before a 2-2 draw at Sporting in the Champions League on Tuesday.

In that Sporting side was Araujo, 26, who is believed to be open to a move to the Premier League.

The Uruguay international has previously been linked with a switch to the north west amid the pending expiry of Luke Shaw’s contract and Harry Amass’ uncertain future at Old Trafford.

Now, Sport Witness claim that United’s scouts took advantage of their fixture in the Portuguese capital to make a proper assessment of Araujo, with there thought to be appreciation of the player.

The Uruguayan is understood to have a €80m release clause in his contract at the Primeira Liga outfit, but it is believed that United would be able to negotiate the asking price down.

In Shaw’s injury-hit absence this term, Pochettino’s predecessor Michael Carrick was forced to field natural right-backs Diogo Dalot and Noussair Mazraoui on the left side of defence, while Pochettino has favoured centre-half Lisandro Martinez in that position since he came in.
