Opinion: There's another Ngumoha and Liverpool can upset Chelsea all over again

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Liverpool can do it to Chelsea all over again, as a new Rio Ngumoha emerges.

It sent shockwaves around English football when the Reds secured the arrival of their exciting mercurial talent from Stamford Bridge.

Ngumoha had come through the ranks at Cobham, but upon celebrating his 16th birthday and having been offered scholarship terms with Chelsea, he rejected them.

The pathway to the first-team was blocked for Ngumoha at Chelsea, or at least that is how he and his representatives saw. Liverpool offered an opportunity.

The club has a good track-record with younger players and they presented Ngumoha with the kind of pathway to first-team football that was easy to see.

While Chelsea sign every other talent on the planet, Liverpool are much more selective with their choices and that strategy has paid off.

Ngumoha has started a civil war in English academy footballThe reality is that the move for Ngumoha set a precedent for the rest of the English footballing landscape. Its ripple effects were well and truly felt last summer. There was a lot of merry-go-rounds and ample aggressive moves made by clubs in order to try and poach the elite talents away from their respective teams. At the centre of it were Liverpool again. Joshua Abe was wanted by Arsenal, Chelsea and Manchester City. He turned down a £50k a week offer to stay at Liverpool because of the pathway. Vincent Joseph however chose to leave Liverpool and join Arsenal. Liverpool were also keen to sign Xavier Parker, but City ended-up offering him such impressive terms he simply could not reject.

Meet Jacob Dako - the next NgumohaOne of the most exciting young stars at Chelsea right now is Dako, who is following in Ngumoha's footsteps. At 15-years-old, Dako is already a regular for Chelsea at U18 level and has even made his UEFA Youth League debut. A left-footed technical central midfielder with great passing range, Dako has seven goal contributions for Chelsea at U18 and U19 level so far this season. He is the same age Ngumoha was in his final campaign at Chelsea's academy and he is looking like another elite talent. At the end of the season, Dako will have the chance to extend his deal with the club and sign scholarship terms or move to pastures new. That's when Liverpool can pounce and upset their rivals again. Dako is exactly the type of midfielder who could flourish under Andoni Iraola and Ngumoha's example has made it clear, there's a fast-track pathway to the senior team at Anfield. That alone should be enticing enough.
