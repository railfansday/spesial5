Manchester United appointed Mauricio Pochettino partly because he can salvage relationships with Bruno Fernandes and Lisandro Martinez, Football Insider understands.

Following the defeat to Como in the Champions League, both players hit out at various levels of the club, with Martinez even targeting his rant at sporting director Jason Wilcox.

United captain Fernandes has even turned down a new contract at Old Trafford, not satisfied by the current terms being offered.

Football Insider understands that the main hope is for Pochettino to use his warm management style to get this duo, as well as long-standing defender Luke Shaw, in a positive and motivated frame of mind in the hope they can influence the rest of the dressing room into getting behind the new boss.

Should results turn around in the coming weeks, sources say the club would not look to spend on strengthening the squad in the January transfer window.

To make matters more confusing, Football Insider has already revealed that Pochettino wants Wilcox replaced as the club's sporting director.

Fernandes has turned down a new contract, but that doesn't mean he won't put pen to paper on fresh terms before his deal expires.

Further negotiations are already ongoing behind the scenes at Old Trafford, and the Red Devils do have an option to extend by a year.

Many fans are of the opinion that the two parties need to go their separate ways, but replacing the 32-year-old will be an extremely difficult task.

Martinez, on the other hand, is definitely expected to leave after his outburst, with Bryan King telling Football Insider that he already wants to move to Atletico Madrid.

To top it all off, Shaw is in the final year of his contract, and there has been no movement over the 31-year-old's situation.

It certainly doesn't look good for Wilcox, who, as revealed earlier, could be on the chopping block after the arrival of Pochettino this season.

The former Tottenham Hotspur boss wants a sporting director that he can trust, and that clearly isn't Wilcox, who is often under pressure.

A lack of transfer activity at Old Trafford is only going to anger the fans more, but that is just reality under INEOS and the Glazers.

Football Insider understands that if the Red Devils are to invest, it will solely be on new defenders, with an entire shake-up needed at the back.
