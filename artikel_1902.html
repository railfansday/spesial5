Sources: Chelsea's stance on Xabi Alonso's future updated

Chelsea’s board have no plans to sack manager Xabi Alonso at the moment, sources have told Football Insider.

The Blues have won just one of their last five games in all competitions, a run which culminated in a heavy 4-1 defeat at home to Leeds United on Saturday.

While there are no immediate plans for his dismissal, Alonso is understood to be under pressure to get results from the next two games against Nottingham Forest and Crystal Palace.

Sources say that the following three games ahead of Christmas could then “make or break” his future, with clashes against Liverpool, Manchester City and Aston Villa all on the horizon.

Chelsea want to give the Spaniard time to get things right, but are also aware that they have shown plenty of faith in him so far, and committed plenty of resources.

They are now sitting as low as eighth in the Premier League table, heading into what is expected to be a pivotal stage of their season.

Chelsea’s form has dipped dramatically in recent months, and the pressure has been building on Alonso with every passing week.

Fans chanted against the 44-year-old during last weekend’s defeat at Stamford Bridge, and they are growing increasingly impatient over the team’s poor form.

However, the club hierarchy feel that Alonso’s chances haven’t been helped by the continued absence of striker Joao Pedro, who was a key figure at the start of the season.

The Brazilian has been out since the middle of October with a thigh injury, and the lack of presence at the top of the pitch caused by his absence has coincided with results falling off.

The board aren’t happy with the situation at Stamford Bridge at the moment, but remain hopeful that Pedro’s return to action could spark an upturn in form among the squad.

Football Insider revealed on Sunday that Alonso is facing a mutiny in the Chelsea squad, with players not buying into his coaching methods behind the scenes.

There is understood to be plenty of discontent on the training ground, with several staff members believing that the players “packed it in” during the recent defeat to Leeds.

Sources say that there is a “terrible atmosphere in the dressing room” at the moment, with the group beginning to rail against Alonso’s authority already.

The former Real Madrid boss only took his place in the dugout in the summer, but it has already started to turn sour for him in West London.
