‘One of my favourite loans’ - Carl Ikeme recalls Middlesbrough FC experience as Championship prediction made

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Carl Ikeme has fondly recalled his loan spell at Middlesbrough - and believes anything but automatic promotion this season would be disappointing for the club.

The retired goalkeeper spent the entirety of his career on the books of Wolverhampton Wanderers, who loaned him out on nine separate occasions.

He spent time at Middlesbrough during the 2011/12 season and made 10 appearances during the reign of Tony Mowbray.

A number of the 40-year-old’s former clubs are in the Championship promotion race, therefore it is something he is keeping a close eye on.

“I think there’s some strong teams,” he told The Yorkshire Post. “I think out of the teams that were left in the Championship, they (Middlesbrough) were the standout team.

“They nearly did it last year as well, they've probably learnt from that experience. They've got a great set-up at Boro as well, a really good club. It was one of my favourite loans, if not my favourite loan, great club, great set-up.

“They've got a great owner as well and he's been there for a long time and he really takes his time and knows what he's doing. I'm not saying they're the team to beat but they've hit form quite soon. I think that obviously Wolves and West Ham will be there or thereabouts as well.

“I think they'll be disappointed if they don't go up automatically this year but that being said, with Wolves, West Ham and Burnley coming down, it's going to be tough to get up for anyone. You've got the teams in and around the play-off picture, you've got the chasing pack as well.”

Middlesbrough came within a whisker of promotion last term, losing to Hull City in the play-off final at Wembley.

Kim Hellberg’s side did not spend too long licking their wounds and sit second in the table after 15 games.

“Out of the teams that were left in the Championship, they do look like the strongest,” Ikeme said. “I think Wolves will be hoping to kick on as the year goes on but Boro do look like one of the teams that will be in and around it.”
