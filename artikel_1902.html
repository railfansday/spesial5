What channel is Celtic v Kilmarnock? TV and live stream plus team news, referee and VAR

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Celtic will look to kick on following their midweek victory in Europe when they host Kilmarnock in the Scottish Premiership on Sunday.

A thrilling 3-2 win over Besiktas at Celtic Park on Thursday finally earned Martin O’Neill’s side their first points in the Europa League after three successive defeats by Ferencvaros, Benfica and Celta Viga.

The triumph over the Turkish giants provided the Hoops with a much-needed boost after the 2-0 defeat by Falkirk last weekend, which left O’Neill under pressure following a run of just three victories in 10 matches.

Celtic were four points clear at the top of the table heading into the weekend, but Rangers closed the gap to just one point when they defeated St Mirren 2-0 in Paisley on Saturday night.

Kilmarnock have struggled to keep the ball out of their net so far this season with 28 goals conceded in 12 games the worst defensive record in the division by some distance. As a result, Neil McCann’s side have collected only nine points from a possible 36 and sit 11th in the table, one point ahead of bottom club Hibs who have a game in hand.

The Ayrshire club are winless in 22 visits to Celtic in all competitions since a 2-0 league victory in October 2012.

Interestingly, no side has won more points from losing positions in the Premiership this season than Celtic (7), while no team has lost more from winning positions than Kilmarnock (7).

The Scottish Premiership match between Celtic and Kilmarnock takes place at Celtic Park, Glasgow, on Sunday, November 8, 2026. Kick-off is 4pm.

The match has not been selected for live TV broadcast in the UK. Highlights will be shown on Sportscene on the BBC Scotland channel at 7.15pm on Sunday with the programme repeated on BBC One Scotland at 11.50pm.

The game is available to live stream in the UK via CelticPlayer. A pay-per-view pass costs £12.99 and can be purchased here.

Yang Hyun-jun could feature for Celtic after a hamstring injury but Alistair Johnston (hamstring) and Auston Trusty (family bereavement) remain out.

Kilmarnock were without Mark O’Hara, Bailey Rice and Johnly Yfeko last weekend while goalkeeper Max Stryjek is a long-term absentee due to a knee injury.

John Beaton will referee the game at Celtic Park. He will be assisted on the touchlines by David Roome and Gordon McCabe, with Colin Steven serving as the fourth official. The Video Assistant Referee (VAR) duties will be handled by Grant Irvine, who will be supported by Assistant VAR (AVAR) Dan McFarlane.
