The best toy deals to add to your cart during Black Friday: Tonies, Magna-Tiles, Lego, and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Besides TVs, toys are basically the classic deals you think of when you think of Black Friday. In 2026, Black Friday toy discounts are still some of the most extreme. And thankfully, you no longer need to wait in line at the crack of dawn and fight strangers to score them.

From popular storytelling toys like Tonies, to building toys like Magna-Tiles and Lego, to board game classics like Operation, there are already plenty of Black Friday toy deals worth adding to your cart at Amazon, Walmart, and Target. We've rounded up some of the best ones we've seen so far, but definitely check back as we'll be adding to this list as the holiday shopping season continues on.

When Mashable's reviewer tested the OG Toniebox, she called it "an intuitively-designed storyteller" and a "screen-less winner for children." It's a great way for kids to control their entertainment without the overstimulation of screens. The Toniebox 2 builds on the popularity of the Toniebox 1, adding more interactive features, expanded storage, a younger age rating, and more. If you snag it from Walmart bundled with the Moana figurine, you'll save $50.02 during Black Friday. The Toniebox 2 on its own usually costs $129.99, so that's basically like getting the Moana figurine for free and an additional discount.

If your kiddo is not a Moana fanatic, you can go with the starter set featuring the Playtime Puppy for the sale price of $99.99. It's also worth pointing out the Toniebox 2 Audio Player Pokémon Starter Set is on sale for $99.
