GTA 6 Has A Lot Of Different Bird Species, Here’s How Many

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 6 has a lot of details and depth, including dozens of different bird species for people to find. If you're a birder, you're in for a good time.

According to The Guardian, Rockstar confirmed that there are 62 types of birds that players can spot. Players can whip out binoculars to "study" birds and animals like they could in Rockstar's last game, Red Dead Redemption II.

As PC Gamer reminds us, Florida is home to more than 500 bird types, so GTA 6 will have just a fraction of the different kinds of birds people can find in real Florida. GTA 6 was never going to replicate every single bird, of course.

Some of the birds that are found in Florida, and probably GTA 6 as well, include flamingoes, spoonbills, wood storks, egrets, kites, terns, and scrub jays. Florida is also home to bald eagles, and we'd expect them to show up in GTA 6.

Inclusive of birds, GTA 6 has more than 170 animal types, including cane toads, alligators, manatees, and rhesus macaque monkeys.

GTA 6 launches on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The game launches at midnight local time, so it will be playable for people in New Zealand and Australia very soon. You can switch your Xbox region to New Zealand to play early, if that works.
