Rangers hitman makes impact off the bench but late goal sees team sink to record breaking low

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Rangers striker Ryan Naderi continued his strong recent form but was denied a morale-boosting draw as a dramatic late goal saw Czechia slump to a record-breaking 2-1 defeat against Croatia in Osijek.

Already relegated to League B before the game, the visitors entered the clash without a win in eight, and had lost all five of their Nations League games, conceding 12 goals and scoring just twice.

Naderi, who opted to represent Czechia last month despite also being eligible to represent Germany and Bulgaria, made his international debut as a second-half substitute in a 3-1 defeat to world champions Spain in October before making his full debut against England at Wembley a few days later.

He had to be content with a place on the bench, though, as Czechia went in search of their first Nations League points against Croatia on Sunday. However, they started the game in the worst possible fashion, with veteran winger Ivan Perisic putting the home side ahead after just 87 seconds via a clinical left-footed strike from the edge of the penalty area.

Searching for a quickfire second, Croatia piled on the pressure following their early opener, but Czechia stood firm until half-time. Looking to restore parity, Czechia began to get back into the game after the restart, with Antonin Kinsky, Idris Hopi and Adam Hlozek all attempting to get past Barcelona stopper Dominik Livakovic in the home side’s net. With the game entering the final stages, Rangers forward Naderi was introduced in the 74th minute as a substitute for Christophe Kabongo, and he helped Czechia level the score shortly afterwards.

Scoring nine goals and assisting five more for Rangers this season, he was on the field just 10 minutes before he found strike partner Adam Hlozek in a dangerous scoring position. His initial effort was blocked, but Hlozek reacted quickest to rifle home a left-footed finish to level the level. However, just as it appeared that Naderi’s had helped his side steal a late point, Croatia restored their lead three minutes later through substitute Lovro Majer.

Taking a pass from Brighton centre-back Luka Vuskovic in the away side’s area, he drilled a low drive into the net to condemn Czechia to a seventh consecutive defeat and the worst losing streak in their history.

Beginning in the group stages of this summer’s World Cup in North America, Czechia’s slide began with a 3-0 defeat to Mexico and has carried straight through into a brutal Nations League campaign against Spain, England, and Croatia.
