Motherwell vs Rangers injury news: 6 out and several doubts as Derek McInnes waits on two at Fir Park

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Here is the latest team news ahead of Motherwell v Rangers in the Scottish Premiership at Fir Park on Saturday night.

Rangers will look to keep the pressure on top-of-the-table Celtic when they make the short visit to Motherwell at Fir Park in the Scottish Premiership on Saturday night (Kick-off: 8pm).

Unbeaten in their last 12 games in all competitions, Rangers sit four points behind Celtic in second place with a game in hand, though they could start the game seven points behind their Glasgow rivals, who face St Mirren earlier in the day.

As for their hosts, Motherwell, they have shown improvement in recent weeks after a six-game losing streak threatened to derail their campaign. Now unbeaten in their last three games, they enter the game four points from the top six and are looking for a victory that could reignite their hopes of a European spot.
