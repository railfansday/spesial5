Chema Andres is Brighton's next superstar, stats show he can take them to Champions League

Chema Andres has been doing Brighton's dirty work as they have climbed into the Premier League's top four.

The summer signing has been an unassuming pick-up so far, but that may be about to change as the Seagulls continue their impressive form.

The Premier League side paid around £15.5million to take him from Stuttgart, in what is looking to be quite the bargain transfer.

While his contributions may not always grab the headlines, his defensive work has been crucial to Brighton.

New Premier League data is emerging after 10 games, showing just how important he has been.

And that will certainly catch attention across the league.

While Brighton are the leading goalscorers in the Premier League, their defensive work has been just as good.

Fabian Hurzeler's side have conceded just seven goals in 10 games, and much of that has been down to both the back line as well as their sitting midfielder.

As per Data.MB, Brighton star Andres is one of the leading midfielders in a number of statistics, highlighting his importance to their early season form.

In the league, onper-90 90 basis, Andres has the most defensive duels won (5.74), while he is a second-place player in possession won minus possession lost (4.64).

Aerially, the Spanish midfielder has also been key as well, coming in second in aerial duels won (2.22).

And then in fifth place for pass completion percentage to the opposition's penalty box (80 per cent).

Overall, he is a multi-faceted midfielder, but his ability to win his duels and regain possession has been key to Brighton's in-game dominance.

Andres is Brighton's next big star, and his performances are up there with the likes of Declan Rice and Elliott Anderson this season, which can send the Seagulls to the Champions League.

Brighton have already seen Andres' price rise during his short time at the AMEX Stadium.

After signing him for around £15.5m, he is now estimated to be worth £20m according to Transfermarkt.

If he continues to showcase the quality he has put on so far, then the Seagulls could have another £100m player on their hands.

Especially if he can push Brighton into Europe's premier competition - the Champions League.
