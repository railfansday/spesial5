Derek McInnes issues stern Rangers transfer warning as Ibrox boss sends key message to possible winter suitors

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Derek McInnes has stressed that Rangers have no intention of selling their key players in January as he looks to maintain the club’s growing momentum ahead of Saturday night’s Premiership clash with Motherwell.

The Ibrox boss believes retaining the core of his squad will be vital to Rangers’ silverware ambitions this season, insisting that the club has no desire to part with regular starters despite potential interest from elsewhere.

While conceding that an offer too good to turn down would have to be considered, McInnes emphasised that Rangers are keen to avoid disruption to the squad when the winter transfer window opens.

“We will be keeping them,” he declared emphatically. “We're not looking to move players out, certainly players who have been playing regularly. We want to shut that down really early. We know and understand that keeping that core of players and having that stability is so important for us.”

Rangers return to league action against a Motherwell side McInnes claims is beginning to rediscover the qualities that made them such a formidable opponent last season under Jens Berthel Askou.

They head to Fir Park aiming to extend their 12-game unbeaten run, but McInnes anticipates a difficult test against a Motherwell side that has shown signs of improvement under Alfred Johansson’s care in recent weeks.

“I think they've started to look a wee bit more like that team that got a lot of success last year,” McInnes admitted. “They've got a lot of traits and similarities. They've lost two or three key players, but they still have the nucleus of that team and you can see they've got a clear idea of what they're trying to do.

“The difficulty when you go away from home is obvious. You've got to make sure you do so much right. It's not just about being good technically. You've got to make sure that all the other facets are there, that you need to compete right from the outset.”

McInnes highlighted Rangers’ recent away win at St Mirren as a blueprint for Saturday’s encounter, adding: “Something similar will be required. If we want to get to where we want to get to this season, it's a game that presents us with three points. And we want to make sure we leave Motherwell with all three, if we can.”

Rangers have kept eight clean sheets during their near two-month unbeaten run, with McInnes eager for his side to maintain their defensive solidity as they enter a demanding run of fixtures.

“I don't like it when we're too comfortable," he explained. “If we're four or five up and we’re deep into the second half, then I'll be all fine. But when the game is still in the balance, particularly away from home, and you're 1-0 up or 2-0 up, there's still work to do.

“We should be proud of that [clean sheets], but I thought we put it at risk a wee bit once or twice against St Mirren and I think it was unnecessary. We've got to make sure that we don't get complacent with what we're doing and we stay on top of the job."

With three consecutive home fixtures following Saturday's trip to North Lanarkshire, Rangers have an opportunity to build further momentum. McInnes, however, knows the immediate priority is on overcoming a rejuvenated Motherwell side.

“We're not expecting it to be straightforward,” he stated. “But I do expect us to bring a strong performance if we're going to win the game. I think everybody, certainly me and the staff, and I'm sure the supporters are the same, are starting to see a team that we think we can get behind and get a bit of confidence from.”

There was positive news on the injury front following the international break, with Daisuke Yokota returning to training earlier this week alongside Ross McCrorie, while Lawrence Shankland and Cammy Devlin are continuing their rehabilitation through increased work on the grass.
