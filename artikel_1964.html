Ahead of each game we ask one of our writers to come up with three positives and three negatives following the match.

Plenty to talk about after this Premier League game.

Newcastle United a goal down at the  break having been totally outplayed by the league leaders, only for then a very different second half.

The only place to start is the way that the team responded after the break.

It was a real tonic after recent home games. The changes worked and they came to life, leaving the Premier League champions rattled

If we really are in a process of rebuilding then the manager was right when he said that this second half spell is what they have to aim for more often.

Lewis Miley had a really good game in his proper position in the centre of midfield, and it was Bruno came off second best, at times.

It was frustrating to see Sean Steur hooked at half time but Jacob Ramsey showed up well when he came off the bench along with Elanga.

Miley is still such a young player, which highlights that we have a great talent on our hands.

It helps ease the pain of letting Elliot Anderson slip through our grasp.

Many hours before this game kicked off, I was of the mind that I’d snap hands off for a draw against Arsenal.

We were at least able to match them in spells.

A point against them might just well be one of those that proves extremely valuable come the end of the season.

The first half contained all of the drift we have seen too many times this season.

If your glass is half empty, then the fightback could be a case of a stopped clock being right twice a day.

Some concerns definitely do remain about our direction and the input of the new manager in this post-Eddie Howe era, the so-called Newcastle 2.0.

Another winning position has been thrown away.

The same thing happened down at Fulham. It drives you crackers at times.

They surely must have to work on how to see a game out? It’s not rocket science.

The manager gets paid to pick the team, and that will always be a talking point.

However, Jaissle left a lot of players out of this game, a total of around £260m worth no less.

Toure and Bamba were also omitted from the actual match day squad.

Wasn’t Toure doing really well when he played over in Germany? Why is he deemed nowhere near for a Premier League game when he’s got bags of pace and skill to burn?

Were these two players signed as first teamers for this season?

I’ve seen reports saying that the manager is calling for more recruitment in January. It’s all a bit baffling.
