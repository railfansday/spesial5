Everton set sights on Samuel Lino

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

According to the Liverpool Echo, Everton have identified Samuel Lino as a potential transfer target as the club looks to strengthen its options on the wing. The Brazilian has been in excellent form for Flamengo, contributing 21 goals and assists in 33 league appearances this season.

Everton are believed to be assessing the wide areas of their squad amid uncertainty surrounding the long-term future of Jack Grealish. While youngster Tyrique George has been trusted on the left flank in recent matches, the club may be keen to add experience and competition for places rather than rely solely on the 20-year-old.

Lino’s attacking output has made him one of Flamengo’s standout performers under Leonardo Jardim. He has played a major role for the Brazilian giants, ranking among the squad leaders for minutes played while also emerging as the team’s top assist provider.

The 26-year-old already has significant European experience. During his time at Atletico Madrid, he made 93 appearances under Diego Simeone between 2023 and 2025, while he has also earned four senior caps for Brazil.
