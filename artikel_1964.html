Sources: Man United to 'assess' Noussair Mazraoui contract as 'defensive overhaul' accelerates

Manchester United manager Mauricio Pochettino is keen to "assess" right-back Noussair Mazraoui in person before deciding whether to terminate his career at the club, sources have told Football Insider.

The Morocco international is being heavily linked with moves to both Italy and Saudi Arabia at the moment, but was highly regarded by the previous regime at Old Trafford.

Mazraoui is out of contract in the summer of 2028, but United have the option to extend that deal by a further year should they wish to.

Sources say that Pochettino wants to take a close look at the 29-year-old in the coming weeks before deciding if a new contract should become a priority.

Neither the club nor the player are in any rush to finalise a new deal quickly, given that his current contract still has just over 18 months left to run.

Mazraoui has endured an injury-hit start to the campaign and has made just five Premier League appearances so far.

United view their defence as their major failing at the moment, and plans are already in place for that area of the squad to be transformed.

The Red Devils are planning a "defensive overhaul" across the next few transfer windows, but Mazraoui could yet escape the fate that is likely to befall several of his teammates.

The former Bayern Munich man is viewed as a key part of the project at Old Trafford, with his versatility giving him an important role in the squad.

Mazraoui can play in either full-back position and has even filled in at centre-back when called upon.

His contract situation is beginning to pose something of an issue for the club, but Pochettino is keen to get his feet under the table before he settles on a decision.

Mazraoui will have to impress the Argentine in the coming weeks, in order to secure a long-term future with the club.

Football Insider revealed on Tuesday that Brentford are "doing everything" in their power to keep hold of centre-back Nathan Collins amid interest from United.

The Red Devils are impressed by the defender's reliability and his leadership skills, and are keen to add him to their backline this winter.

However, the Bees are under no pressure to sell their captain, as he is undr contract until 2029, and they also have an extension option on that deal.

United are clear admirers of Collins' ability, but it has become clear that they may have to pay over the odds in order to tempt Brentford to sell.
