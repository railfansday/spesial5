Ring rusty Hammer makes memorable international return – for all the wrong reasons | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

An unfortunate return to international football last night for one of West Ham’s senior stalwarts was seized upon by his native media as the briefest re-introduction to the Czechia side after a five month absence turned a wee bit sour for Tomas Soucek.

Following his ankle ligament injury and three month layoff after the summer World Cup, Soucek was a 85th minute substitute, called onto the pitch with his side already 1-0 down to Spain with just a handful of minutes left on the clock: Sadly for West Ham’s senior midfielder, his return did not go as he’d have envisioned.

Sub – par sub: Soucek’s return to international football went horribly wrong

Playing out from the back has never been Tornado Tom’s strong point and it all went horribly wrong as the Spaniards were gifted a second goal.

Seznamzpravy  described Soucek’s brief calamity cameo:

“In the last seconds, Pubill punished Soucek’s unnecessary hesitation in front of his own penalty area.

“Instead of a quick pass, Soucek held the ball, lost it in a duel and then watched the destruction lying down.”

Soucek would have been hoping for minutes to build up his own fitness and confidence after a shaky return to domestic football: He’ll get anther chance on Sunday when his national side take on Croatia.

Hopefully, Tornado Tom will be error free next time out, else you do start to fear for the international future for Soucek who, at 31, is getting pretty close to the sunset of his stellar Czech Republic career that has seen the player captain the side in many of his 91 appearances.
