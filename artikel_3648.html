Rockstar Explains Why GTA 6 Has No Prostitutes In It, A Hallmark Of The Series Since 2001

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grand Theft Auto 6 is a very big game and includes almost every feature seen in a past GTA game, including the ability to drive tanks, customize cars with turbo, and rob stores. However, one feature that isn’t included in GTA 6 is the ability to hire a sex worker off the street, something players have been doing since 2001.

In my over 150 hours playing GTA 6, which is a very good game in case you didn’t hear already, I never encountered a sex worker on the streets of Vice City. To be honest, I never really sought out prostitutes, as I was roleplaying Jason and Lucia as a loving couple who cared about each other deeply. But yeah, when I did look around for sex workers walking the street, I didn’t spot any. And that’s because Rockstar Games didn’t include them in GTA 6.

In an interview with Shack News, Rockstar said that it only included features that felt necessary and complementary to Jason and Lucia’s criminal tale.

The first Grand Theft Auto game to include prostitutes that players could hire was GTA 3, released in 2001. Since then, every mainline GTA game has included sex workers that players can pay for services. However, GTA 6 is breaking that 25-year trend, and honestly, I didn’t even notice until someone else pointed it out online. Turns out, the ability to hire prostitutes isn’t necessary to make a good GTA.

There are still strip clubs with plenty of nudity in GTA 6. And you can always come home and make Jason and Lucia have sex if you want some fun.
