GTA 6 Music Festival Announced For Miami With A Stacked Lineup Of Artists

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Rockstar Games has announced Grand Theft Auto 6: The Festival, a concert coming to the Mana Wynwood in Miami on November 21.

The festival features a lineup of artists from the game and the official soundtrack, including Cardi B, Travis Scott, Kodak Black, and Justice. The event is sponsored in part by PlayStation and produced by the team that created the III Points event in Miami. The "one-of-a-kind launch event," as Rockstar calls it, will feature two stages of "non-stop music."

You can't buy tickets. Instead, you need to enter a lottery and hope to be chosen. The lottery is open now and closes tomorrow, November 10, at 4 PM ET.

Winners will be drawn the same day, and they can buy tickets on November 11. There is a two-ticket maximum, tickets are non-transferable, and you must be 17 or older to attend.

GTA 6 launches on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the festival celebrates the game's release.

Justice, the French electronic music duo, composed the score for GTA 6. The game’s in-game radio stations are filled with big hits from famous bands and artists, and podcasts will also be available to listen to in your earbuds as you commit various crimes across Vice City.
