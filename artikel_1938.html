Eastbourne United stun Bognor Regis Town in the Sussex Senior Cup - in pictures

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

It was goalless midway through the second half but all the drama was to come.

Ex-Rocks striker Callum Barlow put United ahead and James Stone made it 2-0 before Billy Allcock pulled one back for the Rocks.

Joe Overy pounced for a 3-1 lead and Cal Laycock scored his second goal in two games to pull it back to 3-2 in the 90th minute. But there was no time for a Rocks rescue act and it is Anthony Storey’s side who go through to the quarter-finals.
