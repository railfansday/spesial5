‘Build slowly’ - Leeds United told what would constitute success this season by ex-Whites defender Danny Mills

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Consolidation in the Premier League would constitute a successful season for Leeds United, according to former Whites defender Danny Mills.

Leeds sit ninth in the table after 10 games and have taken already taken points off the likes of Manchester United, Tottenham Hotspur and Brighton & Hove Albion.

Some pundits have suggested the Whites could push for European football, but Mills believes the securing of safety at the earliest point possible takes precedence.

The 49-year-old, who represented Leeds between 1994 and 2004, told BOYLE Sports, who offer the latest football odds: “I think what Leeds did last season was to fall into a formation that worked for them.

“They're high energy, they're aggressive, they're physical, and they've caused teams a lot of problems.

“What would be success for Leeds this season? Safe by March, I would say. The second season in the Premier League, if you can consolidate again, then you start to go, right, okay, now we're here and hopefully here for a while and you build slowly.

“They did fantastically well last season to stay up. Now you're looking at them going, okay, they're solid. They don't get beat easily. It's a fantastic basis to work from.”

Leeds have enjoyed success with the use of a flexible 3-4-2-1 formation, with the system spearheaded by talismanic frontman Dominic Calvert-Lewin.

Mills said: “They've got a formation that's worked, they've recruited for that formation. They will be difficult to beat, and they will cause teams problems with their athleticism in the wide areas. Dominic Calvert-Lewin up front as well.

“They do have replacements and they might even look in January thinking, ‘okay, it looks like we're gonna be safe’. They might even recruit, you know, whether it be a back-up or someone to assist Dominic Calvert-Lewin in that area. I think they're in a strong position at the moment.”
