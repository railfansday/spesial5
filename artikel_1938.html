Crawley Town earn first home win in league since March after determined display against Fleetwood

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The win came in Kevin Blackwell’s first game as permanent manager after his appointment earlier this week.

A goal in either half from Kai Jennings, Pemi Aderoju and a resolute back-line performance earned Crawley a deserved win.

It takes the Reds to within three points from safety, with as many games in hand against 22nd-placed Rochdale.

Reds made a fast start to the first half and took the lead in the fourth minute through Jennings, following Kellan Gordon’s cross from a darting run down the right wing.

Fleetwood, Arsenal’s conquerors and League Cup quarter-finalists, failed to clear their lines on the edge of the six-yard box and Jennings pounced on their indecision to smash home into the roof of the net.

New Reds centre back Ben Heneghan blocked a well-hit shot from range in the Cod Army’s first effort on goal in the ninth minute.

Vito Mannone calmy claimed Jake Batty’s left-wing cross to dispel Fleetwood as the away side got a foothold in the game and started to up the pressure, shortly before Reds nervily dealt with an Andy Cannon cross.

On the 15-minute mark Aderoju found himself through one-on-one but shot straight into the welcoming arms of Fleetwood keeper Jay Lynch.

Mannone was called upon to punch clear a couple of searching Fleetwood crosses into the box and lifted the pressure of his side in an open context.

Dylan Mogan nearly put the Reds 2-0 up on the half hour, he tricked his way past the Fleetwood right-back but curling shot from the edge of the box clipped the post and went out for a goal kick.

Just before the break Fleetwood’s Aaron Loupal-Bi got into a good position on the left after a one-two but his tame effort along the ground was collected by Mannone.

Reds flew out the traps to double their lead within a minute of the restart, Morgan stormed through Fleetwood’s midfield and slotted Aderoju in on-goal and the big target man rifled a left-foot shot into the back of the net.

The visitors responded by almost pulling one back from a curling shot from the edge of the box and then hit the post moments afterwards.

Reds first chance of the second half fell to Aderoju who rose well but headed just wide from Gordon’s good right-side delivery.

Fleetwood gave themselves a lifeline thanks to substitute Will Davies’ firing a shot past Mannone from the middle of the penalty area to give the travelling fans behind the goal a reason to cheer.

Reds matched Fleetwood as the physicality grew as the half wore on and dug in to match their opponents.

Fleetwood substitute Lewis McCann stung the gloves of Mannone and produced a match-winning save from the big Italian who’s clean sheet was hard-earned.

Duke-McKenna had the chance to seal the points for the Reds at the end after chopping back onto his left foot down the right side, but his curled shot was parried away by Lynch.

Crawley are back at Broadfield in league action again this Tuesday (November 24) as they aim to climb out of the drop-zone in a tough test against high-flying Barnet.
