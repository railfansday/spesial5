Leeds make Kelvin Yeboah transfer decision ahead of January

Leeds United are not pushing to sign Kelvin Yeboah despite his strong MLS form and family links to the club, sources have told Football Insider.

Yeboah has attracted widespread transfer interest across Europe as he proves his quality in the United States.

The 26-year-old has scored 18 goals in 32 MLS appearances for Minnesota United this season after bagging nine in 34 games in 2025.

He is carrying on a fine goalscoring legacy left by his uncle, Tony, who scored prolifically for Leeds between 1995 and 1997 and was one of the best strikers of his generation.

Despite rumours to the contrary, Leeds are not tracking Yeboah's progress ahead of a possible move for his services, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider’s Transfer Insider podcast.

He is on the radar of some other Premier League clubs, but the Whites are looking to bolster other positions in the next window.

Yeboah is an Italian youth international and has already played in several European leagues, such as Serie A, the Bundesliga, Ligue 1 and the Belgian First Division.

He looks to have found his feet in the MLS over the last few seasons and is now ready to have a crack at the Premier League, but his uncle's links will not coax Leeds into a move.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I think [links between Leeds and Yeboah] are just speculation. It’s the obvious connection because of his famous uncle, Tony Yeboah, who’s a Leeds legend. I don’t think there’s much in it at the minute.

"He’s going into the final 13 months of his contract, expiring in December 2027, and he has done very well for Minnesota United."

Everton are lacking numbers in attack and Thierno Barry is the only senior recognised striker at David Moyes' disposal right now.

Sources have exclusively revealed to Football Insider that the Toffees are keen admirers of Yeboah and would like to sign him in the January window.

They do hold some reservations over his suitability for the Premier League, after previous European top-flight struggles, but a cut-price move could be in the offing as he nears the end of his Minnesota deal next December.
