Pour one out for the PC gamers who are now forced to watch everyone frolic in the streets of Grand Theft Auto 6 on consoles this week. The next best thing on PC is GTA 5, and its recent Steam concurrents suggest people are trying their best to fight the FOMO until Rockstar decides to bring the new one over.

You might assume GTA 5 player counts would tank now that it's old news, but the numbers have actually jumped up a bit in the last month. SteamDB shows a peak of around 87,000 concurrent players in the last day, an almost 10% increase from October. There's been a bit of a dip today—which could partially be attributed to GTA 6 finally becoming available in every region—but it's not as sharp of a drop as you'd think it'd be for a 13-year-old game.

In fact, GTA 5 has steadily kept a good amount of players over the last year, with major spikes happening around the occasional price discount. It usually hovers around 40,000 to 50,000 average concurrent players, and GTA 6's release hasn't broken the pattern. That's the beauty of PC gaming for you: Steam sales and mods keep games like this chugging along years after they'd be considered cutting edge.

GTA 5 holdouts might have a long road ahead of them though. GamesBeat reported last month that "four industry sources familiar with the studio's plans" said GTA 6 will make its way to PC in the first three months of 2027. In the past, Rockstar held PC releases back for at least seven months (GTA 5 PC took a year and six months), so an early 2027 release would be unorthodox if the report is true.

GTA Online, or some kind of multiplayer mode, will hopefully come at some point and it would make some sense to drop it at the same time as a PC release, or at least that's what I tell myself at night. I've only played a few hours of the game and I'm already imagining the kinds of mods that will inevitably show up in the first week. Someone is going to make it so Jason can instantly gulp down his coffee for the stamina buff, I know it.

For now, PC players will just have to continue biding their time in GTA 5 and pray that Rockstar at the very least announces a GTA 6 PC release date sooner rather than later.
