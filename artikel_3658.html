Denim trends are constantly coming and going — and I’ve learned the hard way to never get rid of a pair of jeans I love, even if they’re ‘out of style.’ This year, I’m thrilled to report that a classic favorite is making a comeback: bootcut jeans. The slimming, elongating style is trending for fall and winter, and they somehow look so much more stylish than they did in the early 2000s.

With their low-rise waist, slim silhouette and slight flare at the hem, bootcut jeans naturally lengthen my legs for a streamlined look that always comes off as polished and put together. They manage to be both casual and elevated, which is why they’re easy to dress up with a pair of heeled booties or Mary Jane flats. Ready to add some to your wardrobe? These writer-approved bootcut jeans from Levi’s, Wrangler, Lee and more are all on Amazon, and start as low as $24.
