Patrick Mahomes Reveals What He Hopes 4th Baby's Sex Will Be Amid Pregnancy

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Patrick Mahomes is ready to add to his family’s roster amid wife Brittany Mahomes’ pregnancy with baby No. 4.

The Kansas City Chiefs quarterback, 31, revealed on Wednesday, November 11, that he would ideally like to have a second baby boy, since he and son Bronze, 3, are currently outnumbered by the girls in their household.

“Obviously, it’s a blessing to have another baby on the way. I’m excited for it,” Patrick said during a team press conference. “I’m hoping that me and Bronze can even up the score.”

While the NFL player — who shares son Bronze as well as daughters Sterling, 5, and Golden, 21 months, with his wife, 31 — said he’d like to have two and two, he’s just focused on Brittany’s safety and the health of the pregnancy.

“I’m just praying for a healthy baby at the end of the day,” Patrick shared.

After a reporter pointed out that every time Patrick and Brittany have made a pregnancy announcement his team has ended their season with a Super Bowl, the athlete admitted he’d love to see that trend continue.

“I’m hoping. I don’t know if Brittany is,” Patrick said with a laugh. “But I’m hoping if we can keep that going and get another Super Bowl win with a baby on the way that’d be awesome for sure.”

Patrick and Brittany, who are high school sweethearts, announced on Tuesday, November 10, that they are expanding their brood once again.

“Another blessing from God🥹🙏🏼 & then there were 6🤍,” the pair captioned a joint Instagram post, sharing a photo of their three children lovingly touching Brittany’s stomach.

The couple welcomed their first baby, Sterling, in February 2021, after getting engaged the prior September.

After tying the knot in March 2022, Patrick and Brittany went on to welcome son Patrick “Bronze” III that November.

Their daughter Golden was born in January 2025, one month before Patrick played in his fifth Super Bowl. (The Chiefs lost to the Philadelphia Eagles during the February 2025 game, but Patrick has three championship wins to his name.)

Ahead of their fourth pregnancy announcement, both Brittany and Patrick claimed they were done having children — and happy to be a family of five.

“We’re done, we’re done for a while,” Brittany said during a June 2025 appearance on the “Whoops” podcast. “I don’t know. I mean, I feel like you can never say you’re done. I think three is all I need.”

Patrick, for his part, told reporters in July 2024 — ahead of daughter Golden’s birth — that he was “done” as well.

In January 2025, Patrick changed his tune, hinting that baby No. 4 wasn’t totally out of the question.

“My goal was always three, so we’ve had three,” he said during a Chiefs press conference. “We’ll stick there for a while and see if we need to come back and get another one later on.”

Patrick noted that “down the line” they could add another little one to their family.
