The Best Gifts for Hunters: Get Them What They Actually Want

Hunters can present a challenge when it comes to gift buying. They already have one of these or two of those. They don’t particularly care for Model A or Brand B. There’s always the $100 Gift Card, but you really don’t want to be that impersonal, now do you?

Fortunately, there’s an almost endless array of “I’d love one of those” gift ideas whether the hunter on your list chases waterfowl, big game, upland birds, or small game. This year, the following gift ideas will help you put the right hunting gear beneath the tree.

Missing a target means lost or broken arrows. Not only does that get expensive, it’s downright discouraging. The BlackOut LongShot 36 is a big target that allows bowhunters to confidently practice shooting their bows at extended ranges without worrying about missing the target. Best of all, it’s affordable. In my testing, I’ve found arrow removal is easy, even though I’m shooting small-diameter arrows over 290 fps.

Even if you’re shopping for a bowhunter who already owns a backyard target, they’ll still want and need this one. It’s a can’t-miss gift for anyone who loves flinging arrows.

Thermal monoculars are handy devices to have around the house and in the field. They can help you locate coyotes in the pitch dark, blood trail a deer (where legal) and make home repairs, such as finding heat leaking out of a window. The key to choosing a thermal as a gift is understanding that the more you spend, the better the image gets. But not all use cases require top-end performance. If someone is going to use their thermal to scan fields, recover game, and use around the property, a lower-resolution sensor works well. If they need to view and identify animals at longer ranges (beyond 100 yards), they’ll need a top-of-the-line unit.

The ATN BlazeSeeker 6 is a compact thermal that slips easily into a pocket and can record photos and videos. It costs only $429 to $489 and is ideal for everyday use around the property and in the field. If you’re buying for someone who needs more capability, I recommend the BlazeTrek 6. The BlazeTrek 6 has a better image and costs $795 to $1,499. Both monoculars are easy to use, and the ATN app has a handy AI assistant to guide you through any snags the recipient might encounter. Any hunter will be thrilled to receive an ATN monocular, and it’s another fantastic gift to consider.

Good gifts are practical, but the best gifts help people have fun. One of the keys to having fun in the outdoors is being comfortable no matter the conditions. The Uncompahgre Foundry Jacket is a highly versatile, packable layer that’s ideal for staying outside when the weather turns cold. From fishing to late-season hunting, this is an outer shell that will get a ton of use. We’ve tested and recommended the Foundry Jacket. You can read our full review to learn more.

Soft cases are the practical way to carry a shotgun to the range or to your hunting spot. They’re something that every bird hunter needs and will use regularly. They’re also relatively affordable, which makes them a great gift. The Evolution Outdoor Rawhide Classic Camo Shotgun Case is a stylish and practical case that’s made from waxed canvas. It will carry shotguns up to 50 inches in overall length, so it will accommodate most semi-automatics with 28-inch or shorter barrels and double guns with up to 30-inch barrels.

It’s tough buying the right knife for an outdoorsman, but you can’t go wrong with the iconic Victorinox Huntsman made by the folks who produced the original Swiss Army Knife™. The Huntsman has 15 functions, including blades, corkscrew, reamer, punch and sewing awl, scissors, wood saw, wire stripper, screwdrivers, and more. It’s available in 7 different color patterns. You can be sure that the outdoorsman on your list will find unlimited uses for this versatile Swiss-made tool through the many seasons that it will serve them.

Built to handle rough roads, bad weather, and air travel, this is a gift that tells someone you understand their passion and want to protect the tools that make it possible. It’s designed to hold two scoped rifles, with thick protective foam inside and six sturdy latches that keep everything sealed tight. Whether you’re shopping for a hunter that drives to the deer camp or flies out west for elk season, this case gives them peace of mind that their firearms will arrive zeroed and ready to hunt.— Scott Einsmann

You can get a turkey hunter a new call, a knife, or this piece of turkey hunting culture. The Tenth Legion is a book that captures the heart of what it is to be a diehard turkey hunter and is guaranteed to be enjoyed by anyone that chases gobbles throughout the spring. It was written by the legendary Colonel Tom Kelly, who recently passed on. I keep a copy of the Tenth Legion in my vest and read through it when the birds aren’t feeling talkative, and I highly recommend it as a gift for the turkey obsessed. — Scott Einsmann

Securely packaging your hard-won wild game, be it fresh venison, wild turkey, or mallard duck breasts has never been as easy as it is now, thanks to Cabela’s Deluxe 12 (D12) vacuum sealer. Weighing just four pounds, the D12 features a heavy-duty commercial vacuum pump designed for years of service. User-friendly, easy-to-clean, and programmable, the D12 takes up little counter space, while offering multiple modes of operation including manual seal, pulse, Auto/Vac, Standard, and Extended, as well as ‘Canister’ option great for preserving tough-to-seal foodstuffs.

This chest pack is designed to hold a hunter’s binoculars, and much much more. For backcountry hunters, a chest pack is a critical piece of gear and there are few out there that offer more features than the Kuiu Pro. It includes: magnetic closure for maximum quietness, adjustable main pouch for proper bino fit, rail-lock system for bottom accessory, rail-lock mount for ammo holder, plus a variety of extra pockets. It comes in five different camo/solid color options (I don’t think you can go wrong with solid Ash). This is an ideal gift for a big game hunter or even a turkey hunter, who needs quick access to optics and other critical gear.

A cellular trail camera is a perfect gift for any deer or turkey hunter. If the hunter already has a cell cam, they’ll be happy to have another (you can never have too many cameras out there). And if they don’t have camera yet, then your gift will get them in the game. And there’s not a trail camera out there that’s more effective and easy to use than the Tactacam 4.0. The Outdoor Life crew thoroughly tests trail cams every year, and the Tactacam 4.0 won the editor’s choice award, hands down, in 2026. Features include: Up to 4K photo definition, up to 96 feet of flash range, Low-glow or no-glow flash settings, 16 gb of internal storage, powered by 12 AA batteries, live video, on-demand photos, GPS enabled.

Sure, the description says ‘Waterfowl Backpack,’ and all duck hunters should have one of these, but don’t let the name fool you. This well-built large capacity pack has 101 uses in the field, e.g. camera bag, lunch box, blind bag, and dry clothes storage to name just a handful. With over 1,550 cubic inches of interior space, the Northern Flight backpack is crafted of rugged 600D polyester with a water-resistant polyurethane coating to help keep what’s inside nice and dry. There’s plenty of pockets, glove-friendly zippers, a huge main compartment, hydration pouch, and MOLLE attachment points for additional capacity. In other words this is an excellent hunting pack for a variety of scenarios.

How many times have you wished for some warmth while waiting out that trophy whitetail from inside the chilly confines of that late January ground blind? Thanks to the innovative minds at TideWe and their heated hunting chair, turning up the thermostat just became possible. At first glance, the chair seems ordinary enough – adjustable height; swiveling through 360 degrees; armrests; and a detachable gear pouch. But this chair adds a little spice in the form of battery-powered heat. Three heat levels (100 through 130 degrees), long 10-hour  battery life at the lowest setting, and a fast three-hour charge time make this ‘hot seat’ a Must-Have this winter.

Hunters constantly need to keep knives and broadheads sharp. This folding field sharpener is a versatile and handy way to maintain everything from pocketknives to axes. It’s only $13, and I’ve been testing it while sharpening broadheads and honing knives. It works great, and I recommend it.

There’s nothing worse than slipping your feet into cold wet boots. Fortunately, the folks at PEET Dryer have the answer in their original boot dryer. The PEET dryer uses convection to push warm air upwards through specially designed chambers and ports to dry shoes and boots of all shapes, sizes, and material types. Safe enough to operate 24/7, this makes the perfect gift for anyone who goes outside, but especially hunters who are prone to get their boots wet.

Hunters often like to forage while they’re in the field. But wild mushrooms are nothing to be guessed at when it comes to their edibility. Some make wonderful meals, while others can make a person terribly sick, or worse. This makes a good mushroom identification book an absolute necessity for the forestland forager. And few are as comprehensive as David Arora’s All That The Rain Promises, and More. An educator in the field of mycology, Arora’s easy to understand descriptive prose, along with his excellent photographs, takes much of the mystery out of separating the ‘good’ from the ‘not so good’ when it comes to fungi.

Virtually indestructible. That’s the best way to describe YETI’s latest introduction, the Loadout Gobox 1 Gear Case. Built out of what the company refers to as ‘Defender II Material,’ the Gobox 1 is the perfect hard case if you’re looking to safely store items such as a wallet, passport, keys, portable power, or anything else. The Gobox 1 seals out Mother Nature, while providing roughly 56 cubic inches of interior space, yet only weighing 1.7 pounds. The Gobox 1 is ideal for kayakers, rafting enthusiasts, off-roaders, mountain bikers, and of course, hunters. And it’s available in five different colors.

There are about 101 different ways to repel mosquitoes but few are as effective and easy to use as a ThermaCELL. The lightweight MR300 uses a cylindrical fuel canister to gently warm a small metal plate, which in turn activates the material infused into the unit’s repellent mat. This repellent diffuses into a 15 square-foot zone of protection around the unit, keeping mosquitoes at bay within 15 minutes and (under optimal conditions) for as long as four hours. No batteries. No DEET sprays. And it’s safe to use around pets.

Firearm maintenance should be a concern of all hunters. Fortunately, the Allen Company’s Breakthrough Clean Technologies Universal Cleaning Kit helps make the job much easier. Included in the tough ammo can container are a variety of nylon brushes, brass patch jags, two multi-section cleaning rods, patches, swabs, a silicone gun cloth, solvent, and a bottle of ‘Battle Born’ high purity gun oil. It’s everything you need to clean a gun in one place.

Even in these modern times of precision air rifles, there’s still something to be said about Old School stuff — like Crosman’s 760 Pumpmaster air rifle. Now here’s a tool developed specifically to introduce young or new shooters to the shooting sports. The 760 handles both .177 caliber pellets and BBs, sending them downrange at 615/700 and 645 feet-per-second, respectively. A crossbolt safety, synthetic stock/fore-end, grooved receiver to accommodate a 4x scope, and traditional front/rear blade sights make this the perfect gift for all ages.
