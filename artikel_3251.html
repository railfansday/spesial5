AirPods Pro 3 were worth the upgrade for one standout feature

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

AirPods Pro 3 (now only $149, down from $249) offer many advantages over AirPods Pro 2, but after a year of use, the improvements to Active Noise Cancellation alone have proven worth the upgrade.

I’ve been using AirPods Pro 3 for over a year, and they had a lot to live up to because I already loved AirPods Pro 2 so much. Initially I found the new ear tips less comfortable, but thankfully that issue went away after a little extra time.

Some highlights of AirPods Pro 3 include the longer battery life, with 8 hours per charge compared to 6 on the previous model. The charging case’s new Ultra Wideband chip also makes it easier to find when it’s lost. Some users will appreciate the heart rate sensor, but I wear an Apple Watch all the time so it doesn’t benefit me much.

These new features are nice to have, but Active Noise Cancellation is where AirPods Pro 3 become a major upgrade. Apple says it’s “the world’s best in-ear Active Noise Cancellation.” Here’s how the new ANC compares to previous models:

I spend nearly my entire workday with AirPods Pro 3 in my ears. Whether I’m working from home with young kids around or at a busy coffee shop, I rely on ANC to block out background noise and help me focus.

One reason I’ve been especially impressed with the ANC upgrades is that I didn’t have any real complaints about ANC on AirPods Pro 2. But Apple managed to deliver improvements in AirPods Pro 3 I had no idea I needed, and when I’ve tried going back to AirPods Pro 2 I couldn’t believe how much noise was let in.

If Active Noise Cancellation is important to you, I highly recommend AirPods Pro 3.
