I've been wearing my Oura Ring pretty much 24/7 for months now and checking my sleep score has become an integral part of my morning routine. A few metrics have been my go to (sleep latency, deep sleep) but in recent weeks another feature has grabbed my interest; the Sleep Debt tracker.

This tracker measures your sleep duration over several nights and compares it to your recommended average. If your hours are down, this gets tracked as sleep debt.

My sleep debt has consistently been below one hour and has often been at a healthy zero (I say, in smug comparison to my coworker who has topped eight hours debt.) So when my sleep debt ticked upwards of an hour with no clear cause, I knew something was happening to my sleep quality.

My sleep debt has coincided with a series of 3 a.m. wake ups (something else the Oura Ring tracks), and a general feeling of low energy. I've been a bit bleh, you might say. I'd assumed the season change was to blame but now I recognize the problem is lying in my sleep. And that's something I can fix.

Having identified my increasing sleep debt and subsequent lethargy are related to a series of late night wake ups, I know I need to change my wind-down routine. Thanks to the Oura Ring's hidden body clock feature, which uses my natural circadian rhythm to send me bedtime reminders, I now know when to start my night-time routine. (That's more warm evening showers, dimming the lights, and swapping my sudden interest in Sudoko for puzzles I'm actually good at. )

And it's paying off. Last night I enjoyed an impressive 1 hour and 32 minutes off deep sleep, alongside 93% sleep efficiency. Hopefully it shouldn't take long repay my sleep debt entirely.
