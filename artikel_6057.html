People often ask me about my favorite hotels or my most memorable travel experiences. But because I travel so frequently (and have been doing so for decades), it’s difficult to pick those all-time favorites. I’ll settle for recapping some of my top travel experiences and hotel stays this year.

More so than ever, I’m focusing on experiencing nature — for me, this is really the way to see the world and create lasting memories. Yes, I can fly in all the first-class cabins, but seeing the world's natural wonders and wildlife has been far more life-changing for me.

This year, I also focused on family travel with my kids and parents. My children will be school-age before I know it, so I want to take advantage of not being tied to school holidays to travel and enjoy shoulder-season and offseason escapes when fewer people are traveling.

These were my top hotel stays and travel experiences of 2026.

I’m actually writing this as I return from an epic Norwegian adventure. I was just in Norway with my two sons and my parents, exploring the country in the blustery weather. I started off with a few days in Tromsø, taking my kids to the aquarium (they loved the seals!) and the library. Then, with my family by my side, I saw the northern lights. I’ve seen them before, but admiring them with my children was extra special.

Then we headed out on boat excursions to realize my dream of swimming with orcas, opting to book with Tromsø Pure because they focus on safety and on finding quieter spots away from other boats for a better experience.

At first, we spotted pods of humpbacks, and I was able to swim with them, which was one of the most out-of-body, exhilarating experiences I’ve ever had. There was even a brief moment when I thought I was about to be kicked and/or swallowed by a whale, which was terrifyingly thrilling (don’t worry — I’m still here).

Even though a few of the days had rough seas and low visibility, I was able to swim with orcas later on during the trip, and I’ll never forget it.

After taking nearly a dozen safaris over the years, I was ready for something different. I’ve done the bigger camps and seen the Big 5, so I wanted to try a more niche wildlife experience.

I stayed at Tswalu’s Loapi Lodge in a two-bedroom villa, complete with a plunge pool and my own private chef. I much prefer a lodge to a tented camp, as hearing lions outside your tent at night and having no running water can be a lot to handle, and I’m done roughing it.

Unlike many safari destinations, Tswalu is unique because there aren’t any elephants, but you can see meerkat colonies and observe them relatively up close. I also spotted many black and white rhinos and cheetahs.

I highly recommend Tswalu to experienced safari-goers, but if it’s your first time, I’d suggest going to Cheetah Plains in Sabi Sands or Royal Malewane in Kruger National Park. You’ll get to see the Big 5 and have the perfect introduction to a safari (and you might get addicted to safaris, like I am!).

Many people skip the Caribbean in summer, but honestly, European summer heat can feel even more brutal, with beaches packed umbrella-to-umbrella in places like Spain, Italy and Portugal. This year, I canceled my summer trip to Portugal after a massive heat wave alert and went to Grand Cayman with my kids instead.

Yes, it was warm, but it didn’t feel any hotter than Europe would have (or even a humid Midwest or East Coast summer), and the beach was uncrowded and uncomplicated — no stairs or rugged terrain, like I’ve had to traverse in Portugal. Plus, no jet lag!

My stay at Palm Heights was peaceful, and even though it's a boutique hotel, it felt very family-friendly. I loved that my suite overlooked the white sands of Seven Mile Beach, and the spa was absolutely gorgeous. I highly recommend it for families, couples, or any type of traveler.

In my opinion, the Ritz Paris represents true luxury. I used Chase Points Boost to book it, getting more than 2 cents per point.

The hotel is unapologetically over-the-top, with gorgeous rooms and lavish decor, but what I really love are the service and amenities. The hotel has a program that lets you check in early in the morning after landing from a transatlantic flight, and consumer-friendly options like this are what make it feel truly luxurious. After all, true luxury goes beyond a beautiful room or view — hotels should solve problems, reduce friction, and make traveling easier.
