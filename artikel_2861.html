Michael Pittman, Patrick Queen among Steelers questionable for Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Steelers have several players listed as questionable for Sunday’s game against the Eagles.

At the top of the list are receiver Michael Pittman and linebacker Patrick Queen.

Pittman has been sidelined by a foot injury since Week 3 and is returning from injured reserve. He was a full participant on Thursday and Friday, ostensibly putting him in a position to be activated and play.

Queen (hand) is also trending toward playing. He was limited on Wednesday and Thursday before being upgraded to a full participant on Friday.

Receiver Roman Wilson (hand), safety DeShon Elliott (knee), linebacker Nick Herbig (hip/ribs), and guard Gennings Dunker (knee) are also questionable.

Wilson and Dunker are returning from IR and need to be activated to the 53-man roster to play on Sunday.

Everyone else on the Steelers roster is set to play.
