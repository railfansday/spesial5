Aaron Rodgers: Sanctity of the locker room doesn't protect the coach from accountability

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

With Al Michaels calling today's game in Pittsburgh, ex-Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers texted Michaels to give his side of the nasty breakup he just had with the team and head coach Mike McCarthy.

During the fourth quarter of the Broncos-Steelers game, Michaels read a text from Rodgers, in which Rodgers claimed he in no way violated the sanctity of the Steelers' locker room when he went on Pat McAfee's show to detail his problems with McCarthy.

"A lot of talk about the sanctity of the locker room," Rodgers said in the text Michaels read during the game. "The code or sanctity exists to protect the integrity of the locker room, not to provide immunity from accountability for its leadership. They took this public. Never heard from the head coach. Then they leaked at 11 p.m. They also barred me from the facility. That's why I went to McAfee."

Rodgers told McAfee that McCarthy, among other issues, wasn't watching enough film, was sleeping in team meetings, and addressed the team as the "Packers" instead of the Steelers during halftime on Sunday.

McCarthy's Steelers are dominating the Broncos today, which stands as a rebuke to Rodgers' claim that McCarthy isn't fit to be the team's coach. But Rodgers was able to get his side of the story into the game, via his text to Michaels.
