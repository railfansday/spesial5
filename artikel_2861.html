Where to watch Grand Slam of Curling National: Schedule, times, channels, live streams, scores for 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Where to watch Grand Slam of Curling National: Schedule, times, channels, live streams, scores for 2026 originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Grand Slam of Curling is back this week with the GSOC National in Sydney, Nova Scotia.

The third of five events in this year's series, the National features some intriguing lineup changes, including the first action this season for some high-profile Americans.

Five-time Olympian John Shuster won a qualifier to get into the field, and Milan Cortina 2026 mixed doubles silver medalist Korey Dropkin will step in as a sub for skip Matt Dunstone's side, which went unbeaten in winning the GSOC Masters two weeks ago.

While Dunstone and Isabel Wrana will look to make it two GSOC wins in a row, Bruce Mouat and Rachel Homan are two-time defending champions at the National.

Who will prevail this week in Nova Scotia? Here's all you need to know to watch all the action this week.

Live streams:Fubo (Canada), Sportsnet+, Rock Channel, ESPN app

Fans can stream the entire Grand Slam of Curling National via Rock Channel, which will have exclusive live coverage of the morning draws each day, along with games not selected for featured spots on the main broadcast the rest of the day.

For viewers in Canada, one featured game during the afternoon and evening draws will air on Sportsnet channels and Sportsnet+ from Tuesday through Friday, along with Saturday's quarterfinals and Sunday's finals. The men's and women's semifinals will air on the CBC and Sportsnet+ and can also be streamed via Fubo.

Fans in the U.S. can stream the Grand Slam of Curling Masters via Rock Channel and ESPN+, which is available via the ESPN app. Each afternoon and evening draw will see a featured game spotlighted on the ESPN+ broadcast, with other games available on Rock Channel. All morning draws will stream exclusively on Rock Channel.

All times listed below are Eastern. The featured game for the broadcast in afternoon and evening draws is in bold.
