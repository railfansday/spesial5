Player Grades: Lakers vs. Pistons

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

One trait JJ Redick teams will have is that they play hard.

Redick had a revelation of sorts early in his coaching career that just trying hard was enough to mask over flaws on most nights in the regular season. From that point on, the Lakers have, for the most part, tried hard.

With previous rosters, playing hard looked different because of a lack of energy or athleticism across the board. On a younger, more athletic, more tenacious team like this, playing hard looks like a team without their superstar jumping all over the Pistons on the road in the first quarter.

It's quite the encouraging trait to have.

So, let's dive into the win. As always, grades are based on expectations for each player. A "B" grade represents the average performance for that player.

28 minutes, 4 points, 2 rebounds, 4 steals, 3 fouls, 2-5 FG, +0

This is Thybulle's third consecutive subpar offensive game. He's hit just one of his last nine threes in the span. Unironically, no longer shooting no-dip threes might have messed with his timing. I don't think his starting spot is in jeopardy yet, but there are plenty of options behind him.

29 minutes, 5 points, 1 rebound, 2 assists, 2 fouls, 1-3 FG, 0-1 3PT, 3-4 FT, +3

An interesting use of LaRavia in this game as he was more of a ballhandler with the Lakers down Luka Dončić. I had hoped that experiment stayed in the 2025-26 season, but it wasn't disastrous on Sunday.

28 minutes, 12 points, 16 rebounds, 4 assists, 1 steal, 3 blocks, 3 fouls, 4 turnovers, 5-10 FG, 2-3 FT, +5

Kessler's ability to stuff the stat sheet is going to make him a sneaky 5×5 candidate. Seven of his 16 rebounds were on the offensive glass and each one felt like it led directly to another basket. A huge showing from him.

31 minutes, 12 points, 1 rebound, 4 assists, 1 steal, 1 block, 2 turnovers, 4-12 FG, 2-5 3PT, 2-4 FT +1

Almost all of Grimes' production came in the first quarter, but it was all very useful. He hit four of his first five shots and was a necessary ballhandler to alleviate some of the pressure off Austin Reaves.

Even if he faded as the game went, this was a far more encouraging performance from Grimes than most of his recent outings.

36 minutes, 43 points, 5 rebounds, 4 assists, 3 fouls, 1 turnover, 14-27 FG, 4-6 3PT, 11-11 FT, +21

This is everything you needed from your nearly max player with your superstar out. He took on every bit of Luka's role, dealt with the intense ball pressure all night long and still put up 43 points on great efficiency.

17 minutes, 7 points, 3 rebounds, 2 fouls, 2 turnovers, 3-5 FG, 1-3 3PT, -7

Another relatively quiet night from Mamu as well. He had a couple moments early but also faded as the game went on.

13 minutes, 2 points, 10 rebounds, 3 assists, 1 block, 2 fouls, 2 turnovers, 1-1 FG, 0-1 FT, +2

Vando was immediately impactful in the first quarter, grabbing nearly as many rebounds in that quarter (5) as he had in his last three games (6). His only basket came on a great finish on an and-one as well.

Credit to him for remaining ready even after falling out of the rotation with Adou Thiero's return.

21 minutes, 17 points, 2 rebounds, 1 block, 2 fouls, 6-10 FG, 4-8 3PT, 1-2 FT, +7

For a number of reasons, this was a really strong performance from Ziaire, none more so than his clutch baskets in the fourth quarter. He stepped up and knocked down some huge shots and rightfully closed this game.

24 minutes, 17 points, 2 rebounds, 2 assists, 1 foul, 2 turnovers, 5-10 FG, 0-2 3PT, 7-8 FT, +1

You know what? Fair play to Sexton. It still wasn't a perfect game, especially with him fouling on a 3-pointer at the end. But the Lakers needed him and he had some important buckets throughout the game.

Another brief cameo from Thiero as he works his way back from injury. He still had a highlight with a big dunk after corralling an offensive rebound. It will be interesting to see how his knee responds and if he'll be available against the Hawks.

Great work from Redick to have the team ready to go from the start on Sunday. They jumped out to an early lead and forced the Pistons to play from behind all game. He has this team in a great spot mentally and schematically right now.

Sunday's DNPs: Bronny James, Cameron Carr, Jaden Hardy, Kevon Looney, Lawson Lovering

Sunday inactives: Luka Dončić, Arthur Kaluma, Chris Mañon
