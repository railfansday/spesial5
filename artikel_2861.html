Indian batsmen to score Test 100s in all SENA countries: Full list as Rahul and Pant join Sachin Tendulkar & Virat Kohli

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Only six Indian batters have Test hundreds in all four SENA countries so far.

The newest member of that group is Rishabh Pant after he reached the three-figure mark in Wellington.

Several great Indian names, including Sunil Gavaskar and Virender Sehwag, never completed the full set.

Only six Indian cricketers in history have scored a Test century in South Africa, England, New Zealand and Australia, and that remains one of the hardest clubs to join in the longer format.

The latest name on the list is Tishabh, who got there with a hundred against New Zealand at the Basin Reserve in Wellington on November 21, 2026.

KL Rahul became the first Indian opening batter to complete the set on Day 3 of the match. He was also the second Asian opener, after Saeed Anwar, to have hit Test hundreds in all four countries, which makes his place on the list a rare one even by the standards of the group.

Azharuddin was the trailblazer, and he completed the full set at Cape Town in January 1997. Tendulkar then pushed the numbers far beyond anyone else, with 17 hundreds in these four countries and 5,387 runs across 114 innings, which are the highest totals for any Indian or Asian batter.

Kohli's strength has been Australia, where his seven Test hundreds are the most by an Indian. Dravid's hallmark was England, where he scored six centuries at an average of 68.80.

Rohit Sharma has a Test century in England but none in South Africa, Australia or New Zealand. Rishabh Pant has Test hundreds in Australia (1), England (4) and South Africa (1), and needs only a New Zealand century to join the club.

Sunil Gavaskar never had the chance to play a Test in South Africa because of the sporting boycott of the apartheid era, although Gavaskar still scored eight centuries across Australia (5), England (2) and New Zealand (1).

Virender Sehwag hit Test hundreds in Australia (2), England (1) and South Africa (1), but missed out on a century in New Zealand.

Rishabh Pant is the closest active contender. He has Test hundreds in Australia (1), England (4) and South Africa (1), so only a New Zealand century stands between him and the list.

Beyond the five Indians, only three other Asian batters have achieved the feat, namely Younis Khan, Kumar Sangakkara and Saeed Anwar, with the former Pakistan ace being the only other Asian opener on that list apart from Rahul.
