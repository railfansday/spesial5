Raiders injury news: Trey Zuhn ready to play Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

It's Week 10 0f the NFL season and most rosters are battered … and then there are the Las Vegas Raiders.

The Raiders are in line to have most of their 53 player son their roster healthy and available to play Sunday when they open the Fernando Mendoza era by hosting the Seattle Seahawks at Allegiant Stadium. Kickoff is set for 1:05 p.m. PT.

Las Vegas head coach Klint Kubiak said rookie right guard Trey Zuhn has cleared the concussion protocol and he will start Sunday. Zuhn had to leave the Week 9 loss at San Francisco. He practiced on a limited basis Wednesday and Thursday. However, he was a full go Friday.

The third-round pick will be making his third straight start.

Kubiak said defensive tackle Thomas Booker and wide receiver Dont'e Thornton are both questionable. There are both reserve players.

The Raiders have had a major injury advantage in virtually every game this season and that will continue Sunday. The Seahawks will be without starting quarterback Sam Darnold as backup Drew Lock will play. Standout left guard Quenton Nelson and left tackle Charles Cross are out as well as standout defensive tackle Leonard Williams.

Kubiak also sad the Raiders are considering adding a third quarterback to the roster behind Mendoza and Aidan O'Connell..
