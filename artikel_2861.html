Chiefs-Jets Week 9 Thursday injury report: Nikko Remigio practices in full

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Each practice day this season, the Kansas City Chiefs release an official injury report ahead of the next game. In Week 9, the Chiefs play the New York Jets on Sunday afternoon.

Here is the Chiefs' first official injury report of the week, courtesy of the team's official website:

Rookie defensive tackle Peter Woods (neck) did not participate in practice for the second consecutive day after suffering the injury early in the Week 8 loss; he walked gingerly to the sideline without stopping the game after taking on an open-field block.

Rookie cornerback Mansoor Delane (shoulder) was a limited participant in practice for the second consecutive day. He has been dealing on and off with the same shoulder injury since training camp.

Defensive tackle Omarr Norman-Lott (knee) was a limited participant for the fifth consecutive practice since being designated to return from the Reserve/Injured list; next week is the final week before he must be activated on the 53-man roster.

Returner Nikko Remigio (hip) was a full participant in practice Thursday after being limited Wednesday. On Wednesday afternoon, the Chiefs signed rookie returner Jacob De Jesus to the practice squad, seemingly insurance in case Remigio did not make progress.

Wide receiver Rashee Rice (hamstring/knee) was a full participant in practice on Thursday after being marked as limited on Wednesday. Speaking with local media this week, Rice said he is "100%" ahead of the limited participation in practice on Wednesday.

Tight end Travis Kelce (ankle) was a full participant in practice on Thursday after being marked as limited on Wednesday. He came up with a limp in his ankle during Week 6, but hasn't missed any game time since.

The following Chiefs players were full participants in practice on Wednesday and Thursday:

No. 1 wide receiver Garrett Wilson entered concussion protocol on Monday after a helmet-to-helmet hit in Week 8 and did not participate in practice on Wednesday or Thursday. He has racked up 527 yards and three touchdowns over 43 receptions this season.

Starting defensive tackle Harrison Phillips (ankle) did not participate in Wednesday or Thursday practice. He has 24 tackles, three quarterback hits, and a fumble recovered while playing 57% of the defensive snaps for New York this season. The Jets placed defensive tackle T'Vondre Sweat (knee) on the Reserve/Injured list last week.

Rookie phenom edge rusher David Bailey (groin) was upgraded to a full participant in Thursday's practice after being limited Wednesday and missing Week 8. He has 5.5 sacks and seven tackles for loss while playing 81% of the Jets' defensive snaps.

Tight end Mason Taylor (back) was a full participant in Thursday's practice after being limited on Wednesday; he missed time earlier in the season due to a thumb injury.

Starting running back Breece Hall (knee) has been on the IR since Week 3 but could return this week, according to reports; he was a full participant in practice Thursday after limited action on Wednesday. He has 265 total yards and a touchdown this season. His backup, Braelon Allen, has 565 rushing yards and three touchdowns in relief.

Rookie safety VJ Payne of Kansas State saw his practice window open for the first time this season after suffering an injury in the preseason.
