Thursday Night Football: Commanders vs. Giants discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Week 10 of the 2026 NFL season will get started with an NFC East matchup between the Washington Commanders (2-5-1) and the New York Giants (5-2-1).

The Giants lead the all-time series between these division opponents, 107-74-6, but the Commanders have won four of their five most-recent meetings. They last faced off earlier this season, just a month ago, with the game ending in a tie after an overtime effort, 27 points each.

We covered the Philadelphia Eagles' interest in this game from our Week 10 rooting guide:

WASHINGTON COMMANDERS at NEW YORK GIANTS: Big Thursday night matchup between two NFC East teams. The Commanders are the bigger threat to move ahead of the Eagles in the draft order. Also, the Giants losing makes it harder for them to run away with the division lead, which the Eagles should prefer since they play New York in Week 18. The Eagles shouldn't want the Giants to be resting their starters in the regular season finale since a Philly win in that spot could mess up Philly's draft positioning. Root for the Commanders.
