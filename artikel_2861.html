Giannis Antetokounmpo Ruled Out for Heat’s Showdown Against Thunder

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Miami Heat will be missing a crucial piece of their lineup as they prepare to face the Oklahoma City Thunder on Sunday. Star forward Giannis Antetokounmpo has been ruled out due to a groin injury, marking his first missed game since joining the Heat in the offseason.

Antetokounmpo, a former two-time MVP, has been instrumental in Miami's early success this season, averaging an impressive 28.2 points, 12.6 rebounds, 5.5 assists, and 1.3 blocks while shooting 60.2% from the field across 15 games. His absence will undoubtedly be felt as the Heat look to maintain their momentum.

The Thunder, too, will be without star power, as reigning two-time MVP Shai Gilgeous-Alexander is sidelined with a lower back contusion and neck strain. Both players were initially listed as questionable, but the decision to keep them out comes as both teams navigate through the challenges of the season.

Miami is speculated to look to Bobby Portis Jr. to step into the starting lineup in place of Antetokounmpo. Portis brings energy and experience, but replacing the production and leadership of Giannis will be a tall order for the Heat.

As the Heat entered Sunday with a record of 9-6, sitting in sixth place in the Eastern Conference, the team is keen to capitalize on their home court advantage. The Thunder, meanwhile, are dealing with a slew of injuries to key players, including Alex Caruso, Jaylin Williams, and Chet Holmgren, which has tested their depth.

While both Antetokounmpo and Gilgeous-Alexander are playing at an All-NBA level this season, the Heat will need to rally together in Giannis's absence. With the stakes high, Miami aims to secure a victory and keep pace in the competitive East.

Fans will be eagerly watching to see how the Heat adapt and who steps up in this pivotal matchup.
