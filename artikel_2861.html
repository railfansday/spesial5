The last time the Baltimore Ravens (4-5) and the Los Angeles Chargers (0-8) met for a Monday Night Football matchup, it was 2024 and the game was billed as the Harbaugh Bowl, with coaches and brothers John and Jim Harbaugh helming their respective teams. (The Ravens won.) This Monday, Chargers coach Jim Harbaugh will not face his own brother; instead, he'll face another familiar rival, the Chargers' former defensive coordinator Jesse Minter, now the Ravens' head coach.

While some fans aren't thrilled that this week's MNF game will feature one winless team and another team that's missing its star quarterback, the game will be an interesting one now that the Ravens have acquired Jacoby Brissett, who will make his first start for the team as QB1 while Lamar Jackson is out.
