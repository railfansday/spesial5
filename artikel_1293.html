Serie A official line-ups: Juventus vs. Venezia

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Juventus look to Randal Kolo Muani for a home Serie A game against Venezia, but Nick Woltemade goes on the bench, and Edon Zhegrova starts instead.

It kicks off at 12:30 CET [11:30 GMT] at the Allianz Stadium in Turin.

Follow the build-up and live action on the Football Italia LIVE BLOG.

Andrea Cambiaso and Zeki Celik have recovered from injuries, and the latter starts in a three-man backline alongside Gleison Bremer and Lloyd Kelly.

Francisco Conceiçao has also returned from injury but is not fit enough to start.

The home side remains without several key figures, as Kenan Yildiz, Nico Gonzalez, Khephren Thuram and Manuel Locatelli are still sidelined.

Venezia have their own injury concerns heading into the trip to Turin. Former Lazio midfielder Toma Basic and ex-Napoli defender Pasquale Mazzocchi are among the absentees, paving the way for USMNT international Gianluca Busio to start in central midfield alongside Kike Perez and Simon Sohm.

Juventus (3-4-2-1): Vicario; Celik, Bremer, Kelly; McKennie, Douglas Luiz, Koopmeiners, Cabal; Zhegrova, Alajbegovic; Kolo Muani.

Venezia (3-5-2): Stankovic; Halhal, Moreno, Juan Jesus; Hainaut, Kike Perez, Sohm, Busio, Haps; Adams, Lauberach.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
