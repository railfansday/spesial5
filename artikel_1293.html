Major Sorensen Upgrade: One Of Celtic’s Best Midfielders is Paulo Bernardo

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Celtic have suffered a major blow on the injury front following the news that Mika Baur faces a lengthy spell out of action.

The midfielder has been a big hit at Celtic Park following his £6m move from German club Paderborn, scoring four goals in his first 19 appearances for the club.

Injuring his ankle in Celtic's 1-0 win over Kilmarnock just prior to November's international break, the 22-year-old is looking at around ten weeks on the sidelines, meaning it could be February by the time he is involved once again.

There are also question marks over Benjamin Nygren's fitness ahead of this weekend's game against St Mirren after he missed Sweden's draw in Poland on Tuesday with a heel issue that has been bothering him for a couple of weeks.

The injuries to the first-choice midfielders will see Martin O'Neill have to switch things about in the middle of the park, thus potentially opening the door for Luke McCowan, Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain or even Oliver Sorensen to come into the team.

Given the injury news regarding Baur and Nygren, some Celtic fans have made the point that Paulo Bernardo should be at the club and involved in the first team rather than spending the season on loan in Poland with Gornik Zabrze.

The 24-year-old midfielder is featuring regularly during his time in Poland, playing 17 games and assisting two goals in the process.

At a time when Celtic are now short on midfield options, O'Neill will have to come up with a plan to ensure his team remain on track and maintain their four-point lead over fierce rivals Rangers.

Paulo Bernardo's 2026/27 season in numbers

Bernardo's current club in Poland is owned by none other than former Germany striker Lukas Podolski.

The ex-Arsenal man was this week asked about the Celtic loanee and whether a permanent deal would be possible given it would take a club record transfer to make it happen.

He even hinted that Bernardo's Celtic career may not be over just yet either.

“Definitely," he said when asked if buying Bernardo would see his club smash their transfer record.

But there’s no point in thinking about that right now. The end of the season is a long way off. Things could go in many ways.

“The most important thing is that Bernardo feels good with us. We’ll see after the season.

“Sometimes a player helps the club for a season and then leaves. Like Lubik, for example. Or maybe Celtic will need Bernardo after a good season at Gornik? All kinds of scenarios are possible here.

“Once again, the goal is to build a healthy, well-functioning club. So that Gornik can function well after me. And I think we’re heading in the right direction.”

Celtic signed the 'next Henrikh Larsson' back in 2011, but the player left the Scottish club without a goal and ultimately retired at 29.
