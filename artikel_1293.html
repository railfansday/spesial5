What’s Happened to Chido Obi Since He Joined Willem II as Man Utd Consider Action

Manchester United have several talented youngsters in their ranks at the moment, but arguably the best of the bunch is Chido Obi.

The Danish striker hit the headlines in 2023 when he scored 10 goals for Arsenal in an under-16 match against Liverpool. Obi decided that Arsenal wasn't the best team to continue his development, though, and he signed for Man Utd in the summer of 2024.

The youngster was just 17 years old when he made his debut for the Red Devils. He went onto play eight times for the first-team in the 2024/25 campaign as he looked to make his mark in the Premier League.

After failing to make a single appearance in 2025/26, Obi joined Eredivisie outfit Willem II on a season-long loan in September. United would have hoped that he could continue his development in the Netherlands and return a much better player, but things haven't worked out as they would have hoped.

Three days after signing for Willem II, Obi made his debut for the club against Excelsior. He played the final eight minutes as Willem II were beaten 3-0.

A few weeks later, he made his home debut for the club, coming on as a late substitute in a 5-1 defeat to Dutch giants Ajax. Obi put in a promising performance as he played a key role in his side's goal. He has failed to kick on from there, though.

It's been a dismal campaign so far for Willem II, who are still waiting for their first win in the Eredivisie. They are currently rock bottom of the Netherlands' top tier, having picked up just three points from their opening 12 matches. They are seven points away from safety.

Despite Willem II's struggles, Obi has struggled for game-time. He has not started a single game for the club, coming on as a second-half substitute on six occasions. He has not found the back of the net in the 71 minutes he has played.

He was given 24 minutes in his side's victory over Helmond in the Dutch Cup on October 27. After the match, caretaker boss Adrie Koster wasn't happy with his defensive efforts when Willem went down to ten men.

Koster said: "It is partly a matter of mindset, but also of youthful exuberance. That boy has never experienced anything like this before. He doesn't do it on purpose either, so we shouldn't focus everything on him. This is simply part of his learning process. But we are going to talk to him about it."

Obi's struggles have been so great that United are said to be considering recalling him in January. The Manchester Evening News report that United insert break clauses into the contracts of each young player they send on loan, and that they will consider activating the clause if Obi's situation does not improve.

Still only 18 years old, Obi still has time to fulfill his lofty potential. His spell in the Netherlands has been nothing short of a nightmare so far, though. It remains to be seen whether he can turn his fortunes around.
