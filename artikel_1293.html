Huge for Iraola: Liverpool About to Officially Agree Terms With Trey Nyoni

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Liverpool appear to have hit a sticky patch under Andoni Iraola following a largely positive start to the season under the Spaniard.

Liverpool were unbeaten in all competitions heading into the first international break, but their first match after ended in a 3-0 home defeat to Manchester City.

The Reds did bounce back from that with five consecutive wins, including a penalty shootout victory against Chelsea in the EFL Cup, but they have since lost twice to Arsenal and then Fenerbahce in the Champions League.

The form of the likes of Florian Wirtz and Bradley Barcola is still up and down despite their £100m-plus fees, while the defence continues to be a cause for concern for the former Bournemouth boss.

Wednesday night's 3-2 defeat to Fenerbahce was the third consecutive Champions League game that they had conceded three goals in, and it ended their 100% record in the competition.

Clearly an improvement is needed on the training ground from a defensive point of view, while Iraola still needs to work out how exactly to get the best out of Wirtz and Barcola, based on what we've seen from the big-money duo this term.

One area the 44-year-old might be happier is in midfield, with Alexis Mac Allister, Dominik Szoboszlai, Ryan Gravenberch and Trey Nyoni pushing for two starting spots in the XI.

After signing new long-term contracts earlier in 2026, you may have expected Szoboszlai and Gravenberch to be the first-choice partnership in Liverpool's engine room, but instead it is the other two who have probably shone most under Iraola to date.

Nyoni in the Champions League this season

Mac Allister looks to be the most motivated of the more senior central midfielders even though the club currently have no plans to offer the Argentina international a new contract, according to GIVEMESPORT sources.

The jury is out on the performances of both Szoboszlai and Gravenberch this term, including when the Reds' midfield instantly lost control when the duo replaced Nyoni and James McConnell in the 3-1 EFL Cup win against Tottenham.

Nyoni has impressed so much that he is now on the verge of signing a contract extension at Anfield.

Nyoni has now made 32 first-team appearances for Liverpool, so while his involvement this term under Iraola shouldn't be a surprise, what is a surprise is the fact that he is getting to the stage where he is making himself an automatic starter because of the quality he is showing.

The midfielder only turned 19 years of age in June, but he looks like he has been playing for the Reds for years and looks completely fearless and composed every time he steps onto the pitch for the Merseysiders.

Nyoni is providing a midfield solidity that isn't there when Gravenberch and Szoboszlai play together, and Wirtz tends to thrive more when the teenager is on the pitch and his defensive work rate is reduced as a result.

The youngster has been especially outstanding for the Reds in the Champions League this term, starting their previous three games in the competition.

While he is primarily a defensively-minded player, he even popped up with a goal against Fenerbahce on Wednesday night.

OptaJoe posted on Thursday morning that the teenager has also completed 92% of his passes under high intensity in Europe's elite club competition, which is the highest accuracy of any midfielder who has attempted 100 passes or more.

It actually puts Nyoni ahead of second-placed Pedri (91%), who is considered one of the best young central midfielders in the world after the impact he has made for Barcelona.

It's still early days in the midfielder's career, but the level he is playing at this season suggests Liverpool have a generational talent on their hands.

If they get the contract renewal over the line in the coming days and weeks, it can only be a big boost for Iraola, who clearly loves the 19-year-old.
