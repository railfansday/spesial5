England left sweating as Harry Kane faces months out with ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

England captain Harry Kane is facing three months out with an ankle injury, Bayern Munich have confirmed.

The Ballon d’Or winner scored against both Croatia and Spain during the recent international break and was seeking a first goal in five matches for the Bundesliga leaders against Cologne at the Allianz Arena on Friday night.

However, as he looked to latch on to Michael Olise’s deflected low cross at the back post, Cologne full-back Joel Schmied got to the ball first and clattered into Kane’s left leg during his follow-through.

Kane immediately clutched his lower leg and writhed in pain for a few moments before gingerly getting back to his feet, but he was a virtual bystander for the next couple of minutes.

The 33-year-old gestured to the dugout, sunk to the turf and then received treatment on his ankle before making way for Tom Bischof, limping off the field alongside two physios.

Kane had a quick exchange with Bayern boss Vincent Kompany, back in the dugout for the first time in six matches after being diagnosed with meningeal irritation last month, before heading down the tunnel.

Speaking at full-time, Kompany confirmed that Kane was facing months on the sidelines with what Bayern director of sport Max Eberl described as a tear of the syndesmotic ligament.

Sky Germany has reported that he can expect to spend three months on the sidelines, meaning he will hope to return before England face Norway in the Nations League quarter-finals in March.

An official Bayern statement read: “Harry Kane sustained a syndesmosis ligament tear in his left ankle during this evening’s home match against 1. FC Köln. This was confirmed by an examination carried out by FC Bayern’s medical department. The 33 year-old will be sidelined for the next few months. Get well soon, Harry!”

Matters only grew worse for Bayern thereafter, as Olise was also forced off shortly after Kane with a foot injury. He was able to walk off the pitch before treatment and has been diagnosed with a bruised forefoot that should only keep him on the sidelines for a matter of days.

Tottenham loanee Mikey Moore opened the scoring for Cologne after just 12 minutes on Friday night, but the match finished 1-1 as Nathaniel Brown equalised for Bayern before the break.
