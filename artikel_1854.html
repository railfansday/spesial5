Prediction: Galatasaray vs Aston Villa

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Turning their attention back to Champions League action on Tuesday evening, Aston Villa will travel to Istanbul when they meet Turkish giants Galatasaray.

With a stacked schedule from across the continent in midweek, there are plenty of eye-catching Champions League betting promotions available.

Ahead of a blockbuster battle from Istanbul on Tuesday, you can find all of the key Galatasaray vs Aston Villa H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, marking their return from November’s International break with a 2-1 victory at home against Samsunspor over the weekend, Galatasaray are enjoying what has become a dominant run of form. Already holding a commanding six-point buffer at the top of the Super Lig standings and universally regarded as a landslide contender to once again defend their Turkish crown, Okan Buruk’s men certainly find the mood in their camp at a red-hot high. In fact, last seen in Champions League action opening November with a deserved 2-0 win at home against Stuttgart, Galatasaray have won each of their previous seven consecutive appearances across all competitions. Likewise, gaining a reputation for their free-scoring performances in the final third, Buruk’s high-flying squad have notched 19 goals on their seven-match winning run.

Undergoing minor knee surgery earlier in the month, Galatasaray will once again be without talismanic striker Victor Osimhen on Tuesday evening. Likewise, on what has a huge blow for Buruk’s camp, the Turkish giants will also be without blockbuster summer recruit Rafael Leao due to a recent muscle issue.

Left as an unusused substitute against Samsunspor over the weekend, one-time Tottenham defender Davinson Sanchez could be handed a full recall. Now in his fifth campaign at Rams Park, former Arsenal midfielder Lucas Torreira will start in the middle of the park.

As for the visitors, marking their respective return on Saturday afternoon with a downbeat display in the Midlands as they were eventually forced to settle for a 0-0 stalemate against Sunderland, Aston Villa find themselves at the heart of what has become an extended wobble. Heading into November’s fortnight interval with an infamous last-gasp collapse at Old Trafford as they played out a thrilling 4-4 draw against Manchester United, Unai Emery’s men have failed to win any of their previous five appearances across all competitions (two defeats, three draws). Blown away in Catalonia earlier in the month as they opened November with a routine 3-0 drumming against Spanish heavyweights Barcelona, Aston Villa now sit in the bottom-half of the Premier League standings. It should also be highlighted, Emery’s struggling squad have shipped 10 goals in their last three showdowns away from Villa Park.

Yet to make a maiden appearance this season after sustaining a long-term knee injury while away with Belgium at this summer’s World Cup, Aston Villa will once again be without ex-Everton star Amadou Onana. Likewise, Italian defender Matteo Ruggeri is still recovering from a muscle issue and won’t play any part in Istanbul.

Forced to settle for a spot on the bench over the weekend, club captain John McGinn will be handed an immediate full recall. Enjoying a sensational start to life in the Midlands and also notching three goals in his opening four Champions League appearances, summer recruit Nicolas Jackson will spearhead Villa’s attack.

Really showing some cause for concern on either side of November’s international break, Aston Villa should be bracing themselves for another stern examination here. Completely blown away in Catalonia earlier in the month as they fell to that 3-0 loss against Barcelona, Tuesday’s guests have failed to win any of their previous five consecutive appearances across all competitions. Meeting a free-scoring Galatasaray who are enjoying a stunning extended flurry, Emery’s men have shipped 10 goals in their last three matchups outside of the Midlands and they could be asked some serious questions on foreign soil.
