Richter: Sullivan ‘not afraid’ ahead of MLS playoffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Philadelphia Union interim head coach Ryan Richter is confident Cavan Sullivan will rise to the occasion in the MLS Cup Playoffs, as he has no fear.

Philadelphia finished sixth in the Eastern Conference, setting up a Round One best-of-three series against the New England Revolution.

Sullivan's form was key to the Union's mid-season turnaround as he spurred the team into a 14-game unbeaten run following the World Cup break, and he scored twice to help them secure a 4-1 win over Toronto FC on Decision Day.

And Richter believes the 17-year-old's fearlessness will be a huge help to the Union as they chase their first MLS Cup.

"The thing is, he has the personality for that," Richter told MLSsoccer.com.

"He has the mentality. He's not afraid of the moment. He's excited by it, and he has the personality to take the pressure that comes with that and perform.

"It's a lot of hard work that got him here. Of course he has the talent, he has the support system, and he has the personality, but he works very hard for everything that's coming to him."

New England, meanwhile, finished third in the East, recording their best finish to a regular-season campaign since topping the charts in 2021.

They are playing in their first playoff campaign in three years, though they followed up a four-game winning run by losing their last two matches.

One boost is that Jack Harrison is closing in on a return, and he hopes he is able to do so in the playoffs.

"I think ultimately it's down to Marko [Mitrovic, head coach] and what the game is asking for," he told The Soccer Show.

"We have to be smart and strategic in the playoffs, with the first round being best of three. It's something to think about, but I'm definitely excited to potentially get some minutes at some point."

Carles Gil was the driving force behind New England qualifying for the playoffs, with 25 goal involvements (13 goals, 12 assists), at least six more than any other player (Dor Turgeman with 19).

Gil also topped the charts for chances created (95) and crosses (183), and his creativity will be key to sparking the Revs' attack.

Sullivan has been a revelation during his breakout season, and he signed off for the regular season with a brace against Toronto to take his goal tally to nine for the campaign.

He overperformed his expected goals by 2.0 (7.0 xG), but he was also stringing things together as well, with no Philadelphia player bettering his tally of 14 assists.

These sides met in the First Round of the Playoffs last time New England qualified back in 2023, and it was the Union who came out on top in the best-of-three series.

Philadelphia won the first game 3-1 at home and followed it up with a 1-0 victory at Gillette Stadium to book their place in the next round.

New England won both meetings with the Union in the regular season in 2026, winning 2-1 home and away. That followed a six-game losing streak against Philadelphia.

However, the Revs head into the postseason having lost their last two matches, though Philadelphia's win on Decision Day ended a four-game winless streak (D2 L2).
