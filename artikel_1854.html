The race for Liverpool & Chelsea target Ismaelo Ganiou heats up

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Newcastle are to latest Premier League club to join the growing race for Lens youngster Ismaelo Ganiou, according to a report from Football Insider.

While the 21-year-old might have missed each of Lens’ final five appearances prior to November’s international break, Ganiou has received a string of plaudits from across the continent.

Bursting onto the scene last season and quickly regarded as one of Europe’s hottest young defensive starlets, the French U21 international was subject to hefty summer interest from Crystal Palace.

However, staying put at the Stade Bollaert-Delelis, Ganiou has become a key part of Didier Digard’s squad and Football Insider are reporting that a host of Premier League giants are keeping close tabs on young central defender.

While Football Insider might be suggesting that Newcastle are the latest club to stake their interest, the St. James’ Park hierarchy have a real heavyweight tussle on their hands.

Alongside continued speculation over a potential move to Liverpool, the likes of Manchester City and Chelsea have also been linked with Ganiou over the past couple of months.

Putting pen to paper on a new deal with the French outfit back in August, Ganiou’s current deal at the Stade Bollaert-Delelis runs until the summer of 2030.
