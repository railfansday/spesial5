Robert Pattinson is the new James Bond, and fans have thoughts

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

After months of casting rumors and fan theories, we finally know who the next James Bond is. It's Robert Pattinson, everyone’s favorite sparkly vampire and Gotham’s most emo vigilante. No, the internet is not okay.

In the hour following Robert Pattinson's James Bond announcement, fans came straight to X, posting more than 33K times, driving 50 million impressions.

"Bravo sir bravo, being batman & james bond is a accomplishment," said one X user. "Bro is collecting Hollywood's biggest franchises like Infinity Stones," wrote another. "He wasn't lying when he said he likes taking jobs from younger actors," wrote Dextero, referring to a comment he made during his Vanity Fair polygraph interview with his Die My Love co-star Jennifer Lawrence.

The actor, who broke out as Cedric Diggory in Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire two decades ago and went on to play Edward Cullen in Twilight, is having a wildly busy year at the movies. In 2026 alone, he’s played Zendaya's fiancé in The Drama, one of Penelope's suitors in The Odyssey, and To Catch a Predator host Chris Hansen in Primetime.

Though Pattinson is a largely favored actor on the internet, not everyone is ecstatic about the choice for him to play 007.

Others made jokes, and complaints, about the sheer amount of movies Pattison's in. One user went as far to say that "Robert Pattinson is nearly everywhere lately — audience fatigue." Another called the announcement "disappointing."

Pattinson takes over from Daniel Craig, who bowed out after five Bond films with 2021's No Time to Die. It's also the first Bond casting since Amazon MGM Studios took creative control of the franchise in 2025. Amazon bought MGM in 2022, then made a separate deal with longtime producers Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson. They remain co-owners, and Amazon MGM now calls the creative shots.

Pattinson's idea of a franchise role can be pretty strange already. His Bruce Wayne is a brooding recluse who looks like he hasn't slept in weeks, and later this year he'll play a shapeshifting assassin in Dune: Part Three. That film's director, Denis Villeneuve, will also direct the next Bond, with Peaky Blinders creator Steven Knight writing the script.
