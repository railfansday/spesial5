Seth Meyers reacts to reporter calling Trump a loser

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Still reeling from the blue wave post-midterms, Donald Trump's media appearances lately have been somewhat subdued.

It's a through-line Seth Meyers picked up on during Late Night on Wednesday, from the president's viral 60 Minutes appearance to his real-time reaction to the election results calling into Fox News.

"The sadder Trump gets, the more emboldened the press gets," said Meyers. "In fact, there's one clip from a few days ago that really sums up the mood at the White House right now."

That clip? Sky News international affairs editor Dominic Waghorn asking Trump, "How does it feel being a loser, Mr. President?" Choosing not to answer, the president can be seen walking quickly away from the group of reporters outside the White House.

"Damn, he was out of there so fast, I thought he was gonna Mary Poppins out of frame," reacted Meyers. "It's so funny to get asked what does it feel like to be a loser and then walk away alone in the rain. How does it feel to be a loser? I'll take my answer non-verbally."
