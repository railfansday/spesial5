Chris Wilder tells Sheffield United – and referees – to be more streetwise

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Japhet Tanganga picked up his fifth yellow cards of the season on Saturday, and the Blades manager is frustrated with that and the way Saturday's 4-0 win over Watford was refereed.

Thirteen players were booked, six of them Blades, and Tanganga's caution came in the 87th minute.
