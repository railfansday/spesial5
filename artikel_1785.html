‘I don’t really care’ – Tillman indifferent to applause from Leverkusen faithful

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Malik Tillman is perhaps on the brink of creating a rift with the Bayer Leverkusen supporters after declaring that their support does not impact him.

Tillman starred in a 4-0 victory over Paderborn on Saturday. The result means Leverkusen now sit fifth in the Bundesliga standings, five points behind leaders Borussia Dortmund.

Afonso Moreira scored his first goals for Leverkusen either side of the break before Patrik Schick netted a third in the 51st minute. Equi Fernandez added further gloss to the scoreline with a strike eight minutes later.

Leverkusen have collected 12 points from their home games in the 2026-27 Bundesliga season; only in their 2023-24 title-winning campaign (15) have they managed as many from their first five home matches in the last 12 years.

Tillman impressed, assisting Moreira's 14th-minute finish. He launched dangerous attacks and constantly won the ball back in midfield. By the end of the game, the United States international had created a joint-team-high four chances.

But the applause from the stands left him perplexed. Speaking to Bild, Tillman said: "These days, I don't really care.

"Of course, it sucks to be booed, but at the end of the day, it doesn't bother me much anymore when people cheer or call my name."

Tillman's comments relate to the booing he received from sections of the Leverkusen supporters after he was substituted during a 4-2 home defeat to Stuttgart last month.

However, Leverkusen boss Martinez was full of praise for Tillman.

"He understood the plan in every area: where he needed to move inside, where he needed to move out of position, and where he needed to create space for Nathan Tella," said Martinez.

"A few weeks ago, we said that it depended on him to perform and believe in himself. I think he's taken that step, and we are very happy about it."
