Klopp reveals failed Ter Stegen transfer bid at Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Jurgen Klopp revealed he tried to sign Marc-Andre ter Stegen while he was Liverpool boss, but the goalkeeper ultimately chose to stay at Barcelona.

Klopp took charge of Liverpool in October 2015, the year after Ter Stegen had sealed a transfer from Borussia Monchengladbach to Barcelona.

Ter Stegen stayed at Barcelona for 12 years, making 423 appearances across all competitions during a trophy-laden spell.

He lifted seven LaLiga titles, six Copa del Reys, four Supercopa de Espanas, a Champions League, a Super Cup and a Club World Cup with the Catalan giants.

Ter Stegen, who is on loan at Ajax, is now Klopp's number one for Germany, with the 34-year-old playing the full 90 minutes of their thumping 5-0 win over the Netherlands in the Nations League on Monday.

And Klopp was thrilled by Ter Stegen's performance, noting that he has always been an admirer of the goalkeeper.

"I have always considered him an exceptional goalkeeper, and I wanted to bring him to Liverpool, but he preferred Barcelona," Klopp said, as quoted by Mundo Deportivo.

"I don't hold a grudge against him," he joked.

On the potential move, Ter Stegen had already said: "Let's put it this way: He was the coach there. We simply had a general conversation back then.

"But in the end, neither of us ever went into it any deeper."
