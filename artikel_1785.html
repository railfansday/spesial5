England vs Croatia: Prediction, kick-off time, TV, live stream, team news, h2h results, odds today

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

England face Croatia tonight in the penultimate game of the Nations League group stage, with captain Harry Kane to become their most-capped men’s player of all time.

Fresh from winning the Ballon d’Or in London last month, Kane is set to make his 126th senior international appearance on Thursday evening to move clear of long-time record-holder Peter Shilton.

Croatia head to Wembley with their mauling at the hands of the Three Lions in Rijeka last month still fresh in the memory.

Thomas Tuchel’s side put seven past their hosts without reply, as Kane and Bukayo Saka both bagged braces. There were further goals from Jude Bellingham, Anthony Gordon and Morgan Rogers in a match played technically behind closed doors, but with thousands of schoolchildren permitted to attend.

England are poised to advance to the next stage of the Nations League but currently via a second-placed finish, which would set up a quarter-final with a big hitter such as France or Portugal.

Victory for England at Wembley would likely tee up a mouthwatering last-game decider away in Spain on Sunday, with the winner of that match topping the group.

England vs Croatia is scheduled for a 7.45pm GMT kick-off tonight on Thursday November 12, 2026.

The match will take place at Wembley Stadium in north-west London.

TV channel: In the UK, England vs Croatia in the 2026/27 Nations League is being televised live and free to air on ITV, with coverage of the match beginning at 7pm on ITV1.

Live stream: Catch the game live online through the ITVX website and app, which is free with a registration.

Live blog: You can follow all the action with Standard Sport’s live blog, featuring expert insight and analysis from England correspondents Dom Smith and Sam Tabuteau.

Injuries to Marc Guehi, Djed Spence and Ezri Konsa have led to late England call-ups for Dan Burn and James Hill, with the latter hoping to make his senior debut.

Other potential debutants include Fulham's Josh King and Borussia Dortmund midfielder Jobe Bellingham, younger brother of England star Jude Bellingham.

England are a bit light on the right flank with Arsenal duo Saka and Noni Madueke both not in the squad, with Chelsea’s Rogers or Liverpool youngster Rio Ngumoha likely to start instead.

For Croatia, ex-Tottenham winger Ivan Perisic is suspended after his red card last time out against Spain in Split.

After last month’s chastening 7-0 loss to England, many in Croatia are writing this game off, with calls to rest legendary 41-year-old Luka Modric for the crunch clash with the Czech Republic on Sunday.

As mentioned above, this fixture has already produced plenty of goals. Croatia have been leaky at the back throughout the Nations League campaign, conceding 14 times in four games.

A 4-1 drubbing at the hands of Spain in their last away match indicates that England should have plenty of chances to score at home.

While the Three Lions were able to keep Croatia quiet in Rijeka, their guests on Thursday night usually score at least once. It would not be a surprise to see England match Spain’s latest result against Croatia.

England and Croatia have crossed paths in three of the last four major tournaments, with the Three Lions victorious on two occasions. However, Croatia had the last laugh - beating England in the semi-finals of the World Cup in Russia in 2018.

Overall, this is a fixture England usually win - but Croatia have inflicted major upsets before, preventing England from qualifying for Euro 2008 with wins home and away.
