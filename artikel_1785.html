Prediction: Gremio vs Bahia

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Two camps who will mark their respective returns from November’s international break in the early hours of Thursday, Gremio will welcome Bahia to the Rio Grande do Sul.

With Europe’s elite domestic leagues all stealing centre stage this weekend, there are plenty of eye-catching betting promotions available.

Ahead of the midweek showdown from Porto Alegre, you can find all of the key Gremio vs Bahia H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, while some in Gremio’s camp might have opened up the 2026 campaign dreaming of the opportunity to mount a genuine Copa Libertadores charge, Renato Portaluppi’s men have endured a public Serie A struggle this season. Marking their return from November’s international break sat in the relegation zone, the Immortal Tricolor have plenty of pressure on their shoulders ahead of Thursday’s showdown in Porto Alegre. However, last seen punching their spot in the final of this season’s Copa do Brasil thanks to a 3-1 aggregate victory against Atletico Mineiro, Gremio have enjoyed an essential recent upturn in fortune. Signing off October with a 2-0 win at home against Athletico-PR, Thursday’s hosts have lost just one of their previous five domestic appearances across all competitions. Likewise, Portaluppi’s camp have registered back-to-back victories in Porto Alegre for the first time since the start of September.

Shown what was a costly booking against reigning Serie A champions Flamengo earlier in the month, veteran defender Walter Kannemann will serve a one-match suspension here. However, serving his own top-flight ban on November 4th, one-time Tottenham striker Carlos Vinicius should be handed an immediate full Serie A recall.

Despite registering just a sole top-flight strike so far this season, former Barcelona star Martin Braihwaite will be hoping to keep his spot from the start. Completing a permanent switch to Porto Alegre over the summer, Croatian international Filip Krovinovic could make a first start since October 11th.

As for the visitors, while Bahia might have signed off October with a much-welcomed 3-2 victory at home against Sao Paulo, Rogerio Ceni’s men were searching for some essential stability prior to November’s international break. Last seen making an immediate return to losing ways as they eventually fell to a dramatic 3-2 loss against Cruzeiro, the Salvador-based outfit now have a major battle on their hands in the race to punch a Copa Libertadores ticket over the next month. Marking their return in midweek holding just a two-point buffer over sacrificing their spot, Bahia have mustered just a single win from any of their previous five appearances across all competitions. With their recent struggles underlined by a 3-0 drumming against Flamengo on October 18th, Thursday’s guests have shown some extended defensive issues.

Still recovering from a long-term knee injury and not making a single appearance across all competitions since the end of May, veteran goalkeeper Leo Vieira remains on the sidelines. However, despite struggling to hold down a regular spot this season, ex-Real Betis striker Willian Jose could return to Ceni’s squad.

Bagging what turned out to be a consolation strike against Cruzeiro prior to November’s international break, Alejo Veliz should keep his spot leading the line. Former New York City midfielder Nicolas Acevedo will start in the middle of the park here.

Looking to skyrocket their way out of the drop zone, Gremio will be bracing themselves for what could be a season-defining showdown in the early hours of Thursday. Last seen in Porto Alegre landing a deserved 2-0 win against Atletico Mineiro, the Immortal Tricolor will be boosted by their recent success on home soil. Landing back-to-back victories in front of their own supporters for the first time since the start of September, they will be looking to exploit a Bahia squad who have shown some extended issues on their travels.
