Onana pens new Aston Villa deal: It’s been amazing so far

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Amadou Onana is relishing the opportunity to extend his stay at Aston Villa after he signed a new deal with Unai Emery's team.

Onana joined Villa from Everton for a reported £50m in 2024, and has made 72 appearances for the Villans, scoring seven goals.

However, the 25-year-old is out for the majority of this campaign after he sustained a cruciate ligament injury.

Yet that has not stopped Villa from offering the Belgium international fresh terms, and he is thrilled to stay on.

"Everyone at this football club just made me feel so welcome, from the first day – the staff, the players," Onana said.

"The fans, I obviously have a very special connection with them as well, so it means a lot for me to be able to renew my contract.

"[Unai is] very important – he's the main man. If we are in this position, this situation we are in, it's thanks to him.

"He's done incredible things at this football club, four years in a row in Europe, winning a Europa League in the third year, I think that's something very special and we all have to thank him for that.

"If I'm renewing and re-signing a new deal, I must be enjoying it! It's been amazing so far. They've definitely improved me as a player and made me a better player, so I'm grateful to them, but there's still a lot of work to do.

"It's a big one for me, for the club to take the initiative to renew my contract while I'm recovering from such a big injury, it means a lot.

"It's definitely made me a lot stronger, and I learned a lot about myself as well. Sometimes it gets dark, and that's when you need those people around you – people from here, the staff – but I'm very grateful for how it's going so far. I can't wait to be back."

Villa sit 10th in the Premier League ahead of facing Sunderland on Saturday.
