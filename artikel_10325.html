Successful high-purity synthesis of the challenging-to-produce cerium orthoferrite!

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

A research team including scientists from Ehime University successfully synthesized high-purity CeFeO3, a difficult-to-produce cerium–iron mixed oxide, using hydrogen reduction. By optimizing hydrogen concentration and gas flow rate, they achieved a phase purity of up to 99 mol%. The team also clarified the formation mechanism and identified factors that hinder synthesis. This method, published in the journal Inorganic Chemistry, provides a foundation for future research into applications in magnetic materials and catalysis.
