Google retiring Nest app and Google Assistant on speakers, displays in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Following the Android deprecation in September, Google Assistant is going away on speakers and smart displays next year. Additionally, Google Home is fully replacing the Nest app in January.

The Nest app on Android, iOS, and the web will stop working on January 25, 2027. The Google Home app, which requires a Google Account, will be needed to “continue controlling and receiving notifications from your Nest devices remotely.”

Officially, Google wants to “focus all of [its] engineering, security, and feature investments on one unified, modern experience.” It reiterated benefits like:

With home.nest.com going away, you have to download any custom video clips since they will not transfer over to Google Home. A one-click “Download All” button is available.

Terrible news. For all my first-gen Nest cameras (wired hello doorbell, wired outdoor cams, and multiple indoor cams), I vastly prefer the Nest app to the terrible Google Home app.

Otherwise, Nest app users do not need to do anything else, with devices automatically transferring over to the Google Home app. No Nest thermostats, cameras, doorbells, smoke alarms, or locks are losing support with this change.

Additionally, Nest Aware (1st Gen) subscriptions will automatically switch to Google Home Premium “with no additional cost and no credit card re-entry required at this time.”

The other change announced today is that Google Assistant on speakers and smart displays is going away on August 2, 2027. It will be replaced by the Gemini for Home voice assistant. Google notes that natural language support replaces “rigid, static and highly specific voice commands.” Additionally:

You can upgrade to Gemini today, while Google is expanding international availability over the coming months. Google will share more updates closer to the deadline.
