West Ham Sack Nuno As Křetínský Loses Patience

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

West Ham United have confirmed that Nuno Espírito Santo has been sacked with immediate effect following mounting pressure on the Hammers head coach over the past few days.

The Portuguese manager’s position had become increasingly precarious following consecutive defeats against Southampton and West Bromwich Albion, with concerns growing behind the scenes that the club’s automatic promotion ambitions were slipping away.

As Claret & Hugh reported earlier, there had been growing indications from within the club that Nuno was no longer safe in his position. Daniel Křetínský was understood to be increasingly unimpressed with performances, and the decision to act suggests the West Ham hierarchy were no longer prepared to allow the situation to continue.

Rumours had also emerged yesterday that discussions were already taking place over the identity of West Ham’s next head coach, suggesting that preparations for a possible managerial change were underway.

The decision is particularly significant given Křetínský’s previous backing of Nuno. However, as our earlier research into his managerial decisions at Sparta Prague demonstrated, the Czech billionaire has a history of making changes when he believes a club’s objectives are under threat.

West Ham have now confirmed that the change has been made following a review of the Championship campaign.

In an official statement, the club announced:

West Ham United can confirm that men’s first-team Head Coach Nuno Espírito Santo has left his position with immediate effect.

Following a thorough review of the start to the EFL Championship season, the Board of Directors believe that a change is necessary at this time in order to ensure that the Club’s objectives and expectations remain on track.

Assistant Head Coach Rui Pedro Silva and Goalkeeper Coach Rui Barbosa have also departed.

West Ham United would like to thank Nuno, Rui and Rui for their hard work and commitment during their time at the Club, and wish them all the very best for the future.

The process of recruiting a new Head Coach is already underway.

Mark Noble, Steve Potts and Gerard Prenderville will take temporary charge of squad preparations ahead of tomorrow evening’s EFL Championship fixture at Cardiff City, and the Club will make no further comment until the appointment of the new Head Coach is confirmed.

West Ham must now move quickly to appoint a replacement, with the Championship promotion race continuing and tomorrow night’s trip to Cardiff City fast approaching.
