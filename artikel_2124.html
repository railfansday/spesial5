Championship striker pinpoints Hammers’ problem and offers hope for week ahead | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Amongst the many voices raised over Nuno’s travails at West Ham following two consecutive home defeats, some opinions  deserve more consideration than others.

One former Championship player who knows only too well the challenges faced by teams struggling to compete in this, the toughest of the English leagues, has added to the growing suggestion that West Ham’s home form is suspect due to the weight of home expectation.

Important to remember that all three of the Irons’ defeats this season have come in front of an increasingly vocal, critical home fan base at London Stadium, and former Hull city and Peterborough striker Aaron McLean is certain it’s all down to the pressures of playing at home.

Writing for the bbc.co.uk after witnessing the Hammers’ second capitulation in two consecutive home games, McLean suggests:

“West Ham just don’t seem to be handling the pressure.

The fanbase is very split in terms of whether they want Nuno Espirito Santo in charge but the thing you have to do when playing for West Ham in front of 60,000 fans is put in a performance and if you’re in the Championship, you have to win.

BBC pundit McLean believes the Hammers squad aren’t coping with the pressure of playing in front of their own, demanding fan base at home.

“When you have a lovely stadium and playing surface, teams want to go there and enjoy it.

If you make your home ground a fortress, that can work for you but at the moment, the London Stadium is not a fortress and teams are going there and enjoying the experience, which is the total opposite of what West Ham need.”

As we wrote last week, the very weight of expectation appears to be hanging heavily around the players’ necks at present. If that is the case then the up and coming away fixtures at Cardiff City and Blackburn Rovers this week will carry no such difficulties – merely unending vocal support from a travelling support that simply sings its heart out – whatever the match situation.

Just what Nuno’s men need right now. A couple of away days before the international break with the chance to get back to winning ways, without the weight of the world on their shoulders. Dealing with the pressure at home is another problem altogether.
