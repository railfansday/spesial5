Dibu Martinez backs Nico Paz to become Argentina’s next No.10 after Messi

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Chelsea goalkeeper Dibu Martinez feels Como star Nico Paz ‘can definitely wear Argentina’s No. 10 shirt’ as the team helps him develop the ‘Argentine fighting spirit.’

Nico Paz scored his first brace for Argentina on Tuesday, helping the team secure a 7-0 win over Costa Rica.

As the debate over Argentina’s next No.10 rages following Leo Messi’s international retirement, Paz has emerged as a credible candidate, scoring five goals in his last five appearances with La Selección.

Dibu Martinez, a key figure in the Argentina dressing room, clearly has great confidence in the Como star’s potential.

“I see him becoming more and more integrated into the group,” the Chelsea goalkeeper said after the game against Costa Rica (via Mundo Deportivo).

“Obviously, he needs to have that Argentine fighting spirit, and we’re helping him develop it. The truth is, he deserves everything that’s happening to him right now. He deserves to be here and to play. The goals he’s scoring are an added bonus.

“If he maintains this humility and keeps performing at this level, Nico Paz can definitely wear Argentina’s No. 10 shirt and take on that responsibility,” Martinez continued.

“We need players like him, players who see themselves as leaders. He can help us win a lot of games.

“We have to help him. We’re a national team. There are no egos here. It’s all about hard work, humility, and respect.”

Nico Paz has just begun his third season at Como, and in the previous campaign he helped the team coached by Cesc Fabregas qualify for the Champions League for the first time in their history.

He’s also had a strong start to the 2026-27 season at Como, with six goals and eight assists in 14 appearances across all competitions.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
