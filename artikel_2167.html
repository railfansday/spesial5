Premier League clubs face Tonda Eckert setback after Southampton make 2029 decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Southampton had a chaotic summer after missing out on promotion to the Premier League last season due to ‘Spygate’.

The fallout from Saints sending employees to watch rivals train saw them kicked out of the play-offs, and they’re still paying the price for that now, with a four-point deduction applied to the current campaign.

However, when Tonda Eckert avoided an immediate touchline ban back in October, it did at least close the case for Saints, and the focus is now entirely on winning promotion this season.

To their credit, Eckert and the players didn’t allow all the off-field noise to distract them, and they carried on the excellent form they had shown under the German last season.

That had put Eckert on the radar of other clubs, with reports claiming that sides in the Premier League and across Europe were monitoring the 33-year-old.

Therefore, Saints have moved to tie down Eckert to a longer contract, and it was announced on Friday that he had signed a deal that runs until 2029.

When you look at how Southampton have been transformed under the boss, it’s fully deserved, as he inherited a team that were struggling towards the bottom of the Championship.

Following on from that, Saints would have been relaxed about interest in Eckert, because the reality is that he was always going to attract attention when you look at their results and performances.

Let's not forget, Southampton didn't just excel in the Championship under Eckert last season, as they beat Arsenal in the FA Cup and pushed Man City all the way in their semi-final clash.

In many ways, it’s a positive that other clubs were tracking Eckert as it shows he is doing something right.

Ultimately, that’s part and parcel of football, and the same applies to players who are delivering on the pitch. So, all Southampton can do is reward those who perform at a high level, which is exactly what they’ve done with Eckert.

Obviously, the situation with Eckert is different to most clubs due to what went on, so he should feel loyalty to Southampton due to how they stuck by him.

In a way, everything that happened has united the club, and all at Southampton are now pulling in the same direction.

By rewarding Eckert with a new contract, this is Southampton showing faith in the boss, and they will have big plans for how they can kick-on over the next three years.

Results will dictate how things play out, but Saints have put things in place for Eckert to lead them to what they will hope is a successful era.

Even though Eckert will appreciate the loyalty Saints have shown to him, he will have his own ambitions as a head coach, and he won’t stick around forever.

Clearly, that will be managing at the highest level, so if offers do arrive for Eckert in the future, he may have a decision to make.
