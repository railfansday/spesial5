JiDion captures EDP445’s arrest on camera after tracking him down in Las Vegas

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

JiDion has released footage showing the exact moment EDP445 was arrested in Nevada, after the YouTuber spent three days staking him out and calling police himself.

EDP445, real name Bryant Moreland, was arrested on November 4 on a warrant out of Kern County, California, after comments he made in the documentary The Sick Mind of EDP445 led prosecutors to reopen a 2021 case.

In a new YouTube video, JiDion shows officers surrounding Moreland’s car outside a dialysis center in Henderson, before taking him into custody as he walked out of the building.

“They got him. They got him surrounded,” JiDion said as he filmed the arrest from his car. “This is absolute cinema.”

The arrest took place at the same dialysis center where JiDion confronted Moreland in 2023. “The place where we first caught him is going to be the place where he finally gets arrested,” he said.

JiDion explained that he flew to Las Vegas after spotting the warrant on Kern County’s website, and reported it in person to the Henderson Police Department, where an officer told him to call if he saw Moreland in public. Three days later, he called 911 when Moreland arrived at the center.

Moreland faces multiple felony charges related to arranging a meeting with a minor, according to FOX5 Vegas. He waived his right to fight extradition at a hearing on November 9, and faces a $30,000 bail once he is returned to California.

Despite his role, JiDion played down his involvement. “I had nothing to do with EDP getting arrested. The only thing I did was call the cops,” he said, crediting Alex Rosen, Mike Clum and Chris Hansen instead.

He then announced “the official end of the show EDP Watch,” explaining that he had been thinking about stopping for a while and wants to make more lighthearted content.

“This is very dark stuff and very dark energy being around constantly all the time. And I knew I wasn’t going to be doing this forever,” he said, adding that he may still do occasional catches, just not full time.

JiDion said his team had carried out 200 catches over two years, leading to more than 100 arrests and 60 convictions. He has also made his paid EDP Watch site free and pledged $70,000 to children’s protection charities.
