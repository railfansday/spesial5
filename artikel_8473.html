I'd be interested to learn more about the "10 year bonus" on these annuities. Will the amounts you quoted be added to the account value at the end of 10 years? And, if so, are the bonus amounts affected if your friend were to take partial withdrawals over the next 6 years?
Here is my understanding of how the bonus works. The bonus equals 7% of the initial premium, and is vested 10% per year into a Bonus Account so that after 10 years the Bonus is fully vested. The Bonus Account is distinguished from the Primary Account. The Bonus Account earns interest at the same rate as the Primary Account. Here is what the Bonus Rider says about Surrenders:

Surrenders

Before the maturity date, you may surrender all or a portion of the surrender
value. The surrender value as defined in the Surrenders section of your annuity
is re-defined to Vested Account Value when this rider is attached. Surrenders,
including surrender charges thereon, will reduce the Primary Account Value and
the Vested Bonus Account Value proportionately. The dollar amount of the
vested portion that is surrendered from the Vested Bonus Account will be
deducted from the Bonus Account Value.

So I get from that that withdrawing 10% would proportionately reduce the Bonus amount.
