Villarreal take record away losing run to Prague, where Slavia are 23 games without a win

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Villarreal visit Slavia Prague after seven straight Champions League away defeats, a record for a Spanish club. Their hosts have gone 23 games without a win in the competition, so one of the two runs must end today.

Villarreal face Slavia Prague in the Champions League today carrying a losing run that UEFA says is a record for a Spanish club. They have lost each of their last seven away games in the competition. Their hosts carry a burden of their own. Slavia have eight draws and 15 defeats in their last 23 Champions League games. That run equals the competition’s worst, set by Steaua București between 2006 and 2013.

One of the two sequences has to end. A Slavia win would end their drought. Any other result would stop Villarreal’s run of away defeats. Kick-off is at 17:45 GMT.

That single win was a 3-1 home victory over Napoli on 13 October. The rest of the league phase has gone badly. Villarreal lost 3-2 at Borussia Dortmund on 8 September and 4-3 at Liverpool on 20 October. PSG then won 6-0 at La Cerámica on 3 November.

That leaves Villarreal 29th with three points. Another defeat would leave them close to elimination with three games to play.

The defence is the clearest problem. Villarreal have not kept a clean sheet in any of those 15 games, a club record. None of their last 35 games in the Champions League proper has ended goalless.

Falling behind early has also been fatal. Villarreal have never won a Champions League game when trailing at half-time, with two draws and 13 defeats. On eight occasions they have gone into the interval losing on the road, and every one of those matches ended in defeat.

Nathan Saliba, Juan Foyth and Ilias Akhomach are all missing in Prague, EFE reported. Gerard Moreno trained before the game and could return.

Trpišovský has six draws and 12 defeats from his 19 Champions League matches, all in charge of Slavia. UEFA describes that as the longest winless run of any coach in the competition. Slavia have also never beaten a Spanish club from the group or league phase onwards, with two draws and four defeats. The two clubs have not met since their two games in 2017.

Villarreal coach Iñigo Pérez still expects a hard night. Speaking in Prague, he said no team in the Champions League is as aggressive as Slavia. He called the game “transcendental” for how the league phase plays out. He added that the city’s cold weather would not be an excuse.

Defender Sergi Cardona said the team must learn last season’s lesson. He expects the game to be extremely demanding.

Slovenian Rade Obrenovič will referee, with his compatriot Matej Jug as video assistant referee.

Prague is the first of three away games in a row for Villarreal across LaLiga, Europe and the Copa del Rey. They do not play at La Cerámica again until 5 December. Their next league-phase game is at home to Sabah on 8 December, before a trip to Fenerbahçe on 20 January and a home game against Manchester United on 27 January.
