Grand Theft Auto 6 keeps the actual mandatory crimes very grounded during its opening hours, far from the previous series openings of massive police shootouts and gang wars bursting at the seams. One of the very first jobs Jason is tasked with sounds fairly simple, but is shockingly involved.

Should you sell pure product or Tyrone’s cut batch in GTA 6? Compare the outcomes, risks, and rewards of each Dealer’s Choice option.

To secure some product left behind by an arrested junkie, Brian tasks Jason with securing a red Phoenix, a sleazy sports car with too much weed in the trunk to just walk away with. Getting it out of the police impound is easier said than done, so let’s go over it step by step.

Before you can yoink the car, you’ll need to start its mission, ‘Unwanted Attention’, by meeting with Cal at one of his fishing spots in Corsair Key. Mark his location via your text messages and drive to the northeast down the highway. These roads are tight, so be careful when trying to pull past other drivers.

Once you’ve snagged Cal, he’ll explain the job, marking the police impound where the Phoenix is currently being held. Once you arrive, you can choose between charging straight in with your newly acquired gear, or sneaking around the side through a hole in the barbed wire fence surrounding the other walls.

Stealthing through the impound is much simpler, with very few guards on patrol. If you’re looking to keep your Criminal Profile at a Professional level, hug the left side of the impound’s outer wall until you see an opening in the barbed wire, giving you an opportunity to hop in.

Now that you’re inside the impound itself, you’ll need to sneak past the two patrolling guards. One is wandering around the gate itself, while the other is deeper inside, near the red Phoenix. You’ll be prompted to use the Waink It app to help you identify the ride, but it always spawns in the northeast end of the impound, still hooked up to a winch.

The guards’ patrol patterns don’t overlap, so feel free to wander past or choke them out at your own discretion. Remember, nonlethal takedowns are easy ways to pivot your Criminal Profile towards Professional, or you can finish them off if you’re feeling a bit crueler.

Once you find the red Phoenix, don’t hop in immediately. Instead, enter the building behind it to find the guard manning the gate itself. Silently take him down, then hit a button on the wall to open the gate. Once the gate is open, lower the winch the Phoenix is attached to and hotwire it.

Now it’s time to bail! Drive straight through the open gate, and you’ll become Wanted, but since all the local cops are either unconscious or checking on their unresponsive allies, you’ve got a window before they arrive. Stick to backstreets or off-road routes to lose the cops quick.

Once you’re all clear, head back over to Hamlet and visit the marked Spray N’ Pray shop to change the car’s paint job. Any will do the trick, even if losing the Phoenix decal is a shame. Once you do, you’ll be able to drop it off at Brian’s Garage for your reward, just enough to buy a new outfit to get off the radar completely.
