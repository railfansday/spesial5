FedEx plane overshoots runway at Miami airport on Thanksgiving eve

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

A FedEx plane from Memphis ran past the end of a runway at Miami airport, shutting most runways and causing delays of several hours on the eve of the US holiday.

A FedEx cargo plane went past the end of a runway at Miami International Airport on Wednesday night, shutting most of the airport's runways as the Thanksgiving travel rush began in the United States.

It forced the closure of all but one runway, and by early Thursday the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) was reporting delays of several hours.

The aircraft was a McDonnell Douglas MD-11 arriving from Memphis. The FAA has launched an investigation.

The airport said the plane overran Runway 26R at about 8:40 pm local time.

FedEx said the plane had "an issue upon landing" and that it is working with investigators. "We are grateful that our crew is safe and there are no reported injuries," the company said.

The incident happened amid heavy rain. No injuries were reported.

The airport advised passengers to check with their airlines before traveling.

It is the second runway overrun at Miami in under three months. An Amazon cargo jet overshot a different runway in September, crossing a road and hitting a van and killing five people on the ground.

Three of the airport's runways meet at their western end, so the FedEx plane came to rest near where the Amazon jet went off. A minor collision also occurred between a Copa Airlines plane and a UPS plane on a taxiway less than two weeks ago.

FedEx only resumed MD-11 flights in May. The type was grounded after a UPS MD-11 crashed in Kentucky last year, killing three pilots and 12 people on the ground.

The incident is the latest in a rough stretch for US aviation, including a nationwide halt to takeoffs on November 13 over air traffic control communication problems.

Up to 80 million people are traveling for Thanksgiving in the country, with a storm expected in the northeastern US.
