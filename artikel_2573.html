Patriots vs. Lions inactives: A.J. Brown, Rhamondre Stevenson given the green light

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The New England Patriots offense received some good news ahead of its game against the Detroit Lions on Sunday. Wide receiver A.J. Brown and running back Rhamondre Stevenson have both been given the green light for the game, despite originally being listed as questionable on Friday's injury report.

The game against the Lions marks Brown's return after missing the previous two games due to ankle and knee issues. Stevenson, meanwhile, is active after hurting his knee last week versus Green Bay. Both participated in a limited capacity at practice on Thursday and Friday.

Here is a full look at the Patriots' inactives for Week 10:

The Patriots already ruled out three players before taking their trip to Germany, including running back TreVeyon Henderson (illness), who has since been placed on injured reserve. With Henderson off the roster, only offensive lineman Ben Brown (knee) and cornerback Carlton Davis (knee) remain on the inactives list out of that group.

Joining them are two players previously listed as questionable on Friday's injury report. Neither special teams captain Brenden Schooler (hamstring) nor reserve interior offensive lineman Walter Rouse (concussion) have been cleared for the game.

Behren Morton and Dametrious Crownover are both healthy scratches.

The Lions ruled out two players before Sunday's game, including starting cornerback D.J. Reed. Wide receiver Amon-Ra St. Brown, who was questionable with a rib injury, is good to go.

Kickoff between the Patriots and Lions at Munich's Allianz Arena is scheduled for 9:30 a.m. ET.
