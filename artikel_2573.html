How to watch LSU football vs Arkansas, if you're feeling brave | TV info

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

This has been a November to forget for first-year LSU football coach Lane Kiffin.

The Tigers (6-5, 3-5 SEC) have lost three straight games to top 10 opponents in Alabama, Texas and, most recently, Tennessee on Saturday, Nov. 21.

Can LSU find its way in the final month of the 2026 regular season?

Arkansas (4-7, 2-6) presents LSU its best shot at getting a win before the close of the regular season in the Battle for the Golden Boot on Saturday, Nov. 28 inside Razorback Stadium.

LSU has won four straight games over the Razorbacks, who have a first-year head coach in Ryan Silverfield. The last time Arkansas played a home game, it upset then-No. 21 South Carolina two weekends ago, 34-6.

The Razorbacks have exceeded expectations, at least outside of Fayetteville, this season by winning a pair of conference games.

Kiffin is 4-2 all-time against the Hogs as a head coach. When he served as Nick Saban's offensive coordinator at Alabama from 2014-16, the Tide went 3-0 against Arkansas.

Here's what you need to know about LSU vs Arkansas.

LSU kicks off against Arkansas at 11:45 a.m. CST, Saturday, Nov. 28 inside Razorback Stadium in Fayetteville, Arkansas.

This game may very well be a toss up. And if you're an LSU fan, that's got to be a tough pill to swallow with how the back half of this football season has gone. The Tigers are clearly the more talented team, but things have not gone right for this team lately. Despite the downturn, it's difficult to see LSU losing to Arkansas, even on the road where Lane Kiffin's team has struggled this season. LSU 31, Arkansas 18
