Kenneth Walker injury update confirms Chiefs RB’s status for Week 12 vs. Bills

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Kansas City Chiefs have reportedly made a decision on running back Kenneth Walker ahead of the Week 12 game against the Buffalo Bills.

Walker, who has missed the last two games with a groin injury, is not going to play against the Bills on Thursday night, NFL insider Jordan Schultz reported.

"Breaking: Chiefs star RB Kenneth Walker will be OUT for Thursday's showdown vs the Bills, per sources," Schultz reported.

The news on Walker comes one day after he returned to practice for the first time since his injury, which we assumed gave him a chance to suit up.

This will be Walker's third missed game in a row after he didn't miss any contests throughout the season before his injury.

In his stead, the Chiefs will once again deploy rookie Emmett Johnson, who has started in each of the last two games in Walker's place.
