Former Kyler Murray backup reveals why he didn’t live up to Vikings’ expectations

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Minnesota Vikings' experiment with Kyler Murray has not produced the resurgence former teammate Brett Hundley expected, and the former quarterback believes Kevin O'Connell's offense has played a role.

Hundley, who backed up Murray with the Arizona Cardinals during his rookie season in 2019, recently told Action Network's Kyle Odegard that Minnesota's timing-based offense has not been a natural fit for Murray's skill set.

"That offense is built around timing. If you threw Brock Purdy in there, he'd be a great fit for that offense. That's the type of quarterback. I think Kyler is a great quarterback, but it's just not a true fit. When he was drafted in Arizona under Kliff Kingsbury, the offense was built around him, and that's where Kyler had his best years. We haven't seen that (in Minnesota)," Hundley said.

Murray has started six games for Minnesota this season, completing 100 of 154 passes for 1,022 yards, five touchdowns and three interceptions. He owns an 86.6 passer rating and has added 167 rushing yards on 29 attempts. Minnesota went 5-1 in his six starts despite modest passing production.

Hundley believes Murray also entered a much different situation than the one he had as the No. 1 overall pick in Arizona.

"When you come in as the No. 1 pick, they will build the offense around you. Now you don't get that luxury. You come in and they say, 'We're running this. Do with it what you can. And if you can't succeed in this and lead us to where we want to go, we're going to find somebody else.' It's not that same pull when you come in as the No. 1 pick. So it's an interesting spot to be in."

Murray has also dealt with multiple injuries this season. He suffered a concussion in Minnesota's season opener before sustaining a hamstring strain following the Vikings' Week 8 loss to the Detroit Lions. O'Connell subsequently described Murray as "week to week," and the quarterback has not played since.

"I personally thought he was going to do a lot better when you look at the Vikings' past QB history of coming in and revamping their career. I said before the season I thought that would happen with Kyler, but I haven't seen what we're used to seeing with him," Hundley said.

Minnesota has since reunited with Kirk Cousins, acquiring him from the Las Vegas Raiders for a 2027 third-round pick. Cousins led the Vikings to a 30-10 victory over the Green Bay Packers in his first start back with the organization.

Cousins will make his second consecutive start when the Vikings (7-2) face the San Francisco 49ers (7-1) on Sunday night in Mexico City.
