Hollow Knight Developer Team Cherry To Air Special Presentation Featuring Silksong DLC News And More

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Team Cherry, the makers of the Hollow Knight series, have announced a special livestream called Cherry Time. The broadcast will air on Friday, November 27, and will feature a Hollow Knight: Silksong update and other news.

The livestream begins at 7 a.m. PT/10 a.m. ET, and the video description only confirms Team Cherry will share an update on Hollow Knight: Silksong’s Sea of Sorrow. Announced almost a year ago, Sea of Sorrow is an upcoming free story expansion, but we know little beyond Team Cherry’s cryptic tease: “Voyage across and beneath the salt-stricken seas in this thrilling expansion, free for all players.” Cherry Time may well give us the first substantial look at Sea of Sorrow, including a possible release window.

As for the other announcements, we’ll have to watch Cherry Time to find out. What else do you think Team Cherry has up its sleeve? Let us know in the comments. Be sure to read our review of Hollow Knight: Silksong, too.
