How To Unlock First-Person Aiming In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

First-person mode sadly isn't in GTA 6 proper, but there are some workarounds both in and out of combat.

One of the big gameplay questions surrounding Grand Theft Auto 6 was whether or not it would include a first-person mode like Red Dead Redemption 2 launched with and GTA 5 eventually got.

Sadly, although also understandably, that isn't the case, as GTA 6 doesn't have a full first-person mode to let players see through Jason and Lucia's eyes. There is a way to add it to your guns, though, as well as a similar function.

As soon as you've started Grand Theft Auto 6 and got access to free-roaming, you'll want to head over to any Ammu-Nation on the map, which is marked with a handgun sign. There's one very close to where Jason lives in the Keys, and you'll actually have to visit it as part of an early story mission anyway, so it's impossible to avoid visiting one if you want to progress.

Once you get there, buy any gun that you want, and then select to add an Optics attachment. There are options for close, mid, and long-range optics that will let you zoom in further, but the most basic option costs just $65, so it's something you can get from the very start of the game.

Optics are available for pretty much every type of gun in the game, so don't worry about it being limited to a plain rifle. That being said, you'll have to wait a bit until you can buy some of the advanced guns and really get the customisation going.

If you purchase an Optics attachment for a gun, you can then press R3 while aiming it to enter first-person aiming. Sure, it's not quite what everyone wanted with a first-person mode in GTA 6, but it's the closest thing we've got to it, and it does let you see combat in a more detailed way if that's your preference.

Now, while there isn't a full first-person mode in GTA 6, there is a way to more closely inspect things from Jason and Lucia's perspective that doesn't require you to get your gun out and potentially scare people. If you've got nothing equipped, hold down L2 on PS5 and LT on Xbox, and then press in the right stick to "Inspect" the world.

Doing this lets you go into first-person aiming while out of combat, although you can't move around and there's not all that much point to it beyond being able to scan things easily. It does let you see things a bit closer, and can be pretty immersive if you're talking to someone or trying to watch something that's going on in Vice City. Again, it's not exactly first-person mode, but it's better than nothing!
