Cowboys WR CeeDee Lamb fined for hit on 49ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

CeeDee Lamb is definitely known for catching the ball. The Dallas Cowboys pay him to do that, and he does it well. Occasionally, though, he is asked to block, whether on a run play, or for a fellow receiver. He did exactly that in the San Francisco 49ers game, and is now going to be a little lighter in the wallet.

The play in question was a George Pickens' touchdown. Lamb was initially flagged on the play, but the referees picked it up after the play and no infraction was enforced. Lamb must have thought he avoided any punishment, but the NFL came for him anyway.

$30,000 is a pretty big fine, probably for the helmet-to-helmet aspect of it. Lamb also expressed his dissatisfaction with wide receivers having to take hits, but they can't dish them out.

Fortunately no one got hurt on the play and Lamb will be out there catching passes for the Cowboys this week against the Tennessee Titans.
