Phoenix Suns vs Utah Jazz: Recap and Final Score

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Utah Jazz are back to their losing ways, falling short against the Phoenix Suns with a final score of 111-101.

The final score depicts a game that was more competitive than reality. Utah's successful trip to Los Angeles was a bit of fools gold; once again, the Jazz were slow in their offense, failed to bring intensity on defense, and had lapses of focus that are unacceptable for a team that can no longer use its youth to justify its mistakes.

The Jazz missed 13 free throws. They turned over the ball 14 times. They spent multiple possessions walking up the court, entering their offense late, dribbling the air out of the ball, and taking a shot without passing the ball. Look no further than Keyonte George for a primary offender: his four turnovers, ill-advised shots, and lackadaisical effort led him to 14 points on 4-17 shooting. The Jazz's shooting struggles from three returned, finishing the night 10-29 from three as a team. The combination of George/Peterson/Bailey ended the night an astonishing 0-21 from three.

Darryn Peterson led the Jazz with 20 points, 10 rebounds, and eight assists. Peterson and Josh Green were the only Jazz players to finish with a positive plus/minus, sharing a plus-4 each. Importantly, Peterson appeared to tweak his leg after landing on a drive to the basket, although he appeared to finish the game without issue.

For the Suns, this wasn't the prettiest win. Phoenix shot only 45% from the field and 29% from three as a team, led by Devin Booker's 23 points on 9-22 shooting. That said, they hustled and played with an intensity that was lacking from Utah all night.

The Jazz return to action on Sunday, November 22nd to host the Dallas Mavericks.
