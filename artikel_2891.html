The Indiana Pacers had high hopes entering the 2026-27 regular season. After trading for Ivica Zubac, the Pacers were hoping the return of Tyrese Haliburton would immediately catapult them back into championship contention in what was considered an open Eastern Conference.

But Haliburton suffered a hamstring strain in his left leg just four games into the regular season and has been out since October 26th. Over the last couple of weeks, Haliburton was reportedly began ramping up his workload as he tries to return, but head coach Rick Carlisle recently cautioned that team will be extremely careful with his return to play process and that he's still a little ways away.

On Friday, ESPN reiterated reports from local Pacers reporters over the last couple of weeks that the Pacers want to avoid any re-injury to the already tricky hamstring of Haliburton's.
