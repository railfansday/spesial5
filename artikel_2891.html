JPMorgan's top books and experiences for the wealthy this holiday season

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

J.P. Morgan released its list of recommended books and experiences for clients, including legendary investor David Booth's latest book, a longevity clinic in Thailand and a dance-floor adaptation of "Much Ado About Nothing" on Broadway.

The "NextList 2026" begins with submissions from J.P. Morgan client advisors, which are narrowed down by a review committee. The list also reflects findings from the bank's global family office survey, which tracks the concerns and interests of ultra-high-net-worth clients, said Darin Oduyoye, JPMorgan Asset & Wealth Management's chief communications officer.

"Geopolitics was a top concern. And as AI continues to be a prominent conversation, clients are looking for perspective on the forces shaping markets," Oduyoye told CNBC. "Specifically, David Booth's story, from selling shoes as a teenager in Kansas to launching the first index fund, offers time-tested wisdom on investing. It's a reminder to look beyond short-term market swings and stay focused on your long-term goals."

With familiar names like Paddington Bear and a commemorative book to celebrate the 125th anniversary of Bergdorf Goodman among this year's picks, the list has a strong note of nostalgia. On the inclusion of books by David Rubenstein, Angela Duckworth and Danny Meyer, Oduyoye said, "Their ideas on leadership continue to resonate in a rapidly changing world, reflecting a desire for trusted voices and enduring wisdom amid the noise."

At the same time, clients are looking to discover new experiences, especially overseas, he said.

Here is the full J.P. Morgan NextList 2026, including the bank's descriptions of recommended items. The entries have been lightly edited for style.

The Swiss longevity clinic Clinique La Prairie has brought its signature approach to Phuket with a retreat at Tri Vananda. The program pairs medical science with personalized nutrition, movement and well-being, and guests leave with a plan to support long-term health.

Fondazione Dries Van Noten, Venice, Italy

Created by designer Dries Van Noten and his partner, Patrick Vangheluwe, the foundation explores craftsmanship from fashion to ceramics through exhibitions, residencies and talks. Its opening presentation, The Only True Protest Is Beauty, explores beauty and creative heritage.

International Dark Sky Discovery Center, Fountain Hills, Arizona

Opening in early 2027, the center will offer an immersive experience of the night sky with a planetarium, observatory and interactive exhibits. Lectures and public viewing nights will invite visitors to experience the truly dark skies.

Developed by tequila brand Clase Azul México, the 50-acre estate combines tequila production with artisan workshops dedicated to the brand's signature handmade decanters. Visitors can explore spaces celebrating regional heritage and dine at OYA, which focuses on traditional Mexican cuisine.

Suzhou Museum of Contemporary Art, Suzhou, China

The new 59,500-square-meter museum sits beside Jinji Lake's "Suzhou's Eye" Ferris wheel. The large-scale public museum allows visitors to wander through a garden of pavilions and courtyards connected by art-filled galleries and porticoes.

Opening in June 2027 on Norway's northern coast, the museum will explore whale biology, evolution and ecology through exhibitions that blend science and art. The multisensory experience aims to deepen visitors' understanding of the scale, intelligence and mystery of the world's largest mammals.

"DOLLY: A True Original Musical," St. James Theatre

The new musical follows Dolly Parton's journey from the Smoky Mountains to global superstardom, featuring hits including "Jolene" and "9 to 5," as well as new songs by Parton. Directed by Bartlett Sher, it celebrates the ambition and reinvention behind her career. Previews begin December 7.

"Much Ado About Nothing," Winter Garden Theatre

Tom Hiddleston ("Loki," "The Night Manager") and Hayley Atwell ("Agent Carter") star as Benedick and Beatrice in Shakespeare's battle of wits. Directed by Tony Award winner Jamie Lloyd, the limited engagement closes Jan. 10, 2027.

"Paddington The Musical," Al Hirschfeld Theatre

The seven-time Olivier Award-winning London hit brings the beloved bear to Broadway. The imaginative musical, which celebrates kindness, belonging and adventure, begins previews on March 30, 2027.

Inspired by Prince's Grammy-winning album and Oscar-winning film, the new musical is set in 1984 Minneapolis and features more than 20 of his songs. Tony nominee Saheem Ali directs Jordan Fisher as the magnetic new rock musician in the production, which begins previews on April 12, 2027.

Aperture Cellars, Sonoma County, California

The cellar was founded by acclaimed winemaker Jesse Katz, who has created more than 60 perfect 100-point wines. Aperture brings together wine, art and nature, drawing on inspiration from his father, photographer Andy Katz.

Founded by Tor Kenward, the family-owned winery recently celebrated its 25th anniversary. TOR has earned more than 30 perfect 100-point scores and focuses on single-vineyard Cabernet Sauvignon and Chardonnay made in limited quantities.
