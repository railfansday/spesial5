Tragic death at Cowboys 49ers game prompts video tribute from cousin

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Mario Narro, a 36-year-old Dallas Cowboys fan, died earlier this week after an alleged altercation at AT&T Stadium when the Cowboys hosted the San Francisco 49ers.

Several members of Narro's family have spoken to the California Post about the tragic death. His niece, Claudia DeLara, claimed that Mario had gotten into a scuffle and was pushed down a flight of stairs by a 49ers fan.

Mario Narro posing for a photo while wearing a Dallas Cowboys jersey.

Meanwhile, Narro's cousin, Marty, posted a heartfelt tribute on Facebook Thursday to commemorate his family member.

Unfortunately we got the news that my cousin Mario TheThird had a sudden incident take place while at the Cowboys game and in result of that was his early death.

We don't have any details atm, my family is still in Texas getting things figured out, but I just ask you to pray for us in these tragic times," the post was captioned.

Mario Narro in a Dallas Cowboys jersey. (Facebook)

"This is the closest thing I had when it comes to pictures together. My cousin was always such a great guy and I'm gonna miss him so much! Rest in Peace to you primo

A video was attached that looked to be several people, including Marty and Mario, celebrating something.

Mario Narro celebrating to the camera. (Facebook/@Marty Narro).

The Tarrant County Medical Examiner is conducting further testing to determine the official cause and manner of Narro's death.

Regardless, this is a heartbreaking situation for not just the Narro family but the entire Cowboys community.

Neither the 49ers nor the NFL responded for comment upon being reached by The Post. The Cowboys organization referred the California Post to the Arlington Police Department, which stated that a death investigation is underway.
