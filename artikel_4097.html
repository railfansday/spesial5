Dedicated PS5, PS5 Pro Patch Aims to Fix Silent Hill 2 Graphics Issues

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Could the excellent Silent Hill 2 remake finally be fixed on base PS5 and PS5 Pro? That's what a new patch for the game seems to be suggesting.

A new update for the survival horror remake, dedicated entirely to PS5 and PS5 Pro, is live now, bringing it up to version 1.08. The patch notes speak of addressing all the graphical issues that have quietly plagued the game for months now.

Having installed the new update, you should discover "numerous fixes" relating to the "lighting, shadows, and reflections [issues] that were occurring in multiple locations". Both of the Performance and Quality modes have been patched with consistency improvements, and the flickering in water surfaces, lighting effects, and reflections has been "corrected".

While these were not major problems, the patch also improves the cutscenes in certain places and fixes a glitch where you wouldn't be able to obtain some items.

The issues surrounding Silent Hill 2 — the PS5 Pro version in particular — date all the way back to a month after the original release. At the time, Bloober Team was aware of the problems and a patch was being worked on. The update didn't work, and eventually, the responsibility shifted to publisher Konami. More updates followed, and now today's new patch 1.08 directly targets PS5 and PS5 Pro fixes.
