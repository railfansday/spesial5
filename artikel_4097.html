Skyrim's Successor Finally Re-Revealed as The Elder Scrolls 6: Sentinel

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

It's official: The Elder Scrolls 6: Sentinel is the full title of Bethesda's next open world RPG.

The subtitle has been announced as part of the developer's 40th anniversary celebrations. Confirmation comes via a very brief trailer at the end of the company's new video, which you can watch below.

The teaser shows sand blowing across an Elder Scroll, and it concludes with an ominous quote from Yazar, a Moth Priest (remember them from Oblivion?) in service of High Queen Iradne of Hammerfell.

It reads: "The scroll offered only a shimmer, a veil thinly lifted to my eyes. A future lost. A victory undone. Sands of time scattered by fate."

Obviously not, but the teaser and subtitle reveal nail down at least one thing: this game's going to take place in Sentinel, which is both a capital city and kingdom within Hammerfell — the home of the Redguards.

You can expect deserts, ancient ruins, and an adventure-filled coastline.

When we'll actually get to see The Elder Scrolls 6: Sentinel is anyone's guess at this point. Bethesda has just warned that "it’ll be some time before we’re ready to share more". The wait continues...
