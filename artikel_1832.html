Liverpool handed unexpected boost ahead of Manchester United clash after lineup decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Liverpool have received a surprise boost ahead of their Premier League showdown against Manchester United, with star striker Alexander Isak handed a rest while on international duty.

Sweden boss Graham Potter pledged to name a strong team against Poland for the Nations League B dead rubber - the Swedes already confirmed as group winners ahead of kick-off.

But despite that promise, Isak remained on the bench for the full 90 minutes on Tuesday night as Sweden secured a 1-1 draw in Poland to conclude a successful Nations League campaign.

Sweden will move up to the top tier of the Nations League for the 2028-29 edition of the competition, allowing Isak to mix with Europe’s elite on the international stage.

Isak missed three games for Sweden during the last international break after picking up a thigh issue, which then prevented the 27-year-old from featuring for Liverpool against Manchester City and LASK.

But rather than returning from international duty with an injury, this time Isak will come back to Merseyside fully rested for Sunday’s clash with Man United.

So far this season, Isak has made 14 appearances for Liverpool across all competitions, grabbing eight goals and one assist.

After an injury-frustrated debut season at Anfield - Isak suffering a fibula fracture in December - following his £130million move from Newcastle, the Swede has become a central figure to Andoni Iraola’s plans.

Aside from the two games he missed due to a thigh issue, Isak has played minutes in every fixture he has been available for.

Isak has only faced Man Utd once as a Liverpool player, taken off after 72 minutes at Anfield during a 2-1 defeat in October 2025 before missing the return fixture at Old Trafford due to an adductor issue.
