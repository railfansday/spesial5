Scholes: Carrick not ruthless enough for Man Utd

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Paul Scholes claimed Michael Carrick was not "ruthless" enough over the summer, which he believes played a part in his Manchester United sacking.

Carrick was dismissed by United on Thursday, following reports that he may be replaced during the international break, with Mauricio Pochettino expected to replace him in the Old Trafford dugout.

While Carrick impressed as interim boss in the second half of last season, leading the club to a third-place finish in the Premier League, he struggled to make the same impact as their permanent boss.

Carrick won 12 of his 17 games at the helm in 2025-26 (70%, D3 L2). The Red Devils also averaged 1.94 goals per game in that span, while conceding 1.05.

But this term, United have won three of 15 games in all competitions (20%, D5 L5), averaging 1.6 goals for and exactly 2.0 goals against per match.

Their dismal start has left them sitting in 18th place in the Premier League, following just one win in their first 10 league games of 2026-27 (D5 L4).

And former United midfielder Scholes believes Carrick should have made more wide-reaching changes to the squad over the summer, with that hindering their prospects.

"Winning one league game in 10 league games is just not going to cut it," Scholes said on 'The Good, The Bad & The Football' podcast.

"He did so well when he first came in. His audition, if you like, was very good.

"But I do think he missed a bit of a trick in the summer; he could have been more ruthless with players that we know, and a lot of people said, just weren't good enough if Man Utd were going to get to the next level.

"They did finish well last year, but Man Utd should be thinking about winning trophies."

United are reportedly finalising a deal with United States boss Pochettino to be their next head coach, having said they "intend to appoint a permanent successor as quickly as possible."

Pochettino is a well-known face in the Premier League, having managed Southampton, Tottenham and Chelsea in the competition.

The Argentinian (150) has recorded the 12th-most wins as a manager in the competition and is just six games off his 300th Premier League match. He would become the 21st manager to hit that milestone.

Pochettino had been linked with the United job in the past and has spoken about his wish to return to the Premier League since becoming an international manager.

While Scholes is a fan of Pochettino, he is worried his appointment will come several years too late.

"Mauricio Pochettino is a great manager," Scholes added. "He's got the presence to manage a big football club like Man Utd.

"He's got experience of working with young players, which is what Man Utd want.

"My only slight concern is that they have come a little bit too late to the party. He's got a bit of a reputation of being a bridesmaid, of not winning trophies.

"That possibly put Man Utd off a little bit in the past. They are in a totally different position now, though. They are possibly not even thinking about winning things."

United's first game back after the international break is away to Liverpool, before a trip to Sporting CP in the Champions League.
