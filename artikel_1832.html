Scaloni opens door for Messi to join Argentina staff after retirement

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Lionel Scaloni says there is a place on his Argentina coaching staff for Lionel Messi if he wants it, though he expects the former captain to be playing for a long time.

Messi retired from international football following the World Cup, having helped Argentina reach a second consecutive final, though they lost to Spain.

In total, the 39-year-old made a record 208 appearances, helping Argentina to back-to-back Copa America titles on either side of their 2022 World Cup triumph under Scaloni.

He also scored 126 goals for his country, including one in his farewell match – a 3-0 friendly win over Benin in the last international break.

Now firmly in the post-Messi era with Argentina, Scaloni, who signed a new deal through to the 2030 World Cup, was asked about a potential return for their former talisman.

"I think he's going to keep playing… He's going to keep playing beyond 2030," Scaloni said in a press conference before a friendly against Costa Rica.

"He already said no to the national team, but I hope he continues as a player as long as he can. And if he wants to come here, no problem at all.

"Now that Walter [Samuel] is no longer here, let him come."
