Too early for Ronald Araujo future decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Following an extremely difficult period in his career, both on and off the pitch, Ronald Araujo left Barcelona to join Liverpool on loan this summer. Though primarily a centre-back, he has established himself as a regular starter at right-back, firmly ahead of the struggling Jeremie Frimpong and in the absence of the injured Conor Bradley, putting in excellent performances week-in, week-out.

The Uruguay international started eight of the 10 Premier League matches Liverpool have played so far, three out of four in the Champions League, and both EFL Cup games. The only game he didn’t play was the 3-2 defeat away to Fenerbahce in the Champions League. He has three goal contributions (two goals, one assist) to his name, all in the Champions League.

Liverpool have the option to turn the loan into a permanent transfer at the end of the season for €55 million, abut while that option seems like a no-brainer for the Merseysiders at this point, the 28-year-old Uruguay international says it’s too early for such a decision to be made.

“I think it’s still very early to talk about that,” Araujo told The Athletic.

“I try to take each day as it comes, enjoy myself and do what I like doing best. There’s still a long way to go and it doesn’t depend on just one person; it depends on a lot of people. So we have to wait and, when the time comes, make whatever decision needs to be made.”

Araujo is under contract with Barcelona until 2031, but at the moment, there doesn’t seem to be a place for him there.

Deco, Barcelona’s sporting director, recently spoke on the subject.

“The most important thing for us is to see a Ronald who has recovered and is happy,” he said.

“If he later decides to stay there, Liverpool has a buyout option and they know what they need to do. They’ll also have to negotiate with him, not just with us. Of course the money wouldn’t hurt us.”
