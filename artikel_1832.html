Eli Junior Kroupi stance on Arsenal transfer revealed as Bournemouth make decision for January window

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Bournemouth forward Eli Junior Kroupi is targeting a move outside of the Premier League despite interest from Arsenal, Chelsea, Manchester City and Tottenham, according to reports.

Kroupi joined Bournemouth in a £12 million deal from French side Lorient last year and scored 13 goals in his first season in the Premier League.

The 20-year-old has suffered a disrupted start to the new campaign as he required surgery on a foot injury sustained during a pre-season training session in Austria in July.

Kroupi returned in Bournemouth’s 3-0 win away to Manchester United on October 25 and has made three more appearances as a substitute for Marco Rose’s side.

During the international break, Kroupi was handed his senior debut for France as he featured in the 3-1 defeat to Italy, as well as in the 2-1 win over Turkey.

The French striker has attracted interest from Arsenal, Chelsea and Tottenham, while Paris Saint-Germain, Bayern Munich and Barcelona have also been monitoring his progress in the Premier League.

However, David Ornstein has claimed that Kroupi will look to move overseas when he leaves Bournemouth, although the club are not looking to sell the forward in the January transfer window.

‘There was a big story recently about Alex Scott possibly leaving in the January transfer window, that was conditional on Bournemouth receiving £100 million and being in a really strong position in the Premier League where that becomes a bit of a no-brainer because in six months’ time he’ll have a year to go on his contract and it’s a very different conversation,’ Ornstein said on The Athletic FC’s podcast.

‘Kroupi has got much longer on his contract for a start, so there’s no massive need to sell in a way you would consider selling Scott.

‘He’s been out injured for longer as well, so I think Bournemouth want to make use of him this season and they don’t think their chances of recouping significant value in the summer will be much worse than in January, probably better actually.

‘My information around Kroupi is he would like to move at some point, outside of England.

‘You’ve seen very heavy reporting around PSG and Barcelona. If he continues to show the level of form that he was displaying prior to his injury, the biggest clubs in world football want him.

‘It’s no surprise he’s high up on Premier League club recruitment lists, but if what I’m hearing to date is anything to go by, those Premier League clubs may be disappointed because he could be travelling back to warmer climes.’
