Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Team news and fitness latest ahead of Rangers’ match with Aberdeen in Premiership

Rangers can cut the gap at the top of the Premiership to one point if they defeat Aberdeen at Ibrox on Tuesday.

This is their game in hand over champions and leaders Celtic, but they face an Aberdeen side who will take confidence from winning 4-2 at Kilmarnock at the weekend.

That was the Dons’ first game under new head coach Mario Frick - and it will be a much tougher assignment in Govan.
