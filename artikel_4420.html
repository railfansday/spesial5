When the Work Gets Heavy, Do Your Tires Hold Up? Cooper’s New Discoverer<sup>®</sup> HT Is Built to Carry the Weight

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Evolved from severe-duty all-terrain designs and backed by an up to 70,000-mile Limited Treadwear Warranty, this highway tire pairs interlocking 3D tread sipes with rigid construction to keep your truck or SUV composed on high-mileage trips.

For enthusiasts, tire shopping is an upgrade, not a chore. While the casual driver might treat new tires like regular maintenance, anyone who cares about vehicle dynamics knows that what meets the road defines how a truck or SUV rides, tracks, and stops. It is the single most transformative upgrade you can make to your vehicle. For more than 110 years, Cooper tires have catered to drivers who look past the showroom floor and seek out products that overdeliver on performance, capability, and long-term durability.

And that level of purpose-built engineering is more vital than ever. As vehicles grow heavier and more capable, tires need to do more than simply roll down an interstate. They have to withstand heavy payload demands, sudden weather changes, and unpredictable pavement. Cooper tires are built to that benchmark. Rooted in American engineering and real-world development, Cooper tires are built to an uncompromising standard that live up to their promises. From the rock-crawling STT Pro to the severe-snow-designated Stronghold AT, the Discoverer® family has earned its reputation through real-world testing from Alaskan ice to jagged mountain trails.

The new Cooper Discoverer® HT adapts that rugged all-terrain lineage for long-distance highway cruising, matching heavy-duty capability with quiet, refined composure. While it retains the structural durability to wander down a gravel trail with a trailer in tow, its core mission is maximizing comfort and control across hundreds of miles of open road.

Where many highway tires trade load capability for a soft ride, the Discoverer® HT is engineered to handle both. Straight, reinforced sidewalls provide the structural foundation needed to maintain an optimal contact patch under load. Whether the vehicle is running light or carrying family, gear, and tongue weight, the tire resists deformation, ensuring consistent steering response and predictable roadholding grip in any condition. It’s engineered for drivers whose tires have to hold their own—providing a dependable, hard-working partner that won’t fold when the truck or SUV is pushed to its limit.

Cooper tires also tackle truck and SUV tire performance, with advanced features. Its proprietary 3-D Micro-Gauge Siping incorporates interlocking zig-zag sipes inside the tread blocks. Under heavy braking, sharp turns, or loaded acceleration, these interlocking sipes lock together to stiffen the tread block, to help support responsiveness. Crucially, these sipes run deep into the tread rubber, ensuring that gripping edges are still present to help with traction. That structural integrity backs up an exceptional UTQG treadwear rating of 720 and a 70,000-mile limited treadwear warranty on passenger fitments.

This is the tire for drivers who expect their trucks or suvs to show up for the long haul. Whether that means towing a camper into the backcountry, handling moving-day payloads, or eating up cross-country interstate miles in quiet comfort, the mission remains the same: a single set of tires that balances real-world traction, refined manners, and hard-working capability.

To fit that wide spectrum of work, Cooper offers the Discoverer® HT across 19 passenger sizes covering 16- to 22-inch wheels (with section widths from 245 to 285 mm), alongside 16 light-truck (LT) options engineered with deeper tread grooves and higher load ratings. Backed by a 45-day satisfaction guarantee and over a century of research and development, the Discoverer® HT delivers what Cooper has always stood for: the quiet confidence that the job was done right.
