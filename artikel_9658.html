Fintech names itself after folklore, sea monsters and shapes. The hottest fight is Robinhood. The legendary outlaw keeps 84% of the combined Wikipedia attention against 16% for Robinhood Markets. Stripe took 98% of the pattern. Bulls and bears still mostly mean animals.

Below are the top 24 Money & Fintech terms by popularity, out of 40 on deduplication.tech. Of these 24, the duplicate owns the word in 11, 4 are contested, and the original still holds in 9.

How to read this list: each word has two meanings with their own Wikipedia pages. Popularity is the combined English Wikipedia pageviews for both meanings over the last 12 months. The Deduplication Rate is the duplicate (newer) meaning's share, listed first, against the original's share. 60% or more and the duplicate owns the word; 40–60% and it's contested; under 40% and the original still holds. Data comes from the Wikimedia Pageviews API.
