Meta&#8217;s Holiday Ad Pitches Its Smart Glasses as the Secret to Present Parenting

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Meta has found a new use for its controversial smart glasses: helping parents capture their kids’ school holiday plays without looking through a phone screen.

After returning to the holiday ad cycle last year with a campaign spotlighting Messenger, the Facebook-owned brand is shifting its focus to Meta’s smart glasses this season with “Stay in the Moment,” a campaign developed by Mother LA.

The campaign will run across social and out-of-home, showing how products from across Meta’s portfolio, including Meta Glasses, Ray-Ban Meta, and Ray-Ban Meta Audio, can help people be more present during the holidays and beyond.
