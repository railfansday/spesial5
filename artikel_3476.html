GTA 6 Players On PS5 Averaged 17+ Hours During First Weekend

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here, and it's breaking records everywhere, including at PlayStation.

The gaming giant has announced that GTA 6 is the "biggest launch in PlayStation history, a record that goes back 30+ years and five console generations. According to the gaming giant, more than 39 million unique players on PS5 have jumped into GTA 6 so far.

Here is a quick rundown of GTA 6's new records on PS5 so far (covering the November 19-22 period):

  • Highest number of concurrent players for any free or paid game on PlayStation, ahead of Fortnite, Call of Duty, and everything else.
  • Players collectively logged 683 million hours of GTA 6 at launch, with each player spending an average of 17.4 hours in the game's first few days.
  • GTA 6's launch led to the largest number of newly connected PS5 consoles on a single day since the system came out in 2020, and outside of the Christmas season.
  • GTA 6 was the first game played on 70% of new PS5 consoles, which suggests a lot of people bought a system specifically for GTA 6.

A big, record-breaking start for GTA 6 was always expected. If the game didn't break records, that would be a bigger story. Additionally, GTA 6 is an anomaly--its success will not save the AAA industry from its many serious, deep-rooted issues, experts have said, and many believe other publishers will learn all the wrong lessons.

GTA 6 sold 32.5 million copies via pre-purchases alone and generated close to $4 billion on day one. The game will continue to sell, and it should get another jolt of sales when the PC edition arrives in 2027, as rumored.
