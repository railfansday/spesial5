3 Best New HBO Max Series To Binge-Watch This Weekend (November 13–15, 2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

ScreenRant's streaming guides on Netflix, Prime Video, and Paramount+ cover all the best new shows worth streaming on these major platforms this weekend, as well as last week's recommendations for HBO Max's hits. All of the following HBO Max series have brand new episodes that were either just released or will arrive in the coming days, making them the greatest binge options on HBO Max right now.

City Of God: The Fight Rages On season 2 just premiered on HBO Max last Sunday, November 8, and is currently the sixth most-watched series on the platform at the time of writing. Serving as a sequel to the acclaimed 2002 movie City of God, the HBO Max series takes place 20 years after the events of the film, bringing back several of the original cast members who return to Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, where the local community is divided between drug traffickers, armed militias, and law enforcement agencies.

City Of God: The Fight Rages On earned an 80% Rotten Tomatoes score for its first season but has mostly gone under the radar since its 2024 release. The original film earned a near-perfect 97% Rotten Tomatoes audience score, 4 Oscar nominations, and ranks #26 on IMDb's Top 250 movies of all time list. City Of God: The Fight Rages On season 2 will consist of 6 total episodes which will be released every Sunday until its December 13 finale.

War has been one of the few new streaming releases on HBO Max's Fall 2026 slate and currently ranks #2 on the streamer's worldwide viewership chart. After reaching a new series high on IMDb with last week's episode 6, War only has one episode remaining after today's episode 7 drop, making it the perfect time to start binge-watching this acclaimed new series. War season 1 earned a strong 85% Rotten Tomatoes score and is already renewed for a second season, which will feature new storylines and characters.

War follows a tense and high-profile legal battle between Sienna Miller's Carla Duval, a movie star, and Dominic West's Morgan Henderson, a tech billionaire. The series was created by George Kay (Lupin, Hijack) and features one of the best ensemble casts on any streaming platform at the moment. While the series' RT audience score is just 69%, it remains one of HBO Max's most popular series this season, and there's no better time to dive in than now.

The Gilded Age is the #1 most-watched HBO Max series in the world heading into the weekend and will release its third of eight season 4 episodes this Sunday, November 15. The Gilded Age is truly stealing the spotlight on HBO Max right now and is by far the most acclaimed and popular new series available to stream on the platform, led by Carrie Coon's magnificent performance as an ambitious socialite named Bertha Russell. The Gilded Age season 4 earned a near-perfect 95% Rotten Tomatoes score and is only getting better this season.

HBO’s acclaimed 8-part crime miniseries turns one deadly night into a gripping murder investigation, with unforgettable performances and suspense.

The Gilded Age season 4 features some of its most intense drama yet after Bertha becomes one of the most influential figures among New York's high society at the end of season 3, representing a "new money" class that disrupts the "old money" status quo. The series is full of social clashes, romances, and other business-related conflicts that are well-crafted and feature exceptional performances. The Gilded Age has already been renewed for a fifth season and will continue to be one of HBO Max's top new shows until its finale on December 20.
