GTA 6: Should You Kill Or Spare Kyle In "Buddy"

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Grand Theft Auto 6 players who have progressed to Chapter 2 and decided to help out Lucia's buddy Kyle with his crop-dusting operation are in for a very unique side mission. What starts out as a rather mundane quest literally evolves into something wild, and players will eventually have to make a decision as to whether or not they want to kill Kyle.

Players will have the opportunity to help out Kyle as a pilot from the very beginning of Chapter 2 if they read some of the flyers posted in the lobby. You'll then be able to drive to McKittrick Farm and start the mission, which is one of several "Strangers and Freaks" questlines in the game. Throughout the mission, you'll see that he's starting to exhibit some weird behavior as a result of over-exposure to dangerous chemicals.

Eventually, after picking up some chemicals from the dealers and progressing his questline far enough, you'll start to receive some strange text messages from Kyle and will be tasked with checking in on him. That's when things get really weird.

Once you reach the farm, there will be a major police presence and a ton of biohazard agents around the area. You can approach and speak with the officers to tell them you know Kyle, and a lead agent will come down and ask you to speak with Kyle inside the barn. You'll suit up in hazmat gear and be able to go inside, and that's when you'll see that Kyle has taken on some sort of Hulk-mutant form from the strawberry juice he's been manufacturing.

At this point, you can speak with Kyle and make a series of "choices" that all more or less result in him hulking out and going feral. At this point, you can either choose to fire on him and take him out, or let him eliminate the cops around the area. Should you choose to shoot him and take him out, the police will reward you with $5000 and Kyle will no longer be around. This is a quick reward that might seem appealing if you're low on funds, but you may want to think twice here as Kyle was a pretty good friend.

Sparing Kyle will result in him hulking out and eliminating all the police in the area, tossing them around like ragdolls and smashing their cars and equipment in the process. You'll need to make sure you don't shoot him at all or he will turn on you and try to kill you. However, if you let him kill all the police, you'll be able to approach him when he's done and give him a hug. You'll take some minor damage and then he will jump away like superman.

Now, this is NOT the last you'll see of Kyle, and sparing him will actually have massive benefits in the future. Namely, Kyle will go into certain drug dens and meth lab operations, such as the Farm Fix Meth Lab in Belville, and completely wipe out the enemies there, letting you simply waltz in and pick up the loot without any resistance whatsoever.

Sparing Kyle here is the much better option. While $5000 is a nice chunk of money early in Chapter 2, it's peanuts in the grand scheme of the game and the benefits of not having to take on a massive enemy presence in some meth labs far outweigh the quick cash infusion. These meth labs have valuable loot as well as cash and safes, so you'll benefit much more from Kyle taking out these drug house operations.

At the time of this writing, it isn't clear exactly how much Kyle will continue helping clear out houses in the future, though it's likely his questline ends at some point down the road. More information will be updated here as it becomes available.

Until then, however, I'd highly recommend sparing Kyle and giving your friend a nice hug during Grand Theft Auto 6's "Buddy?" quest.
