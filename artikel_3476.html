Spoilers ahead for Boston Blue season 2, episode 5, "Blood Diamond."Nearly two years after Blue Bloods ended its run, CBS finally confronts the painful truth about it with regard to Boston Blue. For a decade and a half, the Reagan family became a staple of the network's yearly slate, with the Tom Selleck-led series becoming a fan-favorite. Unlike the traditional police procedural, Blue Bloods differentiated itself by making it a family show first and foremost. The project dealt with a group of New York public officials, serving different agencies in the city.

After evading cancellation a couple of times, CBS officially cancelled the series in 2024, with Blue Bloods ending with season 14. The final stretch of the Reagans' run on TV was full of compelling cases and major personal milestones for the clan. It left viewers with the idea that while the series was wrapping, the Reagans' life in New York was going to continue. That was until it was announced that CBS was launching a spinoff with Donny Wahlberg's Danny spearheading Boston Blue. The offshoot has been a success thus far, and both Blue Bloods actors and their characters have been supportive of the expansion project. That said, it has always just been a matter of time before the long-ignored truth about Boston Blue comes to light.

Boston Blue has brought in multiple Reagans throughout the course of its run thus far. That said, its return during the CBS's fall 2026 slate marks the first time that it is featuring Will Estes as Jamie Reagan. The youngest of Frank's kids makes a trip up north to see how his older brother is doing after moving out of New York. Unlike Danny's interactions with Erin, Henry, and even Joe Hill, this latest reunion is tense, as Jamie finally confronts Wahlberg's character about leaving New York, and in effect, him.

Tackling this particularly difficult topic is long-overdue in Boston Blue. Granted that Sean's near-death experience was a justified reason for Danny to follow his son to Beantown, the offshoot arguably glossed over the specifics of his decision to not just stay with Sean temporarily, but uproot his whole life to be up north. For a clan that likes to discuss everything, Jamie reveals in his Boston Blue appearance that Danny barely talked to him about leaving. Blue Bloods was a family show; while the spinoff offered details about their conversations about the move, it didn't offer the whole picture, as it's difficult to imagine that it was an easy transition for the whole Regan clan. It took CBS a while, but thankfully, CBS finally addresses the matter.

It's no secret that Blue Bloods' cancellation was quite controversial. CBS had tried pulling the plug on the series a few times, but the cast and crew rallied to keep the series on the air. Despite the family procedural's popularity during its run, the network's budget cuts motivated them to cancel the series, which was expensive to make, considering that it was filming primarily on location in New York. Still, CBS recognized the popularity of the series and found a way to continue taking advantage of it, which resulted in Boston Blue.

The Raegans are preparing to say goodbye as Blue Bloods heads to its final episodes. Luckily, network TV already has a perfect 100% on Rotten Tomatoes

Considering the core message of Blue Bloods, splitting up the Reagans was the biggest hurdle when justifying the existence of Boston Blue. For over a decade and a half, the family came together every Sunday for family dinner, so seeing Danny leave would have been a major change for the clan. Jamie's debut in Boston Blue offers viewers an idea of how painful that transition was, even as the matter was originally ignored. That said, considering that his move was also for family, it's easier to accept it.

Blue Bloods follows the Reagan family, a multigenerational dynasty dedicated to law enforcement and public service in New York City. As Police Commissioner Frank Reagan and his children serve as a detective, patrol officer, prosecutor, and investigator, each episode balances the demands of policing with the family's personal lives.

Boston Blue follows NYPD officer Danny Reagan as he transitions to a role in the Boston Police Department. Partnered with detective Lena Peters, the eldest daughter of a notable law enforcement family, the series explores their collaboration within the dynamics of Boston's criminal landscape.
