Keanu Reeves Is Officially Ready to Party for New Bill and Ted Spin-Off

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Hannah covers horror, sci-fi, and games at Collider, where she writes news and features, reviews new releases, and interviews the people who make them. She’s helping build out Collider’s gaming coverage, with Pokémon as one of her specialties. If she isn’t writing about a monster, she’s probably watching one on a tape from her VHS collection.

Before Keanu Reeves started taking apart criminal organizations over the death of a puppy, his biggest concern was passing history with his best friend. Bill & Ted’s Excellent Adventure made a potentially disastrous school presentation into an excuse to kidnap historical figures, introduce Napoleon to a water park, and put the future of civilization in the hands of two teenagers who desperately needed tutoring. Somehow, they were the right men for the job.

That friendship carried the pair through the afterlife and a long-awaited third movie, with Reeves and Alex Winter making their relentless enthusiasm difficult to resist. Bill and Ted might misunderstand almost everything happening around them, but they approach each new disaster with the confidence of people who have absolutely no idea how much trouble they are in.

Now, Bill & Ted’s Excellent Adventure: Arcade is bringing the duo back in a new video game for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch, and PC. Developed by Art of Play Interactive, the 16-bit-inspired adventure features the voices of Reeves and Winter and lets players tackle its time-traveling chaos alone or alongside a friend in couch co-op. A crowdfunding campaign is scheduled to launch on BackerKit in February 2027, meaning the game itself does not yet have a release date.

You belong in the only decade that genuinely believed a song could change something, and then kept finding evidence for it. The Sixties is where pop music stopped being entertainment and started being an argument.

You belong in the decade where how it looked became as important as how it sounded, and the people who understood that immediately became enormous. MTV rewrote the job description overnight and you would have read the memo first.

The game returns to the original movie’s history-report predicament, sending Bill S. Preston, Esq. and Ted “Theodore” Logan through 15 side-scrolling levels. Players will encounter Billy the Kid, Socrates, Napoleon, Beethoven, and Genghis Khan, challenging the historical guests in individual minigames to secure their help. Recruiting the people who actually lived through your homework remains an impressively excessive response to the threat of a failing grade.

Brawling accompanies those encounters, with hand-crafted pixel art and an ’80s rock soundtrack setting the tone. Completing story mode unlocks arcade mode, minigames, and additional content. Couch co-op also feels like an appropriate choice for a franchise whose heroes would struggle to make it through an afternoon without checking in with each other. There is something reassuring about knowing a friend can join you when your academic future depends on impressing Genghis Khan.

The developers are also planning digital and limited physical editions, with merchandise and apparel included in the crowdfunding plans. Art of Play’s previous projects include The Phantom, giving the studio experience translating another established property into retro-styled action. Fans can already follow the BackerKit campaign for updates ahead of its launch. After the elaborate journey to get Face the Music made, another trip through the phone booth is welcome, especially one that lets fans bring their own equally unqualified partner.
