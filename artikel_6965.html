Frontline Wildfire Defense: Chief Financial Officer

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Please note that we do not accept unsolicited candidate introductions, referrals, or resumes from external recruiters or staffing agencies.

Location: Remote (must be located in MST/PST time zone)

As part of our hiring process, Frontline Wildfire Defense conducts comprehensive background checks for all new hires. These checks typically cover the past 7–10 years and may include employment and education verification. For roles that require work in the field, driving record verification is also required. Employment offers are contingent upon the successful completion of these background checks.

Frontline Wildfire Defense is the fastest-growing company in the wildfire protection industry, serving as the premier manufacturer and installer of exterior wildfire defense systems for residential and commercial structures. Operating across 12 Western states, our organization is dedicated to safeguarding residential properties, commercial sites, communities, and critical infrastructure from the destructive impact of wildfires.

As wildfires rapidly intensify across the West, they threaten communities and destabilize property insurance markets—creating a vital need and a powerful market opportunity for proven structural defense solutions. Beyond our core technology, Frontline is strategically positioning itself to capture high-growth adjacent opportunities, including strategic insurance partnerships and municipal fire service integration.

Our proprietary defense platform integrates real-time wildfire tracking and data fusion with an advanced hydration system designed to increase moisture levels on and surrounding structures to prevent ignition. The efficacy of this technology is well-documented: during the 2025 Los Angeles fire events, Frontline successfully defended 59 of 61 equipped properties, representing a 96% success rate. This performance has earned notable industry honors, including recognition as one of TIME Magazine’s Best Inventions, the National Association of Homebuilders IBS Best Home Technology of 2025, and recognition in Inc. Magazine’s ranking of the fastest-growing enterprises in the Pacific Region.

Frontline was founded by Harry Statter and is headquartered in Jackson Hole, Wyoming.  The company is backed by Norwest Venture Partners.  Frontline maintains a modern, flexible corporate culture supported by a predominantly distributed workforce. We welcome inquiries from qualified professionals seeking to contribute to a high-growth enterprise dedicated to preserving lives and property.

Frontline is seeking a strategic, operationally minded Chief Financial Officer to lead our finance function and help drive the company’s next phase of growth. Frontline is scaling quickly, and wildfire risk is reshaping the availability and affordability of property insurance across the West.  This creates a large market for effective structure defense, along with strategic opportunities in the insurance sector, recurring-revenue services, and fire department and public-sector partnerships.

As a member of the executive team, you will partner closely with the CEO, COO, leadership team, and Board to turn strategic priorities into clear plans and measurable results. You will bring financial rigor, operational discipline, and a commercial mindset to company-wide decisions, and you will help identify, evaluate, and execute new growth opportunities, including strategic insurance partnerships and financial offerings.

This role reports directly to the CEO and leads the existing finance organization, as well as Frontline’s legal function.

We’re looking for a finance leader who has helped a company through this kind of growth before, and who can build the foundation for our next stage, including a potential IPO and/or other strategic growth opportunities.
