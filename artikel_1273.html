Anguissa targeted by Marseille and Besiktas, but Napoli want €6m for sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Napoli midfielder Andre Frank Zambo Anguissa will not sign a new contract, but the club wants €6m to sell in January, with Olympique Marseille and Besiktas interested.

Today is the 31st birthday for the Cameroon international, who is only under contract at the Stadio Diego Armando Maradona until June 2027.

Negotiations for an extension seem to have stalled, and he is expected to leave at the end of the season as a free agent.

According to Il Mattino newspaper and Tuttomercatoweb, Olympique Marseille have gone directly to the player’s representatives and agreed personal terms for the January transfer window.

However, their approach depends entirely on getting the midfielder for free, something that Napoli have absolutely no intention of accepting.

They are asking for €6m to make an early sale, otherwise will hold on to Anguissa for another six months before losing him for free in July 2027.

Marseille could also face strong competition for Anguissa’s signature from Besiktas, as Turkish media suggest coach Vincenzo Italiano has requested him to reinforce his squad.
