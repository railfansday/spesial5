How much Inter paid for Spence, Tottenham pocket €30.75m plus bonus

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The financial details behind Inter’s deal with Tottenham Hotspur for Djed Spence have been revealed, with the cost reaching €30.75m plus a series of add-ons.

The 26-year-old made the move from Spurs to Inter in mid-August, after an excellent performance for England at the 2026 World Cup.

He was their second choice for the role of right wing-back, after being out-bid by Chelsea for Marco Palestra, and will eventually cost around half that fee.

Calcio e Finanza looked through the Inter accounts and found the precise details of the agreement to buy Spence from Tottenham Hotspur.

The basic fee is worth €30.75m split into three instalments in the summer of 2026, 2027 and 2028 of €10.25m each.

There are also potential add-ons worth up to a total of €5.125m tied to various achievements for the club and the player.

This is €512,500 for each Serie A title won by Inter, as long as Spence played at least 50 per cent of those matches, but can only be used at most twice for a total of €1.025m.

In order for it to count, Spence must’ve played a minimum of 45 minutes per match.

The same fee and system are used for reaching the Champions League semi-final, again playing 50 per cent of games, and at most in two seasons for a maximum of €1.025m.

Simply qualifying for the Champions League is worth €512,500 when playing 50 per cent of Serie A matches, but this bonus can be activated up to three times for a peak of €1.5375m.

Winning the Champions League is a one-off bonus worth €1.025m, again with Spence participating in half those games.

If the player reaches 30 goal contributions (between goals and assists) in all competitive games, there is another bonus going to Tottenham of €512,500.

This is not necessarily in one season, but once the target is reached, it cannot be repeated.

If all these bonuses are activated, Spence will have cost €35,875m.

That is not all, because Tottenham also have a percentage of his future sale fee worth 10.25 per cent of the total, but it also applies in case of a loan with option to buy.

If Spence is released as part of an exchange of players, then Inter must give Spurs 10.25 per cent of the calculated value.
