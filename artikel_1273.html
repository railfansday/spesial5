Real Madrid tie Tchouaméni and Güler to 2032 contracts within 24 hours

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Real Madrid have extended the contracts of Aurélien Tchouaméni and Arda Güler until June 2032, a day apart. With captain Fede Valverde also reportedly agreeing new terms, the club are securing its core midfield for years to come.

Real Madrid have committed two midfield regulars to long-term deals in the space of 24 hours. Güler’s announcement came first, and Aurélien Tchouaméni’s followed.

Both players are part of the core of Mourinho’s midfield. The renewals keep them at the club for six more seasons.

Tchouaméni arrived in 2022 and has made 205 appearances for the club. He has won eight trophies in that time. They include one Champions League, two Club World Cups and two UEFA Super Cups. He has also lifted one LaLiga, one Copa del Rey and one Spanish Super Cup.

His standing goes beyond the club. He has 50 France caps and won the 2021 Nations League. The new contract is worth around €9 million net per season, GOAL reported. The agreement came after interest from Manchester United.

Güler’s record covers a shorter spell. He was 18 when he joined. He has 38 caps for Turkey. In September he was named Breakthrough Player of the 2025-26 Champions League. His new salary is reportedly about €8m a year.

The two profiles differ. Tchouaméni is an established international with a full set of club honours. Güler is a younger player whose contract now runs almost nine years from his arrival. Tying both to the same end date suggests a midfield the club intend to keep together.

The club may not be finished. Captain Fede Valverde has agreed to extend his contract from 2029 to 2031, according to GOAL. The new deal will make him one of the squad’s highest earners. An announcement is expected soon, as Madrid work on further renewals after Güler and Tchouaméni.

If Valverde’s extension is confirmed, none of the three midfielders would have a contract ending before 2031. That would remove contract questions over a key part of the squad for several seasons. The two completed renewals arrived during the build-up to a busy run of home matches.

Tchouaméni has not been fully clear of fitness concerns this month. Earlier in November he left the France squad with a muscle problem. He was back in training with the Madrid squad on the day his renewal was announced.

Real Madrid host Celta at the Santiago Bernabéu on Saturday 21 November at 21:00 in LaLiga matchday 13. PSV then visit for a Champions League game on Tuesday 24 November.
