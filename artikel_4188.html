Rogue Adventure offers classic, no-frills roguelike deckbuilding fun, finally on iOS

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Roguelike deckbuilder, roguelike deckbuilder, roguelike deckbuilder. A genre so storied that, even a few years after being invented, it recently got an entire game riffing on the title alone. But that doesn't mean it's devoid of the fun like all good trends eventually are, especially with Rogue Adventure now available on iOS!

Originally released back in 2019, Rogue Adventure is a roguelike deckbuilder (see?) that has you picking from over 200 different cards as you journey across a fantasy land, doing battle with various monsters and enemies. You'll have 16 classes with different starting decks to choose from, four distinct modes and even weekly tournaments to test your mettle in. All this, and more than seven years of updates and fixes too.

And really, that simplicity is arguably Rogue Adventure's biggest strength. There are no gimmicks or twists, no hint of AI art or anything that plagues mobile gaming today. Instead, it's simple, straightforward deckbuilding where you build a hand of abilities to use against your foes and pick from synergies.

With so many cards to pick from and plenty of time to iron out the kinks with updates and tweaks, if you've been looking for a roguelike deckbuilder to play on mobile, it's well worth seeing what Rogue Adventure has to offer!
