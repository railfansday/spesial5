Hornbeck Offshore Services, Inc. (HOS -2.97%) reported its first financial results following the Sept. 1 completion of its combination with Helix, establishing a diversified platform across marine transportation, subsea services, and well intervention. Management indicated the transaction enables the company to participate across the full offshore project life cycle, from development through decommissioning. The combined entity is pursuing an integrated services strategy, aiming to capture higher margins by bundling vessel, robotics, and engineering capabilities. Management issued full-year 2027 adjusted EBITDA guidance of $500 million to $550 million, supported by a $1.9 billion contracted backlog and the expected delivery of two high-specification newbuild vessels starting in late 2027.
