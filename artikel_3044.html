Stock Market Today, Nov. 17: Stocks Slide on AI Chip and Tech Pressures, Salesforce Plummets 9%

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Major indexes fell again as AI fears and technology outages weigh on confidence, and Nvidia's after-the-bell earnings beat expectations, today, Nov. 17, 2026.

Falling 1.03% to 7,341, the S&P 500 (^GSPC -1.03%) marked its fourth day of consecutive losses, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -0.74%) dropped 0.74% to 24,328 on technology sector weakness, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI -1.64%) tumbled 1.64% to 50,759.

Gold price dipped 0.18% to $3,849.20 as of U.S. market close, while the 10-Year Treasury yield edged down 3 basis points to 5.11% during a session in which energy stocks were the only real gainers, with financial services and healthcare stocks under the most pressure.

Salesforce (CRM -9.45%) plummeted 9.45% as security jitters and further AI replacement fears combined to punish software stocks. UnitedHealth Group (UNH -5.42%) dropped 5.42% on sweeping anti-fraud proposals for Medicare & Medicaid. Crypto stocks, including Coinbase Global (COIN -4.02%) and Robinhood Markets (HOOD -3.98%), tumbled as Bitcoin (BTC +0.35%) continued its losing streak -- the leading crypto is now down over 20% in the past month.

The recent string of technology outages that have hit air travel, banks, and other firms weighed further on stocks today, compounding (artificial intelligence) AI chip concerns and broader negative sentiment. Consumers are stockpiling essentials, which helped Costco Wholesale Corp (COST -0.33%)as Mizuho Securities just upped its price target on the stock.

Post-close earnings from Nvidia (NVDA -0.44%) beat analyst expectations, offering some reassurance on AI infrastructure demand and boosting peers in after-hours trading. Investors are still digesting the impact of new U.S. measures restricting international access to advanced AI chips, not only for Nvidia but for the whole sector.
