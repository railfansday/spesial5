Wall Street Confronts AI Reality Check

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Bloomberg US equities reporter Natalia Kniazhevich tells Bloomberg This Weekend that Anthropic’s record IPO failed to reverse deteriorating market sentiment as investors grapple with Nvidia turmoil, AI spending concerns and selling by systematic funds. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Kniazhevich explains why Goldman Sachs’ Panic Index has reached 9 out of 10 and what heavy positioning, weak liquidity and a wave of potential new stock issuance could mean for markets.
