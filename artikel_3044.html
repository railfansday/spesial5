Billionaire Ron Baron Says SpaceX Will Be Worth $30 Trillion by 2040. At $141 a Share, Here’s the Annual Return That Requires.

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Hitting this market cap milestone will be no small feat, given how the space and AI company is starting off in the trillion-dollar club.

It's safe to say fund manager Ron Baron is a big fan of Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX -1.32%), better known as SpaceX. The billionaire investor first invested in SpaceX nearly ten years ago. Even after its trillion-dollar initial public offering (IPO), Baron remains bullish.

In fact, back in May, when interviewed by CNBC about the IPO, he stated that SpaceX could "become the largest company on the planet," becoming worth as much as $30 trillion over the next "ten to fifteen years." Baron further backed his statement by buying an additional $1 billion in SpaceX shares. Yet while Baron may be extremely confident in SpaceX's long-term potential, achieving this goal will require exceptional growth.

SpaceX currently trades for around $141 per share, giving it a market cap of around $1.92 trillion. That means a move to a $30 trillion market cap means around a 1,460% gain. If SpaceX were to make this move in 10 years, this would equate to a 31.6% annualized return. If it hit it in 14 years, this would equate to a 21.7% annualized return.

Of course, these calculations are based on the assumption that there's no major share dilution during this time frame. If recent reports prove true, and SpaceX and Tesla merger talks are moving along, the combination of these two Elon Musk investments would result in a company with a market cap exceeding $3 trillion. This would result in a much lower annualized return hurdle to hit the $30 trillion mark.

Achieving 20% to 30% annualized returns for a decade or more is no small feat. Some megacap stocks have achieved it in the past, but none of them began these impressive runs from a trillion-dollar starting point.

Baron believes Musk's space exploration and AI innovations "could make the economy grow 10 times in 10 years, not double." Still, given Musk's tendency to overpromise and underdeliver, it may be best to wait for SpaceX to further execute on its Starlink, Starship, and X.ai growth efforts before entering a long-term position.
