ITA Airways Discount Code: 10% Off This Black Friday | Condé Nast Traveler

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

There’s never a bad time to embrace la dolce vita and head to Italy for a dreamy vacation filled with pasta dinners, languorous afternoons sipping wine, and views that belong on a postcard. One of the easiest ways to get there is by zipping over to the beautiful region via the country’s airline carrier, ITA Airways. Whether you’re leaving from the East or West Coast, ITA Airways serves top hubs in the US like NYC, Boston, Atlanta, and San Francisco. Plus, the airline connects travelers with top cities all across the world outside of Italy, from Delhi to Paris to Dakar, Senegal.

Personally, I’m a big fan of long-haul flights—they give you enough time to take a break before or after an intense vacation and let you spend six hours straight watching movies without feeling guilty. And when your final destination is somewhere dreamy like Florence or Trieste, your flight is full of excitement and anticipation. Whether you’re popping between Italian cities, crossing oceans, or traversing continents, ITA Airways has the tools to get you from A to B in style. Read on for the best ways to save on your travels, including ITA Airways promo codes, Black Friday discounts, and offerings to keep more money in your pocket next time you fly ITA Airways.

ITA Airways is participating in Black Friday this year, so if you've been thinking about planning a vacation, let this be your sign. From now through November 30, you can use the ITA Airways Black Friday promo code CYBERDAYS to score 10% off your base fare for one-way or round-trip tickets departing from the US and going to any ITA Airways destination, for travel from December 1, 2026, to March 22, 2027. Note that the code only works for economy class tickets and is not valid for multi-leg itineraries. Visit the promo page to find your next big flight.

ITA Airways frequent flyer and rewards program, Miles & More, opens even more doors for saving big. The airline shares its frequent flyer and rewards program with Lufthansa Group, so you’ll be able to earn across all airlines. Plus, it’s free to join and start saving.

There are three membership tiers: Frequent Traveller, Senator, and HON Circle. To achieve Frequent Traveller status, guests must earn 650 points and 325 qualifying points in a calendar year. While you’ll earn regular points when flying on all travel classes across an array of Star Alliance airlines, you can only earn qualifying points when you fly on the following airlines: Air Dolomiti, Austrian Airlines, Brussels Airlines, Discover Airlines, Eurowings, ITA Airways, Lufthansa, SWISS, Croatia Airlines, LOT Polish Airlines, and Luxair. Benefits include business lounge access, the opportunity to get up to 50% extra miles that can be redeemed for additional rewards, and increased free baggage allowance.

To achieve Senator status, you must earn 2,000 points and 1,000 qualifying points in return for benefits like first-class check-in, priority boarding, and access to Senator/Star Alliance Gold lounges. As for HON Circle? That costs a whopping 6,000 HON points, which can only be earned by flying business or first class on select airline partners. Perks include access to first-class lounges, exclusive chauffeur and transfer services, and free upgrade vouchers. Miles & More members can also earn up to 25% cashback on intercontinental award travel bookings departing from the US, or up to 15% cashback on general intercontinental itineraries.

Those between the ages of 12 and 25 are eligible for ITA Airways youth fares to help cut down on travel costs. Simply head to the airline’s Youth Offers page and pop in your desired arrival and departure dates and locations, and scroll through the plentiful options. Discounts will be applied directly to your booking when made through this page (it will say “youth fare” under the correct price), and you’ll be able to follow through with your booking as usual.

Offers are only eligible for those who will be 12–25 years old on the date of travel, and passengers may need to prove their eligibility upon boarding (so have your ID ready). This discounted fare is only valid for flights to destinations in Europe and is not applicable to unaccompanied minors under 14; 14-year-olds may travel unaccompanied to domestic locations only.

If your ITA Airways discount code isn’t working, don’t fret. There are a couple of things you can do to get your code up and working. The first thing to check is whether you’re applying your promo code to the correct fare class—ITA Airways discount codes are often class-specific, so for example, basic economy fares may not apply. Also be sure to check your discount code to confirm your proposed booking is for an eligible route, not during a blackout date, and hasn’t expired. If all else fails, don’t forget to double-check your code for any spacing errors or mistaken capitalizations.

It's also worth keeping in mind that, like for most other products and services, discount codes do not work on taxes and fees. Your discount may look a bit different when you actually book than during advertising, since the percentage is only applied to the base fare with taxes and fees added on afterward.
