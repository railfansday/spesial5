Harvest Cabbage Salad with Date Dressing

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Recipe by Susan Bronson, Rhinelander, Wisconsin

This harvest cabbage salad with date dressing is fit for all of your fall festivities (and easy lunches).

Our harvest cabbage salad with date dressing was inspired by a viral recipe that happens to be perfect for fall. London-based cook and writer Natalie Chassay created her original recipe as an homage to the celery salad served at The Four Horsemen in Brooklyn, New York.  The true star of the show—in both her recipe and in ours—is the date dressing: a sweet and savory mixture of thinly sliced Medjool dates, shallots, lemon juice, grated Parmesan and extra virgin olive oil. The ensemble of sweet, sour, tangy and savory flavors is the perfect complement to a simple salad, whether it’s made with finely sliced celery like Natalie’s, or finely shredded cabbage like ours.

In our salad, we’ve combined finely shredded green cabbage with red cabbage for an extra pop of color. Shredded carrots add a touch of sweetness, while a topping of crunchy toasted pecans turns the crunch up to 11. Whether shared at a fall potluck, served alongside a warm and cozy autumn dinner, or tossed as a quick and easy lunch, this crunchy cabbage salad is sure to hit the spot.

In a large bowl, stir together the dates, shallots, olive oil and lemon juice. Season the mixture with flaky sea salt to taste.

Editor’s Tip: Allow the dressing to sit for at least 10 minutes before adding it to the salad. Allowing the dressing to rest softens the dates and allows the flavors time to meld together.

In a large bowl, toss together the finely shredded green cabbage, red cabbage and carrots.

Editor’s Tip: To easily core the cabbage, first slice the cabbage head in half. Then make a diagonal, V-shaped cut on each side of the core to separate and remove it.

Pour the salad dressing over the shredded cabbage and carrots and toss to combine them. Top the salad with toasted chopped pecans.

Leftover harvest cabbage salad with date dressing should be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator.

Harvest cabbage salad with date dressing will last up to four days when stored properly in the fridge. However, over time its texture may become a bit soggier and the green cabbage may take on a pink hue from the red cabbage. It will still be quite tasty.

Letting the shredded cabbage and dressing sit together lets the flavors meld, so making the salad a few hours in advance is perfect—but any longer is not ideal. You could also prepare the shredded veggies and dressing up to a day in advance. Store them in separate containers in the refrigerator.

You don’t need to salt the cabbage before making cabbage salad. However, salting the cabbage beforehand can reduce bitter flavors and enhance its crunch factor. To do so, toss the cabbage with salt and allow it to sit for about an hour. Use a clean kitchen towel or paper towel to wring out the excess moisture.

This recipe calls for a half-head each of green cabbage and red cabbage. Alternatives include savoy and Napa cabbage, both of which have a more tender texture.

Crunchy apples like tart Granny Smith or sweet Honeycrisp, diced or julienned, would make a tasty addition to this harvest cabbage salad. Other fruits that would work well with this recipe include diced pear, halved red or green grapes, and dried fruits like cranberries or tart cherries.
