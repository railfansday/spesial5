From Madrid to Mainz: Lennart Karl strikes again as Bayern extend unbeaten run

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Bayern Munich maintained their unbeaten start to the Bundesliga season with a 2-1 victory away to Mainz 05. Goals from Joshua Kimmich and Lennart Karl put the visitors in control, although a late strike from substitute Philipp Tietz ensured a nervy finish.

The Bavarians ultimately secured all three points to head into the international break on a positive note.

Here is our official match report for the game:

Mainz had a little extra motivation heading into Saturday's clash. Urs Fischer, who made his debut as Mainz coach in last season's 2-2 draw against Bayern, was hoping to cause another upset. After all, the hosts had won four of their previous six home meetings with the record champions - a remarkable record against the Bundesliga giants.

Fischer also had a special message for Nadiem Amiri. Left out of Germany coach Jürgen Klopp's squad for the upcoming Nations League fixtures, the Mainz boss encouraged his star midfielder to use the disappointment as motivation and "show everyone what he's capable of".

Unfortunately for the hosts, Bayern had other plans.

The visitors dominated possession and looked the more dangerous side from the opening minutes. Mainz tried to disrupt their rhythm and create opportunities through set pieces, but Bayern eventually found a way through in the 14th minute.

A misplaced pass in Mainz's build-up handed Harry Kane the opportunity to launch a counterattack. As Sano stepped out to challenge the Englishman, Joshua Kimmich was left completely unmarked in the centre. Ballon d'Or winner Kane picked him out perfectly and the Bayern star made no mistake, smashing the ball underneath the crossbar to put the visitors 1-0 ahead.

Bayern continued to create chances but, much like in recent weeks, struggled to convert several promising opportunities.

Mainz thought they had found their equaliser shortly before the break. Amiri delivered a dangerous free-kick into the box, Jonathan Tah got the slightest touch, and Manuel Neuer missed his attempt to reach the ball. Lee was waiting at the back post and headed it over the line.

However, the celebrations were short-lived. An offside position in the build-up ruled out the goal.

Neuer appeared to pick up an injury during the set-piece incident and was unable to continue. Jonas Urbig replaced the veteran goalkeeper before half-time.

The second half initially offered little in terms of excitement, but Bayern remained firmly in control.

Then came the 57th minute. Konrad Laimer spotted Harry Kane making a run in behind and delivered a superb long ball towards the striker. Kane got his shot away, but after the rebound fell back into his path, he cleverly squared it for Lennart Karl.

The teenager made no mistake, firing home to double Bayern's advantage.

After finding the net against Atlético Madrid in the Champions League, Karl was at it again – this time in Mainz. Not a bad week for the young Bayern sensation!

The goal put Bayern in a comfortable position for the remainder of the match. The visitors appeared to tire somewhat, particularly given their demanding schedule and recent injury concerns.

Bayern appeared to have the game under control in the closing stages, but Mainz made a late attempt to turn things around. Substitute Phillip Tietz pulled one back for the hosts in the 92nd minute, setting up a tense finish. However, the late goal proved insufficient to prevent Bayern from taking all three points.

The victory provides a welcome boost for the visitors ahead of the international break while extending their unbeaten run in the Bundesliga.

Mainz, meanwhile, will have two weeks to regroup and address their recent struggles as they look to get their league campaign back on track.
