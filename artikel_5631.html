Product Pick of the Week: CertainTeed Expands Solar Solutions

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

CertainTeed recently launched its Landmark PWR system, a solar roofing system designed to make integrated solar easier to install, easier to service, and more affordable.

Built on the Landmark roofing portfolio, the Landmark PWR system combines durable roof protection with renewable energy generation in a single integrated solution.

The system enables homeowners to take control of rising utility rates, lower their monthly bills, and gain protection from grid instability, especially when paired with a home battery solution.

“The Landmark PWR solar roof represents the next evolution of our integrated roofing and solar strategy,” says Andrew Wickham, director of product management. “As homeowner demand for energy-focused solutions continues to grow, contractors and builders need products that help them differentiate their offerings while simplifying the path to solar adoption.”
