‘Breaking Bad’ Star Dean Norris Lists $3.2 Million California Estate—After His Wife Files for Divorce

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Dean Norris is looking for a buyer for his 11-acre Temecula, CA, estate, listing the hilltop property for $3,199,000—after his wife, Bridget Norris, filed for divorce.

The “Breaking Bad” actor is bidding farewell to the property he and his estranged wife bought for $1.7 million in 2024.

According to Page Six, the property first came on the market in September, roughly a year and a half after Bridget filed for divorce in Riverside County.

The property, which is listed with Sherrie Snyder of Allison James Estates & Homes, is described as a "timeless architecturally designed estate with explosive commanding views from one of the most desirable area's of De Luz."

The palatial compound sits on 11.37 acres and totals 7,315 square feet of living space across the main residence and guesthouse.

Together, the two homes have nine bedrooms, seven full bathrooms, and two half-bathrooms. The current asking price is about $1.47 million above what the couple paid.

At roughly 6,067 square feet, the main house contains seven of the estate’s nine bedrooms, with six on the first floor.

Both the living room and the family room boast cozy fireplaces. A third fireplace is in the primary suite, which features exposed ceiling beams and a walk-in closet.

"At the heart of the home, the spectacular family room offers an impressive setting appointed with a fireplace & custom built-ins," reads the listing.

The kitchen is set up for large crowds, with an eight-burner Wolf range, three ovens, and a walk-in pantry. There’s seating at the center island and space for a table in the adjoining dining area, in addition to a formal dining room.

But the real jewel of the property is the primary suite, which comes with "a fireplace, exposed wood beam ceiling, walk-in closet & luxury bath."

Overnight guests have a residence of their own on the property, which has the remaining two bedrooms.

"The park-like setting acreage is perfect for entertaining from all aspects, featuring an infinity edge pool with a walk-in beach entry, spa, waterfall, fire pit, fountain, outdoor shower, gazebo, dog kennel, lush mature landscape & tree's, outdoor kitchen with BBQ, 2 power burners, sink, ice bin & dining counter," reads the description.

"For the Equines there is a 4 stall breezeway barn with a hay loft, wash room, tack room, storage, two paddocks & a half bath. The family fruit orchard, garden bed's & chicken coop's add to the properties character & country lifestyle," the listing adds.

The estate was listed as active when the divorce drew public attention in November. According to court documents, Bridget filed for divorce on Feb. 14, 2025, and gave Jan. 15, 2025, as the date of separation.

The couple tied the knot in October 2001 and share five children. Dean, best known for playing DEA agent Hank Schrader on “Breaking Bad,” responded to the filing in May 2025. The divorce remains pending.

In her filing, Bridget cited irreconcilable differences and requested spousal support. She also asked to share legal and physical custody of their two youngest children and sought a court determination on the division of marital property.

The listing puts one of the couple’s Temecula properties in the spotlight at a time when another part of their life in the city is also in flux.

The business behind their Swing Inn Cafe has filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection. The couple bought the Swing Inn Cafe in 2022, TMZ reported. According to bankruptcy documents reviewed by the outlet, the restaurant business reported approximately $147,000 in assets and more than $1.8 million in liabilities.

Its gross revenue fell from about $6.8 million in 2024 to $5.9 million in 2025.

Bridget later asked the bankruptcy court to let the business fund payroll. She warned in the filing that staff could leave if they went unpaid, putting the cafe’s ability to stay open at risk. The bankruptcy case is still ongoing.
