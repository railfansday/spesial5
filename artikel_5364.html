Scott Bessent is pushing Nvidia to make a 'dramatic shakeup' and hire an outside CEO

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The Treasury secretary said he wants answers from Nvidia's board and outside counsel within 10 days on export compliance after Jensen Huang resigned

Aaron Schwartz/CNP/Bloomberg via Getty Images

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent on Sunday called for a "dramatic shakeup" at Nvidia $NVDA and said the chipmaker's board should seek its next CEO from beyond its own ranks.

Bessent spoke three days after Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's CEO. The board named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO and began a search for a permanent successor, with internal and external candidates under consideration, the company said. Nvidia will report third-quarter fiscal 2027 on Tuesday.

"This makes me think that as the board conducts a search for a new leader, that they need to go outside the company," Bessent said on Fox News. He added, "I think we need answers in the next 10 days from the board and from the outside counsel."

Bessent questioned whether Nvidia pressed "seemingly illicit sales to goose their revenues and their earnings." He said he is seeking to learn if the company disregarded red flags that shell companies, many based in Singapore, were diverting chips to China.

Bessent said the examination of export-control compliance should extend to other executives as well. "If this were a bank and they hadn't complied like this, the OCC would be in there right now, and a lot of the executives would be removed," he said. He cited rising revenue that Nvidia booked in Singapore while its sales in China declined. Nvidia stopped breaking out Singapore revenue in November 2025 and said that tying sales to the location of customers' headquarters gives a more accurate picture of where they occur.

Bessent rejected calls to codify the chip export restrictions in law. "We have all the rules we need," he said.

Congress is coalescing around a bipartisan bill on export controls that could be approved within weeks, according to Bloomberg.

Huang stepped down after acknowledging an affair with Alice Huang, a former executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed whom U.S. intelligence officials suspect of spying for China. Jensen Huang and Alice Huang are not related. Megaspeed is suspected of diverting Nvidia chips to China in breach of U.S. export controls.

Bessent said Friday that Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick's department "has the pen" on adding Megaspeed to the export blacklist, though the Treasury, State and Defense departments and the Office of the U.S. Trade Representative supported the move. Commerce has declined to list Megaspeed. Lutnick has agreed to testify before Congress.
