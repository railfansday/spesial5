ThreatLocker Receives FedRAMP Certification, Expanding Support for Federal Government Customers

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Certification builds on company's recent investment in a dedicated Public Sector team

ORLANDO, Fla., Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- ThreatLockerÂ®, a global Zero Trust cybersecurity leader, today announced that the ThreatLocker Zero Trust Platform has received FedRAMP Class C (Moderate) certification. The designation applies to the company's dedicated FedRAMP cloud deployment, making it easier for federal agencies to evaluate and adopt ThreatLocker cybersecurity solutions under established federal security requirements.

"ThreatLocker was founded with the mission of shifting cybersecurity to a Zero Trust, deny-by-default defense posture," said Danny Jenkins, CEO and co-founder of ThreatLocker. "That means helping organizations control what software, users, and AI agents are allowed to run, access, and connect to, rather than relying solely on detecting threats after they enter an environment." FedRAMP certification is an important step in making that approach more accessible to federal agencies and allowing us to better serve our government customers."

The ThreatLocker Zero Trust Platform has received FedRAMP Class C (Moderate) certification.

The Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program (FedRAMP) establishes standardized security requirements for cloud services used by federal agencies. The ThreatLocker Zero Trust Platform for Government provides preventive security controls, including Allowlisting, Ringfencingâ¢, and Zero Trust AI Control (ZTAIC) that limit which applications and agents can execute and what they can access or do once running.

The news comes on the heels of ThreatLocker establishing a dedicated Public Sector team to support the unique needs of government customers. The team brings together specialized resources to help federal, state, and local government agencies navigate cybersecurity challenges, procurement processes, and compliance requirements.

"Government customers face a distinct set of challenges including complex procurement requirements, tight budgets, and the responsibility of protecting essential public services," said Steven D'Amico, Vice President for Public Sector at ThreatLocker. "Our job is to understand those challenges and make it easier for agencies to get the protection they need. Between FedRAMP certification and our dedicated public sector team, we're taking important steps to become an even better partner to government."

ThreatLocker is a global cybersecurity leader that stops cyberattacks before they happen. The company's Zero Trust Platform prevents breaches from both known and unknown threats by allowing only explicitly trusted software and activity across endpoints, networks, and cloud systems. Built to deploy quickly and scale across complex environments, the platform reduces operational overhead while keeping business running uninterrupted. Headquartered in Orlando, Florida, with offices in Dublin, Reading, Dubai, and Brisbane, ThreatLocker protects over 70,000 organizations worldwide.
