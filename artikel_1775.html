Mauricio Pochettino urged to axe ‘amateurish’ Manchester United star

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Mauricio Pochettino has been urged to consider making the first ruthless decision of his Manchester United tenure in the wake of last night’s 2-2 draw against Sporting Lisbon.

Having trailed at half-time, the Red Devils looked on course for a morale-boosting victory following a quickfire Bruno Fernandes brace.

The United captain equalised from the penalty spot, before scoring an outrageous fluke when an innocuous looking shot hit a divot and flew beyond the grasp of a bamboozled Kaique Pereira.

Any suggestion that fortune had turned for Pochettino’s ailing side, however, was dispelled with 10 minutes remaining when Senne Lammens made his second inexplicable error of the game by dropping a routine cross and gifting Luis Suarez a goal from close range.

The Belgium international was signed 18 months ago Royal Antwerp and initially looked to have solved United’s long-standing goalkeeping issue.

Lammens is now, however, making mistakes at a similar rate to that of his predecessor, the hapless Andre Onana, and Jamie Carragher believes taking the confidence-starved 24-year-old out of the limelight for the spell would represent a sensible course of action for Pochettino.

Delivering his verdict on Lammens’ performance, Carragher said on CBS: ‘You worry about the confidence of that goalkeeper now.

‘That mistake for the equaliser, the 2-2 goal, you say sometimes ‘an amateur goalkeeper wouldn’t make that mistake’. If that was an amateur goalkeeper you’d be thinking ‘that can’t happen’. That just can’t happen at this level.

‘It is getting to the stage with the goalkeeper now. The focus on a Man Utd goalkeeper is always big if he’s making mistakes. I don’t think the back-up’s even at the level where, no matter how poorly the goalkeeper plays, you change it.’

Pochettino has the experience Karl Darlow, signed from Leeds United in the summer, to call upon but the new United boss was keen to back Lammens last night following his latest disastrous display.

Pochettino said: ‘That is football. Football is about making mistakes. I always want to support my players. It’s something that happened in that case. I think we need to talk about responsibility and it’s our responsibility too to provide the best tools.

‘I think in this type of action, our defensive line also needs to cover the keeper better because it was very difficult. It’s not to blame the defensive line, but it’s two things that we need to improve, details that we need to work on.

‘Professional players need to refresh concepts, refresh details until that becomes a habit. And I think things that we need to improve are habits in a defensive way, offensive way, in all the areas.

‘It’s to blame us because I think we need to manage this type of situation better, because this situation, when you are a keeper, is not easy and you always need help from your teammates. But of course, we accept the challenge and we are here to try to improve the small details – for sure we will improve.’
