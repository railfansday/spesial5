Champions League striker needs to prove himself all over again | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

There must be a sense of disappointment amongst West Ham’s academy that one of their much-heralded strikers, poached from north of the border in July 2025, hasn’t yet progressed on a more impressive trajectory.

After all, Daniel Cummings joined West Ham as the latest nineteen year old striker ‘wonderkid’ with Champions League experience having featured in Celtic’s European campaign before quitting the club and heading for east London last summer.

Sending the young forward out on loan to fifth-tier Aldershot Town (no disrespect to Aldershot) could be seen as West Ham’s tacit admission that the striker has not yet delivered on the precocious talent he displayed in Celtic’s youth team scoring, seemingly, at will.

After all, West Ham academy contemporary Josh Landers headed on loan to Scottish Championship side Ayr United – not exactly the Premier League either – but more challenging surely than the Vanarama National League in which Cummings now finds himself.

Fellow academy Scot Josh Landers is at Ayr United on loan

Of course, there’s a certain amount of schadenfreude from his old club. Celtic fan site celticshorts.com proclaim with glee the apparent stalling of Cummings’ progress:

“Grass is seldom greener after leaving Celtic – Daniel Cummings heads to Aldershot Town…From playing in Champions League at Villa Park for Celtic to Aldershot Town”

Which Cummings will just have to deal with, get his head down, score some goals and answer the criticism from his former club in the best possible way.

In case they hadn’t noticed, now more than ever there’s an opening back at home for any young striker who creates a splash and one or other of Landers or Cummings could easily find themselves recalled after a prolific, headline-making few months.
