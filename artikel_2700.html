AFC playoff standings after 5 Thanksgiving Week 12 games: Browns fall for now

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Cleveland Browns matchup with the Las Vegas Raiders is likely to have huge AFC playoff implications, with the home team heading into Week 12 solidly in the playoff picture while the visitors have a tough road ahead of them. Browns versus Raiders has a lot of details that are important to fans, including some significant injury concerns.

One of the reasons Cleveland was the right decision for Sunday Night Football was all the other games during Thanksgiving's Week 12 schedule. As of Friday night, five games are already in the books, including two AFC matchups that impact playoff standings.

Heading into Sunday's slate of games, the Browns have fallen to the seventh seed, potentially temporarily, with a few other changes as well. The Pittsburgh Steelers' win on Black Friday moved them a half-game ahead of Cleveland, but the Browns would re-take the six slot with a win on Sunday.

A Week 12 victory for the Browns would slide them back into the sixth spot.
