Tennessee held Michigan State to a historic nine made shots in win

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Tennessee changed the narrative on its early season concerns with a statement win against Michigan State, and it did so with a historic defensive performance.

In the 61-46 Volunteers win, Michigan State made just nine shots, its fewest in a game over 30 years, according to the ESPN broadcast, in what was one of the roughest shooting nights in Tom Izzo's entire tenure. It was also the fewest made shots by any Division I team since January 2024, according to ESPN Insights.

"We got punched in the mouth tonight," Izzo said postgame.

It started off a defensive battle, with Tennessee holding a 27-25 lead at halftime after the Spartans made six shots in the opening frame. But out of halftime, the clamps were on Sparty.

In the second half, Michigan State went 3-for-26 from the field. The Spartans didn't make a shot until five minutes into the half, and then made another nearly four minutes later.

Michigan State then went seven minutes without making a shot, getting its final field goal with four minutes left in the game. In the final 11 minutes, the Spartans 1-for-13 from the field, resulting in the 9-for-50 (18%) night with a 4-for-19 (21.1%) mark from 3-point land, with Izzo calling it an "an all-time low."

That also includes star Jeremy Fears Jr. not making a single shot on the night.

The only reason Michigan State stayed in the game for the majority of the second half was getting to the foul line. In total, Michigan State's 46 points came from the free throw line, with Fears getting all 16 of his points from the charity stripe.

It was an epic and signature defensive performance for Rick Barnes, who is now a perfect 7-0 at home against teams in the top five of the Associated Press poll. With some big performances from Miles Rubin and Dewayne Brown, the Volunteers have gotten back on course to assert themselves as a contender.
