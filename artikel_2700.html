Miami Heat go all out with Vice City night ahead of 'Grand Theft Auto 6' release

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

MIAMI — In this corner of Vice City, at least it was dry.

Outside, torrential downpours that lasted all day and had threatened flooding throughout South Florida didn't deter thousands of fans from lining up outside the Kaseya Center for a Miami Heat-Milwaukee Bucks game that was rebranded to celebrate the midnight release of "Grand Theft Auto 6." The game is set in a fictional city named Leonida, otherwise known as Vice City, that is very closely based on Miami.

The video game franchise has a massive fan base and this one has been in production for more than a decade, leading to intense enthusiasm and anticipation for the latest release. Fans, donned in ponchos and raincoats being blasted by the wind, were lined up outside the Kaseya Center more than four hours before tipoff.

Inside, the vibe was euphoric and festive, and the interest in the activations was overwhelming. A custom court with electric pink and blue paint matched the pink at center court, where "Vice City" was painted in the video game's font.

Vice City-branded apparel was sold at several stores across the arena, and lines to get in to purchase items wrapped around more than half of the bowl. One fan said he had been in line for around two hours; he was still several hundred patrons away.

A store selling jerseys, the Heat Jersey Lab, was stocked only with special edition Vice City jerseys to commemorate the activation. With about 45 minutes to go until tipoff, some of the slots where jerseys had hung had been cleared out.

"This is a really unique night," Heat coach Erik Spoelstra told reporters before the game. "It's going to be a lot of fun. It's going to be a global game tonight because of the setup with GTA. I think it's awesome. I think it's fun. It's 10x to everything."

The activations were consistent throughout the night. Fast-food restaurants had been rebranded into ones made popular in the video game franchise; in sections 105, 120, 308 and 324 were "Burger Shots" and in section 325 was a "Cluckin' Bell," a fried chicken joint.

During stoppages in play, the jumbotrons above the floor played advertisements that promoted fictional businesses from the video game; late in the first quarter, one for "Status Auto Imports" was played, with the footage all being gameplay from Grand Theft Auto.

Even the Heat's public address announcer, Gale Nelson, introduced the team to fans as "your Vice City Heat." Later, throughout the night, entertainers did not mention Miami whatsoever, instead referring to the home city by the video game moniker.

"It's interesting," Bucks coach Taylor Jenkins told reporters before the game. "I'm not one to play Gand Theft Auto, never was. I was more of a sports video game guy. But anytime you can cross collaborate with sports and entertainment, I think it's a great opportunity to generate fandom for all parties involved.

"Driving to the arena, even with all the rain coming down, seeing all the people waiting outside, there's going to be a lot of electricity in here."
