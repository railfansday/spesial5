Kickoff time between the Jets and the Bills is scheduled for 1:00 pm Eastern from MetLife Stadium. Kevin Harlan and Trent Green will be on the call for CBS. This game will be shown on CBS affiliates in New York State, New England, and Wyoming. To see whether the game will be broadcast in your area along with the games you will get, check out 506sports.com.

If the game is shown on your local CBS affiliate, you can stream it on Paramount+ or on mobile devices using NFL+. If the game is not being broadcast by your local CBS affiliate, you can stream it using NFL Sunday Ticket through YouTube and YouTubeTV. Separate subscriptions are required to utilize all of these options.
