Fernando Mendoza throws first touchdown pass, Raiders trail Broncos 17-10

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Fernando Mendoza ran it into the end zone for his first professional touchdown last week.

Mendoza fired a beautiful touch pass to Ashton Jeanty down the right sideline and the running back took it all the way for a 68-yard touchdown.

The TD cut Denver’s lead to 17-10 with 2:45 left in the second quarter.

Mendoza is now 6-of-13 for 127 yards with a touchdown so far on Sunday.

Jeanty has also had a big first half, rushing for 64 yards on seven carries — including a 46-yard run. Jeanty also leads so far with three catches for 78 yards with a score.
