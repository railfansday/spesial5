AEW Collision Thanksgiving Results & Highlights: Trios Championship on the line (11/26/2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

AEW is back with a special Thanksgiving night edition of Collision.

The show was taped last night at the Liberty First Credit Union Arena in Omaha, Nebraska, and will air tonight at 8 p.m. ET/7 p.m. CT on TNT and HBO Max. The November 26 episode of Collision will feature two Blue League matches and one Gold League match in the Continental Classic. Elsewhere on the card, The Dangerous New Level will defend the AEW World Trios Championship against Hechicero, Josh Alexander, and Rocky Romero of the Don Callis Family.

TBS Champion Queen Aminata will team up with Mina Shirakawa to take on ROH Women's World Champion Athena and Billie Starkz in a tag team match. Meanwhile, AEW National Champion Ilja Dragunov will respond to Don Callis' lucrative offer, while the Kasper Brothers and the Sisters of Sins will also be in action.

Below you can find the full match card as well as the show's results and highlights.

AEW World Trios Championship Match: The Dangerous New Level (Swerve Strickland, Austin Creed and Kofi) (c) vs. Don Callis Family's Hechicero, Josh Alexander & Rocky Romero

Continental Classic Blue League Match: Brody King vs. The Demand's Ricochet

Continental Classic Blue League Match: "The Alpha" Konosuke Takeshita vs. ROH World Champion Bandido

Continental Classic Gold League Match: "Psycho Killer" Tommaso Ciampa vs. "The Prodigy" Nick Wayne

TBS Champion Queen Aminata & Mina Shirakawa vs. ROH Women's World Champion Athena & Billie Starkz
