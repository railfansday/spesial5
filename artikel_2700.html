Bills news: After sluggish start, offense comes alive in second half to clip the Jets

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Buffalo Bills (8-1) picked up their first AFC East victory of the year Sunday afternoon, overcoming a sluggish offensive performance in the first half to overwhelm the New York Jets (2-8) by a score of 31-12 at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey.

Today's edition of Buffalo Rumblinks recaps the good, the bad, and the ugly from Buffalo's fifth straight win as the Bills are off to their best start since the 1991 season.

Check out the assessments below and then let us know why you're optimistic about this Bills team and what concerns you the most in the comments section.

The Buffalo Bills averaged only 4.3 yards per play, put only 10 points on the scoreboard and were clearly frustrated with a first-half effort that resulted in a 10-6 lead after two quarters.

Then, Buffalo's offense came alive, averaging 14.4 yards per play while scoring three touchdowns and compiling four explosive plays of 20+ yards, including three plays that went for 40+ yards, in a dominant second half.

We break down how Josh Allen and the Bills offense turned on the afterburners to throttle the Jets in the second half, and praise the running of James Cook, who finished with a season-high 157 yards, the third-highest total of his career, including a 74-yard touchdown run in the fourth quarter.

We learn how newly-acquired safety Amani Hooker was an instant upgrade and made a huge impact in his first game with Buffalo; find out why head coach Joe Brady says this was "the best win" the Bills have had all season; examine how Buffalo's defense is thriving, having not allowed more than 12 points over the past three games while keeping the opposition out of the end zone over the last 33 possessions; and more!
