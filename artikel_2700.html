Packers vs. Patriots live updates, inactives, where to watch: A.J. Brown, TreVeyon Henderson out for New England as Jordan Love, Drake Maye face off in Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Green Bay Packers and New England Patriots present what could be Sunday's best matchup, with both teams bringing 5-3 records into the game.

New England arguably took an easier path to Week 9 with wins over the Las Vegas Raiders, New York Jets and Miami Dolphins. However, the Patriots also earned a win over the Buffalo Bills before losing to the Chicago Bears. An offense that ranks third in the NFL might provide an edge against a middle-of-the-pack Packers defense.

Green Bay faced tougher competition during the past five weeks, defeating the Tampa Bay Buccaneers, Chicago Bears and Dallas Cowboys but losing a key NFC North matchup to the Detroit Lions.

Though the NFL season is at its halfway point, the Packers and Patriots each need a win to maintain a hold on the third wild-card playoff spot in their respective conferences. New England is slightly ahead of the Cleveland Browns due to point differential, while Green Bay is one spot in front of the reigning Super Bowl champion Seattle Seahawks (4-4).

New England Patriots running back TreVeyon Henderson released a statement on social media Friday explaining that his absence from the team is due to "unexplained neurological symptoms" that forced him to be hospitalized. Some of his "physical functions" have also been affected.

Henderson says he was examined, and while the results were "positive and reassuring," doctors have yet to determine a cause.

"Recently, I experienced an unexpected and unusual medical emergency involving a series of unexplained neurological symptoms that required hospitalization for several days," Henderson wrote. "During that time, I underwent extensive medical testing and evaluation. While the results of my comprehensive medical testing have come back positive and reassuring, the cause of this remains unknown and unexplainable.

"I will continue working closely with specialists and physical therapists to focus on my recovery, as these symptoms have affected my strength, coordination, and some of my physical functions. I am committed to doing everything necessary to return to full health. My health and long-term well-being remain my foremost priorities."

The 24-year-old Henderson suffered a high ankle sprain in Week 4 and was initially expected to be out for multiple weeks. He did not miss a game as he played against the New York Jets and Chicago Bears, rushing for a total of 40 yards on 10 carries.

Henderson was ruled out of last week's win over the Miami Dolphins and again in Week 9, listed with an "illness."
