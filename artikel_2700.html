Washington Commanders vs Cincinnati Bengals: NFL Week 11 MNF game chat and live discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Washington Commanders are back home in primetime and are hosting the Cincinnati Bengals. Joe Burrow is having an MVP-caliber season leading the Bengals to a 7-2 record. The Commanders are 3-5-1 and very much alive in a weak NFC East division race.

The Commanders are coming off a big road victory over the division-leading New York Giants and are looking to keep that momentum going with a big upset over the Bengals. Washington will be looking at their improved defense to slow down a Bengals offense that has looked unstoppable over the last month.

The Bengals have their eyes on the postseason after taking care of the Pittsburgh Steelers last week to take sole possession of first place in the AFC North. Is this finally the year for Burrow's Bengals to go all the way? An improved defense featuring Jonathan Allen an Dexter Lawrence will be a big help getting to that goal.

The Commanders are 5.5-point home underdogs, and would change the narrative on the season with another primetime win. The Bengals would be their biggest win of the season, topping their Week 3 win of the reigning Super Bowl champion Seattle Seahawks.
