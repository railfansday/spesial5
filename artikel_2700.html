All four NFC South teams are terrible at home, and one will get a home playoff game

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The NFL’s playoff seeding procedures guarantee that an NFC South team will host a playoff game. It may prove to be the easiest place to play that any road playoff team has ever visited.

All four NFC South teams are terrible at home this season: The first-place Panthers are 1-3 at home. The Buccaneers are 1-4. The Saints and Falcons are both 1-5. Add it up, and the four NFC South teams are a combined 4-17 at home.

But someone has to win the NFC South, and that “someone” will be at home during the wild card round of the playoffs, almost certainly playing some second-place team that finished the regular season with a much better record. Most likely, the NFC South winner will be the No. 4 seed, and the second-place finisher in the NFC North will be the No. 5 seed.

Currently, the NFC North features a three-way tie among the Lions, Vikings and Bears at 7-2. For those three teams, coming in second in their division and starting the playoffs on the road against the NFC South winner might actually be a better path to the Super Bowl than winning the division and starting the playoffs at home against a wild card team, likely an NFC North rival or the Rams or Seahawks.

The major prize to earn in the regular season is the No. 1 seed, which comes with a first-round bye and home-field advantage. For an NFC contender that falls short of the No. 1 seed, the best bet might be the No. 5 seed, and an easy place to play in the first round of the playoffs.
