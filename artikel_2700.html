Falcons steal K Jake Moody from Ravens’ practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Baltimore Ravens need a new kicker. Again.

The Atlanta Falcons are signing Jake Moody off of the Ravens' practice squad, per NFL insider Jordan Schultz, robbing Baltimore of another kicker less than a week after Week 1 starter Tyler Loop landed on injured reserve with a groin injury.

The move raises questions about the Ravens' approach to their kicker situation with Loop sidelined for at least four games. The team had a roster spot available, but opted to elevate Moody from the practice squad to face the Jacksonville Jaguars last Thursday. With only two elevations remaining, he would have required a promotion to the 53-man roster to see out Loop's absence, which would have also precluded his departure to Atlanta. Logic suggests that the Ravens should have just promoted Moody knowing they would need him to kick for the rest of the month, maybe longer.

Additional reporting from Jeff Zrebiec of The Athletic indicated that the Ravens were "willing" to promote Moody to their active roster on Monday. The Falcons, though, offered a better situation after releasing kicker Jason Sanders: a potential long-term job for Moody and a home field enclosed by a dome.

It still seems, though, that Baltimore could have headed off this entire issue had they promoted Moody to the active roster last week. It is possible that his agent was keeping tabs on the Falcons' kicking situation and preferred a practice squad stay to preserve his options. However, Loop has not exactly inspired much confidence that he can deliver key kicks for a team that will need to win at the margins to make the playoffs this year. There is an argument that the Ravens should have identified Moody as their kicker for the foreseeable future and adjusted their roster accordingly.

Instead, they will have to scour the NFL's practice squads and free-agent pool to find their next kicker. Signing Nick Folk, who the Falcons cut to add Moody, would be an ironic twist. After three straight years of leading the league with a field goal conversion rate of 95.5% or better, the 42-year-old's numbers have collapsed in 2026. He made just 72.2% of his field goals to start the season with two misses inside of 50 yards and a one-for-four mark from beyond.
