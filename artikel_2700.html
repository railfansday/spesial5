Saints vs. Panthers: The good, the bad, and the ugly in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Saints rookie wide receiver caught 11 of 13 targets for 80 yards in just his second career game. He made a great diving catch over the middle to move the chains and also had some tough catches in traffic. Had this been his first game, he would have tied the record for most receptions in a debut since the AFL-NFL Merger in 1970. Tyson played 45 snaps on Sunday compared to just 18 last week and looks to be fully integrated into the offense now.

The Saints rushing attack once again produce a lackluster performance on Sunday. Tyler Shough was the leading rusher for the second time this year with just 25 rushing yards. Running backs Travis Etienne Jr, Audric Estime, and Alvin Kamara ran 17 times for just 24 yards. The Panthers are far from a good run defense, as entering the game on Sunday they were allowing an average of 133 rushing yards per game which was one of the worst marks in the NFL. The Saints have no variety in their offense and have no ability to sustain drives if the passing game is shut down. Compare that to the Panthers who were able to run the ball consistently with Chuba Hubbard. The Panthers did not turn the ball over and won the time of possession battle thanks to their commitment to the ground game, something the Saints have not been able to do all year.

The Saints are the worst first half team in football. They have trailed in every first half this season, and once again failed to do anything at all on offense until the middle of the third quarter. They turned it over three times (including a turnover on downs) and punted twice. Their deepest drive into Panther's territory ended at the 25-yard line when they gained 9 yards on 1st down and then ran the ball three times for no gain, no gain, and -1 yards resulting in a turnover on downs. All this while the defense held Carolina to just 7 first half points makes it even worse. This has been a problem in every game under Kellen Moore in the last two years. This must be fixed otherwise this coaching staff may be shown the door.
