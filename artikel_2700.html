Atlético plot Tresoldi move, hold interest in Esposito - report

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Atlético de Madrid are eyeing Club Brugge forward Nicolò Tresoldi for a possible transfer next year, while Inter Milan's Pio Esposito is another option under consideration from the club, per Javi Gómara in Mundo Deportivo on Friday.

"The sporting management, led by Mateu Alemany, is clear-eyed about the profile they want to support Atlético's attack," Gómara writes. "They want a center-forward with a goal instinct whose current performance is strong, and who can improve further in the future."

Atlético's forward line has been a source of much consternation and few goals in 2026/27. Julián Alvarez and Alexander Sørloth have combined for one goal in LaLiga, while Jonathan David has scored three goals but has fallen down the pecking order of late. Sørloth seems likely to leave the club in 2027, while David is only here on a season-long loan from Juventus. More help is needed.

On paper, the 22-year-old Tresoldi would be that help. Tresoldi is one of the hottest names on the market following his breakout season in Belgium last year, when he scored 23 goals in 58 appearances across all competitions and impressed during Brugge's Champions League play-off tie against Atlético. He has followed up that breakthrough campaign with 14 goals across 17 appearances this season, all while debate rages over whether he should represent Germany, Italy or Argentina at international level; he has scored 16 goals in 27 appearances for Germany's U21 side.

Gómara writes that Tresoldi will cost "more than €35 million," his value on Transfermarkt. Given his age and performance, Tresoldi surely will fetch Brugge a club-record fee, likely no less than €60 million. He has two years to run on his contract in Belgium.

The other player Atlético like (Gómara says "there are others") is Esposito, who has emerged as the forward of the future for Italian champions Inter and the Italy national team. Esposito is a strong, physical striker who has scored 10 goals in 15 appearances for Italy while becoming a starter for the Nerazzurri. The 21-year-old is under contract in Milan through 2031/32, and Atleti are unlikely to meet his price tag.

"These aren't the only forwards on Atlético's list, and it will be very complicated to sign either of these two forwards," Gómara writes. "But the point is, this is the forward profile they want at the Metropolitano next season."

Of course, this doesn't solve Atleti's goal problem right now. Diego Simeone and the club continue to back Alvarez to rediscover the feeling of scoring goals at club level, and the Rojiblancos' success or lack thereof in 2026/27 hinges on whether that happens.
