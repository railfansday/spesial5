Trump, Xi, Putin said they plan to boost oil production, revamp UN after trilateral talks

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The U.S., China and Russia on Thursday vowed to boost production of petroleum products and reaffirmed their commitment to reducing nuclear risks following their leaders' historic trilateral summit.

President Donald Trump, Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin "had an in-depth exchange of views on the current international situation and committed to maintaining strategic stability and international security," the countries said in identical statements shared after the meeting in Shenzhen, China.

The statements were brief and broad, containing few details about what the leaders agreed on during their talks. They did not include any mention of Russia's war against Ukraine, which continues to rage in eastern Europe despite recent U.S. efforts to broker a ceasefire. They also made no reference to the U.S.' war in Iran, in which Russia and China have both been accused of aiding Tehran.

"The three countries plan to increase the production and supply of refined petroleum products for the benefit of all nations. They reaffirmed the importance of stable energy flows," according to the statements, described as a "Heads of State Declaration toward Peace."

The three presidents also "reaffirmed that a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought," and committed to a "dialogue on reducing nuclear risks and preventing the proliferation of nuclear weapons."

And they committed to "reform" the United Nations so it "can respond more effectively to global challenges and conform to its fundamental mission of international peace and security," according to the statements.

The leaders also agreed that "the International Criminal Court and other similar bodies must not abuse their jurisdiction," the declarations stated. The Trump administration has heavily criticized the ICC, and Secretary of State Marco Rubio last month announced new U.S. sanctions against what he called a "rogue court."

In opening remarks earlier Thursday, Trump said the U.S., Russia and China are "uniquely positioned" to reduce the world's nuclear weapons stockpile.

"As the world's three largest nuclear powers by far, the United States, Russia and China are uniquely positioned to discuss how to reduce the number of nuclear weapons on earth and also to lessen the risk that those weapons will ever be used," Trump said.

"The power is unbelievable, unthinkable," he said, adding that it's "a very hard thing" for countries to reduce nuclear weapons.

That would include not letting Iran have nuclear weapons, Trump said.

He said the three leaders would also discuss the war between Russia and Ukraine, and criticized "rogue globalist organizations," including the International Criminal Court.

The closely watched meeting lasted about an hour and a half late Thursday afternoon China time, following a working lunch between Trump and Xi that started at least an hour later than originally planned.

Trump told Xi and Putin they were "really making history" with the first-ever trilateral meeting between the three countries' leaders.

He said it was an "honor" to be at the meeting with Putin and Xi, whom he congratulated on a "very successful APEC summit." The three were among the world leaders attending the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit.

"Too often past leaders have avoided talking to nations with whom they had disagreements," Trump added. "We feel the opposite."

At the trilateral, Xi said that for large nations, having good interactions is not a choice but a necessity, according to a translation by state media.

In the report, Xi noted China's partnership with Russia, work towards constructive strategic stability in U.S. relations, and called on the three countries to respond to the world's challenges.

Xi laid out four "correct" ways for large countries to interact: mutual respect, peaceful coexistence, win-win cooperation, and adherence to dialogue, according to state media.

The Chinese president echoed Trump's comments on nuclear weapons, calling for such arms to be comprehensively prohibited and destroyed, adding that a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought.

Putin said Russia, China and the U.S. can strengthen global stability if they cooperate constructively, calling for the three leaders to discuss energy security and global and regional issues — including the war in Ukraine.

Beijing only confirmed the closely watched meeting on Monday. The three leaders had briefly gathered Wednesday evening as part of a welcome banquet for the APEC leaders more broadly.
