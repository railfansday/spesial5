Knicks vs. Cavs injury report: Tonight’s game sponsored by Icy Hot

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

If you've attended a Knicks home game this season (lucky you, Moneybags), you must've seen the NBA Cup banner hanging in the rafters. Savor it, because the likelihood of a Cup repeat this season is slim. After losing to the Sixers and Pacers in tournament play, our heroes sit last in East Group B with a point differential of -7. They have two games remaining in first-stage play, one against the Cavs, one against the Heat, and neither team's a pushover.

Plus, the Knicks (11-6) are banged up. They have three game-time decisions to sort out before Wednesday's NBA Emirates Cup tip at Madison Square Garden. According to ESPN's injury report, Karl-Anthony Towns, Josh Hart, and Miles McBride are all iffy against the Cavaliers (9-9). OG Anunoby and Jordan Clarkson are out.

Karl-Anthony Towns (knee) — Game-time decision

Josh Hart (shoulder) — Game-time decision

Miles McBride (groin) — Game-time decision

Towns' availability could swing the frontcourt matchup. With Mobley out, KAT can pull Jarrett Allen away from the rim and open driving lanes for Jalen Brunson. If Towns sits, Allen will have an easier evening against Andre Drummond and, presumably, short stints by James Wiseman.

Hart and McBride matter plenty, too. Anunoby's absence already leaves New York down a defender to throw at James Harden or Donovan Mitchell. McBride can pick up either guard early in the possession, and Hart can help finish the job as a second-line defender / rebounder. With Clarkson out, the Knicks lose an invigorating bucket-getter off the bench and a surprisingly good glass-crasher.

Cleveland is 2–6 on the road, but one of those wins came at the Mecca, 115–107. The Ohio Players average 118.2 points and nearly 15 made threes a game. Harden dropped 51 on Indiana on Friday, and Mitchell followed with 28 in Monday's loss to Chicago. Even without Mobley, Cleveland has two stars who can make a short-handed Knicks defense spend the whole night chasing.
