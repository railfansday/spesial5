Week 9 Yahoo Fantasy x Arena Club Slab Packs: Chase Joe Burrow, Ja'Marr Chase and Josh Allen grails

The NFL is back and so are the Yahoo Fantasy x Arena Club Slab Packs!

Loaded with top performers and fantasy stars, the Yahoo Fantasy x Arena Club Slab packs will be available each week during the regular season for collectors and fans to digitally rip.

For those new to Arena Club, it is a new way of collecting. As the premier tech-powered marketplace and grading platform, Arena Club brings the physical experience of card collecting into the digital age. Users can purchase and open virtual packs of real, pre-graded cards ("slabs"), buy and sell on the marketplace and manage their card showrooms all in one place. Each Arena Club Slab Pack displays the full lineup of potential cards and the likelihood of ripping each.

Yahoo Fantasy Slab Packs contain graded NFL trading cards curated by Arena Club. Fans can purchase a slab pack, each containing a graded card of an NFL player, via the Arena Club platform. This week, we're upping the ante with grails of Joe Burrow, Ja'Marr Chase and Josh Allen being available as chases in both tiers of packs. For the Silver ($70) tier, each grail is worth roughly 35-60x of the pack price. In the Gold ($200) tier, each grail has an estimated value of 25-40x the pack price.

The Yahoo Fantasy Slab Packs will go on sale on at kickoff of the Jaguars-Ravens game (8:15 p.m. ET, Thursday) and be available until the conclusion of the Monday night game (or until they sell out) every week through the end of the season.

You can head to ArenaClub.com or the Arena Club app and use the promo code YAHOO20 at checkout for 20% off your first slab pack or card purchase.

Joe Burrow, Bengals – Week in, week out, Burrow continues to make a case to win the first MVP award of his career. Last week, the Bengals star overcame three interceptions with a six-touchdown performance to lead his team to a shootout overtime win over the Titans.

Ja'Marr Chase, Bengals – Chase had the best game of his season in the Bengals' aforementioned win over the Titans. Burrow's top target hauled in 11 passes for 141 yards and a touchdown, making it three straight games the LSU product has found the end zone.

Josh Allen, Bills – With a three-touchdown showing against Baltimore in Week 8, Allen now has six games this season with at least one passing and one rushing score, and has multiple TDs in every game this season. Buffalo has the NFL's top offense in terms of points scored and the former MVP quarterback is the clear reason why.
