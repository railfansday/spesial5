Dan Campbell: I'm not telling players to stop celebrating because of Aidan Hutchinson's injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Lions head coach Dan Campbell isn't going to tell his players to stop celebrating in the wake of defensive end Aidan Hutchinson's torn ACL.

Hutchinson will miss the rest of the season after injuring his right knee while celebrating a sack of Drake Maye in Sunday's game. Hutchinson jumped in the air and immediately grabbed his right knee after suffering the injury when he landed back on the turf in Munich.

During an appearance on 97.1 The Ticket on Tuesday, Campbell called the injury "one of those things that happened" and that "this game is built off of emotion" that he doesn't want to take away from his players.

"I hate it, I know he hates it. But I mean, the number of moves that I've seen Hutch make and the number of plyometrics, explosive movements, the spins, the one leg lateral movements, lateral balances, I'm just like, it was one of those things, man," Campbell said. "I hate it. I think we all understand what it is, and let's be smart, but no, I'm not gonna say, 'Nobody celebrates anymore.'"

Campbell recounted a story from his own playing days when he tore his biceps while celebrating a play and that didn't lead him to make celebration avoidance a rule for the Lions when he got the job. That doesn't make Hutchinson's injury any easier for the Lions to deal with, but it makes his response to questions about the advice he'll give in the future easier to understand.
