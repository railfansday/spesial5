What we learned from Giants' breakthrough victory over Eagles in Philly

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

PHILADELPHIA - John Harbaugh promised what transpired over this five-day stretch would determine which path the New York Giants will take the rest of the 2026 season.

The scene that played out during a timeout in the fourth quarter of Sunday's 19-11 comeback victory over the Philadelphia Eagles speaks volumes about how things continue to turn in the right direction for this franchise.

With the Giants ahead during a change of possession with 6:43 remaining, Giants players turned to the stands here at Lincoln Financial Field, waving their arms and urging the crowd on. There were mostly Big Blue-clad fans behind their bench, but still, it's been a long time since we've seen that here for the road team from 90-plus north up the Turnpike.

The Giants (5-2-1) picked up their first victory here at the Linc since 2013. Harbaugh's teams in his 19 years as a head coach in the NFL (the previous 18 in Baltimore) are now 16-3 coming off the bye week.
