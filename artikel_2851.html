Amon-Ra St. Brown listed as questionable for Lions

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Lions ruled out several starters for Sunday’s game against the Dolphins and they’re still deciding the status of their top wide receiver.

Amon-Ra St. Brown was limited in practice on Friday for the third straight day because of a rib injury. St. Brown, who has not missed a game since 2023, is listed as questionable to play.

Center Cade Mays (ankle), cornerback D.J. Reed (hip), and defensive end DJ Wonnum (concussion) have all been ruled out. All three players missed practice all week.

Linebacker Jack Campbell (neck) was formally ruled out as well. Safety Kerby Joseph (knee) got in another week of practice, but will not be activated from injured reserve this weekend.

Running backs Jacob Saylors (neck) and Sione Vaki (ankle) are listed as questionable, so the Lions may be shorthanded behind Jahmyr Gibbs this Sunday.
