Deer rescued after spending 10 days with Halloween pumpkin stuck on its head

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A deer in Michigan has finally been rescued after spending 10 days with a plastic Halloween pumpkin stuck on its head, following a search involving animal rescuers and local residents.

The young buck was first spotted in Livonia, near Detroit, with a bright orange pumpkin-shaped container covering its nose, preventing it from eating or drinking properly.

The unusual sight sparked concern among residents, who began sharing it on social media to help rescuers track down the animal.

The Detroit Animal Welfare Group (DAWG) spent more than a week searching for the deer before finally locating it on Sunday, November 8.

The rescue operation took place in Hines Park in Wayne County, Michigan, where volunteers pursued the deer through dense woods, thick brush, and muddy terrain.

According to the rescue group, the team was forced to cross a 20-foot-wide river four separate times while attempting to get close enough to safely tranquilize the animal.

The water was “up to our thighs” and “freezing cold,” making the search particularly challenging.

As darkness approached, the rescuers were preparing to abandon their efforts for the evening when another sighting gave them one final opportunity.

“We were getting ready to leave because dark was setting in and darting in the dark is not ideal; there was a sighting,” the group explained.

The team managed to safely tranquilize the deer and remove the plastic pumpkin from its head, bringing the 10-day ordeal to an end.

The deer was suffering from mild dehydration after struggling to eat and drink, but received treatment and quickly recovered.

Once the tranquilizing medication was reversed, the animal returned to its feet and was released back into the wild.

According to the rescue group, the buck stopped to groom himself for several minutes before walking away, appearing to glance back at the volunteers who had freed him.

The Detroit Animal Welfare Group have since posted about another deer with its head stuck in a pumpkin container, which has apparently been removed by a local vet.

Commenters appealed for people to be more careful with the plastic decorations, suggesting they snip the handles off to lessen the chance of animals getting caught in them.
