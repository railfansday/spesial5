Mark Zuckerberg’s Meta AI predicts that Nvidia stock is positioned for a move that could redefine its valuation ceiling by the first day of 2027. The recent reinstatement of Jensen Huang as President, CEO, and board member has already sparked a sharp rebound, but the full implications of his return are only beginning to unfold.

In a hypothetical yet not entirely impossible scenario, Huang leverages his restored authority and unique relationships to secure a landmark multi-year exclusive partnership with a consortium of major sovereign wealth funds and national AI initiatives.

Under this framework, Nvidia becomes the sole primary supplier of next-generation accelerated computing platforms for a coordinated global “sovereign AI infrastructure” build-out, locking in unprecedented volume commitments and preferential pricing power that far exceed current data-center forecasts.

This catalyst would transform Nvidia from the dominant AI chip supplier into the indispensable backbone of national-level AI strategies worldwide, driving both near-term order visibility and long-duration growth.

NVDA has endured a substantial correction, falling from highs above $240 earlier in the cycle to a recent low near $125 before rebounding. The sharp recovery following Huang’s reinstatement has produced a higher low on the daily and weekly charts, with volume expanding on the upside.

The stock is now attempting to stabilize above the $140–$150 zone after testing deeply oversold conditions. Weekly RSI has begun turning higher from extreme lows, and a sustained break and hold above the $170–$180 resistance cluster would confirm that the multi-month distribution phase is complete.

With a major fundamental catalyst, measured-move extensions and longer-term Fibonacci projections from the recent base become relevant once price enters a fresh discovery phase.

If Huang’s return unlocks the sovereign-scale AI infrastructure deals outlined above, restored leadership confidence, accelerating demand, and improved technical structure could propel the shares dramatically higher.

By January 1, 2027, this over-the-top scenario supports a target in the $480 to $620 range, with an aggressive stretch objective above $750 if momentum and narrative fully align.

This remains a highly speculative, over-the-top outlook rather than a base-case forecast. The catalyst would require a fundamental shift in how AI infrastructure and autonomous systems settle value, something that is currently a long shot.
