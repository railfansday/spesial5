Zcash leveraged bullish positions have come under pressure in the last 24 hours, with reported data showing a 1,483% liquidation imbalance in favor of long-position wipeouts.

According to CoinGlass data, Zcash recorded more than $14 million ($14.07 million) in liquidations over 24 hours, with nearly 93% coming from long positions. Liquidated longs were $13.16 million against shorts of $917,620, a difference of 1,483%.

Long traders have taken most of the forced closures in this market, with Zcash being hit hard by the ongoing market sell-off. Significant long liquidations were seen for Zcash, accelerating its price decline as leveraged positions unwind, particularly with traders being heavily concentrated on the same side of the market.

At the time of writing, ZEC was down 14.53% in the last 24 hours, recording the heaviest losses in the top 20, while NEAR Protocol was down 13.03% in the same timeframe.

Zcash is currently testing support at the 200-day MA ($809); a further drop would target $775 and then $700. A short-term price recovery for Zcash would see it aim for a return to four figures, eyeing the 50-day MA at $1,164.

Zcash is entering its third day of a drop that began on November 18, even as the broader crypto market slid as the tech-stock selloff spread. The rapid unwind suggests a leverage shakeout amid volatility following Anthropic’s IPO.

Anthropic debuted on the Nasdaq on November 19, 2026, under the ticker symbol ANT, raising about $81.3 billion in the public offering (and $107.3 billion combined with a $26 billion private placement).

Over the last 24 hours, volume across crypto futures rose 29.31% to $250 billion, while open interest (OI) fell 3.07% to $109.29 billion, according to CoinGlass data. That combination suggests traders are unwinding positions, with net capital exiting the futures market.

Liquidations across the crypto market increased by 88.66% to $773.76 million, and longs accounted for 87.2% of the total, meaning leveraged bets on higher prices were forced shut as prices fell.
