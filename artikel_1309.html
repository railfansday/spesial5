Isaksen top of Lazio’s renewal list after Floriani Mussolini extension

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Gustav Isaksen is Lazio’s top priority for a new contract after the club extended Romano Floriani Mussolini’s deal until 2031.

According to TuttoMercatoWeb, the leaders face a delicate situation, with around half the squad’s contracts due to expire within the next 18 months. Lazio could also face another transfer block in January. If it’s confirmed, they wouldn’t be able to raise their wage bill, so renewals would have to be on the same or lower terms.

Isaksen’s deal runs until 2028, and Lazio are ready to reward his best spell since joining the club. The Danish winger has scored four goals in his last six games, and he could see his salary rise from €1.5m to around €2.5m net a season. He’s reportedly keen to commit his long-term future to the club.

There’s less urgency with Nicolò Rovella. He has made only 12 appearances in 2026, totalling just over 600 minutes, and neither side is in a rush, although a decision will be needed by the end of the season. Fisayo Dele-Bashiru’s future also needs to be resolved.

Mattia Cataldi, Manuel Lazzari, Matteo Cancellieri and Luca Pellegrini all have contracts expiring in 2027 and could leave next summer. Josip Šutalo, Davide Frattesi and Albert Gudmundsson are on loan.
