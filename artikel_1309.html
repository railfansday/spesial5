Yildiz targets December return as Juventus plan comeback date

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Kenan Yildiz is getting close to a return from injury, and Juventus are targeting next month’s game against Monza for his comeback.

According to Tuttosport, the 21-year-old will soon resume individual training at the Continassa. He’ll then gradually increase his workload, but he won’t be ready straight after the international break.

Juventus have been without their No 10 since August, a big blow that Luciano Spalletti has never used as an excuse. The player himself said he’ll be back “in December” while collecting the Bulgarelli Number 8 award in Bologna on Monday.

The most realistic date is Monday 14 December, when Monza visit the Allianz Stadium. Juve are hoping he could make the squad sooner, either for the home game against Udinese on 6 December or the Europa League trip to face Hapoel Be’er Sheva in Bucharest four days later.

“I feel good and I hope to be playing again soon,” Yildiz said. “I miss Juventus so much. I miss the feeling of playing football.”

Fabio Capello, who presented the award, also had high praise for him, calling Yildiz “the Lamine Yamal of Italy”.
