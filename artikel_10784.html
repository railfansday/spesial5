Burger King brings GTA-inspired burger to life with massive 7-patty Whopper

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Burger King is bringing a massive burger inspired by the Grand Theft Auto franchise into the real world, introducing a seven-patty Whopper based on a fictional fast-food restaurant featured in the games.

The new creation, called the HeartWhopper, is part of a limited-time promotion from Burger King Belgium that allows GTA fans to win a real version of the oversized burger.

The campaign launches on November 19 and takes inspiration from the fictional fast-food chain found throughout the GTA series, turning one of its recognizable menu items into an actual meal.

The HeartWhopper features seven flame-grilled beef patties stacked with cheese, tomato slices, lettuce, and onion rings, recreating the exaggerated burgers associated with the fictional restaurant.

According to Burger King, the promotion is intended as a parody of the video game franchise’s own parody of fast-food chains, with the company claiming its restaurants have served as inspiration for fictional businesses in gaming.

Rather than introducing the HeartWhopper as a traditional menu item, Burger King Belgium is giving players the chance to win one through a social media competition.

A post shared by Burger King BE & LUX (@burgerkingbe)

To participate, fans must share a video clip showing themselves enjoying a burger from the in-game restaurant and tag either Burger King Belgium or Burger King Luxembourg.

The company will select the most creative submission each day, awarding the winner a real-life HeartWhopper. The competition will run for two weeks, giving players multiple opportunities to participate.

Burger King is also promoting the campaign through YouTube advertisements, YouTube Shorts, and gaming communities on Reddit, with teasers released ahead of the November 19 launch.
