Cyberpunk TCG bot kept winning so devs made it the final boss instead of banning it

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A bot that taught itself the Cyberpunk 2077 trading card game climbed to number one on a fan-made simulator, and instead of banning it, the site’s developers turned it into a boss for players to beat.

The bot, now known as The Rogue AI, plays on ChoomBattler, a free online simulator for the official Cyberpunk TCG, where it has won around 97% of its games.

ChoomBattler’s developer explained on Reddit that one account had reached the top of the site’s ranked ladder with a 92-5 record, playing most of those games in a single day.

“It played most of that in one day and never seemed to sleep. When we looked into it, it turned out not to be a person,” the developer wrote.

Rather than ban the account, the team contacted the player who built it, who had made the AI as a personal project. “It taught itself the game by playing millions of games against itself, using ideas borrowed from AlphaGo,” the developer said, adding that it never trained on games from the site.

The bot has since been removed from every leaderboard and given its own spot at the top of the site’s play page, where anyone can challenge it in an unrated match.

According to the developer, it gets no advantages. “It connects like any other player. Our server only sends it what it’s allowed to see: never your hand, your deck or your face-down Legends.”

When the challenge launched, the bot was unbeaten at 25-0. Its record has grown since, standing at 15,591 wins and 468 losses in a recent post from X user saldamico02.
