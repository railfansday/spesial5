Camara to drive for Haas again at Las Vegas GP

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Rafael Camara to continue as Esteban Ocon's replacement for Haas in Las Vegas; team yet to confirm who will drive alongside Oliver Bearman in Qatar and Abu Dhabi ; watch the Las Vegas GP on Sky Sports F1, with qualifying at 4am on Saturday and the race at 4am on Sunday

Haas have confirmed that Rafael Camara will continue as Oliver Bearman's team-mate for this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix after making his Formula 1 debut last time out in Brazil.

The American-owned squad have been left with a vacant seat for the final five rounds of the season following a fallout between French driver Esteban Ocon and team boss Ayao Komatsu last month in Mexico.

Japanese driver Ryo Hirakawa, who had already featured in first practice in Mexico, drove in the Mexico City Grand Prix, before Camara, who had earlier in October been confirmed as Ocon's replacement at Haas for the 2027 season, was given the opportunity to make his debut at his home race in Sao Paulo.

The 21-year-old endured a challenging debut amid wet conditions at Interlagos, crashing out of qualifying before finishing the race last of the runners to take the chequered flag.

Komatsu said: "In Brazil, our 2027 recruit Rafa Camara made his debut under extremely challenging conditions and showed us and the world why we signed him for 2027.

"We're looking forward to building on that for his second race weekend with us."

Haas have yet to confirm who will drive alongside Bearman at the final two races of the season in Qatar and Abu Dhabi, which follow immediately after the race in Las Vegas as part of a triple-header.

It remains to be seen whether Camara will prioritise gaining further F1 experience ahead of his full rookie campaign or attempting to close out the Formula 2 championship, which he leads by seven points going into the final two rounds in Qatar and Abu Dhabi.

It's possible that Camara could drive in both F1 and F2 at those events, but that would create both an intense workload and an additional challenge of repeatedly switching machinery.

Komatsu also revealed that Hirakawa will drive in place of Bearman in first practice in Las Vegas, in what will be his fourth practice session outing of the season.

"Our reserve driver, Ryo Hirakawa, also put in an impressive performance when he was drafted in on Saturday morning in Mexico. He will be back in the car for FP1 in place of Ollie Bearman, now as the 2026 WEC World Champion," Komatsu added.

The team have yet to confirm Bearman will drive for them again in 2027, despite both the British driver and Komatsu having repeatedly suggested it's a formality that a deal will be done.
