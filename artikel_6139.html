John Kerry Is Dead Wrong on Global Warming’s Role in the Midterms

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The former presidential candidate’s views are stuck in the ice age.

Former Secretary of State John Kerry speaks at the U.N. Climate Change Conference (COP31) in Antalya, Turkey, November 10, 2026.

Poll after poll shows global warming collapsing as a political priority, in America and abroad, no matter what certain over-the-hill Democrats might have you believe.

This past week, former Democratic presidential nominee, secretary of state, and presidential envoy for climate John Kerry falsely declared that Blue America’s electoral victory in the recent midterms showed that Americans voted mostly to stop global warming. “This, I promise you, is a transition that the world wants, and no greater manifestation can be seen than the fact that one week ago in the United States people made climate change and its exigencies a voting issue,” Kerry said at the United Nations Climate Change Conference 31 (COP 31) in Turkey. “At every level of the ballot, from Congress to Governors mansions and other statewide offices, Americans did vote for clean affordable energy and they also voted for clean governance.”
