CBRL's Technology Push: Can Digital Investments Improve Efficiency?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Cracker Barrel Old Country Store, Inc. CBRL is increasing its technology investments to improve operational efficiency, strengthen customer engagement and support long-term profitability. Digital initiatives are becoming an important part of the company's strategy to streamline operations and enhance the guest experience.During its fourth-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings call, management highlighted progress in technology, particularly in digital engagement and marketing. The company recently launched a new website and mobile app to make interactions easier and more seamless for customers. It is also enhancing personalization capabilities through Cracker Barrel Rewards, which has more than 12.5 million members and accounts for over 40% of tracked sales. These capabilities provide opportunities to deliver targeted offers, strengthen customer relationships and encourage repeat visits.Management is also leveraging social media and other technology-driven marketing channels to improve promotional effectiveness. Its large loyalty membership base allows the company to engage customers more directly while seeking better returns on marketing investments.

Technology is also central to Cracker Barrel's margin improvement efforts. CEO David Deno emphasized investing in technology and strengthening store-support operations rather than cutting costs that could affect the guest experience. For fiscal 2027, approximately 35% of the company's projected $110-$125 million capital expenditures is earmarked for technology and other strategic initiatives.While these investments offer opportunities to improve productivity and profitability, their returns will depend on effective execution. Cracker Barrel's ability to translate digital capabilities into stronger guest engagement, better efficiency and sustainable margin improvement will be important to its turnaround.

Darden $DRI Restaurants, Inc. DRI is advancing its digital capabilities through investments in online and mobile ordering, payment systems, customer relationship management and data analytics. These initiatives help its restaurant brands personalize marketing campaigns based on guest preferences, visit history and loyalty, while improving customer engagement and operational efficiency. Darden is also integrating technology platforms to strengthen cost management and employee effectiveness.Texas Roadhouse, Inc. TXRH is focusing on technology to improve restaurant productivity and guest convenience. The company has deployed digital kitchen display systems to streamline food preparation, manage higher order volumes and improve coordination among employees. Texas Roadhouse's digital guest management platform also helps manage waitlists, floor plans and server rotations. Additionally, online ordering, curbside pickup and contactless payments enhance convenience and support faster service.Both Darden and Texas Roadhouse demonstrate how technology can strengthen restaurant operations, making digital investments an important competitive advantage for CBRL.

From a valuation standpoint, Cracker Barrel stock trades at a forward price-to-sales ratio of 0.49, below the industry’s average of 3.27.

The Zacks Consensus Estimate for CBRL’s fiscal 2027 earnings per share (EPS) implies a year-over-year uptick of 95%. The EPS estimates for fiscal 2027 have remained stable in the past 30 days.
