Kyle Martino suggests Emma Hayes coach USMNT, USWNT simultaneously

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Kyle Martino has a unique suggestion for the next U.S. men's national team head coach: the U.S. women's national team head coach.

The analyst and former U.S. international put forth Emma Hayes to take over for the departing Mauricio Pochettino with the USMNT, suggesting that Hayes could even coach both the USMNT and USWNT at the same time.

Martino made the unorthodox suggestion on TNT Sports before the USMNT defeated Haiti on Saturday, Nov. 14, Pochettino's penultimate game in charge before he departs to take over Manchester United.

"This isn't clickbait. This is a conversation starter. I'm sure trolls are gonna come out from underneath their bridges and get their thumbs busy," Martino said.

"Hear me out here. Emma Hayes is one of the best coaches in the world. She's got an incredible resume. She's done it abroad. She's done it here. She has a great relationship with U.S. Soccer, a great relationship with the fans.

"We have equal pay for our [men's and women's] players. We were a country that led the charge there, with other nations, let's lead the charge on equal opportunities. Very rarely does it overlap. The Women's World Cup overlaps with the [men's] Gold Cup [next summer] but I've got an answer there. Let [Steve] Cherundolo [men's under-23 coach] take the Olympic team to the Gold Cup."It's a commercial business at the end of the day. Let Emma do it. You've got a national training center – both teams there at the same time. Let Emma do both!

"Manage both teams. Manage the men and women at the same time. No one's ever done it, let's do it. Let's lead the world of sports."

Martino would later acknowledge one of potentially several obstacles to his dual-role suggestion: Hayes is part of the search committee for Pochettino's replacement.

"Unfortunately she's on the committee," Martino said. "Take her off the committee and think about her for the U.S. men. Pochettino and her have a great relationship. So it'll be a nice little transition, they have similar principles."

There would, of course, be other obstacles, including the fact that national team head coaches have plenty of responsibilities outside of international windows. Those include scouting work, maintaining relationships with players and clubs, recruiting dual-nationals, federation responsibilities and more.

Hayes is also currently working building a broader women's soccer development structure through her "WNT Way" strategy.

The summer of 2027 also presents an issue as Martino acknowledges: Hayes would be busy coaching the USWNT at the World Cup, forcing the USMNT to play its confederation championship under an interim coach.

Martino earned eight caps for the USMNT between 2002 and 2006, playing for the Columbus Crew and LA Galaxy in MLS.
