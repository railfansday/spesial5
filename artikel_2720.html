Malachi Toney Named Finalist Despite Ankle Surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Miami Hurricanes wide receiver earned this spot after catching 73 passes for 1,027 yards and scoring 13 times in just eight games. Miami shared the news on X with a post that read, "Malachi Toney has been named a finalist for the 2026 Biletnikoff Award, presented annually to college football's most outstanding receiver 🙌." This honor recognizes the best receiver in the entire sport regardless of where they line up.

The numbers behind Toney's season are hard to ignore for any fan watching the game. He has five games where he gained at least 100 yards receiving, including a massive day against Stanford where he caught three touchdowns for 234 yards. He also topped 150 yards in three separate games this year. According to Yahoo Sports, Toney also earned semifinalist status for the Maxwell Award and the Walter Camp Player of the Year Award. These accolades show he is doing more than just catching the ball; he is changing the game every time he steps on the field.

Head coach Mario Cristobal gave fans an update on Friday about Toney's return from ankle surgery. The coach noted that the receiver had "some work done about two weeks ago, and as of yesterday he was running what, 19, 20 miles an hour," Cristobal said. When asked how much longer Toney needs to get back to full speed, Cristobal replied, "Maybe a little bit more. So yeah, he's really close." Despite this progress, the coach estimated that the receiver was "probably a week and a half out." It remains unclear if this timeline means he will play in the upcoming game or just return to full practice.

The Hurricanes are preparing for their final regular-season matchup without their star player before the ACC Championship game. On Saturday November 28, at noon ET, Miami will host Boston College, followed by an ACC title game a week later. The team faces Wake Forest in the championship game on Dec. 5 as they have already clinched their berths. Following tightrope surgery, Toney secured a spot in the final three after delivering one of college football's most productive seasons. The award winner will be announced by the selection committee on Dec. 11.
