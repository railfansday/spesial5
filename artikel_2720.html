Aaron Rodgers is one win away from tying Drew Brees for fourth-most of all time

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

If the Steelers beat the Bengals on Monday night, Aaron Rodgers will move into a tie for fourth time on the NFL’s all-time wins list.

Rodgers’ teams have won 180 games he has started, counting the playoffs. His next win will give him No. 181, the same number Drew Brees retired with, and the fourth most of all time.

The only quarterbacks in NFL history who have won more starts are Tom Brady with a preposterous 286 wins, Peyton Manning with 200 and Brett Favre with 199. Rodgers has said this will be his last NFL season, so he won’t catch Favre for third place.

Rodgers won 158 games as starting quarterback for the Packers, six with the Jets and has now won 16 with the Steelers.
