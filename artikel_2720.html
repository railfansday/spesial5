Cooper Flagg on what it was like to play in front of Maine-heavy crowd vs. Boston Celtics

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

While the outcome of the game was about as far as one might want for one's ball club, the Boston Celtics' 131-94 drubbing of the Dallas Mavericks still enabled Cooper Flagg to show out in front of as close to a home crowd as the Maine native will get in an NBA arena as he put up 17 points, 3 rebounds, 4 assists, a steal, and a block for the Mavs at TD Garden Sunday (Nov. 15) night.

"I feel confident. I'm confident getting to my spots," said Flagg after the loss, who had a bit of a tough time connecting from those spots as Boston held him to 5-of-15 overall and Dallas to 40 percent from the floor overall. "I know the shots I want to get, make or miss. I'm happy with the result, and I know where I want to be on the court"

"It was great, always, being back in New England," said Flagg with a reference to his many fans who made the trip from Maine to see him play. "It's always a really warm welcome and a ton of support. So it's always a pleasure to be able to play in the New England region and see all the love and support."

"It's special," continued the rising Mavs star. "Like I said, just being able to come here and play and feel that love and support. Every time, it's going to be really special and it's not something I'm going to take for granted."

"So, it's such a blessing to have people that will show up and love and support me like that, and I'm not going to take it for granted."
