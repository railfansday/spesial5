Rams-Packers takeaways: Shake & Blake!

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Los Angeles Rams made a statement on Thanksgiving Eve, taking down the Green Bay Packers 26-13 in a dominant performance on both sides of the football. With the regular season entering its most important stretch, the Rams delivered the type of complementary football that can make them a dangerous team come January.

There were plenty of reasons to be thankful for this performance, but two things stood out above the rest: a powerful running game and a defense that made life miserable for Jordan Love and the Packers' offense. Blake Corum helped lead the charge on the ground, while the Rams' defensive stars made sure Green Bay never found much rhythm. Let's break down the biggest takeaways from a Thanksgiving Eve victory.

In 2025, the Rams identity was to let Matthew Stafford cook. They rode the arm of the veteran quarterback and he turned in an MVP season. But with a slew of injuries to the offense and Stafford not quite playing up to par in 2026, Sean McVay adapted the offense; using 20 and 13 personnel. McVay has leaned into the running game – working Kyren Williams and Blake Corum to wear down defenses. 44 running plays tonight!

On the other side of the ball, Chris Shula's defense just continues to stifle offenses. The defensive line pressure on the quarterback and the secondary's suffocation of perimeter options leaves little weakness on the defensive side of the ball.

If you did not think there was a new RB1 for the Rams yet, you have to believe now. The Rams fed Corum to the tune of 32 rushing attempts. Happy Birthday to Blake Corum for a historic game and Thanksgiving Eve player of the game!

Brandon McManus connected on all 4 field goals.

Ronnie Rivers averaged 25.5 yards per kickoff return.

The Rams simply won the field position battle with quality execution with their special teams. Perhaps they have turned a corner?

Another second half shutout by the Los Angeles Rams defense. Incredible. That's how you honor the 1960s Fearsome Foursome unit. In addition, the Rams did not give up a first quarter touchdown for the 12th consecutive game.
