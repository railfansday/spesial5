Jerry Jones on Christian Parker's status: We're talking about that

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Cowboys defensive coordinator Christian Parker failed to get a vote of confidence from head coach Brian Schottenheimer on Monday and he didn't get one from Cowboys owner Jerry Jones on Tuesday either.

Parker's defense has underperformed all season and they hit another low point during Sunday's 46-40 loss to the 49ers. The Cowboys gave up 584 yards over the course of the game and the lowlights of the unit's performance included three touchdowns that covered at least 66 yards.

Schottenheimer said on Monday that the Cowboys were "still in the process of going through all the film and evaluation of where we stand" when asked if there would be a change involving Parker. During an appearance on 105.3 The Fan on Tuesday, Schottenheimer's comments were brought up and Jones was asked if the team is considering any changes involving Parker's role or responsibilities heading into this week's game against the Titans.

"We're talking about that. I'm not going to talk about it here, but we are talking about those things," Jones said.

Jones gave a similar answer to a question about simplifying the defense, but having these conversations 10 weeks into the season means that there's an acknowledgement that the status quo isn't working. It also means that anything other than a significant shift to the team's defensive operations is unlikely to result in the kind of change the Cowboys need to salvage their season in the final seven games.
