Buffalo Gap volleyball advances to regional final

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

At the beginning of the season, Buffalo Gap coach Elizabeth Ashby said that last year's playoff run motivated her team.

The Bison got to the Class 1 state quarterfinals before its season came to an end, but with just one senior most all of the players this year experienced that run.

"Obviously the Shenandoah District is really tough," Ashby said at the time, "but when we get to our region and we're playing teams our size, that's when they really shine."

No lies detected. Gap had an up-and-down regular season, finishing 12-8, but the No. 1-seeded Bison beat Rappahannock County in four sets to open the Region 1B playoffs Nov. 2 and followed that up with a sweep of Surry County Nov. 5.

Bailey Tribble, a first-team all-Shenandoah District selection, finished the night with 18 kills and 11 digs. Avery Wheeler had 24 assists and eight digs, while Rachel Baber had seven kills and five digs. Brailyn Green finished with 12 digs while Madison Botkin added three kills and a pair of digs in the win.

Gap will host No. 3 Luray for the Region 1B championship Nov. 10. Luray beat No. 2 Riverheads in the other semifinal 25-19, 25-20, 24-26, 25-13.
