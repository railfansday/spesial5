Rams featuring both Blake Corum and Kyren Williams, will it last?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Will the offense for the Los Angeles Rams run through Blake Corum and Kyren Williams? The running game for L.A. has been a massive part of their 3-game win streak, and with Puka Nacua missing time, they'll likely need the run game even more. Will they keep using both backs or will one start to get more run than the other?

Sean McVay may be tested to see if he can stay with the run as he and many coaches sometimes abandon the run or don't use it enough to keep the defense in check. Even if the run game isn't producing, it can still help take pressure off the quarterback and tire out an opposing defense.

The Rams will need to continue to lean on both backs who can both run, block, catch, and seemingly do whatever the Rams are ask. How well can the run game be without Puka Nacua assisting? Week 10 will be a big test for the Rams and their offense.

They'll hope their running backs can help steer their team to their fourth win in a row.
