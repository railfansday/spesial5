Sean Miller singles out Arch Manning as inspiration to Texas after escaping Baylor

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Arch Manning effect has rubbed off on Texas basketball too. Coach Sean Miller himself watched the epic final second throw that beat LSU on Saturday. Miller's own team endured their own emotional win on Sunday, beating Baylor 75-72.

But Miller singled out the Longhorns quarterback as a great example for his players to follow.

"Arch Manning should be an example to every young athlete, regardless of sports, of just his competitive spirit, being able to handle adversity. To watch UT, watch Texas, that fight that thrill of victory, it inspires us to wear the burnt orange," Miller said postgame with reporters.

Manning endured a rough three-interception night down in Baton Rouge. Texas even faced potential elimination from the College Football Playoff picture too with a loss. Manning even looked like he was bothered by pain in his throwing shoulder. But Manning put together the final drive amid the obstacles and elevated his Longhorns football team to 8-2.

Miller and his own team took on an unbeaten Bears bunch. And one aiming to pull off the early upset of the nation's seventh-ranked men's basketball team Texas. Baylor even led by seven points at one point.

But Texas erased that deficit by cleaning up the glass, grabbing 41 rebounds compared to Baylor's 39. Miller turned to the Longhorns' length too, pounding Baylor with 42 points in the paint. Yet Baylor showed its defensive mettle too, blocking five shot attempts compared to the Longhorns' four.

Texas still clamped down on defense by bottling Baylor to 36% field goal shooting. So now the Longhorns stay unbeaten at 4-0, this time using a non-basketball talent in Manning as the inspiration.
