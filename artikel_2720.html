Chelsea facing huge injury scare after Leeds United defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Chelsea have another problem to manage, and this one matters. Moises Caicedo went down early in the defeat to Leeds at Stamford Bridge, and Xabi Alonso admitted the club will need more time before understanding the full extent of the injury.

The midfielder pulled up just after the 20-minute mark, with no obvious contact in the moment that ended his game. He received treatment and tried to continue, but was eventually replaced by Jordan Henderson. For a player who has already had fitness issues across the season, it is a worrying development.

Caicedo had only just returned after another physical concern on international duty. He asked to come off at half-time in Ecuador's 2-0 win over Bolivia, yet was still cleared to start against Leeds. Early in the match he was involved in a series of heavy challenges and also needed treatment after landing awkwardly on his back. None of that helps the picture.

Alonso was clear that Chelsea do not yet have answers. Instead, they have another wait, another assessment, and another key player in doubt at a point when the squad is already stretched.

The timing mattered. Chelsea were still level when Caicedo went off, and for a while they looked in control. Emmanuel Emegha put them ahead, but Noah Okafor responded quickly. From there, Chelsea folded. Okafor added a second and Dominic Calvert-Lewin converted from the spot to seal a painful result.

After the match, Alonso said: "It went wrong after the equaliser, after the change (with) Moi, we completely lost the momentum. Until then, we were doing okay, I think that we were creating, playing a lot on the pitch, we were coming out of the pressure.

"But from that moment, they started believing and we started not believing.

"And after that, we never got the good feeling mentality in any moment and we were not able to come for the pressure and we didn't get close to their goal, so it completely changed."

That matters even more because Chelsea are already without Joao Pedro, Maxence Lacroix and Reece James. Alonso suggested Lacroix is closer to returning than James, saying: "I think that Max has a better chance than Reece. It's not definitely that Reece won't be back before then, but Max has a better chance."

Right now, Chelsea need clarity on Caicedo. Until they get it, the concern is obvious.
