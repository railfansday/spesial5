Champions League Liveblog: PSG vs. Roma and Inter vs. VfB Stuttgart

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens from the Champions League, as Roma visit Luis Enrique’s Paris Saint-Germain, and Inter host VfB Stuttgart.

They both kick off at 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET).

The Giallorossi have had strong performances, but lack of experience showed when throwing away leads with Real Madrid and Manchester United.

They reunite with Luis Enrique, who was coach of Roma in 2011/12, and has won the last two Champions League trophies on the trot.

Inter are one point behind Stuttgart going into tonight’s clash, so it’s a must-win fixture to climb the table after losing the first two, then winning the next two.

Cristian Chivu insists Inter had the right attitude in their 2-0 win over VfB Stuttgart, warning ‘in the Champions League you cannot dominate from start to finish.’

Gian Piero Gasperini is ‘happy and proud’ that his Roma gamble paid off with a 2-2 Champions League draw away to PSG. ‘If we stayed in the game, the second half would be decisive.’

Roma earned a more than creditable 2-2 draw away to reigning Champions League holders PSG, as Mario Hermoso and Donyell Malen’s penalty cancelled out Illia Zabarnyi and Ousmane Dembélé.

Inter managed to get the better of VfB Stuttgart and leapfrog them in the Champions League table thanks to Petar Sucic and Lautaro Martinez.
