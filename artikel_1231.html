Leeds Send Scouts on Transfer Mission for Celtic Striker Camilo Duran

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Leeds United have made their first move as they ramp up their interest in Celtic striker Camilo Duran.

The Colombian, 24, has been in red-hot form since signing for the Hoops in a £6million deal from Azerbaijani side Qarabag. Indeed, he has scored 14 goals and provided six assists in 22 appearances across all competitions this season.

Duran found the net twice in a 3-2 win against Benfica in the Europa League earlier this month, and was on the scoresheet as Martin O'Neill's men thumped St Mirren 4-1 on Saturday. He has been a revelation for Celtic, but they could find it difficult to keep hold of him if he continues to score at the rate he has been, with Leeds among those keeping a close eye on his situation and progress.

According to The Herald's Stephen McGowan, Leeds have scouted Duran and have sent people north to watch him at Celtic Park.

He said: "Duran's 14 goals in 22 appearances show why clubs like Leeds United have sent scouts north for a look. While the Colombian and Baur look like quality footballers, too many of the summer signings have added little bang for their buck."

Leeds are a little light up front and could dip into the market for a new No.9 in January. Dominic Calvert-Lewin is their first-choice centre forward and is expected to be that for the long-term, but beyond him, the Whites don't have a strong back-up.

Duran could be the perfect second-choice striker for Leeds. A clever and versatile operator, Duran has been clinical in front of goal this season and has qualities that can adapt to the Premier League. He is fast, likes to dribble, link up the play and get a shot off.

From watching him, he does seem like a similar mold to Joel Piroe - but a better version. Duran is such a good ball striker and once he has a chance, you don't feel like he is going to miss. That was the same as Piroe, though he couldn't cut it in the Premier League.

Celtic Shorts said Duran is the 'best striker in Scotland' earlier this month, following his brace against Besiktas. With his goal against St Mirren, he has moved up to the top of the scoring charts in the Scottish Premiership, and is on fire right now.

After scoring for Celtic against Hibernian last month, O'Neill heaped praise on Duran. “He’s brilliant! A really brilliant finish,” O’Neill told Celtic TV.

“The minute that he picks that ball up on the edge of the penalty box, I almost feel as if he’s going to score a goal as well, too. So it was fantastic. Eventually, it turned out to be the winning goal, but it was a great goal anyway.”

Celtic thrashed St Mirren on Saturday in the first game since it was confirmed that Mika Baur will be out for at least 10 weeks with an ankle injury
