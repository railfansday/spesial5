Referee Accused of Being Drunk During Match Releases Statement

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

A referee accused of being drunk during an AFC Champions League Two match has released a statement regarding the allegations.

Hanna Hattab, a FIFA-licensed Syrian official, had to be replaced by fourth official Ammar Abu Alou at half-time after appearing visibly confused. Footage circulated online of the referee looking 'lost' during the match between Emirati club Al Jazira and Iranian outfit Gol Gohar.

The most perplexing moment, which ultimately prompted the half-time change, came in the 32nd minute, when Hattab booked Al Jazira goalkeeper Ersin Destanoglu after a foul on Ali Asghar Ashouri in midfield. The shot-stopper was completely uninvolved, standing in his own box when the incident occurred close to the half-way line, and this unsurprisingly provoked an angry response from the Al Jazira players and coaching staff.

After several minutes of significant consultation with the assistants, the booking was rescinded.

At half-time, the Iranian giants cited this bizarre moment as a reason not to resume the match, claiming that Hattab was 'not in a normal state' and that they had seen him drinking alcohol before kick-off. The decision was then made to promote Abu Alou to oversee the second period.

After being accused of such a lack of professionalism, Hattab has broken his silence.

Releasing a statement on his Facebook page, the referee condemned those defaming his family as a result of the incident.

"A brief explanatory statement," Hattab wrote.

"Unfortunately, I am not used to that (but the wise understand from the hint).

"I will wait for the official communication and official stance from the Syrian Arab Football Federation, and I will also communicate with the people and parties responsible for me locally and internationally, before issuing an official statement that clarifies all the details and facts related to what happened in a friendly match between a Syrian and a national team in the Asian Champions League 2 yesterday, with full transparency and clarity."

"In conclusion, I confirm that family and personal life are not the responsibility of others, and are not a game in your hands.

"I also confirm my full right to take all legal and judicial measures against anyone who published or made a statement or contributed to the insult and defamation of me, regardless of their position or status, whether they are an individual, an institution, or a sports entity.

"Thank you to all the good among you and the evil among them."

Barcelona players called for UEFA to investigate referee Szymon Marciniak back in 2025.

Arabic sports journalist Issa Said believes that Hattab was wrongly accused of being drunk, claiming that what happened to the official was a result of 'low blood sugar'.

"What happened to him was the result of low blood sugar, which caused him to lose focus and consciousness," Said wrote on X.

"He underwent a medical examination after the match and was not drunk, contrary to what was being circulated. I ask everyone, just as the insults and smears spread, to help spread the truth and set the record straight, and not to be the reason people lose their livelihoods and have their reputations tarnished without justification.

"Behind every name is a person, a family, and a reputation that deserves respect."
