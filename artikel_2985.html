Here’s the 1 Number Anthropic Investors Must Know

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

By Keithen Drury – Nov 19, 2026 at 8:19AM EST

Anthropic (ANT +0.00%) is going public, and I think there's one number investors need to focus on. It relates to growth.

Over the first six months of the year, Anthropic grew its revenue at a 1,305% pace to $16.3 billion. Anthropic updated this figure to include Q3 results, bringing revenue to $33.2 billion. However, what investors are watching more closely are quarterly revenue trends. In Q2, it generated $11.6 billion in revenue. In Q3, that figure rose to $17 billion, good enough for 47% quarter over quarter growth. In Q2, it put up 147% growth.

While that's a slowdown, Anthropic is still growing lightning fast. I think it's by far the most compelling reason for prospective Anthropic investors to purchase the stock, and they could be well rewarded years down the road as a result, but only if it can maintain a fast enough growth rate.

Most investors will never encounter a company that's growing as fast as Anthropic is. This throws traditional valuation analysis out the window because so much of the modeling depends on the growth rate.

Anthropic's revenue grew at a 1,127% year-over-year pace in the first nine months to $17 billion. If we make the faulty assumption that Anthropic can maintain this growth rate forever, in just a few years, that figure multiplies to tens of trillions of dollars. That's not going to happen, so investors need to discount that growth rate, but by how much is important to know.

Even if Anthropic's revenue growth is cut in half over the next few years, it will still result in one of the largest companies by revenue on the planet. There is a lot of money funneling through AI right now, and Anthropic is one of the largest beneficiaries. There are countless opportunities AI hasn't even scratched the surface of yet, and that growth is why buying Anthropic shares today may be a wise idea.

Knowing the growth of Anthropic and following it is key to being a successful Anthropic investor. If Anthropic can keep it up long term, Day 1 investors stand to make a huge profit on their investment, even if they didn't get in at the Day 1 price tag of $83 per share.

Anthropic will be one of the fastest-growing companies available on the stock market when it goes public. When you account for its trillion-dollar-plus market cap, it easily takes the crown for the fastest-growing megacap company. For reference, the current king of growth, Nvidia (NVDA +2.66%), grew 20% quarter over quarter during its most recent results. This can make the stock hard to value, so expect some erratic share price movement over the short term. But if you can take a long-term view, Anthropic could be a wise investment if its growth keeps up.
