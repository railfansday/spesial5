Mannelly: The Chicago Bears are a Complete Team

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Bears are playing as well as, if not better than, every team in the NFL. What a time to be alive!

To discuss where this Bears team is at, we talked to Chicago Bears legend Patrick Mannelly to get his opinion on the 2026 squad. Patrick joined us on behalf of HeartFlow. HeartFlow helps people get better insight into how their heart and cardiovascular health status is. For more info, go to Heartflow.Com/Football.

Patrick discussed how impressed he is with Ben Johnson, Caleb Williams, and the offense. He talked about how impressed he was with Jeffery Simmons' debut and the defensive outlook of the team moving forward, and he also, as he always does, broke down the Bears' special teams and gave us his expertise as to where Cairo Santos, Tory Taylor, and the return and coverage units look at this point in the season!
