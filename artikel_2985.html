High Roller (ROLR) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Casino exits cut revenue as prediction markets take center stage.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

High Roller Technologies, Inc. (ROLR -7.05%) transitioned its primary focus from online casino operations to its newly launched prediction markets platform during the third quarter. Management reported that the commercial launch of the ROLR brand signifies a move into the expanding derivatives market for real-world events. The company is executing a strategy to reduce its casino footprint in jurisdictions with unfavorable economic or regulatory conditions while investing in proprietary technology and U.S.-based distribution partnerships. The company maintains a debt-free balance sheet with a cash position intended to support its operational roadmap through late 2027.
