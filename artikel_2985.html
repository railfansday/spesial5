Zeta Global: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Zeta's Palantir partnership could add over $100 million in annual revenue, a bet on expanding beyond marketing into enterprise planning.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 9, 2026, Zeta Global (ZETA +8.17%) raised its full-year 2026 revenue guidance midpoint to $1.855 billion after generating $493 million in quarterly revenue. Enterprise clients are replacing legacy marketing software with native artificial intelligence tools. That shift creates a durable path for long-term expansion. Three things management said stand out.

Requests for proposals, which are formal bid solicitations from corporate clients, doubled compared to the prior-year period. Enterprise clients are leaving legacy marketing technology (martech) vendors to capture better returns on their technology spend.

Native artificial intelligence, or AI, algorithms rely on massive data sets to generate precise customer recommendations. Zeta Global uses its identity database to deliver higher marketing returns than traditional search channels, driving faster expansion among major accounts.

Connecting internal customer records with external market intelligence expands the platform into enterprisewide operational planning. Management projects that this partnership can generate over $100 million in incremental annual revenue in the coming years.

Quarterly revenue growth accelerated to 46% alongside record free cash flow, reflecting high sales force productivity and expanding operating leverage. Proprietary data scale, native artificial intelligence capabilities, and deep platform integration give the company lasting competitive advantages as management tracks sales execution and platform adoption.

Expanding beyond marketing software into enterprise intelligence positions the platform to capture a broader share of corporate technology budgets over the coming years.
