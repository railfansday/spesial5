I’ve been looking into how many England caps have been picked up by Newcastle United and Sunderland players.

So this is England Caps picked up by the players of Newcastle United and Sunderland whilst the individuals were at these clubs. Not England Caps picked up before or after their times playing on Tyneside and Wearside.

Last night England delivered a superb 3-2 away victory against Spain.

The World Cup winners very lucky that they only lost by one, England roaring into a 3-0 lead and then a trio of individual errors threatening to undermine a brilliant team performance.

In that match Lewis Hall started and was his usual brilliant self, picking up his 9th cap.

Dan Burn coming off the bench late on to win his 12th cap and help see England over the line.

Those two international appearances for the United pair in Madrid now make it 347 England Caps picked up by 44 different Newcastle United players (whilst they were also playing for NUFC).

Having a look at the Sunderland players and their stats, in the entire SAFC history only 101 England Caps have been won by 29 Mackem players..

Eight different Newcastle United players played for England during the four and a half years Eddie Howe was in charge at NUFC, with seven of them signed by him. Under Eddie Howe there was almost 70 England caps won by Newcastle United players whilst also playing for NUFC. Now Lewis Hall and Dan Burn picking up what will hopefully the the first of many under Matthias Jaissle.

Jermain Defoe picked up two England Caps nine years ago and he is the only Sunderland player to appear for England since 2012.

Indeed, only nine England Caps have been won by Sunderland players since 1999. Only 21 England Caps for the Mackems in the last 50 years and eight of those 21 were picked up by Kevin Phillips.

The entire Sunderland history of 101 England Caps could be exceeded by their old mate Jordan Pickford in the near future, he has 94 England Caps whilst playing for Everton.

Intriguingly, Sunderland are a distant third when it comes to the north east clubs, as when it comes to England Caps it is now Newcastle United 347, Middlesbrough players 167 and then Sunderland on that total of 101.
