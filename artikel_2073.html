Bristol City size up managerial candidates despite recent Michael Skubala statement

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Bristol City sit 17th in the Championship table after winning only one of their last seven Championship games. Michael Skubala has come under fire with some early calls for his sacking, but chief executive Charlie Boss stated that the summer appointment will be given time to turn around the Robins’ fortunes.

Now though, reporter Alan Nixon states that City are doing background checks on potential replacements if they are forced into a decision over Skubala’s future. The hierarchy are checking into the availability and cost of possible targets, and there are funds in place to pay compensation for an in-work target.

Although the Robins have stated Skubala is getting a chance to find form, the belief is that they should be far higher up the Championship table. If form doesn’t turn and the right replacement candidate is up for grabs, the former Lincoln City boss could be moved on.

Boss didn’t say how long Skubala has got to turn around his fortunes to offer any hints untoward, but the fact that the pressure has been publicly addressed shows that Bristol City are acutely aware that they need to be doing far better than they are at present.

Unfortunately for Skubala, in the bid to find form, he’s got some tough opponents to face. Kim Hellberg’s Middlesbrough are up first after the ongoing international break ends, before an important home match against Wrexham the following Tuesday.

The Robins are back on the road at another promotion hopeful afterwards as they head to The Hawthorns to face West Brom. December then begins with a home tie against Swansea City, with clashes against Derby County and West Ham (both away) following.

It marks a tough run of fixtures for Skubala as he desperately looks to find the form to secure his Ashton Gate position.

Bristol City have a squad capable of far more than 17th in the Championship. The likes of Scott Twine and Jason Knight are both star players at this level, while Rio Cardines has impressed on loan from Crystal Palace and big money was spent on Dom Ballard and Lorent Tolaj.

It means that pressure on Skubala is justified, but the club are taking a smart approach in holding fire on sacking the boss for now. With new chief executive Boss, sporting director James Ellis and the new manager, it is the start of a real refresh at Ashton Gate as they hope to build towards long-term success.

A lot of work went into hiring Skubala from Lincoln City as they deduced him to be the perfect candidate to lead this new chapter. More was expected of the manager in the short term, but he will be judged on long-term results, so it is only right that he is given time to get out of this rut.

If it comes as any reassurance, Skubala endured a tough start with Lincoln. He won only two of his first 14 games, but he was given time to recover and built them into a League One title-winning side.

Bristol City will hope for a similar turnaround, but if it doesn’t come, they will be ready to act.
