Man United 'exploring' Jean-Philippe Mateta deal ahead of January - Transfer expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Manchester United are exploring the possibility of signing Jean-Philippe Mateta ahead of the Janaury transfer window.

The 29-year-old could be made available by Crystal Palace as he has just months remaining on his contract at will be allowed to leave on a free transfer next summer.

His performances at Selhurst Park in recent seasons have placed a number of top clubs on high alert, while Michael Carrick's side are aiming to bolster their options up front.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that a move for Mateta is being considered due to his availability as the club weights up their options.

A number of strikers are currently being linked with a move to Old Trafford, but the Crystal Palace star's contract situation and impressive record has him high on their list.

However, there are also some concerns about whether he would be the right fit for Man United, with questions asked about his clear desire to leave Crystal Palace.

Football Insider revealed on 3 October that Man United could be forced to sign a new striker in the January transfer window if they suffer injuries or any major departures.

Benjamin Sesko's injury has since exposed a lack of depth in the position, and forced the recruitment team to assess a number of options to strengthen their attack.

Mateta's proven goalscoring record in the Premier League, and potential avaialbility, has now placed him firmly on Man United's radar despite the doubts over a move.

"Man United will be exploring deals for a number of strikers because they need to strengthen," Brown told Football Insider.

"Jean-Philippe Mateta will be one they're looking at because of his situation at Crystal Palace, they'll be keeping an eye on what happens there and ready to take advantage.

"His contract situation is interesting, he's not going to sign, so will he leave in January or at the end of the season?

"If they are going to let him go in January, Man United could be tempted, but I do think there are some concerns about whether he's the right fit for what they want.

"I've always said he's a Crystal Palace player, he suits that type of club where he can be the main man and maybe they cut him some slack when he's not quite up to scratch.

"You don't get that type of time and patience at a club like Man United, there's more pressure, so will he deal with that? Plus, I do have a few questions about his attitude.

"This business with pushing to leave Crystal Palace raises a few red flags for me, but whether it will be a problem for Man United, we'll see."

Much of whether or not Man United are tempted to make a move for Mateta in January will be based on whetehr Crystal Palace are prepared to see him leave.

Football Insider revealed on 31 October that Crystal Palace have yet to make a decision on Mateta, with his value to the team potentially trumping the funds raised from a sale.

Jorgen Strand Larsen has led the line for the majority of his season so far, while Eddie Nketiah remains an option, but the France international remains their best option.

His performances have made him a key part of Pierre Sage's plans, at least for the immediate future, and Palace must now weigh up whether there is any value in cashing in.

Similar to their £20million sale of Marc Guehi to Man City last January, the Eagles could be tempted to cash in at the right price, regardless of his role in their plans.
