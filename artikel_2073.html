Sources: Rayan Ait-Nouri is finished at Man City

Rayan Ait-Nouri is unlikely to return to Enzo Maresca's Man City plans and could push to leave in January, sources have told Football Insider.

Ait-Nouri is yet to make a Premier League appearance for City since the start of this season.

He has been limited to just four appearances across Champions League and Carabao Cup games, with three assists in that time.

The 25-year-old is behind both Nico O'Reilly and Josko Gvardiol in the battle for places at left-back.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider’s Transfer Insider podcast, he will look to depart in the next window if nothing changes under Maresca.

Man United made a surprise approach for Ait-Nouri at the end of the summer window and could look to try and sign him again.

Ait-Nouri registered five assists in 31 appearances for the Citizens last season but has failed to make an impression the new boss.

He remains keen to fight for his place in the side but may end up losing the battle because two top players are ahead of him in Maresca's left-back pecking order.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "He potentially could move away. United did make a surprise move for him in the summer transfer window which was quickly rebuffed by City.

"The problem for Ait-Nouri is that he’s down the pecking order in Enzo Maresca’s plans. He’s behind Josko Gvardiol and Nico O’Reilly, two quality players, and is he ever gonna get ahead of those two? I'd say it is unlikely. If Ait-Nouri feels there’s no road or pathway for him at City then he might seek a move away from the Etihad."

While they are well-stocked at left-back, City do not have a natural right-back in their squad as it stands.

Matheus Nunes has been the first choice for the last 18 months, despite being a natural central midfielder, with Rico Lewis as his back-up.

Sources have exclusively revealed to Football Insider that Man City remain interested in Newcastle's Livramento ahead of the new year, having kept tabs on him for a while now.

Villarreal defender Freeman, 22, is also on their radar as he nails down a place in their side and the USA national team just months after joining from Orlando City.
