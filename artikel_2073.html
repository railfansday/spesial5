Ex-Celtic star backs 'amazing' former boss to deliver amid growing pressure

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Vidar Riseth believes Celtic should stick with Martin O’Neill and has backed his former boss to mastermind a huge European result against Besiktas.

The Norwegian played under O’Neill during his first spell at Parkhead and remains convinced the 74-year-old is the right man to steer the Hoops through their current difficulties.

Speaking to SunSport, Riseth insists Celtic are fortunate to have a manager of O’Neill’s calibre in the dugout as he urged the club hierarchy to keep faith with him.

O’Neill is under increasing pressure following six defeats in Celtic’s last ten matches and desperately needs a victory against Besiktas to keep their slim hopes of reaching the Europa League knockout stage alive.

But Riseth has no doubts over his former gaffer’s ability to turn things around. He said: “For Martin to come back how he did last season was amazing. And now the best thing Celtic can do is to keep Martin as long as they can, because he is one of the great coaches.

“It doesn’t surprise me he is still going at his age. As long as Martin can talk and can sit in the dugout, he will do a good job because he’s an amazing guy.

“Straight away, Martin was such a good leader for us - as soon as he came into the dressing room, you just knew Martin was going to be a great coach.”

Riseth experienced O’Neill’s impact first-hand after the Northern Irishman took charge of Celtic in 2000.

Celtic’s European campaign has been a major source of frustration this season, with Riseth’s former club LASK producing a dramatic turnaround to knock the Hoops out of the Champions League.

Their difficulties have continued in the Europa League, where three defeats have left Celtic 35th in the 36-team league phase. However, Riseth insists there is still time for O’Neill and his players to salvage their campaign.

He said: “After seeing the first leg with LASK, I was sure Celtic were going to win. That was one of the most disappointing games I’ve ever seen from Celtic. They’ve still hopefully got enough games left to turn it around in the Europa League.

“They still have a chance, so they just have to believe and keep going.”
