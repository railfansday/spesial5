David Raya names Arsenal teammate as dressing room’s worst DJ in revealing teammate verdict

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

David Raya has offered a glimpse into the lighter side of Arsenal’s dressing room. He’s revealed which teammates make the most noise, take training too seriously and have the worst taste in music.

The goalkeeper was in a jovial mood during his Q&A with GOAL. And offered his thoughts on a number of players in Mikel Arteta’s squad.

When asked who he thought was the worst DJ in the dressing room, Raya was quick to point out Noni Madueke.

But the Spaniard also revealed that the team have a far from relaxing time travelling abroad. With a number of Arsenal stars always making an impact on the bus.

One of the more entertaining answers came from Raya, as he named Madueke as the player with the worst music taste.

But he wasn’t the only one to get a bit of friendly ribbing.

Raya said the three that make the most noise in their away games are Eberechi Eze, Madueke and Bukayo Saka.

They love playing Uno on the bus. However, they have such a competitive nature that it is rarely quiet.

It also gives a good insight into the character within the Arsenal squad. As the goalkeeper has said, there is a lot of character in the group.

Raya also mentioned the high standards in the Arsenal training environment.

He said that Mikel Merino is the player who hates losing the most due to his natural competitive nature.

Merino was also Raya’s choice to cook him a meal.

Raya was not particularly impressed with Max Dowman’s culinary abilities and indicated that the young player has much to improve in the kitchen.

He also disclosed some of the antics of the players off the field. Tommy Setford was picked as the player who occupies the front of the mirror for the longest time, while Piero Hincapie received the title of the most active player on TikTok.

One of the more unusual questions Raya was asked was if he would pick a teammate to be stuck in a desert with.

He said he would pick Jurrien Timber due to his intelligence. This is a glowing compliment for the Dutch defender who has become an important part of Arteta’s defensive options.

The answers given by Raya give us a fun insight into the relationships within the Arsenal squad. Whilst the players are fighting for major honours, there is clearly no shortage of banter between them.
