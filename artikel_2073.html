Blackburn Rovers 2- 1 West Ham | Boos and ironic cheers highlight massive concern

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

THERE was an ironic cheer from the West Ham away supporters at about 88 minutes, which considering their team was 2- 1 down and looking clueless was even more remarkable.

The cheer was for Mads Hermansen finally making a save, with a shot hit straight at him being pretty much the first time he’d connected with anything, as another two goals had found their way under and past the hapless Dane.

7000 West Ham fans had made the trip to Blackburn in expectation of a triumphant start to Parker’s era and yet saw their starting eleven dominate the ball but fail to score. Hermansen had dealt with a smattering of booing from his own fans ever since Rovers took the lead with a header that went between the Dane’s legs.

Times’ up, Mads. The big concern was that Scott Parker picked him ahead of Areola. We told him….

Hardly a confidence boost for the young keeper who surely should not have been selected in the first place.

No criticism through could be attached to Hermansen for Blackburn’s second goal but West Ham found themselves two goals behind, chasing the game and seemingly nothing to offer, back passing and fiddling around in the last few minutes.

Parker can have no excuse now for continuing to select his hapless keeper. The Dane plays as if he’s got arthritic knees (take it fromm one who knows) and doesn’t dive for anything, just collapsing to the left or right, week after week.
