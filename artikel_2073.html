Pochettino identifies January transfer priority as Manchester United’s goals dry up

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Manchester United head coach Mauricio Pochettino reportedly wants to sign a striker in the January transfer window, with Benjamin Sesko not expected to return from injury until the new year at the earliest.

The Argentine’s first match in charge ended in a 4-0 defeat at Liverpool on Sunday, leaving United 19th in the Premier League with just one victory from their opening 11 matches. While defensive shortcomings attracted much of the criticism following the defeat at Anfield, strengthening United’s attack appears to be a priority for the new head coach.

According to The Times, United’s hierarchy and Pochettino agree that the squad requires strengthening in January, despite the club’s preference for conducting major transfer business during the summer. Sesko’s prolonged absence has limited United’s attacking options, while Joshua Zirkzee has scored just 11 goals in 86 appearances since joining from Bologna’s Serie A rivals Fiorentina in 2024.

Monaco striker Folarin Balogun has been identified as a potential target. The United States international worked under Pochettino at international level and has expressed a desire to leave the French club.

Writing in The Independent, Miguel Delaney suggests United could have scope to spend £150m in January, although such expenditure would affect the club’s longer-term recruitment plans. That figure does not represent a confirmed transfer budget, and United have yet to announce any agreement to sign a new striker.

Pochettino’s immediate challenge is finding a solution with his existing squad. United face Sporting in the Champions League on Wednesday before returning to domestic action against Brentford.

With more than a month before the transfer window opens, the Argentine has little choice but to find a way of improving United’s attacking output with the players already at his disposal.
