Two positions West Ham boss Scott Parker wants to strengthen in the January transfer window

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

West Ham opted for change earlier this month as they parted ways with Nuno Espirito Santo to bring in Scott Parker as boss. The former three-time WHUFC Player of the Year will be expected to deliver improvements with his current squad, but new signings will be sought in the winter transfer window too.

According to Alan Nixon, the Hammers’ boss is eager to add new options at left-back and striker when January comes around. His preference is to sign a centre-forward with Championship experience, while a ‘specialist’ is left full-back is sought given that Morato is primarily a centre-back while Ezra Mayers and Ollie Scarles are only young.

Parker has tasked scouts with finding standout targets in both positions and the money is there to spend. It remains to be seen just who emerges on the radar with around six weeks until the window opens.

Parker hasn’t even been at West Ham for two weeks, but he already has a good idea of where they need to strengthen if they are to achieve their goal of promotion back to the Premier League.

And, with reinforcements sought at striker and left-back, many supporters will be behind the boss too. Striker has been a problem area with none of Joel Piroe, Taty Castellanos or Pablo proving they can be the Hammers’ leading talisman as of yet.

There are options at left-back, but Morato felt very much like a Nuno player given their past spell together at Nottingham Forest. His defence-first game suited the former boss’ style too.

Youngsters Mayers and Scarles may hope to prove they can step up for Parker, but a specialist left-back would not go amiss. It could also mean the writing is on the wall for Adama Boiro, who has not played since his debut on September 5th.

Parker has won three promotions from the Championship, and he is targeting a fourth with West Ham. Given his past success, the club hierarchy will be more than willing to put faith in his transfer opinions ahead of January.

WHUFC seem set to heed his advice and target reinforcements at both striker and left-back ahead of January. There are already plenty of players in these positions, but with neither locked down by a clear standout, there is a need for ruthlessness in upgrading in both areas.

Not only will it strengthen the squad, but it will help Parker make his mark too. Players signed specifically for his setup and tactics will be backed to come in and make a swift impact as the Hammers take action in the bid to win promotion.
