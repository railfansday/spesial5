Interesting to read through these BBC Sport ‘neutrals’ comments, following on from Newcastle United drawing 1-1 at Fulham.

A very open match with plenty of action at both ends of the pitch.

Newcastle United though with the clearer chances and the better team in the second half.

Jacob Murphy with a superb left foot long range strike that looked to have won it on 80 minutes, only for Fulham to hit back with an equaliser three minutes later as United failed to clear their lines and a couple of deflections aided Fulham.

Referee Rob Jones was woeful as usual with Newcastle United, looking to give Fulham as many decisions as possible.

Four games in London for Matthias Jaissle in all competitions and still unbeaten, three wins and a draw in the capital.

Always interesting to hear the neutral perspectives, as well as the perspectives of fans of Fulham, Newcastle United and…Sunderland.
