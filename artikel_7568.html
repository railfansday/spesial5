TikTok Shop Plus is a subscription tier that unlocks shopping perks for $9.99 per month

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

TikTok Shop is delivering a perk for shoppers this holiday season: a new subscription service.

It's called TikTok Shop Plus, and it brings TikTok's ecommerce wing in line with its competitors. Similar to Walmart+ and Amazon Prime, TikTok Shop Plus will reward subscribers with deals, shipping discounts, and coupons that will refresh on a regular basis.

TikTok is commodifying its ecommerce platform around the world

Initially, Shop launched as an offshoot of TikTok, but in recent months, the app's commercial wing has been positioned as a standalone product.
