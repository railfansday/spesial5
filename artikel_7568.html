New Mexico Supreme Court permanently disbars prosecutor after disciplinary findings

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The New Mexico Supreme Court, after hearing from a disciplinary board, has permanently disbarred a prosecutor who was found to have pattern of misconduct that denied defendants fair trials. (Image from Shutterstock)

The New Mexico Supreme Court, after hearing from a disciplinary board, has permanently disbarred a prosecutor who was found to have pattern of misconduct that denied defendants fair trials.

Cosme Ripol, a prosecutor from the district attorney’s office for the Eighth Judicial District in Taos, New Mexico, was disbarred Monday, effective immediately.

All five justices on the state supreme court agreed on the disciplinary board’s recommendation. The proceedings focused on three criminal cases that Ripol prosecuted between 2022 and 2024.

According to coverage by the Santa Fe New Mexican, a pivotal case in the investigation was State v. Desiree Lensegrav, where the defendant was convicted in 2022 of first-degree murder, kidnapping, conspiracy and tampering with evidence.

The New Mexico Supreme Court vacated Lensegrav’s convictions after it learned that Ripol, who had been licensed to practice law in the state since 1986, used inadmissible evidence and made derogatory statements about the defendant during the trial.

“As the supreme court noted, prosecutors are held to a higher standard because of their unique responsibility to seek justice,” said Marcus Montoya, the district attorney for the Eighth Judicial District, in a statement Monday. “When improper tactics are employed in the courtroom, even by an attorney with more than 30 years of criminal law experience, that standard is not met.”
