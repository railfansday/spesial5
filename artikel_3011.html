Rocky Mountain volleyball returning to state for first time in 8 years

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

After an eight-year wait, a late-night regional final was worth another wait.

Rocky Mountain High School's volleyball team made sure of that, winning its Colorado Class 5A home regional on Nov. 7 and making the state tournament for the first time since 2018.

Emotions poured out on the floor afterwards as the clock reached 11 p.m. Exhaustion. Elation. Tears. Joy let out with a Lobo roar.

Rocky is heading back to state, continuing a special season that is culminating with goals checked off one by one.

"Every practice, we write out our daily, weekly and monthly goals. Every monthly one has been to make state. The girls put their heart and soul into making this happen," Rocky head coach Matt Duran said.

No. 7 Rocky Mountain (21-4 overall) can add another realistic goal to the list now: a state championship.

The Lobos beat Grand Junction 3-1 (23-25, 25-23, 25-11, 25-14) in an afternoon matinee with school still in session.

They waited through a lengthy middle match that Fountain Fort-Carson won, setting up a late-night party that doubled as a regional final in 'The Den.'

The Trojans pushed Rocky hard in an intense first set. Once the home team prevailed 25-21, the ball was rolling downhill. The Lobos took the second 25-18, bounced back from a tight 23-25 third set loss and dominated the final frame 25-15.

A healthy student section stuck around, rallying Rocky back from that third set defeat and storming the court in celebration once it was all over.

"I literally can't say enough about our student section. Being here at 11 on a Friday night is insane, that's crazy," Rocky senior Grace Bovaird said.

These Lobos have earned that support all fall.

They became the first city team to knock off Fossil Ridge since 2018, bested the SaberCats for the Front Range League title with a 13-1 conference mark and earned a top-10 seed in Class 5A.

It paid off with a rocking home regional that sent away a sensational senior class in style, including Bovaird, Anya Rainbolt, Sydney Daniels, Haven Gaustad, Avery Bartholomew and Kaitlynn Jacobson.

"I tore my ACL last year. To be able to come back and help my team to make state for the first time in forever and our seniors going out with a bang, it's just a huge accomplishment," Bovaird said.

"This is the first group I've had for four years," Duran added. "I'm the coach but I'm also just such a fanboy for this team."

The Lobos are finally off to the Denver Coliseum again. They'll have plenty of time to rest up from the long regional day ahead of the Nov. 12-14 state tournament.

Can Rocky possibly dream even bigger after an already-magical ride?

"Nobody really expects us to be this good. I think we can get pretty far and I'm really excited to see what we can do (at state)… This is what we want, to play high-level volleyball and win," Bovaird said.

Chris Abshire covers high school and community sports for the Coloradoan.
