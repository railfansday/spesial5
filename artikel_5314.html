NEAR Mobile Hits No. 1 in Finance App Rankings Across 38 Countries as NEAR Ecosystem Gains Momentum

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

NEAR Mobile, the largest mobile wallet in the NEAR ecosystem, is gaining international traction — appearing in App Store rankings across 38 countries over a recent 31-day period, according to app ranking data from Toplify. The milestone arrives alongside the launch of gasless cross-chain swaps spanning 120+ assets and 35+ blockchains, powered by NEAR Intents.

The self-custodial wallet reached #1 in Finance in Serbia, #2 in Hungary, and #4 in both Moldova and Oman — with recent appearances at #7 in Finland and Nigeria — underscoring its growing visibility across international markets.

The momentum reflects rising attention around the NEAR ecosystem and its chain abstraction infrastructure. NEAR Intents, the protocol powering cross-chain execution across the ecosystem, has now surpassed $33 billion in cumulative volume across 35 blockchains.

With more than one million downloads, NEAR Mobile continues to expand its capabilities through version 4.0.0, introducing a gasless experience designed to make blockchain interactions dramatically simpler.

As its international reach grows, NEAR Mobile’s latest update targets one of crypto’s biggest usability hurdles: gas fees.

Traditionally, users interacting with multiple blockchains must hold different native tokens just to pay transaction fees—even when they already own the assets they want to transfer or swap. With version 4.0.0, NEAR Mobile sponsors relayer fees for supported transactions, letting users swap and move assets across supported networks without holding NEAR or maintaining native gas tokens on each chain.

Powered by NEAR Intents, the wallet provides access to more than 120 assets across 35+ blockchains — combining cross-chain execution, unified balances, staking, and self-custodial asset management in a single mobile interface.

Beyond removing gas complexity, NEAR Mobile tackles another persistent challenge: fragmented liquidity.

A user holding USDC across Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, and TRON traditionally juggles separate balances, often requiring manual bridging and network switching to use them together. Through NEAR Intents, NEAR Mobile can present supported liquidity from multiple networks as a single unified balance — allowing users to send or swap aggregated assets without bridging funds first. Cross-chain swaps can settle in seconds, bringing the experience closer to using one financial account than navigating a dozen independent blockchains.

By abstracting away networks, bridges, and gas tokens, NEAR Mobile puts NEAR’s chain abstraction technology within reach of everyday users.

With over one million downloads and rising visibility across international Finance rankings, NEAR Mobile has established itself as a leading consumer application within the NEAR ecosystem.

Available on both iOS and Android, the self-custodial wallet lets users create and manage NEAR accounts, send and receive assets, stake NEAR, and access multichain functionality directly from their phones. Onboarding is available through Google, Apple ID, or email — pairing a familiar mobile experience with self-custody and on-chain account recovery — while experienced users can create or import wallets via a traditional mnemonic phrase. The wallet also offers NEAR staking and access to NPRO staking, currently offering up to 8.58% APY.

As NEAR’s chain abstraction ecosystem continues to expand, NEAR Mobile aims to make that infrastructure accessible through an intuitive, consumer-facing app.

NEAR Mobile is the largest self-custodial mobile wallet in the NEAR ecosystem, with more than 1 million downloads across iOS and Android. Powered by NEAR Intents, the wallet lets users manage NEAR, stake assets, and access more than 120 tokens across 35+ blockchains through gasless cross-chain functionality and unified balances. Its mission is to make NEAR’s chain abstraction infrastructure accessible to everyone by removing the complexity of networks, bridges, and gas tokens.
