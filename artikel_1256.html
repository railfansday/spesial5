Maresca: Man City need one thing against Napoli, I ‘still follow’ my favourite Italian club

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Manchester City coach Enzo Maresca says his team will need to recover ‘defensive balance’ against Napoli, but their Champions League meeting won’t feel like a derby, despite him being a Salernitana fan.

Manchester City host Napoli in the Champions League on Tuesday, and the Premier League giants’ coach Enzo Maresca previewed the game during an interview with Sky Sport Italia.

Manchester City are winless in their last four Premier League matches, grabbing three defeats and one draw.

Despite their poor run of form, they remain third in the table, six points below leaders Arsenal.

“We need to find our defensive balance. We’re creating so much and giving away so little, but unfortunately we’re conceding goals from those few chances,” Maresca said.

“We definitely need that defensive balance: my teams have always been good at keeping clean sheets. It’s certainly something we need to improve. I’ve watched a lot of Napoli’s games, against Lazio and Torino. They’re in good shape, they create chances, and they’re physically strong, with quality players who can hurt you at any moment, like De Bruyne, McTominay, Hojlund, and Lang. We’ll have to be careful.

“We’ll see whether Napoli sit in a low block,” Maresca continued.

“They might, but when they do press, they’re quite aggressive, so we need to be careful.”

What will Maresca and Man City players need to do to get the best out of Erling Haaland against the Partenopei?

“The aim is to get as many balls into the box for him as possible,” Maresca replied.

“We have a lot of tired players, so we’ll see who plays.”

After their game against Napoli, Manchester City will play Arsenal in the Premier League. Will it affect their preparation for the game?

“Not at all, also because tomorrow is Tuesday and the Arsenal game is on Sunday, four days later,” Maresca said.

“There will be time to recover. Like Napoli, we have to try to win tomorrow and keep ourselves well placed in the table.”

Kevin De Bruyne will reunite with his former club for the second time in two years. The Belgian and Maresca spent one season together at Manchester City in 2022-23, when they ultimately won the treble, beating Inter in the Champions League Final. Back then, Maresca was Pep Guardiola’s assistant.

“When De Bruyne has the ball, anything can happen: a great pass or a great shot,” Maresca said.

“He’s one of those players who see things others don’t. I have no doubt he’ll do well at Napoli. If he’s happy off the pitch, that carries over onto it, and if he’s enjoying himself, everyone else enjoys themselves too. No, I haven’t spoken to him.”

Maresca was born in Pontecagnano Faiano, just 60km away from Napoli. As a kid, he’d go to the stadium to watch Salernitana, one of Napoli’s harshest rivals. Will it feel like a derby for him? The Granata are now playing in Italy’s third division, Serie C.

“I was born a Salernitana fan, and I’m still a Salernitana fan,” Maresca said, smiling.

“We’re not going through a good spell, but I’ve been away for so long that I don’t pay as much attention [to the rivalry] any more. I still follow Salernitana, and hopefully in a few years we can get back to Serie B or Serie A.”

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
