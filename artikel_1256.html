Lazio give injury updates for Frattesi, Zaccagni and Nuno Tavares

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Lazio have given injury updates on Davide Frattesi, Mattia Zaccagni and Nuno Tavares, with positive news for all three ahead of the return of Serie A fixtures this weekend.

The Biancocelesti went into the break for international duty as Serie A leaders, and will be back on the field on Saturday afternoon when hosting struggling Lecce at the Stadio Olimpico.

Frattesi scored for Italy in the 3-1 Nations League victory over France at San Siro, but then pulled out of the squad for the trip to Belgium due to a muscular injury.

The midfielder underwent tests that confirmed a muscle strain, but the good news is that there were no lesions.

That means he will be monitored over the next few days, and could well be available against Lecce on Saturday.

Zaccagni was ruled out of the Italy squad entirely due to a left quadricep injury, and the scan showed improvement in the condition, so over the next few days could be brought back into full training.

As for Nuno Tavares, he limped off with an injury in the game with Napoli, and is continuing the scheduled physiotherapy programme, as Lazio still expect to be without him for three weeks.
