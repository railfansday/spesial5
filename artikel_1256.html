Ben Jacobs: Tottenham Could Sign Ivan Toney On One Condition

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Tottenham could make a move to sign Ivan Toney during the January transfer window, on one condition, according to GIVEMESPORT sources.

Signing a new centre-forward could be one of Spurs' priorities in the upcoming window.

Although Tottenham brought in Omar Marmoush from Manchester City in the summer, they have lost Brazilian forward Richarlison as an option.

The former Everton man was close to a departure in the summer but remained at Tottenham. Roberto De Zerbi then opted to not name him in his Premier League squad, meaning he hasn't been involved with the first team this season.

As a result, he's likely to depart in January, so we could see Tottenham look to sign a replacement.

Dominic Solanke has also been incredibly inconsistent since his move to Spurs, so signing a prolific forward could be a smart move.

Speaking on the GIVEMESPORT Market Madness podcast, senior correspondent Ben Jacobs has confirmed that Tottenham could be interested in signing Toney in January.

It will depend on whether a loan is possible for the England international and, of course, if he's keen on leaving the club.

"I would imagine if Toney says that suddenly he's not happy in Saudi Arabia, and it's an increasingly volatile, political situation there, if a loan becomes available, clubs like Man Utd or even Spurs may be prepared to entertain that one. It's kind of too early to tell. One, because we have to see whether the Saudi Pro League continues to be affected by politics, and two, because Toney has always said, certainly when Matthias Jaissle was at Al-Ahli, that he was happy in Saudi. If that changes in the winter market, then a move to the Premier League could be of interest."

Signing a player of Toney's calibre on loan would certainly be a smart move for Tottenham.

Of course, the wages would be a concern, but if they can secure his signature in a deal that makes sense financially, he'd certainly benefit De Zerbi's squad.

Well, they're playing in Saudi. Toney is still banging in the goals, with 15 in 15 games so far this season.

Spurs are ready to sanction their first January transfer window deal

If Toney, described as 'one of the best strikers in the world by Al-Ahil sporting director Rui Pedro, does become available on loan in the January transfer window, you'd imagine a host of different clubs will show interest in securing his signature.

He could be the perfect addition for many sides, especially in the middle of the season when you just need some extra firepower up top.

Manchester United, for example, could certainly do with another striker, while he might look to reunite with Thomas Frank at Crystal Palace.

Tottenham would likely have to offer him guaranteed minutes, and it will be interesting to see who De Zerbi thinks is his first choice if Toney does arrive.
