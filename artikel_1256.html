Genoa defender Ostigard spends night in hospital with high fever

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Genoa defender Leo Ostigard spent Monday night in hospital after suffering a sudden high fever, although tests have since ruled out anything serious.

According to ANSA, the Norway international was taken to Genoa’s San Martino Hospital in the early hours, after the fever came with a severe headache.

He was examined by infectious disease specialist Professor Matteo Bassetti and his team. The routine diagnostic tests came back reassuring and ruled out any significant medical problems. Ostigard will nonetheless remain under observation at the hospital for the rest of today.

The scare came just a day after one of his best performances of the season. Ostigard was among Genoa’s standout players as Daniele De Rossi’s side won 2-0 at Sassuolo on Sunday, with Milutin Osmajić scoring both goals and the defence keeping a clean sheet.

That result made it three wins in four league games for the Grifone, who are 10th on 16 points.

Genoa will hope to have their defender back for a demanding week. They visit Inter at San Siro on Saturday at 20:45 CET [19:45 GMT], before facing the Nerazzurri again in the Coppa Italia next Tuesday.
