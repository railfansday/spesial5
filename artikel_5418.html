TJX raised its full-year outlook and expects holiday sales to accelerate

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The T.J. Maxx parent expects full-year comparable sales growth of 4%, versus a prior forecast of 3% to 4%, after a 4% third-quarter gain

TJX Companies $TJX raised its full-year outlook on Wednesday, projecting comparable sales growth of 4% for the fiscal year after the owner of T.J. Maxx, Marshalls and HomeGoods reported third-quarter results above its plan.

CEO and President Ernie Herrman said unseasonably mild conditions across the U.S. and Canada have softened sales in certain categories, though the company expects momentum to build through the holiday season, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Third-quarter comparable sales rose 4%. Net sales rose 6% to $15.95 billion, and net income was $1.54 billion, or $1.39 per diluted share, up 9% from a year earlier. Adjusted earnings, which exclude a benefit from tariff refunds, were $1.34 per share, up 5%.

Comparable sales at Marmaxx, the division that includes T.J. Maxx, Marshalls and Sierra in the U.S., rose 2% on top of a 6% gain a year earlier. HomeGoods, TJX Canada and TJX International also posted gains. Herrman expects further improvement at Marmaxx in the fourth quarter, according to The Wall Street Journal.

For the fourth quarter, TJX plans consolidated comparable sales growth of 2% to 3%. It expects a pretax profit margin of 11.5% to 11.6%, or 11.8% to 11.9% excluding costs tied to tariff refunds, and adjusted earnings of $1.42 to $1.45 per share.

For the full year, TJX expects a pretax profit margin of 12.4%. It continues to expect an adjusted pretax margin of 12.0% to 12.1%. The company expects adjusted earnings of $5.17 to $5.20 per share, which raises the low end of a prior range of $5.15 to $5.20.

TJX received $132 million in refunds of tariffs it paid under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act during the third quarter and $463 million over the first nine months. The net benefit to third-quarter pretax profit was $77 million. The company does not expect additional refunds in the fourth quarter. Adjusted pretax profit margin for the third quarter was 12.5%, down 0.2 percentage point from a year earlier. TJX returned $1.5 billion to shareholders through buybacks and dividends during the quarter.

TJX stock rose 6.6% to $138.42 in premarket trading.

In August, TJX raised its full-year profit forecast after second-quarter results while keeping its comparable sales outlook at 3% to 4%. Marmaxx comparable sales rose 1% in the second quarter. The company guided to third-quarter adjusted earnings of $1.30 to $1.32 per share.
