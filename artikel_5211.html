PrimeXBT Launches Anthropic Pre-IPO Crypto Futures, Opening New AI Trading Opportunities

PrimeXBT, a global multi-asset broker and crypto asset service provider, has announced the launch of Pre-IPO Trading — a new category within its Crypto Futures offering — starting with ANT/USDT. The instrument lets traders trade the market-implied pre-IPO valuation of Anthropic, the company behind Claude and one of the year’s most anticipated listings, before it goes public.

ANT/USDT is a USDT-margined perpetual Crypto Futures contract available on PrimeXBT’s native PXTrader 2.0 platform. Traders can open long or short positions, trade 24/7 including weekends, and use leverage of up to 1:5, with a minimum order size of 0.01 contract.

Prices are set through a live order book — reflecting market expectations rather than any official company valuation. As part of PrimeXBT’s Crypto Futures, Pre-IPO Trading carries competitive fees of 0.01% for makers and from 0.045% for takers.

Anthropic is expected to begin trading on Nasdaq on November 19 under the ticker ANT, with an indicative IPO price range of $70–$78 per share. Upon listing, PrimeXBT plans to transition ANT/USDT into a standard perpetual Crypto Futures contract linked to the listed stock. If the IPO is canceled, the contract will be delisted and open positions may be closed or settled at a reference price, with advance notice to clients. The contract does not convert into actual shares.

“AI is driving some of the most significant developments in financial markets, with Anthropic at the centre of that momentum,” said Jonatan Randin, CMT, Senior Market Analyst at PrimeXBT. “Pre-IPO Trading gives our clients a new way to participate in major market trends, making emerging trading opportunities more accessible through innovative technology and competitive trading conditions.”

The launch builds on PrimeXBT’s offering of more than 170 Crypto Futures markets, including contracts linked to major technology companies such as Tesla, NVIDIA, SpaceX, Circle, and Robinhood. Clients can access Pre-IPO Trading alongside cryptocurrencies and CFDs through a single PrimeXBT account, with fiat- and crypto-denominated account options in USDT, USDC, BTC, and ETH—letting traders stay crypto-native and access multiple markets without converting holdings to fiat or funding a separate brokerage account.

With the launch of Anthropic Pre-IPO Trading, PrimeXBT further strengthens its position as a leading multi-asset trading provider — bridging crypto and traditional financial markets, and expanding trading possibilities across AI, technology, and equity markets.
