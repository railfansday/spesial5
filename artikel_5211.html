Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse has linked the recent slide in XRP and Bitcoin to investors pulling cash out of crypto to buy into Anthropic’s record IPO. Speaking to CNBC in Singapore on Thursday, Garlinghouse said part of the selloff came from money shifting into large stock market listings rather than investors giving up on digital assets. He expects prices to recover once the IPO excitement settles, pointing to the pattern after SpaceX went public in June. “What we saw after the SpaceX IPO was a rally in the crypto markets,” he said, adding that he believes the same could happen again. Anthropic raised $81.3 billion in its public offering, plus $26 billion through a private placement, making it the largest IPO ever. Its shares opened 34% above the $83 offer price on Thursday. XRP traded near $0.96 to $0.97 during the dip, well below its $1.55 high earlier this autumn.
