Having redeemed itself on streaming after a disappointing box-office run, Guy Ritchie's latest theatrical release has relinquished its streaming crown. Like every other movie directed by Ritchie in the last five years, the new crime thriller underperformed at the box office. However, it's clear that Ritchie's films essentially use their theatrical runs to market their eventual home-video releases. That's where they thrive, as has the new crime thriller for several months.

The movie reunited Ritchie with three stars he's worked with before — Eiza González, Jake Gyllenhaal, and Henry Cavill. He directed González and Cavill in The Ministry of Ungentlemanly Warfare, which grossed around $30 million worldwide against a reported budget of $60 million. She also starred in his Apple TV movie Fountain of Youth. He directed Gyllenhaal in his best-reviewed movie ever, The Covenant, which made $22 million against a reported budget of $55 million.

However, the new crime thriller was arguably an even bigger flop​​​​​​​, given its higher reported budget of $70 million. The film grossed around $27 million worldwide; of this total, only $5.7 million came from the United States, making it the lowest-grossing Ritchie film in more than two decades.
