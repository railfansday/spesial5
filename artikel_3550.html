Hollow Knight: Silksong Studio Breaks Silence On Sea Of Sorrow DLC With A Showcase Announcement

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Team Cherry has posted. I repeat, Team Cherry has posted. The Hollow Knight: Silksong developer has announced that it will hold a livestream event on Friday, November 27th on YouTube. Tune in at 10 AM ET for what Team Cherry says will be an update on Silksong DLC Sea of Sorrow, plus a bunch of other “exciting” announcements.

Hollow Knight: Silksong released over a year ago, and last December the devs at Team Cherry teased that the game would get a nautical-themed DLC sometime in 2026. The majority of 2026 has now come and gone, but there’s still a month and some change left in the year so technically we’re still within the release window.

Now the announcement could very well be that Sea of Sorrow has to be delayed into 2027. Because this livestream is coming so late in the year, I think we have to brace ourselves for that possibility.
