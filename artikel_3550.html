Rockstar Could Have Released GTA 6 Sooner As GTA 5.5, But “You Have To Take Big Swings”

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

After years of development, multiple delays, and loads of hype, Rockstar Games is finally releasing Grand Theft Auto 6 on November 19. What took so long?

According to Rockstar publishing executive Jennifer Kolbe, the team at Rockstar "could have made GTA 5.5, and done it faster." However, "Ultimately, that's not what they wanted," she told The Guardian.

Rockstar boss Rob Nelson, meanwhile, said it was important for the studio to go above and beyond with GTA 6, and that takes time. "You have to take big swings. It can’t be just more of the same," he said.

GTA 6 looks to be a big step up from GTA 5 in a number of ways, including the visuals and technical improvements. Beyond that, the story this time around is a Bonnie-and-Clyde-style narrative about a crime-loving couple--Jason and Lucia. It's also the first time Rockstar is releasing a GTA game with a playable female protagonist.

GTA 6 took so long to come out because Rockstar is seeking "perfection," according to Take-Two. “The team at Rockstar really does seek to do something that’s never been done before. That’s really hard and it takes a long time,” he said.

Rockstar and its owner, Take-Two, stand to benefit greatly from what's expected to be a commercial blockbuster with GTA 6. But could future GTA game releases come at a faster pace? According to Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick, the "nature and velocity" of GTA releases will look "dramatically different" over the next decade, but he didn't share more or commit to any specific timelines.

GTA 6 launches on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. People in Australia can attend a funeral at KFC to get the day off work to play it.

GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time and not simultaneously around the world. This means people in the US will be among the last in the world to get to play.
