Parasite protein expands understanding of how mitochondria build their ribosomes

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Mitochondria are essential components of the cells of most organisms, including humans. They are often referred to as the cell's powerhouses because they produce the energy needed for cellular functions. However, mitochondria originally evolved from free-living bacteria that were engulfed by other cells in the distant past.
