Everything to Know About Ariana Grande’s Upcoming Eternal Sunshine Tour Film

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Ariana Grande’s Eternal Sunshine Tour may have wrapped in September, but fans will soon get another chance to experience it. On November 23, the singer shared a video teasing an upcoming concert film documenting the tour, which is set to premiere on Peacock.

The announcement comes nearly three months after Grande concluded her first concert tour in seven years, an experience she has previously described as a significant chapter in her career.

Here’s everything we know about the project so far.

Grande has yet to share specific details about the project, including whether it will feature a complete concert performance, behind-the-scenes footage, or a combination of both.

Her Eternal Sunshine Tour began in Oakland, California, on June 6 and concluded on September 1 following 10 performances at London’s O2 Arena. The shows featured music from across her discography, including Eternal Sunshine, Positions, and her latest album, Petal, which was released in July.

The tour also marked Grande’s return to the concert stage following her 2019 Sweetener World Tour. Throughout the run, she performed in custom looks from designers including Alexander McQueen, Givenchy, and Vivienne Westwood, styled by Law Roach.

Grande shared the first teaser on Instagram on November 23, in collaboration with Peacock and Team Ariana.

The video features footage of Grande performing the title track from Eternal Sunshine. As the performance ends, the screen reveals the message: “Coming soon to… Peacock.”

During an August interview with Vosk in the City, Grande reflected on how her latest tour differed from her previous experiences performing live.

“I think my last tour I wasn’t necessarily in the same place in life, and I wasn’t able to be as present as I am this time,” she explained, describing the Eternal Sunshine Tour as “such a beautiful corrective experience.”

Grande also spoke about reconnecting with longtime fans through songs spanning more than a decade of her career.

In August, her representative confirmed that the singer planned to step away from public-facing work following the tour. Grande later addressed the decision during a concert in Chicago, explaining that it had been made well in advance.

“It is something that I have decided; it’s a plan that I had quietly made a long time ago, and it’s a decision that was made from a thoughtful and empowered place,” she told the audience.

An official release date has yet to be announced. For now, Grande’s teaser only confirms that the film is coming soon to Peacock.
