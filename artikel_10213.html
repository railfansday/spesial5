This Fitbit Air app buzzes your wrist with phone notifications, finds a lost tracker, more

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Google’s Fitbit Air is a simple screenless fitness tracker, but it can do a bit more, and now someone has built an app that lets the Air buzz your wrist when phone notifications come through.

The era of vibe-coding has been very quickly bringing ideas to reality, and these new Fitbit Air notification apps are a great example.

“Air Relay” is the result of a developer managing to reverse-engineer a way to control haptics on the Fitbit Air via Bluetooth without affecting Google Health sync. “Cuff” uses the same method while also showing health stats if you don’t want to use Google Health.

The idea here is pretty simple. You can have apps on your phone ping your Fitbit Air as you choose. “Air Relay” lets you pick select apps and even change the vibration pattern or strength based on the app. You can set “Quiet hours” to prevent notifications while you’re sleeping, and there’s a WhatsApp-specific setting that lets you only get notifications from specific groups or people. A “Find My Air” setting uses Bluetooth strength you help you track down a lost tracker.

While a big part of the appeal of Fitbit Air is its screenless, disconnected nature, having the option to set up alerts for important apps or contacts is certainly a useful option.

“Air Relay” is free on Google Play, and Fitbit Air is available for $99.
