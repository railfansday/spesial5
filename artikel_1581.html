Report: Chelsea's attempt to ruin Liverpool transfer plan revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Liverpool's biggest need in the upcoming transfer window is a midfielder.

The Reds have enjoyed a fairly strong start to life under Andoni Iraola, but strengthening the midfield would transform them into legitimate title contenders.

One of the biggest criticisms aimed at the team this season has been that the midfield lacks balance, and that has cost Iraola's side at times.

As a result several top midfielders have been linked with a Merseyside move ahead of the winter window, but Liverpool aren't the only team looking to reinforce their midfield in the New Year and a bitter rival could ruin the Reds' transfer plans.

Two of the names most frequently linked with Liverpool in recent months are Bournemouth's Alex Scott and AS Monaco's Lamine Camara, but the Reds are facing tough competition for both.

Multiple top teams across Europe are also monitoring the pair, including some Premier League sides, and interest is only growing as the transfer window edges closer.

Chelsea are one of the sides interested in both players, and now the Blues are reportedly considering signing both midfielders in January.

That's according to a report from TEAMtalk, which claims that Chelsea will not rule out moving for both players in one window.

The Blues' start to the season hasn't gone to plan, and they are preparing to right the wrongs of the summer window this winter.

It would come as a massive shock to see Liverpool move for both players in the same window, but the expectation is that at least one of the pair will be a priority target for the Anfield recruitment team.

To miss out on both players to the same team, a rival nonetheless, would be an unmitigated disaster, and the hierarchy at Anfield must act quickly to avoid the worst-case scenario.

Chelsea need more than just two midfielders to regain their place as a Premier League contender, but signing the pair would help them and massively hurt Liverpool.
