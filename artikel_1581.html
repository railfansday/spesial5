Svilar announces return from injury: “I’m back.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Mile Svilar is preparing for his return to the pitch and sending a message to Roma fans.

The Giallorossi goalkeeper posted a story on his Instagram profile with a photo taken during today’s training session at Trigoria, accompanied by a single word: “Back.”

The Belgian is continuing his recovery from a microstress fracture in his foot and also worked only partially with the rest of the team in Wednesday’s session.

Svilar still experiences some discomfort when kicking the ball, which is why his participation at the Tardini remains in doubt.
