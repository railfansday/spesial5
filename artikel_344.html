Meghan Markle Makes First Public Appearance Since Her Father’s Arrest

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Duchess of Sussex accompanied her husband, Prince Harry, in Brussels at an event about online safety for children.

Meghan, the Duchess of Sussex, made a public appearance in Brussels on Tuesday, the first since her father was arrested on charges related to the possession of child sexual abuse images.

Meghan was with her husband, Prince Harry, who spoke at the European Parliament for an event about digital safety and child protection. Meghan did not speak at the event, though she had planned to introduce Harry, according to Liam Maguire, a spokesman for the duchess. Mr. Maguire declined to comment further.

The duke and duchess were also attending the opening of the Lost Screen Memorial, a photo campaign by their Archewell foundation that commemorates children who died after suffering harm on social media.

Videos showed Meghan arriving at the European Parliament holding hands with Harry. She passed flashing cameras and reporters shouting out questions. She did not engage.

Thomas Markle Sr., who is estranged from Meghan, was arrested in Florida on Friday. According to the U.S. attorney’s office in Los Angeles, the arrest came after law enforcement officials discovered sexually explicit photographs of him with prepubescent girls in two Los Angeles-area storage units that he had rented for nearly two decades.

Last month, before Mr. Markle’s arrest, Meghan said she was appalled by the allegations surrounding him, which she said helped explain why she had kept her children away from him. Meghan first learned of allegations against her father around eight years ago when a letter was sent to the unit of London’s Metropolitan Police that was at the time responsible for her security, she said.

Harry and Meghan, and their two children, moved back to Britain from California in August. Since then, the duke and duchess have largely laid low with relatively few public appearances.

Late last month, they spent a day in Nottingham, a city in England’s Midlands, in what was Meghan’s first public appearance since her return to the country.

Claire Moses is a Times reporter in London, focused on coverage of breaking and trending news.
