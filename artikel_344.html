Best Robot Vacuum Early Black Friday Deals from Roborock (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Robot vacuums are more expensive than ever, with many models from popular brands like Dyson and Shark starting at $1,200. But you usually don't have to pay such a high price tag for a great model—you just have to wait for a good sale, and with early Black Friday deals already kicking off, it's one of the best times to shop for one.

One of our favorite robot vacuum brands, Roborock, began its early Black Friday deals yesterday, and some of our favorite models we've tested are available for the best prices we've seen. Here's which models I'd snatch up while the prices are low.

The Roborock Qrevo 2 Pro launched in late summer as the newest addition to the brand's more affordable Qrevo line, and it quickly landed as a pick on my guide to the Best Robot Vacuums. It's often on sale for around $600, and thanks to Black Friday it's down to the lowest price I've seen it for: $450, nearly half off.

Overall, Roborock's vacuums are great, but I've been especially impressed by this one's corner cleaning, thanks to its extendable mop arm. It won't always spot cords, but it does a better job at navigating furniture and getting underneath my cabinets than more expensive robot vacuums I've tested.

If you'd rather have a roller-mop-style robot vacuum, the Roborock Qrevo Curv 2 Flow is another great option from Roborock's Qrevo line. This one came out in January of this year, and our reviewer instantly loved it.

The Qrevo Curv 2 Flow is a more expensive robot vacuum than the Qrevo 2 since it comes with the brand's SpriaFlow roller-mop. The internal roller spans nearly the entire width of the vacuum and can extend to edge-clean your home. It even has a built-in scraper to move dirty water into the tank instead of rolling it back onto the floor. If you're looking for an affordable mopping powerhouse, this is the one to choose. It's also nearly half off, down to $600 off the $1,000 price tag.

There's the affordable Qrevo line from Roborock, and then there's the higher-end Saros line. While both I and former WIRED reviewer Adrienne So weren't particularly impressed with the Saros 20, I've been much happier with the performance of the Saros 20 Flow. Like the Curv 2 Flow, it has a roller mop instead of spinning mop pads and did well in my mopping tests. It also didn't leave debris around, even with its high suction of 36,000 Pa.

It's one of Roborock's most expensive vacuums, and it comes across while using it—it feels very premium. I've been preferring it over the new Dyson robot vacuum I've been testing. Luckily, you can get it on sale right now for Black Friday. It still isn't cheap at $1,300, but it's one of my all-time favorites and one I have kept coming back to after trying other models.

If you're looking for something truly unique in a robot vacuum, Roborock's Saros Z70 might be what you're looking for. It has a mechanical arm built into it to pick up small items like socks while it vacuums, which feels handy, but didn't work as well as we'd hoped during testing. Still, maybe you feel that's the solution you've been dying for. If that's the case, it's available for a nice discount right now at $950.

It also packs 22,000 Pa of suction (not as powerful as the Saros 20 Flow, but still plenty of suction to clean carpets and rugs alike!) and still executes fast, effective cleaning. But the battery life on this vacuum isn't as long as that of other Roborocks we've tested. Again, you're mostly getting this one for the mechanical arm.
