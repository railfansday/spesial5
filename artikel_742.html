John Summit Brings Squishmallows to the Club With New Collab: ‘A Dream Come True’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The collection launches the toy's "Signature Series" that will feature limited-edition designs inspired by globally known artists.

Squishmallows are going to the club via John Summit‘s new collaboration with the plush toy brand.

Inspired by the escaping the 9-to-5 themes of Summit’s 2026 CTRL ESCAPE album and its subsequent arena tour, the collection features two limited-edition 10-inch Bull Squishmallows and a keychain plush named “Summit.” These items will be available at select CTRL ESCAPE tour locations and via Summit’s website. See images of the collection exclusively below.

“I’ve been a huge Squishmallows fan for years,” the producer says in a statement. “I have my own collection at my home; it’s like my second family. So when I got the opportunity to be able to create my own, it was honestly surreal. This collaboration launches the “Signature Series” Squishmallows Collection from Squishmallows manufacturer Jazwares that will feature limited-edition designs inspired by globally known artists and other pop culture trends.

“John has been a huge Squishmallows fan, so it was a natural fit for us to collaborate with him and bring something he is truly passionate about to his massive fanbase,” adds Jazwares chief brand officer Gerhard Runken. “Our Squishmallows Signature Series opens the door for new creative exploration with today’s top talent from music, pop culture, fashion, and beyond, giving them a new platform to authentically engage their fandoms like never before.”

Jazwares notes that since Squishmallows launched in 2017, the toy has sold more than 590 million plush units worldwide. Meanwhile, Summit’s CTRL ESCAPE TOUR continues through December, with upcoming stops in Miami, Chicago, Philadelphia, Brooklyn, Oakland and Los Angeles.
