Poch denied first Man Utd win by Lammens howler at Sporting

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Sporting Lisbon vs Manchester United. UEFA Champions League Round 1.

Report as Man Utd self-destruct in bizarre Champions League draw at Sporting after having two goals controversially ruled out; Bruno Fernandes and Luis Suarez both scored two goals, one penalty each, with the Man Utd captain's second goal freakishly bouncing in off a divot in the ground

Mauricio Pochettino was denied a first victory as Manchester United head coach by a goalkeeping howler from Senne Lammens in an incident-packed 2-2 draw with Sporting in the Champions League as Youri Tielemans was sent off late on.

United looked to be on course for a first away win of the season after captain Bruno Fernandes scored two goals in the first five minutes of the second half only for Lammens to spill the ball in the box for Luis Suarez to equalise with his second goal of the game.

Pochettino thought United had gone back in front for a second time shortly after Lammens' error when Sporting goalkeeper Kaique punched into his own net at a corner. However, it was ruled out following an on-field review for a foul by substitute Patrick Dorgu.

It was a damaging night for Lammens, who gave away the penalty for Suarez's first goal just 75 seconds after saving the Sporting striker's poorly taken spot-kick. The man brought into replace the error-prone Andre Onana is now beginning to resemble his predecessor.

Fernandes' equaliser three minutes after the restart came from the third penalty of what was a bizarre match. Few moments topped the United skipper's freakish second goal which came thanks a tame effort bouncing up off a divot in the ground and into back of the net.

Tielemans' stoppage-time red will only add to United's frustrations. It was his first booking, for a tame follow-through on Goncalo Inacio, that denied Amad Diallo from equalising late in the first half in one of the more baffling refereeing decisions you will see.

Pochettino's first two matches have seen his side have two players sent off and concede two penalties. If he did not know it before, he certainly knows now that nothing comes easy at United, who sit 19th in the Champions League and Premier League.

The last thing Pochettino needed after the 4-0 defeat at Liverpool - and Leny Yoro's now overturned red card - was more refereeing controversy. However, there was contention around both goals United had ruled out in Lisbon.

Amad had the ball in the back of the net toward the end of the first half to equalise - but not before the whistle had gone for what was judged to have been a foul by Tielemans, who clearly won the ball with his slide tackle.

The United midfielder received a booking for his follow through on Sporting defender Inacio, who went down dramatically despite receiving the slightest of contact. VAR could not intervene as the whistle had gone.

"It is a terrible decision," said Sky Sports pundit Clinton Morrison. "Tielemans won the ball."

United's second disallowed goal, which was ruled out following VAR intervention, saw Dorgu penalised for making contact with Sporting goalkeeper Kaique before he punched into his own net.

Sky Sports pundit Jamie Carragher felt it was "very harsh" for the referee to disallow the goal after being sent to his monitor.

Sky Sports pundit Jamie Carragher to CBS:

"I do think United were a little bit unlucky tonight with the goal disallowed just before half-time. The goal at the end felt very harsh to me, the one where it could have been 3-2...

"The focus on a Man Utd goalkeeper is always big if he's making mistakes. The back-ups aren't even at the level where you could say no matter how poorly the goalkeeper plays, you could change it. You worry about the confidence of that goalkeeper now.

"That mistake for the equaliser, the 2-2 goal, you say sometimes 'an amateur goalkeeper wouldn't make that mistake' and that just can't happen at this level."

Man Utd head coach Mauricio Pochettino to TNT Sports:

"I'm so proud of the players. It was a tough game. I liked the team we played for 90 minutes, the personality, the character. It's difficult to find a game where you get punished in one game. We were punished a lot in key actions. Today we showed how we want to play after just one week of working together.

"We need to concentrate and focus more, try to recover better. The performance like today's is the best medicine to prepare for the next game."
