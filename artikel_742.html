Ashe Addresses Rumor Her Song "Stop the Wedding!" Is About Finneas

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

After Ashe's hit single "Stop The Wedding!" was released, three weeks before Finneas married Claudia Sulewski, she reacted to claims the track was inspired by unrequited feelings for her collaborator.

Ashe wasn't wasting her night out and wearing her heart out over these romance rumors.

Nearly three months after the singer released her track "Stop The Wedding," she addressed speculations that she was inspired by an unrequited love for longtime friend and collaborator Finneas ahead of his August nuptials to Claudia Sulewski.

"We were in a full band. People were very upset about that," Ashe explained on a recent episode of the Big Small Talk podcast. "Some people thought that I wrote 'Stop the Wedding!' to stop his wedding with Claudia, which is the most insane thing, because I had just gotten married."

The 33-year-old (born Ashlyn Rae Willson)—who tied the knot with John Clark Canada in October 2025—noted that the rumors grew more and more ridiculous over time.

"It's so stupid until it's funny," she explained. "You let yourself get mad about it, and then it becomes funny and then you let it go."

Ashe—who co-released The Dream (2025) with Finneas and The Favors—emphasized that if she had actually written the song about Billie Eilish's older brother, it wouldn't have gotten her guy.

She sardonically added, "So, you're going to pick me now, right? Like what?"

Ashe previously revealed that the song was actually inspired by her "horrifying" marriage to her ex-husband—whom she has not publicly identified—after their 2019 split.

"Do I wish I'd had that advice at the time? 1,000 percent," she told the BBC in October. "If someone had given me the insight that I was not stuck, and that I could run, I could have changed things sooner."

"Maybe not at the altar," she acquiesced. "Maybe weeks before—but it was something I really could have used."

As their relationship continued while her career thrived, she felt her forever falling apart. In fact, he was anything but a fan after she decided to pivot from songwriting—including co-writing Demi Lovato's "You Don't Do It for Me Anymore" in 2017—to hitting all the right notes herself.

"I remember him saying, '[After this] no-one's ever going to love you for you,'" she recalled. "'Anyone you meet now is going to meet you as Ashe, and I met you before.'"

"It was truly manipulative, and it kept me stuck," she admitted. "It kept me stuck for a long, long time."

But Ashe eventually found the guy of her dreams in Canada.

As she put it, "I'm in a happy, healthy relationship and I have total autonomy over my own life."
