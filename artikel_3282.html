Grateful Dead’s Official Blanket, Towel, and Poncho Collab Is 20% Off for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

Outdoor accessories brand Slowtide is putting its latest Grateful Dead collection on sale for Black Friday, with 20% off merch and home goods inspired by the band.

Part of the ongoing collaboration between the Costa Mesa, California, brand and the legendary jam band, the Slowtide x Grateful Dead drop includes throw blankets, towels, ponchos, and bags inspired by popular Deadhead colors and motifs.

The Black Friday sale gets you 20% off almost everything side-wide at Slowtide.co with free shipping on all orders of $100 or more. There is no promo code needed to claim the deal. The sale also arrives just in time for the holiday gifting season, with products from this collab a no-brainer to impress music fans and Deadheads alike.

This cozy throw blanket features a sunburst-like reimagining of the Grateful Dead’s iconic “Steal Your Face” logo.Made from a recycled polyester blend, the oversized blanket measures 55 x 70 inches in size with a super soft, velour-style hand feel.

One of Slowtide’s newest releases is this Grateful Dead blanket, made from a premium plush knit material.The reversible blanket features the band’s logo on one side, and a solid color (dubbed “avo”) on the other. This blanket measures 54 x 72 inches in size.

Slowtide introduced its collection of packable picnic blankets earlier this year, and this Grateful Dead version is ideal for park hangs and music festivals alike.The 72 x 60-inch blanket is machine washable and packs into a lightweight carrying bag when you’re ready to go (reviewers say the storage bag makes a great seat cushion at concerts too). Choose from multiple designs online.

This versatile tote features a super soft terry cloth material with a colorful Dancing Bears print. The tote bag measures 19 x 17.5 inches in size.

You can also pick up the same tote in this classic “Fillmore” print, named after the band’s iconic run of shows in the 1960s at Fillmore West in San Francisco (you can also pick up a vinyl recording of those shows here).

Whether for at-home lounging or festival hangs, the Slowtide x Grateful Dead Fillmore Poncho will keep you cozy.The cotton terry material absorbs moisture (say, if you throw it on after a surf or swim) while a large hood and kangaroo pouch keep your head and hands warm. This is an updated version of a Grateful Dead poncho first released by Slowtide four years ago, with the brand’s designers wanting a heavier-weight, “extra warm” version. Choose from a S/M and a L/XL size.

This quick-dry towel features original artwork by Gordon Thomas, VP of Creative at Warner Music Group and a self-proclaimed Dead Head.The 30 x 60-inch towel has a reversible design, with the skull and peace sign logo set against a cream backdrop on one side, and a holographic-inspired print on the other.

All of the products are officially licensed by the Grateful Dead, and Slowtide says they surveyed Deadheads and customers on the designs and styles they liked best. The brand says Deadheads have taken home more than 4,000 pieces from its Grateful Dead x Slowtide collection in the past year alone. In other words: You’ll want to take advantage of this Black Friday sale and add your favorite pieces to cart fast, before they sell out.

Shop the Slowtide Black Friday sale on their Grateful Dead blankets, towels and more on Slowtide.co.

The sale arrives on the heels of a new book about the Grateful Dead dubbed Friends of the Devil: West Texas Cowboys, Brown-Eyed Women, and Other Sunshine Daydreamers From the Songs of the Grateful Dead. From journalist Alex Pappademas and artist Joan LeMay, the book is billed as a “fresh take on the world’s greatest jam band.”
