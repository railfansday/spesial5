Fable Xbox 25th Anniversary ‘Quest’ gameplay

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Publisher Xbox Game Studios and developer Playground Games have released a new four-minute gameplay walkthrough of the Fable reboot as part of todays’ Xbox 25th anniversary celebrations.

Here is an overview of the demo, via Xbox Game Studios:

Celebrate 25 years of Xbox with an exclusive look at brand new Fable quest gameplay. The Hero of Briar Hill has entered the depths of Deepwood alongside Arden, a local Hero, to investigate why strange magical happenings have been troubling the people of Oakshire. Here, they cross a threshold where the natural magic of Deepwood appears corrupted by an outside force, and they discover hints as to who or what could be responsible…

Fable is due out for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC via Steam and Microsoft Store on February 23, 2027. It will also be available via Game Pass.
