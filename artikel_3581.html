Legendary 31-Year-Old Space Western Officially Returns With New Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

While the Western genre is experiencing a bit of a comeback on-screen with the likes of Taylor Sheridan putting a modern twist on the genre, the same can't really be said for the space western. Plenty of sci-fi titles still share some loose, pulpy roots with classic Westerns, but there aren't many that evoke the same vibes as something like Firefly, Outland, or the classic anime Cowboy Bebop. This year did, however, see The Mandalorian and Grogu premiere in theaters and continue Jon Favreau's Star Wars spin-off series with a standalone cinematic adventure following the titular bounty hunter on a new mission. A storied anime Western is also about to make a return to the genre in a new form.

Per Anime News Network, Shueisha's Jump SQ. RISE magazine announced the return of Trigun in its autumn issue on Thursday, with a new novel penned by the legendary manga's creator Yasuhiro Nightow in collaboration with Trigun Stampede writer Takehiko Oxi. Titled Trigun Manuscript, it's slated to take place in the same world and continuity as Stampede and the most recent television anime, Trigun Stargaze, which premiered back in January. Stargaze was billed as the "final phase" of Stampede, closing out the reimagining of the story told in the source material and the Studio Madhouse series. While it's connected specifically to the 2023 anime, Nightow is also providing new art on both the cover and interior of the new release.

As far as the translation goes, it's described as "the long-awaited novelization," suggesting that it'll be a retelling of the events of Stampede and Stargaze. Shueisha hasn't announced an exact release date other than a general window of this winter. Further details are slated to be announced at a later date. Expectations are, however, that there will be some changes to Vash the Stampede's adventures now that the anime is wrapped up.

Produced by BEASTARS studio Orange, Trigun Stampede was the second adaptation of Nightow's 1995 manga, taking place in a far-flung future on a wasteland planet known as "No Man's Land." Vash the Stampede, also known as the Humanoid Typhoon, has earned himself a notorious reputation and a staggering bounty of $$60 billion for the accidental destruction he leaves in his wake. However, when rookie reporter Meryl Stryfe and her mentor Roberto De Niro run into the gun-slinging outlaw himself, they learn he's actually a pacifist who hates blood, using his skills to resolve conflicts rather than start them. Their chance encounter begins a wild adventure following the trail of Vash's despicable twin brother, Millions Knives, while avoiding the bounty hunters who inevitably come to claim the reward on Vash's head. Stargaze, meanwhile, picks up two and a half years later, after the Lost JuLai tragedy, with Vash now living in hiding under a different name until circumstances and Knives' genocidal plans force him back into action.

Trigun Manuscript releases this winter. Get a look at the cover and the official announcement above.
