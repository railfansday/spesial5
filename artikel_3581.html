Brooks Nader Savagely Responds to Rumors About Sister Dating Pete Davidson

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Brooks Nader is evidently not a fan of her sister Sarah Jane Nader potentially dating Pete Davidson.

“I don’t approve of it, no. She can do whatever she wants, but I’m not into it,” Brooks, 29, candidly said during a Sunday, November 22, interview with E! News. “She doesn’t get into it with me when she knows I don’t approve of someone. She doesn’t tell me anything, so I find out online.”

The Love Thy Nader reality star’s brutal response to the romance rumors comes just days after Us confirmed that Sarah Jane, 24, and Davidson, 33, are not dating but are, however, friends.

Brooks also gave the prospective romance a shake of her head and a thumbs-down as she offered her verdict to the outlet on Sunday.

When asked how long the Saturday Night Live alum and Sarah Jane may have been dating, Brooks reiterated that she’s in the dark when it comes to her sibling’s love life. “You’d have to ask her,” Brooks said in response.

Social media users began to speculate about a potential romance between Davidson and Sarah Jane after they were spotted on a night out together in New York City in August. The pair were seen again at an AMC movie theater in NYC’s East Village earlier this month.

Davidson is currently single, having most recently been in a relationship with model Elsie Hewitt, with whom he shares 11-month-old daughter Scottie. Hewitt, 30, and Davidson announced four months after Us confirmed that they were dating in March 2025 that they were expecting a baby.

Us exclusively reported in May that the former couple had split just over one year of dating.

For Sarah Jane’s part, the model has been linked to various public figures over the years, including Eliana Orly, who appeared in the Nader family’s reality TV show, and Alexis Williams.

Brooks and Sarah Jane, who are also siblings to Love They Nader’s Mary Holland Nader and Grace Ann Nader, maintain a playful public rapport via social media. Earlier this weekend, Sarah Jane posted an Instagram carousel of photos that captured her walking through the streets of NYC in a sleek black ensemble. Brooks shared praise for the post before writing, “Give my damn sunnies back” in the comments section.

Days earlier, Brooks commented on another of Sarah Jane’s Instagram posts that promoted her brand collaboration with a clothing label. “But can you restock,” Brooks teased in the comments section after sharing red heart emojis.
