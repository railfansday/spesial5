Pregnant Jen Affleck, Ex Zac and Their Kids Reveal Sex of Baby No. 4

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Jen Affleck and her estranged husband, Zac Affleck, have revealed the sex of their fourth baby.

In a sweet Instagram post uploaded on Thursday, November 26, Jen, 27, Zac, 30, and their three kids got involved in the beach picnic gender reveal. (Jen and Zac are already parents to daughters Nora, 5, and Penelope, 16 months, and son Luca, 3.)

The family cut into a cake with wine glasses, revealing that Jen and Zac are expecting another daughter.

“Our story may be changing, but the love we have for our babies never will. 🤍we love you baby girl,” the caption accompanying the post read.

Jen announced in June that the pair were expanding their family by sharing a carousel of photos where she showed off her baby bump. “Chapter Four. 🤍,” the Secret Lives of Mormon Wives star wrote alongside the post.

Months later, the duo dropped the bombshell that they had split, with Jen filing for divorce on November 3 after seven years of marriage.

Jen exclusively told Us on November 10 that her pregnancy was one of the catalysts that led her to choose to end her marriage, insisting that “the same reason why I filed for divorce is the same reason why it was really hard.”

“It was this fourth child. I had to ask myself very hard questions like, ‘Is this the type of relationship I want this child to look up to?’” she told Us. “When I realized it was a no, that’s when I realized that I needed to make a change. You’ll see a lot of that unfold in season [1 of the Orange County spinoff].”

Jen explained that filming Mormon Wives put a magnifying glass on a lot of her marital problems as well, accelerating the decision to leave the relationship.

“I think doing reality TV for sure sped up the process [of calling it quits] because when you’re filming and you’re rewatching your life unfold in front of millions of people, it’s the difference between looking in a mirror,” she continued. “It highlights things.”

After their separation was made public, Zac posted a TikTok video addressing the split and admitting to “mistakes.”

“I don’t want anyone speaking bad about Jen, I don’t. I’ll just be the first to say I’ve made plenty of mistakes in our marriage, and it just really was time for us to go our own ways,” Zac said via the post on November 5. “We got married so young, and it sucks because I really did feel like we had something pretty special when we got married, but life happened. We had four kids. We signed up for reality TV on a show that’s really hurt a lot of people’s lives.”

Zac also candidly admitted that he was concerned about how the relationship breakdown will affect their kids – including their unborn daughter – but said he and Jen were committed to coparenting amicably.

“I feel like I failed my kids and I’m worried that I’m going to cause them damage and I just hope that we can still give them a normal childhood,” Zac confessed.
