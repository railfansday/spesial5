NBA 2K27’s GTA 6 Crossover Includes A Whole GTA-Inspired Limited Edition Of The Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Ahead of the GTA 6 release this week, publisher Take-Two Interactive and 2K Sports have announced an NBA 2K27 crossover with the game. Starting November 27, Vice City will take over the digital basketball courts, and a special-edition NBA 2K27 Vice City Edition will also be released to promote GTA 6.

The $80 digital-only NBA 2K27 Vice City Edition features Miami Heat player Bam Adebayo on the cover, and for that GTA 6 touch, the art also includes the signature Vice City neon glow. This is something of a first for Take-Two Interactive--and possibly, the rest of the industry--as releasing a special edition of a game that promotes another game isn't something that we've seen before. This version also includes bonus Vice City-themed content and several benefits for GTA+ subscribers.

For Season 3 of NBA 2K27's live-service league, Vice City-themed content and rewards across The City social hub and MyTeam mode will be available to unlock. This three-week promotion will include bonuses and access for GTA+ Members, and Take-Two says players can expect themed apparel, including Vice City and Los Santos jerseys, Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell Employee Outfits, and more.

Vice City-themed limited-time events will run throughout the season, with players competing in Conquer Leonida and Vice Run. Wednesdays will offer a chance to earn extra reputation by wearing Rockstar Games collaboration gear in Wear & Earn events, and one of the new modes is Neon vs. Noir, a 5v5 full-court clash pitting a Vice City team against a Los Santos lineup. MyTeam will also feature Vice City-themed cards, a powerful new Premier Exchange card, and limited-time modes.

The new gear will cost you some bucks, as the Season 3 Pro Pass--which offers access to the premium track with Vice City rewards like the Burger Shot gear, Cluckin' Bell outfit, Cactus Jack jersey, and Macca the Gator--will cost $10. The Hall of Fame Pass, which includes everything from the Pro Pass, 10 instant Level Skips, an extra 15% XP booster, and 15,000 VC, is priced at $20, and to get all the GTA-themed cosmetics, you have to grind through the 40-level season track. If you purchase the Vice City edition, the Season 3 Pro Pass is bundled with your copy.

GTA+ members on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S can also play the standard edition of NBA 2K27 for free from November 27 to January 15, 2027. If they like what they see, they'll get 20% off the game if they choose to purchase. GTA 6 preorders also include a one-month trial of GTA+.

Finally, GTA+ members can also download a Vice City bonus content pack featuring a Vice City-styled blue boat hat and oversized T-shirt, 30 skill boosts for five games, and 15 Gatorade boosts for five games.

GTA 6 marketing has been heating up as the game gets closer to launch, and this isn't the only basketball promotion on the table. For one night only, the NBA's Miami Heat will wear GTA 6-inspired jerseys when they host the Milwaukee Bucks on November 18, and the match will take place on a court inspired by Vice City.
