How old are your kids & when do you foresee them starting to withdraw from the trust? It's clear that your wife will not need any trust income.

With a 20-30 year time horizon, I would be comfortable with an aggressive AA of 90/10. For the fixed income piece, remember to use municipal bonds only, because regular bond interest/dividends will get taxed at 37% inside the trust once ordinary income rises above $16,500. Trust LTCG tax rates of 23.8% are higher than most people face, but only by 5 points compared to 18.8% with the NIIT. Ordinary income tax rates of 37% inside the trust vs 22%/24% on your personal Form 1040 is pretty heinous by comparison.

I would make zero distributions on any trust income until your kids inherit the trust, unless you wish to provide one or more of them with a house down payment, etc. Keeping 100% of the money inside the trust for as long as possible, while you & your wife pay 100% of the taxes from outside the trust is what makes a SLAT/IDGT work to create wealth.

I would avoid individual stocks & focus on broad-based index funds, such as VTI/VOO/VUG/VTV, plus municipal bond fund(s) for the fixed income AA. Note that VXUS and similar international funds throw off a lot (~40%) of unqualified dividends, which inside the trust suffer the same fate as regular bond interest- 37% taxation. I am not an expert, but I would avoid a large concentration of foreign stock ETF/MF inside your trust for this reason, unless you deem the diversification worth the tax pain. YMMV.
