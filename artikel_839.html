Wales and West Ham's Morgan suffers serious injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ffion Morgan joined West Ham from Bristol City in 2025

Wales and West Ham winger Ffion Morgan is facing a lengthy spell on the sidelines after suffering an anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) injury.

Morgan has undergone surgery having been helped off during West Ham's Women's Super League win over Charlton earlier this month.

The 26-year-old is now likely to miss the rest of the season.

"Ffion will now begin her rehabilitation programme under the supervision of the club's medical team," a West Ham statement said.

The injury also makes Morgan a major doubt for next summer's Women's World Cup, should Wales qualify.

Rhian Wilkinson's side take on Norway in a two-legged play-off final next week as they bid to reach the tournament for the first time in the nation's history.

Morgan, who has 56 caps, is an international regular and would be a shoo-in for the Wales squad if fit.

She joined West Ham on a two-year deal in 2025 after her contract at Bristol City expired.

Morgan has also played for Cardiff City, Coventry United and Crystal Palace.

She suffered an ACL injury while playing for Wales Under-19s as a teenager, while she had only recently returned to action for West Ham after another lengthy fitness lay-off.

Wales face Norway at the Cardiff City Stadium on Tuesday, 1 December, before the return leg in Oslo four days later.

Wales are hoping to cause an upset as they bid to reach the Women's World Cup for the first time, though beating former champions Norway may not be enough to guarantee a place in Brazil next summer.

The complex nature of the qualification process means that Wales may have to negotiate inter-confederation play-offs next February to secure a World Cup place, even if they triumph over Norway.
