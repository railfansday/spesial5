Mormon Wives' Salomé Andrea Gives Birth, Welcomes Baby No. 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Salomé Andrea, star of Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County, gave birth to her and husband Aaron Brimhall's third baby, joining the couple's twins Luca and Rue.

No secrets here: A new member has joined the Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: OC family.

Following the Hulu show's Nov. 12 premiere, star Salomé Andrea announced the birth of her and husband Aaron Brimhall's third child, a baby girl named Shiloh Andrea Brimhall.

"One of Sal’s favorite books growing up was Shiloh, so that name always stuck,” the couple, who also share 8-year-old twins Luca and Rue told People of Phyllis Reynolds Naylor’s 1991 children’s book Nov. 16. “When she came, we realized we created a triplet. She looks exactly like Luca and Rue. Everyone is in love and we are just soaking in the newborn baby time.”

Last month, the influencer shared a message about her squad's new chapter.

"Feeling extra emotional knowing our little family of four is about to become five," Salomé wrote on Instagram Oct. 22. "For so long it’s been just the four of us, and there’s something so bittersweet about watching this chapter come to an end."

"I’m so excited for all the love this new little baby will bring," she noted, "but part of me is holding on a little tighter to the family we’ve been for all these years. I know our hearts are only going to grow, but I’ll forever cherish the memories we made as just us four."

Salomé announced her pregnancy in May amid ongoing speculation about the status of her marriage to Aaron, simply captioning a series of family photos on Instagram: "Surprise."

Since then, the duo has remained a united front, updating followers on their baby journey, including her pregnancy cravings.

"This baby luvs sausage McMuffins w a hash brown," Salomé shared on Instagram in late May, "just like its mama."

Amid their baby prep, the couple also celebrated another milestone: their twins' birthdays.

"How are you two 8?" Aaron captioned an August tribute post. "Being your dad is the best thing that’s ever happened to me. I love you guys."

As Salomé and Aaron settle into life as a family of five, keep reading for more details on the Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County couples.

Following her divorce from Cory Althoff, podcaster Bobbi Althoff is dating Tyler Hawkins, who plays a special role in Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County.

"He styles me, that’s how he supports me,” she told E! News in May, adding that the basketball player, "picks out my outfits every day. He styles me every day for the show. It’s great."

Aspyn Ovard, who filed for divorce from Parker Ferris in 2024 after eight years of marriage, is currently in a throuple relationship with married couple Billy Davis and Bri Davis.

After many ups and downs, Jen Affleck—who is pregnant with her and Zac Affleck's fourth baby—officially filed for divorce from her husband of seven years, E! News confirmed Nov. 4.

Despite ongoing relationship speculation, Salomé Andrea and Aaron Brimhall are still going strong as they prepare to welcome their third baby together. The duo, who married in 2012, also share 8-year-old twins Luca and Rue.

McCall DaPron—the sister of Mormon Wives' Mayci Neeley—shares four kids with husband Kirk DaPron, who she wed in 2011.

Ashleigh Pease and Nate Pease are rock solid after over a decade of marriage. "This 13th year together is one that has felt full circle!" she wrote on Instagram in September. "A season of fruits. Comfort. The happiest milestones."

Madison Bontempo and Kyler Steven Fisher, who wed in 2013, have been open about the highs and lows of their relationship. "Kyler and I aren’t perfect," she wrote on Instagram in October. "We’ve had arguments. We’ve had hard seasons. We’ve had moments we wish we could take back. And we’re not afraid to admit that."

But, she added, "Those moments aren’t our whole story. There’s also laughter, friendship, forgiveness, growth, five beautiful children, a million memories, and a love that has continued to evolve through every season of our lives."
