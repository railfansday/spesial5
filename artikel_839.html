Barnet midfielder charged with rape

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Warning: this story contains themes of rape; If you have been affected, please visit www.sky.com/help/articles/viewersupport

A League Two footballer has been charged with three counts of rape and one count of engaging in sexual communication with a child.

Isiah Noel-Williams, 23, has also been charged with one count of assault by penetration and one count of supplying a controlled drug (Class B), Hertfordshire Constabulary said.

Noel-Williams, a midfielder for Barnet, was remanded in custody following a hearing at Hatfield Magistrates' Court on Saturday.

He will next appear at St Albans Crown Court on December 22.

Hertfordshire Constabulary said Noel-Williams, of High Oaks in St Albans, was charged following an investigation by the force's public protection command.

Barnet FC said in a statement: "The club is cooperating fully with the authorities and will determine any further action in due course.

"As this is now a matter for the courts, the club will not be making any further comment."

He has previously played for clubs including Hemel Hempstead Town, Matlock Town, Kettering Town, Harrow Borough and Cheshunt.
