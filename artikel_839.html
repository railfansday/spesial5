Kanye West Makes Rare Appearance With All 4 Kids at Yeezy Runway Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Kanye West was joined by his and Kim Kardashian’s kids North West, Saint West, Chicago West and Psalm West at the Yeezy Flagship Droam 1 runway show in Los Angeles.

The new Yeezy runway show was family business.

After all, Kanye West was joined by his and Kim Kardashian’s kids North West, 13, Saint West, 10, Chicago West, 8, and Psalm West, 7, for his brand’s latest Droam 1 flagship show in Los Angeles.

In photos from the event, Kanye wrapped his arms around his two eldest kids, North and Saint, while Chicago and Psalm stood in front of him. North, for her part, kept her signature edgy look for the Nov. 4 event, sporting her long blue hair, paired with blue contacts and dimple piercings.

North’s younger siblings, meanwhile, appeared to dress to theme, sporting monochromatic sweat sets reminiscent of Yeezy’s style, with Saint and Psalm sporting gray, while Chicago opted for a cream. For his part, Kanye, 49, sported a black leather quarter-zip jacket.

Kanye’s outing with his kids comes shortly after Kim shared insight into their, at times, difficult co-parenting dynamic in her new biography Citizen Kim.

“I worry about him all day,” Kim explained of her dynamic with her ex—from whom she divorced in 2021. “All the time. That’s the father of my kids, I’ll never not worry about him.”

The 46-year-old admitted that her personal dynamic with Kanye can be a “big burden” when it comes to looking after their kids. As she put it, “Is the father of my kids going to be okay?”

And when it comes to communicating with her ex, Kim admitted that she keeps direct contact “minimal,” opting to iron out details with Bianca Censori—whom Kanye wed in 2022—or other people in her ex’s circle.

“I’ll communicate through his wife,” she explained. “Or through his friend who is also his manager.”

Overall, Kim noted that she does what is necessary to protect her and Kanye’s four kids.

"It is challenging coparenting with someone who comes by every few months," she elaborated, "but I’m always like, 'Oh, Dad’s traveling.' I still make up stories and protect them because I want them to think their dad is the greatest. But I invite him over all the time.”

Although their family dynamic isn’t perfect, Kim doesn’t see it changing in the foreseeable future.

“It does make me so sad and I don’t think it’s going to get better,” Kim said. “I pray it does. For my kids.”
