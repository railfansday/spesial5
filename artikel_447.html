We found 14 tablet deals worth shopping ahead of Black Friday from Apple, Amazon, and Lenovo

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Around the holidays, we're always thinking about tablets. Maybe it's because they make such great gifts. Or perhaps it's because when traveling, we want something a little more portable than a laptop. Either way, as we hurdle toward Black Friday, we're hunting for the best deals on tablets.

All of our favorite tablet models are on sale. Apple iPads are up to $100 off, and you can find Lenovo tablets for under $200. One of our favorite deals is on the recently released Amazon Alexa Tablet 12 Pro, which is now down to $449.99.
