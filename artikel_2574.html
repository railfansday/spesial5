NFL Mock Draft 2027: Eagles 7-round simulation, Version 2.0

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Eagles notched their first win in two months on Sunday. However, the game didn't give much hope to the prospect of actually turning the season around. Unless they beat Dallas on Thursday, Philly is a team that should be looking ahead to the 2027 NFL Draft. Due to their win and Jacksonville's loss, the Eagles currently own the ninth and 24th pick in the first round.

Right now, the biggest determinants of the Eagles' draft strategy has to do with what is happening at quarterback and head coach. There are many factors that would lead to the Eagles not needing to draft a quarterback, but I am curious what that draft may look like if the position is taken care of ahead of the draft without sacrificing picks… IE, Jalen Hurts returns from his blood clots.

Using the PFF Mock Draft Simulator with their draft board and not making any trades.

1.09: Charlie Becker, Wide Receiver, Indiana: Charlie Becker is a menace. Becker has 11 touchdowns through the air, 1,100 yards receiving, is averaging nearly 16 yards a catch and has yet to drop a ball this season. The 6'4", 207 pound receiver is a physical, athletic deep threat who is able to use his long arms to win at the catch point and his long strides to pull away from defenses. The Eagles lack a true X-Receiver right now and Becker would immediately make the receiver position in Philadelphia a strength.

Trevor Lauck, Offensive Tackle, Iowa: The Eagles need a youth movement on their offensive line. Trevor Lauck is a phenomenal prospect at left tackle, and would likely slide over to the right side to take over for Lane Johnson. He'd be a day one starter and day one difference maker.

Boubacar Traore, Edge Defender, Notre Dame: The Eagles edge defender situation is bleak. Their best player right now is Nolan Smith, who may not even be with the team by the time the draft rolls around. Boubacar Traore is a bit leaner, but makes up for it with great length, burst, and a high motor. He is a tremendous pass rusher and has shown a lot as a run defender this season. Traore makes a ton of sense in the second round as someone who can really give a boost to this defense.

Isaac Brown, Running Back, Louisville: The 2027 Eagles have been struggling in all phases of offense, but injuries have made their running game borderline unwatchable. Save for some big Saquon Barkley runs, they just don't have quality depth at the running back position to move the ball in a meaningful way on the ground. Isaac Brown is a smaller running back prospect, but he is tremendous when he can get on the field. He has great vision, burst, and runs with a lot of grit. While he may not be an immediate every down back, there is a lot he can bring to the Eagles offense.

Zabien Brown, Cornerback, Alabama: This pick gives the Eagles an athletic, ball hawking cornerbacker. Brown needs some coaching, but his tools are really exciting and he has some great flashes as a perimeter defender. The Eagles need more depth in their defensive backfield.

Jelani Thurman, Tight End, North Carolina: The Eagles need some quality depth at tight end. Even if Eli Stowers works out, the roster doesn't look too good beyond him in 2027. Jelani Thurman is a versatile presence on the UNC offense and could come in as a quality TE2 or 3 in Philly.

Ashlynd Barker, Safety, Florida State: Safety is another position in need of some juice in the spring. Ashlynd Barker is an intriguing player who may do his best work as a box safety. He is big, physical, and unafraid to mix it up as a run defender. However he has the athleticism to cover the intermediate area as well.

Deshaun Lee, Cornerback, Iowa: You've heard this before… If Deshaun Lee was a bit taller, he'd be considered a Top 50 prospect. He is physical, athletic, with great ball skills and the ability to lock up a team's best player, but he is 5'9" on a good day. Is he a slot cornerback in the NFL? A free safety convert? Luckily for the Eagles, their secondary is so thin they could try him all over.

Griffin Wilde, Wide Receiver, Northwestern: Wilde is going to be one of those late round players I feel can make an impact early. He has the size and athleticism to win against NFL cornerbacks, but just doesn't have a high profile due to where he plays.

Devon Dampier, Quarterback, Utah: Is Devon Dampier an NFL quarterback? Well, his height gives plenty of cause for concern but there's little arguing with just how good he has been at the college level. I'd be really interested in seeing what he'd do in preseason if given the chance because he still has a big arm and a lot of the tools you want in a passer… he's just my height. It's an experiment, and if it fails, his athleticism could help him translate to another offensive position.
