Report: NFL, YouTube are negotiating a three-game package for 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

YouTube's on-again, off-again arrangements to stream select NFL games may be on again.

John Ourand of Puck reports in his Varsity newsletter that the NFL and YouTube have been negotiating a three-game package for the 2027 season. The arrangement would include a Christmas Eve game, the Week 17 Saturday game to be televised by NBC, and the Week 10 European game that was televised by Fox.

This year, Christmas Eve lands on a Thursday; Prime Video will televise the Texans-Eagles game that night (barring a flex). Next year, Christmas Eve lands on a Friday.

YouTube was at one point negotiating the five-game package for 2026 that landed with Netflix. It includes Wednesday night's first-of-its-kind Thanksgiving Eve game between the Packers and Rams. YouTube walked, as Ourand reported at the time, when the league tried to split the five-game package between multiple partners.

The arrangement between the NFL and YouTube currently includes Sunday Ticket. That package could be disappearing in the next round of broadcast deals.

The inevitable expansion of the regular season to 18 games per team will create another 16 games that can be sliced and diced into special packages.
