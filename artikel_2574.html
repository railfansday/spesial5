Report card grades from the Vikings' Week 11 loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Minnesota Vikings traveled to Mexico City in hopes of hanging with the top of the NFC. However, they were met by a San Francisco 49ers squad who was relentless on Sunday, beating Minnesota 35-10. This week's edition of winners and losers will only have one winner and three losers, as there was not a lot to write home about from Estadio Azteca.

San Francisco came into Sunday Night Football as the top-ranked rushing defense in the NFL, and it showed in their win. They stifled Minnesota all night long, limiting them to 69 carries on 21 attempts, which is 3.3 yards per carry. Aaron Jones Sr. and Jordan Mason never got anything going, resulting in the passing game to remain heavily involved.

Even though the 49ers rank toward the bottom of the NFL in sacks, it was a huge positive to see this Vikings offensive line allow just one sack on Kirk Cousins. Kevin O'Connell did a great job scheming up quick throws so as not to allow the pass rushers to get active early, keeping those guys off-rhythm all night long.

If you had told 49ers fans three years ago that they would have Mike Evans and Keenan Allen on their team, they would have been dreaming. Both are rostered in the Bay, and still found a way to terrorize this Vikings defense from the jump, combining for nine receptions for 147 yards and a touchdown. George Kittle was heavily involved in the red zone, scoring a pair of touchdowns on the evening.

This Vikings defense entered Sunday's game with one of the top defenses in the league, but was simply outmatched from the jump. Minnesota allowed 343 yards of total offense and 35 points in a losing effort. San Francisco is as hot as any team in the league right now, and their path toward a top seed in the NFC remains intact.
