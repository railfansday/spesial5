Confirmed Lineups: SC Paderborn vs. Eintracht Frankfurt

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Newly-promoted Paderborn host Eintracht Frankfurt on matchday 9 of the Bundesliga. The hosts are unbeaten in their last three games, while Frankfurt are hoping to extend their winning run to three games after 3-1 victories over HSV in DFB-Pokal and Bundesliga. Younes Ebnoutalib starts on the bench days after his second nomination for Germany national team.

Here are the lineups for the duel at Home Deluxe Arena in Paderborn.

Paderborn: Noll; ter Horst, Scheller, Hansen; Curda ©, Castaneda; Obermair, Baack, Müller, Vidovic; Marino

Frankfurt: Atubolu; Knauff, Koch ©, Brassier, Baum; Onyedika, Aseko; Chaïbi, Uzun, Doan; Burkardt
