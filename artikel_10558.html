Japanese pro wrestler allowed to wear mask in city council after winning election

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

A Japanese professional wrestler who was elected to his local city council has been given permission to wear his signature mask during official meetings, but there’s one condition.

Billyken Kid, a 52-year-old wrestler with Osaka Pro Wrestling, was elected to the Kawanishi City Council in Japan’s Hyogo Prefecture on October 18.

The masked wrestler didn’t just scrape into office, either. He received 4,040 votes, finishing first among 37 candidates competing for 24 seats.

However, his election raised an unusual question for fellow council members: Would he be allowed to wear his wrestling mask while conducting official government business?

According to The Mainichi, the issue sparked discussions among council representatives before they ultimately agreed to let him keep his trademark appearance.

On November 6, representatives from the council’s five political groups voted on whether Billyken Kid could wear his mask inside the chamber.

Four groups supported the request, while one opposed it, raising concerns about why he needed to wear a mask when he had previously attended other meetings without one.

The council eventually approved his request, provided he allows officials to verify his identity by showing his face before entering the chamber.

He must also wear the same mask featured on his election posters and maintain proper conduct during proceedings.

As reported by MBS News, Billyken Kid attended his first council meeting on November 9 wearing a suit and his signature gold wrestling mask.

“Putting on the mask gets me fired up. It also makes me feel more focused,” he explained.

The wrestler added that he hopes to help resolve residents’ problems during his time in office.

While he can wear the mask during council meetings, Billyken Kid says he will remove it for certain ceremonies, including memorial services and funerals.

Interestingly, he’s not the only professional wrestler making headlines for entering politics.

Just days earlier, American pro wrestler David Morales, who plays a corrupt politician, was elected mayor of Providence, Rhode Island.
