‘Heated Rivalry’ Is Getting Its Own Monopoly Game—and Yes, the Cottage Is Included (Exclusive)

But who will be picking the stupid Canadian wolf bird token?!

We’ve seen Ilya and Shane get ultra-competitive when it comes to hockey and video games together, but what about a game of Monopoly? Now we actually don’t even have to imagine it thanks to a brand-new Monopoly: Heated Rivalry edition that will have you running to play as a stupid Canadian wolf bird.

Cosmopolitan has an exclusive first look at the brand new edition, that is currently available for preorder on Amazon and Walmart, and will be released on May 1, 2027. According to Hasbro, “Inspired by the acclaimed international hit series created by Jacob Tierney and produced by Accent Aigu Entertainment, the gamefollows Shane and Ilya’s love story in chronological order.”

“Players claim iconic locations, build Hotels and Cottages, and draw Texts and Tabloids cards featuring familiar messages from ‘Lily’ and ‘Jane,’ sponsorships and gossip about your favorite MLH players. They can also choose from six show-inspired tokens, including a Straw + Berry smoothie, the MLH Cup, a tuna melt, an extra banana, and, of course, a stupid Canadian wolf bird.”

The board itself is being kept under wraps, but we imagine that there are going to be some very fun locations that are key to their relationship coming into play. But we do have a special sneak peek of it below including one of the text messages coming into play revealing that one of the properties will be a gym!

The game also features two of the show’s most iconic images: them in Las Vegas in episode 1 and the iconic face-off from the season 1 poster.

Don’t forget: your trip to the Cottage is scheduled for May 1, 2027, so preorder on Amazon or Walmart now so you don’t miss out!
