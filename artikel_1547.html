CAN 2025: The Senegal-Morocco final replayed?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Could the CAN 2025 final between Senegal and Morocco experience a new episode on the pitch? While the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) must deliver its verdict to the “end of November”the hypothesis of a match to be replayed raises questions.

On January 18, 2026, Senegal won the final against Morocco (1-0), after extra time. But on March 17, the appeal jury of the Confederation of African Football (CAF) finally declared the Teranga Lions forfeited, approving a Moroccan victory with a score of 3-0. A decision contested by the Senegalese Football Federation (FSF), which appealed to the CAS to recover the title.

Can the CAS then decide to replay the match? The body has extensive powers of examination. It can confirm the decision of the CAF, cancel it and render a new decision, or refer the file to the competent authorities of the African Confederation. It is in particular this last possibility which could, indirectly, open the door to a new meeting. The CAS cannot, however, automatically order that a match be replayed simply because it overturns the contested decision.

A precedent exists. In 2019, after the final return of the African Champions League between Esperance de Tunis and Wydad Casablanca, CAF ordered the match to be replayed. The CAS annulled this decision, considering that the CAF executive committee was not competent, before referring the case to the African football authorities. They then declared Wydad forfeited, a decision finally confirmed by the CAS in September 2020. The match was therefore never replayed.

But the two situations are not identical. The major difference with lies in the facts. Indeed, Wydad refused to resume play after the interruption, while the Senegalese players returned to the pitch and finished the match, winning 1-0. This element could weigh in the TAS analysis.

The possibility that the final of this CAN 2025 will be replayed therefore remains legally possible, but very far from being certain. Especially since the two federations have not publicly requested a new match. After the hearing organized in October, all eyes are now turned towards the end of November, the period at which the CAS must render its decision.
