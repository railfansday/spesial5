Roma creating 145% more chances compared to 2025/26

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Roma has become increasingly Gasperini-esque at the start of this season, game after game.

The Giallorossi team has become a reflection of the coach’s approach to football in every match, regardless of the opponent.

The Roman side has already scored 19 more goals than a year ago at this point in the season: from 12, they’ve increased their attacking turnover to 31, averaging 3.37 goals per win.

Roma’s attacking play has improved significantly compared to a year ago. As Corriere dello Sport reports, shots on target increased from 57 to 72, touches in the final 16 meters increased from 267 to 409, and successful dribbles nearly doubled (from 60 to 102).

The Giallorossi are proving more effective with and without the ball: they’ve already recovered 13 more balls in the opposition’s half (from 47 to 60), although the most impressive statistic is the 145% increase in the number of great chances created (from 20 after 11 matchdays, now 49).
