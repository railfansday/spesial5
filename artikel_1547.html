Liga: FC Barcelona wins the clash against Atlético de Madrid thanks to Fermín López!

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

It’s a rivalry that grows stronger season after season… Opposed on numerous occasions in recent years, on the pitch and in the transfer window, Atlético de Madrid and FC Barcelona are now real enemies. The Catalan press indicated before the match that the Blaugrana players wanted to fight, to take revenge for the eliminations in the Champions League and Copa del Rey last season in particular. For this shock, Diego Simeone brought out a fairly classic eleven, with his captains Koke, Barrios, Baena and Julián Álvarez, eagerly awaited this evening, among others. Opposite, Hansi Flick relied on Marc Bernal to replace the absent Rodri, while the young Brian Fariñas started on the right side of the defense. Otherwise, it was classic, with Lamine Yamal, Raphinha and Gordon in front. In the end, Barça took the three points, not without suffering, particularly in the first half.

Very quickly, Atlético showed offensive intentions, with Grimaldo who projected himself quickly, and Baena very much in sight from the start. Joan Garcia, as often, saved his team by winning a mano a mano with Julián Álvarez, who left alone behind the Barcelona defense (6th). Behind, on the corner that followed, Pubill smashed a header onto the bar (6th). The Colchoneros were much superior, but Barça could be dangerous, and on the only Catalan offensive at the start of the match, Pedri also found the bar (8th). The Madrilenians, however, would quickly score the net, taking advantage of Barça’s very high defensive line. On another quick transition, Kang-in Lee, on the right side, shifted Julián Álvarez. The latter put a perfect ball for Giuliano Simeone, who arrived in force, who crucified Joan Garcia (1-0, 10th). The Blaugranas were in really bad shape, and only Lamine Yamal managed to make a little difference on his side, worrying a little for a generally serene and calm Atlético.

Joan Garcia saved his team again on an attempt by Julián Álvarez in the area (26th), while the Barcelona defense was still at bay. The pace decreased little by little, with sequences of possession on one side or the other, and, it should be noted, players with good technical quality on both sides, with a very good Barrios among the Madrilenians for example. But just before the break, Barça got back on track. After a good rush from Pedri, Fermín López recovered the leather and fired a powerful shot to beat Oblak (1-1, 45th). Enough to put the Catalans back in the match at the best of times. Returning from the locker room, De Jong came into play in place of Fariñas, and the entry of the Dutchman would greatly help Barça in the construction of the game, even if he mainly played as a full-back. We quickly saw a Barça a little more dominant, who had more of the ball than during the first act, and who played much closer to the Madrid area.

But Lamine Yamal’s gang could not discover each other. Still going behind the defense, Julián Álvarez attempted a lob, but missed (55th). Second interesting situation missed by the Argentinian this evening, against what could have been his team. The Barcelonans tried their luck a lot with shots from outside the area, via Fermín López or Bernal (64th). Inspired, Lamine Yamal and his sidekick Fermín López would once again make the difference. The Barcelona number 10 launched the midfielder, who had made a superb call in the area, and crossed perfectly in front of Oblak (1-2, 67th). A double, and a superb score rendered by Fermín, lethal between the lines. Barça were comfortable and had control of the ball, and managed their lead, without revealing too much behind, while Hansi Flick’s changes proved more effective than those of Simeone. Gordon sent a death cross that Lamine Yamal was a few centimeters away from being able to recapture in front of goal (83rd). Barça could not make the break, and the score no longer changed. The Spanish champion made a big move in the race for the La Liga title, regaining a three-point lead over Real Madrid and five over its evening rival.
