Reports: Ramos needs check-up on shin issue with Cisse poised for Cagliari – the latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Goncalo Ramos was withdrawn at half-time during the game against Olympiacos last night after taking a knock, reports confirm.

As Ruben Amorim explained in his post-match press conference after Olympiacos 2-4 Milan, Ramos was substituted at the break due to a physical problem. The Portuguese striker suffered a severe blow to his shin that prevented him from continuing the match.

He was forced to leave the pitch, replaced by Christian Pulisic who scored the third goal to complete the comeback. As reported yesterday evening by MilanNews and confirmed this morning by La Gazzetta dello Sport, the former PSG player’s condition will be assessed today at Milanello.

Milan will train today to begin preparations for Monday’s league game away against Cagliari, hoping to make it four wins in a row. If Ramos is unable to recover, Alphadjo Cisse will play in his place as a striker, with Francesco Camarda having been absent last night due to an ankle issue.

Amorim’s words from yesterday’s press conference are as follows: “I didn’t change the game with my substitutions. Ramos came off because he was in pain. The substitution was normal. Olympiacos were very aggressive in the second half, and we knew they would lose their composure and run into trouble.

“We need to understand the game better. Everything comes together in the substitution. Hutchinson changed the game more than I did. What changed the game was the first goal. 4-2 is a great result.

“I think the players, the staff… believed in the victory. I’m grateful for everyone. Today we showed we can do more, that we need everyone’s help, but the spirit is there.”
