Watch Taylor Swift Perform ‘Our Song’ With ‘I Knew It, I Knew You’ at the 2026 CMA Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Taylor Swift embraced her country roots last night, taking the stage at the CMA Awards for the first time in 13 years. The singer performed a mash-up of her Toy Story 5 song “I Knew It, I Knew You” and her 2007 country hit “Our Song,” making it a sweet, nostalgic homecoming.

The next morning, she posted her full performance on YouTube. Watch it below:

She wore an embellished white sleeveless dress with a red skirt and bedazzled matching cowboy boots. Just like old times, she played along with a sparkly guitar. Swift’s performance began backstage, and she sang while making her way down the halls, nodding to fellow country music stars along the way. She was even joined by Keith Urban onstage.

“I Knew It, I Knew You” was nominated for Single of the Year, but Ella Langley ultimately took home the win with “Choosin’ Texas.” Swift received two Grammy nominations for the song last week as well: Song of the Year and Best Song Written for Visual Media.

During a virtual press conference for the Critics Choice Awards earlier this month, Swift spoke about connecting with Toy Story’s cowgirl Jessie, whose perspective shaped the song. She reflected on her own career trajectory and the ways she has reinvented herself as an artist over the years.

“It’s interesting because being in the entertainment industry, I’ve always just felt like it’s harder to hit a moving target,” she said of people’s changing tastes. “Once people feel like they’ve figured you out creatively and they’ve figured out what you can do, they usually move into the discardment phase and so that would always be where I would try to pivot and if they think you’re going to zig, you zag instead. Rather than looking at something as exhausting and daunting, I look at as just a new adventure. Once I’ve done one thing, I look for something else that inspires me.

“I feel like with Jessie, she’s always going on these adventures and she’s always trying to help someone new and there’s always something she feels really inspired by and some new challenge ahead of her and she takes them on with such fearlessness,” Swift continued. “Except we see her have this moment of crisis in this film, where she does feel hopeless and she does feel useless and she’s saying to herself, ‘Am I a bad toy? Does any of this matter?’ And it’s that moment that just breaks your heart because you’re like, ‘No, Jessie,’ and then you get that amazing resolution—obviously no spoilers—but in the film she reaches a place that was ultimately what inspired me to write the song I wrote.”
