Jenna Johnson Called Out “Online Bullying” and “Hate” from ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

“Hate has become so relevant and intertwined with the show.”

It’s been almost a week since Jenna Johnson and Harry Shum Jr.’s shocking elimination from Dancing With the Stars, and now that she’s had some time to process, Jenna says she’s hoping fans of the show can get back to spreading joy instead of online bullying.

“I feel like Dancing with the Stars used to be the show that everyone tuned into to turn off the noise and to just experience two hours of pure joy, and entertainment, and excitement,” she told People. “So it’s sad that this online bullying and hate has become so relevant and intertwined with the show.”

Jenna certainly isn’t the only person on Dancing With the Stars season 35 to speak out about the increasingly negative vibes on social media.

Harry previously told E! News, “I hope they address that at a certain point. Because I think there’s a good amount of bullying that doesn’t feel really good, and I think the rest of the cast feels it.”

Meanwhile, Jenna Dewan told Extra when she was eliminated, “I know it’s hard to know how this all plays into it. I know this was a particularly tough week for a lot of the online noise and all stuff that comes with the show. I don’t know, I’ll never know how, or if, any of that plays into this. But I know that we went out there and had the best time of our life.”

Plenty of shows have had to issue anti-bullying PSAs to their audience ahead of the show (including Love Island and The Summer I Turned Pretty), so it’s possible Dancing With the Stars will follow suit next season.
