The Denim Trends You’ll Be Seeing All Winter Long

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

These four styles are about to dominate the street style on your FYP.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Let’s talk denim. You probably already have a solid rotation of jeans in your closet (and if you don’t, what in the world have you been wearing this fall?), but it never hurts to add to it. And you’re going to want to add to it, especially because this winter’s styles are going to be so good.

We’re not just talking low-rise or bootcut; we’re getting specific with it. Don’t worry, there’s a trend out there for everyone. If you’re feeling bold, you might find yourself taking an interest in the animal-print barrel-leg styles (Mango’s Bambi jeans have been ever-popular this season) that we saw striding into runway shows this fall. Or, perhaps, Chloé’s ultra-pleated denim has the dramatic flair that you’ve been coveting. On the other hand, if you’re looking for a subtle style to bring into your wardrobe folds, an inconspicuously ripped pair or a baggy low-rise cut might be the newness you needed.

We’ve found the four denim trends set to take over this winter, so get ahead of the game and start shopping for a pair today. With a novelty jacket and a square-toe boot, your trend-forward outfit will be complete.

We’re not talking full 2000s shredded jeans; rather, we’re seeing a slight distressing that adds a casual worn-in feel to a new pair. In bootcut, straight, relaxed, and wide-leg silhouettes, this subtle rip is taking over the streets this season.

Both low-rise and baggy denim aren’t anything new, but we’re seeing both trends combined this winter, creating a slouchy, nonchalant vibe. We would recommend pairing them with a cropped puffer or fitted jacket so that the bagginess doesn’t overwhelm your figure.

Chloé put an avant-garde spin on pleated pants, going for a drop-waist denim with a voluminous leg. We’re digging the exaggerated pleats, and we’ve found similarly dramatic designs across price points (in case the $2,200 price tag isn’t speaking to you).

Take a ride on the wild ride with these animal-print barrel-leg pairs. We’ve been loving the prints—cheetah, zebra, cow, leopard, and so on—and the distinct barrel silhouette, so naturally, blending the two trends together is really working for us.
