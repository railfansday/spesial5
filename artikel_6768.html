Less Space, More Beauty: Products That Do It All

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

From clutch-friendly essentials to giftable sets, these multitasking Ulta Beauty finds do double (or triple) duty, making your bag a little lighter.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Proof that good things come in small packages—and can serve more than one purpose. These beauty finds from Ulta Beauty help you pack less for the holidays. With a social calendar full of office parties, family dinners, and everything in between, the last thing you need is a makeup bag that weighs more than Santa’s sack. Enter hardworking formulas, all-in-one palettes, easy-to-use touch-up essentials, and a few pint-sized favorites that make getting ready—and staying polished—so much easier. Whether you’re looking to streamline your own evening bag or find a little something special for the beauty lover on your list, these holiday picks are worth making room for.

The secret to a holiday party glow without an overflowing makeup bag? Choosing products that offer more than one way to use them. Think customizable color combinations, multi-use formulas that work on different areas of the face, and products that deliver more than one beauty benefit.

Choose your own adventure with the about-face Ballet Beam Highlighting Pearls, which bring several shades together in one case, letting you play around with different finishes without carrying multiple products. Swirl your brush over all the pearls for a custom blend, or pick up a single pearl when you want a more focused glow.

If subtlety isn't on the agenda, Fenty Beauty Limited-Edition Midnight Diamonds Diamond Bomb All-Over Diamond Veil brings the drama with a diamond-dusted finish that catches the light from every angle. And while the compact may be small, the effect is anything but. A quick sweep across your cheekbones or collarbones is all you need to make an entrance.

Prefer something a little more understated? NARS Light Reflecting Luminizing Blush combines the color payoff of a blush with the soft sheen of a highlighter, giving cheeks a pretty wash of color with just enough radiance to catch the light. One product, two jobs, and one less thing to squeeze into your bag.

When holiday plans take you from the office to dinner or straight to a party, having a few easy-to-use beauty essentials on hand can make all the difference. Look for products that simplify application, make touch-ups effortless, and help you refresh your look without starting from scratch—or packing a full kit.

Ulta Beauty Collection Eyeshadow Singles let you bring along only the shade(s) you'll actually use—because when was the last time you touched every color in an 18-pan palette? With these silky powders, a quick swipe of your fingertip is enough to refresh your eye makeup, no brushes required.

Ideal for when you need a complexion pick-me-up, L'Oréal Paris Infallible Cushion Foundation puts liquid foundation in a purse-friendly case, so you can touch up coverage around your nose, even out any patchiness, or conceal redness that suddenly make an appearance halfway through the evening. The built-in cushion applicator also means one less tool to pack.

Brows have a habit of doing their own thing after a few hours. Luckily, Benefit Cosmetics’ The Brow Booth Full-Size & Mini Brow Trio includes a full-size brow pencil and wax for perfecting your arches before heading out. Consider it a little insurance against unruly brows, even after a night on the dance floor.

As for lips, you can't go wrong with Clinique Almost Lipstick in Black Honey. The sheer berry shade leaves behind a just-bitten tint, and its balm-like texture is so forgiving that you can reapply without a mirror—because nobody wants to spend the party worrying about whether their lipstick has wandered outside the lines.

The best beauty products don’t just deliver great results—they make your routine easier, too. Long-wearing formulas mean fewer touch-ups throughout the evening, and the less makeup you need to bring, the more room you’ll have for your phone, keys, and other actual necessities.

The Tarte Tartelette Tubing Mascara coats each lash in tiny tubes of pigment for impressive length and definition without the usual smudging or flaking. Even better, those little tubes slide right off with warm water at the end of the night, so you won’t have to spend ages scrubbing at your lashes when all you want to do is go to bed.

If eyeliner is nonnegotiable, the Anastasia Beverly Hills DramaLine Longwear Waterproof Gel Eyeliner Pencil has a waterproof, smudge-resistant formula that can create—and maintain—everything from a sharp wing to a colorful flick along the lash line.

For lips that can hold their own through a packed social calendar, MAC MACximal Silky Matte Lipstick delivers rich, full-coverage color with a creamy feel that's surprisingly comfortable for a matte.

Sometimes the smartest beauty buys come in sets. Whether they bundle different makeup essentials, offer complementary shades, or give you multiple ways to create a look, these curated collections make it easy to get more out of a single purchase. They’re also lovely gifts for the beauty enthusiasts on your list.The whole face, fast

The Rare Beauty Bestselling Faves 4-Piece Set, which includes mini versions of the brand's popular liquid blush, lip gloss, and mascara, plus a full-size brow gel, is a great way for newcomers to get acquainted with the brand's most popular products—or for longtime fans to have smaller versions of their favorites handy for travel. With four different beauty essentials in one set, it's a versatile collection that's easy to take on the go.

Shopping for someone who loves a dramatic eye but doesn't need another full-size palette? Polite Society Dual Intentions Eyeshadow Duo pairs two complementary shadows in a single case, taking the guesswork out of putting together an eye look. It's just enough color to play with, without adding another bulky palette to an already crowded makeup drawer. It's a simple way to get more variety from fewer products.

If you’ve got multiple people on your shopping list, the Tarte You Know You Love Me Collector's Set includes two mini eyeshadow palettes (one warm-toned, one cool-toned), two mascaras for length and volume, and two Maracuja Juicy lip products for plumping and shine. Each palette comes with its own reusable wristlet clutch, so you can turn the collection into two separate presents or mix and match the products for several smaller stocking stuffers.
