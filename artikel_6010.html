What Elizabeth Holmes Got Right

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

At the same time as the investigation, trial, and conviction of one rogue revolutionary, a genuine revolution, in diabetes management, was taking place.

Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes speaks at the Clinton Global Initiative annual meeting in New York City, September 29, 2015.

We say that culture flows downstream to politics, but we don’t always say what kind of flow.

In the case of health care, that flow can be more tsunami than rivulet.
