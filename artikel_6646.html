The Starbucks Bearista Cup Is Coming Back—with an Early Surprise Edition

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The little glass bear is headed back to Starbucks, and this year, it's bringing company.

Starbucks knows how to make a holiday coffee run feel like an occasion. The seasonal drinks return, the cups get a festive makeover, and even a quick stop for caffeine feels a little more special.

Last year, though, one cup became a holiday event of its own. The Bearista Glass Cold Cup—a glass cup shaped like a bear—sold out quickly and began appearing on resale sites for hundreds and even thousands of dollars. That’s a lot of attention for a cup, even one as cute as this! The bear was popular enough that Walmart even sold a lookalike.

For anyone who missed out on all the fun last year, though, we have some good news. Starbucks is bringing the Bearista Bear back, along with a new collection of mystery cups.

The original Bearista Glass Cold Cup returns to select Starbucks coffeehouses on Dec. 1, just in time for holiday gifting. The bear-shaped glass comes with a green beanie-shaped lid and striped straw, ready to hold your cold brew.

After the cup sold out last year, Starbucks offered thousands more as prizes in its holiday game. This time, you’ll be able to look for the original in coffeehouses again.

Look for the original Bearista cup at select Starbucks locations starting Dec. 1. Starbucks hasn’t shared how many will be available, but given how quickly they sold out last year, you may want to check your local store early.

Before then, there’s another opportunity for Bearista fans. On Nov. 10, Starbucks is releasing a Glass Bearista Cold Cup Surprise Box Edition, featuring a collection of festive characters. Each one comes in a sealed box, so you won’t know which bear you have until you open it.

The surprise boxes will be available in limited quantities, while supplies last.
