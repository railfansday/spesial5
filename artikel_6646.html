Come, gather ’round, children, and let me tell you a tale about the Black Fridays of yore, when shoppers would rise before first light and journey forth to a Walmart for discounted TVs and pajamas in bulk. That was the old way. Back then, a shopper had but one day. Now we get most of November: Amazon kicked off its Black Friday Week on November 19 and is running it through Cyber Monday on November 30, Target started its sale on November 22, and plenty of fashion brands beat them both to it.

Lucky us! With weeks to work with instead of a single day, you can shop like a reasonable person: You can think about whether you'll wear the thing, sleep on it, and still get the discount in the morning. (No such luxury for our forebears.) So whether you call it Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Cyber Week, or just November, you have options, and I combed through all of them so you don't have to keep 40 tabs open. (Hallelujah.)

Below are the best Black Friday fashion deals you can shop right now on clothing, activewear, shoes, bags, and jewelry, plus a handful of home and wellness finds that Cosmo readers keep buying. I only included sales that are worth it, meaning real discounts on things you'd want at full price, from retailers you already know and shop. It's a great time to knock out your holiday gifting, too. (For skincare, makeup, and hair tools, head over to our list of the best Black Friday beauty deals.) I'll keep adding to this list through Cyber Monday as retailers announce new sales, so check back often, lest you miss something good.
