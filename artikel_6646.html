The Fall Winter 2026 Shoe Trends to Shop Now—From Kitten Heels to Great Boots

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Fall’s top shoe trends pick up where we left off in the spring—iterating on classic shapes while infusing them with personality-driven touches. The result? Styles that feel both familiar and new; wearable yet a touch unexpected. And with the spring/summer 2027 collections now unfolding in real time, it’s clear that many of fall’s biggest trends are here to stay.

The kitten heel was a reference point for many designers—Celine, Prada, and Proenza Schouler included—who reworked pared-back silhouettes with playful, feminine flourishes. We’ve seen plenty already on the streets of New York Fashion Week. (Those looking for a little more height can turn to options from Victoria Beckham, Jil Sander, and Stella McCartney.)

Elsewhere, designers reimagined the T-strap—in both heeled sandal and pump form. It’s a perfect transitional shoe—sturdier than a flip-flop, but still perfectly suitable for a warm day. Though, for the colder months, you can pair it with knee-high or crew socks the way Dries Van Noten and Fendi did. Speaking of the colder months, boot season is upon us, and the runways displayed a handful of key silhouettes in that space, including the stretchy sock boot in both knee-high and ankle-height iterations, as well as rugged biker boots.

Slippers made their mark for fall too—from the return of classics like Gucci’s fur-lined horse-bit style to patterned and embroidered takes from Ralph Lauren and Conner Ives. A slip-on brings an ease to daily dressing, adding a polished feel to worn-in jeans and a laid-back cool to skirts. Plus, it’s a welcome flat alternative for party dressing and winter occasions. If heels are more your speed, you’ll find lovely festive options, from Chanel’s two-tone metallic glove pumps to Michael Kors’ red satin square-toed heels—like ornaments for the feet!

Already spotted on the spring summer 2027 runways in New York, kitten heels, textured pumps, and slip-on silhouettes are set to continue their reign. Now is the time to invest in styles that will carry you through the winter and into spring. Below, a closer look at all the top fall/winter 2026 shoe trends.

Biker boots are back in the driver's seat for fall, bringing a rugged, rebellious spirit to even the most feminine looks. At Balenciaga, slouchy black leather boots had a deliberately worn-in attitude, while Dries Van Noten and Proenza Schouler played with the contrast between sturdy footwear and softer, more romantic clothes. For a similar effect, try Balenciaga's buckled motor boots or Dries Van Noten's oxblood leather styles, while Dior's more polished riding-inspired boots offer a sleeker alternative. Frye's classic harness boots and distressed brown suede styles with spurs bring a little Western flair into the mix. Whether you prefer a tall, slouchy silhouette or something shorter and more rugged, these boots are particularly good at toughening up a feminine skirt or dress.

Sleek, streamlined, and endlessly flattering, the sock boot offers a more refined alternative to fall's rugged footwear. At Chanel, pastel-colored boots brought an unexpected softness to eveningwear, while Celine's pointed-toe iterations lent a ladylike finish to romantic skirts. Calvin Klein and Altuzarra favored a more minimalist approach, with supple leather boots that hugged the leg. Celine's glossy black stilettos and Reformation's buttery cream ankle boots capture the silhouette's elegant appeal, while Toteme's burgundy knee-highs introduce a welcome touch of color. For something more understated, Vince's slender black boots and Staud's low-heeled leather ankle style are equally compelling. Whether worn with a midiskirt or tucked beneath tailored trousers, the appeal lies in the silhouette's clean, uninterrupted line.

'Tis the season to put your best foot forward! With holiday invitations piling up, there's no better excuse to indulge in a little festive footwear. On the fall runways, Carolina Herrera and Michael Kors embraced old-Hollywood glamour with elegant evening pumps, while Tory Burch and Ferragamo made a case for shoes with a little extra shine. And why stop at a little? Chanel's crinkled gold leather pumps, Aeyde's silver metallic heels, and Ferragamo's sculptural gold mules are practically made for the dance floor. For a pop of festive red, look to Michael Kors's satin pumps or Aeyde's sleek mules, while Tory Burch's embellished Mary Janes and Herbert Levine's satin heels bring a little whimsy to the occasion. Whether you're dressing for a black-tie affair or cocktails with friends, consider these your invitation to have a little fun from the ankles down.

Fall’s kitten heel marries uptown elegance with downtown edge, thanks to unexpected touches like intricate embellishments and modern hardware. For the on-the-go woman, it’s a sensible style for the 9-to-9—with plenty of personality, to boot.

The slipper’s (or soft loafer, in some instances) effortless yet trend-forward nature was illustrated at Conner Ives and Calvin Klein, where it grounded statement skirts and sequined dresses, respectively. A hybrid of the ballet flat and mule, we love it styled with baggy, wide-leg jeans and a military jacket for an eclectic twist.

The fall/winter 2026 runways went wild for animal print. Leopard, snakeskin, zebra, and even abstract iterations appeared across footwear styles. For the minimalist, take a cue from Khaite and pair snakeskin pumps with rinse-wash denim. Or, for a more maximalist take, look to Isabel Marant, where crimson jeans proved the ideal complement to zebra-print tall boots.

Playing with shape also meant designers revisited the heel itself. The result? Cone heels made their way en masse onto the fall/winter 2026 runways. As seen on pumps, slingbacks, and leather and suede boots, the sculptural silhouette instantly sharpens your look. For right now, reach for a rounded-toe style that slips effortlessly beneath tailored trousers and straight-leg jeans.

A favorite in recent seasons, the fashion pump continues to reign supreme. For fall 2026, fresh takes walked the runway at Stella McCartney, Chanel, and Tory Burch—from barely there glove pumps to pop of color slingbacks. We love it rendered in unexpected hues and playful prints for right now.

The wild card crowdpleaser? Statement shoes! Whether in heeled or flat form, these whimsical creations balance summer’s playful energy with fall’s gravitas. Reach for textured pumps or sequined sneakers to add an unexpected pop to any look. No matter the occasion or destination, they bring a little fancy footwork to every step.

The T-strap makes its return for fall! Dior and Givenchy presented open-toe styles that can easily be added to your transitional rotation. Come later in the season, lean into the trend with a sharp pointed toe and a sock styled underneath. The shape is a natural match for an elegant midiskirt, as seen at Calvin Klein, Dries Van Noten, and Fendi.
