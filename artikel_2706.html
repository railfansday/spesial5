Warriors changes coming after second straight blowout loss without Steph Curry

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

NEW ORLEANS — When you lose in blowout fashion to a red-hot team that was in the NBA Finals five months ago, that's one thing. When it's followed by an almost-as-disastrous defeat to a bottom-of-the-barrel squad that had dropped its past six games, something's got to give.

"Especially in the fashion that we've lost," guard De'Anthony Melton said, "expect things to change. As they should, given the last two losses, how we lost."

Golden State Warriors head coach Steve Kerr looks on during an NBA basketball game against the New Orleans Pelicans in New Orleans, Monday, Nov. 16, 2026. AP Photo/Morgan Simmons

And changes are afoot for the Warriors after their lousy 115-94 loss to the Pelicans on Monday.

"We tried to change it up from San Antonio, change the starting lineup, but [it] just didn't seem to have a lot of juice," coach Steve Kerr said. "We're gonna have to change some combinations and find some things that work for us."

The task is easier said than done with Stephen Curry out until at least Thanksgiving.

Without Curry, it's pretty much back to the drawing board for Kerr and his staff. Throw out the window all the lineups that produced the NBA's second-best offensive rating and an eight-game winning streak before Curry tore a muscle in his oblique last week in Houston.

"I've got to do my job as a coach to find the right starting group, to make sure we have good five-man combinations coming off the bench," Kerr said. "It's kind of the early part of the season where you're experimenting, you're looking at different combinations — that sort of thing. That's our job as a staff, and it's the players' job to find that energy and that juice."

In each of their past three losses, the Warriors have fallen behind by double digits in the first quarter. They lost Curry five minutes into their first game of this road trip against the Rockets and fell behind by 15 early. They allowed the Spurs to open an 18-0 lead en route to a 38-14 advantage after the first quarter on Saturday.

And on Monday, they converted just two attempts from the field on 18 tries while allowing the lowly Pelicans to build a 35-11 first-quarter lead.

"It felt like we had no force, no energy," Kerr said. "Without Steph, we've got to mix and match a little bit. We'll have to experiment with some different lineups.

"But I'm looking for the force of cutting and playing with a sense of urgency, the connection that comes when the ball starts to move off of those cuts — we need much more of that."

Golden State Warriors guard De'Anthony Melton dribbles during an NBA basketball game against the New Orleans Pelicans in New Orleans, Monday, Nov. 16, 2026. AP Photo/Morgan Simmons

Kerr said he would take a look at the Warriors' best two-man combos from last season. By net rating, that would mean more time together on the floor for Brandin Podziemski and Melton (plus-6.1 net rating), Podziemski and Al Horford (plus-4.1), as well as Melton and Gui Santos (plus-2.5).

But here's the Warriors' harsh reality: Of the 13 two-man combos that produced positive net ratings last season, 10 of them are unavailable due to injuries.

Kerr will also study the tape, as he did after the Warriors' 48-point loss to the Spurs on Saturday. He applied what he saw on film and adjusted the starting lineup, subbing out Melton in favor of two-way point guard and 3-point marksman LJ Cryer. That wasn't the answer, either.

Golden State Warriors dribbler LJ Cryer (18) controls the ball against the New Orleans Pelicans in New Orleans, Monday, Nov. 16, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

"One of the things that I saw in San Antonio was [that] Draymond [Green] needed LJ's shooting with him in the pick-and-roll game," Kerr said. "That's why we started LJ. Draymond got him a couple great looks early that didn't go. It's going to happen. It's just one of those nights for LJ."

The Warriors can only afford to have so many more of those nights. The margin for error was already thin waiting on the returns of Jimmy Butler and Moses Moody in January or February.

Now, without Curry, the heat is on Kerr to find a way to win a game. Just one would be a start.

"Without Steph, obviously, we're looking for different combinations that are going to click," he said. "We've got to find combos that provide the shooting and the spacing that we need — the cutting — it's early in the season, so we've got to experiment as well."
