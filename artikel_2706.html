Inter and Stuttgart: Special teams take to the pitch

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

As has become tradition, Champions League fixtures extend beyond the 90 minutes on the pitch, bringing clubs and participants together in their social projects in order to meet and share experiences. The main aim is to offer them a unique opportunity while promoting the fundamental values of sport: friendship, cooperation, and dialogue. These initiatives demonstrate how clubs can build genuine relationships despite their rivalry on the pitch and work together to share positive messages with fans around the world.

On Tuesday, the Stuttgart Special team travelled to Milano by car for two days of inclusion and fun. The highlight was a friendly against Inter Special, a project run in partnership with ASD Sporting 4E that involves more than 30 young people with cognitive disabilities every week. The players train hard for occasions like these: the chance to face prestigious opponents in front of a very special audience! Cheering on the players and offering advice from the sidelines were Hansi Muller, a Legend of both clubs, and Francesco Toldo.

After the friendly, the players had the afternoon free before heading to San Siro, where the two teams will watch the evening's Champions League match together from the stands. It's a fitting way to celebrate a friendship that transcends borders, built on a shared passion for football.
