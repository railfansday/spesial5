Jaguars vs. Giants: Matchup History Through the Decades

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Happy Giants Week, Big Cat County! This is a rare event, as the Jaguars have played the Giants only eight times in franchise history, and the series is currently tied. Out of the four wins, three of them were in Jacksonville, so let's revisit all of the wins while hoping the Jags bring home another one on the road.

Week 2 at Alltel Stadium on September 7, 1997Final score: Giants 13 – Jaguars 40Attendance: 70,581

James Stewart and Natrone Means both scored two touchdowns. Steve Matthews threw for 252 yards and was sacked once. Means led the team with 85 rushing yards, and Jimmy Smith led with 117 receiving yards. Mike Hollis made the four attempted field goals. Travis Davis had a 23-yard interception. Clyde Simmons and Joel Smeenge recorded sacks.

Week 11 at Alltel Stadium on November 20, 2006Final score: Giants 10 – Jaguars 26Attendance: 67,164

A dominant Week 11 win with a 40:19 time of possession. David Garrard completed 19 out of 32 attempted passes for 249 yards. Fred Taylor and Maurice Jones-Drew scored touchdowns. Taylor led the team with 79 rushing yards, and Ernest Wilford led with 72 receiving yards. Josh Scobee made four field goals, the longest being from 48 yards. Deon Grant and Clint Ingram intercepted two passes from Eli Manning, and Bobby McCray sacked Manning for a loss of eight yards.

Week 13 at EverBank Stadium on November 30, 2014Final score: Giants 24 – Jaguars 25Attendance: 64,687

The Jags overcame a 21-point deficit, starting with Josh Scobee making a field goal to end the half. The defense stepped up to the plate with J.T. Thomas and Aaron Colvin returning fumbles for touchdowns. Marqise Lee led the team with 75 receiving yards, scored the only offensive touchdown, and the two two-point conversions failed. Blake Bortles completed 21 out of 35 attempted passes for 194 yards, was sacked seven times for a loss of 54 yards, and led the team with 68 rushing yards. Scobee made both attempted field goals. Geno Hayes, Sen'Derrick Marks, and Tyson Alualu sacked Eli Manning four times for a loss of 34 yards.

Week 1 at MetLife Stadium on September 9, 2018Final score: Jaguars 20 – Giants 15Attendance: 77,992

T.J. Yeldon scored the offensive touchdown. Bortles completed 18 out of 33 attempted passes for 176 yards and was sacked once for a loss of eight yards. Yeldon led the team with 51 rushing yards, and Keelan Cole led with 54 receiving yards. Josh Lambo made the two attempted field goals. Myles Jack turned a 32-yard interception into a touchdown and led the team with ten total tackles. Calais Campbell and Lerentee McCray sacked Eli Manning twice for a loss of 14 yards.
