Mason Rudolph reveals how Aaron Rodgers drama has brought Steelers together

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Mason Rudolph reveals how Aaron Rodgers drama has brought Steelers together appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Pittsburgh Steelers were the center of drama throughout the week leading up to the Week 12 matchup against the Denver Broncos, all thanks to Aaron Rodgers airing out Mike McCarthy on "The Pat McAfee Show." While many believed the team was doomed going up against the Denver Broncos, Pittsburgh walked out of the contest with a 27-5 win. After the game, Mason Rudolph revealed how Rodgers' saga brought the team closer together.

Rudolph, who stepped in as the starting quarterback on short notice after the Steelers released his 42-year-old teammate, claimed that the adversity and outside noise that were created helped the Steelers unite more than ever. Overall, Rudolph was proud of how he and his teammates handled the situation and got the win on Friday.

"Yeah, it was a lot," Rudolph said. "Sort of unprecedented. Nothing I've ever been through, and probably the same for all my teammates. [I'm] just so proud of the way we turned the page, really on Wednesday. And the veteran leadership [from] the Cam Heyward's, Jalen Ramsey's, the T.J. Watt's, really led us forward.

"So thankful to God for keeping me here, eight years later… I think the leadership sort of just rallied around Coach McCarthy," Rudolph continued. "I think, in a weird way, the adversity brought us probably closer together. And I think we'll just keep carrying that into week after week."

The Steelers defense led the way against the Broncos on Friday, forcing three turnovers and turning two of them into touchdowns. Rudolph had a decent performance. He didn't do anything too special, but also didn't do anything detrimental to hurt the team. The seven-year veteran (didn't play the 2022-23 season) finished with 142 yards and a touchdown while completing nine of his 15 pass attempts.

Mason Rudolph will have another chance to lead the way as the Steelers' quarterback in Week 13. Pittsburgh aims to keep its playoff chances alive on December 6 against the Houston Texans.
