Dak Prescott scorches Tennessee defense with four TDs in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Dallas Cowboys QB Dak Prescott completed 28 of 33 passes for 355 yards with four touchdowns without an interception or sack in Week 11 against the Tennessee Titans. He also ran four times for nine yards with another score.

Why It Matters: Prescott led the Cowboys to seven TDs and a field goal on eight possessions (not including the kneel-down on last play of the game). Prescott completed 19 passes in a row – the second-longest streak of his career – and his nine TD passes over the last two weeks are a franchise record. Prescott also surpassed 300 yards passing for a third straight week and for the fifth time this season. He topped 3,000 pass yards for the season and is sitting on 27 total touchdowns against six interceptions. With a sinking Philadelphia Eagles team coming to town on Thanksgiving Day, look for Prescott to post another QB1-level performance.
