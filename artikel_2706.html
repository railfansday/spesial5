Tom Brady raises eyebrows with Drake Maye interception comment

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

NFL legend Tom Brady was on the broadcast call for the New England Patriots' blowout loss to the Green Bay Packers on Sunday.

Patriots quarterback Drake Maye reverted to his turnover-prone ways, throwing three interceptions in the 45-27 loss to the Packers at Gillette Stadium. Brady gave Packers safety Evan Williams his "LFG" Player of the Game award, and during a postgame interview, the seven-time Super Bowl champion made a comment that rose eyebrows in New England.

"Xavier [McKinney] had two picks, you had one on the sideline. There was some deli meat given out by Drake Maye today," Brady told Williams.

Laughing at the comment, Williams responded, "I'm not going to say that, but he was giving us some gifts."

Brady wasn't lying about Maye's bad afternoon, and he was clearly having some fun while doing his job for FOX.

But that comment obviously irked some of the same fans who cheered for Brady during his run as the Patriots' quarterback for nearly two decades.
