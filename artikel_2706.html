Longhorns leap Alabama in new College Football Playoff Rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Texas Longhorns are now in position to get a first round bye in the College Football Playoffs. The CFP rankings committee has jumped Texas up two spots to No. 3 overall in the nation. The Horns now just sit behind No. 1 Georgia and No. 2 Utah.

Alabama has been the higher ranked two-loss SEC team in the CFP rankings the past couple of weeks. But UT has leapfrogged the Crimson Tide. Texas also moves head of UCLA. The Bruins lost to Michigan last weekend.

Quality wins is what has carried Texas to the top three, ahead of one-loss Notre Dame and Miami. The Horns have five wins against teams that were ranked at the time, including then No. 1 Ohio State.

The Fighting Irish are No. 4 in the ranking. Georgia, Utah, Texas and Notre Dame would get the four first round byes.

If the Longhorns beat rival Texas A&M on Friday, then Texas could face Georgia in the SEC Championship Game.
