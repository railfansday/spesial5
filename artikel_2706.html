NFL playoff picture: What does Chiefs-Bills mean for AFC standings?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Two AFC titans renew their rivalry on Thanksgiving night, when Josh Allen and the Buffalo Bills host Patrick Mahomes and the Kansas City Chiefs.

With both teams near the top of the AFC standings, this game has serious playoff implications for both teams and the AFC as a whole.

Let's dive into the playoff scenarios for both the Bills and the Chiefs ahead of this game.

Here is the AFC standings ahead of Week 12:

1. Buffalo Bills (9-1)2. Denver Broncos (8-2)3. Jacksonville Jaguars (7-3)4. Cincinnati Bengals (7-3)5. Kansas City Chiefs (8-2)6. Cleveland Browns (6-4)7. Pittsburgh Steelers (6-4)

8. New England Patriots (5-5)9. Houston Texans (4-6)10. Las Vegas Raiders (4-7)11. Baltimore Ravens (4-7)12. New York Jets (3-8)13. Indianapolis Colts (3-8)14. Los Angeles Chargers (1-9)15. Miami Dolphins (1-9)16. Tennessee Titans (1-9)

With a 9-1 record, the Bills sit atop the AFC standings and in position for the No. 1 seed in the conference, as well as the lone bye week. Buffalo also has a comfortable four-game lead over the New England Patriots in the AFC East, but New England won their meeting earlier this season.

Should Buffalo win on Thursday night, the Bills would improve to 10-1 and maintain control of the top spot in the conference.

A loss, however, would drop Buffalo down to the No. 2 line, and put Kansas City in control of the top seed, at least for the moment.

If the NFL playoffs began today, the Kansas City Chiefs would be in, but as the No. 5 seed in the AFC.

Why? That comes down to tiebreakers. The Chiefs are tied with the Denver Broncos with an 8-2 record, and those two teams have already split their season series. Since both teams are 3-1 in AFC West games, under the league's tiebreaking procedures, the next step is win-loss-tied percentage in common games.

Both teams have defeated the Las Vegas Raiders, while the Chiefs lost to the Seattle Seahawks, a team the Broncos beat. So for the moment, the Broncos are ahead in the AFC West, and they take the No. 2 spot.

However, the Chiefs would not only take control of the top spot in the division, but they would move to the top spot in the AFC overall with a win on Thursday night. That would pull Kansas City level with Buffalo, and the Chiefs would have a head-to-head tiebreaker advantage over the Bills with the victory.

With a loss, the Chiefs would remain in the No. 5 spot. They would not be in danger of sliding down further, as both the Cleveland Browns and the Pittsburgh Steelers — the teams at No. 6 and No. 7 right now — are 6-4. But the Chiefs would be on the wrong end of the head-to-head tiebreaker with Buffalo.

The winner of this game will have a head-to-head tiebreaker advantage, which is the first step in deciding tiebreakers between division winners when it comes to seeding.
