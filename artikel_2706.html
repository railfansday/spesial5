Vincenzo Iaquinta says Juventus let the ideal striker go

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Nick Woltemade has been making an increasingly positive impression at Juventus in recent matches, showing signs that he could become the leading striker the Bianconeri desperately need. The German forward joined from Newcastle United on loan in the summer and has been working hard to adapt to his new surroundings while establishing himself as a regular goalscorer.

Juventus also brought in Randal Kolo Muani during the summer as they attempted to rebuild their attacking options. Both forwards arrived with expectations of strengthening the team's frontline, and Woltemade's performances have reportedly encouraged the club to consider keeping him beyond his current loan spell.

However, former Juventus striker Vincenzo Iaquinta is not convinced that the club have found the complete number nine they need. He believes they may have made a mistake by allowing one of their previous forwards to leave.

Juventus allowed three strikers to leave during the summer transfer window as they prepared for a significant change in their attacking department. Dusan Vlahovic departed as a free agent after failing to agree on a new contract, while Jonathan David and Lois Openda were both sent out on loan.

The club subsequently brought in three forwards to rebuild their options, with Jeff Ekhator joining Kolo Muani and Woltemade. Despite these additions, Iaquinta believes Juventus already possessed a striker capable of leading their attack and should have done more to keep him.

His assessment suggests that the club may have overlooked an important solution when deciding to reshape their frontline.

(Photo by Valerio Pennicino/Getty Images)

Speaking about the club's attacking decisions, Iaquinta made it clear that he believes Vlahovic could have been particularly effective under the current manager, Luciano Spalletti.

He argued that the Serbian forward already possessed the qualities Juventus needed and that keeping him would have been a better decision than allowing him to leave.

As quoted by Tuttomercatoweb, Iaquinta said:

"Juventus already had a complete striker at home (Vlahovic). If he had stayed, he would have done very well under Spalletti."

His comments raise questions about Juventus' summer recruitment strategy, particularly given the resources invested in rebuilding the attack. Although Woltemade and Kolo Muani have opportunities to establish themselves, Iaquinta believes Vlahovic was already the answer Juventus needed.
