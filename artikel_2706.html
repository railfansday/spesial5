Cam Skattebo done with backflip celebration in wake of Aidan Hutchinson injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

That's the message from New York Giants running back Cam Skattebo in regard to his famed touchdown celebration. Skattebo, who has never been one to shy away from contact or a moment to celebrate, saw all he needed to see when it came to Aidan Hutchinson's season-ending injury.

The Detroit Lions' star celebrated a sack of New England Patriots QB Drake Maye by jumping in the air. When Hutchinson landed, he suffered a torn ACL.

And that was enough for Skattebo to retire his backflip celebration – at least for the time being.

"It's hard to see," Skattebo told reporters, via the New York Post's Ryan Dunleavy, after Wednesday's practice. "I'm saying yeah (no more). Mentally, I'm like, 'I'm not doing that.' That ain't going to happen to me."

This isn't the first time that the running back's celebration has been a topic of discussion.

Skattebo was seen on video backflipping multiple times last offseason, despite coming off a serious season-ending ankle injury in 2025. It got to a point where new head coach, John Harbaugh, was asked for his reaction. Harbaugh joked about the situation, saying in part, "Dude, at least stick it."

The 24-year-old brought the celebration back to the field in the Giants' season opener when he scored a touchdown against the Dallas Cowboys.

At the time, he left the door open for a new celebration to emerge, but the backflip resurfaced again as recently as Week 10 against the Washington Commanders on "Thursday Night Football."

So is the backflip done? Maybe for now. Or maybe not.

Skattebo noted it could come back in a big moment, like if the Giants make the playoffs.

In other words, Skattebo and the backflip are taking a break. History says they'll be back together soon enough though.
