Mariska Hargitay, Steve Carell and Jimmy Fallon Among Stars Set for NBC’s 100th Anniversary Special

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Mariska Hargitay, Steve Carell and Jimmy Fallon are among the stars set to take the stage during NBC’s forthcoming anniversary special. Titled “NBC100: The Live Celebration,” the show will air on Dec. 10 at 8 p.m. ET from the Dolby Theatre in Los Angeles on NBC and Peacock.

In addition to Hargitay, Carell and Fallon, announced stars also include: Adam Levine, Andy Buckley, Amy Poehler, Angela Kinsey, B.J. Novak, Blake Shelton, Bob Costas, Brian Baumgartner, Chris Sullivan, Chrissy Metz, Conan O’Brien, Creed Bratton, David Letterman, David Hyde Pierce, Debra Messing, Ellie Kemper, Eric McCormack, Goldie Hawn, Ice T, Jane Leeves, Jenna Fischer, Jerry Seinfeld, John Krasinski, Kate Flannery, Kate McKinnon, Kelsey Grammer, Larry David, Leslie David Baker, Mandy Moore, Mel Brooks, Melora Hardin, Michael Phelps, Mike Tirico, Mindy Kaling, Oscar Nuñez, Peri Gilpin, Phyllis Smith, Queen Latifah, Rainn Wilson, The Roots, S. Epatha Merkerson, Seth Meyers, Simone Biles, Sterling K. Brown, Taylor Goldsmith, Ted Danson and Tina Fey.

Stars from “ER,” “Parks and Recreation,” “30 Rock” and alumni cast from “Saturday Night Live” will also join the special, plus additional cast reunions, musical performances and special appearances, which will be announced at a later date.

“NBC100: The Live Celebration” is produced by Done + Dusted. Raj Kapoor, Katy Mullan and Sarah Levine Hall serve as executive producers with Rob Paine, Taryn Hurd and Patrick Menton serving as co-executive producers.
