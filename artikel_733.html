Miranda Lambert and Ella Langley Perform Their Fierce Collaboration ‘Snakeskin Boots’ at CMA Awards 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The two took a break from accepting several of the night's top honors to perform together.

After gracing the CMA Awards stage together to accept single, song and album of the year – for “Choosin’ Texas” and Dandelion, respectively – Miranda Lambert and Ella Langley joined forces yet again to perform their empowered collaboration “Snakeskin Boots” at the 60th annual CMAs on Wednesday, Nov. 18 in Nashville. Explore Ella Langley Miranda Lambert See latest videos, charts and news

“Snakeskin Boots” appears on Lambert’s 10th album, Crisco, that arrived in October. The album debuted at No. 19 on the Billboard 200, No. 4 on the Top Country Albums chart and No. 5 on Top Album Sales. Meanwhile, “Snakeskin Boots” entered the Billboard Hot 100 at No. 42 and Hot Country Songs at No. 12. “Snakeskin Boots” is the second collaboration from the pair, as Lambert first featured on Langley’s “Butterfly Season” off of Dandelion. Lambert co-produced Langley’s chart-topping second album, including its breakout hit “Choosin’ Texas,” which she also co-wrote. (And, last year, the two performed Lambert’s Kerosene” together at the 2025 ACMs).

Speaking to Billboard for a May feature on Lambert, Langley said of the icon: “There’s not a rule book to this job. So [it’s helpful] to be able to have someone like her to ask questions [like], ‘Should I be doing this?’ She’s like, ‘Hell yeah, you should be doing this!’ It’s just a cool time for the both of us.”

In May, when Lambert released her album’s title track, “Crisco,” it marked her first release since signing to Nashville-based label MCA after two years on Republic Records. As Lambert told Billboard in the same interview, “When I started my career, I told my manager I wanted a 50-year career. I’m over 20 years in and still have a lot of fire left in me…I just want to be open and let the world tell me the direction I’m supposed to go.”
