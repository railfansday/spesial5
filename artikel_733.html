“Wicker Nipples” and All: ‘Wicker’ Cinematographer Celebrates Steamy Film’s Viral Success

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

"Now I know what 'wicker nipples' is in Polish," Lol Crawley quipped at Camerimage about the film that stars Olivia Colman and Alexander Skarsgard.

Wicker‘s filmmaking team is relishing its viral moments and box office success — not to mention its horny memes.

During a screening of Wicker at ToruńCamerimage Film Festival in Poland on Thursday, cinematographer Lol Crawley introduced the movie and expressed appreciation for it having become a social media sensation. Olivia Colman stars in directors Eleanor Wilson and Alex Huston Fischer’s romantic fantasy as a lonely woman who commissions a basket weaver (Peter Dinklage) to create a husband (Alexander Skarsgard) for her. “It’s my fourth time back at the festival and having the honor of screening a film in competition, so thank you so much to Camerimage,” Crawley said from the stage about the festival that honors cinematography. “I really do hope you enjoy it as much as we enjoyed making it.”

Crawley went on to recount the movie’s unlikely path from the festival circuit to internet obsession: “The film premiered at Sundance in January of this year, and since the trailer came out and thanks to all the love on social media, it’s just kind of snowballed. It’s gone from its premiere to wicker nipples being available and cardboard cutouts of Alex [Skarsgard] in all his fine wicker glory.”

Wicker has managed to heat things up since Black Bear released it theatrically on Oct. 23. With the makings of a sleeper hit, the film has grossed $23.7 million domestically and an additional $13.4 million internationally through Tuesday. In his review for The Hollywood Reporter, critic Richard Lawson noted its “winning mix of irreverence and compassion.”

The cinematographer acknowledged that the project, notable for its comedic moments, has a bit of a different tone than his previous work. Crawley won Camerimage’s coveted Golden Frog in 2024 for lensing The Brutalist before going on to win the Academy Award for the film.

“It’s a fantastic dark fable — quite different material for me,” Crawley added of Wicker. “But very, very enjoyable experience and very, very proud to present the film.”

Later, after a festival representative had finished translating Crawley’s speech into Polish for the crowd, the Wicker cinematographer quipped, “Now I know what ‘wicker nipples’ is in Polish, which is a takeaway for me.”
