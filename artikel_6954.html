Your Aquarius Monthly Horoscope for December 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Your social calendar is booked up, Aquarius!

You’re chasing after your dreams with all you’ve got, Aquarius! The month begins with the Sun traveling through Sagittarius, helping you pour more effort into your goals and make your future a reality now. Supportive people might also show up in your life or you could learn about an opportunity that feels expansive and exciting. Community is what fills your cup and you’re also craving more time with people who make you feel seen.

You’re receiving your flowers once Venus enters Scorpio on December 4. You might gain more attention at work, be up for a raise or promotion, or find that others are just gravitating toward you. There’s also more support available to you, and this is a season of manifestation. Let others help you bring your visions to life, and let yourself sink into joy and the present moment, too. It’s time to celebrate yourself and witness your own growth and how far you’ve come.

Here comes a wave of optimism thanks to Mercury entering Sagittarius on December 6. You’re more willing to say “yes” to opportunities that come your way and try a new approach. You might be in a more social or adventurous mood—meeting new people, trying out new events, and pushing yourself outside of your comfort zone. You’re also feeling hopeful about some of your goals, finding new ways to pursue them and make progress on what excites you.

A New Moon in Sagittarius gives you the faith necessary to chase after one of your dreams on December 8. This could show up as a job offer or a feeling of being in the right place at the right time. There is a serendipity quality to this new moon that helps you make aligned decisions about your future. It’s also possible a new friendship or alliance is formed, or you’re finally finding your place within a group or social circle that gives you purpose.

Conversations are tough but help you address what you’ve ignored when Saturn stations direct in Aries on December 10. You might need to set a firm boundary or learn disappointing news. This can also bring contracts your way, but make sure you read the fine print and are being recognized for your worth, talent, and what you bring to the table before singing on the dotted line.

It’s easier than ever to share your feelings when Neptune stations direct in Aries on December 12. This energy is beautiful for journaling or creative writing. There’s some extra texture, charm, and warmth to your words. Healing conversations can also take place, bringing sensitive topics to the surface. This is also a great time to dust off your tarot deck and pick up a consistent spiritual practice or do some divination. Some interactions might be confusing, asking you to reflect on what is real vs. what is fantasy.

Your relationships are undergoing seismic changes when Jupiter stations retrograde in Leo, also on December 12. This can feel like an all-or-nothing moment, causing larger ripple effects in your closest connections. You could have a conversation or interaction with someone that tests the foundation of your relationship, prompting more reflection before you make any major decisions. There could also be a development regarding a contract or client relationship—a turning point that will take some time to unpack before you decide what comes next or how you want to react.

The Sun enters Capricorn and the Winter Solstice takes place on December 21, encouraging you to spend some time alone. You might be socially drained and this isn’t the time to give into FOMO. Spending time alone and even honing a spiritual practice—whether that’s an evening walk, nightly meditations, or something your Ancestors practiced—will bring you the grounding, clarity, and guidance that you seek.

A Full Moon in Cancer on December 23 helps you change up your lifestyle. If there are certain habits and patterns that have kept you repeating the same old cycles and living in an eternal groundhog day, this is your chance to make a change. You might also deal with a reality check regarding your own wellbeing, recognizing you have to stop sacrificing your body, mind, and spirit’s needs to keep other people happy. Setting boundaries and finding a more consistent routine for self-care can be just what you need.

The month wraps up with you relying more on your intuition once Mercury enters Capricorn on December 25. Your connection to the other Realms is evident, and you’re trusting your own instincts more and more. You might also feel more exhausted and worn down. This energy can show up when you’re struggling with insomnia, overextending yourself, or needing to be accountable for your own behaviors. This can help you close out cycles and situations in your life that have run its course by confronting the programming that’s running in the background of your subconscious. It’s time for an upgrade!
