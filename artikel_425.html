This hologram box makes a glowing gift — and it’s just $90 for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

TL;DR: Spectra’s 7-inch Hologram Box is just $89.99, using rotating LEDs to display floating-image effects, with preloaded animations and custom uploads.

Your desk probably has enough things that display notifications. Here’s something that can simply look interesting. The 7-inch Spectra Hologram Box is $89.99 (reg. $149.99) during this early Black Friday sale, turning animations, artwork, and logos into glowing images that appear to float inside a clear case.

The effect comes from 276 LEDs on a rotating blade, which creates the illusion of a three-dimensional image. It’s a visual effect rather than a solid object floating in midair, and the spinning components sit behind an acrylic cover.

For a home office or gaming setup, think of moving the centerpiece rather than adding another work screen. On a reception desk or retail counter, it could display a business logo or a compatible product animation. It’s also a gift option for someone who enjoys tech with a visual twist. You don’t have to create anything to get started. Demo animations come preloaded on the included microSD card, and an online library offers additional animations and assets without an account or subscription. Want something personal? Upload compatible images, logos, or animations, or create 3D text effects.

The iPhone and Android companion app handles content and starts or stops the display. Your phone connects directly to Spectra’s own Wi-Fi hotspot, so controlling it and uploading files doesn’t require a router or internet connection.

Prefer to leave your phone alone? The included remote controls playback, stored content, brightness, and image angle. A 160-degree viewing angle gives you flexibility in where to place it.
