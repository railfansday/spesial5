Thom Velde, Longtime AMC Theatres Executive, Dies at 83

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

He served as head film buyer and oversaw Canadian operations and real estate ventures during his nearly three decades with the company.

Thom Velde, who served as head film buyer at AMC Theatres during his 27 years with the chain, died of cancer on Oct. 31 in Rancho Mirage, his family announced. He was 83.

Velde negotiated film agreements with domestic and international studios and oversaw AMC’s Canadian operations and real estate ventures during his long tenure at the company.

After leaving AMC, the University of Arizona graduate worked independently in motion picture distribution and consulted for clients including Microspace Communications and CJ America. Survivors include his wife, Deborah; son Sam; daughters Andrea and Courtney and their respective spouses, John and Greg; stepsons Matthew and Spencer; grandchildren Aubrey and Jack; and step-grandchildren Jackson, Sammy, Cruz, Bianca and Capri.

He was preceded in death by his wife, Kathy, and his daughter, Jennifer.

A celebration of life is set for 3 p.m. on Dec. 8 at Woodland Hills Community Church, with a reception to follow at the church.

Donations in his memory may be made to the Will Rogers Motion Picture Pioneers Foundation or to the Motion Picture & Television Fund, two organizations that serve the industry community and were “such an important part of his working life,” his family noted.

“Thom was an avid golfer and a devoted college football fan,” they continued. “Above all, he loved his family and friends, who will remember his strength of character, his generosity and the way he made time for the people in his life. He was always willing to lend a hand and had a special gift for keeping friendships alive over the years.”
