Man Utd plotting Toney move - Monday's gossip

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Manchester United eyeing move for Al-Ahli striker Ivan Toney, Fulham holding contract talks with Calvin Bassey and Paris St-Germain could step up pursuit of Bournemouth forward Eli Junior Kroupi.

Manchester United are looking to sign Al-Ahli's England striker Ivan Toney in January, but other Premier League clubs are also chasing the 30-year-old. (Teamtalk), external

Arsenal and Chelsea both hold a long-term interest in Como boss Cesc Fabregas, who played for both London clubs, becoming their next manager. (Football Insider), external

Nigeria defender Calvin Bassey has been linked with Manchester United, but the 26-year-old says he is in talks about extending his deal with Fulham. (Standard), external

Liverpool and Argentina midfielder Alexis Mac Allister, 27, is desperate to sign a new deal at Anfield and contract talks are accelerating. (Football Insider), external

Arsenal and Liverpool both hold a long-term interest in Jamal Musiala, 23, and could make a move for the Germany midfielder whose future at Bayern Munich is uncertain. (Talksport), external

Paris St-Germain could step up their pursuit of Bournemouth's 20-year-old France forward Eli Junior Kroupi, after his international team-mate Desire Doue, 21, sustained a serious knee injury. (Teamtalk), external

Schalke 04 keeper Lorius Karius has been linked with Bayern Munich but the 33-year-old German says he has not spoken to anyone about a move. (Sky Sports Germany), external

AC Milan favour a move for Real Madrid and Brazil striker Endrick, 20, over an attempt to sign Club Brugge's 22-year-old Germany youth international Nicolo Tresoldi. (Gazzetta dello Sport - in Italian), external
