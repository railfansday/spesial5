Fourth ‘Terminal List’ Show in the Works at Prime Video

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

This one is based on Jack Carr's Vietnam-set novel 'Cry Havoc.'

The Terminal List keeps getting longer at Prime Video.

On the heels of the Amazon-owned streamer ordering a series based on Jack Carr’s novel Savage Son, Prime Video on Monday announced it’s developing a fourth show in the franchise. Cry Havoc is based on Carr’s 2025 novel of the same name, which is set in Vietnam circa 1968 and centers on Tom Reece, the father of The Terminal List’s James Reece, played by Chris Pratt.

The show’s logline reads, “Tom Reece is a brash Navy SEAL assigned to the top secret MACV-SOG [Military Assistance Command, Vietnam-Studies and Observations Group] unit, which runs harrowing cross-border operations in Laos. When Tom’s recon team comes across plans for the upcoming Tet Offensive, it unleashes a chain of events that pulls the young warrior out of the jungles and into the shadows of Cold War espionage.” Related Stories TV Streaming Ratings: 'From' Dominates an Otherwise Quiet Halloween Week TV Prime Video Unveils 'Maxton Hall' Final Season Trailer

Max Adams, a writer on The Terminal List, and showrunner David Digilio, are writing the pilot script. They developed Cry Havoc for television along with Carr and Jared Shaw, an executive producer (and actor) on the main series.

Digilio will also serve as showrunner on Cry Havoc. He executive produces with Adams, Pratt (via his Indivisible Productions), Carr, Shaw, and Antoine Fuqua and Kat Samick of Fuqua Films. The studios are MRC and Amazon MGM.

The flurry of activity around the Terminal List franchise comes a few weeks after the show’s second season premiere, which has scored solid viewing numbers for Prime Video. A decision on season three hasn’t come yet. Savage Son will also center on Pratt’s James Reece; the source novel also features some characters from The Terminal List, though Pratt is the only actor attached at the moment.

The franchise also includes a prequel series, Dark Wolf, that premiered in August 2025.
