GIVĒON Returns to Long Beach for the Ultimate Victory Lap | Billboard Presents

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

In a special Driven by Legacy episode of Billboard Presents, presented by Lexus, eight-time Grammy nominee GIVĒON takes Billboard’s Carl Lamarre on a nostalgic ride through his beloved hometown of Long Beach, California, revisiting the people, places and memories that shaped his journey to stardom.

From bringing a student to tears during a surprise visit to his alma mater, Long Beach Poly High School, to returning to Bubba Gump Shrimp Co., GIVĒON reflects on his humble beginnings. He also stops by Fingerprints Records, the local record store where his own albums now line the shelves — a full-circle moment years in the making.

From teenage dreams of stardom to performing at Madison Square Garden, the hometown hero reveals a more personal side of himself, celebrating the roots that shaped his sound, the community that raised him and the West Coast legacy that continues to drive him forward.

Long Beach has always been a city that writes its own rules when it comes to West Coast hip-hop and R&B.
