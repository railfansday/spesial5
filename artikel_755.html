Burnley break winless home record as Birmingham win at Turf Moor

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Burnley vs Birmingham City. EFL Championship.

Report and free match highlights from the Sky Bet Championship match between Burnley and Birmingham City at Turf Moor on Wednesday night; Clarets make it 24 winless games in a row at home in the league, the longest run in English Football League history.

Burnley set a new record for the longest home run without a league win as Birmingham reaffirmed their Sky Bet Championship promotion credentials with a 2-0 victory at Turf Moor thanks to August Priske's strike and Jaydon Banel's own goal.

It is now 24 games since the Clarets last celebrated three points in front of their own fans, a nightmare run that has now stretched to 403 days. It represents the worst sequence in the history of English football.

Burnley, relegated from the Premier League last season, remain firmly ensconced in the drop zone. Coach Fabio Celestini has picked up just three points in the five games he has taken charge off since succeeding Nicky Hayen last month and his team were jeered off at the end.

For City, however, it was a timely return to form following the 1-0 defeat at Stoke on Saturday. This was a seventh win in nine for Chris Davies' team and they are just two places and two points off the automatic promotion spots.

Burnley started on the front foot, with Aaron Ramsey forcing goalkeeper James Beadle into action with a low drive from 25 yards.

But the visitors went ahead in the seventh minute when James Stansfield was found in space by Carlos Vicente and his driven cross was diverted home by the sliding Priske. It was the Dane's eighth goal of the season.

The Clarets responded positively, with Largie Ramazani forcing Beadle into two smart saves and also having a shot deflected over. Zeki Amdouni also sent a decent chance too high.

Birmingham should have doubled their lead just before the break, but after Liam Millar had shaken off a couple of weak challenges, his shot lacked pace and precision and home keeper Max Weiss was able to save.

The visitors doubled their lead in the 52nd minute when Millar's inswinging corner from the right flicked off Banel's head and looped into the far corner of his own goal.

Priske saw the ball shave the post as he arrived under pressure from Max Alleyne to meet Bright Osayi-Samuel's cross as the visitors looked to turn the screw, with Burnley's fans beginning to voice their discontent.

The Clarets' only win this season came at Watford last month. They have now conceded 35 goals, the same number as bottom club Derby.

It needed a sprawling Weiss save to prevent defender Igor scoring the second own goal of the game when he sent an attempted chest pass towards the corner of the net before the keeper intervened.

Celestini sent on Jamie Vardy in an attempt to salvage something, only for fellow substitute Andreas Hountondji to see his shot saved by Beadle after breaking clear.

Birmingham could have scored again four minutes from time but Demarai Gray's penalty was saved by Weiss after he had been fouled by Alleyne.

"I understand the fans are frustrated. They have been waiting for a victory here for a long time. The reaction the fans showed is right at this moment, so it is head down and work harder.

"If they see some good things then they are with us 100 per cent, like they were at the start. They are frustrated but we have to change that.

"We lost the game because Birmingham were better than us in both boxes. In the 70 metres in the centre of the pitch, we were better than them. But they have one shot and score one goal and that made it very difficult.

"If we can be more efficient then I will be in absolutely no doubt about my team because I see good things. Tomorrow is another day and we must keep fighting.

"When I came here, it was clear that we would have to fight. This is not a surprise. But I really do see some good things."

"It was an excellent win. It was all about solidity, appetite and application.

"I am told this was our fifth away win, which was as many as we managed in the whole of last season, so that shows you something.

"I made a few changes and I think the balance I was trying to strike came to fruition. We scored twice and could have scored even more. We created some very good chances.

"I heard some of our fans had a problem on the M6. It took some of them three hours to get here on a freezing midweek night in the middle of winter.

"So it was good to play like that, get the win and send them home loud and proud and happy with their team."
