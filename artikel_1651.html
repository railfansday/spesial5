Opinion: Liverpool have found their next Mo Salah, he's going to be incredible

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Liverpool have found their next Mo Salah, and he's going to be incredible.

Nobody wanted things to come to an end this summer. Things had gone badly for Salah in his final season at Anfield.

The conflict between him and Arne Slot ultimately forced him to make a decision he might no have made otherwise.

With the benefit of hindsight. Knowing both Slot and Richard Hughes as well as Michael Edwards left the club, Salah might have opted to stay put.

However, he made the decision to leave and agreed with Liverpool to rip up the final year on his contract in return for a new adventure.

The Egyptian opted to find that adventure in Turkey, where he was welcomed like a hero in Trabzonspor and the rest is history.
