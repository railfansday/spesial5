Jamie Carragher issues Manchester United relegation verdict after Liverpool defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Jamie Carragher believes that Manchester United face a fight to stay in the Premier League.

The Red Devils remain mired in the top flight’s relegation zone after their heavy 4-0 defeat by Liverpool on Sunday, which saw a nightmare debut for new manager Mauricio Pochettino.

The Argentine, who began work at Carrington last Wednesday, watched on in horror as his new side were outplayed from the off at Anfield, with Leny Yoro sent off inside eight minutes for hauling down Alexander Isak, who was clean through on goal.

Dominik Szoboszlai, Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz got the goals to round off the rout on Merseyside, and it leaves United with just one win in 12 matches in all competitions.

On Sky Sports, Carragher said: “They’re in a relegation fight right now because of where they are in the table. You can’t say anything else when you look at their record this season.

“If the name wasn’t Manchester United and you’re watching the performances, you’d say that is a team that’s in a relegation fight. They’re conceding lots of goals, comedy of errors, red cards, they’ve won one game all season – that is a team in a relegation fight.

“We don’t believe it will happen over a 38-game season but they haven’t had the most difficult of starts. They’ve played a lot of promoted teams, they haven’t played the best team in the league Arsenal yet.

“I don’t think they’ll go down, but this idea that they haven’t got a scrap on their hands- they have.”
