Star Wars: Galactic Racer Speed Legends Update Brings New Podracers, Scenarios, Purchasable Vehicles, And More Next Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Star Wars: Galactic Racer launched on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, and PC last month, and since then, players have taken a liking to Biddy Blas, completed hundreds of podraces, and more. More content is on the way, according to developer Fuse Games, which has revealed an upcoming "Speed Legends" update coming sometime next month.

More specifically, the free Speed Legends update will include private lobbies, input re-binding, two new podracers, eight podracing Scenarios, and more. This update also marks the first premium content: the M-68 Landspeeder Icon vehicle from Solo: A Star Wars Story and three premium speeders from Star Wars: The Mandalorian.
