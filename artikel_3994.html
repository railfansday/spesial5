GTA 6 Ties With Zelda: Ocarina Of Time As The Highest-Rated Game Ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Well folks, it's official: Grand Theft Auto 6 reviews are out, and it's currently tied with The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (N64) as the highest-rated game ever on Metacritic.

There may yet be time for things to change as more reviews trickle in, but at the time of writing, GTA 6 is sitting at a remarkable 99 on the aggregate website, based on a total of 134 reviews.

Ocarina of Time, meanwhile, has a mere 22 critic reviews making up that lofty 99 average (it was a different time, man), so one could argue that GTA 6's result is probably more impressive – especially in an age in which it's getting harder and harder to make such an impact.

Our pals over at Push Square were emphatic in their praise, calling GTA 6 "the greatest open world game" in their 10/10 review. Meanwhile, our other pals over at Pure Xbox also gave it 10/10, stating "Rockstar Games has crafted the most incredible open world I've ever experienced in a video game, packed with so much detail that I'll want to continue exploring it for months and likely years to come".

There are 10s all over the damn place, with the only outliers awarding it 9s or 90s at the lowest. It's pretty unprecedented stuff.

We'll keep an eye on things and update this article if GTA 6 does happen to slip below Ocarina of Time. Elsewhere, the latter's Switch 2 remake also released to almost universal acclaim earlier this month. Critic reviews on Metacritic average out at 96, while it also boasts the highest user review score of all time at 9.6.
