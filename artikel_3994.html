Cozy life simulation game Wish Upon a Llama launches December 9 for PS5, Xbox Series, PS4, Xbox One, Switch, and PC

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Cozy life simulation game Wish Upon a Llama will launch for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Switch, and PC via Steam and Epic Games Store on December 9, publisher Whitethorn Games and developer Millionhare Studios announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via its store pages:

Animals and people of Llama Town once lived side by side in harmony, a special bond that has since faded. It’s up to you as Llama Town’s new rancher to rekindle this forgotten connection! You’ll be raising generations of companions such as bunnies, capybaras, and horses, and running the adoption center to help them find loving homes. Which unique breeds will you discover? Who among the townsfolk will you befriend, marry, and maybe even start a family with?

Care for a growing family of lovable animals, each with their own unique colours, patterns, personalities, and inherited genetic traits. Babies inherit characteristics from both parents, so thoughtful breeding is key as you discover 50+ unique breeds and nurture generations of furry friends!

Strengthen the bond between you and your animals by providing wholehearted love through activities like:

Run Llama Town’s adoption center and bring the community back together one friendship at a time! Choose which of your animals are ready to meet their forever families, fulfill each resident’s personal adoption requests, and manage the center to make it a warm and welcoming space. Different residents connect with different companions depending on their personalities and traits, so finding the perfect match matters. Don’t worry, you’ll continue to see the critters you adopt out with their new families around town, so this isn’t goodbye!

Llama Town boasts a diverse and vibrant population for you to meet! From the bookish doctor to the rugged handyman, self-taught physicist, and aspiring designer, every resident has their own personality and story waiting to unfold. Forge new friendships and perhaps discover romance!

Challenge your friends to activities like jump rope, town cleaning, trivia games, spelling bees, and more! There’s never a dull moment in Llama Town!
