These 8+ PS5 Games Will Be Delisted in November and December 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The end of the year is always a prime time for PS5 game delistings and server closures, but fortunately we’re being spared a large list this year.

There are about eight titles with impending changes you need to be aware of, so here’s the full list:

Sony game Destruction AllStars will go completely offline on 25th November, following some alterations earlier in the year.

This will render most of the single player content unplayable, outside of a few arcade challenges, although the platform holder says your overall experience may be affected in those too.

In-game purchases will then be removed from Synduality: Echoes of Ada, the Bandai Namco live service shooter. This is the beginning of the publisher repurposing the game as a single player-only experience, with more changes planned in 2027.

PSVR2 MMO Zenith: Nexus will go offline on 18th December, and then the end of the year brings a slew of delistings, primarily from publisher GameMill.

This is due to licenses expiring for franchises like Avatar: The Last Airbender and Street Outlaws. If you purchase these games before they’re removed, they’ll remain in your library.

While it never officially released, PlayStation fans may want to pay attention to the impending closure of Ratchet & Clank: Ranger Rumble, which will be removed from mobile storefronts on 21st November.

One final thing to note is that 2K Sports will disable the online servers of NBA 2K25 on 31st December, but single player modes will remain.

That’s the full breakdown of everything being delisted in November and December 2026, then. I’ll update should any other announcements be made before the end of the year.
