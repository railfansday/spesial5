Asheville Pediatric Dentist Blue Ridge Pediatric Dentistry Launches New Website for Western North Carolina Families

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Blue Ridge Pediatric Dentistry, the North Asheville pediatric dental practice founded in 2011 by board-certified pediatric dentist Dan Knechtel, DDS, has launched a new website at www.brpedo.com. The site walks parents through the practice's preventive, restorative, comfort and emergency care for children from their first birthday through their late teens, along with resources to prepare for a child's first visit.

ASHEVILLE, N.C., Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Blue Ridge Pediatric Dentistry has launched a new website at www.brpedo.com. The site introduces families to the practice at 218 Elkwood Avenue in North Asheville, just off Merrimon Avenue, where Dan Knechtel, DDS, has provided dental care exclusively for children and teens since opening the practice in 2011.

The new website is organized around the stages of a child's dental care. Preventive care pages explain the age-one dental visit, infant oral care and teething, cleanings and exams, fluoride treatments, dental sealants, cavity risk assessment, growth and development monitoring and nutrition and home care for kids. Restorative care pages cover tooth-colored fillings, pediatric crowns, pulp therapy, tooth extractions and space maintainers, and a dedicated emergency care page tells parents what to do when a child has a toothache, a chipped or broken tooth or a dental injury.

A detailed Behavior Guidance and Comfort Options page explains how the practice helps nervous children through a visit. Most children do well with clear explanations, encouragement and distraction, and the page also describes nitrous oxide, moderate sedation and general anesthesia, which is provided in the office by an anesthesiologist while Dr. Knechtel completes the dental treatment. Parents are welcome to stay with their children, and no referral is required to schedule an appointment.

The site also gives families practical tools before they arrive. Parents can review Your Child's First Visit, complete new patient forms, read insurance and financial information and browse frequently asked questions, an office tour and an educational blog. A Spanish-language section invites Spanish-speaking families to call the office, where Spanish-speaking staff are available.

Dr. Knechtel earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill in 2002 and practiced general dentistry for four years before completing his pediatric dentistry residency at Yale New Haven Hospital in 2008. He is board certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, serves on the medical staff at Mission Hospital and is a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry and the North Carolina Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. He served as president of the Buncombe County Dental Society in 2013, and he has coached his children's basketball and baseball teams in the community for the past 14 years.

"Parents know their children better than anyone, so I want them in the conversation," said Dan Knechtel, DDS, of Blue Ridge Pediatric Dentistry. "We have been caring for Asheville's kids since 2011, and so many of the questions we hear from parents come before the first appointment, about when to start dental visits, what to expect or how we help a nervous child feel comfortable. The new website answers those questions in plain language so families can walk in feeling prepared, and it makes it easy for them to reach us when their child needs care."

More About Blue Ridge Pediatric Dentistry

Blue Ridge Pediatric Dentistry is a pediatric dental practice in Asheville, North Carolina, providing care for children from age one through their late teens since 2011. Led by Dan Knechtel, DDS, a board-certified pediatric dentist and member of the medical staff at Mission Hospital, the practice offers preventive care, restorative care, behavior guidance and comfort options including in-office general anesthesia, and emergency dental care. Dr. Knechtel earned his dental degree from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and completed his pediatric dentistry residency at Yale New Haven Hospital. Spanish-speaking staff are available.

Blue Ridge Pediatric Dentistry is located at 218 Elkwood Avenue, Suite 101, Asheville, NC 28804. For more information or to schedule an appointment, please call (828) 575-2345 or visit www.brpedo.com.
