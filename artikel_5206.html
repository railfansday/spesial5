Sky offers nearly 30% yield but 99.96% of its lending vault is unavailable for immediate withdrawals

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Sky’s stUSDS vault had just 0.04% of assets available for withdrawal, leaving savers dependent on loan repayments or new deposits.

Sky’s stUSDS lending vault had $60,484 worth of USDS available for immediate withdrawals at 17:50 UTC on Nov. 20, about 0.04% of the vault’s $161.6 million in total assets, according to Spark.

Spark’s dashboard showed about $161.5 million in USDS out on loan at the time, and those loans account for nearly all of the vault’s reported value.

A holder could redeem only from the portion available in USDS. For example, a saver with $100,000 worth of stUSDS could not have redeemed that entire position directly at the time of the reading, even if nobody else withdrew first.

Savers deposit USDS and receive stUSDS tokens representing their position, and the pool lends USDS to borrowers who put up SKY tokens as collateral. Spark’s dashboard displayed a 29.74% annual percentage yield at the time.

Sky explains that the yield varies with lending conditions. Funds in use earn a rate tied to what borrowers pay, and unused USDS earns the Sky Savings Rate.

The Spark reading measures withdrawal liquidity across the entire vault.

Larger direct withdrawals may have to wait for more USDS to enter the lending pool.

Divide the $60,484 of withdrawal liquidity by $161,583,152 of total vault assets and multiply by 100. The result is 0.03743%, rounded to 0.04%. The connectors show possible sources of liquidity described by Sky.

When borrowers repay, the USDS they return can become available for withdrawals. New deposits can also add USDS to the pool before borrowers pay down their loans.

When nearly all of the pool is already lent out, a holder may have to wait for more USDS to come in before redeeming their full position.

Sky says the module has no mechanism to liquidate borrowers early to free up funds for depositors. The loan collateral is in SKY, while holders redeem their stUSDS shares for USDS available in the vault.

Sky Frontier Foundation separately said on Nov. 20 that users exchanged more than $2 billion of USDS for USDC the previous day through Sky’s Peg Stability Module. The foundation said the facility continued to swap the two stablecoins at a one-to-one rate during the Nov. 19 activity.

The swap facility serves users who already have USDS to exchange. An stUSDS holder using the vault would first have to redeem for USDS before using that route to get USDC.

The amount a vault depositor can withdraw depends on how much USDS is in the lending pool when the redemption is attempted.
