Dexsport, a Web3-native iGaming platform, has expanded the centralized exchange presence of its native utility token, DESU, with listings on LBank, Toobit and WEEX over a six-week period.

The latest addition went live on November 10, when LBank opened DESU/USDT spot trading. It followed the launch of DESU on Toobit on November 2 and WEEX on October 1.

Together, the listings extend a CEX expansion that began in June, when DESU made its centralized exchange debut on MEXC alongside a $40,000 community campaign.

The expansion is part of Dexsport’s effort to make DESU easier to access for users who approach crypto in different ways and from different parts of the world. DESU remains the utility token of the Dexsport platform, while the growing number of centralized venues gives users more choice in how they acquire it before moving it into the product.

For most of its history, DESU was primarily available through PancakeSwap on BNB Chain. That changed with the MEXC listing in June.

The next three listings arrived much faster.

WEEX opened DESU/USDT trading on October 1. Toobit followed on November 2 after rescheduling its original October 30 launch, while LBank added the same spot pair on November 10.

The four centralized exchanges now sit alongside DESU’s original decentralized trading route on PancakeSwap.

Each venue operates independently and applies its own account, trading and withdrawal rules. The listings do not change DESU’s role within Dexsport; instead, they create additional entry points for users who prefer acquiring crypto through centralized exchanges before transferring it to a Web3 wallet.

“When DESU first moved beyond decentralized trading with MEXC, the goal was to give users another practical way to access the token,” said Ivan Müller, CEO of Dexsport. “Some users are comfortable with wallets and swaps, while others already use centralized exchanges every day. As DESU has a practical role inside Dexsport, we want it to be easy to reach for users in different parts of the world. Adding WEEX, Toobit and LBank gives them more flexibility in how they acquire it.”

DESU is used within Dexsport’s product and reward structure and can be deposited and withdrawn through the platform on BNB Chain.

Dexsport has also continued its token burn programme. According to Dexsport, 450 million DESU have been permanently removed from the original one billion token supply across 21 burn events, bringing the reported total supply to 550 million DESU. The burn transactions can be tracked on-chain.

The exchange expansion comes during a year in which Dexsport has also broadened the product around the token.

The year also saw Dexsport deepen its presence in esports through a partnership with OG Esports. Dexsport became the official Web3 betting partner and headline sponsor of the organisation’s Counter-Strike 2 roster, now competing as OG.Dexsport.

In October, the company made its first major offline appearance at TOKEN2049 Singapore, where DESU was among the subjects discussed with exchanges and other Web3 companies.

Dexsport is a Web3 iGaming platform combining sportsbook, casino and Prediction Markets in a single ecosystem. The platform supports more than 110 cryptocurrencies across 29 blockchain networks, allowing users to connect and play through a crypto wallet. Dexsport also operates its native DESU token and continues to expand its product range across sports betting, casino gaming and event-based prediction markets.
