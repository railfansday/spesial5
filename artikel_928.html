Ballon d'Or winner Kane signs new Bayern deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

England captain Harry Kane signs new two-and-a-half-year contract at Bayern Munich; the 33-year-old joined from Tottenham in 2023 and has scored 156 goals in 161 games for the Bundesliga giants; Kane became the first Englishman to win the Ballon d'Or in 25 years last month

Harry Kane has signed a new contract with Bayern Munich.

The deal will see him remain with the Bundesliga giants until the summer of 2029.

Sky in Germany are reporting Kane's new salary is at least £20.9m (€25m) gross per year.

"I feel right at home at FC Bayern," Kane told the club's official website.

"I love the club, the fans and the city of Munich. We have a fantastic team and, in Vincent Kompany, a fantastic manager. It makes me very proud to be part of this club.

"My whole family is very happy here. I was keen to stay. I'm now looking forward to the future and I want to win many more titles with this great club."

Kane has scored a phenomenal 156 goals in 161 matches since moving from Tottenham to Bayern in 2023.

His 73 goals for club and country last season - including six in the 2026 FIFA World Cup with England - helped him become the first English winner of the men's Ballon d'Or for 25 years.

During his time at Bayern, Kane has won two Bundesliga titles - the first in 2025 acting as the first major trophy of his career - as well as last season's German Cup and the German Super Cup in the last two years.

In the last three years, Kane has also won the European Golden Shoe twice - given to the top scorer on the continent - and has won the Bundesliga Golden Boot in each of his three seasons at Bayern.

Kane also took England to the Euro 2024 final during his time with the German giants, as well as leading the Three Lions to their best World Cup finish since 1966 earlier this summer.

Last month he equalled Peter Shilton's England appearance record by winning his 125th cap, which he will hope to beat in the upcoming Nations League matches.
