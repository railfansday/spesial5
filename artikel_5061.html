Happy Wednesday! Bitcoin is trading around $62,400, with a K33 analyst saying the cryptocurrency is showing signs of a local bottom, calling the pullback a "gift from the market."

In today's newsletter, Binance secured a $6.5 billion freezing order from an Abu Dhabi court, CryptoQuant said bitcoin has entered a bearish phase but is in a "value zone," the World Bank Group tapped Circle and Citi to test stablecoin-based fund disbursements, and more.

Binance secured a $6.5 billion worldwide freezing order from the Abu Dhabi Global Market Courts as it seeks to recover crypto stolen in its Oct. 29 hack.

CryptoQuant said bitcoin has returned to a bearish phase after falling below its 200-day and 365-day moving averages of $72,900 and $75,900, respectively.

The World Bank Group is working with Circle and Citi to explore using regulated U.S. dollar stablecoins to make fund disbursements faster and cheaper.

Onchain derivatives protocol Variational raised $110 million in a Series B round at a $1.5 billion valuation, led by Bain Capital Crypto, with participation from Dragonfly, HSG, Peak XV, and others.

Coinbase said its AI-powered scam defenses prevented $4.7 million from being sent to scammers who used an Elon Musk deepfake in a fake Tesla crypto giveaway livestream.
