Not everything that sounds plausible works: Testing ideas to win support for carbon pricing

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

What makes people support a carbon price? To find out, 108 researchers, led by a research team at the University of Innsbruck, Austria, tested 55 different ideas at the same time, from information campaigns to reflection tasks. On average, there was a small but statistically significant positive effect. Individually, the effect was detectable for only 11 of the 55 ideas. One of the strongest effects came from writing a letter to a fictional person of a future generation. The results were recently published in the journal Nature Human Behaviour.
