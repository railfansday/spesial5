Anthropic goes public after blockbuster IPO but fails to revive AI trade

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Anthropic has become the first pure-play AI developer to be publicly traded on the stock market, following a record-setting IPO. The stock opened 34% higher, briefly valuing the company at more than $2 trillion, before ending its first day of trading up 17%. However, Wall Street indexes closed sharply lower as investors took profits, reflecting underlying market jitters about the AI trade.
