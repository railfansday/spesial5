Blackpool sack their manager just seven hours after wishing him happy birthday: 'Have a great day, Gaffer!'

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Published: 15:24 EST, 19 November 2026 | Updated: 16:32 EST, 19 November 2026

Blackpool have sacked manager Ian Evatt just hours after wishing him a happy birthday.

Evatt had been in charge for a year and his final match was Saturday's 1-0 away defeat by fellow strugglers AFC Wimbledon, when his squad was decimated by illness. The result left the Seasiders immediately above the League One relegation zone and followed a run of one win and six defeats in ten league games.

Blackpool’s social media team had earlier wished Evatt a happy 45th birthday with the pay-off line: 'Have a great day, Gaffer!'

Three hours later, Evatt was relieved of his duties, and the club began the search for their ninth manager in eight years.

Evatt’s contract was set to run until June 2028. He joined last October following his exit from Bolton Wanderers in January 2025.

Blackpool later thanked the 45-year-old for his efforts during his time with the club.

Blackpool posted the birthday message just after 9am in the morning, with Evatt's sacking then confirmed at 4pm

Former Crystal Palace chairman Simon Jordan famously claimed to have sacked Trevor Francis on his birthday back in 2003.

Francis' dismissal has become an often told tale, with Jordan writing sixteen years after the event that he had even offered to send the former England star a birthday card.

Recent reports have also involved the suggestion that Francis had mumbled 'but its my birthday' back to Jordan when he was given his marching orders.

Whether these events played out as told are unclear. But Crystal Palace officially announced Francis' departure on April 18 in 2003, with his birthday falling a day later.

'I am totally committed to playing in the Premier League and I will ensure the new manager can achieve this aspiration,' Jordan wrote on Crystal Palace's website at the time. 'I would like to thank Trevor for all his hard work and would like to wish him the very best for the future.'

Jordan then turned on the players, adding: 'I'm not watching next year what I've had to watch this year. At times we've been a disgrace.

'I've asked the management staff to tell me who should go. They can see the players who are performing and those who are not and you will see in the summer, out they will go. The players will be told when it suits me. I care about them as much they care about me.'

Francis had reportedly told his players in a parting shot: 'Well, lads, I've been sacked – have a good summer holiday.'

Palace appointed Steve Kember as manager in the summer and despite Jordan's hopes of automatic promotion, rather than the play-offs, found themselves in a relegation battle in November.

Iain Dowie was appointed as manager the following month and guided Palace into the play-offs, with the Eagles triumphing in Cardiff to secure promotion to the top flight.

Steve Thompson will step in to take charge of the team against Wigan Athletic this weekend, his fourth spell as caretaker, alongside Stephen Crainey, Stephen Dobbie and Steve Banks.

During a hugely successful playing career at Bloomfield Road, Evatt made over 250 appearances for Blackpool. In his six years at the club, he played a vital role in their rise from League One to the Premier League, which included two successful play-off campaigns.

On arrival as a manager, he amassed 13 points from a possible 15 in December 2025 to lift them out of the relegation zone, although they lost ten and won two of their next 15 matches in all competitions.

They finished the campaign strongly, with six wins in seven games as part of a sequence of one defeat in 13 between mid-March and the end of August, only to struggle again this season.
