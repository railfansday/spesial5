Open the Pennsylvania 529 Investment Plan if you live in Pennsylvania, since your state tax deduction works with any 529 but only PA 529 savings don’t count against state financial aid. Elsewhere, compare it with cheaper plans Morningstar also rates Gold, and pick your own state’s plan if its tax break requires it.

The Pennsylvania 529 Investment Plan is Pennsylvania’s 529 plan open to savers in any state, and its 26 portfolios, including target-enrollment ones that shift toward bonds as college nears, are all managed by Vanguard. At an assumed 6% yearly return, saving $200 a month for 18 years in a target-enrollment portfolio costs about $1,000 in fees, against about $1,700 at the 0.30% that plans sold without an advisor averaged in 2025, Morningstar found.

Pennsylvanians should open it, since their deduction works with any 529 but only PA 529 savings stay out of state aid calculations. Savers elsewhere should start with their own state’s plan when its tax break requires it, and otherwise can pay about half as much in fees at Bright Start.
