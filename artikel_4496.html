Current classics: Lord mayor of London’s limo latest to go electric

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

If you wake up tomorrow as the esteemed Lord Mayor of London, you’ll want to make a properly dignified entrance. To make sure your arrival turns the right heads, Electrogenic has taken the Mayor’s 1973 Roll-Royce Phantom VI and turned it into a properly traditional standard-bearer with modern electric power.

The stately, stylish, and silent electric Rolls-Royce car made its public debut at last month’s Salon Privé weekend at Blenheim Palace, where it joined a growing fleet of classic Rollers that have received Elctrogenic’s electric conversion, including the stunning Mulliner-bodied 1929 Phantom II that we covered back in 2023.

For this project, Electrogenic carefully removed the internal combustion-based engine, transmission, and fuel system from the City of London Corporation’s official ’73 Phantom VI limo and replaced it with a 120 kW (~160 hp) electric motor putting out 310 Nm (~230 lb-ft) of torque. Sharp-eyed readers will note those numbers are significantly lower than what you’d expect from the 6.2L L410 V8 fitted with twin SU carburetors (as it was in the standard ’73 IV), but since those numbers were never officially published, we would say that the power available from the new electric drivetrain is similarly adequate for Mayoral use.

Power for the electric motor comes from a 62 kWh li-ion battery fitted under the hood bonnet, and offers more than enough range to get the Lord Mayor to their appointments in and around the city.

To ensure the heads of the heads of the city don’t bob in an undignified manner while on parade, Electrogenic added a “ceremonial” drive mode that trims power and torque and softens the throttle map, so the chauffeur can more easily hold the crawling pace demanded by civic processions and state occasions.

“We were honored to be asked to electrify this significant motor car, and to play a part in the City of London Corporation’s journey to net zero,” says Steve Drummond, chief executive of Electrogenic. “When it was launched in the late 1960s, the Rolls-Royce VI was the most refined and dignified limousine on the road. Now, thanks to electrification, its core character has been enhanced further: it is whisper quiet, effortless to drive with swift performance, and emits zero tailpipe emissions.”

Only 374 Phantom VIs were built between 1968 and 1990, with this 3 meter-long coach-built limousine having been commissioned during the mayoralty of Alderwoman Dame Susan Langley DBE, the 697th Lord Mayor of the City of London. The Phantom was converted as part of the Corporation’s broader effort to achieve a net zero carbon emissions goal from its own operations in 2027.
