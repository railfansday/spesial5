Where do the Texans rank statistically heading into Week 12 vs. Ravens?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Are people buying the Houston Texans in 2026?

After last week, it's hard to say that the team that lost after a dominant win is dead.

Following a 26-3 win over the Indianapolis Colts, the Texans seem back in the hunt for a wild-card bid. Yes, the Colts are punting on the season, but every victory counts toward the Texans' success on getting back in the race.

Texans Pro Bowl quarterback C.J. Stroud delivered on primetime while rocking the new rivalry uniforms as the Texans jumped out to a 14-3 lead over the Colts and never looked back in front of the hometown crowd. He threw for 194 yards and three touchdowns as the Texans played a balanced game on both sides, including putting up promising numbers in the rushing attack.

Stroud, who has now thrown for at least three touchdowns in four of his last five games, made sure to attack the outside with both of Indy's cornerbacks sidelined. In the first quarter, he found Nico Collins on a 42-yard touchdown to make it 7-3. He also found Kayshon Boutte for a 24-yard touchdown and rookie tight end Marlin Klein for a 13-yard strike.

With Week 12 here, let's look at how Houston stacks up among all 32 NFL teams stat-wise entering their matchup against the Baltimore Ravens.
