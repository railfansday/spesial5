Best Week 12 fantasy football waiver wire pickups at QB, RB, WR, TE

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Welcome to the Week 12 waiver wire. We don't have any "league winners" this week, but there are a few players with potential to have a strong end to the season. This is the time of the year we can see talented rookies get expanded roles on teams not headed to the playoffs. Rookies Fernando Mendoza and Ted Hurst popped in Week 11 and I'm more than happy to add them in case this is the start of something big.

Jacobs continues to heal from a fractured ring finger. The Packers face the Rams on Wednesday night, and Jacobs is unlikely to play, per Rob Demovsky. Kaleb Johnson should get the lead in rushing attempts, while Marshawn Lloyd works in passing downs. The Rams have Myles Garrett and Aaron Donald on their defensive line. I'm staying away.

Bowers missed last week with ankle and quad injuries, but head coach Klint Kubiak said he was optimistic that he could play Sunday night against the Browns, per Ryan McFadden. This could put a damper on WR Jack Bech, but there is still reason to add him this week.

Herbert suffered a concussion late in their loss to the Jets Sunday. He'll need to be cleared from concussion protocol to be able to play against the Patriots on Sunday. If he can't go, backup DJ Uiagalelei would likely face Tommy Devito in what should be a Week 18 matchup, but it ain't.

Tuten has been beat up of late, landing on the injury report over the last three weeks. He's been dealing with ankle and foot injuries and then limp off the field in Week 11 with five minutes remaining and never returned. Chris Rodriguez was the obvious beneficiary and would be a good add if Tuten were to miss any time.

Johnson hurt his ankle early in their Week 11 matchup with the Saints and didn't return. Starter D'Andre Swift lost yet another fumble as well. With a game on Thanksgiving against the Lions, we could see a little extra work for Kyle Monangai, who returned last week from injury.

Just how good Mendoza is for fantasy moving forward is truly unknowable, but with a three touchdown game against the Broncos in Denver, in his second start, I'd like to learn more. Mendoza's arm looked good and he showed good rushing ability in both his starts. He's worth taking a flier on if you are need.

Smith didn't need to a whole lot as completed 17-of-21 passes for 176 yards and three touchdowns in Sunday's 27-24 win over the Chargers. He had been. Next week he faces the Dolphins, who he put up 287 yards and three touchdowns against in Week 7.

The Ravens have started throwing the ball more with Brissett in the fold and Lamar Jackson on IR. In Brissett's two starts he's thrown the ball 49 and 37 times. Jackson attempted 20, 20, 31, and 25 passes in his four full starts this year. More passing is great for Zay Flowers, Mark Andrews and for Brissett and RB Justice Hill. This week against the Panthers, Brissett completed 24-of-37 passes for 276 yards, one touchdown and one interception, while running twice for seven yards. It wasn't a great fantasy day, but his usage in this game carried good upside.

Derrick Henry rushed 13 times for 94 yards and a touchdown, while catching four passes for 17 yards. He's still good, but the offense doesn't run through the rushing threat of Jackson and Henry like it has. Brissett is throwing more, while the Ravens defense continues to give up yardage. That should continue to put receiving back Hill in position to see more work. Against the Bills he rushed six times for 69 yards and a touchdown, while catching 4-of-5 targets for 27 yards. He also lost a fumble in the back of the end zone, which should have been his first touchdown of the day.

Bayshul Tuten left their game against the Giants with five minutes remaining and didn't return on Sunday. He came into this contest with ankle and foot injuries that have kept him on the injury report over the last three weeks. He likely needs some rest as the Jaguars make a push to go deep in the playoffs. I'd expect him to miss a game at some point moving forward. Against the Giants, Rodriguez was the clear backup and had better efficiency stats as a runner than Tuten.

Kyle McCord in't playing well, but he continues to target Douglas like a No. 1 wide receiver. This week against the Bills he targeted Douglas 10 times, but only completed two passes for 30 yards to the rookie. They are going to connect on some of those deep passes and as long as the usage continues, I'm still starting the rookie moving forward. Next week the Dolphins get the Jets, who Douglas caught two touchdowns against last time they met in Week 7.

Hurst led the Buccaneers in targets, receptions, yards and touchdowns on Sunday. He caught 5-of-7 targets for 56 yards and a pair of touchdowns against the Lions much improved secondary of late. The rookie has good upside and has flashed a few times this season. The Bucs really need someone to step up in the receiving game, and Hurst very well could be that guy to finish out the season.

Dike led the team in receptions and yards against the Cowboys, catching 6-of-7 targets for 69 yards. He was the primary slot receiver in this matchup, which was an upgrade to his usual usage. If he can keep that job, he should have a good PPR floor as the Texans continue to ramp up their pace and passing.

Jack Bech seems to have a fan in Fernando Mendoza, as he's targeted him on 25% of his passes through his first two games. This week against the Broncos, Bech caught 8-of-9 targets for 88 yards and a touchdown. Brock Bowers could be back next Sunday, but it's worth seeing if Mendoza continues looking his way when Bowers returns.

Aiyuk has been released by the 49ers and is free to sign with another team. The odds that everything comes together for him are low, but there is talent there and if he can land in a good spot, maybe he helps fantasy teams in the playoffs.

There aren't any great tight end pickups this week, but Freiermuth has a strong matchup in Week 12 at home against the Broncos. Denver has been weak against tight ends this season, giving up the third-most fantasy points to the position.

Freiermuth is very much a touchdown dependent tight end, but he does have two touchdown receptions in his last three games and five on the season. Over their last three games, the Broncos have allowed touchdown receptions to Noah Gray, Tommy Tremble, Darren Waller, and Ian Thomas.

Engram hasn't done much this season as a bit of an afterthought in the Broncos offense. But, we've seen signs over the last two games that he may have gained some usage. Week 11, following their bye, Engram caught 6-of-8 targets for 74 yards against the Raiders. These were season highs in targets, receptions, and yards. The game before their bye, Engram caught 4-of-5 targets for 33 yards. Did HC Sean Payton want to get Engram more involved following the bye? Maybe and if so, he could have some fantasy usefulness to end the season.
