ESPN analyst believes Bears DT Jeffery Simmons has good chance to win NFL award

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

ESPN analyst believes Bears DT Jeffery Simmons has good chance to win NFL award originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

A lot of teams made big moves before the 2026 NFL trade deadline this year, but no team made a more impactful addition than the Chicago Bears.

They swung a blockbuster trade with the Tennessee Titans, acquiring superstar defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons in exchange for multiple first-round picks. But so far, it's been worth it. Simmons has been awesome for the Bears.

Things are going so well that ESPN analyst Ben Solak believes Simmons has a real chance at winning the NFL's Defensive Player of the Year Award. While he has to stay hot through the end of the season, Solak sees a pathway for Simmons to win this award.

"More likely: He ends up with around 14 (sacks). The last defensive tackle to win the award -- Aaron Donald -- did so in 2020 with 13.5 sacks. If the Bears win the division and Simmons gets to that number, I think he'll do it," Solak writes.

Simmons is averaging 1.66 sacks per game with the Bears so far, and he's sure to continue to dominate, just maybe not at that high of a level. But if he did, he'd compete for the league lead in sacks.

Currently, Will Anderson Jr. of the Houston Texans leads the NFL with 11.5 sacks. Greg Rousseau of the Buffalo Bills is second with 10 sacks, while Boye Mafe and David Bailey have 9.5 sacks each.

Simmons, through 11 weeks, has nine sacks. He's the sack leader among defensive tackles, and since coming to the Bears, he's been a true game-changer for their defense.

A defensive tackle winning Defensive Player of the Year doesn't happen often, but Simmons seems to have a real chance this year.

Solak believes the Bears will need to win the NFC North and for Simmons to reach 14-plus sacks for a chance at winning this award.

Seeing the Texans and Anderson Jr. miss the playoffs, while Rousseau cools off a bit, could open the door for Simmons to win this NFL award.

Simmons and the Bears face the Detroit Lions on Thanksgiving, and with both teams 8-2, the winner will have a big advantage in the divisional race this season.

If Simmons can be a game-wrecker this week and the Bears win, he could start making a strong case as a top-tier Defensive Player of the Year candidate, if he's not already one of the top candidates for the award entering Week 12.
