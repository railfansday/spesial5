Confirmed Lineups: RB Leipzig vs. FC Augsburg

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

RB Leipzig will hope to extend their perfect home record in Bundesliga as they lock horns with out-of-form Augsburg on matchday 9. Martin Demichelis’ men are the only team yet to drop points at home this season.

Augsburg are winless since starting the season with two victories. Manchester City loanee Juma Bah is suspended, while fellow Augsburg center-backs Noahkai Banks and Calvin Brackelmann are out with injuries. Marc Guiu and Assan Ouedraogo remain on the sidelines for the hosts, while Antonio Nusa finally gets a breather as he starts on the bench. Willi Orban will make his 400th competitive appearance for the hosts.

Here are the two lineups for the game at RedBull Arena.

Leipzig: Vandevoordt; Baku, Orban, Lukeba, Raum ©; El Aynaoui; Banzuzi, Nkunku; Gruda, Gomis, Rômulo

Augsburg: Dahmen; Gouweleeuw ©, Keitel, Matsima; Fellhauer, Massengo, Rieder, Behrens; Kade, Ibrahimović; Ribeiro
