49ers, Brock Purdy en fuego in Mexico City, torch Vikings' NFL-best defense in rout

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The San Francisco 49ers earned a resounding win over the Minnesota Vikings on Sunday night in Mexico City, thrashing their NFC counterpart 35-10 at Estadio Banorte.

Four years after the Niners trained for a week in Colorado Springs, Colo., ahead of a Week 11 matchup in Mexico City against the Arizona Cardinals (an eventual 38-10 win), they trained in Colorado Springs for a Week 11 matchup in Mexico City against the Vikings. The results of the 49ers' two games were strikingly similar. But the 2026 edition felt a lot more consequential.

This game was billed as a potential fight for the conference's No. 1 seed. Minnesota (7-3) entered with a chance to catch San Francisco (9-1) at the top of the playoff bracket. It entered with a chance to make a statement - that an elite defense is more valuable than an elite offense. That Brian Flores still has the secret formula on 49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan, who was previously winless against Minnesota's defensive playcaller when the two matched up.

Instead, quarterback Brock Purdy - with his embarrassment of riches on offense, headlined by the return of wide receiver Mike Evans - shredded the Vikings for three-and-a-half quarters.

Purdy, who exited midway through the fourth with the 49ers up 25 points, completed 21 of 26 passes (80.8% completion rate) for 255 yards and three touchdowns. Evans, in his first action since Week 6 in his return from Achilles tendinitis, hauled in six catches for 109 yards and a touchdown. Tight end George Kittle added two first-half touchdowns, finishing with three receptions and 34 yards.

Meanwhile, the 49ers collected 28 first-half points, more than Minnesota had allowed in a full game this season, and largely coasted after safety Ji'Ayir Brown picked off Vikings quarterback Kirk Cousins and returned it for a pick six in the final two minutes of the first half. Cornerback Deommodore Lenoir added a forced fumble on Vikings receiver Justin Jefferson for the 49ers' second takeaway of the night.

A closer look at the box score tells less of a one-sided story. First downs: Minnesota 19, San Francisco 20. Net yards: Minnesota 320, San Francisco 344. Third-down conversions: Minnesota 7-13, San Francisco 4-8.

But the final score - and the eye test - indicated a blowout of epic proportions, as the 49ers continue to roll through their schedule and look the part of a true Super Bowl contender.
