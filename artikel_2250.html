Middlesbrough team news and predicted XI to face Watford: David Strelec latest amid Will Lankshear concerns

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Middlesbrough remain top of the Championship table despite suffering a disappointing 2-0 defeat away at Wrexham on Wednesday night. Kim Hellberg’s side missed the opportunity to extend their advantage at the summit but remain firmly in the promotion picture.

Boro are currently one point above Wolves in second place and two clear of Birmingham City, Stoke City and Swansea City. They were without key striker Will Lankshear in North Wales due to injury and struggled to find the firepower needed to get a result at the Racecourse Ground.

Watford, meanwhile, have endured a difficult start to the Championship season under head coach Alessio Dionisi. The Hornets sit 20th in the table after 14 fixtures and are just four points clear of the relegation zone.

However, they secured a much-needed victory last time out, beating Lincoln City 1-0 at Vicarage Road. Summer signing Stephy Mavididi scored the decisive goal to give Watford a welcome boost.

Hellberg will assess David Strelec’s fitness ahead of Middlesbrough’s trip to Watford on Saturday, but has hinted the striker may not be ready to star, as reported by Teesside Live.

Strelec made a positive impact from the bench during Wednesday’s defeat to Wrexham, offering better hold-up play during an 18-minute cameo. However, having only recently returned from injury, Hellberg believes starting him could carry too much risk.

The Boro boss said: “David has just come back and trained with us for only one week. We will see now, but, in general, I didn’t think he was fit enough, and there was too big of a risk to start him in a game now because of just two days in between.”

Lankshear remains hopeful of being available against Watford, although his absence was felt again on Wednesday as Kyle Joseph struggled to lead the line. Boro’s top-scorer also missed out on an England call-up due to his injury concerns.

Strelec’s campaign has already been disrupted by injuries, including a recent setback on international duty. Summer signing Rokas Pukstas and defender Alfie Jones remain out.

Vitek; Phillips, Malanda, Ayling; Brittain, Berhalter, Morris, Borges; Cozier-Duberry, McGree; Strelec

Two changes from the disappointing performance against Wrexham in the week. However, Lankshear once again misses out.

Strelec gets the nod in attack after his impressive cameo at the Racecourse Ground. That sees Joseph drop to the bench while Riley McGree returns to the side ahead of Tommy Conway.
