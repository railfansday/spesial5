Elon Musk Admits He Feels ‘Pretty Foolish’ Having Spent Nearly $500 Million on Midterm Election Races Won by Democrats, Claims He Is ‘Not MAGA’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Elon Musk spent big backing Republicans in the 2026 midterm elections — which resulted in a sweeping defeat of the GOP as Democrats took over the House and Senate.

Musk spent nearly $500 million during the midterms, including $200 million to try to defeat Senator-elect James Talarico in Texas in his race against Lone Star State attorney general Ken Paxton.

“I feel pretty foolish about having spent so much money and not seeing much for it,” Musk conceded, “or maybe it would have been much worse without that.” Musk appeared virtually Wednesday at the New York Times’ 2026 DealBook Summit in New York City, connecting via videoconference from China, where the tech mogul is part of President Donald Trump’s American delegation to the region. Earlier at the conference, Skydance CEO David Ellison said Musk — whose roles include founder, CEO and chief engineer of SpaceX and CEO of Tesla — has become an investor in Skydance, the company formed through the megamerger of Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery. “[T]o have him as a shareholder is an honor and a privilege,” Ellison said. (The subject of Skydance did not come up in Musk’s appearance.)

On the topic of the midterms, Musk said he believes that the newly elected Democratic governor of Texas, Gina Hinojosa, “is going to be quite moderate. I don’t think she’s sort of a far-left governor, and based on everything I’ve heard about her, I think things will be fine in Texas with [Hinojosa] as governor.”

Musk, who ranks as the world’s wealthiest individual, insisted that “I’m not MAGA” — even though he has worn “Make American Great Again” hats at multiple public events and served as head of DOGE, the Trump administration’s ad-hoc cost-cutting department, for several months in 2025 before he exited as a “special government employee” amid a dispute with Trump.

Said Musk, “I’m not sure what MAGA means” but “if that means sort of blind adherence to a set of policies by the Republican Party then I certainly don’t have that. I think there are positives and negatives in both parties… but politics is very tribal. A lot of people cannot see any good in the other party, and they cannot see any bad in their own party because they’re more or less invested in their party like it’s their hometown sports team, and it’s just their team, and they won’t accept that there’s anything that the other team can do that’s good or anything that their team can do that’s bad.”

Musk last month said SpaceX and Tesla are exploring a potential merger. “There’s more and more areas of interaction or overlap with SpaceX and Tesla that that didn’t exist before,” he said at the DealBook conference. “So if you’d asked me five years ago, do I ever think there’s any purpose in SpaceX and Tesla getting together? I would have said ‘no.’” He continued, “really, it’s just a question of do SpaceX and Tesla execute better together and create more value than if they’re apart… and that would be up to the shareholders to decide.”

Musk also spoke about Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s resignation last week, after the revelation that he had engaged in several affairs, including with an alleged Chinese spy and an Nvidia employee. Musk, who has suggested Huang be reinstated as Nvidia’s CEO, said Haung’s removal as chief executive of the AI company was like “kicking Michael Jordan off your team,” while also acknowledging that Huang “certainly made some mistakes.”

Musk, interviewed by the New York Times’ Andrew Ross Sorkin, discussed the controversy over Starlink’s recent restrictions on satellite internet access to the Ukrainian military amid the war being waged on the country by Russia. Musk said, “From the beginning, I’ve said that Starlink is a civilian system. It is not meant to to be used for offensive drone strikes that kill people…. If you want to do that you need to do Starshield, which is a separate system that is controlled by the U.S. government, but do not ask me as as a private individual to make kill decisions that sometimes result in the loss of innocent lives, not just soldiers.”

Musk appeared at the DealBook conference in 2023, where he accused advertisers who had pulled ads from X over his support of an antisemitic conspiracy theory of “blackmail” and told them — calling out Bob Iger, then CEO of Disney, by name — to “Go fuck yourself.”
