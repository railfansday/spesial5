Lance Broadway, Former MLB Pitcher and ‘Agents Of S.H.I.E.L.D.’ Actor, Dies at 43

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Lance Broadway, a former Major League Baseball pitcher-turned-actor who appeared in Marvel’s “Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.,” has died at 43.

Broadway was found dead on Nov. 17 in an apartment complex stairwell in Dallas, Texas, according to the Dallas County Medical Examiner. His cause of death is pending autopsy results.

Broadway was a pitcher who played for the Chicago White Sox and New York Mets. He made his feature film debut in 2013’s “Olympus Has Fallen” as Secret Service Agent O’Neill under President Benjamin Asher (Aaron Eckhart). His television career included appearances on “Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.,” “Lucifer,” “Saint George,” “Kevin From Work” and “Real Husbands of Hollywood.” His most recent credit was on two episodes of USA Network’s “Colony” in 2016-17.

Broadway was cast as Commander Linden in “Teleios,” which was later renamed “Beyond the Trek,” in 2014. The science fiction thriller was released as a TV movie in 2017. He was cast as Devon in the short film “Shattered Reflections” in 2015.

Born in Bryan, Texas, Broadway was an all-district baseball player in high school and went on to pitch for Dallas Baptist University. He transferred to Texas Christian University, where he was named conference pitcher of the year before being selected 15th overall by the Chicago White Sox in the 2005 Major League Baseball draft. He made his Major League debut in 2007 and was traded to the New York Mets in 2009. He signed a minor league deal with the Toronto Blue Jays that same year but never played for the team.
