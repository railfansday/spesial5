‘GTA 6’ Earns Rave Reviews and Lands 99 Metacritic Score, Tying for Highest-Rated Game of All Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The review embargo on “Grand Theft Auto VI” has finally lifted and the game, which on Tuesday was nominated for Game of the Year ahead of its launch, is considered Rockstar Games’ latest masterpiece.

“GTA 6” currently holds a score of 99 on Metacritic, which ties it with the original 1998 Nintendo 64 version of “The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time” for the highest-rated video game of all time on the platform. (The recently released remake of “Ocarina of Time” for Switch 2 has a score of 96 on Metacritic and is also a 2026 Game of the Year contender.) “Many superlatives will be heard in the discussion around ‘Grand Theft Auto VI‘ – biggest world, most activities, best graphics, deepest narrative – but its real triumph is the way all those features and others work in service of a cohesive, believable immersion in a criminal fantasy that is hard to grasp until you’ve played it for yourself,” Game Informer’s Matt Miller wrote in his review, which gave the game a perfect score of 10.

Thirteen years in the making, “GTA 6” is a massive open-world game that follows protagonists Jason Duval and Lucia Camino (voiced by Manni L. Perez) who find themselves stuck in the midst of a criminal conspiracy in the fictional state of Leonida after a score goes wrong.

“Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are an interesting pair of playable characters, and unlike any leads that have come before them in the series,” Luke Reilly wrote for IGN. “Their motivations and goals are very different from the main characters of previous ‘GTA’ games, and their priority is really about escape rather than revenge or empire building. They’re not angels, and it is invariably their recklessness as small-time crooks that puts them in the wrong place at the wrong time, but the overall sliminess of their far more powerful antagonists does make the couple easy to root for throughout their story – which unfolds like four or five seasons of a prestige TV show.”

“GTA 6” costs $80 for the standard edition and $100 for the ultimate version and reportedly offers approximately 80 hours of gameplay to complete its main storyline alone.

“I have quibbles about some clunky controls and some story pacing problems in the game’s later chapters, but like crappy cupholders in a million-dollar supercar, they don’t really get in the way of what’s here. Instead, they’re minor issues to point at as I hit the gas and go 200 MPH with the radio blasting,” Kotaku’s Zack Zwiezen wrote.

“GTA 6” officially launches Thursday in the U.S. but has begun rolling out worldwide as the clock strikes midnight on Nov. 19 in different territories, starting with New Zealand, which got access to the game Wednesday at 6 a.m. ET. While fans had been waiting to see if and when “GTA 6” reviews would drop before the game did (with several debating The Game Awards nominations being revealed before “GTA 6” was out), Rockstar Games chose to set the embargo in line with the game’s public release.
