Microsoft Announces Nine Games Coming To Xbox Game Pass (November 18 - December 1)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Although five are only new for Premium members

Microsoft has revealed the latest official roundup of Xbox Game Pass titles for November 2026 and the start of December 2026 today, with nine games joining various tiers of the service from November 18 - December 1.

The highlights here are the likes of Arkheron, The Outlast Trials and Hela: Of Mice & Magic, while it's important to note that five of the nine games do already exist on XGP Ultimate, but are now coming to XGP Premium.
