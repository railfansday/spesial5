Tinker Islands launches in Early Access on December 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Cozy multiplayer sandbox game Tinker Islands will launch in Early Access for PC via Steam on December 10, publisher Headup and developer Goon Squad announced. A demo is currently available.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:

Tinker Islands is a multiplayer sandbox set in unique ocean worlds filled with tropical islands for you and your friends to explore. Some islands are abundant in resources; others hide forgotten treasure, dangerous creatures, or places full of mystery. Whether you’re sailing solo or with friends, there’s always a new island on the horizon worth checking out.

Choose the perfect place to settle: From sandy beaches to deep jungles, or towering cliffs—build your own outpost, hideout, or cozy township. Progress by exploring the world, gathering materials, crafting new tools and equipment, and finding powerful blueprints.

The journey is its own reward: Set sail, follow your curiosity, and create your own stories along the way.
