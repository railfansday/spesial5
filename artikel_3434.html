Star Trek Ascendancy: The Final Frontier Officially Announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Despite Star Trek: The Original Series celebrating its 60th anniversary this year, being a Trekkie still pays off as the beloved sci-fi franchise continues to welcome new and exciting releases which honor both the original story and characters, whilst adding that all-important modern spin.

With upcoming rogue-lite survival games on the horizon, as well as an update on the next Star Trek movie, it is an exciting time for fans and this latest project is doing nothing to dampen the hype.

Created by GF9 over on Gamefound, Star Trek Ascendancy: The Final Frontier is "the ultimate collection of the most thematic 4x space game in the universe." Featuring brand-new and exclusive civilizations such as The Gorn, The Tholians and The Terran Empire, this TTRPG also introduces two new game modes such as Campaign and Non-Player Civilizations.

The Campaign Mode features unique scenarios and endless replayability, whilst Non-Player Civilizations will allow you to undertake the story solo or, if you are up for gathering some friends, the multiplayer options guarantee hours of fun.

Despite intially starting the crowdfunding with a $50,000 goal, Star Trek Ascendancy: The Final Frontier has achieved a whopping $2,281,058.81 thanks to over 6,000 backers, which just goes to show what Trekkies are capable of when they put their mind to it.

Star Trek is one of pop culture's biggest multimedia franchises, spanning multiple movies, TV shows, books, comics, video games, and various other media. The franchise was created by Gene Roddenberry and started with the 1960s TV series starring William Shatner and Leonard Nimoy. Over the decades, several equally popular series have come out since as Star Trek: The Next Generation and Star Trek: Discovery.
