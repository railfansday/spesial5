Amy Poehler’s New Peacock Series Brings Back One Very Specific ‘Parks and Rec’ Tradition

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Editor's Note: The following contains spoilers for Dig Season 1.

Amy Poehler has a new comedy series, and all is right in the world. Trading Indiana for Greece, Poehler stars in Dig as archaeologist Elise DeBellnick, who's spent much of her career as right-hand to Dr. Neville Wycombe (Hugh Laurie), a charming yet arrogant professor who keeps teasing retirement but never actually following through. When she uncovers an artifact at a previous dig site, she decides to finally take credit for herself and not report it to Wycombe. However, she finds out that comes with its own chaos.

A great sitcom is only as good as its ensemble, and the latest Mike Schur series doesn't disappoint. With Poehler as the lead, she surrounds herself with the likes of Antonia Thomas, Fina Strazza, and Geraldine Viswanathan; together, this quartet of women embarks on a journey that "digs" into the past and alters their future forever... with plenty of laughs along the way. Their unforgettable summer also includes Hugh Laurie, Kayvan Novak, and Jason Mantzoukas.

Ahead of Dig's release, Collider sat down with Antonia Thomas, Fina Strazza, and Geraldine Viswanathan to discuss the new Peacock series. The three actresses praise the leadership of Poehler as well as the brilliantly written scripts. Plus, they speculate what a second season could look like, from next season's location to a new character vibe.

COLLIDER: Congratulations on Dig. The three of you and Amy have such great onscreen chemistry. How did you bond together in order to translate that chemistry onscreen?

GERALDINE VISWANATHAN: Thank God it was just there.

VISWANATHAN: We just dove right in. My first week of shooting was all my scenes with Amy in the pit when we got stuck, and we had to get out. We just had the best time in the pit. [Laughs] We were just having so much fun, and I couldn't believe that I was wrestling around with Amy Poehler in a hole in the ground. [Laughs] It was definitely a pinch me moment. But it was just so easy. I think this script is so brilliantly written, and it's so sharp. Those characters and dynamics are so clear. That's Mike Schur's specialty, I think, and then with these brilliant actors and women, it all just fell into place.

What did it mean for each of you to have Amy as a leader, not just as a co-star but also as one of the co-creators?

THOMAS: We all grew up watching Parks and Recreation, and she is an absolute queen, and she is a complete inspiration. I listen to "Good Hang" as well. She's just such an incredibly kind, brilliant, wonderful person. First of all, having her as a creator, her mind is so brilliant; so you're just like "brilliant." But then actually getting to share a screen with her too, [it was] just sort of like a dream come true. It meant an incredible amount and feels like a pinch me moment, right? Just amazing.

Seeing you in the pit also was like, "Close enough, welcome back to 'Parks and Rec.'"

VISWANATHAN: Totally, they're obsessed with pits. What's up with that?

FINA STRAZZA: It's a safe place. It's the safety thing, probably.

STRAZZA: Just settled, nestled in the ground.

It'd be a different scenario if you were in The Pitt, in the hospital.

VISWANATHAN: Someone asked me last night, though, about what would be a good crossover episode with Dig, and I was like, "Oh, just thought of it, 'The Pitt' would be good."

THOMAS: I said that last night! I said The Pitt. That was my crossover!

VISWANATHAN: I'll see you in the hospital.

THOMAS: Less comedy in The Pitt, though. The Pitt is intense.

VISWANATHAN: Right. We're bringing archaeology energy to The Pitt.

At the end of the season, Clare and Dylan go off to India for their own kind of adventure. What do you imagine they get up to while they're there, and how long do you think that actually lasts?

VISWANATHAN: Hmm, Clare probably gets food poisoning immediately. [Laughs]

VISWANATHAN: Which is what happened when I went to India. [Laughs] But oh, we're having a great time.

THOMAS: I think we're having a great time. I think Dylan's leading Clare into all sorts of crazy adventures, and she's like… her Type A-ness is like "Oh my God!" but going with it and having a wild time.

VISWANATHAN: I think you're having your Eat, Pray, Love moment.

VISWANATHAN: I think she probably, by the end of it, she's like, unpreened, totally hippie. They did some retreats.

If we pick back up in Season 2 and Clare has a whole new vibe, we'll know.

VISWANATHAN: That's funny. That's a good idea.

Now, Fina, Patty is super obsessed with Greek mythology. How do you think she'd react to The Odyssey this summer and Percy Jackson right around the corner?

STRAZZA: I mean, I hope she's living in the same world as us because this would be her type of media. I think she'd be so thrilled. I wonder if she would even know what's happening, though. [Laughs] I don't think Patty is too caught up with [what's] coming out in the movies and the shows.

VISWANATHAN: She likes animations. She likes children's movies.

STRAZZA: Disney movies, Disney cartoons. If Christopher Nolan could re-release The Odyssey as a cartoon, she would be all over that.

Now Geraldine, if you could recruit any of your Thunderbolts co-stars to take place in a dig, who do you think would actually survive a whole summer at the site?

VISWANATHAN: Ooh. Hmm. I feel like Yelena's (Florence Pugh) so cool that I feel like she would rock out, I think. I'd love to see her in that pit just like being a badass.

Antonia, I love Tommy & Tuppence so much. Should we get a Season 2, how do you think you would see Tuppence explore her relationship with her sister?

THOMAS: Ooh, good question. Well, it's teased at the end that her sister sort of invites them into this bigger governmental spy thing. I think it would be an interesting push-and-pull with her sister being her boss and then also still being the annoying big sister. That's a very fun dynamic. It would be a very fun dynamic to see. I hadn't thought about that, though. Nice.

I love how this season totally wraps everything up, but should we get a Season 2, where are you pitching the next dig to take place? Are we heading to Scotland, or are we going somewhere else?

STRAZZA: Ooh. Wow. Some movement. [Laughs]

VISWANATHAN: Euro-summer? That's the dream.
