BAFTA Film Awards Losing Headline Sponsor EE After 30 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The BAFTA Awards is losing its long-standing headline sponsor.

Brit mobile and internet provider EE, which has sponsored the U.K.’s biggest film event for almost 30 years, is ending its partnership with the awards following the next ceremony in February 2027. The decision was driven by EE’s “business strategy,” according to the British Academy, which said it was first told about the move at the end of 2025. “2027 will mark the 30th year of the EE BAFTA Film Awards,” said a BAFTA spokesperson. “After a long-standing, successful and trusted partnership, EE advised us more than a year ago that it would not be renewing its support of the Film Awards beyond the end of the current contract which runs to the end of 2027, confirming that this decision is entirely driven by EE’s business strategy.”

The spokesperson added: “We are incredibly proud of what we have achieved together over three decades and are fully focused on delivering an exceptional EE BAFTA Film Awards in February, where we look forward to celebrating the very best in film with EE and audiences throughout the UK and world-wide.”

EE first became title sponsor of the BAFTA awards in 2013, taking over from its sister company Orange, which started the relationship in 1998. Alongside being the chief sponsor of the event, it adds its name to the EE Rising Star Award. P&O Cruises, meanwhile, is the headline sponsor of the BAFTA TV Awards.

BAFTA is already in discussions with potential sponsors to take over for the 2028 film awards ceremony.
