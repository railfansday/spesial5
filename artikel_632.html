Shannon Elizabeth to Star in and Produce Psychological Thriller ‘SweatBox’ (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Faith Knapp, Lynn Kim Do and Nicholas Alexander will co-star in the film written and directed by Tim Donner with production starting March 2027.

Shannon Elizabeth is ready to frighten you again.

Elizabeth (“American Pie,” “Love Actually”) is set to star in and produce “SweatBox,” a psychological thriller written and directed by Tim Donner.

The actress, animal rights activist and creator, who kicked off her film career with the slasher “Jack Frost” and also featured in “Scary Movie,” “Thirteen Ghosts” and “Cursed,” will lead a cast that includes Faith Knapp (“The Thundermans Return”), Lynn Kim Do (“FBI: Most Wanted”) and Nicholas Alexander (“Good Girls”). Following a Halloween party where three people end up murdered, five teens are taken into the titular “SweatBox,” the police department’s claustrophobic, high-pressure interrogation rooms, in order to find the killers, where dark truths emerge that force both the investigators and the suspects to confront the worst in themselves. They explore the weight of guilt and fear, and unravel ominous secrets that threaten their own sanity.

“I love psychological thrillers, so being able to produce ‘SweatBox’ and help shape it from beginning to end is really exciting. It lets me be creative in a whole new way. Tim Donner has written a story that keeps you guessing and makes you question everything,” said Elizabeth.

Production on “SweatBox” is set to begin in March 2027, and Elizabeth will produce through her company Ganesha Entertainment with Provocagent.

Elizabeth is represented by Reitman Management; Knapp by LA Talent; Do by CESD Talent Agency and Malissa Young Management; and Alexander by AKA Talent Agency and Strand Entertainment.
