Yes, you can go birdwatching in GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Ahead of Grand Theft Auto VI’s Nov. 19 release on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, fans are getting a better sense of what everyday life looks like in Leonida, Rockstar's fictional take on Florida. There are a variety of forecasts, including heavy rain in the Leonida Keys and high humidity in Gambit Bay. As for date night, Jason and Lucia can go out for drinks, hit the gym, or rob a liquor store together.

There's room for a little birdwatching in Leonida, too. Players can spot 62 bird species and record their sightings in a wildlife logbook. During The Guardian's hands-on preview, reporter Keza MacDonald spotted a gull perched on a post by the beach and asked what kind it was. Jason pulled out a pair of binoculars, and the gull went straight into the logbook.

Those whose interests overlap with the natural world and the classic chaos of GTA are excited. As one X user wrote, "Never played GTA but in this new version, they offer bird spotting which is very cool. Beauty & a kind of contemplation amidst chaos & violence. Yeah. I like it."

For users in Reddit's r/birding, the chance to fill that book is already prompting a few launch-week plan changes. "I think the crime sprees are going to have to wait," wrote one user. started the thread. Another plans to spend the first week finding cute birdwatching outfits for Lucia. "The story & heists & Jason can wait," they wrote.

Some of the birders had already spent hours with their binoculars out in another Rockstar game, Red Dead Redemption 2. "RDR2 flipped the birding switch on for me," wrote one user. "I have identified actual birds irl because I saw them in the game. No shooting though!" Another commenter pointed out a favorite detail from the game: The woodpeckers actually peck wood.

There was still room for a little crime in the thread. "Best crime I'll commit is illegally trespassing for a lifer," wrote one user. (A lifer is a bird a birder has never seen before.) Other players are already thinking beyond their first sighting. "I have found my people. Hopefully there's an achievement for finding them all."
