Long Island Man Convicted of Crimes Against Children, Including Sexual Exploitation and Receipt of Child Pornography

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Earlier today, a federal jury in Central Islip convicted Jacob Israel Walden on all eight counts of a superseding indictment charging him with sexual exploitation of children, receipt of child pornography, possession of child pornography, and access with intent to view child pornography. The verdict followed a three-day trial before United States District Judge Gary R. Brown.
