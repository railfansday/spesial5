The numbers behind Hermoso’s monster performance vs PSG

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Mario Hermoso was Roma’s man of the match at PSG.

The Spanish center-back never feared the team that has won the last two Champions Leagues and scored the goal that temporarily led 0-1.

The former Atlético Madrid player delivered a performance worthy of a total leader: high recoveries, intercepted balls, quality with the ball at his feet, and constant support for the attack, even finding himself consistently in the French team’s penalty area.

Hermoso, after the match, commented with a hint of bitterness on the 2-2 draw against PSG: “An important point. It’s a shame not to take home three points, but one is good. We want to fight for everything, we have the mentality to do so, and we also have the quality.”

Roma fans have fallen in love with Mario Hermoso. In a very tough match, the Spanish defender showcased his leadership skills with a near-perfect performance.

As reported by Corriere dello Sport, the 1995-born defender won 11 possessions, won 6 duels, and took 5 shots, 4 of which were from inside the opponent’s penalty area. A complete player.
