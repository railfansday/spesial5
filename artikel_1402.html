The press was not enthusiastic about the Blues, Belgium makes fun of Italy

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

“It’s not Byzantium”title The Team this morning, in reference to the Turkish city of Istanbul, formerly named like that. Understand here that it was not luxury, and that’s the least we can say after this 2-1 victory acquired in pain. A feeling shared by Le Figarowhich for its part headlines “slow, but qualified”. Without appearing brilliant, but rather messy, the Blues returned to success and above all finished first in the group, thanks to Italy’s misstep in Belgium. Now let’s talk about the captain of the Blues, Kylian Mbappé, who made his return and was aligned for the first time at the forefront of the attack during the Zidane era. “The legs and the head, but not the goals”here are the words of The Teamto qualify the performance of the number 10 tricolor. In the leg and despite an interesting activity, the Bondynois narrowly missed the finish, but still showed that there was a French team with and without him.

Remember yesterday, Gazetta dello Sportheadline: “make us fly” before a decisive match against Belgium, and it is clear that the Squadra Azzurra never took off, swept away 4-1 on Belgian soil. “All the way back”here is what the pink paper headlines this morning, after an insipid performance by Roberto Mancini’s men. First at kick-off, the Italians ultimately did not even see the quarter-finals of the Nations League and finished 3rd in the group in which the Blues appeared. “It was beautiful to believe it” exclaims from his side Tuttosport. Despite an interesting start to the match and a quick opening of the score, the Italian defense gave in too easily and three times in just 11 minutes. But for coach Roberto Mancini, there is no question of feeling sorry for himself, and we already prefer to look forward. On the Belgian side, on the other hand, we absolutely do not want to move on to something else. The time has come to savor and even gently humor Italy like the Flemish daily life Het Nieuwsbladwhich indicates “Ciao Italy”. And it must be said that on the Belgian side, we can afford to sleep. Thanks to this success, Marc Van Bommel’s men beat the Italian team to the post and qualify for the rest of the competition.

Jürgen Klopp is already playing very big against the Netherlands. And the German press is not mistaken in the image of Bildwho speaks of this meeting as already decisive for the German coach, who will officiate for only the 6th time on the bench of the Mannschaft. And if Klopp declared before the match that he was not thinking about a possible non-qualification, the situation is simple: only a victory against the Netherlands would allow the Germans, currently 3rd tied on points with Greece, but behind in the standings due to direct confrontations, to qualify without having to worry about Greece’s result against Serbia. You will have understood, this match is therefore of capital importance for Jürgen Klopp and his gang against Xavi’s Oranjes.
