Watford hold talks with 48-year-old boss who has been out of work since October 2025

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Watford are sat 20th in the Championship table with 15 points from as many games. The Hornets have struggled for any sort of momentum, putting pressure on manager Alessio Dionisi despite the club hierarchy’s reported desire to give the boss more time than some of his predecessors.

Now though, a report from Romanian journalist Emanuel Rose states Watford have held talks with Edward Iordanescu over the managerial position at Vicarage Road. Contact has been described as ‘positive’, but there have not been any concrete English reports that the Championship side are considering a managerial change.

Iordanescu is available having been out of the game since leaving Legia Warsaw in October 2025. He previously managed Romania’s national team, alongside prestigious clubs such as FCSB and CFR Cluj.

Iordanescu is a 48-year-old coach who started his career in the dugout in 2010 after retiring in 2004. He was only 32 when he was first named as interim manager of Steaua Bucharest, where he stayed as an assistant coach to Ilie Stan until 2012.

He spent short spells in charge of Fortuna Brazi and FCM Targu Mures in Romania before first earning acclaim at Pandurii Targu Jiu, where his work earned him the CSKA Sofia job in Bulgaria. That stint lasted only 12 games though, and Iordanescu would return to his native in spells at Astra Giurgiu, CFR Cluj (x2), Gaz Metan and FCSB.

The rumoured Watford target managed the Romania national side from January 2022 to July 2024, winning 10 of 28 games and leading them to the UEFA Euro 2024 round of 16 before leaving at the end of the tournament.

A return to club football followed at Legia Warsaw in Poland, but they struggled in European and domestic football with Iordanescu criticised for inconsistent team selection, fruitless tactical changes, poor physical output metrics and disenchantment within the squad.

Iordanescu won the Romanian Superliga once and their Super Cup twice, and the same trophy in Poland once. His most used formation is a 4-3-3, but has also deployed his sides in a 4-2-3-1 setup.

As touched on earlier, nothing has been concretely reported by English or local outlets that Watford are planning a change in the dugout. However, rumoured talks with Iordanescu could indicate that they are staying prepared if they do sack Dionisi at the very leasy.

If there is a time to change manager, it is now. The international break offers a chance to get a new boss in and settled before the return to league action.

Iordanescu could be brought in swiftly and given time on the training ground to work with his new squad before club football returns against Sheffield United on November 21st. However, it remains to be seen if anything comes of the links, with managerial speculation never far around the corner at Vicarage Road.
