Three goals conceded: Ex-Sheffield Wednesday man endures a tough night on UEFA Champions League debut

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Eyebrows will have been raised in both Sheffield and Rome when Devis Vasquez was picked to start for AS Roma in their UEFA Champions League clash against Manchester United on Tuesday night. The ex-Sheffield Wednesday loan man has served as backup ‘keeper in Mile Svilar’s absence, but came into the first XI at Old Trafford.

All was going well for former SWFC loan man Vasquez and Roma as they went 2-0 up within 17 minutes. However, the Colombian shot-stopper would concede three second-half goals as United fought back to win 3-2.

Vasquez made two saves but finished with an xG prevented of -0.50. having conceded three times from an xG of 2.50 (Fotmob). He was nearly a hero as he saved a penalty from Bruno Fernandes in the second half, but Roma’s Bryan Cristante was cruelly deemed to have encroached and the Manchester United man fired home the retake.

Vasquez was a 26-year-old goalkeeper on the books of AC Milan when he joined Sheffield Wednesday on loan in 2023. He came in and was the Owls’ starting no.1 under Xisco Munoz, but was dropped from the side after the Spaniard’s sacking and would never play for the club again.

The Long-serving Cameron Dawson stepped back into the starting role, before James Beadle joined in January. Vasquez was then recalled by Milan and promptly loaned out to Ascoli. Almost his entire time with I Rossoneri was spent out on temporary deals, and when he was released in 2025, Roma snapped him up.

Vasquez has served as cover while with Rome and did so in a half-season loan at Besiktas in 2025/26 too. He played once for the Turkish giants, keeping a clean sheet in a 0-0 draw with Fatih Karamguruk back in April.

Barring his loans to SWFC and second-tier Italian sides Ascoli and Empoli, Vasquez has mainly served as a cover option for all of his clubs. The now 28-year-old has the pride of making his UEFA Champions League debut though, despite the result.

Vasquez is looked back on as a fairly minor part of the Sheffield Wednesday side that endured dismal fortunes under Xisco Munoz. He didn’t give a great account of himself with a save percentage of only 59% while at Hillsborough (Fotmob), but the Owls had far bigger problems than their loan goalkeeper at the time as they went winless in their first 10 Championship games that season.

After the Colombian, the Owls had far better fortunes in the goalkeeping department. He was succeeded by loan hit Beadle in January, and the Brighton & Hove Albion prospect would return for the 2024/25 campaign.

The emergence of Pierce Charles would gather pace too, as he cemented himself as one of the UK’s brightest goalkeeping prospects despite shoulder injuries.

Now, as Vasquez earns opportunities with Roma, SWFC have Joe Lumley and Ashley Maynard-Brewer battling for the starting berth. The new ‘keepers will hope to play key roles under new boss Dave Challinor as the club rebuild after administration and relegation.
