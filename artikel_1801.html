JJ Gabriel announces Manchester United exit as Real Madrid move nears

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

JJ Gabriel has announced his departure from Manchester United, with Real Madrid working to complete a deal for the 16-year-old forward.

Sky Sports reports that Madrid reached an agreement with United earlier this month. One source told the broadcaster that the remaining details could be settled within two or three days, although the transfer has yet to be finalized.

Madrid intends to bring Gabriel to Spain in January. He remains registered with United’s academy until the end of the season, having asked to leave in September and stopped training with the club.

In his Instagram farewell on Tuesday, Gabriel thanked the coaches, staff and teammates who had supported him since he joined United in 2020. He singled out scoring at Old Trafford and receiving the Jimmy Murphy Award among the memories he would take with him.

Gabriel leaves without having played a competitive match for United’s senior team. According to Sky, the youngster and his representatives believe Madrid can offer a route into its first team, a central consideration as he prepares for the next stage of his career.
