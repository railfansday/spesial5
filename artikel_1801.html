Xabi Alonso issues ‘harsh’ VAR verdict as Chelsea fall foul of penalty controversy in Leeds loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Xabi Alonso believes that Leeds would not have been awarded a penalty in their 4-1 thrashing of Chelsea had referee Michael Oliver not given the decision on the field.

Leeds came from behind to secure their first win at Stamford Bridge since 1999, heaping pressure on Alonso as Chelsea lost for the third time in four Premier League games.

The hosts took the lead in the 25th minute through Emmanuel Emegha but were quickly pegged back when Noah Okafor squeezed his shot into the far corner three minutes later.

Leeds took the lead early in the second-half after they were awarded a contentious penalty when Morgan Rogers was adjudged to have handled Jayden Bogle’s attempt at goal.

Dominic Calvert-Lewin scored the resulting spot-kick, which was awarded after a VAR review, and from there Leeds ran away with the game scoring through Okafor and Brenden Aaronson.

Calvert-Lewin’s penalty was a turning point in the game despite Leeds’ dominance, and asked about the decision Alonso said he did not believe the referee and the VAR made the right decision. “No, I don’t agree, I don’t agree,” Alonso said in his post-match press conference.

“I think that it was harsh from Michael Oliver to give (the decision). Probably the VAR would not have acted in that way. For me it was not a penalty.”

Despite Alonso’s defiance over the penalty, the Spaniard admitted Chelsea lost control of the game long before Calvert-Lewin’s goal and said his team are in a “fragile moment” after their latest defeat.

“(It) went wrong after the equaliser, after the change, we lost completely the momentum,” Alonso continued.

“Until then we were doing okay, I think that we were creating, playing a lot on the pitch, we were coming out for the pressure, but from that moment they started believing and we started not believing and after that we never got the good feeling mentally in any moment and we were not able to come from the pressure and we didn’t get close to the goal so it completely changed it.

“Today I felt that probably we are in a fragile moment mentally, confidence when we receive a punch that we didn’t recover that strong, that tough enough and today that was my feeling.”
