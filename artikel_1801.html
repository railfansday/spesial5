Arsenal vs Hull LIVE: Premier League result, latest updates and reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Arsenal eked out Hull City 1-0 in a tense affair at the Emirates Stadium. Martin Odegaard notched the elusive winner on 72 minutes in a game that the Gunners dominated, but they were toothless in attack.

Chance after chance went awry for Mikel Arteta’s hosts, and they were denied a penalty by VAR, with Kai Havertz having his shirt tugged by Nobel Mendy. The referee explained to the bemused home faithful that the German had been offside, and as such, a free kick was given to the visitors rather than a spot kick in the hosts’ favour.

It seemed as though the resolute Tigers would hold out for an unlikely point in their determined effort to frustrate the champions, but Odegaard popped up 18 minutes from time to knock home a rebound from Noni Madueke’s initial strike to secure all three points. The result leaves the north Londoners six points clear of Manchester City at the summit, with Enzo Maresca’s challengers to face Nottingham Forest later on Saturday.

Arsenal huffed and puffed and eventually the Hull house came down.

A rare outing in the Saturday 3pm slot offered an opportunity. Win and the Gunners would, for a couple of hours at least, go six points clear at the top of the Premier League. Eventually, it was a chance they took full advantage of.

For much of the afternoon, that did not necessarily feel inevitable. For more than 70 minutes, Hull held firm and the champions were not exactly creating an abundance of chances.
