Who Michael Carrick is working with in training as Manchester United book new sack decision meeting

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Michael Carrick has taken charge of Manchester United first-team training, with the final international break of 2026 officially underway.

Carrick’s presence at Carrington on Tuesday morning came as somewhat of a surprise given widespread reports overnight that he is set to depart as manager.

Instead, an announcement regarding the future of Carrick has been delayed, with BBC Sport reporting that a meeting has been scheduled for Wednesday in Monaco has been scheduled for owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe and executives Omar Berrada and Jason Wilcox.

“No, no. Just carry on as absolute normal,” Carrick told reporters of his plans for the international break before the 4-4 draw against Aston Villa.

“Maybe three or four senior players will be in and around [the training ground], but we’ll certainly train next week and maybe have the weekend off.

“We’ll see how that develops, who’s left behind, who’s with us and what they need. But there’ll certainly be training next week.”

To Carrick’s point, he will have been working with a limited number of senior players during training on Tuesday as several members of the Man United first-team squad have been selected for international duty.

Karl Darlow, Dermot Mee, Senne Lammens, Youri Tielemans, Diogo Dalot, Bruno Fernandes, Leny Yoro, Andrey Santos, Lisandro Martinez, Amad, Noussair Mazraoui and Bryan Mbeumo will be in action over the next fortnight, with some important Nations League matches, Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers and friendlies taking place across the globe.

Youngsters Harry Amass, Ayden Heaven and Shea Lacey have also been named in the England U20s squad for matches against the Czech Republic and Portugal. Tyler Fletcher has earned a Scotland U21 call-up.

Meanwhile, the injured group of Manuel Ugarte, Marcus Rashford, Benjamin Sesko, Luke Shaw, Harry Maguire and Carlos Baleba have all stayed behind for treatment.

This leaves Tom Heaton, Matthijs de Ligt, Patrick Dorgu, Mason Mount, Kobbie Mainoo, Matheus Cunha and Joshua Zirkzee as the seven senior players available to work with Carrick in training as the world awaits an official announcement regarding the club’s managerial position.
