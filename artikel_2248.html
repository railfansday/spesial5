Nicky Hayen eyed for potential Belgian job after Burnley sacking and Aberdeen links

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Burnley parted ways with Nicky Hayen in October after a dismal start to the 2025/26 campaign. The Belgian boss oversaw just one win in 12 Championship games, and a 4-1 loss to Lincoln City proved to be the final nail in the coffin.

Since his departure from Turf Moor, Hayen has been linked with the Aberdeen job. They ended up appointing Mario Frick, but now, Het Nieuwsblad reports that Hayen is under consideration for the vacant head coach role at Jupiler Pro League side OH Leuven.

Leuven are eager to appoint a Belgian boss and are keen to avoid paying compensation for an in-work man. Hayen fits the bill, but it will come down to whether a deal is financially feasible after Carl Hoefkens and Karel Geraerts were ruled out of the running.

Hayen had a decent track record in Belgium before his move to Burnley. He won the Belgian Pro League, Cup and Super Cup with Club Brugge and had a solid rate of 1.68 points per game in his time in charge of Genk as well.

The Aberdeen job may have gone elsewhere, but new interest from OH Leuven shows his Turf Moor stint hasn’t done too much to damage his stock. Many are aware that the Clarets’ problems are largely down to off-pitch problems, with Alan Pace under fire and senior players struggling to step up.

Leuven could be a good job for Hayen to make his return. Not only would he be managing in a familiar league, but he spent three years there as a player and won the Belgian second tier during that time.

A quick return to the dugout will help Hayen leave his Burnley nightmare in the past. At 46, he’s still got plenty of years in management ahead of him.

Burnley’s disjointed managerial search ended in a risky move for Hayen, and it didn’t work out.

Appointing Fabio Celestini as his replacement raised eyebrows, and it is another gamble. However, his experience in turning around struggling clubs and building strong defensive foundations makes for good reading.

The new Clarets boss is yet to oversee his first win with two draws and a defeat in his first three games. Up next for Celestini and Burnley is a huge East Lancashire derby against Blackburn Rovers on Sunday.
