Zola assesses Italy after Belgium defeat: ‘Two or three players make the difference’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Gianfranco Zola says that the difference between Italy and Belgium comes down to the quality of ‘two or three’ individual players, but that this difference is what separates the top sides in the world from the rest: ‘The difference in quality hurt us a lot’.

Zola, who is currently serving as vice-President of the Lega Pro, gave his assessment of the Italy national team squad while speaking to reporters at the Serie C Next Stop event on Thursday.

There have been encouraging signs during Roberto Mancini’s first six matches back in charge of the Italy national team, including the performances of the likes of Pio Esposito and Michael Kayode, as well as picking up four points from two matches against the third-ranked nation in the world, France, over the last few weeks.

Italy’s Nations League group stage campaign ultimately ended in disappointment, though, with a 4-1 defeat away against Belgium which saw the Azzurri drop from top spot to third place in their group on the final day of fixtures.

Though Italy picked up 10 points from their five most recent matches, they were beaten home and away against Belgium in the first and last of their Nations League group stage games.

Zola feels that the difference between Italy and Belgium comes down to the individual quality of ‘two or three players’ and that it will take time and patience for Italy to produce and bring through talents of this level.

“Aside from the last match, which was painful, you can see something is starting to come through: Good performances, interesting young players who can grow in this set-up, but there is also an awareness that there are other teams that are ahead of us in terms of quality,” said Zola (via TMW).

“We’re competitive, yes, but there’s still a lot of work to be done to produce players who really stand out. The difference against Belgium, after all, came down to two or three players, but the difference in quality hurt us a lot.”

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
