Clint Dempsey Reveals Conversation With Thierry Henry About USMNT Job

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The USMNT is on the hunt for a new manager after Mauricio Pochettino left to join Manchester United, and Clint Dempsey thinks he knows just the man.

Pochettino had been at the helm since 2024 and signed a new contract following an impressive 2026 World Cup campaign. Therefore, it might take some time for the USA to get their ducks in a row for a potential replacement.

Pep Guardiola has emerged as the dream candidate after leaving Manchester City in the summer, while Nashville SC boss BJ Callaghan and USMNT Under-23s coach Steve Cherundolo are the current favourites.

But Dempsey, who scored 57 goals in 147, has thrown another name into the hat. The former Fulham and Seattle Sounders forward revealed a conversation he's had with 'one of the best players to have played the game'.

Speaking on the Men in Blazers YouTube channel, Dempsey revealed that Thierry Henry had expressed an interest in becoming the USMNT manager.

When assessing Henry's credentials, he said: "Yeah, I’d buy him too, just because of being around him, the way he sees the game, the confidence that he would give those players.

"I think when he’s telling them stuff, they’re going to listen.

“There’s a reason why there’s a statue outside of the Arsenal stadium, the Emirates, of him, because of what he was able to do. He’s one of the best players to have played the game. So if he can figure out a way to give some of that confidence and that knowledge of the game to some of these players, I think it could make a big difference."

Dempsey has worked with the Arsenal and France icon on CBS Sports' coverage of the Champions League. He claimed Henry had previously told him that it's a job he would like.

He continued: "In the Olympics, he was able to get France to a second-place finish versus Spain. He’s someone who loves the game. He loves the US. He’s passionate about it. He loves New York, the time that he spent there. So, you know, I say, why not?

"Because someone of his pedigree, and being around the game, and me watching the way that he watches the game and sees football, I think there’s a lot of positives there. I know that it’s a job that he would want to do because we’ve had conversations about it. And I think he’s someone who could make a difference."

Henry spent four years of his playing career in the US, representing the New York Red Bulls in a significant coup for the MLS. After ending his on-pitch career in the Big Apple, he has regularly been seen on UK and US television as a popular pundit.

However, he has also dipped his toes into coaching and management. Henry was appointed by AS Monaco in 2018, but lasted just 20 matches, in which he won five.

He then headed to Canada to take charge of Montreal Impact. Unfortunately, he didn't fare much better there. Henry oversaw nine wins and 16 losses in his 29-game spell.

As Dempsey mentioned, he then embarked on a stint with the French youth team, even leading the side that represented his country at the 2024 Olympics in Paris. Henry's side earned a silver medal after losing in the final against Spain. That was his last coaching job.
