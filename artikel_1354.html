Why Juventus have not called Conte as Carnevali’s main candidate named

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Tuttosport reports that Juventus continue to have confidence in Luciano Spalletti and are not considering replacing their coach mid-season, but if they were to make a change in 2027-28, Giovanni Carnevali would prefer Simone Inzaghi over Antonio Conte.

Former Juventus midfielder and coach Conte has strongly confirmed his desire to return to the Allianz Stadium one day, predicting that “sooner or later the circle will close.”

Conte already coached Juventus from 2011 to 2014, winning the first three Serie A titles in the post-Calciopoli era. This is not the first time the Italian tactician has openly admitted his desire to return to the club one day, but that doesn’t mean it will happen.

Tuttosport insists Juventus are not considering replacing their coach, Spalletti, this season. First, the Serie A giants still trust the Tuscan tactician; second, they are reluctant to make another managerial change that would bring in their fourth coach in four years.

Club owners and directors are convinced that even appointing Conte midseason would not address the team’s lack of personality or the players’ lack of responsibility, especially with frequent changes on the bench in the coming years.

Spalletti remains under contract until 2028, and the club will assess his results at the end of the season.

If, however, the Bianconeri change coach in 2027-28, Conte might not be their top candidate, Tuttosport claims.

Carnevali, in fact, seems to favour a different option, such as ex-Inter coach Simone Inzaghi. The Piacenza-born tactician might not be every Juventus fan’s cup of tea because of his past with the Nerazzurri, but even Conte’s potential appointment isn’t widely approved, given how he left the club one day into the 2014-15 pre-season and his move to Inter a few years later.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
