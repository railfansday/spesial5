Europa League: Milan vs. Ferencvaros – probable line-ups, where to watch on TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Milan coach Ruben Amorim will rotate his squad for a Europa League game against Ferencváros, but Gonçalo Ramos and Adrien Rabiot are still expected to start.

It kicks off at 18:45 CET [17:45 GMT] at the Stadio Meazza in San Siro, Milan.

The Rossoneri have grabbed only three points in the first three Europa League matches this season, with one win and two defeats, so this is a must-win game for them, as Amorim said in his pre-match press conference on Wednesday.

Milan are still dealing with a selection crisis as Fikayo Tomori, Ruben Loftus-Cheek, Alexis Saelemaekers, Matteo Gabbia, Pietro Terracciano, Samuel Chukwueze and Strahinja Pavlović have been ruled out with injuries, while Mike Maignan is suspended.

There will be rotations in today’s game, but some regular starters will still get the nod, according to Sky and Gazzetta.

Among them are Koni De Winter at centre-back, Yunus Musah in central midfield, Adrien Rabiot as an attacking midfielder, and Gonçalo Ramos up front.

Mario Gila, Luka Modric, Christian Pulisic and Diego Moreira are available on the bench.

TNT Sports 4 and HBO Max will broadcast the match in the UK and Ireland, and Paramount+ in the USA. Football Italia will provide live updates via a live blog.

Milan (4-4-1-1): Torriani; Terracciano, De Winter, Diawara, Bartesaghi; Hutchinson, Jashari, Musah, Cisse; Rabiot; Gonçalo Ramos.

Ferencváros (4-2-3-1): Dibusz; O. Nagy, Gomez, Zohoré, O’Dowda; Madarasz, Á. Nagy; Arzani, Zachariassen, Birmancevic; Yusuf.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
