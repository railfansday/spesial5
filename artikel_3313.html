Scammers Tried to Trick Noah Kahan Fans With a Fake Show Outside an L.L. Bean Store

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Two New England icons will definitely not be teaming up this summer, with the clothing company L.L. Bean recently warning fans that a purported Noah Kahan concert outside its flagship store is a scam.

Last week, word started spreading online that Kahan was set to play a special pop-up gig outside the L.L. Bean flagship in Freeport, Maine on June 12, 2027. Tickets were even being sold on an Eventbrite page, which (per screenshots) billed the show as “Summer Camp Featuring Noah Kahan.”

A description read. “Summer Camp is here. Explore the pop-up, and when the sun goes down, the lawn opens for an evening concert with Noah Kahan.”

On Saturday, L.L Bean warned fans about the scam, writing on Facebook, “The listing has no affiliation with L.L. Bean and no such event is occurring at our Flagship campus. Once it was discovered we immediately notified Eventbrite and they have disabled the listing.”

It’s unclear how tickets were sold to the fake show, or how refunds would be handled. Eventbrite did not immediately return a request for comment. Kahan hasn’t spoken on the scam attempt either, and his reps did not immediately return a request for comment.

The concert industry has, unfortunately, long been rife with outright scams and sketchy practices. Typically problems arise around ticketing, especially on the secondary market, where phony tickets are often advertised on social media and sold through bogus websites. But fully making up a show appears to be a relatively new, if unfortunately inevitable, scammer escalation. That someone would try the same scam with Kahan, one of the biggest artists around, feels especially bold. That said, this particular scam seems to have been kinda well thought out. The L.L. Bean flagship in Freeport does host an outdoor concert series during the summer, and Kahan has collaborated with the brand in the past. And while the flagship, as many Kahan fans noted online, is not as big as the stadiums Kahan is used to playing these days, the singer-songwriter has been known to do an underplay on special occasions.

So while the scammer may have been shrewd in concocting this part of their plan, there was one particularly glaring flaw: Kahan already has a real show scheduled on the same day.
