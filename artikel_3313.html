Mariah Carey’s Big Christmas Hit Gets Released on Tiny Vinyl

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

Mariah Carey‘s “All I Want For Christmas Is You” has become synonymous with the holidays, and the singer is giving fans a whole new way to listen to the festive track this year.

Carey has just dropped a Tiny Vinyl edition of her Christmas song, which features “All I Want For Christmas Is You” on a playable, four-inch record.

Available now at Target, the mini LP comes in an exclusive pale pink colorway, with accompanying full-color album art. It features “All I Want For Christmas Is You” on one side and “Christmas (Baby Please Come Home),” Carey’s famous cover of the Darlene Love and Phil Spector classic, on the other.

This is an officially licensed release from Carey, Sony Music, and Tiny Vinyl, and the LP is compatible with most manual turntables (it may not work on automatic turntables or record players with tonearms that cannot reach the center).

Carey took to Instagram to share a photo of her Christmas Tiny Vinyl release alongside her new holiday Barbie doll, which was released Nov. 18. Both items make ideal Christmas gifts for music fans, while the mini LP is a great stocking stuffer, too.

Both songs on the Tiny Vinyl release come from Carey’s 1994 album Merry Christmas, which has been certified diamond, and is one of the bestselling holiday albums of all time.

Carey released a special deluxe edition of the album on vinyl in 2024 to celebrate the 30th anniversary of Merry Christmas.The two-disc set includes all 10 tracks from the original release plus remastered versions of the live performances from her holiday concert at St. John the Divine church in New York in December 2024. Amazon still has this limited-edition vinyl release available online.

Amazon is also where you can find a host of new holiday merch from Carey, including this retro-inspired sweater.

The singer’s official Amazon store also has Christmas-themed ornaments, clothing, home decor, accessories, and other gift ideas that you can shop here.

Carey is currently in the middle of her 2026 Christmastime Tour, which takes the Queen of Christmas through Brazil and the U.K. “All I Want For Christmas” held the record for the longest-running Billboard Number One song with 22 weeks at the top, until it was dethroned by Ella Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas” earlier this year.
