What Is the SuperHalfs Series? Inside the Popular Half Marathon Challenge

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

All the information you need, including race dates, registration and course details, and how to prepare for racing abroad.

According to the 2026 Running USA Global Runner Survey or more than 11,000 runners, the half marathon is the most popular race distance. Not only that, but almost half of those runners would be happy to travel more than 500 miles for a good half marathon (and marathon). This might be why the SuperHalfs, a European series of six races through Lisbon, Cardiff, Prague, Berlin, Valencia, and Copenhagen, have become so popular.

These races are not neighborhood events, but international spectacles, and they are difficult to get into. A few of the courses, including Copenhagen (2014), Cardiff (2016), and Valencia (2018), have even hosted the World Athletics Half Marathon Championships. Dubbed “a super series for the ordinary runner,” according to Mark Janik, CEO North America of Sports Tours International, the SuperHalfs are perfect for runners who want to combine racing with travel.

While the early 2027 races are sold out, you can start planning for the autumn 2027 and 2028 races. No rush, though, as you have a lifetime to complete all six races and, once you do, you’ll earn more bling—a seventh medal. If you’re ready to set some international race goals, we have the training plans you need to get to the start lines of these extraordinary competitions.

Date: March 7, 2027 (Sunday)Location: Almada, PortugalNumber of Runners: 35,000Time Limit: 3 hours

A favorite course of many elite runners, racers enjoy views along the River Tagus and journey over the 25 de Abril Bridge, once the longest suspension bridge in Europe and a twin of the Golden Gate Bridge in San Francisco. This race is the only time when pedestrians can cross, says Sadie Lowell, JetSetting_Runner, who has helped runners plan their international runcations.

Although Lisbon is a hilly city, the course is flat, and typically serves up nice weather, as Portugal’s capital city boasts about 300 days of sunshine most years. It is also famous for record-setting runs including in 20216 Jacob Kiplimo from Uganda broke the world record—again—during this race with a time of 57:20.

The lottery for this race typically opens in June. Follow the race on Instagram for updates!

Date: April 3, 2027 (Saturday)Location: Prague, Czech RepublicNumber of Runners: 17,000Time Limit: 3 hoursREGISTER

Organizers of the Prague Half Marathon, with a theme of “all runners are beautiful,” may have been inspired to create that phrase because the race course itself is considered so lovely. The sights include castles, rivers, bridges, and historic parts of Prague. For the 13.1-mile course, runners will complete a few loops and turnarounds, with the race starting at Bubenské Nábřeží, Holešovice and ending at Jan Palach Square in the Old Town of Prague.

The idea for the SuperHalfs was born in Prague by Czech creator, Carlo Capalbo, and the series is managed by RunCzech. It’s a truly international event: More than half of the runners who participated in 2025 were from other countries.

The lottery for this race typically opens in August. Follow the race on Instagram for updates!

Date: April 4, 2027 (Sunday)Location: Berlin, GermanyNumber of Runners: over 42,000Time Limit: 3:15 hours

This is the largest of the six races, and, throughout the half, you’ll run past many of Berlin’s most iconic landmarks starting with the Victory Column monument in the center of the Tiergarten district. If you’re lucky, you might also spot a glimpse of Checkpoint Charlie as you make your way through the course. The race ends with a celebratory run through the Brandenburg Gate before reaching the finish.

After the race, celebrate your success with delicious German pretzels—the salt is good for rehydration!

This lottery usually opens in April, with results announced in June. Follow the race on Instagram for updates!

Date: September 19, 2027 (Sunday)Location: Copenhagen, DenmarkNumber of Runners: over 45,000Time Limit: 3 hours

Located in the heart of Copenhagen, the course takes runners through the historic inner city as well as the city’s charming and colorful bridge districts. The race prides itself on being a party filled with music from DJs, bands, and choirs. It also features high-energy cheering sections called “Power Zones” designed to energize you through the miles. And if you were lucky enough to snag a bib for 2026, you’ll be in for a treat because Copenhagen is set to host the 2026 World Championships.

The 2027 lottery for this race will likely open in March. Follow the race on Instagram for updates!

Date: October 3, 2027 (Sunday)Location: Cardiff, WalesNumber of Runners: over 28,000Time Limit: 4 hours

One of the smallest cities in the series, Cardiff, the capital of Wales, is 150 miles west of London. On the half-marathon route, runners travel past Cardiff Castle, the Principality Stadium, Civic Centre, and Cardiff Bay—all favorite tourist spots.

The race is known for its cheering sections and you may hear: “Pob Lwc!” or Welsh for “Good Luck!” as you’re running through the course.

While smaller than the other cities that are part of the series, this half marathon began in 2003 with only 1,500 people and now is part of the world stage of running.

The lottery for this race is usually open from through October. Follow the race on Instagram for updates!

Date: October 24, 2027Location: Valencia, SpainNumber of Runners: 25,000Time Limit: 2:45 hours

While you’ll run through the center of this Spanish city, the roads are wide so you don’t have to worry about overcrowding or slowing down. On course, you’ll see the Torres de Serranos, one of the 12 gates that formed part of the ancient city wall built at the end of the 14th century, along with the city’s famous murals, created by local artists.

Valencia is on the east coast of the Iberian Peninsula on the Mediterranean Sea, so the views of the water as you run to the finish are spectacular—and so is the food you get to indulge in afterward. (This is where paella was invented after all!)

You can register for this race just a few weeks after it’s over, typically in early November. Follow the race on Instagram for updates!

Unlike driving to an American city for a half, if you sign up for a SuperHalf race, you do need to handle more details to make sure your travel and race go smoothly.

While all of the races are in major cities with international airports, you can also travel with a company, such as Sports Tours International. They create group schedules that include multi-day hotel stays, race briefings, escorted trips to expos, some walking tours, shakeout runs, and, most importantly, race registration (which can fill up quickly!).

Whether you travel independently or with a group, it’s smart to fly in at least 48 hours before your race, Lowell tells Runner’s World. “This way you will have a buffer to pickup your bib in case of flight delays or cancellations. Many races don’t allow pick up on the morning of the race,” she explains.

You’ll also want to account for jet lag. “If you typically struggle to adjust your sleep cycle, plan to run the race on the last weekend of your trip,” Lowell says. “If you are able to adjust quickly, you can plan for running the race on your first weekend and exploring the rest of the trip.”

Once you’ve arrived, take Lowell’s advice and keep track of your eating and drinking prerace. “It can be easy when you are in a new city to forget about all the carbs you need to consume and all the water you need to drink,” she adds. “I always recommend stopping at a local grocery store and picking up some food to eat the morning of your race.”

While it may not be a selling point, keep in mind that many of these city streets are old—Lisbon was established in 1200 BCE—and weren’t designed for runners. At times, large crowds may run on narrow roads. While the courses are flat and fast, and most of the running is on large boulevards, you may have to be cautious on cobblestone streets. Of course, this is a small price to pay for an athletic adventure in a picturesque location.
