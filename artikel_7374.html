TikTok UK publishes live shopping report

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

TikTok says that almost one-third of live viewers have eventually gone on to buy a displayed product in-store.

TikTok UK published its first Live Shopping Report last week, which provides insight into how TikTok is becoming a more prominent retail destination in the U.K., and how livestreaming is changing the game for sellers.

The 20-page report looks at top product categories for live shopping and includes a range of notes on the increasing value of live shopping,

TikTok said that live shopping in the U.K. is on the rise, and has shared a range of engagement stats to underline this:

The guide includes overviews of the most popular live shopping categories, such as vertical-specific trend data and case study notes.

The most popular product categories driving TikTok sales in the U.K. are beauty, fashion, fast-moving consumer goods, pre-loved products, electronics, home and living and books and collectibles.

The breakdowns in the guide provide trend notes on all of these sectors, and could help marketers map out a more effective approach in the app.

TikTok is still trying to make in-stream shopping a bigger element, following the success that it had with in-app shopping in the China-based version of the app, called Douyin.

And in the U.K. at least, TikTok live shopping does appear to be catching on, albeit at a slower pace than what TikTok’s management would like.

Last year, TikTok Shops in the U.K. reported 60% year-over-year growth for the beauty category, making the app the fourth-largest beauty retailer in the country.

TikTok still has a way to go to convince more users to merge their shopping and social media engagement activities into a single app, but the company is making steady progress at driving more in-stream browsing and buying.
