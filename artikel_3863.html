You'll be able to start looking for GTA 6 Transgressor locations after you complete the Thrillbillies mission in Chapter 2 and collect the Vapid Dominator from Wyman (if you have the Ultimate Edition, that is).

He'll text to ask for help finding old wrecked Transgressors he can use to restore the classic car, which you'll then get to drive. There are three of these junks located around Port Gellhorn which you'll have to scan with the WAiNK It app.

The first wrecked Transgressor you can find is in a trailer park north of Sarena's Bargain Store in the middle of Port Gellhorn (you might have to zoom in to see the store). Head north, cross the road, and jump over the wall to find it there.

The second wrecked Transgressor is further north in the garage of the house opposite the Kolt & Associate's Stash House robbery (there's a metal sunflower standing in the garden, which makes it easier to recognise.) It's also to the east of Brook's Boatworks and the west of Wasteland Offroad. Head around the side of the house via the driveway to find the car in an open garage at the back.

This last one is nice and easy, behind the Dirt Merchants mod shop in the south of Port Gellhorn. Simply head right via the track that leads behind the shop, following the fence on your right and then turn left to find the wreck at the back behind the kind of trailer park area.

Once you have all three cars scanned, Wyman will take a little time to assemble it, and then call you asking to Buck him some money. Open the app, select the payment requests option, and then pay him the $1,500 for the car. This will give you a quest marker to collect it from his scrapyard.
