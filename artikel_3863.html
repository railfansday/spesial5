Wuthering Waves version 3.8 update ‘Lilies in a Dying Dream’ launches November 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Kuro Games will release the version 3.8 update for free-to-play open-world action RPG Wuthering Waves, dubbed “Lilies in a Dying Dream,” on November 12, the developer announced.

Here is an overview of the update, via Kuro Games:

Wuthering Waves is available now for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, PC via client download, Steam, Epic Games Store, and Microsoft Store, iOS via App Store, and Android via Google Play.
