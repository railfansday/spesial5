Birmingham City’s Top 3 players of the 2026/27 season so far, including star Premier League loanee

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Birmingham City currently sit top of the Championship with the November international break upon us. After a tough start, BCFC have been on fire in recent weeks with their latest result being a vital 2-1 win over high-flying Swansea City.

Chris Davies has managed to silence a lot of the doubters as there were some fans who were pessimistic going into this season. So far, the Blues have had a way better start to the campaign than what was expected in the summer.

It has been a real team effort to rise to such a strong position, but the break offers a good chance to highlight some individual stars. In no particular order, we put forward Birmingham City’s top three players of the 2026/27 season so far…

Liam Millar has previously had strong seasons in the Championship with Preston North End and Hull City. Despite this, It is safe to say this season, the Canadian has taken a major step up since joining the Blues.

Playing on the left wing, Millar has been one of Birmingham City’s best attacking outlets this season. The 27-year-old already has seven goal contributions in 11 appearances in the Championship this term.

Birmingham City fans were originally very disappointed when they failed to re-sign Ibrahim Osman in the summer. However, it is clear that Davies highly rates Millar and the winger has been a threat in every game he has played this season.

Even in matches where Millar doesn’t grab a goal contribution, he is heavily involved in BCFC’s success. Birmingham City’s most recent match against Swansea City shows this, as Millar created one big chance, had three successful dribbles and two shots on target (FotMob).

German centre-back Phil Neumann has had a big turnaround in form this season. In his debut campaign, he struggled to consistently keep his spot in the Blues defence, but he is now irreplaceable in Davies’ lineup.

After the signing of Dael Fry, it looked like Neumann would have his place taken by the new arrival given his strong performances with Middlesbrough. However, the 29-year-old has been arguably the best centre-back in the league so far this season.

His speed and ability on the ball have been vital part of Birmingham City’s strong start to the season. His defensive contributions have been particularly impressive with 115 clearances, 25 tackles and 26 interceptions (FotMob).

There will be no surprise that the two centre-backs have featured in this top three. Klarer and Neumann have been arguably the main reason that Birmingham City have conceded the least amount of goals in the Championship this season, with 14.

The Austrian is the backbone of Davies’ side as he is the captain and has been absolutely colossal at the back. His ability to win duels and clear any danger is remarkable. According to FotMob, he is amongst the best centre-backs in the league when it comes to aerial duels won with 105 and recoveries with 60.

Klarer was already extremely highly rated after his performances in 2025/26, but he has been near faultless for BCFC this term.
