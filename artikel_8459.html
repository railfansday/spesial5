Personal Investments • Re: Should I switch to 529 and pause retirement savings?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Don't let the tax tail wag the dog.

First, you need to decide how you want to fund your child's college. If you were planning to pay for it anyway, then investing that money in a 529 lets you take advantage of tax free growth as well as get the tax credit. If you were relying on some combination of loans, scholarships, and financial aid (which, honestly, isn't completely out of the question even at your income level, depending on the school) and weren't planning on paying for it out of pocket, then obviously a 529 has less of a benefit.

Only after sorting that out should you think about whether and how much to contribute to a 529 account.

Personally, given a choice between finding out retirement and our child's college, I'd generally pick retirement. Some else used the analogy about how, when you are on a plane and they give you the safety briefing, they say that you should put on your own oxygen mask first before your child's. By securing yourself first, you are also benefiting your child so that you aren't a financial burden to them.

Some other considerations:
1. If you get a company match for your 401k contributions, definitely contribute enough to get the maximum match regardless of what you do.
2. The $1500 state tax credit would likely be offset by the loss of any tax deduction (presumably both federal and state) that you would have gotten if you had contributed to a Traditional 401k or IRA. Need to take that into consideration.
