Find the Cheapest Flight – Search Flexible Dates & Nearby Airports, Then Track the Price

The cheapest flight usually goes to the traveler who can shift dates or airports. Search a calendar of fares instead of fixed dates, include every airport near you, and set a price alert so you can book when the fare dips. Compare fares only after adding the bags and seat you’ll pay for.

Changing only your travel days can cut the fare. Google’s analysis of five years of its flight data found that leaving on a Monday and returning on a Tuesday or Wednesday costs about 14% less than flying on the weekend.

On Google Flights, open the date grid or price graph to see which travel dates around your trip have the lowest fares. Add every airport within an easy drive to the search, since a smaller airport nearby can be cheaper than your closest one. Also compare the round-trip fare with two one-way tickets on different airlines.

Say a family of four moves its departure from Saturday to Tuesday and flies from a smaller airport 40 minutes away, and each round-trip ticket costs $90 less. That’s $360 saved, minus gas for the longer drive.

Then turn on price tracking for the route, so Google emails you when the price changes significantly. Google’s analysis found domestic fares low from 22 to 57 days before departure and cheapest around 39 days out. International fares were low from 50 to 133 days out. For Thanksgiving or Christmas, book holiday flights in October instead of waiting for a sale.

Before you compare a basic economy or budget airline fare with a regular one, add the extras you’ll pay for. Basic economy, an airline’s most restricted ticket, usually limits bags and comes without a seat assignment. Google Flights’ bags filter adds checked or carry-on bag fees to the prices it shows. Add the cost of choosing a seat for each traveler who needs one.

Book the lowest total on the airline’s own site. The Transportation Department’s 24-hour rule covers tickets bought at least seven days before departure. Under it, the airline must either give a full refund on a ticket canceled within 24 hours or hold the fare for 24 hours without payment. Tickets bought through travel booking sites and agents aren’t covered.
