Rangers backed to sell 22-year-old to fund Badredine Bouanani deal - Keith Wyness

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Rangers could potentially sell striker Youssef Chermiti when he returns to fitness in order to keep hold of loanee Badredine Bouanani long-term.

That is according to former Aberdeen chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that there is still a market to cash in on the Portuguese.

Chermiti, 22, is currently expected to be out until the latter stages of the season with his ACL injury, which he sustained just days after Rangers rejected a £22million bid for him from Besiktas back in September.

Wyness believes that the Gers will need to make a sacrifice if they want to keep Bouanani beyond his initial loan spell, and Chermiti could be the perfect option.

Bouanani has impressed during his loan spell from Stuttgart so far, and Rangers have the option to buy him for £15m at the end of the campaign.

He has forced his way into Derek McInnes' starting XI of late, and has the most expected assists of any player in the squad in league action so far this term.

Aberdeen's former chief Wyness - who served as CEO at Pittodrie between 2000 and 2004 and now runs a consultancy business advising elite clubs - believes it is a genuine "possibility" that Rangers cash in on Chermiti next summer.

They will have had to live without him for several months by that stage, and Ryan Naderi is already beginning to fill his boots at the top of the pitch.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how Chermiti may hold the key to Rangers buying Bouanani.

"I think it's going to be dependent on other factors like, if you remember, we talked about when Chermiti got injured; the old name that crops up now, apparently, he's recovering well," he said.

"If they can find some sort of value for a player like him, then that goes towards funding this Bouanani situation.

"So I think the answer is that there's going to have to be a couple of sales in order for that to happen. But it's now looking like that may be a possibility.

"There is a market there for Chermiti. So let's just see with Bouanani, but I think it will be dependent on a couple of other exits happening first."

Wyness previously told Football Insider earlier this week that Rangers now have "two options" when it comes to the long-term future of midfielder Lyall Cameron.

The 24-year-old is impressing out on loan at Dundee United right now, but has had hugely limited football since moving to Rangers in the summer of 2025.

Wyness believes that Rangers must either sell Cameron when his loan spell finishes, or hand him more opportunities at Ibrox, rather than allowing him to stagnate.

Dundee United are already keen to try and sign him permanently at the end of his loan spell, which could make Rangers' decision a little easier.
