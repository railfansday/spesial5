Sky: From Galliani to Kaka – Cardinale treated Milan figures to dinner to explain project

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Gerry Cardinale hosted a dinner on the eve of the big derby clash between AC Milan and Inter, explaining his projects to Adriano Galliani, Ricky Kaka, Ezio Simonelli and many others.

Cardinale has been in Italy for the last week or so, attending the new stadium unveiling as well as the games against Udinese and Bologna. Tonight, he will be at San Siro for the derby and to mark the big occasion, he hosted a big dinner last night.

According to Peppe Di Stefano of Sky, as relayed by MilanVibes, Cardinale invited about 60 people to dinner. It wasn’t just an opportunity to catch up, but also for the American to explain what he has planned for Milan, seeking the support of some club legends.

As mentioned at the top, the likes of Galliani, Kaka and the Serie A president Simonelli attended the dinner. Other notable Milan legends present were Marcel Desailly, Serginho, Cristian Brocchi, Mathieu Flamini, Mauro Tassotti, Filippo Galli and Daniele Massaro.

It was definitely nice to see Galliani turn up at the dinner, especially since he bumped into Kaka outside and we thus got a chance to see their warm embrace. The two still have a great relationship after experiencing so much success together over the years.
