Arsenal eventually found the breakthrough required to narrowly beat Hull City 1-0 in North London on Saturday.

Martin Odegaard was on hand to turn in from close range after Noni Madueke's strike was parried by Konstantinos Tzolakis.

The visitors stayed in the game, however, and pushed for an equaliser in the closing stages.

But Arsenal held on, making it nine Premier League wins and seven clean sheets from 10 games to start the new season.

Hull remain 13th, having failed to win any of their previous nine games across all competitions despite a positive start for newly-promoted side.

Here, Football Insider has picked out five moments you may have missed from Saturday's hotly-contested clash.

All three points for @Arsenal, courtesy of Martin Odegaard 🤝 pic.twitter.com/WSQyuUDrNC

After making a strong start to the game, the away end showed their appreciation for their team's efforts.

According to BBC Sport reporter Alex Howell, the Hull fans sang: "You're only here for the Tigers".

Fans of the newly-promoted club have once again become linked with the song, which can be heard at every stadium they play.

It is no secret that Mikel Arteta is prone to testing the limits of what a manager can do on the touchline.

The Spaniard was at it again on Saturday, racing across his technical area to half-volley the ball back to Jurrien Timber after it had gone out.

One of Arsenal's brightest moments in the first half came when Christos Tzolis was found in the box by Noni Madueke's cross.

The winger met the ball with a sweet volley - a little too sweet. The ball sailed high over the bar and into the air.

According to BBC Sport, it landed deep into the Emirates Stadium's upper tier, some way from the goal.

A huge chance for Hull came just after half-time, when Gabriel's mistake left them with a three versus two opportunity.

Mohamed-Ali Cho attempted to go alone but met Ezri Konsa, who was superb in the tackle.

The England international got back to his feet and bellowed at his Arsenal teammates, who had been too slow to get back and support him.

As Arsenal's frustration grew in the second half, Hull looked to slow down their momentum.

Semi Ajayi went down and took his time removing the strapping around his legs, before taking off his shin pads.

Arteta was not happy with the time he took, standing on the touch line with his hands outstretched towards the fourth official.
