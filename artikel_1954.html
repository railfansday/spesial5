Aston Villa 'will not entertain' Boubacar Camara offers amid 'serious' injury worries - expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Aston Villa are not prepared to entertain any offers for Boubacar Kamara in the January transfer window or beyond.

The 26-year-old remains a crucial part of Unai Emery's plans, having started 14 games across all competitions this season, including all ten in the Premier League.

Meanwhile, there are concerns about a lack of strength in depth in central midfield areas, where Amadou Onana's long-term injury has caused further complications.

Former Aston Villa scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Emery has no plans to weaken his squad, and Onana's injury means no midfielders will be sold in January.

Kamara has attracted high-profile interest from a number of European clubs, most recently including Chelsea and Inter Milan, as well as some in the Saudi Pro League.

However, he is regarded as a key part of this Aston Villa squad, and the club will not entertain offers for the France international ahead of the January transfer window.

Former Aston Villa scout Bryan King told Football Insider on 12 November that Kamara is one of just three players in Emery's starting XI whose position is currently guaranteed.

The midfielder continues to play a crucial role in the midfield, as he has done as long as he has been fit since his arrival at the club in 2022.

As a result, there are not plans to let Kamara leave the club any time soon, and concerns about a lack of midfield depth and Onana's long-term injury will not support an exit.

"Kamara is still a very important player for Aston Villa, he's playing every week at the moment," Brown told Football Insider.

"Unai Emery likes him, he sees him as an important part of their plans, so I expect Villa will not be entertaining any offers for him, regardless of who might be interested.

"They're not exactly blessed with an abundance of midfield talent, it's an area where they will probably have to improve, so they're not going to be prepared to weaken there.

"Plus, you've got Onana there who has been ruled out for a long time with a serious injury, so that makes any decision to sell midfielders complicated.

"They're already lacking depth, and with Onana out, that's a big asset and a big influence who is missing, so they certainly won't want to lose Kamara as well."

Kamara is not the only midfielder whose future at Aston Villa has been up for discussion in recent weeks, with speculation starting to circle ahead of the January transfer window.

Ross Barkley has recently faced questions about his long-term future at Villa Park, with his increased role in the first-team squad meaning Villa could be forced into a decision.

Brown told Football Insider on 29 October that Aston Villa are planning to offer Barkley a contract extension to ensure he remains at the club beyond next summer.

Doubts had crept in surrounding the midfielder's future in the summer, when he was linked with a move away, and could grow again if he does not extend his deal.

However, after Emery branded Barkley "a very good player" and praised his experience and recent performances, work is now ongoing to ensure he remains part of his plans.
