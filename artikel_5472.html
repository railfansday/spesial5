Longstanding AIM Mail Centers Franchisee Renews Commitment to Serving Lake Wylie, South Carolina

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Annex Brands, Inc. Celebrates Franchise Renewal, Ensuring the Continuation of Essential Shipping and Office Services

SAN DIEGO, Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Annex Brands, Inc., a leading franchisor in the packing, shipping, and office services industries, is proud to announce the renewal of a franchise agreement for the AIM Mail Center location in Lake Wylie, South Carolina. This renewal reflects Richard Winter and Ashli Ornoski's ongoing dedication to providing essential shipping and office services to the local community.

Located at 168 Highway 274 in Lake Wylie, SC, this AIM Mail Center offers a comprehensive array of services, including shipping options with UPS, FedEx, and USPS, expert packing services, private mailbox rentals, notary public services, and office solutions such as printing and packaging supplies.

"Since acquiring our AIM Mail Centers location in 2010, we've been proud to serve the Lake Wylie community with dependable shipping, business, and mailbox solutions," said Ornoski. "Renewing our franchise agreement reflects our continued confidence in the brand and our commitment to providing the personalized service and convenience our customers rely on every day."

Annex Brands recognizes the importance of franchise renewals for strong, locally-owned businesses that support their communities.

"Richard and Ashli have consistently demonstrated a dedication to customer service and operational excellence throughout their years with AIM Mail Centers," said Patrick Edd, Chief Executive Officer and President of Annex Brands. "Their commitment to meeting the needs of local residents and businesses has helped make their location an important part of the Lake Wylie community, and we are pleased to celebrate this renewal and their continued success."

Franchise renewals reflect the strength of locally owned businesses and the important role they play in serving their communities. By renewing their agreement, Richard Winter and Ashli Ornoski can continue leveraging the resources, support, and brand recognition of AIM Mail Centers while providing the dependable shipping, mailing, and office services customers have come to trust.

For more information about AIM Mail Centers #147, located at 168 Highway 274 in Lake Wylie, SC, and the services offered at this location, please visit www.aimmailcenters.com/147.

Annex Brands, Inc. licenses and franchises over 850 locations in the United States, Canada, and Mexico under the brands PostalAnnex, Pak Mail, AIM Mail Centers, Parcel Plus, Sunshine Pack & Ship, Navis Pack & Ship and Handle With Care Packaging Store. Headquartered in San Diego, Calif., Annex Brands has been providing business ownership opportunities since 1985. Locations offer a range of shipping options from multiple carriers such as UPS, FedEx and USPS, as well as freight shipping for large, awkward, high-value, or fragile items. Additional offerings include packaging supplies and custom packing solutions for parcels and wooden crates, as well as mailbox services and other office services such as notary public services, copy and print services, and more. For more information, visit www.annexbrands.com.
