The Crypto Fear & Greed Index Is in "Extreme Fear" Territory. History Says This Happens Next.

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Periods of fearfulness like this one are rarely reversed quickly, but conditions still improve eventually.

The Crypto Fear & Greed Index, a high-level measure of market sentiment, is, as of Nov. 16, at an "extreme fear" reading of 13, marking a reversal from the widespread bullish mood this summer that saw the index at 80 on Aug. 25. Bitcoin (BTC +0.85%) traded at $78,565 on Aug. 25, versus $64,931 on Nov. 16.

Wild volatility and extremes of sentiment have been common for Bitcoin. Here's what history suggests will happen next, given the fearful market.

In an analysis in February 2026, Coin Bureau co-founder Nic Puckrin found that when crypto's Fear & Greed Index fell or lingered below 25, Bitcoin's average 90-day returns afterward were just 2.4%. For example, when the index was at 24 on Jan. 21 of this year, Bitcoin's price was $89,364, and it has fallen 26% since then.

Part of the reason this dynamic exists is that extreme market fear usually occurs during crypto bear markets or immediately after crashes or hacks that destroy investor confidence. But even if the 90-day returns aren't that great on average, waiting longer -- perhaps even a lot longer -- tends to give way to a recovery eventually.

In the coronavirus crash of March 2020, the index sank to 3. Despite plunging along with everything else during that period, Bitcoin is now up nearly 1,190% since then. Buying during that panic took confidence in the asset, but it paid off.

Nearly two-thirds of 2021's 100 largest altcoins — esentially all cryptos aside from Bitcoin — were down by more than 90% as of Oct. 21 of this year, and nearly all coins outside the top 100 effectively lost all their value. That means it is not wise to simply buy a basket of declining cryptocurrencies and hope that time will heal all wounds afterward. Focusing on assets that were growing strongly during the current slump is a better approach.

Neither of those coins needs its price to go up for its fundamental value to increase. And while Bitcoin hasn't done well, it's still getting scarcer over time, regardless of what its price is doing. Each of its halving events cuts by 50% the reward miners receive for each block added to the chains, occurring roughly every four years, and slowing the supply growth rate.

There's no rush to buy anything right now. Don't let the market's fearfulness become yours, and stay focused on the long term in the meantime.
