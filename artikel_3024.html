Allegro: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Factory cost cuts and pricing power are structurally protecting margins against raw material inflation.

On its second-quarter fiscal year 2027 earnings call, held on Oct. 29, 2026, Allegro (ALGM -4.98%) reported total sales of $277 million, representing a 29% increase year over year. Long-term investors should note that market share gains in artificial intelligence data centers and automotive safety systems are expanding chip content per unit and accelerating sales growth.

Management shares three key takeaways that explain where the business is heading.

High-density artificial intelligence workloads require high-voltage power supplies, forcing server builders to replace bulky legacy transformers with integrated circuit current sensors, which are microchips that measure electrical current.

Automakers upgrading steering and braking to advanced driver-assistance systems, which are automated vehicle safety features, are quadrupling chip content per vehicle as electromechanical braking replaces traditional hydraulic systems.

Management is converting internal package wire bonds from gold to copper and implementing targeted price increases to structurally lower manufacturing costs while offsetting raw material inflation.

Order bookings jumped 45% year over year as customer demand for specialized power and sensing chips expanded across data centers, automotive, and robotics. Propelled by structural advantages in high-voltage isolation and custom chip packaging, leadership is monitoring pricing discipline and manufacturing capacity rollouts closely.

Expanding dollar content per vehicle and server rack provides a multiyear tailwind that protects profitability against raw material inflation.
