Lionesses star Chloe Kelly cuts a glamorous figure in see-through dress at GQ awards night - hours after being left out of the England squad again

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Published: 14:13 EST, 18 November 2026 | Updated: 16:48 EST, 18 November 2026

Lionesses and Arsenal star Chloe Kelly stole the show at the GQ Men of the Year Awards on Tuesday night, going braless in a see-through dress just hours after missing out on Sarina Wiegman's latest England squad.

The two-time Euros and Champions League winner arrived on the red carpet in a long-flowing navy dress, showing off her toned body and glamorous long blonde hair. She was joined by former England stars Mary Earps and Alex Scott.

Kelly was once again left out of Wiegman's selection for the play-offs against Ukraine due to a hamstring injury, as the winger's battle to make the plane for next summer's World Cup continues.

Wiegman announced her squad on Tuesday morning, turning to England's younger generation for the double-header against Ukraine.

Izzy Goodwin is the latest emerging talent to receive a call-up to the Lionesses squad, after Lexi Potter, Erica Meg Parkinson and Freya Godfrey won their first caps in the previous round against Greece.

The 23-year-old London City Lionesses striker scored the equaliser against Kelly's Arsenal last weekend at the Emirates, with the home side dropping points yet again.

Chloe Kelly looked glamorous in a long-flowing navy dress at GQ Men of the Year 2026

The 27-year-old missed out on Wiegman's England squad for the second time in a row

Arsenal have had an underwhelming start to the season, with just three wins in 10 games, and they currently sit in sixth place in the WSL table.

Kelly was dropped for the first time by Wiegman for the previous qualifiers against Greece, alongside fellow forward Ella Toone.

'The main reason is that I think the levels weren't what I expect from them on the pitch,' Wiegman explained at the time.

Toone has returned to the latest squad, with Wiegman praising her reaction to being dropped last time out.

'She responded really well (to being dropped last camp),' Wiegman said: 'Working really hard, she came in and was ready straight away. Going back to club she's done well and that's what has been fed back to me, too.'

As for Kelly, however, it has since been confirmed that she has been struggling with a hamstring injury which has kept her from competitive first-team action since the end of September.

The winger has started just one game for Arsenal, a 0-0 draw with Crystal Palace, and has not registered any goals or assists. She has yet to complete 90 minutes in the WSL this season.

She was previously omitted from Wiegman's squad in 2025 for a lack of playing time as she fought with Mary Fowler for minutes - a disappointment that inspired her move to London from Manchester City.

Known for showing up in big moments, it was a gamble that paid off and Kelly scored the winning penalty against Spain to win Euros 2025, earning her the nickname Chloe 'clutch' Kelly.

Now, she faces fierce competition from new German signing Lisa Baum, with other talented wingers Caitlin Foord and Olivia Smith refusing to roll over.

Kelly will have to steal the spotlight yet again to get a glimpse of Wiegman's England side.
