Chelsea: Why Wesley Fofana avoided a red card against Nottingham Forest as Xabi Alonso forced into change

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Chelsea defender Wesley Fofana found himself at the centre of controversy during the Premier League clash against Nottingham Forest.

With the Blues trailing to an Igor Jesus goal, Fofana gave his team-mates an even bigger mountain to climb early in the second half at the City Ground on Friday night.

After misjudged the bounce of the ball, thus allowing ex-Chelsea striker Liam Delap to latch onto the long ball and advance towards goal, the Frenchman panicked in an attempt to recover and ended up making a rash challenge in the box and conceded a penalty in the process.

Morgan Gibbs-White made no mistake from the spot to double Forest's lead, though Emmanuel Emegha reduced the deficit three minutes later to give Xabi Alonso's side hope of avoiding a third straight defeat.

Fofana’s additional punishment for the tackle was a yellow card, though there has been some questions asked on social media as to why he was not sent-off.

Ultimately, the Premier League have provided an explanation as to why.

A statement reads: “The referee’s call of penalty to Nottingham Forest and yellow card for Fofana for the challenge on Delap was checked and confirmed by VAR.

“VAR: “It's clearly inside, the main contact is with the leg and upper body. So it’s inside, the penalty’s fine and the yellow card's fine”.”

Alonso reacted immediately to the threat of Fofana being sent-off for two bookable offences by substituting the central defender, replacing him with Levi Colwill who had been dropped in favour of Moises Caicedo starting in central defence.

That introduction did not have the impact the Chelsea boss would have hoped for as Gibbs-White ran off the back of Caicedo to restore Forest’s two-goal lead and leave the visitors staring another defeat in the face.
