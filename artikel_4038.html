Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United Will Launch Earlier Than Expected On Switch 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Square Enix popped up at the September Nintendo Direct to reveal Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United for Switch and Switch 2. It came with a 16th February 2027 release date, but the studio has changed its plans in the months since, and we're now getting our hands on it a few weeks earlier.

According to Square Enix, the HD remake will now launch on 4th February 2027, with pre-orders going live on the eShop next week on 19th November.

For those who missed the initial announcement, this remake gives the 1995 RPG a modern facelift, complete with brand-new visuals, fully voice-acted scenes, new arrangements of Kenji Ito's iconic score, and a few other tweaks previously deployed in Romancing SaGa 2: Revenge of the Seven.

Most importantly, this new version has made some changes to the original's Free-Form Scenario system with the goal of making the main story and its eight (yes, eight) protagonists easier to follow.

Alongside the standard edition, Square Enix is also releasing a Digital Deluxe Edition for the game, which bundles in bonus costumes and equipment, a battle music toggle, and additional items for the Business and War Mode minigames. You'll even get to start the game 72 hours earlier than everyone else. Nice.
