Everything going down at once wasn't evidence of a conspiracy – but the truth may be scarier

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Rogue AI or nation-state hackers would be preferable to the amount of technical debt the past week of wobbling services laid bare

Have you or someone you love been affected by a tech outage this week? Yeah, us too.

In a week of big stories the biggest one seems to be the fact that everyone from Microsoft to the NSA was dealing with outages, and here's what might be the worst part: It doesn't seem to be AI, nor hostile countries intent on destroying our infrastructure.

Unfortunately for all of us, what the past week of outages reveal is that – as we discussed on The Kettle less than a month ago – all of our essential tech infrastructure is basically held together with duct tape and twine.Join host Brandon Vigliarolo, cybersecurity editor Jessica Lyons, and open source reporter Liam Proven to discuss what comes next, and whether we can fix any of this without tearing all of it out and starting from scratch.
