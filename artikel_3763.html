Everything you need to know about managing your loadout in GTA 6, including how to store and recover your favorite guns in your car

Unlike previous Grand Theft Auto games, there are now restrictions on how you manage your loadout and how many weapons you can carry at any one time. That means you need to put a bit of effort into thinking about the sorts of weapons you want available to you, both on your person and ready in the trunk of your car.

Thankfully, it's pretty easy to understand once you wrap your head around the basics. So I'm going to walk you through everything you need to know about your GTA 6 loadout right now.

Regardless of whether you're on-foot or behind the wheel of a vehicle, you can hold L1/LB to open your loadout. This is split between Weapons and Items, and you can cycle between the two tabs with R1/RB.

On the Weapons tab of your loadout, you'll find slots for two sidearms, one for your hands, one for equipment, slots for two longarms, one for a melee weapon, and one for explosives. On the Items tab of your loadout there are single slots for: Masks, Headgear, Headwear, Outfits, Bags, Gear, Eyewear, and Smokes. You can hover over any slot on either tab with the right thumbstick and press X/A to equip.

You can manage your loadout in GTA 6 while on-foot in one of two ways:

Head over to the trunk of your Personal Vehicle in GTA 6 and press Triangle/Y to open it. From there, hold L1/LB when prompted to open up your loadout. Use the thumbstick to move to any of the slots, and then cycle through available weapons and items using left and right on the D-Pad. This will let you swap around any items that you have stored in your trunk, equip any owned sidearms and longarms that you have stored, and more. You can also stow your longarms in GTA 6 by pressing Square/X.

Open the GTA 6 Index and head to the weapons tab to locate your closest Ammu-Nation store. Every one of these locations has a weapons locker inside which you can use to manage your owned weapons and transfer them to your personal vehicle's trunk for easier access on the go.
