I Hosted My First Friendsgiving &amp; These No-Stress Recipes Made It A Breeze

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Warning: You might be asked to do it again next year.

I just hosted Friendsgiving in my tiny New York City apartment, and it was an absolute blast. I had a few of my best friends come over, and their favorite part of the night unfortunately wasn't hearing me yap, but rather all of the tasty meals we had, courtesy of Delish.

These eight recipes made hosting our first Friendsgiving in our small New York City apartment a breeze. I will be honest, we did have a little mishap. Let's just say, be careful around hot things right out of the oven. The biggest hit of the night was my cranberry brie bites. Every single one of my guests asked for seconds. We also went a little nontraditional (and made things easier) with handheld turkey legs that everyone could gnosh on. The crescent apple dumplings for dessert and the mulled wine were my personal favorites. I honestly couldn't get enough of them. And they were so easy, I've been eating the leftovers all week.

If you want the easiest Friendsgiving with the tastiest recipes, these are eight I would recommend. All of my friends loved them, and they made for a night of amazing memories. I hope you enjoy them just as much as we did!

We engineered this bite-sized app to have everything you could ever want in a holiday appetizer, all in one bite: a flaky pastry base made from crescent dough, sweet cranberry sauce, rich, cheesy brie, and crunchy pecans. Ready in a little over 30 minutes, these bites are the ideal last-minute appetizer, and are so good, they just might steal the show at your Thanksgiving dinner.

Slow-cooked turkey legs are this year’s ultimate Thanksgiving hack. No basting, no inevitable dry meat—no fuss at all. All you have to do is make an easy festive herb butter, wrap everything in foil, and let it roast unsupervised. This recipe allows for all of the Thanksgiving sides that really can’t fit in your oven alongside a 15-pound turkey.

Think glazed sweet potatoes always need brown sugar, orange juice, or pecans? Think again. Coated in a savory maple-miso sauce, these melting sweet potatoes are here to challenge everything you think a sweet potato can be—and win you over with their deliciousness.

Croissant stuffing brings the light, airy texture of French pastry straight to your Thanksgiving table. While most traditional stuffings rely on denser breads, I opted for croissants to create a lighter, fluffier bite, and the result is everything. Trust me, this is the stuffing that has everything—savory, sweet, a little crunch, a bit of freshness—all tossed together with dreamy croissants in your 13" x 9" pan.

The perfect mac & cheese will have you dreaming about its cheesy goodness for days after you eat it. Obviously, the cheese has to be top-notch, but the real secret to the best version is the perfect crunchy topping. My recipe delivers on all of that and more, so you won't even think about reaching for another mac & cheese recipe for the rest of your gatherings.

While I love a hot toddy or spiked apple cider, mulled wine is the warming, boozy beverage I love to cozy up to as soon as the weather starts to grow cold. My classic recipe uses red wine, traditional warming spices, and brandy to create the perfect holiday drink to serve a crowd—or just yourself, no judgment here!

This fall-flavored iced tea is made with brewed black tea, apple cider, and maple syrup for a cooling drink to welcome the transition between summer and fall. Serve it for game day to wash down seven-layer dip and pigs in a blanket, or sip it as an afternoon pick-me-up that’s just sweet enough and totally refreshing.

Are you tired of spending hours making apple pie but still obsessed with the flavors of this beloved fall dessert? I have the perfect solution—crescent apple dumplings. This innovative recipe combines refrigerated biscuit dough and lemon-lime soda to simplify apple pie without sacrificing its classic taste. Top it with ice cream, and you've got a picture-perfect fall dessert on your hands in under an hour!
