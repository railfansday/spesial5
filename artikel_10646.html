Dunkin’ reveals free donut giveaway & 15-foot-tall nutcracker statue

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Dunkin’ is celebrating the holiday season with the return of its free donut giveaway, a giant nutcracker statue, and a limited-edition merchandise collection.

The coffee chain introduced its Donutcracker character as part of its 2026 holiday packaging, featuring the festive design on cups, donut boxes, and Munchkins boxes.

Now, starting on Black Friday, November 27, Dunkin’ is bringing the character to life for the first time alongside a series of holiday promotions.

Dunkin’ is installing a 15-foot-tall Donutcracker statue at Historic Fort Steuben’s Christmas at the Fort celebration in Steubenville, Ohio.

The city is home to the world’s largest collection of life-size nutcrackers, with the Dunkin’ character set to join more than 200 others at the holiday attraction.

Visitors can also purchase exclusive Donutcracker merchandise at the Dunkin’ restaurant at 1987 Sunset Boulevard in Steubenville while supplies last.

Additional holiday collectibles, including tumblers, ornaments, and mugs, will arrive at participating restaurants nationwide in early December, with prices ranging from $12.99 to $34.99.

Alongside the Donutcracker’s debut, Dunkin’ is bringing back its Free Donut Fridays promotion for three consecutive weeks.

Dunkin’ Rewards members can claim a free Classic Donut with any qualifying beverage purchase on November 27, December 4, and December 11 at participating US locations.

Customers can redeem the offer by ordering through the Dunkin’ app, scanning their Rewards ID at checkout, or paying with an enrolled Dunkin’ card.

The promotion is limited to one free donut per member each Friday and excludes specialty donuts. Both the beverage and donut must be included in the same order, and the offer cannot be combined with other promotions or Meal Deals.
