Nicholas Hoult’s First TV Series Since ‘The Great’ Officially Reveals Full Cast

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Nicholas Hoult is headed back to Hulu. He's set to star with fellow Hulu alumnus Daisy Edgar-Jones in the new dramedy series Mosquito. Now, that series is buzzing to life; production has started, and a swarm of new cast members have been added to the call sheet.

Hoult previously starred in the Hulu historical drama series The Great as Peter III, the wife of Russian tsarina Catherine the Great; that series was created by Poor Things screenwriter Tony McNamara, who's reuniting with Hoult for Mosquito. They'll be joined by Edgar-Jones, who starred in the hit Hulu drama Normal People. In Mosquito, Hoult and Edgar-Jones will play Ed and Kate, a recently married couple whose lives begin to crumble as their dark secrets are revealed...all because of one annoying, blood-sucking insect.

Now, we know that they'll be joined by a bevy of recognizable faces. Kyle Soller (Andor) will play Hunter, Ed's half-brother and business partner; Phoebe Fox (Task) will play their cousin and co-worker Gail; and Francis Magee (Game of Thrones) will play Viktor, a long-time employee with a simmering grudge. Belinda Bromilow (The Roses) will play Kate's boss, Agnes, while John Macmillan (Surface) and Liv Hill (The Serpent Queen) will play her coworkers Brian and Georgie. Akshay Khanna (Murderbot) will star as Kate's university friend Will, with Phoebe Campbell (House of the Dragon) as his wife, Cressida. Bamshad Abedi Amin (Kill Jackie) will play Gail's PhD supervisor, while Den Rasaq (Criminal Record) will play Kate's work rival. The six-episode miniseries is now in production in the UK; no release date has yet been announced.

Hoult can currently be seen in the crime thriller How to Rob a Bank, which debuted in theaters this weekend. Next year, he'll lend his voice to CoCoMelon: The Movie and reprise his role as Superman's archfoe Lex Luthor in Man of Tomorrow. He's also slated to star alongside Aaron Taylor-Johnson in Cry to Heaven, an adaptation of the 1982 Anne Rice historical novel of the same name; it will be director Tom Ford's first film since 2016's Nocturnal Animals. Edgar-Jones currently stars in Georgia Oakley's new adaptation of Sense and Sensibility. Next year, she'll star in the legal thriller A Place in Hell and the heist movie The Take.
