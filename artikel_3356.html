Netflix’s 8-Part Psychological Thriller Officially Debuts With 498 Million Minutes Viewed

Netflix's The Body is off to a promising start on the streaming charts.

According to Nielsen's Top 10 Streaming Chart, the eight-part psychological thriller accumulated 498 million minutes viewed during the week of November 9–15, equivalent to 8.3 million hours. The series, which released all eight episodes on November 11, secured the No. 6 spot on Nielsen's Original Streaming Programs chart within its first five days of availability.

The Netflix series outperformed several other new streaming titles, including Hulu's The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County (461 million minutes) and Peacock's The Good Daughter (398 million minutes). Meanwhile, Prime Video's The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power topped the Originals chart with 866 million minutes following its season 3 premiere.

Created by Quinn Shephard, the writer-director behind the Emmy-nominated crime drama Under the Bridge, The Body follows a group of Catholic schoolgirls who begin experiencing prophetic visions after a dance-team initiation goes wrong, triggering mass hysteria throughout their town.

The main characters of The Body are played by Kristina Bogic, Sara Boustany, Geena Meszaros, Nnamdi Asomugha, Shirley Chen, and High School Musical: The Musical: The Series star Sofia Wylie. The ensemble cast also includes The Traitors winner Gabby Windey.

The Body was the third most-watched Netflix series on Nielsen's Originals chart, behind Black Doves and Love Is Blind, while outperforming established titles such as The Great British Baking Show.

Per Netflix, Shephard revealed that she drew inspiration from horror movies, coming-of-age stories, and psychological thrillers she loved growing up, including Carrie, Jennifer's Body, Heavenly Creatures, and Mean Girls. The series also marks a creative reunion between Shephard and Emmy nominee Riley Keough, who previously starred in and executive produced the true-crime drama Under the Bridge.

In addition to its streaming success, The Body has been well received by critics and audiences. According to Rotten Tomatoes, the series currently holds a Certified Fresh 84% critics' score from 32 reviews, alongside an even higher audience score of 95% from over 1,000 ratings.

In ScreenRant's review of The Body, Dani Kessel Odom praised the series' religious satire and campiness but criticized its inconsistent pacing and cliffhanger ending.

The Body isn't the only horror series finding success on Netflix. During the same measurement period, the acquired sci-fi horror series From topped Nielsen's overall streaming chart for the fourth consecutive week with approximately 1.55 billion minutes viewed. Since joining Netflix on October 19, the series has accumulated 6.92 billion viewing minutes.

Despite its strong reception from critics and audiences, Netflix has yet to officially renew The Body season 2, as of November 17. The thriller leaves several of its major storylines unresolved. However, showrunners Gina Gammell and Shephard already have ideas for a potential second season.

While Netflix has a history of canceling supernatural teen dramas after just one season, including shows such as First Kill and I Am Not Okay with This, The Body's impressive streaming debut could bode well for its future. With The Body season 1 leaving so many unanswered questions, a renewal would give the creative team an opportunity to further explore the show's central mystery.
