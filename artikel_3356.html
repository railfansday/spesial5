Marvel’s Spider-Man Officially Gets Stunning Makeover For New Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Hannah covers horror, sci-fi, and games at Collider, where she writes news and features, reviews new releases, and interviews the people who make them. She’s helping build out Collider’s gaming coverage, with Pokémon as one of her specialties. If she isn’t writing about a monster, she’s probably watching one on a tape from her VHS collection.

Peach Momoko is responsible for some of the most gorgeous and downright unsettling artwork Marvel Comics has published in the last five years. Her horror-inspired take on Ultimate X-Men feels right at home alongside Japanese horror manga, while her adorable illustrations of Jeff the Land Shark are practically begging to be framed. That range has made the two-time Eisner Award winner one of the most exciting artists to collect, especially for Marvel fans who have developed the habit of buying variant covers.

Some of Momoko’s most memorable covers feature the many faces of Marvel’s Spider-Verse. With a new collection celebrating her career at Marvel, Spider-Man fans will soon have the opportunity to appreciate those illustrations on a much larger scale than the standard comic book cover.

Collider can exclusively reveal Spider-Man portfolio artwork from Clover Press’ upcoming release, The Marvel Art of Peach Momoko. The eleventh installment in the publisher’s The Marvel Art Of… series is an oversized, 200-plus-page hardcover collecting artwork from Ultimate X-Men, Demon Days, Demon Wars, and Momoko’s extensive catalog of Marvel covers. The Kickstarter campaign launched November 19, with exclusive 11" x 17" print portfolios among its available rewards. Check out our exclusive artwork below!

Momoko’s collection joins previous installments dedicated to Marvel artists David Mack, Alex Maleev, Michael Allred, Russell Dauterman, Mike Del Mundo, and others. Alongside the 9" x 12" hardcover, Clover Press is offering Kickstarter-exclusive posters, stickers, puzzles, print portfolios, and signed editions. “Stunning watercolors and gorgeous compositions set Momoko’s work apart from anything else in comics,” said Clover Press publisher Hank Kanalz. “We’re excited to add her collection of work to our line of books and prints.”

It’s a well-earned celebration for an artist who only began working with Marvel in 2019. Momoko won her first Eisner Award for Best Cover Artist in 2021, followed by a second in 2024 for her work on Demon Wars: Scarlet Sin and various Marvel covers. That same year, she took over writing and illustrating Ultimate X-Men, reimagining the franchise through the experiences of teenage mutant Hisako Ichiki, better known to longtime X-Men readers as Armor.

The Marvel Art of Peach Momoko is currently available to back on Kickstarter. Stay tuned at Collider for more.
