Now Is The Perfect Time To Stream Netflix’s Internationally Acclaimed Thriller Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

It's no secret that Netflix has some of the best thriller TV shows available to stream right now. From its original programming to its backlog of series, Netflix has no shortage of projects that fill every niche of the genre. There are gory, terrifying murder mysteries, cozy detective stories, and puzzling narratives following the lives of brilliant, charismatic thieves. In fact, season 4 of Netflix's perfect thriller about a gentleman thief just dropped.

Lupin season 4 heralds the return of the beloved mystery-action TV show, loosely based on the characters and stories created by the French author Maurice Leblanc. The protagonist, Assane Diop, is a gripping character who turns the archetype of the gentleman thief on its head, making for an individual who feels vividly real but still has the gravitas to pull off a mind-bending heist.

New Netflix thrillers all raise the bar for the genre on the streaming service, making it easy to see why Lupin has received so much praise. Fortunately, it's not too late for viewers to get in on the action of Lupin. Hearing about the hype is one thing, but viewers should see for themselves why Lupin is making waves around the world.

Since 2021, Lupin has steadily been establishing itself as the best thriller series available to watch on Netflix. Omar Sy stars as Assane Diop, a young man who follows in the footsteps of the famed fictional French thief Arsène Lupin, in the wake of his father being framed for stealing a diamond necklace. Lupin season 4 recently dropped on Netflix on October 23, 2026, following a lengthy hiatus, as season 3 aired in October 2023.

However, based on Lupin season 4's Rotten Tomatoes score and the buzz surrounding the latest installment of the show, it's safe to say that the series came back for good reason. As one of the most popular non-English language shows on the streaming service, Lupin has demonstrated the story's international appeal. While the critics' score speaks to the quality of Lupin, there's a lot more to the series than its accolades. Viewers are drawn to Lupin because of the brilliantly plotted thefts that Assane has to pull off in every season.

Sherlock was great, but four seasons in, Lupin is Netflix's current most globally watched show for a reason, and it may be the better detective story.

However, it isn't just the action and criminal elements that draw viewers into the series. Lupin season 4 pushes the emotional stakes of the show further than ever, as the audience gets to see Assane develop beautifully over the course of the story. It's always fun to see Assane don a disguise and charm his way out of impossible situations, but it's even more rewarding to witness him struggle to find a balance between a normal life and a life of thrills and heists.

As of now, Lupin has not been renewed for season 5 by Netflix, but this doesn't mean we've seen the last of the gentleman thief on our screens. With such a strong critic score and a massive uptick in viewership, it would be shocking if Netflix didn't capitalize on this momentum. As Lupin season 4 continues to capture the attention of viewers across the world, the French-language series has to return for season 5, completing Assane's journey and returning audiences to the exciting world.

This time, Lupin shouldn't wait three years between seasons, as the hiatus between seasons 3 and 4 was nearly too long to wait for the expansion of Assane's story. Lupin seasons 1 and 2 were both released in 2021, and season 3 in 2023, so there's an existing precedent for Lupin to release an additional season sooner rather than later.

As Netflix looks to the future of its thriller TV show programming, Lupin and its global success can't be overlooked. For viewers seeking their next great pulse-pounding watch, it's not too late to join the legions of audiences who can't get enough of Lupin.

Guided and inspired by his love for Maurice Leblanc's classic Arsène Lupin stories, a charismatic gentleman thief sets out for revenge against the wealthy family responsible for his father's death.
