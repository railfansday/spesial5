Southwest Gives You 55 Days Of Companion Pass For One Holiday Roundtrip (Even With Points) — Book By Sunday

Southwest Airlines will give you a Companion Pass for a single holiday roundtrip, even if you book the cheapest Basic fare with points. From January 8 through March 3, 2027, you can bring someone on as many trips as you like for just the taxes and fees (from $5.60 each way). You can use points for your seat then, too.

Register for the Black Friday offer first, then book by 11:59 p.m. Central on Sunday, November 29. Put your Rapid Rewards number on the reservation when you book.

Already booked for Christmas? If the same award or paid ticket still costs the same, then register, cancel and rebook. And for a winter trip where you’ll use the pass, you can book your seat now and add your companion once the pass is active, as long as there’s still a seat for sale.

Southwest gets your Christmas booking this weekend and gives you the second seat after the holiday rush. If you earned the pass they offered in August, yours starts January 5 and also ends March 3. This offer adds zero days. You get one designated companion at a time across all your passes, with three changes for 2027 after you pick the first person.

I used to buy my ticket, bring my wife as my companion and redeem points for our daughter. With two kids, I’d register both parents. Each can earn a pass and designate a child. If one parent already earned August’s pass, only the other needs to qualify this time. Fly Austin to Orlando and back nonstop in February and the two kids’ tickets together would cost $22.40 in taxes and fees, even if both parents use points.

This year it takes 135,000 Companion Pass qualifying points or 100 qualifying one-way flights to earn the full pass. Under the Rapid Rewards changes for 2027, unlimited companion travel comes with Platinum status starting January 1. That takes the same 100 flights – or 140,000 tier qualifying points. At 75 flights or 95,000 tier qualifying points you get three companion trips, each of which can be a roundtrip. What made the old pass so easy to earn was that a credit card initial bonus could cover most of those 135,000 points. Next year initial bonuses, referral and anniversary points, extra points from card bonus categories, and points from hotel and rental car partners stop counting toward the pass. A Southwest credit card earns one tier qualifying point per dollar, and cardmembers get a 10,000 point boost when they reach 130,000. Earning the full pass on card spend alone takes $130,000 in purchases. Southwest has also extended the credit card application deadline from December 2 to December 14. Apply by then and get approved, and the 80,000 or 90,000 point initial bonus can count toward the old 135,000 point requirement for a companion pass earned in 2026 even if you finish the required spending in early 2027. Southwest Rapid Rewards® Plus Credit Card (See rates and fees) earn 80,000 bonus points after spending $1,000 on purchases in the first 3 months from account opening. Southwest Rapid Rewards® Priority Credit Card (See rates and fees) earn 90,000 bonus points after spending $3,000 on purchases in the first 3 months from account opening. Any ordinary purchase points you need to close the gap still have to post in 2026. The pass you earn this way runs through December 31, 2027.
