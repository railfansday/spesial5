Delta Air Lines Launches 15 New International Routes: See Full List Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

As the northern aviation winter season is here, airlines are increasingly finalizing their plans for next summer. Initial information for Q3 2027 (July-September) shows that Delta Air Lines is planning 258 daily international departures in the all-important period (double for total movements).

The SkyTeam member's offering is currently expected to be 6% higher than for Q3 2026, with 14 daily departures added. It will be the seventh-highest volume on record for that quarter. However, it is subject to change, as Delta firms up its plan. Still, 15 new and returning routes will help, some of which will be operating in the third quarter of 2027, having not done so in 2026.

For this article, Delta's international network in Q3 2026 was compared to what is planned for Q3 2027 using Cirium Diio data. 15 international routes were identified, eight of which are long-haul. As all markets have already been officially announced, this article simply summarizes what is happening and provides some additional context.

Only routes that have already been disclosed and are scheduled are included. As such, any upcoming new link from Seattle, expected to be revealed on December 2, is excluded. While flights to Singapore have been increasingly speculated, this is not entirely certain. Even so, it is unclear when, precisely, they'd begin.

Many of the entries are markets that Delta already serves. They are simply included because flights did not operate in Q3 2026 but are scheduled to do so in the same quarter next year. In many ways, this is a vote of confidence for a route. Obviously, the reverse (a shorter operating period with fewer flights and less capacity) may indicate problems. Just ask Southwest about one of its links to Alaska.

For obvious reasons, Delta's total flights on the aging, fuel-inefficient, maintenance-heavy, but clearly characterful Boeing 757 have fallen dramatically in recent years. Only 8,455 movements are scheduled on the 757-200 and 757-300 in Q3 2027, down by 60% compared to Q3 2026. Retirements have contributed to this reduction, while nine A321neos were delivered in September and October 2026 alone.

The decrease is due to the domestic market (-67% year-over-year). In contrast, 30% more international 757 flights are planned between next July and September, although this is still far below historic levels. This is not because of the relaunch of service from Boston to Iceland after a five-year absence, as that is just replacing the 757 link from Detroit to the nation. In contrast, Q3 flights from Los Angeles to Mexico City do contribute, although this is only one part of the picture.
