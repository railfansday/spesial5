Snapchat enables Lens creation via third-party AI tools

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

The new Easy Lens capability enables users to build Lenses using their chatbot of choice.

Snapchat is looking to help creators use their favorite AI chatbots and tools to create Lens effects for the app, with the launch of its new Easy Lens CLI, which provides a simplified connective process to link third-party AI tools into its Lens Studio system.

As shown in this image, Snapchat’s Easy Lens CLI provides a connector to help creators build Lenses via conversational prompts using the AI chatbot of their choice.

Creators can tap on the CLI option on the EasyLens website, which will provide them with instructions on how to guide their chosen AI chatbot towards Snap’s Lens creation parameters.

From there, creators can instruct the chatbot to make whatever Lens they like, and the system will convert those ideas into relevant instructions and code.

The system expands on Snapchat’s existing Easy Lens process, which makes it easier for anyone to create a Lens effect using AI prompts. This new expansion will essentially bring that same functionality to external AI chatbots, utilizing their processing capacity to build Lens features.

As explained by Snapchat: “Easy Lens already makes it simple to create a Lens from a single prompt on web and mobile. Easy Lens CLI brings that same creation flow into the tools developers use every day, so their AI assistant can take on the technical work while they focus on the creative vision.”

Snapchat said the process will enable creators to build everything “from Web Lens games with leaderboards to camera Lenses and beauty effects.”

“Their AI assistants can generate assets, build and test each Lens, fix errors along the way and send new versions to their phone in minutes,” Snapchat said. “That means a full game can go from an idea to a published Lens in a single day, without writing any code.”

The company added that all lenses created through this process will go through the same content checks as any other Snap Lens.

CLI-created Lenses will also be eligible for monetization for enrolled creators under Snapchat’s Lens+ payout scheme. Snapchat said it awarded $1.8 million in payouts to Lens+ community creators last month.

Easy Lens CLI will run on Mac, Windows and Linux, and is also available as a plugin for Claude and Codex.
