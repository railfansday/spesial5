Grocery giant Aldi recalls tortelloni over potentially ‘life-threatening’ health risks: Avoid these products

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Aldi has issued a recall due to the potential presence of Clostridium botulinum. 

Two tortellini products from the Illinois-based grocery store chain’s Priano brand might contain the life-threatening bacteria, according to a recall notice from Aldi that was reposted by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA).

Clostridium botulinum, or C. botulinum, can cause botulism, a very dangerous form of food poisoning.

According to the notice, the potential contamination resulted because the products may have been stored, distributed, or sold at improper temperatures.  

Here’s what you need to know about the Aldi recall. 

What products are affected?

The Aldi recall is for two types of its tortellini:

Priano Protein Carbonara Tortelloni, 9 oz, plastic unit 

  • Lot number: T9101926
  • Best-by date: December 18, 2026

Priano Protein Herb & Cheese Tortelloni, 9 oz, plastic unit

  • Lot number: T9101926
  • Best-by date: December 18, 2026

Where and when was the product sold?

The announcement doesn’t say exactly when the tortellini products were sold, but we do know where. Aldi sold the recalled products in-store and online from stores in the following states:

  • Alabama
  • Georgia
  • North Carolina 
  • South Carolina

What should I do if I have this product? 

You should either throw out the recalled tortellini or return it to Aldi for a full refund. “Consumers are warned not to prepare or consume the affected products from the impacted states, even if they do not look or smell spoiled,” the announcement states.

You can contact Aldi with any questions at (844) 484-0332 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET, Monday to Friday. 

What botulism signs should I look out for? 

Get medical attention immediately if you’ve eaten the recalled tortellini and experience any of the following symptoms of botulism: 

  • Abdominal distension
  • Constipation
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Dizziness
  • Double vision
  • General weakness
  • Muscle weakness
  • Trouble speaking or swallowing
