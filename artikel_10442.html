GTA 6 Contains A Very Hidden Secret That Teleports You Back In Time To Vice City On The PS2

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Easter EggGrand Theft Auto 6GTA 6GTA Vice City

Grand Theft Auto 6 is a big game filled with a lot of secrets, Easter eggs, mysteries, and more. And one of the most elaborate and well-hidden secrets in Rockstar’s new game has just been revealed by a YouTuber. It takes some time and involves going to 12 different spots across the map to hit buttons, but you’ll be rewarded with a trip back to the original Vice City and a classic shirt.

All credit for discovering and figuring out this wild secret must go to content creator Memeio, who found a tower in the southern part of the map that contained a button that, when pressed, played a small noise from GTA: Vice City. I also found this tower while reviewing the game, but didn’t think to go hunting down a bigger secret. Memeio did, and after a few days of searching the map for power stations, they were able to unlock the wildest GTA 6 secret I’ve seen so far.

I discovered this GTA 6 easter egg about 3 days ago, here it is in video form, I think I'm the first person to ever crack this code in the world so enjoy! #GTA6 pic.twitter.com/vTORjUlYNi

In that previously mentioned tower, Memeio discovered that same little sound secret, but also spotted a poster seemingly referencing a famous cheat code from the original Vice City on PC: “Life Is Passing Me By” which, in the original game, you could enter to speed up time. Memeio then explored the entire map and started to find various electrical substations that each seemed to be marked with letters from that cheat code. Eventually, they were able to figure out a series of switch and button puzzles spread across 12 spots in GTA 6. Memeio hit the buttons in a particular order to spell out the cheat code, which resulted in Memeio receiving a text message hinting at… something being available during specific hours at a power plant. They traveled to the spot at the right time and found a portal that teleported Jason back to the original Malibu Club, as seen in Vice City on PS2.

I’ll be honest; when I first saw people talking about this secret on social media, I dismissed it. It sounded too wild and impossible to be true. But then I remembered that tower with the Vice City noise, and so I decided to give it a shot. It took me about 30 minutes of flying around the map while following Memeio’s video guide to hit all the buttons. At one point I got in trouble with the cops, but thankfully, my dumpster lawyer let me hide in his office (the dumpster) to avoid them. GTA 6 is a weird game, folks.

Anyway, after hitting all the buttons at all the stations, I got the text message. I then headed to the power station within the correct time window, and to my surprise and delight, I found a big pink portal waiting for me behind a metal door. Opening the door activated a cutscene that teleported Jason back to the original Vice City, complete with stiff animations and Tommy’s famous blue Hawaiian shirt.

Here’s a video of me finding the portal and exploring the classic club:

There’s not much to see in the club. You can dance a bit, which is fun, and you can try to get a drink at the bar. However, because everyone in the old PS2-era GTA games had big blocky hands with no fingers, Jason can’t grab his wine and spills it.

After that, I headed up the back stairs and found Kent Paul (a character from the original Vice City) hanging out and doing coke. Before I could chat with him, a new portal opened up and sucked me out of 2002 Vice City and back into 2026 Vice City. But I did get a nice gift: Tommy Vercetti’s iconic blue shirt, as well as a chopper motorcycle inspired by the American flagâpainted bike you get early in GTA: Vice City.

I highly recommend you go hit these buttons and see this secret yourself, as it’s very impressive and is a real treat for longtime GTA fans. Now, I guess I’ll believe basically anything people claim is in this game because, at this point, it could be true.
