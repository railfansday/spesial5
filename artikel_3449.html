The Android camera app that doesn’t overprocess adds new color grading sets, expands to iPhone

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

VWFNDR + MBL, an Android camera app that doesn’t overprocess and delivers high-quality DNG (RAW) files, is getting a 2.0 release with improved color grading and an expansion to the iPhone.

VWFNDR + MBL launched on Android in May 2026 with a few points that stood out. A unique UI that adapts to the shot, minimal processing for a natural look, and support for DNG files at a level that only Google’s Pixel Camera app can truly compete with.

The app has been downloaded over 300,000 times since then, and is now getting a pretty substantial update.

On Android, this comes in the form of new color grading profiles built into the app. “NATURAL,” “NIHON,” or “B+W.” The app still saves an unprocessed DNG alongside the JPG and the grading adapts across the frame rather than having a fixed result, keeping highlights and shadows in place.

Colour grades: MBL still records unprocessed Bayer RAW DNG, and the JPG it renders alongside now uses one of three grades: NATURAL, NIHON, or B+W. Grading happens on VWFNDR’s own spatial engine. Instead of applying a fixed LUT, it adapts across the frame, much like a raw editor, so highlights and shadows keep their detail. The DNG stays untouched.

The rest of VWFNDR + MBL 2.0 takes place off of Android.

In a rare case of the tables turning, MBL was released first on Android, and only on Android for the first several months. But VWFNDR is now bringing the app to iPhone with support for variable aperture on iPhone 18 Pro and Content Credentials, Apple’s version of the C2PA standard that’s meant to prove a photo is real and not AI-generated.
