Millie Bobby Brown Sets New Netflix Movie Ahead Of David Harbour Reunion

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Millie Bobby Brown attending the Enola Holmes 3 premiere

Ahead of her Netflix reunion with David Harbour, Millie Bobby Brown has lined up a new project with the streaming service.

Harbour recently shared with ScreenRant that his new Netflix series with Brown had reached an important milestone, as "we have a bunch of scripts and we’re going into production early next year. It’s a really, really great show. This guy, Jack Thorne, who wrote Adolescence, is such a brilliant writer, and it really explores a lot of interesting themes that I think Millie and I want to explore. And it gets into that dynamic in a really rich and propulsive way, and I think it’s just going to be spectacular. I can’t wait."

Now, Netflix has announced that Brown will be starring in and producing Non-Refundable, a comedy film also starring Adults actor Jack Innanen. The story is centered on Dani (Brown) and Nate (Innanen), a former couple who travel to their booked, non-refundable wedding venue in Mexico, so the purchase doesn't go to waste and to show each other up with new dates. Saturday Night Live alum and Emmy Award nominee Paul Briganti will direct based on a spec script by David Bernstein.
