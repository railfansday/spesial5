Magic: The Gathering Designer Reveals the Biggest Problem With Marvel’s Infinity Stones

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

“Three of the six stones are about as blue as it comes.”

When Magic: The Gathering‘s Marvel’s Spider-Man set launched in 2025, the inclusion of The Soul Stone hinted at the scope of Wizards of the Coast’s partnership with Marvel. Including one of the six cosmically powerful gems as the set’s headliner card practically guaranteed the rest would eventually be released, a plan that was confirmed with the printing of The Mind Stone in the 2026 Marvel Super Heroes set. It’s a clever strategy, but one that Magic head game designer Mark Rosewater told Polygon demonstrates a problem with Universes Beyond design.

“We wanted to do something collectible, and we just said to ourselves, ‘In the Marvel property, is there anything that's cool as a collectible?’" Rosewater told Polygon at MagicCon: Atlanta 2026. “Well, the collection of six objects has been the cornerstone of multiple comic book series and the movies. The awareness of the Infinity Stones is so high because of the movies. They are just the obvious choice, so we went with them.”

Rosewater was part of the team that crafted the Infinity Stones as a way to connect the Marvel sets. The cards haven’t been finalized yet, giving Wizards flexibility to fit them into sets as they develop, but the colors and core utility have already been set. They’re all two-cost mana rocks that can be harnessed for a much higher cost to unlock a powerful persistent effect.

“A two-mana stone is just good enough that most decks probably can play that if they want,” Rosewater says. “We make the harnessed ability something that's gated so you don't get it right away, but when you do get it, it can be this pretty glorious thing.”

The hardest part was figuring out how to get six stones to fit into Magic’s five colors.

“One of the challenges of UB is we have to make something attached to our color pie that was not remotely designed for the color pie,” Rosewater says. “Three of the six stones are about as blue as it comes: the Mind Stone, the Space Stone, and the Time Stone. They can’t all be blue. The challenge was to divvy them up. They were close enough to their color matching the color of Magic that we finally decided let’s just make the green stone the green one. If you think of purple and yellow being black and white, we found most of them lined up. There are individual choices that might not be perfect, but each stone is the color that it is was the easiest and simplest way to explain it.”

The Mind Stone became white. The Time Stone will be green and be the headliner for next year's Darkhold Destiny, where its bearer, Doctor Strange, plays a key role in the story. The Space Stone will actually be blue when it appears. Rosewater says his team considered making those three stones blue to go with their thematic affinities, but decided against it.

“It caused a bunch of problems,” he says. “Now blue just has more access than anybody else. That means somebody doesn’t get it, so the people that love that color are yelling at us that everybody gets a two-drop except white. Why doesn’t white get it? For balance purposes and audience expectation purposes, we really, really wanted it to be a cycle.”

The team also considered having a bigger disparity between the core functionality of the cards.

“In the end, we really liked the idea that they’re mana stones,” Rosewater says. “They have a functionality in every deck. If you’re playing that color, you kind of want to play it. The extended effect might not be as effective in your deck, so maybe you don’t play it, but we wanted to make it so a lot of decks of that color would at least heavily consider it.”

There are six Infinity Stones and only five Magic colors. Rosewater says that’s not a problem.

“Magic can do six because we can do five colors and colorless, so Magic has a way to do six that’s built into the game,” he says.

While Wizards of the Coast has stopped short of confirming it outright, that implies we’re due for three more Marvel sets, each prominently featuring the Reality Stone, Power Stone, and Space Stone. The real question is whether Thanos might try to collect them all with the Infinity Gauntlet as some kind of culminating set-based event.
