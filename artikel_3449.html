As more cast members are revealed for the Marvel Cinematic Universe's X-Men reboot, speculation on who will round out the iconic team's roster runs rampant.

X-Men will star Sadie SInk as Jean Grey, Kit Connor as Scott Summers/Cyclops, Christopher Abbott as Charles Xavier/Professor X, Inde Navarette as Anna Marie/Rogue, Samara Weaving as Emma Frost, Maya Boyd as Ororo Munroe/Storm, and Adam Driver as Nathaniel Essex/Mr. Sinister. Other rumored castings include Karate Kid alum Ben Wang as Bobby Drake/Iceman, though Rhenzy Feliz, Gabriel LaBelle, and Jackson Kelly have also been named as contenders for the role.

Potentially the most anticipated X-Men recasting is Wolverine/Logan. For two decades, Hugh Jackman has portrayed the gruff mutant across both team-up and solo films, becoming a huge fan-favorite. As such, all eyes are on the actor who succeeds Jackman. Now, a new interview with Variety hints that I Swear star Robert Aramayo may be on the shortlist for the next Wolverine.

When asked directly about the possibility of playing Wolverine, Aramayo kept his cards close to his chest, saying: "I can’t even imagine. But you know what? You’re not the first person who’s said it, though." He doubled down on deflecting when asked if he was open to playing Logan: "Can you imagine if that happened?"

Aramayo also addressed the difficulties of joining such a beloved franchise. "I would be so scared," he said. "I absolutely love what [Jackman has] done with that role." Additionally, he paid tribute to the late legend Sir Patrick Stewart, who played the first live action Professor X.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day's Sadie Sink opens up about Marvel Studios having reportedly cast its new Cyclops for the X-Men reboot in the MCU franchise.

"Since we’re talking about X-Men, you’ve got to acknowledge Patrick Stewart," Aramayo added. "I was absolutely gutted. He’s one of those actors who was a big part of my childhood, like Harrison Ford. He did incredible work that so many different audiences watched. It’s so sad."

Notably, Aramayo and Abbott share a PR representative, but when confronted with this information, the Game of Thrones actor played it safe. "I am working on something, but I can’t talk about it," he confirmed.

X-Men releases in theaters on May 5, 2028.
