My Hero Academia Officially Returns December 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

My Hero Academia ended in May 2026 with the conclusion of its anime run. It was an epic finale, one teeming with the high-octane action that had peppered the rest of the series and popularized the original manga, as well as the tear-jerking sincerity that makes MHA so special. Naturally, with such a phenomenal farewell, fans are eager to see it return, a curse of great endings that remind you why you fell in love with a piece of art in the first place. Fortunately, while MHA is over, a plethora of My Hero Academia spin-off projects have been released.

From the ongoing My Hero Academia: Vigilantes (which fans hope will get a third season) to the recently released third My Hero Academia video game, All's Justice, fans have been treated to a smattering of exciting new projects that have helped keep the spirit of the legendary manga alive. Adding to this pantheon of great MHA content is a brand-new release, one that is set to see fan-favorite characters reborn in an unexpected way.

It turns out that the recently released My Hero Academia Switch 2 game was not the series' final foray into the world of video games, as it is getting a huge crossover event with the popular Ubisoft-published 2D platform fighting game, Brawlhalla. For those not in the know, Brawlhalla was released back in 2017 as a free-to-play platform fighter akin to the Super Smash Bros. series. Its developer, Blue Mammoth Games, was acquired by Ubisoft a year later in 2018, and since then it has served as an excellent and crossover-heavy fighting title that's available across all platforms, including mobile.

This My Hero Academia crossover joins a long line of similar events for other IPs, including WWE, Adventure Time, Hellboy, Rayman, Ben 10, The Walking Dead, and more. This time, fan-favorite characters Midoriya, Bakugo, and Todoroki, as well as a handful of other currently undisclosed characters, are heading into the arena to join the abundance of other existing original and IP-based characters. The event will happen across all platforms, meaning no one will miss out on the opportunity to beat up Ben 10 as Midoriya.

Like previous Brawlhalla crossover events, the newly added characters will exist as unlockable skins for existing Legends. This means that they'll function exactly the same as the Legends they are ostensibly replacing, including sharing the same stats, weapons, and hitboxes. However, they do get their own roster spot, have their name announced when entering the arena, and unique signature effects that help differentiate them from the other characters.

The My Hero Academia characters coming to Brawlhalla have all been reimagined in the game's signature art style so as to better fit alongside the other characters, although they still retain much of their original look. They will be released on December 9 and will be purchasable from the in-game store using the premium currency, Mammoth Coins. If you feel like the existing My Hero Academia fighting games aren't enough, or are a Brawlhalla fan already and want to play as your favorite MHA characters, then this is undeniably the crossover event for you.
