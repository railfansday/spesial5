Amy Poehler’s ‘Parks and Recreation’ Replacement Officially Arrives Next Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

It's been over ten years since Amy Poehler left Pawnee, Indiana, and Parks and Recreation behind, and since then, she's been less of a defining presence in live-action television comedy. Her most recognizable role since starring in the beloved mockumentary sitcom for seven seasons has easily been as the emotion Joy in Disney's Inside Out movies, though she's also been quite busy behind the camera. She co-created the Netflix hit Russian Doll, as well as her own animated comedy series, Duncanville, and served as producer on everything from Broad City to Harlem and the recent Cinderella-twisting animated film Steps. In just over a week, however, she'll be back to bringing the laughs on-screen with help from a familiar collaborator.

Poehler is set to resurface at Peacock for the new series Dig, which she both leads and co-created with one half of the Parks and Recreation creative duo, Michael Schur​​​​. Based on Kate Myers' bestselling novel Excavations, it centers on four women at vastly different stages of their careers working on an archaeological dig site in Greece over the summer who set their differences aside after making the discovery of a lifetime. Their history-changing big break also puts them at the center of an international conspiracy that spans far wider than they could possibly imagine. All episodes are slated to drop on November 23, just in time for a holiday binge over Thanksgiving.

After leading Pawnee's Parks and Recreation department, Poehler leads the cast of Dig as the excavation site's lead archaeologist, Elise, with Hugh Laurie playing the long-standing source of her ire, Neville, a British scientist who sincerely loves archaeology but has simply refused to retire from the dig and always gets the credit for any discoveries. The main group is rounded out by Geraldine Viswanathan, Fina Strazza, and Antonia Thomas, though there's also a wealth of stars attached. Jason Mantzoukas, Noma Dumezweni, and Brennan Lee Mulligan are set to recur, while What We Do in the Shadows alum Kayvan Novak headlines a group of guest stars including Max Greenfield, Jon Pointing, Bonita Friedericy, and Dan Bucatinsky.
