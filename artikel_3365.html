Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando Marry at Palm Springs Wedding

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Miley Cyrus and her fiancé Maxx Morando married Saturday at a wedding ceremony in Palm Springs, California.

Photos of the wedding emerged Saturday night, showing the pair exchanging nuptials while standing under an arch covered in white flowers.

On Friday, members of the Cyrus clan — including mom Tish and sister Noah — were spotted gathering for the couple’s rehearsal dinner in Southern California; the daughter of Tish Cyrus’ husband revealed the rehearsal dinner on social media, TMZ reported. Billy Ray Cyrus was also in attendance for his daughter’s wedding, People reported.

The couple became engaged in December 2025 after four years of dating. Cyrus and Morando were first linked in 2021 after meeting on a blind date. Morando is currently the drummer of the band Liily, a band that Cyrus featured in Miley’s New Year’s Eve Party in 2022.

Cyrus has since worked with Morando on songs from her last two albums, Endless Summer Vacation and Something Beautiful. She thanked Morando when she took home her Record of the Year Grammy Award for “Flowers” in 2024. In September, the pair hinted at their impending nuptials with Cyrus’ “Let’s Get Married” video, where she co-starred with her fiancé as they romped around Los Angeles’ Chateau Marmont.

Cyrus was previously married to Hunger Games actor Liam Hemsworth. She began dating Hemsworth in 2009 and the pair were engaged twice, first in 2013 and later in 2016. They married in 2017, separated in 2018, and their divorce was finalized in early 2020.
