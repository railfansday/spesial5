‘The Land Before Time’ Officially Returns With New Release as 40th Anniversary Nears

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Hannah covers horror, sci-fi, and games at Collider, where she writes news and features, reviews new releases, and interviews the people who make them. She’s helping build out Collider’s gaming coverage, with Pokémon as one of her specialties. If she isn’t writing about a monster, she’s probably watching one on a tape from her VHS collection.

There’s a particularly cruel moment in The Land Before Time when Littlefoot sees a longneck’s shadow on a rock wall and runs to greet his mother. For a few seconds, he’s so happy to see her that it takes a beat to realize he’s running toward his own shadow. Don Bluth knew exactly how long to let a kid believe that reunion was possible. Thirty-eight years later, that scene still hurts.

Littlefoot eventually found his way to the Great Valley, where he and his friends had enough adventures to keep the direct-to-video aisle stocked for years. By the time Universal released the 14th movie, Journey of the Brave, in 2016, Littlefoot was leading the gang on a rescue mission to find his dad. Ten years after that outing, a group of animators is taking him back to the days when his mother still had to show him the way.

The first trailer for Littlefoot, a fan-made, 3D-animated reimagining of The Land Before Time, is here. Director Joey Lever shared the footage on November 18, the 38th anniversary of the original movie. Due to release in 2027, the independently produced short from Imoria Pictures runs about 15 minutes and centers on Littlefoot’s bond with his mother. Summer-Joules Saunders voices Littlefoot opposite Larissa Loeffler as his mother. Among the other young dinosaurs is a voice X-Men ’97 fans know as Gambit. A.J. LoCascio plays Petrie, the one member of the group who ought to be able to scout the road ahead if he could just work up the nerve to fly. Sarah Natochenny, who spent years voicing Ash Ketchum on Pokémon, plays Ducky, while Cristina Vee takes on Cera. Mark Meer, familiar to Mass Effect players as Commander Shepard, gets to be the adult laying down the law as Cera’s dad. Lever saved himself a part, too, as he has stepped into the role of Spike.

Lever first tried to get Littlefoot going in 2017, barely a year after Universal’s last sequel. In his anniversary post, he credited three years of work by artists, animators, musicians, and actors around the world who volunteered their time. The choice of what to do with those 15 minutes says quite a bit about what stuck with them. Littlefoot’s mother only gets part of one movie with her son. Every kid who followed him through another 13 knows who taught him to look for the Great Valley in the first place.

Littlefoot is expected to release in 2027.
