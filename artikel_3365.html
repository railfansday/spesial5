‘Yellowjackets’ Stars Melanie Lynskey & Christina Ricci On Misty’s Plot “To Save Themselves” From Shauna: “She’ll Be The Downfall Of Everybody”

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

SPOILERS: This post contains details about the Yellowjackets Season 4 premiere episode “Hivemind”

As the final season of Yellowjackets debuts, it appears the team could be prepping one last sacrifice to the wilderness.

While discussing Friday’s Season 4 premiere episode “Hivemind” with Deadline, Christina Ricci explained that the episode picks up shortly after the Season 3 finale, as her character Misty is “trying to get rid of Shauna [Melanie Lynskey], to save themselves, really, because they feel that she’ll be the downfall of everybody” following the death of Van (Lauren Ambrose).

“It’s an interesting thing to play because I think Shauna is always kind of thinking that Misty might try to kill her,” explained Lynskey. “And then Taissa [Tawny Cypress], it’s a different story. There’s so much love and trust that she has for Taissa, so that whole journey is an interesting one.” Watch on Deadline

After Melissa (Hilary Swank) killed Van in the Season 3 penultimate episode “How the Story Ends,” Shauna and Tai have hired a private investigator to track her down, only for their former teammate to come out of hiding and move her family in to the house next door to Shauna.

Lynskey said, “Yeah, I think it’s such a weird choice. And it just makes [Shauna] a thousand times more paranoid, and she’s already very, very paranoid. So, it just kind of goes off the rails from there.”

In Yellowjackets, a 1996 New Jersey soccer team’s plane crashes in the Canadian wilderness, where they struggle to survive while they’re stranded for 19 months as a dark supernatural force seemingly lurks in the woods. Meanwhile, their adult counterparts cope with the memories of what went down in the wilderness.

With new episodes available to stream Fridays on Paramount+ with Premium Plan, the fourth and final season sees the Yellowjackets rescued and returned home, where they must adjust to civilization and take their secrets to the grave.
