XRP Ledger's surge in payments volume is important and brings a lot to the table. According to the network metrics, payment volume reached 746.3 million XRP, while the number of payments climbed to 1.1 million. Payments volume growth is substantial, but not record-breaking by any means, as it still sits far below the all-time high of 7 billion XRP.

The lack of price volatility on the market shouldn't be a major issue here, as the movement on the network most likely reflects improving activity that might proceed to price moves.

It's not clear where the movement is coming from per se. The market is moving forward, but unfortunately, there's a good chance that prices won't react to it, despite it being a positive development.

Other metrics, like Active Accounts, Active Users and Accounts Created, are also on the rise. Active accounts now sit at 41,600, and active users at 251,100. That brings up a good possibility for investors: the network is responding positively and might provide us with a recovery ground sooner rather than later, even though the performance of XRP leaves a lot to be desired.

Price is already struggling, trading at $0.9581. The $1 threshold is broken, which essentially enabled a rapid decrease, with the asset dipping to around $0.89 during the drop.

XRP is trading below all the moving averages shown on the chart. The downtrend is way stronger than the bullish reversal attempts XRP had recently, and RSI is hovering near the 30 line, so the asset is close to oversold territory.

Unfortunately, the market is brutal right now and clearly showing some serious pressure. Network activity rises, but so does the bear market pressure, so a quick reaction in price shouldn't be expected.

The confirmation of a market recovery would only come if we witness specific signs of a bull run: higher highs and sustained buying. For now, that recovery is yet to be confirmed.
