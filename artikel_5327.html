Hoskinson: Cardano Not Done Yet

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Charles Hoskinson made sure to clear up misunderstandings about whether he thought the work on Cardano was done.

"I never once said Cardano is finished," Hoskinson said in a Nov. 15 post on X.

The comments came after he talked about the completion of Cardano's original roadmap, which raised some questions about his role in the blockchain's future.

He also called out the CEO of the Cardano Foundation, Frederik Gregaard, for not mentioning the Midnight chain when discussing everything the network has accomplished.

In his latest video, uploaded on Nov. 17, Hoskinson celebrated the fact that Midnight had just brought on board its first UK-based banking partner, Monument Bank.

Monument Bank is the first UK bank to offer interest-bearing tokenized retail deposits on a public blockchain, according to an announcement from the Midnight Foundation.

Up to £250 million in customer deposits will be added to Midnight in the first phase, and each tokenized deposit will be backed 1:1 by sterling cash held at the bank.

Monument Bank customers can now earn a fixed 5.2% annual equivalent rate on deposits for 12 months, all without having to deal with cryptocurrencies directly.

Hoskinson was very excited about the scale of the initiative, comparing the planned amount of deposits with the current decentralized finance ecosystem on Cardano.

"It's five times the TVL of Cardano," he said.

The £250 million is the planned initial size of the banking product, not a verified figure for how much is currently deposited on Midnight.

Hoskinson called this one of the most important milestones in his career.

"This is just one of the many things to come, but I think it's one of the most significant things of at least my career," he said.

He added that the team behind Midnight had moved the originally scheduled hard fork from December to November to accommodate the technical needs of Monument Bank.

Despite the milestone, Hoskinson emphasized that it's only the first step in a much larger mission: bringing traditional financial assets onto Midnight.

Hoskinson thinks the main barrier to cryptocurrency adoption is the lack of trust among consumers. He believes that simplifying the user experience without compromising regulatory requirements will allow billions of people to join the blockchain economy.

Hoskinson also shared that there's a big event coming up for the Midnight Foundation on Dec. 15. Developers will showcase future products and technological advancements scheduled for 2027.
