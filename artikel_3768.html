FC 27 Road to the Knockouts Challenge 2 SBC solutions and rewards

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Road to the Knockouts Challenge 2 SBC is now live in FC 27, and you can win a free pack by completing it in time. Like all fodder challenges, the requirements for this SBC are easy to manage. You probably already have most of, if not all, the cards to complete the SBC.

Let’s find out the requirements and the rewards of this SBC.

The Road to the Knockouts Challenge 2 SBC has just one task, but there are strict requirements.

The best solution is to use cards that are already present at your club. This will reduce the cost of completing the challenge, and you can raise the value of your rewards. However, you also need to meet the requirements of the SBC. If you get stuck, you can use the following solution to unlock the rewards more quickly.

Once you complete the challenge, you’ll get a Small Mixed Players Pack. While the rewards could be better, this is a tradeable pack. Whatever you find inside can be sold for coins. If you’re lucky, you could end up with a promo/walkout item. At worst, you’ll find more fodder to complete future SBCs.
