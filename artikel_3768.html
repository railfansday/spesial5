All season 11 battle pass skins in Marvel Rivals – The Incursion

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Season 11 of Marvel Rivals is here, bringing with it an iconic new battle pass to tackle. As you work your way through this pass, you’ll find an array of exclusive skins available to unlock.

If you’re unsure whether or not you want to purchase this pass to gain access to all outfits featured in it, reviewing the skins ahead of time is a handy way to help make this decision easier. Here are all of the season 11 battle pass skins in Marvel Rivals.

In total, there are 10 skins you can unlock throughout the season 11 battle pass for Marvel Rivals. Here’s a breakdown of the rarity for each one, which page you gain access to them on, and what they all look like.

There are two free skins up for grabs on the season 11 battle pass, which means you can unlock them without purchasing it. These skins are Ultron: Avenger Ultron and Iron Man: Silver Centurion.

The season 11 battle pass costs 990 Lattice. There are some free rewards featured throughout it, but to unlock the majority of the skins and items, you need to pay this fee.

If you’re looking for some tasks to tackle after checking out all season 11 battle pass skins, consider working on the Night of Illusions event. There are plenty of tricky quests to solve throughout it, including Chapter one – Infirmary puzzle, Chapter four – Hall of Eternal Night puzzle, and Chapter five – Unknown Space puzzle.
