Depth Scout Online Fishing Maps, Reviewed

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

If you’re new to the fishing world, Depth Scout is one of the latest in an ever-expanding lineage of resources meant to make fishing more accessible to the masses. What’s different about Depth Scout? Depth Scout contains a treasure trove of intel on a wide range of fisheries, with both Free and Pro versions available. So, whether you’re wanting to really dive off into the deep end of your local fishery or access information on 168 species that swim in 42,193 fisheries in 23 different countries, Depth Scout has you covered.

Depth Scout is a web-based platform that gathers information from public resources as well as users to create in-depth looks into a multitude of fisheries. Data is collected from organizations like the U.S. Geological Survey, National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, and the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, then distilled to build user-friendly maps and bios for thousands of fisheries. Governmental fisheries surveys are used to list out the species and general populations that can be found in a fishery, and then fishing spots are suggested on the map for whichever species the angler chooses to target.

What separates Depth Scout from other similar platforms is the sheer breadth of information. Depth Scout is not just maps and contours. The platform helps saltwater and freshwater anglers alike to figure out when and where to fish based on weather, solar calendars, lunar phases, and more, and provides the rules and regulations for the fishery you’re interested in. Public access and boat ramps are even highlighted on the maps, all in one place.

At first, Depth Scout can be a little overwhelming. If you don’t really go in with a goal in mind (i.e., Is X a good fishery to go to next Wednesday to target largemouth bass), then Depth Scout can feel like drinking water from a fire hydrant. There is an extensive amount of information available here. But, using the dropdown options for species, fishery, and what type of information you’re looking for, the platform can be very helpful for anything from finding a depth chart to strong suggestions on where to fish to information on where to find boat ramps and public access points. The suggested fishing locations even have info boxes you can click to see Depth Scout’s reasoning for the suggestions, tailored to the targeted species.

You can save maps to use offline and export GPS waypoints that can be imported into chart plotters on your boat. You can also setup bite alerts to come to your phone, notifying you of the best upcoming windows to fish based on location, weather, sonar and lunar charts, and more. I sampled this on a couple of my local lakes and the suggested fishing spots were pretty legit, highlighting areas where I have caught fish before.

There are even lure suggestions to give you a good starting point, and you can log your catches and keep them private or share them with other users. Depth Scout also displays applicable regulations for the fishery and advises on where to buy any necessary fishing licenses.

The primary goal of Depth Scout is to make fishing more accessible to the amateur fisherman and make sure he or she knows the rules and regulations. If you’re overwhelmed, start with the fishing spots and you can ignore everything else. Then, as you progress, look at bait recommendations. The further you go down the rabbit hole, the more you will find. But take what you need and don’t be afraid to leave the rest. Depth Scout is not about knowing everything, it’s about gathering everything in one place so you can quickly find anything.
