Dig Dig Dino was originally released on the Playdate before coming to Steam this week. It’s from the developers of the well-liked western action game Arco, though this time they’ve gone for something much smaller, simpler, and cuter. In Dig Dig Dino you play as an paleontology intern tasked with using your shovel and a growing arsenal of tools to find dinosaur bones. You start with limited stamina, only able to dig so much in a day. But as you chew through layers of dirt and sedimentary rock, you pick up treasure that gets exchanged for cash with which you can buy more more stamina, upgrades for your tools, and new tools like a dog who will sniff out bones for you.

The game sounds almost too simplistic to be any kind of fun, but I found myself spending hours digging, hoping the next run would reveal the final piece to my saurian puzzle. There are different dig sites that require different tools and yield different treasures, but don’t expect to discover the kinds of finds that would excite the dearly departed Dr. Alan Grant. The dinos you discover in this game are weird, and though I’m only a few hours (and a few competed skeletons) in, I suspect there’s a greater mystery about the world to discover as you dig deeper.
