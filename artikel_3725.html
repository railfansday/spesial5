How to search containers while listening to the Jam Track Player in Fortnite Treasure Hunters

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Fortnite oftentimes has weekly quests that task you with using gameplay features you may not have known existed. One such mission appears in Fortnite Treasure Hunters and asks you to search containers while listening to the Jam Track Player.

The Jam Track Player is quite hidden, so figuring out how to activate this special feature the first time around is fairly tricky. You need to get as many weekly quests done as possible this season, as checking them off is crucial for unlocking all Captain X items. To help you get this one done, here’s how to search containers while listening to the Jam Track Player in Fortnite.

You can use the Jam Track Player by selecting a song to play from the Jam Tracks Collection page under the Emote Wheel option in Fortnite. It takes quite a lot of searching to locate the correct option to trigger it, so here’s a full step-by-step breakdown of how to get it done.

You’ll know that you’ve correctly started the Jam Track Player if you see the name of your selected Jam Track pop up near the middle right edge of the screen. Once you’ve started playing it, it stays on for the rest of the round unless you choose to stop it.

To complete the search containers while listening to the Jam Track Player quest, you need to loot a total of 25 containers while playing a song through the Jam Track Player. To get this quest done, simply loot all containers you come across after activating any song on the Jam Track Player using the steps outlined above.

Containers include Treasure Chests, Ammo Boxes, Produce Boxes, Rare Chests, and any other such loot capsules you encounter around the island. Anything you can interact with and open to find loot counts as one of the 25 containers for this mission.

With this quest now complete, you can move on to other key tasks waiting to be tackled around the island. Next, you might get to work dancing on a driveable car, checking how many chests you’ve opened, and reviewing all Treasure Hunters loot odds and drop rates.
