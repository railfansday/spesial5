2027 Ram 1500 Rumble Bee Foose Edition Is a Lowrider with a Truck Bed

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Along with having up to 1000 horsepower and a factory warranty, Chip Foose made this street truck look good.

Stellantis might be taking its time figuring out how to get the Dodge Charger back to its original popularity, but when it comes to street trucks, things have never been better. Anyone missing that Hemi V-8 thunder need look no further than the Ram Rumble Bee lineup, a trio of street-fighter pickups set up for tarmac handling and the drag strip.

Sometimes, though, buyers want a little something extra that still has a factory-backed warranty, which is exactly what you get with this new team-up between noted designer Chip Foose and Fox Factory vehicles. It takes the burly platform of the Ram Rumble Bee and kicks things up a notch, while still offering three years or 36,000 miles of bumper-to-bumper coverage.

Foose’s styling extends to a new front splitter and tailgate spoiler, side skirts, and a full graphics package. The exterior is then capped off by massive 22-inch wheels wearing meaty 305-section-width rubber.

Mechanically, the Foose Rumble Bee can be ordered with either the 705-hp supercharged 6.2-liter V-8 or upgraded to a bigger SRT supercharger running more boost. That upgrade nets you a ferocious 1000 horses, with either option breathing through a MagnaFlow dual exhaust. Plywood never got home from the local DIY center so fast.

The truck’s suspension is from Ridetech, with a lowered ride height on adjustable coil-over suspension. The damping has been retuned for improved cornering, with the rear control arms designed to get the power down with minimal wheel hop under hard launches.

This is a lot of Ram, and it costs a lot of money. The starting price for the entry-level Rumble Bee is $62,790, while the Foose Ram Rumble Bee kicks off at $154,990. That’s a big ask, but it’s available with more than twice the power of the 5.7-liter V-8 Rumble Bee. Further, sourcing all these parts from the aftermarket individually would ring up a long invoice, and that’s without the factory-backed warranty.
