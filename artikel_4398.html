Bentley CEO Teases a Production Version of the Off-Road-Focused Bentayga X

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

“Yes, we have this on the horizon,” Frank-Steffen Walliser said. “Time will tell us when it’s ready.”

Bentley may not exactly be the first name in off-road performance, but the British luxury brand made its first moves toward that horizon with a surprise concept in January. Now, it looks like that Bentayga X concept might be headed for production in the near future.

In an interview with Automotive News, CEO Frank-Steffen Walliser says that reaction to the Bentayga X was “really surprisingly positive.” When asked directly if the brand was considering building the concept as a production model, Walliser said that “yes, we have this on the horizon. Time will tell us when it’s ready.”

That is not quite a direct confirmation of production plans, but it is pretty much as close as an executive will get without directly announcing a new model. It’s also great news for buyers hoping to get their hands on a Bentayga with more off-road capability—an option that has never before been available in the company’s century-long history.

The concept shown in January added over two inches of ride height and nearly five inches of track width to the basic Bentayga Speed, bringing total ground clearance over 12 inches and total wading depth up to 21.6 inches. While we do not know how much of the concept would make its way into a theoretical production Bentayga X, it would be safe to assume that the final product would ride higher and sit wider than a typical Bentley SUV. All-terrain rubber and extra lighting elements, both seen on the concept, could also presumably make their way to the finished product.
