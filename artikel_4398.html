8 Remakes That Would Be Just As Meaningful As The Ocarina Of Time Remake

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Aden Carter is a Contributor at DualShockers who has been covering games professionally since 2020. He specializes in lists and guides, with a focus on RPGs, horror games, survival horror, Soulslikes, cozy/sim games, and action-adventure titles. Before joining DualShockers, Aden contributed to GameRant, XDA, Gamepur, I

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake was one of the most anticipated remakes of all time. Year after year, fans would beg Nintendo to remake this masterpiece. In 2026, Nintendo finally listened, and the Ocarina of Time Remake was officially released.

The remake has received constant praise across the board, and fans are once again excited to jump into the land of Hyrule to stop Ganondorf and save the world.

This got me thinking. There have been many impactful games over the years, and while it is nice to see Ocarina of Time again, I bet there are other games that would be just as meaningful to fans if they were to be remade in the modern era.

Released back in 1999 on the PlayStation, Dino Crisis struck fear into the hearts of many, and it hit the same note as Resident Evil from years prior. Fans loved Capcom’s new style of horror game, and it has since become a cult classic, with a fan base that has been begging for the game to get a remake.

Dino Crisis took a step forward from the Resident Evil franchise, using real-time 3D environments. Along with this, the game featured slightly different mechanics that made it feel more tense.

While there have been some ports of the game, there has never been a remake. However, it is a game that is constantly mentioned whenever a discussion of remakes comes up, showing that fans are craving the original title.

There are quite a few Forgotten Capcom Games That Deserve A Remake in the modern day.

Whenever people talk about the Fallout franchise, they tend to gravitate towards either Fallout 3 or Fallout: New Vegas. All other Fallout titles are often ignored, and many even regard these two as being some of the best games ever made.

However, when it comes down to choosing between the two, people generally prefer Fallout: New Vegas. This is because of its superior roleplaying style and story. Meanwhile, the other is often considered to have a better atmosphere.

It has been said that a New Vegas remaster is in the works, and it will be around the same scope as the Oblivion remaster. However, if Bethesda were to completely remake the game, making it look better than even Fallout 4, then fans would go crazy for it. At least this is one game we know is coming back.

Star Wars has always been a huge franchise, and coming off the heels of the Star Wars: Episode II release was the hit titled Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic - a game that would become a fan favorite for generations.

This was one of BioWare’s best titles, and many remember it fondly to this day. It is a game that fans have begged to have remade. Unfortunately, a remake has never seen the light of day.

There have been rumors that got shut down, and even companies that have said they were working on it. Now, Saber Interactive is working on the Knights of the Old Republic Remake, but there has been little word about it for years. Fans never know if they will see this game in a new light, but when it finally gets released, it will rock the gaming industry.

Half-Life is considered one of the biggest masterpieces of gaming, and is also considered one of the most influential games for the shooter genre. It told the story of Gordon Freeman without ever needing to pull the camera away or break the mystique of the game.

The game pioneered seamless, scripted events, which made missions feel more cinematic. It also is known for having amazing environmental storytelling, with it using audio cues and NPC behaviours to communicate the story effectively.

There is a reason that Half-Life is known as one of the greatest games of all time. The franchise as a whole has been so impactful that fans are still begging for a third entry in the series.

Today, many are focused on the release of Grand Theft Auto VI, and for good reason. Many are saying it will be Rockstar’s greatest game. However, a remake of a past title of theirs would be a bit more meaningful.

If the team decided to remake Grand Theft Auto III, it would hit hard for many who lived through the era of the early 2000s. After all, GTA III is said to be the pioneer of the modern open world, with it giving players access to a fully open 3D environment known as Liberty City.

This game went on to influence many others, such as Saints Row and Mafia. Without it, we might not have open world games as we know them, and if it were to be remade in a modern engine, fans would lose their minds.

There is no denying that Ocarina of Time is the most influential of all the Zelda titles released over the years. Also released around the time of the original Ocarina of Time was a narrative sequel that fans remember just as much to this day.

Of course, I am referring to The Legend of Zelda: Majora’s Mask. This is another masterpiece of a Zelda title that has stood the test of time and is still talked about to this day.

While Ocarina of Time made fans hopeful, Majora’s Mask took the story down a dark path, and it is one that heavily impacted those who played it back in the day. If there is another Zelda title that deserves the same remake treatment, it is this one.

Eiji Aonuma and the development team for the Ocarina of Time remake discussed how one of the original's infamous dungeons is being improved.

If you owned an N64 back in the day, odds are you played Super Mario 64. This is yet another classic N64 title that fans love and has been played to death. If any other game matches up to the popularity of Ocarina of Time, it would be Super Mario 64.

This game pioneered special movement in a 3D environment, allowing players to freely explore the Mushroom Kingdom with a fully functional camera. It is one of the most impactful games that has ever been released.

For many, this is the first time they saw Mario enter the 3D realm, and it is a game that has stuck with gamers for generations. There are even those who speedrun the game on a yearly basis.

Chrono Trigger is one of Square Enix’s most celebrated titles. Released back in 1995 on the SNES, the game told a story that spanned multiple eras of time, as heroes tried to prevent the world from ending.

Over the years, Chrono Trigger has received multiple ports, with it appearing on mobile devices, the Nintendo DS, and also the PlayStation. These ports have received praise from the community, with the DS port receiving a 92 from review scores.

Many have asked for this masterpiece to be reimagined in HD-2D over the years, and some have even asked for it to receive the Final Fantasy VII Remake treatment. Unfortunately, there has never been an official announcement of a remake, but it would instantly become a major hit if it was.

While some JRPGs disappear, others return from oblivion after years without new releases.
