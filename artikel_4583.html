Hera reveals stadium-sized asteroid crater from spacecraft impact

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Arriving at the site of humankind's first planetary defense experiment, the European Space Agency's Hera mission has found a miniature world of surprises. Dimorphos—the small asteroid whose orbit around a second, larger asteroid was deflected by a spacecraft impact—now has one face flattened with a stadium-sized crater and is tumbling chaotically. Meanwhile, hundreds of previously unknown boulders encircle the binary pair.
