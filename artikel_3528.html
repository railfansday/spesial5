How Long Does It Take To Fly Across The Entire GTA 6 Map?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Grand Theft Auto 6 map is absolutely massive, gigantic, and at least one more synonym to encapsulate its size accurately. Whether it's land, sea, or air, covering it is going to take a lot of time, but just how many hours should you set aside for a flight across Leonida?

We found out for ourselves by piloting a single-propeller plane across the map, which took us an entire four minutes and 10 seconds between the two points on the map. That's pretty big!

We've known for a while that the GTA 6 map was going to be big--with roughly more playable space than GTA 4, GTA 5, and Red Dead Redemption 2 combined--but even that claim doesn't put into perspective just how enormous the game truly is until you experience it for yourself. Another fascinating tidbit is that escaping GTA 6 is nigh-impossible, but definitely worth attempting.

As one streamer has discovered, if you try to cheese it on water using a jet ski, you'll run out of fuel and be left stranded in the water. There might be a happy ending to this scenario, as some streamers have posted clips of a dog on a wave runner coming to their rescue. We've tried to replicate this ourselves, but we've had no luck yet.

But if you choose to stay within Leonida's invisible boundaries, you won't run out of things to do for quite some time. Between missions to improve your lot in life, pursue relationship goals, and soak up the scenery, the reactive open world of GTA 6 will keep you entertained for hours. Our GTA 6 review gave the game a rare 10/10 score, and dozens of other reviewers worldwide have also been very impressed with what Rockstar Games has achieved.

You can experience all of that for yourself soon, and if you're not planning to do some digital tourism with a long walk, check out our GTA 6 guides hub for all kinds of walkthroughs to help you survive and thrive in the game.
