A host of new spy-thriller shows have been dominating the streaming charts in recent years, with some of them emerging as critically acclaimed gems. Apple TV has found a platform-defining hit in Slow Horses, which recently concluded its sixth season with a perfect 100% score on Rotten Tomatoes. Meanwhile, Peacock delivered the subversive Cold War-era series Ponies, which holds a 94% score on the aggregator website. This year also saw the return of The Agency, a sleek spy series starring Michael Fassbender. Amid the genre's revival, a classic from the Golden Age of Television is also having a resurgence.

The series in question aired for eight seasons, from 2011 to 2020. This was the era of shows such as The Walking Dead, Breaking Bad, and Mad Men. For the spy series to stand out and establish an identity for itself amid such tough competition was nothing short of remarkable. The show was a major hit for Showtime, along with the comedy-drama Californication, with which it partially overlapped.

Set in the shadow of the September 11 attacks, the series explored the geopolitics of the 2000s and the hyper-paranoia of the American public during that time. It follows a CIA agent with grave personal demons who investigates the return of a prisoner of war who may have been turned by the enemy. Claire Danes plays the CIA agent, while Damian Lewis appears as the anti-hero under suspicion.

By now you've probably guessed that we're talking about Homeland. Based on the Israeli series Prisoners of War by Gideon Raff, who went on to direct the very similar Netflix titles The Spy and The Red Sea Diving Resort, Homeland holds an overall 85% score on Rotten Tomatoes. Remarkably, it maintained a high standard throughout its run, with the first season scoring 100% on Rotten Tomatoes, and the eighth season sitting at an 85% score.

According to FlixPatrol, Homeland found a spot in the top 10 of the domestic iTunes chart this week, along with fellow new entrants Killjoys and It: Welcome to Derry. The leaderboard was topped by the sci-fi hit From, which was recently added to the Netflix library. Meanwhile, Danes received high praise for her performance in the Netflix series The Beast in Me, which debuted as a limited series in 2025, but has since been renewed for a second season.

You can watch Homeland on Paramount+, and stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
