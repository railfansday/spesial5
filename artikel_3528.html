Joe Amabile Gives 'Great' Update After MRI Amid Brain Surgery Recovery

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Bachelor Nation’s Joe Amabile is offering a positive update after undergoing an MRI amid his recovery from brain surgery.

“Got my results back from my MRI. It’s clean,” Amabile, 40, shared in a Wednesday, November 18, Instagram video. “Recovering well. Surgery went great. So, I’m good to go.”

He echoed in the caption, “MRI came back clean. No signs of a tumor and my brain is recovering great.”

Days prior, Amabile opened up about feeling nervous before undergoing his MRI.

“I’ve been feeling a little out of it the past couple days, but the weather is so nice I just went for a run. My first MRI from my surgery is next week, so I’m a little worried about that. But …” Amabile said in a November 11 Instagram Story as he shrugged his shoulders.

Amabile shared in July that he was preparing to undergo brain surgery after participating in a Prenuvo full body scan, which revealed a “blueberry size lesion.” Amabile underwent surgery in August and later revealed that he had been diagnosed with a “very low grade” astrocytoma — a type of brain or spinal cord tumor that may result in headaches, seizures, nausea or changes in mood or behavior, per the Mayo Clinic.

“They did get the whole thing,” he said in his video at the time. “When it comes to malignant brain tumors, they don’t know what’s left over, so I will be going on treatment, which is just a pill every day for the rest of my life to put the bad cells to sleep, and hopefully they stay asleep.”

Amid his recovery, Amabile began training for the New York City Marathon.

“Having brain surgery is, kind of, out of your control and having a brain tumor is something that’s out of your control, so to give myself a goal that gave me control back just made me feel like it was important,” Amabile exclusively told Us in October. “I just kept my mindset positive.”

“I didn’t think I was retired from running marathons, but then I was like, ‘You know what? I’m gonna do it. I’m gonna do it again this year, three months after I had brain surgery,’” he continued. “The feeling I will get when I cross the finish line, I’m sure will be greater than both marathons, even though I’m gonna run it slower.”

Just 88 days after undergoing brain surgery, Amabile crossed the finish line in the Big Apple. His wife, Serena Pitt, cheered him on from the sidelines.

“WHAT A DAY! My worst time yet and my greatest accomplishment at the same time,” Amabile wrote via Instagram, to which Pitt, 29, commented, “I’ll never forget today. You inspire me and so many around you!!”
