3 Best New Prime Video Series To Binge-Watch This Weekend (November 20–22, 2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The 3 best new Prime Video series to binge-watch this weekend, November 20–22, 2026, are an epic fantasy, a historical drama, and a romantic thriller. Prime Video currently has plenty of great streaming options across all genres to dive into this weekend, including major franchises like The Terminal List and Reacher to fan-favorite coming-of-age dramas such as Campus Drivers and Off Campus.

That said, the following three series are the most binge-worthy options on Prime Video right now, all of which are currently dropping new episodes. If you're looking for even more new shows to stream, check out ScreenRant's weekend binge guides for Netflix, Paramount+, and HBO Max, bringing you the latest and greatest choices across these major platforms to take the stress out of streaming this weekend.

The popular romantic K-drama series Love in Disguise is nearing the end of its season 1 run with its finale episode set to arrive on Tuesday, November 24. The 12-part series premiered back in mid-October and has been one of the most viral new releases on Prime Video this Fall 2026, currently ranking #3 on its worldwide viewership chart. Love in Disguise is celebrated for its high entertainment value and outstanding performances from its lead actors, who, according to audiences, have undeniable and infectious on-screen chemistry.

Love in Disguise follows a developing love story between a wealthy businessman and his new secretary, although his employee is much more than meets the eye. Once a special forces officer, she is now secretly working undercover to investigate a murder case in which her boss is a suspect. What's more, the covert secretary is also disguised as a man, revealing a repressed sexual interest within her boss. Now is the perfect time to binge the available 10 episodes of Love in Disguise before its final two episodes land early next week.

The Chosen is once again one of the top trending series on Prime Video worldwide following its season 6 premiere last Sunday, November 15. The epic historical series depicts the life of Jesus as described in the Bible and has become one of the most-viewed Prime Video series ever made, having surpassed 300 million views across its first five seasons. The Chosen season 6 chronicles one of the darkest eras of Jesus' life during his final 24 hours. The fan-favorite series has already been renewed for a seventh and final season.

The Chosen is currently #2 on Prime Video's U.S. streaming chart and sixth worldwide, indicating that the long-running biblical series still has momentum since its 2019 debut. The series also earned a near-perfect 97% Rotten Tomatoes audience score and a remarkably high 9.1 IMDb score, placing it just outside IMDb's Top 50 TV shows of all time. While The Chosen has obvious religious themes that may not interest all viewers, it still serves as one of the most talked-about series worth watching on Prime Video this weekend.

Prime Video has already released six of the eight episodes in The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power season 3, with its 2-part finale set to drop this Wednesday, November 25. The Lord of the Rings prequel is officially the highest-rated installment of the series in terms of critical acclaim, earning a Rotten Tomatoes score of 86%, a slight improvement on season 1's 84% and season 2's 85% ratings. The epic high-fantasy series is the #1 most-watched Prime Video series in the world and has already been confirmed for a fourth season.

Disney's 8-part supernatural fantasy epic returns this Friday with a highly anticipated new chapter featuring Greek gods, monsters, and an epic quest.

With just two episodes remaining in The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power, there has never been a better time to press play on the series than right now, even if you have yet to watch the first two seasons. Despite mixed reception from audiences, The Rings of Power is undeniably one of the most popular Prime Video series of the 2020s and still holds the record for the streamer's biggest premiere of all time. With its talented cast and expansive world-building, The Rings of Power is our top pick to binge on Prime Video this weekend.
