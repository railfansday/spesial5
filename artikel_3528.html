Xbox Game Pass November 2026 Lineup Announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

November 2026 additions include a new co-op adventure, a kingdom-building game, and a familiar fantasy universe

Among the biggest additions are Escape Academy 2: Back 2 School, Thronefall, and Warhammer Survivors, with all three arriving before the middle of November. Here's what to know about these games and everything else coming to Xbox Game Pass.

One of the biggest additions is Escape Academy 2: Back 2 School, the sequel to Coin Crew Games' 2022 puzzle adventure. Arriving Nov. 5, the game expands the escape room formula into an open-world experience, with online and local two-player co-op allowing friends to explore a school campus and solve puzzles together.

Another notable addition is Warhammer Survivors, an officially licensed bullet-heaven game developed by Auroch Digital in partnership with Vampire Survivors creator poncle. Launching Nov. 10, it brings Warhammer champions, enemy factions, and weapons into the survival formula, with players unleashing barrages of bullets, blades, and magic.
