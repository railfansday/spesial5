XRP printed a 7% price recovery in yesterday's trading session, which barely helps the price, considering its long-term market performance. Given the volume we are witnessing on the market, there is barely any traction out there.

The bounce started from $0.894, and at press time XRP is trading at $0.948. The recovery candle came on lower volume than the red one before it.

Since early November, XRP has lost roughly 30% of its value and given up everything it built throughout the August rally. In October, the 200 EMA acted as a strong resistance that XRP was unable to break.

The main problem here is the lack of bullish narratives on the market. Bitcoin lost almost 15% of its value in the blink of an eye, without any solid catalysts or news, apart from ones that have been around for months now: AI, the Nvidia crash, the Anthropic IPO and cybersecurity risks. On November 15, BTC touched a four-month low around $62,000.

Investors aren't buying XRP en masse either. US-listed XRP ETFs saw $19.13 million in net outflows in the week through November 13, the heaviest weekly redemption since March.

The possibility of a recovery is real, but it is minuscule. The RSI dipped below 25 before the bounce, so part of yesterday's move is simply a reaction to the oversold market. There are little to no fundamental factors that can help XRP stay above key levels for longer than a day or two.

Whether buyers can reclaim the $1 level or not is the main test for XRP this week. A short-lived price bounce here won't change the situation as a whole, unfortunately.
