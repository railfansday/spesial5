Fluidkey’s $1.6 million hack exposes a security risk even when private keys never leave users’ devices

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Sending funds requires an app update, and users who spot an unfamiliar transfer can look up their own receiving address or transaction hash on the relevant blockchain.

The company announced the return of its web, iOS, and Android apps in v1.79.22. It said older versions would stay offline and told users to open Settings and select Update to re-enable transactions.

Fluidkey attributed the incident to flaws in a third-party logging tool and said users’ private keys stayed on their devices, and said it took services offline on Nov. 19. In its update that evening, it said it had seen no further unauthorized transactions since the shutdown and was working with SEAL 911 to investigate and trace funds.

Its initial warning to avoid the site and app came before the new version was available.

Blockchain analytics firm Bitquery traced roughly $1.6 million in payouts to wallets it linked to the incident across six chains, including Base and Ethereum.

The independent Fluidkey drain tracker, maintained by pepebndc, put the total near $1.63 million across seven chains in its Nov. 20 morning snapshot. That estimate includes suspected destinations and excludes likely owner withdrawals.

Bitquery says the tracker labeled one Polygon payout as an owner withdrawal, linking it to the incident after following the recipient’s activity on Base.

The firm also separated the incident-linked payments from about $349,000 sent to wallets with an established history during its hour-long scan.

That group includes ordinary payments before the drain and about $277,000 sent during it to an older wallet across Ethereum and Base. Bitquery interpreted the latter as one user moving their own funds, and left another $65,000 unclassified.

If you have a receiving address or transaction hash, look it up in a block explorer for the relevant chain. The ethereum.org guide to block explorers explains how those records are organized.

Compare the time, amount, and destination with your own records and check the balance at your known address. The chain records the transfer, but the record alone cannot establish who authorized it.

Fluidkey’s standing recovery guide describes SARA as a last-resort option for verifying addresses or accessing funds. It warns that moving funds outside the regular app may impair app functionality.

In its incident update, Fluidkey said it would contact affected users individually and publish a full post-mortem. It warned about impersonation and said its staff would never contact users privately through social media, and directed users to its official website for support.
