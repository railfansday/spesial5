Litecoin is showing signs of recovery after a sharp November sell-off pushed its price towards the $40 support region. LTC has rebounded above $44, supported by renewed derivatives activity and continued network usage. However, the recovery faces a major technical hurdle near $47, where short-term resistance could limit further gains.

Meanwhile, ETF-related indicators remain mixed, leaving investors to assess whether the latest rebound reflects stronger buying demand or a temporary pause in the broader downtrend.

Litecoin price traded around $43.46 in the November 26 market brief, gaining 7.41% over 24 hours and bringing its market capitalization to approximately $3.38 billion. The recovery continued into November 27, with LTC near $44.72.

Derivatives activity also increased during the rebound. Futures open interest reached approximately $302.65 million, up 10.19%, while the futures-to-spot volume ratio stood at 5.76x.

Rising open interest indicates that more positions are being established in the derivatives market. However, it does not reveal whether traders are predominantly bullish or bearish. The elevated futures-to-spot ratio also highlights the influence of leveraged trading, which can amplify price movements in either direction.

Network indicators offer another positive signal. Litecoin recorded approximately 180,131 daily transactions, a hashrate of 2.49 PH/s and an average block time of 2.55 minutes. These figures point to continued network activity despite the recent price weakness.

Institutional demand could become an important factor in Litecoin’s recovery, particularly following the launch of spot ETF products. The Canary Litecoin ETF (LTCC) was quoted at $10.31, gaining approximately 4% in the November 26 market brief.

However, ETF-related indicators do not yet provide a clear confirmation of sustained accumulation. The supplied market data flags conflicting LTCC flow figures and a reported 12.61% discount to net asset value for the Litecoin investment product LTCN.

Litecoin’s daily chart shows an initial rebound from the $40 support region, but the broader technical structure remains bearish. LTC has broken below its previously rising trendline and continues to trade beneath several moving averages.

The next resistance area appears around $52â$55, where additional moving-average barriers could restrict the recovery. On the downside, $40 remains the critical support area. Litecoin recently tested this region before rebounding. A sustained breakdown below it could weaken the recovery attempt and expose LTC to further losses.

Litecoin’s next move will depend on whether buyers can reclaim $47 and sustain momentum above the short-term moving averages. Rising open interest and continued network activity offer supportive signals, but they do not yet confirm a trend reversal. A decisive break above resistance would strengthen the recovery case, while another rejection near $47 could leave LTC vulnerable to a retest of $40.
