Pin (pin.com) is an AI recruiting platform that assists recruiters by finding candidates across the 850M+ profiles Pin says it covers across North America and Europe, writing outreach from each person's profile signals and booking interviews. Candidate data comes from professional networks, GitHub, Stack Overflow, open-source contributions, patents, academic publications and the broader web. You search in plain language, paste a job description, upload ideal résumés or import a role from your ATS. Each ranked candidate has a fit score and an AI-written reason, and the model learns from your team's accept and reject feedback.

The Pin recruiting agent has two modes. In Assist you approve each step. In Autopilot it sources, screens and ranks, drafts outreach, sends the email steps, follows up on silence, schedules interviews and updates your ATS. LinkedIn and SMS steps arrive as tasks with an AI-drafted message, and you send them. Closing and offers stay human. The agent asks clarifying questions ("Is remote considered?"), shows every action in a feed and works 24/7 alongside your team. Josh Volinsky, Co-Founder of Synergy Search, says: "Pin makes sourcing candidates so natural and I love how you can just talk to it like it's a recruiter on your team and it goes hunting."

Noon (noon.ai) is an AI recruiting platform from Portal Inc. in New York whose agents source across the open web, run email and SMS outreach and book interviews. You start from a pasted job description or posting URL. Noon extracts the title, location and "non-negotiables," and its Autopilot sourcing agent keeps searching as new people enter the market. Candidates land in a ranked feed with reasoning, and thumbs-up and thumbs-down feedback calibrates the search per role.

Noon also extends further down the funnel. Its AI Interviewer runs voice screening calls, its Notetaker joins live interviews, and Inbound Screening ranks ATS applicants and flags suspected fraud. Those tools sit downstream of sourcing. Pin concentrates on the outbound workflow, from search through scheduling, and adds the team layer around it: a shared inbox, a dedupe layer so two recruiters never contact the same person, funnel dashboards and a multi-client CRM for agencies.

Pin gives you natural-language filters plus advanced filters you set yourself, and paid plans include unlimited searches.

Noon steers the search through criteria set per role. Its pages describe titles, locations, years of experience, target-company lists and must-haves, plus non-negotiables Noon says it never relaxes. You adjust by chat and feedback. We found no public description of an open-ended filter panel like Pin's, so ask for one in a demo if ad hoc searching matters.

On consistency, Pin returns a ranked shortlist with a reason for every candidate from the first search, then improves as your team accepts and rejects. Noon says its feedback loop calibrates the model per role, but it doesn't say how long that takes. Compare day one with day ten yourself (see the trial plan below).

Pin writes each message from the candidate's profile signals and calibrates to your writing voice. Its sequences run across email, LinkedIn and SMS: automated email steps plus AI-drafted LinkedIn and SMS tasks in the same sequence, which the recruiter sends. Sequences adapt per candidate, and any reply pauses the sequence. Free is email-only. Replies go to a shared team inbox, and a dedupe layer stops two recruiters contacting the same person.

Noon writes an intro for each candidate and drops it into sequence templates you control. Sequences run on email and SMS.

The practical difference is how contact data is packaged. Every Pin plan includes contact credits (5 a week on Free, 500 a month on Solo, 1,000 per user a month on Professional, unlimited on Business), and a personal email, work email or phone number uses 1 credit. Noon says contact enrichment is unlimited. Revealing email and phone for 300 candidates a month uses 600 of the 1,000 credits included with each Professional seat.

Pin's AI interview scheduling handles time zones, hiring panels and reschedules, and confirmations land in your ATS. Pin lists 120+ ATS integrations, including Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Bullhorn, iCIMS and Jobvite. ATS integration is an add-on on Solo and Professional and is included on Business. Teams without an ATS can track candidates in Pin's Kanban pipeline, a mini ATS from first touch to signed offer.

Noon's AI Coordinator answers candidate questions and books interviews through your Calendly availability, then handles reschedules. Its integrations page lists 40+ systems, including all six above plus Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, UKG, Loxo and Recruiterflow, and Noon says ATS sync is part of its plan. Several other Noon pages still say "20+," so confirm your ATS is live before you commit.

Pin publishes its pricing on its pricing page (checked October 14, 2026). Free is $0 (5 contact credits a week, 1 job, 1 agent, email-only sequences, 3 searches a day). Solo is $99 per user per month billed annually, for one seat, with 500 contact credits a month included. Professional is $135 per user per month billed annually or $179 month to month, with 2 agents and 1,000 contact credits per user each month, a shared inbox and 5 mailboxes per user. Business is custom, with unlimited seats, credits and agents and ATS integration included.

Noon sells one plan, quoted per team. Its pricing page lists unlimited sourcing, email enrichment, contacts, agents and seats, with no credits or per-seat fees. Its get-started page invites teams to create a free account, yet most "Start free" buttons open a demo-booking calendar, and Noon does not say what the free account includes.

With Noon, you won't know the number until after a sales call. Noon prices one flat plan per team and says adding seats doesn't change it, while Pin's cost is known before you talk to anyone and scales predictably per seat. Get Noon's quote in writing and compare it against these figures, keeping in mind that Noon's bundle includes screening and note-taking tools you may not need.

Noon also offers a 10x ROI guarantee on annual plans. According to Noon's guarantee terms (checked October 14, 2026), it values recruiter time at $60 an hour, relies on Noon's own measurement of time saved and requires minimum weekly use.

Pin is SOC 2 Type 2 audited by a third party, encrypts data at rest and in transit, and is GDPR and CCPA ready, with documents in its trust center at trust.pin.com. SSO/SAML and SCIM come with the Business plan.

Noon says it holds SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliance, runs an annual third-party penetration test and supports SSO and SAML. According to its security page (updated September 3, 2026), Noon also offers data residency options for enterprise agreements and makes the SOC 2 report and pen-test summary available under NDA. The page does not mention CCPA.

Pin is built for agencies scaling outbound, and for recruiters and teams of any size that want a known price and hands-on control. It brings a multi-client recruiting CRM, per-seat pricing that scales predictably and a Business plan with unlimited seats. Pin assists recruiters rather than replacing them. Noon's homepage claims 40% faster time to hire, a vendor-reported figure.

Noon is good for teams with heavy inbound application volume that want voice screening and interview notes bundled with sourcing, and that are comfortable buying through a sales quote. The caveats are an unpublished price and free-account details, and only three reviews on Trustpilot.

When Pin is not the right fit: teams that only need high-volume inbound applicant screening.

When Noon is a harder fit: buyers who need a price before a sales call, teams that want a free plan with published details, and anyone who wants independent reviews to check (three on Trustpilot, no G2 listing found).

Choose Pin if you: - want a published price and a free plan with no credit card - want to start in Assist mode and hand steps to Autopilot as you build trust - run a team where several recruiters share candidates and need dedupe and a shared inbox - run an agency and need pipelines per client

Choose Noon if you: - want one quoted plan that Noon says does not change with seats or lookups - need voice screening, a notetaker, inbound screening or fraud detection in the same product - are comfortable buying through a demo and a custom quote
