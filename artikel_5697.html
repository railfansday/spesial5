Live Webinar Nov. 17: Measles Elimination Status Revoked

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

— A pediatric vaccine expert and a global health researcher weigh in

The U.S. has just lost its measles elimination status. Beginning with the West Texas outbreak in January 2025, major outbreaks have emerged across the country. Case counts for the vaccine preventable illness have broken records with over 5,400 in 2026 so far. States including Pennsylvania, New York, and Wisconsin have recently had measles-associated deaths for the first time in three decades.

Meanwhile, federal leaders have shared misinformation about the measles, mumps, and rubella (MMR) vaccine, which is 97% effective against the measles virus, and kindergarten vaccination rates are inching down below levels associated with herd immunity.

Researchers with direct knowledge of the deliberations revealed that the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO) last week concluded that the U.S. has lost its measles elimination status, which was originally declared in 2000.

So, what's next? What would the change in status mean for future public health efforts? Did anti-vaccine sentiment play a role? A pediatric vaccine expert and a global health researcher will answer these questions and more LIVE -- join us!
