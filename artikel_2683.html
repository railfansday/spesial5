🔼 Vila Nova end 41-year wait with return to Brasileirão Série A

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

One of the longest droughts in Brazilian football is over.

And Vila Nova Futebol Clube are back in the top flight of Brazilian football.

Their last appearance was way back in 1985.

They will play in the 2027 Série A after securing promotion against rivals Atlético Goianiense on Friday night (27).

After a 1–1 draw in the first leg of the playoffs, Tigre won 2–0 in front of their home fans at the OBA.

And they went into half-time having had the best chances, with Paulo Vítor saving Dragão twice in quick succession.

The goal that set Vila on their way to promotion came from a moment of misfortune for Junior Barreto.

After the ball was knocked clear, Dudu tried his luck.

His shot was going wide, but it deflected off the Paraguayan defender.

With no other option, former centre-forward Roger sent on three attackers for Dragão.

The red-and-blacks piled on the pressure, but Vila defended resolutely.

And they got the counterattack they wanted to "kill off" the tie.

Marquinhos Gabriel got away from Cristiano and teed up João Vieira for a simple finish in the 77th minute.

Promotion rewards the team that spent the most rounds in Série B's top four.

And brings an end to an ordeal that haunted generations of Vila fans.

Champions Grêmio Novorizontino, now FURIA Novorizontino, and Juventude had already secured promotion.

The fourth team promoted to Série A will be decided this Saturday (28).

Fortaleza and Criciúma will face off at 8pm (Brasília time) at the Arena Castelão.

Tigre won 1–0 at the Heriberto Hulse and therefore only need a draw.

Laion need only a one-goal win, as their better league record means they go through if the tie finishes level on aggregate.
