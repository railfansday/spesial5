Raiders Week 12: Meet the Browns

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Las Vegas Raiders are preparing to play their 12th regular-season game of the year, the seventh on the road, at the Cleveland Browns on Sunday night. Kickoff is set for 5:20 p.m. PT.

The Browns are 6-4 and are coming off their bye week. This is the Raiders' second straight opponent coming off its bye and third overall. The Raiders don't have their bye until next week. Rest will be an issue, especially since the Raiders are traveling for the second straight week.

These two franchises, which have struggled for some time over the years, have met often. This is the fifth time this decade these two teams have meet and the third straight season. The Browns bounced the Raiders 24-10 in Las Vegas on November 23 last year. It spanned a five game Raiders' winning streak over Cleveland dating back to 2015.
