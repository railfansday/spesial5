Falcons are "aware" of suggestion that James Pearce Jr. has been trying to contact Rickea Jackson

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Falcons defensive end James Pearce Jr. has returned from an eight-game suspension arising from an incident with his former girlfriend, WNBA player Rickea Jackson. On Wednesday, coach Kevin Stefanski addressed a suggestion on social media that Pearce has been attempting to contact Jackson, in violation of his plea deal.

“I’m aware,” Stefanski told reporters, via Marc Raimondi of ESPN. “We’ve spoken to James. But we don’t have much more to add.”

It’s not clear that Pearce has been trying to contact Jackson. Her online comments carry a clear implication that she believes “dozens” of calls from a phone number with no ID came from Pearce.

Raimondi also notes that, since his return from suspension, Pearce has not spoken to reporters, declining multiple attempts by the media to question him. League rules require all players to be available after every game and once during the week.

Pearce faces potentially significant consequences if it can be proven that he has violated the terms of his one-year diversion program. Beyond the possibility of an additional suspension, Pearce could face a prison term for the charges that will be dismissed only if he fully complies with the program.
