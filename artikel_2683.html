Joe Burrow matches Peyton Manning's record for consecutive games with at least four TDs

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Joe Burrow has accomplished something that had not been done for over 20 years.

With his 4-yard touchdown to Chase Brown in the third quarter, Burrow became the first since Peyton Manning in 2004 to have at least four total touchdowns in five consecutive games, which is the longest streak in NFL history.

That score gave the Bengals a commanding 31-3 lead with 4:12 left in the third period.

Burrow has been excellent all season, but that's particularly been the case on Sunday night. So far, he's completed 32-of-41 passes for 359 yards with four TDs.

The one difference between Manning's streak and Burrow's is that Manning did it with all passing touchdowns. Between Weeks 8 and 12 in 2004, Manning threw for five, four, five, four, and six TDs with the Colts.

Manning would win his second MVP award for his 2004 season.

Burrow has thrown for four, four, five, three, and now four in his last six games. He's also had a rushing touchdown in each of the last two weeks before Sunday's matchup against Pittsburgh.
