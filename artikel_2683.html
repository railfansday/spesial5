"Honestly, just let me play football!" – Mohya responds to Bayern links

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Having established himself as a key player for Borussia Mönchengladbach at just 17, Wael Mohya has attracted interest from several top clubs, with Bayern Munich among those keeping a close eye on his development.

The German champions' board member for sport, Max Eberl, recently confirmed that the club are monitoring the youngster, who was asked about Bayern's interest following his first senior Germany call-up .

“If there really is interest, it is a nice recognition of my performances – but nothing more than that. Honestly, just let me play football. That's also what I've agreed with Gladbach," Mohya told Bild.

“I want to keep developing and do everything I can to help us avoid relegation. I think everyone knows that I have a very close and emotional connection with Borussia.”

Bayer Leverkusen are reportedly showing concrete interest in signing Mohya next summer. However, Gladbach are said to be hoping to tie the teenager down to a new contract beyond 2028 in a bid to fend off interest in their prized asset.
