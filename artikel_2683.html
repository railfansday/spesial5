Aidan Hutchinson shares thoughts on injury: ‘I always come back stronger’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Detroit Lions All-Pro defensive end Aidan Hutchinson took to social media to deliver a message to fans for the first time since tearing his ACL in Week 10 against the New England Patriots in Germany. Hutchinson's injury occurred moments after a drive-ending sack of QB Drake Maye, as the star edge rusher leaped in celebration and his knee buckled on landing. The perennial Defensive Player of the Year candidate will require surgery, and his 2026 season is unfortunately over.

Here's the message Hutchinson wanted people to see:

I honestly can't believe it. I've jumped hundreds of times on a football field and never in a million years did I think this would happen to me.

But even something like this won't hold me back.

I can ask why over and over again, but it'll never make sense. All I can do is surrender to the path. Only God knows why and I trust in Him.

Very grateful for all of your prayers and well wishes.

Two things I know for sure:1. I always come back stronger.2. That's the last time I go airborne 😁👍

Hutchinson is no stranger to recovering from season-ending injuries. In 2024, he broke his leg and spent several "miserable" days in a Dallas hospital, and gained a new perspective on life. Early signs for Hutchinson are that he didn't suffer any other significant knee damage, but they can't be certain until the surgery takes place. If it goes well, he's expected to have a chance to recover and return to play sometime in 2027, hopefully "stronger," as he did after his 2024 injury.
