Aidan Hutchinson injured celebrating after sacking Drake Maye in Germany

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson was injured celebrating after sacking Drake Maye on third-and-goal at the end of the Patriots’ first possession early Sunday in Munich, Germany.

Maye appeared to be banged up on the sack as well, but Hutchinson got up, jumped in the air celebrating, and then immediately seemed to feel something wrong as he landed. He fell to the ground and grabbed at his right lower leg.

Hutchinson was able to walk off the field with a limp. After getting examined on the sideline for a few minutes, he walked to the locker room with the Lions’ medical staff. There was no immediate word on the nature of his injury.

The Patriots kicked a field goal to take a 3-0 lead.
