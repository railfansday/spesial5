Rob Gronkowski, Adrian Peterson, Ben Roethlisberger among players advancing in Hall of Fame voting process

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Pro Football Hall of Fame announced the names of 52 players who have advanced to the next round of voting for possible enshrinement as part of the Class of 2027.

The group includes five players who are in their first year of eligibility. They are tight end Rob Gronkowski, running back Adrian Peterson, quarterback Ben Roethlisberger, wide receiver Antonio Brown, and cornerback Richard Sherman.

After changes to the selection process this year, there are no longer Modern and Senior candidates in the player pool. The group of 52 players will be cut to 25 in the next round of voting and then 13 finalists will be selected from that group. They will join Bill Belichick and Robert Kraft in the final pool of eligible candidates with the selection committee selecting a group of between three and seven members.

The full list of players who have advanced to the next round of voting appearsa below.

OFFENSIVE LINEMEN: Willie Anderson, Lomas Brown, Jahri Evans, Joe Jacoby, Nick Mangold, Logan Mankins, Maurkice Pouncey, Richmond Webb, Steve Wisniewski, Marshal Yanda.

QUARTERBACKS: Ken Anderson, Eli Manning, Ben Roethlisberger.

RUNNING BACKS: Shaun Alexander, Frank Gore, Marshawn Lynch, LeSean McCoy, Adrian Peterson, Fred Taylor, Ricky Watters.

WIDE RECEIVERS/TIGHT ENDS: Anquan Boldin, Antonio Brown, Rob Gronkowski, Torry Holt, Billy “White Shoes” Johnson, Steve Smith Sr., Otis Taylor, Hines Ward, Reggie Wayne, Jason Witten.

DEFENSIVE BACKS: Rodney Harrison, Lester Hayes, Albert Lewis, Eddie Meador, Richard Sherman, Earl Thomas III, Everson Walls, Darren Woodson.

DEFENSIVE LINEMEN: John Abraham, Geno Atkins, L.C. Greenwood, Jim Marshall, Robert Mathis, Haloti Ngata, Vince Wilfork, Kevin Williams.

LINEBACKERS: Maxie Baughan, London Fletcher, James Harrison, Karl Mecklenburg, Tommy Nobis, Terrell Suggs.
