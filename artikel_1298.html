Man Utd Eyeing Alex Freeman as Mauricio Pochettino's First Signing

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Mauricio Pochettino's arrival as new Manchester United manager looks set to help influence the club's transfer business towards his former players with the USMNT.

The Argentine boss has been confirmed as the new man in the hot seat after Michael Carrick's era in permanent charge rapidly fell apart this season, and is tasked with lifting the club out of the bottom three in the Premier League after the international break.

The squad has proven itself to be severely lacking so far this term, with the spotlight turned on Director of Football Jason Wilcox over his summer recruitment by Lisandro Martinez among others.

With the January transfer window already within reach there could now be an American influx thanks to Pochettino's two years in charge of the US squad, with help at full-back belatedly on the agenda.

Wilcox's focus during the summer was heavily skewed in favour of the central midfield rebuild, which has so far delivered below-par results after a hefty spend of over £150 million on Youri Tielemans, Andrey Santos, and Carlos Baleba.

Many fans had expected Carrick to be given reinforcements at one or both of the full-back positions as well, with Lewis Hall heavily linked before he signed a new deal at Newcastle.

Instead, injuries forced the former boss to improvise as form collapsed in recent weeks, with Diogo Dalot preferred out of position on the left, and the likes of Leny Yoro pushed out onto the right.

Portuguese right-back Dalot has come under major fire for his form during the recent run of dreadful results, and according to TEAMtalk the club have their eye on a new addition at his preferred position.

Villarreal's Alex Freeman is believed to be in United's sights just over six weeks from the window opening, and with Pochettino expected to be allowed a significant say in transfer business moving forward his arrival could now heavily influence a reunion with the 22-year-old.

The son of former Green Bay Packers Super Bowl winner Antonio Freeman, Alex was given his international debut by Pochettino in 2025 and subsequently established himself as a regular starter.

He has since won 26 caps and scored at their home World Cup in the summer, so should have a positive relationship with his former international coach.

Former Orlando City player Freeman looks to be in the ascendancy after heading to La Liga midway through last season, and has Champions League experience with Villarreal.

A versatile player who can be deployed further forward, and could also fill in on the left if Luke Shaw's fitness issues persist, he has made 16 appearances in all competitions this season.

If he does head to Old Trafford in January, he will be faced with helping Pochettino with the huge challenge of turning around United's sinking ship and putting an end to a damaging cycle of managerial turnover.
