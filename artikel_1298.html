Better Than Emery: Man Utd Must Hire Elite 57-Year-Old Manager, He Wants the Job

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Manchester United chiefs have reportedly been dealt a 'blow' with the news that Aston Villa manager Unai Emery is close to signing a new contract with the Midlands outfit.

Man United decision-makers have been speaking in recent weeks about what they will do next, following a poor start to the campaign for the Red Devils.

Michael Carrick looked set for the sack following successive defeats by a three-goal margin against Como and Bournemouth, but the club decided to stick with him and give him the opportunity to turn things around.

The 45-year-old then lost his next game in the Premier League against Chelsea at Stamford Bridge, which left the club in 17th position and only outside the relegation zone on goal difference.

Carrick was given the Champions League clash against AS Roma too, but it looked as though he was heading for the sack with the club trailing 2-0 at half-time at Old Trafford.

However, the hosts produced a remarkable second half turnaround to win 3-2 against the Serie A giants, to give Carrick a reprieve ahead of hosting Villa at the Theatre of Dreams on Sunday.

Ironically, it has been widely reported that Emery is United's 'dream' appointment if they do decide to make a change in the coming weeks, with the Spaniard having his internal admirers, alongside the likes of Eddie Howe, Julian Nagelsmann and Antonio Conte, according to GIVEMESPORT sources.

However, the Telegraph's John Percy reported on Wednesday that the Spaniard is now in advanced talks to extend his stay at Villa Park, which is a blow for United.

Now, Emery could potentially end Carrick's managerial reign if he wins at Old Trafford this weekend, but Red Devils chiefs will have to look elsewhere if that happens, and they decide to axe the man they appointed on a permanent basis in the summer.

They do have an elite option available to them in Conte, who is open to replacing Carrick in mid-season. The Italian is increasingly the obvious choice if a change is to be made.

United may not be able to appoint Emery, but they could land a manager even better than the former Arsenal boss in Conte.

Emery may have won the Europa League on five separate occasions, but he only has one league title to his name.

There is no doubting the incredible job he has done at Villa Park to get them competing at the highest level in the Premier League and in Europe's elite club competition, but he has also been given huge influence over every aspect of the club.

That is something that he would be unlikely to get at Old Trafford, and you only have to look at Conte's record to see why he would be a great appointment for United.

The 57-year-old has managed some of the biggest clubs in Europe, including Juventus, Inter Milan, Chelsea and Napoli, winning league titles at each.

The Italian did an impressive job at Napoli in his previous job, taking a team that finished 10th in Serie A to the Scudetto in his first season in charge.

Conte is seen as a defensively organised manager, but when he leaves clubs he usually goes with a big positive goal difference in tow, including at Tottenham Hotspur.

He may have been sacked by Spurs in 2023, but winning 41 of his 76 matches in charge doesn't look too bad considering how bad the north London outfit have generally been in the last few years.

Conte would immediately inject urgency and energy into a group who are underperforming right now, and he has proven before that he can go into a club and instantly improve their fortunes.

That is exactly what the Red Devils are crying out for, and the fact he is available now and wouldn't cost them anything in terms of compensation is just an added bonus.

Manchester United secured a 3-2 victory over Roma in the Champions League on Tuesday night.
