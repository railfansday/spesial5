Report Estupinan offered to Fiorentina after Milan flop

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Pervis Estupinan has received a great deal of criticism at Milan after the move from Brighton, so there are reports his agent has proposed a potential loan to Fiorentina in January instead.

The Ecuador international was a big investment at €18m from Brighton and Hove Albion in the summer of 2025, but he never quite managed to deliver on that promise.

There were already calls for him to be sold this season to Aston Villa, only for coach Ruben Amorim to publicly insist he was the one who wanted to keep the player.

According to Sport Mediaset and Firenzeviola, even Amorim has now conceded that Estupinan is out of opportunities at San Siro, and ought to look elsewhere.

It is reported that his agent Jorge Mendes has contacted Fiorentina director Fabio Paratici to suggest a possible loan deal in the January transfer window.

This would also include an option to buy at the end of the season, although it is unlikely the Rossoneri will make back the money spent to sign him.

Estupinan has made 10 appearances for Milan so far this season between Serie A and the Europa League, but only started one of the last five league games.
