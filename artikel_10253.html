All three northeast Indiana counties reject casino, leaving HEA 1038 license with no host

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Voters in Allen, DeKalb and Steuben counties each rejected inland casino gambling by double digits on November 3. The single new license in House Enrolled Act 1038, tied to a project of at least $500 million, now has no county that can host it.
