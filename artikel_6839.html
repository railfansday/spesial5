Gray Jeans Are the Coolest Denim Staple You’re Not Wearing Enough

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

From soft pink knits to leather jackets, these outfits make gray jeans feel fresh.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Somewhere between the elevated black jean and the trusty blue classic lies underrated gray denim. What we love about this misty shade is that it looks polished without trying too hard and relaxed without looking sloppy. It sidesteps the heavy contrast of black and the weekend-casual shadow of classic indigo, landing in a sweet spot that works for brunch, the office on a dress-down Friday, and everything in between.

So you see, it’s much more wearable than it gets credit for. For a little inspiration, we’ve rounded up a few of our favorite looks featuring gray jeans in a range of wide, straight, and barrel styles.

Whether you’re hunting for fresh ways to wear a pair you already own or considering your first, here’s how to make gray jeans complement your wardrobe.

Gray and pink is a sweet color combo that’s highly underrated, if you ask us. Pairing wide-leg drawstring jeans with a fuzzy pink sweater and silver metallic boots is the perfect way to add a little softness and shine to your everyday denim routine.
