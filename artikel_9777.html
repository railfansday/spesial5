Apple rejected our Flutter app update for one sentence in the paywall fine print

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Small one, but it cost us a full review cycle, so maybe it saves someone else a week.

Full disclosure: we make SENEKA, a game of everyday dilemmas, and this is about our own app. We build it in Flutter and ship one codebase to Google Play and the App Store. An iOS update came back from App Review with three issues. None of them was a crash.

Our subscription fine print under the paywall said:

Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account at confirmation.

One shared string for both platforms. It's accurate, and Apple doesn't care. An iOS binary can't mention another store, even in legal small print.

The fix is boring: a separate iOS string, picked at runtime.

The annoying part was doing it in all 16 languages we ship. Before you submit, grep your localisation files:

Every hit that can reach an iOS screen is a possible 2.3.10.

The screenshot attached to the rejection showed Apple's own system alert, "Account Not In This Store". The reviewer device was on the Singapore storefront and the sandbox account was from the US. Nothing in our code was involved.

If your app is available in many storefronts, it's worth saying in the review notes that purchases were tested with a sandbox account of the same storefront as the device.

Our paywall only shows up in a few places: a locked pack, the end of a free journey, and settings. "It's in settings" wasn't enough. They want exact steps:

Write it like the reviewer has never seen your app, because they haven't.

While re-testing the paywall we noticed the yearly plan's "per month" price didn't always match the store's own price formatting (currency symbol position, decimals). We were using a ready-made per-month string from our purchases SDK. Now we divide the yearly price by 12 ourselves and write it in the same format as the store's own yearly price string, with a small unit test so it can't drift again. Small, but a paywall that shows two price formats side by side looks broken.

The update is resubmitted and waiting for review. If you want to see what the app is: one everyday dilemma a day, two defensible answers, and you can compare your choices with a partner or friends, who can answer in the browser without installing anything. Try it in the browser or get the Android app.
