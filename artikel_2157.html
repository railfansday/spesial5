Max Dowman’s Arsenal return: Is the teenage star one to watch, and can he keep his nose clean?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Lucie Turner writes about Arsenal for Read Arsenal and Newcastle United for Read Newcastle. A long-time Arsenal supporter, she also follows rugby, snooker, darts and Formula 1. Her work covers match previews and reports, breaking news, player profiles and opinion pieces.

Max Dowman shortly returns to action for Arsenal after serving a three-game suspension.

His absence has been disappointing, but the experience should give the youngster an important lesson. Can he put the incident behind him and show Mikel Arteta that he can be trusted?

Dowman received a red card against Fleetwood Town after kicking out at Harrison Neal. Arsenal appealed the decision, but the FA rejected the club’s appeal.

The suspension ruled him out of three domestic fixtures against Liverpool, Hull City and Newcastle United.

He’ll be back on 29 November as Arsenal take on Manchester City. This should give the young star a chance to get his season back on track and showcase what makes him such a highly-rated player.

Dowman looks very promising for the future. He has shown great adaptability playing with the more experienced players and has the self-belief to make an impact in games.

He has performed well in the Carabao Cup. In the 4-2 win against Ipswich, Dowman scored two goals, showing his flair and poise in front of goal.

Arteta has given him opportunities to play in the Premier League and Champions League, meaning that he appears to have a clear route into the first team.

Although Dowman still has a lot to learn, he displays great potential in his technical ability and combative nature. As he gets more games under his belt, fans will be excited to see how he progresses.

Dowman was sent off in an unfortunate moment. He was heavily challenged by Neal and, after lashing out, was shown a straight red card.

Although this one moment should not be enough to dismiss a young player from the game completely, it can serve as a valuable lesson on the pressure that comes with playing at a higher level.

Opponents will test Dowman physically, but he must learn to keep his composure. As he starts making an impact, some players may target his age and inexperience to unsettle him.

As he gets more game time, consistency will be key. Arteta must be confident that Dowman can be relied on in high pressure situations.

Arsenal do not need to rush Dowman into the first team. The squad’s quality allows Arteta to manage his minutes and give him time to develop without putting too much pressure on him.

If Dowman is to play against Manchester City, it will depend on Arteta’s plans, but he has certainly done enough to warrant consideration.

The suspension should not define his season. What matters now is how he responds, both on and off the pitch.

Dowman has the talent to become an important player for Arsenal. Keeping his discipline will be just as important as displaying his ability.
