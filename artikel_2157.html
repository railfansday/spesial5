QPR backed to seal 'ideal' Fulham transfer for star with 131 PL apps

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

QPR have had a brilliant start to the season, and the prospect of finishing in a play-off spot for the first time since their relegation to the Championship way back in 2015 seems a lot more attainable as the year goes on.

Julien Stephan's side currently occupy the final position in the revamped play-offs and are playing some of the best football in the second tier, per the underlying numbers. They have the fourth-best xG difference in the league, and only Middlesbrough and Wolves have a better xGA.

A lot of the players who QPR signed in the past few seasons, either from League One or as youngsters from Premier League clubs and abroad, are starting to blossom into very good top-end Championship players, and it's being reflected in their results.

There's apt depth everywhere in the squad, perhaps other than in central midfield. Ball-playing centre-back Ronnie Edwards, who has played in the engine room before at Peterborough United, has been called in to deputise at times, either due to Nicolas Madsen's injury or a lack of form for Jonathan Varane.

Glen Kamara was QPR's marquee midfield signing this past summer, with the former play-off finalist at Leeds United joining on loan to form an engine room core with Madsen and Varane.

However, the Hoops' unlucky history with injuries in recent times caught up with Madsen, who should be back shortly. But it did leave Julien Stephan light in those areas and, as mentioned, Edwards was called in to deputise as Kamara worked his way into the side, coming in instead of Varane.

Therefore, FLW's QPR fan pundit, Louis Moir, wants to see his side bolster their midfield options in case injury strikes again, and would like to see Fulham's Harrison Reed arrive on loan.

"One player I'd like to see join, and I'm not sure it's even that ambitious, is Harrison Reed. I've always liked him as a player across the years; he's really tidy, neat, does the hard work and has shown his ability in the Premier League," he said.

"I think with this season, Madsen has been out a bit, Kamara has obviously come in and has been class, but Varane hasn't been at his best, so we're kind of lacking in midfield.

"You never want a serious injury to occur to someone like Kamara, for example, and Edwards has come in and done a great job in midfield, but I just feel we could do with one more, and that's mainly due to Varane not being at his best. I think it [the midfield] can be a weak point if Madsen or Kamara are out."

Varane was one of QPR's best performers pre-Stephan, to the point where the Hoops were reportedly rejecting £4 million offers from La Liga side Espanyol for his services. There is a player there, but that hasn't been seen this season, especially. With Reed in the squad, the R's could maintain this early-season play-off form if injuries do continue to haunt them.

Reed is well-respected at Fulham and has been a great servant to the club in the seven years he's been playing at Craven Cottage. However, the club is starting to outgrow the 31-year-old.

He made just seven league appearances last season, remaining unused from the bench on 22 separate occasions. Funnily enough, one of those cameo bench appearances saw him score the Premier League's Goal of the Season in a 2-2 draw with Liverpool in January.

This season, under Alvaro Arbeloa, Reed's role in the squad has decreased even further. Now, the midfielder isn't getting into matchday squads in the Premier League, and all his appearances so far have been in the EFL Cup.

"I think Reed may be of interest to a few Championship clubs," says Moir, due to his standing in the Fulham fold. "But him on loan would be more than ideal and a really top signing.

"I think it's more than realistic, more than ambitious, and would fit in really well. He'd add a lot of bite to the midfield and more depth. You wouldn't have to worry about him starting each week, but he'd come on and do a good job."

Reed's long-term future at Fulham is up in the air. The 31-year-old's four-year deal, which he signed back in 2023, is up at the end of the campaign.

There is an option for the Cottagers to extend that by a year, but the likelihood of that happening seems quite slim, especially if Arbeloa's side remain in the Premier League this season.

So, Reed could be in a situation where a loan move in January could almost be a way for a loaning team to have a six-month trial before a decision is made about handing him a longer-term contract.

If QPR remain a Championship side, this could definitely be a decision that they make. Reed has 131 Premier League appearances and has won promotion from the Championship twice with Fulham. At 32, he could be the perfect veteran option in the middle for a side that were on the cusp of promotion and want to go again in 2027/28.

Obviously, the Hoops will want to go up this year, and Reed could help aid in that, too, but thinking longer term, getting the 31-year-old through the door could be quite an underrated move for this R's side.
