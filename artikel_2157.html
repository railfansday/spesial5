Tom Brady issues farewell message to ‘special’ Birmingham City after unceremonious exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Birmingham City announced on Monday that Tom Brady‘s involvement with the club had come to an end. He arrived as part of Knighthead’s investment group upon the takeover in 2023 and featured in the advisory board at BCFC.

However, he was not heavily involved in football decisions and, barring the Amazon documentary ‘Built in Birmingham: Brady & The Blues’, he wasn’t a particularly prevalent figure at St Andrew’s and attended only four games in total. It meant that, despite his public profile, a brief social media statement made for quite an unceremonious exit.

Now, Brady has issued his goodbyes on his Instagram story. He took the chance to thank all those involved in his time at a ‘special’ Birmingham City, stating he’ll be rooting for the club as their journey continues after his departure.

Writing on his Instagram story, Brady said:

“Being part of Birmingham City Football Club the last three years has been one of the most rewarding journeys of my life.

“Thank you to the players, Chris Davies and his staff, the front office, and to everyone working behind the scenes, whose commitment I saw up close at every level of the club.

“Most of all, thank you to the people of Birmingham. Your passion, your loyalty, and the way you show up for this club through every high and every hard day are what make it so special. I’ll always be rooting for your success.”

The message shows Brady’s sentiment towards the club and appreciation of the supporters’ backing throughout his three years. However, the fact his close associate and friend Tom Wagner was not mentioned raises some eyebrows. Perhaps it was just an inadvertent omission, but it still comes as a surprise.

Tom Brady is a recognised name around the world, so his involvement with Birmingham City quickly brought attention. His prevalence in their Amazon documentary perhaps indicated his influence at St. Andrew’s would be more significant.

The former American football star’s profile and the attention he brought certainly plays a part in his legacy. Brady’s presence no doubt brought more eyes from a wider audience to BCFC, aiding from a commercial perspective.

Brady’s friend and body coach Alex Guerrero joined an overhauled medical department, which has decreased injuries. This and his commercial value make up his legacy, but the rather abrupt exit perhaps shows that his lasting mark at Birmingham City isn’t particularly significant – especially in comparison to the likes of Wagner and Davies.
