Blackburn Rovers should look to sign Coventry City outcast after Ryan Alebiosu blow

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Blackburn Rovers weren't handed the news they wanted to hear over the weekend regarding Ryan Alebisou's scan following a knee injury picked up against Stoke City, and they should look to the Premier League loan market to replace him.

The 24-year-old has been one of the more impressive attack-minded right-backs in the Championship over the past couple of seasons, picking up six goal involvements last season and starting brightly under Tony Mowbray this year.

He'd started every league game for Rovers this season, and other than their 3-1 win over Millwall in September, had seen out 90 minutes in each, too. But when he was forced off against Stoke just shy of half-time, fans feared the worst.

Mowbray revealed that Alebiosu will be out for three to four months with a knee injury, meaning that, in a worst-case scenario, he won't be back available until mid-March and may only be 100% for the final month or so of the campaign.

Blackburn do have sufficient depth at right-back, but the question stands whether it has the quality to maintain the performance levels without Alebisou in the squad.

20-year-old Gady Beyuku was handed his first league start in the win over West Ham United last weekend in Alebisou's absence. He was one of just four new arrivals at Ewood Park this summer, signing from Italian second-division side Modena.

Rovers also have Lewis Miller close to a return from an Achilles injury he picked up back in February. The 26-year-old's initial diagnosis was nine months, so if he's on track, he shouldn't be far away from playing again.

The Australian international was bought as a right-back from Hibernian last summer, but Alebiosu's presence meant he was deployed mainly as a centre-back in a back three under Valerien Ismael. Perhaps he could get an extended run at right-back?

But overall, Rovers need someone they know can provide quality both going forward and in defence, and they should look at someone unfavoured in the Premier League to provide that.

Kaine Kesler-Hayden surely has to be looking for a fresh start this upcoming January, after a really difficult 18 months at Coventry City.

The 24-year-old swept the end-of-season awards at Preston North End during his loan spell from Aston Villa during the 2024/25 campaign, winning Player of the Year, Players' Player of the Year and Young Player of the Year.

That season earned him a permanent move to the previous year's play-off semi-finalists, Coventry, who signed him for £3.5 million. With Milan van Ewijk reportedly on his way out to Bundesliga side Wolfsburg, it seemed a clear succession plan at the right-back position.

The Dutchman stayed, though, and Kesler-Hayden has been a very solid backup option since, but is deserving of more minutes. He's started just five league games in a Coventry shirt, playing just 588 minutes. Three of those minutes have come in a single cameo appearance off the bench in the Premier League.

The 24-year-old needs a change of scenery, and if the Sky Blues want to keep him under contract at the CBS Arena, loaning him out in January could be a good option, and Blackburn should be right at the front of the queue of suitors.

Kesler-Hayden didn't just win all of Preston's Player of the Year awards in 2024/25 on a technicality because he played for a side that only just stayed up on the final day; he more than deserved them and was a bright spark in an otherwise underwhelming squad in league action.

Per FotMob, the then-22-year-old landed in the top 5% of Championship right-backs for the number of duels he won, and no player in his position recorded more recoveries.

Then, going forward, he ranked in the top 4% of right-backs for the number of fouls he won, the number of successful dribbles he completed and the number of touches he recorded in the opposition's box.

He registered two goals and five assists, taking close to one shot per game, despite his position. For a Tony Mowbray side who prioritises attacking football, he'd be a perfect signing and would help maintain, or even raise, the levels of the squad while Alebiosu remains sidelined.

Blackburn and Preston aren't exactly the most bitter of rivals, but when Burnley or Blackpool aren't in the division, their matchup is treated as a big Lancashire derby.

There arguably hasn't been a loan player that PNE fans have fallen in love with more, just to see them leave after their spell and not come back, in recent times than Kesler-Hayden, so if Blackburn were to secure his signature and bring out some top performances, they may start to feel some envy.

North End could more than do with someone of the 24-year-old's quality in their side as they remain stuck at the bottom of the Championship table, whereas a strong January window could lead Blackburn to think about gatecrashing a top-eight party if they can stay within touching distance in the meantime.

If Kesler-Hayden can help raise Rovers' floor and make them a better team, not only could Blackburn profit in the short term, but they could get some very small bragging rights over a rival, who are struggling down where many predicted Tony Mowbray's side to be this season.
