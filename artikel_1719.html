Chelsea are set to welcome back a key man ahead of their trip to Nottingham Forest on Friday.

The Blues open up matchday 12 of the Premier League season at the City Ground, looking to bounce back from their 4-1 defeat by Leeds last time out.

Pressure has piled on manager Xabi Alonso after just one win in their last five matches, and they lost one of their star midfielders just 20 minutes into the encounter.
