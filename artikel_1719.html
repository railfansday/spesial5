Transfer news LIVE: Arsenal get Kroupi update; new Man Utd target; Scott to Chelsea twist; Liverpool latest

With the January transfer window approaching, Arsenal, Chelsea, Manchester United, Liverpool and Tottenham are among the clubs expected to pursue major signings. Jamal Musiala has emerged as a surprise name for the Gunners, along with Eli Junior Kroupi who is also attracting interest from Paris Saint-Germain. Tornike Kvaratskhelia, the younger brother of Khvicha, is another player on their radar.

Manchester United could pursue Ivan Toney as Mauricio Pochettino reportedly makes a striker his key aim for the window. Defence will also need to be strengthened and, with Fulham’s Calvin Bassey in talks over a contract extension, Nathan Collins is the next name on Pochettino’s wishlist. Chelsea are reportedly considering Alex Scott and Lamine Camara to address midfield concerns, though the former could cost in the region of £100million.

Liverpool are monitoring Real Madrid forward Endrick and are finalising a new deal for 18-year-old winger Rio Ngumoha. Tottenham have agreed a new contract with highly-rated teenager Luca Williams-Barnett. Follow all the latest transfer news, rumours and gossip live below!

Liverpool have successfully negotiated the option to sign Senegal wonderkid Sidy Barhama Ndiaye once he turns 18.

Ndiaye, currently 16, trialled with Liverpool’s academy in the summer of 2025 and now has a clear pathway to Merseyside.

Per reports from The Athletic, the Reds have pipped a number of European rivals to get right of first refusal for his signing in January 2028.

English clubs cannot sign players from overseas until they have turned 18, which Ndiaye will do on New Year’s Eve in 2027.

Keane Lewis-Potter has committed his future to Brentford by signing a new six-year deal.

He will now remain in west London until 2032, with the club holding an option for a further year.

“Keane is someone who is constantly growing. He's become part of the fabric of the club over recent years, from a very young man coming from Hull to establishing himself in the group,” explained head coach Keith Andrews.

“Over the period I've been here, we've seen real progression in his game and his versatility in terms of where he plays it. His consistency has also been very good.”

Lewis-Potter has four goals and one assist to his name in 11 Premier League appearances this season.

Chelsea should sign Virgil van Dijk as a free agent if he decides against extending his Liverpool contract, Jamie Redknapp believes.

Xabi Alonso already has a lengthy to-do list as he looks to get Chelsea on song, but patching a leaky back line is a top priority. They have already shipped 20 goals in the league this season and have only kept two league clean sheets in their last 27 matches.

A fine market opportunity could arise in the summer as they look to bolster their ranks, though, as Liverpool captain Van Dijk could become a free agent.

For Redknapp, Chelsea would be foolish not to make a move for Van Dijk, who he believes could emulate Thiago Silva’s impact on the club.

Manchester United fans will be dismayed by reports that Mauricio Pochettino will have just £50million to improve his squad during the January transfer window.

A leaky defence has led to calls from ex-players such as Gary Neville for significant investment, but it appears Man Utd will try to do their business on a tight budget in January.

The second window of the season often leads to inflated prices, with clubs capitalising upon other teams’ struggles and desperate need for new players.

The defence is the priority, with Man Utd reportedly keen on finding a new left-back and centre-back, while Pochettino also wants a new striker.

Richarlison is open to moving to Saudi Arabia to end his Tottenham exile.

The striker has been frozen out at Spurs by manager Roberto De Zerbi after failing to agree a summer exit.

The 29-year-old has a contract running until the end of the season, which means the January window is Spurs’ final opportunity to secure a transfer fee.

Recent reports have linked Richarlison with Saudi Pro League side Al-Ahli, which has seen his agent, Fidel Carvalho Soares, play down claims of negotiations but talk up the chance of a move to the Middle East.

"What is being said about negotiations between Al-Ahli and the player are just rumours," he told Al-Maidan.

"However, Richarlison would welcome the opportunity to play in the Saudi league soon.”

Arsenal are keeping tabs on one of Paraguay’s brightest young prospects, with Eduardo Delmas attracting attention after an impressive breakthrough season at Olimpia.

The 19-year-old attacking midfielder is reportedly being monitored by several European heavyweights, including Real Madrid and Paris Saint-Germain.

According to Radio Nanduti, an Arsenal scout watched Delmas during Olimpia’s 2-0 victory over Cerro Porteno on November 1, when the youngster scored a superb solo goal.

The left-footed playmaker remains under contract until 2029, and his family would reportedly like him to play in next year’s Copa Libertadores before considering a move to Europe.

Manchester United have added Brentford defender Nathan Collins to their January wishlist as they look to strengthen their defence.

According to talkSPORT, Collins is high on United’s shortlist as Mauricio Pochettino looks to address defensive concerns at Old Trafford.

However, the report indicates that the interest remains at the target-identification stage, with no indication yet of a formal bid or negotiations with Brentford.

Bournemouth have no intention of selling highly-rated Eli Junior Kroupi in January and have placed a £100million valuation on the forward amid interest from several major clubs.

Arsenal, Barcelona and Paris Saint-Germain are among those monitoring the 20-year-old, with PSG particularly keen to strengthen their options out wide.

TalkSPORT report that Bournemouth are determined to keep Kroupi until at least the summer, although the France international is reportedly keen on joining PSG when he eventually leaves the south coast.

Kroupi, who is contracted until 2030, has scored 13 goals in 37 Premier League appearances for the Cherries.

Tottenham and Chelsea are both keen to sign Joao Felix in January, per reports from FourFourTwo.

The Portuguese playmaker has already enjoyed two stints with Chelsea - one on loan, and one permanent - before leaving under a cloud.

Now at Al Nassr, the Blues and Spurs are reportedly interested in bringing him back to the Premier League, with Barcelona also sniffing around.

Wesley Fofana has revealed he almost quit football during Enzo Maresca’s time in charge of Chelsea.

The French centre-back says he considered retiring early due to the psychological strain that came with a rash of injuries.

Fofana, 25, did continue playing and is starting regularly this season for Chelsea, but has been much criticised in recent weeks for his performances under Xabi Alonso, including in Saturday’s 4-1 home defeat to Leeds.

“There was a period when I was recovering from my cruciate ligament and meniscus injuries,” Fofana said.

“Then I suffered two consecutive hamstring strains under Enzo Maresca, when I really decided: ‘That’s it, I’m retiring, I’m done playing.’ I was serious.”
