Is GTA 6 Too Easy? Rockstar Weighs In

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here, and one of the things people are talking about is the game's difficulty level. Some are saying it's too easy. Rockstar boss Rob Nelson has now entered the conversation, telling Destructoid that Rockstar's data showed that GTA 4 was "a little too punishing in places" and GTA 5 was "perhaps too easy." So with GTA 6, the team trying to "land somewhere in the middle."

GTA 6 has no defined difficulty levels, which is in keeping with the franchise's tradition. That said, players can adjust things like aiming controls, while players can unlock cheat codes to make the game easier or harder.

Design producer Scott Mitchell, meanwhile, said the fail states in GTA 6 were designed to feel "appropriate."

“We wanted to keep things open and not limit the players’ use of mechanics, such as stealth, but we did also want to make sure we retained the cinematic, high production value, explosive sequences that we’re known for, and people expect. It feels like we’ve managed to strike a good balance," he explained.

Also in the interview, Nelson said figuring out the difficulty level for GTA 6 is itself challenging because the designers themselves know enemy positions better than anyone after playing and testing the game so much.

"There’s a real process in unpicking some of that to create an experience most players will be able to enjoy, rather than endure," Nelson said. "The game is so big, and has such a wide range of content that we can really lean into that, making certain open-world content--compounds and fronts for example---more difficult, to give players seeking a challenge something to sink their teeth into as well.”

GTA 6 launched on November 19 and had a gigantic debut, selling 32.5 million copies by launch and generating around $4 billion on day one.
