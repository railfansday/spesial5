How to watch Fenerbahce vs Liverpool: TV channel and live stream for Champions League today

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Liverpool will be reunited with a familiar face for this evening’s Champions League clash against Fenerbahce.

After Ismail Kartal resigned from his Fenerbahce post last week, Dirk Kuyt has been installed as the Turkish side’s interim boss which means he will face the club he played for between 2006 and 2012.

The Dutchman has got his work cut out to get Fenerbahce firing in this season’s league phase, having been thumped 6-0 by Aston Villa on matchday two and drawing with Roma and Slavia Prague.

Speaking in the pre-match press conference, Kuyt admitted he will need to keep his emotions in check for what he describes as an “special occasion”.

He said: "Of course, tomorrow will be a very special occasion for me but at the stage we're in, I'm not here for myself. I'm here for the club and that's what is important - to show the fans who deserve much more, a reaction, positive energy and a good performance."

Liverpool, meanwhile, have won all three games in the league phase so far, scoring 12 goals but have conceded seven as Andoni Iraola has not yet struck the right balance between attack and defence.

The Reds are seeking to bounce back from defeat to Arsenal at the weekend, though they can take confidence from the fact that they have won four of five away games across all competitions this season.

While Liverpool are no strangers to Turkey, particularly Istanbul, this will be the first-ever meeting with Fenerbahce at any level and the attacking talent across both squads suggests an entertaining game is on the cards.

Here’s everything you need to know about where to watch the match...

TV channel: You can watch this game in the UK on TNT Sports 1, with coverage set to start at 5pm GMT ahead of the 5.45pm GMT kick-off.
