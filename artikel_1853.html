Who will England face in Nations League quarter-final? Next opponents confirmed after Spain win

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

England are on course to record their best ever performance in the Nations League, having qualified for the quarter-finals as group winners ahead of world champions Spain.

Including its inaugural season in 2018/19, the Three Lions have competed in every single Nations League campaign, experiencing some highs and the lowest of lows.

From topping their group and qualifying for the Nations League finals and finishing third to suffering a shock relegation in 2022/23 and then immediate promotion, England have done almost everything except win the competition.

Having started the 2026/27 campaign with a loss to Spain, despite leading 2-1 at one stage, England bounced back in style by beating the Czechs twice and then thumping Croatia 7-0 in a remarkable, record-breaking rout.

Another win over Croatia followed at Wembley to confirm a top-two finish in the group and qualify for the Nations League knockout stage.

England then beat Spain in a one-game shootout in Madrid to win the group and with that, potentially securing, an easier quarter-final tie next March.

The four group winners will face the four runners-up in a two-legged Nations League quarter-final.

The group winners will all be seeded and will have home advantage in their respective second leg ties. Teams from the same group cannot be paired together in the knockout stage, therefore England and Spain - for example - will be kept apart.

As a result of finishing top in Group A3, England’s confirmed potential Nations League quarter-final opponent are Belgium and the Netherlands after they were pipped to top spot in Group A1 and A2 by France and Germany respectively.

England’s remaining possible quarter-final opponents will be revealed on Tuesday night, but as things currently stand, they could be drawn against either Portugal, Denmark or Norway.

The draw for the 2026/27 Nations League quarter-final will take place on Friday, November 20 with the ties scheduled to take place in March 2027.
