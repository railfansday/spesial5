Returning star makes 'up my game' admission amid striker battle

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Joe Westley believes his battle for game time with fellow Dundee attackers will only benefit the Tayside club.

With just one defeat in the last five, the Dark Blues are in fifth place in the William Hill Premiership and the fourth-highest scorers, with Ashley Hay notching three in the last four games and Bradley Fink with four in the last six.

Westley missed three games with an ankle injury but replaced Fink in the 3-1 win over Kilmarnock last Wednesday and, ahead of the Tayside derby against St Johnstone at McDiarmid Park on Saturday, the 22-year-old, with five goals in all competitions this season, said: “I think it’s a healthy competition.

“Everyone wants to be playing football and be in the team. So when we’ve got Ash and Brad scoring, it means I need to up my game and score as many as I can when I’m given the chance because you know that when you’ve got the shirt, people are waiting to take it off you.

“It’s a healthy competition and I think we all drive each other to be the best versions of ourselves.

“I think when all the strikers are scoring and doing well, it’s good to be a part of an attack that’s doing well because you create for each other. If everyone’s firing and everyone’s doing well, then it increases your chance of getting goals and assisting as well.

“It’s a really good time to be an attacker at the club. I think it’s starting to click between everyone and whoever’s on up front has got a great understanding of what’s wanted from them. It gives them the best possible chance to score goals and win games.

“I think everything can come down to hard work. As a group everyone around the club works so hard day in, day out and we’re pushed in training, we’re pushed in the gym and I think we try and always gain the little one percent that we can every week.

“But also there’s the environment that the manager has created where everyone’s in it together. Everyone wants to do the hard yards for each other and I think the hard yards and the basics of football are things that can separate you from other teams in the league.

“I think so far this season we’ve been good at the basics and as long as we continue to do them we’ll be fine.”
