Sheffield Wednesday told they should have signed Mansfield Town and Bradford City pair in the summer by reporter

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Sheffield Wednesday will have the chance to bolster their squad when the January transfer window opens up for business. SWFC are currently positioned in 9th place in the League One table.

Smith, 35, joined Mansfield Town on a free transfer. Meanwhile, Beesley, 29, signed for Bradford City from Burton Albion.

Staton reckons they would have both been ideal for the Owls and has said, as per a report by BBC Sport: “Wednesday missed a trick during the summer. Two players spring to mind — Michael Smith, the former Owl who joined Mansfield, and Jake Beesley who moved from Burton to Bradford.

“Smith was available on a free transfer and would’ve done exactly what Wednesday need – lead the line, frustrate defenders, help the Owls get up the pitch and chip in with goals. He might be 35 now but he can still do a job as he’s showing for the Stags.

“Beesley might’ve been harder to acquire given he joined Bradford for an undisclosed fee. The financial restrictions placed on Wednesday made any signing a challenge over the summer. People will point to the fact he has only one goal for the Bantams so far, but he’s a pain in the neck to play against – big and strong, he batters defenders and can hold it up.”

Sheffield Wednesday fans know all about Smith from his spell at Hillsborough from 2022 to 2025. He made 127 appearances for SWFC in all competitions and scored 33 goals.

He spent the last campaign at Preston North End before he was allowed to leave for Mansfield Town in mid-July. The ex-Swindon Town, Portsmouth and Rotherham United man has since found the net on six occasions in 15 outings for the Stags altogether since his switch to Field Mill.

If he had re-joined the Owls, he would have given them more competition and depth up top. In addition, he could have injected some serious experience into their ranks.

As for Beesley, he has struggled for form since his transfer to Bradford City. He has scored only once for the Bantams in 15 games. He was handed a deal until 2028 by the West Yorkshire club.

The Sheffield-born man was a hit during his time at Burton Albion and was one of their key players. He has also had spells in the past at Chesterfield, Salford City, Solihull Moors, Rochdale and Blackpool and is well travelled.

As Staton has alluded to, Sheffield Wednesday are lacking a proper number nine. SWFC could do with landing a forward this winter.

Smith and Beesley have only just joined Mansfield Town and Bradford City respectively so they are unlikely to be on the Owls’ radar. There are plenty of other options they can identify over the coming weeks though.

Dave Challinor will be eager to put his own stamp on his newly inherited squad with some reinforcements. He was picked as the replacement for Henrik Pedersen and will be looking to guide his new team back to the Championship at the first time of asking.

Sheffield Wednesday lost 2-1 away at Oldham Athletic in the FA Cup on Sunday afternoon. They are back in action on Wednesday at home to Accrington Stanley in the EFL Trophy.
