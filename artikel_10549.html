Zoo gives up on helping overweight viral leopard lose weight

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

A zoo in China has ‘given up’ on helping an overweight and viral leopard finally drop some pounds, admitting he is “too old” to help.

You might think that just because an animal is in a zoo, it’s being fed perfectly and to a specific diet plan. However, there are some animals that manage to pack on the weight and keep it on, no matter how much exercise they do.

Over in China, this has been the case for a few years with a leopard known as Pan Baobao. The 19-year-old leopard has gone viral a few times now, especially for his resemblance to Officer Clawhauser from the Zootopia franchise.

Keepers at the Panzhihua Zoo have, over the years, attempted to help the chunky predator lose a few pounds by changing his diet and getting him moving more. However, with the new year approaching, they’ve decided to put an end to those attempts.

As per CNN Travel, a keeper told them that Pan Baobao has become too old to help him stick to a diet.

“He’s getting old, so weight loss isn’t advisable. The priority is to maintain his current condition and keep him healthy,” they said.

An official at the zoo, Zhang Songwen, echoed this while speaking to First Post. “It’s not that we’re not controlling its diet, but it’s getting old and shouldn’t be losing weight,” Zhang added.

Keepers have noted that the big cat is in “good condition,” and they just want to provide a comfortable life for Pan Baobao, who is still in “healthy spirits” too.
