Fruity Pebbles Rice Krispies Treats

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Recipe by Cori Cooper, flagstaff, Arizona

Fruity Pebbles Rice Krispies treats are party-ready, from the bright rainbow colors and fruity flavor to the crisp, chewy texture and marshmallow pull.

If there are Rice Krispies treats on any table full of treats, I’ll make a beeline for them before even glancing at brownies or cupcakes. The combination of light, crisp cereal and chewy marshmallow hits every time. There will always be a place in my heart for the classic ones, but playing around with different Rice Krispies treats variations brings me endless joy. Cori Cooper, a Community Cook from Flagstaff, Arizona, feels the same way, which led her to create these colorful Fruity Pebbles Rice Krispies treats.

By just switching to a rainbow-colored, fruity cereal, Cori transformed the dessert without changing the beloved texture of the original or making it too sweet. I’ll be making these for my next bake sale. Hopefully they make it out of my kitchen!

Place the cereal and half of one package of mini marshmallows in a large bowl.

In a microwave-safe bowl or a large saucepan over low heat, melt the remaining marshmallows in butter, stirring until they’re smooth. Stir in the salt, if using it, then remove the mixture from the heat. Pour it over the cereal mixture.

Editor’s Tip: If using the microwave, heat the butter and marshmallows in 30- to 45-second intervals, stirring in between, to prevent the butter from getting too hot and splattering.

Press into a lightly greased 8-inch square baking pan using waxed paper or a lightly greased spatula. Let the mixture cool to room temperature. Cut it into bars.

Editor’s Tip: For bars that hold together but still have an airy bite and chewy texture, press the cereal mixture evenly into the pan, but don’t use all your might and pack them in.

Store Fruity Pebbles Rice Krispies treats at room temperature, tightly wrapped, for three to five days. Technically, they last longer, but the cereal will start to get stale and the treats will become brittle over time. You can leave the treats in the baking pan and tightly wrap the entire pan, or transfer cut squares to an airtight food storage container, adding a piece of parchment between layers to keep them from sticking together.

You can freeze Fruity Pebbles Rice Krispies treats for up to six weeks, but they won’t have the same soft, stretchy texture as freshly made treats. Pack the treats in a freezer-proof food storage bag, pressing out the excess air, or place them in a container with an airtight lid, adding a piece of parchment between the layers. They will thaw quickly, so just let them stand at room temperature for 10 to 15 minutes before eating them.

Cereal treats can end up hard instead of soft, stretchy and chewy if the marshmallows are stale or overheated, or if you pack the treats too tightly into the baking dish. Stale marshmallows don’t have the same moisture and bounce as fresh ones, making them tough from the start. And since marshmallows are mostly sugar, they can burn or become candy-like if overcooked, which makes the treats unpleasantly firm. If you compress the cereal too much, you crush out all its air, which results in dense treats that are hard to bite through.

You can use several different pans for these Fruity Pebbles Rice Krispies treats to vary their shape or thickness. For thinner treats, press the cereal mixture into a 9-inch square baking pan. For a different shape, use an 8-inch or 9-inch round cake pan and then cut the cereal treats into triangles, like slices of pie.

Since these are Fruity Pebbles treats, you should keep at least half the amount of Fruity Pebbles, but if you’re a few cups short (this happens to me all the time), you can make up the difference with one of the best childhood cereals, like Cheerios or Lucky Charms. You can use almost any crisp, puffed or flaked cereal, but consider what will taste the best with the fruity base. I’d recommend a cereal with light sweetness and a nutty or vanilla flavor so you don’t overshadow the Fruity Pebbles.
