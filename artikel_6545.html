How to Pack for Vegas Like a Vogue Editor

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

After years of over-packing, under-packing, and everything in between, I’d like to think I’ve finally mastered the art of packing for a work trip. Most of my work trips take me to the fashion capitals, where I can more or less anticipate what my itinerary–and, naturally, my personal lookbook–will entail. After countless fashion months, I have a fairly reliable formula down–but every now and then, a destination comes along that throws my blueprint for a loop. My latest challenge? Las Vegas for the F1 Grand Prix.

Why is packing for Vegas so difficult? Mainly because dressing for the glitz of Sin City falls rather outside my usual minimalist style. But, as a devoted F1 fan, I decided to embrace the challenge.

I was headed out there to celebrate the launch of the new, limited edition Wynn Las Vegas x Olivia Von Halle collection, so I knew there were a few event-specific outfits I needed to prepare. My action-packed itinerary (and hyper-glam Las Vegas alter ego) required sparkles, velvet, and more than a few statement jackets, so I started there, but generally my packing philosophy for all work trips is fairly simple. I start with understanding my itinerary, then work in outfits. I like to scribble my packing list by hand, try on the full look before it makes it to my suitcase (unless I have worn it before), and sometimes commit to a mirror selfie as a reminder. Once I have the key looks figured out, I typically throw a few wardrobe staples in as back-ups. A great pair of denim, black pointed-toe heels, a few white t-shirts, and a black clutch always come with me.

My first stop on this trip was brunch at the recently opened Zero Bond, hosted by Olivia Von Halle and Kelly Piquet. They brought together a mix of familiar faces from the worlds of fashion and Formula 1, including Susie Wolff, CEO of F1 Academy, Bianca Bustamante, racing driver, and jewelry designer Isabela Gruttman. Beyond the collection launch, there was no shortage of entertainment. From being able to look out my window and see the race track, to race cars installed between blackjack tables, to a host of driver pop-up activations, Formula 1 truly took over the city and the energy was incredible.

Ahead, everything I packed and wore for a long weekend work trip to Las Vegas.

Simple, comfortable, and practical is always my philosophy when it comes to a travel outfit. My Away luggage comes with me on every trip, and my travel uniform remains the same as always: a white t-shirt, Agolde denim, Nike sneakers, and in this case, my largest coat that simply could not fit in my suitcase.

I picked up this custom pearl-embroidered Róhe jacket during Paris Fashion Week this past season. It’s heavy and added several pounds to my suitcase, but felt brunch-appropriate styled with a simple black slip skirt and Chanel pumps.

This festive velvet red jacket from Liberowe was certainly on-theme. By day, I wore the jacket with a simple white tee, jeans, and a faux fur snood; then, for an evening at the casino, I switched it up with the matching mini skirt, sheer tights, and patent heels.

Race night was just incredible; but the dressing brief was tough. I needed a statement-making outfit that was appropriate for the Wynn Skybox but warm enough to spend a few hours outdoors. With 352 overtakes and 31 lead changes, it was nearly impossible to take my eyes off the track… thankfully, I stayed cozy in the Maeve shearling jacket from Ducie. Then came a swift change for my after-party look, where I traded denim for sequins with this semi-sheer Alfie dress, and styled it out with the same giant shearling and a Julietta collar necklace.

There are a few things I never leave behind when traveling for work; good skincare, luxurious pajamas, and jewelry are at the top of that list. I’m quite particular about keeping to my usual morning and evening routines, even when I’m away. My travel skincare is an extension of what I use at home, with a few indulgences that make the end of a long day feel slightly more familiar. The same goes for sleepwear; I almost exclusively travel with a pajama set from Olivia von Halle, this time in an on-theme casino print!

Great jewelry is truly what makes every look your own, and thanks to Michael Rider at Celine my collection has been growing recently… including a dice charm I purchased on this trip as a very chic souvenir.
