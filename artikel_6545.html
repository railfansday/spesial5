This Home Locker Made My Entryway So Much More Functional

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Mustard Made home lockers blend utilitarian functionality and bold color for the perfect entryway and mudroom furniture.

Our editors and experts handpick every product we feature. We may earn a commission from your purchases.Learn more.

I’m always on the hunt for products that nail the sweet spot between form and function. And with a toddler at home, let’s just say we’ve accumulated stuff. Toys, shoes, kid gear, you name it. Our entryway, laundry room and pretty much every shared space could use a little organizational intervention.

So, when I first saw Mustard Made lockers online, I was intrigued. The bold colors and playful, schoolhouse-inspired design, coupled with the range of sizes, made them feel like a storage upgrade with personality. I was looking for a compact catch-all near the front door for the shoes, hats and everyday essentials we’re constantly grabbing on our way out.

The more I thought about it, the more I liked the idea of a home locker: sturdy metal, magnetic doors and an easy-to-clean finish. Sold. I ordered the two-door Midi locker from Mustard.

Of course, choosing a color was a whole ordeal. I played it safe with Chalk, expecting a crisp white, but it turned out to be more of a warm off-white. It’s not quite what I pictured, but it works in our space. That said, if I could do it all over again, I’d tell myself to be bold and go with Mustard or Olive.

Here’s how the locker came together, how it’s holding up and whether it’s helping tame the everyday chaos.

A utilitarian storage cabinet that pairs versatile functionality with a pop of color

The Midi arrived on my porch in a flat, narrow box that was easy to lift and carry where I needed to build it. Once I unpacked everything, I was happy to find that most of the locker simply slots and clicks into place. No tools, screws or complicated assembly required. The only tool I needed was a Phillips-head screwdriver to attach the door handles.

Mustard Made includes printed instructions, and you can also find them on the website, along with a step-by-step assembly video if you need a little extra guidance.

The trickiest part? Getting the doors on. They can take a bit of fiddling, but gently bending the small tab helps hold the L-shaped bracket in place and keeps the door steady while you assemble it.

Once assembled, the locker feels solid, sturdy and made to last. But it’s the brand’s attention to detail that really makes it functional. There are attachment points on the back for securing it to the wall (a welcome feature with a toddler around), interior hooks on each door for hanging smaller items, adjustable shelves and even a cable hole in the back for managing cords.

And the customization options are a bonus, even though they’re sold separately. Mustard Made sells extra shelves, super-strong button magnets and metal hangers for the Skinny and Twinny lockers, which can double as mini wardrobes. They also offer adorable alphabet and number magnets for displaying messages, labeling spaces or giving older kids something to play with.

Another thing I love? Mustard Made offers six different locker styles and sizes, so there’s an option for just about every room and organizational storage need.

My daughter loves using the locker as a little art gallery, proudly displaying her masterpieces. And it’s not just for artwork. The home locker is a fun spot for handwritten notes, photos, collectible magnets and other little things we want to put on display. The Mustard Made button magnets are surprisingly strong and sturdy, too, holding much more than your average fridge magnet.

If you have things you want to keep under lock and key, Mustard Made has you covered. Each locker door comes with its own lock and set of keys. It took me a little while to realize that the locks are different, so a heads-up: each door comes with two keys, and you’ll want to keep them organized. Mixing them up (or losing them altogether) could lead to frustration.

In our house, the locker is primarily a shoe drop zone by the front door, so we haven’t had much reason to use the locks. But I can see this feature coming in handy for storing valuables or keeping potentially hazardous household items out of little hands. Just be sure to use appropriate child-safety measures for anything that poses a risk.

With so many shapes, sizes and colors to choose from, Mustard Made lockers are a versatile storage solution for just about any room. Think mudrooms, laundry rooms, playrooms, entryways, porches and beyond.

They’re not limited to hiding clutter, either. Depending on the style and size, they can pull double duty as entertainment consoles, wardrobes, buffet tables or even drink cabinets. Basically, they’re as flexible as they are functional.

A colorful home locker for your entryway, mudroom, playroom, garage, porch and beyond

You can shop the Midi and the full range of lockers directly from Mustard Made. The Midi retails for $369 and comes in 12 colors. Want something a little more design-forward? The brand also offers lockers with fluted glass doors for a more elevated look.
