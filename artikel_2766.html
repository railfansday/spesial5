Isas Waxter, two other Chargers defenders injured in Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Los Angeles Chargers defensive lineman Justin Ebolgbe (ankle) left the Week 9 game against the Houston Texans due to an ankle injury, and he did not return.

Cornerbacks Cam Hart (ankle) and Isas Waxter (ankle) also left with ankle injuries, and neither could make it back into the game.
