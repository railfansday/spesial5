Frankie Muniz Shares Health Update After Frightening Daytona Crash

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

What was supposed to be a night honoring military veterans at Daytona International Speedway took an unexpected turn for Frankie Muniz.

The former "Malcolm in the Middle" star and NASCAR driver was hospitalized Wednesday, Nov. 11, after crashing during a practice session ahead of Ford's Proud to Honor charity event. Muniz was evaluated at a nearby medical facility and ultimately released, but the accident brought his night at Daytona to an early end.

Fortunately, the 40-year-old had encouraging news to share afterward.

"I'm a little sore, but my Mustang did its job in protecting me," Muniz said in a statement following his release.

It was a reassuring update after an afternoon that had raised concerns about his well-being.

Muniz had traveled to Daytona to participate in Ford's Proud to Honor event, which brought drivers together to recognize military veterans while supporting six nonprofit organizations serving military and veteran communities.

He was scheduled to race alongside fellow NASCAR driver Austin Cindric.

Instead, his day ended with a trip to the hospital.

Ford Racing confirmed shortly after the accident that Muniz had been transported to a local medical facility for further evaluation. The organization also reported that he was awake and alert.

Muniz was eventually cleared to leave, although he would not be returning to the track to compete.

"I am disappointed I won't be able to take part in Ford's Proud to Honor event tonight," Muniz said. "I was looking forward to honoring the service of our veterans and racing against these other amazing drivers."

He also thanked the medical personnel who treated him, along with Ford Racing, Daytona International Speedway and those who reached out with messages of support.

The crash occurred during practice, before the event officially began. Daytona International Speedway was not open to the public at the time.

The accident comes after another significant year in Muniz's transition from Hollywood to professional stock car racing.

Muniz spent the 2025 season competing full-time in the NASCAR Craftsman Truck Series, driving the No. 33 Ford for Reaume Brothers Racing. He returned for a second season in 2026. It marked another major step in a racing career he has pursued seriously for years, long after becoming a household name on television.

His appearance at Daytona wasn't part of a NASCAR championship race, but it was another opportunity to get behind the wheel while supporting a cause important to Ford and the racing community.

That opportunity disappeared before the event even started.

For Muniz, the disappointment of missing the charity race was clear. But after a crash serious enough to require a hospital evaluation, being able to walk away with soreness was a far better outcome than the alternative.

And judging by his first update, Muniz knows it.
