Yahoo Fantasy Football Expert League Recap: We've got a three-way tie for first place after Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

If you're unfamiliar with the Yahoo Fantasy Football Expert League, let's fill you in a bit before we go over Week 10. Yahoo Fantasy houses some of the leading analysts in the industry, and we were fortunate enough to get all of them in a league for the 2026 NFL season. To keep you up to date on things, we'll be providing weekly recaps on all the action. Let's get into it.

Here's a look at the standings with the playoffs coming up soon:

Here's a look at the Yahoo Expert League standings after Week 10.

Boy, do we have a recap for you! Week 10 brought us a few marquee matchups, one that had big first-place implications. We'll start there with Scott Pianowski taking on Hayden Winks. Pianowski entered this week clinging to his first-place standing at 7-2 while Winks has been eyeing the spot for some time now.

There's a lot to break down here. The stars really aligned for Winks in Week 10, sporting good matchups left and right. He posted a high score of 159.86 on the week, so if you're Pianowski, you kind of have to just tip your hat. Five of Winks' players went for 19+ fantasy points, including Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Jayden Daniels, Chuba Hubbard and Isaiah Likely.

We also saw the matchup of the Rams' backfield — Pianowski with Kyren Williams while Winks rostered Blake Corum. Had a few more players stepped up on Scott's side, Kyren versus Corum could have decided the week entirely. Unfortunately, Williams was one of many players who fell short for Pianowski, with just 4.2 fantasy points to Corum's 19.4.

Pianowski also got a rare poor showing in a smash spot from the Jaguars D/ST (3 FPTS). Plus, with the Bears on bye and no Luther Burden III and Colston Loveland, Pianowski got sunk by Darren Waller and Dylan Sampson in his FLEX. It also didn't help that Zay Flowers didn't show much pop with Jacoby Brissett under center.

With a loaded roster and Josh Jacobs and Mike Evans waiting in the wings to return from injury, Winks is among the favorites to win the Expert League going into Week 11.

In our closest matchup of Week 10, Eric Edholm was able to sneak by Josh Norris 111.76-108.24. Edholm didn't have much pop throughout his lineup with Puka Nacua on IR. He also left Patrick Mahomes' 35.42 fantasy points on the bench. But that's fine when you have MVP favorite Joe Burrow, who went for 33.16 points. The rest of Edholm's lineup did juuuuust enough to outlast Norris, who only got one TD from his skill position players.

With that win, Edholm moved into a three-way tie with Pianowski and Winks for first place at 7-3. Winks has the edge in total points scored this season by almost a 100-point margin, however.

Matt Harmon was able to take down podcast partner Justin Boone in a 90.68-80.22 rock fight. Had Drake Maye not gone down, things likely would have been different for Boone, who also saw plenty of points on his bench in the form of Deebo Samuel Sr., Jordyn Tyson and Kaelon Black.

Harmon is currently clinging to the sixth and final playoff spot despite being ninth in total points scored. He's not letting that keep him down.

A few managers to keep an eye on as we head into the push for the postseason are Joel Smyth and Ray Garvin. Both topped 150 fantasy points in Week 10 and are positioning themselves to push for one of the final playoff spots with four weeks left in the fantasy regular season. Garvin's team is very boom-bust with George Pickens, Parker Washington, Matthew Golden and Jameson Williams. But you can see what happens when it booms.

Meanwhile, Smyth's team is second in points scored in the league and is on a three-game heater after a slow start.
