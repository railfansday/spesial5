Christian Parker considered not staying with the Cowboys

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Cowboys have removed play-calling duties from defensive coordinator Christian Parker. Parker initially considered leaving the team. He decided not to.

Parker told reporters on Thursday that he decided to process the “significant” disappointment by remaining in his job, albeit with the most important role taken away.

“That would have been running from it,” Parker said as to the possibility of resigning.

As to whether it will be awkward moving forward, Parker said, “I think it’s only awkward if you make it, and we don’t have to make it that way.”

But it will be awkward. The defensive coordinator isn’t fully coordinating the defense. And it’s not like the situation in Denver, where the head coach assumed the play-calling duties from the coordinator. Parker’s biggest responsibilities were given to someone other than the head coach — a consultant who arrived just last month.

The cleanest approach for the Cowboys, given the team’s obvious lack of confidence in Parker, would have been to fire him. But that would have come with a buyout of the balance of his contract. And owner/G.M. Jerry Jones doesn’t like paying coaches to not work.

Jones likely hopes Parker will eventually realize that the situation isn’t sustainable and (as the lingo goes) “self-select.” That avoids the buyout and conclusively ends the inherently awkward situation.
