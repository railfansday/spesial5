Fernando Mendoza reflected on his first NFL start on Linkedin

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Fernando Mendoza's first NFL start wasn't particularly impressive, as he completed 17 of 29 passes for 143 yards and a rushing touchdown against the Seattle Seahawks. But beyond the numbers, he finally fulfilled the role envisioned for him by the Raiders as the first overall pick in the 2026 draft.

Mendoza and the Raiders may have lost that Week 10 game against the defending Super Bowl champions, but it is a learning experience for the former Indiana standout. The 2025 Heisman Trophy winner even shared his sentiments about that game on social media, but not through the platform you'd expect.

Typically, athletes would express their thoughts through X, Facebook, TikTok, or Instagram. Instead, the 2026 CFP National Champion shares his thoughts on LinkedIn.

"A lot to learn from my first NFL start. Grateful for my family, friends, and Raider Nation for the support! Looking forward to getting better and earning the trust of my teammates and coaches every day."

Mendoza is very active on LinkedIn. With his profile summary reading:

"My experience at the quarterback position has shaped how I approach leadership, communication, and time management. I take pride in being a dependable teammate, adapting quickly to new systems, and bringing a team-first mindset into every environment I'm part of."

It seems like Mendoza is a quick learner because he did significantly better in Week 11 against the Denver Broncos. Despite the loss, Mendoza threw for 267 yards and three touchdowns, a significant improvement from his debut. Perhaps he will have another LinkedIn post once he gets his first victory as a starter.
