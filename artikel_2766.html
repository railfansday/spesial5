3 Titans players should dominate vs. Cowboys in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Tennessee Titans made an offensive shift over the previous three games. They've prioritized the development of sophomore quarterback Cam Ward by calling a pass-happy, no-huddle offense. The results have been extremely encouraging.

The Titans are averaging nearly 30 points per game on offense since letting Ward execute his preferred approach. On Sunday, they'll play against a Dallas Cowboys defense allowing 28.2 points per contest. The following Titans players should thrive in Sunday's Week 11 matchup.

Cam Ward is arguably playing the best football of his career right now (certainly of his sophomore season). His 28 completions versus the Jaguars tied a previous career high, and his 71.8% completion percentage was a season-best result. In the previous game, Ward had his first-ever 300 yard passing performance against the Bengals. He'll look to keep the momentum rolling versus a struggling Cowboys defense.

Carnell Tate is returning to the lineup after a two-game absence with a shoulder injury. In one appearance since the Titans made notable changes on offense, he recorded five catches for 69 yards against the Browns. With Ward currently playing admirably, getting Tate back into the lineup should have another positive effect on a surging offense. The rookie fourth overall selection could dominate the Cowboys' undermanned secondary.

The Titans opted to retain veteran running back Tony Pollard at the NFL trade deadline after he rushed for 100+ yards in consecutive games (Browns, Bengals) and scored a combined five touchdowns. Pollard didn't find much rushing success versus the Jaguars, but he did accumulate 66 receiving yards and a touchdown on six catches, including an impressive individual effort on his 32-yard passing score. His steady presence continues to remove the load from Ward's shoulders.
