Here’s your 17 LHSAA second-round playoff football matchups

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The 2026 Louisiana high school football playoffs head into the second round of playoffs this weekend with 17 games involving Shreveport and Bossier City area teams in action.

There will be seven area teams hosting games including North DeSoto, Ruston, Loyola, Calvary, Haynesville, Logansport and St. Mary's. All games will be at 7 p.m. except for Haynesville versus North Iberville game, which is at 7:30 p.m.
