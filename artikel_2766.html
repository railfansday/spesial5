Here’s where the Broncos stand right now in the division and the AFC playoff picture

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Denver Broncos were blown out by the Pittsburgh Steelers on Black Friday, which has some big impacts on the AFC Playoff Picture. With this loss, the Broncos' odds of winning the number one seed in the AFC have taken a big hit, and it also keeps the Chiefs within striking range for the AFC West. The Broncos still do have a favorable schedule upcoming and play the Bills, but no Broncos fan is thinking one seed after a loss like this. With the Chiefs' loss on Thanksgiving Night, the Broncos still lead the AFC West but both teams are tied at 8-3.

As for the Steelers, their wild week continues. It looks like they imploded. Aaron Rodgers was released, McCarthy was embarrassed on National Television, and the locker room appeared to be in disarray. Well, that all changed on Friday when they came out and absolutely dominated the Broncos at home, improved to 7-4, and kept their playoff hopes very alive. They move up to the 6th seed right now, but that could change after the weekend finishes.

Looking ahead to the rest of the games this weekend, the Bengals play the Saints, the Jaguars play the Titans, and the Browns play on Sunday Night vs. the Raiders. Meanwhile, the Patriots, Texans, Raiders, and Ravens will look to get back on track and keep their postseason hopes alive.
