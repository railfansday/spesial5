Titans get Carnell Tate back at practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Titans won't be getting wide receiver Wan'Dale Robinson back on the field this season, but they could be getting first-round pick Carnell Tate back in the lineup against the Cowboys this week.

Tate was able to practice on a limited basis on Wednesday. He missed the last two games with a shoulder injury and had 34 catches for 413 yards and a touchdown in the first seven games of the season.

Running back Tyjae Spears (knee) and safety Tony Adams (concussion) were both out of practice. Defensive lineman John Franklin-Myers (back) and linebacker Anthony Hill (neck) were also listed as limited participants.

The Titans designated rookie guard Fernando Carmona (ankle) for return from injured reserve and he was limited in his first day back on the field.

Linebacker Cody Barton (concussion), tight end Kyler Granson (ankle), and safety Kevin Winston (shoulder) were all listed as full participants.
