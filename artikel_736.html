Brielle Biermann Marries Billy Seidl Amid Family Troubles

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Brielle Biermann—the eldest daughter of Kim Zolciak—said "I do" to Billy Seidl on Nov. 21, two years after the former minor league baseball player proposed.

Kim Zolciak's daughter tied the knot with Billy Seidl in a beachside ceremony on Nov. 21, nearly three years after announcing their engagement.

Alongside the Instagram video posted the same day of Brielle, 29—whose adoptive dad is Kroy Biermann—and the former baseball player sharing a sweet kiss by the ocean, she wrote with a white heart emoji, "Mrs. Seidl."

She was a blushing bride in white as she walked down the aisle in a strapless ball gown with a tulle skirt and a large black bow adorning her waist, accessorized with a matching white veil. For Billy's part, he said "I do" in a classic black tux.

Brielle's younger sister Ariana Biermann, 25, wasn't tardy to the party, commenting under the announcement, "I'm crying. I love you so much."

Gia Guidice—who stars on Next Gen NYC with Ariana—also celebrated the nuptials IRL and online, writing, "Beautiful [red heart emoji] so so stunning!! so happy for you both."

Meanwhile, Kim shared the clip to her Instagram Stories after the milestone, celebrating her eldest tying the knot.

But as Brielle embraces her new era with Billy, the ongoing sexual assault case against her 15-year-old brother Kroy "KJ" Biermann Jr. has been a shadow over the family.

The teen—who pleaded not guilty to assaulting a 15-year-old in a Lifetime Fitness changing room—was indicted Sept. 3 on seven counts, including aggravated child molestation, aggravated sexual battery, aggravated sodomy, sexual battery against a child under 16 and false imprisonment.

His then-attorney Jason Sheffield (replaced by Bryan Steel earlier this month) argued that as the case moves forward, KJ's innocence will be proven.

"I respect the difficult responsibilities carried by prosecutors and victim advocates," Sheffield wrote in a September statement to E! News. "Cases involving children require patience, care and a commitment from all of us to understanding the full picture as the process continues."

Kim—also mom to 12-year-old twins with Kroy—threw her support behind KJ after his August arrest, describing the allegations against him as "nothing short of reckless and deeply troubling" in a September statement to E! News.

"False accusations can have devastating consequences not only for the person being accused, but also for genuine survivors of sexual assault and abuse," the statement read. "Every legitimate allegation deserves to be taken seriously and investigated based on the facts and evidence. At the same time, no one should be publicly condemned based solely on an accusation."

But the allegations against KJ didn't end with the 15-year-old girl. As prosecutors sought to block the transfer of his April case to juvenile court, they shared insight into 16 previous offenses against the teen from a separate 2025 sexual assault case, according to court documents obtained by E! News.

KJ—whose attorney denied the allegations on his behalf—was charged with incest, eight counts of child molestation, sexual battery, statutory rape and five counts of the sexual exploitation of children in April 2025 after the Milton County Police Department discovered "videos and photos" in a hidden folder on his phone.

Sheffield accused the prosecutors in the case—which is currently with the Fulton County Juvenile Court—of "grossly" misrepresenting the facts in a separate September statement to E! News, adding that the "matter arose from inappropriate, crude behavior involving two middle-schoolers."
