Marlon Jackson Reveals Stage 4 Prostate Cancer Diagnosis: ‘God Has Me’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Jackson 5 member said he's been battling cancer for four years now and is keeping a positive outlook.

Marlon Jackson, member of The Jackson 5 and brother of Michael Jackson, announced that he’s currently battling stage 4 prostate cancer.

During a recent appearance on the Farah Shammas Podcast, Marlon revealed that he’s been undergoing treatment and had his prostate gland removed. “I’ma break some news to you that you don’t know,” Jackson began. “And I’ma tell people right now, God has me. He already told me he has me. I’ve had cancer for four years. But I’m great. I’m good. Bad grass don’t die. So I’m going to be around for a long time.”

“I’ve had my prostate gland removed and I’ve had radiation treatment and everything is fine. Everything is great,” he added. “I walk almost 4 miles four times a week. You know, and that’s great for me. Marlon is a year and a half older than Michael, making him the second youngest member of the influential group.

In other Jackson 5 news, the four surviving members — siblings Jackie, Jermaine, Marlon and Tito Jackson — have a 90-minute documentary in the works. The untitled project will be directed by Benjamin Hirsch and will reportedly feature Tito Jackson’s last interview before he passed away at 70 in 2024.
