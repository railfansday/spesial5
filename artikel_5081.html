Top 13 Telegram games to try out in November 2026

Telegram has become more than just a communication app for people around the world. What started out as just a messaging app has grown into an ecosystem of its own. Users can enjoy casual games, puzzles, and simulation-based games. Accessibility is another key reason why players are attracted to Telegram games; it has an easy point of entry and reward mechanisms as well.

Here are our top picks for the best Telegram games in the month of November 2026:

Chat Wars is a traditional role-playing game that is built around fantasy world-building, heroes, guilds, resources, and strategic decisions. Players start by building their characters and can collect resources, improve equipment, participate in activities, and contribute to their guild.

Combat, crafting, and resource management are all part of the game’s overall experience, so players can become part of a larger experience rather than just collecting points. Guild participation is a major part of the game, and groups can cooperate, coordinate, and work towards shared objectives.

Catizen lets users develop, combine, and manage digital cats. The main objective of the game is to collect new cats, merge similar ones, expand facilities, complete missions, and grow. Players can collect rewards, unlock new buildings, and participate in seasonal events.

The game has a more holistic approach than many other tap-to-earn games as it focuses on community interactions and long-term engagement. The game has evolved over time into an ecosystem of its own with a native token called CATI.

A very popular game in the space, Hamster Kombat also has a tap-to-earn format where the original gameplay revolved around managing a virtual crypto exchange owned by a CEO who was a hamster. Now the experience has evolved into HamsterVerse, bringing additional games and experiences into the ecosystem.

One such experience is Hamster GameDev, which allows players to manage a virtual game development studio where they can hire team members, create games, upgrade their operations, and generate virtual income. Instead of a repeated tapping gaming, this gives it a more structured managment style traditional approach.

Pig of Ton is a hyper-casual merge-and-battle game that integrates character collection, merging mechanics, and competitive rankings. The game has futuristic cyber pigs that can be collected, upgraded, and developed, and are a central part of the gameplay.

The futuristic pig theme gives it a very distinct identity in the space, and instead of using traditional fantasy characters, cyber pigs are its central characters.

PlayDeck has a large collection of casual games in a single Telegram Mini App, making it convenient for players who enjoy playing in new genres. It gives players a chance to explore challenges, puzzles, and reaction-based games.

PlayDeck offers convenience and accessibility to all its users and can compete with other open PlayBoxes, spin the Wheel of Luck, and earn $PlayCoin.

MemeFi is a tap-to-earn combat game where players fight meme-inspired bosses, earn crypto rewards, and make character progressions. The main USP of the game is its combat, where players go up against enemies and defeat challenging opponents.

Users can also create various clans, manage their energy levels, or use boosters. MEMEFI is the utility and governance token of the platform and has a total supply of 10 billion.

Rocky Rabbit is a tap-to-earn mini-game that was built on the TON network. Players need to train their rabbits in the game, participate in duels and complete any tasks to rank higher. The game takes its inspiration from fitness and boxing.

The platform’s native token RBTC (RabBitcoin) was launched on TON in 2024, and players can join competitions and battles, earn rewards, and bonuses for referrals.

A famous retro-style pixel mini-game on TON, Yescoin lets users swipe their fingers across the screen to catch falling coins similar to Fruit Ninja. Players can complete missions, collect coins, upgrade their skills, and be a part of the community.

Players can buy upgrades and power-ups to boost their earnings, as it is managed by an energy system that regenerates over time. There are quests, missions, events, and various other options for players to earn rewards throughout the game.

A play-to-earn mini-game built around the Minesweeper mechanics that mixes it with dungeon exploration. Players need to explore a 5×5 grid where they tap blocks one by one and earn gold. If they choose the block that has a trap, the game is over.

As a bonus after every round, there is an option to play a clicker side game where players need to tap the skull to farm gold. Besides the game itself, players can collect keys for passive income and tickets for daily cash prizes.

Pixel Tap is a cyberpunk combat-themed mini-game where players can tap the screen to earn in-game coins, upgrade robot fighters, and take part in real-time battles against each other. It is part of the wider Pixelverse ecosystem, and the game revolves around tapping mechanics and PvP battles.

In-game currency can be earned through referrals, daily tasks, battles, and general activity. Players can get into battles and earn points and tokens in PvE and PvP settings. $PIXFI is the native token of the platform, and rewards can be earned in it.

Egg Drop is a play-to-earn game on the BNB Chain where players drop virtual eggs into target zones and score points while collecting points and levelling up “Birdies”. The eggs fall under various categories, such as white, yellow, royal, energy, punch, shield, attack, and steal types.

Accessibility and engagement are important parts of the game where the sessions are short and easy to navigate and aimed at users looking for time-intensive games. Token incentives and collected items can be accumulated through regular activity in the game.

RollerTap has a tapping mechanism where players collect RCC or Roller Community Coins that are in-app currency. The rewards can be used to increase their tap level and how much they earn overtime creating a system built around steady activity and upgrades.

Leaderboards in the game also allow players to compete with each other and track their rankings. They can also collect cards that boost their profits and take part in limited eveents and challenges for extra rewards.

A post-apocalyptical setup where players have to survive the zombie attack and earn the $Bombie token, this Telegram game was launched in 2024. The objective is simple: shoot the zombies, defeat the bosses, and upgrade your weapons and gears.

Players can earn the token through the play-to-earn model and also by taking part in airdrops and boss raids. There are community events that help players interact with each other as well.

Telegram games give an easy and convenient way for players to engage with a lot of these tap-to-earn apps. They started out as simple games and have evolved into much more complex ecosystems of their own overtime, offering a wide variety of gaming experience.

Each game brings something different and can be appealing to a different type of gamer. However, it is advisable to do your own reseach before monetarily investing in any game.
