Monero Delisted From KuCoin, XMR Plunges 13%

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Home » Altcoins » Monero Delisted From KuCoin, XMR Plunges 13%

Right after the bearish XMR news hit, the altcoin witnessed a massive drawdown to find its footing at the July, 2026 lows.

This comes just a few days after MEXC Global, a popular competing crypto exchange, delisted Monero on Spot markets, keeping the Futures markets services running. Surely, the shrinking support from major platforms could manifest itself in big-time sell-off sentiment among investors.

The quarterly-low levels on the Bull Bear Power (BBP) metric is already winking at this trend.

KuCoin halted all deposits immediately with a withdrawal time window until November 30, 2026.

That’s when KuCoin’s going to terminate the whole package of Monero (XMR) related services, including the high-demand XMR trading bots, crypto conversions and high-liquidity pairs such as XMR/USDT, XMR/USDC, XMR/ETH and XMR/BTC. On social media, XMR HODLers are worried about the trend’s continuation onto other CEXs.

If Monero’s (XMR) price manages to restore the $441 confluent resistance level, it can attract the $491 level, dropped yesterday. Judging from the Bollinger Bands (BOLL) on the one-day charts, Monero’s price would need to sustain above $501 for a trend switch confirmation, while the $599 line remains the bullish trend check-point.

Meanwhile, the broader crypto market witnesses another downshift with Bitcoin’s (BTC) pullback below $59,000, briefly dipping below the recent day’s highs of roughly $64,000.
