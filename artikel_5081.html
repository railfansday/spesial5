Bitcoin [BTC] traders were down nearly 20% on average. In general, bull market corrections form a bottom at 12% in unrealized losses for the average active trader.

This level of unrealized losses has historically come alongside bottom formations, though it could take at least two months before making a reliable recovery.

Bitcoin holders realized a net loss of 25.8k BTC during the recent sell-off, according to the crypto intelligence platform CryptoQuant. This was the largest 1-day net loss reading since the 13th of July.

The metric uses the price of the coin’s acquisition versus the price at which it was last moved to compute net profit or loss. In early November, the previously positive readings flipped, showing holders were realizing losses.

As Bitcoin prices fell, the net realized losses grew, indicative of investors selling at greater losses. This behavior is commonly seen during bear market phases.

AMBCrypto reported that the leverage flush that led to nearly $3 billion in long liquidations last week might be over, due to the decline in Open Interest.

The importance of sustained demand for a BTC recovery cannot be overstated. However, the market is still in deeply unfavorable conditions for buyers.

The demand growth metric was contracting at a rapid pace, the fastest since early July. The 30-day futures demand growth had been positive in October, but has faded since. The spot demand growth has been negative since the peak in mid-August.

Increased liquidations hastened the perpetuals futures demand contraction, and bearish market sentiment meant the combined total demand growth metric’s 30-day average was -400k BTC.

There were few buyers to absorb the supply being sold.

Glassnode presented evidence that spikes in realized losses mirrored past Bitcoin bear markets and represented capitulation episodes. Increased loss-selling has accompanied the sweep of a considerable magnetic zone of liquidations, from $59.5k-$62.2k.

The $62k-$66k block represented a zone where a fifth of the coins in short-term holders’ hands was acquired. The price is already well below the STH cost basis at $74.1k, where recent buyers would be at break-even.

A daily session close below $62k would mean more panic among STH. This could transform a bearish break into a rout, Glassnode warned, making the current block the support bulls must defend.
