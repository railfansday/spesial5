Pep Guardiola and Brendan Rodgers agreed on Birmingham City star's talent

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Birmingham City's impressive start to the Championship season has laid the foundations for a sustained promotion bid, some 15 years after the club last played Premier League football.

Chris Davies has endured mixed fortunes during what is still his first job in full-time management, having previously developed a strong reputation as an assistant at some of British football's biggest institutions such as Liverpool, Celtic and Tottenham Hotspur.

Having led Birmingham to their best finish in a decade after breaking several records in League One, the 41-year-old has since refined his tactical approach, and it has paid dividends, with his squad looking far more efficient all across the pitch.

In recent times, Blues have added several high-profile names to their squad, such is the backing Davies has received from Knighthead Capital Management, but it's fair to say that not all of said transfer dealings have paid off.

However, whilst his start to the current campaign has been mixed, Patrick Roberts is still seen as an integral part of the squad at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park, with the winger's technical ability being well-known for several years.

Roberts rose to initial stardom after breaking through at Fulham and for the England youth ranks, with Cottagers boss, Felix Magath, predicting a strong career for the winger.

After impressing in the Championship following the club;'s relegation in 2014, Roberts suddenly became the subject of interest from Liverpool, who were managed by Brendan Rodgers at the time.

Having made 17 appearances in the second tier, he would make a swift return to the top-flight, but with Manchester City, in a deal which had the potential to rise as high as £11m, under Manuel Pellegrini.

Roberts then made all three of his senior appearances for the North West side in a short period of time, with his debut coming in the EFL Cup against Sunderland, before Celtic struck an 18-month loan agreement for the highly-rated talent.

His impact at Parkhead was immediate, scoring six times in his first 11 appearances, before netting 11 times in 47 appearances across all competitions in 2016/17.

However, it's fair to say that his goal for the Hoops in the 1-1 draw at the Etihad Stadium in December 2016 raised question marks over a potential long-term future in Manchester under the legendary Pep Guardiola, who, like the aforementioned Rodgers, was in agreement over his qualities.

“We know his quality but the decision is not about one game. It is for one season for the perspective," Guardiola stated, whilst the Northern Irishman said: "You’ve got a big English talent there and I’m sure there’s a big career for him."

Unfortunately for Roberts, his final season in Glasgow was hampered by injury, but he still won a total of seven trophies at the club before spending time on loan with Girona, Norwich City, Derby County, Middlesbrough and Troyes.

The winger then eventually settled at Sunderland, where his nine goals and 18 assists in 147 appearances, as well as his overall influence in games, were key in the Black Cats' rise from League One to the Premier League between 2022 and 2025.

In need of more depth in the wide areas last summer, Davies was already aware of what Roberts could bring to the table from their Parkhead days, with the former revealing: "I know the manager - I used to work with him when I was younger, so one phone call from him was all I needed."

Roberts has already made a strong impression at St. Andrew's, and was rewarded by seeing his loan move turned permanent in January.

Despite perhaps not always living up to his full hype, the attacker has already provided some magic moments at St. Andrew's in terms of goals and assists - notably a last-gasp equaliser against Leeds United in the thrilling FA Cup clash on February 15th, even if Birmingham were beaten on penalties.
