Sebastian Berhalter makes ‘promise’ to Middlesbrough fans amid £4.3m man’s tough start to life on Teesside

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Middlesbrough signed Rokas Pukstas from Hajduk Split for £4.3m in the summer transfer window. Boro saw off interest from Birmingham City to land the attacking midfielder, but he has not had the impact hoped with only two senior appearances before injury hit.

The slow start is an obvious source of frustration, but one man backing Pukstas to come good is fellow summer signing and compatriot Sebastian Berhalter. As quoted by The Northern Echo, he expressed that Pukstas needs time to adjust and develop at only 22 before making a promise to the Riverside Stadium faithful.

“Once he gets back from his injury, he’s only going to come back better,” Berhalter said. “He works so hard; he’s one of the hardest-working guys I’ve ever met and he’s going to be just fine, I promise you that.”

Pukstas’ two substitute appearances have brought just 12 minutes on the pitch for Middlesbrough. He was unused on the bench in four Championship games before his hamstring injury hit, so supporters haven’t really had a chance to see what he’ll bring to Kim Hellberg‘s side.

The 22-year-old mainly operates in attacking midfield, offering a box-crashing threat as he powers into the box with well-timed runs to have efforts on goal. He has played in a deeper role before too though, as his physical traits translate to duel success and ability on the ball means he can be trusted in possession.

His well-rounded skillset and physical profile mean he is ideal for Hellberg’s flexible tactics. Expect to see him covering plenty of ground and popping up in both thirds, much like his supportive compatriot Berhalter.

His age means there is plenty of time for Pukstas to grow too. He needs time to get back up to fitness and settle into the Boro side, but Berhalter’s faith is well placed.

Understandably. Pukstas’ limited impact has brought some pessimism from sections of the Middlesbrough support. The fact he barely played before picking up an injury means they are yet to see why £4.3m was spent on him.

However, the success of Boro’s recruitment means that they have good reason to have faith he will come good on his price. Berhalter’s message should only increase the confidence that the American ace can deliver, but it also serves as a timely reminder that he may need time to show his best as he develops as well.

At present, the expectation is that Pukstas will not be able to return from his hamstring injury until December. Hellberg will likely offer a fresh update ahead of Boro’s clash with Bristol City this Saturday.
