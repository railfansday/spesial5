Bristol City urged to give Michael Skubala more ‘time’ by ex-EFL manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Bristol City appointed the 44-year-old in the summer and managed to lure him away from Lincoln City. However, it has been a tough start for him at Ashton Gate and results have been poor.

The Robins are positioned in 17th place in the Championship table after 15 fixtures and they are six points off the play-offs. They lost 5-0 at home against Norwich City last time out before the international break.

Woodman, who has recently been sacked by Bromley in League One, has delivered his verdict on Skubala on BBC’s 72+ podcast: “For me, Michael needs a bit of time to put his identity on (the club). He’s got to find out who works in what system – and I know supporters think he should know that. You can look at them all day in training but when it comes to a Saturday, it’s got to work. He’s probably searching and finding.”

He added: “He felt he took Lincoln probably to the promised land in their respect and he wants to test himself with a bigger budget and a slightly bigger club and I think he will come good.”

Bristol City have won only one of their last seven matches. It has been a rough run of form and pressure is mounting on Skubala.

The Robins turned to the Leicestershire-born man in late May after Roy Hodgson’s brief spell in the dugout. Prior to his switch, he caught the eye at Lincoln City and guided the Imps to the League One title in the last campaign.

Skubala have picked up five wins from his first 16 matches in charge at Bristol City to date. He was handed a long-term contract running until the summer of 2029 meaning he still has plenty of time left to run down on his deal.

He joined Leeds United back in 2022 and worked in the coaching set-up at Elland Road. He also had a stint as their caretaker boss in 2023.

His reputation was high heading into this term and many thought he would succeed with the Robins. Things haven’t gone to plan yet though.

Bristol City aren’t too far off the top eight right now. They still have plenty of time to get things right and get themselves back on track.

The Robins are back in action next Saturday with a tricky away trip to the Riverside Stadium to take on automatic promotion chasing Middlesbrough. Boro are joint-top along with Birmingham City and Stoke City but they are winless in their last two outings.

If Bristol City gave Skubala the chop, there are a few available managers who they could consider. Steve Cooper, Rob Edwards and Steven Gerrard seem the three most obvious candidates they may look at. Other unattached names consist of Paul Heckingbottom, Gary Rowett and Matt Bloomfield, although they probably wouldn’t be much of an upgrade on Skubala.
