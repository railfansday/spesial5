After Finishing GTA 6, I Can Confirm Its Trailers And Teasers Mostly Featured Very Early Moments From The Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

I’ve played over 135 hours of Grand Theft Auto 6. I finished the story and did most of the optional side missions. So trust me when I tell you that the vast majority of the content featured in past GTA 6 trailers, gameplay videos, and teasers comes from the prologue and first two chapters of the game, and there’s a whole lot you haven’t seen yet.

To figure this out, I rewatched the main Grand Theft Auto 6 teasers and trailers released so far by Rockstar, meaning the first trailer, second trailer, extended gameplay trailer, launch trailer, and recently released pre-load trailer. I kept track via a notepad of any time a scene appeared that was from a moment or mission later in the game.
