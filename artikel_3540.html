Peaky Blinders' Tommy Shelby Replacement Has 2 Advantages Over Cillian Murphy's Character

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Before receiving critical acclaim for his Oscar-winning role in Christopher Nolan’s 2023 film Oppenheimer, Cillian Murphy first gained worldwide recognition for playing the iconic and eminently quotable Thomas Shelby in Peaky Blinders. Murphy’s masterclass performance took Peaky Blinders to new heights, making it one of television’s gold standards for period crime drama. For six seasons, he brilliantly portrayed its protagonist, who was introduced as the cunning and formidable leader of the Shelby crime syndicate. The character was a tragic anti-hero who built his criminal empire from the ground up through his ruthlessness, calculating intellect, and magnetic personality.

Earlier this year, Murphy returned as the legendary kingpin in a Peaky Blinders movie, which was written by series creator Steven Knight. The Netflix film fast-forwards to 1940 amidst the Birmingham Blitz, showing an older Tommy living in isolation to hide from the shackles of his past. Peaky Blinders: The Immortal Man ends with Tommy Shelby finally meeting his fateful end after successfully stopping a Nazi agent's counterfeiting operation to destabilize the British economy.

With Tommy officially gone, the Shelby family legacy now rests on the shoulders of his sons Duke and Charlie, who will be portrayed by Jamie Bell and Charlie Heaton in the new Peaky Blinders sequel series. In the original show's sixth season, Tommy learned that he actually has a teenage son named Duke with a gypsy woman, whom he met before enlisting in the war. Fans met Duke again in The Immortal Man, where he became reckless with the Peaky Blinders after his father retreated from public life.

At the end of Peaky Blinders: The Immortal Man, it was clear that Tommy had passed down his throne to Duke, formally legitimizing his title as the titular gang's newest boss. Because of Cillian Murphy's unforgettable leading performance, Jamie Bell definitely has massive shoes to fill as the new face of the popular franchise. However, given Bell's commendable acting reputation, he surely has what it takes to succeed Murphy, especially when his character, Duke, already has many advantages over Tommy.

While Duke shares many similarities with Tommy, like their fierce loyalty towards family and their Romani roots, they are so different in many ways that this may actually serve as Duke’s advantage in his journey to the top. When Tommy began building his empire, he didn’t have anyone important to base his path on. All he had was his fiery ambition and unwavering determination to raise his family’s status by making their illegal bookmaking operations into a protected legal business. With Tommy as his father, Duke has the ultimate blueprint to successfully lead the Peaky Blinders into the new era.

Pushed by the motivation to escape Tommy's shadow, Duke will actively do his best to prove why he deserves his position. The drive to surpass his father's legacy will also prevent Duke from committing the same mistakes as his predecessor. Another advantage that Duke has over Tommy is the privilege and influence that come with the Shelby name. Unlike Duke, Tommy started with no connections and no existing family empire. Thanks to Tommy's cleverness, fearlessness, grit, and sacrifices, the Shelby family and the Peaky Blinders became a respected name and organization.

The BBC's gritty Liverpool underworld crime drama channels Peaky Blinders' tense power shifts while shredding any sense of safety or certainty.

In the upcoming Peaky Blinders sequel series, Jamie Bell's Duke has the chance to utilize these advantages to make a name for himself beyond his father's notoriety, while ensuring that the family legacy lives on. However, starting from the top of the food chain doesn't necessarily mean that Duke will have an easier journey. Inheriting the crown will also come with its own unique set of challenges, as Duke must earn his place at the table and find a way to defend the Shelby empire.

In the new Peaky Blinders show, Bell's Duke will be facing different challenges that Cillian Murphy's Tommy didn't undergo. One of these obstacles is the immense pressure that comes with inheriting Tommy's criminal empire. Duke will have to constantly feel the weight of expectation from people who want him to become like his father, as well as from people who don't believe that he'll live up to his father's legacy.

Unlike Tommy, Duke doesn't have deep psychological trauma from the war that would hold him back. His leadership will be fueled by pure ambition and the desire to surpass his father's legacy. However, will this be enough to make him take bigger risks for the sake of the organization? Experiencing a lifetime of hardship and suffering made Tommy a ruthless leader who isn't afraid of making hard decisions. Meanwhile, Duke's different upbringing might become a challenge for him down the line.

Another challenge that Duke will have to face in Netflix's new Peaky Blinders series is the lack of family guidance. With most of the key Shelby family members gone, Duke will also be leading the Peaky Blinders without the foundational support that Tommy previously had. In the original show, Aunt Polly, Arthur, John, and Ada were very instrumental in Tommy's success. Without that kind of support, Duke will have to rely on himself to keep his family's legacy alive, especially when his younger brother Charles has severed his ties to the family's criminal side.

Peaky Blinders is a historical crime drama created and written by Steven Knight and starring Cillian Murphy, Sam Neill, and Helen McCrory. The television show is based on the gang, Peaky Blinders, a group that banded together after the end of World War I.
