Substitute Teacher Arrested After Alleged Romantic Relationship With Student

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A substitute teacher in Florida was arrested after she allegedly began a romantic relationship with a 17-year-old student.

The Hernando County Sheriff’s Office confirmed in a Tuesday, November 17, press release that they learned of the allegations after receiving a tip on November 5 that substitute teacher Mia Acevedo Gallardo had been in a relationship with “one of her students.”

The sheriff’s office said that Acevedo Gallardo, 20, met the student while she was working as a “long-term” substitute teacher at Grace Education Academy in Spring Hill, Florida, from August 7 until November 4.

Authorities became aware of Acevedo Gallardo’s alleged romance with the student when the teen’s mother found “lewd photos” that appeared to be of the substitute teacher on his phone, according to a probable cause affidavit viewed by People.

Detectives spoke with the student, and he reportedly claimed that he and Acevedo Gallardo had been in a romantic relationship “for a couple of months.” He also claimed that they had sex in the back of her vehicle on October 31.

A forensic review was performed on the student’s phone, and authorities allegedly found “several messages” of Acevedo Gallardo’s explaining sexual acts she wanted to perform on the student.

Acevedo Gallardo allegedly told the teen that she wanted his “first time” to be special, and they discussed having sex in the back of her vehicle.

Acevedo Gallardo quit her job on November 4, and she was arrested one week later on November 11. Following her arrest, she was transported to the Hernando County Detention Center and charged with soliciting/engaging in a romantic relationship with a student as an authority figure, transmission of material harmful to a minor and unlawful use of a two-way communication device.

She is currently being held on a $105,000 bond, according to court records viewed by Us.

Acevedo Gallardo has also been ordered to have no contact with the victim or any minors, and she is prohibited from using electronic devices with access to the internet, according to the court records.

It’s not currently clear whether Acevedo Gallardo has entered a plea or retained legal counsel following her arrest. The Hernando County Sheriff’s Office did not immediately respond to Us’ request for comment regarding updates in the case.

The investigation into the allegations made against Acevedo Gallardo remains ongoing.
