Kat Cunning Warns 'Line of Fire' Fans They Should Be Nervous Before Finale

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Line of Fire still has a long way to go before the end of its inaugural season — and star Kat Cunning teased why viewers “should be nervous” about what’s to come.

“You should be nervous because in Line of Fire, you really never know what’s going to happen,” Cunning, 37, exclusively told Us on Tuesday, November 17, while attending Tony Bennett’s 100th Birthday Benefit in New York City in celebration of the news about the Tony Bennett Institute for Arts Education. “To be honest, I’m even kept on my toes playing Claire.”

Cunning, who uses they/them pronouns, has been just as surprised as the audience, adding, “Every episode that I read, I’m on the edge of my seat. But she’s going to go through more when it comes to love and she’s also going to go through a lot more when it comes to her family.”

Line of Fire, which airs Mondays, centers around a family of agents from various branches of law enforcement who find themselves tangled in a deadly conspiracy. In addition to Cunning, the show stars Peter Krause, Hope Davis, Tommy O’Brien, Taylor Bloom and Charlie Barnett.

“I think the coolest thing about our show is that there’s something that happens every episode — and Claire is often the person solving that mystery,” Cunning told Us. “But also, the long arc of what’s going on with her family is like right now in filming.”

Cunning promised the show is “finally starting to unfold the most dramatic underbelly of the mystery.”

“I am so excited to be learning about her every day. All I can say is keep tuning in because it’s not a one-episode thing,” they continued. “Literally today at work, I was like, ‘You guys, what’s going to happen?’ It continues to surprise us and I love playing Claire because she gets to be somebody who’s like on the case and solving things like the strong woman that she is. But she also gets to have emotions and be bratty and have love surprise her. A lot is coming.”

While NBC’s newest show’s first season is not even halfway finished, Cunning already has ideas for season 2. More specifically, Cunning is hoping their partner Jules Latimer will make a cameo — or they could make an appearance on Latimer’s Fire Country.

“I would love a crossover. I’m trying to be a hot ranch girl in Fire Country. Write me in. And Jules, we’re already pitching [a cameo on Line of Fire],” Cunning told Us. “Don’t you think she could be the chef in the White House or something? I feel like we should absolutely be cross pollinating. In general, I am manifesting a chance to work with my fiancée because I think the world of her as an actor and we haven’t ever done it yet.”

They concluded, “But we’re both tuning into each other’s shows and it’s a crazy thing to be queer characters on network TV in 2026.”

Cunning took a break from set to support Tony Bennett’s 100th Birthday Benefit. During the event, it was announced that Tony’s legacy of love, gratitude and spreading the gift of music would be highlighted with Exploring the Arts (ETA) and Third Street Music School planning to open the Tony Bennett Institute for Arts Education at Third Street (TBIAE).

“‘The Way You Look Tonight’ is one of the songs that made me learn how to sing,” Cunning told Us. “I’m a deep alto and I’ve learned other parts of my voice, But as a singer, those jazz songs are the things that give me my entry point because they’re all style and all swag and no belting — at least at first. So I fell in love with that song.”

Line of Fire airs on NBC Mondays at 10 p.m. ET. New episodes stream the next day on Peacock.

For more details on the Tony Bennett Institute for Arts Education visit www.exploringthearts.org.
