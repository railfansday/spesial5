The Eufy C28 robot vacuum is over $400 off in Amazons Black Friday sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

SAVE $420: As of Nov. 25, the Eufy C28 robot vacuum is on sale for $379.99 at Amazon. That's a 53% discount on the list price.

Vacuuming is enough of a chore on its own without having to mop the floors afterward. If you'd like to eliminate both from your list of house chores, then meet the Eufy C28. This robot vacuum can do both, and Amazon has knocked more than $400 off the price for Black Friday. As of Nov. 25, it's down to $379.99.

The C28 has 15,000Pa of suction and a DuoSpiral brush system that's designed to pick up hair without it getting wrapped around the roller. Eufy says it can handle strands up to 30 centimeters long, which is ideal for homes with big, fluffy pets that shed year-round.
