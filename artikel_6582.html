Jennifer Lawrence Makes the Business Suit Her Own

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Haven’t you heard? Jennifer Lawrence is a certified businesswoman now. The actor launched her haircare brand, Mes, late last month and she’s bringing the boardroom into her closet.

Lawrence left her house today—with a to-go coffee cup in one hand, house keys in the other— looking perfectly entrepreneurial in a long, slim-fitting black blazer and flowy trousers. While the suit itself was a departure from her standard jeand and T-shirt fare, Lawrence found a way to make even a business suit work with her undone, bohemain style.

An expert accessorizer, Lawrence added all of her style signatures to her sharp suiting. Beneath her blazer, the actor wore a lavender long-sleeve tee and a black floral velvet burnout scarf—a switchup from the vibrant pink-and-gold checked Yaser Shaw shawl she wore out yesterday, but a statement scarf nonetheless. Just as classic J.Law were her easy yet luxe burgundy velvet slip-on shoes from The Row. (No matter the season, Lawrence is liable to rock a slide, from her leopard-print Ancient Greek slippers to a pair of embellished Vans.)

But the most Lawrencian touch of all had to be the actor’s boxy black croc Hermès Kelly, which she prefers to wear haphazardly flung open. That’s one way to say, I’m not a regular boss, I’m a cool boss.
