Are the Patriots playing today? NFL Week 11 schedule, AFC standings

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Before Munich, Tommy DeVito had thrown eight passes all season. He may have to throw a lot more now.

The New England Patriots are off in Week 11 after losing Drake Maye to a right ankle injury on the first drive of a 35-10 loss to the Detroit Lions in Germany. Maye was 3-of-4 passing for 48 yards when Aidan Hutchinson sacked him. DeVito took over and went 12 of 24 for 81 yards, adding a touchdown and an interception. Behind him is seventh-round rookie Behren Morton.

The team still hasn't announced the diagnosis. ESPN reported Nov.18 Maye has "some type of fracture in his ankle" and will be out "indefinitely, if not for the season." Coach Mike Vrabel wouldn't confirm anything, but he didn't sound hopeful.

NFL Network reported the Patriots are expected to look at outside quarterbacks during the bye week.

Munich only got worse after Maye left. Wide receiver A.J. Brown left with a hamstring injury. The defending AFC champions are 5-5, losers of two straight and three of their last four.

The division is getting away from them. The Buffalo Bills are 8-1 and lead the AFC East by 3 1/2 games. New England sits outside the AFC playoff picture right now, but does hold a 3-0 record in the division, with a win in Buffalo in October.

Next up is "Sunday Night Football" on Nov. 29 against the Los Angeles Chargers.
