Pick Six: New York Jets at Los Angeles Chargers

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The New York Jets are, embarrassingly, a three-point underdog on Sunday against a team who didn't earn their first win until less than a week ago. The Los Angeles Chargers will be looking to build on their 23-20 win over the Ravens, and the Jets are making the long trip out west to take them on.

Here are six storylines to watch as New York seeks to inject some positivity into their season:

Last week, we discussed the Jets' varying options for their slot cornerback position and they eventually went with one of our suggestions, which was to primarily use Minkah Fitzpatrick there in nickel packages, bringing Malachi Moore off the bench to fill in at safety.

The Jets potentially face a similar dilemma in the slot this week, albeit on offense. Both Omar Cooper and Isaiah Williams have been unable to practice, so if neither can go on Sunday, the Jets will have to make a change.

One option could be to go with the guys currently on the roster. You could reactivate Jelani Woods and Tim Patrick and either run more multiple tight end packages, or use players like Garrett Wilson there. Another option could be to bring up Sterling Shepard or Jamaal Pritchett from the practice squad.

The job share at running back remains a work in progress

The Jets are still trying to figure out how to get the best out of Braelon Allen and Breece Hall now that both are healthy at the same time. Allen's performances while Hall was out proved that he can benefit from a bigger workload. However, Hall brings different things to the table in the passing game, so the Jets will want him on the field more often than not.

Last week was interesting because they employed Hall more in the passing game in the first half with Allen getting more carries. At the half, Allen had six carries and Hall had just two. However, after halftime, Allen carried the ball just twice and Hall had nine carries. It's possible the Jets' thinking was that Hall might produce better after halftime if he was fresh and the defense was tired. In the end, the pair had similar rushing numbers, but Hall produced more as a receiver.

You can probably expect the Jets to keep working on this with one option being flipping last week's gameplan on its head and using Hall early, then keeping Allen fresh to be the hammer in the second half. The Chargers' run defense did a great job against the Ravens, holding them to 16 yards on 15 carries, so it might not be easy to get a running game established.

The Chargers' leading tackler is linebacker Daiyan Henley, but he's been managing a knee issue all season and has been missing practice this week.

Henley, who is also second on the team in defensive snaps played, has been exploited in coverage at times this year, but is still a key contributor to the Chargers' run defense and tends to be the guy who wears the defensive headset.

If Henley can't go, the next man up would be Marlowe Wax, who has played under 100 defensive snaps in his career.

Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert has been struggling in the passing game. A large part of that is that the Chargers have struggled to protect him. Herbert has been sacked 33 times so far this season; more than every NFL quarterback apart from Deshaun Watson. Only two of their starting offensive linemen have started all nine games, which hasn't helped matters.

Last week, the Jets increased Will McDonald's workload as they had promised to, but it meant that Kingsley Enagbare only got to rush the passer a season-low 15 times. With Joseph Ossai now also back in the rotation, sustaining McDonald's level of opportunities might prove tricky. He did respond with a sack last week, though.

The Jets's run defense continues to struggle with two of their starting interior defensive linemen on injured reserve, although one – David Onyemata – was activated for practice this week, so is eligible to return.

New York gave up over 200 yards to the Bills, including a back-breaking 74-yard touchdown in the fourth quarter. The damage could have been even worse had it not been for the efforts of Harrison Phillips, though, as he stopped 10 runs for a gain of four or less.

Los Angeles has the NFL's worst scoring offense but they actually boast a top-10 running game and are coming off a game against the Ravens where Omarion Hampton rushed for over 100 yards for the first time all year. In addition, Herbert gave them a boost with a season-high 51 rushing yards, including his first touchdown.

When the Jets won games last year, a feature of these performances was good production in the return game but that hasn't been happening in 2026. If Williams is out this week, maybe that wouldn't be the worst thing, because he's been much less productive than last season.

In last week's game, the Jets used Isaiah Davis on kickoffs and he did quite well, having always put up solid numbers in the past. If Williams is out, then they may elevate someone like Pritchett to return punts, although the depth chart on the team's official website lists Davis as the backup punt returner too.

The bad news is that the Chargers' coverage numbers are pretty solid, led by ex-Jets linebacker Del'Shawn Phillips who was the NFL's leader in special teams tackles last season. On kickoffs, Cameron Dicker has allowed the third lowest return average in the NFL and their punter JK Scott is the number one graded punter according to Pro Football Focus rankings.
