Alonso issues injury updates on Joao Pedro, Moises Caicedo, Maxence Lacroix, Reece James and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Chelsea boss Xabi Alonso has confirmed Joao Pedro and Maxence Lacroix will both miss Saturday's Premier League clash with Leeds United.

Joao Pedro has not featured since the 2-0 win at Everton on October 17 after suffering a thigh injury and is still not ready to return to team training.

Asked if the Brazilian is fit, Alonso said: "Not yet. He's not fit yet to train with the team.

"He's not going to be available for Leeds. We need to see game by game how he's feeling."

Lacroix will also be unavailable after sustaining a hamstring injury in Chelsea's Carabao Cup defeat to Liverpool last month.

"He's not fit for tomorrow," Alonso said. "Similar situation as Joao."

There was better news on Moises Caicedo, who will be included in the matchday squad despite asking to come off at half-time during Ecuador's win over Bolivia.

Alonso also provided updates on Jamie Gittens and Reece James, with the former back in team training but not yet ready to return, while James remains a few weeks away.

"Jamie is better, he is training with the team, but we need to give him more time," Alonso said.

"Reece is getting there, but still some weeks away."

Alonso also confirmed Josh Acheampong is fit to face Leeds, but Jorrel Hato is set to miss out again.
