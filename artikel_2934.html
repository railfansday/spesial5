Jerry Jones: We've had damning setbacks, but we won't make a coaching change

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Cowboys owner Jerry Jones has stuck with head coach Brian Schottenheimer through a number of painful losses, but Thursday's loss to the Eagles felt like the team reached a new low.

Dallas led 31-10 with 3:35 left in the third quarter, but were outscored 29-7 the rest of the way on their home field by a division rival that was being quarterbacked by a player signed off of the Cowboys' practice squad. Once you throw in the fact that it was Thanksgiving and that the Cowboys squandered a chance to put pressure on the Giants in the NFC East, it felt like it might be the straw that broke the camel's back.

Jones said otherwise after the game, however. Jones said that we can give ourselves a better chance to win these coming games and be competitive in my mind without coaching changes at this time" and said he still believes Schottenheimer can succeed.

"I do. I do. But on the other hand, these are setbacks, and they're damning setbacks," Jones said, via the team's website. "There's no question about it. Especially when we feel like that we can look at parts of the game where it was ours to take and we didn't. And so when you look at every bit of it, then you have to question everything."

Jones has generally shied away from in-season changes at head coach, but it's getting more and more difficult to see enough peaks to stay the course through the valleys that the Cowboys find themselves in every few weeks.
