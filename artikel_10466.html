Suspected poacher electrocuted to death by his own trap as animals avoid it

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A suspected poacher has reportedly been electrocuted to death by a trap intended to kill wild animals, while police are searching for two men who accompanied him on a hunting trip.

The incident occurred in India, near Bhitarkanika National Park, where farmers have reportedly been using illegal traps to kill wild animals that damage their crops.

According to the reports, the deceased was identified as a resident of Bhopal village named Nayak.

His wife, Sabitri, filed a police complaint alleging that villagers had taken her husband out to hunt wild boars at around 7 PM on November 24.

When he failed to return home, his family began searching for him. Local residents discovered his body in an agricultural field the following morning.

According to Ommcom News, Nayak had burn injuries on his hands, while a flashlight, stick, and wire were recovered at the scene.

Subsequent reports suggest Nayak had set the deadly trap himself, but instead the animals stayed clear from it.

The farmer reportedly intended to kill wild boars and spotted deer but forgot to disconnect the electricity before returning to his field to harvest crops, accidentally electrocuting himself.

Police have opened an investigation and sent his body to a hospital for an autopsy. The whereabouts of his two alleged hunting companions remain unknown.

The incident comes just two months after another suspected poacher was reportedly trampled to death by elephants after illegally entering South Africa’s Kruger National Park.

Earlier in 2026, a California millionaire was killed by five elephants during a licensed hunting expedition in Gabon.
