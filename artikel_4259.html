10 Details That Show Rockstar Went Above and Beyond With GTA VI

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

He specializes in lists and guides, with a focus on RPGs, horror games, survival horror, Soulslikes, cozy/sim games, and action-adventure titles. Rockstar has made some amazing games over the years, and one thing that always remains the same is that the team has an amazing attention to detail. The maps are massive, full of life, have tons of activities, and even have insane levels of detail that you would never even think to look at.

A small instance of this can be seen in Arthur Morgan, as his hair grows, can be cut, and even styled. It is easy to see why Rockstar often gets praise from gamers. However, now it is time for GTA VI to shine.

Like before, Rockstar has definitely gone above and beyond with the attention to detail, and this is just part of the reason why GTA VI is a great game.

Ah yes, just when I am trying to get any work done, I turn to my phone, pick it up, and start flipping through social media. Specifically, I start swiping through shorts on YouTube or reels on Instagram.

If you are addicted to doomscrolling through life like many people in the world, the good news is that you can do that in GTA VI. Rockstar has never shied away from emulating life in some aspects of each of their games, and this time, you can use the Snapmatic app in GTA VI to scroll through posts and viral clips.

Of course, this has a connection to the actual gameplay, and no, you can’t become a viral star in GTA VI, but it does show the level of detail Rockstar is going for in the game, as the feed gets updated. Just don’t doomscroll very long.

The wanted system has evolved over the years that the Grand Theft Auto series has existed, but it has never been this detailed. Previously, if you became wanted, you could simply hide, wait out the cops, and leave when they forget you exist.

Now, the cops remember you. They know what you look like, whether you were with a partner, what car you drive, and the clothes you wear. Each of these gets marked, and you need to take care of them if you want to avoid detection.

If they know your outfit, you need to change. If they know your car, you need to get a new one. If you don’t wear a mask, they will know your face. It is a level of detail never seen in the wanted system.

Grand Theft Auto V had a decent number of buildings you could enter, with the total being roughly around 100. Still, when you are living in a city, that number is pretty small and mostly impressive by game standards.

Grand Theft Auto VI takes this to a new level, and it shows how dedicated Rockstar is to going above and beyond with detail, as it is estimated that 70% of the buildings are enterable. The confirmed number by the mapping community based on trailers and available data is sitting at around 400, that is already over four times the number in the previous game.

It is amazing that Rockstar has taken the time and energy to put in so many details and fully flesh out all of these buildings.

GTA Online 2.0 will be coming eventually, and there are some changes that Rockstar should make.

Leonida is based on Florida, and if you have ever been to Florida, you know that there is a lot of wildlife there. Venture outside of the city, and you can find numerous animals living in the wetlands.

Rockstar made sure to go above and beyond when it comes to the game’s animals with the team putting in more than 170 different animal species. This includes over 60 bird species, 30 land mammals, and also various aquatic life and reptiles.

For context, Grand Theft Auto V has around 28 different animal species, and Red Dead Redemption 2 has over 200. Leonida will be just as wild as the real Florida.

The Grand Theft Auto series obviously has a large emphasis on committing crimes, with each game giving you the ability to rob stores, steal cars, and more. However, none of the previous titles have the same level of detail when it comes to committing a crime.

GTA VI has multiple options when committing a crime. Will you wear a mask? Do you want to bring a partner? Are you going to use a silencer on your weapon? This all plays into whether you get caught in the act and how the police respond.

On top of this, other crimes like stealing a car are now more detailed as well, with you having the ability to bust windows or even use tools to pop locks. Some cars even have trackers. You can even use an app to determine a car’s resale value.

It is easy to overlook NPCs in a video game unless you are directly interacting with them. All the people on the street tend to blur together at times, but when you stop to take a look, you will see various NPCs that look alike.

In GTA V, it is estimated that there are around 800 unique NPCs roaming the streets of Los Santos and beyond. However, when tested, players have shown it doesn’t take very long to run into the same NPC but in a different location.

Let’s scale this up to GTA VI. There isn’t an official number yet, but Rockstar has stated that the NPCs will be far more diverse, with they varying in height and weight. They will also have defining details like birthmarks, moles, skin conditions, and more. Even the sizes of their muscles, butts, and chests can differ. That’s a lot of detail.

The weather often gets crazy down in Florida. It is normal for residents of the state to deal with torrential downpours, strong winds, tropical storms, and even hurricanes. In other words, you can’t expect Leonida to not have similar weather.

GameInformer detailed some of the weather effects that will be in GTA VI, and according to them, there will be tropical storms and hurricanes that make “water gush off roof edges and lamposts,” and make it so “everything is dripping wet.”

This means that you will need to unleash your inner Florida Man and run through a hurricane to see what happens. Maybe you will get tossed around. I just hope Rockstar captured the downpour that seems to happen every day around 3pm.

GTA 6 features a completely rebuilt dynamic wind and atmospheric system designed to bring the tropical world of Leonida to life like never before.

Being a Rockstar game, there is a degree of change that can be expected to happen to your character. This didn’t exist in GTA V, but in Red Dead Redemption 2, Arthur Morgan’s weight could change, his hair could grow and be cut, and his face changes.

Rockstar has taken this a step further for GTA VI, as you have two protagonists, and both of them can have their appearance change over time. Overeating causes weight gain, undereating causes weight loss, working out makes your muscles grow, and much more.

The protagonists aren’t just static characters that don’t change with time, they evolve and grow. Their hair also grows and needs to be cut. This level of detail is above and beyond what happens in a normal game.

While GTA V is great, the NPCs in that game are pretty lackluster. When you commit a crime, they mostly switch between two actions; fight or run. Mostly, they run away from guns, car crashes, or whatever else.

The NPCs in GTA VI, however, act much differently. They are more lifelike. They will take out their phones to record you. You can listen in on conversations, and the NPCs will get angry if they spot you. The NPCs will go so far as to remember you.

This means that if you continuously wreak havoc in Leonida, the NPCs will act differently towards you. Some may even be hostile as soon as they see you. A lot of work goes into making NPCs feel lifelike, and Rockstar is going above and beyond with it.

If it wasn’t obvious already, GTA VI is about a couple named Lucia and Jason. The pair are trying to create a better life and get out of crime. A big part of the game revolves around their relationship.

The couple can spend time together, furthering their bond. They can play games like mini-golf, go scuba diving together, and much more. The more they spend time together, the stronger their bond will become.

Rockstar put a lot of detail in just the relationship aspect of the game, with your partner messaging you throughout the day to check in and more. You actually need to care about who you commit crime with, as if you don’t your relationship will fail and affect the story.
