Guinea baboons respond flexibly to changing environmental conditions

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Climate change is affecting the environmental conditions of wildlife around the world: Rising temperatures, changing precipitation patterns and more frequent extreme weather events can lead to important food sources becoming unavailable, at least temporarily. How well animals cope with such changes also depends on how flexibly they can adapt their behavior to new conditions.
