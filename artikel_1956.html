Celtic ‘platform’ can revitalise forgotten star as Man Utd move labelled a mistake amid Bellingham comparison

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Celtic have been hailed as the perfect destination for England international Jadon Sancho to reignite his career and shine again as he continues the search for his next club.

Sancho, 26, is currently a free agent after his contract at Manchester United expired in the summer, but was part of the Aston Villa squad that won the Europa League with a 3-0 win over Freiburg after spending last season on-loan in the Midlands. He also won the Conference League with Chelsea in the previous campaign.

A £73million signing for Manchester United in the summer of 2021, Sancho was viewed as one of Europe’s most promising talents during his time at Borussia Dortmund, scoring 50 goals in 137 appearances and forming a lethal partnership with former Signal Iduna favourites Marco Reus, Erling Haaland, and Jude Bellingham.

However, the pacey winger was never able to replicate his Dortmund form at Old Trafford, culminating in a high-profile public fallout with former Manchester United manager Erik ten Hag over training performances in September 2023. Sancho spent the following three seasons away on loan from the club before being released, but he’s now been urged to consider Celtic as his next destination by former Parkhead icon Paul Lambert.

“Jadon Sancho to Celtic? You could see something like that working,” Lambert told BetVictor. “I don't think he should have left Dortmund for Man United. I thought he should have stayed, like Jude did. Like I said, I don't think Jude Bellingham would be the player he is if he'd stayed in Britain. He made that move to Borussia Dortmund and propelled himself. I think his brother Jobe is the same, a terrific young player.

“Leaving Britain to play in Germany at Borussia Dortmund was the right decision. Would Harry Kane have won the Ballon d'Or if he was still at Tottenham? Absolutely no chance. He went to a club with incredible standards and a great league, and it propelled him. I'd argue with anyone until I'm blue in the face, would Jude Bellingham be the player he is if he went straight from Birmingham to Real Madrid?

“Absolutely no chance. Borussia Dortmund gave him the platform, and Jobe is the same. I saw him play against Bayern Munich at the weekend and he was excellent. Moving abroad makes you grow up, and if you pick the right club, your game improves tenfold. Looking back at Jadon going to Man United at that point, I would have stayed in Dortmund.

“As long as Jadon gets his creative head back on, because he's definitely a threat when he has the ball. He's got to get that back. You could see it happening, and he wouldn't be adverse to it as long as he has the fire and hunger, because he has the ability. He just has to pick the right club to give him that platform to express himself again, that could be Celtic.”
