Virgil van Dijk has suggested that he may be playing the final season of his Liverpool career.

The 35-year-old's contract with the Reds expires at the end of the season and it has been unclear if he will be offered a new one.

The Netherlands international remains a key part of Andoni Iraola's team and has been excellent so far this season alongside Jeremy Jacquet.

But Liverpool will be aware that Van Dijk's drop-off could be coming at any time over the coming months and years, with decision-makers keen to avoid a repeat of the Mohamed Salah situation.

The legendary winger's final year at Anfield was full of disgruntlement amid a public fall-out with Arne Slot, leaving his legacy slightly tarnished when he left in the summer.

With Iraola having replaced Slot, it seems unlikely that the same situation will take place, but the Reds cannot be too sure.

"I will forever be grateful for everything that happened over those years" 🥺Virgil van Dijk has his say on what his future holds with Liverpool 🔴 pic.twitter.com/Y6k8HpsBvC

Van Dijk joined Liverpool from Southampton in 2018 for £75million - a fee that was huge at the time, but has looked like a bargain in the years since.

The defender has consistently been among the world's best, and he continues to excel even in his mid-30s.

But there is an inkling that this could be his final year in the Premier League, and that his time at Liverpool may be drawing to a close.

"At this moment of time, if anyone says that or put out that they know what I'm going to do next season, it's untrue because at this moment of time, speaking to you, I've got no idea either," Van Dijk told Sky Sports in the build up to Sunday's game against Manchester United.

"But I'm relaxed with it because if you know me, I only focus on what I can control and that's at the moment the Manchester United game. Then we'll focus to the Champions League and whatever happens in the new year coming, we'll see then.

"But it's been an incredible journey, almost 10 years this summer and it's been a big part of my life and I'll forever be grateful for anything that happened over those years.

"If it's getting extended, we'll see then. If not, then I'm going to say goodbyes in the summer."

Liverpool will not take great certainty in Van Dijk's comments, with the defender seemingly preparing for his departure.

It appears the Reds have a fight on their hands to convince the 35-year-old to stay, and it will be a worthy fight.

What has always been Van Dijk's best quality is his tremendous reading of the game.

Even at his age, the centre-back has the recovery pace to get back and cut out an attack by simply predicting where the pass will be.

Liverpool have averaged just one goal conceded per game while Van Dijk has been on the pitch this season, with the Dutchman perfect for such a high line.

Per game, the veteran is averaging 7.1 clearances, 1.10 interceptions, 3.5 recoveries, 0.5 blocks and 4.8 duels won, with a staggering 75 per cent win rate that ranks in the Premier League's 93rd percentile.

In the 2020-21 season, a Van Dijk in his prime performed worse in some of the same stats. He averaged just 4.12 duels won to a 63 per cent win rate, and only made 0.24 blocks.

It should be clear to Liverpool that the defender is far from finished, and that a new contract is a must.
