Sandro Tonali is over-rated, 12% stat underlines rotten display in Tottenham loss to Leeds

Sandro Tonali delivered a rotten performance as Tottenham Hotspur were beaten 1-0 by Leeds United on Saturday.

The Lilywhites were outplayed at Elland Road virtually from minute one to minute 90 and never looked as if they get a foothold in the game, with Noah Okafor's second-half strike ultimately proving decisive.

In the centre of the pitch, Tonali struggled to compete and was constantly left on the backfoot as the home side dominated the midfield battles.

The 26-year-old joined Tottenham in a £100million move from Newcastle United but has rarely lived up to that fee so far, despite a recent improvement in form.

Roberto De Zerbi's side had won three Premier League games in a row, but were swiftly brought back down to Earth by Leeds on Saturday.

The Italian will now have two weeks to evoke a response from his team, with the visit of Ipswich Town coming after the international break.

Tottenham signed Tonali hoping that they had finally acquired a destroyer in the middle of the park.

The Lilywhites had so often been accused of being weak in midfield, and spent big money to help change that.

But Saturday was a revival of those criticisms - Tottenham and Tonali were bullied throughout the game by a Leeds side who looked far hungrier.

The midfielder completed only 70 per cent of his passes, misplacing a dismal 11 throughout the game.

He did not create a single chance or take a single shot, and was unsuccessful with his only attempted dribble.

Tonali was dispossessed twice, dribbled past twice - the joint-most of anybody - and made two fouls, but that was not all.

Perhaps most shockingly of all, the Italy international won just one of the nine duels he contested on the day.

Despite Tonali's terrible display, he remained on the pitch throughout the 90 minutes.

His midfield partner, Mateus Fernandes, looked to be faring much better, and made 14 defensive contributions.

Yet the 22-year-old was brought off in the 70th minute, replaced by Rodrigo Bentancur, while Archie Gray was replaced by Conor Gallagher in the midfield.

The decision perhaps raises questions of an over-reliance on Tonali, who clearly still has plenty to prove.
