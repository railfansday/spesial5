RB Leipzig winger Antonio Nusa would be keen on the idea of joining Arsenal next summer, sources have told Football Insider.

The 21-year-old is a long-term target for the Gunners, who may get the chance to sign him ahead of next season.

Nusa is understood to see a potential future for himself at the Emirates Stadium and would be open to making the switch.

Sources say that the wideman views Arsenal as a "very attractive project" at the moment, after their success in recent years.

Mikel Arteta's side are looking to add a new left-sided attacker to their squad within the next 12 months, and Nusa is one of their top targets.

Nusa has been a standout player in the Bundesliga so far this season, and already has 11 goal involvements from just nine games.

Football Insider revealed earlier this week that Arsenal are more likely to "seriously consider" a move to sign Nusa next summer than in January.

Leipzig are determined not to lose their star man in the middle of the campaign, especially while they are still competing in the Champions League.

The German side have set a £66million valuation for Nusa, which is also understood to be prohibitive for the Gunners in the winter window.

However, Arteta's side are much "more likely" to make a move for the Norway international next summer, when they will have more time to negotiate a deal.

Nusa's price could potentially have risen even more by then, if he continues the sparkling form that he has shown in the early stages of the season.

Former PGMOL chief and ex-FIFA referee Keith Hackett told Football Insider on Thursday that referee Chris Kavanagh is being strecthed too thinly across this weekend's games.

The 41-year-old will be in charge of Arsenal's trip to face Newcastle on Saturday, before travelling across to Liverpool to be fourth official of their clash with Manchester United on Sunday.

Hackett believes that the Premier League are asking too much of their officials, given that Kavanagh could potentially be forced to step into Sunday's game if Sam Barrott gets injured.

Arsenal will be hoping that Kavanagh enjoys a controversy-free evening on Saturday, and they can take three points away from St James' Park.
