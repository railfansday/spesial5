It should be quite an atmosphere at the Metropolitano as Atletico Madrid take on champions Barcelona, who are two points ahead of them at the top of La Liga.

Atleti have been in scintillating form in the league, winning seven successive games to start the weekend sandwiched between Barca and their city rivals Real Madrid. They have already beaten one of the El Clasico heavyweights here this season, seeing off Real 2-1 in September, and will believe they can repeat the trick tonight.

Diego Simeone will want a reaction from his team after they lost 2-0 to Bayern Munich at the Metropolitano in the Champions League in midweek, a result that ended their unbeaten home record this term.

The return of midfielder Johnny Cardoso means centre back Cristian Romero is Atleti's only absentee, as they hunt a first home league victory over Barca since October 2021.

Barcelona have been in imperious form this season and beat Aston Villa 3-0 in the Champions League in midweek, but Hansi Flick's side still have a point to prove.

Their only two defeats of the campaign came against Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League and Real Madrid in La Liga, so despite racking up 13 wins and 60 goals in all competitions, they are yet to claim a statement victory.

That would change if they take all three points at the Metropolitano and Barca know what it takes to achieve that, having already beaten Atleti twice in Madrid in 2026.

However, they have already suffered a setback after influential midfielder Rodri injured his hamstring against Villa, ruling him out of the visit to his former club. Of the other injured players, Andreas Christensen, Eric Garcia and Jules Kounde appear to be closest to returning, with Dani Olmo, Roony Bardghji and Xavi Espart longer-term absentees.

We expect Barca to edge a closely-fought contest thanks to their extra attacking quality.
