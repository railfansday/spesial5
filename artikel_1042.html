Aston Villa and Sunderland drew a blank in their Premier League match at Villa Park.

Unai Emery’s team had the better of a game of few chances but failed to make the breakthrough and are now without a win in five games in all competitions.

Sunderland, bidding to move away from the relegation zone, ended a run of three successive away defeats with the 0-0 draw.

Aaron Wan-Bissaka and Emiliano Buendia returned for Villa at the expense of Victor Lindelof and John McGinn, who was on the bench.

Sunderland also made two changes, with Jules Ahoka and Wilson Isidor replacing the injured Granit Xhaka and Brian Brobbey.

The home side were on the front foot from the off and forced a couple of early corners, which came to nothing.

They had to wait until the 19th minute for their first chance, with Johan Manzambi’s low effort saved by Robin Roefs.

The Sunderland goalkeeper had a let-off in the 40th minute when he gifted possession to Buendia but recovered to claim the Villa midfielder’s weak shot.

Ian Maatsen threatened to break the deadlock in the early stages of the second half, but he could only follow an impressive run with a shot straight at Roefs.

Soon after the Black Cats, who created little of note before half-time, forced their first corner but failed to produce anything worthwhile from it.

Villa’s Leon Goretzka was then booked for pulling back Chemsdine Talbi, but Enzo Le Fee’s free-kick evaded Nordi Mukiele and was collected by goalkeeper Zion Suzuki.

As Villa fans chanted for the introduction of McGinn, Le Fee fired narrowly wide.

The home faithful got what they wanted just before the hour mark when McGinn was introduced, alongside Ibrahim Mbaye.

The Scotland international had a chance to open the scoring midway through the second half but fired over.

Sunderland won a free-kick 25 yards out when Goretzka fouled Le Fee, but the midfielder’s effort was straight at Suzuki.

Villa boss Emery made another double substitution in the 73rd minute, with Brian Madjo and Ross Barkley replacing Nicolas Jackson and Goretzka.

The home side pressed for a winner and, after McGinn failed to find the target three minutes later, Barkley forced Roefs to tip over a powerful effort from distance.

Roefs reacted sharply to prevent Mukiele heading into his own goal, then Alysson fired over.

The visitors almost stole a win in the first of three added minutes, but Talbi’s curling right-foot effort went narrowly wide of Suzuki’s far post.
