The Jewish Vote Moves Toward the GOP

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

As a group, American Jews rejected Democrats’ most radical class ever.

An attendee wears a “Trump 47 Jewish Leadership” kippah at the Republican National Convention in Milwaukee, Wis., July 18, 2024.

November 3 was a hard night for Republicans, and I would be less than candid if I wrote otherwise. We lost good friends and principled leaders who stood shoulder to shoulder with the Jewish community for years. We owe them our sincere gratitude, and we will feel their absence.

But if you want to understand what this election really means for American Jews, look past the top-line results and look at whom the Democratic Party just sent to Washington.

Matt Brooks is the CEO of the Republican Jewish Coalition.
