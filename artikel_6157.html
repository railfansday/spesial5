Buy Garuda Indonesia Miles With 100% Bonus (1.08 Cents Each): Worth It?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

GarudaMiles, the loyalty program of Garuda Indonesia, is currently offering a promotion on purchased miles. Through November 29, 2026, you can buy miles with a discount or bonus, and this has the potential to be a great deal.

I’d consider this to be a niche opportunity that won’t have widespread appeal. However, if you do actually want to redeem for travel on Garuda Indonesia (especially in business class), the award rates through the program are excellent, and this can represent an exceptional deal. As always, you should only buy miles if you’ve crunched the numbers for yourself.

Link: Buy Garuda Indonesia GarudaMiles with a bonus

Between 12AM WIB on November 14 and 11:59PM WIB on November 29, 2026, the GarudaMiles program is offering a bonus on purchased miles. The standard version of the offer seems to be for a 100% bonus or 50% discount, structured as follows:

Of course you’ll want to check your account to see what you’re eligible for, as it’s possible that some people may be targeted for better (or worse) offers.

Ordinarily you can buy Garuda Indonesia miles for 2.15 cents each, including all taxes & fees, before any discounts or bonuses.

If you were eligible for the 100% bonus or 50% discount and purchased 250,000 miles, you’d pay a total of $2,687.50, which is a cost of 1.075 cents per mile.

Garuda Indonesia GarudaMiles members can purchase up to 250,000 miles per account per calendar year, and that includes any bonuses. Purchased GarudaMiles are non-refundable and non-transferable, and don’t count toward elite status with the airline.

When Garuda Indonesia GarudaMiles offers a promotion on purchased miles, it’s common to see a 100% bonus or 50% discount, so this offer is in line with the best versions of the offer that we see. The program tends to offer a promotion like this a handful of times per year.

Garuda Indonesia mileage purchases are processed by points.com, meaning they don’t count as an airfare purchase for the purposes of credit card spending.

Therefore I’d recommend using a card on which you’re trying to reach a minimum spending requirement, or otherwise, a credit card that maximizes your return on everyday spending. See this post for more on which credit cards are best for buying miles.

Let me cut to the chase here. The cost of purchasing GarudaMiles during a promotion is exceptional, as you’re paying just over a cent per mile. The catch is that I’d consider the practical redemption opportunities with the miles to be fairly limited, at least from a US-centric perspective.

I wouldn’t buy Garuda Indonesia miles for redemptions on SkyTeam partners, because it’s all just too complicated and tough to book, and there are other programs that are a better bet, like Air France-KLM Flying Blue and Virgin Atlantic Flying Club.

I would buy Garuda Indonesia miles if you want to redeem for travel on Garuda Indonesia, especially in business class. This will almost always represent huge savings over the cost of a cash ticket, assuming you can find availability. For example, below is Garuda Indonesia’s one-way award chart.

As you can see, a Tokyo to Bali award will cost you 63,000 miles in business class, while a Jakarta to Amsterdam award will cost you 110,000 miles. At an acquisition cost of just over one cent per mile, that’s like paying around $680 for the former, and $1,200 for the latter, both of which are great deals.

Do note that Garuda Indonesia redemptions have carrier imposed surcharges, and they are on the steep side right now, given jet fuel prices. For example, a Tokyo to Bali award in business class will cost you $294.60 in cash, beyond the miles.

In the past, one of the best reasons to buy Garuda Indonesia miles was to be able to redeem for first class on the carrier, as that would lead to huge savings. The issue is that the airline seems to have quietly eliminated first class, as it was a dying product for the carrier.

Garuda Indonesia GarudaMiles is offering a promotion on buying miles. Members can purchase miles with a 100% bonus or 50% discount, which gets you a cost of 1.075 cents per mile. That’s a really low acquisition cost per mile.

The catch is that I’d only consider Garuda Indonesia miles to be worthwhile for redemptions on the carrier, rather than for redemptions on partner airlines. If you can find award availability, you’ll almost always come out ahead by buying miles for business class, rather than paying outright. So while I’d consider this offer to be niche, I think it’s still worth being aware of.
