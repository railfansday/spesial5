The Air Canada Aeroplan program is currently offering a bonus on purchased points. While this is a mystery bonus, where different members may be eligible for different offers, it seems the best version is for up to a 100% bonus.

Is there value with this offer? With a specific use in mind, absolutely, in my opinion. Between 12AM PT on November 19 and 11:50PM PT on December 10, 2026, the Air Canada Aeroplan program is offering a promotion on purchased points.

Different members may be eligible for different offers, so you’ll want to log into your account to see what you’re eligible for. However, it appears that the best version of the offer is for up to a 100% bonus, structured as follows:

I should emphasize that other members seem to be eligible for offers of 85%, 90%, or 95%, so it’s no guarantee that everyone will see the 100% offer.

Ordinarily, Air Canada Aeroplan charges $0.0375 CAD (~0.026 USD) per point. Any purchases made with US credit cards and US billing addresses aren’t subject to sales tax either.

If you purchased 500,000 Aeroplan points with a 100% bonus, you’d receive a total of 1,000,000 Aeroplan points at a cost of $18,750 CAD (~$13,101 USD), which is a rate of 1.31 cents (USD) per Aeroplan point.

If you wanted to purchase the fewest possible points at the best possible price, you’d want to buy exactly 90,000 points at a cost of $3,375 CAD (~$2,358 USD), which would potentially get you 180,000 points, for the same cost per point.

While you can purchase up to one million Aeroplan points per account per calendar year, there’s ordinarily a per-transaction limit of 250,000 points pre-bonus. However, during this promotion that has been doubled, to 500,000 points.

Aeroplan points purchases typically post pretty close to instantly, though in some situations, it could take up to 72 hours for points to post. Note that the purchase of Aeroplan points is non-refundable, and Aeroplan points purchases also don’t count toward elite status.

When Air Canada Aeroplan offer a promotion on purchased points, we most commonly see a bonus in the range of 90-110%, but the exact offers do vary. Personally I’d buy points based on when you’re ready to redeem them, rather than the exact cost. After all, the cost to buy points with a 100% bonus vs. a 110% bonus isn’t vastly different.
