Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake Has Become One of the Highest-Rated Games Ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Covering the video games industry since 2017, with experience in news, articles, and reviews. Currently a writer at DualShockers and gaming magazines.

I've been waiting for another excuse to return to Hyrule, and apparently, I won't have to wait much longer! The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is getting a fresh start with its upcoming remake, and if the first reviews are anything to go by, Nintendo might have another all-time classic on its hands.

In fact, the game has already joined a pretty exclusive club of the highest-rated titles in gaming history.

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (2026), a remake of the beloved Nintendo 64 adventure originally released in 1998, has made quite an impressive debut on both Metacritic and OpenCritic, two of the biggest review aggregators in the gaming industry. And considering the legacy the original has left behind, those are some pretty big shoes to fill!

Eiji Aonuma and the development team for the Ocarina of Time remake discussed how one of the original's infamous dungeons is being improved.

At the time of writing, the remake holds an impressive 96 Metascore based on 80 critic reviews on Metacritic, earning a classification of universal acclaim. That puts it among the platform's highest-rated games of all time, with only 34 titles boasting a score of 96 or higher (excluding collections, naturally).

Interestingly, the list already has some familiar faces for Zelda fans, as Breath of the Wild, Tears of the Kingdom, and The Wind Waker are all part of that exclusive group, alongside the original Ocarina of Time, which remains the only game to achieve a perfect 99 score.

And OpenCritic tells a similar story. The remake also currently holds a 96 average score, placing it fourth on the aggregator's Best Games of All Time ranking. It's currently behind only Super Mario Odyssey (97), Baldur's Gate 3 (96), and Breath of the Wild (96), which, truth be told, are masterpieces in their own right!

That's quite an achievement for a game revisiting an adventure that many players already consider one of the greatest ever made. It's especially fitting that the remake arrives as part of the celebrations surrounding The Legend of Zelda's 40th anniversary, giving Nintendo the perfect opportunity to bring one of its most iconic adventures to a new generation.

Of course, this isn't simply a fresh coat of paint. The remake introduces modernized graphics, a free camera system, and even a dedicated jump button, bringing several changes to how players experience Link's journey through Hyrule while preserving the adventure that made the original so memorable.

And honestly, I don't think I'll need much convincing to revisit this particular adventure – and I have a feeling I'm not alone in that. The moment the game becomes available, I'll probably be too busy exploring Hyrule and getting lost in one of my favorite Zelda games all over again. With reviews this impressive, I have a feeling plenty of players will be doing exactly the same!
