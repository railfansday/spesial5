Prediction: Scotland vs North Macedonia

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Seeking an essential Nations League boost on Friday night, Scotland will welcome North Macedonia to Hampden.

While Europe’s elite domestic leagues might be in the midst of November’s international break, there are plenty of eye-catching betting promotions available.

Verdict: Scott McTominay to score anytime

Starting with the hosts, while Scotland might have touched down in North America over the summer with a real party atmosphere in their camp, the Tartan Army drastically failed to find their feet during their iconic World Cup adventure. Limping their way back home without progressing to the knockout stage, new boss Sebastien Pocognoli has a tremendous amount of expectations on his shoulders at Hampden. However, last seen signing off September’s extended international break with a disappointing 2-1 defeat at home against Slovenia, Scotland have shown a string of worrying issues since getting their Nations League charge underway. In fact, Friday’s hosts have mustered just a sole win from any of their first four League B appearances and they find their promotions dreams hanging by a thread. Likewise, the Tartan Army have failed to register back-to-back competitive victories over the past 13 months.

In what is a huge boost for Scotland this month, the Tartan Army will welcome back talismanic midfielder and Napoli ace Scott McTominay. Likewise, Celtic defender Kieran Tierney has also returned to Pocognoli’s Nations League squad.

Now playing his domestic football with MLS outfit San Jose, Angus Gunn should keep his spot between the spot between the sticks at Hampden. However, the duo of Kenny McLean and Scott McKenna are dealing with respective injury issues and won’t play any part this month.

As for the visitors, last seen signing off September’s extended international break with a routine 3-0 drumming at the hands of Switzerland last month, North Macedonia have endured a public Nations League crisis. Looking set to lose their League B status with a whimper, the Lynxes have shown a string of glaring issues and have rightfully come under plenty of public scrutiny. In fact, opening their Nations League nightmare with another 3-0 defeat against the Nati on September 26th, Friday’s guests have lost each of their opening four appearances by an aggregate score of 10-0. Likewise, failing to claim a single competitive victory across any competition since back in September 2025, North Macedonia have gained a reputation for their glaring attacking struggles. In what has become a remarkable rut, Goce Sedloski’s camp have drawn a blank in all of their eight appearances so far this calendar year.

The trio of Nikola Serafimov, Imran Fetai and Stefan Despotovski have all been recalled to North Macedonia’s squad after missing September’s fixtures. Now in his second campaign with Turkish outfit Konyaspor, skipper Ends Bardhi will lead Sedloski’s side out at Hampden and earn an 87th-cap this weekend.

Valencia goalkeeper Stole Dimitrievski will start between the sticks in Glasgow. Despite notching just a sole domestic strike with Greek giants Panathinaikos so far this season, 25-year-old Elmin Rastoder should keep his spot leading North Macedonia’s attack.

With their promotions dreams hanging by a thread, Scotland have a huge amount of pressure on their shoulders when they return to Hampden on Friday night. However, with their sole Nations League victory coming thanks to a 2-0 win away in Skopje on October 3rd, the Tartan Army will certainly be smelling a golden opportunity this weekend. Meeting a North Macedonia squad who have remarkably failed to register a single strike across any competition so far this calendar year, Pocognoli’s squad can’t afford to suffer what would be an infamous slip-up in Glasgow.

Verdict: Scott McTominay to score anytime
