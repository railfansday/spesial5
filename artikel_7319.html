Hearts United Wins $110M Pinterest Media AOR in First Pitch Since Launch

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Hearts United has won Pinterest’s U.S. and U.K. media account following a competitive pitch, headed by Hwy 111 Media, that included both holding companies and independent agencies, the agency shared with ADWEEK.

It’s the first pitch win for Hearts United since it launched in August after Omnicom combined Hearts & Science and Mediahub into a global media network.

It’s a retention for the Mediahub team, which has worked with the visual discovery engine since 2019. Mediahub became part of Omnicom in 2025 through its acquisition of IPG.

According to data from COMvergence, Pinterest’s U.S. and U.K. markets total a combined $110 million in media spend.
