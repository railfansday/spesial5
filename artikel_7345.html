GitLab: Sr. Manager, Support Engineering (Public Sector)

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

GitLab is the intelligent orchestration platform for DevSecOps. GitLab enables organizations to increase developer productivity, improve operational efficiency, reduce security and compliance risk, and accelerate digital transformation. More than 50 million registered users and more than 50% of the Fortune 100* trust GitLab to ship better, more secure software faster.

The same principles built into our products are reflected in how our team works: we embrace AI as a core productivity multiplier, with all team members expected to incorporate AI into their daily workflows to drive efficiency, innovation, and impact. GitLab is where careers accelerate, innovation flourishes, and every voice is valued. Our high-performance culture is driven by our values and continuous knowledge exchange, enabling our team members to reach their full potential while collaborating with industry leaders to solve complex problems. Co-create the future with us as we build technology that transforms how the world develops software.

As a Senior Manager, Support Engineering on our U.S. Government support team, you’ll co-lead a team of engineers supporting complex enterprise deployments in highly regulated environments. You’ll combine people-focused leadership, technical judgment, and business acumen to develop engineers, strengthen our support program, and help customers realize value from GitLab.

You’ll also connect the team’s technical contributions to outcomes that matter across the business. This includes telling a compelling, evidence-based story about how Support Engineering helps protect revenue, enable growth, remove barriers to adoption, and accelerate customer time to value. You’ll help executives and sales teams understand the significance of the work engineers perform and how that work supports customer and organizational success.

Beyond individual cases, you’ll recognize Support Engineering as a valuable source of business intelligence. By bringing together engineers’ firsthand observations, product trends, and recurring customer pain points, you’ll turn field signal into actionable insights for Product, Engineering, Sales, and Customer Success.

Our U.S. Government Support team formed in 2019 and has grown alongside the adoption of GitLab within government agencies. We help customers troubleshoot complex environments and use GitLab’s DevSecOps platform securely and effectively.

Distributed across the United States, we combine synchronous and asynchronous collaboration to support customers and build strong working relationships in a remote-first environment. Our team includes both tenured and newer engineers, bringing diverse technical experience and perspectives.

We’re focused on developing our people, scaling our operations, strengthening crisis management for sensitive systems, and deepening our partnerships across GitLab. As a senior leader, you’ll help make the team’s technical expertise and customer knowledge more valuable to the broader organization, while ensuring engineers have the support, direction, and development opportunities they need to succeed.
