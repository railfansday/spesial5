Targets free Lego holiday event takes place on Nov. 21 — how to score free gift tags and totes

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

TL;DR: Target is hosting a free Lego holiday event on Saturday, Nov. 21, from 12-4 p.m., featuring a Lego Harry Potter: Forbidden Forest demo, free gift tags, and a free festive tote with purchase (plus make-and-take builds at four flagship stores).

If you've begun your holiday shopping — or you're looking for an easy weekend activity to keep the kids entertained — Target is teaming up with Lego for a free in-store event this weekend. On Saturday, Nov. 21, participating Target locations are hosting a four-hour Lego Holiday Event from 12 p.m. to 4 p.m. local time, complete with hands-on building demos, freebies, and gift-with-purchase perks.

Because Target is splitting the activities between standard stores and a handful of flagship locations, here's how it works and what you can walk away with:

If you stop by your neighborhood Target between 12 p.m. and 4 p.m., you can browse Lego holiday gift ideas for everyone on your list and try out a live demo of a Lego Harry Potter: Forbidden Forest set.

You can also grab a free Lego-themed gift tag with no purchase required, and if you buy a Lego set while you are there, you will score a free festive tote bag to carry it home.

Keep in mind that every freebie and make-and-take kit is available only while supplies last, so showing up closer to noon gives you the best shot at snagging the gift tags and totes before they run out.

Before heading out, you can check Target's events page to confirm your local store is participating.
