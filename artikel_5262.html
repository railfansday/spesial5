Washington, D.C., November 6, 2026 — The Digital Sovereignty Alliance (DSA), a nonprofit organization dedicated to advancing clear and ethical public policy, research, and education related to emerging technologies, participated in the Strobe Summit, where DSA Managing Director Adrian Wall moderated a fireside chat with former Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) Chair Christiopher  Giancarlo on the evolving regulatory landscape for digital assets and the future of financial markets.

Wall and Giancarlo also examined the regulatory challenges surrounding prediction markets, perpetual futures, and decentralized finance (DeFi). Topics included the CFTC’s role in overseeing event contracts, bringing offshore derivatives activity into regulated U.S. markets, the potential for 24/7 trading, and establishing accountability for decentralized platforms without traditional intermediaries. The discussion highlighted the need for regulatory frameworks that address market integrity, cybersecurity, customer asset protection, and the operational realities of increasingly interconnected financial markets.

DSA’s participation reflects its commitment to advancing informed policy dialogue and practical regulatory approaches that support responsible innovation, market integrity, and the integration of digital assets into the broader financial system.

The Digital Sovereignty Alliance (DSA) is a nonprofit social welfare organization committed to advocating for public policies that support ethical innovation in decentralized technologies, blockchain, cryptocurrency, Web3, and artificial intelligence. DSA conducts research, organizes educational events, and promotes policies that prioritize public welfare and digital sovereignty.
