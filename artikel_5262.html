XRP price dropped below $1 to around $0.997 amid a broader cryptocurrency market pullback. The decline follows a fall from highs above $1.60, triggering liquidations and weakening retail sentiment. Short-term traders, often described as market tourists, appear to be exiting as volatility increases. However, experienced market participants are watching for a potential recovery, with support near $0.985 and oversold signals suggesting selling pressure may be easing. Meanwhile, institutional interest remains a positive signal. JPMorgan’s latest filing revealed increased holdings in the Bitwise XRP ETF, indicating growing Wall Street exposure to XRP even as retail investors turn cautious. Some market optimists see similarities with previous market cycles, where sharp corrections eventually led to recoveries. Whether XRP can defend the $0.985 support level will be an important test of market confidence.
