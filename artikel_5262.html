Anthropic’s Record $81B IPO Is Here—Will It Test Crypto?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Home » Blitz news » Anthropic’s Record $81B IPO Is Here—Will It Test Crypto?

The Claude maker priced shares at $83 for a $1.5 trillion valuation as Bitcoin dips 1.5% ahead of Nasdaq trading.

Anthropic has priced its initial public offering (IPO) at $83 per share, raising roughly $81.3 billion in the largest public market debut in history—just as digital asset markets pull back and traders weigh whether the historic AI listing will siphon retail and institutional liquidity away from crypto.

According to Anthropic’s official announcement, the Claude AI developer priced its shares roughly 6.4% above its previously marketed range of $70 to $78, assigning the company a valuation of approximately $1.5 trillion. Trading on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT is set to begin Thursday, November 19.

While Wall Street absorbed the massive tech offering, the broader cryptocurrency market traded lower on Thursday morning.

Total crypto market capitalization dropped 1.3% over the past 24 hours to $2.253 trillion, with Bitcoin (BTC) falling 1.5%, the most among the other top 10 biggest cryptos. Bitcoin is currently trading at $63,585.

Anthropic has expanded its IPO to 980 million shares, up from the initially planned 825 million, following exceptionally strong demand from institutional and retail investors.

Priced at $83 per share, the public offering is expected to raise approximately $81.3 billion, surpassing SpaceX’s $75 billion base offering in June and setting a new record.

The company is also raising an additional $26 billion through a private placement of non-voting shares, priced at the same $83 per share. Reported strategic investors include Nvidia, Microsoft, Google, AMD, Broadcom, Micron and TSMC. Combined, the public offering and private placement are expected to generate more than $107 billion.

Retail investors have been allocated up to 20% of the public offering, representing approximately $16.2 billion. Demand has been particularly strong, with total orders reportedly reaching nearly seven times the number of shares available, according to CNBC.

The deal values Anthropic at approximately $1.5 trillion, marking a dramatic increase from its $965 billion private valuation when it confidentially filed for an IPO in June.

Anthropic’s IPO could have implications for crypto markets beyond the AI sector. The $155 billion in combined IPO capital raised by SpaceX and Anthropic within five months highlights growing competition for institutional liquidity, potentially diverting funds from risk assets such as Bitcoin and altcoins.

The listing also gives retail investors a direct way to bet on AI, potentially reducing demand for crypto-native AI tokens that have attracted investors seeking similar exposure.

ANT’s market debut will also be closely watched as a gauge of risk appetite. A strong opening could lift sentiment across speculative assets, while a weak performance could reinforce risk-off sentiment in both equities and crypto.
