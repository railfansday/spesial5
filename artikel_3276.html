A Bunch of Our Favorite Headphones and Earbuds Are Discounted for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

It’s been a big year for audio releases, with major players like Apple, Beats, Bose, Sennheiser, and Sony all dropping new models. While a pair of flagship headphones can cost a premium, Black Friday deals are here. Many of the best headphones on the market are getting discounts, including models that were released just months ago.

Best of all, most of these Black Friday deals are on headphones we’ve tested, including our favorite travel headphones, styles for the gym, and noise-canceling cans. We’ve also included deals on wireless earbuds if you’re more partial to in-ear styles.

There are admittedly a lot of deals to sift through, so these are a few that are especially worth it:

You can check out the best Black Friday headphone and earbud deals below, organized by brand.

The Space 2 headphones are a great option if you’re looking for budget noise-canceling headphones, and they’re under $100.

In our testing, Anker’s Liberty 5 Pro earbuds lived up to the bold claim of delivering the best voice-calling performance, particularly in loud environments. They’re available for $130 during Black Friday.

Soundcore’s X20 earbuds have long been among our favorites for workouts, and they’re a good deal for $60.

The second-gen AirPods Max headphones impressed us with great noise-canceling and the most impressive transparency mode of any over-ear headphones. They’re pricey, but discounted right now.

When we tested the AirPods Pro 3, we found that they were a worthy upgrade over the second-gen, thanks to the better noise-canceling and improved fit. They’re $100 off right now.

The Beats 360 headphones quickly became our favorites for gym use, thanks to the removable, replaceable fabric earcups and headband. The brand new headphones are already $100 off.

If you’re looking for workout earbuds, the Powerbeats Fit are one of the best options thanks to the tactile controls and in-ear wings that keep them in place.

The Powerbeats Pro 2 earbuds are built for workouts, with a built-in heart rate monitor, tactile controls, and a secure behind-the-ear fit.

Bose’s just-released QuietComfort headphones are discounted to $200. They feature upgraded audio performance and customizable noise-canceling modes.

The first-generation QuietComfort headphones are still a great option, thanks to their intuitive tactile controls and customizable ANC modes. They’re over half off in several colors.

Bose’s QuietComfort Ultra headphones deliver exceptional comfort and noise-canceling, plus handy features like an auto-sleep mode when you set them down.

The QuietComfort Ultra earbuds feature the top-tier noise-canceling Bose is known for, and they’re comfortable enough for all-day wear.

You can get a small discount on Bowers & Wilkins flagship headphones, which feature Nappa leather and aluminum details and high-performance audio and noise-canceling.

The Px7 S2e maintains the high-end performance of Bowers & Wilkins’ other headphones in a more affordable package.

Nothing’s over-ear headphones feature a quirky but stylish design, solid audio quality, and fun tactile controls. They’re $170 for Black Friday.

The Nothing Ear (a) earbuds are a great budget option, especially at this price. The case is compact and pocket-friendly, and the audio and noise-canceling performance is surprisingly solid for the price.

It’s a small discount, but you can save on Nothing’s open-ear earbuds, which feature an ear hook design that makes them well suited for workouts.

Sennheiser’s Momentum 5 over-ears were one of the most impressive headphone releases of the year. The noise-canceling was significantly improved over the last generation, and the over 50 hours of listening time is rare for headphones in this class.

The Momentum 5 may be an upgrade over the fourth-gen model, but you’d be hard-pressed to find Bluetooth headphones that sound this good for this price.

Prefer to go wired? Sennheiser’s open-back HD 650 headphones are 55% off and are a great option for reference-level audio.

Sony’s WH-1000XM6 headphones feature great sound and noise-canceling, plus a dizzying number of features. The earcups are foldable, unlike on the XM5 model, making them more travel friendly.

If you’re looking for a pair of clip-on-style earbuds, these are a solid option. They’re $81 off, too.

Sony revived one of its most popular headphone styles with the XM4C, featuring a nearly identical build to the original while adding a few updated features.

Shokz’s earbuds are among our go-tos for runners; the open-ear design allows for awareness of your surroundings, and the secure fit stays put during workouts.

While Sonos’ newest Ace Ultra headphones aren’t on sale, the original Ace model is. You can pick them up for under $250.

Despite their anonymous look and cheap price, EarFun’s Air Pro earbuds are a surprisingly rich-sounding pair of earbuds. They’re a great deal for $80, but a steal for $50.

JBL’s Live 780NC headphones have a stylish monochromatic look that comes in a range of colors with earcups that rotate and fold in for easy storage.
