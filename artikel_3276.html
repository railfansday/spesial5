David Leitch Gives ‘Ocean’s 14’ Update, Notes “Lot Of Motivation” To Make Sequel

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Nearly 20 years later, Danny Ocean is trying to get the gang back together for Ocean’s 14, which was expected to begin filming in 2026.

David Leitch, who is attached to direct the fourth installment in the heist film franchise, recently noted that George Clooney and his Smokehouse producing partner Grant Heslov have delivered a “great script,” but scheduling issues stand in the way.

“Oh, I mean, there’s a great script and I think everybody’s excited about moving it forward,” he told People. “I think it’s always been about getting all those people together, and their schedules are always the issue.”

Leitch added, “Look, there’s a lot of excitement around it and … George and Grant did incredible work on the script. So, yeah, a lot of motivation to make it happen.” Watch on Deadline

Clooney starred as Danny Ocean in the 2001 Steven Soderbergh-helmed remake of the 1960 Rat Pack film, reprising the role in Ocean’s Twelve (2004) and Ocean’s Thirteen (2007).

In January 2025, Leitch was in early talks to direct the next installment. Last October, the 2x Oscar winner Warner Bros. approved the budget for Ocean’s 14, which he expected to begin filming this year.

Ocean’s 14 will mark the first installment in the main franchise not directed by Soderbergh, after Gary Ross helmed the 2018 female-led spin-off Ocean’s 8, starring Sandra Bullock as Danny’s sister Debbie Ocean.

Meanwhile, Bradley Cooper is attached direct an Ocean’s 11 prequel, which will star himself and Margot Robbie as Danny Ocean’s parents.
