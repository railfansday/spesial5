Bakery Tourism Is Booming—These Are The 15 Spots Everyone Wants To Visit

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Your next trip might come with a side of cardamom bun.

Bakeries are no longer just a stop along the way—they’re the final destination. According to Yelp’s 2027 Food and Drink Trend report, bakeries are becoming such a hot commodity that people are planning entire trips around viral treats. Yelp searches for “bakeries near me” are up 561% from last year, while searches for a “sweet treat” are up 303%. Treat yo’ self culture is officially here.

Bakery tourism is on the rise, and honestly, I'm not surprised. I, myself, am prone to walking unfamiliar city streets just to get my hands on the town's most beloved pastry. And people are getting pretty specific about what they want. Searches for cardamom buns are up 24%, kouign-amann 67%, and salt bread a whopping 616%.

It’s not just individual pastries getting all the attention, either. Yelp found that searches for “hot and new bakeries” jumped 359%, while “best gluten-free bakery” rose 113%. Bakeries are also the most-saved type of specialty food shop on Yelp’s My Lists feature. Even New York City's Radio Bakery, which you’ll find below, saw searches increase by 176%. It seems we’re all doing some serious research before our next pastry run.

So which hot spots should you add to your must-visit list? I asked Yelp to do a deep dive into the most-saved bakeries across America to see which ones people are bookmarking. Here's what came out on top.

This famed Taiwanese bakery opened its first U.S. outpost earlier this year in Pasadena, California, and has already gone viral for its unique cream cheese-filled bagels and flaky pastries.

What started out as a pandemic-era creation has now morphed into a line-worthy brick-and-mortar. Bad Butter regularly draws block-long lines, with its breads, croissants, and pastries often selling out.

In Santa Ana, California, 61hundred Bread is known for its signature heirloom blue-corn masa sourdough bread, along with Filipino baked goods like pandesal and ube-filled cruffins.

At this New York City French bakery, the jambon-beurre sandwich is the star item. Fresh baguettes and flaky croissants are also among the other reasons locals and tourists flock to the shop.

Located at the Halepuna Waikiki hotel in Honolulu, Hawaii, Halekulani Bakery is known for its signature coconut cake and assortment of pastries.

Located in Los Angeles, California, Out Of Ordi specializes in Korean-style salt bread.

New York City’s Sunday Morning frequently sells out of its viral cinnamon rolls.

San Diego’s Relic Bakery & Kitchen offers a mix of sweet and savory baked goods, from pies and croissants to breads and danishes.

In New York City, you’ll find Justin’s Salt Bread, voted the city’s best new bakery of the year and known for—you guessed it—its salt bread.

San Francisco’s Butter & Crumble is known for its crumble cakes and laminated pastries, with offerings that rotate seasonally.

Another Honolulu bakery makes the list, although this one comes with a twist. Part bakery, part butcher shop, The Local General Store sells everything from quiche and danishes to sausage and ham.

It looks like my travel schedule just got a little busier (and tastier).
