Who Is Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s Wife Lori Huang?

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang admitted to stepping outside his marriage with a woman believed to be a foreign spy. Meet the woman caught in the middle.

The Nvidia CEO made headlines earlier this month after he admitted to having an extramarital affair with Alice Huang, a woman the US government suspects to be a spy for China’s Ministry of State Security. And while his actions may have posed a threat to national security, it was the breach in Jensen’s personal life that he expressed the most regret over.

“I was unfaithful to my wife. My actions hurt her and our children. There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry,” he wrote in a Nov. 8 statement. “Throughout my life, my family has given me love and support. They deserve my honesty and loyalty.”

“Instead, I let them down and damaged the life we built together,” he added. “I take full responsibility for what I did and the hurt I caused, and I accept the criticism that comes with it.”

And though Jensen resigned from his position, less than two weeks later the tech company’s board ultimately decided to reboot his position as President and Chief Executive Officer while an investigation into the matter continues.

“I am honored to return to NVIDIA and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution,” he said in a Nov. 23 statement. “I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment.”

But “above all, I am grateful for the love and support of my family.”

Sparks flew when Jensen, 63, and Lori, 64, were paired up as lab partners during electrical engineering class while attending Oregon State University in the early 1980s.

“There were, like, 250 kids in electrical engineering, and maybe three girls,” he told The New Yorker in 2023. “I tried to impress her—not with my looks, of course, but with my strong capability to complete homework.”

And it was over doing homework together than Jensen finally gained the courage to ask her out on a date, a piece of their love story he shared he’d never forget.

As he put it in a 2022 press release, “That was the most important, single event of OSU, and of my life.”

In 1985, the couple tied the knot, eight years before Jensen co-founded Nvidia with Chris Malachowsk and Curtis Priem in 1993.

Though Jensen certainly put in the work to get where he is today, he previously credited his marriage to his wife (born Lori Mills) for the success of his multi-trillion-dollar tech company.

“It’s good to have someone in your life who unconditionally believes in you, who believes you’re doing your best,” he told the Oregon State College of Engineering publication in 2014. “You need people like that, and Lori’s always been that person for me—ever since I was 18, she was there believing in me and knowing I could do it.”

He continued, “Even when I had self-doubts, she didn’t.”

After the Huang family—and Nvidia—was shaken by Jensen’s affair, he reiterated his commitment to his and Lori’s decades-long marriage.

“To my wife and family: I love you,” he said in a Nov. 8 statement. “I know an apology cannot undo what I have done. I hope our family will heal and that, over time, I can earn back your trust.”

Jensen and Lori share daughter Madison, 35, as well as son Spencer, 36, together—and they’re both in on the family business.

Madison earned her MBA from London Business School and joined her dad’s company in 2020, where she currently works as Nvidia’s senior director of product and technical marketing, according to her company bio.

Meanwhile, Spencer earned his MBA from New York University and joined Nvidia in 2022, where he works as the company’s director of product management for robotics, according to his LinkedIn.

Lori is president of her and Jensen’s Jen-Hsun and Lori Huang Foundation, the couple’s private charitable foundation, according to IRS filings cited by CNBC.

Established in 2007, the company was estimated to have $9.2 billion in assets in June 2025, per the outlet, and even gave a $50 million grant to build the Jen-Hsun and Lori Huang Collaborative Innovation Complex at their alma mater back in 2022.

In a press release about the sizable donation, Jensen and Lori emphasized the critical role OSU played in their early careers as microchip designers in Silicon Valley after graduation.

“We discovered our love for computer science and engineering at OSU,” they said in a joint statement. “We hope this gift will help inspire future generations of students also to fall in love with technology and its capacity to change the world.”
