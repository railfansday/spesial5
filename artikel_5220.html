Bitcoin Shows Unprecedented Weakness, Price Hits 4.5-Month Low

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Bitcoin (BTC), the world’s largest cryptocurrency, is flashing a major, ‘scary’ technical signal that was absent even during its last major crashes in February and June 2026.

The flagship coin has crashed to a fresh 4.5-month low amid persistent institutional outflows totaling billions from cryptocurrency exchange-traded funds (ETFs).

$BTC hasn't formed a higher high for 16 consecutive days now. This didn't even happen during the Feb 2026 and June 2026 crashes. https://t.co/I6IPmdueEc

Bitcoin has failed to form a higher high for the past 16 days. This is a very long stretch, one that was absent during Bitcoin’s February and June crashes.

A higher high, in the context of technical analysis, is formed when an asset’s price reaches a new high point above its previous swing high. The absence of higher highs signals that buyers are having a hard time taking back control of the market.

The latest Bitcoin price action adds further to concerns surrounding the future of the flagship cryptocurrency.

Earlier today, BTC crashed to $61,225, its lowest price point in around four and a half months.

Bitcoin's failure to create higher highs despite several attempts to recover shows that selling pressure remains quite high.

Nevertheless, this technical pattern of 16 days without a higher high does not necessarily spell more pain for Bitcoin. If the cryptocurrency can decisively push above recent resistance zones, it may be a sign that buyers are starting to make a comeback.

Bitcoin is facing persistent withdrawals of huge amounts of money from U.S.-based spot Bitcoin ETFs.

BlackRock’s iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) witnessed its biggest-ever daily net outflow on Nov. 19.

The crypto fund suffered a whopping $541.1 million in net withdrawals on the day. At the same time, total net outflows from U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs amounted to around $582.3 million across the entire market.

More alarmingly, the fresh wave of withdrawals brings cumulative net outflows from U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs to around $3.47 billion so far this November.

The ETFs have seen negative net flows on 12 of the first 14 trading days of the month.
