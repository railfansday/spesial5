Shiba Inu (SHIB) Stands Just $10 Million Short of Flipping Barry Silbert’s Favorite AI Token

As of Nov. 20, Shiba Inu (SHIB) is about $10 million in market capitalization short of overtaking Bittensor (TAO) for the No. 33 spot in CoinMarketCap's global crypto ranking. TAO has a market cap of $2.18 billion at a price of $186.41, and SHIB has $2.17 billion at $0.000003691 and ranks No. 34.

A gain of about 0.5% in SHIB's price, with TAO's price unchanged, would be enough for the meme coin to take Bittensor's place in the ranking.

TAO is the token of the Bittensor AI network, and Digital Currency Group (DCG) founder Barry Silbert has been one of its most vocal backers. Silbert also runs Yuma, a DCG company that builds on Bittensor.

At Fortune's Brainstorm Tech conference in September 2025, Silbert said that Bittensor was the thing he had gotten most excited about since Bitcoin. "I want to open up the development and the access to AI, and Bittensor is enabling that," he said at the time. Silbert also said that Bittensor is building a "world wide web of intelligence."

On Dec. 30, 2025, DCG's Grayscale filed with the SEC to convert its Grayscale Bittensor Trust into a spot exchange-traded fund on NYSE Arca under the ticker GTAO. The filing came after Bittensor's first halving in mid-December 2025, and at the end of that month, TAO was trading at about $222. "We're pleased to take this early step as we continue to expand the tools available to investors," a Grayscale spokesperson said.

The gap between the two tokens closed because TAO fell. TAO was one of the market's strongest tokens this year. In April, the token was up 55% since January, and in October it rallied again and was up 43% for the year, according to TradingView data.

The November correction erased those gains. On Nov. 20, TAO was trading 16.95% below its January level, and it lost 12.16% over the past week.

SHIB spent most of the year in the red. The meme coin rose about 25% in January, and it has not been in positive territory since February. By Nov. 20, SHIB had lost about 50% since the start of the year, and it fell 13.79% over the past week.

TAO had a 24-hour trading volume of $172.19 million on Nov. 20, and SHIB had $55.17 million, according to CoinMarketCap data. TAO has a maximum supply of 21 million tokens, the same as Bitcoin's, and SHIB has about 589 trillion tokens in circulation.

Shiba Inu developer Mazrael responded to the drop on X on Nov. 17. "1 SHIB is always 1 SHIB," he wrote in reply to a user who pointed to the falling price.
