While deals on consoles and key PC components are in short supply during Black Friday (or simply not good enough to warrant covering), there are numerous fantastic discounts on video games, accessories, and monitors to make up for it. We thought it’d be useful to display all of the deals we think are worth your attention on one page instead of sprinkled here and there in our coverage.

Below, you’ll find the very best discounts we’ve found for PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC players to shop. You can filter the table below to display exactly the type of products you’re looking for.

Both the white and midnight black versions of the DualSense Edge are discounted. Refurbished versions (with the same warranty as new controllers) are another $25 off.

The Nintendo Switch 2 features a 7.9-inch LCD display, magnetically attached Joy-Con 2 controllers, backward compatibility with most original Switch games, and exclusive titles like Donkey Kong Bananza and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Read our review.

The P9 is great if you need more storage for your Nintendo Switch 2. The microSD Express card offers sequential read speeds of up to 800MB/s, but only gadgets with a microSD Express card slot can take full advantage of the highest speeds. That includes certain cameras, tablets, and drones with a microSD USH-I slot.

Donkey Kong Bananza is one of the Switch 2’s first exclusive must-have titles. Its destructible environment allows you to smash your way through each of its 17 levels, and the paths you make will remain intact unless you specifically choose to reset the world. Your companion Pauline’s singing allows you to transform into animals that have abilities that allow you to reach previously inaccessible parts of levels. Read our review.

The Pro 3 features swappable ABXY face buttons and joystick caps, providing players with a highly customizable experience. It also features new L4 and R4 macro buttons between the triggers and its USB-C charging port, along with more durable and power efficient TMR joysticks.

Nintendo’s latest Switch 2 exclusive is the best way to relive what is arguably the best Zelda game of all time. The revamped visuals look gorgeous, and while the game’s improved controls might annoy some purists who still cling to the heyday of ’98, we reckon most folks won’t bat an eye.

The Elden Ring: Tarnished Edition includes both the base game and the “Shadow of the Erdtree” expansion, along with new customization options.

The Fractal Scape gaming headset has a sleek, refined design, with a 2.4GHz low-latency connection and excellent sound for gaming or music.

The Meta Quest 3S is a battery-powered standalone VR headset that has the same chipset as the pricier Quest 3, but with the same lenses and screen resolution as the Quest 2. In addition to being able to play many games from the Meta Store, it can also be connected to a gaming PC to play VR games like Half-Life Alyx. Read our review.

The Nex Playground is a motion-controlled console that connects to your TV and uses a built-in camera and AI to track your movements as you play. Designed for ages five and up, it comes with games like Fruit Ninja and Whac-a-Mole. You can download more games, including titles featuring popular characters like Elmo, with a paid Play Pass subscription.

The World of Warcraft Celebration Pass cuts the price of a 12-month subscription during Black Friday, offering access to World of Modern WoW, WoW: Forever, and WoW: Classic at a discounted rate.

The Witcher 3 lets you explore the war-torn Northern Kingdoms as Geralt of Rivia while preparing to face the mysterious Wild Hunt. The recent remaster adds improved visuals, smoother combat, and better movement, cementing the game’s status as one of the best RPGs of all time.

Clicking the Superstrike feels like using a regular gaming mouse, which is impressive considering there are no mechanical switches inside. Instead, it uses haptics to emulate the effect (to great effect).

One of the most affordable Tandem RGB Stripe OLEDs is even cheaper during Black Friday. The AW3226Q monitor has a 165Hz refresh rate, G-Sync compatibility, better-looking text rendering than most other OLED monitors, and unbeatable contrast.

Make this the year that the gamer in your life has an OLED TV to play their console games on. Despite being affordable, the B6E doesn’t skimp on features.

A gift for the gamer who already has all the gear they need (and who loves to read about gaming when they’re not gaming). The site focuses on well-researched deep dives and oral histories on classic video games.

The 1440p 240Hz refresh rate screen is sleek, but pretty basic for the price. It stands out, though, thanks to its DualSense-charging cradle that extends from its bottom. This is the first discount.

If you’re seeking out a prettier DualSense, the Chroma series of controllers are just a few bucks more than the standard color schemes.

No game has delivered more thrills per minute (TPM) this year than Star Wars Galactic Racer. Podracing is back, and it’s better than ever with this title’s stellar sense of speed, plus it has a surprisingly memorable cast of characters.

Everyone knows gift cards aren’t the best gift, but with such steep discounts happening on Nintendo eShop credit, you can save a lot on a Switch 2, or games and accessories, if you’re alright with jumping through a couple extra hoops.

Halo: Campaign Evolved lets you revisit the original Halo: Combat Evolved campaign with better graphics, updated controls, and improved sound. The well-crafted remake also adds three new missions starring Master Chief and Sgt. Johnson, which take place before the main campaign. Read our review.

USB-C display glasses can make travel (or relaxing on the couch) more fun by putting a huge virtual screen in front of your face. Xreal’s One Pro are some of the best out there at displaying content from your phone, tablet, laptop, or portable handheld.

Nintendo’s latest Pokémon spinoff for the Switch 2 is part Pokémon, part Minecraft, and part Animal Crossing. Yet, despite borrowing gameplay mechanics from each, the cozy life sim is more than the sum of its expansive, building-centric parts. It’s breezy and warm-hearted, with enough secrets to keep you occupied for hours on end.

The MK.2 version of the standard Stream Deck comes with 15 programmable buttons, a swappable faceplate, and a detachable stand.

The latest entry in the long-running survival horror series returned in 2026, with two different styles of gameplay (tense first-person and guns-blazing third-person perspectives) via its dual protagonists. Read our review.
