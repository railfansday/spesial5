Every word Mancini said in first press conference ahead of France and Belgium clashes

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Italy coach Roberto Mancini holds his first press conference in the November break. Follow it live here.

Football Italia is among the accredited media for today’s press conference in Coverciano.

The Azzurri boss has called up 32 players for upcoming matches against France on November 12 and Belgium on November 15.

Matteo Palma has replaced Roma’s Gianluca Mancini, but Juventus defender Federico Gatti has also been added to the squad, receiving his call-up during Sunday’s Serie A match between the Bianconeri and Fiorentina at the Stadio Franchi in Florence.

Alphadjo Cisse, Mattia Liberali, Riccardo Cassano and Simone Lontani have been called up to the senior team for the first time.

However, Cassano and Lontani will train only with the senior Azzurri squad, then rejoin the U18 and U19 squads, respectively.

Italy coach Mancini will explain his line-up choices during today’s press conference, which you can follow live below.

The press conference begins at 13:30 CET [12:30 GMT].

“It’s not normal. It was a clash, and unfortunately, it’s hard to find the words. A 30-year-old man lost his life. I hope it won’t happen again. Surely, it was dramatic, something that has shocked everyone.”

“Usually, in the history of football and national teams, Juventus, Inter and Milan were the teams giving a block to the national team, making the CT’s work a bit easier, and this is why we try to find other players outside, even younger. Obviously, having a block from one team or another would be an advantage. Inter have some anyway.”

On Cambiaso: “We wanted to call him the other time, but he was injured. This time around, he’s not fully fit either, but he can be useful for us.”

“I believe Cisse can play both roles up front, but he’s still very young. But one thing is to see him on TV, and another is to see him live. Zaniolo can play everywhere with his qualities. Attacking midfielder, winger or striker.”

“You can tell that Croci has high footballing quality. Even if he’s born in 2010, he plays with those born in 2007 and he has quality.”

“I have to say that coaches have truly improved in recent years. Many teams play football even when they risk losing games, and this benefits everyone. Having coaches who want to play their playing style, despite risk losing games and even putting their careers in danger is good.

“I’m sorry for Aquilani, who I think was doing a good job. I think he had started really well. De Rossi is doing well. Pisacane is doing well.”

“I watched Lontani in the first game of the season and he had already made a good impression. Then he improved game by game. It looks like he has technique and personality, as shown by the goal in Bergamo.

“Cassano might be a player we missed right now. He must improve physically, but he has the quality for a regista. He plays in a team that plays football in a certain way and they can be important for the future of the national team.”

“The other debutants have been doing well for a while, but obviously they need a chance to play.”

“There are some aspects of the game in Paris which we reviewed, and we can improve. I can’t say which ones, but we can improve.”

“It doesn’t cause embarrassment; surely they didn’t make a good impression.”

“I can say I didn’t like the four games during the break. It’s heavy, especially for the players, because there are two trips, and you don’t have much time to work. Obviously, all club coaches are unhappy with not having players available.”

“I have an excellent relationship with him. We hope the U21 wins the Euros and qualifies for the Olympics.”

“I expect the same thing as everyone else. Perform well and be consistent. Start becoming decisive because time passes by. He used to be a kid, and now he should be a player who makes a difference with the national team.

“Back in 2018, we had more experienced players. Giorgio, Leo, Verratti, Jorginho, were the most experienced ones. Today we don’t have many.”

I like that we are building a group of young players, along with some experienced ones who aren’t here now because they have issues. We played some good games and they are building a nice bond.”

“I spoke to Tresoldi, Ranieri too. But I believe that he must feel that he wants to play for the national team. His father is Italian; he won the Euros with the U21. I think there’s a chance. He knows he can be an important player for the future; we hope he makes the right choice.

“I spoke to Federico many times, also recently. He’s not playing right now and must regain fitness, but he is 29, still in his physical maturity; I think he can return soon.”

“Romano was another guy who was halfway, but picked us [Italy]. He’s young; he needs to play, and he can make mistakes. Nobody is perfect. My regista? Right now we don’t have a regista. Are you talking about someone like Jorginho? We don’t have one. We hope someone can emerge in the coming months. We tried Tonali and other players can adapt.”

“Most of these players follow a pathway in the youth national teams. It’s normal.”

“We have two tough games in three days, so we’ll have to make some changes.”

“There are many players with dual citizenship. Every national team does it. We try to select some players who are Italian and hold dual citizenship. We managed to get Palma who, I believe, can become a very strong defender. We managed him and other players.

“We are pleased with the fans’ enthusiasm. In football, results change everything, but we still need to work, raise the bar and improve as a team, results are not enough.”

“I think he’s a very offensive right-back, I don’t think he can be a winger, but he’s very young so he can become as such. He comes from an injury so it’ll take time to regain fitness, but he has great quality, he is an attacking full-back and we it suits us.

“The young players who are here have given us good signals; we think they can be useful. Liberali is a young guy, with not many appearances in Serie A. He has done well with the Italy youth teams.”

“We know Bernardeschi and Cristante, so we don’t need to call them to try them. When we need players of this level, we’ll call them. Now we need to find different solutions and try to make young players earn experience. Calò is one of the players doing well. Frosinone have 3-4 players who are good. We can’t call players who are already 29 or 30 right now. When we need them, we’ll do it, but not because his agent tells us so.”

“I don’t think [something has changed after the loss v. Bosnia]. It’s quite similar. The percentage hasn’t increased. Some coaches play young players; others don’t. We have the chance to see and know these young players with technical abilities. We call them to assess them because we think they can become good soon.”

“Given how the situation evolved in the first four games, we find ourselves in this position, and we can play for the qualification. The road is still long anyway and there’s a lot of work to do. This is a certainty.”

Even without considering Lontani and Cassano, this team has the youngest average age ever called by Mancini, at 24 years and 171 days.
