Napoli sign agreement to purchase land for new state-of-the-art training centre

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Napoli President Aurelio De Laurentiis has confirmed that he has signed a preliminary agreement to purchase up to 18 hectares of land to build a new state-of-the-art training centre in the Qualiano area of Naples, with work scheduled to get underway in early 2027.

De Laurentiis took to social media on Wednesday afternoon to share images of the signing of the contracts, along with the message: “It’s done, full steam ahead.”

According to Sky Sport Italia, Napoli have agreed to purchase 13 hectares of land in Qualiano with an option for an additional five hectares.

The new Napoli training ground project will include eight pitches, plus two more if they acquire the additional five hectares, a specialist goalkeeping facility, a small stadium for youth team matches, reception and lounge areas for players and staff, office spaces and a pond that will provide water for pitch irrigation.

The works are expected to get underway in early 2027. There are still a few bureaucratic steps to undertake first, though, including gaining formal permission to build upon the plot, which is currently designated as agricultural land.

“Napoli has always had its home in Castel Volturno, where we won two league titles,” De Laurentiis told reporters on Wednesday.

“Have you seen where Maradona trained? Today is definitely a new beginning, because we have completed the first phase and now we can move onto the second.”
