Serie A: Atalanta vs. Inter – probable line-ups, where to watch on TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Lautaro Martinez returns from injury, but is expected to start on the bench as Inter visit Maurizio Sarri’s Atalanta.

It kicks off at 20:45 CET [19:45 GMT] at the Gewiss Stadium in Bergamo.

The defending Serie A champions must cope without Ange-Yoan Bonny and Piotr Zielinski, but Lautaro will be available for the first time in over a month.

The Argentine returned to group training earlier this week, but may still start on the bench as the Nerazzurri face a tight fixture list ahead. Sky Sport and Gazzetta claim that Marcus Thuram and Francesco Pio Esposito will lead Inter’s attack in Bergamo tonight.

Zielinski will be replaced by either Petar Sucic or Curtis Jones, while Benjamin Pavard is expected to start in a three-man defence, letting Manuel Akanji rest.

Home side Atalanta lack depth at the back, missing Odilon Kossounou and Isak Hien, while Jonathan Rowe is expected to start on the left wing, facing off against his English compatriot Djed Spence.

The match is available on TNT Sports 1, DAZN and HBO Max in the UK and Ireland and on Paramount+ in the USA. Football Italia will provide updates via a live blog. This match will kick off right after Milan-Frosinone.

Inter (3-5-2): Martinez; Pavard, Bisseck, Bastoni; Spence, Barella, Calhanoglu, Jones, Dimarco; M. Thuram, P. Esposito.

Atalanta (4-2-3-1): Carnesecchi; Bellanova, Scalvini, Kolasinac, Zappacosta; Kessie, Ederson; Elmas, De Ketelaere, Rowe; Krstovic.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
