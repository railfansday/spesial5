KFC is hosting a fake funeral so GTA 6 fans can take the day off

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

A KFC in Australia is hosting a funeral for a fake person, just so customers have a valid reason to take the day off work and play GTA 6.

With November 19 now just days away, the launch of Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally in reach. In a matter of hours, New Zealand-based players will be among the first to jump into Leonida and experience Jason and Lucia’s story.

As a result, plenty of fans have been plotting time off work, just so they don’t have any distractions. In the United States, the game is expected to cause $1 billion in disruption to the economy, with around 1.5 million people estimated to be taking the day off.

Not everyone has the time booked off, however, and some have been looking for other reasons to ditch work. Well, in Australia, one KFC is hosting a funeral, aimed at giving customers a legitimate excuse for taking the day off.

The restaurant, which is based in Robina, Queensland, is celebrating the life of ‘Ann Alibi’, and luckily enough, the ‘ceremony’ will start right as GTA 6 goes live.

“She was always there for us when we needed her. In sickness and in perfect health,” the event invite says. “By remarkable coincidence, her service will take place on the very same day something 13 years in the making finally arrives.”

KFC also added that “the service will include a hearse, coffin, floral tributes, a book of condolences, an order of service and even a heartfelt eulogy delivered by a surprise guest.”

Anyone who does show up for the ceremony will get free fried chicken, but that’ll mean pulling yourself away from the long-awaited game.

There are other opportunities for GTA fans to get rewarded for playing the game. One company is offering to pay someone $100,000 to be their official GTA VI Researcher.
