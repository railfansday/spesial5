Wolverine PS5 Slashes Its Price for the First Time in Black Friday Sales

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Sony has reduced the price on its biggest first-party game of 2026, Marvel's Wolverine, as part of its Black Friday offerings.

The superhero action title typically retails for $69.99/£69.99 on PS Store, but the digital version of the game has had a decent percentage slashed away.

Until 30th November, you can grab it for $49.69/£52.49, a reduction of -29%/-25%. That's not bad for a major PS5 exclusive that only released a couple of months ago.

In the land of physical games, it's good news for Marvel's Wolverine too. In the UK, the game is discounted at all the main retailers you'd expect; the cheapest I've seen is Amazon, where you can grab it for £33.32.

Some deals are cropping up in the US as well. For example, Best Buy is offering the game for 20 bucks off at $49.99.

Black Friday itself isn't until 27th November, so it's possible the game will be reduced at other places as the day draws closer.

Sony has announced its Black Friday activities, cutting prices on certain hardware and accessories, as well as games and more.

A big part of all that is the PS Store's massive Black Friday sale, which is running now through to the end of the month. We've compiled more than 80 offers we reckon are worth considering — many of the games are cheaper than ever.
