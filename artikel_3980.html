Real-Life GTA 6 Trading Cards Are About to Get Scalped

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

You too can add Maleek Rivers to your collection

If you’re having fun finding GTA 6’s collectible cards while out and about in Leonida, then soon you’ll be able to have fun finding them in real-life.

And based on my experience trying to buy One Piece cards recently, it may actually be a lot easier finding these in the game – especially if you use our GTA 6 guide. (Okay, sorry, I’ll stop with the shameless SEO.)

Panini is manufacturing these in partnership with Rockstar, and they’ll be based on the GTA universe’s fictional sports teams only – no Vicki from the Delights Dancers set, I’m afraid.

There’s no word on pricing, but bundles will be available in four-pack boxes, with a total of 480 cards available to collect across 16 sets. The cards are due out imminently, in December.

As you’d expect of any trading card series, there’ll be some rare pulls to look out for, including team colour-specific parallels for each franchise.

I’ll be interested to see how these sell. Obviously GTA 6 has just become the biggest entertainment launch of all-time, so don’t count anything out – but is there really a market for its roster of fictional sports stars?

Vice City Manatees player Maleek Rivers does feature briefly in the story, where he’s voiced by real-life wide receiver Tyreek Hill, but it’s not like anyone has an attachment to the rest of these fictional teams, right?
