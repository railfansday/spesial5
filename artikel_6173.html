How the Electoral College Dies

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Blue-state activists are about to be perfectly positioned to execute an end run around the constitutional system.

In the wake of the 2026 midterms, we are on the cusp of a total transformation of the U.S. election system. After decades of Republican dominance in state legislatures, Democrats now control enough state governments to push the National Popular Vote Interstate Compact past its 270-vote activation threshold and dismantle the Electoral College from below.

But it seems like the right has barely realized the importance of the fight that’s about to begin.

Inez Feltscher Stepman is a senior policy and legal analyst at the Independent Women's Forum.
