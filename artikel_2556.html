Monday Night Football: Joe Burrow's second TD pass puts Bengals up 17-10

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow has been throwing touchdown passes in bunches recently and he’s up to two in Washington on Monday night.

Burrow found rookie wideout Colbie Young for a 4-yard touchdown while rolling to his right and the Bengals are now up 17-10 on the Commanders with 7:04 to play in the third quarter. It’s the sixth straight game with multiple touchdown passes for Burrow and he threw at least three in each of the first five.

It was the first touchdown catch of Young’s career. The fourth-round pick now has three catches during his rookie season.

The Commanders got the ball to open the second half and moved into the red zone on a 47-yard run by quarterback Jayden Daniels. The lights at Northwest Stadium went out a couple of plays later and the Commanders had to settle for a field goal after an incompletion when the power came back on.
