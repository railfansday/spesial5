Jacob Misiorowski named National League Cy Young winner: Brewers ace takes home unanimous honors

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

Milwaukee Brewers ace Jacob Misiorowski has unanimously been named National League Cy Young Award winner for the 2026 Major League Baseball season. He beat out the Phillies' Cristopher Sánchez and the Braves' Chris Sale.

Misiorowski takes home the honors of the Baseball Writers Association of America vote, which was revealed on Wednesday night. Misiorowski's triumph comes after a stellar age-24 season for the World Series champions:

Misiorowski's WAR of 6.4 ranked third in the NL behind Sánchez and Jesús Luzardo of the Philadelphia Phillies.

Misiorowski was perhaps even more notable for his blazing fastball than he was the dominance of his statistical outputs. While Misiorowski's playoff performance was not part of the Cy Young criteria – ballots are submitted before the playoffs begin – he did post a 1.89 ERA in four starts this postseason for the Brewers, who wound up winning it all for the first time in franchise history.

Misiorowski is now the fourth Brewer to win the Cy Young Award. Corbin Burnes won in 2021, and Pete Vuckovich and Rollie Fingers won in 1982 and 1981, respectively, when the franchise was part of the American League.

Given his level of dominance and relative youth, Misiorowski figures to enter the 2027 season as the odds-on favorite to win the hardware again. If he does, he'll become the first back-to-back Cy Young winner in the NL since Jacob deGrom of the New York Mets in 2018 and 2019.
