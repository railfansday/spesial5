Alienware’s AW2726DM QD-OLED gaming monitor was a great deal even at its original $349.99 price. The low price and impressive specs were enough to convince me to get one at launch, and I’m glad that I did. I’m a little jealous that you have the opportunity to save $70 on the monitor I’ve been using for work and play for several months. Best Buy and Dell are selling the 27-inch 1440p monitor for $279.99 ahead of Black Friday.

If you’re on a tight budget, but want spectacular contrast and brightness control that only an OLED can provide, here’s a deal for you. Alienware’s 1440p 27-inch QD-OLED monitor with 240Hz refresh rate is selling at its lowest price yet. Read our hands-on.

The AW2726DM has more features than other similarly-priced monitors that I was comparing it to, and that still holds true when comparing it to more modern models. It has a 1440p QD-OLED screen that delivers tremendous contrast and deep blacks, and its 240Hz refresh rate provides a high ceiling for my graphics card to deliver fast frames. It’s equipped with HDMI and DisplayPort inputs, and really one of its only flaws is that it lacks USB-C video support. The monitor works great for PC games, as well as console titles. I think it’d be a sweet gift to yourself or to a gamer in your life this holiday.
