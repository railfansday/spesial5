Affirm: Accounting Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

At Affirm, we exist for the moments that matter—giving people a clear, predictable way to pay over time, with no hidden fees, no surprises, and no tradeoffs on what matters most.

About the Finance team The Finance team ensures Affirm remains financially sound and strategically positioned for growth. Our team manages financial planning, accounting, pricing, vendor management, tax, investor relations, and corporate development. We deliver timely insights, accurate reporting, and careful analysis to support decision-making. From day-to-day financial operations to major investments, we enable sustainable, informed growth by maintaining strong fiscal discipline.About the roleAffirm is looking for an experienced Accounting Manager to support and manage our international accounting operations, with a focus on statutory accounting, statutory audits, financial statement preparation, local compliance requirements, transfer pricing, and monthly close activities.

In this role, you will help ensure Affirm’s international entities are supported by accurate books and records, timely close execution, audit-ready documentation, and compliant local reporting. You will coordinate closely with internal Accounting, Tax, Treasury, Legal, Payroll, People, Finance, Technical Accounting and Financial Reporting teams, as well as external auditors, accounting firms, tax advisors, and statutory service providers.

You will bring strong US GAAP and local statutory accounting awareness, excellent project management skills, and the ability to manage multiple workstreams across jurisdictions. The ideal candidate is detail-oriented, highly organized, proactive, and comfortable driving work through cross-functional partners and external providers.

You will be expected to deliver reliable, high-quality results, communicate clearly, and improve processes that make statutory accounting, audit support, and transfer pricing activities more scalable over time.  You are a strong problem solver and communicator who is excited to support Affirm’s continued growth and mission to deliver honest financial products that improve lives.

Employees new to Affirm typically come in at the start of the pay range. Affirm focuses on providing a simple and transparent pay structure which is based on a variety of factors, including location, experience and job-related skills.Base pay is part of a total compensation package that may include equity rewards, monthly stipends for health, wellness and tech spending, and benefits (including 100% subsidized medical coverage, dental and vision for you and your dependents.)

USA base pay range (CA, WA, NY, NJ, CT) per year: $145,000 - $205,000

USA base pay range (all other U.S. states) per year: $128,000 - $188,000

Please note that visa sponsorship is not available for this position.
