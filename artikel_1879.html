Tottenham Hotspur target Hakan Calhanoglu would prefer to stay at Inter Milan rather than move elsewhere next summer, sources have told Football Insider.

The midfielder is out of contract with the Italian giants at the end of the season, but has already entered preliminary talks over a new deal.

However, that hasn't stopped Spurs from showing an interest in a potential free transfer next summer, as well as other Serie A clubs and teams in Turkey.

Sources say that Calhanoglu's "first choice" at the moment is to pen a new deal with Inter, to prolong his stay at the San Siro.

The 32-year-old is understood to be happy and settled in Milan, where he remains a key player for Inter in all competitions.

Calhanoglu notched 19 goal contributions from just 30 games last term, and already has three goals from his opening 10 league games in 2026-27.

Tottenham are long-term admirers of Calhanoglu, but they may be about to see another opportunity to sign him pass them by.

The Lilywhites face an anxious wait to see whether the veteran can reach an agreement over a new deal at Inter, knowing that their chances of swooping in will end if a deal is done.

Spurs are not prepared to pay a fee for the Turkish star at this stage of his career, but would pounce on the opportunity to bring him in for free.

However, Calhanoglu has a strong reputation within European football, and top clubs from across the continent are expected to crowd around him if he becomes available.

For now, there is little Tottenham can do other than sit and wait for the outcome of the contract talks, but it could quickly become a race to a deal if Inter cannot reach an agreement.

Former Tottenham scout Bryan King told Football Insider earlier this week that Luca Williams-Barnett's emergence at Spurs may prompt Mikey Moore to explore a permanent exit next summer.

Williams-Barnett notched a goal and an assist last weekend against Ipswich in his first ever Premier League start, while Moore is struggling out on loan with FC Koln.

King pointed towards the fact that the pair both play in similar positions, and he already feels that Williams-Barnett is a more accomplished player than Moore.

Williams-Barnett's career has blossomed in recent months, and that may potentially have a detrimental effect on Moore.
