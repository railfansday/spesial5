Alan Shearer’s Premier League predictions including Liverpool vs Manchester United

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Alan Shearer is back with his latest predictions as Premier League action resumes after the international break.

The majority of the eyeballs will be on Anfield, where Mauricio Pochettino takes charge of his first Manchester United after replacing Michael Carrick.

The Red Devils are winless in their last eight games, but will hope that their new head coach can inspire them to a famous win against their arch-rivals Liverpool.

Elsewhere, Chelsea and Manchester City will look to get back to winning ways after defeats before the international break, while Thomas Frank begins life at Crystal Palace with a trip to Coventry.

As ever, England legend and Betfair ambassador Alan Shearer has revealed his Premier League predictions to Metro.

I think City will get back on track here. I know it’s sometimes difficult after international breaks, but I saw Fulham against Newcastle and they deserved the point. But I think City will be too strong for them to get back to winning ways, and they need to get back to winning ways here.

I don’t think Sunderland will get a result. I think Villa will have too much for them. I think Sunderland will struggle, you can see that they are clearly finding it difficult with European football.

They’ve a couple of injuries, when Brian Brobbey is not in the team, clearly, they’re not as potent. So, I think Villa will have too much for them on Saturday.

This is a tough one to call. Forest will take big confidence from that performance before the international break against Man City, and deservedly so, the two goals from Liam Delap were brilliant and he can take confidence from those strikes as well.

This is a Difficult one to call it because Bournemouth are flying. Forest got a great result. I’m just going to go for Bournemouth just because of home advantage, but I think Forest will make it tough for them after that win against City.

I think Leeds will make it tough for Chelsea at Stamford Bridge. They know that they’ve not got the speed with Joao Pedro, and I don’t think they’re great defensively. I think it’ll be a very up and down season for Chelsea.

They’ll have a good result one week and a poor result the next, so I think it’s very difficult to predict their scoreline. Just again, because they’re at home, I think they’ll just have enough to beat Leeds on this occasion.

This is a huge game. It could have huge ramifications even so early in the season. Already you could feel as if this is a big, big game for relegation candidates.

It is also a big one for Thomas Frank to go into. Frank Lampard will know the same, and being at home, he’ll be expected to get something out of the game. I’m going to go for an away win, though.

It will be a good appointment Frank at Palace. He has to get back into the game at some stage. I worked with him on the BBC and it’s a good appointment, I think, for Crystal Palace and where they are at this moment.

I think Spurs will take the points. I know they’ve had a big uplift in form from where they were at the start of the season and I think that they can bounce back from the loss to Leeds before the international break.

Spurs are another team that need their centre-forward to start scoring goals, but I still think they’ll have enough for Ipswich at the weekend.

This is a tough one to predict. I’d love to see Newcastle win, but that would be my heart ruling my head. I think it’s a tough game because of the way Arsenal are playing at this moment in time, and the way Newcastle are. I have to say an away win.

The problem is Arsenal are so brilliant defensively. Over the last few years, Arsenal have known that coming to St James’ Park because of the atmosphere is a tough game, and I’m expecting the same, but I just think that Arsenal might nick it by an odd goal.

Brighton have been sensational and the more wins they tick off, the more people are going to have to take them seriously. They’ve beaten City and they’ve beaten Arsenal. So, I do expect them to go to Hull and win.

I think Liverpool will win it. It depends on Mauricio Pochettino, though. Is he going to have enough time to actually get his ideas and his beliefs and his thoughts across?

I think he will eventually, but it will take longer than people think, and for that reason, I’ve got no faith defensively in Manchester United, and I think Liverpool will be too strong for them in both departments.

I think it’ll be a draw. I think defensively Everton might be tough to break down. They’ll set up tough to frustrate Brentford. Brentford are not in great form at the moment and could do with a big result. However, I’m going to stay on the fence and say a point each on this one.
