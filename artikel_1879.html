GdS: ‘The Italian option’ – Milan also considering move for ‘blossoming’ Calvani

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

AC Milan could launch a bid to sign Gabriele Calvani next year, with the young Italian coming into his own in Serie A.

Whilst Milan are becoming more comfortable with buying experienced options, Gerry Cardinale will not abandon the future. So, they’ll continue to try to buy the right players for the future where the opportunity to do so is right; for example, with Sankhoun Diawara.

Gazzetta dello Sport states that Goncalo Inacio is an ‘of course’ option, whilst Virgil van Dijk sits as a ‘why not’. For Calvani, though, the reasoning is more in-depth. He’s not up to the level of his European counterparts just yet, but a promising Italian is something in its own right.

He’s blossomed this year with Frosinone, playing on the right in a 4-2-3-1 set-up. So, there’d be a potential spot for him to replace Fikayo Tomori if he were to depart next year. However, they’d have to deal with Genoa, who are his parent club.

Now could be the perfect time to introduce him to San Siro, allowing him to develop in his own time without needing to push him. For his first season in Serie A, he’s taking to it brilliantly from his pressing to his aerial ability.

They’ll hope to be able to watch him at San Siro this weekend, but it’s tough to know if they’ll be able to, given he’s struggling with a flexor issue currently. Nevertheless, he’s a concrete target; practical and with potential for the future.
