Blackpool FC next manager: Ex-Doncaster Rovers, Barnsley, Leeds United and Hull City men among favourites

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Favourites for the Blackpool job - including ex-Doncaster Rovers, Barnsley, Leeds United and Hull City men

Figures familiar with fans of Doncaster Rovers, Barnsley, Leeds United and Hull City are among the favourites to land the Blackpool job.

The Tangerines have parted company with Ian Evatt, who has departed Bloomfield Road with the club sat 20th in the League One table.

He has been temporarily replaced at the helm by Stephen Crainey, Stephen Dobbie and Steve Banks, who will oversee first-team duties while a permanent successor is sought out.

A club spokesperson said: “Blackpool Football Club can announce that Ian Evatt has departed his role as Head Coach after 12 months in charge.

“During a hugely successful playing career at Bloomfield Road, Ian made over 250 appearances for the Seasiders. In his six years at Blackpool, he played a vital role in the club’s memorable rise from League One to the Premier League, which included two successful play-off campaigns.

“The club thanks Ian for all of his efforts during his time at Bloomfield Road. Steve Thompson will take charge of the team for this weekend’s fixture, along with Stephen Crainey, Stephen Dobbie and Steve Banks.”
