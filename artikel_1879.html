Bognor Regis Town lose at Windsor and Eton - match in pictures

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Goals by Sam Hutchings and Alex Donville put the home side 2-0 up by the midway point of the first half and although the Rocks improved, particularly in the second half, they could only pull one back – through Luke Roberts in the 89th minute – they could not rescue a point.

The Rocks have slipped to third in the Isthmian SC table and will look to return to winning form when they host Littlehampton Town next Saturday.
