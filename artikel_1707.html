Who will start in goal for Arsenal tonight? Mikel Arteta facing tricky decision after David Raya injury blow

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Mikel Arteta is facing a tricky decision between the sticks as Arsenal face Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League tonight, with usual No1 David Raya ruled out.

Raya was forced off with a shoulder injury during Arsenal’s 2-2 draw with Newcastle United in the Premier League. Ruled out of tonight’s game at the Emirates, Arsenal fans are now wondering who will start in Raya’s stead.

Arsenal’s goalkeeping pecking order has been established by Mikel Arteta since the start of the campaign. When available, Kepa Arrizabalaga has been named on the bench - only missing the game with Nottingham Forest due to a knock.

Kepa’s introduction as Raya’s replacement on Saturday further cements his position as first-choice back-up, despite having played the same amount of games as Meslier prior to the weekend.

Former Chelsea man Kepa had been entrusted with the gloves in Arsenal’s first Carabao Cup fixture of the season - a victory against Ipswich - before Illan Meslier was handed a start against Fleetwood Town - the Gunners shocked by League Two opposition.

Since joining Arsenal, Kepa has made 14 appearances - starting every game last year in both the FA Cup and Carabao Cup. That included Arsenal’s run to the Carabao Cup final, with Kepa in between the sticks at Wembley as Manchester City ran out 2-0 winners.

Kepa was also given one run out in the Champions League, helping Arsenal to a league phase win over Kairat Almaty. Arsenal fans may take confidence from the fact that their number two has played 22 games in the Champions League, featuring for both Chelsea and Real Madrid prior to his move to North London.

Meslier has been included on the bench for Champions League fixtures. Arteta generally names two keepers to his substitutes, meaning an academy talent can expect a call-up.
