After looking around for laptop deals, we finally found one that’s worth sharing. Newegg has the Asus ROG Strix SCAR 16 (G635LX-XS97) on sale; it's one of the least expensive RTX 5090-equipped gaming laptops we’ve seen so far for Black Friday week. The $350 discount drops the price to $4,149, with others at ~$4,500 or more.

Hardware-wise, the SCAR 16 comes with a 8p/16e (24 threads) Intel Core Ultra 9 275HX processor with peak boost speeds up to 5.4 GHz, along with 13 TOPS from the NPU. It also comes with 32GB (2x16GB) of DDR5-5600 RAM. While this isn’t the fastest RAM available, it’s

still good enough for high-refresh-rate gaming or productivity. The 32GB capacity is the sweet spot for most users in 2026, so you should be set there for a while. On the storage front, it includes a 2TB M.2 PCIe 4.0 x4 NVMe SSD to store your OS, applications, and plenty of games. It also has a second PCIe 4.0 x4 socket for expansion.

Get a great deal on this flagship-class ROG Strix Scar 16 gaming laptop from Amazon. You'll be able to play most games at high frame rates or even run your own local AI.

The RTX 5090 laptop GPU comes with a 150W TDP plus 25W MHz dynamic boost. Asus lists the clock speeds of this pixel-crunching device at 1,647 MHz using the full 175W envelope for dynamic boost. Otherwise, the clock speed is limited to 1,597MHz. And of course, it comes with the full complement of 24GB GDDR7, easily running today's (and tomorrow’s!) games at WQXGA 2560x1600 (16:10 aspect ratio). It’s also ideal for running large AI models, too. The 240Hz Mini-LED display covers 100% of the DCI-P3 color gamut and has a 3ms response time. So it’s good for productivity and gaming purposes.

For ports, the laptop offers three USB 3.2 Gen2 (10 Gbps) Type-A ports, two Thunderbolt 5 80 Gbps ports (w/DP support), and a single 3.5mm combo audio jack. On the networking front, you get 2.5 GbE and fast tri-band, 320 MHz Wi-Fi using the Intel Wi-Fi 7 BE200. The SCAR 16 comes with a chiclet-style keyboard with per-key RGB and a touchpad.
