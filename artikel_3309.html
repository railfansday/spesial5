Skydance Chief Marketing Officer Rebecca Mall Sets Leadership Team, WBD’s Meg Maro To Depart

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Chief Marketing Officer Rebecca Mall is the latest member of Skydance co-CEOs David Ellison and Ynon Kreiz’s senior leadership team to unveil the structure of her unit a month after the completion of the Paramount-Warner Bros. Discovery merger. She did it in a company memo, which you can read below.

With Mall at the helm, Skydance will move to a centralized marketing operation, a change from the previous setup at Paramount and WBD where the various business divisions had their own CMOs.

It is not a complete departure as Mike Benson, head if TV marketing; Shauna Spenley, head of DTC marketing; Josh Goldstine, head of Film marketing; will continue to report to their respective business leaders, George Cheeks, JB Perrette and Dana Goldberg & Josh Greenstein, respectively, with a dotted line to Mall.

Also new is the creation of a Franchise and Kids & Family marketing unit led by Nickelodeon’s Angie Sharma. Watch on Deadline

Departing in the restructuring is Meg Maro, WBD’s Global Head of Franchise, who will leave at the end of year.

Hi everyone,At Skydance, we’re building on more than a century of extraordinary storytelling and some of the most iconic brands and franchises in entertainment. We have so much to be proud of, and I truly believe our greatest opportunities are still ahead of us. With this in mind, I’m excited to introduce the marketing leadership team that will help us harness the full creative power of this company and set a new standard for our industry.Great storytelling is at the heart of everything we do. And marketing plays an essential role in bringing those stories to the world – capturing people’s attention, sparking their curiosity, building anticipation and creating the kind of excitement that turns audiences into lifelong fans. We have the ability to amplify the extraordinary creativity across our company, strengthen our brands, expand our reach and forge deeper connections with audiences around the world. When we get it right, we don’t just promote great entertainment. We help make it part of the cultural conversation.That’s what makes this next chapter so exciting. We have an incredible opportunity to build a more unified, innovative and effective marketing organization – one that breaks down silos, understands our audiences better than anyone and harnesses the power of creativity, data and technology to set a new standard for our industry. Our ambition is not simply to market what we create, but to help drive the growth and success of our entire company.To achieve this, we’re bringing Cross-company Marketing and Corporate Brand, Data & Analytics, Media, Partnerships, and Franchise and Kids & Family together under one roof. This structure will allow us to harness the full strength of Skydance – bringing our best ideas, talent, technology and resources to every business and campaign. By working more closely and strategically across teams, we can move faster, execute more effectively and deliver greater impact across our businesses, all while making it easier for our partners to work with us.Below are the leaders who will help us deliver on these ambitions:Annah Zafrani will lead Cross-company Marketing and Brand, coordinating priority campaigns across our businesses and leading corporate brand marketing. Katie Hutchings, SVP, Brand Marketing, Creative and Strategy, and her team will move into this more centralized group.Angie Sharma will lead Franchise and Kids & Family, building one plan per franchise across content, consumer products, games, and experiences, with Kids & Family as a key growth priority.Jamal Salmon will lead Data & Analytics for marketing, connecting audience insights and measurement across businesses so teams can better understand our audiences and quickly act on what’s working. He will dotted line to Josh Goldstine, President of Worldwide Marketing & Distribution, Hugh Williams, Chief Engineer and EVP, Consumer and Monetization Engineering and Josh Walker, Chief Financial and Strategy Officer, DTC.Michelle Hagen will lead Brand Partnerships, coordinating brand and marketing partnerships across our businesses under one enterprise strategy. Julie Moore, EVP of Brand Partnerships will dotted line into Dana Nussbaum, Co-Head of Global Motion Pictures Marketing, Warner Bros., with a focus on theatrical, and Malene Sam, SVP, Originals, will dotted line into Shauna Spenley, with a focus on TV and DTC.Seth Goren will lead Media, bringing together media strategy, media buying, and agency management to get the most of our marketing investment across the company. Theatrical media will dual report to Dana Nussbaum; DTC media will dual report to Shauna Spenley; and TV will dual report to Mike Benson.Equally critical to our success, as the other business leaders have laid out, Mike Benson will lead TV marketing; Shauna Spenley will lead DTC marketing; and Josh Goldstine will lead Film marketing. Each brings deep expertise, creative vision and a proven ability to develop campaigns that break through and deliver results. They will continue to report to their respective business leaders, with a dotted line to me, maintaining clear accountability for their organizations while working in close partnership with our centralized marketing team. This approach will give each business the dedicated leadership and focus it needs while ensuring we are fully aligned, sharing our best ideas and leveraging the collective strength of Skydance.Lastly, I want to recognize and thank Meg Maro for her leadership as the Global Head of Franchise. Meg will be departing at the end of year, and I want to thank her for all she has built and contributed during her time with the company.As we bring our teams together, I encourage all of us to learn from one another, share ideas openly and draw on the tremendous talent and experience across this company. We’ll continue to listen, learn and evolve, adjusting along the way to ensure we’re setting ourselves up for success. We have an extraordinary opportunity to redefine what marketing can achieve in our industry, and I can’t wait to see what we accomplish together.The future is bright!Rebecca
