Apple TV’s ‘MobLand’ Meets ‘Ocean's 11’ Heist Thriller Officially Gets New Look From Next Episode

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

As the year winds down, so too is Apple TV's slate of originals for the year. The prestige streamer still has a few exciting titles to fill out the schedule in the run-up to the holidays, though, with Season 2 of the Owen Wilson-led comedy Stick and the debut run of the Liev Schreiber and Stephen Graham serial killer thriller Nocturne keeping viewers coming back with new episodes. This week, McG's adaptation of The Way of the Warrior Kid will also bring the heartfelt thrills led by Chris Pratt to wrap up the platform's high-profile film slate. In the midst of it all, Anthony Mackie and Jamie Dornan's game of cat-and-mouse is quietly reaching its halfway point, and the two may be about to collide as the snow begins to fall.

Apple's new heist thriller 12 12 12 pits the two stars against each other across three separate timelines of an elaborate bank robbery as FBI Agent Jack Strickland and criminal mastermind Paul Galvin. After the epic raid of a vault beneath the streets of Zurich, the disgraced Strickland is on a tireless mission to capture Paul and his brother, Bobby (Jack Kesy), on the streets of Europe, though that's easier said than done. Despite questionable loyalties in the days after the heist and a missed detail that could've blown the case wide open, the sibling duo has thus far managed to evade capture. In Episode 4, however, as Jack and Paul narrow in on the same informant, a tense run-in awaits.

Ahead of the premiere, Collider can exclusively share a new sneak peek that picks up on Christmas Eve, with Strickland away from his family. Holed up in a Marriott hotel, he reminisces with his sister in the late hours of the evening just before the clock turns over to twelve. Though he claims to be going on a late run, in reality, he's up for work, and a glance at the street below offers a hint at what, or who, he's looking out for. Paul Galvin and his associate are in a bind, as they need to get a train moving and quickly, lest they wind up stuck in the city. The good news is that nobody works on Christmas... or so they think. As Strickland's sister implores him to call their mom for the holidays and try to get better rest, he spots his criminal mastermind right in front of him, and he isn't about to lose the opportunity to catch him then and there. If he pulls it off, there'll be nothing stopping him from getting home for Yuletide celebrations next year.

The clip illustrates the drama behind the tense, action-packed hunt for the Galvin brothers, showing how it's taking a toll on Strickland and the people who love him as the days pass in 12 12 12. Dudi Appleton and Jim Keeble brought the story to life, having previously co-created the Rob Lowe-led crime comedy miniseries Wild Bill. Alongside the headlining duo of Mackie and Dornan, they had a strong ensemble to work with, including Agathe Rousselle, Sallie Harmsen, José Garcia, and Kali Reis, among others, as well as a BAFTA-winning director, The Falcon and the Winter Soldier's Kari Skogland, behind the camera for multiple episodes, including the pilot. Altogether, they make up a series that Collider's David Caballero called "a very strong heist thriller that doubles as the perfect holiday viewing" in his review, praising Mackie and Dornan's performances in particular for carrying the thrills forward.

12 12 12 Episode 4 premieres on Wednesday, November 25, on Apple TV. Check out our exclusive sneak peek in the player above.
