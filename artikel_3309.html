Nicolas Cage’s Terrifying Serial Killer Thriller Officially Comes to Netflix

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In his long-spanning career, Nicolas Cage has given us numerous amazing movies across various genres, including Western, drama, comedy, and superhero. The actor keeps his audience engaged by consistently delivering a range of diverse characters, be it a fictionalized version of himself in The Unbearable Weight of Massive Talent; playing a down-on-his-luck superhero, Ben Riley, in Spider-Noir; a toxic boss who also happens to be Dracula in Renfield; or playing the titular character in David O. Russell’s biographical drama Madden, his latest film.

One of his most iconic roles in recent years is the titular serial killer in Osgood Perkins' Longlegs. The 2024 psychological horror-mystery, written and directed by Perkins, is among the most eerie and borderline disturbing features in the past decade. The film follows FBI Agent Lee Harker (Maika Monroe), who is assigned to an unsolved serial killer case that takes an unexpected turn, revealing evidence of the occult. Things take a darker turn when Harker discovers a personal connection to the killer and now must stop him before he strikes again.

Perkins created an unsettling atmosphere not by traditional jump scares, but through sensory manipulation, framing, and uncanny character design. The vast empty spaces in frames let fans check every shadow in the corner for dread. Cinematographer Andres Arochi uses muted grays, washed-out browns, and harsh winter lighting to strip away any sense of comfort. Cage played the character with an eerie feel under heavy pale prosthetics, a ragged blonde wig, and vocally gave Longlegs an erratic shift from whispers to screams to make him more unpredictable. The marketing of the film kept his evil avatar under wraps, so fans were scared firsthand in theatres.

The film proved a commercial success, earning approximately $128 million worldwide on a $10 million budget. It further earned critical acclaim, garnering an 85% Rotten Tomatoes rating, with critics who collectively agreed, “Saturated in disquieting mood while leveraging a nightmarishly gonzo performance by Nicolas Cage, Longlegs is a satanic horror that effectively instills panic.” Fans who’d love to check out the feature or revisit it are in luck as Longlegs is making its way to Netflix this December, as per ComicBook. The film also features some compelling performances from Monroe as Lee Harker, Alicia Witt as her religious mother, Ruth Harker, Kiernan Shipka as Carrie Anne, among others.

Check out Longlegs on Netflix starting December 10. Stay tuned to Collider for more such updates.
