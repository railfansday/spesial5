Miss Universe 2026: Puerto Rican Social Worker Becomes First Mom to Win Crown

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The latest beauty to win the Miss Universe crown is a Puerto Rican-born psychologist and social worker with a daughter.

Gorgeous 27-year-old Jennifer Barreto became the first mother to ever be crowned Miss Universe during Tuesday's 75th annual 2026 international pageant at Coliseo de Puerto Rico Jose Miguel Agrelot in San Juan, Puerto Rico.

Barreto was handed the crown by Fatima Bosch -- Miss Universe 2025 -- and what a crown it is. The eye-popping tiara -- named "The Radiance of an Eternal Legacy" is newly designed by George Wittels and Maison d'OR and features 3,150 diamonds set in 18-karat white gold. WOWZA!!!

And Barreto looked absolutely amazing walking around the stage with the crown perched on top of her head.

Barreto beat out 4 other Miss Universe finalists from other countries, namely the Philippines, New Zealand, Venezuela, and the Dominican Republic. Contestants from over 116 countries and territories competed in the 2026 pageant.
