After testing it, we gave the Classic a firmness rating of 8/10, and found it was best suited to back and stomach sleepers, thanks to its full body support and light contouring.

The side sleeping members of our testing panel found the Classic didn't provide enough pressure relief across the shoulders and felt it was too firm.

If you're a side sleeper, we recommend the DreamCloud Classic Hybrid Mattress instead.

It's one of the best hybrid mattresses we've tested, with the inclusion of robust coils and dense foams providing the right level of 'give' for side and back sleepers.

According to DreamCloud, the Premier is the brand's most popular memory foam mattress — and, after sleeping on if for the past few weeks, I can see why.

Despite being rated as firm by DreamCloud, I place the Premier as closer to medium-firm. That's mostly down to the inclusion of the plush Euro top, which offers blissful body cradling support when lying on my side and back.

It also comes with American Chiropractic Association approval, so you can be reassured that it boasts healthy back support.

Of course, mattress firmness is subjective. For context, I weigh in the region of 150lbs but lighter people who predominantly sleep on their sides will be better suited to the more luxurious Luxe below.

The Euro top is topped with the same breathable CloudQuilt cover you'll find on the Classic above. While not cool to the touch, I haven't once woken up hot on the Premier, despite it's dense structure. Once I've finished testing I'll report back with a full review, but this could be a contender for our best memory foam mattress guide.

If you mostly sleep on your side and appreciate a mattress you sink right into, the DreamCloud Luxe Memory Foam Mattress is a great option. At 14" tall and packed with high-density foams and specialist cooling features like phase change material.

But don't be fooled by the marketing jargon, our lab tests showed a fairly average temperature increase, while our tester reported waking up feeling hotter and sweatier than usual. (If you sleep hot, I recommend the Premier above).

But if you sleep predominantly on your side, the Luxe is comparable to the best hotel mattresses you can buy. And under $1,600 for a queen in the current sale, it's also excellent value for money.
