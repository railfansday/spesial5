6 Upcoming Prime Video Series, Ranked by Hype

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Chris is a UK-based film and TV writer, editor and occasional filmmaker. For more than a decade, he has written news, reviews and features covering movies, television and pop culture. He has also made short films and worked in video production, giving him experience on both sides of the screen. Chris runs the We Love Movies More Than You TikTok channel and has a particular interest in overlooked and short-lived TV shows.

2026 has been another busy year for Prime Video, with fresh original series like Young Sherlock, the Reacher spin-off Neagley, and Off Campus, all of which will return for another season. There were some surprises with the short-lived Spider-Noir starring Nicolas Cage, and The Boys wrapped up its run with the expected divided fan opinion that most big shows seem to receive.

The year might be winding down, but there’s still plenty of unmissable television coming to Prime Video in the months ahead and well into 2027. From adaptations of iconic games and the first live-action miniseries in the Blade Runner franchise to an underrated mystery thriller that deserves more attention, Prime Video’s upcoming lineup has something for everyone.

After three movies and the recent Netflix animated show, Tomb Raider is finally getting the live-action series treatment. Game of Thrones’ Sophie Turner takes on the iconic role of archaeologist-turned-adventurer Lara Croft, and she’ll be joined by Jason Isaacs, Martin Bobb-Semple, and Sigourney Weaver. Developed and co-created by Phoebe Waller-Bridge (Fleabag) with Chad Hodge, the series’ plot remains as hidden as a lost artifact, but we do know it won’t be an origin story for Lara Croft — she’ll be in full action mode when the series starts.

Famed for her sharp writing and grounded characters, Phoebe Waller-Bridge is an unexpected yet welcome creative force for a new take on the beloved game franchise. The gritty 2018 game reboot heavily inspired the most recent movie version starring Alicia Vikander. However, based on the earlier images released of Turner’s Croft, it looks very much like the character's original 1996 design, so we could be in for a nostalgia-driven globe-trotting adventure. Production is reportedly nearing competition, and the series is set to launch sometime in 2027.

Having recently celebrated the 20th anniversary of its first edition, Criminal finally brings Ed Brubaker and Sean Phillips’ celebrated comic books and graphic novels to life. Lifelong friends Leo (Charlie Hunnam) and Ricky (Gus Halper) were both born into a life of crime, but a murder from their childhood pushed them in different directions. Leo became a master thief, and Ricky followed his father’s footsteps down a dangerous path. Years later, a heist gone bad reunites them, bringing family secrets to the surface, and Ricky's volatile older brother, Tracy (Luke Evans), back into their lives. At the same time, Ricky's girlfriend Mallory (Emilia Clarke) worries his growing recklessness can only end in tragedy.

Mirroring the graphic novels, the series will be released in two parts: ”Coward” arrives on January 13 with the first four episodes, and "Lawless" lands two weeks later on January 27 with the remaining four. Unlike other adaptations where the original creative team isn’t actively involved, Brubaker is co-showrunner and has promised fans a faithful adaptation. Based on the trailer, he’s made good on that promise, with a character-driven thriller full of tense heists and plenty of double-crosses. With interlocking stories and Adria Arjona, John Hawkes, and Richard Jenkins among the already impressive cast, Criminal could be Prime Video’s next big hit.

On the surface, The Devil’s Hour looks like an intense crime drama with a hint of the supernatural, and while that’s a fair assessment, Tom Moran’s fiendishly clever series is so much more. Social worker Lucy Chambers (Jessica Raine) has woken up at 3:33 am for most of her life, and her disturbing dreams and visions pull her into a string of murders and disappearances that defy explanation. She crosses paths with incarcerated criminal Gideon Shepard (Peter Capaldi), who claims to know her, telling her they’re connected in impossible ways. As the mystery deepens, Lucy begins to lose her grip on what’s real, and time is running out to find answers.

The Devil’s Hour is an ambitious series, and it would be unfair to spoil its many twists and genre-blending turns, but if you're a fan of Netflix's sublime sci-fi series Dark, you'll be right at home here. Devised as a three-season story, the final five episodes promise to deliver a satisfying ending to what has consistently been one of Prime Video’s most underrated originals. If you haven’t seen it yet, now is the perfect time to catch up before the third season is released on December 4.

The Boys franchise rewinds to the 1950s for this stylish prequel series that charts the early days of Vought International, the mega-corporation that manufactures superheroes. Jensen Ackles reprises his fan-favorite role as Soldier Boy, yet he’s not the jaded, morally bankrupt character we know and love from The Boys. He’s just starting his superhero journey, full of hope and optimism, but that soon changes when he meets Clara Vought (Anya Cash), long before she became the diabolically evil Stormfront.

The final season of The Boys revealed the true depth of Soldier Boy and Stormfront’s relationship, which Vought Rising intends to explore further, along with the dark origins of the serum that makes superheroes: Compound V. With its 1950s setting laced with film noir and Vought’s blood-soaked beginnings, the series carves out a retro chapter that showrunner Paul Grellong calls a “twisted murder mystery.” There’s no fixed release date, but Vought Rising will very likely drop on Prime Video in 2027.

The wait is almost over, as Blade Runner 2099 is about to pull us back into a world of Replicants, neon lights, and bold fashion choices. Showrunner Silka Luisa (Shining Girls) jumps the story five decades on from Denis Villeneuve’s sequel, where Los Angeles is unrecognizable after a replicant uprising left humans second-class citizens. Hunter Schafer plays Cora, a human on the run and forced to take on the identity of a Blade Runner. She teams up with Olwen (Michelle Yeoh), a dying replicant Blade Runner with only days left to complete one final mission. Their search for runaway replicants unearths a mystery that will shake the neon-lit city to its foundations.

Ridley Scott transformed Philip K. Dick’s existential novel into one of the most influential sci-fi movies of all time, so expectations are high for the first live-action series in the Blade Runner universe. The trailers tease a more vibrant Los Angeles than we’ve seen before; the sun shines even if the streets are no less dangerous. Blade Runner 2099 blends breathtaking action scenes with the emotional and philosophical edge that helped define the franchise. It's easily one of the year’s most eagerly awaited Prime Video originals. Also starring Dimitri Abold, Tom Burke, Lewis Gribben, Amy Lennox, and Matthew Needham, all eight episodes will arrive on November 25.

Adapting the two Santa Monica Studios Norse mythology-era games in the bestselling franchise, the God of War live-action series is now back in production after suffering a major setback. Months after the first-look images of Ryan Hurst as Kratos and his boy Atreus (Callum Vinson), Hurst had to drop out of the series due to an on-set injury that left him with a torn bicep. Shortly after, Dave Bautista stepped into the role and has since undergone intensive training to bulk up to play the towering Kratos.

There’s always a risk when beloved games get the television or movie treatment, as they don’t have the most consistent track record. However, with sci-fi legend Ronald D. Moore (For All Mankind, Battlestar Galactica) serving as showrunner, fans can trust that the source material will be treated with the respect it deserves. At the heart of the series is the fierce yet complex bond between father and son, as Kratos tries to school Atreus in survival while they both reckon with the horrors of their past. With a strong emotional core and a pantheon of monsters promising epic battles, there’s every reason to be excited about the God of War series. There is currently no fixed release date, and given the production delay, it might not hit Prime Video until late 2027 at the earliest.
