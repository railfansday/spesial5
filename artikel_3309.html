Blink-182 and Pierce the Veil Lead When We Were Young 2027 Festival Lineup

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

When We Were Young is coming back in 2027. The fifth iteration of the pop-punk and emo music festival will be headlined by Blink-182 and Pierce the Veil, while Limp Bizkit, Third Eye Blind, 5 Seconds of Summer, and many more will also perform in Sin City next year.

When We Were Young 2027 takes place on Oct. 16 in Las Vegas. This will be Blink-182’s second time headlining following their 2025 appearance. Other bands set to appear include All Time Low, Simple Plan, Sleeping With Sirens, Hot Mulligan, State Champs, and Portugal. the Man. Solo artists Michelle Branch, Natasha Bedingfield, and Ashlee Simpson are set to perform as well.

Circa Survive will rock out as part of their reunion tour, and so will New Found Glory, who recently lost their guitarist Chad Gilbert in September after battling brain cancer. The first batch of tickets for the festival are available on Friday, Nov, 20 at 10 a.m. PT via a passcode-protected presale (to access the presale, head to the festival’s official website). The general sale continues the same day at 11 a.m. PT, so long as any tickets remain following the presale.

In February, WWWY organizers announced they were cancelling the 2026 edition of the festival. “After an unforgettable run in Las Vegas, we’ve decided to take 2026 off to give this festival the care it deserves and to make sure what comes next feels just as special as what came before,” they said without providing specifics. “This isn’t goodbye — it’s just a pause.”
