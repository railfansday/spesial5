When Is ‘Rivals’ Season 2, Part 2 Out? Full Release Schedule and How to Watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Rutshire’s messiest residents are back for more drama.

The shoulder pads are back, the champagne is flowing, and the drama in Rutshire is showing absolutely no signs of slowing down.

If you've been counting down the days until Rivals returns, you're certainly not alone. Disney+'s gloriously scandalous adaptation of Jilly Cooper's best-selling novel has given us everything we could possibly want from a period drama: outrageous affairs, ruthless power plays, spectacular ‘80s fashion, and a cast of characters who are almost incapable of behaving themselves.

But rather than dropping every episode at once, Disney+ has split season 2 into two parts, leaving viewers with a wait between installments.

So, when exactly does Rivals season 2, part 2 arrive, and how many episodes are left? Here's everything you need to know.

The wait is almost over! Rivals season 2 part 2 will premiere on Disney+ on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, with three brand-new episodes landing at once.

That's right, we'll be getting a triple helping of Rutshire scandal before the series switches to weekly releases for its final three episodes.

The second season consists of 12 episodes in total, with the first six having aired earlier this year.

And following THAT devastating mid-season cliffhanger, there's plenty of unfinished business to get through.

Disney+ will release the remaining six episodes across four Wednesdays, with the season finale arriving in December.

So, you'll have three episodes to binge on November 11, followed by one new episode every Wednesday until the finale.

*Warning: Spoilers for season 2, part 1 ahead!

When we last visited Rutshire, things had taken a devastating turn.

Lady Monica Baddingham (Claire Rushbrook) was killed in a car accident after discovering that her husband Tony (David Tennant) had been having an affair with Maud O'Hara (Victoria Smurfit).

And with Tony now grieving, the battle between Corinium Television and rival company Venturer looks set to become even more ruthless.

Meanwhile, Rupert Campbell-Black (Alex Hassell) is facing some difficult decisions of his own, particularly when it comes to his relationship with Taggie O'Hara (Bella Maclean).

According to Disney+, the second half of the season will see affairs unravel, alliances fracture, and long-buried secrets threaten to come to light.

Expect plenty more ’80s excess, from lavish parties and the Hampshire Hunt Ball to Bonfire Night celebrations, and a particularly chaotic Christmas.

There are also some new faces joining the drama, including Rachael Stirling as Monica's younger sister Araminta Pemberton, Rupert Evans as headmaster David Hawkley, and Santiago Cabrera as Argentinian polo player Alejandro Mendoza.

In other words, Rutshire isn't about to get any quieter.

There's good news for anyone already dreading the season 2 finale.

Disney+ has officially renewed Rivals for a third season, with another 12 episodes on the way.

While there's no confirmed release date just yet, at least we know the scandals, romance, and spectacularly questionable decisions aren't ending with season 2.

Both seasons of Rivals are available on Disney+ and Hulu.

All eight episodes of season 1 and the first six episodes of season 1 are available to stream now, meaning there's still time to catch up before part 2 arrives.

And if you've somehow managed to avoid the drama until now, consider this your invitation to enter the wonderfully chaotic world of Rutshire.
