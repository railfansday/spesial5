The Fidget Game: Senior Head of Marketing

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

We're both an educational company and a toy company. We make multisensory educational board games for Pre-K through Grade 6, built on the science of reading and aligned with math standards, plus collectible toys, squishies, and unboxing products kids love to open, trade, and share.

Founded by a former teacher with six years of classroom experience across six countries, The Fidget Game has grown into a fast-growing consumer brand:

Our next chapter: becoming a household name in children's educational games and toys.

We're hiring a Senior Head of Marketing to lead our marketing department, strengthen our brand, and drive profitable growth across educational games, sensory toys, and collectibles.

This is a hands-on senior leadership role covering both strategy and execution. You'll set the plan, direct the team, and own the results, so our founder can step back from day-to-day marketing decisions.

What you own vs. what you influence. You own marketing strategy, creative direction, campaign and launch priorities, team performance, and the approved marketing budget. The Head of Growth owns the marketing KPIs and paid buying, marketplace teams run Amazon advertising and channel budgets, and the CEO approves the overall budget and major investments.

Hit agreed targets for revenue, acquisition, retention, and profitability.

Have repeatable systems running for launches, creators, organic social, and creative testing.
