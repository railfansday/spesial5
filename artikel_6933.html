The MacBook Neo Is on Sale for the First Time Ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Grab this early Black Friday deal before it sells out.

I am not the first person to point out that MacBooks are expensive. I will not be the last. If you monitor this stuff like we do, you’ll have seen the irony that Apple released the budget-conscious MacBook Neo earlier this year—only for the global computing shortage to almost immediately raise the price from $599 to $699.

But the Neo is still cheap. You could buy it for full price and get your money’s worth—although scoring it on sale makes the whole thing even sweeter. Just in time for Black Friday, Amazon shaved $50 off of the buzzy MacBook. Chalk it up to a Christmas miracle.

Designed as Apple’s answer to the lower-budget laptops running Windows, the Neo’s target audience is technically students—hence the good branding and cool colors. That being said, if you need a personal computer for watching Netflix and doing your taxes but don’t want a full desktop, it’s a perfect choice.

The Neo runs on Apple’s A18 Pro chip—the same one that powers the iPhone 16 Pro and Pro Max. If the idea of buying a laptop that runs on a phone chip gives you pause, your stress is better channeled elsewhere. While this isn’t the right device for someone who is planning to run heavy-duty software, it can handle standard Internet browsing, video calls, and the like with ease. Just keep in mind that it has only 8 GB of RAM, so it’s best suited to basic workflows.

Of course, this computer has the added benefit of four jazzy color options: blush and citrus for the bold, indigo for those more subdued, and silver for that classic MacBook aesthetic. Wrapped in an aluminum body with a 13-inch Liquid Retina display, the Neo weighs in at just 2.7 pounds (same as the MacBook Air) and is 0.5 inches thick. (For reference, the iPhone 17 Pro Max is 0.35 inches thick.)

Despite our best efforts, we don’t quite know how Apple determines its deals for big sale events, so there’s no telling if this $50 discount is as good as it’s going to get for Black Friday. In all honesty, we were hoping Amazon would shave $100 off to get the Neo back to its pre-increase price. In our wildest dreams, we’re gunning for a deal that would make this the first-ever sub-$500 MacBook.

We’re not giving up on a deeper discount once Black Friday proper arrives, but for now the Neo is undoubtedly a smart buy for anyone who wants a laptop that can handle all of the basics. At $649, it’s still a fraction of the cost of the MacBook Air and Pro.
