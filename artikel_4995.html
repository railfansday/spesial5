Bitcoin to $1M by 2030? ‘Almost certainly not,’ says Dragonfly’s Qureshi

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Dragonfly’s Haseeb Qureshi says Bitcoin could reach $400,000 by 2030 but doubts it will return to $100,000 by year-end with the rout in AI stocks weighing on crypto.

Bitcoin is unlikely to reach $1 million by 2030 — though it could reach $400,000 — according to Dragonfly managing partner Haseeb Qureshi.

“The amount of capital that would have to come into crypto to get to million-dollar Bitcoin is an astronomical sum,” Qureshi tells Cointelegraph’s Trade Secrets. “The idea that you could get that amount of capital in five years or four years is just not plausible.”

A former professional poker player and software engineer at Airbnb, Qureshi now backs crypto startups at Dragonfly, which launched a $650 million fund in February.

Qureshi also says he doubts Bitcoin will return to six figures by year-end, linking the current crypto sell-off to the rout in AI stocks.

Nvidia (NVDA) shares closed 4.3% lower on Thursday after the chipmaker announced co-founder Jensen Huang’s resignation as CEO following a series of scandals. Huang reportedly had an affair with a suspected Chinese spy. Beijing denied the espionage allegation and President Donald Trump has expressed doubts too.

Nvidia is the leading AI chip supplier and accounted for 4.66% of the MSCI All Country World Index at the end of October, second only to Apple.

Bitcoin fell alongside Nvidia shares in the week Jensen Huang stepped down as CEO. Source: TradingView

“Crypto is not the main story this year for financial markets,” Qureshi says. “Crypto is downstream of these other stories.”

Qureshi says investors grew concerned after Democrat sweep in the US midterms that new data centers would be harder to get approved. With high oil prices and inflation already weighing on the economy, the prospect of weaker AI investment prompted investors to cut exposure to riskier assets, including Bitcoin, he says.

That leaves crypto “largely along for the ride” through the year-end.

Crypto has spent much of 2026 in a bear market despite Bitcoin’s 43% recovery in the third quarter. Bitcoin traded near $65,000 on Monday, almost 50% below its October 2025 all-time high.

Bitcoin is far away from its $126,080 all-time high recorded in October 2025. Source: CoinGecko

The US Senate failed to advance the CLARITY Act in September, stalling the industry’s push for federal rules for crypto markets. In October, suspected North Korean hackers stole about $6.3 billion from Binance in the largest crypto theft on record.

Over the next 15 to 20 years, Qureshi says Bitcoin could compete with gold as a store of value outside government control.

New deposits or advances in mining could add to the supply of gold, he says while Bitcoin’s protocol caps its supply at 21 million units.

“There will never be an asteroid that you will find a bunch of Bitcoin on.”

However, Qureshi sees a risk that AI could discover a flaw in the mathematics that secures Bitcoin transactions.

Bitcoin uses digital signatures to verify that a transaction was signed with the corresponding private key. If a future mathematical breakthrough makes it possible to forge those signatures, attackers could move coins without their owners’ permission.

Such a threat is often discussed alongside quantum computing risks. A powerful quantum computer could derive a private key from its corresponding public key using Shor’s algorithim, putting coins with public keys visible at risk of theft, which includes some of Satoshi Nakamoto’s early holdings.

In October, OpenAI said one of its models had found a faster way to solve certain logic problems, challenging a conjecture known as the Exponential Time Hypothesis.

Qureshi says a future AI-assisted discovery could break the cryptography Bitcoin uses to verify who can spend coins, undermining its case as a store of value.

“That, I think, is what you should price in as the chance this all breaks, this all fails,” he says.

Dragonfly has a history of backing innovative projects, and led stablecoin project Ethena’s $6 million seed round in 2023. Ethena had about $4.9 billion of its dollar-linked USDe token outstanding at the end of September. The protocol uses derivatives to offset price swings in the assets backing USDe. Some of the income those positions generate goes to users who stake the token.

Users who stake Venice’s VVV token can mint DIEM, which they can stake for daily credits to run AI models.
