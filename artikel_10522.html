Runaway horse visits McDonald’s before leading police on neighborhood chase

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A runaway horse in Virginia made an unexpected trip to McDonald’s before leading police on a chase through a residential neighborhood.

On November 17, police in Colonial Heights were called to a McDonald’s after a horse named Rolo was spotted wandering along a busy road.

The horse had escaped from Hickory Haven Equestrian in South Chesterfield after managing to roll underneath an electric fence.

Speaking to 12 On Your Side, owner Ashley Moyer explained that she believes the restaurant’s lights and the presence of people may have attracted Rolo.

“McDonald’s is a pretty straight shot,” she said.

Moyer also revealed that this wasn’t the first time the horse had escaped from the equestrian center, joking that “Rolo is a repeat offender.”

Two Virginia State Police troopers who were heading home from work encountered the horse while local officers were already in pursuit, joining efforts to bring the animal to safety.

Dashcam footage released by Virginia State Police shows officers following Rolo as he makes his way through the area before eventually being cornered at Shepard Stadium.

Officers used lassos to secure the horse, with one officer helping to keep him calm until he could be reunited with his owner.

“Using a little teamwork, some lassos, and the power of friendship, everyone worked together to safely corner and wrangle our four-legged runaway away from traffic,” Colonial Heights Animal Control said in a Facebook post.

Rolo’s owner was alerted to his escape after receiving several phone calls in the middle of the night, followed by a police officer knocking on her door.

The horse even received a police escort on his journey back to the stables.

“He just kind of hung out and walked behind. We had a police escort on the way back … and he seemed to enjoy his walk,” Moyer said, as reported by People.

In a Facebook post, the equestrian center thanked the officers involved in Rolo’s capture, joking that despite escaping his enclosure, the horse had been a law-abiding road user.

“The good news… Evidence shows that Rolo obeyed all traffic laws on his road trip, even coming to a complete stop at the sign,” the center said.
