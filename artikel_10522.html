Pokemon manga writer reveals he also wrote Christian ‘Manga Bible’ series

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Longtime Pokemon Adventures writer Hidenori Kusaka has revealed that he also worked on a manga adaptation of the Christian Bible, publishing the series under his real name.

Since 1997, Kusaka has been responsible for bringing the stories of Pokemon’s mainline games to life in manga form, starting with Red and Green before moving on to subsequent generations.

However, catching Pokemon wasn’t the only subject the author was tackling throughout his career. In addition to writing for one of the biggest entertainment franchises in the world, Kusaka was adapting the Old and New Testaments into manga.

On November 27, an X user reached out to Kusaka, asking whether he had worked on a manga depicting the life of Jesus Christ that credited Hidenori Kumai as its writer.

Kusaka responded by confirming that the two authors were actually the same person.

“That’s right. ‘Hidenori Kusaka’ is a pen name; my real name is ‘Hidenori Kumai,’” he revealed.

“I’ve also done work and written books under the name Hidenori Kumai, so check those out. I also worked on the comic adaptation of the Bible you mentioned.”

The book, titled Manga Messiah in English, was published in North America in 2007 and in Japan by the Japan Bible Society in 2008.

Featuring artwork by Kelly Kozumi Shinozawa, the 288-page Japanese edition adapts the four Gospels, chronicling Jesus Christ’s life, miracles, crucifixion, and resurrection.

However, Kusaka’s contributions to Christian manga extend well beyond the story of Jesus.

The Manga Bible series spans six volumes, with Kumai credited for adapting biblical stories ranging from the creation of Adam and Eve and Moses leading the Israelites out of Egypt to the reign of King David and the events of Revelation.
