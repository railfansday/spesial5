As a retiree, I'm not concerned with the number of consecutive down years, but prolonged, secular bear markets. Since 1970, a 60/40 AA had a max drawdown of 36%, with many lasting 10-14 years.
https://portfoliocharts.com/portfolios/ ... portfolio/

OP, look at diversifying your portfolio within and across asset classes. AAs with lower volatility and small/shorter drawdowns help mitigate SORR. You can compare your portfolio against others here:
https://portfoliocharts.com/charts/portfolio-matrix/

That said, the Trinity Study showed 50/50 AA, rebalanced yearly, will last 60 years at 3%. 3% is an extremely conservative withdrawal rate.
