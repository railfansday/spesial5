What It Really Takes to Run High Mileage Without Giving Up Your Life

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Between meetings, commutes, family obligations, and recovery, it can be tough to fit high-milage training into an already-full schedule.

You shouldn’t have to choose between chasing your running goals and showing up for the rest of your life.

On this week’s episode of The Amazing Runner’s World Show, Aly and Jeff discuss how to fit a high-mileage training schedule into your busy life.

In the previous episode, we talked about how the wrong running shoes can sabotage your speed.

Jeff is Runner-in-Chief for Runner’s World and the director of product testing. He has tested and reviewed running shoes, GPS watches, headphones, apparel, and more for nearly two decades. He regularly tests more than 100 pairs of shoes each year, and once had a 257-day streak running in different models. Jeff can usually be found on the roads, racing anything from the mile to a marathon, but he also enjoys racing up mountains and on snowshoes. When he’s not running, you’ll probably find him hanging from a ladder making repairs and renovations to his house (he’s also director of product testing for Popular Mechanics).

Aly is the Director of Content Hype at Runner’s World. She started running over a decade ago and has run 8 marathons, plus hundreds of shorter distance races. When she isn't running, she loves watching reality competition shows, reading and curating the perfect Taylor Swift playlist.
