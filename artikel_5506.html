Invitation Homes, HBI team up to train vets in skilled trades

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Home Builders Institute and Invitation Homes have launched a partnership that channels Invitation Homes’ Invitation to SkillUp initiative into HBI’s Military Services program. The effort expands recruiting at HBI military sites such as Fort Stewart and Jacksonville and uses Invitation Homes’ Veteran Employee Resource Group for mentoring.  AI Summary

Invitation Homes and Home Builders Institute (HBI) have formed a partnership to expand training and job placement for transitioning armed services members, veterans and their families seeking careers in construction, property maintenance and other skilled trades, according to a company announcement.

The collaboration is part of Invitation Homes’ Invitation to SkillUp initiative and will support HBI’s Military Services program with hands-on, instructor-led training, industry-recognized certifications and job placement services.

Under the agreement, HBI and Invitation Homes plan to connect HBI-trained veterans, military spouses and family members with roles in the build-to-rent and single-family rental sectors, expand recruitment at HBI Military Services locations including Fort Stewart, Georgia, and Jacksonville, Florida, and use Invitation Homes’ Veteran Employee Resource Group for mentorship and career guidance. The partners also aim to highlight success stories of veterans and family members who move into skilled trades careers.

“Veterans and their families have dedicated themselves to serving our country, and we are proud to support them as they transition into rewarding civilian careers,” Invitation Homes President and CEO Dallas Tanner said in the release. “Through our Invitation to SkillUp initiative, we are investing in talented individuals whose dedication and service strengthen our communities, while helping create meaningful opportunities in the skilled trades.”

HBI President and CEO Ed Brady said the nonprofit has spent the past decade training, certifying and placing transitioning military members, veterans and their families in skilled trades roles. Nearly 90% of its military program graduates secure jobs, earning on average more than three times the federal minimum wage, according to the announcement.

“We know this model works, and with this important investment from Invitation Homes, we can help more veterans enter the skilled trades every year because the people who served this country deserve a career as strong as their service,” Brady said.

Invitation to SkillUp is the company’s national skilled trades development platform, which partners with trade schools and workforce organizations across the country to broaden access to skills education and promote trades careers.

Invitation Homes joins other national backers of HBI’s military programs, including The Home Depot Foundation and DEWALT. A recent $1 million gift from Google, followed by the new investment from Invitation Homes, underscores rising industry interest in supporting veterans and their families in building long-term skilled trades careers.

For homebuilders, the partnership represents one more channel to address chronic labor shortages in residential construction and property maintenance by tapping into a pipeline of trained veterans at a time when the industry continues to struggle with aging trades workers and elevated repair and maintenance demand.
