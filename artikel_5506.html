Amazon is adding Uber Eats delivery as a new perk for Prime members

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Prime members get a $0 delivery fee on Uber Eats orders of $25 or more placed through Amazon, replacing the Grubhub perk

Amazon $AMZN is adding Uber $UBER Eats restaurant delivery to its Prime membership in the U.S., waiving the delivery fee on orders of $25 or more placed through Amazon.com or the Amazon Shopping app, the companies announced in a joint release on Thursday.

Prime members do not need a new app or an Uber account to order, the companies said. The benefit covers all Prime members, including those on the half-price Prime for Young Adults and Prime Access plans. Members can share it with one adult in their household through Amazon Family.

The service reaches nearly 10,000 cities and towns and hundreds of thousands of restaurants on the Uber Eats network, according to the release. Options include Wingstop, Chipotle $CMG and Sweetgreen, as well as regional and small-business restaurants.

Customers new to Uber Eats can take $20 off their first two orders of $25 or more, though the offer is only available during their first 14 days. Prime Visa $V cardmembers earn 5% back on all Uber Eats orders through Dec. 5, 2026.

The companies said members could save more than $175 a year from the $0 delivery fee and Prime-exclusive promotions. That estimate rests on a typical number of orders, annualized from August 2026 order data, and compares Prime pricing with ordering on Uber Eats without an Uber One membership. The companies said savings vary by order and are not guaranteed.

"Whether it's game night, family night, or date night, Prime's new Uber Eats membership benefit makes getting dinner delivered even easier," Jamil Ghani, Amazon's worldwide vice president of Prime, said in a statement.

Andrew Macdonald, Uber's president and chief operating officer, said in a statement that the companies "built an experience for Prime members designed to make restaurant delivery feel as natural, convenient, and affordable as ordering anything else on Uber Eats."

The Uber Eats arrangement supersedes Amazon’s earlier Grubhub offering for Prime members, according to Bloomberg. An Amazon spokesperson told Bloomberg that Prime will stop offering the Grubhub+ subscription as a continuing benefit once Thursday passes. Amazon first added Grubhub for Prime members in 2022, according to The Associated Press.

An Uber spokesperson would not discuss the financial terms of the arrangement, such as whether Uber owes Amazon a referral fee for orders placed on the site, according to Bloomberg.

Prime costs $14.99 a month or $139 a year in the U.S.
