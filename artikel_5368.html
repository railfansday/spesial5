Disney is merging Hulu into Disney+ this December for a unified app

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Disney has not announced a shutdown date for the standalone Hulu app, and U.S. standalone Hulu subscriptions remain available

Disney $DIS plans to unify its Hulu and Disney+ apps beginning in December, the company said in its quarterly letter to shareholders last week.

The executives said the change is intended to make it easier for viewers to find and explore content from Disney's many brands and franchises. They said they expect ongoing work on the product and its underlying tech to lift engagement and reduce churn gradually.

Disney has yet to say when the Hulu app will be shut down, and U.S. customers can still sign up for standalone Hulu subscriptions, according to Variety.

Disney has not said how the unification will affect pricing or bundling, according to TechRadar. The full Hulu catalog is slated to be carried over into Disney+.

Disney announced yet another round of streaming price increases in September, according to Variety. The Disney+/Hulu Premium bundle now costs $21.99 a month, up from $19.99, while the version with ads held steady at $12.99 monthly.

Disney started integrating Hulu into Disney+ in 2023 under then-CEO Bob Iger, according to Variety. The company closed a deal in June 2025 to buy out the one-third stake in Hulu held by Comcast $CMCSA's NBCUniversal.

Hulu got its start in 2007, when News Corp and NBCUniversal teamed up to create it, according to Gizmodo. Disney gained control of the service in 2019 through its purchase of 21st Century Fox.
