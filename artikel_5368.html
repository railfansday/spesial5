Justin Schoenfeldt Joins SJE ECS Division as Director of Operations for Revere Control Systems

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Manufacturing and multi-site operations leader will guide Revere sites

BIRMINGHAM, Ala., Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- SJE has hired Justin Schoenfeldt as Director of Operations for the Revere Control Systems sites within its ECS Division.

Schoenfeldt's addition is an important step as SJE continues to build on the strong foundation in place at Revere and strengthen the business for the future.

In his new role, Schoenfeldt will lead day-to-day operations across Revere's four locations, with responsibility for engineering, project management, control panel assembly, procurement, logistics and facility operations. He will partner with Revere leadership and teams across the business to strengthen operational execution, advance continuous-improvement efforts and improve performance in safety, quality, delivery, cost and customer satisfaction.

"Justin brings extensive manufacturing and multi-site operations experience, along with a practical, hands-on approach to building teams and improving performance," said Andy Wilson, President of SJE's ECS Division. "His leadership will be a meaningful addition as we build on Revere's strong foundation and continue to strengthen the business for the future."

Schoenfeldt brings more than 17 years of manufacturing and multi-site operations leadership. Most recently, he served as Vice President and General Manager at A. Stucki, a designer, manufacturer, and reconditioner of engineered railcar components, where he led five facilities and oversaw operations.

Earlier, as a Founder and Chief Operating Officer of Wheelworx, Schoenfeldt helped build and scale a multi-state manufacturing business. He led teams through significant growth and acquisition integration and oversaw investments in equipment, facilities, and people. His experience includes Lean manufacturing, supply chain, safety, quality, team development, business development, and P&L management.

Outside of work, Schoenfeldt has been married to his wife, Michelle, for 15 years. They have two children, Taylor Grace, 12, and Easton, 9, who are active in sports. Taylor Grace cheers for Chelsea Middle School, and Easton plays football, basketball and baseball. The family also has two West Highland Terriers, Beau and Bella.
