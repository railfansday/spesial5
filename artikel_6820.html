The Watches of Grand Theft Auto 6 (and Their Real-Life Inspiration)

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

From Rolex Day-Dates to Casio digitals, a guide to the timepieces of Vice City.

For one glorious moment, the world appears to be united. Grand Theft Auto VI has finally arrived, to near-universal acclaim, with millions of people abandoning work, school, and presumably all their other responsibilities to explore the streets of Vice City. In these divided times, it's enough to warm the cockles of your heart.

And amid all the fast cars, guns, nightclubs, and criminal capers, there's something else we pay attention to here at Esquire HQ: the watches!

Rockstar Games' latest blockbuster reportedly features 34 "collectible" luxury watches scattered throughout its fictional version of Florida. But that's only part of the story. GTA VI also has its own watch brands, although some of the designs look ... suspiciously familiar.

There's Crowex (a dead ringer for Rolex), Takio (distinctly Casio-adjacent), and Covgari, whose watches owe more to Audemars Piguet than the Italian-o jeweller its name suggests.

From gold Day-Dates to pocket-money Casio digitals, here are some of our favorite real-world watches that appear to have inspired the game's timepieces.

The gold Crowex is a near dead ringer for the Rolex Day-Date 40 (ref. 228238). The fluted bezel, black dial, and day window at 12 o'clock are all present and correct, although the bracelet differs somewhat from Rolex's iconic President. The Day-Date celebrates its 70th anniversary this year. Evidently, it remains the universal symbol of having made it, even in the virtual world.

The silver chronograph worn by a character overlooking Vice City has more than a passing resemblance to the mighty Daytona. The round case, contrasting sub-dials, and metal bracelet suggest that famous racing chronograph. As we know, the Daytona is one of the world's most sought-after sports watches, making it a natural fit for GTA's world of expensive cars, conspicuous consumption, and bling.

Brian Heder, a veteran drug smuggler, wears a metal sports watch that certainly seems to recall Rolex's Sub. There's the substantial case, dark dial, and the bracelet. The Submariner has been a fixture of luxury watchmaking since 1953, and its more ostentatious steel-and-gold versions would be right at home on the streets of Vice City.

Jason Duval, one of GTA VI's two protagonists, wears a chunky digital watch resembling something from Casio's Pro Trek range. The large circular display, prominent buttons and rugged case certainly suggest its something from the range of the Japanese juggernaut. A more practical choice than the gold watches favored by some of his associates.

Not everyone in Vice City is wearing a gold Rollie lookalike. The Takio F-BLAST is an unmistakable nod to Casio's inexpensive digital classics, particularly the bestselling F-91W. It has the familiar rectangular display, black dial surround, and simple, functional design. There's even a more colorful version, with a red case and two-tone metal bracelet, spotted in the game's Hamlet Thrift store.
