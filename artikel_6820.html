Queen Letizia Just Wore This Winter’s Most Versatile Pant Trend

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

It’s a trouser style that straddles multiple sensibilities at once, feeling “classic yet of the moment,” “polished yet unpretentious,” and “textural yet easy to style.” That’s according to a recent Vogue story exploring one of the biggest winter trends for 2026. And by “they,” in this case—drumroll, please—we mean corduroy pants.

Corduroy pants have already appeared in the runway collections of The Row and Miu Miu, and by this winter, they’ve firmly established themselves as an indispensable wardrobe staple. Above all, it’s their versatility that makes them so desirable—they pair effortlessly with a wide variety of shoes and tops, while the ribbed fabric strikes a balance between classic elegance and rugged functionality.

It’s a combination Queen Letizia of Spain clearly appreciates, too. On November 13, during a visit to Campus Diversia—a socio-ecological project for inclusion and environmental education set within a 412-hectare nature reserve of forests and olive groves—the monarch wore corduroy pants with a subtly flared silhouette, looking particularly chic in a rich dark blue (one of 2026’s biggest color trends).

Queen Letizia styled the pants with lug-sole brown boots and a collared dark brown coat. A black sweater rounded out the outfit, echoing the dark soles of her boots. Together, the brown and navy created a harmonious palette in which warm and cool tones balanced each other perfectly.
