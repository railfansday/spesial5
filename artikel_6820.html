The Ultimate Holiday Gifting Experience, Curated by Cosmo and Walmart

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Step into Walmart’s Holiday Wonderland with Cosmo for the festive experience you won’t want to miss.

This year, Cosmopolitan editors partnered with Walmart to curate the ultimate holiday shopping experience, packed with all the gifting inspo you need. Just ahead of Black Friday and Cyber Monday Deals Events, Walmart’s Holiday Wonderland is a free, two-day event inside a restored 1850s NYC townhouse at 16 West 10th Street in Greenwich Village. It runs Friday, November 20 from 12 p.m. to 6 p.m. ET and Saturday, November 21 from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. ET. RSVP here!

Inside, you’ll find Cosmo’s very own pop-up: a lounge and game room where you can get a personalized color reading and a take-home palette card. To add to the fun, you can also compete in a timed holiday gaming challenge with a daily leaderboard.

Featured products stretch across a diverse range—from collagen face cream to Oura rings—we know that there is no such thing as a one-size-fits-all when it comes to finding the perfect gift for your special someone.
