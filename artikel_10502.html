Woman fined $27 for crying on bench at children’s playground

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A woman in Germany was slapped with a €25 ($27 USD) fine after sitting down on a bench at a children’s playground to cry, unaware that she was breaking the law by being there without a child.

The 31-year-old woman, identified as Hana, had gone for a walk through Glückaufpark in Gelsenkirchen on October 25 to clear her head after becoming overwhelmed by financial and academic pressures.

The chemistry student eventually broke down in tears, taking a seat on a bench at the playground and calling her aunt.

“I just wanted to be alone and not be seen by anyone,” she told German broadcaster WDR.

However, three municipal enforcement officers approached her and asked whether two boys playing on the swings were her children. When she said no, the officers demanded her identification and issued her a €25 fine.

Under Gelsenkirchen’s municipal regulations, playgrounds are reserved for children aged 14 and under, along with their parents, guardians, and supervisors.

While there was a sign outlining the playground’s rules, BILD reports that it did not explicitly state that unaccompanied adults were prohibited from sitting on the benches.

Hana argued that the officers could have simply asked her to move instead of issuing a fine.

“I felt as though I was worth nothing as a human being,” she said.

Despite criticism, the city defended the officers’ actions, describing the fine as relatively mild and proportionate. According to WELT, Hana has since paid the fine to avoid incurring additional costs.

Rather than relaxing the rules, the city council discussed increasing the maximum penalty for playground violations from €55 to €60 ($66) on November 17.
