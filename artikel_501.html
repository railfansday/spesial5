Chewys Black Friday and Cyber Week sale runs in two waves — when to shop for the biggest savings

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

TL;DR: Chewy is running early holiday deals with up to 40% off and buy-three-get-one-free offers on select toys and apparel through Nov. 22, before stepping up to 50% off and buy-two-get-one-free deals for Cyber Week from Nov. 23 through Dec. 1.

Your dog has no idea what Black Friday is, though they will definitely notice if a new squeaky toy shows up in their crate. If you want to stock up on treats, kibble, or holiday gifts before the Thanksgiving shipping rush hits, Chewy Black Friday deals are already underway — and the retailer is rolling out its holiday discounts in two distinct waves.

The early holiday sale, now live, gives pet parents an easy head start on everyday essentials and stocking stuffers. Starting Nov. 23, Chewy will step up its markdowns for its main Cyber Week event, with steeper percentage discounts and a better multi-buy ratio on toys and pet apparel through Dec. 1.

Here's how both waves of Chewy's holiday sale break down so you can decide what to grab now and what to save for next week:

Chewy's first wave of holiday offers is live on its website through Sunday, Nov. 22:

Up to 40% off select pet supplies, treats, and everyday essentials, plus additional sitewide markdowns

Buy 3, get the 4th free on select toys, pet fashion, and more

If you are already running low on food, litter, or treats, you may as well place your order now before holiday shipping slows down.

If you are mainly shopping for holiday toys, cozy pet beds, or festive sweaters, waiting until Monday, Nov. 23 gets you noticeably better deals across the board:

Up to 50% off select categories, plus additional holiday markdowns

Buy 2, get the 3rd free on select toys, pet fashion, and more

Chewy is also rolling out new daily Cyber offers through Dec. 2, giving you an extra day to catch rotating deals after Cyber Monday wraps up. Keep in mind that specific brand and item exclusions apply to each promotion, so check the offer details on Chewy's site before checking out.

Chewy offers free standard shipping on orders of $49 or more (or $25+ if it is your first order), so bundling a few multi-buy toys or bags of treats into one cart is an easy way to avoid the $6.95 flat shipping fee.

If you are new to Chewy or have never tried its recurring delivery program, you can also take 35% off your first Autoship order (up to $20 in savings on eligible items, followed by 5% off future shipments) — and you can adjust, pause, or cancel Autoship anytime from your account.

Besides its holiday sales, Chewy is running its annual Write to Chewy Claus program through Dec. 24. You don't need to buy anything or even have a Chewy account to participate — you can submit one free online letter on behalf of your pet sharing their holiday wish list.

Every letter sent helps fund up to 4,000 free shelter pet adoptions nationwide through Greater Good Charities during Chewy's Free Adoption Week (Dec. 11–17) at participating shelters in the contiguous U.S. (plus, Chewy is donating $5 in supplies per purchase, up to $10 million total). As a bonus, Chewy Claus is also sending surprise holiday gifts to tens of thousands of lucky pets — from dogs and cats to birds, reptiles, and horses — throughout December.

Whether you are refilling the treat jar today or sending a free letter to Chewy Claus, you can check everything on Chewy's website right now.
