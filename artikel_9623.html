How I Built a Privacy-First Cost of Religious Belief Calculator with AI Assistance—Without Collecting a Single Byte

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

When people begin questioning or deconstructing lifelong religious beliefs, the conversation usually stays abstract—focused on theology, philosophy, or history. Rarely do people stop to measure the concrete, cumulative cost of religious commitment in everyday hours, money, missed opportunities, and emotional energy.

As a journalist, author, and founder of Evidence-Based Strong Atheism (EBSA), I wanted to build a practical, grounded tool that helps people see those numbers clearly—without sign-ups, tracking pixels, or storing sensitive personal disclosures on a server.

That is why I built the Cost of Religious Belief Calculator—combining my research on faith deconstruction with AI-assisted development to create a completely client-side web app hosted at god-doesntexist.com that runs 100% in your browser, requires zero personal data, and calculates the multi-year toll of religious participation in seconds.
