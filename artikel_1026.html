MVG stars in Grand Slam epic clash with TWO nine-darters to book semis spot

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Michael van Gerwen and Tom Sykes both hit nine-darters in their quarter final at the Grand Slam; he will next face Gian van Veen, who came through a tough contest against Nathan Aspinall; watch the Grand Slam of Darts in Wolverhampton live on Sky Sports

Michael van Gerwen put in a performance to remember and prevailed against Tom Sykes in a Grand Slam classic to make it to the semi-finals, with both players hitting nine-darters.

Van Gerwen's nine-darter came in the opening leg and marked his first on Sky Sports since 2012. After two 180s, he pinned T20, T19, then D12 to end a 14-year wait for perfection and send the Wolverhampton crowd into a frenzy.

Despite the barrage from the Dutchman Sykes stayed composed, and in the sixth leg he replicated the three-time Grand Slam champion and hit nine perfect darts of his own. It even earned a wry smile from his opponent.

At that point, Van Gerwen was averaging 124 and Sykes 112, their levels so incredible they were hard to believe.

It is the third-ever televised match to feature a nine-darter, adding to the 2010 Premier League tie between Phil Taylor and James Wade and the 2014 World Grand Prix contest between James Wade and Robert Thornton.

Eventually, Van Gerwen found a mid-match burst that was too much for Sykes to deal with and - with a bullseye finish - he sealed a 16-11 victory.

The 'Green Machine' is aiming to win his fourth Grand Slam title, adding to his 2015, 2016, and 2017 triumphs.

Sykes also comes out of the match with enormous credit as he averaged 101.50, provided 12 180s, and made his mark on an incredible debut.

In the semi-finals, Van Gerwen will take on his Dutch compatriot Gian van Veen after he held off a Nathan Aspinall comeback for a 16-14 victory.

"For everyone who's doubted me, I'm still here, don't worry," Van Gerwen told Sky Sports.

"No, no, I'm never going to run away. But of course I had tough times.

"It's been hard, it's been tough. I've not been producing what I'm capable of, but today I think I played a fantastic game, and then to play against Tom Sykes, because he was amazing, you know what I mean?

Following the nine-darters that highlighted a sensational opening six legs, Sykes and Van Gerwen continued to battle and provide brilliance.

Sykes reeled in a Big Fish in the ninth leg and looked to be taking the contest to Van Gerwen. However, Van Gerwen then put his foot on the gas.

His purple patch saw him go from 8-6 behind to 12-9 ahead, winning five out of the next six legs.

That run increased to eight out of nine legs, a break of throw helping Van Gerwen into a 14-9 advantage before the 'Castleford Tiger' could stop the rot.

After holding his throw to go one leg from the semi-finals, Van Gerwen then broke Sykes' throw with a massive bullseye checkout to ensure he returns for what is set to be an epic day on Sunday.

Van Veen scraped through to victory over Aspinall in what was an incredibly hard-fought contest.

In the opening session, Van Veen was allowed to take a 4-1 lead without any pressure thanks to Aspinall's poor scoring on the trebles.

The second session followed a similar pattern to the first with Aspinall unable to muster his best level. That saw Van Veen find a run of three legs in a row, a big 116 checkout on tops from 'The Asp' stopping the Dutchman from moving five legs ahead.

However, Aspinall then came to life. From 7-3 down, he found three breaks of throw and won four out of the next five legs to make the game a real contest at 8-7. He continued to hang onto Van Veen, with both players experiencing a drop in scoring, to bring the match to 11-9.

Nothing was coming easy to Van Veen and he suffered over 30 trebleless visits. That saw Aspinall in control and finally level the game at 12-12.

From there, both players held their throw well until the crucial 30th leg. Aspinall fell away on his own throw and so with the finish line firmly in his sights, Van Veen nailed a maximum and then wrapped things up on D8 to make it into his first Grand Slam semi-final.
