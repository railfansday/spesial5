Bournemouth's incredible run of form before the international break has put them on the brink of two club records ahead of the visit of Nottingham Forest.

Victory over Oliver Glasner's side would see the Cherries win nine successive games for the first time in their 127-year existence, while it would also be their fifth Premier League triumph in a row — another first.

Brazilian striker Evanilson has been key to that run of form, becoming the first Bournemouth player to score in five successive top-flight matches, while fellow attackers Eli Junior Kroupi and Rayan found the net on international duty for France and Brazil respectively.

It could see Kroupi make his first start of the season, while midfielder Alex Scott is back in training and closing in on his return from injury.

Forest's win over Manchester City last time out was only their second victory of the season, as Glasner's men finally found the cutting edge they have been lacking.

However, it will be tough to follow up that result against in-form Bournemouth, who they have never beaten in eight Premier League meetings.

Forest are yet to keep a clean sheet on their travels this season and may be without full-back Neco Williams, who suffered an injury on international duty with Wales, and Ivory Coast duo Ousmane Diomande and Ibrahim Sangare, who only returned from the Ivory Coast camp yesterday morning.

Xaver Schlager and Dan Ndoye are also doubtful, while Nicolas Dominguez and Callum Hudson-Odoi will definitely miss the trip to the south coast.

The international break may have affected the Cherries' momentum, but they should still have enough to beat Forest.
