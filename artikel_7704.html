Judiciary efforts to create rules for AI stalling amid concerns

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Judiciary efforts to create rules for artificial intelligence use in the courts have been stalled amid concerns over the feasibility of reaching consensus on how the issues should be handled. (Photo by imaginima/Getty Images)

Judiciary efforts to create rules for artificial intelligence use in the courts have been stalled amid concerns over the feasibility of reaching consensus on how the issues should be handled.

The Advisory Committee on Evidence Rules, which advises the Judicial Conference of the United States, has been developing rules for AI use for several years, but efforts have encountered setbacks and resistance, slowing down the possibility of any new policy.

Attorneys, scholars and judges have criticized proposed evidentiary rules, such as one involving how to handle AI deepfakes, according to a story by Law360.

“Bottom line: I’m not hopeful at this point,” Maura R. Grossman, a professor at the University of Waterloo in Canada whose research has shaped the AI proposals, told Law360.
