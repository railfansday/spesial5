£5m Wolves man faces fresh dilemma: Cardiff City & Norwich City must act

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The recruitment team at Wolves will already have one eye on the January transfer window as they look to strengthen Cesar Peixoto’s squad to help their promotion push.

It seems inevitable that Wanderers will try to bring in a new striker, but, aside from the lack of depth up top, the Portuguese head coach will be pleased with the overall state of the squad.

However, one problem facing Wolves is the size of the squad, as they are already at the 25-man limit. Therefore, to secure new additions, players will have to leave.

And, one man who may be open to a move is Tommy Doyle, with the midfielder having only played 281 minutes in the Championship this season heading out of the midweek fixtures.

As well as that, Peixoto doesn’t seem to know Doyle’s best position, as he had operated higher up the pitch before coming on as a number six in the recent win over Bristol City.

So, the £5m signing may feel he is at a stage in his career where he can play regularly and nail down a permanent home.

With that in mind, we look at TWO Championship clubs who should be targeting the 25-year-old in the New Year…

The Bluebirds have had some fantastic moments back in the Championship under the guidance of Brian Barry-Murphy, who has implemented a clear style of play with the Welsh side.

Yet, the one major problem Cardiff have right now is a lack of depth, and, in truth, they could do with strengthening in various areas in the January transfer window.

A new midfielder would certainly be welcome, even if it isn’t a priority, and Doyle is ideally suited to the way that Barry-Murphy wants to play, as he is excellent on the ball.

The Cardiff fans got to see Doyle’s qualities during a loan spell in the 2021/22 campaign, but he has matured a lot as a player since then, and he could now slot in as an important figure in the XI.

Again, he should be able to flourish in a possession-based approach, as Doyle can help control the tempo, and he would get the ball forward quickly. Plus, his mentality and experience at this level should help a relatively young Bluebirds squad.

After a slow start to the season, Norwich look to have finally turned a corner, and there will be a belief that they can still win promotion this season, which was always the aim under Philippe Clement.

But, the first few months of the season have shown that Norwich are still lacking depth, even if they have been very unfortunate with injuries.

With Kenny McLean missing too many games through injury, Norwich are in need of a new midfielder who can hold the ball, move it quickly, and help control games - which is what Doyle is all about.

It remains to be seen whether Wolves would be willing to sell to the Canaries, as they could be promotion rivals, but it’s certainly something that Norwich should explore.

That’s because, on paper, it could be a great move for Doyle and Norwich, and he is the sort of addition that Clement should look to make to ensure his group can last the distance in the promotion race this season.
