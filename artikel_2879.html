Who’s in, who’s out, and who might get the call? Buffalo’s roster is getting thin ahead of Sunday.

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Buffalo Bills will play the Miami Dolphins on Sunday. The question right now is which Bills will actually play. Buffalo played their first of two straight divisional games last week and came back to Orchard Park with another win, but it may have cost them a significant portion of their roster.

After a walkthrough practice on Wednesday, here's what the Bills' practice looked like today:

Dee Alford (concussion) – upgraded to Limited

Cole Bishop (shoulder) – upgraded to Full

Jackson Hawes (shoulder) – downgraded to Limited*

T.J. Sanders (shoulder) – downgraded to Limited

O'Cyrus Torrence (personal) – returned to practice

*On the report that Buffalo Bills PR published yesterday, Hamlin was listed as limited and Hawes was not listed at all. Today, the adjusted report shows today shows them as DNP and Limited respectively.

The Bills defense is still looking thin despite the fact it looks as though Alford has progressed to the next step of the NFL's concussion protocol. He will need a full practice on Friday and then no new symptoms on Saturday in order to completely clear the process and be ready to play.

I would still look to see Tre'Davious White called up this weekend. The second practice squad elevation could go to Shaq Thompson. The veteran linebacker has been waiting for a game jersey this season, and with Williams not practicing for the second straight day, it looks like this could be the week.

It's also looking like the tight end room is struggling, but the Bills now have six wide receivers on the roster – plus they still have two more tight ends – so it's doubtful they adjust much in their game plan.
