ESPN is overreacting to Puka Nacua’s injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Puka Nacua's career with the Los Angeles Rams has "probably ended", according to ESPN's Jeremy Fowler. But that report feels speculative, uninformed, and flat-out ignorant by Fowler.

The Rams will let Nacua leave in free agency? That seems ridiculously unlikely. Players of Nacua's age and ability are rarely allowed to hit free agency and no matter what the situation is on Puka's side or L.A.'s side with a long list of free agents and needs, the Rams would sooner franchise tag the receiver than let him walk for a third-round compensatory pick in 2028.

Nacua is worth at least two first-round picks, and the Rams know it.

"Up in the air as it pertains to his future with the Rams. Going into free agency, he has two significant injuries, including an MCL injury that Sean McVay says will keep him out a few months ago. Probably ends his tenure with the Rams," says Fowler.

Jeremy Fowler is typically a solid reporter, so it's hard to fathom why he would go to the lengths of saying that Nacua's career with the Rams is "probably over" while in the next breath acknowledging that Nacua is the best free agent of 2027.

The "best free agent" next year is never a free agent.

Although the Rams have challenging test ahead for Les Snead when it comes to their free agents and budget, keeping Nacua — or at least tagging him — is non-negotiable. Snead will not let Nacua leave "in free agency" like Fowler says under any circumstances, unless pigs fly by March.

The only rational way that Nacua's career ends is if the Rams tag him and he is traded for at least a first and a second-round pick, if not better. That will also be tough for L.A. to manage given the complexity of trades of that magnitude and the need to find a team that is not only willing to pay Nacua like a top-tier receiver, but also sacrifice the draft picks to complete a deal.

Hypothetically let's say that the Raiders want to trade for Nacua and Las Vegas is holding pick 10. Will the Raiders be willing to trade pick 10 and pick 42 and pay Nacua a four-year, $170 million contract? If there's any team willing to do that it would be the Raiders, but Las Vegas is under new management and Tom Brady may not be so quick to rush a deal like that for Nacua.

As bad as things look now, the fact is that Nacua is the best player on the Rams and this is a roster that is about to get a whole lot less talented in 2027 if Matthew Stafford and Davante Adams and Aaron Donald retire/walk away.

What star power is left? Ty Simpson and Konata Mumpfield?

Unless the Rams want to do a full blown rebuild — which may not be a bad idea — then Nacua should stay and get the contract he deserves. Even if the Rams do rebuild, who is Simpson supposed to be developing as a quarterback with?

Fowler says Nacua's career with the Rams is probably over. I say that's probably a shortsighted overreaction.
