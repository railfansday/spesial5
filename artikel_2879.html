Insane new NBA stat proves Warriors are in serious trouble without Stephen Curry

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Warriors were on the wrong side of history Monday night.

Golden State lost 115-94 to the Pelicans and became the first team in the NBA history to win a game by 50-plus points, but then lose their next three games by a combined 50-plus points, according to OptaSTATS.

Yaxel Lendeborg #1 of the Golden State Warriors reacts during the fourth quarter of the NBA game against the New Orleans Pelicans at Smoothie King Center on Nov. 16, 2026 in New Orleans, Louisiana. Getty Images

The absence of Steph Curry has been palpable.

The Warriors have turned to rookie Yaxel Lendeborg as a temporary replacement for Curry, who is sidelined with an oblique injury. But even with 28 points and eight rebounds in New Orleans, Lendeborg struggled for most of the team's second straight blowout loss.

Golden State last faced the Pelicans on Nov. 9 with Curry, earning a 138-87 win. A week later, the victory couldn't be replicated. The Warriors have lost their last three games to the Rockets, Spurs and Pelicans.

It has been a humbling experience for the Warriors, who have looked completely lost without their superstar.

Golden State Warriors forward Draymond Green (23) sits on the bench against New Orleans Pelicans during the first half at Smoothie King Center. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

"We're gonna have to change some combinations and find some things that work for us," head coach Steve Kerr said. "We got some good looks early and it was one of those nights where shots didn't go, and it felt like that really deflated us."

Lendeborg was visibly frustrated on the bench with a towel over his head. Veteran Draymond Green had an extended talk with the 24-year-old in what was another difficult night for Golden State.

Golden State Warriors head coach Steve Kerr looks on against New Orleans Pelicans during the first half at Smoothie King Center. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

Green spoke about the necessary adjustment the team must make when it comes to the Warriors rookie.

"Depend on him, but lower the expectation. It's unfair to make him step into Steph [Curry's] role 13 games into his career," Green said.

"Right now we're putting too much pressure on him… We can control what we can control."
