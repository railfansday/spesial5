Bills list Dion Dawkins as questionable to play on Monday night

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Dion Dawkins didn’t practice at all this week, but the Bills aren’t counting him out for Monday night’s game against the Vikings.

Dawkins has been listed as questionable to play. A rib injury is the reason that Dawkins is at risk of missing a game for the first time this season.

The Bills ruled out wide receiver Joshua Palmer after he missed practice all week with a hamstring injury.

Offensive lineman Alec Anderson (ankle), defensive lineman Greg Gaines (calf), defensive back Jordan Hancock (hamstring), and wide receiver Tyrell Shavers (knee) are listed as questionable. Hancock and Shavers would need to be activated from injured reserve and the physically unable to perform list in order to play on Monday.
