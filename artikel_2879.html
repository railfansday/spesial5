Colts release QB Daniel Jones

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

We predicted that Colts quarterback Daniel Jones will be benched, sooner than later. Technically, he has been.

The Colts have announced that Jones has been released.

It accelerates a process that would have begun with the Colts benching Jones to avoid having a $10.51 million injury guarantee for 2027 becoming a full guarantee if Jones were to suffer a serious injury over the balance of the season. Jones would have then asked to be released, and the Colts would have likely done it.

So the Colts went straight to the final move on the chess board.

Jones will, once officially released, go to waivers. If/when (when) he clears waivers, he’ll become a free agent.

That’s the same thing that happened in 2024, after the Giants benched Jones and he asked to be released. He later signed with the Vikings, remaining there for the rest of the season before signing with the Colts in March 2025.
