Li (LI) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Net loss narrowed and free cash flow turned positive as its lineup changed over.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 25, 2026 at 11:38AM EST

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Li (LI +5.37%) reported a narrowing net loss and a return to positive free cash flow during the third quarter, supported by a 7.2% increase in vehicle deliveries. Management reported that the company has completed a generational changeover of its vehicle lineup and is scaling the deployment of in-house drivetrain and chip technologies. The company stated that it is transitioning its product update cadence to once every two years to maintain market competitiveness and improve supply chain planning. Financial results reflected a sequential improvement in gross margin to 16.7%, which the company attributed to an optimized product mix and efficiency gains. For the fourth quarter, management expects further improvements in profitability and cash flow as new model deliveries ramp up.
