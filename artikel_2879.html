Jaguars waive CB Alijah Huzzie

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Jaguars spent Monday adding players to their roster and they moved in the other direction on Tuesday.

They announced that they have waived cornerback Alijah Huzzie. The Jaguars acquired safety Jessie Bates, defensive tackle Jordan Davis, and defensive tackle Davon Godchaux in trades on Monday.

Huzzie was signed off of the Jaguars' practice squad last month. He appeared in one game for the Jags and saw all of his playing time on special teams.

Huzzie also played in two games for the Texans last season. He had four tackles and an interception while in Houston.

Travis Hunter, Montaric Brown, Jourdan Lewis, Jaylon Jones, and Jarrian Jones remain on the roster at cornerback for Jacksonville.
