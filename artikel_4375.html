Bentley CEO Says an Off-Road Bentayga Is ‘On the Horizon’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Bentley is reportedly developing a new Bentayga variant based on the X concept, which featured myriad off-road-minded upgrades.

Everyone’s going off-roading these days—or at least buying cars that look like they can. Even Bentley, the British automaker synonymous with wealth and high-class style, is reportedly getting in on the off-road action.

Earlier this year, Bentley revealed an off-road concept based on the Bentayga Speed that featured more suspension travel as well as an increased track width and ride height. The Bentley X concept rolled on forged 22-inch wheels wrapped in all-terrain tires. It also debuted at the FAT Ice Race in Austria with over a foot of ground clearance, partnership decals lining the sides and hood, spotlights, and a roof-mounted go-kart.

Bentley CEO Frank-Steffen Walliser said public response to the X concept was “really surprisingly positive” in a recent interview with Automotive News. He added that a production Bentayga based on the X concept is “on the horizon,” though nothing is finalized yet.

The current Bentayga is approaching its 10th year on the market, still in its first generation. Walliser also said the luxury SUV is due for a redesign, though it’s still too early for details. However, he confirmed the Bentayga is here to stay as the larger, gas-powered counterpart to the electric Torcal SUV, which is set to hit the market for the 2028 model year and will occupy a similar segment as the Bentayga.

As with most concepts, the production version of the off-road Bentayga will probably be much less flashy than the X concept—and most definitely won’t come with a go-kart. But knobby tires, a taller ride height, and more exterior lighting would be plausible in a production variant. Paired with the Bentayga Speed’s 641-hp twin-turbo V-8, a grittier version would be a notable addition to the off-road luxury SUV landscape.
