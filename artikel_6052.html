Park Hyatt Beaver Creek Offers Up To 90,000 Points Back Over Christmas And New Year’s—If You Email The Hotel

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Park Hyatt Beaver Creek is offering 15,000 World of Hyatt points back per night over Christmas and New Year’s. Christmas Eve and Christmas night run 55,000 points, so you wind up paying net 80,000 points for the two nights after 30,000 points back at a hotel where you can walk out one side to ski school and the other to the village ice rink. It looks open to any member who makes a qualifying booking. Make a new reservation on points November 25 through December 5, 2026 at Park Hyatt Beaver Creek Resort and Spa for a stay December 18, 2026 through January 3, 2027. Book at the full points price. Then email your reservation confirmation to beave-reservations.static@hyatt.com to claim the rebate. You get 15,000 points back per night, up to six nights (90,000 points). Existing reservations don’t qualify so you’d need to re-book. Park Hyatt Beaver Creek points deal by u/David_Copperfield in r/hyatt This amounts to a 27% discount against 55,000 point per night pricing, though this hotel can get even more expensive (e.g. December 22 at 65,000 points). And rooms run around $1,000 per night leading up to Christmas around around $2,000 between Christmas and New Year’s. 40,000 points against peak rates works out nicely. Last Christmas the hotel offered 10,000 points back on a 45,000 point peak night. It was Category 8 then, too, but Hyatt pricing is now much higher at the top end. This year you’re getting 5,000 more points back and still spending 5,000 more for a comparable night. Last year’s deal ended on Christmas Day, while this one covers New Year’s Eve. I’ve never actually stayed here, although I had it booked on points. I caught Covid shortly before the trip, inside the cancellation deadline, and faced a huge cash penalty. I told the hotel that if I had to show up to avoid the charge, I’d sit in the lobby and explain to everyone why the hotel had made it too expensive to avoid exposing them. The hotel’s winter policy is full room rate plus tax if you cancel inside 31 days.
