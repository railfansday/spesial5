Alaska, your next governor is a Wikipedia nerd

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

We have arrived at a moment in American politics when people who are young enough to have grown up on the internet are being elected to office. That means there’s a lot more snooping into politicians’ online pasts to come. This month’s elections were no exception.

Earlier this month, 37-year-old Democrat Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins won the election for governor of Alaska, making him the first person to hold the office who was born in Alaska after it became a state in 1959. But when people went looking for more information about the Alaskan governor-elect, they didn’t find ill-advised tweets or compromising photos — they found out that he used to be an extremely active Wikipedia editor.

On a Reddit page dedicated to discussing Wikipedia, someone posted that they had found what seemed very likely to be Kreiss-Tomkins’ account — a page for a user named Jonathan.s.kt from Sitka, Alaska who went to Yale, liked mountaineering, and made 13,358 Wikipedia edits between 2005 and 2011. The governor-elect is also from Alaska, went to Yale, and climbs mountains.

Wikipedia editors are an impressive community: They keep one of the most important sources of information online (mostly) clean and accurate. This only makes Kreiss-Tomkins look better as a legislator. Check this guy out! A dedicated public servant to the global constituency of Wikipedia! But a moderator on the Wikipedia subreddit pointed out that we cannot be 100% certain that this account is his.

“The odds that somebody would spend six years creating articles about Sitka, Alaska, Yale, and uploading PD photos of mountains, all to deceive people into thinking they were a disenchanted Howard Dean volunteer who was from Sitka, attended Yale, and was a mountaineer, is effectively zero. Plus they uploaded a photo of themself that looks like every other photo of JKT, minus the bald part,” one commenter replied.

Fair enough. If someone created a Wikipedia account pretending to be some guy in Alaska, made over 13,000 Wikipedia edits, and then waited two decades until that guy became the governor of America’s 49th state in order to sabotage him by making him look like a conscientious denizen of the web, kudos to them. But I’m a journalist. I have literal nightmares about getting stuff wrong. If you’re not careful, it’s easy to get duped.

So I emailed Kreiss-Tomkins, who is probably fielding many more important messages right now about policy, and asked if this is, in fact, his Wikipedia account.

So, there you have it, people of Alaska — your next governor is a nerd.
