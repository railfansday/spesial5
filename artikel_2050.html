Oscar Cortes transforms Rangers fortunes as stunning form revival earns Colombia call

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Rangers misfit Oscar Cortes has sparked an incredible career U-turn after his eye-catching form earned him an unlikely Colombia call-up.

The wide man is rebuilding his career with Huracan in Argentina during a season-long loan that is due to expire in the coming weeks. Cortes has made 31 league appearances, scoring three goals and providing five assists during an impressive campaign.

Big things were expected of the 22-year-old when he arrived in Glasgow on an initial loan deal, which eventually turned into a £4million permanent transfer and he remains under contract at Ibrox until 2029, although Huracan retain an option to buy.

But the former Lens man struggled at Ibrox, with injuries and a lack of consistent form pushing him down the pecking order.

He later moved to Sporting Gijon, but it is his return to South America that has reignited his career and earned him a fresh opportunity with his national team.

Cortes has been drafted into Colombia's squad as a replacement for the injured Jaminton Campaz.

He will now link up with Celtic forward Camilo Duran for a double-header against Chile in the coming days.
