Arsenal receive mixed verdict after Liverpool penalty review

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

We’ve an important update on the Arsenal Liverpool penalty decision. Arsenal received a mixed reaction from the Premier League’s independent panel after their controversial win at Anfield.

The panel were unanimous in their decision that Liverpool should have been awarded a penalty after Jurrien Timber tugged Alexander Isak’s shirt. However, they were divided on whether it should have been reviewed by VAR.

The panel also cleared the officials on the incident involving Noni Madueke. This means that Arsenal were justified in their win due to the wrong decision being made on the penalty.

The incident happened during the 1-0 win for Arsenal at Anfield on November 1. Isak was trying to run onto a cross from Bradley Barcola after Kai Havertz had given the Gunners the lead.

As Timber pulled him back, he was unable to cleanly head the ball. The referee Andy Madley allowed play to continue. The decision was then made by VAR Stuart Attwell that there was not enough evidence to suggest a review.

The five-man Key Match Incidents panel has now ruled that this was the wrong decision. Four of the panel voted that Liverpool should have been awarded the spot kick after Timber’s handball.

However, there was a tight vote on whether VAR should have intervened. Three panellists felt that Attwell should have shown Madley to the pitchside monitor, whilst two felt it did not reach the threshold.

One panellist felt the length of the hold was not long enough to affect the movement of Isak. The other two members who felt that VAR should not have intervened also said that it is subjective as to how much the hold would affect the striker.

This is an important point to note. Whilst they agreed that the penalty was deserved, there was less agreement on whether it was VAR’s responsibility to overturn the decision.

There was also good news for Arsenal in the panel’s decision regarding Madueke’s late altercation with Alexis MacAllister.

The Liverpool midfielder fell to the floor following a headbutt from the Arsenal winger. The incident was reviewed by the VAR, but Madueke was spared a red card. Mac Allister received a yellow.

All five panel members agreed that Madueke should not have been sent off. They felt that there had not been any contact, and Mac Allister’s reaction was certainly excessive.

It certainly gives us an alternative standpoint into the situation. Whilst Arsenal benefited from the incorrect decision regarding the penalty, the officials were backed up with the disciplinary incident.

This won’t have any impact on the result of the Anfield game, as Havertz’s goal was the winning goal and gave Arsenal their first league victory at the stadium in 14 years.

However, it does show how risky Timber was to make the challenge and how close the panel were with whether they should have intervened with VAR.
