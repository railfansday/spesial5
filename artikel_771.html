Melissa Gorga to Debut ‘Sunday Sauce’ Video Podcast This December (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The launch of the new podcast arrives just in time for the return of 'The Real Housewives of New Jersey.'

Melissa Gorga isn’t only returning to reality television this December; she’s also returning to the podcasting space.

The Real Housewives of New Jersey star is set to debut her new Sunday Sauce podcast on Sunday, Dec. 6, on the fwd. Network. The weekly video podcast will feature a rotating panel of celebrity guests every other week; her husband, Joe Gorga, will join her in episodes on the alternating weeks.

“Every family’s got one night a week where the truth comes out. In my family, it’s always Sunday,” Gorga says in the trailer for the new show. “When you join me on Sundays, nobody’s safe. Some of her first guests include Hilaria Baldwin, Dolores Catania, Harry Jowsey, Nicole “Snooki” Polizzi and the upcoming new season 15 cast of the New Jersey-based Real Housewives.

“Growing up in an Italian family, Sunday sauce meant everyone around the table, talking, laughing and holding nothing back. That’s exactly what this podcast is,” Gorga said in a statement. “Fans have watched my life on TV for more than 15 years. This is a chance to have the conversations that don’t make it to air. They’re honest, they’re funny, and they’re a little bit saucy.”

Gorga previously hosted the On Display with Melissa Gorga podcast, which ran weekly from December 2021 to July 2025. Her new podcast announcement trails the long-awaited return of RHONJ on Bravo this December.

Sunday Sauce will feature segments such as “Big Glass, ‘Tini Questions,” where Gorga will answer fan questions out of an oversized martini glass, and “Cheers and Booze,” a rapid-fire round of one win to celebrate and one gripe to let out.

The podcast name “Sunday Sauce” is a callback also to Gorga’s recipe of the same name that she’s shared online.

“Melissa has built a remarkable career on her own terms, from television to the businesses she’s created, and women see themselves in her story,” said Cathy Csukas, CEO of AdLarge and the fwd. Network. “fwd. was created so women like Melissa have a place where their voices are heard, and Sunday Sauce is exactly that: honest, funny and unapologetically her. We’re thrilled to welcome her to the network.”

Gorga returns to RHONJ alongside former Housewives Teresa Giudice and Catania, alongside a fully rebooted cast. The series has been on pause, with the most recent episode, the season 14 finale, airing over two years ago in August 2024.

Tune into @SundaySauceShow on YouTube, plus Gorga’s socials, for episode updates and behind-the-scenes moments.
