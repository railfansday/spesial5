11 US Vacation Rentals Where the Hot Tub Comes With a View

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Travelers love to debate window vs. aisle, or whether or not a group trip is a good idea, but I’ve never met anyone who didn’t love a hot tub. It’s the amenity that separates a good vacation home from a great one—the ultimate way to wake up your system in the morning or relax tired muscles in the evening, no matter the season. The rentals on this list, from cozy log cabins in Tennessee to glassy beach houses in coastal Oregon, all promise steamy hot tubs worth planning a trip around. Whether you want to experience the stillness of the New Mexican desert, breathe in the Blue Ridge mountain air, or look out for wildlife in Texas Hill Country, you can do it all from the comfort of your very own bubbling sanctuary. For a rejuvenating weekend away, where the agenda is resetting your nervous system or simply sharing a few laughs with friends, these hot tub–included rentals in the US are just the ticket.

We've selected these listings based on Superhost status, ratings, amenities, location, decor, editor stays, and previous guest reviews.

Just a short drive away from some of Tennessee’s best theme parks (including Dollywood), this Smoky Mountain retreat is a home the entire family will love. The 2,100-square-foot cabin is divided into three levels, each with its own bedroom (all have king-size beds) and bathroom (one has a freestanding bathtub). Kids will love the game room in the loft, as well as a few hours spent at nearby Wild Bear Falls Indoor Water Park, Anakeesta, or Ripley's Aquarium of the Smokies. After all that fun, adults can end their days in the outdoor hot tub, perfectly positioned for watching the sun set over the mountain ridge.

Ideal for a couples getaway in the woods, this glassy cabin, with its soaring, floor-to-ceiling windows, is sure to provide a nature-friendly reset. It’s located just a five-minute walk away from Hocking Hills State Park and has all the modern comforts you need: a newly renovated kitchen and dining area, a queen-size bed upstairs, and, of course, an outdoor patio with a firepit and hot tub for nights spent nestled in the trees. Many reviewers gush over how spotless the place is and how close it is to hiking trails. One thing to note: AWD/4WD vehicles are recommended due to the rugged landscape.
