Liverpool learns €30m asking price for Palmeiras teenager Erick Belé

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Liverpool asked Palmeiras about Erick Belé shortly before the 19-year-old signed a new contract on November 6. According to Sport Witness, Palmeiras told Liverpool it would discuss a sale starting at €30 million. Liverpool has yet to submit a bid.

Paris Saint-Germain has also been in touch, the report adds. Parma tried for Belé last summer with a loan offer that included an option to buy. Brazilian outlet Itatiaia reports that Palmeiras rejected the offer and later wrote a €150 million release clause into his new deal.

Belé joined the Palmeiras academy at 11 and scored his first senior goal in May, in a 4-1 Copa do Brasil win at Jacuipense. He had made seven first-team appearances when Palmeiras extended his deal through October 2031.
