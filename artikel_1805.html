CM: Cardinale prepares ‘aggressive’ mercato – the targets for each department

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Gerry Cardinale has declared that AC Milan will be active in the winter mercato, but who is on the potential shopping list?

Calciomercato.com write that Milan are constantly evolving, with owner Gerry Cardinale fully immersed in the club’s dynamics, aiming to create a winning team. The foundations have been laid with the signings of Mario Gila, Diego Moreira and Goncalo Ramos from the summer.

Then came the renewals of two key players, Strahinja Pavlovic and Christian Pulisic. Official announcements regarding the contract extensions of Adrien Rabiot and Alphadjo Cisse, two of the players who have had a devastating impact this start to the season.

However, Cardinale spoke to La Gazzetta dello Sport and revealed a desire to do more: “We need to continue improving the squad because I wasn’t able to do everything I wanted in the summer. It will probably take a couple more transfer windows to complete everything.

“We’re only a quarter of the way there, and the priority is building a team that can deal with injuries. That’s why we’ll continue to be aggressive in the January transfer market. I won’t waste money, but when I set my mind on signing a player, I do everything I can to achieve it.”

There’s no doubt about one thing: Milan will sign a defender in January. Maybe two. The hottest name – Ruben Amorim’s favourite – is Goncalo Inacio from Sporting CP, with whom discussions began last summer.

Also on the list is Kim Min-Jae at Bayern Munich, with direct contact having been established in recent days. Looking to the future, Borussia Dortmund’s Luca Reggiani and Manchester United’s Leny Yoro are being eyed.

A potential right-back signing is also being considered: Argentine international Agustin Giay is among those being monitored . The 22-year-old from Palmeiras is worth around €13m.

In midfield, Pierre-Emile Højbjerg remains a real possibility for January if Milan decides to sign a central midfielder in the January transfer window. In that case, Christian Comotto would be loaned out: Parma, Monza, and half of Serie B are following him.

Sassuolo’s Darryl Bakola is highly sought after, while Bournemouth’s Alex Scott is the dream for next season. Two players Milan are following are the Swede Yasin Ayari and the Paraguayan Diego Gomez of Brighton. Cagliari’s Alessandro Romano is also highly sought after.

The idea is to make a five-star signing for the attack, and Cardinale’s obsession is Real Madrid’s Endrick. Nicolo Tresoldi is liked, but the problem remains Club Brugge who are tough negotiators. For the future, the focus is on Cagliari’s Paul Mendy and Monza’s Gustavo Varela.
