Giants QB J.J. McCarthy returns to win over Eagles after apparent hip injury after taking shot to his side

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

New York Giants quarterback J.J. McCarthy briefly left Sunday's 19-11 win over the Philadelphia Eagles early with an apparent hip injury after he took a direct shot to his side.

McCarthy, however, returned two drives later at Lincoln Financial Field and appeared to avoid any major injury.

McCarthy, on New York's second play of the third quarter, scrambled for a few yards up the left side. As he went down, McCarthy turned and lowered his right shoulder, which was drilled hard by Eagles linebacker Zack Baun.

McCarthy was quickly replaced by backup Jameis Winston, and went over to the sideline.

McCarthy threw the ball on the sideline, but he was in so much pain and actually dropped down to the ground after screaming out while reaching for his side. He slammed his helmet down to the bench out of frustration.

McCarthy eventually jogged off to the locker room. The Giants said he was questionable to return with a hip injury.

McCarthy went 9-of-13 passing for 101 yards before he went down. The Giants entered halftime trailing by two points after they kicked a pair of field goals in the first quarter. Saquon Barkley found the end zone for the Eagles on a 3-yard run early, and the Eagles completed the 2-point conversion.

While Winston initially struggled, he mounted a 14-play, 76-yard touchdown drive on his second time out on Sunday afternoon to put the Giants ahead. He capped that with a 5-yard toss to Isaiah Likely, which gave New York a 13-8 lead.

McCarthy returned for the Giants' next drive, and seemed back to his old self. Any significant hip injury, it seems, has been avoided.

McCarthy got his NFL start with the Minnesota Vikings, but he ended up getting only 10 starts with the franchise before Minnesota traded him to New York in September. Sunday's outing was only his second start since taking over for Winston, who was in for the already injured Jaxson Dart.

The Giants kicked two more field goals the rest of the way to secure the eight-point win, which pushed them to 5-2-1 on the season and kept their lead in the NFC East intact. McCarthy ended up with 127 passing yards, but did not throw a touchdown in the contest. Winston went 6-of-8 for 71 yards and a touchdown.
