LaMelo Ball’s injury status for Timberwolves-Grizzlies game is great news after ankle scare

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Minnesota Timberwolves fans can breathe a sigh of relief regarding the status of LaMelo Ball.

The star point guard appears to be trending in the right direction concerning his availability for Friday's game against the Memphis Grizzlies at Target Center in Minneapolis amid an ankle issue.

Ball left Wednesday's NBA Cup showdown on the road against Shai Gilgeous-Alexander and the Oklahoma City Thunder with just 2:30 remaining in the fourth quarter due to an apparent lower-body injury.

However, it seems that the ankle injury is not as serious as initially feared.

"LaMelo Ball listed as questionable with the ankle, tracks with what I saw last night. He just did not seem too concerned by it," Jon Krawczynski of The Athletic shared via a social media post on X, formerly Twitter.

The 25-year-old Ball appeared to land awkwardly on Oklahoma City big man Isaiah Hartenstein's feet. He had to be helped off the floor before going to the locker room for further evaluation. He left the contest after scoring 19 points on 7-for-18 shooting from the field and 5-for-9 from behind the arc to go along with seven rebounds and eight assists in 29 minutes of action.

The Timberwolves were only ahead by a point when Ball exited the Thunder game, but Minnesota broke away later for a 113-106 win.

Now on a three-game winning streak, the Timberwolves could have Ball back in action as they look to stretch their undefeated run when they meet with the Grizzlies again. Minnesota took down the Grizzlies in a 104-95 win in Memphis on Nov. 21, with Ball posting 12 points, five rebounds and seven assists in 32 minutes.
