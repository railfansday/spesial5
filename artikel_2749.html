B/R lays out path for Falcons to upset Vikings in Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

There are no guarantees in the NFL. We learned that much when the Atlanta Falcons somehow managed to upset the San Francisco 49ers back in Week 7, proving that literally anything is possible in any given matchup.

That said, these Falcons have fallen far since that outing, losing three straight games while Michael Penix Jr. has looked like a shell of the quarterback he was just one month ago.

So, it's a really long shot for the Falcons to hope for another upset this week against the Minnesota Vikings, who have the best scoring defense in the league this year and finally got a decent quarterback by trading for former Falcons starter Kirk Cousins at the deadline.

The greatest obstacle is Brian Flores and his highly-aggressive defense, which will present major problems for Penix, who has struggled mightily against pressure this year.

However, there is a narrow path to victory for Atlanta - and it all starts with running the ball. Here's Brent Sobleski at Bleacher Report pointing to the way forward to a potential upset.

"Everyone has learned a few things about the Vikings in recent weeks. If an opponent can run the ball, Brian Flores' defense is not nearly as effective. In fact, Minnesota ranks 15th in run defense. Enter Bijan Robinson. Now, Michael Penix Jr. isn't capable of getting the ball out as quickly and accurately as Brock Purdy did this past weekend, but the Falcons did have two weeks to game plan for this contest, which should help them be completely prepared for what they're about to face."

Atlanta's offense only functions right when it goes through Robinson and not Penix. The trouble is that recently the Falcons have consistently fallen behind on the scoreboard early in games, making it difficult to lean on the run game.

If they can figure out a way to start off on the right foot and establish a lead, they'll have their foot in the door at least. From there, they have to punish Minnesota's defense on the ground and only throw in occasional play action shots from Penix.

On the other side of the ball they have to commit to stopping Justin Jefferson, even if it means three defenders at a time. If they can do that and run the ball consistently there might just be a chance.
