Lakers win streak snapped as offense goes ice cold against Jazz

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Lakers' winning streak was finally snapped on Wednesday in a rather unceremonious way as the Jazz won going away in the fourth quarter, 109-97.

LA struggled all night offensively, finishing the game shooting just 38.2% from the field and 27.3% from three. They turned the ball over 18 times, leading to 26 points.

Their defense kept them in the game as they forced the Jazz into 23 turnovers for 29 points of their own. However, Utah eventually took control in the fourth to put the game to rest.

Luka Dončić started the game with a layup. Utah, though, had a slight upper hand, with Keyonte George scoring five. Quentin Grimes continued his hot shooting from the previous game by draining a 3-pointer.

On the flip side, Ziaire Williams picked up three quick fouls and had to be subbed out.

Still, the Lakers went on a 7-0 run to give themselves a three-point lead.

Utah answered with a run of their own, scoring six in a row to retake the lead. George and Ace Bailey combined for 10 points. Luka had six, and Williams had five for Los Angeles.

The purple and gold ended the quarter on a 14-4 run, led by Luka, who now had 15 points, giving them a five-point lead.

Collin Sexton was the first to score for Los Angeles in the second period. He had four points. The team, though, was 1-7 from the field. Grimes finally scored for LA on a midrange jumper.

Svi Mykhailiuk started cooking for Utah, draining four triples. He had 12 points in nine minutes. The Lakers were a rough 3-10 from the field in the period.

The half stayed back-and-forth, with the teams trading buckets and the lead. George tallied 11 points for Utah. Luka took over most of the scoring as the quarter wound down, pushing his point total to 22.

LA shot 31% from the field and shot 26% from behind the arc. They were down by two at halftime.

The third period began with the Jazz in control. Josh Green was fouled from behind the arc and converted a four-point play. Luckily for LA, Grimes was still scorching hot, draining two triples and slamming home a dunk to take the lead.

Grimes now had 13 points. He was also 3-4 from behind the arc.

Luka and Grimes kept the Lakers in the lead with their firepower as nobody else on the team was in rhythm offensively. Los Angeles had 21 points off Utah's 16 turnovers, a major reason why they were up.

With 4:25 left, the purple and gold were up three.

The rest of the quarter was a drag for both teams with heavy legs and rough scoring for both sides. Los Angeles did get a small boost from Sandro Mamukelashvili with four points. However, Utah was up by two at the end of the third.

Both teams had a slow start to the final frame. Walker Kessler started to find some offense, scoring five points. George put the Jazz up by three with a triple. He had 26 points.

With 7:50 left in the quarter, the purple and gold once again had to claw their way to a win.

George and Bailey combined for five points, putting Utah up by eight. Things kept getting worse for the Lakers as the Jazz extended their scoring run to 15-0, thanks in part to George and Bailey lighting it up. They had 30 and 15, respectively.

A sudden momentum shift happened when Sexton was subbed in, bringing some energy to the team. LA went on a 7-0 run to make it an eight-point game with 4:15 left, forcing a Utah timeout.

Everything went bad once again out of the break as the Jazz just turned the jets on and made it an 18-point game. LA emptied the bench with 1:51 left.

Luka finished with 29 points, five rebounds and seven assists. Kessler had a slow game with 10 points and 11 rebounds. Grimes ended with 20 points, seven rebounds and four assists.

Williams logged seven points. Sexton scored 12 points off the bench. Matisse Thybulle dished three dimes and had four steals. Jarred Vanderbilt and LaRavia combined for 11 rebounds, five assists and four steals.

The Lakers' next game will be against the Sacramento Kings on Friday at 7:00 PM PT.
