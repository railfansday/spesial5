Rams WR Puka Nacua reportedly out 4-5 months following knee surgery, will be sidelined for playoffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

While the Los Angeles Rams try to make the most of a 2026 campaign that began with astronomical expectations, wide receiver Puka Nacua is already working toward 2027. He had MCL surgery on Tuesday, and his recovery timeline is considered to be four to five months, ESPN's Adam Schefter reported on Saturday night.

Based on that reported prognosis, the 25-year-old Nacua will miss the remainder of the regular season and the playoffs.

The two-time All-Pro wideout received recommendations from three doctors to undergo the procedure, per ESPN's latest report, which noted that team doctor Neal ElAttrache performed the operation.

When the NFL announced this week that it was suspending Nacua three games for violating the league's personal conduct policy, it reportedly wasn't aware that he had already had surgery.

The Rams placed Nacua on injured reserve with knee and turf toe injuries on Nov. 12, a week before the NFL imposed its suspension. Like any player who lands on IR, Nacua had to sit out four games before he could return. By the time the suspension was announced, the length of that ban and his remaining time on IR were aligned.

While that situation understandably turned heads, it's worth noting that Nacua isn't the first player to serve a suspension while on IR.

The last year has been a head-scratching one for Nacua, who's among the best wide receivers in the sport but has run into off-field trouble.

This past offseason, news broke that a woman was suing Nacua. She alleged that, on Dec. 31, 2025, he made an antisemitic statement and later bit her on the shoulder. Nacua's attorney denied the woman's claim that he made such a comment and downplayed the biting as "horseplay."

Nacua voluntarily entered a holistic care facility in March to focus on his personal growth. He reflected on that experience in May.

In seven games this season, Nacua — the league's 2025 receptions leader — piled up 70 catches for 715 yards and 4 touchdowns. He also scored on the ground once for a 6-4 Rams team that's hardly had the start it envisioned this fall.

Nacua's injuries occurred on the final offensive snap of L.A.'s Week 9 win over the Washington Commanders. He's in the last year of his rookie contract.

His future in L.A. has yet to be determined, and he won't be available for the Rams in the short term. That said, the Rams are hoping to get veteran wide receiver Davante Adams back down the stretch, as they make a push for the playoffs.
