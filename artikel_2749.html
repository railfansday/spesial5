&#8216;I didn’t feel comfortable to sign&#8217; &#8211; Monaco striker Folarin Balogun on why he rejected Everton move

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

AS Monaco's Folarin Balogun (25) came very close to returning to the Premier League last summer. On the verge of joining Everton in the final hours of the transfer window, the US international ultimately decided against signing for the Toffees. This was despite the English club's decision to see the deal through, even after a physical issue was detected during the medical.

Whilst he had recently confirmed that he had been very close to signing for the Hill Dickinson Stadium side, Balogun, whilst at the USMNT training camp, went into detail about the specific reasons that led him to call off the negotiations.

"My position was very clear. I haven't said anything publicly, but I flew to Everton, obviously, with a set contract," explained the former Arsenal striker. "And then by the time I left, the contract had been changed. So, that was the situation, in short. The contract was changed, and in the end, I didn't feel comfortable to sign."

Having ultimately stayed at Monaco this summer, Balogun has had a difficult start to the season, playing just 230 minutes across seven appearances in all competitions – one goal. Under contract with the French club until 2028, his departure this winter now seems inevitable.
