VOTE: 12 nominees in Nov. 2-7 SW FL Girls Athlete of the Week poll

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Each week during the school year, The News-Press and the Naples Daily News select high school girls athletes who stood out the week before based on a combination of coach reports sent to sports@news-press.com and sports@naplesnews.com and school nominations sent over email by noon Sunday. Those athletes are put in a poll each Monday on news-press.com and naplesnews.com, and you, our readers, vote on who you think did it best.

The winner of the Southwest Florida Girls Athlete of the Week poll will receive a T-shirt provided by BSN Sports and a tote bag provided by The News-Press and Naples Daily News. Voting ends at 10 a.m. Thursday each week. The winner will be announced on social media.

The previous SWFL Girls Athlete of the Week winner was Community School of Naples golfer Norah Urrea.

Here are the Southwest Florida Girls Athlete of the Week nominees for Nov. 2-7:

Frea finished as low medalist at the Region 2A-4 tournament, shooting a 70 to power the Cougars to the regional title.

Emily Del Mazo, Community School of Naples

Del Mazo finished second at the District 1A-6 championship in a time of 19:20.

Moss won the District 3A-6 individual title in a time of 19:57.

The freshman won the District 1A-6 championship in a time of 19:08.

Almaraz scored eight goals and added three assists in wins over Port Charlotte and Mason Classical.

Manning scored seven goals in the King's win over Golden Gate.

Mantilla scored four goals in the Vikings' win over Palmetto Ridge.

Varga scored five goals and added three assists last week for the Seahawks in wins over Marco Island and Golden Gate.

Burke won the 100 free (50.22) and 200 free (1:47:07) and was part of a first-place finish in the 400 free relay to help lead the Cougars to the Region 2A-3 championship.

Maltz won the 200 IM (2:03:72) and 100 butterfly (55.38) and was part of first-place finishes in the 200 medley relay and 400 free relay to help lead the Cougars to a District 2A-9 championship.

The freshman won the 1-meter diving event (470.95) at the Region 1A-3 championship.

Dalton won the Olympic and Traditional lifts in the 119-pound division at the Lady Tarpon Invitational.
