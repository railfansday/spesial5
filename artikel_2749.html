Fernando Mendoza's first NFL TD puts Raiders up 7-0

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Fernando Mendoza helped secure a national title at Indiana with a scramble for a touchdown and his first NFL touchdown came the same way.

Mendoza capped the first Las Vegas possession of Sunday's game against the Seahawks with a 10-yard run up the middle and into the end zone. The extra point made it 7-0 Raiders with 7:41 left to play in the first quarter.

The scoring drive covered 46 yards after Foley Fatukasi forced Seahawks quarterback Drew Lock to fumble on Seattle's second offensive play.

Raiders tight end Brock Bowers left the game during the scoring drive and got checked out in the sideline medical tent before returning to action.
