Sixers' LeBron James praises Labaron Philon Jr. for 'big-time minutes'

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

ORLANDO, Fla. -- Thrust into a larger role, rookie Labaron Philon Jr. gave the Philadelphia 76ers a much-needed lift to defeat the Orlando Magic and improve to an Eastern Conference-best 13-3.

Philon produced 10 points, two assists and one rebound in the 121-116 victory on Wednesday at the Kia Center. He finished by shooting 4-for-5 from the field, including 2-for-3 from 3-point range, in a season-high 17 minutes off the bench.

The 22nd overall pick was called into action with several key players sidelined, including Jaylen Brown (hamstring), Tyrese Maxey (foot) and Dean Wade (hamstring). He helped the Sixers stay afloat early, scoring seven points in the first quarter alone.

"He is our first-round draft pick," LeBron James said. "He is not a guy that is afraid of the moment as well. His number was called; he came in and gave us some big-time minutes and big-time shots. He gave us another ballhandler, with 'Rese being out, with J.B. being out. He was big-time."

Joel Embiid scored a team-high 26 points to lead the Sixers, while VJ Edgecombe had 24 points and James added 22 points. The group also received key contributions off the bench from Jabari Walker (six points), Caleb Love (three points) and Tacko Fall (three blocks).

Trailing most of the game, the group outscored the Magic 29-19 in the fourth quarter to overcome a 17-point first-half deficit. They limited Orlando to 31.6% shooting from the field and went 8-for-10 from the free-throw line in the period.

"We did a very nice job tonight of our execution offensively down the stretch," Sixers coach Nick Nurse said. "We worked on some stuff this morning that we hadn't used before and they ran all of that stuff very, very well; very calmly, with composure."

Before Wednesday, Philon had played sparingly for Nurse, totaling nearly 52 minutes in 10 appearances. He had only logged more than 10 minutes in a game once, recording five points, two rebounds and one assist on Oct. 25 in a loss to the Detroit Pistons (13 minutes).

Philon filled in for the short-handed Sixers on Wednesday, offering a glimpse of the production he can provide in spurts. The team is confident in his abilities and believes he can continue performing at a high level when called upon.

"He went out there and played poised," said Anfernee Simons, who had 18 points. "He played to the standard that we hold him to. I think he showed what he can do. Everybody has been talking about his talent level, his ability to play at his own speed, play with some good space, make tough shots, make plays for others and play good defense. He had a great night."
