NFL Honors moves from Thursday back to Saturday

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The NFL Honors ceremony is on the move, again.

The made-for-TV awards show, which shifts this year from broadcast to streaming, will also be sliding back to its original day.

John Ourand of Puck reports that NFL Honors will be held not on the Thursday of Super Bowl week but on the Saturday of Super Bowl weekend. The show slid to Thursday in 2022.

Netflix has acquired the rights to the show, which attracted 3.7 million viewers in February 2026.

During the event, the various Associated Press awards are announced. Also, the new class of Hall of Famers is unveiled.

As NFL programming goes, the numbers aren’t great. As live TV goes, three million people at one time is nothing to sneeze at. Whether Netflix will realize that kind of audience remains to be seen — if the official figure is even disclosed.

Regardless, the show will quickly be forgotten as the NFL pivots the next day to the biggest event of the year.
