If this is the end, it’s a disappointing one

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

If Puka Nacua has played his final game as a member of the Los Angeles Rams, it's a disappointing end for fans.

Nacua elected to have what seems to be season-ending surgery to repair his knee injury in a contract year. The Rams will likely regret not extending his contract before the year started, as his lack of long-term security likely factored into the business decision. If Nacua had future assurances, he might have put off surgery in order to make a comeback towards the late regular season into the playoffs.

And to make matters worse, Nacua suffered said injury on a meaningless play. Sean McVay called a receiver run to salt away an insurmountable lead against the Washington Commanders. It didn't make sense at the time. It felt even worse once the injury news broke. Somehow, it seems even more nonsensical now.

Nacua's production through his first four NFL seasons—when healthy—rivals that of former top draft picks in Odell Beckham, Justin Jefferson, and Ja'Marr Chase. The key difference: Nacua was the Rams' third draft pick in the fifth round back in 2023. He went on to record the most yards by a rookie receiver and he's second to only Jefferson through three seasons.

This is the kind of found money that should set a franchise up for years. Instead, the Rams might have wasted their opportunity.

Despite what looks to be a large amount of available salary cap space heading into next offseason, Los Angeles has a handful of difficult decisions to make—Nacua included. They will also decide whether Kobie Turner, Byron Young, Steve Avila, Kevin Dotson, Coleman Shelton, Warren McClendon, Colby Parkinson, and others over the next couple of years. That money is reserved for taking care of their own. If they don't, the cupboard will be left so bare that the Rams risk falling back into irrelevance.

And separately there is the issue of how the Rams have used Nacua.

The team has failed to properly stack the receiving corps around their star pass catcher. Often times over the last few seasons it has seemed like their passing game was simply Nacua or bust. As a result, he's handled a historically large workload. That has taken a toll on his body especially with the physical nature in which he approaches the game.

That could be the reason why the Rams are hesitant to invest in Nacua long-term, which would be another sign that they failed to protect the luxury of their 2023 draft day steal.

If this is the end for Nacua with the Rams, it's an incredibly discouraging way to separate. The team has turned a historic advantage into a few years of great production with little to show for it thereafter.
