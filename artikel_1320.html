Chelsea star Wesley Fofana apologises for interview laughing off Liverpool penalty miss and admitting his £200,000-per-week deal stopped him from quitting - as Xabi Alonso warns star 'to take things seriously

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Published: 08:00 EST, 24 November 2026 | Updated: 10:10 EST, 26 November 2026

Wesley Fofana has apologised to Chelsea for his unauthorised interview and the club now consider the matter ‘closed’, with Xabi Alonso saying it is time to take things 'seriously'.

Fofana spoke with a French YouTuber called Zack Nani and within the two-hour conversation, the centre back laughed off his penalty miss which condemned the club to their Carabao Cup exit. Chelsea were beaten in a shootout by Liverpool last month after he skied his decisive spot-kick.

Fofana also revealed how his representatives advised him to honour his lucrative contract, worth £200,000 per week, when he was tempted to quit football amid his injury hell. Both revelations sparked a backlash from supporters online.

Alonso confirmed that Chelsea had spoken privately with Fofana, and the manager said: ‘We have addressed that with him and for sure, it was unfortunate.

‘He apologised. He knows it was not right, the moment, and it was not right, the content. We are here to take things seriously and he said that it's not going to be repeated. We know that it's something that we don't want, these kind of things. They are not going to be repeated for sure.’

When asked if that is case closed or if he will now be unavailable, Alonso said: ‘Closed.’

The YouTuber who conducted the ill-timed interview hit back at the coverage in England of Fofana’s quotes. Specifically, he was unhappy with how the focus was on Fofana joking about his penalty miss at Liverpool and what he said about considering retiring from football. That was despite the YouTuber’s own social media channel posting those precise clips which led to widespread criticism.

Regardless, Fofana has now seen fit to apologise to Chelsea, but it remains to be seen whether Alonso will stick with the Frenchman and Levi Colwill for Friday’s trip to Nottingham Forest.

Both Fofana and Colwill were bullied by Sunderland’s Brian Brobbey and Leeds United’s Dominic Calvert-Lewin, and Liam Delap is the next challenge. Alonso spoke of the ‘standards’ needed from his central defenders to deal with such strikers.

‘That's the standards of the Premier League and for sure, it's an important thing that we have spoken about,’ Alonso said. ‘That we need to show much better, to do much better, because if you don’t, then you are not able to control important situations in the game.’

Delap will have a point to prove versus Chelsea, who sold him to Forest after a single season for £50million.
