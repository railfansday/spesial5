Sarri: ‘Atalanta strength of character, but hire someone else for man-marking’

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Maurizio Sarri hails Atalanta’s ‘character’ to fight back from 4-2 down for a 4-4 draw with Inter, but warns he will not abandon his tactical ideas. ‘If they want man-marking, then hire someone else.’

La Dea have the unexpected honour of playing in both 4-4 Serie A thrillers this season, as they were held here by the same result against Frosinone on October 25.

They refused to give up, even when trailing 4-2 to a Pio Esposito hat-trick and Petar Sucic penalty, because Eljif Elmas, a Charles De Ketelaere double and stoppage-time Franck Kessie spot-kick allowed them to snatch a point from reigning Champions of Italy Inter.

“This team is starting to have some strengths, and the first is its character, because this is not the first time that the team has fought back, while we are also scoring more goals recently,” Sarri told DAZN Italia.

“Unfortunately, we also still have a big weakness, which is the defensive fragility that sees us concede far too many. I am seeing progress, though.”

Atalanta remain in 12th place in the Serie A table, with just one win in their last six league games, but in the circumstances this point can be a valuable confidence-booster.

They conceded 24 goals, the seventh worst defensive record in the top flight, and only scored 21.

“We had a lot of injuries, that never allowed us to start the same set of defenders. There is also a packed fixture list, so we do not have time in training to work on the defensive movements,” noted Sarri.

“We must not forget this was a squad accustomed to defending in a completely different way. I don’t see it as just one problem, but rather a collection of circumstances that are making things difficult for us.”

Gian Piero Gasperini set the template in Bergamo for so many years with his three-man defence, so changing to Sarri’s four at the back and zonal marking is proving a very difficult transition.

“If Atalanta want someone who tells their defenders to man-mark, then they need to hire someone else,” smiled the coach.

“I have never done that and I have absolutely no intention of doing it in future, in all honesty.”

De Ketelaere was named Man of the Match by the Lega Serie A, despite Pio Esposito scoring a hat-trick, as he bagged two goals and earned the penalty.

“His last few performances for us have been good, and he carried that on when playing for Belgium. Charles has been on form for over a month now, he’s strong physically, talented technically, has immense potential. He has these ups and downs, which he has had throughout his career, and we are working on smoothing those out.

“I’ve coached some talent in my time, but few with his potential.”

Atalanta have a famously poor record against Inter, scraping seven draws and 11 defeats since the 4-1 victory here in November 2018.
