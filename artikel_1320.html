Romano: JJ Gabriel '95%' Likely to Leave Man Utd as Pochettino Takes Charge

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

New Manchester United manager Mauricio Pochettino is already facing losing a player in the early days of his reign at Old Trafford.

Pochettino has been appointed by Man United on a two-and-a-half deal, although he won't officially take charge of the Red Devils until November 18, following the United States' double header against Haiti.

The Argentine will only have a couple of days to prepare his team for their big Premier League clash against arch-rivals Liverpool at Anfield a week on Sunday, as they look to climb out of the relegation zone following a disastrous beginning to the season.

Pochettino has some big decisions to make on his United players in the coming weeks and months, with many of the club's most experienced and highest-profile players out of contract next summer, including Bruno Fernandes and Harry Maguire.

United do have 12-month options with both of those and Lisandro Martinez, but the likelihood is that the Argentine will be leaving in 2027 having recently been fined for a public outburst towards Jason Wilcox.

One other player set to leave who Pochettino will have no say in is teenager JJ Gabriel.

The hope for many years has been that the 16-year-old would be United's next academy superstar, but instead the forward decided that he wanted to leave Old Trafford.

The teenager was linked with every top club in Europe, before it was confirmed that it was Real Madrid who are set to win the race for his signature.

Now, despite the arrival of Pochettino, Romano has confirmed in a video on hs YouTube channel that Gabriel is on the verge of making the move to the Bernabeu.

He said: "JJ Gabriel to Real Madrid, the deal is happening. It's gone a little bit under the radar in the media in recent weeks.

"Why? Because Real Madrid didn't want this deal to become front-page news, to become the covers of the big newspapers in Spain, to become so big in the media.

"Man United are still waiting for any kind of official communication, and so Real Madrid didn't want to create any kind of legal problem.

Real Madrid were hoping to keep this deal under the radar, but the deal is almost there. The deal is very close. The deal is, according to my information, 95% done.

"Just waiting on the final touches and then Real Madrid can complete the deal for JJ Gabriel. It's just a matter of time.

"The agent of JJ Gabriel is working to close all the details in the next days, and then to have the 'here we go'. So I would say less than one step away."

When you see how well Pochettino worked with the likes of Shaw and Dele Alli in the past, the Argentine will surely be frustrated that he will have no opportunity to work with such an exciting talent in JJ Gabriel.

Of course, one of the youngster's complaints has been a lack of first-team opportunities following the progress he has made, and perhaps he may have been handed more chances by Pochettino, who is known to value youth.

But the decision has been made, and he is now seemingly set to follow in Jude Bellingham's footsteps and make the move to the Spanish capital, and he will hope to shine for Los Blancos.

Elsewhere, Pochettino does have some ready-made young talents to work with in this United squad, not least Shea Lacey, who is enjoying a breakthrough campaign at Old Trafford.

Lacey is probably seen as the youngest player by some distance, but at 19 years of age, he is only one year younger than both Leny Yoro and Ayden Heaven, and two years junior to Kobbie Mainoo.

Pochettino has plenty of talent to work with, and he must work out how he can get the best out of them, and the club's senior stars, to move United up the standings.
