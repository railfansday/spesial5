Moyes Reacts To Everton's Below-Par Brentford Display

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

David Moyes reflected on a performance where Everton 'didn't deserve anything' from a 4-0 defeat at Brentford.

The Blues went into the clash at the Gtech Stadium buoyed by a three-match winning streak before the recent international break. But they were well below their usual standards in west London, going 2-0 down before the half-hour mark, albeit in unlucky circumstances through two cruelly-deflected goals from Keane Lewis-Potter.

The night got worse before half-time when captain James Tarkowski was sent off for a high tackle on Yehor Yarmoliuk, having initially been shown a yellow card by referee Craig Pawson prior to VAR intervening.

And although Thierno Barry was denied by a fine save from Caoimhin Kelleher seconds before the interval, Brentford inflicted only a third defeat of the season on the Blues with second-half goals from Vitaly Janelt and substitute Fabio Carvalho.
