Nobody is Talking About The Tottenham Star Who Must Be Axed After 2-0 Win vs Ipswich

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Tottenham Hotspur returned to winning ways with a dominant 2-0 win over Ipswich in the Premier League on Saturday afternoon.

Luca Williams-Barnett took all the headlines as he scored the opening goal and then set up Dominic Solanke for the second, helping the Lilywhites to their fourth win in five games in the top flight.

The youngster was handed his first start for the club, as Roberto De Zerbi admitted that a number of factors had led to the decision, and also contributed to a number of other changes being made to the starting lineup for the clash.

Sandro Tonali missed out due to illness, while Mathys Tel has been ruled out until next month with an ankle injury. Destiny Udogie, Conor Gallagher, James Maddison, and Solanke all also earned starts after missing out last time, with Andy Robertson, Archie Gray, Omar Marmoush, Tel and Tonali all dropping out.

But there was a more unexpected change in the defence for the game too, with Marcos Senesi coming in alongside Jan Paul van Hecke, with Micky Van de Ven dropping to the bench.

Van de Ven played 90 minutes in both of the Netherlands fixtures during the international break, as they claimed a 7-0 win over Greece before being crushed 5-0 by Jurgen Klopp's Germany, but despite being fit and available, he was left out against the Tractor Boys.

Spurs didn't miss him in the slightest, however, as the Argentina international stepped into the side and turned in a brilliant performance to help unlock the low block of Gary O'Neil's side with his excellent passing out from the back and reading of the game.

It was the third time this season that Van Hecke and Senesi have started together, and it was their first win, having previously played in the 3-0 defeat to Brentford on the opening day, and the 1-1 draw against Man Utd at Old Trafford.

But the draw against United should have been a comfortable win but for some poor finishing from the forwards, and this performance against Ipswich was among the best the team have had this season. It's no coincidence that the control of the team was far more stable with Senesi in the side, and it now looks like he should be keeping his place in the team.

With the way that De Zerbi wants to play, control is paramount. Van de Ven is a top defender, and the club clearly rates him highly after handing him a bumper new contract in the summer.

But Senesi has shown, whenever he has played, that he is more of a fit for the manager's plans and style, and that is now leading to an uncomfortable conversation about him being in the team ahead of the captain.

If the manager is serious about creating an environment and atmosphere in the squad that is competitive, then he shouldn't be afraid to drop big names when he has other players who are outperforming them.

If Van de Ven is the leader that the club see him as, then he should react to the decision to leave him out by stepping up in training and being ready to claim his place back. Senesi deserves his spot in the side, and his performance proves it. Now it's on De Zerbi to make the decision to actually pull the trigger.
