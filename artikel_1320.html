Spain vs England: James Trafford Delivers Sensational Post-Match Interview

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

James Trafford gave Thomas Tuchel a real headache after a brilliant performance in England's Nations League victory against Spain.

The Leeds United shot-stopper made a strong case in his battle to take the number one jersey from Jordan Pickford, who has been first-choice since 2018.

England ended Spain's unbeaten run at the Metropolitano on Sunday night, and the local press have had plenty to say.

Trafford made an incredible double-save to seal the win for his team after Spain threatened to come back from three goals down. It wasn't just his shot-stopping ability that won the heart of the nation on Sunday night.

Trafford could do very little about either of Spain's goals, which came from the penalty spot and a Nico Williams effort that went in off the post. The 'keeper made six saves in total, which included three inside the box and two double saves.

During his post-match interview with ITV's Gabriel Clarke, Trafford was asked for his thoughts on the double save he made in the dying stages. "Which one?" he cheekily replied, then winked at Clarke.

"Yeah obviously, I can’t actually remember, to be fair.

"I think Yamal cut in short… it happened so quickly, and that’s what we train everyday, every week for, is split-second decisions, and just happy to help the team."

His interviews have gained attention in the past as Trafford exudes a positive and honest energy whenever he's in front of a microphone. Asked if the save was his best, he had another brilliant answer.

"I made an incredible one away at Port Vale, when I was at Bolton - that will forever be my best save! But for the moment, what it [the England save] meant, it’s up there."

Instead of focusing on what the game meant for him individually and what his future holds in an England shirt, Trafford stated how ecstatic he was at helping his team get the result.

The Spanish teenager's comments have resurfaced after England beat Spain and Harry Kane won the Ballon d'Or.

"I’m just happy to get the win, to be honest," he said. "It was our aim to finish top of the group. We knew how tough the game was going to be, and we knew what we demanded of ourselves coming into the game... and I’m just happy to play my part and get the win, and finish top of the group ultimately.
