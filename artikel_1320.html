French Media Dub Chelsea Ace ‘Biggest Disappointment of Zidane Era’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Zinedine Zidane's era as manager of the French national team is now well underway, and the country's media certainly haven't been convinced by what they've seen thus far.

After a relatively successful international break for Les Blues last time out, when they won three of their four Nations League matches, this iteration has been significantly less impressive. The 2018 World Cup winners lost 3-1 to Italy at the San Siro on Thursday, before scraping past Turkey with a 2-1 victory in Bordeaux on Sunday.

While the general sentiment around the team's performances is negative, these results were enough to see France top their Nations League group.

Despite this, French media have been quick to express their disappointment with a number of players over this most recent break, with one star in particular being intensely scrutinised for his displays.

Malo Gusto has found himself on the periphery of Xabi Alonso's squad at Chelsea this season, starting just three Premier League games under the Spanish manager so far. This lack of minutes for the full-back at Stamford Bridge has prompted speculation that Manchester United could be set to make a move for him.

Nevertheless, this limited role for his club hasn't cost Gusto a place in France's squad. Zidane called the defender up for the matches against Italy and Turkey, and the Chelsea star was even named in the starting eleven in Milan on Thursday.

Conceding the penalty that led to Italy's third goal, Gusto was hooked with 13 minutes left to play and was left on the bench for the following game against Sergen Yalcin's team.

French publication RMC Sport (via the Daily Mail) subsequently produced a scathing review of the 23-year-old's contributions to Zidane's first few fixtures in the France hot seat, with journalist Alexandre Jaquin describing the player as 'the one who disappointed the most during this second international window of the Zidane era.'

"Fielded in Italy despite little playing time with Chelsea, the right-back completely fell into the San Siro furnace," Jaquin added.

"Overwhelmed by his opponents' energy and little helped by Michael Olise's lack of defence, the former Lyon player lived a nightmare in Lombardy, notably conceding a penalty. A match quickly erased from his memory."

Following Gusto's disappointing display against Italy, Zidane turned to Les Blues newcomer Sael Kumbedi at right-back. The Wolfsburg star, who has shone in the German second division at the start of this season, impressed against Turkey, threatening Gusto's position in the France squad moving forward.
