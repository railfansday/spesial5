Black Friday is in full effect, and while the deals have been rolling for a while now, the best bargains just hit the digital shelves. Walmart has thousands of items on sale, so we’re spending our post-Thanksgiving time digging through them to find the absolute best. The picks span a ton of different categories, so no matter what you’re shopping for, we can probably save you some money. Check back regularly for more additions and save us a turkey sandwich because we love leftovers. If you want more deals, go check out our big list of Black Friday deals from across the web.

Walmart has the AirPods Pro 3 for $149, $100 under the $249 list price. That’s the noise-canceling model, and Walmart’s listing says the ANC cuts up to twice as much noise as AirPods Pro 2. The bigger change is fitness: the listing says built-in heart rate sensing tracks heart rate and calories burned across up to 50 workout types. Battery life is the trade-off to know about. Apple rates them at up to 8 hours with ANC on, and the USB-C MagSafe case ships without a power adapter.

Not sure which pair fits you? Our guide to the best AirPods lays out the whole lineup.

At $228, the Hisense 65-inch H5 Series Roku TV costs $170 less than Walmart’s $398 list price, which works out to about $3.50 per inch of screen. It’s a 2026 model with full-array LED backlighting, and Walmart’s spec sheet lists Dolby Vision, HDR10 and HLG support, three HDMI ports and Roku OS built in. Here’s what the low price buys you: a 60Hz panel. That’s fine for streaming and cable, but PS5 and Xbox Series X owners who want 120Hz gaming should spend up.

For pricier step-ups at this size, see our guide to the best 65-inch TVs.

The Nespresso Vertuo Pop is $59 at Walmart, half its $119 list price, and the box includes a 12-capsule welcome set. It reads a barcode on each Vertuo pod and sets the brew to match. Nespresso lists espresso, double espresso and 5-, 8- and 12-ounce coffees, with a 30-second heat-up. The 19-ounce water tank is small enough that you’ll refill it after a mug or two. The bigger commitment is the pods: the Pop only takes Vertuo capsules, not Nespresso’s Original-line ones.

Compare it with the rest of the lineup in our guide to the best Nespresso machines.

This is the steepest discount among our featured picks. The Shark Detect Lift-Away ADV is $144 at Walmart, 62 percent off its $379.99 list price. Shark says its sensor reads the floor type and adjusts brushroll speed on its own, and the self-cleaning brushroll keeps pet hair from wrapping. Step on the pedal and the canister pops off for stairs and couch cushions. It’s corded, with a 25-foot cord, and weighs about 13 pounds by Walmart’s spec sheet, so it’s a whole-house cleaner rather than a quick-grab stick.
