Caribbean corals are in crisis. Fossil reefs can help point to where corals may persist

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The fossilized remains of ancient coral reefs often sit just meters from living corals in the Caribbean, where reefs have declined sharply over the past 70 years. Those fossils could help show where corals might survive a warming future. In two new papers, researchers at The University of Texas at Austin identify which coral species dominated ancient reefs in northeastern Jamaica and suggest how that record could guide reef rehabilitation.
