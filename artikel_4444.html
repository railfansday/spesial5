The 2028 Audi Nuvolari shares most of its architecture and hardware with Lamborghini’s Temerario, but the exterior and interior designs are for the most part completely different.

The interior is a cocoon of microfiber and leather, with aluminum accents. It’s a welcoming place.

The interior gives off fancy-dinner vibes more than qualifying lap-time vibes.

Do this steering wheel and infotainment screen look familiar? They’re shared with the Lamborghini Revuelto and Temerario.

These vents are machined aluminum, and the lever has a lovely mechanical snick when operated.
