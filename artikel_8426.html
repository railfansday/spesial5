Personal Consumer Issues • Re: Suggestions for replacing bathtub

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Wife & I are contemplating replacing our old 1st floor bathtub with a modern walk-in shower unit. We want it custom built, not a pre-built insert.

If any BH has gone through such an experience I would like to hear the do's and don'ts. Also like to hear what accessories ( shower head, seat, soap shelf, etc.) to consider. Also curious as to how many quotes you solicited.

Thank you for any info you are willing to share.
Look into a cultured marble assembly as well.
Less seams than tile and almost endless ways to setup.
Floor is poured with traction in the casting.

I used to have people stand in showers and have them reach out and touch areas where they would need too to turn on valves, reach for soap, handicap bars both standing and sitting, shower heads heights &: positions, etc.

Note or mark as needed. Then figure out what they wanted and install hitting those marks as close as possible.

For my end, (the plumbing) I always told everyone to get two more estimates besides mine & make sure to compare apples to apples with their other companies.

The other companies:
Must be licensed.
Must show proof of insurance.
Must pull permits.
They must present a payment schedule.
Must indicate what will be done.
And most importantly, they MUST have a currently licensed person on the job whenever ANY work is done related to the plumbing.

Beat of luck with your project.
