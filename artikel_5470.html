TSMC is buying stakes in Anthropic and OpenAI worth up to $1 billion

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The chipmaker's board approved purchases of Anthropic common stock and OpenAI preferred stock, each capped at $500 million

TSMC $TSM approved investments of up to $500 million each in Anthropic and OpenAI on Tuesday, the company said.

The board authorized the purchase of Anthropic common stock at the company's per-share initial public offering price. It also authorized the purchase of OpenAI preferred stock. TSMC expects to put as much as $1 billion into the pair of AI developers, according to Reuters.

TSMC filed the board resolutions with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission in a Form 6-K. The filing is signed by Wendell Huang, the company's senior vice president and chief financial officer.

At the same meeting, the board approved an investment of no more than $500 million to establish an investment company. The company said the new entity will make strategic investments in the semiconductor industry and related ecosystem.

Anthropic put a price of $70 to $78 per share on its IPO on Tuesday, according to Reuters. The company is targeting a valuation as high as $1.4 trillion, which would rank its listing among the biggest market debuts ever.

Anthropic is raising a further $26 billion in a separate private placement priced at the IPO price, according to Reuters. Investors in that placement include TSMC, Google $GOOGL parent Alphabet, Nvidia $NVDA, AMD $AMD and Microsoft $MSFT.
