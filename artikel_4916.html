BlackRock opens tokenized money market fund with $100 million in assets to Hong Kong investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Standard Chartered and Ant International seeded the fund, which BlackRock said is the first Hong Kong-domiciled money market fund offering a constant net asset value.

BlackRock has opened its first tokenized money market fund in Hong Kong to retail and institutional investors, the asset manager said in a statement on Tuesday.

Dubbed BlackRock HKD Digital Liquidity Fund, the product offers conventional and tokenized units, allowing investors to access the same money market strategy through traditional or blockchain-based ownership records.

The fund had $100 million in assets under management as of Nov. 20, according to the statement. It invests in short-term instruments, including government debt, overnight and term deposits, and other eligible securities.

"As traditional and digital markets become more connected, investors want cash solutions that are simple, trusted and available when they need them," said Dan Maley, head of APAC digital assets at BlackRock.

BlackRock said its fund offers accumulating share classes allowing the NAV to change, as well as distributing share classes that seek to maintain a constant unit price and pay out income.

It is the first Hong Kong-domiciled money market fund to offer a constant net asset value to both institutional and retail investors in the local market, according to the statement. For distributing share classes, the CNAV structure targets a stable unit price, with investment income distributed to investors rather than added to the value of their units.
