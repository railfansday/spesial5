Walmart Early Black Friday Deals Double as Great Gifts (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The month of November is basically one big shopping holiday at this point, but I'm not complaining. Walmart is the latest retailer to officially launch its selection of early Black Friday deals, and many of them happen to make great gifts for yourself or somebody else. Walmart+ members can shop select deals even earlier, while supplies last. We've gone through and highlighted the best discounts on WIRED-tested gifts below, and we'll keep this story updated through Black Friday and Cyber Monday.

Walmart+ gets you free delivery on orders over $35, free shipping, access to Peacock or Paramount+, gas savings, and more. You can try it for 30 days for $1, which may be worthwhile if you plan on doing some shopping at Walmart for the holidays; Just keep in mind that you'll want to manually cancel your membership if you decide it's not for you after 30 days, otherwise, it's $13 per month (or $98 per year). (Note that qualifying government assistance recipients can get this plan for 50 percent off.)

This extremely portable power station is actually pretty easy to tote around, unlike some other gadgets we've tested in this category. Coming in at 17 pounds with a convenient grab handle on the back, it has a massive 768-watt-hour capacity and recharges in just over an hour. The port selection has everything they'll need to stay topped off on a camping trip or for a few days without power. If your prepper friend loves hoarding power banks, this will be greatly appreciated.

The Beats Solo 4 are a little older now, but in a world rife with noise-canceling earbuds and expensive over-ears, these still have a place. The on-ear cups are pretty comfy, especially if your giftee's noggin isn't too big, and they have the iconic Beats sound with a little less overt bass than their predecessors. They don't have noise cancellation, they don't automatically pause, and they aren't dust- or water-resistant. But for the right person, this price isn't bad. They might also be a good pick for teenagers who want cool over-ears.

We've seen this deal pretty consistently for this excellent Bluetooth tracker, which can add anything to your Apple Find My app. Use it to track down your luggage, your pets, your keys, or your wallet. I personally keep one in my car so I can remember where I parked it when I haven't moved it for a few days. The battery is replaceable, and it has a built-in speaker, so you can ping it to find it if needed. It would make a great gift for your scatterbrained friend.

This is one of the best prices we've seen for this wearable reading light that any bookworm would appreciate.

We think this little karaoke machine makes a great gift, especially for couples.

Editor and handywoman extraordinaire Kat Merck says this is a “knockout deal” for everything you get. From pliers and screwdrivers to wrenches, bits, and a tape measure, it's hard to beat for an at-home tool kit. It comes with most of the non-power-tools you'd need for basic home projects. You'll probably still want a drill or other specialty tools, but this would make a great gift for a new renter (or for yourself if your tool kit currently consists of random Allen keys in a junk drawer).

This is a solid discount on the very best automatic litter box we've tried. It strikes a great balance between budget and features and should work well with most one- or two-cat households (provided your kitties aren't too oversize). It doesn't have AI or a camera inside, but it is easy to use, easy to keep clean, and it even sprays a deodorizing mist as needed to keep things smelling fresh. If your cat's litter box is a source of marital strife, perhaps it's time to employ a robot.

This is the best bottle washer we've tried thanks to its quiet operation and continuous sterilization. It's perfect for new parents.

This over-the-top litterbox has more features than necessary, but the price is still better than normal.

We love this self-emptying cordless vacuum. It's efficient at cleaning (though it works best on carpet) and has an auto-cleaning and auto-charging station. It's especially good for pet parents since it can convert into a hand vacuum and includes several attachments for detailed cleaning tasks. I'm at the point in my life where a vacuum is a genuinely exciting gift prospect; perhaps someone on your list is, too.

This adorable smart nesting box has an internal hygrometer and thermometer as well as two cameras (one interior, one exterior). It lets you witness the entire cycle of bird birth with an up-close and personal view of nesting parents. It's easy to set up and use, and while the app doesn't always identify everything correctly, it can still help you make sense of what you're seeing. And the built-in solar panel helps ensure that everything keeps operating with minimal input from you. Kat Merck says it makes an excellent gift.
