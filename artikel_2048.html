Fabian Hurzeler issues strong Brighton response after Tony Bloom Bayern Munich claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Fabian Hurzeler has confirmed his commitment to Brighton after receiving strong backing from Seagulls chief Tony Bloom.

Bloom said this week he would fight “ridiculously hard” to keep hold of Hurzeler following reports that Manchester United had considered him as a potential replacement for sacked boss Michael Carrick.

Bloom also said that he expects to one day manage his former club Bayern Munich.

Hurzeler is getting rave reviews for his work on the south coast and is preparing for his side’s trip to Hull on Sunday sitting second in the Premier League table.

Hurzeler told a press conference: “I’ve spoken a lot about his (Bloom’s) support, trust and belief.

“It’s a privilege to work here and since my time here there’s been a lot of rumours with other clubs, but they are just rumours and I am fully committed to this club.”

Hurzeler added: “For me, it's a privilege to work here. Of course, since my time here in good phases, there were a lot of rumours of other clubs, but these are just rumours, and I'm always fully committed to this club.

“I never had this feeling of leaving this club because this club gives me a lot. I still go to bed and have the feeling I'm not done. I'm not done with the development.

“I'm not 100 per cent satisfied where we are. I still think there's potential to get out of this team, to get out of this club.”

The Seagulls have mixed injury news with the likes of Chema Andres and Yankuba Minteh available and Stefanos Tzimas and Kaoru Mitoma nearing returns to fitness, but Zadok Yohanna is out after a setback on international duty.

Hurzeler warned Hull will be no pushovers after their impressive return to the top flight and the tough test they gave leaders Arsenal at the Emirates Stadium two weeks ago.

He added: “They are doing an impressive job. They are very structured. Arsenal suffered so we need to be patient.

“We know they are good in transition and have individual quality, as well as set-pieces. I don’t think it’s an easy place to go.”
