Once dubbed the next Gerrard, midfielder confirms he's returned to Liverpool in unique role

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A once exciting midfielder who was labelled as the next Steven Gerrard is back at Liverpool.

The Reds' academy has done some astonishing work over the last decade. Liverpool were not always recognised as the best academy in England.

Traditionally the likes of Arsenal, Manchester United and even Chelsea have come to be known as the conveyor belt for the country's best prospects.

However, in the last decade Liverpool's reputation has changed.

Thanks to Jurgen Klopp, Alex Inglethorpe and a strategy to increase recruitment and scouting at youth level Liverpool are now the place to be for the country's leading talents.

Just look at the likes of Rio Ngumoha, Trey Nyoni as well as Luca Eden, Erik Farkas and Joshua Abe coming through.

There's also Tyler Morton, Caoimhin Kelleher, Neco Williams and plenty of others who have gone onto have successful career in the Premier League or one of Europe's other top five leagues. And that will likely continue. Inglethorpe continues to play a key role and Liverpool are leading the race when it comes to their strategic player development. Every season, a new talent earns a spot in the club's first-team and has the chance to showcase their ability. Sometimes, it's even more than one player. Lussey's career has since taken him to non-league, and the 32-year-old took to confirm on Twitter that he's also returned to the Reds. In a new role working as Liverpool's Alumni Officer, Lussey works with academy graduates who have been released by the club to help guide their career.
