‘I’m so happy’ – Ronaldinho not counting out playing again after remarkable comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Ronaldinho was delighted to get back out on the football pitch for the first time in 11 years, and hinted he could yet play for Ravenna again.

The 46-year-old joined the Serie C club in August but has been working his way up to being able to feature.

And he finally started in their 2-0 win over Forli on Sunday.

Ronaldinho had been due to only play the first 30 minutes, but was able to push on through to the break, putting in a steady display that saw him complete 17 of his 21 passes and create a chance.

Ronaldinho retired with 299 career goals to his name, having played for the likes of Paris Saint-Germain, AC Milan and Barcelona.

While he failed to come close to scoring the elusive third goal, failing to have a single shot, Ronaldinho was thrilled to be back out on the pitch for the first time since 2015.

"It was great; I'm so happy," he told Sky Sport Italia.

A post shared by Ravenna Football Club (@ravennafootballclub)

"Time flew by – for me, this is what football has always been about – and everything turned out perfectly."

And when quizzed on whether he will play for Ravenna again, he added: "I don't know yet.

"It depends on the president – if he calls me, I'll come back. I always enjoy playing. I told him: Whoever needs me can count on me – I'm here.

"It's all been wonderful. Football is the most beautiful thing I've ever experienced in my life."
