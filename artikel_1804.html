Manchester United target issues transfer update amid contract limbo: ‘We’ll see’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Manchester United transfer target Yasin Ayari appeared to pour cold water on any potential move to Old Trafford - before implying that he is keeping his options open.

Ayari has been a key part of Brighton’s bright start to the Premier League campaign, hitting three goals in nine appearances.

The Swede grabbed the winner against Manchester City at the end of October, cementing Brighton’s position in the Champions League places.

Ayari is out of contract this summer, though, leading to speculation regarding his future. United are said to be among the clubs keeping an eye on his situation.

They are far from the only club credited with an interest in the 23-year-old, though. Premier League leaders Arsenal, Serie A outfit Roma, and Turkish duo Fenerbahce and Galatasaray are all reported to be keeping tabs on the Swede.

But, speaking from the Swedish national team camp this week, Ayari appeared to play down reports linking him with a move away from the AmEx.

The Seagulls attacker shared: “I’m at Brighton and I’m happy where I am. Things are going well for me right now. Then we’ll see what’s to come.”

Brighton paid AIK Stockholm just £3.5million to secure the services of a then-teenaged Ayari in January 2023. He has gone on to make 91 appearances for the Seagulls, scoring nine times since his debut against Grimsby Town in the quarter-finals of the 2022/23 FA Cup.

Asked about Brighton’s exciting start to the season, Ayari continued: “We have a clear identity and everyone sticks to it to the letter.

“We’re like one big family. Everyone loves playing football and everyone’s having fun. That’s the main thing.

“Then everyone has fantastic qualities. And the manager (Fabian Hürzeler) has done a great job of getting the best out of everyone on the pitch.”
