PM: Camarda absent with ‘ankle issue’ as Tomori fully recovers – updates from Milanello arrive

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Some updates have arrived from this morning’s training session, ahead of AC Milan’s clash against Olympiacos tomorrow.

There is no understating how big tomorrow’s game is for Milan. If they want to qualify for the Round of 16, they must win tomorrow, especially if they want to avoid an extra elimination round.

Of course, it will be difficult to do so, but history suggests that it’s not impossible to recover from their current position in Europe.

An update from training has arrived this morning, with PianetaMilan reporting that Tomori has indeed returned to training with the group at Milanello, as was hoped last night.

In fact, he has fully recovered and will be in contention for the game against Olympiacos tomorrow night, which is a major boost for Ruben Amorim, with Strahinja Pavlovic still absent.

However, Luka Modric may not be the only other player who’ll be missing tomorrow. Indeed, the report states that Francesco Camarda could also be absent.

The young striker missed training today – as he’s suffering from some ankle pain – and his presence in tomorrow’s match is now in doubt. Considering Goncalo Ramos’ early substitution on Saturday, though, it may not be too much of a concern for Ruben Amorim, who seemingly wasn’t going to look to the youngster as a starter.
