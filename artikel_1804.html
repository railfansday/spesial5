'That was tough' - Crawley Town boss gives honest assessment of stalemate with high-flying Barnet

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The stalemate made it eight points in four games in charge for Blackwell as Reds closed the gap on safety in League Two.

Reds had chances in the first half but second-placed Barnet put on the pressure in the second half but a mixture of solid defending and an inspired Vito Mannone kept the visitors at bay.

“Wow, bloody hell, that was tough”, said Blackwell. "It's a very direct game, that's for sure, and they played it quite well, so I'm really pleased with the way the players dealt with it.

“They're an experienced side and we had a lot of youngsters again today, so all credit to them. "I thought Ben Heneghan, once again, at the centre of the back three has done really well.

“It's a typical system to play against and I can see why they're high up in the league, it's causing teams problems. But I think overall, you know, Vito's not had many saves to make it, has he?.”

Blackwell was pleased with how his reduced the amount of gilt-edged chances Dean Brennan’s side had. “There wasn't clear-cut chances,” he said. “It's a system that I've not seen for a long time, but it just meant we had to be focused all the time, they do things quickly, they're long throws, long balls, the keeper kicks it from box to box.

“They know that the big nine is going to flick it on and they make runners in behind and your midfield and your centre-halves have to stay with runners. So it was a disciplined performance tonight against a team, like I say, who play a very direct style, but are good at what they do.”

And the former Leeds boss highlighted that despite the gap in league positions, there was not much in quality between the two sides.

Blackwell said: “You look at it, it was top versus bottom, but you wouldn't have known. I don't think you'd have known.

“Yes, they put us under a bit of pressure towards the end and exactly what a team at the top, full of confidence, you'd expect them to do that. But we were resilient and it's a difficult one for me to express the game other than it was concentration, do your job, throw your body in the way when required and stand up to a team that can bully you if you allow them to. Like I said, you know, Pemi [Aderoju] and Osman [Kamara] and Duke [Stephen Duke-McKenna, they've got a lot of youngsters we have and you're going to learn a lot from that.

“I hope they learn a lot. But what I'm pleased about is the resilience.”

And Blackwell added: “The question was about people coming to Crawley and getting easy points and I think we put a statement down certainly today that if you want to come to Crawley, you're going to have to earn your points if you want to work away with anything.

“So, once again, it's all credit to the players.”
