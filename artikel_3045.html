There’s something to play whether you’re into science fiction, horror or other genres.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is out now, and while it might be one of the most anticipated game releases of the year, not everyone likes the series or is interested in it. If you want to play something more fantastical, Xbox Game Pass has you covered with High on Life 2, Hela: Of Mice and Magic and more soon.

Xbox Game Pass, a CNET Editors’ Choice award winner, offers a wide selection of games you can play on your Xbox Series X, Xbox Series S, Xbox One and PC or mobile device for as little as $10 per month. With a higher-tier Game Pass Ultimate subscription ($23 per month), you can access hundreds of titles, including new ones on release day.

Here’s everything Microsoft is adding to Xbox Game Pass soon.

New to Game Pass Premium. Previously added to Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass.

This sequel to the 2022 first-person shooter High on Life has players once again traveling throughout space and taking down bad guys with the help of an arsenal of talking guns. The game comes from Squanch Games, which was founded by a co-creator of the Rick and Morty series, so expect some raunchy humor and antics.

This prequel to the Outlast series retains the horror of the other games and adds a Cold War-era twist. You’ve been abducted and are subjected to medical and psychological experiments. To survive, you have to fight and evade other test subjects in a massive facility run by the shady Murkoff Corporation.

You’ve seen mice, right? Small, cute creatures that scamper around and are sometimes kept as pets? Well, in this co-op game, you get to see the world through the eyes of one of those critters. You’ll solve puzzles, explore landscapes inspired by Northern Sweden and take in a heartwarming story told via fuzzy friends.

While Microsoft is adding several games to Game Pass, it’s also removing eight others on Nov. 30. So you still have some time to complete your main campaign or any side quests before you’ll have to buy these games separately.
