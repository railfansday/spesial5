Dejounte Murray requests trade from Pelicans

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

After two-plus seasons, Dejounte Murray's time in New Orleans may be coming to a close.

ESPN's Shams Charania reported Friday that the veteran point guard has requested a trade, with the Pelicans confirming the news shortly thereafter. This comes on the heels of two blowout defeats for the Pelicans: a 51-point thrashing by the Warriors on Monday, followed by a 34-point loss to the Nets on Wednesday.

In their statement, the Pelicans said that they will "evaluate potential trade opportunities that are in the best interest of our organization."

Murray has appeared in nine games this season, averaging 13.4 points, 4.6 rebounds, 4.4 assists and 1.6 steals in 31.4 minutes per game. He's started alongside second-year guard Jeremiah Fears, whom many view as the Pelicans' point guard of the future.

Murray missed three games in late October/early November for personal reasons, and the Pelicans won all three, with Fears flourishing and Herb Jones providing quality defensive play as the replacement in the starting lineup. In a since-deleted social media post, Murray responded to a post noting the Pelicans' record in the games he missed, saying, "From what's going on INSIDE, this sounds like someone hit you to say this! THE 'SABOTAGE' IS REAL!"

Acquired from the Hawks during the 2024 offseason, Murray has appeared in 54 games for the Pelicans over two-plus seasons. His 2024-25 campaign was cut short by an Achilles tendon rupture, which also sidelined him for most of the 2025-26 season. He had already been ruled out for Friday's game against the Grizzlies with an ankle injury by the time reports of a trade request surfaced.
