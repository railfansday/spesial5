Original Story: GTA 6 only officially launched late last week, but Rockstar Games has already pushed out a new update (01.002.002) for the game across Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S (along with PS5), rolling out as of a few hours ago.

You're looking at a download size of around 1.6GB for update 1.0.2 on Xbox Series X and S.
