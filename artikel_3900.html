Guide: GTA 6: Who Is the Real Actress Lucia Is Based On?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

As one of the two main protagonists in GTA 6, you're going to spend a lot of time playing as Lucia. As such, you may be interested in learning more about the real-life actress she's based on. In this GTA 6 guide, we are answering: who is the real actress Lucia is based on in GTA 6?

GTA 6's Lucia is based on Manni L. Perez — the game uses both her voice and her likeness.

For months, she was rumoured and speculated to play Lucia in the game, but her involvement in GTA 6 was confirmed by her nomination in the Best Performance category at The Game Awards. She now also appears in the GTA 6 credits.

At the time of the game's release, Perez is 34 and resides in the USA.

She's been an actress since 2013, and GTA 5 was actually where she made her debut. Perez lent her voice to a clubber in GTA 5, and then she did the same for a blackjack dealer in GTA Online. Now, she's sharing the leading role with Jason in GTA 6.
