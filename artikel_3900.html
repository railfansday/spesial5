CD Projekt Red Expects GTA 5 to GTA 6-Like Upgrade in The Witcher 4

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

"You should expect us to make a bigger jump"

CD Projekt Red thinks it can deliver the same sort of "leap" in The Witcher 4 as Rockstar Games has just accomplished going from GTA 5 to GTA 6.

The 13-year gap between instalments in the GTA series has allowed Rockstar Games to create the "greatest open world game ever made", as our GTA 6 PS5 review explains. Within that, it's created a more mature look at the world and then delivered incredible and immersive content alongside it.

The Witcher 4 is targeting a release in 2028, which will be 13 years after The Witcher 3 came out, as long as there aren't any delays. CD Projekt Red is working to the same timescale as Rockstar, and both developers even did the same thing of having a game in a different series release in the middle of it: Red Dead Redemption 2 for the latter and Cyberpunk 2077 for the former.

To that end, CD Projekt Red believes it can deliver a similar leap in quality.

"We believe there's going to be a comparable leap between The Witcher 3 and The Witcher 4 — I think so, yes," said joint-CEO Michał Nowakowski.

"It's been 11 years; we are working on The Witcher 4 to make it significantly better-looking and improved in terms of pretty much every possible facet of the game. So yes, you should expect us to make a much bigger jump between Witcher 3 and Witcher 4."

News Sony Will Support The Witcher 4's Marketing, Much Like GTA 6

Just like Rockstar Games, marketing for The Witcher 4 will be backed by PlayStation, as CD Projekt Red has already signed a collaboration with Sony. GTA 6 has just been marketed as playing best on PS5, but that might not be true.

As an out-and-out RPG, what Rockstar has focused on in its open world won't be the exact same things CD Projekt Red is leaning into with The Witcher 4. Instead, the Polish team will likely be more about main and side quest design, your abilities, and character customisation.

On whether the release of GTA 6 and what CD Projekt Red has seen of it has impacted the development of its own games, Nowakowski responded: "We have certain ambitions when it comes to Witcher [4] and Cyberpunk 2; they have not changed when it comes to GTA 6. If anything happened as a result of GTA 6, it's [that it's] always great to see games like that because they show you how much cool stuff can be done, and that's for sure an inspiration in many ways for our developers."
