Hearts have lost three successive games and conceded 10 goals in the process. So what’s gone wrong at Tynecastle? Martin Dowden is joined by former Hearts defender Ryan McGowan and Hearts Standard editor Joel Sked to assess the problems. How much blame lies with Wouter Vrancken's tactics and how much with individual mistakes? Is the punishing schedule exposing Hearts’ injury crisis? Does Vrancken need a Plan B?
