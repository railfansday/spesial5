Oklahoma AD fires back at 'carnival barker' Paul Finebaum

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Along with disrupting travel nationwide, the FAA-issued ground stop has led to Oklahoma athletic director Roger Denny and ESPN college football analyst Paul Finebaum trading barbs.

On Friday night, Denny released a statement blasting the decision made by Commissioner Greg Sankey and the SEC to move Oklahoma's game vs. Ole Miss from Saturday to Sunday due to travel issues for Ole Miss.

And on Saturday morning's edition of SEC Nation on SEC Network, Finebaum went off about Denny's statement and defended the SEC and Ole Miss. Finebaum said that Denny was "pathetically pandering" to Oklahoma fans.

"I understand the frustration, and none of us really know what happened to Ole Miss. But I believe the administration and Ole Miss did everything they could," Finebaum said. "What I don't think is correct is Roger Denny, the athletic director there, pathetically pandering to his fanbase at midnight to try to cover up whatever, I don't know."

"Mr. Denny, the commissioner of the Southeastern Conference made a decision for the entire conference and for the safety of everyone," Finebaum added. "He's not concerned about what you're doing with your fanbase. And what Roger Denny has been doing all this year is pandering to his fanbase."

Well, later on Saturday, Denny responded to Finebaum's comments during an in-studio appearance on On3's Sooner Scoop.

Denny was asked, "Do you think this hurt [Oklahoma's] relationship with [the SEC?]?"

He proceeded to refer to Finebaum as the SEC's "carnival barker."

"I don't know. They'll probably send their carnival barker out there to accuse me of pandering again," Denny said. "And candidly, I don't care. My job and my duty is to our players, our university, and our fanbase. If they don't like the way we handle their business, I just don't much care about that."

And you can be sure that Finebaum, ESPN's voice on all things SEC, will have a response to those comments in the coming days.

The Ole Miss-Oklahoma game was originally scheduled to kick off at 4:15 p.m. ET on Saturday but was moved to 1 p.m. ET on Sunday. It will be televised on SEC Network, fittingly.
