The clubs were a combined 23-11 in the 2025 regular season. Both head coaches in DeMeco Ryans and Jim Harbaugh now seem on hot seats. Houston won at least 10 regular-season games each of the past three seasons and reached the Divisional Round of the playoffs. I thought the Texans might actually be better this year with some offseason moves, quarterback C.J. Stroud in a contract-eligible year and a return to health of players like promising wideout Tank Dell, who missed all of 2025.

Oddsmakers listed the Texans at 9.5 wins entering the season (Over around -150) and they were -180 to make the playoffs, an implied probability of 64.3%. Now those numbers are 7.5 (Over -175) and +190 (implied probability of 34.5%). The SportsLine Projection Model gives the Texans a win total of 8.44 and a 30% playoff shot that jumps to 35.9% with a win – not a huge jump, as the model largely expects a Houston win.

Stroud has been great after a disappointing 2025 season and is going to make major bank soon playing on his fourth-year rookie deal without an extension. It's not all on the usually terrific Houston defense this year, but a lot of it is: That unit is allowing 6.9 points per game more than last season (17.4 to 24.3). That's the worst such rise in the NFL.

The Chargers are only the third team in the past 30 seasons to start 0-7 after making the playoffs along with the 1997 Colts and 2011 Colts. The '97 season was the year before Peyton Manning arrived, and he missed '11 with a neck injury. Los Angeles quarterback Justin Herbert hasn't missed a game. He has just been bad while making $52.5 million per season. Now, have injuries around Herbert affected things? No doubt about it, especially his offensive line. Amazingly, Herbert is the only quarterback in NFL history with an interception and multiple sacks taken in each of his team's first seven games. His 10 picks are seven more than he had in his 2024 breakout season and only three shy of last year's very good campaign.

Herbert has all of five touchdown passes and none in the fourth quarter with six interceptions. He is the first quarterback ever to start 0-7 coming off a season in which he made a Pro Bowl and his team made the playoffs. And really the offense under first-year coordinator Mike McDaniel, the very highly regarded former Dolphins head coach, has been the issue. I mentioned the Texans' defense had the worst PPG rise in the league from last year. The Bolts have the biggest PPG drop offensively at minus-7.5 points (21.7 to 14.1).

Many of L.A.'s futures odds in the preseason were very similar to Houston. Needless to say, the playoffs are not happening now and the updated win total is only 4.5. If you bet Under 9.5 in the summer, you could already cash Sunday with a loss. It would be the quickest team to clinch the Under for its win total since the 2020 Cowboys.

One more insane statistic for Los Angeles: It is the first team with zero straight up and ATS wins (0-6-1 there) in its first seven since Washington in 1998. Maybe we should have seen this fade coming, especially with Herbert? It actually started in Week 17 last year when the Bolts lost at home, 20-17, to the Texans. Herbert then sat the meaningless regular-season finale but it didn't help at all as he was brutal in the wild card loss at New England. The Bolts are 0-10 SU and 0-9-1 ATS in their past 10 overall.
