Joe Scarborough goes after ESPN’s Pat McAfee: ‘It is a shame for the viewer’

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Pat McAfee is a problem for ESPN's "College GameDay," Joe Scarborough of "Morning Joe" hammered away at Tuesday during election day.

McAfee's recent headline-grabbing issues with the University of Utah was the tipping point.

"I've just had enough of this Pat McAfee ruining 'College GameDay,'" Scarborough said Tuesday. "I look forward to waking up on Saturday mornings and watching that. ... It's like he's melting down. ...

"It's really ESPN. I mean, they run their company the way they want to run their company, but man, it is a shame for the viewer."

McAfee revealed there have been death threats against him as a result of the controversy surrounding ESPN's "College GameDay" visit to Utah.

"I've stayed away from my mentions and DMs this month since these back to back days but ... I'm sure if I went in there now, I'd find some wishes for and threats of death for me," McAfee posted on X. "'Don't read them.' They're everywhere."

The post includes screenshots from direct messages threatening violence against him and tweets expressing a desire to see him dead.

The issue, according to Scarborough, is the show has gotten away from college football being played on Saturday and it's more about itself.

"I love watching Rece (Davis)," he said. "I love watching those guys and watching Nick (Saban) on there talking. ... Kirk Herbstreit's amazing. Desmond (Howard) — they're all great. And they're all being eclipsed by ESPN's just insatiable desire to give this guy more time."

"Maybe you aren't having to be yelling at people and being freaked out because two kids in Utah put up signs or say something that might offend you," Scarborough said.

Meanwhile, McAfee said he felt "uncomfortable" after the University of Utah posted a since-deleted video of students "dogging" him. He also said the video shared by the university account was inciteful against him.

McAfee cited the visit to the University of Utah reminded him of assassination of Charlie Kirk.
