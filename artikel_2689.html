Scottie Barnes just passed this Raptors legend in points scored

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

After his last game, Scottie Barnes snagged the title of player with the seventh most points ever for the Toronto Raptors. Having already vaulted over Fred VanVleet and Jonas Valančiūnas earlier in his career to comfortably move up the top 10, Barnes has just passed Morris Peterson, who played seven years in Toronto in the 2000s.

Mo Pete was a Michigan State swingman who was drafted 21st overall in the 2000 NBA draft, and finished fourth in the rookie of the year race in his first season with the Raps. He had four ironman seasons for the Raps, playing all 82 games from 2003-2006, starting in many of them. His durability and place as a secondary offensive weapon allowed him to rack up points during his tenure in the purple and red, often averaging double digits.

During his time on the Raps, Peterson went to the playoffs several times alongside both Vince Carter and Chris Bosh, ultimately leaving during the latter's time as the face of the Raptors' franchise.

In many respects, what people said about Mo Pete in his heyday reflects similarly onto Scottie Barnes. "The fans are going to love him," said former Raptors coach Lenny Wilkens about Peterson. And indeed, the fans love Scottie. CBC heaped praise upon Peterson for his maturity, and seeing how Barnes has stepped into a leadership role reflects the continued legacy of accountability amongst Raptors legends.

In 2007, after his departure from the team, Adam Francis for RHQ wrote "A Eulogy for Morris Peterson," where he wrote further about what Peterson brought to the team.

I don't expect to see his jersey retired at some point and he was never as popular as Alvin or JYD…but I can't think of anyone in the history of the organization who better exemplifies all things Raptors; from the highs to the lows, Peterson's career has in many ways echoed the franchise's success.

Scottie has just moved past one of the team's great role players, and it is unfortunate that this means that there is one less reason to talk about what Mo Pete did for the franchise. But, Scottie Barnes is ascending. By the end of the season, Barnes is all but guaranteed to pass up the next Raptor on the list: number one overall pick, Andrea Bargnani.
