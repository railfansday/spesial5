Colston Loveland goes to locker room in the first quarter

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A key member of the Bears offense has gone to the locker room with an injury in the first quarter.

Tight end Colston Loveland was checked out in the sideline medical tent and then loaded onto a cart to head inside for further evaluations. Loveland had one catch for five yards, but then lost the ball on a hit by Lions defensive end Derrick Moore. Linebacker Malcolm Rodriguez recovered the ball to end the first Bears offensive possession of the day.

Loveland went to the locker room after the second Chicago possession and center Garrett Bradbury also suffered an injury on that drive.

The Lions drove for a field goal to open the game and remain up 3-0 with under three minutes to play in the first quarter.
