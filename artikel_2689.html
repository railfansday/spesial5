NBA Trade Rumors: Trade coming for the Jazz

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

According to multiple reports, the Utah Jazz are looking to make a trade. The first report comes from Shreyas Laddha, who reported that the Jazz are going hunting.

According to multiple team sources who were granted anonymity because they had not been cleared to speak publicly on the matter, the Jazz don't appear to have any real interest in Irving.

One league source told The Tribune that Utah has spoken with the Los Angeles Clippers about a couple of different players.

According to the source, the Jazz initially reached out about various Clippers bigs, but the focus has shifted to wing depth.

The source noted that Utah didn't have specific players in mind, but the Jazz have "cast a wide net."

If Utah does make moves, they will likely come after Dec. 15, when players who signed contracts during the 2026 offseason become trade-eligible.

Knowing the Jazz talked about the Clippers bigs means they likely asked about Brook Lopez and obviously nothing happened. Could this also mean the Jazz asked about Kris Dunn? It would have been a great addition to this team and would have helped an already improving Jazz defense. Having Dunn would also mean the Jazz get another ball-handler who could relieve some pressure on Keyonte George and Darryn Peterson.

But that obviously didn't happen, and the Jazz are switching to candidates that are available on December 15th when more players will be available.

We know that Utah reached out to the Chicago Bulls about Tre Jones. They didn't want Isaiah Collier, but maybe they asked them about Norm Powell? I ask because I think that's a player who would be fantastic for the Jazz.

But now we have another rumor about the Jazz looking for a trade from Jake Fischer on the latest Stein Line.

The Jazz did not have nearly the contending-level aspirations that Indianaharbored at the start of the season, but Jazz team owner Ryan Smith isknown to be eager to be competitive after a few lean seasons in Salt LakeCity.

A slew of frontcourt injures has contributed heavily to Utah's 3-16 start, butsources say that the Jazz have been actively canvassing the market insearch of trade additions to help them at least reach the level of Play-InTournament contender that it expected to be going into the season.

Jaren Jackson Jr., Lauri Markkanen and Jusuf Nurkić are all currentlysidelined for the Jazz, but we've been repeatedly advised to keep our eyeson them for potential moves as Dec. 15 draws near and more teamsgradually get serious about doing deals.

Clearly, the Jazz will be active, and we should see something soon. For Jazz fans, it'll be a welcome sight to see something added to the team. As Fischer mentioned, the Jazz have dealt with a slew of injuries, and it has contributed to a very dissapointing start to the season.

Yes, the Jazz have shown some real positives this season. They've seen Darryn Peterson show incredible potential, Ace Bailey continues to be a high-impact player, and Keyonte George has shown some real all-star upside. Those things have been great to see, but they haven't translated to many wins. The issues have been injuries, a lack of consistency from the young core, a very underwhelming bench, and some very odd lineup decisions from Will Hardy. Yes, Utah has also had some impressive performances and has been close in many of their losses, but they need help to start winning. Hopefully the Jazz can make some moves soon to get this season back on track sooner rather than later.
