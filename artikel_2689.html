Von Miller blames himself, not the play call, for Christian McCaffrey's late TD

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Cowboys pulled within 38-32 of the 49ers with 3:42 left in the fourth quarter on Sunday. Only 19 seconds later, Christian McCaffrey was celebrating in the end zone.

Cowboys defensive coordinator Christian Parker is under fire for calling cover zero on second-and-10. He sent a six-man blitz, leaving man-to-man coverage and Von Miller matched up with McCaffrey.

McCaffrey called it “a perfect look for that play.”

The running back ran a seam route, quickly got a step (or two or three) on the 37-year-old edge rusher, caught the ball at the 40 and was off to the races. Miller reached 18.5 mph while chasing McCaffrey, but it wasn’t fast enough.

Miller, though, defended Parker and the play call on Monday.

“As a defensive coordinator in that situation, you want to be aggressive,” Miller said, via Nick Harris of the Fort Worth Star-Telegram. “We were trying to win the game. We were trying to create momentum. We were down by six points. If we could have just got off the field. . . . As a defensive coordinator, you want to be aggressive and want to win the game. That’s the type of mindset you want in these games.”

Parker is three years younger than Miller, but has Miller’s full support despite the struggles of the Cowboys defense. Dallas allowed 584 yards to the 49ers.

“Nah, that’s crazy,” Miller said of the calls for Parker’s job. “Christian is an incredible leader of men. All of these offensive and defensive coordinators are geniuses. It’s really getting your players to buy in. What happened with me yesterday is a microcosm of what has happened the whole season. Instead of me looking for a simpler way to do that job, I have to find a better way to do it. That had nothing to do with Christian Parker. I had been coached on how to play that route. I’ve had examples of that route, went over that play in practice this week. I just didn’t make that play. That had nothing to do with Christian Parker. I just didn’t execute.”
