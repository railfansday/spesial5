NFLSU rookie check-in: Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Delane was able to bounce back from his injury woes and was dominant against the Cardinals. He didn't make any crazy plays, but he also didn't make mistakes, which is exactly what you want from a CB1.

The Chiefs will be playing on Thanksgiving against the Buffalo Bills, which will be the biggest game of Delane's early career.

We finally got the A.J. Haulcy game. The rookie showed out against the Houston Texans, ending the game with an impressive interception and led the team in run stops. Indianapolis still got destroyed in this game, like usual, but Haulcy truly stood out.

Next game will be against the New York Giants, another game where Haulcy can get a turnover.

Next up is Zavion Thomas, and he had a really good game last week. He had two receptions for 56 yards, with one being a huge completion for the Bears, and he also got a 30-yard run. In the battle with Barion Brown, who we will get to, Thomas was utilized much better and did really well when called upon.

Another one of our Thanksgiving games, Thomas and the Bears will be playing against the Detroit Lions, and maybe Thomas can have yet another big play.

It was nice to see Sharp get a target this week, but once again, we are not seeing enough usage.

As I mentioned earlier, Thomas and Brown faced off last week, and Thomas clearly won. Brown had some moments on special teams, but his usage on offense wasn't great, and he did much worse on an identical run that Thomas took for 30 yards.

The Saints offense is all over the place, so Brown will need to rely on special teams to make a difference.

The Falcons were on their bye week, and their first game back will be against the Minnesota Vikings. It will be the Kirk Cousins revenge game, so now it's Perkins' job to stop that from happening.

Did not play because Patrick Mahomes is healthy.
