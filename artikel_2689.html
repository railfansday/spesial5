Why did Pacers teammates force T.J. McConnell to sit down? To help him up

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Indiana Pacers improved to 4-8 with a 114-101 win over the Washington Wizards Wednesday night, but not without a little fun from T.J. McConnell and company.

McConnell's finger roll with 3:19 to go in the third quarter sent the guard to the baseline floor when contact was made by Washington's Tre Johnson. He quickly popped up after the play only to be directed back to his seat on the baseline by his teammates.

Why? So they could help him up, of course.
