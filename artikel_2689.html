Vikings rule out Jordan Addison, Christian Darrisaw, Isaiah Rodgers for Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Vikings will not have a few key players when they host the Falcons on Sunday.

Head coach Kevin O’Connell told reporters in his Friday press conference that receiver Jordan Addison (thumb), offensive tackle Christian Darrisaw (shoulder), and cornerback Isaiah Rodgers (thigh) are all out for Week 12.

O’Connell noted that the team is hoping Darrisaw will return for Week 13. Ryan Van Demark is expected to start at left tackle.

Safety Harrison Smith (thigh) is questionable for the contest.

Defensive lineman Jalen Redmond (ankle), receiver Tai Felton (hip), receiver Myles Price (knee/groin), and quarterback Kyler Murray (hamstring) are all off the injury report and are healthy to play.
