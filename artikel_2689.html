The latest about Kawhi Leonard’s health

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Earlier this evening the Raptors put out a short statement regarding the state of Kawhi Leonard. Sidelined since game two with issues regarding his left knee, things seem to be proceeding well, albeit gradually, with the forward's return to the court.

The organization has outlined that "Leonard has progressed" regarding his ramp up, but has not given any indication as to when the forward might progress significantly back to a playable status. Similarly to last year, Leonard missed a stretch of 10 games before the All-Star break before bouncing back to near MVP-level play for the rest of the year.

Hopefully, the Raps are getting the same Klaw back — and the sooner, the better.

Week by week, there has been no major indication that anything unexpected is happening with Leonard, one way or the other. Progression seems to be slow, and whether they are present or not, serious concerns have not been divulged to the media or the public. Leonard, who has already missed more than 10 games this season, will almost undoubtedly be unavailable for the foreseeable stretch of games. Seeing Leonard in December would be a breath of fresh air for this stagnant team, but that itself is far from a certainty.

Tonight, the Raptors are matching up with the Knicks without Leonard, and are certainly missing his scoring punch, as they have since game two. Without Kawhi, much has been revealed about the team, but it has also led to a lot of vitriol and frustration being expressed as to the state of the team's construction.

These are flaws that Leonard might have been able to cover up with his stellar play — and maybe it's better for all of that to be laid out for the team to see and understand. But nevertheless; this year was never supposed to be a Kawhi-less campaign. When he returns, Leonard will ideally bring consistency beyond what he is usually known for, to stabilize both the team, and the Raptors' fanbase. Until then, the Raps fight on.
