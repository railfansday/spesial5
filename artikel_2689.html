Stampede Blue Reacts Survey (Week 10): Are Colts fans confident after major roster changes?

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Welcome to SB Nation Reacts, where Colts fans and NFL fans nationwide share their opinions throughout the season.

The Indianapolis Colts have officially entered a new chapter following one of the most eventful trade deadlines in franchise history. After parting ways with Quenton Nelson and DeForest Buckner two weeks ago, Indianapolis continued its roster overhaul Tuesday by sending veteran wide receiver Keenan Allen to the San Francisco 49ers and Josh Downs to the Kansas City Chiefs. The departures of four established players have allowed the Colts to accumulate additional draft capital, although they have also created significant holes across a roster that was already struggling to compete. With Chris Ballard no longer in charge and a new general manager expected to take over during the offseason, the organization appears increasingly focused on building toward the future rather than salvaging the remainder of 2026.

Interestingly, Jonathan Taylor was one of the few major names who ultimately remained in Indianapolis despite considerable interest from around the league. The Colts reportedly received multiple offers involving second-round selections but maintained a much higher asking price for their star running back, deciding to retain him rather than accept compensation they considered insufficient. Keeping Taylor provides some stability for an offense that has already lost several important contributors, although questions remain about whether his remaining prime seasons will align with the franchise's next competitive window. The decision also leaves the next general manager with an important choice regarding the long-term direction of the offense.

Meanwhile, the situation on the field hasn't improved much. Indianapolis suffered its fifth consecutive defeat Sunday, falling 27-23 to the Dallas Cowboys and dropping to 2-7 on the season. Last week's Reacts survey showed that just 19 percent of Colts fans believed the franchise was headed in the right direction, down from 31 percent the previous week and 47 percent two weeks earlier. While the continued losing has understandably frustrated fans, the organization's willingness to make significant changes could provide some optimism that a more sustainable future is being built. With several additional draft selections now available but plenty of uncertainty surrounding the roster, coaching staff and quarterback position, the question remains whether fans believe the Colts are finally taking the necessary steps toward returning to contention.
