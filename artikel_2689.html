Half of Chiefs Kingdom believes KC wins AFC West. Do you?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Last week our SBN Reacts question focused on a situation that 24 hours later completely dissolved — the Broncos' trade with the Chargers for Daiyan Henley.

Which was a shame for multiple reasons, mainly that 87% of us generally liked the trade.

Of course, that was before anyone knew of the injury issues. So in retrospect, I imagine that 87% with a B-grade or higher would also grade the decision to get out of the trade as a good one.

But it turns out that Chiefs Kingdom did some work for us as well by asking its fans if the Chiefs would win the AFC West — and surprisingly (at least to me) it was only 50% said they would.

Given the Chiefs' 35-13 beatdown of the Falcons yesterday, it's possible that number is higher now.

And with the Chiefs' win over the Falcons, they reclaimed the top spot in the AFC West (for now), putting the Broncos as the fifth seed Wild Card team for the playoffs — a very different position than the No. 2-knocking-on-the-door-to-No.1-seed that they were a week ago.

Chiefs still play the Cardinals, Bills, Rams, Bengals, Patriots, Niners, Chargers, Raiders.

Broncos' remaining schedule is Raiders, Steelers, Dolphins, Jets, Raiders, Bills, Patriots, Chargers.

On paper, the Broncos' path seems much easier, but the Broncos don't make anything easy and a healthy Patrick Mahomes always makes it difficult for opponents.

The Broncos can still win the AFC West — and even regain the No. 1 seed in the AFC — but it could be a neck-and-neck race all the way to the finish line.

So Broncos Country, what do you think about the Chiefs' chances…which team ultimately sits atop the AFC West by the end of the season?
