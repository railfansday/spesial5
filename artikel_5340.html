Retail Dumps XRP as Smart Money Starts Buying Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

XRP holders are moving billions of tokens toward exchanges, but ETF investors just started buying again. Spot XRP ETFs recorded $4.07 million in net inflows, their first positive day since late October. The timing stands out as retail sentiment sours and XRP price struggles to regain momentum.

CryptoQuant data shows 4.61 billion XRP flowed into exchanges over the past 30 days, with Binance receiving the largest share. That’s down from October’s peak, but the volume remains substantial. Investors are still moving XRP toward trading venues where they can sell.

Institutional buyers are going the other way. After weeks of disappointing flows, the ETFs are back in the green.

Are retail holders selling into weakness just as institutional investors begin buying the dip?

November has been a rough month for XRP ETFs, with flows largely flat or negative. Tuesday’s $4.07 million inflow isn’t enough to reverse that trend, but it marks a change after weeks of withdrawals.

Retail investors have far less reason to feel optimistic. XRP’s price weakness has tested patience, while billions of tokens are moving onto exchanges, which could eventually result in another wave of selling. The mood is considerably different from the optimism that fueled previous rallies.

Institutions, however, may be looking at the same price action differently. ETF investors can build positions without chasing a rally, and periods of widespread pessimism often offer better entry points for buyers willing to wait. The latest inflow could be an early sign that some are taking that opportunity.

Crypto sentiment is looking battered, but some investors are already positioning for the next cycle. Bitcoin Hyper has attracted $33.3 million in funding, with investors betting that Bitcoin’s next generation will need more than just a store-of-value narrative.

Its pitch centers on bringing faster transactions and utility to Bitcoin, potentially opening the door to applications that today’s network struggles to support.

The timing could prove interesting. HYPER is launching in just hours, while the market looks increasingly beaten down. Buying during a downturn carries obvious risks, but it also means entering before a potential recovery puts speculative projects back in the spotlight.

HYPER is currently priced at just $0.0136877 for now; investors are getting in before exchange trading begins, with staking rewards at 35% APY as a bonus.

With the current APY rewards, early buyers can earn additional tokens while waiting for the market to recover, rather than simply sitting on their allocation. Rewards can increase token holdings.

XRP once offered investors an opportunity to get in before its utility narrative reached mainstream attention. Hyper is chasing a similar early-stage opportunity around Bitcoin infrastructure.

HYPER is launching in just hours; the best time to buy could be today.

The timing could prove significant if ETF inflows continue while exchange deposits begin to fade. That combination would show that institutional demand is returning just as potential selling pressure starts easing, giving XRP a better shot at reversing its decline.

For now, neither side has won. ETF demand remains modest, and exchange inflows are still elevated. But retail holders appear to be getting increasingly nervous while institutional money has finally returned for a day.

XRP could find support from buyers who are comfortable accumulating while everyone else waits for a reason to sell.
