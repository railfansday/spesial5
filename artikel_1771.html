Prediction: Poland vs Sweden

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Verdict: Over 2.5 goals Best odds: 69/100 Bookmaker: 1xBet

UEFA Nations League, League B, Group 4 | Tuesday, November 17, 2026 | Tarczyński Arena, Wrocław | 7:45 PM GMT

Sweden arrives in Wrocław with promotion secured, while Poland still has an uncomfortable evening ahead. Saturday’s defeat in Romania left Jan Urban’s side just one point above last place. A victory would guarantee third and a relegation playoff, though anything less would leave Poland dependent on the result in Zenica, where Romania visits Bosnia and Herzegovina.

Poland had opportunities to make Saturday’s game in Bucharest considerably easier. Robert Lewandowski put the ball in the net early, only for an offside flag to rule it out, and later forced a save from Otto Hindrich with the score still level.

Instead, Romania scored twice in quick succession through Alexandru Cicaldau and Dennis Man. Marcel Reguła’s 82nd-minute reply gave Poland something to chase, but the equalizer never came. Urban now has to get more out of his attack while rebuilding a defense that lost two starters over the weekend.

There is encouragement in Sweden’s second-half performance against Bosnia. Two deliveries into the penalty area produced two goals, and Poland has Lewandowski to attack those situations. Getting enough players forward to support him, however, will leave space for an opponent that already scored three times against Poland in September.

Matty Cash has left the squad after suffering a concussion against Romania. Jakub Kiwior is suspended, while Przemysław Frankowski had already withdrawn through injury. Arkadiusz Pyrka replaced Cash in Bucharest and is an option to start on the right. Piotr Zieliński remains a fitness concern after missing Saturday’s game.

For 45 minutes on Saturday, Sweden looked ready to celebrate promotion without much fuss. Daniel Svensson scored his first international goal, Viktor Gyökeres added two more, and the hosts went into halftime 3-0 ahead.

The second half was considerably less comfortable. Armin Gigović scored shortly after the restart, Sead Kolašinac headed in another, and Sweden needed some desperate defending to preserve its 3-2 lead. Graham Potter got the result, but Poland will have noticed how much trouble Bosnia caused in the air.

Gyökeres now has six of Sweden’s ten group goals. Even if Potter uses this final game to give others minutes, Benjamin Nygren and Alexander Bernhardsson provide attacking alternatives with experience from earlier in the campaign.

Potter has room to rotate after securing first place. Dejan Kulusevski’s workload is still being managed following his lengthy injury absence, while Besfort Zeneli and Patrik Wålemark have withdrawn through injury. Any changes to the starting attack remain a selection decision.

Poland has a reason to push forward and an opponent whose defensive weaknesses were exposed on Saturday. Sweden should also find opportunities against a reshuffled home defense. A 2-2 draw is my preference, with over 2.5 goals the selection.

Verdict: Over 2.5 goals Best odds: 69/100 Bookmaker: 1xBet
