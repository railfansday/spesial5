Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Noah Okafor came off the bench to end Tottenham’s winning run and secure a 1-0 win for Leeds in the early kick-off at Elland Road.

A feisty encounter looked to be heading for a stalemate before Okafor collected a loose ball inside the area and fired home to break the deadlock for Leeds in the 79th minute.
