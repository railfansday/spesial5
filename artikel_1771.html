Worthing Women: Two-goal Nicole not quite enough for Reds

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Despite another brace for a red-hot Nicole Mackay, Worthing Women were edged out in a five-goal ding-dong at Eastbourne Borough.

There was some good news pre-match with the return of Captain Holly Talbut-Smith, in the only change to the starting line-up from last week’s win over Aylesford. Kendall Varitek also came back in for Ludmila De Sousa on the bench.

Aiming to build on three precious points achieved at home the previous Sunday, the Rebels travelled East to the Connect Management Stadium to face bottom-of-the-table Borough, although it was the struggling hosts who almost caught their guests by surprise in under a minute. Isobel Langridge sent in a cross that had goalkeeper Sian Woods backtracking to palm the ball to safety.

Moments later, the visitors retaliated by way of Neve McEvaddy releasing the nippy Jess Richardson through the inside-right channel who, in turn, picked out Nicole Mackay for a shot which flashed across the face of goal.

The same player went close again when a lucky break in the box failed to extend to Mackay, as her effort went wide once more.

However, Worthing’s number nineteen wouldn't be denied for much longer.

Richardson won a free-kick in a central position around twenty yards from goal and up stepped the Red’s top scorer to fire home the set-piece via a deflection, which wrong-footed Sophia Moule between the home posts.

The lead was nearly doubled shortly afterwards, only for the crossbar to keep out Mackay following a flicked header applied to a deadball delivery from McEvaddy.

Borough bit back via a misplaced pass providing an opening for Langridge to land a long ranger on top of the net, around the half-hour mark.

Then some excellent defending by first, returning Skipper Talbut-Smith resulted in Molly Hill being ushered away from the target, allowing netminder Woods to pounce on the ball and complete the clean up operation.

A presentable chance offered an opportunity to an unmarked Richardson in the penalty area in the closing stages of the first forty-five, though the fleet-footed winger couldn’t keep her attempt down.

McEvaddy was forced off at the break, to be replaced by Varitek but it would be the Reds of Eastbourne Borough who came out of the blocks quickest after the turnaround.

A mere three minutes into the second-half, Lola Dulake located the dome of Hill, who offered Worthing a possible mountain to climb by heading home to level the score.

Steepness then nearly became the order of the day, thanks to Hill coming mighty close to doubling her tally and putting her side in front within a matter of seconds.

Showing no signs of letting up, Hill, who else ? was inches away again when she put a good chance over the apparatus, before the deadly duo of Dulake and Hill linked up to devastating effect to extend the homesters’ advantage, just shy of the hour.

The former crossed from the right and the latter gleefully gobbled up the opportunity to nod in her brace.

Roles were slightly reversed at the point Duklake fizzed a low attempt dangerously close to the target and Woods was called into action to repel a close-range effort, as the home team threatened to pull clear of their beleaguered opponents.

Dulake turned Ella Terry but could only find the hands of Woods, by which time Skye Birkinshaw had entered the fray in place of youngster Hattie Rainey, as Manager Jesus Cordon and his Coaching Staff tried to find a way back into the contest for the underfire travellers.

Fellow substitute Daisy Taylor almost had the opposite effect, as her long distance rocket forced a fine diving save out of Woods, at the expense of a corner.

A through ball might have put the game beyond Worthing’s reach, had Woods not had her wits about her to race out of her goal to intercept and smother at the feet of the hat-trick hunting Hill.

Six minutes from the end of ninety, Hill claimed the matchball by getting the better of Terry and gaining added purchase on an effort which crashed off the underside of the crossbar and fell the right slide of the line, for Borough.

Not letting their heads drop though, Rebels let out a yell when Mackay made it five goals in her last three games, as she swivelled on the edge of the eighteen yard box to beat Moule and set up a tense conclusion.

Unfortunately however, three-two to an understandably elated Eastbourne saw them collect only their second league win of the campaign, although, with just four points separating the bottom five clubs, nobody is safe.
