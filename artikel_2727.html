Spurs' Dylan Harper suffers apparent injury late in Monday night's loss to Detroit

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Dynamic Spurs guard Dylan Harper suffered an injury late in Tuesday night's 105-93 loss to Detroit at the Frost Bank Center.

Harper headed to the locker room aided by teammate Keldon Johnson and a team staffer after slipping and landing awkwardly on the court while attempting to catch a pass from Stephon Castle with 53.6 seconds left. He grabbed at his left groin and hip area while in visible pain before Spurs star Victor Wembanyama rushed over to check on him.

There was no word from the Spurs (12-3) after the game about the extent of the injury. It also wasn't immediately known if a wet spot on the floor caused Harper to go down.

"It just looked like he slipped, obviously, so hoping for the best, but don't know anything yet," Spurs coach Mitch Johnson said.

Harper finished with 20 points on 8-of-14 shooting (0 of 4 from 3-point range), four rebounds and two assists in 35 minutes.

Wembanyama said it was a "terrible feeling, terrible sight" to see the 20-year-old New Jersey native in pain.

"I know as much as you guys, so I hope he's fine," said Wembanyama, who had 25 points and 18 rebounds in the loss, which snapped the Spurs' 12-game winning streak. "I'll check on him today. But, yeah, I just hate this. I don't know how else I can say this."

Harper entered the game averaging 18.3 points on 50% shooting from the field and 34.0% from 3-point range to go along with 5.5 rebounds, 4.7 assists and 1.0 steals in 31.5 minutes. He was fresh off scoring a career-high 31 points in a 110-102 victory at Utah on Sunday afternoon.

"He's really fit into this role where we can trust him and we know we can go to him because he's relentless for defenses," Wembanyama said. "They can't rest when he's on the court because he's so able to attack the rim and so smart with his body. So he's just a reliable guy."

Several NBA stars have suffered injuries this season, but the Spurs have yet to have a major setback. That could change, though, after Harper's ugly mishap.

"At certain parts of (last) season we didn't have certain guys and what made us go is we were able to kind of cover for guys when they were out," Spurs guard De'Aaron Fox said. "So, right now, it looks like we might have to do that again."
