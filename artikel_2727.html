Giants sign WR Xavier Gipson, DB Tavierre Thomas to their active roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Giants signed wide receiver Xavier Gipson and defensive back Tavierre Thomas to their active roster on Monday, the team announced.

Gipson was on the Eagles’ practice squad, and Thomas was on the Vikings’ practice squad.

The Giants cut wide receiver Charlie Jones and defensive back Nikko Reed from their practice squad in corresponding moves.

This is Gipson’s third stint with the Giants.

The Eagles signed Gipson to their practice squad on Sept. 11 after he spent training camp with the Giants.

Gipson, 25, was with the Eagles and Giants in 2025.

He has appeared in 41 regular-season games with three starts for the Jets (2023-25), Giants (2025) and Eagles (2025). Gipson has yet to appear in a game this season.

Thomas played all 17 games each of the previous two years, but has appeared in only one game this season. He made his 2026 debut last Monday night against the Bills, playing 24 snaps on special teams.

In 116 regular-season games with 22 starts for the Browns (2018-20), Texans (2021-23), Buccaneers (2024) and Vikings (2025-26). His career numbers include 193 tackles, seven forced fumbles, six tackles for loss, two forced fumbles and two interceptions.
