Ravens not planning change to starting offensive line after Ryan Neuzil trade

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Ravens made a trade for center Ryan Neuzil this week, but they aren’t planning on throwing him right into the lineup in Week 10.

Neuzil started every game for the Falcons this season and he also made 17 starts in 2025. The Falcons’ offensive line coach in 2025 was current Ravens offensive line coach Dwayne Ledford, so Neuzil feels he understands “what [Ledford] wants and what he sees in the linemen.”

That knowledge should help Neuzil pick things up on the fly, but head coach Jesse Minter said Neuzil’s arrival doesn’t mean current center Cam Jurgens will be knocked out of the lineup.

“I think he brings versatility,” Minter said, via the team’s website. “I don’t anticipate anything changing with Cam and what position he plays. We think he’s like the future of our offensive line. I think it’s just a piece that adds depth. I do think he’ll have flexibility as he gets here, as he gets going. Wherever that leads I think we’ll see as we go.”

The Ravens have had to change plans at center multiple times since Tyler Linderbaum’s departure during the offseason. If they need to make another one, Neuzil will give them an experienced option to plug the hole.
