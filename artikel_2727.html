Rams C Coleman Shelton to miss 4-6 weeks with a hamstring injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Rams are on their bye this week and that gives them some extra time to figure out how to address the center position before their return to action against the Packers in Week 12.

Starter Coleman Shelton hurt his hamstring during Sunday’s win over the Cardinals and a team spokesperson announced on Monday night that he is expected to miss 4-6 weeks as a result of the injury. Shelton started all 10 games this season.

The Rams’ issues were compounded when Shelton’s backup Dylan McMahon also left the game. The team announced that he will be placed on injured reserve with a torn patellar tendon.

Beaux Limmer started the win over the Cardinals at guard in place of the inactive Steve Avila and moved over to center after the injuries. Bill Murray took over at guard and the Rams will hope to have Avila back when they return to the field the night before Thanksgiving.
