Jalen Hurts returns to Eagles facility, provides morale boost to team

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Philadelphia Eagles received encouraging news Wednesday, as quarterback Jalen Hurts returned to the team's practice facility for the first time in a while while continuing to deal with a serious medical condition that has sidelined him indefinitely.

According to ESPN's Tim McManus, Hurts was at the Eagles' facility Wednesday and attended the team meeting along with other meetings. Although the veteran quarterback did not address his teammates, his presence boosted morale for some players who had not seen him since Philadelphia placed him on injured reserve Oct. 17. The development comes during a difficult stretch for the Eagles, who have lost six consecutive games and fallen to 2-6 heading into Sunday's NFC East matchup against the New York Giants. Philadelphia has struggled to find consistency on offense while dealing with a series of injuries, including the loss of its franchise quarterback.

Hurts has been sidelined since being diagnosed with a blood clot in his calf, with subsequent medical evaluations raising additional concerns. His condition has made his health and recovery the organization's top priority, and he is expected to miss the rest of the season.

While Wednesday's appearance does not signal a change in Hurts' medical status or suggest an imminent return to football activities, it represents an encouraging development for a locker room that has endured significant adversity.

Hurts has been one of Philadelphia's most important leaders throughout his tenure, providing stability at quarterback while helping establish the team's expectations and competitive identity.

His absence has been felt beyond the football field, particularly as the Eagles have struggled to regain their footing following a promising start to the season.

Head coach Nick Sirianni previously acknowledged how difficult it has been to be without his starting quarterback, emphasizing their relationship and expressing concern for Hurts' well-being.

The Eagles have also been forced to adjust their quarterback situation since Hurts landed on injured reserve. Tanner McKee started the past three games, but Philadelphia is making another change, turning to Joe Milton for Sunday's matchup against the Giants.

With the Eagles approaching their Week 10 bye, the organization is trying to stabilize a season that has quickly moved in the wrong direction. Philadelphia remains searching for answers on offense, and the upcoming matchup provides another opportunity to end its losing streak.

For Hurts, the focus remains on his recovery rather than any potential return to the lineup. However, his appearance at the facility Wednesday gave teammates an opportunity to reconnect with their quarterback and provided a positive moment during an otherwise frustrating season.

The Eagles still have significant problems to solve on the field, but seeing their franchise quarterback back around the team offered something that has been in short supply recently: an encouraging development.
