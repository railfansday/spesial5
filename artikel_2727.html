The Sixers need to be smart with Jaylen Brown’s hamstring injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Sixers staved off a ferocious comeback from the Brooklyn Nets on Monday, but they didn't emerge unscathed. Jaylen Brown suffered a cut on his forehead after running into Julius Randle early in the fourth quarter, although he was eventually able to return to the game. However, he left with what head coach Nick Nurse called left hamstring "tightness" with less than two minutes remaining.

The Sixers have already ruled Brown out from Wednesday's game against the Orlando Magic with what they labeled a "left hamstring strain." He told reporters on Monday that his hamstring "just got a little tight" against Brooklyn, but he didn't seem worried about it.

"I've had a lot of hamstring stuff," Brown said, according to Gina Mizell of the Philadelphia Inquirer. "I don't think it's concerning."

Even if Brown's hamstring injury winds up being much ado about nothing, the Sixers should err on the side of caution this early in the season. They just need to look down the street to DeVonta Smith and the Eagles as a reminder of how quickly a hamstring injury can spiral into a much bigger issue if a player returns too quickly.

ESPN's Kris Rhim reported that Brown is set to be reevaluated ahead of Saturday's game against the Milwaukee Bucks. The Bucks, who are currently 1-13, will be heading into their third game in four nights against the Sixers on Saturday after facing the Miami Heat on Wednesday and Portland Trail Blazers on Thursday. The Sixers, who'll have two days off after facing Orlando on Wednesday, should be able to beat the Bucks with or without Brown.

That game against Milwaukee begins a three-game homestand for the Sixers, who face the Portland Trail Blazers on Monday and the Miami Heat in an NBA Cup game on Wednesday. The Sixers then head to Indiana for their final NBA Cup game on Friday and take on the Houston Rockets on the second night of a back-to-back on Saturday.

After these next two games, five of the Sixers' next six opponents currently boast a winning record. The past week provided them with a much-needed reprieve after a tough start to the season, but their schedule is about to stiffen back up again. It's far more important for them to have Brown available for that stretch than it is these next two games. And if he misses these next two, he'll have nearly a full week to recover.

The Sixers soon may be getting reinforcements in the form of Dominick Barlow, who's set to be reevaluated later this week, according to Tony Jones of The Athletic. "If all goes well, the sense is he is nearing his season debut," Jones added. Barlow is nowhere near the three-level scorer that Brown is, but the Sixers might be able to better survive Brown's potential absence against the Bucks on Saturday if Barlow is able to suit up for that one. (He's already been ruled out of Wednesday's game.)

Brown has missed only one game this season—the Sixers' 98-93 victory over the New York Knicks in both teams' NBA Cup opener—and is fresh off back-to-back 31-point outings. He told reporters Monday that he's "feeling a bit more comfortable… starting to feel a little bit more at home." The last thing the Sixers should do is jeopardize that progress by allowing him to rush back from a hamstring injury.
