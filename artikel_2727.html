Chargers score first TD since Oct. 11, going 37 possessions without one

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Chargers went 37 possessions without a touchdown before Quentin Johnston scored on a 58-yard reception from Justin Herbert. It was the longest touchdown-less streak for a team — by possessions — since the 2024 Dolphins.

The Chargers trail the Texans 38-15 with 12:47 remaining in the fourth quarter.

Their previous touchdown came with 57 seconds left in the third quarter against the Broncos on Oct. 11 when Kimani Vidal scored on a 42-yard run in the 23-17 loss to Denver.

Herbert is 13-of-23 for 193 yards with a touchdown and an interception today.

The Texans have outgained the Chargers 460 to 240, with C.J. Stroud doing 21-of-34 for 384 yards with five touchdowns and an interception.
