Tech stocks rally after Nvidia board approves Jensen Huang's return as CEO

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Major tech stocks rebounded across the board Monday, following Friday's steep sell-off when artificial intelligence stocks fell in the Nasdaq's worst week since 2020.

Shares of Nvidia surged 30% after Jensen Huang was reinstated as CEO of the company.

Nvidia's surge and Huang's reinstatement follow a more than 40% slump in the stock over the past month, as Huang was ousted as CEO of the company he founded after he admitted to having an extramarital affair with a woman U.S. intelligence reportedly believes to be a Chinese spy.

The Nasdaq slid 10% last week and opened more than 6% higher Monday. All three major averages ended last week in correction territory, and the Nasdaq turned negative for 2026. Tech stocks remain rocky as investors remain nervous about the valuation of some AI companies and business models.

Stocks of Big Tech companies and other stocks related to the AI trade also saw a lift Monday after the Nvidia news.

Alphabet stock surged 6%, while Meta added more than 5%, Amazon was up nearly 5% and Microsoft jumped 4%. SpaceX climbed 9% and AMD added over 8.5%.

Neoclouds, which have seen a particularly choppy few months, surged. Nebius was up over 30%, CoreWeave 28% and Iren 28%.

Anthropic shares were flat after a brief premarket surge, in its third day of trading following its initial public offering on Thursday.

Anthropic raised a record $93.5 billion in its IPO. The stock gained 17% in its debut on Thursday, before closing down 8.5% on Friday.
