Tom Brady gets honest about Aaron Rodgers' Steelers exit: 'Nobody wins when the family feuds'

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Tom Brady unquestionably had his share of contentious moments during his 23 years in the NFL, but he just about always kept those disagreements in-house. Aaron Rodgers has routinely taken a different approach.

While Rodgers often reserves his public criticism of teammates and coaches for after the fact — he sharply criticized the Jets' regime once his time in New York ended — the four-time MVP ripped coach Mike McCarthy in shocking fashion on Wednesday.

Brady made it clear during Thursday's broadcast of Eagles vs. Cowboys that he would have approached Rodgers' situation much differently in his first comments on the matter.

Brady addressed the fallout from Rodgers' benching and release during Thursday's Fox broadcast, telling Kevin Burkhardt that the locker room should be kept "sacred" after Rodgers made several claims about McCarthy.

"The sanctity of an NFL locker room is a very sacred place, and we should never lose sight of that as NFL players," Brady said, with Burkhardt calling it a "sad way to end" if this is indeed the conclusion of Rodgers' career.

Brady stopped short of calling Rodgers a bad teammate, but he certainly seemed to imply it.

"I was fortunate to be around a lot of great teammates over the years, and what made a great teammate to me was you cared about your teammates and your coaches, and you cared about what the team was trying to accomplish," Brady said.

The future Hall of Famer discussed his own motivations during his career, explaining that he played for the individuals around him.

"I played for my teammates, I played for my coaches. Nobody wins when the family feuds."

While Brady didn't directly compare himself to Rodgers, the implication was pretty clear.

Rodgers passed through waivers unclaimed on Thursday, and it's unclear whether he will get another chance to play in the NFL.
