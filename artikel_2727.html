Audubon, Haddon Township to end Turkey Day football tradition

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

It's the final feast next week for one local Thanksgiving high school football tradition.

The Audubon-Haddon Township football series will be playing on the holiday for the last time with officials from both schools confirming the news on Thursday.

The final Turkey Day game is slated for 11 a.m. at Audubon High. The Green Wave enter the game with a 6-3 record under head coach Dan Reed while Haddon Township is 3-5 under head coach Bill Furlong.

Audubon won a 56-0 decision in last year's meeting, one that featured Haddon Township's debut of its new artificial turf field as well as the game being broadcasted on SportsRadio 94.1 WIP's Turkey Bowl.

As Thanksgiving football in South Jersey has dwindled in recent years, the clash for the Curley Cup on the holiday will be next to end.

Audubon leads the series 44-17-1 and boasts a 13-game winning streak in the rivalry, which began in 1963. The two teams play for the Curley Cup, named after Tom Curley, who coached football at both schools.

"We want to honor the rivalry and the tradition that Coach Curley brought to both schools, but the game won't be on Thanksgiving Day in the future," Haddon Township athletic director Tim George said.

"It's a very tough decision," Audubon athletic director Chris Baker said. "It's always a great atmosphere and it's a chance to see people you haven't seen in years or only see once a year.

"It's a great tradition and I hate to be on the tail end of it, but that's where things are trending (throughout South Jersey)."

George said that the two schools have already agreed on a date for next season. The game will be played on Veterans Day - Nov. 11 at Haddon Township.

Both George and Baker said the earlier start to the season has been one of the factors in the elimination of Turkey Day football.

Football practice began on Aug. 9 and the Hawks' last originally scheduled game was on Oct. 23. Township did pick up a crossover game against Riverside on Nov. 11. Audubon hasn't played since a 38-0 loss to Paulsboro in the Central Jersey Group 1 playoffs on Nov. 7.

"When I was playing, we didn't start practicing until Aug. 27," said George, who graduated from Collingswood High in 1994. "I love those Thanksgiving Day games during the '90s, but things are different now."

Once a staple on the scholastic sports calendar, there are only seven games being played around the holiday this season. Camden Catholic and Paul VI will play on Thanksgiving Eve, while the other six contests will be played on the holiday itself.

The NJSIAA hasn't made playing football on Thanksgiving any easier, either. The organization has put the state championships on the weekend after the holiday.

If Atlantic City, which plays Winslow in the state Group 4 semifinal on Friday, advances to the championship game, the Vikings will likely have to play reserves in their Thanksgiving Day contest at Holy Spirit. The state final is scheduled for two days later at MetLife Stadium in the Meadowlands.
