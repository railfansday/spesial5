Vikings won't put Jordan Addison on IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

When Vikings head coach Kevin O’Connell announced that wide receiver Jordan Addison needed thumb surgery earlier this week, he said that an injured reserve stint was a possibility.

O’Connell offered a positive update on Addison’s outlook on Wednesday. Addison had the operation and doctors have given the Vikings hope that he’ll be back before missing four games.

“I did want to give you guys some encouraging news on Jordan Addison,” O’Connell said, via the team’s website. “The updates following the surgery, he will not, as of right now, go on short-term IR. Just looking at the potential timeline for that, so that was really encouraging. Obviously subject to change as he gets into his rehab. He joined us back here today, and we’re excited to get him going.”

The Vikings had to rely on Tai Felton and Myles Price alongside Justin Jefferson in last Sunday’s win over the Packers, but they hope to get Jauan Jennings back from a calf injury for this week’s date with the 49ers.
