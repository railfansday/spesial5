Patrick Mahomes a full participant at walkthrough

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Kansas City Chiefs QB Patrick Mahomes (elbow, knee) fully participated in practice Monday, Nov. 23. The Chiefs only held a walkthrough Monday, so this is an estimation.

Why It Matters: Mahomes continued his terrific play in Week 11 and posted at least three touchdown passes for the third time in the last four games. He should not have any limitations in Week 12 despite the ailments and can be a No. 1 quarterback in fantasy leagues.
