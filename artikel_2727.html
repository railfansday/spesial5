2 49ers, CeeDee Lamb fined in Niners vs. Cowboys

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The San Francisco 49ers saw two players issued fines in the aftermath of the Dallas Cowboys game last weekend as defensive backs Siran Neal and Marques Sigle were both flagged for infractions.

Neal was fined $8,639 for an unnecessary roughness penalty, stemming from a punt return in the fourth quarter, where he was flagged for a facemask. The punt had initially pinned Dallas inside the 20, but Neal's penalty on the tackle gave the Cowboys field position at their 34-yard line.

Dallas would end up going 10 plays for 66 yards and a touchdown, cutting the lead to 38-32.

Sigle was fined $6,142 for unnecessary roughness for a hit on a defenseless player. He was not flagged on the play, which came on a third-down incompletion early in the second quarter. Dallas would punt on the next play.

Those weren't the only two fines from the game. CeeDee Lamb was fined $23,882 for an illegal use of the helmet while delivering a block on Deommodore Lenoir on George Pickens's 39-yard touchdown in the second quarter that put the Cowboys up 16-10.

There was initially a flag thrown on the play, but it was picked up.

On replay, it looked like Lamb delivered a hit with his shoulder to Lenoir's shoulder on the block. He expressed frustration earlier in the week with the penalty when revealing he got fined.

"I guess as a receiver, we can get hit, but can't deliver the punishment," Lamb said, via the Dallas News's Joseph Hoyt.

The 49ers ended up winning 46-40, and are now moving on to face the Minnesota Vikings this weekend, with kickoff set for 5:20 p.m. on Sunday night in Mexico City. Meanwhile, the Cowboys will face the Tennessee Titans at home, looking to bounce back from the loss.
