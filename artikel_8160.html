Three Big New York Ski Resorts Just Joined the Indy Pass Network

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Indy Pass is expanding its footprint for the 2026-’27 season, adding 15 new mountain partners across the globe, including several U.S. ski areas and three prominent New York resorts that are joining its Allied Program.

The biggest news for East Coast skiers is that Whiteface, Gore, and Belleayre are all coming aboard as Allied Resorts, giving Indy Pass holders a new way to save on skiing at three of New York’s most popular destinations.

Unlike full Indy Pass partners, which provide two days of skiing at no additional charge, Allied Resorts offer pass holders up to two lift tickets at a 25-percent discount off the daily window rate.

The additions are notable because all three mountains have increasingly embraced multi-resort pass partnerships in recent seasons. Whiteface joined the Mountain Collective ahead last winter, while all three New York resorts are also part of the Snow Pass and Snow Triple Play, the newest multi-resort pass to hit the market.

For Indy Pass holders, the Allied additions mean more opportunities to ski the Adirondacks and Catskills at a discount. Whiteface, home to the biggest vertical drop in the East at 3,430 feet, is known for its steep terrain and Olympic pedigree, plus the awesome town of Lake Placid. Gore offers an extensive trail network across four peaks, while Belleayre serves up a mix of terrain in the heart of the Catskills.

Beyond the New York additions, Indy Pass is adding 10 alpine ski areas as full partners for 2026-’27. Each new alpine partner will offer pass holders two days of skiing.

With the new resorts adding skier capacity, Indy Pass is back on sale for a limited time starting today (Nov. 13), with first dibs going to the people on the waitlist. The Indy Base pass is currently $419, and the Indy+ is $469. If you’re not already on the waitlist, you can join it here.
