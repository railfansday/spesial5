Porsche is transforming its current lineup of luxury SUVs and sports cars into a veritable stampede of electrified steeds. As the gas-powered 718 Cayman and 718 Boxster bid farewell, an all-electric 2028 718 EV Boxster is scheduled debut in early 2027. We expect a hardtop variant to follow. Both will ride on a newly developed two-passenger, EV-exclusive platform with an 800-volt architecture.

Details continue to trickle in, but an early drive of a 718 EV Boxster prototype revealed sharp steering and a rear-biased weight distribution that deliver the lively handling and slightly tail-happy personality of a traditional sports-car. We're speculating it to have at least 250 miles of driving range and a DC fast-charge at a rate of up to 270 kWh, but we'll update this space with official information as it becomes available.

The company isn’t saying how much power this Boxster’s single rear motor will make—all electric Boxsters will be rear-drive only—but they do claim the sprint to 60 mph will require less than 3.0 seconds. The steering, inherited from the 992.2 911 Carrera GTS, is eager and sharp, and rear-axle steering will also be available. We can attest to feeling tons of grip, but appreciate the way the Boxster’s rear-biased weight distribution lets the rear end step out when lifting off the throttle.

The model we drove wore Pirelli P Zero R Elect tires—and for the first time—mount on staggered-diameter wheels just like the latest 911. The tires measure 255/35ZR-20 up front and 305/30ZR-21 in back, which is more meat than even the last 718 Spyder RS. As the Boxster slithered through transitions and dove into apexes, we can’t say it felt heavy, although the squealing from its wide tires sounded at times more tortured than those we recall from the just-over-3000-pound gas-powered Boxster.
