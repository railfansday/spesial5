Take a Varo Advance for a one-time gap before payday if your paycheck already lands at Varo. With $800 in deposits there this month or last, it costs $8 per $100 borrowed, with up to 30 days to repay. Without them, the fee is $20 per $100, so don’t open an account just for it.

Varo Advance works only inside a Varo Bank Account, where each advance lands as soon as it’s approved. Qualifying customers can borrow on the same day Varo approves their account.

For a Varo customer paid $800 a month there, a $200 advance for up to 30 days costs $16, a fair price for a one-time need, though one every month adds up to $192 a year. Anyone paid into another bank should compare cash advance apps that work with that bank, some of which charge nothing for a transfer that takes a few business days.
