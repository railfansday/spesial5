Thursday saw six Newcastle United players with their national teams in action.

Five of the six seeing victories for their countries.

Lewis Hall and Dan Burn starting for England at Wembley and both playing the full match against Croatia.

Not the most entertaining match as England eventually won 2-1 with goals from Rogers and Kane (pen), Quansah with a terrible error to gift an equaliser for 1-1.

However, both Newcastle United players with excellent performances and Lewis Hall the best player on the pitch. The England fans voted Lewis Hall their man of the match and Dan Burn with the second highest rating of all 14 England players who got on their pitch.

Bazoumana Toure also saw his country win but the United winger sat out the 2-1 Ivory Coast victory on the bench.

Yoane Wissa though had an excellent game and took only 189 seconds to volley home the opening goal as DR Congo beat Sierra Leone 3-1.

Lukas Hornicek was the only one of the six Newcastle United players to see their team lose on Thursday. He sat on the bench as the Czech Republic lost 2-0 at home to Spain.

The sixth United player was Ousmane Diabate, the exciting midfield prospect played the full match as the 19-year-old helped Guinea to a 2-1 AFCON qualifying group win over Eritrea.

This is the November 2026 international schedule for Newcastle United players:

DR Congo 3 Sierra Leone 1 Yoane Wissa volleying home after 189 seconds for a 9th goal in his last 13 internationals, a 3-1 final scoreline in this AFCON qualifying group win.

Ivory Coast 2 Gambia 1 Bazoumana Toure an unused sub for the Ivory Coast in this AFCON qualifying group win.

Guinea 2 Eritrea 1 Ousmane Diabate played the full match in this AFCON qualifying group win.

England 2 Croatia 1 Lewis Hall and Dan Burn voted England’s best two players by the fans as they were excellent in this Nations League win, Rogers and Kane (pen) with the England goals.
