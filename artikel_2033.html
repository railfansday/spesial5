Charlie Austin drops 'downstairs toilet' claim about Millwall's away dressing room

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Charlie Austin compared the away dressing room at Millwall to a downstairs toilet due to how cramped it is.

The Den is widely regarded as one of the most intimidating stadiums in English football due to the atmosphere that the Millwall fans can generate, and it remains something of a throwback in the modern era.

Millwall supporters embrace that, and they pride themselves on how difficult they can make it for the opposition when they arrive in South Bermondsey.

However, it seems the club are also doing their part to make things tough for the away team, as Charlie Austin opened up on his experiences visiting The Den.

Speaking on the Chaz Kicks Off podcast, the ex-QPR striker discussed the different times he went to Millwall, as he also namechecked the players that could make it hostile, before sharing a light-hearted comparison of the state of the dressing room.

“I remember playing against Millwall for Swindon, when we got beat 3-2 on the final day. I was ****, and I was so nervous because I’d built it up in my head.

“I went back there with Burnley, and we won 2-0, which wasn’t too bad. With QPR, we drew 2-2 and they scored right at the end, and I scored in the game.

“But, Danny Shittu, their centre-half, was as wide as the tunnel at Millwall, and he’s in there shouting, just trying to intimidate, so that’s when it starts. Then you had David Forde, the goalkeeper, he used to walk out last, which is unusual for a keeper, and he had a big presence about him, where you’d think ‘I wouldn’t want to catch a right-hander off him’!

“They don’t do anything in the dressing room, other than, the only way I can explain it - Millwall’s away dressing room is the equivalent of your downstairs toilet! It is small, and it is tight. It’s what you expect.”

This is nice for Millwall fans to read, as every football fan wants their team to make their home ground a difficult one for opponents - and the Lions have managed that.

Clearly, things are different now, as you won’t get players like Shittu shouting in the tunnel to intimidate the away team, but it’s a good story showing how football used to be.

A look at the amount of pubs within a two-mile radius of every Championship ground this season.

It’s fairly common for the away dressing room to be worse than the home one, and, again, it makes sense, as you want the away team to feel as uncomfortable as possible without crossing the line.

So, this is a good insight from Austin, and it shows what clubs will do in order to try and gain even the smallest edge to help them on the day.

Whilst The Den understandably has a reputation, it’s fair to say it hasn’t been the fortress it once was so far this season.

That’s not to blame the fans, as they’ve made it raucous, and the 2-2 draw at home to West Ham produced a brilliant atmosphere.
