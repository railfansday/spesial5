This tenth round of Premier League fixtures.

Fulham v Newcastle United plus three others.

As a Newcastle United fan, have a look at these Premier League fixtures on Saturday and have a think about what you’d like to happen and what is most likely in terms of results.

My conclusions and predictions on these Saturday matches now coming up:

Tottenham have won their last three Premier League matches but haven’t looked convincing in my opinion.

Leeds have only won one of their eight EPL matches after beating  Forest on the opening day of the season, though they have drawn five of the last eight, as well as defeating Newcastle.

The five draws have been three 1-1s and two goalless draws. I fancy 0-0 or 1-1 today.

Arsenal are grinding the results out without looking particularly good. They won 1-0 at Liverpool with pretty much their only big clear chance of the game and at Forest they scored with their only shot on target. In between they won 2-0 against Everton but were fortunate to do so.

Pretty much everybody will predict a home win here and probably that will be the case. However, Hull are putting in gutsy performances and whilst they haven’t won since picking up six points in their first two games, they have drawn five of the last seven.

Arsenal should win but if Hull could find a goal from somewhere then it could make it interesting.

The only match that really matters today of course. United desperately in need of a positive result and performance ahead of the international break. A case of hoping for the best but fearing the worst I think today.

After seven EPL wins in a row, Man City have lost to Villa and Brighton who both played really well.

Forest are looking desperate and have only picked up one point in their last five matches and only have Sunderland keeping them off rock bottom.

Forest also scoring the least goals of any Premier League side, six in nine games.

Man City have the perfect opposition to get them back on the winning trail and they will do so.
