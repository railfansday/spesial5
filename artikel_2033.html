Newcastle United 2 Arsenal 2 – Saturday 21 November 2026 5.30pm

Instant fan/writer reaction from some of our regular contributors on The Mag.

‘I wouldn’t believe anyone if they claimed to have predicted that the second half would have been like that!

After such a poor first half what a pleasure it was to be inside the ground for the second half – we even got a bit of atmosphere which was non-existent pre-match and during the first half.

That second half performance has restored my faith for the run of games coming up.

Have to mention the comeback of Tino, he’s top class along with our other England full back.

Captain’s performance as well tonight from Big Dan.

In the end a very well deserved point against the champions and current league leaders (could be greedy and say it could / should have been all three…).’

‘It’s not an idiom to call that a game of 2 halves. They looked lost for the first 45 and were lucky to be just a goal down. We were by far the better team in the 2nd half and should have probably finished the game off on multiple occasions.’

‘Excellent second half after a first in which we looked low on confidence and bereft of ideas.

Fair play to Jaissle who had seen enough and his triple substitution paid dividends, United taking the game to Arsenal and unlucky not to have won.’

‘Wow, terrible first half, brilliant second.

Atmosphere was flat considering the number one rat’s return but thanks to our players for finally realising that effort equals reward.’

‘First half, woeful. Arsenal simply a class apart and it didn’t stick for any of our players.

At half time, I found myself wondering how long it would be until we could compete with a team like Arsenal again.

The two teams completely switched in quality and momentum. Unbelievable transformation. Have to give Jaissle some credit. Three changes at half time and he must have rallied them in the dressing room.

Ultimately, we’d only earn a point despite being worthy of more with that second half performance.

In a tough season, we’ve earned 5 points against our former star names. That’s a decent return. Please, please, please let us see that second half performance again soon.’

Every credit to him for making such decisive substitutions at the break.

We got a bit of luck with our equaliser via an Odegaard mistake (just like Arsenal did with their opening goal, mistake by Hall). But then the lads had the bit between their teeth and I haven’t seen Arsenal rattled before this season like they were here in the second half (I didn’t see the game when they lost to Brighton).

Arsenal had won nine of ten Premier League matches and that was just brilliant from Jaissle and these players who clearly are very much still playing for him.”

‘Some much much-needed signs of life as the champions are held on Bruno’s return to Tyneside.

Folklore denoting he was bound to get booed.

But on to the game and It was stirring to see us come back and turn the game on its head.

Sadly, it wasn’t quite enough for a win, yet we become the only side bar Brighton to get something off the Gunners so far this season.

Heading into this one I did wonder if a point would be considered a great outcome.

The players duly obliged amid some major talking points, not least the manager leaving £259m worth of signings on the bench, and a further £72m (Toure and Bamba) in the stands.

‘That’s a point more than I thought we’d get.

How come every game we play in the first half after a week’s training and pre match instructions still fresh in their ears, we are awful?

And then when the players play the game in front of them in the second half they suddenly improve despite / regardless of the half time talk from the Head Coach?
