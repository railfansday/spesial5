All Treasure Rooms in Queen’s Domain and how to find them

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Queen’s Domain has five Treasure Rooms, and locating them can be a difficult task. These secret areas are challenging to find, but they offer valuable rewards. In this guide, I will make things easy for you to find all five of them.

Before we can venture to find the treasure vaults, the first task is to get the Keystones. You will need them to open the vaults. Once you reach Obmuer Temple, you can find the first Keystone.

Make your way through the water until you reach a point where a broken structure slopes upwards. Go past it and use the edge to air dash to move forward. You’ll come across a small plot of land where you find one of Aganmi’s missing raiders.

From there, you’ll find an opening in the temple to your right. Drop, and then turn left to find the dark cave. Continue straight, and you’ll find a ledge that drops into a large room with pillars. Go to the other side of the room, enter the open vault, and you’ll find a skeleton sitting with a note next to it.

Read this note to get the Blood Keystone from the shop for 24 Ancient Coins.

There are two Blood Treasure Rooms, and you can find the first one in the basement of the Tower of Knowledge. Once you defeat the boss, clear the growths. This allows you to access a room filled with candle crawlers.

Clear out the enemies, and you’ll discover the door to the Blood Vault. Inside, you’ll get the Rusted Greatsword.

To find the next Blood Vault, head to Verdant Hollow. Go through the Petrified Garden and walk out of the gate that connects the Rotting Cavern and Verdant Hollow.

You can do so using the elevator from the Verdant Hollow wing altar. Walk into the cave from the ledge, and you’ll find the dark room.

Head to Neasied to find the first Velvet Treasure Room. You will find a cave on one side of the area that has the altar on the cliff face. Enter the cave and go all the way down to find a hole. This hole will lead you to another room. Follow the path, and you will eventually reach the elevator that takes you all the way down. You will find the treasure room entrance at this location.

To find the next one, head to the Rotting Cavern. Look for a shine-like structure and follow the path that leads you to it. Eventually, you will come across a sealed door that requires four dark triangular slit pieces. You can find these pieces around the area, and I believe their locations are random. Once you find them, they will lead you to the vault.

There is only one Jade Treasure Room, and you will find it in the Deimpo Mausoleum. Go to the wing altar and take the door on the right. This will lead you to a giant stairwell, and as you descend, you will find the door to the vault.
