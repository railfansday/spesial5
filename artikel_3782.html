Incremental mining and blacksmithing game Mello & Omega announced for PC

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Banchou Tactics and Neko Odyssey developer Secret Character has announced Mello & Omega, an incremental mining and blacksmithing game with shop automation. It will launch for PC via Steam in spring 2027.

Here is an overview of the game, via Secret Character:

Mello & Omega is an incremental game starring Mello, a young blacksmith, and Omega, her robot best friend. Take Omega into the mines, bring ore back to Mello’s forge, and sell weapons to adventurers.
