GTA 6 safe codes will let you unlock sealed loot containers in the main story, but also during the lucrative robbery activity, which sees you knock over gang hideouts, banks, and other cash and contraband rich institutions.

The safe codes you need in the main story or specific missions are given to you, but in order to get a safe code during a robbery (and you'll need to because they're randomised), you have two main options:

First off, you can sometimes find the code written on something inside the store or bank, like a note on a board in the back office perhaps, or maybe a guard you've incapacitated is carrying it—press L3 and R3 to activate Focus Mode and help you find it.

You can also get the code by interrogating employees. Aim your weapon at them and use the "Threaten" or "Safe Code?" prompt shown on the screen to force them to give you the information. In certain robberies, this will also force cashiers to empty the register, as in previous GTA games.

Once you have the safe code, it will appear in the lower right corner of the screen when you're interacting with the safe. You'll have to enter the four-digit code to unlock it.

Time is of the essence, so the faster you can get the code, the quicker you can open the safe and load the cash into your bag. You can tell if an establishment has a safe by selecting it on your map, which will also give you some idea of the security level (low to high) and the approximate value of the goods, so you can pick a lucrative target for your next stick up.
