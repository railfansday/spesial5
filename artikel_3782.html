While you're out stealing cars for Moses, the GTA 6 BF Bandito will appear on your list, taking you out to the very rural roads of South Leonard County and the Buckskin Swamp. This GTA 6 heist is slightly harder than the others you might have carried out so far though, as this car will be on the move.Like all of Moses' targets, the rough location of the BF Bandito is marked on the map, but as you make your way there, you'll pass by some whoopin' and hollerin' country dwellers who are already taking the buggy for a spin. Here's how to get your hands on the GTA 6 Bandito and get paid.

The map marker will take you to the rough location of the BF Bandito, where you should keep your eyes and ears open for some locals riding around the swamps on buggies - one of these is the Bandito.

To claim it for yourself, you'll need to stop the Bandito driver, and there are a few ways to do this. If you clock him fast enough, particularly when crossing water, you can simply get in front of him, and he'll stop. Otherwise, you can chase down the Bandito, shooting the driver and his pals. Weirdly, though, when I did this challenge, the driver eventually just stopped and bolted, leaving the buggy free for me to take, though I'm not sure exactly what triggered him to do this. Essentially, just stay close, don't destroy the car by shooting it too much, and it'll be yours in no time.
