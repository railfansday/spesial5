Katseye Postpones Detroit Show As Megan Recovers From Illness

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Katseye has postponed tonight’s show in Detroit. In a statement shared hours before the group was set to take the stage at Little Caesars Arena, Katseye said that member Megan Skiendiel would need time to recover from an unspecified illness.

“We’re so sorry to share that tonight’s show at Little Caesars Arena has been postponed due to illness,” read the group’s post on Instagram Stories. “Unfortunately, Megan is unwell and unable to perform tonight, and we want to make sure she has the time she needs to rest and recover.”

Katseye addressed fans, many who had traveled out of state for the event, in their update. “We know so many of you have traveled, made plans, and been looking forward to tonight. We are incredibly disappointed that we won’t get to see you, and we’re truly sorry for the inconvenience,” said the group. “Thank you for your patience, understanding, and for always showing us so much love. Earlier this year, Katseye dropped out of Olivia Rodrigo’s highly anticipated Daisy Chain Fields Festival after Skiendiel suffered a sprained ankle.

The North American leg of Katseye’s Wildworld Tour kicked off Oct. 13 and runs through Nov. 28. Two of the six members are currently on hiatus. In August, six months after Katseye’s Manon Bannerman announced she was taking a break for mental health reasons, Sophia Laforteza revealed she would be temporarily stepping away to focus on her mental health. While Laforteza is set to rejoin the group in January, no official date has been announced for Bannerman’s return. Neither Lafortza nor Bannerman are taking part in the North American dates.
