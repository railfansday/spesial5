Frankie Muniz Posts Health Update After NASCAR Crash: “I’m Grateful To Be Walking Right Now”

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Frankie Muniz says he is “grateful to be walking right now” after what he described as “a pretty bad wreck” in a NASCAR warmup yesterday that sent him to the hospital. Muniz was behind the wheel for Team Air Force in its No. 30 Ford, prepping for the Proud to Honor Veterans Day Classic exhibition race.

“I’m doing OK. I’m extremely sore, but fortunately no major injuries,” said the former Malcolm in the Middle actor in a video post to social media. “I’m not 100% sure how that’s the case, but that’s just a testiment to how well-built the Ford Mustang Dark Horse are.”
