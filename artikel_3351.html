Microsoft To Do is retiring, including Mac and iOS apps

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Microsoft is saying goodbye to its task management service To Do, including the Mac and iOS apps. Here’s what users need to know.

The last day to use the To Do apps is June 30, 2027. That date also applies to Windows, Android, and the web. The underlying service and sync support will retire later, according to Microsoft’s support document.

“Microsoft To Do is retiring,” Microsoft explains. “For work or school accounts, Microsoft Planner will be the destination for managing personal work tasks alongside assigned work. The updated My tasks experience begins rolling out in December 2026.”

On the Mac, you can use Planner on the web or in Teams. Microsoft explicitly says it has no plans for a native Mac app.

You can still see your existing To Do tasks in Planner through June 30, but your tasks and lists won’t carry over. Microsoft plans to offer an export option before the apps retire.

Planner doesn’t support personal Microsoft accounts however. If that’s how you use To Do, you’ll need to pick another task manager, even if you pay for Microsoft 365 Personal or Family, according to the company.

The June shutdown will end a run of just over a decade for To Do on Apple platforms. Microsoft bought the company behind Wunderlist in 2015 and released the first iPhone preview of To Do in April 2017.

Looking for a proper replacement? Apple’s built-in Reminders app can keep lists in sync between Apple devices, including a grocery list you share with family through iCloud.

Microsoft has been trying to make Teams as the super app for some time now, as Planner is integrated into Teams. Teams is one of the prime examples of why super apps are terrible, with a bloated, web app-like experience.

If you liked To Do because it worked across platforms, Todoist has apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows, and Android.

Does this sunsetting affect you? Were you a Wunderlist user back in the day? Share your thoughts in the comments.

Update: Howard Crow, Director of Product, Agents and AI, Microsoft, shared this statement with 9to5Mac regarding the transition:

“We’re bringing task management experiences together across Microsoft 365 to make it easier for customers to manage individual tasks and team plans. As part of this transition, Microsoft To Do apps will retire on June 30, 2027, with work and school experiences coming together in Microsoft Planner. Existing integrations across Microsoft 365, including Outlook and Teams, will also be updated to work with Planner. We recognize this change will affect customers differently, particularly those using personal Microsoft accounts, and we’re committed to helping them prepare. Customers will be able to export their To Do data before retirement, and we’ll share additional guidance to support the transition.”
