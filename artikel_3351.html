Jason Momoa's New Netflix Action Movie Officially Breaks 7-Year Rating Streak

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Jason Momoa has a new action movie lined up on Netflix, and its official rating continues a streak that’s lasted on for nearly a decade.

2026 has been a big year for Momoa, with the actor starring in four movies. The Wrecking Crew, Supergirl, and Street Fighter have all been released, and Dune: Part Three arrives next month. Looking to 2027 and beyond, one of Momoa’s next movies sees him teaming up with Zombieland director Ruben Fleischer. This movie, titled Protecting Jared, keeps a specific streak going for the filmmaker.

According to Filmratings, Protecting Jared has been rated PG-13 by the Motion Picture Association for "strong language, crude sexual material, action/violence, drug references and partial nudity.” That marks yet another PG-13 movie for Fleischer, following Uncharted and Now You See Me: Now You Don’t. He hasn’t made an R-rated movie since 2019’s Zombieland: Double Tap, which was released 10 years after its predecessor. Fleischer’s other credits include 30 Minutes or Less, Gangster Squad, and Venom, and Protecting Jared is his first time helming a Netflix original movie.

Although the streamer hasn’t shared an official plot synopsis for Protecting Jared yet, Production Weekly reports that the upcoming comedy movie stars Momoa as William Ikaika, an underachieving former military cadet who takes a security job to protect tech billionaire Jared Bachman, played by Andy Samberg. After armed commandos nearly kidnap Jared, he and William are forced to flee into the Hawaiian wilderness to evade their pursuers. Aimee Carrero and Jai Courtney also star in Protecting Jared, but their roles haven’t been disclosed.

It was revealed in June 2025 that Netflix scooped up the pitch for Protecting Jared, which was designed as a comedy vehicle for Momoa and Samberg. Along with performing onscreen, Momoa and Samberg are executive producing Protecting Jared movie through their respective On The Roam and Party Over Here production banners. Flesicher, Carrero, and Courtney’s involvement was unveiled this past March, shortly after principal photography began. Filming concluded in May.

While Momoa is best known as an action star from movies like Aquaman, Dune, and Fast X, he’s also repeatedly showed off his comedy chops. Last year, he played Garrett "The Garbage Man" Garrison in A Minecraft Movie, and he’ll reprise that role in 2027’s A Minecraft Movie Squared. Protecting Jared sounds like it will hit a similar action and comedy balance as the aforementioned The Wrecking Crew, which co-starred Dave Bautista. Momoa has also hosted Saturday Night Live twice, and he made a cameo appearance as Hagrid in the “Finn Wolfhard/ASAP Rocky” episode earlier this year.

Influenced by The Shield and The Wire, this compact, morally tangled, grimy Neo-western cop drama places Jason Momoa in a fiercely conflicted role.

This is Momoa’s first Netflix original movie since 2022’s Slumberland. While Samberg was most recently seen being interviewed in the documentary Lorne, his last onscreen performance was in the 2025 black comedy movie The Roses. The SNL veteran also recently lent his voice to Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse, Arco, Zootopia 2, and Digman!.

Protecting Jared doesn’t have an official release date yet. Momoa can be seen playing Hayt, the ghola of the deceased Duncan Idaho, when Dune: Part Three is released in theaters on December 18. Samberg will next be heard reprising Ben Reilly in Spider-Man: Beyond the Spider-Verse, which comes out on June 18, 2027.
