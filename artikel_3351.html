John Summit Is Extremely Mad Online About the Grammys

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Every year, countless artists have their hopes and dreams dashed when the Recording Academy reveals its nominees for the Grammy Awards. For the most part, the unfortunately snubbed choose to lick their wounds in private. Maybe they toss out an oblique or pithy quip on social media. Or maybe they make a video like Muna’s Katie Gavin, who copped to feeling disappointed, discussed the challenges of trying to shoehorn the band’s music into the Grammys’ sometimes overly-rigid genre categories, and still celebrated the artists who did get nominated.

But few show the chutzpah of DJ-producer John Summit, who took to X to proclaim, “Never submitting to the Grammys again unless shit changes, it’s all a fucking joke.”

never submitting to the grammys again unless shit changes, it’s all a fucking joke. i’m not just talking about myself either there’s zero respect for real electronic dance music & dj’s. the whole dance category is just music industry fueled pop stars

Summit — who dropped his second album, Ctrl Escape earlier this year and was recently seen performing on the floor of the New York Stock Exchange (cool) — was specifically frustrated with how the dance and electronic music categories shook out. “The whole dance category is just music industry fueled pop stars,” he lamented, insisting that the choices showed “zero respect for real electronic music & dj’s.” (And, he swore, “i’m not just talking about myself.”)

It is true that that the dance and EDM categories didn’t really feature any top-line DJs/producers besides Fred Again and Skrillex. And the categories were otherwise dominated by artists like Madonna, Robyn, Tinashe, Slayyyter, and Zara Larsson, who do present, at least on first glance, as pop musicians. But Summit went so far as to claim that these acts are “just not dance music, they’re pop,” and that “no DJ is playing that music in the clubs.”

not saying the artists nominated aren’t good, they’re just not dance music, they’re pop.no dj is playing that music in the clubs.

Essays — perhaps even dissertations — could be written about how a person, let alone a professional DJ and producer, comes to think that, somehow, Madonna and Robyn do not make dance music. And that’s before you start to wonder if there’s maybe something telling about Summit’s focus on “pop stars,” “pop,” and the “lil teeny bopper pop stan accounts” flooding his replies, when noted psych rock band Tame Impala also got nominated in several dance and electronic music categories.  Editor’s picks The 250 Greatest Albums of the 21st Century So Far The 100 Best TV Episodes of All Time The 500 Greatest Albums of All Time 100 Best Movies of the 21st Century

On top of all that, Summit’s tweets also manage to erase all the work put into this music by producers just like him. Nightfeelings, who worked on Tinashe’s Grammy-nominated “Melatonin,” astutely pointed this out to Summit, writing, “I actually come from dance music, my first project released on the same labels as aphex twin … I’ve performed at Berghain .. Andy weatherall played my tune Cheaters in every set for a while, I made a song with Romanthony ..  just gonna leave it there.”

He then added, “Not to mention Tinashe is literally a dj.”

In vintage “im not mad. please dont put in the newspaper that i got mad” fashion, Summit ended his little polemic by trying to laugh off the whole thing. “Oh no i’m getting attacked hard on here. time to log off and continue my sold out arena tour for my real fans :)”  Trending Stories Raye, Rosalía, and Ella Langley Lead 2027 Grammy Nominees Jennifer Lopez Teases New Music on ‘Fallon’: ‘This Does Feel Like a Happy Era’ Watch Buffalo Springfield Come Together for the First Time in Many Years Robert Pattinson Is the Next James Bond

Not being able to help himself, he later hopped back on to pen, “i realized i didn’t do a good job of making my point earlier. my intent was not to drag other artists down and i seriously congratulate everyone who was nominated they fully deserve the recognition. i just think there should be an extra category for more dance music djs & producers much love everyone.” Summit then promised, signing off from twitter now for a while.”

Sure, man, whatever. Or, as Charli XCX put it, “not this guy again!”
