ALL Accor reward points have a fixed value when you redeem them toward eligible hotel stays: 1,000 points get you 20 euros off. That gives each reward point a hotel stay value of 2 euro cents and is why TPG's November 2026 valuations peg Accor points at 2.25 cents.

ALL Accor reward points expire after 365 consecutive days without eligible point-earning activity. Any qualifying activity that credits at least one reward point extends the validity of your entire balance for another 365 days. An eligible donation of reward points can also extend the validity of your balance.

ALL Accor doesn't price hotel awards as traditional free nights. Instead, you can generally redeem 1,000 reward points for 20 euros off an eligible stay. When redeeming online, you can use 1,000 points for 20 euros off or 2,000 points for 40 euros off; after that, redemption amounts generally increase in increments of 2,000 points.

You can earn ALL Accor Silver, Gold or Platinum status through either status nights or status points within a calendar year. Silver requires 10 nights or 2,000 status points, Gold requires 30 nights or 7,000 status points, and Platinum requires 60 nights or 14,000 status points. Diamond status requires 26,000 status points and can't be earned through nights alone, while Limitless status is available by invitation only.

An eligible stay paid for in part or in full with reward points can still earn status nights. However, you'll only earn reward points and status points on the eligible portion of the bill that you pay without reward points.

Accor may not have the same name recognition or footprint in the U.S. as some other major hotel groups, but its portfolio may be difficult to ignore if you travel internationally. Its brands range from luxury names such as Raffles and Fairmont to budget-friendly options such as Ibis.

The ALL Accor loyalty program works differently from many hotel programs familiar to U.S. travelers. Reward points have a fixed value in euros toward most eligible hotel stays, elite status can be earned through nights or eligible spending, and paid memberships can help you save on stays and accelerate your progress toward status.

Here's what you need to know about earning and redeeming ALL Accor points, qualifying for elite status and deciding whether the program is a good fit for you.

ALL Accor is the hotel loyalty program for Accor Group. The program covers over 5,800 hotels across more than 45 brands in 110-plus countries worldwide, ranging from Fairmont and Pullman to Mercure and Ibis.

Accor has an especially strong footprint in Europe, the Middle East, Africa and the Asia-Pacific region. Its comparatively limited North American footprint makes ALL Accor less appealing to many U.S.-based travelers.

You can become a member of ALL Accor by visiting the program's website and clicking the sign-up button in the upper-right corner. From there, it's a quick and easy process to sign up for the free-to-join ALL Accor program.

You'll see two different points balances in your ALL Accor account: reward points and status points. Reward points are what you can redeem for stays and other rewards, while status points are one of the ways you can earn ALL Accor status.

When you book an eligible stay — which generally excludes stays booked through online travel agencies — you'll earn reward points based on the brand, your status tier and your eligible pretax spending, as well as status points based on the brand and your eligible pretax spending.

The ALL Accor hotel loyalty program has an entry tier, four earnable elite status tiers and an invitation-only Limitless tier. Members can also benefit from select paid memberships and occasional status match or challenge opportunities.

Status nights and status points reset at the start of each calendar year. Once you reach a new tier, you'll keep it for the rest of that calendar year and the following calendar year. To retain it beyond then, you must requalify during the next calendar year.

I've considered buying the ALL Accor+ Ibis membership, as its 15% discount could cover the 99-euro annual fee once you spend about 660 euros on eligible stays booked at the subscription rate.

After linking your ALL Accor account with an eligible airline loyalty account, you may be able to earn reward points on select flights in addition to the rewards you earn with the airline program. For example, you can earn 1 reward point per $2 spent on eligible Qatar Airways flights and 1 reward point per 2 euros spent on eligible Air France-KLM flights.

Elite members with Gold status or higher in ALL Accor or Qantas Frequent Flyer can earn 1 reward point per 4 Australian dollars (about $3) spent on eligible Qantas flights, while ALL Accor members with Silver status or higher and Azul Fidelidade members with Diamante status can earn 1 reward point for every 7.50 euros spent on eligible Azul flights.

You can earn 1 reward point and 1 status point per 2 euros spent when you host a group of at least eight attendees (or at least eight rooms) at a participating hotel. It doesn't matter whether your event is a professional gathering or personal celebration.

You have plenty of options for redeeming Accor points. As with earning, we'll focus on redemptions most relevant to U.S. residents.

ALL Accor lets you redeem 1,000 reward points for 20 euros off an eligible stay at a participating hotel. You can use points toward part or, in some cases, all of your stay, although some prepaid rates require a portion to be paid by card.

To redeem points for a hotel stay online, log in to your ALL Accor account and search for a stay as normal. When you choose a rate at a specific hotel, look for one with a purple "Pay with your points" designation if you want to redeem points for part or all of your stay.

Near the end of the booking process, you can opt to redeem points toward your stay. For example, I could redeem 1,000 points for 20 euros off my stay and pay the remaining balance with a credit card.

These hotel stay redemptions provide a fixed value of 2 euro cents per point, so you generally won't get outsize value from your reward points.

You can redeem — and, in some cases, bid — Accor points for select entertainment, dining and sports experiences through the ALL Limitless Experiences page. There are some great deals to be found, too, such as this Christmas Eve dinner for just 600 points.

You can also redeem Accor points for select activities through ALL Accor Activities & Events.

It's worth checking both of these programs periodically to see whether any experiences fit your upcoming schedule and tastes.

ALL Accor is most compelling for travelers who frequently visit Europe, the Middle East, Africa or the Asia-Pacific region, where Accor has a much larger footprint than in North America. Its hotel redemptions are also easy to understand: 1,000 reward points generally get you 20 euros off an eligible stay.

However, that simplicity means you generally won't find the outsize hotel redemptions that are possible with programs that use award charts or dynamic pricing. ALL Accor also lacks a U.S.-issued cobranded credit card, and its U.S.-accessible transfer partners generally transfer at less than a 1:1 ratio.

Elite status can still be useful, especially at the Platinum and Diamond levels, which offer perks such as lounge access, suite night upgrades and complimentary breakfast in certain regions or on certain days. Finally, ALL Accor+ subscriptions may be useful for some travelers.

ALL Accor probably won't be the best primary hotel program for most U.S.-based travelers given its relatively limited North American footprint, lack of a U.S.-issued cobranded card and less-than-1:1 transfer ratios. But it may be useful if you frequently stay at Accor brands in Europe, the Middle East, Africa or the Asia-Pacific region.

Reward points are easy to use at a fixed value toward eligible hotel stays, while higher elite tiers offer potentially valuable benefits such as lounge access, suite night upgrades and complimentary breakfast. Frequent Accor guests should consider whether an ALL Accor+ subscription could pay for itself through discounts, status nights and other benefits.

Even if you don't plan to pursue status, it's worth joining ALL Accor before your next eligible stay to access member rates and earn reward points.
