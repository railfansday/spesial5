Two key Derby County players return with behind-closed-doors Everton friendly score confirmed

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Derby County have been without Patrick Agyemang all season after he ruptured his achilles in April. Matt Clarke meanwhile, suffered a calf injury in DCFC’s Carabao Cup loss to Lincoln City at the start of the campaign and has been out since.

On Thursday, the key duo took big steps in their respective recoveries. The Rams revealed on X that both Agyemang and Clarke were involved in a behind-closed-doors friendly against Everton at the Hill Dickinson Stadium as they stepped up their comebacks.

The game ended 3-1 to Everton. Lars-Jorgen Salvesen put the Championship side ahead, but the Toffees fought back with goals from Charly Alcaraz, Tyler Dibling and teenager Jonathan Nsangou.

John Eustace has been dealing with a string of injury problems in the early stages of the campaign. His defence has been heavily impacted, but Clarke is arguably the biggest loss of the lot.

The 30-year-old was named Derby County’s Player of the Season last term, and also won the Players’ Player of the Season award. Clarke provides an assured presence in possession, progressing the ball well from left centre-back, and pairs that with a love for defending, which he does efficiently as well.

As for Agyemang, his absence has been felt in a big way amid DCFC’s attacking struggles. The American acclimatised to Championship football well in his debut campaign, notching 10 goals and four assists in 37 league games.

The Rams have missed his physicality, speed and dual threat of goals and assists. Both he and Clarke will be welcomed back into the first team with open arms when they are fully fit.

Agyemang and Clarke’s involvement in the Everton friendly marks a big step in their comebacks. Although not competitive, their return to match action shows they’re building up their ability to perform intense actions rather than just improving their fitness and getting involved in training drills.

Derby County will be eager for their Championship returns, but caution must be exercised. The duo will benefit from more match involvement before playing in the league, so perhaps this friendly outing is followed by U21s appearances to build them up gradually.

Rushing them back increases the risk of re-injury or new setbacks, so a cautious approach is best.

DCFC sit 23rd in the Championship table and five points from safety, despite the 3-1 win over Bolton Wanderers before the international break.
