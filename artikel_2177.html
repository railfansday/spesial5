Player who left Sheffield Wednesday in the summer in line for new EFL contract

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Sheffield Wednesday cut ties with the centre-back at the end of last season following their relegation from down to League One. SWFC decided not to hand him a fresh contract and he subsequently became a free agent in late June.

Iorfa, 31, weighed up his options over the summer as he searched for the right opportunity. He then eventually penned short-term terms with Rotherham United in September running until January after opting to stick around in South Yorkshire.

In this latest update regarding his long-term future with the Millers, McClaren has declared their intention to keep hold of him and has said, as per a report by Rotherham Advertiser: “He’s performing well for us and he’s a good leader. The lads like him, I like him. He’s got energy and personality. We’re conversing with him every day. We’d like to extend it. Nearer the time, I’m sure we’ll address it.”

Rotherham United went down to League Two earlier this year and have since struggled to adapt to life in the fourth tier. They are only above the drop zone on goal difference ahead of Colchester United.

Iorfa was a good servant for Sheffield Wednesday during his time with the Owls. He spent seven years at SWFC and made 222 appearances in all competitions, chipping in with seven goals from the back.

Despite Rotherham United’s struggles on the pitch over recent times, he has enjoyed plenty of game time with the Millers. He has made nine outings since his switch, eight of which have come in the league.

Iorfa was snapped up by Alex Bruce’s side to boost their defensive department. He gives them more competition and depth in his position.

The ex-England youth international spent the last three years in the Championship before his transfer to the New York Stadium. Therefore, the ex-Wolves man was seen as a bit of a coup for Rotherham United at the time.

Handing an extension to Iorfa is a no-brainer for Rotherham United. He is a vastly experienced operator in the EFL and will be a useful character to have in and around the dressing room.

The Millers need to rise away from the bottom two soon though. A club of their size should not be battling to avoid slipping into non-league.

Bruce was appointed as their head coach in the summer but he has won only two league games. He was previously Karl Robinson’s number two at Salford City and has also managed Macclesfield in the past.

Rotherham United won 2-0 away at Bromley in the FA Cup first round last time out. Next up is a trip to Blundell Park to take on Grimsby Town after the international break.
