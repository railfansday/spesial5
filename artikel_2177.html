Harry Kane sends message to England fans and media amid hype over Middlesbrough star Will Lankshear

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Middlesbrough have seen Will Lankshear emerge as a new star since his summer arrival. The 20-year-old’s record of 15 goals in 16 games has brought great excitement, with England boss Thomas Tuchel monitoring his performances and pundits touting him as a future replacement for Harry Kane.

However, the man Lankshear is drawing comparisons to has urged patience over his development. As quoted by Sky Sports, Ballon d’Or winner and England star Kane said on the Boro ace: “We talk about a striker having that goalscoring instinct and I think he’s one of those players for sure.

“He’s still in his early development. What he’s doing right now is perfect for him, playing loads of games, scoring goals, getting confidence. Whether he comes into the senior side it’s down to Thomas and if he did, I’m sure he would learn loads here surrounded by the attackers we have.

“It’s not a time to put too much pressure on him. I know there’s a lot of talk about who will replace me, but just let him do what he’s doing. It’s great for his development, he’s doing extremely well.”

Lankshear has long been viewed as a striker to keep an eye on in the years to come. He spent time in the Arsenal academy and only spent a year at Sheffield United before Spurs paid to bring him back down to North London.

His promising displays on loan at Oxford United meant there was some frustration among Spurs fans over the decision to sell him. Lankshear has quickly come good on a sizeable £20m total deal at Middlesbrough, and naturally, 15 goals in 16 games has brought excitement among England fans and in the wider media.

Based on what we’ve seen so far, there’s no reason to believe that Lankshear won’t reach a high level in the game. However, aged 21 and with 80 senior appearances to his name, the Boro man is very much in the formative years of his career.

From an international perspective, while Kane is still playing and firing in the goals, there is no drastic urgency to rush the next star striker through the setup. Lankshear and the coaches around him at club and international level can afford to take their time, allowing him to stay focused on his game and keep growing on and off the pitch.

Both publicly and privately, Lankshear will have people around him telling him to stay focused on his game amid growing noise around his name. However, there is perhaps no one better to say this than Kane – the man he is being compared to and touted to replace for England.

The 33-year-old wasn’t an overnight success himself, spending time out on loan at Leyton Orient, Millwall, Norwich City and Leicester City before breaking into the Spurs first-team shortly before he turned 21.

It took time for Kane to reach his lofty heights and become the star we know today, so there is no need to rush Lankshear. He’s thriving with Middlesbrough and gaining both experience and confidence, which will be key if he is to live up to his exciting potential.
