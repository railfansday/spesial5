Michigan Fans Rip School After Stunning Trev Alberts Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Trev Alberts "has been informed by the search firm representing Michigan that he will not be the Wolverines' next athletic director," ESPN reported Tuesday, citing what his attorney Tom Mars told Pete Thamel. Alberts got calls from the firm Monday night and Tuesday morning, Mars said, and has not been given a reason. Sources told ESPN last week that Alberts had reached an agreement on Nov. 17 to take the job.

Mars told ESPN that Alberts signed a binding memorandum of understanding that requires Michigan to pay his full contract buyout. His side puts that number at $17.2 million. Michigan will now have to find a new athletic director, and fans have interesting words about the latest debacle within the university.
