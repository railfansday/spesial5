Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The San Antonio Spurs opened the year 0-2. Victor Wembanyama's first three games were a little underwhelming for his mammoth standards. Since that 0-2 start, they've won 10 in a row. Over his past nine games, Wembanyama has been unbelievably dominant, averaging 33.7 points (67.9% true shooting), 11.9 rebounds, 4.6 blocks, 3.0 assists and 1.3 steals.

Wembanyama isn't the only star spearheading a tidy stretch for his team. Luka Dončić and the Los Angeles Lakers have won seven in a row to sit atop the West. LaMelo Ball, Rudy Gobert and the 9-3 Minnesota Timberwolves are thriving despite Anthony Edwards' prolonged absence; they own the NBA's top net rating at plus-14.1. Jayson Tatum and the Boston Celtics lead the East at 10-2, headlined by their rout of the Philadelphia 76ers Tuesday night.
