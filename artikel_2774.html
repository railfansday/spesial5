Resurfaced clip reveals Michael Jordan link that changed Kobe Bryant’s entire career

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Kobe Bryant's career trajectory changed because of Michael Jordan.

In 1996, there were reportedly rumors that the Chicago Bulls superstar was going to retire and Bryant didn't want to pass up a chance to play against him.

Lower Merion's Kobe Bryant goes to the net at the Palestra in a Feb 27, 1996 playoff game against Coatsville. MediaNews Group via Getty Images

In a clip from a 2024 appearance on Byron Scott's "Fast Break" podcast, former NBA player John Salley recalled Bryant explaining why he skipped college and entered the 1996 NBA Draft directly out of high school.

"I said to him, 'Why'd you not go to college?' And he says, 'I heard MJ was going to retire,'" Salley said. "And that was his reason for not going to college."

Bryant was selected 13th overall in the 1996 NBA Draft by the Charlotte Hornets and was immediately traded to the Los Angeles Lakers.

Los Angeles Lakers guard Kobe Bryant(L) and Chicago Bulls guard Michael Jordan(R) talk during a free-throw attempt during the fourth quarter 17 December at the United Center in Chicago. AFP/Getty Images

Lakers #24 Kobe Bryant pumps his fist during the fourth quarter. Anthony J. Causi

Jordan ultimately continued playing for Chicago through 1998. After retiring for a second time, he returned with the Washington Wizards in 2001 and permanently ended his career in 2003.

Bryant and Jordan faced each other eight times during the regular season between 1996 and 2003. They never met in the playoffs or NBA Finals, but their matchups helped connect two of basketball's most celebrated careers.
