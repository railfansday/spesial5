To recap, here are ways we recommend watching NFL games in 2026.

YouTube TV gets you nearly every channel you need to watch the NFL, including Fox, NBC, ABC, CBS, ESPN and NFL Network. Plus, YouTube TV is the home of NFL Sunday Ticket. The Sunday Ticket package, while pricey on its own, gets you all the Sunday out-of-market games (more than 160 games per season).

Current pricing for new users of the 2026 Sunday Ticket is $27 per month for four months. Factor in the cost of YouTube TV's Sports and News plan, currently available at $49.99/month for your first 5 months, then $71.99/month thereafter.

That's $76.99 a month for the next four months of YouTube TV + NFL Sunday Ticket. You can also opt, with or without a YouTube TV subscription, to pay for the rest of the season of NFL Sunday Ticket upfront for just $108.

DirecTV's Choice tier (currently $87.98/month after fees for your first month, $107.98/month after fees for months two and three) gives you access to nearly every channel you need to watch NFL games year-round: NFL Network, ESPN, NBC, CBS, Fox, SEC Network and an abundance of regional sports networks. DirecTV Choice (and most other DirecTV plans) also comes with free ESPN Unlimited, which gets you access to every ESPN channel, an amazing value and a must for watching tons of college and pro sports all year round.

An ad-supported Peacock subscription ($12.99/month) lets you stream live sports and events airing on NBC, including Sunday Night Football and the rest of the NFL season. Plus, you'll get access to thousands of hours of shows and movies, including beloved sitcoms such as Parks and Recreationand The Office, every Bravo show and much more.

For $19.99/month, you can upgrade to an ad-free subscription, which includes live access to your local NBC affiliate (not just during designated sports and events) and the ability to download select titles to watch offline.

You can also get an ad-supported subscription for free with a Walmart+ membership, which is now included with a Sam's Club Plus membership. Sam's Club Plus costs $120 a year and includes members-only pricing on certain Sam's Club products, free delivery, a membership to Walmart+ (valued at $98 a year), and a subscription to either Paramount+ or Peacock ($90-130 a year). That's a lot of savings and perks rolled into one subscription.

Fubo's Sports package gives you access to ESPN, NFL Network, Fox, ABC, CBS, NBC and 20-plus more live channels. Starting at $54.99 for your first month, it offers most of the channels you'll need to watch the NFL season.
