Las Vegas Raiders’ Wild Card hopes take massive hit in AFC Playoff standings after Week 10 loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Week 10 was a massive hit to the Las Vegas Raiders' playoff hopes. Not only did the Raiders' 21-13 loss to the Seattle Seahawks drop Las Vegas' record to 4-6, but the Cleveland Browns managed to pull off a fourth-quarter comeback to take command of the Wild Card race for the conference's third and final postseason spot. With that in mind, let's take a look at the AFC Playoff picture.

[Editor's note: The standings below are before Sunday Night Football between the Pittsburgh Steelers and Cincinnati Bengals, and Monday Night Football between the Los Angeles Chargers and Baltimore Ravens games. Silver and Black Pride will update when those games go final.]

2. Kansas City Chiefs 7-2 (3-1 AFCW for tiebreaker over DEN, 6-1 AFC for tiebreaker over JAX)

4. Pittsburgh Steelers 6-2 (win over CIN for tiebreaker) – Sunday Night Football

6. Cincinnati Bengals 6-2 (loss to PIT) – Sunday Night Football

9. Baltimore Ravens 4-5 – Monday Night Football

14. Miami Dolphins 1-8 (1-5 AFC for tiebreaker over TEN)

16. Los Angeles Chargers 0-8 – Monday Night Football

The Raiders did get some help with the Patriots losing to the Detroit Lions in Germany, but that was wasted with the Silver and Black's loss. Additionally, Houston was up 17-0 early in the second quarter against Cleveland and had a four-point lead with 1:40 to play, only to lose. The Browns' win puts the Raiders two games back of the seventh seed in the conference. Diving deeper, the Raiders are a game-and-a-half behind the Patriots since New England owns the head-to-head tiebreaker, and Baltimore has to win on Monday to stay in front of Vegas.

Next week, the Raiders head to Denver, the Browns and Patriots have byes, and Baltimore heads to Carolina.
