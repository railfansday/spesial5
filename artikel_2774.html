Neo Avdalas injury update: How long will UNC basketball player be out?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

UNC basketball's Neo Avdalas underwent surgery to repair a fractured left wrist and is expected to miss approximately two months, the Tar Heels announced Nov. 17.

The 6-foot-9 sophomore wing from Greece sustained the injury late in UNC's 78-70 loss to Georgia on Nov. 14 at the Smith Center. A starter in the first four games of the season as a Virginia Tech transfer, Avdalas was the first player in the transfer portal to commit to first-year coach Michael Malone in April.

Avdalas is averaging 9 points per game and leads UNC with 15 assists. UNC is off this week until it welcomes Marshall to Chapel Hill on Friday, Nov. 20 for a 9 p.m. tip on Roy Williams Court.
