Bears lead Saints 16-10 at halftime

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Saints have put some pressure on the Bears, but Chicago still has a 16-7 lead over New Orleans at halftime.

Chicago took a 16-0 lead with a Cairo Santos 41-yard field goal, a 3-yard touchdown reception from D’Andre Swift, and a 15-yard scoring strike from Caleb Williams to Colston Loveland. Santos missed the extra point on Loveland’s touchdown wide right to keep the score at 16-0.

But the Saints then got on the board with a 1-yard run from Alvin Kamara. They could’ve scored three more points, but Charlie Smyth’s 47-yard attempt was extremely wide right and no good.

Smyth made up for it with a 56-yard field goal to end the half, sending the ball right down the middle, hitting the crossbar to send it through the uprights.

New Orleans QB Tyler Shough finished the first half 9-of-17 passing for 93 yards with a touchdown and an interception.

On the other side, Williams is 10-of-16 for 113 yards with two touchdowns and a pick.

Bears running back Roschon Johnson is doubtful to return with an ankle injury.

New Orleans will have a chance to take the lead to start the third quarter, as the Saints will receive the second-half kickoff.
