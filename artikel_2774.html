Chiefs-Falcons Week 10 Wednesday injury report: Xavier Worthy practices

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Each practice day this season, the Kansas City Chiefs release an official injury report ahead of the next game. In Week 10, the Chiefs play the Atlanta Falcons on Sunday afternoon.

Here is the Chiefs' first official injury report of the week, courtesy of the team's official website:

Rookie defensive tackle Peter Woods (neck) did not participate in practice and hasn't since exiting the Week 8 game against the Denver Broncos with a neck injury.

Starting right tackle Jaylon Moore (ankle) did not participate in practice after leaving the Week 9 game in the third quarter with an ankle injury.

Second-year defensive tackle Omarr Norman-Lott (knee) was a limited participant in practice. He has been limited in practice for going on three weeks and will need to be activated to the active roster by next Tuesday, November 17, in order to play this season.

Wide receiver Xavier Worthy (hamstring) was a full participant in practice after leaving Sunday's game with an apparent hamstring injury; head coach Andy Reid labeled him as "day-to-day" during his Monday press conference.

Quarterback Patrick Mahomes (elbow) was a full participant in practice and doesn't seem to be garnering much concern from the team about the injury from Week 9.

Check back in later for the Falcons' first injury report of the week.
