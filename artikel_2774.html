'I question his motives': Host weary of Stephen A. Smith political views

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

By landing an interview with President Donald Trump last week, Stephen A. Smith cemented himself as a legitimate player in political media.

Some may question how Smith got the chance to interview Trump, and many have criticized the contents of the sit-down. However, very few people in the world have ever interviewed a sitting president. And hardly any who came at that opportunity were from the sports world.

Yet in the aftermath of the interview, Smith has continued to sidestep legitimate criticism of his refusal to hold Trump's feet to the fire. More broadly, Smith has avoided directly discussing his political views while interviewing numerous right-wing guests and frequently criticizing the Democratic Party on his SiriusXM show.

"It seems to me that his ambitions at some point are going to collide with his comfort," Anderson said.

Anderson, who covers the media business on The Press Box podcast, said the Trump interview served little purpose beyond giving Smith's critics fuel to take him less seriously.

"All he can do is make [Smith] look bad to people whose respect [he] wants," Anderson said.

In the segment, Anderson and host Bomani Jones debated Smith's ultimate ambitions in political media. They agreed that he wants to become an interviewer with whom any candidate with national political ambitions must sit down to display their ambitions to the Black community.

Still, Anderson warned against Smith pursuing that lane without being clear on his own beliefs and policy aims. Smith has long insisted he is merely a vessel for politicians to express themselves and go on the record, but that may not last in a country that is increasingly desperate for meaningful change.

"I question his motives, and I also think that people might be able to deal with this better if he was a little more clear and honest about his political beliefs," Anderson said. "I don't think he can pull that off."
