Packers WR clears waivers, set up for practice squad return

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

On Saturday, the Green Bay Packers waived receiver Coleman Owen from the 53-man roster — in part to make room for the addition of receiver Skyy Moore, who was coming off the injured reserve. Owen, who also returned kicks and punts, directly replaced Moore when Moore went down with an ankle injury. Now, Owen has cleared waivers without being claimed, so he's free to sign with anyone's practice squad.

Notably, the Packers have an open spot on their practice squad, as the team just released defensive lineman David Blay Jr. from the 17-man unit. Things are lining up so that Green Bay can bring back Owen to the practice squad, based on the team's actions.

If you need a reminder, this is what the Packers' practice squad currently looks like, with an open spot available:

ED: Nyjalik Kelly, Arron Mosby and D.J. Johnson

The only players currently on the practice squad who have been elevated for game action this year are Dabney (Weeks 9, 10) and Mosby (Weeks 6-8). Mosby has now exhausted his call-ups this year, while Dabney still has one available. Owen was elevated once as a member of the practice squad before being signed to the 53-man roster, so if the Packers bring him back to the practice squad, he can be elevated two more times in 2026.

Former practice squadders Mekhi Becton Sr., Pierre Strong Jr. and Kristian Welch remain on Green Bay's 53-man active roster.

On Monday, head coach Matt LaFleur stated that the team will know more about Moore's ankle injury on Friday, when players return to the club after a three-day break. If Moore is out, it could open the door for Coleman to be elevated on game days as a returner.

The Packers also worked out defensive lineman Haggai Ndubuisi today, a player who would qualify for the international exemption. Currently, that role is filled by kicker Lenny Krieg. Ndubuisi previously has been with the Arizona Cardinals, Denver Broncos, San Antonio Brahmas (UFL), Washington Commanders, New England Patriots, Houston Texans and Tampa Bay Buccaneers.

The Nigerian trained in London for six months as a combo offensive lineman and defensive lineman in the NFL's international program. He began his NFL career in 2022 as an offensive lineman before switching to the defensive line in 2023. His time in Arizona did not overlap with defensive coordinator Jonathan Gannon's time in Arizona, but he was in New England when DeMarcus Covington, currently the Packers' outside linebackers coach/run game coordinator/assistant head coach – defense, was the defensive coordinator of the Patriots.

Since being let go by the Buccaneers in late July with an injury settlement, Ndubuisi has worked out with the Indianapolis Colts, Minnesota Vikings, Buffalo Bills, Las Vegas Raiders and now the Packers. In-season, Green Bay has only worked out one other international player: Australian edge defender Cruz Smith.
