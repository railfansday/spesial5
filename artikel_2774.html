Brock Purdy caps first drive with TD pass to George Kittle, 49ers lead 7-0

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Much was made about the matchup between 49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan and Vikings defensive coordinator Brian Flores in advance of Sunday night’s matchup between San Francisco and Minnesota.

Shanahan has officially landed the first punch.

Quarterback Brock Purdy capped San Francisco’s first possession with a 17-yard touchdown pass to George Kittle, giving the 49ers an early 7-0 lead.

It took the 49ers just seven plays to get down the field, with the club needing to convert just one third down. Purdy connected with Mike Evans on third-and-1 for a 14-yard gain to move the chains.

Purdy was 4-of-4 passing for 55 yards with a TD on the first drive.

Christian McCaffrey took two carries for 10 yards, while Deebo Samuel also had 7 yards on one carry plus an 18-yard catch.
