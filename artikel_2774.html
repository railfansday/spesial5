Chiefs QB Patrick Mahomes’ wife Brittany jokes she’s doing her part in wild Super Bowl pregnancy trend

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Yes, Patrick Mahomes' wife, Brittany Mahomes, has noticed the trend regarding her pregnancies and the Kansas City Chiefs' Super Bowl appearances, and she's just doing her part in the team's success.

She took to her Instagram Stories to respond to the trend that has gone viral after she announced her latest pregnancy. Mahomes is "glad someone finally figured" it out before joking that she's "just doing my part over here."

Patrick and Brittany Mahomes have had three children together (two daughters and a son). In all three years she was pregnant, the Chiefs made it to or won the Super Bowl (they lost two of those appearances). They hope the trend continues this year.

Time will tell if the Chiefs are able to keep the streak up. They are currently 6-2, which matches their win total from last year, and rebounded after back-to-back losses to the Seattle Seahawks and the Denver Broncos by beating the New York Jets.

They will next play the Atlanta Falcons before facing the Arizona Cardinals. The Chiefs then have a Thanksgiving date with the Buffalo Bills.

The Chiefs have made five Super Bowls with Mahomes under center. Three of them have happened during the same season that his wife was pregnant.

In his first decade in the NFL, Mahomes has won three Super Bowls and is a six-time Pro Bowler. He has also won two NFL MVP awards and was named MVP of all three Super Bowls he won with the Chiefs.

Patrick and Brittany Mahomes are high school sweethearts. He proposed to her in September 2020, and they got married in March 2022. Brittany Mahomes played soccer and is now a co-owner of the Kansas City Current.
