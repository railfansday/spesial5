Vote for Brevard Girls Athlete of the Week for Nov. 2-7

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

It's time to vote for the next 2026-27 Brevard Girls Athlete of the Week at 321preps.com and floridatoday.com

The following athletes were nominated for their performances during the week of Nov. 2-7. Voting will be open until noon on Thursday, Nov. 12.

The winner for Oct. 26-31 was Mika Trujillo of Rockledge swimming & diving.

Coaches and athletic directors are invited to nominate athletes each week. E-mail the athlete's accomplishments for the previous Monday through Saturday to sficarro@floridatoday.com by 10 a.m. each Sunday.

Armstrong represented a third of Melbourne Central Catholic's nine goals in an eight-goal win over Heritage on Nov. 5. In her first career varsity match, the freshman netted her first hat trick and tallied one assist.

Dobbin finished as the District 4-2A runner-up on Nov. 7 as she led the Minutemen to a fourth-place finish. The eighth grader crossed the finish line in 18:58.4 for a new PR.

Geraghty contributed to Holy Trinity's District 4-1A championship on Nov. 4. The freshman posted a time of 22:08.9 for fifth place as the Tigers won the meet by 13 points.

Michell recorded a double-double in the Class 3A state semifinals on Nov. 3 in a four-set defeat to Pine Crest. The junior tied for the team-lead with 14 kills and led the team with 21 digs.

Mirda captured two Region 2-2A championships on Nov. 5 as the Scorpions won the region title. The junior swam to gold in the 100-yard free with a time of 54.39 seconds. She led off the winning 200 free relay and placed second in the 200 medley relay and fourth in the 100 back.

Reed recorded a hat trick on Nov. 6 in a 3-1 win over Hagerty. It was the senior's first career hat trick after posting five matches with two goals.

Wolff qualified for state in two events at the Region 2-1A Championships on Nov. 4. The junior won the region title in the 200 free after recording a time of 1:55.13. She secured a state berth in the 100 free following a fifth-place time of 53.35.
