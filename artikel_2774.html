Thursday Night Football: Jaguars lead Ravens 17-3 at halftime

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Ravens were hanging . . . until they weren’t.

Baltimore was within one score with less than 2 minutes to play in the first half. But Parker Washington beat Malaki Starks, and after a bobble of Trevor Lawrence’s pass, secured the catch and raced 50 yards for a touchdown with 58 seconds left in the half.

The Jaguars lead the Ravens 17-3 at halftime.

The Ravens started undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano with Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley on injured reserve. They opened the game with Derrick Henry in the wildcat and called seven plays out of the formation.

The Ravens had 17 runs and 12 passes as they shortened the half. Henry had 12 carries for 51 yards and threw a pass that lost 3 yards.

Zay Flowers had two rushes for 16 yards and two catches for 2 yards.

Fagnano, who lined up at quarterback for only 12 plays, was 7-of-11 for 30 yards.

The Jaguars outgained the Ravens 223 to 101, with Lawrence going 9-of-15 for 197 yards and a touchdown.

Running back Bhayshul Tuten, who was questionable to play with ankle and foot injuries, scored a 1-yard touchdown on the team’s opening drive. He has 22 yards on eight carries.

Jakobi Meyers has four catches for 65 yards, with Washington adding two for 61 and rookie tight end Nate Boerkircher one for 52.

Linebacker Foyesade Oluokun leads the Jacksonville defense with six tackles and a sack.

The Ravens held the ball for 17:31 of the first 30 minutes.
