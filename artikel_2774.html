Josh Hokit challenges ‘washed-up’ Jon Jones to UFC fight — ‘Try and whoop me’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Former Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) Heavyweight title challenger Josh Hokit and retired two-division champion Jon Jones have spent the past several days trading increasingly personal insults on Twitter/X following UFC 334.

Jones kicked off the latest exchange by celebrating Hokit's unanimous decision loss to Ciryl Gane (watch highlights), mocking the previously undefeated contender for falling short in his first UFC title fight. Hokit responded by unloading on Jones' troubled past, and the argument quickly spiraled from there.

On Tuesday, Jones criticized Hokit for blaming an early eye poke for his loss and guaranteed that "The Incredible Hok" would never capture UFC Heavyweight gold.

That was enough for Hokit to finally issue a direct challenge.

"How about you try and whoop me?" Hokit wrote. "Old, washed-up b*tch. Remember, I ain't a Welterweight you are talking to. You'll really need that strap that you like to pull out on civilians if you f—k with me. LOL. P.S. This new version of Ciryl would destroy you, by the way. Tom would have too."

Hokit then compared himself to Jones' longtime rival, Daniel Cormier, while referencing Jones' failed drug tests.

"He's the same age that [Cormier] was when they fought," Hokit wrote. "I'm DC's size, just reverse the age gap. Jon needed PEDs too. But I already know DC is more of a man and has much bigger balls than Jon ever has. Shit, DC fought Anderson [Silva] on short notice, but Jon needed a full camp for Chael [Sonnen]. Bitch-made motherf—ker. LOL."

Jones did not appear particularly interested in accepting Hokit's challenge. Instead, "Bones" laughed it off before claiming he repeatedly dominated his former Jackson-Wink teammate during their sparring sessions.

"LOL, wouldn't you like that? The opportunity to lose again? You literally just lost," Jones responded. "Let's not pretend I didn't do to you in sparring what I did every single time. Go ahead and lie to the fans if you want. Greg saw it, and the whole team saw it."

"You literally couldn't beat Gane. You had the opportunity to be considered the baddest man on the planet, and you had 25 minutes to prove it. And let's not start talking street stuff in Albuquerque. It's really not that serious, and you're not about that life."

Jones submitted Gane in just over two minutes to capture the vacant Heavyweight title in 2023 (watch highlights). Hokit, meanwhile, battled the French champion for 25 minutes at UFC 334 but ultimately lost on all three judges' scorecards.

A fight between Jones and Hokit is highly unlikely. Jones insists he is retired, while Hokit is coming off the first defeat of his professional career.

Then again, if these two keep tweeting at this pace, they may eventually have no insults left and be forced to settle it inside the Octagon … probably not, though.
