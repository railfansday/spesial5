Ocarina of Time Remake Has Fans Freaking Out Over Possible Majora’s Mask Hints

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Covering the video games industry since 2017, with experience in news, articles, and reviews. Currently a writer at DualShockers and gaming magazines.

It hasn't even been a week since The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake launched, and fans are already wondering whether Majora's Mask, the beloved sequel's darker adventure, will receive the same treatment in the future – not that I'm one of them (okay, maybe I am!).

And apparently, people are already going to some pretty wild lengths to find clues hidden throughout the new remake. But hey, we love a good fan theory, especially when it involves getting excited about what's next for one of gaming's most iconic franchises. So, naturally, we decided to join in on the fun.

Basically, the most popular theory right now is that the latest version of Ocarina of Time already contains a few hints suggesting that a Majora's Mask remake is in development, potentially arriving much sooner than you might expect.

And the whole thing started in the very first area of the game: Kokiri Forest. Some players quickly noticed that Link's house has a slightly different shape in the remake. The most interesting part, though, is that some fans believe its new design might actually be a direct reference to Majora's Mask.

Thomas Mahler believes Nintendo missed an opportunity to improve the level design in the Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake.

According to the theory, the house looks remarkably similar to the Deku Butler's Son, who happens to be at the center of one of the sequel's most heartbreaking stories. In case you need a little refresher, here's what happens: After completing the game's first dungeon, you can take part in a mini-game offered by the Deku Butler near the Deku Palace. Once you finish, he reveals that he used to play the same game with his son, who is no longer around.

What makes this even sadder is that, at the very beginning of the game, Link actually encounters the Deku Butler's Son by accident. He's the small tree-like figure found in the Lost Woods, whom Link comes across while chasing Skull Kid to retrieve his stolen ocarina.

But that's not all! Players have also found some rather interesting dialogue lines involving the Skull Kids in the Lost Woods.

For starters, there's the area with two Skull Kids who ask Child Link to play music with them (this only works as Child Link, by the way, since they will attack you as an adult). Besides obviously sharing their appearance with one of Majora's Mask's most recognizable characters, there's another curious detail: the opening notes of one of the songs they play are remarkably similar to the beginning of the Song of Healing, arguably the most iconic piece of music in the Nintendo 64 sequel.

The remake's dev team included younger folks, several of whom never played the original Ocarina of Time, who provided a much-needed modern view of things.

Moving on, there's another area in the Lost Woods where a lone Skull Kid sits atop a pillar. He's part of the Happy Mask Shop side quest, and if you offer him the correct mask (the Skull Mask), he'll respond with the following line: "I'd look tougher wearing that mask."

Interestingly, the dialogue box also highlights the words "that mask" in red. And, well, we all know which mask can really make a Skull Kid look intimidating, don't we? Here, take a look at these moments for yourself (again):

With all these little details adding up, fans are already starting to believe that Nintendo isn't simply considering a Majora's Mask remake but may actually have one in the works. The theory also suggests that the project could reuse assets from the new Ocarina of Time, while including even more direct connections between the two games.

Of course, it's entirely possible that people are just really, really hoping for a remake of the sequel – and honestly, I can't blame them! I share that excitement, after all. Still, it's worth remembering that Nintendo hasn't even hinted at the possibility of a new Majora's Mask remake, let alone confirmed that one is in development. So, for now, all we can do is keep our eyes open, enjoy the theories, and hope that this particular dream eventually becomes a reality.
