Shoe Factory Gatehouses / Xie Jie Architects

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Text description provided by the architects. A new shoe factory complex in Wenzhou is about to open, but two gatehouses at its south entrance remain unfinished. Through the existing frames, one sees the West Yandang Mountains and a silver metal warehouse at their foot, diagonally across the entrance. The client wished to retain the frames to maintain continuity with the complex, while giving the entrance a distinct character of its own.
