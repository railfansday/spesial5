AC Milan to scout Feyenoord right back Givairo Read

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

AC Milan plans to watch Givairo Read during the international break after Ruben Amorim asked the club to sign a right back in January. According to TuttoMercatoWeb, Feyenoord values the 20-year-old at €25 million to €30 million.

Read is in the Netherlands Under-21 squad for a two-legged European Championship qualifying playoff against Denmark. The Dutch play the first leg in Vejle on November 13 and host the return in Almere four days later.

Read marked Rafael Leão when Feyenoord knocked Milan out of the Champions League in February 2025. Roma tried to bring him to Italy last summer before turning to Nahuel Molina. Read stayed in Rotterdam and opened the Eredivisie season with five assists in seven games.
