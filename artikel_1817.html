‘Starboy!’: Tottenham fans hail Luca Williams-Barnett after first senior goal and assist

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Tottenham fans have a new hometown hero as Luca Williams-Barnett scored his first goal for the club on his full Premier League debut.

Much is expected from the teenager, who was named in the starting line-up for Saturday’s visit of Ipswich.

The 18-year-old made his senior debut earlier this season, in a 5-1 win over Charlton in the Carabao Cup, before two short cameos in the Premier League, against Crystal Palace and then Leeds.

And Williams-Barnett had done enough to convince manager Roberto De Zerbi to throw him into the starting line-up, and was rewarded with the opening goal after only 15 minutes.

The youngster nipped in ahead of Abdoul Ouattara to latch onto a Pedro Porro cross and poke to ball inside the near post.

Williams-Barnett later turned provider to put the ball on a plate for Dominic Solanke to score, as Spurs ran out 2-0 winners.

One fan wrote: “Luca Williams-Barnett has really arrived. Start him every week.”

Another hailed the youngster as a “special talent”, while a third the forward was his “favourite young English talent.”

And rival fans had to congratulate Williams-Barnett and Tottenham.

Solanke said after the game: "Obviously I am so happy for him, every day in the training session he shows his quality. Honestly he's just an incredible football player. I hope he keeps doing what he's doing.

"Obviously he is such a young lad. Doing what he is doing in training and in the game is just unbelievable. I just wish him the best."
