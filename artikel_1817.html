Prediction: Manchester United vs Aston Villa

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Two camps who will turn their attention back to Premier League action this weekend, Manchester United will welcome Aston Villa to Old Trafford on Sunday afternoon.

With England’s elite stealing centre stage this weekend, there are a host of eye-catching Premier League betting promotions available.

With all eyes falling on Old Trafford, you can find all of the key Manchester United vs Aston Villa H2H stats here.

Verdict: Nicolas Jackson over 0.5 shots on target

Starting with the hosts, mounting what was an iconic Champions League comeback at Old Trafford on Tuesday night, both Michael Carrick and Manchester United claimed an essential European boost. Eventually dragging themselves to a remarkable 3-2 victory at home against Italian giants Roma, the Red Devils are now desperately searching for some crucial stability. However, despite drawing a line under their record-breaking nine-match winless rut, United are sensationally scrapping it out at the foot of the Premier League standings. Landing just a sole win from any of their first nine top-flight appearances and opening this weekend’s fixtures holding just a one-point buffer over slipping into the relegation zone, Carrick’s side also know the importance of finding some defensive grit. In fact, Manchester United are still waiting to put a maiden domestic clean sheet on the board since opening up the 2026/27 campaign.

While Brazilian international Matheus Cunha might have been shown a dramatic Champions League red card on Tuesday night, the ex-Wolves star is free to feature at Old Trafford. However, limping off in midweek with a reported hamstring injury, Manchester United will be without big-money summer recruit Carlos Baleba.

Carrick’s side have confirmed that former RB Leipzig striker Benjamin Sesko will miss the remainder of 2026 due to a long-term shin issue. Belgium international Youri Tielemans should be handed an immediate full recall in the middle of the park.

As for the visitors, completely blown away in Catalonia on Tuesday night as they slumped to a 3-0 drumming against Spanish icons Barcelona, Aston Villa will know the importance of immediately dusting themselves down. Watching their EFL Cup adventure come to a thumping end last week as they were hit with another 3-0 defeat against Bournemouth, Unai Emery’s men have failed to win their previous three appearances across all competitions. Despite finding a route into the top-half of the Premier League standings, Aston Villa will be concerned by their most recent trip to Old Trafford. Falling to a 3-1 loss when they last met Manchester United for a Premier League showdown back on March 15th, Villa are also seeking their own respective defensive grit. It should be noted, Emery’s side have mustered just a sole clean sheet from any of their previous six straight matchups outside of the Midlands.

Yet to make a maiden appearance this season after sustaining a long-term knee injury while away with Belgium at the 2026 World Cup, Aston Villa will once again be without Amadou Onana here. Likewise, one-time Manchester United defender Aaron Wan-Bissaka remains a doubt with a muscle injury.

Enjoying a sensational start to life at Villa Park and already notching five Premier League strikes, summer recruit and ex-Chelsea marksman Nicolas Jackson will lead the line in Manchester. Likewise, fellow summer arrival and Swiss starlet Johan Manzambi will join Jackson in the final third.

Producing a sensational Champions League comeback at Old Trafford on Tuesday night as they dragged themselves to a remarkable 3-2 victory, Manchester United will be desperate to use that gutsy performance as a platform to build on. However, still waiting to claim a maiden domestic clean sheet since opening up the 2026/27 campaign, Carrick’s side will meet an Aston Villa squad who are searching for their own essential boost on Sunday afternoon. Two camps who have shown their own defensive struggles so far this season, we could be set for an enjoyable Premier League battle this weekend.
