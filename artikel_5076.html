XRP Price Rejects $1 Fib Pocket; Stoch RSI Overbought

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Home » Blitz news » XRP Price Rejects $1 Fib Pocket; Stoch RSI Overbought

The game-tested altcoin restored $1, but then slipped right back under the fundamental Fibonacci pocket.

XRP ran into its first Fibonacci resistance pocket near $1.00 and worked into the daily 20 EMA at $1.01 before rejecting, according to technical analyst Chart Nerd. The token is now ranging between roughly $0.85 and $1.00, with the Stochastic RSI still overbought.

XRP’s price currently sits near $0.92 after failing to hold the $1.00 area. The analyst noted that both XRP and Bitcoin face nearby resistance (XRP in the $1 to $1.20 zone and Bitcoin between $63K to $65K, and until those levels are reclaimed, further weakness remains plausible.

The XRP price chart shows the OG altcoin testing the 0.236 Fibonacci level near $1.00 before turning lower. The daily 20 EMA, marked around $1.01 to $1.03, acted as immediate overhead resistance.

The same death cross that opened the previous unwind and prepared us for the most recent. This bounce isn't the wave i'm watching, it's when this move tops out and retraces back to the low we have formed. https://t.co/qoFi9ArJVa

A d*ath cross remains visible on the daily time-frame from earlier, keeping the broader structure under pressure.

The Stochastic RSI is still in overbought territory after the recent bounce, consistent with the rejection.

ChartNerdTA described the move as a relief rally within the prevailing downtrend rather than a confirmed reversal.

The analyst is watching the $0.85 to $1 range as the current operating zone. A clear of the $1 to $1.20 pocket would open the next targets, including the daily 50 EMA and the 0.382 Fibonacci level near $1.10.

On the downside, a loss of $0.85 would shift focus toward the lower $0.80 area.

Until $1 to $1.20 resistance is claimed back, ChartNerdTA recommends respecting the trend. The setup remains one of range-bound action with overbought momentum indicators capping the upside for now.
