Episode 7: Plants That Set a Mood

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Introducing joy, elegance, or whimsy into a design could be as simple as a trip to the nursery. The trick is to decide what kind of vibe you’d like your space to have, then seek out the plants that evoke those feelings. In this entertaining episode, designers Catharine Cooke and Susan Morrison share some choices that will spark your imagination and get you thinking about what kinds of moods you might want to bring into your own designs.
