Real Madrid: Endrick counting on Brazil and Ancelotti to reverse the trend

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Severely lacking playing time at Real Madrid, Endrick could once again take advantage of the international break to find rhythm with Brazil. The situation of the 20-year-old striker is particularly telling: in his last eight appearances with the Merengues, he has only accumulated 136 minutes, compared to 139 minutes in just three meetings with the Seleção during the previous gathering. A contrast which has not escaped José Mourinho, who hopes to see his player benefit from significant playing time under the orders of Carlo Ancelotti. “My opinion is clear: I want Endrick to play. If Ancelotti plays him during this international break, I will be delighted”declared the Portuguese technician.

Despite this lack of opportunities in Madrid, Mourinho continues to believe in the young Brazilian’s potential and does not plan to let him leave this winter, despite announced interest from several clubs, including AS Roma, AC Milan and Aston Villa. “He’s a player that I adore, a player that I love deeply. He has great potential and he is very important for us. Anyone interested in Endrick will have to fight me.”he warned. For his part, the striker hopes to take advantage of this gathering with Brazil to regain confidence and accumulate minutes, before trying to establish himself further in the Madrid rotation. Thus, this truce could represent a real breath of fresh air for the young center forward.
