Delivery driver goes viral throwing coffee at customer after delivering it to her

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

A delivery driver has been put on blast in a viral TikTok after being confronted by a customer as he was seemingly trying to steal a coffee that he had just delivered.

When delivery drivers drop off parcels, food, or anything else you’ve ordered online, they typically have to snap a photo to prove that it has been delivered. However, there have been cases where orders are stolen from people’s porches too.

In the case of Karmen Karma, the TikToker put a delivery driver called Darian on blast after he appeared to be trying to steal her coffee order on November 3. “Your coffee is right here, I don’t want your $4 coffee,” the driver said before throwing the cup at the TikToker.

“He delivered coffee to my front door, took a photo, and then went running back to his car with my coffee,” Karmen explained. “Tried to drive off fast but got stuck in a cul-de-sac, so on his way back down my street he decided to do this with my two small children present next to me. Also, tell Darian my coffee was $19.70.”

A number of commenters urged Karmen to provide proof to the delivery service as a way to get the driver punished in some way.

She did just that a few hours later, showing footage from her doorbell of Darian dropping the coffee off and snapping a photo of the delivery.

In that video, the driver can be seen speeding off too with the TikToker also in view, recording him doing so. Karmen added that she had tipped 33% of the cost of the order too.

Some viewers argued that there is “more to the story,” while others are completely on the TikToker’s side.
