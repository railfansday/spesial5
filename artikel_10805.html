Pokemon Go rewards your Duolingo lessons with a chance at Shiny Lucario

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Pokemon Go has teamed up with Duolingo for a crossover that lets players earn Pokemon encounters by completing language lessons, including a chance to catch Shiny Lucario.

Pokemon Go players are used to walking for their prizes, but its latest collaboration with language-learning app Duolingo will now reward players for their linguistic learning.

From November 17, Trainers can do lessons in Duolingo to earn a code that unlocks special Timed Research events to earn XP, Stardust, and encounters with themed Pokemon that have a Special Background.

Players who join Duo’s Pokemon Party in the Duolingo app can earn rewards by completing at least one lesson a day in any course. These shiny new rewards include up to four Pokemon suits for your Duolingo avatar, while completing lessons on seven different days unlocks a code for Pokemon Go’s event-themed Timed Research.

Once redeemed through the Pokemon Go Web Store, the research lets trainers choose a language and complete tasks written in it. Professor Willow will be on hand to provide hints if you get stuck during your language learning studies.

If you manage to successfully complete the tasks, you’ll be rewarded with XP, Stardust, and encounters with Pikachu, Meowth, Chatot, and Lucario. All four of these Pokemon come with a special background, and encounters also have a chance to be a Shiny.

Certain Pokemon obtained through the research will also feature a new “language tag” on their summary page, where you’ll be able to see your freshly caught companion’s name in different languages.
