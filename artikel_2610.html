Cameron Boozer injury update: Grizzlies share status of Rookie of the Year contender

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Memphis Grizzlies have an injury update on their star rookie.

On Monday, Nov. 23, the Grizzlies released a statement saying that Cameron Boozer suffered a sprained right ankle in the third quarter of their game against the Golden State Warriors. The Grizzlies went on to win 126-98 on Thursday, Nov. 19. The 19-year-old "underwent imaging that revealed a moderate-grade medial sprain," the team said.

Boozer is out at least four weeks, when he will be reevaluated. Memphis said he is expected to make a full recovery.

He already missed the Grizzlies' 104-95 loss to the Minnesota Timberwolves on Saturday, Nov. 21.

Last week, USA TODAY Sports ranked Boozer as the top rookie of the season so far, beating out top pick AJ Dybantsa of the Washington Wizards and rookie sensation Yaxel Lendeborg of the Warriors.

Boozer was selected out of Duke by the Grizzlies as the No. 3 overall pick in this spring's NBA draft. He was behind Dybantsa and the second pick, Darryn Peterson, who was taken by the Utah Jazz.

Memphis is fifth in the Western Conference with a record of 12-5.

Boozer is averaging 16.6 points with 9.2 rebounds and 5.3 assists per game in his rookie year.

He is the Grizzlies' second-leading scorer behind Ty Jerome, who is averaging 24.8 points per outing.

In a 136-120 win against the Wizards last week, Boozer had his best scoring night yet with 29 points. He added seven boards and seven assists and went 7-of-13 from behind the arc.

The former Blue Devils standout has notched four double-doubles so far this season. He had a triple-double with 18 points, 12 rebounds and 12 assists in a 124-103 win over the Portland Trail Blazers last month.
