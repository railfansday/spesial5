Thanksgiving Day Open Thread

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Happy Thanksgiving, everyone! Like most, we are enjoying the day with family but wanted to give you a space to chat all things sports.

The initial Iron Bowl injury report dropped, and it's encouraging for Alabama.

Alabama quarterback Keelon Russell was not listed on the first SEC availability report released Wednesday, Nov. 25. Linebackers Luke Metz and Caleb Woodson were both probable, as was linebacker Desmond Umeozulu, who has not played since the Florida State game. Defensive lineman Fatutoa Henry was listed as probable.

Russell is expected to be a "full go," per Alabama coach Kalen DeBoer, after sitting out the Crimson Tide's 47-0 win against Chattanooga. Russell played through an ankle injury suffered against LSU in Alabama's 21-6 road win against Vanderbilt.

Edwards was previously announced as out against Auburn. Woodson and Metz, offensive lineman Mal Waldrep Jr. defensive backs Ivan Taylor and Chuck McDonald III were all deemed expected to be available.

It's great to see Zulu back in action for the first time in many weeks. Auburn coach Alex Golesh listed QB Byrum Brown as questionable on their side, but there is no way that Brown sits out his last shot at Alabama.

Alabama got a commitment from the nation's top ranked JUCO linebacker prospect.

He's a thumper who will have a chance to compete for playing time, especially if Caleb Woodson decides to forgo his final season of eligibility.

Consider this your open thread for everything today. There are a couple of college football games, starting with the 102nd annual Tuskegee vs Alabama State Turkey Day Classic in Montgomery. The only way to catch it is free streaming on SWAC TV. Texas Tech and TCU play at 7pm on ESPN.

Of course, there is a full day of NFL football as well, and Alabama Basketball looks to bounce back against Kansas State this evening.

Enjoy the conversation, be kind to one another, and I hope everyone has a safe and Happy Thanksgiving.
