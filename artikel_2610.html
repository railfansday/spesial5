LOCAL ROUNDUP: Rivera-Torres, Tippner named Big South players of the week

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

CHARLOTTE — High Point University's Jason Rivera-Torres on the men's side and Meredith Tippner on the women's side have earned Big South Conference Player of the Week for this week's basketball accolades.

Trey Pearson was also selected men's Freshman of the Week.

Rivera-Torres averaged 17.5 points, 6.5 rebounds, 5.0 steals, 2.0 assists and 1.5 blocks in leading the Panthers to two wins last week.

He opened with a season-high 19 points plus five rebounds, three assists and three steals against Southern Utah. He then guided the Panthers to a 68-58 road win at Saint Louis with 16 points, eight boards, career-high seven steals and two blocks. He had just one turnover in 61 minutes.

Pearson matched his season-high with 11 points against Southern Utah as he was 5 of 8 from the field. He also grabbed five rebounds (three offensive), had five assists and two steals in 22 minutes off the bench.

Tippner had consecutive double-doubles, averaging 14.5 points, 14.0 rebounds and 3.0 assists in High Point's wins over East Carolina and South Carolina State. She contributed four steals and one block.

Against ECU, Tippner posted a team-high 19 points and 13 rebounds while making 5 of 5 from the free-throw line. She followed with 10 points and a game- and career-high 15 rebounds against South Carolina State, adding four assists, four steals and a block.

HIGH POINT — High Point University's Dakota Phillips has been named a Kay Yow Servant Leader for the 2026-27 women's basketball season.

During the 2026-27 season, she will wear a Kay Yow Servant Leader Patch on her jersey that reflects her servant's spirit and a commitment to selfless leadership both on and off the court — the unique characteristics that embodied coach Kay Yow.

Recipients of the Servant Leader Award are people of great character who put the needs of others before their own, who uplift others, and who do it with grace and humility. They are nominated by their coach and exemplify Yow's spirit, reflecting her resolve, grace, generosity and servant's heart.

HIGH POINT — High Point University has set its full schedule for the upcoming spring season. The Panthers will open their season with a three-game series at home against Delaware starting Friday, Feb. 19.

They will have home series against nonconference opponents LIU on Feb. 26-28, Wagner on March 5-7 and College of Charleston on April 23-25; Big South opponents Gardner-Webb on March 19-21, UNC Asheville on April 9-11, USC Upstate on April 16-18, Charleston Southern on May 14-16.

High Point will also have home games against UNCG on March 9, Wake Forest on March 16, Queens on March 23, Duke on April 6, UNC Wilmington on April 27 and NC A&T on May 11.

The Panthers will have road series against nonconference opponents Georgia Southern on March 12-14 and Tarleton State on April 3-May 2; conference opponents Presbyterian on March 25-27, Winthrop on April 2-4, Radford on May 7-9 and Longwood on May 20-22.

They will also have away games against Tennessee on Feb. 23, Charlotte on March 2, Duke on March 30, Wake Forest on April 20 and UNCG on May 4.

The Big South Conference tournament will be May 26-29 at HomeTrust Park in Asheville.

MATTEWS — Wesleyan Christian defeated Covenant Day 68-49 in nonconference boys basketball Monday at Covenant Day. The Trojans improved to 2-1 overall.

In the girls game, Wesleyan won 73-10 to improve to 1-2.

WINSTON-SALEM — Area athletes posted several top finishes in the Triad Invitational indoor track meet Monday at JDL Fast Track.

Posting top-three finishes were: High Point Central's Souleyman Issakh (second — boys 500m, 1:10.50); TW Andrews' Madison Hamn (first — girls 500m, 1:18.48); Wheatmore's Daylan Rada (third — boys 1600m, 4:55.12) and Noah Wyrick (second — boys shot put, 41-07.75).
