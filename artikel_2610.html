Eagles Injury Report: DeVonta Smith and Dontayvion Wicks return to practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Philadelphia Eagles issued their first official injury report of the week on Wednesday in advance of their Week 11 game against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

The Eagles listed one player under DID NOT PARTICIPATE: Zack Baun.

Baun is dealing with a back injury. Not a good development for the Eagles' defense if he can't suit up. Baun has been a rare bright spot for the Birds this season.

The Eagles listed seven players under LIMITED PARTICIPATION: DeVonta Smith, Dontayvion Wicks, Will Shipley, Nolan Smith, Michael Carter, Britain Covey, and Jonathan Jones.

Smitty and Wicks returned to practice after missing multiple games. Nick Sirianni indicated the Eagles have hope they can return to game action this week. Smith told reporters that he's "100% healthy." Getting their top two receivers back would be a nice development for Philadelphia's offense.

The Eagles opened Shipley's 21-day practice window to return from injured reserve earlier this week. Seems like he has a chance to play on Sunday as Saquon Barkley's backup. The Eagles also have Jaydon Blue at running back.

Nolan Smith was less than 100% due to illness. Guessing he'll be able to play.

Carter hasn't been able to play since getting banged up in the Eagles' Week 8 loss. Maybe he'll return this week.

Covey could be set to return as the Eagles' punt returner.

Jones could be back as the Eagles' top outside backup cornerback option.

The Eagles listed six players under FULL PARTICIPATION: Saquon Barkley, Jihaad Campbell, Jonathan Greenard, Andrew Mukuba, Tanner McKee, and Smael Mondon.

Good to see Barkley was able to practice in full early in the week coming off the bye. Big help for the offense if he's healthy.

Campbell will return to the field after missing the Eagles' last two games. He figures to start next to Jeremiah Trotter Jr. if Baun can't play. Otherwise, Campbell and Trot Jr. could split snaps.

Greenard getting healthy could really help the Eagles' pass rush in the second half of the season.

McKee will serve as Joe Milton's backup on Sunday with rookie Cole Payton set to be the emergency third quarterback.

The Steelers notably were without Nick Herbig (younger brother of former Eagles blocker Nate Herbig), who ranks tied for second on the team in TFLs and fourth in sacks. He got banged up in Week 10.

The Steelers also notably opened practice windows for Roman Wilson and Michael Pittman Jr. to return from injured reserve. Pittsburgh getting two of their top receivers back could be a help for their struggling offense.
