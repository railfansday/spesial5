NHL Power Rankings: Maple Leafs continue to slide as Sergei Bobrovsky struggles

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

If you were wondering how long it would take for the pitchforks to make an appearance in Toronto, the answer was 20 games. After a promising start, the Maple Leafs have lost four in a row and six of their last seven.

One of the key factors in this early slide is the play of goaltender Sergei Bobrovsky. Who could have guessed that signing a 38-year-old goalie to a big-money contract was a bad idea? Bobrovsky ranks 62nd in the NHL with -3.68 goals saved above average, and his .881 save percentage is tied for 42nd.

It's been a concerning stretch for Bobrovsky, but he also doesn't shoulder all of the blame for this skid. The Leafs have scored just 17 goals in their last seven games, and a forward group that lacks depth has been devastated by the loss of Kirill Marchenko.

Gavin McKenna has looked like the rookie he is on a regular basis, John Tavares has been banged up and Easton Cowan has cooled down after a hot start.

Toronto is now in jeopardy of falling behind in a competitive Atlantic Division. Only the Detroit Red Wings have been worse, and that organization has been a dumpster fire for months now. The Leafs have just a couple weeks to turn things around before Thanksgiving, a key date in the NHL season as the playoff picture begins to solidify.
