Emeka Egbuka has taken a big step back in Year 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Buccaneers wide receiver Emeka Egbuka had just two catches for 18 yards in Sunday’s loss to the Lions. During his breakout rookie season last year, that would have been a surprisingly bad game for Egbuka. This year, there’s nothing surprising when Egbuka has a bad game.

Last year Egubka played all 17 games and finished the season with 63 catches for 938 yards and two touchdowns. This year Egbuka has missed two games with a foot injury, and 10 games into the season he has just 33 catches for 296 yards and two touchdowns.

Egbuka’s average has dropped from 14.9 yards per catch last season to 9.0 yards per catch this season. Last year Egbuka had 20 catches of 20 or more yards, and three catches of 40 or more yards. This year Egbuka has three catches of 20 or more yards, and no catches of 40 or more yards.

Last year Egbuka had 34 receiving first downs. This year Egbuka has 14 receiving first downs.

It’s been more than a full year since Egbuka gained even 65 receiving yards in a season. The last time he topped 65 yards was Week 10 of 2025.

Of course, Egbuka is far from the only part of the Bucs’ passing game that has severely regressed this season. Baker Mayfield is having his worst year, and undrafted rookie Jalon Daniels started three games when Mayfield was out. It’s fair to question whether the fault lies with Egbuka, or with the quarterbacks throwing to Egbuka.

Either way, after being one of the NFL’s best rookies in 2025, Egbuka has been one of the NFL’s biggest Year 2 disappointments in 2026.
