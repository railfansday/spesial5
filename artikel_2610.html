Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Cavaliers "hosted a shootaround at the Obama Presidential Center on Monday morning," becoming the first NBA team to practice at the facility that opened in Chicago over the summer, according to Jamal Collier of ESPN.com. The WNBA became the first professional sports league to use the court during All-Star Weekend in July, and its players were given a surprise visit from former President Barack Obama. And although he was not in town Monday morning, Cavs G Donovan Mitchell still called it an "honor and a blessing" to use the courts.
