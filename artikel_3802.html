You cannot replay missions in GTA 6, but you can use multiple save files, as Rockstar explains how it made missions "harder to fail"

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

You cannot replay GTA 6 missions again once you've completed them. This might be a shock to some people, since the option to do so was included in Rockstar games as recently as GTA 5, but here once you've made progress in the story, that's it. Our reviewer confirmed this in his 100+ hour playthrough of the title.

You can, of course, use the game's 15 save slots instead - but considering that there will be many, many more missions than there are slots, you're going to have to think about when and where you want to make a record of your progress. By my maths, that means you're going to want to make a new manual save for every 6.6 percent of the game you complete. There is, of course, an auto-save slot, too, that should help you out if you get Busted or Wasted. There will also be key story missions - you will be able to tell when they are, much like you could tell when there was about to be a big heist in GTA 5, a showdown in GTA: San Andreas, or a raid on a gang lord's mansion in GTA: Vice City - that you may want to save before attempting.

It seems, though, that it's going to be a bit harder than in previous games to hit that fail state in GTA 6. "The team embraced more of an open-ended design philosophy on this project," says David Hynd, vice president of technology on GTA 6. "For example, we really wanted to reduce the number of Mission fails for the player experimenting or trying something different. And we wanted to encourage the player to do what felt right to them for their particular play style."

That means that Rockstar, by considering its deployment of standard gameplay mechanics and enemy AI, and re-evaluating how it approached mission and level design, has created "many different ways" for you to approach any given mission in the game. The result? "It's a lot harder to keep Missions as open as possible (especially in a game as large as this), but the freedom and flexibility it allows the player was worth it," says Hynd. He also notes that the team expects players to "learn and reapply" consistent rules in terms of how the world works - and be surprised when they do and don't apply.

In the previous game, you could access any previous mission you'd completed and be able to play it again on a whim. For GTA 5, you'd simply boot up Story Mode, open the pause menu, go to Game > Replay Mission, and pick the mission you want to play. GTA 6's structure is more complicated, though, hence why Rockstar made the decision to make progress this linear.
