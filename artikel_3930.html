Commandos: Origins Deluxe Edition coming to Switch 2 on December 15

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Publisher Kalypso Media and developer Engine Software will release stealth tactics game Commandos: Origins Deluxe Edition for Switch 2 on December 15 for $59.99, the companies announced.

Commandos: Origins first launched for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC via Steam, Epic Games Store, and Microsoft Store on April 9, 2025, followed by PlayStation 4 and Xbox One on September 17, 2025.

Here is an overview of the Switch 2 version, via Kalypso Media:

Following its successful launch on other platforms in 2025, the legendary tactical stealth series is ready to take its next mission on the move—with both post-launch expansions, Shadows over Crete and No Man Left Behind, available to be purchased separately.

The Commandos: Origins Deluxe Edition on Nintendo Switch 2 takes players back to the very beginning of the elite special forces unit, telling the story of how the legendary Commandos came together. Led by Jack O’Hara, the Green Beret, players will infiltrate enemy territory, sabotage key targets and lead their elite squad in World War II.

On Nintendo Switch 2, players can put the Joy-Con 2 mouse controls to work for a more precise approach to battlefield command. Switch to mouse control to navigate the battlefield with greater accuracy, select targets and units with precision, and issue commands with the control expected from a tactical stealth classic. Furthermore GameChat and two-player cooperative multiplayer mode are supported, either online or via local split-screen.

The Commandos: Origins Deluxe Edition on Nintendo Switch 2 delivers the full experience by including the complete base game, six iconic Commando skins from Commandos 2 adapted for Commandos: Origins, a 3D printing template for the Green Beret, a digital Commandos: Origins calendar and the original soundtrack with 15 symphonic tracks.

Alongside the main campaign, both post-launch downloadable contents “Shadows over Crete” and “No Man Left Behind” on Nintendo Switch are available from day one, adding further missions and challenges to the Commandos’ World War II campaign. Each downloadable content is available for $14.99 USD.

The Commandos: Origins Deluxe Edition will be released digitally for Nintendo Switch 2 on December 15, 2026, for $59.99 USD
