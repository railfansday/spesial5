The Backworld launches in Q2 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Backworld, a turn-based RPG with narrative-driven horror elements, will launch for PC via Steam in Q2 2027, publisher Top Hat Studios and developer Numor Games announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:

Slip between the cracks of reality into a broken world where danger awaits at every turn. Explore endless dimensions to defy beings born out of pure chaos. Use your hoverboard to fly across gaps of darkness, hack into cyberspace with an alien engineer, and absorb the soul of a dead god.

Forge your own path—and bend this shattered reality to your will.
