Xbox CSO Responds To Criticism Around Game Branding, Promises To Fix In 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

"We are fixing this. Will roll out in 2027."

Every now and again, we hear grumbles from some parts of the Xbox community that they're frustrated when games are announced and Xbox doesn't make a big deal about how those titles are specifically first-party creations.

In other words, some fans want Xbox to shout from the rooftops about its own games for its own platform, and not to make them immediately look like third-party titles. There's been some discussion about this on social media this week with Xbox megafan Klobrille and Windows Central editor Jez Corden, highlighting situations like StarCraft, Diablo V and new Fallout games where it felt like they were announced without any real references to Xbox.

And, when Corden stated that "Xbox is really weak at linking its games to its brand", it prompted Xbox CSO Matthew Ball to get involved in the discussion and promise that the situation is being fixed in 2027.

Who knows what "fixed" means in this regard, but it sounds like the team at Xbox is going to be more proactive at promoting the brand when an Xbox Game Studios, Activision Blizzard or Bethesda title is announced / shown off.

That doesn't necessarily mean anything in terms of exclusives and multi-platform titles (we assume we'll still get some of the latter), but hopefully it'll be much clearer to the average consumer when a game is an in-house Xbox game.

Let's see what happens in the New Year, eh? We've got Fable just around the corner in a couple of months' time, and then the likes of Clockwork: Revolution and Spyro: A Realm Reborn (which is only published by Xbox, but nevertheless still counts!) along with whatever else Xbox might have in store for us in 2027.
