Arsenal handed new William Saliba boost after France squad announcement

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Arsenal have been handed a welcome boost over William Saliba after Zinedine Zidane named his second France squad.

Former Real Madrid head coach Zidane is preparing to take charge of Les Bleus for just the fifth time after replacing Didier Deschamps, who stood down following the 2026 World Cup in north America earlier this summer.

The first post-World Cup international window was an extended one which lasted three weeks with France facing Belgium twice, Italy and Turkey in a Nations League quadruple-header.

France took four points from a possible 12 to sit top of League A, Group 1 ahead of facing Italy and Turkey within the next nine days.

Zidane’s first France squad did not contain Saliba as the Arsenal defender was still recovering from a back injury sustained while playing for the national team in the World Cup semi-final against eventual winners Spain.

Saliba has since resumed full fitness. He played 45 minutes for the Gunners in a behind-closed-doors friendly last week before travelling with the squad to the Czech Republic for the 2-1 win over Slavia Prague in the Champions League.

However, the 25-year-old has not been selected by Zidane for France duty this month.

The news will be welcomed by Mikel Arteta as Saliba should benefit from more time in training, though the Frenchman will miss out on some valuable minutes to build up his match sharpness.

“We’ve been in very close contact,” Arteta said on Friday ahead of Arsenal’s Premier League clash against Hull. “They’re announcing [their squad] at 5pm this evening.

“They have all the information, now it’s about them, about Zinedine in this case to make the call. We will accept whatever it is.”

After the international break, Arsenal face Newcastle at St James’ Park, with home games to follow against Borussia Dortmund and Manchester City.
