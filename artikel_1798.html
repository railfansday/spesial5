JJ Gabriel confirms Manchester United exit with emotional farewell statement

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

JJ Gabriel has confirmed his decision to leave Manchester United in a statement, with Real Madrid expected to win the race to sign the highly-rated young winger.

The 16-year-old has played in United’s academy since 2020 and was expected to start appearing in the first team this season.

Until, that is, he made the shock decision to lodge a transfer request in September, amid reports he and his entourage were frustrated with his lack of first-team opportunities.

United worked hard to convince him to stay and offered him a new contract, but the player has not trained at the club for some time and is now expected to join Real Madrid. He will leave Old Trafford without having made a senior appearance.

After months of speculation as to where he might end up, Gabriel has now issued the clearest update yet on his situation, confirming his decision to leave United in a post to his Instagram page.

Speaking over clips of himself playing for the club, he said: “Dear Manchester United, I arrived in 2020 as a 10-year-old boy with big dreams.

“Here, I grew as a player and a person. From scoring at Old Trafford to receiving the Jimmy Murphy Award, you gave me moments I’ll always treasure.

“To every coach, every member of staff, to my team-mates and everyone who supported me, thank you for believing in me and helping me grow.

“As I begin my next chapter, Manchester United will always be a part of my story. Thank you for everything, JJ.”

Gabriel has reportedly been training alone in Madrid while waiting for his move away from United to be ratified.

Gabriel signed with the mega-agency Epic Sports this week, and is now represented by the super-agent Ali Barat. Epic is one of football’s most prestigious agencies, with Moises Caicedo, Hugo Ekitike and Nicolas Jackson all on the books.

United are expected to receive around £350,000 in compensation following Gabriel’s exit, though they would have been due far more than that had he opted to join another Premier League side.
