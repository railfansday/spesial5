Krispy Kreme is giving away free Thanksgiving doughnuts today — how to claim yours

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

TL;DR: Krispy Kreme has two Thanksgiving doughnut deals running right now, including a $1 Original Glazed dozen and a freebie that runs through Nov. 23.

Does your Monday morning WFH routine need an immediate morale boost before Thanksgiving travel chaos completely derails your week? Krispy Kreme is quietly running a free promotion that's worth the detour.

Through Nov. 23, Krispy Kreme rewards members (it's free to join) can score one free Thanksgiving collection doughnut with any purchase at participating U.S. shops and online. And if you miss today’s deadline, Krispy Kreme is also offering a $1 Original Glazed Dozen when you buy any dozen through Nov. 29 in the app.

To claim the limited-time freebie, you simply need to buy any menu item (even a single drip coffee or glazed doughnut) and either scan your Rewards barcode in the Krispy Kreme app (iOS or Android) at the register, provide your account phone number in-store, or log in and apply the reward at checkout online.

You can choose from four seasonal flavors: Cinnamon Apple Filled, Double Chocolate Crumble, Caramel Pumpkin, or Berry Cobbler.

Is this truly a "free" promotion when you're being asked to make a qualifying purchase? It's definitely a grey area, and frankly, we're not going to get into it. I'm simply pointing you toward bonus doughnuts, and for that, I will not apologize.
