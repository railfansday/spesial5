The Magic: The Gathering Marvel Super Heroes Doom Prevails Commander Deck is under market price for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

SAVE OVER $4: As of Nov. 24, the Magic: The Gathering Marvel Super Heroes Doom Prevails Commander Deck is back to its lowest-ever price of $59.99 at Amazon. That saves you $4.46 off its typical cost of $64.45.

This year's Black Friday sale has already dropped some great trading card deals for collectors to comb through, including select Magic: The Gathering decks. In what feels like perfect timing after the recent Avengers: Doomsday trailer drop, Amazon's even discounted the Marvel Super Heroes Doom Prevails Commander Deck down to its lowest-ever price of $59.99.

This is about $4 cheaper than its typical price at the retailer ($64.45), and it also beats its market price on TCGplayer. If this 100-card deck has felt like a must-buy for your MTG Marvel collection, this is a great time to grab it and save a few bucks.

Amongst that packed stack of 100 cards, you'll find 30 brand new Marvel-themed Commander cards plus a Legendary Creature card that features borderless art and a traditional foil treatment. The deck also comes with 10 double-sided token cards, a strategy guide and reference card, and the deck box itself to keep everything sorted (which features some very cool art of Doctor Doom and Loki).

While it's not specifically listed as a Black Friday deal, it also may not be on sale for very long. Don't miss out on this chance to save some cash on the Magic: The Gathering Marvel Super Heroes Doom Prevails Commander Deck at Amazon.
