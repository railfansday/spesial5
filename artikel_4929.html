‘Very racist’ Netflix SBF series, BitMart’s $319M shortfall: Asia Express

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Netflix’s The Altruist has been slammed for its ‘very racist’ portrayal of CZ. BitMart reportedly has a $319.5 million shortfall and just $1.9M in liquid assets.

Former FTX chief operating officer Constance Waing has slammed Netflix’s portrayal of Binance founder Changpeng Zhao in The Altruists as racist, saying it relied on Asian mafia stereotypes.

“It’s very racist,” Waing told Cointelegraph. The Altruists is a dramatization of the rise and fall of Sam Bankman-Fried’s FTX crypto empire and his subsequent imprisonment for fraud.

Waing took issue with one scene in which FTX founder SBF and Alameda CEO Caroline Ellison enter through a back door to meet CZ, saying it suggested he was “trying to get these two innocent people into something bad.”In another episode the fictional CZ urges SBF to give Alameda Research advance access to FTX customer orders so it can trade ahead of them, a suggestion the real CZ labelled “insane.” Ethereum creator Vitalik Buterin said the portrayal exaggerated Zhao’s accent and questioned whether it was racist.

Woke 1 may have been crazy, but racist is still racist says Vitalik Buterin.

South Korean crypto exchanges Upbit and Bithumb suspended deposits and withdrawals of CYBER on Sunday after the project reported suspicious activity involving its cross-chain bridge.

On Saturday, Cyber said it detected unexpected minting of CYBER tokens on Solana, followed by a transfer of tokens out of its bridge’s Ethereum lockbox. Cyber, formerly CyberConnect, operates an Ethereum layer-2 network designed for social applications.

The project said it was investigating the incident and advised users to avoid using the bridge or depositing CYBER to exchanges while the investigation continues.

South Korea’s Financial Services Commission (FSC) unveiled a draft bill Tuesday that would initially limit issuance of won-denominated stablecoins to groups majority-owned by banks and generally cap shareholders’ stakes in crypto exchanges at 20%.

The draft also seeks to lift South Korea’s 2017 ban on domestic initial coin offerings.

The 20% ownership rule is set to affect a deal that Naver struck for Upbit owner Dunamu. The deal gave Naver full control, but under the new rules shares above the 20% cap would lose their vote.

South Korean fintech companies could gain influence in bank-led stablecoin groups by signing up partners that help people spend won-denominated tokens, Tiger Research said in its Tuesday report.

“Those relationships are likely to matter more to the business than the volume of stablecoins issued,” the Asia-focused research company said.

The US Department of Justice is seeking to recover more than $1.1 billion in crypto stolen from cryptocurrency exchange Binance in October by North Korean hackers.

In a court filing Monday, prosecutors asked a federal court in Washington, DC, to permanently forfeit the funds, which are held in the USDC stablecoin. Since the attack, US authorities said they have helped to freeze, seize or return nearly $3 billion of the more than $6 billion taken from Binance.

Japanese financial giants SBI Holdings and Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group (SMFG) have launched a joint venture with the Solana Foundation to tokenize assets, develop stablecoin payment infrastructure and expand onchain financial services across Asia.

Japan’s Financial Services Agency has published briefing materials considering proposals to regulate apps and sites that connect to DeFi, and whether to discourage access to protocols that lack money laundering controls.

Daiwa has completed a technical verification in which AI agents processed natural language instructions to screen various DEX options and to conduct stablecoin and security token swaps.

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) launched a platform Wednesday that supports round-the-clock settlement of tokenized debt securities in multiple currencies.

The CMU Digital Asset Platform, developed and operated by CMU OmniClear Limited on behalf of the HKMA, uses cash tokens for payment and connects with conventional payment systems and central bank digital currencies.

Hong Kong has published a bill that would bring crypto custodians, advisers and other virtual asset service providers under mandatory licensing or registration.

On Friday, the government officially published its Virtual Asset Services Legislation (Amendment) Bill 2026, proposing that firms carrying out those activities would need to obtain a license from, or register with, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC). The bill is set for its first reading in the Legislative Council on Dec. 2.

The Monetary Authority of Singapore has clarified that an on-chain token is not automatically a bank deposit. It draws a line between genuine tokenized deposits and crypto-like bearer instruments.

A UK court has approved an asset recovery settlement totaling 55 million pounds from Su Haijin and Xu Haika, two defendants in Singapore’s largest money-laundering case. Haika is being forced to sell 16 London apartments held through affiliated companies to repay the funds. The August 2023 police operation saw crypto worth more than S$38 million seized.

According to Nikkei Asia, Bitmart has a $319.5 million shortfall but just $1.9 million in liquid assets available for customer repayments. Numerous users report they have been unable to access their accounts.Bitmart disclosed the figures to Alvarez & Marsal, which is overseeing the restructuring process. The collapse highlights the dangers of high yield crypto products, with Chinese investors reportedly drawn to the platform by short term savings products offering 18.8% annualized yields for 14 day deposits.
