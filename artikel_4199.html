Call of Duty: Warzone Mode Lets You Drop a Nuke for $1 Million, But There’s a Catch

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Season 1 of Modern Warfare 4 saw the beginning of a new era for Call of Duty: Warzone with the debut of the Zodiac map. As a Resurgence map, Zodiac is compact and blends numerous combat environments for diverse and action-packed gameplay.

Despite Warzone’s full integration with Modern Warfare 4, battle royale modes feature unique mechanics, new equipment, and a fresh set of mastery camos to work towards.

This is DualShockers' review of COD: Modern Warfare 4, covering all the ins and outs of the campaign, multiplayer, and the new DMZ Extraction Mode.

Call of Duty players will know that earning a nuke is the ultimate display of dominance. Nukes are back in Warzone, but the way you acquire one is different from what you may be used to.

In previous iterations of Warzone, to drop a nuke, you would have to complete the Champion’s Quest. To unlock the Champion’s Quest, you needed to win five Warzone matches in a row or win 30 games within a season. Meeting either of those requirements would see the Champion’s Quest contract spawn at the beginning of your next match.

After accepting the Champion’s Quest, you and your squad would have to collect three radioactive elements from around the map that were needed to assemble the nuke at a bomb site. This would also mark you on the map for the entire lobby to see. Then, you’d have to defend the nuke, as other players could come in and defuse it. If the nuke was defused, your squad was wiped out, or the nuke timer hit zero before completing all the requirements, you’d fail to detonate the nuke.

It was by no means an easy feat, and it required a lot of teamwork to get the job done. With the launch of Warzone Season 1, you can still nuke the lobby, but some of the grind has been taken out of obtaining one.

To drop a nuke on the Warzone map, Zodiac, you must be the first player to purchase it from a Buy Station for $1 million cash. This means you and your squad must work together to loot as much cash as possible, earn cash from completing contracts, eliminating bots, and more, all while staying alive to keep it in your pocket until you reach the $1 million mark.

However, you can only detonate a nuke in Zodiac via Warzone’s Rally Point game mode. Rally Point is a training ground experience that supports a maximum of 20 players. It’s designed to allow you to explore the map, try out different weapons, and take your time mastering the mechanics. A nuke can’t be purchased from Buy Stations or acquired any other way in regular battle royale matches.

Anyone can load into a match of Rally Point and begin racking up cash to purchase a nuke, as there are no other requirements to unlock it.

When you gather enough cash to purchase the nuke, you and your squad will exfil from the battlefield while you watch the map get obliterated, and the match comes to an end.

You and your squad must work together to loot as much cash as possible, earn cash from completing contracts, eliminating bots, and more, all while staying alive to keep it in your pocket until you reach the $1 million mark.

For your efforts, you will earn the Perfect End weapon blueprint for the HAN 86 assault rifle, and you can show it off across Modern Warfare 4 and Warzone multiplayer modes.

At the time of writing, it’s unknown if a nuke will be added to standard battle royale modes. Those looking to secure the ultimate bragging rights will have to master Rally Point matches, for now.

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 multiplayer has arrived, and it features skill-based matchmaking, with no Open Matchmaking playlist options in sight.
