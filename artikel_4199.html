The wait for Grand Theft Auto 6 is almost over. Rockstar's long-awaited open-world adventure launches on November 19, and the first reviews are already rolling in, with the game currently sitting at a staggering 99 on Metacritic, tying the original The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the highest score in the site's history.

But while most players are still getting ready to explore Leonida for the first time, some fans are already thinking about a much more important part of the GTA experience: cheat codes. And, perhaps unsurprisingly, they are already circulating online!

According to GTA Base, a well-known resource among Grand Theft Auto fans, there are already 61 cheats cataloged for GTA 6. They cover everything from getting extra cash and spawning vehicles to changing the weather and applying bizarre visual filters. But there's one catch: you won't be able to use any of them until you finish the main story. So yes, Rockstar really wants you to earn your chaos this time.

Unlike previous entries, GTA 6 doesn't let you activate cheats right from the beginning. First, you need to complete the story, which unlocks the Cheats section under Progress Menu. Each cheat also has its own requirement, so simply knowing a button combination isn't enough.

Once you've unlocked the cheat you want, bring up your in-game phone, hold L1 + R1 on PlayStation 5 or LB + RB on Xbox Series X|S, and enter the corresponding combination using the D-pad. Interestingly, all the sequences use directional inputs, meaning the codes are identical on both platforms.
