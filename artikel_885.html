DJ Khaled Details Journey to 75-Pound Weight Loss Transformation

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

After DJ Khaled underwent a 75-pound weight-loss journey the “I’m the One” rapper revealed his health secrets in an exclusive E! News interview as part of his “Walk, Golf…And Wegovy” campaign.

These days, DJ Khaled isn’t going for another one—especially when it comes to food.

After the “I’m the One” rapper sparked major attention for his 75-pound weight loss, he confirmed his transformation happened with the help of the GLP-1 Wegovy, as well as some major lifestyle changes.

“My kids and my wife inspired me,” the 50-year-old told E! News’ Will Marfuggi in an exclusive interview. “When I turned 50, I woke up and thought, ‘You know, it’s time to take it to the next level.’”

And he’s taking fans inside his journey with his “Walk, Golf… And Wegovy” campaign. Along with his medication, Khaled has found that the best music, or rather method, for his weight loss has been through walking—specifically on the golf course. But much like beats, he also switches it up.

“I do gym,” Khaled explained. “I’ve got two treadmills here. I’ve got an elliptical here. I’ve got ropes tied to my tree. I dip and dab into some of the things. When it’s raining, I try to do at least 10,000 steps on the treadmill.”

Starting out, the “All I Do Is Win” hitmaker’s health goal was to simply max out his personal, daily pedometer, but he’s far exceeded his own expectations, noting, “I’ve been getting more than 10,000 steps in a day.”

And while Khaled—dad to Asahd, 10, Aalam, 6, with wife Nicole Tuck—explained that along with his walking routine his doctor “loved the idea” for him to get on Wegovy, he considered that it’s all worked in tandem with his lifestyle changes.

“I stayed consistent with the golf and I noticed I started eating two times a day,” he continued. “I eat lunch around 1 o’clock and then if I want a snack, I’ll eat olives or cucumbers or my kids’ apple sauce—the ones that you squeeze. And then I eat dinner around 8 ‘cause I’m on my kids’ schedule and we eat dinner together when I’m home.”

Reflecting on the last year of his health journey, Khaled called his transformation “beautiful.”

“When you start getting healthier, you just want to do a lot more healthier things because it makes you happier,” he shared. “I think that’s the key. To challenge yourself to do better. Me turning 50, I feel younger than I’ve ever been.”
