EWR Digital Houston Industrial AI Search Pilot: ChatGPT and Perplexity Had 16% Supplier Overlap in EWR Digital's Houston Industrial Search Pilot

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

For one question about testing fire pumps and backflow preventers at a Houston industrial site, the two tools surfaced 26 companies across six answers. Only one appeared on both.

HOUSTON, Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- EWR Digital found that ChatGPT and Perplexity surfaced different sets of companies when asked the same questions about Greater Houston industrial suppliers. Across 12 questions covering metal fabrication, equipment rental, and fire protection, 37 of 225 distinct question and supplier combinations appeared on both tools, or 16%. Each supplier counted once per question, even if it appeared in multiple answers.

The result gives industrial suppliers a concrete reason to check how they appear across AI tools. In the pilot's temporary chiller rental question, the two platforms surfaced 18 companies between them and shared just one. For large structural steel fabrication, they shared three of 25.

The platforms also drew on different websites. On Nov. 22, they shared 15% of cited domains when compared question by question. When EWR Digital repeated the same questions on Nov. 23, citation overlap was 14%. The supplier comparison used the Nov. 22 responses.

"A buyer can ask the same industrial sourcing question in two AI tools and come away with different companies to call. For suppliers, that makes it worth checking where and how they show up. For buyers, it makes the usual checks on capabilities, capacity, and certifications as important as ever."

EWR Digital used its internally built AI Visibility Tracker to run 12 questions against web-enabled ChatGPT and Perplexity models. Each tool received each question three times on each of two dates, producing 144 answers. EWR Digital compared suppliers and cited website domains within each question. The pilot measured the answers these models returned for the prompts and dates tested. Buyers should verify supplier qualifications, capacity, and availability directly.
