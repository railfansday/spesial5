Strategy's overall value has dropped below its bitcoin holdings again

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Bitcoin broke below $56,000 on Nov. 21 as the company raised its preferred stock buyback authorization to $4 billion

Strategy has an enterprise value of about $47 billion, below the roughly $52 billion value of its 851,305 bitcoin, according to Bloomberg. The gap opened after bitcoin fell below $56,000 on Nov. 21. Investors pulled back from riskier assets as worries grew that the AI trade was coming apart.

Strategy reported its latest bitcoin purchase on Monday, saying it bought 457 bitcoin for $29.6 million from Nov. 16 to Nov. 22, at an average price of $64,898. As of Nov. 22, the company's holdings stood at 851,305 bitcoin, acquired for an aggregate $64.23 billion, or an average $75,446 per coin.

The company said its board raised the authorization for its digital credit securities repurchase program from $3.0 billion to $4.0 billion. After the increase, $1.12 billion remained available under the program as of Nov. 22.

Strategy repurchased 2,183,747 shares of its STRC preferred stock for $211.8 million last week. Of that, $147.0 million came from net proceeds of common stock sales and $64.8 million from USD Cash. The company raised $176.7 million in net proceeds from common stock sales under its at-the-market program. Of that total, $29.6 million went to bitcoin purchases and the rest to STRC repurchases.

The board also authorized management to reallocate up to $1.0 billion from the USD Reserve to USD Cash. After the reallocation, the USD Reserve held $3.73 billion and USD Cash held $1.09 billion as of Nov. 22.

The enterprise value metric includes debt and preferred stock. Co-founder and Executive Chairman Michael Saylor calls it mNAV, and it crossed the same threshold in June, according to Bloomberg.

"The market is quite clearly showing that it has very little interest in leveraged Bitcoin exposure at the moment," said Stéphane Ouellette, founder and chief executive officer of FRNT Financial, according to Bloomberg.

Saylor declared an end to the crypto bear market in October and predicted bitcoin would move above $100,000. He said at the time that bitcoin had bottomed slightly below $60,000. Bitcoin was trading around $83,000 then.

Strategy sold bitcoin in late May for the first time since late 2022. It later adopted a capital framework that authorized bitcoin sales and set aside cash pools to cover preferred dividends and debt obligations.
