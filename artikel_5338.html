Palantir shares dropped 3.60% in premarket trading on Friday to $148.15, down $5.53 from Thursday’s close of $153.68, as the broader selloff in AI and tech stocks continued. The company is also in the news after the US Justice Department charged a Russian national with acting as a foreign agent to spy on a Silicon Valley technology firm. The DOJ did not name the company, but two sources told Reuters it was Palantir, targeted over its support for Ukraine. Prosecutors allege the man photographed employees’ laptop screens at a nearby coffee shop, left a laptop near the office so handlers could try to collect data remotely, and followed staff, once trailing a woman for about 50 kilometres until she called police. Separately, a CNBC analysis found President Trump’s investment accounts bought Palantir shares before the company won federal awards worth about $42.5 million. CNBC found no sign Trump directed the trades, and the White House said his portfolio is managed independently.
