Justice Department Announces Actions to Disrupt North Korea’s Massive Theft of Cryptocurrency

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

These actions include the seizure of cloud-based infrastructure used in the FaceStealer social engineering scheme that has resulted in the theft of more than $400 million worth of cryptocurrency and the court-authorized seizure and restraint of more than $1.2 billion worth of cryptocurrency recently stolen from Binance, a virtual currency exchange.
