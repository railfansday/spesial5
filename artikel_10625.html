NASA is hiring astronauts who could live and work on the Moon

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

NASA is looking for its next generation of astronauts, and you could be among those selected to travel to the Moon or even help prepare for humanity’s first trip to Mars.

The space agency has officially opened applications for its latest astronaut recruitment drive, offering successful candidates the chance to work on some of its biggest upcoming missions.

It’s a pretty well-paid job, too. The position comes with an estimated annual salary of $164,395, although there’s a fairly demanding list of requirements.

NASA is hoping to recruit people who could eventually set foot on the Moon, help establish a permanent lunar base, or contribute to future missions to Mars.

“This is an extraordinary time to join NASA’s astronaut corps,” said NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman in a November 7 announcement.

“The people we select could walk on the Moon, live and work at the Moon Base, and help pioneer the systems and operations that will ultimately carry crews to Mars.”

Anyone who believes they have what it takes can submit an application through the official job listing. But before you start packing your bags for space, there are a few boxes you’ll need to tick.

Firstly, applicants must be US citizens and typically need a master’s degree in a science, technology, engineering, or mathematics (STEM) field. However, certain medical qualifications, doctoral studies, and test pilot training can also meet the education requirements.

Candidates also need at least three years of relevant professional experience, although pilots can qualify through specific flight-hour requirements.

They must also pass NASA’s medical examinations and demonstrate strong communication, teamwork, and leadership skills.

Those selected will undergo approximately two years of training, learning everything from spacewalking and robotics to operating spacecraft.

However, completing the training doesn’t guarantee a trip into space. The competition will be fierce, as during its previous recruitment drive in 2024, more than 8,000 people applied, but just 10 were selected.

NASA is accepting applications until December 3, 2026, with its next astronaut class expected to be selected in late 2027.
