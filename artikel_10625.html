How to save in GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Unless you're keeping the game suspended while your console sleeps, you need to know how to save in Grand Theft Auto 6. This is the sort of game where not saving can undo hours of progress, which is absolutely gutting when it happens. There are also questions about the GTA 6 autosave function and how it works, which we can also help explain.

Here's the lowdown on exactly how to save in GTA 6 and how the game's autosave works — and why it might seem like it isn't working at all.

To manually save in GTA 6, you need to pause the game, navigate to the "Game" option on the menu, then select "Save." This will show 15 save slots, of which you can choose any to save your game. If you suspect you may want to load an old save to play through missions another way or make a different choice, I'd recommend using a rolling save system, where every time you save, you overwrite the oldest save file you have.

However, there are restrictions around when you can manually save. You cannot save during a mission, if you're in combat, if you have a wanted level, or if you're falling. Therefore, you must wait until a moment of calm to save your game.

GTA 6 does have an autosave function, however it's a little sporadic in how it actually works. The game should autosave after missions are completed, whether they are main missions or optional ones. However, there are reports that this hasn't been working properly, so you should be manually saving your game on a regular basis in case the autosave lets you down.
