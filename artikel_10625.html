Five convicted after man gifted $100K by MDMotivator was kidnapped and left beside burning SUV

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Five people have been convicted in a kidnapping conspiracy targeting Detroit man Curtis Dixon weeks after YouTuber Zach Dereniowski, known online as MDMotivator, gifted him more than $100,000 and a new vehicle following a viral act of kindness.

Dixon went viral in August 2025 after helping someone who appeared to be blind. Dereniowski rewarded him with more than $100,000, a Yukon Denali and other gifts. Dixon and his wife were later kidnapped and robbed in an alleged plot targeting the money.

According to a November 4 announcement from the U.S. Attorney’s Office for the Eastern District of Michigan, Roland Chatmon, Leica Lopez Martinez, Ieasha Stroman and Dneiqua Stroman pleaded guilty to kidnapping conspiracy and federal firearms offenses.

Kenneth Anderson had previously pleaded guilty to money laundering for his role in the case.

The DOJ said the conspiracy was organized by Chatmon, who is Dixon’s nephew, after he learned about the money Dixon had received. Chatmon allegedly discussed the windfall with his brother during a recorded jail call before recruiting others to help carry out the robbery.

On September 1, 2025, one of the conspirators allegedly lured Dixon by pretending to need a ride. After he arrived at a Detroit home, the group carjacked him at gunpoint, tied him up and drove him around while attempting to withdraw money from his accounts.

The group then took Dixon to his home, where they allegedly stole a safe and tied up his wife. Dixon was later left beside his truck, which the conspirators set on fire. Anderson helped move money from Dixon’s Cash App account into accounts controlled by the group.

The four defendants who recently pleaded guilty face mandatory minimum sentences of seven years in federal prison and a maximum sentence of life. Anderson was previously sentenced to probation and ordered to pay restitution. Sentencing is scheduled for February and March 2027.

Dixon told WXYZ that he still has not recovered all of his money and has struggled to find housing since the attack.

“I’d rather be broke and happy than rich and have to look over my shoulder,” he said.
