The 5 Must-Have Fall Boots I'm Wearing Straight Through Winter

Growing up in Southern California, I was a flip-flop girl through and through. But since moving to New York, I've come to appreciate the importance of a cold-weather shoe collection. In fact, boots now outnumber sandals in my closet.

Whether I'm wearing a plaid mini skirt and sheer tights or straight-leg denim and a knitted sweater, boots complete the look. They're what ballet flats are to the warmer seasons, providing a tried-and-true footwear staple to rely on for the rest of fall and all winter long.

While I certainly own a few striking pairs of boots (like bubblegum-pink iterations and cow-print block heels), curating a timeless collection that effortlessly pairs with your wardrobe is essential. This way, you can build upon the curated collection season after season, rather than splurging on trend-specific footwear every fall. Because these styles can be worn for years to come, investing in quality pieces that will stand the test of time is key.

There's still a month of fall left, and these five styles will carry you through winter too. Ahead, discover the ultimate fall boots starter pack, including classic knee-highs, Western boots, and rich burgundy hues.

If you only own one pair of boots, make it these. The little black dress of footwear can be dressed up or down, transcending occasion, age, and style. The sleek and timeless shoe has even elevated my simple shift dresses with ease. Suede iterations offer a cozy appeal, slick leather delivers sophistication, stilettos are sultry, and block heels are comfortable.

Stuart Weitzman Stuart Leather Power Boots

On casual fall days, the '70s-style pull-on boot will be your best friend. The straight, stovepipe shaft skips zippers in favor of pull tabs. Typically hitting at the mid-calf or knee, the heritage style boasts a low, stacked heel and sturdy sole. Wear the boots with a mini skirt, tights, and a chunky button-up cardigan for a cozy, laid-back look.

Steve Madden Riggs Banana Leather Square Toe Boots

Like black, brown, and navy, burgundy acts as a neutral come fall. The deep, saturated color feels grounded, while still bold enough to make an impact with a simple outfit. If you're looking for a style that is fashion-forward but still timeless, this is it. Rich velvets, warm suede, and textured bouclé effortlessly pair with the deep red shade.

When I don't want to tuck a tall boot beneath my pants (think flare jeans and billowy trousers), I reach for ankle boots instead. The short shaft looks clean underneath denim, silk, cotton, and wool, creating a smooth finish that never looks frumpy. A neutral beige shade ensures your short boots pair with everything from a colorful knit shrug to a cream sweater dress.

Tory Burch Asymmetric Cap Toe Ankle Boots

You don't need to be on a ranch to wear Western boots. The rodeo-inspired footwear is officially a part of the cool-girl uniform because it's both utilitarian and dressy. While it's certainly more adventurous than the other options on this list, it's just as practical, especially when you choose an earthy hue. Style your cowgirl boots with a Fair Isle pullover and billowy skirt for an of-the-moment take.
