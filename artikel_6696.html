Lily James and Lucas Bravo Bring Coordinating Date-Night Style to the Governors Awards Bash

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Lily James shimmied into the Governors Awards in a sparkly silver flapper-style Tamara Ralph dress that dripped with crystal fringe. Silver was a big theme at the event’s starry red carpet, and yet the Rebecca star’s gown stood out for its lively silhouette.

After the Oscars were handed out—honorees includes Glenn Close, Ridley Scott, and Floyd Norman—and the champagne was popped, attendees headed out to the official after-party, held in Los Angeles’s historic Chateau Marmont.

For the occasion, James performed a quick change, trading her electric gown for something a little more subtle (but just as hot): a little black dress.

The mini had a little bow up top, and was layered under an oversize leather blazer for a cool touch. James slipped her legs into sheer black tights and dipped her toes into a pair of Miu Miu’s strappy patent-leather slingbacks with tiny buckles.

She shaded her eyes with skinny black sunglasses, while another Miu Miu staple—the mini Arcadie bag in cherry-red crocodile-embossed calfskin—infused the monochrome look with a welcome pop of color.

Another great part of her look? Her arm candy, beau Lucas Bravo, who was stylish in his own all-black outfit. A classic black T-shirt was layered under a zip-up hoodie and paired with loose trousers. Brown leather loafers broke up the inky color scheme, while chunky black sunglasses with red-orange lenses mirrored James’s pop of red.

The actors began dating just this summer, but as they’re already coordinating looks, we’ll bet the romance is going well.
