Inter joy at good injury news for Lautaro Martinez, Thuram and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

There is good news for Inter, as Lautaro Martinez and Marcus Thuram returned to training, while Manuel Akanji and Piotr Zielinski also shrugged off issues on international duty.

The Nerazzurri have some big games coming up, as at the weekend they face Atalanta in Serie A, then a must-win match with VfB Stuttgart in the Champions League.

Coach Cristian Chivu is therefore eager to have as many options as possible to call upon for squad rotation.

According to Sky Sport Italia, both Lautaro Martinez and Thuram were back in training today along with the rest of the group.

This was the first session back for the Argentina striker, who has been out of action for over a month with a thigh strain that forced him to miss seven games.

Thuram also had to withdraw from Nations League duty for France with a muscular issue, but he has recovered.

Zielinski and Akanji sustained minor problems playing for Poland and Switzerland respectively, although Sky Sport Italia assure the news from their camps is reassuring for both players.

However, Ange-Yoan Bonny and Aleksandar Stankovic are still on the treatment table and won’t be back for a couple more weeks.
