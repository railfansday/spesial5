Liverpool midfielder Dominik Szoboszlai has come under criticism after footage emerged of his frustrated reaction towards teammate Kostas Tsimikas during Wednesday’s 3-2 Champions League defeat against Fenerbahce.

The Hungarian international was visibly annoyed during a second-half corner-kick situation, with cameras capturing him throwing his hands into the air before appearing to complain towards manager Andoni Iraola and the Liverpool bench.

The incident happened during the second half. Liverpool won a corner and as Tsimikas went up to take the set-piece, Szoboszlai appeared to become frustrated with the situation.

Cameras captured the Hungarian throwing his arms into the air before turning towards the Liverpool technical area, seemingly directing his complaints at Iraola and the coaching staff.

The footage quickly circulated on social media following Liverpool’s defeat, prompting criticism from some supporters.

Several fans questioned Szoboszlai’s body language, while others drew comparisons with Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes, who has also faced criticism for his consta t frustration during matches.

The latest footage has also brought renewed attention to a previous incident involving Szoboszlai and his Liverpool teammates.

During pre-season, the Hungarian was reportedly involved in a heated exchange with Curtis Jones and Tsimikas.

While on-field disagreements and frustrations are normal, it is important that these are managed to make sure the team bond is not spoiled.

Szoboszlai was deployed in an advanced midfield role as Iraola experimented with a 4-2-3-1 formation.

Goalscorer Trey Nyoni and Alexis Mac Allister operated as the two deeper midfielders, allowing the Hungarian to play further forward.

However, Szoboszlai struggled to make a meaningful impact as Liverpool surrendered the lead twice before eventually losing 3-2.
