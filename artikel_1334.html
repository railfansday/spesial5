Football Italia Show: Conte desperate for Juventus return, Tresoldi or Endrick for Milan?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Lorenzo Bettoni and Giancarlo Rinaldi return with a new episode of the Football Italia Show, discussing the never-ending speculation of Antonio Conte’s potential return to Juventus and a transfer dilemma Milan will face in the January window.

Today, the duo discuss Antonio Conte’s recent interview, analysing his comments and his evident desire to return to Juventus. Could the former Bianconeri coach be heading back to Turin, and what would such a move mean for the club’s future?

Attention also turns to Milan and their January transfer plans, with the Rossoneri facing an intriguing dilemma over two potential attacking targets.

Should Milan pursue Nicolò Tresoldi or make a move for Brazilian talent Endrick? Both players could offer different qualities to the team, but which would be the ideal signing for Ruben Amorim’s side

Lorenzo and Giancarlo break down the options, explore the latest rumours and debate who could make the biggest impact at San Siro in the coming months.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
