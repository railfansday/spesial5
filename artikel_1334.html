Forget Mount: Matheus Cunha is Man Utd Heir to Bruno Fernandes

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Speculation around the future of Bruno Fernandes has been an enduring theme of the chaos at Manchester United in recent seasons, but the club have an obvious replacement in-house already.

The Portuguese captain's current contract expires at the end of the season, although the club are believed to hold a one-year option they can trigger to prevent him from leaving for free.

But either way, he is 32 and his criticism of Michael Carrick, as the Englishman's reign at Old Trafford came crashing down, makes it unclear what the short-term outlook is, let alone the longer-term.

Prior to last season, Fernandes rejected a lucrative move to the Saudi Premier League to continue playing in the Premier League, but he is now said to be unhappy with how the season has fallen apart and has yet to agree a new deal despite being in extended talks with the club.

The midfielder can't be more unhappy than most of the fanbase and Mauricio Pochettino's arrival may alter the landscape entirely, but the star man's status is far from secure.

Losing a player of Fernandes' quality would be a blow to most sides, even if his demeanor at times has been called into question, but for those waiting in the wings to take up the role of United's key man it would be a major opportunity.

Given the clamour for the club to make a splash and to provide the sort of immediate external boost that his own arrival from Sporting did nearly seven years ago it is very possible that a big-money signing is eventually made to fill the gap.

Indeed, The Sun report that Como's Nico Paz is in the club's sights for that very reason, with Fernandes in line to be sold or even released thanks to his downturn in form and public criticism this season.

But with full-back, striker, and probably centre-back already high on the list of areas to strengthen in the transfer market it would be extremely helpful if United could find someone to deliver Fernandes' usual level of production from among the current squad.

Step forward Mason Mount as a goalscoring midfielder, entering his prime years, with experience as a leading light for a Premier League giant, and who already cost a hefty fee when he arrived from Chelsea in a £60 million deal in the summer of 2023.

The England international has been a major disappointment since swapping blue for red, but could completely alter his story at Old Trafford if he slipped into the Fernandes role and finally came good.

However, after three and a half years and numerous injury setbacks it seems far more likely that Mount would, and probably should, be overlooked in favour of a more recent arrival who has already surpassed him.

Matheus Cunha cost United £62.5m from Wolves less than a year and a half ago, but has already contributed more goals and assists, while he is rapidly gaining in the appearance charts already.

The Brazilian has largely featured off the left or led the attack in Manchester but frequently operated as a central attacking midfielder or a second striker for Wolves.

He is only four months Mount's junior, and with a cleaner injury record the in-house solution to a Fernandes departure is surely Cunha, especially if United back Pochettino in the transfer market to sign an alternative to Benjamin Sesko up front.

The incoming manager could do far worse than asking his bosses to properly address the central striking options while priming Brazil international Cunha to be his main man at number 10.

Goals and an ability to win soft penalties have already gone a long way to filling in for the captain's eventual exit, and more opportunities at that sport would likely allow him to make the position his own.
