Today’s Papers – Inter call Calhanoglu, Rome’s anti-Chivu pair

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Inter call Calhanoglu: ‘Sign here’ Inter want to tie down Hakan Calhanoglu before the end of the year. The 32-year-old’s contract expires in June, and the Turkish midfielder is now ready to stay at the Nerazzurri.

We’re the land of goals There have been 342 goals in 11 rounds of Serie A, 98 more than at the same stage last season. That’s more than three per game, and goalless draws have all but disappeared. Roma are breaking records, and their 31 goals are the most in the division, with Donyell Malen leading the way.

What an idea: Cisse with Pio Roberto Mancini is considering a Milan-flavoured Italy at San Siro for Thursday’s game against France, pairing 20-year-old Cisse with 21-year-old Francesco Pio Esposito up front.

Sørloth wants Juve Talks have reopened over Alexander Sørloth. Juventus already liked the Norwegian striker in June.

Calò: ‘At 29, I can still make the national team’ Frosinone’s Giacomo Calò still believes an Italy call-up is possible despite being left out of Mancini’s latest squad.

The anti-Chivu pair For the first time ever, two teams from the same city are unbeaten after 11 games of a Serie A season, and Lazio and Roma are now challenging Inter for the title. Gennaro Gattuso’s Lazio are top with 29 points, a better start than Lazio managed under Tommaso Maestrelli or Sven-Göran Eriksson. Gian Piero Gasperini’s Roma are third, two points behind, and their 31 goals are the best tally in 65 years. Gattuso has three problems in Josip Šutalo, Nuno Tavares and Mattia Zaccagni, while Gasperini gets Mile Svilar, Paulo Dybala and Gianluca Mancini back.

Juve, zero penalties Juventus haven’t been given a single penalty in 11 games. Roma haven’t had one either among the top sides. Como have had one, Inter and Milan two each, and Lazio three. Kenan Yıldız is getting close to a return (“I’ll be back in December”), but Pierre Kalulu is out for a month with an injury.

Napoli’s first two targets: Varela and Gittens Gustavo Varela and Jamie Gittens top Napoli’s shopping list. Monza’s Varela is Serie A’s top scorer, and Gittens is Chelsea’s English winger. Both are still young.

Cisse has taken over Milan in 90 days Alphadjo Cissè has scored eight goals in 13 games since arriving at Milan. Among Rossoneri under-20s, only Alexandre Pato has made a better start.

Sassuolo sack Aquilani and bring in D’Aversa Alberto Aquilani has been dismissed after one point from the last seven games and 29 goals conceded. Former Torino coach Roberto D’Aversa takes over to lead the relegation fight.

Mancini on the attack: ‘What a poor showing from AIC and AIAC’ The Italy coach distances himself from the players’ and coaches’ associations, which sided against Giovanni Malagò. Meanwhile, Adriano Galliani is appealing over the FIGC commission.

Once upon a time there was a big United From Sir Alex Ferguson to Michael Carrick, Manchester United’s decline began in 2013. They are now third from bottom in the Premier League.

Yıldız: ‘Juve, I’m coming’ Kenan Yıldız says he’ll be back in December and misses his teammates badly. He received the Bulgarelli Award in Bologna and confirmed that his recovery is going well at the Continassa, where his training will now step up. “I feel good, and I’m very nostalgic,” he said. Fabio Capello calls him “the Italian Lamine Yamal”. Luciano Spalletti called in his staff and the players still in Turin at 8am to study the team’s mistakes. Kalulu has a muscle problem and could be back for Como.

Cubarsí Golden Boy: ‘I won’t stop here’ Barcelona’s World Cup-winning defender Pau Cubarsí is the 24th winner of Tuttosport’s award for the best under-21 player in Europe, and he will be presented with it in Turin on 14 December. Juventus’ Kerim Alajbegović challenged him in the online fans’ vote.

Mancini to Tresoldi: ‘Make the right choice’ Italy are battling Germany for Club Brugge striker Nicolò Tresoldi. “There used to be blocks of players from Juve, Inter and Milan. Now we have to go out and find players,” Mancini said.

Toro cling to Mandragora Without Rolando Mandragora, Torino have lost twice and drawn 1-1 with Lecce. The midfielder is ready to return after the international break, and Ignazio Abate will have his playmaker back.

Sassuolo sack Aquilani and bring in D’Aversa Sassuolo have chosen former Torino coach Roberto D’Aversa to replace Alberto Aquilani.
