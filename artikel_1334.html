Cardinale: ‘I still expect Milan to compete for the Scudetto’

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Gerry Cardinale says he still expects Milan to compete for the Scudetto. He also warned that the Rossoneri will only make a January signing if it’s one that genuinely makes a difference.

With quotes via MilanNews, the Milan owner spoke to Michael Cuomo of TeleLombardia after Sunday evening’s 5-1 win over Frosinone at San Siro. Adrien Rabiot set up two goals as five different players got on the scoresheet. The result lifted Ruben Amorim’s side to fourth on 27 points, three behind Roma and five off leaders Lazio.

“Well, I’m never satisfied, but the results speak for themselves. The boys were fantastic tonight,” Cardinale said.

“I hope you’re happy. I hope the fans are happy. I think the team is really coming together. It’s one game, so we keep the enthusiasm in check, but I’m really proud of the boys. This is a team, and it’s a team that wants to win right to the end.”

“Look, it’s November. We still have a long way to go, but we want to see improvements in every game. This weekend has been very interesting if you look at the results. You can see something is changing in Serie A.

“I was very happy to see us not only win, but win by that margin. We have to build on it. As Amorim says, every game is something to build on.

“Personally, I still expect us to compete for the Scudetto, but it’s a long way off. One game at a time. Now the focus is on the Europa League, and as Ruben said, we have to do both. European competitions are important, and everything is a test. If you want to win a Scudetto, you have to go all the way, and that’s what we’ll do.”

Milan now travel to Olympiacos on Thursday, with kick-off at 18:45 CET [17:45 GMT]. Amorim’s side have lost their last two league-phase games, against Bournemouth and Ferencváros, so there is little margin for error in Athens.

Cardinale was also asked whether a new player could arrive in January.

“Again, January is just another stage. As you know, the January market is complicated, so you have to be disciplined. You can’t just throw yourself at something at random.

“The one thing that will happen under my ownership is that whenever we enter the transfer market, we will play to win. That’s the theme I set out at the start of this year. So if we make a move in any window, January or June, it will be a move that counts.”
