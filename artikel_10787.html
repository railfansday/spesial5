GTA 6 running at 30 FPS “makes sense” experts say after Rockstar’s confirmation

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Digital Foundry says that Rockstar Games would be “asking for trouble” in trying to run GTA 6 at anything other than 30 FPS after the feature was confirmed.

In just a matter of hours, Grand Theft Auto fans will finally get to boot up GTA 6 and experience Rockstar Games’ long-awaited game. As the day draws nearer, though, there has been plenty of attention on how the game looks.

As part of their preload trailer, Rockstar confirmed that GTA 6 will run at 30 FPS. This has been the case for their games at launch, but it still disappointed some fans who wanted a boost to 60 FPS or 40 FPS after such a long wait for the game.

It was also a talking point for Digital Foundry during episode 290 of their DF Direct Weekly, as Richard Leadbetter explained why he believes Rockstar couldn’t scale things up.

“There’s no comments on whether QSSR will be on PlayStation 5, so we’re just going to have to wait and see what happens there,” he said, noting that the “world was crying out” for the PS5 Pro features.

“You’ve got to consider the frame time budgets. At 30 FPS, they’ve got 33.3 milliseconds to process the game world, the simulation, all of that stuff and thats a fair amount of time but they’ve decided from the design perspective that they need it.

“Now, 60 FPS, you’ve got half that. You’ve got to do the same job in half the amount of time. It’s not viable. The obvious thing is to accept that and say, what about 40 FPS? It’s only 10 FPS more than 30, but that’s not the way it works.”

Leadbetter noted that even at 40 FPS there would be a “huge amount of optimization or cutbacks” to hit the 25 millisecond frame time.

Digital Foundry have long been skeptical about GTA 6 running at 60 FPS too, previously saying it felt “like a bridge too far.”
