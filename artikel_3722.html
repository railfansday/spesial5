The wait for GTA 6 was undeniably long, with 13 years passing since Rockstar's last Grand Theft Auto entry dropped and its new behemoth – but fans aren't the only ones who felt the wait… those behind the game did, too.

Especially co-protagonist Lucia's own actor, Manni L. Perez, who says she's worked on Grand Theft Auto 6 for nearly ten years now – the majority of those 13 years following GTA 5. "Life has been such a whirlwind these past couple of days and I just want to take a moment to say thank you," she writes on Instagram. "Thank you to all the new followers, to all the beautiful messages, and the positive reception the game and my character have received."

She then recalls the amount of time she's spent in Lucia's shoes: "I've dedicated almost a decade to bringing Lucia to life. She's been such a significant part of mine and I am beyond grateful she now gets to be a small part of yours." That's one heck of a role, then, considering the amount of work Perez has evidently poured into it – and I'd say it's more than paid off. As it stands, GTA 6 continues to smash records as the highest-rated game of all time.

It's also already proven "the biggest launch in PlayStation history" (with Sony reporting "more than 39 million unique players" playing on just the PS5). Phew. Jason's own actor, Dylan Rourke, has posted on his profile as well, seemingly celebrating his time with Rockstar. "Thankful for this wild ride," Rourke's story reads. "Gratitude won't cut it. Make today about the ones you love. Family."

Wild ride, indeed… and I'd say it's far from over. The community is still eagerly awaiting a PC release (and no, I'm not talking about the unofficial ports in progress), and, of course, the hotly anticipated Online counterpart. Rockstar has yet to announce either, though, but both are sure to propel GTA 6 further into relentless popularity – if you can fathom it becoming any bigger than it is now.
