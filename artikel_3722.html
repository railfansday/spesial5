Grand Theft Auto 6 pre-load begins on 10th November, file size and regional timings released

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Rockstar Games have revealed the pre-load date and time for Grand Theft Auto 6. You better be prepared to clear out some storage space, and burn the midnight oil.

Per the official Rockstar Games website, Grand Theft Auto 6 will be pre-loadable starting on the 10th November. On PlayStation 5, it'll be available starting at midnight in your local time, but on the Xbox Series X/S, it'll be 12am GMT on the 10th November. In total, you'll need 212GB free on your console in order to download it. So it's probably a good idea to say your fond farewells to any beloved single player games or tried-and-true live service titles sitting on your consoles.

On the Rockstar Games support site, detailed instructions on how to pre-load GTA 6 on both Xbox and PlayStation are present. On the PlayStation Store you'll need to find the game on the digital store and pre-order the game. On the Xbox Series X/S, the same is true. Simply navigate to the store and pre-order it to proceed with the download.
