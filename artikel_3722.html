All Cloudreach Falls Aniimo locations

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Whisperwake Isles region has opened up in Aniimo, and by exploring this vast land, you can find and collect all kinds of new creatures. One of the areas you must traverse is Cloudreach Falls, which has quite a collection of Aniimo for you to find.

In each unique area you can explore, tracking down all of the Aniimo who call the region home can be tough, as they’ve all got only a few specific spawn locations, and many of them can only be found under special conditions. If you’re struggling to find any of these creatures, here are the locations of all Aniimo found around the Cloudfall Reach region in Aniimo.

In total, there are 14 unique Aniimo you can encounter around Cloudreach Falls. Some of them are exclusive to this region, while others can be found around a few different areas in Aniimo.
