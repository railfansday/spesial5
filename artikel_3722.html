The biggest game of the year and the biggest superhero team-up of the year collide as Marvel's Shang-Chi star Simu Liu uses GTA 6 to promote Avengers: Doomsday.

Over on Twitter, Liu shared a mock screengrab of the new game on Twitter, which reads, "Lucia will appreciate this: Buy tickets for Avengers: Doomsday." Well, we better do what she says and preorder tickets right now. Check out the post below.

There is some confusion as to whether this is a real screenshot. However, it looks like the graphic was made by artist Rafael Caban, who is taking a break from Doomsday art to play GTA 6. The mock-up replicates optional tasks that players can choose to complete when they play as Jason. Completed tasks boost your relationship rating with Lucia. The way your relationship pans out affects the overall story.

It wouldn't be that out of the ordinary to see a Doomsday Easter egg in the new game, as fans have already found a bunch of nods to a number of TV shows and movies in GTA 6. This includes Dexter's Miami apartment from the hit Showtime crime series, as well as references to Game of Thrones author George R.R. Martin. As for Marvel, the game includes an X-Men comic and a figure who looks a lot like the Hulk.

However, we don't think Marvel fans need a reminder from Lucia to buy tickets for the upcoming Marvel Phase 6 movie. Presales crossed $50 million at the start of September, 100 days before release. Some reports now suggest that the movie has crossed $120 million, but Marvel Studios has yet to confirm.

Doomsday is set to follow "courageous teams from three universes" who must band together to face Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom. The synopsis continues, "Their choices and actions in this crucial moment will lay the foundation for the future of the Multiverse." Alongside Liu, the movie also stars Chris Hemsworth's Thor, Chris Evans' Steve Rogers, the Thunderbolts, a bunch of the original Fox-era X-Men, the Wakandans, and more,

Avengers: Doomsday hits cinemas on December 18, and December 16 in the UK. For more, check out our guide to the Marvel timeline, and keep up with upcoming Marvel movies and shows on the way.
