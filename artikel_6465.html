Khanom Tom (Coconut Balls)

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The first time writer Ali Domrongchai went to Thailand with her grandmother, they had a mission: eat as many khanom (the Thai word for desserts and small snacks) as possible. They wandered from market to market, weaving through the stalls and sharing everything that caught their eye. Their hands-down favorite was the khanom tom, which her grandmother grew up buying from a street vendor in Bangkok as an after-school treat. Traditionally made with both fresh coconut and coconut milk, the bite-size sweets are chewy, fragrant, and irresistible.

Thankfully, khanom tom are easy to pull together at home and require no special equipment or technique. Glutinous rice flour gives the dough a pleasantly bouncy texture, and a palm sugar and coconut filling imparts a rich, caramel-like sweetness. After a quick boil, each ball is rolled in shredded fresh coconut for a soft, snowy finish.
