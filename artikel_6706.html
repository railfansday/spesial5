The Best Eye Makeup to Wear Over 40, From Cream Shadows to Soft Liners

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Soft browns and subtle shimmer can define eyes without emphasizing fine lines.

Let’s be clear: there are no age-specific makeup rules. Wear whatever shade or trend you feel best in, no matter your age. That said, it’s no secret that our facial features change as we get older, and to keep looking your best, the skin care and makeup products in your rotation may need to be adjusted accordingly. After 40, that powder eye shadow that once looked perfect across a smooth eyelid may now crease and settle into fine lines. Or your signature eyeliner may have started to look a little less visible because of eyelid hooding. Maybe your lashes are more sparse? Before dumping your entire eye makeup kit, take some cues from a pro on choosing and wearing the best eye makeup after 40.

Before choosing colors, take a look at your current products to assess textures. “As we get older, the skin on our eyelids gets thinner, and a little more crinkled, so heavy or dry powder shadows can settle into fine lines and actually emphasize them,” celebrity makeup artist Brandy Allen tells ELLE. You’ll want to swap out chalky, dry formats for cream-based shadows and liners.

How you treat the skin in your eye area matters too—both for how well and how long the makeup wears. Allen recommends using an eye cream or eye patches to hydrate the skin. “I have been loving the Parivie Smooth & Brighten Eye Masks because of the collagen and skin-loving ingredients they have,” says Allen. “Give the leftover serum a minute to sink in, and then go in with a thin layer of primer like the Urban Decay Eyeshadow Primer Potion so your shadow has something to grip onto and stays crease-free all day,” Allen suggests.

Many hybrid products now combine skin care with color cosmetics. A win-win for aging skin. Look for hydrating ingredients like hyaluronic acid, smoothing niacinamide, ceramides, and squalene that will treat skin while delivering solid color payoff.

Look for creamy formulas that blend easily like the M.A.C Dazzleshadow Eye Shadow Stick. These will crease less. “It has a soft metallic shine and 12-hour wear, and it doubles as a liner, so it’s an easy one-step product,” Allen notes.

Satin and soft-sheen finishes are the best here, too. Allen says they give the lid a little luminosity that reflects light, which makes the eye area look more lifted and fresh. “A finely milled shimmer on the center of the lid is great too, especially for the holidays. If you prefer a powder, I love the Jones Road The Best Eyeshadow. It has a soft texture that blends out so easily, and it comes in both matte and shimmer finishes.”

Allen says soft, warm neutrals like taupes, espresso and chocolate browns, bronze, and soft rose gold tones are most flattering for 40 and over eyes. “I’d also recommend swapping a jet-black liner for a deep brown. Brown still defines the eye but looks softer and less harsh against the skin.” Allen’s top pick is the Glampton Cream Eyeshadow & Gel Liner Duo in Espresso & Rose Gold, which she says is a great option here because it gives you both shades in one. “The shadow stick is creaseless, blends out with just your finger, and the gel liner glides on without tugging on the lid,” Allen notes.

Just because your skin is maturing doesn’t mean you can’t try trends. Allen says it’s more about adjusting the trend to work for you. “The lived-in, smudged smoky eye trend is actually great for more mature skin; just keep it soft, and focus the liner on the outer half of the lower lash line instead of going all the way around,” says Allen. One product she suggests avoiding is chunky glitter, as it can settle into lines and emphasize texture.

Once your products are in place, perfect your application technique. It’ll make a difference. “When blending shadow, angle it upward toward the tail of your brow instead of dragging it down. This gives the eye an instant lift,” Allen instructs. You can also tap a little rose gold or champagne shimmer onto the inner corners and center of the lid to brighten. And don’t forget the under-eyes and brows. “A color corrector like the Bobbi Brown Skin Long-Wearing Color Corrector Stick cancels out darkness, and well-groomed, filled-in brows frame the whole eye area,” Allen explains.
