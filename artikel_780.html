The lineup includes the Twilight Saga Classic Clog, priced at $74.99, and the Classic Platform Clog for $89.99. Fans can also personalize their pairs with themed Jibbitz charms, including a Team Edward design.

Of course, Twilight has always had a special place in music history. When the original soundtrack arrived in November 2008, it debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 with approximately 165,000 copies sold during its first week. The achievement made it the first soundtrack since 8 Mile in 2002 to top the chart before its film premiered.

The album also gave fans some unforgettable musical moments, from Paramore’s “Decode” to Muse’s “Supermassive Black Hole.” It remained on the Billboard 200 for 48 consecutive weeks before returning to No. 3 in 2009, following a deluxe edition release and the movie’s DVD arrival.

The collection is available through the official Crocs website, with select styles and accessories returning as part of a limited restock.

There’s no telling how long the fan-favorite designs will stick around, so shoppers hoping to secure a pair shouldn’t wait too long. After all, you can always rewatch Twilight for the hundredth time. Getting another shot at these Crocs? That’s a little less certain.
