Miley Cyrus’ Wedding Dress Revealed: Details on Her Backless Gown

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Miley Cyrus wore a stunning long-sleeved gown for her and Maxx Morando’s Palm Springs wedding, where she said "I do" in front of mom Tish Cyrus, dad Billy Ray Cyrus and more.

Miley Cyrus’ wedding dress is one in a million.

The Hannah Montana alum tied the knot with Maxx Morando in Palm Springs, Calif., Nov. 21 after five years of dating. And while the ceremony was an emotional affair for everyone in attendance, it was Miley’s dress that stole the show like a wrecking ball.

The 33-year-old was a vision in white as she donned a long-sleeved, backless gown designed by Saint Laurent by Anthony Vaccarello to exchange vows with Maxx, according to photos of the newlyweds, shot by photographer Jose Villa, which Miley shared on Instagram Nov. 22.

To really allow the most-wanted gown to shine, the 33-year-old wore her hair in a tousled up-do.

And it’s clear the nuptials were something beautiful as photos from the ceremony showed Miley wiping away tears as she stood in front of the Liily drummer, per photos obtained by DeuxMoi.

As for the loved ones who watched their butterflies fly away to a new chapter, Miley’s mom Tish Cyrus and stepdad Dominic Purcell were just two family members who witnessed Miley and Maxx throw down their vows.

Additionally, her sisters Brandi Cyrus and Noah Cyrus watched on as the “Flowers” singer proved her heart beats for love. Plus, Miley’s father Billy Ray Cyrus and his partner Elizabeth Hurley did the same alongside Elizabeth's son Damien Hurley.

One night before the ceremony, Miley’s stepsister Lily-Rose Purcell shared special details from the couple’s rehearsal dinner Nov. 20.

In fact, she shared a close-up of the event’s dinner menu on Instagram Stories, which included a main course of filet mignon and Maine lobster ravioli, followed by a dessert selection of chocolate caramel layer cake, vanilla crème brûlée and raspberry sorbet.

But the breakout detail had to be the “M+M” printed at the top of the menu—a nod to the couple’s first initials.

Maxx, 28, and Miley began dating in 2021 after a mutual friend set them up on a blind date. Four years later, the pair confirmed their engagement in December 2025 after the “Toro” musician popped the question during a vacation in Japan.

"I’m not totally the boss in our house," Miley told Zane Lowe in an interview shared by Apple Music Sept. 17. "It's like a sense of relief that I can actually soften into, because he’s going to watch this and be like, 'You’re not the boss. We’re both the boss.'"

And it’s that playful banter that she’s especially excited to bring into their marriage.

As she put it, "I think that’s the thing that maybe generations prior didn’t teach us, that marriage doesn’t need to be a prison. It can actually be a playground."
