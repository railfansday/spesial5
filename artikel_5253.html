Bitcoin's recent sell-off has raised questions about whether investors are selling liquid crypto holdings to secure allocations in Anthropic's massive IPO.

Anthropic's offering has attracted strong demand, with its order book reportedly reaching six times the shares available during the roadshow.

Fundstrat's Tom Lee and Xapo Bank's Seamus Rocca have pointed to the IPO as a potential source of capital rotation, although direct evidence of Bitcoin whale selling to fund the offering remains limited.

Bitcoin (BTC) is facing renewed selling pressure as Anthropic’s massive IPO draws billions of dollars in investor demand, raising a new question across crypto markets: are investors selling Bitcoin to get into artificial intelligence? BTC has fallen sharply from its October highs, while Anthropic’s upcoming Nasdaq debut is attracting significant interest from institutional and retail investors. Some market strategists believe the IPO could be pulling liquidity away from cryptocurrencies as investors prepare cash for the offering. Is Anthropic’s IPO becoming an unexpected competitor for Bitcoin investment capital?

Anthropic’s upcoming IPO is emerging as a major liquidity event at a time when Bitcoin is struggling to regain investor demand. The company’s offering has reportedly attracted orders worth six times the available shares by the end of its roadshow’s first week. Anthropic is targeting a valuation of approximately $1.3 trillion and could raise more than $61 billion, making it one of the largest public offerings in market history. Its shares are expected to trade on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT.

$BTC looks like another source of funding for $ANT IPO based on its price action.

Investors seeking allocations in a high-profile IPO may need to free up cash from existing holdings. Bitcoin is among the most liquid assets that investors can sell quickly to raise capital. Seamus Rocca, CEO of Xapo Bank, has suggested that investors preparing for Anthropic’s offering may be selling Bitcoin and other liquid assets to secure IPO allocations. He described the offering as a potential source of liquidity pressure on crypto markets.

The possibility of capital moving from crypto into AI stocks has also attracted attention from Fundstrat’s Tom Lee. Lee suggested that retail investors could be selling cryptocurrencies to fund purchases of Anthropic shares ahead of its Nasdaq debut. He also warned that the IPO could create a supply shock across equities as investors liquidate existing positions to participate in the offering.

His comments come as Bitcoin faces broader selling pressure despite continued interest in its long-term investment case. Lee also pointed to weakness in technology stocks and margin calls among retail traders as possible contributors to the recent market decline.

The argument is straightforward: when a major IPO attracts exceptional demand, investors may shift money from existing positions into the new opportunity. Bitcoin’s liquidity makes it a potential source of that capital.

Bitcoin’s decline has intensified as several sources of market pressure converge. BTC recently dropped toward $65,000, extending its correction by more than 20% from early October levels. Anthropic’s IPO is one possible explanation for the liquidity shift, but it is not the only factor. Technology-stock volatility, concerns surrounding Strategy’s market position, rising short positions and outflows from BlackRock’s Bitcoin ETF have also weighed on market conditions.

Anthropic’s IPO could create short-term competition for investment capital, particularly if demand continues to exceed the available share supply. Once the offering is completed, some of that temporary pressure could ease. However, a recovery in Bitcoin is not guaranteed. Investors will continue to monitor ETF flows, derivatives positioning, macroeconomic conditions and demand for digital assets.

The larger question is whether Anthropic represents a temporary liquidity drain or a broader shift in investor preferences toward AI-related equities. If Bitcoin continues losing capital while Anthropic attracts record demand, the market may be witnessing more than a routine crypto correction. It could be an early sign of investors changing where they want their money invested.
