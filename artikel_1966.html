Rangers hero makes ‘ruthless killer’ forward plea as Celtic pressure builds

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Former Rangers striker Kris Boyd would like the club’s current forwards to develop a “ruthless” streak as he claimed they were in the ascendancy in the title race.

Ryan Naderi took his tally for the season to nine goals in Rangers’ 2-0 win over St Mirren on Saturday while Kevin Kelsy has struck five times in 10 appearances.

But with attacking midfielder Kim Min-su still to get off the mark and winger Badredine Bouanani on one goal, Boyd feels Rangers can step up another level.

“Rangers could quite easily have won by four or five on Saturday night,” said Boyd, who struck 300 career goals.

“I don’t quite know what it is because you look at Bouanani and Kim Min-su and you feel as if there are players in there who are contributing.

“But I think in the modern game you’re looking and saying, can their numbers increase? Of course they can.

“When you look at Naderi at the weekend, he takes his penalty superbly well, but he could quite easily have walked off with the match ball.

“Kevin Kelsy is a threat and he’s shown that over the period of time he’s been here.

Kevin Kelsy celebrates with Ryan Naderi (Image: Stuart Wallace / Shutterstock)

“Rangers are creating chances, but I don’t see that ruthless killer at the top end of the pitch that is obsessed with scoring goals and that’s all they want to do. Maybe it’s just me, I’m not sure there are many of them in the modern game right now.

“But I would like to see somebody step forward and be that man who wants to go and score every single week, wants to get a second, get a third and get a fourth, because there are chances being created.

“I don’t think you can knock what the two strikers have done because when Derek (McInnes) left Kelsy out at Dundee, Rangers didn’t look the same team. I thought they struggled at Dundee for large parts.

“So you can’t criticise because I think both of their contributions have been very good. With (Youseff) Chermiti and (Lawrence) Shankland out, it’s important they continue that.”

Rangers are four points adrift of William Hill Premiership leaders Celtic but they have a game in hand.

Boyd said: “The start that Rangers had wasn’t great, but they’ve managed to correct that and they’re definitely a team that’s in form and don’t look as if they’re going to lose games of football.

“Rangers have had a lot of tough away games and you’d expect them to keep this run going. Rangers do seem as if they’re the team in the ascendancy and they’ve got that belief behind them.

“Whereas with Celtic, they’re winning games of football, maybe not convincingly, but you feel as if there’s just a crisis around the corner. It seems to be that with any dip, it’s a full-blown attack on the board, a full-blown attack on the manager and then the players get it.”
