Brighton XI at Hull City confirmed as Fabian Hurzeler makes huge call on £40m striker

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Brighton boss Fabian Hurzeler delivered a selection curve-ball ahead of the Premier League clash at Hull City.

Striker Georginio Rutter makes just his second start of the season in place of Greek forward Charalampos Kostoulas.

Georginio's only previous start was on the opening day of the season in the 4-0 win against Aston Villa at the Amex Stadium.

The £40m striker then suffered a foot injury and missed four matches in all competitions. Kostoulas then performed well in Georginio's absence and Greek striker has five goals and four assists this term.

Georginio's inclusion from the off at the MKM Stadium is a surprise for Brighton fans but Hurzeler may have felt Georginio is slightly fresher after the international break.

Aside from that, there a few surprises in Brighton's line-up as Hurzeler's second placed team look to close the gap on leaders Arsenal.

Dutch keeper Bart Verbruggen - fresh from conceding five goals for The Netherlands against Germany during the international break - keeps his place between the sticks.

Costinha starts once again at right back despite the return to fitness of Ferdi Kadioglu who remains on the bench.

Young Croatian Luka Vuskovic and skipper Lewis Dunk continue their impressive partnership at the heart of the defence, with the ever-present Olivier Boscagli at left back.

German midfielder Pascal Gross signed a new contract on Friday and keeps up his record of starting every match in all competitions this term.

He's joined in midfield by young Spaniard Chema Andres who has shrugged off an injury concern that saw him withdraw from the Spain under-21 squad.

Diego Gomez continues on the right of the midfield with the fit again Yankuba Minteh on the bench. Yasin Ayari is expected to be the more advanced of the midfielders and operate in the No 10 role.

Maxim De Cuyper will hope to continue his impressive form on the left wing and help supply the ammunition for Georginio, who leads the line.

Hull: Tzolakis, Coyle, Ajayi, Mendy, Stroud, Belloumi, Crooks, Slater, Iroegbunam, Cho, McBurnie.

Brighton: Verbruggen, Costinha, Vuskovic, Dunk, Boscagli, Gross, Chema Andres, Gomez, Ayari, De Cuyper, Rutter. Substitutes: Steele, Hinshelwood, Minteh, Osman, Kostoulas, Kadioglu, Wieffer, Svoboda, O'Riley.
