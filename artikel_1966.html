Gallery: ‘Derby chills’, ‘Pulisic is the Inter killer’ – Today’s front pages in Italy

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The big day is here! Milan-Inter is scheduled for this evening and the newspaper certainly made sure to give the game a lot of coverage today.

Due to the defeat against Bologna mid-week, the hype hasn’t been as big as perhaps expected ahead of this derby. Milan could have been just four points behind Inter, but right now, the distance is seven points and could thus be increased to ten points.
