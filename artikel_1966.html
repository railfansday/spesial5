Zirkzee refuses to blame referee after Manchester United’s Sporting draw

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

He also writes about Manchester City for Read Man City and motorsport for Read Motorsport. A former accountant, he became a freelance writer in 2011 and has written for BBC Sport and Metro.

Joshua Zirkzee believes Manchester United should take responsibility for their failure to beat Sporting on Wednesday rather than blame referee Szymon Marciniak.

Mauricio Pochettino’s side had two goals disallowed during the 2-2 Champions League draw in Lisbon. Bruno Fernandes scored twice against his former club early in the second half to put United ahead. However, an error by goalkeeper Senne Lammens allowed Luis Suarez to equalise with 10 minutes remaining.

“We shouldn’t look at the referee, we should look at ourselves instead,” Zirkzee told MUTV. Although frustrated by some of Marciniak’s decisions, the Dutchman felt United had sufficient quality to win the game regardless.

Wednesday’s appearance was Zirkzee’s first start for United since May’s goalless draw at Sunderland. The forward benefited from Matheus Cunha’s European suspension and received considerable vocal support from the travelling fans in Lisbon.

Zirkzee was replaced after 64 minutes, with Bryan Mbeumo moving into the centre-forward position. With Cunha available again for Saturday’s visit of Brentford, Pochettino has a decision to make over the composition of his attack.

United’s improvement in Lisbon was evident, particularly during the opening stages of the second half. However, their inability to protect a winning position ultimately proved more costly than any decision made by Marciniak.

Pochettino is still awaiting his first victory as United manager, something Zirkzee is clearly anxious to help deliver against Brentford.
