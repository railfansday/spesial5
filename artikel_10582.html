Valorant 13.10 update bring Sentinel buffs and big changes to assists

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Valorant’s 13.10 update is here, with some buffs and nerfs for different Sentinels, as well as a change for in-game assists. So, lets get into the patch notes.

Valorant has undergone some big updates over the last few months, including the arrival of Hush in 13.09, a new rifle in the form of the Warden coming in 13.06, and a raft of Agent ability changes in the 13.00 patch.

Now, with the 13.10 update rolling out, Riot Games has, again, put Sentinels under the microscope with changes coming to a number of those Agents, specifically: Cypher, Killjoy, and Vyse.

Now, these three agents will have a shorter cooldown when it comes to their abilities coming back into play after being destroyed. Cypher’s Spycam, Killjoy’s Turret, and Vyse’s Arc Rose will all take 45 seconds to regenerate.

“We’re making some light changes to Sentinel cooldowns to help encourage more strategic back and forth between teams. This is a small, targeted change while we continue to look at ability cooldowns and Sentinel states across VALORANT more broadly,” Riot said.

We’re decreasing to a shorter cooldown after these abilities are destroyed to see if it gives Sentinels a bit more room to place utility early and reset mid-round, while still rewarding the team that breaks their setup.”

However, the reclaim cooldown on Cypher’s Spycam has been increased from 15 seconds to 20 seconds, bringing it in-line with other Sentinels.

Sage mains will also notice a small change too, with Riot increasing her accuray when targeting allies for healing.

Additionally, healing assists will now only count when a player you’ve healed gets a kill within a 10 second window after the heal effect ends.

Similarly, damage assists have been tweaked, meaning you’ll need to deal at least 10 damage to an enemy within three seconds of your teammate bagging a kill.

The full patch notes for the 13.10 update can be found below.

We’re making some light changes to Sentinel cooldowns to help encourage more strategic back and forth between teams. This is a small, targeted change while we continue to look at ability cooldowns and Sentinel states across VALORANT more broadly. We’re decreasing to a shorter cooldown after these abilities are destroyed to see if it gives Sentinels a bit more room to place utility early and reset mid-round, while still rewarding the team that breaks their setup. This puts these cooldowns slightly below Initiator signature cooldowns (50s). We’ll be watching how it affects the back-and-forth around ability timing, and what we learn will help shape any bigger changes down the line.

We’re also improving Sage’s Healing Orb targeting accuracy to match Miks’ Harmonize, making it easier to not accidentally heal the wrong teammates.

In Patch 13.08, we updated how assists were awarded alongside our Performance Score updates, but missed including them in the patch notes then (whoops!). These changes are intended to more visibly encourage trading, better reward players who contribute to securing kills, and make sure healing assists reflect their impact on a fight.

We’re bringing some fan-favourite bugs back as proper glitches in the draft pool!

We’re also tuning existing upgrades, with the goal to make more choices competitive, giving some abilities more opportunities to shine while reining in a few stronger options.
