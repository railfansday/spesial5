Man Killed Ex-GF and New BF in Murder-Suicide Following History of Abuse

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A heartbroken mother shared details about her daughter’s abusive relationship with her ex-boyfriend after police said he allegedly killed her and her new boyfriend in a horrific murder-suicide.

Verchna Philippe and her boyfriend, Darnell McLean, were fatally shot by her ex Scloiwen Jean-Baptiste while they were driving in Miami Gardens in the early hours of Sunday, November 15. Investigators said that after Jean-Baptiste, 27, pulled up to their vehicle in an Uber, he shot Philippe, 25, and she crashed into a fence. McLean, 25, then tried to run away, but Jean-Baptiste chased him and shot him.

Jean-Baptiste returned to the car and shot Philippe a second time before he died by suicide.

McLean and Jean-Baptiste died at the scene, while Philippe was taken to Aventura Hospital for treatment before she died from her injuries.

“She was my right hand, my everything,” Philippe’s mother, Nadege Jean Pierre, told NBC Miami of her late daughter.

Jean Pierre explained that she had waited all night for her daughter to come home, and she didn’t learn what happened until detectives reported to her home the next morning.

“I asked, ‘Is she dead? What happened to my daughter? Where is Verchna?'” she recalled.

Jean Pierre went on to reflect on her daughter’s relationship with Jean-Baptiste, whom police said had a criminal history that dated back to 2015.

“He is a psychopath; he is a maniac,” Jean Pierre said, claiming that Jean-Baptiste has threatened Philippe in the past. “He was obsessed with my daughter.”

Police said that he threatened to kill her in 2024 and also stabbed her during a 2025 incident, according to the outlet.

Jean Pierre said that the stabbing incident happened when Philippe was getting into an Uber and Jean-Baptiste jumped in behind her. “She tried to take the knife from him, but he refused, and he stabbed my daughter,” she said.

McLean’s mother, who asked to not be publicly named, also opened up about the tragedy while speaking to the outlet. “I don’t have words to say how deep it hurts,” she said. “You took my life when you took my son’s life.”
