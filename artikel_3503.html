2026 CMA Awards Arrivals: Stars Deliver Country Couture on the Red Carpet

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The stars over Nashville aligned as country music’s biggest names showed up and showed out for the 2026 CMA Awards.

Celebrities stepped onto the red carpet at the Bridgestone Arena in Tennessee on Wednesday, November 18, serving up a mix of high-glamour gowns and boots, statement-making suits, dazzling rhinestones and of course, plenty of Southern flair.

In addition to the fashion, fans are in for a treat as Lainey Wilson is back for her third year as host.

“I couldn’t be more excited to be hosting the CMA Awards again this year, especially with it being the 60th anniversary,” she gushed in September. “I’ve had so much fun hosting the last two years, and this one’s going to be extra special. Can’t wait to see y’all!”

Plus, there will be a heartwarming tribute honoring late music icon Dolly Parton.
