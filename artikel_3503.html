Aaron Rodgers’ Ex-Packers Teammate Slams His 'Despicable' Mike McCarthy Remarks

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Aaron Rodgers’ former Green Bay Packers teammate T.J. Lang is calling a flag on the play after the quarterback publicly ridiculed Pittsburgh Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy, claiming he was “unhealthy.”

“I think it’s despicable,” Lang, 39, said during a Wednesday, November 25, appearance on Detroit’s 97.1 The Ticket. “I think that Aaron Rodgers just became everything that he has b***ed about and moaned about over the last 10 years with former coaches, former teammates, airing grievances out, giving opinions out in the media.”

Lang, who played alongside Rodgers on the Packers from 2009 to 2016, claimed that the quarterback’s allegations about McCarthy were “unnecessary,” calling Rodgers’ outburst “victim mentality. It’s a poor me, woe is me.”

Us has reached out to a rep for Rodgers for comment.

On Wednesday, news broke that the Steelers had benched Rodgers, 42, as the starting quarterback ahead of the team’s highly-anticipated matchup against the Denver Broncos on Friday, November 27.

That same day, Rodgers appeared on The Pat McAfee Show and aired his grievances against McCarthy, 63, who was his coach at the Packers before McCarthy became the head coach for the Steelers in 2026, where Rodgers had been playing since 2025.

Rodgers claimed that McCarthy was “unhealthy” after allegedly falling asleep in a meeting and missing a walkthrough.

The four-time NFL MVP also alleged that McCarthy broke the huddle during a recent Steelers game yelling, “One, two, three, Packers!” hinting he didn’t know what team he was working for.

Following the controversial interview, the Steelers announced on Wednesday that Rodgers was “released” from the team.

McCarthy confirmed the news during a Wednesday press conference, sharing that after talking “about some things” Rodgers allegedly gave an “ultimatum” that was “clearly out of his job description” which led to the coach benching his star quarterback.

When asked about Rodgers’ firing, which transpired in the hours after his radio interview, McCarthy told reporters, “Aaron and I have a long history.”

McCarthy noted, “He has a lot of opinions, and those are things we always talked about. Once again, I consider those private conversations.”

Lang, for his part, backed the NFL coach, saying on Wednesday, “Mike McCarthy was my head coach for eight years, and I love Mike McCarthy. And I talked to Mike McCarthy on Monday night, and still have a great relationship with him.”

According to Lang, the same can’t be said about Rodgers, whom he claimed isn’t close with many of his former teammates.

“I haven’t talked to Aaron Rodgers in probably five years,” Lang said. “I can’t remember the last time he returned a text. I don’t have a relationship with him.”

McCarthy was the head coach of the Packers from 2006 to 2018 when he was fired. He helmed the Dallas Cowboys from 2020 to 2024 and became the head coach for the Steelers in 2026.

Rodgers, meanwhile, played for the Packers from 2005 to 2022, the majority of which was coached by McCarthy. Rodgers had a brief stint at the New York Jets from 2023 to 2024 before joining the Steelers in 2025.
