Super Bowl 61: Tracking every ad

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Let Marketing Dive's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

Stay tuned here for the latest marketing moves, sorted by brand, agency and category, as we count down to kickoff on Feb. 14.

Advertiser excitement is already nearing its fever pitch ahead of Super Bowl 61 on Feb. 14, 2027. Disney announced in August that it has sold out of all ad inventory for the game — which for the first time will be played on Valentine’s Day — with a 30-second spot reportedly costing advertisers between $8 million to $9 million.

The big game has attracted new entrants, including Ferrero’s Nutella and Waymo, the provider of driverless robotaxi services owned by Alphabet, along with repeat advertisers including Ritz, Svedka and Cutwater Spirits. The Super Bowl continues to provide advertisers a valuable opportunity to reach consumers, with the 2026 game ranking as the second most-watched Super Bowl in history with an estimated 125.6 million viewers.

Super Bowl 61 will be played at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California and will air on ABC, ESPN and Disney+. The halftime show, which will be headlined by Jay-Z, is sponsored for a fifth consecutive year by Apple Music.

Marketing Dive will keep this tracker updated with all the brand announcements leading up to the big game — check back for the latest on advertising’s biggest night.

Instacart will return to the Super Bowl for a third consecutive year, the company confirmed. The effort will be handled by Instacart’s in-house creative agency, Local Produce, with support from McCann for a second consecutive year.

Instacart advertised in the 2026 Super Bowl with a 30-second spot, “Bananas,” that starred Ben Stiller and Benson Boone and was centered around a fictional Euro-pop duo called Benissimo. The campaign drove an eight-point spike in new user traffic immediately following the spot, according to the brand. Additionally, the effort scored Instacart three Cannes Lions awards.

“We went from zero to Super Bowl in four years, and since then, our big bets have continued to pay off for the business,” said Instacart CMO Laura Jones.

Mondelēz International cracker brand Ritz will return to the Super Bowl with a 30-second ad airing in the second quarter, per details shared with Marketing Dive. The spot, which marks the brand’s third consecutive appearance, was created by agency 72andSunny and produced by Publicis Production. The commercial is meant to introduce a new creative direction for the brand and will use humor to spotlight Ritz Bitz Cracker Sandwiches and Ritz Drizzled Minis Crackers.

Sazerac’s Svedka Vodka will return to the Super Bowl following its debut during the 2026 game, the brand confirmed. The marketer is turning the effort over to consumers, asking them to submit a 15-second ad featuring its new ready-to-drink Vodka Water cocktail offering.

Consumers can submit their ideas until Dec. 1, and the winner will receive a $500,000 prize. To launch the contest, Svedka linked with Andy Cohen, who will also help evaluate submissions. For its 2026 ad, the marketer used AI to bring its fembot mascot to life, an effort which generated a wave of controversy.

Nutella, the hazelnut and cocoa spread made by Ferrero, will make its debut Super Bowl appearance, per a company announcement. Sister brand Kinder Bueno, which made its debut in the 2026 game, will also return.

Ferrero in October announced that it is committing over $50 million across its portfolio to sports marketing, representing the confectionary giant’s largest individual marketing investment to date.

Ferrero-owned Kinder Bueno will return to the Super Bowl for a second consecutive year, according to a company announcement. The popular confection made its big game debut last year with a campaign that drove a 140% lift in volume sales over the year prior.

The decision to return to the game is in support of a hefty investment by Ferrero in sports. The company announced in October that it is committing over $50 million across its portfolio to its sports strategy, representing its largest individual marketing investment to date.

Waymo, the robotaxi service owned by Google parent Alphabet, is conducting a creative review for its debut Super Bowl appearance, Ad Age reported. The company recently named WPP as its media agency of record for North America, Europe, the Middle East and Africa.

Cutwater, the spirits-based cocktail brand owned by Anheuser-Busch InBev, will return to the Super Bowl for a second year with a national ad, the brand confirmed. The decision is intended to drive further growth for Cutwater, which is currently the fastest growing brand in the total U.S. beverage alcohol industry, per Circana data shared in the company’s announcement.

Cutwater made its Super Bowl debut in 2022 with a 30-second spot called “Here’s to the Lazy Ones” celebrating individuals who have no problem taking a shortcut.
