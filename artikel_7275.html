Country’s Next It Girl Carter Faith Got Ready With Us for Her First CMA Awards as a Nominee (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Cherry Valley hitmaker gave Cosmo an exclusive look at her big night.

Country music has found its next It Girl in Carter Faith. The North Carolina–bred singer-songwriter has written songs since she was 16 and started seriously pursuing a career when she attended Belmont University in Music City—and spoiler alert: Her efforts have seriously paid off.

With more than 3 million monthly listeners on Spotify and Rolling Stone declaring her 2025 album, Cherry Valley, as “one of the best country debuts in recent memory,” it’s no wonder Carter has had a massive year. Her signature sharp, unfiltered songwriting has landed her a coveted spot on The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping soundtrack plus major nominations at the 2026 CMA Awards and next year’s Grammys. Her cosigns from industry giants like Post Malone, Noah Cyrus, Glen Powell, and Willie Nelson also prove she’s one to watch.

Even more exciting: Cosmopolitan had the pleasure of getting ready with Carter for her big night at the 60th CMA Awards on Wednesday, November 18. Although she has attended the Nashville ceremony before, she was one of 13 first-time nominees at this year’s show—and she totally dressed the part. So come for the gorgeous pics of Carter’s CMA look and stay for her cute captions, straight from the brain under her teased-up tresses.
