Drake Maye remained in Germany as ankle injury continues to be evaluated

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Patriots quarterback Drake Maye was knocked out of Sunday’s loss to the Lions in Munich early in the first quarter and he did not return to the United States with the team after the game.

Head coach Mike Vrabel did not have an update on Maye’s outlook during a Monday morning appearance on WEEI, but he did share that Maye remained behind as the team continues to evaluate his injury.

“The update that I have is just out of caution, Drake stayed back,” Vrabel said. “He’ll be traveling back here shortly, but just wanted to make sure that everything was good for him to travel back and safely. The things that go around with injuries, just wanted to make sure everything was in line for him to travel and it seems like he is. He’ll be returning here, I think, shortly.”

PFT reported on Sunday night that Maye is expected to be out a while and multiple reports on Monday morning have indicated fear that he may not be back on the field this season. An update on that from the team may not come until Maye is back from Europe, but it looks like the 5-5 team’s chances of staying in the playoff hunt are going to rely on backup quarterback Tommy DeVito for the indefinite future.
