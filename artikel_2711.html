Eagles waiving veteran OT Fred Johnson

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Eagles waiving veteran OT Fred Johnson originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Eagles have officially waived OT Fred Johnson and WR Hollywood Brown.

Even though they're veterans both Johnson and Brown are subject to waivers now that the trade deadline has passed. We learned about the pending Brown release on Thursday.

With the Eagles sitting at 2-7 at their bye week, these moves will allow for younger players to see playing time and develop. In the case of Brown, the Eagles are also expecting DeVonta Smith and Dontayvion Wicks to return to action after missing three games with their respective injuries.

Johnson, 29, initially joined the Eagles in 2022 and has played in 58 games with 14 starts with the team during his time in Philly. This season, Johnson has played in seven games but has been behind rookie Markel Bell, who took over at right tackle when Lane Johnson retired earlier this season.

With the Eagles, Johnson was able to save his career and played an important role during the 2024 Super Bowl season. He started 6 games that season.

Brown, 29, signed a one-year deal with the Eagles this offseason but didn't see much playing time. He has just 5 catches for 63 yards this season. The Eagles were unable to trade him at the deadline and Brown reportedly requested his release.

The Eagles and Brown reached a non-injury settlement, ESPN's Tim McManus first reported.
