Robinson vows to get Bromley winning

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

New Bromley boss Karl Robinson insists he will "get this team winning" in their fight to stay in League One, despite an early FA Cup exit to League Two strugglers Rotherham United.

In Robinson's first game in charge, the Ravens, bottom of the third tier, were beaten by a Rotherham side who had not won any of their previous 10 matches in all competitions.

"We're trying to change the style completely," Robinson told BBC Radio London.

"In the first 20 minutes, we got in some really good areas and turned down shooting opportunities.

"Then we gave a silly goal away. But that wasn't style, that was the discipline of detail. Our detail wasn't there and that's something, moving forward, we'll put together.

"The players are disciplined and together. I will get this team winning because, as a collective, they want to win. So I know it's going to come."
