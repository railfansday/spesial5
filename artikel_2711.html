NFL news, live updates: Colts release Daniel Jones, Anthony Richardson Sr. to start at QB; Puka Nacua potentially out for season after knee surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

We are officially halfway done with the 2026 NFL season, and the NFL is now into Week 11, highlighted by the year's final international game: the Minnesota Vikings take on the San Francisco 49ers in Mexico City, Mexico, for "Sunday Night Football."

The Indianapolis Colts and Houston Texans opened up the week on "Thursday Night Football," but quarterback Daniel Jones continued to struggle, completing just 44.4% of pass attempts for 144 yards and a pick. The Colts followed up with a statement move on Friday, announcing the team would release Jones and promote former No. 4 overall pick Anthony Richardson Sr. to the starting position in a stunning move.

Los Angeles Rams receiver Puka Nacua might be done for the year after undergoing surgery on his MCL on Tuesday. Nacua was already on injured reserve with multiple injuries, but head coach Sean McVay says that he is now poised to miss "a couple months."

That news came one day after Nacua was reportedly suspended three games by the NFL for violating the league's personal conduct policy, as a result of the receiver's series of incidents this offseason; Nacua is serving the suspension while on IR.

Yahoo Sports surveyed high-ranking league sources to better understand the debates from recent annual meetings as well as the most likely factors to motivate any changes.

Sources were split on whether they wanted any shift to the structure — and how that shift should materialize if it does unfold.

One high-ranking NFC executive who favors revamping the playoff structure was skeptical anything will change in the next five to 10 years, even if the schedule expands to 18 games.

"I think it'll take an egregious deal," the executive told Yahoo Sports. "It'll take freaking someone winning the NFC South with seven wins and somebody coming in with like 12 wins. It'll take something like that, and it'd probably take an influential owner to be on that 12-win team."

Over the past five weeks, Vikings players have subjected themselves to aerobic workouts inside the chamber, using treadmills and stationary bikes to simulate the demands of exercising at altitude. The Vikings hope that altitude training inside their own building reduces the shock factor they experience Sunday night, Tyler Williams, the team's vice president of player health and performance, told reporters in Minnesota on Wednesday.
