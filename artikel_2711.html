Jim Harbaugh on finally getting a win: "Let's do it every week"

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Before the season, many expected the Chargers not only to get back to the playoffs for the third straight year but to finally make some noise.

Instead, it took them more than two months to make a peep.

But they roared to life on Monday night, with a win over the Ravens in Baltimore. The 23-20 victory instantly restored the boundless optimism of coach Jim Harbaugh.

“Thrilled, let’s do it every week,” Harbaugh told reporters after the game, via Jeff Legwold of ESPN. “Good to see the smiles back on the faces.”

Harbaugh should be smiling the most. His stubborn refusal to make major changes — starting with firing or reassigning offensive coordinator Mike McDaniel and continuing with a failure to trade players who won’t be there next year, like Khalil Mack — was putting Harbaugh in increasing danger of being fired. While the playoffs are a practical impossibility, Harbaugh’s obvious goal is survival for 2027.

That’s ultimately why there was no fire sale; moving good players would have made Harbaugh’s firing more likely. Surely, it’s one of the reasons why he resisted stockpiling future picks. If he’s not there to use them, who cares?

Could Harbaugh and the Chargers stack some wins? They’ve got the 2-8 Jets, the 5-5 Patriots (without quarterback Drake Maye), the 3-6 Buccaneers, and the 4-6 Raiders over the next four weeks.

It changes after that, with three of the last four games against the 8-1 49ers, the 7-2 Broncos, and the 7-2 Chiefs.

Will they run the table and sneak into the playoffs? Most likely not. Can they win enough to get Harbaugh a fourth season with the Chargers?

At this point, that’s the only realistic goal left.
