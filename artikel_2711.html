Sandy Brondello reacts to Tempo winning No. 1 pick in 2027 WNBA Draft lottery

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Sandy Brondello reacts to Tempo winning No. 1 pick in 2027 WNBA Draft lottery appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

With her daughter beside her in Phoenix, Sandy Brondello celebrated Sunday as the Toronto Tempo secured the No. 1 pick in the 2027 WNBA Draft.

Speaking the day after the lottery, Brondello described her reaction to learning Toronto would have the first selection as the franchise prepares for its second season.

"Yeah, well, back in Phoenix, my daughter and I were watching it, and it was quite nerve-wracking," Brondello said in an interview on "In Case You Missed It" with Khristina Williams."I've never been in this situation before because I've always been in the playoffs and never been in the lottery."

Toronto entered the lottery tied with the Houston Comets, formerly the Connecticut Sun, for the best chance of securing the top pick at 33% each. The Tempo won that selection after finishing their inaugural season with an 11-33 record.

Brondello said the celebration at home caught the attention of more than just her daughter.

"You know, there's a lot of screaming in my house. My dogs were very stunned," Brondello said. "They weren't sure what was going on and why we're screaming."

The result gives Toronto the opportunity to select USC guard JuJu Watkins, who is in her senior season. Watkins is averaging 24.3 points, 8.3 rebounds, 4.7 assists, 3.7 steals and 1.0 blocks for the Trojans.

Toronto's roster includes Marina Mabrey, Brittney Sykes and Kiki Rice. The first selection gives the Tempo another opportunity to add to that group as they continue building the franchise.

According to ESPN's Alexa Philippou, Toronto is the first franchise to hold the No. 1 pick entering its second season since the Atlanta Dream in 2009. Atlanta selected Angel McCoughtry with that pick.

Houston received the second selection in Sunday's lottery in New York. The Washington Mystics will pick third, followed by the Seattle Storm and Portland Fire.
