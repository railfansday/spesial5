Joe Milton III praises the Eagles, possibly criticizes the Cowboys

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Eagles quarterback Joe Milton III returned to Texas on Thursday and threw a significant wrench into the Cowboys’ postseason plans (and, possibly, Philly’s thinly-veiled soft tank).

After the game, Milton reflected on his days in Dallas.

“It was a lot going through my head from when I first got here in Dallas to when I left, all the time I spent doing scout team quarterback, all the times where I was trying to get in the game, when they were up in games, just trying to get more experience,” Milton said, via Nick Harris of the Fort Worth Star-Telegram. “I mean, it was just great coming back here and playing in this stadium in Jerry’s World and seeing the guys that I worked hard with in fall camp and OTAs.”

He then focused on his current team. While possibly adding a little commentary regarding his former team.

“But I mean, it’s about the Eagles,” Milton said. “These guys show up every day. Nobody’s late to anything. Nobody’s complaining about anything. That’s all you can ask for. That’s the most important thing that I love about this team. I just know they’re going to keep fighting.”

The obvious implication is that, in Dallas, some guys were not showing up every day. Some guys were late. Some guys were complaining.

There are surely complaints today, one day after the Cowboys lost their fourth game of the season while scoring 30 or more points.
