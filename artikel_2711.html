Who is the Buffalo Bills’ biggest threat for 1 seed in 2026 AFC playoffs?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Welcome to SB Nation Reacts, a survey of fans across the NFL. Throughout the year we ask questions of the most plugged-in Bills fans and fans across the country.

The 8-1 Buffalo Bills remain one of the most talk-about teams in the NFL this season. They have an all-world quarterback in Josh Allen, and feature a run-first offensive approach led by running back James Cook III and a very talented offensive line that celebrates road grading the rest of the league.

Offensively, there's currently room only to critique things on a microscopic level. It might not always look "conventional," but it's impossible to ignore Buffalo's scoring proficiency. Now, a reformulated defense has settled in a bit, rising to become a middle-of-the-pack group after beginning the season as one of the NFL's worst stop units.

That 8-1 record has the Bills in first place in the AFC, which would grant Buffalo the one seed in the conference and hand them a bye week to begin their playoff run. There's a ton of football left to play, and that means plenty of scenarios can still play out over the course of the last eight weeks.

Which team is the Bills' biggest threat to their hopes of finally claiming the one seed? There are four very strong candidates, which may or may not set off alarms in your brain. Among the likeliest teams to challenge Buffalo are a quartet of teams sitting at 7-2 in Cincinnati Bengals, Denver Broncos, Jacksonville Jaguars, and Kansas City Chiefs.
