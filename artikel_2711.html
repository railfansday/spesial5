Balanced attack propels Wisconsin volleyball past Ohio State | 3 takeaways

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Wisconsin volleyball's penultimate match of the 2026 regular season was far from perfect, but the ninth-ranked Badgers did enough to defeat another unranked opponent.

The Badgers outlasted Ohio State in four sets, 23-25, 25-18, 25-19, 25-22, on Nov. 13 at the Covelli Center in Columbus, Ohio.

After the Buckeyes won the first set – their third set won against a ranked opponent in 2026 – the Badgers comfortably won the second and third sets and narrowly won the fourth set en route to their 93rd consecutive win over an unranked team.

Wisconsin showed off its array of attack options with some exceptionally balanced performances.

Outside hitter Decelise Champion led the Badgers with 15 kills while only committing one attack error and hitting .467.

Fellow outside hitter Grace Egan, middle blocker Jaela Auguste and right-side hitter Grace Lopez each finished with 13 kills. Middle blocker Lynney Tarnow also had 12 kills.

The five aforementioned players each took a range of 20 to 34 attacks, and four of them hit .367 or better. Egan's 13 kills were her second-most in a match this season, trailing only her 17 kills in a five-set thriller against then-No. 17 Florida. The big performance came against her former school.

The Badgers' .366 hitting percentage against the Buckeyes was their sixth-highest of the season (and second-highest on the road).

Wisconsin had an obvious advantage at the service line, finishing with a season-high 10 aces. Those were the most for the Badgers in a Big Ten match since the 2024 win over Maryland.

Kristen Simon – Wisconsin's starting libero for the second consecutive match – was responsible for four of those aces, marking a new career-high. Middle blocker Natalie Wardlow had another four aces, which tied her career-high.

Wisconsin committed four service errors in four sets – down from its previous season average of 2.33 service errors per set. Ohio State, meanwhile, finished with four aces and eight service errors.

Ohio State's success in the first set and competitiveness in the fourth set had two constants – Naomy Govou and Hannah Jones.

Govou ultimately led the Buckeyes with 25 kills while hitting .442. Jones had 17 kills with a .484 hitting percentage. No other Buckeyes finished with more than six kills against the Badgers.

In the first set in particular, Ohio State benefited from Govou's 11 kills and Jones' eight kills. Neither outside hitter committed any attack errors in the set. The Buckeyes finished the set with a whopping 22 kills.

Fast-forward to the fourth set, and Govou delivered another seven kills while committing only one attack error. Jones was quieter, recording three kills and two errors in the deciding set.

The two star outside hitters helped Ohio State finish the match with a .360 hitting percentage – higher than any other team that Wisconsin has defeated in 2026.
