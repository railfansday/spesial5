DeVonta Smith still sidelined with injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Philadelphia Eagles WR DeVonta Smith (hamstring) did not participate in practice Wednesday, Nov. 4.

Why It Matters: Smith has been sidelined for several weeks because of the injury and will likely miss another game if he does not practice at some point this week. Fantasy players can monitor the situation but should not count on Smith to play in Week 9.
