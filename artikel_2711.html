Sean Payton honored Tom Jackson's family with a game ball after win over Raiders

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The entire NFL world was saddened to learn about the passing of Denver and ESPN legend Tom Jackson over the weekend.

After the Broncos defeated the division-rival Raiders on Sunday, head coach Sean Payton presented Jackson's family with a game ball.

"I think I'd be remiss if we didn't start by honoring Tom Jackson tonight," Payton said in his postgame press conference, via transcript from the team. "A lot of these players — as a coach, you're always teaching. A lot of these players certainly won't remember him as a player, maybe not as an ESPN media member. He got a game ball tonight, his family, as part of the rich [team] history. Obviously, [a] tough loss for everyone."

A member of the Broncos Ring of Fame, Jackson played 191 games across 14 years for the franchise from 1973-1986.
