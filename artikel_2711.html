Saquon Barkley injury update as Eagles RB leaves game vs. Cowboys

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Philadelphia Eagles have lost another one of their stars on offense.

Already missing quarterback Jalen Hurts with his blood clot issue, the Eagles have lost running back Saquon Barkley with an injury of his own on Thanksgiving.

Barkley sustained a stinger in the second quarter of Philadelphia's clash with the Cowboys on the national holiday. He has been ruled questionable to return to the Week 12 matchup.

Prior to his injury, Barkley had recorded seven rushing yards on five carries.

Barkley has been ruled questionable to return with a stinger on Thursday, Nov. 26. While the Eagles have not updated his status, the running back remained sidelined throughout the third quarter.

The stinger injury was the second that the running back has sustained this season. Barkley previously suffered a stinger on the first play of the Eagles' Week 2 win over the Tennessee Titans. He eventually returned to that game in the second half.

Barkley has also missed a couple of games this year with a hamstring injury he suffered in the first quarter of a Week 4 game against the Rams.
