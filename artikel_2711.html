Jaylen Harrell allegedly assaulted rideshare driver before crash

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Titans announced on Saturday that defensive end Jaylen Harrell was hospitalized in Tampa after a “serious car accident.” It doesn’t sound like it was the usual car accident.

Via WTSP, the Tampa Police Department said that Harrell was the passenger in a rideshare and that “he reportedly assaulted the driver while the vehicle was in motion.”

The driver then swerved, hitting another car before colliding with a utility pole. The impact caused Harrell to be thrown forward, striking his head against the windshield and losing consciousness.

Harrell was already on injured reserve. Per the Titans’ statement, he remains hospitalized.

Criminal charges are possible for Harrell, obviously. The driver suffered minor injuries in the wreck; the driver of the other car was not injured.
