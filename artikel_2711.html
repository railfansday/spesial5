Former Real Madrid star urges Mourinho to hand more minutes to Carlos Espi: &#8216;He&#8217;s a problem solver&#8217;

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Speaking on COPE (h/t Defensa Central), former Real Madrid goalkeeper Santi Canizares has urged Jose Mourinho to hand a greater role to young striker Carlos Espi.

This comes after the summer arrival came up clutch for Los Blancos against Celta Vigo on Saturday, scoring the match-winning goal at the Santiago Bernabeu.

Having come on as a substitute against the Galician outfit in the 79th minute, the 21-year-old Spaniard found the back of the net six minutes later to help Real Madrid secure a 2-1 win.

Since joining Real Madrid from Levante last summer, Espi has had to settle for a secondary role, given that Kylian Mbappe has been the undisputed starter at centre-forward.

In all, the towering striker has played only 62 minutes this season for the club, across 12 matches, but has chipped in with four goals already, which include three match-winners against Espanyol, Elche, and now Celta Vigo.

Carlos Espi has excelled in his limited opportunities. (Photo by Denis Doyle/Getty Images)

And Canizares acknowledged that the youngster is unlikely to displace Mbappe from the starting line-up. However, he questioned why he could not receive more playing time in matches that are already under control.

"He can't be a starter because Mbappe is there, but there are many minutes in the second half where Madrid is winning 2-0 or 3-0 with half an hour left," he said.

"It would be fair for him to get more playing time, and there are ways to find it," the former goalkeeper added.

Canizares then went on to compare Espi with former Real Madrid striker Joselu, who also became known for his impact as a penalty-box striker from the bench.

Real Madrid's new super-sub. (Photo by Francisco Macia/Getty Images)

But the former shot-stopper believes that the 21-year-old is much better than the veteran forward, as he said:

"For me, Carlos Espi is a much better striker than Joselu. Every goal he scores is worth points; he's a problem solver, and it's the worst thing that can happen to you if you're winning against Madrid, when this player comes on."

Further highlighting the youngster's attributes, he added: "Espi is a player who has everything, he can play in all systems and has the aura of Real Madrid, of epic moments."

Despite his admiration for Espi, Canizares was far less impressed with Real Madrid's overall display against Celta.

"Real Madrid weren't a good team today; they only won thanks to the individual talent of their players," he argued.

"Real Madrid's defensive system can't handle the movement of the players in front of them," he noted.
