Darren Waller, Tommy Tremble out of practice for Panthers

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Panthers practiced without their top two tight ends on Wednesday.

Darren Waller and Tommy Tremble were both sidelined by knee injuries. Waller had two catches for 24 yards in last Sunday’s win over the Saints while Tremble had four catches for 48 yards.

The Panthers were also without linebacker Bobby Okereke (foot), safety Isiah Simmons (knee), and running back Chuba Hubbard (rest). Okereke has missed the last two games.

Linebacker Trevis Gipson (ankle) was a limited participant. Cornerback Will Lee (hamstring), offensive lineman Brady Christensen (hamstring), cornerback Mike Jackson (groin), running back Jonathon Brooks (groin), quarterback Bryce Young (foot), linebacker Jackson Kuwatch (shoulder), and tight end Feleipe Franks (wrist) were all full participants.
