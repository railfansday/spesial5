Sporting 2-2 Manchester United: Mauricio Pochettino denied first win as referee at centre of chaotic draw

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Senne Lammens went from hero to zero as the Manchester United goalkeeper’s howler denied Mauricio Pochettino a first win in charge during a crazy 2-2 Champions League draw at Sporting Lisbon.

Wednesday’s breathless Estadio Jose Alvalade encounter featured three penalties, a pair of controversially disallowed goals, a bobble-assisted effort, a costly goalkeeping error and a stoppage-time red card.

Pochettino’s reign began with a chastening 4-0 loss at rivals Liverpool and further frustration followed in Portugal, where Lammens saved a Luis Suarez penalty only to gift Sporting another that the striker duly converted.

Amad Diallo saw a first half leveller ruled out as referee Szymon Marciniak had incorrectly blown his whistle early meaning the VAR could not intervene, but United turned things around just after half-time.

Lisandro Martinez gave away the first penalty and earned the visitors one of their own that Bruno Fernandes scored, before the former Sporting star fired an effort that hit a divot and flew over goalkeeper Kaique Pereira.

More drama would follow as Lammens flapped at a cross under pressure from Suarez as the striker scored, before Patrick Dorgu was adjudged to have put undue pressure when forcing an own goal from Pereira.

United will be furious with the lack of consistency after Marciniak was sent to the pitch side monitor to review the second decision and their mood only darkened when Youri Tielemans was sent off for a second booking at the end.

Pochettino’s will hope to secure his first win – and the Red Devils’ second of the Premier League campaign – when he takes charge at Old Trafford for the first time against Brentford on Saturday.

The former Argentina international oversaw an unusually serene start by the Red Devils’ recent standards as Sporting failed to create clear-cut chances and Joshua Zirkzee, making his first start of the season, slammed off target.

But a quiet, controlled start changed as referee Marciniak pointed to the spot after Martinez clumsily caught Geny Catamo.

Lammens denied Suarez’s poorly hit 30th-minute penalty but, with adrenaline running high from that save, he charged off his line and gifted Sporting another opportunity from 12 yards.

The United goalkeeper bundled over Maximiliano Araujo and Suarez again stepped as he atoned for his earlier miss by scoring his 33rd-minute spot-kick.

United eventually overcame that wobble and were inexplicably denied a 40th minute leveller.

Tielemans prodded through for Amad to get away a shot that Pereira took onto the crossbar, only for Marciniak to blow his whistle just before the winger headed in the loose ball.

Halting play early meant the VAR could not review a decision that had seen the Polish referee hoodwinked by Goncalo Inacio, whose wailing led the official to urge on physios and wrongly book Tielemans.

United returned from the break rejuvenated and equalised three minutes after the restart.

Martinez smartly drew a foul from Eduardo Quaresma and Fernandes kept his cool, scoring with a lot shot before collecting the ball and running back to the halfway line.

There was a sense of urgency about United’s play and a stroke of good luck about their second in the 50th minute. Fernandes’ hopeful effort hit a divot just outside the six-yard box and flew over Pereira.

It was a bizarre goal which was so nearly followed by the former Sporting favourite completing his hat-trick with a wonderful free-kick that bent just wide from the edge of the box.

United attempted to hold out for a much-needed win but Lammens was looking nervy and surprisingly spilled a Sergi Altimira effort.

Those issues continued in the 80th minute when he was nudged off balance by Suarez when attempting to gather a high ball and the striker acrobatically equalised.

Calls for a foul fell on deaf ears, unlike when Sporting goalkeeper Pereira flapped at a corner moments later and took the ball into his own net.

Marciniak was sent to the monitor and adjudged Dorgu to have fouled the goalkeeper, leading to it being ruled out.

Tielemans’ second yellow card in stoppage time for a challenge added salt to the wound.
