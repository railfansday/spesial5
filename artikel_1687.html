Xabi Alonso reveals details of Chelsea talks after Leeds thrashing

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Xabi Alonso has revealed what he discussed with Chelsea’s hierarchy in the aftermath of a bruising 4-1 defeat by Leeds.

Last weekend’s heavy loss at Stamford Bridge made it two defeats in a row for the Blues and has led to plenty of criticism of Alonso and his team’s style of play.

Chelsea will bid to get back to winning ways when they travel to face Nottingham Forest at the City Ground in the Premier League on Friday night.

At his pre-match press conference on Thursday, Alonso was asked what conversations he has had with owner Behdad Egbhali and Chelsea’s sporting directors during the club’s recent drop-off in form.

“The focus has been tomorrow [Friday], it’s been the game [against Forest],” he said.

“It has been about showing a strong reaction, showing a competitive reaction, a competitive level that we need for tomorrow, for the rest of the games. That we can do much better, that it was not good enough in the last game.

“That has been the main topic for us, for the team. If you look just on Saturday, we missed not just players, we missed many basics to compete in the game.

“So that’s been clear during the week: talk about how we need to have those basics in football to compete.”

Alonso also revealed that while neither Joao Pedro nor Maxence Lacroix have returned from injury in time to be involved against Forest, Moises Caicedo will be available to play.

It comes after the Ecuador midfielder was forced off in the first half of the Leeds game with a knock. He was later assessed and has been deemed fit for the trip to Forest in a major boost for Chelsea.
