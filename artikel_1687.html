De Winter discusses clean sheet chances, relationship with Pavlovic and starting role: “I feel good”

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Today, Koni De Winter sat alongside Ruben Amorim for a press conference ahead of AC Milan vs. Ferencvaros in the Europa League.

It’s been a big year for De Winter, with the Belgian going from strength to strength. After the Supercoppa Italiana game against Napoli, the defender has seemingly improved constantly,  even becoming something of a regular for Massimiliano Allegri towards the tail end of last season.

Then, Ruben Amorim has relied on him this season. So far, he’s played in every game of the campaign, and whilst the defence has been hampered by injuries around him, he’s been an ever-present option for the Portuguese.

After the birth of his child today, he was celebrated by the team at Milanello, and he will hope to make a strong week – after the derby win, too – an even better one, with a win tomorrow.

Speaking alongside Amorim, De Winter answered several questions from the media, as MilanNews relays.

This year you became a regular for AC Milan and now you’ve also become a father…

“Becoming a dad is a wonderful thing. On the pitch, I feel more important than last year and I feel more confident. I think it’s a great moment, but there’s still a long way to go.”

You’ve played pretty much all the time so far. Did you expect that?

“I feel good. When there are a lot of games, you train by playing. For me, it’s better this way. So for me, it’s fine like this; I have to be ready.”

You almost always play alongside Pavlovic. How do you get along with the Serbian?

“I feel good with all my teammates; we know each other well. I like playing with Pavlovic; he’s strong and we understand each other very well.”

How difficult will it be to keep a clean sheet tomorrow?

“They’re a good team, they’re having a good Europa League, they have great confidence, but we’ll do our best to stop them from scoring.”

Milan has had many great defenders. Do you have any role models?

“I have several. If I have to name one who played for AC Milan, I’d say Thiago Silva, whom I followed a lot and who is very good. If I have to name one, I’d say his.”

“As the coach says, he’s someone who works hard and always gives his all. Everyone loves him. When you work hard, your chance comes, and he grabbed it with both hands. We’re all happy for him.”
