Opinion: The next Jude Bellingham is ready to join Liverpool, he just proved it

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

An exciting midfielder is ready to join Liverpool, and he just confirmed it.

The football world is ever evolving. That's what is most alluring about it. In football, anything is possible. New heroes are born, old ones rise from the ashes and sometimes turn into villains.

Every year, there are break-out stars. New names emerge onto the scene who etch themselves permanently into the annals of the game.

Jude Bellingham was one of those players.

At 16-years-old, he was already a regular for Birmingham City. As a teenager, he conquered Borussia Dortmund. And when Liverpool wanted to bring in a midfielder, the Reds were close to luring him to Anfield.

But the cajoling calls of Real Madrid are impossible to turn down. Bellingham was swayed by the allure of becoming a Galactico.

The club's sporting director has been in his post for a long time, but he has already explored a vast myriad of options. Among the most likely of those is Lamine Camara. Watch the Senegalese midfielder, and you already see the vision of what he can bring to Andoni Iraola's side. He's fast, he is a relentless presser and he causes problems for the opposition time and time again. Simply put, Camara brings energy and control which Liverpool are lacking in the middle of the park at the moment. There are other candidates of course. Sporting CP's Sergi Altimira and Athletic Bilbao's Mikel Jauregizar are also onthe club's radar. Alex Scott is another option who has been linked. But Camara is the cream of the crop.

Diego Pelembe confirms he's ready to join LiverpoolAnother African midfielder on Liverpool's radar is Diego Pelembe. Pedro Marques and his scouts spotted him playing for Mozambique at the U17 AFCON earlier this year. The son of Mozambique's greatest ever player, Elias Pelembe, it's clear the apple did not fall far from the tree. Pelembe is an exquisite midfielder in the mould of Jude Bellingham. He has a great stride, he is very technical and his positioning is immaculate. The long gait that he has makes him really difficult to face for opposition midfielders. Now catching the eye at the U17 World Cup, Pelembe produced a sensational performance in Mozambique's opening victory over Denmark. He completed five out of nine dribbles, completed 17 of 19 passes in the opposition's half and also made five progressive runs all while making five recoveries and winning eight out of his 16 duels. It was a performance that confirmed that the RB Bragantino midfielder is ready to join Liverpool. Given the connection between Red Bull and the Reds, that might come to fruition soon, too.
