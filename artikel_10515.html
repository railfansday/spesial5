Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold 8 returns to its lowest price yet ahead of Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

No, your calendar isn’t lying to you — Thanksgiving really is next week, and with it comes the official start of the holiday shopping season. It’s never too early to get a start on your list, though, and if you’re hoping to surprise someone with a brand-new foldable under the tree, all three of Samsung’s 2026 foldables are currently marked down on Amazon, and that includes an all-time low for this year’s hit Galaxy Z Fold 8.

While iPhone Duo owners contemplate returning their devices to the Apple Store, you can pick up Samsung’s excellent Galaxy Z Fold 8 for just $1,550 at Amazon today. This deal is good on the 256GB variant in Graphite or Lavender, though you can score the 512GB model in all three colors for an additional $200. Samsung.com has the same deal going, and if you’re lucky, you might even score an additional $50 off; while I’m seeing the same $1,550 price, other writers at 9to5 have seen the price as low as $1,500.

The Fold 8 introduces an all-new form factor for Samsung’s foldable lineup, and so far, it appears to be a slam-dunk success. As 9to5‘s Ben Schoon found in his review, this is a shape and style that feels much more welcoming to gamers, streamers, and movie fanatics, not just spreadsheet masters. The wider outer display might not be to everyone’s taste, but it’s a throwback to a simpler time in smartphone design, and for novelty’s sake alone, it’s honestly a lot of fun.

If you’re not into Samsung’s new passport-style, the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and Galaxy Z Flip 8 are both discounted as well. The more traditional follow-up to last year’s Fold 7 can be yours for $1,750 for the 256GB model, while the Flip 8 drops to just $999, or $10 away from its previous all-time low. Neither deal — or phone, for that matter — is quite as exciting as the regular Fold 8, but with three different foldable shapes to choose from, you’re bound to find something for anyone in your life who needs a new phone.
