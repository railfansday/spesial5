‘Only one winner’: Birmingham City vs Bolton Wanderers predictions

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Birmingham City returned to winning ways on Wednesday night as they beat lowly Burnley 2-0 at Turf Moor. Chris Davies‘ side won their fifth away game of the season; only West Brom have picked up more points on the road this season.

Once again, August Priske and Jay Stansfield were key to BCFC’s victory. The attacking duo combined again on Wednesday night, with Stansfield setting up Priske for the side’s opener. Between them, they have scored and created 15 goals for Birmingham City, and Davies will expect more of the same on Saturday.

Bolton Wanderers meanwhile are starting to get a little repetitive, showing spells of competing at Championship level before finding a way to lose. It was exactly the same on Wednesday as Steven Schumacher‘s side lost 3-1 to West Brom. The Trotters fought back after chasing the game, but just as they took control, they conceded twice, leaving the same old feeling at the Toughsheet Community Stadium.

For Schumacher, his issues include poor home form and a failure to convert chances, but the clear issue is in defence. BWFC have conceded 33 goals this season, conceding three in each of their last three games, and only the Championship’s bottom two have conceded more.

“Birmingham City are clicking at the right time. There’s a lot of pressure on Chris Davies to deliver and aside from the loss to Stoke City, it is exactly what they are doing. Beating Burnley on Wednesday was routine for this group of players, and this game should be too.

“Bolton Wanderers have shown they can compete, but down the stretch, they just don’t have the quality. The Trotters need to head to St Andrew’s and set up to be hard to beat, or they could face a really long afternoon in the Second City.

“On form and quality alone, there’s only one winner in this game. For me, that is BCFC.”

“Birmingham City produced an ideal response to their Stoke City defeat by beating Burnley in midweek. The Clarets might have been a favourable matchup for the Blues, but they got the job done in a professional manner to keep pace with the three Championship sides above them.

“Professional is a good way to describe BCFC’s season so far. Unlike last season, they’ve not got bogged down after negative results. All three times Chris Davies’ side have lost this campaign, they’ve responded with a victory in their next game, highlighting their strong mentality.

“Bolton Wanderers have shown they’re dangerous this season, but they are beatable. Steven Schumacher’s men come into games to impose their will and they are capable of doing that, but I think a problem is that Schumacher is still working out his best defence, and that is preventing them from finding success at the back – 33 goals conceded in 17 games shows that.

“You can’t write off Wanderers, but you have to think the Blues win here. I’m going for a home win.”
