Steven Schumacher confirms Bolton Wanderers boost

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Bolton Wanderers boss Steven Schumacher has no long-term concerns about club captain Eoin Toal and expects to see him back from his current setback around Christmas.

The 27-year-old Northern Ireland international is currently recovering from an ankle injury he picked up in a training session he picked up ahead of their 3-2 defeat against Bristol City in late October. He's missed the last three games.

Toal has had a history of ankle problems dating back to the 2023 EFL Trophy final. This is said to be an issue with the opposite ankle, but it adds to the narrative amongst some Bolton supporters that the centre-back can't stay fit for lengthy spells.

Since arriving at the Toughsheet Stadium in the summer of 2022, Toal has played 155 times for the Trotters, but only managed 22 league games in his first season and 19 in 2024/25. Despite a decent injury record in the past couple of years, though, this setback has raised some alarm bells, which Schumacher was quick to quieten.

Toal missed the majority of Steven Schumacher's first half-season at the club, after he arrived in late January 2025. In his first full campaign in charge, though, the 27-year-old was nearly ever-present.

The Northern Irishman missed the play-off final win over Stockport County with a hamstring injury, but before then, he made 39 league and play-off appearances, while also being left on the bench four times. Indeed, he missed only six games, including that Wembley final.

Then this season, when Toal wasn't playing, he was unused from the bench. This setback is his first this year, and his first lengthy one in terms of missing consecutive games since the 2024/25 campaign. Therefore, Schumacher isn't concerned about his long-term fitness.

"I think he will be fine – I mean, last year he played virtually every game up until the play-off semi-final,” Schumacher said, per the Bolton News. "He was available for pretty much every week and had overcome the injuries he had the previous season when we first came in.

"He had been really stop-start that first season, but I think he did enough to show that his body is durable, and I think he has been excellent for us. Injuries can happen; that’s football, but no, I don’t have any long-term concerns with Toally."

The Bolton boss was quick to outline that his recent injuries haven't been a recurrence of one issue, and his current ankle problem is "innocuous."

"He’s had a calf issue, this one is a different ankle, the hamstring at Bradford. And this one was such an innocuous thing. He just stood on somebody's foot and rolled his ankle," he explained.

"So, it's not ideal for him. But I don't think it's going to be a major long-term thing either. We will get him back out there as quickly as possible, and he’ll be fine."

Toal is in the final year of his Bolton deal, which he signed back in 2024. Wanderers fans appreciate his contributions, but believe that the Northern Irishman's time at the Toughsheet could be coming to an end given his injury record.

When the extent of his injury was revealed earlier this month, one Bolton supporter wrote on X, "Toals career at boltons coming to an end i think." In the replies, another fan agreed, stating, "He is always injured."

Even in response to Schumacher's comments, someone was rather skeptical in the replies, assuming that the Bolton boss almost had to say that to "maintain some market value," and that he "always seems to be injured."

Ultimately, it's a perception that will follow Toal throughout the rest of his time at Bolton, even if this proves to be his only slightly major setback in the last two seasons.

And if his contract is up at the end of the season, a lot of Wanderers fans may be happy to shake hands, thank him for his five years of service and move on.

Steven Schumacher has plenty of backup in the central defensive area, with the likes of Chris Forino, Lewis Brunt, Ben Davies and Akin Famewo joining Toal as senior centre-back options.

Therefore, a setback to the captain shouldn't leave Bolton too short of defensive options, but there's an argument to be made that having the 27-year-old fit and healthy wouldn't go amiss.

The Trotters have struggled defensively this season. No side has allowed more expected goals, and only Derby County and Cardiff City have faced more shots on target.

In the seven games that Toal has started, they have kept two clean sheets. They've also conceded at least three to West Ham United, Norwich City and Middlesbrough, but they went on to do that in two of the three he's missed due to this ankle issue.

Having the leadership of the Northern Irish international may be what Bolton needs this year. Even if his injury record is perceived to be poor, he's still a helpful player to have amongst the squad.
