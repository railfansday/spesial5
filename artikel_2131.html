Play: Taarabt's Dribble Dash

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Few sights in Championship history are as fondly remembered as Adel Taarabt picking up the ball, dropping a shoulder and leaving a defender sitting on the turf.

The Moroccan playmaker had his critics, and his showboating could drive team-mates and managers to distraction. But when it clicked, there was nobody in the second tier quite like him. In 2010-11 it clicked like never before.

Neil Warnock took charge of QPR in March 2010 and over that summer decided to make the 21-year-old the focal point of his side. It proved a masterstroke.

Taarabt captained Rangers to the Championship title and was named the division’s Player of the Year after a season in which he scored 19 goals, helping the club return to the top flight after a 15-year absence.

He also picked up a place in the PFA Championship team of the season, and won QPR’s Goal of the Season for a superb solo effort against Swansea on Boxing Day.

Now it’s your turn to channel that magic. Taarabt’s Dribble Dash puts you in control of the QPR maverick as he runs at the heart of the opposition.

Swipe, tap or use your arrow keys to switch lanes, then weave past cones, lunging defenders and flying slide tackles on your way to goal. Every level ends with the ball rippling the net, and the further you go, the faster and more ruthless the defending becomes.

There are 15 levels to conquer, from a gentle warm-up session all the way to a nerve-shredding title decider. But you only get three lives for the whole run. Collect gold stars along the way for bonus points, and clear levels five and ten to win a life back. Chargers, trackers and sliding challenges will all be waiting for you before you get anywhere near promotion.

Can you guide Taarabt all the way to the title, or will you be scythed down on a cold, wet Tuesday night? Have a go below, then share your score on Facebook or X and challenge your mates to beat it.
