New Jersey Doctor Charged With Coercion And Enticement Of A Minor

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

United States Attorney for the Southern District of New York, Jamie McDonald, Assistant Director in Charge of the New York Field Office of the Federal Bureau of Investigation (“FBI”), James C. Barnacle, Jr., Superintendent of the New York State Police, Steven G. James, and Chief of the Port Jervis Police Department, William Worden, announced today that Mohammad Harun Rashid has been charged for communicating online with an individual he believed to be a 14-year-old girl and attempting to meet the individual to engage in sexual activities.
