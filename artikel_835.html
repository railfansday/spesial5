Leicester split with sponsor after porn actress row

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

BC Game has been the main shirt sponsor of Leicester City for two and a half years

Leicester City have parted ways with their main shirt sponsor after the gambling firm appointed a porn actress as an ambassador.

BC Game - which was named as a sponsor for the League One side at the start of the 2024-25 season - named Eva Elfie as a brand ambassador earlier this month.

The online gambling company posted a video of Elfie and a delivery driver on social media, with the caption: "Welcome to BC Game Eva Elfie! Let the fun begin."

The club, which had faced criticism from fans' group The Foxes Trust, said it had reached a "mutual agreement" to end its partnership with the firm, and supporters who had bought shirts in the past 28 days could exchange them.

Announcing the club's partnership in 2024, Leicester City said BC Game - which is not available to users in the UK - was a "socially responsible platform" and aimed to provide an "enjoyable and safe gaming experience".

When Elfie was confirmed as BC Game's brand ambassador, the firm - in a post on LinkedIn on 3 November - said she had an "established presence in gaming communities".

Chief executive Ali "Ako" Muhanned previously added: "Eva has built an incredible relationship with the gaming and esports community through genuine passion and consistent content creation."

In response, The Foxes Trust said: "Both gambling and pornography can have devastating impacts on people's lives, in particular young people.

"Partnerships like these bring these two worlds to a larger audience and, by association, link them to Leicester City. Is this who we are now?"

On Wednesday the club issued a statement, which said it and the sponsor had "reached a mutual agreement to conclude our commercial partnership with immediate effect".

It also thanked the firm for the "constructive approach taken in reaching this agreement".

The statement confirmed an "enhanced returns policy" in which it would accept BC Game-branded shirts bought since 21 October for exchange or refund, even if they had been worn or personalised.

The company posted a video announcing its partnership with Eva Elfie on social media

The Foxes Trust said it welcomed the decision.

"Since the partnership was announced in 2024, the trust has consistently made clear that BC Game, for numerous reasons, does not reflect the values of our club or its supporters," a spokesperson said.

"That view has been shared by the great majority of fans who responded to our surveys. The recent partnership with an adult content creator only deepened those concerns and the trust felt the need to once again speak out publicly.

"This statement gained strong traction, emphasising the weight of feeling on this matter. Although we believe that the club should never have gone into partnership with BC Game, we nevertheless welcome this decision to part ways."

A spokesperson for BC Game added: "BC Game confirms that its principal partner sponsorship agreement with Leicester City Football Club has ended.

"We thank Leicester City for the partnership and wish the club and its supporters all the best for the future."
