Where Hunger Games’ Jena Malone Believes Johanna Mason Is Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Ahead of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping premiere, Jena Malone, who played Johanna Mason in the original series, shared where her character is now in an exclusive E! News interview.

Jena Malone thinks her Hunger Games character is still making them pay for it.

Over 10 years after The Hunger Games: Mockingjay—Part 2 hit theaters, Malone, who played District 7 victor Johanna Mason, revealed her hopes for where she ended up after the Panem revolution.

“I would love her to be like deep in the forest,” Malone told E! News in an exclusive interview at The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping premiere in New York Nov. 16. “Training women to do somatic warrior, like healing exercises to sort of exercise their grief.”

Given her character’s fierce personality, the Saved alum admitted that Johanna would likely “have her own army basically.”

But as she’s looking ahead at Johanna’s journey, the film franchise itself is winding the clock back with the new film, set 25 years before fans met Malone’s character.

For the actress, it was “really lovely” to reunite with former costars like Jennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson, Woody Harrelson, Elizabeth Banks and Willow Shields at the Sunrise on the Reaping premiere.

“It’s kind of like a homecoming,” she mused. “I feel like you get to see long lost friends. The fanbase, I feel like their enthusiasm never changes.”

And considering the upcoming film will follow a young Haymitch Abernathy—with Joseph Zada taking over the role from Harrelson—during the 50th Hunger Games, Malone considered that an origin story for her character may feature another fan favorite: District 4 victor Finnick Odair, who was played by Sam Claflin.

“It would have to be Johanna and Finnick,” she posited. “‘Cause they were in a similar game.”

Nevertheless, Malone is admittedly more interested in the reconstruction of Panem after the revolution.

“What happened to the Capitol after?” Malone considered. “I’m interested. I love when governments get overthrown, but I’m also wildly fascinated with what comes next. How do we rebuild?”

Zada, for his part, might know what lies ahead for his character Haymitch after the upcoming prequel—which hits theaters Nov. 20—but he shared he is at a loss for words after going through his own experience alongside McKenna Grace, Whitney Peak, Jesse Plemons and more.

“I don’t know how to feel,” he told E! News in a separate interview. “It feels so full circle.”

He emphasized, “I’m just so proud—I could cry right now.”

As a lifelong Hunger Games fan, Grace similarly joked that she’s been asking herself, ‘Real or Not Real?’ since scoring her dream job as Maysilee Donner.

“It’s very strange to be here and to be part of this in any regard,” she told E! on the red carpet. “It does not feel real. This is constantly like a dream I’ll wake up from. I feel so thankful.”
