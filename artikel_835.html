Nicholas Hoult, Wife Bryana Holly Privately Welcome Twins

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Nicholas Hoult confirmed that he and Bryana Holly are now parents to four kids, with his How To Rob A Bank costar Pete Davidson revealing that the couple welcomed twins while filming the upcoming movi

Nicholas Hoult and Bryana Holly know how to privately expand their family.

The Warm Bodies actor and his longtime partner are now the parents of four, his How To Rob A Bank costar Pete Davidson confirmed in a recent interview alongside Hoult.

“He’s a great dad,” Davidson said during an interview with Entertainment Tonight, which also included Anna Sawai. “Father of four. Four under 7.”

And when Davidson—who welcomed his daughter Scottie in December 2025 with ex Elsie Hewitt—nodded toward Hoult to confirm, the 36-year-old confirmed, “Yeah.”

“Which is nuts,” the Saturday Night Live star continued. “Elsie was pregnant while we were filming and he had just had his twins and he would fly back every weekend to go see his family and that was really awesome.”

E! News has reached out to reps for Hoult and Holly regarding his latest comment but has not yet heard back.

While Hoult did not say much about welcoming twins with Holly—with whom he also shares son Joaquin, 7, and another child born in 2022—he is notoriously private about his inner world.

In fact, when the couple—who started dating in 2016—sparked speculation they had welcomed another baby following their appearance at the Oscars 2026 in March, Hoult stayed out of the conversation.

However, not everyone stayed so quiet. Responding to a user noting that Holly’s “surgeon did an amazing job,” regarding her appearance at the Vanity Fair Oscars afterparty, the fashion brand Lurelly—who designed Holly’s look—responded from its official account, “If by surgeon you mean her newborn baby. I can’t wait for a world where we root for each other rather than assume.”

Lurelly added of Holly’s appearance that “some people are naturally blessed,” with the original commenter writing back that they “would have never known she just had a baby.”

For Holly’s part, she appeared to subtly confirm that she and Hoult had welcomed more than just one baby in a separate post slamming the speculation on her breast size.

“I am seeing the comments around my chest and not that *anyone* is owed an explanation,” she wrote on Instagram Stories March 17, “but this is the product of a breast feeding mother who hasn’t fed her babies in hours ok!”

Not wholly confirming she was a mother of four, Holly added, “If you know, then you know.”

For a look into what Hoult and Holly have shared about their romance, keep reading…

Nicholas Hoult first met Bryana Holly while in California, The Favourite actor told the Evening Standard in January 2019. Although he’s kept mum on the details of their romance, the model shared a loving tribute for their 7th anniversary in 2023 featuring snaps of them from over the years.

The Great star previously shared insight into how his upbringing ultimately shaped aspects of his relationship with Bryana, including smaller moments like sharing a space together.

“I don’t feel as though I’m that possessive over things like that,” Nicholas told The Journal in November 2017. “She can put her stuff wherever she likes. My dad was an air pilot so he was flying a lot, and I grew up with my two sisters and my mum so I was used to ‘girl things’ being around and a ‘girl environment’ in the household.”

Nicholas and Bryana privately welcomed their first baby, a son named Joaquin, in 2018. While he’s largely grown up out of the public eye, he made his first public appearance in March 2024 while attending an Atlanta Hawks home game alongside his dad.

The Skins alum has given glimpses into his life as a dad and the learning curve that comes with it.

"There's a lot to take in," he told James Corden on The Late Late Show in May 2020. "But overall, it's like that time again, it brings back a lot of memories of your childhood. It makes you play again—which I'm really enjoying."

Nicholas and Bryana welcomed another baby, whose name they have not publicly shared, in 2022.

The couple have stepped out for a rare date night over the years, including a courtside outing at a game between the Los Angeles Lakers and the Philadelphia 76ers in 2023 and a stylish trip to Paris Fashion Week.

In a November 2026 interview, Hoult appeared to confirm that he and his wife had welcomed twins, agreeing as his How To Rob a Bank costar Pete Davidson detailed his life as a dad.

"He had just had his twins," Davidson explained of Hoult's dynamic while filming, "and he would fly back every weekend to go see his family and that was really awesome.”
