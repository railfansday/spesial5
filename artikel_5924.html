ICE nabs illegal immigrant days after he claims $1 million Massachusetts State Lottery prize

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Federal immigration officers arrested Ariel Antonio Velasquez-Morales in East Boston after a brief foot chase. The arrest came days after he claimed a $1 million Massachusetts State Lottery prize.

A man who smiled for a Massachusetts State Lottery photo with a $1 million check was arrested by federal immigration officers days later after a brief foot chase in East Boston.

Three ICE sources told Fox News that Ariel Antonio Velasquez-Morales, an illegal immigrant from El Salvador, ran when officers moved in to arrest him early Tuesday but was quickly caught.

Multiple officers’ body cameras captured the encounter, the sources said.

Velasquez-Morales had been wanted by ICE under a final deportation order issued in April 2014, the sources said.

He crossed the U.S. border illegally near Roma, Texas, in 2013, according to the sources.

The Massachusetts State Lottery awarded Velasquez-Morales the $1 million prize Nov. 18, and posted a photo on Facebook three days later showing him holding the oversized check.

The lottery said he bought The Lottery's winning "$2,000,000 50X Cashword" $10 scratch ticket at a market in East Boston, the same neighborhood where ICE arrested him.

Ariel Antonio Velasquez-Morales is shown in ICE custody after his arrest in East Boston, Mass., on Tuesday, six days after he won a $1 million Massachusetts State Lottery prize. (ICE Boston)

The Massachusetts State Lottery says anyone 18 or older can buy a ticket from a licensed retailer. The governor appoints two members of the five-member commission that oversees the lottery.

Fox News Digital reached out to Massachusetts Gov. Maura Healey’s office and the lottery for comment.
