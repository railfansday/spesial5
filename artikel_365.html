I Found the Best Black Friday Streaming Deals (2026): Apple TV, Disney, and Paramount Over Half Off

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Black Friday streaming deals are some of the only “free money” hacks in all of shopping, and they are one of my favorite things to highlight during this shopping bonanza. Sure, that brand-new TV you've been eyeing looks nice, but have you considered shaving 50 percent off that streaming subscription you've been wanting to sign up for?

Most of the biggest streaming services are offering Black Friday deals this year, as they do every year. Nearly all of these deals are for new and former customers only. Easy enough if you've canceled a subscription recently and want it back, but a little trickier if you aren't exactly a “new” customer. The ol' second (or third … or fourth) email address sign-up trick still works sometimes, but duplicated credit cards can sometimes flag an error.

Either way, here are the best Black Friday streaming deals I found. I'll update this list periodically through Cyber Monday as things change. And if you're looking for more deals, check out our guides to the Absolute Best Black Friday Deals and Best Black Friday TV Deals.

Deal Until December 1: $6/month for six months, billed monthly

Look, this list is alphabetical, but that works in my favor because I get to put Apple TV first. It's all killer, no filler. Widow's Bay, Pluribus, Ted Lasso, Slow Horses, Severance, Shrinking, Silo, and other series that begin with the letter S—Apple TV just has the most memorable shows. And six months is just enough time to binge most (if not all) of them. (See WIRED's Guide to the Best Apple TV+ shows.)

Deal Until December 1: $4/month for six months, billed monthly

I may have fallen off the anime train, but I do find myself subscribing to Crunchyroll for at least a month every year, just to catch up on a series or two. Either way, this is a great discount if you've been meaning to try out or catch up on some anime.

Current offer: $12/month for six months, billed monthly

Deal Until Dec. 1: $12/month for four months, billed monthly (locked in)

Watching all your local sports team’s games online in 2026 is an M.C. Escher-ian nightmare that somehow requires four different subscriptions and possibly a live-TV add-on package. Any deal that can soften that blow is a good thing.

Deal Until December 2: $3/month for two months

This is more of a trail subscription that a bonafide deal, but hey, let's all take whatever savings we can get. After all, two months might be all you need to binge the new Dexter: Resurrection or watch Avatar Aang: The Last Airbender.

Black Friday Deal: $12/year billed annually

This is a steep discount for a whole year of Starz streaming. Though, keep in mind, you're paying for the full year up front ($12), and you better set a calendar reminder 11 months from now if you don't want to end up paying $70 for Starz next year.

Deal Until December 2: $50/month for six months

If you're really trying to watch all the sports games (plus recreate that classic TV-package feel with local channels), YouTube TV's Sports+News package is a good option. You can get about $20 off each month's rate for six months with this deal.
