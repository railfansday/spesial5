England boss Thomas Tuchel angry at being dragged into race row after being told by Luis de la Fuente: 'There is no racism in Spain!' on eve of Nations League showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

By OLIVER HOLT, CHIEF SPORTS WRITER and CRAIG HOPE, CHIEF FOOTBALL REPORTER

Published: 16:03 EST, 14 November 2026 | Updated: 16:48 EST, 14 November 2026

Thomas Tuchel was dragged into a bitter race row on the eve of England's Nations League showdown with Spain.

Tuchel had been asked before England's departure for Spain about England's protocols in case their players suffered racist abuse at the Metropolitano Stadium on Sunday evening.

England players were notoriously subject to racial abuse at the Bernabeu in 2004 and England have not played in Madrid since.

But Spain manager Luis de la Fuente reacted angrily when he was told that Tuchel had addressed the subject.

'Tell Tuchel that there is no racism in Spain,' de la Fuente said. 'Just like in England. We are not racist.'

The row then deepened when a Spanish journalist told Tuchel about De La Fuente's reaction and the England coach grew exasperated with the line of questioning.

Tuchel had been asked before England's departure for Spain about England's protocols

'Tell Tuchel that there is no racism in Spain,' de la Fuente said. 'Just like in England. We are not racist.'

'I was polite enough to answer a question from your colleagues,' Tuchel said, 'and you run and ask Spain's coach and tell them that I talked about racism in Spain.

'I never talked about racism in Spain. I answered a question politely. I never expect racism. The dressing room is the most anti-racist place in the world. This is a perfect example of how you create confusion out of nothing.'

Tuchel had earlier said: 'It's important that the players know about the protocol. And then, yeah, we will give our answers if stuff like this happens. Best answers are on the pitch.

'We had a same discussion before the Serbia game [in Belgrade in September 2025]. There is a protocol in place.'

England midfielder Declan Rice said that Tuchel had not yet addressed the group about racism protocols but that they were clear about what they would do if any England players were abused.

England's game against Bulgaria in Sofia in 2019 was halted after allegations of racism against England players but the game was completed.

Thomas Tuchel has revealed that James Trafford will face world champions Spain on Sunday night, raising questions over Jordan Pickford’s status as England’s No 1.

It was assumed that Pickford, who the head coach maintains is his first choice, would keep the jersey given the identity of the opposition and the chance for England to top their Nations League group with victory here in Madrid.

However, Tuchel will hand Leeds stopper Trafford his fifth cap at the Metropolitano Stadium.

’We want to try and see how far Traff is,’ said Tuchel, who rotated his keepers during last month’s international break. ‘So he gets a highly competitive match. We had some changes in the back four in the last match (2-1 win over Croatia on Thursday), so we went for more stability and more experience with Pickers.

‘Tomorrow is a high-pressure match, on the biggest stage, and it's exactly what we need to see where we are with him (Trafford). Hopefully he's not too over-excited or overly nervous, because there's no need to be. We trust him. But it is different to play for England than to play for your club.

'To have the opportunity to play matches where it really matters is the best way to show up, and to show that you're ready.’

It was put to Tuchel that it would make more sense to play the goalkeeper he believes is his No 1 in the biggest game from this round of fixtures.

’It was the circumstances of the last match, that we had some changes in the back four, and that was the decision in the goalkeeping team,’ he said. 'Jordan is at the moment our No.1. I have a very clear picture in high-pressure moments. He played in tournaments and played for me in the World Cup.’

Meanwhile, centre back Ezri Konsa is also set to start after recovering from a knee issue.

‘I’m very pleased with how he has fitted in seamlessly at Arsenal,’ said Tuchel. ‘It just looks like he's always been there. For us, it’s amazing that he plays on the highest level, competes for every title, is in an atmosphere and in a situation where it really matters that you stay focused and you show up every three days. It's only positives for us.’

PROBABLE ENGLAND XI: Trafford; White, Quansah, Konsa, Hall; Anderson, Rice; Rogers, Jude Bellingham, Gordon; Kane
