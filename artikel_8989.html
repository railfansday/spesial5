rounded volumes and sweet color palettes shape dessert-inspired lighting collection

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

what if a lamp could be as tempting as your favorite dessert? decadent studio presents chubby, a collection of table lamps inspired by the shapes, colors and textures of sweet treats.

decadentstudio edited by christina vergopoulou November 27, 2026 2 min

Chubby is the first lighting collection by Decadent Studio, an independent design studio based in Galicia, Spain. Inspired by desserts such as pavlova, cherry pie and tangerine ice cream, the collection explores the relationship between food, design and desire. The lamp is defined by its rounded shapes, generous proportions and soft curves. Each color is inspired by a different dessert, which also gives the lamp its name. When switched off, Chubby is a sculptural object. When lit, its colors take on a different character, creating a soft, warm glow.

Each lamp is made to order in Galicia using a recyclable, plant-based biopolymer, avoiding unnecessary stock and overproduction. Every piece is individually numbered and comes with its own certificate of authenticity. Decadent Studio pays special attention to every detail, from the color of the textile cable to the back of the lamp. Chubby is designed to be seen from every angle. Behind the collection is a simple idea: designing objects that bring us pleasure, just as our favourite desserts do. Objects of desire. Guilt-free pleasures.
