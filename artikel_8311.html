Te Araroa day 19: An unplanned zero

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The morning dawned very blustery. I woke up to my tent shaking itself silly and decided I had better take it down promptly before it took itself down! I was not feeling enthusiastic about walking that day, and although it wasn’t raining at that moment, the forecast was for even more rain than the day before. I wrestled with the idea of taking a zero day there, instead of walking the 4km or so to the next campsite. But that felt like giving in, or something, and I reaffirmed my decision to move on to Uretiti Beach, a mere 4km down the trail.

In the campsite kitchen, various hikers were getting ready to hike out and face the weather. I announced my plan to the room, to be told that the campsite I had been planning to head for was closed! They were dealing with an ant infestation. That made my decision for me – I would stay where I was and take a zero day. Most people seemed keen to head off in spite of the weather. But another trio of hikers were undecided as to what to do; they were working to time and budget constraints and were not to keen on taking an unplanned zero day. They ended up staying sitting there and deferred the decision until a bit later. I sat at the table with them and got on with blog writing.

At 9am, the office opened and I went to renew my site. I mentioned that it had been a bit windy the previous night, and the lady at Reception offered me a more sheltered site, which I gratefully accepted. On my return to the kitchen, I managed to convince the others that it wouldn’t be worth walking today, and they decided to stay.

Thus commenced a very lazy day. I sat in the kitchen with the others for literally the entire day, eating snacks, writing blogs, and chatting. For a short while, the weather threatened to be not as bad as was forecast. But eventually it obliged with repeated torrential downpours and howling wind. We were all happy with our decisions to stay, but a little concerned for the friends we had sent off into the weather. And perhaps a tiny bit smug.

Over the course of the day, a couple of things of note happened;

It stopped raining for long enough at the end of the day to get a nice sunset

Look, you can see something! That’s Bream Head, where I had come from the day before
