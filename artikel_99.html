Kraków shooting latest updates: six killed and more than a dozen injured – as it happened

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk heads to the city after four women and two men are killed and more than a dozen people injured

A British national was among the six victims killed in the shooting in Krakow, Polish police chief Marek Boron said on Saturday.

“There are six fatalities: four women and two men – three Polish women, one British national, and two people who have not yet been identified,” Boron told reporters.

Six people have been killed and 16 injured after a gunman opened fire in a busy market square in Kraków on Saturday in what Poland’s defence minister, Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz, called an “act of terror” on the people of the city.

The suspect, a 25-year-old Polish citizen, opened fire with an automatic weapon from inside a car in the city’s main square at about 3pm. He was fascinated with far-right ideologies and held “very radical” and “ultra-nationalistic” views, the Polish interior minister, Marcin Kierwiński, said.

Authorities said the gunman was dressed in “military-style” clothes and used a long military-type firearm.

Four women and two men were killed, one of them a British citizen, Polish police said. Three of the victims were Polish women and the remaining two victims had not been identified.

Poland’s prime minister, Donald Tusk, said two more women and one man had also been found dead in the suspect’s home, most likely members of his family, he added.
