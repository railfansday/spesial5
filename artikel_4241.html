8 Most Unhinged NPC Inner Truths From The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Aden Carter is a Contributor at DualShockers who has been covering games professionally since 2020. He specializes in lists and guides, with a focus on RPGs, horror games, survival horror, Soulslikes, cozy/sim games, and action-adventure titles. Before joining DualShockers, Aden contributed to GameRant, XDA, Gamepur, I

You’ve spent time wandering around Hyrule in The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake, and you decided to pay the Happy Mask Salesman a visit. He gave you a few masks, you made a few sales, and you have received the Mask of Truth in return.

What does this mask do? Well, it reveals the inner thoughts of the game’s various NPCs. I am not sure if you have paid attention to Nintendo games recently, but they have been adding in some interesting NPC dialogue.

Listening to the inner truths of the game’s various NPCs can get a bit wild, but these are the most unhinged and strange thoughts to be found in Hyrule.

If you walk around the town square in Castle Town, you can find some rather colorful NPCs, but one of the most unassuming of them is the old woman who stares at the fountain. She seems so sweet, but she is actually a thief.

Listening to her inner thoughts, you see that they say, “The quality of the items people are losing has really gone downhill recently… Is that a recession indicator?”

This hits a little close to home considering many have questioned if the U.S. is entering a recession. Along with this, it is obvious that she is taking some of the goods that people have lost and keeping them for herself.

If you continue your search around the fountain in Castle Town, you can find a young couple who are dancing. While they seem rather sweet, their inner thoughts tell a completely different story.

When you read the thoughts of the woman, it will say that she hopes that the man doesn’t break her heart. It is a sad thought to be had because she is clearly worried about her future.

When you read the man’s thoughts, you see they say, “Wait… What was this one’s name again?” Clearly, this isn’t his only crush, and he is stringing this poor woman along so he has someone to dance with. Yes… dance with.

Link has always been known as the silent hero of Hyrule, but the remake appears to have given him a very brief spoken line.

Some of the guards around Castle Town aren’t very excited about their job. In fact, some of them find it boring doing patrols all the time. One guard in particular thinks about it pretty frequently.

When interacting with the guard in the room with the pots, his inner truth will say, “Guards… Soldiers… Heroes… It’s their fault there’s so much peace around here. Hm… What if I…”

The thought trails off after that, but you can clearly tell what he is thinking about. The man wants some chaos, and he is clearing thinking about being the cause of said chaos if something doesn’t happen soon.

You may have seen the bearded man wandering around Castle Town in the past and Kakariko Village in the future. His beard is magnificent, and he will definitely tell you about it. However, he is secretly questioning its cleanliness.

When you listen to his inner thoughts, they say, “I wonder if it’s true that beards are about as dirty as toilets…” For someone with such a massive beard, he shouldn’t be thinking that he is dirty, unless he wants to shave it off.

This is a massive contrast to what he will tell you when you speak to him, as he will typically say that he has a fantastic beard, and will even offer to locate Ganondorf.

Shiro is one of the many NPCs you can find around Kakariko Village, and you also help him out by rescuing him from the Gerudo as an adult. Well, he isn’t the happiest carpenter around. In fact, he is rather depressed.

His inner thoughts read, “How does one escape their own dreary life? ‘Cause I can’t do this forever.” Granted, he didn’t seem the happiest when I rescued him from the Gerudo, but I never would have guessed that he was this depressed.

Looking into his thoughts, he seems like he could use some therapy because it sounds like he has been hanging around Lumiose City in Pokémon Legends: Z-A for too long.

There’s a pair of laughing men who appear in Castle Town and Kakariko. No matter what is happening, the pair consistently laugh at one another. They typically talk about stories they find funny, with the one laughing at the other for getting caught sneaking into the castle or falling off a horse.

However, when you read their inner thoughts, the one says, “It’s exhausting wearing this forced smile all the time.” The other one will remark about how he knows his friend’s smile is fake.

It’s sad that they continue to laugh constantly. Maybe they should just try to chill out for once and give up on the laughter. Are they cursed? Maybe they are cursed.

Nintendo has expanded one of the original Zelda: Ocarina of Time's most heartbreaking side stories.

If you have explored Kakariko Village, you are for sure aware of the cucco lady. All of her cuccos get out of the pen, and you must go rescue them for a reward. In the future, she is living with a questionable character.

If you use the Mask of Truth while you are an adult, you will catch her inner thoughts. They say, “That guy who lives with me always makes the most intense eye contact. He must really love cuccos…”

What she is describing does not sound healthy at all. In fact, it sounds almost like something bad is going to happen and she is just waiting for the inevitable. Maybe she should choose a different place to live or kick the guy out.

There is an old man who likes to wander around Castle Town. He will go in and out of shops, but mostly he will walk around the fountain. Just be careful listening to his thoughts because he has a particular woman on his mind.

His inner thoughts read, “This kid… The mask’s neat and all, but I’m more interested in finding a particular damsel…” While it doesn’t seem so bad at first, if you talk to him, you can piece things together.

Talking to him, he will make remarks about how he is looking for a lady who can make a potion, and will gawk at how beautiful she is. He will also casually talk about how we are at the age to be thinking about that.

From hearing the iconic Zelda theme again to facing Dark Link and climbing Ganon's Tower, these 10 moments brought back the childhood magic of Ocarina of Time.
