Warhammer Survivors sees the grimdark future take on the Vampire Survivors formula, out now

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

With Warhammer having gone from a niche tabletop board game to a bona fide cultural phenomenon, that popularity also brings dozens of video game spinoffs. But Auroch Digital aren't going the shooter or strategy route, as today their long-awaited collaboration with Poncle lands on iOS and Android, as Warhammer Survivors launches for mobile!

Warhammer Survivors sees the Vampire Survivors format translated both to the universe of Warhammer 40,000 and Warhammer: Age of Sigmar. As iconic characters from both series, you'll fight your way through hordes of enemies, across a variety of stages, and take on powerful bosses with increasingly ludicrous, game-changing weaponry of your own.

And, of course, Poncle's influence is readily felt in the many, many unlockable secrets and easter eggs you can find throughout. Whether that's powerful new abilities or stages which take you from the battlegrounds of Warhammer to the hobby table, as you duke it out between pots of paint, brushes and glue.

Warhammer Survivors looks to be the perfect game for fans of both Warhammer and Vampire Survivors, offering a whole new take on a universe that (let's be honest) has been stuck in a bit of a rut of strategy, while offering even more weapons and characters to unlock for longtime fans of Poncle's iconic bullet heaven series.

So jump in now, and benefit from a limited-time 10% discount for Warhammer Survivors on iOS and Android!
