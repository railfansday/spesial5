Alonso ready to sell £47m Chelsea signing for a Barca star

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Chelsea transfer news: Xabi Alonso wants Barcelona right-back Jules Koundé at Chelsea and is ready to sell Marco Palestra, his £47m summer signing, to make room, according to El Nacional. The outlet says Alonso has pushed the case, a claim it credits to FourFourTwo, and that Palestra has Serie A interest from Juventus, AC Milan and Napoli, though it mentions no bid.

Barcelona would reportedly listen to offers of around €50m, roughly £43m, because Hansi Flick doesn’t treat Koundé as untouchable. The 28-year-old has started three of six LaLiga games this season after an ankle sprain on 17 October kept him out for over a month. El Nacional calls him a backup behind Eric García and says he’d welcome the move.

Koundé completes 93.9% of his passes, wins 58.8% of his duels and has 58 France caps, a composed defender who could play right-back or on the right of a back three. His body is the worry: a hamstring problem in March, then an ankle injury that lingered.

Palestra won Serie A’s Defender of the Year award at Cagliari, and his 81 completed dribbles topped every defender in Europe’s top five leagues. A thigh injury delayed his Chelsea debut until October, and El Nacional puts him at 202 minutes across six matches. Alonso prefers Josh Acheampong at right-back and, per BBC Sport, uses Palestra from the bench before Malo Gusto. He scored once in 37 league games, and his crossing remains modest.

Not as a swap. Palestra is a wing-back who beats opponents on the outside, while Koundé is a passer and cover defender best placed on the right of three centre-backs. Selling one doesn’t remove the need for the other, and Chelsea’s real shortage is right-sided cover: Reece James is injured and has been used in midfield, and Acheampong, a centre-back by trade, is filling in at right-back.

Koundé would solve that, but Gusto is the sale to make. He has lost his place, is reportedly open to a January exit, and Chelsea priced him at £75m in June, so even a discounted fee would cover much of Koundé’s £43m.

Selling Palestra after 202 minutes would mean writing off a player Alonso recruited and said needs time to adapt, to buy a 28-year-old with a muscle-injury record. Chelsea were fifth, 11 points behind Arsenal, before Leeds visited, without European football. Buy Koundé if the medical is clean, sell Gusto and keep Palestra.
