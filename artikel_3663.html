'Beverly Hills, 90210' Actress Kimmarie Johnson’s Cause of Death Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

New details surrounding Beverly Hills, 90210 actress Kimmarie Johnson’s death have been revealed.

According to a death certificate obtained by People on Friday, November 20, Johnson’s cause of death is listed as cardiopulmonary arrest, with an underlying cause of metastatic granulosa cell ovarian tumor with peritoneal carcinomatosis.

The National Library of Medicine describes cardiopulmonary arrest as the cessation of adequate heart function and respiration and results in death. Oftentimes, the condition is found in patients with coronary artery disease.

News broke on November 5 that Johnson had died at the age of 59. Family members told TMZ that the actress and model had a “heart event” at her home in Los Angeles on October 27.

She was later rushed to Cedars-Sinai Hospital, where doctors diagnosed her with a “heart aneurysm condition.” Johnson died on November 3, according to her death certificate.

“She radiated a light so strong and bright,” Johnson’s niece Rhonda Scott told People. “People who came in contact with her felt nothing but positive energy. We will miss her tremendously and will continue to honor her life forever.”

Johnson began her acting career in 1998 when she appeared in one episode of Beverly Hills, 90210, as Judy.

She later appeared in the film Dude, Where’s My Car? and the 2000 TV series V.I.P.

Outside of acting, Johnson worked as a flight attendant before winning the Miss Pennsylvania USA beauty pageant in 1991. At the 1993 Miss USA beauty pageant, Johnson finished in the top five.

“I come from very humble beginnings. I’ve seen bad things in my neighborhood. … I saw an ad in the newspaper about winning $2,000 in the Miss Pennsylvania. I decided to enter, and I won,” she recalled to Voyage LA in August 2025. “Thereafter, with no training or coaching, I found myself in the finals at The Miss USA.”

In 2011, Johnson  — who welcomed a son in 2003 — founded the skincare and beauty line SkinGlow by Kimmarie.

She was working as a model at the time of her death, last appearing on a digital cover of Grazia Slovenia magazine in August.
