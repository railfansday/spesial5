These Pokemon Anniversary Fender Guitars Have A Secret Weapon: Game Boy Sounds

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Pokemon franchise turned 30 this year, and while you'll have to wait until next year for a new mainline game installment, you can celebrate that anniversary with something that's much cooler: Rock-and-roll-type electric guitars.

The Pokemon Company revealed that it teamed up with guitar manufacturer Fender to produce a Stratocaster model inspired by Pokemon Red, and a Telecaster model inspired by Pokemon Green. Each guitar features an aesthetic that pays tribute to the original Pokemon Game Boy adventures, including pixel art pulled from the games, graphics, and color overlays for an extra pop.

The guitars really shine with a special toggle that warps the music they produce into an 8-bit retro sound that’s reminiscent of vintage gaming hardware. The neck also pays homage to Pokemon, with a special design that commemorates the collaboration between Fender and Pokemon. Just remember: No Stairway.

As you'd expect, these won't be cheap. While US pricing and availability will be confirmed later on the Pokemon Center retail site, in Japan, these will retail for around $2,600 each. You'll also need to enter a lottery to stand a chance at winning the option to purchase one of these Fenders, which should prevent the items from being pounced on en masse by Pokemon scalpers.

This year has seen several promotions kick off to celebrate 30 years of Pokemon, and one of the biggest involved Krispy Kreme. The doughnut chain's boxes of digestible pocket monsters proved highly popular, and unsurprisingly, Pokemon scalpers wasted no time trying to flip those treats and packaging for cash online. At least the legally distinct Pokemoan sex toys re-released this year proved to be somewhat immune to scalper efforts.
