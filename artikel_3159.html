Is Moderna's Cancer Vaccine a Game Changer for the Stock?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The healthcare stock has hit multi-year highs this year amid positive results from its personalized cancer vaccine.

The market has been wildly excited about Moderna (MRNA -3.63%) this year, as it has soared more than 600% since January. The stock has been red hot on news that the cancer vaccine it has been working on with healthcare giant Merck has been shown to be highly effective in clinical trials.

Moderna's market cap has swelled to close to $90 billion, up from less than $12 billion at the start of 2026. Is the company's cancer vaccine truly a game changer for Moderna, or is hype and speculation fueling its returns this year?

On Aug. 19, Moderna announced phase 3 clinical trial results that would send its stock skyrocketing. The results showed that its cancer vaccine, intismeran was highly effective in treating melanoma patients when paired with Keytruda, Merck's top cancer drug. The combined treatment reduced the risk of death and of the cancer spreading or coming back, and was more effective than Keytruda on its own.

While approval hasn't happened yet, it does appear highly likely. But there are multiple things to consider here. The stock's mammoth price movement may appear to price in the potential for intismeran to treat other cancers as well, which is by no means a certainty. Plus, producing a personalized cancer vaccine at significant scale can be costly and time-consuming. And with the companies splitting the profits on the treatment, that will also diminish just how much profit may flow through to Moderna's bottom line in the long run.

This is why, although the treatment is incredibly promising and could unlock significant growth potential for Moderna, many investors may have bid the stock up too aggressively, pushing its valuation to an unsustainable level.

Intismeran has the potential to certainly be a game changer for Moderna, but it's not there yet. It's not approved to treat a single cancer, and the stock appears priced as if it'll treat several.

The danger is that investors appear to be getting ahead of themselves and paying too much for the healthcare stock when there are still so many question marks about just how profitable intismeran will be, given the operational challenges that will come with producing a personalized cancer vaccine at scale.

I wouldn't buy Moderna's stock at its current price point, as it may be approaching a peak, if it hasn't already hit one. The stock could have considerable downside risk at this point.
