Fresh Will Lankshear, England update will have Middlesbrough fans laughing at Spurs

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Although it is still fairly early in the Championship season, many onlookers are now starting to see exactly why Middlesbrough were viewed as promotion material under Kim Hellberg in pre-season.

Middlesbrough's Swedish head coach has enjoyed a fine 12-month period at the Riverside Stadium, even if last season ended in heartbreak on two counts.

The style of play at the Riverside Stadium has been completely transformed within said timeframe, and across the opening three months of 2026/27, one man has finally solved the club's biggest achilles heel from last season's final weeks - the ability to score goals.

That is, of course, none other than Will Lankshear, who was one of several high-profile names to have moved to Teesside throughout the summer, doing so from Tottenham Hotspur.

The 21-year-old was no stranger to the Championship at the time of his initial £10m switch from the North London club, but his remarkable adjustment to life at the Riverside could be rewarded with the biggest compliment.

Spurs sold Will Lankshear to Boro in the summer, but, they have clauses written into the contract to protect their position in the future.

The aforementioned deal could still rise as high as £20m with add-ons, meaning Spurs can still benefit financially through their academy graduate's success.

However, right now, no one is benefiting more from Lankshear's goalscoring prowess and all-round game than Hellberg, with his recent double against Charlton Athletic taking the 21-year-old's tally to 13 goals in as many Championship games.

There were concerns over his fitness for Wednesday night's clash at Wrexham, but after being given the all-clear, it has been revealed that a senior England scout will oversee Lankshear's performance on Thomas Tuchel's behalf, with the Three Lions boss set to confirm his latest squad on Friday morning.

Such feats come less than two months after Lankshear marked his Under-21s debut with a goal in the 4-2 win over Kazakhstan at Molineux, further highlighting his remarkable short-term progress.

By mid-October, Lankshear's rapid ascent into double figures was only matched by Manchester City's Erling Haaland, and given his remarkable goalscoring feats, comparisons have already been drawn between him and the 2026 Ballon d'Or winner, Harry Kane, who also began his stellar career in North London.

"I can understand the comparisons with Harry Kane," claimed former Spurs and England goalkeeper, Paul Robinson. "He has headed down a similar career path to Harry and, physically, he has a similar stature as well.

"Like Kane, he looks like a player who is developing a little later, compared to others who are Premier League regulars before the end of their teens.

Spurs' recent fortunes have certainly been put under the microscope, and whilst Roberto De Zerbi's side have won their last three Premier League games, the Italian's attackers are still yet to fully step up to the plate.

Dominic Solanke, in fact, has only scored 24 goals in his first 76 appearances for the club, with one of those coming in the current campaign's opening weeks.

It was discussed at length whether Lankshear should have been retained at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium after his 12-goal haul at Oxford United last season, and given the latest set of developments, De Zerbi may be having second thoughts over comments made over the transfer in September.

“Three strikers, how many strikers do you want in the squad? Three strikers I think are enough. To answer about the player in Middlesbrough, no (regrets)."

The Championship has been a breeding ground for homegrown talent in recent years, with many of Tuchel's England regulars starring in the second tier at some point of their careers.

However, they have, by and large, only gained national recognition since featuring regularly in one of Europe's major top-flights, but Lankshear could be about to buck such a trend.

The winger earned two caps for the Three Lions before switching allegiances to the Ivory Coast, and during the season in which his call-up was earned, his remarkable performances helped Crystal Palace reach the Premier League, which is another dream Lankshear will hope to replicate with Middlesbrough.
