Head of Ecosystem at Supabase

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Supabase is the Postgres development platform, built by developers for developers. We aim to empower developers by providing a complete backend solution including Database, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions, Realtime, and Vector Search. All services are deeply integrated and designed for growth.

We're looking for a Head of Ecosystem to lead the teams that make Supabase the backend for the platforms and tools developers already use. That covers AI app builders and coding agents, integrations with the developer tools teams rely on, and the Supabase Marketplace. You'll build this org from the ground up and shape how Supabase extends beyond its own platform.

You'll also drive hiring, onboarding, and career development while maintaining high engineering standards.

Build and lead a new engineering org across platform partners, integrations, and Marketplace, hiring EMs and engineers as it grows

Own the technical relationship with the platforms building on Supabase, from integration health to escalations

Set and maintain clear roadmaps that balance partner needs with platform priorities

Partner with Product, Partnerships, and Sales to define and execute our ecosystem strategy

Ship a unified, self-serve surface for integrations and Marketplace

Maintain high engineering standards: roadmap hygiene, issue tracking, on-call, incident response, and responsiveness to partners

Proven experience leading multiple engineering teams, including managing other managers

Experience building and running platform, API, or integration surfaces that third parties depend on in production

Technical credibility with partner engineering teams, and the judgment to say no

Experience building teams or an org from a small base, including hiring and developing engineering managers

Strong organizational and execution skills: setting roadmaps, driving accountability, and keeping teams moving under uncertainty

Experience managing remote, globally distributed teams

Excellent cross-functional communication skills, especially with Product and commercial teams

Have experience with OAuth, platform APIs, or app marketplaces

Have worked on a developer platform's partner or app ecosystem

Have worked with AI app builders or coding agents

We hire globally. We believe you can do your best work from anywhere. There are no Supabase offices, but we provide a WeWork membership or co-working allowance you can use anywhere in the world.

Every team member receives ESOP (equity ownership) in the company. We want everyone to share in the upside of what we’re building together.

Use this budget to set up your ideal work environment—laptop, monitor, headphones, or whatever helps you do your best work.

Supabase covers 100% of health insurance for employees and 80% for dependents, wherever you are. Your wellbeing and your family’s health are important to us.

Once a year, the entire company gathers in a new city for a week of connection, collaboration, and fun. It’s a highlight of our year.

We operate asynchronously and trust you to manage your own time. You know what needs to be done and when.

Every team member receives an annual education allowance to spend on learning—courses, books, conferences, or anything that supports your growth.

Supabase was born-remote and open-source-first. We believe our globally distributed team is our secret weapon in building tools developers love.

Over $1B raised (including our $500M Series F)

We move fast, build in public, and use what we ship. If it’s in your project, we probably use it in ours too. We believe deeply in the open-source ecosystem and strive to support—not replace—existing tools and communities.

New remote jobs delivered to your inbox, every week.

Location independence is the future of work. It serves companies with best talent from around the globe. And benefits employees with a greater work life balance and the ability to work in their pyjamas.- Nithur, founder.
