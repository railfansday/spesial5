GTA 6 Online files discovered and show major changes from GTA 5

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Grand Theft Auto fans waiting for GTA Online to launch in GTA 6 might not have long to wait, with multiplayer settings already being found.

With the launch of Grand Theft Auto 6 finally in sight, a number of cards that Rockstar Games have been keeping close to their chest are slowly starting to be revealed – especially as the pre-load period begins.

Players have found new tracks from the game’s soundtrack, discovered how many achievements GTA 6 has, and have begun to pick up hints about the story through a number of new screenshots.

One of the biggest question marks, though, centres on GTA Online. It isn’t set to launch right away – unless Rockstar changes things. However, it could release sooner than some think.

PlayStation Size – which initially revealed that GTA 6 will take up 210GB of your console’s memory – noted that there are settings in the files for GTA Online already.

“Grand Theft Auto VI supports :intentType”: “joinSession” intentType”: “launchActivity” intentType”: “launchMultiplayerActivity,”” they posted on X on November 8.

Additionally, TexFunz added that GTA 6’s main game and online can be installed separately, which is different from how Grand Theft Auto 5 operated.

“GTAV does not separate assets, so it’s new. This allows you to uninstall online or story mode at a later point so you can save some space, and re-download them whenever you like,” the insider posted on X (Formerly Twitter).

As noted, Rockstar has been quiet on all things GTA Online in GTA 6. It hasn’t been mentioned at all in any of their trailers or social media posts.

Twitch CEO Dan Clancy said that he had spoken to the developers about GTA Online, claiming that it is releasing “next year” after spending a “lot of time” talking to Rockstar about it.

Take-Two boss Strauss Zelnick said that the publishers have shown a “willingness” to support games when players are still interested, noting that the current version of GTA Online could live on alongside GTA 6 as well.

Ultimately, it will be Rockstar’s call and they won’t want to follow the buggy launch of GTA Online in GTA 5. Yet, there are reportedly more monetization plans in place already.
