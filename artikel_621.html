T-Mobile is giving away the Apple iPhone 18 Pro for free: How to qualify

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

TL;DR: The Apple iPhone 18 Pro is free when you switch to T-Mobile and bring your number on a qualifying T-Mix plan. The Apple iPhone 18 Pro Max is under $100 when you switch and bring your number on a qualifying T-Mix plan.

The iPhone Duo launched, and the whole world went wild for Apple's first-ever foldable. The launch had some serious issues, but the iPhone Duo still dominated the tech conversation for weeks. It had a big impact, good and bad.

Now that the noise has died down, the big question for shoppers heading into Black Friday is whether to buy the iPhone 18 Pro or the iPhone Duo.

It's a tough decision, but T-Mobile is helping you make up your mind. For a limited time, you can get your hands on the Apple iPhone 18 Pro for free with T-Mobile. To qualify for this wild deal, you simply need to switch to T-Mobile and bring your number on a qualifying T-Mix plan. You can get the iPhone 18 Pro for free if you have an eligible phone in any condition on a T-Mix plan.

The full savings are distributed as monthly credits over a 36-month payment plan. If you leave T-Mobile before the end of the three-year contract, you will be charged the remaining balance. You also need to pay local sales tax on the phone's full retail value upfront, plus a standard $35 device connection fee per line at checkout.

Score the Apple iPhone 18 Pro for free this weekend with T-Mobile.
