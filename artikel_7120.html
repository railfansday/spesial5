72andSunny Appoints WPP Exec to Lead New York Office After Layoffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

72andSunny has announced a new CEO to lead its New York office after a layoff in October that impacted its C-suite.

Rebecca Pagis joins as CEO of 72andSunny New York from WPP, where she was North American president of design agency Design Bridge and Partners. She fills a role previously held by Ali Cornford, who was let go along with chief creative officer Juliana Cobb and chief strategy officer Rich Santiago.

72andSunny declined to comment on how many staff were let go in total. ADWEEK understands the agency currently has fewer than 100 staff in the office, and less than a quarter were impacted.
