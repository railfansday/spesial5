Hadrian Announces Iron Bowl Partnership with The University of Alabama and Auburn University

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Three-year partnership connects collegiate athletics with advanced engineering education, industry mentorship, and experiential learning to build pathways into America's most sophisticated manufacturing environments

CHEROKEE, Ala., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Hadrian, the advanced manufacturing company building highly automated factories for America, today announced a historic first that brings together two of Alabama's proudest traditions â college football and American manufacturing. The Iron Bowl partnership with The University of Alabama and Auburn University extends beyond football to matchups across multiple sports and into the academic community, creating meaningful learning opportunities through engineering education, mentorship, and hands-on experience in advanced manufacturing.

Beginning in 2027, the three-year agreement covers matchups on both campuses, making Hadrian the first company to partner with both universities using the Iron Bowl name. The partnership was facilitated by the University of Alabama Athletics' and Auburn University Athletics' respective multimedia rights holders, Learfield and Playfly Sports.

"Alabama has a remarkable legacy of building America's industrial strength, and we're excited to help shape what comes next. Few things bring Alabamians together quite like their pride in the storied rivalry of the Iron Bowl," said Chris Power, Founder and CEO of Hadrian. "We want to build on that passion and create the same excitement around engineering and advanced manufacturing. By partnering with two institutions so deeply rooted in the state's identity, we hope to inspire a new generation to take pride in building the next chapter of American reindustrialization."

The Iron Bowl takes its name from Birmingham's historic iron industry. Hadrian is carrying that tradition forward at Factory 4 in Cherokee, which opened in March 2026 to expand production capacity supporting the Navy's Virginia- and Columbia-class submarine programs. The partnership builds on Hadrian's roots in the Shoals, introducing its manufacturing careers to students and families across Alabama.

Ahead of the agreement's 2027 start, Hadrian will have a presence at the November 28, 2026, Iron Bowl football game alongside Build Submarines, this year's official game sponsor.

The partnership was announced at a press conference at Factory 4 with Hadrian and university leadership.

Hadrian is rebuilding America's industrial base with highly automated factories that combine process engineering, AI, and robotics. Its mission is to help space and defense manufacturers produce full programs at scale in the U.S. while giving American workers the tools to compete globally.
