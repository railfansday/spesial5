Barclays warns U.S. economy hinges on AI — other pillars irrelevant if trade unwinds

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The bank forecasts U.S. GDP growth of 2.4% in 2026 and 2.1% in 2027, with business investment tied to AI carrying much of the load

Barclays warned in its 2027 outlook that any weakness in AI-related investment could dampen U.S. economic prospects.

Barclays strategists named consumer strength, company results and AI capital spending as the pillars supporting the U.S. economy. They said the other two would be "irrelevant" if the AI trade unwinds, according to MarketWatch.

Barclays said the global macroeconomic outlook for 2027 hinges on two variables, energy prices and AI. The bank said so much of the global economy's resilience rests on the AI infrastructure build-out that any slowdown would have significant repercussions for growth prospects. It cited recent jitters around data center financing as evidence the risk is no longer theoretical.

Barclays expects growth in capital spending to slow after 2026’s strong pace. Combined with higher interest rates weighing on consumption, that should slow global growth modestly in 2027, the bank said.

For the U.S., Barclays forecasts GDP growth of 2.4% in 2026 and 2.1% in 2027. With real wage growth turning negative, consumption is expected to slow, leaving business investment, much of it tied to AI, to carry much of the load.

Barclays listed AI as one of three key risks to the U.S. outlook, along with energy prices and the bond market. A slowdown in hyperscalers' spending would ease pressure on long-end yields but would remove one of the U.S. economy's key growth engines, the bank said.

In a separate outlook published on Thursday, Barclays said markets are correcting rather than collapsing. The bank said AI has added more than a percentage point to U.S. growth this year and that cloud providers remain compute-constrained while cloud revenue growth is strong. It described recent weakness as a mid-cycle reset in valuations rather than the end of the cycle, and called AI returns and overcapacity longer-term risks. Barclays Research remains overweight equities versus fixed income.

The warning follows a selloff in chip and data center stocks on Nov. 5, when the Cboe Volatility Index jumped to 28.09 and the Nasdaq $NDAQ Composite fell more than 5%.

Former Securities and Exchange Commission Chair Gary Gensler has called the AI boom a bubble likely to burst and drag the economy into recession before the 2028 presidential election. Ed Yardeni, president of Yardeni Research, called that scenario "plausible" but disagreed that a burst is coming.
