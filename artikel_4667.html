1.2 Million Ford Explorers Under Federal Probe Over Reports of 3rd-Degree Burns from Airbags

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The seat-mounted side airbags ⁠have allegedly caused serious ​injuries, including to law enforcement officers.

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration has opened a probe into 1.2 million Ford Explorer vehicles over allegations that seat-mounted side airbags have accidentally deployed and given passengers third-degree burns.

NHTSA’s Office of Defects Investigation opened the investigation on Friday, November 6, after receiving eight Vehicle Owner Questionnaires and four Early Warning Reporting “Death and Injury” reports describing burn injuries following side airbag deployment in model year 2021-2026 Ford Explorer vehicles. The reports claim that crashes involving deployment of the seat-mounted side airbag have resulted in second- or third-degree burns for both drivers and front passengers.

Many law enforcement agencies across the country have adopted the Ford Explorer Police Interceptor as their vehicle of choice. As a result, NHTSA has received reports of injuries to law enforcement officers. A total of 10 injuries have been reported to the federal safety regulator.

A preliminary investigation has been opened, and the Office of Defects Investigation will evaluate the scope, frequency, and potential safety-related consequences of the alleged defect.

“Safety is a top priority at Ford. We are working with NHTSA to support its investigation,” a Ford spokesperson said to Road & Track.
