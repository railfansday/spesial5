‘DWTS’ Pro Jenna Johnson Reveals She Broke Her Nose Less Than 24 Hours Before Grammy Night

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Just weeks after Ezra Sosa nearly broke his nose while practicing with partner Amber Glenn, another Dancing With the Stars pro is dealing with a similar injury…except with an actual fracture this time. On November 10, just hours before the premiere of Grammy Night on DWTS season 35, Jenna Johnson took to social media to share the bad news.

According to Jenna (who is partnered with Harry Shum Jr. this season), the incident took place during camera blocking the night before. “So last night I definitely broke my nose during camera blocking,” she explained, pointing to the swelling in her face.

She also captioned the post, “Salsa: 1. Jenna’s Nose: 0.” But here’s what fans really want to know: Is Jenna still dancing tonight despite her injury?

While she didn’t explicitly say whether or not she’s still dancing, Jenna did add “Happy Show Day” to her caption, so it seems the show will go on!

Oh, and Harry also took to her comments section to jokingly write, “WHO DID THIS TO YOU?? ….oh…sorry…BUT YOU ARE A TOTAL BADASS!!!! 💪🏼”

Luckily, Jenna only has to dance one number tonight, as she and Harry earned immunity from this week’s dance-off after scoring a 40/40 for their jazz performance to “Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious” from Mary Poppins. Instead of going head-to-head with another pairing, Harry and Jenna will automatically get dance-off bonus points added to their score.

And what is their routine this week, you ask? The pro and the Grey’s Anatomy star are tackling a salsa to “Die With a Smile” by Lady Gaga and Bruno Mars.

Here’s hoping that Jenna’s smile isn’t affected by her broken nose!!
