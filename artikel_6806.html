What Jan Ravnik Actually Said Under His Breath Amid Claims He Swore at Julianne Hough During ‘Dancing With the Stars’

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Today in Dancing with the Stars drama (really, more like “drama”), some corners of the internet seem to think Jan Ravnik swore at Julianne Hough while waiting to get his scores during Disney Night.

The moment in question happened right after Tatyana Ali and Jan performed their contemporary routine, and rather than go straight to scores, Julianne cut to commercial break. Jan was filmed muttering something under his breath, which some fans thought was “f*cking bitch.” Which, pretty sure, nope, absolutely did not happen!

As many commenters/lip-readers have pointed out, Jan is very clearly saying “son of a bitch” as a jokey reaction to the commercial break cut. Just a cute funny moment, everyone!

Though, the misunderstanding about Jan’s comment might be because people are convinced he and Julianne have beef over Jan not being nominated for Sexiest Pro. But anyone who’s watched these moments knows they’re clearly joking and there’s no drama to be found:

Reminder: the remaining couples will be competing in dance-offs to live performances from Brandy, Monica, Maren Morris, and Sheryl Crow. Well, the remaining couples except for Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson, who have immunity from the dance-off portion of the evening thanks to getting the highest scores during Disney Week!
