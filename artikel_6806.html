‘TMZ’ Confirms That [SPOILER] From ‘Love Is Blind’ Season 11 Got Divorced in June

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The paperwork reveals exactly when Love Is Blind was filmed.

Only one couple from Love Is Blind Season 11 ended up saying “I do” this season, and it looks like they’re already done. Sarah and John got married in front of their friends and family in the show’s finale, but TMZ just confirmed that—according to Massachusetts court documents—Sarah filed for divorce on June 4.

The official reason? “An irretrievable breakdown of their marriage.” The docs go on to confirm that Sarah asked to split their finances, go back to her former name, and asked a judge “to bar John from restricting her freedom.”

The paperwork also gives us an exact filming date for Love Is Blind amid fan speculation that the show was in production well over a year ago. And...yep! Turns out Sarah and John got married all the way back on March 27, 2025, and then moved into separate homes a little over a year later on April 15, 2026.

So, what went wrong in their relationship? Fans saw them disagree on several major political issues throughout the season, but according to spoilers from Reality Receipts podcaster Rikkii Wise (@storytimewithrikkii), John had an “emotional affair” with an escort during the marriage. Truly yikes.

The exes will presumably get into the specifics during the Love Is Blind reunion, which was reportedly filmed a few weeks ago and airs this weekend on Netflix.
