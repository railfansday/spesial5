Teyana Taylor Does Tuxedo Dressing in a White Corset and Silky Oversize Pants

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

She meant business at the NYC premiere of All’s Fair season two

It turns out that an All’s Fair press tour is even better the second time around. Last night, the cast of Hulu’s star-studded legal drama hit the red carpet to promote their sophomore season, which drops tomorrow and promises even more of the high-stakes courtroom drama and glamorous fits we saw in the first installment. Once again, the Ryan Murphy-created series features a core cast of Kim Kardashian, Teyana Taylor, Sarah Paulson, Naomi Watts, and Niecy Nash—all of whom graced the New York City premiere in striking fashion.

In the show, Taylor portrays an aspiring lawyer, named Milan, who works under Allura Grant (Kardashian) at a high-stakes, all-women divorce firm in Los Angeles. On the red carpet, she made a nod to her role and embraced her inner corporate side—putting her signature stamp on suiting.

Pulled from Nicolas Oakwell Couture’s Fall 2026 collection, which referenced the work of couturier Charles James and the twentieth-century women he dressed (think Marlene Dietrich), the singer-slash-actor’s tailored look offered a fresh take on tuxedo dressing.

With a sculpted, exaggerated silhouette, the layered ensemble featured a sheer tulle button-down pinned with a little white bow tie and tucked into a white corset bodice with stylized bra cups and contoured hips. A cropped black tuxedo jacket with sharp shoulders and rounded lapels sat on top, and the whole thing was paired with oversize black trousers. These statement bottoms were crafted from a wool-silk blend with an asymmetrical waistband and a massive bow detail.

Taylor, who previously wore Nicolas Oakwell Couture to the Critics Choice Association’s Celebration of Black Cinema and Television event last December, worked with stylist duo Wayman Bannerman and Micah McDonald for the occasion.
