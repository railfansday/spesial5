New Limited-Time Pokémon Champions Distribution Is Now Available

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Just in time for the Latin America International Championships, The Pokémon Company has released a new limited-time distribution event for Pokémon Champions (thanks, Serebii).

The code for this one is LA1CTRA1N1NG27, and it'll reward all those who enter it with two Training Tickets — so you can make sure that your in-game team is in tip-top condition just in time to watch the Latin American pros go head-to-head later this month.

The distribution is live now and will be sticking around until the end of the regional competition on 22nd November at 11:59pm UTC.

Serebii Update: A new code has been released for Pokémon Champions offering 2 Training tickets Code: LA1CTRA1N1NG27 Runs until November 22nd at 23:59 UTC www.serebii.net

The Latin America competitions will once again be using Champions as its main VG battler, with the Switch and mobile app taking the place of Scarlet and Violet earlier this year.

Those who want to watch along can do so via the official Pokémon YouTube channel, where much of the event will be livestreamed.
