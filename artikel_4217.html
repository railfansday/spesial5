30 Days: Outbreak is the new zombie extraction shooter from Raid: Shadow Legends' Plarium

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Like it or loathe it, Raid: Shadow Legends has been a major boon for developer Plarium and propelled them to stratospheric success. But now, the developer is taking their latest project in a whole new direction, as zombie-themed extraction shooter 30 Days: Outbreak hits soft launch for Poland and Ukraine.

Set during the middle of a zombie plague, 30 Days: Outbreak sees you take on the role of a survivor venturing into the city to grab loot and supplies. Of course, you not only have to cope with all the zombies, but also other survivors willing to kill you too to survive.

Admittedly, I think the last thing the world needs is another extraction shooter. But aside from the battle royale genre, I'd struggle to think of one that's really made an impact on mobile. So maybe that's a niche that Plarium can exploit?

Either way, 30 Days: Outbreak is a big change of pace for them, going from turn-based RPG to third-person shooter. With 30 Days: Outbreak currently only in soft launch for Poland and Ukraine on Android, you'll want to keep your eyes open to see when it expands further into soft launch!
