PSA: Want To Learn Link's Ocarina Tunes? Nintendo's Got The Sheet Music For All Of Them

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Whether you have been playing Ocarina of Time on Switch 2, watching the trailers, or just been in the ether of its release, you almost certainly have an ocarina melody or two stuck in your head right now. For those who want to channel their earworm into a lesson, Nintendo has shared the sheet music for each of Link's in-game tunes, so you can stop whistling Zelda's Lullaby and learn to play it instead.

Nintendo previously announced that it'd share music how-tos via the Zelda Notes app, so you could work out how to play the tunes on any real-world ocarinas you may have accumulated over the years. But look, all of them are also available on the game's Japanese website, so you don't need to worry about unlocking them in-game before you start learning!

Alongside the sheet music, the website also includes specific ocarina finger placement to follow along with on the titular instrument.

Oh yes, and just in case these 12 tunes aren't already ingrained in your heads, the site (and app) also features a handy 'Play' button, so you can listen to how each of them is meant to sound before taking a stab at 'em yourself.

Of course, you might be waiting to learn any of these until you have one of the official tie-in ocarinas in your hands. Pre-orders for HORI's electric model went live back in September, and we are now seeing orders slowly trickle out for the playable version in select regional Nintendo stores.
