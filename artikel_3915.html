Chasing Kaleidorider closed ‘Doki Test’ begins December 17

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Fizzglee Studio will host the closed “Doki Test” for “3D RIDER girl dokidoki romance RPG” Chasing Kaleidorider starting December 17 across PC, iOS, and Android, the developer announced. Sign-ups are available here. The game has also surpassed two million global pre-registrations.

During the test, you will dive into a fully upgraded audiovisual romance experience alongside the gorgeous RIDERS! Meanwhile, Doki Test sign-ups and official website pre-registrations are running simultaneously. Join fellow NAVIGATORs for an adrenaline-fueled, heart-racing romance with the girls!

Launching globally on December 17, 2026, this is a limited-capacity, non-monetized closed beta (all data will be wiped after the test).

In this test build, you get the chance to meet and connect with 16 stunning RIDERS, take them on romantic dates, and ultimately confess your feelings to your closest match to unlock sweet everyday life together.

Over 300 minutes of free ASMR voiced by top-tier voice actresses and a gripping main storyline brought to life with high-spec 3D cutscenes await you. Available across Android, iOS, and PC, this test lets you jump into your romantic journey with the RIDERS wherever and however you please.

Stepping into Terminus, you will dive into a wealth of content—fighting alongside the RIDERS to repel invading HYSTERIA while deepening your emotional bonds through combat and daily moments, uncovering each girl’s hidden secrets along the way.

Once the Chasing Kaleidorider Doki Test wraps up, the official global launch will follow shortly after. Stay tuned!
