If You See a Taco Bell With THIS On It, Here’s What It Means

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A strange metal frame on some new Taco Bell restaurants has people wondering if construction crews forgot something. Here's what it's for.

There are certain restaurant chain designs everyone has come to recognize. McDonald’s golden arches. Pizza Hut’s red roof. But what about the open framing on the side of new Taco Bell buildings?

If you haven’t seen the new Taco Bell design, it includes open framing on the second floor on one side. But why? A recent Reddit post sparked plenty of guesses about what the framing was for.

New Taco Bell Building – What’s the Open Framing? by u/Kindly_Blueberry2814 in whatisit

The design seems to be more aesthetic than functional and is part of Taco Bell’s newer restaurant design, known as Kitchen X. It is part of the company’s move away from its older Spanish-style buildings, which featured arches and stucco exteriors.

“That open metal cage on the left is just decorative framing to make the building look sleek and balanced,” one Reddit user commented. “The left side of the restaurant has lower ceilings for storage, coolers, and the drive-thru lane, so they added that hollow steel frame on top to match the height of the main dining room on the right. There are no stairs or deck up there, just standard roof stuff and AC units hidden behind the wall.”

Another commenter agreed, saying, “It’s just an architectural design feature to give visual balance.”

Taco Bell has introduced Kitchen X at select locations across the United States, including new restaurants in Carver and Rochester, Minnesota. However, the exterior can vary between locations, so not every restaurant has the same framing.

Before Reddit users settled on the decorative explanation, they had plenty of other guesses.

“It’s to land the tacocopter,” one Reddit user joked.

“Future terrace dining,” another Reddit user speculated.

The discussion also turned into a debate about Taco Bell’s changing appearance. Some users said they missed the chain’s older buildings with more character, while others shared photos of former locations with more traditional designs.

Whether you love or hate the look, there’s no denying that it’s eye-popping and hard to miss. Which is probably what the company was hoping for when coming up with the new look.
