Jisoo to Release Original Song for Upcoming ‘The Angry Birds Movie 3’ (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The single, "Sun Showers," was co-written by the K-pop star and will be featured in the film and its soon-to-be-released trailer.

Jisoo, a member of K-pop supergroup Blackpink, is slated to contribute a song for The Angry Birds Movie 3, The Hollywood Reporter has exclusively learned.

The K-pop star has co-written an original song “Sun Showers” for the upcoming animated film. The song is expected to be used in the film and its soon-to-be-released trailer. The album artwork for the song also features the character “Shumon,” a little dustball creature that Jisoo first drew in middle school before she debuted as a K-pop idol.

“I’m so happy to be part of The Angry Birds Movie 3,” the singer said in an exclusive statement to THR. “I’ve always loved how the films bring together such colorful characters, humor and heart. With ‘Sun Showers,’ I wanted to capture the movie’s playful and bright energy through a fun, feel-good song that makes people want to smile and dance along.”

She added, “I really hope everyone will enjoy hearing it when they see the film over the holidays.”

Hailing from Paramount Pictures, the holiday animated film is expected to be released on Dec. 23. The synopsis reads, “This holiday season, one angry bird will face his greatest challenge yet, surviving fatherhood… while saving the world!”

Jason Sudeikis, Josh Gad, Emma Myers, Rachel Bloom, Walker Scobell, Danny McBride, Marcello Hernández, Tim Robinson, Anna Cathcart, Maitreyi Ramakrishnan, Nikki Glaser, MrBeast, Salish Matter, Psalm West, Sam Richardson and James Austin Johnson are set to star, with Lily James and Keke Palmer also joining the cast.

Louis Koo, Toru Nakahara, Ben Mattes, Hanna Valkeapää-Nokkala, Crosby Clyse, Chris Pflug, Simon Hedges, Christopher Milburn, Jamey Heath, Paul Kang, Geneva Wasserman and Cathy Boxall are executive producing. John Cohen, Dan Chuba, Carla Connor and Namit Malhotra are producing the film directed by John Rice and featuring a screenplay by Thurop Van Orman.
