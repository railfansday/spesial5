Black Friday isn't until Nov. 27, but Amazon is already offering some substantial discounts on OLED gaming monitors from LG, Samsung, and ASUS, with savings ranging from $100 to nearly $580. And since OLED monitors generally offer near-instantaneous pixel response times, excellent contrast, and deep blacks, they're particularly appealing for games with fast movement or darker environments. The most affordable option is Samsung's 27-inch Odyssey OLED G6 at $409.99, while the biggest dollar discount goes to LG's 45-inch Ultragear at $1,121, down from $1,699.99.

The 32-inch LG UltraGear 32GX870A-B is down to $819.99 from $1,399.99, saving you nearly $580 on a monitor that works well for both visually demanding games and competitive shooters. Its biggest advantage is LG's Dual Mode feature, which lets you switch between sharp 4K resolution at 240Hz and 1080p at 480Hz when faster gameplay takes priority. That makes it particularly versatile if you enjoy cinematic single-player games but also spend time playing competitive titles. That said, you'll need a powerful gaming PC to take full advantage of its higher refresh rates, particularly at 4K.

The 27-inch LG UltraGear 27GX790B-B is down to $649 from $999.99, a $350 discount that brings it to its lowest price ever, according to price trackers. It’s built for speed, with a 1440p OLED panel that runs at 540Hz and a Dual Mode that pushes the refresh rate to 720Hz at 720p—making it particularly appealing for competitive games like Counter-Strike 2 or Valorant, where smooth motion and quick reactions are essential. Just keep in mind that you'll need a powerful PC capable of delivering exceptionally high frame rates to take full advantage of those refresh rates.

The 45-inch LG UltraGear 45GX900A-B is down to $1,121 from $1,699.99. Its expansive 800R curved OLED display uses a 21:9 aspect ratio and 3440 x 1440 resolution, giving you a wider field of view that's particularly enjoyable for racing games, flight simulators, and cinematic adventures. You also get a 240Hz refresh rate, 0.03ms response time, and support for G-Sync and FreeSync Premium Pro.

The 27-inch Samsung Odyssey OLED G6 (G61SH) is down to $409.99 from $599.99, making it the most affordable OLED monitor in this roundup. Despite the lower price, you’re still getting a 1440p OLED panel with a 240Hz refresh rate and 0.03ms response time, which is more than enough for smooth gameplay in most modern titles.

The 27-inch Asus ROG Strix XG27ACDNG is a QD-OLED gaming monitor designed for fast-paced action, and it is currently down to $549 from $759. Its 1440p resolution and 360Hz refresh rate offer a good balance between sharp visuals and responsive gameplay, while the 0.03ms response time helps keep fast-moving objects clear. At this price, it's the more affordable alternative to the faster LG 27GX790B-B if you don't need refresh rates beyond 360Hz.

The 34-inch Alienware AW3425DW is down to $699.99 from $799.99, saving you $100 on a curved QD-OLED gaming monitor. Its 3440 x 1440 resolution and 21:9 aspect ratio give you more horizontal screen space for immersive games like racing simulators and open-world adventures, while the 240Hz refresh rate and 0.03ms response time help keep fast-moving action smooth and responsive. You also get FreeSync Premium Pro and G-Sync compatibility to help prevent screen tearing.
