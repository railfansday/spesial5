Brandy and Monica Set ‘Boy Is Mine’ Residency In Las Vegas: ‘This Is Where We Belong’

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

Brandy and Monica are back for more, and taking their hit show to Vegas.

Hot off last year’s sold-out, co-headlining “The Boy Is Mine Tour,” the Grammy-winning singers are heading to Resorts World Las Vegas for a 10-date residency that kicks off in May. The singers revealed the news during a special Grammy-themed episode of Dancing With the Stars Tuesday evening (Nov. 10).

Produced by The Black Promoters Collective, The Boy Is Mine Vegas Residency will bring Brandy and Monica to Sin City for a “major new production showcasing their individual catalogs, shared musical history and the timeless songs that have connected generations of fans,” according to press materials.

Tickets for The Boy Is Mine Vegas Residency go on sale to the general public on Friday, Nov. 20 at 10:00 a.m. PT at AXS.com. An American Express presale goes live on Nov. 16 followed by an artist presale Nov. 18.

For Monica, the Vegas residency is an organic continuation of the Boy Is Mine tour, which saw the R&B singers reunite on stage to celebrate their chart-topping single, while performing fan favorites from their respective deep catalogs.

“I realized that people had this really genuine love and excitement for the two of us reconnecting in a musical way,” Monica tells Rolling Stone. “It made me look forward to rehearsals and everything that we were doing. And each time we’d present something to fans, they would fully support it.”

Though the tour ended in December 2025, Monica said she and Brandy continued talking about what they could do together next. That’s when Brandy presented the idea of a stint in Vegas. “I always just loved her passion for us as a brand and ‘Bronica’ as a whole,” Monica says. “And when she brought it to me, I loved the idea so much, because being stationary for me is such an incredible thing, because I have such an in-depth bond with my children, that it gives me the opportunity to be in one place, and they can join me and see me in one space, consistently night after night.”

“We were very fortunate that, at the mention of it, Resorts World started to reach out to us,” Monica continues. “And that also let us know it was confirmation that this is where we belong.”

It’s been almost 30 years since “The Boy Is Mine” was released, and while both Brandy and Monica have gone on to successful solo careers, the singers know they will forever be linked together, something Monica is starting to appreciate.

“This is the right time [for the residency] because we’ve definitely established ourselves as a unit,” she shares. “I think people recognize that we work hard at being our absolute best for them in the process. And I also feel like the joy and nostalgia that it brings is really irreplaceable, and it’s something that I don’t take for granted or take lightly at all.”
