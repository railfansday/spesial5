‘Spider-Man: Brand New Day’ Re-Release With New Rosario Dawson Night Nurse Footage Sets Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

That re-release of the highest grossing motion picture in the U.S. and third-highest grossing worldwide, Sony’s Spider-Man: Brand New Day, which we first told you about, is hitting theaters on Dec. 4 in time for awards season.

The new cut will be titled Spider-Man: [Brand New] Brand New Day.

The extended cut will feature new scenes, including Rosario Dawson as Night Nurse; that character has appeared in Marvel TV’s Daredevil, Iron Fist, Luke Cage, and The Defenders. In interviews, Dawson was already telling the press that she had shot a scene for Destin Daniel Cretton’s-directed Brand New Day, but it was cut. Tickets go on sale November 19. The first weekend of December at the domestic box office typically eases post the moviegoing surge during the Thanksgiving stretch, and it’s typically a home to genre movies, this year’s being Universal’s Violent Night 2. Watch on Deadline

Spider-Man: Brand New Day opened on July 31, breaking all sorts of records including best North American opening at $360M, best previews ever with $72M, in addition to posting the second biggest global opening ever with $932M. The Marvel Studios/Pascal Pictures production at $959M is the highest grossing at the domestic box office and worldwide ranks third on the all-time box office chart with $2.5M WW.

Sony told Deadline’s Pete Hammond that they’re launching an awards campaign for Brand New Day.

“The Academy is meant to recognize greatness. So, to be honest, I think comes down to greatness,” chairman and CEO of Sony Pictures Motion Picture Group Tom Rothman told Hammond last week. “But also to receive wisdom about what greatness is. If there aren’t people thinking about it, we’re gonna do what we can just to make people think about it. I think it’s also incumbent upon studios who make these kinds of quality commercial films to get people to think about it, and here’s what I ask people when I walk around: ‘Are you telling me that there are 10 films this year that are better than this film?'”
