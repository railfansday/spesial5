XRP’s Volatility Puts $0.7 Retest in View: Kalshi

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

XRP’s price correction has continued, drawing the asset back to levels last seen during the November 2024 bear market while the broader crypto market continues to see intense selling pressure.

While market sentiment has shifted massively, the latest data on the Kalshi prediction market shows that the odds of XRP experiencing deeper price losses this year have increased.

While XRP’s price correction has intensified with the asset posting a daily price decrease of over 8% in the last 24 hours, traders on Kalshi believe the volatility may be far from over.

As such, data shows that traders on the platform are increasingly betting on XRP retesting $0.7 before the year runs out, causing the probability of XRP falling back to that level to sit at 55% as of November 21.

With just over a month left before the year wraps up, XRP’s chances of defeating this bearish prediction are slim, as the asset has continued to struggle amid the market downturn, with retail and institutional demand currently on hold.

As XRP continues to show zero signs of recovery, data from CryptoRank shows that the asset has posted a massive quarterly loss for Q4.

Even though the quarter has yet to run out, XRP has already suffered a price loss of 39% since October, the weakest quarterly return seen so far in 2026.

If momentum persists, XRP might be at risk of falling below the $0.7 mark as investor confidence is rapidly fading.
