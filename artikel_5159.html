Tracy Schuchart: Bitcoin Trading “Like a Risk Asset” as Fed Hikes Loom

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Tracy Shuchart says Bitcoin's biggest long-term argument is its monetary scarcity, but investors need to separate the long-term thesis from how it's trading today.

Tracy Shuchart says Bitcoin’s biggest long-term argument is its monetary scarcity, but investors need to separate the long-term thesis from how it’s trading today. In tightening liquidity, with rising yields and a stronger dollar, she says Bitcoin “behaves much more like a risk asset than a safe haven.” She adds that Bitcoin transactions have stayed strong even as price falls, and “the long-term argument hasn’t changed.” She expects continued adoption from here.
