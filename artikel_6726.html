Is There “Animosity” Between Val and the Other Eliminated ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Pros? Source Reveals What’s Going on Behind the Scenes

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Jenna Dewan and Val Chmerkovskiy were cut from Dancing With the Stars right before the semifinals, but the show must go on for Val—who will (presumably?) be joining his fellow eliminated pros in helping out with group dances for the semifinals and finals.

And apparently there’s absolutely no drama behind the scenes with Val and his fellow pros—despite a very dramatic season (reminder: Val was suspended for an entire week after his controversial comment to Carrie Ann Inaba).

“Val gets along great with the other eliminated pros. The troupe has welcomed him and they’re all playing nice,” a source tells The Sun. “There is no animosity, despite what some fans might be speculating.”

The insider adds that “It helps Val and Jenna [Johnson’s] niece, Hailey, is part of the group but the other dancers look up to Val so there’s no drama.”

Val’s had a pretty good attitude about his surprising elimination from the competition, telling Extra, “Every week’s a shock. It’s very competitive. Lot of great couples out there, wishing everybody the best, gonna be rooting from home. Definitely a sad result, but no regrets, we had a great season.”

He also posted a sweet video of him telling his son that it’s “fine to loose,” and “you gotta learn how to win, you gotta learn how to loose.”
