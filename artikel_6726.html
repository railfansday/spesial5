Here’s Where Wilnie and Wilson Stand Now After Breaking Up on ‘Love Is Blind’

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Attention everyone wondering if they got back together….

Not gonna sugarcoat it: This wasn’t a very successful season of Love Is Blind. Multiple couples broke up either at the altar or before making it there at all, including Wilnie and Wilson. We had high hopes for these two, but things changed after their respective bachelor and bachelorette parties, and they ultimately decided to part ways. The issue? Wilson thinking Wilnie should do more in the way of household responsibilities (including…cooking for him) and wanting her to be more “fun.”

Meanwhile, Wilnie said, “I don’t think you really like me, and I’ve been thinking you’re not going to say yes at the altar.” She also told producers, “He expected me to be more like his mom,” while Wilson’s parting words were essentially “grow up, dude.”

Of course, we can’t forget that Love Is Blind season 11 was filmed over a year ago (if fan sleuthing is to be believed), and a lot can change in that time. But the cast just got back together for a reunion, where they updated everyone on where things stand. And we have intel!

Reality Receipts podcaster Rikkii Wise (@storytimewithrikkii) dropped a slew of spoilers and revealed that Wilnie and Wilson are not back together, which was confirmed during an early fan viewing of the reunion on Saturday.

Sorry to everyone hoping they’d work it out! The good news, though? They appear to be fast friends, and considering they both live in the Boston area, there’s always a chance at reconciliation going forward? As Wilson put it, “Me and Wilnie, we check in on each other. More recently…maybe not?”
