Director of Technology Partnerships at Supabase

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Supabase is the Postgres development platform, built by developers for developers. We provide a complete backend solution including Database, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions, Realtime, and Vector Search. All services are deeply integrated and designed for growth.

We’re looking for a Director of Technology Partnerships to lead the teams responsible for growing Supabase through technology integrations and our marketplace ecosystem.

You’ll bring together two complementary motions: partnerships that embed Supabase into the products developers already use, and a marketplace that helps developers discover and connect complementary technologies to their Supabase projects. Your job is to turn both into durable sources of revenue, adoption, and customer value.

Reporting to the Head of Partnerships, you’ll lead and develop the Integrations and Marketplace teams, set their commercial strategy, and work hands-on with our most important partners. A central part of the role is working closely with AI labs to build strategic relationships, develop integrations, and bring joint opportunities to market. You’ll partner closely with Product, Engineering, Sales, Marketing, and Finance to take integrations from idea to launch—and grow their impact long after launch.

This role is based in the San Francisco Bay Area, where you’ll build relationships with leading AI, developer tools, and infrastructure companies. Supabase is remote-first, and you’ll work across a globally distributed team.

Lead, coach, and develop the Integrations and Marketplace partnerships teams, including team leads and partner managers.

Set a clear strategy for where Supabase invests across strategic technology partnerships, marketplace categories, and emerging ecosystem opportunities.

Define team objectives, partner coverage, investment priorities, and operating cadence. Make thoughtful tradeoffs between high-touch strategic partnerships and scalable programs.

Build a culture of ownership, commercial discipline, and strong cross-functional execution.

Drive revenue from technology integrations through partner-driven customer acquisition, product consumption, expansion, retention, co-sell opportunities, and commercial arrangements.

Own the commercial performance of the technology partnerships and marketplace portfolio, including forecasting and reporting on progress, risks, and return on investment.

Build and deepen relationships with AI labs, working closely with their product, engineering, business development, and executive teams to identify and execute high-impact partnership opportunities.

Develop executive relationships with strategic partners across developer tools, cloud, and adjacent infrastructure.

Lead or support negotiations for complex partnerships, including integration, referral, revenue-sharing, and joint go-to-market agreements.

Help partner managers build joint business plans that connect partner priorities to measurable outcomes for Supabase and its customers.

Lead and develop the Marketplace team, setting its commercial and partner strategy and holding the team accountable for outcomes.

Guide the team in identifying the capabilities developers need, recruiting the right partners, and helping those partners launch and grow.

Set priorities and allocate resources across strategic, high-touch partners and scalable paths for emerging and self-serve partners.

Ensure the team works effectively with Product and Engineering on integration quality, partner onboarding, discovery, activation, and the developer experience across product surfaces.

Enable the team to work with Marketing and Sales to launch integrations, enable teams, and drive ongoing adoption—not just announcements.

Establish clear measures for partner-sourced and influenced pipeline, integration adoption, revenue, consumption, retention, and marketplace performance.

Work with Finance, Data, and Partner Operations to improve attribution, reporting, forecasting, and partner revenue processes.

Represent the voice of partners and customers in product and go-to-market planning, while aligning stakeholders around shared priorities.

Identify risks early, remove blockers, and communicate decisions and outcomes clearly to leadership.

Have significant experience in technology partnerships, business development, or ecosystem leadership at a developer tools, cloud, infrastructure, or SaaS company.

Have led and developed partner-facing teams, including managers or senior individual contributors, and can balance strategic direction with hands-on execution.

Have a track record of building partnerships that produced measurable revenue, product adoption, or customer growth.

Have negotiated complex commercial agreements and understand partnership economics, including revenue share, referrals, and consumption-based models.

Have worked closely with Product and Engineering to ship and grow technical integrations. You can engage credibly with technical teams, including those at AI labs, even if you aren’t the person writing the code.

Know how to build relationships with executives while earning the trust of product, engineering, and go-to-market teams.

Use data to prioritize opportunities, assess partner performance, and make decisions when attribution is imperfect.

Are comfortable building in an evolving environment, communicating in writing, and working asynchronously across time zones.

Built or scaled a developer marketplace, integration ecosystem, or partner platform.

Worked with AI LLMs, AI app builders, databases, open-source products, or developer infrastructure.

A background in software development, with an understanding of how developers build and integrate products.

Experience growing both a small number of strategic partnerships and a broader, scalable partner program.

A strong network in the San Francisco Bay Area technology ecosystem.

We hire globally. We believe you can do your best work from anywhere. There are no Supabase offices, but we provide a WeWork membership or co-working allowance you can use anywhere in the world.

Every team member receives ESOP (equity ownership) in the company. We want everyone to share in the upside of what we’re building together.

Use this budget to set up your ideal work environment—laptop, monitor, headphones, or whatever helps you do your best work.

Supabase covers 100% of health insurance for employees and 80% for dependents, wherever you are. Your wellbeing and your family’s health are important to us.

Once a year, the entire company gathers in a new city for a week of connection, collaboration, and fun. It’s a highlight of our year.

We operate asynchronously and trust you to manage your own time. You know what needs to be done and when.

Every team member receives an annual education allowance to spend on learning—courses, books, conferences, or anything that supports your growth.

Supabase was born-remote and open-source-first. We believe our globally distributed team is our secret weapon in building tools developers love.

Over $1B raised (including our $500M Series F)

We move fast, build in public, and use what we ship. If it’s in your project, we probably use it in ours too. We believe deeply in the open-source ecosystem and strive to support—not replace—existing tools and communities.

New remote jobs delivered to your inbox, every week.
