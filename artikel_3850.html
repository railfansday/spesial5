Team Cherry Presents and Makoto Koji announce plant mystery adventure game The Botanist and the Translator for PC

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Team Cherry Presents, a new publishing label under Hollow Knight developer Team Cherry, and developer Makoto Koji have announced The Botanist and the Translator, a planet-spanning plant mystery adventure game set in a lush, natural world. It will be available for PC via Steam. A release date was not announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:

Solve a peculiar plant mystery on a distant planet.

The talented botanist Hatti and her trusty translator Oliver are summoned to investigate a planet blanketed in thick, sticky pollen. Investigate, identify and diagnose the mystery to protect all of the lifeforms on the planet!
