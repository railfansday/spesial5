Black Forest Games splits from THQ Nordic

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Black Forest Games has split from THQ Nordic after nearly 10 years of ownership, the companies announced.

The Offenburg, Germany-based developer was acquired by THQ Nordic in August 2017.

Following a management buyout by the leadership of Black Forest Games, the two companies will pursue different business priorities.

Both companies, however, will continue to collaborate on projects within game development going forward.

Black Forest Games, meanwhile, has founded joined the newly established Third Signal, an independent group of game studios making premium games, as a founding member and co-owner of the group.

“Some of the best games of the last decade came from teams of fifty, not five hundred,” said Third Signal founder and CEO Andreas Tobler in a press release. “Black Forest Games is one of them, and the right studio to launch Third Signal. Our vision is to build a developer driven group and give teams the independence, resources and creative freedom to continue their creative legacy and bring their own ideas to life.”

Black Forest Games co-founder and CEO Adrian Goersch added, “We are grateful to THQ Nordic and Embracer for nine good years and the games we made together. Now, we’re excited to shape our own future as owners again, to work on an exciting new project with a great team and strong partners behind us.”
