All Stages in Warhammer Survivors and how to unlock them

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The two universes in Warhammer Survivors come with several stages to grind, and they hide plenty of unique challenges, bosses, and more. At first, you’ll only have two stages (one in each universe).

However, you can then start working towards unlocking the other stages.

First, we will take look at how to unlock all the stages available in the 40K universe. There are six different stages, and you have to unlock five of them.

Just like the 40K universe, Age of Sigmar also has different stages.

Unlocking the stages isn’t just important for expanding your in-game experience, but it’s necessary if you want to meet and defeat all the bosses.
