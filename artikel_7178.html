Leighton Meester on the New Blair Waldorf Novel—and Whether She’d Play the Character Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The former Gossip Girl star has already read part of the book.

In February, news broke that Gossip Girl author Cecily von Ziegesar was writing a new standalone novel about Blair Waldorf, aptly titled Blair, that would capture the iconic character’s life 20 years after the original novels took place. Leighton Meester, who famously played Blair in the 2007 show, is well aware of the project, which is tentatively set for a summer 2027 release. In fact, she told ELLE in a new interview that she has already read the first seven chapters.

When deputy editor and features director Kayla Webley Adler suggested that she could play the role again if the book was adapted into a show, she simply smiled.

She is open to revisiting the character, though. “I’ve been asked this exact question forever, but I never have a good answer because I just am always like, ‘I never say never because I don’t know,’” Meester said. “Obviously that character meant something to me. And I’m grateful that it meant something to other people. So you never know, but I really just don’t know.”

Of course, the actress didn’t share any details about Blair. Deadline wrote in February that its plot is “still under wraps, but it’s expected to follow the former NYC socialite as she returns to the Big Apple to climb her way back to the top of Upper East Side food chain.”

For her part, Meester is just happy to keep acting. “I’m sure that this is cliché at this point, but every time I finish a job, I don’t know what my next job is,” she said. “There’s nothing that’s guaranteed, so every time you get a role, you’re like, ‘I get to do this again. Here we go.’”

It’s a privilege to have a career in the industry, she said: “To be an actor is such a dream, and it’s never lost on me. I’m almost nostalgic about it because I can’t believe I still get to do this. Not to sound like a crazy person, but sometimes before I do a scene, I will tell myself, ‘This is my dream, and my life, in one way or another, has led to me being able to be there when they yell ‘action!’”
