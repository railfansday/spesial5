CoinStats: Social Media Manager for X

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

CoinStats is an all-in-one crypto portfolio tracker. Our mission is to empower people to effectively manage their crypto portfolio via an easy-to-use platform.  CoinStats started off as a small group of crypto enthusiasts that needed a simple tracker for their own assets back in 2017. Once they put it on App Store though, they realized that there were thousands of others who needed a tool just like that! Users were and still are the main drivers of our growth.

Since then, the CoinStats Team has grown into a vibrant group of 50+ professionals making crypto management easy for more than a million users. The team fosters a productive, supportive, and environment making the journey toward our shared goals a pleasure. The many perks and opportunities for professional development offered to the team members add to the invaluable experience gained in the crypto field.

CoinStats is a place to grow. In the past 3 years, our marketers moved up fast. Our former Social Media Manager landed at Binance. Now we’re looking for the next CT manager (aka Twitter intern, reply guy, shitposter, and content creator).

The main focus is X, but skills across other platforms are welcome. We want someone who lives and breathes Crypto Twitter, knows the culture, and can build CoinStats’ voice in the community. We’re also hiring for other marketing roles, so if you bring extra strengths, we’ll shape responsibilities around you.  This is a remote role, full-time or part-time, open to mid-level and senior candidates anywhere in the world.
