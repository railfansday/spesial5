2 BigLaw firms face proposed class actions over cyber breaches

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

In newly filed proposed class action lawsuits, plaintiffs are alleging that cyberattacks at Baker McKenzie and Squire Patton Boggs led to exposure of their personal information. (Image from Shutterstock)

In newly filed proposed class action lawsuits, plaintiffs are alleging that cyberattacks at Baker McKenzie and Squire Patton Boggs led to exposure of their personal information.

The complaint against Baker McKenzie was filed in the Northern District of Illinois on Wednesday. According to Reuters, Baker McKenzie said last week a cybercriminal in August “socially engineered two of our users” to obtain a limited number of files, but that “there was no independent malicious access to our system.”

Squire Patton Boggs disclosed its breach in October, saying it involved “an unauthorized third party obtaining a limited set of information from the firm,” Reuters reports.

The complaint against Squire Patton Boggs was also filed Wednesday but in the Northern District of Ohio.

Neither law firm responded to requests for comment from Reuters.
