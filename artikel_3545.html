T-Mobile launches 5-gigabit fiber for $90/mo with 10-year price guarantee

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

T-Mobile has announced a new 5 gigabit fiber broadband service priced at $90 a month, starting today. The company also says that if you sign up now, the price is guaranteed not to increase for a full decade …

The company now offers a total of 4 different home broadband plans with fiber connections:

All pricing is based on choosing autopay billing and without taxes and fees. The company says that for a limited time you can also get bill credits equivalent to two months free, and $300 in virtual prepaid cards provided you don’t cancel within the first year.

The company says its fastest ever service is intended for the most demanding households.

With 5 Gig, busy households have the bandwidth for more people and devices to be online at the same time — whether that’s gaming, working or keeping a growing number of smart home devices connected. And because T‑Mobile Fiber runs on a 100% fiber-optic connection directly to the home, customers get fast, consistent performance with symmetrical speeds, meaning uploads are just as fast as downloads. That matters because households aren’t just downloading. Video calls, security cameras, cloud backups and more depend on fast upload speeds, too […]

“Between online gaming, smart devices and AI-powered tools, there’s more happening online in our households than ever,” said chief commercial officer Andre Almeida. “Our fastest 5 Gig plan gives customers incredible capacity for everything they’re doing today, with room to grow for whatever comes next.”

As always, you have to check the small print on price guarantees, which in this case is as follows:

Taxes & fees, voluntary equipment or speed upgrades, select limited-time promotions, per-use charges, third-party services, and network management practices aren’t included.

The most obvious potential gotcha is that broadband speeds will continue to increase over the years, and choosing to upgrade to a faster service at a later date eliminates the price guarantee.

You can check availability on the company’s website.
