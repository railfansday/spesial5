Jamie Lynn Spears’ Daughter Maddie Pokes Fun at Her Mom’s Teen Pregnancy

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Jamie Lynn Spears’ daughter, Maddie, is having a little fun at her mom’s expense!

Maddie, 18, poked fun at Jamie Lynn, 35, in a cheeky TikTok video shared on Friday, November 13, joking that she had managed to make it through high school without becoming pregnant.

In the clip, Maddie can be seen spinning around and dancing with her hands in the air as Jamie Lynn watches on. As the song continues, the Zoey 101 alum puts her hands on her hips before crossing her arms, seemingly playing along with the joke.

Maddie added the text, “Dance if you beat high school pregnancy,” over the video. She tagged her mom in the caption, writing, “lol @Jamie Lynn Spears.”

The playful post comes years after Jamie Lynn welcomed Maddie with her then-fiancé, Casey Aldridge, in June 2008. The actress was just 17 years old at the time, while Aldridge was 19.

Earlier this year, Jamie Lynn set the record straight as fans continue to speculate that her pregnancy was the reason her show, Zoey 101, was axed.

“If we’re talking about a concrete misconception, it would be that everybody thinks Zoey 101 ended because I got pregnant. And that is not true,” Jamie Lynne said during an August appearance on the “Momarchy” podcast.

Jamie Lynn shared that Maddie often gets comments on TikTok from fans of the Nickelodeon sitcom accusing her of being the reason it was canceled after four seasons.

“We laugh about it,” the Sweet Magnolias star continued. “But it’s just not the truth.”

The actress explained that Zoey 101 had already finished filming by the time she got pregnant, but the final season had not yet aired on TV.

“That’s how it is in entertainment. A lot of people who aren’t in the business don’t understand. They think we shoot it, it comes out,” she said. “No, you shoot it and it comes out maybe the next year or something. So I think that’s where people are confused. But we were wrapped, we were always wrapped, and there was never going to be another season.”

Spears concluded, “My beautiful Maddie is not the reason the show ended.”

Zoey 101, which starred Jamie Lynn as the titular Zoey Brooks, premiered on Nickelodeon in January 2005 and ran until May 2008. The fourth and final season ended production in August 2007 — two months before the show’s staff learned about Jamie Lynn’s pregnancy with Maddie. (The cast later reunited for a follow-up film called Zoey 102 in 2023.)
