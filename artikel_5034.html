American Bitcoin (ABTC) Lands $150 Million Bitcoin-Backed Credit Line From FalconX

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

American Bitcoin Corp. (ABTC) has secured a $150 million revolving credit facility from FalconX that is backed by its bitcoin, the miner said Monday, giving it a way to raise cash without selling coins from its reserve.

The facility was signed by an American Bitcoin subsidiary and is secured by certain of the company’s bitcoin holdings. American Bitcoin said the money can go toward strategic growth opportunities and general corporate purposes. “This $150 million facility provides American Bitcoin with non-dilutive capital to support our continued operations and growth initiatives,” CEO Mike Ho said in the release.

The FalconX line is committed up to $150 million, and each draw is subject to bitcoin collateral requirements and other customary conditions. Because it is revolving, the company can draw, repay and redraw funds during the term.

Borrowings carry a 5.95% loan fee, plus a commitment fee on any undrawn amount. The initial term is two years, with an optional three-year extension at FalconX’s discretion, subject to customary conditions. The company did not name the size of the commitment fee.

The facility has an initial collateral ratio of 143%. At that level, drawing the full $150 million would require about $214.5 million in pledged bitcoin, or roughly 3,500 BTC with bitcoin trading near $61,300 early Monday.

American Bitcoin did not say whether it has drawn on the line yet. Its release lists the risks, including the possible liquidation of pledged bitcoin if its value falls below required levels, and requirements to post more collateral or repay borrowings on short notice.

“Collateralized lending gives American Bitcoin a way to access liquidity while it continues to grow its Bitcoin reserve and scale its business,” said Oliver Sitt, a sales director at FalconX. The prime broker said the deal shows how it can structure working capital for public companies that do not want to sell their holdings. FalconX provides financing, trading and asset management services to institutional clients.

American Bitcoin is majority-owned by Hut 8 and counts Eric Trump as a co-founder and chief strategy officer. On its third-quarter earnings call on Nov. 4, the company said it held 8,427 BTC at the end of September and that its reserve had since passed 8,500 BTC.

Eric Trump said on the call that the company did not buy any bitcoin on the open market in the quarter or issue shares through its at-the-market program. Every coin added came from its own mining, though the company did sell part of its production to cover operating costs.

It mined about 891 BTC in the third quarter, down from 932 in the second. The company reported net income of $126 million, largely from gains on its bitcoin as the price rose about 39% in the quarter.

That price has since reversed. Bitcoin ended September near $83,400, according to the company, and now trades more than 25% below that level. Other miners have turned to their treasuries for cash, with MARA selling 13,597 bitcoin this month and Riot selling more than 20,000 BTC this year to fund data center construction.

Collateralized debt lets a miner raise money while keeping its coins, but it also ties the loan to the price of bitcoin. Margins across the industry are already thin, with mining costs running above the bitcoin price for parts of this year.

Hut 8, American Bitcoin’s parent, has taken a similar route at the corporate level. It disclosed in its third-quarter results that it had closed a $1.07 billion senior secured revolving credit facility that was undrawn at the end of the third quarter.

“It reflects the strength of our balance sheet and gives us a disciplined way to deploy capital and secure the infrastructure that supports our business,” Ho said.
