J.J. Abrams’ ‘Jurassic Park’ Replacement HBO Max Release Date Officially Confirmed

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

2026 would be viewed as a breakout year for Anne Hathaway if she wasn’t already one of the biggest movie stars in the world. 2025 was a quiet year for Hathaway after starring in The Idea of You for Prime Video back in 2024, but she got off to a hot start in 2026 with Mother Mary, the A24-produced epic that’s currently streaming on HBO Max. She followed Mother Mary by reprising her role as Andy in The Devil Wears Prada 2, which grossed nearly $700 million at the box office before going on to become a streaming hit on Disney Plus. Hathaway then reunited with Christopher Nolan for The Odyssey, and she can even be seen in theaters now in the new Colleen Hoover-inspired erotic thriller, Verity.

Between The Odyssey and Verity, though, Anne Hathaway teamed up with Star Wars veteran Ewan McGregor for a wild ride of a sci-fi movie that’s finally heading to streaming. We’re talking, of course, about The End of Oak Street, the Jurassic Park replacement film that was written and directed by David Robert Mitchell. The movie went viral the week before release for AI marketing tactics that turned some fans off from watching it, but it still managed to scrounge together $121 million at the box office — the studio would have liked to see more, though, considering the film cost $85 million to make. After a successful run on VOD the last few weeks that has seen The End of Oak Street surge into the top 10 on Prime Video, HBO Max announced this afternoon that the sci-fi film will officially join its streaming library on November 13.

Produced by J.J. Abrams, the same director behind Star Wars: The Force Awakens and The Rise of Skywalker, The End of Oak Street also stars Maisy Stella as Audrey Platt and Christian Convery as Brian Platt. The official synopsis for the film, which holds solid marks of 86% from critics and 76% from audiences on the aggregate site Rotten Tomatoes, reads as follows: “After a mysterious cosmic event rips Oak Street from suburbia and transports their neighborhood to someplace unknown, the Platt family soon discovers that their very survival depends on them sticking together as they navigate their now unrecognizable surroundings.”

Check out The End of Oak Street on HBO Max starting on November 13 and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of Anne Hathaway’s future projects.
