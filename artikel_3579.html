The GTA 6 performance analysis by tech experts Digital Foundry confirmed, unsurprisingly, that the game runs best on PS5 Pro, but is still decently stable at around 30fps on base PS5 and Xbox Series X. The Series S is another story, however, with an internal resolution of just 720p and a framerate that occasionally dips into the low 20s. “Series S offers the loosest 30fps lock, with large portions of the game playing out at frame-rates from the mid to high 20s,” they wrote. “Again, it’s possible to depress the frame-rate to around 20fps with vehicular destruction. The other consoles do a credible job of hitting 30fps, but Series S often lies in a 25-30fps no-man’s-land.”
