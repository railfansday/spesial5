The Much-Rumoured Simpsons Hit & Run Remake May Have Been Cancelled

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

After a lot of fan requests for a remake of The Simpsons: Hit & Run, there were many clues suggesting the project was finally up and running — but now, things don't look so certain.

As reported by VGC, Darren Evenson — who was a senior game designer at New Radical Games, the team thought to be producing the Hit & Run revival — has posted on LinkedIn about projects being cancelled at the studio.

"It seems that not even a fantastic project with an absolutely incredible team is immune from the current state of the industry right now, as what we were working on at New Radical Games (although mind-blowingly awesome) was part of a whole gaggle of cancellations," Evenson says.

"Oh, you silly suits with spreadsheets... will you EVER learn? If you don't make the game, you can't make the money... and if you don't make the money, then you don't get the power... and... well, there's more to that quote, but I digress.

For the first time in my career, I am Available For Work and looking for a new exciting opportunity where I can make some fun games with some great people!

Please reach out, shout out, or peform some interpretive dance if you know of an opportunity that a hip cat like me could groove to."

Fans who have spotted this message are fairly confident, based on certain quotes, that it's referring to The Simpsons: Hit & Run, though others have suggested it could actually be Scarface: The World Is Yours.

New Radical Games is a recently founded studio with many key staff from Radical Entertainment, the Canadian outfit that made the original Hit & Run.

Between that and the show's creator, Matt Groening, practically confirming the remake's existence, it was thought that it was finally going to happen.

While this new message doesn't outright say the remake is cancelled, something has been, and Hit & Run was the most obvious candidate for the studio's main project.

We'll have to wait and see how things play out, but for now, it feels like the elusive remake may not be so close as we thought, and may in fact not be coming at all.
