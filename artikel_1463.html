Roma still interested in Gittens, Gasperini keen on Englishman

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Roma continues to monitor Jamie Bynoe-Gittens ahead of the upcoming winter transfer window.

The English winger, born in 2004, is one of Gian Piero Gasperini’s most sought-after prospects, as he seeks a player capable of enhancing the team’s attacking capabilities.

Currently at Chelsea, Gittens is having a difficult season, marked by limited use due to various physical issues. Roma’s interest is nothing new. During the summer transfer window, the Roman club reportedly welcomed the possibility of bringing the Englishman to Trigoria, but Chelsea had not been open to a loan option.

According to Fabrizio Romano in a recent video posted on his YouTube channel, Gasperini considers Gittens a particularly good fit for his system.

Explosiveness, pace, and the ability to outmaneuver a man in one-on-one situations are the qualities that make the striker a potential reinforcement for Roma’s attacking lineup.

The main question mark, however, concerns the Englishman’s fitness. Before even assessing Chelsea’s willingness to negotiate a temporary transfer, Roma will need to understand Gittens’s condition when the January transfer window opens.

The injuries that have limited his playing time are a factor to carefully evaluate, especially considering the needs of Gasperini’s squad.

The Giallorossi’s goal is to acquire a full-back who can be used immediately, capable of providing consistency and making a real contribution to the second half of the season. Roma, in fact, cannot afford to invest in a player who risks spending too much time away from the pitch.
