Real Madrid already has big doubts about its new recruit JJ Gabriel!

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

It was a soap opera that fascinated England, but not only that. Indeed, the one who is considered the country’s biggest hope, JJ Gabriel, had made the decision to leave Manchester United before signing his first professional contract with the Red Devils. Almost all the big clubs in the Old Continent looked into the matter, and it was ultimately Real Madrid who won the jackpot, ahead of clubs like FC Barcelona or Bayern Munich.

The player’s entourage also had some serious demands, and the amount of the operation is estimated at around 15 million euros. This includes the player’s salary, bonuses, commissions as well as compensation to be paid to Mancunians. Jude Bellingham would even have intervened to convince the attacking midfielder who recently celebrated his 16th birthday, and who should join Real Madrid Castilla, in Spanish D3. If the deal has not yet been made official, the player has already posed with the front page of the newspaper Marca announcing his arrival, which no longer leaves much room for doubt.

But Real Madrid would have already been cooled by certain behaviors of the teenager. A recent video of the player in a Barça store, carrying one of the Catalan club jerseys in his arms, for example circulated on the networks. But above all, as explained The Sunthe Madrid leaders are already very suspicious of the player’s father, who is particularly demanding and already has a rather sulphurous reputation.

In particular, he would have had a very tense relationship with Manchester United, and inevitably, in Madrid, we are already afraid of seeing him reproduce this behavior on the Valdebebas side. They also fear that he could have a bad influence on his son, and are also worried about the father’s actions on social networks. Within the Merengue club, some people, including players, were also surprised to see the young Englishman declare that he dreamed of the Ballon d’Or, without even having played a single professional match. The operation is not a priori in danger, but what is certain is that the behavior of JJ Gabriel and his father will be closely scrutinized in Spain…
