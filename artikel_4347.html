Girl Wars tier list & reroll guide

If you are a new player and you want to get a glimpse of the meta game, our Girl Wars tier list will do just that. Find out who the best girls to include in your team are, which ones you shouldn't really bother with, and how to reroll to get the most favourable start.  Girl Wars Tier List: How do we rank the girls? The game has been available in Asia for quite some time now, so we do have a good amount of data available. That said, the girls don't have exactly the same stats, so their ranking evaluation is going to be different.

Currently, there are not many UR girls available, but you should expect this to change soon, as many will be released in the future. And, with future releases, our Girl Wars tier list will obviously change accordingly.

About Tomoe Gozen Tomoe Gozen, with her special abilities, such as damage immunity, powerful counterattacks, and a game-changing ultimate, is an extremely effective girl in PvP (but not just there). She is a well-rounded character who excels in both stats and mechanics. You'll notice that she's a staple in many teams. About Christian Christian is a top-tier support character in Girl Wars, as she excels at controlling her opponents and countering fast-paced playstyles. She has the ability to freeze enemies, which makes her a staple force in PVP.

Here's the thing with SSR girls in Girl Wars. For the most part, they've been outdated by the UR ones. That's power creep for you. In many cases, it's better to use SR girls, especially if you are an F2P player, but more about that on the next page.

Even though it's inefficient to max out SSR girls early on because you don't have many food resources, some of them are still very good and worth the investment. About Siegfried The good thing about Siegfried is that you can get her fragments quite easily. So, for F2P players, she's a good, solid option. Her skill, Spear Thrust, is a powerful attack that deals high damage, drains the target's energy and defence, and boosts block rate. It also has a chance to restore a protective Dragon Blood Mark that makes her immune to normal and skill attacks. About Joan of Arc Arguably the best SSR, Joan of Arc, has both abilities, which are attacks that damage a row of enemies, prioritising those with the Imprisonment status. If the front row target has Imprisonment, it spreads it to the back row enemies. Imprisonment prevents the target from moving and reduces healing.

Some of the best SSRs are Frederick, Diao Chan, Guan Yu (She's a great option as a leader), and Oda Nobunaga.

About Satutobi Sasuke Satutobi Sasuke is a valuable support who can be effective once you upgrade her to seven stars. She provides a 10% attack boost to the entire team and her ultimate significantly increases the critical hit rate and damage for her team.

It is recommended to place her in either position 1 or 3 while using Nene as a substitute in one of these positions for additional synergy. Make sure Sasuke's equipment is the best you can get for her. About Nene Even though I wouldn't suggest investing much in Nene, getting her for free to 7 stars is worth it. Especially if you are F2P, she's going to be helpful in the earlier stages of the game, thanks to her ability that increases the back row damage and the team's attack. Her ultimate decreases enemies' defence. So all in all, she's a very solid support that can fit into pretty much any team. About Honda Komatsu Honda Komatsu is a hybrid healer/damage dealer who attacks enemies with low HP and restores HP to allies. Her skills deal damage, heal, and provide buffs like increased block chance.
