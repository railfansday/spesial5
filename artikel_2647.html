Tyler Herro’s Miami return ends in historic low point for Bucks

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Milwaukee Bucks guard Tyler Herro earned a mix of cheers and jeers when he was introduced before tipoff against the Miami Heat on Wednesday. It was Herro's first game at Kaseya Center since being shipped to the Bucks as part of the trade package for Heat forward Giannis Antetokounmpo.

After the game, Herro probably felt a mix of despair and embarrassment. Milwaukee fell short against Miami, 137-130, despite a furious rally in the fourth quarter.

The Bucks suffered their 11th straight defeat and fell to 1-14, the worst record in the league. They logged their worst 15-game start in franchise history, according to ESPN Insights.

The Bucks also registered their first 11-game losing streak since 2013, when Antetokounmpo was still a rookie.

Herro struggled with eight points on 4-of-11 shooting. He had two rebounds and four assists.

Kel'el Ware, Jaime Jaquez Jr., and Kasparas Jakucionis also battled their former team, as they were part of the trade package for Antetokounmpo as well. Ware tallied 11 points and three rebounds, Jaquez added 10 points, two rebounds, and five assists, while Jakucionis had 20 points, three rebounds, five assists, and two steals.

Kyle Kuzma led Milwaukee with 26 points, five rebounds, and two steals.

Antetokounmpo had a near triple-double with 27 points, 14 rebounds, and nine assists. He also had a monster block on Brayden Burries in the second quarter.

Herro has not hidden his dismay amid the Bucks' challenging campaign, stressing that he is not used to losing a lot. He is averaging 19.4 points, 4.0 rebounds, and 4.2 assists.

Milwaukee will try to end its horrendous stretch on Thursday against the Portland Trail Blazers.
