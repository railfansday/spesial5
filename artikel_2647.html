NFL news, live updates: Browns to start Deshaun Watson in Week 10; Vikings trade for QB Kirk Cousins

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The 2026 NFL trade deadline is officially in the rearview mirror. Here's what you missed:

The biggest news of the day — the Minnesota Vikings have traded for Kirk Cousins in a reported deal with the Las Vegas Raiders, sending a third-round pick to Vegas in exchange for the vet. The move reunites Cousins with the Vikings, for whom he last played in 2023 before suffering a torn Achilles, and gives Minnesota some much-needed support at quarterback following Monday night's abysmal loss under Carson Wentz.

The Jacksonville Jaguars kicked off the NFL trade news cycle for the week with a splash move to bolster the secondary, sending a 2027 second-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick to the Atlanta Falcons in exchange for Pro Bowl safety Jessie Bates III. They continued adding on defense throughout the day, also acquiring Davon Godchaux from the New Orleans Saints and former first-round pick Jordan Davis in a blockbuster deal with the Philadelphia Eagles.

The Indianapolis Colts were another team to watch ahead of the trade deadline, with star running back Jonathan Taylor at the crux of it all. But as was first reported by NFL insider Tom Pelissero, the Colts did not trade Taylor ahead of Tuesday's deadline. The Colts did, however, trade wide receiver Josh Downs, who gets a fresh start with the Kansas City Chiefs and quarterback Patrick Mahomes.

Now, with all of that officially a wrap, it's time to gear up for Week 10.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for the latest NFL news, injury updates and more ahead of Week 10.

After a great deal of speculation was made that Jonathan Taylor could be moved ahead of the NFL trade deadline, the Indianapolis Colts are reportedly standing pat.

The team will not trade him ahead of the deadline, NFL insider Tom Pelissero reported Tuesday afternoon.

Pelissero reported that the Colts had received multiple offers for Taylor of a second-round pick-plus, but instead, the team will move forward with him as a foundational piece within the locker room as they look to rebuild.

Riley Patterson will kick for the Ravens this week in their Monday Night Football matchup with the Chargers, according to the NFL Network's Mike Garafolo. Patterson spent the past two seasons with the Dolphins, but was released last month.

The Patriots will be playing the Lions in Munich, Germany, on Sunday.

Henderson was listed on the injury report as dealing with an illness. The running back revealed last Friday that he recently experienced "an unexpected and unusual medical emergency involving a series of unexplained neurological symptoms." Henderson was hospitalized, but has said that test results have been positive and reassuring.

Starting running back Rhamondre Stevenson also didn't practice on Wednesday due to a knee injury, but has not yet been ruled out, though he is expected to miss a few weeks.

Sean McVay delivered a mix bag of news Wednesday regarding injuries to the team's top two receivers.

Davante Adams could be back sooner than expected from a hamstring injury, potentially in Week 13 vs. the Chiefs. Puka Nacua, meanwhile, will remain sidelined for a "significant period of time" with knee and toe injuries.

San Francisco head coach Kyle Shanahan said on Wednesday that Mike Evans remains week-to-week due to an Achilles injury. At this point, he has not been added to IR, so Evans could suit up this week against the Cowboys, but he is starting the week by not practicing.

Shanahan added that currently there is no timeline for Evans' return.

The Rams are expected to be without star wide receiver Puka Nacua for more than a month after suffering two knee sprains in the team's most recent game.

Nacua, who would normally be added to IR to free up a roster spot, won't be, according to head coach Sean McVay. L.A. is still expecting Nacua to miss extended time, but NFL teams are only allowed eight player activations from IR per season.

If Nacua gets added, with the intention of activating him in December, he would take up an activation from another player that the Rams were planning on activating, which would rule them out for the year.

L.A. currently has WR Davante Adams, G Justin Dedich, DT Poona Ford and DE Kobie Turner on IR and the Rams likely want to be able to activate all of them later this year. Los Angeles has already used four activations with EDGE Myles Garrett, TE Terrance Ferguson, OT Keagan Trost and RB Ronnie Rivers.

The 49ers acquired Keenan Allen ahead of Tuesday's trade deadline to help bolster the banged-up wide receiver room.

The only problem is that Allen could still miss time this season. Kyle Shanahan acknowledged to reporters on Wednesday that the veteran wideout could still face league discipline this season stemming from his DUI in August.

According to the current CBA, a violation of the league's substance-abuse policy, which would include a DUI, comes with a minimum three-game suspension. Additionally, Allen's BAC was so high that the league can increase the suspension if it so chooses.

The league has reportedly been waiting for the DUI case against Allen to play out legally before issuing the suspension.

At 3-6, and with a rash of injuries — including one to their most recent QB1 — the Arizona Cardinals are turning to rookie Carson Beck.

Arizona benched and then traded Jacoby Brissett. His replacement, Gardner Minshew, suffered a UCL injury this past week in Seattle during a loss to the Seahawks.

In their absence, the No. 65 overall pick in this year's draft is getting the nod in the desert.

Beck will make his first career NFL start on Sunday when the Los Angeles Rams come to town, head coach Mike LaFleur said Wednesday. The former Georgia and Miami star will be tasked with moving the ball against a defense that features premier edge rusher Myles Garrett and stout defensive tackle Aaron Donald. Beck will have to do so with a thin receiving corps.

Pittsburgh head coach Mike McCarthy announced on Wednesday that the team is opening the 21-day practice window for defensive tackle Derrick Harmon.

The 23-year-old defender has been sidelined because of a sprained foot he suffered against the Bengals in Week 4. In limited action this season, he has 12 tackles, including 1.5 sacks and a forced fumble.

Pittsburgh is allowing the seventh-fewest yards per game and the third-fewest rush yards per game this season.

The Patriots will cross the pond this weekend to take on the Detroit Lions in Munich, Germany, on Sunday morning.

New England wide receiver A.J. Brown will travel with the team, head coach Mike Vrabel said. While this doesn't automatically mean he will play in Sunday's game, Vrabel noted it gives him the chance.

Brown has missed the Patriots' last two games due to a knee injury. The wide receiver has only played in three games for New England this season, as he missed the early part of the season because of a sprained ankle in Week 1.

The wide receiver has caught nine of his 20 targets for 101 yards. Brown has yet to score a touchdown this year.

Tuesday's trade deadline was highlighted by the return of quarterback Kirk Cousins to the Minnesota Vikings.

Despite the move, on Wednesday, Minnesota head coach Kevin O'Connell wasn't ready to commit to Cousins as the starter this weekend against the Packers.

This could just be gamesmanship by O'Connell, but he told the media about Cousins starting: "I will say [we're] excited about the opportunity of spending every moment we can between now and then to get him comfortable and allow him to play for us this Sunday. That's what we're working towards, but also want to be fair to Kirk and make sure he feels good about it going to Lambeau Field."

Green Bay head coach Matt LaFleur said in his Wednesday press conference that he was surprised that the Raiders didn't get more for Cousins. "I was surprised [the Raiders] didn't get more for him, quite honestly. I was shocked when I saw what they traded him for."

Green Bay Packers running back Josh Jacobs may not end up on injured reserve despite needing surgery on his broken ring finger.

Jacobs, who is set to have that procedure later this week, could be back as soon as 2-3 weeks, according to NFL Network's Ian Rapoport, who also reported that Green Bay will decide Saturday if IR is necessary.

The Packers play the Minnesota Vikings on Sunday. But then they have a bye. There's a chance Jacobs returns for the following week's road game against the Los Angeles Rams.

The Chiefs will stick with Harrison Butker in Week 10, giving the kicker another chance to turn things around after weeks of struggles, head coach Andy Reid announced Wednesday.

After Sunday's win over the Jets, in which Butker missed another field goal attempt (a 51-yarder), Reid said that he would "look at everything" to assess whether it was time to move on. Evidently, Reid decided to stick with his long-time kicker.

Butker, who has spent his entire 10-year NFL career with the Chiefs, has seen his field goal accuracy crater this season. He's missed seven field goals in eight games so far this year, going 12-of-19 on attempts. That 63% completion rate is a noticeable dip from his previous decade of experience.

New York Jets wide receiver Garrett Wilson will be back at practice Wednesday, albeit in a limited capacity while coming off a concussion, per ESPN's Rich Cimini.

Wilson was sidelined during last week's 35-12 road loss to the Kansas City Chiefs.

He absorbed a blow to the head from safety Jeremy Chinn late in a Week 8 defeat to the Las Vegas Raiders. Wilson then reported concussion symptoms the following Monday, Jets head coach Aaron Glenn told reporters.

Wilson has piled up 43 receptions, 527 receiving yards and 3 receiving touchdowns this season.

The Cleveland Browns are expected to turn back to quarterback Deshaun Watson in Week 10, according to The Athletic.

The move was expected, as reports emerged Monday saying Watson would start for the team after clearing concussion protocol.

Browns head coach Todd Monken shot down those rumors Monday, however, saying he had yet to make a decision on the team's starter in Week 10.

With Watson sidelined, Shedeur Sanders drew his first start of the 2026 season. He nearly led the Browns to an overtime win in that start.

Watson, who missed the contest due to a concussion, reportedly cleared league protocol Friday. Despite that, the was not active for Sunday's game against the New Orleans Saints.

The Dallas Cowboys have signed former Saints running back Kendre Miller to their practice squad, according to multiple reports.

Miller played sparingly in three-plus seasons as a backup for the Saints, tallying 656 rushing yards on 3.6 yards per carry in 27 appearances. The Saints released the former third-round pick on Saturday after signing Audric Estime off waivers from the Cleveland Browns.

With backup running back Tyler Goodson sidelined with a knee sprain, Miller adds a potential depth piece behind starter Javonte Williams.

Acquiring Kirk Cousins wasn't the only notable Vikings roster move on Tuesday.

Shortly after Tuesday's 4 p.m. ET NFL trade deadline, the Vikings announced that they have released wide receiver Odell Beckham Jr. Per NFL insider Tom Pelissero, Beckham will re-sign with Minnesota's practice squad.

The transaction clears a spot on the 53-man roster that can make room for Cousins.

Beckham joined the Vikings after being released by the Giants three games into his return to New York. Beckham played in just one game for Minneosta, Monday's disastrous loss to the Bills that ultimately prompted them to trade for Cousins on Tuesday. He did not catch a pass for the Vikings.

The Ravens and Falcons pulled off a buzzer-beater trade right at the deadline.

Baltimore sent G Andrew Vorhees and a 2028 6th-round pick to Atlanta for C Ryan Neuzil, per The Athletic's Jonathan Jones.

Vorhees was a backup guard for the Ravens, while Neuzil was the Falcons' starting center.
