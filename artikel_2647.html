NFL news, live updates: Aidan Hutchinson suffers 'significant' knee injury while celebrating sack; Brock Bowers exits early with ankle injury in Fernando Mendoza's first start

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Another NFL Sunday is in the books, with just one game left in Week 10 as the Baltimore Ravens host the LA Chargers on "Monday Night Football."

Sunday kicked off with a bang as the Detroit Lions and New England Patriots faced off in Munich, Germany, with injuries to two stars that will have all eyes drawn as the new week plays out. Lions star defensive end Aidan Hutchinson suffered what is feared to be a season-ending knee injury while celebrating a sack against Patriots quarterback Drake Maye … who suffered an ankle injury on that very sack, which knocked him out of the game and could cost him the season.

Week 10 also offered us our first look at Las Vegas Raiders No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza as a starter. He led scoring drives on each of the Raiders' first two possessions and entered halftime with a 10-7 lead over the Seattle Seahawks, but Drew Lock rallied with two passing touchdowns in the third quarter to take the lead, improving to 3-0 as a starter for Seattle this season. Raiders star tight end Brock Bowers exited the loss with an ankle injury.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for the latest NFL news, injury updates and more coming out of Week 10.

Detroit Lions pass rusher Aidan Hutchinson reportedly tore his ACL while celebrating a sack of Drake Maye, per NFL Network.

Early reports Sunday suggested Hutchinson was dealing with a "significant" knee injury. Initial tests reportedly confirmed as much Monday.

Hutchinson will miss the rest of the 2026 NFL season as he recovers from the injury. It's unclear whether his availability will be impacted next season.

Brock Bowers didn't last long on Sunday afternoon.

The Las Vegas Raiders tight end was ruled out of their 21-13 loss to the Seattle Seahawks due to an ankle injury he suffered early in the first quarter. That knocked him out for the majority of Fernando Mendoza's starting debut at Allegiant Stadium.

Bowers, in the first quarter of the contest, went up to block Seahawks safety Nick Emmanwori for running back Ashton Jeanty. But as Jeanty cut the other way, Bowers had his legs rolled up on in a pile, and he went down awkwardly.

Bowers remained down for a few moments while the team's training staff helped him, and he then limped to the sideline.

Bowers did return to the game briefly after that, which helped lead the way for Mendoza's rushing touchdown to get them on the board. But he left the game again late in the second quarter, and the team ruled him out for good just before the start of the second half with the ankle injury. Clearly, it was just too much for him to overcome.

Further specifics of his injury are not yet known.

The Tampa Bay Buccaneers are currently on bye, but unfortunately, they are still in the news.

Buccaneers quarterback Baker Mayfield is dealing with a lower-leg injury that he suffered during last week's loss to the Chicago Bears, NFL Network's Mike Garafolo reported on Sunday morning.

Mayfield's status is uncertain for Tampa Bay's Week 11 matchup with the Detroit Lions. It is unclear where Mayfield picked up the injury against the Bears. The quarterback was sacked four times, but he never left the game. This week, he was reportedly spotted in a walking boot, rehabbing and resting.

This would not be the first time this season the Buccaneers have played without their starting quarterback. Mayfield dislocated his thumb in Week 3, which kept him out for three games. Tampa Bay turned to undrafted rookie quarterback Jalon Daniels, who went 1-2 as the starter.

Mayfield has played in six games so far this season and is 2-4. He has not looked like himself all year, throwing for just 922 yards, 3 touchdowns and 4 interceptions. In a bad-weather Week 8 game against the Atlanta Falcons, he finished 0-for-3 for zero yards in the team's 16-0 win, making infamous history.

A frustrating season for Los Angeles Chargers wideout Ladd McConkey will continue in Week 10. While the receiver was initially listed as questionable on the team's final injury report, he will not play against the Baltimore Ravens on "Monday Night Football."

McConkey was ruled out for the game due to back spasms. He did not travel with the team to Baltimore, the Chargers announced.

McConkey has been limited by multiple injuries throughout the regular season. He was held to just 35 yards in Week 2 thanks to a rib injury and missed Week 5 due to a foot issue.

He caught just one ball in Week 6 before resting during the bye week. While the wideout looked to be close to 100 percent with an 86-yard performance in Week 8, McConkey missed Week 9 with back spasms. The injury reportedly occurred just before game time. He was expected to play in the contest.

Those back spasms have apparently continued to plague McConkey throughout the week. He was held out of practice each day leading up to Week 10, though the team still held some hope he would play, initially giving him the questionable tag.

But the issue was bad enough that the team opted against throwing McConkey on the field Monday night.

Raiders tight end Brock Bowers reportedly avoided a major ankle injury in Week 10. Bowers will have more tests done Monday, but the early indication is that he's more day-to-day.

Bowers left Sunday's game against the Seahawks due to the issue. He finished with just 18 yards.

The injury put a damper on the debut of No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza, who lost his best receiver in the contest.

The New England Patriots could be without quarterback Drake Maye for the rest of the season. Maye may have sustained a serious leg injury during Sunday's loss to the Detroit Lions, with the Patriots fearing a possible ankle fracture.

Maye did not travel to the United States with the team after Sunday's game, staying in Germany to undergo additional tests. Those tests could reveal whether Maye needs surgery.

No matter what those tests reveal, Maye is looking at an extended absence, if not a season-ending one.

Detroit Lions star Aidan Hutchinson made his presence known early against the New England Patriots. On the Patriots' first offensive drive, Hutchinson broke through the line on third down and sacked Drake Maye.

Following the play, Hutchinson got up to celebrate the sack. He jumped into the air and immediately fell to the ground after landing. Hutchinson appeared to be holding his right knee.

Hutchinson walked off the field, but headed straight to the medical tent on the sideline. He was later spotted on the broadcast heading to the locker room.

He was deemed questionable to return with a knee injury, but late in the third quarter, he was ruled out for the remainder of the game.

Detroit eventually cruised to a 35-10 victory.

Early reports suggested Hutchinson may have sustained a "significant" knee injury, per NFL Network. Detroit is reportedly not expecting good news. While a second opinion is coming, it's possible Hutchinson will not return this season.
