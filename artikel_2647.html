Dan Campbell says Buccaneers are better than their record says they are

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Lions head coach Dan Campbell played in the NFL for Bill Parcells, who liked to say, “You are what your record says you are.” That’s not how Campbell views this week’s opponent.

With the Lions preparing to face the 3-6 Buccaneers on Sunday, Campbell said he thinks they’re a much better team than that record suggests, and he noted that Tampa Bay is only a game out of first place in the NFC South.

“It’s a physical team. These guys play with violence. Todd Bowles always does a great job. Their record is not indicative of the type of team they are. They’re in the hunt for the South. So we know what we have in front of us,” Campbell said.

In the NFL’s worst division, the Buccaneers have a real chance to be a playoff team, and Sunday’s game could even be a playoff preview. That’s how Campbell wants his players to view it: As a game against a playoff opponent, not against a team that’s 3-6.
