Astros DH Yordan Alvarez Wins Second Silver Slugger Award

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

HOUSTON, TX – Louisville Slugger announced tonight that Astros designated hitter Yordan Alvarez has won his second career American League Silver Slugger Award. Additionally, it was also announced that the Astros have won the AL Silver Slugger Team Award for the first time in franchise history.

Established in 1980, the Louisville Slugger Silver Slugger Awards honor the best offensive producers at each position in both the American and National Leagues. Positional winners are determined by a vote of managers and coaches who cast ballots in their respective leagues for players at each position. The Silver Slugger Team Award was added in 2023.

Alvarez, who was named as the AL MVP last week, batted .316 (179×567) with 105 runs, 34 doubles, one triple, 42 home runs, 106 RBI, 109 walks and a 1.033 OPS in 158 games. Alvarez won the American League and Major League batting titles, becoming the third player in franchise history to win a batting title, joining 1B Yuli Gurriel (2021) and 2B Jose Altuve (2014, 2016-17). He also led the AL in OPS, slugging percentage (.601), on-base percentage (.432), total bases (341), walks (109), intentional walks (33), wRC+ (181) OPS+ (190) and extra-base hits (77), while also ranking tied for second in home runs, second in RBI, second in hits, fourth in doubles and fourth in runs scored. Additionally, Alvarez was the only player in the Majors this season to post an OPS over 1.000.

He became just the third player since 1970 to win the AL batting title while hitting 40-or-more home runs, joining NYY OF Aaron Judge (2025) and DET 1B Miguel Cabrera (2012-13). This season, Alvarez won three AL Player of the Week honors along with two AL Player of the Month honors. Alvarez was also selected to his fourth career All-Star Game, and it was the second time that he was voted as a starter (also, 2024). In addition to earning AL MVP honors, Alvarez also won the AL Hank Aaron Award, the AL Comeback Player of the Year, the Edgar Martinez Outstanding Designated Hitter Award and was an All-MLB First Team selection. He also was named as the Astros MVP by the Houston Chapter of the BBWAA.

In their franchise history, the Astros have 14 Silver Slugger Award winners who have now combined for a total of 29 awards overall: Altuve (2014-18, 2022, 2024), OF Kyle Tucker (2023), DH Yordan Alvarez (2022, 2026), 3B Alex Bregman (2019), OF George Springer (2017, 2019), OF Carlos Lee (2007), 3B Morgan Ensberg (2005), LHP Mike Hampton (1999), OF Moises Alou (1998), 1B Jeff Bagwell (1994, 1997, 1999), Craig Biggio (catcher in 1989, second baseman in 1994-95, 1997-98), 1B Glenn Davis (1986), OF José Cruz (1983-84) and SS Dickie Thon (1983).

As a team in 2026, the Astros led the Majors in home runs with 226 and ranked first in the AL in OPS (.733) and slugging percentage (.417). This year was the first time that the Astros led the Majors in home runs since 2000, hitting 249 home runs that season.
