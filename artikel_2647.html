Ben Johnson: D'Andre Swift doesn't need to regain trust after fumble

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Bears running back D’Andre Swift did not return to last Sunday’s game after losing a fumble in the fourth quarter and his role moving forward was a topic at head coach Ben Johnson’s press conference on Tuesday.

Swift has now lost three fumbles this season and he’s spent time on the bench after two of them, which made it natural that Johnson fielded a question about whether the back needs to regain the trust of the coaching staff. Johnson said that is not the case.

“Nope. He just needs to hold on to the ball and we’ll be fine,” Johnson said.

Swift had 51 yards on 13 carries against the Saints and is averaging 3.7 yards per carry on the season and the Bears backs have been less efficient running the ball since a 232-yard game against the Jets in Week 4. The Bears have still won five of their last six games, but finding a way to be more productive on the ground would be a good way to keep the wins coming in a competitive race for playoff positions in the NFC.
