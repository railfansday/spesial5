Most underrated NBA players, Nuggets-Mavs takeaways & Magic frustrations with Dave DuFour

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Kevin O'Connor is joined by The Athletic's Dave DuFour to dissect why Denver's aging core, led by Nikola Jokic and Aaron Gordon, is raising alarm bells and what it means for their playoff hopes. Meanwhile, Minnesota's league-leading defense powered by Rudy Gobert and Jaden McDaniels could elevate them to true contender status in the West.

The duo also breaks down Detroit's surprising upside thanks to players like Ausar Thompson, while Orlando's lack of flexibility and shooting sparks frustration for fans and the front office alike. Plus, KOC & Dave answer listener mailbag questions including which players, like Dyson Daniels, Cedric Coward and Deni Avdija don't get enough credit in the league.
