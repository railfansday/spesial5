Packers Roster: Green Bay’s emergency signing board

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Throughout the season, the Green Bay Packers bring in players for tryouts to either push the current practice squad as an immediate signing or to build a board of emergency players if a few injuries start to build up in the future. Since it's the bye week, I decided to take a fresh look at all of the players that the Packers have brought in so far in 2026 (56 in total) and paint a picture of who is still available.

We'll break these tryout players down into five groups:

Players who are signed with other NFL teams

Players whose rights have been claimed by UFL teams

DL Haggai Ndubuisi – international exemption, PS

The Packers have signed two offensive linemen to their 53-man roster after tryouts (Kevin Zeitler and Mekhi Becton), but Becton was initially signed to the practice squad before being promoted up. Currently, 10 of Green Bay's 17 practice squad spots have been filled by players brought in on tryouts. The only practice squad players who have been with the team since training camp are WR Isaiah Neyor, TE Drake Dabney, OL Dalton Cooper, OL Dillon Wade, ED Arron Mosby, ED Nyjalik Kelly and CB Domani Jackson.

RB Damon Bankston (Vikings PS) – previously on Packers PS

OL Cordell Volson (Seahawks PS) – previously on Packers PS

None of these players are available for the Packers to sign to the practice squad, since they are already signed to another team's practice squad, but Green Bay could sign them to the 53-man roster as a poach. If the Packers sign another team's practice squad player, they must guarantee that player's pay and roster spot for three full weeks of the regular season.

DL David Blay Jr. – previously on Packers PS

DL Eric Johnson II – previously on Packers PS

ED Cruz Smith – international exemption eligible

S Kahlef Hailassie – previously on Packers PS

These are players who are completely on the street. If you want to think about the emergency board, these are the bones of that board right now.

The UFL just had its draft, which included teams selecting multiple Packers visitors to the league. Players with the "retained" label are 2026 UFL players whom Green Bay worked out, while players with the "draft" label are new players to the UFL whom the Packers worked out before they were picked in the recent draft.

Players who were picked in the recent draft are not all under contract with the league yet, so there's no guarantee they even play spring ball.

Usually, the UFL allows its players to seek NFL employment up to the week after the Super Bowl. If players sign reserve/futures contracts with NFL teams, though, they become ineligible to play in the upcoming UFL season, as they're under contract with another league.

This one is a special case. The Packers worked out cornerback Zy Alexander, coming off a season in the CFL, last week. Alexander cannot sign with an NFL team (would likely be a reserve/futures deal) until after the regular season is over, but he also cannot sign with an NFL team after the CFL free agency period starts in early February.

So right now, Alexander can't sign with an NFL club, but he will have a window when he can be added. He can currently work out with teams, though, as long as NFL clubs file a request with the player's CFL club (which can't be denied).
