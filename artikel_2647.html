Chicago Bears stock watch: Zavion Thomas arrives, defense shows out

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Chicago Bears started their second-half slate with a 21-13 win over the New Orleans Saints.

But there were concerns among fans about how the Bears looked in their victory. Or rather, how they didn't look.

Not enough points. Sluggish run game at times.

The Bears led from wire-to-wire, again. They had 146 team rushing yards.

For an 8-2 team with sights on a deep postseason run, the expectation is that you win by two touchdowns over a three-win team.

This is still new territory for Bears-dom.

There is still a lot to like about the Bears' win Sunday, so let's get to it.

Zavion Thomas, WR – Ben Johnson seems to like to bring his rookies along a bit slower. Maybe it's trust, maybe it's his sizeable playbook, but whatever it is, it is the norm. But it appears those rookie training wheels might be coming off now. Thomas had 86 yards from scrimmage with a 30-yard run and 56 receiving yards.

Clark Phillips, CB – The DB, who came to Chicago as part of the late-August Gervon Dexter trade, has started making an impact. He had a huge game against the Saints. He had a sack, a forced fumble, a pass breakup and a tackle-for-loss.

Montez Sweat, DE – In his first five games this season, prior to his suspension: 2 sacks, 2 TFLs, 4 QB hits. In the three games since the Jeffery Simmons trade: 2 sacks, 3 TFLs, 7 QB hits and 1 safety. Simmons has elevated the defense as a whole, but perhaps no one player got better than Sweat.

Garret Bradbury, C – Bradbury has been the weak link on the OL all season, but it was painfully obvious on Sunday. He was able to return to the game after missing one play with an injury, but the calls for rookie Logan Jones are getting louder.

D'Andre Swift, RB – Unfortunately, Swift didn't appear to be much healthier following the bye week. A few of his early runs had some pop, but he was still under four yards per carry. Worse, though, was his third lost fumble of the season.

Tyrique Stevenson, CB – Chicago has to get the best out of what it has in the secondary. It's too late in the season to make impactful additions. Stevenson has had some moments, but not many of them came Sunday. Chris Olave is a tough player to defend and Stevenson was often playing catch up.
