LSU Soccer upsets No. 23 Auburn, Advances to SEC Tournament Quarterfinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The 11th-seeded LSU Soccer team (5-9-5) opened its 2026 SEC Tournament campaign with a 1-0 upset victory over sixth-seeded and No. 23 nationally ranked Auburn (8-3-7) on Sunday afternoon at the Ashton Brosnaham Soccer Complex in Pensacola, Florida.

The Tigers secured their third consecutive SEC Tournament opening-round victory over Auburn, continuing a streak that dates back to the 2024 tournament. With the win, LSU advances to the SEC Tournament quarterfinals, where it will face third-seeded and No. 8 nationally ranked Alabama on Tuesday, November 10, at 2 p.m. CT.
