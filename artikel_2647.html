Micah Parsons, Josh Jacobs listed as limited in practice again on Monday

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Packers’ injury report for Monday looks a lot like the one they turned in on Sunday.

Defensive end Micah Parsons (knee) and running back Josh Jacobs (finger) remained limited participants. Parsons is expected to make his 2026 debut on Wednesday night against the Rams and Jacobs is trying to return after missing the Packers’ last game in Week 10.

Linebacker Zaire Franklin (shin), offensive lineman Travis Glover (shoulder), linebacker Isaiah McDuffie (rib), wide receiver Bo Melton (thigh), wide receiver Skyy Moore (ankle), center Sean Rhyan (ankle), and guard Kevin Zeitler (triceps) were limited participants. Glover was the only new addition to that group on Monday.

Cornerback Brandon Cisse (rib) moved from limited to full participation while guard Aaron Banks (knee) remained in that category.
