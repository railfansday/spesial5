Malaysia Removes About 40,000 Bullying Posts in Online Crackdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Malaysia said online platforms removed more than 40,000 pieces of bullying-related content this year at the government’s request, as the Southeast Asian nation steps up efforts to protect children from harmful material on social media.
