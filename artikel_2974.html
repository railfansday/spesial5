Falcons Draft 2027: Five Prospects for Falcons Fans to Watch for Week 12 of College Football

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

College football's regular season is in its waning moments, and every game counts as we approach the final season of the 12-team college football playoff. This week is full of trap games where teams fighting for their playoff lives meet teams who want nothing more than to play spoiler, and players who want nothing more than to boost their draft stock against some of the more prominent prospects in the 2027 NFL Draft class.

Here are five prospects who Atlanta Falcons fans should keep an eye on in this intriguing Week 12 slate of college football games.

Arkansas has a chance to put the nail in the coffin for Texas, which LSU simply couldn't do last week, and if they do, it'll start with a big performance from their standout receiver, Chris Marshall. At 6'3", 210 pounds, I see a clear vision of adding him to the Falcons' receiver room and immediately adding some verticality to the passing game. He will have to get past Texas cornerback Bo Mascoe and safety Jelani McDonald to pull off the upset.

The Falcons need a franchise right tackle and Jawaan Taylor simply isn't it. Drew Azzopardi already has the size and technique to be a good blindside protector of Michael Penix or a solid right tackle who can win in pass pro in general. However, he still has a way to go in the run game, but he has the athleticism to do it. With Bill Callahan in tow, I think Azzopardi would be a solid day-two prospect to invest in. Against Indiana, he has another chance to show off his pass protection against one of the Big Ten's best edges, Tobi Osunsanmi.

This safety class is absolutely electric and Oklahoma's Peyton Bowen is no exception. A versatile safety who plays the run well and can get his hands on the ball, if Oklahoma is going to make good on their late bid for a playoff spot, it will be behind this Oklahoma defense. The Sooners should make light work of Texas A&M, as long as the Oklahoma front can get past Mark Nabou Jr. and this Aggie offensive line.

There is a lot of talk about how this quarterback class has not lived up to the billing, and for the most part, it hasn't. With most of the highly touted guys potentially going back to school to be a part of the 2028 class, including Drew Mestemaker and Arch Manning, it opens the door for guys like Darian Mensah to play into QB1 territory. Mensah is a highly accurate passer who has shown he knows how to use his talent, especially with top target Malachi Toney. The question lies in whether he can continue his high level of play against higher competition outside of the ACC.

Despite an up-and-down 2026, Jacarrius Peak is still on my radar as a potential day-two tackle the Falcons could be in on. A smooth mover in the run game, great feet in the pass game, he is extremely raw in terms of technique, but there is so much athletic upside. Peak has a great opportunity to showcase how well he can keep LaNorris Sellers upright against NFL-caliber talent as he goes up against Chris Cole, Raylen Wilson, and this Georgia defense.
