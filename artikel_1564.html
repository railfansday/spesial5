PSG: former Titi Oumar Camara reveals the reasons for his departure

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

19-year-old left winger, Oumar Camara is performing well in Portugal. The Vitória Guimarães player has 7 goals and 3 assists in 46 matches since his arrival in the summer of 2025. The native of Aubervilliers quickly reached a milestone after being trained at Paris Saint-Germain. A club where he didn’t really have any prospects, as he confided to The Team : “playing time in the first team. And then the confidence, the certainty that it is a springboard club. In 2024, at PSG, the Espoirs group was not yet something consolidated. I had just completed two seasons in U19. I had the feeling of having validated this first step and of not having the prospect of going higher. »

Winner of the Portuguese League Cup with Vitória Guimarães and with regularity, he found an easier context in which to flourish than within the Ile-de-France club. According to him, it remains difficult for a young person trained at PSG to have a chance: “It really depends on the case. But I perceived the project as vague. They tell you that you’re going to start again with the pros, but after that, what happens? Will there be continuity? Are we going to go back down? I also think that quite a few generations were held back by the elimination of the reserve team (in 2019). There is a luck factor, too. Quentin Ndjantou, last year, he scored a hat-trick in the Youth League, there were injuries in the first team, and he is progressing. He played a little. The most important thing is to string together the minutes. At our ages, that’s the main thing. And here in Vitória, I do it. If I had to make this choice again, I would 1000% do it again. »
