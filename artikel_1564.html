Manchester United makes a radical decision with Michael Carrick!

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Third in the Premier League last season under the orders of Michael Carrick, Manchester United thought they were on the verge of lastingly returning to the top of the English championship. However, this was not confirmed during the current financial year. After ten league matches, Manchester United have won only one match and are in eighteenth place in the Premier League. Relegated, the Red Devils are one point behind first non-relegation Coventry.

A very complicated situation which has made the future of coach Michael Carrick uncertain for many weeks. “No. Continue as usual. What must happen will happen», he confided this Saturday. It’s been a stunning week for the Red Devils. Led 2-0 by AS Roma last Tuesday, Manchester United finally won 3-2 at the end of the match. This Sunday, the Red Devils were led by Aston Villa 2-0 then 4-2 but snatched the 4-4 following two goals in added time.

A crazy new scenario that is not popular with the local media. THE Daily Mail notably shot the team and its coach: “Comebacks won’t save United and Carrick failure. Manchester doesn’t have a football team, but a collection of adrenaline junkies. Over time this will not be enough. Carrick’s players are so deficient that they won’t be able to keep their coach any longer, they look untrained.”

However, this seems to have granted the Mancunian coach a reprieve. The latter declared that he was not going to resign: “it won’t happen. We haven’t won enough games, but I’m looking forward to continuing to work, tightening things up and winning football games.» And his managers do not seem to want to fire him, according to certain English media. Although the international break represents an ideal opening to change coaches, the tendency seems to maintain continuity as reported The Mirror And the Manchester Evening News. For the moment it’s dead calm on the Carrington side, it remains to be seen whether the Mancunian board will change its tune or not.
