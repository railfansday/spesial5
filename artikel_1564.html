Roma set sights on Lookman, but Atletico ward off interest

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Roma has turned its attention to Atlético Madrid’s Lookman.

After making a difference in the first few months, the player hasn’t been able to maintain the consistency needed for a team like Simeone’s.

In Spain, questions are being raised as to whether the Nigerian is eager to regain the coach’s trust, and it’s been highlighted that Roma is considering a January signing.

In any case, any easy enthusiasm is being dampened: without a signing of equal or superior quality, according to Mundo Deportivo, Simeone is unlikely to give the green light to the move. This is why the transfer has little chance of coming to fruition.
