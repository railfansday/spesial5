PSG: Lucas Beraldo reacts to his 100th in the Parisian jersey

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A symbolic evening for Lucas Beraldo. On the occasion of Paris Saint-Germain’s victory against Troyes (1-0), this Saturday evening, the Brazilian defender reached an important milestone by playing his 100th match in Parisian colors. A special moment for the 22-year-old, who did not hide his emotion at the final whistle. “It was an incredible match for me and my family, I hope to do more. We won tonight, that was the most important thing.”he confided in the mixed zone.

The Brazilian also wanted to acknowledge the support of the Parisian public, particularly present for this special evening since the Virage Auteuil was celebrating its 35th anniversary. “The fans are always amazing, it was a special day for them and I want to say thank you for the support and for the party”he said. Finally, Beraldo looked ahead to the rest of the season, satisfied to see PSG approach the international break with positive momentum: “it was very important for us to arrive now with all these victories and we hope to continue after the break”. A 100th appearance celebrated in the best possible way.
