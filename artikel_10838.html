“Determined” woman completes 7 marathons in a year while pregnant

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A woman has revealed that she completed seven marathons in 2026 despite being pregnant for a few of them, admitting that it was a bit “bizarre” at times.

For a lot of people, running a marathon is a goal that they work towards for months, if not years. Some want to set an impressive time, while others just want to be able to say they’ve completed the 26.2 miles.

In the case of Deirdre Keane, the 38-year-old is a seasoned long-distance runner, completing more than 60 marathons to this point. However, throughout 2026, she has been running marathons with an additional hurdle to deal with: she has been pregnant throughout.

“I knew how bizarre it was to be running a marathon at 34 weeks, but I also knew how fit I was and self-aware of my body at this point,” she told the Associated Press, admitting it is a “very unusual” challenge, but noted that she isn’t the first woman to do so.

As per the AP, the 38-year-old had signed up for 11 races in 2026, including the seven major marathons: Berlin, Boston, Chicago, London, New York, Sydney and Tokyo.

“I was like, this is going to be my last hurrah before settling down and becoming pregnant, becoming a mother,” Keane added. However, she didn’t discover she was pregnant until after running the Tokyo marathon, when she noticed her pace had started to slip.

“I don’t think she thought I would do them towards the end, especially being so pregnant at New York. But I am very stubborn and determined,” Keane added. “Plus, I got lucky; I was feeling good. So I was able to do it.”
