Maitake “Bacon”

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

“Maitake is one of the tastiest and most versatile things you’ll find growing at the base of a rotting tree,” writes Alexis Nikole Nelson, better known as the Black Forager, in her debut cookbook, Happy Snacking, Don’t Die! Here, Nelson harnesses the savory, meaty qualities of the mushroom (which also goes by the name hen of the woods) to turn it into little hunks of bacon-y goodness that are salty, smoky, and the tiniest bit sweet.

Adapted from Happy Snacking, Don’t Die! by Alexis Nikole Nelson with Jeanelle Hayner Olson. Copyright © 2026. Available from Simon Element.
