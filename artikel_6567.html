Warm Up Your Thanksgiving Weekend With Fresh From the Runway Sweet Potato-Orange Dressing

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A 70 degree day followed by a powerful coastal storm? The week of Thanksgiving? You know what? Sure. While this unexpected bit of weather whiplash may be throwing a sartorial wrench into your traditional fall dressing, we over here at Vogue HQ are nonetheless taking the opportunity to warm up our palettes with some autumnal holiday-friendly hues. It’s become something of an annual tradition to pluck holiday dressing inspiration straight off of our plates, and this year’s sweet potato orange tips its hat to the shocking shade that we can’t escape.

Mildly abrasive, yet comforting, and a whole statement on its own, orange has wiggled its way back into the zeitgeist as the unexpected little sister to the cherry red that swept the runways for the better part of this year (it was also well represented here at the Vogue offices). So, this holiday season, consider the jubilant brightness of a raw sweet potato orange Prada knit (Mrs. Prada has an eye for anointing It colors before we even knew we needed them, after all), or the shimmering shade of a maple-glazed Chanel sequin set. For a purist autumnal feeling, Dior’s petaled dresses have got you covered. Meanwhile, Marni is all about mod; and for the guys, a chunky Ralph Lauren rugby shirt is always a hit. What are you waiting for? Dig in!
