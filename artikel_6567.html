Who Will Be the Next-Gen Bond Girls? Here Are 45 Contenders

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Who will be the next James Bond? With final screen tests having recently taken place, we may now be closer to an answer than ever before.

But when the news does come, attention will quickly turn to another pressing question: who will make up the next generation of Bond girls? Far from being the helpless ingénues of yore, under the direction of new Bond helmer Denis Villeneuve, we presume these women will be more than a match for 007—rival agents and worthy adversaries, perhaps, alongside the inevitable love interests. Here are 45 contenders who would fit the bill.

If her husband, Jack Lowden, does indeed become 007, could the Irish four-time Oscar nominee come aboard, too? They’d be very lucky to have her.

Post-Obsession, the 2027 Oscar hopeful has joined the MCU as Rogue in the next X-Men movie, so she’s clearly not opposed to blockbuster franchise work. As ridiculously talented newcomer-turned-overnight sensations go, she feels destined to be a Bond girl.

Having already collaborated with Denis Villeneuve on the Dune trilogy—not to mention being one of the most bankable actors working today—the fashion favorite should be at the front of the queue.

Like mother like daughter: back in 2015, Monica Bellucci was a Bond girl opposite Daniel Craig in Spectre. Could her 22-year-old progeny, a vision in Netflix’s recent ravishing period drama The Leopard, follow in her footsteps?

From Squid Game to the barnstorming new Korean monster movie Hope, the model-turned-actor has proved her chops and would be a worthy partner in crime for 007—particularly if the new plot line takes him east.

After collecting a number of big-screen credits—Erupcja, 100 Nights of Hero, The Moment, I Want Your Sex—would the Brit join the Bond franchise as a scintillating fellow secret agent? Hell, she could write the new song, too.

The Bridgerton breakout is a national treasure who’s long deserved a splashy big-screen part.

As a wisecracking foil to a somber Bond, who could be better than the Bottoms and The Bear star?

If Bond’s latest mission takes him to Scandinavia, it’s easy to see the Norwegian Oscar nominee and indie darling–best known for The Worst Person in the World, Sentimental Value, Backrooms, and now Fjord—joining him as a steely colleague.

The Mancunian Oscar nominee lit up the screen in Sinners and would do the same in any Bond movie.

Mosaku’s The Social Reckoning co-star, a recent Oscar winner, has been tipped as a potential Bond girl ever since her magnetic turn in Anora.

If Bond heads to the Indian subcontinent on his mission, might I suggest casting a Bollywood powerhouse and red-carpet regular with more than 85 million followers as his local liaison?

Having delivered wonderfully sensitive performances in everything from Waves to Bones and All, as well as on the National Theatre stage in The Effect, the Canadian actor would be a thoughtful, high-brow choice.

The chameleonic Past Lives star could be a quippy fellow agent (remember her in Russian Doll?) just as easily as she could morph into a terrifying villain.

Since Normal People, the Londoner has been ticking projects off her bucket list: she’s starred in a big-budget summer blockbuster (Twisters) and now played a Jane Austen heroine (Sense and Sensibility), so could taking the part of a Bond girl be next?

After her awards-laden run on Shōgun, the Japanese actor will be playing Yoko Ono in the four Beatles movies. If I had to put money on anyone snagging a Bond girl role, it’d be her.

Another Bridgerton fixture who’s since gone stratospheric, the Brit fits the classic Bond girl mold—impossibly beautiful and surprisingly steely (never forget her throwing punches in the Regency romp’s first season).

The One Day star is showing off her action-heroine chops on the second season of Black Doves opposite Keira Knightley. Could that act as an audition for a bigger big-screen part?

She played Carolyn Bessette Kennedy to widespread acclaim—slipping into a Bond girl’s stilettos feels like the obvious next move.

Her turn as Catwoman proved she’s a high-kicking, gun-toting force to be reckoned with. After that, being a Bond girl would surely be a walk in the park.

She’s an Oscar-nominated British A-lister, outstanding in everything from Lady Macbeth and Little Women to Midsommar and, recently, East of Eden. Need we say more?

With her winking sense of humor and laid-back glamour, it’d be a thrill to see the Vogue favorite as a Bond girl.

After One Battle After Another brought her to prominence, the American actor led Hulu’s The Testaments. A plum part as a Bond girl could be an ideal next step.

One of the most powerful actors and producers in Hollywood right now, the Australian is prime Bond girl material in every sense.

She leapt to the top of Bond girl leaderboards back in 2025, when she performed in a 007 tribute number at the Oscars. She’s been sensational in everything from Fosse/Verdon and Maid to The Substance, and certainly has the charisma to pull it off.

Another fashion favorite who ought to get a look-in is the Chicago-born star of BlacKkKlansman and Hollywood.

Could the Brit of House of the Dragon and The Girlfriend fame get Bond to trade in his vodka martini for a negroni… sbagliato… with prosecco in it? We think so.

If we need a madcap Bond girl to get 007 out of a bind, the Marty Supreme and I Love LA star feels like a safe bet.

We’re so ready for the Hustlers and Nope star to say “sorry to this man” to 007.

Now that Euphoria is over, the LA native is due her big-screen breakout moment.

Funny Bond girls were few and far between in the Daniel Craig era, but casting the Sex Education and The White Lotus breakout would be hearkening back to that tradition in classic Bond movies of the ’70s.

Another star of One Battle After Another who really should be considered is the very funny, always sharply dressed and extremely talented Oscar nominee.

Like Alexa Demie, the fashion stalwart has graduated from Euphoria and, despite parts in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes, Kinds of Kindness, and Mother Mary, is still waiting for her big-screen movie star moment. A Bond girl role would be just the ticket.

After her stint on The Celebrity Traitors, the Industry star is at least an honorary Brit. Make her a Bond girl, stat.

Her extraordinary lead performance in How to Have Sex put her on the map, and she’ll soon be in the Beatles movies (as Ringo Starr’s first wife, Maureen Cox). The Brit feels ripe for another high-profile casting moment.

After Wednesday, could the Californian play a deadpan Bond girl next?

The Emma, The Queen’s Gambit, and Dune franchise star is another actor who feels like something of a no-brainer for the part of the next Bond girl.

The exceedingly gifted Londoner could easily go head-to-head with 007; just recall her sly turn in Prime Video’s Mr. and Mrs. Smith reboot.

A scene-stealer in the final season of Euphoria, she could bring the same flair and ferociousness to the Bond franchise. (She could pen the next Bond song, too!)

Another Industry alum who has the chops and innate glamour needed to pull off a Bond girl role is the captivating Brit.

Following the end of Stranger Things, the now-all-grown-up Texan could very well be in with a shot.

The Puerto Rico-born, Mexico City-raised actor charmed us as the sizzling femme fatale in Hit Man opposite Glen Powell—and seems set to do so again in Michael B. Jordan’s The Thomas Crown Affair. She would undoubtedly be a stellar foil for Bond.

From Yellowjackets to Heretic, Companion and Her Private Hell, the American actor has become her generation’s most sought-after steely scream queen—resilient, mesmeric, even deadly. Ideal qualities for a Bond girl, too, one could argue…

The Liverpudlian has herself played a notorious assassin in Killing Eve, meaning a match-up with Bond would be one for the ages.
