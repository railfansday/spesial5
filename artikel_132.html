Trump reportedly says pardoning Jensen Huang is ‘an option’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

During an interview with the New York Post on Tuesday, President Trump said that pardoning Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, who has been ensnared in a scandal involving an alleged Chinese spy, is “an option,” albeit one that he doesn’t think he will have to exercise.

“It’s always an option, I guess, but I don’t think it’ll be necessary,” the president reportedly told the outlet.

Huang, who was ousted from his own company less than two weeks ago, was reinstated Monday. He faces ongoing questions about his relationship with Alice Huang, an executive with alleged ties to Chinese intelligence who previously worked for a company that has been accused of illegally diverting Nvidia chips to China.

Huang is scheduled to testify at a public Senate Banking Committee hearing next week.

When asked about the hearing, Trump reportedly said: “I think I want him to just tell the truth, and I mean, his lawyers are going to have to advise him, but I’ve always found him to be 100% aboveboard, and you know a very smart person.”

At another point in the interview, when asked what he knew about Huang’s other extramarital affairs, Trump reportedly said: “I really didn’t get into that. I got into the business of chips, much more than the business of sex.”

TechCrunch has reached out to the White House for comment.
