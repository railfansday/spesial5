Airbus may be leaving Microsoft Office, but for its 36,000 defense staff, Office isn't leaving them

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Planemaker's 2-to-3-year plan to ditch Redmond's suite stops short of the 22% who can't go near the cloud

Airbus has a plan to phase out Microsoft Office, but about 22 percent of its roughly 165,000 employees will stay on it because their sensitive data must remain on-premise.

In March 2018, Airbus signed a contract with Google to move its workforce - 130,000 strong at that time - onto the cloud-based Workspace suite. Years later, compatibility issues mean some users still need Excel.

Catherine Jestin, Airbus's executive vice president of digital, told The Register that Google is still working to resolve software interface conflicts between departments and improve features.

Last year, for example, Google tripled the number of cells available in Sheets, which let Airbus "reduce significantly the number of users on Microsoft." This year, Google improved the compatibility of document watermarking. Contracts, for instance, are often exported from Excel to Sheets by one department, edited by another, and sent back.

"Sometimes we had compatibility issues when you were commenting, the comments were lost, or they were not transferable in one way or the other," she said.

"Now they [Google] have made it totally compatible, which means that it makes it much easier for us to decommission people working on contracts… the legal department, the sales department, purchasing department, making it much easier to have that internal collaboration."

Airbus and Google have a roadmap to eliminate Microsoft Office. Jestin expects the migration to finish in "another two to three years."

Not every part of the company will switch. The Defence division, which accounts for the 22 percent (more than 36,000 people), will keep using classic Microsoft Office.

"We will not get 100 percent out of Microsoft because again, for the defense industry people, they cannot [use] Microsoft Office 365 or Google Workspace, they have to stay on-premise, and the only solution that is available on-premise is Microsoft. So Microsoft Office will remain for colleagues who are working in the defense and space programs."
