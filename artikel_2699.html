Chargers reveal Week 10 inactives vs. Ravens

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Los Angeles Chargers are getting ready for their Week 10 matchup against the Baltimore Ravens at M&T Bank Stadium.

While most of the players are getting ready, a few members of the team will be on the inactive list.

WR Ladd McConkey, S Kendall Williamson, CB Isas Waxter, OLB Bud Dupree, WR Brenen Thompson and DL Justin Eboigbe are all out for the Chargers against the Ravens.

McConkey is the most notable inactive for the Chargers, which leaves them with another top player on offense on the sidelines. This means Quentin Johnston, Tre' Harris, KeAndre Lambert-Smith and Derius Davis will be the ones catching passes from Justin Herbert this evening.

Meanwhile, the inactives for the Ravens are DL Aeneas Peebles, G Emery Jones, CB Chandler Rivers, WR Elijah Sarratt, S Jaylinn Hawkins and QB Kedon Slovis (emergency third quarterback).

The Texans will turn to Woody Marks as their top running back with Montgomery on the sidelines, but for the most part, the team is pretty healthy after coming off their annual bye last week.

Kickoff between the Chargers and Ravens is set for 5:15 p.m. PT inside M&T Bank Stadium in Baltimore. Fans can watch the game on ESPN or stream it on the ESPN app.
