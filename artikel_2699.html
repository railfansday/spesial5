Bears work to get a stadium deal in Illinois by December 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Bears continue to work toward getting a new stadium deal in place. And, once again, the clock is ticking.

Via Ian Rapoport of NFL Network, Bears chairman George McCaskey and Bears president and CEO Kevin Warren met with the league’s stadium committee on Wednesday to provide updates on potential sites in Illinois and Indiana.

Indiana is ready to go. The fact that the Bears have yet to go strongly suggests that they prefer Illinois.

And while prior reports indicated that a deal is expected with the Illinois legislature, the current session ends on December 3. If a deal isn’t reached by then, the can gets kicked again.

Which, in turn, raises the question of whether the Bears will finalize an agreement with Indiana.

Nothing is binding until contracts are signed. Even then, it won’t be real until construction begins.

The ultimate goal is to begin construction. At the league meetings in March, Warren told PFT Live that, with each year that the project is delayed, the expected cost increases by another $150 million.
