Jon Scheyer teases more lineup changes for Duke against UConn

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Duke Blue Devils got back into the win column Saturday, narrowly defeating the Washington State Cougars 78-74 behind another monster performance from junior Patrick Ngongba II.

However, considering Duke's early season struggles, head coach Jon Scheyer wasn't afraid to change the starting lineup for the first time. Scheyer moved both Dame Sarr and Cayden Boozer to the bench in favor of Sebastian Wilkins and senior guard Caleb Foster.

Following the game, Scheyer answered questions about the move.

"We're looking for some answers lineup-wise" he said. "I thought Dame had a great response. We had a great burst during the second half with Cayden in the game. It's less about those two guys and more about just the lineup connections.

"It's just still in the early stages of identity of our team and figuring out all that stuff. And so we're going to continue to look at some different things as guys continue to grow, as guys continue to figure out, and as I continue to figure out what each guy can do and how they work together. And so I won't be surprised if you saw a different lineup again on Wednesday."

Starting the season 0-2 against ranked opponents, Wednesday's matchup against No. 3-ranked UConn will provide Scheyer's squad with another early-season test.
