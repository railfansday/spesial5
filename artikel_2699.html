There's more to the Celtics vs. Sixers than Tatum and Brown

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Heading into the Boston Celtics matchup against the Philadelphia 76ers on Tuesday, November 10, all eyes will be on the potential matchup between Jayson Tatum and Jaylen Brown. After all, the two All-Stars spent nine years establishing themselves as one of the best duos in the NBA, winning a championship in 2024, only for Brown to be traded earlier this summer.

Brown wasn't the only player to change teams during that trade, though. Paul George was shipped to Boston to make the salaries work, and is already looking like an ideal fit next to Tatum and within Joe Mazzulla's system. In fact, both George and Brown have enjoyed strong starts to their life with their respective new teams, and on Tuesday, both will likely be motivated to prove the opposing team wrong for parting ways with them.

Once you factor in the deep rivalry between Philadelphia and Boston, and the presence of long-time Celtics enemy LeBron James, you have some can't-miss television that will appeal to die-hards and casual fans alike.

In a recent video uploaded to YouTube by 98.5 The Sports Hub, Chris Gasper joined Mike Felger and Tony Massarotti to discuss the upcoming Celtics and Sixers game, looking at multiple angles that will draw attention, both leading into the game and throughout the night itself. The trio obviously spent some time talking about the relationship between Tatum and Brown, along with the impact George has made to begin the season, before turning their attention to the other matchups we will see throughout the night.
