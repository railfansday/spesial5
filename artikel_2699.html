LSU loses safety Ty Benefield for targeting, ejected vs. Tennessee football

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Tennessee football caught a couple of major breaks on its final drive of the first half vs. LSU on Nov. 20. Two Tigers defensive penalties set up the go-ahead score that put the Vols ahead at halftime.

With 26 seconds left in the second quarter and Tennessee trailing 6-3, starting LSU safety Ty Benefield was flagged for targeting. His penalty proved to be a major lift for a Vols offense that was struggling to get into gear at that point.

Benefield was going for a tackle on Tennessee tight end DaSaahn Brame as he attempted to spin for a first down at the LSU 20-yard line. Benefield lowered his head and put the crown of his helmet right into Brame's midsection. After a review, officials ruled it was indeed targeting and ejected Benefield from the game.

In addition to costing the Tigers one of their best defensive players, the penalty moved Tennessee up to the two yard line. Two plays later, quarterback George MacIntyre connected with Brame for a two-yard touchdown reception, that gave the Vols a 10-6 lead at halftime.

Benefield, a transfer from Boise State, had made 49 tackles and 2 interceptions going into the Tennessee game. He had made a team-leading eight tackles, to go along with a sack, a forced fumble and pass defended, against the Vols before being ejected.
