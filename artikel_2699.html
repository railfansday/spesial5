Jerry Jones makes candid admission after Cowboys allow 584 yards vs. 49ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Dallas Cowboys owner Jerry Jones expressed his disappointment after his team suffered a 46-40 loss to the San Francisco 49ers on Sunday.

The defeat came after Dallas surrendered 584 total yards at AT&T Stadium, exposing major problems on the defensive side. Jones acknowledged that San Francisco delivered the performance his team expected but still found ways to dominate.

Speaking after the NFL game, Jones admitted the Cowboys failed to maintain their first-half performance.

"We had the right assessment of what kind of team they are," Jones said. "Their record speaks for itself. They're an outstanding team. We didn't play as well in the second half as we did in the first half. You can call that inconsistent, whatever you want to call it."

Jones also credited San Francisco quarterback Brock Purdy for his outstanding performance. The 49ers quarterback completed 22 of 28 passes for 389 yards and three touchdowns.

Jones acknowledged that San Francisco repeatedly exposed Dallas' defensive problems.

"But we played a very good team tonight," Jones said. "I actually saw us at the end really show some things. But at the end, the things that we thought they were going to be, they beat us with it."

The 49ers produced three touchdowns of at least 66 yards. Christian McCaffrey caught two long touchdown passes, while Deebo Samuel scored on a 70-yard reverse.

Jones described the result as particularly disappointing after the Cowboys showed some fight late in the contest.

"And the quarterback was outstanding," Jones said. "They made plays, they made a lot of yards, and we're all aware of that and very disappointing."

Dallas quarterback Dak Prescott threw five touchdown passes, matching his career high. George Pickens caught three touchdowns and finished with 145 receiving yards. However, the offensive production could not overcome the defensive collapse. Dallas dropped to 4-6, while San Francisco improved to 8-1.

The Cowboys will now host the Tennessee Titans in Week 11. Jones and head coach Brian Schottenheimer face growing pressure to address the defensive issues.
