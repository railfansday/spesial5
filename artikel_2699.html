Steelers bench Aaron Rodgers for Mason Rudolph

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers turns 43 next week. He’s gotten an unexpected, and unwanted, early birthday present.

The team has announced — after 11:00 p.m. ET on Tuesday night — that Mason Rudolph will start at quarterback on Friday against the Broncos. Rodgers will be inactive for the game.

It’s a stunning move. And it’s not because Rodgers has an injury. He did not appear on Tuesday’s official practice report.

On Monday, coach Mike McCarthy was asked whether one of the three other quarterbacks could be used at some point to mix things up. Once McCarthy understood the import of the question, he said unequivocally, “Aaron Rodgers is our quarterback.”

The next question is whether the team will release Rodgers, and whether he’ll ask to be released.

Why wouldn’t he want his freedom, if he’s not playing? If/when he clears waivers, he’d have the ability to sit and wait for an opportunity to arise with a contender, likely if an injury to the starter happens.

Regardless, it’s an unexpected and frankly seismic move during what is Rodgers’s final season. The fact that the Steelers held the information until late Tuesday night shows that they appreciate the significance of the move.
