Did Victor Wembanyama shade Luka Doncic with 'stat padding' jab?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Victor Wembanyama has never been shy to share his thoughts on the state of the NBA and his peers in the league. That continued Saturday after the San Antonio Spurs star recorded his second 50-point game of the season, adding 19 rebounds, five blocks and four steals.

As special as Wemby acknowledged his latest masterpiece to be, he said after the game he could score 50 points more often if he was more like other NBA players. And it doesn't sound like he meant it to be a compliment.

"It is very special, but unlike many of my peers, I'm not very good at stat-padding. So I haven't reached it as many times as I should have," Wembanyama said.

This is a popular sentiment on social media. Most likely because Doncic is leading the NBA in scoring by a wide margin at 37.2 points per game, with two 50 balls of his own on this season. But Wembanyama didn't single out any one player.

He did, however, name several superstars -- including Doncic -- in his postgame presser to make a point that players of that caliber don't concern themselves with the ethics of scoring.

"The greats, they don't think about that. I don't think LeBron, Shai, Jokic, Doncic, I don't think they think about that. And I have them as examples, so I'm trying not to think about it either," Wembanyama said.

It's hard to know his exact intent, but it sounds like he was taking a jab at them all -- not just Doncic. Wembanyama seems to be acknowledging his own stat-padding to reach 50 and how he's learning to be OK with it because other stars do it too.
