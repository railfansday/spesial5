Here’s where the Bears sit in the NFC playoff standings right now

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Chicago Bears beat the New Orleans Saints 21 to 13, but the Detroit Lions beat the Tampa Bay Buccaneers 31 to 20, so Detroit will maintain first place in the NFC North. However, the Bears are up to the fifth seed in the conference and second in the division.

The Minnesota Vikings lost to the San Francisco 49ers on Sunday Night Football, so they stay in third in the North and sixth in the Conference.

The Thanksgiving game between the Bears and Lions will see the winner move into sole possession of the NFC North and the No. 2 seed in the NFC.
