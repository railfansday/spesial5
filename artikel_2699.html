2026 Dodgers season review: Michael Siani

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Michael Siana ended the 2025 season with the St. Louis Cardinals and is now a free agent. In between, the defense-first center fielder was a part of five other organizations. That included the Dodgers, for whom he spent a total of 140 days on the 40-man roster in two stints with the organization.

On November 6, 2025, the Atlanta Braves claimed Siani off waivers from Cardinals, but on December 10 when outfielder Mike Yastrzemski signed with Atlanta, Siani was designated for assignment.

Two days later, the Dodgers pounced and claimed Siani, giving them some outfield depth. But when the Dodgers finalized their four-year, $240 million contract with Kyle Tucker on January 21, Siani was once again a roster casualty, designated for assignment.

The New York Yankees were next, claiming Siani off waivers, but he lasted on New York's 40-man roster for only five days. Siani was designated for assignment on January 28 when the Yankees traded for pitcher Angel Chivilli. On February 3, the Dodgers claimed Siani back off waivers, and designated Andy Ibañez for assignment only three weeks after the infielder was signed as a free agent.

Siani lasted with the Dodgers through spring training and played in 16 Cactus League games, starting eight games in center field. He hit .225/.355/.303 with a 82 wRC+ in 29 games for Triple-A Oklahoma City, which was to be expected from a player with a career .344 slugging percentage in eight minor league seasons.

Siani's time with the Dodgers came to an end on May 12, designated for assignment once again when the Dodgers traded for Alek Thomas, another defense-first center fielder but one with more power and years of major league experience.

The Baltimore Orioles claimed Siani off waivers on May 18, and he played another 29 games for Triple-A Norfolk. Siani also got into one major league game for the Orioles, and it came at Dodger Stadium, on June 21. He played center field for the final three innings of a blowout of the Dodgers, and grounded out in the ninth inning against pitcher Miguel Rojas.

Siani was designated for assignment by Baltimore on June 22, but this time he made it through waivers was was sent outright to the minors. After another six weeks in Triple-A, Siani was released by the Orioles on August 3. He signed a minor league deal with the Detroit Tigers on August 14, and had eight hits in 57 at-bats with two home runs for Triple-A Norfolk.

In 78 total games in Triple-A for three organizations this year plus two more in High-A, Siani hit .160/.262/.251 with a 40 wRC+ in his 283 plate appearances.

Stats: 0 for 1 in majors with Orioles (in LA); .225/.355/.303 in 29 games for Triple-A OKC

Siani on April 14 for Oklahoma City had two singles and two walks, reaching base a season-high four times. He stole a season-best two bases, scored a run, and drove in another in a road win at the Albuquerque Isotopes.

Siani on November 2 became one of hundreds of minor league free agents.
