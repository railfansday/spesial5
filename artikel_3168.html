Cineverse Corp. (CNVS +23.76%) reported a transition to a technology-centric business model as Advertising Technology and Media Services now generate more than 50% of total revenue. Management reported that the integration of recent acquisitions has driven significant top-line growth and a third consecutive quarter of positive adjusted EBITDA, even during a seasonally slow period. The company stated it is executing a $14 million annualized cost-saving program, largely through the migration of manual workflows to its Matchpoint platform and shifting operational roles to lower-cost regions. Management reaffirmed its full-year guidance for fiscal 2027, citing strong momentum in streaming engagement and a high-profile theatrical slate highlighted by the acquisition of rights to the Jason Universe franchise.
