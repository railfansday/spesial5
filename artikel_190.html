Congressman Jim Jordan, a Republican hardliner from Ohio, has won the election to become the next House minority leader in Congress. Jordan beat Representative Steve Scalise, the current majority leader, by 106-73 in a closed-door vote to lead Republicans from January. He replaces the current House speaker, Mike Jonson, who said he would not run when the GOP cedes control to Democrats in the new year.

Jordan, 62, who currently serves as chair of the House judiciary committee, is known as a pugnacious representative and fierce ally of Donald Trump. Jordan was also the first chair of the far-right Freedom Caucus in the lower chamber. He repeatedly pushed the conspiracy that the 2020 election, which Trump lost to Joe Biden, was stolen. Jordan was also subpoenaed in 2021 by the House committee investigating the January 6 attack on the US Capitol, which identified Jordan as a “significant player” in the president’s attempts to overturn the election.
