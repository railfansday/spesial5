The wait is over. Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally out, and gamers are now getting to grips with its stunning graphics and sprawling map. But fitness fans are also paying special attention to a new “muscle system” that allows the main characters, Jason and Lucia, to change their physical appearance and build both strength and stamina in the gym. Here’s how it works.

In the GTA6, workouts are divided into cardio and strength sessions so that you can power up your player. In the strength mode, you choose what weight is added to each exercise, such as the trusty barbell back squat, but the controls require gentle management in order to hit proper form. From the beginning of GTA6, you’ll have a home gym where you can undertake the following strength workout, as shared in a video explainer by popular content creator, “BullSheepParty.”

You will also want to consume a virtual protein shake after your workout, to maximize those gains.

Just like in the real world, increasing the weight will add more muscle, but take care with your form to avoid dropping the bar on your chest. BullSheepParty shares that the kettlebell swing is the easiest movement to master, while the squat takes longer to perfect.

Once you are done pumping iron, drinking the protein shake is essential or you won’t maximize your muscle. In GTA6, you’ll simply head to the fridge and grab that shake — no prep necessary.

The strength and stamina levels that you earn by working out can be tracked from GTA6’s own in-game fitness app, and as you continue through the game, players unlock new gyms on the map so that you can maintain your gains. Be sure to take regular workouts, or you’ll wind up too weak to wage further carnage.
