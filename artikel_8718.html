My friend Sean Mullaney, the FI Tax Guy, recently published an interesting article about the potential downsides of annuities. One of his observations is so simple that I think it's easy to overlook:

Most Americans already have an annuity. It's called Social Security.

Think about what an annuity is trying to accomplish. You give up some control over a pool of money in exchange for a predictable stream of income, often for the rest of your life. Social Security already provides lifetime income, with the added benefit of inflation adjustments.

For a married couple receiving substantial Social Security benefits, that could represent tens of thousands of dollars of annual income that they don't have to generate by selling investments.
