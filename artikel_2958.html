Bill Simmons has insatiable thirst for watching Joe Buck and Troy Aikman broadcast through hatred

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Joe Buck and Troy Aikman might not love each other, but they should feel the love from Bill Simmons, who can't get enough of their "mutual hatred" on Monday Night Football.

By now, everyone knows Buck and Aikman had a blowup before a Monday Night Football broadcast last month stemming from their differing contract situations with ESPN. Aikman remains headed for free agency with ESPN appearing to have no serious interest in retaining him, while Buck quietly agreed to a long-term extension with the company last May. And in the wake of the reported blowup, all eyes and ears have attempted to decipher whether the feud is spilling onto the broadcast.

During Friday's mailbag portion of his podcast, Simmons read a note from a listener who claimed watching Buck and Aikman on Monday Night Football is getting too uncomfortable amid their reported feud.

"I feel completely the opposite. I love it," Simmons insisted. "I love it, I want them to stay together and just be in mutual hatred."

Simmons proceeded to mimic the Monday Night Football opens since their reported blowup, where Aikman now appears to be staring into the abyss as Buck welcomes the audience with his monologue.

"He looks like somebody is in his earpiece giving him the worst news possible about the stock market that day, or that his crypto account got wiped out," Simmons continued. "I love it, I don't want them to breakup. Keep them together."

ESPN probably isn't as enamored with the feud as Simmons is. But the question is whether ESPN views this as an "any publicity is good publicity" situation, or if the network is getting concerned this rift could eventually grow to the point where Buck and Aikman are unable to work together?

Many sports fans have shared Simmons' curiosity over how the Buck and Aikman situation will play out. And considering nobody has been arrested or committed any offense worthy of being fired, it's fair to wonder whether ESPN is welcoming the added attention to an extent.

Simmons is likely to get his wish of watching this continue. Unless Aikman starts publicly badmouthing Buck and the company beyond what's been reported, ESPN isn't going to make an in-season change, especially as they prepare for their first Super Bowl broadcast in three months.
