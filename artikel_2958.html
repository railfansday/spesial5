Nets shake up everything but the outcome in heartbreaking fourth straight loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Team meetings. Lineup changes. The Nets tried everything to shake out of their funk.

They finally showed some heart. But then they got it broken in a 113-111 loss to the Magic at Barclays Center.

They saw Franz Wagner torch them for a game-high 35 points, including a go-ahead midrange basket with 2.8 seconds left.

And Julius Randle — whose turnover in the final minute had let Orlando knot the game at 111-all — missed an open 3-pointer at the buzzer to seal the loss.

Randle had 27 points, nine rebounds and six assists. Brooklyn fought; it just couldn't quite close the deal.

After the Nets (3-12) had embarrassed themselves in Wednesday's 137-107 non-effort against Chicago, they held a team meeting, with some real talk about what had gone wrong that night and how to fix it.

Brooklyn Nets forward Julius Randle shoots as Orlando Magic forward Franz Wagner defends on Friday, Nov. 20, 2026, at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, N.Y. Robert Sabo for NY Post

Friday's effort showed it wasn't all just talk, but it just wasn't quite enough.

"It's hard to come up here and keep having these moral victories. There's been a lot of those this year," said Danny Wolf, who replaced Egor Dëmin in the starting lineup. "We responded very well after that Chicago game. But with how hard guys played, we really wanted that win and we needed that win … We've got to keep doing what worked and playing with that tenacity and that effort. But definitely a tough loss.

Brooklyn Nets forward Danny Wolf shoots as Orlando Magic center Wendell Carter Jr. defends on Friday, Nov. 20, 2026, at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, N.Y. Robert Sabo for NY Post

"After that Chicago game, you guys saw it, we saw it, everyone saw it … it was flat-out embarrassing and everyone knows it. We all knew it. And we knew that change was needed. A conversation like that, it's open, it's an honest conversation and everyone speaks up. There's still a lot of stuff we need to work on, but you saw a different team on the defensive end and with how hard we were playing."

After their embarrassment two nights earlier, the Nets had a meeting that sources said included not only players but coaches as well.

Nets coach Jordi Fernández shuffled his rotations, with Dëmin coming off the bench for the first time since Nov. 5 of his rookie season.

Brooklyn Nets forward Julius Randle (30) goes up for a shot against the Orlando Magic at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, N.Y., Friday, Nov. 20, 2026. Robert Sabo for NY Post

Instead, he went with the 6-foot-11 Wolf as a jumbo off-guard. The move paid off, as Wolf finished with 20 points, five boards and a team-high plus-15.

"We were struggling rebounding and with our defense, and I felt Danny was playing well," Fernández said. "And also I wanted to see that couple of Mikel (Brown Jr.) and Egor play together. That was great minutes where they can build chemistry."

Brown finished with 18 points, including a dozen in the third quarter when starting point guard Nolan Traore left with a hamstring injury.

Orlando Magic forward Franz Wagner (22) dunks against the Brooklyn Nets on Friday, Nov. 20, 2026, at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, N.Y. Robert Sabo for NY Post

Dëmin stumbled out of the gate and shot 1-of-6.

"It was a little bit of a rough start for me: I didn't catch the ball and got my nose bleeding … But I want to go out there — I have to go there — and be ready," Dëmin said. "I'm willing to do whatever it takes for this team to be successful. So I'm good with getting off the bench and going with the second unit. A little more time playing with Mikel, too, which is a great opportunity for us to get more experience."

Michael Porter Jr.'s layup gave the Nets a 63-62 edge coming out of the half.

Brooklyn Nets center Moritz Wagner pulls down a rebound against Orlando Magic center Nikola Vucevic on Friday, Nov. 20, 2026, at Barclays Center in Brooklyn. Robert Sabo for NY Post

The Nets coughed up a 10-0 Magic run, capped by Franz Wagner's bucket.

They trailed 72-63, and things got worse when Traore limped off with eight minutes left in the third with a right hamstring injury.

Brooklyn trailed 89-85 after a Desmond Bane 3-pointer with 1:24 left in the third, but Wolf's 3 capped a 13-4 run that gave the Nets a 98-93 lead with 8:36 to play.

Brooklyn Nets center Moritz Wagner defends against Orlando Magic forward Franz Wagner during a game Friday, Nov. 20, 2026, at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, N.Y. Robert Sabo for NY Post

They still led 111-109 after Randle's free throws. But he spun in the lane and kicked to the corner, expecting Brown to be there. The rookie had vacated and the ball sailed out for a turnover with 49 seconds left.

The Nets gave up a tying Wendell Carter Jr. layup. And after Brown missed a step back 20-footer with nine seconds remaining, Wagner didn't miss on the other end.
