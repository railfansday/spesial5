Why Micron Stock Is Up Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Micron stock jumped after Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang, which eases fears that an AI slowdown would hit memory demand.

By Johnny Rice – Nov 23, 2026 at 10:27AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Micron Technology ›

Micron Technology (MU +19.19%) stock is up 20% as of 9:40 a.m. ET on Monday, Nov. 23, after Nvidia (NVDA +32.52%) announced it is reinstating its CEO.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC +4.85%) and the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +6.76%) are up 5.2% and up 7%, respectively.

Nvidia confirmed on Monday that its board has reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO. The announcement shocked the market, given that just last week the board was insisting his return was not an option. Nvidia's stock soared on the news, up 26.2% so far this morning.

Nvidia is a key partner for Micron, whose advanced high bandwidth memory (HBM) is a key component in the company's AI chips. The return of Jensen Huang helps calm fears that his absence would lead to a slowdown in the AI market and thus, a slowdown in Micron's business.

Last Wednesday, CEO Sanjay Mehrotra said the company has already sold 75% of its 2027 supply and that customer talks have moved into 2028. And on Sunday, analysts at Citi (C +3.47%) estimated that even if Nvidia-related financing deals dried up completely, global demand for memory would only fall slightly.

That's reassuring, but I still have concerns about memory prices in the long term and what that will do to Micron's earnings.
