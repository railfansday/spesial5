Remember Bebe? He’s just produced one of the funniest footballing moments you’ll see all year

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

One of the great joys of doing a Panini sticker album is coming across names and faces you’ve not thought about in years, sometimes literally decades.

The 2026 World Cup album was full to the brim of such players.

Marko Arnautovic! Nabil Bentaleb! Fernando Muslera! Enner Valencia! All the stars are here.

The absolute peak was seeing the face of Bebe, a full 15 years after his inauspicious handful of appearances for Manchester United.

Unfortunately for the Cape Verdean winger, he didn’t actually make their eventual 26-man squad for the tournament itself. He was part of a sad cohort in the sticker album but not at the actual World Cup. The curse of printing deadlines.

Cape Verde were one of the stories of the World Cup, an absolute joy to watch. The only way their adventure could possibly have been better was if they’d found a place for Bebe – one of the great curiosities of 2010s football.

Bebe has always been an easy player to root for. His Manchester United career was a disaster, there’s no denying that, but the idea that he was some Ali Dia-esque chancer who somehow blagged his way into the club is an unfair distortion of the truth.

The reality is that Bebe is a perfectly respectable professional footballer. He’s earned 26 caps for Cape Verde, scoring six goals, enjoyed a lengthy career across several countries and worked hard to become something of a cult hero at Rayo Vallecano.

Admittedly we’ve kind of lost track of his career since he left Rayo a few years back, with a couple of stints back in the second tier, but we were thrilled to see the latest reminder of his existence.

His latest effort is a thing of beauty, involving a wildly uncontrolled swing of the leg and a ball sailing straight into the club bar. The light being taken out was a perfect piece of comic timing straight out of The Simpsons.

Bebé takes out the lights of Teruel’s stadium bar with his shot. by u/Sparky-moon in soccer

We’ll always be in Bebe’s corner, but we’re not going to pretend moments like this don’t make us love him even more. He’s a wonderfully ridiculous footballer.

After a spell without a club, he joined third-tier CD Teruel earlier this month on a short-term deal that runs to the end of the season. At that point, he’ll be 37, and we fear that this might be the last viral clip of his career.

God bless you, Bebe. We hope you remain this chaotic until the very end.
