UEFA backs France's Diallo to stand against Infantino in FIFA election

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

European football's governing body UEFA has given its "unanimous" backing to French Football Federation chief Philippe Diallo to stand against incumbent Gianni Infantino in March's FIFA presidential election, it said in a statement Monday. Under its Slovenian president Aleksander Ceferin, UEFA has led the opposition to Infantino since his announcement in late July of a project to sell stakes in the World Cup to private investors. FRANCE 24's Selina Sykes speaks with Dr. Emanuel Macedo de Medeiros, Co-founder and Global CEO of the Sport Integrity Global Alliance.
