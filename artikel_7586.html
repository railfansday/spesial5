This term is not just short on blockbuster cases. It’s short on cases, period.

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Type to search articles, cases, and authors.

As the 2026-27 term got underway this fall, several commentators observed that it was shaping up to be a quiet term compared to the one before it. There are important disputes tied to climate change, immigration, and the Second Amendment. But no case is quite comparable to last term’s battles over tariffs, birthright citizenship, executive authority, and the Voting Rights Act.

Just over a month later, the oral argument docket still remains relatively short on blockbuster cases. Perhaps of even greater note, though, is that it’s short on cases in general, as the court is running behind its typical pace for grants.

Kelsey Dallas is SCOTUSblog's managing editor and the primary author of the SCOTUStoday newsletter.
