Elder Scrolls 6 merch is being sold years before game is close to release

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Bethesda is already selling official merchandise for The Elder Scrolls 6, despite the long-awaited RPG still being years away from release.

It’s been 15 years since Skyrim launched in 2011, and fans have been waiting for its sequel ever since Bethesda first announced the game back in 2018.

On November 3, the studio finally revealed that the next installment will be called The Elder Scrolls VI: Sentinel, although there was no gameplay footage or release date to accompany the announcement.

But while fans might have a lengthy wait before they can actually play the new game, they can already buy a T-shirt or hoodie to show their support.

Bethesda’s official merchandise store has started selling two items celebrating Sentinel, despite the game still being deep in development. The first is a black T-shirt priced at $34.99, while the second is a matching hoodie for $64.99.

Both feature branding for the upcoming RPG and share a similar description that reads: “Your next journey through Tamriel will be in The Elder Scrolls VI: Sentinel. Show off your excitement with the official tee!”

However, there’s still no indication of when players will actually get their hands on the game itself. Bloomberg journalist Jason Schreier suggested that Sentinel is still more than two years away from launching.

That would put a potential release in late 2028 at the earliest, although Bethesda hasn’t announced a launch window. The developer also made it clear that fans shouldn’t expect another major reveal anytime soon.

“Development continues, and while it’ll be some time before we’re ready to share more, it feels great to finally reveal the name and location with you,” Bethesda said in its November 3 announcement.
