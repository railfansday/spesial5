UnitedHealth, Humana, and CVS stocks are falling on proposed Medicare Advantage ratings rule

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

CMS proposed raising the bar for Medicare Advantage Star Ratings and Quality Bonus Payments; Molina Healthcare stock fell 7.8%

UnitedHealth $UNH Group stock fell 6.4% in premarket trading on Monday after the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) proposed a rule that would make high Medicare Advantage quality ratings harder to earn.

Humana $HUM stock dropped 4.1% and CVS Health $CVS stock fell 4.3% in premarket trading. Molina Healthcare stock slid 7.8%.

CMS issued the proposed rule on Friday. It would narrow the Medicare Advantage and Part D Star Ratings to 15 measures that emphasize clinical outcomes and patient experience, while keeping select process measures in areas where performance remains low. CMS also proposed changes to the ratings calculation that raise the bar for earning high ratings and the Quality Bonus Payments tied to them.

The agency proposed eliminating the reward factor. It would also limit the hold harmless methodology for improvement measures to contracts receiving 4.5 or higher stars for their highest rating, according to the CMS fact sheet. The measure removals would apply to the 2028 measurement period and the 2030 Star Ratings.

A separate piece of the proposal, a mandatory 10-year model called ADVaNCE MA, would replace Star Ratings and Quality Bonus Payments in select states with a localized, cohort-based system called the Diamond Ratings System. If finalized, the model would begin in 2029, with a pre-implementation period starting June 8, 2027.

"These proposals are about creating a Medicare Advantage marketplace that rewards better care and better value while empowering beneficiaries with the options and knowledge they need to make informed decisions about their health," CMS Administrator Dr. Mehmet Oz said in a statement.

The proposed rule will be open for public comment for 60 days after it is published in the Federal Register.

The proposal follows a period of pressure on insurers in the program. UnitedHealth and Humana announced in October they are discontinuing Medicare Advantage plans ahead of the 2027 enrollment year. UnitedHealth said about 390,000 members are in plans being discontinued, and Humana announced cuts affecting around 600,000 enrollees.

CMS finalized a 2.48% average payment rate increase for Medicare Advantage plans in 2027, worth more than $13 billion in additional payments. Insurers have said government reimbursement has not kept pace with rising medical costs.
