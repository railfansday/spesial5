Zero In Hagerstown, MD

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Today’s post is a brief memo covering my emotions heading into my final 4 day hiking stint. Mostly I am praying I don’t forget anything!

It was difficult to write up the posts from the last two days. The problem was two-fold. Nerves for the upcoming 4 days on trail and ensuring I had thought of everything so I could attempt the 4 state challenge on Sunday. It would be easy to forget a single thing, like my power bank which I rarely take with me. I was also trying to give myself a mental break, but writing the blog posts takes mental energy, so I was battling a catch-22.

The latest weather outlook looks almost too promising to be true. It looks like it will be reaching 60 degrees each of the upcoming 4 days. Overnight lows are hovering around 40 degrees. That would be a miracle given the time of year. Sunday does look cloudy, so catching the sunset at Pen Mar park might fall a bit flat, but if I am successful with my 4 state challenge attempt nothing will be able to dull the ceremony.

Perhaps the only item of note is that I have been unable to restock my whole milk powder, all the stores in the area have run out and there seems to be a supply issue. I am making a plan to replace my usual breakfast and dinner with something else, probably granola bars and beef sticks. Not ideal, but hopefully a minor change that I can negotiate without impacting my 4 state challenge attempt.

I’ll wrap up the post here, its only been two days since my last zero. There isn’t anything to report that you haven’t read at least a few times already.

Hopefully the butterflies in my stomach are more due to the 3 cups of coffee rather than actual nerves that will seep over into my hiking time tomorrow.
