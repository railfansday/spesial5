Jesse Derry back at Chelsea tonight in Sporting loan twist

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Champions League football at Stamford Bridge awaits Jesse Derry this evening.

The Chelsea youngster is on loan at Sporting in Portugal this season, but is back to the Blues’ home ground in west London to face off against Shakhtar Donetsk, who are calling the Bridge their home in Europe's premier competition this season due to the ongoing conflict in the Donbas region.

Derry will be hoping to be given the chance to make an impact, with some Chelsea fans no doubt watching on among those who head to Stamford Bridge for an evening of Champions League entertainment.

It will be the first time Derry has appeared at Chelsea’s home ground since his Premier League debut in May, when he was taken to hospital midway through the game after a sickening clash of heads with Nottingham Forest’s Zach Abbott from which he has now recovered.

Derry has had to be patient so far for opportunities to show Sporting why they opted to take him on loan for the season but may have turned a corner last week.

After all of his first six appearances for the club came in the form of cameos as a substitute, he was finally handed his first start for the Lisbon giants when they faced Maritimo in the Portuguese League Cup. Derry chipped in with his first goal involvement for the club, assisting the opening goal as Sporting prevailed 2-1 on the night.

The chance is there for Derry to make his first Champions League start this evening if interim head coach Tiago Fernandes calls on him.

The 19-year-old will be hoping his 73 minutes in the tank last week can pave the way for further minutes this evening, especially after struggling to play as much as he’d have liked so far. He has played only 32 minutes (not including stoppage time) in the Portuguese League, spread across four substitute appearances.
