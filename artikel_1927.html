Three Bridges show signs of life with much-needed win against Cheshunt

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Following a more encouraging performance against Dartford on Tuesday evening, Bridges knew that nothing less than three points would do if they were to stand any chance of remaining a Step 3 side next season. And it was the home side who started on the front foot, dominating much of the first half and creating a number of opportunities. Noel Leighton, Reece Hallard, Hayden Velvick and Charlie Bennett all went close inside the opening 25 minutes, but none could find a way past Cheshunt goalkeeper Rex Porter.

Against the run of play, however, the visitors took the lead. Hayden Neathey conceded a needless free-kick just outside the penalty area, and Joe Re stepped up to curl the ball past Sam Roberts. That familiar sinking feeling descended over Jubilee Field, and despite having enjoyed the better of the half, Bridges went into the interval a goal behind.

But this time, there was no heads dropping. Bridges continued where they had left off, and their positive approach was eventually rewarded.

After seeing an attempted lob saved by Porter, Leighton and his teammates finally found a way through, with the lively Hayden Velvick restoring parity. Picking up the ball on the right-hand side of the penalty area, the winger unleashed an effort which somehow found its way past Porter at his near post. However it went in, nobody in amber and black was complaining, and Bridges were back on level terms.

The hosts continued to press for a winner, and debutant Jadyn Dundas should have put them ahead just minutes after coming off the bench. Arriving at the back post with the goal at his mercy, he was unable to get his feet sorted out in time to convert. What then followed was an end-to-end contest, with Sam Granville striking the crossbar for Cheshunt before Leighton and Velvick both saw efforts bravely blocked by visiting defenders.

This time, though, Bridges were not to be denied. Cheshunt failed to deal with the danger inside their own penalty area, and after Porter could only parry an initial deflected effort, the ball fell kindly into the path of Noel Leighton. The striker made no mistake from close range, sending Jubilee Field into celebration and, more importantly, putting Bridges 2-1 ahead.

The home side looked relatively comfortable heading towards the final whistle, but injury time brought one final scare. An almighty scramble inside the Bridges six-yard box threatened to produce a late Cheshunt equaliser, with the visitors seemingly doing everything they could to force the ball over the line. Somehow, the Bridges defence stood firm and managed to clear their lines before the referee finally blew for full-time.

There was an enormous sigh of relief around Jubilee Field as Three Bridges secured just their third league victory of the season. A hugely important result for Jamie Crellin's side, and one which could prove to be a turning point in their season.

If the great escape is to become a reality, Jubilee Field must now become a fortress, starting next Saturday afternoon when Wingate & Finchley visit for another crucial encounter.

Bridges team: Roberts, Ferreira, J Hallard (Irving 75), Adeyinka, Neathey, Leighton (C), R Hallard (Dundas 65), Malone, Saunders, Velvick, Bennett (Idowu-Johnson 87)
