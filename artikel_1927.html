Parker needs to gamble again as injuries pile up for Portsmouth | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Tomorrow’s trip to Fratton Park is the first time West Ham have faced their south coast opponents in the league away from home since 2012. The Hammers have the advantage of the bragging rights from a solid 3-1 Carabao Cup victory back in August but the Pompey side who they come up against tomorrow night is likely to look very different from that which succumbed at London Stadium.

According to Sportsmole, Portsmouth manager John Mousino has significant challenges all over the pitch:

“Portsmouth’s lengthy absentee list features Marko Milovanovic, Colby Bishop, Luke Brooke-Smith, Keshi Anderson, John Swift, Mark Kosznovszky, Benjamin Arthur and Connor Ogilvie.

Jayden Meghoma is a doubt after being forced off in the closing stages against Bolton with an ankle problem, while a calf issue prevented Marlon Pack from starting Saturday’s draw.“

Scott Parker will face the dilemma over whether to continue with Saturday’s centre-back pairing of Morato and Mavropanos, Maximilian Kilman now available once more having served his suspension.

Fans will be divided over the return of Max Kilman for Portsmouth: Will Parker gamble on Morato?

Similarly, retaining Finn Herrick in goal will be something of a gamble but one that most fans will hope Parker takes as the young ‘keeper made saves on Saturday that Hermansen has failed to do in the four games preceding Saturday’s draw.

There are no good options for West Ham’s manager with Castellanos so out of sorts and Pablo unlikely to make the squad.

Josh Ajala looked so good the last time these two sides met that there’d be a temptation to at least name the home grown talent  for the bench, especially considering the little-known Championship requirement for a minimum of seven English players to make up West Ham’s match day squad.
