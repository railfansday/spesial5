Ancelotti swipes back at Romario: ‘He talks a lot’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Carlo Ancelotti took a swipe back at Romario after the latter called for the Brazil coach to step down.

Brazil suffered a 3-2 defeat to Japan on Friday. Vinicius Junior scored twice, but Raphinha was sent off, with Ancelotti also seeing red at full-time.

In the wake of that defeat, Romario – who earned 70 caps for Brazil – called for Ancelotti to quit, posting on social media: "Ancelotti, it's done! Take your things and go."

When asked about Romario's comments ahead of Brazil's friendly against Singapore, Ancelotti said: "In a democratic country with freedom of speech, everyone is allowed to express their opinion.

"He can tell lies, he can tell the truth or talk nonsense.

"It's not up to me to say whether the things he says are correct, wrong or just stupid. The truth is that Romario talks a lot. A lot. It's his right."

Despite the criticism he has faced, Ancelotti feels Brazil are on the right track.

"It's a new project, I really like what we're doing," Ancelotti said.

"I have to do more, and I will. I like being here, I like being part of the national team, and I have to improve.

"I have to work harder to make the national team competitive again. We haven't been competitive for too long. I like being here, and I want to give my all to make the national team the most competitive in the world."
