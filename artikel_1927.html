Newhaven halt Robins’ flight towards play-off place

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Dockers flew out of the traps and would have been ahead with barely a minute on the clock were it not for a great save from the Hassocks keeper to keep out a Matt Gunn header.

Within a minute, Gunn had gone close with another header, before a brilliant Harry Reed cross narrowly evaded the onrushing Tyler Capon.

Thomas Sekyere then went close before Hassocks mustered their first attempt on target; young keeper Jake Rhodes, making his senior debut in Pike’s absence, pulling off a great save from close-range to keep the score goalless.

Newhaven soon resumed control of proceedings, with Matt Feeney-Hill drawing a save from the keeper, before Lee Robinson headed a pin-point Sekyere cross wide of the post.

Despite the game being open and flowing, it took until just after the hour mark for the first goal to arrive.

With 63 minutes on the clock, a Sekyere ball over the top set Robinson away, and he produced a sublime rasping finish from a tight angle to fire the hosts into a deserved lead.

Seven minutes later, it was two as Robinson notched his second. Hassocks failed to fully clear a set-piece, Ross Barclay hooked the ball back into the danger zone, Gunn won a header, and Robinson produced a fine finish on the turn to double the Docker’s advantage.

There was to be no coast to the finish line for Newhaven, though, with the Robins roaring back into the game in the last 20 minutes.

Harvey Blake was inches away from pulling a goal back, with his well-struck effort cannoning off the upright, before Lewis Finney found space in a crowded penalty area to curl the ball beyond the grasp of Rhodes with 13 minutes of normal time left.

Aside from a Robinson effort that was smothered away by the Hassocks custodian, the final stages were largely played out in Newhaven’s defensive half.

With two minutes left of normal time remaining, the visitors seemed almost certain to complete their comeback when a loose ball fell kindly to Finney just inside the six-yard box, only for him to be denied by another superb close-range save by Rhodes.

However, the Dockers managed to cling onto what were a hard-fought but fully-deserved and mightily impressive three-points.

They now look to build on that victory this weekend when Lancing, themselves boosted by a 5-0 win at the weekend, visit Fort Road.
