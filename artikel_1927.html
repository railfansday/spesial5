West Ham Exclusive | Star Midfielder Receives January Premier League Offer

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Claret & Hugh understands that West Ham midfielder Tomáš Souček has received an offer to join a Premier League club in January, with two other top-flight clubs also interested in signing the Czech international.

The exclusive information has come to us directly via one of Souček’s representatives, who told us in his own words:

“Tomas has an offer from a very good Premier League team to join in January, and two other Premier League teams are also interested.”

Souček, who emerged from the defeat against Portsmouth bloodied and bruised but was probably West Ham’s best player despite coming off the bench, has struggled for regular game time since returning from injury.

It’s something that hasn’t gone unnoticed by Premier League clubs, who clearly see his goalscoring record and top-flight experience as attributes they’d like to add to their squads.

Soucek’s lack gametime for West Ham has attracted interest from Premier League clubs

It has been reiterated to Claret & Hugh by Souček’s representatives that he is not pushing for an exit from the London Stadium. That remains consistent with what we have previously been told by the midfielder’s camp.

However, it goes without saying that he would probably like to be involved in more games than he currently is.

Ultimately, it will be for manager Scott Parker and head of recruitment Nils Koppen to decide whether they wish to retain or cash in on the 31-year-old midfielder, who only signed a contract extension in October.
