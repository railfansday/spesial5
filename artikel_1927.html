Liverpool are prepared to explore a deal for Jamal Musiala next summer if he can prove his fitness between now and then, sources have told Football Insider.

The Reds are long-term admirers of the Bayern Munich ace, who is currently battling against a neurological issue.

Recent reports in Germany have suggested that the attacking midfielder has a release clause in his contract that can be triggered next summer, and Liverpool are on alert.

Sources say that it would be a "very expensive" deal to sign the 23-year-old, but they first need him to prove his fitness.

Bayern believe that if Musiala's health issues continue long-term, then it will put top clubs off from making a move for him.

He has managed just one start in the Bundesliga so far this term, despite featuring heavily for Germany at the World Cup in the summer.

Musiala has developed a neurological condition that has caused him to have seizures during games at times, and Bayern are working to help him get to the bottom of it.

However, the Bundesliga giants are sceptical as to whether the starlet will ever be able to get back to his best at the moment.

Football Insider revealed on Tuesday that Bayern are "worried" about Musiala's future, and his ability to feature regularly right now.

Musiala is occupying a "huge contract" at the club, who are concerned about paying such lofty wages for a player who isn't playing.

It is proving to be a controversial issue within German football at the moment, and one that Liverpool are paying close attention to.

The Reds are prepared to make a move to sign Musiala next summer, but they need his situation to improve before they can commit to a deal.

Football Insider revealed on Wednesday that Liverpool are well-placed to be able to "tempt" Monza striker Gustavo Varela into joining them next summer.

The 21-year-old has been a surprise package in Serie A so far this term, and has netted 11 goals from his opening 11 league games in 2026-27.

Sources say that Monza are likely to entertain a sale of their star man next summer, with Liverpool already paying close attention to his progress.

However, the Italian side won't make it a cheap deal, and they are prepared to hike up his valuation if he continues to perform.
