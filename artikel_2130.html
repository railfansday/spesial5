Ex-Nottingham Forest, Leeds & Sunderland star lands manager's job at non-league club

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Liam Bridcutt has become the latest one-time Championship player to land a job on the managerial circuit, with his appointment at Ashington AFC confirmed on Wednesday evening.

The 37-year-old was a regular fixture in English football's second tier throughout the 2010s, having initially helped Brighton and Hove Albion return to the division after a six-year absence under Gus Poyet for the 2011/12 campaign.

Bridcutt also made several appearances in League One for various clubs, as well as achieving gametime in the Premier League during a mixed spell with Sunderland.

There have been many surprise moves into the non-league game from former Championship stars in recent months.

Kyle Hudlin and Britt Assombalonga are now a strike partnership at National League South outfit, Harborough Town, all of Chey Dunkley, Ryan Woods, Marvin Johnson and Elias Kachunga linking up at Solihull Moors of the National League, whilst former Burnley and Derby County star, Jeff Hendrick has confirmed his role as ambassador at Ebbsfleet United.

Bridcutt ended his playing career two years ago, and since then has obtained coaching roles, but will now lead a side in the dugout for the very first time.

After confirming his retirement from playing in February 2024, the two-time Scotland international landed his first coaching role seven months' later by becoming a sessional coach within Newcastle United's academy setup despite his prior connections with the Magpies' bitter North East rivals.

Soon afterwards, though, Bridcutt joined another local side in the form of Gateshead, becoming assistant to Carl Magnay in October 2024, although the boss of the National League club would resign at the end of 2024/25 after missing out on a play-off spot by one point.

Just two weeks ago, the former midfielder became part of Ashington's interim management setup with Paul Dunn, having previously been an associate of head of football, Ian Skinner, during his spell at Gateshead.

The Northern Premier League Division One East side then confirmed that Bridcutt had accepted the offer of becoming permanent manager at Woodhorn Lane in midweek, with it being claimed: "The appointment represents an exciting step for Ashington AFC as the club looks ahead to the next chapter of its footballing journey."

Ashington are currently 20th in the table, inside the relegation zone, having accumulated 16 points from their opening 18 fixtures.

Many who supported clubs in the Championship within recent times are likely to have been intrigued by Ashington's managerial appointment, as Bridcutt was often credited for being underrated in his defensive midfield role for various clubs in the division.

He made 182 of his 383 career appearances in the second tier, which included guiding Brighton to the 2012/13 play-off semi-finals, where they were defeated by M23 rivals, Crystal Palace.

After signing for Sunderland in a £2.5m deal, Bridcutt made just 30 Premier League appearances and later returned to the Championship to turn out for the likes of Leeds, where he was appointed captain in 2016 under Garry Monk, as well as Nottingham Forest and and Blackpool.

As well as earning promotion from League One with Brighton, Bridcutt also made appearances in the third tier for Lincoln City and Bolton Wanderers, with the Imps defeated against his future employers, Blackpool, in the 2021 play-off final at Wembley.

Bridcutt is yet to speak to the media following his appointment with Ashington, but given his vast experience within the EFL and Premier League as a player and coach, he will be hoping to continue the area's proud footballing legacy.

Indeed, two of the country's most famous footballers, Sir Bobby Charlton and Jack Charlton, hailed from the Northumberland village before being a part of England's only FIFA World Cup-winning squad in 1966.
