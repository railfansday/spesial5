Dion Sanderson issues damning Derby County admission as Rams struggles continue

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Derby County have been one of the surprise packages in the Championship so far this season, but for all the wrong reasons.

The Rams sit 23rd in the table after 16 games with just 10 points to their name, and their misery was compounded after returning from the international break with a 2-0 defeat at the hands of Southampton on Saturday.

There was a sense of optimism around Pride Park ahead of the start of the new season. After last term's excellent eighth place finish and the expansion of the play-offs, there was a real sense that Derby could finally have a crack at promotion to the Premier League.

John Eustace had worked wonders since his arrival in February 2025, building a squad that was seemingly capable of fighting at the sharp end of the Championship.

However, with just one win in 14 league matches to start the new season, the Rams were left cut adrift with Burnley and Preston in the relegation zone, with no escape looking likely.

A much-needed victory over Bolton Wanderers in the final game before the early November international break gave supporters hope that things could turn around, but Saturday's defeat at St Mary's was a depressing thud back down to reality.

Following Saturday's defeat at Southampton, Rams defender Dion Sanderson faced BBC Sport Derby to give his verdict on a difficult run of fixtures ahead, and it's fair to say his words will cut deep for supporters.

"Everything's different, different teams, we prepare differently each week," Sanderson said.

"But I think from a personal note, it doesn't matter what team you're up against, you've got to play for the badge on your shirt, play for the club, and play for yourself, a bit of pride.

"I think the lads this week, especially, have got to continue [the intensity in training]. We've got to take that onto next week and the week after.

"We are in a relegation scrap and that's the reality of it. The lads need to start pulling their socks up now, and we're asking a lot from everyone," the defender concluded.

After just 16 games, a player admitting that a relegation scrap is on the cards certainly isn't what fans will want to be hearing, especially after how much optimism there was heading into the season.

After the interview with Sanderson was posted to X by BBC Sport Derby, some Rams supporters reacted to what their centre-back had to say in the wake of yet another disappointing defeat.

One fan remained surprisingly positive, suggesting Derby did enough to earn a point and encouraged the players to keep their heads up.

Another wasn't quite as forgiving, calling Sanderson 'hopeless'.

Another also voiced their frustrations at Sanderson himself, suggesting he should let his performances do the talking rather than post-match interviews.

Finally, one fan blamed club CEO Stephen Pearce for the mess the club currently finds itself in, and suggested nothing will get better until he is replaced.

Martyn Waghorn believes senior Derby County players could have prevented the Richard Keogh car crash incident which led to injury and backlash.
