Leeds United ace Dan James 'would be an incredible signing for Sheffield United' in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Sheffield United have been urged to make a move for one of Leeds United's players in the January transfer window, with Chris Wilder's side lacking width and creativity.

Following the exits of Gus Hamer, Callum O'Hare, and Andre Brooks, Sheffield United were left short of those profiles. Ben Brereton-Diaz and Romelle Donovan were brought in on loan but bolstering that area could make the difference in January.

Sheffield United have scored the 15th-most goals in the division from the 16th-most xG (FotMob), which will have to improve if they are to make the top eight under Chris Wilder, but a Leeds United fan favourite could perhaps be the answer to their problems.

FLW’s Sheffield United fan pundit Jimmy was asked about the fact the January transfer window is fast approaching. We asked him to name one player that he'd absolutely love his club to make a move for, while keeping it realistic but also being free to be ambitious as well.

He answered with a player who has torn up the Championship in recent years, saying: "I know we have talked about [Ryan] Kavuma-McQueen.

"But in a similar vein, I think that probably one of the more ambitious signings that Sheffield United could realistically look at is Dan James at Leeds United.

"Donovan is currently playing on the left-hand side and doing a good job. He can't play on the right.

"But another man who can play right or left, and probably leans more towards the right, is Dan James at Leeds.

The 25 top scorers in the Championship since the second tier was rebranded back in 2004/05, including Billy Sharp and Aleksandar Mitrovic.

"I think it would be an incredible signing for Sheffield United. And he'd be a game-changer, really.

"Somebody that would transform our fortunes and possibly our chances of making the play-offs and having a real go this season.

"I think it would be a coup. I know it's another Leeds player, which, you know, there is some ridicule between the fanbases about that.

"But, ultimately, if he improves our first 11? Get him in."

Sheffield United were already lacking genuine pace in their squad, but that problem has only been exacerbated by Femi Seriki's injury, which will rule arguably their quickest player out for the remainder of the 2026/27 season.

Speed in the wide areas is something the Blades desperately need more of, particularly when looking to stretch opposition defences and carry a greater threat in transition. However, pace is not their only shortcoming, with creativity and firepower also in relatively short supply.

Bringing in somebody who can offer at least some of all three attributes should therefore be a January priority. James would be an excellent option, with his explosive speed, directness, and goalscoring instincts potentially addressing several of Sheffield United's biggest attacking weaknesses.

It may be an ambitious move for Sheffield United to pull off, but it is certainly not unrealistic. James will have two years remaining on his Leeds contract this coming summer, has recently turned 29, and has slipped down the pecking order somewhat at Elland Road due to the form of Noah Okafor and the signing of Harry Wilson over the summer.

That combination could make him an obvious candidate for Leeds to offload, particularly if they feel they can reinvest his wages or any transfer fee elsewhere in their squad. There is definitely a chance he available this January, which will definitely be of interest to Championship sides.

James is arguably one of those classic players who is too good for the Championship but has never quite been able to establish himself as a consistent Premier League performer. He has 29 goals and 25 assists in 109 Championship appearances, compared to 12 goals and 13 assists across 129 Premier League outings.
