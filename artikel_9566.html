10 Gifts Under $25 That Delish Editors Actually Want This Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Trust, these are the items worth unwrapping.

Delish editors handpick every product we feature. We may earn commission from the links on this page.

Finding a good gift doesn't have to mean spending a fortune. In fact, some of the best presents are the little things people would love to have but probably wouldn't think to buy for themselves. And when you work with a team that spends its days thinking about food, cooking, and entertaining, you get some pretty good gift recommendations.

We asked Delish editors what's on their wish lists this year, and their picks range from clever kitchen gadgets to surprisingly useful everyday essentials. Whether you're shopping for an aspiring home chef, an enthusiastic baker, or someone who loves hosting dinner parties, these affordable gifts prove that a little thought goes a long way.

Picked by Steven Morea, Contributing Writer

For the person who's been talking about making sourdough but hasn't actually started, this kit might be the push they need. The 50-ounce wide-mouth jar includes a thermometer and date band to help you track the starter as it develops. It's a practical gift for beginners who want to get into bread baking without immediately investing in a kitchen full of equipment. Plus, having a dedicated container makes the entire process feel a little less intimidating.

Picked by Steven Morea, Contributing Writer

Anyone who takes their morning coffee seriously deserves a good travel mug. This 12-ounce insulated stainless steel tumbler from Owala features a sliding lid and a comfortable sipping spout, making it a convenient option for commuting, running errands, or keeping coffee close by throughout the workday. It's one of those simple gifts someone will get plenty of use out of.

Picked by Steven Morea, Contributing Writer

Picked by Samantha MacAvoy, Senior Editor

A good pair of kitchen tongs might not sound like the most exciting gift, but Samantha MacAvoy insists they're one of the most useful tools a home cook can own. These 12-inch stainless steel tongs have a comfortable grip and locking mechanism, making them great for everything from flipping meat to retrieving food from boiling water. MacAvoy especially appreciates the difference a sturdy pair makes when handling hot food and says they're the kind of kitchen workhorse that can last for years.

Picked by Samantha MacAvoy, Senior Editor

MacAvoy has another gift suggestion that might make you rethink what's hanging next to your oven: new pot holders. As she points out, people probably aren't washing their oven mitts and pot holders often enough, so the holidays are a perfect excuse to upgrade. This KitchenAid set includes two ribbed silicone pot holders for handling hot cookware, and they come in bright red. It's a simple, practical gift that can help someone finally retire those old, stained kitchen essentials.

Picked by Camille Lowder, Deputy Editor, Community

Camille Lowder is such a big fan of her serrated paring knife that she uses it practically every day. She received hers as a Christmas gift a few years ago and thinks more people should have one in their kitchens. This four-inch knife is great for smaller cutting tasks that don't require hauling out a massive chef's knife, particularly when working with produce that has a delicate interior and tougher skin. It also comes with a protective sheath for storage.

Picked by Camille Lowder, Deputy Editor, Community

Lowder loves the idea of receiving something that makes a dinner table feel a little more special, especially since she doesn't regularly buy it for herself. These handmade, olive-green spiral taper candles have a playful twisted shape that makes them just as decorative as they are functional. They're unscented, too, making them a nice option for dinner parties when you don't want competing fragrances at the table.

Picked by Camille Lowder, Deputy Editor, Community

If you're gifting taper candles, you might as well give someone somewhere to put them. Lowder suggested pairing her candle pick with fun holders, and this set of three red glass candlesticks would make a lovely addition to a dinner table. The ribbed design adds texture, while the red glass provides a pop of color without requiring a complete tablescape makeover. They're especially practical for anyone who loves entertaining but doesn't have an extensive collection of table accessories.

If you've ever tried carrying a heavy Dutch oven full of food to someone's house, you'll understand why Gabby Romero wants this bag. Romero regularly brings dishes to friends' homes for her cookbook club, and she's hoping to find this under the tree. The bag features sturdy handles and extra pockets, giving cooks a more convenient way to carry and store compatible Dutch ovens. It's a particularly thoughtful gift for the person who never shows up to a potluck empty-handed.

Alison Dominguez describes bench scrapers as one of those kitchen tools that aren't strictly necessary but are absolutely life-changing. Once you start using one, it's easy to see why. This OXO scraper features a stainless steel blade and a comfortable handle, making it useful for dividing dough, transferring chopped vegetables from a cutting board, and scraping flour or dough bits off the counter. It's a great gift for anyone who loves baking, though even someone who rarely makes bread can find plenty of uses for it.
