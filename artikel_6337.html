The 40 Best Tech Gifts of the Year Are Here

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

From phone and headphones to laptops and tablets, this is your one-stop shopping guide for the tech junkie in your life.

Gear-obsessed editors choose every product we review. We may earn commission if you buy from a link. Why Trust Us?

The holidays are almost here. Black Friday is still a couple weeks away, but many brands and retailers have already started trying to draw shoppers in extra early holiday sales on all kinds of tech. Finding the perfect tech gift can feel overwhelming—especially with so much awesome new gear coming out every year. And in 2026, with tech prices on the rise, giving someone a new pair of headphones, a game console, or even a TV feels like a big deal. These are gifts you want to get right.

At Popular Mechanics, we pride ourselves on finding, testing, and recommending the tech that you can take home and use to change your life. To help you kickstart your shopping, we’ve curated a list of the best tech gifts to give this season, from home enhancements to entertainment gadgets and must-have smartphone, tablet, and gaming console accessories.

We’ve spent countless hours researching everything from phones and laptops to drones and 3D printers. We’ve also tested them to ensure they deliver on their promises. Here are our recommendations for any tech lover in your life, guaranteed to impress even your most discerning, gadget-obsessed loved ones.

Upgrading from a standard black-and-white e-ink e-reader to color model opens the door for you to read comics, cookbooks, and editions with color illustrations that wouldn’t look right in black and white.

The latest Kindle Colorsoft has a beautiful anti-reflective color screen and ample 32 GB of storage. Its sleek aluminum housing is also waterproof, ensuring the recipient can use it in the bathtub or by the pool.

Amazon also has a cheaper Kindle Colorsoft with less storage and a waterproof plastic body, which seems perfect for kids.

A follow-up to Bose’s legendary noise-canceling headphones, the second-generation Bose QuietComfort is a foolproof gift for anyone who needs an audio upgrade. These over-ear cans can tune out ambient distractions better than just about any other pair of headphones and deliver top-quality sound. Their spatial audio capabilities are also excellent.

Compared to the previous generation, the latest QuietComfort offers spatial audio capabilities and USB-C wired playback. Plus, the new cans come in several stylish colors.

The Fitbit Air fitness tracker is vastly more capable than its understated looks suggest, and a great gift option for touchscreen-averse wellness enthusiasts. With a comprehensive set of sensors, including heart rate and oxygen trackers, this nifty gadget can accurately monitor the recipient’s activity, recovery patterns, and sleep habits. The insights it gathers are easy to access in the Google Health app for Android and iOS, which also offers personalized coaching through a reasonably priced subscription.

Of course, the Fitbit Air is also easy to customize via swappable bands. Options include silicone, fabric, and metal.

Do you know someone who’s constantly microwaving coffee? If yes, we implore you to give them an Ember smart mug. It will keep their coffee hot—at their ideal temperature, in fact—for up to three hours on a full charge. Plus, it might be the only thing on this list that’s dishwasher-safe.

The newest model, Ember 3, comes in three sizes and a wide array of colors, so there’s a perfect model for every coffee lover in your life.

If your giftee is a tinkerer, having a good set of tools for tech repairs on hand can save you from a lot of headaches. This set from the device-crackers at iFixit is specifically designed to help you get into and repair computers, smartphones, tablets, and gaming devices, and it comes with thousands of instructions for various repairs.

This set includes a 64-bit precision driver set, tweezers, flex extension, opening tools, and an anti-static wristband. Everything is stored in a compact magnetic case to keep all the small pieces together, even in transit.

No touchpad can match the ergonomics, precision, speed, and customizable features of the recently launched Logitech MX Master 4. What’s more, the gift-worthy wireless mouse is also the first to offer haptic feedback, elevating countless mundane tasks—from switching between apps to navigating audio playback.The MX Master 4 has a USB-C adapter for wireless connectivity, as well as Bluetooth. Its battery can last for weeks between charges.

Camera drones can capture cinematic footage of the environment from impressive heights and distances. The Atom SE from Potensic is one of our favorites and a bargain, touting an HD camera that can shoot 12MP photos and 4K video at 30 frames per second.

Plus, it's easy to control, so it's a great gift for new drone users looking to earn their (remote) wings.

A new iPad is always a great gift. It’s always nice to have a larger secondary screen to scroll with that doesn’t make you squint while you’re reading. The latest iPad Air offers a perfect balance between performance and value, just like its predecessor. The slate features Apple’s M4 processor, which delivers laptop-caliber compute power for creative work and smooth gaming.

You can order the slate with an 11- or a 13-inch screen. For the full laptop-replacement experience, I'd recommend bundling it with Apple’s Magic Keyboard and the Pencil Pro, which give you a full range of control for creative projects and deep work.

The Sharge Icemag 3 is my favorite magnetic battery pack right now, and a fantastic gift for frequent flier, students, and anyone on the go all day with no time to stop and top off their phone if the battery runs low. In addition to offering fast wired and Qi 2.2 wireless charging speeds, this pocket-friendly power bank features a built-in metal stand that lets you use the pack as a stand, so you can set your phone down and watch videos or play games while it charges up. And, unlike most battery packs, it looks cool as hell. A battery pack is a fairly utilitarian gift, but this one’s going to get the right person really excited.

This expert-recommended Bluetooth speaker is rugged, versatile, and capable of producing surprisingly powerful sound for its size. It’s an excellent gift for folks who like to entertain in the backyard, bring tunes on a camping trip, or hang out with friends at a park or the beach.

The IP68-rated speaker can withstand being soaked and exposure to dust and debris. Heck, it’ll float in the pool if you need it to. It’s loud and clear, but durable and waterproof, too: It's a real go-anywhere, do-anything gadget.

The Dreamie smart alarm clock is one of Stefan’s favorite home gadgets of the year and a great gift for anyone in your life who’s been thinking about getting a better night’s sleep. It offers a rich selection of relaxing soundscapes and, out of the box, the option to stream podcasts, plus an eclectic lineup of alarms to help wake them up. I like Dreamie’s intuitive touchscreen interface and volume control knob. The device’s built-in dimmable light is also super helpful.

Unlike its rivals, the Dreamie smart clock doesn’t require a companion app and a subscription, so you don’t have to worry about giving your loved ones present with a secret price tag.

The Twelve South Valet will become an instant favorite among gift recipients by upgrading their nightstand, desk, or side table. Crafted from metal and genuine leather, this tech accessory includes a fast wireless charger and ample space to keep jewelry and other small items organized. It also features an additional USB-C charging output under its metal base, and its elevated feet make it perfect for a smartwatch or fitness tracker.

You can order the Twelve South valet in two sizes and a variety of colors, each bundled with a matching wall charger and cable.

A home projector can bring the movie theater experience right to your loved one's living room. This compact XGIMI model can stream content from popular services in crisp Full HD (1080p) resolution. And despite its compact size, it can create a massive 200-inch image—bigger than any TV screen on the market.

Everyone, from tech-averse consumers to serious enthusiasts, should keep a pair of reliable wired earbuds handy in their bag. Whether they’re your daily driver or a backup in case you leave your go-to headphones at home.

Marshall’s Mode wired earphones make an excellent gift, offering an understated design, an integrated three-button remote, and a USB-C connector that lets the recipient use them with any device—from a phone to a laptop. Their sound quality is also excellent for an under-$100 headphone set.

This recently launched Anker charger is the ultimate tech travel accessory and gift, guaranteed to leave a lasting impression. The compact, nifty gadget plugs into any outlet via retractable prongs, so the recipient can use it anywhere—from home to a hotel room across the globe. They will no longer need extra adapters and can pack only the charger and cables.

Anker equipped the charger with four USB ports and a maximum power output of 100 watts—enough to power a big-screen laptop via a single USB-C output—plus a convenient color screen to show the charging output. You can order it in silver or dark gray.

The gift-worthy Ray-Ban Meta (Gen 2) Wayfarer smart glasses are part fashion accessory, part wearable gadget. They have a host of handy onboard features, headlined by a high-quality image sensor, a pair of open-ear speakers, a touchpad, and a capture button.

The gadget not only ups the recipient’s social media game and keeps them entertained, but also offers instant answers to questions, live language translation, and many other helpful bits via access to Meta’s powerful AI Assistant.

You can order the smart glasses in multiple styles with clear, black, or transitional lenses (our favorite). Consider the Meta Oakley Vanguard smart glasses if you are looking for a flashier design.

The Apple Watch Ultra 4 is the brand’s most powerful smartwatch to date. If you are gift shopping for someone who owns (or may be getting) an Ultra, Stefan’s favorite band is the Nomad Stratos, which launched last year along. By combining a synthetic rubber interior and a titanium exterior, the band is comfier than metal-only bracelets without skimping on wrist presence or durability.

Nomad offers the band in natural or black titanium finishes; a perfect match for the Ultra. The soft interior is available in black, orange, or neon green, dubbed Volt.

The Hoto Compressed Air Capsule is a great gift for anyone who uses electronics—not just hardcore gearheads. By combining a powerful vacuum and an air duster into one compact tool, the product keeps all the recipient’s gadgets in pristine condition. It has two levels of air intensity and several bundled attachments, making it incredibly versatile.

Conveniently, it charges via USB-C, so you can charge it with the same cords you use to charge your phone, your laptop, and all the other tech at your desk. Its battery can deliver up to 10 minutes of vacuuming or dusting—more than enough to hoover up the crumbs between the keys of a keyboard and blow the dust off a mousepad.

Peak Design Slim travel backpack is an excellent carrier for your loved one’s tech and commute essentials. It features multiple compartments for laptops, tablets, smaller gadgets, and daily essentials, so the recipient can neatly organize and access what they need on the road. And if you need a little extra room, it can unzip and expand to a slightly larger 23L size so you can get a few more things in.

Another reason we recommend this backpack is its water-resistant exterior, which reliably protects against the elements. Available in black, sage, or stone, it’s also very easy to clean after long trips.

We think 20L is the perfect size for an everyday commuter bag or a daypack for travellers, but folks who carry a lot of gear may want to go with a bigger size: Peak Design makes this bag in a large 30L for gearheads who carry around a ton of stuff, or a truly massive 45L that could replace a carry-on suitcase.

The Native Union Pocket USB-C cable is as thoughtful a tech gift option as it is inexpensive. This tiny accessory can be a lifesaver for someone on the move, letting them charge a phone, tablet, or laptop quickly with a battery pack. It can also come in handy for transferring data.

Designed to look like a pocket knife, the Native Union is more characterful than any run-of-the-mill charging cable. You can order the product in several colors.
