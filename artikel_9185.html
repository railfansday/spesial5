Christopher Glass is an award-winning production designer whose work spans feature films, television, and advertising. Throughout his career, Christopher has worked on movies like The Jungle Book, Ms Marvel, Batgirl, and most recently, Boots Riley's I Love Boosters. In the advertising world, Christopher has collaborated with big brands like Coca-Cola, Microsoft, Mercedes-Benz, and Apple.

His work has seen him become a 2x ADG and Gold Cannes Lion award winner, shaped by his radically practical approach to production design amidst the rise of AI. As part of our 5 Questions series, I caught up with Christopher to discuss pet peeves, imposter syndrome and "learning by fire".

I’ve ignored most advice and learned by fire. I can’t say that’s the best for everyone. It’s made me who I am, for better or for worse.

When set dressers lay out books and magazines on a table at weird angles.

That time I was actually an imposter. I am kidding… probably recently when I went to the Production Designers Collective Gathering in Greece. There were so many amazing and talented Production Designers there.. Some that are my heroes that inspire me and l look up to and I was like, WTF am i doing here? It was a great experience.
