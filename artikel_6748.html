Boots, Bags, and All the New Launches Esquire’s Editors Loved This Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Every product was carefully curated by an Esquire editor. We may earn a commission from these links. Here’s how we test products and why you should trust us.

Plus an incredible Filson and Baracuta collab.

This is odd to say, but new releases rarely hit at the right time for me. What I mean is, new things typically get dropped early season. Or they are announced on a runway half a year before they ever come out. I never see a new clothing release—I guess it’s more common in home and tech—where I say: I need this right now.

But it happened this week. The first two picks on this list are really calling after my wallet. First, Bennett Winch (a favorite around here) put out its newest suede, this classy navy. Though I’m more of a browns and earth tones, it’s got me really excited to get a travel bag packed for the holidays. And right after that, two more favorites, Filson and Baracuta, put together their collective calling cards for an incredible Mackinaw wool G9. After a cold and wet week here in New York, I’m getting real close to buying.

Elsewhere, it’s all leaning a bit gift-y. Fitting as we start to get through our Esquire gift guides: Luxury gifts, gifts for your wife, the tech that everyone’s talking about. It’s small collabs, colognes, and plenty of stuff that’ll look nice under the tree.

Bennett Winch makes the most gorgeous men’s bags you can find anywhere in this world. This navy suede is the latest in a long line of incredible releases. You could walk into the snobbiest first class cabin on a JFK to CDG, and this bag will be the apple of everyone’s eye. —Luke Guillory, commerce editor

Our friends at Baracuta and Filson UK cooked up some insane heat. Like the loggers wearing the original Mackinaw or mechanics wearing the original Baracuta, I shall wear this to my job: Sitting in a coffee shop writing about bullshit. —L.G.

Southern California footwear meets Scottish knitwear in a collaboration that brings each brand’s expertise to the table. —Andrea Ríos, fashion assistant

Level up your shower game with The Jolie Filtered Handheld. Clinically proven to improve skin dryness and reduce hair shedding and frizz, their newest model is built for flexibility and ease without sacrificing sleek design. —Emma Saxon, contributing fashion assistant

John Elliott expands its partnership with Japanese jewelry label MARS to include pendant necklaces. The two brands first teamed up in 2019 after Elliott’s discovery of the brand in Tokyo. —A.R.

The ATP, Palmes, and Pro Shop NYC teamed up to create a collection of apparel and accessories that combines the historical legacy of the ATP Tour with contemporary fashion and modern tennis culture. The result? A collab that showcases a true love of the sport, with heritage logos, fun graphics, and killer style. —Bellamy Richardson, associate commerce editor

I love when J. Press and Alex Mill get together because they take the simple J. Press shapes and give them a bit of umph. This red Shaggy Dog is a perfect example. —L.G.

The thinking man’s navy quarter-zip. —L.G.

Not the best fleece of the week. Not the best fleece of the year. It might be the best fleece of all time. —L.G.

I’m on the record as an R.M. Williams super fan. This country tan only furthers my love. —L.G.

Not new for Ralph Lauren, but newly stocked at Mr Porter. This little set is my number one gift of the season. —L.G.

Two classic brands sit down, and what do they put out? A goddamn classic. —L.G.

Sorel has reimagined its classic Caribou boot as a snowboard boot—and the brand also teamed up with Nidecker for a special Sorel Surfer Snowboard whose top sheet features a graphic of, you guessed it, a caribou. We’re so ready for winter sports to ramp up. —B.R.

Admittedly, Clarks haven’t always been my thing, but this buckled desert boot, created in collaboration with menswear favorite NN.07, is as practical as it is cool. They make a case to take up precious closet real estate as they are subway and date night appropriate. Definitely a shoes for the guy in-the-know. —Alfonso Fernández Navas, senior market and style editor

I don’t golf and I’m okay with that reality. All said, these Eastside Golf jackets made me do a double take and reconsider. —A.F.N.

It’s always a good day when Stella McCartney dabbles in menswear. This third iteration of her collaboration with artists Yoshimoto Nara is a reminder of how she can seamlessly blend art, rock-n-roll and fashion, into desirable clothes. One mustn’t forget the shoes either, the adidas sneakers are wild and wearable. —A.F.N.

Maison Millais teamed up with Sophie Kamdar, a New York-based ceramicist, to place its limited-edition holiday-themed fragrance “La Maison de l’artiste” in beautiful bottles with porcelain caps inspired by the iconic street columns of Paris. —B.R.

This fruity, aquatic eau de cologne captures the nostalgia of a summer morning in Italy. —A.R.

Afform (formerly Parallelle) makes one of our favorite Dopp kits out there thanks to its unique construction, unmatched durability, and cool look. Now the brand has recreated its signature design in a larger scale with its new Weekender and Voyager bags. —B.R.
