Not to be dramatic, but a good hair tool can change your life. The right dryer can cut your styling time in half; a well-designed curling iron can make a polished wave look effortless; a red light hat can support hair growth over time; and a multitasking styler can replace several tools crowding your bathroom counter. Whether you’re treating yourself to an upgrade or checking someone off your holiday shopping list, the only downside is that the best devices tend to come with a high price tag.

That’s what makes Black Friday such a good time to shop for hair tools. Rather than paying full price for a device you’ve been considering all year, you can make the investment at a much more appealing price—or treat someone else to a luxe beauty gift for less. Several of our favorite hair brands have impressive discounts on the tools and devices we reach for again and again. In other words, there’s no better time than now to take a closer look at what’s actually marked down and decide which deals are worth your money.

There’s no shortage of options, from high-tech hair dryers and stylers to red-light therapy tools and multi-use devices designed to do more than one job. But a markdown alone doesn’t necessarily make a hair tool worth buying. This year, we’re focusing on the standouts: the tools that combine smart design, impressive performance, and a price that makes them particularly tempting right now.

Whether you’re replacing a well-loved tool, upgrading your routine, or shopping for a beauty-obsessed friend or family member, Black Friday is a particularly good moment to make the purchase.
