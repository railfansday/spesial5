Mashed Sweet Potatoes

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Sweet potatoes are a fall classic, and they make up some of the best dishes of the season, from dishes like sweet potato pie to sweet potato casserole. But the flavors of the sweet potato still shine in its simplest form. When we’re craving the flavors with no distractions, we turn to this simple, satisfying side. Blended with plenty of butter and half-and-half, these easy mashed sweet potatoes are creamy while being just sweet enough, but you can even turn them savory if you’re missing classic mashed potatoes.

Add the potatoes to a large pot and cover them with at least 2" of water. Sprinkle in a generous amount of salt. Bring everything to a boil, then cook the potatoes until they're very tender—this usually takes about 15 to 20 minutes, depending on their size.

Mash the potatoes with a potato masher. Pour in the half-and-half, add the butter and syrup, and stir everything together. Season with salt and pepper to taste, then finish by sprinkling some thyme on top.

If you’re looking for a make-ahead Thanksgiving side to help your holiday go smoothly, these mashed sweet potatoes are perfect. Simply make them the night before, store in the fridge in an airtight container, then reheat when you’re ready to serve. When reheating, you may want to add more liquid to loosen it up.
