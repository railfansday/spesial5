Woman evicted after apartment’s AI assistant mistakenly approves rent extension

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

A North Carolina woman was evicted from her apartment after an AI leasing assistant mistakenly told her she could delay paying nearly $4,000 in overdue rent.

Laura Mack, a resident of Colony Townhome Apartments in Raleigh, received an email on November 8 asking when she could pay her outstanding balance of $3,934.17.

Mack responded that she wanted to pay on November 30 and received written confirmation that the date was acceptable. However, eight days later, she was served with an eviction notice.

When Mack contacted the leasing office, she said an employee explained that the emails had been sent by Olivia, the apartment complex’s AI assistant, which had mistakenly approved her request.

The complex had introduced Olivia to residents in December 2025, explaining that the assistant could answer questions about rent and maintenance around the clock.

Although previous emails from Olivia had been clearly identified as AI-generated, the November 8 messages were signed by Colony Townhome Apartments and did not disclose that they had been written by a chatbot.

Mack said she believed she was communicating with a member of staff, particularly because the emails referenced a bathroom ceiling leak that had taken almost two weeks to repair.

“I thought it was someone from the office because all my information was in there and it mentioned my bathroom,” she told WRAL.

Despite having the agreement in writing, Mack was evicted on November 19. She subsequently stayed in a hotel while trying to determine her next steps.

“If you told me till the 30th, I’ll get out by the 30th,” she said. “But give me that. You at least honor that. You have to have somewhere where you’re taking responsibility.”

North Carolina state Sen. Jim Burgin subsequently commented on the case, calling the situation “ridiculous.”

“Before someone evicted that lady, they should have reviewed their documents,” he told WRAL in a follow-up to the original story.

A spokesperson for Colony Townhome Apartments told the site that the property’s owners were gathering information about the eviction and expected to provide an update at a later date.
