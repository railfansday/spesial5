An important addition to the series’ lore, One Piece Chapter 1200 reveals the contents of Lili’s letter and sheds light on Luffy’s new monkey form.

Luffy first appeared in a monkey-like form resembling Gear 5 in Chapter 1198. In Chapter 1199, he seemed to lose control and went after Dorry and Brogy. The giants also noticed that the rhythm sounded different from the usual Drums of Liberation.In Chapter 1199, Luffy leapt at Dorry and Brogy with a bolt of lightning in one hand and a shield in the other, echoing the Elbaf mural of the Sun God Nika.
