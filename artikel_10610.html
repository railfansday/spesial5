The rewards can make a noticeable difference to how quickly you build your collection. Lucky Shots guarantee a successful catch, which is particularly useful when you’re aiming for rare or high-level animals that would normally have a low catch rate. The two temporary boosts target different parts of your progression. 2x Training Boosts double the Shot Power you gain at training stations, making it easier to meet the requirements for higher-level zones.
