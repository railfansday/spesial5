Deciding whether to participate or not in GTA 6's Safe Word mission is tricky. On the one hand, you don't want to blow your cover, on the other, the setup is incredibly funny. Jason and Lucia head to a mansion to steal a safe, masquerading as pool cleaners, but once Jay heads inside, it's clear that he's actually playing a poolboy in an erotic flick.

While applying lotion to the female performers, Lucia will get increasingly agitated in his earpiece, and then you'll finally have to choose whether to actually participate in the scene or not:

Jason will say he needs to go to the bathroom and make his way inside the mansion, continuing the mission to find and steal the safe. So, on the whole, choosing not to participate is better if you care about your romance level and how it might impact things later. If not, say what you will.
