Death Stranding 2 Headlines PlayStation Plus Game Catalog For November

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The next batch of games for the PlayStation Plus Game Catalog has been revealed. PS Plus Essential and Premium subscribers can grab 10 games in total this month, starting November 11. In the US and UK, several of these games will roll out later in the month.

This month's headline act is Death Stranding 2: On the Beach, a post-apocalyptic open-world game from the mind of Hideo Kojima. A game about human connection at its core, Death Stranding 2 is a polarizing and delightfully weird game, so if the idea of GTA 6's bustling metropolis doesn't appeal to you, then maybe delivering packages in a desolate Australian landscape might appeal to you.

Or if you want the hustle and bustle of a sandbox without the consequences GTA 6 is teasing, Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii is worth checking out. This spin-off features fan-favorite character Majima as the protagonist, and as you'd expect from a Yakuza game, it's downright strange, melodramatic, and action-packed.

Other highlights this month include the Dragon Quest 3 HD-2D Remake for a modernized spin on a JRPG classic, survival-action meets the irradiated British countryside in Atomfall, and Trepang 2 is a blistering blast of first-person action.

The hardcore World War 2 first-person shooter Hell Let Loose features massive multiplayer battles, and if you're feeling sporty, you can attempt to hit a six in Cricket 2026 or taunt gravity with some exceptionally tight cornering in MotoGP 26.

PlayStation Plus Premium subscribers can also add two classics to their library: Soul Reaver 2 and Freedom Wars Remastered.

PlayStation Plus Game Catalog for November 2026

  • Death Stranding 2: On the Beach (PS5)
  • Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii (PS5, PS4)
  • Atomfall (PS5, PS4)
  • Dragon Quest 3 HD-2D Remake (PS5)
  • Trepang 2 (PS5)
  • MotoGP 26 (PS5)
  • Hell Let Loose (PS5)
  • Cricket 2026 (PS5, PS4)
  • Soul Reaver 2 (PS5, PS4) | PS Plus Premium required
  • Freedom Wars Remastered (PS5, PS4) | PS Plus Premium required

Sony says it'll also retire the PlayStation Plus app on PC on December 10. The company says fewer than 0.4% of subscribers used the app, so the PlayStation Plus cloud-streaming app on PC will no longer be available starting next month.

All PlayStation Plus subscribers can also grab a trio of freebies between now and November 30: Dead Island 2, Tomb Raider 4-6 Remastered, and Monster Train 2.
