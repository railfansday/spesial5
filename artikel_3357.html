International Insider: Pattinson Becomes Bond; SkyShowtime Shutting Down; The Brit List

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

We’re shaken and stirred after this week in film and TV, Insiders. Jesse Whittock with you to recall events as the world was introduced to the latest James Bond.

“Welcome to the world’s most exclusive club”: And now we know the identity of the next 007. Robert Pattinson is stepping into the well-heeled shoes of James Bond and will star in the next three films from the spy franchise. Pattinson beat out competition from the likes of Jack Lowden, Callum Turner and Josh O’Connor after emerging as one of the five Deadline revealed a few weeks back would test for the iconic role. The decision was immediately divisive: Many people believe Pattinson has the look and the acting chops to do a great job with Bond, while many others feel he’s probably too well-established already (he is the current Batman!) for a role that has broadly made stars of relative unknowns. Baz Bamigboye’s wonderful column on the casting summed it up, including a quote from a feature film biz source who groaned, “Bond is mysterious. Bond creates the stars. The stars don’t create Bond… For f*ck’s sake!” In the end, Denis Villeneuve went for an actor he knows and trusts – Pattinson will soon be seen in the Canadian director’s Dune: Part Three. The revelation that Amazon boss Jeff Bezos played a large role in the selection didn’t go down well below the line, with one commenter writing, “Pretty awful seeing the fate of James Bond in the hands of a real-life Bond villain.” Since Amazon bought the Broccoli family out of the Bond franchise, there has been industry chatter that the tech and streaming giant would need a bona fide star – someone seriously bankable – to recoup the massive outlay. In R-Patz, they’ve certainly got that: he’s acted in everything from the Twilight movies to Christopher Nolan’s Tenet through to The Batman, and much-loved indie pics such as The Lighthouse and this year’s Primetime, as Dessi Gomez’s gallery item shows. Villeneuve is now designing Bond’s next assignment, but we don’t know when exactly the first Pattinson film is coming. For now, the newest spy on the block should grab a minute to take it all in, say Bond’s of years past. “Welcome to the world’s most exclusive club, but remember to enjoy yourself,” On Her Majesty’s Secret Service star George Lazenby told Pattinson in a sweet video in the hours after the biggest casting news of the year. Accountability demanded: It’s been a traumatic few weeks in the British entertainment industry. First, Russell Brand was found guilty of sex crimes, followed by revelations about David Walliams’ alleged conduct. Former Doctor Who actor Noel Clarke was also in court this week. He will stand trial next year after pleading not guilty to five sex offence charges. The UK industry is facing a reckoning, and this week a group of actors and other creatives – including Natalie Dormer, Richard Armitage and Susan Wokoma – signed an open letter demanding an end to a “culture of open secrets” in the entertainment industry. The letter called out broadcasters, studios, indies, industry organizations and unions, urging them to review processes, protect whistleblowers and those speaking out, and to “become less offended by your inaction being named than you are by the actions you are being asked to recognize.”

Digging it: Another group of young British writing hopefuls had their moment in the sun this week as the latest Brit List of yet-to-be-produced film and TV screenplays was announced. Topping the pile was Gemmy Addy’s coming-of-age feature script Dig For Victory and Archie Thomson’s Cornish crime series Petroc, both of which got 25 industry recommendations. We once again partnered with The Brit List to bring you interviews with the writers, with Addy telling us her grandmother’s World War II experiences inspired her story and Thomson attributing his script’s success to influence from England’s West County… and Taylor Sheridan.
