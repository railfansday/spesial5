Lionel Messi will play his first match in Argentina since his international retirement when he takes part in Carlos Tevez’s testimonial in December.

Messi called time on his 21-year career with La Albiceleste in October, scoring his 126th and final Argentina goal in a 3-0 friendly win over Benin.

It was an emotional send-off for a player that is deified in his homeland, but Argentinians haven’t had to wait long for Messi to come back, with the 39-year-old confirming his next footballing commitment in the country.

The Inter Miami superstar will feature in the testimonial match of his former international team-mate Tevez, which is being held at Boca Juniors’ iconic stadium La Bombonera.

Tevez saw out his career at boyhood club Boca Juniors in 2022, bringing down the curtain on a career that included spells at Manchester City, Manchester United and Juventus.

Tevez made 76 caps and scored 13 times for Argentina, winning an Olympic gold medal with his country at the 2004 Games.

Nicknamed “Apache”, 42-year-old Tevez said last year he wanted Messi and former United team-mate Cristiano Ronaldo to play on opposing teams in his testimonial.

And while Ronaldo will not be involved barring a dramatic late addition, Messi will turn out for a man he shared the international stage with for a decade.

"It will be a ⁠true honour to welcome the best player in the world to Apache’s last dance," organisers posted on Instagram on Thursday.

Other stars involved in Tevez’s testimonial includes ex-United team-mate Rio Ferdinand, former City strike-partner Sergio Aguero and goalkeeper Gianluigi Buffon, who was Tevez’s captain at Juventus.
