Bearman confirmed to stay at Haas for 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Haas have confirmed Oliver Bearman will stay at the team for the 2027 Formula 1 season, after protracted contract talks resulted in a one-year extension.

Despite both the British driver and American-owned team having repeatedly insisted they wanted to continue together, negotiations had dragged on for several months, leading to speculation that his links to Ferrari - who he remains tied to after coming through their academy - were a factor in the delay.

The 21-year-old said ahead of last weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix that he was "disappointed" a resolution had not been found, but the wait for confirmation was finally ended by Haas on Thursday ahead of this weekend's Qatar Grand Prix.

"It's no secret that I'm delighted to be continuing with TGR Haas F1 Team into 2027," Bearman said. "This team has played an integral role in my development, and I've felt a huge amount of belief and support from everyone here.

"We've got big ambitions to keep pushing forward, and I'm excited to lead that work, building on what we've achieved, and striving for even more together - we have unfinished business."

Bearman completes Haas' 2027 line-up, with Brazilian rookie Rafael Camara having last month been confirmed as Esteban Ocon's replacement.

Ocon was meant to see out the 2026 season but a fall out between the Frenchman and the team saw him replaced by reserve driver Ryo Hirakawa in Mexico, before Camara was given the final four races of the season to prepare for his full debut campaign.

Confirmation of Bearman's new deal also completes the 2027 Formula grid, with all 11 teams having now confirmed both of their drivers for next year.

Haas team principal Ayao Komatsu said: "Continuing with Ollie into 2027 was a natural decision for us.

"We've seen his growth, his commitment, and the quality he brings to the team, both on and off the track. He has a tremendous work ethic and a strong desire to develop both himself and the team, and there is no better person, in my opinion, to lead this team forward."

Bearman has been affiliated to Ferrari since joining the Scuderia's driver academy as a 16-year-old in 2021, with those ties to the Italian team continuing during his time at Haas.

The Briton has made no secret of the fact that he considers racing for Ferrari in future as "the dream" but, for now, that path to F1's most-famous team is blocked with Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton staying in tandem next year - and potentially beyond.

Leclerc extended his deal with the team earlier this year for an undisclosed multi-year term, while Hamilton is understood to have a potential option to remain with the Italian squad into 2028.

Depending on whether Ferrari's line-up remains unchanged or not for 2028, and what that would mean for Bearman's overarching contractual situation with the Scuderia, his one-year Haas renewal potentially gives him flexibility to move up the grid somewhere should his strong form continue.

Speaking at United States Grand Prix in October, Bearman said: "I also feel like I'm ready to fight for podiums and fight at the front.

"In terms of a midfield team, Haas is the team that I want to be with and I see my future with. But at some point as well, I feel like I've earned the opportunity to go out and look for a top-four team.

"And of course, with my link with Ferrari, that would be my ultimate goal."
