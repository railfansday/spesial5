Sources Reveal Why Alix Earle Is “Snubbing” ‘Dancing With the Stars’ After Coming in 2nd Place Last Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Alix lives in L.A. but hasn’t stopped by—and apparently it’s intentional.

The Dancing With the Stars Season 35 audience has been absolutely packed with friends, family, random celebrities, and former contestants—but one person who hasn’t popped by this season? Alix Earle, who came in second place with Valentin Chmerkovskiy during Season 34 last year. And apparently the snub is intentional (though, to be clear, there are no hard feelings).

“Alix blew up after the show, so long story short, she isn’t posting about it because she doesn’t need to,” a source tells The Sun. “She’s gotten bigger than that. She is so grateful for the show and all it did for her career. But she is ready to move on from that chapter.”

Per the insider, Alix isn’t particularly close to Val anymore—and when it comes to his DWTS journey with Jenna Dewan, “She wishes them well, but honestly, she doesn’t really care. They don’t talk regularly, she’s more removed from that world now.”
