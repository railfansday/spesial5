Elle and Dakota Fanning Are Starring as Sisters in <i><em>The Nightingale</em></i>: What We Know So Far

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Despite being sisters who both work in Hollywood, Dakota and Elle Fanning have starred opposite each other just once. The movie, titled I Am Sam, was released in 2001 and featured the girls when they were 6 and 2 years old. That remained their only joint acting credit—until now.

Twenty-five years later, Elle and Dakota are sharing the screen for the second time ever in the film adaptation of The Nightingale, a war story about two sisters who take separate paths during a trying time in France’s history. Here’s everything we know about the film so far.

The Nightingale is adapted from Kristin Hannah’s 2015 novel of the same name. The summary for the book reads: “The Nightingale tells the stories of two sisters, separated by years and experience, by ideals, passion, and circumstance, each embarking on her own dangerous path toward survival, love, and freedom in German-occupied, war-torn France—a heartbreakingly beautiful novel that celebrates the resilience of the human spirit and the durability of women.”

Elle and Dakota will star as Isabelle Rossignol and Vianne Mauriac, respectively. The cast also includes Douglas Hodge, Edmund Donovan, Mark Rylance as Julien Rossignol, Albrecht Schuch, Gwilym Lee, Shira Haas as Rachel de Champlain, and Vinette Robinson.

In the sisters’ joint interview with ELLE earlier this year, the pair admitted that they were nervous about acting together on-screen for The Nightingale, but “the power of the book” drew them in.

“There are lots of complicated things within the story, but at its core, it’s actually a quite simple story, which is the everlasting love between these two sisters, set within a time period that is so rife with tragedy,” Dakota explained. “It’s clearly a story that people are able to put themselves in, in some capacity, and feel all those emotions and the dynamic between our characters. So that was just impossible to ignore.”

Dakota continued, “It’s the first time that it felt like an undeniable thing. Whereas when we were younger, it felt a little more clichéd and it didn’t feel emotionally right.”

Elle noted that coming together on a project feels fitting now because they’ve had time to grow as separate individuals. “At the age we are now, after having forged our own paths and our careers and acting styles separately, being able to come together just feels right. And we have to do it so people will stop asking us,” she said. Dakota added, “We’re like, ‘We’re going to literally disappoint everyone we’ve ever met if we don’t make this happen somehow.’”

The trailer for the movie arrived on November 19. In the footage, the sisters share a moment of tenderness, lying in bed together and smiling at each other, but Vianne (Dakota) quickly reminds her sister Isabelle (Elle) that she needs to keep herself out of harm’s way.

“I have one job: to keep my family safe. And you’re part of my family whether you like it or not,” Vianne tells her sister. Isabelle, however, has a different attitude. “They’re at our doorstep. In our homes,” she retorts. “We must show them that we will fight back.” Isabelle ultimately uses her rage to join the rebellion, putting herself and her family at risk.

The Nightingale opens in theaters on March 19, 2027.
