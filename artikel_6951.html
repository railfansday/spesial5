These 5 Brands Are Making the Holiday Pajama Sets I Want Most—I've Rounded Up the Best Ones

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

I'd happily make these my everyday uniform if I could!!

Sure, decorating my house, lighting a fresh pine-scented candle, and listening to Christmas music all day, every day get me in the holiday spirit, but what really does it for me? A pair of matching holiday pajamas. As someone who wears a two-piece set to bed pretty much every single night, I love swapping in new prints and colors to fit the time of year. There’s just something about a festive set that really completes the cozy vibe!

That said, I’m not the type of person who’ll settle for a basic plaid set and call it a day. With brands dropping holiday PJs in everything from cool patterns to out-of-the-box color combos, you'll find plenty of unique options to choose from. Ahead, I rounded up five brands with seriously cute designs that'll give your nighttime wardrobe a seasonal refresh. They also make great gifts for loved ones or the perfect picks for your next matching-pajama party with friends and family!

For as long as I can remember, Old Navy has been one of the go-tos for holiday pajama sets. The brand always comes through with cute designs at affordable prices—something that feels increasingly rare in this economy! Its iconic flannel sets come in all kinds of styles, from classic plaid and gingerbread prints to lively options featuring elves, stripes, and mistletoe. Even better? Tons of this season’s styles are currently 60% off, so I’d run, not walk!

Known for its fun, quirky designs, Roller Rabbit has perfected the art of making PJs that feel anything but basic (the monkeys!) while still being incredibly cozy. Its holiday collection is no exception, with colorful prints, funky patterns, and plenty of personality. If you're a maximalist or you just love a bold pajama set, this is where to get them! I know they’re on the pricier side, but I promise they’re worth the splurge! (Ahem, these tend to sell out pretty quickly, so I’d add them to your cart ASAP if you have your eye on a pair!)

Right now, the holiday sets are especially gorgeous! I love how the brand sticks to the classics with familiar silhouettes, like cami sets, while giving them a festive update. Personally, I’ll be buying the bright pink plaid set!

From my teenage years to now, Victoria's Secret has always been at the top of my list for bras, underwear, loungewear, and, of course, incredibly cute pajama sets. I remember feeling so grown-up whenever I'd get a new pair from the brand, and honestly, that hasn't changed! Its 2026 holiday collection is just as good as ever, with on-trend designs that feel a little more dressed-up than your everyday PJs. Choose from cute short sets and long flannel styles for those who love something extra cozy, to silky satin sets and lightweight Henley styles for the girls who tend to run hot. There's something for every kind of sleeper!
