PS Plus Is Up To 33% Off In Black Friday Sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The best and biggest discounts of all, as is typically the case, are for those who don’t have an active PS Plus subscription at all right now. The discounts also get bigger the higher up the tier list you go.

If you sign up to Essential by November 30, you can get 20% off the usual price of the lowest tier for 12 months, dropping the price from $79.99 to $63.99. That’s just $5.33 a month, so less than half the cost of rolling monthly payments.

Only 12-month subscriptions have been discounted for Black Friday across all three tiers.

Every game coming to Essential, Extra, and Premium and the dates you'll be able to play them for free.

Again, the higher the tier you commit to for the next 12 months, the bigger the discount you stand to get. Extra, which grants you access to an ever-changing library of PS4 and PS5 games on top of multiplayer access and monthly free games, is 25% off if you sign up this weekend, dropping the annual cost from $134.99 to $101.24.

Signing up for Premium gets you all of the above along with PS Portal cloud streaming capabilities and access to the Classics Catalog, among other things. A 33% discount to be had on that, which means its yearly price is currently less than Extra’s usually is at $107.19. A year of Premium will cost you $159.99 if you wait until the Black Friday deal has ended to sign up.

PlayStation runs these PS Plus offers every year for Black Friday. That means there will be people out there whose discounted annual deals run out just in time for them to take advantage of the next one each year. If you always feel like you’re on the outside looking in when everyone else is paying less for PS Plus on Black Friday, maybe try to time it right in 2027 so that your subscription expires just in time for you to get Plus for less during next year’s inevitably similar sale.

It's in addition to the feature that tells you a game's lowest price in the last 30 days.
