Try One of 2026's Best PS5 Games on PS Plus Premium Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The new James Bond simulator from IO Interactive is available to play for 60 minutes on the top tier of the membership service, which begins from the start of the campaign. That one hour of play should see you through the entire prologue mission, Against the Odds, and then you should have enough time left to sample the training mission, The Needle's Eye.

If you like what you play, then your progress will carry over into a full PS5 game purchase. From tomorrow, the Deluxe Edition of 007 First Light will go on sale in the PS Store Black Friday promotion, so you'll be able to pick it up cheaper than full price.

The social media post confirming the Game Trial states it's open to "all PlayStation Plus members", but this isn't actually true. Game Trials are only found on the PS Plus Premium tier.

007 First Light is one of our favourites from 2026, with our PS5 review concluding: "The characterisation of this young Bond and his associates is really strong, and the globetrotting story lets you explore some great locations. It really shines in its sandbox missions, but it weaves these together with its more linear moments in a seamless, cinematic way."
