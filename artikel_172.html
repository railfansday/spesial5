It is nearly 6pm ET in Washington DC where a shooting this morning left 4 people injured and one person dead. Here is a wrap-up of the day’s key events and details:

Police have identified the deceased victim as 46-year old James Stuart of northwest Washington DC. Police have also identified the suspect as 55-year old Matt Wade Lambert of northwest Washington DC, who died from a self-inflicted gunshot wound.

2 men and 2 women were injured in the shooting. One of the victims is in critical condition while the others have non-life-threatening injuries.

Police said that the victim killed inside of the gym was the building manager for that location. They added that they believe the shooting was related to the suspect’s grievances with the building’s management over maintenance issues.

The suspect broke the gas line inside his apartment fireplace, police said, adding: “We know he started a fire.” “There were fire flames that were visible on the outside of the apartment building. We also know that there was gasoline and potential other flammable materials that were inside of his apartment,” police said.

Three firearms were located inside the suspect’s apartment. Those were a shotgun, a rifle and a handgun – all of which were used by the suspect.

VIDA Fitness, the gym at which the shooting unfolded, remains closed until further notice and access to the facility remains restricted. “We are devastated by the tragic loss of life at our Logan Circle location earlier today,” the gym owners said.

The justice department has set up a website for victims impacted by the shooting. The website includes resources for victim service providers, law enforcement, first responders, and community and faith leaders responding to the shooting.

A vigil is expected to be held at some point for the victims impacted by this morning’s shooting, NBC Washington reports.

Meanwhile, an investigation remains underway into the suspect’s exact motives.

Joel Braithwaite, an attorney who previously represented in a civil matter the suspect involved in Thursday’s shooting, just held a press conference.

Braithwaite spoke of the suspect’s litigation with his apartment building’s management and said he was “at a loss for how things took any sort of turn that would have us here today.”

VIDA Fitness has issued a statement following this morning’s shooting, which it called “frightening and deeply unsettling.”

“We are devastated by the tragic loss of life at our Logan Circle location earlier today. Our deepest sympathies are with the family, friends, and loved ones of the individual who lost their life. Our thoughts are also with our members, employees, and everyone who was affected by this tragedy,” the gym owners said.

They added that although there is no longer an active threat, the gym’s Logan Circle location remains closed until further notice and access to the facility remains restricted.

“We do not yet know when members and employees will be able to retrieve personal belongings left inside the club. We will communicate that information as soon as it becomes available,” they continued.
