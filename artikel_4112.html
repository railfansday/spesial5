How To Unlock Character Switching In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

It takes longer than you think to unlock switching between Jason and Lucia in GTA 6. Here's what you need to do.

Just like in the series' last game, Grand Theft Auto 6 features multiple playable protagonists in the form of Jason and Lucia, giving you two characters to swap between and experience Leonida with.

Whereas GTA 5 gave us access to character swapping fairly early on in the game, the process is a little bit longer in GTA 6, with it being quite a while before you have full control over Jason and Lucia. Here's what you need to know.

As we highlighted in our guide about how to unlock Lucia, you'll spend almost all of the game's first chapter playing exclusively as Jason, before moving over to just Lucia for almost half of Chapter 2. The circumstances of the story mean that you'll only be playing as Lucia for a good number of missions before the pair reunite.

Once you've completed the mission Breakfast Run, which sees Jason and Lucia starting their life of robberies and crime together, you'll essentially have moved on to a new part of the game. After that story mission is complete, you'll start Coffee Break, which introduces the ability to swap characters and unlocks it from there on in.

To actually swap characters, just hold Right on the D-Pad and then press R1 on PlayStation or RB on Xbox. Depending on how close the two characters are, the swap will either be instantaneous or zoom out on the map GTA 5 style and take a few seconds to load and reach whichever protagonist you're moving over to.
