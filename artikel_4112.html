How To Appraise Goods In Probably Stolen

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

How to inspect goods, evaluate their worth, and level up your appraisal skills in Probably Stolen.

On day eight of running your shop in Probably Stolen, you'll be visited by a member of the Appraisal Board, who has a job offer for you: appraise items for scavengers and get a fee for it. The more you appraise and the better you are at the job, the more you can earn from each appraisal.

So how do you score top dollar in this system? Learn what you're looking at, check your prices, and keep the work consistent. Here's everything you need to know about appraisals in Probably Stolen.

To appraise something is to give an estimate of its value based on its condition, the materials it's made of, and where it comes from. It can also include factors like whether it's a limited edition product or whether it has historical value.

As I said, you unlock appraisals on day eight if you pay the 85 credit fee to join the Appraisal Board. This fee covers your appraisal starter kit: a magnifying glass, a guide for cybernetic hand valuation, and a junior appraisal certificate.

For every day that you've got your certificate in your showcase, you'll get at least one customer come to your window with items to appraise. There are three basic categories of items that can be appraised in the game: cybernetic hands, Ribwich sandwiches, and plasma cartridges. They unlock as you rank up your certification.

Accept the Appraisal Board's offer on day eight and pay the 85 credit fee.

The basic appraisal kit and unlocks Cybernetic Hands. 33% chance of an appraisal customer coming in per day.

Get 10 perfect appraisals and pay the 40 credit fee.

Unlocks Ribwiches. 42% chance of an appraisal customer coming in per day.

Get 15 perfect appraisals and pay the 60 credit fee.

Unlocks Plasma Cartridges. 48% chance of an appraisal customer coming in per day.

Get 25 perfect appraisals and pay the 100 credit fee.

55% chance of an appraisal customer coming in per day.

If you don't have the money to pay for your promotion, you can ask the board member to come back in seven days, which gives you a chance to save up. Your certificate stays valid for your current level if you do this, so you can keep appraising in the meantime.

Getting perfect scores on your appraisals is relatively simple. Your guidebooks will tell you exactly what you need to look for, so as long as you follow them carefully, you can get a perfect score every time. Remember, your interactions with customers aren't timed; you can take as long as you need to get everything right. I highly recommend keeping the guidebook open beside your magnifying glass tray. I also recommend going page by page for each item rather than trying to remember the amounts you need to add or subtract.

You're always starting from the base value of the item and adding or subtracting credits based on the characteristics of each one, so any set bonuses are given on top of the base value. You can adjust values at the bottom of the magnifying glass tray in increments of five, 10, and 100 credits in either direction.
