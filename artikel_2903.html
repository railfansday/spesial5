Lakers vs. Kings Preview: Time to start another winning streak

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

After witnessing their nine-game losing streak come to an end, the Lakers (13-3) have the opportunity to get back in the win column on Friday against the Sacramento Kings (1-13). This is considered an NBA Cup Game for both teams under Group C.

Who: Los Angeles Lakers vs. Sacramento Kings

Nobody is more disappointed in the Lakers' losing streak ending than the team themselves. After Wednesday's loss to the Utah Jazz — where they put up their worst offensive game of the season — head coach JJ Redick and Luka Dončić were pretty bullish on not bringing up the fatigue card despite this being their third game in four nights.

That alone says a lot about this team's character and their determination to be the best they can be, night in and night out. It's what makes the upcoming match against the Kings quite interesting because we're going to see how the Lakers bounce back, especially with the added stakes of an NBA Cup Game.

On Friday, Los Angeles will see a Sacramento team that has lost seven games in a row. That means the Kings could come into this matchup either very hungry for a win, struggling to find their footing or perhaps a little bit of both. Maybe playing on the road will help these Kings because they just finished a disastrous 0-7 homestand.

Ideally, the Lakers shouldn't need Dončić to score 40 points again in this one because it would nice to see a collective team effort win. In their last meeting against the Kings, when the Lakers came away with a 13-point win, we saw arguably their best defensive effort of the season. Hopefully, we see that same level of intensity again because the Lakers could use as convincing a win as possible to keep their NBA Cup hopes alive.

If it's not too much to ask, perhaps an 11-point win or more, so the purple and gold can climb to second place, with the hope that the Warriors lose their remaining NBA Cup games as well. That would set the Lakers up as the wildcard team in Group C.

The Lakers need to make sure that Darius Acuff Jr. — the only bright spot for the Kings so far this season — doesn't go off. Same goes with a microwave scorer like Zach LaVine and even Domantas Sabonis, who combined for 37 points last time. That was the same L.A. team that lost the rebounding battle (46-36), which can't happen again.

It would be nice to see a bounceback performance from Walker Kessler, who struggled mightily against the Jazz last Tuesday. Same goes with Sandro Mamukelashvili, Colin Sexton and Jake LaRavia, who need to provide some scoring to ease off the pressure on Dončić and Quentin Grimes.

If the Lakers regain their offense and play like the team that had just won nine straight games, then they should be able to take care of this one. Let's see if they can start another winning streak by defeating the Kings on Friday.

The Kings have struggled to close out games, which could work in the Lakers' favor. Ideally, L.A. won't need a clutch-time performance but if it comes down to that, they should be able to capitalize.

The Kings also don't guard the 3-point line very well. In their previous game against the Phoenix Suns, the latter shot 44% from deep.

On to the injury report, only Austin Reaves (left heel contusion) is out.

As for the Kings, Ben Simmons (right Achilles tendon repair) and Maxime Raynaud (right patella subluxation) are out. Precious Achiuwa (left lower leg contusion) is doubtful, while De'Andre Hunter (left hip soreness) is questionable.
