The Rock Returning To WWE TV Soon, But Not In The Way You’ll Want

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Rock is one of the biggest WWE names of all time. The young Dwayne Johnson debuted in the Stamford-based promotion in 1996 at the Survivor Series PPV, which kick-started one of the greatest careers in professional wrestling. Three decades later, WWE reportedly has plans to honor the legacy of the Final Boss.

According to WrestleVotes Radio on FightfulSelect, WWE has plans to celebrate 30 years of The Rock this month. The TKO-led company plans to release a commemorative championship belt, special merchandise, and social media campaigns honoring his debut.

At the WWE Survivor Series 1996, The Rock debuted as 'Rocky Maivia' with the event taking place at Madison Square Garden. The Brahma Bull teamed with Marc Mero, Jake Roberts, and The Stalker against the team of Crush, Goldust, Hunter Hearst Helmsley, and Jerry Lawler. It was the traditional 5-on-5 Survivor Series match, and The Rock was the sole survivor for his team. He made a great impression as a blue-chip prospect that night.

However, things went downhill as the fans booed him out of the building for months, including 'Die Rocky Die' chants. This led him to make a huge change to his character and transition into becoming The Rock. After garnering popularity as a member of The Nation of Domination, he transitioned into a singles performer and became a marquee name with his electrifying personality, in-ring acumen, and connection with the fans.

The People's Champion's full-time career came to an end around 2003. However, he has wrestled some matches and made sporadic appearances since then. Years later, The Rock is now a part of the Board of Directors of TKO, WWE's parent company, taking the business ahead in a different capacity. As for his wrestling career, The Rock's latest match saw him and Roman Reigns defeat Cody Rhodes and Seth Rollins at WrestleMania 40 Night 1.

With The Rock completing 30 years since his debut, it will be interesting to see this celebration unfold.
