Gators’ O’Connell Center to be renamed in lucrative deal with Tower Hill Insurance

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

GAINESVILLE — The University of Florida is renaming the arena at the Stephen C. O'Connell Center after securing a new naming-rights agreement with Tower Hill Insurance, a source confirmed to the Orlando Sentinel.

Gainesville-based Tower Hill and UF's University Athletics Association have agreed to a five-year deal running through June 30, 2031, with an option to extend the agreement for another five years. The two parties declined to disclose financial terms, but the source said the deal is the most lucrative naming-rights agreement for a college sports arena.

The agreement follows the expiration of the university's 10-year, $5.9 million deal with Exactech, a Gainesville-based medical device company. Signed in 2016 after a $64.5 million renovation of the O'Connell Center, the deal gave the facility the name Exactech Arena at the Stephen C. O'Connell Center

Tower Hill Insurance will now hold the naming rights to the arena, which UF hosts men's and women's basketball, volleyball and gymnastics competitions.

The UF Board of Trustees approved a naming-rights arrangement Thursday, according to the Gainesville Sun, along with a $22.5 million loan for the $77 million expansion of the Lemerand Athletic Center.

The expansion will allow UAA offices, including the athletic director's office, to relocate from Ben Hill Griffin Stadium. The project is part of the $1.45 billion renovation of the Swamp.

The existing 46,000-square foot facility includes three full volleyball courts, training and locker room areas, team meeting spaces and coaches' offices for several sports. The new three-story Lemerand Center will also feature a state-of-the-art training facility.
