The Launch: November’s Must-Know Fashion News

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Fall is in full swing, and with it, so is the fashion industry. As the leaves continue to turn and preparations for the holiday season get serious, brands are finding new ways to celebrate with exciting new capsules, collaborations, and stocking-worthy releases. Look no further than J.J. Martin, whose new book, Table Energy, explores the art of Italian entertaining, and Rimowa x Maison Margiela, which turns travel into something even more desirable. Below, all the fashion news to know this month.

What: High jewelry collection and new boutique

Where: 237 Worth Ave, Palm Beach, Florida

Why: A 1920s Palm Beach estate is a fitting setting for Bulgari’s first U.S. presentation of “Eclettica.” Designed by Addison Mizner, the waterfront home hosted a gala that also marked the opening of the house’s new boutique. Venus Williams, Chanel Iman, and Becca Bloom were among the guests, with Jewel performing. The collection includes 15 transformable designs—the most Bulgari has presented in a single collection—alongside more than 50 high jewelry pieces. With its Roman roots and sculptural approach to high jewelry, Bulgari’s “Eclettica” brings a distinctly Italian point of view to Palm Beach.

Where: Online at rimowa.com and maisonmargiela.com and select Rimowa and Maison Margiela stores

Why: Some things look better after they’ve been lived in. That idea is at the heart of Rimowa and Maison Margiela’s new travel collaboration, which brings together two signatures: the German luggage house’s familiar grooves and Margiela’s hand-painted white Bianchetto finish. Designed to change with use, the suitcase and matching bag will take on marks and patina as they travel. A campaign by Dexter Lander brings that idea to life in a Haussmannian apartment, where the white paint seems to spill beyond the pieces themselves.

What: Bird on a Rock installation, window displays, and holiday décor

Where: The Landmark; 727 5th Ave, New York City

Why: Tiffany & Co. has switched on the holiday season at The Landmark, with Amanda Seyfried leading the way. The actress lit the flagship’s Bird on a Rock installation by placing an ornament on its Blue Box tree, marking the third year of the display and a full-circle moment. Seyfried stars in the house’s new holiday film, “You & Me,” which travels between present-day New York and a memory from the ’90s. Mikey Madison, Jordyn Woods, and Louisa Jacobson joined the celebration.

Tiffany & Co. Bird on a Rock Wings Narrow Ring

Why: Just in time for the holidays, La DoubleJ founder J.J. Martin has released Table Energy, a new book about the art of Italian entertaining, with a foreword by fashion writer Leandra Medine Cohen. Its pages move from alfresco lunches to candlelit dinners, all in the colorful, convivial spirit of La DoubleJ. The book made its debut at the brand’s Upper East Side boutique, The Lighthouse, where Martin, ELLE Decor’s head of editorial content Elisa Lipsky-Karasz, and ELLE editor-in-chief Nina Garcia gathered to celebrate l’art de la table.

Where: Online at tecovas.com, aninebing.com, and select retail locations

Why: Anine Bing has a new way to kick up a look. The designer has teamed up with Tecovas on a capsule that reworks the label’s Loretta and Jamie boots with star embroidery and polished goat leather. Cowboy hats and belts round out the lineup, which launches alongside Anine Bing’s spring/summer 2027 collection.
