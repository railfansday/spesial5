Gift shopping can feel like a full-time job—as a shopping editor, mine literally is!—but taking inspiration from a gifting expert is a simple way to keep your ideas fresh. Oprah just released her official Favorite Things List including all the products she’s been obsessed with in 2026, and it’s the perfect way to find someone a thoughtful present.

This year’s picks include plenty of splurge picks, ranging from a Louis Vuitton TK to a Saatva mattress, but Oprah’s Favorite Things also proves you don’t have to blow your budget to give someone a little luxury. We rounded up 10 of our favorite picks for under $50, including lippies they’ll wear all season long and gifts for the budding creative and grandma hobbyist. (I’m eyeing winter essentials like Kiehl’s Hand Salve and a chic reversible shawl they’ll love to wear for holiday parties.)

“If someone needs a hard stop between daytime and bedtime, wrap up these elevated bath salts,” Oprah says. Each container comes with seven fizzy tablets infused with jojoba oil and magnesium sulfate to soften the skin and relax muscles. Choose from three essential oil blend scents. Oprah also loves that they’re “made by a company that gives women rebuilding their lives after incarceration and addiction a second chance at employment.”

When in doubt, a hand cream is a stocking stuffer that will always come in handy, especially during the colder, drier months. “I tried this thick and creamy hand salve with shea butter, avocado oil, and sesame seed oil at a friend’s house,” Oprah says. “It was so surprisingly good that I now keep one in my purse at all times.”

“This shawl is made with a bamboo-based, cashmere-like soft fabric that the person in your life who’s always cold will appreciate,” Oprah raves, noting it can also be used as a scarf or travel blanket. It comes in plenty of hues and color block options to suit your giftee’s style.

A practical stocking stuffer? Matador’s Global Travel Adapter is a smart pick for the frequent traveler on your list—because realizing your devices or hair tools won’t work abroad is a real vacation buzzkill. “This one works in 170-plus countries, charging up to six devices at once. Anyone with wanderlust will appreciate this snappy device,” says Oprah.

Perfect for creatives, the grandma hobbyist, and the “young and young at heart,” according to Oprah, Crayola’s Dual-Tip Alcohol-Based Markers are perfect for the artist in your life. Each of these markers has a fine tip for precision and a brush tip for blending and shading. “I still remember the Christmas I got my first big box of Crayola crayons! I loved it,” Oprah says.

We can never have too many lip glosses, and one (or two) makes a cute stocking stuffer. Available in eight shades, “the colors in these glosses are gorgeous, and I love that you can see them in their containers,” Oprah says.

Cosmo editors are also big fans of M.ph. Beauty editor Beth Gillette says the Le Skin Serum Foundation leaves her skin with a natural glow, and she describes the Overliner Lip Pencil as “artistry-level.” Dare we say this lip gloss is the perfect addition?

“I can’t think of a better stocking stuffer: Wearing one has changed the way I sleep forever, and I promise, they’ll use it every single night,”Oprah shares. “This accommodates lash extensions, provides total blackout, and is great for travel.” Made from mulberry silk, this contoured eye mask is cushioned to block light without adding pressure to your eyes, and it comes in several pretty colors.

“How do I love Truff hot sauce?” Oprah says. “Let me count the years I’ve had it on the list.…Six times, I think. This year’s set has the white truffle hot sauce, white truffle oil, and new smoky pineapple hot sauce.” It’s a great gift for the foodie who loves to splurge on gourmet groceries, the hostess who does the most, and, of course, hot sauce lovers.

A white shirt is a closet essential, and this one is made from cotton with a boxy, cropped fit that pairs perfectly with a straight jean or a body-hugging skirt. “I’ve searched far and wide for the perfect white shirt—and this is it,” Oprah says. “I bought it myself off Amazon, and then saw one just like it in a boutique in Santorini for 15 times the price! I walked out so proud.”

“If you like a matte finish, Anastasia’s lip mousse is perfect. You’d find one in my makeup bag right now,” Oprah says of this lip gloss trio. It makes the perfect present for the makeup lover who knows the power of a bold lip. Each one delivers opaque pigment with a velvety smooth, non-drying feel.
