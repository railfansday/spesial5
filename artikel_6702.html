The 2026 Way to Style Boots and Jeans Is Easier Than You Think

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The options are endless, and the results are timeless.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

If we could only choose two wardrobe staples to wear for the rest of our lives (okay, maybe just for the rest of this season), boots and jeans would be our top picks. The combinations are endless: You could opt for wide-leg baggy jeans or Parisian stovepipe jeans; a kitten-heel ankle boot or an over-the-knee option. The looks you can create are endless, but there are still a few standout silhouettes that work especially well.

Whether you’re getting ready for the office, a date, a casual evening, or a fancier occasion, there’s a way to pair boots and jeans for any dress code. Below, we found the five best ways to style boots with jeans in 2026.

It depends on exactly how slim your straight-leg jeans are, but for the most part, you shouldn’t have any trouble pairing the silhouette with boots (unless they have an extra-wide calf). Moto boots add an edgy element to an otherwise polished striped button-down.

Cropped lengths can be perfect for showcasing your favorite boots. Regardless of hemlines, consider tucking one or both pant legs into your boots for an especially high-fashion styling.

This is my preferred way of styling jeans and boots. I can’t help it—I love a chunky shoe, and I’m partial to my Dr. Martens. I’d skip her open-front jacket and instead opt for a boatneck top adorned with a brooch for a pop of color.

Kick flares and boots go together like peanut butter and jelly. Try an all-black ensemble and step into a pair of pointed-toe stilettos for a look that goes from day to night.

Boot is literally in the name here, so how could you resist pairing these two when they’re so clearly meant to be? This look feels very Carolyn Bessette Kennedy to me, don’t you think?
