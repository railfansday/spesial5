Your Scorpio Monthly Horoscope for December 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Your time is precious, Scorpio, how do you want to wield it?

You’re focused on how you invest your energy, Scorpio. The month begins with the Sun traveling through Sagittarius, bringing attention to your resources. You’re more aware of how limited your time is and want to use it toward something meaningful. This can be a season of making investments—whehther it’s a new idea, signing up for a class that can help you down the line, or making an important purchase. You can also gain more attention at work and stand out for your gifts and expertise.

You’re drawing in more attention once Venus enters your sign on December 4! This is your season to take up space and let others be infatuated by your mysterious aura. You might focus more on how you look, changing up your sense of style and feeling more confident when you choose to self-express authentically. This can also bring support into your world—new relationships or opportunities that stir up passion, creativity, and pleasure in your world.

There’s a new money-making idea you’re ready to explore once Mercury moves into Sagittarius on December 6. You might want to apply to a different type of job or ask your boss for a raise. You’re also thinking more about your finances and how to get your money in order. This energy can also help you invest in yourself and even some of your most outlandish ideas so you can bet on yourself and bring some of your dreams to life.

You might start a new job or make an impromptu purchase under the New Moon in Sagittarius on December 8. This energy can also help you quit a job or contract that’s felt limiting or constrictive. You might also make an important purchase that brings a sense of freedom into your daily life. This energy also helps you stand up for what you believe in and can help you reflect on your relationship with your personal values. Could you write a list of them if someone asked you? That’s a great exercise to try under these moonbeams.

It’s time to clean up your act when Saturn goes direct in Aries on December 10. You’re very well aware of certain habits and patterns you need to break and what you need to replace them with. If you’ve been in a season of saying “yes” to everyone else at your own expense, this can help you firm up on your boundaries. This is also a reminder to tend to your mind, body, and spirit—you have to put your oxygen mask on first before you can help others.

Asking for help can bring the relief you so desperately need when Neptune stations direct in Aries on December 12. This is your reminder that it never hurts to ask for help when you need it. You might also want to incorporate more mystical or creative elements into your daily routine if life is starting to feel too rigid and bland.

You’re making an important decision about your life path and direction when Jupiter stations retrograde in Leo, also on December 12. You could quit a job, change career paths, or accept a role that requires an adjustment period. You’re also standing out for your good nature, talent, and generosity. You could receive positive compliments or feedback and your lesson here is to soak up the praise!

The Sun enters Capricorn and the Winter Solstice takes place on December 21, bringing important news your way. You might finally express something important to someone or perhaps you learn a secret that brings new understanding to a situation. You’re also in a more curious mood and this season helps you commit to studying subjects and learning skills that have always interested you. You have the dedication to stay the course.

A Full Moon in Cancer on December 23 helps you speak your truth. An ongoing conversation can reach a boiling point or new, critical information is revealed. This can also look like learning a piece of information that you can’t unlearn—that also changes your perspective in your personal life or worldview. If you’ve been studying something this can mark a moment of proficiency, gaining recognition, or getting something published. Traveling can also bring ah-hah moments that shake up what you thought was once possible.

The month wraps up with Mercury moving into Capricorn on December 25, helping you to commit to what piques your interest over the next few weeks. You might sign up for a workshop online or try out new events in your neighborhood or community. This is also lovely energy for brushing up on your own divination and spiritual practices. Connecting with your Ancestors and ancestral spiritual practices can bring the guidance you’ve been seeking.
