Joel Embiid OUT (illness) for Monday vs. Trail Blazers

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Update: 5:50 p.m.: The Sixers have ruled out Embiid. They'll have to wait at least another game before playing with the full roster available.

We have a Philadelphia 76ers injury report update for you from late Monday morning. Joel Embiid, who was not on the report previously, has appeared on the list as of 11:45 AM as questionable due to illness.

Obviously the hope here is that it's nothing serious for Embiid, but rather just a minor illness that he woke up with today. The best case scenario would be him feeling better by the time tipoff rolls around and being able to suit up for this one. The big fella has been very available to start off this season, missing just three of the Sixers' 14 games so far — all of which were part of back-to-backs.

We will keep you posted when we have more definitive information on his availability for tonight.

After a bit of an up and down start to the season, Embiid has been really finding his footing as of late, most recently dropping a 26-point, 17-rebound double-double with nine assists in the Sixers' win over the Bucks this past Saturday afternoon. Over the last three contests, all wins for Philadelphia, Embiid has averaged 29.7 points, 9.3 rebounds, 5.7 assists, 1.0 steal and 2.0 blocks per game.

Other than Jo, Jaylen Brown (hamstring), Adem Bona (cervical spine sprain) and Dean Wade (hamstring management) are all probable for Monday night.

The Blazers have been dealing with injuries of their own, with Ja Morant (hamstring), Shaedon Sharpe (meniscus), Vit Krejci (ankle) and Chris Youngblood (heel) all out. Despite that, Portland have still been finding ways to win, notching a 10-6 record on the season so far. This is their first meeting with the Sixers this season.

The Sixers will look to extend their win streak to six when they tip off with the Trail Blazers from South Philadelphia at 7 p.m. ET tonight.
