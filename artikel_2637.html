The Rams’ season starts now

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Los Angeles Rams dug themselves out of a 2-4 hole en route to 6-4.

They put together a four-game win streak. On the surface it seems like their early season struggles have all been corrected. In reality, their last four opponents have a combined record of 11-26-1.

It's reasonable to look at the last month's schedule and conclude we really learned nothing about the Rams. Of course, they should be given credit for keeping losing teams losing.

Who are the Rams following their recent rash of injuries? What happens when stiffer competition slows LA's run game and they must rely on an inconsistent and short-handed passing attack? The defense has stymied offenses of late. What happens when they face the likes of Jordan Love, Patrick Mahomes, Brock Purdy, and Dak Prescott in the coming weeks?

One thing is still true: the Rams have three lives left in the NFC playoff picture

It will take 10 wins to make the playoffs. In other words, they cannot take more than three losses moving forward without leaving their destiny up to factors outside of their control.

When looking on the horizon, LA's season effectively starts out of their bye week.

Perhaps the single-most important game left. The Rams are competing directly with NFC West teams in the race for their own division, although the 49ers may be running away with it. They are also indirectly competing against the NFC North. This Wednesday night game is the perfect opportunity to ding Green Bay and bolster their own chances. It carries significant playoff weight.
