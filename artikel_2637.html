Mavericks score just 9 points in opening quarter during historic loss to Jayson Tatum, Celtics

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Dallas Mavericks got out to a horrific and historically bad start on Sunday night at TD Garden.

The Boston Celtics rolled to a 42-9 lead in the opening quarter. That marked Boston's largest lead at the end of the first period in franchise history.

It gets even worse for the Mavericks. The Celtics' 42-9 scoring margin was the best from any team in the league in any quarter since 1987, when the Los Angeles Lakers outscored the Sacramento Kings 40-4 in the first quarter, according to ESPN. It was also the third-best mark by a team in a quarter in the shot-clock era, which started back in 1954.

Remarkably, it wasn't the Mavericks' worst quarter in franchise history. They managed just two points in a quarter in a game against the Los Angeles Lakers in 1997.

On Sunday, the Mavericks made just two of their first 18 field-goal attempts in the opening quarter, and went a brutal 0 of 8 from behind the arc. Of their nine points in the opening frame, five of them came from the free-throw line.

"I didn't feel like we were turning it over early," Mavericks coach Dusty May said, via the Dallas Morning News' Mike Curtis. "I felt like we had some decent looks. We didn't convert them. I'd have to go back and look at the film for any specifics of why we weren't able to score."

And while things did get better for the Mavericks, it was far too late to make a difference. The Celtics took a 30-point lead into the locker room at halftime, and then rolled to the 131-94 victory.

Jayson Tatum led Boston with 32 points, 6 rebounds and 6 assists while shooting 11 of 18 from the field in the win. Derrick White added 16 points, and Baylor Scheierman finished with 12 points and five rebounds off the bench. Boston shot 51% from the field as a team and forced 17 turnovers.

The Celtics are now 11-2 on the season. They have won six straight, too, and hold a slight lead over the Philadelphia 76ers in the Eastern Conference standings.

Zaccharie Risacher led the Mavericks with 18 points off the bench after he went 8 of 9 from the field. Cooper Flagg finished with 17 points, and Walter Clayton added 13 points. The Mavericks were coming off a loss to the Cleveland Cavaliers in Ohio on Saturday night.

They have now lost three in a row and sit at 7-7 on the season. Thankfully, after a rough three-game road trip capped by a truly bad outing on Sunday, Flagg and the Mavericks will have three days to recover. They'll host the Golden State Warriors on Wednesday night.
