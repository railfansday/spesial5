Australia breaks through in win over Wales in Nations Championship rugby

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

CARDIFF, Wales (AP) — Australia rallied after giving up a 12-point lead in the second half to beat Wales 29-26 and bring some consolation to southern hemisphere teams in rugby's Nations Championship on Saturday.

The Wallabies strained Wales' passionate defense through 24 phases before center Len Ikitau scored in the 64th minute to recover the lead for Australia. Flyhalf Carter Gordon added a penalty in the 73rd to make Australia's lead safe at eight points.

The win is unlikely to dim the new optimism which has taken hold in Welsh rugby after last weekend's historic win over New Zealand.

Wales looked likely to repeat that success when winger Louis Rees-Zammit tip-toed down the touchline and passed infield to fullback Blair Murray for a try that gave Wales its first lead of the match in the 55th. Welsh fans will also savor a try-scoring double from lineout drives by captain Dewi Lake.

The new spirit of Welsh rugby showed itself again when replacement hooker Ryan Elias scored from a lineout drive in the 79th, cutting Australia's lead to three points. Sam Costelow's conversion attempt was wide.

Wales attacked again from the restart but a handling error caused the referee to sound the final whistle.

"Wales are a tough outfit. What a match!" Ikitau said after he was awarded player of the match.

"It's been a tough couple of weeks for us. I know the heart's always there but when you don't get the results it's pretty disheartening. I'm proud of the boys and proud of the effort today."

Australia stopped a four-match losing run, including Nations Championship matches this month against England and Scotland. The Wallabies will play Ireland in next weekend's playoffs while Wales will play Japan.

Both sides on Saturday produced superb defensive performances. Australia captain Harry Wilson was prominent in his team's much-improved defensive effort.

Jac Morgan was equally outstanding for Wales, which wasn't quite able to duplicate the defensive performance which was at the heart of its win over New Zealand. Alex Mann was adept at winning breakdown turnovers for Wales.

The home team's most effective attacking weapon was its lineout drive from which it took three tries. The spirit of the Welsh was obvious in the way backs joined forwards to promote the rolling mauls.

The first points came in the 21st with a try to Australia prop Taniela Tupou. Flyhalf Gordon gave Australia field position with a touch-finder from a penalty. The Wallabies kept possession through several phases before fullback Tom Wright slipped a pass to Tupou who dashed 20 meters to score.

Wales was unlucky early. It was held up over the Australia line on two occasions in the first half and Rees-Zammit lost the ball while trying to score in the corner in the eighth.

Australia scored through hooker Brandon Paenga-Amosa in the 33rd, using the lineout drive effectively and taking 12 points from its two entries into the Wales 22 in the first half.

Wales got on the scoreboard with a lineout drive try to Lake just before halftime when Australia led 12-7.

Australia used the high ball effectively at the start of the second half. Winger Max Jorgensen scored after lock Josh Canham recovered possession from an up-and-under and Ikitau spirited the ball to the wing.

Australia's 19-7 lead was cut to five points by Lake's first try, then eclipsed when Murray scored, setting up a thrilling finish.
