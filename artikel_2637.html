Despite the loss, Rockets Alperen Sengun continues his career-best shooting

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

When the Houston Rockets traded for Alperen Sengun in the 2021 NBA draft, they knew they were getting one of the best international players. An MVP of the Basketball Super League at 19, Sengun was already dominating overseas professional leagues.

Over the years, Sengun has gone from an up-and-coming young star to one of the best players in the NBA and a two-time All-Star. Throughout his NBA career, Sengun has also been one of the best post players in the NBA. He has shown he can score in the paint against the NBA's best defenders. Even with his scoring ability in the post, his offensive game has at times been limited by struggles not only in the mid-range but also from the 3-point range.

NBA teams are smart; they have multiple scouts and entire departments dedicated to knowing every strength and weakness of their opponents. Opposing teams quickly realized that if Sengun gets to his spot in the paint, it's usually too late to stop him. So teams started sagging off Sengun on pick-and-rolls, and whenever he got the ball away from the basket, they forced him to take a shot from outside.

That defensive strategy made life a lot harder for Sengun at times, as it became every opposing team's strategy when facing the Rockets. Sengun shot just 24.8% from the 3-point range in his first season, on 1.6 attempts. The next season, Sengun shot a higher percentage at .333 but at half the volume of the first season, as he sometimes hesitated on wide-open 3-pointers, choosing to pass on them and drive into a crowded paint.

Sengun's 3-point shooting dropped over the next two seasons from .333 to a career-worst .233 in his fourth season. Sengun has often said that is the one part of his offense that needs to improve if he wants to unlock his entire game. Sengun worked tirelessly on his 3-point shooting every summer, every practice, and before every game. Sengun has talked for years about his confidence in shooting 3-pointers, as he told SB Nation three years ago during training camp.

The hard work is starting to pay off in his sixth season, as Sengun is off to the best 3-point shooting start of his career.

Sengun, now in his sixth season, is starting to translate all the hard work he puts into his 3-point shot into the game. Not only has Sengun improved his outside shooting, but he leads the Rockets in 3-point shooting at 48.1 percent. It is on only 2.1 attempts per game, but that is also a career-high for Sengun. A high volume of shots from downtown has never been Sengun's goal; it is more about him being a threat from long range, which opens up the court for the Rockets' center. It's not just that he is making 3-pointers; it's that he isn't hesitating from deep when teams play him for the drive.

Sengun has made a 3-pointer in a career-high six straight games and in eight of his last ten games. For Sengun, if he keeps improving from deep, teams will have to respect him from that range, which gives him more space; with his ability to handle the ball and his court vision, it could take his offensive game to an entirely different level.

With a reliable 3-point game and improved play on the defensive end, Sengun could be in serious contention for MVP votes at the end of the season. Pretty good for a player 16 other teams passed on in 2021.
