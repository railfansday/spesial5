Ja'Marr Chase: Losing when you know you're better is the worst

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Bengals came into Monday night’s game with the Commanders on a five-game winning streak fueled by explosive offensive performances, but both runs came to an end in Washington.

The Commanders claimed a 22-20 win with a field goal as time expired and wide receiver Ja’Marr Chase gave a succinct answer when asked about the hardest part of losing after enjoying so much recent success.

“Losing when you know you’re better, I think that’s the worst,” Chase said, via Jungle Roar.

The Bengals may believe they’re a better team, but the Commanders came up with an excellent approach to taking away the things that make them so dangerous. Chase and Tee Higgins combined for seven catches for 74 yards with Chase picking up 31 yards on his five carries. The rest of the offense was not able to pick up the slack — Bengals running backs had 32 yards on 12 carries — and the Bengals will need to find ways to make sure they can keep producing even if defenses are able to take away their first option for moving the ball.
