Dalton Kincaid active, Christian Benford inactive for Bills-Chiefs

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Bills will have one of their key offensive weapons for tonight's game against the Chiefs, but they won't have a key defender.

Tight end Dalton Kincaid is active for the Thanksgiving matchup while cornerback Christian Benford is inactive.

Kincaid was listed as questionable with thumb and ankle injuries. He missed Sunday's win over the Dolphins.

Benford also missed Sunday's game with a groin injury.

For the Chiefs, Kenneth Walker will miss his third straight game with a groin injury as he was ruled out on Wednesday.

Buffalo's inactives are Benford, receiver Skyler Bell, safety Jalon Kilgore, outside linebacker Mike Danna, offensive lineman Jude Bowry, and defensive lineman Greg Gaines.

Kansas City's inactives are Walker, quarterback Garrett Nussmeier, cornerback Melvin Smith, defensive tackle Omarr Norman-Lott, offensive tackle Diego Pounds, offensive tackle Jaylon Moore, and defensive tackle Marcus Harris.
