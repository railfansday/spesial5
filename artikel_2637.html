Kimani Johnson thrives as Mizzou takes down Auburn, advances in SEC Tournament

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Mizzou volleyball is moving on in the SEC Tournament after taking down Auburn 3-1 Friday night in Savannah, Georgia.

The Tigers (15-10) earned the four-set victory with scores of 25-23, 25-19, 22-25 and 25-20. The win also provided a measure of redemption for Mizzou, which is back in the SEC Tournament quarterfinals after falling to Ole Miss in the first round of last year's tournament.

Kimani Johnson led the way with a monster performance, recording 17 kills on 37 attacks while hitting .270. She added three solo blocks and three block assists, finishing with 19.5 points to lead all players.

Johnson's performance was a testament to the improvement she has made throughout the season. The fifth year was a consistent presence in the Tigers' offense, delivering when Mizzou needed an answer and making her presence felt at the net. Her six total blocks also helped anchor a defensive effort that limited Auburn to a .169 hitting percentage.

Paige Felder provided another major contribution with 14 kills on 33 attacks, hitting .333. Lauren Larkin was particularly efficient, putting away eight kills without an error on 12 attempts for a .667 hitting percentage. Setter Nina Mandovic orchestrated the offense with 29 assists.

Mizzou opened the match by narrowly taking the first set 25-23 before building momentum with a 25-19 second-set victory. Auburn responded in the third, escaping with a 25-22 win to extend the match. The Tigers answered in the fourth, using a strong offensive finish to secure the 25-20 set and close out the match.

The Tigers finished with 54 kills and six total blocks. Auburn managed 43 kills but struggled to find consistency, hitting just .169 compared to Mizzou's .172.

Mizzou now turns its attention to Vanderbilt, which it will face Sunday night in the SEC Tournament quarterfinals. The Tigers already have a win over the Commodores this season, giving them another opportunity to carry regular-season success into postseason play.

With a spot in the semifinals on the line, Mizzou will look to build on its performance against Auburn and keep its tournament run alive.
