Browns QB update: Todd Monken hasn't decided between Deshaun Watson and Shedeur Sanders for Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

The Cleveland Browns' quarterback situation has once again become a topic of conversation. Deshaun Watson led the Browns to a 5-3 start before an injury sidelined him for Sunday's overtime loss to the Saints. Shedeur Sanders, in defeat, played well in what was his first start of the season.

While ESPN has reported that the Browns plan to go back to Watson this Sunday against the Texans, Browns coach Todd Monken said that he was not ready to announce which quarterback would start vs. Houston.

"I really haven't thought through it other than getting through this morning's tape," he said. "We just got done meeting with the guys. They'll flush the game, and then we'll have a chance to meet offensively more extensive a little bit later today."

Monken was asked directly if a player can lose his job after getting injured, which would essentially be the case if Sanders stays in the starting lineup.

"I don't know what that means," he said. "Each week we put the best players on the field that give us a chance to win."

Sanders got a chance to start this past Sunday after Watson suffered a head injury during Cleveland's Week 8 loss to the Steelers. Watson, who returned to practice last Friday as a limited participant, was sacked eight times during last week's 27-6 loss. He briefly left the game early in the first quarter after taking a big hit from Steelers defensive back Jaquan Brisker.

Sunday was arguably Sanders' best NFL game to date. He went 19-of-31 passing for 297 yards, two touchdowns and no interceptions while posting a career-high 114.6 passer rating. Sanders' big day included a 64-yard touchdown pass to rookie wideout KC Concepcion and a 39-yard scoring strike to tight end Harold Fannin Jr. in overtime. The Browns lost the game after Sanders failed to connect with Denzel Boston on Cleveland's ensuing 2-point attempt.

Sanders, a 2025 fifth-round pick, attempted just three passes during Cleveland's first eight games. Last season, he was selected as an alternative for the Pro Bowl while making seven regular-season starts. He went 3-4 as a starter while completing 56.6% of his passes with seven touchdowns and 10 interceptions.

"Well, I'm awfully proud of him," Monken said of Sanders. "It's been 10 weeks, you know, since he had reps with the ones. ... I thought he battled. I thought he showed composure in the pocket, getting to his checkdowns at times. He made plays with his feet, and escaping. ... So there was a lot there to like. Certainly a lot to clean up, and that would be expected from a player that, you know, hasn't really had [much time] with the ones. And I thought there was a lot to build on."

If Watson gets the start, this would be his first start against his former team. The Houston Texans traded Watson to Cleveland back in 2022 in a blockbuster trade that saw Cleveland send three future first-round picks to Houston.

Watson, 31, beat out Sanders for the starting job this summer. While his play has been shaky at times, Watson has played well enough to help Cleveland get off to a surprising 5-3 start. Watson is credited with leading five game-winning drives this season, the second-most among quarterbacks this season behind only Bo Nix (6). The Browns are third in the AFC North standings behind Pittsburgh (6-2) and Cincinnati (5-2).
