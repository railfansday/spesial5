Michaels is giving away Countdown to Craftsmas advent calendars for free on Black Friday: How to claim $10,000 grand prize

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

TL;DR: On Black Friday, Michaels is giving away 500 free Countdown to Craftsmas Advent Calendars per store with no purchase required — plus one $100 gift card per store and a $10,000 grand prize.

If you are doing any in-person Black Friday shopping on Nov. 27, Michaels has a pretty solid freebie waiting at checkout. Starting Friday, Nov. 27, Michaels stores are handing out 500 free Countdown to Craftsmas Advent Calendars per location while supplies last, with no purchase required.

This 25-day box packs a daily craft idea, decorating tip, or store coupon for Dec. 1 through Dec. 25. Each door is designed to be approachable for crafters of any age or skill level, with QR codes linking directly to step-by-step tutorials.

Screen-free projects — Simple at-home crafts like friendship bracelets, Creatology Color Your Own mugs, and family crafting challenges.

Decorating and styling ideas — Tips for personalizing Christmas trees, making hand-lettered gift tags, and putting together festive accessories like Christmas tree headbands.

Exclusive coupons — Special in-store and online offers on staples like yarn, ribbon, and strung beads so you can keep making projects all month.

The best reason to show up early on Nov. 27 is what is hidden inside a handful of boxes. One calendar at every Michaels store contains a $100 Michaels gift card, and one calendar nationwide hides a $10,000 grand prize.

Because stock is strictly capped at 500 calendars per store — and most Michaels locations open at 7 a.m. local time on Black Friday — getting there near opening gives you the best shot at grabbing one before they are gone.

If you end up browsing the aisles while picking up your calendar, Michaels is pairing the giveaway with its biggest sales of the year: a 10-day Black Friday event running through Nov. 29, followed by Cyber Week from Nov. 30 through Dec. 5, with up to 70% off storewide across both waves.

Check your local store's Black Friday opening time before heading out — and try to get there early if you want one of the 500 free calendars while supplies last.
