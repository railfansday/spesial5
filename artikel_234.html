No Good Choices on Iran

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

As top Trump aides clash over a strategy to end the war, the administration is weighing escalation, narrower strikes and diplomacy.

It didn’t take long after last week’s midterm losses for the Trump administration to begin publicly fighting over the war in Iran.

Divisions are now out in the open. “The reason we lost is the war,” Vice President JD Vance declared. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, in contrast, posted a defiant video defending the war. He also had to defend himself from a now-shelved effort — led by a Republican congressman with Democratic support — to impeach him. (More on that below.)

We’re seeing a formidable U.S. military buildup in the Middle East — a third aircraft carrier is expected to arrive soon. Fighter jets, long-range bombers, warships and 50,000 troops are all on standby.

So what does this all mean for the war? Today, my colleagues David Sanger and Eric Schmitt outline four potential scenarios.
