Hibs learn match officials for Saturday evening Aberdeen showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Officials have been confirmed for Hibernian’s Premiership trip to Aberdeen.

Hibs travel to Pittodrie on Saturday, November 7, with the league clash scheduled for a 5.45pm kick-off.

Duncan Nicolson has been appointed as the man in the middle, with Graeme Stewart and Christopher Gentles running the lines. Jamie Wilkie will be the fourth official, while Kevin Clancy is on VAR duty with Grant Irvine assisting.

It will not be Nicolson’s first involvement with Hibs this season. He took charge of their 1-0 League Cup victory over Partick Thistle at Easter Road in August.

Nicolson also refereed one Hibs match during the 2025/26 season, when the Easter Road side suffered a 2-0 Premiership defeat away to Motherwell in November. That encounter saw one red card as Motherwell claimed all three points at Fir Park.

Saturday’s appointment will therefore be Nicolson’s third Hibs match across the last two seasons.

The Aberdeen encounter will be Hibernian’s 12th Premiership fixture of the 2026/27 campaign.
