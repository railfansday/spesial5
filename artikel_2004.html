Koppen feels the heat as recriminations over one summer signing grow | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

IT IS open season right now on just about every aspect of West Ham’s play, their squad, their recruitment and their coaching. With the club at something of an all time low in the modern era, mid table in the second division being their lowest standing in living memory, critics are lining up to have a pop at events on and off the pitch.

Head of Recruitment Nils Koppen does not escape.

His marquee signing of Arne Engels has come upper fire from several sources. The Belgian midfielder actually didn’t have too bad a game – by comparison – against Portsmouth, but that hasn’t stopped many from pointing the finger at West Ham’s summer signing,  with Talk SPORT’s Tom Rennie (Reported with glee by the glasgowtimes.co.uk) apparently leading the charge:

I just don’t see one thing Engels does that James Ward-Prowse couldn’t do,” [Rennie said]. “

That £22m could have been spent on RB, CB, LW, GK. Koppen can’t be allowed another transfer window.”

I’d have to beg to differ, Engels’ through ball that set up Piroe against Preston North End was pure Paquetá in his pomp.

Ward Prowse could only move the ball sideways and backwards. In addition I remember one notorious foot race between Ward Prowse and an opposition goal keeper which the ‘keeper won comfortably, leaving JWP for dead over 40 yards.

True enough, Engels may not be the finished article. But he’s more combative in the tackle, faster across the turf and he has been known to pass the ball forwards. A little like Mateus Fernandez, he’ll likely grow into the season and become undroppable by May.

There are plenty in this West Ham side who are not worth their starting spot or their wages but I’d not put Engels even close to the front of that particular list.
