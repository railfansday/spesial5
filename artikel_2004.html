What channel is FC Thun v Hearts? TV and live stream for Conference League clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Hearts will look to bounce back from their Edinburgh derby setback when they face the reigning champions of Switzerland away from home in the Uefa Conference League on Thursday night.

The injury-ravaged Tynecastle side take on FC Thun at the Stockhorn Arena after being beaten 2-1 by their city rivals in the Premier Sports Cup semi-final at Hampden on Sunday.

The Edinburgh club are without 10 first-team players and head coach Wouter Vrancken will have to put together a side capable of getting a first win in the competition, after a 1-1 home draw against Nordsjaelland and a 2-0 defeat away to Trabzonspor.

FC Thun won the Swiss title last season but have struggled this term, sitting ninth in the table after 12 matches. They are also yet to register a point in the Conference League after opening their campaign with 1-0 defeats by FC Twente and Hadjuk Split.

Hearts next Conference League fixture sees them host French side Monaco on November 26. They then conclude their league phase campaign at home to Borac and away to Pafos on December 10 and 17 respectively.

The Uefa Conference League match between FC Thun and Hearts takes place at the Stockhorn Arena, Thun, on Thursday, November 5, 2026. Kick-off is 5.45pm.

The match will be shown live on TNT Sports 3 with coverage starting at 5pm.

Free highlights will be posted on the TNT Sports Football YouTube channel shortly after full-time.

Hearts have added Calvin Miller (knee) and Tomas Magnusson (concussion) to an extensive list of absentees. Head coach Wouter Vrancken is also without Craig Halkett, Harry Milne, Blair Spittal, Tom Renaud, Ageu, Oisin McEntee, Rogers Mato and Christian Borchgrevink. As a result teenagers Matthew Gillies and Gus Stevenson are in the frame to feature, with Finlay Pollock also on standby after 19 months out injured.

FC Thun have midfielder Fabio Saiz injured alongside defenders Genis Montolio and Mats Seiler. Another defender, Louis Passavant, is an injury doubt.

A team of Cypriot officials will take charge of proceedings. The match referee is Kyriakos Athanasiou, with Marios Christoforou serving as the Video Assistant Referee (VAR).
