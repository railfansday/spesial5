‘Upset’ - Ryan Alebiosu sends message to Blackburn Rovers fans after suffering new blow

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Blackburn Rovers star Ryan Alebiosu has seemingly confirmed that he will not be available for selection again until 2027.

The athletic right-back limped off during the first half of Rovers' 2-1 loss to Stoke City in midweek, as he managed just 40 minutes before being forced out of the game with an issue.

The 24-year-old has been a key figure for the Lancashire outfit so far this campaign, featuring in all of their opening 14 league matches.

So his absence will come as a big blow for Tony Mowbray, who will have to handle missing one of their top stars for the remainder of the calendar year.

Summer signing Gady Beyuku was brought on to replace Alebiosu late in the first half, with the team going 1-0 down just a few moments later, with the Potters sealing all three points in the second half.

Alebiosu has confirmed on his Instagram page that he will be absent for the remainder of 2026, ruling him out for a number of weeks with an issue.

The defender was initially a doubt for this weekend’s clash against West Ham, with Mowbray hopeful he could be available, but a further scan has revealed a more significant issue than first feared.

“Gutted, upset but god has bigger plans,” wrote Alebiosu.

“Will be back stronger for sure, see you 2027 Rovers.”

Alebiosu also missed a period of games for Blackburn last season due to the Africa Cup of Nations, but his absence will still be a big blow for the team.

The defender has previously been linked with the likes of Everton off the back of his performances at Ewood Park, following a 2025 move from Belgian outfit Kortrijk.

This injury also means he will miss the upcoming Lancashire derby against bitter rivals Burnley, which will be a particularly painful pill for Mowbray to swallow.

No exact timeframe has been given for his potential return beyond 2027, meaning he will be absent for at least eight weeks.

Alebiosu had started each of Rovers’ first 14 league fixtures, and he’s been one of the standout performers under Mowbray so far this term.

The full-back has impressed going forward, completing 0.85 dribbles per 90, and getting 1.85 touches in the opposition area each game on average (all stats from Fotmob).

He contributed one goal and five assists from 39 appearances in the Championship last season, highlighting his attacking threat, although he is yet to register either a goal or assist in 2026/27.

Alebiosu’s absence is a massive blow for Mowbray, as he has become a very dependable performer for the club.

And with a more inexperienced figure in Beyuku coming in for the Nigerian - one who hasn't necessarily impressed in his cameos so far - there may be some short-term pain at Ewood Park whilst the French defender gets to grips with the Championship.
