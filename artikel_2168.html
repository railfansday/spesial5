Watford’s midfield struggles will continue until reinforcements are signed – here’s three January targets they must consider

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A major issue for Watford this campaign has been the midfield. The summer departure of Imran Louza was a major blow, with his technical qualities, set-piece delivery and ability to dictate games sorely missed.

Similarly, Giorgi Chakvetadze’s transfer to Udinese has left the Hornets devoid of a creative spark, with his tendency to drift centrally and unlock defences. The club have simply not recruited adequate replacements, and they are suffering for it.

The current midfield options at Vicarage Road consist of Hector Kyprianou, Edo Kayembe, Edoardo Bove, and recent signing Martin Payero. None of these options are particularly offensive or astute going forward, lacking the ball-carrying and forward-thinking impetus of Chakvetadze, and the game-changing potential of Louza.

In order for Watford to change their fortunes this season, the club’s hierarchy need to address the glaring issues in midfield. Here, we put forward three targets the Hornets should consider moving for ahead of the January transfer window.

Patrick Kelly had a breakout season for Barnsley in 2025/26, establishing himself as one of League One’s most exciting prospects. He was touted for a potential step up, but stayed put and has remained a standout at Oakwell.

A busy and energetic player, Kelly could well offer Watford the spark they need in midfield. His excellent dribbling and physical attributes would complement a side in need of inspiration and dynamism. He ranks among the top 10% of players in his position League One for dribbles completed (FotMob).

Barnsley have had a disastrous start to the current campaign, and as such, a cut-price deal for the 22 year old could tempt clubs further up the footballing ladder.

Bate has been a critical part of highflying Stockport County’s success this season. The diminutive playmaker has contributed four assists and started all but one of the Hatters’ league games this campaign. He excels with his technical ability, ball retention and progression, and eye for a pass.

Having put behind him his injury issues from last season, he appears to have recaptured his impressive form from the 2024/45 campaign, cementing himself as a potential coup for alert Championship sides.

At 24 years of age, he represents a good blend of experience and potential to improve further. His position as club captain further demonstrates the leadership qualities necessary to navigate a Championship relegation scrap, which may await at Watford.

Hull City’s promotion and busy summer window has left players such as Kieran Dowell with limited minutes, opening the door to a potential exit. Dowell has been omitted from the Tigers’ Premier League squad, and the prospect of a January exit appears increasingly likely.

Dowell has donned the colours of six different Championship clubs, and has developed a reputation for scoring spectacular goals, as well as being an apt creator and technician. With three promotions at this level, he has the necessary pedigree and experience.

Out of contract in the summer, Dowell would represent a cost-effective option on either a short-term deal or a loan. His lack of playing time in recent seasons is a potential concern, but should he regain match fitness quickly he could be a useful addition to a threadbare Watford midfield.

As Watford seek to steer clear of the Championship’s lower reaches, bolstering the midfield is of high importance. The only summer addition in this area, Martin Payero, has been underwhelming when given the opportunity.

The fitness issues plaguing Edoardo Bove, the most talented player of the current options, has further stunted Watford’s progress.

The January window offers the club a chance to make amends for poor summer transfer business and to provide the squad with the best opportunity to consolidate their Championship status. The three midfield options put forward would help to push the club in the right direction.
