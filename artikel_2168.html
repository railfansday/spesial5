30-year-old’s goalless run extends to 22 months after short West Ham stay

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

West Ham saw multiple players depart shortly after their relegation from the Premier League. While valuable assets like Mateus Fernandes and Crysencio Summerville were sold, others left WHUFC at the end of their contracts.

Among those to do so was Adama Traore, who had only arrived from Fulham on a short-term deal in January. He joined to offer depth on the wings for the Hammers, bringing his electric pace and power with him.

However, went goalless in his six months at the London Stadium. This run is part of a whopping 22-month spell without a goal for the 30-year-old winger, who now plays back in his native Spain for Deportivo La Coruna.

Traore has never been a player renowned for his goals, scoring 33 goals in over 450 senior matches across his career. He offers a chaotic presence on the right, with his speed and strength proving too much for plenty of full-backs over the years.

It means he is primarily a creative threat, getting to the byline and delivering for onrushing attackers or box-crashing midfielders. Traore mainly served as an impact sub in his time at West Ham, but he failed to score for the club.

And, back in Spain with Deportivo La Coruna, his wait continues. Traore has mainly played off the bench for the club, and he is yet to score in 10 games across all competitions. It means that, to date, his last goal came in a 2-0 win over Leicester City for Fulham back on January 18th, 2025.

Traore has retained his impact sub role with Deportivo. He completed six successful dribbles and created three chances (FotMob) in 34 minutes off the bench against Osasuna before the international break.

Traore might not be a goalscoring star, but he brings an impact. For better or worse, the physical traits he offers can add an element of unpredictability to attacks, and against tired legs, his speed his a serious threat.

This combined with his strong Championship record means he could have proven to be a handy squad player for West Ham. He previously managed five goals and 10 assists in 35 games in England’s second tier and has added strong pedigree since then.

WHUFC perhaps aren’t longing for Traore given his limited impact after joining in January. However, someone of his unique abilities can make for a real dangerman when chasing a game.
