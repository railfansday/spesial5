Cardiff City face Celtic regret: Blackburn & Leicester City should react

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Cardiff City will feel they are heading in the right direction under Brian Barry-Murphy, who has done a terrific job since his appointment.

The Bluebirds won promotion from League One last season in style, with the Irishman building a young, hungry team that plays the possession based football that he demands.

So, all connected to the Welsh club will hope that the project under Barry-Murphy continues for the years to come, and there will be full trust in the boss to make the right decisions along the way.

Inevitably, that will lead to changes in the squad, and David Turnbull is someone who faces a fight to stay at Cardiff beyond this season, as his contract expires in the summer of 2027.

The midfielder has struggled for regular game time under Barry-Murphy, with only one of his ten appearances this season coming as a starter.

At his best, Turnbull has shown he is a good player who can score goals and make things happen, but he has struggled for consistency, and there’s no denying that many Cardiff fans won’t be too bothered to see the back of the ex-Celtic man if he does move on.

Of course, his contract situation means the Bluebirds are in a difficult position, but they won’t want to lose Turnbull on a free, so they will surely be open to offers for the 27-year-old in January, even if it’s not going to be a substantial sum.

With that in mind, we look at TWO clubs that should be lining up a move for the Scotland international in the New Year…

We know that Blackburn don’t have the biggest budget in the league, so they are always on the lookout for potential bargains, and Turnbull could fall into that category.

Plus, Tony Mowbray needs to add depth to his squad, and whilst Turnbull wouldn’t be a guaranteed starter, he would beef up the group for the remainder of the campaign.

As well as that, Mowbray could be the perfect manager to get Turnbull’s career back on track. This is a player that scored seven goals in 16 games in half a season at Celtic before he moved to Cardiff, and he hit at least double figures in goals and assists in his three full years in Glasgow.

Obviously, that was part of a dominant Celtic team, but it shows that Turnbull can contribute going forward.

There are many examples of players who have improved under Mowbray, so a fresh start at Ewood Park could be just what he needs, and, if he does get back to his best, he could produce some big moments for Blackburn.

Turnbull’s main quality is his technical ability, so he could easily slot into Russell Martin’s team given the way they like to control possession and keep the ball.

Even though the Foxes are in League One, they still have a talented midfield, so, once again, there’s no guarantee that he will play.

But, Leicester need to manage exciting youngsters like Sammy Braybrooke and Louis Page carefully, so adding another option in the middle of the park could give Martin another much-needed experienced option as they chase promotion.

Leicester aren’t exactly struggling for goals at the minute, but they aren’t finding the net at the same rate as the best teams in the league, so Martin will want another threat from midfield.

As outlined above, this was Turnbull’s game at Celtic, and whilst he didn’t replicate it at Cardiff, playing for a Leicester side that looks to dominate, in a similar way to the Scottish champions, means this could be a good fit for both parties.
