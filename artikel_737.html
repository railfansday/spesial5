The Hotel Bel-Air’s Menu Returns to Tradition

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Everything old is new again, as classic hits are revived, from the Thai-style chicken salad to the house burger.

Since Wolfgang Puck’s 2023 departure from the Hotel Bel-Air and its eponymous restaurant, the luxury lodging has been tweaking its Cal-Med menus with classical French techniques and hyper-seasonal ingredients. The approach was ambitious, evolving, at times even experimental. Yet the largely local dining clientele — 70 percent of weekly regulars are neighbors — who treat the place as a second home (or maybe a fourth, given their net worth) have made it clear they prefer familiarity over ingenuity. Under new executive chef Ralf Schlegel (far left), previously of The Peninsula in Beverly Hills, the kitchen has turned back toward the classics.

The bill of fare has long had a dedicated section of “Bel-Air Classics”: the tortilla soup, the club sandwich, the chopped salad named for late No. 1 fan Nancy Reagan. Now, the restaurant has restored several other salads that had been relegated to off-menu, only-by-request status — Thai-style chicken, soy ginger BBQ salmon, burrata with squash — along with the house burger.

“When you go home, you want what you remember,” explains Hotel Bel-Air manager Tiffany Lai, who insists the restaurant’s back-to-basics pivot won’t condemn it to culinary ossification. “We’re still busy creating new favorites.” She points to the German-born, Austria-raised Schlegel’s signature dish: chicken schnitzel with lemon paprika (left). “Maybe in 15 years that’ll be the next classic.”
