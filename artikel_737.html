Where to Watch the 2026 CMA Awards, Featuring Taylor Swift’s Return to the Stage

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

This marks 13 years since the singer-songwriter last performed at Country Music's Biggest Night.

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, The Hollywood Reporter may receive an affiliate commission.

The 2026 Country Music Association Awards, hosted for the third consecutive year by Lainey Wilson, celebrate the show’s milestone 60th ceremony and mark Taylor Swift’s first CMA performance in 13 years.

This year’s CMAs will air on Wednesday, Nov. 18 at 8 p.m. PT/ET on ABC, available to stream live via DirecTV (with a five-day free trial), YouTube TV (with a 10-day free trial), Fubo (with a five-day free trial), Sling and Hulu + Live TV.

The awards show will also stream at 5 p.m. PT/8 p.m. ET on Disney+, which doesn’t offer a free trial of its own, but is included in any of DirecTV’s Signature packages, which do offer the aforementioned free trial. The ceremony is preceded by a red carpet pre-show, airing live at 3 p.m. PT/6 p.m. ET, also on Disney+. At a Glance: How to Watch the 60th Annual Country Music Association Awards

The 60th Annual CMA Awards are set for Wednesday, Nov. 18 at 8 p.m. PT/ET on ABC.

Since the awards show airs on ABC, it will also be available to watch live on any streaming service that carries said network, including DirecTV (with a five-day free trial), YouTube TV (with a 10-day free trial), Fubo (also with a five-day free trial), Sling and Hulu + Live TV.

The ceremony will also stream at 5 p.m. PT/8 p.m. ET (without the tape delay) on Disney+, which doesn’t offer a free trial of its own, but is included in any of DirecTV’s Signature packages, which do offer the aforementioned free trial.

To claim the free trial, select your preferred Signature package. Once it prompts you to choose how many Gemini devices you’d like to connect, you’ll select “0 – Stream without the Gemini Air” as the five-day free trial is only available to customers without a leased Gemini device.

Learn more about each plan option, including how to build your own channel lineup (starting at just $20.99 per month), at directv.com.

Watch ABC with a subscription to YouTube TV, which includes a generous 10-day free trial period. Following the trial, there’s a limited-time offer that gives you five months for $67.99 per month. The subscription will then continue at the regular $82.99 per month.

After the trial, plans start at $54.99 for the first month and $64.99 monthly thereafter.

In select markets, ABC is included in Sling’s budget-friendly Select plan, from $19.99 per month; Sling’s Blue plan, from $45.99 per month; and Sling’s Orange & Blue plan, from $60.99 per month.
