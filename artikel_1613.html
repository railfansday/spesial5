Report: Liverpool take first step to sign new Florian Wirtz, they want him badly

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Liverpool have reportedly taken their first step towards signing the next Florian Wirtz. The German could already have a successor.

Florian Wirtz enjoyed a magnificent day against Manchester United on Sunday. The no.7 lit up Anfield with an incredible performance, creating 10 chances (five considered 'big') and scoring himself.

It was the Wirtz show and demonstrated why Liverpool bought him in the first place. Yes, it's a shame we've had to wait well over a year to actually see why but this has been a long time coming.

Wirtz has not played poorly. Things have rarely gone his way, though, and this dysfunctional Liverpool team hasn't made it easy for a player who relies on finding space and having runners beyond him.

When he gets that space and gets those runners, though, Wirtz tears teams apart. As he did against United - they had no idea how to cope with him and that was evident with 11, let alone 10.

But there's always been an extra problem with focusing the team around Wirtz - there's only one of him. Liverpool don't have another natural playmaker who can operate behind a striker but they need one if they're to go all-out on building around the position.

There are always wonderkids coming through in the Bundesliga who catch they eye. Few reached Wirtz's level, admittedly, but it's a fantastic league for young attacking talent to thrive in.

And that's caught Liverpool's eye, it seems. Christian Falk claims they've now got a 'concrete' interest in Wael Mohya, who is lighting the Bundesliga up for Borussia Mönchengladbach.

Jürgen Klopp actually had a look at Mohya for Germany in the latest international break - a massive show of faith in the 17-year-old, even if he didn't make his debut. But it feels as though it's just a matter of time before he's in that senior team.

And maybe in Liverpool's, too. They view Mohya as potential backup for Wirtz, as well as long-term competition for the player. They're not alone in their pursuit, mind, as Brighton would also like to make him their latest young gamble.

We imagine Mohya will cost a fortune for an 18-year-old next year, though it won't be in January. Gladbach are trying to tie him down to a new deal and refuse to sell in the winter window - so it'll be the summer at the earliest if Liverpool are to get ahold of him.

Bayer Leverkusen and Bayern Munich are both keen admirers as well, meaning the price here could actually get out of hand. But Liverpool are fans and certainly want a player in that position. They'll just be tested for how much they want that player to be Mohya.
