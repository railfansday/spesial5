Opinion: Liverpool reportedly in talks to sign the next Luis Suarez, he is phenomenal

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Liverpool are about to sign someone absolutely brilliant if their talks are successful.

El Pistorelo was his name. Liverpool fans simply could not get enough of him. Luis Suarez. The player who probably came an era too soon.

Imagine what he would have been in Jurgen Klopp's team. Playing alongside Roberto Firmino, Sadio Mane and Mohamed Salah.

Suarez would have been unstoppable then. Klopp would have been able to rein in his temper on the pitch too.

Even without that supporting cast, Suarez's brilliance shone during a very mediocre period for Liverpool. With teammates like Jonjo Shelvey and Charlie Adams, Suarez was still able to be one of the best players in the Premier League.

Without a question of a doubt in terms of quality, he is one of the best to have ever player for the club - even if he didn't win many accolades during his time with Liverpool.
