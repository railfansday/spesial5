Ellen White and Ben Haines are back at St. George’s park in the company of Em Sanders to look ahead to England's World Cup play-off final against Ukraine. Manchester United captain, Maya Le Tissier, chats to Ellen and Ben about navigating a difficult start to the season under new manager Eva Olid, learning from the unusual feeling of spending time on the bench during international breaks and the secret behind her consistency. A ‘hangry' Lauren Hemp pops by to chat Manchester City’s current form and her 2035 World Cup ambitions, but she may need to re-think how she’ll get there! One of the newest members of the Lionesses senior team, goalkeeper Katie Cox, also joins Ellen and Ben to discuss her first conversation with Sarina Wiegman, her current connections in the squad and the next generation starting to come through. Plus, all three Lionesses pay tribute to Lucy Bronze, who is expected to make her 150th cap in the second-leg against Ukraine, and ask whether it really is that wild to think she will be starting at the 2035 World Cup on home soil!
