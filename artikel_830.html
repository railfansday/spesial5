Failed to deal with Brobbey - player ratings for Sunderland v Chelsea

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Brobbey won the penalty in Sunderland's second league win of the season

Enzo Le Fee's penalty proved the difference as Sunderland climbed off the bottom of the table with a win over Chelsea.

Chelsea defender Levi Colwill brought down Brian Brobbey in the 36th minute, with Le Fee sending goalkeeper Mike Penders the wrong way from the spot.

Here is how I rated the players and how you rated them.
