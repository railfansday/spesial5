Mariah Carey, Nick Cannon's Twins Make Rare Comments About Siblings

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Nick Cannon as well as his and Mariah Carey’s kids Moroccan and Monroe revealed what led to his divorce from the “Honey” singer in the official trailer for Netflix’s Raising the Cannons.

Nick Cannon didn’t give his all during his and Mariah Carey’s 8-year marriage.

The Wild ‘n Out star recently revealed what led to his 2016 divorce from the Grammy winner in the trailer for his upcoming Netflix docuseries Raising the Cannons, out Dec. 18, revealing it was his own actions that ultimately led to the split.

As Nick put it in the trailer which was released Nov. 17, “My ego drove me away from Mariah.”

Meanwhile, the pair’s 15-year-old twins Moroccan and Monroe appeared to agree as they shared rare insight into their parents’ breakup, as well.

For Monroe, it’s her father’s blended family—Nick is dad to 10 other children with five separate women—that has kept the couple from rekindling their romance.

“If you didn't have all these kids,” she said during a car ride with Nick, “you would have been back together.”

Echoing his sister, Moroccan added in a later confessional, “This lifestyle, I don't think it's sustainable in any way.”

Nevertheless, Nick, 46, recently revealed that he and Mariah, 57, have the best dynamic yet nearly 10 years after their divorce.

“We talk almost every day or every other day, specifically just about the kids,” Nick told Us Weekly in an interview published in September. “We were laughing about [reports we’re not close]. I was like, ‘Who is telling people that?’”

After all, the Love Don’t Cost a Thing actor emphasized that his and Mariah’s split “wasn’t messy,” and that they "were able to move on and be the best parents we can.”

And while these days Nick engages in consensual nonmognaomy in his love life, he made it clear that wasn’t the case during his and Mariah’s marriage.

“I was married for seven-plus years, and my wife was the only person I was monogamous with because I didn’t want to f--k it up,” he admitted. “I was like, 'This is Mariah Carey. I’m not messing this up!’”

Still, Nick wouldn’t change his choice to expand his family.

“But I have an abundance of love, and I want to be the best provider and partner,” he added. “I have the means to—not even speaking financially—be a helpmate to people and I think that’s the root of it.”
