Nations Championship: France vs South Africa in final as England face NZ rematch

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

World Rugby's 12-team Nations Championship launched in 2026, uniting Six Nations sides, Rugby Championship teams, Fiji, Japan; tournament saw Tests in July, November before Finals Weekend at Twickenham Nov 27-29; France to face South Africa, England vs New Zealand, Ireland vs Australia

South Africa will face France in the inaugural Nations Championship final at Allianz Stadium Twickenham on Sunday November 29, despite suffering back-to-back defeats.

The last round of regular fixtures for the tournament saw South Africa suffer defeat to Ireland in Dublin, a week on from losing to France in Paris, but owing to New Zealand losing to England at Twickenham - and failing to pick up any bonus points - the Springboks still finished top of the southern hemisphere table.

France defeated Argentina with a bonus point to confirm their place at the top of the northern hemisphere table ahead of England and Ireland.

The Nations Championship finals take place at Allianz Stadium Twickenham over from November 27-29.

Wherever each northern hemisphere nation finishes in their table decides who they will face next weekend, setting up Tests with the corresponding finishers in the southern hemisphere.

As a result, England will face New Zealand in a rematch on Saturday, while Ireland will play Australia on Friday night.

Fourth-placed Scotland will face Argentina on Sunday, fifth-placed Wales play Japan on Saturday, and sixth-placed Italy will face Fiji on Friday, having just lost to them in Udine.

Final: France vs South Africa (4.40pm kick-off)

The Nations Championship is a joint venture between Six Nations and SANZAAR that pits the giants of northern and southern hemisphere rugby against each other on a more consistent basis.

The tournament is designed to align northern and southern hemisphere rugby calendars, guaranteeing high-profile fixtures to add more structure and meaning to the existing international windows between World Cups.

Each team will play six matches: three in July in the southern hemisphere and three in November in the northern hemisphere.

Twickenham's Allianz Stadium will then host Finals Weekend from November 27 to 29, where the inaugural champions will be crowned.

Rankings for Finals Weekend will be determined by results from the opening six matches, with each northern hemisphere team playing an additional fixture against the correspondingly ranked southern side.
