AirPods Pro 3 just got even cheaper, now $169 ($80 off)

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Apple’s AirPods Pro 3 have now dropped even lower for Black Friday, with a solid $80 discount to $169.99 shipped – within $1 of the best price ever. Amazon’s Black Friday sale officially started a few days ago, but the deals are heating up again this weekend with the first discount on Apple’s brand new OLED iPad mini, the 2026 AirPods Max 2 down again at $130 off, even deeper discounts on the current-generation Apple Watch SE 3 from $179, and now, an even lower price on the world’s greatest buds.

Amazon kicked off this year’s massive Black Friday event with a notable discount at $180 for AirPods Pro 3, down from the regular $249 list price. However, as we push deeper into the sale, it has now dropped the price again down to $169.99 shipped.

This is on par with the limited and short-lived summer Prime Day offer it featured back in late June – the only other time we have ever tracked AirPods Pro 3 down in a price range this low. We might see it knock another $1 off the total here before the giant November holiday shopping event is spent, but anything more than that would be unheard of.

It was unclear whether Amazon would bring back this particularly deep discount for Black Friday this year, but it has indeed arrived.

We are still awaiting a potential discount on the 2026 AirPods 5, but with all of that said, it’s not going to make much sense for most folks to opt for the less powerful AirPods 5 over the Pro set at this point – unless you specifically prefer the more open-ear experience, you can score the far more powerful AirPods Pro 3 for just $20 more than the Wireless Charging Case AirPods 5 config right now.
