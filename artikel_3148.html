iOS 27 has clever new Messages feature I’ve been using a lot

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

iOS 27 gives the Messages app a variety of welcome upgrades, including one I’ve found myself using a lot: a Smart Reply feature that’s now personalized to the user’s writing style.

When the first wave of Apple Intelligence features were announced in 2024, one that I immediately thought sounded useful was Smart Reply.

The idea behind Smart Reply is simple. Rather than needing to manually type out replies in Messages and Mail, your iPhone can offer prewritten replies you can choose and send with a couple taps.

Great in theory, but the ‘Smart’ part of Smart Reply struggled to materialize in iOS 18.

Fortunately, with a key Apple Intelligence upgrade in iOS 27, the new version of the feature finally lives up to its name. Per Apple:

Smart Reply in Mail and Messages can now draw on a user’s personalized writing style.

Before, Smart Reply would look at the context of a conversation to craft its suggestions. But now, it also aims to match your writing style, punctuation, and tone.

In iOS 18, I almost never used Smart Reply. That’s because despite being fine at suggesting replies that technically worked, those suggestions didn’t sound like me at all.

That’s changed in iOS 27. Smart Reply now consistently impresses me. It no longer offers generic responses that sound like AI wrote them. Instead, its suggestions actually fit with my normal style of communication.

The only drawback is that right now, you’ll only ever see two Smart Reply suggestions. If Apple expanded the feature by letting you scroll horizontally through more options, I suspect I’d use Smart Reply even more.
