Colman Domingo Responds to Post Telling Oscar Pundits Not to Add His ‘Michael’ Performance to Predictions: ‘Who Hurt You?’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Coleman Domingo is firing back at a critic of “Michael” and his performance as Joe Jackson, which some members of the film press forecast will earn him major nominations this awards season.

“Hot take: Awards prognosticators adding Colman Domingo for Michael, you really don’t need to add a mid performance/bad movie. You really don’t,” posted a user to X on Wednesday. In the musical biopic, released back in April, Domingo plays the domineering father of pop superstar Michael Jackson. His turn as the patriarch of the Jackson family earned him praise from critics when the film first hit theaters, so much so that several awards prognosticators, as the X user wrote, are predicting Oscar and Golden Globe nominations for the actor.

Ahead of the film’s premiere, the “Euphoria” actor told The Wall Street Journal that he focused on finding the father’s “humanity” in order to play him. “He created some of the most iconic artists that have ever walked this earth,” he added. “What goes into the making of that man?” Of the alleged abusiveness of the father, Domingo said: “I will say that he chastised his children. It’s always going to be arguable what’s right and a great method.”

Because of the allegations surrounding Michael Jackson’s alleged sexual abuse of children (which the executors of the Jackson estate, who produced the film, have maintained are false), the film was controversial even before it hit theaters. Speaking about the film with Variety in Nov. 2024, Coleman said that “anything surrounding those ideas about him never came into play with me… It’s about the character, more than anything. And the idea of working with the estate, Antoine Fuqua and Jaafar Jackson — who is exceptional.”

Alongside Domingo, the film stars Jackson’s nephew Jafaar Jackson as the titular pop star, Nia Long as his mother Katherine, Miles Teller as lawyer John Branca, Laura Harrier as Suzanne de Passe, Jamal R. Henderson as Jermaine Jackson, Tre Horton as Marlon Jackson, Rhyan Hill as Tito Jackson, Joseph David-Jones as Jackie Jackson and Jessica Sula as La Toya Jackson.

While the film holds the record for the highest-grossing biopic ever made, Variety exclusively reported that it was not submitted for the 2027 EE BAFTA Film Awards.
