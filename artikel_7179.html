Senior Implementation Engineer at Clutch

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Clutch helps credit unions launch digital lending and account opening. As a Senior Implementation Engineer, you're the product specialist who solves the hardest technical problems on those projects.

You specialize in one Clutch product and cross-train on a second. You join each project at key moments and whenever it needs something beyond the default setup. An Engagement Manager owns each project and the relationship with the credit union.

This role fits someone who can diagnose a complicated setup, explain it to a non-technical audience and decide when a request is worth building.

Diagnose complicated setups and find working answers through configuration, mapping, integration design and workarounds

Design and build non-standard configuration, core mapping and integrations

Decide whether a requested change is worth making. When it isn't, say no, explain why and offer an alternative

Own the testing strategy and lead live testing sessions with credit union teams

Advise our implementation team on what's possible, what needs a workaround and what's a true product gap. File clear tickets with our product and engineering teams for the gaps

Help write and improve our best-practice default setup, workbooks and test scripts with our Product and Engineering teams

Coach Engagement Managers to run standard setup themselves

Use AI to speed up your work, and standardize what works with the team

Work on many projects at once. Occasional onsite visits with customers.

Our Professional Services team implements Clutch for our credit union customers. Every project starts from a best-practice default, and we change it when the change improves the credit union's outcome. We test the setup with the credit union and take them live. Our top metric is customer success through renewal and expansion. We look for people with high agency, sound judgment and a strong technical foundation, who keep teammates informed.

Our company is growing quickly, and so is our team. We're looking for people who want to help shape a world-class delivery motion.

Learn our default setups, decision log and the core and vendor systems around your product

Join your first projects alongside a tenured Implementation Engineer

Complete onboarding and lead the technical design on several projects

Handle your first non-standard configurations and integrations on your own

Be the go-to person for your product area

Contribute to our default setups, workbooks and test scripts

Coach Engagement Managers on standard setup

5+ years of experience in implementation, solutions engineering or technical consulting, ideally in fintech or banking software

2+ years integrating with web APIs. You read and write code (Python, JSON, JavaScript or similar), work comfortably in GitHub and the terminal, and debug payloads yourself

A record of diagnosing complex setups and finding working answers without engineering work

Sound judgment on which changes are worth making, and the confidence to say no and explain why

A grasp of why a product works the way it does and what it does for a customer's business

A named result from using AI in your work, such as time saved or a faster diagnosis

Comfort with ambiguity. You build a plan, tracker or checklist when none exists

The ability to explain technical concepts clearly to non-technical audiences

You communicate proactively. You flag risks early and keep Engagement Managers and customers informed

The ability to keep many projects moving in the same week

Experience with core banking or credit union core systems, Alloy, Stripe, Plaid or ACH

This approach not only preserves but revitalizes the longstanding tradition of community and member-focused service inherent to credit unions.
