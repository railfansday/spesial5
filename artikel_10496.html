Could Meta fix its glasses privacy issues with an LED redesign?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Meta launched its own-branded smart glasses this summer (read: with no Ray-Ban or Oakley branding), and they seem to have done well for a number of reasons. Some models are much cheaper than Ray-Ban-branded pairs, while other Meta glasses carry Kylie Jenner or LISA names, bringing in a unique note of fashion, but none of these models changed the look of the camera LED indicator on the front.

Meta has come under fire over the past two years as people have abused the company’s smart glasses, giving them the unfortunate term “perv glasses” on social media because some people disabled the camera LED, which is supposed to illuminate when the camera is recording. Meta has issued several different software patches to disable the glasses’ camera when the LED is tampered with, but many have begun to feel like having a camera on smart glasses just feels wrong after people have abused the technology.

Meta glasses product lead David Woodland posted a render of the Gentle Monster cateye style glasses in a now-deleted Tweet on X on November 17, simply saying “I plead the fifth.” Presumably, he was calling out how similar these glasses look to the Meta Starfire Kylie Jenner style, but we’ll likely never know now.

Samsung and Google’s Warby Parker and Gentle Monster glasses certainly resemble Meta’s glasses, but they also do something noticeably different: the camera LED doesn’t look like the camera on the other side.

So this begs the question: can Meta do anything to salvage the unsavory association its glasses now have attached to them? We covered this topic on episode 125 of our Pixelated podcast, and Woodland’s post further highlights the question. A camera LED that’s obviously different from the camera on the other side would likely prompt people to ask about your glasses, while Meta’s design is purposefully stealthy because of its symmetry.

Does this design difference help Samsung’s Gentle Monster/Warby Parker glasses feel more private, and would a similar redesign help Meta’s glasses? I think it would help a lot, but I also want to know what our readers think. What say you, folks?
