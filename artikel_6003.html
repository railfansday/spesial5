Middle East Tourism Has Split. Which Demand Gets Capital?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Gulf travel now runs at two speeds. Pilgrimage and resort demand held through 2026, while long-haul and corporate travel fell away. Every 2027 capital and capacity plan for the region depends on which market an operator underwrites.

Accor’s Duncan O’Rourke told Skift that UAE city hotels cut rates 15% to 20% in August and September to protect occupancy, while Gulf resort and Holy Cities hotels ran ahead of last year. Then the split moved inside Saudi Arabia. After Houthi strikes on Riyadh’s airport in October, 86% of scheduled departures were canceled and Riyadh RevPAR fell 65% to $65 for the month.

For owners, lenders and investors, the 2027 question is which of those markets to underwrite, and on what recovery date.

Developers kept building. The Middle East hotel pipeline reached a record 771 projects and 188,960 rooms in the third quarter, according to Lodging Econometrics data. Saudi Arabia holds 401 projects, up 15%, and most owners are pushing ahead, though Diriyah canceled a $191 million hotel contract in November. Transactions moved the other way. Knight Frank told Skift that pre-war asking prices no longer match buyer underwriting, and investors are rerunning feasibility studies built on pre-conflict occupancy.

The terms behind that pipeline are changing too. Gulf sovereign funds now expect returns and are phasing projects and selling assets to meet them. Owners are reopening hotel management agreements with operators over performance tests and when management fees get paid. Colliers’ Nicolas Nasra expects the toughest negotiating environment in a decade.

Forecasts disagree by most of a year. Before the October strikes, Accor expected UAE hotels back to pre-war levels by late in the first quarter of 2027. S&P Global Ratings does not expect that before the end of 2027. Since then, Accor has said regional RevPAR may fall about 12% in the fourth quarter, the region’s peak season.

Fourth-quarter results arrive after most 2027 budgets lock in. Every underwriting call made this month carries a recovery date the data cannot yet confirm.
