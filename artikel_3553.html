Apple TV Officially Confirms Presumed Innocent Season 2 Release Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Apple TV's Presumed Innocent season 2 just got a release date.

The streaming service has confirmed the return of its riveting legal thriller with a new teaser trailer, alongside confirmation of when the series returns. Presumed Innocent season 2 is not a direct follow-up, instead sending the series into an anthology direction. The new season pulls from the pages of Jo Murray's 2026 novel Dissection of a Murder, featuring a new cast of characters and new case. The new episodes will star Rachel Brosnahan, Matthew Rhys, Jack Reynor, Fiona Shaw, Courtney B. Vance, and Lesley-Ann Brandt.

Now, Apple TV has released a new trailer for Presumed Innocent season 2, with Brosnahan and Rhys in the series' core roles. The trailer features Brosnahan, Rhys, Vance, Shaw, and Reynor taking the stand, all asked if they "swear to tell the truth, the whole truth and nothing but the truth, so help you God?" Tense sequences between various characters are intercut between as a crescendo of music plays, ending when Reynor's character says "I do." The trailer confirms the series will return on February 19, 2027.

While Presumed Innocent season 1 ended with a riveting twist that recontextualized the show's case, season 2 is taking a very different approach to its core subjects. The series focuses on Leila Reynolds (Brosnahan), a defense attorney, who goes head-to-head with her husband (Rhys), a district attorney who is leading the charge of a major murder case. Their opposing sides in the courtroom threaten the stability of their marriage, as well as the viability of their careers.

Other characters in Presumed Innocent season 2 that have recurring roles include John Magaro, Michael Hsu Rosen, Ji-young Yoo, and Judith Light. As for the series' premiere, the show's February 19, 2027 return will feature two episodes, before the series airs weekly until the finale on April 2. This makes season 2 eight episodes long, the same length as season 1. It also means weekly twists and turns as the case develops, and as friction grows between Brosnahan and Rhys' characters.

David E. Kelley returns as showrunner and executive producer, while J.J. Abrams also returns to executive produce and develop. Though not returning onscreen, Jake Gyllenhaal continues to serve as an executive producer as well. Others with executive producer credits include Brosnahan, Matthew Tinker, Rachel Rusch Rich, Dee Johnson, and Dustin Thomason. Murray and Scott Turow are co-executive producers, the latter of whom is the author of the original Presumed Innocent novel from which season 1 was adapted.

With the series' return just three months away, the teaser trailer for Presumed Innocent season 2 offers but a taste of what to expect from the show's evolution into an anthology series. Although it's unclear if there will be any tangential connections between both seasons, the main story is taking the show in its own direction, centering around a couple whose legal battle begins to impact everything else in their lives. The extent of the damage will be revealed once the show comes to Apple TV next year.
