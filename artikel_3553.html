Ruby Franke's Daughter Says Mom, Jodi Hildebrandt Inflicted ‘Lifetime of Hurt’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Ruby Franke’s eldest daughter, Shari Franke, shared a lengthy message with the parole board as her mother and Ruby’s business partner, Jodi Hildebrandt, prepare to attend parole hearings three years after they were arrested for child abuse.

“It’s been 4 years since I last talked face to face with Ruby. It was the most stressful experience of my life. She told me that through my speaking out against her publicly, I didn’t want to be part of the family,” Shari, 23, wrote in the email obtained by WHAM.

Shari explained that after she spoke out about her mom’s abuse, she “was cut off from all my siblings” and felt that “something terrible was happening” inside of her family’s home.

Nearly one year after Shari left her home in 2022, Ruby and Hilderbrandt’s abuse became known when the former influencer’s 12-year-old malnourished son escaped Hildebrandt’s home with duct tape and rope lacerations.

“I want them to know I fought for them. I called the police, I called CPS, I spoke to lawyers. And Ruby and Jodi evaded all of them,” she wrote.

Ruby, 44, and Hildebrandt, 57, were each sentenced to serve between four to 30 years in prison in February 2024 after they pleaded guilty to four counts of aggravated child abuse in connection to their treatment of Ruby’s children.

Both Ruby and Hilderbrandt will attend their first parole hearings on December 7. Despite being convicted of related child abuse charges, both Ruby and Hildebrandt’s cases will be reviewed separately.

Each hearing will determine when each woman could potentially be released from prison.

Shari is unable to attend their parole hearings, but she didn’t hold back while discussing her mother and Hilderbrandt’s futures in a lengthy email to the parole board, admitting that she’s struggled “with what forgiveness means over the last 4 years.”

“Maybe my definition is a little different than most. I will never accept Ruby’s apology for what she has done. She has been a monster her entire adult life, and there’s no way that’s changed in 3 years,” she stated. “She is not in prison for what she did to me or my brother Chad growing up, but she has never taken accountability for that.”

She continued, “I was forced to watch and listen to her beat my brother Chad mercilessly, I would slide him tissues under the door for the bloody noses she gave him, and we would often talk together about calling CPS on our own mother.”

Shari claimed that her mother believes “all her problems started and ended with Jodi.” However, Shari argued that Ruby was abusive towards her kids well before she met Jodi through their church community in 2019.

“Until she realizes that is not the case, and acknowledges she has issues that go way beyond Jodi, I don’t think she can fully heal,” Shari wrote of her mother.

“To me, forgiveness means I get to live my life in a way where I am not held back by Ruby. Every year, I am a little happier than the last. But I think I will forever have trauma around what Ruby has done to me and my siblings,” Shari continued. “Even after being in therapy for 3 years, I continue to have nightmares at least weekly of being trapped back in Ruby’s home, trying to save my siblings from her. But I try to live my life in a way where her voice in my head has no influence on the choices I make. That is forgiveness for me.”

Shari said that if her siblings had not eventually intervened, “Ruby would have killed them.”

“It was only after she learned Jodi had turned on her that she began to talk and apologize,” she added.

Shari then pointed out that Ruby “emptied all my siblings savings into a literal bag of cash to pay for her defense” and she referred to her children as “objects that obediently follow their leader.”

She concluded that Ruby should not be granted parole because “three years cannot fix a lifetime of hurt.”

“To Jodi, my feelings are more simple. She has repeatedly tried to take back her plea deal, or shorten her prison sentence. She is not sorry, and she is one of the most dangerous people I know,” Shari went on. “When I had to do counseling with her, she made me feel small and insignificant. She taught me to doubt myself, and judge others. She is a master manipulator who is good at reading social situations and can convey whatever she feels will build people’s trust in her.”

Shari also shared an update about her personal life, saying that the “family is happy” and they’re “happy without Ruby.”

“Even if she got out, I fear she would try to insert herself into our lives directly or adirectly, despite the tact she has a no contact order,” Shari stated. “I ask that the parole board deny parole.”

Shari’s email concluded with her stating that Hilderbrandt is a “dangerous woman who shows no signs of genuine remorse or change.”

“I would personally blame the state of Utah if she were released and did something like this again,” she wrote. “I ask that her parole be denied as well.”
