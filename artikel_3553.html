Zach Shallcross' Wife Kaity Biggar Leaves Wedding Rings on 'Bachelor' Cruise

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Kaity Biggar claimed she left her wedding rings behind on the Bachelor Nation Vacation at Sea cruise.

“So when is it a good time to tell my husband that I forgot my wedding rings on the cruise ship?” Biggar, 32, wrote over a Saturday, November 21, Instagram video. The clip, filmed by Biggar on the plane back home with Zach Shallcross, showed her looking remorseful in her seat while Shallcross, 30, slept two seats away from her.

“The feeling like you forgot something hit hard 😭🚢 💍,” Biggar captioned the video, which attracted a string of empathetic comments.

“Omg Kaityyyy,” wrote the official Bachelor Nation Instagram account while a fan responded to her question with, “Not until you find out that the ship staff have secured them for you.”

One fan mentioned that fellow Bachelor Nation star – and fellow Bachelor Nation Vacation at Sea cruise attendee – Kathy Swarts may have secured Biggar’s rings. “Oh girl!!! My heart hurt for you!! So glad I saw Kathy has them!!!” the fan commented before another fan checked in, “So did Kathy really find them? You gotta let us know, hopefully?”

Biggar and Shallcross met on 2023’s season 27 of The Bachelor, with the lead popping the question to his winner during the season finale. Two years later, the couple got re-engaged before secretly tying the knot in May 2025.

Six months after their quiet nuptials, the duo threw a wedding celebration in Austin, Texas.

In July, Biggar said in a TikTok post that the duo had put plenty of love into their wedding rings, holding off for “quite some time” as they got them resized to perfection. “They’re finally here!” she said in the clip, noting at the time that she hadn’t worn her engagement ring or wedding band in “four months.”

The video also saw Biggar provide detail into the rings’ creation, revealing that a thicker band was worn on her engagement ring because she didn’t like how “dainty it was.” Shallcross’ wedding band was also shown: a shiny and thick gold ring that his wife labeled “hot.”

Biggar added that her own wedding band featured a mix of “marquise and circular diamonds” set on gold.

Shallcross originally proposed on the reality TV show with an oval-cut Neil Lane ring, featuring a gallery set with 150 smaller diamonds. During his second proposal, he produced Biggar’s current rectangular silhouette.

“How lucky am I to get to say ‘yes’ to you twice,” she captioned photos of the follow-up moment, which were snapped during a sunset boat ride in Portugal.
