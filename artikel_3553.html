Kylie Jenner Sued by Another Housekeeper Who Was Allegedly Locked in Closet

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Kylie Jenner and members of her team are facing a lawsuit filed by a former housekeeper who alleges she was locked inside the star’s closet, bitten by a dog and struck in the eyes with pepper spray while on the job.

Court documents filed with the Superior Court of California on Thursday, November 5, and obtained by Us on Tuesday, November 10, state that Dinora Carolina Cabria Amaya is claiming that Jenner, 29, her staff supervisor Laura Dooley and other team members were responsible for her being locked inside Jenner’s closet “for more than one hour” before being “mocked” when asked to exit and experiencing a panic attack while kept inside.

The documents also detail Amaya’s claims that she was bitten by a dog on October 28, 2025, after being asked to clean the animal’s “doghouse.” She also detailed an alleged September 4, 2025 incident that involved her “cleaning and organizing a drawer in the laundry room [when] an unsecured canister of pepper spray discharged directly into” Amaya’s eyes and face.

Us has reached out to a representative for Jenner for comment.

Amaya, who states that she was paid $40 per hour from March 2025, is also alleging that she was wrongfully terminated on February 6 due to her “protected wage-related complaints” and “workplace treatment.”

In an additional claim, Amaya alleged that “she was constantly asked to set up a massage table for Jenner in the glam room or lift heavy 13-gallon trash bags to throw away.”

Amaya is seeking compensation from Jenner and her team for “lost earnings, lost employment benefits, emotional distress, humiliation, anxiety, and other damages according to proof,” with no numerical figure specified.

The filing is the fourth lawsuit lodged against Jenner and her team since April 17 when another former housekeeper Angelica Hernandez Vasquez claimed that she was harassed and discriminated against while working for the cosmetics queen.

The following month, Jenner was sued by a further part-time housekeeper, Juana Delgado Soto, who alleged that she experienced racial harassment and racial discrimination while employed by her team.

One month after that, a former private chef filed a lawsuit alleging pregnancy discrimination, wrongful termination and workplace strain.

Jenner has not publicly addressed any of the lawsuits, however her attorney Michael Rhodes told TMZ on Tuesday that each case is being filed by a single lawyer. “These latest cases are being brought by the same lawyer who previously filed claims on behalf of Kylie’s former household staff,” the statement read.“It’s clear that this lawyer wants to use publicity to try and extract an unwarranted payday.”

It concluded, “We’re not going to be intimidated into doing that. As such, we’ll defend these meritless cases vigorously and look forward to telling our side of the story.”

Amaya’s lawsuit includes 21 individual complaints that extend beyond her allegations of physical strain including failure to pay minimum wages, failure to pay overtime compensation, failure to provide meal periods, failure to authorize and permit rest periods and failure to pay all wages due upon termination.
