Jessica Alba Ordered to Pay Ex-Husband Cash Warren $577K After Divorce

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Jessica Alba has reportedly been ordered to pay her ex-husband, Cash Warren, a significant sum of money after their divorce was finalized.

According to court documents obtained by People on Monday, November 23,  Alba, 45, will pay Warren, 47, $577,481 under a profit-sharing plan involving her company, HonorBar Inc.

Warren can choose to receive the payment in full immediately, an individual retirement account or another retirement plan.

Court documents state Alba will not be required to pay the Pair of Thieves cofounder any additional benefits related to her company beyond the explicitly stated amount, which will be adjusted to reflect any investment gains or losses through the date of payment.

Us has reached out to Alba and Warren’s attorneys for additional comment.

The exes — who share Honor, 18, Haven, 15, and Hayes, 8 — split in January 2025 after 16 years of marriage. At the time, Alba said it was time for a “new chapter.”

“I’ve been on a journey of self-realization and transformation for years — both as an individual and in partnership with Cash,” Alba wrote via Instagram at the time. “I’m proud of how we’ve grown in our marriage over the last 20 years, and it’s now time for us to embark on a new chapter of growth and evolution as individuals.”

The pair were able to finalize their divorce in February and reportedly agreed to share joint custody of all three kids, with neither party paying any spousal support.

Court docs obtained by TMZ at the time also revealed Warren would receive $3 million to even out the division of the former couple’s assets.

“They are good parents and partners in business together but lost their spark and grew apart,” a source previously told Us. “Jessica and Cash made the decision together that this was no longer working or healthy for anyone. The kids are their priority right now, and they will be cordial while coparenting. It isn’t messy behind the scenes at all.”

Since their split, Alba – who is currently dating Danny Ramirez — and Warren have reunited on multiple occasions for their kids, including a sporting event and holidays such as Easter.
