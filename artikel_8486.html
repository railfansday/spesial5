Get the Wise Multi-Currency Card if you spend, hold, or get paid in other currencies. Spending a currency you hold costs nothing. For any other currency, Wise uses the mid-market rate, the one Google shows, and charges about 0.3% to convert dollars to euros or pounds. Skip it if you only travel abroad once or twice a year.

It’s a Visa debit card that spends from a Wise account, which holds money in 40 currencies and also sends money abroad. It suits freelancers paid by foreign clients, expats, and frequent travelers.

Freelancers paid in euros or pounds by local transfer receive the full amount and can spend it with no fee. An occasional traveler is better served by a credit card with no foreign transaction fee, which needs no money loaded before the trip.
