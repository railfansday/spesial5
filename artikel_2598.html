Preview: Warriors welcome Kings, Acuff Jr. to Chase Center

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Let's talk turkeys and trap games, Dub Nation. The night before Thanksgiving, the Sacramento Kings roll into Chase Center at 2-15, losers of nine of their last 10, with absolutely nothing to lose. That's the most dangerous guest you can have at the table. They're starving for wins!

The Warriors (9-8, 11th in the West) have won seven straight at home and come in as the betting favorites, so on paper this is a comfortable Cup Night. Paper doesn't box out, though. Golden State is 1-4 in the five games Stephen Curry has missed, and that one win came Sunday when they squeezed past Boston 88-87 to snap a five-game skid.

Sacramento also beat Boston by one on Saturday, so this is the battle of Pacific Division teams who have recently seen Jayson Tatum bow his head in shame.

Golden State grabbed 19 offensive rebounds against Boston in an ugly contest where every extra possession mattered. Brandin Podziemski led the way with 18 points, seven rebounds and four threes. Charles Bassey went 6-for-7 for 13 points with nine rebounds, three assists and three steals, and Gui Santos added 13. Yaxel Lendeborg chipped in 12 points and 8 boards. With Steph Curry, Jimmy Butler, the center post designated for Kristaps Porzingis, and Moses Moody in street clothes, this is the blueprint for now. We're talking winning ugly, physical, and with everybody eating.

The rookie duel between Big Yax (if he plays) and Sacramento's Darius Acuff Jr. could very well be the best part of the night. Acuff Jr., the No. 7 pick, is averaging 24.6 points and 7.2 assists on a team with two wins. Sacramento asks him to create a whole lot of offense every night for a team going nowhere fast.

The Warriors' No. 11 pick looks like the Rookie of the Year frontrunner to me. Lendeborg is putting up 19.2 points a night with 7.4 rebounds, and he's questionable with an illness. With Steph out and Lendeborg up in the air, Golden State might spend Wednesday trying to figure out how to replace the offense its own rookie creates.

Per the latest injury report, here's the Warriors' list:

Stephen Curry: OUT (right oblique tear; re-evaluation scheduled for Thanksgiving)

Moses Moody: OUT (left patellar tendon surgery)

Brandon Williams: QUESTIONABLE (left hamstring strain)

Wednesday will be Steph's sixth straight game in street clothes.
