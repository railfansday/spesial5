Burger King reveals $22 Magic: The Gathering meal with deck boxes and MTG card sleeves

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Burger King has teamed up with Magic: The Gathering for an unexpected collaboration, offering fans a special meal that comes with a deck box and protective sleeves for their trading cards.

The fast food giant is bringing the popular card game to its restaurants in Italy with a promotion called ‘Burger King Quest: The Great Gathering,’ which launched on November 10.

Rather than simply offering a themed burger, the collaboration includes official Magic: The Gathering merchandise that players can use to protect and store their cards.

According to Burger King’s official website, the promotional bundle costs €19.90 (around $22 USD) and includes a meal alongside two exclusive accessories.

Customers can choose between a Chicken Krispper, Double Cheese King, or Plant-Based Whopper, with medium fries and a drink included.

However, the real attraction for Magic fans is the merchandise.

Each bundle comes with a rigid deck box featuring Burger King and Magic: The Gathering branding, alongside 100 protective card sleeves featuring the same collaboration.

The accessories sport an orange and red design inspired by Burger King’s branding, with the deck box resembling a miniature fast food package.

Unfortunately for fans outside Italy, the promotion is currently exclusive to the country, with promotional materials advertising it until November 30.

And Burger King isn’t stopping at collectible accessories.

As reported by Engage, the chain is also hosting a national Magic: The Gathering tournament, with qualifying events taking place at nine restaurants across Italy.

The collaboration was first teased during Lucca Comics & Games in late October, where attendees received Burger King-branded Magic card sleeves.

It’s also the latest unusual food collaboration for the trading card game. Earlier in November, Magic: The Gathering launched 15 different flavors of beef sticks, with flavors inspired by the game’s five basic land types and ten Ravnica guilds.
