A very positive record: Luca Pairetto will officiate Milan’s game against Frosinone

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Luca Pairetto has been assigned to the game between AC Milan and Frosinone on Sunday evening, which is good news for the Rossoneri.

Ruben Amorim and his men will be looking to make it three consecutive wins against Frosinone, after beating Inter and Genoa before the international break. It might not be such an easy task, though, as admitted by the gaffer himself in his press conference.

Frosinone have indeed started the season well, currently sitting in sixth place, and they don’t really have anything to lose from their trip to San Siro. Only something to gain, which can always make things tricky for the favourite.

Given the above, as always, Milan will be hoping for good officiating to keep the away side from disrupting the play too much. The Italian Referees Association, also known as AIA, announced the referees for this weekend’s game a few days ago and for the Rossoneri, it will be Luca Pairetto.

The 42-year-old is definitely one of the more experienced referees around, even if he has never reached the heights of some of his colleagues. In any case, he has officiated 12 games involving Milan so far and the record is positive: 9 wins, 2 draws and 1 defeat.

It’s been a while since he last officiated a Milan game, though, as we have to go back to the 2024-25 season. The Rossoneri played Empoli and beat them 2-0, courtesy of goals from Rafael Leao and Santiago Gimenez.

Despite the win, the game wasn’t without controversy. A red card was not given to Empoli despite a reckless challenge, and Fikayo Tomori was then given a second yellow when the whole sequence should have been stopped due to offside.

However, most of those errors were made by VAR or the assistant referee and not Pairetto himself.
