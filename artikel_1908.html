‘Standards needed to be raised' – New Crawley Town defender gives 'brutally honest' assessment following Fleetwood win

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Experienced Heneghan came straight into Kevin Blackwell’s starting XI in the 2-1 win against Fleetwood Town on Saturday (November 21), playing as one of three centre-backs in a 3-5-2 formation.

And he repaid the new permanent manager’s faith with a dominant aerial display.

Following the game, Heneghan said: “Standards needed to be raised, I’ll be brutally honest, and as time goes on we’ll get those standards better and better.

“We’ve just got to keep working and sticking together. It’s a good group and the more we play together we’ll win some points.”

The 33 year-old’s performance was an example of driving the Reds’ standards forward from the back.

He won more than his fair share of headers and set the tone inside the first 10 minutes with a sliding block to stop a fierce Fleetwood strike from range in their first effort on goal.

Speaking about the importance of breaking their Broadfield Stadium hoodoo and earning a crucial first three points at home in the league, Heneghan said: “For the group it’s great, obviously they [Crawley] won against Oldham as well so it’s back-to-back wins in the league, which is great for confidence and morale and building on that really.”

Reflecting on his first league match in six months, the former Fleetwood player added: “Personally, it’s massive just to be back out there playing football and the relief of getting three points, so we’ve got to build on that.

“It’s the best, it’s all I’ve known since I started playing at the age of four. So, I want to continue for as long as possible until the body doesn’t work.”

Blackwell’s Reds remain unbeaten in his opening three games since taking over the reigns and singled out the performances of Heneghan, Pemi Aderoju and Danilo Orsi in Town’s first league victory since March this year.

Speaking in the post-match press conference, Blackwell said: “I thought Ben Heneghan came in, his first game for six months – I thought he did terrific. I thought [it was] maybe Pemi’s (Aderoju) best game since I’ve been here.

“[Danilo] Orsi – he’s worked so hard in training and I thought, today, he covered every blade of ground. I thought they all did well. It looked a very good unit today.

“But I want more. I need more, from certain aspects of the game and we’re hopefully trying to develop that now.”

Heneghan is only focused on the Reds’ future performances on the pitch and made it clear that the squad understands the manager’s individual and collective expectations.

He said: “What’s happened has happened and it’s gone now. The manager’s committed until the end of the season so that’s reassuring for everyone. Everyone knows their role and everyone knows what part they’re going to play.”

Crawley welcome second-placed Barnet to Broadfield on Tuesday night (November 24) and have the chance to climb out of the relegation zone with another win.
