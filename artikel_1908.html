Sources: Bayern Munich increasingly likely to sell Jamal Musiala amid Liverpool and Arsenal interest

Bayern Munich are increasingly likely to sell Liverpool and Arsenal target Jamal Musiala next summer, sources have told Football Insider.

The attacking midfielder has a release clause in his contract which comes into play at the end of the season, which could allow clubs elsewhere to swoop in.

Both Liverpool and Arsenal are long-term admirers of the 23-year-old, who is suffering from continued health issues at the moment.

Sources say that Musiala is occupying a "huge contract" in Bavaria, with Bayern "worried" about paying those lofty wages if he cannot get back to his best form.

Musiala is suffering from seizures caused by a neurological dysfunction and has been limited to just four appearances and one start in the Bundesliga so far this term.

Liverpool and Arsenal are both weighing up a potential move for him, but the youngster's health issues are set to come into consideration.

Musiala established himself as one of Europe's brightest young prospects when he first came through at Bayern, but his career has hit the buffers in the last 18 months.

A serious leg break limited him to just 15 league appearances last term, while his health issues are now causing major concern within his club.

Musiala returned to Bayern's matchday squad for the draw with FC Koln last weekend, but has only played 20 minutes of football since the middle of September.

Liverpool and Arsenal are both keeping a close eye on his progress at the moment, but he will need to prove his fitness for a move to happen next summer.

Musiala is a two-time German Player of the Year winner and also finished as the joint Golden Boot winner for Euro 2024.

His quality is undisputed by almost all of Europe's top clubs, but Liverpool and Arsenal have questions that they need answers for before they make a move.

Former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness recently told Football Insider that Liverpool are likely to lose out to Real Madrid in the race to sign Brighton goalkeeper Bart Verbruggen next summer.

Both clubs are searching for long-term solutions between the posts, and have identified the Dutchman as an ideal option.

However, Wyness expects Brighton to fuel an auction for the 24-year-old, and feels that their stance suits Real better than it does Liverpool.

The Merseysiders are clear admirers of Verbruggen, but they may ultimately have to look elsewhere to find their next number one.
