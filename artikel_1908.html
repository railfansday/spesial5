Xabi Alonso’s Chelsea sack prediction could come true as Leeds expose everything wrong with BlueCo project

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Xabi Alonso had been clear that he wanted his Chelsea team to match Leeds physically and mentally in the spirit of this historically feisty fixture.

What he got in the end, though, was yet more evidence that this young team are there for the taking by those who want to take the game to them.

Leeds were sharper and nastier in every aspect today, and while Chelsea took the lead through Emmanuel Emegha, it came as no surprise to see Daniel Farke’s men snap back to equalise before running away with the game with such ferocity that Alonso’s future will, for the first time, come under some scrutiny.

There were boos at the break with the score level, but Leeds firmly on top, and the shower of condemnation that followed the full-time whistle vocalised in the clearest terms that Alonso is under pressure.

Alonso’s Blues started the season ferociously and looked like they could challenge for major honours, but Chelsea supporters have been here before.

They were always going to need more than a few exhilarating attacking displays to nail their names to the mast of a title challenge, and so it has proved.

Chelsea are nowhere near runaway leaders Arsenal, and they are once again looking over their shoulders at sides who are less well-resourced but more tactically defined and mentally resolute.

Leeds dominated both boxes and were first to every ball, bullying Chelsea’s defence just as Sunderland and Brian Brobbey had before the international break.

Lessons have not been learned from last week, from last season or, frankly, any point of the BlueCo ownership. Chelsea as a team, while not short on quality, remain short of leaders.

They have looked to shoehorn young men into positions of responsibility, and this is the inevitable result. Teams with nous, experience and bite will continue to see Stamford Bridge as a challenge they can scale.

This was Leeds’ first win in this corner of west London since the turn of the millennium, the latest in a long list of teams to head to a formerly impenetrable Stamford Bridge and make history.

Chelsea rarely have answers for teams who seek to raise the temperature of the game. When the game becomes stretched, Chelsea become increasingly erratic and tempestuous.

It is in these moments that the typically composed Alonso must do more to change the tide. His changes this afternoon came too late and made no difference. Leeds were in control of the game and the scoreline when Alonso turned to his bench just past the hour mark.

During his introductory press conference over the summer, Alonso was reluctant to say he believed he would get time to instil his ideas. His pessimism appears well placed because, just 11 games into the season, the pressure is ramping up.
