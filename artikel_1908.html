Thomas Frank calls Arsenal cup gaffe while Tottenham manager a ‘fantastic story’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Thomas Frank hopes to not repeat the gaffe of drinking from a rival’s coffee cup after he was reminded of his infamous Tottenham faux pas during his unveiling as Crystal Palace manager.

Frank, who succeeded Pierre Sage as Palace manager this week, came under heavy scrutiny during his time in charge of Spurs after being pictured drinking from an Arsenal-branded coffee cup ahead of their 3-2 defeat to Bournemouth in January.

The incident was the last straw for many Spurs supporters, who had already become disillusioned with the Dane’s style of football, and he was promptly sacked a month later after failing to win any of his next five league games.

Though Frank appeared frustrated that an honest mistake had, to his mind, been blown out of proportion, the moment became a snapshot of his disastrous spell in north London.

Since leaving Spurs, Frank has taken some time out from the game, spending time with friends and family and travelling to Australia and Japan to recharge before agreeing to succeed Pierre Sage as Palace boss.

The time off has seemingly rejuvenated the former Brentford man, and when asked about the coffee cup incident, he appeared in good spirits as he joked that it was a “fantastic story” but one that he did not wish to repeat.

“I hope not,” Frank said when asked if there was a chance he could get caught drinking from a Brighton coffee cup, the Seagulls being one of Palace’s fiercest rivals.

“I still think it’s a fantastic story,” he continued.

“I know, and I still don’t understand, how anyone can think that I will drink from a cup from an opponent’s team, that their supporters don’t like, on purpose. Come on. But what a story, huh, to discuss?”

Frank endured a torrid time at Spurs, coming under fire for his pragmatic style of play and leaving the club precariously above the relegation zone before being belatedly sacked.

Reflecting on his spell in charge, Frank hinted there was more behind the story than had been revealed.

“If you don’t learn from any of your jobs, I don’t think you reflect, I don’t think you improve, and I don’t think you get better.

“Of course, there are a lot of things I have been reflecting over. The details, that will be for another time, but I can say I’ve learned, definitely.”
