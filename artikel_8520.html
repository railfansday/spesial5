Open a Lively HSA if you want a health savings account with no monthly fee that you can invest yourself at Schwab. Keep at least $3,000 in cash to invest with no Lively fee, or pay $24 a year, and expect that cash to earn almost nothing.

Lively charges no monthly fee to hold its HSA, while HealthEquity may charge a monthly fee that depends on your plan and can change when you leave your employer. It suits anyone whose health plan qualifies for an HSA, the account with a triple tax break, and who wants to open and invest one on their own.

Lively fits savers who pick their own investments and keep at least $3,000 in cash, since they pay Lively nothing, and those who mostly spend from the account. Savers who’d rather not keep $3,000 in cash or pay 0.50% for a managed portfolio do better with a Fidelity HSA, which has no account fees or minimums and no advisory fee on Fidelity Go balances under $25,000.
