Big Ten Championship scenarios: Oregon, UCLA, Indiana and more still in play for title game berth

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Big Ten Championship scenarios: Oregon, UCLA, Indiana and more still in play for title game berth appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Big Ten championship game got thrown into chaos this past weekend. UCLA and Indiana both had the inside track before this past weekend. UCLA fell to Michigan in triple overtime, while Indiana lost to Washington in overtime. The winner of the Big Ten championship game will be heading to the College Football Playoff, but six teams are still alive for the game.

UCLA still sits on top of the standings, at 10-1 on the year and 7-1 in conference play. The only loss of the year for UCLA was to Michigan this past weekend. They will host USC this upcoming weekend. UCLA has not played for a conference championship since 2012, when they lost the Pac-12 championship to Stanford. They have not won a conference title since 1998.

Indiana is currently second at 6-2 in conference play and 9-2 overall. The Hoosiers lost to Ohio State earlier this year, and then to Washington this past weekend. Indiana is the defending Big Ten champion after knocking off Ohio State last year. It is their only appearance in the Big Ten championship game.

Iowa is 9-2 on the year as well, and 6-2 in conference play. They have losses to both Ohio State and Minnesota this year. The Hawkeyes have played three times in the Big Ten Championship, losing in 2015, 2021, and 2023. Kirk Ferentz has been the head coach since 1999. He has two Big Ten titles, coming in 2002 and 2004, but both are shared titles. They last won the conference outright in 1985.

Oregon has rebounded from a horrible start to the year. They are 8-3 on the season, but also 6-2 in conference play. Oregon has won four straight games after losing to UCLA and Illinois earlier this year. They took the Big Ten title in 2024, defeating Penn State in the conference title game.

USC has not won a conference title since winning the Pac-12 in 2017. Lincoln Riley is looking for his first conference title with USC, and has a chance. USC is 8-3 this year and 5-3 in conference play. Still, they have losses to Oregon, Wisconsin, and Indiana.

Ohio State is also still alive in the conference championship race. The Buckeyes are 7-4 and 5-3 in conference play. They have lost three of four games, falling to USC, Oregon, and Nebraska. They played for the conference title last year, but have not won the Big Ten since 2020.

UCLA is the most likely team to make it to the conference title game. For UCLA, it is a win-and-in situation. They will face USC this weekend. With a win, they will go to the Big Ten Championship. Still, there are multiple scenarios where they get in with a loss, but all of them take some help. If Indiana loses, they get in with an Iowa or Oregon loss. They also get in with both Iowa and Oregon losing, or both Iowa and Oregon winning and Indiana losing. The most complicated situation comes if UCLA loses, and Indiana, Illinois, Iowa, Maryland, Michigan, Michigan State, and Wisconsin all win, while Oregon loses. Then it will come down to SportSource Analytics rankings.

Indiana now is in need of help to make it to the Big Ten Championship. They first need to win this week against Purdue. They cannot make the conference title game with a loss. If they win, and both Iowa and Oregon win, they are in. They also get in with a win and an Oregon and UCLA loss, or an Iowa and UCLA loss. The last situation is Indiana wins, UCLA loses, and Iowa, Minnesota, and Oregon all win.

Iowa would need a win over Nebraska this weekend to have any chance of making the Big Ten Championship game. They would also need some help. The easiest scenario is both Indiana and Oregon lose. They also get in with an Oregon win plus Indiana and UCLA losing. They can move on with Indiana, Oregon, and Wisconsin all winning, but that would need an UCLA loss. Chances are slim for Iowa, but they do exist.

Oregon likely has the best chance to make the title game. They can get in with a win over Oregon and UCLA win over USC, matching the two together. They also get in if both Indiana and Iowa lose. Oregon can get in with a loss, but ned a lot of help. They would need an Ohio State win, plus other results, or a Wisconsin win, plus Indiana, Iowa, Ohio State, and USC all losing. Still, this is a likely win and in a position for the Ducks.

USC is almost the lowest chance to get into the conference title game. They have to beat UCLA this weekend, but also need help. They get in if Indiana, Iowa, Ohio State, and Oregon all win with a Wisconsin loss. The Trojans also move on if Indiana, Iowa, and Oregon all lose, plus either Wisconsin wins and Ohio State loses, or Ohio State wins and Wisconsin loses. If both Ohio State and Wisconsin win, they then need Indiana, Iowa, and Oregon to all lose.

Ohio State has the slimmest chance to make it to the conference title game. They have just one path. Not only does Ohio State need to beat Michigan, but they also need Maryland, Rutgers, and Wisconsin to win, while Indiana, Iowa, Oregon, and USC all to lose. They will then face UCLA in the conference title game.

With so much at stake this weekend, every game on the Big Ten schedule could play a role in the title race.

Friday: Nebraska at Iowa, Minnesota at Wisconsin.

Saturday: Michigan at Ohio State, Illinois at Northwestern, USC at UCLA, Penn State at Maryland, Michigan State at Rutgers, Purdue at Indiana, Washington at Oregon.
