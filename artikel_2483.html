Bayern boss confirms interest in Mohya

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Bayern Munich board member for sport Max Eberl has confirmed that the German champions are monitoring Borussia Mönchengladbach prodigy Wael Mohya.

However, he played down reports that Bayern have already made an initial approach to the youngster’s representatives.

“In general, FC Bayern keeps an eye on every young German talent. We are monitoring the boy,” Eberl told Sky on Saturday.

“However, we have not yet spoken with the player, his agency or Borussia Mönchengladbach.”

Bayern are among several clubs keeping tabs on Mohya, who received his first senior Germany call-up earlier this week.
