Biden admin weighed quitting Twitter after Elon Musk purchase, new HHS doc drop reveals

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. and Medicare Chief Dr. Mehmet Oz announce the first 15 organizations receiving federal funding to combat chronic disease through nutrition and lifestyle care.

Amid a push to bolster transparency, the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) launched a searchable trove of public records, including previously unreleased memos from the Biden administration and Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr.’s calendars.

While the website will be updated over time, the initial drop includes a Biden-era memo detailing how the National Institutes of Health (NIH) considered no longer having a presence on Twitter after billionaire Elon Musk acquired the platform in October 2022, renamed it X and promised to scale back moderation amid free expression concerns.

Also included within the department's so-called Radical Transparency dashboard are Kennedy’s calendars, which reporters have previously requested and have not been publicly disclosed before now.

"When President Trump asked me to lead HHS, I promised to bring transparency back to the American health agencies," Kennedy said in a video announcing the launch of the website. "If the government makes decisions that affect your health, you deserve to see the evidence behind them. Not simply to be told, ‘trust the science.’"

Kennedy claims that HHS’ previous method of presenting public records was disorganized and confusing to use. He said public accountability would be aided by consolidating the documents in one simplified dashboard.

The Cabinet secretary noted that his department plans to upload more documents to the website during his tenure and the first drop includes previously available documents that are now easier to search and locate.

Though many on the political right are likely to hail the move as HHS’ increasing transparency efforts, Democrats have criticized the agency for lacking transparency under Kennedy's leadership.

Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. speaks during an announcement at HHS headquarters in Washington, D.C., on July 21, 2026. (Tierney L. Cross/Bloomberg via Getty Images)

Congressional Democrats, for instance, have repeatedly said Kennedy stonewalls their oversight inquiries and claim that HHS, under his leadership, has provided incomplete responses to their questions. A letter sent by 18 Democratic lawmakers on Nov. 19, 2026, called out "an alarming lack of public transparency" following a delay to the disbursement of rural healthcare grants.

In early 2025, HHS reduced staff working on Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests and public records at the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) and Food and Drug Administration (FDA) — another move Democrats claim stifles or at least slows transparency.

Previous releases of public records by the Trump administration have enabled journalists to uncover millions of dollars in welfare fraud.

President Donald Trump has charged Vice President JD Vance, seen by some as his heir apparent, with leading the White House charge against fraud, signaling the high importance of the issue to the administration.

In addition to public records releases, HHS has also pushed regulations requiring that healthcare companies make more information public about their pricing practices.

While the administration focuses on fraud, concerns about corruption within its ranks have taken root with voters amid press reports regarding conflicts of interest and alleged self-dealing. A Fox News survey found that 60% of Americans were concerned about government corruption ahead of the recent midterm elections.

HHS did not respond to a request for comment when reached by Fox News Digital on Tuesday.
