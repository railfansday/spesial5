Here are some tips for beating one of Phantom Blade Zero's most challenging bosses, the Blizzard Blademaster

The Phantom Blade Zero Blizzard Blademaster is arguably one of the hardest bosses in the entire game, thanks to his lightning-fast attacks and ice powers. He can freeze you solid, which is extremely irritating as there are very few ways to counter it other than simply not getting hit by any of the Blademaster's attacks – just be glad this is a one-phase fight! But don't despair as he can be beaten with a bit of guidance and the right equipment, so here's what you need to do to defeat the Blizzard Blademaster.

Blizzard Blademaster is an optional boss found at the end of the Four Guardians' Mirror area in the frozen part of Meigu Lake – bear in mind you can only get here if you've opened the Phantom Blade Zero Crimson Manor!

Despite being optional and rather unforgiving with his powerful freezing strikes and dashes, I still highly recommend finding and defeating this boss, both for story purposes and because you get his excellent ice sword as a reward.

Make sure your weapons are fully upgraded (I used the Baishan Stormswords, Armybreaker, Bashpole, and Ink Cut in case that helps) and that you've got the best Phantom Blade Zero Accessories equipped. Otherwise, this is what you should do to beat him:

Persevere and you'll eventually overcome Blizzard Blademaster, getting his fantastic two-handed sword Whiteshadow that lets you freeze your enemies too. Hopefully you also collected every clothing item in the Four Guardians' Mirror area to get the Boy's Worn Outfit, letting you immediately Soul Temper Whiteshadow too.

I won't spoil exactly what happens next, but you also finally get to use the Yin and Yang crystal eyes which finally leads to the end of the 'Secrets at the Heart of the Lake' side quest, which provides some additional story context and gives you a Soul Tempering item for one of your Phantom Edges.

As an ice-cold boss, you could try the Phantom Blade Zero Flaming Mane weapon. If you're still struggling with this boss, make sure you find as many Long Pillars as you can to fight the Phantom Blade Zero Gold-Masked One for practice.
