Team Cherry Announces A Silksong Presentation Showing Off Sea Of Sorrow

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

While the long-awaited release of Hollow Knight: Silksong received glowing reviews and record-breaking player counts at launch, the success has only added even more excitement over when the officially announced DLC would be released. While fans have had nothing but speculation to go off of since the Sea of Sorrow expansion was teased last year, it seems to be coming sooner than expected thanks to its developer's latest reveal.

Shared on the official Team Cherry YouTube channel, Silksong's developers have scheduled a stream premiering on November 27 at 7:00 AM PST, which promises to provide "A special presentation with a bunch of exciting announcements, and an update on Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea of Sorrow!" as noted by Wario64 on X, who helped share the news.
