Xbox Snags PS5 Exclusive 'Nioh 3' For January 2027 Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Team NINJA's bloody samurai RPG features Xbox Play Anywhere

Xbox is bagging up former PlayStation exclusives like crazy, with Nioh 3 Complete Edition making its way to Xbox Series X|S on January 28, 2027.

Nioh follows Helldivers 2, the Final Fantasy VII Remake trilogy and PHYSINT by hopping aboard Xbox hardware. It's lovely to see renowned franchises like the dark samurai souls-like Nioh arrive on Xbox. It certainly beats seeing Halo and Gears launch on PS5 without anything major coming the other way.

Nioh 3 Complete Edition is an action RPG, in which players don the role of shogun Takechiyo, facing off against punishing soulslike scraps and challenges. Team NINJA shared a press release confirming the game is headed to Nintendo Switch 2 and Xbox on January 28, 2027, complete with Xbox Play Anywhere support.

Nioh 3's second DLC, Bloody Insurrection, is set for December 17, 2026 for PS5 and Steam. The DLC will be included in the Complete Edition when it arrives early next year. As a franchise, Nioh has consistently wooed players with it's punishing combat and flashy action, with the third game holding an 86 on Metacritic.

Exciting times, eh? I wonder if this means Xbox players might gain access to Nioh 1 or 2 ports at some point in the future. Personally, I've always heard good things about the Nioh games, so it would be great to experience the entire series on Xbox, should Koei Tecmo make them available.

We'll leave you with an official description for the game along with some screenshots to look over:

Takechiyo must traverse time to change their fate and rescue a Japan overridden with yokai. Explore rich open fields and switch between Samurai and Ninja Styles instantaneously in battle. Encounter figures from history, and experience the tense battles characteristic of the Nioh series.
