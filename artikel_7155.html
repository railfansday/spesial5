ChiroCat: Humbly Confident Marketing Lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

ChiroCat builds cloud-based software that chiropractic practices across the United States love and trust. From digital intake forms to insurance billing and post-visit communication, we support doctors and their staff at every step, so they can spend less time on paperwork and more time caring for patients. Our mission is simple: empowering caregivers to transform lives through tools they trust.

We’re a small, privately owned, and stable company based in southern Utah, with most of our team working remotely across the U.S. We work real 40-hour weeks, take real vacations, and care deeply about our customers and each other. We’re humbly confident, we get stuff done, and we love to help people who help people.

We're ChiroCat, and we build cloud-based chiropractic software that practices across the United States love and trust. From the first patient interactions with digital intake forms to the final insurance processing and post-visit communication, our system supports doctors and their staff every step of the way. We’re modernizing the chiropractic software space in big ways, but much more importantly, we’re bettering the lives of our doctors, their team members, and especially their patients.

The bottom line: we LOVE to help people who help people. And that’s where you come in!

We’re building something special at ChiroCat; both with our product and the people behind it. We’re not like “the other guys,” and our customers know it. They tell us, and more and more, they’re telling their peers about ChiroCat, too. But truth be told, we haven’t done a great job of showcasing what sets us apart to the rest of the world. And now we want everyone to feel that from the first time they come across us.

That’s where you come in! We’re hiring our first dedicated marketer. If you’re the right fit, you know that a brand is much more than a logo, voice is more than just words, and a website isn’t just a funnel. You’re a skilled writer and will help us find our voice and strengthen it. You’ve been around the block and know how to build a strategy toward a better future, but you’re not satisfied to sit behind reports and dashboards. You’re excited to roll up your sleeves and start doing the work yourself. Website, ads, social, email, internal guidelines, in-app messaging: you’ll touch all of it. And as we grow, your opportunity grows with us.

Have at least 4 years of hands-on marketing experience, including real ownership of brand, messaging, and content (not just executing someone else’s plan)

Be an exceptional writer who can adapt to a brand’s voice, and have samples to show us

Have hands-on experience running paid social campaigns: building them, testing creative, and improving results based on what the numbers tell you

Have experience managing a company’s social media presence, including engaging with the community, and not just publishing posts

Be comfortable writing and updating website and landing page content in a CMS (Currently Webflow)

Be self-directed: you can take an ambiguous list of needs, decide what matters most, and make a case for it; continuously iterating your way toward excellence

Be a thoughtful communicator in written and spoken forms. It’s an art you enjoy improving!

Love to learn. In short order, you’ll need to get fluent in the world of chiropractic, medical practices, and the people who run them.

Marketing experience in healthcare, medical practice, or B2B SaaS

If this sounds like your ideal environment, read on because now we’ll dig into the role itself.

At ChiroCat, marketing isn’t about being the loudest voice in the room. It’s about being the most trustworthy one. Chiropractors get plenty of noise from software vendors: big promises, long contracts, and slick ads for products that feel twenty years old. We want people to meet the real ChiroCat, the same one our customers already know. Everything we put into the world should sound like us, look like us, and be true.

Your early focus will be brand: making sure that whatever someone sees of ChiroCat, whether it’s our website, our ads, our social posts, our reviews, or the messages inside our app, gives them an accurate (and awesome) picture of who we are. Next up is widening the funnel and building our inbound lead flow. Down the road, perhaps you’ll build a marketing team, one amazing hire at a time, but let’s not get ahead of ourselves.

This role reports to the CEO and works closely with our Head of Customer Success, our CTO, and our Sales team.
