XRP is approaching a critical weekly support zone, but the latest XRP price prediction comes with a serious complication. The chart points to a potential recovery toward $5, while exchange inflows show billions of tokens continuing to reach trading platforms. Buyers have a level to defend. Sellers have plenty of potential supply to watch.

The weekly chart shows XRP trading around the $0.91 area, where a green support zone meets a long-term rising trendline. The price structure has narrowed as descending resistance presses toward that same area.

A successful bounce could put several resistance levels back into play. The first targets sit at $1.40 and $1.90, followed by $3.40. The chart’s most ambitious target is $5.

But these are conditional levels, not guaranteed destinations. XRP needs to hold the current support area and establish a convincing reversal before those targets become relevant.

On-chain data shows around 4.61 billion XRP flowed into exchanges over the past 30 days. Binance received around 1.221 billion XRP, followed by OKX at 866.51 million and Bybit at 662.57 million.

Upbit recorded approximately 176.96 million XRP in inflows, while Bitget received around 140.02 million.

The total is still significant, but it’s below October’s near-6-billion-XRP reading. That suggests incoming transfer activity has slowed from the previous month.

Still, exchange inflows aren’t proof of selling. Tokens can move for liquidity management or wallet redistribution, too.

The next move depends on whether XRP can defend the $0.91 support zone while exchange supply dynamics improve. Falling inflows could ease concerns, but outflows, net flows and exchange reserves also matter.

For now, the XRP price prediction remains cautiously conditional: $1.40, $1.90, $3.40 and $5 are potential recovery targets, provided support holds and buyers return.
