West Ham, Millwall and Norwich City among clubs interested in League Two loan star

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Clubs up and down the country will be preparing for the January transfer window, and among those likely to be active are Championship trio West Ham, Millwall and Norwich City. It has been said that WHUFC boss Scott Parker is prioritising a new striker and a left-back, but TEAMtalk states the club have eyes on centre-back Jack Taylor.

It is reported that the Hammers are alongside the Canaries and arch-rivals Millwall in tracking Taylor, who has thrived on loan at Exeter City from Stevenage this season. A host of clubs scouted the 21-year-old in action against Huddersfield Town earlier this month – a game in which he scored.

Taylor has Premier League admirers too though, with Brighton & Hove Albion, Everton, Brentford, Crystal Palace and Fulham taking an interest. Clubs are considering winter moves with the possibility of keeping him on loan at Exeter City for the remainder of the season.

Taylor, born in Bexhill-on-Sea in 2005, has risen through non-league to the EFL. He started out in the Eastbourne Borough setup before moving to Sutton United, and a string of loans in the lower leagues ultimately earned him a first-team spot at Gander Green Lane.

Stevenage came calling in January 2026, but limited opportunities prompted his summer loan to Exeter City. The 21-year-old has had to wait for his chance, but Taylor has now started the last eight League Two games on the trot. The Grecians have won five of those and kept five clean sheets in the process.

Taylor’s per 90 stats show just how effective he has been since breaking into Matt Taylor’s side. And, it is little wonder that sides like West Ham, Millwall, Norwich City and Premier League clubs are taking note.

No League Two centre-back betters Taylor’s 17.27 defensive contributions per 90, and he ranks top for his 79.2% duel win rate as well. He also averages 6.24 aerial duel wins, 11.51 clearances and 2.69 tackles per game (all stats via FotMob).

It isn’t often that young players rise through non-league and reach high levels anymore, such is the level of competition throughout the English pyramid. So, for Taylor to emerge as he has and draw interest from top clubs speaks volumes about his potential.

However, given his steep trajectory and age, admirers like West Ham, Millwall and Norwich City will have to be patient with his development. Taylor could yet benefit from seeing out a full season in League Two or playing some League One football before moving to the Championship – or even the Premier League.

Unfortunately for Exeter City, they are passengers when it comes to a decision over Taylor’s future. The call will be Stevenage’s to make, and they may well be inclined to cash in if the opportunity arises amid interest from WHUFC and more.
