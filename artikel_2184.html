Sheffield United summer signing scores in 4-0 win on international captaincy debut

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Bangladesh ran out as 4-0 winners over Sri Lanka to progress to the semi-finals of the SAFF Championship, where they will lock horns with Pakistan. After Shamit Shome broke the deadlock inside nine minutes, Sheffield United man Hamza Choudhury was on hand to double the lead.

The SUFC man ventured forward with a darting run in behind the defence before emphatically slotting home.

Further goals from Boreham Wood’s Farhaan Ali Wahid – currently on loan from Fulham – and Rakib Hossain put the finishing touches on a convincing victory in the second half. It made it back-to-back victories for Bangladesh for the first time since 2023.

A former England U21s international, Choudhury stepped in to lead the team for the first time in the absence of long-serving captain Jamal Bhuyan.

Ex-Leicester City and Watford man Choudhury changed allegiance from England to Bangladesh in December of 2024, a move which is beginning to pay dividends.

His debut came in March last year against India, before netting his first goal in a 2-0 win over Bhutan just over two months later. Choudhury now adds another feather to his international cap by captaining his country for the first time.

Switching allegiance to Bangladesh marked a significant turning point for the South Asian nation, with Choudhury becoming their first player to play in the Premier League. He has been hailed as the figurehead to drive forward a country not renowned for its football exploits.

After a damaging 9-2 defeat to Indonesia, which saw them crash out of the FIFA ASEAN Cup in the last international break, Choudhury’s side have bounced back in perfect fashion and he has been at the heart of that.

Deployed in both his conventional midfield role as well as at right-back, the former Burton Albion loanee will be pivotal if they are to end their 23-year wait for an international title in the SAFF Championship.

While his form on the international stage has gone to another level, Choudhury has lacked consistency at Sheffield United this term.

The 29-year-old put pen to paper on a permanent deal at Bramall Lane following a successful loan stint and has been a mainstay at right-back after Femi Seriki was ruled out for the season with a patellar tendon rupture.

He produced impressive displays in their three-game unbeaten run, victories over Preston North End and Wrexham and a draw with Derby County, but he was taken off at half-time in their latest defeat to Charlton Athletic.

Given Seriki’s absence, Choudhury will be keen to regain his best form and will need to do so if SUFC are to enter the Championship play-off conversation, with his recent exploits for Bangladesh potentially providing the confidence to reignite his season.
