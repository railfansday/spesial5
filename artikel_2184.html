West Ham need to sign Newcastle United man in January to fix problem position: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

West Ham’s new boss Scott Parker will be looking to put his own stamp on his newly inherited squad with some reinforcements this winter. WHUFC have slipped out of the Championship play-offs after 15 fixtures as they eye an immediate promotion back to the Premier League.

The Hammers need to bring in a new goalkeeper amid Mads Hermansen‘s struggles. They should target a move for Newcastle United stopper Nick Pope, who is facing an uncertain long-term future in the North East and is likely to move on for a new challenge soon.

Pope, 34, is a vastly experienced player and would fit the bill for Parker’s side if they managed to lure him down to the London Stadium. He has been on the books of his current club since joining them back in the summer of 2022 and he has since made 131 appearances in all competitions, 36 of which came last season.

However, the veteran, who has made 10 caps for England in the past, has slipped down the pecking order at Newcastle United over recent times behind Lukas Hornicek. Therefore, he is likely to be available.

Pope would be a safe pair of hands between the sticks for West Ham and has played just under 450 games in his career to date. He is someone who WHUFC could trust and he has top flight pedigree.

He is out of contract at the end of this campaign. Newcastle United risk losing him for free if they don’t sell him in the next transfer window meaning the Hammers could try and get him for a cut-price fee.

The Cambridgeshire-born man played for Charlton Athletic from 2011 to 2016 before Burnley came calling. He then went on to make 155 outings for the Clarets during his time at Turf Moor and was their first choice.

Pope hasn’t played in the second tier for 10 years now but may have to drop down a division now to get first-team football. West Ham would be the perfect destination for him and he would be guaranteed regular game time there under Parker.

West Ham have conceded too many goals this term and a new ‘keeper needs to be a priority. Hermansen has become a bit of a liability for WHUFC, whilst Alphonse Areola is out of favour.

If the price is right, it wouldn’t be a surprise to see Newcastle United allow Pope to head out the exit door. A change of scene would do him good and a switch to the Hammers would suit all parties involved. He was linked with Hull City and Ipswich Town earlier this year.

Parker‘s men lost 2-1 away at Blackburn Rovers last time out. They are back in action next weekend at home to Preston North End.
