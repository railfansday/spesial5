Celtic star handed 'ideal springboard' as sensational transfer return route emerges

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Carl Ikeme reckons Sam Johnstone has landed the "ideal springboard" at Celtic to revive his career, with the former Wolves goalkeeper insisting his old club can also benefit.

Johnstone was pitched straight into the heat of battle at Ibrox after his arrival, before briefly coming back out of the side. But the 33-year-old has since established himself as Celtic's clear first choice between the sticks and is beginning to put together the regular run of football he was craving after finding opportunities limited at Wolves.

And Ikeme believes playing every week at Celtic can help the former England international rediscover his best form, putting him in a position to either secure a permanent switch or return to Wolves ready to fight for the No.1 jersey.

Ikeme was speaking as part of the Midnite Express, providing luxury coach travel for 100 Wolverhampton Wanderers supporters' and said: "It simply didn't happen for him at Wolves, largely because José Sá kept the shirt and the club went through a turbulent period last season. Sam was in and out, but he’s a top goalkeeper who needs to be playing week in, week out.

"It makes no sense to have both Sam and José sitting in the same squad, especially with John Ruddy returning to provide veteran cover.

"Going to Celtic is a fantastic move. It’s a massive football club where he’ll play every week and rebuild his confidence.

"Whether he uses it to secure a permanent move or returns to challenge for the number one shirt if Wolves go up, it’s a win-win. Goalkeeping is unique: you can find yourself out of favour very quickly through no fault of your own. The crucial thing is that he’s playing regularly again.

"It's an ideal springboard. You step outside the intense Premier League goldfish bowl, even though playing for Celtic or Rangers brings immense pressure of its own.

"It allows experienced keepers to get regular minutes, find their rhythm, and compete for trophies. It’s worked brilliantly for several top keepers, and it will do the same for Sam."
