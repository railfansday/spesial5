Celtic's tactics under Martin O'Neill torn apart as ‘biggest pet hate’ laid bare

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Celtic’s tactical problems have come under the microscope after former midfielder Simon Ferry questioned the instructions being given to Martin O’Neill’s players.

The Premiership leaders fell to a 2-0 defeat at Falkirk on Sunday, with Ferry identifying three recurring mistakes he believes are hurting Celtic when they attempt to build attacks from the back.

Speaking on his Open Goal podcast, Ferry picked apart Celtic’s possession play and admitted he “couldn’t believe” what he was watching during the defeat.

The East Kilbride coach said: “When you talk about possession-based football, these are the three passes I hate most when playing out from the back, and Celtic play them more than any team I’ve ever seen.

“Liam Scales to Kieran Tierney with a winger a yard away from him is a pass they play constantly. Don’t know why they keep playing it. Nothing comes of it.

“The next pass, how many times does Haissem Hassan get a pass straight to his feet from Donovan with a full-back right up behind him? Constantly.

“And then the third, and my biggest pet hate, is when you’ve got an eight-versus-six overload in your half of the pitch because your front three have pinned their back four and you constantly pass the ball into your centre-forward’s feet with a man at his back.

“You’ve got two spare men somewhere else on the pitch; you should never need to play that pass.

“Celtic do those three passes continuously.”

Ferry believes the frequency of those mistakes raises questions over the tactical instructions being given to the players.

He added: “They do it in every game I watch. Why have they not looked at it and said to stop doing it? That’s when you think: is there any instruction here?

“I couldn’t believe what I was watching.”

The latest criticism comes as debate continues over where responsibility lies for Celtic’s recent problems, with the board, players and coaching staff all coming under scrutiny.

O’Neill’s side drew another blank at Falkirk, with Kasper Høgh starting on the bench before being introduced as a substitute, as the hosts inflicted another damaging setback on the champions.
