How Did Investigators Find Baby Who Survived Being Buried Alive? What to Know

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The astonishing rescue of an 11-month-old baby who spent roughly 24 hours underground inside a dog crate has gripped a community in South Carolina and drawn national attention to a case authorities are calling miraculous.

Koah Sharp was pulled from the dirt alive on November 10, 2026, after his father allegedly took him from a relative’s care and buried him in the woods near Batesburg-Leesville.

Us breaks down what is known about how investigators located the boy, the charges against his father and the small details that may have saved Koah’s life.

A search party combing the area around the home of the suspect’s father noticed a patch of “freshly disturbed” dirt on the afternoon of November 10, 2026, according to Lexington County Sheriff Jay Koon.

Investigators began digging and soon heard the baby crying from beneath the soil, a sound Koon described at a news conference as “the most joyful sound.”

Koah was found inside what Koon called a dog crate wrapped in a towel, and authorities believe that towel, combined with colder temperatures, likely created an air pocket that allowed him to survive.

“So as you imagine, the race was on. Everybody’s getting down there trying to get him out,” Koon said.

The sheriff said Koah was “screaming at the top of his lungs” when rescuers reached him.

He was rushed to a hospital and released that same evening, according to the Lexington County Sheriff’s Department, which announced in a November 11 news release that “Koah Sharp is alive and well.”

Michael Comer, Koah’s father, was arrested and now faces charges of attempted murder, unlawful conduct toward a child, custodial interference and burglary, according to the Lexington County Sheriff’s Department.

He was initially taken into custody on a burglary charge on the evening of November 9, 2026, after being located at an outbuilding in Batesburg without the baby. He was denied bond on November 12.

Tyler Douglas, the lead investigator with the Lexington County Sheriff’s Department, told a judge that Comer showed no remorse for the attempted murder of his son, according to reporting from the Associated Press and CBS News.

Last month, Comer pleaded guilty to a domestic violence charge and was sentenced to probation, per the Associated Press. He has reportedly not yet been appointed legal counsel.

Koah was in the care of his paternal grandmother on November 9, 2026, when Comer arrived at her home.

“She left Koah with Comer to run an errand while another caregiver was coming over,” Koon said in a statement. “When the caregiver got there, Comer was gone and Koah was with him.”

A desperate search was launched across the area around Batesburg-Leesville, with the sheriff’s office posting on Facebook a description of a blue-eyed “bright boy” with reddish blond hair who regularly uses a pacifier.

Patricia Comer, Michael’s mother and Koah’s grandmother, told NBC News that her son is “where he needs to be” following his arrest. “Honestly, I’m his mother, and I never expected this from him,” she told the outlet.

John Comer, the suspect’s father and Koah’s grandfather, told the Associated Press that he witnessed his grandson being pulled from the ground on the property near his home. He said the baby was crying and covered in dirt when rescuers lifted him out.

“I became ecstatic, my hands straight in the air. I’m looking in the sky, jumping up and down, praising God,” John said. “And at the same time I hit my knees and I’m just crying.”

Koah was released from the hospital the evening of his rescue, and Koon said he “hopefully will make a full recovery.” The sheriff’s office initially spelled the boy’s last name as Sharpe before updating it to Sharp in subsequent releases. Comer remains in custody after being denied bond, and information about his legal representation was not immediately available at the time of the sheriff’s department’s announcements.

Investigators have continued to emphasize the narrow margin by which the baby was found alive, pointing to the towel-wrapped crate and the cooler weather as the likely reasons an air pocket formed beneath the dirt.
