Selling the OC's Tyler Stanaland and Wife Hannah Welcome 1st Baby

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Selling the OC alum Tyler Stanaland and his wife, Hannah Morrissey, are officially parents.

“Welcome, Westley Grace Stanaland. Born 11.6.26, 7lbs 6oz and 20” long. Both mom and baby girl are happy and healthy despite 36 hours of labor and an emergency c-section,” Stanaland, 37, wrote via Instagram on Sunday, November 8, announcing his daughter’s birth. “Still in complete awe of my wife, you’re a real life superhero @hannahstanaland_.”

He continued, “My cup is overflowing in a way I didn’t know possible. What a blessing it is to be your Dad, Westley 🎀.”

Tyler and Hannah announced in May that they were expecting their first baby.

“Half of you and half of me. Baby Stanaland coming October 2026,” the pair captioned Instagram footage of the two of them wearing matching pink Converse next to a baby-sized pair.

In the months that followed, Tyler offered glimpses of Hannah’s pregnancy via social media.

“Happy birthday America 🇺🇸,” he wrote via Instagram, sharing a snap of his wife’s white, bump-baring two-piece on July 4.

A commenter replied, “Hot parents era activated 😍🇺🇸.”

Later in July, Tyler uploaded a carousel captioned “midsummer life update” alongside an image of Hannah rocking a red maternity bikini during a beach date.

Us broke the news in May 2025 that Tyler started dating Hannah following his divorce from ex-wife Brittany Snow.

“While it’s relatively new, they’re having a lot of fun,” a source exclusively told Us. “They’ve been traveling a bit together, and Tyler’s really happy.”

Tyler popped the question nearly five months later.

“When the right person enters your life, it’s not just love, it’s a transformation. All the uncertainties are replaced with peace and partnership,” the reality TV realtor wrote via Instagram in October 2025, announcing their engagement. “When you’re also aligned on all the important things it’s absolute magic. I have never had more fun or been more inspired and challenged in all the best ways.”

Tyler and Hannah eloped the next month in an intimate ceremony in Laguna Beach, California, with just family members and their dog in attendance.

“The wedding and getting to marry my best friend was an absolute dream come true in every way,” Tyler gushed to People of the nuptials. “It was fun and intimate and intentional and about all the things it should be — two families becoming one and a celebration of love. [It was] more of an intimate, anti-wedding elopement.”

He added at the time, “As Christians, being married is super important to both of us and I feel so lucky to have met someone like Hannah. We want to build a life together, till death do us part. Having a true partner like her is something I’ve always wanted and can honestly say I’ve never experienced before. It’s a really beautiful thing when you find it.”

Prior to meeting Hannah, Tyler was married to the Hunting Wives actress from 2020 to 2023. Snow, for her part, is now engaged to Hunter Moreno.
