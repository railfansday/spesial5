Grand Theft Auto 6 is more leak than ship at this point, with the anonymous individual known as Cyberleek in particular responsible for feeding the hype machine in ways that Rockstar weren't big fans of. The latest leak came from elsewhere, however, via a video of someone handling advance copies of the vinyl soundtrack. The album's track listing is visible, and has been confirmed as accurate by Rolling Stone's sources as well as Billboard's.

This is the soundtrack rather than the score, which will be by Justice—the French electronic duo responsible for the excellent 2007 electro house album Cross—though they do feature here a couple of times as well. Their songs Flower in a Hurricane (with Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard) and Starting from Zero (with Michael McDonald of the Doobie Brothers and Steely Dan) are both on side A.
