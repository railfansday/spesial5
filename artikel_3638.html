EA is resurrecting a dead commentator with AI for EA Sports FC 27, years after his last appearance in a FIFA game

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

"He was the very first voice of our game - so we really wanted to do him justice and bring him back."

EA Sports FC 27 is using gen-AI to digitally recreate the voice of veteran football commentator John Motson - the FIFA video game series' first ever real-world announcer who died in 2023.

John Motson commentated on hundreds of football games over the course of five different decades, starting in the 1970s. The English commentator then became the mainstay voice of EA's dominant football simulation series, beginning with FIFA 96 all the way up until FIFA 2005, when the publisher decided to go in a different direction.

But EA's now decided to digitally resurrect Motson - or, at least, his incredibly recognisable voice - with permission from his family. An update with Motson's voice will go live on 26th November. "There was no way we would have moved forward with this in any way, shape or form if they weren't fully on board," senior game designer director Mat Prior said in a statement given to the BBC earlier today.

"At the outset we wanted to pay tribute to to Motty as the commentator and as this emotional connection - he was the very first voice of our game - so we really wanted to do him justice and bring him back," Prior added, explaining that Motson's posthumous role in the game is being handled with a mixture of previously recorded voice lines and AI. EA supposedly went down the AI route since it doesn't have recordings of Motson commentating over newer, more fresh faced footballers.

Fred Motson said it "means an awful lot" to hear his dad's voice in the game again, and he reckons his dad was "always really keen to get involved in these kinds of projects," even if he wasn't the most technologically savvy person. EA said it had paid the family for the rights to use Motson's voice and likeness.

Save Our Voices Now founder Peter Caulfield - who campaigns for the government to regulate the use of generative AI voice cloning tech - told the BBC he wasn't comfortable with the project. "In life or in death, unless you have specifically agreed to it, AI cloning of any kind should not be allowed," he said. "Being a family member shouldn't automatically give you that right either." He doesn't have a problem with actors consenting to AI voice replication while they're still alive, however.

A Japanese acting union recently fought back against this very same practice by proposing new guidelines that would stop the replication of dead actors' voices without their permission.

Generative AI has been widely controversial in the games industry for other reasons, too. In recent months, there's been a huge impact on smaller game developers who are worried that their games could be cloned by AI copycats and some who have had to delay their release dates because of Steam's growing approvals backlog.

"The Grounds comes across as a frippery, Ultimate Team rumbles ever on, but FC 27 brings meaningful changes to the fluidity and speed of play. Given that its practical football-game monopoly is more or less unchallenged, EA could do a lot less than this," Josh Wise wrote in Eurogamer's EA Sports FC 27 review.
