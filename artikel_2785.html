Justin Herbert is in concussion protocol

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert played the entire game in Sunday’s 27-24 loss to the Jets, but he did not emerge from it unscathed.

The team announced after the game that Herbert would not be speaking to the media because he is in the league’s concussion protocol. Herbert will need to progress through all the steps of the protocol before being cleared to play again.

The Chargers host the Patriots next Sunday.

Herbert was 15-of-21 for 131 yards and two touchdowns during the loss to the Jets. He also ran eight times for 32 yards, including a 10-yard touchdown run on the final play of the first half, and was sacked six times.
