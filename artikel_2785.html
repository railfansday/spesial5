Game Preview: San Antonio Spurs vs New Orleans Pelicans

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Basement dwellers for seemingly the zillionth season in a row, it's tough not to feel for fans of the New Orleans Pelicans. They still have Zion Williamson on the roster and he's had a solid start to the season. However seven years into his career, the oft-injured Williamson, once seen as the NBA's next sure-fire superstar, has been unable to drag New Orleans out of irrelevancy. They've also dealt with injuries up and down the roster in the early going this season, as well as some roster drama involving a trade request that was made public last Friday from former San Antonio Spurs guard Dejounte Murray. With so much inner turmoil and unfortunate luck, the path forward for New Orleans is as murky as it's ever been.

Meanwhile for San Antonio, their generational draft pick has already made good on his upside, but there is still much work to do for Victor Wembanyama and the Spurs. Without Dylan Harper for the immediate future, they're coming off a win against another mess of a team in the Los Angeles Clippers. That win came with some concerns, namely the season-high 19 turnovers they accumulated and a 17/42 three point shooting performance that kept Los Angeles in the game until the 4th quarter. Warts and all though, San Antonio has continued to take care of business as they approach the 20-game mark. They'll need to bring that professionalism once again tonight against an opponent that is this far out in the weeds.

Spurs Injuries: Tobias Harris, (out — calf), Dylan Harper, (out — hip)

Pelicans Injuries: Dejounte Murray (out – ankle), Bennedict Mathurin (out – foot), Trendon Watford (out – ankle), Bryce McGowens (out – elbow); Trey Murphy III (questionable – knee).

With San Antonio's decision to roll with the three guard line-up to start this year, Stephon Castle has sacrificed some of his game and has spent a lot more time this season off-ball to help accommodate both Harper and De'Aaron Fox when all three are sharing the court and it's been admirable to watch. As a result though, he's been relegated to being a spot-up shooter at times, being an outlet rather than being the one to get downhill to try and bend the defense like he did all of last year with the starters. With Harper out, expect to see a little bit more of the Castle from last season, though hopefully without some of the turnover woes he was prone to at times. Ironically, he had a season-high 8 giveaways in the win over the Clippers, the first game without Harper.

Zion Williamson remains the focal point of this struggling Pelicans team. He's putting up solid numbers and has been efficient doing so, relying on a still-explosive first step to get in the paint and either get shots off close to the rim or draw fouls that lead to him getting to the line. The effectiveness of that plan of attack though will be put to the test tonight with reigning DPOY Victor Wembanyama patrolling the paint with his 8-foot wingspan. The stellar San Antonio defense has been the best team in the league so far this season at not fouling and keeping opposing team's off the free throw line. Whether or not that will hold true tonight against an athlete like Williamson, who is still so strong and explosive, is arguably the main thing to watch for tonight.

Dylan Harper being out doesn't only mean more opportunities for Castle, it also puts Julian Champagnie back into the starting role he occupied all of last year. Champagnie is one of San Antonio's most versatile team defenders and is their best shooter. 3-point shooting in particular is arguably the biggest weakness of the 3-Guard lineup, as all three go to their jump shot when they need to but are at their best putting the ball on the floor and trying to get into the paint. Having to account for a shooter like Champagnie means one less guy that's playing with a foot near the paint clogging up the lane for a Wemby that is already making it a habit to dunk everything this season.
