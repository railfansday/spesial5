Thursday Night Football: DE Travon Walker, RB Bhayshul Tuten active for Jaguars vs. Ravens

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Jaguars will have running back Bhayshul Tuten and defensive end Travon Walker for Thursday Night Football against the Ravens.

Both players are active after being questionable on the final injury report.

Walker has a rib injury and Tuten injured his ankle/foot last week.

Walker has played in every game, seeing action on 330 defensive snaps. He has totaled three sacks, seven quarterback hits, one pass defensed and 18 tackles.

Tuten has played 241 snaps in seven games, totaling 125 touches for 673 yards and six touchdowns.

The Jaguars’ inactives are wide receiver CJ Williams, cornerback Alijah Huzzie, safety Cam’Ron Silmon-Craig and defensive end Wesley Williams.

The Ravens’ inactives are quarterback Kedon Slovis, wide receiver Elijah Sarratt, offensive lineman Emery Jones Jr., defensive lineman Aeneas Peebles, cornerback Nate Wiggins (stinger/illness) and safety Jaylinn Hawkins (knee). Slovis is the emergency third quarterback, meaning Austin Reed, who the Ravens signed on Monday, will be the No. 2 quarterback behind Joe Fagnano.
