Packers waive RB Pierre Strong Jr.

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Green Bay Packers have made the decision to waive running back Pierre Strong Jr. on Thursday. While Strong is a vested veteran, a player who is usually not subject to waivers, those rules change post-trade deadline — as all players moving forward are now subject to waivers. Strong is just the second player to be let go from the Packers' 53-man roster this season, as receiver Coleman Owen (who was brought back to the practice squad after being cut last week) was the first.

Why did Green Bay make this move? Well, the Micah Parsons of it all. Parsons opened up a 21-day practice window several weeks ago, meaning that the team will need to activate him from the physically unable to perform list soon.

My guess is that the Packers will try to keep Strong on the practice squad, which is currently full, to give the team some depth there. Green Bay has four running backs on the 53-man roster at the moment in Josh Jacobs, Chris Brooks, Kaleb Johnson and MarShawn Lloyd. Jacobs is unable to play right now, as he's recovering from surgery on a broken finger on his right hand. The team does not have a practice squad running back, which is generally unusual for the team.

Strong and Damon Bankston had previously filled that role for Green Bay this year. Bankston, who has been cut from the Packers' practice squad twice this season, is now on the Minnesota Vikings' practice squad.

If Strong passes through waivers on Friday (expected), look for Green Bay to ditch a practice squad player in order to make room for Strong to return to the team. As a reminder, the Packers' players will get back to Green Bay on Friday after a three-day break from the facility. They'll have Saturday, Sunday and Monday practices to prepare for the Los Angeles Rams before traveling on Tuesday for their Thanksgiving Eve (a totally real thing the NFL didn't create) game.

Below is the current layout of the Packers' practice squad:

WR: Coleman Owen, Isaiah Neyor and Quincy Skinner Jr.

ED: Arron Mosby, Nyjalik Kelly and D.J. Johnson

DL: Mazi Smith and Haggai Ndubuisi (international exemption)
