UNLV gets tough down the stretch, tops Stanford 76-68

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

LAS VEGAS, Nev. (AP) — Cam Miles scored 17 points and UNLV held off a late rally to defeat Stanford 76-68 on Friday night.

A 3-pointer by Sebastian Mack gave the Runnin' Rebels a 69-61 lead with about four minutes left in the game.

Near the two-minute mark, Aziz Olajuwon's 3-pointer drew Stanford to within 69-66, but Isaac Williamson answered with a 3 that kept UNLV in control.

Tyrin Jones scored 16 points and Mack, who averages 24 points, scored 15 for UNLV (3-0).

Austin Maurer had game highs of 20 points and nine rebounds for Stanford (3-1). He made 10 of 11 shots. Julius Price had 16 points and Nick Townsend scored 11.

UNLV attempted only 45 shots, making 26 for 58%. Stanford shot 40% (24 for 60) and made only 3 of 21 3-pointers.

UNLV led 36-18 late in the first half before Mauer scored eight points in a 15-3 run that got Stanford within 39-33 at halftime.
