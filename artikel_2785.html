Vikings-49ers SNF score predictions from the Bring Me The Sports staff

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

This is about as good as it gets for a November regular season game. The 8-1 49ers against the 7-2 Vikings, in primetime, with the winner taking the lead in the race for the No. 1 seed in the NFC. Kyle Shanahan's offense against Brian Flores' defense. Kirk Cousins' second game back with Minnesota. That it happens to be taking place in Mexico City only adds to the spotlight and intrigue.

Who will fly back from Mexico with a victory? Our staff writers have made their score predictions.

I picked against the Vikings last week in Green Bay. I'm not doing that again. The 49ers are better than the Packers, but after watching that game, it's hard to go against this Minnesota team right now. This is an all-time great defense that is getting Blake Cashman back this week, which is big when trying to limit Christian McCaffrey and George Kittle. Kirk Cousins hit the ground running in his return to the Vikings and should only be more comfortable after a full week of preparation. I've got Justin Jefferson breaking out for a huge game and Brian Flores moving to 4-0 against Kyle Shanahan.

All this talk about the 49ers being dominant when they've lost to the Falcons and gave up 40 points to the Cowboys, while also barely beating Arizona and Washington? Please. They're good, but not the world-beaters everyone makes them out to be. The Niners are going to get a rude awakening against the NFL's best defense, and you can bet your bottom dollar that Kirk Cousins isn't going to throw multiple interceptions like he did against San Fran with the Raiders on Nov. 8. I think Minnesota basically beats the hell out of the 49ers, leaving no doubt as to who is the best team in the NFL.

The Vikings' offense looked completely different with Kirk Cousins under center last week against the Packers, which should spark some confidence for this week's game against the 49ers in Mexico City. We're going to find out real quick if Minnesota's a true Super Bowl contender in this matchup against one of the favorites on a neutral field. I lean towards San Francisco in this matchup, partially based on the fact that their quarterback has been on their team all season. It should be a great football game that will be decided by just a few plays.

This has all the makings of a game-of-the-year candidate. Brock Purdy has been great against pressure this year, but we'll see how he handles the pressure Flores' defense will bring this weekend. With aother week in the offense and some confidence off last week's win, I think Kirk Cousins looks and plays even better when the Vikings need a big performance.

I predicted the Vikings to lose to the Packers and was proven demonstrably wrong. I therefore have no choice but to keep my record going for the greater good and go for a 49ers win. It should be a classic, but the Vikings defense won't have nearly as much success as they did against Jordan Love and the Packers' porous O-line last week. Offensively, I expect Kirk Cousins to overshoot several throws due to the thinner air in Mexico City, but put enough on target for a couple of tuddies either side of Will Reichard's 60-yard-plus FGs. Expect a close one but San Fran to run out on top.
