Cavs lose 122-108 to Bulls after walloping in first half

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Cleveland Cavaliers lost 122-108 to the Chicago Bulls after taking a beating in the first half. Donovan Mitchell led the team with 28 points.

The second quarter was an outright disaster. Chicago opened up with a 19-6 run in the first four minutes, and would extend that to 32-10 on their way to a dominant lead.

Cleveland entered tonight as the 10th-worst transition defense in the league. It showed.

The Bulls broke free in transition over and over again in the second quarter. The Cavs couldn't keep up or slow them down, and Chicago reaped the rewards. Everything from poor effort to bad communication contributed to this. But more than anything, the Cavs couldn't score the ball to regain control of the pace. Missed shots and turnovers led to more fast-break opportunities for the opponent.

That wasn't all. Chicago's bench opened the game by outscoring Cleveland 48-9 in the first half. The first quarter was mostly fine, but the second quarter was a running rampage from the Bulls.

The Cavs trailed by 22 points going into halftime.

It was an inspiring start to the third quarter that gave the Cavs life. They jumped out to a quick start, chipping away at the Bulls by working downhill into the paint and playing with an advantage. Converting those opportunities helped them regain control of the game's pace.

James Harden was instrumental in the Cavs' run. He probed into the paint and either scored, dished an assist, or drew a foul throughout the third quarter. Harden scored 11 of his 21 points in the third quarter.

Cleveland would eventually get the game to within two possessions, but fell back down by double digits once the Bulls reignited their flame in the fourth quarter.

It was a strong second half from the Cavs, but they gave themselves too thin a margin of error to complete the comeback. That's what happens when you lose the second quarter by 25 points. Not to mention, the Bulls took 17 more shot attempts than Cleveland.

Jordan Hawkins lit it up for 19 points off the bench on 7-for-13 shooting. It was a confident performance from a younger player who is trying to establish himself in the NBA. The Cavs certainly won't forget him the next time they play Hawkins.

Cleveland falls to 9-9 on the season and will play the New York Knicks on Wednesday.
