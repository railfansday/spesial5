49ers injury report: Only 1 player misses 1st Week 11 practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

San Francisco 49ers wide receiver Mike Evans was the only player who missed the team's first Week 11 practice due to injury.

Linebacker Dre Greenlaw, running back Christian McCaffrey and left tackle Trent Williams were all given their standard veteran rest days to start the week. The 49ers also listed Greenlaw with a neck issue.

Evans is in Colorado Springs while the 49ers prepare in elevation for playing in Mexico City, but he didn't participate in the session.
