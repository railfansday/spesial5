Seahawks sign Deven Eastern, call up Bobby Hart, Montorie Foster Jr.

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Seahawks are making some last-minute adjustments to deal with several key injuries ahead of Sunday's game against the Arizona Cardinals.

First, the defending Super Bowl champions signed Deven Eastern. Eastern, a rookie defensive tackle from Minnesota, has been a member of Seahawks' practice squad, and will play his first regular season game Sunday.

With both Leonard Williams and Brandon Pili out Sunday, Eastern will likely see plenty of playing time.

In addition to Eastern, the Seahawks are adding Bobby Hart and Montorie Foster Jr. to their roster from their practice squad for the Cardinals game.

Similar to Eastern, both Hart and Foster will add depth to their respective position rooms, as multiple offensive tackles and wide receivers deal with injuries.

As for the current quarterback situation, Seahawks.com Senior Reporter John Boyle notes that the team will roll with just Drew Lock and Jalen Milroe Sunday.
