FILM ROOM PODCAST: Weston Yannone joins Season 3 Episode 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Welcome to Season 3 Episode 11 of the Film Room Podcast! The Times-Tribune Athlete of the Week Dunmore football player Weston Yannone joins hosts Joe Baress and Joby Fawcett to discuss what it means to capture the District 2 Class 2A title, the importance of the offensive line, how the coaching staff has helped him and the team, how the team has developed, what it's like to handle the media attention and pressure of an undefeated season and more.

We also look back at the District 2 championship games in Class 2A (Dunmore-Lakeland) and 3A (Scranton Prep-Wyoming Area), the subregional title game in Class 5A (Abington Heights-Hollidaysburg) and the Class 4A semifinals (North Pocono-Valley View, Dallas-Wallenpaupack). We also look ahead to Friday's Class 4A title game and first-round state playoff games.
