What Vogue Editors Are Gifting This Holiday Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

For some, gift-giving is an innate skill, but for others (myself included), it requires a little extra effort. I often find myself overwhelmed by the sheer number of options yet somehow lacking inspiration, never quite sure where to start when looking for something thoughtful and original for my loved ones. This is where being part of the Vogue Shopping team has its advantages; over the years, I’ve developed a better eye for presents that go beyond the basics. Being surrounded by people with great taste and a knack for shopping has certainly helped.

For those who may not have a fashion editor to call on for gifting ideas, I polled colleagues across Vogue’s office to see what they’re planning to give this holiday season. Their picks certainly have every type of person on your list covered—whether you’re looking for small trinkets to stuff some stockings, something to bring your favorite host at a holiday dinner, or just some little luxuries for the special people in your life. Read on to see what 26 Vogue editors and contributors are gifting this holiday season.

I live for Christmas and am always thrilled to give and receive festive gifts. I love these peppermint-striped tapers from House & Parties, Liberty print holiday crowns, and these Nutcracker crackers, and I plan to get them for myself and others. I love these cozy sock slippers from The Six Bells x Madewell and will gift them to friends for holiday mornings.

It’s often the little gifts that have the biggest impact. Something small yet thoughtful goes a long way, and our best host gifts are packed with ideas for as little as $20. From pantry supplies like tinned fish and fancy olive oil (a total crowd-pleaser) to elevated aperitivo glasses, I’ll be gifting several of these to friends, my mother, and my host for Thanksgiving lunch.

I’m keeping the bigger gifts on my list under wraps—partially for the element of surprise, mostly because I haven’t bought them yet. In the meantime, I have some smaller items on hand to give as host gifts for holiday parties (like a beautiful soap) and to weekend visitors (like a pair of nice gardening gloves for my mom). I also host a lasagna party every December and like to put tiny ornaments at each place setting so everyone can take something home besides leftovers. Toast’s hand-painted ones double as wine-glass charms, and every person gets a different one.

I’m an impassioned gifter with strong feelings about which olive oil should be on the table. Food-related gifts are my favorite to give and get, especially those I keep in my own kitchen. Incense papers are easy to tuck into a thank-you envelope alongside tea and taralli, and no one expects you to show up at their house with a fancy jar of mustard, which is exactly why you should.

These two scents from Flamingo Estate are absolutely divine; I might be addicted. The bar soap makes a great gift for anyone, from a close friend to your holiday host or even a distant relative. I love anything from Officine Universelle Buly because everything is special and you can personalize most items. The packaging is also incredible, so you don’t even have to think about wrapping them yourself—perfect for anyone who needs to travel with gifts! I am a firm believer that you don’t need to spend a lot of money on a well-received gift. Beautiful matchboxes, playful lemon squeezers, and trompe l’oeil chocolate sardines have always been fail-safe holiday presents from me, and they won’t break the bank.

Golden Door (from our Spa Guide of the best in the world!) was my most magical summer trip, and I’m gifting my sister-in-law Sarah, whose love language is cooking, its hardcover cookbook, The Golden Door Table. I love the accessories Dame Pat McGrath is creating for Louis Vuitton, and I’m buying the new Monogram Rouge Eyeshadow Pouch for myself, adding to a collection my mom started! I’m in my linen-bedding era, and my mother-in-law will get a fresh quilt from Thuma (even better for my naps when we visit!). I wore Alo Yoga out to my dad’s ranch, and now he loves the brand, so I’m getting him suited up in its soft fabrics for his winter cycling championship off-duty looks!

The holiday is fast approaching, and I’m still finalizing my gift list. A few items I’ll be buying this holiday season are a pair of shell coasters (a perfect gift for the host); a fashionable accessory for a mother, sister, or friend; a Dries Van Noten lipstick case that makes putting on lipstick a bit more exciting; and a scooter for my daughter, who has just learned how to ride one.

I’ve always been a fan of gifting items that are as functional as they are pretty. You can’t go wrong with Flamingo Estate’s personalized olive oil (adding your loved one’s name and address makes it a thoughtful housewarming gift too!) or Santa Maria Novella’s terra-cotta diffuser, my personal favorite.

As expected, my first instinct is to gift beauty goodies. I’m so blessed to test the absolute best of cosmetics, and I like to treat my loved ones to that same level of care. I always say Chanel Beauty is underrated, so it’s a good place to start when you want luxury that meets efficacy. Its gift sets are always crowd-pleasers and often arrive in a pouch you can repurpose as a toiletry bag. Otherwise, I love to gift a unique fragrance they likely haven’t heard of—like Guerlain’s 2026 debut—or little luxuries. Think: a chic leather toiletry bag (Etoile makes my favorite ones), Freja’s wine carrier, Gohar World’s tasseled comb, or Abask’s guest book.

I consider gift-giving a deadly serious ritual—not because of its materialistic nature but because it offers a chance to be particularly thoughtful when handpicking something for a loved one. As sickeningly earnest as this might seem, one of my greatest joys is making people I care about feel happy and seen. Whether the recipient has everything or has hyper-specific vibes or niche interests (I’m surely guilty of that), I have a method for finding the perfect present. An easy formula is to select a relatively ordinary item with an especially lovely or personalized design. Suddenly, an everyday object becomes a thing of beauty in their daily life. For the planner or poet, a lovely journal. (My friend Lea just gave me this for my birthday, and I adore it.) For the bookworm, a custom book stamp. For the friend that’s always touching up on the go, a gorgeous mini brush that won’t get lost in their handbag. For the decor enthusiast or as a housewarming present, a whimsical lemon squeezer. And for the glamorous friend or family member? A gorgeous lipstick case or sleek nail polish trio does the trick. I love all of the above—but when you’re not sure what to give me, fueling my original-press vinyl collection is always a win.

I love the holiday season because it’s an opportune moment to gift friends and family objects of affection that show you care. I like to think of myself as a selective, personal gifter, and I love the hunt for pieces that match each person individually. For myself, I’d love to elevate my winter wardrobe with this absolutely divine Verena Shearling Coat from Arma and the Dallen hat from Nour Hammour. Autumn Sonata makes wonderful linens and towel sets using archival prints and motifs; they transform your bathroom into an art gallery of sorts (and make excellent gifts). Though perfume is highly personal, anyone would be happy to receive a bottle of Frisson D’Hiver, described as “an audacious whisper of frost-kissed citrus and solar blooms.” These black glass coasters from Casalta are chic too.

With end-of-year holiday parties, family reunions, and dinner parties with friends filling up the calendar, I’ve found myself reaching for the tried-and-true host gift duo: flowers and a bottle of wine. But this year, I’m determined to add a little more pizzazz! Why not pair those beautiful bouquets with a fun, eclectic vase? I’m loving these options from Jonathan Adler. Or level up your favorite bottle of wine or cocktail gift with some playful glassware or an easy bottle holder.

To me, there’s no better feeling than finding the perfect gift for a loved one. I often struggle to keep gifts under wraps until the holidays, simply because I’m too excited to give them. Having grown up in a household of girls, I rarely struggle to find things my sisters or mother will love, since they are usually items I cherish or covet as well. Speaking of which, one of my best beauty discoveries of the year is Celisse’s nail products: I cannot say enough good things about them! Its polish dries in seconds and doesn’t chip for days, which is something I’ve never experienced with traditional lacquers. So someone will definitely be finding a Celisse set under the tree. And as for my dad, the only man in the house, I’ll keep adding to his espresso-cup collection.

I’m determined to have all of my holiday shopping wrapped up by Cyber Monday! I put a lot of thought into what I give each holiday season, and gifting is truly one of my favorite things to do. That said, I find the season is so much more joyful—and considerably less stressful—when I stay ahead of my lists. Flamingo Estate is always my first stop for gifts, no matter the occasion, and I’m particularly excited to give its Fig Trio set this year. This Misette candlestick is incredibly chic and comes in a range of colors, but I’ve managed to snag the red one for my mom, whose home features a red-drenched library. Maison d’Etto’s Pinehurst perfume is another lovely gift, especially for its rich, woody base notes—which make it a fitting winter fragrance. I’ve been singing Barefaced’s praises for years, and its newly launched Discovery Kit is the perfect opportunity to introduce friends and family to the brand so that they can finally see what all my fuss is about. For the phone-addicted people in my life, Brick makes a particularly clever stocking stuffer; I can think of a few people who could use one! And finally, because my husband is constantly receiving compliments on just about everything he owns from Buck Mason, I’ll be adding one of the brand’s quarter-zips to his collection this year.

I love gifting the special people in my life items that actually serve a purpose while remaining incredibly chic. Cozy winter accessories are always a go-to, like these sleek leather gloves from Agnelle and plush slippers from Ugg. A festive coffee-table book is another gift sure to delight, and this one dedicated to the legendary Grace Coddington is perfect for any fashion lover. I’m also a sucker for a beautifully scented bath oil, so why not pass that little luxury on to someone I love? And then, of course, I couldn’t help but practice a little self-love this year and include two gifts I’m either hoping to receive or might just buy for myself: Foundrae’s personalized diamond necklace and this Call It By Your Name x Minicotton tote, as I await the arrival of my baby boy early next year.

I received a match holder from my brother’s fiancée last Christmas, and at the time it was so not me. Now, I am obsessed. I have always been a lover of fire, a passion I express through my very public obsession with candles. Unfortunately, unless you’re shopping at Bath & Body Works (no judgment!), candles are expensive (and even then!). And if you burn them incorrectly (like me!), you can watch your investment go up in smoke in a matter of hours. Lately, I have become just as enthusiastic about gifting matchboxes, particularly the very chic ones from MG & Co.—a little unexpected, endlessly useful, and considerably less expensive than any high-end candle. If you’re feeling particularly generous (or confident in your relationship), go the extra mile and have the matchbox engraved. Nothing says commitment quite like putting someone’s name on a box of matches.

Gift-giving is my love language, which is great for my loved ones but not so great for my wallet! I like to give homeware items, as I find that people rarely justify buying themselves a fancy blanket or chic serveware, so they are usually overjoyed when they receive those sorts of items as a gift. My mother and I both adore anything Nickey Kehoe, so that’s my first stop when holiday shopping for her. My father is impossible to buy for, but he loves to cook, so I tend to get him fancy olive oil (you cannot go wrong with Flamingo Estate!), and who doesn’t love a new, beautiful cheese knife set for hosting season? I also discovered the brand SBE this year, so my sister and cousins will most likely receive one of its lip balms or brow gels as part of my gift to them this year. A pro tip I recently learned from a fellow gifty friend is to buy a holiday gift set, divide up the contents, rewrap them yourself, and gift the products to different friends. Genius!

I’m deep in the season of friends getting engaged, married, and buying homes, and almost everyone is keen to host large groups for special occasions. Ahead of the holidays, I’m looking for gifts for loved ones that can help them spruce up their spaces and make feeding more than just two people easier. A lot of my friends are baking bread, so the Le Creuset Dutch oven is perfect for those savory sourdough loaves. And what better way to serve some slices, along with other snacks and treats, than on gorgeous wood serving trays from Anthropologie? There’s also something to be said for adding a little whimsy around the house these days, and this magnetic photo-strip frame is perfect for that—especially for putting fun memories on display.

In my quest for a bedding refresh, I stumbled upon the perfect gift for my sister. I heard about John Robshaw from a family friend whose taste I trust endlessly; she recommended the brand’s long-lasting organic cotton percale sheets and duvet covers, which are on my wish list, but its velvet offerings felt better suited to a glamorous, cozy winter at home. So I’m planning to get my sister one of its green velvet quilted pillows, which happens to match her couch perfectly.

As someone who’s product obsessed (in both fashion and home goods), I see the holidays as my chance to share my favorite finds with the people I love. I make a point of gifting things I actually own and use because that makes them so much more personal. Below are the things I reach for constantly in my own life, from the best water filter I’ve found to delicious-smelling hand soap and my favorite multipurpose scarf. I also think everyone needs a good toiletry bag, and I’m obsessed with one from Autumn Sonata that I discovered at a local shop in Brooklyn.

To me, the best gifts come from listening to what those you love are obsessed with and figuring out how to give it to them. At a slew of parties I hosted to celebrate my new Ninja Slushi Machine, people were “joking” that they wanted to take it home; now I’m gifting the machines to friends so I will never visit a house party without fresh margs or piña coladas flowing from the spout. For my wellness-minded friends, the Biodance Collagen face masks I stocked up on in Korea will give you crazy plump skin overnight—and if they’re always comparing sleep scores, the new bands from Whoop turn the (sometimes unsightly) fitness tracker into something much more aesthetic.

I love gifting Sézane sweaters for the holidays. Who doesn’t love a cozy sweater that comes at a great price point? For stocking stuffers, I’ll be giving everyone my favorite moisturizer ever from Aestura, now sold at Sephora.

I’m a huge fan of gifts that make everyday moments feel a little more special. Whether it’s elevating an everyday essential, introducing a new hobby, setting the scene for a night of hosting, or adding a cozy touch to someone’s space, I love pieces that feel both thoughtful and useful—things they might not necessarily buy for themselves but will enjoy having.

This year I’ve found so many fun pieces on the market that would make the perfect gifts for my friends and family. From the Ro Cashmere Set by All My Love (because who doesn’t love a cozy moment during the holidays?) to the gorgeous leather notebooks from Mémoire, these finds are making holiday shopping extra exciting. Of course, while I may be shopping for others, I wouldn’t be surprised if a few of these end up in my own cart too.

Like many of us, I’ve found my screen time has gotten pretty ridiculous lately, so I’ve opted to replace scrolling with evening “sound walks” on my TP-7. I will be gifting these to my fellow audio-lover friends who want to unplug a bit, paired with the latest Nothing Audio headphones I’m constantly getting complimented on.

My partner is a silver-jewelry obsessive and has had a specific pair of Panconesi earrings on their wish list for a long time, so I will be dropping them into their stocking this Christmas!

As I’ve gotten older, candles have become one of my favorite gifts to give and receive. My friend recently recommended Keap’s Wild Fig Candle, and I will be adding it to my cart for a loved one and myself!

My younger sister told me she recently got into perfumes, so I will be sending her the Nostros discovery set so she can find the scent that’s right for her!

I’m gifting everyone Damson Madder socks this year! I know socks are sort of a blah gift traditionally, but I’m obsessed with having a particularly cute pair of rugby socks to slide on when it’s cold out (or as cold as it ever gets in LA, anyway). This female-owned London brand is committed to sustainability, which makes me feel particularly good about slipping its socks into everyone’s stocking this Chrismukkah.
