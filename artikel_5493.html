Digital Information Resources Announces Launch of ManhattanHockey.com, Its First Team-Specific Sports Media Property

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

NEW YORK, Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Digital Information Resources (DIR), a technology and digital media company that builds, acquires, and operates specialized digital properties, today announced the launch of ManhattanHockey.com, its first team-specific sports media property, dedicated to the New York Rangers and their passionate fan community.

The launch comes as the Rangers mark their 100th anniversary in 2026, celebrating a century of hockey history dating back to the franchise's founding in 1926. As one of the NHL's Original Six franchises, the Rangers have played a defining role in New York City's sports culture, building generations of loyal fans.

ManhattanHockey.com is designed to provide accessible information, insights, and resources for hockey enthusiasts, with a particular focus on the Rangers and the city's broader hockey community. The platform aims to serve everyone from longtime fans to those discovering the sport for the first time.

"New York has one of the most passionate hockey communities in the world, and the Rangers' 100-year milestone makes this an especially exciting time to launch ManhattanHockey.com," said Michael Gursha, Co-Founder and CEO of Digital Information Resources. "As our first team-specific sports media property, this launch represents an important step in expanding our sports portfolio and creating more focused digital destinations for passionate fan communities."

The launch is part of DIR's broader strategy to develop authoritative digital resources for focused audiences. By leveraging proprietary technology, content infrastructure, and digital publishing expertise, the company continues to expand its portfolio of specialized media properties across sports, technology, hobbies, and other categories.

ManhattanHockey.com is now available at www.manhattanhockey.com.

Digital Information Resources (DIR) is a technology and digital media company that builds, acquires, and operates a growing portfolio of specialized digital properties. The company leverages proprietary technology, content infrastructure, and operating expertise to develop and scale digital media brands serving audiences across a diverse range of categories.

For more information, visit www.dir.info.
