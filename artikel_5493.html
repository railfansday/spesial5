Why This Winter Could Be a Hidden Opportunity for Homebuyers—Plus a New Rate Discount From Chase

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The housing market remains tough this winter—but prepared buyers will be presented with opportunities.

"Preparation is everything," says Erik Schmitt, head of consumer direct sales at Chase Home Lending, which is part of JPMorgan Chase's commercial bank.

The outlook for 2027 appears tough. The latest Realtor.com® quarterly construction insights report shows that prices on new homes are holding steady, but builders are having to get creative to attract buyers. Many have said they expect difficulty for much of next year.

On top of that, leaders at the Federal Reserve have signaled that they're poised to raise interest rates multiple times in the next few months to tame inflation. Homeowners are also managing rising insurance costs, HOA fees, and utilities.

Do anything you can now to improve your credit, pay down debt, and understand your budget and what you can afford will help, says Schmitt.

"Get pre-approval because you never know when the market will turn," he advises.

Improving credit scores and paying off higher interest-rate debt will give you more purchasing power.

This week, the National Association of Home Builders/Wells Fargo Housing Market Index plunged to a 14-year low. Meanwhile, the S&P Cotality Case-Shiller Index showed home values fell for the 16th straight month.

So, in the coming winter there's no question about seasonal weakness in the housing market. In some markets, that means more negotiating power and possibly some better options for buyers. But shrinking inventory might also mean there are fewer options.

For that reason, Schmitt says it's best to be proactive this winter.

"It's difficult to time the rate environment," says Schmitt. "What we advise customers to do is have a strong understanding of what they can afford and what they feel comfortable with. It's difficult to time the market, so if you get in a home that meets your needs, meets your affordability target, in the event rates do rally or reduce, you can always refinance."

From Nov. 30  to Dec. 13, Chase is running a mortgage rate sale of up to 0.25% off for qualified buyers. Both new and existing customers will be able to lock in a lower rate on a new home purchase or refinance loan.

The rate sale discount can be stacked with all existing available Chase Home Lending discounts and programs like its Chase Homebuyer Grant and Relationship Pricing Program.

The stacked savings are still subject to the same terms and conditions, and other programs must have structure that means maximum discounts and limitations.

The bank determined a lot of good buyers remain on the sidelines.

JPMorgan Chase announced earlier this year it would invest $750 billion into the housing market. Its American Dream Initiative aims to build or preserve 1 million affordable housing units and help a half-million customers purchase homes—including 200,000 first-time homebuyers.

"Our focus is where we can help with affordability," says Schmitt.
