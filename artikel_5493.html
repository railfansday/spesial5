Bombardier is raising its free cash flow outlook after record services revenue

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Quarterly services revenue rose 19% to $701 million, while net income slipped to $81 million from $85 million

Bombardier raised its 2026 free cash flow guidance, lifting the target above $1.3 billion from the earlier level of more than $1.0 billion, according to third-quarter results released Thursday. It is the second increase to that target this year.

Services revenue reached a record $701 million, up 19% from a year earlier, and topped $700 million in a quarter for the first time. Total revenue rose 3% to $2.4 billion on 33 aircraft deliveries.

Net income from continuing operations declined to $81 million, down from $85 million in the same quarter last year. Diluted earnings per share were $0.73, compared with $0.77. Adjusted net income rose 41% to $182 million, and adjusted earnings per share were $1.72, up from $1.21. The adjustments include a net loss of $94 million tied to certain financial instruments.

Adjusted EBITDA increased 7% to $382 million, with a margin of 16.1% compared with 15.4% a year earlier. Reported EBIT was $266 million, up 17%.

Free cash flow for the quarter was $183 million, up 20%. Cash flows from operating activities totaled $259 million, up 36%, while net additions to property, plant and equipment and intangible assets were $76 million. Free cash flow for the first nine months was $771 million.

Bombardier left its other 2026 targets unchanged: more than 157 aircraft deliveries, revenue above $10.0 billion and adjusted EBITDA above $1.625 billion. Net additions to property, plant and equipment and intangible assets are expected to be near $300 million.

Backlog stood at $22.2 billion on Sept. 30, up $4.7 billion from the end of 2025. The unit book-to-bill ratio was 1.7 for the quarter.

The company ended the quarter with $2.4 billion in available liquidity, including $1.6 billion in cash and cash equivalents. It has cut debt by $1.1 billion this year and reached its target adjusted net debt to adjusted EBITDA ratio of 1.5.

"We are entering the final stretch of 2026 with a healthy backlog, strong demand and solid visibility on all remaining deliveries," President and CEO Éric Martel said in a statement.

Bombardier plans to hold an in-person Investor Day on Feb. 18, 2027, to update its strategic priorities and outlook. The company said details on timing and location will follow in the coming weeks.
