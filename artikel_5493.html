DeFi Technologies Inc. Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results with Revenue of $18.3 Million, Operating Income of $7.0 Million, and Net Income of $22.3 Million

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

TORONTO, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- DeFi Technologies Inc. (the "Company" or "DeFi Technologies") (Nasdaq: DEFT) (CBOE CA: DEFI) (GR: R9B) (B3: DEFT31), a regulated capital markets platform for digital assets, today announced its financial results for the three months ended September 30, 2026. All dollar amounts in this press release are in U.S. dollars, unless otherwise stated.

Comment from Johan WattenstrÃ¶m, Chief Executive Officer of DeFi Technologies

"The third quarter demonstrated how our business can respond to improving digital asset markets. We reported $18.3 million in revenue, $7.0 million in operating income, and $22.3 million in net income, with trading gains and investment revaluations contributing materially to those results.

Valour's AUM increased approximately 62%, from $397.2 million at the end of June to $643.7 million at September 30. We generated $26.1 million in positive net inflows during the quarter, bringing net inflows for the first nine months to $48.9 million. Investors continued to allocate capital to our products through difficult market conditions, and I see that demand as an important foundation for growth.

We are expanding the ways investors can access our platform and the ways we can generate revenue. Since quarter-end, Valour launched eight additional ETPs, each carrying a 1.9% management fee. During September, we also launched Valour Funds SPC and its Smart Crypto Fund SP, extending our offering into actively managed strategies for professional and qualified investors, with additional institutional offerings in development.

Stillman demonstrates the contribution our broader platform can make. It generated $7.6 million in trading commissions through the first nine months, up approximately 22% year over year. We are building on that foundation by expanding its institutional reach and client relationships.

Looking ahead, we are optimistic that the lows of this bear market may now be behind us. If that proves to be the case, we believe DeFi Technologies is entering the recovery with a broader revenue opportunity than in previous cycles. Higher AUM can increase management fees and staking income on eligible assets, while greater trading activity supports our institutional business. With much of our supporting infrastructure already in place, revenue growth can translate into meaningful operating leverage and margin expansion.

Our financial position supports our ability to pursue that opportunity. At September 30, we reported $165.8 million in total shareholders' equity and approximately $159.1 million in balance sheet holdings and investments, compared with a recent market capitalization of approximately $130 million. Considering those resources alongside Valour's asset management platform, Stillman's profitable trading business, and the new offerings we are developing, we believe our valuation materially understates the value and earnings potential of the company.

I see considerable potential in the business we have built. With a wider product range, continued investor inflows, and more ways to generate revenue, I believe we can deliver substantially stronger results in a sustained recovery than in previous bull market cycles.

Our focus now is on attracting capital, expanding distribution, and bringing our new offerings to market. I am confident in our direction and excited about what we can achieve from this position. Our responsibility is to turn that opportunity into stronger earnings and long-term value for shareholders."

DeFi Technologies Inc. (Nasdaq: DEFT) (CBOE CA: DEFI) (GR: R9B) (Brazil B3: DEFT31) is a regulated capital markets platform for digital assets. Its subsidiary Valour, is a leading issuer of digital asset investment products, with more than 100 exchange traded products on major global exchanges and, through Valour Funds SPC, a fund platform for professional investors. Its trading business, Stillman Digital, provides institutional execution, liquidity and settlement. The Company also operates research and index capabilities and holds a private investment portfolio.
