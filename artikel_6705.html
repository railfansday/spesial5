The Vogue Business Beauty Trend Tracker

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Vogue Business Beauty Tracker, produced in collaboration with Spate, tracks the top five brands, trends and ingredients that are driving the most significant positive change in year-on-year growth that have also demonstrated consistent upward growth historically. Each week, we’ll spotlight a different beauty category and the top five beauty hashtags on TikTok.

At-home application and retro, graphic designs are among this month’s biggest nail trends. Searches for solid gel polish are on the rise, reflecting demand for a thicker, pot-based gel formula designed for more controlled application, while press-on sets remain popular for their reusability. White chrome powder, which creates a pearly, mirror-like finish when buffed over gel polish, is trending in time for the winter months, as well as geometric nail designs for their clean lines and shapes. Meanwhile, vintage nail polishes, including Urban Decay’s discontinued metallic foil shades, reinforce consumer interest in discontinued products that allow for a nostalgic aesthetic.

For nail brands, DIY manicure solutions, art-driven designs, and everyday nail tools are proving a success. The rise of Cyprus-based brand Doonails reflects demand for do-it-yourself manicure and pedicure products designed to deliver salon-quality results. Never Have I Ever reflects demand for reusable press-on nails featuring licensed artwork from independent artists and cultural icons, while Double Rhythm underscores interest in accessible soak-off gel polishes and nail art supplies. Heyhae offers sun-cured gel nail strips that harden naturally in sunlight without requiring a traditional UV or LED lamp, and Diamond Cosmetics signals continued interest in accessible nail products that offer variety and affordability.

Hair how-to content is ever-growing on TikTok, with creators twisting small sections of coily hair into a #coiltwist or #CurlsWithStraightener tutorials doing the rounds. Meanwhile, the rise in videos showing off #DarkBalayage points to demand for soft, dimensional color finishes on brunette and black hair, which offers a subtle look that minimizes root visibility. There’s also rising demand for lip stains that provide #AllDayLipColor, while #TestingMakeup content shows creators trying out new viral products in their full-face routines, sharing verdicts as they go.

The makeup products consumers are searching for the most are those combining precise application, long wear, and shimmering finishes. Tools such as winged eyeliner stamps take the stress and guesswork out of creating a sharp, even cat-eye look, while creamy lip liners offer buildable definition through soft, blendable formulas. Gripping primers and longwear lipsticks also reflect demand for staying power, from tacky primers that give makeup a sticky base to lip formulas designed to resist fading and transfer. Meanwhile, the rise of rose gold eyeshadow highlights interest in pink-toned metallic shades that add dimension to eye looks.
