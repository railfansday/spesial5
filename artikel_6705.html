Your December Horoscope by Zodiac Sign

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

We’re wrapping up the year with great momentum going into the new year.

December’s astrology helps you wrap the year up with some forward momentum! At the start of the month, the Sun continues its joyful journey through Sagittarius, putting you in a more adventurous mood and encouraging you to expand your horizons and perspective. It's a great time to get in a debate during a holiday dinner and stand up for what you believe in, but to also practice your active listening skills and remain open to the possibility that you could learn something from someone else. You’re zooming out and thinking more about the world around you and eager to soak up life’s experiences by trying new things and meeting new people.

You’re feeling all-or-nothing when it comes to love once Venus moves into Scorpio on December 4. For the next few weeks, Venus is retracing its steps that it took during the retrograde, so you might finally experience relief or turning points regarding some of your key relationships and contracts. This can help you navigate uncomfortable terrain in your close connections with honesty and vulnerability. You’re feeling funnier than normal once Mercury enters Sagittarius on December 6. When our communication planet moves through jokester Sagittarius, your conversations are a bit more playful but words can also sting from time to time. It’s easier to speak what’s on your mind in the moment and regret it later. You might also have a harder time finding a plan or strategy to reach certain goals, but right now you’re not meant to sort through the details. Focus on the big picture and soaking up new information and experiences instead. If you’re traveling, you might also deal with more snafus, so plan accordingly!

A New Moon in Sagittarius on December 8 helps you reconnect with a source of hope or optimism. Conversations feel expansive and information that you learn might inform your worldview in a new way. This energy is also helpful for reframing the way you speak to yourself and releasing limiting beliefs. You might find yourself eager to book some travel or sign up for a workshop to hone a skill or learn something new. You’re not afraid to set firm boundaries when Saturn stations direct in Aries on December 10. This will bring you back to July 26, the day the retrograde began. Similar themes and topics are resurfacing and asking you to be accountable for your own limitations or to set necessary boundaries with courage.

A few days later, on December 12, Neptune stations direct in Aries, helping you to dissolve your ego. This brings you back to July 7, and puts you in touch with certain topics and conversations that were a focus around then. You might feel more tender and sensitive, or feel a burst of creativity. This can help you channel your own creative energy for a higher purpose or ideal to make a positive impact in the collective. On the same day, Jupiter stations retrograde in Leo, bringing a crisis of faith. You could learn news or experience something that rattles your foundation or makes you question your own belief system. This can put you in a philosophical mood, asking you to reflect on whether you’re living in alignment with your beliefs. There might also be developments regarding teachers and mentors in your life, decisions about going back to school, or turning points regarding some of your goals and dreams.

The Sun enters Capricorn on December 21, marking the Winter Solstice in the Northern Hemisphere and the longest night of the year. From now on, the daylight will slowly begin to increase once again. But first, you must move through winter and preserve your precious energy in the process. This is not the time to overdo it or overextend yourself. It’s time to prune people, patterns, and situations from your life that extinguish your drive and essence instead of replenishing them. A Full Moon in Cancer on December 23, highlights your relationship with the past, primary caregivers, and security. There might be significant developments within your chosen family or perhaps you’re making a decision about where you live. People are in a more sentimental mood and the past can stir up major feelings and reflections. Be gentle with yourself and others.

The month wraps up with Mercury entering Capricorn on December 25, lending you a more structured approach to your ideas and communication style. You might find yourself navigating boundaries more or even find yourself speaking with a little more directness. There’s no need to sugarcoat to keep others comfortable, your time is limited and you’ve got things to do! This energy can be productive, help you problem solve, and stick with habits and plans that build toward your longterm success.
