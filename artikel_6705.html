Harry Shum Jr.’s Surprising Reaction to ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Elimination: “I Don’t Feel Sad”

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson were eliminated from Dancing With the Stars during the semifinals despite two flawless performances—including an emotional tribute to Harry’s late Glee costar Naya Rivera.

But, despite not making it to the finals, Harry says he feels at peace with the decision. “I oddly feel at ease. I don't feel sad—it's really weird,” he told E! News after getting eliminated. “I kind of felt like we accomplished everything we wanted to.”

Harry also spoke about dancing to Naya’s cover of “Valerie” by Amy Winehouse, saying the song “meant so much to so many people” and “It was a moment where you kind of saw the underdog be able to have the spotlight, and I always remember that feeling.” He also teased another routine from him and Jenna on their own terms, saying, “It would have been nice to do a finale, but I think we'll have our own finale in some sort of way, shape, or form in the future.”

The actor also took a moment to gush about dancing with Jenna, saying, “I want to make more art with her, and that's what it's really about. I like making art that people enjoy. So, to me, I felt that energy [in] the people that I did see and the people that really loved what we were able to do in this dance. I'm now a trained dancer because of her.”

Meanwhile, Jenna mentioned that they had an “epic” dance planned for the finale, saying, “That’s the part that I'm most heartbroken about. We had concocted this incredible journey that the ballroom won't see this time. But maybe we'll put it out there somewhere.”
