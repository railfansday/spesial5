Buy This Custom 1978 GMC Van, and Elvis Is Your Co-Pilot

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

With this GMC Vandura on Bring a Trailer, every drive is like a trip to Graceland.

If your baby has left you, and you need to find a new place to dwell, may we suggest this rocking custom van? It's a 1978 GMC G2500 Vandura that’s a full-on Elvis tribute, and it's just turned up over at Bring a Trailer (which, like Car and Driver, is part of Hearst Autos).

The custom-van craze of the 1970s will no doubt be studied by future archaeologists, and maybe you’ll be able to find a Chevy with an airbrushed unicorn on its side in a museum someday. The vans of that era were rolling hangout spots, an extension of the hippie-friendly appeal of the Volkswagen Type 2 into the domestic market, and their broad sheetmetal sides were the perfect canvas for art of all kinds.

You might expect a 1978 rock ’n’ roll tribute custom van to feature Led Zeppelin or maybe Foghat. However, there’s only one King of Rock ’n’ Roll, and even if Elvis had left the building by 1977, the creator of this van went all in on a rolling temple to their hero.

The exterior features porthole windows, a full-length airbrushed portrait of Elvis on both sides, musical notes, and record details. Out back, there's the King’s massively side-burned visage and his indisputable title “The King of Rock ’n’ Roll.” Chrome side pipes and five-spoke 15-inch chrome wheels finish out the look.

Powering this roving Graceland is a 350-cubic-inch small-block V-8 with a four-barrel Edelbrock carburetor. Power gets to the ground through a three-speed automatic transmission, and the odometer currently shows 42K miles. The original artwork dates back to the early 1980s, and the vehicle was comprehensively refurbished in 2019.

And, in case you have to ask, yes, it’s got an eight-track player for a stereo. Break out your collection of Elvis’s greatest hits and get ready to go cruise on through the night. One for the money, two for the show, three to get ready, then go, van, go.
