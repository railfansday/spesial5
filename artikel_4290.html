10 Pokémon You Could Take in a Fight

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Have you ever thought about the various types of Pokémon out there? There are some that are quite terrifying. After all, some of them are sharks, others are literal dragons, and there are even Pokémon gods.

Living in the world of Pokémon, you need to be able to defend yourself, and instead of using another Pokémon to fight your battles, you need to start throwing hands. So, I pose the question, “What Pokémon do you think you could take in a fist fight?”

Do you think you are able to beat up an Eevee? Maybe you could take down an Arcanine. Here are some Pokémon you would definitely stand a chance against.

Hear me out, but I think we could all easily take a Ditto in a fight. It has the ability Transform, but in battle, it only transforms into what it is currently fighting. Therefore, the Ditto would transform into you.

Do you think you could take yourself in a fight? I certainly think I could take myself in a fight. It wouldn’t be the easiest battle, but it is certainly better than dealing with a fox that can breathe fire or a mouse that can electrocute you.

You know your strengths and weaknesses, and so all you really need to do is use that to your advantage. Ditto doesn’t know anything about your weaknesses, so you have an advantage.

If it wasn’t obvious, Komala is based on the Koala Bear, and its Pokédex entries make it out to be one in the same. It eats poison leaves that only it can digest. This makes it tired, and it continuously sleeps.

It is said that Komala is asleep from the time it is born to when it dies, and that all of its movements are based on dreams that it is having. This makes it a prime target, and a Pokémon that you could easily take in a fight.

The only issue you may have while fighting this Pokémon is that it will thrash about wildly if you remove the log it is holding. Just don’t mess with its log and you will be fine. It will just think the fight was a dream.

Spoink is an adorable little Pokémon that nobody would ever hurt, but if it came at you looking to fight, you could easily take it. Its Pokédex reveals your entire plan of action, as it states that it needs to keep bouncing in order for its heart to keep beating.

All you need to do is knock it over. It might give you some trouble with its bouncing, but if you can get ahold of its tail, all you need to do is get its back to the ground and its heart will stop.

That is pretty grim, but you have to remember that it is survival of the fittest out there, and Spoink is one of those Pokémon that can easily be defeated with little prep time and some strong actions.

Pokemon isn't all about types and stats. With the right strategy and a bit of training, any Pokemon can be a force to be reckoned with.

Slowpoke is constantly the butt of the joke when it comes to the Pokémon being slow. Its reaction timing is so poor that you could probably land 20 punches before it even realizes what is going on.

Think about it. In the games, people eat Slowpoke tails. It is so common that there are even restaurants in Lumiose City that sell it for dinner every night.

I can only take that as a sign that Slowpoke is easily defeated and captured. How else would they be able to get so many of those tails? After all, you wouldn’t be able to do the same to a Pokémon like Totodile. It would immediately bite your face off.

Sudowoodo would be a terrifying Pokémon to fight, but its pre-evolution, Bonsly, would be an easy victory. Bonsly isn’t much of a fighter, preferring to rest for hours in arid climates.

Enemies don’t typically attack Bonsly because it exudes moisture from its eyes that look like tears. However, we can see through this deception, and that makes us a difficult foe for this Pokémon.

It is said that Bonsly can easily be defeated if it gets too wet, so all you really need to do is dump a bucket of water on it and it is done for. You just need to have no empathy while dealing with this Pokémon or you won’t be able to finish the job.

Some Pokémon are tiny and have barely any defense mechanisms. One such Pokémon is Caterpie. While it may be a cute bug, it is also a bug that you could easily take in a fight.

The only real issue here is that it has a defense mechanism that allows it to release a horrifically foul odor from its body, but if you bring some nose plugs, you will be perfectly fine. Maybe fight it while wearing a gas mask just to be safe.

Other than that, you don’t have to worry about anything from this Pokémon. It has a massive appetite for leaves, so all you really need to do is wait for it to start eating and jump it. Everything will be fine unless you are afraid of them like Misty is.

Milcery is a strange Pokémon, as its body is literally cream. Its Pokédex entry says that it is born from “sweet-smelling particles.” These particles condense into a cream that forms Milcery’s body.

Well, if Milcery is made of cream, that means you just need to eat your way to victory. That sounds like a simple victory to me.

The Pokédex entries give an idea about how this Pokémon fights. It says that Milcery just covers attackers in cream and runs away. In other words, find a way not to get blinded by cream, and you will have no problem taking this Pokémon out. You will even have desert for later.

This list covers the worst Pokémon from generations 1 through 9, and why they fail to capture the attention of players.

Fidough is adorable, there is no denying that one, but it also wouldn’t be difficult to take it in a fight. All it is known for, according to the Pokédex, is producing yeast with its breath to help baked goods rise.

It is also dependent on humans. One of the Pokédex entries says that it has been protected by humans for generations, so it probably doesn’t know how to defend itself without the help of a human companion.

Plus, its short and stubby legs are a bit of a disadvantage. This is a fight that you could easily win. Now, it might be a completely different conversation when Dachsbun is involved, but luckily, we are just talking about Fidough here.

You might see me calling Magikarp weak and pathetic and think that I am just being cruel, but believe me, I am only following what the Pokédex says. Some of the early Pokédex entries say that Magikarp is a weak and pathetic Pokémon that only flops about.

Magikarp is known for getting easily pushed around. It may have some tough scales, but all you need to do is bring a slightly strong weapon and you are golden. Maybe a few kicks would be enough.

During the fight, it will only splash about. Just watch for when it hits seven feet in the air, because that is the height limit for it. You may think the water would save it, but there are so many of them, you are bound to catch one easily.

There are some very weak Pokémon in the games and anime. For instance, Wishiwashi is very weak in its solo form, but I don’t feel like fishing for hours or jumping into the water. However, Sunkern doesn’t have that issue and it is also one of the weakest Pokémon in the game.

With 30 points in each stat and a base stat total of 180, it is amazing that Sunkern can even survive a single battle against any Pokémon. To further show how weak it is, its Pokédex entry says that it tries to move as little as possible to conserve energy.

With how little Sunkern likes to move and how weak it is, I bet anyone could take it in a fight. I’d be shocked if anyone lost to this Pokémon.

From Ultra Necrozma’s stat boosts to Volo’s grueling final battle, these Pokémon encounters bend the rules to deliver memorable challenges.
