French team: the last chance rally for Ibrahima Konaté?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Last season left its mark. Between the sporting difficulties encountered in Liverpool and personal trials, notably the death of his father, Ibrahima Konaté went through a particularly trying period. A difficult context which inevitably weighed on his performances, without him really managing to regain the consistency which had been his strength with the Reds a few seasons previously. Despite this, Didier Deschamps retained his confidence in him for the 2026 World Cup, even if the central defender was no longer considered an indisputable starter. And his rare appearances had not really reassured. His match against England, during the small final of the World Cup, was the most striking illustration of this. Overwhelmed by events, like a completely submerged French defense, Konaté was one of the symbols of the French shipwreck in the first period, which ended with a terrible 0-4. He was also replaced at the break by Dayot Upamecano, whose entry immediately made an impression. A contrast which further questioned the place of the former Liverpool defender in the hierarchy of the Blues.

But since then, the scenario has changed radically. Free from any contract after the end of his adventure with Liverpool, Ibrahima Konaté signed up with Real Madrid during the summer and seems to have definitively turned the page on his last complicated months in England. Under the orders of José Mourinho, the Frenchman quickly established himself as an essential element of Madrid’s defense. The figures speak for themselves: 13 appearances and 13 starts since the start of the season, or 1,170 minutes played. No other central defender in the squad has been used as much in this register. Solid in duels, valuable in the air and much more serene in his interventions, he gradually returned to the standards that had made his reputation. A renaissance that is also felt off the field. Since his arrival in the Spanish capital, the defender seems to have become even closer to his compatriots Eduardo Camavinga and Kylian Mbappé, with whom he is regularly seen at the table, just like Michael Olise. A new environment which seems to have allowed him to find serenity.

It now remains to transpose this excellent dynamic into the blue jersey. Because if Konaté has perfectly relaunched his career in Spain, he still has not had the opportunity to show Zinedine Zidane what he could bring to his defense. Summoned to the first rally in September, the former Leipzig player finally had to withdraw after a sprained right knee contracted in training. An absence which notably caused him to miss the new coach’s first four matches. This time, the meeting is therefore particularly important. Especially since the competition has seriously intensified during his absence. Jérémy Jacquet, who replaced him at Liverpool, made an impression during his first appearances with the Blues, with particularly convincing performances. Leny Yoro also took advantage of the opportunity to reveal himself, making a very solid debut. Two young defenders who are starting to establish themselves in the international landscape and who have no intention of giving up their place. With the absences of William Saliba and Maxence Lacroix, Konaté however has an additional opportunity to find a place. Alongside captain Dayot Upamecano, it is now up to him to demonstrate that he can regain starting status.

And his partner in central defense was not mistaken. Present at a press conference this Wednesday evening, on the eve of the trip to Italy, Dayot Upamecano was keen to underline the very good dynamic of his friend from Madrid: “Konaté has had an excellent start to the season. He is impressing at Real Madrid and I hope it continues like this. » A particularly positive statement from the man who could be his main partner in the axis against the Italians. It now remains to be seen what place Zinedine Zidane reserves for him in his plans. One thing is certain, the context is favorable to him. For Ibrahima Konaté, this November gathering could well mark the start of a new chapter in the France team.
