Sporting Lisbon 2-2 Man United: Mauricio Pochettino "so proud" of players following chaotic Champions League affair

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

By Matt Law | 25 Nov 2026 22:38, Last updated: 25 Nov 2026 22:41

Manchester United head coach Mauricio Pochettino has bemoaned the decisions that went against his side in Wednesday's 2-2 draw with Sporting Lisbon in the Champions League.

An incredible match saw three penalties awarded, while Man United had two goals controversially disallowed, one in each half of the European fixture.

Luis Suarez missed his first penalty of the night for Sporting before converting his second, while Bruno Fernandes also netted from the spot early in the second half.

A brace from Fernandes saw Man United turn the match around to lead 2-1, but a horror mistake from Red Devils goalkeeper Senne Lammens allowed Suarez to score his second of the game for 2-2.

Man United thought that they had won it late on when Sporting goalkeeper Kaique Pereira punched a corner into his own goal, but it was disallowed due to Patrick Dorgu's challenge on the 23-year-old.

The 20-time English champions also had a goal in the first half controversially chalked off, with Youri Tielemans penalised for what looked to be a fair challenge before Amad Diallo converted.

The result has left Man United, who had Tielemans sent off late on, in 19th spot in the Champions League table ahead of their final three games in the league stage against RB Leipzig, Bayern Munich and Villarreal.

"I was so proud of how my players handled such a tough game. Across the 90 minutes, the personality and character. It's difficult to find in one game where we were so punished," Pochettino told TNT Sports after the contest in Portugal.

"Only one week working together, but I feel so proud, I told them that. We have to concentrate and focus more, and recover better. With a performance like today, it's the best medicine for preparing for the next game.

"We need to believe more and more. We need to improve things, but that's what we need to show. In the second half we recovered the ball a lot in the opposition's half. That's the way we want to play."

Man United will be aiming to post their first win under Pochettino when they welcome Brentford to Old Trafford in the Premier League on Saturday afternoon.
