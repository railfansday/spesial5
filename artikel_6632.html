These Quick &amp; Easy Friendsgiving Recipes Are Perfect For Last-Minute Party Invites

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

If you or your friends are procrastinators, save these recipes.

Some people love to plan Friendsgiving weeks in advance with spreadsheets and personalized table settings, and others, well, let’s just say panicked group texts a few hours before dinner aren’t uncommon. While we’ll never be mad at a Friendsgiving invitation, late-notice parties don’t allow a lot of time to prepare time-intensive casseroles, apps, and sides. That’s why we’ve rounded up our favorite quick and easy holiday recipes that are perfect for when you’ve gotta run to a party, but still want to show up with something fun, festive, and delicious. These recipes either come together in under an hour (some under 30 minutes!) or use store-bought hacks that make preparing them a breeze. So if you get a party invitation for the next day, no need to show up with just a bottle of wine—these recipes will make you seem more prepared than the host (and you may just be!).
