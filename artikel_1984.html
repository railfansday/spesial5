Sean Steur was called up in the last international break for the Netherlands Under 21s, a very first time, thanks to his impressive early season Newcastle United form.

The 18-year-old then made his first ever appearance at that level for his country as the Netherlands played out a goalless draw at home to Norway on Friday 2 October, the teenager coming off the bench in the 83rd minute. Ironically it was Kees Smit he replaced, the AZ Alkmaar midfielder who United had been regularly linked with in the past.

A week later and Sean Steur started his first Netherlands Under 21s match, playing the full match and impressing as the Dutch U21s beat their Slovenia counterparts 4-2.

The Newcastle United teenager once again starting for the Netherlands Under 21s on Friday as they faced a crucial match.

In the absence of an injured Kees Smit, extra responsibility on Sean Steur in the first leg of this two-legged European Championship qualifying play-off against the Denmark Under 21s.

The 18-year-old not disappointing as he helped the young Dutch side to a commanding 3-0 win, playing the opening 78 minutes and as well as an outstanding all action display, also providing an assist for the third goal.

The Netherlands U21s now have one foot in the European Championships after this 3-0 win in Denmark, the return leg on Tuesday now looking all but a formality as they look to qualify for the finals of the tournament that will be held next year.

Also on Friday, it ended France U21s 1 Italy U21s 2. Newcastle’s Aladji Bamba playing the final 11 minutes of this friendly, France have already qualified for the 2027 European Championship finals.
