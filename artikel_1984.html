Interesting to see which Premier League clubs featured the most when it came to UK broadcasters selecting their matches across the 2025-26 league season, plus how it impacted on facility fees.

The information made public via the publication of the EPL’s official 2025-26 annual report.

This report included a breakdown of how much money each of last season’s 20 Premier League clubs banked.

In total, the 20 Premier League clubs received £3,142m for the 2025-26 season, this was a 10.9% increase on the 2024-25 season.

Banking the most were Arsenal with £198.1m, whilst Wolves received the least with £117.2m. The Premier League is actually one of the very fairest major European leagues in terms of the gap between which clubs get the very most and very least. Arsenal getting 1.69 x what Wolves received.

This is how many times each of the 20 Premier League clubs were shown live in the UK across the 2025/26 Premier League season:

34 Man U, Aston Villa, Forest 33 Arsenal 32 Man City 31 Newcastle United, Liverpool, Chelsea, Spurs 30 Crystal Palace 28 Leeds 26 Fulham, Everton 25 Brentford, West Ham 23 Bournemouth 22 Sunderland 21 Wolves 20 Brighton 17 Burnley

The facilities fees portion of the Premier League share out each season is overwhelmingly based on how many times each of the Premier League clubs were chosen for UK TV.

So last season Manchester United finished third in the 2025-26 Premier League table but received more in facilities fees (£24.om) cash than title winners Arsenal (£23.3m), Man U having 34 matches shown live by UK TV and Arsenal 33.

Wolves finished 20th last season with Burnley 19th, yet Wolves got £13.9m in facilities fees compared to the £11.5m that Burnley got, due to Wolves picked 21 times for UK live TV and Burnley only 17.

Sunderland finished five places above Newcastle United in the Premier League table (7th and 12th) but Newcastle got  £20.3m in facilities fees and Sunderland only £15.5m. TV chiefs showing Newcastle 31 times and Sunderland on only 22 occasions.

The number of times clubs are shown on UK Live TV is now significantly impacted for some clubs if they are in the Europa League or Conference League. Every Premier League match is now automatically shown on TV in the UK if not a 3pm Saturday game. The new TV deal saw that implemented from the 2025-26 season onwards and even if TNT Sports or Sky Sports didn’t originally select them, all the Premier League matches moved due to clubs’ Europa League or Conference League games are automatically shown on Sky Sports. This played a major part in both Villa And Forest shown 34 times of 38 Premier League matches last season on UK TV. So clubs such as Sunderland and Bournemouth are all but guaranteed to be shown more times in the 2026-27 season on UK TV than they were in 2025-26.

For Newcastle United so far this season, nine of the eleven Premier League matches that have been played have been shown live on UK TV, the exceptions Hull at home and Fulham away. The selections have now been made until the end of January 2027 by Sky and TNT, of these next 12 EPL rounds of games another nine Newcastle United matches are scheduled to be shown by UK broadcasters, that will mean after 23 Premier League games for Newcastle United, 18 of the 23 will have been shown. The upcoming exceptions are Ipswich away in December and then the home games against Fulham and Brighton in late January 2027.
