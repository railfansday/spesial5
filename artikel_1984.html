Dominik Szoboszlai has been far from his usual self during the 2026-27 campaign as Liverpool attempt to regain some momentum.

The 26-year-old already has one goal and three assists to his name, but he's only started seven of the Reds' Premier League games.

A consistent thigh problem has kept him sidelined for a few of the games. Yet, he's still managed to join up with the Hungary squad during the international break.

Despite feeling a discomfort in his knee in the warm-ups ahead of Hungary's 2-0 win over Georgia, he managed to complete the full 90 minutes.

Unsurprisingly, the Liverpool star had a key impact, assisting the second goal to ensure that his national team finished second in Nations League B Group 2.

Northern Ireland are up next on Tuesday, 17 November, and all signs point towards him captaining the side out once again.

Back at Anfield, Andoni Iraola would be hoping that, with Hungary definitely through to a play-off, Szoboszlai might be rested.

The latest on the Hungarian superstar, however, is that he's "ready to play," which points to yet another start for the 26-year-old.

Given Szoboszlai's fitness struggles so far this season, Iraola will have every right to be fuming if he sees the Liverpool star's name in the lineup.

The player himself even admitted that he was close to pulling out against Georgia. It is really not good news if he plays another 90 minutes.

Liverpool desperately need him back after the break, but only at his very best, which won't happen if he's forced to play through an injury.

Liverpool's midfield currently consists of Florian Wirtz, Alexis Mac Allister and Ryan Gravenberch, though the latter is slowly being replaced by Trey Nyoni.

It remains to be seen where Szoboszlai fits in anymore, despite being such a key character in Iraola's squad last season.

Wirtz has finally burst into life, ruling out the attacking midfield role for the Hungarian, unless his successor is forced out onto the flank.

As well as notching two goals and four assists for Liverpool this term, Wirtz also has four goals for the national team in 2026, three of which have come in the last four days.
