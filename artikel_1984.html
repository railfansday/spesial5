Sources: Aston Villa believe Johan Manzambi's panned teammate can become 'best in the world'

Aston Villa are "relaxed" over the future of new goalkeeper Zion Suzuki despite interest from Europe's elite clubs, sources have told Football Insider.

The 24-year-old, who is already being tracked by both PSG and Real Madrid, was one of many new arrivals at the Midlands giants in the summer along with star signing Johan Manzambi.

Nevertheless, Villa are not under any pressure to cash in on the Japan international, and are not concerned about the growing interest in him.

Sources say that Villa's staff believe Suzuki can develop into "one of the best goalkeepers in the world."

Unai Emery views the youngster as a key part of his plans to establish the Villans as a regular Champions League club.

Suzuki has had a mixed start in the Premier League, starting well before making a few high-profile errors in recent weeks and he has been panned by former Villa scout and ex-goalkeeper Bryan King.

Villa may not be worried by the interest from PSG and Real Madrid right now, but it is also not likely to go away any time soon.

The French champions came close to landing the shot-stopper when he left Parma in the summer, only for Villa to ultimately swoop in and get their man.

Real Madrid are searching for a long-term successor to Thibaut Courtois, and while a new goalkeeping addition is not an immediate priority for them, it is a clear target moving forward.

Suzuki's rising stock makes it almost inevitable that both clubs will continue to keep tabs on his interest.

This is likely to become a recurring issue for Emery and Co. as a result, even if they are not willing to consider selling him at this stage.

Former Villa scout Mick Brown told Football Insider on Tuesday that winger Alejandro Garnacho may already be "finished" at Villa Park.

The Chelsea loanee was left out of the matchday squad for the trip to face Manchester United last weekend, and has played just 34 minutes of Premier League football this season.

Brown believes that Garnacho has failed to impress Emery, and believes there are now serious question marks regarding his future.

Villa have an obligation to buy the wideman once he makes a certain number of appearances, but that feels a very distant possibility right now.
