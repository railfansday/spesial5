The Amazon Black Friday sales are in full swing, so now's the time to find a bargain on some essential tech. Crucial's P310 1TB SSD is down to £122.99 at the moment. A £77 reduction from the original list price of £199.99. However, the more realistic savings on this SSD are around £42, as the drive was most recently listed at a peak of £164 in early November. If you're a PlayStation 5 owner looking for a cheeky 1TB expansion drive for installing Grand Theft Auto 6, or for making some space to install future games, then this deal is worth a look.

Crucial's P310 SSD uses the Phison E27T controller along with Micron’s 232-layer QLC NAND. With sequential read and write speeds of 7,100 MB/s read and 6,000 MB/s write, the Crucial P310 isn't the fastest Gen 4 SSD you can buy, but it is significantly faster than older Gen 3 drives and a perfect fit for the PS5 expansion bay.

The Crucial P310 M.2 2280 SSD uses a Phison E27T controller and 232-layer Micron QLC NAND flash memory. This is one of the cheapest 1TB brand-name SSDs from a decent manufacturer available on Amazon. The P310 sports sequential read and write speeds of 7,100 and 6,000 MB/s, along with a 220 TBW endurance rating.

In our benchmark testing from our Crucial P310 review, we found that the Crucial P310 performed well, offering decent, all-round performance. In the chart below, you can see the drive sitting above even the Samsung 990 Pro in our PCMark Storage test.

SSDs, along with RAM and GPUs, have faced tremendous price rises in the last year, so any opportunity to grab some extra storage at a discount is not to be sniffed at. If you've recently grabbed GTA6 for your PS5, you're already aware that it takes up 210GB of your PlayStation's standard storage and doesn't leave much room for the rest of your digital games library. So if you want a little more space, you can take advantage of the expansion bay slot and install this SSD. Grab one while you can at this lower price.
