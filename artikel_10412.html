‘Enough Phone’ is an Android phone roughly the size of iPhone 13 Mini, launching in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Everyone says they want small Android phones back, and 2027 might be your chance to actually buy one. The “Enough Phone” has a mere 5.3-inch display, decent specs, and a price of $599 and less.

Launching in May 2027, the “Enough Phone” is a 5.3-inch Android phone that simply does want to be “enough.”

The company says the 5.3-inch LCD display comes in at 1520×720 and that the phone will be powered by a MediaTek Dimensity 7500 chipset, 8GB of RAM, and offers 128GB of storage. It’ll come with either “stock Android” or OdysseyOS, an Android skin used by SLEKE and designed to be minimal and act like a modern dumbphone. SLEKE currently sells repurposed Pixel phones running this software.

Android 17 will run out of the box with updates through 2032.

“Enough Phone” measures in at 132.8 x 63.8 x 12mm and will have a battery between 3,500 and 4,000 mAh which will be user-replaceable. While it’s thicker, the physical size is roughly the same as iPhone 13 Mini.

Rounding out the package would be a fingerprint sensor in the power button, a single 50MP (Sony IMX766) camera on the back, and a 32MP selfie camera up front.

The only redeemable aspect of this phone is the small display but nothing about it makes it a good value in the 2nd half of 2027 when you have much more capable phones like the Pixel A series for nearly the same money or a bit more but at least you're getting a lot more phone for your money.

The company says that these specs are “mostly final” and that pricing is planned for $599.

“Enough Phone” is funding on Kickstarter with $399, $449, and $499 early bird reservation tiers ahead of the May 2027 shipping estimate.
