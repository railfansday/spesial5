DeMellier’s Black Friday Sale Arrives Early

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Exclusively for Bazaar readers—now through November 19

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Black Friday is fast approaching, and DeMellier’s highly anticipated sale is arriving early—exclusively for Bazaar readers. Just in time to help you complete your Thanksgiving look or refresh your cold-weather outfits, there’s never been a better time to shop this editor-favorite label. Now through November 19, the luxury British bag brand is giving Bazaar readers exclusive early access to its 20 percent off sitewide sale with code BAZAAR20 before it goes live to the wider public on November 20. Stock tends to sell out quickly, so don’t wait to shop anything that catches your eye.

Use this opportunity to stock up on A-list and royal favorites like the classic New York tote, worn by stars like Katie Holmes, Reese Witherspoon, and Emily Blunt; the petite New York Shoulder Bag, carried by Anne Hathaway and Emma Roberts; and the cult-favorite Hudson, as seen on Meryl Streep, Kristen Bell, and Kate Middleton. Or consider investing in some of the brand’s latest launches, like the Brooklyn—a flap-bag version of the New York with an added interior zipper—or the roomy, folded-panel Sloane, which can transition seamlessly from the office to after-work drinks.

Along with the Midi New York tote, I own two sizes of the bowling-style Stockholm bag. The large, rich chocolate suede version has easily become my new favorite fall work bag, and I love styling it with the mini taupe leather crossbody for a practical, chic double-duty bag look.

DeMellier bags are known for their exceptional quality and craftsmanship, with most styles running in the $400-$500 range, and everything is under $900. With this exclusive offer, you can save more than $100. If you’re planning to shop only a few Black Friday sales this year, make sure to put this one at the top of your list.
