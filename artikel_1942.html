Leeds United condemn 'disgusting' racist abuse directed at Jayden Bogle as Tottenham Hotspur issue statement

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Leeds United have condemned “disgusting” racist abuse directed towards Jayden Bogle following their 1-0 win over Tottenham Hotspur on Saturday (November 7).

The 26-year-old defender caught the back of Mathys Tel’s ankle in the game, which forced the Spurs winger out of action. Bogle did not receive a red card and has since been targeted by racist abuse directed at him via social media.

Leeds have issued a strong statement to condemn the abuse, vowing to work with the Premier League and relevant authorities to identify those responsible.

A club spokesperson said: “Leeds United Football Club are appalled at the disgusting, racist social media abuse Jayden Bogle suffered following Saturday’s Premier League victory against Tottenham Hotspur.

“Leeds United condemns this behaviour in the strongest possible terms. There is no place for racism in football or society.

“The club will work with the Premier League and relevant authorities to identify the individuals and push for the strongest possible action to be taken against them.

“Jayden has the full and unconditional support of the club and his teammates, and we all stand with him at this time. Nobody should ever be subjected to racist abuse.”

Spurs have also issued a statement to condemn the abuse and have offered Leeds support with the identification process.

The club said: “We are disgusted to hear that Leeds United’s Jayden Bogle has been subjected to racist abuse on social media following our Premier League fixture at Elland Road on Saturday.

“Racism is abhorrent behaviour for which there is no place in football or society.

“We have offered Leeds United our support in identifying those responsible and we will pursue every available avenue to ensure they are identified and face the full consequences of their actions.

“Regardless of club allegiances and on-pitch results, no-one should ever stand alone in the face of this.”
