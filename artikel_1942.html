Liverpool XI vs Crystal Palace: Confirmed team news, predicted lineup and injury latest for Premier League

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Liverpool will hope to bounce back immediately from their loss to Fenerbahce when they visit Crystal Palace in the Premier League.

The Reds are back in action on Sunday, when they face a Palace side with less time to rest. Pierre Sage’s struggling team meet Hoffenheim in the Europa League on Thursday.

However, Iraola was reluctant to swap goalkeepers when manager of Bournemouth, despite calls from fans to drop Neto after some shaky performances.

However, the temptation to drop the fit-again Dominik Szoboszlai deeper and play Florian Wirtz in the No10 role could prove too much for Iraola.
