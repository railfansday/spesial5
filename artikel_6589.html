Beauty Marks: The Best Beauty Looks of the Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Welcome back to Beauty Marks: Vogue’s weekly edition of the best moments in celebrity beauty, from Vogue editors’ IG feeds, and all the glam of the fashion and pop culture landscapes. Each week, we curate the nail art to pin for your next nail appointment, new recruits to ‘Team Bob’, and major red carpet moments from the week’s most glamorous affairs. As always, it’s as much about celebrity beauty as it is about the makeup artists, hairstylists, and nail artists, as well as the creators crafting the trends you’re about to see everywhere.

A little flourish goes a long way. This week, the beauty set found new ways to make the smallest details count. Phoebe Bridgers punctuated her impossibly sleek platinum lengths with a single black bobby pin, while Nicole Warne brought a flash of pearlescent shimmer to the inner corners of her eyes. Chappell Roan, never one for half measures, took things a few steps further with an elaborate flame-red braid and icy blue lids. Elsewhere, Rosie Huntington-Whiteley showed off her sculpted cheekbone, Pauline Chalamet embraced the charm of a tousled bob, and Demi Lovato served up a golden honey-inspired manicure. Consider these the finishing touches worth stealing.

Scroll through the week’s best of celebrity beauty and pop culture’s glam below, and head to the app to vote for your favorite.

A little French-girl beauty for a night in Paris. Sarah Pidgeon paired luminous skin and softly defined eyes with a chocolate-stained lip, courtesy of Florrie White, who used Violette_FR’s Bisou Balm in Chocolat. Halley Brisker kept her strawberry-blonde lengths sleek and tucked behind one ear.

Rosie Huntington-Whiteley went full femme fatale at the LACMA Art+Film Gala. Hung Vanngo delivered smoky, elongated eyes, sculpted cheekbones, and a plush terracotta lip, while Danielle Priano pulled her hair into a severe, slicked-back updo. High glamour for that equally high Balenciaga collar.

Amanda Seyfried served a little mid-2000s blonde bombshell nostalgia at the Steps premiere. Renato Campora styled her buttery-blonde lengths into a dramatic side part, with silken, inward-curving ends falling across one shoulder. Makeup by Genevieve Herr.

Phoebe Bridgers’s icy platinum hair was positively angelic. Mara Roszak fashioned her lengths into a glassy, center-parted style, with softly curved ends and a single black bobby pin at the temple. Amber Dreadon supplied the delicate pink cheeks and fluttery lashes.

It's all in the inner corners! For a Paspaley Pearls event, Nicole Warne wore flashes of pearlescent white shadow against smoky taupe lids, rosy cheeks, and a muted mauve lip. Makeup by Soraya Boularras, with her dark hair drawn tightly back by Sophie Roberts.

A slick little bob and a peachy-pink flush: Meghann Fahy knows the power of restraint. For The Good Daughter premiere, Jacob Rozenberg combed her dark blonde hair straight back, with the ends flicking out at her nape. Shayna Goldberg added gleaming cheekbones, delicate lashes, and a rosy nude lip.

Demi Lovato’s manicure is looking positively delicious. Nail artist Natalie Minerva created this Manuka honey-inspired set, with molten amber, deep chocolate edges, and flashes of golden cat-eye shimmer. The glossy almond-shaped nails look almost good enough to eat.

Pauline Chalamet’s tousled brunette bob features piecey, eyebrow-skimming bangs and outward-flicking ends that frame her bright green eyes, while Tobi Henney added a generous flush of pink blush and a softly glossy lip. Perfectly romantic with her floral Zimmermann dress.

There's nothing low-key about Chappell Roan’s latest redhead moment. Hairstylist Virginie Pineda worked her fiery copper lengths into wild, undone waves and one gloriously long, thick braid. Frosted baby-blue eyeshadow, dramatic lashes, and a dusty rose lip by Dee Carrion gave the look the theatrical finishing touch.

A crown fit for a queen! For the Ebony Power 100 celebration, Chelley B wore an intricate braided updo by Joyboy Lace, with chunky twists woven around the crown and a long braid trailing down her back. Razor-sharp eyeliner, bronzed skin, and gleaming nude lips by Blessed By Saint brought the drama.
