Green Goddess Dijonaise

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

This herby dip is extra tangy with a generous addition of Grey Poupon Dijon mustard, sour cream, and lemon juice.

Dijonaise—a classic blend of mayonnaise and Dijon mustard—marries green goddess dressing for this condiment-turned-dip mashup. Tangy Grey Poupon Dijon Mustard, sour cream, and lemon juice provide extra oomph, and a trio of fresh, tender herbs—cilantro, parsley, and dill—add freshness. It couldn’t be simpler—everything is whirred together for a quick dip that’s perfect for dunking veggies.
