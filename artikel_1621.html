Chelsea hierarchy hand Xabi Alonso their full support

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The complete Chelsea hierarchy retain full faith in Xabi Alonso and trust the 44-year-old implicitly, according to Italian transfer guru Fabrizio Romano.

While there might have been a huge amount of excitement around west London following Alonso’s blockbuster arrival at Stamford Bridge over the summer, Chelsea suddenly find themselves at the heart of another public crisis.

Marking their return from November’s international break with a nightmare 4-1 drumming at home against historic rivals Leeds on Saturday, the Stamford Bridge support have made their frustrations abundantly clear.

Tumbling down the Premier League standings, Chelsea have now lost four of their previous five appearances across all competitions on either side of November’s fortnight interval.

Opening the 2026/27 campaign with their sole focus set on booking an essential Champions League return, pressure has started to mount on Alonso’s shoulders in west London.

However, Romano suggested on Monday that both the Chelsea ownership and football leadership team maintain full faith in the former Real Madrid boss and their focus is now on fixing their glaring defensive issues.

Hit for four against Leeds at Stamford Bridge over the weekend, Chelsea have already shipped 20 Premier League goals so far this season and the Blues have mustered just a pair of clean sheets from any of their 13 appearances against top-flight opposition across all competitions.

Alonso officially put pen to paper on a four-year deal in SW6 back in May and remarkably became the third permanent manager in west London in the space of a calendar year.
