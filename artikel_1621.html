Report: Liverpool learn asking price for in-demand forward

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Liverpool are in the process of rebuilding their squad, and they have made some big acquisitions.

Florian Wirtz then Alexander Isak both arrived for British record transfer fees, and then last summer Bradley Barcola became the third player to arrive at Anfield for over £110 million.

The work isn't done yet though, and Julian Ward still has plenty to do to return Liverpool to the very top of English football.

There are several elite players currently being linked with Merseyside moves, but the Reds will face tough competition to bring in all the targets they've shortlisted.

A new report has revealed how much Liverpool will have to pay to get one of their top targets in the summer of 2027.

One of the most exciting targets linked with an Anfield switch is Antonio Nusa, RB Leipzig's electric winger.

Capable of playing on either wing, Nusa is capable of playing on either wing and has recorded six goals and eight assists in 15 appearances this season for Leipzig.

The Norwegian forward also scored for his country against Wales on Saturday night, putting Norway 1-0 up with a strong finish from the edge of the area.

Now, according to an update from Ekrem Konur, the Reds have been told that it will take a bid of €80 million (~£67m) for the 21-year-old's signature next summer.

Liverpool are not the only club listed as a potential landing spot for Nusa though, with Juventus, Arsenal and Tottenham all also chasing a deal for the Leipzig winger.

It seems unlikely that Liverpool would add another wide player when so many are already at the club, so it could take a sale to get a deal for Nusa over the line.

If Nusa can continue his current run of form then €80 million will be a bargain fee come the summer and Liverpool should start laying the groundwork for a deal as soon as possible.
