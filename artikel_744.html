Chris Pratt “Would Love” to Make a Fourth ‘Guardians of the Galaxy’ Film With James Gunn “If He Wanted to” Amid DC Exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

"I love working with James [Gunn]. He’s the man. I haven’t had a chance to speak with him since that news came out," the actor said of Gunn's exit from DC Studios.

Chris Pratt “would love” to make a fourth Guardians of the Galaxy movie if James Gunn wanted to revisit the Marvel film franchise after his exit from DC Studios.

“That would be awesome,” Pratt told MovieZine when asked about the potential of a fourth film. “I love working with James [Gunn]. He’s the man. I haven’t had a chance to speak with him since that news came out… it’s interesting, because anytime something like this comes up and you say it, it kind of could become a headline.”

“So all I’ll say is I love James. He directed all three [Guardians] movies, and that’s pretty rare in those types of trilogies to have the same director through all three, so if he wanted to come back and bring it back, man, I would love that,” he added. Pratt starred as Star-Lord/Peter Quill in all three Guardians of the Galaxy films for Marvel alongside Zoe Saldaña, Karen Gillan, Dave Bautista, Vin Diesel and Bradley Cooper. Pom Klementieff joined the cast in its second installment.

Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 released in 2023, and was meant to be the final film in the Guardians franchise. Before the movie came out, it was revealed that Gunn and Peter Safran were set to become co-chairs and co-CEOs of DC Studios. It was announced last Monday that the duo will be replaced by Marvel alum Jeremy Latcham, who will now serve as president and chief creative officer of DC Studios.

Gunn and Safran were presumed safe from being removed from their posts at DC Studios amid the merge of Paramount and Warner Bros., though Skydance co-CEOs David Ellison and Ynon Kreiz opted to bring Latcham in.
