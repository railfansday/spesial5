Ariana Grande Recalls ‘Wildest Place’ She’s Heard Her Music Playing: ‘Getting a Bikini Wax’

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The star told the story of the slightly awkward encounter while promoting new movie Focker-in-Law.

Ariana Grande has a funny story about the time her song started playing mid-bikini wax.

She recalled the slightly awkward encounter in a new video segment with Focker-in-Law co-star Beanie Feldstein ahead of the comedy’s Nov. 25 opening in movie theaters. Grande (who plays Olivia Jones in the film) and Feldstein (Samantha Focker) sat down for a casual on-camera art session/Q&A with Harper’s Bazaar in which they painted pictures for each other.

“What’s the wildest place you’ve heard your own song playing?” Grande was asked mid-interview.

“Yeah, I was one time getting a bikini wax,” she answered without hesitation, as seen in the video clip below.

“Yeah, I was, and this song came on,” continued Grande, who this week was nominated for four Grammys, including a song of the year nod for “hate that i made you love me.”

“I didn’t think she knew who I was at all,” added Grande of the woman waxing her. “I never assume anyone does, and she was, you know, doing her work at her job and my song came on.”

The pop star/actress reenacted the esthetician’s reaction to unexpectedly hearing an Ariana Grande song play while waxing Ariana Grande, and the intimate little moment they shared through facial expressions.

“And she was like, ‘And my kids love your show, by the way,'” Grande recalled, sharing that her own response was, “Oh, perfect.”

“You know, it was just very, it was wonderful. I love women and being a woman. It was very bonding,” she said.

Focker-in-Law, the latest installment of the Meet the Parents franchise and the family hijinks to come when the Focker and Byrnes families are involved, reprises Robert De Niro’s role as Jack Byrnes and Ben Stiller’s as Greg Focker opposite Grande, who’s introduced as the fiance of Greg’s now-grown son, Henry Focker (Skyler Gisondo). Feldstein joins the cast as Greg’s daughter.
