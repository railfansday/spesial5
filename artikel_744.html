Price out in Players Championship Finals first round in night of upsets

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Report as Grand Slam champion Gerwyn Price, World No 2 Gian Van Veen, and No 1 seed Wessel Nijman all knocked out in first round at Players Championship Finals; watch the World Darts Championship at London's Alexandra Palace, live on Sky Sports from December 11-January 3

Three of darts' biggest names were subject to shock upsets in the Players Championship Finals as Grand Slam champion Gerwyn Price, World No 2 Gian Van Veen, and No 1 seed Wessel Nijman were all knocked out in the first round at Minehead.

Price was beaten by 6-4 Sebastian Bialecki - the first time the Pole had beaten the Welshman in his career - while Jeffery de Graaf won two legs on the bounce to consign van Veen to defeat by the same scoreline.

Nijman was upset by 20-year-old Henry Coates despite posting an average of 110 - the second-highest average in Players Championship Finals history.

World No 4 Luke Humphries, meanwhile, battled through his recurring back injury to secure a routine 6-3 victory against Darryl Pilgrim, while Michael van Gerwen withstood Connor Scutt to secure safe passage with a 6-3 first-round win of his own.

Beau Greaves also progressed to the second round with a scrappy 6-3 victory against former Lakeside champion Christian Kist despite a mediocre average of 89.64, and will face Rob Owen in the next round.

Van Veen, Nijman and Price joined James Wade, Gary Anderson, and Rob Cross as six of the top 25 players in the world were eliminated at the first opportunity in the last major before the World Darts Championship, which is live on Sky Sports from December 11th.

Wade was beaten 6-4 by Justin Hood and 2014 winner Anderson slipped to a 6-4 loss against Karel Sedlacek, while Cross fell to a thrilling 6-5 loss to Mickey Mansell.

Van Veen - who finished runner-up to Price at the Grand Slam - struggled from the outset and throughout his match, managing only one maximum and checking out a lowly 15% of his finishes.

The Dutchman had three darts at double 16 to keep himself in the tournament, but failed to level the scores at 5-5 before de Graaf ruthlessly produced a 112 checkout to produce the first upset of the round.

Price, fresh off his first major win in four years after three successive final defeats, was heavily favoured against 22-year-old Bialecki, and looked to have rediscovered his tournament-winning form at the perfect moment when he produced a stunning 120 checkout to peg the deficit back to 5-4.

But Bialecki kept his cool and threw for the match, claiming his first career win against Price at the seventh attempt. The Pole will face off against de Graaf in round two.

The status quo was somewhat restored when Humphries and van Gerwen produced characteristically routine wins against lower-ranked opponents, with Humphries silencing doubters by averaging 93.32 in a week dominated by coverage of his injury.

But the show was stolen by Coates. The Derby native overcame his opponent's stellar average by notching an incredible 85.71% checkout success rate to claim victory in only his fourth-ever televised game.

Elsewhere, Stephen Bunting edged into the next round after beating German Max Hopp in a last-leg decider, while Kevin Doets averaged 107.34 in his win over Ritchie Edhouse and Chris Dobey came from behind to beat Mike De Decker 6-4.
