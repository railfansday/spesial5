Jennifer Lawrence’s ‘Hunger Games’ Premiere Look Proves the Boudoir Coat Is Back: Here’s Where to Shop Similar Styles Online

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Variety may receive an affiliate commission.

At the premiere of “The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping” on Monday night, Jennifer Lawrence embraced one of the biggest outerwear trends this season: the fur-trimmed satin coat.

The actress, who reprises her beloved role as Katniss in the prequel film, walked the carpet in a floor-length black silk coat from Jonathan Anderson’s Dior Pre-Fall 2026 collection. The robe-like design featured an oversized white fur collar, colorful floral embroidery cascading down one side and a dramatic front slit, which Lawrence paired with black satin mules by Gianvito Rossi and vintage diamond-and-onyx earrings from Fred Leighton. The boudoir-inspired silhouette, which combines silky fabrics, sweeping hemlines and oversized fur collars, has become a favorite among designers and celebrities alike. London-based designer Conner Ives helped popularize the look with his demi-couture silk robes, first introduced on his Fall 2025 runway and subsequently worn by Zoë Kravitz, Sienna Miller, Hailey Bieber and Kendall Jenner. Lawrence herself wore one of Ives’ black fur-trimmed designs at the “Die, My Love” premiere in London last year.

The trend extends well beyond Ives. Miu Miu incorporated fuzzy fur trimming into distressed leather outerwear for Fall 2026, while Dries Van Noten embraced satin finishes on its runway. Balenciaga, Zuhair Murad and Stéphane Rolland have also explored dramatic, robe-inspired silhouettes in their recent couture collections.

While Lawrence’s Dior creation and Ives’ silk robes carry luxury price tags, shoppers don’t have to spend thousands to get a similar look. More affordable retailers, including Zara, Mango and ASOS, have embraced exaggerated faux-fur collars, belted silhouettes and floor-sweeping coats.
