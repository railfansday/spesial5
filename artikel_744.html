From Inde Navarrette’s Stunning 40-Carat Diamond Necklace to a Surprise Role Model Performance: Inside David Yurman’s VIP Dinner Party at Chateau Marmont

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Celebrity jeweler David Yurman hosted a dinner at Chateau Marmont Tuesday night to celebrate the reopening of his flagship boutique on Rodeo Drive.

The evening began with a cocktail hour where Inde Navarrette – wearing several David Yurman pieces, including a 50-carat diamond and 40-carat tanzanite necklace in white gold – sipped a martini and chatted with Eiza González. “Off Campus” star Stephen Kalyn posed for photos with Yurman and his artist wife Sybil before snapping some shots with his fiancée Victoria Lovatsis.

Yurman offered brief remarks during dinner. He thanked actor Neal McDonough for their friendship. Yurman recalled meeting McDonough for the first time, saying he got the same feeling he did when he met Roy Rogers at Madison Square Garden when he was just nine years old. When a guest asked what the secret was to his and Sybil’s 59-year marriage, Sybil cracked that her husband was a “good kisser.”

The evening was capped off by a surprise performance by Role Model. Yurman took to the stage at about one song in to reprimand some chatty guests who didn’t stop talking during the singer’s set. “This is absolutely disrespectful,” he said, adding, “Just be quiet and listen. Absorb the vibration. Absorb the beauty of this man.” Later, as Yurman and Sybil danced to “Sally, When the Wine Runs Out,” Navarrette, Kalyn, González, Tyriq Withers and Emily Bader applauded and cheered them on.
