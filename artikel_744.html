Governors Awards 2026: Glenn Close, Ridley Scott and More Receive Honorary Oscars From The Academy (Live Updates)

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

After a weekend of flight delays, Hollywood’s 2027 awards season is finally ready for take-off.The Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences is kicking off the Oscars race in Los Angeles with its 17th Governors Awards, a major campaign stop for contenders looking to mingle with Academy governors representing its 19 branches. Creatives behind sweeping spectacles like “The Odyssey” and “La Bola Negra” to intimate dramas like “Wild Horse Nine” and “The Debut” will walk the red carpet before the star-studded ceremony, where some of Hollywood’s most accomplished veterans will receive long-overdue accolades from their peers.Glenn Close, who has earned eight competitive acting nominations over her career (four for Best Actress, another four for Best Supporting Actress), will finally receive an Oscar recognizing her decades of awards-caliber performances. Ridley Scott, a three-time Best Director nominee for “Thelma & Louise,” “Gladiator” and “Black Hawk Down,” will not attend in person but will accept his honorary Oscar through a prerecorded video.Also receiving an honorary Oscar is animator Floyd Norman, best known for his work on Disney classics including “The Jungle Book” and “Sleeping Beauty,” while Killer Films producers Christine Vachon and Pamela Koffler will receive the Irving G. Thalberg Memorial Award.
