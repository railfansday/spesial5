Pistons vs. Magic Discussion: Game Time, TV, Odds, and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Jalen Duren remains out as he works his way back from a hamstring strain. That's unfortunate because I am desperate to see what this team looks like with Duren back in the fold and eager to leave behind the John Collins starting-center era for good. In Detroit's most recent loss against the Atlanta Hawks, they were outrebounded by 19 and outscored in the paint 27-6. Tonight, they face the Orlando Magic. Orlando has righted the ship a bit, winning five of its past seven games. They win by protecting the paint, and that has been Detroit's Achilles' heel since Duren went down. They don't let you score inside, and their defense is top 5 in limiting second-chance points, paint points, and fast-break points. Detroit is not going to find a clear path to easy points, which means their half-court offensive execution must improve significantly over their recent outings.

The Pistons are coming off of an epic fourth-quarter meltdown against the Hawks. Detroit went from leading by 11 entering the final quarter to giving up a 18-0 and getting blitzed 33-12 in the final frame. The Magic, meanwhile, are licking their wounds after losing by 20 to the struggling Washington Wizards. It's also an NBA Cup Game, if you care about that kind of thing.
