4 key matchups to watch in Cincinnati Bengals vs. New Orleans Saints

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The 7-3 Cincinnati Bengals will host the 3-7 New Orleans Saints. The Bengals better not be overlooking another team under .500 after the performance they put on the field against Washington this past week.

While the Saints haven't had the season they were expecting, they are like everyone else in the National Football League and can spring the upset on any given Sunday.

Let's take a look at some of the key matchups to watch for in this one.

Chase Young is having an incredible season for the New Orleans Saints. He is up to 42 pressures and 8 sacks in the 2026 season. While the defense as a whole has been underwhelming for New Orleans, Young and Carl Granderson are two pass rushers that can get after it at a high level. Granderson doesn't have as many pressures as Young, but he has 8.5 on the year. Both guys will be given opportunities to match up against Orlando Brown Jr. this week.

Orlando Brown Jr. has been playing really good football, but he is still the guy that all pass rushers would rather face instead of Amarius Mims for the Bengals. Brown has allowed just 3 total pressures with 0 sacks over his last two games. He will need to continue playing at that level when facing off against Chase Young and Carl Granderson this week.

This should be a big test again this week for the secondary. Turner and Hill will be tasked with going up against Chris Olave and Jordyn Tyson. Olave is a former first round draft selection who has over 850 receiving yards this season. Tyson was the Saints' first-round draft selection this year. While he has dealt with injuries throughout parts of the season, just 2 games ago he put up 100 receiving yards in a game. After what rookie Antonio Williams just did against this secondary, these receivers could be a big problem for Cincinnati this week.

It has felt like Turner is trending in the wrong direction the last couple of weeks. If he wants to get paid like one of the best corners in the league, he needs to start playing like it. The same goes for Dax Hill. With both guys up for potential big contracts in free agency, now is the time they start putting more consistent performances on the field.

The New Orleans Saints are ranked 27th in run defense so far during the 2026 season. The Saints are allowing 129.8 yards per game rushing this year. Meanwhile, the Cincinnati Bengals are ranked 30th in rushing yards per game offensively. They are averaging only 79.4 rushing yards per game. Something has to give in this one.

Chase Brown is coming off one of his worst performances of the season. He caught 5 passes for 30 yards, but he had two crucial drops as a receiver. He also had just 6 carries for 6 yards, which is an abysmal 1.0 yards per carry. The Bengals need a better performance from their top running back, or questions will continue about Samaje Perine potentially taking on a bigger role in the offense.

Kaden Ellis is the leading tackler for the Saints. The veteran linebacker is effective at rushing the passer as well. That should mean that when he blitzes, we will see him versus Chase Brown, but we will also see that matchup with Brown rushing and receiving the ball out of the backfield.
