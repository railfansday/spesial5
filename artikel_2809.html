Los Angeles Chargers news: Bolts earn ‘B’ grade despite loss to Jets

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Chargers started on a lower note than the Jets on Sunday and they never quite were able to flip the script, even if the game eventually came down to the final minutes.

Justin Herbert, for the first time this year, threw multiple touchdown passes in the same game while the entire offense avoided turning the ball over altogether. Unfortunately, for the first time against a lowly opponent, Chris O'Leary's defense faltered. They were down starting players Daiyan Henley and Tarheeb Still, and it seemed to show as Geno Smith and Co. put up 341 yards and 27 points.

Even with the loss dropping the team to 1-9 on the year, CBS Sports' John Breech still gave the Chargers a respectable "B" grade for their performance. Here's what he had to say:

"The Chargers defense might have finally hit rock bottom. They didn't force the Jets to punt a single time and if you don't get any stops, that forces your offense to play perfect football and the Chargers just don't have the offensive talent to play perfect football this year. The offense was good against the Jets, but it could have been better if the offensive line could have protected Justin Herbert. The Chargers QB got sacked six times, but the offense was still able to move the ball thanks to a suddenly revived rushing attack that went off for 158 yards, the team's second-highest total of the season. The Chargers' lost season has somehow gone from bad to worse."
