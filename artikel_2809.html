Bhayshul Tuten returns to practice, has no injury designation for Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Jaguars are set to have their top running back in the lineup against the Giants on Sunday.

Bhayshul Tuten missed practice on Thursday because of ankle and foot injuries, but he was back for a limited practice on Friday and he has no injury designation for the weekend. Tuten has 148 carries for 609 yards and five touchdowns this season. He also has 18 catches for 206 yards and three touchdowns.

Center Robert Hainsey (knee) was ruled out early in the week and the Jags announced on Friday that defensive end B.J. Green (forearm) will not be activated from injured reserve.

Defensive tackle Matt Dickerson (neck) and defensive end Danny Striggow (concussion) are listed as questionable for the trip to New Jersey.
