Josh Jacobs a limited participant at practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Green Bay Packers RB Josh Jacobs (finger) was limited during practice Sunday, Nov. 22.

Why It Matters: Fantasy players will need to monitor the situation to see if Jacobs has a chance to play in Week 12. He will see most of the work at running back if he plays and can be a No. 2 fantasy back. Kaleb Johnson and MarShawn Lloyd should share playing time if Jacobs misses another game and can both be No. 3 running backs or flex options.
