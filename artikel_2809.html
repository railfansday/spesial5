Aaron Rodgers claims he was ‘barred’ from Steelers facility in shocking text to Al Michaels

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Aaron Rodgers claimed he was "barred" from the Steelers' facility, leading to his decision to take his side of the divorce with the team and head coach Mike McCarthy to "The Pat McAfee Show" on Wednesday, according to Prime Video play-by-play broadcaster Al Michaels.

Michaels, citing a text from Rodgers early in the fourth quarter of the Steelers' game against the Broncos on Black Friday, also read a portion of Rodgers' message regarding the discussion about the "sanctity of the locker room," which "exists to provide the integrity of the locker room — not to provide immunity from accountability for its leadership."

It's unclear when specifically in the timeline of events Rodgers was barred from the facility. He didn't show up to a team meeting Wednesday morning, but the Steelers described it at the time as an excused absence.

Aaron Rodgers of the Pittsburgh Steelers reacts against the Philadelphia Eagles in Philadelphia on Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. The Eagles won 19-16. Getty Images

Hours later, Rodgers went on McAfee's show — a platform on which he has appeared numerous times dating back to his years with the Packers — and accused McCarthy of sleeping in meetings, breaking down a halftime huddle with 1-2-3 Packers and skipping walkthroughs. McCarthy acknowledged the locker room gaffe, which occurred Sunday at the end of halftime in Philadelphia, but didn't specifically address the sleeping allegation when addressing reporters Wednesday.

The Steelers have remained steadfast in their support of McCarthy, who had them on the brink of moving to 7-4 with a commanding lead against the Broncos on Friday.
