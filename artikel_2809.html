Cardinals downgrade Carson Beck to out; Josh Johnson will start

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

As expected, Cardinals rookie quarterback Carson Beck won’t be making his second career start on Sunday at Kansas City.

Beck has been downgraded from questionable to out, due to back spasms.

He’ll be replaced by Josh Johnson, who will be making the 12th career start in a career that dates back to 2009. Johnson has thrown 412 career passes, with 242 completions, 2,669 yards, 14 touchdown passes, and 18 interceptions.

He’ll be the fourth quarterback to start a game for the 3-7 Cardinals, joining Jacoby Brissett, Gardner Minshew, and Beck. Brissett has since been traded to the Ravens and Minshew is out for the rest of the year with an elbow injury.

The Cardinals enter the game as 15.5-point underdogs. The spread was as high as 16.5 points this week.
