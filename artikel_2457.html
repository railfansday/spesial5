Confirmed lineups: 1. FC Köln vs Bayer Leverkusen

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Köln host Bayer Leverkusen in the Bundesliga with a bid to maintain their unbeaten record in five home games this season in all competitions.

Contrastingly, Bayer Leverkusen have lost three of their last four games in all competitions including a 1-0 loss to Marseille on Thursday ending their perfect Europa League campaign.

Köln will see the return of Mikey Moore to the starting lineup this weekend with a three-back formation being utilised once again by Rene Wagner.

Bayer Leverkusen start with young star Ibrahim Maza against Köln.

Schwäbe - Schmied, Simpson-Pusey, Krauß - Sebulonsen, Maina, Okon-Engstler, Moore, Sosa - Bülter, El Mala

Flekken - Quansah, Tapsoba, Medina, Gutiérrez - Fernández, García - Moreira, Tillman, Maza - Schick
