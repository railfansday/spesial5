Confirmed Europa Conference League Lineups: Trabzonspor vs. SC Freiburg

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

SC Freiburg face Trabzonspor in the third matchday of the UEFA Europa Conference League away from home. Julian Schuster's side lost their first away fixture in Monaco, while Thomas Reis’ in-form side won both games with the help of Mohamed Salah's goals.

Here are the lineups for Thursday evening fixture at the Papara Park.

Trabzonspor: Onana; Pina, Nwaiwu, Ozkacar, Eskihellac; Fabinho, Mendy; Salah ©, Muci, Saviolo; Onuachu

Freiburg: Backhaus; Kübler, Ginter, Lienhart, Makengo; Eggestein ©, Engelhardt; Irie, Goto, Grifo; Matanovic
