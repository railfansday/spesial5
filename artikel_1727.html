Simeone defends ‘incredible’ Alvarez amid more jeers from Atletico fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Diego Simeone insisted Julian Alvarez retains his full support after the Atletico Madrid forward was subject to more boos during their Champions League win over Viking.

Alvarez scored a superb free-kick in the 24th minute of Atletico's 2-0 victory at the Metropolitano on Wednesday, with Alexander Sorloth also on target in the first half.

The Argentine became the second player to reach 25 goal involvements (19 goals, six assists) in the Champions League while playing for Madrid, after Antoine Griezmann (56).

Since joining Atletico in 2024-25, Alvarez has also scored more direct free-kick goals (three) than any other player in the Champions League, but that did not save him from a mixed reception among the home supporters.

When Alvarez was replaced by Giuliano Simeone in the 57th minute, a section of the Atletico supporters jeered him off the pitch amid a failed move to Barcelona in the summer.

The 26-year-old admitted he wanted to join Atletico's LaLiga rivals, but Alvarez was refused the chance to leave, but Simeone remained firmly behind his player.

"I have a lot of respect for him. I don't have to downplay my responsibilities," Simeone said. "I trust him, I believe in him, I love him as a person, and I defend him as a footballer.

"I'm happy with him. He's an incredible player, a really good guy, I like him as a person. I'm going to support him as a footballer. I understand him as a player.

"In previous matches he didn't score goals, but all the attacking plays go through him and to get where we want we need the best Julian Alvarez."

— Atlético de Madrid (@atletienglish) November 25, 2026

Atletico have now won 10 of their last 15 Champions League games against sides outside the big five European Leagues (D3 L2), scoring 44 goals.

It was an important victory for Simeone's side, who bumped themselves up to 18th in the 36-team standings and within three points of the top eight in the league phase.

Atletico have won 11 of their last 14 home group stage games in the Champions League (L3), scoring 2+ goals in 11 of these matches, with Alejandro Grimaldo impressing.

Grimaldo followed up his goal from Sunday's win over Getafe with an assist for Sorloth's opener, while also finishing with a joint-team-high total of chances created (two).

"He's good for the team. We were talking to Alejandro about how he might not face much competition," Simeone said of Grimaldo.

"The truth is, his standard hasn't dropped. He's giving us so much. Let's hope we can look after him a bit better and that he continues to give us everything he's got."

But Grimaldo was keen to focus on the result rather than his own performance, with Atletico facing PSV, Bodo/Glimt and Fenerbahce in their remaining league phase fixtures.

"We needed the win. I was able to help the team, and that's what matters," Grimaldo said. "We faced some very tough opponents early on, and we knew this was a crucial day.

"Our goal is a long-term one. I'm happy. I've wanted to come here for years, and things are going really well."
