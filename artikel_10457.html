Shark Poop DNA Could Revolutionize How Scientists Study Shark Diets

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

What if studying a shark’s diet did not require cutting it open? A new study found that DNA collected from cloacal swabs can reveal shark prey with surprisingly similar results to stomach samples, offering researchers a faster, minimally invasive way to understand what sharks eat.
