Prince William Will Allegedly Push to Strip Sussexes of Titles in Secret Christmas Meeting They Aren’t Invited To

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The royals are gathering in Sandringham for their annual Christmas festivities, and apparently Prince William has...plans. Specifically, plans to convince King Charles to strip Prince Harry and Meghan Markle of their titles now that they are once again living in England.

“William believes this has gone on long enough,” a source tells Rob Shuter. “Harry cannot move back to England, keep his royal titles and pretend he has nothing to do with the monarchy. His position is simple: Britain or the titles. He cannot have both.”

In other words, William thinks that Meghan and Harry should only get to keep their Duke and Duchess of Sussex titles if they live in America (or elsewhere outside the UK). Meanwhile, Harry and Meghan aren’t spending Christmas in Sandringham, which means they won’t be part of these discussions—but apparently the royals will present them with whatever plan they come up with. Per the source, William doesn’t want to become king with this “mess still hanging over the monarchy,” and thinks “The family needs to agree on a way forward. There will be no more half in, half out arrangement.”

Meghan and Harry seemingly haven’t seen the royal family since moving back to England in August, but will allegedly also be spending the Christmas holiday in England. A source tells Rob Shuter, “They want to show that royalty doesn’t need a palace, a grand ballroom or an extravagant banquet. They want to be seen as modern, accessible and connected to everyday people. This is the direction they’re taking.”
