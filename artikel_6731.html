Quick Refresher on Ariana Grande’s Full Dating History Over the Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Including Dalton, Ethan, Pete, Mac, and Ricky’s surprise round two.

Ariana Grande has been in the spotlight since she was a teenager, meaning her private life has never exactly been all that private. Ariana has been pretty open about sharing her relationships over the years, and they’ve been the source of inspiration for some of her best songs (“thank u, next,” anyone?).

Check out everyone Ariana has dated—from her ex-husband Dalton Gomez, to her whirlwind Wicked showmance with Ethan Slater, to her second-chance romance with Ricky Alvarez.

Graham and Ariana starred in the Broadway musical 13 and apparently started dating while working together. They’re still friends, and Graham came to Ariana’s defense during Doughnut Gate, saying, “She’s such a sweet girl. It’s tough. We all say things that we regret. I don’t think it speaks to her character at all.”

TBD how often Graham and Ariana hang out these days, but they had a 13 reunion back in 2016:

And in 2019, they were spotted going to celeb hot spot Carbone.

Ariana briefly dated Victorious backup dancer Jordan following her split from Graham, with their romance lasting just about a year before they split in February 2012.

At the time, Ari told Just Jared Jr. that her “heart was broken and shattered,” adding, “I was crying through hair and makeup, like I couldn’t even pull myself together.”

After briefly dating in 2012 (side note: Jai accused Ari of cheating with Nathan Sykes, which she denied), these two got back together in 2014 only to break up again.

A source told Us Weekly, “It has not been an easy few months. Jai just wasn’t as supportive as he could have been.”

Ariana dated The Wanted’s Nathan Sykes in 2013—around the time their song “Almost Is Never Enough” (which I definitely still fully listen to on repeat) debuted.

According to People, Ariana confirmed their split in January 2014, saying they ended things because of distance. The ’ship doesn’t appear to have made too much of a lasting impression on Ariana, because Nathan was not name-dropped in “thank u, next,” which he told TMZ was “good.”

Meanwhile, a source told Us Weekly, “They made the decision to part ways because their conflicting touring schedules would keep them apart over the next year.”

And, yes, for all of you wondering, Big Sean was indeed name-checked on “thank u, next,” when Ariana sang, “Thought I’d end up with Sean / But he wasn’t a match.”

Ricky and Ariana dated during her aforementioned Doughnut Debacle and got close when he was working as one of her backup dancers. A source told E! News re: their split, “She broke up with him. He didn’t cheat or anything. They just found it difficult to make it work. They are still friends though.”

According to Victoria Monét, the song “Moonlight” is about a romantic night Ariana and Ricky shared.

“That’s what Ricky called her one night. I think it was after their first kiss,” she told Billboard. “He wanted to kiss her for a long time, and she was really impressed. He’s such a gentleman, and the song is a great little bookmark of the start of their relationship.”

P.S. Here’s Ricky reacting to the “thank u, next” lyrics “Wrote some songs about Ricky / Now I listen and laugh.”

Ariana and Mac Miller dated for two years before parting ways in May 2018.

After Mac’s tragic death in September 2018, Ariana told Vogue in 2019 that “by no means was what we had perfect. He was the best person ever, and he didn’t deserve the demons he had. I was the glue for such a long time, and I found myself becoming...less and less sticky. The pieces just started to float away.” Over the years, she’s penned songs alluding to their relationship, from “Better Off” to “Ghostin.’”

Who can forget the whirlwind relationship that was Pete and Ari? The matching tattoos! The Starbucks cups! The lollipops! This!

It was all a lot! But after a fast-moving few months that included an engagement, Pete and Ariana parted ways in October 2019.

A month later, he told Paper magazine, “I don’t ever make public statements about relationships ’cause I just don’t think it’s right, you know? I usually express how I feel about anything through work. So I hope she’s well. I hope she’s very happy. And that’s pretty much it. And print doesn’t usually age well.”

Ariana and Social House’s Mikey Foster sparked dating rumors thanks to their vibes in this music video:

But it was short-lived, and they ended things in early 2020—before Ariana started seeing Dalton Gomez. Which brings us to...

Ariana and Dalton got together right around the start of the COVID-19 pandemic, and by December 2020, they were engaged. The two then tied the knot in May 2021 at her Montecito home, and all seemed to be going well...until just about two years later, when their split was announced.

TMZ broke the news that Ariana and Dalton had been separated since January 2023 and were “heading toward divorce” in July 2023, with sources saying distance (at the time, Ariana had been in England filming Wicked) played a big part. In September 2023, the couple officially filed for divorce.

To the shock of many/all, TMZ reported that Ariana was dating her Wicked costar Ethan Slater shortly after her split from Dalton Gomez went public. According to Us Weekly, Ariana and Ethan got together after Ethan broke things off with his wife Lilly and after Ariana ended things with Dalton.

A source at the time explained, “Ariana and Ethan are dating. However, things are fairly new. Ariana and Dalton split in January, and Ethan is separated from his wife. Ariana and Ethan have only recently begun seeing each other but they have a lot of fun together and are enjoying each other’s company.”

A whirlwind relationship followed, though in October 2025, breakup rumors began swirling. Still, it wasn’t until June 2026 that TMZ broke the news of their split, with a source claiming they parted ways “quietly” a few months ago.

Which brings us back to Ricky! Soon after the Ethan breakup news dropped, Ari started quietly changing the Ricky lyrics in “thank u, next” during her Eternal Sunshine Tour. Several sightings of the two together followed until, eventually, the couple showed up at the Focker-In-Law premiere together, making clear they are very much back on.
