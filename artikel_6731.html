Rosalía Wins With Her Style at the 2026 Latin Grammy Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

She wore a quartet of designer looks from Matières Fécales, Erdem, Vivienne Westwood, and Dior

Rosalía won big at the 27th annual Latin Grammy Awards last night. For her fourth studio album, Lux, the Spanish pop singer took home her third trophy for Album of the Year, becoming the first woman in the history of the ceremony to ever do so. She collected a total of five awards, coming out on top in other major categories like Song of the Year, which was awarded to her track “La Perla” (featuring Yahritza y Su Esencia), and Best Short Form Music Video, which went to her visual for “Sauvignon Blanc.” And to coincide with such a major night, Rosalía brought out a few major designer looks, all styled by Chloe & Chenelle Delgadillo.

To start the evening, the “Berghain” singer hit the red carpet in the avant-garde label of the moment, Matières Fécales, which has been embraced by everyone from Zendaya to Rihanna and Demi Moore.

Courtesy of the label’s Spring/Summer 2027 collection, Rosalía’s angelic white gown featured a ruched tulle bodice with semi-sheer folds that hugged her body and culminated in the house’s signature déchiré detailing. The tattered fragments arched from Rosalía’s shoulders like wings, while also dangling from the ends of her cropped sleeves. Down low, there was equal drama courtesy of a dramatic tulle bubble skirt that trailed around her in a voluminous cascade. Of course, her signature halo-dyed hair acted as the perfect complement to the heavenly ensemble.

Later, Rosalía hit the stage to perform a medley of songs from Lux, including “La Perla,” “La Rumba del Perdón,” and “Dios Es un Stalker.” For her performance, she wore a bespoke satin gown from Erdem, pulled from the house’s Fall/Winter 2026 collection. (Just last month, she turned to the same London-based label for a stunning balletcore street-style moment.)

The golden-hued design featured a corset-style bodice and knotted bows along the neckline, with a basque waist and flared ruffle skirt that was overlayed with black tulle and trimmed with ostrich feathers.

Once she returned to her seat, it turned out Rosalía had another surprise look in store, as she changed into a custom white dress from Vivienne Westwood. The bridal-inspired design featured a strapless, plunging sweetheart bodice decorated with a tiny bow in the middle, while layers of fabric were draped along the waist for a subtle peplum effect.

All of the fabric, meanwhile, was embroidered with floral lace details and microscopic pearl embellishments, and the singer wore a scarf to match, while slipping into a pair of contrasting black pumps.

To finish her quartet of looks, Rosalía switched into a custom black Dior dress by Jonathan Anderson, who, fittingly, also helped design many of the looks for the Spanish superstar’s recent Lux Tour.

The silky black slip featured two delicate lace straps that hung off Rosalía’s shoulders, while the bust tied into a knot that dangled in front like a tassel. The top half of the dress was decorated with subtle floral details, while the skirt featured panels of semi-sheer material. It all worked perfectly to serve as an inky backdrop as Rosalía posed with her stack of gramophone trophies.

As she accepted her prize for Album of the Year, Rosalía got teary-eyed, saying: “This is the third time I’ve won this award. If El Mal Querer was a dream, and Motomami was a bold gamble, then Lux has been a leap into the unknown. I believe it’s very important to speak your own truth—and for others to be able to speak theirs—and this record is mine. I thank God, and I thank my loved ones: my family and my friends. Thank you for your love and for shaping me into who I am today. The truth is, I know I still have a lot to learn, and I’m certain that I likely haven’t even created my best work yet.”
