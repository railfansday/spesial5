Paris Hilton’s New Old Navy Jingle Jammies Collab Is a Pink Holiday Dream

It’s nearly impossible to think about the holidays without thinking of Old Navy Jingle Jammies. The brand has become a beloved go-to for cute, festive, and affordable seasonal sleepwear, dropping a fresh lineup each year that people can’t get enough of. With so many fun designs and cozy styles, there’s something for every theme and aesthetic. This year, Old Navy is taking things up a notch with a special collaboration with Paris Hilton. And yes, they’re pink. (And yes, there’s even something for your dog, so they can match, too.)

Whether you’re dressing up for a holiday party with your besties or coordinating a family look for the annual holiday card, these pajamas are a playful alternative to the traditional winter palette. We also got the chance to chat with Paris about everything from the design process and collaborating with her longtime friend Zac Posen, Old Navy’s Chief Creative Officer, to how she’s celebrating the holidays this year.
