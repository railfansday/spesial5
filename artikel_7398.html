Philadelphia whips up bigger holiday marketing spend amid brand revamp

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Let Marketing Dive's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

Brand character Phillyboy returns in a Santa-like role to dish out blocks of cream cheese to be used in cheesecake recipes.

“Really Philly Good,” which debuted in February, was developed to broaden consumer perceptions of what Philadelphia has to offer. Rather than only showing the product as a cold spread for bagels and snacks, the platform highlights how cream cheese can be used to enrich more involved meals. A larger marketing push around Q4 responds to the insight that one-third of Philadelphia cream cheese bricks are sold during the November-December period and Google search trends data that show spikes in interest for cheesecake recipes during the season.

In the new work, Phillyboy acts as a Santa-like figure, trotting into houses saddled on his dairy cow to dole out blocks of cream cheese that are then used to make cheesecake. The rugged Western-themed character is accompanied by a spin on Christmas standards like “Jingle Bells.” Agency Johannes Leonardo is behind “Really Philly Good” while Nalle Sjöblad, from Spark & Riot, directed the holiday ad spots. The full media plan spans TV, streaming, connected TV, online video, social, creators and retail elements.

Beyond video advertising, Philadelphia is running a national integration with “Happy Hour with Ina Garten” that is supported by sponsored social content and a custom cheesecake recipe on the Barefoot Contessa website and Substack. Other social and digital activations include a cheesecake-building experience on Pinterest, an augmented reality Snapchat lens and a focus on TikTok search and trending food and drink Reels content on Meta.

In addition, the Kraft Heinz marketer enlisted TikTok and Instagram creators, including Tineke “Tini” Younger and Kiley O’Donnell, for holiday cheesecake content. The creator program started on Nov. 2 and has so far drawn nearly 1 million organic views and 43,000 engagements, the brand said.

Kraft Heinz is broadly revamping its portfolio as it tries to win back consumers who have shifted toward protein-rich and better-for-you offerings. In August, it announced plans to spend an additional $100 million on its turnaround, with the lion’s share of the investment concentrated in marketing. Marketing will represent at least 6% of the company’s net sales this year.

Jell-O, another brand under Kraft Heinz, unveiled a new creative platform on Monday that is backed by its largest marketing investment in more than a decade. Johannes Leonardo also handled “Oh Hello, Jell-O,” the shop’s first work for the brand.
