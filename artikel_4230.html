8BitDo Reveals Its Own GameCube Controller For Switch 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

8BitDo, known for its work in providing excellent retro-inspired controllers for modern systems, has announced the GC Bluetooth Gamepad to celebrate the GameCube's 25th anniversary. It's currently scheduled to launch on 10th December 2026.

Compatible with Switch, Switch 2, and Windows, the GC controller maintains the core shape and layout of the iconic GameCube pad, but adds in a bunch of modern upgrades like TMR sticks and Hall Effect triggers. Priced at $49.99, it undercuts Nintendo's official offering by $20 and is available for pre-order now.

Colours include purple and orange, which are arguably the most recognisable from the original release (though we're hoping for a Wavebird-inspired silver variant in the future, too). The pad also features single-rotor rumble to replicate the feel of the original, while a new 'ZL' button – which also features on the official Nintendo GameCube pad for Switch 2 – has been designed to be symmetrical to the 'Z' button on the right.

While we do have a dedicated capture button, there unfortunately doesn't appear to be a 'C' button for chat purposes. It's also not clear at this stage whether the GC controller will have wake-up support for Switch 2, but 8BitDo has generally been pretty good at including this, even if it's not necessarily supported immediately at launch.

Here's an official list of features from 8BitDo:

- Classic GC design and iconic button layout - TMR joysticks with octagonal gates - Hall Effect linear triggers - Single-rotor rumble - Symmetrical Z and ZL bumpers - Capture button for screenshots and recording - Turbo function - Support for multiple simultaneous gamepad connections

Finally, it's worth noting that if you purchase the 8BitDo Retro Receiver for GC, you can use the new controller – or indeed the official GameCube pad – with original hardware.
