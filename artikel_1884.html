Chelsea fans given Estevao reality check, Brazil performance paints worrying picture

Chelsea fans have been eager to see more of Brazilian winger Estevao.

The young talent has been sporadically used by manager Xabi Alonso, with his common formation not having room for wingers.

Instead, Morgan Rogers and Cole Palmer have often been used as two wide number 10s.

Nonetheless, Estevao is regarded as one of the best wonderkids in the world, and to many, Alonso's treatment of him has been strange.

Over this international break, the Chelsea ace has been called up by Carlo Ancelotti, with a chance to impress away from Stamford Bridge.

But in Brazil's latest game, his statistics are worrying, and seemingly paint a picture that Alonso has been right in his usage of him.

If Estevao was hoping his time with Brazil could change his stature in Chelsea's squad, he looks to be proven wrong.

Alonso will be looking for consistency from the teenager if he is to earn a regular place in his starting XI.

His talent is unquestionable, but his performances suggest he still has plenty of development to go through before he can be fully trusted.

As per FotMob, Estevao struggled to impose himself against Japan, with the Chelsea winger's stats being quite poor from an attacking sense.

The 19-year-old attempted two dribbles during the game, but on both occasions he failed to successfully complete them.

Additionally, he had just 30 touches in his 58-minute performance, with only three of those coming inside the opposition's box.

Estevao did look to score plenty as well, taking three shots at goal, with one of those being a big chance missed.

A 25 per cent success rate in his ground duels also encapsulated his performance, with the Japanese defenders seemingly able to deal with him quite well.

With a performance like that, he will not have done much to move the needle back in London.

Estevao is no doubt talented, but at 19 years old, Chelsea fans must remain patient with him.

He is not quite at the consistent level to produce the goods, even if he does show flashes of his quality.

Chelsea are a team on the rise and need definitive results, which attackers Rogers, Palmer and Joao Pedro can provide.

Until Estevao can prove that delivering consistent contributions is part of his skill set, Alonso is well within his rights to continue to snub him in his starting XI.

There is a role for Estevao at Chelsea, but perhaps not yet.
