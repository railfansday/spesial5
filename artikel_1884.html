‘I worked really hard’ - Leeds United’s Noah Okafor details fitness battle after brace against Chelsea

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Noah Okafor notched a stylish brace as Leeds United dismantled Chelsea - and later paid tribute to the club’s medical staff for getting him fit.

There were plenty of stellar displays as the Whites ran out as 4-1 winners at Stamford Bridge yesterday (November 20).

Okafor was among Leeds’ star men and scored two of their four goals, first firing across Emiliano Martinez to find the bottom corner and then hammering home from an Ethan Ampadu across.

He now has three goals across his last two games, but it was not long ago the winger was being gnawed at by an injury.

The 26-year-old said: “The last two, three weeks I was struggling a little bit with my adductors and I worked really hard.

“Also a big compliment on the medical staff, like they put me back as quick as possible. I have full trust from the manager, from the whole team, from the whole players. They know my qualities.

“I try to help them with full intensity, with my potential, with my game, how I beat the opponent how I score. Like this, I can help the team. And yeah, when I have the confidence, I can perform. I’m happy about my goals, that I can help the team with this.”

Okafor is among Leeds’ brightest attacking outlets and has a penchant for rising to the big occasions. However, he is yet to complete 90 minutes in a Premier League game this season.

He said: “I just have to keep fit, train hard, keep smiling - and then I can help the team every game.”

Okafor’s late goal gave Leeds a 1-0 win over Tottenham Hotspur prior to the international break and the Whites picked up where they left off in the capital.

The wideman’s goals came either side of a Dominic Calvert-Lewin penalty and Brenden Aaronson notched the fourth late on. Prior to the win over Spurs, Leeds had blanked across back-to-back games.

He said: “It means a lot. I think the hard work in the last days, in the last couple of weeks [paid off]. In some other games, we weren't able to score. That's football.

“We did a lot of draws, so we trained a lot in front of the goal. Today was a back-to-back win and that's why we celebrate.

“We know we have quality up front and also quality behind. We are a strong team. We have a strong mentality. We work hard. And today we gifted ourselves with this [win].”
