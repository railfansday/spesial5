Key midterm election results

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Heading into Election Day, the fight for both the Senate and House had expanded into traditionally Republican territory. Inside Elections with Nathan L. Gonzales rated 16 Senate races and 82 House races as competitive.

CQ Roll Call is tracking the results in these battleground races, as they are called by The Associated Press. The results also include seats that weren’t considered on the battleground but flipped. The AP projects Democrats to win majorities in both chambers.
