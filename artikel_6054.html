Hilton’s Cyber Week Bonus Is Worth Just $8—But You Can Stack Three Offers For 10,000 Points On A Single Night

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Hilton Honors will give you 2,000 bonus points per stay if you book by Tuesday, December 1. I value Hilton points at 0.4 cents apiece. That’s $8 for a night at a Hampton Inn or $8 for a week at a Conrad. Book directly with Hilton. The booking window opened November 24, 2026, for stays completed November 25, 2026 through April 2, 2027. There’s no registration required or cap. (Hat tip to Head for Points.) For stays through December 31, 2026, register for More Points to Play before checkout. It adds 1,000 points at most hotels or 3,000 at listed luxury, lifestyle, resort and all-inclusive properties. With both offers that’s 3,000 points ($12) or 5,000 ($20). Award stays qualify for the fall offer. Hilton’s Cyber Week terms aren’t explicit on this but don’t exclude them. Book a Hilton Sale rate and pay the whole bill with a U.S. Hilton American Express card and you can earn another 5,000 points once per cardholder (book by December 22, stay by April 2). Book by December 1 and check out by December 31 to combine all three offers. That’s 8,000 bonus points ($32) at most hotels. At a property on the higher bonus list, one night can earn 10,000 bonus points ($40) before you count the usual hotel and card earnings. In the U.S. and Canada, the sale rate takes up to 20% off Hilton’s best available rate and requires booking at least seven days ahead. At most Hilton and Marriott brands, $100 in eligible room charges earns 1,000 base points. That’s $4 back at Hilton against about $6 at Marriott. Back in 2010, Hilton’s Jeff Diskin explained that Hilton got too little credit for what it gave every member and so promotions let them drive business. For years Hilton offered double points almost all the time. That lifted the return at most brands from 4% to 8% before elite or card bonuses. This year two months went by between the summer offer ending August 15 and the fall one starting October 15. Hilton seems to run promotions less frequently than they used to, but promotions are core to the program’s earning proposition being competitive. It’s worth repricing refundable Hilton stays booked before November 24. The fall offer already covers existing reservations, so it’s the Cyber Week add-on that’s worth considering (I wouldn’t bother rebooking if the room rate has gone up).
