Moment Lakers coach knew starter was ‘in the zone’ en route to career night

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The eight points scored in each of the first, second and third quarters provided hints that Quentin Grimes was on his way to having a special night, with his 24 points entering the fourth on Tuesday night already being a season high.

But it was a key sequence early in the final period of regulation of the Lakers' eventual 118-114 overtime win over the Hornets at Crypto.com Arena when coach JJ Redick knew the sixth-year guard was "in the zone."

Quentin Grimes knocked down 10 3-pointers during the best offensive game of his career. NBAE via Getty Images

With the Lakers down 99-87 with 9 ½ left in the fourth, Grimes trailed Sandro Mamukelashvili as he was dribbling up the court in transition.

Recognizing who already had the hot hand, and with the Hornets having four defenders inside of the 3-point arc to cut off a drive to the basket, Mamukelashvili pitched the ball back to Grimes, who instantly rose up with the ball and knocked down a catch-and-shoot 3-pointer to cut the Lakers' deficit to nine points and give him 29 points for the game.

And on the Hornets' ensuing possession, the former Houston Cougar poked the ball loose from Ryan Kalkbrenner's hands while he was trying to initiate offense near the 3-point line for one of the Hornets' eight turnovers during the fourth.

The Lakers needed every bit of Quentin Grimes' scoring barrage to beat the Hornets. Getty Images

Ziaire Williams grabbed the ball after Grimes forced the steal before passing ahead to the 6-foot-5 guard, who took one dribble before knocking down a 29-footer for his first 30-point game as a Laker to cut the Lakers' deficit down to six points with nine minutes left in the fourth.

"The second one in transition," Redick said, "That was, 'Okay, this is, this is different'."

Grimes scored 15 more points in the fourth after that sequence, including five crucial points in the final 20 seconds of the quarter, to help the Lakers come back from down 18 points to force overtime and two more points in the extra period.

His scoring barrage not only gave the Lakers their ninth straight win, but also gave Grimes a new career high of 47 points.

Grimes felt the momentum shift in the Lakers' favor after the back-to-back 3s he hit early in the fourth.

Quentin Grimes of the Los Angeles Lakers drives to the basket against the Charlotte Hornets during his career night. NBAE via Getty Images

"I feel like we were chipping away, chipping away, getting stops and I was able to hit a 3 and then get the steal and then you get the crowd going, you can feel the energy start to change," he said.

"You can feel the other team start to shift away from their game plan and just, I shot it. I was in rhythm, pulled it pretty deep in transition and then those are the kind of shots that can turn the game in the right direction for us."

After a slow start to the season when he didn't always shoot when he was supposed to, Grimes didn't hesitate to let it fly Tuesday night.

"Honestly, I'm super, super proud," Collin Sexton said. "I would say he's been in one of those places like, 'should I shoot or should I not shoot?' And each and every day, we all been telling them, 'let it fly. We believe in you. We know how good you are. We know what you can do.' And sometimes it's good to have that togetherness, and also your teammates putting that battery [on] your back."

And on Tuesday night, Grimes had his teammates' back with the performance of his career.
