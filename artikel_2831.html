Why Indianapolis Colts didn’t even ‘consider’ offers for Jonathan Taylor ahead of trade deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Indianapolis Colts were rumoured to be heavily interested in trading Jonathan Taylor, but passed on making a move before the trade deadline.

Taylor is set to stay with the Colts until the end of this season, and the franchise wasn't able to agree on a trade in time before the deadline.

However, this wasn't because the Colts had a change of heart and wanted to keep Taylor on their roster for the foreseeable future.

According to NFL insider Tom Pelissero, the Indianapolis Colts received multiple trade offers for Jonathan Taylor ahead of the trade deadline.

They received offers which included second-round picks and more draft assets, but they decided that Taylor was worth more and wanted a blockbuster offer to part ways, which didn't come before the trade deadline.

Taylor is considered one of the strongest RBs in the NFL, so it makes sense why the Colts would want to hold off and wait for a better offer.

However, given the massive trades the Colts have already made, they run the risk that Taylor's trade value will plummet due to fewer opportunities on the field in the remaining games this season.

Shutting him down for the season would have a similar effect, as it would lead to some executives questioning whether he is still at the level he was at prior to the trade deadline.

For the Colts, they must do everything in their power to make sure that Taylor's value stays the same or improves in the remaining games with some strong performances.
