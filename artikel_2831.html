How did former Colts WR Josh Downs perform in Chiefs debut?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

How did the now-former Indianapolis Colts wide receiver Josh Downs perform in his debut with the Kansas City Chiefs?

Downs was traded from the Colts to the Chiefs last Tuesday and was already playing a key role on Sunday against Atlanta.

Easing into his new team and playbook, Downs played only 10 of the Chiefs' 64 offensive snaps. However, when on the field, Downs was targeted heavily.

Downs was targeted five times and caught four passes for 53 yards with a touchdown. He was third on the team in targets, receptions, and yards, despite playing so few snaps.

ESPN's Ben Solak believes that Downs can have a major impact on the Kansas City offense.

"Eight routes, five targets, four receptions and a touchdown," Solak wrote. "Now that's efficiency. I thought Downs looked so skilled as a receiver; he always has, but especially against the backdrop of the Chiefs' alternatives at the position, his reliability is already apparent."

The Colts traded Downs and a 2027 seventh-round pick to the Chiefs for a 2027 fourth-round pick and 2027 fifth-round pick in return.
