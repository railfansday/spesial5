Titans WR Calvin Ridley involved in incident at Nashville International Airport

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Tennessee Titans receiver Calvin Ridley, who has been the subject of recent NFL trade talks, was involved in an incident at Nashville International Airport this weekend, the team confirmed to The Tennessean on Nov. 8.

In a statement, the Titans said they are aware of the incident and communicating with Metro Nashville Airport Authority.

The Titans were off this week. Coach Robert Saleh has a regularly scheduled call with reporters Nov. 9.

Ridley has six catches for 91 yards in seven games. He's considered a potential trade option, with the NFL trade deadline approaching Nov. 10.

He led the Titans with 64 receptions and 1,017 receiving yards in 2024 but never returned to that form. In 2025, he caught 17 passes for 303 yards and no touchdowns.

Ridley's four-year, $92 million deal is set to expire after the 2027 season.
