Chiefs News: Cardinals preparing to start 4th QB in 4 weeks

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Kansas City Chiefs are 16.5-point favorites at home against the Arizona Cardinals in Week 11, and that number could grow once the game-day inactives are finalized if Arizona is down starting quarterback Carson Beck (back spasms).

Beck is questionable, and the next man up would be 40-year-old journeyman Josh Johnson, who signed with the team earlier this month.

With Carson Beck injured, Cardinals preparing to start fourth different QB in 4 weeks | The Athletic

In a Friday appearance on the "Wolf & Luke Show," general manager Monti Ossenfort said Beck suffered back spasms about halfway through Thursday's practice. The Cardinals limited Beck the rest of the session and held him out of Friday's workout. Ossenfort did not see this as a long-term issue.

"I don't think so," he said. "We're just concerned right now here with the next 24 to 48 hours. We're giving Josh the reps and getting him ready to play. Carson dealt with this a little bit earlier in the season, and in his role at the time, we were very safe with what we asked him to do and took our time with it. We'll see how it goes here."

NFL Makes Announcement on Chiefs Rookie Emmett Johnson | Yahoo! Sports

The NFL announced that Emmett Johnson has won its weekly honor. The league also uploaded a video of his Week 10 highlights on Instagram.

Johnson has outplayed his draft status and making a name for himself as a rising star. Kansas City will continue to rely on him as the season progresses, especially with his ability to contribute as a running back and receiver.

Injuries to Drake Maye and Puka Nacua Lighten the Challenge of the Chiefs' Upcoming Gauntlet | Sports Illustrated

Without Maye, New England's offense is led by backup quarterback Tommy DeVito—an obvious downgrade for the 5-5 Patriots.

Meanwhile, Rams wide receiver Puka Nacua certainly won't be on the field against the Chiefs in Los Angeles one week after Thanksgiving due to an injury and suspension. Without getting into the details of the (injury/suspension) designation, the already-injury-riddled Rams will be missing at least one key weapon when they host KC.

Veteran receiver Davante Adams could potentially return in time to face the Chiefs, but that's far from a certainty. On the defensive side of the ball, former Chiefs cornerback Jaylen Watson has been ruled out for the rest of the year due to a shoulder injury.

Recalling some great moments in Arrowhead Stadium history as Chiefs prep for move | The Kansas City Star

Some top moments since the venue opened in 1972:

The most unforgettable game in Arrowhead history came on Jan. 23, 2022, when Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes hit Travis Kelce for an 8-yard overtime touchdown to beat the Buffalo Bills 42-36 in the AFC Divisional Round — a finish that later won an ESPY as ESPN's "Game of the Year."

The venerable stadium just east of downtown also set a Guinness World Record in 2014, when crowd noise hit 142.2 decibels during the Chiefs' "Monday Night Football" rout of the New England Patriots.

Colts waive QB Daniel Jones in puzzling cap move, turn to Anthony Richardson in Week 12 | The Athletic

Jones, 29, has struggled all season, throwing 13 interceptions to just seven touchdowns in 11 games while completing a career-low 58.4 percent of his passes. The Colts (3-8) have lost six of their past seven games. Their decision to cut Jones now will result in a $35.2 million dead cap hit for the 2027 season — a figure that could have been significantly reduced had the Colts simply benched him and waited to cut him in March.

Patriots WR A.J. Brown expected to miss multiple weeks due to hamstring injury, per report | CBS Sports

Brown suffered the hamstring injury during their Week 10 loss to the Detroit Lions in Germany. The 29-year-old has dealt with numerous injuries this season, finding himself on the injury report more times than not.

The three-time Pro Bowler suffered an ankle injury in their season opener and was placed on injured reserve, missing four games. After he returned, he then missed two games due to a knee strain.

NFL will consider requiring minimum-night stays for international games | Pro Football Talk

"Currently, we leave those football decisions on when the team arrives in the market to the club," NFL executive V.P. of club business, major events, and international Peter O'Reilly told The Athletic. "It is something that we'll consider moving forward on: Do we put some minimum nights in a market in there? It's something we'll look at."

The league already does it for the Super Bowl, mandating a full-week experience that includes multiple press conferences, the annual "Opening Night" media extravaganza, and practicing at designated facilities.

Have the Chiefs finally fixed their pass rush?

Brandon Thorn of Establish the Run is as respected as any offensive line analyst covering the game, and he currently has the Jets ranked as his 12th-best offensive line in the league. He has the Falcons at 19th. Not world beaters, but this wasn't the Chiefs taking advantage of the early-season Packers MASH unit offensive line.

This weekend should set up well for another dominant performance by the Chiefs' D-Line. The Cardinals' quarterbacks have been sacked 8 times over the past two weeks. Thorn ranks their offensive line as the third worst in the league. This could be yet another get-right game for the defensive side of the ball before things start to get real.
