SMCC, Bedford to play in regional volleyball finals, Flat Rock falls

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

St. Mary Catholic Central and Bedford are moving on.

The greatest collection of volleyball players in school history is packing up the equipment at Flat Rock.

Regional semifinal matches were no problem for SMCC and Bedford as they both rolled to three-set victories on Tuesday, Nov. 10.

But Flat Rock, which reached the Division 2 state finals last season, saw its' state tournament run come to an end.

SMCC and Bedford advance to championship matches on Thursday, Nov. 12.

OTTAWA LAKE – Kim Windham always looks calm on the sidelines.

But the cool demeanor doesn't show what is going on inside.

Even in the midst of a dominant 13-0 run in the opening set in the semifinals of the Division 3 Regional at Whiteford, Windham said she couldn't just step back and enjoy it.

"Uh, no," she said. "You have to keep talking to them to make sure they stay composed and don't make an error."

SMCC didn't make many errors in rolling to a 25-12, 25-13, 15-10 victory over Hanover-Horton.

"The first thing about tonight was our defense was phenomenal," Windham said. "You have to play great defense to allow you to run the middles like we did tonight."

Middle hitter Avery Yaeger was the dominant force in the match.

She had 3 kills and a block kill during the 13-0 run. Allie Ruhlig added a pair of kills and Reese Foster served all 13 points.

"Reese served phenomenally," Windham said.

Alexa Turner and Claire Ryan also were active at the net for the Kestrels, who carry a 33-3 record into the championship game at 6 p.m. Thursday against Clinton.

LINCOLN PARK – The opening set was tied 15-15, then Bedford hit another gear and pulled away for a 25-15, 25-11, 25-11 victory over Dearborn.

"We played really, really well," Bedford coach Bree Russow said. "We battled and battled in that first set, then kind of got into a groove."

Mady Wallace led with 10 kills and hit .364. Presley Stark added 7 putaways and hit .444.

The defense was led by Kenna Goulet (16 digs) and Blair Russow (10 digs).

"We controlled our side of the net," Russow said. "That's what we focused on."

Next up for 26-12-1 Bedford is a 7 p.m. Thursday date in the finals against Detroit Cass Tech.

"Cass Tech brings a lot of offense," Russow said. "They are a super athletic team. It will be a good battle."

DEARBORN – Dearborn Divine Child took full advantage of homecourt advantage to trip the defending state runners-up 25-19, 25-21, 22-25, 25-23.

"We didn't play super well, but the environment was tough," Flat Rock coach Morgan Delhey said. "The place was packed. I think they had their entire student body there. It was loud and intense."

Sarah Giroux capped her outstanding career with 28 kills and 27 digs. Claire Kujat was second in kills with 7 and Zoe Ryan conbuted 13 digs and 2 blocks.

The Rams, who finish at 29-12-1, will lose eight seniors to graduation.

"This is one of the most talented groups we've ever had," Delhey said. "Their leadership and experience will be irreplaceable."
