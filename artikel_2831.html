Cam Skattebo renews backflip celebration days after vowing to shelve it: 'It's hard to hold back from'

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

New York Giants running back Cam Skattebo vowed in the aftermath of Aidan Hutchinson's season-ending celebration injury that he was done doing backflips. That promise lasted less than four quarters.

Skattebo iced New York's 23-18 win over the Jacksonville Jaguars on Sunday with a run for a first down at the 2-minute warning that allowed the Giants to finish the game in victory formation.

When the play was done, Skattebo stood up and flexed. Then he did a backflip.

That was four days after Skattebo said this regarding backflips in the aftermath of learning that Hutchinson tore his ACL while jumping in celebration of a sack:

"It's hard to see," Skattebo said Wednesday of Hutchinon's injury, per the New York Post. "I'm saying yeah. Mentally, I'm like, 'I'm not doing that.' That ain't going to happen to me."

Per the Post, Skattebo reserved the right to renew his signature celebration "if we go to the playoffs or something."

Sunday's game must have fallen under "or something."

Skattebo doesn't have to look to Hutchinson as an injury reference point. He sustained his own season-ending injury as a rookie, a dislocated ankle that left him in fear that he'd need to have his foot amputated.

He, of course, did not lose his foot and was back to backflipping over the summer before returning to football — or he was trying, at least.

The backflip then made multiple on-field appearances prior to his vow to stop them. He was asked about the decision to renew the flip at his locker postgame.

"When energy calls for it, it calls for it," Skattebo said. "My teammates, I do whatever I can to bring the energy for the team. I play this game with my heart and soul.

"When that happens, it's second nature and it's hard to hold back from."

Giants head coach John Harbaugh was also asked about Skattebo's backflip at his postgame news conference.

"He couldn't help himself, could he. "He' just couldn't help himself."
