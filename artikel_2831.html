Theo Benedet, Grady Jarrett return to Bears practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Bears got starting linemen from both sides of the ball back at practice on Thursday.

Left tackle Theo Benedet (shoulder) and defensive tackle Grady Jarrett (groin) were both limited participants as the team continuing preparing for Sunday’s game against the Buccaneers. They were both listed as non-participants on Wednesday’s practice report.

Linebacker T.J. Edwards (personal) was also listed as out on Wednesday. He fully participated on Thursday.

Linebacker D’Marco Jackson (ankle) moved in the opposite direction and missed practice after not being on Wednesday’s report. Running back Kyle Monangai (thumb, toe) and left guard Joe Thuney (rest) were also out of practice.

Defensive back Malik Muhammad (toe) joined Benedet and Jarrett in the limited category.
