Few things get gamers going more than digital nudity, and plenty of chapters in GTA 6’s leak saga focused on just how naked the residents of Leonida are willing to get. If you followed the leaks, you may remember talk of a nudist resort, well, we managed to find it!

Grand Theft Auto 6 delivers an enormous open world, a gripping story, a vibrant Vice City and an astonishing range of activities.

If you want to see some of Leonida’s freest citizens, look no further. We’ve covered exactly where to find the spot, plus everything you can do in this little chunk of paradise.

Finding the nudist resort will require you to swing a bit out of the way if you want to find it early, before the main story leads you further out than Hamlet. You’ll want to head northwest, towards Watson Bay along the highway.

As you drive, you’ll eventually pass a small trailer park with a notably heart-shaped pool at its far end. This is the main way to spot the resort from a distance, giving you the chance to pull into the RV park and head down the central path.

Sadly, finding the resort is the most exciting part of it, as the actual area is both incredibly small, and doesn’t have anything really unique. The NPCs are notably more chill with either protagonist streaking than most, as you’d expect, but besides a small bar there isn’t much to see.

You can confirm one white whale of the leak era off your checklist at least, and keep your eyes open for more of the quirky little spots that make Leonida so fun to explore.
