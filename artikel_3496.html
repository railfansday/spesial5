’Obsession’s Breakout Star Is Officially Breaking Records Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

There are few names that had a bigger breakout year in 2026 than Inde Navarrette. From being relatively unknown last year to one of the hottest names in Hollywood right now, Navarrette's rise to the top has been quick and justified. Her success can be chalked up to a starring turn in the year's most viral film, where she stunned as Nikki Freeman in Focus Features' Obsession.

Called "one of the year's best" performances by Hannah Hunt in her review for Collider, some are even calling for Navarrette to become an Oscar winner for the role, although she remains a firm second favorite behind Julianne Moore for her lead role in Jesse Eisenberg's The Debut. However, now that she is set to portray Rogue in the MCU's X-Men reboot, whether she wins the Academy Award won't change how big a presence she will have on our screens for years to come. But Navarrette's success so far isn't limited to Obsession.

After helping the horror breakout achieve multiple records at the box office, Navarrette has shifted her stardom to one of America's oldest and most beloved institutions and made waves there too. It's been reported that Navarrette's Halloween episode of Saturday Night Live earned 5.2 million viewers, the biggest of the season so far. In fact, these figures rank high when compared to previous seasons, with only a handful of episodes outperforming it over recent years. Gracie Abrams joined Navarrette as the musical guest on her season-high episode, with the next challenge for the throne being the pairing of Druski and The Strokes, who will take to the stage this Saturday.

Navarrette is making a habit of setting trends and breaking records. During its well-documented box office run in which it toppled the latest Star Wars movie, Obsession became the highest-grossing non-animated original film of the past decade, the highest-grossing original horror movie of this century, and the fifth highest-grossing horror film of all time. Outside the holiday corridor, it also became the first film since E.T. to grow in ticket sales across three consecutive weekends, and did all this on a budget of less than $1 million.
