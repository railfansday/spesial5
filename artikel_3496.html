HBO’s Acclaimed Historical Masterpiece Is Officially Dominating Streaming Charts

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

All eyes might be wandering towards HBO's new Wizarding World, which debuts this Christmas, but first, one of the network's best shows is thriving in all its costumed glory. The fourth season of The Gilded Age has not disappointed. The drama, romance, and ball gowns have all been the most indulgent yet, as Downton Abbey creator Julian Fellowes turns up the dial on the show he once intended as an America-set prequel to the beloved Brit hit.

"The biggest point in the series' favor, four seasons in, is that it's officially settled into the most confident version of itself," said Carly Lane for Collider in one of many glowing reviews. Season 4 ties for the joint highest critics' score on review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes (96%), tied with Season 3, illustrating how this costume drama is growing from strength to strength. The series has also been a ratings hit. Reports state that the series is averaging 5.9 million U.S. cross-platform viewers per episode, which marks a 20% increase on Season 3.

This ratings and critical success is reflected in the show's streaming position. At the time of writing, The Gilded Age ​​​​​​is the most-watched show on HBO Max in the world. This is made all the more impressive when considering its tough competition, including the pulse-pounding legal drama War, the sitcom spin-off Georgie & Mandy's First Marriage, and even the popular magical reality series, Harry Potter: Wizards of Baking. Ahead of the last batch of Season 4 episodes, The Gilded Age has been officially renewed for a fifth outing.

Up next for The Gilded Age is Season 4, Episode 4, "Good Taste," which follows the chaos of the third installment, which ended with Agnes (Christine Baranski) storming across the street to confront Bertha (Carrie Coon). "Agnes tries to regain control of the dinner; Peggy's career sparks a conflict with William; Ada meets Dr. Harris," reads a logline for the fourth episode, which is penned once again by Fellowes and Sonja Warfield. Following this weekend's installment, which is exactly the halfway point of Season 4, just four episodes will remain, with the finale airing on December 20, 2026.
