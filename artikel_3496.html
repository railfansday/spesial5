Houston Mayor Limits Cooperation With ICE: ‘Elections Matter’

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Words to live and die by in American politics. Famously uttered by former President Barack Obama in 2009, versions of the aphorism have since become a cutting political shorthand for the sentiment that “we won, you lost, deal with it.”

Following Democrats’ generational victory in the 2026 midterms, the combined threat, taunt, and promise is already being wielded with gusto. On Friday, Houston Mayor John Whitmire signed an executive action ordering the Houston Police Department to end cooperative policies with Immigrations and Customs Enforcement (ICE). Earlier this year, Texas Republicans passed a controversial law, SB 4, that allowed state law enforcement to engage in immigration enforcement activities. The same law banned cities and local jurisdictions from enforcing “sanctuary” policies protecting undocumented migrant residents from immigration enforcement. Now, with Democrats controlling every statewide seat in Texas as well as the statehouse, the fear of retaliation by the state government is no longer a barrier.

“Elections matter,” Whitmire said during a press conference on Friday. “I was on the Senate floor when SB 4 was approved. I voted against it […] I voted no because we were concerned about the fear, the anxiety that it would create.”

“ICE will only be contacted if someone commits a jailable offense,” he explained, adding that he believed the policy change could begin to rebuild “trust” and “reduce the fear and anxiety and not just in some of our communities but all across Houston.”
