'Maybe I will cry': Oh reflects on 'hard time' at Celtic before emotional return

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Oh Hyeon-gyu departed Celtic for Genk in 2024 after falling out of favour under Brendan Rodgers.

Following 18 goal-laden months in Belgium, the South Korean striker joined Besiktas for €14 million at the start of this year.

In 34 appearances for the Turkish side, Oh has scored 12 goals.

It's a tally he wants to increase against his former employers at Celtic Park in the Europa League on Thursday evening.

"First of all, it's very special for me to be back here," Oh said. "I couldn't speak English properly back then. But everyone welcomed me like family here. And Celtic became like my second home.

"Maybe I will be emotional tomorrow. I'd be happy to score tomorrow. I'm Besiktas now. I will give everything for Besiktas."

Oh joined Celtic in January 2023 from Suwon Bluewings in his homeland.

"What can I say? I came here as a young boy with a dream," he continued. "It was sometimes hard for me, sometimes good for me.

"I have got good memories here. I will always be grateful for everything and the fans.

Now 25, Oh has progressed both on and off the pitch since swapping South Korea for Scotland almost four years ago.

"I learned a lot about my position from Kyogo Furuhashi and Adam Idah," Oh added. "It was a hard time for me because I didn't play a lot, sometimes I played, sometimes I did not.

"In my last season here, I didn't play for the last six months so it was a hard time for me. But I'm very happy to be back here because I love Celtic so much. It has a special place in my heart."

Ahead of Thursday's match, Besiktas boss Vincenzo Italiano said: "We have got points already but this is another important game.

"We are a team that’s very hungry. There may be changes to our team but we will give everything to get a positive result.

"We have been going well in our European games, winning our last game 3-0.  We have a different attention span for these games and have done a good job. It has not been easy but we have worked really hard. We are focused on qualifying on this phase and this game will be very tough.

"It’s a an amazing stadium and we are going to play against a very strong team with nice supporters. Celtic will want to win because they haven’t collected points yet.

"We have to be as hungry as them because it’s going to be a very difficult game. I played against Celtic at Bologna and it was a fantastic night. But this will be different.

"I am very much surprised they have zero points so we may see an angry Celtic team. This may complicate our job. It will be an important test for us. Everybody has to be really prepared."
