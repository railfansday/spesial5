Arsenal Women vs London City Lionesses preview: Gunners face tough test at the Emirates

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Arsenal look to build on their recent win over Liverpool as they return to the Emirates Stadium but will face an impressive London City Lionesses side who are a potent attacking threat on their travels.

Renée Slegers’ team have struggled to convert their strong performances into points in the league this season. They have lost their last four WSL games at home, however, becoming the first team in WSL history to draw four successive games at home.

Their bad run must come to an end. They have taken just three points from their first nine league games so far this season, scoring a miserable total of 10 goals. However, they look like they have more in the tank and will need to be much more clinical in front of the goal.

The day also marks Slegers’ 50th game in charge of the WSL. The Dutch manager has only lost six league games so far this season, with only Chelsea having a better record from teams that have been ever-present in the division.

Arsenal can’t take their opposition lightly after London City’s impressive recent form. They have won two and drawn once in their last three away league games, scoring 12 goals.

Eder Maestre’s side has become a potent attacking force. Daniëlle van de Donk has provided four assists this season, and Delphine Cascarino has scored three goals and provided two assists.

Kadidiatou Diani can be dangerous. She has scored or assisted in all four of her league games away from home this season.

London City’s attacking prowess means they are a far different proposition to some teams Arsenal have faced at home. They have been in a positive situation for 62.7 per cent of their league minutes this season, the highest in the WSL.

Arsenal will have to deal with the attacking threats but look to utilise their ball possession better. Giving London City an opportunity to counter could make an already tough afternoon even tougher.

Alessia Russo comes into the game having found the net in her last three WSL games and will be key in converting Arsenal’s chances into goals.

The Gunners also showed their fighting spirit in their midweek clash against Barcelona. They drew 1-1 at Camp Nou to become the first side to take points off the Spanish giants in the Women’s Champions League at home since April 2024.

Slegers’ team will feel emboldened by that result but will have to change their tactics against London City.

They have won all seven games against Arsenal so far, scoring an impressive 22 goals and only conceding once. However, given London City’s recent away form, they will be hotly favoured.

They certainly have the quality to take all three points, but they must finally convert their dominance into a comfortable home win.
