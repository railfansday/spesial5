Russell Brand's Shop Launches 'Free Russell Brand' Merch Amid Rape Sentence

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Russell Brand’s Reborn wellness shop is selling various “Free Russell Brand” merchandise after his rape conviction and subsequent prison sentence.

“Stand with Russell against injustice,” the website homepage reads. “Silenced from speaking out.”

A “Stand with Him” button takes site visitors to the merch shop, now selling two “Free Russel Brand” T-shirt designs and a baseball cap bearing the same phrase. All items are sold for $34.99.

Brand, 51, was accused of misconduct and sexual assault in 2023, vehemently denying the accusations and pleading not guilty to three counts of rape, three counts of sexual assault and one count of attempted indecent assault. The disgraced comedian, who has maintained his innocence, was convicted of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault in an October trial. (Brand was found not guilty of the remaining charges.)

Following his rape conviction, Brand was sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison.

“Beloved brothers and sisters in Christ, God bless you for your support,” he wrote in a statement read aloud by his wife, Laura Gallacher, in a November 15 YouTube video. “As you know, I am not guilty for the offenses I have been jailed for, so something strange is happening.”

He continued at the time, “The good news is that Christ is with us when we choose him, and I do. I am incarcerated in HMP Littlehey. Little as in small, and hey as in, ‘Hey, I’m in the biggest sex offenders prison in Europe.’ The good news is that I have already seen that the staff here are strong, they are kind and they are dedicated. And as for my fellow prisoners, I now know that I am in no position to judge anyone.”

Brand and Gallacher, 39, have been married since 2017. She has remained steadfast by the Get Him to the Greek actor’s side throughout the misconduct scandal and trial.

“We trust God, and we trust his plan, and we trust everything He’s doing right now, even though it’s painful,” Gallacher, who shares three children with Brand, said in her own statement shared via YouTube. “Obviously, [it] will take some getting used to for me to even sit here and not have my companion and my person with me.”

She concluded at the time, “Although Russell is not here now in this moment, the holy spirit is moving and the holy spirit cannot be restrained. I’m speaking to Russell. He’s OK and he’s praying, unsurprisingly. He’s praying and we decided for me to continue [our channel].”
