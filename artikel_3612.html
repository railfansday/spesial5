Taylor Swift Cheers on Travis Kelce's Chiefs Game With Her Parents, In-Laws

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Taylor Swift has publicly pledged allegiance to her husband Travis Kelce’s hands, team and his vibe, so naturally, she didn’t miss his Sunday, November 22, Kansas City Chiefs home game.

Swift, 36, stepped out on Sunday at the Chiefs’ football game against the Arizona Cardinals, where she watched the matchup from her usual perch inside a private box at Arrowhead Stadium. The pop star dressed to impress in a Chiefs-branded WEAR by Erin Andrews Crinkle Full-Zip hooded windbreaker, as she was joined by her parents, Scott and Andrea, mother-in-law Donna Kelce and sister-in-law Kylie Kelce.

Just days earlier, Swift made her triumphant return to the 2026 CMAs for the first time in a decade. While she lost the Single of the Year trophy to Ella Langley on Wednesday, November 18, Swift commanded the stage for a nostalgic-fueled mashup of “I Knew It, I Knew You” and “Our Song.”

Swift attended the awards show with Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes’s wife, Brittany Mahomes, while both of their respective spouses were presumably busy with NFL commitments. Travis, 37, did “like” social media footage of Swift’s performance, subtly supporting his wife from afar.

Swift and Travis have been together since 2023, soon kickstarting the “Patient Zero” singer’s football fandom.

“Football is awesome, it turns out,” Swift joked in a December 2023 TIME profile. “I’ve been missing out my whole life.”

While Swift has seldom missed a Chiefs home game, her game day presence has sparked backlash from certain viewers.

“I don’t know how they know what suite I’m in. There’s a camera, like, a half-mile away, and you don’t know where it is, and you have no idea when the camera is putting you in the broadcast, so I don’t know if I’m being shown 17 times or once,” she told the outlet. “I’m just there to support Travis. I have no awareness of if I’m being shown too much and pissing off a few dads, Brads, and Chads.”

Swift continued at the time, “When you say a relationship is public, that means I’m going to see him do what he loves, we’re showing up for each other, other people are there and we don’t care. The opposite of that is you have to go to an extreme amount of effort to make sure no one knows that you’re seeing someone. And we’re just proud of each other.”

Swift and Travis said “I do” three years later in a star-studded wedding held at New York City’s Madison Square Garden in July.

“I appreciate everybody who came out and celebrated and had fun with us. That’s about all I really got from that night. It was a crazy night,” the tight end gushed about their nuptials in an August press conference. “It was full of a lot of celebration. … It’s kind of cool to get to live out my childhood dream of being in that venue, the mecca of all sports venues, [by] being able to get married in there.”
