Is PlayStation down? Some gamers report problems with PlayStation servers amid GTA 6 launch.

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Updated on Thursday, Nov. 19 at 12:57 p.m. ET — The PlayStation Service Status page now states that all services are up and running. This article has been updated to reflect PlayStation's current status.

Updated on Thursday, Nov. 19 at 12:41 p.m. ET — Sony has updated its PlayStation Service Status page to report issues with "Gaming and Social" systems for the PS5. A note reads, "You might have difficulty launching games, apps, or network features. We're working to resolve the issue as soon as possible. Thank you for your patience."

Many gamers have already taken advantage of a sneaky little trick that allowed them to play the long-awaited Grand Theft Auto VI one day early on New Zealand time. However, the vast majority of gamers in the U.S. waited for the official launch at midnight on Nov. 19 — and the big moment has finally arrived.

Unfortunately, the PlayStation servers may have temporarily gone down as countless gamers try to access the game all at once.

At 12:33 a.m. ET, Sony updated the official PlayStation Service Status page to reflect a possible outage. According to an update, game streaming on the PS4 and PS5 had been impacted, and gamers "might have difficulty launching games, apps, or network features."

Predictably, user error reports spiked sharply on the platform DownDetector right before midnight ET on Nov. 19, minutes before the official U.S. launch. Some social media users also reported problems accessing the PlayStation Network.

Up until midnight, the PlayStation status page showed that all systems were go for launch.

User error reports for PlayStation also spiked on DownDetector around 2 p.m. ET on Nov. 18, but the midnight increase was far larger. However, DownDetector only shows user-submitted error reports, not verified outages. And with countless gamers all firing up their PlayStations at once, user-side errors could be to blame.

Don't forget: You have to update your PlayStation console before you can play GTA 6.

With so many different companies and services suffering from outages recently, gamers may be understandably nervous. If you're encountering any issues with your console on launch day, here's how you can quickly check if there's really a PlayStation outage — or if it's just you and your internet connection.

If you run into any issues while trying to load up GTA 6 on your PlayStation, head over to the official PlayStation Service Status page. If there is a problem, you can also check the PlayStation Support account on X for updates.

These official resources will let you know at a glance whether Sony PlayStation services are up and running. And if there is an issue, the status page will tell you which PlayStation service is having problems.

Again, as of publication, Sony states that there are no problems with game streaming on the PS4 and PS5.

Microsoft has a similar status page for Xbox users. If you run into trouble trying to enter Vice City on GTA 6 launch day, simply head to the official Xbox status page.

This page will tell you if any Xbox services are having problems.

As of publication, Xbox lists its status as "All services up and running," despite some slightly elevated activity on DownDetector.
