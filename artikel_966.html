Joshua unable to see new baby daughter until after Fury fight

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Anthony Joshua says ongoing training camp for his fight against Tyson Fury on December 11 means he will not see his new-born daughter until the days after the fight; "It's not ideal but she is in good hands", says Joshua, who describes daughter's arrival as a "big blessing"

Anthony Joshua became a father for the second time after the birth of a daughter last weekend, although ongoing training for his fight against Tyson Fury means he is not able to see her until mid-December.

Joshua, whose son JJ was born in 2015, is currently in training camp in Marbella in preparation for his blockbuster all-British fight against Fury on December 11 at Cardiff's Principality Stadium, and was unable to be at the birth.

Joshua says the arrival of his daughter, who has not yet been named, is "amazing" and "a big blessing" as the 37-year-old paid tribute to Nicole, the mother of both children.

While he has been able to have "many" FaceTime calls with mother and daughter in recent days, Joshua says he wished he would have been able to be there but preparations for the Fury fight mean mean he is currently "on a mission".

"It's not ideal but she is in good hands," Joshua said in an interview with The Sun.

"She's got an amazing mother, she's got amazing family round her and the people at the hospital, I heard they were amazing. I spoke to them and said thank you, so I'm pretty content at the minute that she's in good hands. But, for me, it would have been much better if I was able to be there.

"But I'm on duty at the minute and we're on a mission. I knew about this nine months ago so I made this commitment that I can't change, unfortunately, but there will be many more birthdays I'll be at, for sure."

Asked when he would likely meet her in person for the first time, Joshua said: "Probably December 12, December 13.

"I'll go and check in and rock her to sleep, let her hear my voice and let her feel my heartbeat, really. The basic stuff."

He said he would be a fully hands-on father.

"I'm going to do it all. I'm going to definitely do it all," he added.

"You might see me with some eye shadow on, some nail polish on, drinking some tea in a doll's playhouse just trying to do the right thing by her."

Joshua said he had wanted to become a father for a second time to "do something positive" after the "painful and traumatic" experience of last December's car crash in Nigeria that killed two members of his team, personal trainer Latif Ayodele, and strength and conditioning coach Sina Ghami.

"After the year that I'm coming off I wanted to do something positive," said Joshua, who was injured in the crash.

"I wanted to give JJ the opportunity to be an older brother. One day I won't be here and he'll have a sibling that he can turn to, be with and plan their future together. It's nice to have a sibling that they can lean on each other when needed, when me and his mum aren't here one day in the future."

The British boxer added: "Nicole is doing good. She is healthy. She said it was an amazing experience. She said it was easier than JJ and she's much prettier than JJ, so she's happy! She's already deeply in love with her."
