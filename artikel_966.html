Van Dijk uncertain on Liverpool future

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Liverpool captain Virgil van Dijk is out of contract in the summer and told Sky Sports he has "no idea" where he will be playing next season; Watch Liverpool vs Man Utd live on Sky Sports Premier League on Sunday from 4pm; kick-off 4.30pm.

By Harriet Prior (interview) | Peter Smith (words)

Liverpool captain Virgil van Dijk believes he can keeping playing for "many more years" - but says he does not yet know whether that will be at Anfield next season.

The 35-year-old centre-back, who has won two Premier League titles and the Champions League during his time at Liverpool, is out of contract in the summer.

He told Sky Sports that, currently, he is uncertain on what will happen beyond this campaign.

"The main thing for me is that I feel good and I'm looking after myself and I still have many more years in my opinion," he said.

"Whatever's going to happen after this season, we'll see.

"At this moment of time, if anyone says that or put out that they know what I'm going to do next season, it's untrue because at this moment of time, speaking to you, I've got no idea either.

"But I'm relaxed with it because if you know me, I only focus on what I can control and that's at the moment the Manchester United game. Then we'll focus to the Champions League and whatever happens in the new year coming, we'll see then.

"But it's been an incredible journey, almost 10 years this summer and it's been a big part of my life and I'll forever be grateful for anything that happened over those years.

"If it's getting extended, we'll see then.

"If not, then I'm going to say goodbyes in the summer."

Asked about how emotional that exit would be, he said: "Well, yeah, probably for me the most and the family because it's been an incredible time.

"But I don't look too far in the future and that's been, in my opinion, also a little bit of a success. I won't change it now either.

Van Dijk has made a positive start to this season, partly thanks to his encouraging connection with new centre-back partner Jeremy Jacquet.

The Dutchman has been impressed by his young team-mate and says they have been working together on and off the pitch to build up their relationship and help each other perform to the highest level.

"He's been outstanding so far I must say," said Van Dijk when asked about Jacquet. "Very young but he plays like a very mature player. He's still 21, he's still going to get so much better and that's a good thing.

"Obviously, I speak to him a lot during the game but after the game and training and certain moments as well where I feel like I can help him.

"As a centre-back partnership, you have to be able to understand what your qualities and what your flaws are at times and then complement each other on those things. You only get there by playing games.

"It's been good because he's helping me, I help him and that's how it should be. I must say I'm impressed with how we started and now it's down to him to keep that level, keep that consistency, stay calm because you can easily get carried away because there's a lot of plaudits coming his way and rightly so. But I know how it can work when you have maybe one lesser game or not up to the standard that you have.

"He just has to keep going, keep staying important and be fit and then things will be working out pretty well."
