23 Iconic ’90s Bobs, as Seen on Celebrities

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Few eras maintain the same grip on our imagination as the 1990s. A last gasp before the rise of social media, the decade feels even more fun, raucous, and carefree. Just look at the beauty trends: eyebrows tweezed to within an inch of their lives. Thick lashings of black eyeliner and cheeks devoid of blush. Brown lip liner and frosted pastel eye shadow everywhere. Plus, a hair look that feels current now (and never truly went away in the interim): the bob haircut.

Plenty of famous faces made the bob their signature style in the ’90s, from Cameron Diaz to Halle Berry, Victoria Beckham to Linda Evangelista and Nia Long. The beauty of the ’90s bob specifically is that it tended to mean something slightly different depending on the wearer. Naomi Campbell embraced the razor-cut bob—a super-short, symmetrical cut that fell on either side of her face, with bumped-under ends. The then Posh Spice made the longer bob her calling card, and Christy Turlington cemented the blown-out glamour of the supermodel bob.

Below, 24 of our favorite bobs from the decade we never tire of looking back on.
