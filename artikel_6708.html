Only the Very Best Gifts for Mom

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

There are two types of moms. There’s the one who immediately tries on the lipstick, lights the candle, and slips into her new sweater, and the one who saves your present, in its original box, for when the time is right. You love her for who she is, even when her quirks drive you mad. One thing’s for certain, though: When the holidays roll around, she’s thinking about everyone but herself.

So what to get the mother figure who puts everyone else first? For new mom Andrea Zendejas, the best gifts are those that fill gaps—usually in her wardrobe or her beauty routine. For lifestyle editor Elly Leavitt, it’s knowing what will actually get used: “I’ve learned over the years what materials and colors she likes, and which she resolutely does not (polyester is public enemy number one). My mom is a chef and a recipe developer, so when in doubt, a beautiful kitchen tool that doubles as a prop is the most foolproof approach.”

A handwritten note and a bouquet of her favorite flowers are where you start; our guide is where you find the accompanying perfect gift. Read on for our edit of gifts for moms they’ll love—even if they’d never ask.
