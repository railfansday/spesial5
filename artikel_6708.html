Surprise! Taylor Swift Performs Medley of "I Knew It, I Knew You" and “Our Song” at the 2026 CMAs

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Taylor Swift just surprised fans with her debut live performance of “I Knew It, I Knew You” paired with “Our Song.” The Grammy-winner showed up at the 2026 CMAs, performing the song while wearing the cutest flouncy orange skirt and cowboy boots (she loves to dress on theme!). And yes, her blue guitar had Toy Story clouds on it.

This is the first time Taylor’s performed live for fans in a minute and was clearly worth the wait. She was also nominated for Single of the Year for “I Knew It, I Knew You” from Toy Story 5, and rumor has it that she could also get an Oscar nom for the song.

Taylor opened up about her creative process when she announced “I Knew It, I Knew You,” saying “Writing this song felt like a musical departure and coming home at the same time. Creating something for Jessie was a new challenge and also felt like second nature all at once. And being a @toystory kid from the age of 5 til now… is an adventure I plan to be on, to infinity and beyond.”

She also shouted out Jack Antonoff with, “we wrote this with so much adoration for these characters that made us laugh and helped us learn lessons and think outside the backyard all throughout our childhoods.”

Kay, off to watch her performance on repeat, bye!
