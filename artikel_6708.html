Shop Our Favorite Celebrity Looks of the Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Fall is finally here! If you’re wondering what to wear to embrace the drop in temperatures, we’re here to simplify matters with outfit ideas, courtesy of our favorite celebrities spotted on the streets. Consider this week’s edit your guide to everything from transitional fall looks to clever layering techniques, all infused with plenty of off-duty flair.

This week, celebrities embraced the seasonal chill, stepping out in their fall outerwear best. Kendall Jenner was spotted wearing The Row’s riff on the barn jacket while strolling through the West Village with boyfriend Jacob Elordi. Styled with Éterne’s bootcut denim, brown leather boots, and a roomy tote bag, the look offered a minimalist take on casual weekend wear. Rihanna wore a classic khaki trench coat for shopping at The Grove in Los Angeles, layering it over a pale pink button-down shirt and relaxed brown jeans, with Miu Miu x New Balance suede sneakers adding a sporty finish. Both looks demonstrate how classic outerwear can pull together a seasonal outfit.

Peplum jackets are also having a moment and celebrities are embracing them in lieu of traditional going-out tops. Léa Seydoux sported a chocolate-brown version from Louis Vuitton, featuring a nipped-in waist and ornamental closures—a perfect match for straight-leg blue jeans and high-vamp pumps. Meanwhile, Louisa Jacobson chose a white peplum topper from Ashlyn’s spring 2027 show for a Tiffany & Co. event, styling it with a slinky green mesh skirt and peep-toe mules—an elegant choice for evenings out and the many holiday gatherings to come.

Keep reading to discover these ideas and more, bringing timeless classics to life with a few standout finds, all reworked by the stars and completely shoppable.

Paloma Elsesser demonstrated how to make a single-tone ensemble feel more interesting, with texture and unexpected silhouettes serving as the secret sauce. Metallic heels, a conceptual handbag, and glasses from the brand brought even more dimension to the tonal pairing.

Rosie Huntington-Whiteley stepped out in Phoebe Philo’s burgundy leather bomber jacket, pairing it with a plain white T-shirt, gray pleated trousers, and pointed black ankle boots from Christen.
