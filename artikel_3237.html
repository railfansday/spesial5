Stabilis (SLNG) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Sequential revenue jumped as a new data center contract ramps ahead of 2027.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Stabilis Solutions, Inc. (SLNG -4.02%) reported sequential revenue growth driven by the commencement of a short-term data center contract and continued expansion in the aerospace sector. Management indicated that the company returned to net profitability during the quarter while preparing for a multiyear power generation contract scheduled for the first quarter of 2027. The company received $25 million in total advanced payments year to date to fund equipment requirements for this 2027 project. Management noted that current growth is focused on behind-the-meter power for data centers and high-purity LNG for aerospace customers, while the timeline for a final investment decision on the Galveston LNG facility remains undefined.
