BGC Group (BGC) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Record quarter, FMX momentum, and a first fully AI-brokered institutional trade.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

BGC Group, Inc. (BGC -7.19%) reported record third-quarter revenue and earnings, supported by double-digit growth across most brokerage segments and the ongoing expansion of its electronic platforms. Management highlighted the rapid scaling of the FMX platform, particularly in Treasury futures following the August listing of the full curve, and reported a significant increase in market share. The company executed its first fully automated institutional trade using artificial intelligence, signaling a broader strategy to integrate AI across all asset classes. Financial results were further bolstered by diverse energy and shipping businesses, which offset slight volume declines in credit and refined products. The company also announced a leadership transition and confirmed its 2026 guidance, expecting continued top-line and earnings growth through the end of the year.
