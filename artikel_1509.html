Manchester City’s £31m left-back wanted in Turin: Loan or sale in January?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

CalcioMercato reports that Juventus have started monitoring Rayan Aït-Nouri ahead of the transfer market reopening, and the Manchester City left-back seems ready to listen. The Algeria international wants to restart his career elsewhere, views Turin favourably and has told his entourage to strengthen contacts, while the interest from Juventus is real and the club intends to act soon to test the deal’s feasibility.

City want to sell outright and reinvest immediately, whereas Juventus prefer a loan with an option to buy, which suits a club bound by a UEFA settlement agreement. They first asked in August and City refused, while Gazzetta dello Sport says Luciano Spalletti wants competition for Andrea Cambiaso.

City paid Wolves a reported £31m in June 2025 and gave him a contract until 2030, yet Enzo Maresca reportedly ranks the 25-year-old behind Joško Gvardiol and Nico O’Reilly. He had played 168 minutes in all competitions by mid-November, none of them in the Premier League, and his only starts have come in the EFL Cup.

His strengths explain the fee. Aït-Nouri scored nine goals and set up 15 in 135 Premier League games for Wolves, added five assists in 1,605 minutes last season, and can also operate in central areas.

The weakness is defensive security, because he is an ultra-attacking left-back. That matters now, with City on one point from their last four league games after losing 3-1 at Nottingham Forest and drawing 1-1 with Fulham on Saturday.

No, not in January, and not on terms that leave City carrying all the risk. Maresca has used Nico O’Reilly in midfield this season, yet O’Reilly started only seven of City’s first 15 games while managing a back problem, so an available specialist left-back is no luxury. Khusanov, Nunes and Guéhi all picked up knocks at Forest, which leaves the back line short.

City should treat the Juventus talks as a pricing exercise. A straight loan sends a 25-year-old on a contract until 2030 away for six months and returns him no more valuable, so only a loan with an obligation to buy close to the £31m fee makes sense.

Otherwise, give him a league start, see whether his attacking threat helps a side that keeps conceding, and let Maresca’s evidence decide. A club that paid £31m for a left-back last year has not shown he cannot do the job; it has shown only that the manager prefers somebody else.
