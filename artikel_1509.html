Lulli: “Gasperini’s playing style is perfect for us.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Lulli was decisive again today. His assist unlocked a game that had been very difficult up until that point.

The winger is continuing the path he began a few months ago and continues to reap the benefits.

“He’s a wonderful player and a really calm, easy-going, and joking person. He’s a truly fantastic player.”

“The coach’s style of play really helps us wingers; it takes advantage of our strengths. It’s a style of play I really enjoy. I have to approach every match as best I can and keep it up.”

“PSG? It’ll be a fantastic match, against top-class players. We need to approach it calmly. We need to play it like any other game, trying not to end up like Manchester United or Roma-Real Madrid.”
