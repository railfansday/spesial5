Roma maintain interest in Gittens, Chelsea consider offers

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Roma will be hitting the transfer market this winter to bolster Gian Piero Gasperini’s squad.

The Grugliasco-born coach wants several signings: strengthening the attack remains a priority. Endrick remains the first name on the Giallorossi’s list, but not the only one.

Jamie Bynoe Gittens is also still in the running at Trigoria.

After last summer, the Roman club are looking to try again in January. However, the situation has changed: according to a report by the English portal TeamTalk, the Blues are open to a loan transfer, although it remains to be seen whether they would allow the interested clubs to include a buy-out clause.

The list of suitors is long, with RB Leipzig, Eintracht Frankfurt, Leeds, Sunderland, Everton, and Crystal Palace all ready to enter the race for the 2004-born player.

Gittens is struggling with injuries. In England alone, he’s missed 33 games due to injury, and this season he’s been sidelined for seven games with an ankle injury. Xabi Alonso was initially inclined to rely on the number 11, but the injury has changed things.

Now, Chelsea is ready to let the 2004-born player leave. Roma wants to add the Englishman to Gian Piero Gasperini’s attack, even though he risks missing several games at a key point in the season.
