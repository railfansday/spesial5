Roma break Serie A’s scoring record: 36 goals in 12 games

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Roma are flying, scoring in every way. But Malen is the one who scores the most.

Gasperini’s team is a wonderful one, and they set another record tonight.

The Giallorossi, with 36 league goals, are the first team to score more than 35 goals after 12 Serie A games in the last 76 years – the last teams to do so were Inter, Milan (both 38), and Juventus (37) in 1950/51.
