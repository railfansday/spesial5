Use Rocket Money if you have more subscriptions and provider bills than you’ll cancel or negotiate yourself, since its paid plans do that work for you. With only a few bills, skip the monthly fee and make the cancellations and calls on your own.

Rocket Money’s free plan finds your subscriptions, but having it cancel them takes Premium, typically $7 to $14 a month at a price you pick, and having it lower your bills takes Premium+ at $15 a month plus tax. A year of either plan runs $84 to $180 before tax, while canceling subscriptions and negotiating your bills yourself costs only your time.

Households with many subscriptions and provider bills they won’t handle themselves should pay for Premium, and for Premium+ during the months its negotiators work on their bills, since they can drop it at the end of any billing cycle. A household with only a few bills saves the fee by making its own calls and cancellations, and anyone uneasy about Rocket Mortgage and the other Rocket companies receiving their transaction data should look at other budgeting apps.
