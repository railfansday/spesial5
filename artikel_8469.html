Open a Chime® account if your paycheck comes by direct deposit and you want checking with no monthly or overdraft fees. Routing at least $200 a month there unlocks fee-free overdraft cover and a higher savings rate. If you won’t move your pay, or you want a branch, look elsewhere.

Chime is a financial technology company, not a bank, so your accounts are held at The Bancorp Bank or Stride Bank, where deposit insurance for app accounts applies.1 Most of its perks beyond free checking require direct deposits.

Workers paid by direct deposit who keep paying overdraft fees get the most from Chime, since sending it $200 a month unlocks fee-free overdraft cover and a higher savings rate. Savers who won’t move a paycheck should keep their savings at an online bank that pays its full rate without direct deposit, and anyone who wants a teller should pick a bank with branches.
