Milwaukee Just Re-Released Its Sold-Out 2026 Advent Calendar on Amazon

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

It includes 24 tools and accessories—some of which are exclusive to the set—made for DIYers and fans of the brand.

Advent calendars are as popular as ever, and as a rule, when they go out of stock, that’s it. But it’s an early Christmas miracle for Milwaukee fans, as the red-and-black brand just re-released its 2026 version on Amazon.

Each gift in the 24-piece set is individually boxed and comes in a two-tier collectible case. The case is filled with an assortment of hand tools, accessories, and equipment, including a slick pair of mirror-coated scratch-resistant protective safety goggles housed in a zippered case with an attached carabiner. Three items packed inside are limited-edition designs made exclusively for the calendar.

On the tool front, there’s an 8-piece 1/4-inch hex multi-material drill bit set, a 6-in-1 multi-bit ratcheting screwdriver, and a 6-in-1 compact multi-bit screwdriver. There’s also a rugged zippered case and, for fun, my favorite item: the angle grinder bottle opener.
