Do you like screens? Then you’ll love the CLA350’s cabin.

The CLA’s front seats are supportive without being too firm, though we wish massage functions were offered.

The steering wheel is pretty standard Mercedes fare, with a physical rocker and thumb wheel replacing some of the capacitive bits.
