Eintracht Frankfurt targets January move for David Boly

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Eintracht Frankfurt is keen to sign Paris Saint-Germain right-back David Boly in January, according to Sky Germany journalist Florian Plettenberg.

The 17-year-old is high on Frankfurt’s shortlist, with his current PSG agreement due to expire in June 2027. Several clubs have already made inquiries, although Plettenberg says his recent Champions League breakthrough has complicated a potential move.

Boly scored on his debut in the competition, coming off the bench in PSG’s 6-0 victory at Villarreal last week.

PSG is also working to retain the academy graduate. CulturePSG, citing L’Equipe and RMC Sport, reports that the French champion has offered Boly his first professional contract and that the defender currently favors staying in Paris.
