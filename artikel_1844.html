‘We don’t have time’: Andy Robertson makes worrying admission as pressure grows on new Scotland manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Sebastien Pocognoli is already under pressure as Scotland boss - with his captain Andy Robertson claiming “we don’t have time” after another defeat.

Scotland’s 2026-27 Nations League campaign came to a close after a 4-0 routing at the hands of Switzerland on Monday night.

Although Scotland finished third and therefore avoided relegation to the third tier of the Nations League, concerns are growing among fans after just six games of Pocognoli’s tenure.

The former Monaco manager was appointed by the Scottish FA on an initial two-year contract, replacing Steve Clarke after he stepped down from the role following the World Cup earlier in the summer.

On what was meant to be a night of celebration for Robertson, the left-back eclipsing Kenny Dalglish to become Scotland’s most-capped player of all time, ended in contemplation - and strong comments.

Robertson reflected: "First half, I thought we were in the game, we lost a stupid goal, but I thought we had chances and could have been 2-1 at half time.
