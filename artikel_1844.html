Huge Michael Carrick twist revealed as Manchester United suffer blow for potential appointment

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Manchester United have been dealt a blow in attempts to provide under pressure head coach Michael Carrick with some additional support.

Carrick’s position at Old Trafford has been subject to intense scrutiny for several weeks and calls for him to be sacked have grown louder, on social media, with Man United languishing in the Premier League relegation zone heading into the international break.

While Man United produced two stirring comebacks in the space of five days at home to Roma and Aston Villa, the situation is that they have won just three games across all competitions so far this season - one of which has come in the Premier League.

However, it has since been widely reported that the Man Utd board are in no rush to make a decision regarding Carrick’s future.

As per Sky Sports, the plan is for the club’s chiefs to use the international break to fully evaluate the season so far and reassess their options, though there is a feeling in the boardroom that Carrick and his coaching staff still retain the support of the fans and players.

In a shock further twist to the saga, talkSPORT have revealed that one option on the table is to add to Carrick's coaching staff.

Furthermore, the report claims that Bayern Munich assistant head coach Aaron Danks was considered by Man United in the summer but hopes of revisiting a potential deal now look very unlikely.

This is because Danks would not be expected to leave the Bundesliga champions mid-season, and has performed well in the absence of Vincent Kompany, who was taken to hospital last month fter collapsing during reserve-team training.

Carrick revealed before the Aston Villa draw that plans were in place for the November international break, with attentions shortly turning to the Liverpool game a week on Sunday.

“No, no. Just carry on as absolute normal,” he told reporters. “Maybe three or four senior players will be in and around [the training ground], but we’ll certainly train next week and maybe have the weekend off.

“We’ll see how that develops, who’s left behind, who’s with us and what they need. But there’ll certainly be training next week.
