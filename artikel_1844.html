What channel is Torreense v Celtic? TV, live stream and highlights for Europa League clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Celtic are in Europa League action on Thursday night when they travel to Portugal to face second-tier side Torreense.

The game will mark the first time in history that the Hoops have contested a European fixture against a team who are not competing in the top flight of their domestic league.

Torreense qualified for the Europa League after a fairytale cup success last season, defying the odds to beat Sporting Lisbon in the final of the Portuguese Cup to claim the first major trophy in their 109-year existence.

They failed to secure promotion, however, losing 2-0 on aggregate to Casa Pia in the promotion/relegation play-off, meaning they remained in Liga Portugal 2 for the 2026/27 campaign, where they current sit 13th in the table.

Celtic put their first Europa League points on the board at the fourth attempt with a 3-2 win over Besiktas on November 5 following three consecutive defeats by Ferencvaros, Benfica and Celta Vigo.

Martin O’Neill’s side sit 32nd in the 36-team league with four games to play as they bid to climb into the top 24 and become the first side to qualify for the knockout stages after losing their opening three league phase matches.

Torreense are in a healthier 19th position having collected six points from their opening four games with victories over Lillestrom and Ararat-Armenia mixed with defeats to Sunderland and Ferencvaros.

The Europa League match between Torreense and Celtic will take place on Thursday, November 26, 2026 at the Dr. Magalhães Pessoa Stadium in Leiria. The venue is 60 miles away from Torreense’s actual home in Torres Vedras, as their own stadium only has 2,500 seats and does not meet Uefa requirements. Kick-off is 8pm.

The match will be shown live on TNT Sports 3 with coverage starting at 7.45pm, following on from Hearts v Monaco on the same channel.

Fans can stream the match live via HBO Max or Amazon Prime Video with a TNT Sports subscription.

Free highlights will be posted on the TNT Sports Football YouTube channel shortly after full-time.

Celtic have added Jota to their Europa League squad in place of the injured Mika Baur. The Portuguese winger was initially left out as he fought his way back from a serious knee injury. But he could now feature in his homeland against Torreense after continuing his comeback with a start in Saturday’s 4-1 win over St Mirren. Baur was recently ruled out for up to 10 weeks with ankle ligament damage.

Benjamin Nygren is nursing a heel problem while Alistair Johnston remains sidelined by a hamstring injury.

Torreense are missing suspended Jorge Aguirre following his red card in the 3-2 win over Aararat-Armenia and the injured Adama Baradji.

An all-Dutch officiating team will take charge of the match. Allard Lindhout is the on-field referee and he will be joined on the pitch by assistant referees Johan Balder and Murat Kucukerbir, while Jannick van der Laan serves as the fourth official. Pol van Boekel has been assigned as the Video Assistant Referee (VAR) with Dennis Johan Higler acting as the Assistant Video Assistant Referee (AVAR).
