The Princess of Wales Swaps Her Classic Waves for a Romantic Boho Hairstyle: How to Recreate It

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

During her three-day tour of India, the Princess of Wales attended the 2026 Earthshot Prize Ceremony in Mumbai. Prince William founded the initiative in 2020, and it awards environmental solutions with grants to scale and advance their projects.

For the event, Kate wore an oxblood, floor-length Anarkali dress by designer Mahima Mahajan and paired it with extra-fluffy, voluminous bohemian curls pinned in a half-updo.

An elegant, extra-glossy wavy hairstyle has long been a beauty signature for the princess, often teamed with a side part, hats, or a polished ponytail. But for the green carpet in India, the royal opted for a much more dramatic style that matches the glamorous mood of her outfit, with tighter, cascading bohemian curls woven into a beautiful half-updo twist for a romantic finish.

"I would create these curls using a medium-sized tong," recommends Philipp Haug, co-founder of Haug London Haus salon. "Take relatively small sections and use the tongs with a twisting technique, curling one section away from the face and alternating with one section towards the face throughout. Slightly root stretch when you come to the top of the head instead of curling too close to the scalp."

To enhance the fluffiness and volume of the curls, shake them out with your fingers instead of combing them.
