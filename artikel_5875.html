Kalshi halts Trump mention markets

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Kalshi has halted markets allowing users to trade on what President Trump might say on specific dates in response to an order by a federal regulator. The prediction market platform said the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) issued an order on Friday to pause Trump-related mention markets, and the company is “complying with the order.”…
