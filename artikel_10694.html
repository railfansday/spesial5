Tinder and Hinge ban AI agents from running profiles and dating for you

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

If you’re considering using AI agents for your dating life on Tinder or Hinge, think again, as measures have been put in place.

The ultimate goal of every dating app is for users to have a genuine connection with people they match with, and ultimately delete the app as they live happily ever after. That connection only works, however, if humans are involved.

There are plenty of inauthentic bot interactions on the apps, mostly fronted by scammers trying to score some cash. However, there has also been a rise in people using AI to do the talking for them.

These agents will match with people and start up conversations, saving time for the users behind the account. However, there has been a clampdown.

“At Match Group, we decided we don’t want A.I. agents to take over the functions of our dating apps, not even if they are representing real people,” Match CEO Spencer Rascoff said in the New York Times.

“We want to ensure humans are reviewing other humans’ dating profiles and sending messages to other users on our apps. That’s why we have put in place guardrails meant to prevent the use of AI agents on our platforms, like Tinder and Hinge.”

Rascoff added that Match is “urging our peers to slow down a bit and think twice about what is best” for their companies and users.

“If we let people use their A.I. agents to message potential dates on our apps, there’s a pretty low chance they’ll actually connect authentically, and, eventually, in person,” he also noted.

Tinder has already made changes to help with those ‘authentic’ connections, even launching in-person meetups.

The app has also introduced some AI-powered features, including Chemistry, which had a tool known as ‘Photo Enhance’ that had altered the appearance of some users.
