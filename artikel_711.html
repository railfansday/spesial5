Five-time Solheim Cup player Khang secures LPGA Tour card for 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The top-25 finishers and ties at the 2026 LPGA Q-Series final qualifying stage have been awarded LPGA Tour status for 2027; Megha Ganne won the event outright, while Megan Khang - part of Team USA for the past five Solheim Cups - is one of 27 players to claim a tour card for next season

Megha Ganne won the 2026 LPGA Q-Series final qualifying stage as 27 tour cards were claimed for the 2027 season, with five-time USA Solheim Cup team member Megan Khang among those successful.

Khang - part of Team USA for the past five Solheim Cups - is returning to the LPGA Tour, on which she's made 259 starts since 2016, winning the 2023 CPKC Women's Open.

The top-25 finishers and ties at the conclusion of the 2026 LPGA Q-Series final qualifying were awarded LPGA Tour status for next year.

American Ganne won the final stage outright on 23 under, one shot clear of Shannon Tan and Julia Gregg in a tie for second. Min Sun7 Kim was fourth on 21 under and Xiaowen Yin one further back in fifth.

Ganne, the 2025 US Amateur champion, had actually already secured her 2027 LPGA Tour status by entering the week by finishing 11-15 on the Epson Tour.

Gregg, who finished in a share of second, has never previously had a start on the LPGA Tour.

"It feels unreal," the 25-year-old American said after clinching her place. "It's been my dream since I was four years old to play on the LPGA and now that I finally did it."
