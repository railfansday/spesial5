T-Mobile Introduces T-Mix, an Innovative Phone Plan That Lets You Choose What’s Included

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

From Apple and Netflix to Delta and DoorDash, customers can pick and choose — and change — their benefits every three months.

While T-Mobile customers are no stranger to perks, a newly launched family of phone plans makes T-Mobile and partner benefits more personalized than ever. Introducing T-Mix: a customizable wireless lineup that lets customers pick and choose — and change — their benefits up to four times per year.

Here’s exactly how it works. First, pick your T-Mix plan: Standard, More or Loaded — each with distinct data offerings, features and a different number of benefit “Picks” (more on this below). Next, make your Picks, aka your customizable benefits across streaming, devices, travel, gaming and more. Then, enjoy four change windows per year (every three months) where there’s no added cost to switch partner benefits. T-Mobile’s Picks lineup can be broken up into three core categories: T-Mobile benefits (e.g. T-Satellite messaging or optimized video calls), partner benefits (e.g. ESPN Unlimited, YouTube Premium or DashPass by DoorDash) and device promotions that change regularly.

As for pricing, the Standard plan starts at $55 per month, the More plan starts at $75 per month and Loaded starts at $95, with prices increasing based on the number of lines selected. Similarly, more lines on an account equals more Picks customers can use.

To clarify, T-Mobile perks come with every plan; Picks are what customers choose on top. Perks, aka what customers get just for being a member, include Club Magenta access at events like Las Vegas Grand Prix, MLB All-Star Game, Lollapalooza and Stagecoach; ticket access and experiences at thousands of events; hotel and rental car perks; a Delta premium in-flight drink on every eligible flight; and T-Mobile Tuesdays (a weekly perks program that offered over $900 in freebies per customer last year).

Compare T-Mix plans, check out the full range of Picks (streaming, AI assistants, travel, etc.) and build your custom Picks lineup at https://t-mobile.com/T-Mix.
