Season over for Colts rookie from Center Grove. What we know

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

INDIANAPOLIS — Caden Curry's rookie season is over.

The Center Grove product tore a ligament in his wrist, according to Indianapolis head coach Shane Steichen, and the injury will force him to miss the rest of the season.

Curry will undergo surgery on his wrist, and the recovery will linger into the offseason.
