'Class act' Jude Bellingham praised for standing up for female journalist in chaotic post-match press huddle after he scored 'the best goal of my life'

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Published: 05:24 EST, 25 November 2026 | Updated: 02:27 EST, 26 November 2026

Jude Bellingham has earned praise after the Real Madrid star insisted a female reporter was able to finish asking her questions in the mixed zone after being interrupted.

Bellingham spoke to media post-match after scoring the 'best goal of my life' in Real Madrid's 5-1 victory over PSV in the Champions League on Tuesday.

The England star had collected a loose ball from around 25 yards outside the box, initially controlling it with his left foot, before flicking it up with his right foot and volleying it with the outside of that boot into the far corner of the PSV goal.

Bellingham's stunning strike meant he was unsurprisingly in-demand post-match, with reporters eager to get his assessment of his goal.

BBC reporter Elizabeth Conway had asked Bellingham in the mixed zone what had gone through his mind when he scored with his sensational effort.

'Yeah, I was a bit surprised honestly,' Bellingham said. 'I knew I could control the ball and get it into my grasp, but normally from there I am looking to see who's on as an option to play through.

'It popped up nicely and I thought, you know, I am just going to hit it.

'It was a nice goal and important for the team being the second goal and gave us control.'

Conway looked to follow-up with a second question but was interrupted by a male reporter, leading to Bellingham intervening.

'Sorry, she's got one more,' Bellingham said, while pointing to Conway to encourage her to continue her question.

When asked by Conway whether he feels like he is in the form of his life for club and country, Bellingham added: 'My mentality is based more around just focusing on the next game, I think you are only as good as your next one. Your last one doesn't really matter.

'Even this one that has just gone past, it is all well and good and speaking to you guys is fun but my focus has to change now to preparing for the next game.'

Conway later shared the exchange on social media, writing: 'Just Jude waiting for me to finish my questions,' adding a handshake emoji while fans were quick to praise Bellingham for his 'classy' moment.

Speaking post-match, Bellingham was clear where he ranked his goal, declaring: 'I think this was the best goal of my life.'

His brother Jobe, who played in Borussia Dortmund's 1-1 draw with Arsenal on the same night, later joked that Jude's shot had 'come off his shin' before acknowledging it was a 'great goal'.

Bellingham's performance, he also set up Vinicius Jr for the fifth goal, was acknowledged by Real Madrid boss Jose Mourinho and captain Fede Valverde, who scored their equaliser, post-match too.

'Obviously Jude is a different type of player and he gave us what we didn't have in the first half,' Mourinho said. 'He changed the game, scored an incredible goal.'

Valverde concurred declaring: 'He did well. We were struggling to keep the ball behind their midfielders and when Jude came in he gave us that tranquillity to control possession.'

The strike comes a day after Daily Mail Sport reported that the 23-year-old is in advanced talks over a new Real Madrid contract that will make him one of the best-paid players in Europe.

Madrid have been eager to tie down the in-form England midfielder beyond his current contract, understood to be worth around £12.5million a year after tax, which runs until 2029, in recognition of his performances.

Talks were initiated in September over an extension until 2031 and have been progressing well since with sources at Madrid confident they are close to reaching a conclusion.

Bellingham is expected to be handed improved terms to put him on par with team-mate Vinicius Jr, who agreed a new deal last summer reputedly on around £500,000 a week after tax.

Footage shared on social media also showed tennis legend Rafael Nadal and his young son exuberantly celebrating Bellingham's stunning goal against PSV.

Nadal last week was officially elected to Real Madrid's board of directors at the club's Extraordinary General Assembly.
