How Vinicius Jr's lavish summer holiday has left him playing catch up: Real Madrid star admits he 'trained less' while on luxury yacht trip and blames Brazil's early World Cup exit - and 'overwhelming' Arsenal rumours - for sluggish start to the season

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Published: 09:07 EST, 16 November 2026 | Updated: 09:16 EST, 16 November 2026

Vinicius Junior has admitted his lavish summer holiday after Brazil's last-16 World Cup exit is behind his slow start with Real Madrid this season.

And the winger says he felt 'overwhelmed' by the interest of clubs such as Arsenal  and insecurity around his contract renewal talks in Madrid.

He has scored one goal and provided three assists in 12 games this season, and amid the criticism, manager Jose Mourinho has even said he should have left this summer to go somewhere he is 'respected'.

Vini Jr said: 'I don’t think I prepared better after the World Cup because the impact of this tournament is very significant for all Brazilians. Losing so early was something we didn’t expect.

'So I took advantage of the holidays to enjoy myself, rest, relax, and spend more time with my family. I ended up training less than in recent years. This may have affected my start to the season.

'Also, regarding my contract renewal, with so many clubs interested last summer, I felt really overwhelmed, as did the situation at Real Madrid. But the season is very long.

'Maintaining such a high level for so long is always very difficult. I believe only Cristiano Ronaldo and (Lionel) Messi have managed to maintain it for so many years, achieving the same numbers.

'I'm human, I make mistakes often, but I always try to improve. I try to understand more about football, about the day-to-day, but I'm 26 years old and I have to enjoy life a little too.

'Last season, until December, I had only scored five goals. Then, I finished with more than 20 goals, as in all other seasons. I believe I am preparing, evolving, because, in the most decisive moment of the season, I am always there.'

Vini Jr spent part of his summer break in his home city of Sao Goncalo, as well as visiting Jamaica, Italy, and Dubai.

His Instagram was full of pictures of him relaxing on yachts, speeding around on a private jet, and showing off his fashionable outfits.

Arsenal enquired about the Brazilian superstar and reports suggested they were willing to offer him a mammoth £18.5million per season - more than any player at the club.

His contract expires at the end of the season and the club's position is that he must lower his demands if they are to agree an extension. They would still be willing to make him their joint-highest earner alongside Kylian Mbappe.

He has often been a target of racism in Spain - there have been more than 20 such cases of alleged abuse towards him since he joined from Flamengo in 2018. The situation has reduced him to tears before, but he has insisted on staying in La Liga so that he doesn't 'give the racists what they want'.

The winger - who has scored one goal and provided three assists this season - was a target for Arsenal and could leave Madrid in January

The winger is currently serving a four-match ban for pulling Villarreal star Nathan Saliba's hair.

And Mourinho said last month: 'If I were in his shoes, I probably wouldn't be here. I would have forced a move to a country where he'd be respected, but out of loyalty to Real Madrid and love for the club, he stayed.

'He believes things can improve, and he still has that smile. Nothing and no one can take it away from him.

'He's a fantastic guy, and it's easy to get along with him. I spend a few minutes alone with him every day.

'I value him immensely because he always manages to live and survive with a smile.'

Vinicius has been a phenomenon at Real Madrid, scoring 129 times in 388 games from the wing.

But the balance of power shifted to Mbappe when he joined from Paris Saint-Germain in 2024 and talk of his future has increased.

There is a chance that, if Madrid go a third season in a row without significant silverware, they choose to rebuild and go in a different direction.

Moving away from the heavy 'superstar' model has worked for PSG and rewarded them with two Champions League titles in a row.

Vinicius had a tricky summer, being knocked out of the last 16 of the World Cup with Brazil and then missing his pre-season return date as his representatives tried to revive talks over a contract extension.

If an agreement cannot be found on a new deal, Madrid could be open to selling in January, rather than lose him for free next summer.

Last year Al Ahli were willing to offer a €1bn five-year contract offer for the 2024 Ballon d'Or runner-up.

Liverpool were also credited with interest earlier this window but are not in the mix for a player who earns around £520,000 a week.
