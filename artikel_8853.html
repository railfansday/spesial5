wade and leta shape human-powered kinetic landscape for play in shanghai sculpture park

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

featuring a four-sided slide, multiple spinners embedded within the ground, and sky-high kinetics, this work transforms sculpture into an exhilarating experience of play for people of all ages.

Leta edited by christina vergopoulou November 25, 2026 3 min

Installed in Shanghai’s Jing’an Sculpture Park for the ninth edition of the Jing’an International Sculpture Project (2026 JISP), Catching Feelings is an interactive sculptural playscape created by multidisciplinary duo Wade and Leta. Curated by Cheng Jing with UCCA Lab of the UCCA Center for Contemporary Art under the exhibition theme Shapes of Becoming, which brings together 33 artist groups from 14 countries alongside figures such as Olafur Eliasson, Tony Cragg, and Jean-Michel Othoniel, the installation translates Wade and Leta’s graphic visual language into three-dimensional public infrastructure. Rather than relying on wind or motorized mechanisms as in the duo’s earlier kinetic sculptures, the work is activated directly through physical occupation across age groups.

Designed by creative duo Wade and Leta to encourage tactile engagement in response to screen-based isolation, the installation integrates a central four-sided steel slide, multiple ground-embedded spinners, and overhead aerial bars into a single climbable structure. By combining sculptural materiality, scale, and weight with functional play equipment, the piece shifts between static public artwork and active civic gathering space, generating enough visitor use to require scheduled daily opening and closing hours for crowd management.
