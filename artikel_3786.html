AI-generated GTA 6 PC ports get nuked in alleged Rockstar takedown spree, as former Bungie dev hails "most ridiculous dumpster fire ever"

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

3007 Github repos allegedly DMCA'd at a stroke

One of the creators of an unofficial AI-generated PC "port" of Grand Theft Auto 6 claims that Rockstar and Take-Two Interactive have already stepped in to shut down the project, though the fearful work of slop-and-pasting GTA 6 to PC appears to be continuing in other hands. Titled "Leonida", after the game's setting, the port is a recompilation – that is, a translation of the game's original code into code that'll run natively on different hardware, rather than being live translated by an emulator as you play. It's based on an incomplete GTA 6 Xbox build of the game, including the all-important executable file, which was leaked earlier in the week by crypto grifter Cyberleek.

As documented by former Bungie developer Amy Hendricks in an increasingly depressed Bluesky thread (excerpt: "This is the most ridiculous dumpster fire ever"), Leonida is or was being made using Claude and Google's Antigravity tools. Going by Hendricks's posts, which are based on watching a Leonida stream, much of the recomp's creation seems to have consisted of one or more high school kids typing prompts like "work faster", while spending hundreds or thousands of dollars in usage fees.

The Leonida team appear to have succeeded in getting the game to boot on PC, but two key members, NotRexed and Shayder, have now withdrawn from the project and scrubbed any mention of it from their social media feeds.

In posts preserved by cheery RPS fanzine PCGamer, NotRexed shared a snapshot of a copyright infringement takedown notification from Discord. They explained their decision to call it quits as follows: "Rockstar taking my server down was only part of the reason. Like I’ve said before, I’m a 15 year old kid. Managing school and sports is hard enough without having middle aged men yell and complain to you on Discord." I myself am a middle aged man, but don't despair, NotRexed! I am not going to yell at you. I am simply going to reach for a moderately small violin.

As reported by PCG, the Leonida Discord server has now been shut down, and the Github repository for the recompilation's code has been nuked. Hendricks has shared an alleged takedown request from a representative of Take-Two Interactive, according to which Github have taken action against 3,007 forks (that is, duplicates for the purposes of making a separate version) from the original Leonida repo. Meanwhile, somebody has set up a leaderboard to chart the progress of several other GTA 6 recomp projects. I'm sure Take-Two's IP enforcement teams appreciate their efforts.

A few days prior to GTA 6's release on console last week, Rockstar North's former tech director Obbe Vermeij told RPS that the GTA makers would release the PC version sooner than anticipated to avoid losing any cash to generative AI rip-offs. IGN subsequently asked Take-Two's CEO Strauss Zelnick for his thoughts on this. Zelnick replied that the company are "not in the least cavalier" about the risk, but are confident people would rather play the game as Rockstar intended.
