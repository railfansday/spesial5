Girls und Panzer Hakunetsu! Mahjong Tag Battle Desu! announced for PS5, Switch 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

MAGES. has announced Girls und Panzer Hakunetsu! Mahjong Tag Battle Desu! for PlayStation 5 and Switch 2. It will launch in 2027 in Japan.

Here is an overview of the game, via its official website:

A Girls und Panzer mahjong game is finally here!

The game will feature 30 characters, including the Oarai Girls’ Academy Anglerfish Team!

A tag-team mahjong game!? Featuring a story mode!? Online battles!?

Please look forward to more details on gameplay, characters, and more.

Some time after the battle against the All-Stars University Team to save Oarai Girls Academy from closure, a mahjong tournament between the strongest tankery schools is held.

The heads of the various tankery schools seem to have proposed that mahjong could be used as a tool to help students develop their tactical skills and competitive instincts, as well as serve as a form of mental training.

Furthermore, upon learning that the winning school would receive a prize granting it a wish to be fulfilled within as much reason as possible, students from each school became eager to join the mahjong tournament, each with their own motives.
