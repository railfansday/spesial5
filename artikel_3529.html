Sony Is Updating The PS5 Again, And It Might Have GTA 6 Pirates In Mind

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A new PS5 firmware update has dropped, but even at a chunky 1.2GB download, version 26.07-14.23.00 doesn't include a comprehensive list of notes.

"We've made some security fixes to the system software," the very short update notes read. Notably, this is the fourth PS5 firmware update since October 1, and typically there's a buffer period of several months between downloads. While Sony hasn't confirmed it, the current thinking is that the company is shoring up its defenses for the release of GTA 6 to prevent the game from being pirated.

The new firmware will be mandatory for new game releases as well, including GTA 6, which means that PS5 hackers and homebrew hobbyists have their work cut out for them. The hacking scene recently made big advances on the console, jailbreaking systems that have update 14 and below installed, and thus allowing more pirated games to be played. Sony is under pressure to prevent GTA 6 from being pirated, and with the PS5 console holding the lion's share of GTA 6 preorders, there's plenty of financial incentive to keep the game secure on that platform.

Security researchers and hackers have been leveraging generative AI and automated decompilation analysis to discover lower-level system vulnerabilities and dump game binaries much faster, creating an arms race between Sony and hackers.  Every time a potential vulnerability or homebrew exploit surfaces--like the recent vulnerabilities that targeted PS5 consoles running 13.60 and 14.22 firmware--Sony rushes out a mandatory console patch to plug the hole.

Beyond security measures, we've also seen base PS5 consoles updated with Quick Spectral Super Resolution (QSSR), which is also rolling out to more games. An AI-based upscaling technology derived from the PS5 Pro's PSSR, it initially supported only Marvel's Wolverine and Ghost of Yotei.
