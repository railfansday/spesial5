Jenna Ushkowitz Says ‘Glee’ Was ‘Never the Same’ After Cory Monteith’s Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Glee actress Jenna Ushkowitz found it especially difficult to mourn her costar Cory Monteith while filming the TV series.

“Well, the passing of Cory was just absolutely tragic and for a million reasons. The show itself was never the same. He was the heart of the show,” Ushkowitz, 40, said on the Tuesday, November 24, episode of the “We Need to Talk” podcast. “[He was] our friend, most importantly, and our leader. He really was the quarterback for us, he held everybody together.”

She continued, “He hosted parties for the crew, he was the kindest, he knew everybody’s names. He just was like such a good person, kind person. So, you know, that was just the worst.”

Monteith, who starred as quarterback Finn Hudson on Glee, died in July 2013 at the age of 31 after suffering an overdose.

“When Cory passed, I think it brought us back together and glued us and we all were like, ‘Oh, it was existential of, like, life is short. Not just the show, not just this thing that’s going to expire, like life,’” Ushkowitz noted. “It brought us together in a really beautiful way, and nobody had the tools to deal with that, even our creators, like, they were dealing with grief too.”

In the wake of Monteith’s death, Glee wrote off Finn in a season 5 episode titled “The Quarterback,” which was filmed around five weeks after he died. (Glee wrapped in 2015 after six seasons. Creators Ryan Murphy, Brad Falchuk and Ian Brennan are now developing a reboot.)

“To shoot ‘The Quarterback,’ I think it wasn’t the right thing, and I think everybody collectively agrees that that’s maybe not the thing we should have done because we were grieving both privately and publicly,” Ushkowitz alleged. “I think the fans definitely needed something because they loved Cory so dearly, like we did, and Finn so dearly, but it’s a really hard thing to be grieving your friend and being a character grieving their friend at the same time. It was like too soon for that.”

Ushkowitz further stressed that she doesn’t “want to remember that time” mourning Monteith’s death while filming a tribute episode to his character.

In addition to Monteith, the Glee stars have lost fellow actors Mark Salling and Naya Rivera in 2018 and 2020, respectively, sparking unfounded theories of a show “curse.”

“It’s a really big cast and crew, so we are going to lose people. It’s life, right? My response to the Glee curse, as it’s always been, is we are human,” she stated. “To hear that people see this as a collective curse that, like, ‘Who’s next?’ is so unkind. It shows the way in which we are able to just sit behind our computers and type off whatever we think is, you know, we’re feeling in that moment without really connecting to what’s behind it and who’s on the other side.”

Ushkowitz concluded, “We lost friends, people grieve those people. This is not something that’s like, ‘Who’s next? This is a game.’ It’s not; enough said.”
