James Bond’s Favorite Luggage Brand Has Broken Its Own Rules

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Globe-Trotter has spent more than a century perfecting its signature suitcase. Now it's trying something different.

James Bond has always travelled in style. And while Daniel Craig's 007 was most immediately associated with his Aston Martins, Omega watches, and Tom Ford suits, his luggage of choice was Globe-Trotter, the Hertfordshire-based manufacturer whose cases have also found favor with everyone from Queen Elizabeth II to Kate Moss.

Now the company has done something completely left-field. After 129 years of making essentially the same kind of suitcase, it has changed the formula.

The new GT897 is Globe-Trotter's lightest case yet, and a significant departure from the design that made the brand famous. Out go the traditional rigid frame and locks, replaced by a zip-around opening and softer, more streamlined silhouette.

That said, some familiar details keep it firmly on-brand: the vulcanized fiberboard, leather trim, nickel hardware, and those trademark exposed rivets. And the cases are still handcrafted in Hertfordshire.

Currently available in two color combinations—bland-and-black and green-and-brown—the GT897 is priced at $1,395. A black-and-oxblood version follows in February.

It's an interesting move for a company whose USP has always rested somewhat on its refusal to change. Whether the new James Bond will follow Craig's lead remains to be seen. But at least there's now a new option for packing his tuxedo.
