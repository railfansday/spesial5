‘Saint-Pierre’ Season 3 Trailer Sees New Faces Shake Up Life For Fitz & Arch

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

EXCLUSIVE: Here’s the trailer for Season 3 of this week’s Global Breakout, Saint-Pierre.

The CBC drama series, which stars Allan Hawco and Jósephine Jobert as a cop duo on a small island off Newfoundland, is returning to Canadian screens on January 4, 2027, and we’ve got a first video for the new season.

We’ve also got some new information on Season 3, which we’ll keep vague to avoid spoilers on previous seasons. The story begins with Jobert’s Acting Chief of the SPMP Geneviève Archambault (Arch) and Royal Newfoundland Constabulary Inspector Donald Fitzpatrick (Fitz) racing to keep the peace on the France-administered Saint-Pierre as a gang war erupts. An organized crime expert, Pascal Lalonde (Assaad Bouab), is sent from Paris as the new police chief and his presence destabilizes Arch professionally and personally, with the pair sharing a previous connection. “What kind of Wild West cop shop have you been running?” he asks her just before the trailer cuts to a deceased young woman covered head to toe in a clay-like substance being lifted out of a trunk. Watch on Deadline

As an aside, Bouab played a Saint-Pierre bartender in Peaky Blinders in a storyline about smuggling channels, though those scenes were shot in Manchester, UK, meaning this was his first time on the island. Saint-Pierre is shot on location.

Season 3 will also introduce mysterious businesswoman Victoria Peters, who arrives on the island with a vision to make it a top tourist destination and instantly has an intense chemistry with Fitz. French-Canadian actress Caroline Dhavernas is playing the character.

Saint-Pierre‘s third season runs to 12 episodes. Hawco Productions is back to produce for CBC and streamer CBC Gem, with Fifth Season once again handling international distribution. Outside of Canada, the show goes out on Sky in New Zealand, Disney+ across Eastern Europe and Belgium, ABC in Australia, FreeTV in Israel, BBC Studios in the Netherlands and Poland, and on U&Alibi in the UK, and France Télévisions.

Given its big ratings in Canada, where it became CBC‘s most popular show in the 2025/26 season and among the most watched across Canada, we selected it as our Global Breakout this week. Fifth Season has struck a slew of deals, but has not sold it into the U.S. yet.

Hawco – who writes, produces and stars in the show – told us this week: “We’re super happy with how it has been performing in Canada… We’ve also been doing very well internationally, and it’s only a matter of time before we find the right home in the U.S. market. Our partner Fifth Season leads the way in sales outside Canada, and I know they’ve been very selective about where that home should be.”

Hawco is the show’s co-creator alongside Robina Lord-Stafford.
