UK may add Vinted and eBay to its under-16 social media ban

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Culture secretary Lisa Nandy cited grooming risks, though the government said in June that e-commerce sites would be exempt.

Lisa Nandy, Secretary of State for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport.

Her comments followed a report in the Sunday Times that the ban would be widened to include the two resale sites. The paper said the concern was that messaging tools let strangers groom children. It said Depop and Facebook Marketplace could also be added.

However, the change would go further than the original plan. When the government announced the ban in June, it said it did not intend to include e-commerce platforms.

Both eBay and Vinted already say users must be at least 18, according to the BBC. eBay told the BBC that the details of the proposal were not yet clear, and that it would keep working with the government. Vinted did not respond to the BBC.

The ban will cover sites whose main purpose is social interaction and that let users post content. Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, and YouTube are among them. The government has not yet published a full list.

Meanwhile, Ofcom set out last month four ways to check users’ ages, including photo ID and facial age estimation. A similar ban took effect in Australia last year, where many children have got around it.
