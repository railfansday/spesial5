Tom Brady breaks silence on Birmingham City exit but snubs Tom Wagner

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Seven-time NFL Super Bowl champion Tom Brady has broken his silence after his departure from Birmingham City's hierarchy was confirmed on Monday night.

Brady had been involved with the club following Knighthead Capital Management's takeover at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park back in 2023.

The past three years have, in typical Blues fashion, been extremely dramatic, with a first relegation to League One in 30 years followed by the appointment of Chris Davies and a season in which the EFL's all-time record points total was broken.

Birmingham are currently top of the Championship after 15 games, with Saturday's dramatic 2-1 victory against Swansea City enough to see them lead the way on goal difference.

As such, there has been plenty of discourse surrounding the timing of Brady's exit from his role as Advisory Board Chairman, especially if Davies' side do go on to end their lengthy exile from the Premier League.

Brady's involvement with the club had been well-documented, particularly through the Amazon Prime docuseries which he was at the front and centre of, which showcased developments behind-the-scenes during Birmingham's 2023/24 and 2024/25 campaigns.

Knighthead's completion of a full takeover 12 months ago meant Brady reportedly owned just 3.3% of the club, although he was only present for four of the club's matches since his initial association was confirmed ahead of the 1-0 win against Leeds United three years ago.

As well as his involvement with Birmingham, the 49-year-old is a regular pundit on NFL matches for FOX Sports, having signed a lucrative $375m 10-year contract with the company four years ago.

Blues released their own statement regarding Brady's departure on Monday, and after a rather cryptic Instagram post, Brady then took to his story to issue his own words to those associated with the Championship side.

"Being part of Birmingham City Football Club the last three years has been one of the most rewarding journeys of my life," he explained.

"Thank you to the players, Chris Davies and his staff, the front office, and to everyone working behind-the-scenes, whose commitment I saw up close at every level of the club. Most of all, thank you to the people of Birmingham.

"Your passion, your loyalty, and the way you show up for this club through every high and every hard day are what make it so special. I'll always be rooting for your success," Brady added.

Despite only being present at St. Andrew's on a smattering of occasions, the aforementioned series proved just how invested Brady was in Birmingham's short and long-term success, even if there was no mention of Wagner within his recent social media revelation.

The American caused a stir when he raised concerns over the work ethic of former head coach, Wayne Rooney, during a disastrous 15-game spell which saw the club plummet down the Championship table.

A 3-1 win over Wrexham in September 2024 was arguably the most high-profile game in recent League One history, with Red Dragons owner, Rob Mac, present alongside Brady for the game, as well as other global stars such as Gary Neville and David Beckham.

Brady was also a key part of the video which unveiled the name and design of the club's upcoming Powerhouse stadium that will be the centrepiece of a multi-billion Sports Quarter project.

Birmingham have been going great guns in this season's Championship, losing just twice and winning five of their last six matches in the process.

A differing approach to transfers this summer has also aided such success, with the likes of Max Bird and Liam Millar making instant impressions, whilst Jay Stansfield and August Priske have flourished in attacking roles within a new tactical setup.

Davies' side have also conceded the fewest number of goals in the division thus far - 14 - and their on-pitch progress will certainly not be impacted by Brady's exit.
