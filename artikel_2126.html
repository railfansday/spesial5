Unseen moment Rangers star warned 'job's not done' by team-mate after semi-final win

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Ivor Pandur delivered a stern warning to one of his Rangers team-mates after advancing to the final of the Premier Sports Cup.

The goalkeeper was caught telling Findlay Curtis to cool his celebrations at Hampden as he insisted the 'job is not done'.

Rangers posted a clip on social media of the players leaving the park in Glasgow after Ryan Naderi's late goal sent them through to the final. Rangers will face Hibernian next month.

And while the players were understandably jubilant as they were greeted by coaching staff and club staff as they left the park, Pandur wasn't allowing anyone to get carried away.

Curtis was passing the camera when he let out a loud celebratory cheer. Pandur then stepped in to motion to the young Scotland winger to calm down.

Pandur could then be seen and heard saying: "Easy bro, job is not done, bro."

After leading Rangers into the final, Derek McInnes commented: "I'd like to have enjoyed it a wee bit more. Enjoyment comes now, to be honest, a wee bit of satisfaction when I see the lads in the dressing room celebrating together.

"But the job now is to go and win the thing, and we'll get six weeks now between now and then where we need to get better at things I just spoke about.

"The players who are in the team at the minute need to play well to stay in the team if they want to be in the cup final, and the players who are not in the team need to keep performing and doing all they can to get in the team.

"We need to drive each other on because we want to get into the cup final on the back of some really strong league form.

"We're on a really strong run just now in terms of wins and being unbeaten over a period of time now. But we know we're not perfect.

"It's important that we keep searching for those improvements. But if we can keep winning, keep putting ourselves in cup finals and keep ourselves satisfied with results, we can keep working on performances."
