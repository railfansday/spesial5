Happy Monday! Bitcoin is trading around $65,000 after falling to a four-month low near $62,000. Binance founder Changpeng Zhao denied that the exchange had sold crypto amid the market downturn.

Bitcoin fell to around $62,000 over the weekend, its lowest level since July, before recovering to about $65,000 now.

Bitwise projects Solana's SOL token could reach $1,100 by 2031 in its bull scenario, compared with $385 in its base case and $80 in its bear case.

Michael Saylor's Strategy bought another 485 BTC for $33.4 million between Nov. 9 and Nov. 15, at an average price of $68,887 per bitcoin.

The U.S. Supreme Court declined to hear Custodia Bank's challenge over its denied Federal Reserve master account application.

Former UK Prime Minister Boris Johnson said London should lead in blockchain development and asset tokenization, eight months after likening bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies to a Ponzi scheme.
