‘I’ve Built Stronger Arms in My 40s Than I Had in My 20s—These Are the 3 Exercises I Never Skip’

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Perimenopause strength coach Chrissa Benson says these three dumbbell exercises have helped her build stronger, more muscular arms than she had two decades ago.

Building (and maintaining) upper-body strength as we age is crucial. As well as supporting everyday, functional movements such as lifting, pushing, pulling and reaching, having strong arms can help us get back up again if we were to fall—ultimately meaning we can remain independent for longer.

Sarcopenia (age-related muscle loss) can begin to affect us as early as the age of 30. From then, research suggests that muscle mass decreases by approximately 3-8% per decade—with the rate of decline speeding up after 60.

But the good news is that we aren’t powerless in slowing that decline. There’s lots of things we can do, with strength training being one of the most important.

As well as building, well, strength, such training supports other areas of health, too—with one recent study associating two hours of strength work a week with a 22% lower risk of developing major cardiovascular disease and a 44% lower risk of having a heart attack.

Someone who is well-acquainted with the benefits of strength training is certified perimenopause strength coach and fitness content creator Chrissa Benson, who says she has more muscle now in her 40s than she ever did in her 20s.

And recently, on Instagram, she shared three arm exercises that have helped her build some of that muscle. In fact, Benson added that she now never skips them.
