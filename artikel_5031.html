Solana Foundation Backs CoinGecko as Crypto Data Giant Eyes Trading

CoinGecko, one of crypto’s most widely used price-tracking sites, wants to do more than show users where markets are heading.

The crypto data platform said Tuesday that it has received backing from the Solana Foundation, the Switzerland-based nonprofit that supports the Solana network, to speed its expansion into market intelligence, new digital asset products and, potentially, trading.

The announcement came on the final day of Solana’s Breakpoint conference in London.

The Solana Foundation did not specify the size of the commitment, but a representative for the foundation told Decrypt it is taking a majority stake in CoinGecko. The terms were not disclosed.

CoinGecko said it will focus on three areas: expanding data coverage and analytics across blockchain ecosystems, exploring trading features that let users act onchain, and building simpler experiences for users worldwide.

“We’ve helped millions of people discover and understand this industry, but the opportunity ahead is much bigger,” said Bobby Ong, CoinGecko’s CEO and co-founder.

Vibhu Norby, chief product officer at the Solana Foundation, said the foundation believes the “next wave of adoption will come from making crypto dramatically easier to use.” (Disclosure: Decrypt’s parent company, Dastan, is building products on Solana, and Decrypt uses CoinGecko market data.)

Founded in 2014, CoinGecko says it provides data on more than 50 million crypto assets across more than 2,000 exchanges.

CoinGecko said it will keep serving users with the same commitment to “reliable, comprehensive market coverage and transparent methodologies.” Co-founder and President TM Lee said its commitment to accurate, reliable data “remains unchanged.”

The deal is one of several Solana announcements timed around Breakpoint. DTCC said last week that it plans to connect its tokenization service to Solana, with tokenized stocks, ETFs and Treasuries expected on the network in the second half of 2027.

At a Breakpoint panel, executives from DTCC and Barclays said tokenization’s biggest near-term payoff lies in putting idle collateral to work.

Editor's note: This article was updated after publication to include comment from the Solana Foundation.
