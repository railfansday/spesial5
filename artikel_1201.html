Report: Mendes offers Conceição to Newcastle as Juventus weigh Woltemade swap

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Francisco Conceição has been offered to Newcastle United, and Juventus could look to use the winger to help keep Nick Woltemade beyond this season.

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, via TuttoMercatoWeb, Conceição’s agent Jorge Mendes has put his client’s name forward to the Magpies ahead of next summer.

No talks have taken place yet, but the Portuguese winger isn’t short of admirers in England. Liverpool and Manchester United both made enquiries about him in the summer, without following them up.

Woltemade is the key as the German striker joined the Bianconeri late in the summer window on a straight loan from Newcastle, which expires on 30 June 2027 with no option to buy. If Juventus want to keep him, they will have to negotiate a new deal with the Premier League club, and Newcastle’s interest in Conceição could give them leverage on the price.

Selling him would also make financial sense. By next summer, the 24-year-old will be on Juventus’ books for less than €20 million, so any fee above that would be a significant capital gain.

The player himself, however, is happy in Turin and has no plans to leave. Luciano Spalletti also sees him as an important part of his attack.

Conceição has been out for around 40 days with a left shoulder problem, but is fit again and is expected to start on the right against Omonia in the Europa League tonight. Kick-off at the Allianz Stadium is at 21:00 CET [20:00 GMT]. You can follow all the action, as it happens, on our Live Blog.
