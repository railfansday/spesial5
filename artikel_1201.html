Flamengo director confirms minimum price tag for Roma-linked Samuel Lino

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Flamengo director José Boto insists the Brazilian club will sell Roma-linked Samuel Lino only for a ‘very high offer’ but refuses to confirm Giallorossi interest.

Several sources report that Flamengo wing-back Samuel Lino is on Roma’s shopping list. Earlier this week, RTI Esporte even claimed that the Giallorossi were ready to submit a €45m bid for the 26-year-old.

“Several clubs are interested, but I won’t say whether Roma is among them,” Flamengo director Boto told TMW.

“I can confirm that the interest in him is concrete, and we receive inquiries about the player every day. We bought Lino at a favourable age for a possible future sale. We paid €25m for him, and obviously we won’t sell him for less. I don’t even know if we’ll sell him at all, unless we receive a very high offer.”

Samuel Lino joined Flamengo from Atletico Madrid in the summer of 2025.

The Brazil international has scored 13 goals and provided nine assists in 35 appearances in the Brazilian Serie A this season.

Roma already did business with Flamengo in recent years, signing Brazilian full-back Wesley for €25m plus add-ons in 2025. The Brazilian club used the proceeds from Wesley’s sale to sign Samuel Lino from Atletico Madrid.

Roma sit second in the Serie A table with 30 points from 12 matches and are the only unbeaten team, alongside their city rivals Lazio, who lead the table.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
