Personal Consumer Issues • Re: ventless dryer

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

If you are going to do this, I’d definitely test the brand before hand. Bring towels or something to try.

Many (all?) popular European brands like Miele leave the clothes damp by *design* because apparently that’s how Europeans like it. They let them fully dry after folding them. My guess is it’s because their energy costs are way more here, so their consumer preferences are different than ours.

Every time I stay at European hotels this is a pain, but that’s how it is.

Some newer Miele’s have modes which are North American friendly like “Cotton Plus” on the Miele at the hotel I’m at now. It doesn’t dry as well as my dirt cheap $400 whirlpool dry at home, but it’s better.

So I’d test it out to make sure you are ok with this.

They do eventually dry on the shelf, but they are noticeably more wrinkled than if you get them bone dry like an NA dryer would.
