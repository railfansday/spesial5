‘Grand Theft Auto VI’: What The Critics Are Saying As The Rockstar Games Series Finally Returns

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The sixth edition in the long-running Rockstar Games series returned this morning, and it has earned rave reviews from critics.

In a glowing New York Times review titled Grand Theft Auto VI Will Steal Your Heart, writer Yussef Cole said the game showed “surprising depth,” anchored by its two main characters, Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, and a rich in-game world.

“The nature of the narrative is less one of rags to riches than a story about desperation and survival, and the tension of throwing in one’s lot with untrustworthy allies,” Cole wrote. Cole added that the game “largely lives up to its sky-high expectations.” Watch on Deadline

In a five-star review, The Guardian said Rockstar has created “the most extraordinarily, indulgently detailed virtual world that we have yet seen.”

“Reviewing it feels a bit like trying to review, say, Paris: it contains so many potential experiences that it’s hard to know where to start,” the critic joked.

The Washington Post described GTA VI as an “astonishing American epic” in another glowing review.

“Written, programmed and performed by a cast and crew of tens of thousands, Grand Theft Auto VI is more than a typical video game; it is a great gathering of art forms and talent, an unruly, comprehensive cultural atlas of American life and manners,” the newspaper said. “Its triumph lies in encounters I just described, where race, class, gender, money and attention shape our circumstances and what we decide to do about them.”

Bloomberg has said the game is a “masterpiece” that depicts such “lifelike graphical fidelity that it can be hard to distinguish the video game from real-life footage.”

Gaming outlet IGN said Grand Theft Auto 6 was “one of the most impressive games ever made” and praised specific in-game details such as the extensive tracklist.

“The mammoth tracklist otherwise includes the return of classic stations V-Rock, Emotion, and Vice City FM, all of which are absolutely overflowing with solid gold (as is new metal station The Chamber),” the outlet wrote. “Just those four stations alone – which account for the lion’s share of my listening – feature nearly 200 songs between them.”

In a comprehensive review, Empire Magazine described the game as a “grand satire of Americana” that hits “painfully close to the mark.”

“A twisted reflection of the contemporary US that makes for a damning, if wildly entertaining, state of the union,” the magazine concluded.

Grand Theft Auto 6 currently holds a 99 rating on Metacritic, matching The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the highest rating ever given to a game on the site.

GTA 6 reportedly crossed $1.5 billion in revenue two days before its release, thanks to pre-orders, according to market intelligence firm Sensor Tracker.

“Next to James Bond, there might not be another franchise of this scale that has been as consistent with its brand,” Larry Vincent, professor of Marketing at USC Marshall School of Business, told Deadline’s Dominic Patten in a interview for a lengthy piece on the game’s release.
