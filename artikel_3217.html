Will Ferrell Comedy ‘The Hawk’ Canceled By Netflix After One Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

EXCLUSIVE: The Hawk did not make the cut for a second season. Netflix has opted not to renew the freshman golf comedy, co-created, executive produced and headlined by Will Ferrell. The news comes more than four months after The Hawk, SNL alum Ferrell’s first TV starring vehicle, premiered on July 16.

The decision fits Netflix’s performance vs. cost renewal criteria. The Hawk was believed to be among the most expensive comedy series on Netflix, with its star Ferrell commanding a seven-figure episodic salary. It spent three weeks in Netflix’s Global Top 10 for English series, peaking at No.2 with 6.2M views for its first full week, a respectable but not outsized delivery.

The show did not score well with critics, averaging 33% in reviews on Rotten Tomatoes but did better with core Will Ferrell fans for a 65% audience score. Watch on Deadline

The Hawk‘s fate was telegraphed by co-creator/executive producer Harper Steele during an interview on Late Night with Seth Meyers two weeks ago in which she said, in passing, “I was working on a short-lived but probably the best show ever, The Hawk.”

Ferrell remains in business with Netflix. Via Gloria Sanchez Productions, he executive produces the streamer’s upcoming series All the Rage, Prep as well as Nikki Glaser’s first scripted show, developed through Netflix Is a Joke Studios.

Of the comedy series that have released new seasons after The Hawk‘s debut, Netflix has renewed Nobody Wants This, whose third season delivered the biggest 2026 opening week for a returning scripted comedy, Tires and freshman A Different World. Leanne is awaiting word on a Season 3 pickup; it’s too early for Minimum Wage.

Upcoming Netflix comedy series also include Uncorked, A Hundred Percent and I Suck at Girls.

In The Hawk, Lonnie Hawkins (Ferrell), 2004’s number one golfer, struggles on the back nine of his career to recapture his magic. His body says retire, but his heart says he’s not done yet. His ex-wife and his son Lance, golf’s new golden boy, know he’s through. But with one more major to win to complete golf’s Grand Slam, Lonnie refuses to believe he’s anything other than one stroke away from the greatest comeback in golf history.

In the series, Ferrell reunited with former SNL co-star Molly Shannon who played his foul-mouthed ex-wife. Jimmy Tatro, Fortune Feimster, Luke Wilson, Chris Parnell, Katelyn Tarver and David Hornsby also starred in The Hawk, which was co-created by Ferrell, Steele and Chris Henchy.

Ferrell, Jessica Elbaum and Alix Taylor executive produced for Gloria Sanchez Productions. Rian Johnson, Ram Bergman and Nena Rodrigue were executive producers for T-Street. Henchy, Steele, David Gordon Green and Andrew Guest also executive produced.
