YouTube True-Crime Channel Dr. Insanity Launches Podcast With Audioboom

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Earlier this summer, Ed Simpson teamed with Content Partners to buy YouTube true-crime channel Dr. Insanity.

Wonderloom Media, founded by Wheelhouse’s former chief strategy officer and the investment firm, is now launching a Dr. Insanity podcast.

They have teamed with Audioboom on the project. Audioboom is behind podcasts including Pod Save America and The Tim Dillon Show and distributes a number of true-crime shows such as True Crime with Kendall Rae and True Crime Obsessed with Patrick Hinds and Gillian Pensavalle. “Dr. Insanity is becoming the home of true crime, and that means meeting fans wherever they listen and watch,” said Ed Simpson, CEO of Wonderloom Media. “Our audience has always gone deep with us, and so many of them already live with true crime in their ears. Audioboom shares our commitment to great storytelling, and we’re excited to bring Dr. Insanity to a whole new community of listeners together.”

“Dr. Insanity has built an incredible audience on YouTube by delivering compelling, high-quality true crime storytelling,” said Brendan Regan, Chief Content Officer at Audioboom. “We see a tremendous opportunity to bring that same experience to podcast listeners and introduce the brand to new audiences. We’re excited to partner with the Wonderloom team to expand Dr. Insanity’s reach and build on its success across platforms.”
