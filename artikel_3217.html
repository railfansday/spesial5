New Apple TV comedy starring Elizabeth Banks, Ted Danson coming soon

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Apple TV has announced a new comedy, She’s Fine, that’s coming soon with stars Elizabeth Banks and Ted Danson. Here are the details.

Earlier this year, Apple announced a high-profile new comedy in the works starring Elizabeth Banks, with later cast additions including Ted Danson and guest star Tom Selleck.

Now today that series, titled ‘She’s Fine,’ has gotten a release date and the first images have debuted.

She’s Fine premieres Wednesday, January 27 with two episodes, followed by weekly releases through March 24.

Fresh off a messy divorce, Heidi (Banks) sets out to secure a lively second act for herself and her kids. But when she stumbles into coordinating her father’s retirement community sexcapades, Heidi is forced into an unlikely alliance with his girlfriend’s perpetually single son.

Katey Sagal and Rob Delaney also star in the half-hour comedy, which hails from creators Liz Heldens and Matt Ward.

Other notable Apple TV comedies include the Owen Wilson-starring Stick, which just returned for season 2. And Bad Monkey from the co-creator of Ted Lasso and Shrinking is coming back soon too.
