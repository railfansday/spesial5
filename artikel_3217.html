Analysts See 40% Boost for Take-Two Stock on Blowout Grand Theft Auto VI Sales. Are They Right?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Wells Fargo's analyst anticipates Take-Two will rake in $9.76 billion in net bookings this fiscal year, 20% more than the video game maker's own guidance.

By Dave Kovaleski – Nov 20, 2026 at 3:55PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Take-Two Interactive Software ›

The reviews are in for Grand Theft Auto VI, and they are glowing. The reviewer for the gaming publication IGN called it "the single most impressive video game I have ever played."

Gamespot's review called it "an astonishing open-world action game that I'd wager won't be outdone until Grand Theft Auto 7."

Metacritic gave it a score of 99 out of 100 based on 151 critic reviews -- the site's highest score ever.

Wall Street is also blown away by the revenue-generating potential of GTA VI and is bullish on Take-Two Interactive (TTWO +0.65%), the company that produces GTA VI. Of the 31 analysts who cover Take-Two stock, 97% rate it as a buy with a median price target of $290 per share, implying 39% upside from its current price.

According to the company, GTA VI sold 32.5 million copies of the game during the pre-sale period, a record. And including pre-sales and sales on the first day it was available to buy on Nov. 19, the game generated $3.79 billion in sales.

That is a one-day record for the entertainment industry across any medium, the company said. This was significanly higher than the projected pre-sale totals and the one-day net revenue projections by industry trackers. The sales numbers already cover the gameʻs production and marketing costs, estimated at $1 billion to $1.5 billion.

Take-Two Interactive expects between $8 billion and $8.2 billion in net bookings this fiscal year ending March 31, 2027. Net bookings refer to revenue from both games sold and in-game purchases. That would be about 21% higher, at the midpoint, than the $6.7 billion in net bookings it saw last fiscal year.

But one Wall Street analyst sees Take-Two blowing past that estimate.

This week, analysts at Wells Fargo (WFC -2.47%) updated their sales projections for Take-Two in light of the pre-sales and sales trends for GTA VI.

Wells Fargo anticipates that GTA VI will sell 65 million copies by the end of Take-Two's fiscal 2027 on March 31. Based on its forecasts, Wells Fargo projects net bookings this fiscal year to be $9.76 billion. That's 19% higher than previous estimates and some $1.6 billion, or 20%, more than Take-Two's own guidance at the midpoint.

The estimate anticipates a significant boost coming from the release of the PC and online versions of GTA VI in early 2027. Currently, only the single-player version of the game has been released. The online version is the multiplayer game. According to reports, GTA V, which came out in 2013, still generates about $500 million per year from the multiplayer game, so it goes without saying that GTA VI online will be a massive sales boost.

Analysts at Wedbush estimate sales of 30 million to 40 million copies by the end of calendar 2026. That would beat the roughly 29 million copies GTA V sold in the first month after its release in 2013. GTA V went on to sell some 235 million copies, making it the second best-selling game of all time.

In its guidance, Take-Two calls for net bookings of $3.35 billion to $3.5 billion in its fiscal 2027 Q3, which ends Dec. 31, and revenue of $3.27 billion to $3.42 billion. That would be up from net bookings of $1.76 billion and revenue of $1.7 billion in fiscal 2026 Q3.

That's why investors should expect GTA VI to provide a strong multiyear boost to revenue and earnings. It's a game that takes 100 or more hours to complete, doing all the side quests, so it will engage players for a long time and prompt in-game purchases. Plus, when the multiplayer game comes out next year, it will generate recurring revenue as players go through the game multiple times with friends and others, and make in-game purchases. The $500 million in annual revenue from GTA V is testament to that.

Take-Two stock had a 25% run-up the week before the Nov. 19 release to $230 per share and has since settled back down to around $208 per share in a sell-the-news scenario. But that just makes for a better entry price.

Take-Two stock is reasonably valued at 28 times forward earnings and is a solid buy right now. GTA VI should generate strong revenue and boost earnings not just this fiscal year, but in the years ahead, as GTA V did following its release in 2013. Over the last 10 years, Take-Two stock has posted an average annualized return of 16%.
