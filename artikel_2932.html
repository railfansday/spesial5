NFL Week 10 odds and predictions: Dolphins vs. Colts, Commanders vs. Giants, full schedule

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Miami Dolphins and Indianapolis Colts face off in Week 10 in a game that could decide the first overall pick in the 2027 NFL Draft. The Colts arrive in Miami as sellers at this week's trade deadline, looking to bolster their draft assets and build for the future rather than continuing to try to win now. The Dolphins, having held steady at the deadline, looked positioned to take advantage of the Colts' semi-fire-sale, only to suffer major losses due to injury: quarterback Malik Willis and defensive tackle Zach Sieler both landed on injured reserve this week.

The Dolphins remain underdogs for Sunday's game, with the line growing from 2.5 points up to 3.5 after the injuries. Will they pull off the upset on the road to improve to 2-7? Or will the Colts come away with their third win this year?
