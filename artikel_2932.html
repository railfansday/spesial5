Saquon Barkley set to play as Eagles remain without DeVonta Smith, Dontayvion Wicks

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Eagles running back Saquon Barkley is on track to play against his former team.

Barkley remained limited in practice on Friday with hamstring and back injuries, but he has no injury designation for Sunday's game against the Giants. Barkley missed a couple of games with the hamstring injury and has been limited to 32 offensive snaps the last two weeks.

While quarterback Joe Milton will have Barkley with him in the backfield for his first Eagles start, he'll be missing wide receivers DeVonta Smith (hamstring) and Dontayvion Wicks (ankle). Smith and Wicks are both going to miss their third straight game.

Linebacker Jihaad Campbell (hamstring, quad), defensive back Michael Carter (abdomen), wide receiver Britain Covey (knee), and cornerback Jonathan Jones (hamstring) have also been ruled out.

Tanner McKee, who was benched in favor of Milton this week, is questionable with a rib injury. Cole Payton is the other quarterback on the 53-man roster.
