Where to watch Auburn vs. Arkansas today: Time, TV channel for Week 10 game

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Auburn Tigers host the Arkansas Razorbacks for a key SEC showdown on Saturday. If you are wondering where to watch the action live, you are in the right place.

Auburn (4-4, 2-4 SEC) is looking to shake off a tough October with a big win over Arkansas (3-5, 1-4 SEC) on Saturday in Jordan-Hare Stadium. The Tigers went 1-3 against four ranked SEC foes in October and need just two more wins to qualify for their first bowl game in three seasons.

A great way for Auburn to achieve that goal would be to beat Arkansas, a team that ranks in the lower half of the SEC in scoring, total yards, yards allowed, rushing, passing offense, and sacks. Auburn, which is in a similar boat, feels it matches up better with the Razorbacks than it did with LSU, Ole Miss, and Tennessee.

Here's where to watch Auburn football vs. Arkansas on Saturday, including time, TV schedule, and streaming information:

Auburn's SEC tilt at Arkansas will be televised nationally on SEC Network in Week 10 of the 2026 college football season. Taylor Zarzour and Matt Stinchcomb will call the game from the booth at Jordan-Hare Stadium in Auburn, Alabama, with Michella Chester reporting from the sidelines.

The Auburn Sports Network will broadcast Saturday's game between the Auburn Tigers and the Arkansas Razorbacks on radio affiliates across Alabama and the Southeast beginning three hours before kickoff. Andy Burcham and Jason Campbell will call the game from the booth at Jordan-Hare Stadium, with Ronnie Brown reporting on the sidelines.
