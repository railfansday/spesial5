Emma Felbermayr Renews With Audi and Stays in F1 Academy

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Emma Felbermayr has confirmed that she will remain in F1 Academy. The 19 year old Austrian driver will compete in her third season in the all-female series with Audi and Rodin Motorsport, as announced by the German manufacturer this Tuesday, a renewal that breaks with the unwritten two-year rule and is supported by the regulation change introduced last year that allows selected drivers to stay for an additional season if their performance demonstrates continued growth potential.

Felbermayr is no rookie, she made her F1 Academy debut in 2025, claiming her first victory in Montreal during her rookie campaign and finishing tenth in the overall standings. This year however, she has made a significant leap forward, quickly putting herself in title contention, with a victory in the Shanghai Feature Race and podium finishes in Montreal and Silverstone, with the final round in Las Vegas still to come, she sits third in the standings behind only the already crowned champion Alisha Palmowski and Nina Gademan.

The Austrian driver wanted to express her gratitude for the trust placed in her: "I am very happy to continue with Audi Revolut F1 Team and Rodin Motorsport for another season in F1 Academy, I have learned so much over the past two years, both on and off the track and I am very grateful for the trust and support they have shown me. We have shared some great moments together and I feel that I have progressed a lot as a driver, especially this season but I also know that there is still plenty of room for improvement. Having the opportunity to return for a third season means a lot to me" Felbermayr said.

Allan McNish, Director of the Audi Driver Development Programme, commented on the renewal: "We are delighted that Emma will continue to represent us in F1 Academy in 2027, since making the move to single-seaters she has shown considerable progress and her performances this year have further confirmed her potential. We know she has the speed and determination to compete at the front of the grid and we believe she still has more to offer. Supporting young talent and providing drivers with the right environment to develop is at the heart of the Audi Driver Development Programme" McNish said.

Felbermayr's continued presence reflects Audi's commitment to building a solid project in F1 Academy. The German manufacturer which inherited the Sauber program following its transformation into a factory team, has found one of its key figures in the Austrian driver. Her progression, from tenth place in 2025 to third in 2026, shows an upward trajectory that the team hopes to extend for another year.

With her future secured, Felbermayr heads into the Las Vegas round with the peace of mind of knowing that her place is guaranteed, the 2027 F1 Academy season will begin in Jeddah in March, continue through Shanghai, Montreal, Silverstone, the Hungaroring, Austin and conclude in Las Vegas in November.

A calendar that the Austrian driver already knows and in which she hopes to continue climbing the standings, her third season is not a consolation prize but recognition of a talent that Audi believes is destined to fight for the title.
