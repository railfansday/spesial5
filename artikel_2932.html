Pirates Avoid Arbitration With Baddoo, Non-Tender Murdock

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Pittsburgh Pirates avoided arbitration with outfielder Akil Baddoo by agreeing to a major league contract for 2027. All 10 other arbitration-eligible players were tendered contracts.

Baddoo was claimed off waivers from the Brewers on Nov. 2. The 28-year-old did not appear in a big-league game this year but slashed .252/.342/.469 with 10 doubles, three triples, 11 home runs, 47 RBI and seven steals in 64 games for Triple-A Nashville.

Baddoo spent five seasons with the Tigers from 2021-25. He carries a .224/.305/.369 batting line with 39 doubles, 12 triples, 28 home runs, 104 RBI and 43 steals in 347 career MLB games.

MLB Trade Rumors projected Baddoo to earn $1.3 million in arbitration.

The 10 arbitration-eligible players to be tendered contracts are: right-handed pitchers Paul Skenes, Jared Jones, Carmen Mlodzinski and Yohan Ramírez, catchers Henry Davis and Endy Rodríguez, infielders Nick Gonzales, Spencer Horwitz and Jared Triolo and outfielder Oneil Cruz.

Additionally, the Pirates non-tendered right-handed pitcher Noah Murdock. The 28-year-old made three scoreless relief appearances this year. Pittsburgh's 40-man roster is now at 39 players.
