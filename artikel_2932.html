Why Digital Realty Trust Stock Moved Over 3% Higher on Monday

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Nvidia Effect was strong that trading session.

By Eric Volkman – Nov 23, 2026 at 5:46PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Digital Realty Trust ›

Investors were more than willing to put their trust in Digital Realty Trust (DLR +3.05%) stock at the start of the trading week. Shares of the data center real estate investment trust (REIT) ended Monday more than 3% higher, on the back of developments with one of the company's most important collaborators.

The No. 1 news item by far in the tech industry -- and indeed, global business -- was the reinstatement of Nvidia's (NVDA +31.48%) Jensen Huang as CEO. The move by that company's board of directors came mere days after Huang resigned his position following his admissions of extramarital affairs, one of which may have been with a person with ties to Chinese intelligence.

This, of course, doesn't impact Digital Realty directly. However, as a major data center operator, the REIT works closely with the world's leading artificial intelligence (AI) chipmaker in various ways. This includes work on hardware design, implementation, and research.

When an influential company changes its leadership -- as Nvidia did temporarily after Huang's abdication -- it can generate fear among investors that its business strategy will change. They worry that a new chief executive or management change will seek better, cheaper, or otherwise more advantageous ways of working with its key partners. With Huang's reinstatement, those fears have vanished -- at least for now.

Should we assume it'll be smooth sailing with Digital Realty and Nvidia from now on?

I think it would be risky to think so, although it's far more likely at this point that Huang stays than departs again. With many tech stocks seeing a lift on Nvidia's Monday news, it's important to distinguish those with good fundamentals and truly strong, durable ties to the chip giant. Those are the companies with potential for sustained gains. Digital Realty, which has lately posted record bookings, is one of them.
