ms-marco-MiniLM-L6-v2, a 23M cross-encoder, was downloaded 55.35M times on Hugging Face in the last 30 days. That's 76.9% of all downloads across the 18 open-weight retrieval models in the TextModels directory, and more than the next 17 combined. Below are all 52 retrieval and reranker models: open weights ranked by downloads, hosted reranking APIs from Cohere and NVIDIA, and the historical models that defined neural search.

Rerankers are the quiet second stage of most RAG pipelines. Vector search pulls 20 to 50 candidates, then a cross-encoder reads the query and each chunk together and reorders them. As one HackerNoon walkthrough of why a correct RAG pipeline still misses the answer puts it, embeddings compare two summaries made in isolation, while a cross-encoder reads both at once. It isn't free: another engineer found the reranker buried the right document that vector search had ranked first.
