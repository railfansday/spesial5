Active-Duty U.S. Army Sergeant Pleads Guilty in Conspiracy to Gather and Transfer National Defense Information

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Jian Zhao, 37, an active-duty U.S. Army sergeant stationed at Joint Base Lewis-McChord in Washington, pleaded guilty to conspiring to gather, transmit and transfer national defense information to individuals connected to China, U.S. Attorney Scott E. Bradford announced today.
