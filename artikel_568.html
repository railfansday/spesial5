21 Best Home Entertainment Gifts (2026): TVs, Lights, and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Maybe I'm just a hermit (or maybe everything in NYC is too damned expensive), but some of the most fun I've had in recent memory hasn't been going out. It's been in homes, whether it's for family movie night or for catching up with friends over drinks.

Upgrading your home entertainment setup is more than just buying a new TV or projector. Pair it with a new espresso machine or pizza oven, and you'll have the recipe for a go-to hosting location for get-togethers of all types. Below are some of the best pieces of WIRED-tested gear to upgrade any home entertainment setup, for yourself or for someone who would be over the moon unboxing a new set of speakers.

Looking for more gifting inspiration? Check out our guides to the Best Gifts for Mom, Best Gifts for Friends, Best Techie Gifts, and Best Gifts for Outdoorsy Folks.

Quite simply the best OLED TV we've tested, the Samsung S95H is bright, clear, and colorful, with outstanding picture quality and AI optimizations that eke out as much detail as possible. It's also a looker, with a silver bezel around the screen that gives the illusion of a metal picture frame. It comes in sizes ranging from 55 inches to 83 inches.

It's the best soundbar you can get, as well as the best all-in-one home theater audio solution. Everything from the unboxing down to the texture of the bar itself is premium with the Sonos Arc Ultra. And most importantly, its sound is stunning, especially considering it's slim footprint.

Looking to replicate the movie-going experience? The LG Sound Suite H7 is a great soundbar that comes in plenty of bundles with other LG subwoofers and wireless speakers. My favorite is the Immersive Suite 5 Pro bundle, which comes with the soundbar, a subwoofer, and two wireless speakers.

A premium home theater projector can make movies look bigger and better than even some of the best TVs. This Leica Cine Play 1 is a perfect example. Colors pop with vivid clarity, and you can project a 65- to 300-inch image—big enough for even the largest of parties.

Bookshelf speakers have a certain je ne sais quoi about them that other speakers can only dream of. The KEF LSX II have that classy bookshelf look with seriously modern smarts inside. You can plug these into your TV, turntable, and additional subwoofers, or forego cables entirely and use them via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.

Want a 100-inch TV that will truly impress? You need an RGB TV like the LG Micro RGB Evo above. RGB is the latest innovation to hit TV screens, with vibrant and bold colors you'd be hard-pressed to find elsewhere.

Light can add a new level of immersion to any home theater. The Nanoleaf 4D Kit did just that for WIRED reviewer Nena Farrell. “I watched Return of the King with 4D lighting, and I never want to go back.”

This weighted blanket comes recommended by WIRED reviewer Molly Higgins, and it makes any movie night instantly cozy, regardless of the weather. It's woven from 100 percent cotton and comes in four different weight options, ranging from 10 to 25 pounds.

On the opposite end of the spectrum, this fluffy, stretchy blanket is like being surrounded by a cloud, says Higgins. “I promise this gift will be fought over at the next movie night.”

Entertainment equipment doesn't have to be drab blocks of black or white. The Audioengine Color of the Year kit comes with a turntable, bookshelf speakers, and a subwoofer, all in matching bright yellow. It's a cinch to set up and a great way to add music and color to any room.

Entertainment isn't restricted to the TV room. A great turntable makes any room a space for gathering and enjoying some music. This one from Fluance is one of our favorites, with a warm, subtle design and premium components.

Pair the RT81+ turntable with a matching set of these Fluance bookshelf speakers and you've got a setup that can deliver incredible clarity for everything, including vinyl and Bluetooth streaming.

An instant camera equals instant party favors—they've certainly been used at basically every wedding I've been to recently. Whether you're hosting a monthly movie night or a big NFL watch party, this instant camera is chic and can instantly print out photos for anyone to take home.

Elegant. Precise. Beautiful. Functional. We love this electric kettle for all these reasons. Why for home entertainment? Well, a lot of people I host prefer tea to beer, especially as winter approaches.

This sleek semiautomatic espresso machine is quite showy in person. It has a dual boiler, meaning you can froth milk as you're pulling a shot, and it comes with plenty of accessories.

Want a drink with your entertainment? This toolkit elevates the mixing experience, with a diamond-cut mixing glass, julep strainer, stainless steel jigger, and stainless steel teardrop bar spoon. All pieces are made in Japan and sourced by Umami Mart, an independently owned small business in Oakland, California.

Take your movie literally anywhere with this compact portable projector. It has a built-in stand, which is also how you adjust the projector angle. The stand also makes it easy to store anywhere in the house. The battery won't last through a whole movie, so it's better for shorter TV shows or paired with a backup portable power station.

If you have an outlet outside (or a portable power station), the Nebula X1 is one of the best compact projectors for entertainment anywhere. It's bright, colorful, and displays an image up to 200 inches. Plus, you can bundle it with wireless speakers or karaoke mics.

The Sonos Move 2 is the most-used speaker in my apartment because it is so versatile. Leave it docked indoors and you have a smart, Wi-Fi connected home speaker for music. Take it outdoors, and you have a powerful, durable Bluetooth speaker with IP56 dust and water resistance. Pair two with a Sonos soundbar, and you have an impromptu surround sound experience.

An outdoor pizza oven is a show all on its own, but pair it with an outdoor projector, and you've got entertainment (and food) for everyone. The Karu 2 is the best one we've tested.

String lights aren't just for holidays anymore. Think of them more like great outdoor lights that can go anywhere: around a big tree, along a fence line, wherever.
