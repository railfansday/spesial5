Use Hopper for its price predictions and Price Watch alerts if you’ll book as soon as a fare or room rate drops. At checkout, remove any tip or add-on you didn’t choose, and compare a hotel’s total with the price on its own site, where your loyalty number counts.

Hopper is an app that books flights, hotels, vacation homes, and rental cars and forecasts whether a trip’s price will rise or fall. Its forecasts help most when your dates can move and you can book on short notice.

Travelers who watch a trip’s price and book on the alert get Hopper’s most useful tool at no cost. Hotel loyalty members can’t enter their number in Hopper, so they do better booking a free-cancellation rate on the hotel’s own site and rebooking when the price drops.
