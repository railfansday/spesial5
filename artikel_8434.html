Get the Ink Business Preferred® Credit Card if your business spends heavily on travel, shipping, and social media or search ads and you’ll book travel with the points. Those costs earn 3 points per dollar, up to $150,000 a year. Cashed out, its points pay just 1% on other spending, less than the 1.5% the no-fee Ink Business Unlimited pays.

Chase lets you apply as a sole proprietor, so you don’t need an LLC, and a side business counts. Its points are worth a cent each as cash and up to 1.5 cents on select hotels and flights booked through Chase Travel.

At 1.5 cents a point, the most Chase Travel pays, owners who book trips with their points and spend $3,200 or more a year in the 3X categories come out ahead of the no-fee Ink Business Unlimited. On $24,000 a year of spending, owners who’d rather take cash need $10,750 of it in those categories just to break even with the Unlimited after this card’s fee.
