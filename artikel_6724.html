All the Stars at the Chanel Watches and Fine Jewelry Holiday Dinner

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

From Sarah Pidgeon to Ella Rubin to Quenlin Blackwell

It’s officially the season of holiday dinner parties. Last night, Chanel Watches and Fine Jewelry ushered in the festive season with an intimate sit-down at New York City’s Humm—a new restaurant from Daniel Humm, the chef behind institutions Eleven Madison Park and Clemente Bar. Bringing together brand ambassadors, friends of the house, and artists, the evening built on the late Gabrielle Chanel’s tradition of hosting a diverse array of cultural leaders under one roof.

Love Story: John F. Kennedy Jr. and Carolyn Bessette actor Sarah Pidgeon, a Chanel ambassador, showed up wearing a cognac-brown set from Matthieu Blazy’s Fall/Winter 2026 Pre-Collection. Of course, she accessorized with several fine jewelry pieces, including a gold Coco Crush necklace, mixed-metal bracelets, and a handful of rings. She finished things off with a gold Première Galon watch.

Joining her, Sterling Point breakout star Ella Rubin arrived at the dinner party wearing a black tweed jacket from the Métiers d’art 2026 runway, with a stand collar, sculptural buttons, and a cropped hem. She dressed the outerwear down with a pair of low-rise jeans, and added a bit of timeless glamour with Chanel’s Première Ribbon timepiece and Coco Crush earrings and rings.

Elsewhere, Quenlin Blackwell wore a red fishnet-covered newspaper skirt from the Cruise 2026 collection with Coco Crush jewelry, costume designer Miyako Bellizzi paired a woven Métiers d’art skirt with Coco Crush pieces, and singer-songwriter Girlsweetvoiced performed in a white tank and jeans, a Coco Crush necklace, and the Première Édition Originale watch.
