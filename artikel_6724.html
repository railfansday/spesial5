Your Sagittarius Monthly Horoscope for December 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Happy birthday, Sagittarius! The month starts out with the Sun moving through your sign, asking you to take up space! This is a season of new beginnings—from new personal goals to new relationships entering your life. You can use this momentum however you’d like, and you’re bursting with the confidence necessary to charge forward boldly with excitement. You might also want to play around with your self-expression and style, from posting sexy selfies to finally getting a big and bold new tattoo. It’s time to try something new!

You’re a bit more nostalgic when it comes to love once Venus enters Scorpio on December 4. You might find yourself fantasizing about ex-lovers or even glossing over the bad and romanticizing the past in some way. This season can also remind you how much you benefit from spending some time alone every now and then. Slowing down and reconnecting with yourself will help strengthen your relationship with your inner voice and intuition, as well.

You’re learning important, personal news once Mercury enters your sign on December 6. You could have a bright idea or new goal you want to set for yourself. This also puts you in a mindset to focus on yourself and your needs, which can bring about progress on your personal goals. You might also have important personal news you want to share with others, letting people catch a glimpse of who you really are and what matters most to you.

A New Moon, in your sign, on December 8 helps you recommit to yourself. This is your yearly permission slip to forgive yourself, release old baggage, and show up differently. You’re ready to show up more authentically by focusing on the people, projects, and hobbies that light you up. Who and what makes you feel most like yourself? Spend more time investing in those areas of your life. This energy also brings new beginnings into your world, and you can use this momentum however you see fit.

You’re setting firm boundaries in your romantic life when Saturn stations direct in Aries on December 10. You might need to end a fling or address an uncomfortable topic. This can also give you the push you need to make a commitment and go all-in. You’re also feeling ready to stick with a certain hobby or project and take it more seriously. You have the structure necessary to pour consistent effort into your vision or whatever brings you joy.

The creative inspiration you’ve been waiting for arrives when Neptune stations direct in Aries on December 12. If you’ve felt stuck dealing with writer’s block or some form of stagnant creative energy, that starts to subside. This can also serve up reality checks regarding your romantic life. Have you been putting someone on a pedestal? Have you been prioritizing your desire and pleasure in authentic ways? It’s time to throw away the rose-colored glasses.

Conversations feel profound and healing when Jupiter stations retrograde in Leo, also on December 12. You might learn news that changes your perspective or opens you up to new possibilities. This new information might take some time to integrate. This energy is also wonderful for going back to school, picking up a writing project, or booking some travel. You will learn more and more through lived experience over the coming months.

The Sun enters Capricorn and the Winter Solstice takes place on December 21, encouraging you to check in with your current priorities. How are you expending your time, energy, and resources? You might gain more attention for your talents over the coming weeks and with the extra attention comes extra pressure. This can also help you refine and reflect on who and what you’re focused on in your life. Does it feel truly meaningful and fulfilling?

A Full Moon in Cancer on December 23 puts you in touch with some emotions you thought you already sorted through. This can drudge up the past, which can feel jarring and overwhelming. This can help you confront and integrate some of your shadow side by feeling, processing, and being accountable in new ways. There might also be conversations around shared resources and finances, or reciprocity in certain relationships, contracts, or obligations in your life. If you feel underappreciated, now is the time to speak up.

The month wraps up with Mercury moving into Capricorn on December 25, helping you make important financial decisions. You might start applying for jobs or feel ready to set firmer boundaries at work or ask for a raise. You might also want to invest in yourself in some way—whether it’s buying a new coffee machine, booking a massage, or finding more time to thrift on the weekends. You know you can’t take your money with you when you leave this place so you might as well enjoy it.
