Glitter Makeup Is Taking Over Party Season—These Picks Make It Wearable

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

From subtle shimmer to disco-ball glam, these formulas bring the sparkle.

‘Tis the season to sparkle, and that includes makeup too. Glitter eyeshadow? Check. A sparkly lipstick? Done! Shimmering cheeks? You got it! Glitter makeup is currently taking over the holiday season and we’re here for it.

Gone are the days when glitter meant a chunky, craft-store mess that followed you home from the party (and showed up in your bed a week later). Today’s formulas are finer, smoother, and far more wearable, with ultra-fine particles, gel and liquid bases that grip the skin, and pressed pigments that deliver a high-shine finish without the fallout. Whether you’re dressing up for an office party, a New Year’s Eve countdown, or simply want your everyday look to catch the twinkle lights, there’s a glittery option made for you.
