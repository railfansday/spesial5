The Hardest-Working Jacket in Kate Middleton’s Wardrobe Was the Late Queen’s Favorite, Too

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Princess of Wales paid a visit to Berwickshire on Thursday afternoon, combining those Kate Middleton staples that define her countryside style: namely, a Barbour jacket with jeans and sturdy boots. It was a far cry, fashion-wise, from the past few weeks in Catherine’s diary. A visit to India for the Earthshot Prize ceremony with the Prince of Wales saw her inadvertently twinning with Bridgerton’s Simone Ashley in Mumbai, and wearing a hand-embroidered anarkali by New Delhi designer Mahima Mahajan, paired with jewels from Queen Elizabeth II’s collection.

Kate wearing the same jacket three years ago in South Wales.

The late Queen, though, would certainly have approved of the princess’s practical winter uniform—Barbour’s waxed jackets were central to her own outdoor wardrobe when staying at blustery Balmoral or Sandringham. (Granted, Her Majesty’s version was not from an Alexa Chung capsule, like Kate’s.) According to fashion folklore, when Barbour offered to replace the monarch’s well-worn Beaufort style, she chose to keep the original for another 25 years. Like her husband’s grandmother before her, given the opportunity, Kate always looks happy to swap occasionwear for clothes better suited to navigating muddy fields—like waterproofs and walking boots. Her check-lined jacket has been in her wardrobe since at least 2023, when she wore it on a visit to South Wales with her husband.

At the Hugo Burge Foundation in Greenlaw, Scotland.

Little wonder the princess looks so comfortable in her outdoor gear. Kate Middleton is famously passionate about nature, and the sporty royal completed the grueling Three Peaks Challenge over the summer after recovering following treatment for cancer. Over the weekend, the palace released an emotional trailer for Heal, a deeply personal documentary in which Catherine reflects on her cancer diagnosis and recovery.
