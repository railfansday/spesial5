The best early Black Friday OLED TV deals we've found so far

The home entertainment world has been shifting toward OLED for a while, and now that the technology has become more affordable, it’s easier to see what the fuss is about with Black Friday deals.

OLED displays can turn pixels off individually, meaning you get much better contrast, perfect blacks, and more vibrant color, and it’s also much more likely you’ll get gaming features like NVIDIA G-Sync and variable refresh rate (VRR) now. In short, they’ve become something of a total package, and some of our favorites are on sale across U.S. retailers already ahead of the Black Friday period.

We know these TVs are great because we've tested them, with several making our best OLED TV list. Here are the top Black Friday OLED TV deals we’ve spotted so far.

Save over $100 on this LG 42” Class 4K UHD OLED TV at Walmart, with the a9 Gen5 AI Processor to adjust for best picture and sound quality automatically, as well as VRR for gaming.

The LG C5 series is a great all-rounder, and you can save almost $100 on this 48-inch model. It has LG's webOS for running apps, over 8.3 million self-lit smart pixels, and a super slim bezel

This 65-inch BRAVIA 6 OLED from Sony is seeing a $660 saving, offering Triluminos Pro for more than a billion colors, Dolby Vision, HDR10, and up to 120Hz refresh rate for gamers.

No, your eyes don't deceive you — that's almost 50% off this fantastic OLED TV from Samsung. With great glare-free picture quality, excellent AI upscaling for 4K content, and HDR, it's hard to beat.

Save nearly 50% on the award-winning LG C6 with a 65-inch screen. It offers perfect blacks, a Brightness Booster for a more luminous picture, and plenty of input ports. It can reach 165Hz refresh rate, too.

Another near 50% discount, this time on a 77-inch OLED TV from Samsung, this model is also glare-free and supports NVIDIA G-SYNC or AMD FreeSync Premium Pro.
