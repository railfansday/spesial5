It is a randomized 1:1 approximately 500-patient trial in Stage IIIb or C or IV disease recommended for neoadjuvant chemotherapy. Turning now to our Phase II translational Minimal Residual Disease or MRD study conducted in collaboration with Break Through Cancer and led by investigators at MD Anderson Cancer Center, we have had 2 notable developments this quarter. First, in July, we reported encouraging preliminary clinical and translational data. Second, in September, we reported that the independent Data and Safety Monitoring Committee overseeing the study had reviewed all safety data to date and recommended that the study continue without modification, identifying no new safety signals, and confirming comparable safety across both treatment arms. That recommendation matters for a specific reason.

The MRD study has now demonstrated the safety and tolerability of IMNN-001, both in combination with bevacizumab and used in the maintenance setting. These are 2 distinct safety objectives in 2 settings we had not previously characterized. This study is designed not only to evaluate clinical activity, but also to better understand how IMNN-001 remodels the tumor immune microenvironment following frontline treatment. Among patients who reached the study's primary assessment at second-look laparoscopy, treatment with IMNN-001 was associated with a lower rate of MRD positivity compared to the control arm, 44.4% versus 66.7%, higher clearance of circulating tumor DNA, 87.5% versus 62.5%, and a higher proportion of patients achieving no evidence of disease following frontline therapy, 100% versus 56%.

While these are preliminary findings from a small cohort, they provide encouraging evidence of a deeper antitumor activity induced by IMNN-001. At R&D Day, we also presented recurrence-free survival for the MRD trial from the intent-to-treat cohort, where median recurrence-free survival was approximately twice as long in the IMNN-001 arm, 26.1 months versus 11.4 months. I would underscore again that this is a 26-patient intent-to-treat cohort and the log rank p-value was 0.17. This is a hypothesis generating, not a confirmatory study. OVATION-3 is the confirmatory study.

But this trial provides evidence from an independent trial that there is directionally consistent and contains novel biomarker data that shows tumor microenvironment remodeling, something that clinicians outside the company continue to remind me has long been a sought-after goal and is thought to be the holy grail in immune drug development for ovarian cancer. The translational analyses continue to support IMNN-001's proposed mechanism of action. We observed robust IL-12 expression within macrophages, activation of downstream cytokines, including interferon gamma, and evidence of both macrophage and T-cell activation, consistent with remodeling of the tumor microenvironment from an immunologically cold state to one that is immunologically active or hot.

This spatial and immune profiling identified macrophages as the principal cells taking up IMNN-001 in peritoneal fluid and tumor tissue and showed expansion of T-cell receptor clones consistent with the induction of anticancer immunity. Finally, these encouraging biological and clinical findings continue to be accompanied by a highly favorable safety profile. Across the MRD study, we have observed no cytokine release syndrome, no systemic toxicities, and no serious immune-related adverse events, further reinforcing our belief that IMNN-001 has successfully overcome the historic safety challenges previously associated with interleukin 12-based therapies.

This work builds on the foundation established by OVATION-2, which demonstrated a 14.7 month increase in median survival, 45.1 months versus 30.4 months and a 24.2 month increase in median survival among patients who received PARP inhibitor maintenance, 65.6 versus 41.4 months with none of the systemic dose-limiting toxicities that closed earlier interleukin 12 programs. It is worth noting how the overall survival signal behaved as the data matured. At the July 2024 readout, the difference in medians was 11.4 months. At the final analysis in December 2025, it was 14.7 months. The benefit widened rather than eroded.

I would also note that OVATION-2 was not powered for overall survival, which is precisely why overall survival is the primary endpoint in OVATION-3. For completeness, the 95% confidence interval around that 14.7-month difference runs from negative 3.8 to 33.3 months with a one-sided p-value of 0.06. In a study of this size, that is what a consistent meaningful survival signal looks like without the statistical power to confirm it.
