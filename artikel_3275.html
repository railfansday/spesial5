‘14th’ Trailer: Ava DuVernay Issues Warning About Threats To Critical Constitutional Amendment: “We Are All At Peril”

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Netflix has released a trailer for Ava DuVernay’s compelling and urgent documentary 14th, ahead of its November 27 theatrical release and December 4 debut on the streaming platform. Watch it above.

The Oscar-contending film argues the 14th Amendment of the Constitution, passed in the wake of the Civil War, laid the groundwork for America’s multiracial democracy. It explores how the MAGA movement has made it a priority to dismantle the rights enumerated in that amendment.

“We are all at peril,” law professor Sherrilyn Ifill comments in the trailer. “If we don’t think it’s coming for us, we are so sadly mistaken.” Former Georgia State Representative Stacey Abrams notes, “The whole of the 14th is under assault. If the 14th falls, then every other right that it guarantees is now in jeopardy.”

One of the amendment’s five provisions mandates birthright citizenship – meaning anyone born in the United States is considered a citizen. The Trump administration has attempted to circumvent that as part of its broader attack on immigration. In the trailer, Vice President JD Vance says, “The concept of birthright citizenship is an absurdity.” Watch on Deadline

In June, the U.S. Supreme Court struck down the president’s executive order that declared children born in the U.S. to parents in the country unlawfully were not entitled to birthright citizenship. But the decision was by no means unanimous. Conservative Justices Clarence Thomas, Samuel Alito, and Neil Gorsuch dissented.

14th premiered at the New York Film Festival in October and screened this weekend at IDFA in Amsterdam, the world’s biggest documentary festival. We spoke with DuVernay briefly before her IDFA Q&A Sunday night and she told us she hopes people will take seriously threats to the 14th Amendment and not be lulled into complacency by the results of the midterm elections, which delivered a decisive repudiation of the president and his policies.

14th is produced by DuVernay, Spencer Averick, Paul Garnes, and Tammy Thomas-Garnes. Samantha Casey serves as archival producer. Spencer Averick edited the film; the cinematography is by Cliff Charles and Michael Fernandez. The score is composed by Jason Moran. The film is a production of Array Filmworks.

DuVernay earned an Academy Award nomination for her previous documentary, 13th. In October, she won the Documentary Filmmaker Award at the Montclair Film Festival in New Jersey and 14th won the Audience Award for Documentary Feature at the Middleburg Film Festival in Virginia.
