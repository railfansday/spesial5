PortuGOAL launches App for iPhone

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Following the success of the PortuGOAL App for Android, today we launch our App for iPhone.

PortuGOAL’s articles are increasingly read on mobile devices. With this in mind we developed the Apps so you never miss an article, even when on the move.

Download our user-friendly App free of charge at the Apple Store (link to PortuGOAL App at the Apple Store here) to keep track of the biggest Portuguese football stories.

With this App on your phone you will have the latest news at your fingertips so you stay bang up to date with all things Portuguese football. Enjoy!
