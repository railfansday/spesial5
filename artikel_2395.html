Ajax, NEC and Twente win in Europe as AZ lose to Juventus

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

See more DutchNews articles in your Google search results

See more DutchNews articles in your Google search results

Three of the four Dutch clubs playing in Europe on Thursday night won. Only AZ Alkmaar lost its match.

AZ lost 2-1 at home to Juventus in the Europa League.

NEC Nijmegen beat Omonia Nicosia 3-1, also in the Europa League. The Cypriot champions scored first, but NEC scored twice to make their first home win since August.

In the Conference League, Ajax thrashed Midtjylland 6-1 in Denmark.

FC Twente beat Pafos FC 3-1 in Enschede. They have now won all three of their games in the competition.
