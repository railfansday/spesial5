‘Bedford Park’ Trailer: Sundance Winner Stars Moon Choi, Who Falls for the Other Driver in Her Mother’s Car Accident

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The last person Audrey (Moon Choi) wants to share a ride with is Eli (Son Sukku), the other driver in her mother’s car accident. But when the New York physical therapist returns to her childhood home in New Jersey to care for her suddenly injured parent (Won Mi Kyung), Audrey finds herself carpooling with the local security guard — now without a car of his own.

Somewhere along the way, she and Eli start looking forward to their next argument. And part of the fun of Stephanie Ahn’s stunning debut, “Bedford Park,” is wondering which of them will catch on first.

Premiering exclusively on IndieWire, the film’s official trailer offers a tantalizing glimpse at the buzzy Korean American romance Sony Pictures Classics acquired out of Sundance this past January. “Bedford Park” won the festival’s U.S. Dramatic Special Jury Award for Debut Feature and later screened at Toronto and Busan. Choi, whose past credits include Bong Joon-ho’s “Okja” and the indie drama “Our Body,” brings a wonderfully short fuse to Audrey. Meanwhile, Son, known for the Korean crime thriller “The Roundup” and the series “My Liberation Notes,” has just the right deadpan delivery to keep both his love interest and the audience guessing how much Eli truly enjoys getting a rise out of his driver.

The duo’s early exchanges have the petty precision of two people who’ve known each other for years, but it’s the silences that give them away. Ahn spent years working as an editor and previously explored this premise in her 2023 short “Accident.” When she finally gets her two star-crossed passengers out in the rain, even the parking lot looks magical. Can either work up the nerve to kiss?

Sony Pictures Classics will give “Bedford Park” an awards-qualifying run in December ahead of its March 5 theatrical release. Watch the exclusive trailer below.
