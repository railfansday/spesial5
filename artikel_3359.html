Megan Thee Stallion and Daniel Radcliffe Reunite for Season Two of ‘Reggie Dinkins’

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Megan Thee Stallion will return as a guest star for the Season Two finale episode of The Fall and Rise of Reggie Dinkins. The rapper will reprise her role as Denise, a divorced mother and mail carrier who struck up a flirtatious relationship with Daniel Radcliffe‘s character, Arthur Tobin, in the first season. Megan announced the news on social media Tuesday. In the quick clip, Megan dons a red dress and raps to her Tina Tuesday Freestyle “Thin Ice” as Radcliffe pops in like a cardboard cutout. The Fall and Rise of Reggie Dinkins centers around a former NFL star, played by Tracy Morgan, who attempts to put his life back together after a gambling scandal ruins him. Naturally, a studio hires a documentary filmmaker, Tobin, to create a movie about it. The cast is rounded out by Erika Alexander, Precious Way, Jalyn Hall, and SNL vet Bobby Moynihan.

Morgan both stars and executive produces the project alongside the 30 Rock duo of Tina Fey and Robert Carlock, Sam Means, Eric Gurian, and David Miner. Megan’s previous screen credits include Dicks: The Musical, Good Girls, and P-Valley. She was also the host and musical guest of a 2022 episode of Saturday Night Live, and recently appeared on Broadway in Moulin Rouge. In September, the artist teased Act III with her new song, “Tina Tuesday Freestyle” and subsequent “Is You Cool?” in October. Megan’s last album, Megan, arrived in 2024. It debuted on the Billboard 200 at Number Three.
