More ads coming to Reddit, platform will evaluate how users respond

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

You can expect to see more ads in the Reddit app as the platform begins placing them in search results. The company says that it will be evaluating how users respond to the development …

Reddit’s announcement says that ads will be based on a range of factors, including the search query.

I’ll respond by blocking them like always.

The goal is to leverage search results pages with a “high purchase and search intent.” In other words, where the company believes that you are searching for advice on buying a particular type of product.

Reddit says this will initially involve a relatively small number of advertisers before a wider rollout.

This is an initial test with a select group of eligible advertisers. During this test, we will evaluate and understand how users respond to these ads so we can refine the experience for users and advertisers over time.

The platform has been making a series of changes intended to reduce AI data scraping, including ones likely to prove very unpopular. The latest of these is requiring you to be logged in to view most content.
