2027 Will Give One Piece A New Lead Hero, And It's Not Luffy

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

For nearly three decades, Monkey D. Luffy has fronted One Piece, and its theatrical adventures have usually followed the Straw Hat captain or put his crew in a new crisis. That pattern is set to change in 2027. One Piece Film: God Valley explores a chapter of history that unfolded long before Luffy began his voyage.

The title points toward the infamous incident involving Gol D. Roger, Monkey D. Garp, and Rocks D. Xebec. Those three figures appeared in the official announcement teaser, signaling a different generation of heroes and threats. Luffy is not being replaced in the main series; this movie simply has a past-era story to tell.

Toei Animation announced One Piece Film: God Valley on August 23, 2026, during One Piece Day '26, confirming a Japanese theatrical release in summer 2027. The teaser offered no full synopsis, but its focus on Roger, Garp, and Rocks makes the historical setting unmistakable. This is not another adventure built around the Straw Hat crew.

The God Valley Incident took place 38 years before One Piece's present-day events. Luffy's voyage begins in a world shaped by its aftermath, not in the era when these legends were making history. A film set during that conflict naturally shifts attention to the people who fought there, rather than the pirate who inherited their legacy.

Roger is a compelling candidate to anchor the story. He was not yet the Pirate King remembered by the world, and his rivalry with Rocks preceded the final stretch of his journey to the One Piece. The teaser places him alongside Garp, suggesting the movie will explore the unlikely partnership that helped define the incident.

The island gives the story a darker foundation than a conventional treasure hunt. In the manga, God Valley was the site of a World Noble hunting competition, in which the island's people were treated as prey. The arrival of powerful pirates and Marines turned that atrocity into a battle whose truth the World Government later tried to erase.

Garp's role complicates the idea of a single central hero. A Marine, he joined forces with Roger, a pirate, during the crisis. The official version of events elevated Garp as the Hero of the Marines while concealing Roger's part in the battle. A film can dramatize the partnership behind a story the authorities deliberately distorted.

Rocks D. Xebec is not simply a name for Roger and Garp to defeat. He led the Rocks Pirates, a crew that included younger versions of future powerhouses such as Edward Newgate, Charlotte Linlin, and Kaido. The manga's extended flashback also gives Rocks personal history and motivations, making him central to the tragedy rather than a faceless villain.

God Valley appears set apart because its title points to a major event in Eiichiro Oda's manga, rather than another separate Straw Hat adventure. Earlier films have largely told original stories, while this project seems poised to bring a defining piece of the series' history to theaters. That could make its different lead perspective more than a casting change.

The timing adds to the film's importance and excitement. One Piece began serialization on July 22, 1997, so its 30th anniversary falls on July 22, 2027, the same year God Valley is scheduled to arrive. It will also be the franchise's first new theatrical feature since One Piece Film: Red opened in Japan in August 2022.

Weekly One Piece episodes are halting indefinitely, but the anime hiatus clears the runway for a major canon movie.

Toei has not revealed the director, screenplay, voice cast, exact Japanese premiere date, or international release plans. The studio has not formally identified one character as the protagonist, either. Roger may be the clearest candidate for the One Piece film's heroic point of view, but Garp and Rocks are essential to the story.
