'KPop Demon Hunters' Is Officially Going on Tour

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Hannah covers horror, sci-fi, and games at Collider, where she writes news and features, reviews new releases, and interviews the people who make them. She’s helping build out Collider’s gaming coverage, with Pokémon as one of her specialties. If she isn’t writing about a monster, she’s probably watching one on a tape from her VHS collection.

The crowd at a HUNTR/X show has a job. In KPop Demon Hunters, the fans singing back to Rumi, Mira, and Zoey help keep the Honmoon intact. It’s a good excuse for a movie to make the audience part of the action, and it raises a practical question for Netflix. What happens if you put a few thousand actual HUNTR/X fans in the same room?

There’s already an obvious candidate for the song they’ll know by heart. “Golden” has passed 2 billion streams on Spotify, according to Netflix, and won both an Oscar and a Grammy. But singing along is only part of what the company has in mind. The Saja Boys and Derpy Tiger are coming along, too.

Netflix and AEG Presents are launching the KPop Demon Hunters Golden World Tour on June 5, 2027, at Baltimore’s CFG Bank Arena. The arena show will feature animated appearances from HUNTR/X alongside live performers, stunts, and choreography. The tour is expected to visit nearly 50 cities across 2027 and 2028, with more than 150 performances planned. Tickets go on sale Friday, November 20, at 10 a.m. local time.

Four festivals, four completely different ideas of what a great set even is. Here is the one built for you, what that stage would demand, and what it would give back if you pulled it off.

You belong on the Pyramid Stage, which is the only festival slot in the world that can rewrite a career in ninety minutes. Glastonbury does not care what you are wearing and it will not help you if the weather turns, and that is exactly the appeal.

You belong in the desert, where the set is only half the job and the other half is the spectacle around it. Coachella rewards people who understand that a show is something you design rather than something you just play.

You belong on the most absurdly ambitious main stage ever built, in front of a field of people from a hundred countries who all want the same thing at the same time. There is no irony here at all, which is precisely why it works.

You belong at the smallest festival on this list and the one with the longest memory. Newport is where the songs matter more than the staging, and where an audience will genuinely stand in silence to hear a verse properly.

Amy Tinkham, who worked on Disney’s Descendants/Zombies: Worlds Collide Tour, is leading the creative direction. Netflix says the animated characters will interact with the crowd, and chief marketing officer Marian Lee says fans will get to “sing, dance and help HUNTR/X seal the Honmoon.” That’s a pretty good reason to bring the Saja Boys. A HUNTR/X concert is one thing, but a HUNTR/X concert where the other group can show up and ruin everybody’s night is a lot closer to the movie.

The Mastercard presale begins Wednesday, November 18, followed by the For the Fans presale on Thursday, November 19. Both start at 10 a.m. local time, as does Friday’s general sale. Fans can register for the November 19 presale on the official Golden World Tour site. Select VIP packages may include reserved seats, onstage photo opportunities, and preshow parties.
