Dune Magic: The Gathering Crossover Officially Confirmed After Major Leak

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Arrakis, its noble houses, and its legendary sandworms are getting the Magic card treatment

The spice must flow, and so must the Secret Lair drops. After several days of leaks circulating online, Dune is officially crossing over with Magic: The Gathering, with Wizards of the Coast teasing the collaboration through the official MTG Secret Lair account.

The announcement follows a series of leaked promotional images that surfaced on Reddit and Discord on Nov. 12, giving players an early look at what appeared to be three Dune-themed Secret Lair collections. The official teaser now confirms that the collaboration is real.

The superdrop is expected to arrive on the Secret Lair website on Nov. 30, giving fans a little time to prepare their spice reserves.

According to the leaked images, the collection features three separate drops. The first introduces eight mechanically unique legendary creatures based on characters from the franchise, including Paul Atreides, Chani, Baron Vladimir Harkonnen, Princess Irulan, and Reverend Mother Jessica.

The second collection focuses on Arrakis and its enormous sandworms, introducing Shai-Hulud as a new Magic card alongside Dune-inspired reprints. The third, titled Spice Harvest, features familiar cards reimagined as items and technology from the franchise. Among the leaked reprints are Darksteel Forge, Revel in Riches, and Halo Fountain.

The leaks have generated plenty of excitement among Magic players, with some already discussing potential Commander decks built around the new characters. Others were less enthusiastic about the use of movie actors' likenesses instead of original character artwork, while several fans were quick to ask whether Duncan Idaho would make the cut.

The timing also makes sense for the crossover. Dune: Part Three is scheduled to hit theaters on Dec. 18, 2026, just weeks after the Secret Lair collection arrives. For now, players won't have to wait much longer to see how much of Arrakis makes its way into their Magic collections.
