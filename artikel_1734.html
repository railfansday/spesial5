Paul Pogba makes Arsenal claim in message to Manchester United and Mauricio Pochettino

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Paul Pogba believes Manchester United should follow Arsenal’s blueprint under Mikel Arteta and has accused his former club of living in the past.

United decided to sack Michael Carrick during the international break and have brought in Mauricio Pochettino to deliver an immediate turnaround after a poor start to the season.

Pochettino suffered a heavy 4-0 defeat to Liverpool in his first game in charge and that was followed by a 2-2 draw away to Sporting CP in the Champions League on Wednesday night.

United have not won a trophy in their last two seasons and Pochettino’s side are now in an early relegation battle as they sit 19th in the Premier League with eight points from 11 games.

Pogba, meanwhile, has endorsed Pochettino’s move to Old Trafford but feels United need to look at Arsenal’s return to the top as a model to follow.

‘I’m happy that Pochettino has gone there because I think he’s a great manager. And I’m sad also for Michael Carrick because I don’t think he had enough time to show and prove himself,’ Pogba told talkSPORT.

‘But I think we just need to give time to this team. We know there’s a lot of fans all over the world and we all want Man United to win and we all think that they should change manager.

‘All I think about this team, I’ve been there, is that you need time, you need to fix the stuff inside.

‘Everybody needs to be in the same position and give time to the manager to do his things.

‘The players also have to perform, have to understand the manager. And this way, they can go somewhere, like Arsenal.

‘Arsenal had a great time with Thierry Henry. They had a great [era], they went to Champions League final, they won the Premier League. And they had a [long] time [before] winning again. It took how many years [between] the last time that Arsenal won the Premier League?

‘But the pressure, I know it’s a big club, it’s Manchester United, was way less. They let Arsenal make their own way back to the top.

‘And that’s all I think Manchester United need. We need to stop talking about them like they are the team that it was back then.

‘You need to respect, you need to let them grow again and get back to the top.’
