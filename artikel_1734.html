Gasperini allows Roma to play with ‘no fear’, says Hermoso

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Mario Hermoso credited Gian Piero Gasperini for allowing Roma to play with "no fear" during their 2-2 draw with Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League.

Hermoso opened the scoring in the fifth minute at the Parc des Princes on Wednesday, only for Illia Zabarnyi's header to level things up for PSG going into the break.

Luis Enrique's side went ahead just after the hour through Ousmane Dembele's penalty after Wesley clumsily brought down PSG captain Achraf Hakimi inside the area.

But Roma levelled things up with a spot-kick of their own in the 78th minute, with Donyell Malen sweeping his effort past Matvey Safonov and into the left side of the net.

The result not only ended PSG's run of 12 straight wins in all competitions, but also edged Roma closer to the play-off places, with just a point separating them and Villarreal.

It was also the first time the Parisiens had conceded, later led, but failed to win a Champions League group stage game at home, with Hermoso saying the draw is further evidence of Gasperini's brilliance and where he is aiming to take Roma.

"Everyone knows what the coach does, this is his identity, his style of playing," Hermoso told Sky Sport Italia, as quoted by Football Italia.

"We defenders get the opportunity to be protagonists, so I am very happy to come to a stadium like this, and see Roma playing in this special way, with no fear.

"We are on the right track and must keep taking these big steps forward. The coach gives you confidence to believe, to have this mentality of reacting to difficult moments that we suffered not just this season, but over the years.

"He brought this identity to Roma, and we are happy to carry it on for a great club like Roma."

Roma have now scored 2+ goals in 10 consecutive matches for the first time on record (since 1929-30), and they could have even had more against PSG.

They finished with an expected goals (xG) total of 2.65 from their 13 shots to PSG's 1.73 from their 15 attempts.

Roma are now one of four teams to go 1-0 ahead in all five of their Champions League games this season, alongside Arsenal, Como and Galatasaray. They have, however, failed to win four of those (D2 L2), the most games failed to win in a group campaign after scoring first since Atalanta in 2021-22 (also four).

Gasperini raised eyebrows ahead of kick-off, with top scorer Malen, as well as Paulo Dybala and Bryan Cristante all starting the encounter on the bench.

But the Roma boss explained the reasoning behind his decision, saying: "Malen has played many games, including international duty, so it was only fair to give Castro a chance.

"It's also true that the plan was to introduce him in the second half, which is when the games are decided.

"Dybala also made his comeback against Parma after more than a month out, so he needed to be looked after. Cristante played a lot too, but I introduced Pisilli, who was fantastic.

"It's not as if these players were replaced by someone inferior. We knew that if we stayed in the game, then the second half would become decisive.

"PSG introduced Dembele and other players of the highest quality, so for a while the game was going our way, and at the end we couldn't wait for the final whistle to come."
