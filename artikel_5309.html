Google Gemini AI predicts a move for Cardano (ADA) that would see it trade at nearly 40 times its current price. Read on to see how Charles Hoskinson’s ADA crypto token could reach such an insane price.

For this outlandish prediction to hit, Gemini AI believes a hypothetical landmark catalyst would need to land for Cardano, acknowledging just how unlikely the event is.

For context, ADA is trading below $0.189 on Tuesday (November 3), after losing a key support zone the previous day, and it has had a rough October, down -22.2%. This is a “what if” scenario, and it assumes the following.

Late in Q4, a consortium of major central banks and Tier-1 financial institutions announces a pilot for wholesale tokenized bond settlement.

After a bake-off, they pick Cardano as the settlement layer, citing built-in compliance that doesn’t depend on bolt-on smart contract logic.

Within days, a US government digital asset reserve discloses that it has quietly accumulated ADA as a settlement-layer strategic asset. Spot ADA ETF inflows go parabolic. Short sellers learn what the word “squeeze” means.

On the weekly chart, ADA has spent a long stretch trapped in a descending channel, with levels at $0.41, $0.78, $1.18, and eventually $3 in play if it breaks out.

In this scenario, the Zurich Accord headline blasts ADA through the $0.25 breakout zone on enormous volume, which flips the channel’s upper boundary from resistance to support.

Each of those historical supply zones then becomes a stepping stone instead of a ceiling: $0.41, then $0.78, then $1.18. Once price clears the $3.09–$3.10 all-time high, there’s no overhead resistance at all, which is the textbook setup for price discovery.

Momentum indicators go deeply overbought and stay overbought, as they famously do in parabolic phases. Fair warning: today’s actual chart is bearish, with ADA slipping out of the ascending channel that carried it for months. Bulls need to reclaim $0.23 before this fantasy even gets off the ground.
