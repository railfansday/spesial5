Synth Riders Dropping '80s MixTape: Vice Wave' DLC to Immerse You in Some Neon Dreams

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Barely a couple of weeks after PSVR2 darling Synth Riders dropped a new free song, we've got yet more content on the way.

Those not satisfied with the previous '80s Mixtape' drops, we've got an additional third set of tunes to liven up your day.

The tracklists of the previous packs, while not necessarily synthwave, at least leaned into the synth pop of the era, and that's true this time as well. Plus the tracklist is just so darn interesting. A number of these are of personal interest to this here writer.

The pack can be had for $13.99, but you can pick and choose tracks for $1.99 if that's more your speed. And you can pick it up now!

It's quite the exciting tracklist, with some amazingly danceable tracks from some unexpected artists. Anything really stand out to you? Sound off in the comments.
