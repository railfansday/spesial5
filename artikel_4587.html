C8 Corvette Manual Conversion: It’s Finally Here, Thanks to the Aftermarket

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Peitz Performance, which installed the TR9060 manual transaxle in the mid-engined ‘Vette, has a running and driving prototype at SEMA.

It was only a matter of time before someone successfully swapped a manual transmission into the C8 Corvette after Tremec revealed its TR9060 manual transaxle at the 2025 SEMA Show; indeed, the company even went so far as to say during last year’s show that it had already test-fit the transmission into a C8. And now, one year later, the swap we’ve been waiting for has become a reality.

Peitz Performance of Tomball, Texas is a well-known Corvette tuning shop, and they’re the first to have a running and driving C8-gen manual. It even has a listing on the company’s website with a price of $74,528.99 for the swap, and suggests the package will be available in July 2027. Exciting stuff, so let’s get into some of the details revealed at the 2026 SEMA Show.

The Tremec gearbox is operated via cable-controlled shifting. Peitz says the shifting mechanism is positioned on the left side of the transaxle housing. It also incorporates a concentric slave cylinder for clutch actuation.

Hot Rod had the chance to drive the C8 at SEMA, and along the way learned some more details about how Peitz made the swap come to life. One of the big advantages the tuner had going into the project is experience with the platform; most importantly, it already had 3D scans of the entire car. This made it easier to plan out how it would arrange everything in the tight confines of a mid-engine sports car.

The shifter itself started out life as a Porsche six-speed shifter, and a unique bracket was made to mount the clutch pedal to the brake pedal assembly. The pedal itself was taken from a CT4-V Blackwing, but modified with a pedal stop and hydraulic clutch assist. It needed a new flywheel, which includes the ring and teeth from a C8 flywheel but, of course, adds the clutch.

Making the new transmission play nice with all of the car’s electronics was one of the taller tasks, but it’s an area Peitz Performance is an expert in. Tuning DCT-equipped C8s and adding power to them is one of the shop’s specialties, so the team already understood the platform well.

The key is a Motec 182 Plug and Play with a Motec 150 for transmission control. With this, Peitz Performance can program in downshift throttle blips, upshift ignition cuts, and relay data like gear positions and transmission fluid temperature to a driver.

This package is still in development, and Peitz believes it will be in a final, installable form in the first quarter of 2027. The Tremec itself is good to support up to about 1000 pound-feet of torque and survive up to 8600 rpm. Its price of nearly $75,000 is mighty steep, but we suspect there will still be plenty of takers who would like a chance to row their own gears in the C8 Corvette.
