Seal Returns Favor, Cover’s Tame Impala’s ‘New Person, Same Old Mistakes’

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Looks like Tame Impala’s Kevin Parker just got an early Christmas present.

This week, Seal released a video of himself nailing an acoustic cover of Tame Impala’s “New Person, Same Old Mistakes,” the defining song from the group’s hit 2015 album Currents. “You sang mine, Tame Impala. Had to sing yours,” the “Future Love Paradise” wrote on Instagram. “Felt a bit rude not to return the honour.“

“Finally taking flight/I know you don’t think it’s right,” Seal sings at Malibu’s Dragonfly Creek, turning Parker’s signature smooth synth into a soulful jam. “I know that you think it’s fake / Maybe fake’s what I like.”

Seal’s not the first person to recognize the hypnotic draw of the Tame Impala song. Rihanna famously also recorded a version of “New Person, Same Old Mistakes” for her 2016 album ANTI. But this specific tribute comes after Tame Impala covered Seal’s “Kiss From a Rose” on Triple J’s “Like a Version” series.

In his Triple J interview, Parker revealed that the song held some incredibly beloved childhood memories for him — mostly because “Kiss From a Rose” was included on the Batman Forever soundtrack. “It was the first CD I bought. So I knew every song on it. I used to listen to it back to back,” Parker said. “Once we accepted that none of us sound like Seal — like, none of us have the insane voice that he does, once we were content with, ‘Oh, it’s us, it’s a bunch of Aussie blokes doing it’ — then I think we were at peace with it. We’re gonna do the best we can.”

It’s the best kind of musical crossover: a cheeky musical appreciation from two top-tier artists. And who knows how fans could benefit from this? What’s that saying — first comes cover, then comes collab?
