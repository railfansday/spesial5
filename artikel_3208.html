Westwater Res. (WWR) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Westwater ends the quarter debt-free, with a narrower loss and a federal loan secured.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Westwater Resources, Inc. (WWR -3.66%) is developing a vertically integrated natural graphite supply chain in Alabama, consisting of the Coosa mine and the Kellyton processing plant. Management reported the retirement of all outstanding debt following the redemption of convertible notes and a narrowing net loss for the third quarter. The company is advancing Phase 1 of the Kellyton facility toward production while navigating federal and state permitting for the Coosa mine. Financing efforts include the approval of a $25 million direct loan from the Export-Import Bank of the United States and a new $50 million equity line of credit to support construction and operational readiness.
