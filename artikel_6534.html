Thanksgiving Sandwich

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

After Thanksgiving dinner, you’re left with piles and piles of leftovers, and the classic way to use them up is a post-Thanksgiving sandwich. This recipe is the easiest way to put those leftovers to good use, turning them into the most mouthwatering bite. We stacked ours full of all the holiday staples: roast turkey, mashed potatoes, cranberry sauce, stuffing, and gravy, with a few extra additions. We think our sandwich is the perfect combination of leftovers, but you can also use it as a base to make it completely your own. No cooking required here—take it easy the day after Thanksgiving and make this iconic sandwich instead.

While a sandwich is a classic way to use up your Thanksgiving leftovers, your options don’t stop there. We’ve created countless ways to use up every bit of your holiday leftovers, from Thanksgiving leftover tacos to our Thanksgiving ring (who could say no to crescent rolls?). Only have leftover turkey? Our turkey casserole and turkey noodle soup are the best ways to use up the rest of your bird.

The full list of ingredients and instructions can be found in the recipe below.

This sandwich is best enjoyed fresh, especially since the toasted bread can get soggy. If you have leftovers, we recommend storing the components separately in airtight containers in the refrigerator for up to 3 to 4 days.
