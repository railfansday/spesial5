Bradford City boss Michael Duff on ex-Barnsley FC striker Stephen Humphrys being in charge of his own destiny

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

For the second successive season, the Lancastrian scored against former club Barnsley at Valley Parade, with his brace in sixth-placed City's 3-0 win on Tuesday night taking his tally to four in his last three matches in all competitions.

In the past, Humphrys has shown a penchant for scoring against his ex-employers and on the big occasion, but the unglamorous side of the game—what he offers without the ball - has been previously used by some managers as a metaphorical stick to beat him with.

Duff, who maintained his good record against former side Barnsley - five wins and a draw since leaving Oakwell in the summer of 2023—said: "Everyone knows what Humps can do. I said to him early in my tenure, I need to see what the worst version of Stephen Humphrys looks like.

"I know what the best version is, and things like that. He can do special things that other people at this level can't.

"But there's also a sort of trust element. We've been working hard and he's been working hard.

"He got his chance when he came off the bench against Stevenage and affected the game, but then got injured within two minutes of coming on in the next game (at Burton).

"It's good competition for places and one thing he has done, he's looked like a goalscorer.

"He knows what's expected. We've had the conversation.

"He'll be in charge of how much game time he gets, ultimately. Because if he delivers those things and he delivers goals like he did on Tuesday, he's definitely got more chance of getting on the pitch more often than not.”
