Kuyt leaves Fenerbahce after Belozoglu appointed head coach

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Dirk Kuyt has left Fenerbahce following a two-week spell as their interim head coach, which included a Champions League victory over his former club Liverpool.

Kuyt took over the Istanbul giants on a temporary basis following Ismail Kartal's resignation in October, and just a few days later, he was reunited with the Reds.

The Dutchman, who made 285 appearances across all competitions for Liverpool, scoring 71 goals as well as providing 34 assists, masterminded a 3-2 victory over them in Turkiye, with Fener twice coming from behind to end the Reds' unbeaten start to the 2026-27 Champions League.

But now, Kuyt has stepped aside for Emre Belozoglu, a former Fener midfielder who has had spells in charge of Istanbul Basaksehir, Ankaragucu, Antalyaspor and Kasimpasa since moving into coaching, to take the job.

"Our yellow-and-navy story began years ago, and the bond between us has never been broken. You were with us on the pitch first, then in the dugout," read a social media post from Fener's official accounts, addressing Kuyt's departure.
