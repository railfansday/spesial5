Watch: Cisse and Pulisic score as Milan open up 5-1 lead against Frosinone

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

AC Milan were in the mood for goals against Frosinone, and they scored twice in quick succession to put the game well beyond the visitors.

Continuing the trend of impact substitutions after Ardon Jashari scored less than three minutes after coming on, Alphadjo Cisse got his ninth goal of the season right on the hour mark.

A Frosinone corner was cleared and Pulisic found Moreira who had the Italian for company, and he finished the through ball by the Belgian past Palmisani with his trademark composure. The offside flag went up, but after a long check the goal was given.

Milan continued to look like scoring every time they got out of their own half, and Pulisic joined the fun in the 68th minute with a goal of his own.

Frosinone again left one man back for a corner that was cleared, allowing the American to easily outpace Terzic with a run from inside his own half, dinking over Palmisani to make it 5-1.
