The GTA 6 pool boy scene in the Safe Word mission sees Jason trapped on the set of a porno after accidentally taking on a new cover story as one of the stars of the show. Initially, Lucia finds this all very funny before it starts to get serious, forcing Jason to make a choice as to how far he'll go to avoid blowing his cover. Of course, it's actually you making this GTA 6 choice, and the reality is that it's nowhere near as impactful as it seems. So, having tested both options, here are all the outcomes to the pool boy scene in Grand Theft Auto 6.

The short answer is that your decision to participate or not in the pool boy scene doesn't really matter as the effects of either choice have a tiny impact:

Choosing not to participate is obviously the better option if you're trying to maintain a good GTA 6 relationship status with Lucia. And even if you do choose to participate, the negative impact on the relationship isn't significant enough to cause a substantial drop in its level – Jason and Lucia's relationship stayed at the maximum Hot Together level for me when trying out both decisions.

Otherwise, the rest of the Safe Word mission plays out the same. Immediately after you make your choice, Lucia sets off a car alarm to interrupt filming, giving Jason a chance to slip away and infiltrate Gil's office to find his vault. There are a few things you can interact with in here but none of them have any effect either besides the remote, which is necessary for revealing the vault and progressing the mission to the final stage.
