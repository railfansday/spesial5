Pope Leo’s Victory Tour

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Pope Leo attends a welcome ceremony at Plaza Independencia on the first papal visit to Uruguay since 1988, as part of his 12-day trip to South America, in Montevideo, Uruguay, November 6, 2026.

‘True victory does not lie in seeking to prevail over others, but in giving yourselves to them.”

So the first pope born in the United States said after the midterm election results. He had already made the news when we found out he voted in Illinois via absentee ballot (where an accurate vote count is miraculous). And then it was hard to escape that, in a major statement after the election, he reflected on victory.
