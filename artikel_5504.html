CAE announces consent and proxy solicitation targeting its 5.541% Series 1 Senior Unsecured Notes

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

MONTREAL, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- (Nasdaq: CAE) (TSX: CAE) â CAE Inc. ("CAE" or the "Company") announced today that it has commenced a consent and proxy solicitation to amend the indenture governing its 5.541% Series 1 Senior Unsecured Notes due June 12, 2028 (the "Notes") representing $400 million aggregate principal amount.

CAE is soliciting written consents and proxies (the "Consent and Proxy Solicitation") whereby holders (collectively, "Noteholders") of CAE's outstanding Notes issued under the master trust indenture, dated as of June 12, 2023 (the "Master Indenture"), as supplemented by a first supplemental indenture dated as of June 12, 2023 (the "1st Supplemental Indenture", and together with the Master Indenture, the "Indenture"), between CAE, as issuer, and Computershare Advantage Trust of Canada (as successor to BNY Trust Company of Canada), as trustee, are asked to consider an amendment to the definitions relating to the accounting principles referenced in the Master Indenture (the "Proposed Amendment") by way of Extraordinary Resolution (as defined in the Indenture) (the "Amendment Resolution"), the full text of which is set forth within the joint consent and proxy solicitation statement (as it may be amended or supplemented, the "Consent and Proxy Solicitation Statement").

As part of our ongoing transformation and in an effort to align the CAE's financial reporting with aerospace and defense peers, CAE is exploring a potential transition from IFRS to United States generally accepted accounting principles ("U.S. GAAP"). If adopted, the change would be applied for the fourth quarter and fiscal year ending March 31, 2027, on a full retrospective basis. We have established a project team and a project plan, and we are currently evaluating the potential impact that a transition to U.S. GAAP may have on our consolidated financial statements. The Proposed Amendment is being sought in connection with this potential transition to U.S. GAAP. The Proposed Amendment is required to reflect in the Master Indenture the change in accounting policies in CAE's financial statements upon the adoption thereof.

CAE is soliciting written consents and proxies for the approval by the Noteholders of the Proposed Amendment. If the threshold described in the Consent and Proxy Solicitation Statement for an approval by a written consent of Noteholders is not achieved, CAE intends to hold a meeting of the Noteholders (the "Meeting"), scheduled to be held in person at the offices of Norton Rose Fulbright Canada LLP at 1, Place Ville Marie, 27th Floor, Montreal, Quebec, H3B 1R1 at 9:00 a.m. (Eastern time) on December 16, 2026.

The record date for entitlement to receive notice of and to provide instructions with respect to written consents and proxies and to vote at the Meeting is 5:00 p.m. (Eastern time) on November 9, 2026 (the "Record Date"). Each Noteholder of record as of the Record Date will be entitled to one vote in respect of each $1,000 principal amount of Notes held by such Noteholder.

The Proposed Amendment is being sought pursuant to the Indenture. The Proposed Amendment is described in more detail in the Consent and Proxy Solicitation Statement and is set forth in the form of supplemental indenture (the "2nd Supplemental Indenture") attached to the Consent and Proxy Solicitation Statement in Appendix B.

If CAE receives the required level of approval for the Proposed Amendment either by written consent or by the Amendment Resolution being passed at the Meeting, and the Proposed Amendment comes into force by the execution of the 2nd Supplemental Indenture, then any Noteholders who authorized and directed their CDS Participant (as defined in the Consent and Proxy Solicitation Statement) to elect to consent prior to the Consent Deadline (as defined below), and whose CDS Participant makes such election through CDSX, or who votes, or causes their Notes to be voted, in favour of the Proposed Amendment at the Meeting, will be eligible to receive a consent fee in the amount of $1.00 per $1,000 principal amount of Notes (the "Consent Fee") held by such Noteholder. Noteholders who fail to deliver a valid consent prior to the Consent Deadline or a valid proxy prior to the Proxy Deadline (as defined below) or who do not vote, or cause their Notes to be voted, in favour of the Amendment Resolution at the Meeting, as applicable, will not be entitled to receive the Consent Fee, regardless of whether the Proposed Amendment is approved. Any Consent Fees that become payable to Noteholders will be paid promptly after the 2nd Supplemental Indenture comes into force by CAE to Computershare Advantage Trust of Canada, as paying agent, which will, in turn, pay the registered Noteholder (CDS & Co.), which will then distribute payment through the applicable procedures of CDS Clearing and Depository Services Inc. Interest will not accrue on or be payable with respect to the Consent Fee.

For the Proposed Amendment to come into force, CAE requires either (i) the written consent of the holders of not less than 66 2/3% of the aggregate principal amount of the outstanding Notes or (ii) a resolution passed by the affirmative votes of the holders of not less than 66 2/3% of the aggregate principal amount of the Notes present in person or represented by proxy at the Meeting who are entitled to vote on such resolution.

The board of directors of CAE unanimously recommends that Noteholders CONSENT TO AND VOTE FOR the Proposed Amendment.

The deadline for the submission of consents by Noteholders is no later than 5:00 p.m. (Eastern time) on November 26, 2026 (the "Consent Deadline") and the deadline for delivery of proxies for the Meeting, if required, is no later than 5:00 p.m. (Eastern time) on December 14, 2026 (the "Proxy Deadline") or, in the case of an adjourned or postponed Meeting as described in the Consent and Proxy Solicitation Statement, 5:00 p.m. (Eastern time) on the third business day prior to the scheduled adjournment or postponement (subject, in each case, to modification, waiver or extension by CAE in its discretion). CAE reserves the right to terminate, cancel, extend or modify the Consent Deadline, the Proxy Deadline and the other terms of the solicitation of consents and proxies for the Proposed Amendment at any time prior to the Consent Deadline, the Proxy Deadline, or the Meeting, as applicable, as described in the Consent and Proxy Solicitation Statement. Noteholders holding Notes through intermediaries (such as a bank, trust company, securities dealer or broker, or trustee or other intermediary) should take note that such intermediaries may set deadlines for the return of instructions that are well in advance of the Consent Deadline and Proxy Deadline. If you have any questions regarding giving instructions with respect to the Consent and Proxy Solicitation, please contact your broker or other intermediary for assistance.
