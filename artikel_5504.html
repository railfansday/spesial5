Uber started offering Amazon's Zoox robotaxis to riders in Las Vegas

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Riders who request UberX and other ride types may be matched with a Zoox vehicle at no additional cost, the companies said

Uber $UBER Technologies began offering robotaxis from Amazon $AMZN-owned Zoox through its app in Las Vegas on Monday, the companies said. It is the first time Zoox has partnered with a third-party platform.

Riders who book UberX, Uber Comfort, Uber Electric, Uber Comfort Electric or Wait & Save could be paired with a Zoox car without paying extra. Riders can raise the odds of landing a Zoox by opting in through "Ride Preferences" in the app settings. Zoox will keep operating its own app in Las Vegas.

Uber and Zoox signed a multi-year agreement with MGM Resorts International under which the two are the official providers of rideshare and robotaxi service at MGM's Las Vegas properties. The companies said they will work with MGM on pickups and drop-offs, on-property wayfinding, in-app guidance and infrastructure changes meant to ease traffic flow.

Samarth Kejriwal, Uber's global head of autonomous mobility, said in a statement that the launch is "another step toward making autonomous transportation part of everyday life." Zoox co-founder and CTO Jesse Levinson said in a statement that the company is seeing "more riders choosing to ride with Zoox" as it expands service in Las Vegas.

Uber and Zoox plan to bring their partnership to Los Angeles in 2027. Zoox is due to begin initial operations there by the end of 2026, through its own app.

The launch adds to competition with Waymo, which has begun operating in Las Vegas, according to Bloomberg. Uber has offered rides in Motional vehicles in Las Vegas since March, but a human safety driver still sits at the controls of those cars. Uber CEO Dara Khosrowshahi, speaking in this month's earnings remarks, predicted the Motional cars will go fully driverless "soon" once Zoox is live, according to Bloomberg.

Khosrowshahi said on the company's earnings call for the third quarter that Uber plans to give its app a separate autonomous ride choice and increase vehicle supply in the months ahead, according to TechCrunch. Customers have no means of requesting a Zoox vehicle specifically at launch.

The Nevada Transportation Authority voted unanimously this year to approve a permit under which Uber can put as many as 1,000 robotaxis on the road in Clark County, using vehicles from its Motional and Zoox partnerships, according to TechCrunch. Uber told TechCrunch it will not handle upkeep or other support for Zoox's vehicles in Las Vegas.
