Derek Hough Recalls Difficult Utah Upbringing with Neighbors Who ‘Terrorized’ Family Before Moving to London

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Derek Hough is opening up about his upbringing, sharing painful details from his childhood in his new Hulu documentary, “The Symphony of Dance.”

In the film, the “Dancing with the Stars" judge returns to his childhood home in Utah, and details the challenging environment he was raised in.

“The neighborhood that we grew up in, we definitely just had some neighbors that weren’t great,” Derek, 41, explained. “They pretty much terrorized my family in a lot of ways.”

“They hung me up in a tree by my ankles. They put a gun to my head, spat on me, hogtied me up in a field and left me there,” he continued. “One of my neighbors was, for lack of a better word, really inappropriate with me, physically.”

Eventually, Derek’s parents separated, and the family moved across town to an apartment building where his mother and father lived in units across the hall from one another.

“I remember moving away from there and feeling relieved,” he said. However, the move didn’t solve all of his problems, and he continued to struggle in the wake of his parents’ divorce.

“I just didn’t understand where I was going. I didn’t understand what I was doing,” he said. “I was just kind of left to my own devices as a 12-year-old. I would skip school. I would skip dance, just floating around with no direction.”

Although he had initially pushed back when his parents wanted to enroll him in dance classes, Derek explained that the artform eventually became his “safe haven”—and his ticket to a new life in London.

When renowned dancers Corky and Shirley Ballas—who “Dancing with the Stars” fans will recognize as the parents of pro Mark Ballas—visited Derek’s dance studio, they saw that he had something special.

“We saw the gift, we saw the talent, and we saw the struggle that his family was having at the time,” Shirley recalled in her documentary interview. “But more than that, he showed his dedication to the art. You could see it from a very, very young age.”

Shirley explained that this potential is what motivated the Ballas family to take Derek under their wings.

“He needed an opportunity. He needed a place to grow. And I remember him walking in, after the end of one of the classes, and saying to me, ‘Can I come and live with you?’” Shirley said. “We spoke to [his father] Bruce, he thought that was a good idea for Derek to live with us in London for a short space of time.”

The arrangement—in which Derek was enrolled in the Italia Conti Performing Arts Academy with Mark—was initially planned to last just six months. But Derek’s father said that by Christmas of that year, the Ballas family suggested bringing sister Julianne Hough over, too.

At the time, Julianne was 10 and Derek was 12.

“I felt like moving away was great because I was getting away from something,” Derek explained. “I was leaving like, this wounded boy, and I was going to go become this success. I was gonna work hard and achieve things and overcome things.”

The stay kept getting extended as Derek became more dedicated to ballroom dancing.

“I every year, sort of begged them to come home, and they begged to stay,” Bruce said of his children’s move to London.

Derek and Julianne felt very at home overseas, however.

“It was almost like this ritual. Every summer we would go home, and my dad was like, ‘Yo, it’s time to come home, You guys. I wanna raise you. You’re my kids. I want you to be here,” Derek said. “And it was honestly this fight where I was like, ‘We’re on this path, and I’m gonna win this championship if I keep going here and going in this direction.”

Derek would ultimately stay in London—and win a handful of titles—until 2007, when he returned to the United States full time and joined the cast of “Dancing with the Stars.”
