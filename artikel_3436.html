Robert Pattinson’s Batman Future After James Bond Casting Unveiled In New Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Following the Warner Bros. Discovery and Paramount merger, there are a lot of questions about the future of DC Studios.

James Gunn and Peter Safran are set to leave their positions as co-CEOs, as Jeremy Latcham takes over for them starting on December 1, which has raised a lot of uncertainty about the DC Universe franchise. However, as the world waits to see what happens with the main continuity, one of the Elseworlds properties may now be facing a larger issue in the near future.

It was officially announced on November 16 that Robert Pattinson, who is also currently playing Batman in Matt Reeves' DC franchise, will take on the role of James Bond in the next installment. While the British actor is shooting The Batman - Part II, The Wrap is reporting that "a source with knowledge" of his deal is claiming that due to "the protracted production" of the superhero sequel, it has allowed Pattinson to "slip out from under his contract," which had him signed for a third movie as the Caped Crusader.

It is worth taking into account that The Batman 3 has never been fully greenlit, as it would all depend on how the 2028 sequel does. Skydance has also not commented on the reports surrounding the contract, and how Pattinson's James Bond casting will affect the lead's future with the DC property, as it has not been confirmed if the upcoming comic book entry will be his final one.

Even with him becoming the next 007, that will have Pattinson signed on for a certain number of projects as the iconic spy; it's also crucial to remember that actors do juggle multiple franchises all the time. Given that there are signficant production breaks in between individual films, and depending on how filming schedules are planned, there could be scenarios where he returns for a hypothetical The Batman 3, especially if Reeves' follow-up becomes a commercial success.

The road to The Batman - Part II film has been a long one, as it has been nearly five years since the first movie came out, which grossed $772.7 million at the worldwide box office, as Pattinson brought a younger iteration of the DC legend to life. While the sequel did get greenlit, it wasn't until this year that the follow-up finally began principal photography.

Given the critical success of The Batman universe, which also brought The Penguin spinoff to HBO, it would be shocking if the Skydance regime doesn't at least try to work things out with Pattinson, if the report about his contract is true, to get him back for a trilogy finale. To this point, the actor has not commented on his future as the Dark Knight.

If The Batman - Part II were to end on a cliffhanger that also sets up a third story, it would be even more crucial to make sure that they can at least wrap up the trilogy with Pattinson. Hopefully, as more details emerge about his James Bond gig, it will also clarify what this means for him as Bruce Wayne.

The Batman - Part II is set to arrive in theaters on February 14, 2028.
