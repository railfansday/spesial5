The Legend Of Zelda: Wind Waker Officially Returns This Winter

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Due to its ongoing 40th anniversary celebrations, as well as the recent success of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake, the beloved Nintendo franchise is truly back in the spotlight. Not that it ever left. With the Nintendo Switch 2 remake now cementing itself as the highest rated game of 2026, at least so far, it is no wonder that fans are hoping that the same treatment will be given to more Zelda classics.

However, at the moment of writing, Nintendo has not officially confirmed the next Zelda remake, but rest assured, it seems as though the studio certainly has plans to. Does this mean we will see beloved entries such as Wind Waker get the modern-day treatment? It remains to be seen, but for now, fans of the 2002 entry have something else to look forward to.

As mentioned, 2026 is the year that celebrates four decades of The Legend of Zelda franchise and, as such, it has been merchandise galore for fans and collectors. Now, the twilight months of the year will be no exception, as The Legend of Zelda: Wind Waker fans can soon get their hands on an adorable breakfast set, complete with matching mug and bowl.

Courtesy of FansSite.be, The Legend of Zelda: Wind Waker breakfast set with mug and bowl is an upcoming set that is available for pre-order now. Not only that, but coming in at €24.99 ($28.00), it will make for a reasonably priced gift ahead of the festive season.

Expected to begin shipping to customers in Q4 of 2026, those living in Belgium and the Netherlands can receive free shipping if their orders reach over €50. However, other countries will expect some shipping fees to be added.

Nevertheless, with a stunning blue design complete with yellow touches, including the Wind Waker symbol and 'The Legend of Zelda' title, you can even expect some diagrams of Link and Zelda themselves. Perfect for groups of two to sit down and share breakfast together, the set is described as the following by manufacturers:

"Start your morning as if you were setting sail across the Great Sea with Link. 🌊 This breakfast set inspired by The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker brings a touch of Hyrule to the table, with a mug and a spacious bowl for your next adventure. Give the set a place in your Zelda collection, or use it to start your day with a little more adventure."

It may not be the Nintendo Switch 2 remake of The Legend of Zelda: Wind Waker, but this ceramic set will definitely be the perfect thing to use when settling down to play the recently released The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake. If you pre-order now, you will not have long to wait until you can eat your cereal and drink your tea in style.
