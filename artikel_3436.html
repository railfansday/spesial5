Judge Sets Charlie Kirk Murder Trial for Almost 3 Years After His Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

2:10 PM PT -- Erika Kirk is blasting the decision to push Tyler Robinson's trial to June 2028 ... calling the delay "deeply disappointing" and saying every additional day without justice prolongs her family's pain.

In a statement posted to X Monday, Erika said nothing can bring Charlie back ... but having to wait even longer for accountability only adds to the family's suffering.

A judge in Tyler Robinson's case has set the trial date for Charlie Kirk's alleged shooter ... and it's slated for almost 3 years after his death.

Utah District Court Judge Tony Graf announced Monday that jury selection for Robinson's trial will begin June 21, 2028.

Erika Kirk wanted a trial by October 2027, while Robinson's legal team had asked that the trial not start until 2029 ... and it seems the judge met them in the middle.

He set 105 days on reserve for the trial ... meaning about five months are calendared for the proceedings.

As you know, Robinson has been accused of murdering the far-right personality on Sept. 10, 2025 ... when he was fatally shot in the neck while speaking at Utah Valley University.

He has pleaded not guilty to aggravated murder ... and the death penalty is on the table if he's convicted.
