‘Spider-Man: Brand New Day’ Returning to Theaters with a New Rosario Dawson Cameo — and Box Office Records and Oscar Noms in Mind

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Usually this is the time of year when early-year and summer awards contenders come back to theaters (see: “The Odyssey”). But even though it only left theaters not long ago, Sony is re-releasing “Spider-Man: Brand New Day,” which is now the biggest domestic release of all time.

The studio is bringing the film back to theaters on December 4, with tickets going on sale November 19. And the new version will be an extended cut of the film (cheekily titled “Spider-Man: [Brand New] Brand New Day”) that will feature one deleted scene in which Rosario Dawson stars as a character called The Night Nurse. Director Daniel Destin Cretton had previously told press that she shot a scene for the film that was cut. The real reason “Spider-Man: Brand New Day” is coming back to theaters is because it gives the film yet another chance to add on to its record box office haul. Maybe it surpasses $1 billion domestic? It’s only $41 million shy, and the re-release of “Avengers Endgame: Encore” managed to gross $40 million domestic, so there’s a real chance of that. It also turns out that Sony really does view the film as an awards contender and thinks it deserves to be back top of mind. SPE chief Tom Rothman recently told Deadline that the studio was launching an FYC campaign for the film and believed “Brand New Day” worthy of a Best Picture nomination.

“If there aren’t people thinking about it, we’re gonna do what we can just to make people think about it,” Rothman said. “I think it’s also incumbent upon studios who make these kinds of quality commercial films to get people to think about it, and here’s what I ask people when I walk around: ‘Are you telling me that there are 10 films this year that are better than this film?’”

The only other new studio wide release on December 4 is “Violent Night 2,” so it’s a relatively light weekend in what figures to be an incredibly crowded Christmas box office, one that even includes Sony’s own “Jumanji: Open World” and of course “Avengers: Doomsday” the weekend prior.
