Trump warns Iran about reported plot against admin officials: 'We take it very seriously'

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

President Donald Trump warned Iran on Monday that it would face unprecedented retaliation if it were to carry out an attack on Washington, D.C.

President Donald Trump warned Iran on Monday that it would face unprecedented retaliation if it were to carry out an attack on Washington, D.C.

Trump made the statement in response to a report from CNN citing multiple U.S. officials stating that Iran is weighing an attempt at killing senior Trump administration officials by detonating a chemical weapon or a dirty bomb in the nation's capital.

"Is there any imminent threat to Washington, D.C.?" a reporter asked on Monday.

"Well, if there is, they will pay a price the likes of which no country has ever paid before. I, I hope it's not, but it's — we take it very seriously because they happen to be crazy. But this is why they can't have a nuclear weapon, by the way, in case people had any questions," Trump responded.

Trump's statement came on the first leg of his trip across Asia this week, meeting with QUAD leaders in the Philippines on Monday, including Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi, Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese.

Trump said he plans to press Chinese President Xi Jinping to help end the war in Iran during their meeting later this week.

CNN's report said Iran's plans for an attack on U.S. soil are in the preliminary phase and are, "for now, an effort to give itself options rather than an indicator of an imminent attack."

White House spokeswoman Lauren Bis told the outlet the U.S. is "monitoring any and all potential threats, but at this time there are no credible threats to the homeland."

The report comes days after news broke that Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and other senior U.S. officials were transferred to military housing in recent weeks.

Threats against Bessent had reached "more than an advanced stage" amid reported Iranian assassination threats, a senior administration official told Fox News Digital on Tuesday.

The Iranian threats against Bessent come amid a documented history of alleged Tehran-backed assassination plots targeting senior U.S. officials.

Attorney General Todd Blanche also relocated to a military base from his home around March, a different source familiar with the arrangements told Fox News Digital.

Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Secretary of War Pete Hegseth have also reportedly taken up residence at Fort McNair in Washington, D.C., while White House chief of staff Stephen Miller and other administration officials have moved to military installations or protected government housing.

The relocations have not all stemmed from the same threats. Some followed concerns about domestic political violence.
