The 007 First Light soundtrack, composed by Joe Henson and Alexis Leon Smith, is in the running for a Grammy award in the "Best Score Soundtrack For Videogames and Other Interactive Media" category, alongside four other contenders:

I had initially reported that the 007 First Light theme by Lana Del Rey—the one I really did not care for—was up for the award, assuming it was part of the soundtrack. It is the theme song, after all. IO Interactive later clarified that this is not the case, however: The Grammy is only for the score soundtrack, which does not include the theme song.

It seems odd to me, but that's the nature of the Grammy Awards, which are a music industry award and subject to some fairly strict rules and requirements. The rules for the videogame category, for instance, state that "videogames that contain music but are not available as stand-alone audio streams/releases" are not eligible for consideration. That means games with soundtracks that aren't released separately in some format effectively don''t exist as far as the Grammy Awards are concerned, no matter how good the music is.
