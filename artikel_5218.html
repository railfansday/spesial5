Bitcoin Drops Below $75K: Key Metric Signals Make-or-Break Support

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Home » Crypto Market News » Bitcoin Drops Below $75K: Key Metric Signals Make-or-Break Support

Shrinking on-chain profits and bearish technicals put Bitcoin’s $74,400 support to the test.

Bitcoin (BTC) price dropped 1.9% over the past 24 hours on Monday, to trade near $74,239, breaking below the psychologically critical $75,000 mark. The decline brings the $74,400 support level into sharp focus as traders weigh the risk of a deeper market correction.

Weakening on-chain profitability and bearish weekly indicators have heightened concerns that BTC could test lower levels toward $70,000.

Derivatives analytics platform CoinGlass recently flagged $74,400 as a key level for BTC, warning on X that a sustained breakdown beneath it would confirm a bearish bias.

With BTC price hovering right near this boundary, buyers must step in to defend the current market structure. A failure to hold $74,400 could open the floodgates for accelerated spot selling.

This is a key level for BTC. A breakdown here would be quite bearish.🤨 pic.twitter.com/q8nmYaQ54G

On-chain analytics reveal a cautious backdrop. Crypto analyst Axel Adler Jr. noted that Bitcoin started the week trading below its one-year median of $77,000.

According to Adler’s analysis, Bitcoin fell 4.4%, from $78K to $74K, between November 1 and 8. Since November 5, it has remained below its 365-day median of $77K, trading between that level and the 25th percentile of $67K.

Adjusted MVRV also declined from 1.075 to 1.047, meaning the average unrealized profit for coins younger than seven years narrowed from 7.5% to 4.7%.

As long as MVRV remains above 1, this group is still in unrealized profit. A drop below 1 would signal unrealized losses.

Adler emphasizes that $77,000 is the key resistance to reclaim. Until Bitcoin regains that median, it remains trapped in a lower range bound by its 25th percentile support at $67,000.

Technical charts align with the cautious on-chain picture. Crypto analyst Ted Pillows pointed on X out that Bitcoin recently closed its fifth consecutive red weekly candle while slipping below its 50-week moving average.

Pillows warned that failing to quickly reclaim this key moving average makes a drop toward $70,000 highly probable—representing an additional 5% to 6% downside from current prices.

$BTC closed its 5th consecutive week in red and also lost the 50W MA.If Bitcoin doesn't reclaim the 50W MA soon, it's highly likely BTC will drop to $70,000. pic.twitter.com/LwcGSkXQoc

Whether Bitcoin price can hold $74,400 and climb back above its $77,000 one-year median will be an important sign of whether selling pressure is easing. If the decline continues and on-chain profitability weakens further, $70,000 could become the next major battleground for bulls.
