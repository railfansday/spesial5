Pony AI’s robotaxi fares surge nearly 400% but its operating losses barely budge

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Pony.ai says fares covered the vehicle costs it tracks in Guangzhou and Shenzhen, leaving the profitability of individual rides unclear.

Pony.ai’s robotaxi fare revenue nearly quintupled year over year in the September quarter, while the company-wide operating loss narrowed to $68.7 million from $69.7 million.

In its Nov. 18 filing, Pony.ai said fare revenue rose nearly 400% year over year. Revenue from its broader robotaxi services business grew 165.4% to $17.8 million from $6.7 million, helped by both passenger fares and higher vehicle deliveries to joint deployments in China and overseas.

In a November service update, separate from its September-quarter figures, Pony.ai said the previously separate operating zones in central Guangzhou and Nansha formed one contiguous area of more than 1,000 square kilometers as of Nov. 17.

Riders can use its PonyPilot app to book trips between Nansha and central Guangzhou, where Pony.ai holds a citywide commercial permit. The area it measured in November covers the connected operating zones where the company says those bookings are available.

CEO James Peng said PonyPilot now lets riders book across Nansha, Haizhu, Tianhe, Huangpu and Panyu. He said the wider network had increased trip distances and the average price per ride in Guangzhou.

On the November earnings call, CFO Leo Wang reported that Pony.ai’s fleets in Guangzhou and Shenzhen had earned enough in fares to more than cover the vehicle costs Pony.ai includes in its calculation.

Pony.ai starts with fares after discounts and refunds, then deducts vehicle depreciation, charging, wages for remote and ground staff, cleaning and maintenance, insurance, parking and communications.

Wang said depreciation accounts for about half of those costs. It covers the vehicle, including the battery and the hardware used to drive it autonomously.

The company said premiums per seventh-generation robotaxi fell 19% from a year earlier at renewal, giving no profit margin for an individual ride.

Across Pony.ai’s robotaxi, robotruck and intelligent solutions businesses, quarterly revenue rose 75.2% to $44.6 million. The reported operating loss fell from 273.9% of revenue to 154.2%, even as the dollar loss narrowed by about $1 million.

Pony.ai’s expansion will test whether more fleets can cover the vehicle costs it tracks as it works to reduce its company-wide loss.
