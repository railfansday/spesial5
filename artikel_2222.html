Jamie McGrath hopeful Hibernian heroics in cup clash can lift league fortunes

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Jamie McGrath hopes Hibernian’s cup heroics can help revive their wobbling William Hill Premiership campaign.

Hibs went into the Premier Sports Cup semi-final clash against Hearts at Hampden on Sunday bottom of the league, having lost four out of four against their city rivals at the national stadium and with Marink Reedijk looking for his first win as boss at the fifth attempt.

It looked like another day of disappointment was on the cards for the Leith side when Jambos attacker Claudio Braga headed in from a Calvin Miller corner with only four minutes on the clock.

However, Hibs fought back and striker Martin Boyle deservedly levelled in the 19th minute and McGrath struck three minutes before the interval with his first goal of the season to ultimately set up a meeting with Rangers in the final at the national stadium on December 13.

Hibs are one point behind second-bottom Kilmarnock ahead of the trip to Aberdeen on Saturday and McGrath, 30, said: “It was a good feeling obviously to give back to the travelling fans.

“We haven’t given them too much to shout about so far this season, so I think they would have bitten your hand off for a win in Hampden against Hearts.

“But yeah, it’s obviously a great feeling, into a cup final now, so something to look forward to.

“Hopefully now this is the start of us getting a turn on things and building on that.

“We have to get our league form back on track because that hasn’t been good enough.

“I think over the last few weeks we’ve shown what we can do in spells.

“It’s just about getting a full 90-minute performance because even on Sunday we were probably on top for a large period of game.

“I know you can’t control the full 90 minutes, but we’re still adapting to what the manager wants and I think when you see on Sunday when it clicks, it can be really, really good, but it’s about keep working hard now.

“We have to build on these small details, Every day is something new for us to be learning and we’re heavily on the video clips and we’re all buying into it. So, yeah, we’re slowly but surely getting there.”

McGrath’s crucial counter after midfielder Joe Newell’s powerful low drive had been blocked off the line and the Republic of Ireland international quickly tucked the ball in at the near post.

He said: “The ball just fell and the next thing I know it was in the back of the net so I didn’t even know how to celebrate or whatever.

“It’s obviously my first of the season, so it’s been a bit of a frustrating period for myself with a few injuries and niggles and stuff like that.

“But hopefully now we can all kind of stay as fit as we can, the squad’s kind of coming back to full fitness over the next few weeks, so hopefully it’s all clicking at the right time.”
