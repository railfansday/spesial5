Scott Parker has been sent £18m West Ham message and Spurs are laughing loudest

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Scott Parker’s start to life as West Ham head coach got off to a thrilling start.

The Hammers drew 3-3 against Cardiff City on Wednesday night in his first game in the dugout, with the team coming from 3-0 behind to secure a point on the road.

Goals from Divine Mukasa, Arne Engels and Max Kilman sealed an exciting comeback in the second half, with the London outfit hoping that they can compete for automatic promotion this season.

But Parker’s first game as manager showed that there is perhaps more work to be done at the London Stadium than initially thought.

The first issue that Parker may have to address as the new West Ham boss is the underwhelming form of summer arrival Manor Solomon.

The winger was brought in with the expectation that he could power the team to promotion, much like he did at Leeds United in 2024/25, where he contributed 10 goals and 12 assists in 39 Championship appearances.

But his form has been inconsistent at the London Stadium so far, and another underwhelming performance on Wednesday night led to him being hauled off after 69 minutes.

While he managed to complete 13 of his 15 attempted passes, the overall total matches that of someone quite uninvolved in the game, and he also failed to create a chance or take a clear shot at goal, leading to a deserved substitution (all stats from Fotmob).

Edvin Austbo was chosen to replace Solomon for the final 20 minutes against Cardiff, making his 10th appearance in the league this season.

This was the eighth time he came off the bench, and his lively performance helped the team in their comeback from 3-0 down.

West Ham United considered the potential appointment of former Tottenham Hotspur and Liverpool coach Johnny Heitinga before deciding on Scott Parker

The Norwegian already has as many goals and assists as Solomon so far this campaign, despite playing only 347 minutes compared to 1,003.

The 21-year-old arrived from Viking IF in a deal worth £18 million in the summer, and now could be the time for Parker to give him an extended run in the team ahead of Solomon.

Austbo hasn’t had an extended run in the team, which impacts how we can judge him, but his appearances so far have shown a lot of promise.

The winger has an expected assists (xA) per 90 of 0.53, creating 0.53 big chances per 90.

He also has 1.32 successful crosses each game at a success rate of 35.7 per cent, which makes him a dangerous threat in the final third, all while also taking 2.63 shots each game himself.

If he can replicate these kinds of performances over a series of starts in the coming weeks, then Parker will have no choice but to keep him in the lineup going forward over Solomon.

West Ham’s rivals Tottenham Hotspur signed Solomon in 2023, but he struggled for game time under various managers at the London outfit, which led to loan spells at Leeds, Villarreal and Fiorentina.

But the Hammers committed to a £5 million deal to sign the winger despite his lack of impact at Spurs.

Given the question marks over his quality now, the Premier League side have had the last laugh on their rival.

Parker dropping him to the bench would be the ultimate vindication for Spurs’ decision to cash in on the forward last summer.
