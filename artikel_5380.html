Prosus is eyeing Delivery Hero markets Uber plans to drop as food bets weigh on profit

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The company's finance chief said it will examine the businesses; ecosystem adjusted EBITDA fell to $492 million from $530 million

Avishek Das / SOPA Images / LightRocket via Getty Images

Prosus is weighing purchases of several businesses that Delivery Hero is offloading while it is taken over by Uber $UBER Technologies, Chief Financial Officer Nico Marais told Bloomberg on Wednesday. Marais declined to comment on specific markets.

Delivery Hero agreed to sell operations in 14 markets, including Turkey, Spain and Greece, to investment firm SSW Partners, according to Bloomberg. The Uber deal is expected to conclude in 2027.

"We have taken note of the structure of that deal where Uber is not going to keep" certain markets, Marais said, according to Bloomberg. "We will see if there are any opportunities that might fit our purpose."

Any move for markets going to SSW Partners would first have to get past antitrust scrutiny, according to Bloomberg. "In some of those markets we have overlaps as well, which would make it more difficult, and in some markets we don't have any presence," Marais said. He said it is premature to talk about particular countries or transactions.

Chief Executive Officer Fabricio Bloisi told Bloomberg Television that mergers and acquisitions are "not our focus today." He described the current year as one of "delivering consistent execution."

Prosus reported first-half results on Wednesday for the six months ended Sept. 30. Ecosystem adjusted EBITDA was $492 million, compared with $530 million in the prior period, and included $344 million of investment in iFood and Just Eat Takeaway.com. Ecosystem revenue grew 59% to $5.7 billion, and adjusted earnings per share rose 12%.

At iFood, the Brazilian food delivery business, revenue grew 20% to $1.1 billion. Adjusted EBITDA fell to $28 million from $184 million, reflecting incremental investment of $278 million, Prosus said.

At Just Eat Takeaway.com, revenue decreased 13% to $1.7 billion, and adjusted EBITDA was $34 million after $32 million of restructuring charges, including market exit costs. Orders grew in September 2026 for the first time in 55 months. Prosus took control of the business in December 2025.

"We said FY27 would be a year of execution, and we are delivering," Bloisi said in a statement. Marais said in the statement that Prosus expects profitability and cash flow to improve in the second half and expects a return to adjusted EBITDA growth in fiscal 2028.

Prosus stock fell as much as 4% after the results.
