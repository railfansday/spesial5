Ripple Moves $389 Million of XRP Amid Aid for Binance Hack

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The San Francisco blockchain firm backing XRP, Ripple, has just pulled a mysterious XRP move that could barely go unnoticed considering the volume of the tokens involved.

According to the latest data provided by blockchain tracker Whale Alert, Ripple has been spotted moving hundreds of millions of XRP, a few hours after it announced support for Binance's attack.

Per the data, Ripple has conducted multiple transactions carrying a total of 310 million XRP worth over $389.08 million with an unidentified wallet address on Friday, October 30.

While the transfers were made in three separate transactions within just a few minutes, the data showed that they were conducted when XRP was trading at around $1.25.

The first transaction saw Ripple transfer 120 million XRP, worth about $150.57 million, to an unknown wallet. Meanwhile, the two other transactions saw 90,000,001 XRP worth $112.96 million and 99,999,999 XRP worth $125.56 million transferred to Ripple from wallets that could not be identified.

The timing and volume of such a transfer, especially from a renowned firm like Ripple, did not go unnoticed, and it has sparked concerns among market participants about the motive behind the transfers, as none has been stated yet.

Although speculations suggest that the move could have been attributed to Ripple selling XRP, especially at a volatile time like this, other commentators are raising a different concern.

Since the transfers arrived just hours after a Ripple executive confirmed that the company was assisting with efforts to address the recent exploit involving Binance, which led to the theft of some XRP, there are questions about whether the moves could be related.

Although Ripple had confirmed that it was actively freezing affected funds while coordinating with relevant parties, it remains uncertain if the XRP move is part of its aid in addressing the disturbing incident.
