LAFC and AccelerAI Partner to Pioneer Co-Development AI Tools to Drive Enterprise Business Value in Professional Sports

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Partnership champions an agile pilot-to-product framework that transforms proprietary machine learning into cross-functional revenue and operational efficiency

SAN JOSE, Calif., Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Los Angeles Football Club (LAFC) today announced a major paradigm shift in how professional sports franchises leverage artificial intelligence, highlighting an innovative co-development framework designed to solve complex business problems. Rejecting off-the-shelf tools, LAFC has partnered with AccelerAI to build a proprietary agentic AI system powered by machine learning (ML) and large language model (LLM) technology that unlock massive downstream value across the entire enterprise ecosystem.

While many organizations deploy AI as isolated IT infrastructure, LAFC's approach focuses entirely on measurable corporate impact. By identifying and solving operational friction points, the club's proprietary AI tools generate cascading financial benefits across multiple business units.

LAFC and AccelerAI launch a co-developed agentic AI system, delivering double-digit growth in enterprise business value.

"Our strategy is built on shifting AI from a speculative tech expense into a core operational multiplier," said Vanessa Montes-Gubin, Chief Marketing Officer of LAFC. "Instead of deploying isolated technical solutions, we are focused on co-developing tools that drive cross-functional enterprise value while simultaneously elevating how our fans engage with the club. When predictive intelligence operates smoothly across marketing, operations, and commercial teams, it allows us to build a more responsive business and deliver highly synchronized, meaningful connection points for our entire global community."

The strength of this co-development model was recently validated through an inaugural matchday attendance pilot. To execute the framework's initial attendance preservation workflows, LAFC and AccelerAI connected advanced predictive engines featuring over 100 contextual data points and partnered with Ticketmaster to validate each seat. By accurately forecasting attendance gaps before kickoff, the system prompted attendance confirmation and organic peer-to-peer ticket transfers through native mobile channels. This single application successfully unlocked double-digit show-rate growth, particularly during volatile weekday windows, while maintaining total ticket price integrity.

"We've always approached technology at LAFC with the expectation that it deliver meaningful business outcomes, and AI is no different," said Christian Lau, Chief Technology Officer of LAFC. "Our work with AccelerAI is focused on bringing together our data, operational expertise and predictive intelligence to address real business challenges. We're moving beyond experimentation by validating these capabilities in a live operating environment and establishing a foundation to scale successful applications across the enterprise. The opportunity isn't simply to deploy more AI; it's to make our entire organization smarter, more efficient and better connected."

"Sports teams have already invested in specialized systems that support every part of the business, but gaps in data visibility and coordination between teams limit their potential," said Albert Shih, Chief Product Officer of AccelerAI. "We see the next opportunity in AI agents that work across those systems to surface insights, coordinate workflows and take action, helping teams move from information to execution. We're bringing our enterprise AI experience to our first partnership in professional sports, and we're grateful to build alongside LAFC. Their deep operational knowledge and commitment to co-development are essential to turning that vision into measurable results."

With its first attendance preservation pilot locked, the collaboration is moving to automate the architecture into a standalone platform, available to other professional sports organizations. LAFC and AccelerAI are already mapping out the next phase of their co-development roadmap, targeting a diverse portfolio of future specialized AI agents designed to drive back-office efficiency, maximize asset utilization, create touchpoints for the LAFC to engage seamlessly with fans and to elevate enterprise revenue opportunities across all lines of club operations.
