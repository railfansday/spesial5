Exclusive: Julian Ward begins talks to sign extraordinary midfield sensation for Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Julian Ward's hands are tied, this African midfielder simply has to be on his way to Liverpool.

Times are changing at Anfield. A new coach, new minority owners, and a new sporting director. But one thing remains the same - the ambition to conquer the world.

Ward has been here before. He knows the demands and the pressures it takes to try to steer one of the biggest clubs on the planet in the right direction.

This time, though, his actions matter more. You only get one chance at a rebuild. If you get it right you sail away towards fertile grounds and all the glory you can imagine. But if you get it wrong, you descend towards a dark abyss, the kind from which it is impossible to claw back to the pinnacle of the sport.

Just look at Manchester United. They will know full well what we are talking about.

What are Liverpool's next steps under Julian Ward?It does feel like the Reds are at least somewhat going in the right direction at the moment. Alexander Isak is scoring goals, Florian Wirtz is putting in man of the match displays and on top of that, you have players like Rio Ngumoha, Trey Nyoni and Jeremy Jacquet quite truly establishing themselves as the future superstars of the game. There's a strong core at Liverpool. The foundation has been built. But you can't have a house just with the foundation. Ward needs to now start laying the bricks. It means recruiting players who are ready to come in and fill areas where the team is short. And it also means bringing in future stars who will be able to increase Iraola's squad depth and give him more tools to work with.

Liverpool trying to sign Ibrahima KonteIt is in the quest for the latter that Ward has turned towards another Senegalese gem. Liverpool have agreed a deal to sign Sidy Barhama Ndiaye, as we revealed to you exclusively almost 18 months ago. Anfield Watch can now exclusively reveal that Liverpool have also enquired about Ibrahima Konte, who is currently starring for Senegal at the U17 World Cup and represents Gambino Stars at academy level. The Reds' recruitment team have initiated talks after Konte caught the eye of the club's scouts and are keen on the midfielder. But Liverpool are not the only club interested in Konte. A midfielder of extraordinary quality. Konte is already being tracked by Barcelona and Chelsea as well as a plethora of other teams. He has gone viral at the U17 World Cup with a sensational Lionel Messi-esque solo goal against Cuba. But there's much more to him than merely that goal. Konte's performance has been extremely impressive across the park. The way he operates in tight spaces, the clever passes that he makes and the way he can dictate the tempo of the game are exactly the traits Liverpool are looking for when recruiting midfielders. Konte won all of his duels (9/9) against Cuba, and looked like an incredible hybrid of Luka Modric and N'Golo Kante. He is to put it succinctly - a remarkable talent, which is why it looks like Liverpool are trying to get ahead of the race to sign him. Ward has already brought in Mor Talla and Sidy Barhama Ndiaye - could he make it a Senegalese trio? Only time will tell.
