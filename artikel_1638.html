Opinion: Liverpool midfielder is absolutely proving his worth

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Dominik Szoboszlai has undeniably been a key figure at Liverpool under Andoni Iraola.

The 26-year-old is clearly held in high regard by the coaching team at Anfield and has been an almost constant feature in the starting XI when he has been available.

The Reds have struggled to find balance in the midfield this season, but it appears that Szoboszlai's place in the team remains safe, and the Hungary international has made some vital contributions in several matches this season.

Szoboszlai's form has continued early in the international break, with the Hungary captain coming up big for his nation when they needed him on Saturday night.
