Brentford 4-0 Everton: Rampant Bees up to sixth in dominant victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Vitaly Janelt scored a 30-yard screamer as Brentford romped to a 4-0 victory against 10-man Everton at the Gtech Community Stadium.

One win in five had seen some of the early season optimism around Keith Andrews’ team fade but this was an emphatic response to fire them up the Premier League table to sixth, even if culpability for Everton’s collapse lay largely with James Tarkowski, red carded in the 40th minute for a horror tackle on Yehor Yarmolyuk.

David Moyes was setting a new record for number of games in charge, this his 593rd match eclipsing the tally of Harry Catterick racked up during the 60s and 70s, but this was a terrible first half and was no way to mark such an achievement.

Everything went wrong for the visitors. Both Brentford goals scored by Keane Lewis-Potter came via deflections and Tarkowski’s momentary abdication of responsibility as captain, lunging studs-up into the shin of Yarmolyuk with his team 2-0 down, would have been impossible for Moyes to mitigate. He was let down badly by one of his senior players in a moment of direst need.

Thereafter the game was little contest. It might have trickled towards a routine two-goal win for Brentford had Janelt not pulled out a brilliant strike from range that ripped past Jordan Pickford to rub salt in the wound for Moyes, and for Tarkowski.

Fabio Carvalho bundled one in from on the goal-line with his knee in stoppage time, the final indignity for Everton.

Brentford were excellent throughout. Early on, Callum Wilson fed Michael Kayode arriving up on the right, the full-back given a clear shot on Pickford’s goal but dragged his effort badly across the face and wide.

Everton were making it too easy. Midway through the half they failed to deal with a ball over the top and Jaidon Anthony sent it low into the middle with a deft stab of the toe. It was a beautifully controlled first-time pass, finding Lewis-Potter arriving in an acre of space and his shot spun in off the lunging Jarrad Branthwaite.

It took less than two minutes for Lewis-Potter to hit a near-identical second, with a ball into the box from Mikkel Damsgaard met with unconvincing Everton defending.

The Brentford midfielder was given two attempts, his first shot rebounding back to him to squeeze the ball in at the second go via a deflection off Thierno Barry.

Everton were sinking and their captain abandoned them to their fate. Tarkowski was first shown only a yellow for a studs-up challenge on Yarmolyuk. A single look at a replay confirmed the foul was far more serious, the defender’s boot connecting high on the shin of Yarmolyuk’s standing leg and his dismissal was inevitable.
