University launches $5m smart system that checks who is actually listening in class

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

A university in China has rolled out a $5 million surveillance system that actually checks if people are paying attention in class.

Plenty of schools across the globe have embraced technology in their classrooms. Some of these have been as simple as letting kids use iPads and computers, while other schools are fully focused on teaching students about AI. Others are even using AI-powered robots to help them teach.

So, these days, it can be pretty difficult to slack off, not pay attention to what your teacher is saying, and then grab some notes from your friend.

Over in China, though, things have been taken to the next level when it comes to checking on who is actually paying attention to the lessons.

As per the South China Morning Post, Nanjing University, in Jiangsu province, has rolled out a smart classroom system that monitors students in different ways.

The system, which cost just under $5 million, tracks attendance and lateness, but is also able to check the “head-raising rate” of students as well. If you’re not looking up at the teacher and paying attention, the system will recognise that and count it against you.

According to SCMP, some students have rallied against the system on social media, forcing the university to scrap the tracking of head-raising rates. However, that hasn’t stopped other schools from going ahead with it.

SCMP noted that the China University of Mining and Technology in Jiangsu tracks the “classroom status” of students. This identified students who checked their phones during class, as well as flagging some as being asleep – even though they had been collecting notes.
