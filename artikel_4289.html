Elder Scrolls: Sentinel has been named though not dated, but Elder Scrolls: Castles is here for a mobile fix

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Hallelujah! It seems that Bethesda Softworks have finally kicked themselves into gear, with the announcement that the hotly anticipated Elder Scrolls VI will be called Sentinel. And... Oh, turns out, that's all. Guess we're back to waiting, but if you need something to tide you over, did you know you can play The Elder Scrolls on mobile?

Well, one of its spinoffs at the very least. With the shuttering of The Elder Scrolls: Blades earlier this year, options can seem thin on the ground. But dig a little deeper, and you'll find that Bethesda's The Elder Scrolls: Castles, which lets you build and manage your own fortress and family dynasty, offers you a hefty taste of Tamriel on the go!

Speaking of which, there's an interesting connection to mobile in The Elder Scrolls VI's setting. The Elder Scrolls Travels series for the N-Gage took players to a variety of then-unexplored regions of Tamriel, such as Stormhold in Black Marsh, Dawnstar in Skyrim and, yes, Hammerfell!

Either way, with The Elder Scrolls VI likely at least a year or more out from release, if you want to get your Elder Scrolls itch scratched on the go, then your best bet is diving into The Elder Scrolls: Castles!
