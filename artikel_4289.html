Reliance on technology is eroding trust in one another and ability to tell what is even real, expert warns

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The danger of generative AI is not some distant algorithmic failure; it is the slow erosion of our ability to tell what's real, to trust our institutions and to think for ourselves at all. These are the warnings of Mateusz Łabuz in his new book, The End of Reality.
