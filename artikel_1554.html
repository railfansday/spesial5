PSG: Hakimi’s amusing anecdote about his exchange with Luis Enrique

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Triple scorer against Le Havre and decisive passer on the other two Parisian goals, Achraf Hakimi brought PSG to the Océane stadium, Friday evening in Ligue 1 (1-5). After the match, the 27-year-old Moroccan right-back returned to a discussion he had with his coach, Luis Enrique, after his first goal. While his achievement was initially refused for offside, it was finally validated. During the video analysis, Hakimi spoke with his coach… who thought his player had gone to complain to the referee.

“ What did he say to me? One moment, I was talking to the linesman. I said to him: “Did you see that it was offside?” He said to me: “I think so.” I continued to interact with him in a funny way, but the coach thought I was complaining! That’s why he scolded me. But he often asks me that, it’s true that I often speak with the referees. But this time it wasn’t for bad faith, it was for my goal and I’m glad they gave it to me “, he related. Everything ended well in the end!
