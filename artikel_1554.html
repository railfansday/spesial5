Cardinale ‘proud’ after turnaround vs. Olympiacos: “Another important milestone”

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Gerry Cardinale wasn’t in Piraeus last night, but he was very pleased with AC Milan’s comeback victory against Olympiacos.

The owner has been present at almost every Milan game this season, demonstrating that he is far more involved and has been since the summer. However, he could not be in Greece last night, and for 50 minutes he might not have regretted that decision as his side fell two goals behind.

The, the Rossoneri turned the tables thanks to goals from Omari Hutchinson, Alphadjo Cisse, Christian Pulisic and Diego Moreira, winning 4-2 in the end. Hutchinson deserves a special mention for his solo effort, two assists and having a big hand in the goal that sealed it too.

With the win, Milan are back in the running for the Europa League play-offs, sitting 20th at present. Who knows, with three wins in their next three matches, they might even dream of a top-eight finish. Cardinale spoke to MilanNews to offer his thoughts.

“This was another important milestone for the project, not just for the victory itself, but for the way the team pulled together and managed to come back, especially in an away game,” he said.

“I’m proud of the team and the coaching staff. Many of the players were exhausted at the end of the game, which shows that they really gave everything on the pitch. I really appreciate that, and this gives us an even more solid foundation on which to continue building.”
