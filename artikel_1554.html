Soule: “We must learn from our defeats.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

To bounce back immediately after the Manchester defeat. This was Soulé’s message, as he posted on social media to express his disappointment over the defeat at Old Trafford.

“We lost an important match, but we must learn, correct, and start again. We will continue to work with the same enthusiasm and commitment to achieve our goals.”

Now Sassuolo are aiming to return to winning ways and stay on the heels of Inter and Lazio in the league. Matias will once again lead the attack alongside Malen.

It’s a golden moment for the Argentine, who has been called up again by Scaloni and will feature in the two friendlies against Uruguay and Costa Rica.
