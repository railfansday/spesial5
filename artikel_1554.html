Mercato: Kevin de Bruyne is available at a gift price!

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

It’s rather tense on the Naples side at the moment. First, because the Partenopei are in a very disappointing tenth place in the Serie A standings after eleven days. But also because of problems in the locker room. Kevin de Bruyne would have clashed with several of his teammates, including Rasmus Hojlund, Di Lorenzo and Lorenzo Lucca, and his relationship with Max Allegri would be far from ideal.

Very quickly, the Italian media began to mention a departure of the 35-year-old Belgian midfielder, who arrived in Neapolitan lands in the summer of 2025 at the end of his contract at Manchester City, and whose lease in the south of Italy expires at the end of the season. An early departure therefore, while he remains a rather important player, with 4 goals and an assist in 11 Serie A matches this season, and four starts in the Champions League.

And as indicated La Gazzetta dello Sportthe Belgian will be available at a particularly low price. Indeed, an amount of 7 million euros would be enough to dislodge him. This is more or less what De Bruyne cost the club in terms of salary. The Neapolitans would simply like to recover their investment.

For the moment, PSV Eindhoven has already positioned itself, and the Dutch challenge would be considered very interesting by the player, who knows several members of the club’s staff. To replace him, Naples could draw on the side of Ligue 1 and AS Monaco, with Mamadou Coulibaly and Lamine Camara who are among the tracks favored by the Italians. Notice to those interested…
