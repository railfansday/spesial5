Get Your Home Holiday-Ready: Essential Maintenance Checks from Authority Brands

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Heating, cooling, electrical, plumbing and restoration experts introduce a shared checklist to help homeowners establish a yearly maintenance habit

COLUMBIA, Md., Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- A warm home, working plumbing, and reliable power are essentials for a house full of holiday guests. As homeowners prepare to host, Authority Brands is encouraging them to add a home checkup to their holiday to-do list, with a practical maintenance checklist featuring expertise from One Hour Heating & Air Conditioning, Mister Sparky, Benjamin Franklin Plumbing and DRYmedic Restoration Services.

From clearing vents and checking for leaks to testing alarms and updating emergency contacts, the checklist helps homeowners identify potential concerns, tackle simple tasks and recognize when to bring in a professional before guests arrive.

"Holiday preparations offer a natural reminder to check on the systems our homes depend on every day," said Steve Clemente, President and COO of Trades Brands at Authority Brands. "Setting aside time for that checkup each year can help homeowners catch small concerns early and plan for maintenance well beyond the holiday season."

Look for Leaks and Check Plumbing Performance

Turn Your Checklist Into a Maintenance PlanAn annual checkup is a starting point for ongoing care. Use your checklist to make a plan for what comes next. If you spot a concern or aren't sure what needs attention, talk it through with a qualified service professional. Ask what should be addressed now, what to keep an eye on and which routine tasks to add to your calendar.
