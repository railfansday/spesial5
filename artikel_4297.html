BYD launches the new Tang EV, a luxury 5-seat SUV with two +30″ screens for under $30,000

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

BYD launched the third-generation Tang, a large, high-tech five-seat flagship electric SUV, starting at under $30,000 in China.

The new third-gen Tang is positioned below the Great Tang, or “Da Tang” as it’s called in China, BYD’s larger seven-seat flagship SUV in its Dynasty lineup.

BYD held a launch event for its new flagship five-seat electric SUV on Tuesday. The third-gen Tang is available in three variants: standard Premium, Laser Premium, and Laser Flagship.

All versions come standard with rear-wheel drive (RWD), but buyers can upgrade the Laser Flagship model with an optional all-wheel-drive (AWD) steer-by-wire package for an extra 20,000 yuan ($3,000).

Prices for the standard Tang Premium start at 187,900 yuan, or about $28,000, significantly lower than its pre-sale price of 225,000 yuan ($34,000).

The Laser Premium and Laser Flagship models start at 207,900 yuan and 219,900 yuan, respectively, also well below the pre-sale price.

All versions use BYD’s second-generation Blade LFP Batteries. RWD models use a single 402 hp (300kW) electric motor, delivering CLTC ranges of 453 miles (730 km) for the base Premium model and 528 miles (850 km) for the Laser Premium and Laser Flagship versions.

Upgrading to the dual-motor AWD powertrain bumps power to 690 hp (515 kW) and delivers a CLTC range of up to 478 miles (770 km).

The optional AWD steer-by-wire package adds a premium touch with a drive-by-wire digital chassis, reclining driver’s seats, a 35.4″ integrated Star Ring display screen, and intelligent electric all-wheel-drive.

All versions feature BYD’s Flash Charging tech, enabling it to charge from 10% to 80% in five minutes, or nine minutes for a full recharge (10% to 97%).

Inside, the flagship SUV adopts a five-seat layout and is packed with huge digital screens and premium extras.

The third-gen Tang features two giant screens: a 35.4″ integrated Star Ring display and a 30″ panoramic theater screen.

BYD said the five-seat setup opens up interior space for passengers, claiming the new Tang SUV has the largest front trunk (frunk) in its class at 205L. It also offers a generous 1,000 mm of legroom for rear passengers.

The flagship electric SUV is equipped with BYD’s latest tech and other features, including its God’s Eye B laser-assisted driving system with DiPilot300 and Disus-A dual-chamber intelligent air suspension body control system.

BYD’s God’s Eye B uses a roof-mounted LiDAR to support ADAS features such as city navigation assist, high-speed navigation assist, and all-scenario parking assist.

The third-gen Tang also has BYD’s new high-speed tire blowout stability system, which can keep the vehicle stable and in its lane at up to 112 mph (180 km/h).

Measuring 5,045 mm long, 1,980 mm wide, and 1,760 mm tall, with a 2,950 mm wheelbase, BYD’s new Tang SUV is about the size of a Tesla Model X or BMW iX.

The third-gen Tang will join the larger Great Tang as BYD expands its flagship SUV lineup. The Great Tang received over 150,000 preorders by the time it launched in mid-June, a record for a single BYD model.
