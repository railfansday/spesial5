While Phantom Blade Zero is a truly excellent game (scoring a 5/5 in my official review), players have been hard at work doing what they can to improve it in the 10 days since it launched. We've seen full Soulslike transformations, game-changing cheat menus, and the addition of Sun Wukong from Chinese folklore.

It feels like the type of game that's going to get consistent support from its already ginormous player base, with that support continuing over recent times. One player is seemingly on a mission to make the game's best bosses into playable characters, with three already completed and ready to go.

Over on Nexus Mods, creator QOutlaw has been creating playable versions of some of Phantom Blade Zero's most memorable bosses. They've made three so far, and they're all excellent.

The first they released was a playable version of Bai Chilian, including her outfits and voice. It's received over 23,000 downloads so far.

Bai Chilian is arguably the best boss from Phantom Blade Zero's second area, Crimson Forest. She has two phases: the first is a more grounded fight where she summons ribbon snakes to aid her in battle. In the second, she becomes possessed, attacking from strings around the arena. If you collect all of the memory flowers related to her, you are assisted in battle by one of Soul's allies. It's a great fight, and while you'll be playing as her base form in this mod, equipping the White Serpent and Crimson Viper swords will make it a lot of fun.

The second mod QOutlaw uploaded is a playable Yuding- No.12 Crescent Blade Puppet. This fight happens late in Phantom Blade Zero and has some of the best animations in the entire game. She also blesses you with what I believe is the best weapon of the game's 30. Probably not as iconic as Bai Chilian, but a great option.

The final mod turns Soul into Mo Yuan, the masked man found in the Loong challenges, played by Donnie Yen. He's a pivotal character in Phantom Blade Zero's overarching story and is vital to the game's true ending. He's also exceptionally cool. And he's Donnie Yen.

I love the work that modders do, and installing any of these will make future NG+ runs of Phantom Blade Zero feel entirely different. A big recommendation from me.
