Toyota is giving the Corolla a radical makeover, including its first 100% electric version

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Toyota is overhauling its best-selling vehicle of all time, which will include the first fully electric Corolla.

The Toyota Corolla is one of the most recognized and best-selling automotive nameplates of all time.

Since its debut in 1966, over 57 million drivers have chosen the Corolla thanks to its reliability, low cost of ownership, efficiency, and affordable price tag.

Toyota sells the Corolla in more than 150 countries and has expanded the lineup over the past 50 years from sedans and hatchbacks to coupes, wagons, a sporty GR Corolla, and the Corolla Cross compact SUV.

For its 13th generation, Toyota is planning a major overhaul that’s expected to be its biggest transformation yet.

The next-gen Toyota Corolla will be based on a new “multipathway” platform that will support gas, hybrid, plug-in hybrid, and, for the first time, fully electric powertrains.

“Whether it’s a battery EV, plug-in hybrid, hybrid, or internal combustion engine vehicle ― whatever the power source ― let’s make good-looking cars that everyone will want to drive!” Toyota’s former president and current CEO Koji Sato said after unveiling the Corolla Concept at the Japan Mobility Show last October.

Toyota has kept most details a secret, including battery and powertrain specs. However, the new version is expected to adopt a similar design to the concept.

“When we created the concept, we wanted to give the Corolla name a new twist,” Norihiko Kobayashi, who has led the Corolla program since 2011, told Motor.es during a recent interview.

The Corolla has undergone several minor updates over the past 60 years, but the next-gen model will look nothing like its predecessors.

“It wasn’t simply about following in the footsteps of the past,” Kobayashi explained, “we wanted to delve into the future while still maintaining the Corolla identity.”

Developing the next-gen Corolla “wasn’t just about making a new car, but about redefining the future without looking back,” he added.

Like the current version, the new Toyota Corolla will come in several different body types, including a sedan, hatchback, crossover, and a GR variant.

Details have yet to be confirmed, but the Toyota Corolla EV is expected to be available in single- and dual-motor configurations and offer a pure electric driving range of at least 250 miles.

The hybrid will reportedly use a new 1.5-liter four-cylinder engine and an electric motor. Toyota’s engineers told Car and Driver the hybrid system would deliver around 134 horsepower. It’s also estimated to be 10% to 20% more efficient than the current Corolla.

Toyota’s next-gen Corolla is expected to debut sometime in 2027 as a 2028 model year. According to Car and Driver, the gas version will likely start at around $25,000, the hybrid around $27,000, and the EV and PHEVs will likely be priced in the $30,000 range.
