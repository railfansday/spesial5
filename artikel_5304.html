Elon Musk spends about an hour a week on his $16 billion foundation. Most of its 2025 giving went to another Musk nonprofit

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Elon Musk’s foundation gave away nearly $400 million in 2025, and more than three-quarters of it went to another nonprofit he founded, according to the Musk Foundation’s latest tax filing, first reported by Bloomberg.

That total is down from the record $474 million the foundation gave in 2024. Over the same year, its assets grew by more than $1 billion, to about $16 billion, helped by a $64 million gift of Tesla shares. That makes it one of the world’s largest private foundations.

The gap between what the foundation holds and what it gives matters because private foundations must generally pay out about 5% of their investment assets each year or face IRS excise taxes. The Musk Foundation has fallen short of that minimum before, Forbes reported after reviewing its 2024 filing. Foundations can make up a shortfall the following year, so one year’s giving doesn’t settle whether the rule was met.

The foundation runs with almost no staff. Its tax return lists three directors: Musk, his family-office head Jared Birchall, and Matilda Simon. Musk, who is also listed as president, reported working for the foundation about an hour a week, Bloomberg reported.

More than $310 million went to The Foundation, a nonprofit Musk set up to run a STEM-focused primary and secondary school in Bastrop, Texas, outside Austin, with plans to eventually add a university.

Another $20 million went to a donor-advised fund at Fidelity Charitable over which Musk has advisory control. Donors to these funds can take a tax deduction when they contribute and later recommend grants to charities. Once the money is in, the sponsoring organization legally controls it, while the donor keeps advisory privileges, according to the IRS.

That structure has drawn scrutiny before. In 2022, Fortune reported philanthropy experts’ concerns that Musk’s giving could lower his tax bill without quickly moving money to working charities.

Other gifts went to Memphis-area organizations, where xAI, the AI company Musk founded and SpaceX acquired in February, runs the Colossus data centers it is racing to expand. Slingshot Memphis, a nonprofit that uses data, research, and evaluations to measure the effectiveness of poverty-fighting programs, received $5.9 million. Regional One Health Foundation received $9.4 million for patient care.

Musk signed the Giving Pledge in 2012, committing to give away the majority of his wealth during his lifetime or in his will. Bloomberg reported that he gave roughly $1.4 billion from 2021 through 2024, even as his net worth grew by hundreds of billions of dollars.

Forbes estimates Musk’s net worth at about $812 billion as of Nov. 21. The foundation’s 2025 giving equals about 0.05% of that, though most of his wealth is tied up in SpaceX and Tesla stock rather than cash.

That number swings hard. Musk became the world’s first trillionaire in June after SpaceX went public, and his fortune has crossed back and forth over that line since.

Musk has argued that giving well is harder than it looks. “It is very difficult to give money away for the reality of goodness,” he has said.
