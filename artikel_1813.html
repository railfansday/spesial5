Huddersfield Town's Bali Mumba on Plymouth Argyle 'vibes' with Terriers - and his long and winding journey

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The South Shields-born player has plied his trade out east at Norwich City and Peterborough United and also way out west at Plymouth Argyle.

It's been quite a journey and now he finds himself back a fair bit closer to his native north east at Huddersfield Town, settled both on and off the pitch.

Not that his initial few months in West Yorkshire were particularly easy.

January moves are often not straightforward and so it proved for the left-sided player.

He joined a Town side wrestling with form issues - a few hours after he made his debut, Lee Grant was sacked - and the mood in the stands hardly improved as winter turned to spring.

Thankfully, the mood music is transformed these days at second-placed Town, who visit Doncaster Rovers in a Yorkshire derby on Thursday evening.

Mumba, whose partner had a baby earlier this year, said: "My family have all settled. My missus, the kids, my daughters, they're all good.

"I've settled. This is home. Obviously, it's not far from where my actual family live, which is up north in Newcastle.

"Everything now, for me, is where I need it to be. I'm exactly where I feel like I'm meant to be at this point in my career.

"I think I was closer to France when I was at Plymouth than actually to my family up north!. I've been all over the place. I started off at Sunderland and went to Norwich, Peterborough and Plymouth.

"It's been quite a journey, to be fair. It's finally good to be in the middle, at the centre of everything and have the support that I have closer to me and around me.

"I also had pre-season to help me find my focus and concentration and really start again and start reaping the rewards."

Mumba is starting to display the form which convinced Town to shelve out a significant fee of around £1million to bring him back north in the January window.

A fan favourite during his time at Home Park, Mumba played a major part in Argyle's League One title-winning campaign in 2022-23.

Highlights included a superb solo goal in the Devonians' first game back in the second-tier after a 13-year absence in August 2023 - ironically against Town.

Mumba, who signed a long-term deal with Town until at least 2029, is now chasing a second promotion from the third tier - in the blue and white of Town as opposed to the green and white of Argyle.

He continued: "Right now, I would say I'm getting that vibe that I got from Plymouth. For me, it's the dressing room, players and togetherness that gives you the success.

"At Norwich, it was the same. The year we got promoted at (Plymouth), I feel like that togetherness was really what was behind the success, what a lot of people don't see. Come Saturday, you're going to war with your mate.

"Whoever's next to you, you're going into the battle. You've got to have that relationship and you've got to care for each other."
