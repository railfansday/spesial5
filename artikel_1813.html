Prediction: Club Brugge vs Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Turning their attention back to Champions League action on Wednesday night, Liverpool will travel to Belgium when they meet Club Brugge.

With a stacked European schedule from across the continent in midweek, there are plenty of eye-catching Champions League betting promotions available.

Ahead of Wednesday’s showdown from the Jan Breydel Stadium, you can find all of the key Club Brugge vs Liverpool H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, while Club Brugge might have opened their Champions League adventure with a rollercoaster 3-2 loss at home against Aston Villa on September 8th, the Belgium giants have come roaring back in a sensational fashion. Embarking on what has become an astonishing European flurry, Ivan Leko’s men find the mood in their camp at a red-hot high and they will certainly be relishing a blockbuster showdown on Wednesday night. Collecting an iconic European scalp last month as they clinched a famous 4-3 win away at reigning Italian champions Inter Milan, Club Brugge have now won all of their previous three consecutive showdowns on the continent. Likewise, marking their return from November’s international break with a 5-1 romp against KV Mechelen over the weekend, Wednesday’s hosts are unbeaten in all of their previous 11 consecutive appearances across all competitions (10 wins, one draw).

Suffering a reported muscle injury during Club Brugge’s 2-1 Champions League victory away at PSV earlier in the month, Spanish youngster Jan Virgili remains on the sidelines. Likewise, yet to make a maiden appearance this season, 22-year-old Joel Ordonez is still recovering from a long-term foot issue.

Enjoying what has become a stunning purple patch this season and registering four direct goal contributions in as many European appearances (two goals, two assists), Portuguese international Carlos Forbs will play a leading role in the final third. Completing a permanent switch from West Ham over the summer, Freddie Potts will start in the middle of the park.

As for the visitors, marking their return from November’s international break with a stunning performance at Anfield, Liverpool will be dreaming of a repeat display here. Eventually storming their way to a sensational 4-0 romp at home against historic domestic foes Manchester United, Andoni Iraola’s men have found top gear. Despite falling to an action-packed 3-2 defeat away at Turkish giants Fenerbahce earlier in the month, the former Bournemouth boss has seen his squad come roaring back in some style. Heading into November’s fortnight interval with a 3-1 win against Crystal Palace, Liverpool have found a recent clinical edge in the final third. Likewise, winning seven of their last nine appearances across all competitions, the English icons will be looking to make a European statement on Wednesday night.

Missing out against Manchester United on Sunday afternoon due to a reported calf issue, Liverpool are once again sweating over the fitness of Dutch international Cody Gakpo. Yet to make a maiden appearance of the season and still sidelined with an Achilles tendon injury, former Eintracht Frankfurt marksman Hugo Ekitike will miss out in Belgium.

Enjoying what has become his most fruitful spell at Anfield and now registering seven direct goal contributions in his previous four appearances for club and country (five goals, two assists), Florian Wirtz could play a leading role. Notching a third Premier League strike of the season on Sunday, summer recruit Victor Munoz could be handed a full recall.

Storming their way to a stunning 4-0 victory against bitter domestic rivals Manchester United on Sunday, Liverpool will be dreaming of a repeat performance here. However, while Wednesday’s guests might have found a recent clinical edge in the final third, they will meet a record-breaking Club Brugge squad in midweek. Including a remarkable 4-3 win away at Italian icons Inter Milan last month, Leko’s side have won each of their previous three consecutive Champions League showdowns. In what could prove to be another frantic European affair on Wednesday night, chances could come thick and fast.
