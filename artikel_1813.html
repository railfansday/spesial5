Cardinale confirms Milan ‘will spend money’ in January and praises Endrick

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

As he left Milanello earlier this evening, Gerry Cardinale stopped to share a few words on the game against Frosinone, the January mercato, Markus Krösche and more.

Cardinale arrived in Milan a few days ago and had a summit with the management at Casa Milan yesterday. In other words, he’s not wasting any time, making every second of his trip to the city count.

Today, he was at Milanello to watch the team’s training session and Amorim’s press conference, and tomorrow he will be at the game against Frosinone.

The owner left the training ground shortly after 4 pm today, but before driving away, he rolled down the window and spoke to the journalists present. In addition to the game, he also spoke about the upcoming mercato and what Milan intend to do.

“I’m ready for tomorrow. We had a great couple of days, including a session at Casa Milan yesterday on the mercato, as well as the rest of our plans for the season going into the summer. I’d say that the mood is really upbeat here, it was a great session today and the team is really coming together.

“I spoke to a bunch of the players, they are all feeling really positive, and they understand as Ruben said how important tomorrow’s game is. Hopefully, we come out strong, get the win and keep going.”

“Yeah I’m ready. We are doing a lot of work. It’s complicated and the January transfer window is always tricky. So you have to be prepared, you have to be opportunistic. The one thing I can promise is that we won’t make any decision that we don’t feel great about.

“We will spend money, but we will spend money smart and with a view to being consistent with the project, in close coordination with Ruben and everyone here at Milanello.”

“With Markus Krösche. I know you guys have been very focused on Markus, but when we’re ready to make an announcement, I will come back to you guys on it.”

“Look, that name also keeps getting mentioned. This project is more than just one player, but he’s one of the best players, if not the best player, in his position. So tremendous amount of respect from me and we will see what’s possible.”

“Look, I love Adrien, he’s one of my favourite players. I think he’s just fantastic and I have a tremendous amount of respect for him and his family. We are in discussions and I hope that we will have a good result. But if it were up to me, he would be a franchise player.”

Clearly, there’s a lot happening right now and Milan are hoping that the addition of Krösche to the management will help them get even better control. It would certainly raise the enthusiasm if they were to have a good mercato and secure a renewal for Adrien Rabiot.
