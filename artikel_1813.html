USA boss Mauricio Pochettino keen on Manchester United job

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

According to talkSPORT, USA national team manager Mauricio Pochettino is very keen on taking over from Michael Carrick at Manchester United, should the Premier League giants decide to part ways with their former midfielder.

United are obviously in a heavy crisis. They’ve shown notable resilience in the last two games by coming back from two goals down to beat AS Roma in the Champions League and scoring twice in injury time to avoid defeat at home at the hands of Aston Villa, but there’s no hiding from the fact that after 10 rounds played, they sit inside the relegation zone with just eight points. Apart from all that, there have been off-pitch issues too, with senior players like captain Bruno Fernandes and defender Lisandro Martinez publicly questioning Carrick’s methods.

Carrick’s fate will reportedly be decided in a meeting senior club figures will shortly hold in Monaco, and it seems Pochettino will be anxiously waiting to find out the outcome.

Carrick took over as caretaker manager in January this year after the sacking of Ruben Amorim, and United’s improved form in the second half of last season and third-place finish earned him a chance as a permanent solution. Ironically, had United decided to part ways with him at the end of last season, they could’ve landed Pochettino as a free agent. As it is, they will have to pay a compensation of £7million if they choose to take him away from the USMNT, as he signed a new contract there after this summer’s World Cup.
