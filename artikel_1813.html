Mauricio Pochettino slams ‘unfair’ decision after Man Utd’s defeat to Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Mauricio Pochettino said Manchester United were on the wrong end of an ‘unfair’ decision during their heavy Premier League defeat to Liverpool.

Pochettino’s reign as United manager began in dismal fashion at Anfield as his side were thumped 4-0 by arch-rivals Liverpool.

The defining moment of the match came after just eight minutes as Leny Yoro was sent off for bringing down Alexander Isak as he raced through on goal.

Isak’s touch was heavy and United goalkeeper Senne Lammens quickly retrieved the ball but referee Samuel Barrott still deemed a red card to be the suitable punishment.

To make matters worse for Manchester United, Dominik Szoboszlai blasted home the subsequent free-kick to put the hosts ahead.

Liverpool scored again just five minutes later – Florian Wirtz volleying home a cross from the lively Rio Ngumoha.

England winger Ngumoha, 18, scored Liverpool’s third 20 minutes into the second half before Víctor Munoz added a fourth late on.

Pochettino described his first match as Manchester United manager as ‘painful and disappointing’ but criticised the early red card that paved the way for the heavy defeat.

‘So painful. So disappointed,’ Pochettino said in his post-match interview. ‘It’s not ok to start a game in the Premier League with this situation.

‘The game changed completely after six minutes. Now we can explain everything but the damage is done, it’s so painful.

‘The players are so, so feeling the pain of the result. I told them to the calm: it was an unfair situation that happened. We need to accept that reality.

‘We need to try to increase our focus, concentration and make better decisions on the field. We also need to be critical on ourselves but some situations happened in the game that we are not happy.’

Pochettino, who was hired as Michael Carrick’s replacement last week, added: ‘The ball was for the goalkeeper. In that situation, if you review the VAR you can see for sure, he never had the intention to connect with Isak.

‘When the control was long with the striker, the ball was going to the goalkeeper; he runs and goes down.

‘In Leny’s case, it wasn’t intentional. It was an unusual situation that is normal, everyone who plays football can feel it.

‘If you look before, on the sixth minute. Jacquet on Mbeumo. Similar? No. If you compare the actions, that was a penalty.

‘How that changes life, if you score after six minutes. Now, for sure, we are talking in a different way. We have to take responsibility; I take the responsibility. We keep pushing.

‘It’s not going to change what happened. Being respectful is the most important. At the same time, I have to defend my club. And tell what I feel.

‘That was an action that everyone who plays football, it was clear that a situation that was not a red card.’

Manchester United dropped into the relegation zone prior to the November international break, when Carrick was relieved of his duties.

Results over the weekend leave the Red Devils second from bottom in the Premier League with just eight points from their first 11 games of the campaign.

Liverpool, meanwhile, climb back into the Champions League places after an emphatic win over United.

Anfield boss Andoni Iraola said: ‘We have been very good away this season but at home we haven’t been good enough and still haven’t been good enough.

‘We needed a big game, and Manchester United is always a big game, to finish like this.

‘It’s true the game’s condition changes because they play with 10 most of the game, but still we’ve done our job, we controlled the game and I think it’s always nice when you can win like this in these kind of games.’

Asked for his take on the early red card, Iraola added: ‘I think he’s the last man, no? It’s a clear foul and a red card, yes?’

Manchester United and Liverpool both return to Champions League action this week, with the former travelling to Sporting and the latter visiting Club Brugge.
