Lulli: “I’m forever grateful to Gasperini.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Emanuele Lulli will start tomorrow’s Italy Under-21 match against Belgium.

On the eve of the match, the Roma winger spoke at a press conference, also discussing the faith he’s received from Gian Piero Gasperini: “I have to thank Gasperini for the faith he’s placed in me, and then obviously the credit goes to the team and my family.”

The Giallorossi player then expressed his satisfaction with his experience with Silvio Baldini’s national team: “I’m honored and grateful to be part of this group.”

The winger then highlighted the similarities between the Azzurrini’s style of play and that of Gasperini’s team: “There are many things in common with Roma, from the great counter-pressing to the man-to-man defensive play, aspects that reflect my characteristics.”
