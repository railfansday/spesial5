SWITZERLAND 4-0 SCOTLAND - PLAYER RATINGS: Did anyone emerge with credit? Which midfielder had a night to forget? Whose injury curse struck again?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Published: 17:27 EST, 16 November 2026 | Updated: 18:25 EST, 16 November 2026

A comprehensive defeat by Switzerland in St Gallen consigned Scotland to third place in their Nations League group and piled the pressure on manager Sebastien Pocognoli.

They trailed by Lewis Ferguson's own goal at the interval, but the roof caved in early in the second half when they conceded two more goals in the space of four minutes.

A late fourth by Zeki Amdouni completed a miserable night for Scotland and their captain, Andy Robertson, whose record-breaking 103rd appearance could hardly have gone any worse.

Paul Forsyth weighs up the individual performances on what was the toughest of nights for Scotland.
