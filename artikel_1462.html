Molina: “We have a tremendous desire to win.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Roma won convincingly against Sassuolo with a resounding 3-0 victory.

Gasperini’s men also defeated the Neroverdi thanks to goals from Hermoso and Mora and an own goal from Walukiewicz.

After the match, Nahuel Molina analyzed the game to Sky Sport.

“This team and this staff have a tremendous desire to win. We have a tremendous desire to win and work hard, I think we deserved today’s match to close out this cycle of close matches.”

“Already last season, we saw a strong team with many good players, and I found myself with a strong team. Off the pitch, there are people who want to win and with whom I get along well.”

Mario Hermoso: “This Roma always wants to win.”
