Roma and Milan to battle it out over Mastantuono

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The future of Franco Mastantuono could become one of the most interesting topics of the upcoming summer transfer window.

The Argentine talent, born in 2007 and owned by Real Madrid, is having a positive experience with Fiorentina, where he arrived on loan. His performances have attracted the attention of several Italian clubs, including Roma and AC Milan, who are interested in assessing his availability for next season.

According to today’s edition of La Repubblica, Fiorentina believes they have a good chance of retaining Mastantuono next season.

Sporting director Fabio Paratici is reportedly working to ensure continuity in the Argentine’s development, as he has quickly become an important member of the team.

His performances confirm his impact: he has already scored four goals in Italy, along with a central role in the team’s technical decisions. This performance strengthens the Tuscan club’s position vis-à-vis Los Blancos, who own his contract and are tasked with deciding on the player’s next destination.

The interest of Roma and Milan is a factor not to be underestimated in defining the future of the 2007-born player. Both clubs have reportedly already begun exploring the prospect ahead of next season, evaluating the possibility of joining the race for the Argentine talent.

His ability to make an impact in attack and his young age make him a particularly attractive prospect for clubs looking to invest in promising players. At the moment, however, no advanced negotiations have been reported, only expressions of interest and preliminary evaluations.
