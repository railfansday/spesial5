‘I’m A Celebrity’s AJ Odudu Gets Support From ‘BBUK’ Co-Host Will Best & More Stars Amid Microaggressions On ITV Reality Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

AJ Odudu is receiving support from celebrity friends amid microaggressions on ITV‘s I’m a Celebrity… Get Me Out of Here.

A clip from the competition series has gone viral on social media, which shows Odudu and Mariella Frostrup in a tense moment. In the exchange, Frostrup accused Odudu of being “aggressive” and creating “drama out of a crisis.” Odudu called Frostrup out and asked her not to “use those words towards me,” adding, “‘Dramatic’ and ‘aggressive’ are very harsh words.”

The words of encouragement for Odudu poured in with her Big Brother UK co-host chiming in the comments, writing, “[Odudu] love you SO MUCH. The kindest, most uplifting person I know BAR NONE. You have nothing to apologise for. But as always, you handled yourself with grace and patience and class. And everyone can see that. There’s a reason you’re literally my biggest inspiration. Best also dedicated a post on Instagram to celebrate Odudu and called her “the best of the best.” Watch on Deadline

“The crème de la creme. The GOAT. The kindest, funniest, most generous, uplifting friend anyone could ever ask for. And anyone who says otherwise either hasn’t met her, or is, sadly, an idiot,” Best shared. “Swipe for a some golden oldies and various pictures of me having to stand on a box next to her during photo shoots. It’s the heels guys!! I’m much taller than her irl!!!”

Jamella Jamil also replied and said, “What in the micro aggression?! AJ you are the epitome of class and patience.”

RuPaul’s Drag Race judge Michelle Visage said, “AJ IS THE SWEETES.”

Made In Chelsea’s Paris Smith, who has called out microaggressions from her fellow castmates on the E4 series, added, “Now why does this always happen on reality tv to black women? Literally aggressive where, so tired of the narrative.”

The X-Factor UK alum, Fleur East, said, “My girl!! @ajodudu I wish I could give her a squeeze. She handled herself so well there. Seeing her cry was really hard to watch. Keep your head up sis.”

Singer Paloma Faith also weighed in and said, “I’m so sad for AJ she is beautiful, intelligent and superior to Mariella in every single way….. it devastates me to watch her put through this BS.”

Supernanny’s Jo Frost also said, “I’m not okay watching this as an educator. I got to be honest here, you know how many black women are TIRED of being called aggressive by white folk when they are actually just simply making a point or being assertive. This is not okay for @ajodudu do you understand that it will make her think about everything she says just so people don’t assume she’s angry black woman! I’m not okay with how this went down. There was no listening on Mariella side and we can not use ADHD as the excuse. No a hug and someone determine you’re okay is not repair. Please don’t make me step inside the jungle to give a 101 on healthy repair !”

Following the public outcry from I’m a Celebrity… fans, ITV issued a statement, which read, ““The production monitors everyone in camp 24/7 and if at any time we feel someone has overstepped the line by using inappropriate language or displaying unacceptable behaviour, we would take steps to intervene and speak to everyone involved.

“The duty of care for our contestants is of the utmost importance to us. We take the welfare of the celebrities very seriously and have measures in place to ensure they are supported at every stage of the process: before, during and after filming.”
