AMC vs. Roblox: Which Entertainment Stock Is a Better Buy for 2027?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Roblox boasts a massive digital audience, while AMC's physical locations are seeing a resurgence.

Physical theaters meet digital worlds as entertainment habits shift. Investors are weighing traditional cinema against virtual ecosystems to decide whether AMC Entertainment (AMC -2.69%) or Roblox (RBLX -2.94%) is the better long-term buy.

AMC is the world's largest movie theater chain, relying on blockbuster releases to drive foot traffic. Roblox provides a user-generated platform where millions create and play in 3D immersive environments. Comparing them helps clarify whether a value play in physical media or a growth play in digital interaction fits your portfolio.

AMC Entertainment operates as the world's largest theatrical exhibition company with over 850 theaters and 9,500 screens. It primarily serves moviegoers through loyalty programs like AMC Stubs, which has about 40 million members. The company operates within the entertainment stocks sector and partners with major film studios such as Universal and Disney. It also utilizes technology from partners like IMAX (IMAX +0.83%) to enhance the viewing experience.

In the trailing 12 months ended Sept. 30, 2026, revenue reached $5.6 billion, representing growth of 15.9% year over year. Despite this top-line expansion, the company reported a net loss of $146.6 million for the period. This follows a period where the net margin improved from negative 13% to negative 2.6%, suggesting narrower losses as moviegoers returned to theaters in higher volumes.

As of its December 2025 balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio was negative 4.3x, which means that total liabilities exceed shareholder equity. The current ratio, which measures a company's ability to cover short-term debts with assets like cash, was 0.4x. In the fiscal year ended Dec. 31, 2025, free cash flow was negative $365.9 million. Free cash flow is the cash a business generates after paying for its essential operating activities and equipment upgrades.

Roblox operates a global user-generated platform where creators build 3D immersive experiences for millions of daily active users. The company reported reaching 138 million daily active users in the third quarter of 2026, and while that is a large audience, the figure represents a 9% year-over-year drop.

Roblox earns revenue through the sale of virtual currency and facilitates payments via partners such as Amazon and Apple. Roblox also maintains a joint venture in China and collaborates with brand partners for virtual events.

During the trailing 12 months ended Sept. 30, 2026, revenue reached $5.9 billion, a growth rate of 32.1% over the prior year. However, the company reported a significant net loss of $972.0 million for that same timeframe. While its top line is expanding quickly, the net margin remains deep in negative territory at negative 16.5%.

As of its December 2025 balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio was 4.6x. The current ratio stood at 1.0x. In the fiscal year ended Dec. 31, 2025, free cash flow was $1.4 billion. Note that stock-based compensation (SBC) represented 62.8% of operating cash flow, which inflates reported cash generation since SBC is a non-cash expense added back in the cash flow statement.

AMC Entertainment faces substantial challenges regarding its significant debt and liquidity needs. The company requires constant capital to meet its obligations, and any failure to secure funds could lead to restructuring. Its success depends on studios like Universal and Disney providing high-quality content, as shifts toward streaming platforms threaten theatrical windows. Furthermore, the stock experiences extreme volatility driven by retail sentiment and frequent share dilution.

Roblox deals with complex legal and regulatory proceedings, including class action lawsuits regarding child safety and platform features. The company depends on maintaining a safe environment for minors, as any failure in moderation can cause severe reputational damage. Roblox also relies heavily on third-party ecosystems like Apple for distribution. Unfavorable changes to app store policies or fee structures could significantly harm its future profitability.

AMC appears significantly cheaper on a P/S ratio basis, which measures price against sales over the past twelve months. Neither have a Forward P/E, which tracks the stock price against future earnings estimates, as they are not expected to be profitable businesses any time soon.

For investors seeking exposure to the world of entertainment, AMC's physical locations contrast sharply against Roblox's digital gaming environment. Both possess different investment pros and cons, so your choice may be based on the factors that matter most to you. Personally, I would buy Roblox stock over AMC.

Roblox's daily active user base dropped in Q3 of this year, a sign of a waning audience. Even so, the company delivered Q3 sales growth of 16% year over year to $1.6 billion. Roblox is shifting toward an older audience demographic, which accounts for some of the active user decline, as adults have less leisure time than kids. That's a positive, since adults have income to spend on the platform.

Moreover, Roblox is offering users artificial intelligence tools to accelerate video game development, making a wider selection of games available more quickly. This combines with its new Roblox Everywhere strategy, which pushes games out to external platforms beyond its walled garden, expanding its reach.

AMC is dependent on studios for content to bring customers in. After a long lull due to the COVID-19 pandemic, consumers are returning to theaters, as evidenced by AMC's record quarterly revenue of $1.7 billion in Q3. However, running physical locations is expensive, and its negative debt-to-equity ratio is a red flag of a distressed balance sheet, making Roblox the safer investment for 2027.
