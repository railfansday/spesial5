Report: €70m Liverpool deal hinges on one major decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Liverpool have already been linked with a lot of players ahead of the January transfer window.

The Anfield outfit are fourth in the Premier League which, after all the money they've spent, is a massive underperformance.

One of the best ways for the Reds to combat this is to sign new players to plug the gaps their current squad are leaving open.

Obviously, some positions are more of a problem than others with Liverpool likely to have a priority position that they want to strengthen.

But, if they manage to cover all the potential positions they need to, they'll have a massive selection of players to choose from which would obviously be beneficial for them.

Since the Reds aren't a club who have an endless amount of money to spend, their transfers need to mean something which is why their next big deal is hinging on a major decision from the Anfield outfit.

Liverpool are unlikely to sign Lamine Camara in January

Lamine Camara was one of the main players linked with a Liverpool transfer in the summer.

It was clear to almost everyone that a new midfielder was one of the main things that Iraola needed to take the Reds to the next level.

Obviously, replacing Mohamed Salah was also one of the main things that they needed to do but, with how much players such as Dominik Szoboszlai and Ryan Gravenberch have struggled to adapt to Iraola's system, the Reds have managed to dig themselves into a bit of a hole.

Because, even though they were desperate for a new midfielder in the summer, by January, it'll be more than clear to every team in the world that a new midfielder is required by the Reds.

In the summer, Camara was available for a cut-rate deal and now, he's worth around €70 million.

The fact of Szoboszlai and Gravenberch also having prominent roles among their ranks also needs to be taken into consideration as, if another midfielder is brought in, the minutes played by each player will diminish.

And, according to David Lynch via Anfield Index, this could be the thing to stop them from signing the Senegal international.

"I know the reports, but still struggle to see Lamine Camara to Liverpool in January without a significant departure," he said.

"Endo doesn’t play at all, more a Gravenberch or Szoboszlai which it’s hard to see, otherwise there’s a question of minutes. Liverpool don’t like to be over stocked as we know."

Lamine Camara would be a perfect transfer for Liverpool

Before the summer, Camara was probably a player that most Liverpool fans won't have heard of.

The midfielder was quietly making a name for himself in Ligue 1 and then, when Filipe Luis took over as their manager, he instantly became one of the first names on their team sheet.

From that point on, clubs from across the world were interested in the midfielder and have remained so ever since.

But, of all the teams he's been linked with, Liverpool are the team he'd be perfect for.

Not only would he perfectly fit into Iraola's plans but, he'd also be able to come in for one of his starting midfielders.

Indeed, the €70 million fee is a bit of a risk but, in reality, that's the going rate for a decent central midfielder these days.

Here's hoping that Liverpool come to their senses and get a deal over the line.
