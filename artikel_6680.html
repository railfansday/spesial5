Meet the Cast and Characters of ‘<i><em>The Hunting Wives’</em></i> Season 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

This show gives a new meaning to Blue Texas.

Heated Rivalry caused such a kerfuffle at the end of 2025 that we all collectively as a society forgot that The Hunting Wives got us all hot and bothered with sexy, covert, queer relationships earlier that summer. The Netflix series is now back for season 2 for more deliciously toxic drama. It’s also back with some new characters. Here’s what you need to know about who plays who in The Hunting Wives season 2.

Spoilers for season 1, but unless Katie Lowes and Chrissy Metz’s characters decide to haunt the Hunting Wives in season 2, they won’t be returning. And before we get into the major players, additional new cast members on The Hunting Wives season 2 include Cam Gigandet as Gentle John Moffitt, WNBA player Angel Reese as “Trainer Barbie,” and Casey Mills as Nico.

In the first season, Sophie was a fish out of water after moving from Massachusetts to Texas for her husband’s job. Now, she’s acclimated to Southern living; but her old secrets have been replaced with new secrets.

Brittany is a former child/teen actress who followed three years on Guiding Light with a main role on American Dreams. She then starred in John Tucker Must Die and played Amber Von Tussle in the movie adaptation of the musical Hairspray. Not long after came Pitch Perfect and its two sequels. You’ve also seen her in X, Someone Great, Crazy Ex-Girlfriend, and The Night Agent.

Margo leads the Hunting Wives, a social group for gun and cocktail-loving women in Texas. She takes Sophie under her wing and seduces her in more ways than one, to the chagrin of some of the other women in the group who feel replaced.

Many would say that Malin broke out in the pre-DCU superhero film Watchmen. But they haven’t seen 27 Dresses, The Proposal, or The Comeback. You may also know her from Dollface, Billions, that one episode of How I Met Your Mother, Trophy Wife, Children’s Hospital, Rock of Ages, and two episodes of Entourage.

Evan played the Marvel comics character Toad in X-Men: Days of Future Past. His other film and television credits include Easy, Bone Tomahawk, Frontier, and Whiskey Tango Foxtrot. If you like The Bear and Girls, you need to watch Jonigkeit as Will in the Starz series Sweetbitter.

Dermot’s romantic comedy fame precedes him. You’ve seen My Best Friend’s Wedding, The Wedding Date, The Family Stone, and Must Love Dogs. On television, he had a short arc on Friends and longer recurring roles on shows like New Girl and Enlightened. He was a regular on Shameless and plays the main character Dom in the Chicago Fire/Med/PD world.

Lincoln is also a new character this season.

Alex Fitzalan has, on two separate occasions, been part of a teenage survivalist ensemble series: The Society on Netflix and The Wild on Prime Video. His film credits include 2018’s Slender Man and 2022’s Chevalier. Earlier this year he also turned up on The F Ward and the Netflix adaptation of My Brilliant Career.

Post-Tanner family, Stamos also starred in ER, Jake in Progress, Grandfathered, Scream Queens, and Big Shot. He has also starred in four musicals on Broadway, and frequently performs at the Hollywood Bowl.
