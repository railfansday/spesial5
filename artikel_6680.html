I Tried Chicken Soup From 3 Popular Chains &amp; This One Tastes Homemade

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Exactly what I want to warm up with on a cold night.

As we enter November, we are getting closer to soup season. As a certified soup lover, I argue that soup season is really year-round, perfect for any occasion. Still, there is something to be said for warming up on a cold winter’s night with a nice, steaming bowl of soup.

With endless kinds of soup to choose from, from creamy and indulgent to brothy and vibrant, none is more classic than a comforting bite of chicken soup. Whether you’re looking to warm up, feeling under the weather, or simply craving a spoonful, nothing’s quite as convenient as ordering a bowl from your local chain restaurant—especially when you don’t have time to make one yourself.

I tried the chicken soup at three of my go-to restaurant chains to determine, once and for all, which one is worth ordering next time I come down with a cold.

While I am generally a Panera stan, the chain’s Homestyle Chicken Noodle Soup was sadly lackluster. Made with chicken bone broth, white meat chicken, curly egg noodles, sliced carrots, celery, and herbs, I expected a bit more from Panera Bread. The broth flavor was mostly pleasant, but it was more watery than I’d like. Plus, the flavor was a bit off, like it had been sitting in a warming pot too long. I did, however, enjoy the big chunks of veggies and thick noodles.

Now we’re getting somewhere! While I found the chicken quite dry, LPQ’s Chicken Soup still tasted good. The menu says the soup contains quinoa, veggies, and spicy harissa—and while I enjoyed it, what I received didn't match the description. I found carrots, celery, and some leafy greens in the mix, but instead of quinoa, the soup had beans—nothing I’m exactly mad about. Plus, there wasn’t even a hint of spice. Again, it’s not a deterrent for me, but if you want what’s on the menu, you’ll have to look elsewhere.

Pret’s Turmeric Chicken and Farro Soup was the clear winner. The soup had a nice spice that really took it to the next level. Plus, it had hearty, big chunks of chicken, plus diced carrots, celery, farro, and herbs. As a sucker for a noodle soup, I didn’t even notice I wasn’t slurping down any noods. This felt like the only soup that could truly be mistaken for homemade, and I’d definitely order it again. The turmeric also gave it a warm, earthy flavor that made the whole bowl even more satisfying.
