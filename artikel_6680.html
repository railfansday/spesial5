French Onion Croissant Strata

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

A breakfast strata is one of our go-tos when we want a hearty, shareable brunch dish, but it’s hard to put into words how good this French onion croissant strata is. It takes a few extra minutes to deeply caramelize the onions, but the extra flavor and richness make a HUGE difference.

Making it French Onion:Caramelizing the onions over medium heat helps them brown evenly and consistently. If the onions begin to stick or burn, add a splash of water to bring them back! Garlic, thyme, and black pepper are the aromatic unsung heroes in the onions, so you don’t want to skip them. Deglazing with white wine is essential in any French onion recipe, so use the one you’d sip! Once the wine has reduced, add some beef stock to impart meaty French onion soup flavor. The caramelized onions then get layered with torn croissants and Gruyère cheese, and topped with dollops of cream cheese.

Delish Official:Eggland's Best Eggs are the Official Eggs of Delish. That means they're the only eggs we're using in our recipe content.

Storage:The beautiful thing about a breakfast strata is that you can make it ahead. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 day and reheat until the internal temperature reaches 165°. This is also great to portion and store for breakfast at the office.
