Girls’ Frontline: The Lunasia Covenant launches in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Girls’ Frontline: The Lunasia Covenant, the May-announced player-versus-environment game that blends third-person shooter action with cooperative combat, will launch for iOS and Android in 2027, publisher Sunborn Network Technology and in-house developer Hecate Team announced.

Here is a brief overview of the game, via Sunborn Network Technology:

Time marches ever onward. The world is transformed anew.

A world-spanning behemoth.A realm born from the void.A power forged by legend. And beyond it all, the blue butterfly watches.

In the liminal space between [Reality], [Virtuality], and […], new seeds quietly take root.

Let bullets write the next chapter.Let courage forge our edge.一As we always have.

Girls’ Frontline: The Lunasia Covenant is Sunborn Network’s upcoming player-versus-environment game, blending third-person shooter action with cooperative combat.
