Herby Sheet-Pan Meatballs With Feta

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

These aren’t your slow-braised, leisurely Sunday-sauce meatballs. They’re let’s-get-this-crew-fed-at-the-end-of-a-busy-workday meatballs. The large amount of breadcrumbs isn’t just a filler; combined with the water, they make the meatballs impossibly tender and springy. You can use any ground meat you’d like; pork, lamb, turkey, or chicken (only dark meat for the poultry, though) all work well. Roasting the meatballs in the oven instead of frying them on the stovetop reduces the active cook time and makes cleanup simple.

Place a rack in bottom third of oven; preheat to 425°. Using a sturdy wooden spoon, vigorously mix 2 large eggs, zest of 1 lemon, 2 garlic cloves, finely grated, 1 cup panko, ½ cup finely chopped dill and/or parsley, 2 tsp. Diamond Crystal or 1¼ tsp. Morton kosher salt, 2 tsp. dried oregano, 1 tsp. freshly ground pepper, 1 tsp. ground cumin, and ½ cup water in a large bowl until nearly a smooth paste. Add 1 lb. ground beef and mix vigorously until well combined and mixture is slightly tacky. Oil your hands; divide mixture into 16 portions and roll into balls. Arrange on a large rimmed baking sheet.

Toss 1 large red onion, cut through root end into 2"-thick wedges, 1 Tbsp. extra-virgin olive oil, a large pinch of salt, and a large pinch of pepper in a small bowl. Arrange in a single layer around meatballs. Bake meatballs and onions until meatballs are deeply browned underneath and an instant-read thermometer inserted into centers registers 160°, 25–30 minutes. Let cool slightly.

To serve, generously top with sliced cucumbers, crumbled feta, and more dill and/or parsley if desired. Serve with country-style bread and lemon wedges for squeezing over.
