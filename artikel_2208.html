Hearts learn match officials for FC Thun and Falkirk double-header

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Officials have been confirmed for Heart of Midlothian's upcoming clashes with FC Thun and Falkirk.

Hearts travel to Switzerland to face Thun at the Visana Stadion in the Conference League before returning to Premiership action against Falkirk at Tynecastle on Sunday.

Cypriot referee Kyriakos Athanasiou will take charge of the European encounter. He will be assisted by Marios Kalogirou and Kyriakos Sokratous, with Chrysovalantis Theouli appointed as the fourth official. Marios Christoforou will be on VAR duty, assisted by Dimitris Solomou.

Thursday's game is set to be Athanasiou’s first appointment involving Hearts. His previous European record also includes Conference League matches.

Hearts will then turn their attention back to domestic action when Falkirk visit Tynecastle on Sunday, November 8, for a 3pm kick-off.

Matthew MacDermid has been appointed as referee, with Chris Rae and Colin Drummond running the lines. Sean Murdoch will be the fourth official, while Steven McLean is on VAR duty and Gary Hilland will assist him.

MacDermid has previous experience of Hearts matches. He took charge of their 2-2 Premiership draw away to St Mirren in October 2025 before refereeing their 3-1 home victory over Motherwell in April.
