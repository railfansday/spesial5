James Morrison issues firm statement over West Brom star’s Premier League potential

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

West Brom missed the opportunity to move into the Championship’s top two after being held to a 1-1 draw by Southampton on Tuesday night. James Morrison’s side now turn their attention to this weekend’s clash with Millwall, with the Baggies preparing to host the Lions in a bid to strengthen their promotion push.

Ahead of this weekend’s clash, Morrison has been asked about his midfielder Ousmane Diakite, who has been in excellent form this season. The WBA boss was questioned on Diakite’s potential, specifically whether he is a Premier League player in the making, as reported by Steve Tudgay.

Morrison issued a confident response, saying, “Yes he is. With us!” His comments back up those of former WBA man Paul Robinson, who was full of praise for Diakite when speaking to the Express and Star.

“He has Premier League attributes, when you look at him,” the former defender said. “It’s about consistency. We have seen Diakite in the Blackburn game, then the Preston game and now he has gone to that next level against West Ham.

“He stood out against players who are expected to win the league. He destroyed them in the middle of the park.”

Diakite has made an encouraging start to the 2026/27 Championship season, establishing himself as an important figure in the WBA midfield. The 26-year-old has featured in 14 league games, making 11 starts and averaging a FotMob rating of 7.22.

His biggest strengths have been his defensive contributions, recording 25 tackles, 19 interceptions and 58 recoveries. He has also won 81.8% of his aerial duels, placing him in the 95th percentile among midfielders.

Diakite has shown composure in possession too, completing 88.5% of his 544 passes, including 86 line-breaking efforts. His recent performances have been particularly impressive, earning ratings of 7.8 against Southampton and Blackburn and 7.7 against Preston.

Although he is yet to register a goal or assist, his physicality, defensive reliability and ability to retain possession have made him a valuable asset for West Brom.

Morrison is building an exciting, well-balanced midfield at West Brom, combining defensive strength, energy and attacking quality. Diakite and Jayson Molumby provide a solid foundation, while summer signing Felix Horn Myhre adds creativity and goals.

Isaac Price has also emerged as a key attacking threat, already contributing five goals this season. There is promising young talent in Rabby Nzingoula, Toby Collyer and Harry Whitwell, too, giving Morrison options for the future.

The group has helped West Brom make a strong start to the campaign, with its blend of experience and potential offering encouragement for a sustained promotion push.
