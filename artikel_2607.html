Rashod Bateman limited Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Baltimore Ravens WR Rashod Bateman (groin) was a limited participant in practice Wednesday, Nov. 25.

Why it Matters: Bateman has been mainly used to stretch the field this season, and he has finished with two receptions or less in eight of his 11 games. At best, he is a touchdown-dependent WR5.
