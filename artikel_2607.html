Patriots’ Mike Vrabel’s defiant answer to question about A.J. Brown injury warning that forced Titans’ trade to Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Patriots' Mike Vrabel's defiant answer to question about A.J. Brown injury warning that forced Titans' trade to Eagles appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

A.J. Brown's move to the New England Patriots has not gone as smoothly as planned, with injuries limiting his time on the field. His latest hamstring injury will reportedly keep him out for several weeks, and a new rumor has surfaced involving Brown that Mike Vrabel allegedly overlooked.

However, Vrabel denied the claim, saying there is no truth to reports that the Tennessee Titans doctors warned the team in 2022 that Brown only had about two years left in his knees. In a statement made by SiriusXM host Jared Stillman on November 17, he said, "Amy Adams Strunk 'did not regret' trading AJ Brown because the Titans medical staff had informed her and team leadership, including Mike Vrabel, that AJ Brown's knees had 'two years left' in 2022."

"No, the owner didn't want to pay A.J. Brown 25 million dollars," Vrabel said. "That's what it was. He went to Philly, and I mean, A.J. has participated in quite a handful of games; and has missed a few since he went to Philly."

The Philadelphia Eagles gave Brown an extension averaging $25 million per year after sending the 18th and 101st picks in the 2022 draft to Tennessee, and Vrabel stated that he saw no red flags following the WR's training camp.

"And then injuries are part of the game. He gets rolled up on [in the season opener]. And then gets dragged down the other day, and that was unfortunate. So we'll just keep trying to get him healthy and whenever he can get back out there to help us, I know that he will," Vrabel added.

The Patriots acquired Brown from the Eagles on June 1 after much speculation, using a 2028 first-round draft choice and a 2027 fifth-round draft choice.

Brown suffered a high-ankle sprain in his Patriots' debut and missed the next four games. But he made a comeback in the Patriots' 26-19 win against the New York Jets on October 18, where he had three receptions for 27 yards.

Brown also later left after suffering a hamstring injury in a 35-10 loss against the Detroit Lions this month.
