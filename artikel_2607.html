MiLaysia Fulwiley plays for LSU after felony charges dropped following arrest

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Nine days after she was arrested, MiLaysia Fulwiley returned to the court for LSU's women's basketball team, scoring 14 points in a lopsided 103-45 win for Kim Mulkey's Tigers over Belmont on Monday, Nov. 23.

Fulwiley – a two-time SEC Sixth Player of the Year and three-time All-SEC Tournament selection – was arrested on Nov. 14 after the LSU football game and faced multiple charges. She was released after posting a $20,000 bond, but did not play for LSU in its two wins last week.

On Friday, Nov. 20, the two felony charges against Fulwiley — aggravated battery with a deadly weapon and felony aggravated assault with a deadly weapon — were dismissed. The LSU star still faces three misdemeanor charges of flight from an officer, resisting an officer and disobedience to an officer, but those were referred to diversion, meaning that a program with classes or Fulwiley completing community services could lead to those charges being dropped as well. She is due to appear in court on March 22, 2027.

"I owe an apology to the officers involved and everyone who calls themselves a Tiger. I also want to apologize to my family and all those who love and support me," Fulwiley said in a statement provided to USA TODAY Sports. "People see the purple and gold before they see me, and I want to carry myself in a way that's worthy of it, on the court and off. Rules and laws apply to everyone, including the instructions of officers working a game night in Baton Rouge."

Fulwiley entered the game to many LSU fans standing, clapping and cheering at the 4:37 mark in the first quarter on Monday. About 40 seconds later, she swished a 3-pointer from the right wing after receiving a pass from Grace Knox.

The senior guard shot 6-of-13 from the floor and also collected five steals, three blocks, three assists and a rebound in 17 minutes of play off the bench.

LSU is 7-0 this season. The Tigers will face Villanova on Friday, Nov. 27 in Fort Myers, Florida.
