State of the NFC South: Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Buccaneers had a tough matchup this week against the Detroit Lions, and Baker Mayfield was coming off an injury, but they really stood strong. They only lost by 10 in the end, but also, the score may have been a bit of a lie, and the Lions should have probably won by more.

Detroit's defense got to Mayfield throughout the game, and their run defense was really good against Bucky Irving. Then on defense, Jared Goff had a consistent day, but the Bucs were able to keep Jahmyr Gibbs under 100 yards.

Their next game is against the division first-place team, the Panthers, and if the Buccaneers want any chance of fighting for first place, they must win.

At the top of the division, it looks like it's another year that the Carolina Panthers will win. Last week's win against the Saints was monumental in the race for first place, and this week, after beating the Ravens 35-33, they grew their lead in the division even more. Chuba Hubbard's rushing ability really took over against the Ravens' run defense, and it helped the Panthers' offense flow.

The defense continues to be an issue that will consistently expose this team, but the offense can always hang in these games. This week will be another massive game for Carolina, with Tampa Bay hosting the Panthers.

This week, the Falcons were on their bye week, and their next opponent is the Minnesota Vikings. Probably one of the worst teams to go up against when you are trying to turn around your season, which is exactly what is happening. They will have to deal with Brian Flores' incredible defense and the return of Kirk Cousins in Minnesota, which will also be a major revenge game for the veteran.

At the bottom of the division, we have the Saints playing some bad football. Brandon Staley is a great defensive coordinator and went toe-to-toe against Ben Johnson, but the offense and special teams rarely do enough. It's a shame that the Saints are wasting some great defensive performances.

It's not going to get easier this week. The Saints have to play Joe Burrow, who is playing at an MVP level, and the Bengals.
