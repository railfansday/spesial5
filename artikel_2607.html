Making sense of Fresno State’s bowl possibilities

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Following a 37-35 loss to the Texas State Bobcats over the weekend, the Fresno State Bulldogs sit at 5-5 overall and 2-4 in the Pac-12 with two games to go, needing to win one of the final two to even become bowl eligible in what has turned into a disappointing season.

However, with the aforementioned sixth win, the Bulldogs can end on some positive notes. A bowl game would be somewhat positive in that for the first two seasons under Matt Entz the Bulldogs would've made a bowl game.

It would appear as though Boise State will represent the Group of Six for the College Football Playoffs, following New Mexico's loss to UNLV on Saturday night, meaning that the position in the Poinsettia Bowl would open up.

Right now, this appears to be the likely landing spot if the Bulldogs can become bowl eligible. Although another win by Texas State could make it very interesting for Fresno State when coming to bowl selection.

ESPN Events runs another set of four bowls that could take potentially bowl eligible Pac-12 teams. The difficulty is that there are only two current bowl eligible teams from the conference with Boise State and Texas State being the only two. Fresno State, Oregon State and San Diego State sit one win away. Utah State is the only other school that can make six wins as they sit at 4-6 this year.

Oregon State and Utah State play each other this upcoming weekend, meaning that if Utah State loses, then they are eliminated from bowl contention. San Diego State plays at Boise State and then ends the year with a likely matchup with Fresno State. And lastly, the Bulldogs take on a lowly Colorado State team this weekend needing that all important last win.

A nightmare scenario is if Fresno State is stunned by Colorado State, much in the same way they were last season, and then lose to San Diego State on Thanksgiving weekend.

Another nightmare scenario is if Boise State drops one of their two contests and pulls another Group of Six team back into the fold for the CFP. If Boise State somehow drops out of the CFP, then the trickle-down effect would likely land Fresno State even lower on the pecking order to the First Responder Bowl or the Gasparilla Bowl. Although it would seem more beneficial for everyone involved if Fresno State stayed closer to home like last year when they were in the Arizona Bowl. If that is the case, not only is the Poinsettia Bowl likely, but also perhaps the Famous Idaho Potato Bowl.

The Hawaii Bowl and the Armed Forces Bowl also appear to be likely landing spots for Fresno State. Depending on how the last two weeks go for not only the Bulldogs, but for all of the Pac-12, will likely decide on the December destination. First things first is to control what you can control and that is to beat Colorado State to grab that sixth win.
