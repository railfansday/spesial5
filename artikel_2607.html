RB Leipzig set asking price for Nusa as Arsenal, Liverpool, Tottenham, Newcastle and others circle

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Antonio Nusa has attracted plenty of attention following an impressive start to the season with RB Leipzig, racking up six goals and seven assists in 14 appearances across all competitions.

The list of clubs interested in the Norwegian winger continues to grow, with Bild naming Bayern Munich, Arsenal, Liverpool, Tottenham Hotspur, Newcastle United, Aston Villa and Crystal Palace as potential destinations.

Leipzig are open to selling Nusa, but only if both the timing and transfer fee meet the club's expectations.

Recent reports from England have suggested that the 21-year-old could be available for around €54 million in the January transfer window.

However, Bild reports that a summer move is far more realistic, with Leipzig reluctant to let him leave midway through the season and demanding as much as €80 million.

Nusa is under contract with the Bundesliga club until 2029, putting Leipzig in a strong negotiating position.
