Pacers get more bad news on the injury report for T.J. McConnell, Pascal Siakam

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

NEW YORK -- Pacers guard T.J. McConnell has been ruled out for Sunday's game against the Knicks at Madison Square Garden with left hamstring soreness and All-Star forward Pascal Siakam is listed as questionable with left hip soreness/tightness.

All-Star point guard Tyrese Haliburton is still out with a left hamstring strain. He hasn't played since Oct. 26.

McConnell was pulled from Friday's game against the Cavaliers in Cleveland when he grabbed at his hamstring. Pacers coach Rick Carlisle said then it was a hamstring issue but the Pacers weren't sure of the severity. McConnell missed the first nine games with a right hamstring strain and his minutes were being slowly ramped up in his recovery. McConnell is averaging 7.0 points and 4.3 assists per game despite averaging just 13.8 minutes per game. He's shooting 52.2% from the floor. Prior to Friday's game he was averaging 19.7 point and 11.6 assists per 36 minutes.

Siakam had not been listed on the injury report prior to Saturday. The four-time All-Star has carried the Pacers with Haliburton out, averaging 23.0 points per game on 51.6% shooting to go with 6.1 rebounds and 3.8 assists per game. He has missed just one game this season for rest.

With McConnell out, the Pacers are down to Andrew Nembhard, Quenton Jackson and Braden Smith as point guard options. Missing Siakam would cost the Pacers their leading scorer and dig into their forward depth and likely push Obi Toppin into the starting lineup.
