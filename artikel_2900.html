Why Micron Stock Dove Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A Wall Street analyst is growing increasingly bearish regarding the chipmaker and the broader memory sector.

By Danny Vena, CPA – Nov 24, 2026 at 11:27AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Micron Technology ›

Shares of Micron (MU -11.72%) stumbled out of the gate Tuesday, falling as much as 12%. As of 11:17 a.m. ET, the stock was still down 11.7%.

The catalyst that sent the semiconductor specialist lower was a downgrade and bearish commentary from a Wall Street analyst.

Investors have been increasingly cautious about the future of demand for artificial intelligence (AI) chips -- and memory chips in particular. One Wall Street analyst gave those concerns a voice.

BNP Paribas analyst Karl Ackerman downgraded Micron to neutral (hold) from outperform (buy), while lowering his price target to $775, down from $1,700. For those keeping score at home, that represents potential upside of less than 10% compared to Monday's closing price.

While the analyst acknowledged the stock's recent decline, Ackerman remains concerned that hyperscalers orders are getting ahead of themselves. Memory chipmakers are scrambling to meet accelerating demand, which the analyst believes will lead to an oversupply that will ultimately drive prices down by mid-2027.

"We think DRAM and NAND supply-demand can approach parity starting in mid-2027 and result in ASP [average selling price] declines, earlier than our prior view of mid-2028," Ackerman said.

This echoes ongoing investor concerns about the durability of AI demand and historical cyclicality. These concerns have driven Micron down roughly 48% from its recent peak (as of this writing), and the stock now sells for just over 8 times earnings. If you believe, as I do, that AI adoption will continue for years -- if not decades -- Micron stock is certainly worth a look.
