Warriors rookie Yaxel Lendeborg reveals two-word mantra key to his NBA journey

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Warriors rookie Yaxel Lendeborg reveals two-word mantra key to his NBA journey originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

Yaxel Lendeborg's NBA journey has been anything but ordinary for the Warriors rookie.

From a hidden gem playing at junior college to a college star for Michigan, and now, an NBA Rookie of the Year candidate as Golden State's No. 11 overall draft pick, Lendeborg suddenly finds himself as the future face of the franchise, as declared by Warriors teammate Draymond Green.

"No, I do not," Lendeborg told NBA on Prime's Taylor Rooks in an interview that aired before the Warriors' game against the San Antonio Spurs on Friday night at Frost Bank Center. "When I first got drafted, I just expected to be they guy that just sits in the corner, waits for his shot, maybe averages, like, seven points a game.

"Now, [I've] somewhat become the second option on offense, and that's definitely something I never thought would happen in the NBA."

Lendeborg currently is the Warriors' No. 2 scorer behind a now-injured Steph Curry, averaging 21.7 points on 51.8-percent shooting with 7.4 rebounds and 3.7 assists per game. He currently sits atop the NBA's Kia Rookie Ladder after establishing himself as a key piece to a franchise looking to make the most of Curry's remaining years.

For a guy that didn't even start playing basketball until his senior year of high school, the early start to his NBA career is unprecedented. But a simple mantra has helped Lendeborg, who "70 percent" believes he can be great, make sense of it all.

"I try to continue to let my mind know and just try to ease it a little bit by saying I belong here," Lendeborg told Rooks. "I always use "you belong" because for a long time, I felt like I didn't belong in the basketball world, especially now in the NBA."

Lendeborg still has a long way to go before he establishes himself as an NBA great, but the early returns are promising. And with Curry sidelined by an oblique injury for at least two weeks, the Warriors will need the greatest version of their rookie they can get.
