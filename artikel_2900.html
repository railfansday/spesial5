Ohio State offers 2028 receiver and makes the top-5 for a 2028 running back

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

With the Ohio high school football season nearing its conclusion, Ohio State's coaching staff has had plenty of opportunities to evaluate some of the top in-state talent throughout the fall. The Buckeyes have already made their presence felt with several prospects in the 2028 class, but there's always room for new names to emerge. Thursday was another example of that, as Ohio State extended its latest offer to Cincinnati Oak Hills receiver Cordell Ball.

A 6-foot-1, 190-pound athlete, Ball has put together an impressive junior campaign, and the attention he's receiving on the recruiting trail certainly reflects that. Programs such as Cincinnati, Illinois, Penn State, Tennessee, and Texas A&M are among the schools already involved, but adding an Ohio State offer to the mix is a significant development for the in-state standout. Taking to social media to share the news, Ball is the latest player to catch the eye of Cortez Hankton and the Buckeyes, who clearly have seen enough to believe he could be a valuable addition to their future receiver room.

Currently the No. 590 player nationally and the No. 80 receiver according to the 247Sports Composite rankings, Ball is a prospect whose stock could continue to rise as more attention is paid to his junior film. With Cincinnati being a relatively short trip from Columbus, getting him on campus should be a priority moving forward, and it wouldn't be a surprise to see him make his way to Ohio State for next weekend's regular-season finale.

The Buckeyes have made recruiting Ohio a major point of emphasis, and Ball is another name worth keeping an eye on as the 2028 cycle continues to take shape. With his recruitment gaining momentum and several major programs already involved, Ohio State getting its foot in the door now is important. There's plenty of time for this one to play out, but the Buckeyes have made it clear they want to be a factor in keeping another talented in-state prospect home.

It wasn't all that long ago that running back recruiting was becoming a bit of a concern for Ohio State, with the Buckeyes missing on some of their top targets and looking for answers at the position. Fortunately, the last couple of weeks have been much more encouraging for Carlos Locklyn and company, as Ohio State has added a pair of four-star backs to help solidify its 2027 class. With some positive momentum now on their side, the Buckeyes are also continuing to look ahead to 2028, where they already have Elijah Newman-Hall committed but aren't done pursuing other top talents.

On Thursday, Ohio State received some more positive news on that front, as four-star running back Zion Coger announced his top five schools and included the Buckeyes among his finalists. A 6-foot, 190-pound prospect out of Jasper, Alabama, Coger is currently ranked as the No. 121 player nationally and the No. 9 running back in the 2028 class according to the 247Sports Composite. With nearly 20 offers to his name, narrowing things down to five programs is a pretty significant step in his recruitment, and Ohio State is right where it wants to be as things start to get more serious.

Joining the Buckeyes in Coger's final group are Alabama, Indiana, Tennessee, and Virginia Tech, giving him several strong options to consider. Of course, with Coger being an Alabama native, it's hard not to view the Crimson Tide as a major player in this recruitment, but Ohio State has shown time and again that it's willing to go into SEC territory to pursue the players it wants.

There's still a long way to go before the 2028 class is finalized, and with Newman-Hall already in the fold, the Buckeyes aren't exactly desperate for an answer at running back. Still, being included among the finalists for one of the nation's top backs is another encouraging sign for Locklyn and the work he's doing on the recruiting trail. Ohio State has put itself in a good position, and now the goal will be to continue building that relationship and see if it can eventually bring another highly rated playmaker to Columbus.
