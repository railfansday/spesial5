France or Spain, the surprising answer from Luca Zidane

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Luca Zidane will be shared ahead of the quarter-final of the Nations League between France and Spain… While the two selections will face each other next March in the quarter-finals of the Nations League, the Algerian international was questioned about this poster during an interview with RNE Audio. And Zinedine Zidane’s son surprisingly admitted not knowing which team to support. And this despite his father being the new coach of the Blues.

“I am for Algeria, but about this match, I don’t know. It’s going to be a great match. The two selections in the best shape at the moment are them”confided the Leganés goalkeeper. Born in France and playing in Spain, where he grew up, Luca Zidane finds himself in a particular position as this confrontation between two countries to which he is linked approaches.

During this interview, the 28-year-old goalkeeper also returned to the question of his last name and the media exposure he has known since his childhood. “I’m very proud to be the son of who I am, the career he’s had, I’m trying to make my way. The comparisons will always be there, the criticism will always be there, but it’s something that I take naturally, I get used to it and I live it calmly”he explained.

The Algerian international knows that his performances are more scrutinized than those of other players. “Another goalkeeper misses in the second division and it appears almost nowhere, although I know that if I miss, it will appear everywhere”he said, before reaffirming his pride in carrying his name and his desire to chart his own course.

Finally, Luca Zidane spoke about his first African Cup of Nations with the Fennecs. Despite the elimination in the quarter-finals of the Greens, the native of Marseille has excellent memories of this experience.

“It’s a very different competition, a very beautiful experience to be able to compete in a CAN at international level, it’s something incredible and it’s a memory that I keep,” he confided. The goalkeeper, however, would have liked to go further with Algeria: “We would have liked to go further because we have a very good team and things are going well. Let’s hope that at the next CAN we can go further than at the previous one. »
