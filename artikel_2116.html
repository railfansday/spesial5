Arsenal mourns Peter Storey: 1971 Double-winning hero dies aged 81

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Arsenal have expressed their condolences in the passing of former player Peter Storey.

Storey was an influential member of one of the most successful sides in the club’s history. The midfielder appeared in 501 games for the Gunners between 1965 and 1977, winning the First Division and FA Cup during the 1970/71 season.

Storey was a key figure in the famous run as he helped keep the FA Cup hopes alive with a display against Stoke City.

A statement from the club confirmed his death on Tuesday (17th November). The club will pay tribute to Storey in their Champions League tie against Borussia Dortmund on 24th November.

Storey signed as an apprentice with Arsenal in 1961, and made his first-team debut in October 1965 in a game against Leicester City.

He was later converted to a central midfielder by manager Bertie Mee and coach Don Howe. Storey became an invaluable member of the squad due to his aggressive play, work-rate and versatility.

In 1970 Storey was part of the side that won the Inter-Cities Fairs Cup, and the following year Arsenal won the league and FA Cup double.

Storey played a key role in an amazing FA Cup semi-final in April 1971. After Arsenal had looked like they were losing, Storey scored a last-minute penalty to make it 2-2.

Arsenal won the replay to get to the final, beating Liverpool to win their first-ever double.

Storey scored 17 goals in 501 games for Arsenal. This puts him in joint 8th place in the most appearances for the club.

Storey enjoyed success at club level and earned his first cap for England in 1971. He earned a total of 19 caps for England.

He played for England until 1973. In 1977, Storey left Arsenal to join Fulham, meaning he had a 12-year stint at the north London club.

He played 19 games for Fulham before retiring from professional football in 1978.

Following his football career, Storey had a difficult time. He was imprisoned for a number of crimes. In 2010, he wrote an autobiography, True Storey: My Life and Crimes as a Football Hatchet Man, detailing these events in his life.

He will be remembered in Arsenal history for his impressive contribution to the club. Having made 501 appearances for the club, he was a key member of the squad that won the Double in 1971.

Playing against Borussia Dortmund will be a great way for fans to pay tribute to one of the key players in a special time in the history of the club.
