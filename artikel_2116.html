Rangers learn match officials for Saturday night Premiership clash vs St Mirren

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The match officials have been confirmed for Rangers’ Scottish Premiership trip to St Mirren.

Rangers travel to The Marbill Coaches Stadium on Saturday, November 7, with the league clash scheduled for an 8pm kick-off.

Nick Walsh has been appointed as the man in the middle, with Frank Connor and Daniel McFarlane running the lines. Gary Hanvidge will be the fourth official, while Greg Aitken is on VAR duty with Peter Stuart assisting.

This won't be Walsh’s first involvement with Rangers this season. He took charge of the Premier Sports Cup clash between Rangers and Celtic at Ibrox in September, which Rangers won 3-0.

Walsh also refereed Rangers’ opening Premiership fixture of the campaign, a 1-1 draw away to Dundee United on July 31.

The experienced whistler had several Rangers assignments last season too. Those included a 1-1 draw away to St Mirren in August 2025, Rangers’ 2-0 League Cup victory over Hibernian, and the 3-1 League Cup defeat to Celtic at Hampden in November.

He was also in charge as Rangers lost 2-1 at Hearts in December, before overseeing their 2-0 home victory against Aberdeen in January.

Walsh later refereed the 3-1 defeat at Celtic Park in May.
