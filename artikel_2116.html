Birmingham City have one condition for January transfer plans, John Murtough confirms

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Birmingham City's new director of football, John Murtough, has outlined exactly what supporters at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park are likely to expect ahead of the upcoming January transfer window.

The 56-year-old was recently installed into said role in B9 after a long-standing search for the successor of Craig Gardner, with Blues currently top of the Championship.

Throughout Knighthead Capital Management's ownership of the club, ambition has been a key buzzword, and that has often been backed up by activity in the transfer market.

Birmingham sent shockwaves through the footballing world two summers ago when they broke their transfer record by luring Jay Stansfield back to St. Andrew's for in excess of £15m on a seven-year contract.

It remains to be seen what position Chris Davies' side find themselves in come January, with there bound to be a focus on potential arrivals to aid a promotion bid.

Last summer saw Birmingham recruit as many as 14 new signings after a record-breaking season in League One, with many outsiders firmly holding the belief that Davies' squad were strong enough to finish inside the Championship's play-off places for the first time since 2012.

However, many of those additions flattered to deceive, namely big-money striker, Kyogo Furuhashi, who was one of five senior departees this summer.

With Davies refining his tactical approach, only seven transfers were made this summer, of which two were already at the club last season in the form of James Beadle and Jhon Solis, whilst Max Bird and Liam Millar, in particular, have made immediate impressions.

Murtough has overseen hierarchical roles at Everton, Fulham, Coventry City and Manchester United, during which Cristiano Ronaldo returned to Old Trafford, and will now be tasked with aiding the recruitment process at St. Andrew's.

It remains to be seen who will become available to the Championship promotion hopefuls in January, but during an in-house interview, the experienced figure outlined exactly what staff behind-the-scenes will be looking at, as the winter window is notoriously difficult for doing business in.

"January, I see as a window where there potentially is opportunities," Murtough explained.

"We're aware of what players are out there. It may be sometimes difficult in actually getting the players that we want. They're usually more available in the summer windows.

"But, if an opportunity becomes available in January which we think can strengthen the squad, and it fits within the model that we're building, I'm sure we'll be ready and able to react to that," he added.

"One of the things we'll be looking for is building towards the summer. But, if something comes available and it fits within the plan that we're working towards, we'll be ready to react."

It is clear that there will be specific types of players eyed by Davies and his recruitment team in a bid to only add to the quality and the character of Birmingham's squad, with the club's promotion ambitions laid bare.

They have, once again, become clear, with Football League World exclusively revealing on Monday that they are to rival Middlesbrough for the potential signing of FC Lorient's highly-rated Cameroonian international, Arthur Avom.

The 21-year-old has already attracted interest from several Premier League sides, but could be available for a cut-price January fee or a free transfer next summer once his contract at Stade du Moustoir expires.
