Issued by the Unified Earth Government, Office of Cosmic Silence

Following the Cascade Incident, the Yarrow Misunderstanding and what is now formally called The Cake Problem, these protocols have been aggressively revised. Read carefully. Your late predecessors did not.

Nothing. All unknown alien lifeforms are extremely dangerous, and death is the usual First Contact result — for the human. Most personnel therefore prefer attending the Second Contact proceedings.

Suddenly remember you have an important appointment on the other side of the Galaxy. Do not answer. You are a rock. If you must answer, say “I am a rock.”

Ask them to state the nature of their emergency. If no emergency, put them on HOLD.

“It’s complicated.” “Around.” Never name a location you would like to keep. You are from nowhere. Continue this trend. Geography is a vulnerability. Wave in a vague direction and mumble, “Over thereabouts …”

Friendliness is a hunting strategy. Your cat is friendly. The alien’s dead bird on the doorstep will be you.

Do not listen to your Engineers. They will say it would be cool. It isn’t.

The first alien species we ever contacted bred by laying eggs in your chest. Bring protection. Flamethrowers are kept in Storage B12.

Nothing good responds instantly. Delay your reply. Aim for disinterest, not malfunction. You are busy doing nothing elsewhere. Maintain that illusion.

You will. That’s how we lost Star Base 2.

No. You are, if a member of the Core, body count. Be a version that could belong anywhere and nowhere at the same time.

They will not define ‘this’. Do not ask. Undefined ‘this’ cannot be repeated and cannot be tracked. Leave it unresolved. Agree but do not fix a specific date. Do not, under any excuse, use the word ‘date’.

Decline. Do not swallow alien embryos. They taste of regret.

Quarantine. Two weeks minimum. Do not assume the thing wearing their face remembers your birthday.

No. Ask about their breathing requirements. Then say, “Oh, sorry, we don’t breathe that,” and change the subject immediately. Do not revisit the topic.

They are always the last of their kind. The last of its kind has a lot of free time and a lot of anger. Do not become its hobby.

You will. Internalize it. You have practice. Panic is normal. Displaying it is optional and discouraged. Don’t be a target.

You are still breathing and they do not know where the rest of us are. That is considered a success under current guidelines. Survival is the only metric.
