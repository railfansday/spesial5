NBA News: Jaylen Clark out 2 weeks

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

After an abysmal performance against a basement-dwelling Chicago Bulls team on Saturday night, things got worse the next day. The Minnesota Timberwolves officially announced that Jaylen Clark, our favorite try-hard defensive stopper, would miss the next two weeks at minimum with a right hamstring strain.

Here's the play where Clark got injured early in the second quarter, making a hustle play and diving into the Minnesota bench.

This is certainly something that will be a significant blow to the Wolves. Though Clark averages just over 13 minutes per game, he is fourth on the team in defensive rating (100.3). He's been one of, if not the best, on-ball defender for Minnesota. He constantly injects energy into his team when they need it most. Jaylen has also been a 36% three-point shooter so far this season, acting as a valuable release valve on the perimeter.

"Clark County" will miss at least the next eight games in the next 14 days before he's reevaluated. Soft tissue injuries are notoriously easy to reaggravate, thus it's safe to expect he will miss well over double-digit games. When you consider Jaylen's style of play – lots of quick burst lateral movement – he could be on the shelf for at least a month next to Anthony Edwards and Donte DiVincenzo.

So who will step up in the rotation from the depth chart?

I have long since been a fan of two-way guard Zyon Pullin, but he is certainly an offense-oriented player who doesn't really provide what Clark does. Rookie Isaiah Evans is earning his stripes in the G League and likely one of the less effective defensive players on the roster. I could easily see Finch divvying Jaylen's minutes over to currently involved players like Bones Hyland and Terrence Shannon Jr.

But can I make a quick case for "no longer the worst player in the NBA," Cody Williams?

The younger Williams brother has appeared in just seven of 13 games for the Wolves this season, all in "development time." However, he did show multiple flashes of his potential during the preseason. Cody impressed in a spot preseason start against the reigning champion New York Knicks, locking up 2026 Finals MVP Jalen Brunson.

Zach Lowe had a lot of positive things to say about Williams, including his defensive improvement, at the end of last season. I'm not calling for Williams to soak up Clark's entire 13 minute role, but he fits what Minnesota is losing in Clark the most. There will certainly be times where LaMelo Ball, Jaden McDaniels, Ayo Dosunmu, or Bones Hyland either falls into foul trouble or just need relief guarding those difficult assignments. When that happens, the need to get an active body who is athletic and long will be especially important.

This is the opportunity that many players in Cody's shoes hope for. He's still just 20 years old (soon to be 21 this week), but has years of experience under his belt for a tanking team. Minnesota will need his services out of desperation and he can shed that early career label as a draft bust. It's the perfect storm of supply meeting demand.

Williams also wears jersey number 9 which, as we all know, is a magical number.
