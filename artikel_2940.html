Roob's bye week stats: It's awful but we do have Makai Lemon and Dallas Goedert

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Roob's bye week stats: It's awful but we do have Makai Lemon and Dallas Goedert originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

How bad has it been? We'll document a lot of it in this week's bye-week stats piece, but just to give you an idea … this is the first time in more than half a century the Eagles have played nine games and they don't have a 350-yard rusher, a 350-yard receiver or a 1,000-yard passer.

Nine games in, their leading rusher is Saquon Barkley with 296 yards, their leading receiver is Makai Lemon with 312 yards and their leading passer is Jalen Hurts with 933 yards.

Last time that happened was 1971, when Pete Liske had thrown for 981 yards, leading rusher Tom Woodeshick had 143 yards and leading receiver leading receiver Ben Hawkins had 349 yards after nine games.

It's not fun documenting just how bad a football team is, but this is our reality right now.

But we'll start with the only positive stats we could find with the Eagles buried on this seven-game losing streak.

DALLAS KEEPS PRODUCING: Dallas Goedert has 31 catches this year and has moved up from eighth to third in career receptions among tight ends who've never made a Pro Bowl. The only tight ends in NFL history who caught more passes than Goedert and never made a Pro Bowl are Ben Watson, who had 547 catches from 2004 through 2019, and Hunter Henry, who caught 487 passes and is now with the Patriots in his 10th NFL season. Including postseason, Goedert has 496 career receptions and needs four to become the 26th tight end ever with 500.

THE EAGLES FOUND ONE: With 37 catches on 54 targets, Makai Lemon has the 9th-highest catch percentage (68.5) of 33 wide receivers with at least 50 targets. Among the eight guys he trails are Puka Nacua, Ja'Marr Chase, Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Amon-Ra St. Brown. That 68.5 figure is highest on record by an Eagles rookie through his first nine career games – Stathead traks catch percentage since 1977. Previous high was Keith Jackson's 64.6 percent in 1988 and previous high by a WR was Jordan Matthews with 63.9 in 2014. Highest catch percentage in Eagles history was LeSean McCoy at 78.5. Top WR is DeVonta Smith at 69.1. Lemon's 25 catches over the last five games are the most by an Eagles rookie in any five-game span since Keith Jackson had 36 in 1988 in the second through sixth games of his career. Jackson's 38 catches in his first six games, most ever by a tight end in his first six career games.

HOW CAN YOU ONLY HAVE 5 TAKEAWAYS? The Eagles' five takeaways is their fewest after nine games since 1937, when they also had five. They had one in 1935 and four in 1936, and those are the only other seasons they've had five or fewer. Only four teams have ever had fewer takeaways through nine games since 1940 – the 2011 Steelers (4), 2014 Jets (3), the 2019 Falcons (4) and 2025 Jets (1). Their three interceptions equals the fewest they've ever had after nine games. They also had three in 1998 and 2020. Their streak of four consecutive games without an INT is 4th-longest in franchise history.

THEY DON'T SCORE MUCH: The Eagles have scored 21 or fewer points in all seven games during their losing streak, their longest streak of games without scoring more than 21 points since 1999. It's the first time since 2012 they've scored 24 or fewer in each of their first nine games. They've lost four in a row by eight or more points for the first time since 2012 and only the third time in the last 50 years. They've been outscored this year by 73 points, the 2nd-biggest scoring margin against them through nine games since 1972. They were outscored by 116 the first nine games of 1998. Their 148 points are their fewest after nine games since 1999, when they had 114.

NICE PASSING GAME: The Eagles' 172 passing yards per game are their fewest through nine games since 2003, when they averaged just 151 through eight games. They have had fewer than 210 or fewer passing yards in seven of their nine games, matching their most games under 210 passing yards through nine games since 1979. Their 797 rushing yards are their fewest after nine games since 2005.

BLOWN LEADS: The Eagles led the Commanders 10-3 after the first quarter and led the Giants 8-6 but lost both games. This is the first time in 31 years the Eagles have lost consecutive games that they led going into the second quarter. In 1995, they led the Chargers 7-0 after the first quarter at the Vet before losing 27-21 on a Junior Seau touchdown return after recovering a Calvin Williams fumble and an 88-yard punt return TD by Andre Coleman and a week later they led the Raiders 17-0 after the first quarter before losing 48-17 in what turned out to be Randall Cunningham's final start in an Eagles uniform.

WORST SINCE 1974: Through nine games, the Eagles are averaging 5.7 yards per pass play and 3.7 yards per rushing play. This is the first time they've been that bad both throwing and running the football nine games into a season since 1974 – when they were at 5.5 and 3.3 – and only the second time since 1941. They're the first NFL team as low as 5.7 and 3.7 in the same season through nine games since the 2014 Raiders (5.5, 3.7) and only the 12th in the last 40 years. The only team since 1943 to make the playoffs with averages that low both rushing and passing was the 1971 Vikings, who had one of the greatest defenses of all time.

CLOSING IN ON A RECORD NOBODY WANTS: The Eagles have been outgained by at least 20 yards in all seven games during their losing streak. That matches their most consecutive games getting outgained by 20 or more yards since 1976, when it happened eight games in a row. Their all-time high is 10 straight games in 1971, so they're only three games shy of that "record." They've been outgained by 839 yards so far this year, the biggest yardage margin against them since 1999. During the seven-game losing streak, they've been outgained by 912 yards, the biggest margin against them in any seven-game span since 1976. They've been out-rushed by 338 yards, the largest rushing margin against them since 2005 (352) and 3rd-largest since 1985. Their passing yards margin is 501, largest since 1999 (625) and 2nd-largest since 1976.

THEY HAVEN'T WON IN A WHILE: The seven-game losing streak is tied for 9th-longest in franchise history, but three of those streaks came in 1942 or earlier. Since 1943, is tied for 6th-longest in Eagles history. A loss to the Steelers next weekend will give the Eagles an eight-game losing streak, which would be tied for 4th-longest in franchise history and match their longest since an 11-game losing streak in 1969. They also lost eight straight in 2012.

THE BOBBY HOYING STAT: With Jalen Hurts going 2-3, Tanner McKee 0-3 and Joe Milton losing his first start last week, this is the first time in 28 years three different Eagles starting quarterbacks have lost a game in the same season. In 1998, Rodney Peete was 1-3, Bobby Hoying 1-6 and Koy Detmer 1-4. Also happened the year before. In 1997, Peete was 2-1, Hoying was 2-3-1 and Ty Detmer was 2-5. In 1991, they had four: Randall Cunningham (1-0), Jim McMahon (7-3), Jeff Kemp (1-1) and Brad Goebel (0-2).

FIRST TIME EVER: The Eagles are averaging 4.29 yards per play and allowing 5.37 yards per play. This is the first time in franchise history they've gained under 4.3 yards per play and allowed over 5.3 yards per play through nine games. The 4.29 is their worst on offense since 1999.

THREE STRAIGHT IN THE DIVISION: With consecutive losses to the Cowboys, Commanders and Giants, this is the first time the Eagles have lost division games three weeks in a row in 27 years. Last time it happened was Weeks 12 through 14 in 1999, when they lost to Washington 20-17 in overtime at FedEx, 21-17 to the Cards at Sun Devil Stadium and 33-18 to the Giants at the Vet. The Eagles lost five NFC East games from 2022 through 2024. They lost three in 21 days.

A WENDELL SMALLWOOD SIGHTING: With Saquon Barkley leading the team with 296 rushing yards, this is only the second time since 1992 the Eagles haven't had a 300-yard rusher through nine games. In 2018 their leading rusher after nine games was Wendell Smallwood with 251 yards. Last time before that they didn't have a 300-yard rusher after nine games was 1991, when Heath Sherman had a team-high 244 yards.

OK, THIS IS REALLY BAD: The Eagles have been out-rushed by 338 yards, the largest rushing margin against them since 2005 (352) and 3rd-largest since 1985. Their passing yards margin is 501, largest since 1999 (625) and 2nd-largest since 1976.
