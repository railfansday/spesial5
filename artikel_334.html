XTEINK e-readers are marked down for Black Friday: Save over $34 on the Kindle alternative

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

For all those e-book fans who want a Kindle alternative on Black Friday, there are discounts on the XTEINK range in Amazon's Black Friday sale.

These clever little devices are ultra-light and literally pocket-sized — the XTEINK X4 is 4.3 inches, and the XTEINK X3 is just 3.7 inches. You can use them as a standalone reader or attach them to the back of your phone. They also sync with your phone via an app, allowing you to upload books straight to the XTEINK reader.
