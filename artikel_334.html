Multiple Microsoft 365 services are experiencing an outage

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Outlook and Teams are among the platforms impacted by “elevated load” issues.

Microsoft is experiencing an outage for several of its services this afternoon. Initial reports focused on users being unable to access their email in Outlook, but more than the one platform in its Microsoft 365 suite appears to be down. The company's support account on X acknowledged the issue.

At 2:15PM ET, many Engadget staff were unable to even load Microsoft's service status page. Those who were saw a message that listed Exchange Online, Microsoft Teams, SharePoint Online, Microsoft Copilot 365 and Microsoft Defender XDR as the potentially impacted services. While the status report points to Business and Enterprise tiers of the 365 tools, individual users also appear to be impacted. The full update states:

"We're investigating an issue impacting multiple Microsoft 365 services and we've determined the affected infrastructure is experiencing elevated load across multiple regions, which is likely contributing to the connectivity errors. We're restarting and repairing impacted portions of this infrastructure to restore capacity, and reviewing an unexpected increase in a specific type of service traffic to determine whether it's contributing to the elevated load. In parallel, we're evaluating options to redirect traffic to alternate infrastructure to expedite relief."

Around 5PM ET, Microsoft Planner, Microsoft To Do, Microsoft Intune, Microsoft Defender for Office 365 and Microsoft Purview had been added to the list of impacted services. Issues with Power BI and Microsoft 365 Apps Admin Center were also noted. The latest update reveals that a fix for this afternoon's disruptions still may not be imminent.

"We're continuing to pursue all available pathways to restore service, as our actions taken thus far have not provided the anticipated relief," the status page states. "Our investigation has found that a core process on the affected servers is becoming overwhelmed, which causes the servers to stop responding and contributes to the capacity loss."

Update, Nov 13, 9:09am ET: "We're seeing continued recovery progress across affected regions, with broader improvements in North America, sustained improvements across much of Europe, and encouraging recovery signals in Asia Pacific," Microsoft wrote on X. "Some users may still experience intermittent access issues or delayed email delivery as mitigation continues across remaining affected infrastructure. Our focus remains on stabilizing recovered infrastructure, completing mitigation in remaining impacted areas, and reducing delayed email queues and backlogs while we validate service consistency across connection methods."
