2026 BCDA Grand: Barrington at SayeBrook

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Built around the idea that great neighborhoods begin on the front porch, the Barrington floor plan reimagines traditional Lowcountry living.

The 2,355-square-foot home at SayeBrook in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, blends timeless architecture with flexible living spaces, emphasizing walkability, indoor-outdoor living, and neighborhood connection.

Designed to complement SayeBrook’s pedestrian-friendly streetscape, the home features an expansive front porch that encourages interaction with neighbors while creating inviting outdoor living areas.

Award: GrandCategory: Production or Spec Home 3,000 Square Feet or LessArchitect: Cooper CarryBuilder: Saussy BurbankSize: 2,355 square feetLocation: Myrtle Beach, South Carolina

Inside, an open-concept kitchen, dining, and family room form the heart of the home, complemented by a private first-floor owner’s suite and additional bedrooms upstairs. A loft or optional fourth bedroom allows the floor plan to adapt to changing household needs.

The result is a home that reflects the architectural traditions of South Carolina’s coast while delivering the functionality, flexibility, and comfort expected by today’s buyers.
