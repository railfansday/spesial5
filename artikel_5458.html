China's industrial profits grew at fastest pace in five months in October

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Profits rose 20.1% from a year earlier as AI hardware sales and higher commodity prices offset weakness in consumer industries

China's industrial enterprises increased profits 20.1% in October from a year earlier, the National Bureau of Statistics said on Friday. Profits for the first 10 months of the year rose 16% from a year earlier, compared with 15.5% growth through September.

October marked the fastest profit growth in five months, according to Bloomberg. The gain topped the 15.5% increase projected by Bloomberg Economics.

"Demand for AI computing power keeps rising, driving sustained profit growth in storage chips and adjacent manufacturing sectors," NBS statistician Yu Weining said in a statement, according to Bloomberg.

Electronics profits were twice their year-earlier level over the first 10 months, driven by manufacturers of complete computers, specialty electronic materials and computer accessories, the NBS said. The sector was responsible for roughly 10 percentage points of the total rise in profits. Non-ferrous metal ore mining and processing added 4.6 percentage points, with profits up at least 60%. Petroleum processors, which lost money a year earlier, posted a combined profit in the period.

Yu said copper and aluminum have become more expensive this year as AI and renewable energy sectors have driven up demand. High-tech manufacturing profits climbed 50.9%, while companies building spacecraft and launch vehicles saw earnings rise 63.2%, according to CNBC.

Consumer-facing and construction-linked industries lagged. Automakers' profits fell 26% over the first 10 months, while earnings dropped 46.7% at companies making building materials like cement and glass and 52.4% at furniture makers, according to CNBC. Utilities, covering electricity, heat, gas and water supply, posted a decline of close to 15%, according to Bloomberg. Retail sales grew 0.4% in October, Bloomberg said.

"The recovery has yet to broaden into a more durable improvement in corporate profitability," Tianchen Xu, senior economist at the Economist Intelligence Unit, told CNBC.

Industrial profits were growing at a fast pace before October. Profits across industrial enterprises above the designated size rose 18.8% in the January-to-May period, while profits at makers of computers, communication equipment and other electronic equipment climbed 103.9%.

Memory chip demand has fed through to company results. ChangXin Memory Technologies reported first-half revenue of 150.31 billion yuan ($22.4 billion), up 874% from a year earlier, as a global shortage of memory chips driven by AI demand sent prices higher.
