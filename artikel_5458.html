SAMILBIO Moves to Launch 'NpillAI' This Year, Pairing AI Consultation with Pharmacist Review

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Platform provides nutrition management information based on health checkup and medication data; kiosk integration development completed

SEOUL, South Korea, Nov. 10, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- SAMILBIO is preparing to launch "NpillAI", a health functional food platform that combines AI chat consultation with direct review by pharmacists, within this year. The service is designed for users who struggle to choose health functional foods, connecting everything from information gathering to product selection, purchase, and delivery.

According to SAMILBIO, NpillAI is in the final stage of development and is being prepared for launch in 2026. The company has built a 24-hour AI chat consultation feature so that users can access the information they need for nutrition management at any time. The service focuses on reducing inconvenience for consumers who find it hard to visit a pharmacy because of busy schedules, or who have difficulty deciding which health functional foods to choose.

The service provides nutrition management information by referencing users' health checkup and medication data, with a pharmacist directly reviewing the recommendation results. Its distinguishing feature is that a pharmacist review step is added to AI-based information delivery. The company plans to keep refining the process after launch so that it operates smoothly in actual use.

The problem NpillAI aims to address is the gap between interest in health functional foods and the actual selection of products. According to the company, even when consumers are interested in nutrition management, it can be difficult to determine which products suit their circumstances, and taking time out for a separate consultation can be a burden. For these users, SAMILBIO is building the process leading from consultation to product use into a single service.

After product recommendations, the company plans to connect users to purchase and delivery. The aim is to reduce the need for users to look up and buy products separately after reviewing information, and to improve convenience so that consultation results translate into actual product choices. The company intends to focus not only on individual features but also on reducing friction across the entire user journey.

The company has also completed development of kiosk integration connected to the online service. Through this, SAMILBIO is preparing to make NpillAI accessible on-site as well as online. The next task is to apply the completed integration feature in real-world usage environments. Rather than specific installation locations or operating methods, the company plans to focus first on the service launch and preparations for on-site deployment.

After launch, the company plans to continuously improve issues identified during actual use. It will review the flow in which users start a consultation, review information, and select products, while refining the service so that the pharmacist review step functions smoothly within it. Preparations will also continue so that the kiosk can be deployed to suit real-world usage environments.

SAMILBIO, which opened for business in April 2023, has pursued an AI-based health functional food platform as one of its core businesses. The company has made launching NpillAI within the year a priority, finishing development while planning to raise the service's level of completion based on user experience after launch.

"NpillAI focuses on reducing inconvenience for people who are interested in health functional foods but find it hard to determine which products they need, or who struggle to find time for a consultation," SAMILBIO said. "We will continue refining the service so that the pharmacist review process operates smoothly within it."
