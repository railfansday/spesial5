Stock futures fall as Huang’s exit sends Nvidia reeling and oil prices rise

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Nvidia named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO after Jensen Huang resigned, as Nasdaq-100 futures lost 1.2% and U.S. crude rose above $87 a barrel

Samuel Boivin / NurPhoto via Getty Images

Nvidia $NVDA stock fell more than 10% after the company announced that CEO Jensen Huang resigned, weighing on U.S. stock futures on Thursday.

Nasdaq $NDAQ-100 futures were down 1.2%, S&P 500 futures slid 0.6%, and Dow Jones Industrial Average futures gave up 47 points, a 0.1% decline. Oil prices rose after President Donald Trump wrote on Truth Social, "We don't need a deal with Iran. Those days have long since passed."

Nvidia named Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress interim CEO. Huang, who started Nvidia with partners in 1993, has said he is not a proponent of succession planning, so no obvious candidate exists for the permanent role, according to The Wall Street Journal. Prediction markets have put forward several people from inside the company as possible picks.

The drop left Nvidia poised to have shed nearly three-quarters of a trillion dollars in market value since the scandal broke, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Shares of Advanced Micro Devices and Broadcom $AVGO rose about 2%. Other stocks tied to artificial intelligence fell with Nvidia. Oracle $ORCL stock lost 4%, and Nebius stock dropped 20% in premarket trading after the company issued third-quarter results and guidance.

West Texas Intermediate futures climbed over 1%, topping $87 a barrel. Brent crude futures added over 1% and moved past $94. Shares of Chevron $CVX and ExxonMobil $XOM rose about 1%, and Eli Lilly $LLY stock gained more than 1%.

Asian indexes fell as losses hit semiconductor makers and other artificial intelligence names. European stocks rose.
