IDA Documentary Awards 2026 Nominations: ‘You Can See Everything,’ ‘Concrete Land,’ and More Earn Nods

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

On Tuesday, November 17, the International Documentary Association (IDA) announced the nominees for its 42nd annual IDA Documentary Awards, including everything from the highly publicized blockbuster “You Can See Everything” to under-the-radar Best Documentary Feature contenders like “Concrete Land.”

“I couldn’t be more excited to celebrate these meaningful and creative documentaries. It’s a challenging time for documentary makers, but also a really inspiring moment for IDA and for the field,” IDA Board President Ina Fichman said in a statement. “This year we see nominees whose deeply personal stories share narratives of resistance and exile, and we see artful, innovative filmmaking that is capturing new and robust audiences. I hope many will join us on December 4 as we honor our incredible community.” Related Stories A24’s TV Division Is Quietly Having as Strong a Year as Its Film Team ‘The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth’ Throws Netflix’s Oscar Campaign a Curveball

The 2026 IDA Documentary Awards will also present the following special honors: the Career Achievement Award for the late Chilean filmmaker Patricio Guzmán; the Truth to Power Award for Amy Goodman and Juan González of Democracy Now!; the Emerging Filmmaker Award for Maia Lekow and Christopher King; and the Amicus Award for Cannes Docs, the documentary program of the Marché du Film led by Pierre-Alexis Chevit.

This year, IDA received a record 650 entries in all categories from 95 countries, an increase over last year both in the total number of entries and the countries represented. 32 countries are represented among the final lineup of nominees.

IDA members from around the world will vote for the winners in the Best Feature Documentary and Best Short Documentary categories from November 17 to November 29, 2026. All other category winners are selected by juries representing the international documentary community.

The Awards will take place December 4, 2026, at The Ebell of Los Angeles, hosted for the second year in a row by documentary field leader Tabitha Jackson.
