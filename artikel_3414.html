Billy Joel Announces Return to Stage and ‘A Little Music’ After Brain Disorder Diagnosis

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Billy Joel will be joining the students at Florida Atlantic University in December to answer questions and possibly perform some music. This will be the singer’s first official appearance after he announced his normal pressure hydrocephalus diagnosis and stepped away from the stage last year.

A post shared to his social media said that the college “will welcome singer, songwriter and composer Billy Joel for Billy Joel: An Evening of Questions & Answers…And a Little Music” at 7 p.m. on Dec. 14. Tickets will be available exclusively to students currently enrolled at the university beginning Nov. 12.

While this is the singer’s first official event, he previously showed up unannounced in Wellington, Florida, during a Billy Joel cover band’s show in January. The Palm Beach Post reported the Joel walked on stage with the help of a cane and joined tribute band Turnstiles as they performed his beloved hit, “We Didn’t Start The Fire,” and “Big Shot.”

Last month, Joel shared an update about his health during an interview with The Hollywood Reporter and said that at the moment, he’s not allowed to drive. When discussing when he would perform again, the singer said, “I have to take a series of tests before I know,” adding, “I can’t drive right now.
