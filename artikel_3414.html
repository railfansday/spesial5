Late-Night Hosts React to Election Results: ‘Like a Reverse Jan. 6’

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The election results are in and late-night TV hosts were ready with a slew of jokes. Several late-night shows addressed the blue wave that swept the country during the midterms, resulting in a huge rebuke for President Donald Trump.

On Jimmy Kimmel Live, Jimmy Kimmel delighted in the Republicans’ big loss. “Last night Americans treated Donald Trump like he was the East Wing of the White House,” Kimmel recounted. “We did it! Transgender for everyone!”

He added that Trump is doing what he does best in the face of defeat: blaming everyone else and absolving himself of all responsibility. He recounted how Trump posted on social media, “I wasn’t on the ballot and if I was we would have won in a landslide.”

“Oh, shut up,” Kimmel replied. “Just shut up please. Give us a break for one day and shut that ridiculous mouth. You are such a spoiled child. Did you ever learn anything about manners or sportsmanship or simple common decency?”
