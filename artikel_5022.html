Fleece is a soft, synthetic fabric usually made from a polyester blend, often mixed with spandex, nylon, or cotton. Designed to mimic wool's insulating properties, fleece is lighter, more breathable, and dries faster, making it ideal for staying warm. Plus, fleece wicks away moisture and dries quickly, keeping you cozy even if you’re caught in rain or snow. Think of it like wearing a warm hug on a cold day.

When it comes to fleece leggings, the fabric blend is key. Most leggings are made from a mix of polyester and spandex, which delivers the perfect blend of warmth, stretch, and comfort. We’ll give you a quick breakdown of why these materials are key here:

Polyester traps heat, making it an ideal blend for staying warm without bulk. Compared to natural fibers like cotton, polyester dries quickly. WH editors love wearing polyester-fleece leggings on long walks since they stay warm without making you feel sweaty. It’s super lightweight yet strong, meaning you won’t feel like the material is clinging to your body. Polyester also helps leggings retain their shape over time, so stretching, sagging, and pilling are likely a thing of the past.

Nylon is a popular material in fleece leggings because of its strength, flexibility, and smooth texture. It resists wear and tear, which helps keep leggings looking brand new after every wash. Nylon also offers water resistance, which is key for outdoor activities in light rain or snow.

Spandex (also known as elastane or Lycra) is the go-to fabric for stretch and flexibility. It fits snugly and moves with you, giving you maximum comfort during high-impact activities like yoga, running, and dance. While it’s not as durable on its own, spandex is often blended with other fabrics (like nylon or polyester) to create leggings that are both stretchy and long-lasting.

Here’s the thing: Fleece leggings with thicker fabric may feel more restrictive than traditional leggings, so if you plan to wear them for high-impact activities, opting for a bigger size might be best. Look for four-way stretch fabrics like spandex for flexibility and comfort. If you're between sizes, choose based on your desired fit—size down for a sculpted look (read: tighter) or up for a relaxed, lounge-friendly feel.
