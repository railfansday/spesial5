2028 Hyundai Elantra N

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Good news for budget-conscious track rats: A new Hyundai Elantra N could be right around the corner. With the standard Elantra receiving a complete redesign for the 2027 model year, we believe that the enthusiast-oriented Hyundai Elantra N will likely follow its pedestrian counterpart through the generational leap. That means crisp new sheetmetal, an updated cabin, and a few other new and enticing goodies.

Its under-hood hardware is still a mystery, but Hyundai has hinted that it has been stress-testing a new gas powertrain in its N1 Cup racecar that will likely make its way into the new Elantra N. We suspect that its output will climb to somewhere around 300 horsepower, which would put it within spitting distance of the 315-hp Honda Civic Type R. Like the previous Elantra N, the new model will surely remain a versatile and enthralling sport sedan, complete with track-ready suspension, a rowdy exhaust note, and, most importantly, a standard six-speed manual transmission.

Expert Tip: If Hyundai does decide to bump the new Elantra N up to a higher performance bracket, it will be competing with models such as the Honda Civic Type R, Toyota GR Corolla, and Volkswagen Golf R.

Like its humble standard-issue Elantra counterpart, we think that Hyundai will give its sportiest N model a complete makeover for the 2028 model year.
