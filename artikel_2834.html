D.J. Reed, Sione Vaki listed as questionable for the Lions

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Lions cornerback D.J. Reed has missed the last two games with a hip injury, but his return to the lineup remains on the table heading into the weekend.

Reed is listed as questionable to face the Buccaneers after three limited practices this week. Reed said earlier this week that he felt he came back too fast from a hamstring injury last season and does not want to make the same mistake this time around.

Running back Sione Vaki (ankle) also drew a questionable tag. He had six carries for 28 yards and one catch for 17 yards in last Sunday’s win over the Patriots.

Center Cade Mays (ankle) has been ruled out. Linebacker Joe Bachie (knee), cornerback Khalil Dorsey (ankle), defensive lineman Tyler Lacy (shoulder), and cornerback Ennis Rakestraw (shoulder) round out the questionable group for Detroit.
