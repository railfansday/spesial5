Michigan 8-Player Football State Championships | Follow along live

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Newest updates from each game will appear at the top.

The Michigan High School Athletic Association 8-player football state championships kick off Saturday, Nov. 21 at Northern Michigan University's Superior Dome in Marquette.

The Division 1 state championship will start off the day with an 11 a.m. kick between Deckerville (11-1) and Blanchard Montabella (11-2).

Deckerville last earned a state championship during the 2024 season in a 30-28 final over Pickford, while Blanchard Montabella makes a return to the state finals after falling to Martin in a 52-8 final a year ago in Marquette.

In Division 2, a pair of unbeatens will meet in a 2 p.m. kickoff, as Gaylord St. Mary (12-0) takes on Mendon (12-0) in a rematch of a 1999 state championship at the Division 8 11-player level.
