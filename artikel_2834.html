Blazers shoot the lights out as Embiid-less Sixers fall to 14-4

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

We haven't seen them yet this year — do all Western Conference teams just never miss their three-pointers?

The Sixers were blown out by the Trail Blazers 128-111 Monday as Portland made 45% of their threes on the night.

After missing his last two games, Jaylen Brown was soaring until he ran into foul trouble, finishing with 20 points on 8-of-14 shooting along with two rebounds and four assists. LeBron James had his best shooting night as a Sixer, going 8-of-13 from the floor to finish with a season-high 23 points along with seven rebounds and three assists.

Tyrese Maxey had 22 points and five assists on 7-of-16 shooting. VJ Edgecombe finished with 12 points, seven rebounds and seven assists shooting 5-of-11 from the field. Deni Avdija led all scorers with 31.

Joel Embiid (illness) was the only Sixer out tonight. Ja Morant and Shaedon Sharp were out for Portland, both out with long-term injuries.

The Sixers got Brown involved with two early touches. He drew a foul on the first and got an open jumper on the second thanks to a healthy stiff arm to Avdija. Jabari Walker found Edgecombe by the basket as the Blazers were trying to get down the floor, capping off a 9-2 Philly run to start the game and prompt an early timeout for the Blazers.

That timeout got Avdija going offensively. After beating Brown off the dribble he nailed a couple of threes. LeBron was creating threes on the other end as well. A pumpfake of his sent Avdija flying by, allowing James to drill an open one from the corner. He drove baseline on the next possession and found Brown, also wide open in the corner.

Coming off the bench, Dominick Barlow was the first able to stop Avdija, swatting away a layup attempt. The Sixers didn't come out with a poor effort defensively. They were able to lock up Damian Lillard for a sho-tclock violation, but Portland opened the game hot from deep, making four of their first nine threes of the game.

Adem Bona also returned after missing time with a neck injury. He was able to quickly grab an offensive rebound upon entering the game and when the ball got swung to him, he was able to put it on the deck and get to the basket for a dunk. He even hit a bit of a move to get by Jrue Holiday. He was relieved by Walker after only a couple of minutes. Walker picked up a missed Dean Wade three and put it back to keep the Sixers up by two after the first.

With some more starters back on the floor the offense started to hum again. Brown and Edgecombe each drilled pull-up jumpers. LeBron threw a pair of nice passes to Walker who converted on both of them, and got a skyhook for himsel.

Defensively, Walker was a pretty small look against Donovan Clingan. The Sixers tried to sit in some zone to combat that, but quickly went back to man. On top of those paint concerns, the Blazers remained pretty hot from three. They hit two more to take a lead of their own, despite LeBron hitting a big three that got the crowd off it's feet in between them.

Brown did not look like he had missed the last two games at all. He just looked so smooth getting to his spots in the midrange. He put up 19 in the first half on 10 shots. The Sixers needed every bit of it because they had less answers for Avdija on the other end.

After starting the game with Brown on Avdija, the Sixers tried Dean Wade before ultimately landing on Barlow to close the half. Barlow did foul him but Avdija missed both free throws. On the next trip down Barlow was able to force a bad airball that happened to fall to LeBron who threw a gorgeous fullcourt pass to Brown for the fast break dunk. After that tied the game, Lillard was able to draw a foul on Edgecombe shooting a three-pointer. Maxey found LeBron in the corner on the ensuing possession for another Sixers' buzzer-beater, keeping the game tied at the half.

LeBron tried to throw another fullcourt bounce pass to give Maxey a fast break layup, but Sidy Cissoko got his hands on it to break it up. They continued to move the ball well though as Edgecombe got open in the dunker for James to find him, before dishing to a cutting Walker who drew a foul. Both teams struggled from the line to start the second half as Walker split his pair. Avdija was unable to convert an and-1 and Clingan gave the fans a five-count of chicken nuggets very early.

The Sixers also had to deal with another extended period of Brown on the bench after he picked up his fourth foul. James seemed to still be up to carry the scoring load, drilling his fourth three of the night on his fourth attempt.

For as good as LeBron was, the Sixers struggled to supplement him offensively with Brown sitting in foul trouble. They had a six-minute stretch only scoring one field goal. Maxey tried to get more aggressive but had a pull-up three attempt blocked by Robert Williams III. Lucky for him the Blazers weren't able to secure the rebound and Maxey was able to scoot to the basket for a reverse layup.

Any minor blip for the Sixers offense was notable because they just could not get stops on the other end. As they sat in a zone Portland continued to drill everything from deep. After Scoot Henderson drifted into a gap to drill a three, Holiday hit one in Wade's face on the following possession. On the other end Barlow was desperately trying to create a hustle bucket, but he came up a bit gingerly after going for a rebound and getting his shot blocked by Cissoko. Jayson Kent drilled one from the corner to give the Blazers a nine-point lead as the third came to a close.

Henderson and Kent drilled a couple more threes to start the quarter, extending Portland's lead to its largest of the night. Nick Nurse tried to let it ride as LeBron got to the basket, but after Edgecombe dribbled the ball off his foot out of bounds and the team surrendered a layup off an offensive rebound, Nurse called an angry timeout less than two minutes into the quarter.

Edgecombe was able to run out off a loose ball and get a transition layup and the Blazers immediately called a timeout to squash a potential run. The Sixers had chances coming out of the timeout, forcing a couple turnovers, but they couldn't take advantage on the offensive end. Lillard hit a pull-up which got the Blazers offense rolling again and the Sixers had no response.

Portland's height advantage had begun to wear the Sixers down as well as they started surrendering more. offensive rebounds. No matter what center option the Sixers tried, Portland's center would have an inch or two in height advantage, there was just nothing to be done about that, save for some Tacko time. Avdija stole an inbounds pass of off Maxey as Sixers fans started to flock to the exits with over five minutes remaining.
