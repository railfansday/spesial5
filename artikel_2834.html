Dolphins waive Jamree Kromah

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Dolphins have let go of an edge rusher.

Miami announced the club has waived Jamree Kromah.

Kromah, 26, joined the Dolphins late last month after he was claimed off waivers.

The Bears had let him go after they traded for defensive tackle Jeffrey Simmons.

Kromah appeared in two games for Miami, playing two defensive snaps and 10 special teams snaps.

He could return to Miami's roster on the practice squad if he clears waivers.
