Standout rookies Cameron Boozer, Yaxel Lendeborg battle in Grizzlies-Warriors game

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Cameron Boozer and the Memphis Grizzlies earned a dominant victory over Yaxel Lendeborg and the Golden State Warriors on Thursday, Nov. 19.

Memphis outscored Golden State 71-51 in the second half in a 126-98 victory at the FedEx Forum.

Boozer, the third overall pick in the 2026 draft, finished with 16 points, six rebounds and five assists in 27 minutes of play. He went 6-for-6 from the field and 4-for-4 from the free-throw line. His fourth made field goal was a pull-up jumper with the shot clock winding down. That make drew a reaction from his father, former NBA star Carlos Boozer, who was sitting courtside.

Lendeborg, the 11th pick in the draft, produced 11 points, eight rebounds and seven assists in 33 minutes for the Warriors. The forward struggled from the field, making just three of his 11 shots.

The Warriors have been playing without Stephen Curry, who suffered an oblique abdominal tear in the first quarter against the Houston Rockets on Nov. 11.

Entering Thursday, Boozer and Lendeborg were cited by the NBA as being the first pair of rookies to average 16-plus points, 7-plus rebounds and 3-plus assists per game through their first 15 games since Larry Bird and Magic Johnson during the 1979-80 season.
