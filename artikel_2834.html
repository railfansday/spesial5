Eagles roster news includes Dontayvion Wicks and Zack Baun to injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Philadelphia Eagles officially announced five roster moves ahead of the team's Week 11 home game against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

WR Dontayvion Wicks and LB Zack Baun were placed on injured reserve.

RB Will Shipley was activated from injured reserve to the roster.

DT Gabe Hall and LB Anfernee Orji were temporarily elevated to the roster.

Note: teams are permitted to temporarily elevate up to two practice squad players per game. A player can be elevated up to three times in the regular season and an unlimited number of times in the postseason.

Let's sort through this news on a player-by-player basis.

Wicks hasn't played since suffering an ankle injury in Week 6. It was looking like he might return to the field this weekend after being limited in practice on Wednesday and Thursday. But then he was limited again on Friday and the Eagles added a foot injury description in addition to his ankle injury on their injury report.

By going on IR now, Wicks (and Baun) are ineligible to play in the Eagles' next four games:

The Eagles are getting DeVonta Smith back at receiver on Sunday, which is a positive development for the offense. But it would've been ideal to get both Smitty and Wicks back on the field together to help out Joe Milton.

Instead, the Eagles will move forward with Smith, Darius Cooper, Makai Lemon, Elijah Moore, and Britain Covey as their receivers for now.

Baun had a strong game in Week 9 before unexpectedly appearing on the injury report coming out of the bye. Baun did not practice this week due to a back issue. Now he'll miss at least the next four games … and maybe more if the Eagles decide to shut him down in a lost season.

The Eagles will move forward with Jihaad Campbell and Jeremiah Trotter Jr. as their starting linebackers. Campbell's had more downs than ups in his second season while Trot Jr. has eaten into his playing time. Now we'll get an extended look at both young players.

The Eagles opened Shipley's 21-day practice window to return from IR earlier this week. He was limited in practice on Wednesday and Thursday before missing Friday's session due to illness.

It seems like Shipley is not yet 100%, so, his role against the Steelers might be fairly limited. But the Eagles needed some more running back depth behind Saquon Barkley since Jaydon Blue is the only other backfield option on the roster.

In the aftermath of the Jordan Davis trade, the Eagles decided to temporarily elevate Hall to have more defensive tackle depth. This game will mark Hall's first regular season snaps since Week 6 of the 2025 season.

With Baun going on IR and Smael Mondon ruled doubtful to play against Pittsburgh, the Eagles were in need of linebacker depth. And so they temporarily elevated Orji to be the top (and only) backup behind Jihaad Campbell and Trot Jr.

A little interesting to see the Eagles went with Orji over the recently re-signed Chance Campbell, who was previously elevated earlier this week.

The Steelers downgraded pass rusher Nick Herbig from questionable to out, as expected. He didn't practice this week.

Pittsburgh also notably activated starting receiver Roman Wilson from injured reserve but kept Michael Pittman Jr. on IR.
