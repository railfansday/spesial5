Eagles snap counts: What happens after Jordan Davis trade?

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Eagles snap counts: What happens after Jordan Davis trade?  originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

In his final game as a Philadelphia Eagle, Jordan Davis logged 38 snaps (57%) against the Giants in Sunday's loss. As he has been for most of the season, Davis was third among the Eagles' defensive tackles in playing time.

Davis on Monday was traded to the Jacksonville Jaguars for a 2027 first-round pick and a 2029 second-round pick.

Here's how those DT snaps broke down on Sunday in the 19-11 loss to the Giants:

Jalen Carter: 58 snaps (87%)Moro Ojomo: 42 snaps (63%)Jordan Davis: 38 snaps (57%)Byron Young: 16 snaps (24%)Ty Robinson: 9 snaps (13%)

In the first half of the season, Davis was third among the defensive tackles in snaps at 52%, while Carter has played 84% and Ojomo has played 60%. Davis played a total of 323 snaps in nine games, which is an average of around 36 snaps per game. Those snaps will need to be made up by others.

It's hard to imagine Carter playing more than he already has been this season and maybe Ojomo could play a bit more. But it seems like Young and Robinson will definitely see more playing time. That is intriguing with Robinson, who is a second-year fourth-round pick who has played just 61 defensive snaps all season. It's time to see what the young defensive tackle can do and whether or not he has a chance to be in the rotation in 2027.

The Eagles also have Gabe Hall and Zion Wilson on the practice squad. It's possible we see one or both of them at times the rest of 2026.

• The Eagles' defense played 67 snaps and Cooper DeJean, Drew Mukuba and Quinyon Mitchell played all of them. The defense was good on Sunday but Mukuba didn't have his best game.

• Zack Baun played 32 snaps (48%) before he left the game with a back injury. Before that, Baun was all over the field; he had 7 tackles and 2 sacks. Jeremiah Trotter Jr. played 64 snaps (96%) and Smael Mondon Jr. played 26 snaps (39%). Remember, Jihaad Campbell missed this game with injuries.

• At edge rusher, Nolan Smith led the way with 44 snaps (66%), followed by Jonathan Greenard – 36 snaps (54%), Jalyx Hunt – 29 snaps (43%) and Arnold Ebiketie – 25 snaps (37%). Greenard got his first sack of the season and Smith got his fifth.

• Without Michael Carter II (abdomen), Marcus Epps took back his safety job and played 43 snaps. Kenny Moore was the dime player with 7 snaps.

• Joe Milton made his first NFL start. Milton and his full offensive line played 70 snaps. Milton was sacked seven times by a team that entered the game with nine all season.

• Saquon Barkley (hamstring/back) looked a little healthier on Sunday but it didn't help. He played 41 of 70 snaps (59%) but had just 22 yards on 13 carries. Elijah Mitchell played 29 snaps (41%) and made some bad mistakes in the fourth quarter. Jaydon Blue was the third running back and didn't see any time on offense.

• Without DeVonta Smith (hamstring) and Dontayvion Wicks (ankle) for the third straight game, both Makai Lemon and Darius Cooper played 65 of 70 snaps (93%), followed by Hollywood Brown – 35 snaps (50%) and Elijah Moore – 26 snaps (37%). Lemon led the team with 6 catches for 61 yards but he also had a couple of costly drops.

• At tight end, Dallas Goedert played 57 of 70 snaps (81%), followed by Johnny Mundt – 8 snaps (11%), Grant Calcaterra – 7 snaps (10%) and Eli Stowers – 7 snaps (10%). It'll be interesting to see if Goedert gets moved today. Either way, Stowers needs to play more going forward.

• The Eagles used Fred Johnson as an extra blocker on 8 snaps and used Willie Lampkin as a fullback on 2 snaps. Let's see some more of Lampkin. Why not?
