Will Rams keep using two kickers?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Los Angeles Rams have been using Brandon McManus for field goals and Josh Karty for kickoffs, and how much longer will Sean McVay keep doing this? The Rams have the bye week to make changes, or keep rolling with the two kickers. Using two kickers is uncommon, but whatever works.

McManus has been perfect on field goals and extra points since joining the Rams, while Karty has been solid when his "dirty kicks" work, but Karty has also had some penalties on kickoffs giving opponents excellent starting position. Karty has been inconsistent throughout his career.

It's been interesting to see the Rams use two kickers, but is it something they'll keep doing or will the experiment be shutdown after the bye week? It's a storyline worth watching.

"After losing Blake Grupe to injury, the Rams didn't stop by adding Brandon McManus, their third kicker this season. They continued by re-signing Joshua Karty. Karty was released last season when his 'knuckle kickoff' attempts tarnished his field-goal accuracy.His return was meant to optimize LA's defensive field position on kickoffs, with McManus used exclusively for field goals and extra points. Unfortunately, the results of Karty's kickoffs over three games are less than encouraging.

The risk was worth taking. Deploying Karty exclusively for his touchback-inducing knuckleball, thus spotting opposing offenses at the 20-yard line, is certainly innovative thinking"
