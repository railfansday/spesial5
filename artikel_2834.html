Ramona Shelburne doubles down on inaccurate Lakers take

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Ramona Shelburne gave her honest thoughts on the Lakers this season.

"It's easy to find your way when you're playing one of the easiest schedules in the league," Shelburne said on ESPN.

Luka Doncic (77) and Austin Reaves (15) of the Los Angeles Lakers look on during a game against the Toronto Raptors on Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, in Los Angeles. NBAE via Getty Images

While Los Angeles' 12-2 record hasn't come by defeating all the NBA's best teams, the Lakers' early strength of schedule ranks around 14th to 16th. That means the cumulative strength of schedule is around league average, not one of the easiest as Shelburne implied.

NBA on ESPN's post of Shelburne's take was not received well by many as a Community Note was added, stating: "Analyses rank the Lakers' played schedule near average or slightly easier than league average in strength, not one of the easiest; Spurs and Memphis rank easier per opponent records and models."

Shelburne chose to defend her take the following day and went on an unfiltered rant on ESPN's radio show Tuesday morning.

"I don't think people understand what it's like to be a female reporter," Shelburne said. "It's an objective thing here that they have not played any of the top teams in the West yet."

Shelburne's assessment is correct; the Lakers have not played a juggernaut like the Spurs or Rockets. But as the saying goes, you can only beat what's in front of you.

The Lakers' early-season schedule has featured more teams above .500 than the Spurs have faced. and LA has won more of those games.

But fans were quick to criticize Shelburne.

"ummmm what…??? this is not a male/female thing

"You were factually wrong, got a community note, just apologize and move on," another user wrote on X.

For many, the NBA insider's Lakers reporting has drawn skepticism in recent years.

ESPN's Ramona Shelburne attends The Annual espnW: Women + Sports Summit day 1 at The Lodge at Torrey Pines on October 18, 2021. Getty Images

Fans have been critical of her MVP voting history, including her past ballots in which she ranked Luka Doncic at No. 4. In 2023, she placed Jalen Brunson at No. 2 over Doncic in the fourth spot.

ESPN's John Ireland said Shelburne is an apologist for the Clippers, which could contribute to her seemingly one-sided opinion on their crosstown rivals.

"You are an apologist for the Clippers," Ireland told Shelburne last December on ESPN LA. "And you're not the only one."

Los Angeles Lakers' Austin Reaves shrugs during the first half of an NBA basketball game against the Milwaukee Bucks, Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, in Milwaukee. AP Photo/Aaron Gash

"For whatever reason, the media is in love with the [Clippers] front office," Ireland said.

Shelburne remains a brilliant writer and respected NBA insider, but her reporting on the Lakers, at times, lights a fire under fans.
