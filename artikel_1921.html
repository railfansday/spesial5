Arsenal told who can challenge them for Premier League title after Man City lose again

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Jamie Redknapp has said Brighton, currently, are the only possible challengers to Arsenal for this season’s Premier League title.

The reigning Premier League champions opened up a six-point lead at the summit with a 1-0 win over Hull at the Emirates Stadium on Saturday, with captain Martin Odegaard netting the only goal of the game.

Manchester City had the chance to close the gap back to three points in the day’s late kick-off, but were beaten by Nottingham Forest at the City Ground.

Trailing 2-0 at the interval, Rayan Cherki’s excellent finish early in the second half gave Man City hope of a comeback before Liam Delap sealed victory for Forest with an emphatic finish to inflict a third straight Premier League defeat for Enzo Maresca’s side.

As such, Redknapp is of the opinion that the Gunners may just be too strong for the rest of the chasing pack.

"I don't really see anything else other than Arsenal running away with this title at the moment," Redknapp told Sky Sports. "I know it's really early but I just think they're so much better than everyone else in every single area.

"That one today is a sore one, it gives you the feeling right now in terms of who can challenge Arsenal? I just don't see one.”

Results on Saturday mean Arsenal will head into the November international break with a six-point cushion, unless Brighton beat Brentford on Sunday afternoon.

The Seagulls, who have beaten both Arsenal and Man City this season, are now being talked about as a potential title challenger due to their impressive start to the campaign.

Redknapp added. "If you look at the league table, I think right now, probably the one you'd point to, and I never thought I'd say this at the start of the year, but it's Brighton."
