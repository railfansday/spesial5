Leeds United icon and ex-Middlesbrough man Jonathan Woodgate follows Michael Carrick out of Manchester United

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Former Leeds United and Middlesbrough star Jonathan Woodgate has followed Michael Carrick out of the door at Manchester United.

The 46-year-old is a familiar face in Yorkshire having made over 100 appearances for both Leeds and Middlesbrough.

He established himself among Europe’s most exciting young talents during his Leeds days and later had three spells on the books at Middlesbrough.

Woodgate also had a spell as Middlesbrough boss and was a first-team coach during Carrick’s reign at the Riverside.

The pair were reunited at Old Trafford in January, but has now departed in the wake of Carrick’s dismissal.

As reported by the BBC, Woodgate has moved on along with fellow coaches Steve Holland, Jonny Evans, Travis Binnion and Craig Mawson.

Reports have indicated USA head coach and ex-Tottenham Hotspur boss Mauricio Pochettino is being lined up to take the Red Devils reins.

A club statement confirming Carrick’s exit read: “Manchester United can confirm that Michael Carrick has departed his role as men’s first-team head coach.

“Michael rejoined the club in January and guided the team to a third-placed Premier League finish by winning 12 out of the remaining 17 games of the 2025/26 season, accumulating the highest points tally in the league during that period.

“After a disappointing start to the season, the club has come to the extremely difficult conclusion that a change is necessary in order to achieve our ambitions.

“Michael is, and always will be, a Manchester United legend. Just as he did as a player, he has conducted himself with the highest standards of dedication and professionalism throughout his time back with the club. His leadership and achievements last season will always be fondly remembered.

“The club intends to appoint a permanent successor as quickly as possible.”
