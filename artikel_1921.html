'Let Fleetwood be the moment we come together and turn the page' - Crawley Town owners' battle cry to fans ahead of vital League Two clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Reds, who have confirmed Kevin Blackwell as boss until the end of the season, are currently bottom of League Two, six points adrift of safety but with three games in hand.

Every game is important from now on but the owners, in an open letter to fans, see Saturday’s game at the Broadfield Stadium as ‘an important moment’ for the club.

They want to draw a line under what has happened so far this season and move on, starting with three points and their first home win of the season.

In the open letter, they said: “Our upcoming fixture against Fleetwood is an important moment for this football club. We want to use it as an opportunity to draw a line under the difficult start to our season and begin a new chapter, together. Fill the stands, make your voices heard and get behind Kevin and the players from the very first whistle.

“We know the difference a united crowd can make, particularly when a team needs confidence and belief.”

Blackwell’s side still have 34 games left this season, so there is still plenty of time to turn things around, and they believe that can happen for the Reds.

They added: “Let Fleetwood be the moment we come together, turn the page and start building the momentum that will define the rest of our season: There is still a long way to go, plenty of football to be played and everything to fight for. We have absolute belief in our ability to turn our fortunes around.

“We remain as committed and determined today as we were on the day we took ownership of this Football Club. Our ambition has not changed, and neither has our belief in what Crawley Town can become. There will undoubtedly be further challenges along the way, but we are determined to face them with honesty, resilience and a shared sense of purpose.

“To every supporter who has continued to turn up, travel, sing and stand by the team during this difficult period, thank you. Your commitment does not go unnoticed or unappreciated.”

And in his first interview following the news he had been appointed for the remainder of the season, Blackwell told his players to match the fans’ passion and desire.

"It's our club, isn't it? Crawley Town is our club. I've said to the players, you've got to fight every minute that you're out on that pitch. You're not just playing for yourself, you're playing for your family, you're playing for a future, you're playing for the fans. Every one of those fans would love to get off that terrace and be on that green base and play.

“You've got to match that desire that the fans have got. If you do that, the fans will back you, even if you don't win the games. But if you're not seen to be giving everything you've got, I'll let them know, never mind the fans.”
