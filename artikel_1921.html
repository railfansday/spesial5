Mourinho offers Endrick hope to Milan: “Real Madrid are more important than him”

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Real Madrid head coach Jose Mourinho gave a strong response when asked about the potential that Endrick could leave.

In the past few days, it has been reported that Endrick could leave Los Blancos in the January transfer window. Not only that, but Real Madrid are apparently opening up to the idea of a permanent sale, with sources in Brazil stating that Milan are ready to offer a €70m package to get him.

There have also been indications that the Spanish giants want to include a buy-back clause of €80m (as they did with Como for Nico Paz in the summer), but in amongst all of this Mourinho seems to be the one opposing any kind of exit for the 20-year-old.

Mourinho spoke at a press conference and he was asked about about the future of Endrick, admitting that the decision to let him leave may come from his superiors. MilanNews relayed his response.

“I love Endrick, and Real Madrid are more important than him. We need Endrick here and Real Madrid coach needs Endrick here,” he began.

“This is the answer to those who write things and agents. Then if the club decides other things, I respect that. But for me, as Real Madrid coach, Endrick is important. I need him.”

The ‘agents’ that Mourinho is referring to could well be Endrick’s entourage, who have given comments suggesting that a move is being explored, while they reportedly visited Milano in recent days too.
