Nothing had been heard of Eddie Howe and Jason Tindall since their official departure from Newcastle United at the end of July.

Apart that is from endless speculation about where the duo might end up next.

Manchester United of course recently speculated as a likely destination for Eddie Howe (and presumably Jason Tindall and most of the rest of Howe’s NUFC backroom team).

Mauricio Pochettino has of course taken on that particular poisoned chalice at Man U (and got off to a great start at Anfield!).

The radio silence on Eddie Howe ended at the weekend and we covered it on The Mag. The former NUFC boss having moved back down to the south coast and Southampton manager Tonda Eckert revealed that Eddie Howe had visited the Saints’ training ground and they’d had a chat.

This has now quickly been followed by a school making public that both Eddie Howe and Jason Tindall have paid them a visit.

The pair visiting Bromley High School GDST, with the school giving a rundown of what happened on the day.

The school stating: ‘Bromley High School students were treated to an unforgettable day of top flight sport as former Newcastle United Manager Eddie Howe and Assistant Manager Jason Tindall visited the school to deliver an exclusive football masterclass and an inspiring Q&A session.’

A few snippets from their report of the day:

‘When asked what defines a great leader, Howe emphasised that leadership begins with personal standards rather than titles. He encouraged students to focus on everyday habits, such as being the first to arrive, the last to leave and treating peers with kindness and integrity. Howe told the students, noting that trustworthiness and consistency are what truly earn the respect of teammates and classmates. Tindall echoed this sentiment, adding that genuine leadership means supporting others and leading through actions rather than words.’

Navigating Pressure and Building Resilience

‘Students asked how top-flight managers handle team selection, media scrutiny, and setbacks. Howe explained that he and Tindall rely on honest, consistent processes, focusing on training effort and tactical preparation rather than outside noise. He also praised Tindall’s loyalty and behind-the-scenes support, highlighting how strong partnerships rely on trust and unity even when difficult decisions are made.

Discussing how athletes bounce back from mistakes, both coaches stressed the importance of blocking out social media negativity and leaning on trusted support networks, coaches, teammates, family and friends. Howe shared how breaking challenges down into simple, achievable steps helps players regain confidence, while Tindall noted how achieving major milestones can fuel lasting self-belief.’

‘In response to a question about player development, Howe shared examples of how dedication and adaptability pay off at the highest level, from players staying after training every day to perfect their technical skills to others successfully transitioning into entirely new positions on the pitch. Howe told the students natural talent must always be matched by a relentless work ethic and coachability.

The duo also highlighted the vital role of the wider squad, noting that a positive attitude and commitment behind the scenes are what truly drive team culture, drawing a direct parallel to school sports teams.’
