‘It was difficult to leave’ - Leeds United icon Pablo Hernandez makes admission over Elland Road exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Leeds United icon Pablo Hernandez has conceded it was “difficult” to end his lengthy association with the Whites in 2021.

The 41-year-old is a beloved figure in LS11 having spent five years dazzling supporters with his trickery and eye for a killer pass.

He was a crucial figure in the club’s Championship title-winning 2019/20 campaign and later represented Leeds in the Premier League.

Now retired and the head coach of his hometown club Castellon in Spain’s second tier, former playmaker Hernandez has discussed his Leeds exit in an interview with the Daily Star.

He said: “It was difficult to leave Leeds. Maybe I could have played another year at Leeds.

“I don't know what would have happened if I'd stayed, but I think whenever I've made a decision in my career, I've always believed it was the right one.”

Hernandez ended his playing career back in his native Spain with Castellon, who he is now leading on a charge up the Segunda Division from the dugout. The club sit second, with only Almeria above them in the rankings.

Hernandez said: “I think everybody in England, and probably around the world, believes Leeds should be playing in the Premier League. For me, they're one of the best clubs in England.

“I feel the same way about Castellon. I think we have some of the best fans in Spain, and we deserve the opportunity to play in La Liga again and show that we're a big club.

“Leaving Castellon for Valencia [as a youngster] was a difficult decision. But at that time, the club didn't have the best owners or the best organisation, particularly within the academy. But I always had the feeling that one day I would come back to Castellon.”
