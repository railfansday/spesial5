Rochester man going to prison for defrauding millions of dollars from lending companies and small businesses using dozens of stolen ID’s

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

U.S. Attorney Michael DiGiacomo announced today that Paul Paredes, 54, of Rochester, NY, who was convicted of wire fraud and aggravated identity theft, was sentenced to serve 87 months in prison by Chief U.S. District Judge Elizabeth A. Wolford. Paredes was also ordered to pay restitution totaling $8,754,446.64 to 57 victims.
