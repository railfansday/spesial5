The world has been waiting with bated breath for the announcement of who will be the next actor to take on the role of James Bond. The rumors have been swirling for months over who would take on the role in the upcoming Denis Villeneuve-directed Bond flick. They've really heated up in the last few weeks as auditions and screen tests have been underway for the upcoming 26th installment of the Bond franchise. Now we know, and it turns out it’s one of the biggest names in Hollywood: Robert Pattinson.

I’m actually a little shocked by this cast, but I’m not mad about it. I was leaning into the Jack Lowden or Callum Turner casting rumors, because I actually didn’t think it would be a star as big as Pattinson stepping into the shoes of the eight men who have previously played Bond. Pattinson has a huge year on the 2026 movie schedule, and this ensures the next couple of years will be busy too, after the announcement was made on social media this morning.

Of course, Pattinson is no stranger to iconic roles. Filming is (hopefully) coming to a close soon on The Batman Part II, in which Pattinson is reprising his role as the Caped Crusader in the second installment of the Matt Reeves Batman franchise. Production on that film has been a slow go, with Reeves recently stepping aside temporarily and Steven Soderbergh joining to finish the principal photography.

Major franchises are also nothing new for Pattinson. Not only did he have his breakout in the Twilight franchise, but he's also joined the cast of Dune: Part III, which is coming to theaters in about a month on what many are calling "DunesDay," as it coincides with the release of Avengers: Doomsday. It is the culmination of the Dune trilogy, also directed by Villeneuve. It certainly bodes well for the next Bond movie that the two clearly have a good working relationship.

I do think the choice of Pattinson to play 007 will come with some controversy, but that’s to be expected. There was never a chance that Amazon/MGM, who own the Bond franchise now, were going to avoid it, no matter who they chose. Pattinson is probably the biggest star to ever be cast in the role for the first time, rivaling when Pierce Brosnan got the role way back in 1994 before GoldenEye. The fact that he is such a big star has caused some blowback, but I think it was to be expected now that Amazon controls the franchise. They are going to invest a lot of money in this. A safe choice of a big star was probably inevitable.

There is still a lot to learn about Bond 26, like who will be the next "Bond Girl" and the rest of the characters we all love, like M and Q. We’ll be busy keeping you updated on all the developments.
