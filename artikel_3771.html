Black Friday deals are officially due to land in just under two weeks, but with Walmart, Best Buy and GameStop (with Amazon not too far behind) dropping early PS5 and Nintendo Switch offers already there's plenty to dive into.

In truth, this sales period has been stretched out to last a little over a month. With Amazon's Big Deal Days firing the starting pistol on holiday sales every year, we often see retailers rotating through doorbuster deals and early Black Friday sales well ahead of Thanksgiving itself.

So what's out there right now? We're still waiting for Nintendo's official Switch offers (they land on November 22), but older games and third party accessories are already seeing record-low prices. Meanwhile, PS5 fans can enjoy savings on some of our favorite controllers, games and more.

My top five early Black Friday deals for PS5, Nintendo Switch, Xbox, and PC players spans rare discounts on tech that sits at the top of our recommendation lists (with a little Control: Resonant thrown in for good measure). We're not stopping there, though, I'm still scrolling through all of this week's early offers to find you the biggest sales live right now.

I've been tracking Black Friday deals for over seven years now, tracing timelines and watching prices rise and fall in the lead up to the year's biggest sales event. I'm also watching those prices across the rest of the year, though, while going hands-on with the tech set to hit the hardest when discounts launch. That means I know which PS5, Switch, Xbox and PC gadgets are actually worth your time and how much you should be paying for them this November.

The official DualSense has returned to its regular Black Friday pricing this week, well ahead of the official event. You'll find the $74.99 gamepad down at $54.99 across the web right now, but if you're going pro it's also worth noting the Edge model is seeing a rare price cut back to $169 as well.

It's been incredibly difficult to find any savings on 2026's Control Resonant since it launched, but Remedy's latest adventure is seeing a record-breaking $10 discount in Best Buy's early Black Friday deals this week. The $59.99 release has sailed down to $49.99.

A super cheap headset going for even less than usual. The Turtle Beach Airlite Fit is one of our favorite sets of drivers on the market right now, and it's worth celebrating even at its usual $17-$18 sales price. We're down to a new record-low $14.99 at Amazon ahead of Black Friday, though.

Yet to work out whether you love or hate Marvel's Wolverine? The audience-splitted Insomniac adventure has stuck to its MSRP since launch earlier this year, but both Amazon and PS Direct have it for $20 off ahead of Black Friday. That's a drop from $69.99 to $49.97 (in Amazon's case, Sony is a few pennies over), making it cheaper than ever before.

Where do we stand? We gave it a 3/5 stars in our own review. That hacking and slashing runs well, with a nice amount of variety to combat and story-pushing mechanics. Inconsistency and a messy story do hold it back though.

The Nacon Revolution X Unlimited has soared to the top of our Xbox recommendation list, and Amazon's 25% discount couldn't have come at a better time. We awarded the pro controller 4.5/5 stars in our review - for those after an all-rounder, it doesn't get better than the premium feel, additional functionality, and wealth of extra buttons here.

Alternatives like the (today slightly cheaper) Victrix Pro BFG Reloaded, have the edge in competitive shoot-outs (that one's down to $129.99 at Amazon, more on that in a moment). With a far sturdier build feel, adjustable grip weights, and a reassuring approach to generalist play (this thing prioritizes comfort just as much as it does speed, which shouldn't be taken for granted), though, Nacon still wins early Black Friday deals.

Switch 2 players, I'm talking to you. The CRKD Nitro Deck 2 finally launched in August this year, bringing adjustable capacitive thumbsticks, four back buttons, and a chunky ergonomic shell to the handheld experience. We rated it 4/5 stars in our review, with that $99.99 price tag contributing heavily to its missing full marks.

Amazon has rectified that problem once in its early Black Friday deals over the last few weeks. In my experience, these kinds of savings typically return once the heat is on, so it's worth keeping a close eye on the numbers here.
