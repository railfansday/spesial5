Bitcoin is grinding through one of its deepest drawdowns in years, yet Mark Zuckerberg’s Meta AI predicts the setup forming on higher timeframes is starting to look like the kind of multi-year base that has historically preceded the most violent upside expansions.

The next major move for BTC could be sharper than many expect. Since retreating from its October 2025 peak above $126,000, it has spent recent months forming a structure that increasingly resembles a cycle bottom.

The slide into the mid-$50,000s has flushed out leverage, while several indicators suggest selling pressure may be fading: declining volume on successive lower lows, repeated holds at long-term support, and a potential higher low taking shape on the weekly chart.

In previous cycles, similar conditions have accompanied the shift from capitulation to accumulation. Bitcoin is now testing a multi-year support cluster that has held through earlier bear markets. That strengthens the case for a developing bottom, though it does not yet confirm that the worst is over.

The hypothetical landmark catalyst that could ignite the next leg is a coordinated sovereign and institutional pivot that effectively turns Bitcoin into a formal strategic reserve asset globally.

Imagine a scenario in which a group of major economies, facing mounting sovereign debt pressures and currency instability, announce simultaneous allocations of several percent of their foreign reserves into Bitcoin.

This would be paired with the formal establishment of a large U.S. Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and the inclusion of BTC in the official reserve frameworks of multiple central banks.

Such a move would instantly reframe Bitcoin from a speculative risk asset into a core monetary reserve, triggering forced buying from every institution that tracks reserve composition and creating a supply shock of historic proportions.

This narrative shift aligns with a textbook post-capitulation expansion. The weekly and monthly charts show Bitcoin having completed a deep, high-volume correction that has reset momentum oscillators to levels last seen at previous major bottoms.

Once price reclaims the major descending trendline that has capped the post-2025 decline and establishes a series of higher highs above the $80,000–$90,000 region, measured-move projections and Fibonacci extensions from the entire 2022–2026 base open the door to an aggressive revaluation.

In prior cycles, the move out of similar multi-year bases produced multiples far beyond what linear forecasts anticipated. A confirmed breakout under the catalyst described above would likely accelerate that process, turning the current base into the launchpad for a vertical advance through 2027.

Bitcoin Hyper aims to combine Bitcoin’s liquidity and established investor base with transaction speeds that meet the needs of modern decentralized applications. Key features include near-instant transaction finality and low costs, both crucial to its market positioning.
