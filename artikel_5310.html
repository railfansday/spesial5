5 superhero teams appear in the Avengers: Doomsday final trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Five factions appear in the final Avengers: Doomsday trailer, from the X-Men to Wakanda and Talokan. See what each brings to the alliance.

Superhero crossovers once meant a single team assembling, but Avengers: Doomsday puts many teams side by side, with heroes drawn from different films, studios and even universes. The crowd is so large that one of its founding heroes cannot place half of it. "I don't know what half of you do. I don't even know who half of you are. Put aside your petty squabbles. The time has come. Team up and stand together," Thor says in the final trailer, as reported by Screen Rant. The greater danger is less any single villain than the chance that rival factions cannot fight as one.

Marvel is betting that audiences want every thread pulled together at once, and fans have already shown that appetite. When Avengers: Endgame returned to theaters as the Encore re-release, fans around the globe paid another $143 million to see it, according to Variety. Fans watched the Doomsday trailer 503 million times in its first 24 hours, according to Yahoo Entertainment. Every faction Marvel adds comes with its own fans, each with a reason to buy a ticket, and that explains why the studio packs in every team it can.

Ars Technica identified the superhero factions that appear in the Avengers: Doomsday trailer. They range from the veteran mutants of Fox's earlier X $TWTR-Men films to the twin kingdoms of Wakanda and Talokan and a Fantastic Four from another universe. The final trailer is the last look audiences get before the film opens in theaters Dec. 18, and the question is not only who shows up but what each faction brings to a fragile alliance.

The Avengers gave the whole franchise its name, and that is why the condition of this team matters more than any other in Avengers: Doomsday. The Avengers of Earth-616 rank first among the factions in the trailer, according to Ars Technica, yet the man who anchors them had to be fetched. Yahoo Entertainment reports that the trailer shows the Fantastic Four arriving at Steve Rogers's home to recruit him. The core that greets him is a reunion of old partners rather than a team that kept working together, pairing Steve with Sam Wilson and with Bucky Barnes, who last appeared in Thunderbolts in 2025.

Steve had found the ending he wanted, and the film reopens it on purpose. He has a teenage son with Peggy Carter, Yahoo Entertainment reports, and in the trailer the boy apparently doesn't know his father was a superhero. "We sort of played one beat past where Steve Rogers's story ended with Peggy Carter," codirector Anthony Russo said. That makes Steve a symbol the whole alliance can rally to, and also the member with the most reason to walk away.

Even inside the core team, the old roles have changed hands. Sam Wilson holds the title of Captain America, and Steve returns to lead the team beside him, with Thor in the ranks as well. The team that carries the franchise name now has two men with a claim to lead it.

The Avengers bring the weight of history to this crowd, but they are leading an alliance before they have finished rebuilding their own team.

Marvel is bringing an entire team from another film series into Doomsday rather than borrowing one familiar face for a cameo. The veteran mutants from the earlier X $TWTR-Men movies return with their shared history intact, according to Ars Technica. Patrick Stewart again plays Prof. Charles Xavier and Ian McKellen again plays Magneto, Variety reports, and audiences have connected those two actors with the series more than anyone else in its cast. "Original" here means the first roster of that film series, the actors who defined these mutants on screen. What this faction adds to the crowd is instant recognition.

The X-Men also arrive as a full squad rather than a few stray members. James Marsden is back as Cyclops, and Kelsey Grammer returns as Beast, Rebecca Romijn as Mystique and Alan Cumming as Nightcrawler. Alongside the six returning actors, Marvel added Channing Tatum as Gambit, the one newcomer on the team. A team with this much history together can fight as a unit from its first scene, but mutants who have long stood together may look to one another before they look to an Avenger.

The X-Men arrive as the most complete team in the film, already assembled under their own banner, and that is why they will be the hardest to absorb into anyone else's.

The Fantastic Four of Earth-828 come to Avengers: Doomsday from a different universe than the Avengers of Earth-616. The family of heroes leaves its own universe to warn the others that worlds are about to collide, according to Ars Technica. That warning is the reason they belong in this crowd. The Avengers learn how large the threat is from a team none of them grew up with. Before any alliance can form, they have to trust strangers.

The Fantastic Four deliver their warning in person. In the trailer, Screen Rant reports, the team reaches Steve's home, he leaves his family behind to go with them, and Thor arrives alongside them. In a crowd that is short on trust, heroes from another universe are doing the work of assembling the Avengers.

Even the location of Steve's home remains uncertain. Screen Rant writer Simon Gallagher places it on Earth-828, the Fantastic Four's own universe. That placement is his reading of the trailer and not a confirmed plot point. If he is right, the Fantastic Four are familiar figures in that household, because Steve's son recognizes them, and they would be neighbors calling on a retired hero, not strangers carrying news.

Whatever their motive, the Fantastic Four are turning a warning into a roster, and the open question is whether a crowd of rivals will follow a team from outside their world.

Marvel is putting its newest team into the crossover almost as soon as that team exists. The Thunderbolts, now billed as the New Avengers, are one of the five factions in the final trailer, according to Ars Technica, and their roster includes Florence Pugh as Yelena, Wyatt Russell as U.S. Agent, David Harbour as Red Guardian, and Hannah John-Kamen as Ghost. Yahoo Entertainment reports that audiences have not seen the group since the Thunderbolts film, so the team has had little time to settle into its roles.

The name "New Avengers" places this group beside the original team rather than beneath it. The overlap reaches down to single heroes. The newcomers have their own Falcon, played by Danny Ramirez, Yahoo Entertainment reports, while Anthony Mackie's Sam Wilson stands with the core Avengers. The heroes on the field now answer to two groups that claim the same title.

The New Avengers add fresh faces and more fighters. They also raise an open question about which team the Avengers name now belongs to.

Wakanda and Talokan have the most crowded teaser of any Doomsday faction, even though the two kingdoms come fifth of the five factions, according to Ars Technica. ComicBook.com reports that their own teaser features five main characters, while the first two teasers showed two each and the X $TWTR-Men's showed three. A studio usually gives its fullest teaser to the heroes it expects audiences to care about. Marvel appears to be counting heavily on these kingdoms, whatever their place in the order.

Wakanda arrives with power divided between two people. Shuri fights as Black Panther, but M'Baku rules as king of Wakanda, according to ComicBook.com. The nation's champion and its ruler are now different people, and both a warrior and a king will have a say in what Wakanda commits to the alliance.

Talokan sends a delegation, and its ruler is the faction's biggest open question. Namor comes with Attuma at his side, and the Wakanda teaser, ComicBook.com reports, gives the first confirmation that Namora is in Doomsday. Two kingdoms in one faction give the alliance more fighters, and also more leaders whose aims have to line up. The idea of Namor siding with Doctor Doom remains speculation, and Doom does not appear in the Wakanda teaser.

The last faction shows how much a crowded alliance gains when it adds a kingdom, and how much the alliance depends on whether one ruler stays.
