XRP Ledger Loses 60% of Users

XRP Ledger's network activity has dropped sharply, losing around 60% of the users it had yesterday. The decline comes amid a serious price correction and increased pressure on the cryptocurrency market. Selling pressure remains high, and the declining number of users is not helping the price.

XRPScan's data showed 250,000 users 24 hours ago, while today's reading shows barely 92,000 users. While the drop is significant, it should not be considered critical on its own. The decline partly reflects a regular weekend slowdown, when traders are generally less active, but it comes alongside the price correction and broader selling pressure.

The highest point for this metric in November was 300,000, with a monthly average of approximately 200,000 active users. The number of users is a critical metric for the network's health, and a decline toward 100,000 often comes before bullish sentiment among investors. The current drop does not guarantee that sentiment will turn, especially as the price remains under pressure.

Apart from the negative on-chain trend, XRP's price performance has also been weak. The price lost roughly 5% from its November 19 close and even fell below the $0.90 price level, which was expected to serve as a foundation for recovery. XRP was around $0.915 on November 22 after closing at $0.9604 three days earlier.

The trend remains bearish, and the decline could become significantly worse.

Few expected a surge in market liquidations so soon, as Bitcoin fell below the $60,000 price level. XRP and other altcoins followed the broader market trend and suffered serious losses.

Interest in the network appears to be weakening as the price correction continues, so this drop in activity is not helping XRP's recovery.

A potential recovery above $0.9 could be the key to a quick rebound. However, the overall trend remains bearish and is unlikely to change unless significant market events occur.
