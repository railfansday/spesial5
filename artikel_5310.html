Village of Angel Fire Completes Smart Meter Upgrade with Sustainability Partners

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Smart water meters help the Village catch leaks sooner and bill more accurately

ANGEL FIRE, N.M. , Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Sustainability Partners (SP), a Public Benefit Company, has completed an Advanced Metering Infrastructure (AMI) water metering project with the Village of Angel Fire, New Mexico, delivered through Metering as a Service (MaaS). The project replaced the Village's water meters with smart meters, giving staff more timely, accurate water usage data across the entire system.

Through MaaS, SP funds the meters and facilitates their installation and long-term care, with no upfront capital from the Village. Angel Fire pays a monthly, usage-based service fee, like a utility bill. SP remains involved for the long term, coordinating preventative maintenance, repairs, replacements, and warranty issues to keep the system accurate and reliable.

Angel Fire is a mountain community in northern New Mexico, where reliable water infrastructure is essential to daily life, public safety, and long-term resilience. Like many aging systems, the Village's older meters were reaching the end of their useful life and could not always capture usage precisely or flag leaks quickly. In a drought-prone state like New Mexico, every gallon counts, and catching leaks early helps the community conserve water it cannot afford to lose.

"In a village our size, Metering as a Service just makes sense because it provides ongoing care for our water infrastructure," said Village of Angel Fire Mayor BJ Lindsey. "Having a partner like Sustainability Partners frees up resources that we can put back into the community."

With more timely data, Village staff can spot unusual usage and act on it sooner, instead of waiting for a high bill or a visible problem. Accurate meters also end estimated billing, and staff receive monthly reports on consumption trends, top users, and year-to-date usage.

The metering upgrade complements other recent improvements to the Village's water system, including upgraded booster pumps that have strengthened water pressure and fire suppression capacity, an important benefit in a mountain community with wildfire risk.

"Our job is to take the burden of essential infrastructure off of Angel Fire," said Jake Beebe, Sustainability Partners Chief Operating Officer. "By providing the upfront capital costs, the Village is free to put its dollars toward the priorities its residents care about most, while still making upgrades to their water system."

About Sustainability PartnersSustainability Partners is a Public Benefit Company that facilitates funding, deployment, and ongoing care of essential infrastructure to help states, municipalities, universities, schools, and hospitals meet their needs. Sustainability Partners can help solve any combination of funding, design, engineering, procurement, installation, and maintenance of essential infrastructure with no upfront costs. Like a utility, Sustainability Partners charges a monthly usage fee based on a month-to-month agreement.
