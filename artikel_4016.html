Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair Gets a PS5 Definitive Edition, Out This December

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Playtonic has announced Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair is being treated to a new Definitive Edition on PS5, and it's right around the corner.

The new version launches on 15th December 2026, and anyone who owns a digital PS4 version of the game gets a free upgrade. It appears anyone with a physical copy won't get the upgrade.

On base PS5 and PS5 Pro, the new edition will support a 4K resolution at 120fps, better lighting and ambient occlusion effects, higher-fidelity shadows, and two orchestrated soundtracks. Also, if you don't already own it, the Trowzer's Top Tonic Pack is automatically bundled in with the PS5 version.

One of the best platforming adventures of its generation is back, enhanced for PlayStation 5. Yooka & Laylee return for a full-scale 2.5D adventure, with a 3D Overworld that's an adventure in its own right, levels you can alter to create new ways to play, and Capital B's Impossible Lair waiting to test your skills.

A 2D and top-down side game that accompanies the main Yooka-Laylee game, the Impossible Lair went down really well upon its original launch. It's got an 82 rating on Metacritic, and in our own review, we called it an "improvement" on the original adventure.
