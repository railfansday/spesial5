Fishing Inc is Snapbreak's newest release for the fishing fanatics out there, now on iOS and Android

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Need to cast your reel? Don't have a fishing spot nearby? Well, then you're in luck because developer Snapbreak have just released their latest mobile game, and this time it's BigBonk Studios and their exciting new incremental fishing game, Fishing Inc, that's out now on iOS and Android!

In Fishing Inc, you, well, fish. No surprises there. But what sets it apart is the depth of content here, letting you fish for a huge variety of species with their own distinct behaviour, including legendary catches, all while travelling to different locations from small fishing holes to the vast blue ocean. You'll also have access to upgrades as you work your way up to bigger and better catches all across the world!

And, to be honest, Reber may not have been far off. Fishing games have always been popular, combining the excitement of increasingly bigger catches while also being surprisingly laid-back. While Fishing Inc is a little more on the fast-paced side, it still doesn't ask more of you as a player than simply casting your reel and bringing in big fish.

Coming packed with a huge variety of species to catch, locations to visit and plenty of upgrades to hunt, Fishing Inc looks to be a perfect new addition to the catalogue of any fishing enthusiast on mobile!
