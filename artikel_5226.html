U.S. Department of Energy Bets on XRP Ledger for Blockchain-Based Energy Market Settlements

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The DOE is exploring Ripple’s ledger for energy-market trade settlements, a development showing the protocol is becoming a serious contender in the RWA space.

The global financial world continues to move towards one of its biggest transformations – tokenization of RWAs. This has been further revealed by the U.S. Department of Energy’s decision to utilize the XRP protocol to build digital settlements for energy trades.

In a statement on its platform, the agency said it is building an energy settlement infrastructure on the XRPL.

The U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) is a government agency responsible for advancing America’s energy security through reliable, clean, and cost-effective energy. In an official communication, the federal department disclosed that it is using the XRP protocol to develop blockchain applications tailored to energy market needs.

As stated in the report, the entity is developing infrastructure to enable digital trade and settlement of electricity and energy transactions based on the XRPL. By using the protocol, the agency wants to improve the efficiency of energy transactions and charge settlements. It is utilizing the ledger to build on-chain utility for energy assets, linking electricity usage to RWA trades.

The development shows that XRPL is rapidly becoming the top blockchain choice for powering RWAs. This shows that rising interest in tokenization is no longer a fad. Big corporations are already developing systems that bridge TradFi with the on-chain world.

The DOE has joined several prominent corporations, believing in the capability of on-chain-based offerings. This demonstrates how TradFi offerings are being innovatively operated, traded, and managed on on-chain networks.

It’s clear evidence that Ripple’s ledger is becoming a vital settlement architecture for RWAs due to its seamless payment network, swift settlement, and affordable fees.

RWAs require strong ecosystems for swift, affordable transfers of utilities. Ripple’s architecture makes it convenient for such digital utilities and institutional capital transfers. Its robust liquidity bridge also makes it a top candidate, allowing utilities to transit effortlessly across various markets.
