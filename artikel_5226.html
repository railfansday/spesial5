Why Germany’s Top Stock Exchange Predicts $9 XRP

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Home » Ripple (XRP) » Why Germany’s Top Stock Exchange Predicts $9 XRP

Regulatory licensing momentum & XRPL’s intended role as core settlement infrastructure serve as key catalysts.

Some weeks ago, Germany’s Sparkassen savings bank, serving ~50 million customers with €1.62T in assets under management (AUM), began a phased roll-out of XRP trading in their banking apps. Now, a crew member of Oliver Michel of Tokentus Investment AG shared his personal bullish view on XRP price, setting a long-term target between $7 to $9.

On DER AKTIONÄR TV, the Frankfurt Stock Exchange’s affiliate dropped some knowledge in regards to Ripple’s long-term vision of revolutionizing & optimizing the classic financial system, as seen in recent examples. Just last month, Ripple inked a traditional trust bank license in the United States (USA), edging closer towards a full-scale XRPL implementation.

BREAKING 🚨: The largest stock exchange in Germany says that #XRP will be the backbone of the new financial system! He also says that XRP could hit $7-$9 pretty soon, and might even soar to over $100 once the system runs on XRPL! pic.twitter.com/ptED0SNEh5

Oliver Michel expresses confidence in XRP ecosystem long-term prospects, suggesting that their most significant developments could take some time to materialize. The daily XRP charts hint at a consolidation phase now, the analyst notes, but then he named a three-headed snake that could turn out as the pivotal catalyst in the bounce back rally.

As strongest points, he references an electronic money license and notes over 60 obtained licenses worldwide. Surely, a $9 price target for XRP would mean nearly tripling the current all-time high (ATH). Set at $3.65, the OG altcoin took a nosedive towards $1.20 earlier this week, but managed to stay afloat at the $1.24 major demand zone since November.

The intersection between traditional finance & digital assets could boost the global crypto market cap by billions, while DLT-based chains have an edge in terms of adoption due to the ISO 20022 compliance.
