XRP, ETH, DOGE: Tim Draper Predicts Bitcoin to Absorb Altcoins

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Billionaire and longtime Bitcoin (BTC) supporter Tim Draper predicts a future where Bitcoin absorbs the innovations of other altcoins.

Draper's statement has major implications for big cryptocurrencies like XRP, Ethereum (ETH) and Dogecoin (DOGE), which have built their own ecosystems and now compete in the wider cryptocurrency market.

In a recent post on X, Draper compared Bitcoin's rising dominance to Microsoft's path to becoming the dominant software company, as the company absorbed successful innovations from other businesses.

"Microsoft never invented the spreadsheet, word processor, or slide deck. They absorbed them," stated Draper.

The billionaire investor brought up pioneering software like Lotus 1-2-3 and WordPerfect, which eventually saw their dominance give way to Microsoft's Excel and Word.

Draper predicts that this could apply to the cryptocurrency industry, as Bitcoin could become the ultimate absorber of innovations from alternative blockchain networks.

"Altcoins are the Lotus 1-2-3s and WordPerfects of this time," wrote Draper.

Smaller cryptocurrencies are thus essentially incubators for technological innovations that could eventually become part of the bigger Bitcoin ecosystem, according to Draper.

"They show that it works, then the innovation that pulls the strongest gets ported to the Bitcoin platform," he added.

Draper also supported his statement by citing the rising market share of Bitcoin in the overall cryptocurrency market.

The billionaire investor recalled observing how Bitcoin dominance rose from about 40% to 50% and then even further to 61%.

"Bitcoin is that platform," said Draper, claiming that the biggest cryptocurrency could follow a path similar to Microsoft's.

This statement has implications for the future of big altcoins like Ethereum, XRP and Dogecoin.

Ethereum is the leading smart contract and dApp platform, while XRP is mainly known for its cross-border payments and the wider XRP Ledger ecosystem. Meanwhile, Dogecoin is one of the most iconic meme coins in the cryptocurrency market.

Still, the growing ecosystem around Bitcoin, which adds more scaling and programmability through solutions such as the Lightning Network and projects like Stacks, shows how Bitcoin is trying to expand beyond its basic use case as a cryptocurrency.
