Connie’s Garden in Griffin, Georgia

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Hi GPOD! Today we’re heading to Griffin, Georgia, for an update from Connie Raines. We first visited Connie’s garden shortly after she moved into her new home and transplanted many of her plants. Now, a little over a year later, she’s back with a few highlights from spring and late summer. Here’s Connie:

I have lived in this house and garden for a little over a year. When I moved here, I worked hard to transplant many of my plants, and the garden continues to be a work in progress.

Delphinium (Delphinium cvs., Zones 3–7) with coleus (Coleus scutellarioides, annual)

Native swamp rose mallow (Hibiscus moscheutos, Zones 5–9) and zinnias

Perennial begonia (Begonia grandis, Zones 6–9), a soft mahonia bush just planted this spring, and amaranth

A watermelon my husband grew in our garden area created by a new stepped retaining wall.

A work in progress… I transplanted this hearts-a-bustin’ (Euonymus americanus, Zones 6–9) from a different spot where I originally had it. It was crowded in and now will be able to spread out.

This celosia (Spicata group) actually grew from seeds of plants that were in a pot last summer. The ground in this area is where a tree used to be, and the roots are rotting and creating wonderful soft soil.

Thanks for sharing another update from your garden, Connie! And I have to know: What variety of watermelon did your husband grow? It’s huge and oblong! I hope it was delicious. Looking forward to the next update! 🙂
