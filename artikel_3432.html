Guillermo del Toro’s Near-Perfect Fantasy Masterpiece Officially Returns in 1 Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Guillermo del Toro is undeniably one of the most renowned horror directors of our time. While slashers, jump scares, and evil monsters (human or otherwise) rule the genre and are often fan-favorite tropes, del Toro brings a unique blend of gothic horror, dark fantasy, and fairy tales to his work, presenting them through a sympathetic, artistic lens. Features like the Jacob Elordi-led Frankenstein, Cronos, Crimson Peak, and more give audiences a unique look at monsters, who are usually the genre's antagonists.

Among such notable titles from del Toro is Pan’s Labyrinth, which seamlessly blends ruthless historical drama with a child’s imaginative fairytale. Set in post-Civil War fascist Spain, ten-year-old Ofelia moves with her pregnant mother to a remote mountain outpost run by her cruel stepfather, Captain Vidal. While Vidal ruthlessly hunts anti-fascist rebels, Ofelia discovers an ancient labyrinth and meets a mysterious Faun. Things take a turn when the Faun reveals she is the reincarnation of an underworld princess and assigns three perilous tasks to prove her immortality before the full moon. Navigating both fairy-tale monsters and wartime brutality, Ofelia succeeds until her final test demands her newborn brother’s blood.

The film explores themes of choice and disobedience through Ofelia’s character arc. Further, the narrative contrasts the brutality of totalitarianism with childhood imagination. As with every del Toro movie, life, death, and rebirth drive the narrative, unfolding each moment with equal wonder and horror. The feature won three Academy Awards that season and earned a 95% Rotten Tomatoes score from critics.

Four worlds and four completely different ideas of what a good life looks like. This is where you belong, what it would ask of you, and what you would get back in return.

You belong somewhere old enough that the mountains have names and somebody can still tell you who gave them. Middle-earth is for people who think the right thing is worth doing even when it will not be finished in their lifetime.

You want the sea, a crew, and absolutely nobody telling you where you are allowed to go. The Grand Line is the world for people whose main ambition in life is finding out what is over there.

You would rather be feared and useful than liked and useless. The Continent is a world where the monsters are the straightforward part of the job and the people are where it all gets complicated.

You want the myths to be real and loose in the actual world, and you want to find out at about thirteen that you were never anywhere near as ordinary as everybody kept telling you.

Now, fans who’d love to rewatch the movie or own a physical copy are in luck. The makers are releasing a 20th anniversary edition of Pan's Labyrinth, a 4K restoration overseen by del Toro, as per CBR. The physical release will come with new special features including a retrospective documentary, comments from Actors on Pan’s Labyrinth and Directors on Pan's Labyrinth, along with legacy bonus features, like The Power of the Myth Featurette, Pan and The Fairies, The Color and Shape Featurette, The Melody Echoes the Fairytale Featurette, 2007 Original Director Commentary, among others.

Pan’s Labyrinth 4K UHD and Blu-ray Collector's Edition Steelbook and Blu-ray Collector's Edition are releasing on December 1, and a 4K and Blu-ray Collector's Edition (non-Steelbook) will drop on December 29. Stay tuned to Collider for more such updates.
