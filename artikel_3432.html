A24’s 2026 Hidden Gem Officially Gets a Huge Boost at the Box Office

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A24 has had an extremely busy year, packed with several big box office and critical hits. Backrooms became one of 2026's most viral hits and made more than $400 million worldwide; the new James Bond, Robert Pattinson, earned an outside shout for a Best Actor Oscar in Primetime; Pattinson also delighted alongside Zendaya in The Drama; Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim combined for the stunning You Can See Everything; and much more.

One of A24's smallest gems of the year is Club Kid, with new filmmaker Jordan Firstman (Search Party, I Love LA) smashing it out of the park on his first try. The story of a party promoter who must take care of his 10-year-old son following a tragedy is both hilarious and utterly moving, and has earned some of the finest reviews of the year, with much of the praise awarded to Firstman and The Invite and The Studio cinematographer Adam Newport-Berra.

Club Kid has earned a near-perfect 98% rating on review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes, with critics hailing it "irresistible," "wildly beautiful," "deeply human," and "so well written it feels like real life." Now it is moving from critical success to the box office, as it looks to turn its reported budget of just under $5 million into a financial triumph, courtesy of positive word of mouth.

After its limited, six-location initial release, the film expanded to 27 locations last weekend, scoring an 87% boost in ticket sales and taking its domestic total to $1.4 million. The film's per-theater average of $28,000 last weekend was higher than any other film released in more than 10 locations at the North American box office right now.

Club Kid's per-theater average might be the best of last weekend, but which film finished top of the charts? That would be Ti West’s Ebenezer, the latest project based on Charles Dickens' iconic 1843 novella, and the Hollywood comeback of Johnny Depp, who last made a major Hollywood production in 2018's Fantastic Beasts: The Crimes of Grindelwald. The film scored a $42 million global haul on its opening weekend, with $19 million in domestic earnings.
