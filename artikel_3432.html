Robert Pattinson Officially Gets His Biggest Endorsement as the New James Bond

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Five years since No Time to Die brought an end to Daniel Craig's defining chapter as 007, his successor has finally been announced. Just a few weeks ago, it seemed as if the shortlist for the new Bond had been reduced to just six names: Josh O'Connor, Jack Lowden, Callum Turner, Jacob Elordi, Paul Mescal, and Harris Dickinson. Then reports of a secret, late-October screen test emerged, and a new name entered the race in earnest.

Now, that new name has defied the odds to take the role, as Robert Pattinson suits up to take down criminal masterminds in a brand-new era of the franchise. "James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime," Pattinson said in a statement about the casting, adding that "to say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team."

Many famous names have come out in support of Pattinson's casting, despite some backlash from fans online. But perhaps the most famous names — or names — to vocalize their appreciation of the news are the British Royal Family, who posted to their social media, "Welcome to His Majesty’s Secret Service, Mr Bond." What is particularly poignant is that Pattinson will be the first to take on the role for His Majesty's Secret Service, with every previous incarnation (Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan and Craig) taking up the role whilst Queen Elizabeth II was monarch.

Playing the next 007 would be a daunting task for any actor, let alone one having to headline a new era of the franchise after Amazon's eye-watering $1 billion acquisition. But, thankfully, there will be a friendly face on set to help Pattinson. With Dune filmmaker Denis Villeneuve set to direct the next movie, following his work with Pattinson on the third film in the trilogy set to release next month, the next Bond is in very safe hands. Villeneuve said in a statement about the casting:

"Before anything else, I want to express my deep gratitude to the remarkable actors who joined us on this journey. We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007. I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond. He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin."
