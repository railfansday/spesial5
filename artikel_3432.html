The Best Christmas Gifts for Kids, Because Another Boring Toy Won’t Cut It

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

Kids might be the most brutally honest gift recipients on the planet. They’ll tell you exactly what they want, change their minds three times before December, and have absolutely no problem letting you know when you’ve missed the mark. Throw in the never-ending cycle of viral toys, influencer collaborations, and pop culture obsessions, and suddenly, buying the right gift requires knowing more about internet culture than you ever signed up for.

Fortunately, our product editors are here to make things a little easier. We’ve rounded up the best gifts for kids in 2026, including the toys taking over TikTok, hands-on activities that don’t require another screen, and collectibles inspired by their favorite characters. From KPop Demon Hunters to sensory toys that are surprisingly hard to put down, these picks cover a range of ages, interests, and budgets. No frantic last-minute toy aisle searches required.

Below, find our favorite toys, viral hits, and genuinely cool gifts for every kid on your holiday shopping list.

The KPop Demon Hunters phenomenon has officially made its way into the toy aisle. This 12.75-inch Rumi doll features five posable joints, her recognizable stage outfit, and a singing function that plays “Golden.”

For the kid who’s already built every Star Wars LEGO set imaginable, this one brings something new to the table. The SMART Play TIE Fighter combines traditional building with interactive lights, sounds, and character dialogue, giving young Jedi (and Sith) plenty to explore.

Jurassic Park has nothing on this Hot Wheels setup. Kids can race through multiple levels, dodge a chomping T-Rex, and send cars flying through loops, all without a single battery.

It’s never too early to develop good listening habits, and we’re not just talking about following directions. The Toniebox Lite introduces kids to music and storytelling through screen-free audio, with interchangeable figures that make choosing what to play part of the fun.

Part robot, part practical joke, this creepy-crawly centipede is guaranteed to get a reaction. Kids can roll it into a ball, sneak it into position, and use the remote to make it burst open and slither across the floor.

Good news: Your kid wants to be a rock star. Better news: This interactive guitar has a headphone jack, so they can experiment with six music modes, built-in melodies, and their own original compositions without turning the living room into a concert venue.

Before viral toys and TikTok trends, there was Tickle Me Elmo. The ’90s sensation returns with a softer, 18-inch design that bursts into laughter and shakes with excitement whenever little hands find his ticklish spots.

A shark with wheels sounds ridiculous until you see this thing in action. Monster Jam’s remote-controlled Megalodon chomps its jaws, performs wheelies, and spins around before breaking free from its chain in Beast Mode.

Mario might get top billing, but Yoshi has always been a fan favorite. This interactive figure hatches from a wobbling, sound-making egg, then comes to life with lights, movements, and plenty of personality.

First came Labubu, now the Care Bears are making their case for the cutest bag charm on the block. Each blind box contains one of six fuzzy collectibles, from perpetually grumpy Grumpy Bear to cheerful Funshine Bear.

Some of the best gifts encourage kids to get a little curious (and maybe a little dirty). This two-in-one digital microscope and borescope camera lets young explorers examine tiny details, investigate hidden spaces, and turn an ordinary walk into a science expedition.

Vinyl collectors, meet your mini-me. This miniature record player actually spins tiny records and plays 30-second music clips, starting with a rock remix of Beethoven’s Fifth Symphony, with additional mini records from artists like Britney Spears and Elton John sold separately.

If your kid keeps stealing your phone to take pictures, this might be the solution. The Momonova camera lets them snap photos, record videos, and print black-and-white pictures instantly, with four rolls of printing paper included.

It’s a beauty haul, it’s a slime kit, and it’s probably going to be all over your kitchen table. Gui Gui’s Mega Slime Haul includes five scented slimes, charms, beads, and miniature tools for creating personalized squishy concoctions.

Santa might need a little extra room in his sleigh for this one. Bluey’s 28-inch-tall house comes with 36 pieces, interactive sounds, and a chimney that projects stars onto the ceiling, making it a standout gift for the show’s biggest (or shall we say little) fans.

The magic here is surprisingly simple, but that’s part of the appeal. A built-in fan sends eight colorful fairies fluttering around the treehouse, while a miniature Gabby figure and accessories keep the imaginative play going.

A life-size-ish Pikachu that actually moves and makes sounds? That’s the kind of Christmas surprise Pokémon fans dream about, especially when this 20-inch puppet offers more than 100 interactive reactions controlled by the person holding it.

Getting kids to eat their greens might be easier with a little help from everyone’s favorite ogre. Grüns teamed up with Shrek for a limited-edition Berry Far Far Away gummy packed with fruit and vegetable ingredients, fiber, and essential vitamins.

They’ve been asking for an Apple Watch since they learned what one was, but this under-$30 alternative might do the trick. It comes loaded with 26 games, a camera, video recording, and a pedometer, giving kids plenty to explore without the grown-up price tag.

Some kids want a puppy for Christmas. Others would rather hatch a prehistoric shark that bursts out of a bubbling egg, flashes its red eyes, and chomps its jaws. Honestly, we respect the ambition.
