3 Elite Netflix Series to Binge This Weekend (Nov 14-15)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Another week of streaming has come to an end, and it's the return of a fantasy favorite that is dominating the headlines. After a shaky second season, The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power returned this week, trying to find form once again. Although Collider's Therese Lacson called Season 3 "a story that's finally worthy of J.R.R. Tolkien's sprawling universe," this latest installment has so far earned the lowest average critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes in the series. So if you don't plan on watching The Rings of Power, what should you watch instead? To help you decide, here's a look at three shows you should binge-watch on Netflix this weekend.

For more recommendations, check out our list of the best shows and movies on Netflix.

Disclaimer: These titles are available on US Netflix.

Of all the new titles on streaming this weekend, this should be the first on your radar. The Body, which is already earning comparisons to Stephen King's Carrie, stars Kristina Bogic and Sara Boustany as Anya and Elise, two of a group of misbehaving Catholic school girls whose lives change when they start to inexplicably experience prophetic visions, sending their town into chaos.

"The Body is like nothing else on streaming right now," wrote Gabrielle Moss in Collider's review, one of several glowing responses to this new series that have helped it reach a perfect 100% on Rotten Tomatoes. Sofia Wylie, Geena Meszaros, Shirley Chen, and The Traitors' winner Gabby Windey also star in this series, which is guaranteed to find its way to the top of the Netflix streaming charts. Don't miss out on the bandwagon this weekend.

Four franchises, four completely different ways of getting underneath your skin. This is the one built out of your particular fear, why it works so well on you, and what it would leave behind.

Your nightmare does not wait outside the door. It waits for you to close your eyes, which you are going to have to do eventually, because nobody has ever won that particular argument.

Your nightmare is not really the doll. It is the face of the person you tell, and the way they decide, very kindly, that you have imagined all of it.

Your nightmare has your number, knows your name, and has almost certainly been inside your house before. The worst part is that you already know them.

Your nightmare is not really about you. It is about the people asleep down the hall, and the feeling that whatever is here is not interested in leaving.

After coming to an end on CBS with its eighth season earlier this year, sitcom favorite The Neighborhood made the move to Netflix earlier this month. Created by Jim Reynolds, this cozy series stars Max Greenfield and Cedric the Entertainer as next-door neighbors who, despite their opposing personalities, become unlikely friends after the former moves to a predominantly Black neighborhood in Los Angeles.

A welcoming, heartwarming series packed with humor and not to be taken too seriously, The Neighborhood is a perfect option to keep your weekend light. Over eight seasons, the show premiered 155 episodes and even earned a Primetime Emmy nomination earlier this year for Outstanding Picture Editing for a Multi-Camera Comedy Series. With so many episodes available, there's no better time to finish a binge-watch than now.

One of the most exciting Netflix releases last month might've fallen under your radar, so why not make up for lost time this weekend and binge-watch Below? Written and created by The Umbrella Academy's Jesse McKeown, the series follows Josh Hartnett as fisherman Calvin, who must fight to defend his humble community from the threat of a mysterious sea creature.

Charlie Heaton (Stranger Things), Mackenzie Davis (The Martian), Ruby Stokes (Rocks), and more star alongside Verity's Hartnett in a series bursting with tension. "By the time you've concluded your own visit to Below, you might not be tempted to return, but you won't regret spending a weekend there," wrote Collider's Carly Lane, who awarded it a 7/10 in her review, so why not do just that and indulge in all six episodes now?
