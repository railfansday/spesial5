Chris Pratt to Star in Sci-Fi Family Drama ‘The Traveler,’ Inks First-Look Producing Deal at Paramount (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Chris Pratt is setting up his next sci-fi adventure: starring in Paramount Pictures‘ big-screen adaptation of “The Traveler,” based on the novel by Joseph Eckert.

The film is part of the new first-look, three-year producing deal Pratt inked at the studio through his Indivisible Productions banner. Pratt will potentially star in select projects.

Eckert’s “The Traveler” tells the story of a reluctant time traveler and his son, and “the bond between them that even millennia cannot break.” A synopsis describes the story as “an adventure full of heartbreak, hope and futures beyond imagination.” Zach Lipovsky and Adam Stein will direct “The Traveler,” based on a screenplay by Justin Rhodes. The film will be produced by Adam Rosenberg and Jules Daly. Additional projects under Pratt’s deal include “Miami Heat,” written by Brad Copeland (“Jerry & Marge Go Large”), with Pratt set to star and produce alongside Hutch Parker (“Venom: The Last Dance”).

Notably, Pratt was among the superstars featured in Paramount’s CinemaCon reel this spring, as David Ellison unveiled his vision for the movie studio under the Skydance umbrella. (Pratt appears in a scene in the executive dining room with director Jon M. Chu, Molly Ringwald, Miles Teller, Damson Idris, Isabel May, Will Smith and James Wan.)

It’s no surprise that Paramount wanted to get in the Pratt business, since it stands to be quite profitable for all parties. The actor has earned more than $15 billion at the worldwide box office, including starring roles in Marvel’s “Guardians of the Galaxy” trilogy and “Avengers” movies, as well as Universal’s “Jurassic World” series and as the voice of Mario in the blockbuster “Super Mario Bros.” movies. (The most recent film, “The Super Mario Bros. Galaxy” movie debuted in April and has grossed over $1 billion globally.) Up next, Pratt produced and stars in “Way of the Warrior Kid” for Apple TV. The film, which adapts Jocko Willink’s bestselling children’s novel, launches on Nov. 25.

After starting his career on TV with shows like “Parks and Recreation” and “Everwood,” Pratt returned in 2022 with the hit series “The Terminal List,” which streams on Amazon’s Prime Video. Pratt produces and stars in the show; the second season, subtitled “True Believer,” debuted on the streamer last month.
