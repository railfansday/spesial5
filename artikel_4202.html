Tanggal: 2026-11-14

There are dozens of weapons and passives to unlock in Warhammer Survivors across its 40K and Age of Sigmar universes; here's how.

Warhammer Survivors has almost 40 base weapons to unlock across its 40K and Age of Sigmar universes, varying from the iconic Chainsword and Boltgun to the Nebulous Sea-Fog and Battle Cannon—which summons a tank that can steamroll hordes of Tyranids.

If you want to unlock Da Lucky Stikk, Ardcoat, and Codex Astartes, you need to first grab the Auspex on Desert Wasteland, which reveals the location of hidden items and marks them on the map across all 40K stages.

You can find the Auspex directly north of the spawn point—just keep heading in a straight line until you come across a chunky sci-fi remote.

Likewise, the Arcanoscope reveals secrets and marks them on the map across all Age of Sigmar stages, so it's necessary to find Kragg Blackhammer's Master Rune. You can find the Arcanoscope directly west from the spawn point on Gnarlwood.

Warhammer Survivors never quite matches Vampire Survivors, but there's something so satisfying about carving through hordes of pixelated tyranids.
