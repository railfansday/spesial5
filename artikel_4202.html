Xbox Black Friday Sale 2026 Leaks Early, Thousands Of Games Set To Be Included

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

We're still a little while away from Black Friday in late November, but Xbox is already preparing for a massive Black Friday sale in a few weeks' time, and the list of potential games has leaked online in recent days.

This is courtesy of a user named "Bloomy" over on the CheapAssGamer website, who says there are specifically 3,519 items in the list. The actual number of discounts is set to be lower than that though, as Bloomy breaks down each "Deluxe Edition" and similar bundles into individual items (some of which aren't purchasable on their own).

There's every chance the list could change between now and November 19th, which is seemingly the start date, but as things stand, the likes of 007 First Light, Ace Combat 8, Star Wars: Zero Company, The Blood of Dawnwalker and Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced look set to receive discounts as part of the sale.

We initially came across this via a post over on the official Xbox subreddit:

This certainly lines up with what we'd expect from the Xbox Black Friday Sale - last year's one began on November 20th (which, like this year's reported date, was also a Thursday) and included over 2000 games.

It's worth keeping in mind that Xbox may start providing discounts for accessories and potentially even consoles (you never know!) before the game-focused sale begins on the 19th, so keep an eye out for those as well.

Naturally, you'll be able to find all the Xbox Black Friday 2026 game deals and their prices on Pure Xbox as soon as the sale begins in mid-November, but you might need to bear with us as it's set to begin on the release date for GTA 6!
