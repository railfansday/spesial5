PS5 Exclusive Marvel's Wolverine Off to a Soft Start, 2.4 Million Copies Sold to Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Sony has officially confirmed sales figures for Marvel's Wolverine for the first time, as part of its recent financial update.

According to the report, the PS5 exclusive superhero action adventure has sold 2.4 million units as of 4th November, 2026.

The game launched on 15th September, so this sales figure is its performance after 50 days on the market.

While it may initially sound reasonably strong, it doesn't make for flattering comparisons with other titles.

For example, Marvel's Spider-Man 2 sold 2.5 million copies in its first 24 hours, and reached 5 million in 11 days.

Last year's Ghost of Yotei, meanwhile, reached 3.3 million sales in its first month.

With Marvel's Wolverine reportedly costing around $300 million to produce, it's seemingly still got some way to go before it's a profitable product.

Previous sales estimates from a couple of analytical firms have been proven fairly accurate, then, but it's always good to get proper, confirmed numbers from Sony itself.

While this sales figure means a fairly slow start for a game of this scope and budget, it's worth remembering that Marvel's Wolverine was the USA's top-selling game of September in full game dollar sales. It was also the best launch month for a Sony game since Helldivers 2.

Having said that, its early momentum doesn't appear to have carried through to October; in last month's PS Store charts, the game dropped to 14th in the US and isn't in Europe's top 20 at all.

So, it's been a success in some ways, but there's still some ground to make up. In a sense, Insomniac's Spider-Man series picks up the slack, having sold around 60 million units total, so I doubt Wolverine's somewhat soft sales performance will be too big a concern for the higher-ups.

Mixed news for this title, then, but overall I think it'll be fine — the game will slowly rack up more sales over time, and it's about to have its first holiday season, where there will no doubt be a spike.
