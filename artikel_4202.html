8 Reasons GTA VI Will Be Remembered For Years To Come

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Aden Carter is a Contributor at DualShockers who has been covering games professionally since 2020. He specializes in lists and guides, with a focus on RPGs, horror games, survival horror, Soulslikes, cozy/sim games, and action-adventure titles. Before joining DualShockers, Aden contributed to GameRant, XDA, Gamepur, I

There are games that come once in a decade and define an era for many. There’s a reason why we still talk about titles like The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time or Grand Theft Auto V, for instance. Both games have made such an impact on the gaming community that they will be remembered forever.

The next game to get remembered for years to come is going to be Grand Theft Auto VI. It has already had such hype built up around it that no matter what happens on the release date, we will be talking about it for years.

Of course, there are several other reasons GTA VI will be remembered, whether it is the level of detail, the time put into it, or even the impact on the economy.

Grand Theft Auto V is remembered for its great story, the attention to detail, and many other things. Red Dead Redemption 2 is remembered for these as well. GTA VI will also be remembered for its attention to detail and how it revolutionized open world gaming.

The next Grand Theft Auto title is set to expand the map, increase the level of detail in NPCs, and showcase a level of attention never seen in a game before. If everything goes smoothly at launch, gamers will be talking about how it is the new standard for open world games.

Every game after this will feel like it doesn’t measure up to the detail of Rockstar’s latest project, and it will be used as the benchmark for nearly every other triple-A studio.

Rockstar's 38 new GTA 6 screenshots showcase incredibly detailed faces, Vice City's nightlife, and the gangs and scenes bringing Leonida to life.

One thing that gamers seem to always bring up is how long they waited for any particular game. We finally got a remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, and some of the first things people said was that they waited almost 30 years for it.

Kingdom Hearts players are the same way, as they often mention the wait between Kingdom Hearts 2 and Kingdom Hearts 3. Whenever there is a long wait for a sequel, we tend to latch onto it and bring it up in conversation.

With there being a significant wait of 13 years between GTA V and GTA VI, gamers are going to be talking about it for years to come, bringing it up like they had to wait in line for their favorite game for all those years or something.

Meme culture is powerful and stays around for a long time. For instance, the show King of the Hill stuck around thanks to meme culture. The same can be said for Skyrim, as it has many dedicated memes that have never gone out of style.

Odds are, there will be moments in GTA VI that get memed, whether it is the characters giving an odd look or one of the many wild NPCs. There are already memes using parts of the footage we have already seen, I can only imagine what will come from the game’s full release.

On top of this, the game has already generated the meme of “We got this before GTA VI.” This will evolve to GTA VII, and people will talk about how it used to be GTA VI.

Development studios are always trying to figure out when the best time to launch a game is. Typically, a studio wants to avoid launching a game at the same time as a competitor if the games are too similar. After all, they want to maximize their profits, so it only makes sense.

GTA VI had a massive impact on release schedules. Before anyone even knew the release date of GTA VI, companies were already trying to avoid being caught in the release window. Some were even trying to wait out giving a release date until GTA VI’s release date was announced.

Everyone knew the sway the game would have on the wallets of gamers, and they wanted to avoid potential losses. With this news hitting the mainstream, it will be remembered for a long time.

There’s often a conversation that people have when it comes to games and the consoles they are played on. It's all about what games are console sellers. This is typically reserved for games that are console exclusives, like Demon’s Souls for the PS5 or Gears of War E-Day for the Xbox Series X|S.

However, there has been a trend of people buying up consoles just so they can play GTA VI. They avoided buying a PS5 or an Xbox Series X|S until now just so they can play this game.

That is something that many people won’t forget. No matter if GTA VI is great or it flops, everyone who bought a console for it will remember the game for years to come. You don’t forget why you bought a console. I bought my PS5 specifically for Demon’s Souls.

Okay, GTA Online 2.0, or whatever they want to call it, isn’t going to last forever, but it will last for a long time. After all, GTA V Online has lasted for many years, and people are still playing it despite the game coming out in 2013.

Thanks to constant updates and a dedicated community, the online mode has stayed relevant for years. Now GTA VI Online will take the spotlight, and it will keep everyone’s attention after they finish the story.

Nobody knows when GTA VII will be released, and so GTA VI Online will be the go-to online mode for the Grand Theft Auto experience for possibly a decade or more. That will keep people talking for sure.

GTA Online 2.0 will be coming eventually, and there are some changes that Rockstar should make.

You may have heard it already. “GTA VI will cost the US economy $1 billion.” While that likely isn’t true, it is a game that will have a cultural impact, and there are estimates that state the US economy will take a hit to its productivity when it is released.

There is no telling how much money the economy could potentially lose on November 19th, but some businesses have already stated that they anticipate their workflow to slow down that day, and there are many who have already said they’re going to call out to play GTA VI.

Even if there is a small hit to the US economy, that will still be remembered for years. It will be a day that shows how many gamers became united by one game.

Let’s talk about sales numbers for a moment, because the gaming community remembers when a game does amazingly well and when it falls incredibly short. GTA VI is already doing amazingly well with pre-orders.

It is being estimated that pre-orders for GTA VI have already generated $751 million, and over 7 million units have been pre-ordered. That is a higher revenue than many other triple-A titles make, and that is also only on console, as the game won’t be on PC for a while.

It is estimated that GTA VI will generate over $1 billion in revenue for Rockstar, making it close to being one of the highest grossing games of all time. Give it a few years and sales of the game will most likely surpass even GTA V, which sold over 230 million copies. That is a number people will remember.
