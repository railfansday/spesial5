Spain's leaders clash over corruption, economy in tense election debate

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Spain's Socialist Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez acknowledged making "mistakes" as he faced off with his main conservative rival in a key debate on Thursday before an election next week that could leave the far right on the cusp of power. The pair clashed over the economy, housing, immigration, corruption, foreign policy, as well as policies towards women, repeatedly interrupting each other during the live televised showdown.
