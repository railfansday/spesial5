Nwaneri and Moore guide England U21 to friendly win over France U21

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Two-time European championship holders England U21 end 2026 with a 3-2 win over France in Bristol. Bundesliga youngsters Mikey Moore and Ethan Nwaneri shone as Lee Carsley's side recovered from their defeat to Afonso Moreira's Portugal at the same venue.

Köln's attacker opened the scoring for the hosts from Nwaneri's assist in the sixth minute. Borussia Dortmund’s attacker added another direct goal contribution when Divin Mubama doubled 4England's advantage at the half-hour mark.

Both Moore and Nwaneri could return to their parent clubs Tottenham Hotspur and Arsenal, respectively, in January as the North London clubs aren't satisfied with their spells so far in the Bundesliga.

France leveled the score with two late goals before Arsenal’s 16-year-old sensation Max Dowman won it for England with his first goal at this level in the 89th-minute. Moore's Köln teammate Jahmai Simpson-Pusey played the second-half for England U21.

France, who have RB Leipzig's duo Abdoul Kone and Tidiam Gomis on their ranks, narrowly lost both of their friendly games after securing their place in the next summer's Euros.
