Calvin Bassey: Manchester United transfer setback as key January target enters contract talks

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Fulham are in talks with captain Calvin Bassey over a new contract as they look to fend off interest from Manchester United.

The 26-year-old centre-back, whose contract expires next summer, is understood to be on United’s shortlist as they look to sign defensive reinforcements in January.

Fulham hold the option to extend Bassey’s current deal by a further year.

The Cottagers are keen to keep hold of Bassey, especially after Alvaro Arbeloa promoted him to captain in the summer.

Nothing has been agreed yet, but Fulham have opened talks with Bassey over a new contract and do not intend to sell him in January.

The Nigerian international has played every minute of Fulham’s league campaign so far this season, and is viewed as an integral part of Arbeloa’s plans.

After failing to win any of their first six league games this term, Fulham have taken eight points from their last four matches, moving two points clear of the relegation zone.

Over that four-game period, Fulham kept two clean sheets and conceded just two goals, with Bassey at the heart of defence.

They are therefore determined to fend off any interest in Bassey by tying him down to a new long-term deal.

United, meanwhile, are expected to be active in the January transfer market, with Mauricio Pochettino identifying centre-back as a priority position for the Red Devils.

His current centre-back options include Harry Maguire, Lisandro Martinez, and the youngsters Leny Yoro and Ayden Heaven.

Martinez will also be out of contract this summer, and his future remains up in the air.
