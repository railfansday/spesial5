The Secret to Kendall Jenner’s ‘Me Mode’

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The model and founder shares her favorite ways to unwind and how the Therabody TheraFace Mask Glo fits into her nightly routine.

For Kendall Jenner, “Me Mode” is a time to unplug from the daily demands of work and social obligations and simply be still. “I have a really busy mind, so switching off has never come naturally to me,” says the model and entrepreneur. “I’ve had to build little rituals that help me slow down.”

One of those rituals involves Therabody’s TheraFace Mask Glo, an LED face mask that bridges skincare with wellness. Delivering full LED coverage across the entire face, including the lips and under eyes, the cordless device features 504 red, infrared, and blue LEDs, plus a relaxing scalp massage to help relieve tension. Best of all, treatment time is only 12 minutes, giving Jenner a quick, easy, and much-needed pause from her busy schedule.

“After a long day, it’s sweatpants, my dogs, and zero plans,” Jenner says. “Putting on the Mask Glo has kind of become my signal that the day is over. For those 12 minutes, my brain finally goes quiet.”

One thing Jenner loves about the TheraFace Mask Glo is how effortlessly it fits into her routine. “It’s hands-free, so I just put it on and do whatever I feel like that night,” Jenner says. “Sometimes that’s music or journaling, and a lot of the time it’s doing absolutely nothing. The scalp massage is my favorite part. It feels less like a skincare step and more like actually relaxing.”

It offers legitimate skincare benefits, too. Backed by the largest clinical study of any LED mask on the market, the TheraFace Mask Glo was shown to reduce under-eye wrinkles and fine lines, enhance radiance, and lift sagging skin in as little as eight weeks.

The TheraFace Mask Glo offers three LED settings to target different skin concerns. Red light helps improve the appearance of fine lines, wrinkles, and dark spots, while red and infrared light work together to boost collagen production. Blue light targets acne-causing bacteria, helping prevent future breakouts.

The key is consistent use, which Jenner has found easy to maintain. “If a routine is too complicated, I’m not going to stick with it, and I’ve learned that consistency matters more than anything,” she says. “With the Mask Glo, I pick red, red and infrared, or blue light depending on how my skin is acting, and that’s it. It’s easy enough that I actually use it, which is half the battle for me. It comes with me when I travel now, too.”

The LED lights are placed across the entire face, even covering overlooked areas like the mouth and eyes. The preset treatment runs for 12 minutes, cycling through four minutes of each light setting. Four-minute individual treatments are also available.

Combining advanced skincare and relaxation into one simple step, the TheraFace Mask Glo is ideal for anyone who wants to level up their nighttime routine with minimal effort. As Jenner has learned over the years, less is more when it comes to beauty and wellness.

“I’ve been sitting in makeup chairs since I was 14, so I went through a phase of trying everything,” she says. “Now I do a lot less, and I’m more intentional about what I put on my skin. I try to give it a break when I can, and I’ll use the Mask Glo before glam on shoot days.”

Jenner’s nighttime routine is just one aspect in living a more holistic lifestyle. “Wellness for me is mostly about balance now: being at the barn with my horses, getting outside, and protecting my nights,” she says. “My friends make fun of me for going to bed so early, but I’m not changing.”
