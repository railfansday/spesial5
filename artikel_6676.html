See the Standout Red Carpet Looks From the 2026 LACMA Art+Film Gala

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Every year, the LACMA Art+Film Gala brings together some of the biggest names in entertainment, fashion, and the arts for an evening that celebrates creativity across disciplines. With the Los Angeles museum’s iconic “Urban Light” installation serving as its backdrop, the event has become a major fashion moment in its own right for designers and guests to make a statement. This year, for its 15th iteration, the gala was no exception.

Last night, the Los Angeles County Museum of Art welcomed guests for the soirée, co-chaired by Leonardo DiCaprio and LACMA trustee Eva Chow. This year’s edition honored artist Vija Celmins, whose detailed works explore the natural world, and filmmaker Denis Villeneuve, whose visually ambitious films have helped shape contemporary cinema. Proceeds from the evening support LACMA’s efforts to make film a more central part of its programming, alongside its exhibitions, acquisitions, educational initiatives, and screenings exploring the intersection of art and film. Gucci returned as the event’s presenting sponsor, continuing a partnership that dates back to the gala’s inaugural year in 2011. Rosé also took the stage as the evening’s musical guest.

Thoughtful looks abounded, with Gucci being particularly well represented in custom designs for model Alex Consani, who arrived in a dramatic, sculptural black gown, model and musician Soo Joo Park, who wore a playful, feathered dress, and writer and curator Antwaun Sargent, who donned an intricately detailed custom ensemble from the house, among the standouts. Elsewhere, Kylie Jenner made an impression in a brown-and-silver deer-print gown from Ashi Studio’s spring/summer 2026 couture collection, while Rosé wore custom Saint Laurent. Anya Taylor-Joy, Nicole Kidman, and a host of other familiar faces brought their own interpretations of whimsical evening wear to the occasion.
