Taylor Swift Returns to Her Roots at the 2026 CMAs

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Taylor Swift hasn’t yee’d her last haw after all.

Tonight, the singer returned to the Country Music Awards for the first time in 10 years, where she is nominated for Single of the Year for her song “I Knew It, I Knew You” from Toy Story 5.

Swift returned to a longtime favorite house for the occasion: Oscar de la Renta. The singer hit the red carpet in Nashville wearing a strapless yellow gown with a billowing high-low skirt—plus pockets—from the brand’s spring 2027 runway. (One eagle-eyed Vogue editor suggested that the dress was an ode to Dolly Parton, whose favorite color was yellow.) While the house currently has no creative director since Laura Kim and Fernando Garcia exited over a year ago, she’s been loyal to the brand across leadership. She accessorized with strappy metallic gold Jimmy Choo heels and a pair of custom Lorraine Schwartz drop earrings with Paraíba tourmaline and white and yellow diamonds.

For her performance, Swift turned to one of her newer faves—Erdem. The singer plucked a minidress with a multicolored, bejeweled mock neck and a flouncy red drop-waist skirt straight off the spring 2027 runway. She countrified with a pair of matching cowboy boots and a rhinestone-encrusted guitar. Swift hit the stage once again to present Brooks & Dunn with the Willie Nelson lifetime achievement award. She brought a little disco to the Country Music Awards in another mock-necked mini from Taller Marmo: a blue and green sequined number with blue fringe hanging from her long sleeves.

The singer last attended the CMAs in 2016, when she was gearing up to release Reputation. The look, a departure from her sweeter look, matched the era’s tone: a slinky Julien Macdonald cutout dress with black sequined patternwork over illusion mesh.

But while it’s been a decade since Swift attended the CMAs, it’s been a whopping 13 years since she hit the stage. In 2013, the singer performed “Red,” for which she wore a classic black gown and strummed away on a rhinestone-encrusted guitar.

Swift performing at the 2013 Country Music Awards.

This year also marks the 20th anniversary of Swift’s first CMAs. The singer attended the awards for the first time in 2006, back when her most acclaimed song was “Teardrops on My Guitar.” For the occasion, she wore a black mermaid gown with matching evening gloves. Taylor Swift has come a long way, but she’s always known how to make a splash on the red carpet.
