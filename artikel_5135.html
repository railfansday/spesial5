Anthropic’s IPO paperwork shows the Claude developer has committed at least $121.3 billion to Google for computing capacity between April 2026 and July 2033. That makes Google Anthropic’s largest disclosed supplier, ahead of Amazon at a minimum of $110 billion and Microsoft at $47.2 billion to $49.2 billion. The commitment applies whether or not Anthropic uses all the capacity, which gives Google Cloud about $16.7 billion a year in locked-in demand. The deal also highlights an unusual relationship, since Google is both Anthropic’s biggest supplier and a direct competitor.
