Big SHIB Development as North American Derivatives Exchange Self-Certifies Strike Contract

Shiba Inu (SHIB) is set to get more exposure in the U.S.-regulated financial market after the North American Derivatives Exchange (Nadex) self-certified a new strike event contract tied to the meme cryptocurrency's price.

On November 10, 2026, an official Nadex notice said the exchange certified a swap based on the price of SHIB at a specific time and date. The product, called the Shiba Inu Strike Event Contract, began trading on or after Nov. 13, 2026.

The filing is available through the official records of the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC). The regulator’s database shows that the SHIB Strike Event Contract is certified as a swap, with November 10 listed as the certification date.

The product allows traders to wager that a particular price of SHIB will be attained or surpassed at some point in the future. This opens another avenue for market players to take positions based on the price fluctuations of Shiba Inu without having to buy or hold the underlying token.

Nadex (North American Derivatives Exchange) is a CFTC-regulated U.S. financial exchange acquired by Foris DAX Markets (part of Crypto.com), bringing the Shiba Inu product to a regulated platform.

Shiba Inu Japan community member Kuro reacted to the development, saying SHIB is now the underlying asset for a new financial product in the U.S.-regulated market.

"Nadex, an exchange owned by Crypto.com, has officially filed a new trading product based on SHIB’s price, and it has also been listed in the official records of the U.S. CFTC. This product lets traders predict whether SHIB will exceed a certain price at a specified time, alongside assets such as BTC, ETH, and XRP," Kuro added, saying another opportunity for SHIB to be used in the U.S. financial market has just opened up.

At the time of writing, SHIB was down 3.33% over the last 24 hours to $0.00000414 amid broader crypto market weakness that coincided with strength in the U.S. dollar.

The dollar index reached its highest level since April 2025 on Friday, following hotter-than-expected inflation earlier this week that increased expectations of further Fed tightening, pushing Treasury yields and the dollar higher while pulling down both cryptocurrencies and gold.
