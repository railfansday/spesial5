Adswerve Evolves Into a Predictive Marketing Consulting Firm, Expanding Client Services and Partner Solutions

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

With 17 years helping brands get more from Google, Adswerve now helps 950+ clients derive higher ROI from their broader adtech and martech ecosystem

DENVER, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Adswerve today announced its evolution to a predictive marketing consulting firm. Building on 17 years as the first and largest independent Google Marketing Platform (GMP) partner in the U.S., Adswerve now helps brands and agencies lower acquisition costs and grow customer lifetime value by connecting data, AI and media across their ecosystem to anticipate customer needs.

Adswerve was founded in 2009 by DoubleClick technicians with one commitment: help clients achieve measurable business outcomes and a positive return on every marketing dollar. That commitment hasn't changed, but the environment has. The typical Adswerve client works with several disparate adtech and martech partners, yet few get full value from their stack because data, intelligence and measurement sit in separate silos. Generative AI has made prediction faster and more accessible, and the marketers who connect their ecosystem are pulling ahead.

Adswerve delivers predictive marketing with solutions across DIEM: Data, Intelligence, Experiences and Measurement. A unified and accurate data foundation improves intelligence, from propensity modeling to predicted lifetime value. That intelligence guides personalized experiences and optimized performance media, and measurement proves what worked and informs the next decision. Every engagement starts with the client's business outcome, whether that's lower cost per acquisition, higher lifetime value, or a more efficient marketing organization. Generative AI and AI agents are applied across every part of DIEM.

Adswerve has grown step by step to meet those needs: from $1.5 billion in annual media management into advanced analytics, from Google Marketing Platform into Google Cloud, and across key partners including Adobe, Amazon, Amplitude, Snowflake, and multiple data and premium supply partners. In 2026, Google selected Adswerve as one of four companies to partner on its new data strength program, GIP. Adswerve is also a leader in adtech and martech training and certification, and its experts hold 350+ partner certifications. The company maintains a 94% client retention rate, with client relationships averaging more than five years.

"Our story has always been about our clients' results, and that's what led us here," said Tom Zawacki, CEO of Adswerve. "Google Marketing Platform is where we built our expertise, and Google remains a key partner in what we do. Now, for our clients to achieve short-term economic ROI and long-term competitive differentiation over the next few years, they need to become leaders in predictive marketing across the entire ecosystem."

What Predictive Marketing delivers for clients:

"When we started Adswerve in 2009, our mission was to help marketers increase the positive return on every dollar spent, which remains the foundational goal as the company continues to scale," said Clint Tasset, co-founder of Adswerve. "With our roots and proven experience translating data into measurable results in Google Marketing Platform, predictive marketing is how we will continue to deliver that same rigor and impact across their entire marketing tech stacks."

Over the coming months, Adswerve will share more about the solutions, people, and intelligence behind its predictive marketing practice. To learn more, visit adswerve.com.

About Adswerve Adswerve is an award-winning predictive marketing consulting firm helping brands and agencies acquire, grow, and retain customers while maximizing ROI. Its 250+ experts combine human expertise, AI, deep platform mastery, and proprietary Adswerve Intelligence to deliver predictive marketing solutions across Data, Intelligence, Experiences, and Measurement. Adswerve is the largest independent US Google Marketing Platform provider, a leader in Google Cloud Platform, a three-time Adobe Solutions Partner of the Year, and a trusted partner to Amazon, Amplitude, Snowflake, and other leading technology companies. This expertise enables 950+ clients to anticipate what's next, act with confidence, and drive measurable business results. Learn more at adswerve.com.
