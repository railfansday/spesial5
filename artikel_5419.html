Anthropic CEO calls reports of surrendering human control to AI 'absurd'

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Dario Amodei said AI should be governed by democratic processes and accused rival executives of "vibes-based" positioning

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei (Krisztian Bocsi/Bloomberg via Getty Images)

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei rejected claims on Wednesday that he believes humans should "surrender human free will" to AI systems, calling the suggestion "absurd" in a post on X $TWTR.

Amodei wrote that AI should be governed by legitimate democratic processes. "We should never sacrifice legitimacy or self-determination at the altar of efficiency or security," he wrote. He said the argument against autocracy applies "word for word" against governance by AI.

Some Anthropic employees believe humanity ought to cede part of its authority over decisions to superintelligent AI systems, according to Axios. An Anthropic spokesperson told Axios that the company trains Claude not to undermine human oversight, and that Amodei has repeatedly called for humans to stay in control of AI in public comments.

Amodei said the debate conflates surrendering free will in "some grand sense of global governance" with AI systems growing able to handle particular jobs with less human involvement, such as "AI driving a car, designing a cancer drug, or writing software end-to-end." He said his view on task automation is mixed. Such automation is "clearly desirable in many cases and will save a lot of lives," he wrote, but it has downsides.

He pointed to recursive self-improvement, in which AI is used to build the next generation of AI systems. "As we said publicly in early November, we don't expect it to be safe for Claude to build successive frontier models fully autonomously, without meaningful human supervision or understanding," he wrote, according to TechCrunch. He added that pausing or scaling back some AI deployments could be warranted so that people have longer to adjust to job disruption.

Amodei said he does not think other AI companies hold significantly different views on automating specific tasks. He wrote that some AI CEOs engage in "vibes-based commercial and political positioning," repeating slogans like "human control and agency" without changing their products, according to TechCrunch. He said he was willing to risk Anthropic's future by refusing to allow its AI models to be used for autonomous weapons, and that no other AI company did the same.

Rival executives have made public statements on the issue. Meta $META Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang wrote on X that "we should not be okay with a 'handover' of society to AI," according to Axios. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman said earlier this month, "I want people to have the agency over the future," according to Axios.

Amodei's concern about recursive self-improvement follows an essay in September calling on AI companies to pull back on their most advanced model development. That essay cited the growing ability of AI systems to build the next generation of AI as a motivating factor.
