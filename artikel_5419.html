Anthropic is guiding its IPO price to the top of its range — or higher

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Anthropic would be valued at roughly $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis if shares priced at the upper end of the range

Anthropic is signaling to prospective investors that it anticipates pricing its IPO at or beyond the top of its range, according to Bloomberg.

The company filed an amended registration statement on Tuesday that lists 825 million shares of Class A common stock at an estimated $70 to $78 each. Anthropic is selling 801,351,354 of those shares, and selling stockholders are offering 23,648,646. The stock has been approved to list on the Nasdaq $NDAQ Global Select Market under the symbol "ANT."

Google $GOOGL, Nvidia $NVDA, Advanced Micro Devices, Broadcom $AVGO, Micron $MU Technology, Microsoft $MSFT and TSMC $TSM have agreed to buy a combined $26.0 billion of Anthropic's Class N common stock in a private placement at the IPO price, the filing said. The placement closes immediately after the IPO and depends on the offering being completed. Anthropic estimated net proceeds from the IPO and placement at about $84.6 billion at a $74 share price, the midpoint of the range.

Including the private placement, the offering could bring in as much as $90.4 billion at the top of the range, according to Bloomberg. Anthropic would be valued at roughly $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis if shares priced at the upper end of the range.

Banks plan to close their order books at 4 p.m. ET on Tuesday, according to Bloomberg. The IPO is slated to be priced Wednesday, with trading starting a day later, though terms may still shift. Goldman Sachs $GS and Morgan Stanley $MS are leading the offering, and the filing lists 20 other banks.

Orders have climbed to almost seven times the supply of stock on offer, according to Bloomberg. Bankers have told prospective investors that current backers will get priority and that some buyers will receive smaller allocations than they anticipate, according to Bloomberg. The filing said up to 20% of the shares offered are expected to go to retail investors.

Anthropic's run-rate revenue stood at roughly $81 billion at Oct. 31, an increase of $10 billion since Sept. 30, the filing said. The company reported revenue of $17.0 billion for the three months ended Sept. 30.

Anthropic also disclosed about $900.9 billion in purchase commitments and lease agreements, substantially all tied to compute and infrastructure. About $70.7 billion of that is due in the 12 months after Sept. 30, 2026.
