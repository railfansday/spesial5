Uber is experimenting with rides for children as young as 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Parents must request every trip for children ages 10 to 12, and an adult must be at pickup, under the Uber Kids pilot

David Paul Morris / Bloomberg via Getty Images

Uber $UBER is piloting Uber Kids, a ride option for children ages 10 to 12 that a linked guardian must request and manage, the company said.

The pilot is available to select riders in pilot cities, Uber said. It is running in Nashville, Austin and Madison, Wisconsin, according to Bloomberg.

Children ages 10 to 12 cannot ride unaccompanied on regular Uber options such as UberX, the company said. Uber Kids is for children in that age group who do not need a booster seat, and they must meet their state's legal requirements to ride without one. The guardian controls booking, destination changes and communication with the driver. The child does not need an Uber account or app and receives trip updates by text message.

Drivers must be background-checked, highly rated and experienced, and must complete mandatory Uber Kids education, Uber said. Drivers are required to record audio and video of each trip, and guardians can watch live video from the driver's phone camera. Neither guardians nor drivers can access the recordings.

An authorized adult must be at pickup, and the driver must enter a unique PIN from the guardian or that adult before the ride can begin. Guardians must add dropoff notes when they request a ride. Neither the child nor the driver can change the destination, and trips must be direct, single-stop rides.

Uber's RideCheck technology detects unexpected stops or route changes, and an ADT safety agent then checks in, Uber said. Guardians can message or call the driver in the app and reach a live support agent. Drivers see the child's first name.

Guardians can request Uber Kids rides from 5 a.m. to 9 p.m., and rides are subject to driver availability, according to Uber.

Bloomberg reported in August that Uber was working on a kids feature. Uber launched a service for teenagers between 13 and 17 years old in 2023.
