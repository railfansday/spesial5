CD Projekt Red says The Witcher 4 will be as big a leap as GTA 6 was from GTA 5

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

CD Projekt Red says the jump from The Witcher 3 to The Witcher 4 will be comparable to the one Rockstar made between GTA 5 and GTA 6, with improvements planned for nearly every part of the game.

GTA 6’s launch has already drawn a reaction from CD Projekt Red, with Cyberpunk 2’s creative director joking that his own game was “cancelled” after seeing what Rockstar had pulled off.

However, the comparison came up again during CD Projekt’s earnings call, when the company was asked whether players should expect a similar leap from its next Witcher game.

As first reported by Eurogamer, CD Projekt Red co-CEO Michal Nowakowski said the studio had looked at what changed between Rockstar’s two games and that he expected The Witcher 4 to make a comparable jump.

“We believe there’s going to be a comparable leap between The Witcher 3 and The Witcher 4, I think so, yes. It’s been 11 years, we are working on The Witcher 4 to make it significantly better looking and improved in terms of pretty much every possible facet of the game, so yes, you should expect us to make a much bigger jump between Witcher 3 and Witcher 4,” said Nowakowski.

Nowakowski was also asked whether GTA 6’s launch would push CD Projekt Red to spend more on its own games. After all, GTA 6 is expected to have exceeded $1 billion in total development costs.

However, the CD Projekt Red CEO said the studio’s ambitions for The Witcher 4 and Cyberpunk 2 had stayed the same.

“We have certain ambitions when it comes to Witcher [4] and Cyberpunk 2, they have not changed when it comes to GTA 6. If anything happened as a result of GTA 6, it’s [that it’s] always great to see games like that because they show you how much cool stuff can be done, and that’s for sure an inspiration in many ways for our developers,” he added.
