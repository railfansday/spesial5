Below Deck captain arrested after Turkey fight leaves 17-year-old dead

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Below Deck star João Franco and his brother have been arrested in Turkey after an altercation at a clothing store left a 17-year-old employee dead.

Franco, 36, is a Portuguese yacht captain who appeared on Below Deck Mediterranean and Below Deck Down Under. He also appeared on multiple seasons of the Bravo franchise, including the most recent season of Below Deck Down Under.

The incident happened on November 7 in Bodrum, a resort city in Turkey’s Muğla Province. Franco and his brother, identified as Carlos Campbell Franco, were allegedly intoxicated when a verbal dispute with employees escalated into a physical fight. Four people were reportedly injured.

The 17-year-old employee, identified by local outlets as Muhammet Koyabakan, suffered a serious head injury during the confrontation. Reports said he fell and struck his head on steps before going into cardiac arrest.

Emergency responders revived him and he was taken to hospital, where he died from his injuries on November 9.

Page Six published CCTV footage it said showed the confrontation outside the store. The footage appears to show Carlos throwing the first visible punch, while João initially tries to separate his brother from the other men.

The fight then continues, with several people punching and kicking each other. João appears to throw a punch before one of the men falls to the ground. Local reports identified that person as Koyabakan, although the footage alone does not establish which blow caused his fall or injury.

João and Carlos were taken into custody and later remanded in prison, according to Turkish reports. Two other people detained in connection with the fight were released under judicial control. The latest English-language reports have not specified the charges against the brothers.

The brothers have denied responsibility for Koyabakan’s death. João reportedly told police that he and his brother had been attacked. Carlos claimed he acted in self-defense, said João did not strike Koyabakan and suggested the teenager may have lost his balance and fallen.

Former Below Deck co-star Colin Macy-O’Toole addressed the incident in a statement to Page Six. Macy-O’Toole was not present during the fight, but said that, after watching the footage, “my impression is that João appeared to be trying to defend himself and de-escalate.”

He added that people should consider the full context of the footage before assigning blame, calling the situation “incredibly tragic.”

Representatives for Bravo, the Bodrum police, the local prosecutor and the courthouse have not provided a substantive public comment. The investigation remains ongoing.
