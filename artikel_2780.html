Down Tatum, White, and Robinson, Celtics lose to lowly Kings 97-96

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Celtics suffered their most embarrassing loss of the season on Saturday night, losing to the now two-win Sacramento Kings, 97-96.

It was the third quarter that pushed the Kings over the top as Boston shot 5-of-26 from the field (missed all thirteen three-pointers) in the period and were outscored 35-13. It was a great game for Mike Conley, who scored 19 points and was a +17.

The Celtics were missing three key players in this one; Jayson Tatum, Derrick White and Mitchell Robinson all sat out this game. They started Payton Pritchard, Sam Hauser, Paul George, Hugo Gonzalez and Neemias Queta. It was the first start of the season for both Hauser and Pritchard. Meanwhile, the Kings started, Darius Acuff Jr., Zach LaVine, Keegan Murray, De'Andre Hunter and Domantas Sabonis. Ben Simmons and Maxime Raynaud remained out as neither player has played all season.

It was a really hot start for the Celtics as they scored the first 7 points of the game — buckets from Pritchard, Queta and George, led to a Sacramento timeout.

The C's missed 11 of their first 13 three point attempts as Sacramento went on a 21-5 run to take a 23-19 lead.

Sam Hauser hit a corner three at the buzzer to give the Celtics a 27-25 lead at the end of the first quarter. Hauser led the team in scoring with 6 points while Hugo Gonzalez had 5.

Mike Conley was the first point guard off of the bench instead of Devin Carter in what was his return to Sacramento. Conley chipped in right away with 5 points and 2 steals in his first 4 minutes of action.

He then had a sweet pass to Ron Harper Jr.

The Celtics took a lot of threes in the first half but most of them did not go in. They went 9/33 from beyond the arc in the half. They also fouled too much in the first half; the Kings spent much of the 2nd quarter in the bonus.

The Celtics led 54-41 after the first half. Hauser had 4 of the 9 Celtics threes and 12 points. The defense was awesome holding the Kings to 41 points and being the reason Boston led by 13 at the break.

The Celtics three-point struggles continued into the 3rd quarter as the Kings made a run to cut the Celtics lead to 2 at 60-58. The primary reason for the Kings run was the Celtics defense fell off. The Kings scored 17 points in the first 6:31 of the 3rd quarter. The Celtics called a timeout and turned it over out of the timeout as Sacramento tied the game. The Kings led entering the 4th, 76-67.

There are really no words to describe how the quarter went. They were bad on offense and defense and this is against the worst 3rd quarter team in the NBA entering the night — the Kings 3rd quarter net rating had been -23.7.

The Celtics started the 4th quarter with a new lineup.

They scored the first 1o points of the quarter to take the lead by 1. Mike Conley had 6 of the 10 points.

The two teams were tied at 82 after the Kings regained a 5-point lead but another Conley three and a Luka Garza put back knotted the game up.

Then, on a night the Celtics could not buy a three, Neemias Queta hit a three.

Darius Acuff hit a tough mid-range shot to put the Kings up 1, followed by a Paul George offensive rebound to put the Celtics up 1, then Acuff made 2 free throws to put the Kings up 1 with 40.5 seconds to go.

After Queta and Acuff traded 2 free throws each, Payton Pritchard, who had struggled all night, hit a clutch mid-range shot to put the Celtics up 1 with 11.8 seconds to go as the Kings called timeout.

Keegan Murray got an offensive rebound and put back right over Devin Carter and Hugo Gonzalez to put the Kings up 97-96 with 5 seconds left.

Payton Pritchard missed what would have been a game winning shot and the Celtics lost 97-96 as the Kings got their 2nd win of the season.

In a game Boston lost by 1 points, they missed 10 free throws.

For what it's worth, Darius Acuff Jr. is a star. The way he took over the game late after struggling all night was really impressive.

The C's shot 34% from the field and 22% from three while the Kings shot 41% from the field and 27% from three. The Celtics next game is Sunday in San Francisco against the Warriors at 8:30 pm EST.
