Thomas Sorber has 3rd knee surgery, out 6-to-8 weeks

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

In some of the worst all-time injury luck to start an NBA career, Thomas Sorber had another setback. The Oklahoma City Thunder announced on Tuesday that Sorber underwent a successful arthroscopic procedure to address inflammation at the patellofemoral joint in his right knee. It developed in the late stages of his return-to-play process.

Dr. Brian Cole at the Rush University Medical Center performed the procedure in Chicago. Thunder medical personnel were present. Sorber is expected to return to on-court activities in six to eight weeks — which should be around mid-January.

Brutal. Just absolutely brutal. If everything goes right, Sorber will be a month shy of the two-year anniversary of when he last suited up for a game — period. The Thunder added him with the No. 15 pick of the 2025 NBA draft. He has not played a lick of basketball in a year and a half since that moment.

Let's list the injuries Sorber has sustained over the last two years — he had foot surgery in Feb. 2025. That cut his sole collegiate season at Georgetown short. He then had a torn ACL in Sept. 2025. That sidelined him for all of the 2025-26 season. He then had a follow-up procedure in July 2026. He was only supposed to be out for a month.

Four months later, Sorber had a third surgery done on his right knee. Now, he's out for two-plus months.

Man, you got to feel for the guy. Sorber is basically going to have his first two years in the NBA washed out by injuries. Important to note that his 2027-28 team option has already been picked up. The 20-year-old looked close to returning, too. He had participated in intense pregame warmups throughout the entire 2026-27 regular season. He even slimmed down by a noticeable amount.

Now, Sorber is back to being out. The Thunder have been hit hard with injuries this year. To the point that their NBA championship aspirations are out the window. With Chet Holmgren, Jalen Williams and Cason Wallace out for the foreseeable future, he could've used the opportunity to get real NBA playing time from the jump.

Instead, Sorber remains out for a longer time. I'm sure all parties here are frustrated with this situation.
