YouTube TV gets you nearly every channel you need to watch the NFL, including Fox, NBC, ABC, CBS, ESPN and NFL Network. Plus, YouTube TV is the home of NFL Sunday Ticket. The Sunday Ticket package, while pricey on its own, gets you ALL the Sunday out-of-market games (160+ games per season).

Current pricing for new users for the 2026 Sunday Ticket is $30/month for eight months. Factor in the cost of YouTube TV's Sports and News plan at $49.99/month for your first five months, then $71.99/month thereafter.

That's $79.99/month for five months of YouTube TV + NFL Sunday Ticket, and $101.99/month for the following three months. You can also opt, with or without a YouTube TV subscription, to pay for the rest of the season of Sunday Ticket upfront for just $240.

Fubo's Sports+ News package gives you access to ESPN, NFL Network, Fox, ABC, CBS, NBC and 20-plus more live channels. Starting at $54.99/month for your first month, the live TV streaming service is definitely pricey (though certainly not the most expensive option on this list), but it offers most channels you'll need to watch both the NFL preseason and regular season.
