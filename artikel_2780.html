CeeDee Lamb totals 72 yards, TD in Week 10 vs. 49ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Dallas Cowboys wide receiver CeeDee Lamb posted seven receptions for 72 yards and a touchdown on 13 targets in Week 10 against the San Francisco 49ers.

Why it Matters: Lamb continues to perform at a high rate, week in and week out. The star receiver is just 24 yards shot of 1,000 receiving yards for a sixth straight season. No player has ever done that in franchise history. He should hit the milestone in Week 11 against the Tennessee Titans.
