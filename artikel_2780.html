Indiana football coach Curt Cignetti blasts Michigan coaching rumors with viral tweet

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

BLOOMINGTON — Indiana football coach Curt Cignetti on Thursday night blasted a report linking him to a potential vacancy at Michigan.

Cignetti, posting from his personal Twitter account, labeled the story from CBS Sports "fake news" and broke out the same laughing clown emoji he used to punctuate his recent criticism of the College Football Playoff committee.

"Fake news created by three SEC media writers," Cignetti posted. "Nice try."

The details: Would Curt Cignetti leave Indiana for Michigan? Thought is '100% false,' source says

The story from CBS Sports discussed potential candidates at Michigan if the school was to move on from Kyle Whittingham. The report suggested Cignetti would be willing to consider going to an intraconference school with more "deeper pockets and resources" amidst concerns over the level of talent IU will be bringing back in 2027.

Before Cignetti's social media post, a source told IndyStar the story is "100% false."

Cignetti, who signed a new contract extension in March through 2033 that's worth more than $105 million, has repeatedly said he plans to stay at Indiana until he retires.
