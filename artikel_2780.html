What do you think of Rams’ Puka Nacua’s suspension?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The speculation surrounding Puka Nacua and his suspension has raised questions such as when did the Los Angeles Rams know the suspension was coming? Did Nacua's potential suspension influence L.A.'s decision to place him on IR?

One question I have, did Sean McVay know the suspension was coming, and if so, was that why Nacua was accounting for 100% (slight exaggeration) of the passing game while Nacua was available? Then again, Matthew Stafford has had a habit of feeding his top targets, so perhaps the two situations are unrelated.

Field Yates of ESPN says that some people do find the timing of the suspension "fishy". Maybe it is, but the rules are the rules and reportedly the NFL Players' Association was able to help arrange this deal for Nacua, and Nacua will take it and serve his suspension.

There has been precedent for this type of situation with players serving their suspension on IR, which indicates nothing truly new is happening for Nacua with regard to how his suspension is being handled. What's done is done.

Will Nacua learn from his alleged negative past behavior? That's a big question for the young receiver. Either Nacua avoids negative headlines or he doesn't. For now, Nacua will serve his suspension, try to get healthy and go from there. 32 teams around the league will be watching how Nacua bounces back or doesn't.

Whether he likes it or not, all eyes on Nacua.

"Timing of Puka Nacua suspension is "awfully convenient"

"The NFL will say that this punishment was handed down because they finally completed the process," Yates said of the league's investigation of Nacua. "We were wondering at the beginning of training camp whether he was going to be suspended to begin the season. …I've had people around the NFL reach out to me and say, 'Funny how that works out, right? Guy gets to miss three games that he would have missed anyway.' So this one smells fishy to a lot of people around the NFL. I find it awfully convenient. I find it awfully convenient."

Whispers about a potential Nacua suspension had hovered over the Rams ever since it was learned this past March that a woman had filed a civil lawsuit accusing him of biting her and of making an antisemitic comment on Dec. 31, 2025. While he checked himself into a rehab facility in the spring to reportedly focus on his "personal growth," it was assumed he'd eventually have to serve a ban."

"There is precedent for players serving suspensions while on injured reserve or otherwise unavailable because of injury. It is not a new rule or an exception created for Nacua.

Most recently, in 2025 the Steelers' Daniel Ekuale was suspended five games for a violation of the performance-enhancing substances policy following a season-ending injury.

In 2024, the Cowboys' Sam Williams was suspended three games for violating the personal conduct policy while already out for the season. The Browns' Mike Woods was suspended six games in 2023 for violating the personal conduct policy while he was out on NFI.

In 2018, the Saints' David Parry, was suspended four games for violations of the personal conduct and substances of abuse policies while on injured reserve."

"The NFL's statement announcing the three-game suspension for Rams receiver Puka Nacua didn't specify the reason for it. Further information provided by the league makes the basis for the ban clear.

It comes from Nacua's entry into a "diversion program," related to the incident that triggered a civil lawsuit alleging (among other things) that he bit a woman on New Year's Eve.

In September, we explained that Nacua had begun the steps required to complete a diversion program, which happened without Nacua entering into a formal agreement to do so. It's one of the unique facets of California law; namely, California gives some defendants the ability to embark on the steps to comply with a diversion program without the kind of express deal that would have automatically triggered review under the Personal Conduct Policy.

The approach ultimately didn't avoid the NFL scrutiny."

"The suspension is effective immediately, and Nacua will be eligible for reinstatement on Dec. 14.

Nacua is currently on injured reserve after suffering MCL and PCL sprains in his right knee, along with turf toe. According to Schefter, players can serve suspensions while on injured reserve.

His remaining three games on IR will count toward the three-game suspension. Nacua missed Sunday's game against the Arizona Cardinals, the first of the mandatory four games he must sit out while on injured reserve."
