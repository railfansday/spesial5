Joey Porter Jr. did not practice Wednesday, but George Pickens was limited

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Cowboys cornerback Joey Porter Jr. (ankle) remained out of practice on Wednesday.

He missed Sunday’s game against the Colts with his injury.

Wide receiver George Pickens (hamstring) also missed Sunday’s game, but he is on the road to recovery with a limited practice on Wednesday.

They are two of 16 players who are listed on the team’s practice report. Half of those didn’t practice on Wednesday.

Besides Porter, outside linebacker Donovan Ezeiruaku (ankle), tight end Jake Ferguson (knee/ankle), running back Tyler Goodson (ankle/knee), safety Malik Hooker (personal), safety P.J. Locke III (concussion), outside linebacker Von Miller (rest) and wide receiver Jonathan Mingo (ankle) also were out of practice.

Linebacker DeMarvion Overshown (hamstring/back) and wide receiver KaVontae Turpin (ribs) were limited.

Defensive tackle Jonathan Bullard (calf), outside linebacker Marist Liufau (forearm), cornerback Devin Moore (hip), tight end Luke Schoonmaker (groin) and tight end Brevyn Spann-Ford (shoulder) were full participants.

The Cowboys opened the 21-day practice window for Moore on Wednesday.
