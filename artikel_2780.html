Jalen Rose admits interest in ‘Inside the NBA’ job after Charles Barkley’s ringing endorsement

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Charles Barkley already has an idea of who he wants to replace him on "Inside the NBA" — Jalen Rose.

Rose, the former NBA player and longtime analyst, said Thursday that he would be interested in joining the award-winning studio show, with Barkley's blessing, of course.

"I love Charles, and yes, that would be something that I would be interested in," Rose told Front Office Sports.

Rose, 53, also expressed appreciation for the opportunities he has received to work alongside Barkley and the rest of the show's cast, as the show opens up to other NBA standouts, like Rose and Draymond Green.

"I appreciate my opportunities the last couple of years to not only contribute to Inside the NBA, but work with TNT on college basketball with Adam Lefkoe, Jamal Mashburn and Bruce Pearl," Rose said.

Rose's comments came more than a month after Barkley publicly endorsed Rose as his ideal replacement while on "BetMGM Gameday Live."

"I think Draymond would do a good job," Barkley said in early October. "I like Draymond, but Jalen Rose is fantastic. He's substituted for us sometimes, Jalen Rose is unbelievable, Vince Carter is unbelievable… but Jalen Rose to me, would be the perfect guy because he's done the most with us."

Rose, who spent 16 years at ESPN before being a victim of the network's 2023 layoffs, occasionally filled in on "Inside the NBA" and other basketball programming on TNT, the show's broadcast home until 2026.

He was greatly appreciative of Barkley's endorsement.

"The one thing I learned about this business is everything isn't transactional," Rose said. "If Charles Barkley is going to say into a microphone that you're the person he feels should replace him, you can't write a check for that, even if it never happens. Basically, what [Barkley is saying] is, 'I'm running one lane in one race, and I'm considered the GOAT to a lot of people, but there's somebody else that I feel like, you know what?

"Y'all should put some respect on his name.'"
