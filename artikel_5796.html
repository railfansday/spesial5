CMS finalized its 2027 Physician Fee Schedule which aims to strengthen primary care, expand preventive services, reduce duplicate billing, and cut waste in Medicare.

Five Massachusetts hospitals sued Eli Lilly, alleging extortion after the drugmaker withheld federal 340B discounts when providers refused to share patient data. (Boston.com)

A quarter of adolescents failed to get at least 60 minutes of physical activity any day the week prior, according to a pooled analysis of 389 surveys. (Lancet Global Health)

A World Health Organization report estimated that six climate and health measures could save 1.6 million lives each year while generating trillions of dollars in economic gains.

A California startup has three orbiting capsules testing microgravity manufacturing, including one that's growing tiny crystals of a potential cancer drug. (The Guardian)

Ohio Gov.-elect Amy Acton (D) paused her acceptance speech Tuesday night to help an attendee experiencing a medical emergency. (The Hill)

The Trump administration plans to cut ties with two medical billing arbitration firms after doctors won over 85% of disputes with huge payouts under the No Surprises Act. (New York Times)

The Pennsylvania health department confirmed a sixth measles-related death in the state as cumulative cases reached 1,467.

Preparing frail, older kidney failure patients for dialysis yielded no measurable quality-of-life advantage over responsive medical management in a parallel-group trial. (The Lancet)

Does eating KFC curse a hospital shift? A Thai surgeon's randomized trial debunked the myth, though local doctors are still wary. (New York Times)

More than 20,000 NHS patients in the U.K. will trial cutting-edge blood tests to detect early-stage cancers. (Independent)

Chemical giant 3M will pay at least $10.3 billion to settle claims that "forever chemicals" from its products contaminated tap water. (AP)

Richard Hodes, MD, who led the National Institute on Aging since 1993, plans to retire next month.

Over 3,500 bottles of blood pressure medication sold nationwide were recalled last month by Macleods Pharma after failing a quality test, according to an FDA report.

Beckman Coulter Diagnostics said the FDA cleared its Access MeMed BV assay, a rapid host-response blood test that helps clinicians distinguish bacterial from viral infections.

Expert groups recommended adding two Ebola vaccines candidates to Congo's ongoing Tumaini trial to test against the deadly Bundibugyo virus.
