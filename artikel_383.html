Rosalía drops 1,000-song Spotify playlist to celebrate LUX

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

If you're spinning the same songs on Spotify over and over again, Rosalía has a monster playlist for you.

Marking the one-year anniversary of her wildly popular, critically lauded, Grammy-nominated album LUX, the Spanish artist shared a 1,000-track Spotify playlist for fans in a Substack post over the weekend.

Titled "1.000 TEMAS QUE TE DAN LUX" ("1,000 SONGS THAT GIVE YOU LUX"), the playlist is a fittingly eclectic, global mix of artists including icons Kate Bush and Patti Smith, Spanish singer Camarón de la Isla, famed opera singer Maria Callas, Icelandic queen Björk (who featured on LUX), Bulgarian ensemble Bulgarian State Television Female Vocal Choir, Egyptian reciter Abdul Basit Abdul Samad, Ethiopian composer Emahoy Tsege Mariam Gebru, as well as German experimental electronica, Spanish flamenco, Ukranian folk songs, Czech polka, Austrian and Italian classical, Japanese Buddhist chants, medieval para-liturgical songs, and Black Sabbath. It's deeply LUX-inspired.

"It's been a year since I gave Lux a try and I still can't believe it, how daring of me, haha," Rosalía wrote on Substack on Saturday.

"I love acts of service; there's something about preparing something for someone else that fascinates me. That's why I've dedicated myself to making a playlist of 1,000 songs that I listened to during the years I conceived, composed, and shared Lux," she added, sharing the anniversary playlist.

Released Nov. 7, 2025, Rosalía’s chart-topper LUX became the most-streamed album in a single day in Spotify history by a Spanish-speaking female artist — a level of popularity echoed in her extremely sold-out LUX world tour. The album was nominated for Album of the Year at the 27th Latin Grammy Awards, with track "La Perla" nominated for Record of the Year, Song of the Year and Best Pop Song.

You can listen to Rosalía's LUX playlist on Spotify.
