This monitor gives sprawling RPGs more room to breathe with its 21:9 aspect ratio and 1,800R curve. The 3440 x 1440 display uses newer QD-OLED Penta Tandem technology, offering up to 1,300-nit peak brightness and improved text clarity.

Whether you want to enhance Red Dead Redemption 2 or get set up for when GTA 6’s PC port is hopefully announced this year, this 34-inch monitor is a fantastic upgrade that’s also fit for work.
