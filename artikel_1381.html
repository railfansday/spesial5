Football Italia Show: What’s really going on with Italy? Mancini, Donnarumma & More

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Lorenzo Bettoni and Richard Hall return with a brand-new episode of the Football Italia Show, taking an in-depth look at Italy’s performances against France and Belgium during the international break.

Lorenzo and Richard assess the Azzurri’s progress, highlight the biggest winners and losers from the latest round of international fixtures, and discuss the key talking points surrounding Roberto Mancini’s side.

They also examine the coach’s mistakes and the decisions that shaped Italy’s recent performances, exploring what went right, what went wrong and what lessons can be learned moving forward.

From standout individual displays to tactical questions and broader issues affecting the national team, Lorenzo and Richard break down all the major talking points in another episode packed with analysis, debate and insight into Italian football.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
