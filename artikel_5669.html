The Finishing Touch: 8 New Stylish Surface Products

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Crossville Reunited is a new porcelain tile collection that brings warmth back to stone with an expression of marble and limestone. The collection features three distinctive visuals and Crossville’s FeatherSoft finish, a surface innovation engineered to create a smooth, silky feel while maintaining the durability and performance of porcelain.

Porcelanosa’s new London collection is a wood-effect ceramic and porcelain tile series suited for both indoor and outdoor spaces. Renowned for capturing the warmth and texture of natural hardwood, it comes in natural tones including light maple (Arce), classic oak (Roble), deep walnut (Nogal), neutral beige (Sand), and warm medium-brown (Olivo).

Marking Anderson Tuftex’s first new wool carpet offering since January 2025, the Alta Moda collection features Mixtura and Velluto, crafted from 100% wool. Mixtura evokes the spirit of heirloom textiles through an organic, patchwork cut-and-loop construction offered in a soft palette of three colors.

Neolith presents a new collection, THE EDIT. A new material language, introducing five surfaces designed around distinct emotional and material expressions. Each model responds to its own code: Pale Fossil evokes serenity; Glazed Opaline, amplitude; Dorset Mist, equilibrium; Serene Emerald, renewal; and Calacatta Viola, empowerment.

Expanding its collaboration with Drew & Jonathan Home, WVH launched two wood surface lines designed to wrap architectural features. A flexible wood veneer panel with a concave tambour profile, Flutto Valley offers shadow, dimension, and warmth to curved walls, columns, and surfaces, complementing WVH’s broader Flutto family.

Expanding its Dekton portfolio, Cosentino introduced two new collections. ARTIK NODES brings icy depth and movement for a modern aesthetic and NOMAK evokes calm through a soft, understated palette. The collections are designed for large surface interior applications including cladding, flooring, and countertops, as well as outdoor applications.

Emser Tile announced the launch of four new collections. Capisco is inspired by the character of quartzite and features soft veining. Duchessa has the appearance of white marble with silver and brown-gold veining. Tortola features subtle tonal variation and offers a refined, contemporary aesthetic. Etrana is a glossy dimensional wall tile with a subtle concave profile.

A reinterpretation of subway tile that combines clean lines and a playful spirit, Tile Club’s Brio 3D Ceramic Subway Tiles feature a multitextured surface, with dimension that contrasts smooth matte and textured gloss within the same tile. Derived from the Spanish word brío, meaning liveliness and energy, the collection embraces a sense of joyful creativity.
