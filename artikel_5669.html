Happy Thanksgiving From Guangzhou, Visiting XPENG

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

While many in the US are celebrating Thanksgiving, I am seeing some of the most advanced technology and most impressive EVs in the world. XPENG offered to bring me to see AI day and Auto Guangzhou. I am thankful for the opportunity. Expect to hear more in depth in the days to come.

After renewing my Chinese driving permit, I was able to drive the sporty P7 again and use NGP with VLA 2.0 to and from XPENG’s increasingly automated and clean factory. That is simply a great car to drive. Acceleration is smooth and powerful. Despite the motor torque, the chassis can more than handle it. It feels both connected and comfortable in a balance that can be hard to find in many cars. And it looks great, especially in the dark green of the test car. I wish I had time to take it to some twisty backroads. However, the intelligent driving system makes dense urban traffic far more tolerable. At the times when you don’t want to drive, NGP drives the car flawlessly. Over the past year I have seen the system evolve, and it now offers even greater perception, smoothness, and efficiency. It is something you must experience to fully appreciate.

I also had the opportunity to ride in the YoYo Robotaxi with my friend Dan Caesar. Compared to some other robotaxi systems, it is a definite step up in comfort. More like limo service than a traditional taxi. The driving is extremely smooth, as is the ride, even in the congested and unpredictable roads in China. It drives much better than a typical human or some of the competitors I sampled. With the seat reclined, massage on and excellent entertainment system playing music or movies, I didn’t want to leave. I could have used another lap. I’ll have more details soon.

The following day, I attended XPENG AI Day. Over the course of the multi-hour event and Q&A, the amount of technological progress I was exposed to was expansive. Not only did I get to hear in depth about all the technological developments at XPENG, but I also was able to ask the IRON humanoid robot a question. With roughly half of the company dedicated to R&D, the speed of progress is impressive.

During the presentation, I heard about major developments in intelligent driving, including innovations that increase efficiency and range. I also heard about milestones being accomplished in flying cars, which took off in a demonstration the next day. However, the humanoid robot walking in to demonstrate the progress ahead of mass production clearly attracted the most attention.

I would like to think that I have a better understanding of these topics than many people, given the experience I have had so far. But the new helping of ideas and concepts was too much for me to process. My brain feels completely stuffed. Like that extra helping of Thanksgiving dinner, it will take a little bit for me to digest everything I have seen. Keep an eye out for more in-depth reports.

However, information was not the only thing I was gorging myself on. The food is exceptionally good in Guangzhou. While I have had great food at restaurants here, my favorite food is at the XPENG headquarters cafeteria at Mom’s Kitchen. This program lets the parents of employees set up shop to sell food at the cafeteria. As many employees come from different parts of the country, the program gives  them a taste of home. I am not sure what the dishes that I was eating were called, but there is something simply better about a home cooked meal. It may not look fancy, but the flavors were amazing. In addition, the hotel had turkey available for dinner, so I didn’t miss out on food.

I also was able to explore some Fall foliage. However, the leaves do not change color here in Guangzhou. Flowers are in bloom year-round, giving it the nickname “Flower City.” As I had done the standard headquarters tour before, I was able to split off with Tanya of XPENG to explore the grounds. Next to the headquarters building is a park with running trails, a pond and a river running through it, including a small waterfall. Throughout the year, there are events in the park, including concerts.

What is particularly impressive in the park is that much of this was bare dirt from the construction of XPENG’s headquarters when I visited in last November. Over the past year, grass, plants and trees were planted. The river was cleaned up to be clean and clear, with people fishing in its waters. Something that I would not imagine doing in most urban rivers. The planting is still in progress, but the environmental restoration is already impressive. And it is a reminder of how important it is to balance industry with nature.

On days that are not too hot, employees will often take their lunches outside or even take out mats to take a nap on the lawn. As the trees grow in further, the shade will increase and improve the comfort further. The park is also open to the public, and you can often see children playing. I heard the most difficult part is that people wish they had more time to enjoy the park. But XPENG has been remarkably busy.

Tomorrow, is Black Friday. A day dedicated to buying stuff. However, I will be at Auto Guangzhou looking at a wide range of exciting vehicles that I can’t buy in the United States. Better cars that are better because they are EVs. Cutting edge technology, premium amenities, and impressive build quality at affordable prices. Vehicles that give you new perspective on what’s possible.

I was able to see clear progress in the industry from the show last November to the Beijing show in April. Although XPENG is launching their models globally, many other brands launch in their home market first. Seeing the speed, progress and competition is invigorating having come from a market that often feels like it is stalling, but it can also be a bit overwhelming. I feel lucky to have the opportunity to see it firsthand and hope I can give people a glimpse of what’s happening.

Overall, I feel thankful for the opportunities to see and experience the some of the most advanced clean technology in the world over the past year with CleanTechnica. I am especially thankful to XPENG for being so open about their technology and the underlying thinking. It has been enlightening to see how the progress is being made in China and how that progress is being brought to Europe. I thank the PR teams for taking such good care of me and answering my questions. I am also thankful to Jackery for showing me innovations in energy storage and solar that create solutions for customer needs in both the US and Europe. And I am also thankful for BMW showing me something to be hopeful about regarding EVs and manufacturing in the US.

I am also thankful for all the great people I have met. From technology leaders to media legends, I have consistently been impressed. I now often feel like I am visiting friends. Business travel can feel lonely, but I have rarely felt that this year.

I am thankful for our readers, who make this possible. I often feel like I am learning from their comments as much as they are learning from what I write.

And I am thankful for my wife for putting up with all my nerdy conversations and trips away from home. And I am thankful for my cats, who are always happy to see me. I’ll be home soon.

Larry lives in Queens, NY, with his wife and cats.
