Scotland face Switzerland to conclude their Nations League group with hopes of promotion still alive.

A 2-1 win over North Macedonia gives Sebastien Pocognoli’s side a chance at the Sitterstadion in St Gallen.

Just one point behind Slovenia in B1, with Switzerland locking up top spot, Scotland must now win, given Slovenia's superior head-to-head record, and then hope for a favour from North Macedonia in the other finale in Skopje.

There is also a knock-on effect for Euro 2028, despite being a co-host, with second in the Nations League likely to hand the Tartan Army an easier qualifying group with a pot two seeding before the draw.

While captain Andy Robertson can become Scotland's most-capped male player in history after drawing level with legendary Scotland midfielder Sir Kenny Dalglish on 102 caps against North Macedonia. Here’s how to watch and follow the Nations League match:

Switzerland v Scotland in the Nations League at the Sitterstadion in St Gallen on Monday 16 November, with kick-off at 7.45pm GMT.

Viewers in the United Kingdom can watch the match live on BBC Scotland, BBC Two and BBC iPlayer. Coverage starts at 7:30pm GMT.

Captain Granit Xhaka is out with a calf injury, while Johan Manzambi also missed the squad to face North Macedonia with a calf problem and has returned to Aston Villa. Dan Ndoye and Manuel Akanji are also out, leaving an opportunity for the visitors against a much-changed side.

Likely to name an unchanged line-up, Pocognoli will be without Stephen Welsh is a doubt with a knock, while Scott McKenna misses out due to a back injury picked up playing for Dinamo Zagreb. Also dropping out of the squad are Josh Doig, Colby Donovan, James Wilson and Lennon Miller.

Scotland confirmed all players trained on Sunday and Pocognoli has no fresh injury concerns for the game.

Switzerland XI: Kobel; Athekame, Elvedi, Akanji, Britschgi; Freuler, Zakaria; Ndoye, Rieder, Amdouni; Embolo

Scotland XI: Gunn; Porteous, Graham, Tierney; Doak, Ferguson, Gilmour, Robertson; McGinn, McTominay; Adams
