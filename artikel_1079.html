When will Mauricio Pochettino take charge of Manchester United?

Michael Carrick has been sacked as Manchester United manager just three months into the new season with the club sitting 18th in the Premier League table.

The Old Trafford hierarchy pulled the trigger on Carrick’s departure on Thursday morning after three days of speculation following a 4-4 draw with Aston Villa which proved to be Carrick’s final match in charge.

Former Tottenham, Chelsea and Paris Saint-Germain manager Mauricio Pochettino has reached an agreement with Man United and will become the new head coach.

Pochettino is the current manager of the United States Men’s National Team (USMNT) but when will he officially begin his tenure at Old Trafford?

Man United’s next match comes on Sunday 22 November when they travel to Anfield to take on Liverpool. It will arguably be the most difficult start possible for whoever takes over and it is a game United need to take something from or else they risk dropping further into the relegation zone.

The current international break would usually provide a new manager with time to train and see the squad in action but Pochettino, should he be given the job, is unlikely to have such a luxury.

That is because the United States have two Nations League matches to play on November 14 and 18 during this window and Pochettino is likely to oversee both fixtures.

The Argentine is still contracted to US Soccer until the 2030 World Cup though an early departure from his deal has now been confirmed. United have made contact with US Soccer over paying compensation to release Pochettino with rumoured figures reaching as high a $10m (£7.4m).

It has also been confirmed that Pochettino will lead the USMNT for their two fixtures with Haiti before flying to Manchester and reporting at Carrington shortly afterwards.

He will be in post at Man United as early as Wednesday 18 November though given the time difference between the US and the UK, Pochettino would arrive late in the afternoon or early evening.

United’s international players are due to return for training on Thursday 19 and Pochettino will oversee his first training session with the club.

That gives him three full days, Thursday 19, Friday 20 and Saturday 21 to settle into his new environment and have time on the grass with the squad.

Whether three days will be enough for the Argentine to inspire a new manager bounce out of United’s playing group remains to be seen.
