C.J. Stroud: I knew what kind of player I could be, amazing to be in this groove

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

After a rough end to the 2025 season, Texans quarterback C.J. Stroud came into the 2026 season needing to show he was capable of the kind of consistent production that would land him a big contract extension from the team.

The Texans' overall season hasn't played out as hoped in Houston, but it's not because Stroud has fallen short. He's putting together his best NFL season and it continued with three touchdown passes in Thursday night's win over the Colts.

It was Stroud's third straight game with at least three touchdown passes and it's the first time that Stroud has put together a stretch like that since entering the league.

"I knew what type of player I could be in this league," Stroud said, via DJ Bien-Amie of ESPN.com. "And to just get in that groove and have a rhythm, it's been amazing. Honestly, I would be lying to you if I said it didn't feel good."

Stroud is one off his career high for touchdowns in a season and on pace for a career high in passing yards as well, which makes him a leading reason for the Texans to hope that they can win enough games to get back to the postseason.
