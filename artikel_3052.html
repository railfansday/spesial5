Luka vs Blazers, Wemby and more: 5 NBA Cup games to watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The second batch of NBA Cup group play games takes place Friday, Nov. 6, with nine games on the schedule.

The slate includes several heavyweight matchups, led by the Los Angeles Lakers hosting the Western Conference-leading Portland Trail Blazers in a rematch of the Blazers' 136-129 win on Oct. 27. The defending conference champion San Antonio Spurs also look to maintain their strong start against the Sacramento Kings.

In the East, the conference-leading Philadelphia 76ers visit the Cleveland Cavaliers, while the Washington Wizards and Atlanta Hawks open the evening's NBA Cup action.

The Luka Doncic train keeps rolling. He enters Friday averaging an NBA-best 38.5 points and has scored 40 or more five times in eight games. But the Western Conference-leading Trail Blazers have won three in a row without Ja Morant and already beat the Lakers 136-129 on Oct. 27.

Can Victor Wembanyama possibly top his 52-point, 24-rebound performance in his last outing against the Dallas Mavericks? It's possible against the 1-7 Kings, who ranked 28th in defensive efficiency.

Oklahoma City has surprised despite a rash of devastating injuries, but must contain Grizzlies guard Ty Jerome, who has become a walking bucket during the first couple weeks of the season, with two 40-point games to his credit.

A possible Eastern Conference final matchup as Cleveland looks for revenge after blowing a 29-point lead to Philadelphia on Oct. 22. The 76ers have three 20-points-per-game scorers and have gotten off to an 8-1 start.

The Celtics have done just fine without Jaylen Brown, as Paul George has picked up the scoring slack in a formidable duo with Jayson Tatum. The Hornets have struggled, losing their past five games and are the only team in the league that doesn't average 100 points a game.

Portland Trail Blazers at Los Angeles Lakers (West Group C) — 10 p.m. ET, Prime

San Antonio Spurs at Sacramento Kings (West Group C) — 10 p.m. ET, NBA League Pass

The NBA's 30 teams are split into six groups of five, with three groups in each conference. Each team plays four group-stage games, two at home and two on the road.

Each NBA Cup game also counts toward the regular-season standings, except for the championship game on Dec. 11. Teams play each of their four group opponents once, with the results determining who advances to the knockout rounds.

Eight teams will advance to the knockout rounds — the winner of each of the six groups, plus one wild-card team from each conference.
