Artists With the Most CMA Awards: Updated Through 2026 Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Miranda Lambert, Chris Stapleton, Brooks & Dunn and Tim McGraw moved up the leaderboard this year.

When the 60th annual Country Music Awards were presented at Bridgestone Arena on Wednesday (Nov. 18), Miranda Lambert, Chris Stapleton, Brooks & Dunn and Tim McGraw moved up the leaderboard of the all-time winners in the show’s history.

Lambert won three awards for her work with the night’s top winner, Ella Langley. This upped Lambert’s career total of wins to 17, extending her lead as the female artist with the most wins.

Brooks & Dunn won one award (vocal duo of the year), upping their career total of awards to 21, more than any other act in CMA history.

Stapleton also won one award, upping his career total of wins to 20, extending his record as the male artist with the most wins.

McGraw also won one award (musical event of the year for a collab with HARDY, Eric Church and Morgan Wallen). He has the most wins in that category and moved up the leaderboard of all-time winners.

Thirteen artists have won 11 or more CMA Awards in competition. Here is a complete list. This list doesn’t count honorary awards. Following the lead of the official CMA list of its all-time top winners, we give artists credit for just one win in a category, even if they also produced or co-produced the recording.
