Taylor Swift Ties Grammy Record for Consecutive Album of the Year Nominations

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

What is perhaps most remarkable about the overwhelmingly female-dominated list of 2027 Grammy nominations is the fact that perennials Taylor Swift, Beyonce and Adele actually aren’t leading the pack.

While Beyonce and Adele were basically off-cycle in 2026 (Beyonce did get a nod for a previously unreleased bonus track for her “Bday” anniversary reissue), make no mistake, it’s another huge year for Swift — her five nominations bring her overall total to 63, with 14 wins, and she’s the only artist in history to win album of the year four times: in 2010 for “Fearless,” 2016 for “1989,” 2021 for “Folklore” and 2024 for “Midnights,” surpassing three-peaters Frank Sinatra, Stevie Wonder and Paul Simon. Her nod this year in that same category for her latest full-length, “The Life of a Showgirl,” makes her only the second artist in history to receive that nomination for five consecutive studio albums, joining Kendrick Lamar — although he actually has never won that prize.

And although Swift’s total 2027 tally was topped by Raye (11), Rosalia (9), Phoebe Bridgers, Miranda Lambert, Ella Langley, Olivia Rodrigo (7 each), Olivia Dean and, oh yeah, two dudes, Bruno Mars and Leon Thomas (6 each), she’s not lacking in big looks: She scored a total of five major nods, three of them in the “big four” categories: along with album, she’s up for record of the year (“The Fate of Ophelia”) and song of the year (“I Knew It, I Knew You,” from “Toy Story 5”), as well as pop duo (for the album’s title track, a duet with Sabrina Carpenter) and song for visual media (also “I Knew It, I Knew You”).

At this point, Swift is such a dominant artist that her past victories actually may be working against her — after a record four album of the year wins, voters may think, “Let’s give someone else a shot this year” — but it seems very unlikely that she will walk away from the 2027 Grammys empty-handed.
