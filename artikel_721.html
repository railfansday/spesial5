Sheila Mangubat, ’90 Day Fiancé: Before the 90 Days’ Star, Dies at 35

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Sheila Mangubat, who appeared on the reality series “90 Day Fiancé: Before the 90 Days,” has died. She was 35.

David Dangerfield, Mangubat’s former fiancé, announced her death in an Instagram post shared on Saturday. A cause of death was not shared.

“It breaks my heart to share the news from her family that Sheila passed away on Saturday in the Philippines,” he wrote. “I am devastated and still trying to process this loss. Please keep her son, her family, and me in your prayers. Dangerfield and Mangubat first connected in 2021 through a Facebook group for deaf singles, and dated long-distance (Dangerfield lived in Omaha, Neb. at the time, while Mangubat was born and raised in the Philippines). The two went on to get engaged, and their relationship was documented on the sixth season of TLC’s “90 Day Fiancé: Before the 90 Days,” which aired in 2023. The couple split in Oct. 2025 following K-1 visa issues.

“90 Day Fiancé: Before the 90 Days” follows Americans who believe they’ve found their soulmate online, per TLC’s official description. They then travel abroad to meet them in person for the first time, navigating cultural differences while deciding whether or not to get engaged. “90 Day Fiancé” follows already engaged couples who must decide whether to ultimately get married or break up.

Mangubat’s sister, Grace, also shared a tribute to the reality star, as was reported by People. “Rest in peace my beloved sister Shiela Mangubat… fly high my sister we will miss you,” she wrote in a Facebook post.

Mangubat is survived by her son, Jhonreil.
