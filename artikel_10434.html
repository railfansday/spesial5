Motorola Razr Flex leaks in real life with Fold 8 and iPhone Duo shape, mystery chipset

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Motorola’s upcoming Razr Flex foldable is expected to have the same wide foldable form factor as Galaxy Z Fold 8 and iPhone Duo, and new leaks not only reveal its design, but hint at some key specs.

Ahead of its supposed 2027 debut, the Motorola Razr Flex has now leaked in a real-life image. The image shows the back of the phone with a dual-camera setup, Pixel-like camera bar, and a design that matches that short and stout look of Galaxy Z Fold 8 and iPhone Duo. It also pretty clearly lines up with past teasers of this device.

Rumor has it that the Razr Flex will be even smaller than Samsung or Apple’s wide foldables, and the proportions of this image make that all the more certain.

Beyond this, some regulatory leaks reveal key specs on the Motorola Razr Flex.

The foldable will apparently run Android 17 out of the box – an update that Razr Fold is only getting just now – and run on the Qualcomm SM8845 Pro chipset. Android Authority notes that this isn’t a chip that’s been announced yet, but it’s thought to be called “Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 Pro.”

Other rumored specs include a 4.97-inch outer display, 6.92-inch inner display, 5,000 mAh battery, 12GB of RAM, and two 50MP cameras around back. Based on information from a past leak, the color variant pictured above is likely called “Coriander.”
