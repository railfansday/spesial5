Fiona the Hippo Fans Furious Over Macy's Hippo Holiday Display

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Talk about tough timing ... Macy's unveiled their hippo-themed holiday display the same day that beloved hippo Fiona suddenly and tragically died.

On Thursday, the department store's Herald Square location debuted happy hippo balloons for its holiday window display ... including a vintage one that was last seen in its 1931 Thanksgiving Day Parade.

But Fiona fans weren't feeling too festive about it ... slamming the unfortunately-timed theme on social media.

Many believe it's too soon after the Cincinnati Zoo’s lovable hippo passed after swallowing a child's hat that went into her enclosure.

TMZ has reached out to Macy's for comment ... so far, no word back.
