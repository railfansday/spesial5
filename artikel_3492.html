Man Killed Coworker With Crossbow After Strangling Woman to Death: Sheriff

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A man is accused of killing his coworker with a crossbow at a Louisiana country club two days after a sheriff claims he strangled a woman to death along a nature trail out of “impulse.”

Jared Schurr, of Mandeville, is facing two separate counts of second-degree murder in the unrelated killings of Lizeht Rodriguez and his former coworker and friend, Woodrow Scott, according to the St. Tammany Parish Sheriff’s Office.

Investigators first learned about the murder of Rodriguez, 24, on November 11, when a citizen alerted authorities to a body off of a trail at the Northlake Nature Center in Mandeville, St. Tammany Parish Sheriff Bret Ibert said during a Monday, November 23, news conference.

An autopsy revealed she was fatally strangled along the nature trail, according to the sheriff.

As authorities investigated Rodriguez’s murder, Ibert said “on Friday the 13th, at 11:30 a.m., Woodrow Scott was brutally murdered in the Covington Country Club.”

Schurr, 36, who’s accused of shooting Scott, 37, in the back with a crossbow, was identified as a suspect through license plate reader technology, according to the sheriff.

He was traced to Mississippi, where he was arrested and booked in the state’s Pearl River County Jail, the sheriff said.

When St. Tammany Parish investigators visited Schurr in custody, he allegedly confessed to killing Scott.

Scott’s loved ones told Fox 8 Live that Schurr was a close friend, with Scott’s three daughters considering Schurr as family, according to his 11-year-old daughter, Kyndall Scott.

“We would call him our uncle. We told him that we loved him,” Kyndall said while speaking to the TV station.

Candice Metz, Scott’s ex-wife, told the TV station that Schurr suddenly shot Scott with a crossbow at a construction site at the Covington Country Club. Scott was reportedly laying down floor tiles at the time of the incident.

Ibert said during the news conference that while Schurr “admitted to the murder of Mr. Scott, at no time did he indicate any involvement in the Rodriguez homicide.”

Detectives investigating Rodriguez’s murder with the help of the FBI searched a creek for evidence relating to her killing and also reviewed digital evidence from local cell towers, according to the sheriff. Evidence uncovered during the investigation pointed to Schurr as a suspect.

At a property Schurr had been renting, detectives executed a search warrant and discovered items connecting him to “the Rodriguez murder scene,” Ibert said.

Afterward, detectives revisited Schurr in jail, where he allegedly confessed to killing Rodriguez.

Schurr was one of several people Rodriguez had passed at the Northlake Nature Center the day she was killed, Ibert said.

Schurr told detectives he visited the park that day for the first time in more than 15 years, according to the sheriff.

He allegedly revealed he grabbed and fatally strangled Schurr in an “impulse” attack, the sheriff said.

Afterward, Schurr allegedly dragged her body off the trail before she was discovered by another person, the sheriff’s office said in a statement.

Schurr remains jailed in Mississippi, pending his extradition.

“This investigation remains ongoing,” Ibert said.

It wasn’t clear whether Schurr had an attorney who could comment on his behalf at the time of publication.
