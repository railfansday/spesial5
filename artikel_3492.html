Inside Mormon Wives' Jen Affleck and Zac's Birth, Parenting Plan Amid Split

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Pregnant Jen Affleck and estranged husband Zac Affleck are doing their best to be cordial as they prepare to welcome their fourth baby amid their ongoing divorce.

“Zac and Jen are in a good place despite their divorce,” a source exclusively tells Us. “They are coparenting and communication between them has been good.”

Although the Secret Lives of Mormon Wives star, 27, filed for divorce from Zac, 30, earlier this month, the pair “talk every day,” according to the insider.

“Zac and Jen are working on baby names, and are really excited to welcome their baby together,” the source says, noting that Zac “will be in the delivery room with Jen and there every step of the way.”

Jen and Zac will “parent together and are still figuring out the plan” in regards to managing their three, soon-to-be four children, the insider adds.

“Jen has asked Zac to continue living together,” the source tells Us, explaining that while the estranged couple are “currently still living together, Zac is looking for his own place and has told Jen that.”

Us has reached out to reps for Jen and Zac for comment.

Jen and Zac tied the knot in 2019, going on to welcome children Nora, 5, Lucas, 3, and Penny, 16 months, November 2021, July 2023 and June 2025, respectively. Jen announced in June that she is pregnant with baby No. 4.

Fans met Jen and Zac on 2024’s season 1 of SLOMW. Throughout the series, the pair weathered a few storms, and they even briefly separated between seasons 1 and 2 of the show.

The former couple relocated from Utah to California in fall 2025 when Jen was competing on season 34 of Dancing With the Stars. They chose to stay after the show wrapped, but their relationship didn’t improve.

News broke on November 3 that Jen filed for divorce from Zac in Utah, where they got married.

“The same reason why I filed for divorce is the same reason why it was really hard,” Jen exclusively told Us on November 10. “It was this fourth child. I had to ask myself very hard questions like, ‘Is this the type of relationship I want this child to look up to?’”

She confessed, “When I realized it was a no, that’s when I realized that I needed to make a change.”

Fans have gotten even more insight into the pair’s split since Hulu spinoff Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County premiered earlier this month.

During one episode, Jen claimed Zac lost $100,000 gambling which added to their marital issues.

“Someone came to Zac’s parents’ house yesterday and was trying to serve him papers,” Jen told the cameras. “They were looking for him and he was like, ‘Remember we lived in that home in Arizona? I guess I didn’t pay all the extra charges for us to move out.’”

Jen claimed that after feeling uneasy about the situation — and feeling like Zac wasn’t being truthful — she checked their bank and “the money is gone.”

“There was over $100,000 in there. He’s done this before multiple times — whether it was through online poker or it was through investing crypto,” she alleged. “I said, ‘You’re not allowed to do that anymore.’ Because he’s done this multiple times.”

The source exclusively tells Us that Zac allegedly lost only $60,000 from day trading, noting, “Jen and Zac have never been in a position where they have been financially unstable.”

The insider adds, “Zac had been pursuing medical school and stepped back to support Jen’s career” before their breakup.
