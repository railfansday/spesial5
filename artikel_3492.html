'NCIS’ Officially Reaches a Series-High Thanks to a Major Formula Change

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

NCIS may be going through a renaissance. While CBS' long-running procedural series hasn't waned much in popularity over the years, the 2026-27 television season has thus far been a very eventful one for the wider franchise. For one, LL COOL J has been given a permanent home once more with the latest spin-off, NCIS: New York, pairing him with a new partner in Scott Caan and a fresh team following the cancellation of NCIS: Los Angeles. Meanwhile, NCIS: Origins Season 3 has Mark Harmon back in front of the camera for a storyline that sees Gibbs' past catch up to him and has reunited him with a few of his old teammates, including Michael Weatherly's Tony DiNozzo and Emily Wickersham's Ellie Bishop.

As far as the flagship is concerned, however, the most impactful difference has been the return of the prodigal son. With his Paramount+ spin-off, NCIS: Tony and Ziva, alongside Cote de Pablo, cancelled at the end of last year, Weatherly returned to NCIS for much more than just a guest appearance or a season-long storyline in Season 24. Tony now has a new position at the Washington Navy Yard, taking up the vacant position of NCIS director left behind by the tragic death of Leon Vance (Rocky Carroll) in the show's climactic 500th episode. Having Gibbs' former protégé as the new man in the chair is more than just a full-circle moment — it's brought new life to the procedural.

Tony's return, combined with the momentum of the spin-offs, powered the NCIS Season 24 premiere to an impressive 6.45 million viewers back in October, officially ranking it as the most-watched program on broadcast television that night. Not only did it rank well against other shows, but it also soared to a high the mothership hasn't reached since 2024 with the Season 21 finale. It marked a 20% increase in viewership from Season 23's average, part of an overall 39% increase for the entirety of the NCIS slate. The momentum has carried over too, with Nielsen recently finding the series still ranking among the top five broadcast series with 10.1 million viewers in live plus 7 days of multiplatform viewership.

It's clear that, at least in the short term, fans are excited to have Tony and Weatherly back in such a symbolic way. His return even made for an especially poignant reunion between him and Gibbs, in which his former boss made it known how proud he is of him. The question on everyone's mind now is how long this stint will last. Weatherly initially departed NCIS back in Season 13 to take on new challenges, namely the CBS legal drama Bull, but that was ten years ago. Showrunner Steven D. Binder recently indicated that his role on the procedural might continue beyond the initial plan, though it's too early to say for certain whether "Director DiNozzo" will be a regular presence for the long haul.

"We take these things one season at a time. On the other hand, you blink and it’s Season 24. So I don’t really know. Network television is a real hand-to-mouth endeavor in terms of getting these scripts on time. We’re doing 20 episodes in a season where a streamer does eight episodes in 25 months. So we’re taking it one step at a time. Certainly, it’s been really great having him in that chair, and I don’t see any reason why it should slow, but I don’t know that we’re looking quite that far yet."
