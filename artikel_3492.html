‘Black Doves’ Season 3 Renewal Officially Addressed After Back-to-Back 10/10 Seasons

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Netflix has released dozens of popular movies and shows so far this year, including I Will Find You, the hit crime show inspired by the Harlan Coben novel of the same name. The show earned well over 100 million views during its first 90 days on streaming, which was more than enough to make it one of the most-watched streaming shows of the entire year. Netflix also found success with the premiere of War Machine, the fan-favorite sci-fi thriller starring Alan Ritchson from the Prime Video show, Reacher. Now that we’re in the final few months of the year, the show taking over streaming on Netflix is Black Doves, which is officially back for its second season two years after Season 1. The show stars Keira Knightley (Pirates of the Caribbean) opposite Ben Whishaw (the Paddington films).

Netflix renewed Black Doves for Season 2 not long after the success of Season 1, but it took some time for the show to go through all the production motions before it was able to come back for its second season on November 5. As with any popular show on Netflix, though, it hasn’t taken long for fans to begin asking questions about the fate of the show beyond Season 2 — this is due in large part to Netflix’s binge model, which allows fans to watch entire shows the same day they are released. While Netflix has yet to officially renew Black Doves for Season 3, creator and showrunner Joe Barton recently confirmed during an interview to break down the finale that he’s already opened a writers room for the third season. As usual for Netflix, it will all come down to viewership regarding a potential third season.

Check out the first two seasons of Black Doves on Netflix and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of a potential Season 3.
