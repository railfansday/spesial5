Forget 'Fallout,' Netflix’s New Sci-Fi Western Officially Rides Onto Streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Wild West has been the hope of some of the most notorious outlaws out there over the years but sometimes, outlaws or ordinary old gunslingers aren't quite enough for the story some people want to tell. And that's exactly the case with Netflix's latest series that mixes the sci-fi wasteland elements of Fallout and the adult adnimation of Arcane.

Netflix's new animated Sci-Fi Western Bass X Machina officially premieres on November 3, 2026, bringing an unusual blend of steampunk technology, supernatural horror, and Western action to the streaming service. Created by LeSean Thomas (Yasuke), the six-episode series features animation from Studio Mir, the acclaimed studio behind The Legend of Korra and The Witcher: Nightmare of the Wolf.

In a lawless Steampunk West overrun by brutal outlaws, machines, and supernatural terrors, a father is forced to become judge, jury, and executioner — knowing that every act of justice meant to protect his family may ultimately cost him the very people he’s fighting to save.

The voice cast is led by Brian Tyree Henry (Atlanta) as Bass, alongside Janelle Monáe (Glass Onion: A Knives Out Mystery) as Glory, Tati Gabrielle (The Last of Us) as Dana, Cree Summer (Rugrats) as Ahni, Chaske Spencer (The Twilight Saga: New Moon) as Lighthorse, Currie Graham (Reacher) as Rivenbark, and Starletta DuPois (The Notebook) as Etta.

The series is executive produced by Henry, Thomas, Jennifer Wiley-Moxley, and Chad Handley, with original music composed by Roman GianArthur Irvin and Nate Wonder through Wondaland. Netflix originally planned to release the show in October before shifting its debut to November.

Bass X Machina has also launched with a stunning 100% score on Rotten Tomatoes, which is a signal that the series is one to be taken seriously. In fact, by comparison, it is officially one of the best-reviewed Western television series of all time, as well as one of the highest-rated science fiction shows ever produced, not just by Netflix, but by any streaming platform. That puts it alongside some of the streamer’s most acclaimed animated originals, including Arcane.
