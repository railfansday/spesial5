/Film Holiday Gift Guide 2026: Cool Toys, Collectibles & Games For Movie & TV Fanatics

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Following our extensive roster of physical media and books for movie and TV fans, we're wrapping up the 2026 /Film Holiday Gift Guide by taking a look at a bunch of toys, collectibles, and games pulling from the latest and most beloved films and TV shows.

Are you in the market for some cool action figures from Marvel and DC movies? Do you want some throwback toys from the likes of the "Star Wars," "Masters of the Universe," "Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles" and "Transformers" franchises? Are you still collecting Funko POPs? Do you need detailed figures of The Muppets? Are your kids still desperate for "KPop Demon Hunters" toys? Maybe it's time to get building with some incredible LEGO sets.

If action figures and collectibles aren't your thing, you may enjoy spending some time putting together some big puzzles from Theory 11, or logging into the latest video games based on movies and TV shows.

Speaking of games, let's kick things off with a fun new holiday movie themed game called We Pitch You a Movie Christmas. Using cards with actors and story ideas, players go head-to-head pitching a wild new Christmas movie, and everyone else decides which one sounds the best. But there are other ways to play too!

Plus, since you like watching movies, don't miss out on the viral sensation that is MGA's Miniverse Real TV and Videos. These tiny toys have a working television that actually plays little VHS tapes, using real clips from movies and TV shows.

Hasbro's Marvel Legends line is aces, delivering figures inspired from the movies and TV shows. Plus, how about that holiday version of Spider-Man?

Meanwhile, the "Star Wars" Black Series figures give us 6-inch scale versions of the saga characters, which puts them ahead of The Vintage Collection for me.

This "Star Wars" Saga Collection Multi-Pack celebrates both of the trilogies that George Lucas created, with one figure representing each of the six movies.

Hasbro's "Transformers" line is still going strong, especially its studio series. These were inspired by the 40th anniversary of "Transformers: The Movie," so that's cool.

LEGO sets offer hours of fun building time, and you're left with an awesome display-piece to show love for your favorite movies and TV shows. Here are the best from 2026.

McFarlane keeps digging into the movies and TV shows of DC Comics to deliver cool new action figures. Their Deluxe Theatrical Edition line of figures is awesome.

However, Mattel is about to enter the DC Comics collectibles fray, and their first figure out of the gate is this "Batman: Hush"-inspired figure, complete with a rooftop diorama and Bat signal that creates moody mist to emulate Gotham City.

The Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles are still going strong too, both with retro action figures and new toys. They even have a collection of fun crossovers with G.I. Joe and Godzilla, inspired by the comic books.
