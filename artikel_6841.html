Willy Chavarria Is Honored at the 6th Annual We Are All Human Gala

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Plus, special recognition for Dolores Huerta and Mark D. Levine and a Cirque du Soleil performance.

Last night at Cipriani Wall Street, the sixth annual We Are All Human Gala celebrated the advancement of the Latin community. “Latinos are prosperity, power, and progress, but the stereotypes, the biases, and the negative narratives have not disappeared,” Claudia Romo Edelman, founder and executive chair of We Are All Human, said in her opening remarks. “That’s why this moment matters, because we have many Latino moments that we together can turn into momentum, and use that momentum to change our perception for good, once and for all.”

We Are All Human, through its Hispanic Star initiative, is a nonprofit aimed at uplifting Hispanics in economic and cultural spaces. According to its website, “Hispanic Star seeks to reframe Hispanics not merely as a fast-growing demographic, but as a vital economic, cultural, and civic force shaping the United States’ future.”

Throughout the evening, many leaders were recognized for their contributions to society. These included fashion designer Willy Chavarria, CEO and creative director of Willy Chavarria; Mark D. Levine, New York City comptroller; Bea Perez, executive vice president and global chief communications, sustainability, and strategic partnerships officer at The Coca-Cola Company, and chair of The Coca-Cola Foundation; and Henry R. Muñoz III, American activist and founder and CEO of Cultural Productions.

“For generations, our people have been told where we can go, how far we can rise, and which doors we’re allowed to walk through on our own land,” Chavarria said in his speech. “Now, it’s the time for us to change all of that. When one of us succeeds, we all get a little bit more powerful.”

Other individuals were also inducted into the organization’s CMO Hall of Fame, and various community organizers and tastemakers gave testimonials on We Are All Human’s impact in their lives. The late labor leader Dolores Huerta was also honored in a moving tribute, and Chavarria also mentioned the feminist activist in his speech. “She carried such strength and humanity and grace, and we all learned from her,” the designer said. “In this time when we see our brothers and sisters and our brown friends and family being treated like animals and abducted and sent away and put in camps and all of that, it’s just so important for us to stand tall in who we are right now.”

Cirque du Soleil took the stage to perform, and guests were given shots of Sotol Romo in champagne flutes for a toast. Co-chairs Gabriel de Alba, founder and managing partner of GDA Luma Capital Management; Karla Martínez de Salas, chief content officer of AVANCE; Bing Chen, CEO and co-founder of Gold House; and Marissa Solis, SVP of global brand and consumer marketing of the NFL, also attended the event. We Are All Human also surpassed its fundraising goals, with two individuals spending $32,000 on a Super Bowl package during the live auction.

“The We Are All Human Foundation is equipped to turn this momentum into results, through data and insights, to guide decisions and to inspire others,” Romo Edelman told the crowd. “Our co-chairs and honorees are the pure embodiment of the Latino brand that we want the world to see.”
