Nina Garcia’s Insider Guide to Istanbul

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

You’re reading Nina Garcia’s Insider Guide newsletter. This edition was originally published in October 2026. Sign up to receive the next edition here.

Istanbul is a city that trades in contrasts—East and West, history and modernity—and its layers go deep. Byzantine cisterns, Ottoman palaces, and a young, fashion-obsessed creative scene all share the same hills. You can spend the morning in a mosque from the 1500s, the afternoon haggling over antique jewelry in the Grand Bazaar, and the evening on a rooftop watching the Bosphorus turn gold.

It’s a city that feels almost unknowable. I returned in August, and I was lucky to see the city through the eyes of some people who know it best: Sinan Sökmen, author of Monday to Sunday Istanbul and founder of Istanbul Tour Studio, showed me around the city’s best-known spots and under-the-radar gems; and Sinan’s longtime friend, Ayşem Erginoğlu, guided me through some of my favorite shopping discoveries, from antiques to jewelry, in the iconic Grand Bazaar and beyond.

For this month’s Insider Guide, I’ve also drawn on the favorites of Turkish designer Dilara Findikoglu, who suggested a few more must-visit addresses, too. Throw in a few visits to the city’s famed hammams and you’ve got a city that blends history, shopping, and the best that two continents have to offer—an irresistible mix.

Istanbul works in every season, but spring and early fall are ideal, with mild weather perfect for wandering. Summer is glorious on the Bosphorus—especially if you’re stopping here on your way to glamorous, jet-set Bodrum—though it can be hot, and winter sees the city at its moodiest.

Getting there has never been easier, withTurkish Airlines connecting Istanbul with the entire world. It’s a fantastic airline: the seats are great, the space is ample. And the service is attentive, with an extensive and interesting menu that makes your in-flight meal part of the trip. My tip? Opt for a pass to the iGA–JETEX Premium Passenger Terminal, which includes fast-tracked immigration, personalized arrival and departure services, and a lounge so incredible you won’t want to leave. Even the longest journeys feel seamless.

Whenever you visit, you must schedule a private boat tour on the Bosphorus—ask your hotel or Sinan to arrange one. Seeing the waterfront palaces, wooden houses, and both shores from the water is the best way to understand how this layered, historic city fits together. Our boat ride was the highlight of the trip, and no matter the time of year, I’d do it again in a heartbeat.
