Lainey Wilson Just Leaked the CMA Awards Seating Chart—Including Where Taylor Swift Is Sitting

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

It’s Tay’s first time at the show in 13 years!

The 2026 CMA Awards are taking place in Nashville tonight, and Swifties can barely contain their excitement over Taylor Swift’s big return to her country roots. This marks the megastar’s first time at the show in 13 years, so…it’s a pretty big deal.

Aside from the T. Swift of it all, it’s sure to be a huge night for other stars like Ella Langley, Megan Moroney, and more. Ahead of Wednesday night’s festivities, host Lainey Wilson leaked part of the CMA seating chart in a hilarious video posted to her social channels on November 17.

She starts by showing her 4 million followers where Ella and her longtime friend and “Choosin’ Texas” collaborator, Miranda Lambert, will be seated.

“Miranda, I told you no smoking in here,” she jokingly says, holding a mini fog machine. She then pointed out Ella’s massive success with her nine CMA nominations—one of which she’s already won.

“Ella, I brought you a bedazzled broom. Because you about to sweep it up,” she added, before moving on to “Dry Spell” hitmaker, Kacey Musgraves.

“You gonna come riding out on that ball sack disco ball?” she teased, referencing her iconic disco “balls” from her Middle of Nowhere Tour.

As for where the woman of the hour/the music industry, Taylor Swift, is seated? Lainey poked fun at her marriage to NFL star Travis Kelce. “So you married you a football player, huh? They’re really something, ain’t they?”

Just a few rows behind Taylor is Philip Sweet, who’s part of Little Big Town alongside Karen Fairchild, Kimberly Schlapman, and Jimi Westbrook. ICYMI, she penned her track, “Better Man,” back in the Red era and passed it to Little Big Town, who released it in 2016.

Tay’s “cowboy like me” collaborator, Marcus Mumford, will also be at the show—but it’s unclear whether he’ll be sitting near her.

“Don’t look at me like that. I will wait. I will wait. I will wait for you,” Lainey said to the Mumford and Sons frontman’s empty seat before adjusting his sign. “But when the show gets here, we just gonna have to sit up straight. Come on.”

Based on this seating chart, I, too, will be sat for Taylor’s big comeback performance at the CMAs tonight!
