20 Years Later, the Best Affordable-Luxury Gift for Teen Girls Is Still a Longchamp Tote

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

There’s a lot of chatter—and, let’s face it, uncertainty—about what teen girls actually want as gifts. I track shopping trends for a living and also happen to be a mom of a Gen Z daughter, so I know how often they change their minds about what’s cool. Fortunately, my daughter is always happy to give me an honest, up-to-date report.

When she graduated from high school, we wanted to get her something nice. More importantly, we wanted to get her something she really wanted. I have to admit I was surprised when she told us she wanted Longchamp’s Le Pliage tote. That’s right, that tote. The one that was all the rage in the mid-aughts.

“It was around 2006 when my best friends and I gifted each other Longchamp totes for our birthdays,” Deputy Managing Editor Cristina Corvino tells me. “They seemed like gold to our teen selves at the time. Who knew they'd still be coveted teen gifts 20 years later? I feel validated!”

On a practical level, getting my daughter an under-$200 designer bag turned out to be a great idea. The tote is made of water-resistant nylon with a leather handle and flap, and I like that it zips all the way shut. It has a roomy main compartment, and it sits comfortably on her shoulder. To this day, it’s the tote she carries around her college campus and the one she brings with her when she comes home to visit.

But, honestly? When I bought it, I had no idea how popular this specific tote is with teen girls. Several of her friends also have the Le Pliage, and when we dropped her off at college, I couldn’t help but notice its iconic silhouette whenever yet another college kid strolled by with one on their arm.

So if you’re looking for a meaningful, decidedly grown-up gift for your stylish teen—and your wallet has grown tired of chasing one fleeting trend after another—I’m willing to bet they’d be thrilled to get a Longchamp. If they already have the Le Pliage tote (clearly, I wouldn’t be surprised), here are a few more thoughtful, under-$250 Longchamp gifts to consider.
