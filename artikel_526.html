Join Costco for $65 and get a free $45 digital Costco shop card — how to claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

TL;DR: A one-year Costco Gold Star Membership is $65, and new members receive a $45 Digital Costco Shop Card.

Groceries, gas, and household basics account for a large share of most family budgets. Buying those staples in bulk may help that money go further each month. If you want to start shopping that way, you can join Costco as a new Gold Star Member for $65 and receive a $45 Digital Costco Shop Card.

A Costco Gold Star Membership lasts one year and works at Costco warehouses worldwide, at Costco Business Centers, and on Costco.com. Costco has more than 500 warehouses across the U.S., and members can shop brand-name products from Kirkland Signature™, Dyson, KitchenAid, LG, and more.

Your membership reaches past the grocery aisles, too. Gold Star Members can fill up at a Costco Gas Station, pick up prescriptions at Costco Pharmacy, and book an eye exam at Costco Optical. Costco Hearing Aid Centers and Costco Travel are also open to members.

Each membership includes one Household Card for an individual who lives at your address. That second card lets one more person in your home shop on the same membership, at the same warehouses and online.

New members receive the $45 Digital Costco Shop Card by email within two weeks of redeeming their membership. If the email doesn’t arrive, check your spam folder. You can use the card at a warehouse or on Costco.com, and you don’t have to spend the full $45 in a single trip. Costco doesn’t accept Digital Costco Shop Cards at the Food Court, and you can’t exchange the card for cash.

This promotion is for new members and for former members whose memberships expired more than 18 months ago. After checkout, you’ll receive a redemption code and instructions by email right away. You need to redeem that code by Jan. 31, 2027, and Costco limits the promotion to one per household. Shoppers in the U.S. and Puerto Rico can purchase it.

Give your grocery budget more room with a year of Costco. Join Costco as a new Gold Star Member for $65 and receive a $45 Digital Costco Shop Card. Promotion ends Dec. 20, 2026.
