US Defense Sec. Hegseth Calls Impeachment Effort a Farce

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

US Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth said the move to impeach him introduced by Rep. Thomas Massie is a farce. He said the resolution amounts to sabotage during a press conference at the Pentagon on Tuesday. (Source: Bloomberg)
