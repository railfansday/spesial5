Le Pen denies receiving leaked information from French interior ministry official

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

French far-right leader Marine Le Pen on Sunday said she did not receive, nor ask for, confidential information on an investigation into her party from a senior interior ministry official. She however could not say if other party members had benefited from the leaks. Hugues Moutouh, the civil servant under suspicion, has denied the allegations and asked to be relieved of his duties pending a probe.
