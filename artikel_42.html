Macy's postpones Thanksgiving parade due to weather

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Macy's has postponed its 100th annual Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York to Saturday because of severe weather.

Preparations to hold the iconic holiday celebration, known for its massive balloons that float among the city's skyscrapers, had already begun when the forecast for heavy rain and strong winds on Thursday led to the delay.

The last time the parade was suspended was during World War Two, but it has never been postponed due to weather. In 1971, Macy's decided to ground the balloons, which often feature beloved characters from pop culture, because of weather.

A powerful coastal storm is set to affect the New York area in coming days.

The potent nor'easter storm is expected to make it too windy and dangerous for the iconic balloons to fly.

Macy's had expected to put 100 inflatables into the sky to mark its 100th parade and feature 10 new balloons, including one depicting the animated dog Bluey wearing a crown and another showing a Minion character riding a banana like a horse.

"While we are heartbroken that our parade was postponed, the safety of our partners, the safety of our participants and spectators is, and always will be, our top priority," said Will Cross, the parade's executive producer, during a news conference on Wednesday with city officials.

Cross also said the giant balloons would be deflated on Wednesday night and stored at a facility in neighbouring New Jersey until they were reinflated for the parade on Saturday.

Macy's confirmed the new parade date and updated a countdown clock on its website to reflect the Saturday schedule. Meanwhile, broadcaster NBC has said it will show the 2025 parade on Thursday.

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani, who will not attend the parade on Saturday because of a scheduled surgery, said at the news conference that "you have to plan for every possibility" and confirmed that "extreme weather has forced" officials to make the change.

"We know that this is an inconvenience for many who come to visit our city. This decision, however, was made with safety being paramount," he said. "We want to ensure that we're delivering a parade that's not only incredible and spectacular, but one also that doesn't come at the expense of anyone's safety."

Mamdani also said that there would be an expanded police presence along the parade route on Saturday and that there were "no known specific or credible threats to the parade at this time".

Cross added that Macy's is working through what costs it could cover as a result of the delay and said the company is in contact with bands, performers and others who travelled to the city for the parade.

During some past parades, strong winds and inclement weather have led to the tearing and deflation of the balloons as well as collisions with lampposts that caused injuries.

The US National Weather Service has issued a high wind warning, valid from 10:00 local time (15:00 GMT) on Thursday until 01:00 (06:00 GMT) on Friday with gusts potentially reaching 60mph (97km/h) - although the very strongest winds are likely to occur after the parade was due to end.

Heavy rain and coastal flooding will pose additional hazards across the eastern US as the storm sweeps through, while some inland parts could also see snow, BBC Weather reports.

The governor of the neighbouring state of New Jersey on Wednesday declared a state of emergency across the state's 21 counties, beginning at 18:00 local time (23:00 GMT).

Governor Mikie Sherrill's office said the powerful coastal storm is expected to bring damaging winds, heavy rainfall, and severe tidal flooding through Friday, with coastal and bayside communities facing the greatest risk. Sherrill urged residents to stay off the roads as much as possible once the storm arrives.

The balloons will be deflated again and then re-inflated for Saturday

Originally called the Macy's Christmas Parade, the celebration, which also features live performers, debuted in 1924 as a way to boost holiday sales. It was founded by Macy's employees and their families.

From 1942 to 1944 New York went without the parade as the US directed materials like rubber and helium toward the country's war effort.

The 2.5mi (4km) procession route begins at Macy's Herald Square flagship store and concludes in Manhattan's Upper West Side, with performers and spectators lining the streets to greet Santa Claus and ring in the holiday season.

Country artist Megan Moroney and Grammy nominee Teddy Swims were slated to perform at the flagship store, but it is unclear whether they, along with the dozens of celebrities and float performers on the roster, would be returning on Saturday.
