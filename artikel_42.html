Malaysia's unwanted refugees: The plight of the Rohingya

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

On November 19, the International Court of Justice delivered a historic ruling: finding Myanmar guilty of committing genocide against the Rohingya in 2017. Malaysia has long been a supporter of the persecuted Muslim minority. But its role as a safe haven is coming to an end, amid a rise in hate speech and calls for the expulsion of Rohingya via social media.
