Tottenham had to wait until matchday seven for their first victory of the Premier League season, but that triumph over Coventry City marked the start of a three-game winning streak that lifted Spurs into the top half of the table. However, they signed off for the November international break with a 1-0 defeat by Leeds United last time out.

Roberto De Zerbi did not pull his punches after the match. The Italian criticized his team's performance at Elland Road, so he will be looking for a response when Ipswich come to town on Saturday. Yet while Spurs are favorites on paper, this has the potential to be a banana skin.

The three promoted teams have been competitive so far. Coventry are on nine points, while Hull City and Ipswich each have 11; none of the trio are in the bottom three. Ipswich will dig in at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, and they have enough attacking talent to ask questions of Spurs on the counter-attack. Julio Enciso in particular has stood out with the Paraguayan ranking amongst the top players in the Premier League for attacking contributions.

Tottenham should nevertheless prove too strong provided they are fully focused on the task at hand, but the north Londoners have been consistently inconsistent over the last couple of years. This one is far from a foregone conclusion.
