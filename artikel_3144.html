SpaceX's AI Business Is Now Nearly Half of Revenue. Here's What Happens to the Stock if Google and Anthropic Both Renew.

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Huge deals with Anthropic and Alphabet have quickly made SpaceX a leader in the AI compute market.

By Keith Noonan – Nov 27, 2026 at 5:06AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Space Exploration Technologies ›

Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX +3.31%) has rapidly orchestrated a pivot that has seen its core focus shifting to artificial intelligence (AI) services. While the company made its name as a provider of orbital launches utilizing reusable rocket technologies and its Starlink satellite-based communication services, AI has already become the biggest sales driver for the overall company.

In the third quarter, SpaceX's super intelligence segment accounted for $7.56 billion of the company's total $14.58 billion in revenue. While the large majority of the company's super intelligence reporting unit generates sales from artificial intelligence services, it also included roughly $538 million in advertising revenue primarily from the X social media platform in the quarter. Even after backing out the contribution from advertising, the super intelligence unit still accounted for roughly 48% total revenue in the quarter.

SpaceX's super intelligence segment revenue increased 195% sequentially in the third quarter and was up 830% on an annual basis. With that kind of momentum, it's virtually certain that AI-derived sales will account for the majority of the company's overall revenue in this year's fourth quarter and going forward. Big contracts with Anthropic and Alphabet are primed to play big roles in driving that growth.

SpaceX has quickly become a powerhouse in the AI compute services category. The company has a contract to provide $920 million a month in AI compute to Alphabet over a 33-month period, working out to a $30 billion deal. It also has a compute deal with Anthropic that's set to ramp up to its full value of $3.1 billion per month this December and potentially extend through 2029 -- working out to a max contract value of $120.5 billion. While the Anthropic deal includes guaranteed provisioning that expires in the first quarter and the option for either party to back out with a 90-day notice, the contract is effectively locked in for the period due to the notice requirements.

To put the significance of just the Anthropic deal in perspective, SpaceX has guided for its Q1 2027 revenue to come in between $33 billion and $37 billion. Meanwhile, that Anthropic service contract is set to deliver sales of $9.3 billion in the quarter -- representing 26.6% of revenue at the midpoint of the company's guidance range. If you include the $2.76 billion in projected sales to Alphabet in the quarter, compute contracts with just those to customers account for 34.5% of SpaceX's midpoint revenue target for the period.

If SpaceX manages to secure contract renewals from Anthropic and OpenAI, it would be a substantial bullish catalyst for the stock. AI has become the company's most important growth driver, and securing contract renewals would be a strong indicator for the company's competitive viability and long-term growth opportunities in the space.
