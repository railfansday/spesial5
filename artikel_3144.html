Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Wildfire integration is moving faster than planned, and debt targets arrived early.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Magnolia Oil & Gas Corporation (MGY +0.64%) reported its first financial results following the closing of the Wildfire acquisition, demonstrating a significant expansion in production and earnings capacity. Management reported that the integration is proceeding ahead of schedule, allowing the company to achieve its leverage targets more than a year earlier than initially projected. The company maintained its disciplined operational model, keeping reinvestment rates below 40% while returning substantial cash to shareholders through an increased dividend and continued share repurchases. Forward guidance for 2027 anticipates moderate production growth supported by a capital budget that focuses heavily on the Austin Chalk formation.
