Abandoned in the wilderness, she discovers she can take the form of anyone she kills. Why you should watch it: "You Won't Be Alone" is a great choice if you're looking for horror that does things a little differently. Its eerie folk-horror setting and unsettling imagery give it a distinctive atmosphere, while the performances help make its stranger moments all the more effective.
