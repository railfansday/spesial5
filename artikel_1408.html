Push it to the Max! Dowman, 16, becomes England U21s youngest-ever goalscorer - as Arsenal starlet scores 89th-minute winner against France

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Published: 18:36 EST, 16 November 2026 | Updated: 18:37 EST, 16 November 2026

Max Dowman broke another record as the 16-year-old became the youngest-ever goalscorer for England's Under-21 side on Monday night.

The Arsenal teenager has already broken a plethora of records at club level, and is now taking that to the international stage too, breaking a record set by senior team star Jude Bellingham, who scored aged 17 years and 67 days in 2020.

Dowman was called into the U21 squad for the first time this international break, and made his debut off the bench in the 1-0 defeat by Portugal on Friday.

On Monday, meanwhile, he was given a start by manager Lee Carsley, and rewarded his manager's faith with an 89th-minute winner to break the record and earn his side a 3-2 win.

It's a record he can add to his already-extensive collection, which includes being the youngest Champions League player, the youngest Premier League goalscorer, and the youngest Premier League winner.

Manager Lee Carsley said after the game: 'He played really well - he was one of four or five outstanding performances. Mikey Moore, Max, Ethan (Nwaneri), they were very good.

Max Dowman became England Under-21s' youngest-ever goal scorer when he scored against France on Monday

'We see how good on the ball he is but he was outstanding off the ball. You have to keep reminding yourself about his age. There's a lot of expectation around him and he seems to handle that so well.

'It's important we keep supporting him and he develops at his own pace. It's so important he enjoys his football.

'Obviously all the players in the squad are under pressure from their clubs and the expectation that surrounds them, so as much as we can we have to make that as easy as we can.'

Despite bursting onto the scene last season, Dowman has just 19 minutes to his name in the top flight this campaign as Arsenal look to defend their title.

He has played a number of games for the Gunners' U21 side, while he was sent off against League Two side Fleetwood Town in the Carabao Cup.

Arteta scolded his player at the time, but said: 'I haven't seen the reaction to my reaction. Whether that's positive or negative. You need to understand the context, the relationship I have with him and what we discussed before we went out.

'If you see the reaction from my mother and father with me sometimes, you'd say "you don't like that reaction", but for me it was the best reaction ever as it enabled me to be who I am.

The Arsenal teenager, 16, broke the record held by Jude Bellingham with his late winner

'But you want to see the best and most beautiful reaction. You want to see the reaction of his team-mates when they have to play with 10 men.

'What do you expect? That's the level and that's the reaction. Whether it's right or wrong, that's the reaction. And the reaction when I don't win, I'm f****d off, big time.

'This is the environment and the reality. We are not in Disneyland, we are in the Premier League. We look after him so much but we are also demanding as he needs to earn the right to play otherwise what are the rest of the boys going to tell me?'
