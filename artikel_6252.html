TAP Air Portugal Adds New Route to U.S.

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

TAP Air Portugal will launch a new route to Washington, D.C., next year.

Starting March 30, 2027, the carrier will connect Porto and Washington Dulles. Flights will operate three times weekly.

Currently, TAP serves Dulles from its hub at Humberto Delgado Airport in Lisbon. Its only U.S.-bound routes from Porto are to Boston and Newark, New Jersey.

TAP officials said the new connection will strengthen the airline’s presence in northern Portugal while expanding access to a vital foreign market.

TAP serves Porto via Francisco Sá Carneiro Airport.

The carrier serves nine destinations in the U.S., the others being New York-JFK, Chicago O’Hare, Orlando, Florida, Miami, Los Angeles, and San Francisco.
