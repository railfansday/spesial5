Korea Box Office: Mystery Thriller ‘White Blast’ Opens at No. 1, Ending Five-Week ‘Chiikawa’ Reign

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Showbox’s mystery thriller “White Blast” debuted atop the South Korean box office over the weekend of Nov. 20–22, ending the five-week chart-topping run of Japanese anime sensation “Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island.”

According to data from KOBIS, the tracking service operated by the Korean Film Council, the newly opened local release generated $2.3 million from 310,890 admissions across 1,244 screens, claiming a commanding 39.6% of the weekend market. Co-directed by Hong Eui-jeong (“Voice of Silence”) and Park Sun-woo and adapted from a stage play, the snowbound thriller stars Kim Yoon-seok as a retiring station master at a remote rural railway station and Koo Kyo-hwan as his newly arrived successor. When a heavy snowstorm isolates the station just as news breaks of an overturned prison bus, the two men discover a corpse inside a railway tunnel and grow increasingly suspicious of one another. Since launching mid-week on Nov. 18, the film has accumulated $3.1 million from 423,196 total admissions.

Toho’s animated hit “Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island” moved to second place in its eighth weekend of release, earning $695,853 from 91,328 admissions across 746 screens for 11.5% of receipts. Directed by Kei Oikawa and adapted from the manga by Nagano, the fantasy adventure crossed the three-million-ticket milestone over the frame, pushing its cumulative gross to $23.8 million from 3,069,422 viewers since its Sep. 30 bow.

Lionsgate’s dystopian prequel “The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping” debuted in third place, pulling in $471,769 from 59,014 admissions across 832 screens to capture 7.8% of the market. Following its Nov. 18 premiere in South Korea, the franchise installment has grossed $716,430 from 91,632 admissions.

Survival thriller sequel “Fall 2: Deadpoint” placed fourth in its sophomore frame, taking in $405,611 from 53,300 admissions across 744 screens for 6.7% of theatrical revenue. The film has accumulated $1.8 million from 240,826 admissions since its Nov. 11 release.

Trot superstar Lim Young-woong’s concert film “Lim Young WoongㅣIm Hero The Stadium 2” debuted in fifth place on the admissions chart with 31,535 tickets across just 154 screens, though it generated the weekend’s second-highest revenue total at $676,840 (11.2% market share). Released via CGV on Nov. 18 to celebrate the singer’s 10th anniversary, the live concert documentary has amassed $1.2 million from 61,861 total admissions.

Damien Chazelle’s 2016 musical romance “La La Land” held sixth place in the second weekend of its theatrical re-release, earning $182,612 from 21,418 admissions across 411 screens for a 3% share. The re-issue lifted the film’s cumulative South Korean haul to $24.6 million from 3,903,309 total viewers.

Next Entertainment World’s corporate thriller “Final Interview” placed seventh in its third weekend, collecting $134,565 from 18,197 admissions across 508 screens. Directed by Kim Jung-hoon and starring Cho Yi-hyun, Kim Jae-won, Shin Seung-ho, and Kwak Dong-yeon, the release pushed its cume to $2.4 million from 325,273 admissions since its Nov. 4 rollout.

Local animated adventure “Maca and Roni The Movie: The Great Memory Exploration Adventure!” took eighth place in admissions with $103,757 from 14,307 viewers across 199 screens, lifting its cumulative gross to $289,844 from 41,101 admissions.

Toho’s anime “Detective Conan: The Counterfeit Case of Ultra 30” placed ninth in admissions with 14,141 tickets across 212 screens, while edging ahead in revenue with $108,272 (1.7% market share) to bring its cumulative run to $1.5 million from 199,460 admissions.

Rounding out the top 10, Warner Bros. Pictures Animation’s family comedy “The Cat in the Hat” brought in $59,263 from 8,938 admissions across 255 screens, pushing its localizd total to $451,759 from 66,730 viewers.

The overall market collective gross for the weekend was $6 million, up from last week’s $4.8 million.
