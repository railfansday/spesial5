Emery praises Garnacho attitude after Villa selection snub

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Garnacho has not played for Aston Villa since making a three-minute cameo in a 1-1 draw against Fulham on 31 October.

Aston Villa boss Unai Emery has praised Alejandro Garnacho's "fantastic attitude" -but suggested other players rank ahead of him after the Argentine was left out of Tuesday's Champions League defeat at Galatasaray.

The 22-year-old winger joined Aston Villa on loan from Chelsea in the summer, but he has played just 34 minutes in the Premier League this season.

Garnacho was left at home for the 2-0 loss by Galatasaray, with Emery bringing 11 substitutes - including two goalkeepers - rather than the maximum 12.

"When he is not involved in the squad it is because I have to choose with other players as well," said the Villa boss on Friday.

"He has my respect first as a person and second as a player, but he is not involved for this reason.

"But football can change. We are playing matches so I will need players."

Not even making a 12-man bench - what next for Garnacho?

Garnacho has played in just four of Villa's 11 Premier League matches this season and has yet to start across all competitions, coming off the bench for all seven of his appearances.

His last appearance - just a three-minute cameo - came in the 1-1 draw against Fulham on 31 October.

"Alejandro Garnacho is training very well. His attitude is fantastic, very good attitude," Emery added.

"He was struggling at the beginning of the season because he was not fit. Now he is fit and he's training at a good level."

Sources have acknowledged Garnacho was not at the level required and were surprised how off the pace he was when he signed in July.

He initially trained on his own, while his problems were not helped by a head injury suffered in Thailand in pre-season.

"Of course now he, and other players as well, are performing well, they are working well, they understood how we are building the squad," Emery said.

"It's not how it was two months ago when he wasn't fit enough to be playing with the consistency we needed.

"So keep going, keep moving forward as a club, as a team, he himself as a player and we will see it."

Garnacho's move to Villa will become permanent for Â£43m at the end of the season should certain criteria be met, but there are concerns his time at the club could become similar to that of Harvey Elliot, who played just nine times last season after arriving on loan from Liverpool.

Fifa rules allow players to play for two clubs in a season - or three if they move to a team whose season overlaps like the Major League Soccer - meaning Garnacho could feature for another team, unlike Elliott as he had already played for Liverpool and Villa.

However, Emery said he was not thinking about the upcoming January transfer window as he added: "It is November, of course there is a transfer window in January, but we are not thinking about the transfer window in January. Me especially."

Does this damage Villa after Elliott lost a year of his career last season?

There was already talk in the game of how players could be fearful it could happen to them - and that caution may increase with Garnacho's situation.

Having happened twice in two seasons it would also leave clubs with second thoughts too given the players are their assets.

Garnacho has managed just 95 minutes across all competitions, having joined Villa to revive a faltering career after an inconsistent first season at Chelsea.

Head of football operations Damian Vidagany was unrepentant when asked about the winger at a fans' forum on Thursday, with Emery's trusted lieutenant fiercely backing the manager's judgement call.

Villa, though, arguably need a player like Garnacho - who can produce moments of magic like his stunning goal for Manchester United against Everton in 2023 - having failed to score in four of their last six winless games.

Emery wanted a pacy winger in the summer and the Argentine had been on their wanted list all year.

They need some need some creativity, so with Emery, at least publicly, refusing to rule out a January departure there could still be a slim chance of redemption.
