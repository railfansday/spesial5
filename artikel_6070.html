There are all kinds of rewards these days — for travel, dining, retail, or even your local coffee shop.

But there's one currency built specifically for sports fans that rewards you with all the things that drive your fandom — tickets, gear, collectibles and even unique experiences.

And thanks to the new Fanatics American Express® Card, there are now even more opportunities to earn it.

Here’s everything you need to know about FanCash, Fanatics' loyalty currency.

FanCash is the one currency built for sports fans. It takes everything we love about a loyalty program — great earning rates and a variety of redemption options — and merges it with the passion so many of us feel for our favorite sports teams. The end result? The best way to turn your fandom into real rewards.

But now, there are even more ways to earn FanCash, thanks to the launch of the new Fanatics American Express Card. This card takes your fandom to the next level by rewarding you with unlimited 5% FanCash on purchases across the full Fanatics ecosystem. Plus, you can earn 5% FanCash on ticket purchases and select streaming partners.*

Any FanCash you earn will expire one year from the date of purchase. However, any subsequent eligible Fanatics purchase will automatically extend the expiration date for qualifying FanCash in your account.

But with the Fanatics American Express Card, the FanCash earned on the card will never expire, as long as you remain an active cardmember.

You can start earning rewards by signing up for a free Fanatics ONE account.

The best way to earn FanCash is with the new Fanatics American Express Card. For starters, you'll earn unlimited 5% FanCash across the entire Fanatics ecosystem — including Fanatics.com, Lids, Mitchell & Ness, Fanatics Collect, Topps.com, League stores and more. You'll also earn 5% FanCash on tickets from other vendors and select streaming partners, plus 2% FanCash on all other purchases.* And when you stack on the Fanatics App, you can earn 8% or more.

In addition, new cardholders earn $100 FanCash after spending $1,000 on purchases in the first 90 days. Plus, earn an additional $100 FanCash for spending $500 a month for 12 consecutive months.**

The Fanatics American Express Card has no annual fee*** and no foreign transaction fees — allowing you to earn a ton of FanCash every year, whether you’re close to home or cheering for your favorite team from abroad.

Finally, eligible U.S. Membership Rewards cardmembers with American Express-issued cards can now transfer Membership Rewards points into FanCash — at a rate of 1,000 points for $10 in FanCash — giving you even more opportunities to spend on gear, collectibles, tickets and more.

Once you’ve earned FanCash, there are a variety of ways to use it across the Fanatics ecosystem. $1 in FanCash gets you $1 in rewards toward your purchase. It’s that simple. No complicated redemption math. That $100 NFL or NBA jersey you've been eyeing? It can be yours for $100 in FanCash.

But perhaps the most powerful aspect of this loyalty currency is that there’s no minimum redemption amount. Unlike other cash-back rewards, you don’t need to wait until you’ve accumulated a certain amount to begin saving — though the more FanCash you have, the more redemption options you unlock.

But it’s the other features of the Fanatics American Express Card that are potentially even more exciting for sports fans.

For starters, cardholders receive 2,500 Fanatics ONE Tier Points upon approval, immediately unlocking Member Pro status and even more rewards and benefits. Plus, you'll receive an additional 2,500 Tier Points every year you have the card.^ This unlocks even more rewards and benefits through the Fanatics ONE program.

Plus, you can select from one of over 500 players for your card design, turning the card itself into a collectible — and a badge of your fandom in your wallet.

Finally, starting in 2027, you’ll have access to the Championship Challenge as a cardholder. If you correctly predict all four champions across the NFL, NBA, NHL, and MLB, you’ll get up to $100,000 of your annual spending back.

Eligibility requirements apply for the Championship Challenge. Program details, including timing and structure, are subject to change. Terms and conditions apply. Full details to be announced prior to launch.

FanCash is the currency designed to reward sports fans for purchases they're already making. And with the new Fanatics American Express Card, plus the ability to convert Amex Membership Rewards points into FanCash, there are more ways than ever to turn your fandom into tangible rewards.

And with even more exciting benefits to come next year, now is the perfect time to join the waitlist and be among the first to know more.
