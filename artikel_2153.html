Birmingham City, Celtic & Blackburn eye Chelsea man who could be 'better than Messi'

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Blackburn Rovers and Birmingham City are both keen on a deal to sign Kendry Paez from Chelsea.

According to Teamtalk, the two Championship sides are both keen on the 19-year-old ahead of the January transfer window.

The teenager, who has been tipped to potentially be 'better than Messi' by ex-Ecuador international Carlos Tenorio, is currently a part of the first team squad at Stamford Bridge after returning early from a loan spell with River Plate.

However, he is not a part of Xabi Alonso’s plans at the moment, and has yet to play a single minute for the Spaniard this season.

The attacking-midfielder could be available in the new year, with the Premier League side seeking a loan move in order for Paez to gain valuable experience.

Chelsea signed the Ecuadorian in 2025 in a deal worth £17.3 million, but he has yet to make his debut for the club.

It’s believed that both Birmingham and Blackburn are keen on a deal to sign Paez when the winter market opens in January.

However, the pair will face competition from Scottish giants Celtic, who are also interested in signing the 19-year-old.

The playmaker previously spent time on loan with Strasbourg, with the French club owned by the same group as the Blues.

But he was only capable of making 15 appearances in Ligue 1, including four starts, scoring on just one occasion before going to River Plate January 2026.

It’s understood that Chelsea have been encouraged by his performances at underage level, and are now seeking a loan deal again in the upcoming January market.

Blackburn and Birmingham are both keen on a deal, but there are clubs from across Europe keeping tabs on the player’s progress, including Trabzonspor and KV Mechelen.

The Belgian side reportedly made an approach last summer, and are aiming to be at the front of the queue again in the new year.

Birmingham moved into top spot in the Championship table last weekend ahead of the November international break, with a 2-1 win against Swansea City lifting them ahead of Middlesbrough in second.

Meanwhile, Blackburn Rovers moved up to 13th with a 2-1 win of their own at home to West Ham, with eight points separating the two clubs chasing Paez’s signature.

Chris Davies’ side will be targeting promotion back to the Premier League in the second half of the campaign, while the Lancashire outfit will be aiming to break into the top eight.

Birmingham will return to action against Stoke City on 21 November, while Rovers will travel to face bitter rivals Burnley the following afternoon.

Paez wasn’t ready for top flight football in France last year, and his underwhelming performances reflected that.

But he’s now had time with Chelsea’s underage team to develop his game and better prepare for senior football, as he is still only 19 and moving from South America to Europe isn’t an easy transition.
