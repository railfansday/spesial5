Barnsley developments hand Sheffield United & Derby County clear Reyes Cleary boost

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Barnsley, Reyes Cleary, Sheffield United, and Derby County could all become central to an interesting January transfer situation, with the talented winger attracting attention from higher up the football pyramid.

Reyes Cleary has emerged as one of the most exciting attacking players in League One with Barnsley, and it is hardly surprising that Championship clubs are monitoring his situation again after having interest in the 22-year-old in the summer.

Both Sheffield United and Derby County have reason to strengthen their attacking options, while Barnsley face an increasingly important period in their own campaign. There could be some significant developments at Oakwell over the coming weeks ahead of January.

Reyes Cleary is arguably one of the most exciting and explosive wide players in League One, so it is little surprise that the likes of Sheffield United, Derby, and Bristol City have been credited with an interest in securing his services.

It's evident that he has the individual quality to make the step up to the Championship and would represent an exciting addition for any of the sides keen on him at a higher level.

However, Barnsley's latest defeat to Wigan Athletic has made their league position even more precarious, with the threat of relegation to League Two becoming increasingly difficult to ignore.

The 25 top scorers in the Championship since the second tier was rebranded back in 2004/05, including Billy Sharp and Aleksandar Mitrovic.

That could have significant implications for Cleary's future. Barnsley adopted a hardball stance during the summer and priced interested clubs out of a move, but their current predicament makes that approach considerably harder to justify.

Cleary might be vital to their survival hopes, but they cannot necessarily expect to retain him on their terms if Championship interest intensifies in January. Barnsley are now second-bottom and three points off safety, albeit with games in hand on some of the sides above them.

Barnsley deserve considerable credit for identifying Cleary's potential in the first place. He had shown promise during his time with Hartlepool United in the National League, but not many League One clubs would have taken the gamble of recruiting somebody two divisions higher and backing him to make the step up.

It has proved to be an inspired decision, although Barnsley now face a difficult situation with just 18 months remaining on his contract come January. Sheffield United and Derby are unlikely to be the only Championship clubs interested, either, with Bristol City also keen.

West Bromwich Albion should also have serious regrets about allowing Cleary to leave as early as they did. He looks every bit a player capable of establishing himself in the Championship, and a return to The Hawthorns could even be something worth exploring if they recognise their previous mistake.

That said, Barnsley have to be braced for offers and interest again, with it becoming increasingly to stand in the player's way if he is determined to test himself in the second tier. That said, Barnsley will be desperate to keep hold of arguably their best player.

There is, of course, another side to this argument. If Barnsley cannot turn their fortunes around and ultimately suffer relegation to League Two, Cleary's market value could fall considerably for clubs like Derby and Sheffield United, particularly with only 12 months remaining on his contract next summer.

From a financial perspective, January might just be their best opportunity to secure a substantial fee. However, selling him could also be a grave sporting mistake, too. Cleary is arguably Barnsley's most talented player and possesses the individual quality to help drag them away from the relegation places.

Losing that sort of match-winner midway through a difficult campaign could make an already challenging situation considerably worse. The Tykes have to weigh up the financial benefits of cashing in against the very real possibility that they need Cleary more than ever to preserve their League One status.

There are only three players with more than three goal involvements for Barnsley in all competitions this season, including Vimal Yoganathan with five, as well as both Cameron McGeehan and Cleary with nine apiece. No one can better Cleary's three assists, either.
