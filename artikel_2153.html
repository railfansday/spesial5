Thomas Tuchel makes Noni Madueke decision as five Arsenal stars earn England call-ups

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Noni Madueke has missed another England opportunity, despite making an encouraging impression during his recent run in the Arsenal starting line-up.

Thomas Tuchel has named his 25-man squad for the Nations League fixtures against Croatia and Spain in November, but Arsenal winger Madueke misses out again after failing to make the cut following a missed international camp.

He has been left out despite making the most of Bukayo Saka’s absence to increase his involvement under Mikel Arteta.

Madueke scored in the recent Premier League win against Nottingham Forest and then again in Wednesday’s Champions League win against Slavia Prague.

He had a legitimate claim to be named in the squad, and Tuchel admitted that Madueke was very close to being included.

The England boss acknowledged that Madueke has benefitted from his extra games at Arsenal due to Saka’s injury.

But Tuchel chose to keep the same front three that he had during the last international camp. The German wants to reward those players who have earned their place in the team and not make changes every time they are playing well at club level.

This means Madueke will be left frustrated, having to wait until next time.

Madueke has played under Tuchel before, so it shows how much he thinks of him as a player. He featured in England’s 2026 World Cup campaign, so it is noticeable that he was left out this time.

Although he can’t control the battle for places, he will now be able to continue building his case. With Saka still injured, he will have the chance to play more regularly and solidify his place in Arteta’s plans.

However, Madueke is not the only player at Arsenal who has had positive news.

Declan Rice, Eberechi Eze, Ezri Konsa and Myles Lewis-Skelly have all earned or retained their spots in Tuchel’s squad. Ben White also comes back into the England squad, having missed the last camp.

It is another chance for White to re-establish himself on the world stage after his knee injury kept him out of the World Cup.

It also means Arsenal have five players in the squad after Saka was left out due to injury.

England play Croatia at Wembley on November 12 before heading to Spain on November 15. Madueke will be watching from home, knowing he was almost part of the squad.

Now he needs to build on his recent form and become a consistent performer for Arsenal. Whilst Tuchel has left the door open, Madueke needs to give the England boss another reason to change his mind.
