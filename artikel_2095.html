Sheffield Wednesday star Barry Bannan posts hilarious Aston Villa throwback

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Sheffield Wednesday icon Barry Bannan is closing in on 500 appearances for the Owls, having returned to the club in the summer. However, he's not always been at Hillsborough and was actually quite the journeyman at the start of his career.

It was at Aston Villa where it all started for the Scottish international, having taken on an opportunity with the club after a couple of years with Celtic's youth academy.

Bannan's debut came in December 2008 in a UEFA Cup clash with Hamburg - a game that saw Villa lose 3-1. That was around the time that the 36-year-old had signed his first professional contract with the club, a moment that he's been reflecting on.

Over on Instagram, Bannan is particularly active on his story, sharing photos and comments.

On Wednesday morning, he shared an image of the day he signed his first professional contract with Villa, around 2008, surrounded by what looks like family, a potential agent and/or club officials.

Bannan can quite clearly see the funny side of the image that was taken 18 years ago, with a laughing emoji accompanying the words: "First ever pro contract at Aston Villa flashback".

The midfielder would play 82 times for Villa in all competitions, scoring two goals. In the period that he was at Villa Park, he had loans with Derby County, Blackpool and Leeds United, before sealing a move to Crystal Palace on summer transfer deadline day 2013.

Bannan was a tidy footballer at Villa and highly-rated young midfielder. His time at Palace brought a loan to Bolton and, ultimately, wasn't the success that the South London club had hoped.

By the time August 2015 came, Bannan was signing with Sheffield Wednesday and tweeting: "Glad to have joined @swfc on a permanent transfer today new start and glad to be going somewhere to play regular football and a fresh start."

Barry Bannan stats at Sheffield Wednesday (18/11/2026)

He got that regular football as Sheffield Wednesday challenged for promotion to the Premier League in his first two years at the club. The Scotland international became a real cult hero in that time, and has subsequently stuck by Wednesday through a lot of on-field and off-field turmoil.

Bannan is now approaching 500 games for the Owls, taking only a short break from representing the club at the start of 2026 when he joined promotion-chasing Millwall at a time Wednesday's off-field turmoil at the hands of Dejphon Chansiri was at its worst.

After returning in the summer, Bannan is eyeing another League One promotion with Sheffield Wednesday - the club that's been his first proper home since he agreed those professional terms with Villa 18 years ago.
