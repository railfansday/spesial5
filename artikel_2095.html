Sheffield Wednesday injury update on Louie Barry pre-Plymouth Argyle clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Sheffield Wednesday have been without the winger since early September due to an ankle injury. SWFC are back in League One action this weekend with a home clash against Plymouth Argyle at Hillsborough.

Barry, 23, was snapped up by the Owls over the summer on a permanent basis from Aston Villa and he penned a four-year deal with the South Yorkshire outfit until June 2030, with the option of a further 12 months on top of that. He has since made six appearances in all competitions this season and has chipped in with three goals and one assist.

In this latest update regarding his condition, his head coach Challinor has said, as per the official club website: “We hope to see him back certainly within the coming weeks. Maybe Saturday; we’ll see what the next few days look like.”

Sheffield Wednesday are positioned in 9th place in the table after their first 13 fixtures and they are two points off the play-offs behind Reading in 6th. They have drawn their last two league outings on the spin against Cambridge United and Blackpool.

Sheffield Wednesday will be looking forward to having Barry back. SWFC’s form hasn’t been great over recent times.

The Owls have won only one of their last eight matches in all competitions. Challinor knows Barry well from their time at Stockport County together and has managed to get the best out of him in the past.

Barry was signed by his current club in the last transfer window to bolster their attacking department. When fit, he will give them more competition and depth to pick from at the top end of the pitch.

The ex-England youth international was on the books of Aston Villa from 2020 to 2026. He played once for their first-team and was loaned out to Ipswich Town, Swindon Town, MK Dons, Salford City, Hull City and Sheffield United, as well as Stockport County, to get some experience under his belt and to boost his development.

Barry is a proven performer at third tier level and will inject some serious quality into Sheffield Wednesday’s starting XI. SWFC fans will be excited to see him back in action for them.

Challinor will be eager to get a win over Plymouth Argyle on Saturday afternoon. He has lost his first two outings against Oldham Athletic and Accrington Stanley in the FA Cup and EFL Trophy respectively.

The new Owls’ manager will get the chance to put his own stamp on his newly inherited squad with some signings in January. He will also have the opportunity to offload some players if needed.
