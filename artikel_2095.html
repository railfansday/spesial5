Mauricio Pochettino set to get involved in Bruno Fernandes contract talks

Fernandes has been one of United’s most influential players since arriving from Sporting CP in January 2020.

Bruno Fernandes has scored 113 goals in 341 appearances for Manchester United and has regularly carried the creative responsibility in midfield for the Red Devils.

His performances have remained amazing despite United struggling in recent times. Despite a sluggish start to the last season, Bruno Fernandes went on to register 21 Premier League assists, breaking the league’s single-season record.

Mauricio Pochettino will now have the opportunity to speak directly with the midfielder and make his own case for him to remain part of the project.

However, there are several issues still to resolve. The financial terms, length of the proposed contract and release clause will all need to be discussed before an agreement can be reached with the Portuguese midfielder.

Mauricio Pochettino is preparing to hold talks with Bruno Fernandes over his Manchester United future, with the club captain yet to agree a new contract at Old Trafford.

According to Fabrizio Romano, the new United head coach will now become involved in discussions with the Portuguese midfielder, whose current deal expires at the end of the season. The club have the option to extend it by a further 12 months, but are also keen to secure a longer-term agreement.

Manchester United have already held negotiations with Fernandes, but the 32-year-old has rejected two contract offers. Romano explained that Pochettino’s involvement could be an important part of the talks, although the final decision will rest with Bruno Fernandes and United.

Speaking on his YouTube channel, Fabrizio Romano said: “First point: Mauricio Pochettino will be involved now in discussions with Bruno Fernandes.

“So, Pochettino will talk to Bruno, and he’s going to enter into this conversation.

“Then it’s going to be on Bruno, it’s going to be on the club.

“It’s a financial point, length of the contract, the release clause.

“It’s not Pochettino deciding yes or not, but Pochettino will be another factor now in the conversation.

Fernandes’ future is not settled at Manchester United and with Pochettino arriving at the club, he may have a different thought about his contract situation. Although a deal is not finalized yet but the talks will give the new head coach a chance to understand Fernandes’ position. However, the final call remains with Bruno Fernandes.
