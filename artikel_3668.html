Man Reportedly Stabbed in Neck During Halloween Party at Jamie Foxx's Home

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

A Halloween party at Jamie Foxx’s California home reportedly turned violent after a man was stabbed in the neck.

A partygoer who attended Foxx and daughter Anelise Foxx’s annual Skvlk Fest event at the star’s Thousand Oaks mansion on Saturday, November 7, was treated for a superficial neck wound, TMZ reported on Sunday, November 8, citing law enforcement sources.

A male suspect was reportedly arrested for assault with a deadly weapon, per the outlet.

Us has reached out to the Ventura County Sheriff’s Office for comment.

Neither Foxx, 58, nor his daughter has publicly commented on the alleged incident.

On Sunday, the Oscar winner shared several clips from the festival via Instagram. “@skvlkfest @anelisefoxx was 🔥🔥🔥. @djsophia_ you killed it once again!!” he captioned one post.

Foxx shares daughter Anelise, 18, with ex Kristin Grannis. He also shares eldest daughter Corinne Foxx, 32, with ex Connie Kline.

News broke earlier this month that the actor had welcomed his third child with girlfriend Alyce Huckstepp.

Foxx first opened up about becoming a dad again back in August.

“Being my age, I was a little on the fence,” he told E! News, adding that he changed his perspective after having a conversation with Al Pacino, who welcomed son Roman, 3, in 2023 at age 83.

Foxx said, “But then I ran into Al Pacino a few months ago. ‘I said, ‘Al, you gonna be back in the drop-off line?’ I said, ‘Aw man, shoot, I’m good.’”

The Back in Action star recently praised Corinne for helping him recover after he suffered a stroke in 2023. A year later, he said the stroke was caused by a brain bleed.

“When you look at The Godfather and Michael Corleone is out with his girl on Christmas, he picks up the newspaper and sees that his father has been shot, and he says, ‘It doesn’t say if he’s alive,’ and he’s not really telling his girl what he’s reading,” Foxx said of Corinne during an August appearance on the “Baby, It’s Keke Palmer” podcast. “Michael Corleone left and he went to that hospital, and he took over. He told one of the guys, ‘There were people coming here to do danger to my father. I need you to help me.’ That’s who she was.”

“When I talked to the doctor, I said, ‘Hey, listen. I hear everybody’s stories. I want to know from you what it was,’” he recalled. “He said, ‘Hey man, without her, you wouldn’t be here.’ So I said, ‘Hey, that’s Michael Corleone forever.’”

Foxx added, “The doctor was like, ‘No one handles it like this … because you were on the slab, TMZ, all these different [people were asking for details], and she handled it.’”
