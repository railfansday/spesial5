Rob Kardashian Shares Rare Photo With Daughter Dream, Blac Chyna Reacts

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Rob Kardashian celebrated his daughter Dream’s milestone birthday with a rare family photo.

On Saturday, November 21, the Keeping Up With the Kardashians alum, 39, shared a photo of himself with Dream in honor of her birthday, as she recently turned 10 on November 10. The former reality star wore a Dodgers cap and Nike T-shirt as he posed side-by-side with Dream.

Rob also included a look at his daughter receiving a leopard-print birthday cake to match her outfit, in addition to showing Dream donning a crown for her special day.

“Birthday fun yay yay I love you Dreammmmmmmmmm,” Rob wrote via Instagram.

Dream’s mom, Blac Chyna, celebrated the special moment as well, commenting on Rob’s post, “✨Dreamy & Daddy✨”

Rob largely eschews the spotlight these days, though he recently stepped out for sister Kim Kardashian‘s 46th birthday in October. He joined mom Kris Jenner and sisters Kourtney Kardashian, Khloé Kardashian and Kylie Jenner, to party with Kim at Baldi at the Waldorf Astoria Beverly Hills on October 21.

Clad in all black, Rob was all smiles as he was photographed leaving the famed eatery with his siblings, per DeuxMoi.

Blac Chyna (real name Angela Renée White) called Rob on her Kick livestream days later to reveal that social media was melting down over his public appearance.

“Oh, and I wanted to tell you, too, I seen your pictures, too, on the internet,” Chyna, 38, told Rob over the phone on her October 24, Kick livestream. “And everybody’s like, ‘Ooo, Rob! Rob!’ They’re going crazy over you.”

Rob seemed to be taken aback by the reactions, admitting to her, “I don’t know why they’re saying that, ‘cause I felt like I looked crazy. But it’s OK.”

Joking that her ex was being “modest,” Blac Chyna assured Rob that everyone in her livestream chat thought “the pictures were fire.”

“No, that’s just how I feel,” Rob replied. “But it’s all good. All good.”

Prior to his birthday outing with Kim, he made a brief appearance during The Kardashians season 7. Khloé, 42, called Rob during the season 7 finale to tell him their fans were “fangirling” and “swooning” because he showed up in the reality show’s trailer.

When an offscreen producer asked if Rob was “coming back for season 8,” he laughed and teased, “I would hope to be on season 8. I definitely want to film on season 8.”

Rob has yet to appear on screen on The Kardashians season 8 though fans shouldn’t definitively count out a cameo before the December finale. He did recently pop up on Prime Video’s Fully Torqued Unleashed to ask host Steve Pazamy to restore his late father Robert Kardashian‘s cousin Cici Bussey‘s 1963 Mercedes.

The Kardashians is available to stream via Hulu and Hulu on Disney+.
