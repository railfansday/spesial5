Vitalik Buterin Backs an AI Slowdown as AI Tokens Slide 30%: Is the AI Crypto Bubble Popping?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Vitalik Buterin backs slowing the AI data center race. AI tokens fell 29.7% in 30 days, three times Bitcoin. Is the AI crypto bubble popping?

Vitalik Buterin said on Sunday that slowing the AI data center race is probably a good thing, as the crypto bubble around AI tokens loses air. The Ethereum co-founder made the case in a post on X.

He pushed back on what he called the “strong emotional reaction” against the anti-data-center movement.

I know this is a predictable opinion from me but I don't agree with the strong emotional reaction against the anti-data-center movements that I've seen this week.

As I see it, algorithmic progress means that whatever you would be able to do with more and bigger data centers in 2…

— vitalik.eth (@VitalikButerin) November 8, 2026

That movement had a big week. On November 3, voters in 10 states decided 41 local ballot measures on data centers, Ballotpedia counted. A “yes” vote on 28 of them would limit building through bans or moratoriums. After the vote, Senator Bernie Sanders called for a “national data center moratorium now,” one of several Democrats pushing to rein in the AI buildout.

Crypto’s AI corner has felt the same chill. Seven of the largest AI tokens fell 29.7% on average over the past 30 days, almost three times Bitcoin’s 10.7% drop.

So is the slowdown Buterin wants already showing up as a popping bubble in AI tokens?

His case rests on three points, and he made it, in his words, “without any controversial ‘doomer arguments’.”

He concluded that “a slowdown in compute hyper-scaling seems quite net good.” Even if it proved a mistake, it would not be worse than “10+ other political things,” he added.

The view fits what Buterin has argued since 2023, when he set out “d/acc,” his name for speeding up defensive technology first, in an essay titled My techno-optimism.

AI tokens are the part of crypto that trades most directly on the AI boom. They include networks that sell computing power, run AI agents or pay people to train models. CoinGecko valued the sector at $18.9 billion on Monday morning.

We compared the 30-day moves of seven of the largest AI tokens on Binance with Bitcoin and Ether.

The selling lines up with doubts about AI spending in the stock market. Last Thursday’s sell-off in chip stocks pushed Bitcoin below its 200-day average, and AI tokens, which rose fastest on the AI story, have fallen fastest as it cools.

A wave of local data center bans adds a new worry for tokens that sell computing power. Buterin’s own argument cuts the other way, though: if better algorithms keep AI improving on today’s machines, a slower buildout does not end demand for AI.

It held on Sunday, but with little room to spare. Ether trades at $2,104 on Binance, down 16.1% in 30 days, after touching $2,060 late Sunday, its lowest price since August 19.

ETH sits below both of its daily exponential moving averages (EMAs), averages that weight recent prices more. They stand at $2,311 for 200 days and $2,370 for 50 days.

For traders asking whether the crypto bubble is bursting, AI tokens are the place to watch. They rose fastest on the AI story, and they are now falling fastest.
