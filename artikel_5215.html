How to Claim Bitcoin Hyper Tokens Ahead of BTC Layer 2 Mainnet Debut

The crypto markets have shown early signs of stabilization today, even as sentiment sits in extreme fear territory. Bitcoin has climbed back above $61,500 after a 1.9% gain over the past 24 hours, while the total market capitalization has reached $2.11 trillion with a 1.31% advance. The recovery has tracked gains in tech futures and commodities following the Thanksgiving holiday, with 24-hour crypto liquidations totaling $228.95 million (mostly in short positions) and open interest at $272.6 billion.

Spot Bitcoin ETFs have recorded their largest monthly outflow on record, but BTC’s price bounce alongside broader risk assets points to resilience after recent volatility. The underlying strength in Bitcoin has also arrived as developers push forward with Layer 2 networks aimed at improving BTC’s transaction speed and expanding its practical uses.

This week’s most prominent BTC Layer 2 project, Bitcoin Hyper (HYPER), has finished its public presale campaign after raising $33.3 million. Buyers are now able to claim the HYPER tokens they purchased, and the token has been listed on Uniswap and Raydium. This project’s completed raise and progress toward a functional Layer 2 network position it to play a major role in Bitcoin’s evolution as the mainnet approaches.

Bitcoin Hyper (HYPER) is building a completely new Layer 2 network that processes transactions off Bitcoin’s main chain while anchoring final settlement back to the Layer 1. The L2 runs high-throughput operations through the SVM (Solana Virtual Machine), allowing near-instant finality for payments, DeFi activity, and decentralized applications.

Users will need to deposit native BTC to a monitored address; a smart contract then verifies the deposit against Bitcoin headers and proofs before minting an equivalent amount on the Layer 2. Batches of activity are compressed, protected by zero-knowledge proofs, and periodically committed to Bitcoin for security.

HYPER is the native token of the new L2, has a fixed supply of 21 billion, and functions as the gas token for fees on the network. Holders can also stake it to earn high-APY rewards. On the tokenomics side, allocations are 30% for development, 15% for rewards, 25% for the project’s treasury, 20% for marketing, and 10% for listings.

The public HYPER sale closed after collecting $33.3 million, supporting testnet components that are already operating, including the bridge, explorer, wallet, and staking portal. These elements form the base for full network operations, where the Layer 2 will handle faster transfers and smart-contract activity while relying on Bitcoin’s proof-of-work chain for final settlement. Audits by Coinsult and Spywolf (covering the HYPER token contract) and Hacken (covering L2 components) have also been completed.

Presale participants can now claim their HYPER tokens by first hard-refreshing the official Bitcoin Hyper site to clear the cache, then connecting the same wallet used for the original purchase. From there, they can choose either to claim the tokens directly or to claim and stake them at the same time.

Purchases made with Solana will be claimed on the Solana blockchain, while those completed with Ethereum, BNB, or a credit card can be claimed on Ethereum (contract addresses are specified on the official Bitcoin Hyper website). Once received, the tokens can be exchanged on Uniswap and Raydium.

Tokens that were staked during the presale become claimable starting December 3, and that date also marks the scheduled activation of the Layer 2 mainnet, which will include a dedicated wallet, transaction explorer, and bridge.
