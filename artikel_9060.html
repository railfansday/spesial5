Place des Montréalaises / Lemay + Angela Silver + AtkinsRéalis

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Text description provided by the architects. Place des Montréalaises: A Monumental Gesture Inscribing Women's Memory at the Heart of a CityPlace des Montréalaises is reshaping Montreal's civic and tourist heart by covering a sunken expressway with a vibrant esplanade. Designed by Lemay in collaboration with artist Angela Silver and the engineering team at AtkinsRéalis, this major intervention connects Old Montreal to downtown and inscribes the memory of exceptional women in the urban landscape.
