smart home technology enters the bathroom with VitrA V-Care 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

smart home technology is making its most significant leap straight into the bathroom. partnering with noa design studio, VitrA bathrooms debuts the v-care 3 smart toilet collection.

Smart home technology is expanding beyond living rooms and kitchens directly into the bathroom. Driven by growing interest in home automation and sustainable living, the toilet is receiving its most significant engineering update in decades. Intelligent water-based hygiene replaces single-use paper with adjustable water streams and warm-air drying, reducing environmental waste while improving daily care.

To integrate this functional shift into modern interiors, manufacturer VitrA Bathrooms partnered with Noa Design Studio on the V-Care 3 Smart Bidet Toilet Collection. Defined by architectural clarity, the design proves that integrated electronic features can deliver an intuitive, high-end experience without adding extra bulk or visual clutter.

Designed with architectural clarity in mind, the manufacturer offers the V-Care 3 collection in both wall hung and back to wall formats, fitting effortlessly into contemporary layouts while pairing with standard concealed frames and touch free flush plates. To suit different spaces and budgets, the lineup breaks down into three distinct tiers.

The V-Care 3 Base handles the fundamentals with adjustable water temperature, mobile app controls, a durable Duroplast seat, and Vortex flushing. Step up to the V-Care 3 Core for microbubble washing, a heated seat, warm air drying, and subtle night lighting so you never have to blind yourself during late night visits. At the top sits the V-Care 3 Prime, which pulls out all the stops: automated UVC sterilization, ambient lighting, oscillating spray modes, an air purifier, and a motorized lid that opens and closes automatically so you barely have to lift a finger.

At its core, VitrA’s V-Care 3 collection keeps control intuitive and sanitation nearly effort free. Users can adjust settings using physical remote controls, built in side buttons, or the dedicated mobile app. The app even lets everyone in the house save their personal wash preferences, fine tuning water temperature, spray pressure, nozzle placement, and cycle length with a single tap or voice command. Water flow gets an upgrade with microbubble technology, blending millions of tiny air bubbles into the stream for a gentler, more thorough rinse across dedicated front and rear modes.

When it comes to self cleaning, the top tier V-Care 3 Prime takes care of the dirty work automatically. Built in UVC lighting sterilizes both the ceramic bowl and bidet nozzle every time the lid shuts. Paired with smooth Duroplast seating, antibacterial glazes, and quiet Vortex flushing, the system stops 99.9% of bacteria before it can even start, turning a basic utility into an effortless, ultra clean routine.
