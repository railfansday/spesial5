Bigfoot Is Back In GTA 6, And Apparently He Smells Revolting

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grand Theft Auto games have been home to some weird phenomena over the years, and in GTA 6, it looks like Bigfoot is back and stinkier than ever. As the game steadily rolls out worldwide--or as people digitally immigrate to New Zealand for early access--streamers have been posting clips online of their encounters with the cryptid, and this time, it doesn't look like a man in a suit.

It looks like the Bigfoot sighting is tied to a mission in GTA 6, as the mysterious creature is wandering the woods of Leonida and it takes off in a panic when you encounter it. Another clip shows a strange encounter in the woods, with the player interacting with an effigy that emits noxious fumes and knocks them out. In a brief cutscene, Bigfoots, uh, really big feet, can be seen approaching Jason as he drifts in and out of consciousness.

In that first clip, Bigfoot is also covered in a cloud of fumes, which likely references stories about the mythical creature's potent stench. For GTA--and Red Dead Redemption--fans, this isn't the first time that they've been involved in a Bigfoot sighting, as encounters with it stretch back decades.

Back in 2004, rumors began to circulate about Bigfoot existing in GTA: San Andreas, but Rockstar dismissed these reports as false in 2005. A few years later, in Red Dead Redemption's Undead Nightmare expansion, protagonist John Marston was tasked with hunting down a pack of Sasquatches, and it was eventually revealed that the creatures were peaceful, capable of human speech, and now going extinct thanks to a certain trigger-happy cowboy.

Bigfoot would appear in GTA 5, as getting 100% completion in the game would unlock a new mission where co-protagonist Franklin hunts down a creature in Blaine Woods, only to discover it was a man in a costume. As an extra joke, the Bigfoot impersonator drops a few references to Red Dead Redemption's infamous encounter, but that doesn't mean the real Bigfoot isn't lurking in GTA 5. You could use the thermal scope on the sniper rifle supplied to you to spot a bipedal creature watching you during the "Predator" mission, and eating a golden Peyote plant under specific environmental conditions would turn players into Bigfoot.

Red Dead Redemption 2 brought an air of mystery back to the Bigfoot mythos, as you could find a gigantic skeleton half-buried in the snow high up on Mount Shann, and protagonist Arthur Morgan could also speak to an unseen figure that was heavily implied to be Bigfoot.
