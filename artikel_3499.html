Why GTA 6 Doesn’t Let You Replay Missions–It Felt “Alien And Disconnected”

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

One of the first things people noticed with Grand Theft Auto 6 is that you cannot replay missions, at least not in a simple, straightforward way. GTA 5 had a mission replay option, as did Red Dead Redemption II, so why doesn't GTA 6?

Senior design director Scott Butchard told Kotaku that Rockstar "certainly" considered a mission replay feature "early on in development." However, Butchard said, "As the game came together, it felt at odds with the story we were trying to tell and felt a bit alien and disconnected."

He went on to say replaying missions "really diminished the choices you had made."

While GTA 6 doesn't make it easy to replay missions, you still can do so by reloading an earlier GTA 6 save. Something else to know is that if you're in the middle of a mission and it's not going to plan, you can open the game menu and select retry, quit, or restart.

GTA 6 blasted off with 32.5 million sales via prepurchases alone and around $4 billion in revenue on day one. The game is only going to get bigger and sell more copies over time, and it's helping drive new PlayStation and Xbox hardware sales.

In other GTA 6 news, the actor who plays the character Mandy has reacted to people being weird online. Rockstar has also reacted to the sentiment that GTA 6 is too easy.
