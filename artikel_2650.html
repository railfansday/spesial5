The offer that the Blue Jays made to Tarik Skubal revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Can't knock the effort but Uncle Steve Cohen always has the trump card.

The Mets signed superstar pitcher Tarik Skubal to a record nine year $450 million contract on Sunday that had the baseball world in a tizzy and it was unclear exactly who they were bidding against at that kind of dollar figure and length.

The Athletic's Mitch Bannon reports that the Blue Jays' final offer to Skubal was nine years and "a little over" $400 million, which still would have been the record for the largest contract ever given to a Major League Baseball pitcher.

The Blue Jays were willing to spend big on Tarik Skubal. Charles Wenzelberg / New York Post

If Skubal had signed the contract, it would have been the second biggest deal in team history behind only the monster 14-year, $500 million contract they signed Vladimir Guerrero Jr. to last year.

Skubal, who was previously on the Dodgers in a midseason trade, never made an offer to Skubal, per ESPN, and there were questions of whether they were willing to make a long term offer to him.

It will be interesting to see what the Blue Jays do now after losing out on Skubal, as they were clearly willing to back up the truck for him but had been mostly quiet on all other fronts, per CBS' Jim Bowden.

Some have speculated that the Blue Jays could turn to former Mets and Rays pitcher Freddy Peralta, who had a massive end to his season after being traded from Queens at the trade deadline.

Tarik Skubal signed with the Mets in a massive contract. Wally Skalij for the CA Post

Peralta was the ALCS MVP this past season and won player of the month in September as he caught fire for Tampa Bay.

It's a little surprising that the are willing to spend big money on a player considering the early returns on Guerrero Jr.'s contract were really poor last season.
