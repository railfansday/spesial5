UNC vs. West Virginia Live Discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Tar Heels are in Charlotte to face off against West Virginia as part of the Dick Vitale Invitational. The Heels are going into this one with just one loss on the season, while the Mountaineers have two losses, both of which were against ranked teams. This will likely be the Heels' toughest test so far, but this is also a bit of a different team from the one that played Georgia. Having Cameron Fens in the lineup has made a difference, and one would argue that Terrence Brown playing point guard in Neo Avdalis' absence has had more of a positive impact on the team than a negative. The thing, though, is his ability to be a facilitator will be tested big in a big way tonight.

The Mountaineers are currently averaging 8 steals per game, which is slightly higher than what Carolina has been doing. Having a team that is that active on defense when you do not have your starting point guard can be tough, so we'll see if Brown is able to take care of the ball. On the flip side, West Virginia is also averaging 9.8 turnovers per game, which funny enough is also somewhat similar to UNC's numbers. This game will ultimately come down to who can take care of the ball better and who will be able to rebound, but of course neither matter if the ball isn't going into the basket. Given how aggressive the Heels have been in the paint — mostly because of the crazy stuff Brown has been able to do — it's hard to see scoring being the big issue tonight.

For anybody that didn't make the trip to Charlotte, feel free to hang out with us in the comments section. As always, we will be back later with post-game analysis and takes. Until then, Go Heels!
