Open Thread: Happy birthday to David Jones Garcia

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Spurs small forward David Jones Garcia has one of the best Spurs "feel good stories," and that's saying a lot on a team that features Julian Champagnie's path from undrafted player to NBA Finals starter, Keldon Johnson's evolution to NBA Sixth Man, and Victor Wembanyama's emergence as the face of the NBA.

David Jones Garcia was born in Santo Domingo in the Dominican Republic. After seeing scouts at FIBA tournaments, his family sent David to high school in the United States to offer him the best exposure for future basketball opportunities. He attended DePaul where he became a starter after his first full season. He transferred to St. John's where he continued to excel, but the year was emotionally fragmented with the loss of his father. David vowed to his father on his deathbed he would play at least one regular-season NBA game.

Jones Garcia returned to college, but this time in Memphis under head coach Penny Hardaway. He declared for the 2024 NBA Draft but was undrafted. He spent the 2024-25 season bouncing around the G-League, signing contracts with the Philadelphia 76ers, Mexico City Capitanes, and Utah Jazz affiliate. He eventually made the 2025 G-League All-Rookie Team.

That summer he signed on with the San Antonio Spurs, initially for Summer League finally landing a two-way contract.

On October 22, in the first game of the season, Jones Garcia fulfilled his promise to his father and clocked 4 minutes against the Dallas Mavericks. He spent first half of the season coming off the bench. With a penchant for hitting threes and no traces of fear in his court demeanor, Jones Garcia revealed a confident and competent competitor.

He played in eleven total games before requiring surgery on his right ankle. On February 4, he was ruled out for the remainder of the season.

Jones Garcia signed on with the Spurs again for a second year with a two-way contract. He's seen minor action in four games thus far and picks up fourth quarter minutes each time the Spurs pull away early.

Today David Jones Garcia turns 25, the median age of the squad. His star still on the rise, he's continued to find opportunity and make the most of any situation he faces.
