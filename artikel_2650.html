NFL Week 12 Injury Report: Drake Maye's updated timeline, Dalton Kincaid returns to practice, and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Staying up to date on NFL injury news is of the utmost importance for fantasy football success. The article below tracks all fantasy-relevant players currently listed on their team's injury report and provides fantasy managers with actionable recommendations as new information rolls in.

Check back for updates from Tuesday through Saturday.

**UPDATED AS OF WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 25 AT 7:30 PM ET**

Kincaid missed Week 11 with multiple ailments but returned to a limited session Tuesday. He is officially questionable to face the Chiefs after three limited practices this week.

Impact: Kincaid's absence against the Dolphins could have been a ploy to get him healthy for the marquee matchup against the Chiefs on Thanksgiving. Consider Kincaid a TE1, if active.

Palmer managed only seven offensive snaps in Week 8 before departing with an aggravation of the hamstring injury he has battled since Week 4. He was placed on injured reserve.

Impact: Not much, honestly. Palmer has played sparingly this season in a situational role, although his absence could leave room for Skyler Bell to be active for the third time this season. The Bills also activated Tyrell Shavers (knee) from the reserve/physically unable to perform list. He could join the wide receiver rotation but does not need to be rostered in 12-team leagues.

Achane suffered a torn ACL in the team's Week 3 loss to the Chiefs, ending his season.

Impact: Achane's unfortunate season-ending injury creates opportunity for Ollie Gordon and Jaylen Wright. The timing of the injury makes it likely that Achane is able to fully recover before the start of the 2027 regular season.

The Dolphins placed Willis on injured reserve in Week 10. He will be eligible to return in Week 14, but Willis is seeking a second opinion after the injury progressed into his abdomen. Season-ending surgery is possible, although the team has yet to decide if or when surgery would take place.

Impact: Kyle McCord's installation as the team's starter hurts all involved. Until we see how McCord fares in this system, only Ollie Gordon is a safe FLEX starter.

Maye will reportedly be sidelined for at least six weeks, giving him the slimmest of chances to return for a playoff run, should the team make it. Impact: The team is reportedly reaching out to quarterbacks as they look to right a sinking ship following consecutive losses for the first time in head coach Mike Vrabel's tenure, although they also said Monday that Tommy DeVito is their starter. More to come on this developing story. Suffice to say, the fantasy value of the primary skill position players in New England takes a significant hit for as long as Maye remains sidelined.

Doubs was placed on injured reserve following a shoulder injury sustained in Week 6. He resumed practicing early in Week 12.

Impact: Doubs could warrant FLEX treatment in 12-team leagues when active.

Impact: DeMario Douglas, Kyle Williams, Mack Hollins, and Kyle Williams led the team in snaps and routes in the absence of Brown in Week 9, with none of the bunch consistent fantasy producers. Fantasy managers are likely forced to wait until more information is released to gauge Brown's expected status in Week 12.

Impact: Rhamondre Stevenson and Corey Kiner are likely to form a one-two punch in the absence of Henderson, with Stevenson the favorite to lead the team in opportunities and snaps. Stevenson can be viewed as a volume-backed RB2 for the time being.
