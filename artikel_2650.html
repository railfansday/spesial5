Mike Breen ‘worried’ about Clyde Frazier after Rockettes comment

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Radio City Rockettes stole the spotlight during the Knicks' 123-116 victory over the Pacers at Madison Square Garden on Sunday.

Members of the famous dance company performed during the first quarter and captured the attention of Knicks legend Walt "Clyde" Frazier, who gushed over them on the MSG Network broadcast with lead play-by-play announcer Mike Breen.

"Hey Alan, if you need any help interviewing the Rockettes, I'm free at halftime," Frazier, a Knicks color analyst, said referring to studio analyst Alan Hahn.

"They're still here, Clyde, if you want to dance with them at halftime," Hahn quipped.

"I could show them the spots around the Garden, you know," replied Frazier, who played 10 seasons with the Knicks, from 1967-77.

"See now this is what I was talking about earlier that I'm worried," Breen said while laughing. "Your filter is starting to come off."

Frazier explained his admiration for the famed dance troupe.

"So they consider themselves as athletes and I agree," he added. "They're very talented, dancin' and prancin'."

Pacers head coach Rick Carlisle explained that he had an interaction with the Rockettes that caused him to be late to his press conference after the game.

"Sorry I was late. The Rockettes were doing a photo shoot in the freight elevator," Carlisle said. "That's what makes New York a special place.

"Luckily there was a ball boy that was able to text somebody and he directed me to a different elevator. I'm appreciative of that."

The Knicks have won five in a row and are in the fourth spot in the Eastern Conference heading into Wednesday's game against the Cavaliers.
