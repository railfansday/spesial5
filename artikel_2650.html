NBA Head Coach Calls LeBron James an “AI Machine”

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Before Sunday's matchup between the Philadelphia Sixers and Indiana Pacers, Pacers head coach Rick Carlisle was asked about LeBron James and said, "… he knows all the plays.. He's a walking NBA basketball AI machine"

James has played the most games in NBA history and has faced or played alongside nearly 40% of all players. The four-time NBA champion has seen it all, and with LeBron's memory and basketball IQ, regarded as one of, if not the, best of all time, it is no surprise that any play the Pacers could run, James can read and stop.

On Sunday afternoon, James helped the Sixers defeat the Pacers 131-124, improving to 11 -3. Still, the main storyline wasn't the win; it was LeBron, who dropped 15 points, grabbed seven rebounds, and dished out 11 assists. With his 11th assist, James surpassed Jason Kidd for third all-time.

To catch second-place Chris Paul, LeBron needs 460 assists, which is very doable. Currently, the 41-year-old averages seven assists per game, and at that pace it will take a little less than 70 games, which would mean LeBron would climb past Paul around this time next season.

If anyone thinks James can get first, the NBA's all-time leading scorer would have to play six and a half full seasons at the current pace of seven assists per game, which would put James in year 30 and a 48-year-old man. Safe to say the record set by John Stockton won't be touched anytime soon.

Through 14 games, the biggest impact LeBron has had on this Sixers squad is on the defensive end. Philly is currently seventh with a defensive rating of 110.3, a major improvement over last season, when the Sixers were 17th.

James acts as an on-court coach, calling out the opposing team's offensive sets and plays, which allows the Sixers' key perimeter players like Jaylen Brown, Tyrese Maxey, and VJ Edgecomb to be more confident in gambling and, in turn, has led to just over 8 steals per game for the Sixers.

Philadelphia 76ers forward LeBron James (23) looks on against the Indiana Pacers during the fourth quarter at Xfinity Mobile Arena. Photo Credit: Bill Streicher, Imagn Images via Reuters Connect

Most NBA players, as they approach their mid-to-late 30s, lose their athleticism, often regress, and eventually retire. That's why people say Father Time also wins. But James is still playing at a high level at age 41 despite his athleticism being nowhere near what it was a decade ago.

The reason James is still great is a testament to the generational basketball IQ the 22-time All-Star possesses, always being in the right place at the right time and making plays that very few humans can anticipate. It is said that no human can defeat Father Time, but maybe an AI machine can.

James is the fourth option on this Sixers team, which is totally different from the previous 22 years when James was either the first or second option. This is a different version of him than in years past, but the rest of the team has absorbed the knowledge and leadership. Maxey is an early-season MVP candidate, and Shaquille O'Neal called the Sixers the best team in the NBA. There are still some problems, but if the Sixers hoist the Larry O'Brien trophy, a large part of that will be because of LeBron's AI-like knowledge.
