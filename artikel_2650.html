Chiefs-Bills Inactives: TE Noah Gray and DT Peter Woods will play

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Kansas City Chiefs have released their list of inactive players for Thursday's game against the Buffalo Bills.

Running back Kenneth Walker, tackles Jaylon Moore and Diego Pounds, defensive tackles Omarr Norman-Lott and Marcus Harris and cornerback Melvin Smith will not dress. Rookie Garrett Nussmeir has been designated as the third quarterback and is eligible to enter the game should starter Patrick Mahomes and backup Justin Fields become unavailable.

Walker (groin) Moore (ankle) and Norman-Lott (knee) had already been ruled out on Friday's final injury report. Defensive tackle Peter Woods (neck) and tight end Noah Gray (rib) had been deemed questionable, but the pair will go after finishing the week as full participants in practice.

Woods left Week 9's contest against the Denver Broncos with a neck injury and his missed the last three games. Gray left Week 12's matchup with the Arizona Cardinals with a rib injury and did not return.

Earlier on Thursday, Kansas City elevated running back Nate Carter from the practice squad, and he is active for Thursday. As an elevated player, he will automatically be returned to the team's practice squad after the game. With Walker nursing a groin injury, this is the third consecutive game the Chiefs have brought him up. Carter has no more permitted elevations remaining.

The Chiefs also elevated tight end JohnFitzPatrick — who just joined the practice squad on November 16 — for the game. The 2022 sixth-round selection of the Atlanta Falcons will make his Kansas City debut on Thanksgiving.

The Bills have also released their list of inactives.

Wide receiver Skyler Bell, safety Jalon Kilgore, cornerback Christian Benford, former Chiefs pass rusher Mike Danna, tackle Jude Bowry and defensive tackle Greg Gaines will not go for Buffalo.

Benford (groin) had been listed as questionable to end the week but will not suit up. Tight end Dalton Kincaid (thunb/ankle), defensvie tackle T.J. Sanders (shoulder), guard O'Cyrus Torrence (ankle) and linebacker Dorian Williams (calf) were also listed as questionable. All four are active.

The Bills elevated practice squad cornerback Kani Walker for the matchup. Walker is active for the game.
