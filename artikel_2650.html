The ‘s****y’ message Warriors coaches delivered players amid brutal losing streak without Stephen Curry

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Golden State Warriors are going through a brutal stretch of games without Stephen Curry. This has resulted in the coaches lighting a fire under the players this season.

Starting 0-3, Golden State rolled off eight consecutive wins to surprise the league. However, Curry suffered an injury as the team since fell down to earth. They lost five straight games before finally ending the losing streak on Nov. 22 against the Boston Celtics.

Stretches like that can be frustrating for teams, especially for the players and coaches. The former is figuring out ways to perform well on the court without their star player, while the latter evaluates options to maximize their current talent while Curry recovers. In the meantime, assistant coaches Frank Vogel and Jacob Rubin are not wasting time to let the players know they are not performing on defense.

"Guys, this defense is shitty, this is really bad," Vogel and Rubin said.

"We can be better than this… Steph's out and it's a crusher… but the defense doesn't have to be this bad," the players said.

The Warriors sorely miss Stephen Curry's presence on the court. They haven't been able to score 100 points in their last five games, showing how Curry elevates their offensive production in significant fashion.

The team has a major bright spot, being Yaxel Lendeborg. He is off to a hot start as one of the best performing rookies this season, presenting a positive long-term trajectory for Golden State to build around.

Golden State has a 9-8 record this season, sitting at 11th place in the Western Conference standings. They are above the New Orleans Pelicans and the Los Angeles Clippers while trailing the Oklahoma City Thunder and the Phoenix Suns.

The Warriors will continue preparations for their next matchup, being at home. They host the Sacramento Kings in the NBA Cup as tip-off commences on Nov. 25 at 10 p.m. ET.
