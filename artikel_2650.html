Emmett Johnson describes his increased role in the passing game

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Kansas City Chiefs running back Emmett Johnson spoke with reporters on Wednesday. He revealed what he learned in the NFL about his role as a backfield receiver.

"Honestly, just coming into this oﬀense, Pat (Mahomes) and Coach (Andy) Reid and these guys are just—they do a good job of getting the running back into space and being able to run routes and be versatile," said Johnson. "That's what I like a lot about this oﬀense. So, just putting me in a good position to be successful. And I catch a lot of passes every day, so I've got a lot of faith in my receiving ability."

Johnson's 171-yard performance in Week 10 was the team's seventh game this season in which a running back recorded 130 or more scrimmage yards, with Johnson at 2 and Kenneth Walker III at 5.

The former Nebraska standout has forced a missed tackle on 45.6% of his touches this season, leading all running backs with at least 50 carries. According to Next Gen Stats, Johnson gained more yards than expected on 54.5% of his 22 rushing attempts, totaling 28 rush yards over expected.
