Get the Platinum Edition® Visa® Card if you’re a Flagstar customer with card debt you can pay off within its 0% period of 18 billing cycles. Each transfer costs 5%, and debt on another Flagstar card can’t move here. If you need more time, some national banks’ cards give three more billing cycles at 0% for the same fee.

Flagstar no longer offers its old Visa Platinum card, and the Platinum Edition is the card it now pitches for consolidating debt or paying off a big purchase over time. First National Bank of Omaha (FNBO) issues it, as it does every Flagstar credit card, and sets your rate and credit limit when you apply.

It suits Flagstar customers moving debt from another bank’s card, as long as they can clear the balance before the 0% ends. Debt that needs more than 18 billing cycles to clear belongs on the U.S. Bank Shield Visa instead, whose 0% covers balances moved in its first 60 days for the same 5% fee.
