Chipotle launches hoodie designed to sneak burritos into movie theaters

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Chipotle is launching a limited-edition hoodie designed to help customers sneak burritos and other menu items into movie theaters without drawing attention.

The fast-food chain has unveiled its new “Sneak It In” hoodie, which features a hidden, insulated pocket large enough to carry an entire Chipotle meal while keeping it warm until showtime.

The unusual clothing release comes ahead of several major movie premieres and the holiday shopping season. Chipotle says the idea was inspired by customers who were already bringing its food into theaters, with the company now offering clothing specifically designed to make the process easier.

The “Sneak It In” hoodie features a concealed, insulated compartment designed to hold a Chipotle burrito, bowl, salad, or tacos. It also includes a separate pocket for napkins and comes in an oversized, unisex fit.

Customers can choose between three colors: Nothing To See Here Black, No Burrito Here Brown, and Got the Goods Gray.

According to Chipotle, the concept was inspired by social media posts showing customers bringing their orders into movie theaters. The company also cited a 2024 YouGov survey that found 63% of Americans admitted to sneaking food or drinks into a movie.

“Everything about going to the movies has gotten better over the years. Bigger screens, better sound, more comfortable seats. But the food? We’re still choosing between popcorn, candy and nachos,” said Kirby Ann Connell, Chipotle’s Senior Director of Social and Influencers.

“Movie nights deserve real, craveable food. And since our fans were already sneaking Chipotle to theaters, we figured we’d make it a little easier. Sometimes innovation is as simple as putting a burrito pocket in a hoodie.”

The hoodie will cost $53 and launches November 18 at 9 a.m. PT exclusively through their website in the US and Canada. Shipping is free, and the limited-edition item will only be available while supplies last.

Each purchase also includes a free Chipotle entrée card, which customers can redeem for a meal to put inside their new hoodie. The card expires December 31, 2027.

Chipotle Rewards members in the US will have another opportunity to get the hoodie through a sweepstakes running from November 16 through November 22.

Members can exchange 10 Rewards points for an entry through the Chipotle Rewards Exchange, with a maximum of 20 entries per person. A free mail-in entry method is also available.

The release is part of Chipotle’s holiday merchandise and gifting plans, which also include seasonal gift cards and an upcoming Black Friday promotion.

From November 27 through November 30, the first 15,000 qualifying online purchases of at least $50 in Chipotle e-gift cards in the US will receive a $10 bonus code. The promotional codes expire on December 11, 2026.
