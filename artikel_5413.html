Maia and Karen Zupko and Associates, Inc. Partner to Bring Independent Coding Expertise to AI-Generated Orthopaedic Coding

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Four plus decades of orthopaedic coding authority meets EHR-integrated AI-native coding support technology. KZA will conduct ongoing independent audits of Maia's coding output.

ROSEMONT, Ill., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Maia, an AI-powered medical coding and revenue cycle platform built exclusively for orthopaedic practices, and Karen Zupko & Associates, Inc. (KZA), the healthcare consulting and coding education firm that has advised surgical specialists since 1985, today announced a partnership pairing independent coding and audit expertise with AI-generated coding at the point of care.

Under the partnership, KZA will conduct ongoing independent audits of Maia's AI-generated coding output across both evaluation and management (E/M) and surgical cases. Each review is performed by KZA's certified consultants against AMA CPT guidelines, ICD-10-CM guidelines, CMS National Correct Coding Initiative edits, and AAOS Global Service Data. KZA provides its findings to Maia's engineering and machine learning teams, which use the feedback to guide improvements to the platform's coding models, rules, and review workflows.

In addition to the ongoing audits, KZA and Maia will explore how specialty-focused education and expert review could support oversight of Maia's coding output.

"AI will play an increasingly important role in how medical practices approach coding. The question is whether that technology is being independently evaluated, and at what level of expertise," said Cathy McDowell, President and CEO of KZA. "What impressed us about Maia was their proactive commitment to independent auditing before anyone required it. That speaks volumes about their approach to developing this platform and their commitment to building trust and confidence among their clients. By incorporating ongoing independent audits and expert feedback, Maia is demonstrating that coding accuracy, compliance, and continuous improvement are priorities from the start."

"Orthopaedic coding is not a general-purpose problem, and it should not be graded by a general-purpose reviewer," said Zach Ruhl, Co-Founder and CEO of Maia. "KZA has spent more than forty years as the authority orthopaedic practices call when a claim is complicated. Having them grade our work every month, against the same standards they hold any coder to, is the most honest test we could put our product through. We would rather find our gaps in an audit report than have a customer find them in a denial."

Maia's platform reads clinical and operative notes and recommends CPT, ICD-10, and HCPCS codes with modifiers and clinical justification, working inside the practice's existing EHR. Maia is live with athenahealth and eClinicalWorks integrations and works with orthopaedic groups including OrthoIndy and Midwest Orthopaedics at Rush.

The two organizations are also collaborating on educational tools for orthopaedic practices, including joint publications and appearances on KZA's podcast, with additional programming planned through 2026 and 2027.

Maia is an AI-powered autonomous medical coding and revenue cycle management platform built exclusively for orthopaedic practices in the United States. Maia reads clinical and operative documentation to recommend CPT, ICD-10, and HCPCS codes with modifiers and clinical justification, and supports prior authorization, denial appeals, and collections. The platform is self-hosted on AWS and integrates with athenahealth and eClinicalWorks. usemaia.com

Founded in 1985, Karen Zupko & Associates, Inc. (KZA) is a nationally recognized leader in healthcare consulting and education, with more than 40 years of experience supporting physicians and healthcare organizations. KZA specializes in medical coding, documentation, reimbursement, revenue cycle management, and physician practice operations, with extensive expertise across surgical specialties, including orthopaedics. Through independent audits, expert consulting, and industry-leading education, KZA helps healthcare organizations strengthen compliance, improve financial performance, and navigate the complexities of today's healthcare environment.
