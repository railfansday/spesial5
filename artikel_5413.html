Nasdaq futures rise 2.1% on an IPO that hasn't opened, with Nvidia stock still down 26% this month

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Anthropic is the first artificial-intelligence developer to sell shares to the public, surpassing SpaceX for the biggest IPO ever

Anthropic was set to debut on the Nasdaq $NDAQ on Thursday under the ticker ANT, and Nasdaq-100 futures rose 2.1% before the open.

Futures tied to the S&P 500 added 1.2%. Dow Jones Industrial Average futures gained 320 points, or 0.6%. Trading in Anthropic stock is set to start at midday.

Anthropic, the company behind the Claude chatbot, set its IPO price at $83 per share, which landed higher than the range that had been anticipated. The company upsized the offering to a total of $81.3 billion, which would surpass SpaceX's record IPO in June. It's the biggest IPO ever, and it makes Anthropic is the first artificial-intelligence developer to sell shares to the public.

Chip and tech stocks moved higher. Shares of Nvidia $NVDA and AMD $AMD rose 1.9% and 2.8%, and Micron $MU Technology stock gained 5.2%. Shares of Microsoft $MSFT and Meta $META Platforms each climbed more than 1%.

Nvidia stock has dropped 26%, and CoreWeave stock has lost nearly 13% of its value so far in November. Investors have been questioning whether the large sums going into AI infrastructure will eventually pay off in real earnings.

Callie Cox, director of investment strategy at Plancorp, called the offering "an important barometer for investors' AI appetite," according to CNBC.

Weekly jobless claims are due Thursday. Walmart $WMT reported earnings before the open, and Gap $GPS is scheduled to report after the market close.
