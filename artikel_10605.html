AI girlfriends based on Lucia from GTA 6 are making creators thousands

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

GTA 6‘s Lucia has inspired a wave of AI-generated girlfriends, with some creators claiming they’re making tens of thousands of dollars a month.

Rockstar’s long-awaited sequel finally launched on November 19, introducing players to Lucia Caminos, one of the game’s two main protagonists alongside Jason Duval.

And according to the people behind them, it’s proving to be an extremely profitable business.

Speaking to the New York Post, 26-year-old James from London revealed that his AI influencer, Adriana Vega, reportedly earned £30,000 (around $34,000) in a single month.

He created Adriana in August after taking inspiration from Lucia’s appearance and personality, and the account has since attracted more than 35,000 Instagram followers.

“I first saw Lucia back in 2023 when the first trailer dropped. I thought she was really hot and knew she was gonna be popular with men, especially GTA fans,” James explained.

“Adriana is a Miami girl, just like Lucia. She has a real attitude, but is passionate and fiercely loyal to those she loves, exactly like Lucia’s character in-game.”

“Some people can be really obsessed with her,” he added. “They’ve asked her to be their girlfriend.”

Katy, a 24-year-old from Philadelphia, created another Lucia-inspired AI influencer called Vicky Torres, who has attracted more than 114,000 Instagram followers.

She told the New York Post that the character brings in around $12,000 a month, with fans paying for personalized content and conversations.

Despite GTA 6 now being available, she plans to continue developing Vicky as an independent influencer separate from Lucia.

“I’ll definitely keep creating content with Victoria, even after the GTA 6 hype dies down,” she swore. “I don’t want her to depend on Lucia or the game to stay interesting.”
