Audi’s family-friendly mid-size Q7 has undergone a glow up as it enters its third generation for 2027.

New styling aligns the Q7 with the rest of the latest in Audi’s lineup.

Broader shoulders and a flatter roof give it a more muscular presence.

Handling is athletic, and the Q7 remains a satisfying SUV in the curves.

The Q7’s turbocharged V-6 engine makes a stout 429 horsepower.

Audi has added its digital matrix LED headlamps this year, which adapt to the road ahead and can deactivate segments to avoid blinding oncoming drivers.

OLED panels serve as the Q7’s taillights and can be customized with several different light signatures.

Audi has illuminated as many bits and baubles as possible, including the logo on both the front and the back.

The Q7 retains its fun-to-drive nature in its third generation.

And its powerful V-6 delivers heady acceleration.
