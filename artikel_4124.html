Everything is Crab, and now you can be too in this Spore-like roguelike, out now

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

There's a term in science, carcinisation, which is the fascinating evolutionary oddity that sees so many different species end up evolving into crab-like beings. Now, in Everything is Crab, you can too! As this evolutionary, Spore-like roguelike hits iOS and Android!

Everything is Crab sees you step into the amorphous shoes of Darwin, a 'blue blob of infinite potential'. From humble evolutionary beginnings, you'll guide Darwin to consume other creatures, growing larger as you might expect from something of this genre. But you won't just get bigger; you'll also gain different attributes from your unfortunate evolutionary victims!

And no matter how you want to approach your struggle for survival, Everything is Crab gives you a huge variety of options. Whether it's overwhelming them with strength, scavenging the remains littered around each level or actually socialising with and befriending them!

There's plenty there to play, and if you want to, you can give it a go entirely for free. Everything is Crab offers a try-before-you-buy approach, letting you sample what it has to offer with an optional purchase to unlock the full game. Not a bad way to see how evolution works, eh?
