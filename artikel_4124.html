NACON Releases REVO Range Of Xbox Controllers Designed For 'Every Gamer Style'

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

"Always Ready. Tailored Control. Pros' Arsenal."

NACON has announced today the availability of its REVO range of Xbox controllers, three officially licensed pads. The trio aims to accommodate the needs of every Xbox and PC player, because "every gamer has a style, every style has a controller."

The REVO X and REVO X MAX are available for purchase today, with the REVO X PRO arriving sometime in 2027. All three controllers are designed for different player styles, and so we've listed the price point and details for each pad. Here's how NACON described the REVO range:

"The new range has been designed to meet the specific needs of every type of player, from the casual gamer looking for the immediate efficiency of a "Plug-and-Play" controller, to the esports expert seeking deep customization."

The REVO controller is the embodiment of accessible and reliable performance. It is ideal for players looking for fluid control and flawless durability.

Announced for 2027, the REVO PRO is designed for the most demanding competitive players. It combines the best features of the range, including instant micro-switch triggers and programmable rear buttons, to offer optimized responsiveness and control for competition.

For players who demand complete customization, the REVO MAX offers advanced adjustment options to adapt the controller to their playstyle; these options are in addition to all the REVO features detailed above.
