SoCal Digital Marketing Launches ChatGPT Ads Management Services to Help Businesses Reach Customers Through AI Advertising

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

California digital marketing agency expands its services with ChatGPT Ads account setup, campaign management, and conversion tracking, drawing on hands-on experience during the platform's beta rollout.

ORANGE COUNTY, Calif., Nov. 27, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- SoCal Digital Marketing, an established SEO and digital advertising agency serving businesses throughout Southern California and nationwide, announces the launch of its new ChatGPT Ads Management Services.

The new service helps businesses explore advertising opportunities within ChatGPT through account setup, campaign strategy, ad creation, conversion tracking, and ongoing optimization.

With ChatGPT advertising continuing to expand during its beta rollout, SoCal Digital Marketing is positioning itself to help businesses evaluate this emerging channel and reach prospective customers during AI-powered research and purchasing decisions.

Bringing Real-World Advertising Experience to ChatGPT Ads

Founded in 2012, SoCal Digital Marketing has spent more than a decade helping businesses improve their online visibility, generate qualified leads, and manage paid advertising campaigns.

The agency has already helped clients establish and manage ChatGPT advertising accounts during beta access, gaining firsthand experience with the platform's evolving campaign structure, advertising relevance signals, and management capabilities.

Founder Andrew Guida has also completed ChatGPT Ads training hosted by OpenAI, building on his experience in SEO and digital advertising dating back to 2011.

"We've spent years helping businesses reach customers through Google Ads, and ChatGPT advertising represents an exciting new opportunity," said Andrew Guida, founder of SoCal Digital Marketing. "What's particularly interesting is how the platform evaluates relevance. Instead of depending primarily on the exact keywords someone searches, ChatGPT Ads considers the broader context of what someone is researching and trying to accomplish."

Guida added, "We've already been helping clients set up and manage accounts during beta access. That practical experience allows us to approach this new advertising channel with a better understanding of its capabilities, challenges, and opportunities."

A New Approach to Reaching Potential Customers

Unlike traditional Google Search advertising, which relies heavily on keyword targeting, ChatGPT Ads uses conversational context and other relevance signals to help determine when sponsored advertisements may be useful.

For example, a business owner researching manufacturing services, software solutions, or professional service providers may encounter an eligible advertisement relevant to their current research.

This creates an additional opportunity for businesses to reach prospective customers while they are comparing solutions and considering their next steps.

Why Businesses Should Consider ChatGPT Advertising Early

As a relatively new advertising platform, ChatGPT Ads may present opportunities for early adopters to begin testing campaigns before advertiser participation becomes more widespread.

While lower costs and stronger conversions are not guaranteed, businesses that begin exploring the platform can start learning which advertising messages, customer needs, and landing pages generate meaningful results.

"There's a potential advantage to learning a new advertising platform while it's still developing," Guida explained. "Businesses that begin testing ChatGPT Ads now can gather valuable information about customer behavior and campaign performance. The goal isn't simply to advertise somewhere new. It's to determine whether ChatGPT can become another reliable source of qualified leads."

SoCal Digital Marketing views ChatGPT advertising as a complement to traditional SEO, Google Ads, and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), rather than a replacement for these established marketing strategies.

The agency's expanded services are designed to help manufacturers, franchises, professional service providers, and other eligible businesses evaluate opportunities to reach customers through AI-powered advertising.

Founded in 2012 by Andrew Guida, SoCal Digital Marketing is a California-based digital marketing agency specializing in search engine optimization (SEO), Google Ads management, Answer Engine Optimization (AEO/GEO), and lead-generation strategies.

The agency works with businesses throughout Orange County, San Diego, and the United States, including industrial manufacturers, franchises, professional service providers, and multi-location organizations.

SoCal Digital Marketing emphasizes personalized service, transparent reporting, and marketing strategies focused on measurable business results.
