Gold falls to a 14-month low after Fed officials signal several more rate hikes

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Spot gold fell 2.3% to $3,762.11 an ounce on Tuesday as St. Louis Fed's Alberto Musalem backed hikes before spring 2027

Brendon Thorne/Bloomberg via Getty Images

St. Louis Fed President Alberto Musalem called for several interest rate increases ahead of spring 2027 in a speech on Tuesday, and gold dropped below $3,800 an ounce.

By 6:56 a.m. ET on Tuesday, spot gold had dropped 2.3% to $3,762.11 an ounce, having slid as much as 2.4% earlier in the session. Bullion traded below $3,800 for the first time since September 2025, according to Bloomberg, and it was a third straight day of declines. Silver lost 3.6% and changed hands at $45.69 an ounce. Platinum and palladium declined.

He estimated the real policy rate at the lower end of a 1% to 1.5% range and said it should reach a level solidly between 2% and 2.5%.

Musalem said headline and core inflation continue to run above 3%, higher than the Federal Open Market Committee's 2% target. He described a scenario in which no further tightening is needed as unlikely but plausible. He said most of the lift in the real rate may have to come from a higher nominal policy rate.

That path suggests hikes at the Fed's meetings in December, January, March and April, according to CNBC.

New York Fed President John Williams said the central bank must do more to return inflation to 2%, according to Bloomberg. Markets are betting on quarter-point hikes in December and January, with a third by April.

The 10-year Treasury yield, the benchmark for government borrowing costs, is closing in on its longest stretch above 5% in nearly 20 years, according to Bloomberg. An index tracking the U.S. dollar also climbed.

Minutes from the Fed's October meeting are due for release Wednesday, according to Bloomberg. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh gave no direct comment on monetary policy in remarks this week.

The Fed held its benchmark rate at 3.75% to 4% on Oct. 28 after a quarter-point increase in September, with two of 12 voting officials dissenting in favor of a hike. Spot gold traded at $3,956.09 an ounce on Oct. 30 after dipping to its weakest price since June 30.

Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan said on Nov. 10 that the Fed needs at least two more quarter-point increases and may be able to move faster this winter. The Fed meets Dec. 8-9.
