Mortgage applications rise 6.1% even as 30-year hits 7.38%

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

MBA reported mortgage applications rose 6.1% for the week ending Nov. 20, 2026. Purchase activity led with a 10% gain, while the 30-year fixed rate averaged 7.38% and ARM share increased to 11.5%.  AI Summary

Mortgage applications increased 6.1% from one week earlier, according to data from the Mortgage Bankers Association’s (MBA) weekly mortgage applications survey for the week ending November 20, 2026.

On an unadjusted basis, the Index increased 3% compared with the previous week.

The refinance index increased 1% from the previous week and was 51% lower than the same week one year ago. The seasonally adjusted purchase index increased 10% from one week earlier. The unadjusted purchase index increased 5% compared with the previous week and was 16% lower than the same week one year ago.

“Mortgage rates moved higher, with the 30-year fixed rate rising to 7.38%, its highest level in five weeks. While mortgage rates increased slightly on a weekly average basis, rates declined over the course of the week, which likely spurred purchase application activity,” said Joel Kan, CMB, MBA’s vice president and deputy chief economist. “Purchase applications reached their strongest weekly pace since September but remained well behind last year’s pace.”

Kan added that a larger share of borrowers gravitated to ARM loans. The ARM share of activity accounted for 11.5% of applications, the highest share in more than a year, Kan noted.

“At 6.39%, the ARM mortgage rate was nearly a full percentage lower than the 30-year fixed rate last week. Refinance activity was flat over the week with conventional applications showing a small increase while VA refinances dropped more than 11%,” he said.

The refinance share of mortgage activity decreased to 40.1% of total applications from 41.1% the previous week. The adjustable-rate mortgage (ARM) share of activity increased to 11.5% of total applications.

By product, the Federal Housing Administration (FHA) share of total applications decreased to 15.9% from 16.1% the week prior. The U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) share decreased to 12.4% from 12.8% the week prior. The U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) share of total applications remained unchanged at 0.5% from the week prior.

The average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with conforming loan balances ($832,750 or less) increased to 7.38% from 7.35%. Meanwhile, the average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with jumbo loan balances decreased to 7.29% from 7.31%.

The average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages backed by the FHA decreased to 7.05% from 7.06% while rates for 15-year fixed-rate mortgages increased to 6.79% from 6.64%. The average contract interest rate for 5/1 ARMs slightly increased to 6.39% from 6.38%.

Xactus’s Mortgage Intent Index — which analyzes aggregated, anonymized credit-pull activity across the Xactus Intelligent Verification Platform — increased week-over-week to a reading of 97.8.

“Mortgage intent edged up approximately 2.2% week over week,” said Thomas Lloyd, chief strategy officer at Xactus. “The uptick follows last week’s Veterans Day-suppressed reading, suggesting underlying borrower demand remains present even in a challenging rate environment.”

Lloyd continued, “Early readings from the Daily Xactus Mortgage Intent Index offer some cautious optimism heading into Thanksgiving week — Monday’s reading of 65.9 reflects expected seasonal softness, but the year-over-year gap of approximately 16.5% is a notable improvement from the roughly 25% deficit seen in recent weeks.”
