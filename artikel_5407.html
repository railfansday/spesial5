Phillips 66 Expands Arkestro's Predictive Procurement Platform Across Enterprise

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Following successful deployment, Phillips 66 formalizes long-term partnership after identifying dramatic savings and sharply cutting procurement cycle times.

HOUSTON, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Arkestro, the leading predictive procurement platform accelerating enterprise scale, today announced that Phillips 66 (NYSE: PSX) has formalized a long-term partnership to expand its use of Arkestro's predictive procurement platform following strong results from an initial deployment across refining, midstream, and lubricants operations.

During the initial deployment, Phillips 66 category managers and buyers used Arkestro across refining, midstream, and lubricants operations categories including services. Since launching the program in June, the team has processed approximately $96 million in procurement spend through the platform, identified savings and reduced average cycle times.

Phillips 66 scales predictive procurement after early results

The initial deployment demonstrated how predictive procurement can help teams use their own data to move faster and create more competitive outcomes across a broader range of spend. Every awarded event included multiple rounds of supplier competition, with some events completed in fewer than three business days.

"Our experience working with Arkestro has been extremely positive. The support throughout our initial deployment has been outstanding, and we've made meaningful progress in a relatively short period of time," said Kevin Stein, Director of Strategic Sourcing, Phillips 66.

"The results have demonstrated the potential to leverage our data to move faster and drive more competitive outcomes across our procurement operations. We're excited to build on that momentum through this expanded partnership and bring those capabilities to more of our business," said Scott McRee, Chief Procurement Officer, Phillips 66.

Phillips 66 and Arkestro also worked together during the initial deployment to expand platform capabilities for complex services, including support for labor-rate and burden pricing. These categories can involve multiple pricing variables and service structures that are difficult to evaluate at scale.

The enterprise expansion will extend Arkestro's reach to accelerate strategic sourcing objectives and bolster our impact within capital procurement. The deployment will extend access to all of Phillips 66's procurement organization, enabling them to achieve a consistent predictive procurement process across critical spend.

"Phillips 66 is showing what the power of predictive procurement can look like at enterprise scale," said Rob DeSantis, CEO and co-founder of Arkestro. "By moving from weeks to days and creating stronger competition across more spend, their team is expanding the amount of the business procurement can influence, and we're proud to build on that progress together."
