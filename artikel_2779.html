El Chiva Mayor Joins the Urban Pitch Podcast

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Discussing the evolution of LAFC from its inception to today, El Chiva Mayor joins the latest episode of the Urban Pitch Podcast!

Few LAFC fans go as far back with the club as Julio Ramos, aka El Chiva Mayor.

From the club's announcement in 2014, to its first MLS match in 2018, first MLS Cup in 2022, and beyond, Chiva has stood behind the Black and Gold, chanting and cheering along the way.

He joins the latest episode of the Urban Pitch Podcast to discuss his journey with the club and how it has evolved, particularly after the MLS Cup victory.

You can watch the full episode on YouTube, or listen to the audio on streaming services including Apple and Spotify.
