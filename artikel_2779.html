Seahawks vs. Cardinals: Week 9 TV coverage map and FOX commentators

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

It's been a month since the Seattle Seahawks last had to play a regionally televised game. They've had the primetime spotlight for three straight weeks, although two of those games were losses and particularly ugly displays for the offense. For the next couple of weeks, the Seahawks will be in the Sunday afternoon window, starting with the Week 9 showdown versus the Arizona Cardinals.

The main FOX late doubleheader game is the New England Patriots hosting the Green Bay Packers, while the Las Vegas Raiders travel to Santa Clara to face the San Francisco 49ers. Unsurprisingly, Seahawks vs. Cardinals as a limited in terms of national distribution as you can get. Most of the country will see Packers-Patriots, while the other two games are basically "the West Coast games."

If you're in the green zone of this 506 Sports map, you'll get Seahawks vs. Cardinals at 1:25 pm PT. Great news if you're in Alaska and bad news if you're in Hawaii.

Kenny Albert and Jonathan Vilma will be on the call, with Megan Olivi as the sideline reporter. We heard a lot of Albert and Vilma during the 2023 and 2024 seasons, but none at all in 2025.
