Jaguars Add Safety Jessie Bates in Trade with Falcons

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

With the Jacksonville Jaguars recently rumored to be in the 2026 trade market for a big fish, the team made its first big splash, completing another deal with the Atlanta Falcons. This one brings in a legitimate difference-maker in the secondary. According to NFL Insider Adam Schefter, Jacksonville is acquiring safety Jessie Bates from the Falcons in exchange for a 2027 second-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick.

It's the latest move in a trade pipeline that has been building between the two franchises since James Gladstone became general manager.

Bates is a player we initially put on the radar about three weeks ago in our defensive trade targets piece. We projected his trade value at a conditional 2027 third-round pick that could rise to a second, so the final price landed right around where we expected. Bates is in the final year of his contract and is expected to want, and likely get, a new deal. Atlanta evidently wasn't eager to pay it, which opened the door for Jacksonville.

Per Jonathan Jones and Ian Rapoport, Atlanta will pay most of Bates' 2026 $6.5M salary to complete the deal.

Bates arrives with a résumé built on production and takeaways:

3× Second-team All-Pro (2020, 2023, 2025)

14 interceptions coming over the last three and a half seasons in Atlanta

2026 PFF overall grade of 84.1 (fourth best)

2026 PFF coverage grade of 87.9 (second best)

Most importantly, against the backdrop of the Eric Murray injury that took the Jacksonville safety for the season, durability matters. Bates hasn't missed a game since signing with Atlanta, starting 60 of 60 games.

With Jacksonville recently losing Murray for the year, that left the safety room needing a boost. While Ray Lane has played well during Murray's absence, rather than patching the spot, the front office went after one of the better playmakers at the position. This move still allows Lane to play the third safety looks that Campanile loves, while pairing Bates with chess piece Antonio Johnson on base downs.

For me, the biggest takeaway is what Bates does for the scheme. He's the first true rangy, sideline-to-sideline safety the Jaguars have had under defensive coordinator Anthony Campanile. Over the past two seasons, Jacksonville has leaned heavily on two-high safety looks. Bates gives them a ballhawk who can play over the top, which opens up more Cover 1 and Cover 3 looks, with Bates playing as the security blanket over top. Campanile's defense already thrives on takeaways, disguise, and variety. With Bates in the mix, it should be even more unpredictable and even more dangerous to opposing quarterbacks. Through week 8, Bates has Pro Football Focus's third-highest safety grade when playing man coverage, coupled with the 11th-highest grade in zone coverage.
