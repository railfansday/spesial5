Second unit has huge fourth quarter, pushes Hawks past Pistons 107-97

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Atlanta Hawks were in action on Sunday afternoon to face the Detroit Pistons. The Hawks were looking to win their fourth straight game, while the Pistons were coming in after a loss to the Toronto Raptors. The Pistons pretty much dominated the Hawks last season, and it came off the strength of Cade Cunningham.

The Hawks got good news before the game, as Kingston Flemings returned from his lumbar sprain, which caused him to miss a few games. As for the Pistons, they were still without Jalen Duren.

It was a fast-paced quarter for the Hawks, which means they were on the run a lot. That helped them get this transition bucket from Jalen Johnson.

Kingston Flemings made a nice move on the baseline, which led John Collins to fall into his trap.

The Hawks were able to maintain the lead throughout the quarter, despite the Pistons shooting the ball better than them. It was the extra possessions that helped the Hawks, as they led the rebounding battle 16-10. Corey Kispert continued his hot shooting and knocked down a 3-pointer in the first.

Going into the second, the Hawks led 29-26.

The Hawks continued to add to their lead, and it was thanks to the second unit, as Kispert continued playing well. Lu Dort got in on the fun as well.

Flemings had moves for days in the first half, and another defender fell trying to guard him. This time, the rookie guard finished the play with a dunk. The Hawks had a lead as big as 10, but the Pistons began to slowly cut into their deficit.

Daniels tried to keep the Hawks afloat and grabbed his third steal of the half and finished on the other end.

The Pistons eventually took the lead, and it was a back-and-forth contest to end the half. Going into halftime, the Hawks trailed 52-51.

The Hawks found some rhythm to start the third quarter, and it was Nickeil Alexander-Walker who got things going and knocked down three 3-pointers.

Unfortunately, the Pistons were able to stay in front, and they even extended their lead. Outside of those early 3-pointers from Alexander-Walker, the Hawks struggled to find anything consistent on offense as the quarter continued.

The Pistons grew a 13-point lead in the quarter, but the Hawks were able to trail 85-74 going into the fourth.

It was a huge turnaround for the Hawks in the fourth quarter, and the second unit helped walk the Pistons down to cut their deficit. Aaron Wiggins used some jelly to get this layup to go.

Kispert came alive for the Hawks at the perfect time once again and knocked down a 3-pointer to get them down by only one.

Dort's 3-pointer afterwards helped the Hawks take the lead.

It was an insane quarter for the Hawks, as they held the Pistons scoreless until the midpoint of the period, which made it an 18-0 run.

The Pistons were able to finally score at the 4:52 mark and were only down 92-88 at that point. The starters were put back into the game for the Hawks, and they were able to keep the bench's momentum going. Dort was the one player who stayed in the lineup, and it made perfect sense with the intensity he brought on the defensive end.

Alexander-Walker put the nail in the coffin for the Hawks, and it turned out to be a 33-12 quarter for the team, which led to a big win.

Alexander-Walker finished with 27 points, Kispert finished with 18 points, Dort finished with 16 points and seven rebounds, and Daniels finished with 14 rebounds, eight assists, three blocks and five steals.

The Hawks will be back in action next Friday against the Boston Celtics.
