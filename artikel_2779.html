Jets lead Chargers 17-10 at halftime

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Jets didn't score a touchdown in Weeks 9 or 10, but they doubled down on them in the first half of their Week 11 game against the Chargers.

Geno Smith was on the delivery end of both scores. A seven-yarder to running back Breece Hall broke the Jets' touchdown drought early in the second quarter and an eight-yarder to wide receiver Sterling Shepard made it three scores in three possessions for the road team.

It also opened up a 14-point lead, but the Chargers were able to cut that in half on the final play of the half. Quarterback Justin Herbert scrambled out of the pocket and invited disaster because the half would end if he was dropped short of the end zone, but he rumbled through Jets defenders for a 10-yard score that made it 17-10 at the break.

Herbert has four carries for 24 yards and is 6-of-9 for 59 yards through the air. Smith is 10-of-11 for 112 yards and Hall has teamed with Braelon Allen for 63 yards on 19 carries.
