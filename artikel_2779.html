Cardinals vs. Seahawks Week 9 TV Map

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Arizona Cardinals (3-5-0) have the opportunity to get back into being competitive in the standings against the Seattle Seahawks (4-4-0), who will be without their starting quarterback. Starter Sam Darnold has been ruled out for the game. His replacement, journeyman Drew Lock, has a good opportunity to show he still has the juice. And of course, the 'Hawks offense has WR Jaxon Smith-Njigba, who nobody has an answer for. The game is played at Lumen Field in Seattle, Washington.

Arizona is in need of a "W" and has not given up on the season. Currently, the Cardinals remain in last place, but just one game out of second place in the NFC West Division.

The Week 9 game between the Cardinals and Seahawks will air on FOX and Paramount+ at 2:25 p.m. MT, or 4:25 PM ET on Sunday.

Arizona Cardinals Radio: Arizona Sports 98.7 FM (English flagship for the Cardinals)
