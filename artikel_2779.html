New Mexico State football travel plans to Springfield unaffected by ground stop

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Federal Aviation Administration grounded all flights until further notice on Friday afternoon, Nov. 13.

With many key college football games around the country, many visiting teams have had flights halted. The FAA explained it was "due to extraordinary circumstances and for reasons of safety."

Missouri State football is welcoming New Mexico State to Springfield for a Conference USA clash. The ground stop has not affected the Aggies' travel plans.

New Mexico State football departed from El Paso International Airport at 1:48 p.m. CT and arrived in Springfield via Sun Country Airlines at 3:33 p.m. CT. The ground stop was issued at 2:54 p.m. CT.

Missouri State and New Mexico State will face each other at 2 p.m. on Saturday, Nov. 14 inside Plaster Stadium.
