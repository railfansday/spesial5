Steph Curry to be re-evaluated on Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Golden State Warriors will officially re-evaluate Stephen Curry on Friday, Nov. 27, marking two weeks since the 38-year-old guard suffered an oblique abdominal tear during a loss to the Houston Rockets.

While Friday's medical check represents a crucial milestone in Curry's recovery, it serves as a checkpoint rather than an immediate green light to play. Even with positive imaging, the two-time MVP will require a ramp-up period of on-court conditioning and contact practices before returning to game action, keeping a mid-December comeback as the realistic target.

Golden State has struggled to generate offense in his absence, dropping four of its first five games without him while failing to reach 100 points in any of those contests. Before exiting the Houston game early, Curry was off to an electric start, averaging 27.8 points on 45.7% three-point shooting. Because oblique muscles are vital to a shooter's rotational mechanics, the Warriors are expected to prioritize long-term health over rushing their franchise cornerstone back.
