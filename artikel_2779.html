Brad Stevens on what he sees from Boston Celtics so far, good and bad

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Boston Celtics President of Basketball Operations Brad Stevens doe snot miss the scrutiny of his NBA coaching days when, as he put it, "when one missed defensive assignment on a down screen would have sent me into a full-blown analysis of the group." A rough start from Mitchell Robinson and a shooting slump from Derrick White contrasts with hot starts from Jayson Tatum and Paul George, though -- despite Boston's excellent play so far this season -- plenty more ink is being spilled about the negatives.

"Whatever the 10,000-foot view means, it's not 15 games," said Stevens via CLNS Media. "That said, you can see trends emerging, but you ... have to be patient and and see how your team deals with its weaknesses that it's been shown, accentuates the strengths that you've shown to be to have, and and then a large part of it is you also don't have an exact schedule for everybody in the league, right? The schedules are so different."

"And one schedule is so much more challenging, or a condensed part of the schedule is so much more challenging," he added. "So, again, it's better to give it more time. I don't know what the right answer is -- maybe mid-December, around Christmas, those types."

"We've got some things that we really need to work on, and that's pretty evident," said Stevens. "I think those have been well pointed out. But we do have a lot of depth, and we do have a lot of ability to get better in the areas we need to, and then keep soaring with what we do best."

"And I think one of the things that this group really has is is a lot of feel for how to play, and you see that when we're at our very best on both ends. The reaction, the reads, the ability to play off one another, the ability to cover for one another on the defensive end."
