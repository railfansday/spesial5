3 causes for concern for Raiders Week 11 vs Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

For the first time this season, the Raiders will face their division foe Denver Broncos. Bo Nix and the gang boast of a 7-2 record this season, including winners of five straight. So, there's plenty of concerns for the 4-6 Raiders heading to Denver. Here are the top three this week.

For the second time in three weeks, the Raiders will be facing a team with an extra week to prepare for them. Two weeks ago, they faced the 49ers coming off their bye and the result was their second most lopsided loss of the season. With the way the Raiders defense has been playing, it may not matter whether the Broncos had a bye week to prepare or not. But Denver will be as prepared as they possibly can be for whatever the Raiders throw at them and their team will be refreshed from a week of rest as well.

It's possible that Bowers is able to play in this game. And it's possible he does not. If he does play, he may not be his usual dominant self. Just last season he was playing through a knee injury and wasn't nearly the same player as he was as a rookie. I wouldn't expect he'd return to the form he had earlier this season when he began the season with at least 70 yards in seven straight games. And if he can't play at all? That would be much worse. Just look at the way the Raiders offense completely collapsed after he left last week, including five straight three-and-out possessions to lead out the third quarter.

While Fernando Mendoza made some nice plays and showed some of the talent that got him drafted first overall, he is still a rookie. And he was still making his first start. He did well in the early going when everything was on script. But after that, things got much more difficult against a tough Seattle defense that found his weakness and exploited them.

That will only continue this week. And this time, he won't just have to deal with the defensive game plan, he'll have to do it outside the comforts of a dome, in sub-45-degree temperatures, in thin air a mile high, while getting bombarded by crowd noise. No warming into this journey to being the Raiders franchise QB. I would expect a few mistakes from Mendoza as he works through it in a tough environment.
