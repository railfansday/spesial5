Brandon Williams on Warriors offense without Steph Curry: ‘That s*** was tough’

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Watching the Golden State Warriors sputter offensively from the sidelines has tested Brandon Williams' patience, and the dynamic guard is itching to inject some much-needed life back into the rotation. Officially listed as questionable for Wednesday's matchup against the Sacramento Kings, Williams made it clear to reporters that he feels ready to suit up and make an immediate impact.

Asked about the frustration of watching Golden State's offense stall while sidelined, Williams did not mince words. "That shit was tough, I ain't gonna lie," he admitted. "I know exactly what I need to do."

That blunt self-assurance is a welcome sign for a Warriors squad desperate for perimeter punch and pace. If cleared before tip-off against Sacramento, Williams' ability to attack the paint, collapse defenses, and create quick-hitting scoring opportunities could be the exact spark Golden State needs to snap out of its offensive funk.

In the five games and around 90 percent of the game that Curry has missed (the 90 percent being the game against the Houston Rockets in which he was injured), the Warriors have scored a league-worst 98 points per 100 possessions in non-garbage time, per Cleaning the Glass — a far cry from the 118.9 points per 100 possessions they were scoring in their first 11 games, where they had an 8-3 record and were exceeding expectations.

Williams is no Curry, but he does provide stability as a ball handler and will drive the Warriors' pick-and-roll offense as a speedy guard who draws fouls.
