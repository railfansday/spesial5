Heroes, zeros from Giants’ win over Jaguars: J.J. McCarthy’s hustle play saved Big Blue

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Heroes, zeros and the full blitz from the Giants' 23-18 win over the Jaguars on Sunday:

Abdul Carter practically called his shot, quieting his critics with two sacks, four tackles and five quarterback hits. The 23-year-old looked like the force worthy of being taken with the No. 3 overall pick out of Penn State, matching his sack total in the first nine games of the season, and setting the tone for a defense that smothered Jacksonville's fourth-ranked offense.

Trevor Lawrence entered MetLife Stadium as an MVP candidate, leading an offense that had averaged 36.5 points in its previous four games. The Jaguars left New Jersey with new doubts about their status as a Super Bowl contender, as Lawrence completed 25 of 47 passes for 301 yards, with one touchdown and two interceptions, while failing to lead Jacksonville to a touchdown for the game's first 53 minutes.

J.J. McCarthy missed multiple open receivers — and multiple opportunities to stretch the Giants lead, while completing 18 of 38 passes for 173 yards and one touchdown — but the quarterback made one of the game's biggest plays, hustling to tackle the Jaguars' Antonio Johnson and prevent a Cam Skattebo fumble from becoming a defensive touchdown in the third quarter. McCarthy, who had 33 rushing yards, also didn't throw an interception against the defense that leads the AFC in picks.
