Louisville vs. Stetson score today, women's basketball live updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Louisville women's basketball might need to tap into its depth — already.

The seventh-ranked Cardinals could be without starters Elif Istanbulluoglu and Mackenly Randolph when they host Stetson tonight. Istanbulluoglu appeared to have injured her ankle, and Randolph hit her head during the UofL's record-setting 119-47 win over Bellarmine in the season opener Nov. 2.

Head coach Jeff Walz said after the game he didn't expect Istanbulluoglu to play against the Hatters, who opened their season with a 75-52 victory over Flagler. The Cardinals are as deep as ever in the frontcourt and scored 66 points in the paint against Bellarmine. So, they have others who can step in for Istanbulluoglu, starting with Amiya Joyner. The LSU transfer came off the bench and performed well against the Knights, totaling 14 points on 5-for-5 shooting, four rebounds and two steals in 13 minutes played.

Grace Mbugua is also a viable option after opening her sophomore season with 10 points and five rebounds. Virginia Tech transfer Carys Baker has versatility; she can shoot the 3-pointer and play in the post. She hit two shots from beyond the arc and scored 13 points in addition to recording four assists and three rebounds.
