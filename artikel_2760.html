Lakers’ Collin Sexton gets stitches after scary collision in win over Bucks

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

MILWAUKEE — Collin Sexton received multiple stitches to close a deep cut over his right eye after the Lakers' 123-112 win over the Bucks on Wednesday night.

Sexton and Ryan Rollins headbutted midway through the fourth quarter while the Lakers guard was defending the Bucks guard.

Play was stopped with 6:51 remaining after it was clear Sexton was bleeding.

A head collision between Collin Sexton (10) and Ryan Rollins (13) led to both players exiting the game in the fourth quarter of the Lakers' 123-112 road win over the Bucks. Getty Images

It wasn't known as of Wednesday night how many stitches the reserve guard needed.

His injury happened during the same game star guard Austin Reaves and Luka Dončić had early exits in the second half because of injury scares.

Lakers head coach JJ Redick said Collin Sexton received stitches after the win over the Bucks. AP Photo/Mike Stewart

Reaves suffered a heel injury midway through the third quarter.

Coach JJ Redick said Reaves' X-rays were negative and that he would get imaging on Thursday. Redick ruled out an Achilles tendon injury for Reaves, who didn't speak with reporters postgame.

Dončić had an injury scare with a few minutes left in the game when he clutched at his right ankle after Kyle Kuzma closed out against him as he made a 23-foot jumper.

Collin Sexton had 10 points and three assists in 13 minutes before his injury over his right eye. AP Photo/Aaron Gash

He sat on the floor holding his ankle before getting up and making a free throw. Dončić told reporters afterward that he was "good" despite the pain.
