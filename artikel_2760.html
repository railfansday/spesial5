76ers’ Jaylen Brown ruled out for rest of Heat game with concerning injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

76ers' Jaylen Brown ruled out for rest of Heat game with concerning injury appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Coach Nick Nurse's Philadelphia 76ers took on the Miami Heat in Philadelphia on a night when LeBron James looked like his old self again with a first-half scoring barrage. Jaylen Brown started for Nurse's team, scoring six points in 17 minutes as James and Tyrese Maxey led the team to a first-half lead, amid Joel Embiid health concerns.

Brown's health status also got a huge scare as Kyle Neubeck of PHLY Sports shared an update.

"Jaylen Brown will NOT return tonight due to hamstring tightness, per Sixers," Neubeck said.

Video showed Brown appearing to clutch his left hamstring after knocking down an impressive stepback fadeaway and barely competing on the defensive end afterward.

Brown has played in 15 of the Sixers' 19 games this season, averaging just over 34 minutes and just over 22 minutes per game. He came to the Sixers this offseason in a trade with the Boston Celtics and has helped lead them to a strong opening month of the season.

The Sixers led 90-81 heading into the fourth quarter as James and Maxey combined for 48 points to lead Nurse's team against Erik Spoelstra and Giannis Antetokounmpo's Heat. Antetokounmpo had 27 points as of the start of the fourth quarter while Bam Adebayo joined him in double digits with 10 points in 28 minutes and Davion Mitchell was second with 18 points in 32 minutes.

The Sixers had eight steals to two for the Heat as the game headed toward its final conclusion, with Joel Embiid sitting the game out for coach Nurse's team.

James' early game heroics came off of comments by Spoelstra about him getting the best of Father Time, which the 76ers star proved yet again against his former coach.
