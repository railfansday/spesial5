Chiefs RB Kenneth Walker remains out of practice Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Chiefs running back Kenneth Walker remained out of practice Thursday.

Walker injured his groin in practice last week and missed Sunday’s victory over the Falcons. The Chiefs play the Cardinals on Sunday and the Bills on Thanksgiving Day, and coach Andy Reid has said the schedule could factor into Walker’s availability.

“If he’s not going to further injure it, then he’ll go play,” Reid said Wednesday. “If he can, if it looks like he’s on the edge there, then he doesn’t play. Keep it simple.”

Rookie Emmett Johnson will again start for Walker, if Walker is inactive. Johnson had 22 carries for 101 yards and six catches for 70 yards and two touchdowns against the Falcons.

Offensive lineman Jaylon Moore (ankle) and offensive guard Trey Smith (concussion) also spent a second consecutive day rehabbing.

Defensive tackle Omarr Norman-Lott (knee) was again limited.

The only change from Wednesday’s report was Nick Bolton (shin) being a full participant after limited work Wednesday.

Quarterbacks Patrick Mahomes (knee/right elbow) and Justin Fields (neck), tight end Travis Kelce (calf/ankle) and Rashee Rice (knee) were among the Chiefs who had a second consecutive day of full participation.
