10 Takeaways from the Celtics loss to the Magic

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

On the second night of a back-to-back coming off of a dominant 131-94 win over the Dallas Mavericks, the Celtics faced off against the Orlando Magic. In a game that was back and forth the entire night, Boston would fall 115-105 after a rough fourth quarter performance.

Going into the final quarter, Boston came off a third quarter that saw them play their best basketball of the night. They outscored Orlando 36-25 and showcased their defensive synergy to take the lead. The fourth was a different story as Desmond Bane started to go off, the Celtics couldn't keep up, shooting 9-22 from the field and 6-16 from three while the Magic shot 16-21 from the field and 5-8 from three. Although it was a valiant effort by the Celtics, Orlando outscored Boston 40-28. The biggest problem in the fourth quarter for the Celtics was their rebounding. The Magic out-rebounded the Celtics 16-4 in the fourth quarter and it felt like they also got to every 50-50 ball. This was mostly due to the work of Jalen Suggs who was a monster inside but it also just looked like the Celtics were gassed and were just a step behind.

One of the biggest reasons why the Celtics lost this game tonight was the fact the Neemias Queta was not in the lineup. He was ruled out pregame due to rest and it felt like with the success Luka Garza has had against the Magic in the past, the Celtics could overcome the loss of Queta but that was not the case.

Garza played solidly but his three-point shots were not falling and that limited his impact. Mitchell Robinson looked lost at times on defense and a step slow on offense while Chris Cenac Jr. was playing his first real NBA minutes of his career. All of this resulted in the Magic shooting 19-23 at the basket tonight and out scoring Boston 54-32 when it came to points in the paint. Franz Wagner was able to get downhill all night long, collapse the Celtics defense, and find a wide-open Magic player on a lot of possessions. This allowed Orlando to keep a lead over Boston for most of the night and it didn't matter what big they through out there, Boston couldn't slow down the Magic from getting to the paint.

This game really highlighted how important Neemias Queta is to the Celtics defense. Boston has a net rating of +17.4 with Queta on the floor and a defensive rating of 108.4. When asked postgame on playing without Queta, Joe Mazzulla stressed how important he is by saying, "It shows the standard he needs to play to and he makes us a different team." Does this game show that we need to start the Neemias Queta for MVP campaign, maybe not. However, it does show Queta's ability to be the defensive anchor of this Celtics team and if he was out there tonight, Boston probably wins this game.

The Boston Celtics drafted Chris Cenac Jr with the 27th pick in the 2026 NBA Draft out of Houston and we saw flashes of a guy who could be a big part of the future plan of this team in Summer League and Preseason. When the regular season started, Cenac Jr. made a few appearances with the Maine Celtics where he performed well, even having a double-double of 19 points and 11 rebounds in Maine's first game of the season. Cenac Jr. got some run during the Celtics blowout win of the Mavericks on Sunday in the fourth quarter and his athleticism was on full display with three impressive dunks.

After that performance and with Queta out, he earned some minutes in this game against the Magic and I think he took full advantage of it. In 12 minutes off the bench, Chris Cenac Jr. finished with 4 points and 2 rebounds on 2-3 shooting. He made his biggest impact during the Celtics third quarter run where he started by making a great cut from the corner to catch a lob from Payton Pritchard. On the next possession got a great defensive stop on Franz Wagner. After Jayson Tatum got the rebound, he found Cenac Jr. for another slam that had him hyped and the TD Garden crowd in a frenzy.

It was interesting how they used Cenac Jr. in this game because he did offer a completely different skill set from Robinson and Garza. He brought energy for the Celtics when they desperately needed it in the second half and they even gave him a chance in the final five minutes of the game before Orlando pulled away. Jayson Tatum said after the Dallas game that "Chris is gonna be good. Chris is already good." After the Orlando game Tatum gave tough love saying, "We're not gonna hold his hand. When we get out there, we expect him to make plays because we know what he's capable of."

I have a hard time disagreeing with him. At only 19 years, it looks like he has a bright future with Boston and I hope he gets more chances throughout the season similar to this one. Brad Stevens might have found another gem with the 27th overall pick at the center position.

Another underrated reason for Boston's loss in this game has to do with the fact that Boston was not capitalizing with their opportunities at the free throw line. The Celtics shot 9-17 (52.9%) from the charity strip in this game and in a game where you lost by 10 points, leaving 8 points on the cutting room floor is just killer. On the season Boston is slightly below average when it comes to free throw shooting, ranking 19th in the NBA at 77.4%.

In the Celtics win against the Mavericks, they put on a historic offensive display in the first quarter. They outscored Dallas 42-9 in the first quarter, the biggest scoring margin in franchise history in a quarter. Naturally due to the fact that it was a back-to-back, it felt like the pendulum of shooting luck was going to swing back in the other direction in this game and that was the case. Boston scored 41 points in the first half against Orlando which just feels kind of funny given what they did on Sunday.

Boston shot 7-27 (25.9%) from three in the first half but as a whole, the Celtics shooting wasn't that bad. They finished shooting 20-56 (35.7%) from three point range and via Sean Grande on X, this game snapped a streak of 25 straight games where the Celtics won when they hit 20+ threes in a game. You could also call Sunday's performance an anomaly since they were coming off three days rest but that is just how the NBA is sometimes. One day the shooting is the best you've ever seen and the next day it's a struggle even though you were getting similar open looks.

Paul George once again put up a solid performance for the Celtics, finishing with 22 points and 8 rebounds on 7-13 shooting from the field and 5-9 from three. It was good to see George play the second half of a back-to-back tonight after sitting the last two times Boston was in this scenario and he was a much needed boost to the offense. He had 15 of his points in the first half and felt like the only guy who was able to hit a shot from three.

When asked postgame on playing in this game and his workload, George seemed happy to just be able to be on the floor. "Just excited that, at year 17, I can still be playing back-to-backs given all the adversity and all the bad luck that I've had from an injury standpoint." It makes me happy that Paul George is finally able to have a healthy start to his Celtics tenure so far. If he continues this play he could end up as an all-star and I think Boston will take that any day of the week.

One trend we have seen from Jayson Tatum in the last two games is when the third quarter hits, he takes over on scoring. For the game, Tatum finished with 24 points but in the third quarter alone he finished with 14 points on 5-11 shooting from the field and 2-5 shooting from three. This follows his third quarter performance against the Mavericks where he had 21 points on 6-9 shooting from the field and 4-6 shooting from three.

This isn't only a two game trend however, Tatum is currently third in the NBA in third quarter scoring at 8.2 Points, only trailing Shai Gilgeous-Alexander and Jalen Brunson. He is also tied for third with 2.3 rebounds and third at 2.0 assists in 11.1 minutes (also ranked third).

This is most likely due to the fact the Celtics rotations have Tatum play the entirety of third quarters before sitting to start the fourth but the last two games just made me notice how often he starts to take over games in the third quarter. It could also just be because Tatum is already warmed up and after halftime he knows he can just empty the tank with a bench lineup alongside him. I do think in this game Joe Mazzulla waited a little too long to bring him back into the game during the fourth quarter but I don't know if that would have made a real difference anyway. It might not be the most meaningful stat but it does showcase Tatum's ability to take over games and I think it's pretty neat to see the stats back up the eye test in this scenario.

The last guy I wanted to highlight from this game who I thought had a big impact was Payton Pritchard. He finished with 21 points, 5 rebounds, and 4 assists on 7-16 shooting from the field and 4-11 from three. This was a just a solid all around Pritchard game and his offense, much like Paul George, was desperately needed at times throughout this game.

The fourth quarter was where he did most of his scoring, finishing with 13 points on 4-9 shooting. Pritchard was the Celtics number one option when Tatum went to the bench and kept Boston in the game with answers to Orlando's offense. At the end of the first quarter, we also saw a signature buzzer beater that although we see all the time from him, is still a joy to watch. His passing also looked really good in this game, setting up multiple alley-oop opportunities with Cenac Jr and Robinson. The scoring numbers are starting to rise for Pritchard as he is averaging 15 points per game over his last 5 games and has been a big reason as to why Boston had previously gone on a 6-game win streak.

In the past, I never understood the hype around Franz Wagner. Like I knew he was a good player and he was part of the Magic's duo alongside Paolo Banchero but it always felt like every time I watched him he either under performed or was out with injury. This game changed my tune on him however as he finished with 21 points, 9 assists, and 8 rebounds. Just watching him play against the Celtics, I couldn't help but notice the similarities he has to Jayson Tatum, especially as a passer.

Tatum is most effective for the Celtics when he is driving into the paint. The defense collapses and at times has all 5 guys focusing on him while Tatum makes the right pass to a wide open shooter at the three point line. In this game, it felt like Franz Wagner was doing the same thing and it was incredibly effective. He is a lot better of a passer than I gave him credit for and to think this is one of the worst games he's had in the last week or so is crazy. I always thought Banchero was going to be the Tatum clone out of this duo for the Magic but I was wrong. Wagner is becoming a legitimate star and I wonder what Orlando will do with their duo in the future.

I don't know why, but every time the Celtics play the Orlando Magic it feels like pure chaos ensues. Over the years there has been one consistent thing in this matchup and that was Boston always beating Orlando in TD Garden. That streak was snapped in this game as the Magic got their first win in Boston since December 18th, 2022.

Over the years though we have seen these teams battle and they kind of remind me of the Celtics from 2020-2023. They have their duo who are still figuring it out in Banchero and Wagner, they have their Marcus Smart in Jalen Suggs, and they have a bunch of other young depth guys who just play hard and contribute like Anthony Black and Tristan da Silva. In the regular season, Outside of Game 5 of the 2025 first round playoff series, the Celtics and Magic have played a ton of close games with an average margin of victory of 10.3 points in the regular season with Boston winning 5-4 in the last 9 matchups. I'm not sure what it is about the Magic, but you always know you're going to be in a dog fight with them every time the Celtics see them on the schedule.
