Eagles roster news: Giants poach practice squad player, plus more happenings

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Philadelphia Eagles officially announced seven roster moves on Monday.

In addition to these transactions, there were some more Eagles-related items listed on the NFL's November 16 personnel notice.

WR Hollywood Brown and OT Fred Johnson cleared waivers.

WR Xavier Gipson was signed away from the Eagles' practice squad to the New York Giants' active roster.

DT Leki Fotu had a tryout with the Eagles.

WR Quentin Skinner had a free agent visit with the Eagles.

Let's sort through this news on a player-by-player basis.

These veterans are now free to sign with any team. The Eagles could add them back to their practice squad … but I'm guessing they'll be looking for new NFL homes. Will be mildly interesting to see if they're able to latch on anywhere.

You may recall Gipson spent time as the Eagles' punt returner in 2025 before a disastrous fumble in a loss to the Dallas Cowboys ultimately contributed to him getting cut.

The Birds originally acquired him off waivers from the New York Giants. Then he returned to the Giants after being waived by Philly last year. Then he signed to the Eagles' practice squad in September after New York cut him.

Now the Giants are bringing him back to the Meadowlands. He must be well-versed with the New Jersey Turnpike by now.

The 28-year-old Fotu is a 6'5", 330 pound defensive tackle who's made 26 starts in 66 career games played from 2020-2025. With the Eagles trading Jordan Davis to the Jacksonville Jaguars, the Birds have a need for more nose tackle depth. Fotu can help to fill that role, be it on the practice squad or the roster.

Skinner previously joined the Eagles' practice squad on October 28 before getting cut on November 3. The Eagles could be interested in him providing some wide receiver depth.
