Roma favored over Milan in race for Endrick

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Endrick option is gaining momentum ahead of the January transfer window.

This time, it's being relaunched by Matteo Moretto, a transfer expert who regularly collaborates with Fabrizio Romano.

Moretto believes Real Madrid's Brazilian talent has a real chance of leaving Spain during the upcoming winter transfer window.

This prospect is of particular interest to Roma, who are listed as the main Italian contenders for the player.

Speaking to DAZN Spain, Moretto reviewed the situation of the 2006-born striker, explaining that Endrick's desire could prove decisive in convincing Real Madrid to open up to a transfer. The Brazilian wants to play more consistently and would welcome a new experience away from the Spanish capital, just as he did last season with his loan spell at Olympique Lyonnais.

According to Moretto, Roma are ahead of Milan in the Italian race for Endrick. Sporting director Tony D'Amico has been following the player for some time and had the opportunity to observe him closely during the Champions League match at the Olimpico against Real Madrid.

The Brazilian, in that match, demonstrated his talent by scoring one of the goals in the Spanish comeback. Milan, however, remain interested, as are several Premier League clubs and Portuguese clubs.

Moretto reiterated that Roma are the Italian team most interested, without however indicating any agreements have already been reached. Competition is strong, and the final decision will depend both on the player and on Real Madrid's willingness to accept a loan.
