Carnell Tate turns failure to touch him down by contact into a 59-yard touchdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

After the Cowboys scored their fifth touchdown in five drives, Tennessee answered with another touchdown.

Receiver Carnell Tate made a catch and went down. He wasn’t touched until after his legs were off the ground. He kept running, and he didn’t stop until he got to the end zone.

There was an immediate question as to whether Tate was down when touched because his towel was on the ground. Fox rules analyst Mike Pereira explained that the towel doesn’t make the player down.

For Tate, it was the longest catch of his career. It also was the longest throw of quarterback Cam Ward’s career.

None of it matters if the Titans can’t stop the Dallas offense. Already, the Cowboys are moving again, with a 34-23 lead.
