Alabama football Week 13 opponent preview: Auburn Tigers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

One of college football's greatest rivalries will be renewed Saturday night when the Alabama Crimson Tide welcome the Auburn Tigers to Saban Field at Bryant-Denny Stadium for the latest edition of the Iron Bowl.

A rivalry dating back to the late 1800's, Alabama and Auburn have played a number of high-profile games across the last decade-plus, with this latest matchup holding high stakes for the Crimson Tide in particular. Alabama is closing in on a College Football Playoff berth, meaning that the lone team standing in the way of the Crimson Tide and a trip to the CFP is Auburn.

The Tigers enter this latest Iron Bowl in the midst of a new era under first-year head coach Alex Golesh. Auburn will also be looking to snap a six-game losing streak against Alabama in the Iron Bowl which dates back to 2020, as well as a seven-game skid in Tuscaloosa.

Here is everything you need to know about the 2026 Auburn Tigers ahead of Saturday's Iron Bowl showdown inside Saban Field at Bryant-Denny Stadium.

Stadium: Jordan-Hare Stadium, Auburn, Alabama

Ranking: Unranked in CFP rankings, Unranked in US LBM Coaches Poll, Unranked in AP Poll

Auburn comes to Tuscaloosa for the Iron Bowl as an unranked team sitting at 7-4 overall (4-4 SEC). The resume for Auburn is headlined by arguably college football's top win this season in a 27-24 road victory at Georgia, while the Tigers have also defeated Arkansas, Mississippi State, and Samford in their current three-game winning streak.

Alex Golesh is in his first season as the head coach at Auburn following three seasons at USF where he held a 23-15 combined record from 2023-25. Joel Gordon is in his first season as the Tigers' offensive coordinator, while on the opposite side of the ball, DJ Durkin is in his third year calling plays for Auburn's defense. Durkin also served as the Tigers' interim head coach during this game a year ago.

The Tigers come to Tuscaloosa ranking No. 59 among FBS teams with an average of 29 points per game on offense, a total which also ranks No. 10 in the SEC. Auburn's offense also ranks among the middle of the SEC in each of total, passing, and rushing yards per game, each of which rank either sixth or seventh-best in the conference.

Points Per Game Allowed: 23.9 (No. 64 in FBS)

Auburn has struggled at times this season on defense as the Tigers currently ranking No. 64 among FBS teams, as well as No. 9 in the SEC, with an average of 23.9 points per game allowed to opponents. The Tigers defense is also allowing the most yards per game to opponents in the SEC at 382.8 a contest, while the passing and rushing yards allowed both rank No. 14 in the conference.

Byrum Brown, Quarterback, Fifth-Year Senior

Chas Nimrod, Wide Receiver, Fifth-Year Senior

Alex McPherson, Kicker, Fifth-Year Senior

The Auburn offense is led by a quarterback that Alabama has already faced twice before in Byrum Brown, as well as a pair of wide receivers who also transferred from USF during the offseason in Keshaun Singleton and Chas Nimrod. Jeremiah Cobb and Omar Mabson II are the Tigers' top two running backs, while Alex McPherson is a veteran kicker who is in his fifth season at Auburn.

Cody Sigler, Defensive Line, Fifth-Year Senior

Auburn's defense is headlined by sophomore linebacker Elijah Melendez, as well as a pair of safeties in Eric Winters and Kaleb Harris, who all currently rank among the Tigers' top tacklers. Shadarius Toodle is another name to know as the freshman linebacker continues to emerge in recent weeks, and is now tied for the team-lead with four sacks in only three games played.
