Division II team loses conference title game after time management meltdown forces OT

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Kentucky State Thorobreds and Benedict Tigers met on Saturday for the Southern Intercollegiate Athletic Conference (SIAC) championship game.

The game went to overtime, but not before the Thorobreds had an all-time meltdown with the clock.

Kentucky State had first-and-goal from the 4-yard line in a 45-45 game with 56 seconds left. When the final whistle sounded, though, Kentucky State hadn't even fully lined up to attempt a game-winning field goal.

The Thorobreds ran the ball on first down for a 2-yard loss. It looked like they were just lining up for the expected field goal attempt, but that is not what happened.

The players walked around, showing little urgency. The broadcast then cut to Thorobreds head coach Felton Huggins Jr., who realized with 12 seconds left on the play clock that he needed to get his kick team on the field because his team had no timeouts left.

At that point, 20 seconds remained on the game clock. Kentucky State ran out its kicking team, but because the offense hadn't gotten the message and had already lined up to run another play, they didn't leave the field until about six seconds remained on the play clock.

Kentucky State ran out of time and was whistled for a delay of game with nine seconds left on the game clock. The penalty brings with it a 10-second runoff. So, despite having nearly a minute to work with, the Thorobreds ran just one play and didn't even attempt a game-winning field goal.

Benedict led 14-7 in the first quarter, but Kentucky State then scored 31 consecutive points to build a 24-point lead. Benedict battled back, tying the game at 45 with 3:15 to go.

After Kentucky State failed to move the ball in what looked like its final possession in regulation, the Thorobreds punted. The Tigers muffed the punt, and Kentucky State took over at the 17-yard line. After getting down to the 4-yard line, it looked like the game was over, with the Thorobreds just needing a field goal to win.

After the clock mismanagement, Benedict scored a TD in overtime, and Kentucky State couldn't match it. The Tigers won the SIAC championship, their third in program history.
