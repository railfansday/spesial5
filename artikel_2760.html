Brian Flores’ future with Vikings in serious danger after MNF loss to Bills

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Brian Flores is arguably the best defensive coordinator in the NFL right now. Monday night's loss to Josh Allen and the Buffalo Bills offered another reminder of just how important he has been to the Minnesota Vikings' success this season.

As has been the case all season, Flores' defense gave the Vikings several opportunities to win. However, the team's offense again failed to hold their end of the bargain.

With the Vikings on a losing skid, the imbalance in offense and defense is becoming increasingly difficult to ignore, and Minnesota could soon face a difficult decision.

Minnesota's 23-6 defeat against Buffalo was another frustrating example of an offense wasting a strong defensive performance. Flores' unit intercepted Josh Allen once, recorded four sacks and limited the Bills to 339 total yards on Monday. The Bills also averaged just 4.3 yards per play.

Meanwhile, Carson Wentz completed just eight of 25 passes for 81 yards. He threw two interceptions and lost a fumble as the Vikings managed only 178 total yards.

Despite entering Week 9 with the NFL's best scoring defense at 13.3 points allowed per game, Minnesota couldn't get enough support that now ranks as the sixth-worst in the league after Monday.

While finding solutions to the problems at quarterback remains the immediate priority, the Vikings' biggest concern might be keeping Flores beyond this season.

The defensive coordinator has attracted head coaching interest for multiple years, interviewing with the Baltimore Ravens and Pittsburgh Steelers in January 2026. He eventually signed a contract extension with Minnesota on Jan. 21.

However, with another coaching cycle approaching in 2027, Flores' work this season will draw interest from several teams, and the Vikings know too well that losing him would mean replacing an important part of their identity.

Head coach Kevin O'Connell has another compelling reason to keep his defensive coordinator around. But how the Vikings finish the season could play a huge part in whether Flores decides to stay or leave.
