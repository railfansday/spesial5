Welcome to HackerNoon’s Meet the Writer Interview series, where we learn a bit more about the contributors that have written some of our favorite stories.

My name is Naresh Waghela, and I am a Senior SEO Executive, technology writer, and digital marketing professional. I specialize in SEO strategy, content optimization, digital growth, and emerging technologies.

I have a strong interest in Artificial Intelligence, Large Language Models (LLMs), Generative AI, and how technology is transforming the digital landscape. I enjoy researching complex technical subjects and turning them into practical, easy-to-understand content that helps readers learn something valuable.

My latest HackerNoon story, "Making Local LLMs Usable on 8GB GPUs," explores how developers can run and optimize local Large Language Models on GPUs with limited VRAM.

The goal was to demonstrate that working with AI models locally does not always require expensive, high-end hardware. With the right optimization techniques, developers can make AI more accessible, practical, and affordable.

I write about a variety of topics, including Artificial Intelligence, SEO, digital marketing, Generative AI, technology trends, and online visibility.

I am particularly interested in the intersection of AI and search, including Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), and how AI-powered search is changing the way people discover information online.

My writing process usually starts with research. I explore a topic, understand its technical aspects, review available information, and identify what readers would find most useful.

I then organize my ideas into a clear structure and focus on making the content informative, practical, and engaging. I also spend time reviewing and refining the article to ensure accuracy and readability.

One of the biggest challenges is simplifying complex technical concepts without losing their technical accuracy.

Technology evolves quickly, especially in AI and machine learning. It takes continuous research and learning to ensure that the information remains relevant, reliable, and useful to readers.

I want to continue growing as a technology writer and digital strategist while exploring the possibilities of Artificial Intelligence.

One of my long-term goals is to contribute meaningful insights to the global technology community and help bridge the gap between complex AI innovations and practical real-world applications.

I genuinely enjoy exploring new technologies, experimenting with AI tools, and discovering interesting ideas that can turn into useful projects. Sometimes, what starts as casual curiosity becomes hours of research and experimentation.

I enjoy spending quality time with my family and exploring new places whenever I get the opportunity. It gives me a refreshing break from professional responsibilities and helps me maintain a healthy perspective.

Readers can expect more content around Local LLMs, Generative AI, practical AI implementations, AI-powered search, and the future of digital marketing.

I am particularly interested in exploring how developers and businesses can use AI efficiently without always depending on expensive infrastructure.

HackerNoon is a valuable platform for independent technology writers. It provides an opportunity to share ideas, reach a global audience, and become part of a community that values technology, innovation, and knowledge sharing.

I appreciate the platform's support for writers and its efforts to make technical knowledge accessible to a wider audience.

I believe that learning should never stop, especially in the technology industry. Every new development brings an opportunity to explore, experiment, and share knowledge.

Thank you to the HackerNoon editorial team and community for providing a platform where writers can share their ideas and contribute to the global technology conversation.
