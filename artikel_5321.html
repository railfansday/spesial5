XRP Price has fallen from around $1.55 in early October to near $1.17, reflecting a 27% drop..

XRP open interest dropped 12.36% as traders reduced exposure amid growing market pressure.

Analysts are eying $1.10–$1.15 zone as a critical support area for bulls.

XRP is facing a critical test as its price falls toward $1.10 after dropping from its October high of $1.55. The decline has erased more than 27% of its recent gains and weakened trader confidence, ETF demand, and leveraged positions.

However, some well-known crypto analysts believe XRP could recover if it holds key support levels despite the growing negative sentiment.

XRP’s recent decline has damaged more than its price chart, as it is facing pressure from both traders and the overall market.

XRP price fell 2.37% in 24 hours to around $1.17, underperforming Bitcoin as leveraged long positions were forced out of the market. The drop below the $1.21 support level triggered further selling, forcing traders to close more than $10 million in leveraged long positions.

Open interest also dropped 12.36%. This means fewer traders are keeping their positions open, showing that many are stepping back from the market as prices become more volatile.

Market sentiment has weakened too. Traders are becoming more negative about XRP, with social sentiment falling to its lowest level since July.

Meanwhile, the pressure is also seen in the XRP ETF flows, as they have also slowed down. ETFs recorded inflows on only six days over the past month, while most other days saw outflows or no inflow. Still, cumulative net inflows remain at $1.81 billion.

Despite the weak market, XRP is now approaching a key support zone that could decide its next move. Well-known crypto analyst ChartNerd said XRP’s weekly chart remains weak after breaking below its 10-week support.

The moment of truth: ripple:native has broken below its 10-week support floor and is searching for a $1.16-$1.04 demand zone. The analyst sees $1.16–$1.04 as an important demand zone, warning that a drop below $1.16 could lead to further losses. However, a move back above $1.34 could improve the outlook.

Ali Martinez is taking a more contrarian view. He said XRP has broken below the middle of its trading channel, putting the lower boundary near $0.99 in focus as the next possible support.

He also pointed to weak XRP trading volume and falling market share, saying a strong rally may be difficult until Bitcoin dominance starts to fall. a future reversal more room.

However, not all analysts see the same level of risk. Well-known crypto trader Dark Defender said XRP’s long-term market structure remains intact despite the recent drop. He sees $1.12 as an important support level.
