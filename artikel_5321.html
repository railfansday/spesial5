XRP Price Prediction: Brad Garlinghouse Faces $1.05 Test as Whales Buy $72M

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

XRP whales accumulated $72 million over four days, but its price remains dangerously close to critical support. Meanwhile, spot XRP ETFs recorded $10.4 million in net outflows, their largest single-day withdrawal since March.

XRP is hovering around $1.05, a level that could determine its next major move. With selling pressure mounting, Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse faces another test of confidence in the token.

Garlinghouse has spent years positioning Ripple as a major player in institutional payments. Its ambitions extend across cross-border transfers, financial infrastructure, and institutional adoption.

However, XRP holders still need those developments to translate into stronger token demand. Ripple’s business expansion and XRP’s market performance are two different things. That gap has widened as investors reconsider their exposure to established crypto. Institutional partnerships can strengthen Ripple’s business without automatically triggering additional XRP purchases.

The latest whale accumulation offers some encouragement. Large holders adding $72 million worth of XRP suggests certain investors see value near current levels. But ETF withdrawals tell a different story. The $10.4 million outflow shows institutional demand remains inconsistent, even as whales accumulate.

Bitcoin’s investment narrative is gradually expanding beyond digital gold. Developers are increasingly exploring ways to bring faster transactions, decentralized finance, and additional applications into its ecosystem.

That shift could create opportunities for infrastructure projects targeting Bitcoin’s limitations. Bitcoin Layer 2 networks aim to make the underlying ecosystem more useful without replacing Bitcoin itself.

Bitcoin Hyper is one project pursuing this direction. Its approach combines Bitcoin-focused infrastructure with Solana Virtual Machine execution, targeting faster transactions and broader application development.

The project has raised $33.2 million at its current presale stage. Its current token price stands at a low $0.0136, with staking rewards reaching 35% APY. But its presale is scheduled to conclude on November 26, putting a deadline on the current entry opportunity.

XRP represents an established payments-focused crypto, while Bitcoin Hyper targets an emerging infrastructure opportunity.

For investors seeking exposure to earlier-stage projects, the potential appeal lies in entering before an ecosystem reaches maturity. Established cryptocurrencies offer greater market recognition, but emerging infrastructure projects can attract attention through their expansion potential.

Bitcoin Hyper’s pitch centers on making Bitcoin more functional. That proposition could appeal to investors who believe Bitcoin’s next growth chapter will involve more than simply holding BTC.

The immediate XRP price outlook remains heavily dependent on the $1.05 support level. Losing this floor could expose XRP to another wave of selling. The next downside target sits around $0.99. A move below psychological support could further weaken confidence among short-term traders.

However, XRP’s Relative Strength Index has fallen to approximately 24. That places the indicator deep inside oversold territory, suggesting selling pressure has become unusually intense.

Oversold conditions can create opportunities for a relief rally, particularly when large holders continue accumulating. But buyers still need to demonstrate enough strength to reclaim lost resistance levels.

A recovery toward $1.30 would provide an initial bullish target. Stronger momentum could subsequently bring the $1.50 area back into focus.

For now, the XRP price faces two competing forces. Whale accumulation and oversold conditions offer reasons for optimism, while ETF outflows and fragile support keep downside risks alive. Defending $1.05 could give bulls another opportunity to rebuild momentum. Losing it would put $0.99 firmly on the radar.
