Rothschild Investment Reveals XRP ETF Stake

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Rothschild Investment LLC, the Chicago-based investment management firm, has filed its latest 13F with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), revealing its stake in the Bitwise XRP ETF.

Rothschild Investment’s filing, made on Nov. 10, indicates that the company has invested in 6,300 shares of the Bitwise XRP ETF.

Based on the information provided, these shares were worth $104,769 as of Sept. 30.

The investment in the Bitwise XRP ETF is listed under CUSIP 09174F107 in Rothschild Investment’s third-quarter report.

Interestingly, Rothschild Investment did not have any reported holdings of the XRP ETF in its previous quarterly filing, which indicates that the firm’s investment in the XRP ETF occurred in the third quarter of 2026.

The Bitwise XRP ETF, listed under the ticker XRP, was introduced in November 2025.

The ETF enables traditional investors to invest in the Ripple-linked cryptocurrency without needing to buy and hold XRP tokens themselves.

Although Rothschild Investment has invested in the XRP ETF, it is not a significant part of its overall portfolio.

The firm’s total portfolio value for 13F-reportable holdings is around $2.69 billion.

Rothschild Investment holds much larger stakes in Bitcoin and Ethereum investments.

When it comes to cryptocurrencies, Rothschild Investment’s largest holding is in the iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF by BlackRock, where the company owns 40,188 shares worth around $1.9 million.

Additionally, Rothschild Investment reported holding $911,300 in the Grayscale Bitcoin Trust ETF and $335,160 in the Morgan Stanley Bitcoin Trust ETF.

Other Bitcoin-related investments include the Grayscale Bitcoin Mini Trust ETF ($125,113), VanEck Bitcoin ETF ($118,100), Bitwise Bitcoin ETF ($116,802) and Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund ($2,107).

In total, the seven Bitcoin investments represent around $3.51 million in reported holdings.

Rothschild Investment also has stakes in the Grayscale Ethereum Staking ETF worth $538,622 and the Bitwise Ethereum ETF worth $476,500.

The company also has a significant holding in the Bitwise Crypto Industry Innovators ETF, with 24,250 shares worth $615,223.

The firm owns $38,360 worth of shares in the Grayscale CoinDesk Crypto 5 ETF.

Finally, it has invested $69,900 in a Solana ETF.

Aside from cryptocurrency investment funds, Rothschild Investment has shares in publicly traded companies that are heavily involved in the digital asset space.

Michael Saylor’s Strategy is one such holding, with a $530,763 investment.

Other holdings include Coinbase Global ($86,681), Block ($125,060) and Riot Platforms ($42,980), a Bitcoin mining company.
