Mesaguamo House / Trópico Arquitectura

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Read the original version in Spanish here.

Text description provided by the architects. Located in the mountainous territory of the Cauca River canyon, a few hours from the city of Medellín in the municipality of Támesis, amidst the heat and tropical vegetation, Casa Mesaguamo is calm and slow. It seeks the tranquility of rest, of days that pass without haste. It is an open question between the construction of the intimate landscape and the contemplation of the distant landscape, developing in a succession of patios and living rooms.
