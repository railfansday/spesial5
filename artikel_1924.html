Declan Rice says England ‘knew’ they would beat World Cup holders Spain

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Declan Rice says the England squad ‘knew’ they would beat World Cup holders Spain in their final Nations League group game.

England earned top spot in their Nations League group and ended Spain’s extraordinary 43-game unbeaten run with a thrilling 3-2 win in Madrid.

Thomas Tuchel’s side scored three goals in 12 minutes either side of half-time to stun Spain before withstanding a late siege to seal their finest win under the German boss.

Harry Kane took his international tally to 93 goals with a penalty on the stroke of half-time as England deservedly ended the opening 45 minutes ahead.

Arsenal defenders Ezri Konsa and Ben White then scored twice in the space of four minutes to open up a commanding 3-0 lead over the shell-shocked Spaniards.

But Elliot Anderson’s red card on 63 minutes and Mikel Oyarzabal’s subsequent penalty offered Spain a route back into a match they looked out of.

England fans feared a collapse similar to the one the team suffered in last summer’s World Cup semi-final against Argentina when Nico Williams scored Spain’s second with 20 minutes remaining.

But this time England kept their opponents at bay with some resolute defending and two superb saves from goalkeeper James Trafford.

Rice was full of praise for his teammates after the Nations League win and said he ‘knew’ England would beat the world champions in their own backyard.

‘We knew we were going to beat them tonight,’ Rice told ITV. ‘The message from the manager after the Croatia game has been so positive.

"We knew we were going to beat them tonight!"Declan Rice after England's win over Spain in Madrid 🇪🇸🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿 pic.twitter.com/dbNiMi74od

‘Our first ten minutes were probably the best we’ve played. It was pressure, pressure, pressure – we could have had two or three.

‘If we don’t go down to ten men, we see more of the ball, we start to see them jump more, we start to see more space, and we start to see our attacking players thrive like they were doing in the second half.

‘With ten men for half an hour, winning 3-2 against that team is massive. It’s a massive win. Credit to everyone.

‘When they got the second, you might have thought that it was going to go the other way with the amount of pressure they were putting on, but that resilience.

‘Keep emptying the tank, doing anything for this badge, for this team, for our players, for the manager.’

Meanwhile, Tuchel hailed England’s ‘brilliant performance’ after they beat a side who last lost in regulation play in March 2024.

‘Brilliant performance. Well deserved win,’ he said. ‘Overall we were the better team. Fantastic performance from start to finish.

‘It was a long 30 minutes after the red card but I don’t want to focus on that. There were so many positive things in the first 60 minutes.

‘Complete performance. We brought everything to the table. We were brave. Spirit wise, energy wise and also football quality was highest level.

‘We deserved it. We focused on our performance and attitude. I can’t praise the team high enough for what they put out there.’
