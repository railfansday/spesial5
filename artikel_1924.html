Dan Ndoye and 21st percentile midfielder axed: Three Nottingham Forest players who should be dropped to pile misery on Chelsea

Nottingham Forest have the perfect chance to take advantage and pounce on an out-of-form Chelsea team at the City Ground.

Oliver Glasner's side fell to a 2-0 defeat at the hands of Bournemouth after stunning Enzo Maresca's Manchester City just a week prior.

Chelsea, on the other hand, are on a two-game leading streak, slipping up to both Sunderland and Leeds United either side of the international break.

Something just isn't clicking for Xabi Alonso and his players, and Forest could win their second meeting in a row against the Blues on Friday, 27 November.

The Reds will have the City Ground atmosphere to back them up, and it will be a tough place for Chelsea to come on a Friday night under the lights.

Glasner will need to make changes, though, so Football Insider has looked at three players who deserved to be axed based on their last appearances.

It has now been five games since Dan Ndoye scored against Crystal Palace, and he was deservedly hooked against the Cherries last weekend.

In 45 minutes, the winger created no chances, had just one shot, completed 50 per cent of his dribbles, committed three fouls and won just 22 per cent of his ground duels [FotMob].

Igor Jesus replaced him during the break, and if Glasner is to properly unleash Liam Delap, having the Brazilian as a striker partner would be perfect.

If Ryan Yates is in the starting lineup, Chelsea will overrun the midfield and completely dictate the play. Glasner cannot have that at home.

Xaver Schlager came off the bench in the last game, so his injury during the international break was clearly not as serious as first feared.

Yates may have won 67 per cent of his duels in the Premier League, but he ranks in the 55th percentile for recoveries per 90 and the 21st percentile for touches taken per 90 [FotMob].

Forest need someone who can dictate the game. Yates isn't that player.

Once again, fans were crying out for Ousmane Diomande to play in the centre of Forest's back three. And once again, they were disappointed.

Instead, Nikola Milenkovic continues to be trusted in the position, only for him to make game-altering mistakes and struggle to deal with pressures at the back.

Forest's team is trying to evolve and move on, but Glasner is holding the rest of the squad back by starting Milenkovic this season.
