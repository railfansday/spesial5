Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Opta’s new predicted League One table with Sheffield Wednesday, Barnsley, Huddersfield Town, Doncaster Rovers & Bradford City calculations

Data experts Opta have deployed their supercomputer to predict how the League One table will look at the end of the 2026/27 campaign.

The international break caused a flurry of postponements over the weekend but there was still some third-tier action to enjoy.

Sheffield Wednesday secured their first win under the stewardship of Dave Challinor, who oversaw a 3-0 win over Plymouth Argyle. Billy Mitchell fired the Owls ahead and the advantage was later built upon by Davis Keillor-Dunn and Di’Shon Bernard.

Barnsley visited the Brick Community Stadium to take on Wigan Athletic after some encouraging cup performances, but were beaten 2-1 by their fellow strugglers. Cameron McGeehan cancelled out Fraser Murray’s opener, only for Jensen Weir to grab a winner for the Latics.

Away from Yorkshire, Stockport County claimed a 3-1 win over Reading, Milton Keynes Dons hammered Burton Albion 6-2 and AFC Wimbledon edged past Blackpool.

Huddersfield Town were not in action but remain top of the League One table, while Bradford City and Doncaster Rovers sit eighth and 21st respectively.
