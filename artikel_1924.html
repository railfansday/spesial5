It caught my eye on the BBC Sport site after the Arsenal match, one of their journalists commenting on the performance of each of the Newcastle United players and giving them ratings.

The proverbial game of two halves for United and Arsenal.

Mikel Arteta’s side totally dominating the opening 45 minutes.

After the break it was Matthias Jaissle’s team that were by far the better side.

BBC Sport journalist Alex Howell giving these comments on the Newcastle United players and a rating for each.

I thought it would be interesting to compare them to The Mag’s very own Davey Hat-Trick’s ratings that were published on Sunday.

Lukas Hornicek: His kicking was slightly erratic. Nearly got caught on the ball by Kai Havertz after taking too long to release it. Could have done better with the second Arsenal goal. 5 (Davey Hat-Trick 5)

Amar Dedic: Booked after pulling back Eberechi Eze and had a tough evening against the England forward. 5 (Davey Hat-Trick 4)

Malick Thiaw: Scored the goal to put Newcastle in front, which capped an excellent performance in central defence. 8 (Davey Hat-Trick 8)

Dan Burn: Some crucial clearances in the first half and played a big part as Newcastle took the lead. 7 (Davey Hat-Trick 8)

Lewis Hall: A big error for the opening goal when he failed to deal with a clipped ball over the top. The left back redeemed himself with a crucial block to deny Noni Madueke and provided some excellent deliveries into the Arsenal box. 7 (Davey Hat-Trick 6)

Sean Steur: Showed quality on the ball and a calmness you do not often see in an 18-year-old, but taken off at half time. (Davey Hat-Trick 5)

Lewis Miley: Quality on the ball and worked well with Steur. Was the deepest of the Newcastle midfield in the second half. 7 (Davey Hat-Trick 6)

Jacob Murphy: An early shot and tried to catch David Raya off his line, but also taken off at the break after a quiet first half. 5 (Davey Hat-Trick 5)

Joe Willock: An all-action performance and his physicality was a big reason why Newcastle improved in the second half. 7 (Davey Hat-Trick 6)

Harvey Barnes: An excellent finish to bring Newcastle level and was always direct. 8 (Davey Hat-Trick 8)

William Osula: Isolated as he led the Newcastle attack but did very well to assist his side’s opening goal. 7 (Davey Hat-Trick 6.5)

Tino Livramento (on 46, Dedic): Made a huge impact after coming on at half-time. 7 (Davey Hat-Trick 7)

Anthony Elanga (on 46, Murphy): His pace meant Newcastle always had an option in behind. 6 (Davey Hat-Trick 5.5)

Jacob Ramsey (on 46, Steur): Added drive to the midfield. 7 (Davey Hat-Trick 6)

Sven Botman (on 92, Hall) (Davey Hat-Trick 5)

The BBC Sport journalist missed the fact that Yoane Wissa came on in the 83rd minute (Davey Hat-Trick 4)

Subs not used: Pope, Schar, Gonzalez, Wissa, Fernandez-Pardo.
