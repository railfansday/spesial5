Check format, resolution, frame rate, and playback, then download and inspect the finished file.

A smooth editor preview does not replace checking the downloaded file. Confirm composition and timing before choosing export settings, then play the actual output, especially its opening, joins, and ending. The free review copy uses2D Light, rather than the reference video’s satellite style.

The actual California → San Francisco Studio project: edit actions on the left, preview the map in the center, and arrange timing below. This is an example copy; blank projects do not show Change places.

Select the intended landscape or portrait format in the toolbar. After changing it, play from the beginning and inspect labels, long routes, and tall buildings near the edges. Do not export immediately after changing the ratio.

California → San Francisco project: Confirm the format before exporting; this example uses landscape 16:9.

**Check: **Every shot works in the final format.

The toolbar offers 30 and 60 fps. Review at 30 fps, then choose 60 if needed. Resolution follows the format; the export dialog shows the current pixel dimensions.

California → San Francisco project: The frame-rate menu offers 30 and 60 fps. The review copy uses 30 fps.

California → San Francisco project: The actual export dialog:1280×720,30 fps,MP4. Confirm the settings and start export.

**Check: **The review copy confirms pacing and composition, and final settings match your use.

Choose Export video, review the settings, and start. Frames are generated in the browser, so duration depends on video length, frame rate, resolution, device performance, and map loading. Keep the tab open and avoid competing heavy tasks.

California → San Francisco project: actual progress just after starting. Wait for completion or cancel this job.

**Check: **Progress advances and the interface reports that the video is ready.

Download the completed video and watch it end to end in a player. Check for black frames, missing map areas, abrupt endings, incorrect proportions, and obstructed labels. Distinguish the player background from blank areas inside the video; full-screen viewing can help.

California → San Francisco project: an actual15-second MP4 review using the free2D Light basemap, with the actual Download video link and free-plan notice.

**Check: **The downloaded file plays completely, with a correct opening, joins, and ending.

Read export errors before retrying. Fix camera conflicts in the timeline and resolve network or map loading failures. Confirm recovery with a short clip before increasing resolution.

Replace the example with a city you know, finish a short sequence, and review it end to end. Make one clear story before adding more effects.
