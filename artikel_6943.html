One Celebrity Just Got Immunity on ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Next Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

There are just seven couples left on Dancing With the Stars season 35, and the competition is fierce as we head into Grammy Night. On top of that, Julianne Hough kicked off this week’s Disney Night episode by announcing that the couple with the highest scores from the night would get immunity from next week’s dance-off!

So...who won immunity? Initially, it looked like the advantage would go to Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson, who got a 40/40 for their jazz performance to “Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious” from Mary Poppins. But then Jenna Dewan and Val Chmerkovskiy joined them at the top of the leaderboard and got a 40/40 thanks to their incredible quickstep to “Be Our Guest” from Beauty and the Beast.

So, what does the show do in the event of a tie? The couples’ scores were tallied throughout the entire season, and Jenna and Val came in at 235, while Jenna and Harry came in with 236—earning them immunity from the dance-off.

Here are the final leaderboard scores of the night (this is the individual dances and group scores together):

1. Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson: 80/801. Jenna Dewan and Val Chmerkovskiy: 80/803. Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach: 77/804. Connor Wood and Rylee Arnold: 72/805. Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov: 70/806. Tatyana Ali and Jan Ravnik: 69/808. Maura Higgins and Mark Ballas: 68/80

Julia Stiles and Ezra Sosa were tied for the number six spot with Tatyana and Jan, but ended up being eliminated.

Dancing With the Stars returns to its normal schedule next week, so tune into Grammy Night on Tuesday, November 10, at 8 p.m. ET on Disney+ and ABC!
