Iowa Governor-Elect Rob Sand Was Also a Prada Model

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

On November 3, as part of the blue-vote tsunami, Iowa elected Democratic Governor Rob Sand. The 44 year-old graduated with a political science degree from Brown University and a J.D. degree from University of Iowa College of Law; he even got a Truman scholarship and an honors thesis. And his 2026 campaign was centered on anti-corruption, bipartisanship, affordable living, and public education. But there’s more—he’s also a pretty face.

As it turns out, the governor-elect helped pay for his prestigious college degrees by walking runways in Milan and Paris. At six-feet, three inches, the young, aspiring politician modeled in shows for Giuliano Fujiwara and Number (N)ine. He even made it into a Prada runway, Newsweek reports. It was the early 2000s, and he was reportedly signed to none other than IMG Models—though his profile seems to have been taken down since.

For whatever reason, his modeling past is not something the chiseled governor-elect likes to talk about, the New York Times noted, but it sure makes for quite the resume.

On January 19, 2004, Sand even missed the Iowa caucus in Decorah because he was modeling menswear in Milan. “It had to be one or the other,” he told Newsweek. “And I chose to go to Europe.” (Who wouldn’t?) While Sand did not win that time around, look at him now, years later, up at the top.

Sand took Iowa this week while in a sharp navy-blue suit and a sky-blue checkered button-down that matched his eyes. “Thank you, Iowa! I love you, Iowa!” he excitedly said after catwalking up to the podium for his victory speech.

Rob Sand’s team has not responded to Harper’s Bazaar’s request for comment.
