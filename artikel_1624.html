Real Madrid vs. Alaves: Head-to-head record and past meetings ahead of La Liga clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

By Matt Law | 26 Nov 2026 14:18, Last updated: 26 Nov 2026 14:18

Real Madrid will be aiming to continue their excellent run of form when they welcome Alaves to Bernabeu on Saturday evening.

Los Blancos will enter the league fixture off the back of a thumping 5-1 victory over PSV Eindhoven in the Champions League.

Jose Mourinho's side have now won each of their last 10 matches in all competitions, including their last six fixtures in Spain's top flight.

Real Madrid beat Celta Vigo 2-1 in the league last time out, and they currently sit second in the La Liga table, three points behind the leaders Barcelona.

Alaves, meanwhile, have been one of the success stories in La Liga this season, picking up 16 points from 13 matches, which has left them in ninth spot in the table.

Here, Sports Mole rounds up the head-to-head record and looks at the previous meetings between the two teams ahead of their clash in the Spanish capital.

Real Madrid have tackled Alaves on 46 occasions throughout history, and unsurprisingly, the Madrid giants lead the overall head-to-head record with 38 wins to Alaves five, while there have only ever been three draws between the two teams.

Karim Benzema is the all-time leading goalscorer in this fixture, having scored nine times against Alaves during his time at Real Madrid.

Real Madrid are on 121 goals against Alaves, conceding just 34, and Los Blancos are on a nine-game winning run against the Basque side.

That said, Alaves do have a recent famous win over Real Madrid to their name, having recorded a 2-1 victory when the two teams locked horns at Bernabeu in November 2020, thanks to goals from Lucas Perez and Joselu.

Alaves have beaten Real Madrid only twice since 2000, though, while there has not been a draw between the two sides since October 2001.

Two of Alaves' five wins over Los Blancos have actually come at the Bernabeu, also triumphing 1-0 in the famous stadium back in 2000.

In La Liga, the teams have met on 40 occasions, with Real Madrid winning 34 times to Alaves' four, drawing twice in the process.
