CM: A chance to change his fate – why Estupinan faces a big night vs. Olympiacos

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Among the many sub-plots to the game between Olympiacos and AC Milan, one concerns the future of the left-back position.

As Calciomercato.com recall, Davide Bartesaghi will be forced to miss the away match due to tendinitis in his right leg. His absence is a significant one, as he was tipped for a starting spot against the Greek side, and he is one of nine players who will miss out.

Bartesaghi had always been a starter in the first four Europa League matches, though Ruben Amorim occasionally deployed him as a winger because he values ​​his attacking capabilities more than his defensive ones. In the league, he has made seven appearances so far, totalling 325 minutes.

Pervis Estupinan will replace the Italian on the left side, and it is a sliding doors moment. The former Brighton player’s last start came a month ago at San Siro against Bologna, where the Rossoneri collapsed against Raffaele Palladino’s side.

Estupinan must raise his level, and he must do so quickly before the decision to sell him in January becomes final. Having arrived for nearly €20m, he has never fully got going at Milan, even despite his winning goal in the derby against Inter last season.

Amorim took it upon himself to scupper a deal with Aston Villa last summer because he held Estupinan in high regard. The Portuguese coach believed that the Ecuadorian had the technical ability to suit his footballing philosophy.

Four months later, these beliefs have faded. Now, the Diavolo are searching for a replacement while agent Jorge Mendes is already working on possible landing spots ahead of the winter for his client Estupinan. However, in football it only takes a game to change things.
