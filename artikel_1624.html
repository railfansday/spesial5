Liverpool star angry at performance, expects 'much better'

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

One Liverpool star wasn't happy with how Sunday went against Manchester United. He expects much better.

Liverpool played Manchester United off the park on Sunday and while that's not quite the statement that it used to be, this was a fantastic day out for the Reds. Florian Wirtz and Rio Ngumoha, in particular, stole the show as United just couldn't handle either of them.

Both finished with a goal and an assist. Both created chances and openings at will - their movement on and off the ball was simply too much for United side with 11 players, let alone 10.

Dominik Szoboszlai and Victor Munoz also got on the scoresheet here. Two fantastic finishes, the former lashing in a free-kick directly after the red card. Really, it's hard to ask for more from Liverpool - and yet it really could have been more.

United were lucky in the end. Lucky that more than one Red had an off day.

Bradley Barcola had an eventful game. He actually finished it with a wonderful assist for Ngumoha's goal - one where he demonstrated how his pace and intelligence can be impossible to stop.

But in front of goal, he had a nightmare. Barcola couldn't sort his feet out on several occasions, unable to take advantage of the space he'd found in United's defence. With what should have been a fantastic opportunity to show what he can really do, the Frenchman instead looked a level behind when it came to actually scoring goals.

And that's a shame. Because Liverpool spent an awful lot of money and there's no question that Barcola is an elite player. He's just one who hasn't found his finishing in Red.

Barcola himself recognises this, too. He spoke to Canal Plus after the match, as relayed by Fabrizio Romano.

“I’m not happy with how I played," he said. "I know I can do much better and I know what I’m capable of”.

“I came away with an assist, but I could’ve scored three or four goals."

He's right, there's no question there. The important thing from a Liverpool point of view is that the player doesn't need reminding of the standards - he expects to be better than that and expects to put teams to the sword.

Which gives us hope that Barcola will eventually find that finishing at Liverpool. We know he's got the quality and we did see that in the UEFA Champions League with his hat trick against LASK. Now he needs to translate it to the Premier League.

But we believe he'll do that. This was a part demonstration of his ability and the full show is still on its way.
