Search Continues for Confidential Informant Who Vanished Over 10 Years Ago

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

A Pennsylvania woman’s disappearance remains unsolved more than 10 years after she vanished before she was set to testify as a confidential informant.

Investigators continue to search for Danielle M. Drozdowski, a Wilkes-Barre resident who went missing in 2014 at the age of 25, according to a Tuesday, November 10, news release from Pennsylvania State Police’s organization, PSP Tips.

Drozdowski “was a confidential informant who was scheduled to testify before a grand jury in August of 2014, however she failed to appear and was subsequently entered as ‘wanted,’” state police said.

Authorities are offering a $5,000 reward for information solving her disappearance. She would be 37 years old today.

Family and friends haven’t seen or heard from Drozdowski since March 19, 2014, the last time she shared a post to Facebook, according to police.

“Her last known location and what she was last wearing are unknown,” police said.

Drozdowski is white, has blue eyes and is described as around 5-foot-2 or 5-foot-3, and weighing around 120 to 165 pounds by the National Missing and Unidentified Persons System (NamUs).

She also has several tattoos, including on both of her hands, her left arm and the back of her neck.

She has the outline of an “Om” symbol tattooed on her neck, according to NamUS. On her right hand, she has a tattoo of a possible shamrock. On her left arm, she has a multi-color skull tattoo and flowers.

She also has a tattoo of an anchor on her left hand.

A woman who commented under PSP Tips’ Facebook news release wrote Drozdowski was her former best friend.

“She was my best friend at one point in time in school, played on the basketball team together did everything together she didn’t have an easy life growing up but she was trying too make the best of it,” the woman, Samantha Lynn, wrote in the comment left under the post.

“I hope and pray she is out there some where and is okay her friends & family miss her terribly,” the comment also said.

Anyone with information on Drozdowski is asked to call Pennsylvania State Police’s Wilkes-Barre station at 570-821-4110 or PSP Tips Toll Free at 1-800-472-8477. Tips can also be shared online.

The news release stated, “All callers to PSP Tips could be eligible for a CASH REWARD for information that leads to an arrest, the solving of a crime/cold case or the location of a wanted person/fugitive or missing person.”
