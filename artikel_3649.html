Rosie O'Donnell Shares Photo of 'Creepy' Figurine of Herself With Grandchild

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Rosie O’Donnell is creating new family keepsakes, including a lifelike figurine of herself holding her grandchild.

“OK, I got this little figurine made online from a photo … me [and my grandson] Anthony. [My son] Blakey thinks it’s creepy … thoughts?” O’Donnell, 64, captioned a Sunday, November 15, Instagram photo that showed the figurine immortalizing a moment spent with 7-month-old Anthony, the firstborn child of Rosie’s son Blake O’Donnell, 26, and his wife, Teresa.

The questionable figurine, snapped on a carpeted floor, showed Rosie dressed in a black T-shirt and pants while holding her grandson across her chest. While Rosie was depicted with a smile on her face, Anthony was reimagined with an intense stare as he locked eyes with the camera.

Fans offered mixed reactions to the former talk show host’s question. Some thought the figurine was “cute” and “awesome” while others weren’t so approving. “Yes, it’s a little creepy. I thought it was an AI picture at first. It can be like an Elf on the Shelf,” wrote one fan, while another commented, “Love you Rosie, but yeah, it’s pretty creepy.”

Other social media users jokingly compared the creation to a candle “that hopefully you can melt,” as well as potential “annual Halloween decor” should Rosie end up deciding she doesn’t like it.

Rosie shared her adoration for little Anthony, who is her fifth grandchild, in April after meeting him for the first time. “Best day ever,” Rosie captioned a post that captured her cradling the newborn at the time. “Met my grandson Anthony – truly a beautiful child – I’m so proud of the happy parents, Teresa and Blake #nana.”

She later posted a snap of two of her children, Vivienne and Clay, also cuddling up with Anthony. (Rosie shares Parker, 31, Chelsea, 29, Blake and Vivienne, 23, with ex-wife Kelli Carpenter. She is also mom to Clay, 13, whom she shared with her late ex-wife, Michelle Rounds.)

“Thrilled to have Vivi and Clay with me as we all meet the new baby #blakeandteresa #anthonyjoel #odonnell,” Rosie captioned her follow-up post.

In June, Rosie spoke to People about the joy she experiences in watching her children raise children of their own. Referencing Blake specifically, she said, “To see my son as a parent and to remember him as a baby … it is absolutely … I can’t stop crying.”

She continued at the time, “[Blake] will say to me, ‘Mommy, I don’t want you to be upset, but the baby’s really into me, and you might not get to hold him that much.’ First I was p***ed, and then I thought, ‘Wait a minute, that’s a beautiful thing.”
