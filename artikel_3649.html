GTA 6 Dog Who Saves You Is Seemingly Based On A Very Cool Real-Life Dog

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

One of the first cool things that people discovered in Grand Theft Auto 6 is that if you swim out into the ocean too far, you'll find a dog riding a jetski who coaxes you back to shore.

As it turns out, the dog looks to be based on a real-life dog named Loosy who does neat stunts in Florida. In real life, Loosy is a golden retriever whose owner, Eddie Inch, gained viral fame when he rode a jet-propelled hoverboard in Fort Lauderdale as he towed a goggle-wearing Loosy behind him on a jetski.

It seems Rockstar took inspiration from this with GTA 6's own jetski-riding dog. However, GTA 6's dog is a different breed, but it still has goggles like Loosy.

Neither Loosy nor Inch are listed in GTA 6's credits, but it seems pretty obvious that this is a reference to the real-world viral dog.

GTA 6 launched today, November 19, for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The game is getting rave reviews, including a 10/10 from GameSpot. According to Sensor Tower, GTA 6 has sold more than 19 million copies and generated close to $2 billion already.
