PS5 Now the Only Console without a Digital Game Gifting Option

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

If even a Nintendo platform has a digital storefront feature and you don't, then you truly are behind the times. The firm notoriously playing catch-up in this space has today confirmed the Nintendo eShop will be receiving the ability to gift digital games.

The PS Store, which will eventually be the only place you can buy new PlayStation games, still doesn't allow it.

Having downloaded a new system update for the Nintendo Switch 2, the groundwork is laid for a Nintendo eShop gifting feature going live next week. You will be able to gift digital games to people within your Nintendo Account family group.

This implementation is a bit more restrictive than what's on Xbox and Steam, where you can gift digital games to anyone on your friends list. The Nintendo Account family group system, meanwhile, is restricted to a maximum of eight members that all share a single online membership to NSO (Nintendo Switch Online).

Despite the Nintendo approach being less expansive than other storefronts, it's better than nothing.

And nothing is all Sony offers in the digital game gifting space.

Feature 8 Ways Sony Must Improve the PS Store for Our All-Digital Future

From January 2028, PlayStation is going entirely digital with game releases, as it's revealed the production of physical copies for new titles will end. As it leans all the way into the digital space, it makes the PS Store the only place you can buy new PS5 (and potentially PS6 by that point) games.

This has put the PS Store under the microscope, as players now judge the only storefront they'll be able to access new PlayStation releases from. The fact that, as of right now, they wouldn't be able to gift games to their family and friends, is an issue. And now, if the Nintendo Switch 2 can support the feature, there are absolutely no excuses Sony can turn to.

It's one improvement we highlighted in a recent feature that PlayStation really should implement. Other enhancements included a better refund policy, the ability to trade and sell digital games, and a loyalty program.

Currently, the only way you could send a PS5, PS4 game to a family member or friend is by buying them PSN vouchers to redeem. By choosing to focus entirely on digital in the future, Sony must implement a gifting feature where the full process is done entirely on the console.
