Manchester United given Unai Emery approval amid new Michael Carrick sack decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Manchester United have been told why Unai Emery is the clear “first-choice” manager target to replace Michael Carrick.

The current Man Utd head coach remains under significant pressure ahead of a crucial double-header against Roma, in the Champions League on Tuesday, and Emery’s Aston Villa, in the Premier League on Sunday.

According to widespread reports, Man Utd chiefs will assess Carrick’s ongoing employment following those two games, and Emery is being suggested as the perfect replacement.

Emery has established himself as one of the Premier League's outstanding coaches through his work at Aston Villa, despite operating with more restricted resources than some of his rivals.

Emery has transformed Villa into a consistent force and Richard Dunne feels Man Utd should make him their priority, even if persuading him to leave Villa Park proves difficult.

"Unai Emery has to be right at the top of the shortlist for Man United," Dunne told Snabbare.

"He's proven what he can do at Aston Villa with restricted funds, making his signings work brilliantly. The success he's had with Villa is as impressive as anyone's in the league.

"Man United should look at him as their number one target, even if it ends in rejection."

Dunne admits he would be surprised if Emery accepted an offer from Old Trafford, given the authority and control he enjoys at Villa.

However, he believes United's managerial uncertainty reflects wider problems at the club, with the ownership, recruitment strategy and executive leadership all requiring scrutiny.

United have appointed highly regarded managers and assembled squads containing outstanding individual talent, but have struggled to restore the consistency and success expected of them.

"You have to look above the pitch at the boardroom," Dunne added.

"Ownership is a long-term problem, and recruitment and executive leadership don't seem aligned. Man United have deeper problems than just the manager.”
