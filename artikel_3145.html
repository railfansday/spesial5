Poland’s defence ministry to sign AI software contract with Palantir

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Polish defence minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz during talks at the Pentagon in Washington, D.C., in May 2025

Poland’s defence ministry will sign a contract with Palantir Technologies on Friday, and it will buy software that uses AI to help the armed forces make decisions, Reuters reported, citing a ministry statement.

Defence minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz will attend the signing at noon at the Polish Army Museum in Warsaw’s Citadel, according to the ministry’s invitation, which PAP Biznes reported. The contract is with Palantir’s Polish subsidiary and covers the supply of software for an AI decision-support system.

The ministry did not say how much the contract is worth or which units will use the software. While, Palantir has not yet commented publicly on the deal or its terms.

The contract is the first binding step in a partnership that began with a cybersecurity agreement in December 2024. In October 2025, Kosiniak-Kamysz and Palantir chief executive Alex Karp signed a letter of intent covering AI, IT and cybersecurity.

At that signing, Kosiniak-Kamysz described the testing that preceded the deal, but the process began in December 2023, he said, and included dozens of meetings, exercises and system demonstrations across all branches of the armed forces.

The ombudsman, Sylwia Gregorczyk-Abram, then wrote to the minister in October about the group’s concerns. In early November, Kosiniak-Kamysz told broadcaster RMF FM that he was waiting for the military to finish its analysis of the partnership.

Negotiations with Palantir have been led by Poland’s Cyberspace Defence Forces Component Command. The minister has defended the choice by pointing out that NATO and Ukraine’s armed forces already use the company’s software.

Poland is moving in the opposite direction to some of its allies, as France and Germany have pledged to build a European Palantir rival, and the Netherlands wants a full alternative to the company’s software within two years.

Warsaw has also been deepening defence technology ties with Washington, and earlier this week, Poland and the US signed an agreement at the Pentagon on joint defence research and development, ending 17 years of negotiations.
