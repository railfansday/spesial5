Under Armour: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Monthly global product launches are replacing scattered regional marketing, a shift that tests whether full-price sell-through can rebuild while direct-to-consumer sales stay under pressure.

On its second-quarter fiscal 2027 earnings call, held on Nov. 6, 2026, Under Armour (UAA -5.21%) announced that revenue declined 7% to $1.2 billion while reiterating its full-year operating outlook. Management is reducing operating complexity and cutting low-volume inventory to protect profitability. Three things management said stand out.

Management is trimming its product assortment to eliminate low-performing inventory. Reducing stock-keeping units (SKUs, or individual product codes) allows the business to lower costs and sell more goods without discounting.

Under Armour is moving its youth clothing line to a licensing partner, Centric Brands, to trim inventory exposure. This asset-light approach helps preserve cash while internal teams focus on core performance apparel.

Management is centralizing marketing under one global voice and launching one primary product story each month. Aligning regional teams behind unified releases aims to boost marketing efficiency and drive full-price sell-through.

Direct-to-consumer sales remain under pressure, but expense discipline is insulating operating margins. Under Armour relies on core strengths in football cleats and established apparel lines to support pricing discipline.

Long-term value depends on converting product innovation into full-price retail demand across global markets.
