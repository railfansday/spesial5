LiberNovo’s Black Friday sale takes up to 42% off ergonomic chairs

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

You can spend plenty of time getting your Mac, display, and desk just right. But what about the chair you use every day? Between typing, taking video calls, and leaning back to watch something, your position changes throughout the day. Your chair should be able to keep up.

That’s the idea behind LiberNovo’s ergonomic chairs, which combine support that moves with you and a choice of sizes, including the Maxis range for bigger and taller users. For Black Friday, LiberNovo is taking up to 42% off in the U.S., with chairs starting at $609 and offers running November 19–30.

Adjusting a chair for your desk is one thing. Keeping that support as you change position is another. LiberNovo’s Dynamic Support System links the neck support, backrest, armrests, and seat so they move together as you recline.

The Bionic FlexFit Backrest adds eight flexible panels that contour independently. Rather than relying on a single rigid backrest, the design lets different sections follow your back as you shift or turn. Once you’ve adjusted the chair to fit, the system is designed to maintain contact through those movements with fewer manual readjustments.

Both the Omni and Maxis families offer five recline positions: 105, 115, 125, 135, and 160 degrees. That gives you options for desk work, a more relaxed position for watching a video, and a deeper recline for a break.

A higher weight limit is only part of finding a chair that fits. Taller users also need enough seat depth, a headrest that reaches the right spot, and armrests with room to adjust.

The LiberNovo Maxis range addresses those details with a 52 cm seat depth, a wider backrest with a flared base, and an extended-travel headrest with 140 mm of vertical adjustment and 120 mm of front-to-back adjustment. Its armrests sit lower and offer a wider, longer adjustment range to accommodate larger builds.

LiberNovo recommends Maxis for users approximately 5 feet 10 inches to 6 feet 7 inches tall, with a maximum supported weight of 399 pounds. Maxis is certified to BIFMA X5.11. The seat cushion uses firmer support at the rear and a softer front edge, while a six-spring Controlled Recline System is designed to build resistance progressively as you lean back.

For a shallower seat, the Omni family offers 45 cm and 48 cm configurations. Choosing between the two families starts with fit, then comes down to which features you want.

There are three feature tiers in each family. Omni SE and Maxis Manual offer manually adjusted lumbar support, without a motor or battery. Both retain the flexible backrest, dynamic support, and five recline positions.

The Maxis Electric includes motorized lumbar adjustment and OmniStretch, a five-minute powered stretch sequence used in deep recline.

Omni Pro and Maxis Airflow add Active AirFlow seat ventilation. A fan draws air through the cushion to carry heat away, and an occupancy sensor pauses the fan when you stand up. These models also include a larger 3,000 mAh rechargeable battery.

LiberNovo’s warranty covers the frame and structural components for six years, and electronic components for two years.
