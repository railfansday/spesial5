Threads teases iPad version of its app

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

Head of Design Christopher Clare posted multiple screenshots showing a Threads feed on a larger tablet screen, but didn’t reveal a release date.

Threads has shared a preview of the iPad version of the app, which will present a bigger version of the feed, aligned to the larger iPad screen.

As shown in this image, first posted by Threads Head of Design Christopher Clare, the iPad version of Threads offers more space to scroll and read posts on-screen.

That will be a welcome update for iPad users, who are currently navigating Threads via a browser interface, or using the iPhone version of the app on their larger screens.

Threads has been working to provide better usage experiences on different platforms, and the desktop version of the app has gone through multiple updates to refine the experience and utilize the extra screen space.

Though it is interesting that Threads is looking to get an iPad version into circulation so fast in comparison to Meta’s other apps. Meta launched a dedicated iPad version of Instagram last year after years of user requests.

Presumably, that saw a good response, which is why Meta is taking iPad versions more seriously this time around.

Clare said that the iPad version of Threads is “coming very soon” but did not provide a launch date.
