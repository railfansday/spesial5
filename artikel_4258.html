Gigabash Mobile brings arena-brawling action to phones with your favourite giant monsters

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Ever since Godzilla was stomping around Tokyo like a big playground, one question has been on everyone's mind: Let them fight. Alright, that's a statement, not a question, but my point stands. Big monsters are cool, so big monster versus big monster is even cooler, and that simple equation is exactly what Passion Republic Games' GigaBash Mobile is the solution to!

In GigaBash Mobile, you're dropped into the open arena of a city, and made to fight against your fellow giant monsters to either save or doom humanity. That means duking it out between fully destructible environments, unleashing devastating attacks and generally laying waste to everything around you.

Oh, and alongside them? You'll also be able to purchase DLC that brings virtually every familiar kaiju and their foe to GigaBash! Ranging from Ultraman to Gamera, and yes, even Godzilla himself! All of them can step into the arena and duke it out in ways fans of the past could only dream of.

GigaBash Mobile also comes packed with its own story and arcade modes, as well as a whole host more to sink your skyscraper-sized fangs into. So keep your eyes peeled for it when it crashes down on the outskirts of Android this November 10th!
