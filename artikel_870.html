Scotland edge out Australia in Nations Championship

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Scotland beat Australia 15-12 but couldn't secure a bonus point; Head coach Gregor Townsend said: "Defensively, that's one of the best performances I've seen in the last couple of years"; Scotland move above Wales into fourth in the Northern Hemisphere table

Scotland eked out a hard-fought 15-12 win over Australia but were unable to get the bonus point they craved in a Nations Championship match that failed to catch fire.

First-half scores from Matt Fagerson and Jack Dempsey gave the Scots the edge at the break, with Harry Potter responding for the Wallabies.

Finn Russell's late penalty eventually gave the Scots some breathing space before Max Jorgensen's 80th-minute try offered the visitors a glimmer of hope but the Scots held on for a win that elevated them above Wales into fourth place in the Northern Hemisphere table.

"When we saw the rain half an hour before kick off, you still hope you'll be able to move the ball. You could tell defences were coming off the line a bit quicker, knowing they could pressure the ball carrier," Scotland head coach Gregor Townsend told ITV,

"Both teams had to adjust and I thought we did that well. Defensively, that's one of the best performances I've seen in the last couple of years. Set after set we were putting them under pressure.

"We were on that edge of physicality but disciplined, hitting hard in the tackle, putting pressure on, listening to the referee, getting back onside. That makes it hard for the opposition to get into our 22m, which was a learning from last week.

"It was just constant effort. Today, we showed we can play another game, too."
