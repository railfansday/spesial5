Harry Kane continues to break records and on Thursday against Croatia in the Nations League he will make history once again, as he’s set to become England men’s all-time record appearance holder, breaking the record held by Peter Shilton since 1989. Football correspondent John Murray and senior football reporter Ian Dennis catch up with the England captain in camp and take a trip down memory lane to reminisce about Kane’s first England cap, breaking records and also winning the Ballon d’Or. Not only that, England head coach Thomas Tuchel joins the pod and gives John a squad update, chats about the call up of James Hill and how the team will celebrate Kane’s achievements.
