CMA Awards 2026: See the Complete Winners List (Live Updates)

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Heading into the 2026 CMA Awards, Ella Langley, Miranda Lambert and more led the pack of nominations. See which artists ended up strutting away with a win.

Some artists are putting down their guitars—but only to pick up their new trophies.

The 2026 CMA Awards, hosted by Lainey Wilson at Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena for the third time in a row Nov. 18, had quite a few musicians who racked up multiple nominations going into country music's biggest night.

Take for instance, Ella Langley, who led the pack with nine nods this year, including earning one for Entertainer of the Year and another for Album of the Year for Dandelion. But that's not all there is to choose from, of course, seeing as how she earned not one but two nominations for Single of the Year in recognition of her songs “Be Her” and “Choosin’ Texas.”

As for her competition in that particular category, Taylor Swift's Toy Story 5 single “I Knew It, I Knew You" is also saying howdy to the list, as well as Wilson's “Somewhere Over Laredo," and rounding out the category would be Cody Johnson's hit "The Fall."

For Swift, not only would this year's CMAs mark as the 26th nomination she's gotten at the awards show, but it's special for another reason entirely. This is also the first time the "Wildest Dreams" singer has returned to perform in 13 years.

But she isn't the only one seeing the starlight, of course, as following Langley close behind in number of nominations was Miranda Lambert, who earned seven nods including one for Female Vocalist of the Year. After Lambert, Kacey Musgraves and Ben West found themselves in the right direction, earning five nominations apiece.
