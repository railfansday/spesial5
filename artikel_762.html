Selena Gomez Helps Couple With Baby’s Sex Reveal at Spurs Game, ‘With No Hesitation’

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The star opened the envelope and announced whether the baby will be born male or female at Saturday night's game in San Antonio, Texas.

Selena Gomez happily announced whether a boy or girl is to be expected for a pregnant fan she ran into at the Spurs-Pelicans basketball game this weekend in San Antonio.

A short video of the precious moment from Saturday night’s (Nov. 21) NBA match between San Antonio and New Orleans started popping up across social media on Sunday. Filmed at the Texas city’s Frost Bank Center, the footage was originally posted online by the expectant couple around midnight local time. The clip shows Gomez opening a sealed envelope containing the big reveal.

Carefully scanning over the paper that listed the sex assigned to the baby, a smiling Gomez slowly said, “You’re … How do I know? … FEMALE!” Then she gave the mom and dad a big hug.

“I cannot put into words just how special this night was!” wrote game attendee/mom @laurenicoleramz on Instagram. “We are expecting baby #4 so soon, already starting the third trimester & planning to wait until birth for our last to find out our gender. We decided to bring our envelope in case we could get someone to open it for us, & the sweetest @selenagomez did that with no hesitation!!”

Captioning the snapshot and personal video clip with Gomez (seen here), she shared: “She was so sweet & it felt so natural just to talk to her because of her gentle & kind personality. Thank you @selenagomez & @spurs for making this a night to remember!! Swipe to see/hear what baby #4 is!! So thankful to God for this sweet baby that will complete our family!!”

Gomez also posed for a picture during their meeting, resting her hand on the mama’s belly.

The star appeared to be in good spirits at the game, where she was also gifted a jersey by the San Antonio Spurs Coyote, the team’s official mascot.

The Spurs defeated the Pelicans 114-91. When Spurs forward Victor Wembanyama was asked about Gomez’s appearance watching the game Saturday night, he quipped, “I had to show off for Selena, you know.”

Gomez’s new trailer for season six of Only Murders in the Building — this season with an Only Murders in London theme — co-starring Steve Martin and Martin Short, arrived last week. The hit Hulu crime comedy returns with its season premiere on Dec. 8.
