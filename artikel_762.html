Bachelor's Peter Weber Announces Engagement to Jadeyn Carr

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Bachelor alum Peter Weber confirmed that he and Jadeyn Carr, with whom he’s expecting twins, are engaged.

Peter Weber has given away his final rose.

Two months after the Bachelor alum shared that he is expecting twins with Jadeyn Carr—whom he hard-launched in September—he announced that they are engaged.

Alongside a snap of the model showing off her oval-cut diamond ring in a Nov. 11 Instagram post, Weber wrote, "Marry Me @jaydencarr."

Meanwhile, Carr reacted to the news on her Instagram Stories, adding, "my forever!!!"

And it was a Bachelor Nation reunion in the comments of the announcement. In fact, fellow Bachelor alum Adam Gottschalk revealed that wife Raven Gottschalk—whom he met on season four of Bachelor in Paradise—had an inkling that wedding bells were in the near distance.

"Dang it, I lost a bet with Raven that you were gonna propose on a plane," he admitted with two laughing emojis. "Congrats bro! You guys are gonna be amazing parents."

Bachelor Nation star Tyler Cameron also congratulated him, writing, "Nicely done sir!!"

Life has been coming up roses for Weber, with the couple announcing Carr's pregnancy in September. The 35-year-old (also known as Pilot Pete) captioned a photo booth strip, in which he was kissing his love's growing bump, "1 + 1 = 4."

But the commercial airline pilot's love life wasn't always a fairy tale. He initially got down on one knee for Hannah Ann Sluss in 2020's season 24, though they broke off their engagement before the finale. He also briefly dated runner-up Madison Prewett, with whom he split from two days after the final episode aired.

He eventually found love with former contestant Kelley Flanagan for seven months, until December 2020, before picking up their romance again from August 2022 to April 2023. Though Weber's past relationships didn't work out, the reality star didn't lose hope about finding his person.

"You can't ever force it, but I have faith in due time that'll work out," he told People in February 2025. "I never lost faith, so I know it'll happen soon, and I'm just enjoying the journey along the way."

As for what kind of woman Weber was looking for? He added, "Just someone that I can truly just be myself with and fully let go and relax with and have that sense of peace with."
