Alec Baldwin & Hilaria Baldwin Joke Gaslighting Is the Key to Marriage

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Alec Baldwin, 68, and Hilaria Baldwin, 42—parents to seven kids together—joked that gaslighting and a good sense of humor are the secret behind their marriage's success.

After 14 years of marriage, Hilaria Baldwin is still Alec Baldwin's rock.

That's why the 30 Rock star and the yoga instructor don't mind teasing each other over the small stuff as they raise their seven kids, Carmen, 13, Rafael, 11, Leonardo, 10, Romeo, 8, Eduardo, 6, María, 5 and Ilaria, 4. In fact, the Baldwins shared why some playful ribbing is the secret to their relationship.

"Gaslighting, but banter," Hilaria told Us Weekly in an interview published Nov. 18. "And then we’re both not sure what’s real and not real anymore, but we both do it to each other."

One harmless example of how they flip the script at home? Hilaria often tries to convince her husband that her frequent redecorating was his idea.

"Every day when he leaves, if I don’t have to work, I come home [and decorate]," she recounted. "I tell him it was his idea, and he’s like, 'Wait a minute.'"

After admitting she eventually persuades the Beetlejuice actor not to worry about the rearranged furniture, she joked, "See, we can both gaslight each other."

And it's that sense of humor that has kept their relationship going strong after the couple's meet-cute in 2011 at a New York City restaurant.

The 42-year-old added of their banter, "It keeps us both getting old very fast and young, both the things at the same time." And Alec, also dad to Ireland Baldwin, 31, with ex Kim Basinger, agreed that their playful rapport is a highlight of the relationship.

"A lot of gaslighting," he teased about rolling with Hilaria's quirks, and vice versa. "The key to a happy marriage is a lot of gaslighting."

And given their sweet bond, it's no surprise Alec's eldest is a big fan of her stepmom.

"She is eccentric and totally bat s--t crazy (in a fun way) but she saved my dad’s life," Ireland wrote in a 2025 post. "She turned his health around and has shown him the forgiveness and kindness that he needed."

And while fans of the Campus Caller actress questioned how having seven younger siblings would impact her relationship with her dad, Ireland gushed about her large family unit.

As she put it, Hilaria "is the reason I get to have siblings/a big family that I’ve always wanted."

Read on for a look inside life with the Baldwins.

Alec Baldwin told the New York Times he gave his future wife, then Hilaria Thomas, his number upon meeting her at Pure Food and Wine in New York in February 2011. The two played phone tag until they finally arranged a time to meet for dinner.

That August they enjoyed their first US Open together, one of Alec's favorite events.

Alec looked into his future and saw Hilaria at the 10th Annual Elton John AIDS Foundation's "An Enduring Vision" benefit at Cipriani Wall Street in October 2011. They moved in together the following month.

Hilaria accompanied Alec to the Cannes Film Festival in 2012. "I've dated a lot of people since my divorce, and have thought a lot about what I wanted and what I wanted to do," he said. "I thought about it too much, I suppose. I wasn't really ready to take that chance and to act until I met Hilaria."

In April 2012, Alec's rep confirmed to E! News that he and Hilaria were engaged.

Hilaria and Ireland Baldwin frequently tweet about their friendship. Ireland joked on the social networking site she was going to serve as a "flower girl" in the wedding, but actually was a bridesmaid.

On June 30, 2012, the happy couple tied the knot in front of family and friends at the Basilica of St. Patrick's Old Cathedral in Manhattan. The bride wore a silk magnolia trumpet Amsale gown and the groom donned a tux from Tom Ford.

The couple are now parents of seven, welcoming Carmen Gabriela (Aug. 23, 2013), Rafael Thomas (June 17, 2015), Leonardo Ángel Charles (Sept. 12, 2016), Romeo Alejandro David (May 17, 2018), Eduardo "Edu" Pao Lucas (Sept. 8, 2020), María Lucía Victoria (Feb. 25, 2021, via surrogate) and Ilaria Catalina Irena (Sept. 22, 2022).

The whole family will be starring on TLC's The Baldwins, premiering Feb. 23, 2025.
