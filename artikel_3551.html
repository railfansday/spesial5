What Mariah Carey and Nick Cannon's Twins Have Said About Their Blended Family

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Mariah Carey and Nick Cannon’s twins, Moroccan and Monroe, aren’t shy about sharing their thoughts on their massive blended family — and their dad has heard them loud and clear.

The teenagers, who are two of Cannon’s 12 children with six women, made headlines more than once for candid remarks about their father’s other kids. Cannon has publicly addressed their comments, offering context and reflecting on his large brood.

In addition to sharing Moroccan and Monroe with Carey, Cannon is a dad to Golden Sagon, Powerful Queen and Rise Messiah with Brittany Bell; Zion, Zillion and Beautiful Zeppelin with Abby De La Rosa; Legendary Love with Bre Tiesi; Onyx Ice with LaNisha Cole; and Halo and Zen with Alyssa Scott. Zen died at 5 months old in December 2021 following a battle with brain cancer.

In November 2025, Monroe took to her Instagram Stories to set the record straight about her siblings.

“Clearing something up guys, I only have ONE brother,” Monroe wrote, tagging her twin’s account.

She did acknowledge her extended family, adding that she has “other half-siblings from [her] dad.” But, she noted, “they are all many years younger than me.”

The post quickly went viral, with many online readers interpreting it as Monroe distancing herself from her half-siblings. The reaction, however, missed the point the teen was trying to make.

Months later, Cannon addressed the viral moment during an appearance on the “Club Shay Shay” podcast, telling host Shannon Sharpe that the online chatter completely misread his daughter’s intent.

“It’s so funny because the world thought that it was about a conversation that was something that it wasn’t,” Cannon said. “It was really about 14-year-olds talking.”

He explained that Monroe was referring to the age gap between herself and her younger half-siblings, not denying their existence.

“So my daughter wanted to clear it up, and be like, ‘I only have one brother,’ like literally in her age group,” he said.

Cannon added that Monroe was “upset” over how her words were interpreted.

“The world jumped in on some teenage gossip conversation and tried to turn it into something else,” he shared. “And then that upset my daughter. So she was like, ‘I didn’t mean that. I said my dad has other kids.’”

The Masked Singer host said he used the moment as a teaching opportunity.

“I was like, ‘Monroe, you don’t have to explain yourself to anybody,’” he recalled. “‘But let this be a lesson that you got to be very careful what you say on social media. People will take something very small and blow it up into something that you don’t have control over anymore.’”

Fast-forward to November 2026, when Netflix released the trailer for Raising the Cannons — and Moroccan and Monroe held nothing back about their parents’ split and their blended family.

“Maybe if you didn’t have all these kids, you would have been back together,” Monroe told her dad while sitting in a car with him in one scene.

Moroccan chimed in later with his own blunt assessment: “This lifestyle, I don’t think it’s sustainable in any way.”

Cannon, for his part, didn’t shy away from the subject. In the trailer, he admitted that his “ego” drove him away from Carey.

Despite the complicated family tree, Carey and Cannon have built a famously amicable coparenting relationship. Cannon exclusively told Us in September 2026 that he and his ex-wife talk “almost every day.”

“We were able to, kind of, move on and be amicable and be the best parents we can,” Cannon said. “And, like, to this day, man, we talk almost every day or every other day or, you know, just specifically just about the kids.”
