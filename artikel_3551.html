Mandy is one of the first NPCs players run into in Grand Theft Auto 6. Jason is sent to collect rent from her and she tries to come onto him despite his Mr. Freeze levels of cold disinterest. At one point he’s trying to unscrew the bolt for the sink trap when she comes back wearing nothing but a pink-trimmed leopard-print string bikini. Clips of the early encounter quickly went viral. Actor Lexie Shoaibi, who voices Mandy, said the instant internet fame has been “bittersweet.”

Like many video game characters, Mandy is modeled after one person but voiced by another. The model is Miami-based influencer Marissa DuBois. The voice belongs to Shoaibi, an actor and model who also promotes herself through Meta’s engagement-driven algorithm. She told Vulture that she auditioned for the role back around 2023, but it was never explicitly confirmed to her that it was for GTA 6.

The context-less gig came with tons of NDAs, but Shoaibi found the performance part surprisingly inviting. She performed opposite Jason’s actor, Dylan Rourke, and said the whole experience felt a bit like an “off-Broadway theater production.” It wasn’t until years later, when she saw clips of streamers playing the game on November 18, that she realized what she’d appeared in and got to see the finished performance.
