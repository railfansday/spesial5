Death of Former Baseball Star and Actor Lance Broadway Takes Bizarre Turn

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The mystery behind the sudden death of former Major League Baseball star Lance Broadway has deepened thanks to a new report.

Broadway’s death has been listed as “unexplained,” according to a Dallas Police Department incident report obtained by the California Post on Tuesday, November 24.

There are no signs that Broadway was under the influence of drugs or alcohol when his body was found in the stairwell of a Dallas apartment complex on November 17. He was 43.

Police were called to the scene at 5:56 p.m., according to the report, and Broadway was formally pronounced dead at 6:20 p.m., per the Dallas County Medical Examiner.

Broadway’s cause of death remains undetermined, and results of his autopsy are still pending.

A first-round pick of the Chicago White Sox in the 2005 MLB Draft, Broadway played three seasons with the White Sox and New York Mets.

After retiring from baseball, Broadway found a career as an actor, appearing in the 2013 movie Olympus Has Fallen and TV shows like Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D., Lucifer and Colony.

More recently, Broadway worked as a coach for Canes Southwest, a youth baseball organization.

“For the past five years, our Canes baseball families had the privilege of experiencing firsthand the passion, intensity and unwavering commitment that defined Lance Broadway,” the organization said in a statement after his death. “There was no halfway with Lance. He was all in, all the time. His energy was unmatched, his expectations were high, and his work ethic was something he not only demanded but demonstrated.”

The statement continued, “If you played for Lance Broadway, you were going to learn what commitment truly looked like. But his investment in our boys extended well beyond the baseball diamond. He showed up at their high school games, followed their seasons, knew their statistics and made phone calls to talk through their performances. He challenged them to think critically, take ownership and understand the game at a deeper level.”

The mother of a Canes Southwest player shared via Facebook, “I don’t know that I’ll ever be able to put into words what Lance Broadway meant to our son or our family.”

“I’m not even sure one post could begin to do it justice,” Stephanie Webb wrote on Thursday, November 19. “But there’s a part of his story that I desperately want people to know. Over the last couple of days, I’ve seen the headlines. His Major League Baseball career. His time in Hollywood. The incredible things he accomplished throughout his life.But there’s a chapter of Lance’s life that won’t make many headlines. And in my eyes, it may have been one of his greatest accomplishments.”

She added, “The years he poured his heart and soul into a group of boys during some of the most formative years of their lives.”
