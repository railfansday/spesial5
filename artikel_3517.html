Bachelor Nation's Hannah Godwin Addresses 'Boob Job Allegations'

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Bachelor Nation’s Hannah Godwin is poking fun at online speculation she underwent a previous breast enhancement surgery.

“So I got my girlies done for Christmas. No, I’m kidding,” Godwin, 31, said via Instagram on Wednesday, November 18. “But I did get boob job allegations, and it’s because of my new bra. This is the smooth lace trim push-up bra from Victoria’s Secret. She’s perfect.”

While filming a video at her house, the former Bachelor in Paradise star gave credit to the bra making her feel cute and confident.

“Obviously she’s super flattering and gives me a little lift, but I also love how smooth it is under different fabrics, including these PJs, and you know I’m the biggest collector of Victoria’s Secret pajamas,” she explained. “It’s, like, my favorite thing and I’ve been waiting to bust these out for holiday movies.”

Shortly after sharing her video, Godwin received plenty of fun comments under her post.

“Ur girlies are looking fab,” Tessa Brooks wrote. Victoria’s Secret added, “Never  beating the allegations with this bra 🤭.”

Godwin’s own husband, Dylan Barbour, had some fun with the video too, commenting, “Hannah!”

The former Bachelor star has been open and honest about any plastic surgery work she has — or hasn’t — done.

In May 2024, Godwin confirmed she got the last of her lip filler dissolved “because some had migrated above my lip (very normal), which doesn’t look super natural.”

In 2020, the content creator also addressed plastic surgery rumors while answering fans’ assumptions about her life.

“I mean yeah, I’ve had my lips done before. It’s not a giant huge secret,” she said via YouTube. “I’ve had my lips done a few times. Literally the smallest amount ever. I have not had anything else done.”

Plastic surgery aside, Godwin continues to live her best life with Barbour, 32, after the pair met in June 2019 while filming season 6 of Bachelor in Paradise in Mexico.

Earlier this year, Netflix announced Godwin would be appearing in the upcoming reality show Squid Game: The VIP Challenge. The Bachelor Nation member will compete alongside real estate mogul Ryan Serhant, NBA champion Tristan Thompson, Spice Girls icon Mel B, Real Housewives of Atlanta’s Kim Zolciak, Dylan Efron and more familiar faces.

“I would say it was a wild ride for sure, and I think it’s going to be a very, very wild season,” Godwin previously teased Us about the show. “I’m excited to tune in along with the rest of us, honestly.”

The show is expected to premiere on Netflix in 2027.
