It’s officially Grand Theft Auto VI launch day. It really does feel surreal to say GTA 6 is a game you can play right now. It’s been a long road, but Vice City is now open to visitors.

To celebrate GTA 6’s historic launch, Tom’s Guide is bringing you live coverage of launch day, with the latest news, our first impressions as we start playing the game for ourselves, and, because it’s Black Friday shopping season, there are also plenty of great gaming deals that can upgrade your experience, letting you experience GTA 6 at its absolute best.

I know you’d probably rather be playing the game right now, but if you can’t get to a console just yet, stick around for all the latest updates and best deals on GTA 6 launch day.

To quote super underrated British indie rockers Circa Waves, "it took a long, long time to get here, but we made it." Rockstar Games has just posted on its social accounts to note that Grand Theft Auto VI is "now available." Okay, so it's been playable in most regions for more than 12 hours now, but still it's a reminder that GTA 6 has arrived, the 13 year wait for a new GTA game is over, and Vice City is our to explore freely.

As noted, I'm only 90 minutes into GTA 6 — sadly couldn't stay up too late last night — but I want to hear how much time you've spend in Vice City already. Is anybody already at the 10 hour mark? Or do you just have a couple of hours in GTA 6 so far?

It's been over 24 hours since GTA 6 reviews went live, and it still holds a near-perfect 99 score on Metacritic. After 150 reviews, it's the current highest-reviewed video game on the platform. Some other titles have the same score, but not with this many reviews accounted for. It's remarkable that Rockstar Games actually delivered on the extremely high levels of hype! Based on my limited playtime, it deserves the 99, too.

I won’t lie — I’m biased as a GTA fan. This is more than a game. It’s a chance to look back at how far you’ve come over 13 years.

But one thing remains the same across both. That feeling of escaping to another living world that nobody but Rockstar can capture. I played four hours when it launched at midnight (currently on my third cup of coffee to keep going), and I’m truly floored by the scope of GTA VI.

Setting aside the fact it’s a beautiful-looking game with a stellar soundtrack and a Leonida that feels truly alive, the biggest thing that’s stood out to me so far is what I’ll call the “Red Dead-ification” of GTA.

I spoke to my boss about doing some day-to-day menial tasks and DIY in-game — such as fixing my roof at 2 am — and the reaction was “...why?” It’s a good question, and the answer is the same as Red Dead Redemption 2. As much as the world feels alive, you feel like a part of it with your own personal stories and supporting cast of people you actually care about.

A skeptic’s take would be to say Rockstar’s made an “adulting” educational game for millennials. But in reality, these small elements are significant in really making you emotionally invested in the characters, their personalities and their stories.

Playing GTA 6 into the early hours of today reminded me how useful a second controller can be. My first DualSense ran out of battery just 30 mins into my session, but because I had a spare fully charged one, I could swap over. No need to pause playing or to play with a wired connection. If you need a spare DualSense, or just a shiny new one, PS5 DualSense controllers are $54 at Amazon (down from $74).

If for some reason you haven't already bought GTA 6, I bet you feeling the FOMO right about now. There's still time to join the party over in Vice City ahead of the weekend. You can either purchase the game digitally on the Xbox and PlayStation Store, or you can grab a "code in box" edition, which comes with a nifty poster (pictured above). I'm far too early to make a definitive judgement on GTA 6, but so far it's blowing me away.

I stayed up past my bedtime last time to play GTA 6 as soon as it unlocked at midnight. I've only played roughly 90 minutes of the game, but I'm already overwhelmed, and seriously impressed. Graphically the game is stunning, but it's the rich detail of the world that has grabbed me out of the gate. Vice City is a place I just want to wander. I didn't even complete any mission beyond the prologue, I just walked the streets, taking in the sounds and signs of this overwhelming location.

The wait is over, Grand Theft Auto VI has arrived. Millions of gamers are taking their first steps in Vice City, and to celebrate, we're talking all things GTA 6 today in this live blog. I'll have news, first impressions from across the Tom's Guide team, some interactive polls (I want to hear your early thoughts, too), and plenty of early Black Friday deals so you can upgrade your gaming setup for the hours of play ahead.
