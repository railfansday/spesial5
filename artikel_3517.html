Ousted Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang Breaks His Silence, Says He Would Come Back

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Jensen Huang, the co-founder of Nvidia who was recently ousted as CEO after he admitted to having an affair with someone that US intelligence believes could by a spy for China, has now said he is open to returning to the company.

In his first public statement since his resignation from Nvidia, Huang told The Wall Street Journal that he is aware of the "outpouring of calls" from Nvidia employees, investors, business partners, and customers for him to come back as CEO. He would come back, if asked.

"If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO," he said.

This statement came a day after a source told CNBC that the Nvidia board is not considering bringing Huang back as CEO.

Huang released his statement on social media, saying, "Thank you to everyone who has shown kindness to my family and me. It means the world to me."

His statement added: "I came to the United States 53 years ago, and becoming an American citizen is one of the greatest gifts and proudest moments of my life. Nvidia has been my life’s work. Building it into the company it is today alongside my colleagues has been a privilege and an honor. Nowhere but in America could a company like Nvidia be possible. I love our country, and I love Nvidia."

Huang acknowledged that he "did not live up to the standards expected of everyone at Nvidia" and owned up to making "serious mistakes in my personal life." He said he created problems for Nvidia's Board of Directors and he said he "deeply" regrets that. "I own my mistakes completely, and I am committed to cooperating with the Board’s independent investigation," he said.

Nvidia was the world's biggest company by market cap until the company's stock price began to crash in October, and it fell further after the controversies came to light, including multiple affairs beyond the one with the alleged spy.

To be sure, Nvidia remains a juggernaut--it's currently the No. 2 biggest company in the world, with a market cap of $3.75 trillion. Only Apple, with a market cap above $4 trillion, is bigger than Nvidia.

The New York Times and other media outlets reported that Huang had a romantic relationship with the former CEO of the AI company Megaspeed, Alice Huang (no relation). US intelligence sources claim that Alice Huang was a spy for China's Ministry of State Security, though China officially denies this narrative and says it's an effort by the US to smear China.

Megaspeed, for its part, said Alice Huang left the company in 2025 and denied any wrongdoing in the Nvidia scandal.

The Nvidia controversies have led to turmoil in the stock market, in part because Nvidia is one of the "Mag 7" companies that are driving growth in the US. Some of the information coming to light suggests that Nvidia's accounting and export control compliance are not buttoned up, and this has fueled further concerns about the growth potential and returns on investment for the AI trade. Numerous other technology and AI stocks have dropped in value significantly since the Nvidia news broke.

Nvidia's interim boss is CFO Colette Kress, who is expected to lead Nvidia's earnings call today, November 17. The earnings report will be closely watched and highly scrutinized--keep checking back with GameSpot for the latest.
