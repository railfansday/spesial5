Britney Spears Spending Thanksgiving With Family in Louisiana: Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Britney Spears will reportedly celebrate Thanksgiving with her family after a long period of estrangement.

Us understands that Britney, 44, is in Louisiana ahead of the holiday on Thursday, November 26. The “Toxic” singer’s parents, Jamie Spears and Lynne Spears, still own property in Kentwood, Louisiana, where Britney, her brother Bryan, 49, and sister Jamie Lynn, 35, spent their formative years.

Britney has been joined in Louisiana by sons, Sean Preston, 21, and Jayden James, 20, and plans to visit her parents and siblings over the festive period, per TMZ.

Us has reached out to Britney’s representative for comment.

Last week, Britney offered a very public olive branch to her loved ones, insisting via Instagram that she was “so blessed to have such a beautiful family.”

“I’m so very sorry for bringing up past issues, but I think I did because I realized I needed to protect my heart,” she wrote via Instagram on Saturday, November 21.

The pop icon went on to write, “It’s the way I love and when I love you, I REALLY love you. My stubbornness is childish and embarrassing. Thoughts on the past, I’m obviously human and there actually might be something to those things I said phycology [sic] wise, either way I’m sorry to my family bringing up those memories.”

Britney said she was eager to work out her differences with the Spears family after years of separation.

“I’ve never actually spoke to them face to face on it,” she added in a since-deleted post. “It’s frankly [because] I just miss them like crazy!!! And I’m very sorry!!!”

Spears wrote in her 2023 memoir The Woman in Me about the way her father, Jamie, 74, allegedly misused a 13-year conservatorship to exert control over every aspect of her life — and she condemned other family members for failing to step in. Jamie and Lynne have always denied any wrongdoing.

A source exclusively told Us in September 2025 that Britney’s relationship with her mom was still very much a work in progress.

“[Britney] and her mom still have highs and lows. One day they’re working on their relationship, the next they won’t speak for weeks,” an insider revealed. “Lynne tries to treat the situation with as much care as possible, but she also doesn’t want to sit back and do nothing.”

The source added, “She’d love more than anything to be close with Britney again. They used to be best friends. Lynne understands Britney is still hurt and traumatized by the conservatorship, but she hopes one day they can focus on the future and not the past.”

Britney’s sons have rallied around her this year in the wake of her DUI arrest in March. Us learned at the time that Sean Preston and Jayden were crucial in convincing their mom to enter rehab following her run-in with the law. (Britney shares her sons with ex-husband Kevin Federline.)

“Britney went to rehab after several conversations with both of her sons,” a source confirmed to Us in April. “They expressed concern about her recent behavior and urged her to seek professional help, which has been long overdue, to get her back on track. All they’ve ever wanted for their mom is health and happiness, even during the years they were estranged. They hope she’ll take it seriously.”

Once she completed rehab, Britney pleaded guilty to a lesser charge known as “wet reckless driving” in exchange for a misdemeanor count of driving under the influence of drugs and alcohol being dismissed. (Britney was sentenced to 12 months of probation and one day of time served in jail, plus she was ordered to complete a DUI class and pay state-mandated fines and fees of $571. She was also required to meet with a psychologist once a week and a psychiatrist twice a month.)
