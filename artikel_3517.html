Controversial Call of Cthulhu Game Canceled After Designer's Child Abuse Conviction

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Publisher pulls crowdfunded miniatures game Call of Cthulhu: Old Ones Origins after court verdict on Friday

Call of Cthulhu: Old Ones Origins, an asymmetrical tactical board game developed by Chaosium Inc. and based on H.P. Lovecraft's Cthulhu Mythos, has been canceled. On Nov. 15, Chaosium posted a message on the project's updates page on tabletop crowdfunding platform Gamefound explaining that the game's designer, Samuel William Bailey, was convicted in Skagit County, Washington on Friday, Nov. 13, of multiple counts of first-degree rape of a minor and child molestation.

According to the message, Chaosium was unaware of the matter until Nov. 13. After receiving official confirmation on Nov. 15, the company decided to stop all development on the project. Chaosium is currently working with its legal counsel and Gamefound to decide how to move forward. Call of Cthulhu: Old Ones Origins collected nearly $770,000 during its crowdfunding campaign; Gamefound is attempting to stop further crowdfunding payments, but Chaosium warned that some may still go through.

"These are very serious crimes, and have significant legal implications on the Old Ones Origins board game, beyond the serious moral implications," Chaosium said.

On the project's comment page on Gamefound, an account claiming to be the father of two children raped by Bailey has been posting as early as Nov. 14, revealing details about the case. These include accusations that Bailey has been facing these charges in court ever since 2023, and that he attended conventions to promote the game while he was out on bail. These claims seem validated by the Skagit Valley Herald's court report from Nov. 13, mentioning court records stating that "in June 2023 police responded to a report that Samuel William Bailey had allegedly assaulted a 5-year-old boy and a 7-year-old girl. Records state that the assaults occurred between June 1, 2022, and June 4, 2023."

If confirmed, this could call into question Chaosium's ability to run proper background checks. The project's backers have shown compassion and solidarity to the family of the victims, while also wondering whether Chaosium will be able to process refunds.

Call of Cthulhu: Old Ones Origins is described as a "competitive, asymmetric game for 2-4 players that lets you command a unique faction — each devoted to a terrifying deity of the Cthulhu Mythos." Chaosium has been publishing games inspired by the Cthulhu Mythos since the 1980s, when the first edition of the Call of Cthulhu tabletop role-playing game was released.

In October 2026, Call of Cthulhu: Old Ones Origins was at the center of another, much less serious controversy. The game changed the name of one of the faction-leading Elder Gods from Shub-Niggurath to Shub-Gorath. The company explained that the name Shub-Niggurath could be associated with the racial slur used to describe Black people, while clarifying that "Lovecraft scholars concur 'Shub-Niggurath' was not derived from the slur."
