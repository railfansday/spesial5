Apple CEO John Ternus criticized for ‘scripted’ approach to first earnings call

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Investors didn’t seem overly happy about yesterday’s Apple earnings call despite the record results. That likely reflects the company’s cautious take on margins in the current quarter, but some have also criticized what they describe as a very “scripted” approach by new CEO John Ternus.

Bloomberg was the biggest critic, describing it as a “timid” performance that left analysts “sorely disappointed” …

Apple investors and fans hoping to get an insight into the philosophy of new Chief Executive Officer John Ternus on Monday were left sorely disappointed. The 51-year-old’s first earnings call was a timid affair, with Ternus taking cover behind a carefully written script as analysts peppered him with questions.
