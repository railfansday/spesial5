Police deny claims pilot deliberately drew a penis in the sky

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Police in the UK have denied that one of their helicopter pilots deliberately drew a giant penis in the sky after its bizarre flight path was spotted on a tracking website.

The National Police Air Service (NPAS) has found itself having to explain the unusual route taken by one of its helicopters over Hampshire, England, on November 2.

Flight tracking data showed the aircraft making several loops and turns, which, when viewed together, appeared to form a penis and testicles.

However, police insist the shape was entirely accidental, explaining that the helicopter was carrying out a legitimate operation at the time.

“The flight was conducted for a legitimate policing purpose, in support of Thames Valley Police,” NPAS said in a statement to the BBC.

The service explained that its helicopter was assisting officers searching for a vehicle that had failed to stop, with the crew directed towards different locations as the operation unfolded. As police helicopters don’t follow fixed routes during incidents, these movements happened to create the suggestive shape visible on flight tracking websites.

The unusual pattern was spotted by 53-year-old resident Gordon Bird, who decided to check the flight’s route online after the helicopter disturbed his two-year-old daughter’s sleep at around 11pm.

“I happened to have my computer on, I hadn’t gone to bed, so I just looked it up on the tracking,” Bird told the BBC. “The actual buzzing around our estate lasted for 15 or 20 minutes, the actual drawing of the phallus took about half an hour.”
