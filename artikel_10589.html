Hila Klein says Harley Morenstein made “incorrect statements” over Ethan Klein’s affair

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Hila Klein has responded to Harley Morenstein after he discussed Hila’s divorce from Ethan Klein after she revealed that her husband had been cheating for over six months.

Back on October 22, after Ethan Klein stated that the H3 Podcast would be going on an “indefinite hiatus,” Hila Klein revealed that her husband had been having an affair for “over six months.”

Hila said that she could “sense something was wrong” and that she had been “living in a nightmare” after being “fed lie after lie after lie for months.” At the start of November, she filed for divorce from Ethan, revealing that the pair had separated on October 13.  Aside from Ethan’s countersuit – where he asked a judge to deny Hila’s request for financial support – the pair have stayed relatively silent on things.

However, on November 16, H3 Podcast crew member Harley Morenstein – known to many as EpicMealTime – went live to discuss his role in things.

After Harley stated that he had not been fired from H3 and was initially unaware of Ethan’s affair, Hila responded via an Instagram story, saying he was making “incorrect statements” about things.

“When I found out about the affair, I also found out that Harley was aware of the affair from the beginning. When the situation first came to my attention, he portrayed himself as an unwilling participant,” Klein said.

“However, as additional information came to light, it became clear that his initial account was not truthful and that his involvement was intentional. After reviewing screenshots that contradicted his original statements, I determined that continuing our professional relationship was no longer possible. As a gesture of goodwill, I chose to honor his contract through the end of this month, at which point it will be terminated.”

The YouTuber also responded to Hila’s post, saying that he told her about the affair after “her stupid ex-husband involved me” and called out a “lie” in it.

“I gave Hila a word-for-word, literal breakdown of the exact timeline of everything Ethan did,” he added. “When Ethan told me what he was doing, first of all, I didn’t believe it.”

The EpicMealTime co-creator added that he has “not been speaking to Ethan” in the fallout of things, but noted there are “whispers” of the H3 creator returning with a new podcast.

He also said he is “not on bad terms” with anyone related to the H3 Podcast. “At least that I don’t know, literally, that I don’t know.”
