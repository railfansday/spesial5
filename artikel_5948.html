Trump pauses turkey pardon to salute US troops guarding Strait of Hormuz: 'Watch over and protect'

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

President Donald Trump thanked U.S. service members enforcing the naval blockade in the Strait of Hormuz during Tuesday’s annual White House Thanksgiving turkey pardoning ceremony, asserting no Iranian ships have gotten through and vowing Tehran will never obtain a nuclear weapon.

President Donald Trump paused Tuesday's annual White House Thanksgiving turkey pardoning ceremony to offer a special tribute and thanks to U.S. service members defending the Strait of Hormuz and enforcing a naval blockade against Iran.

Speaking from the White House South Lawn during the 79th National Thanksgiving Turkey presentation, Trump thanked American troops deployed overseas and praised those working to prevent Tehran from acquiring a nuclear weapon.

"We want to say a special thank you to them, the people that are guarding the strait know or the blockade we call it," Trump said. "It’s a blockade, the greatest naval blockade in history."

Trump asserted that the U.S. maritime cordon has stopped every Iranian vessel from passing through since its establishment, save for a brief pause earlier in the standoff.

"Not one Iranian ship has gotten through to the blockade since we established it, other than we had a little moratorium. And we learned that you can’t trust them," Trump said. "But not one ship from Iran has gotten through. Our naval blockade is the most incredible naval blockade, probably in the history of the world. It’s amazing."

Trump also contended that U.S. forces have severely degraded Iran's conventional military capabilities and restored oil shipments through the Strait of Hormuz to levels higher than before the conflict began.

The U.S. naval blockade targeting vessels entering and leaving Iranian ports began in April amid the Strait of Hormuz crisis and resumed in July following a temporary pause.

Before returning to the holiday ceremony, Trump offered a Thanksgiving prayer for deployed U.S. troops and expressed pride in the country.

"And we send them all our best wishes, Thanksgiving, and is very special. It’s a very special time for me for a lot of reasons," Trump said. "And I ask God to watch over and protect all of our military and all of our people. I’m so proud of our people, and I’m so proud to be your president because we’ve never had a time like this."

Following his remarks, Trump pardoned this year's National Thanksgiving Turkeys, Freedom and Feathers — though Feathers briefly ran into the bushes during the presentation — before sending them to retire at Texas A&M University.
