Get The American Express Blue Business Cash® Card for business spending under $50,000 a year, since it pays 2% on that spending with no annual fee or categories to track. Past $50,000 it pays 1%, so if you’ll spend more than about $80,000 a year, an uncapped 2% card earns more than this one, even after a $295 fee.

A freelance practice or side business is enough to apply. American Express says you can apply as a sole proprietor, using your Social Security number if you have no employer identification number (EIN).

Owners who put less than $50,000 a year on the card get 2% on every eligible purchase with no annual fee. By our math at standard rates, owners spending more than $79,500 a year earn more with the Graphite Business Cash Unlimited Card than with this one, even after its $295 fee, because its 2% has no cap.
