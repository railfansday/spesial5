Of Course Trump’s Judicial Nominees Are a Bunch of Election-Denying Yahoos

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

At a Senate Judiciary Committee hearing on Wednesday, seven fools who could soon have federal seats refused to admit that Joe Biden won in 2020. Yikes.

The Republicans in Congress are scrambling to fill as many government positions with unqualified replicants as they can find before the clock runs out on their majorities. The giddyap on these nominations has impressed Democrats as being unseemly. From Nomination Notes on Substack:

This has resulted in some hilarious testimonial follies and at least one near hooley between the two senators from Texas. On Tuesday, it was a panel full of judicial nominees, and there were some real lulus in there. The Democrats on the committee decided to test the nominees’ independence. Who, they asked, won the 2020 election?

This is just equal parts silly and insulting by now. Just say it, people. Joe Biden won the 2020 election.

The real dustup came over the nomination of one T. Elliot Gaiser, the head of the DOJ’s Office of Legal Counsel, to a seat on a federal district court in Texas. Outgoing senator John Cornyn refused to return the “blue slip” on Gaiser’s nomination, at least in part for the reason that Gaiser is from Ohio, which makes sense to me.

Cornyn lost the Republican primary for reelection to the inexcusable Ken Paxton, who had the support of the president and the president’s poodle, Senator Ted Cruz, Cornyn’s colleague from Texas.
