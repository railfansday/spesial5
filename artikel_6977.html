Appleseed: (Senior) Behavioral Researcher & Strategist

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Appleseed is a behavioral agency for global development. We are obsessed with one big problem across the sector: important solutions failing to stick.

Our clients are fighting to end poverty or protect the planet. When they struggle to engage people or drive uptake of a service, product, or idea, they put us on the case. Appleseed consultants dive deep — taking an ethnographic approach to investigate the challenge and uncover human insights. Then we help our clients design accordingly and bring about the changes required for impact.

We partner only with nonprofits and funders who seek to preserve human dignity as they go about fostering change within communities. We have enabled smallholder farmers in Senegal to space crops for higher yields, schoolchildren in Kenya to stop drinking contaminated water, and farmers in Guatemala to plant a new kind of maize fortified to combat malnutrition.

What you’ll find here: field-based work that is meaningful, challenging, and real; a small, candid, curious team with high standards and low ego; real ownership and client partnership from day one; and a flexible, learning-rich culture that prizes rigor and honesty.

Are you a behavioral marketing or design researcher/strategist, ready to use your expertise to tackle some of the world’s toughest challenges? Join Appleseed and create lasting social and environmental impact in communities across the globe.

This is a remote position with travel required. You may be based anywhere.

Appleseed is building a team of seasoned behavioral strategists who are also skilled researchers and client partners. This is not an academic or theoretical role: our consultants deliver B2B client services, leading field-driven research and crafting human-centered strategies that stick. We work only with nonprofits and funders fighting poverty and protecting the planet. This role requires behavioral insight, leadership & storytelling ability, and a deep commitment to continuous learning.

We are seeking to fill both full-time core team and freelance project lead roles with significant ownership and influence in shaping Appleseed’s craft and culture. We are excited to meet exceptional candidates who prefer ongoing project-based work, as well as those who would rather join the core team full-time.

Feel free to apply even if you are not immediately available — we will have ongoing project work, and strong candidates are kept on file and contacted when the right project comes up.

All projects begin with part-time remote prep (onboarding, lit review, stakeholder interviews, research design), followed by 2–3 weeks of full-time in-country fieldwork, then part-time remote work for reporting and strategy. Each project has two consultants: a Lead and a Deputy. For this first engagement, you’d start as Deputy, partnering closely with a Core Team Lead who will manage the project.
