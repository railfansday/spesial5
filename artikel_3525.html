Patrick Stewart Was 'Struggling With His Health' on 'Avengers: Doomsday' Set

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The late Patrick Stewart managed to shoot one last movie before his death, but it was not without difficulty.

The legendary actor died in October from Alzheimer’s disease and Lewy body dementia. He was 86.

Stewart’s final movie, Marvel Studios’ highly anticipated Avengers: Doomsday, is set for release on December 18, and directors Anthony and Joe Russo, as well as Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige, recalled how he gave a speech on his final day on set.

“I think it was in the back of all of our heads [that] we may never see him on set again,” Anthony told Entertainment Weekly in an interview published on Tuesday, November 24.

“It was clear that he was struggling with his health, and he worked so hard to deliver a performance and a presence — for us as directors, for his other actors, for the crew,” Anthony continued.

Anthony added, “You could see it wasn’t just the effort of a regular actor showing up and delivering that kind of presence and performance and experience for others, collaboration. It was someone who was really being challenged in a very deep way on a health level, showing up and doing that. For him to have muscled through and delivered the performance that he did in that movie and the experience that he delivered for all of us as collaborators, it made it intensely emotional.”

Stewart reprises his role as X-Men leader Professor Charles Xavier in the new Avengers movie.

According to Entertainment Weekly, he was offered the chance to reprise his character in both Avengers: Doomsday and Avengers: Secret Wars, which is due for release next year, but he decided to only feature in the first film.

Feige told the outlet that Marvel is planning to include a special tribute to Stewart — who first played Professor X in 2000’s X-Men nearly three decades ago — in Avengers: Doomsday.

“We’re long done filming that movie, and he did an amazing job for us,” he told the outlet. “We’re looking now at how we can put something together that honors and respects our friend.”

Stewart’s spokesperson confirmed his death on October 29.

“He has been held in the warm embrace of family and friends, surrounded by live music, prayer, tears, laughter and the kind of profound emotion that only he could inspire after a lifetime of devotion to us all,” his rep said in a statement to Us. “We will forever cherish the peaceful ease and reflection on his face, an enduring reminder of his invincible grace.”

Stewart’s widow, Sunny Ozell, paid tribute to the actor in an Instagram statement on November 10.

“The grief continues to come in waves. But I’ve also been experiencing such deep, profound gratitude. We left nothing unsaid,” Ozell, 47, shared. “He knew I loved him, and I know he loved me. Patrick and I truly, authentically saw one another, and we amplified each other’s best qualities.”

Avengers: Doomsday is in theaters on Friday, December 18.
