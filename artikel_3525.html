Spider-Man’s Biggest Movie of All Time Officially Returns to Theaters in 1 Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Marvel Cinematic Universe has regained its spark with releases like Spider-Man: Brand New Day and small-screen adventure like VisionQuest, The Punisher: One Last Kill, and Wonder Man. Each release brings us closer to Avengers: Doomsday, which will pit Doctor Doom (Robert Downey Jr.)against the Avengers and even the X-Men. Fans are gearing up for the upcoming superhero mashup by revisiting the old titles for Easter Eggs and connective tissue. Series like Loki, along with Infinity War and Endgame, which elaborate on the multiversal connections, have returned to streaming charts.

The studio also released Avengers Endgame: Encore, an updated version by the Russo Brothers, which brought a plethora of new scenes that bridge the gap between the two features. Among the most recent titles, Tom Holland's Spider-Man: Brand New Day came out earlier this year and had an otherworldly reception. Fans were eager to see how Peter’s life fares after the events of No Way Home, and director Destin Daniel Cretton brought the right sensibilities to bring that story to life. Cretton brought a grounded, mature shift to Peter and touched upon the themes of the burden of adulthood and isolation that turn into a mutation within Peter.

We follow an adult Peter Parker, who lives a life of radical, self-imposed isolation. Because the spell erased the world's memory of his identity, he focuses entirely on being a full-time Spider-Man. Things take a turn when he faces a mysterious new threat: a telepathic villain capable of body-hopping and mind-control. As he transitions from a teenager, we find Peter as a self-reliant, gritty, street-level hero dealing with tactile, realistic hardships. The themes, performances, and Cretton’s vision made Brand New Day a record-breaking box office juggernaut, comfortably tracking as the highest-grossing film of 2026 and one of Marvel's biggest movies ever, having earned over $2.5 billion at the box office.

Now, before we see the ultimate clash between Doctor Doom and the Avengers, Marvel is bringing Brand New Day’s extended cut to the theaters. The new version has added between seven and 10 minutes of footage. Further, this footage will not be available on the Blu-ray release coming out next month, which doesn’t include any deleted scenes. The release seems similar to Spider-Man: No Way Home “More Fun Stuff Edition,” which added 11 minutes to the film that wasn’t on the home video release.

Check out Spider-Man: Brand New Day in theaters on December 4. Stay tuned to Collider for more such updates.
