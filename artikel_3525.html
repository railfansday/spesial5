How to get mighty Pokémon in Pokémon Go

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

These Pokémon essentially come combat-ready

These Pokémon essentially come combat-ready

Mighty Pokémon are particularly strong Pokémon that you can find during the Pokémon Go Wild Area 2026 event on Nov. 14 and 15.

These monstrous beasts are just found in the wild as normal, but all is not what it seems — these fully-evolved Pokémon are beefed up stat-wise and are pretty hard to catch.

Below we explain how mighty Pokémon work, the schedule for mighty spawns, and what mighty Pokémon are the most meta-relevant.

Update (Nov. 13): We’ve updated this guide with the roster of mighty Pokémon available as part of the 2026 Wild Area event.

Despite the difficulty in finding and catching, mighty Pokémon are made for combat right away, and are pretty powerful! You'll want to nab any you find during the Go Wild Area event — and you should use your Go Safari Balls to nab them. (There's a reason these two things exist as part of the same event at the same time.)

Different mighty Pokémon spawn depending on the days:

Alolan Exeggutor*, Scizor*, Flygon*, Walrein, Metagross, Empoleon*, Magnezone*, Conkeldurr, Haxorus

Azumarill*, Kingdra*, Aggron*, Ferrothorn, Hydreigon*, Goodra, Noivern, Tinkaton, Clodsire

*These Pokémon will spawn on both days via Incense.

Notably, some of the other mighty Pokémon are good in PvP, but since they show up at a high level already, lots of them have been powered up too high, disqualifying them from their relevant leagues. (For example, Clodsire is a beast in Great League, but... it's definitely going to be over 1,500 CP here.) The only Pokémon on the mighty list that's good in Master League is Goodra, Metagross, and Hydreigon.
