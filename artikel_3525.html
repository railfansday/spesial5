'St. Denis Medical' EP Addresses Ariana Madix Exit After Shocking Show Split

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The newest episode of St. Denis Medical took a shocking turn with a surprise onscreen split — but does that mean Ariana Madix has officially left the show?

During the Monday, November 23, episode of the NBC series, Brooke (Madix) accepted a new job, marking the end of her romance with Bruce (Josh Lawson). Us subsequently asked creator Eric Ledgin whether the story line had reached its natural conclusion — or if it was a result of Madix’s busy schedule.

“I think both can be true. [Bruce] was certainly too much for [Brooke]. Too much or not enough, I’m not sure. Maybe both of those are true as well,” Ledgin exclusively told Us. “But I think we’re just trying to make the most entertaining, funny and logical choices for the characters in the stories we’re telling.”

Ledgin knew Bruce’s personal life had to take a turn, adding, “For me, Bruce had to be crushed, and it just had to happen. It reverberates throughout the season for him. Bruce has a lot of growing to do. I think as a person, often the way that growth happens is from being hurt and so it just made a lot of sense for the character.”

He continued, “And Ariana is so lovely and funny and we would love to have her back. It’s certainly not off the table for the character to make an appearance again.”

St. Denis Medical, which premiered in 2024, follows overworked doctors and nurses trying their best to care for patients at an underfunded Oregon hospital. Madix, 41, joined the show in season 2 as Bruce’s ex Brooke, who works at the same place as him and who later got back together with the surgeon.

“I’ve done guest spots before, but this is my first recurring role and it’s been really exciting because I feel like that trajectory of scripted is something that I started a very, very long time ago,” Madix explained to Us in April. “Maybe before Vanderpump Rules. So I feel as though it feels like coming home — in a sense — of being able to pick that back up and also be able to be a part of such an amazing show.”

Madix’s opportunity on the sitcom felt extra special since she was already a fan.

“St Denis is one of the best shows that we have going on — especially for comedies — on TV right now,” she said. . “It’s the type of show that I’ve always wanted to be a part of. For them to have me around for a few episodes and be able to play this recurring character? Honestly, it’s been really, really awesome.”

At the time, Madix didn’t rule out another return in the future.

“I would love to come back. I had such an amazing time. I’ve always dreamed of the day I get to do what I love and make a living doing it,” she said. “I would love to see what Brooke is like outside of that hospital.”

As for whether Us‘ pitch for a Brooke spinoff was too rich to pass up, Madix replied, “Maybe one day.”

For now, Madix isn’t expected to appear in more episodes in season 3. Ledgin, however, had ideas for other potential reality cameos.

“He’s not a reality star but we did [have] an athlete on the show — Draymond Green — last year. I’m manifesting Josh Allen, who also happens to be you know married to one of the most talented actresses of her generation,” he noted. “So I’m manifesting something for Josh Allen and maybe a Hailee Steinfeld edition. That’s a dream casting for the show.”

St. Denis Medical airs on NBC Mondays at 8 p.m. ET before streaming the next day on Peacock.
