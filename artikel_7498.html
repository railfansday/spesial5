Salesloft: Regional Vice President, Enterprise Account Management

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Job Title: Regional Vice President, Enterprise Account Management

This is a fully remote opportunity and can be worked from any location in the United States.

Revenue is complex, and clarity is rare. Our mission at Salesloft is to transform revenue complexity into clear, decisive action for every team driving growth.

We’re building the industry’s first Predictive Revenue System, where humans and AI work together to make confident decisions based on real signals. Thousands of customers run on our platforms every day, which gives us Revenue Context: a view into what happens in a deal, when, and with what outcome.

Our vision is a future where revenue growth is predictable and the teams driving it are unstoppable. If you want to help build it, alongside people who Move as One and let Evidence over Ego guide the work, we’d love to meet you.

THE OPPORTUNITYLead one of our highest-performing enterprise teams as it shows customers why a Predictive Revenue System is the way to run pipeline, forecast and growth.

As our Regional Vice President, Enterprise Account Management, you’ll lead 6 to 8 seasoned enterprise sellers who own our largest customer relationships. At Salesloft, we connect pipeline generation, deal progression and forecasting in one closed loop through our Predictive Revenue System, and you’ll help your team bring that story to senior revenue and technology leaders. WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FORYou’re a sales leader who makes experienced sellers better, builds trust with executives, and stays close to the business without taking it over. You’re as comfortable inspecting a forecast as you are coaching a hard conversation. If you want to learn more, do more, and become more, this is your next move.

THE TEAMYou’ll lead 6 to 8 seasoned enterprise sellers on one of the strongest teams in our enterprise segment, with several already at or pacing toward President’s Club. The team owns both retention and growth across our largest customers, at a time when Salesloft is changing how we sell and who we sell to. It’s a great place to grow: you’ll have a high-performing team, real influence on how we go to market, and the backing of leadership and enablement to do it well.
