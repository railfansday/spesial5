The Vuori and Baleaf Black Friday Leggings Deals We’re Shopping This Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

And which label gives you a better bang for your buck.

Black Friday is arguably the best time of year to stock up on activewear and athleisure—especially those pricier pieces you’ve been eyeing for months. And if your leggings drawer is due for a refresh, chances are you’ve perused styles from Vuori and Amazon favorite Baleaf. Both brands have amassed a loyal following for their comfy, functional activewear, albeit at very different price points. The good news? Black Friday deals make a new pair from either brand an even sweeter purchase.

As a style writer who’s spent years testing leggings, I’d steer those looking for buttery-soft fabrics and versatile styles that transition seamlessly from Pilates to lounging toward Vuori. Baleaf, on the other hand, excels at affordable, performance-driven leggings with practical features like roomy pockets and fleece-lined interiors for outdoor winter workouts. Ultimately, the better buy depends on how you plan to wear your leggings—which is why I’ve broken down the best Black Friday deals from both brands below.

Baleaf’s leggings are already a steal at full price, ringing in at less than half the cost of many Vuori styles. Throw in Black Friday discounts of 20 to 40% off, and you can stock up on multiple pairs without putting much of a dent in your wallet. We especially love Baleaf’s more workout-focused styles, from its fleece-lined leggings that make winter walks considerably more bearable to its workout tights with generously sized pockets and moisture-wicking fabric. The fabrics may not feel quite as luxe as Vuori’s, but in terms of functionality for the price, Baleaf is tough to beat.

The Cali-label makes some of our favorite leggings for low-impact workouts and everyday wear, thanks in large part to its ridiculously soft fabrics. The brand’s leggings strike a nice balance between gentle compression and unrestricted movement, making them especially great for yoga, Pilates, or simply hanging around the house. The downside? A single pair can set you back around $100. With select styles marked down by 20 to 40% for Black Friday, now’s an especially good time to invest in a pair you’ll reach for well beyond your workouts.
