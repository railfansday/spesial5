After a slow start to their Champions League campaign, Galatasaray are back in the hunt for a place in the knockout stage following consecutive wins on gameweeks three and four.

The 4-2 victory at Lille and 2-0 home triumph over VFB Stuttgart were part of the Turkish giants' ongoing seven-match winning run and put them on six points heading into the second half of the league phase, with 10 points generally seen as the target to finish in the top 24.

Another victory would therefore leave Galatasaray on the brink of qualification and the vociferous Rams Park crowd will be as deafening as always, as they try to roar their side onto a third straight success in this competition for the first time.

However, they will be without star striker Victor Osimhen due to a knee injury, while several other key players including Rafael Leao, Roland Sallai and Ismail Jakobs are also out with various fitness concerns.

Unai Emery's men are in a strong position to qualify in the top eight of the league phase following wins against Club Brugge, Fenerbahce and Viking, although their 3-0 defeat at Barcelona came as part of their ongoing five-match run without a victory in all competitions.
