'Price knows he can win Worlds after Grand Slam glory'

Wayne Mardle says Gerwyn Price knows he can win the World Championship at Alexandra Palace again after ending his major trophy drought to claim a fourth Grand Slam title.

'The Iceman' had not won a major televised event since 2022 and may have thought it might not happen again after losing three finals this year alone.

But with Luke Littler and Luke Humphries exiting early, Price took his chance with a brilliant 16-11 win over Gian van Veen in Wolverhampton.

🏆 World Darts Championship (2021) 🏆🏆🏆🏆 Grand Slam of Darts (2018, 19, 21, 26) 🏆 World Grand Prix (2020) 🏆🏆 World Series of Darts Finals (2020, 22) 🏆🏆 World Cup of Darts (2020, 23)

Price claimed Grand Slam glory for the first time since 2021 - the same year he won his sole world title, when he was forced to celebrate behind closed doors due to the Covid-19 pandemic.

"Gezzy knows he can win it. Yes, winning the Grand Slam four times is amazing for him, but he knows he can win the Worlds," said Mardle.

"He's done it before and he's got a new-found fanbase.

"He's enjoying himself and it's incredible the way that everything's turned around for him. And, look, he's enjoying life.

Looking ahead to Ally Pally, 'Hawaii 501' said: "Those that are at the top of the tree right now, call it top six, top five, top four, they're the ones to beat. They really are.

"Luke Humphries, Luke Littler, Gezzy Price.

"Let's be honest, we know that he got beat [in the semi-finals] and didn't play great but Michael van Gerwen shone this week.

"Don't rule out a fourth world title for him. With timing and a bit of luck, anyone can win."

Mark Webster said it does not look like Price is done after returning to the winners' circle with his eighth major title.

Price will be eyeing further televised success at the Players Championship Finals which takes place at Butlin's Minehead Resort from November 27-29.

Van Veen, Humphries and Van Gerwen will also headline, as a 64-player field compete in the final tournament before the 2027 World Darts Championship.

"He's still got tournaments to win. He's talking about the next one already," said Webster.

"He goes to Minehead and he's talking about this trend with winning this and winning the Worlds.

"He wants another World Championship, and particularly all the heartache he's had getting to finals this year, to get over the line, really important for him. It could open up some floodgates for him now to go on and win major after major."

The 2008 Lakeside world champion believes the cream will rise to the top on the grandest stage of them all.

"At the Worlds, there'll be upsets, but I think it will come to the top players," said Webster as Price aims to follow Michael Smith, Humphries and Littler - twice over the past four years - to win the Slam and then go on to win the Worlds in just over a month.

"I think the two Lukes left this event - they just got beat by the better player. There's no real concern in the way they played, so they'll be a force, along with Price, Gian, and it's good to see Michael [Van Gerwen] in glimpses, but it's going to be a brilliant three weeks at Ally Pally."

Victory for Price means he is provisionally up to world No 3, with Littler still No 1, Van Veen at No 2, and Humphries down to No 4 - potentially shaking up the draw heading into the Worlds.

Price, who took home the £200,000 jackpot in Wolverhampton, has also overtaken Littler with the most ranking money earned this season.

Commentator Dan Dawson explained: "Price has had to battle to this title, and I think he's had to battle to win this Grand Slam more than he has in any other since his first, because we've seen him play unbelievable stuff to win Grand Slam titles.

"But I think that's what he's had to do to become a winner again, and seeing him smile and seeing that pressure lift, just hearing him talk, I think that is an enormous sign because he's already looking ahead. What can I win next?

"We know that at Ally Pally he is up into No 3 in the world, with Humphries and Littler on the same side of the draw provisionally.

"That game there between Gian and Price, that would provisionally be the semi-final in the other half of the draw.

"So that has done him a favour if he is looking to become a world champion again."
