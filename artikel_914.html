Gary Neville on De Ligt selection and 'negligent' Man Utd

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Manchester United were thrashed 4-0 by Liverpool in Mauricio Pochettino's first game as head coach after Leny Yoro's eighth-minute red card; Red Devils have conceded 34 goals in all competitions this season - the most by any side in Europe's major leagues

Gary Neville accused Manchester United's hierarchy of negligence for failing to strengthen their back line as their 4-0 loss to Liverpool left them with the worst defensive record in Europe.

Liverpool ran riot after Leny Yoro's eighth-minute red card at Anfield as goals from Dominik Szoboszlai, Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz ensured Mauricio Pochettino suffered a horror start to his Manchester United tenure.

United have now conceded 34 goals in all competitions this term. It is the highest total of any side across Europe's top-five leagues. The result keeps them second from bottom in the Premier League.

"It's negligent," said Sky Sports pundit Neville.

"The negligence is not bringing in defenders in the transfer window, because we've seen this for two or three years."

Pochettino made the decision to hand Matthijs de Ligt his first Premier League start in 357 days at Anfield but the Netherlands international was partly at fault for the opening goal and endured a torrid evening along with the rest of United's defenders.

Watching back the opener, when De Ligt misjudged a high ball, Neville said: "The minute that De Ligt makes that run in that channel, Yoro has to come across within 10 metres of him, and [Diogo] Dalot has to come across. This is so bad from Yoro and Dalot.

"De Lift makes a right mess of it. He can't believe it though, De Ligt. He's played centre-half before. He can't believe Yoro's not there. Then Yoro makes contact and it's clumsy.

"Honestly. You can't get worse that that.

"You've got a back four who are looking after their own jobs, they're in fear.

"De Ligt and [Lisandro] Martinez have dropped off, Dalot's not coming quickly enough.

"It's a mess because of the fact they've got no confidence and haven't got the ability to play out there at Anfield in a game against Liverpool."

Neville was baffled by Pochettino's decision to start De Ligt, who was similarly poor when returning from injury in last month's 4-1 loss to Como in the Champions League.

"We were in the trucks over in the compound and when the team came through straight away we were very surprised," added Neville.

"I just didn't think Manchester United would win, and I thought they would concede lots of goals and make mistakes.

"Maybe the options on the bench aren't brilliant either, but it's really desperate and it's tiring watching them.

"Mauricio Pochettino's options are not great, but I didn't get De Ligt.

"Having been out for a year myself, it's really difficult to come back into a game like that.

"I'm not saying that you can't, but as a centre-back getting your bearings… You saw him in Como, he was all over the place in Como.

"It's almost like you don't know what's happening.

"I found that coming back into it when I was 32, he's obviously more experienced as well.

"I was very surprised he played but then the others haven't been doing great either."

United's total of eight points after 11 games is their lowest at this stage of a season since the 1972/73 campaign.

"You wonder how low it can get, but this is as low as it's been in 54 years," added Neville.

"A few weeks ago against Fulham, I said it's the worst 40 minutes or 30 minutes I've seen in my lifetime.

"At the time people said that was maybe a bit too much, but this is actually the worst Manchester United have ever been in 54 years. It's a fact.

"The worst defensive team in Europe, second bottom in the league, they've never started this badly."

He added: "I would have started with Harry Maguire today. Manchester United's best back four - and I think we're informed enough to know, we've watched these lads for two or three years - is [Luke] Shaw, Dalot, Maguire and Martinez.

"Now, it's a poor back four, let me be really clear. But it is Manchester United's best back four.

"Now, if you can get that back four out on the pitch, it does win games, against Liverpool last year, it does win games against City and Arsenal, it can compete slightly.

"But that back four that started today, and the one that finished against Villa a few weeks ago of [Noussair] Mazraoui, [Patrick] Dorgu, Yoro, and [Ayden] Heaven - that will get you relegated, that's a fact."

Roy Keane, meanwhile, was amazed to see some travelling fans at Anfield give the players a positive reception after the game.

"Has a team ever got a result before with 10 men? It can be done," he said on Sky Sports.

"Are you telling me Man Utd couldn't have done better and everyone is saying it's not bad?

"At least show a bit more fight, a bit more quality, I can't believe what I'm hearing.

"Fans are applauding the players. Applauding what? Applauding rubbish. When do the players take responsibility for some of the decision-making? Liverpool should have scored eight or nine.

"I feel like swearing. How can anyone say 4-0 is not too bad?"
