Bruno Fernandes names club he could join after Man Utd: ‘I’ll never close the door’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes is open to a return to Sporting CP before his career ends.

Fernandes spent three years in the Portuguese capital before joining United in January 2020. Th attacking midfielder has been the club’s best signing of the post Sir Alex Ferguson era but his future at Old Trafford is in more doubt than ever amid the club’s worst season in 40 years.

The Portugal international has already rejected to new contract offers. His current deal is set to expire in the summer but United do have the option to extend that by a further 12 months.

Fernandes is back at his old club this week with United taking on Sporting in the Champions League on Wednesday night. Speaking on Tuesday, the skipper revealed talks over a new deal are on hold now but was also quizzed on his club future.

Al-Hilal have made repeated attempts to sign the attacking midfielder but Fernandes says he would consider a return to Sporting if the chance were to present itself.

‘I can’t promise that,’ Fernandes said when asked about the possibility of rejoining Sporting. ‘I don’t like making those kinds of promises.

‘The time I spent here makes me think I’d like to come back. There are many things beyond football. If that possibility arises one day, why not? I was happy here and I’d like to be again.’

United have won two of their four Champions League games this season with Europe offering slight respite having won just a single game in 11 Premier League outings.

‘It’s a difficult time for us, things aren’t going as planned,’ Fernandes said. ‘We have the opportunity to play in a different competition.
