Tom Lee Blames Nvidia and Anthropic IPO for Ethereum Plunge as Bitmine Buys the Dip

Bitmine Immersion Technologies kept buying Ethereum through a punishing selloff, adding 7,653 ETH worth roughly $14 million, as chairman Tom Lee argued the token’s steep decline runs counter to improving fundamentals and echoes a dip that preceded a major tech IPO earlier this year.

The NYSE-listed company said Monday its holdings reached 6,077,745 ETH, worth about $11.1 billion at a reference price of $1,823 per coin, far below where the firm’s stack was valued just weeks ago.

That marks a stark drop in paper value: Bitmine’s total crypto, cash and “moonshot” holdings now stand at $11.8 billion, down from $14.3 billion a week earlier, almost entirely because of ETH’s price slide rather than any selling.

The position still represents 4.97% of Ethereum’s 122.2 million token supply, leaving Bitmine 99% of the way to its goal of owning 5% of the network, the target it calls the “Alchemy of 5%.”

Lee did not sugarcoat the move. “ETH tumbled 28% in the past two weeks,” he said, calling the “waterfall decline” inconsistent with what he views as positive fundamentals, including banks and financial institutions announcing tokenization projects built on Ethereum. (Disclosure: Tom Lee is an investor in Dastan, Decrypt’s parent company.)

He pointed to external pressures weighing on the price, including the fallout from Nvidia’s turmoil triggering margin selling in Asia and investors raising cash ahead of Anthropic’s initial public offering, whose pricing is reported for Nov. 18.

Lee also drew a historical parallel, arguing the drop mirrors the sell-off ahead of SpaceX’s $75 billion June IPO, when ETH fell 39% over eight weeks and bottomed three trading days before pricing. Lee suggested the current slump, down a similar amount ahead of the Anthropic listing, could follow the same pattern.

Bitmine remains the world’s largest Ethereum treasury and the second-largest crypto treasury overall, behind Michael Saylor’s Strategy, which now holds 849,327 BTC. Strategy also kept buying through the downturn, with Saylor nudging the market to rally with the quip “this dip could use a little orange.”

Bitmine has 5,227,037 ETH, about 86% of its holdings, staked through its MAVAN platform, with projected annualized revenue of roughly $245 million at current levels.

It also holds 216 Bitcoin, $448 million in cash and marketable securities, a $180 million stake in Beast Industries and a $67 million position in Eightco Holdings.
