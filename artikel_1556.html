Real Madrid: José Mourinho does better than Guardiola, Ancelotti and Zidane

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Back on the Casa Blanca bench this summer, José Mourinho has established himself statistically as the coach with the best winning percentage in the history of La Liga among technicians who have managed at least 100 matches. As reported by the Spanish daily A.S.the Portuguese has won 97 of his 126 league matches, a success rate of 77%. He is thus ahead of several renowned coaches, starting with Pep Guardiola, second with 76.3% of victories (116 successes in 152 matches). Carlo Ancelotti (71.1%), Xavi Hernandez (69.6%), Zinédine Zidane (67.8%) and Luis Enrique (66.4%) complete this ranking.

A particularly symbolic statistic for Mourinho, who notably faced Guardiola’s great FC Barcelona during his first stint on the Madrid bench. Asked about this statistic, the Portuguese technician however preferred not to dwell on his figures: “This statistic tells me that we have to win the next game. This is obviously a source of satisfaction, because La Liga has had so many very high level coaches throughout its history. » A way of putting into perspective a record which nevertheless places him ahead of several of the greatest technicians of his generation.
