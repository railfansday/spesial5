Roma interested in Villareal defender Pau Navarro

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Among Roma’s most closely watched players ahead of the winter transfer window, according to Tuttomercatoweb, is Villarreal defender Pau Navarro.

The 2005-born defender is one of the most interesting players in the Yellow Submarine squad, having made 11 appearances this season, becoming a key player.

His strong performances in Spain haven’t gone unnoticed by the big clubs.

In addition to Juve, Juventus are also interested in Pau Navarro, who his coach has compared to Carles Puyol.

Meanwhile, Roma is closely monitoring the situation ahead of the winter transfer window opening in January.

The player has a contract with Villarreal until 2031, a condition that puts the Spanish club in a strong position in any potential negotiations.
