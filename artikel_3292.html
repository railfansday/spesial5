Apple has three products coming next year that I’m very excited for

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Apple is wrapping up a packed year for new products, but there are already several 2027 launches I’m very excited for. Here are my current most anticipated new products rumored for next year.

The iPhone’s 20th anniversary is rumored to bring a special new model that’s been dubbed ‘iPhone 20 Pro.’

iPhone 20 Pro is expected to introduce a new design inspired by the iPhone Air, reintroducing curves and possibly titanium, and with a quad-curved display that wraps around the edges for a bezel-free appearance.

I recently decided the iPhone Duo wasn’t the right model for me. While I’ll certainly be interested to see how the device evolves in its second generation, I’m far more excited for the iPhone 20 Pro as a fresh iteration on slab phones. Combining the strengths of a Pro model (cameras, battery) with the design traits of iPhone Air sounds very compelling.

I’ve been using Apple’s HomeView a lot since getting one earlier this month. And despite some Siri hiccups likely tied to the iOS 27.3 beta, I’m overall really loving the product and my family is too.

Because HomeView has been a hit for us, I’m eager to see how Apple evolves and expands it with the rumored ‘HomeView Ultra’ with a 9-inch screen that can automatically rotate 360° to face whoever’s speaking.

I have no complaints about the current HomeView’s size, but am intrigued by a larger screen’s impact on the overall experience. Mostly though, the idea of a HomeView that automatically rotates to face you sounds really useful. There’s a spot in my kitchen that would be perfect for kitchen and dining room use if the screen could swivel automatically.

The Apple Watch Series 13 might introduce an overhauled design, with one leaker claiming it will break compatibility of watch bands.

I’m tempering my expectations for this, since plenty of Apple Watch redesign rumors have come and gone with little actual change. But Mark Gurman has hinted we might get a redesigned Watch next year too. And after years of the same basic look, I’m fascinated to see what the next generation of Apple Watch design could be.

I’m very happy with my Apple Watch Series 12, and it’s becoming even more powerful with watchOS 27.3. But it’s very much an update focused on internals, so if Apple does deliver an external overhaul, the new Watch will merit its spot among my most anticipated products list.

Which new Apple products are you most hoping for or anticipating for 2027? Let us know in the comments.
