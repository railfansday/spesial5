Microdrama Writers At DramaWave Unionize With WGA West

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Writers at one of the top microdrama platforms have organized with the Writers Guild of America West.

Following a successful card check, the guild revealed Monday that the writers at DramaWave have organized a union to bargain over workplace issues such as compensation and benefits, job security, a fair crediting system, and guardrails on the use of AI in writing.

“We love writing for DramaWave’s rapidly growing audience, and we’re excited to organize with the WGA for a voice on the job and the protections and benefits a union contract can provide,” Senior Writer and DramaWave Organizing Committee member Peter Forbes said in a statement. “DramaWave did the right thing by quickly recognizing the union and we look forward to negotiating a fair contract.” Related Stories News Will The Congressional Blue Tsunami Help Or Hurt The Plan To Save Hollywood? Per the WGAW, DramaWave currently employs 27 writers, who began their organizing effort over the summer. They delivered The writers delivered a letter to DramaWave demanding recognition of the WGA as their collective bargaining representative on October 28. The company agreed to give the union voluntary recognition pending the card check. Watch on Deadline

Bargaining dates for the first contract have not yet been determined.

Adds WGAW President Michele Mulroney: “DramaWave is a top microdrama app because its writers tell stories that captivate audiences worldwide. We are proud to welcome them into our union. Microdramas are a growing part of the entertainment industry and it’s critical that these writing jobs be union jobs.”
