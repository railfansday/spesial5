Influencer under fire for asking fan for money to post selfie with her

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

A TikToker has gone viral after claiming that she was told she’d have to pay to post a photo that she’d taken with an unnamed influencer in California.

If you come across your favorite athlete, actor, or influencer in person, chances are that you’re probably going to ask for a selfie. For the most part, the quick pictures have replaced autographs as the main way to prove that you’d met someone of note.

However, in the case of TikToker Caraline Catlett, she has drawn attention after revealing that she’d snapped a selfie with an influencer and was told that she’d have to pay to post it online.

“I know that there are a lot of influencers that live in San Diego, LA, everywhere in between but never in my life have I seen one out in the wild – until last night,” Catlett said back in late September, noting that she had been “obsessed” with the person in question.

“She said, ‘If I take a picture with you, you can’t post it without paying me. I don’t let people capitalize off my face or grow off my face. There’s not enough room in this industry for everyone.”‘

Catlett added that the influencer, who she refused to name, only had a “couple hundred thousand followers,” and she is now “hyperfixated” on gaining a bigger following than them.

“The entitlement from these influencers needs to be talked about.”

Plenty of viewers have urged Catlett, who has around 200,000 followers on TikTok, to name and shame the influencer. The video has since gotten close to 4 million views too.

Others have reassured her that this isn’t standard practice either. “I promise you this isn’t normal,” said Emilie Kiser. Popular TikToker HALEYYBAYLEE replied: “That’s literally such weird behavior because there is enough room in this industry for everyone.” Another added: “she’s 100% jealous of you.”

Catlett said she won’t reveal who the influencer is, as she doesn’t want to “send people to hate on someone on the internet.”

That has not deterred viewers from trying to get a name out of her. “Girl post that picture anyway I bet I don’t know her,” another commented.
