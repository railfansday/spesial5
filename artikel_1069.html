Norway will be out for revenge against England, who could face Jurgen Klopp’s Germany if they reach the semi-finals.

England have been handed a suspended ban for away supporters as a result of “racist and/or discriminatory behaviour” by fans during Sunday’s 3-2 win over Spain in Madrid.

The ban, which would apply to one match, is suspended for a period of two years, meaning any similar offence during that period would enforce it.

The FA has also been fined €15,000 (£12,600).

The four Nations League quarter-finals will be played across two legs during the international break in March 2027.

The first legs will be played over the 25-27th March with the second legs played between 28-30th March.

The semi-finals and final will follow in June 2027 following the end of the 2026/27 domestic season.

Jurgen Klopp must be feeling confident about Germany’s chances of progressing further in the competition.

The German team haven’t been anywhere near their best so far but they’re drawn against an aging and rebuilding Beglium team in the quarter-finals.

Belgium managed to come through a pressure-filled contest against Italy in the group stage in a sign of their lasting quality but Germany will be favourites.

The Germans also have the added motivation of a possible semi-final with England to come so they should be firing on all cylinders come the quarter-finals in March.

In the third quarter-final the Netherlands will host Portugal.

Portugal have very fond memories of the Nations League having won the competition twice including the inaugural tournament.

They are also the reigning champions having beaten Spain in a penalty shootout last year.

Can the Netherlands muster up enough quality to overcome a Cristiano Ronaldo-less Portugal side and reach the final four?

Elsewhere the Nations League quarter-finals have also thrown up another World Cup rematch.

This one is from the semi-finals as Spain take on France once again.

Spain have won each of the last three knockout games against France with those coming in three different competitions.

They won 2-1 in the Euro 2024 semi-finals before a 5-4 victory in the Nations League semi-finals in 2025.

Most recently, of course, they won 2-0 in the World Cup semi-finals in a match they completely dominated.

Can France overcome them across two legs this time around?

England’s quarter-final tie against Norway will be a rematch of their meeting at this summer’s World Cup.

The Three Lions triumphed on that occasion with Jude Bellingham scoring twice to overturn Andreas Schjelderup’s opener and secure a 2-1 win after extra-time.

Norway were arguably the better team on the day though and will feel confident about challenging England over two-legs come March.

Thomas Tuchel’s men will take on Norway in the quarter-finals.

Should they progress from that tie they will face either Germany or Belgium in the semi-finals.

If they reach the final they will play one of France, Spain, Portugal or the Netherlands.

Now, a draw is made to determine the semi-final fixtures.

In the first semi-final will be the winners of quarter-final three.

They will take on the winners of quarter-final one.

In the second semi-final the winners of quarter-final four will face the winners of two.
