Zlatan Ibrahimović to play villain in MobLand series

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A few days ago, season 2 of the series MobLand broadcast on Paramount ended. Centered on the Harrigan mafia family, this series was a success in terms of audiences with Tom Hardy as the main character in the role of Harry Da Souza. Actors Pierce Brosnan and Helen Mirren also star in the roles of Conrad and Maeve Harrigan. However, a new character will join the cast and he will not be played by just anyone. Former Swedish striker and AC Milan manager Zlatan Ibrahimović will play an antagonist.

This is what reveals The Sun. “There is great enthusiasm for Zlatan’s arrival in the team. The program is already made up of stars, but this surprise signing will certainly help to increase its notoriety and get people talking about it. Zlatan has exactly the ideal physique to play a bad guy, and at 196 centimeters tall, he certainly looks impressive“, a source close to the shooting told the tabloid.
