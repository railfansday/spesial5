Birmingham City fired ‘long way to go’ promotion warning by ex-EFL striker

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Birmingham City sit above Middlesbrough and Stoke City on goal difference at the summit after their first 15 fixtures of the season. BCFC beat Swansea City 2-1 at home before the international brace after two goals by attacker Jay Stansfield.

The Blues finished 10th in the table in the last campaign following their promotion from League One under the guidance of head coach Chris Davies. They have since built on that and have been consistent so far this term as they eye the Premier League.

Sharp, who is most known from his Sheffield United days, has delivered his verdict on the Midlands outfit on Sky Sports’ Essential EFL Podcast: “They’ve done it very quietly actually, nobody has really spoken about them. He’s got the two settled centre-halves who I’ve liked since last season when I’ve seen them both. I think he’s playing like a 4-2-3-1 which does really suit them at the minute.”

He added: “Am I surprised they are top of the league? Honestly I am. I think we said that last time on this podcast about Swansea. It is still tight at the top. Fair play to Birmingham going into this international break. They’ve got the top spot at the minute but there’s a long way to go.”

Birmingham City’s success over recent times has been built on solid foundations. BCFC have let in only 14 goals and they have the best defensive record in the division at the moment ahead of West Brom and Southampton.

The Blues’ backline has been rock solid, mainly due to the form of Phil Neumann and Christoph Klarer. They then have Dael Fry as back-up if needed.

Further up the pitch, they have some useful players at their disposal in the middle of the park like Jhon Solis, Paik Seung-ho and Tomoki Iwata. Liam Millar, Carlos Vicente, Demarai Gray and Patrick Roberts then provide an outlet on the wing.

Stansfield is getting better and more confidence and he can play anywhere in the attacking department. Meanwhile, forward August Priske provides a good focal point up top and at the age of 22, he will develop further the more he plays.

Birmingham City boss Davies came under a bit of pressure last season. BCFC were expected to get into the play-offs under their ambitious owners but they fell well short in the end.

Nevertheless, the Blues stuck by their manager and that decision has paid off. His team are more organised and harder to break down now.

They are back in action next weekend with an away trip to the Bet365 Stadium to take on promotion rivals Stoke City as they aim to keep their momentum going for as long as possible. They will need to be at their best to get something against the Potters.
