Data estimates how much Sheffield United star Sydie Peck is worth as midweek hattrick continues excellent start to the season

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Sheffield United continued their impressive run of form on Tuesday night, securing a 3-2 victory away at Preston North End. SUFC are now unbeaten in four matches and have won four of their last six Championship outings.

It was an impressive all-round team display, but Sydie Peck was undoubtedly the standout performer. The 22-year-old midfielder produced a superb hat-trick in a man-of-the-match performance, becoming the first Sheffield United player to score a competitive first-team hat-trick since Billy Sharp against Aston Villa in February 2019.

Peck’s display was another reminder of his growing importance to the Blades, having also been the subject of recent exit speculation. The midfielder was linked with a move to now fellow Championship side West Ham over the summer.

Peck arrived at Bramall Lane from Arsenal’s academy as a free agent in 2021 and has made 105 appearances for the first team after a brief National League loan at Oldham Athletic.

SUFC will likely not allow Peck to go for a small fee, with a fee of £15m as a baseline the likely ball-park. Whether sides keen on the England U21 midfielder would value him at that is unknown.

FootballTransfers.com’s Estimated Transfer Value (ETV) model places him at €16.2 million (approximately £13.5m at current rates), with a range of €14.6m–€17.8m. This sits above the current FootballTransfers valuation of Divine Mukasa (around €12.8m) and is higher than most Championship players of a similar age-range.

Peck’s age as well as his status as an England youth international will see his value be higher than some other Championship midfielders.

Peck made his senior breakthrough during the 2024/25 campaign before taking another significant step forward last season, registering five goals and five assists in 41 appearances across all competitions.

The London-born midfielder attracted interest during the summer, but he made clear his desire to repay those who have shown faith in him at Bramall Lane, including manager Chris Wilder.

Peck remains happy at Sheffield United, and his performances this season suggest staying put has done little to slow his development. However, if he maintains his current level, further interest is inevitable, potentially making a move away increasingly difficult to turn down.

Sheffield United are keen to secure a new contract for Peck, with his existing agreement due to expire in 2028. The Blades would be wise to act sooner rather than later, with his recent displays likely to attract plenty of attention from elsewhere.
