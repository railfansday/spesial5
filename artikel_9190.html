Robert Pattinson has been announced as the next Bond. Whether or not you agree with that decision on a Bond level (our resident Bond fan isn't sure), a running joke has emerged that Pattinson is getting every film role going. And brands on social media have jumped onto that sentiment with hilarious posts declaring Pattinson to have been offered all sorts of roles, from headliner at Glastonbury to Regional Sales Manager at Subway.

Below I've listed some of the best, but I'm sure your social feed has been full of memes and jokes from brands and individual creators hopping onto the trending moment, and there are many more you could think of. Comment below if so.
