Air fryers, whether in the form of an oven, basket air fryer, or glass air fryer, have slowly crept their way up from gratuitous gadget to near necessity. If you’re deep into your air fryer era or just starting one, we have already found the best air fryers out there.

Using that list as a guide, I’ve also culled the best Black Friday air fryer deals on brands and models we’ve tested and liked over the years.
