Chocolate-Covered Caramel Apple Slices Are the Easiest Treats for Fall Get-Togethers

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

These chocolate-covered caramel apple slices turn the fruit into a glorified candy bar. Bring them to Friendsgiving or keep them in the fridge for a quick weekday snack.

With two little girls who have been loving all things fall these past couple months, my social algorithm has been filled with cozy crafts, Halloween costume ideas, and, of course, all the seasonal treats. When I scrolled to these chocolate-covered apple slices on a stick from @Crowded kitchen, I knew my daughters would go crazy for them.

Whole, uncut candy-coated apples are too big and too difficult for them to eat, but their eyes widen with delight every time we see them in the store. That’s exactly why this tip is genius. All the chocolate, caramel and fun toppings of a whole apple, but served in easy-to-enjoy slices. My girls loved decorating these treats as much as they loved eating them.

And to be honest, even though they’re easy for kids, they’re easier for adults just the same! Plus, the slices are shareable. This format makes them ideal if you’re looking for simple Friendsgiving ideas or low-lift Thanksgiving desserts.

You’ll start by cutting 1/2-inch thick slices from a whole apple. I like to trim the curviest part first and leave it for a snack, while focusing on the broader part of the apple for the slices. You will also need to strategically work around the core and pick out any seeds before proceeding. From there, you can either insert a candy stick or simply use a bamboo skewer, as I did here.

For the chocolate coating, I prefer to use almond bark chocolate coating instead of traditional chocolate bars or chips. It melts more smoothly and sets quickly without the fussy tempering required for real chocolate.

Cut the apples lengthwise into 1/2-inch thick slices. Insert a stick or skewer into one end of each slice. Cover a baking sheet in parchment, and set aside.

In a microwave-safe bowl, heat the almond bark in 30-second intervals until melted, stirring after each interval.

Spoon the melted chocolate coating over the apple slices, and place them on the prepared baking sheet.

Decorate each chocolate apple slice as desired. Allow the chocolate coating to firm up and set at room temperature before enjoying.

I love serving these candy caramel apple slices on a stick because it makes them so easy to eat. My girls loved them because it’s like having giant, fancy lollipops. You can use either a candy stick (you might be able to find them in the baking section of grocery stores) or a bamboo skewer. Candy sticks might not be as readily available, but they are sturdier. Bamboo skewers are effortless to insert and easy to find, but they are a little flimsier, and you have to be careful not to poke yourself while eating the apple.
