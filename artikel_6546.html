Rihanna and A$AP Rocky Are in Physical and Sartorial Lockstep

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

It’s been a minute since we’ve seen Rihanna and A$AP Rocky out together. Today, on a break between the European and Oceanic legs of Rocky’s Don’t Be Dumb tour, the two showed us that time apart didn’t keep them from dressing in sync.

Rih and Rocky were spotted walking arm-in-arm in Beverly Hills. Rihanna, for her part, was thinking pink, coordinating her blush sweater with a pair of funky rose-tinted aviators from Dior. While she kept things on the light side with white trousers and strappy snakeskin sandals, Rih toughened up her outfit with an army green, faux fur-trimmed anorak. While she kept jewelry to a relative minimum, her gold collar necklace always adds a little pizzazz to an outfit.

Rocky stuck to a more subdued color palette, but followed the same sartorial path. The rapper wore a black T-shirt, an open seersucker button-down, and navy trousers as his base. He finished off the look with an indigo denim chore coat, aviator sunglasses, and a pair of oversized diamond studs—for a touch of glamour.

While each half of the power couple took their own path, both Rihanna and A$AP Rocky know that statement-making outerwear, sunglasses, and jewelry are the key to leveling up the basics.
