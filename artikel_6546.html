Meet the 2026 Next Gen Style Stars

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

With each next gen of young stars comes a whole new language of style. Our 2026 best-dressed honorees are red-carpet and street-style regulars, having homed in on their signature aesthetic over many a press tour. In contrast, our 2026 next-gen stars are all relatively new to the scene but are quickly making their sartorial mark. Check out who we’re keeping our eyes on below.

True Whitaker broke out last year as one of the stars of the ensemble cast behind Rachel Sennott’s HBO Max hit I Love LA. Since then her red-carpet style off-screen has continued to wow us. Her best look of 2026: a buttoned-up gray skirt suit from A.W.A.K.E. Mode.

You know Amelie Hoeferle as the hilariously brusque, jeans-and-hoodie-wearing half sister on Amazon Prime’s Sterling Point. Off-screen she also prefers the classics, like little black dresses and the most tried-and-true outfit of them all: a perfect black jacket with a pair of mid-wash jeans and some open-toed pumps.

Also hailing from Sterling Point, Ella Rubin has quickly made her mark as a master of pert American sportswear. At the 2026 US Open, Rubin opted for a true preppy handbook look: a suit jacket and tie spiced up with a retro pair of high-waisted jeans.

The youngest of the Sterling Point cohort, Bo Bragason is already demonstrating her sartorial chops with a self-assurance far beyond her age. Unsurprisingly, Prada snapped her up during Milan Fashion Week, where she appeared in an alluringly androgynous look: a faintly checked double-breasted suit and a baby-blue button-down shirt with an extra-wide folded cuff.

Havana Rose Liu is best known for her performance in the 2023 comedy Bottoms, but her red-carpet style is the tippy-tops. At the 2026 Cannes Film Festival, Liu channeled silver screen glamour, stepping out onto the carpet for a screening of Her Private Hell in a red Balenciaga dress with a flowing draped train and a 1920s wavy bob.

Fcukers’s Shanny Wise and Jackson Walker Lewis first hit the scene in 2024 with a viral pop-up show in the East Broadway Mall. With the debut of their first official album, things have gotten a bit more official—you can catch their next New York show at Brooklyn Paramount. At the 2026 GQ Men of the Year party, Wise wore custom Jordan Lea Allen and Haider Ackermann, while Lewis donned a Dior Men’s top and Balenciaga pants.

Lola Petticrew’s starring roles in Say Nothing and Furious have been somber, but the actor approaches day-to-day dressing with a fresh eclecticism. On a recent trip to New York, they donned a devil-horned headband, Collina Strada sunglasses, and a graphic T-shirt that read “Girls Invented Punk Rock Not England.”

In the past few years, Maya Boyd has made a name for herself on the stage; now, she’s on the verge of making her silver screen debut as Storm in Marvel’s X-Men reboot. IRL, she’s carving out her red-carpet persona with her stylist sister by her side.

Now that the former child actor Rowan Blanchard is taking on grown-up roles—most recently in The Testaments, a series based on the dystopian novel by Margaret Atwood—she’s ready to take red-carpet risks. For the SAG-AFTRA event she attended earlier this year, she chose a deconstructed white slip dress from Paris-based cult label Vaquera.
