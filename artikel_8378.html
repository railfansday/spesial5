Personal Investments • Re: Six Years of Roth Conversions Completed - Next Steps?

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

I would say that you have reached a point where any further Roth conversions should be considered "for entertainment purposes only". That is, they are unlikely to make a meaningful difference, especially one that can be reliably predicted. In the years before you hit RMDs, you might consider taking some approximation of an RMD (4% of IRA balance?) and seeing what it would do to your predicted taxes for the year (treat it like a "practice RMD). That will help understand the tax effects of the future RMDs for you. In your particular case, with no IRMAA consideration and being past the SS taxation bump, I wouldn't expect much effect on marginal rates.

One thing I would suggest doing is making sure you have secondary beneficiaries set up for the IRA. If, as seems likely, your wife will be very well provided for when you pass, she might choose to disclaim inheriting the IRA to pass it on to your kids at that time. I've had experience with dealing with that for a traditional IRA at Schwab, and it was a very simple process. I'm not sure how easy or difficult that might be with TSP (implied by the reference to G-fund).
