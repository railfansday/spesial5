Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: Early retirement health coverage and COBRA/ACA decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Using the youngest spouse as the primary ensured is a great tip. I didn't do that and it ended up being a lot bigger hassle transitioning to just one person having ACA coverage. Naturally the oldest spouse, even if only by a couple months will drop coverage first with the younger spouse still needing coverage so changes must be made midstream.

If the younger spouse is primary, you can go on line and drop the older spouse with a future dated effectivity date when the time comes. It's pretty seemless. If the older spouse is primary you have to actually cancel the entire policy by calling them. Don't try to do it on line. Calling and doing this can be a crapshoot as there always seems to be an interpetation issue on when this will actually happen (one day offset, etc.) when you make the request. Basically when cancelling the entire policy you have to do this on the very last day of the month then start a new plan. I was worried I'd have an inadvertant gap in coverage due to miscommunication. I was also worried it would cause the deductible and out of pocket limits to reset too. In my case this was all happening in late December so little risk for me on that part. The younger spouse has to file a completely new application complete with requalifying for credits, etc. They help you do the new app on the phone so at least that part is pretty convienent.

Long story short making the younger spouse will save you a lot of potential grief when transitioning coverage to just the younger spouse.
