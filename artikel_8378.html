Tanggal: 2026-11-11

There where big changes to HSA eligibility in the OBBB Act.

Currently any Bronze or Catastrophic plan is HSA eligible, even if it has copays or other benefits before the deductible. This might make such plans more attractive than a traditional "HSA" plan (which still exist), but in some cases the deductible and OOP can be even higher than what would qualify as a HDHP, because HDHP plan's deductibles are limited by statue, while Bronze plans (ex-HDHP) are only limited by actuarial value and not by deductible directly.

So, long story short, it might be worth looking into this plan a bit more.
This is an employer plan and unlikely to qualify. The bronze rule only works for plans that are offered on the exchange.
