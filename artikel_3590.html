Russell Wilson References Aaron Rodgers’ Wife After Shocking Steelers Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Russell Wilson called on Aaron Rodgers’ inner circle — including his wife, Brittani — to talk some sense into the quarterback after his shocking release from the Pittsburgh Steelers.

“If I’m his family members, if I’m somebody close to him, I’m his significant other, whatever it may be,’ [I’d ask], ‘Is this how you want to go out?’” Wilson, 37, said Thursday, November 26, on CBS’ The NFL Today, according to Front Office Sports. “After all the success he’s had, is this how you want to go out?… This is a disappointment.”

Rodgers, 42, and his wife got married in early 2025, though specifics about her identity have yet to be publicly revealed.

The quarterback was released by the Steelers on Wednesday, November 25, shortly after he went on ESPN’s The Pat McAfee Show and blasted head coach, Mike McCarthy, calling him “unwell,” “unhealthy” and alleging that he routinely fell asleep in team meetings.

“We’ve all been in the room,” Wilson added. “Like, we’ve seen coaches fall asleep in moments — long days, long hours. We’ve seen players fall asleep in moments.”

The Steelers initially announced Rodgers has been benched on Tuesday, November 24, listing him as “inactive” for their game against the Denver Broncos on Friday, November 27. (Back-up quarterback Mason Rudolph will get the start.)

Hours later, Rodgers went on to tell his dramatic side of the story during an appearance on The Pat McAfee Show.

“There’s no hyperbole, there’s no trying to stick it to you,” Rodgers said of McCarthy, 63. “You’re unwell. What’s best for the team is you give it up to somebody and you take care of yourself and work on your health and do the schedule and do the team meetings.”

After the Steelers lost to the Philadelphia Eagles on Sunday, November 22, Rodgers claimed he had a conversation with McCarthy about the dysfunction within the organization.

“I came in on Monday and said, ‘Hey, this last week is not what we talked about,’” Rodgers explained. “We’ve had these conversations for months now. It began as a concerned friend about somebody who’s unhealthy. If you’re sleeping in meetings, you’re unhealthy.”

Aaron’s younger brother, Jordan Rodgers, addressed the situation during an appearance on Good Morning America on Friday, November 27.

​​“That was something we weren’t all preparing for,” Jordan, 38, quipped on the Friday, November 27, broadcast of Good Morning America. “It was a crazy couple days for the Steelers and Aaron. I think that first and foremost, it’s just kind of sad to see it end this way in Pittsburgh.”
