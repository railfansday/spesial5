These Are Some Of The Biggest Snubs At The Game Awards 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The nominees for The Game Awards have been announced. Some of the biggest games--and those that aren't out yet--make appearances on the list, including Grand Theft Auto 6 and Phantom Blade Zero, but some of the games could've--and maybe should've--been nominated in multiple categories. So, let's go over some of the biggest snubs at this year's TGAs.

Let's start with the big one: Game of the Year. You've got six games nominated here: 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Grand Theft Auto 6, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. All of them are fabulous and well-deserved, but a couple of games are missing: House House's co-op adventure Big Walk and Rebel Wolves' vampire RPG The Blood of Dawnwalker. They appear elsewhere, sure, but I think they deserve GOTY contention, as well.

Let's move onto Best Performance. You've got some stellar voice work here: Angela Sant’Albano as Grace Ashcroft in Resident Evil Requiem, Liam McIntyre as James "Logan" Howlett in Marvel's Wolverine, Manni L. Perez as Lucia Caminos in GTA 6, Patrick Gibson as James Bond in 007 First Light, and Rahul Kohli as Arjun Devraj in Saros. Not taking anything away from these incredible voice talents, but Nick Apostolides as Leon Kennedy in Resident Evil Requiem, Sean Durrie as Dylan Faden in Control Resonant, and Will de Renzy-Martin as Coen in The Blood of Dawnwalker deserve some recognition, too.

Marathon could've easily been nominated for Best Art Direction and Best Action Game. Overwatch and WoW/WoW: Forever would've made sense in the Best Ongoing Game category. Denshattack's soundtrack is too good not to be nominated for Best Score and Music. And Castlevania: Belmont's Curse could slot into a number of categories, including Best Action/Adventure Game, Best Art Direction, and Best Narrative.

There are probably many others that have come out this year that maybe could--and maybe should--have been nominated but weren't for one reason or another. (Note: GameSpot is among the publications that contribute to the nomination process.)

Regardless, we won't have to wait too long to learn what games win what categories, as The Game Awards will air live on December 10 at 4:30 PM PT / 7:30 PM ET.
