Rachel Lindsay Confirms Willie Jones Split: ‘Maybe It’ll Come Back Again’

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Former Bachelorette Rachel Lindsay and country singer Willie Jones have officially called it quits.

“We are not together,” Rachel, 41, said on the Friday, November 20, episode of Good Night With Ben Gleib when asked for an update on her romance. “It just didn’t work out, but [we have] a lot of respect for each other, and maybe it’ll come back again.”

Rachel was first romantically linked to Willie, 32, in December 2024 following her split from ex-husband Bryan Abasolo. They often kept their romance under wraps until their recent breakup.

“It’s just, like, not right now,” she said on Friday. “We still talk and we’re on good terms. It’s just, like, not right now. I have nothing but good things to say about him, and yeah, [he is a] really special person.”

Rachel, who did not publicly share when she and Willie ended their romance, has since dipped her toe back into the dating pool.

“It’s tough out here, it’s the wilderness,” she quipped, hinting that “maybe 2027” she’d consider trying out a dating app. “I don’t know; I’m better off meeting someone in the wild, like, through friends or in the grocery store.”

Prior to her relationship with the musician, Rachel was married to Bryan, 46, from 2019 until their 2023 separation. Rachel and Bryan, who met and got engaged on her season 13 of The Bachelorette, finalized their divorce in January 2025.

“I think I read this somewhere, I’m not sure, but by the time women decide they want a divorce, they’re done [and] I was done,” she told Scott Evans in a 2024 YouTube video about her reason for ending her marriage. “Doesn’t mean it wasn’t hard or a re-adjustment. It’s still a struggle daily, but emotionally, I felt I was very sure this was what I wanted to do.”

At the time, Rachel was “very open” to finding love again.

“My dating life right now is for fun. I would have to be swept off my feet to really be like, ‘Oh, he’s The One.’ I’m trying to have fun and have a roster,” she quipped. “I do want you to be loyal, have a personality, adventurous, charming and maybe even a little corny. … I want them to be ambitious but also very understanding.”

She added, “My therapist said I have broken wing syndrome. I date guys that I see potential in. I see [that] they are a good person, but maybe they need a little guidance or a little help. Rather than dating the person who is already there.”
