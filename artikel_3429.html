‘The Matrix’ Star’s Supernatural Series Is Officially Breaking Records

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Hannah covers horror, sci-fi, and games at Collider, where she writes news and features, reviews new releases, and interviews the people who make them. She’s helping build out Collider’s gaming coverage, with Pokémon as one of her specialties. If she isn’t writing about a monster, she’s probably watching one on a tape from her VHS collection.

Before Henry Park goes missing, he interviews one of his professors for his podcast. He wants to talk about bones and Baba Yaga, the witch of Slavic folklore whose house stands on chicken legs. Carrie-Anne Moss plays the professor, and Heated Rivalry’s Hudson Williams plays the student holding the microphone. Once Henry disappears, there’s a good reason to listen to that conversation again and pay a little more attention to the guy asking the questions.

Henry’s family owns a powerful fishery, which makes him a recognizable face in the small coastal town where he disappeared. The private investigator who comes looking for him is another matter. Rapp (Noah Reid) has barely started asking around before he finds himself at odds with Carson (Clark Backo), the local detective working the case. U.S. viewers got their introduction to the pair on October 23. By the time AMC+ released the fourth episode, the series had already broken a record.

AMC+ will release Episodes 7 and 8 together on Friday, November 27. With six half-hour episodes available this weekend, anyone starting from the premiere has about three hours to catch up. The U.S. run opened with two episodes and continued one at a time through November 20. Next Friday’s two-part finale means viewers can go straight from the penultimate chapter to the ending over Thanksgiving weekend.

The TV cast had considerably more room to work than the actors who first played Rapp, Carson, and the rest of the town. Writer and showrunner Kat Sandler told Entertainment Weekly that Yaga began as a play with “a cast of three in a 200-seat theater.” Sandler adapted it herself for the Canadian streamer Crave, with Rachel Talalay and David Frazee directing and Moss also serving as an executive producer. Theatre Communications Group has since published the play, so after the finale, viewers can see how Sandler pulled off the same missing-person investigation with just three people onstage.

The final two episodes of Yaga release November 27.
