Black Friday is the huge annual shopping event that takes place the day after Thanksgiving in the United States, and it’s considered the official kickoff to the holiday shopping season. The term dates back to the 1960s, referring to the point when retailers move from operating “in the red” to “in the black” thanks to massive sales.

Today, Black Friday is known for its deep discounts, limited-time offers, and doorbuster deals. Black Friday 2026 is expected to feature some of the biggest markdowns of the entire year on beauty, tech, fashion and home.

Black Friday 2026 officially falls on November 27, but many retailers now launch their early Black Friday deals well before the holiday—sometimes weeks ahead. Many of the best deals will roll out in waves, beginning in early November, intensifying over Thanksgiving week, and continuing through Cyber Monday (November 30) and Travel Tuesday (December 1).

Inventory sells out fast, so shopping early is the best way to secure top discounts before they disappear!
