Bella Hadid Says She Wants "Lots" of Kids Amid Nathan Lacy Romance

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Bella Hadid shared her desires to become a mom and have "lots" of kids amid her romance with boyfriend Nathan Lacy: "My intuition is screaming so loudly at me about it."

Bella Hadid is building her own slice of heaven.

The Victoria's Secret Angel, who recently hard launched her relationship with cowboy Nathan Lacy, detailed her desires to become a mom, sharing that she's ready to settle down in a quieter life.

“I’ve always wanted to start a farm-to-table bakery, with flowers, and everything comes from the community," Hadid told Harper's Bazaar in an interview published Nov. 23. "I want to do things that are fulfilling for me in my soul: having a greenhouse that I can garden in, having my glassblowing and ceramic studio, and having lots of children and being a mother."

“It wasn’t a dream that I’ve always had, but it’s something that now is so intense for me," she continued. "My intuition is screaming so loudly at me about it."

In fact, the supermodel—who previously dated The Weeknd, art director Marc Kalman and rodeo star Adan Banuelos—views on personal success have now shifted.

“From an external space, success is about how known you are, how much you make, how many times you can be on TV, how many covers you’re on, how many fashion shows you do. And that is really important in our industry, especially when you’re building,” she explained. “But for me, I am different.”

That's why the 30-year-old—already aunt to sister Gigi Hadid’s daughter Khai, 6, with ex Zayn Malik—is pivoting her goals. As she put it, "Success, for me, can be living in peace, in a small town, with a man that I love, with my child, having my animals."

So much so, Hadid has already embraced her boyfriend's homebase of Colorado with open arms. Most recently, with a PDA-filled vintage shopping outing around Aspen.

The couple also enjoyed a hockey date night at the Detroit Red Wings vs. Colorado Avalanche game, with Hadid captioning photos of herself and Lacy on Instagram Nov. 9, "Saturday night was especially meaningful."

"I’ve always loved hockey living in NYC," she added. "And being a Colorado girl at heart, growing up here since i was young, I guess it was only a matter of time before I found my way to the @coloradoavalanche. "
