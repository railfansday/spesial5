Sadio Mane hails Cas decision to reinstate Senegal as Afcon champions

Sadio Mane has reacted to the seismic decision by the Court of Arbitration for Sport (Cas) to reinstate Senegal as Africa Cup of Nations 2025 champions, saying the verdict “fills us with joy”.

Senegal contested the Confederation of African Football's (Caf) decision to declare Morocco champions despite the Teranga Lions winning the Afcon final 1-0 after extra time in Rabat this January.

The final was fraught with controversy after Senegal staged a walk-off in protest to a penalty was awarded against them in the dying stages of normal time, with players ordered into the dressing room by manager Pape Thiaw, where they remained for 14 minutes.

Play was eventually resumed before Morocco’s Brahim Diaz missed the spot-kick, with Senegal going on to win the match in extra time through Pape Gueye.

Caf’s appeal board stripped Senegal of the title two months later, ruling that they had forfeited the match by walking off the pitch, and awarded Morocco a 3-0 victory.

Senegal responded by lodging an appeal with Cas in March, with sport’s highest court ruling in their favour and restoring the original 1-0 victory in a verdict on Tuesday.

Teranga Lions captain Mane posted a carousel of photos of him and his teammates celebrating the victory on Instagram, alongside the caption: “The decision of the Court of Arbitration for Sport to restore Senegal to its title of champion of Africa fills us with joy.

“I want to first say thank you to all Senegalese people, at home and in the diaspora. You have supported us from the first match to this decision. Your trust has given us strength during the most difficult moments.

“Thank you to my teammates. I know the effort and the sacrifices that everyone has made for this second star. I’m proud to have lived this adventure by your side. Thank you also to all the staff and those who have supported the Lions.

“I send a message of respect and encouragement to the people of Morocco and the Moroccan players. They had a great run and can be proud of themselves. I wish them the best for the future. Senegal and Morocco are two brother-peoples. May passion for football continue to bring us together.

“Congratulations to all Senegalese. This victory is also yours.”

Senegal’s federation was also quick to applaud the ruling, issuing a statement saying: “The Senegalese Football Federation welcomes this sentence with deep satisfaction”.

The saga, which has now been running for nearly a year, is still unlikely to end now, with Morocco’s Football Federation vowing to appeal the Cas verdict.
