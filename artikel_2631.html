Abdul Carter: I showed I'm the best in the world

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Giants head coach John Harbaugh said last week that he was confident sacks would start to come for linebacker Abdul Carter and he was proven correct on Sunday.

Advanced stats have graded Carter well as a pass rusher all season, but he came into Sunday’s game against the Jaguars with two sacks and eight quarterback hits on the year. Carter doubled his sack total against Jacksonville, hit Trevor Lawrence five times and was credited with 12 pressures to help the Giants to a 23-18 win that maintained their breathing room ahead of the Cowboys in the NFC East.

After the game, Carter was asked what he showed during the victory.

“I’m the best in the world,” Carter said.

Others will have other opinions about that question, but the Giants will be happy to reap the benefits of Carter’s continued efforts to prove that point in the weeks, months and years to come.
