Mike McCarthy: Aaron Rodgers is our quarterback

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Before an NFL team benches its starting quarterback, someone asks the coach whether the quarterback could be benched.

That happened for the first time during Aaron Rodgers's two-year tenure with the team on Monday, during a press conference with coach Mike McCarthy, one day after the team's latest loss, to the Eagles.

It came up as innocuously as possible. McCarthy wasn't asked whether Rodgers could get the hook; McCarthy was asked whether he's considering using one of the other three quarterbacks on the roster in an effort to mix things up when the offense is struggling.

"Aaron Rodgers is our quarterback," McCarthy said.

Of course he is. Even if Rodgers's play merits benching, Aaron Rodgers isn't getting benched. It's implicit in the arrangement.

Still, Aaron Rodgers is a far cry from what he once was. In 10 games this season, Rodgers has completed 220 of 361 passes for 2,241 yards, with 16 touchdown passes and six interceptions. His passer rating of 86.6 is the lowest of his career since becoming Green Bay's starter in 2008, with the exception of a four-snap performance in 2023 with the Jets (39.6, based on one incomplete pass thrown).

At 6-4, the Steelers remain in contention for another one-and-out playoff appearance. That makes it easier to justify staying the course. With upcoming games against the Broncos, Texans, and Jaguars, that could change.

Even then, Rodgers would never be benched. He'd be "injured." And his career would end not with a bang but with a whimper.
