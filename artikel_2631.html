Packers make 5 roster moves, don’t place Josh Jacobs on IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Green Bay Packers, as expected, had to make a couple of roster moves on Saturday to piece together a 48-man game-day roster for Sunday. Today, the Packers announced the activations of running back Chris Brooks and receiver Skyy Moore from the injured reserve. To make room for those additions on the 53-man active roster, Green Bay placed tight end Josh Whyle (concussion) on the injured reserve and released receiver Coleman Owen. On top of these moves, the Packers also elevated tight end Drake Dabney from the practice squad for a second consecutive week.

Brooks gives Green Bay five running backs on their 53-man roster at the moment, as Josh Jacobs, who had surgery on his broken finger on Saturday, was not placed on the injured reserve. Jacobs will miss Sunday's game against the Minnesota Vikings, meaning the Packers are likely to carry three of their four healthy running backs — Brooks, MarShawn Lloyd, Pierre Strong Jr. and Kaleb Johnson — into tomorrow's action.

Moore is a slot receiver and a returner, the latter being a role that Owen filled while Moore was on the IR. Moore took over as the Packers' starting slot receiver once Jayden Reed had his season-ending neck injury. Moore was later replaced by Savion Williams. It will be interesting to see if Moore takes over in the slot this week or if Williams will continue in that spot.

Below is the full breakdown of the Packers' 54 roster options going into Week 10:

RB – 5: Josh Jacobs, Chris Brooks, MarShawn Lloyd, Pierre Strong Jr. and Kaleb Johnson

WR – 6: Christian Watson, Matthew Golden, Skyy Moore, Savion Williams, Bo Melton and J. Michael Sturdivant

TE – 4: Tucker Kraft, Mark Redman, Jonnu Smith and Drake Dabney

OL – 11: Jordan Morgan, Aaron Banks, Sean Rhyan, Laken Tomlinson, Anthony Belton, Kevin Zeitler, John Williams, Travis Glover, Mekhi Becton Sr., Jager Burton and Donovan Jennings

DL – 5: Karl Brooks, Devonte Wyatt, Chris McClellan, Jordon Riley and Anthony Campbell

EDGE – 4: Lukas Van Ness, Barryn Sorrell, Brenton Cox Jr. and Dani-Dennis Sutton

LB – 5: Zaire Franklin, Isaiah McDuffie, Ty'Ron Hopper, Nick Niemann and Kristian Welch

CB – 5: Keisean Nixon, Brandon Cisse, Carrington Valentine, Benjamin St-Juste and Kamal Hadden

S – 4: Xavier McKinney, Evan Williams, Javon Bullard and Kitan Oladapo
