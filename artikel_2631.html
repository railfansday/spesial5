LeBron James’ latest triple-double gives him more than 4 NBA legends’ combined total

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The NBA continues to witness history from LeBron James, who added another remarkable achievement to his résumé during his record-breaking 24th season. The Philadelphia 76ers star recorded the 126th triple-double of his career on Wednesday, putting him ahead of four basketball legends combined in the category.

The 41-year-old finished with 25 points, 12 assists and 10 rebounds in Philadelphia's 111-104 victory over the Miami Heat. James now has more career triple-doubles than Larry Bird, Michael Jordan, Kobe Bryant and Scottie Pippen combined.

It was his first triple-double of the 2026-27 season, and at 41 years and 330 days old, James broke his own record as the oldest player in NBA history to accomplish the feat. His 25 points also marked a season-high.

The four-time NBA champion and four-time league MVP showed he still has plenty left in the tank against Miami. James scored 14 consecutive points in the second quarter and knocked down four of his six attempts from beyond the three-point line.

Speaking after the game, James joked about his ability to deliver when his team needs him.

"If they need me to dust off the cape from time to time, I can still accomplish that," James said.

Heat head coach Erik Spoelstra, who coached James to back-to-back NBA championships in Miami in 2012 and 2013, also praised the veteran's continued excellence.

"Father Time always wins," Spoelstra said. "But right now, he's giving Father Time hell."

Spoelstra added that what James has accomplished during this stage of his career represents "another form of brilliance."

James will turn 42 on December 30, but his production remains impressive as he continues to compete at the highest level.

Through his first 15 games with Philadelphia this season, James is averaging 17.1 points, 6.9 assists and 6.9 rebounds per contest.

With 126 career triple-doubles already recorded, James continues to separate himself from generations of NBA greats. His latest performance serves as another reminder that even in his 24th season, he remains capable of producing historic performances.
