Miami Dolphins Fans: Your Week 10 Vs Indianapolis Colts Predictions

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

With our Miami Dolphins traveling this week to take on the Indianapolis Colts tomorrow, I asked the following question this past week:

What are your predictions, bold or otherwise, for the Miami Dolphins' defense and offense in this coming Sunday's game vs. the Indianapolis Colts? Who do you expect to lead the team in key categories like tackles, tackles for loss, pass breakups, interceptions, defensive touchdowns, etc., on defense, and receiving yards, touchdowns, receptions, rushing production, and first downs, etc., on offense? What are your stat-line projections for our key playmakers this week, and/or who will be the team's MVPs on each side of the ball?

Below are some of your thoughts and predictions for tomorrow's game-

sdphinsfan sees this team's young and improving defense leading the Phins to their second win.

Over the last month, our young Defense has been ascending on a really nice arc. I'm going out on a limb to say they play lights out this week and they win this game. Time for a Defensive touchdown and I say JRod or Johnson deliver. The Offense has the potential to be effective this week, even though we have no clue what we'll get from McCord. I think this OL hears the noise and criticism…so challenge them…run the ball, run it again, and run it some more. The Colts won't be able to hold up. Granted, watching paint dry is probably more exciting, but when you're coaching and developing a young team, you look for opportunities like this to challenge them to deliver.

McCord throws for over 300 yds and 3 TDs in relief of Willis as the Dolphins beat the Colts 31-17….not gonna happen. I figure this will be a pretty low scoring game especially for the Dolphins who are 31st in passing yds with Willis.

A matchup of two of the worst teams in the NFL. The Dolphins offense will rely on their running game and short passes; the Colts know this and will game plan accordingly. The Dolphins may still be able to pull this one out with a big day on defense which must shut down RB Jonathon Taylor and force Daniels into throwing interceptions that result in short field situations.

The Dolphins offensive line has to give McCord maximum protection which is possible since the Colts pass rush is weak. No chance if the Dolphins turn the ball over and continue their penalty dance party resulting in long yardage situations.

One thing is certain is that the Dolphins great special team's unit cannot give up long returns to the Colts. Yes, I'm being sarcastic with "great".

Shutting down Taylor will be the key; however Daniel Jones will be the difference factor despite a rough season, and the one-dimensional offense of the Dolphins will struggle with McCord as a first-time starter. I hate to be negative however; reality tells me that the Dolphins are going to go winless with McCord at QB.
