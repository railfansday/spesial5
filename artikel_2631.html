SV Academy: Pressing Made Simple

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

von MR am 25.03.2025 in den Kategorien SV Academy,Tactical Theory mit 0 Kommentaren

We’ve made defending complicated – now we’re making it simple again!

In this seminar, we try to break down the topic of pressing in a way that makes it easy to understand and describe, but also more efficient to coach. Recommended for fans, analysts and coaches!

Date, Duration & Cost The seminar will last around two hours plus Q&A afterwards. The seminar will take place online via Zoom. The price is 40€.

The recordings of the seminars will also be available (same price) and are included for all participants. Teaser: We will also be launching a detailed series on defending soon.

To register or to purchase the recording, just send an informal email with your name and desired date(s)/recordings to [email protected]. Payment options are PayPal and bank transfer.

Included in the price are the participation in the online-seminar, the discussion afterwards and receiving the seminar-recording and the presentation-slides.

Further communication will be done via mail.

Martin Rafelt is Spielverlagerung-writer and co-owner since 2011, has published a book about Jürgen Klopp, is a freelance writer for Der Spiegel, has done analysis for Thomas Tuchel and worked for three years as an assistant coach at Youth-League-finalists Hajduk Split (Under-19 and Hajduk B) and as the Head of Analysis and Tactics at the Hajduk Academy. In The 3rd Circle he discusses football philosophy.

He gave lectures at the Belgian and the Dutch FAs, works with professional players in individual analysis and as a tactical consultant for clubs. On Spox and Transfermarkt there can be found interviews.

If you would like to be informed about future events, you are welcome to join our mail newsletter or info groups on WhatsApp or Telegram. For this, too, just send an informal mail to [email protected]. (Also possible if you are not interested in the current seminar.)

We also offer our Academy events for clubs. Exact details and prices will be arranged individually. If your club is interested, please contact us with your wishes at [email protected].
