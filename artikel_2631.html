Renner's post-deadline 2027 NFL mock draft: How the trade deadline affected the first three rounds

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

Dante Moore, Julian Sayin, CJ Carr, LaNorris Sellers -- what do all four of those quarterbacks have in common? Well none of them appear in this mock draft because all of them have more years of eligibility. With Sayin and Carr I think physical development and mastery are needed for them to get drafted where they want to. For Moore it's a full healthy season after his scary head injury last weekend. And for Sellers, well, there's a lot that needs to change.

While those four returning to school makes this "loaded" quarterback class far less exciting, the draft is still loaded at other positions -- especially the ones that get paid the big bucks. Three receivers, two offensive tackles, and two edge rushers go within the top 10 of what's shaping up to be a blue-chip-heavy draft class.

That certainly bodes well for teams like the Philadelphia Eagles and Tennessee Titans, who picked up some top-end draft capital in the weeks leading up to the NFL trade deadline. They now join the New York Jets and Cleveland Browns as teams with multiple first-round picks in the 2027 draft. Tennessee, the Atlanta Falcons, and the Arizona Cardinals also picked up second-round picks over the last few weeks. With the dust settled after the deadline, let's take a look at how those new picks are being spent.

One last thing, and this is important: The draft order was determined using the reverse Super Bowl odds at DraftKings Sportsbook. The Jets have three first-round picks: their own, the Cowboys' from the Quinnen Williams trade and the Colts' from the Sauce Gardner trade. The Cowboys have the Packers' selection from the Micah Parsons trade. The Browns have the Rams' first from the Myles Garrett trade. There have also been a total of 18 trades made before the deadline, nine of which involved picks in the first three rounds of this draft.
