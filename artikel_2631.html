Sean McVay: Puka Nacua is obviously a very important piece for us now and for the future

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Rams receiver Puka Nacua just underwent successful knee surgery that is likely going to sideline him for the rest of 2026.

But Nacua is also a pending free agent who is currently serving a three-game suspension for violating the league’s personal conduct policy.

All of these elements complicate Nacua’s future with the Rams.

Still in the middle of the season and preparing to play the Packers on Wednesday, head coach Sean McVay told reporters on Monday that he’s focused on the immediacy of the week ahead when asked about Nacua being a part of the team in 2027.

“He’s obviously a guy that is a very important piece for us for now and for the future, but there are a lot of guys that fall under that category,” McVay said, via transcript from the team. “I can’t say for certain anything. Here’s what I’m not interested in is something being taken out of context because my focus and concentration is on a ‘Friday’ practice, even though I know it’s Monday. I am trying to be able to see how well we can play as a team against an excellent opponent on Wednesday night.

“When you go through the amount of free agents that we have, Puka is a huge part of the conversation that you love, but there are also guys that are part of his draft class. There are guys on our offensive line, there are some guys defensively — so there are a lot of guys that are up that as of right now would not be under contract. There are mechanics that we’d like to be able to try to figure out how to keep these really good players and people that we love and certainly Puka is at the forefront of our mind.”

McVay also noted that Nacua is expected to be healthy by the start of the 2027 season. But there are layers to how the Rams and Nacua will go about navigating the potential continuation of their partnership.

“You love the human,” McVay said. “There are a lot of different things that he’s navigated. Some of the stuff that’s recent were things that have been dealt with that was just working through. That’s what’s hard for him because we’re dealing with things, but then there are actual things that come out in the public that go back to New Year’s last year. You guys know, like every player, the relationship that we established with our guys, a lot of love. A great competitor, great person where I trust his heart like I’ve told you guys.

“I think you’re watching young guys, no different than…I’m continuing to try to mature as a man, figure out how do you navigate these responsibilities and all parts of your life, being a professional. I think he’s growing and I trust his heart. I think he’s making a lot of steps that are in the right direction. I’m bummed for him that he got injured. It was a tough year because he was banged up and playing through stuff even more so than normal with the style of play. Then obviously his season is over now with regards to him getting that surgery but I do expect him to return and be back in a couple of months and ready to go.”

A fifth-round pick in the 2023 draft, Nacua has been one of the league’s most effective receivers since he entered the league. Last season he led the NFL with 129 receptions, going for 1,715 yards with 10 touchdowns. He’ll likely finish 2026 with 70 receptions for 715 yards with four TDs.
