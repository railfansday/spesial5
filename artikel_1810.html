How Nigeria can qualify for AFCON 2027 after Tanzania draw

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Nigeria’s hopes of qualifying for next summer’s Africa Cup of Nations have taken a further hit, after a disappointing goalless draw with Tanzania.

The Super Eagles, who back in the autumn international break were beaten 3-0 by Guinea-Bissau at the Estadio 24 de Setembro, appeared to have got back on track with victory over Tanzania last Friday.

A 3-1 win in Uyo - achieved without Victor Osimhen - coupled with Guinea-Bissau being held to a surprise draw away at Madagascar, was the perfect scenario for Nigeria heading into the reverse fixture at the Amaan Stadium on Tuesday afternoon.

However, Eric Chelle’s surprising decision to start without Alex Iwobi and Ola Aina in Zanzibar ultimately backfired. Despite dominating possession and creating numerous chances, the Super Eagles simply could not find a breakthrough and will look back at the game as two points dropped.

How costly a 0-0 draw against a side ranked 117th in the world actually is will become clearer after Guinea-Bissau face Madagascar, though it is unlikely that Nigeria will get two favours in the space of four days.

Now, while the result moves Nigeria level on points with Guinea-Bissau in their AFCON 2027 qualification group, they have played a game more and it’s important to remember a runners-up spot may not be enough to advance to next summer’s tournament.

This is because Tanzania have already qualified for AFCON 2027 by virtue of being co-hosts alongside Kenya and Uganda.

By losing to Guinea-Bissau and drawing with Tanzania in their two away matches so far, Nigeria simply have no margin for error from here on in.

The likely scenario, regardless of the other result on matchday four, is they will have to take six points from their final two matches.

Should Guinea-Bissau beat Madagascar and Tanzania, it will set up a winner-take-all match between the two nations in March 2027 where the Super Eagles will almost certainly find themselves needing to win - and by at least three clear goals.
