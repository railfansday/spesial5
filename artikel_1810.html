Arsenal told new asking price for 'key January transfer target'

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Arsenal are expected to explore attacking signings when the transfer window reopens in January, with Antonio Nusa a name prominently linked.

Arteta’s options on the wing were reduced in the last transfer window, with Leandro Trossard and Gabriel Martinelli leaving the club.

Greece international Christos Tzolis was brought in from Club Brugge but has struggled with injuries, with the Gunners unsuccessful in their pursuit to add another winger, failing to convince Morgan Rogers or Real Madrid star Vinicius to sign.

Reigning champions, the Gunners sit top of the Premier League but are yet to blow teams away, relying one again on their defensive strength to grind out wins by a single goal.

German newspaper Bild report that Arsenal have already sent scouts to take in Nusa’s recent performances in the Bundesliga.

The 21-year-old had a solid World Cup, helping Norway to reach the quarter finals, and so far this campaign Nusa has scored four goals and set-up a further seven for teammates in nine Bundesliga games.

It is reported that Arsenal previously tracked the winger prior to his move to Germany, Leipzig paying Club Brugge €21m for his services in 2024.

Arsenal’s fellow Londoners Brentford pulled out of a move for the then-teenaged Nusa, their total package worth €37m before doubts over the winger’s back and knees during the medical led to the Bees cancelling the transfer.

Leipzig are said to be reluctant to sell in the winter transfer window, explaining why the Saxony-based outfit have slapped a €80m (£67m) asking price on their asset. This is more than the £50m fee that was mooted recently.

The Gunners have previously made three signings for €80m or more, including summer recruit Bruno Guimaraes. Arsenal paid Newcastle €87.5m (£75m) for the Brazilian, making him the second-most expensive player in Gunners’ history after fellow midfielder Declan Rice, who transferred to north London from West Ham for £105m in 2023.
