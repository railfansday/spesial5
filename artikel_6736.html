How Many People Will Get Sent Home During the ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Tonight? Here’s How the Semifinals Work

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

To quote the immortal words of Mariah Carey: It’s tiiiiiiiiiiiiiime. The Dancing With the Stars season 35 semifinals have arrived, and the show’s six remaining couples are stepping it up with multiple performances. Here’s what to expect during tonight’s episode, and how many couples will be going home.

Most likely, the show will eliminate just one couple during tonight’s semifinals—which means five couples will be in the finals next week.

However, it’s possible that the judges will decide to keep everyone. Host Alfonso Ribeiro was asked about the possibility of six couples being in the finale back in September and had this to say:

Just FYI, everyone was safe during the season 32 and season 33 semifinals, but the season 31 semifinals included a double elimination. In other words, anything could happen!

All signs point to yes! That said, there have been reports that Maura had to postpone/cancel several rehearsals due to injury.

“She reinjured the rib over the weekend and had to postpone another rehearsal and get looked at by medics again,” a source told The Sun. “She’s trying to give it her all but giving it her all is costing her physically. Her body is hanging on by a thread but she’s more determined than ever to push through.”

The six remaining couples will perform two dances each tonight: a Latin dance and a ballroom dance.

Connor and Arnold: a Jive to “Blue Suede Shoes” by Elvis Presley and a Quickstep to “Dance Dance” by Fall Out Boy.

Amber and Pasha: a Salsa to “Bemba Colorá” by Celia Cruz and a Viennese waltz to “Dangerous Woman” by Ariana Grande

Tatyana and Jan: a Cha-cha to “I Like It Like That” by Pete Rodríguez and a Waltz to “Open Arms” by Journey

Harry and Jenna: a Paso doble to “We Found Love” by Rihanna feat. Calvin Harris and a Quickstep to “Valerie” by Mark Ronson ft. Amy Winehouse

Ezra and Daniella: a Jive to “Footloose” by Kenny Loggins and a Quickstep to “Kryptonite” by 3 Doors Down

Maura and Mark: a Samba to “Samba Vocalizado” by Luciano Perrone and a Viennese waltz to “Shrike” by Hozier

Dancing With the Stars airs its semifinals on Tuesday, November 17, at 8 p.m. ET on Disney+ and ABC.
