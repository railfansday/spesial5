Jennifer Lopez in Her ‘Happy Era,’ Has Recorded 20 New Songs Since Kids Left For College: ‘Unencumbered With Anything Unhealthy’

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The singer also gave a preview of her upcoming Thanksgiving special The JLo Show: Live in Vegas on Sunday night's (Nov. 15) Tonight Show.

Jennifer Lopez is definitely feeling the joy and pain of being an empty nester. The singer has been talking a lot lately about the rush of emotions she’s been feeling since her twin children with ex Marc Anthony, Emme (who now goes by Oskar) and Max, 18, went off to college.

On Sunday night’s (Nov. 15) special edition of The Tonight Show, the singer found the one upside of being home alone: she’s been cranking out new music at an epic pace. “I’m alone, I’m free and I can do whatever the f–k I want,” she told host Jimmy Fallon. “It’s kind of like amazing, but also weird but also amazing.”

Lopez, 57, said this fall has been the first time since she was in her early 20s that she hasn’t either been with someone or had her kids at home. And while she, of course misses them — one of the twins was actually backstage at the show on Sunday — Lopez said she’s in her “happy era,” explaining that, “When the kids were leaving I was having so many feelings and so many emotions that I went into the studio and started kind of like playing around and making music. And now I’ve made like 20 songs and I feel like I’m only halfway done. Like, I need to be in there longer.”

The good news is that there are already a couple certified bangers that Lopez said she’s excited about sharing with fans. “This does feel like a happy era where I feel unencumbered with anything unhealthy. It’s just like a very healthy time in my life. I’m on my own,” she said. “I feel like it’s another coming of age that happens in our lives. I think a lot of parents will relate to that. It happens when you leave home for the first time — my kids are having it right now, they’re coming of age — and you have it again when your kids leave. Because for 20 years that’s all it’s about, you know? And now it’s just me. So it’s a very happy time in a lot of ways even though it had a lot of other emotions involved with it.”

In addition to doing a very silly holiday wig swap gag (see below) with Fallon in which the pair sang a Christmas song with increasingly more ridiculous hairpieces, Lopez plugged her upcoming Thanksgiving night TV special, The JLo Show: Live in Vegas. The show that will air on CBS and Paramount+ on Nov. 26 was taped during the singer’s 12-show Las Vegas residency that wrapped up on March 28 of this year.

She said it will be a real “full-circle moment” for her because when she was little her mother would wait for West Side Story to air around Thanksgiving so the whole family could watch the original 1961 movie version starring Natalie Wood and Rita Morenob together. “It inspired me to want to sing and dance and act, not just one thing, you know?” she said.

That’s why the Vegas show incorporated a lot of Lopez’s inspirations, including the musicals she watched as a kid with her mom, which she melded into her hits. “It sounds crazy, but it works,” she assured viewers. “Just trust me. It is a beautiful thing.”

Lopez also teased that she plans to hit the road again next year. “I will be going on tour next summer, I believe,” she said of the follow-up to her 2025 Up All Night world tour and subsequent Las Vegas residency of the same name at the Colosseum at Caesars Palace from Dec. 2025 to March 2026.
