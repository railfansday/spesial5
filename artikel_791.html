Lily Tomlin Reveals Dolly Parton’s Surprising 9 to 5 Preparation

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Lily Tomlin exclusively shared with E! News what she remembered most from working with the late Dolly Parton on 1980's 9 to 5: "She hit with all chargers blazing."

Lily Tomlin will always love her time with Dolly Parton.

Over two months after the country legend died at the age of 80 following a brief battle with cancer, Tomlin shared what it was like for her and Jane Fonda to costar with Parton on 9 to 5—especially when it came to how the Grammy winner prepared for her role in the 1980 comedy.

“She came on the set, she'd memorized the whole script because she thought she was supposed to, and she hit with all chargers blazing,” Tomlin told E! News’ Will Marfuggi in an exclusive interview. “Jane and I looked at each other, ‘Dolly is so great.’ And she was. She was great.”

The Grace & Frankie alum—who was joined by friend and Laugh-In creator George Schlatter for the interview—also expressed how grateful she was to have Parton as a friend for so many years, describing what being so close to the “Jolene” singer meant to her.

“It meant the innocence that was totally Dolly,” Tomlin raved. “The absolute perfection that she brought to her look—her everything.”

“That was like out of the genesis of something brilliantly thought out and wholesome, because she was that person,” the 87-year-old continued. “She just delivered the essence of that to the audience.”

As Tomlin mused, it was a feat only Parton could have achieved, quipping that while Parton was “extremely well endowed,” she managed to also be “totally innocent.”

Tomlin added, “You could totally see the innocence in her.”

Of course, her friendship with Parton wasn’t the only Hollywood relationship the Oscar nominee cultivated over the years, as she’s still very close with Schlatter over 50 years after starring on Laugh-In. Their relationship is one she speaks of fondly in the upcoming documentary Sock It to Me: The Legend of George Schlatter, which will be available to rent or buy on all digital platforms beginning Dec. 8.

And for his part, Schlatter is grateful to have cast Tomlin all those years ago.

“You just walked in the room and the room lit up,” he recalled. “The room just elevated into another space where it stayed, and Lily became a major, major part of my life, my career, and my everything.”

“When you think back about that time, it just happened,” the 96-year-old said with amazement. “It was like a magical, magical moment.”
