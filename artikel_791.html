Violet Witchel Won’t Judge You for These Thanksgiving Dinner Shortcuts

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Thanksgiving is practically here, and we turned to content creator and cookbook author Violet Witchel for some last-minute hosting tips, dinner shortcuts and more.

While we're getting ready to shop until we drop on Black Friday, let's not forget about what comes first: Thanksgiving. The holiday that's all about food and family can also come with a side of stress, especially if you're hosting.

We chatted exclusively with Violet Witchel, content creator and cookbook author, whose dense bean salads have totally transformed how I think about meal prepping for the week. And she came through with the best tips to help us with hosting, prepping food and even being a great holiday guest!

But if you're feeling stressed about your Thanksgiving menu and how you can get all the cooking and prep work done in time for dinner... don't be! Because guess what, even chefs like Violet pick up a few pre-made ingredients to help save time.

"Pre-made pie crust, mixes - like if you're going to make cornbread, get cornbread mix. Canned cranberry sauce, totally fine," she said of the items she'd get from the store. "Pre-bought stuffing, like the mix, totally fine. Pre-minced vegetables, totally fine."

But one thing you won't catch her using? Powdered mashed potatoes. "I think on Thanksgiving, you've gotta do that from scratch."

And whether you're team pumpkin, apple or pecan, supporting a local bakery is not only tasty but also a time-saver she added. Don't forget the canned whipped cream and your favorite ice cream!
