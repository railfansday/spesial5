John Mulaney Reveals Parenting Hack for Teaching Son to Ride a Bike

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Madden star John Mulaney revealed to E! News the unusual way that he taught his and Olivia Munn's 4-year-old son Malcolm how to ride a bike.

And after nearly five years as a dad under his belt, the training weeks are certainly off when it comes to parenthood—especially around the holidays. In fact, the Everybody's Live with John Mulaney host is really looking forward" to the upcoming holiday season, which is also when his son will celebrate his birthday.

"He's gonna be 5," he shared. "My son has his demands, but he's very good at communicating them. If I say, 'You seem really frustrated about this,' he can kinda collect himself and say, 'I just want you to leave, because Mama will let me watch more TV.' And I go, 'OK, so you don't hate me, I just won't let you watch Duggee,' or whatever the hell."

Mulaney also shared why he took on the role of Electronic Arts founder Trip Hawkins in Madden, which tells the story of John Madden and the eponymous video game empire he spawned.

"What's funny about the game itself—and this is touched on in the movie, but this was really true in the way they looked at it," he said. "This sounds like wishful thinking, but they kind of they realized the game would make kids want to play football and go outside."

"Hard to predict when you're designing a video game that this will bring people outside again," Mulaney added, "but they really did want to design a game that got people so involved in football and young people in football that they go, 'This is great! I want to take these plays outside.'"
