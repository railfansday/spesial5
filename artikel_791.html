Olivia Rodrigo’s ‘you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love’ Scores Fifth Week at No. 1 on Billboard 200

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The set ties SOUR for her longest reign. Plus, Lil Baby and P1Harmony debut in the top five.

Olivia Rodrigo’s you seem pretty said for a girl so in love notches a fifth nonconsecutive week at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 chart (dated Nov. 21). The set now ties with SOUR for Rodrigo’s most weeks atop the list among her three leaders. Explore Lil Baby Olivia Rodrigo P1Harmony See latest videos, charts and news

Further, you seem pretty sad… now has the most weeks at No. 1 in 2026 for an album released this year, surpassing Drake’s four-week stint with ICEMAN. Rodrigo also ties Taylor Swift’s 2025 release The Life of a Showgirl for the most weeks at No. 1 this year among all albums. (Showgirl notched five of its 15 weeks at No. 1 in 2026.) In the tracking week ending Nov. 12, you seem pretty sad… earned 62,000 equivalent album units in the United States (up 9%) according to Luminate.

Also in the top 10, Lil Baby clocks his eighth top 10-charted effort as FRESH starts at No. 2, while P1Harmony achieves its third top 10-charted set with the No. 4 bow of BLOW UP.

The Billboard 200 chart ranks the most popular albums of the week in the U.S. based on multi-metric consumption as measured in equivalent album units, compiled by Luminate. Units comprise album sales, track equivalent albums (TEA) and streaming equivalent albums (SEA). Each unit equals one album sale, or 10 individual tracks sold from an album, or 2,500 ad-supported or 1,000 paid/subscription on-demand official audio and video streams generated by songs from an album. The new Nov. 21, 2026-dated chart will be posted in full on Billboard‘s website on Nov. 17. For all chart news, follow @billboard and @billboardcharts on both X and Instagram.

Of you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love’s 62,000 equivalent album units earned in the latest tracking week, SEA units comprise 51,000 (up 2%, equaling 54.48 million on-demand official streams of the set’s tracks; it falls 1-2 on Top Streaming Albums), album sales comprise 11,000 (up 53%; it rises 11-5 on Top Album Sales), and TEA units comprise a negligible sum (down 7%).

Lil Baby bounds in at No. 2 on the latest Billboard 200 with FRESH, scoring his eighth top 10-charted project — all of which have debuted in the top five. The album launches with 59,000 equivalent album units earned. Of that sum, SEA units comprise 55,000 (equaling 56.81 million on-demand official streams, it debuts at No. 1 on Top Streaming Albums), album sales comprise 4,000 (it debuts at No. 33 on Top Album Sales) and TEA units comprise a negligible sum.

FRESH was announced Oct. 20 and released on Nov. 6. The set was preceded by its lead single, “Dead Fresh,” which marked the rapper’s 28th top 20-charted hit on the all-genre Billboard Hot 100, debuting at its No. 18 high August.

Morgan Wallen’s former leader I’m the Problem is a non-mover on the new Billboard 200 at No. 3, earning 50,000 equivalent album units (up 2%).

P1Harmony’s BLOW UP bows at No. 4 on the latest Billboard 200, equaling the pop group’s highest rank ever. It matches the debut and peak of UNIQUE in March. All told, BLOW UP marks the ensemble’s third top 10-charted set. The act also scored a top 10 with EX (No. 9) in 2025.

BLOW UP debuts with 45,000 equivalent album units earned in the tracking week ending Nov. 12. Of that sum, album sales comprise 43,500 (it debuts at No. 1 on Top Album Sales, the act’s first leader on that chart), SEA units comprise 1,500 (equaling 1.56 million on-demand official streams of the set’s songs) and TEA units comprise a negligible sum. The set’s first-week sales were powered by its availability across 25 CD variants and five vinyl variants (all containing collectible items such as photocards and posters, with some items randomized).

Ella Langley’s chart-topping Dandelion dips 4-5 on the latest Billboard 200 (45,000 equivalent album units earned, down 1%), Noah Kahan’s former leader The Great Divide is down a spot to No. 6 (44,000, up 10%) and Kahan’s Stick Season shifts 8-7 (35,000, up 8%). Olivia Dean’s The Art of Loving skips 12-8 (32,000, up 14%), SZA’s former No. 1 SOS climbs 11-9 (31,000, up 6%) and Michael Jackson’s chart-topping Thriller falls 2-10 (29,000, down 47% — coming down from its Halloween-inspired 13-2 jump a week ago).

Luminate, the independent data provider to the Billboard charts, completes a thorough review of all data submissions used in compiling the weekly chart rankings. Luminate reviews and authenticates data. In partnership with Billboard, data deemed suspicious or unverifiable is removed, using established criteria, before final chart calculations are made and published.
