Recently sacked EFL boss backs ‘really impressive’ West Brom to maintain promotion push

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

West Brom are currently sat in 5th place in the Championship table after 15 fixtures. WBA are in the play-offs along with Stoke City, Wolves, Swansea City, Southampton and QPR, with West Ham and Sheffield United chasing them down.

The Baggies turned to James Morrison as their head coach earlier this year. He has since done an eye-catching job in charge at The Hawthorns.

Woodman, who has recently been sacked by Bromley in League One, has delivered his honest verdict on the Midlands outfit on the latest episode of BBC’s 72+ Podcast: “We played West Brom in pre-season out in Portugal. Let me tell you, I was very impressed. I was impressed with him (Morrison), his staff and the squad. The squad looked strong, fit. Of course they’re going to be levels above us.

“They had some good young players playing against us. Just the whole way they conducted themselves. I remember saying to my staff after the game, I said ‘they’ll be close this year’. You could just feel it and smell it. They looked really impressive pre-season.”

West Brom goalkeeper Max O’Leary joined just under 12 months ago and he has since proved to be a safe pair of hands. WBA’s number one is an experienced operator in the EFL and was previously at Bristol City.

The Baggies are solid at the back and only league leaders Birmingham City have a better defensive record. George Campbell is a key player for them in defence, whilst the likes of Nat Phillips and Chris Mepham have a lot of know-how.

Further forward, Ousmane Diakite has become one of their most prized assets in the middle of the park. He is energetic and gets across the pitch well.

West Brom have some dangerous attacking players at their disposal including Isaac Price and Aune Heggebo. Meanwhile, Jimmy-Jay Morgan was snapped up from Chelsea in the summer and he has a very bright future ahead of him in the game.

West Brom are a tough nut to crack and have threats at the top end of the pitch. WBA also have a bright coach in Morrison who has worked wonders so far.

The Baggies are unbeaten in their last six outings. Therefore, their confidence will be sky high and they will fancy their chances against anyone.

Morrison’s men won 3-0 at home against Millwall last time out before the international break. Price, Felix Horn Myhre and Jayson Molumby got their goals against the Lions as they secured another useful three points.

They are back in action next weekend against Swansea City away. Their upcoming opponents are also in the top eight with them.

West Brom will get the chance to bolster their squad when the January transfer window opens up for business. They will also have the opportunity to let some players head out the exit door as well if needed to help free up space and funds.
