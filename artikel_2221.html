Veteran with 522 EFL caps backs Lincoln City 23-year-old for ‘a big future’

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Lincoln City have fared fairly well in their first 13 games of the 2026/27 Championship season, sitting in 16th with 15 points to their name. The Imps spent on new players like Mikkel Ladefoged and Andrei Coubis in the summer, but among those to impress on the step up from League One has been Deji Elerewe.

Now, the 23-year-old centre-back has received high praise from defensive partner and EFL veteran Sonny Bradley. Speaking to the club’s official media, he said: “I recognised it before the first couple of weeks that he came in the door. I could tell straight away in training that he was a big lad. He was aggressive, he was ready to get stuck in and everything that he was asked to do, he could do comfortably.

“I recognised pretty early that he’s got a big future in the game. He’s had a great start to the season, but it’s just the start. With the mentality that he’s got, I’m pretty sure he’ll want to take it into the rest of this year and certainly into the second half of the season.”

Having made 522 EFL appearances across all three leagues, Sonny Bradley knows what he’s talking about when it comes to impressive young prospects. Lincoln City’s stand-in skipper has played alongside and come up against plenty of top talents across his storied career, so his praise of Elerewe comes as a glowing review.

After starting out at Charlton Athletic, Elerewe made a name for himself at Bromley and earned his move to Sincil Bank in the winter. He started encouragingly, but has really stepped up in the Championship.

Elerewe has played all but six minutes of the Championship campaign so far, locking down a starting spot. He’s among the league’s top 13% of centre-backs for defensive contributions (113) and ranks highly for his interceptions (21 – top 7%), aerial duels won (40 – top 11%) and touches in the opposition box (30 – top 4%).

The Imps ace has the physical stature and power to hold his own against big strikers and he serves as an aerial asset in both boxes. At 23, he is of the age and profile to kick on further and challenge himself at a higher level in the years to come.

Elerewe’s progress at his young age means that excitement over his future is more than justified. He has the physical profile to be a success at all levels, so it will be a case of rounding out his game both defensively and in possession to push to new heights.

Lincoln City will have the firm belief that Elerewe can become a high value asset in the transfer market in the years to come. He has quickly grown into an influential player at Sincil Bank, and that will bring admiring glances from clubs higher up in the food chain.

For now though, Elerewe will be focused on the task at hand with the Imps, where he is under contract until 2029. Alongside his own personal development, the goal for this season is to stay in the Championship, and the London-born defender can have a key part to play in that.
