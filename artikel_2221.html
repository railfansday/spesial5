The Millwall team Alex Neil should start vs West Brom to recover from 3-0 midweek defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Millwall were well-beaten on Tuesday night as Birmingham City scored three goals inside 30 minutes to secure the victory. The loss sees the Lions pick up just four points from their last five games.

Alex Neil’s side had an xG greater than BCFC’s at 1.42, but errors cost them any potential result. The South London outfit were no match for the Blues’ superior defence, who have conceded just 13 times this term.

Heading to The Hawthorns on Saturday, Millwall need to pick up a result. With just one point per game on average on the road this campaign, the Lions have a lot to do against a West Brom side who are averaging 1.79 points from their last five matches.

Alex Neil will make changes after the defeat to Birmingham City. The Lions’ head coach should revert to a 4-2-3-1 shape after the 4-4-2 formation proved unstable at St Andrew’s. Max Crocombe is currently the only true senior goalkeeper that Millwall have right now.  His poor form continued in midweek, where he prevented -1.31 goals. He will retain his place as will the rest of the back line.

Jordi Osei-Tutu is certain to play at right-back following another injury to Tristan Crama. The rest of the back four will also be the same, with a partnership of Jake Cooper and Caleb Taylor, with Zak Sturge at left-back. Millwall’s defence are averaging 1.71 goals conceded per game, and Neil will be hoping for a better performance on Saturday.

Jenson Metcalfe should come back into the starting XI after his suspension, and he will partner Derek Mazou-Sacko, who made just one accurate long ball against BCFC. Ahead of them in the advanced midfield role should be Camiel Neghli, who has been the Lions’ best performer this season. He has created 26 chances for the South London outfit and made 20 successful crosses.

Mark Sykes should come in on the right wing to bring some more stability to Millwall’s shape. The Irishman has made 29 defensive contributions and 12 clearances this campaign. On the left side, Kyrell Lisbie must retain his place to give the Lions that attacking quality. Despite his xG of 0.25 on Tuesday, the summer signing has won his side 13 fouls this term.

Up top, Neil has options to choose from with Mihailo Ivanovic and Josh Coburn back from injury. However, the Serbian was arguably rushed back against Birmingham City, and Lyndon Dykes should lead the line at The Hawthorns.

The Lions are averaging 1.29 points from their last five games, and it has seen them drop to 13th in the table and four points from the top eight. Their defeat to Birmingham City proved that Millwall need a reset to come back stronger.

Neil’s side entered the last break just a point from the play-off places, and they could enter the next one seven points away. The South London outfit had 25 points at this point last season and were sitting in 6th.

Millwall are clearly suffering from their number of injuries at the start of the campaign. Once key players like Coburn are back to full fitness, the Lions will have some catching up to do.
