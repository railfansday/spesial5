Air France A350 Nearly Collides Midair With American A321 After Wrong Turn At LAX

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Aircraft belonging to American Airlines and Air France came within 400 feet (121 meters) of vertical separation as both jets departed from Los Angeles International on November 7th. Quick actions by air traffic control and the pilots onboard averted a close call that could have had catastrophic consequences.
