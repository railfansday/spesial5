Brit Jailed In Singapore After Hidden Camera Found In Airplane Lavatory

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A man is facing quite some legal challenges after installing a hidden camera in an airplane lavatory, and then trying to control it remotely. Of course this is not only incredibly creepy and illegal, but if you’re going to do something like this, it probably shouldn’t be on a Singapore-bound flight, given the country’s strict laws.

The Straits Times has the story of how a 52-year-old British man has been jailed in Singapore, following an incident that happened on an October 7 flight from Osaka Kansai (KIX) to Singapore Changi (SIN). The man was traveling on a Boeing 787 belonging to Scoot, Singapore Airlines’ low cost subsidiary.

A short time after takeoff, the man reportedly installed a camera in the airplane lavatory, which had the function to be controlled remotely through an app on his phone. A short while later, a flight attendant, who was using the lavatory, found the hidden camera. She removed the device, and of course alerted the crew.

The passenger reportedly tried to remove the camera from the lavatory later, only to find that it was no longer there. The crew managed to figure out who it belonged to (it’s not entirely clear how), though in the meantime, he had already deleted the app on his phone, and all related pictures, to avoid detection.

He was detained upon arrival in Singapore, and his camera and phone were seized. The man was jailed, and his trial will take place in the coming weeks.

He faces charges of voyeurism and obstructing the course of justice. For voyeurism, one can be jailed for up to two years, fined, caned, or a combination of these punishments. For obstructing the course of justice, one can be jailed for up to seven years, fined, or both.
