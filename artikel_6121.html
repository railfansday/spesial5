Hilton Honors Amex Card Bonus: Points + Free Night Award Welcome Offers

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

For applications through January 13, 2027, American Express and Hilton are offering increased welcome offers on their four co-branded credit cards, and these are among the best deals we’ve seen on the cards. With these lucrative welcome offers, you can earn both points and an uncapped free night award (valid at properties costing up to 250,000 points per night, which is Hilton’s highest award cost for standard room redemptions).

Not only are these great welcome offers, but these cards offer valuable benefits for those who stay at Hiltons with any frequency, so if you travel often, it’s worth having (at least) one of these cards in your wallet. In particular, I love the free night awards, given that they can even be redeemed at top Waldorf Astoria or Conrad hotels, or even Small Luxury Hotels of the World (SLH) properties.

Link: Learn more about the Hilton Honors American Express Card, Hilton Honors American Express Surpass® Card, Hilton Honors American Express Business Card, and Hilton Honors American Express Aspire Card

For applications through January 13, 2027, Hilton Honors’ co-branded credit cards are offering limited time welcome offers, which are among the best that we’ve ever seen on these cards.

In addition to offering bonus points, three of the cards also include a free night award as part of the bonus (and the fourth card offers a free night award as an annual benefit, including the first year). For the purposes of framing the value of the points, based on my latest miles & points valuations, I consider Hilton Honors points to be worth 0.5 cents each.

These are standard Hilton Honors free night awards, which can be redeemed at a vast majority of Hilton Honors properties around the world for a standard room, and they’re valid for 12 months from when they’re issued. I’d consider this to be the single most valuable hotel credit card free night award out there, given that it can be redeemed at a property costing up to 250,000 points per night.
