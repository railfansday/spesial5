BBC chief football news reporter Simon Stone, Jay Motty from Stretford Paddock & Tom Allnutt from The Times join Rick & Lloyd for reaction to Michael Carrick’s sacking. Was he treated unfairly? When will Mauricio Pochettino arrive? And will Rick mention the time he saw him by a swimming pool in Qatar? You better believe it. Simon explains how things have unfolded over the past 24 hours and why the news that felt so inevitable took so long to arrive. Jay reflects on how Man Utd fans are feeling for Carrick with everything unfolding so publicly, including talks with Pochettino – which Simon explains took place a while ago. Plus, Tom tells Rick and Lloyd why Pochettino is a throwback manager – and how his way of doing things could suggest that major changes to the hierarchy are on the horizon. Plus, there is reaction to the sacking of Pierre Sage at Crystal Palace.  As ever, send us your thoughts on WhatsApp to 08000 289 369.
