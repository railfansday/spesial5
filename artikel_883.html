Celtic claim dramatic Europa League win against Besiktas

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Celtic vs Besiktas. UEFA Europa League.

Report as Celtic secure their first Europa League win; Junior Olaitan put the visitors ahead from the spot after a needless challenge from Jordan Lotomba; substitute Callum Osmand equalised with his first touch; Camilo Duran scored either side of Orkun Kokcu's strike to secure the points

Football Journalist @AlisonGConroy

Martin O'Neill's inspired substitutions saw Celtic come from behind to breathe life into their Europa League campaign with a much-needed 3-2 home victory over Besiktas.

Junior Olaitan's goal - a rebound after Sam Johnstone saved his penalty - looked like condemning Celtic to a fifth consecutive European defeat for the first time in the club's history.

But the introduction of Callum Osmand, Camilo Duran and Colby Donovan just after the hour was a masterstroke from O'Neill.

Osmand fired in the equaliser with his first touch before setting up Duran to round the goalkeeper and put the Hoops ahead.

With five minutes left, Celtic suffered a major blow as Orkun Kokcu came on for Besiktas to fire into the bottom corner from 25 yards.

But Duran was not done. He picked up the ball from Kieran Tierney and poked it into the net two minutes later to restore the Hoops' lead.

Celtic remain in the bottom 12 of the Europa League table, but, with four games left, they still have a chance of reaching the knockout stages.

Sky Sports' Gordon Duncan at Celtic Park:

"You have to wonder where that second-half performance has been for the last two months.

"Quality, desire, running power - all of the things the team's been accused of lacking recently were there.

"It's hard to overstate just how difference-making the substitutes were.

"Two goals for Camilo Duran and a goal and an assist for Callum Osmand. Inspired changes or the wrong team to begin with?

"A discussion for another day, perhaps. It's all about a Europa League campaign possibly back on track and a big lift in the general mood around the club."

"We've given ourselves a chance because of the result.

"The game was pretty difficult but we came through it, we came through it with flying colours. I just said to the team, they were a credit to the football club and you can't get a bigger accolade than that.

"We were doing fine after the penalty goes in, but we came roaring back and the substitutes made a big, big difference as well too, got a lot of pace up front, but I was just delighted with the whole team.

"It might just give us a platform to charge on. It's not going to be easy, but this definitely gives us a chance.
