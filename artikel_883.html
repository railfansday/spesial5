Wiegman cites young talent as key to new England deal after calling up Goodwin

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Head coach excited by ‘high potential’ of players

Striker Goodwin and keeper Cox in squad for first time

Sarina Wiegman says the potential in England’s emerging young players helped convince her to extend her contract until the end of 2029’s European Championship.

The Lionesses head coach, whose previous deal was due to expire next year, gave first senior call-ups to the striker Isobel Goodwin and goalkeeper Katie Cox on Tuesday as she named her squad for December’s two-leg World Cup qualifying playoff against Ukraine.

Speaking to the media for the first time since signed her new contract in October, Wiegman described the Football Association as “a trailblazer with the women’s game and a great federation to work for” and said: “I’m really happy with the team but also talents coming through, which is exciting, and there’s now some high potential, and hopefully these talents go from high potential to world-class players, if not already. And I hope to have a part in that, too, and have a contribution to that. While I’m talking about that, I’m getting excited.

“The majority of their development is happening [at their clubs] but when they come to our environment, I hope to have a little part of that contribution.”

The Dutchwoman has led England to three consecutive major tournament finals, lifting two European titles, since taking charge in 2021 on a deal to 2025. Asked whether she had expected that to become an eight-year stay, she said: “No. I thought: ‘Four years, that’s a long time.’ Then it became six years and that looks like it’s becoming eight years. I would never have expected all of the things that we have reached.”

Will she call it a day in 2029? “Well, I tend to think that’s the end point, but you never know! I don’t plan that far ahead. So let’s see where it gets to.”

Wiegman, whose latest 25-player squad includes 10 players with fewer than 10 caps, watched from the stands at the Emirates Stadium on Sunday as Goodwin scored for London City Lionesses with a terrific finish against Arsenal. She said of the 23-year-old: “We always knew she’s a huge talent [with] a high potential. We’re watching a lot of games over periods of time, all the time, so we [had] kept an eye on her anyway, but I think it also helped that she had that great goal and a great performance last Sunday.

“She has some different qualities. She’s really fast too. She can head – she showed that also in attacking set plays and defending set plays also – and she can score goals. Does she need to step up to the next level? Absolutely. But you can tell that she’s improving.”

Wiegman was unable to call upon several injured players, including Lauren James and Alex Greenwood. She included Manchester United’s Ella Toone, who was initially left out of September’s squad before being recalled for the playoff against Greece. Wiegman said of Toone: “She has impressed us really well. She responded really well. And going back to [her] club, she’s done well too. And that’s what has been fed back to me too.”

Cox is rare in getting an England call-up while playing in the second-tier WSL2. The 20-year-old is on loan at Nottingham Forest from Chelsea.

The Lionesses face Ukraine on neutral territory in Spain in the away leg on 1 December before playing them at Wembley four days later.
