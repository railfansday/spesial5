Hojgaard shares PGA Tour lead as RSM Classic dragged into Monday finish

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Saturday's play was washed out by heavy rain at the RSM Classic, dragging the tournament into a Monday finish; Rasmus Hojgaard and Michael Thorbjornsen share lead heading into the final day; watch live on Monday from 3pm on Sky Sports Golf

Rasmus Hojgaard holds a share of the lead heading into a weather-delayed final day of the RSM Classic, with England's Marco Penge in contention as he looks to secure his PGA Tour future.

Hojgaard entered the week 122nd in the FedExCup standings and among the players looking to break into the top 100 and remain fully exempt on the PGA Tour next season, with the Dane now in prime position to do that.

The Ryder Cup star posted a five-under 65 on the Seaside Course at Sea Island Golf Club, firing five birdies to move to 13 under alongside Michael Thorbjornsen, with Max Greyserman a shot off the pace in third.

The Dane carded back-to-back birdies from the sixth and followed a 10-foot birdie at the 12th by holing from 30 feet for another at the 16th, before closing a blemish-free day by draining a 25-footer at the par-four last to move joint-top of the leaderboard.

"Today felt very free - tomorrow's a completely different day," Hojgaard said. "I'm going to feel the nerves. I think that's what we all play for is to put ourselves in those positions, and we'll see what happens."

Penge needs at least a top-four finish to have a chance of breaking inside the top 100, with a two-under 68 keeping him within two of the lead heading into a second successive Monday finish on the PGA Tour.

Haotong Li and Max McGreevy join Penge on 11 under, while Jackson Koivun is in the group three strokes back with Denmark's Rasmus Neergaard-Petersen and Belgium's Adrien Dumont de Chassart.

The tournament was washed out by heavy rain on Saturday, leading to the final round being held on Monday. The Austin Championship last week also was delayed by rain, marking the first time since 2015 that successive PGA Tour events have been dragged into an unscheduled fifth day.

Those 51st to 60th in the FedExCup standings after this week qualify for the first two Signature Events of 2027, while those who end between 101st and 125th on the PGA Tour only receive conditional status and will be unable to feature in certain events.

The final round starts on Monday at 7.20am local time (12.20pm UK time), with a two-tee start in operation and players going out in threeballs.
