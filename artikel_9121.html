air-powered vehicles take passengers on a journey beneath the water’s surface

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

developed by sub sea systems, the attraction combines a gravity-fed track, an air-powered propulsion system, and enclosed passenger cabins to create a new kind of aquatic entertainment.

Sub Sea Systems’ Aquaticar is an underwater adventure ride that takes water park visitors beneath the surface in small, enclosed submersible vehicles. Developed by the company’s Sub Sea Creative division, the attraction is designed to bring a different dimension to aquatic entertainment, combining a gravity-fed course, an air-powered propulsion system, and an immersive underwater environment. The first installation is now open at Aquarabia Water Theme Park, where visitors travel through a themed underwater landscape without needing to swim or dive.

Aquaticar places passengers directly beneath the water. Two guests board each vehicle before a transparent canopy closes over the cabin. The cars then descend along a submerged track, traveling past artificial reefs, shipwrecks, and aquatic scenery, with fish and stingrays forming part of the designed environment. Passengers remain dry from the chest upward while moving through the underwater course.

Sub Sea Systems’ patented Bubble Engine, a propulsion system that uses compressed air, plumbs integrated beneath the track and supplies air through specialized triggers, releasing bubbles underneath passing vehicles and propelling them along the course.

The system is designed around a 137-meter circuit, with a ride lasting approximately 6.8 minutes. According to the manufacturer, the attraction can accommodate up to 300 guests per hour, depending on its operating configuration.

The use of air as a propulsion medium shapes the safety infrastructure of the ride. Sub Sea Systems describes a redundant air supply and communication system, with fresh air continuously circulated inside the passenger cabins. Emergency provisions include multiple levels of Emergency Air Supply, an independent onboard air supply, and an airbag system intended to bring vehicles back to the water’s surface. The manufacturer also states that the ride is TÜV SÜD certified.
