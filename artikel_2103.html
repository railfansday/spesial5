Chelsea’s Reggie Watson gets next chance under Alonso

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Reggie Watson Chelsea news continues to build, according to football.london, with the 16-year-old further establishing himself as one of Xabi Alonso’s wildcard options in the first-team squad. Watson has been named in both of Chelsea’s senior squads for their Carabao Cup ties this season.

He was an unused substitute in the second-round win over Luton Town, then made his full Chelsea debut in the 6-3 win over Leeds United at Stamford Bridge. Watson played the first 45 minutes against Leeds, a reward for his progress since Alonso took charge at Chelsea over the summer.

Every stage of Watson’s rise has come through football.london’s reporting, with Chelsea yet to make any official comment on his long-term role in the squad. The outlet’s football.london report traces how the teenager has moved from pre-season fixtures into genuine first-team contention this term.

Watson played a big part in Chelsea’s pre-season tour, completing the full 90 minutes of the opening match against Western Sydney Wanderers. He was also part of Chelsea’s travelling squad for the Brentford defeat before the international break. Watson was left out of the matchday squad list for that game, though, as explained in our Brentford line-up report.

Mahdi Nicoll-Jazuli was the Chelsea youngster named on the bench for that Brentford trip and came on late in the match, football.london added.

Watson’s form has caught attention away from the first-team picture too. He was one of three Chelsea youngsters nominated for Premier League 2’s Player of the Month award for September, alongside Chizaram Ezenwata and Leo Black, according to football.london.

Black recently signed a two-year contract at Chelsea after impressing on trial, while Watson built his own case by scoring three goals in September. That form explains why the Reggie Watson Chelsea story keeps developing. Alonso keeps pulling him into senior squads instead of leaving him with the academy alone. The head coach has shown similar willingness to adapt his squad elsewhere, as detailed in Alonso’s recent rotation plans.

Chelsea are reported to be facing Portsmouth in a behind-closed-doors friendly this weekend, according to football.london, though the club has not confirmed the fixture publicly. Alonso reportedly wants to keep his squad sharp during an extended three-week international break.

Chelsea’s academy side, known as Cobham Next Gen, have no fixture of their own this weekend. That gap could leave Watson free to train or travel with the first-team squad again, football.london reports.

The teenager will try to impress Chelsea’s first-team staff in that friendly. Nothing is confirmed yet about what his involvement will be once the senior squad returns to action after the international break.
