Kalshi is in talks to raise money at a $65 billion valuation

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The prediction market closed a round at a $40 billion valuation weeks ago that was expected to be its last before an IPO

Kalshi is in talks to raise new funding from investors at a $65 billion valuation, according to Bloomberg. A Kalshi spokesperson declined to comment.

The talks come only weeks after Kalshi wrapped up a financing that valued it at $40 billion. That round, which the company disclosed in late October, had been seen as the final private raise ahead of an IPO, according to Bloomberg. Kalshi opted to raise private money again before going public, and volatility in financial markets did not influence the choice.

Kalshi, founded in 2018, sells financial contracts whose payouts depend on how real-world events turn out. Sports betting has made up the bulk of trading on the platform during the past year, according to Bloomberg.

Kalshi's monthly notional trading volume grew by more than 100% between August, when it stood at $40 billion, and October, when it reached close to $100 billion, according to Bloomberg. Gross margins were about 90% during the last funding round, according to documentation Bloomberg viewed. Kalshi's annual run rate, a projection of yearly revenue if present activity holds steady, has surpassed $5 billion.

Kalshi's financial and betting products have drawn backing from the Trump administration. Scrutiny of them has now increased, after Democrats, who have been more critical of prediction markets, gained ground in the midterm elections, according to Bloomberg.

Kalshi announced Thursday that it had severed its association with Donald Trump Jr., one of its advisers, according to Bloomberg. Kalshi pointed to his connection to Polymarket, its chief competitor.
