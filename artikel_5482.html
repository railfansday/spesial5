Globant Reports 2026 Third Quarter Financial Results

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

LUXEMBOURG, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Globant (NYSE: GLOB) today announced results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

"As an AI-native company, we are seeing our momentum accelerate, with AI Pods reaching an annualized run rate of $106.6 million in the third quarter, more than doubling quarter-over-quarter and positioning us to exceed $130 million by year end. This quarter, we completed the rollout of Glob.AI, our AI-native services platform comprising our AI Pods and Glob.AI OS, an operating system that provides governance and traceability. We are seeing growing enterprise adoption as clients shift from hourly billing toward outcome- and consumption-based delivery. Supported by an expanding partner ecosystem and two decades of engineering playbooks, we believe we are well positioned to help our clients accelerate their AI transformation", explained MartÃ­n Migoya, Globant's CEO and co-founder.

"In Q3 2026, Globant delivered $611.1 million in revenue, in line with the midpoint of our guidance, and expanded adjusted operating margin sequentially to 14.1%. Core client expansion remained strong, highlighted by a 5.7% year-over-year increase in top 50 accounts and record revenue per head of $99.8 thousand. Supported by streamlined operations, debt maturities extended to 2031, and $25.0 million in Q3 share repurchases, we believe our strong balance sheet and robust cash conversion will enable us to navigate regional headwinds while advancing our business model transformation. Given current market valuations, we have upsized our share repurchase program from $125 million to $250 million through December 2027," stated Juan Urthiague, Globant's CFO.

Globant (NYSE: GLOB) is a global AI-native services company that helps organizations reinvent themselves and drive measurable business results by combining technology, creativity and innovation with AI at its core. Through Glob.AI, its AI-native technology services model and its signature AI Pods, agentic service units supervised by experts, Globant delivers enterprise-grade technology services tied directly to core business metrics.

Present across 30+ countries, Globant operates a network of industry-specific AI Studios and partners with global brands including FIFA, Google, Riot Games, the LA Clippers and Santander. Recognized by IDC MarketScape as a Worldwide Leader in Experience Design Services (2025) and a Leader in AI Services (2023), Globant's work is featured as business case studies at Harvard, MIT and Stanford. The company collaborates with leading technology platforms including Anthropic, NVIDIA, AWS and Unity.
