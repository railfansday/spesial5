Myopia is a major public health challenge in China

China has an estimated 600 million people with myopia, with particularly strong demand for refractive surgery among young and middle-aged adults. As a growing number of young people enter adulthood with moderate-to-high myopia, the need for individualized refractive-care pathways will only grow. Recent research shows that two in three (67.2%) people aged 15 to 19 in China have myopia, with 9.5% having high myopia, according to a recent systematic review.1

The Work Plan for Accelerating the Provision of High-Quality and Efficient Eye Health Services (2026-2030) calls for increased coverage of effective refractive error correction. As one of the mainstream procedures for refractive correction, phakic IOL surgery has undergone more than three decades of clinical validation and gained widespread recognition among physicians and patients. However, as expectations increase for visual quality, demand for surgical options and interest in alternatives continue to rise, options for advanced phakic IOLs remain relatively limited. ZEISS PT LUNANA pIOLs were developed to address this gap, offering myopic patients in China a new, personalized option for refractive correction.

The ZEISS PT LUNANA pIOL is the first step in establishing a broader phakic IOL portfolio, driven by the company's rich optical heritage combined with local expertise and R&D. The first public showing at the CIIE marks the company's plans to expand its comprehensive refractive solutions, providing physicians with more vision correction options for a wider group of patients.

"Since the introduction of phakic IOLs, both physicians and patients have been looking forward to more diverse and innovative products and technologies. The debut of ZEISS PT LUNANA in China in the coming year will not only allow patients to benefit from this innovation firsthand but also will provide ophthalmologists with an opportunity to gain valuable clinical experience early on and strengthen their influence in cutting-edge academic research," said Professor Zhou Xingtao, President of the Eye and ENT Hospital of Fudan University, Shanghai.

ZEISS to extend refractive portfolio, expanding options for high-quality vision

ZEISS offers options for managing myopia throughout a person's lifetime, from MyocareÂ® lenses for children, Ortho-K lenses, and glasses through to refractive correction, such as SMILE proÂ® from ZEISS, and intraocular lenses. Patients seeking refractive correction options who are not suitable for laser correction may face challenges in finding a suitable solution for their individual eye conditions. ZEISS PT LUNANA integrates aspheric optical design into its next generation phakic IOL and features AQUAFLEX hydrophilic polymer material, designed for flexibility, clarity and stability. The phakic IOL will accommodate a wide range of myopia levels, as well as benefit and may provide a refractive correction option for patients with thin corneas or high myopia, who may not be candidates for laser vision correction.

Each patient's eyes are unique, making precise, individualized fitting essential for phakic IOLs. Professor Wang Xiaoying, Chief Physician at the Eye and ENT Hospital of Fudan University in Shanghai, emphasized that the ZEISS PT LUNANA pIOL offers a considered selection of seven lens sizes and 0.25-diopter increments, enabling physicians with greater choice when selecting a lens for individual patient needs. She added, "It supports physicians in their procedures and can help patients feel confident in their treatment choice."

Michael Achtelik, Global Head of Business Sector, Refractive Lasers, ZEISS Medical Technology, explained, "The NMPA approval in China marks an important step in our journey to bring the ZEISS phakic IOL portfolio to ophthalmic customers worldwide. By combining ZEISS' global expertise in precision optics and refractive workflows with local manufacturing expertise and market insights close to our customers in China, we are strengthening our ability to deliver more personalized solutions for patients across China."

ZEISS PT LUNANA has completed pre-market clinical trials and two years of follow-up at nine Grade 3A hospitals in China. ZEISS plans to commercialize this product in China first in 2027 and eventually will bring the platform to the global stage.
