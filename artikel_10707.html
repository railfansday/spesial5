‘Minecraft Mommy’ goes viral on TikTok for giving good boys gaming tips

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A TikTok creator dubbed “Minecraft Mommy” is going viral for teaching her “good boys” how to find diamonds and kill zombies in one hit.

The diamond-hunting clip appeared on footyfanatics5 under the title “Minecraft Diamond Hunting: Find Diamonds Like a Good Boy”, a TikTok with 1.1 million views at the time of writing. The account’s bio says “Mommy got banned on this account” and directs viewers to @MommyRosie.

MommyRosie is where the newest tutorials appear, and the profile’s bio sets a goal of reaching “100k good boys.” A newer clip with over 1 million views promises to teach players how to kill Minecraft zombies in one hit.

Rosie’s TikToks found their way onto X, where viewers actually took her advice to heart. One viewer agreed that specializing weapons could make them stronger and suggested a Bane of Arthropods spear, a Smite sword, and a Sharpness axe. Another wrote, “Might have to finally make a smite sword.”

However, most comments came from those who were more focused on the character’s personality than her actual tips.

“Why do i feel loved after hearing she is proud?” one viewer asked. Another said they almost cried, and a third thanked “mommy” for helping them kill mobs more efficiently. One commenter said people were overlooking Minecraft mommy audios in general, while another was relieved they weren’t the only person seeing her videos.

The format also had its critics. One viewer called the video “purely for the mommy gimmick,” and another said it was getting tiring. Some speculated that AI might have been used, with one pointing to the script and voice as “clearly AI.”

Footyfanatics, Rosie’s original account, posted AI-generated soccer content with a male voiceover up until November 11, when their content switched to Minecraft Mommy gameplay tips.
