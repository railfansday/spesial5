Businessman arrested after booby-trapped game console explodes at rival’s electronics store

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A businessman in Brazil has been arrested after allegedly sending an Xbox 360 packed with explosives to a rival electronics store, injuring two people in what police believe was an attempted murder.

Everton Yoshizumi Lima, 39, is accused of orchestrating the attack on October 23 in Santo André, near São Paulo, on the same day the rival store was celebrating its grand opening.

According to g1, the suspect allegedly hired a delivery driver through an app to transport the console to the store under the pretense that it needed to be modified to play games.

The driver, who was reportedly unaware that he was transporting a bomb, delivered the console at approximately 5:30 PM.

The store owner placed the Xbox 360 inside a drawer, where it exploded around 40 minutes later.

Security footage captured the terrifying moment the device detonated, damaging the ceiling and furniture while leaving two people injured, including the store’s owner.

One victim could be seen with his clothing on fire, while another limped away holding his injured leg.

According to Metrópoles, investigators discovered that the console contained two explosive charges of equal strength, but only one detonated.

Police believe the device was deliberately designed to kill, with forensic examinations revealing that it contained a timer and two cylinders filled with pressurized flammable liquid.

Investigators suspect the attack was motivated by business competition, as Lima reportedly owns three electronics stores, including two on the same street where the victim had just opened his business.

Authorities believe the opening of another store nearby threatened the suspect’s business interests, prompting him to allegedly target his competitor.

Lima was arrested on November 14 after police investigating a suspected stolen motorcycle located him at a business and discovered an outstanding warrant for his arrest.

On November 16, authorities carried out additional searches at four locations connected to the suspect as part of their investigation.

He is being investigated for attempted aggravated murder involving an explosive device. His defense told local media it had requested access to the case files and was not yet in a position to comment.
