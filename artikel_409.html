The Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Tech Sticker Collection is back under $40 — save $10 with this early Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Tech Sticker Collection is on sale for as low as $39.99 at Amazon, down from its $49.99 list price.

With Black Friday just over a week away, Amazon has dropped the price of another popular Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration product.

As of Nov. 19, the Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Tech Sticker Collection (either the Alolan Exeggutor or Lucario version) is available for as little as $39.99 at Amazon across various sellers. Knocking up to $10 off its $49.99 list price, that 20% discount means you’ll be getting three individual booster packs for $13.33 each.

$39.99 is the lowest third-party price for the 30th Celebration’s Tech Sticker Collection on Amazon.

What’s more, these new low prices mean the Tech Sticker Collections are now much closer to the estimated TCGplayer market price — now just $7.04 higher than Alolan Exeggutor’s $32.95 and Lucario’s $37.10.

Each collection contains three 30th Celebration booster packs, a foil promo trading card, and a matching sticker sheet. You'll receive either the Alolan Exeggutor or Lucario promo card version at random.
