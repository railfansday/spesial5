Chipotles new hoodie hides a warm burrito in your pocket — how to buy this much-needed drop

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

TL;DR: Chipotle is dropping its limited-edition Sneak It In hoodie on Nov. 18, at 12 p.m. ET for $53 (including free shipping), featuring a hidden insulated pocket to keep a burrito warm, a built-in napkin pocket, and one free regular-priced entrée card.

Movie theater snack prices are rough, and trying to smuggle a foil-wrapped burrito inside a regular sweatshirt pocket is always a risky move. Chipotle has watched fans pull off that trick for years, so the chain finally turned the internet joke into a real piece of outerwear built specifically for sneaking takeout past the ticket taker.

Launching Nov. 18 at 12 p.m. ET, the limited-edition Chipotle Sneak It In Hoodie looks like a regular pullover on the outside while hiding a few upgrades underneath. Tucked inside is a secret zippered, insulated pouch sized to fit one full Chipotle burrito — or a bowl, salad, order of tacos, or chips and dips — basically anything, and keep it warm until the movie previews wrap up.

There is also a dedicated built-in napkin pocket so you are not fumbling around in the dark once the lights go down. To help keep your movie snacks under the radar, the hoodie comes in three discreet colorways: Nothing To See Here Black, No Burrito Here Brown, and Got the Goods Gray.

Since the hoodie comes with a roomy, oversized cut, Chipotle suggests sizing down for a closer, true-to-size fit. Every sweatshirt even ships with a physical "burrito buck" card tucked inside, good for one free regular-priced entrée at participating Chipotle locations in the U.S. and Canada through Dec. 31, 2027.

Just keep in mind that orders come with free shipping, are limited to two per customer, and all sales are final, so double-check your size before checking out.

Grab the limited-edition Chipotle Sneak It In Hoodie and walk past the ticket taker like you own the place.
