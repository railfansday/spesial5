This GTA 6 Clip Looks So Real, Fans Can't Believe It's In-Game Footage

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

If you're at all interested in GTA 6, you'll probably have seen this doing the rounds already. A video is going viral on social media, because it's fooling people into thinking it's real-life footage — but it's actually the game.

Originally posted by onlyindade on Instagram, the video has since been shared by the official Rockstar Games account, with many taking that as confirmation it's actually a video of Vice City within GTA 6.

The clip begins with a view from inside a moving vehicle as it drives past the Cortez Hotel, a Vice City location inspired by the real world Colony Hotel in Miami. The view flips half-way through, looking out onto the road.

We see several custom cars (the same ones that have been recreated in real life) as well as an impressive number of NPCs going about their business.

It's extremely convincing; the way the camera sways mimics the sort of videos captured on smartphones. You can actually see a direct comparison of the video against real life here:

Fans are getting really excited about this new clip as it really highlights the game's attention to detail and realism, with some still unconvinced it's actually GTA 6.
