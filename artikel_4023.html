GTA 6's PS5 Platinum Already Unlocked By One Player

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

There are, quite literally, millions of people playing GTA 6, as Rockstar revealed its hit new game sold more than 32 million copies on its first day. That means the solitary player to have unlocked the game’s Platinum Trophy on PS5 is, quite literally, one in more than 32 million at this moment in time.

PSNProfiles is a website that tracks the trophy stats for every PlayStation game, both for personal use if you want to check in on your own milestones, and as a collective so you can see how you measure up against everyone else.

That means it’s the best place to go to see exactly how many people have unlocked a game’s Platinum rather than going by the rough percentage you’re shown on a game’s actual trophy list. It’s particularly helpful in GTA 6’s case, as that percentage is still locked at zero, but there’s actually one player who has managed to unlock it.

The 28-year-old, critically-acclaimed fighting game has a lot of new, very upset players all of a sudden.

According to PSNProfiles, that world first has been achieved by a player who goes by Danpool85. It also shows that they’ve been playing the game for over a week, which means they had a head start on most of those 32 million who have only been playing for four days. Either they bent the rules or, the more likely scenario, they were provided a review copy early by Rockstar.

They really are the only one right now, too. The Xbox equivalent of PSNProfiles, TrueAchievements, is still showing a zero when it comes to how many players have unlocked GTA 6’s Mythic Achievement. Mythic is the new Platinum-equivalent Xbox achievement added very recently, turning all of those 1,000 Gamerscores into Mythics, dating all the way back to the 360, and seemingly even a few original Xbox titles.

There are 55 trophies to unlock in GTA 6, and its Platinum isn’t currently the only one with so few unlocks that they’re still claiming no one has unlocked them. Performing 250 stunts is another trophy that barely anyone has, as is the trophy you get for scanning 100 different objects using your WAiNK app.

Even though there’s still so much more left to do and see, as demonstrated by literally one person in the world reaching 100% completion so far, some of the secrets the millions of us playing have collectively discovered so far are nothing short of incredible.

In less than a week, players have already managed to stop the GTA 6 train - something that was infamously impossible in previous entries - and discovered an easter egg that mentions the GTA 5 protagonists by name, confirming the games take place in the same shared universe. The tip of the hat to the last GTA can be found in a GTA 6 true crime podcast where the host interviews Wade, another character from GTA 5. Wade comments on Trevor, Franklin, and Michael not being responsible for the murders of Deborah and Floyd, blissfully unaware that they did kill those characters and then framed him for their murders.

GTA 6 was a monumental launch for Rockstar, with over 50% of all active console users playing the game on day one.
