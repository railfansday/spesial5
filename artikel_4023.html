Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Full list of The Game Awards 2026 category nominees

Geoff Keighley has today confirmed the nominees for all the categories at The Game Awards 2026, with the show set to take place on 10th December 2026.

The nominees for Game of the Year are: 007 First Light, Control Resonant, GTA 6, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.

GTA 6 has been nominated the most, in eight categories in total. Its other nominations include Best Game Direction, the Best Narrative, and Best Performance.
