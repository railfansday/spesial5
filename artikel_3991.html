Where To Find Vespucci Island In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Vespucci Island in Grand Theft Auto 6 is a great place to find some high-value cars, weapons, and sellable goods.

Grand Theft Auto 6's map is massive, so it's no surprise that players are finding themselves lost from time to time. Eventually, many will likely come to know Leonida's roads as well as those in their real-life hometowns, but for now we thankfully have guides like this and in-game GPS.

Vespucci Island is one of the many islands in Leonida, and while knowing its name could be enough to locate it on the map, it's still hard to find a small location if you don't know where to start looking. So, if you're looking for Vespucci Island because you heard about some Easter egg or car there, keep reading to find out just where to find it.

Vespucci Island is one of the three Rialto Islands in Vice-Dale County. The Rialto Islands are the islands along one of the bridges from Vice City to Vice Beach, and Vespucci Island is the middle one with a large oval shape. If you zoom in on this area, you should see it clearly labeled, or at least see a faint outline since you haven't discovered it yet.
