Guide: GTA 6: How to Get Ultimate Edition Items & Pre-Order Bonuses

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

GTA 6's Ultimate Edition features a collection of different items and pre-order bonuses, including vehicles, weapons, and cosmetics. These are unlocked at different points in the story, and some need to be collected from locations around the map. This GTA 6 guide includes step-by-step instructions on how to collect them and when they become available.

GTA 6's Ultimate Edition costs $99.99/£89.99/€99.99. The Standard Edition costs $79.99/£69.99/€79.99.

Yes, I personally think GTA 6's Ultimate Edition is worth it, but it really depends how excited you are for the game and whether you want to get the most out of it.

All of the additional Mod Shops and Stores exclusive to the Ultimate Edition, along with their unique inventory, means you're really adding a lot of cosmetic and personalisation options to the title with the Ultimate Edition.

If you're not the type of player that plans to dress up Jason and Lucia and fiddle with their vehicles, then you may not find value in this.

But it's worth adding that the Classic Car Collection, which adds collectibles quests involving Wyman, are also exclusive to the Ultimate Edition.

You can still enjoy GTA 6 to its fullest without paying for the Ultimate Edition, but you'll get much more out of the game if you do.

Yes, the Ultimate Edition Upgrade can be purchased from the PS Store at any time for $20.00/£20.00/€20.00 and will automatically add all of the extra content to your game once installed.

You'll need to buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade if you purchase a physical edition of GTA 6, as this content isn't included with the retail release.

If you've followed this guide carefully, then you should now have collected all of your Ultimate Edition items and pre-order bonuses in GTA 6. Congratulations for getting them all!
