PSYCHO DEAD launches April 15, 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Third-person survival horror game PSYCHO DEAD will launch or PlayStation 5 and PC via Steam, Epic Games Store, and GOG on April 15, 2027, developer MOON CATALYST announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via MOON CATALYST:

PSYCHO DEAD is a third-person survival horror game centered on Elixia, a mysterious girl with psychic powers, who must escape a secret laboratory infested with grotesque and monstrous creatures. Explore a science-fiction setting filled with mystery and danger, where terror awaits at every corner, and discover the truth behind the horrific incident.
