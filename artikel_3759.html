Here’s What People Are Playing That’s Not GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Yes, GTA 6. Biggest game of the generation. You feel every penny and hour that went into making it, very good. But there are dozens, dozens! of people out there who, because of a lack of an appropriate platform, funds, or simply interest, are not playing this game at all. I wanted to hear from them, so I posted a call on Bluesky, “Hey, tell me what you’re playing that’s not GTA 6.” Here’s a summary of what I heard.

Queen’s Domain came up early and often as a GTA 6 alternative. In fact, perusing my thread, it was the game that came up the most. I don’t know anything about the game (and I plan on fixing that as soon as possible) but what I’ve gather so far is…this is a game about holes. WoW Forever was another high-volume contender, as was the Ocarina of Time remake which has somehow tripped me up at the Water Temple even though I’ve been told it’s been nerfed from its original difficulty 30 years ago. And shout out to the people who are still in part one of Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave, you are real and you are valid (and you are also me).

Speaking of “things that are me,” I have to mention all the homies participating in a Warhammer game of some stripe. So far in my quest to sample every genre of Warhammer game, I’ve gotten around to Warhammer Survivors, Rogue Trader, and Space Marine 2. The only genre I haven’t touched yet (I think) are the strategy games and I’m in luck that Dawn of War IV will be out at the end of the month.

So many cozy indies got a mention too, including Milki Delivery, Tanuki: Pon’s Summer, and of course Dressmaker (which will be featured during AGDQ 2027 and I’m excited to see how a dressmaking game can be speedrun.)

The way that GTA 6 is dominating every airwave and will continue to do so for at least a few weeks longer is probably isolating for folks who don’t have any interest in that game. It is the game of the moment and deserves this level of discussion, as tedious as it can be sometimes. But I wanted to make a space for folks to have a different discussion, and I was surprised at how swift and voluminous of a response I got. I wanted to share that here, to break up the GTA 6 stream for just a little bit.

So tell us, what are you playing that’s not GTA 6? Let us know in the comments below.
