‘Hunger Games’ Producer Admits ‘Mockingjay’ Should Have Been One Movie

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Franchise producer Nina Jacobson says adapting one book into two movies rubs fans the wrong way: "I think audiences find it cynical."

The first two Hunger Games movies? Terrific. Critics and fans agree.

Author Suzanne Collins’ climactic Katniss novel Mockingjay was infamously split into two films, Part 1 and Part 2. Both are considered a step down from the previous two Hunger Games blockbusters, with lower Rotten Tomatoes critic and audience scores. The uneven Part 1, in particular, felt like a franchise spinning its wheels. To fans, it all looked like a studio trying to wring more ticket sales from the story. In a new interview with Empire, franchise producer Nina Jacobson says that if she and director Francis Lawrence had a second chance, they’d adapt Mockingjay into a single movie.

“Even though I love the Mockingjay films, if I had it to do over again, I think both Francis and I might try to do it all in one,” Jacobson said. “Mockingjay was such a big book, and it takes you to so many different places. But in the end, I think that Francis and I both felt that splitting them up… even if it did come from an inside-out place of really feeling like we needed two films to tell the story, I think audiences find it cynical. Even if its intentions are earnest, which they were. They feel like it’s a play to make them see two films instead of one.”

The producer added: “We really put the burden on ourselves [now], even when it’s a big book, to transform it into a theatrical experience, to not take advantage of your audience’s patience, to make it as tight as we can. [Sunrise On The Reaping] is the longest [film] by a minute or two of all of them.”

At the time, splitting the final book in a fantasy franchise into two films was a bit of a trend. Warner Bros. did it with JK Rowling’s Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows and Summit did it with Stephenie Meyer’s’ Breaking Dawn.

And while the Mockingjay films were considered creatively weaker than the previous two movies, they still were generally liked — neither was an outright misstep — and did boffo box office, with 2014’s Part 1 grossing $775 million and 2015’s Part 2 grossing $653 million.

The new film, Sunrise on the Reaping, grossed a strong $120 million in its opening weekend, marking a rebound for the franchise after the soft launch of 2023’s The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes ($44.6 million).
