'Things Went Even Better Than Expected': What We Heard This Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

— Quotable quotes heard by MedPage Today's reporters

"Things went even better than expected." -- Shaughnessy Naughton, founder and president of 314 Action, on her organization's efforts to help get physicians and scientists elected to Congress.

"The case count is a floor." -- Amesh Adalja, MD, of the Johns Hopkins Center for Health Security in Baltimore, on the likely underreporting of bird flu cases during an outbreak in Washington state.

"Having letters behind our names as physicians does not grant us any special privilege of not having an underlying mental health condition." -- N. Adam Brown, MD, MBA, discussing clinician mental health in light of a doctor's mass shooting.

"These children were thriving." -- Ania Jastreboff, MD, PhD, of the Yale School of Medicine in New Haven, on children as young as age 6 with obesity who lost substantial weight on semaglutide (Wegovy) in a clinical trial.

"A lot of people are going to be very unnecessarily worried about a useless test that we should not be ordering or recommending in the first place." -- Rita Redberg, MD, MSc, of the University of California, San Francisco, on guidelines recommending universal adult screening for lipoprotein(a).

"Allergists sometimes need to investigate ingredients that might not be immediately obvious." -- Zachary Rubin, MD, a Chicago-area pediatric allergist, discussing an unexpected ingredient in an energy drink.

"Our findings reinforce the importance of obesity prevention and treatment as a part of cancer prevention." -- Digantkumar Patel, MD, of the Springfield Clinic in Illinois, discussing data showing deaths from cancers linked to high body mass more than doubled over the past 30 years.

"AI-assisted documentation could strengthen the quality of information transferred at discharge." -- Kathleen Goldsmith, MSc, of Imperial College London, on a study showing that artificial intelligence (AI) outperformed clinicians in drafting hospital discharge summaries.
