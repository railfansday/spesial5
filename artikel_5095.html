Pearl [PEARL] has vastly outperformed the rest of the crypto market. The Layer 1 proof-of-useful-work blockchain project, aimed at utilizing artificial intelligence computation, has rallied 20.47% in 24 hours and is up 46% over the past week.

Co-founder of thesis-driven crypto hedge fund Syncracy Capital, Ryan Watkins, wrote that Pearl could address some shortcomings of the preeminent store of value in crypto, Bitcoin [BTC].

The potential of quantum computing to break Bitcoin (and most of the rest of the financial landscape), as well as Bitcoin competing with AI for the same data center infrastructure, were two hurdles that Bitcoin has no answer to, Watkins believed.

At its core, the L1 network allows a way for data center infrastructure to simultaneously handle AI inference workloads while also working to secure a network.

Arbitrary matrix multiplication could be transformed into useful proof of work, research showed. Being able to address one of the “biggest structural flaws” of Bitcoin was central to Pearl’s value proposition.

Only time will tell whether the market is swayed by this thesis.

The 4-hour chart showed a healthy uptrend in progress. Interrupted earlier this month by a drop to $1.12, the bulls drove prices higher. The retracement to $1.62 last week tested a key Fibonacci retracement support level.

Yet, the CMF remained above +0.05 for the most part, even during the pullback from $2.45 to $1.58 last week. At the time of writing, recent gains and heavy demand have caused a CMF surge to +0.28, indicating sizeable capital inflows.

The A/D indicator agreed with these findings of steady demand. The MACD also reflected prevailing bullish momentum behind the altcoin.

In times of market-wide stress, an altcoin with sizeable short-term momentum tends to draw more capital into it, fueling further short-term gains.

This can also come with an increased threat of profit-taking and volatility.

At the time of writing, a bullish PEARL move is beyond $2.45 to $2.76, and $3.27 can be expected.
