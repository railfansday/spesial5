RLUSD Surpasses Trump’s USD1 Stablecoin but XRP Still Sinks 26%

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

RLUSD briefly overtakes Trump’s USD1 stablecoin at $2.87B, while XRP struggles below $1 amid persistent selling pressure.

Following the latest Ripple news, RLUSD (Ripple’s stablecoin) reached a new milestone over the weekend, with its circulating supply hitting a record $2.87 billion on Saturday, November 21. The surge also pushed RLUSD ahead of USD1, the stablecoin issued by the Trump family’s World Liberty Financial.

The lead did not last, though. On Tuesday, RLUSD’s supply slipped to $2.82 billion, while USD1 held at about $2.85 billion.

The two coins are now separated by about $30 million, and they are heading in opposite directions. RLUSD has more than doubled in a year. USD1 has lost almost half its supply since February.

Meanwhile, XRP, the token closely tied to Ripple, trades near $0.93, down 26% this month.

So how did Ripple’s dollar catch up, and does it help XRP?

We pulled the daily supply of both coins from DefiLlama’s stablecoin dashboard, which adds up every chain they run on.

We also checked the contracts directly. RLUSD has $2.04 billion on Ethereum and $761 million on the XRP Ledger. USD1 is spread across BNB Chain ($1.10 billion), Solana ($868 million) and Ethereum ($852 million).

RLUSD’s growth has come in steps. Supply jumped from about $1.5 billion to $2.4 billion in August, then climbed again from late October.

Ripple has pushed it hard, and that push is behind most Ripple news this autumn. In October, the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency gave Ripple National Trust Bank final approval to manage RLUSD’s reserves. Robinhood expanded its RLUSD deal the same month. Binance has also run repeated campaigns that pay XRP to RLUSD holders.

USD1 is moving the other way. Its supply rose quickly early in 2026, then fell from $4.47 billion on October 7 to $2.85 billion now.

The XRP Ledger matters most for XRP holders. About $761 million of RLUSD sits there, roughly a quarter of the total. That is where RLUSD activity can create demand for XRP, which pays the ledger’s fees.

The problem is, for XRP holders, the RLUSD Ripple news has not translated into buying yet. We measured taker flows on Binance’s XRP/USDT spot market, meaning trades where the buyer or seller crossed the spread. Sellers outweighed buyers on six of the last seven days, for a net $25.5 million. Over 30 days, the net selling reached $140 million.

The selling is fading, though. It dropped from $17 million on November 14 to $2.1 million on Monday. A cross-exchange flow chart shared by traders showed spot buyers absorbing futures selling on Monday, with $11.7 million of net spot buying against $26.9 million of futures selling.

XRP trades at $0.9295, below its 50-day exponential moving average (EMA), an average that gives recent prices more weight, at $1.1499, and its 200-day EMA at $1.2996.

The next Ripple news test is whether RLUSD can retake the lead and hold it. For XRP, the signal to watch is simpler: a daily close back above $1.00 while sellers keep fading.
