10 Best Early Game Vehicles in GTA VI

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Shane is a Contributor for DualShockers writing guides and other materials. He has a degree in Game Design and Criticism.

Vehicles can be tough to come by in the early hours of GTA 6, and it doesn’t get much easier the farther into the game you get. Most every car you get has to be stolen in one way or another, especially after unlocking some new personal vehicles.

ATM machines hold a lot of cash in GTA 6. Learn how to break them open and steal everything inside with this guide.

There are a lot of good starter cars to register out there, but once you’re ready to expand, you might want something a little different. You won’t have an infinite amount of money, so you’ll need to be a little picky about what you want to keep.

Every vehicle on this list serves a good purpose and won’t run you into the ground money-wise, which is a nice bonus given your bank account problems. These are most of the general vehicles you can find around Port Gellhorn, although a couple are special finds.

Sometimes it’s nice to just have something a little fancy that’ll ride nice and easy down the streets. This Lowrider is by no means the faster or toughest car around, and it’s fairly average all around.

That’s what makes it a good extra car to have, because it’s fairly decent at everything. If you get it suped up, it can get much better, but since money is tight, it’s a relatively cheap option to buy, saving you some much-needed cash.

This one is a ton of fun to drive, mostly because it’s a pretty speedy vehicle on asphalt, but not so much on dirt. That’s really what drags the car down, but still it’s a nice car that’ll get you most anywhere in a jiffy.

If you’re a bit accident-prone, or prone to running into crazy NPCs, you may want to find something a bit sturdier. Still, it looks good and runs smoothly on asphalt, so it’s definitely one of the better vehicles on the road.

This car is a bit of an oddball for the area, as it’s clearly more of an upper-middle-class family car, which is not exactly what you think of in Port Gellhorn. It’s not a bad car for moving from point to point on the road or dirt.

It’s got pretty good handling and has some good size to it, making a good car for taking up space on the road. You won’t have to worry too much about getting hit with this big boy backing you up.

There are a lot of crazy looking cars on the road in GTA, but this is mostly a pretty normal-looking car. That said, it still can get going on the open road without wasting too much time, so as a getaway vehicle it’s not a bad choice.

It’s on the cheaper side and seemingly pretty abundant, so getting one shouldn’t be too difficult at this point in the game. The only real issue with the vehicle is that it’s got limited handling on both dirt and asphalt.

For the most part, you’re going to want a car over a bike, but when the moment for a bike comes, the Enduro is a great choice. It’s great for making a quick getaway on the road, provided you don’t crash into anything.

This one handles surprisingly nicely, but since it is a bike, you’re in a much more dangerous position to get hurt in an accident. Sounds silly, but when you’re holding a bag full of goodies, it’s not fun to lose it.

This is one chunky vehicle, but in a game like this, that comes in handy. It’s not bad on any terrain, and it can get up to speed pretty quickly, making it an excellent vehicle to go on a stealing spree with.

It can take a hit as good as it gives one, but its overall size might make squeezing through some tight streets a little difficult. It’s able to just move people out of the way with ease, but we all know that brings out the crazies.

While you’re mostly going to want something for the road in the early days, getting something that can do both is helpful. It’s not great on the road, but better than most, and it takes off from zero pretty quickly.

The major issue is that road handling is terrible, and the overall speed drops off pretty quickly; still, if you need to get off the road, this thing is great. You can even abuse it a bit since it’s a little stronger than most vehicles.

The Outlaw is one of the best and easiest-to-get vehicles in the game; while it might not be as nice as Paul Walker’s car, it does the job. Clearly a four-wheeler made for running off the road and making your own path.

It’s surprisingly good on both the dirt and road, but especially in the dirt, you can basically go wherever you want. It’s a beast, especially if you’re making your way through the swampy areas. It’s a bit expensive, but well worth the price.

One of the special vehicles you can find in a gang territory, this Caracara 4X4 is a phenomenal vehicle that really does it all. It’s got everything you might need in the early hours, and with some bonus strength, you can really push people around.

The only weak spot it has is that its asphalt handling is pretty bad, but who needs a road when you can just make your own? It’s a great car to keep Jason and Lucia happy and safe, without risking too much from either of them.

Another special vehicle you can steal from a gang hideout, the Firebug Dominator is an absolute godsend early on in the game. It’s got quick acceleration and some terrifying speed that’s only enhanced by the free nitro boost it comes with.

If there’s any car you need to have, it’s this one, if only because it can absolutely outrun most anyone or anything. You do have to be a little careful because of the speed, or you might find yourself off the highway and in the ocean meeting the Kraken.
