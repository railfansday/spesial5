Ocarina Remake's 'Positive' User Review Score Is Now Under Fire

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

GTA 6 fans are reportedly review bombing the game

Update [Sat 21st Nov 2026, 6pm]: Although this story started as a win for the Ocarina remake, it's taken a rather unexpected turn. As our colleagues at Push Square report, fans of GTA 6 are now allegedly going after Zelda's user scores on Metacritic.

Push Square: "In response to a coordinated review bombing effort on GTA 6, fans of the Rockstar release appear to have launched a counter-attack on The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, believing Nintendo gamers are to blame for the situation...The Switch 2’s universally adored remake of Ocarina of Time has seen its user score drop from 9.6 to 8.9 at the time of writing."

Zelda has retained a user score of 9.6/10 on Metacritic since its launch earlier this month, but as mentioned above, it's now dropped. At the time of publishing this update, it still has a "positive" rating after more than 32,000 user reviews.

As we noted in our original story, there can be all sorts of factors in play when it comes to Metacritic user scores, and they're subject to change over time (and can bounce back).
