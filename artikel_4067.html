Twitch Increases Monetization For Playing Adult Games Like GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Grand Theft Auto 6 has already been having a massive impact on the gaming landscape even before its release, with numerous games moving their launch dates away from the gaming giant or seeing how they can replicate its financial success. While not all of these have been positive so far, like the switch away from physical discs, GTA 6's sheer popularity is having one surprisingly helpful change for Twitch users.

While adult games like GTA 6 have always struggled to find a foothold on Twitch thanks to their strict gameplay guidelines, the newest updates and partnership between the streaming giant and Rockstar will not only alleviate some of the biggest hurdles, but also offer monetary incentives for anyone streaming the game.

As noted by Jake Lucky on X, Twitch is making some experimental changes to monetization on the platform which will "allow streamers to make MORE money while playing mature games like GTA," in addition to giving brands more freedom for which categories they want to advertise within.

With the information paraphrased from Bloomberg's interview with the Twitch CEO Dan Clancy, who describes GTA 6's release as "our biggest moment" for the company, the announcement has already been going over incredibly well with fans who are taking it as a "big win" for the platform that previously shunned players streaming adult games like Grand Theft Auto 5.

Even though Twitch has been under fire lately for feeding streamer data to AI and introducing a controversial new age-verification system, the change will also address the massive influx of streamers that have been abandoning the platform for other streaming services like Kick due to the lackluster monetization offered to games like GTA 5 and its massively popular roleplay streams.

While only time will tell if these changes are enough to convince previous users to come back to the platform, GTA 5's Nopixel server overhauls combined with the monolithic release of GTA 6 will make the franchise one of the most watched categories for quite some time, which Twitch seems more than ready to capitalize on if these recent changes are anything to go by.
