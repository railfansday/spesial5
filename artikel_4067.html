Modern Warfare 4 Boosts Call Of Duty Players On Xbox, But Falls Short Of Black Ops 7 Impact

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

We heard plenty of noise when Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 launched, earning the highest review scores the series has seen since Black Ops on Xbox 360. Now, Circana's Mat Piscatella has shared some new data showing just how well the game performed during its opening week.

Cricana ranks games based on its weekly player counts (not concurrent players), and places them into a top 10 list. Call of Duty shot up the standings following the release of Modern Warfare 4 (W/E October 24), ranking highly across PlayStation (PS4 and PS5) and Xbox's worldwide player standings.

Circana Player Engagement Tracker - Top 10 Titles by Weekly Active Users (Not Concurrent) - W/E Oct 24, 2026 - Call of Duty lifted across ecosystems with MW4 launch, including jumping from 32nd to 6th on US Steam - Cyberpunk 2077 returned to top 20 across ecosystems with Edgerunners 2 Update

As you can see above, COD climbed two places to take 2nd spot on Sony hardware. Whereas, on Xbox, the shooter leapfrogged NBA 2K27 to claim the bronze. It's biggest jump in weekly active players came on Steam's US charts, where Call of Duty rocketed from 32nd to 6th.

It's no surprise that Modern Warfare 4's release gave COD's active player numbers a significant boost, however, when compared with the impact of Black Ops 7's launch in 2025, the numbers aren't quite as positive.

Circana Player Engagement Tracker % of Active Panel playing Call of Duty: Week Ending Oct 24th, 2026 (Modern Warfare 4 Launch): US PS - 19% WW XBX - 17% US Steam - 4.2% Week Ending Nov 15th, 2025 (Black Ops 7 Launch): US PS - 23% WW XBX - 29% US Steam - 1.5%

On Xbox, Call of Duty accounted for 17% of active players during the week of MW4's launch, compared with 29% during Black Ops 7's 2025 launch. Of course, BO7 had a much larger player pool to draw from, having launched as the last Call of Duty day one title on Xbox Game Pass. On top of that, Modern Warfare 4 had a staggered launch, offering early access to its campaign and DMZ modes ahead of the full release on October 23rd.
