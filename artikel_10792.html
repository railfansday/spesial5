Murder Drones voice actor accuses Voicemod of selling her voice without asking for permission

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A voice actor has accused voice-changing software company Voicemod of using her voice without consent, after it released a voice changer based on her character, Cyn.

Allanah “Fitzy” Fitzgerald is an Australian voice actor best known for portraying Cyn and the Absolute Solver in animated sci-fi horror series Murder Drones. The eight-episode YouTube series premiered in 2021 and concluded in August 2024.

Voicemod, a popular real-time voice-changing platform that allows users to modify their voices during gaming, streaming, and online calls, has advertised its AI-powered voices that can mimic different characters, including Fitzgerald’s character Cyn.

On November 15, Voicemod advertised a free Cyn voice changer, encouraging fans of Murder Drones to access the feature using the promotional code “VOICEMODDRYN.”

However, Fitzgerald subsequently responded to the announcement on X, claiming the company had used her voice without consulting her.

“Haha hey… So.. what do I even do about this?” she wrote. “A whole a** company has taken my voice and is selling it to people who use their platform. I did not approve this, I was not even ASKED.”

Her response attracted attention from fans, who criticized Voicemod over the alleged lack of consent. Despite advertising the voice changer as free with a promotional code, Fitzgerald has alleged that the company profited from her performance through its platform.

At the time of writing, Voicemod has not issued a response to Fitzgerald’s statement.
