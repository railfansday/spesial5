Bella Hadid Goes Full Après-Ski Mode in Aspen

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

When the holiday season rolls in every year, you can always count on a few surefire things: Mariah Carey starts defrosting, the winter coats come out. . . and Bella Hadid heads to Aspen. The supermodel has made it a tradition to head to the Colorado ski town around this time of year—and better yet, she always brings an impeccably cozy, après-ski wardrobe with her.

Last night, Hadid headed out for dinner in Aspen wearing her latest bundled-up look. She wore a plush faux-fur coat, and layered it over a navy zip-up windbreaker and classic skinny jeans. If you think she was wearing snow boots (or her sister Gigi Hadid’s new Guest in Residence x Moon Boot collab), think again: Hadid wore a vintage pair of Chanel cap-toe heels instead.

On her arm, the model—who most recently walked in the 2026 Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show—also carried a sleek brown leather Coach bag. Her outfit’s assortment of pieces falls right in line with her typical Aspen signatures: In the past, she’s also worn similar shearling coats or leather bags while out and about in the picturesque ski destination.

Consider Hadid’s off-duty Aspen dressing the perfect reference for how to dress for a night out in the winter. Turns out, you can be cozy and chic. Though, fun fact: The model actually does know how to ski, even if she didn’t hit the slopes this time around. But one more goes to Aspen for the chic dinners and cozy cocktail bars, no?
