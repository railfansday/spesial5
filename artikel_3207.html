Block's Adjusted Profit Jumps as Losses Climb: Rapid Earnings Reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Block's third-quarter letter shows a payments company growing faster and running leaner, plus two costs its adjusted numbers leave out.

Block (XYZ -1.40%) grew gross profit 22% in the third quarter, ahead of its own guidance, and raised its full-year outlook again. Shares rose about 8% in early after-hours trading. Its smaller workforce is now turning growth into profit, but GAAP results show two costs that the adjusted figures skip: loan losses and a third straight quarter of large legal charges.

Square's growth is real and speeding up. U.S. payment volume growth rose to 10%, and gross profit grew in step with volume as field sales reps and reseller partners won sellers still on older systems. In payments, more volume at steady pricing compounds.

The adjusted jump comes from February's staff cuts, which shrank product spending while gross profit kept rising. The catch is what adjusted results exclude: $435 million of contingency, restructuring, and other charges, mostly accrued legal costs, after similar charges in each of the prior two quarters. The letter doesn't name the legal matter.

Credit is the other cost: transaction and loan losses rose 66%, three times as fast as gross profit, as Cash App Borrow and Afterpay lending grew. Loan fees count in gross profit, while the losses land below it as an operating expense. Management says lending margins held steady with last year.

Watch whether loss growth slows in the fourth quarter, as Block expects, once Borrow limits start rising again. Block also guides gross profit growth to slow to 15% that quarter.
