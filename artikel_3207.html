Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Fuel Tech, Inc. (FTEK +1.41%) reported a 16% revenue increase for the third quarter of 2026 while detailing a new strategic framework led by its recently appointed chief executive officer. Management reported that the company is shifting toward a more sales-driven and commercially focused organization to capitalize on growth in power infrastructure and industrial markets. The company stated it is currently engaged in a strategy planning process with external advisors to identify organic and inorganic growth opportunities, including potential acquisitions that fill product or channel gaps. Fuel Tech is also evaluating its R&D spending to ensure focus on the most promising paths to commercialization, particularly for its water technology platform. Management confirmed a pro forma backlog of $20 million and intends to complete its comprehensive strategy and M&A playbook for review by the board of directors early next year.
