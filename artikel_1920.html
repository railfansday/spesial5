‘Real quality’ – Frank Lampard praises £100m Chelsea transfer target

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Frank Lampard appears to have endorsed Chelsea’s pursuit of Alex Scott while revealing his ‘love’ for the ‘quality’ Bournemouth and England midfielder.

Scott has established himself as one of the most sought-after central midfielders in English football having joined Bournemouth from Bristol City back in 2023.

With attentions beginning to turn towards the January transfer window, speculation is mounting that the 23-year-old could have his head turned amid strong interest from the Premier League’s elite.

Though he picked up a thigh injury while on international duty with England last month, Scott’s outstanding performance in the Three Lions’ thumping 7-0 win over Croatia did little to quieten rumours of a potential big-money move.

Arsenal, Manchester City and Tottenham have all been credited with an interest in recent weeks and months, but Chelsea reportedly find themselves in pole position to land Scott this winter.

The Blues are actively scouring the market for midfield reinforcements after failing to bring in a replacement for Enzo Fernandez in the summer and are said to be ‘increasingly confident’ of reaching an agreement for Scott.

Up until this point, Bournemouth have publicly played down speculation that Scott could be on the move, especially midway through the campaign when they risk derailing the side’s encouraging progress under Marco Rose.

However, it’s understood an offer in region of £100million could be enough to convince the Cherries into selling Scott in what would undoubtedly be one of this January’s biggest deals.

One man who knows more than anyone what it takes to thrive in Chelsea’s midfield is Lampard, arguably the club’s greatest ever player, who won 14 trophies across an illustrious 13-year career at Stamford Bridge.

During a wide-ranging interview on the Rio Ferdinand Presents YouTube channel, Lampard made it clear just how highly he rated Scott when he was asked to name his dream Premier League midfield three on current form.

‘I don’t want to leave anyone out because there are some teams that are doing some stuff… I’m loving what Brighton are doing at the moment but you do come back to the bigger players sometimes, maybe Cole Palmer or Rayan Cherki,’ the Coventry head coach told Ferdinand.

‘I love Alex Scott at Bournemouth, I think he’s quality.

‘He’s injured now for a short period but I think he’s real quality in that modern all-rounder [role], in the double six/eight.’

Revealing other candidates for his midfield three, Lampard added: ‘I’ve got to put Elliot Anderson in there, in my opinion, for the way he’s playing.

‘I’m trying to think of the attacking options… I think I’d put Anderson and Declan Rice as the two sixes and then I’ll go for an eight/ten.

‘I’m trying to think of the eight-ten that’s really being decisive at the minute. Rayan Cherki is doing bits, I love Cole Palmer.’

Lampard went on to express his admiration for Martin Odegaard, explaining how he had shut down one of his close friends who had been criticising the Arsenal captain.

‘I really like Martin Odegaard. A mate of mine, an Arsenal fan, doesn’t like Odegaard, he gets on my nerves! He’s a good player,’ he went on.

‘He [Odegaard] brilliant. I bumped into him with my son as well recently, him and Kai Havertz, I know Kai, at a hotel, my boy was buzzing and they were so lovely with my boy, not that that makes them good players.

‘They were brilliant, the pair of them. And I said to my son, “This boy [Odegaard] is a good player”, because you see all sides of the game, especially when you do my job and you watch him press and what he does for the team.’

Lampard also praised Odegaard’s midfield partner Rice and questioned the ‘harsh’ criticism the England international has at times received during his career.

‘I do love Declan Rice and I think he’s become a conversation point because when you get to be so good and compete at such a level, people try to pick holes in your game and I think it’s harsh,’ he continued.

‘I think he’s a really good player and it’s just a common thing that comes with the territory.

‘It’s fine and Declan will just shrug it off.’
