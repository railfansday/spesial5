Sources: Celtic to block Mika Baur offer

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Celtic are not considering the sale of Mika Baur as a possibility in the January transfer window, sources have told Football Insider.

The Hoops paid just £4.5million to sign the 22-year-old from Paderborn in the summer transfer window, in a deal that looks to have been a bargain.

The midfielder has been superb this season, and was rewarded with his senior debut for Jurgen Klopp's Germany in October.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Celtic have no intention of cashing in on Baur's excellent form despite growing interest.

Clubs in the Premier League and across Europe will undoubtedly be keeping an eye on the German, who was targeted to replace Arne Engels.

The Belgian had also joined Celtic from Germany in 2024, and was sold to West Ham in the summer transfer window after a deal worth £22m was agreed.

Celtic's current concerns surrounding Baur are limited to an injury he suffered against Kilmarnock last week.

The midfielder has since pulled out of international duty, and will undergo scans on an knock to his ankle.

Any extended absence will be a real concern for Martin O'Neill, who has been impressed by Baur's adaptation to Scottish football.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I think he’s too important to let go, especially midway through the season.

"He's become a key man for Martin O'Neill's side since his arrival, Mika Baur. 19 appearances, four goals, starting to build a relationship now with [Callum] McGregor, too.

"He’s going to be vital for Celtic if they are to retain their Scottish Premiership title, and if they are to progress any further in Europe, he is going to be instrumental in that as well.

"I don’t think Celtic will be contemplating cashing in on Mika Baur in January even if there was interest."

It has become more and more clear by the passing weeks this season that Celtic have signed a gem in Baur.

That realisation often comes with the expectation that the player will not hang around for long, but the Hoops will be keen to hold onto him until at least the end of the season.

"Terrific today, really terrific," O'Neill said of Baur in October after he had produced a superb display against Motherwell.

"You know, considering there’s not much to him in terms of physicality, he can definitely play the game, and I thought he was very impressive."
