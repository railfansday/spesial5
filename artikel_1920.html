Barnsley FC head coach Daniel Stendel handed vote of confidence as Reds stalwart steps up

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The 23rd-placed Reds, kept off the bottom of the table only on goal difference, are winless in nine league games since September 2.

They host Sheffield Wednesday in a televised Yorkshire derby on Friday night.

A meagre return of just two points from the last 27 has put the future of Stendel, in his second spell at the club, under scrutiny.

But Parekh insists that the focus is on giving 'full support' to the German and his staff - amid a period of change behind the scenes at Oakwell.

Outgoing chief executive officer Jon Flatman has now left the club to take up a CEO role at Cardiff Rugby after serving out his notice period.

Club stalwart Bobby Hassell will take charge of the footballing side of the operation on an interim basis ahead of the Reds appointing a new CEO - with a leading recruitment firm co-ordinating the search.

Parekh said: "I want to reassure our supporters that the club is in good hands in the interim.

"Bobby has lived and breathed this club as a player and a member of staff and he will lead our football operation alongside Daniel and his staff through the January window.

"He performed a similar role during the January 2024 window and has a strong working relationship with Daniel.

"I would also like to reiterate that Daniel and his staff have our full support.

"Stability matters and continuous change will not build strong foundations.

"The process to appoint a new chief executive is well under way and we will ensure we get the right person. In the meantime, our focus is where it should be: on the pitch and on our preparations for January.

"At the end of the day, we all care about this club and want it to succeed and that can only happen if we come together as a collective."

A club statement has also confirmed that its commercial operations and governance will be overseen by existing senior staff - following Flatman's exit.
