Stefan Borson: Why Everton are a 'more attractive' takeover opportunity than Newcastle

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Friedkin Group (TFG) should not be concerned about their sale of Everton after the latest news emerging from Newcastle United.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that the Toffees could easily been seen as the "more attractive" proposition.

TFG officially announced their decision to explore a sale in October, very soon after a very frustrating summer window at the Hill Dickinson Stadium.

Newcastle's owners, PIF, are now the latest to seek new investment, and are open to selling 25 per cent of their stake to aid stadium redevelopment.

The Magpies have already been in talks with potential US investors, and understandably, this has raised concerns over a potential sale on Merseyside.

TFG are still yet to find a solution to their desire to leave Everton already, and now there appears to be more competition in the market.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson believes that the Toffees' stadium is a big enough selling point to avoid any concerns about TFG getting stuck.

"Now, the price level is totally different, but they're still selling a story around a football club development, a good stadium and a good training ground possibility," he said.

"And then you've got Everton looking to sell completely. You've got all of the stuff I've just mentioned about: interest rates, inflation, everything else.

"They're all drivers that are negative to football clubs. Somebody might take it the other way. To take it the other way, is the Everton opportunity now more attractive because they built the stadium?

"That was always a big selling point, but actually, in an environment where it would cost even more to build that stadium today...

"...Does that make the club much more attractive to be purchased today. Looking to go forward, yes, I can see that."

Investors choosing Newcastle shouldn't be totally ruled out, though, as PIF have built an incredible club at St James' Park as well.

"Newcastle have a lot of features that are going to be attractive, of course. As do Everton. They're actually in a similar kind of position for the next phase," Borson said.

"Everton have lower revenues, but haven't invested anywhere near what Newcastle have over the last few years on the playing side.

"So there is that blank piece of paper opportunity there for them, especially given that they don't look like they've got the relegation risk."
