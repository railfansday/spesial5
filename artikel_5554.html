Coca-Cola HBC is listing on the Johannesburg Stock Exchange ahead of its $2.6B Africa deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Trading on the JSE is set to start Nov. 23, and the bottler expects to complete its Coca-Cola Beverages Africa purchase on Nov. 27

Coca-Cola $KO HBC announced a secondary listing on the Johannesburg Stock Exchange on Monday, with trading set to begin Nov. 23, ahead of its $2.6 billion deal to acquire a 75% stake in Coca-Cola Beverages Africa.

The JSE approved the fast-track listing of all issued Coca-Cola HBC stock by way of introduction, the company said. Trading is scheduled to start at 2 a.m. ET under the share code CHB. The stock is primarily listed in London, with a secondary listing in Athens. Because the listing is an introduction, no stock is being offered to South African investors.

The company expects to complete the acquisition on Nov. 27. Completion depends on the JSE listing becoming effective and on approvals for trading of the new stock to be issued in the deal.

Coca-Cola HBC is buying the stake from The Coca-Cola Co. and Gutsche Family Investments for a combined $2.6 billion at the time of signing. Coca-Cola Co. is selling a 41.52% interest for about $1.3 billion in cash. Gutsche Family Investments is selling its 33.48% interest for about $308 million in cash plus new Coca-Cola HBC stock equal to 5.45% of the enlarged share capital. That new stock is expected to be admitted to trading in London, Johannesburg and Athens on Nov. 30.

Coca-Cola HBC has received merger control approvals in five of six jurisdictions. It agreed with the sellers to complete the deal without implementing it in Tanzania until the Fair Competition Commission of Tanzania gives its approval.

Coca-Cola Beverages Africa is headquartered in Johannesburg, has more than 13,000 employees and is the largest Coca-Cola bottler in Africa, the company said. The acquisition results in a combined group that ranks No. 2 worldwide among Coca-Cola bottlers in terms of volume. Coca-Cola HBC will also hold an option to buy the remaining 25% stake from Coca-Cola Co. between three and five years after completion.

Once the deal closes, and assuming no other stock is issued, Kar-Tess Holding will own 22.14% of Coca-Cola HBC, Coca-Cola Co. will own 20.30% and Gutsche Family Investments will own 5.45%. Kar-Tess is a vehicle through which relatives of Chairman Anastassis David hold their investments, according to Bloomberg.

The company said the listing demonstrates its commitment to South Africa and the African continent and gives global and local investors another exchange on which to buy its stock. As of Nov. 13, Coca-Cola HBC had a market capitalization of about £15.45 billion ($20.2 billion).
