E.U. ministers are pushing for a December deal on ECB's next president

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Finance ministers took stock of nominations from Klaas Knot and Pablo Hernández de Cos as leaders weigh filling three ECB jobs as one package

Euro zone finance ministers reviewed two candidacies to succeed European Central Bank President Christine Lagarde on Monday and passed the matter to EU leaders, who are aiming to settle it in December, according to Bloomberg.

Eurogroup President Kyriakos Pierrakakis said in a statement that the group received two candidacies by the Nov. 6 deadline: Pablo Hernández de Cos of Spain and Klaas Knot of the Netherlands. Lagarde will resign by Feb. 7, 2027, the Eurogroup said.

“It is possible that all three appointments will be considered together at the level of European leaders in order to facilitate consensus,” Pierrakakis told reporters in Brussels on Monday, according to Bloomberg. Before the meeting, he told reporters it would be mainly an information point and a discussion, because leaders have traditionally driven the decision, according to RTÉ.

Leaders also have to fill the seats held by Isabel Schnabel, who oversees market operations, and Chief Economist Philip Lane. Officials said the three jobs are likely to be negotiated as a package among France, Germany, Spain and the Netherlands, according to RTÉ.

Knot is a former Dutch central bank governor, and Hernández de Cos heads the Bank for International Settlements, according to RTÉ.

Under the process the Eurogroup described, the European Council appoints ECB Executive Board members on a recommendation from the Council. That recommendation needs a reinforced qualified majority: at least 16 of the 21 euro area countries, representing at least 65% of the euro area population. The ECB and the European Parliament are consulted before the European Council makes a final decision.

Leaders may take up the issue as early as Friday, when they gather informally in Dublin, according to Bloomberg. Meetings are also scheduled for Nov. 26-27 and Dec. 17-18 in Brussels, Bloomberg said.

Dutch Finance Minister Eelco Heinen said the pick should turn on merit rather than on political horse-trading, according to RTÉ. Spanish Economy Minister Carlos Cuerpo voiced optimism that Hernández de Cos will get the job, according to RTÉ.

Lagarde announced on Oct. 29 that she would step down before her term ends. Schnabel is leaving on Jan. 3 to join the International Monetary Fund, and Lane's term ends May 31.
