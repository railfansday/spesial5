Procter & Gamble is cutting up to 30% of its supply-chain jobs by 2030

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The company plans a 20% to 30% cut in its product-supply workforce, contractors included, and targets $1.8 billion in annual savings

Procter & Gamble $PG said at its investor day on Thursday that it plans to shrink its product-supply workforce by 20% to 30%, including contractors, by 2030.

P&G listed the reduction alongside two other commitments for 2030: $1.8 billion in productivity savings and free cash flow productivity of 90% or more. Chief Product Supply Officer Luc Reynaert presented the commitments.

The cuts are part of Supply 3.0, the company's supply-chain program. The program aims to hold supply steady, bring more digital and automated processes into operations, teach workers to use the new tools and make operations more sustainable, according to The Wall Street Journal.

P&G is aiming for as much as $10 billion in total productivity savings. The target spans the period from fiscal 2027 to fiscal 2030, and the $1.8 billion falls within it, drawn from the cost of goods sold, according to Reuters. CFO Andre Schulten said the savings are intended chiefly to fund reinvestment.

P&G has opened 15 "unattended" logistics sites and intends to add more over the next few years, according to Bloomberg. Reynaert said the operations should help productivity climb 20% by 2030, "freeing up our employees for higher value work."

About 52,000 employees spread across roughly 35 countries worked in manufacturing as of a May 2025 filing, close to half of P&G's headcount, according to Bloomberg.

P&G announced a portfolio and productivity plan in June 2025 and expects about $2 billion in before-tax restructuring costs over two years. The company said last year that 7,000 corporate roles would be eliminated across a two-year span as part of the plan, according to Bloomberg.

P&G cautioned last month that higher costs for raw materials, energy and freight would squeeze annual profit further, according to Reuters. P&G stock gained 1.2% before the opening bell on Friday.
