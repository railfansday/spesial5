Ryan Naderi backed to take Rangers goal touch into daunting Spain showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Rangers striker Ryan Naderi is at the top of his game just now, reckons Czech Republic No.2 Radek Bejbl.

And he could be set to get the nod to play against reigning World and European champions Spain on Thursday night.

The 23-year-old opted to represent the Czechs in the last international break and his paperwork was rushed through to allow him to make his debut. Naderi has been in fine form for Rangers and has scored six goals in his last seven games for Derek McInnes' side.

And their assistant manager - former Atletico Madrid, Lens and Slavia Prague player Bejbl - has been impressed with his progress at Ibrox, ahead of their games against Spain in Prague and Croatia in Osijek.

He said: "Every manager is excited to have a goal-scoring striker so we are delighted with Ryan. We know his strengths and we know from which positions he scores goals. Most of the time he is where he should be and he is in the right spot to score.

"So we are happy that he has come to this international window with such good recent scoring form. We are aware that we have a lot of inexperienced players in the squad at the moment.

"But these players have already shown how dangerous they can be at their clubs and they will prove this at international level too."
