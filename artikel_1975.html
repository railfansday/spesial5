Ex-Tottenham scout: 'The writing is on the wall' for Mikey Moore, deal should be terminated

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Mikey Moore must be brought back to Tottenham Hotspur in January after an unsuccessful loan move to FC Koln.

That is the opinion of former senior Tottenham scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 19-year-old has not scored a single goal or provided an assist in nine appearances for the German side so far this season, and appears to be struggling to adapt.

It was not clear whether Moore would be loaned out at all this season. He enjoyed a strong pre-season with Tottenham and scored and assisted against Charlton in the Carabao Cup in August.

The winger spent last season with Rangers and seemed to thrive off the experience, with many believing him ready to make an impact in Roberto De Zerbi's team.

Ultimately, however, the arrivals of Savinho, Mykhailo Mudryk and Omar Marmoush made a loan likely the more beneficial decision for Moore.

Tottenham's relief will be that Moore is, at least, getting the minutes he needs at Koln - the winger has appeared in every game since he arrived, starting six of nine.

And the Lilywhites will hope that if the team's form improves over the coming months, so will the winger's.

Koln have won just one game in the Bundesliga since Moore arrived, scoring just seven goals and currently sitting 15th.

"Now, that is a disappointment, isn't it? Wrong place to go on loan for me," King told Football Insider. "Again, that's Tottenham.

"They got rid of the boy [Will] Lankshear, and now this kid's gone to Germany. It's one thing going to Glasgow Rangers. At least you haven't got a problem with the language.

"He had a decent start to the season at Tottenham. For me, it was the wrong club. He'd have done far better staying, okay, he might not have been playing in the first team, but then you don't know.

"I would have been happier for him to be working and training with the first team at Tottenham, and maybe looking for a Championship club.

"He's had five starts at Koln. I would say the writings on the wall. January, come home, son."

Moore started and scored for England's Under-21s on Monday as they beat France 3-2.

Speaking after the game, he was asked about his season and said: "Obviously it’s not been an easy start. It’s been a tough run of results, a tough start for us as a team.

"I think I’m doing quite well with my performances but I’ve got a lot more to add with goals and assists because that’s what everyone looks at.

"For me personally, that’s what I need to add and then as a team we need to climb the table because we don’t want to be so close to that relegation zone.”
