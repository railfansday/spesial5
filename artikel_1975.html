Crawley Town's match at Rotherham United postponed due to international call-ups

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Crawley Towns’ trip to Rotherham United has been postponed.

Their match at the the New York Stadium on Saturday, November 14, has been called off due to international call-ups.

Reds recently had two weekends off during the last International break due to call ups.

The club posted on X: “ Crawley Town Football Club can confirm that next Saturday's away match at Rotherham United has been postponed owing to the home side receiving three international call-ups.

“A new date and time for the fixture will be communicated at the earliest possible opportunity.”

Rotherham said: “ With news of those individuals’ involvement for their respective nations under embargo until such time that the squads are announced, we are not yet able to publicly confirm which players will jet off for international duty, but are keen to inform supporters at the earliest possible opportunity of the upcoming postponement.”
