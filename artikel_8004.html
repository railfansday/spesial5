What To Buy With Gift Cards: 28 Best Things To Spend Your Holiday Money On

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Because nobody else is going to buy you that weighted vest you’ve been eyeballing for months.

And listen, don’t do that thing we all sometimes do and leave them to languish in a desk drawer or a dusty corner of your inbox. January is the perfect time to treat yourself to the stuff you’d never splurge on otherwise. Buy the fancy version of something you use every day! Hit Ulta to refill your empty beauty bottles! Put that Visa gift card toward something that will make you feel better!

Here’s what the Scary Mommy team is spending our holiday gift cards on this year, and we hope you’ll find some inspiration here for your own post-holidays shopping spree.

I love the concept behind this knife: A female chef was tired of all kitchen knives being designed and fitted for men, so she literally took matters into her own hands. Enter the Cardinal knife, which comes with multiple handles for the size of your grip. This is such a great, high-quality knife, and having it fit my hand makes chopping so much easier and more enjoyable. I love the red color, too. And like everything I buy these days, it's dishwasher-safe. — Sarah Aswell, Deputy Editor, News & Social

I love to use holiday gift cards to zhoosh up my normal routine by spending a little more on “everyday” stuff, like fancy toothpaste. Before Oral Care’s whitening toothpaste uses nano-hydroxyapatite, a mineral ingredient that’s having a major moment. Plus, it skips the peroxide, so it’s gentler than the strips that leave my teeth zinging. I also really appreciate that it comes in a recyclable, plant-based tube. — Julie Sprankles, Deputy Editor, Lifestyle & Entertainment

I feel like I am constantly looking for a flashlight in my house, and when I finally find one, it's out of batteries or not bright enough to help me. This rechargeable flashlight has solved all of my problems. It is incredibly bright, it can either be plugged in or used with traditional batteries, and it is substantial enough to beat an intruder to death with it if it comes to that. I have it in one safe, special place where I know I can grab it quickly when I need it. — Sarah Aswell

If you want to spend a gift card on something you’ll use every single night, a pillow is the way to go, and this one has a curved shape that’s designed to cradle your neck and shoulders whether you sleep on your side or your back. The fill is a copper-infused, CertiPUR-US certified foam that stays cool, plus there’s a hidden zipper so you can add or remove fill until it’s exactly the right height, and it comes with a cooling organic bamboo pillowcase. It’s handmade in California, comes in queen, king, and travel sizes, and has a 60-night trial, so it’s a low-risk splurge. — Katie Garrity, Senior Editor, News & Social

I’m spending one of my gift cards on this for my daughter because it’s a screen-free drawing toy that actually teaches her how to draw. The robot scans a card, then uses light and voice guidance to walk kids through each drawing step by step, and it comes with 150 word cards and 12 color pens, so she can learn to draw and read new words at the same time. It’s a Montessori-style, ages 3 to 8 gift that she can use on her own, which means I get a few quiet minutes too! — Katie Garrity

If I’m putting my gift card toward something practical, I want it to be something that still feels like a bit of a splurge. The Shark AquaReach Pro is just the investment. It vacuums and mops at the same time, and the detachable wand lets me clean way past the floor: baseboards, tile walls, under the couch... the whole shebang. This saves me so much time when I’m cleaning the kitchen and bathrooms! — Julie Sprankles

My dad gets me an REI gift card every year — it's a reminder of all the outdoors stuff we did together when I was a kid, which often involved trips to the store (my dad is actually one of REI's earliest members!). This year, I'm going to pick up this Veer Hauler, after years of seeing my friends use similar wagons to haul equipment and luggage on trips. I love that you don't have to pack and unpack with this version, and it won't take up the entire back of my car when I bring it places. Cannot wait for this to change my game when car camping this summer. — Sarah Aswell

It has a two-stage filter designed to cut down on chlorine and buildup without sacrificing water pressure; I genuinely feel like I can already tell a difference in my hair and skin. — Julie Sprankles
