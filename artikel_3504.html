A carefully selected coat can make even the most casual outfit look expensive, and Hilary Duff was spotted wearing a cropped peacoat style that doesn’t overwhelm petite frames. She paired the rich-looking layer with a surprisingly casual outfit, proving that getting dressed in cold weather is so much easier when you have a versatile, throw-on-and-go coat that reads sophisticated, not sloppy.

On a coffee run in Los Angeles, Duff sported a chic, cropped peacoat that has us rethinking how we wear the timeless jacket style. To recreate the look for less, we rounded up 13 similar peacoats and jacket-inspired alternatives from Amazon, featuring everything from oversized lapels and double-breasted fronts to cropped hems and dramatic sleeves. They’re not Duff’s exact coat, but they capture that same polished, yet casual-chic appeal.

1. Pocket Perfection: This cropped peacoat keeps things simple with a single-breasted front, practical pockets and a shorter hem that complements high-waisted denim. It’s church without trying too hard, and it’s still warm even though it’s fashion-forward.

2. Tailored Touch: Between the sharp notched lapels and classic double-breasted front, this peacoat has the refined appearance of traditional tailored outerwear. The cropped cut makes it feel modern without giving up its timeless appeal.

3. Short and Sweet: This overcoat takes the sophistication of a traditional notched-collar jacket and reinterprets it in a cute, cropped design. The result is a versatile layer that won’t hide your favorite jeans, trousers or skirts.

4. The Oversized Edit: Channel Duff’s relaxed approach to outerwear with this roomy peacoat in classic black. The oversized fit creates that effortless cool A-lister effect, and the double-breasted front keeps it feeling sophisticated.

5. Classic With a Twist: The oversized design of this classic peacoat gives it a laid-back feel, while traditional double-breasted detailing adds just enough structure to elevate the overall look. Consider it your shortcut to making leggings and sneakers look like more than just a gym outfit.

6. Asymmetrical Appeal: This jacket shakes things up with an asymmetrical design that instantly makes a statement, even if the rest of your outfit is super simple. Add in a classic collar and cozy wool-like material, and you’ve got an eye-catching piece that looks like it came from an LA boutique.

7. Cropped and Cool: This lightweight jacket is an easier alternative to long, heavy coats on days when it’s not super cold outside. The oversized fit leaves room for cozy layers underneath and makes it perfect for pairing with high-waisted jeans or wide-leg trousers.

8. Dramatic Flair: The sleeves steal the show with this exaggerated batwing-style peacoat. The cape-like effect instantly makes your outfit far more interesting while keeping everything underneath clean and simple.

9. Double Take: It’s hard to beat a classic double-breasted peacoat, especially when it gets a modern makeover. This version trades the traditional longer length for a fresh, cropped hem that makes it your new goes-with-everything staple.

10. Polished Layer: If your outfits are yearning for a finishing touch, consider this cropped peacoat, with its double-breasted design that brings a touch of tailoring to casual looks. Throw it over your favorite sweater and jeans, and suddenly you’ll feel polished and put together.

11. Modern Classic: This jacket strikes a nice balance of timeless and trendy with its traditional double-breasted front and contemporary cropped length. It has the sophistication of a classic peacoat without the formality of a full-length overcoat.

12. Collegiate Charm: If your style skews sporty, swap your traditional peacoat for this varsity-inspired style. Its cropped, laid-back design lends itself to casual looks, like jeans and sneakers or your favorite matching workout set.

13. Statement Sleeves: With oversized batwing sleeves and a cropped hem, this jacket has an eye-catching, nearly cape-like shape that makes it feel more fashion-forward than your typical peacoat. Even the simplest outfit makes a statement when layered on top.
