Olympian Stephen Nedoroscik Introduces GF Months After Tess McCracken Split

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Olympic gymnast Stephen Nedoroscik is ready to introduce his girlfriend, Annalise Schnier, to his social media followers.

Schnier, an engineering student at the University of Arkansas, uploaded a photo booth strip taken with Nedoroscik, 28, via her Instagram Stories on Thursday, November 26. She tagged the pro athlete’s page.

Nedoroscik, who reposted Schnier’s upload, sweetly kissed his new girlfriend’s cheek in one of the images. The couple cuddled up in the other snaps.

Neither Nedoroscik nor Schnier has further detailed their relationship timeline, which comes seven months after his ex-girlfriend Tess McCracken announced their breakup following a decade of dating.

“There’s not really a good way to make this video, so I’m just gonna say it, I guess. Stephen and I are no longer together. We haven’t been since the beginning of January,” McCracken, 28, said in an April TikTok video. “It’s been long enough, and people have figured it out. [My] social media has been cleaned out. I can’t post anything without getting a few comments, and I know there have been multiple Reddit threads. I feel like it’s just easier to have it out in the open and not really have to hide it anymore.”

McCracken and Nedoroscik met in college at Penn State, often supporting one another’s gymnastic meets. McCracken even cheered on Nedoroscik at the 2024 Olympics and during his Dancing With the Stars season 33 run later that year.

While announcing their split, McCracken stressed that she holds no ill will against Nedoroscik.

“It’s weird to have to announce your breakup,” she acknowledged. “There wasn’t any drama, there wasn’t some big, blowup fight where one of us stormed out, there wasn’t any cheating [and] neither of us came out as gay. There was no drama.”

McCracken continued, “It was really just time, and that sucks a lot. There’s really no other way to put it. We were together for almost 10 years. We met and got together when we were 17 and 18, and we’re now 27 and 28. As it turns out, there are things that didn’t matter at 18 that matter when you’re in your later 20s.”

At the end of the video, McCracken wished Nedoroscik “all the best.”

“There’s no bad blood,” she concluded at the time. “I want to see him succeed in everything, but the way that support is coming has to be different now.”

Nedoroscik never publicly addressed their breakup.
