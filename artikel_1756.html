Man City wear ‘special edition’ orange kit vs Napoli in Champions League

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Manchester City have ditched their traditional sky blue home kit for their Champions League tie against Napoli on Tuesday night.

Instead of their usual home strip, City will be wearing a ‘special edition’ orange kit which has been produced by Puma but also alongside the club’s ‘official style fashionwear partner’ CP Company.

Ahead of kick-off at the Etihad Stadium, City’s players also turned up in bright orange jackets to complement the limited edition kit.

In a statement released before the game against Napoli, City said: ‘Manchester City have this evening debuted a special edition kit in collaboration with Club partners, PUMA and C.P. Company.

‘The kit will be worn for select fixtures in the Champions League, and forms part of a wider collection that will be on sale globally from Tuesday 1 December.

‘There will also be an exclusive presale event for fans on Monday 30 November at the City Store in Medlock Square.’

Meanwhile, Enzo Maresca has made five changes for his side’s Champions League game against Napoli.

From the side that started the 1-1 Premier League draw at home to Fulham on Saturday, Rayan Cherki, Enzo Fernandez, Josko Gvardiol, Matheus Nunes and Allan have all been brought into the starting line-up.

Abdukodir Khusanov, Ruben Dias, Iliman Ndiaye, Phil Foden and Jeremy Doku have all dropped to the bench.
