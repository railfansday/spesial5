What to Wear to Thanksgiving: 6 Outfit Ideas for Every Type of Gathering

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

From dressier holiday looks to comfy and stretchy.

With Thanksgiving just around the corner, I’ve already started thinking about two very important things: what’s going on the dinner table and what I’ll wear. I’m especially looking forward to my mom’s baked mashed potatoes (a family favorite) and the homemade mac and cheese I’ll be bringing along. But, especially as a style writer, half the fun of the holidays is having a good excuse to put together a good outfit.

My family’s Thanksgiving gatherings tend to be pretty casual, so I’m not one to show up in anything overly dressy or uncomfortable. I’m usually somewhere in the middle, opting for an outfit that looks like I made an effort but still lets me enjoy a second helping of stuffing without worrying about a too-tight waistband. Think a great sweater with loose-fitting jeans, a knit dress with boots, or even a smartly-styled leggings outfit. Because as much as I love dressing up for the holidays, I also want to enjoy post-dinner card games or sit comfortably on the couch afterward.

If you’re approaching Thanksgiving dressing the same way, I’ve rounded up six outfit ideas to help get you inspired. I’ve included a few options perfect for a Christmas card photoshoot, along with more laid-back looks that are just as fitting for a casual family gathering. No matter what your holiday plans look like, these outfits strike that happy medium between looking good and feeling comfortable. Now, if you’ll excuse me, I have some mac and cheese to make.

In my opinion, there’s no outfit more perfect for the holidays than a sweater dress and boots. It’s cute enough for family photos but loose and soft enough to keep you comfy all day (especially if you kick the boots off at the door). Grab a long-sleeve knit dress that hits somewhere between mid-thigh and the knee and pair it with some fleece-lined tights and your fave knee-high boot. A midi or maxi dress with an ankle bootie works too.

Start by pulling out a denim jacket and denim skirt. From there, add a plain white T-shirt. I love brown for the fall, so I wore my version of this outfit with brown boots and a matching brown belt and bag. I love this combo because it looks super stylish, but I don’t feel overdressed or uncomfortable when I wear it. Don’t have a denim skirt or jacket? No problem. You can recreate this look with a leather jacket, jeans, or any neutral mini skirt.

Start with a pair of satin or silk pants and pair them with a sweater and ballet flats in the same shade. This outfit has been one of my go-tos lately because it’s so comfortable, but the monochrome palette and shiny finish of the pants make it look like I tried way harder than I actually did. You can even make it feel a little dressier by swapping the flats for a pair of pointed-toe ankle boots.

It doesn’t get comfier than leggings and slippers. For ultra-casual gatherings when you just want to focus on family and food without looking frumpy, just style yours a little smarter. Go for a pair of leggings with minimal seaming (especially a pair without a front seam), which makes them look sleeker. Then grab a dressy-looking oversized sweater—something with a boxy cut, a turtleneck, or a nice knit. I find that my tweed stitch J. Crew Rollneck Sweater is perfect for this look. Finish it off with a neutral slipper and a pair of scrunchy socks.

Opt for a pair of baggier jeans for this more casual outfit, which will be way comfier than a restrictive skinny or straight-leg jean. A looser pant also pairs better with a cozy boot, like this platform ankle boot. Finally, throw on a long-sleeve top or sweater with a cute vest for an outfit that feels very fall.

Banana Republic The Icon Classic Wide-Leg Jean

Okay, you caught me—I actually wore this outfit for Christmas last year instead of Thanksgiving, but it definitely works for either holiday! For this combo, start with your favorite skirt and a turtleneck top. Then top it off with a relaxed-fitting blazer (I love this houndstooth blazer, but any pattern or solid color will do). On the bottom, throw on a pair of tights and a knee-high boot.
