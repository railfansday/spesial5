XRP Treasury Takes $505 Million Accounting Hit

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Home » Ripple (XRP) » XRP Treasury Takes $505 Million Accounting Hit

Evernorth, the openly-traded XRP-only corporate treasury, took in a big loss despite not selling one coin.

Evernorth Holdings has marked down the value of its pre-listing XRP holdings by roughly 60%, booking about $505 million in cumulative impairment charges despite not selling the tokens.

The company’s latest quarterly filing showed 346 million XRP carried at approximately $341.9 million as of Sept. 30, down from an original recorded cost basis near $847 million.

The write-down reflects accounting treatment rather than a realized sale or reduction in the size of the treasury.

Under the accounting rules applied in the filing, XRP is treated as an indefinite-lived intangible asset. That requires a company to reduce the carrying value when the token’s market price falls below its purchase cost.

Those impairment charges cannot simply be reversed if XRP later recovers, meaning the balance-sheet value may remain below the market value of the holdings unless the assets are sold.

The accounting treatment can therefore leave a lasting gap between a company’s reported digital-asset value and the price investors see in the market.

The filing put Evernorth’s total assets at about $348.9 million, almost entirely represented by digital assets. It also listed total liabilities of roughly $534.5 million and an accumulated deficit of about $185.6 million.

The 346 million XRP figure covers Evernorth’s pre-merger balance sheet at the end of September.

Following the completion of its merger in October, the combined business was reported to hold about 473 million XRP, with additional tokens previously held through a separate affiliated entity.

That makes the filing an important snapshot rather than a complete measure of the company’s current XRP exposure.

It also underscores the concentrated nature of Evernorth’s strategy: its asset base and future operating plans remain closely tied to one highly fluctuating cryptocurrency.

Evernorth has outlined plans to seek returns from its XRP reserves through liquidity provision, lending and options-related activity on the XRP Ledger.

Those initiatives could eventually create revenue, but the filing showed no revenue through the period covered.
