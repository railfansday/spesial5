If you've been tracking Switch 2 prices since it launched earlier this year, you'll have spotted that they haven't followed the usual slow downward drift. Instead, September brought a hefty price rise. So what happened?

The blame lies largely with power-hungry AI data centers. Throughout 2026, the big tech players hoovered up much of the world's memory chip supply, creating a RAM shortage that sent component costs soaring. Throw in a tangle of international hardware tariffs (and I wonder who we have to thank for those?!), and Nintendo's margins were suddenly stretched worryingly thin.

Rather than sell consoles at a loss, Nintendo raised prices across the entire Switch range. It wasn't alone either, as Sony and Apple have taken the same route.

The upshot? Expect some Switch deals this Black Friday, but bear in mind they'll probably be undercutting the September prices, not the original launch ones!
