FIFA: UEFA unanimously supports Philippe Diallo against Infantino

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

UEFA has officially given its support to Philippe Diallo. In a press release published this Monday, the European body confirms that the national associations represented within its executive committee and the FIFA Council have unanimously invited the president of the French Football Federation to run in the next FIFA presidential election, scheduled for March 2027. The meeting, chaired by Aleksander Ceferin, thus made it possible to validate the choice of the French leader to run the European candidacy against Gianni Infantino. The European body clearly displayed its position in its press release. “The European national associations represented on the UEFA Executive Committee and the FIFA Council unanimously support Philippe Diallo for the presidency of FIFA. At a meeting held today and chaired by UEFA President Aleksander Ceferin, the national associations represented on the UEFA Executive Committee and the FIFA Council invited Philippe Diallo, President of the French Football Federation (FFF), to put himself forward as a candidate in the FIFA presidential election in March 2027 and unanimously expressed their full support.specifies the press release.

“Philippe Diallo has extensive experience at all levels of football, having led the organization representing French professional football clubs, served on the board of directors of the French Professional Football League and within FIFA’s dispute resolution structures, before becoming President of the FFF and member of the UEFA Executive Committee in 2023. Philippe Diallo accepted the invitation to stand. His candidacy should be formally ratified by the FFF Executive Committee at its meeting tomorrow», It is written in the rest of the missive. The president of the FFF must now have his candidacy validated by the executive committee of his federation.
