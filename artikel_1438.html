Dele Alli risks the wrath of Spurs fans as he wears an ARSENAL shirt on livestream - with ex-Tottenham star livid after losing bet to influencer iShowSpeed

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

By BEN WILLCOCKS, ASSISTANT SPORTS EDITOR

Published: 09:49 EST, 7 November 2026 | Updated: 09:53 EST, 7 November 2026

Dele Alli has risked infuriating Tottenham supporters after wearing an Arsenal jersey on a Twitch livestream.

The former England midfielder, whose once-bright career has been in freefall since his exit from Tottenham in 2022, has been keeping himself busy on a series of livestreams with fans as he continues to look for a new club.

The 30-year-old star, who has been a free agent since leaving Como in September 2025, joined iShowSpeed on stream after the American influencer challenged him to a game of EAFC 27.

Dele, who played as Saudi Arabian outfit Al-Qadsiah, was beaten 4-2 by iShowSpeed's Al-Nassr and reacted so angrily to missing a late chance that he furiously smashed his controller.

Egged on by the influencer saying, "I ain't gonna lie, throw the controller, break it", as he missed an open goal, the footballer left his chair and started repeatedly launching it against the floor.

Dele's punishment for losing the bet was to wear an Arsenal shirt live on stream, and the former Tottenham midfielder reluctantly obliged.

Sporting the jersey of his old team's bitter north London rivals, which had been delivered to his home by a friend, Dele burrowed his head in his hands with discomfort as fans caught a glimpse of him representing Arsenal for the first time.

Gunners supporters were greatly amused as videos and images of Dele, who in 2016 said he would rather retire than play for Arsenal, went viral on social media.

'Dele Alli looks good in an Arsenal kit, he must be feeling victorious,' one fan posted to X with a string of laughing emojis.

He later posted a selfie to his Instagram, saying: 'Sorry Spurs fans but a bet is a bet. Congrats iShowSpeed.'

Although a regular streamer nowadays, Dele was once lauded as one of English football's greatest talents, having burst on to the scene under Mauricio Pochettino and carving out almost 200 appearances for the north London club.

He is now making a habit of phoning old team-mates for a chat in front of his audience of followers on stream, having held brief conversations with both Jack Grealish and Tottenham legend Harry Kane.
