Adam Schefter names which NFL team will be the busiest before the trade deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

As some teams sell to commence a rebuild and other teams look to buy to make a playoff push, the NFL insider has named which team is the most motivated to do business.

The NFL trade deadline is in just four days' time and right now, it's hard to say which teams might make big moves. The Indianapolis Colts have committed to their rebuilding, and Jonathan Taylor remains one of the biggest names on the market as a result.

But after the Chicago Bears addressed their defensive issues and the Seattle Seahawks bolstered their offensive line, it's hard to think of any teams particularly looking to buy. NFL insider Adam Schefter has thrown one name into the mix though, which isn't too surprising.

After week eight of the new season, the New York Giants find themselves sitting atop the NFC East. With a 4-2-1 record, they are the only team in their division above .500. The Cowboys are struggling, the Eagles have been decimated by injuries, and the Commanders have disappointed.

As such, this is the time for the Giants to make some quick improvements and potentially make some noise when the end of the season comes around. And it seems that is very much the plan.

"I think a team that seems h***bent on making a move and upgrading its roster before the trade deadline would be the New York Giants," Schefter said.

"And I think if they beat the Eagles on Sunday, that just adds fuel to the fire and makes it even more so that they make an impact move before the trade deadline. That would be a team I'm watching. They feel like they want to make a move."

The Giants could really use an elite running back, so Taylor's name has been in the mix. Derrick Henry and Breece Hall have also been mentioned. The team could also do with a DT, with Dalvin Tomlinson being mentioned as a potential option. It remains to be seen what they decides to pursue.
