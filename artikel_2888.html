ESPN predicts the outcome of Saturday's Auburn-Samford game

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Auburn is on a two-game winning streak after beating Arkansas and Mississippi State, and now has six wins this season. They hope to pick up their seventh win on Saturday at home against Samford.

The Tigers will be a heavy favorite when they play the Samford Bulldogs on Saturday in the final game of the season at Jordan-Hare Stadium. According to the ESPN Matchup Predictor, Auburn has a 99% chance of winning in week 12.

While Auburn is expected to defeat Samford on Saturday, they still need to play clean football. It will be important for Auburn to carry momentum into the annual Iron Bowl in Week 13. This game will also be a great chance for some inexperienced players to get some quality playing time.

A win on Saturday would give Auburn seven wins, which would snap the Tigers' streak of losing seasons at five. They have not finished a season with a winning record since the final season with Gus Malzahn as head coach in 2020.

The game will begin at 2:30 p.m. CT, and it will be broadcast by the SEC Network.
