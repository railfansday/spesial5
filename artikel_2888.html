Packers Injury Report: 9 players limited on Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Green Bay Packers had their entire active roster available to practice for the second day in a row. Considering how banged up the roster has been this season, that's a massive win in itself.

The Packers didn't release a Saturday injury report, though, so Sunday was our first look at details on Green Bay's injuries. Today, the Packers listed cornerback Brandon Cisse (rib), linebacker Zaire Franklin (shin), running back Josh Jacobs (finger), linebacker Isaiah McDuffie (rib), receiver Bo Melton (thigh), receiver Skyy Moore (ankle), edge defender Micah Parsons (knee), center Sean Rhyan (ankle) and guard Kevin Zeitler (triceps) as limited participants.

Cisse, Melton and Moore were unable to finish the game against the Minnesota Vikings last week, while Franklin, Jacobs, Parsons, Rhyan and Zeitler never suited up. The only surprise on this list is that McDuffie is still dealing with a rib injury, as he missed just one snap of action against the Vikings and came back into the game.

Guard Aaron Banks is listed with only a knee injury, instead of both a knee and groin injury like in previous weeks, and was a full participant on Sunday. According to head coach Matt LaFleur, Banks is competing at both the left guard and right guard positions for playing time this week. If Banks doesn't earn a starting role in practice, the assumption is that those gigs will go to Laken Tomlinson (left guard) and Kevin Zeitler (right guard).

It's also worth noting that teams are only allowed three padded practices from Week 12 to Week 18 of the NFL regular season. In preparation for their Week 12 matchup against the Los Angeles Rams, the Packers decided to use a padded practice on Sunday.
