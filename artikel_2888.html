Ravens rule out Nnamdi Madubuike, Jaylinn Hawkins

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Ravens head coach Jesse Minter ruled out a pair of players for Sunday’s road game against the Panthers.

Minter told reporters that defensive tackle Nnamdi Madubuike and safety Jaylinn Hawkins will both miss the game. Minter announced earlier in the week that cornerback Marlon Humphrey will not play due to derogatory comments he made about the city of Baltimore, so they will be short three defensive players overall.

Madubuike hurt his hamstring last Monday and did not practice at all this week. Hawkins has missed the last four games with a knee injury.

Right guard Vega Ioane (knee, shoulder) returned to full practice participation Thursday and appears to be on track to play this weekend.
