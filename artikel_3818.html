It's come to my attention that some of us aren't having a very fun time with the NPCs in Grand Theft Auto 6. I've seen videos and threads of players complaining about not being able to steal cars because of crack head drivers, getting hunted down by killer NPCs, and just generally having a harder time traversing Leonida because who knows what bloodthirsty beast hides behind each corner? And to this I say, your steak is too buttery? Your lobster too juicy?

My first interaction with one of the unfriendly residents of Leonida started when I accidentally bumped into their car. Because I am wholly unaware of my surroundings, I didn't even notice that anyone was mad at me as I listened to my pop songs and carried on driving towards my mission. Little did I know that right behind me was a four-wheeler with a driver who had steam coming out of her ears.

So I pull up to the side of the road, ready to head in and start my mission, when seemingly out of nowhere the four-wheeler comes barreling in, almost taking me out completely. The driver gets out and starts screaming about payback. I have no idea what I've done, but this is GTA 6, I'm not taking this slander. So I start swinging.

The fight ends with me knocking this lady out, but she doesn't go down without a fight. At one point, I actually thought that was it for me—taken out on the pavement by a soccer mum, what a way to go.

But even if I had taken a long sleep on the sidewalk, that would've been OK, because it's all part of the beautiful package that GTA 6 offers.

I don't want vapid NPCs that buckle at the first sight of a handgun, or have the object permanence of a goldfish. Filling Leonida with some actually competent, and dare I say dangerous NPCs makes this game infinitely more interesting. I like not knowing how one small action will ruin my whole day, or having to be a little wary of the people around me lest I want to incur a murderous chase across the city streets.

Terrorising helpless NPCs is fun, but only for a short while.

I have a theory as to why players are pushing back against aggressive NPCs: they just don't like having to put in the effort after years of being able to do whatever they want in GTA Online. There's certainly some that will look back on the mostly timid NPCs of Los Santos and miss when all they had to do to steal a car was swing open its door and throw the limp driver out. Aside from shouting, there wasn't really anything a regular NPC could do to pose a threat to you back then.

Rockstar amped up the mayhem and carnage you could cause, turning it into a chaotic playground with few repercussions. GTA Online players have become accustomed to walking around like some vengeful god, wreaking havoc wherever or whenever they see fit, and only very occasionally dealing out mercy. Now these fickle deities have been forced to become human again.

A part of me understands the appeal. Terrorising helpless NPCs is fun, but only for a short while. NPCs that actually have a backbone offer an experience that will keep me interested for far longer.

Plus, Leonida is based on Florida, do you honestly think you could terrorise the streets of Miami or Tampa Bay and walk out completely unscathed? Yeah, I thought as much.
