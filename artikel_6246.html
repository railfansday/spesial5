Marriott Bonvoy Bevy Card Review: Strong Bonus Categories, Weak Overall Value

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Both American Express and Chase issue co-branded Marriott credit cards, meaning consumers have lots of options to choose from. This is my Marriott Bonvoy Bevy® American Express® Card review, as I’d like to take a look at whether this Marriott credit card is worth it.

Personally I don’t think that the Bonvoy Bevy Card is Marriott’s most compelling credit card option, and given how the offers on some cards are mutually exclusive, I think there’s a better strategy. However, everyone should decide for themselves, based on their own situation. Let’s look at the value proposition of the Bonvoy Bevy Card, including the welcome offer, annual fee, return on spending, and perks, so you can make your own decision.

When applying for a credit card, obviously the welcome bonus, eligibility rules, and fees, are major factors that people consider. After all, this determines how much value you can get upfront, and also how much this card will cost you in the long run. With that in mind, let’s take at the bonus and fee details for the Marriott Bonvoy Bevy Card.

The Marriott Bonvoy Bevy Card currently has a welcome offer where you can earn 85,000 Bonvoy bonus points after spending $5,000 within the first six months. Based on my latest miles & points valuations, Bonvoy points are worth 0.7 cents each, meaning I value the welcome offer at $595.

This is the standard welcome offer on the card, though we’ve definitely seen better bonuses in the past. For example, in the past I recall seeing a bonus of up to 125,000 points plus a $150 statement credit upon completing minimum spending. So it could be worth waiting to apply to see if a higher offer comes back around.

If applying for the Marriott Bonvoy Bevy Card, Amex’s general application restrictions apply, and on top of that, there are some additional restrictions for Marriott credit card eligibility.

The most important thing to understand is that you are eligible for this card, including the welcome offer, if you have the Marriott Bonvoy Business® American Express® Card (learn more). However, you aren’t eligible for the welcome offer if you have the Marriott Bonvoy Brilliant® American Express® Card (learn more).

The Marriott Bonvoy Bevy Card has a $250 annual fee (Rates & Fees), and you can add authorized users at no extra cost.

Among Marriott personal credit cards, this card has the second-highest annual fee (there are also no annual fee and $95 annual fee options), with the Marriott Bonvoy Brilliant® American Express® Card (learn more) being the only card with a higher annual fee — it has a $650 annual fee (Rates & Fees).

The Marriott Bonvoy Bevy Card offers a variety of perks for being a cardmember. Some perks are offered just for having the card, while other perks require spending or other activity. Let’s go over the details, in no particular order.

The Marriott Bonvoy Bevy Card offers 1,000 bonus points per paid eligible stay with Marriott, which is a perk that’s unique to this card. You need to pay for your stay with the card to be eligible, and only paid stays booked directly with Marriott qualify (so third party bookings and award stays don’t count). Bonus points will post within six weeks of an eligible stay.

For the very frequent Marriott guest, this has the potential to be pretty useful, especially if you make frequent, short stays. Based on my valuation of Bonvoy points, this is like an incremental return of roughly $7 per paid stay.

Unfortunately, the Marriott Bonvoy Bevy Card doesn’t offer a free night award just for being a cardmember. Rather, you can earn a free night award valid at a property costing up to 50,000 points when you spend $15,000 on the card in a calendar year.

It’s also possible to top off Marriott free night awards with up to 25,000 additional points to redeem at more expensive properties. This means you could redeem a free night award plus 25,000 points to stay at a hotel costing 75,000 points per night.

This is a disappointing aspect of the card, as several other Marriott credit cards don’t require you to spend any amount to earn such a certificate.

Just for having the Marriott Bonvoy Bevy Card you receive 15 elite nights toward status annually. This can help you if you’re looking to earn a higher elite tier, like Marriott Bonvoy® Platinum Elite status, Titanium Elite status or Ambassador Elite status, and it can also help you with earning Choice Benefits (like Nightly Upgrade Awards).

It’s also worth noting that you can stack the 15 elite night credits on this card with elite night credits earned on a Marriott business credit card. That gets you a long way toward the Marriott Bonvoy Platinum Elite status requirement of 50 nights.

Looking at the card in isolation, there’s a case to be made for getting the Marriott Bonvoy Bevy Card, especially if you’re a very frequent Marriott guest. If you put all your Marriott spending on the card, you’d earn 1,000 bonus points per stay, 6x points on Marriott spending, and you could earn a free night award after spending $15,000.

But even if you’re set on a Marriott credit card, there are better options, as I view it, especially given how some cards are (somewhat) mutually exclusive, at least when it comes to welcome offers.

If you ask me, the Bonvoy Bevy Card is the weakest card in the Marriott credit card portfolio in terms of value for the annual fee, and I think you’re much better off going for the higher annual fee card.

The Marriott Bonvoy Brilliant Card (learn more) has a $650 annual fee, and offers:

As I see it, the Marriott Bonvoy Brilliant Card’s annual fee easily pays for itself, between the dining credit, anniversary free night award, elite status, Priority Pass™ membership, and more.

The Marriott Bonvoy Boundless® Credit Card (learn more) has a lower annual fee of $95, but in my opinion it offers significantly more value than the Marriott Bonvoy Bevy Card. Its perks include:

The free night award alone makes this card worth it. You get this once every account anniversary year, and you can top off the free night with an additional 25,000 points. Even though the Marriott Bonvoy Bevy Card’s free night award is valid for rooms that cost up to 50,000 points, it’s not worth spending $15,000 on the card in a year just to earn it.

If you’re not actually that committed to the Marriott Bonvoy ecosystem, there are lots of other great cards to consider. In particular, these are cards with relatively reasonable annual fees and great rewards structures, and they’re very well rounded:

The Marriott Bonvoy Bevy Card has a $250 annual fee, and offers some solid bonus categories, plus 1,000 Bonvoy bonus points per stay. The card also offers Bonvoy Gold Elite status, 15 elite nights annually for being a cardmember, and a free night award when you spend $15,000 on the card in a calendar year.

Ultimately, I’d say this is a pretty weak card, especially compared to the rest of Marriott’s card portfolio. It doesn’t offer statement credits or an automatic free night award to offset the annual fee, and you can’t even spend your way to a higher status level on this card. Personally, I’d much rather get the Marriott Bonvoy Boundless Card or Marriott Bonvoy Brilliant Card, as I think the cards represent a better overall value.
