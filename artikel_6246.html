The Best Stocking Stuffer for Jet-Setters Is This Viral Handbag Accessory

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

If there’s one thing I love almost as much as planning a trip, it’s having a cute lineup of travel gear to help me get there. The perfect carry-on, a reliable travel tote, a passport holder that doesn’t look like everyone else’s—and, as of late, a bag charm (or three). The accessory du jour has officially made its way from our handbags to our airport ensembles, and I can’t think of a more delightful stocking stuffer for the traveler in your life.

Commerce writer Jessica Chapel is showing off Coach's signature cherry bag charm

Bag charms have a longer fashion history than their current moment suggests: Jane Birkin famously adorned her namesake Hermès with personal keepsakes. Hermès then introduced its Rodeo charm in 2005, and Fendi sent its furry Monster charms down the runway in 2013. After a runway revival at Miu Miu and Balenciaga shows in 2024, the look made its way back to stylish travelers everywhere.

It makes sense, really: We put so much thought into what we pack and what we carry, so why not have a little fun with the bags that get us there? A bag charm is an easy way to give a well-loved weekender a fresh look, or make that familiar canvas tote feel a bit more like your own. There are colorful beads, mini leather accessories, nostalgic trinkets, whimsy characters, and even designs with a practical purpose. Some bag charms are simply there to look good; others offer a clever place to tuck away small essentials. And when your suitcase is circling the baggage carousel among a sea of lookalikes, a fun, personal detail is hardly a bad thing.

Beyond the styling, I like to think of bag charms as a new kind of travel keepsake. We’re always bringing pieces of our trips home: a street market find, a knickknack from a beach town, some wonderfully impractical souvenir that means everything to us and very little to anyone else. Rather than tucking them away on a shelf, clip these charms onto a tote or suitcase, and they’ll join you for the next adventure. The same goes for a charm gifted by a friend or one that reminds you of home. Nothing says, “think of me on the road,” quite like this.

Bag charms add a pop of color to a neutral bag or suitcase

And there’s no shortage of ways to make the look your own. Layer a few bag charms together, mixing colors, textures, and shapes, or keep things more understated with a single leather accent or jewelry-like detail. Think of it as accessorizing your luggage with the same consideration you give the rest of your travel outfit. Whether you’re shopping for the friend who’s always planning a weekend away, the family member with an enviable vintage luggage collection, or the frequent flier who appreciates thoughtful detail, there’s something in the mix for just about every kind of traveler.

Bag charms are small, they’re personal, they’re wonderfully extra, and truthfully, a whole lot more exciting to unwrap than another pair of compression socks. (Sorry, compression socks). Ahead, I’ve rounded up the best bag charms to gift this season, from playful fashion finds and colorful conversation starters to travel-friendly designs that do a little more than just look pretty.

A little cherry on top, quite literally. Made from glittery resin and metal, this bright red pick brings a playful pop of color to a tote, backpack, or set of keys. It’s a particularly good choice for the friend whose wardrobe could always use one more fun thing.

For the person who prefers their accessories a little more understated—but still appreciates a good detail—this bad boy pairs deep green beads with gold-tone hardware and a lobster clasp, offering their favorite bag a subtle hit of color and texture.

For the New Yorker in your life, here's some Big Apple nostalgia to take on the road. Kate Spade pays homage to its hometown with this mini MetroCard-inspired design—and for those who remember the ol’ swipe of a subway card, it's a fun yet tasteful piece of the city to carry along.

I’m adding this gold boot gem to my cart. For a little Western flair—minus the need to break in a new pair of boots—the iconic brand Tecovas has turned its Loretta boot into a mini accessory crafted from solid brass with an antique finish. It comes with both a key ring and a D-ring, so it can go wherever its owner does—whether that’s a road trip, a dude ranch weekend, or simply the daily commute.

Talk about chic! Sézane’s Juliette keychain has a caramel-color resin exterior with delicate white lace detailing, but the real treat is inside: a pocket mirror that opens with a pivoting mechanism. It’s ideal for a quick lipstick check before stepping off the train or heading out to dinner.

For the friend who has approximately 1,000 photos of their dog on their phone—and would happily add one more to the rotation. This little Jack Russell is embroidered onto supple full-grain leather, with a debossed Lauren Ralph Lauren logo on the back. A rather handsome stand-in for a four-legged travel buddy.

A handbag for your handbag? I’m listening. This mini zippered pouch is styled like a classic carryall, with leather construction and gold-tone hardware, and clips onto a larger bag to keep small essentials close. It’s available in red, cobalt blue, and silver, and can be personalized with a foil-debossed monogram—making it an especially sweet gift for a loved one.

A little luck for the road never hurts! This Madewell design brings together a wishbone, stone, and moon motif in a red-and-gold-toned cluster, and is finished with a dog-clip closure. It’s a playful pick for the friend who loves a meaningful trinket—or could use a little good fortune on their next big adventure.

For the friend who's always planning their next trip, this miniature version of Away’s signature hard-shell carry-on is made from the same durable polycarbonate shell and opens into two little compartments for small essentials like a spare key or folded cash. It clips onto a bag or backpack, and the Coast Blue shade is a nice nod to the real thing.

Meet the mini-me of one of our editors' favorite travel bags. Calpak's best-selling Luka duffel—this compact zip pouch comes in a pearlescent finish and clips onto a larger bag with a carabiner-ready loop. Better yet, it actually earns its keep: There’s room for keys, earbuds, cash, or whatever small essentials you’d rather not fish around for at the bottom of your tote.

A flower that doesn’t need watering—or a vase. Inspired by the marigolds of Diwali, this three-dimensional design is made from layered Italian leather petals and comes in yellow or orange. The leather leaf can also be monogrammed, making it a thoughtful gift with a personal touch. It’s a lovely way to bring a little color to someone’s everyday bag.
