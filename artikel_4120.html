Wolfenstein 3 Is Officially In Production

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Wolfenstein 3 is officially underway at MachineGames, Xbox CEO Asha Sharma has announced.

The announcement of the third mainline Wolfenstein title in MachineGames’ trilogy comes over seven years after the launch of the fourth and most recent spin-off entry, Wolfenstein: Youngblood. Sharma’s announcement includes a teaser image of the script for Wolfenstein 3's first chapter. MachineGames echoed this reveal with its own post, which includes a photo of a motion capture session featuring actress Debra Wilson, who plays Grace Walker in the series.

Wolfenstein 3 is officially in production at @MachineGames. One of gaming's most important franchises, and a team that has done remarkable work bringing it forward. Looking forward to sharing more. https://t.co/YyX8LrbTvh

MachineGames’ last release was 2024’s well-received Indiana Jones and the Great Circle, but studio co-founder Jerk Gustafsson stated in a NoClip documentary last year that he hoped to finish BJ Blazkowicz’s story. Based on Gustafsson’s comment, it sounds like Wolfenstein 3 very recently started production, so we probably shouldn’t expect any substantial update for a good while.

This won’t be the only new Wolfenstein project, as Amazon is developing a live-action series based on the franchise. Until either the new game or the show releases, you can tide yourself over by reading our reviews of Wolfenstein: The New Order, Wolfenstein II: The New Colossus, and Wolfenstein: Youngblood.
