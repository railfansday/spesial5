Tanggal: 2026-11-26

If you were to believe Claude, based on simulations, fine tuning the duration matching algorithm is unlikely to make a major difference and can easily be swamped by changes in the yield curve after you set up or rebalance the ladder, especially since the wait for the next 10 year TIPS is about one year. Since this aligns with my priors, I believe it, but am open to reasons for other views.
(underline mine)

This is why I've emphasized that periodic rebalancing is required for effective duration matching. If the yield curve changes enough to warrant it, you can rebalance more often than annually. Very easy to see if this is warranted by importing your TIPS ladder to TIPS Ladder Manager, then clicking Rebalance Ladder. Even better if you've created a DARA plan, and import that before rebalancing.

To test it out, use the ladder manager app to build a ladder, but set the gap year DARA values to something different than the overall DARA. Export the holdings and dara plan, import both into the Rebalance tab, change the gap year DARA values back to the standard DARA value, then rebalance ladder. It will show you how many of the excess bracket TIPS (Jul 2035 and Feb 2040) to buy or sell to bring duration matching back to target. Other trades also will be indicated because the different quantities of the bracket year TIPS will generate different amounts of interest that contribute to the earlier funded years.
