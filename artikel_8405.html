SHT Day 13: Wanna Split It?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

I was worried how my body would perform today, considering the beating I felt I gave it yesterday. Before I tuck in for the night, I always stretch really well and roll out the sore spots with my little cork ball. Most nights I take some ibuprofen or acetaminophen too, just to help with inflammation.

I’m glad I decided to camp at Middle Gooseberry. The next one was half a mile off trail!

My body felt good when I woke up, and for the most part it held up all day today. Sometimes the knee feels tweaky, and this trip has my left Achilles a little sore, but not bad. A few exercises and stretches have kept that at bay so far. Regarding the knee, I think 90% of it is mental. I have a baseline worry that IT band syndrome will creep back in. If I just distract myself with the surroundings and/or music, walk like a normal human being, then my knee is absolutely fine.

A girl stopped by my campsite this morning before I left. I barely heard her, she was almost totally silent. The little noise I did hear I thought was maybe a squirrel or something. I told her that as she gathered and filtered some water. We laughed about it and I said maybe I’d see her down the trail as I packed up and took off.

The trail this morning first splits a pair of trees.

A few miles later I stopped at a nice shelter called Skunk Creek Shelter, which I think was within Gooseberry Falls State Park. I was having second breakfast and stretching, then sure enough I saw her approach. We chatted for a while, and she told me she was from Alaska. She was in the area for a wedding soon and was trying to make big miles on a time crunch, like 20 per day. She was very nice, but it’s likely I won’t be seeing her again, lol.

A little while later I stopped for L&L (lunch and latrine) at Blueberry Hill Campsite. The guidebook described this site as having unreliable water, but there was a bit of water in a nearly dry creek nearby thanks to the recent rains. I made sure to filter some here, having learned my lesson from yesterday.

This afternoon I passed through Split Rock State Park. The trail takes you way up along the Split Rock River for quite a ways before crossing it to come all the way back down the other side. I didn’t mind this seemingly extra mileage because it was very pretty. The bridge was out where the SHT crosses, but at this time it was easily rock hoppable.

Again, there were four campsites clustered along the river, and the last one would have been such a cool place to stay, but I was only at 10 miles for the day so I kept going. The campsite I’m planning to stay at tonight is dry though, so I made sure to fill up with water again before leaving the river’s easy access.

Walking next to the Split Rock River and all its glory.

Former bridge site. I was able to rock hop across the water just a few feet downstream from here.

I stopped mid-afternoon for a snack at another day use shelter. This one was pretty dilapidated, but it had a great view. In fact, I’ve had great views for most of the day. In addition, the weather today has been warm and nice, and the trail has been forgiving.

A little after 5 pm I rolled into camp at Chapins Ridge Campsite. As the name suggests, it rests up on a pretty little ridge away from any creeks or rivers, but it was surrounded by big, beautiful pines and soft pine needles completely covered the ground. This will do nicely. There was nobody else here, but this site was giving off good vibes.

Tonight’s dinner was a homemade Pad Thai with a Thai flavored tuna packet. It was ok. I definitely prefer just a plain chicken packet in that meal. I was able to connect with my husband for a bit and confirm our meetup spot for tomorrow. He’s all packed and ready to drive up, and I’m excited to see him!
