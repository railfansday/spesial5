Lawyer, business partner accused of defrauding cannabis investor of $750K

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

A Los Angeles attorney and his business partner are accused of paying for their personal expenses with investor money instead of funding business-to-consumer cannabis trade shows. (Image from Shutterstock)

A Los Angeles attorney and his business partner are accused of paying for their personal expenses with investor money instead of funding business-to-consumer cannabis trade shows, including the unsuccessful Green Street Festival in 2022.

A Los Angeles County court complaint filed by investor Jamie Chisick alleged that attorney Joshua Shelton of the Winston Wolfe Law Firm and his business partner, Rama Mayo, took $750,000 for a 15% ownership stake in the Green Street Symposium.

According to the Oct. 19 complaint, Shelton and Mayo, cannabis entrepreneurs, promised to organize large trade events that would attract thousands of attendees and generate “millions of dollars in revenue,” according to coverage on Law360.

Chisick obtained documents that he said shows that his money had been used for “personal payments, including but not limited to private jets, health insurance, bar dues (Shelton) and general overhead unrelated to GSS operations.”

The 2022 Green Street Festival brought in about $190,000 with about $178,000 refunded. A 2023 festival with projections of attracting over 10,000 attendees and up to $4.7 million in revenue drew a smaller crowd and did not produce a profit.

Chisick seeks to recover his investment and punitive damages, according to the complaint.
