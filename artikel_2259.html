Birmingham City team news and predicted XI to face Swansea City: August Priske remains a doubt

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Birmingham City welcome Swansea City to St Andrew’s this weekend looking to strengthen their bid for automatic promotion. The Blues currently sit 3rd in the Championship table with 26 points from 14 games, level with Stoke City and just two points behind leaders Middlesbrough.

Chris Davies’ side head into the contest in excellent form after a convincing 3-0 win over Millwall last time out. Alex Cochrane, Max Bird and Jay Stansfield all got on the scoresheet in the first half, while summer arrival Liam Millar has also made an impact with four goals to his name.

Swansea City are also firmly in the promotion hunt, occupying 5th place with 26 points from their opening 14 fixtures. Vitor Matos has enjoyed an encouraging start to his tenure in South Wales, with the Swans demonstrating plenty of quality throughout the campaign.

However, the visitors will be keen to bounce back after suffering a damaging 3-1 defeat away at Portsmouth. Swansea looked to have rescued a point after fighting back from a goal down, but two goals in stoppage time, arriving in the 92nd and 98th minutes, condemned them to defeat.

August Priske is a major doubt for Birmingham City’s Championship clash with Swansea City this weekend, with the striker set to be assessed on Friday before a decision is made on his availability, as reported by Birmingham Live.

The Dane missed Tuesday night’s 3-0 victory over Millwall after picking up an injury during the previous weekend’s meeting with Lincoln City. Priske has scored seven goals this season and would be a significant absence for Davies’ side.

The Blues boss provided a positive update but admitted it remains too early to know whether his leading scorer will feature. He said: “He’s doing OK. He’s made progress. It was a heavy knock that he took and he’s made progress but it’s probably too early to say.”

Davies added that it would be a close call, with Birmingham hoping to get Priske involved in some capacity. Paik Seung-ho and Dael Fry could return to the squad, while goalkeeper James Beadle is expected to miss out again.

Allsop; Laird, Neumann, Klarer, Cochrane; Solis, Iwata; Roberts, Bird, Millar; Stansfield

After the performance against Millwall in midweek will likely see Davies keep the same team. The only change may depend on Priske’s fitness.

Should Priske be fit then Bird will likely drop out of the side with Stansfield sitting deeper. However, both Bird and Stansfield impressed in midweek so it would be harsh to drop them.

Ryan Allsop will keep his place between the sticks with Beadle remaining out. While Fry and Paik will start from the bench should they return to the squad.
