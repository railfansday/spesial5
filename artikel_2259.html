Position change may await £5.5m Middlesbrough signing to improve chances of game time: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Middlesbrough are flying high in the Championship this season. Boro currently sit top of the table and have bounced back well from their play-off final defeat last campaign.

Kim Hellberg’s side strengthened in key areas in the summer, and their squad has drastically improved despite losing key man Hayden Hackney. Their influx of talent has meant playing time has been hard for some, and none more so than Max Arfsten.

Middlesbrough signed the United States international from Columbus Crew for a reported fee of £5.5m. It has been a slow start to life at the Riverside for the 25-year-old, with just three starts this term. However, changing his position could help improve his minutes for Boro.

Arfsten is capable of playing anywhere on the left or right side of the pitch. Middlesbrough have seemingly brought the 25-year-old in to play at left-back, where he has featured 12 times this campaign.

However, the United States international was mainly used as a left-winger for former side Columbus Crew, and he excelled over three years there. Last term specifically, he created 25 chances across 17 games and had 44 successful dribbles.

Arfsten’s weaker traits come defensively, and being dribbled past 16 times is not a component of his game that Boro will want to inherit. His side also conceded 24 times whilst he was playing.

By utilising him further forward, Hellberg is more likely to witness his brilliance in the final third. His best display for the Teesside outfit this season came against QPR, where he featured as a left-wing back. He made three successful dribbles and created two chances.

Middlesbrough have to show some patience with the 25-year-old as he adapts to the Championship. Allowing him that freedom in attack will be crucial, and his 17 successful dribbles so far this term prove that.

Arfsten’s lack of playing time hasn’t been completely down to his performances. Middlesbrough have witnessed consistently great displays this season from Tommy Conway on the left wing and Neto Borges at left-back.

Even on the right side, Callum Brittain is irreplaceable on the right side of defence, and Amario Cozier-Duberry is proving to be one of the best wingers in the Championship. With his preference to play on the left, however, he has to compete with Borges and Conway.

The Boro attacker has netted four times this campaign. With one assist too and a successful cross rate of 35.7%, Arfsten has a tough task to get ahead of the Scotsman.
