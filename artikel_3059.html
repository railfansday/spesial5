On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 4, 2026, Colliers International (CIGI -1.95%) announced low-teens growth expectations across key full-year metrics. Commercial real estate transaction slowdowns affect near-term performance. Expanding engineering and investment management services generate recurring revenue streams.

Three takeaways from the call explain the investment outlook.

Colliers relies on stable service streams to cushion transaction dips in commercial property markets. Repeat contracts in engineering and asset management create steady cash flows when capital market transactions slow down.

Management expanded its infrastructure capabilities through strategic purchases in engineering and energy services. These deals added $80 million in annualized earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, or EBITDA, a core profitability metric.

Leadership maintains direct financial exposure to long-term share performance through substantial personal equity holdings. More than 1,500 managers and senior executives hold a significant stake in the business.

Geopolitical tension and interest rate uncertainty are slowing commercial real estate deal closings, prompting a modest trim to full-year growth targets.

Colliers relies on a broad global footprint, diversified engineering capabilities, and deep customer relationships to weather near-term capital markets turbulence. Management focuses on capital deployment discipline and expanding recurring revenue streams over the long term.
