LifeVantage (LFVN) Q1 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Sales slid 22% as management overhauls strategy and shifts its fiscal calendar.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

LifeVantage Corporation (LFVN +1.34%) reported a double-digit revenue decline for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 as management initiated a comprehensive strategic transformation. The company is shifting toward a brand-driven, consumer-focused model intended to stabilize a base of active accounts that decreased by 19% year over year. Financial priorities include resetting the cost structure and reinvesting in digital capabilities to modernize the selling proposition. To align strategy with performance cycles, the company will transition its fiscal year to match the calendar year beginning Jan. 1, 2027. Management did not provide formal financial guidance for the upcoming period but emphasized a focus on operational efficiency and the stabilization of average order sizes.
