Pulmonx: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Gross margins above 75% and a narrowing path to cash flow breakeven give the turnaround real financial teeth.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 27, 2026, Pulmonx (LUNG +0.00%) returned to year-over-year top-line growth with total revenue of $22.8 million. Commercial realignments and expense controls are narrowing losses while expanding the reach of its medical devices for severe lung disease.

Three management statements highlight the drivers behind this financial turn.

Pulmonx reduced its third-quarter adjusted EBITDA loss, or earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, by nearly 60% year over year. Lower operating expenses provided immediate financial leverage as revenue expanded.

Management prioritized high-yielding sales accounts and filled substantially all open sales seats in the U.S. This tighter operational focus helped drive a 7% year-over-year revenue increase in domestic markets.

The company is enrolling patients in a trial for AeriSeal, a specialized foam designed to seal lung air leaks. Expanding into untreated patient segments broadens the total addressable market, or TAM, which measures the full revenue opportunity for its treatments.

Top-line growth is returning across both domestic and international markets as sales team productivity improves. High gross margins above 75% and spending discipline provide financial flexibility, while management watches sales force tenure and clinical trial milestones.

Re-establishing commercial momentum in global regions and commercializing new therapeutic tools position the business to generate operating leverage without requiring heavy capital additions.
