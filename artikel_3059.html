Every Gigawatt of GE Vernova's HA Turbines Is Expected to Generate $500 Million in Services. Here's Why That Outlasts the Boom.

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Record gas turbine orders are rapidly expanding GE Vernova's installed base, locking in decades of high-margin, recurring service revenue.

Data centers and utilities are scrambling for power, and GE Vernova (GEV +2.14%) has seen incredibly strong demand for its high-efficiency, air-cooled, heavy-duty (HA) gas turbines. The company is experiencing unprecedented growth as technology companies, data center developers, and utilities seek to secure power amid rising electricity demand.

GE Vernova's large-scale HA gas turbines provide multiple gigawatts of behind-the-meter power, a capability that has become increasingly important as utilities and hyperscalers seek to increase capacity without driving up electricity prices.

The company is seeing strong product sales and slot reservation agreements extending to 2032, highlighting the robust demand for gas turbines. But that's not all: For every gigawatt of HA turbines, GE Vernova expects to generate roughly $500 million in high-margin service revenue. Here's why that's the real story for GE Vernova that outlasts this historic equipment boom.

During its third-quarter earnings call, GE Vernova CEO Scott Strazik said we are in the early stages of an "electricity investment supercycle," driven by robust demand from data centers and the electrification of the power grid, with utilities scrambling to add power capacity. All these forces have been a powerful tailwind for GE Vernova's stock, which is up 515% since its 2024 spinoff from General Electric (now GE Aerospace).

In the third quarter, GE Vernova's remaining performance obligations (RPO) reached nearly $197 billion, a 45% increase from one year ago. This backlog is driven by strong growth in equipment sales, which grew 92% from last year to $104 billion, while its services backlog increased 14% to almost $93 billion.

Equipment sales include everything from heavy-duty HA gas turbines to aeroderivative turbines, transformers, switchgear, and wind turbines. Over the next five years, GE Vernova expects to recognize 97% of its equipment RPO.

That said, GE Vernova has a massive installed base that powers up to 25% of the world's electricity, providing a major source of services revenue for routine maintenance, upgrades, and plant life extensions. For every gigawatt of HA gas turbines installed, Strazik expects GE Vernova to earn $500 million in service revenue.

While equipment provides a boost to earnings over the next few years, the services revenue attached to that equipment will continue for decades. Over the next five years, GE Vernova expects to collect 54% of its services revenue, and 91% within 15 years.

GE Vernova has seen significant volatility as AI stocks face a big test, with investors questioning the sustainability of the AI build-out amid recent election results and pushback from residents around data center developments. That said, Strazik noted that roughly 20% of GE Vernova's power sales are from data centers, with the remainder coming from conventional customers like utilities.

GE Vernova continues to see a historic boom in demand for its power equipment and has slot reservation agreements in place over the next five years, which should boost services revenue over the next couple of decades. This makes GE Vernova a solid long-term stock to invest in the ongoing power boom.
