The Ultimate Growth Stock to Buy With $1,000 Before the End of November

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Amazon stock hasn't been this cheap in years.

By Dave Kovaleski – Nov 21, 2026 at 7:00PM EST

One of the best growth stocks over the past 30 years has been Amazon (AMZN -4.10%). Since it went public in 1997 as a place to buy books online, it has grown into the world's fifth-largest company, with a market cap of $2.8 trillion.

Amazon has gotten there by averaging a 31% annualized return over the past 29 years -- performance that few growth stocks can match over such a long period.

Over the course of its run, there have been few better times to buy Amazon stock than now, given its low valuation. Amazon stock is trading at just 21 times earnings and 23 times forward earnings. It has a five-year P/E-to-growth (PEG) ratio of 0.56, which indicates it is a long-term value.

Its price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio is currently hovering around the lowest it has been in at least 10 years; probably longer. When you combine that with its growth and backlog, it is an obvious buy right now.

Amazon stock is up 11% year-to-date and 10% over the past 12 months. It tanked in the first few months of the year on concerns about slowing cloud growth and massive artificial intelligence (AI) spending. But since then, cloud growth has accelerated, and investors are recognizing that AI spending has been necessary to add the capacity needed to grow cloud businesses.

This was evident in Amazon's performance in the third quarter. Net sales rose 12% to $202 billion while operating income surged 61% year over year to $28.1 billion. That jump in operating income was driven by its higher-margin Amazon Web Services (AWS), its cloud business, which saw operating income increase 56% to $17.8 billion. Net sales with AWS jumped 39% to $46 billion, its fastest growth rate in nearly five years.

It also reported a backlog of $578 billion, up triple digits year over year. The recent tech stock slide has kept Amazon at a historically low valuation, making it a great time to capitalize.
