From CFO to Interim CEO: Kress Takes Charge at Nvidia

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Longtime Nvidia CFO Colette Kress is stepping into the spotlight as interim CEO, taking the helm after Jensen Huang’s resignation and one of the biggest leadership crises in the company’s history. Bloomberg’s Nina Trentmann looks at Kress’s 13-year tenure at Nvidia, how her role evolved alongside the company’s transformation into an AI powerhouse, and the new challenge of managing operations, investors and Washington as she takes on a far more public-facing role. She joins Ed Ludlow on "Bloomberg Tech." (Source: Bloomberg)
