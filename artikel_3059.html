Black Friday deals are coming in fast, and that's great news if you're on the hunt for one of Apple's tablets. Right now you can save up to $100 on the best iPads.

One of the best deals right now is the brand-new iPad mini 8 for $100 off. The 11-inch iPad Air M4 is also $100 off, as is the 13-inch iPad Pro. Here are the best iPad Black Friday deals I've vetted. And don't forget to check out the best Apple Black Friday deals to save on MacBooks, AirPods, and more!

The iPad (11th Gen) doesn't shake up the formula, but it remains the Apple tablet for most people. In our iPad 11 review, we said it has significantly improved performance thanks to its A16 chip, longer battery life, and a larger 128GB starting storage option; Apple's entry-level tablet is among its best.

The last-gen iPad mini 7 is a fantastic compact tablet that's now on sale for Black Friday. As I said in my iPad mini 7 review, it delivers excellent performance and battery life while retaining its predecessor's vibrant display and portable design. Though it keeps the portrait-oriented front camera and is incompatible with Apple Pencil 2, this tablet’s strengths outweigh its flaws.

The iPad mini 8 is the most significant upgrade to Apple’s smallest tablet. As I said in my iPad mini 8 review, its thinner, lighter design makes it more portable than ever, while the new 8.5-inch OLED panel delivers stunning image quality for videos and books. Powered by the A20 Pro chip, it also delivers fast performance and more than all-day battery life. Though pricier and missing features like Face ID and a 120Hz refresh rate, it still delivers a first-rate iPad experience.

The iPad Air M4 retains everything we loved about the M3 iPad Air, but now it has a faster processor and Wi-Fi 7 — all for $100 off. While not perfect, as we said in our iPad Air M4 review, that doesn't prevent it from being the best tablet for most people.

This is the same iPad Air M4, only in a larger 13-inch size. This configuration packs an M4 chip and 128GB. This is worth getting if you want a 13-inch iPad that doesn't cost as much as an iPad Pro.

The iPad Pro M5 retains the same elegant, lightweight design and beautiful OLED panel as its predecessor. Like I said in my iPad Pro M5 review, even though it’s not a substantial upgrade, the M5’s fast performance and the productivity-focused iPadOS 27 make this the most powerful and useful iPad Pro yet.

If you want a larger screen, you can get the 13-inch iPad Pro model. This tablet is my daily driver thanks to its gorgeous tandem OLED panel that makes everything I watch look its best. And when paired with the Apple Magic Keyboard, it's also a decent laptop replacement.
