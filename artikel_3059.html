Is Alphabet Stock Cheap Right Now?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The stock looks too good to be true at current valuations.

It hasn't been the best year for Alphabet (GOOG -0.96%)(GOOGL -0.98%), down nearly 6% as of Nov. 13. This underperformance isn't ideal, but one silver lining is that the stock is trading almost as cheaply as it ever has, with a price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio of 15.8.

That makes Alphabet the cheapest of the "Magnificent Seven" stocks, and it's well below the 28.7 P/E ratio it has averaged over the past decade.

A company's P/E ratio tells you how much you're paying for every $1 of its earnings. The higher the P/E ratio, the more expensive a stock is considered, though different industries have different standards for what is "expensive" or "cheap."

For a blue chip company that has routinely delivered high growth, a 15.8 P/E ratio is very cheap. Alphabet has a virtual monopoly in internet search, Google Cloud just increased its revenue by 103% to $30.7 billion in the third quarter, and its balance sheet is rock solid. And despite concerns about the potential impact of AI on its search franchise, Alphabet has aggressively pursued its own  AI capabilities to answer fears.

If you're a long-term investor, Alphabet is a no-brainer buy right now. It might not turn around immediately, but I'm confident it will get out of this slump and return to market-beating growth.
