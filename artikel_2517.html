Uncertainty grows over injured Stuttgart star Stiller with no return in sight

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Things appear to be going from bad to worse for Angelo Stiller and VfB Stuttgart as he commences his return from injury.

Stiller, who has been out for two weeks with a muscle injury in his adductor area, is encountering difficulties with getting fit.

The midfielder's rehab is progressing slowly, and it could be even longer before he returns to the pitch which BILD is reporting.

Additionally, Stiller and his agency Volker Struth [Sports 360] have also parted ways. He has yet to find new representation.

Therefore, a return for the Bundesliga match against Schalke on Matchday 10 is naturally considered unlikely for Stiller.
