PSEG Declares Regular Quarterly Dividend for the Fourth Quarter of 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

NEWARK, N.J., Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The Board of Directors of Public Service Enterprise Group (NYSE: PEG) today declared a $0.67 per share dividend on the outstanding common stock of the company for the fourth quarter of 2026.

All dividends for the fourth quarter are payable on or before December 31, 2026, to shareholders of record on December 10, 2026.

About PSEGPublic Service Enterprise Group (PSEG) (NYSE: PEG) is a predominantly regulated infrastructure company operating New Jersey's largest transmission and distribution utility, serving approximately 2.4 million electric and 1.9 million natural gas customers. PSEG also owns an independent fleet of 3,758 MW of carbon-free, baseload nuclear power generating units in NJ and PA. PSEG is a member of the S&P 500 Index and has been named to the Dow Jones Best in Class North America Index for 18 consecutive years. PSEG's businesses include Public Service Electric and Gas Co. (PSE&G), PSEG Power and PSEG Long Island (https://corporate.pseg.com).
