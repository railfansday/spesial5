MGMLS is merging into FMLS, what it means for central Georgia agents

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Mid Georgia Multiple Listing Service will merge into First Multiple Listing Service, extending FMLS compulsory coverage into 12 central Georgia counties. The groups said the deal builds on a 6-year data-sharing partnership, with timing and terms not disclosed.  AI Summary

Mid Georgia Multiple Listing Service (MGMLS) has agreed to merge into First Multiple Listing Service (FMLS), expanding FMLS’s footprint into 12 additional compulsory counties in central Georgia and giving MGMLS members access to a larger technology, data and education platform, the organizations announced Thursday. Terms and an effective closing date were not disclosed.

“Our members are at the heart of every decision we make, and this opportunity is ultimately about creating even greater value for them,” said Laura Kostovetsky, president of the MGMLS board of directors. “We have built a strong organization and a strong community of real estate professionals and joining forces with FMLS gives us an opportunity to build on that success.”

MGMLS CEO Suzanne Beers said the combination expands services while keeping local expertise.

“This combination will give our members access to greater resources and capabilities while preserving the local knowledge and relationships they respect,” she said in a statement.

FMLS President and CEO Jeremy Crawford, a HousingWire Insider, added that the deal is designed to strengthen the regional “real estate ecosystem” and continue a broker-governed model focused on data integrity and cooperation.

For existing FMLS participants, the merger will extend the MLS’s compulsory coverage area deeper into middle Georgia, according to member communications. FMLS’s required-coverage footprint will grow to include Baldwin, Bibb, Butts, Crawford, Houston, Jasper, Jones, Monroe, Peach, Putnam, Twiggs and Wilkinson counties.

Those counties border areas where many FMLS brokers and agents already show and sell property under current data-sharing arrangements. With MGMLS folded in, FMLS will be the primary MLS serving that market, backed by local support.
