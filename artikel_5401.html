Stock futures tumble as Nvidia leads an AI sell-off and inflation data shows prices picking up

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Nasdaq-100 futures are down about 3% and Dow futures 635 points after PCE inflation accelerated to 3.8% in October

Photo Illustration by Matteo Della Torre/NurPhoto via Getty Images

Nvidia $NVDA stock fell 6% in premarket trading on Wednesday, leading AI-related stocks lower as U.S. stock futures dropped after data showed inflation accelerating in October.

Nasdaq $NDAQ-100 futures dropped about 3%, S&P 500 futures fell 2%, and Dow Jones Industrial Average futures lost 635 points, or 1.3%. Anthropic stock fell 4%, Oracle $ORCL stock declined about 6% and Alphabet $GOOGL stock shed 4%. The State Street $STT Technology Select Sector ETF (XLK) was down by over 3%.

The personal consumption expenditures price index accelerated to an annual rate of 3.8% in October after rising 0.3% for the month. Core PCE, which excludes food and energy, rose 3.3% from a year earlier, above a Dow Jones forecast, according to CNBC.

Federal Reserve rate increases are expected in December and next year, according to The Wall Street Journal. Analysts cited by The Wall Street Journal said expectations of rate increases, along with unease over borrowing to fund AI data center buildouts, are dragging down expensive technology shares.

The Trump administration revealed it intends to levy fresh duties on over a dozen trading partners, China and the European Union among them.

Across Asia on Wednesday, South Korea's Kospi index plunged by more than 6%, and the Nikkei 225 in Japan gave up 2.9%. SoftBank stock slid 15% in Japan. The pan-European Stoxx 600 index traded about 0.3% higher.

The decline came after Nvidia's Jensen Huang returned to the company on Monday, according to The Wall Street Journal. Nasdaq-100 futures are now at levels beneath those seen ahead of his comeback.

On Tuesday, the S&P 500 fell 0.5% and the Nasdaq Composite slid 1.5%. The Dow added over 400 points, a gain of 0.9%. Nvidia stock sank during that session, and Anthropic stock slipped under the price at which it went public.

Liz Ann Sonders, chief investment strategist at Schwab Center for Financial Research, said the AI sell-off began before the Nvidia leadership news. "Let's remember that the sell-off in the AI ecosystem predated the news about Huang and Nvidia," Sonders told CNBC's "Closing Bell" on Tuesday.
