Wick building up to big Sussex Senior Cup clash with Worthing

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The match was originally scheduled to be played at the McDonalds Community Stadium @ Crabtree Park a fortnight ago but was postponed when heavy downpours made the pitch unplayable and the match was postponed.

The Dragons biggest home crowd in recent seasons was against Worthing United in the Southern Combination League Division One play-off semi-finals two seasons ago in front of 329 fans.

Lee Baldwin’s team sent the majority of supporters home happy when they won a penalty shootout before doing beating Seaford in the final after another spot-kick showdown.

Worthing are currently seventh in the National League table and in the thick of the race for promotion to League Two.

Wick prepare for the big match when they entertain Newhaven at Bognor’s Nyewood Lane ground on Saturday (3pm).

The Dragons then head along the coast next Saturday when they play sixth-placed Bexhill at the Polegrove Recreation Ground.
