‘Extraordinary’ Malen the best striker in Serie A, says Roma boss Gasperini

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Gian Piero Gasperini believes there is no better striker in Serie A than Roma's Donyell Malen after he scored four goals in Saturday's 5-1 thrashing of Parma.

Roma ensured they remained within touching distance at the summit with a dominant display at the Stadio Ennio Tardini, with Rodrigo Mora also on target for the visitors.

Malen opened the scoring in the 39th minute after superbly controlling Emanuele Lulli's cross into the area before picking out the bottom-left corner with an emphatic finish.

But Parma hit back through Nesta Elphege within nine minutes of the restart, though three goals in the space of nine minutes later in the second half did the damage for Roma.

A 68th-minute strike from Malen was followed up by Mora's close-range finish, but there was still time for Roma's in-form striker to score and bring up his hat-trick, with the Netherlands international tucking home Wesley's inviting centre.

Malen added further gloss to the scoreline in the 90th minute, as Roma became the first team since the 1950-51 season to score more than 35 goals after 12 Serie A matches (36). In that campaign, Inter (38), Juventus (37) and AC Milan (38) all achieved the feat.

And 12 of those have come from Malen this season. He is the second player to score at least four goals in a Serie A match for Roma in the last 40 years, after Vincenzo Montella on 10 March 2002 against Lazio.

Malen is also only the third player to score three or more goals in three different Serie A matches for Roma in the three-points-per-win era (since 1994-95), joining Abel Balbo (five) and Montella (five), leading Gasperini to lavish praise on his forward.

"Malen was already a strong striker in the Netherlands, Borussia Dortmund and Aston Villa. You don't play at that level without quality," Gasperini said.

"Perhaps I played him where he expresses himself in the best way. There are a lot of good strikers in Serie A, including young talents.

"And, of course, Lautaro Martinez, but right now Malen is extraordinary.

"He combines so well with our team, we perhaps get more out of Malen than we would do with a striker who is stronger on paper, but doesn’t fit as well with our style of football."

Malen (26 in 2026) is only the second player in Roma history to score more than 25 goals in a single Serie A calendar year, after Pedro Manfredini (26 in 1960).

But, more importantly for Roma, they have now collected 30 points after 12 Serie A matches for the fourth time, following 2003-04 (30), 2013-14 (32) and 2017-18 (30).

Despite their thumping win, Roma remain two points behind Lazio after they beat Lecce 3-0 earlier on Saturday, with Tijjani Noslin starring with a brace at the Stadio Olimpico.

Lazio have won four consecutive home matches in Serie A for the first time since the period between September and November 2024 (five in that case under Marco Baroni).

However, Gasperini insisted his team must improve moving forward if they are to maintain their challenge with Gennaro Gattuso's side at the top of the table.

"We can always improve, both individually and as a team, but undoubtedly this side is growing," Gasperini said.

"We conceded a bad goal at a tough moment of the match, but I saw a strong reaction from the team, and that shows the confidence is there.

"When we are faced with a negative or unlucky moment, we have the confidence to keep playing and get over it."

Como also got themselves a win to move, albeit temporarily, into the top four. They beat Cagliari 2-0 thanks to strikes from Jacobo Ramon and Lucas da Cunha in either half.
