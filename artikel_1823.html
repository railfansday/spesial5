Mbappe should not be expected to defend, claims Real Madrid team-mate Rudiger

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Antonio Rudiger is baffled by criticism over Kylian Mbappe's defensive work rate.

Indeed, Rudiger feels it would be unfair to ask Mbappe, one of world football's great goalscorers, to "do my job".

Mbappe has already scored 17 goals and laid on three assists for Real Madrid this season. Of players in Europe's top five leagues, only Barcelona's Raphinha (19) has netted more goals this term.

Since the start of 2026, Mbappe's tally of 30 goals is bettered by only Raphinha (33) and Ballon d'Or winner Harry Kane (41).

— Kylian Mbappé (@KMbappe) October 27, 2026

Last month, Mbappe surpassed 500 goal involvements in his club career, yet the Frenchman has still faced some criticism over a seeming lack of willingness to do the dirty work, something which has perplexed his Madrid team-mate Rudiger.

"Does it make sense for me to ask a man who has scored more than 80 or 90 goals in two years, and has now reached 100 goals for the club, to do my job? That makes no sense," Rudiger told The Athletic.

Rudiger then compared Mbappe to some star forwards of Madrid's recent past.

He added: "Let's go back to the past. What was it like in the era of Gareth Bale, [Karim] Benzema and Cristiano Ronaldo?

"Look at their success. We don't always need to talk about their defensive abilities."

Madrid sit second in LaLiga, three points behind leaders Barca. Jose Mourinho's side face Celta Vigo on Saturday.
