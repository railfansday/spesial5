Tottenham will hope to make it four straight wins when they face Leeds in the Premier League this afternoon.

Spurs have turned a corner since the last international break, earning a point away at Manchester United with 10-men before following that up with victories over Coventry, Chelsea and Crystal Palace.

Attentions now shift towards a difficult encounter at Elland Road where the incentive for Roberto De Zerbi’s side is a win would guarantee a top-10 finish heading into the November international break.

While Tottenham are one of the form teams in the Premier League, the same cannot be said for Leeds as they are without a win since beating Newcastle back in September and have failed to score in their last two outings.

Although, Daniel Farke’s side are unbeaten at home in the league since March, the omens do not look good for Leeds ahead of welcoming Spurs to West Yorkshire for today’s lunchtime kick-off.

Tottenham have not lost to Leeds in the last six meetings, leaving Elland Road with maximum points on their last three visits - including the corresponding fixture just over a year ago when Mathys Tel and Mohammed Kudus were on target in a 2-1 win.

Here’s everything you need to know about where to watch the match...

TV channel: You can watch this game in the UK on TNT Sports 1, with coverage set to start at 11am GMT ahead of the 12.30pm GMT kick-off.
