Exclusive: Tottenham face triple transfer decision after surprise recall

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Tottenham goalkeeper Luca Gunter will assess his loan options in January after being recalled from Boreham Wood last month.

Gunter joined the runaway National League leaders over the summer and immediately secured his spot as their first-choice option, starting each of their first 11 league games and making two appearances in the National League Cup.

Boreham Wood remain unbeaten in the National League this season, winning 11 of their 13 matches, but Gunter has returned to Spurs to train with the first team following an injury to third-choice goalkeeper Brandon Austin.

Standard Sport understands that Gunter will continue to be around first-team training before looking to go back out on loan when the January transfer window opens.

Gunter is one of several academy talents at Tottenham who are assessing their options ahead of the January window, with Tyrese Hall expected to have both loan and permanent offers on the table.

Under-21s captain James Rowswell is another player who will be potentially looking to leave Spurs on loan in the New Year, having failed to secure a summer switch.

First-team head coach Roberto De Zerbi has been more open to using the club’s academy in recent weeks as results have begun to pick up, and he handed 18-year-old Luca Williams-Barnett his Premier League debut against Crystal Palace last weekend.

Several academy players, including Tye Hall and Callum Olusesi, also travelled with the first team for their warm-weather training camp in Marbella last month.
