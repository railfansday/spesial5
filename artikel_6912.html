Get Ready for the Holiday Season With These Timeless, Customizable Cards

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Minted makes it easy to send personalized cheer to everyone you love.

Last year I spent a long weekend with a friend I hadn’t seen in a decade. When I got home, I sent her the usual ebullient text about how wonderful it was to see her again at last and how much the time meant to me. Oddly, she didn’t reply; I thought she’d ghosted me.

About two weeks later, I dragged myself begrudgingly to my mailbox to collect the usual bunch of mailers, bills, credit card offers, and insurance solicitations. I’d planned to stop by the recycle bucket and toss the whole stack. Then I saw something amid the circulars: a thick, silvery envelope, addressed with lavishly scrawled panache, stamped with sealing wax and postmarked with a limited edition portrait postage stamp featuring a favorite poet. The return address showed it came from my friend.

I was over the moon. Amid all the usual generic garbage, here was a gem—something written just for me. I rushed into the hall, found an actual letter opener in a drawer, and mindfully—lovingly—slit the envelope open to find an artist-designed card by Minted. Inside was a loving note from my friend, far more profound and personal than my erasable text to her. This card was something almost sacred that I could touch, feel, save, and read over and over again.

In a digital age of AI generated missives, disappearing chats, and emails nobody takes the time to read more than the first two sentences of, a letter or note in the form of a greeting card is an anomaly. Even holiday cards have, in many cases, gone digital, crammed between promotional emails and work obligations in our inboxes, blasé versions of their former snazzy, personalized selves. But aren’t we all craving more? Something we can treasure? Don’t we hanker to send something to friends and loved ones as special as the festive season itself?

Enter Minted, a clever stationery and design brand with a mission to use the designs of independent artists and elevated printing techniques—such as letterpress and foil—to bring back the art of card sending and letter writing. Their high end products provide first class alternatives that infuse any dispatch with gravitas, making communicating a mindful act. Bring on the sealing wax and calligraphy pens!

These stunning cards make a statement anytime of the year—but especially over the holidays. Their soul-felt artistry embodies the Japanese concept of geidō: the way of the arts, where every action is meant to be a ceremonial—artistic—celebration of life. Minted’s 2026 array of holiday options embrace heritage motifs, vintage details, vibrant colours, and candid photography. Minted Concierge offers complimentary one-on-one guidance to help you find holiday cards that fit your style and message, then personalize every detail—from the layout and photo to envelopes, liners, and stamps.

For a more keepsake-worthy finish, Photo Mount™ pairs a semi-gloss photo print, mounted by hand on thick premium cardstock, with a choice of foil, letterpress, or standard printing; Minted’s letterpress option gives soft cotton paper a deep, tactile impression, with ink hand-mixed in small batches and each piece reviewed for color and texture. New this year, both Peel & Seal and Mail It For Me make sending holiday cards easier. For larger mailings, Peel & Seal envelopes make sending simpler: a concealed adhesive strip seals with a press, no moisture needed, and select envelope colors are available. On select eligible card formats, Mail It For Me can handle printing, addressing, assembly, postage, and mailing through USPS for an additional fee. Minted confirms when the cards are handed off and will ship you any extra unaddressed cards and envelopes for last-minute list adds.

Greeting cards weren’t always computer generated, overly edited, and filtered to death; they’ve evolved over two centuries. While the first holiday greeting card was introduced in the UK in 1843 as a festive, shorthand option for Christmas letter writers who lacked the time to pen several missives at once, they were popularized during the Victorian era. For decades afterwards, decorative cards trended, each an elaborate work of art. As printing and personal photography became more sophisticated and widespread, so did the cards. Sending and receiving holiday cards was a de rigueur tradition, their appearance evoking the styles of each decade. But in digital times, greeting cards began to lose favor, often replaced (poorly!) by a quick email or text. The pandemic brought a resurgence—an indication that people sought to connect in old school ways—and new generations have taken a renewed interest in sending unique holiday cards.

Designed by independent artists, more relevant than ever, Minted’s custom cards bring the age-old tradition into the future. Combining modern tools and the human touch, Minted ensures that sending holiday cards personalizes the moment, with artistry and emotion that make opening every card feel like unwrapping a meaningful gift. Offering cards with contemporary flourishes as well as those that are imbued with vintage vibes, Minted’s options allow the senders to express themselves and set a mood.

Consider Starry Wonder by Alston Foster, one of 2026’s most celestial designs. It boasts a retro-inspired border made of glittery stars, designed to frame a favorite photo.

Whimsical Cut Paper Party by Inkblot Design pairs fluid, colorful shapes with a look that feels both sophisticated and playful. Fully customizable, the artist-designed card is available in myriad shapes and formats.

Photomatic by Ani Somi delves into the photo booth realm, presenting a perfect place to display your silliness and party spirit. The foil-pressed accents gleam, catching the light like tinsel on a Christmas tree.

Choose Modjeska by chocomocacino to connect with the past, perhaps your own childhood memories of the season. From the atelier of an independent artist, this design comes in a range of shapes and formats, each crafted to help tell your story.

Customize your cards with distinctive fonts and greetings, plus colorful backers or envelope liners, to make them uniquely yours. With dots that call to mind holiday lights aglow, Delicate Dots by Ekko Studio pairs shimmering colors with foil-pressed accents for a celebratory, bespoke card.

In the same way you might enjoy meticulously wrapping a gift, baking a holiday dish, or trimming a tree, taking the extra time to send a meaningful greeting to families and friends may be as much a gift to yourself as it is to those who receive your brilliant Minted-made card.
