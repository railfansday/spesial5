Margot Robbie’s Side-Parted ‘Buttercup Bob’ Is Polished and Easy to Wear: How to Try It Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The actor and producer proves how a simple styling switch can make all the difference.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

There are few hair updates as simple, yet as impactful, as switching up your part. After several seasons dominated by center-parted lengths and deliberately undone styling, the side part is making a convincing return. The latest to make a case for its appeal? None other than Margot Robbie.

Attending Chanel’s Cruise 2026/2027 show in Australia, the actor and producer stepped out with a softly structured ‘buttercup bob,’ styled with a deep side part and flipped-out ends. The result was equal parts polished and quietly sophisticated. And while Robbie has recently been favoring the waterfall bob, this latest iteration feels notably sleeker, offering a more refined take on the trending cut.

“This bob is an effortless take on a classic jaw-length cut. It’s neat without feeling rigid, with soft ends and a clean shape that gives hair an expensive-looking finish,” Luke Hersheson, hairstylist and CEO of Hershesons previously told ELLE. In short, it trades lived-in texture for a more polished silhouette, with precise ends and subtle flicks upward that create a sharper finish.

Robbie’s latest style embodies the look perfectly, pairing a side part with softly sculpted ends for an updated take on the preppy bob. The part allows for a sweeping side bang, adding a touch of glamour to an otherwise classic cut.

When it comes to re-creating Robbie’s look, Talia Hurley, a stylist at Blue Tit London, recommends blow-drying the hair with a round brush for a glossy, classic finish. This will also help create added volume and the bend needed to achieve the look’s side-swept shape.

Luke Hersheson recommends finishing the look with a pea-sized amount of smoothing cream to add shine and tame flyaways without leaving the hair greasy, ensuring the style retains its polished, glossy finish.
