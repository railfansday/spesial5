The Best Dressed Celebrities at the CFDA Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

All decked out in their favorite American designers

At most awards shows that we cover—the Oscars, the Golden Globes, the Grammys—fashion is a secondary concern for the judges and nominees. At the CFDAs, however, the entire evening is dedicated to the American fashion industry, and, much to our annual delight, the attendees always dress with that in mind. Last night’s wardrobe selections were not dominated by European fashion houses (or celebrity contracts). Instead, guests seized the opportunity to celebrate American talent, much of which is based here in New York, with collections equally worthy of a spot on the red carpet.

Take, for instance, Mona Tougaard in a sweet, colorblocked look from Lii by Zane Li, who was nominated in the emerging category this year. Or actress Sydney Lemmon, who wore a long-sleeved, button-front black sheath worn just undone enough to reveal a pair of red, bedazzled panties underneath, created by Emily Dawn Long, another emerging nominee. And then there was returning nominee—and winner!—Colleen Allen, who outfitted guest Gracie Abrams in a strapless, twisted, black velvet gown.

Those who represented the larger American brands leaned into a more relaxed approach to dressing. Emily Ratajkowski wore a simple black sweater paired with a low-rise orange skirt and black boots from Tory Burch. Elle Fanning showed up in a dusty pink slip beneath a floor-length leather trench from Coach by Stuart Vevers. (Each was nominated for awards: womenswear and accessory designer, respectively.)

And then came Anthony Vaccarello, nominated for International Designer of the Year, swanning in from Europe with his high-glam, gold-foiled posse. Hailey Bieber, Zoë Kravitz, Rosé, and Anja Rubik each wore a look from his megawatt Spring 2027 collection, which was almost entirely blanketed in gold. But they still managed to retain some semblance of casual American ease with bomber jackets layered over knee-length skirts and bedazzled, midriff-baring two-piece sets.

All in all, it was a very well dressed celebration of the country’s fashion industry, so picking our favorites was difficult. But ahead, we’ve rounded up the best-dressed guests of the 2027 CFDA Awards.

In archival Bob Mackie, Levian earrings, and a Zydo Italy ring

Rosé, Anja Rubik, Anthony Vaccarello, Zoë Kravitz, and Hailey Bieber
