Putin Gains From Trump, Xi Summit Spotlight

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Bloomberg News White House Correspondent Jeff Mason tells Bloomberg This Weekend that Vladimir Putin emerged as a key beneficiary of his historic meeting with President Donald Trump and Xi Jinping, returning to the global stage alongside the US and Chinese leaders despite Russia’s continuing war in Ukraine. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Mason says the summit reflected Trump’s preference for leader-level diplomacy and explains why a Democratic-controlled Congress and Stephen Miller’s appointment as White House chief of staff are unlikely to fundamentally change the president’s foreign policy approach. (Source: Bloomberg)
