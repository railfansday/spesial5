Kane set to miss three months with ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Harry Kane was withdrawn after 20 minutes of Bayern's Bundesliga game against Cologne; the injury comes days after the England captain became his country's most-capped player; Kane won the Ballon d'Or earlier this month; Michael Olise also taken off injured in 1-1 draw

Harry Kane is expected to be sidelined for three months after suffering an ankle injury during Bayern Munich's Bundesliga game with Cologne on Friday.

Sky Germany report Bayern sporting director Max Eberl confirmed shortly after the 1-1 draw that Kane had suffered a "syndesmosis ligament tear" - also known as a high ankle sprain.

The Ballon d'Or winner - who became England's most-capped player last week - was seeking a first goal in five matches for the Bundesliga leaders.

However, as he looked to latch on to Michael Olise's deflected low cross at the back post, Cologne full-back Joel Schmied got to the ball first and clattered into Kane's left leg in his follow through.

Kane immediately clutched his lower leg and writhed in pain for a few moments before gingerly getting back to his feet, but he was a virtual bystander for the next couple of minutes.

The 33-year-old gestured to the dugout, sunk to the turf and then received treatment on his ankle before making way for Tom Bischof, limping off the field alongside two physios.

Kane had a quick exchange with Vincent Kompany, who was back in the Bayern hot-seat for the first time in six matches after being diagnosed with meningeal irritation last month, before heading down the tunnel.

To make matters worse for Bayern, Olise was also taken off injured at half-time.

A three-month period on the sidelines would rule Kane out for 11 Bundesliga games, the DFB-Pokal round of 16 tie against Hertha Berlin, as well as all four of Bayern's remaining Champions League league phase matches.

Providing his recovery goes to plan, that would give the striker just over a month to prepare for England's Nations League quarter-final.

The Three Lions face Norway in Oslo in the first leg on March 27, before the return leg at Wembley on March 30.

Henry Winter speaking on Back Pages Tonight:

"I've covered Harry Kane's career for so many years and there was a period where he had almost a chronic problem with his ankles.

"It's three months and, most immediately, it's Bayern Munich's concern. But England have the home and away Nations League quarter-finals against Norway in late March.

"The timeline would suggest he'll be back in that time, but thoughts with Thomas Tuchel tonight because he'll be absolutely concerned about this.

"He's such an important player. England's No 9. There's a bit of a gap behind him if you're looking for No 9s. I know Jude Bellingham can play there and that's probably the long-term plan. But this is hugely alarming."
