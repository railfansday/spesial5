Littler opens bid for third Grand Slam crown with stunning Greaves win

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Luke Littler makes a statement with emphatic victory over Beau Greaves as European champion Ross Smith suffers a shock opening defeat; you can watch the Grand Slam of Darts in Wolverhampton live on Sky Sports up until the final on November 22

Luke Littler started his pursuit of a Grand Slam of Darts hat-trick with a commanding 5-2 victory against Beau Greaves in Wolverhampton on Saturday night.

In a Group A blockbuster, 'The Nuke' struck seven maximums and a 109.97 average to defeat the Women's Matchplay champion.

Littler has yet to taste defeat in 15 Grand Slam matches, as he bids to join Phil Taylor and Michael van Gerwen by becoming the third player to win three successive titles in the West Midlands.

Greaves had memorably beaten Littler in the semi-finals of last year's World Youth Championship, but the back-to-back world champion had revenge on his mind.

After looking invincible in the first half of 2026 as he won the first five majors, Littler had come up short at the World Series of Darts Finals, then slipped to a first-round exit at the Grand Prix before another last-eight defeat in the European Championship.

Littler, though, produced a vintage clinical display at WV Active Aldersley as he broke Greaves in the second leg before soon opening up a 3-0 lead in rapid time.

Greaves held to cut the deficit back to 4-2 before Littler swiftly wrapped things up by taking out tops with his first match dart.

Speaking to Sky Sports, Littler said: "I'm happy with the win. I needed to get off to a strong start because Beau started to find it at the end.

"I just had to finish how I started. I was very good at the scoring, but 29 per cent on the doubles needs to improve.

"Beau was fantastic, but I'm happy with myself and I'm still 100 per cent here in Wolverhampton."

The 19-year-old from Warrington is now aiming to find his Alexandra Palace form, adding: "I love it here. I remember my first year; last year was the same, and this year is the same.

"If I am performing like this, then I'm going to be happy.

"It's what I need. The last three or four majors haven't gone well, but I've been practising, and I'm glad I've shown it.

"It's perfect timing. I'm not going to Minehead for the Players Championship, this is the last major before the World Championship.

"After this, there are a few exhibitions, and hopefully it works out."

John Part, speaking on Sky Sports, said: "His 180s and scoring were absolutely phenomenal. Beau didn't come out of the gates at her best, but you wouldn't say it was one of her better performances. We know she can play better than that.

"We're not sure Luke has been fully tested yet, but let's see."

Nathan Aspinall averaged an incredible 111.68 to dispatch Maik Kuivenhoven 5-1 in a remarkable Grand Slam of Darts clash in which 'The Asp' missed a nine-dart attempt - going seven darts into the perfect leg.

Aspinall landed 149 and 120 checkouts on the way to a thumping win as he destroyed his Dutch opponent and sent out a warning to the rest of the field.

Sky Sports Darts' Mark Webster said: "Just an incredible performance wasn't it and he was just setting his stall out.

"Getting through the group is paramount but if you can do it performing well, you're going to take confidence from it.

"He's still got work to do; he needs to beat Karel Sedlacek to confirm his spot in the next round but that was a brilliant display.

"He looked focused, he looked composed, he even had a dart to win the opening leg as well he went seven into a perfect leg.

"Nathan Aspinall is on fire, and he was saying the right things before the game as well, he was confident that he was going to win this game and he did it in such style."

Swedish star Anton Ostlund marked his Wolverhampton debut with a last-leg victory over fifth seed Jonny Clayton.

Showman Tommy Morris stayed calm and composed as he put on a destructive display to smash Josh Rock 5-2.

The part-time gardener made the most of his TV debut with a brilliant walk-on and then pinned a 121 en route to victory in Wolverhampton.

He will take on Kevin Doets on Monday evening, while Rock will face the Dutchman on Sunday in a must-win game.

Morris, who became just the sixth non-Tour Card holder to win a ranking title at Players Championship 34 earlier this month, said: "I was very nervous, but I take everything in my stride. I'm enjoying my progress. Even if I lose, I'm losing graciously."

Webster said: "From the minute he [Tommy] walked on to the first three darts of the match he just enjoyed himself; it was a composed display.

"It was great to watch as well, the pace he played at, maybe Josh got caught up in that, but Tommy was the better player and deserved to win."
