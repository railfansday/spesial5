Patriots QB Maye feared to be out for season with ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The New England Patriots face the prospect of being without quarterback Drake Maye for the rest of the season after he suffered an ankle injury in Sunday's loss to the Detroit Lions in Munich, Germany; Maye was replaced by Tommy DeVito at quarterback

New England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye could miss the remainder of the 2026 NFL season after suffering a suspected ankle fracture in Sunday's loss to the Detroit Lions.

Maye was injured on the opening drive of the game in Munich when he was sacked by Lions pass rusher Aidan Hutchinson, who would himself then also suffer a game and potentially season-ending knee injury by landing awkwardly during his post-sack celebration.

Maye limped to the locker room and was replaced by Tommy DeVito for the remainder of the game as the Patriots lost 35-10 to drop to 5-5 on the year.

NFL Network's Ian Rapoport reported Monday that the Patriots fear Maye's injury could keep him out for the rest of the campaign, with ESPN's Adam Schefter adding that it is feared to be a fractured ankle.

Patriots head coach Mike Vrabel said on Sunday that the team would run more tests upon returning to the United States, with Maye staying in Germany overnight to determine whether he required surgery.

The Patriots offense collapsed in Maye's absence as back-up quarterback Tommy DeVito finished 12 of 24 for 81 yards, one touchdown and an interception.

It comes ahead of a difficult run that will see the Patriots follow a meeting with the winless Los Angeles Chargers by facing the Buffalo Bills, Minnesota Vikings and Kansas City Chiefs in a bid to keep their season alive.

Maye has struggled to hit the MVP-contending heights of last season so far this year, and has gone 181 of 276 passing for 2,330 yards and 18 touchdowns to 11 interceptions.

The Patriots were dealt another blow in the fourth quarter when star wide receiver AJ Brown exited with a hamstring injury having only just returned from a two-game absence with a knee issue.

"He wasn't able to finish. We'll see what the severity of that is once we get back and kind of go from there," Vrabel added.
