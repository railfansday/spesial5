Avengers: Endgame Encore is set to hit Disney Plus on November 13, and you'll be able to rewatch those wild post-credits scenes as many times you want.

The film was re-released in September in honor of the film's seven-year anniversary, but also to get Marvel fans hyped for Avengers: Doomsday. The Encore version of the film is more or less the same except for four bonus scenes added to the end credits. The scenes in question are: Loki goes back to the past to visit Steve Rogers and Peggy Carter; Doctor Doom has captured Hulk after the events of Spider-Man: Brand New Day; an incursion is coming to destroy the Fox X-Men universe; and Tony Stark appears to be making a Doctor Doom Mask (which is terribly confusing and also lends credence to the rumor that Downey isn't actually playing Doom after all).

These scenes are meant to bridge the gap between Endgame and Doomsday, and will therefore not appear in the latter. We can also assume that that absolutely wild surprise post-credits scene at the end of VisionQuest will also not be in Doomsday, and is another 'gap-bridging' moment.

You can also check out the Avengers: Endgame Encore landing page on Disney Plus should you feel like refreshing the page until the movie finally drops.

Avengers: Doomsday hits theaters in the UK December 16 and in the US on December 18. For more, check out our guides to the Marvel timeline and how to watch the Marvel movies in order.
