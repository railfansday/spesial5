Switch 2 Surpasses 26 Million Units Sold, Hardware Sales Down By 37.4%

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Nintendo has released its latest financial report, confirming that the Switch 2 has now sold over 26 million units since its launch last year.

Coming in at more accurate 26.35 million, unit sales for the first half of the current fiscal year totalled 6.49 million. This represents a year-on-year decrease of 37.4%, with the company shifting 10.36 million during the same period last year.

Don't fret, though! This is to be expected. Console sales for the Switch 2 were astronomical during its first year on sale, so a decrease during its second year isn't going to come as a surprise. Couple in the hardware price hikes earlier this year, and it's clear that momentum has taken an expected downward turn.

Still, 26 million though folks. 26. Million. It's creeping up on the N64's lifetime sales at 32.93 million.

Software performed much better. Nintendo shifted a total of 24.49 million Switch 2 games over the first half of the year, an uptick of 18.8% over the same period last year. Similarly, Switch 1 software sold 65.72 million, an increase of 6.8&%. Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream and Rhythm Heaven Groove helped Nintendo achieve this, selling 8.81 million and 2.89 million respectively.

Nintendo's forecast for the fiscal year ending March 2027 remains unchanged at this time, so it's currently expecting to sell a total of 16.5 million Switch 2 units during the period.
