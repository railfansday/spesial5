Video: We Join IGN's 'Review Crew' To Discuss Zelda: Ocarina Of Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

By now, you've hopefully caught up with our review of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. We gave the remake a score of 9/10 and reckon it's a bloomin' great game, indeed.

Chances are you're likely playing it right now, but it you have roughly 50 minutes spare, then consider watching IGN's latest 'Review Crew' video, featuring.... us! Well, one of us to be precise.

We chat with IGN's Daemon Hatfield and reviewer Logan Plant along with Mashable's Alex Perry to dive deeper into what we love about Ocarina of Time and what we think could have been better. Because yes, as incredible as it is, there are indeed a few things we think could make it even more incredible.

We also touch on whether brand-new Zelda fans can start with Ocarina of Time, how it compares to open-world titles like Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom, and whether Majora's Mask might receive the same treatment in the future.

Thanks to IGN for having us over for the chat!
