Marriott Promo Codes: 35% Off Hotels | Condé Nast Traveler

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

I’ve been a travel editor for almost a decade now, so to say I’ve experienced my fair share of hotels would be an understatement. I’ve stayed in five-star estates in South Africa, beach shacks in Tulum, stylish suites in Paris, and every accommodation in between. I also know the big chains like the back of my hand. And when I’m traveling for personal reasons, I’m always scouring Marriott's website to see what stays they have on offer for my destination of choice.

As the umbrella company behind popular hotel brands like Ritz-Carlton, Westin, Le Méridien, St. Regis, Edition, Sheraton, and so many more, Marriott’s portfolio has properties for every traveler—and every budget. And if you, like me, are always looking for the best deal when planning your next vacation, you’re in luck, because Marriott offers a number of promo codes and exclusive discounts, and I rounded up the best of the best below. (Looking for more savings? Check out our coupons for brands like Atlantis Resorts, Booking.com, Expedia, and more.)

Love a design hotel and looking to get out of town this fall? Boy, do I have good news for you. Marriott has a series of fall promotions that will save you 15% on advance bookings at several Marriott properties, plus 20% off longer stays at others. Check out the offer page to see what properties and dates are up for grabs before planning your next big trip. Looks like fall just got a lot more fun!

If you're looking to book multiple vacations at the same time (hello, proactive!), you'll be happy to hear there are great savings to be claimed through Marriott's bundle and save deal. That's right, book two or more stays at the same time, and you can take up to 35% off your final tab—just check the offer page to see which popular hotels and resorts are participating in the promotion. Right now, the list includes CN Traveler favorites like the St. Regis Aruba; the Ritz-Carlton, Grand Cayman; and La Concha Resort in Puerto Rica.

Eager to get out of town and stay out of town? This new promotion lets you save more on hotels the longer you stay. Book by March 28, 2027, for stays through March 31, 2027, using promo code S6058, and you'll get 10% off when you stay at a Marriott hotel or resort for three nights, 15% off when you stay between four and six nights, or 20% off when you stay for seven or more nights. Marriott Bonvoy members also get an additional 5% off their stay using the code, so now's the time to sign up and save.

Marriott is encouraging travel lovers to get out and go by offering savings on packages and properties across the US, Canada, Latin America, and the Caribbean with Marriott promo code ZJL, valid for stays from now through June 30, 2027. Take a peek at this page of the Marriott website to see what hotels and resorts are participating in the promotion—there are thousands, from Miami to Vancouver—then make sure to use the code when booking to get your special rate, which will vary by property and date. Do I see a vacation in your future?!

I’m a big fan of travel loyalty programs, and Marriott Bonvoy is one of the best out there. Right now, the brand is offering 2,000 bonus Marriott Bonvoy points when you stay at participating AC Hotels, Courtyard by Marriott, Fairfield by Marriott, or Four Points by Sheraton hotels (this page has the full list) with the promo code 53M. Be sure to sign up—or sign in—on the Marriott Bonvoy site before you book.

If the stress of planning a vacation is enough to stop you from actually going on vacation in the first place, you might want to consider booking a Vacations by Marriott Bonvoy package, where flights, rooms, dining, and even rental cars are taken care of ahead of time. Whether you’re looking for a romantic getaway or family adventure, you’re sure to find a great way to use up your PTO this fall. The best part? You can use all the bonus points you rack up on a future stay or experience—you know, for when you inevitably fall in love with your resort.
