The Envelope System Caps Spending – Use Cash Only for Your Problem Categories

Use the envelope system on the few categories where you keep overspending, such as groceries, dining out, or entertainment. Each payday, put the cash budgeted for each category in its own labeled envelope, and when an envelope runs empty, that category is done until the next payday. Keep rent, utilities, and savings moving electronically.

A card doesn’t stop you when the month’s grocery money runs out. The purchases keep going through, and the overspending shows up on the next statement.

An envelope of cash stops you. You can count what’s left before you reach the register, and you can’t spend money that isn’t there. Cash also can’t run up interest or an overdraft fee.

Say your family budgets $700 a month for groceries but has been spending $900. With two paydays a month, you’d put $350 in the grocery envelope each payday. Buy groceries only from the envelope, and the month’s total can’t pass $700, so the $200 overrun ends in the first month.

Look at last month’s bank and card statements, and give an envelope to each category that went over budget. Withdraw the cash in one trip on payday and split it among the envelopes before you spend any. With bills and savings on autopay, the only cash you carry is money you plan to spend. To plan the rest of your money down to the last dollar, use a zero-based budget.

Cash has costs. Lose it, and it’s gone, while federal law limits what you owe for charges you didn’t approve on a lost or stolen card. A card that pays 2% cash back would also have earned $14 a month on the family’s $700 of groceries. And cash can’t pay for most online orders.

For those purchases, and for anyone who’d rather not carry cash, Goodbudget tracks the same envelopes in an app, and its free plan includes 10 regular ones. It records purchases after you make them, though, so it can’t stop one. A prepaid card loaded with one category’s money can, and a debit card on its own checking account usually can too, with overdraft coverage turned off.

Start on your next payday with one envelope, for the category that went furthest over budget last month.
