Buy things used when they drop in price fast but last for years, like solid-wood furniture, tools, books, bikes, and kids’ clothes. A used one costs far less than new and does the same job. Before you pay, test it, look it over for damage, and search its model for safety recalls.

In September 2026, used Nintendo Switch OLED consoles sold on Swappa for $205 on average, 49% less than the $399.99 list price of a new one, whose 12-month Nintendo warranty covers only its first buyer.

Plenty of things built to last sell for much less once they’ve had an owner but keep working for years, which is also why a two- or three-year-old car is a better buy than a new one.

Buying used pays off most on things made of solid wood, steel, or cast iron, which barely wear, and on things kids outgrow before they wear out.
