Public Service Loan Forgiveness cancels your remaining federal Direct Loan balance, tax-free, after 120 qualifying payments made while you work full time for a government agency or 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Certify your job with the PSLF Help Tool every year and at every job change, so you catch a problem within a year, not after a decade.

Your count of qualifying payments updates only when you certify. Wait until payment 120, and you could learn a decade late that an employer never qualified.

Employer eligibility hasn’t changed. A 2025 rule that would have let the Education Department disqualify employers it judged to have a substantial illegal purpose never took effect. A federal court struck it down in June 2026, and the government dropped its appeal in October, California’s attorney general announced.

Certify your job for the months your payments were paused in deferment or forbearance, too. Once you have 120 months of approved employment, you can buy back paused months that didn’t count, except months on RAP, by paying what an income-driven plan would have charged.

Take a public school teacher with $60,000 in Direct Loans at 6% interest who pays about $250 a month on an income-driven plan. Over 10 years she pays about $30,000. Because $250 never covers the monthly interest, PSLF then forgives more than $50,000.

Open the PSLF Help Tool with your latest W-2 or your employer’s tax ID number, then send the form to your employer to sign electronically. Most people finish in under 30 minutes, so never pay a company to file for you.
