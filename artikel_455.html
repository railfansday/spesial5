Woodside Energy CEO on LNG Development in the US

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Woodside Energy CEO and Managing Director Liz Westcott discusses the company’s efforts to build awareness among U.S. investors. Westcott said the company brings 35 years of LNG operating experience and aims to create additional shareholder value as new projects come online. She speaks with Romaine Bostick and Vonnie Quinn on The Close. (Source: Bloomberg)
