“Without any pressure” – Frosinone midfielder Calò looking forward to Milan test

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Frosinone midfielder Giacomo Calò has fired a warning to AC Milan ahead of Sunday’s game, insisting they do not feel any pressure.

In many ways, Frosinone have been the surprise package of Serie A so far this season. Having been promoted from Serie B in 2025-26, they have racked up six wins in 11 games, scoring 24 goals in the process and allowing 15.

They are 13 points above the relegation zone, which is very impressive given that the mission before a ball was kicked will likely have been just to get above the dotted line by any means. Now, they face a trip to San Siro where they can really test how far they have come.

Milan may be four points ahead, but Frosinone will come to try and cause a bit of a stir. Giacomo Calò gave an interview to Il Messaggero (via MilanPress) in which he discussed the upcoming games for the Lazio-based side.

What are Frosinone’s aims for the season?

“At first it was to stay up. Then we’ll see during the year, because we’ve only played eleven games and there are still a lot to go. We know it’s a tough league, that we could have some very difficult moments, with the schedule that Milan, Inter, Roma, and Lazio now have in store for us.

“This start is helping us today and maybe it will be useful in a few months. We’ll take it game by game, without any pressure.”

What do you like about the head coach Alvini?

“His tremendous enthusiasm and how he prepares for the game. On the pitch, you always know what you have to do, where your team-mates are, where you need to go. We never arrive at a game with any doubts. In the game, we find everything he says during the week.”

Milan are expected to welcome back Strahinja Pavlovic for the game, while encouraging news also arrived regarding Mike Maignan’s condition on Tuesday too.
