Opinion: Liverpool will sign their own N'Golo Kante

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Liverpool's midfield has needed upgrading for a while now.

Ryan Gravenberch, Dominik Szoboszlai, Alexis Mac Allister and Trey Nyoni are the four options that Andoni Iraola has at his disposal right now.

Indeed, James McConnell has also made a couple of appearances but, he's more of a back-up option in case three of the other four central midfielders are injured.

Fans have known that ever since Fabinho left, they've needed to sign a new number six.

And, because that hasn't happened yet, their title rivals have managed to get even further away from them.

Many different rumours and links have emerged over the last couple of years but now, the Reds' very own version of N'Golo Kante could be on his way to Anfield.

Liverpool have tabled a €70m bid for Lamine Camara

Defensive-midfield is the main position that Liverpool need to upgrade right now.

Even though the Anfield outfit have also got to sign players in other positions, midfield should be their next area of focus.

During the summer, several different players emerged as potential options for Liverpool but, one player who kept popping up was Lamine Camara.

The Senegalese midfielder has quickly become one of AS Monaco's most important players and, he's only 22 years old.

Indeed, he's never played outside of Ligue 1 before but, if he hits the ground running like Mamadou Sangare did for Brentford, the Reds will be onto a winner.

That's why this latest update from journalist Ekrem Konur is rather exciting.

"Having tracked AS Monaco's 22-year-old Senegalese midfielder Lamine Camara for months, Liverpool have tabled a massive €70 million bid to land their top target," he wrote.

"Following the deadline-day collapse of his proposed summer move to Arsenal, Manchester United and Chelsea, the Reds have made strong progress in talks with the player's representatives and are close to reaching an agreement on personal terms."

Lamine Camara will become Liverpool's own N'Golo Kante

N'Golo Kante was probably one of the Premier League's best defensive midfielders over the last 10 or so years.

The Frenchman managed to win the Premier League title twice and he even added a World Cup to his trophy cabinet back in 2018.

Obviously, he's now playing for Fenerbahce in the Turkish Super Lig but, at 35 years old, it's clear that he's coming to the end of his career.

Camara, on the other hand, is only just getting started with his and, right now, he's one of the most talked-about players in the world.

In fact, his performances have been that impressive that some people haveeven compared his skillset to Kante's .

The €70 million fee now required to sign Camara is obviously a bit of a stumbling block but, right now, it feels like the 22-year-old is their best option.

Here's hoping that Liverpool can strike a deal for him in January, otherwise, they could end up falling even further behind their title rivals.
