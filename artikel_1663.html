Fabregas taking inspiration from Frosinone boss ahead of AEK clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Cesc Fabregas revealed he will approach Como's Champions League encounter with AEK Athens with the same approach as Massimiliano Alvini did with Frosinone on Sunday.

Como welcome AEK to the Stadio Giuseppe Sinigaglia on Tuesday, hoping to maintain their strong start to their debut season in Europe's premier competition.

The Serie A side have won seven of their last eight games across all competitions, including a 9-1 win over Lens last time out in the Champions League, a result that puts them fourth in the 36-team table.

Fabregas' side also claimed a third consecutive Serie A home victory while also keeping a clean sheet for the first time since between October and November 1984 after beating Cagliari, but the Como boss was instead impressed by another managerial display.

Upon their return to Serie A, Frosinone are seventh in the standings, but were thumped 5-1 by Ruben Amorim's AC Milan.

However, Fabregas applauded Frosinone boss Alvini for his approach at San Siro, adding his tactics would be used as a template for their upcoming Champions League clash.

"I'm taking the same approach as Alvini against Milan. I'm sticking to my ideas," Fabregas said via Gazzetta, as quoted by Football Italia.

"Of course, there are games we need to know how to manage in order to win, but we're not going to change all the good things we're doing.

"I'm proud of our last match against Cagliari, which featured so many new elements.

"I still want to hear people say Alvini is a great coach. That is identity and courage, even if he lost 5-1. When you lose, you're rubbish; when you win, you're a genius.

"You need to keep things in perspective. Football is certainly changing, partly because referees are blowing their whistles less.

"Fewer players are staying down because that tactic no longer works. It's a positive turning point for the Italian league, which is becoming one of the most entertaining in Europe."

Among all the Italian sides in the Champions League in 2026-27, Como have been the standout performers so far.

Como have scored 19 goals in their four Champions League matches. In European Cup history since 1955, only three teams have scored 20+ goals in their first five games – Spartak Trnava, Leeds United and Borussia Monchengladbach all scored 23, a record in five games.

They have also won both of their Champions League home games this season, while the last Italian side to win their first three home matches in the European Cup/Champions League was Sampdoria in 1991-92 (first four).

Manchester United, RB Leipzig and Lens have all been swept aside by Como, who are assigned a 71.2% win probability by the Opta supercomputer against AEK.

"Nothing changes for us. We're a small club that knows exactly who we are," Fabregas said of his team's approach to the match. "There's a fantastic atmosphere at Mozzate.

"We know our strengths and weaknesses. We can't control what people say; we have to be stronger and manage our emotions.

"The players are getting a lot more attention, and that helps them mature, too. If they want to reach the top, they have to know how to handle that too.

"We're still right at the beginning. We're focusing on the journey, not the destination. AEK are a great team. They caused problems for Real [Madrid] and Shakhtar [Donetsk].

"They're physically strong and have a great mentality. We'll need to be very clinical."
