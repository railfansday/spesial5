With full board support and political clearance, Huang’s decision carries unmatched weight. In this scenario, reinstating Jensen Huang becomes the unlikely spark that finally unlocks institutional-scale utility for XRP. Even against the recent soft price action, the setup contains genuine sparks of hope. XRP has already shown it can react violently to Nvidia-related news, briefly reclaiming the $1 level on the day Huang’s return was confirmed.

Exchange balances have been trending lower, RLUSD continues to grow, and the broader market is searching for a catalyst strong enough to reverse the multi-month grind lower. A direct, high-profile endorsement from the most important CEO in technology would provide exactly that catalyst, and then some.

On the higher timeframes, XRP has spent much of 2026 carving out a broad consolidation after failing to hold earlier cycle highs. The recent decline into the $0.86–$0.93 zone produced a deep but potentially constructive washout, with the weekly chart now sitting near multi-month support.

A decisive break and sustained hold above the $1.20–$1.40 resistance cluster would shift the structure from corrective to impulsive. From there, measured-move projections based on the depth of the 2026 base, combined with classic Fibonacci extensions of the prior major advance, open the door to dramatically higher territory.

Momentum indicators on the weekly and monthly charts still have substantial room to expand into the extreme overbought readings that historically accompany XRP’s strongest parabolic phases. Volume expansion on any catalyst-driven breakout would be the final confirmation that the multi-year range is resolving decisively to the upside.

The combination of a once-in-a-cycle leadership event at Nvidia and a sudden, massive real-world use case for the XRP Ledger creates the kind of demand shock the market has never fully priced. In that environment, previous cycle highs become little more than waypoints.
