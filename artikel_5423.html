McDonald's is rolling out a 'Home Alone' holiday meal starting Dec. 1

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The meal includes collectible socks, pizza-flavored fries and a new McFlurry, with a Los Angeles pop-up event set for Dec. 4-5

McDonald’s announced a “Home Alone”-themed meal on Tuesday that will go on sale at participating U.S. restaurants on Dec. 1, while supplies last.

Each meal comes with one of four limited-edition collectible socks, the company said. The designs draw on characters, scenes and quotes from the 20th Century Studios film, whose lead character is Kevin McCallister. Customers can choose a breakfast, lunch or dinner entree, ranging from a Sausage Egg and Cheese McGriddles to a Big Mac.

Lunch and dinner meals include two limited-time items, according to McDonald's $MCD. Medium Little Nero's Pizza McShaker Fries come with a seasoning flavored with cheese, garlic, tomato and Italian herbs, and customers coat the fries by shaking them in a bag. Lovely Cheese Pizza Dipping Sauce is served on the side with Chicken McNuggets or McCrispy Strips, or can be ordered a la carte.

The meal is packaged in a box styled after the McCallister family house, according to NBC’s “Today”. The sock designs include Kevin’s scream along with Grimace, Birdy and the Hamburglar.

Actor Macaulay Culkin, who played Kevin in the original film, reprises the role in the campaign. "What I love about this collaboration is that the Home Alone Meal captures something bigger than just one character, it's about the playful spirit that resonates with people across generations," Culkin said in a statement. In the ad campaign, Culkin is shown passing the hours by himself in an otherwise vacant McDonald's, according to "Today."

McDonald’s will host the Home Alone Challenge, a pop-up event in Los Angeles, on Dec. 4 and 5 from 5 p.m. to 10 p.m. PT (8 p.m. to 1 a.m. ET). Fans who work their way through several tasks set in themed rooms, among them a ball pit slide and a slingshot test, earn a free Home Alone Meal. Fans can register for the event through McDonald’s.

Through the McDonald's app, customers can knock $5 off the price of a McDelivery order worth $20. They can also spend MyMcDonald's Rewards points on a two-week free trial of the Disney $DIS+Hulu bundle. Customers can round their totals up as a donation for Ronald McDonald House families whose child is undergoing medical care.

McDonald's reported a 0.2% decline in U.S. comparable sales for the third quarter earlier this month. The company is testing two value menu formats at about 1,900 U.S. locations, and the format that wins over franchisees and shoppers will go national in early 2027.
