The Nvidia stock slump continues: More than $600 billion in market cap erased in days

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Nvidia stock is down 14.5% over the past six trading days, a stretch that includes CEO Jensen Huang's resignation

Nvidia $NVDA stock traded 2.5% lower in the afternoon on Thursday, stretching a six-day slide that has wiped $611 billion from its market value. The stock has shed 14.5% of its price across those six sessions.

Nvidia stock began the session on Thursday with a gain, buoyed by hopes tied to an Anthropic IPO, according to MarketWatch. A total of 23 companies globally carry valuations of at least $611 billion.

Nvidia announced on Nov. 12 that Jensen Huang had resigned as president, CEO and a board member. Colette Kress, the company's executive vice president and CFO, is serving as interim CEO while keeping her CFO role. Harvey C. Jones was named the board's first-ever chair. The board has started a search for a permanent CEO and will consider internal and external candidates.

Nvidia reported third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion on Tuesday, up 103% from a year earlier. Data center revenue was $107.6 billion, up 110%. The company expects fourth-quarter revenue of $130.0 billion, plus or minus 3%, and said it is not assuming any data center compute revenue from China in that outlook.

"Our momentum is real," Kress said in a statement.

Nvidia stock fell by as much as 14% on Nov. 12, the day the resignation was announced, wiping out up to $601 billion in market value. Huang, 63, had admitted to infidelity in a note to employees on Nov. 8, following reports of two extramarital affairs.

As of Nov. 12, the stock had lost about 35% since reaching a record in early October, a decline of $2 trillion in market value, according to Bloomberg. At least seven Wall Street firms had downgraded Nvidia over the prior week.
