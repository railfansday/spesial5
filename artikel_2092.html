Arsenal Confirm 19 Players on November International Duty

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Lucie Turner writes about Arsenal for Read Arsenal and Newcastle United for Read Newcastle. A long-time Arsenal supporter, she also follows rugby, snooker, darts and Formula 1. Her work covers match previews and reports, breaking news, player profiles and opinion pieces.

There is no breathing space for the Arsenal squad. 19 players are going to represent their countries in international fixtures in the final break of 2026. Many will face crucial moments in the coming days with Nations League promotions and relegations still at stake.

There are plenty of important fixtures in November. A number of Arsenal players will be looking to help their countries qualify for the Nations League quarter-finals. However, whilst others will be battling to avoid relegation. A number of Gunners will also be facing their club teammates in some interesting fixtures.

One of the biggest matches will be the clash between England and Spain on November 15th in Madrid.

Spain includes David Raya, Martin Zubimendi and Mikel Merino in their squad, meaning they could face five Arsenal teammates. Declan Rice, Eberechi Eze, Ben White, Myles Lewis-Skelly and Ezri Konsa are all in Thomas Tuchel’s England squad.

Spain lead England by three points in their Nations League group, but the Lionesses have a chance to keep their quarter-final hopes alive if they can defeat Croatia at Wembley on November 12.

Konsa will be absent during the first match as he undertakes personal training, but he could return in time for the encounter against Spain.

There is more Arsenal interest in Group A2. Jurrien Timber’s Netherlands facing Christos Tzolis’ Greece on November 13, followed by a clash with Kai Havertz’s Germany three days later.

Both the Netherlands and Greece are tied on eight points. Germany will be looking to move up in the standings against Serbia.

These games could affect how a number of Arsenal players come back into the fold with such a busy run of fixtures.

Viktor Gyokeres has the chance to help Sweden move up to League A.

The forward has scored four goals in four Nations League games. Sweden are two points clear at the top of their group. A win against Bosnia and Herzegovina on November 14 will guarantee them a promotion.

They then head to Poland for their final game. Gyokeres will be looking to continue his goal scoring and return to Arsenal with confidence.

However, Martin Odegaard will have a tough task ahead. Norway currently sit bottom of their group having only picked up three points from four games.

With three points behind second place Denmark, Norway will host Wales before heading to Denmark on November 17. Both games will be crucial for the Norwegians to avoid relegation.

Riccardo Calafiori will be on duty for Italy against France and Belgium. Piero Hincapie has two friendly matches against Ecuador, one against Costa Rica and one against Bolivia.

Bruno Guimaraes and Gabriel Magalhaes will be travelling to play against Japan and Singapore with Brazil. Kepa Arrizabalaga has received his first call-up to the Basque squad for their friendly against Venezuela.

James Scanlon could have a successful campaign with Gibraltar. Two wins against Andorra and Malta would see them top their group and progress to League C.

With so many Arsenal players playing in competitive matches, the international break could have an impact on a number of countries. Mikel Arteta will be hoping his players return in good condition before they resume their domestic campaign.
