The 8 players set to miss Birmingham City vs Bolton Wanderers ft £1.6m winger

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Birmingham City returned to winning ways in dominant fashion on the road at Burnley on Wednesday night, and now return home to defend their unbeaten record at St Andrew's against Bolton Wanderers.

After four straight draws at home against tough opposition, Chris Davies' side are starting to find their groove infront of their fans, winning all four games at St Andrew's in the mini-block of fixtures between international breaks by a 12-1 aggregate scoreline. That one goal conceded against Swansea City was a Christoph Klarer own goal, too.

However, Bolton travel to Birmingham knowing that their away form is miles better than their home results.

Steven Schumacher's side are the only ones to take points away from Southampton at St Mary's, and have beaten both Norwich City and Wolves away from home, while drawing 0-0 at QPR early in the campaign, too.

So, this is by no means an easy task for Birmingham, even if they're in much better form than their visitors. These eight players won't be able to feature for either side, as they both search for a big three points.

Birmingham and other Championship sides are hitting that point in the season where cumulative yellow cards are affecting team selection.

At Burnley on Wednesday night, Klarer was suspended, meaning Dael Fry was handed a rare start, and left-back Alex Cochrane picked up his fifth yellow card of the season during that 2-0 win at Turf Moor.

Kristoffer Lund is back in training, so we'll see if Chris Davies hands him a start, or if he'll hand Lee Buchanan his first league minutes since December 2024.

Ethan Laird was a notable omission from the squad on Wednesday, as he was starting to nail down that starting right-back spot. He'd started four straight — his only four starts of the league season so far.

After the game, Chris Davies said that it was just a knock which would keep him out for "one or two games max." With this being the second game he'd be absent for, he'll likely remain out.

Marc Leonard looked likely to be on his way out of Birmingham, given he was loaned to Hearts midway through last season. However, a ruptured Achilles weeks out from the end of the campaign saw him back at St Andrew's to recover.

The 24-year-old shouldn't be back until the New Year at the earliest. Then, it'll be intriguing to see if he's re-integrated into the Birmingham fold, or if he finds his fitness out on loan elsewhere.

Bolton are close to having a full bill of health, but will still be without a few players for their final game of November, including captain Eoin Toal, who has missed the last five now with an ankle injury.

Speaking before the defeat against West Brom on Wednesday night, Wanderers boss Steven Schumacher stated that he'll be back fully training at the end of next week.

Canadian winger Jayden Nelson is in the exact same boat as Toal, with Schumacher revealing that he, too, will rejoin the group next week, with the view to being in full training come the end of it.

Ethan Erhahon, like Toal, has missed the last five games after going down with a thigh injury.

The 25-year-old has struggled with niggling injuries in the past few years. He missed 15 league games in his final season at Lincoln City in 2024/25, and then 13 the following year in his first campaign at Bolton.

His return isn't too far away, with Schumacher revealing that he'll be back in training the week after next.

So far this year, he's played just 153 minutes of Championship action under ex-Plymouth Argyle boss Schumacher, whom he reunited with in the summer.
