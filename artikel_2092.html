Gianluigi Donnarumma has become a 'very expensive problem' at Man City - Stefan Borson

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Manchester City and Enzo Maresca cannot accept another sizeable mistake from goalkeeper Gianluigi Donnarumma.

That is according to former Man City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that the 27-year-old's form has become a "big problem" at the Etihad Stadium.

Donnarumma is the No.1 choice for the Citizens, with Geronimo Rulli and Marcus Bettinelli as back-up options in Maresca's squad.

Despite winning several major trophies and being a fully fledged international for Italy, his mistakes are causing concerns in between the sticks.

Some fans have even suggested that selling James Trafford to Leeds United in the summer was a huge mistake, and that he should be the first-choice goalkeeper right now.

Donnarumma's latest mistake came in Man City's five-goal thriller against RB Leipzig on Wednesday, 4 November, allowing Ridle Baku's shot to squeeze in at the near post.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson believes that Donnarumma's expensive wages will make him very difficult to offload.

"Clearly those sorts of mistakes are not sustainable. I don't think they're going to make a change this season," he said.

"But, going forward, they're not going to... Man City can't accept a mistake again of that size. He's obviously very, very mixed at the moment.

"He's made some good saves, but he's made some very bad errors. And there could have been at least one more in that game, one in the first half [versus RB Leipzig].

"So there's a big problem, but he's very expensive. He's therefore very expensive to get rid of. And it's hard to see who would pay.

"Now, he'd clearly go back to Italy, I'm sure, but it wouldn't be on his current salary; that's for sure. So, he's one to watch."

With Erling Haaland scoring a much-needed winner at the Red Bull Arena, Donnarumma's performance has been mostly overshadowed.

Had another of his mistakes found the back of the net for RB Leipzig, though, and he would be in an entirely different situation right now.

A first-half shot from Antonio Nusa stung the palms of Donnarumma, nearly looping over him and into his own net.

Nusa's shot, instead, looped over the crossbar, sparing the goalkeeper's blushes. But it can't be a moment that is simply brushed under the rug.
