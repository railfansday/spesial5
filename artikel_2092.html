Wayne Rooney drops fresh Plymouth Argyle transfer revelation

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Former Plymouth Argyle head coach Wayne Rooney has dropped a fresh revelation on the transfer strategy at Home Park during his short tenure in charge of the club.

The England and Manchester United legend was relieved of his duties by the Pilgrims after a dismal run of form in the Championship during their eventual 2024/25 relegation campaign.

Argyle are currently in their second season in League One under his former Old Trafford teammate, Tom Cleverley, and after narrowly missing out on a spot in last season's play-offs, the Devon outfit are fifth in the table having won seven of their first 14 matches.

The mood at Home Park has returned to one of positivity after previous frustrations relating to on-pitch matters, whilst it has often been debated just how much longer Simon Hallet will be the man calling the shots from the boardroom.

Rooney was one of three hires made in the space of 12 months just two years ago, with his spells sandwiching those of Ian Foster and Miron Muslic.

As such, turnover was particularly rife and led to a period of significant instability, which Rooney has discussed with Ashley Young and Mark Goldbridge on the latest 'Stick to United' YouTube episode.

Plymouth have been told to demand up to £2m for Bim Pepple, who had been on the radar of Sheffield United, Bolton and Portsmouth in the summer.

Rooney's managerial career has been well-documented, particularly after underwhelming spells at Birmingham City and Plymouth, with both clubs eventually being relegated in successive seasons after parting company with the Liverpudlian.

The 41-year-old only oversaw one transfer window at Home Park, during which 11 players were recruited.

He has previously discussed the club's data-driven transfer policy during said timeframe, and did so again when analysing recent flaws at Old Trafford, which has led to sack calls emerging over former Middlesbrough boss, Michael Carrick.

Asked whether they can see similar traits between the recruitment issues during Ole Gunnar Solksjaer's time at the Red Devils and now by Young, Rooney related by saying: "I had it at Plymouth.

"It's all ran on data. You're looking at players, but then you get a couple of players in who you don't really know and haven't seen much of them.

"All of a sudden, you sign them and then you've got to try and make it work, but it's not what you identified," he added.

Unsurprisingly, such discourse has already alerted some on the South Coast, with one Plymouth supporter stating: Well I'm not sure about this because the start was quite good, then we had a spiral, then we had injuries and Rooney couldn't dig us out of a hole."

Another commented: That’s the Hallet way, I’m not a huge fan of it. I still believe Tom as Head Coach needs to ensure they fit in with how he wants them to play. I hope with Derek (Adams) in place this has changed."

"He sounds a bit unconvinced by his own point," another Pilgrims fan claimed.

Some were also previously unaware of such developments occurring, with another comment reading: "Didn't realise we did it purely off data that's strange."

Rooney was sacked by Plymouth after winning just five of his 20 matches in charge, but almost two years later, even though the club is in a lower division, such times are firmly behind its supporters.

Instead, they have certainly been encouraged by the form showcased by Cleverley's side in recent weeks and months, particularly after the Argyle bosses' response to last season's alarming start.
