New Phantom Blade Zero PS5 Update Brings Features, Upgrades, and Fixes

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Phantom Blade Zero studio S-Game has announced the game's next PS5 patch will arrive this Saturday, 28th November 2026, weighing in at around 7GB.

It's already posted the patch notes ahead of pushing the PS5 update live at the weekend, and they reveal new features, technical enhancements, and bug fixes coming to the game.

The most substantial additions are two fresh features: a Combat Assist section in the settings and an Automatic Strong Camera Assist setting.

"With this option turned off, strong camera assistance applies only while you are attacking an enemy. At other times, the camera makes only minor adjustments."

On the technical front, the amount of video memory and system memory the title uses has been reduced, which should improve the performance of cutscenes and general gameplay in snow-covered areas. Performance in "certain areas and boss fights" has then been enhanced, and you should see less stuttering after applying the PS5 update.
