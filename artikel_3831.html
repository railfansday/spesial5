Danganronpa 2×2 details Akimbo Consensus system, refined returning systems

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Publisher Spike Chunsoft and developer Gemdrops have released new information, video, and screenshots for Danganronpa 2×2 introducing details about the Class Trials featured in Slayhem Mode, including the new Akimbo Consensus system.

■ A New Class Trial System: Akimbo Consensus

When a murder occurs among the students, an initial investigation takes place, followed by a Class Trial. This is where Slayhem mode introduces the new Akimbo Consensus system.

During a Class Trial, parties may voice various, often conflicting, testimonies, causing the discussion to become chaotic and difficult to follow. This is the point where Akimbo Consensus comes into play. You must evaluate the validity of each statement and use either an “Affirm” or “Refute” Truth Bullet to address it, thereby organizing the discussion and steering it in the right direction.

Once you have correctly organized the statements, three options will appear at the end, allowing you to decide what should be discussed next. If you make the appropriate choice, it is considered a “Consensus,” and the Akimbo Consensus is successful. The Class Trial will then proceed in the direction you have established.

Slayhem Mode Also Utilizes a Familiar System From a Previous Title

Slayhem mode also includes the Debate Scrum that was featured in Danganronpa V3: Killing Harmony. A Debate Scrum is a team-based trial battle that happens during Class Trials when the students split into two opposing groups with conflicting opinions.

■ Class Trials in Original Mode Have Also Been Refined

This occurs when other students challenge the deductions made by the protagonist, Hajime Hinata. You can slice through their statements using Truth Blades, an element that offers a unique gameplay experience.

In Logic Dive, you’ll navigate a tube-like course representing a path of synapses in the brain, dodging obstacles and answering questions along the way. Known for its high difficulty in previous game, this version includes features designed to make the experience more accessible to players.

Danganronpa 2×2 is due out for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, Switch, and PC via Steam and Epic Games Store on January 14, 2027.
