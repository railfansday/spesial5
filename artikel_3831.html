Mandalorian and Han Solo Vehicles in Star Wars: Galactic Racer Next Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Fuse Games has revealed a Speed Legends update to Star Wars: Galactic Racer dropping in December will include both free and premium content, the latter of which contains new vehicles from Star Wars films and TV shows.

Available to purchase, there will be the M-68 Landspeeder Icon Vehicle from Solo: A Star Wars Story, and three speeders inspired by The Mandalorian. You can see them in the graphic on the right side below:
