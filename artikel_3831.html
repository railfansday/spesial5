How to collect Nami’s Tangerines to help Sanji protect them in Fortnite Treasure Hunters

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Fortnite’s Treasure Hunters season has quite a few different One Piece-themed missions available for you to tackle. One of the first ones you’ll encounter tasks you with collecting Nami’s Tangerines to help Sanji protect them.

The loot pool is massive, so figuring out how to find any specific item around the island can be tricky. If this task is giving you trouble, here’s how to collect Nami’s Tangerines to help Sanji protect them in Fortnite.

Nami’s Tangerines are a random loot drop item you can find just about anywhere on the island while looting. There is no specific, guaranteed way to find them, which means you instead have to regularly loot and hope you eventually come across them.

You have a chance of finding Nami’s Tangerines when opening any loot container you come across while exploring. This includes:

You can also find Nami’s Tangerines sitting on the ground anywhere around the island. They’re fairly common, so once you’re actively looking for them, finding the total amount required for this mission should be fairly easy.

Each time you come across Nami’s Tangerines, you’ll generally find more than one. Typically, they come in bundles of around three. This helps you progress through the mission associated with them much faster, as you need to find quite a few of them to get it done.

To complete the collect Nami’s Tangerines to help Sanji protect them quest, you need to find a total of 16 Nami’s Tangerines around the island. You don’t actually have to eat them; instead, you simply need to pick them up and have them in your inventory briefly for them to count toward this task.

You can find all 16 Nami’s Tangerines in one match or instead slowly work your way through this task across a few different ones. All that matters for this mission is that you eventually do manage to collect a total of 16 at some point before the season ends.

There are plenty of other missions you can find and work through around the island after checking this one off.
