Only 1 NFL team makes sense for Aaron Rodgers, and even it comes with a caveat

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

There aren't too many teams that make sense right now for Aaron Rodgers.

For one, he went out guns blazing on his former head coach, which led to his release from the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Another reason: There aren't too many obvious football fits right now.

If all you do is comb through the list of potentially QB-needy teams right now, in terms of strictly needing help down the stretch, it's not that long. Especially when that quarterback is 43 years old next week, largely struggling on the field and willing to publicly question his head coach.

Perhaps Rodgers is done, and this is his way of announcing it. But maybe he's not done and harbors hope that he can finish out what's presumably his final NFL season on something of a high note.

Is there a team that would give him that opportunity? Here are some of the potential options, ranked in descending order — and those odds get pretty low the farther down the list we go:

Strictly from a football standpoint, there's some sense, especially if you're not the biggest Tommy DeVito enthusiast. With Drake Maye injured, and possibly for the duration of the season, the Patriots are nominally still in the race at 5-5. With at least four tough games remaining, the margin for error is slim.

Rodgers could be viewed as an upgrade over DeVito, and there'd be no reasonable undercutting of Maye, who is out indefinitely. This would be the kind of arrangement that lasts a month or two, no longer.

Rodgers considers Mike Vrabel a friend. It's fair to wonder if that friendship still exists after Rodgers has several times brought up how the Vrabel-Dianna Russini story has been covered in the media, calling it improper handling and pointing out the potential conflicts of interest involved in the story.

That might be enough for the Patriots to cross their fingers and hope for miracle work from Tommy Cutlets. Or perhaps Vrabel and Rodgers could see past that for the next several weeks. Hard to say.

For fans of revenge narratives, the Ravens belong on your list. Tableau: Rodgers signs with Baltimore, Jacoby Brissett gets hurt and it's Rodgers vs. Mason Rudolph in Baltimore, Week 18, with a playoff spot on the line. Can't script 'em much better than that.

Realistically? The Ravens probably won't sign Rodgers. It certainly would be fun to consider, and there could be a possibility if Brissett can't go and Lamar Jackson is unable to return from a thumb injury, but a few things likely would need to happen before this is even considered.

Heck, if Philip Rivers can come out of retirement and play for the Colts four years removed from the NFL, what makes Rodgers landing in Indianapolis so wild? Again, this would strictly be from a football standpoint, and it would take the Colts being completely done with Anthony Richardson Sr. to feel probable. Even Rodgers might pass on playing with the Colts considering where they're at. And they've already faced the Steelers, so the revenge possibility is a non-factor.

Similar situation to the Colts in that it could be a football need, but there's also the connection to Cards offensive coordinator Nathaniel Hackett, one of Rodgers' favorite assistant coaches over the years. If Carson Beck's back injury continues to flare up, perhaps Rodgers rates as an upgrade over Josh Johnson. Rodgers has no meaningful relationship with Cardinals head coach Mike LaFleur, but as everyone knows, Rodgers did play for LaFleur's brother, Matt, for many years in Green Bay.

This would be a contingency situation only, as Brock Purdy is playing at an MVP level and the Niners have one of the better backups in the league in Mac Jones. The connection here is thin, but Rodgers has called Kyle Shanahan a "phenomenal play-caller" and "fantastic coach," and Shanahan did reach out to Matt LaFleur to inquire about trading for Rodgers. This one feels thin at best, but not impossible.
