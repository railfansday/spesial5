The little things are adding up to something bigger for the Suns

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Games in November count just as much toward the standings as games in March, and what we saw from the Phoenix Suns in their overtime win over the New Orleans Pelicans is something we might look back on in March and appreciate, both for how it unfolded and the result it produced.

Going into any opposing team's arena isn't easy, and we know the Pelicans have the positional archetypes that can trouble Phoenix. They have size and length defensively, with the ability to jam up Devin Booker and effectively close out on shooters.

So what if they entered the game 5-13? All it takes is one of their players getting on a roll, feeding off the Smoothie King Center crowd, and suddenly the possibility of losing to them becomes very real.

Monday night's game was a physical one, defined by both teams exchanging runs. Ultimately, the team that made the final run was going to walk away victorious, and to the Suns' credit, it took a collective effort to ensure that was them. Five players scored in double figures, four of whom came from the starting five. Everybody contributed, and when you take care of the little things, the big things tend to take care of themselves.

That big thing was a fourth consecutive victory on this road trip for Phoenix, further reinforcing that the Suns are figuring out how to play team basketball this season. 16 games in, there's still plenty of room for growth. Playing perfect basketball is impossible, but that doesn't mean you shouldn't chase perfection. The one thing potentially keeping this team from going from a good basketball team, which is what they currently are, to a great one is their ability to rebound. From a connectivity and cohesion standpoint, you're beginning to see a consistent level of play from this group. It's another solid win.

What I appreciate most about this stretch of basketball is that the Suns are finding different ways to win. They're learning how to navigate adversity, respond to opposing runs, and trust one another when possessions become increasingly valuable. The rebounding deficiencies remain a legitimate concern, but the collective identity of this team is beginning to take shape. And banking these victories while they're still figuring things out? That's something we might be especially thankful for come March.
