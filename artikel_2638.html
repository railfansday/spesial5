Rob Gronkowski names the New England Patriots player he blames more than Mike Vrabel for team’s struggles

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

New England Patriots legend Rob Gronkowski has a bleak view of the team's chances this season after Drake Maye suffered an injury.

The New England Patriots' trip to Germany was a total disaster. They lost 35-10 in a blowout loss to the Detroit Lions, and lost star players Drake Maye and AJ Brown to injuries.

It means the Patriots' season is teetering at 5-5, with backup quarterback Tommy DeVito struggling. Former player Rob Gronkowski admitted he was so dismayed at the team's performance that he stopped watching.

Gronkowski told Up & Adams: "With Drake Maye going down, when Tommy DeVito went in, I texted my buddy and was like, 'Hey, I don't think they're going to get more than 75 yards of offense for [the] rest of the game.'

"I didn't even watch the fourth quarter. I think it all came in garbage time when they stacked up those yards, but it just looked brutal. It looked horrendous."

Rob Gronkowski says he does not believe the New England Patriots are equipped to muddle through their quarterback issues, because their defense is playing so badly.

Prior to the 35-10 loss to the Lions, the Patriots were demolished 45-27 at home to the Green Bay Packers.

Gronkowski pointed to star cornerback Christian Gonzalez's recent underperformance as the most troubling.

He said: "The whole secondary looked like they didn't want a piece of tackling at all in that case. Christian Gonzalez, over the last two games, I don't know what's going on in that situation right there specifically. I mean, the guy is an All-Pro corner. You saw what he did versus JSN in Week 1; I mean, he basically locked him down.

"The guy's an incredible corner. He just has the yips right now—the hiccups, I guess. I don't know, because he needs to bounce back as well.

"I mean, it starts with him on the defensive side of the ball. If he's not making the plays, if he's not covering the guys, I feel like it kind of lets everyone else loosen up as well."

New England Patriots head coach Mike Vrabel has come under criticism for the New England Patriots' poor start to the season.

They are 5-5, with AFC East leaders Buffalo Bills out of sight at 8-1. Gronkowski refuses to blame Vrabel for the problems.

He believes that right now the biggest problem is out of Vrabel's control, and points to the injuries the Chiefs suffered last season.

"I mean, it's so hard to pinpoint [Mike] Vrabel in this situation because you see how many players are injured.

"Like, it doesn't matter what the situation is with the coaching staff if you don't have all your starters there. Yeah, one or two starters go down, whatever, that's when coaching comes into play.

"But look at last year with the Kansas City Chiefs when Mahomes went down, for example, it just all goes to shambles then because a quarterback means that much to an organization as well. But I just can't pinpoint it on Vrabel at all."
