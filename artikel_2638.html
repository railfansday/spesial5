Wooden Shoes top Patriots, host Tigers romp in Paris NAL girls hoops tourney

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A second-quarter surge proved enough for Teutopolis to edge Terre Haute North 42-36 on Monday's opening night of the Paris North American Lighting Thanksgiving Tournament in the host Tigers' gym.

The home squad made its presence felt, too, as Paris rolled to a 93-14 victory over Danville in Monday's nightcap.

In the first game, Grayce Renn poured in a game-high 17 points for the Patriots. Madison Jack and Alexis Hornbrooke added six points apiece.

North played Teutopolis dead-even in the first (8-8), third (10-10) and fourth (9-9) quarters. But the Wooden Shoes outscored the Patriots 15-9 in the second period for a 23-17 halftime lead, and that made the difference. Maggie Dreas led Teutopolis with 16 points.

In Paris' big win, four Tigers scored in double figures. Pailynne Camp and Kailey Young led Paris with 18 points each, while Macy Bicknell added 14 and Paedyn Keys 11.

The Tigers broke the game wide open immediately, rolling up a 33-4 lead at the first quarter break.

North's loss gave the Patriots a 3-2 record going into Tuesday's 6:45 p.m. CT second-round tourney game against Charleston, following Danville (0-1) vs. Richland County at 5:15 p.m. Meanwhile, Teutopolis (1-0) plays Charleston at 6:45 p.m. Wednesday. Paris' win gave the Tigers a 1-0 mark going into Wednesday'sd 5:15 p.m. game vs. Richland County.
