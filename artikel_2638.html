Brian Schottenheimer names three underrated Dallas Cowboys players who excited him more than Dak Prescott

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Dak Prescott led the Dallas Cowboys to a big win over the Tennessee Titans.

With four passing touchdowns and one rushing touchdown, Dak Prescott was unstoppable as he led the Cowboys to a 51-30 victory.

After the win, Brian Schottenheimer was asked if he had ever seen Prescott play a better game.

Schottenheimer dodged the press conference question, noting that he has seen Prescott play like this several times, adding: "He was incredible."

The head coach moved the conversation on to pick out three underrated players who he feels deserve recognition.

Schottenheimer said: "You know what I got excited about today, is the performance of guys like James Mitchell, TJ Bass, Jashaun Corbin, guys like that that don't get the same recognition.

Bass has been with the Cowboys since 2023, while Mitchell and Corbin are in their first seasons with the team.

Running back Corbin was elevated from the practice squad, and his impact was felt most in pass-protection, where he was able to keep Prescott clear. The Titans did not manage to sack Prescott even once.

The Dallas Cowboys' win moved them to 5-6, with a home game against the Philadelphia Eagles on Thanksgiving.

For all the problems the Cowboys have had this season, with their struggling defense, they are still in touch in the race for the playoffs, and could even end up winning the NFC East.

Dak Prescott has 27 touchdowns in total for the season. He could eclipse his best ever record of 38, set in 2021 and 2023.
