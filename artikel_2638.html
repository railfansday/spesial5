Colts’ rookie pays Jonathan Taylor touching compliment in postgame interview

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Indianapolis Colts beat the Miami Dolphins, 24-22, in Week 10. The Colts won the game on the ground with Jonathan Taylor and Seth McGowan combining for 160 yards on 28 carries. In the process Taylor hit a major milestone as he now has over 10,000 scrimmage yards in his career.

The Colts decided to keep Taylor at the trade deadline while they dealt other players like Quenton Nelson, DeForest Buckner, Josh Downs and Keenan Allen. Taylor rewarded them with his solid play on Sunday. Then rookie linebacker CJ Allen explained why Taylor's impact goes well beyond what happens on the field.

Allen, who had nine tackles, was interviewed by CBS's Aditi Kinkhabwala alongside Taylor after the game and revealed just how important the running back is to younger players like him.

"Man, when I see JT play it just inspires me because of how hard he works," Allen told Kinkhabwala before turing to Taylor. "I never told you this, but I watch you, bro. In the locker room and stuff, bro. How you come in. You get your treatment, routine, every single day. I'm just proud of you. I'm glad to be here with you."

Taylor looked genuinely touched. After the trade deadline he said he is happy to go to work and "have my best day each and every single day for my coaches and my teammates." Clearly he's accomplished his goal and at least one of his young teammates is using him as the example he's trying to be.
