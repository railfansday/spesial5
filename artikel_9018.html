Leica Introduces Two New Camera Bags Made by Billingham

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Leica Introduces Two New Camera Bags Made by Billingham

Leica has partnered with British camera bag manufacturer Billingham to introduce two new camera bags designed specifically for its mirrorless cameras.

The new bags come in two sizes: a compact version designed for the Leica M and Q cameras, and a larger model intended for the Leica SL system, such as this year’s Leica SL3-P. Both feature interiors tailored to their respective camera systems, with space for a camera with an attached lens and an additional lens.

Each bag also includes dedicated pockets for SD and CFexpress cards, an internal mesh pocket for accessories, and customizable dividers to keep equipment organized and accessible. An exterior strap allows the bags to be attached to a luggage trolley, making them easier to transport when traveling.

Both models are manufactured in the U.K. using Billingham’s FibreNyte, a proprietary waterproof synthetic material, combined with leather detailing. The bags are available in three color combinations: black, sage/black, and khaki/chocolate.

The Billingham for Leica M/Q bag and Billingham for Leica SL bag will be available from today (November 26, 2026) at selected Leica stores worldwide and through the Leica online store.

The M/Q model has a recommended retail price of £300 ($395) / €350, while the larger SL version costs £380 ($500) / €450.
