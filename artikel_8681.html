Open Bright Start 529 if you pay Illinois income tax, since you can deduct contributions on your state return, or if choosing it costs you no home-state tax break. Its index portfolios charge 0.09% a year or less. If your state’s break requires its own plan, fund that plan first.

Bright Start is Illinois’s 529 plan for savers who invest without an advisor, open to residents of every state, and its age-based portfolios shift from stocks toward bonds and cash as a child nears college. At a 6% yearly return before fees, a family saving $200 a month for 18 years pays about $500 in fees in the index versions by our math, against about $2,400 at Morningstar’s 0.43% average for age-based portfolios in 2025.

Illinois families should start here, since they get a state deduction on top of index fees about a fifth of the 0.43% average, and so should savers who lose no home-state tax break by choosing it. Savers whose state gives its 529 deduction or credit only for its own plan should open that plan first, since that break is a sure saving each year they contribute.
