Bayern Munich cools the prospect of a new transfer at €100 million

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Could Bayern Munich make a new transfer worth 100 million euros to strengthen its squad before the 2028 Champions League final, which will be played at the Allianz Arena? Asked about this possibility after the historic recruitment of Harry Kane in 2023, Herbert Hainer preferred to calm expectations. The Bavarian president assures that the club does not intend to spend considerable sums with the sole objective of playing this final at home. “ Reaching the Champions League final in our stadium would be the icing on the cake. However, it is certain that we will not invest millions of euros just for that day, or for this season “, he explained. If the manager does not explicitly close the door to a new major operation on the transfer market, he insists on the need to maintain a coherent financial strategy, despite Bayern’s ambition to continue to compete with the best European teams.

Herbert Hainer believes in particular that Bayern Munich must compensate for its financial resources lower than those of certain competitors with a more intelligent recruitment policy. “ There are clubs in Europe who simply have more money at their disposal. This is why we must be smarter and better than others “, he continued. To illustrate this strategy, the Bavarian president cited the arrivals of Harry Kane, Michael Olise and Luis Díaz, three operations that were particularly complex to carry out, but which he considers today to be real successes. “ These three players were major challenges to recruit, but they turned out to be real success stories “, he stressed. Hainer also praised the work of CEO Jan-Christian Dreesen, as well as that of Max Eberl and Christoph Freund, who are responsible for the club’s sports policy. A way of reminding us that Bayern intends to continue its development in the long term, without disrupting its strategy for a single European event.
