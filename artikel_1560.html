Arsenal returns to attack for Vinicius Jr, the press goes crazy for Eli Junior Kroupi

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Vinícius Júnior has not yet found his best face, and his future is already starting to be talked about! As the Brazilian prepares to return to competition after four matches of suspension, his sporting situation continues to raise questions. In the columns of SPORTseveral observers analyze his loss of confidence and believe that media pressure as well as recent disillusionments have ended up curbing his natural audacity. And in fact, an English suitor keeps a very close eye on him. According to Josep Pedrerol In El ChiringuitoArsenal would be particularly interested in the Madrid number 7. Mikel Arteta would appreciate his profile, but the Gunners will first have to make room in their finances, with important sales like those of Martín Zubimendi or Mikel Merino considered. However, the Brazilian does not seem to be in a hurry to pack his bags. Under contract until 2032, he still wants to win in Madrid and no concrete offer has been sent by the London club. The soap opera is therefore launched, but Real has not yet said its last word!

This is one of the nice surprises of this gathering of the France team: Éli Junior Kroupi will discover the Blues! Called by Zinédine Zidane to replace Marcus Thuram, the young Bournemouth striker is rewarded for his spectacular progress in the Premier League, where he has already scored 13 goals. And the French press was quick to welcome this first summons. The Parisian title ” It’s Kroupi time “, while The Team believes that this selection was simply written. It must be said that the player trained in Lorient never ceases to impress. Despite physical problems which delayed his start to the season, he quickly established himself in the English championship and is already attracting the attention of big European teams, notably PSG and FC Barcelona. But Kroupi already had a very clear idea of ​​his international future. Last spring, he rejected the approaches of Roberto Martínez’s Portugal to favor the Blues. A decision rewarded today by this first call-up, which could well mark the start of a great story with the France team!

In England, it may be Michael Carrick’s last chance day! An emergency meeting around Sir Jim Ratcliffe should allow the management of Manchester United to decide on its future. With only one victory in ten games and a situation that has become particularly worrying in the standings, the Red Devils are going through one of the worst periods in their recent history. To replace their coach, several names are circulating, including Eddie Howe, Antonio Conte and especially Mauricio Pochettino. That’s good, the Argentinian now seems very close to Old Trafford! According to The AthleticManchester United and Pochettino are reportedly finalizing a deal. The Mancunian club would have to pay around 9 million euros to the American federation to release the United States coach, who would arrive with his own staff. THE Daily Mail the question already arises: “ Is Pochettino the man capable of turning around England’s most dysfunctional club? » The daily newspaper highlights its human management, its tactical demands and its ability to rebuild a collective. It remains to be seen whether the former Tottenham coach will be the one to bring the Red Devils back to the top. One thing is certain, Carrick seems more than ever on the start and the project promises to be colossal!
