The Washington Commanders won the Week 10 "Thursday Night Football" game against the New York Giants.

In a back and forth affair, it was the road team that left with a 24-21 victory in a game they had to win. Stefon Diggs scored the game-winning touchdown with under a minute to go and McCarthy tossed an interception to Rasul Douglas on the ensuing Giants' drive.

The Commanders' season would've been on life support with a loss, putting them four games back of the Giants without owning the tiebreaker.

Instead the Commanders now own the tiebreaker and are just two games behind the NFC East-leading Giants.

The NFC East is now wide open: The Giants came into the night with a chance to essentially eliminate the Commanders from postseason contention. New York would've owned the tiebreaker with a win and held a four-game lead over Washington. Instead, it's Washington that now has the tiebreaker. And Dan Quinn's team is only two games back. With the Cowboys still lurking, the Giants can no longer feel comfortable atop the NFC East. In some ways, this race is just getting started.

The Giants' special teams are far from special: It's easy to point to J.J. McCarthy's interception at the end or how the loss of Jaxson Dart is being felt. Special teams was the difference in this one though. Dominic Zvada, the rookie kicker, missed a 33-yard field goal and had a kickoff out of bounds. Jordan Stout's 12-yard punt gave the Commanders good field position on the go-ahead touchdown drive. It was Zvada that missed multiple kicks in Washington that helped lead to a tie. The Commanders now own the tiebreaker instead of splitting the series. That could loom large as the season heads down the stretch.

Jayden Daniels reminded everyone of who he is: He tossed two uncharacteristic interceptions, but Daniels appeared to be in control all night long. He was active with his legs and efficient through the air, while showing plenty of poise on the go-ahead touchdown drive. Daniels has been the subject of jokes all season long for his late-game failures. On this night, it was Daniels that got the lost laugh.
