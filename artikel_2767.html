Kirk Cousins looks sharp in 1st game after being traded by Las Vegas Raiders

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Week 10 saw the Las Vegas Raiders lose their sixth game in seven tries, falling 21-13 to the Seattle Seahawks. But let's check in on a few old friends and see how the weekend treated Kirk Cousins, Amik Robertson and Jakobi Meyers.

Stats: 21/31 (67.7%), 207 yards, 2 TDs, 1 INT, 3 rushes, 11 rushing yards

Cousins didn't get to practice with his new teammates until last Wednesday, but that was hard to tell on Sunday. He was very sharp and hit the ground running, allowing Minnesota to jump out to a 17-0 halftime lead in Green Bay. Justin Jefferson was certainly happy to see his old quarterback back, as Cousins targeted Jefferson 13 times, connecting for nine catches and 74 yards, the most catches and third-most yards the receiver has had in a single game this season. That helped the Vikings keep pace in the NFC North, as they're tied with the Detroit Lions and Chicago Bears for first place at 7-2.

This was a crazy game to start the weekend, as the Washington Commanders pulled off a comeback on Thursday Night Football to take down the division-leading New York Giants. Robertson was a big part of the effort, making a couple of notable plays against the run and coming up with a PBU. According to Pro Football Focus, he was targeted seven times and gave up four catches for 43 yards against the Giants. The 2020 fourth-round pick has quietly been having a good year with a career-high 75.7 PFF grade and an elite 90.1 mark against the run.

Meyers didn't have a big game on Sunday, but it's been a while since we've checked in on him. The veteran receiver is far from the No. 1 target in Jacksonville, Parker Washington is, but he plays the role of a reliable safety net well. Fairly consistently, Meyers sits around 30 to 60 receiving yards per game and has totaled 26 catches for 374 yards and two touchdowns through nine games.
