Cam Skattebo on Giants not adding a running back: They know what I can do

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Cam Skattebo is set to return to the Giants lineup for Thursday night’s game against the Commanders and he won’t be looking over his shoulder at any new additions to the team’s running back depth chart.

The Giants were reportedly bidders for Jonathan Taylor at one point and they were linked to other potential moves in the backfield, but they stood pat on deadline day. Skattebo said on Tuesday that he understands why some would be “skeptical” that the Giants have enough at running back while citing his average of around 60 rushing yards per game, but that he wasn’t bothered by any interest the Giants had in other backs because “they know what I can do”

“You’ve seen what I can do,” Skattebo said, via Darryl Slater of NJ.com. “When I come out and I’m ready to play football, I can be one of the most dangerous backs in the league. Simple as that.”

Skattebo was up to full practice participation Tuesday after missing Week 9 with a hamstring injury, so he should have every opportunity to prove himself right about what he’s capable of doing for the Giants offense.
