Thanksgiving NFL Slate Proves Streaming Is The New Standard

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The 2026 NFL Thanksgiving schedule proves the league is done with the old ways. Five games span three days, but the real story is where you watch them. Traditional TV networks still hold some slots, yet the biggest changes land on streaming platforms.

Wednesday night kicks things off with a massive shift in how fans consume football. The Packers face the Rams in a game that will stream exclusively on Netflix. This marks the first time the NFL has held a game on Thanksgiving Eve. Yahoo Sports NFL noted the match starts at 8:15 p.m. ET, with a pre-game show running two hours prior.

Netflix is hosting a game and bringing a rock concert to the field. Legendary Irish rockers U2 will perform a special halftime show during the Packers-Rams matchup. This move signals a clear direction for the league's future revenue and reach. The NFL is betting that fans will peel their potatoes and brine their turkey while tuning into a stream rather than a cable box.

Other games follow a similar pattern of digital access. Prime Video will broadcast the 3 p.m. ET Black Friday matchup featuring the Steelers against the Broncos. Even the traditional broadcast games offer robust digital options. The Eagles-Cowboys clash on Thursday airs on FOX, but also streams live in 4K on Tubi, the network's ad-supported service. Fox Sports confirmed that Spanish-language fans can catch the action on FOX Deportes.

The stakes are incredibly high for the Wednesday night game beyond just the streaming numbers. Micah Parsons has been activated by the Green Bay Packers for this particular contest versus the Rams. This event represents the first appearance by the star pass rusher since his ACL tear nearly one year ago. Following his trade from Dallas, Yahoo Sports NFL noted that Parsons recorded 12.5 sacks across 14 games in his Packers debut season.

Parsons is ready to help Green Bay secure a playoff spot. Now that he has been cleared to play, he told the media on Monday that he anticipates facing the Rams and helping Green Bay reach the playoffs. "I'm so locked in on what's ahead of me and I understand what this game Wednesday means to the organization and I myself and what it means for playoffs," Parsons said. "And if I can be out there, I want to be out there, and I'm excited for any time. Because we only got seven more games left, so I want to capitalize on all those if I can."

With both squads sitting at 6-4, this Wednesday night contest becomes crucial for securing an NFC wild card position. A victory over Green Bay might allow the Rams to briefly claim the 7-seed.
