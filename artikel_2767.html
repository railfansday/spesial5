Jaguars Reacts Survey: Who is Jacksonville’s Top Threat in the AFC?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Welcome to SB Nation Reacts, a survey of fans across the NFL. Throughout the year we ask questions of the most plugged-in Jaguars fans and fans across the country.

Jaguars fans — Which AFC team is the team's biggest threat this season?

Here's a look at the conference standings through eight weeks.

In his career, Trevor Lawrence has winning records against the Steelers (1-0) and Patriots (2-1), and he's split his matchups with the Bills (2-2). He has a 1-3 record against each of the Broncos, Bengals, and Chiefs.

New England doesn't seem like too much of a threat. They already got whooped by 29 points in Jacksonville earlier this season, and it'd be a surprise for them to win the AFC East title over Buffalo (thus gaining home-field advantage in the playoffs over the likely AFC South champion Jaguars).

Between those other five teams… which one is keeping you up at night?
