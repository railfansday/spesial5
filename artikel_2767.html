Los Angeles Chargers news: Bolts land OSU’s Smith in new as QBs take priority in top 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Chargers went and won their first game against the Ravens in Week 11 but followed that with yet another loss this past Sunday against the Jets. They're now 1-9 with seven games to go.

If the Chargers continue their losing ways, they will stay in the mix for one of the top players in the upcoming draft class. Obviously Ohio State's Jeremiah Smith is the crown jewel of the group, but will the Bolts truly have a shot at taking Smith if they don't pick number one overall?

Quarterbacks always end up being the biggest priority amongst the league's worst teams. If the Chargers end up picking at No. 3 — like they do in this new mock at The Athletic — then there's always going to be a chance that two passers go before them which sets the table for the Bolts picking the Heisman favorite.

In Nick Baumgardner's latest mock, he has the Titans trading up with the Jets to pick Oklahoma State quarterback Drew Mestemaker at No. 1. The Miami Dolphins then take Notre Dame's CJ Carr at No. 2. In this scenario, that's all it takes for the Bolts to land Smith, who currently has 86 receptions for 1,482 yards and 17 touchdowns through 11 games this year.

Here's what Baumgardner had to say about the pairing with the Chargers:

"Smith would be like a swim-up bar in the desert for Justin Herbert and the Chargers, who need playmakers more than anyone right now. Jim Harbaugh's team — specifically, his pass offense (ranked 29th in the NFL) — is floundering. Second-year receiver Tre' Harris leads the team with 28 catches; 48 other receivers in the league have more. The Bolts are out of power there."

"As college football's best player, Smith should win the Heisman Trophy. Though he has the goods to become the first receiver to go No. 1 since Keyshawn Johnson in 1996, a QB or two could bump him (slightly) down the board."
