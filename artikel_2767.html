Lakers desperately need Austin Reaves. When will LA star return?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Los Angeles Lakers put together a nine-game winning streak to start the month of November. It just recently came to an end with a 109-97 loss to the Utah Jazz at home on Nov. 18.

Shooting guard Austin Reaves contributed to the early success but landed awkwardly on his left heel after missing a transition dunk attempt in the Lakers' eventual 123-112 victory over the Milwaukee Bucks on Nov. 11. He's been out of the lineup ever since but has continued to work his way back.

Reaves' injury is among the most concerning for the Lakers. While he's been considered day-to-day by coach JJ Redick, Reaves has missed the last four games.

Redick told reporters on Nov. 20 that he's hoping to have Reaves back in the lineup sooner rather than later.

The earliest Reaves could return would be the Lakers' next game, at home against the Utah Jazz on Monday, Nov. 23.

"I think getting through this stretch for all of our guys is important," Redick said. "We've made a lot of progress. … We're trying to manage (Reaves') pain. So hopefully he'll be back in one of the next two games."

Reaves and the Lakers' medical staff had previously opted against having imaging done on the injury.

Quentin Grimes has taken on more responsibility when the Lakers have been without a star player in the lineup.

"I'm not set in stone on anything," Redick said about fitting Grimes in as a starter when Reaves returns. "The reality is that Quentin has been a starter, either A.R. or Luka has been off, so naturally he will assume some of that usage."

Grimes scored a game-high 47 points on 16-of-24 shooting from the field against the Charlotte Hornets on Nov. 18.

Reaves has started all 12 of the games he's played in for the Lakers this season. The guard is averaging 24.7 points, 3.3 rebounds, 4.7 assists and 1.2 steals per game, while shooting 51.3% from the field and 40.9% from beyond the arc.
