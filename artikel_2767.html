Several Colts players still out on Week 11 injury report

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A number of key Indianapolis Colts players were still non-participants on Tuesday's Week 11 injury report.

With it being a Thursday night game this week, the Colts will only hold walk-throughs.

Jerry Tillery (calf), Jalen Travis (knee), and Will Mallory (hamstring), all of whom did not play against Miami, were non-participants.

Also not practicing were Sauce Gardner (calf), Charvarius Ward (knee), and Bernhard Raimann (concussion). These injuries were sustained in Sunday's game.

Luke Rhodes (hamstring) was upgraded to a limited participant on Tuesday.

Antony Gould (toe) was limited, and Dalton Tucker (shoulder) was a full go.

Charlie Heck (personal) was added to the injury report as a non-participant.
