Knights lose game, Stone in 4-1 defeat to Sharks

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

There's a statistic that points out that 77 percent of NHL teams that are in the playoffs on Thanksgiving Day make the postseason come April.Try telling that to the San Jose Sharks.As of Thursday, the Sharks were on the outside looking in. Friday, they looked every bit a playoff team, riding the great play of goaltender Yaroslav Askarov to get a much-needed 4-1 win over the Vegas Golden Knights at the SAP Center. It was the first time the Sharks had beaten the Knights in regulation during the regular season in San Jose. Vegas held a 16-0-3 edge over nine seasons.Of bigger concern to the Knights is the health of their captain, Mark Stone. He left the game late in the second period, returned briefly for the third, taking just one shift before returning to the locker room midway through the final period. His status for Saturday afternoon's game against Montreal at T-Mobile Arena is unknown.Coach Ryan Craig did not have an update on Stone following the game."He's being evaluated," Craig said.Askarov was sensational, making key stops throughout the contest. He denied Victor Olofsson in the first period, stopped Tomas Hertl in close in the second and Olofsson again in the third. Hertl, the former Shark, was playing in his 900th NHL game Friday.Jack Eichel was the lone Vegas player to get the puck past Askarov, scoring on the power play which cut the deficit to 2-1. It was the sixth straight game Eichel had registered a point. It appears his slump of a couple of weeks ago is a thing of the past.But the Knights were unable to draw even. And when Zack Ostapchuk scored off the rush to beat Carter Hart and make it 3-1, the Sharks finally had a win over the Knights in their home rink in regulation.It was a huge bounce back for San Jose, which had been shut out 7-0 in Anaheim Wednesday night. Michael Mina's goal off a rebound of Alexander Wennberg's shot beat Hart with 4:09 left in the second to open the scoring for San Jose. Adam Gaudette made it 2-0 5:22 into the third as the hill got a little steeper for the Knights.And with Askarov holding down the fort, stopping 27 of the 28 he faced, the Sharks were able to put their poor performance against the Ducks behind them as Macklin Celebrini sealed things with an empty net goal with 2:11 remaining as the Sharks have leaped over Calgary into the third spot in the Pacific Division. So if the playoffs started today, San Jose would be in.For second-place Vegas, it snapped a two-game road win streak and kept the Knights three points behind first-place Edmonton in the Pacific. Saturday, they'll face a Canadiens squad which has dropped three straight, is dealing with injuries, but has the services of veteran center Dylan Larkin."We have to continue to play the way it got us those chances," Craig said of the early opportunities that didn't go in. "When the goaltender has a good period to start, we have to find a way to stay with our game, continue to push and have some patience."I thought we got away from managing the puck. They were a team that came ready to play. We talked about following up the win (in Edmonton). Our guys were ready. We had some opportunities in the first period. It's a matter of staying with it."
