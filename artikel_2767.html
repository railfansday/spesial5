Here is where the Lions fit in the NFC playoff picture right now

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Detroit Lions have won six straight games and will finish Week 11 at 8-2, including a 2-0 record in the NFC North. The Chicago Bears (8-2) will keep pace with the NFC North-leading Lions after beating the Saints, but their 0-2 divisional record will land them in second place. But both the Bears and the Lions are now a step ahead of the rest in the NFC North.

Here's a look at the NFC playoff picture after Week 11, with "Monday Night Football" still pending.

The 49ers stand alone atop the NFC after beating the Minnesota Vikings on Sunday night. They're in the driver's seat of the conference, but still have a challenging schedule ahead. Their next five games: Seahawks, Giants, Rams, Chargers, Chiefs. Even one slip up could open the door for the Lions or Bears to take over the top seed. But the Vikings' shot at a first-round bye is nearly closed.

1. Detroit Lions (8-2, 2-0)2. Chicago Bears (8-2, 0-2)3. Minnesota Vikings (7-3, 3-1) 4. Green Bay Packers (6-4, 1-3)

The Thanksgiving game between the Lions and Bears on Thursday will be for sole possession of first place in the NFC North. Detroit could make a huge statement with a win, as they'd not only be the only 9-2 team in the division, but they would almost move to 3-0 in division games, with wins overall all three teams.

If the Bears win, it'll be their first NFC North win of the season, but Detroit will likely have to beat Chicago on the road in order to jump them in the standings.
