Deebo Samuel's 70-yard TD run gives 49ers 31-19 lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Welcome back, Deebo Samuel. The 49ers have missed you.

The wide receiver, who hadn’t played since Oct. 11, scored his second touchdown. His first came on an 11-yard reception. His second came on a reverse.

Quarterback Brock Purdy flipped to Kaelon Black, who was running right, and the running back flipped it to Samuel running left. Trent Williams and Purdy were among the caravan of blockers Samuel had in front of him.

The 49ers lead 31-19 with 6:59 remaining in the third quarter.

Samuel has three carries for 78 yards and two catches for 22 yards.

The Cowboys also allowed Christian McCaffrey to score on a 74-yard catch-and-run in the first half.

It is the 17th consecutive game the Cowboys have allowed at least 20 points.
