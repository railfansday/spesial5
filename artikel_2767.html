Jeff Hafley provides mixed bag of updates on trio of injured Dolphins

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Miami Dolphins have been fairly lucky when it comes to injuries this year, but heading into Week 12, they have a few starters whose status is up in the air for their matchup with the New York Jets.

However, before Friday's practice, head coach Jeff Hafley spoke to the media and provided updates on a trio of injured Dolphins.

Hafley revealed that while right tackle Austin Jackson and safety Dante Trader Jr. are both expected to suit up on Sunday, Miami will be without cornerback JuJu Brents due to shoulder and knee injuries.

Without Brents, the Dolphins will have to lean on Darrell Baker Jr., who just returned to the field from the PUP list last week, to play a significant role alongside fellow cornerbacks Chris Johnson and Jason Marshall Jr. Marcellas Dial Jr. and Jaylin Simpson also may provide depth at the position as well.
