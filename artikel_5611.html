Your Voice Matters: RISMedia Launches New Letters to the Editor Feature

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

From premarketing and private listings, to data ownership and MLS antitrust lawsuits, to massive consolidation, AI advancements, brokerage model productivity, affordability and so much more, the real estate industry is evolving faster than ever and on many fronts all at once. As news breaks almost daily and we jump between covering the topic of the day and breaking down what it all means via insights from industry leaders, the views of boots-on-the-ground, everyday practitioners–those in the trenches whose lives these big decisions affect daily–can sometimes get lost in the shuffle.

As the headlines come and go, you may nod in agreement or vehemently disagree; you may be aware of something a reporter overlooked or of an important viewpoint that hasn’t surfaced yet. You might chat around the water cooler the next day, text a colleague or post your thoughts on social media or under an article in the comments section, only to have them disappear as quickly as the next round of posts come flooding in. Your voice matters and deserves to be elevated.

As a leading industry media organization charged with the important responsibility of keeping you informed on the issues that matter to your bottom line, we also view our space as your “town square” in a sense, a place your professional community turns to for fair and balanced coverage, and to read what’s on the minds of your peers and how they are responding to the news of the day. We recognized in our current choice of options for that platform, an opportunity for improvement.

With that in mind, RISMedia is asking our readers to continue conversations that begin in our news coverage, offering a new Letters to the Editor platform where working agents, brokers, executives and industry thought leaders can speak out. Our goal with this new feature is to allow real estate professionals at every level to share their perspectives, opinions, commentary and criticisms related to issues that matter to our industry—just your words and thoughts, no writing experience necessary and no pay-to-play.

The space, prominently accessible on our website navigation, was created for you to share thoughtful, original letters responding or relating to an issue, event or industry practice that was covered in our digital news over the previous few weeks.

Your viewpoints can provide a vital reality check on high-level reporting, where a national headline might only tell the story from the thousand-foot level, but your local market insights can offer invaluable perspectives that can enlighten your peers and possibly influence the industry’s top leaders or policy makers who look to trade media to weigh decision-making.

Oftentimes in our industry, the narrative is shaped by a small but vocal few at the top of the ranks while the challenges of today’s market–of which are many–and the daily grind of doing business right now–are carried out by the quiet majority of independent contractors at the heart of the business, doing their level best every day to move the market forward.

It’s never been a more dynamic time to be a real estate professional in the United States, and the stakes are too high on too many issues to stay silent. So the next time you pause on a headline, we hope you’ll take a moment to step into the town square and share your thoughts with your community. You can read the guidelines for submitting a Letter to the Editor and share your thoughts with us here.

Beth McGuire is RISMedia’s vice president of online editorial.
