Exclusive: The New Oura Ring 5 Will Go on Sale for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The wearable our editor called the best-looking Oura Ring yet will get its first-ever discount.

Women's Health may earn commission from the links on this page, but we only feature products we believe in. Why Trust Us?

If you’ve been waiting for a reason to try an Oura Ring fitness tracker, Black Friday might be your moment. Women’s Health got the inside scoop that the brand-new Oura Ring 5 is set to be discounted for Black Friday and Cyber Monday, giving shoppers a chance to save on the smart ring that earned a glowing review from a Women’s Health editor.

What makes this little wearable worth a look? For starters, it tracks sleep, activity, heart rate, and menstrual cycle data, then delivers insights through the Oura app. You can check your sleep score, explore trends in stress and recovery, and track how they change over time, all without wearing a screen on your wrist.

After testing the Ring 5 for nearly a month, WH’s associate editor Olivia Luppino praised its comfortable, jewelry-like design and approachable, beginner-friendly app. The newer model is slimmer and lighter than the Ring 4, making it easier to wear alongside other jewelry. Oura says its battery lasts between six and nine days, and Luppino charged hers just three or four times during her month of testing.

While we don’t yet know the exact date when the Oura Ring 5 Black Friday discount will go live, the Oura Ring 4 is currently up to 30 percent off if you don’t want to wait. It’s important to note that your Ring 4 size may not translate if you’re upgrading, and Oura recommends using its Ring 5 sizing kit before ordering to make sure you get the best fit.

A couple of things to consider before adding the Ring 5 to your cart: the ring (like all smart rings) can get in the way when lifting weights, and an Oura membership costs about $6 per month or $70 per year on top of the device.

Whether you’re shopping for a wellness-minded gift or treating yourself, this is one deal to keep on your radar. We’ll update you as soon as the sale goes live.
