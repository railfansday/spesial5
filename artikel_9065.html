The Best Deals on Canon Cameras and Lenses

With the holidays just around the corner, the deals are coming in full force. This week, we’re focused on Canon gear, including discounts on DSLR and mirrorless cameras, lenses, and more.

The Canon EOS 5D Mark IV, arguably Canon’s last great DSLR camera for most photographers, remains a popular choice for those who either have a lot of DSLR lenses they want to keep using without adapters or just prefer the DSLR experience. It’s $1,799 right now thanks to a $700 holiday discount.

On the entry-level side of things, the Canon EOS Rebel T7 is $80 off for the holidays, both in a kit with an 18-55mm lens and one with the 18-55mm and 75-300mm lenses. The one-lens kit is $499, while the two-lens kit is $619. The Rebel T7 is certainly old-school by modern standards, and those seeking a solid camera at an affordable price may also want to consider a used Canon DSLR.

On the mirrorless side of things, there are way more deals. It's also possible to use Canon EF lenses on Canon EOS R-series mirrorless cameras, by the way, and the required adapter, the EF-EOS R, is $149.

The Canon EOS R6 Mark II is an incredible camera. Even though the R6 III has arrived and is technically even better, the R6 II is no slouch. It's $1,999 thanks to $300 in instant savings.

But hey, maybe you want to spend the extra money and get the 32.5-megapixel R6 III. Good news: it's also on sale. It's $200 off for the holidays, which brings its price down to $2,599. It was PetaPixel's Camera of the Year 2025 for good reason: it's superb. You all agreed, too, as it was also the People's Choice Winner.

The Canon EOS R50 V is a compelling camera for burgeoning content creators. As Jordan Drake wrote in PetaPixel's review, the R50 V is good for both photo and video, which is a relatively rare combo at this price point. Speaking of price, the R50 V is $150 off for the holidays, which makes it just $569.

If you're even more into video, the R6 V is an interesting option. It borrows heavily from the Canon EOS R6 III, which is a great hybrid camera, and tilts everything a bit more toward video workflows. We really like it.

The 45-megapixel Canon EOS R5 II is probably Canon's best mirrorless camera overall. It combines resolution and speed and captures great video to boot. It's an excellent camera and one that many different types of enthusiasts and professionals can use to great effect. It's $3,999 right now after $400 in instant savings.

The Canon PowerShot V1 is an all-in-one camera with a 22.3-megapixel Type 1.4 image sensor and 16-50mm-equivalent f/2.8-4.5 lens. It has a built-in ND filter, captures 4Kp60 video, and shoots photos at up to 30 frames per second. PetaPixel declared, "PowerShot is back," when we reviewed the camera last year. It's $849 thanks to a $180 discount.

The Canon RF 24-70mm f/2.8L IS USM is a workhorse lens for many photographers and videographers. It's pricey, but a $200 discount helps a bit. It's $2,399 right now.

Another go-to zoom for pros is the original RF 70-200mm f/2.8L IS USM (not the newer "Z" version). Unlike the Z lens, this model employs a telescoping design, allowing it to collapse into a much more compact size. It's $2,699 after a $300 discount.

On the complete opposite end of the price scale is the RF 50mm f/1.8 STM, the "nifty fifty." It's a great lens for a lot of photographers, especially those looking for something faster than their kit zoom. It's $199 thanks to a $40 discount.

This doesn't fit nicely into any other category above, but the Canon IVY 2 Mini Photo Printer is $20 off, bringing it to $99. It prints pocket-sized photo stickers and works alongside your smartphone. It uses ZINK ("Zero Ink") technology. I actually own one of these and use it to make fun gifts for people, and it's a blast.

For those who want really good print quality and big prints, the Canon imagePROGRAF PRO-1100 is a professional-grade 17-inch-wide printer that delivers fantastic results. It is $254 off until the end of the day on November 14, which brings its price down to $1,149.
