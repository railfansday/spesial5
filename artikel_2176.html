EFL expert on whether Derby County can survive ‘difficult’ Championship relegation battle

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Derby County are positioned in 23rd place in the table after their first 15 fixtures. DCFC are in the drop zone along with Preston North End and Burnley and they are five points from safety behind Watford and Lincoln City.

The Rams won 3-1 at home against Bolton Wanderers last time out before the international break. Ryan Nyambe, Jack Robinson and Rhian Brewster got their goals against the Trotters at Pride Park as they secured a valuable three points.

Elek has delivered his verdict on John Eustace’s side on the Not The Top 20 Podcast: “Relegation standard, they have to be. It comes with all the caveats, he’s a manager we like in John Eustace, their injuries are one of, if not the main reason, they are as poor as they have been this season. They did put in a much better performance last time out, we saw them win a game.”

He added: “Of course they can (get out of trouble), when (Patrick) Agyemang gets back he will give them so much more from an attacking perspective. They have so many defenders out. Matt Clarke probably being the most important one, he’s set to come back relatively soon which will improve them. But even so, from this moment on, whether it’s Eustace or whoever else, if someone can keep them up it will be one hell of an effort because they’ve really made their task pretty difficult to stay up given the way they have started.”

Derby County’s victory over Bolton Wanderers was a massive one and has eased some pressure off Eustace. However, DCFC’s head coach still has heat on him.

The Rams are back in action this weekend as they aim to build some momentum and claw their way closer to safety. They face a tricky away trip to St Mary’s to face Southampton and they will need to be at their best to get something against the promotion chasing Saints.

Eustace joined his current club back in February 2025 and left Blackburn Rovers in the process. They were in a relegation battle when he joined and he managed to keep them up.

The ex-Birmingham City man had Derby County competing for the play-offs in the last campaign. They missed out on the final day at the expense of eventual winners Hull City after they lost to Sheffield United.

Derby County’s squad has been plagued with injuries this term, as Elek has alluded to. DCFC need to get their injured players back fit as soon as possible.

Patrick Agyemang will give them more firepower up top and will be itching to return. The Rams also remain without defenders Matt Clarke, Eiran Cashin and Craig Forsyth, hence why they have delved into the free agent market over recent times to bring in experienced duo Robinson and Shane Duffy.

If the Rams were to sack Eustace in the near future, there are plenty of available managers who they could consider. Rob Edwards and Steve Cooper seem the most likely candidates, whilst the likes of Sean Dyche, Steven Gerrard, Paul Heckingbottom and Gary Rowett are unattached.
