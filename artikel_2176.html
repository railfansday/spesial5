Max Lowe breaks silence as Sheffield Wednesday situation changes

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Dave Challinor has taken over from Henrik Pedersen as the manager of Sheffield Wednesday, and he may find it a bigger challenge than he first thought.

The Owls were defeated by Oldham Athletic of League Two in Round One of the FA Cup last weekend despite lining up with a strong team, before a more youthful side were hammered 4-0 by Accrington Stanley in the EFL Trophy.

Challinor is still yet to take in a league game, which comes this weekend against Plymouth Argyle on home soil, and he'll soon welcome back his former Stockport County favourite Louie Barry into his plans, having been sidelined since early September with an ankle injury.

But with Barry heading back to fitness, Challinor is now having to deal with one more injury to a key player at the opposite end of the pitch.

One of Sheffield Wednesday's key players for a number of years now has been Max Lowe - the left-sided defender has been a top performer and has stuck with the club through their tough period when he could've easily left for another side.

If there's one drawback to Lowe though, it's his injury record. The defender missed the final eight games of his debut 2024-25 season with a knee injury, and in 2025-26 he'd miss a full three months with a calf problem.

More setbacks followed in late August this year when Lowe suffered with a hamstring injury, and after returning to action a month ago, it's now been revealed that the 29-year-old has yet again suffered a reoccurrence of the problem he picked up two-and-a-half months ago.

Lowe entered the Oldham defeat from the bench after 71 minutes at the weekend, and whilst finishing the entirety of extra time at Boundary Park, the after-effects of his injury means that he's going to be missing for at least four weeks, and potentially up to six.

The Owls and Challinor himself could do with Lowe back for the busy Christmas period, where they'll play three times between Boxing Day and New Year's Day, but given it's his second spell on the sidelines already this season, it's likely that no risks will be taken with the ex-Sheffield United left-back.

And Lowe himself has taken to his Instagram account to send a message to Owls fans regarding his new injury, and it's one that's come from the heart.

Some Sheffield Wednesday fans have been left frustrated at Lowe's newest injury though, with one claiming that he should've been replaced in the summer knowing what his fitness record was like.

And another Owls supporter believes that the medical department itself may need a complete overhaul, given the amount of niggling injuries that keep flaring up across the squad.

If Wednesday were playing in a back four under Challinor, then Reece James would be the obvious replacement like-for-like with Lowe, and that would be the case too in what looks to be the manager's preferred left of a back three.

However, James is also on the sidelines right now, having suffered a groin injury, and he's also expected back around the same time as Lowe.

It'll now likely be Sil Swinkels who comes in for Lowe on the left-hand side of a back three, having served his one-match suspension for a red card against Blackpool, although he too has already had a period on the treatment table.

Challinor may of course look to play a back four in some matches, given his last few months at Stockport saw him pivot to more of a 4-3-3 and sometimes a 4-4-2 given the attacking options he had to choose from, but for now, it may make more sense to utilise the 3-4-2-1, with Jordi Liongola and Yan Valery operating as wing-backs.
