3 managers Rochdale must consider ASAP with Ian Watson sacked after Northampton Town loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Rochdale have been left 22nd in the League Two table after a seven-game winless run. The dismal streak extended with a 3-2 loss against fellow strugglers Northampton Town on Saturday, putting head coach Ian Watson in real danger.

And, on Sunday morning, it was confirmed that the Dale had parted ways with Watson, who was appointed as Jimmy McNulty‘s replacement on a three-year deal in the summer.

The search for Watson’s replacement has already begun. Here, we put forward three contenders who should be on Rochdale’s radar…

Given his close connection with rivals Bury, Ryan Lowe may not be minded to take the vacant post at Spotland. However, Rochdale need EFL experience after poaching Watson from South Shields didn’t work out, and Lowe would bring that in abundance.

His stint with Wigan Athletic was tough, but he did a steady job over the course of nearly three years at Preston North End and delivered League Two promotion in his time at Plymouth Argyle. He had them 4th in League One when he left for Deepdale too.

Lowe’s EFL pedigree could be just what the Dale need to push away from the drop and further up the table in the long term. His locality ticks a box as well.

Plenty of League Two clubs considering a change will have eyes on Andy Woodman. He was controversially let go by Bromley after only 10 League One games, despite leading them from the National League to the third tier in his five years in charge.

Talk around a potential move to Crawley Town has been brought to an end by their appointment of Kevin Blackwell. Woodman is available for nothing and his pragmatic management focuses on simple but effective football, and he led a truly united squad at Bromley, helping them battle against the odds to reach League One.

Woodman could be the leader Rochdale need to ease nerves and move away from the relegation battle. He won 123 of 289 games in charge of the Ravens and could deliver results at Spotland too.

Last but not least is Stuart Maynard, who led Rochdale’s 2025/26 National League promotion rivals York City. His side came out on top in that battle for the title, but the Minstermen surprisingly sacked the 46-year-old before a ball had been kicked in the EFL.

Maynard made a name for himself at Wealdstone before falling short in the League Two play-offs with Notts County. His York City record was fantastic though, proving himself as a boss capable of taking to the next level in impressive fashion.

Having been let go by York, Maynard will be desperate for a chance to prove he can deliver EFL success. Rochdale could do with his galvanising management and intense football to breathe life back into the team.
