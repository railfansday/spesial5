19-year-old Southampton starlet showcasing exciting potential in European loan after early transfer interest

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Southampton sent eight players out on loan this campaign. With a mixture of academy talents and players who are on their way out of St Mary’s, the Saints are seeing mixed success from the departed members of the squad.

Moses Sesay, Nicholas Oyekunle and Jay Robinson were the three teenagers to leave for the season. It’s the latter who Tonda Eckert will be monitoring closely after a breakout term last year in the Championship.

Robinson made 28 appearances for Southampton, scoring twice and providing an assist. Minutes were always going to be hard to come by for the 19-year-old this season. With a move to Monza secured, the attacker has plenty to prove in the Serie A.

Securing a loan move to Serie A is a positive sign of how much potential Robinson has to unlock. It’s safe to say that despite a slow start to life at Monza, the 19-year-old is becoming a key player for the Italian outfit.

The Southampton academy graduate had to wait until September to make his first start, and he impressed in a 1-0 win against Torino. Featuring in an attacking midfield role, he completed the most dribbles in the game with four and had nine touches inside the opposition’s box.

His first goal came in the next match, in which he completed 78% of his passes and was dispossessed only once. Former Saints boss Ivan Juric has shown full faith in Robinson, starting the teenager in three of the last four outings.

The 19-year-old’s best display came against Cagliari, where he scored once in a 4-0 win. Two out of his three dribbles were successful in the game, and he made three line-breaking passes. It has been an impressive loan stint for the youngster with Inter Milan registering their interest.

Southampton have the option to recall Robinson from his loan spell in January. However, given his promising spell so far, the Saints would be reckless to do so.

At left-wing, the South Coast outfit currently have the options of Leo Scienza and Sam Edozie, as well as Lewis Dobbin, who can play there too. Edozie is likely to depart in January, though, after putting in a transfer request late in the summer window.

This would ultimately leave Eckert’s side short of attackers in that position. However, they must avoid recalling Robinson to improve their depth. Even if Edozie were to leave, his exit would give Dobbin a better chance of breaking into the first team after just 409 minutes played.

Most importantly, it would do no favours to the 19-year-old’s development. The Southampton prospect has only recently become settled at Monza, and with his minutes increasing over the last couple of months, the South Coast outfit can’t afford to ruin his crucial playing time.
