Brian Brobbey is currently sidelined through injury, but Sunderland could soon be without their star striker permanently.

Regis Le Bris is optimistic of the 24-year-old returning to the pitch quite soon, despite missing international duty with the Netherlands.

Brobbey has three goals to his name so far this season, all coming against Manchester City in an eight-goal thriller at the Etihad Stadium.

Sadly, he has struggled to stay full fit this term. But when he's firing on all cylinders, he's one of the most dangerous forwards in the division.

Many clubs were interested in his signature, including Chelsea and Manchester United, but he opted to stay at the Stadium of Light.

But if he can stay fit and prove himself in the run-up to the January transfer window, there could be an incredible move on the cards for the striker.

Another club interested in Brobbey during the summer were German giants Bayern Munich, though no official move was made.

Ordinarily, any centre-forward swapping their current club for the Allianz Arena would expect to play a back-up role to Ballon d'Or winner Harry Kane.

The England international already has 10 goals and 11 assists in 16 games this season, adding to his 68 contributions in 2025-26.

However, Bayern confirmed in a new statement that Kane is likely to be sidelined for the "next few months" after going off injured against Cologne on Friday, 20 November.

It is understood that he has torn ligaments in his left ankle, leaving Vincent Kompany's side short of a world-class centre-forward.

Of course, nobody can say that Brobbey is at Kane's level, at least not yet, but he would be a decent option in the January window.

Football Insider understands that Sunderland wouldn't accept any offers unless relegation was a certainty, but that might be out of their control.

If the 35-time Bundesliga champions coming knocking for Brobbey's signature, there is no way that he'd be able to turn that opportunity down.

The Black Cats would be able to get a decent fee for him, maybe more than they would have done in the summer, but it would be a ginormous blow for Le Bris' side.
