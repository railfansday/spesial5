Too late, FA overturns Leny Yoro’s Liverpool red card in Pochettino’s first game

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Manchester United have received a major boost ahead of their Premier League meeting with Brentford after the Football Association overturned Leny Yoro’s red card against Liverpool.

The defender was sent off just eight minutes into Sunday’s 4-0 defeat at Anfield after bringing down Alexander Isak during a challenge that referee Sam Barrott judged to be a denial of a clear goalscoring opportunity.

However, Manchester United decided to contest the decision, and their appeal has now been successful. The FA confirmed that Yoro’s one-match suspension has been removed following a successful claim of wrongful dismissal.

“The Manchester United player was sent off for denying an obvious goalscoring opportunity during their Premier League match against Liverpool on Sunday 22 November. The independent Regulatory Commission’s written reasons for its decision will be published in due course,” the FA’s official statement read.

The decision means the French defender is available for Saturday’s meeting with Brentford at Old Trafford, giving Mauricio Pochettino another option in defence.

Mauricio Pochettino was furious with the original decision, particularly because it came so early in the game and left United playing with ten men for almost the entire match.

The United manager believed Isak’s heavy touch had taken the ball away from the Liverpool forward and put Senne Lammens in a position to collect it.

Speaking after the defeat, Pochettino questioned whether Yoro had actually committed a foul and insisted the defender had no intention of bringing Isak down.

“The ball wasn’t in his control,” Pochettino said.

“Isak, when he saw the control wasn’t good, it’s normal to delay, and stop to the run. In that moment that Yoro came from the back, and you go down.”

The Argentine also made his feelings clear about the nature of the contact.

“But all that we play football; you know that wasn’t intentional. That is never a foul,” he added.

The FA’s decision will come as a relief to Manchester United but perhaps too late. United suffered a heavy 4-0 defeat at Anfield after Yoro’s red. Pochettino would have had a chance in the game if VAR had overturned the red card, but United got another decision against them in the Premier League.

Although the appeal cannot change the outcome of the heavy defeat at Anfield, it at least means Pochettino will not lose one of his defensive options because of a decision that the club successfully challenged.
