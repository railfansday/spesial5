Things are looking up for Horsham YM – Midhurst and Billingshurst are both beaten

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Last Tuesday night YM enjoyed a crucial away win, moving them off the bottom of the table by beating Midhurst and Easebourne comfortably 3-1.

YM joint manager Will Hendon told the County Times before Saturday’s clash with Billingshurst: “We’re very much looking forward to it, hoping that Tuesday’s result will build some confidence.

"It takes time to create a completely new team, but the application from the players has been brilliant.”

Having purchased their Gorings Mead ground, received planning permission for an all-weather pitch, and welcomed new sponsor HSM Global, the future is bright at YM, Hendon adding: “Very exciting times ahead – we’ve just got to keep pushing up the league now.”

And, on Saturday, YM duly responded with their best performance of the season securing a timely win over Billingshurst, with the three points moving them up to 17th – one below Billingshurst.

YM starting threateningly with a free kick pushed away for a corner by Hurst keeper Fraser Trigwell. Four more corners followed – two apiece – but the first half finished goalless after YM’s Etienne Frimpong was hospitalised with a dislocated shoulder.

Restarting, YM came close when Will O’Brien’s cross was headed just over by sub Ryan Riddles, Ash Mutongerwa also being too aerial ahead of a pair of YM corners.

Hurst replied with YM indebted by skipper Rojo Grant’s clearance, but they were promptly rewarded by an outstanding goal from Callum Dowdell, before Tom Lyons scored direct from a corner six minutes later.

Although Hurst kept scrapping they were unable to get the ball in the net, Hendon saying: “We targeted six points from the Midhurst and Billingshurst games and we’re very pleased to have achieved that.

"We’ve now got two away fixtures followed by four at home and are looking to continue the momentum from the back-to-back wins.” On Saturday YM go to upper-table Lingfield, who beat them 3-1 in September, while Billingshurst have a tough assignment with a visit from high flying Hassocks.
