55 photos as Eastbourne Borough see off Littlehampton Town in the Sussex Senior Cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Sports took an early lead through Liam Nash but Marcel Powell levelled for the Isthmian South Central visitors – who had knocked Borough out at the semi-final stage in 2025.

Max Sheaf restored Steve King’s side’s lead early in the second half and there was no looking back from there – and Gold Omotayo (2), Owen Bellamy and Zack Tarrant all found the net for a 6-1 final scoreline.

It means two Eastbourne sides are in the last eight – United went through with a 3-2 win at Bognor. But Eastbourne Town are out after a home defeat to Lewes.
