When is the Nations League quarter-finals draw? TV and streaming details, plus how to watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The group stage of the Nations League is almost at a close, with the final fixtures taking place this week across Europe.

Set to begin in March, sides from League A will have their sights set on lifting the Nations League title, while League B, C and D nations fight it out for promotion and relegation.

The draw for the Nations League quarter-finals will take place on Friday, 20 November. The draw begins at 11am UK time and will take place at UEFA headquarters in Nyon, Switzerland.

As of November 15, the following teams have confirmed a place in the Nations League quarter-finals: France, Belgium, England, Portugal and Spain.

In total, eight teams qualify for the quarter-finals via League A. The four League A group winners are seeded and placed in Pot 1 of the draw, with the four League A group runners-up are placed in Pot 2 and unseeded. Each group winner is drawn against a runner-up from a different group. Teams from the same group cannot play each other in the quarter-finals.

Teams placed in Leagues B, C and D all compete for promotion and relegation, and are unable to qualify for the knockout stage of the competition.

The upcoming quarter-final ties will be played over two legs during the March 2027 international break. The seeded group winners will play the first leg away from home, with the second leg on home soil. First legs are scheduled to take place from 25–27 March 2027, and second legs follow from 28–30 March 2027.

The draw will not be broadcast in the UK and Ireland. However, they are several options to stream the draw live, which are detailed below.

If you want to stream the draw via your smart TV, laptop, tablet or mobile phone, the entire ceremony will be streamed for free via UEFA.tv and via the official UEFA YouTube channel.
