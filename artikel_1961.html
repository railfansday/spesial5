Cheltenham Town v Halifax Town: John McGrath optimistic about chance to showcase talents

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

For the second year running, McGrath is taking a team to Cheltenham Town. Buxton lost 6-2 at Whaddon Road, but McGrath fancies the Shaymen's chances.

"It allows me to pit myself against a League Two manager and to witness my players against League Two players," he said.

"It's the place we want to take the football club, and it'll be a real good indication of where we sit.

"We've had reports on them, and I think we've got a real chance.

"They've got some really good game-changers, so have we.

"We (Buxton) went there last year and we were 2-1 up at half-time, playing with a real intensity," he said.

"We just came off that in the second-half and ended up conceding five second-half goals. I believe this squad is better equipped."
