A Runner Took a Midrace Puff of Weed at the NYC Marathon. Now, She’s Disqualified.

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

This Houston attorney lost her official result, but kept her medal—and her sense of humor.

Having your first marathon result erased after months of training might be enough to send anyone into a downward spiral. Andrea Sager, though, has been getting some serious mileage out of the bad news on Instagram.

"Damn, I can't send my 5:54 to Boston now 💀," the 35-year-old joked in a recent post, referencing the news that she’d been disqualified from the 2026 NYC Marathon despite crossing the finish line.

The Houston attorney and mother of two found out she was DQ’d days after the November 1 race when footage surfaced of her smoking marijuana on the course. The moment she decided to accept a puff from a spectator was caught on video, and New York Road Runners subsequently removed her finish time from its records.

As far as the rules go, there's little room for debate. NYRR's Code of Conduct explicitly prohibits "smoking, vaping, or use of electronic smoking devices, tobacco products, or marijuana products" in the starting corrals, on the course, and in the finish area, with the restriction extending 1,000 feet beyond those locations.

In a video explaining her disqualification, Sager acknowledged breaking the rules and said she understood the decision. Even so, she claimed she wasn’t the only runner to drink alcohol or smoke weed during the race, and suggested the difference was having the moment seen by millions of people on social media.

"I wasn't planning on smoking weed on the course, but I was in so much pain and needed something to help me finish," she told the New York Post.

Sager hasn't let the penalty spoil her memories of race day. Her original finish-line recap described a grueling 26.2 miles that involved "approximately 47 mental breakdowns," although she was thrilled to check the race off her bucket list. She later reminded followers that the experience could not be taken away from her.

She'd been having plenty of fun long before arriving in New York, too. One prerace Instagram video joked about giving up dating apps in favor of training for a marathon, while her bright pink marathon shirt doubled as a personal advertisement. The front read, "Andrea, 35, Single. Great Credit. Bad Judgment," while the back added, "Looking for a man with more endurance than my ex."

Her latest social media introduction has since acquired another word: "35. Single. Great credit. Poor judgment. Disqualified."

Sager still has her finisher medal, and the extra attention has brought some unexpected opportunities.

"I'm talking with the brands now, so there's a lot of upside, but still annoying," she told the Post.

Sager said she was told she can participate in future NYRR events, despite the removal of her November 1 result. Runner's World contacted the organization for comment and confirmation of her eligibility but received no response.

As for racing plans, Sager said she has HYROX Houston on her calendar for April 2027. And someone in her Instagram comments has suggested a shirt idea for her next event:

"I smoked weed at the NYC marathon and all I got was a DQ."
