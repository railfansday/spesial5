On its second-quarter fiscal year 2027 earnings call, held on Oct. 28, 2026, Houlihan Lokey (HLI +2.51%) announced $692 million in revenue and flat earnings per share. The firm's ability to drive fee expansion in Corporate Finance, its advisory business for corporate mergers and acquisitions, while capturing market share in Financial Restructuring, its advisory segment for distressed debt reorganizations, demonstrates the durability of its middle-market model.

Management offers three takeaways on how the firm is positioning itself for long-term growth.

Corporate Finance increased revenues 10% year over year even as completed deal counts dropped. Management focused on larger, higher-fee transactions within its core middle-market mandate of deals valued under $2 billion.

Financial Restructuring expects elevated activity through fiscal 2028 as debt maturity walls approach. Dislocation across software companies is generating new mandates that will convert into closed transactions over the next two calendar years.

Financial and Valuation Advisory, which evaluates illiquid assets for private funds, expanded revenue behind steady demand for portfolio pricing. This recurring work provides stability against broader market fluctuations in transaction-driven fairness opinions.

Middle-market deal activity accelerates across Europe and the U.S., giving the firm momentum. Its combined focus on corporate M&A advisory and debt restructuring protects earnings across market cycles.

Strategic acquisitions broaden global talent, positioning the firm for expansion.
