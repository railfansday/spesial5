3 Falcons defenders hit with 4 fines from Week 10 loss to Chiefs

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Falcons racked up nine penalties in last week's lackluster loss to the Kansas City Chiefs, but they were all procedural — there weren't any flags for actions that typically lead to fines. But that didn't stop the NFL from issuing four fines total to three different defensive players stemming from last Sunday's action.

Defensive lineman Brandon Dorlus was the lucky guy who got hit with two fines, each for $7,114. One was for unsportsmanlike conduct/taunting, and one was for a late hit on a quarterback.

DeMarcco Hellams, who slid into the starting spot opened up by the Falcons' decision to trade Jessie Bates III to the Jaguars before the NFL's deadline, owes the NFL $6,509 for unnecessary roughness/a hit on a defenseless player, and linebacker Harold Perkins was also fined $5,203 for unnecessary roughness/use of the helmet.

The NFL splits the fine money it collects from players between the NFL Foundation to support the health, safety and wellness of athletes at all levels of football and the Professional Athletes Foundation to support NFL Legends in need.

Jake Matthews, $11,941: Unnecessary roughness, face mask, Week 2

Cameron Thomas, $8,333: Unnecessary roughness, use of the helmet, Week 3

Chris Blair, $5,972: Unnecessary roughness, blindside block, Week 6

Drake London, $12,537: Unnecessary roughness, striking/kicking/kneeing, Week 8

Drake London, $23,882: Unnecessary roughness, use of the helmet, Week 9

Brandon Dorlus, $7,114: Unsportsmanlike conduct, taunting, Week 10

Brandon Dorlus, $7,114: Late hit on a QB, Week 10

DeMarcco Hellams, $6,509: Unnecessary roughness, hit on a defenseless player, Week 10

Harold Perkins, $5,203: Unnecessary roughness, use of the helmet, Week 10
