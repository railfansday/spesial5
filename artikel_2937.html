Mike McCarthy has confidence in Spencer Anderson to step up for Steelers offensive line

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Not a single thing went well for the Pittsburgh Steelers in their 38-18 loss to the Cincinnati Bengals on Sunday Night Football. The play on the field was bad enough, but the Steelers suffered a major loss to their offensive line in left guard Mason McCormick.

The interior offensive lineman suffered a gruesome ankle injury in the contest as he was carted off the field wearing an air cast. It's hard to put into words just how important McCormick is to the Steelers offensive line, but head coach Mike McCarthy is confident in backup Spencer Anderson to carry the load that McCormick left behind.

"Spencer's, you know, he's just a 360 football player, in my view," McCarthy told the media during his Monday press conference. "I mean, just to coming in here to see to see him play the different positions.  You know, last year, obviously compete. You know, he started out in his right guard and then also the ability to play left guards. Played all the different, you know, combinations of the tight ends on the ball and off the ball for us too, caught a few routes last week in practice. So I mean, he's a he's a gifted football player, this will be his opportunity."

McCarthy did mention that the team is still in the "information gathering" phase of the injury to McCormick, but called the injury "significant." It is safe to say that the guard will be out for an extended period of time.

Anderson started the season as the starting right guard, where he was replaced by veteran offseason acquisition Brock Hoffman by Week 3. The positional flexibility is something McCarthy clearly values, as Anderson's experience playing on both sides of the line has earned him another opportunity to start.

The Steelers will undoubtedly miss McCormick as a key piece of their young offensive line that finally seemed to be turning the corner this season. They will now turn to Anderson once again to hold things down.
