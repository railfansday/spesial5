Nauticus Robotics (KITT) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Management weighed UAE expansion delays against growing defense demand.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Nauticus Robotics (KITT -17.70%) reported third-quarter revenue of $2.2 million and a net loss of $4.9 million while executing three offshore wind and subsea cable projects. Management highlighted the extension of debt maturities and efforts to maintain NASDAQ listing compliance. The company is establishing a manufacturing and operations center in the UAE and integrating its ToolKITT software into existing ROV systems. Strategic focus remains on the defense market, specifically autonomous underwater systems for mine countermeasures and infrastructure protection.
