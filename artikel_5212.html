DBS is building two paths for 24/7 dollars with JPMorgan and Circle using USDC

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

DBS and Circle’s partnership gives Asian institutions a 24/7 USDC redemption route, expanding options for dollar liquidity.

DBS and Circle announced a partnership on Nov. 18 to let eligible institutions in Asia turn USDC back into US dollars directly through DBS.

For treasury teams that hold the stablecoin, the bank route would offer an alternative to selling it on an exchange or elsewhere in the secondary market.

The partners described near-instant, round-the-clock settlement when clients mint or redeem USDC through DBS. The announcements describe the conversion itself and give no timetable for when clients could use the resulting dollars for other payments.

Under the arrangement announced by DBS, an eligible institution could request dollars for its USDC through the bank. A treasury team could choose between the two routes based on the transaction it needs to make and the terms available.

A secondary-market sale requires a buyer. The bank route announced on Nov. 18 gives eligible institutions in Asia another option.

The announced alternative is to request redemption directly through DBS.

The releases give no rollout schedule, detailed eligibility rules, fees, transaction limits or measured settlement times.

DBS had announced a separate service with Kinexys by JPMorgan on Nov. 16, connecting tokenized dollar deposit accounts in Singapore and the US so institutions can move dollars between them.

The Circle partnership adds a way for eligible clients to convert between dollars and a stablecoin.

DBS and Circle said the partnership builds on their participation in Project BLOOM, an initiative led by the Monetary Authority of Singapore. Its work includes helping different forms of tokenized money operate across financial networks.

The companies gave no market-by-market rollout or detailed eligibility rules, and they also left the onboarding process, supported blockchains, fees, and transaction limits unspecified.

The round-the-clock settlement the companies described would address one part of an after-hours conversion.

To decide whether to use the route for a payment or other treasury need, an institution would still need to know whether its account qualifies, what size redemption DBS will handle, and what the transaction will cost.
