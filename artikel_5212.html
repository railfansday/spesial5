Bitcoin failed to hold above $64,000 earlier this week, fell below $60,000 and was trading at around $58,580 on Wednesday morning, down 1.97% over 24 hours, according to TradingView data. The Crypto Fear and Greed Index stood at 13 on Wednesday.

Ethereum lost 3.18% to $1,641, Solana dropped 3.72% to $67.86, and XRP fell 2.23% to $0.908 over the same period, according to CoinMarketCap data. The TOTAL3 index, which tracks the market value of altcoins without Bitcoin and Ethereum, was at $621.3 billion. Among large assets, Tron was the exception and traded at $0.3077, up 0.10%.

The combined share of USDT and USDC in the crypto market rose 1.73% to 12.4%. On Tuesday, the Nasdaq 100 closed down 1.99%, and the S&P 500 lost 0.50%. Nasdaq 100 futures were down 2.93% on Wednesday morning, and the VIX volatility index jumped 16.61% to 30.75, according to TradingView data. The yields on 2-year and 10-year U.S. Treasuries fell to 4.604% and 4.848%, respectively.

According to SoSoValue data, U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs saw $231.56 million in net outflows on Tuesday, and U.S. spot Ether ETFs lost another $64.02 million. It was the sixth trading day in a row of outflows for Bitcoin ETFs. Besides, the Coinbase Premium Index has stayed negative for 63 days in a row, according to CryptoQuant data, and Bitcoin has been trading cheaper in the U.S. than on global exchanges.

On Wednesday, SBI Holdings, Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group (SMFG) and the Solana Foundation announced that SBI Solana Global had commenced full-scale operations. According to the announcement, the three parties completed a series of agreements for a new joint venture structure, and SBI R3 Japan now operates under the new name.

The plans for the venture were first announced on July 13, when the Solana Foundation agreed to take a stake in SBI R3 Japan. Solana said on X that SBI Holdings and SMFG bring "$2.3T in combined assets and 80M+ customers" to the venture.

According to SBI, the company will start with use cases in Japan and then expand across Asia and connect with global on-chain markets. SBI listed four business lines for the venture:

SBI Holdings is one of Ripple's oldest partners in Asia. SBI owns around 9% of Ripple Labs and holds 60% of SBI Ripple Asia, its joint venture with Ripple, and SBI Remit uses XRP for money transfers from Japan to the Philippines. Earlier in November, SBI chief Yoshitaka Kitao publicly confirmed his support for XRP.

Bitwise CIO Matt Hougan also spoke about Solana in an interview with Real Vision. According to Hougan, Solana could become "a $300 billion chain" in five years if it captures 10% of global stock trading through tokenization. Hougan called it "a completely reasonable valuation."

On Wednesday, Binance also launched spot trading of five tokenized exchange-traded funds, called bStocks, against USDT. One of them, IBITB, is linked to BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust, and the others include SOXXB, IGVB, ARTYB and FRHCB.

Ripple also announced a partnership with Yapı Kredi, one of the largest private banks in Türkiye. According to Ripple, Yapı Kredi uses Ripple Custody to power its crypto platform Yapı Kredi Kripto. In June, the Turkish Capital Markets Board (CMB) approved the bank's application to operate as a crypto asset custodian, according to Yapı Kredi's filing on the KAP disclosure platform.

"With XRP among the most widely held assets in one of the world's largest retail crypto markets, banks are rapidly embracing enterprise-grade custody," Ripple executive Reece Merrick wrote on X.

Ripple also posted all sessions from its Swell conference, held on Oct. 27–29 in New York, on YouTube. At Swell, Ripple said that 1,700 institutions use its solutions. On Tuesday, AI project Heurist said on X that AI agents had made more than 10 million inference payments on the XRP Ledger, settled in RLUSD through t54's x402 facilitator.

On Wednesday, charts shared on X showed Bitcoin completely below the lowest band of the Rainbow Chart, a long-term model of the Bitcoin price. According to Glassnode data, the Bitcoin hashrate is down 8% year over year, and it is the first annual decline since China banned mining in 2021. Hashprice also fell to a record low, according to Glassnode. The market capitalization of Strategy (MSTR) fell to $35.7 billion, and the Bitcoin on the company's balance sheet is worth about $51.3 billion.

Joao Wedson, CEO of the analytics platform Alphractal, said on X on Wednesday that retail traders were buying Ethereum and whales and sharks were selling. According to the cumulative volume delta (CVD) data shared by Wedson, orders below $1,000 added $132 million since the Sept. 21 peak. Sharks, with orders from $100,000 to $1 million, sold $4.29 billion, and whales, with orders above $1 million, sold $2.5 billion.

"In my view, a sustainable Ethereum bull market will only return when sharks and whales start buying again," Wedson wrote.

The Ethereum Foundation also published the PQAI #1 roadmap on protecting the network from quantum computers. According to the roadmap, the developers picked the Keccak-256 hash function over faster ZK-friendly options such as Poseidon2 and BLAKE3.

According to CoinGlass data, $362 million worth of positions were liquidated over the past 24 hours, and 81% of them were longs. The U.S. dollar index (DXY) stood at 103.92 and USD/JPY at 159.25 on Wednesday morning, according to TradingView.

BitMEX co-founder Arthur Hayes posted a Bitcoin chart on X when BTC was at $58,773. Hayes wrote that Bitcoin "will retest $55k, hold on to your butts!" On Saturday, Nov. 21, Bitcoin fell as low as $55,150, its lowest price since September 2024.

Some traders took the other side. One of them opened a $500,000 long with 75x leverage and called prices below $60,000 "pure heaven to buy" on X.
