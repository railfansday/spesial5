$13T Charles Schwab adds SOL, AVAX, and LINK as altcoins search for a bottom

If the trend does not reverse itself, the altcoin market could be heading into another capitulation phase.

Looking at this from a technical point of view, TOTAL2 is just 15% away from erasing all of its Q3 profits. This could put short-term holders in the red and continue to cause capital outflows. This is getting increasingly risky as Ethereum’s Fear and Greed Index has already fallen by nearly 60% in Q4. In short, the overall sentiment in the altcoin space is now looking weaker.

Against this backdrop, Charles Schwab’s latest announcement has given the altcoin sector a boost. The $13 trillion TradFi giant took to X to announce it has added Solana, Avalanche, and Chainlink to its crypto trading accounts, allowing eligible retail clients to buy and sell all three tokens directly.

Naturally, the bigger question is what this means for DeFi-TradFi integration.

With a vast network of around 40 million active brokerage accounts and an attractive 0.75% fee on crypto transactions, Charles Schwab offers easy trading functionality through Schwab.com, Mobile app, and Thinkorswim. While this is less of an institutional investment and more of a retail adoption milestone, it will make investing in altcoins much easier for traditional investors. This could increase the reach of altcoins and bring more retail liquidity into the market.

The key takeaway? The timing of Schwab’s move could hardly be better.

The altcoin market is looking for fresh liquidity and renewed institutional interest.

The stablecoin space has been struggling of late, with the overall market cap dropping below $300 billion. This week, Solana’s stablecoin market cap dropped by more than 4%, showcasing a loss of nearly $2 billion in liquidity. Other large altcoin ecosystems have also been struggling.

However, with Charles Schwab moving into altcoins, this may provide some much needed liquidity boost.

The altcoin sector is also going through an important phase. As mentioned previously, another crash could cause more capitulation, with TOTAL2 just 15% away from erasing all of its Q3 gains. However, trading volumes may be starting to normalize. In September, altcoin volume reached nearly 4x Bitcoin’s, hinting at heavy speculation. The latest cooldown could point to a healthier market structure.

In this context, Charles Schwab’s move could be more than just another step in TradFi-DeFi integration.

Instead, Schwab’s addition could create a new avenue for retail liquidity. Whilst it may not result in an instant revival, it will make SOL, AVAX and LINK more accessible, increasing retail involvement and helping revive a September-style wave of altcoin adoption.

Altcoins face more selling pressure as market sentiment and liquidity weaken.
