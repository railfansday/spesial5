Devolver Digital is Running a Giveaway for Anyone Willing to Delete their GTA 6 Preload

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Daniel is a lifelong gamer with a love for various genres and gameplay styles who enjoys exploring games and playing from various angles.

Devolver Digital has always had something of a reputation amongst game publishers as a wild card, a cheeky band who will take unusual, often silly measures to promote their upcoming games. Said measures can range from creating entire standalone games like Devolverland Expo to hitching a ride on whatever the current trend is in pop culture, and whether or not they work, you have to respect the publisher’s sheer commitment to the bit.

Now, if I asked you, “what’s the biggest buzz in pop culture right now,” the answer would undoubtedly be Grand Theft Auto 6. The game is finally launching in two days after over a decade of development, and everyone who plans on playing has likely had their preloads locked and loaded since they opened up last week. However, here’s the big question: would you be willing to part with all 212 GB of that preload for a chance to win a copy of a pixel art sidescroller?

On the official Devolver Digital X, formerly Twitter account, the publisher announced its latest marketing stunt: an official giveaway contest for its next upcoming title, Volvy’s Adventure: Reslimed, which just so happens to be launching the same day as GTA 6 on November 19. Do you want to win? All you have to do is bin your preload.

With 62 bird species to discover and a wildlife logbook to fill, GTA 6 might turn even the most dedicated criminals into birdwatchers.

As Devolver Digital explains in its X post, it’s offering the chance to win a free copy of Volvy Adventure: Reslimed to anyone who reposts with the hashtag “#MakeRoomForVolvy,” alongside a clip of them deleting their GTA 6 preload from their console. Said clip can either be direct captured footage from the console in question, or just a filmed clip taken with your phone, but either way, that preload has to go bye-bye if you want a chance to win.

A follow-up post notes that Volvy’s Adventure is exclusively launching for PC, so while the filmed clip does need to be of a preload deletion on a console, you will need a PC to actually redeem and play the game through Steam if you win.

That preload has to go bye-bye if you want a chance to win.

Obviously, the contest doesn’t require you to skip playing GTA 6 altogether; you can immediately preload the game again after deleting it, and Devolver won’t care. You just have to show your commitment to the bit, and considering the game’s massive preload has reportedly taken some users several days to download, that’s a bigger commitment for some than others.

Even so, the contest has drawn some interest, with a number of retweeters deleting their preloads in the name of Volvy, though others have apparently already pre-ordered Volvy’s Adventure on Steam and weren’t planning on getting GTA 6 anyway. Life is funny like that sometimes.

The release of Grand Theft Auto 6 isn’t just a product launch, it’s a full-on social phenomenon. Everyone is talking about it, even those completely outside of the gaming culture sphere, and businesses around the world are bracing for large swaths of their employees to spontaneously develop the flu on release day to skip work.

Not dissimilar to Devolver, many other businesses in and out of the gaming industry are looking for their own ways to capitalize on this major pop culture moment. Just today, KFC’s Australian branch announced it would be holding a fake funeral to give people an excuse to call out of work, alongside offering free delivery throughout the country on launch day.

Similarly, DJ Khaled is running a promotion alongside XBOX where he’s signing sick day notes under the guise of “Dr. XBOX,” warning that many people are experiencing a severe “vitamin G deficiency.”

I’m honestly uncertain whether this mass marketing push will slow once GTA 6 actually comes out, or just ramps up further, at least for a month or so afterward.
