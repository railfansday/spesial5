Zelda: A Link Between Worlds Listed As 'Playable With NSO', But Let's Not Jump To Conclusions

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Nintendo's official website for the Zelda series has updated its entry for A Link Between Worlds with a mysterious 'Playable with Nintendo Switch Online' icon, leading some to believe that it's a hint of an upcoming expansion to the paid service. However, we're not so sure.

The site is home to a series timeline, listing every mainline game from the NES original through to the Ocarina of Time remake. The likes of A Link to the Past, Link's Awakening and Oracle of Seasons are all marked with a 'Playable on NSO' button and, indeed, you can play all of them on the service. A Link Between Worlds has been locked to the 3DS since its launch back in 2013, however. Not only is it decidedly not playable on Nintendo Switch Online, but there isn't even a library for the console.

However, there are just as many replies that seem to take a similarly cautious approach to us. You see, the button is missing on Oracle of Ages — a game that notably is on the NSO library — and none of the series' other DS games have any sign that they're playable, too. Hmm.

We're chalking this one up to a simple mistake, then — we wouldn't be surprised to see the message removed soon enough. There have been rumblings of a dual-screen addition to the NSO library ever since we first learnt about the Switch 2's bonus USB-C port (remember those mock-ups for an extra screen add-on?), but it feels very unlikely we'd find out like this.

Ah well, back to our 3DS copies we go, then...
