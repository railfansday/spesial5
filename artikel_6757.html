A Look Inside the 2026 Dia Art Foundation Fall Gala

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Attendees included Sofia Coppola, Marion Cotillard, and Matthieu Blazy

Last night marked a busy evening in the world of fashion. In New York City, the 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards were hosted at the American Museum of Natural History, while in Los Angeles, a party was held to celebrate a new book documenting photographer Richard Avedon’s work in Harper’s Bazaar. But that wasn’t all—even more members of the fashion and art worlds united for the Dia Art Foundation’s annual fall gala in NYC.

The event acted as a celebration of two creatives—artist Roni Horn and British film director Steve McQueen—as well as a fundraiser for the arts. Guests gathered at the Dia Chelsea museum before migrating to a cocktail party and dinner where the honorees were toasted.

Some of the starry names in attendance included director Sofia Coppola and Oscar-winning actor Marion Cotillard. This is the first year that the gala has been sponsored by Chanel, after the French fashion house announced its partnership with the non-profit organization back in May. So, this naturally meant that Chanel’s current creative director, Matthieu Blazy, was also present (and he has been teaming up with the organization for years, even since he was at the helm of Bottega Veneta). In 2027, Chanel will sponsor the Dia Beacon gallery’s annual Spring Benefit luncheon, too.

Remarks were shared by the chairman of the Dia Foundation’s board of trustees, Nathalie de Gunzburg; the Dia director, Jessica Morgan; as well as by Matthew Barney, who paid tribute to Horn. Proceeds from the evening will go towards the foundation’s restoration projects, while also contributing to art-related lectures, commissions, educational programs, and more.
