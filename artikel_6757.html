Rihanna Completes Her Sporty Shopping Look With a Pair of Suede Sneaker Mules

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Rihanna is simply not on the same fashion playing field as the rest of us. Sure, she’s besties with some of the world’s biggest designers, and she has access to many of the latest collections before they even hit the stores, but she also just has an utterly sui generis approach to styling. In her universe, a burger run calls for a tiered, tiramisu-colored maxi dress and clerical errands are done in oversize, avant-garde suits. When it comes to tricks, like wearing a foulard as a skirt or treating an exposed-bra as a statement piece, she’s always at the head of the pack. Then, of course, there’s her maternity wear, which speaks quite loudly for itself.

So, it should come as no surprise that, even when she’s keeping things casual, Rihanna still manages to slip in a fabulous surprise or two. And on Sunday, that intriguing touch came courtesy of some hybrid footwear, which RiRi casually incorporated into her sporty off-duty look.

As she enjoyed a bit of nighttime shopping at the Grove in Los Angeles, the “Diamonds” singer bundled up in a baby-pink button-up and cocoa-colored slacks, which she layered underneath an ecru trench with a shiny golden lining.

Rihanna tied the coat loosely around her waist so as not to conceal the Neapolitan-colored outfit underneath, and she complemented her trench’s interior with a thick gold collar necklace. In her hand, she carried a monogrammed Speedy from Louis Vuitton, and she popped on a pair of chunky creamsicle-colored sunglasses from Saint Laurent.

But it was the most nondescript element that turned out to be the most striking. To match her coat, the singer and business mogul slipped into a pair of buff-colored suede sneakers from the New Balance x Miu Miu collaboration, which were first unveiled back in 2024. However, these hybrid shoes weren’t actually sneakers at all, but rather mules, giving new shape to the classic New Balance 530 SL silhouette that they were inspired by.

And this is just the latest evidence of the ongoing designer deconstruction of the shoe. At Chanel, Matthieu Blazy is offering up sandals that only cover the wearer’s heels. At Saint Laurent, Anthony Vaccarello presented Oxfords made out of see-through PVC.

Now, leave it to Rihanna to pull out the perfect transitional footwear for fall.
