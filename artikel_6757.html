Is Belrose University From ‘Campus Drivers’ Real? Let’s Break Down the Dreamy Filming Locations

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Living vicariously through the characters in a romance show is always fun, but when the love story unfolds in a gorgeous location, it makes the fantasy even better. Are you enjoying the French series Campus Drivers on Prime Video, and feeling the itch to start exploring the area on Google Maps, Airbnb, and even Zillow? These Campus Drivers filming locations will get you started.

The series follows a college student named Lois, who becomes temporarily homeless after getting dumped by her boyfriend/roommate and moves in with her neighbor. He happens to have a ride-share app, which is definitely not just for transporting drunk coeds he and his friends want to hook up with, and is named Lane. Normal meet-cute stuff. Also, Lois and Lane... get it?!

But the setting really stands out. Dare I say it, the hallowed hall and shiny shores of Campus Drivers is far more glamorous than anything you’d see on Off Campus, Gossip Girl, and even Elite. The beaches, clubs, and architecture are beyond what we’re used to on collegiate shows like this. Here’s how they brought it to life.

Nope! The Prime Video series takes place at a fictional school on the French Riviera. However, according to Decider, Quill attended Carlone University (locally referred to as Campus Carlone) in Nice. So if you’re looking to study abroad, that might be a good place to start.

This is a major change from the book series, however, which takes place in the United States at a fictional school called Sycamore University. But since Quill herself is French, and went to school in France, this doesn’t feel like that big of a change.

The Prime Video series filmed on the French Riviera, a.k.a. the Côte d’Azur, in the South of France between Nice, Cannes, and Antibes. Lane’s apartment is filmed at the Marina Baie des Anges, a famous waterfront complex in Villeneuve-Loubet. And finally, Villa Eilenroc in Cap d’Antibes is where they filmed Belrose University.

While we can’t promise that your ride-share driver will become a fling (or more), these are fairly easily attainable vacation spots. Beaches like Les Ondes and Port Mallet on the Cap d’Antibes appear on the series and are open to the public. Start your planning now!
