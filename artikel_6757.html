When Is ‘Dancing With the Stars’ on This Week? About That!

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

It’s Grammy Night on Dancing With the Stars this week! The show is gearing up for its last theme episode of the season before semifinals and finals, and if you’re wondering whether it’s on tonight...yes!

While Dancing With the Stars was on Monday of last week, it’s back to its regular schedule this week—and will be for the rest of the season. In other words, it’s on tonight, November 10, at 8 p.m. ET on Disney+ and ABC. The only reason the show switched things up last week was for election coverage, so it’s back to Tuesday nights henceforth.

As for Grammy Night details, the dances and songs have been revealed:

Dancing a paso doble to “Bring Me to Life” by Evanescence

Dancing an Argentine tango to “SexyBack” by Justin Timberlake

Dancing an Argentine tango to “Crazy in Love” (Remix) by Beyoncé

Dancing a salsa to “Die With a Smile” by Lady Gaga and Bruno Mars

Dancing jazz to “All for You” by Janet Jackson

Dancing an Argentine tango to “Thong Song” by Sisqó

Dancing a tango to “Boom Boom Pow” by Black Eyed Peas

The show is also entering dance-offs, where couples will “face off against one another on the ballroom floor as they dance head-to-head in the same style at the same time.”

Both couples will perform a rumba to “The Boy Is Mine,” performed live by Brandy and Monica.

Both couples will perform a cha-cha to “The Middle,” performed live by Maren Morris.

Both couples will perform a cha-cha to “All I Wanna Do,” performed live by Sheryl Crow.

This is all going down tonight, so see you then!
