QQ: Exactly Whomst Has Joe Jonas Dated Over the Years?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Big news for JoBro fans everywhere: Joe Jonas is (reportedly) back on the market now that his yearlong romance with Tatiana Gabriela has come to an end. According to an Us Weekly insider, “Things started to shift as Joe got busier with work commitments and they were spending more time apart,” and though rumor has it that Joe “may have reconnected with a former girlfriend” (per Deuxmoi), he has yet to confirm any of this so...stand by for updates!

And if you’re wondering which of Joe’s former girlfriends he may have reconnected with, that’s fair, because there’s a lot of them! From Sophie Turner to Gigi Hadid to Taylor Swift, Joe has romanced his fair share of famous ladies, and the time has come to take a walk down memory lane. Meet ’em all here.

Joe and fellow former Disney star AJ Michalka dated way back in the day after the Jonas Brothers and her group, Aly & AJ, went on tour together. The two were a thing for about a year and a half before breaking it off, and AJ later admitted during a May 2026 appearance on Call Her Daddy that Joe was her first kiss.

“We met on tour,” she said of Joe. “The Jonas Brothers opened for us, and that’s when we started—like, there was a spark. I was like, ‘Oh, this would be fun.’ We started dating on that.”

Taylor Swift (you might’ve heard of her?) and Joe dated for roughly three months in 2008, but things did NOT end well. After they broke up, Taylor went on The Ellen Show and revealed that he'd dumped her during a 27-second phone call.

The two have since made up (she did send his baby a present, after all!), but that 27-second phone call will forever live in infamy.

Camilla Belle and Joe met when she starred in the Jonas Brothers’ “Lovebug” music video, and they began dating right after the musician ended things with Taylor Swift. Tay wasn’t too happy about the timing, and actually told Us Weekly, “They’ve been together since we broke up. That’s why we broke up—because he met her.”

FWIW, Camilla and Joe only dated for about seven months between October 2008 and July 2009 before going their separate ways, with a rep for Camilla saying, “There is no third party involved and they care deeply about each other and will remain friends.”

Joe met Demi Lovato on the set of the forever iconic Disney movie Camp Rock in 2007, but they didn’t start dating until 2010, and the relationship was shorter than you might have realized.

“We eventually dated for a month. I really got to know her and got to see the ins and outs of what she was struggling with, like drug abuse,” Joe later recounted to Vulture in 2013. “I felt like I needed to take care of her, but at the same time, I was living a lie because I wasn’t happy but felt like I had to stay in it for her because she needed help. I couldn’t express any of that, of course, because I had a brand to protect.”

Thankfully, both Demi and her relationship with Joe are in a much better place now.

Shortly after Joe and Demi ended things, Joe began dating Twilight star Ashley Greene in the summer of 2010. Joe called this his “first serious relationship,” and in that same 2013 Vulture interview, he explained why things went south: “We were together for almost a year. I was living out in L.A. by myself, and at the end of the day, long distance didn’t work. It’s incredibly difficult.”

What’s probably most, uhm, notable about this romance is that years later, during a Reddit AMA, Joe revealed that he lost his virginity to Ashley. His exact words, you ask? Ahem: “I lost my virginity to this girl named Ashley. You can probably just Google it.”

Joe and Blanda Eggenschwiler, a Swiss-born model and graphic designer, reportedly began dating in November 2012 after being introduced by a mutual friend and were together for almost two years. At the time, Joe called Blanda “someone I really care about” and said “we get each other.”

Sadly, no word on why they called it quits. A rep for Joe simply confirmed to Us Weekly that “Joe and Blanda have mutually decided to part ways” but “the split was amicable and the two remain friends, with great respect for one another.”

Joe dated yet another super-famous lady when he coupled up with Gigi Hadid in June 2015. At the time, Taylor Swift insisted it was totally fine that one of her BFFs was dating one of her exes, and Gigi even brought him to Taylor’s Fourth of July party. But after five months together, things seemed to end on a sour note.

The model ultimately began dating Zayn Malik two weeks after her split from Joe, of which he said publicly, “I think it’s interesting that she moved on so quickly. I mean it was definitely very quick.” Joe proceeded to unfollow Gigi on social media and delete a picture of himself with Zayn. Yikes.

And if that wasn’t enough, when Joe was asked to play “Shag, Marry, Kill” with his exes on Watch What Happens Live, Joe chose to marry Demi, shag Taylor, and kill Gigi. Okay!

Joe and his ex-wife Sophie Turner first sparked dating rumors in October 2016, and soon thereafter, the actor was spotted spending the holidays with the musician and his family. They kept their relationship pretty private at the beginning, but once they got engaged in October 2017, they started sharing their love with the public.

In July 2019, they tied the knot at Château du Martinet in Provence (that is, after a very campy Vegas ceremony a month prior), and went on to welcome two daughters together. Fans were then shocked when TMZ broke the news of their split in September 2023. Three days later, Joe and Sophie released a statement on Instagram, sharing that they’d “mutually decided to amicably end our marriage.”

It took a full year, but in September 2024, their marriage was officially dissolved.

Romance rumors between Joe and model Stormi Bree initially began swirling when they were spotted walking through a private airport terminal in Los Cabos, Mexico in January 2024. Soon after, the celebs continued to take their love ~international~ with trips to Aspen and even Australia ahead of the Jonas Brothers’ February shows in Sydney.

Fast-forward to March of that year, and a source told Us Weekly that “Joe is still dating Stormi and things are going really well. They haven’t used the love word yet, but they’ve been seeing each other for a few months now and have been getting more serious over the past several weeks.” Meanwhile, “He hasn’t introduced his kids to Stormi yet, but if things continue going well like they have been, then he would definitely be open to them meeting sometime soon.”

Sadly, by June 2024, these two were done, with multiple outlets reporting on the split. But the musician didn’t fly solo for long, because just two weeks after, he was snapped with...

Joe and Lebanese actor Laila Abdallah raised eyebrows when they were photographed looking very coupley on a beach in Athens, Greece. However, a source later told Us Weekly that the two were “just friends” and “were together at the One and Only opening in Greece” with a group that included Luke Evans and Liam Hemsworth.

Joe was first linked to Puerto Rican model Tatiana Gabriela in September 2025, with a source later telling People that they “first started to get to know each other late last summer.”

The two kept the cuteness going for just about a year before Us Weekly revealed in November 2026 that the couple had called it quits, with a source saying, “They had a really fun summer traveling together, but then things shifted in the relationship. Things started to shift as Joe got busier with work commitments and they were spending more time apart.”
