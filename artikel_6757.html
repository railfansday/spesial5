Doctors Have Allegedly Told Maura Higgins She Needs to “Drop Out” of ‘Dancing With the Stars’

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Here’s the latest on whether or not Maura will be dancing this week.

This has been an especially intense season of Dancing with the Stars for mannnny reasons, and now fan-favorite Maura Higgins is battling a serious rib injury behind the scenes. While Maura confirmed last week that she was feeling a lot better, The Sun reports that she’s still in pain—and doctors have told her the only way to heal is to stop dancing.

“Maura was told by the doctors that there’s nothing they can do and nothing that will get better or feel better until she’s done dancing,” a source tells the outlet. “She would have to drop out of the show to fix the problems in her ribs, which she refuses to do. She’s not quitting this far into it and made that clear. So she’s going to just work through the agony for the next week, hopefully two weeks, and deal with her body and health issues after.”

The outlet also reports that DWTS pro Mark Ballas has adjusted choreography to help Maura out, and that she’s postponed several rehearsals going into the semifinals in order to rest up. That said, she was pictured at rehearsals on November 15, so it looks like she’ll be dancing on Tuesday night:

Meanwhile, Maura confirmed after Grammy Night that X-rays showed “no crack” in her rib, but “like any muscle damage or cartilage damage, it would feel the exact same, and it’s the same treatment. You kind of just have to let it heal.”

Dancing With the Stars airs its semifinals live on Tuesday night, November 17, and Maura and Mark will be performing a samba to “Samba Vocalizado” by Luciano Perrone and a Viennese waltz to “Shrike” by Hozier.
