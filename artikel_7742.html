Operation Get Your Kicks Supported by the Homeland Security Task Force Leads to the Disruption of an Alleged Firearms Trafficking Network, 23 Arrests, Seizure of 122 Firearms, and More Than 126 Pounds of Methamphetamine

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

 TULSA, Okla. – The United States Attorney's Office for the Northern District of Oklahoma, along with the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives, announced the successful conclusion of Operation Get Your Kicks, a coordinated law enforcement initiative that resulted in the arrest of 23 individuals, the recovery of 122 firearms, and the seizure of more than 126 pounds of methamphetamine.
