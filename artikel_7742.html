Illegal Immigrant Sentenced for Attempting to Have Sex with a Minor

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Bryan Silva, age 34,was sentenced on November 5, 2026, by United States District Judge Anthony J. Brindisi to 10 years’ imprisonment for attempting to persuade or entice a minor to engage in prostitution.  First Assistant United States Attorney John A. Sarcone III and Craig L. Tremaroli, Special Agent in Charge of the Albany Field Office for the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI), made the announcement.
