Bailee Madison To Lead Indie Film ‘In The Blue’; Adam Goldberg, Nat Faxon & Veronica Falcón Also Among 8 Cast

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

EXCLUSIVE: Bailee Madison (Pretty Little Liars: Original Sin) has been cast as the lead in In the Blue, the darkly comic thriller from writer-director duo Delilah Napier and Lucy Powers. Uri Singer’s Passage Pictures is producing.

Rounding out the cast are Maddox Blackwell, Adam Goldberg, Sasha Lane, Nat Faxon, Veronica Falcón, Betsy Brandt and Fran Kranz.

Madison will play Brie, an aspiring actor and tutor who’s offered a large sum of money to help her tutoring client, Seth (Blackwell), finish his college applications during his family’s holiday yacht trip. Roles for Lane, Faxon, Brandt, Falcon, and Kranz are under wraps.

“I am very excited to have this incredible ensemble on board and bring these filmmakers’ vision to life with such a talented group of actors,” shared Singer.

Singer is producing alongside Isabella Zanobini for Passage Pictures. Christa Campbell and Michael Peen are producing alongside Singer and co-financing the film through XTA Media. Carlos Cardona will serve as Director of Photography, and Mona Mekkawi is the production designer.

Madison is represented by UTA and TFC Management. Goldberg is represented by Buchwald, LuberRoklin, and attorney Matt Rosen. Lane is represented by Untitled. Faxon is represented by CAA andArtists First. Brandt is represented by Talent Works and Patty Woo. Falcon is represented by IAG andCarol Bodie. Blackwell is represented by The Osbrink Agency and Landsburg Management. Kranz isrepped by Gersh, Berwick & Kovacik and Sloane Offer Weber & Dern. Napier and Powers arerepresented by UTA and Artists First. Singer is represented by Knol Hanly PC.
