Comedian Dyon “Mojo” Brooks Expands Outta Pocket Tour With 25 Dates, Multiple Arena Shows For 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

EXCLUSIVE: Coming off his first two sold-out arena headlining shows and the No. 1 U.S. debut of his first stand-up special, Netflix’s I Know You Lying, comedian Dyon “Mojo” Brooks has added 25 dates to his 2027 Outta Pocket Tour.

This includes arena headlining performances at Houston’s Toyota Center on March 27, Atlanta’s State Farm Arena on April 16 and St. Louis’ Chaifetz Arena on June 4.

The artist presale begins Wednesday, Nov. 11 at 10 a.m. local time. All presales conclude Wednesday, Nov. 11 at 11:59 p.m. local time, with the general on-sale beginning Thursday, Nov. 12 at 10 a.m. local time. View Brooks’ dates below. The newly announced shows extend Brooks’ biggest headlining tour to date, with additional stops in Providence, Raleigh, Washington, D.C., San Antonio, Cincinnati and more. His expanded routing includes a previously announced April 17 appearance at Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena. Watch on Deadline

A comedian, actor, writer and producer (also known as Mojo Brookzz) with millions of followers across social, Brooks has had an astonishingly rapid ascent from viral digital creator to sold-out arena headliner — he’s gone from headlining clubs to theaters to arenas in less than two years’ time.

Brooks’ recent pair of sold-out arena shows took place at Wintrust Arena his hometown of Chicago. This followed sold-out shows at the Chicago Theatre, Detroit’s Fox Theatre and Netflix Is a Joke Fest at The Broadway Theatre. Brooks’ international debut in the U.K. also drew multiple sold-out audiences with several added shows, while his upcoming Nov. 15 performances at New York City’s Beacon Theatre as part of the New York Comedy Festival sold out during presales, prompting additional performances to be added.

Brooks released I Know You Lying on Oct. 6 — just weeks after his arena debut. The No. 1 Netflix special shares its name with his sold-out 2025 headline tour, where he moved more than 100,000 tickets across 40 cities. Prior to that, he sold more than 65,000 tickets to his 2024 Unhand Me Now Tour.

Outside of stand-up, Brooks has been seen in Tyler Perry’s Netflix series Miss Governor and Nick Cannon’s Wild ‘n Out. He is repped by Bernie Cahill and Grant Pennel at Activist Artists Management, WME and Todd Rubenstein at Yorn Levin, Barnes.
