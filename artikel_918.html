It is already clear that Coventry City's home form will be key to their survival bid, with the Sky Blues looking to make it three wins out of four at the Coventry Building Society Arena.

Frank Lampard's side scored seven goals as they saw off North East neighbours Newcastle United and Sunderland, although those victories were sandwiched by a disappointing 1-0 home defeat by Fulham.

Coventry's next two home games against Crystal Palace and Ipswich Town look inviting and they will be targeting a healthy points return, but today's match is now trickier than it looked a fortnight ago after Thomas Frank was appointed Eagles manager.
