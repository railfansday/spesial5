Rangers eye top spot vs flying Falkirk as Celtic face Tynecastle test

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Celtic are away to Hearts on Sunday, live on Sky Sports; Rangers host Falkirk; Aberdeen are at home to Dundee; St Mirren face Kilmarnock; Dundee United take on St Johnstone; Hibernian travel to Motherwell

Football Journalist @AlisonGConroy

There could be a change at the top of the Scottish Premiership if Rangers can halt the league's form team, but how long would they keep Celtic off the summit?

Martin O'Neill's side are away to Hearts on Sunday, live on Sky Sports, meaning Rangers can move above them by ending Falkirk's six-game winning run - for 24 hours at least.

Kilmarnock risk being cast further adrift at the bottom, so is it win or bust for Neil McCann against St Mirren?

Here, we take a look at the big talking points ahead of this weekend's games...

Rangers are hoping to make it lucky 13 this weekend as they bid to reclaim top spot in the Scottish Premiership and put the pressure on Celtic.

They edged Aberdeen in a five-goal thriller in midweek to extend their unbeaten league run to 12 in the league. However, it was a performance Derek McInnes was not happy with, as he accused his players of "naivety" in the game.

The Ibrox side are on the longest unbeaten run in the league, but Falkirk are on the longest winning run as they continue to upset the odds to remain third in the table.

The Bairns were winless when Rangers beat them at the Falkirk Stadium earlier this season. Now, after winning six top-flight games in a row for the first time since 1966, they are looking to go one better.

Based on first halves only, no side would have more points in the Scottish Premiership this season than Falkirk (26, level with Celtic). Based on second halves, Rangers would be top (28 points).

A big week for Hearts and Celtic will end with a Premiership showdown at Tynecastle Park, live on Sky Sports.

The Jambos suffered a stoppage-time Conference League defeat to Monaco, while Celtic beat Torreense to secure back-to-back Europa League wins.

Martin O'Neill's side put seven past Wouter Vrancken's side earlier this season, but have failed to win on their last two visits to Gorgie.

Goals are not an issue for Hearts now. They became the first top-flight side to score eight goals in a single game since 2022, as they demolished Dundee at Dens Park last weekend.

The Jambos have scored in 21 successive league matches, the longest run by a non-Old Firm club for more than a decade.

The Hoops have won their last four games in all competitions. Will they make it five to guarantee they remain top, or will Hearts be able to get revenge?

Kilmarnock are four points adrift at the bottom of the Premiership and have conceded a league-high 33 goals this season.

Neil McCann was not a popular appointment with the Killie fans, but he saved the side from relegation last season with the hope of better to come at Rugby Park.

If they lose at St Mirren on Saturday, they will have nine points after 15 games - just two of the last eight sides to have below 10 points at that stage of a top-flight campaign avoided relegation.

McCann and his players may take confidence from the fact that the Buddies are winless in their last four, and have scored fewer goals so far.

But one result would not ease the pressure completely, as the Killie fans demand another change less than a year after Stuart Kettlewell's departure.

While Neil McCann looks likely to be the next managerial casualty if results do not improve, there are other teams in need of a win.

St Mirren have won just four league games under Craig McLeish this season, while Motherwell have dropped to 11th after their late defeat to Rangers last time out.

Alfred Johansson believes his squad is finally getting to the position he wanted before the season started as players return from injury.

But, after a fourth-place finish under former boss Jens Berthel Askou, the Steelmen are in danger of being in this season's relegation battle. Defeat at home to Hibs on Saturday could leave them just one point ahead of Kilmarnock, if they manage to get a result.

Dundee made a strong start to the season, but have lost their last three games while conceding 12 goals.

Next is a trip to Aberdeen who appear rejuvenated under new head coach Mario Frick.

Steven Pressley said they got a "degree of pride back" against Hibs in midweek after being "embarrassed" by their 8-0 home defeat to Hearts.

Pride is one thing, but results are what matter.
