Hansen ruled out of Ireland's clash with Australia due to injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Ireland have been forced into making two changes ahead of their Nations Championship clash against Australia on Friday; Andy Farrell's side will be without Dan Sheehan after he was suspended; Mack Hansen has been ruled out with an injury

Mack Hansen has been ruled out of Ireland's starting XV to face Australia in this Friday's Nations Championship finals clash at Allianz Stadium.

Andy Farrell has been forced to make two changes to the side that beat South Africa 20-17 last Saturday, with Ulster's Jacob Stockdale being handed a surprise return as he steps in for Hansen.

Stockdale hasn't featured for Ireland at all during the Northern Hemisphere series, with Hansen featuring in all three of Ireland's Autumn Tests.

Dan Sheehan was suspended for two matches after he had a yellow card for a croc-roll against South Africa upgraded to a red card.

Sheehan had started at No 8 for Saturday's epic win in Dublin and will be replaced by loose forward Nick Timoney, who came on to play the final 20 minutes of that match.

On the bench, Farrell has made a further two changes with James McNabney replacing Timoney on the bench, while Nathan Doak comes in for Craig Casey.

Meanwhile, Australia head coach Les Kiss has made several changes to his starting XV, with uncapped James Ramm set to replace Harry Potter on the left wing.

Taniela Tupou, who started against Wales, moves to the bench, with Allan Alaalatoa taking over at tighthead prop. Alaalatoa will also captain Australia in what will be his 100th appearance for the Wallabies.

Tom Hooper moves to blindside flanker, with Jeremy Williams stepping into the second row, while Rob Valetini shifts to No 8 in place of Harry Wilson, who drops to the bench.

Ryan Lonergan replaces Kalani Thomas at scrum-half.

"He is a talismanic figure within the group, and I know the whole squad and support staff will be doing everything they can to make this rare milestone a memorable one for him, his family and for us," Kiss said of Alaalatoa.

"We've taken some steps forward, grown our game and tested our squad depth this year as a squad and I know the team is highly motivated to put in a performance that reflects the hard work put in by everyone in our final Test."
