The 2026 NWSL Semifinal: Where to Watch Gotham FC vs. Kansas City Current

Eight months after the season kicked off, the National Women’s Soccer League (NWSL) semifinals have arrived. On Sunday, Nov. 15, at 12 p.m. PT/3 p.m. ET, NJ/NY Gotham FC will face the Kansas City Current at Icahn Stadium in New York to determine who will advance to the NWSL Championship Final. ESPN Unlimited will be the go-to streaming destination for Sunday’s matchup, with the platform also streaming the 2026 NWSL Awards live on Wednesday, Nov. 18.

Gotham FC enters the semifinal with 18 wins this season, compared with Kansas City Current’s 16 victories. Betting and prediction platforms are giving Gotham a slight edge heading into Sunday’s showdown, with Opta Analyst estimating a 45.8 percent chance of Gotham advancing to the final. However, the projections suggest a closely contested matchup, leaving both teams with a good shot at the championship.
