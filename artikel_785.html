CMA Awards 2026 Red Carpet Fashion: See Every Celebrity Look

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Taylor Swift, Ella Langley and Luke Combs are among the nominees at CMA Awards 2026, hosted by Lainey Wilson at Nashville's Bridgestone Arena Nov. 18. See all the celebrities on the red carpet.

These celebrities are choosin' the 2026 CMA Awards.

Some of music's biggest stars were two steppin' 'round the red carpet at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville Nov. 18. And judgin' by the smiles written their faces, they were ready to celebrate country music's biggest night.

Take Lainey Wilson, for example, who is returning for her third consecutive time as host.

"I’ve had so much fun hosting the last two years," Wilson said in a statement ahead of the show, airing live on ABC and Disney+ and on Hulu the following day, "and this one’s going to be extra special."

That's partly because Taylor Swift is making her long-awaited return with her first CMA Awards performance since 2013, when she received the Pinnacle Award and marked the special milestone by hitting the stage with Vince Gill and Alison Krauss.

As the 12-time CMA Award winner quipped on Instagram after her Toy Story 5 song "I Knew It, I Knew You" landed on this year's nominations list for Single of the Year, "Looks like I haven’t yee’d my last haw thank you CMA."

But hang tight, honey—because Swift is facing against some fierce competition in the category, including Ella Langley's mega hit "Choosin' Texas" and its follow-up single "Be Her." (Click here to see the complete list of nominees.)

As the leader of this year's pack of nominees with nine nods, the "You Look Like You Love Me" is also up for Entertainer of the Year, Album of the Year, Female Vocalist of the Year, Song of the Year, Music Video of the Year and Musical Event of the Year.

As for performers at the 2026 CMA Awards? In addition to Swift and Langley, the star-studded lineup boasts Miranda Lambert, Tim McGraw, Kacey Musgraves, Chris Stapleton, Mumford & Sons, Tucker Wetmore, Cody Johnson, Megan Moroney and more.

Plus, late country music legend Dolly Parton—who died on Aug. 25 at age 80—will be honored with a show-stopping musical tribute. Meanwhile, Brothers Osborne, Luke Bryan, Halestorm, Hardy and Marcus King will take the stage for a Brooks & Dunn-themed performance to celebrate the duo's Lifetime Achievement Award win.
