Adam Levine Proves His Kids Are His No. 1 Fans in Parenting Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Voice season 30 coach Adam Levine shared insight into how he parents his three kids with wife Behati Prinsloo during an exclusive E! News interview.

Though The Voice coach joked that it's "true" how contestants on the NBC singing competition series take his advice more seriously than his three kids with wife Behati Prinsloo do, he knows his family has his back.

"They're pretty good," Levine said of his children in an exclusive interview with E! News' Will Marfuggi during The Voice's live show Nov. 9, adding with a laugh, "They still respect me."

In fact, the Maroon 5 frontman—who shares daughters Dusty Rose, 10, Gio Grace, 9, and a 3-year-old son whose name has not been revealed with Prinsloo—brought two of his kids along for the live taping of The Voice.

"They're super excited," he continued. "Two of my kids are here, so they're really pumped."

And Levine is equally thrilled to sit alongside fellow coaches Kelly Clarkson, Queen Latifah and Riley Green this season.

"I love this panel of coaches," he shared. "I love my team this year. It's been super fun. It's really been a blast."

So much so, the Grammy winner doesn't feel like it's harder to breathe when he's mentoring his team.

"Doing this show kind of taught me that I actually have something to offer advice-wise," he shared. "Until I did the show, there were many years ago when it started, I had never really been in that position to give advice and to help coach."

Explaining how the "biggest thing" for his career so far is to "help people with what they're doing," Levine added, "It was cool to feel like I had something to offer in that way."

So, will the "Moves Like Jagger" singer's kids follow his footsteps into the spotlight? Not quite.

Prinsloo told E! last year that the couple is focused on giving their little ones space to explore their passions.

“They’re still pretty small, I don’t think they even think about things like that career-wise yet,” the supermodel said. “Right now, my daughter is like, ‘I want to be an artist! Or a basketball player!’ But they love it."
