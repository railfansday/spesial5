Holders Celtic win 17-0 in Women's Scottish Cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Isabella Fisher scored four goals in Celtic's win

Holders Celtic breezed through to the fourth round of the Women's Scottish Cup with a 17-0 victory over Forfar Farmington.

Morgan Cross and Casey Howe both netted hat-tricks, Isabella Fisher scored four times and Amy Gallacher bagged a double.

Lauren Doran-Barr, Addie Handley, Maria McAneny and Evie Rabjohn also scored and Forfar conceded an own goal.

Last season's beaten finalists, Rangers, were 5-0 victors at home to Motherwell.

Mijke Roelfsema opened the scoring with her first Rangers goal, Eilidh Austin made it two and Laura Berry converted a penalty shortly before going off injured.

Fallon Connolly-Jackson got her maiden Rangers goal early in the second half and Hannah Blake completed the scoring.

Glasgow City defeated Cambusdoon 10-0. Leah Lane scored her first City goal, with Kelly Brady, Lisa Forrest, Abi Harrison, Yuuka Kurosaki, Jesi Rossman and Emily Whelan also on target.

Sofia Maatta netted twice and Cambusdoon conceded an own goal.

Reigning Scottish champions Heart of Midlothian won 6-0 away to Stenhousemuir.

Lucy Jane Hope put the visitors in front and Jenny Smith doubled their lead. Ava Crawford got the third, Monica Forsyth also netted and Hope and Forsyth added to the tally.

Montrose won 13-0 at Dunipace, with Neve Guthrie, Kaela McDonald-Nguah and Hannah Stewart each scoring once, Francesca Ogilvie netting twice, Darcie Miller bagging a hat-trick and Demi Taylor picking up a four-goal haul, with an own goal also aiding the visitors.

Spartans were 11-2 winners at home to Bonnyrigg Rose. Zara Andrews and Ronaigh Douglas both scored once, there were doubles for Emily Cadell, Tegan Reynolds and Izzy Young, while Emma McMurdo scored a hat-trick.

Mya Christie, Holly Daniel, Christie again and Briana Salvetti all netted in Aberdeen's 4-0 home win over Dryburgh Athletic.

SWPL2 side Boroughmuir Thistle won 2-1 on penalties against top-flight Partick Thistle after a 1-1 draw. Imogen Longcake gave the visitors a second-half lead, but Amy Denholm levelled to force extra time.

Falkirk were 7-0 winners at home to Renfrew. Maisy Stewart, Skye Neillie, Mhari Smith, Caitlin McFarlane and Olivia Murphy all scored first-half goals, with McFarlane and Emma Lawson adding to the rout after the break.

Queen's Park beat Dundee United 2-1, with Ellie Kane and Darcy Molloy putting the hosts two up at the break and Lucy Stevenson replying in the second half.

Rossvale won 3-2 at home to Gartcairn. Maddie Wilson and Sarah Petherick had Rossvale 2-0 up, but Clare Docherty replied for the visitors before the break. Petherick's second proved to be the winner, with Gartcairn's Madison Maxwell netting a late consolation.

Livingston v Hamilton Academical was abandoned at half-time with Hamilton a goal up, while Inverurie Loco Works v Bishopton, Kilmarnock v Hibernian and Westdyke v St Johnstone were postponed.
