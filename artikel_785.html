Grammy Nominations 2027: See the Complete List

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

RAYE leads all nominees with 11 nods, followed by Rosalía with nine.

RAYE is heading into the 2027 Grammy Awards ceremony as the top nominee, picking up 11 nominations on Monday morning (Nov. 16).

Rosalía is right behind with nine nominations, followed by Phoebe Bridgers, Miranda Lambert, Ella Langley and Olivia Rodrigo with seven each. Olivia Dean, Bruno Mars and Leon Thomas each picked up six nods.

RAYE’s nominations are powered by the release of her second studio album, 2026’s This Music May Contain Hope. In addition to three nods in the Big Four categories — album of the year, plus record and song of the year for “Where Is My Husband!” — the British artist is also nominated for songwriter of the year, thanks to songs she penned for herself and others.

Rosalía also has three Big Four nods: album of the year for Lux (Complete Works), plus record and song of the year for “Berghain” with Björk and Yves Tumor. At the 2026 Latin Grammys just last week, the Spanish singer/songwriter took home album of the year for Lux as well as song of the year for “La Perla” from the project.

Before Monday, both RAYE and Rosalía had four Grammy nominations each, though Rosalía has two wins — for best Latin rock or alternative album in 2020 and 2023 — under her belt, while RAYE has yet to win. Both were previously nominated for best new artist: Rosalía in 2020 and RAYE in 2025.

We’ll see who is the night’s big winner when the 2027 Grammy Awards arrive on Feb. 7, with the prime-time broadcast airing at 7 p.m. ET/4 p.m. PT on ABC and streaming live on Disney+ and Hulu and the Premiere Ceremony taking place earlier in the day, where the bulk of the 100 prizes will be handed out.
