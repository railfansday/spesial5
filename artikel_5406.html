Can MRADR Deployment Strengthen Leidos' Defense Technology Growth?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

LDOS' $40.8 million MRADR contract for the U.S. Marine Corps advances radar deployment, potentially boosting its defense technology growth amid rising demand.

Leidos Holdings LDOS is expanding its portfolio of advanced defense sensing and surveillance technologies as military customers seek capabilities to detect and counter evolving aerial threats. Demand for mobile, persistent surveillance systems that can operate in contested environments could create growth opportunities for defense technology providers.

Leidos was awarded a $40.8 million contract to build and support 14 Medium Range Air Defense Radar (MRADR) systems for the U.S. Marine Corps. The award moves the program beyond its prototype phase, following a $32 million contract in 2023 to develop four prototype systems.

MRADR is designed to provide persistent, 360-degree airspace surveillance, helping Marines detect, track and identify aerial threats while minimizing the risk of revealing their location. Its mobile design, advanced antenna technology and signal processing capabilities are intended to support operations in increasingly contested environments.

The new award includes engineering, manufacturing and integration work, with completion expected by August 2028. It also includes an option to retrofit previously developed prototype systems and existing Marine Expeditionary Long Range Persistent Sensor systems, potentially expanding the program's scope.

Leidos also develops other advanced sensing technologies, including long-range surveillance radar, counter-drone systems and infrared sensors for missile warning and tracking. Growing military investment in air defense, surveillance and threat detection could support demand for its defense technology portfolio. The transition of MRADR toward broader fielding further strengthens Leidos' position in this market, although execution and production ramp-up will remain important.

Growing demand for advanced radar, surveillance and air-defense technologies could also benefit other defense contractors.

RTX Corporation RTX: RTX develops advanced radar, air-defense and missile-defense systems, including technologies supporting Patriot and other integrated air-defense platforms. Rising demand for threat detection and layered defense capabilities could support its defense business.

Northrop Grumman $NOC NOC: Northrop Grumman provides advanced radar, sensors, electronic warfare and command-and-control technologies. Its expertise in integrated air and missile defense and surveillance systems positions it to benefit from increasing investment in detecting and countering evolving aerial threats.
