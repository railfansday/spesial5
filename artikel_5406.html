U.S. weekly jobless claims fell to a four-month low, beating forecasts

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Initial claims dropped 13,000 to a seasonally adjusted 189,000 in the week ending Nov. 21, matching the mid-July low

The Labor Department reported on Wednesday that initial jobless claims fell by 13,000 to a seasonally adjusted 189,000 in the week ending Nov. 21. The reading is the lowest since mid-July, when claims also stood at 189,000, according to Reuters.

That undercut the 200,000 claims economists expected, according to Reuters. The department revised the prior week's level down by 1,000 to 202,000. The four-week moving average was 196,000, a decrease of 1,500 from the previous week's revised average.

Before seasonal adjustment, states recorded 212,945 initial claims, an increase of 15,894, or 8.1%, from the previous week. Seasonal factors had called for an increase of 30,549, or 15.5%. The comparable week in 2025 had 244,713 initial claims.

Continuing claims, which count people still collecting benefits after an initial week, fell by 95,000 to a seasonally adjusted 1,697,000 in the week ending Nov. 14. The seasonally adjusted insured unemployment rate was 1.1%, down 0.1 percentage point from the previous week's revised rate. The department revised the prior week's continuing claims up by 29,000 to 1,792,000.

The continuing claims figure spans the same stretch in which federal officials polled households to calculate the jobless rate for November, according to Reuters. The unemployment rate was 4.1% in October.

Among states, the largest increases in unadjusted initial claims for the week ending Nov. 14 were in Michigan (+6,036), Wisconsin (+210) and Kentucky (+106). The largest decreases were in Missouri (-3,286), New Jersey (-2,717) and Texas (-2,069). Missouri cited fewer layoffs in manufacturing, accommodation and food services, and retail trade.

The Labor Department had put claims at 203,000 for the week ending Nov. 14, before revising that figure to 202,000. That left claims above 200,000 for two straight weeks. Claims were at or below 200,000 in each week from Sept. 12 through Oct. 31.

Claims reached 189,000 in mid-July, a low last seen in 1969. The highest weekly reading this year was 230,000.
