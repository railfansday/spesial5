Geralt of Rivia Officially Returns in New Witcher Expansion

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Although there won’t be another season of The Witcher on Netflix this year, fans of the hit fantasy franchise still have a lot to be excited about in the next few years. Many gamers are still working their way through The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt after CD Projekt Red released a new next-generation remaster of the game back at the end of September. It features a slew of added accessibility options, as well as enhancements to make the game feel more like a game that was released in the last few years and not over 10 years ago. CD Projekt Red also has one final expansion for The Witcher 3, Songs of the Past, set to be released sometime next year. It will be The Witcher 3’s final expansion centered around Geralt of Rivia before Ciri takes center stage in The Witcher 4 in 2028.

It’s safe to say The Witcher 4 is one of the most anticipated games currently in development, but there is still plenty of Geralt’s story left to tell before Ciri takes over in a few years. In fact, The Witcher 3 players on PC were treated to a new expansion mod featuring more story for Geralt of Rivia via the new mod, Annals of Duchy, which is described as a “lore-friendly unofficial expansion to Geralt’s life in Toussaint.” Those who have played The Witcher 3 know well that Toussaint is the area in which the beloved DLC, Blood and Wine, takes place. The area is vastly different from other famous Witcher 3 areas such as Skellige or Novigrad. It offers big cities and more rural areas, both flush with color and danger.

Four worlds and four completely different ideas of what a good life looks like. This is where you belong, what it would ask of you, and what you would get back in return.

You belong somewhere old enough that the mountains have names and somebody can still tell you who gave them. Middle-earth is for people who think the right thing is worth doing even when it will not be finished in their lifetime.

You want the sea, a crew, and absolutely nobody telling you where you are allowed to go. The Grand Line is the world for people whose main ambition in life is finding out what is over there.

You would rather be feared and useful than liked and useless. The Continent is a world where the monsters are the straightforward part of the job and the people are where it all gets complicated.

You want the myths to be real and loose in the actual world, and you want to find out at about thirteen that you were never anywhere near as ordinary as everybody kept telling you.

CD Projekt Red has already confirmed that Geralt of Rivia will be in The Witcher 4, but the nature of his role is unclear at this time. Most fans are expecting Geralt to act as more of a mentor to Ciri in The Witcher 4, but a playable section as Geralt also can’t be ruled out, especially considering how many sections of The Witcher 3 required fans to play as Ciri. It would be undeniably cool if CD Projekt Red gave Geralt a big moment as a sort of send-off after playing as him for so many years.
