Marvel’s ‘WandaVision’ Trilogy Ended Without Its Original Creator Giving ‘VisionQuest’ a Single Note

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

2026 has been an exciting year for Marvel fans, and it all started with the release of Wonder Man back in January. Marvel has continued this run of success with new Disney+ entries such as second seasons of Daredevil: Born Again and X-Men '97, but the franchise really picked up steam late in July with the launch of Spider-Man: Brand New Day. The movie grossed over $2.5 billion at the box office and is now one of the highest-grossing feature films of all time. Everything in the last few years for Marvel has been leading to the behemoth event film, Avengers: Doomsday, which is slated for release in theaters on December 18, the same day as Dune: Part Three. The final project before Doomsday was VisionQuest, the series starring Paul Bettany that's now streaming in its entirety on Disney+.

VisionQuest is viewed as the third installment in a trilogy that started with WandaVision back in 2021 and continued with Agatha All Along in 2024. Both of the first two shows were written and created by Jac Schaeffer, who has also been tasked with writing the new Stargate series in the works at Prime Video. However, Marvel enlisted Star Trek icon Terry Matalas to serve as the lead writer and showrunner for VisionQuest, a decision that clearly paid off after the show was a hit with both critics and audiences.

Collider's Maggie Lovitt recently sat down for an episode of Collider's Behind the Scenes series with Matalas to speak about the season finale of VisionQuest, and she asked if he had any conversations with Schaeffer about how to tie up the WandaVision trilogy, or if she had any advice to impart on finishing this story. His answer might shock you:

“No, not at all. I called her early on, right when I got the job, because I wanted to just tell her what an honor it was to do this. I love WandaVision. I saw every episode twice when it came out. I would pop it back on. And the first thing she said is, ‘I don’t want to talk about anything creative. You have your blank canvas here.’ And I thought that that was really generous, you know? She certainly could have [said], ‘Be mindful of X, Y, and Z,’ and she didn’t. She wanted us to go do our own thing. And so, that was that. We had exactly that, a blank canvas.”

Throughout the first season of VisionQuest, fans certainly enjoyed the performances from Paul Bettany and James Spader, who was back playing Ultron for the first time since Avengers: Age of Ultron in 2015. However, there were questions from the first episode about whether Elizabeth Olsen would return as Scarlet Witch after she was last seen at the end of Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness with a mountain being dropped on her. It wasn't until the post-credits scene that Doctor Doom (played by Robert Downey Jr) showed up and revived Wanda to use her power. The Scarlet Witch is expected to play a major role in Avengers: Doomsday, which opens in theaters on December 18.
