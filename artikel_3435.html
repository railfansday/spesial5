GTA 6: How To Fix Up Jason’s House

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Grand Theft Auto 6 has a lot going on, and it's really easy to miss things. For instance, after you start your journey, you'll take control of Jason, who is trying to make the Florida Keys feel like home while waiting for his partner, Lucia, to get out of Leonida Penitentiary.

In the process, all sorts of errands open up, including shopping for new clothes and groceries, as well as fixing up the initial house the game provides as a home base. While it's not required to do housework in GTA 6, there is a benefit.

You might be wondering: "Why even bother fixing up Jason's house?" Well, the jobs don't take as long as you'd think (they start as realistic handiwork, then shift into a montage, and finish up in a few minutes), plus they can add extra relationship points for Jason and Lucia. Fixing everything up will allow Jason and Lucia to get off on the right foot.

When you start the game and wander over to Jason's house as part of the story, you'll notice it's in disrepair. Jason will fix the roof a bit, but you can visit later to fully repair it and eliminate the leak.

This is one of the easiest spots to find since you'll just need to climb up a ladder near the front door (the front exit is near the bathroom). Do it early, before you forget.

The outdoor ground stairwell that leads up to the house is another spot that's easy to miss. Walk over to the stairwell closest to the mailbox and interact with it to fix it, grab new boards, and nail them in.

Lucia will be thankful when she returns from jail; it's one of the first things she'll notice about the house, and all the work Jason has done while off on his own.

The other bit you'll need to fix is at the top of the same stairwell outdoors. Before you go up another flight of small stairs into the house, check out the deck fence; it needs repairing.

Before you can really transform the room into something special, you'll need to put up drywall in your bedroom. Head over to your master bedroom (next to the wardrobe, connected to the main living room) and interact with the wall. You'll nail in some drywall and create a real living space.

After that, you can paint the room and complete your house transformation. You're almost there, and since the entire process takes around 15 minutes total, it's a small ask.

One of your last steps to painting the bedroom. Jason will grab a roller and start rolling, eventually transforming the entire bedroom into a nice little area. With all the repairs complete, the game gives you a quick congrats with an in-game message, and your heart meter rises.

This allows Lucia to be in good spirits after coming home. In fact, she was "crazy in love" with Jason, the third tier (of four) on the relationship meter. Doing this small task will help you make up for some relationship mistakes you'll eventually make in GTA 6.
