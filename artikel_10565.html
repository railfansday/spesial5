Fortnite responds to viral claim that Sprites make slur-like sound

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Fortnite has denied a viral claim that one of its Sprites makes a sound resembling a racial slur, after an X clip passed 2.7 million views.

The video, posted by X user crazyasffr on November 8, shows a player interacting with a Sprite while a noise plays that some viewers heard as the hard-R slur.

Sprites were introduced in Fortnite’s v41.00 update as small companions that can be found, equipped, and leveled up to provide special abilities. Epic describes them as “little helpers on the battlefield,” but has not publicly listed voice lines matching the sound in the viral clip.

Fortnite Status responded on November 9, writing: “This video is fake, this isn’t a sound Sprites make.” The account did not explain what had been edited or identify the source of the audio.

SPRITES CAN NOW SAY THE HARD R IN FORTNITE pic.twitter.com/Istx5XT27v

The original poster pushed back against Fortnite’s statement, claiming the effect was created by two separate sounds playing close together.

In a follow-up post, crazyasffr named one of the alleged files as “VO_S41_Sprite_Air_Item_Drop_01” and said they did not feel like searching for the second file. They later told Fortnite that “its 2 sounds” and suggested the game may need to remove one of the effects.

its 2 sounds you might want to remove one of the dropping ones

The posts do not identify the second sound file or provide a full recreation showing how the effects were combined. As a result, the explanation has not been independently verified, and it remains unclear whether the clip was edited, produced by overlapping effects, or caused by an in-game audio issue.

The debate also resurfaced an older TikTok posted by user zomqie on June 22. The clip appears to show another Sprite producing a similar vocal-like sound at a lower pitch, although the recording does not establish whether the noise is an intentional voice line or an edited audio effect.
