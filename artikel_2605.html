49ers extend G.M. John Lynch's contract

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In the offseason, the 49ers quietly extended the contract of coach Kyle Shanahan. His partner has now gotten a new deal, too.

Jay Glazer of Fox Sports reports that the 49ers and G.M. John Lynch have agreed to a contract extension. Lynch, who also serves as the team’s president of football operations, was hired in early 2017. The Hall of Fame safety previously had worked for Fox.

Glazer explains that Lynch’s deal was due to expire after the 2026 season. Last night, Lynch and owner Jed York struck a handshake deal on a new contract before the Week 11 win over the Vikings.

With Shanahan and Lynch working together, the 49ers have qualified for a pair of Super Bowls. They have contended far more often than not, qualifying for the postseason in 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023, and 2025.

Before Shanahan and Lynch arrived, the 49ers hadn’t been to the playoffs since 2013.

This year, the 9-1 49ers have the inside track to the No. 1 seed in the NFC playoffs. They arguably have an excellent chance to win the franchise’s first Super Bowl since 1994.
