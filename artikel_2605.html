Joey Loperfido removed from Houston Astros' 40-man roster, sent to Triple-A Sugar Land

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Houston Astros sent outfielder Joey Loperfido outright to Triple-A Sugar Land on Tuesday, the team announced, removing him from their 40-man roster.

Loperfido was Houston's opening day left fielder in 2026 but played in just 40 games due to injuries and demotions to the minors. He totaled a .214/.311/.330 slash line in 121 major-league plate appearances this season and spent most of the second half at Triple-A.

Last month, Loperfido had arthroscopic surgery on his right hip to address "a labral injury and hip impingement that were identified shortly after the season ended," the Astros said. The team said Loperfido's recovery would delay his availability for the start of spring training next year.

The Astros did not specify a timeline for Loperfido's recovery. Tuesday's transaction removed him from their 40-man roster, which is at 39 players. More moves could occur by Friday, the deadline for clubs to extend contract offers to players who are eligible for salary arbitration.

Loperfido, 27, owns a career .678 OPS in 162 major-league games. Drafted by the Astros, he was traded to the Toronto Blue Jays at the 2024 deadline and re-acquired last offseason in a deal for Jesús Sánchez. He remains in the Astros' system now as outfield depth.

Houston has a clear need to address its outfield this offseason. Astros outfielders combined to rank 29th of 30 major-league clubs in OPS in 2026, with Houston starting 12 different players in left field and eight different players in center.
