Cardinals extend TE Elijah Higgins for three-years

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

This is the time of year that in the NFL, if coaches and GMs want to keep players whose deals are about to expire, they begin doling out contract extensions. On Tuesday, the Arizona Cardinals gave TE Elijah Higgins a three-year $25 million extension that will keep him in-house through the 2028 season.

This new Cardinals offense requires two and three tight ends, and Higgins fit the bill.

The new deal has $17.087 million guaranteed plus a $1.5 million signing bonus. In addition, his 2028 salary has an injury guarantee of $6 million, workout bonuses, and annual per-game bonuses.

Higgins' (6'-3", 235 pounds) production this year isn't great. In fact, it isn't even good.

He has played in all 11 games with 7 starts, but his receiving numbers are pedestrian. He has 21 targets with 19 receptions for 166 yards, an 8.7 yards per catch average, has converted 8 first downs, scored a single TD, 0 rushes, 122 YAC numbers, and one drop. He has played on 319 snaps, or 44% of offensive plays. He also has 155 snaps on special teams.

His cap hit this year is $4.1 million. That comes out to be $213,668 per reception so far this season. Of course, that will increase beginning next year.

Currently, the Cardinals have Trey McBride, Higgins, Hunter Long, and Teagan Quitoriano in their tight end room. They also have Shawn Bowman and Kenny Yeboah on the practice squad and Tip Reiman on IR. This offense plays a lot of 12 and 13 personnel.

Higgins is a Stanford graduate and was selected in the sixth round of the 2023 NFL Draft by the Miami Dolphins. He was a last day cut to which Arizona then claimed him off waivers and has been a roster member ever since. He is very durable and played in all 17 games the past two seasons. He has 802 career receiving yards with 4 touchdowns.

Originally, Higgins was a wide receiver in college and then transitioned to tight end as he got bigger. He is quick and runs a 4.54 in the 40. He is just okay in in-line blocking but is willing, and is just average on contested balls. His forte is intermediate throws and is a physical runner once it's in his hands. Like McBride, Higgins has a large route tree.

The extension is a sign that the coaching staff is preparing for future seasons, and likes his play in the tight end group.

On November 15, 2026, the Cardinals posted Higgins was their "2026 Salute to Service Award" nominee.
