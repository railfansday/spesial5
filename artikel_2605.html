United-Republic playoff match postponed to Monday due to flight issues

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The USL Championship announced Sunday morning the Western Conference final between the New Mexico United and the Sacramento Republic FC will be postponed to Monday due to travel delays related to the Federal Aviation Administration's ground stop on Friday.

Because of multiple flight cancellations Friday and Saturday, Sacramento won't arrive in Albuquerque until Sunday evening. Originally scheduled for 5 p.m. Sunday, the league rescheduled the match for 7 p.m. Monday at Isotopes Park.

Sacramento Republic was supposed to arrive in Albuquerque Friday, but that plan changed when the FAA ordered a nationwide grounding that affected thousands of flights throughout the weekend. In a news release Sunday, the New Mexico United said the team suffered multiple flight cancellations Friday and Saturday morning before bussing to San Francisco in anticipation of a Saturday evening flight. However, that flight was canceled.

The team then chartered a flight into Albuquerque for Sunday.

The United announced that all tickets purchased for the match will be valid for Monday's rescheduling. Limited tickets are still available and can be purchased through SeatGeek.com. Also, the match will be aired on ESPN+.

The schedule change will not impact next week's USL Championship finals, which will be hosted by the United-Republic winner. The opponent will be Louisville City FC, which beat the Charleston Battery, 2-0, Saturday night.
