Better Than Ronaldo and Kane: Gareth Bale Named Man Utd Icon His Greatest Teammate

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Gareth Bale had a long and distinguished career, winning some of football's biggest prizes.

With an honours list as long as his - five UEFA Champions League winners' medals, three La Liga titles, three UEFA Super Cup wins, three Club World Cup triumphs, a Copa del Rey, a Supercopa de Espana and an English League Cup - he is certainly among those who can be considered to be among the greatest football players of all time.

It was not only domestically where he showed his class either, as he guided Wales to Euro 2016, Euro 202 and then the World Cup in 2022, with the nation reaching the semi-finals of the 2016 tournament.

Wales getting to the finals of big tournaments was unheard of, with their appearance at the 2022 World Cup the nation's first since 1958. The 2016 Euros was their first ever at the UEFA tournament, with Bale at the centre of the country managing to create history.

During his career he has shared a pitch with some extraordinary players. With Real Madrid he played alongside Ballon d'Or winners Cristiano Ronaldo and Luka Modric, with whom he also played for Tottenham Hotspur, where he also shared the grass with current Ballon d'Or winner Harry Kane.

So he has a big pool of players from which to choose the greatest teammate he has ever had.

Yet none of those three, who have won the biggest and best individual prize football has to offer are who he went for. Instead, he went with one of his countrymen, one of the greatest Premier League players of all time in Ryan Giggs.

Speaking in 2016, Bale told Foot Locker: "For me, the greatest player is Ryan Giggs. He was my hero growing up and is a legend in world football. It also helps he's a fellow Welshman."

In fairness to Bale, he had not played with Kane by then, with his stint with the Bayern Munich striker coming when he was getting towards the end of his career, when he went back on loan to Spurs from Real Madrid in 2020/21.

Incredibly, Bale and Giggs only shared the pitch on three occasions. They first played together in 2006/07 during a friendly against Brazil, before then playing together in European Championship qualifiers in 2007/08 against the Republic of Ireland - which they lost 1-0 - and San Marino - during which both Giggs and Bale scored in a 3-0 win.

Bale may not have played that often with his idol, but the Manchester United legend's career speaks for itself. He won the UEFA Champions League twice and the Premier League 13 times with United, with a UEFA Super Cup and Club World Cup and four FA Cups and four League Cups thrown in there as well, making him one of the most decorated players in football history.

The admiration between the pair was mutual and when Giggs was Bale's manager in the Wales national team, he said: "To win four, to go to Real Madrid, and be so successful, he's got to be [the best].

"He's the greatest I've seen. Obviously I didn't see John Charles play and many others around that time but he's the greatest I've seen.

"Great players turn up at the right moments and make the difference, and he's done it twice now in Champions League finals."

United face a fast decision as Pochettino weighs squad fit and a Brazilian star with real Premier instant impact.
