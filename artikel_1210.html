Why Juventus replaced graphics with children’s drawings during 4-0 Venezia win

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Sharp-eyed supporters may have noticed that Juventus replaced their line-up graphics with a series of portraits drawn by local children ahead of and during their 4-0 victory at home against Venezia. Here is the reason behind the initiative.

Juventus were taking part in several charitable schemes over the weekend: The club took part in World Children’s Day celebrations on Friday, November 20, inviting children from the Punto Luce centre in the Vallette district of Turin to the training ground.

🤩 #SerieA : La compo de la Juve présentée avec des portraits dessinés par des enfants sur l’écran géant de l’Allianz Stadium !

Ce midi, les petites mains avaient leur place dans la cour des grands. 🤍🖤

Juve also launched a competition named ‘draw my idol’, inviting Junior members, children from the Punto Luce centre, youngsters from the youth academy and children of club employees to draw pictures of their favourite player from the men’s or women’s first team.

A selection of the submissions were displayed on the big screens and on social media in place of the regular line-up graphics for Sunday’s 4-0 victory at home against Venezia.

These events were part of a partnership between Juventus and Save the Children Italy.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
