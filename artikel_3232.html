Anthropic shares jump 34% at open in record Nasdaq debut

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Claude maker opened at $111.50, a bigger first-day pop than SpaceX got in June.

Anthropic’s shares opened at $111.50 on Nasdaq on Thursday, 34% above the $83 IPO price, valuing it at more than $2T, per Business Insider. The company raised $107.3B with its IPO and a private placement, the largest listing on record.

Anthropic’s shares opened at $111.50 on Nasdaq on Thursday, 34% above their $83 IPO price, Business Insider reported. The jump, bigger than the 27% grey markets had pointed to, valued the Claude maker at more than $2T. The stock trades under the ticker ANT.

Dario and Daniela Amodei rang the opening bell with staff earlier in the day. By midday in New York, the shares had eased to about $107, still up nearly 29%, according to Bloomberg data.

The open beat SpaceX’s debut in June. SpaceX, which priced its IPO at $135 a share, opened 11% higher and closed its first day up 19%.

Anthropic raised about $81B by selling shares at $83 on Wednesday, above its $70 to $78 range. It is also raising $26B from a private placement at the same price, with Alphabet, Microsoft and Nvidia among the buyers. Together, that makes $107.3B, the biggest IPO on record.

The debut lifted the wider market, with the Nasdaq Composite up more than 1% after the open. As the first big pure-play AI stock, Anthropic is likely to be watched as a test of investors’ appetite for AI.

Not everyone is convinced. In October, research firm New Constructs said Anthropic might be worth as little as $144B. On Wednesday, a group of scientists signed a statement calling it “not a safe AI company”.
