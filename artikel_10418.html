New OpenAI and Anthropic models sound alarms on safety and control, report

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Frontier AI models that have yet to be released by OpenAI and Anthropic are reportedly reaching the point where employees believe they cannot be safely released or controlled, employees reportedly say.

Axios cites “more than two dozen” employees inside of OpenAI and Anthropic who are sounding warnings around unreleased models from both companies.

While both OpenAI’s GPT and Anthropic’s Claude public models are increasingly powerful, these employees say that new models behind the scenes are starting to beat out the technical solutions for controlling them. This has led to debates between those who think they’ll be able to fix these problems, and others who believe companies should stop training new models altogether due to the risks.

If we continue trying to make models much smarter, such that they’re actually smarter than us … they will in fact be able to trick us successfully. And once you get to that point, many of the schemes that we’ve had at work up until now sort of go out the window.

The report goes out to point out that some researchers feel that executives at major AI companies have become “out of touch” with the severity of safety risks, though Axios was told that OpenAI is adopting new safety practices while Anthropic said that “Keeping a model internal doesn’t make it safe,” adding that “we may slow down or hold back even when it costs us.”
