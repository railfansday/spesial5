Personal Consumer Issues • Re: Costco Enterprise Car Rental

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

I have almost never beaten the price that Costco gives, and I am top tier at three different rental companies. I don't know how Costco does it, but they almost always win.
I seem the be able to beat Costco’s deals about half the time, although the competition usually does not include a 2nd driver. Yesterday my non Costco 17 day rental cost $482, while the best deal at Costco was $649. Also, I checked Costco several times starting about 8 weeks in advance and the same rental varied from $649 to about $1150.
I started using Costco car rental a couple of years ago. It just seemed to be easy. Also allows for my wife and I both to drive the vehicle with no extra charges.

Price wise it is usually fairly good. There are places like Autoslash (I think that is the site) which will look for better pricing if you don't want to use Costco.

Generally if pricing is close I will go with National for several reasons including the fact that I'm Emerald status with them.

And yeah, car rental pricing can be all over the map, usually because of good reasons (major tourist event going on).
