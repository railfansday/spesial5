Todd Chrisley Slams Negative Comments of Pregnant Daughter Savannah's Body

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Todd Chrisley has a pointed message for any critics of his pregnant daughter Savannah Chrisley’s body.

“I [went] with Savannah and Jason [Galanis, her husband] to Mar-A-Lago for an event,” Todd, 57, said on the Wednesday, November 11, episode of his “Chrisley Confessions 2.0” podcast. “She looked so pretty, and she was so nervous. She was like, ‘I’m big, I’m big.’”

He continued, “To all y’all hating bitches out there that was saying negative stuff about her gaining weight in her face, I hope you feel good now to know that you were shaming a pregnant woman. That’s what you get for running that mouth without knowing what’s going on.”

Savannah, 29, announced in October that she is expecting her first baby with Galanis, 56, whom she has been dating since July 2025. (Galanis proposed in December 2025, months before Savannah found out she’s pregnant. They secretly wed in the Cayman Islands in July.)

Before revealing her pregnancy news, Savannah attended Mar-A-Lago’s annual Halloween gala with her father and her husband.

“She was like, ‘I just feel so fat.’ And I said, ‘You are gorgeous. You look stunning,’” Todd recalled. “And you know, when the designer was working on her gown, she was kind of, like, thinking, ‘I’m going to wear something that’s going to disguise.’ I said, ‘Don’t disguise your baby.’ I said, ‘Embrace your baby, embrace your bump. You didn’t know you were going to have one [and] you worried about not having one, embrace your bump.’”

In the end, Todd said Savannah was “so grateful” to wear a bump-baring green gown with butterfly decorations across the midsection.

“She looked at me and smiled, and it was just a wonderful moment when they were dressing her and they were placing the butterflies,” Todd tearfully told his wife, Julie Chrisley. “Someone walked in and they said, ‘You are gorgeous.’ And she looked at me and her eyes filled up with tears and she says, ‘I’m going to be fine. I’m good.’ And she says, ‘And you were right about showing her bump.’”

As Savannah is embracing her journey to motherhood, her parents are ready to become grandparents again. (Todd’s children from his first marriage, Lindsie and Kyle, are both biological parents. Todd and Julie adopted of Kyle’s daughter, Chloe, while Lindsie and son Jackson are estranged from her family.)

“I am so excited to watch Savannah become a mother because she’s been a mother ever since Grayson came home from the hospital,” Todd gushed on Wednesday, referring to his 20-year-old son. “That bond that those two share is unbreakable, and to watch how she’s going to be with her own son, I already see a change in her. I see how she’s so focused on making sure her house is put together the right way and making sure that she’s doing the right things.”
