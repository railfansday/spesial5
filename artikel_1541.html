Brest: Julien Lachuer pushes for Ludovic Ajorque in the French team

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Author of a goal during Brest’s victory against Lorient (2-0) this Monday evening, the 32-year-old striker received strong support from his coach Julien Lachuer. Asked by League 1+ on a possible call-up to the Blues. The technician did not hesitate to put his player forward and present him as a credible option for Zinédine Zidane, as he had already done a few weeks ago.

“We have very great players in France, Ludo is one of them”launched Lachuer, before emphasizing the collective qualities of his center forward: “He is a player who makes the team better, he has this knowledge of the game, this sense of the collective. » Convinced that his attacker would know how to respond to a possible request, he even concluded: “If he needs him, he will respond. » With four goals and two assists in nine Ligue 1 games this season, Ajorque, who has no caps for the French team, perhaps hopes to attract the attention of the new coach.
