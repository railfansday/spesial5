PSG – ESTAC: probable line-ups

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

After its demonstration on the Villarreal field in the middle of the week, PSG returns to business as usual. A week after crushing Le Havre at home, the leader of the French championship faces another student from the back of the class. It’s Troyes’ turn to come to the Parc des Princes for a meeting which looks unbalanced on paper, despite the numerous absences on the Parisian side. Achraf Hakimi is left to rest while Khvicha Kvaratskhelia treats his knee. Ousmane Dembélé, Marquinhos and Nuno Mendes are in the recovery phase. Maghnes Akliouche is hit in the calf.

This leaves a lot of uncertainty around Luis Enrique’s starting lineup. One thing is certain, he will line up a very overhauled team this evening (9:05 p.m.) against the 16th in Ligue 1. Safonov will be there, but he will have in front of him a defense made up of Zaire-Emery in the right lane, rather than the young David Boly, despite having had a brilliant week (first appearance of the season at Le Havre, first goal of his career at Villarreal). He will undoubtedly come into play during the meeting. To support the captain for the evening, Zabarnyi could be associated with Lucas Hernandez in the hinge. Lucas Digne is expected to start on the left side.

In midfield too, the coach promises change. Two of the three starters are on track to start on the bench before the international break. The young Dro Fernandez would enter the eleven, just like the very young dad Lucas Beraldo. Fabian Ruiz is expected to continue, four days after his performance at Villarreal. We should expect a bit of turnover up front with Ferran Torres on the right flank, he who has not scored since September 20, before the last international break. Mika Godts would play on the other side to leave the lead to Senny Mayulu, back in shape and in a state of grace.

It’s up to the promoted to try to take advantage of this reshuffled team to bring something back from the capital. Stéphane Dumont should present himself with a 4-3-3 where Konaté would confirm his starting place in goal acquired two days ago. Maronnier on the right and Boura on the left should occupy the sides of the defense to surround the pair composed of Monfray, the captain, and Diaz. In the midfield, Antoine Mille, revelation at the start of the season, would be associated with Karim and Diop, in order to supply their offensive teammates with balls. Ifnaoui would start in the right lane, while the experienced Ripart would take the center. Diawara would be preferred to Nkada to start on the left.
