GdS: Cardinale, the Yormarks and Jay Z – the alternative route Milan are taking for Endrick

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

AC Milan are desperate to sign Endrick in the January mercato and there could be unconventional routes used.

Signing a player like Endrick in January is never easy, especially when his manager, Jose Mourinho, is completely against a sale.

Nevertheless, one cannot be ruled out. There have already been hints that the Portuguese may not have much of a say in the deal, with Milan prepared to offer a significant fee in the winter.

That’s not the only thing that they’re willing to do, too. Instead, connections could be utilised.

According to Gazzetta dello Sport, there would still be opposition from Mourinho in his sale. However, other routes could help the Brazilian move to San Siro.

Indeed, the Rossoneri could use these ‘alternative routes to reach places where it might be difficult to operate using the usual dynamics’. Of course, this links to the club’s excellent relationship with Roc Nation, who represent the youngster.

Gerry Cardinale is close to the agency, after working on several projects, and Brett Yormark – the new CEO of MSE – used to work for the company and still has ties through his brother.

The ties between Endrick and the Diavolo, whilst not completed, are already well-established. It’s just a case of whether they can be sewn into a checkered flag now.
