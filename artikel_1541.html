Hamraoui affair: Aminata Diallo referred to justice

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

As reported byAFP this Friday, former PSG player Aminata Diallo was referred to the Versailles criminal court for complicity in violence and criminal conspiracy in the attack on her former teammate Kheira Hamraoui, which occurred in November 2021. Aged 31, she will have to answer for her alleged involvement in this affair which had greatly shaken French football.

Six other people are also sent to correctional facilities, including César Macavala, sports advisor to several Parisian players, prosecuted for illegal exercise of the activity of sports agent. This new legal step comes after the Versailles prosecutor’s office requested a trial against Aminata Diallo in December 2025.
