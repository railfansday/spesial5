Beckham tries to recruit Dybala to Inter Miami

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Paulo Dybala’s future may lie far from Roma and Serie A.

The Argentine striker, currently sidelined with a muscle injury, has reportedly been targeted by two Major League Soccer clubs.

Among the most likely destinations is David Beckham’s Inter Miami, a club that also hosts Lionel Messi and Rodrigo De Paul.

This prospect could open up new horizons for the Giallorossi’s number 21, whose contract remains a key issue in the coming months.

According to Radio Manà Manà, Beckham has already initiated initial contact with Dybala’s entourage to gauge the player’s willingness to move to the United States at the end of the season.

The prospect of joining Messi in Florida would not be unwelcome to the Argentine, who may be considering a new professional experience after his years in the Italian league. New York City is also said to be pursuing him.
