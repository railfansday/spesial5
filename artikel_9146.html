South London Gallery Director Margot Heller to Step Down After 25 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Margot Heller, the director of the South London Gallery (SLG), is stepping down after 25 years in the role. She will leave the institution in spring 2027 to work as an independent curator and adviser.

Heller joined the gallery in 2001 and has since tripled footprint and increased annual visitor figures tenfold. Under her tenure, artists like Christian Boltanski, Steve McQueen, Eva Rothschild, Rashid Johnson, Simeon Barclay, and others have staged exhibitions at SLG.

In a statement, Heller said it’s been a “privilege to lead the physical and organizational expansion of the SLG over the years, and to work with an exceptional staff team and brilliant board of trustees. I have relished programming and curating countless exhibitions in the beautiful gallery spaces. Heller’s impact is most noticeable with regards to SLG’s physical presence. In 2010, she worked with 6a Architects to introduce new gallery spaces, a café, an apartment for an artist in residence, and a new education building. SLG continued to grow after that expansion, adding the Orozco Garden in 2016 and converting a former fire station into an annex gallery space in 2018.

She also launched its Post Graduate Artist in Residence Programme, which ran from 2011 to 2024 and in 2010, established the Louis Vuitton Young Arts Project , a three-year arts and education partnership with Tate, Whitechapel Gallery, the Royal Academy, and Hayward Gallery.

Serge Ramin, chair of the SLG’s board of trustees, said in a statement that Heller’s impact is “immeasurable,” applauding her for being a “visionary curator and cultural leader.” SLG said that it will be working closely with Heller on ensuring a “smooth transition and to build on the foundations she has laid” as the search for a new director begins.
