Cisse reacts to matching Shevchenko stat and insists winning matters more than scoring

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Alphadjo Cisse is happy to keep taking the opportunities that are coming his way in what is fast becoming a brilliant debut season with AC Milan.

The fantastic start to life as a Milan player continued for Cisse at the Stadio Luigi Ferraris today, as he was on hand to tuck in Adrien Rabiot’s cut-back to get his eighth goal of the season across all competitions. Not long after that, Christian Pulisic doubled the lead for the Rossoneri.

Ellertsson’s goal in the final few minutes made it a tense finish, but Milan did hang on in the end for a win that was needed after the defeat against Ferencvaros on Thursday night. While waiting for the result of Inter-Como, Milan could go into the break in the top four.

Cisse spoke to DAZN following the conclusion of the game at the Marassi and he offered his thoughts on another positive 90 minutes personally and for the team. MilanNews relayed his comments.

“We have to try to figure out what’s wrong. Today we came with the right attitude and brought home the three points.”

Did you know that Shevchenko scored eight goals in his first 13 games for AC Milan? Like you have…

“There’s no need to comment (laughs). I try to seize the opportunities the coach gives me. Whether I score or not, the important thing is to win. And if a goal comes, I’m even happier.”

“I’m very happy. I want to demonstrate my skills and prove that I can compete. I’ll give it my all, as always.”
