Girls' Frontline: The Lunasia Covenant reveals 2027 release date in new trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

While the big news this week is undoubtedly the launch of GTA VI, don't let that distract you from some equally exciting reveals for mobile. Most notably, Girls' Frontline: Blue Butterfly Contract, now known as Girls' Frontline: The Lunasia Covenant, has finally revealed a release date for 2027!

The Lunasia Covenant release reveal was made in the latest trailer, which also gave us some glimpses of what to expect from the Girls' Frontline spinoff. Third-person, over-the-shoulder combat, and many recognisable characters and weapons are all present, as are stylish graphics in the vein of something like Arknights: Endfield. But Lunasia Covenant is undoubtedly a Girls' Frontline game at its core.

I've seen some people comparing The Lunasia Covenant to something like Helldivers, and while that may be stretching it a bit, it does show how excited people are to see Girls' Frontline do something new. And truth be told, a third-person shooter is likely to attract sceptics who might otherwise give it a miss.

As for when exactly we can expect it, the 2027 release window is an impressively broad one. I'd hazard a guess that we'd see it no earlier than summer, or maybe even later, as this'll be a massive undertaking for the MICA Team, and a gamble they'll want to get right.
