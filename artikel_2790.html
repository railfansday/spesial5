Mike McCarthy explains ‘ultimatum’ behind decision to bench Aaron Rodgers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Mike McCarthy said the decision to bench Aaron Rodgers for Friday's game against the Broncos was his — after the future Hall of Fame quarterback issued an "ultimatum" to the Steelers head coach.

The decision that came down late Tuesday night kicked off a wild day in Steelers country, which included Rodgers making bombshell accusations against McCarthy in a nationally broadcast interview with Pat McAfee and ended with Pittsburgh releasing the veteran QB.

Aaron Rodgers #8 of the Pittsburgh Steelers and Head Coach Mike McCarthy of the Pittsburgh Steelers chat against the Philadelphia Eagles prior to an NFL football game at Lincoln Financial Field on November 22, 2026 in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Getty Images

"It was a process that went on throughout this week and we got to a point, we talked about some things – communication and things like that," McCarthy told reporters Wednesday evening. "There was an ultimatum made clearly outside of his job description, and so it was my decision to bench him."

McCarthy declined to expand on what exactly the alleged ultimatum was, describing it as "private conversations."

During the lengthy interview with McAfee on ESPN on Wednesday, Rodgers claimed he told McCarthy there needed to be a change for the "betterment of the team."

"I just said [to McCarthy], 'Listen, I think there's got to be a change,'" Rodgers said. "'You don't want me to call it because you don't think I'm going to call enough runs? Fine, let somebody else call it, that's what's in the best interest of the team.'"

Rodgers made a number of damning allegations against McCarthy, including that he had been sleeping through meetings and that he called the team the "Packers" instead of the "Steelers" as he was breaking them down at halftime last weekend.

Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers (8) checks with head coach Mike McCarthy after calling a timeout. USA TODAY Network via Reuters Connect

McCarthy did not deny the allegation about mixing up the Packers and Steelers, calling the situation last Sunday in Philadelphia – when the mix-up allegedly happened – "very emotional" and saying it was an "embarrassing moment."

Rodgers also claimed McCarthy still holds a grudge against him for McCarthy's midseason firing in 2018 when he was coaching the Packers.

Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers (8) reacts after the game at Lincoln Financial Field. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

"Yeah, that's not accurate," McCarthy told reporters on Wednesday evening in response to a question about the claim.

And when he was asked if he still feels that players have faith in him, he answered affirmatively and said he had just come out of a meeting with team leadership.

"I thought that it was a meeting [that was] very direct, very honest," he said. "I appreciated the opportunity to talk through numerous things that have been on their mind. Comments that brought discussion to the forefront, so I'm proud of our leadership."
