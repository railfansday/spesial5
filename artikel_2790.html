Missouri football flips four-star running back from Florida State

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Missouri football now has the commitment of four-star running back Jayden Miles, getting a Thanksgiving Day pledge for its offense from the former Florida State commit. The early signing period for 2027 recruits begins Wednesday, Dec. 2.

Florida State fired coach Mike Norvell on Nov. 1 and recently announced Georgia defensive coordinator Glenn Schumann will take over the program next season. Miles, who is from Baton Rouge, Louisiana, and is listed at 6-foot-2 and 203 pounds, visited Columbia for the Tigers' game against Kentucky on Nov. 21. He had been committed to FSU since June.

Miles is ranked as the No. 402 prospect in the Class of 2027 and the No. 27 running back. He is the second running back to commit to Mizzou's next recruiting class, following fellow four-star prospect Kingston Miles out of St. Mary's in St. Louis.

Mizzou now has 19 players committed in its 2027 recruiting class. According to 247Sports' composite rankings, the Tigers have the nation's No. 31-ranked class after the addition of Miles.

Running back is an interesting position for Missouri in 2027, as the Tigers can return both Jamal Roberts and Ahmad Hardy.

Roberts, who is a senior, may also have an opportunity to turn pro. Hardy, who was shot in the thigh in May and has only recently returned to game action in a limited capacity, is more likely to return to college than declare for the draft, but it's unclear whether or not the former All-American will be able to lead MU's rushing workload as he continues to recover.

Mizzou also can return Malae Fonoti, who sustained an ACL tear in the Tigers' season-opening game this season, and Xai'Shaun Edwards, who has been a backup. Preston Hatfield has emerged as MU's third running back as a rookie, and Mizzou also has true freshman Max Warner on the roster.

Depending on the future decisions of Roberts and Hardy, the Tigers may look to add a RB1 candidate out of the transfer portal when it opens in January.
