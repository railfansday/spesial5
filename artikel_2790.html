Lakers share update on Austin Reaves’ injury: a ‘pain tolerance’ issue

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Lakers coach JJ Redick gave an update on Austin Reaves' left heel bruise before the team played the Suns on Sunday.

"He's improving," Redick said. "It really comes down to just like a pain tolerance thing. Again, nothing structural beyond a bruise. And so I think every day it's been better, and we'll see where he's at in 48 hours when we play on Tuesday."

Austin Reaves #15 of the Los Angeles Lakers shoots a free throw against the Milwaukee Bucks on Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, at Fiserv Forum in Milwaukee. NBAE via Getty Images

Reaves suffered the injury in the third quarter of the Lakers' 123-112 win over the Bucks on Wednesday. After subbing himself out of the game, Reaves had X-rays that were clean.

He was expected to get an MRI when the Lakers returned to Los Angeles on Thursday, but the team's medical staff and Reaves jointly determined that he didn't need further imaging after seeing how he felt that morning.

Los Angeles Lakers' Austin Reaves (15) dunks between Jaime Jaquez Jr. and Kyle Kuzma during an NBA basketball game, Thursday, Nov. 12, 2026, in Milwaukee. AP Photo/Aaron Gash

Reaves is averaging 24.7 points (51.3% from the field, 40.9% from beyond the arc), 3.3 rebounds and 4.7 assists per game for the Lakers, who entered Sunday's game tied for the best record in the league (11-2).

Reaves is the 15th-leading scorer in the league this season.

Of the players ahead of him, only Anthony Davis, Jamal Murray, Victor Wembanyama and Giannis Antetokounmpo are shooting as high of a field goal percentage. And only Murray and Steph Curry are ahead of him in 3-point percentage.

Reaves signed a four-year, $180 million contract with the Lakers this summer, the richest contract in NBA history for a player who went undrafted. He has been instrumental to the Lakers' hot start. Entering Sunday's game, the Lakers had won seven straight contests.

Los Angeles Lakers' Austin Reaves drives against Milwaukee Bucks' Nate Ament on Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, in Milwaukee. AP Photo/Aaron Gash

Reaves was ruled out Saturday for Sunday's game. He's day-to-day ahead of Tuesday's contest against the Hornets.

Because of a favorable break in the schedule, which included three days without games, Reaves will have six days of rest and he will have only missed one game by Tuesday.
