Kenneth Walker limited in his return to Chiefs practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Chiefs head coach Andy Reid said he expected running back Kenneth Walker back at practice on Tuesday and the team's injury report confirmed that Walker was a limited participant.

Walker has missed the last two games with a groin injury, but his return to work would seem to open the door to a return to the lineup against the Bills on Thursday night.

Walker ran 159 times for 872 yards and seven touchdowns in eight games before the injury. He also has 26 catches for 172 yards and four touchdowns.

Tight end Noah Gray also returned to practice which left right tackle Jaylon Moore (ankle) was the only Chief out of practice. Defensive tackle Omarr Norman-Lott (knee) was listed as limited while Gray (rib), cornerback Mansoor Delane (shoulder), wide receiver Rashee Rice (knee), right guard Trey Smith (concussion), and defensive lineman Peter Woods (neck) were full participants.
