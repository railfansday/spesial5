Saints player fined for hip-drop tackle vs. Panthers despite no flag during the game

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Just one Saints player was fined in Week 10's 20-17 loss to the Panthers despite a whopping SEVENTEEN penalties called on the Panthers during the game. Vernon Broughton owes the NFL $8,381 for a hip-drop tackle against Carolina running back Chuba Hubbard.

Broughton was not flagged for the violation during the game. Hubbard was a problem for the Saints all day long, racking up 92 rushing yards and two rushing touchdowns, along with 80 receiving yards from five catches on five targets.

The money the NFL collects from players' game checks to cover fines is donated, with funds split between the NFL Foundation to support the health, safety and wellness of athletes at all levels of football, and the Professional Athletes Foundation to support NFL Legends in need.

Chris Olave, $11,941: Unsportsmanlike conduct, taunting, Week 2

Danny Stutsman, $7,011: Unnecessary roughness, full body weight on a QB, Week 3

Isaiah Stalbird, $5,972: Unnecessary roughness, use of the helmet, Week 4

Vernon Broughton, $8,381: Hit on the QB, blow to the head/neck, Week 6

Christen Miller, $11,915: Unsportsmanlike conduct, violent gesture, Week 6

Chase Young, $14,926: Unsportsmanlike conduct, violent gesture, Week 6

Chris Rumph, $11,111: Unsportsmanlike conduct, taunting, Week 7

Vernon Broughton, $8,381: Unnecessary roughness, hip-drop tackle, Week 10
