Brock Bowers returns to limited participation Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Raiders tight end Brock Bowers returned to limited participation on Thursday. His ankle and quadriceps injuries kept him sidelined on Wednesday.

Bowers left Sunday's game after only 11 snaps with his ankle injury.

He has been on the injury report most of the season with hamstring, calf and knee issues.

Bowers leads the Raiders with 50 catches, 676 receiving yards and six receiving touchdowns this season.

Defensive tackle Folorunso Fatukasi (toe) and kicker Matt Gay (hip) did not practice Thursday after being limited Wednesday. The Raiders worked out three kickers Thursday.

Tight end Ian Thomas (calf) was downgraded to a limited participant after being a full participant Wednesday.

The Raiders added offensive guard Trey Zuhn (illness) to the practice report as a limited participant.

Defensive tackle Tonka Hemingway (stinger) was again a non-participant, and defensive tackle Thomas Booker (ankle) remained a full participant.
