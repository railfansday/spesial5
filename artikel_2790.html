Mike McCarthy Makes Eye-Opening Admission after Steelers’ Win

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

PITTSBURGH — Pittsburgh Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy admitted after the Steelers' 27-5 win over the Denver Broncos on Friday that he needs to spend more time with the defense. That was one of the points that Aaron Rodgers made in his tirade on The Pat McAfee Show Wednesday. He said McCarthy doesn't care to get to know the defensive players.

"I have not spent enough time with the defense," McCarthy said. "And that's something that, as far as job responsibility, job description, these are things that will be looked at in the offseason. It's a point in our year where we're making adjustments and definitely conscientious of it. But at the end of the day, we have to all do our jobs and giving the players the plan and all the other things they need."

Steelers outside linebacker T.J. Watt said McCarthy has opened up to the defense.

"Mike just admitted that he has got to spend some more time with the defense. We've had a lot of really good conversations, especially recently, and look forward to continuing to build off of what you saw tonight," Watt said.

McCarthy also touched on the game being a players' game. McCarthy said he had a great meeting with the leadership council during the tumultuous week.

"There's a lot going on anytime you're going through a situation where time's taken away from winning, and your team responds. It is extremely important and gratifying. But the reality of this game, and I know it'll always be as far as my approach, this is a players' game," McCarthy said. "You know, we have a very important role in this sport, but you have to make sure you keep it about the players."

The defense played lights out in Friday's win over Denver.

Denver mustered only 205 yards of offense. The five points scored by Denver were the fewest since Sean Payton became the team's head coach in 2023.

The Steelers had two defensive touchdowns in the first half. Mason Rudolph completed a 65-yarder to Germie Bernard and a 22-yard touchdown to DK Metcalf. The halftime score of 24-5 was also a first in NFL history.

The Steelers are the only team in the NFL with two defensive touchdowns in a game this year.

The Steelers also became the first team in 2026 to score both a fumble return touchdown and an interception return touchdown in the same game.

Both came in the first half against Denver, marking the first time Pittsburgh has recorded both defensive scores in the same half since against the San Diego Chargers on Oct. 1, 1995, when cornerbacks Willie Williams and Alvoid Mays each returned interceptions for touchdowns in a 31–16 victory.

Alan Saunders and Brendan Howe provided reporting from Pittsburgh.
