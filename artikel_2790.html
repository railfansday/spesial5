Bears stun Lions on Thanksgiving with Rome Odunze last-second catch

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

End zones in the NFL are 10 yards deep and 53 ⅓ yards wide. And Rome Odunze needed every last inch to score the game-winning touchdown on Thanksgiving.

The Chicago Bears receiver capped off a thrilling comeback win over the Detroit Lions at Ford Field, gobbling up the pass from Caleb Williams in the back corner of the end zone with just three seconds left. It was going to be a touchdown or overtime at that point, but Odunze seemingly didn't want to be late for dinner.

Odunze was quiet in the first half, catching just one pass. As the old saying goes, it's not about how you start, it's how you finish.

The receiver finished the game with six catches for 87 yards and two touchdowns, both coming in the fourth quarter as Chicago overcame the 11-point deficit it faced with just over eight minutes left in the contest.

The Bears took over first place in the NFC North, improving to 9-2 with the win. The Lions now fall to second at 8-3.

For Bears coach Ben Johnson, it was his first win against the Lions – his former team. Johnson's team was outscored 71-35 in the two previous matchups that took place last season. And he wasn't the only one that finally got one in the win column against the Lions.

Williams is now 1-4 against the Detroit and it couldn't have come at a better time.

Whether this propels the Bears toward a deep playoff run remains to be seen. At the very least, Odunze and Williams etched their names into NFL Thanksgiving history forever.
