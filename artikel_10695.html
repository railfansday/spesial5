Professor cancels homework and exams during GTA 6 launch week

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A professor in Spain cleared his class’s calendar for GTA 6‘s launch week, and the email explaining why quickly went viral.

The gesture landed while grown-ups were already plotting their own escapes from the hotly anticipated release, with one economist predicting 1.5 million Americans could skip work on November 19.

Brands piled in, too, as Xbox enlisted DJ Khaled to hand out doctor’s notes for launch day and KFC lined up a fake funeral as an excuse to stay home.

His students, however, had someone in their corner who’d spent the week before quietly clearing their schedule for launch.

In an email written in Spanish and shared on X on November 16, the professor told his students they wouldn’t have any homework, projects or deadlines in his subject during GTA 6’s launch week.

On top of that, he pushed an exam back from the 23rd to the 30th “so you can organize yourselves better and have more free time during those days.”

“When GTA V came out in 2013, I was your age,” he explained, recalling how badly he’d wanted to get home to play and how it was all he and his friends talked about.

The surprise came with a catch, though. He apologized for the heavier workload the week before, explaining he had kept the class toiling precisely so they could get ahead on the syllabus, and warned they’d have to buckle down again once launch week was over.

He also urged them to “enjoy responsibly,” adding that some things in the game weren’t quite appropriate for their age, with GTA 6’s age ratings already flagging sex, drugs and graphic violence.

“I had my moment with GTA V, and I was excited for you to have yours with GTA VI,” he wrote near the end. The email couldn’t be independently verified, though it spread quickly after accounts including content creator ElBuni shared it.

He isn’t the only one clearing the decks with the launch barreling toward November 19, either, after a car company announced back in June that it would pause operations for the day so its entire staff could play.
