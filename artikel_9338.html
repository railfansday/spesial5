Thaddaeus Washington: A Music-Based Meditation for Rest & Relaxation

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The most trusted resource for mindfulness and meditation.

Teacher and founder of MIND Music Thaddaeus Washington leads a music-based guided meditation to help you rest your mind, body, and heart.

Our guest this week is Thaddaeus Washington, Mindful’s featured Teacher of the Month for October.

He brings a new experience to our meditation lineup: a music-based meditation entitled “Vapour,” where sound is the anchor for our attention. Music is the main experience in this practice, with exposition only in the first few minutes. You’re invited to simply rest, bring your whole self, and allow the music to unwind the worries of the day.

Learn more about Thaddaeus, his music, his work, and his journey with mindfulness in his featured essay here.

Read and practice the guided meditation script below, pausing after each paragraph. Or listen to the audio practice.

Thaddaeus Washington is a musician, singer, songwriter, and founder of Mind Music—a company dedicated to making mindfulness accessible through music and immersive experiences.
