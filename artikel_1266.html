Sir Alex Ferguson Named 57-Cap Icon as ‘Best English Player Since Bobby Charlton’

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

When it comes to judging the quality of footballers, few people are better placed to offer an informed opinion than Sir Alex Ferguson. The legendary former Manchester United manager is widely considered one of the greatest coaches in modern football history, and there are plenty of reasons to support that reputation.

During his remarkable career, Ferguson collected 13 Premier League titles, more than any other manager in the competition's history. He also secured two Champions League crowns, numerous additional trophies at Old Trafford and European silverware during his time with Aberdeen. His achievements firmly establish him among football's most successful and respected figures.

Consequently, Ferguson's assessments of players have always carried considerable weight. One particularly interesting example emerged when he revealed that a footballer outside Manchester United was, in his opinion, the finest English talent to emerge since Sir Bobby Charlton.

Ferguson made the admission when discussing the player he would most have liked to work with during his managerial career. He identified five players in total, although one name stood out above the rest: Paul Gascoigne. Explaining his admiration, Ferguson said:

“Paul Gascoigne. Without question, I think he was the best English player since Bobby Charlton. He was a fantastic player. Unfortunately, we didn’t get him and I think looking back now, he made a big mistake.”

Ferguson also believed Gascoigne could have flourished in the Manchester United dressing room, suggesting the club had the right personalities to help him settle. He explained: "He recognised himself years later, but we had Geordies in the camp.

"We had Bobby Charlton, Bryan Robson, Steve Bruce, even Gary Pallister from Middlesbrough. We had people there that would have taken care of him, particularly Bryan Robson. I loved his strength."

The praise Ferguson reserved for Gascoigne becomes even more significant when considering his previous assessment of Charlton. The former United manager had already described the Manchester United great as the greatest English footballer of all time. Following Charlton's passing, Ferguson paid tribute to his remarkable legacy:

“It’s no surprise to me that we’ve seen tributes to Sir Bobby from everywhere in the world, on every TV channel and in every newspaper, because he was without question the greatest English player of all time.

“People loved him because of all those thunderbolt goals but it was more than that. My dad used to say that humility in success is a sign of greatness, and that was Bobby. He never used to boast about his own achievements; it was always about the team and the club.”

Across his international career, Gascoigne earned 57 England caps and found the net 10 times. He also represented several prominent clubs, including Newcastle United, Tottenham, Lazio and Rangers. Although his trophy collection and career achievements did not match Charlton's, his natural ability and technical brilliance placed him among England's most gifted players.

Gascoigne later reflected on his encounter with Ferguson after turning down the opportunity to join Manchester United, recalling: "I honestly just looked at him and the hairs on the back of my neck stood up." He subsequently experienced Ferguson's famous hairdryer treatment, offering him a glimpse of the intense environment he might have encountered had he moved to Old Trafford.
