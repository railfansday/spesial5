Champions League: Man City vs. Napoli – Probable line-ups and where to watch on TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Kevin De Bruyne is set to start for Napoli when they take on his former club Manchester City at the Etihad Stadium in the Champions League on Tuesday evening, as Massimiliano Allegri prepares to go without 11 first-team players in England.

Napoli are under pressure in the Champions League having lost three and won just one of their first four matches in the competition in 2026-27. The Partenopei have been beaten by Arsenal, Villarreal and Porto so far, with their only victory coming at home against Bodo/Glimt last month.

They find themselves in 27th place on Tuesday morning, currently outside the play-off round spots. City, meanwhile, are in seventh place with three wins and one defeat from their first four matches of the competition.

City were 2-0 victors when the two sides met at the Etihad in the same stage of the Champions League at the beginning of last season: De Bruyne sacrificed after an early red card for defender Giovanni Di Lorenzo.

There will be no Di Lorenzo this time around, still nursing an injury that has kept him out of action since the beginning of the international break.

Napoli’s injury list also includes goalkeeper Alex Meret, ruled out for several months with a back problem, as well as Benoit Badiashile, Alessandro Buongiorno, Stanislav Lobotka, David Neres, Alisson Santos and most recently Antonio Vergara, who was injured at the weekend.

Rafa Marin is suspended and Italian pair Luca Marianucci and Costantino Favasuli are not on the club’s UEFA squad list and are therefore ineligible to play.

This means that there will be minimal changes to Napoli’s team that beat Torino 1-0 in Serie A over the weekend. Rafa Marin will have to come out of the XI and is likely to be replaced by Matias Olivera, but that is the only alteration predicted by Sky Sport Italia.

Scott McTominay is set to feature on his return to his hometown, as is De Bruyne, who won six Premier League titles and the 2022-23 Champions League during his decade with Manchester City. Rasmus Hojlund is also expected to start on his return to Manchester.

City will have an Italian in their starting line-up with Gianluigi Donnarumma in goal. Erling Haaland is expected to lead the line for Enzo Maresca’s side, and could be supported by Iliman Ndiaye, Rayan Cherki and Jeremy Doku.

Man City (4-2-3-1): Donnarumma; Nunes, Dias, Guehi, Gvardiol; Fernandez, Anderson; Ndiaye, Cherki, Doku; Haaland.

Napoli (3-5-1-1): Milinkovic-Savic; Beukema, Rrahmani, Olivera; Politano, Anguissa, Gilmour, McTominay, Spinazzola; De Bruyne; Haaland.

Tonight’s game will be available to watch live in the UK on TNT Sports 1 and online through HBO Max. Supporters in the USA are able to tune in via Paramount+.
