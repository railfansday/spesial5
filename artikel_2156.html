Out-of-form West Ham man set to keep starting berth vs Preston North End with potential replacement ‘struggling’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

West Ham goalkeeper Mads Hermansen has been under fire recently, with poor performances prompting calls for Alphonse Areola to be brought into the starting XI. However, the Frenchman suffered an injury in the warm-up before WHUFC’s defeat to Blackburn Rovers before the break, ruling him out.

And, after the international break, doubts remain over Areola’s availability. Speaking to the official club media ahead of Saturday’s clash with Preston North End, Scott Parker said: “We’re struggling a bit with Alphonse at the moment. He probably looks a bit unlikely. We’ll probably leave it as late as we can to see if he’s fit to be involved.”

Elsewhere, Max Kilman is suspended, but Kyle Walker-Peters could be in line for a return. Parker made no mention of the full-back in his pre-match interview, saying: “There’s nothing really jumping out, other than Alphonse.”

Areola isn’t ruled out of West Ham’s clash with Preston North End, but it would be a surprise to see him start. If Parker goes into the weekend planning to start the Frenchman and it transpires that he isn’t ready to go on matchday, it throws that section of the pre-match preparations out of the window.

Despite his struggle for form, it is smarter to prepare for the game with Hermansen in goal. That will frustrate some sections of the WHUFC support, but it is the sensible move to make.

Hermansen has started every Championship game this season, but his performances have been far from convincing.

He isn’t solely responsible for the underwhelming defensive record of four clean sheets and 24 goals conceded in 16 games across all competitions. However, the Dane’s displays have left a lot to be desired with an inability to command his box and errors when coming to claim crosses leading to chances for the opposition.

Given his form, a fully fit Areola may have got the nod. For this weekend though, it seems Hermansen will keep his place.

When Areola is at 100%, Hermansen will have a fight on his hands to keep his place.

It means that if he does start again against Preston North End as expected, he needs to put in a far more assured performance. A clean sheet would not go amiss, but the most important thing is to act with confidence and conviction. If he can command his box, comfortably claim crosses and show composure on the ball, it will go a long way to easing concerns around his form.

If not, Areola will be waiting in the wings to come into the WHUFC side when fit. If his absence extends longer, then youngster Finlay Herrick may be considered if Parker feels a chance is needed.
