Cardiff & Coventry stars should be on Wolves' transfer radar with Arsenal's Max Dowman

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Wolves have had a positive start to the season under Cesar Peixoto as they look to bounce back to the Premier League at the first attempt.

It was a strange summer at Molineux, with the Wanderers hierarchy promising a huge squad overhaul that didn’t really happen after Rob Edwards was surprisingly dismissed for Peixoto.

Despite that, there’s no denying the Portuguese head coach has a talented group by Championship standards, and he has two senior players for every position, even if injuries are a concern right now.

Yet, as is always the case, the January window will be key in the promotion battle, and whilst Wolves won’t need to rip things up, they are likely to make a few tweaks - and bringing in attacking reinforcements is almost certainly going to be the priority.

The club have been linked with an ambitious move for Arsenal’s Max Dowman, and it would be a major coup if they could bring the outstanding talent to Molineux for the remainder of the campaign.

But, further additions would help Peixoto, and here we look at TWO more players the club should be trying to do deals for…

Obviously, this will be a very difficult deal to do, as Moylan is Cardiff’s best player and they will be planning on building their team around the talented attacker moving forward.

However, Wolves should have money to spend, and even Cardiff fans would worry about the prospect of Vincent Tan cashing in on their star man if the chance to make a big profit pops up.

From a football perspective, Moylan is exactly what Wolves need. With Fer Lopez now operating from the right at times, he could fit in as an attacking midfielder, and the ex-Lincoln man could also play up front if required.

Moylan has pace, spark and his form this season shows he is already one of the best players in the Championship, and you’d back the 25-year-old to make the step up to the Premier League as well.

Peixoto’s side are lacking a spark at times, and the Irishman would add speed, intensity as well as another goal threat, which could help Wolves get over the line. Again, it’s not going to be easy, but it could be a statement signing that the Midlands outfit need.

Raul Jimenez’s knee injury has robbed Wolves of their only physical striker, which has obviously influenced what Peixoto’s team can do.

Therefore, bringing in someone who can play with their back to goal is going to be a priority when the window opens, even though the Mexican should be back in January.

Simms fits the bill, and with the likes of Taiwo Awoniyi and Sidiki Cherif ahead of him in the pecking order at Coventry, it seems probable that he will depart in the New Year.
