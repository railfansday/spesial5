Cyberpunk 2 Is Canceled, Director Jokes, After GTA 6 Raises The Bar

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Grand Theft Auto 6 has raised the bar for open-world games, and Cyberpunk 2 creative director Igor Sarzyński is feeling it. Online, the developer jokingly commented about GTA 6 setting a new gold standard for the genre, a level of quality that makes him want to pack up his Cyberpunk 2 development bags and call it a day.

"GTA 6-induced-open-world-game-dev-depression is real," Sarzyński wrote online. Probably the single greatest artistic achievement in history, I mean it. Scale, density, coherence, thoughtfulness of it all, just a technological and creative miracle. Cyberpunk 2 is canceled, Imma go sweep autumn leaves in my backyard now."

Don't worry, Cyberpunk fans, because Sarzyński believes the game will offer a unique "flavor" compared to GTA 6. "Seriously though, I think we're doing something of our own, with a different, unique flavor," he wrote in a follow-up post. "GTA is impressive in a way all great art is; it expands what is possible, a captivating vision flawlessly executed."

Meanwhile, Cyberpunk 2077 has had a great 2026, as the release of the new Cyberpunk Edgerunners anime series once again drove the game's numbers up. A meaty update also helped draw new players, and Cyberpunk 2 game director Gabe Amatangelo recently shared more thoughts on how the sequel is evolving beyond his original vision as more people join the project.

Other developers across the industry have also commented on GTA 6, and Naughty Dog world design director Michael Barclay also believes that it is a technological achievement. "I've made video games for 19 years, and GTA6 appears to me as a Bugatti Veyron would to a nervous Victorian child," he wrote.
