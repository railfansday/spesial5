Kate Middleton, Prince William, King Charles, and Queen Camilla Gather For Remembrance Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

This Sunday, November 8, King Charles III, Queen Camilla, Kate Middleton, Prince William, Princess Anne and other members of the royal family came together to observe Remembrance Sunday, one of the most solemn events on the United Kingdom's calendar. The day marks the anniversary of the armistice that ended World War I, signed on “the eleventh hour of the eleventh day of the eleventh month” in 1918.

The focal point of every Remembrance Day is the Cenotaph in Whitehall, the Portland stone monument where tribute is paid to those who fell in the wars and conflicts in which the country has participated. Every year, representatives of the royal family, political leaders, the Armed Forces, and veterans gather there: This year, more than 10,000 marched alongside the king and the prime minister.

True to tradition, Charles III laid a wreath at the memorial on behalf of the nation and led the royal family. At his side was Prince William, Princess Anne, and the Duke of Edinburgh, who also paid their respects with a floral tribute. Queen Camilla and the Princess of Wales, Kate Middleton, watched the ceremony from the balcony of the Foreign Office, as is customary. On the political front, Prime Minister Andy Burnham laid a wreath on his first Remembrance Sunday as head of the government, accompanied by Opposition Leader Kemi Badenoch and other political leaders.

The day before had already been a day of remembrance: the King, the Queen, and the Princes of Wales attended the Festival of Remembrance at the Royal Albert Hall, the major tribute to the armed forces that precedes Remembrance Sunday.
