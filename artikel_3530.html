If you want something that's new to Prime Video, but Madden isn't to your taste, then how about one of the most underrated horror movies of the year? They Will Kill You, which somehow made just $10 million at the domestic box office, stars Zazie Beetz as a woman who takes a job as a housekeeper in what she soon learns is a demonic death trap, where she must fight to survive the night and save her life.

A fast-paced, adrenaline-fueled bloodbath, They Will Kill You lives up to its premise and then some. Ready to warm your hearts this cold November with a touching tale packed with funny moments and a toe-tapping soundtrack, Sing 2 is everything you could want.
