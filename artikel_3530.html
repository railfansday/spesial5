Xbox Game Pass Is Losing 8 More Games In November

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Don't get too comfortable with your Xbox Game Pass library, because you never know which game will be removed from it next. This month, several more games are on their way out on November 30, so say goodbye to deadly xenomorphs, even deadlier house spiders, and a Cold War battleground infested with killer robots.

One of the biggest departures this month is Aliens: Dark Descent. It's a solid entry in the Aliens franchise if you don't mind some base-management chores, as it nails the atmosphere of the classic sequel while giving you plenty of acid-blooded bugs to mow down.

This month will also see the 17th Century Ghostbusters game, Banishers: Ghost of New Eden, leave Game Pass, along with a few kid-friendly titles. As a reminder, you can save 20% on these games if you decide to buy them before they leave Game Pass.

Leaving Xbox Game Pass on November 30

  • Star Trucker
  • Aliens: Dark Descent
  • Kill It With Fire 2
  • Dreamworks Gabby's Dollhouse: Ready to Party
  • Banishers: Ghosts of New Eden
  • Totally Reliable Delivery Service
  • Moving Out 2
  • Generation Zero

Earlier this month, 14 games left Game Pass. The list was pretty diverse, including several simulator games from different publishers, the polarizing Football Manager 26, and Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2.
