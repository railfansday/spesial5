A Europa League clash was halted after chaos ensued due to fans displaying a flag in support of Palestine, with local police accused of using ‘disproportionate force’.

On Thursday night, following the end of the latest Champions League matchweek, the Europa League and the Europa Conference League took centre stage.

Inside the Batumi Arena in Georgia, a Europa League clash took place between Turkish Super Lig giants Besiktas and Israeli club Hapoel Be’er-Sheva.

The match was halted by referee Morten Krogh with just 26 minutes on the clock, with the score level at 0-0. Eventually, Besiktas went on to win the match 2-1, with Junior Olaitan and Orkun Kokcu scoring.

The match was marred by the chaos that led to the temporary suspension. According to The Guardian, Besiktas fans displayed a pro-Palestinian banner inside the stadium, with local police forced to step in.

It has been reported that Turkish media claimed that ‘Georgian police ‌entered the stands and used pepper spray on Besiktas fans’. Meanwhile, it is also believed that Turkish fans chanted ‘down with Israel’.

It was also reported by Turkish media that before kick-off, Besiktas players refused to shake hands with their opponents.

Reacting to the chaos that marred the Europa League match, the Turkish football federation slammed the local police in Georgia; a statement read: “We strongly condemn the unacceptable attitudes, behaviour and actions directed at Besiktas supporters inside and outside the stadium before and during the UEFA Europa League match between our representative Besiktas and Hapoel Be’er-Sheva in Georgia.”

Meanwhile, Besiktas issued a strong statement, accusing Georgian police of using ‘disproportionate’ force against their supporters.

The statement read: “We strongly condemn the security forces for their use of disproportionate force against our fans.

“We also extend our best wishes for a speedy recovery to all our fans affected by these incidents.”

Taking to social media, former Arsenal and Real Madrid star Mesut Ozil, who played in Turkey for Fenerbahce and Istanbul Basaksehir, slammed local police and congratulated Besiktas fans for taking a stand.

Ozil wrote: “I condemn the police intervention against Beşiktaş fans in Georgia who support the Palestinian people's struggle for freedom.

“I congratulate the Beşiktaş fans for taking this meaningful stand, refusing to remain silent in the face of the injustices in Palestine and speaking to the shared conscience of humanity. I wish a speedy recovery to all the fans subjected to the intervention.”

Meanwhile, Besiktas’ rivals, Galatasaray and Fenerbahce, also issued statements of their own as they condemned the action that was taken against Turkish fans.

An official release from the police has been issued following the incident.
