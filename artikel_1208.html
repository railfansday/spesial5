Donnarumma left furious after chants about his mother during Man City 2-2 Napoli

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Gianluigi Donnarumma was not best pleased after full-time of Manchester City’s 2-2 draw against Napoli in the Champions League on Tuesday evening after hearing insults and chants directed at his family.

Napoli were praised for their performance away against Man City on Tuesday night, picking up a useful 2-2 draw against the European heavyweights despite their rocky form across the first few months of the 2026-27 season. The Partenopei took the lead twice on the night through Rasmus Hojlund and Noa Lang, but were dealt equalisers by Ayyoub Bouaddi and Rayan Cherki; all four goals coming in the second half.

It wasn’t the most pleasant evening for Italy national team goalkeeper Donnarumma, who lined up in goal for the hosts. He was born in Castellammare di Stabia in Naples and has previously admitted that members of his family are Napoli supporters.

When Manchester City hosted Napoli in the Champions League last season, Donnarumma’s first start in the competition after joining from PSG, the stopper admitted that he had left his mother to watch from home given her conflict of interest.

After full-time on Tuesday evening, Italian reporters in the mixed zone asked Donnarumma how his mother was doing after the 2-2 draw between the two sides at The Etihad. “I’m angry because they insulted me,” said a visibly angered Donnarumma while making his way out of the room.

According to the Corriere dello Sport, Donnarumma was repeatedly subjected to insulting chants from the Napoli fans during Tuesday’s game, and was called a ‘son of a b****’ on several occasions by the travelling supporters.
