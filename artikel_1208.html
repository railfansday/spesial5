How Milan winger Moreira infuriated Olympiacos fans and players after Europa League goal

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Milan winger Diego Moreira infuriated Olympiacos fans and players with his goal celebration in a Europa League game in Athens, prompting Rossoneri coach Ruben Amorim to apologise at full time.

The incident happened in the final minutes of the game, after the Belgian winger scored to make it 4-2 for the Rossoneri.

La Gazzetta dello Sport describes what Moreira did to irritate the home fans and players.

The Belgian scored in front of the Greek supporters’ stand and stared at it, motionless, for a few seconds.

Safe to say that neither the home fans nor their players enjoyed the celebration, as Manolis Saliakas and João Silva confronted the Milan winger.

Silva wagged his finger at him, as to say: ‘You don’t do that in our house.’ A few seconds of tension followed. With Saliakas, there was a grim face-to-face that lasted a few seconds.

“It won’t happen again. Moreira is a young lad who plays, but he has to grow,” Milan coach Amorim said at full time.

“We know we wouldn’t like it if they did that to our fans. It’s my responsibility, and I apologise, but he didn’t do it with bad intent.”

Moreira has now scored five goals in 12 appearances across all competitions with Milan.
