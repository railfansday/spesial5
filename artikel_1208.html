Fabregas praises Alvini despite 5-1 loss vs. Milan: Como will use the same approach

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Como coach Cesc Fabregas says he’ll take the same approach as Frosinone coach Massimiliano Alvini for the upcoming Champions League match against AEK Athens: ‘I’m sticking to my ideas.’

Como are preparing for a home Champions League game against AEK Athens, and Fabregas spoke at a press conference on Monday, the eve of the game.

Como are the best-placed Serie A club in the Champions League so far as they sit fourth with nine points in four matches.

They’ve won seven of their last eight games across all competitions, including a 9-1 win over Lens in the previous Champions League round.

Fabregas said he wouldn’t change the team’s identity for their upcoming European match, citing Frosinone coach Alvini the day after a 5-1 loss to Milan.

“I’m taking the same approach as Alvini against Milan. I’m sticking to my ideas,” Fabregas said via Gazzetta.

“Of course, there are games we need to know how to manage in order to win, but we’re not going to change all the good things we’re doing. I’m proud of our last match against Cagliari, which featured so many new elements: players returning from international duty, Da Cunha playing as a No.10 instead of Paz, a role he hasn’t played in very often.

“Then Ricci and Liberali started and did well, and Lahdo made a positive impact off the bench. All of that tells you the team is there, that they understand what they want to do and how to do it. I like stability and being able to control everything, even if it’s difficult.”

Frosinone remain the most surprising team in Serie A as the newly-promoted Serie A side sit seventh and even beat Como 2-0 in September.

Against Milan, they refused to sit deep and remained true to their identity, but ultimately conceded five goals on the counter away at San Siro.

“Today, I still want to hear people say Alvini is a great coach. That is identity and courage, even if he lost 5-1,” Fabregas insisted.

“When you lose, you’re rubbish; when you win, you’re a genius. You need to keep things in perspective. Football is certainly changing, partly because referees are blowing their whistles less. Fewer players are staying down because that tactic no longer works. It’s a positive turning point for the Italian league, which is becoming one of the two or three most entertaining in Europe.”

Como have already beaten RB Leipzig, Manchester United and Lille in the Champions League, and tomorrow they’ll be the favourite side against AEK.

“Nothing changes for us. We’re a small club that knows exactly who we are,” Fabregas said.

“There’s a fantastic atmosphere at Mozzate. We know our strengths and weaknesses. We can’t control what people say; we have to be stronger and manage our emotions. The players are getting a lot more attention, and that helps them mature, too. If they want to reach the top, they have to know how to handle that too. We’re still right at the beginning. We’re focusing on the journey, not the destination.

“AEK are a great team. They caused problems for Real and Shakhtar; they’re physically strong and have a great mentality. We’ll need to be very clinical.”

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
