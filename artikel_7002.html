Staff Compliance Engineer at LiveKit

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

LiveKit is building the infrastructure layer for the voice-driven era of computing. Our platform gives developers everything they need to build, test, deploy, scale, and observe agents in production. Founded in 2021, LiveKit powers voice AI applications for OpenAI, xAI, Salesforce, Coursera, Spotify, and thousands of others, collectively facilitating billions of calls each year.

Enterprise customers trust us with real-time audio, video and data belonging to their own end users, and before they build on LiveKit they want proof that we protect it. Our compliance program gives them that proof, and enterprise deals close on it. We are hiring a Compliance Engineer to lead the customer-facing side of that program. You will be the strategic partner to our sales team on security and compliance in enterprise deals, and you will own how we answer customers: security questionnaires, our answer library and the security terms in customer contracts.

You will join a small Trust team that covers security, IT and compliance, and you will own real parts of the program from your first week. You will share the day-to-day running of the compliance program with the rest of our Compliance team. We want someone who works themselves out of a job on the repeatable work. You will automate and simplify what you own, so your time goes to the larger deals and our compliance work grows more slowly than our customer base. We run SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI-DSS and HIPAA today, with ISO 9001 in progress. This role is remote, based in the United States or Canada.

Be the strategic partner to our sales team on security and compliance: get into enterprise deals early and work directly with customers’ security teams.

Own customer security questionnaires, DDQs and our answer library, including the questionnaire automation we run in Vanta.

Advise on the security and compliance terms in customer contracts, working with our legal team.

Automate and simplify the repeatable work you own, and make common customer asks self-serve through our trust center and first-line support, working with IT and Customer Support.

Share the day-to-day running of the compliance program on a business-hours rotation: vendor reviews, policies, evidence collection, the risk register and questions from product and engineering.

Lead or support certification audits, working with our external auditors and with the engineering, IT and security teams that own the controls.

5+ years in compliance engineering, GRC, customer trust or security assurance, with hands-on ownership of controls.

A track record of scaling a compliance service: you took a recurring workflow, cut the manual effort or turnaround, and can show the before and after.

Experience owning customer security questionnaires and DDQs for enterprise customers, working directly with a sales team on deals.

You like customer-facing work and have joined calls with customers’ security teams on large enterprise deals.

Hands-on experience with Vanta, including questionnaire automation and keeping tests and evidence healthy.

Experience managing an audit end to end with an external auditor.

The judgment to work from the intent of a control: you know where a requirement leaves room, and you can agree that with an auditor.

Scripting and automation skills in a language such as Python, and the ability to read cloud, identity and CI/CD configuration well enough to judge whether a control works.

Experience reviewing security and compliance terms in customer contracts.

Experience building AI-assisted workflows for compliance or customer assurance.

Experience advising product and engineering teams on compliance, or working in Kubernetes-native environments.

Third-party risk or sub-processor program experience.

An opportunity to build something truly impactful to the world

Contribute to open source alongside world-class engineers
