Giant Code Geass C.C. plush stands over three feet tall and costs $420

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

C.C. from the popular anime Code Geass is getting a plushie that turns the green-haired girl into a giant three-foot-tall collectible.

It’s been a big year for monolithic anime merch, with everything from a life-size Marin Kitagawa figure with an $8,000 price tag to giant Snorlax body pillows.

However, fans of the popular mecha anime Code Geass will soon be able to celebrate the show’s 20th anniversary in style with a plushie so big that it needs its own dedicated spot on your sofa.

The massive merch in question is a three-foot-tall C.C. plushie that comes with the character’s signature vibrant green hair, white straitjacket, and a $420 price tag.

According to the official store page, the plush recreates the white restraint outfit C.C. wears in her first appearance, including its black belts, fastenings, and long, flared sleeves. The C.C. plushie also comes with a hidden feature under the fringe, as her red Geass symbol has been embroidered on her forehead.

As for just how much space she’ll take up, C.C. measures approximately 39.4 inches tall, 27.6 inches wide, and 29.5 inches deep. The polyester plush also weighs around 19.4 pounds, making this a particularly hefty addition to any Code Geass collection.

The made-to-order doll will be available to pre-order for residents in Japan from November 6, 2026 until December 21, with shipping scheduled for May 2027. It also comes with a pretty big price tag as well, as the store page lists C.C. for ¥66,000 (around $420).
