Tencent launches AI gaming companion that watches your screen and gives advice

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Chinese technology company Tencent has revealed an AI gaming companion that can watch gameplay and respond to what’s happening on screen with real-time conversations.

AI companions are becoming increasingly popular, with everyone from Genshin Impact’s HoYoverse releasing virtual waifus you can study alongside to Razer’s AVA, which allows you to summon Hatsune Miku-like holographic assistants.

Tencent is also aiming to catch gamers’ eyes with Eciyuan, a new PC app that features AI companions that watch your gameplay, offer in-game advice, and chat with you using voice controls during solo gaming sessions.

According to the translated website, players can choose between four characters and select the appearance/personality that suits them. For example, Shen Lubai is described as a 24-year-old aspiring female esports commentator whose gaming specialties include Valorant, League of Legends, and Counter-Strike.

Whether Shen Lubai can actually get you out of Elo hell in League of Legends or improve your aim in Valorant is another matter, but she can at least offer you advice and a virtual shoulder to cry on when your matches don’t go to plan.

Tencent’s AI assistants won’t just backseat your gaming sessions either, as users can also chat with them about their interests and keep conversations going on the go via the mobile app. The four characters can also appear as desktop pets, while players can set wallpapers that match their companion’s outfit.

There are even plans to add a voice-changing feature and a mode where the AI companions can speak on your behalf, which would certainly make for some rather interesting Discord discussions.
