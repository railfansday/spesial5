Advance Your Creative Practice With a Graduate Degree From the University of Louisville

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

All MFA and MA students receive generous scholarships, with additional support for research, creative projects, and travel.

With substantial scholarships, exceptional facilities, accomplished faculty, and private studios, students pursuing an MFA in Studio Art and Design at the University of Louisville have the resources and creative freedom to develop their artistic practice within a dynamic, supportive community.

The rigorous 60-credit MFA program encourages experimentation, critical inquiry, and interdisciplinary exploration. Students may pursue interdisciplinary practices or concentrate on individual media, including ceramics, drawing, glass, painting, photography, printmaking, sculpture, design, new media, and installation.

Located in Louisville's Portland arts district, the MFA program occupies a dedicated, newly renovated warehouse featuring individual graduate studios, state-of-the-art production facilities, laboratories, a seminar room, and an exhibition gallery. This distinctive environment provides students with the space, resources, and opportunities to develop ambitious creative projects.

The Hite Institute also offers a 31-credit-hour MA in Art History or Curatorial Studies. Combining critical theory, art historical research, contemporary art, and hands-on exhibition experience, the program prepares students for careers in museums, galleries, curatorial practice, arts administration, and art writing, as well as further academic study.

Founded in 1946, the endowed Hite Institute of Art + Design has built a distinctive identity grounded in its avant-garde origins and oriented to creative experimentation and critical inquiry. Its accomplished faculty of practicing artists, designers, and scholars encourages students to expand their creative and intellectual horizons while engaging with contemporary cultural, social, and environmental issues.

Both graduate programs maintain small, selective cohorts, allowing for close faculty mentorship, individualized attention, and meaningful collaboration. Students benefit from the Hite Institute's extensive resources, including six galleries, outstanding glass and additive manufacturing facilities, partnerships with museums and cultural institutions, an active alumni network, and visiting lectures by internationally recognized artists, curators, and arts professionals.

In addition to guaranteed scholarships, graduate students may qualify for teaching assistantships, internships, and additional awards that provide valuable professional experience and support their creative and scholarly development.
