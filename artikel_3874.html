PS Plus Essential's Next Update Looks a Bit Better in Japan Than the Rest of the World

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It’s all subjective, of course, but Japan’s PS Plus Essential update for December 2026 looks a little bit better than what we’re getting in the west.

Now if we’re looking at pure value here, the Japanese lineup is objectively better: the Bleach game retails for $59.99, while Amnesia is $24.99. The former is also available on PS5 and PS4, whereas the latter is only available for PS4 (although you can of course play it on PS5 via backwards compatibility).

Bleach is also newer, having released in 2025, while Amnesia is from 2023.

But it does ultimately all come down to taste, I suppose; survival horror fans may be much happier with Frictional’s title – especially if they have no real interest in anime. I suppose the lineup in Japan does lack variety, as you’re getting two very similar titles from Bandai Namco there.

Amnesia: The Bunker did release in Japan, so I’m not entirely sure why the lineup is different between regions.

Sony is putting a lot of marketing into its domestic region at the moment, as it tries to reverse its flagging fortunes. It’s also just launched a new PS Portal there, and is putting a pretty big promotional campaign behind it. Maybe it thinks this lineup will be more enticing to Japanese players.

Interesting stuff, though, which selection would you have preferred? I’ve had the Bleach game on my wishlist for a little while, so I think I would have rather got that – but I can certainly see why people would prefer the more varied worldwide selection as well.
