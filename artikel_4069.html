Guide: Cyberpunk 2077: Best Mods on PS5

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

What are the best Cyberpunk 2077 mods on PS5? In this guide, I'm going to highlight which mods I think are well worth installing to make your dark future experience more enticing.

On this page: Cyberpunk 2077: Best Mods on PS5

Mod support for Cyberpunk 2077 was added with Patch 2.5 on PS5, allowing for user-created content on Sony's console.

Choosing and installing mods is easy: just click the 'Mods' option on the game's main menu and you'll be taken to the current list of available mods. You can scroll through pages manually or you can use the search bar.

If you find a mod you like, select it from the list and then click the 'Install' button, or just press the Options button on your controller to install it right there and then.

You can also hit the 'Like' button to add the mod to your 'Liked' list. Doing this lets you keep track of your favourite mods via the 'Liked' filter button new the top of the screen.

You can have a maximum of 150 mods installed at any one time, and said mods need to have a combined file size of 15.76GB or under (although this isn't really an issue as of yet, as standard mod sizes tend to be very, very small).

Unfortunately, yes, using any mods disables Trophies when playing Cyberpunk 2077 on PS5.

This counts for any save file that has ever been used in conjunction with mods.

As such, you may want to beat the game at least once, or go all the way and get the Platinum, before diving into the world of mods.

Below, you'll find a list of what I think are currently the best Cyberpunk 2077 mods available on PS5.

Admittedly, the selection is far from vast at this point in time — but new mods are being added on what seems to be a weekly basis, and so this list should only grow over the coming months.

A mod that's bound to be at or near the top of Cyberpunk 2077's modding menu for years to come, Deceptious Quest Core is a necessary framework mod for a number of Deceptious' other mods.

Basically, you need to have this mod installed if you want a few of the other mods featured on this list — it's just the way that the game's quest-based modding works.

Any mod that has [DQC] in its title requires Quest Core to function.

Note that you need to enter V's H10 apartment (and then possibly exit it) for this mod to activate, and subsequently activate all of the [DQC] mods.

This mod aims to fix a surprisingly long list of bugs that are still found throughout Cyberpunk 2077.

The vast majority of these bugs are minor and only affect very small aspects of the overall game. We're talking things like slight animation glitches, specific audio mixing (those annoyingly quiet, and sometimes annoyingly loud TVs), and quest-related oddities.

Still, it's a mod worth installing when you start adding all of these fixes up — especially if you plan on starting a new playthrough.

This mod allows Johnny to appear in all of V's apartments, not just their starting home in the H10 megabuilding.

If you enjoy Johnny's company, this mod's good for immersion purposes. He'll lounge around the apartments, and add to the ambience by playing his guitar when V asks him to.

One of my favourite mods on PS5 so far, Weather Probability Rebalance does exactly what its name suggests, adjusting the chances of different weather patterns appearing as you carve out a life in Night City (and its surrounding wastelands).

For those who have already spent tens or even hundreds of hours playing Cyberpunk 2077, you may have noticed that the weather can be a bit... boring. The whole atmosphere of the city can change when it's raining, or when there's a sandstorm — but it can feel like it just doesn't happen often enough.

According to modder RMK_, the game's weather system is actually a little funky. In short, sunny weather can become 'stuck' under certain circumstances, and so this mod attempts to fix the problem.

As a result, adverse weather is much more common and lasts longer, allowing you to soak up the vibes.

A great mod for immersion purposes, Night City Interactions lets you go out drinking at various locations across the map — but the best part is that you can invite other characters, like Panam, Judy, River, and Kerry, to come along with you.

As a bonus, this mod also includes a Cyberpunk Edgerunners tour quest. As you may know, the anime features a number of backdrops that are based on actual locations from the game, and you can check these specific places out at your leisure.

Note that you need to enter V's H10 apartment (and then possibly exit) before this mod activates.

Three small mods that add just an extra touch of immersion to ripperdoc visits.

Instead of jumping straight to the cyberware menu upon talking to a ripper, V will actually sit down in the clinic's chair before undergoing surgery.

The Dogtown mod does the same for rippers in the Dogtown region of the map, while the Vic-specific mod uses Vic's existing animations from early in the game.

These are mods that, when used in conjunction with other immersion-focused mods, add up to a more detailed experience.

Another two mods that bring just a little more immersion to your time in Night City.

They let you sit down at the game's various bars and actually take a fully-animated drink. You can even select the kind of drink that you want from a dialogue menu.

What's more, the drinks give the same kind of temporary stat buffs that you get from consuming drinks in your inventory. A quick pre-gig bar crawl never hurt anyone!

Just like the Immersive Bartenders mods, this one lets you grab a fully-animated snack or drink from the numerous food vendors found across Night City.

Stat buffs are applied as if you were using items from your inventory, so it actually makes food stalls worth visiting if you're gearing up for some action.

This mod improves the readability of shards, which can be difficult to properly parse — especially if you're in a rush.

Basically, Readable Shards adjusts the text's formatting, making it slightly bigger and adding coloured names to the people who actually wrote the conversations in the first place.

This is a small mod but it can be a surprisingly big difference, particularly if you're a sucker for reading every bit of lore that you find.

A great little mod that adds a security camera system to NCARTS (metros).

By pressing left and right on the d-pad, you can switch to the cameras while you're riding the train, allowing you to view four new perspectives: two cameras shows the length of the carriage that you're on from either end, while the other two shows the outside of the metro from the front and back.

If you want to see more of Night City during your NCART travels, then this mod's an easy recommendation.

If you're absolutely sick of El Capitan giving you unreasonable time limits during his dynamic car-jacking gigs, then this mod's for you.

It simply increases said time limits by 25%, which usually equates to much easier missions. It also means that you can better enjoy the more combat-focused gigs without having to race ahead.
