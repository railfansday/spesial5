PS6 Development on Track, Sony Making 'Solid Progress' Towards Next-Gen

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Work on the PS6 and the next console generation as a whole is currently "progressing as scheduled" at PlayStation, it's been confirmed.

Speaking alongside the release of Sony's latest quarterly financial results, CFO Lin Tao said the company is on track to deliver its next PlayStation system, and it intends to make "solid progress" towards the next generation within the next fiscal year (via VGC).

The quote from Tao reads: "Looking ahead to the next fiscal year, we aim to continue to enhance user engagement and further strengthen monetisation through the release of strong titles and the expansion of our service. We also intend to make solid progress in preparing for the next-generation platform."

Tao continued by talking up "investment in the new platform" from the start of the next fiscal year. It has to attach "great importance" to a new console and "make the necessary investments" for it. "We have to support the soundness of our platform and think about how rationally we can invest in that platform. We will announce that at an appropriate time in the future."

A few months ago, Sony CEO Hiroki Totoki claimed the firm hadn't yet decided when the PS6 will come out. Consistent rumours point to a release in the next few years, however, with a potential $1,000+ price point and a handheld device to go with it.

With the prices of components still on the rise — which Sony would need to manufacture PS6 hardware — the potential cost of a next-gen machine looks like it'll be the biggest factor in whether PlayStation fans jump on board or not. When we polled Push Square users, 65% of you said you wouldn't pay four figures for a video game console.

Microsoft will also have a new Xbox to launch in the coming years, currently codenamed Xbox Helix, and that's suggested to cost as much as $1,800, depending on whether it decides to subsidise the box. PS6 is at least rumoured to be cheaper to make than Xbox Helix, so the cost shouldn't get quite that high.

Elsewhere in its financial report, Sony confirmed PS5 sales have reached 97.9 million. With a boost from GTA 6, it should hit the 100 million sales milestone by the end of the next quarter, and become the fourth PlayStation system to do so.
