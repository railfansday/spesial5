Jennifer Lawrence and Kim Kardashian’s NYC Night Out Adds to Their Quirky 11-Year History

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Jennifer Lawrence and Kim Kardashian enjoyed a night out together in New York City yesterday, beginning the evening in regular attire and then ending it in matching Skims pajamas and robes as they left the Four Seasons hotel. At first glance, the pairing might seem unexpected, but Lawrence’s history with the Kardashian family goes back for over 10 years.

In 2023, Lawrence told Kylie Jenner while interviewing her for Interview magazine that she started following the family’s original reality show Keeping Up With the Kardashians when she was a teenager: “I’ve been watching the show since I was, not to date myself, 14, so I would say I know everything about your family,” she shared.

Her first big public moment with the Kardashian-Jenner family was in 2015. Kardashian’s mother, Kris Jenner, posted a photo of Lawrence in bed with her along with a birthday message.

In November 2017, Lawrence interviewed Kardashian on Jimmy Kimmel Live! while serving as a guest host for Jimmy Kimmel.

Kardashian revealed where they first met. “We were at a hotel in New York, and I don’t know where we were coming from, but it was like, 8, and we were going up to see this Axel Vervoordt suite at the Greenwich [Hotel] and I’m getting in the elevator with Kanye [West] and the doors are about to close and I hear Jennifer screaming, ‘I love your show!’ so loud across the whole lobby,” she recalled.

They also discussed Lawrence having dinner at Kris’s house two weeks before their interview and Kardashian dressing her up.

It marked their first significant public conversation:

Less than a year later, Lawrence quipped during a February 2018 event at The Wing that her friendship with Kardashian doesn’t go both ways. “I don’t know that she’d call me her friend,” Lawrence said. “It’s probably a one-sided friendship.”

She recalled learning she’d interview Kardashian on Kimmel’s show. “When I found out that I was going to be interviewing her, I was like, ‘Oh my God, my whole life has been building up to this moment,’” she said. “I wanted to get as much information as I could. I wanted to do my due diligence on Kim Kardashian. I went over to Kris [Jenner’s] house and had dinner with the whole family.”

Of the family generally, Lawrence said, “They are smart, grounded and normal and funny. They’re very nice people, [they’re] very close, [they have a] loyal bond with one another, which I think is a positive thing to put out into the world. They’ve been absolutely lovely to me.”

Lawrence has remained close to the Kardashian-Jenner family in the years since. (Kris even attended Lawrence’s wedding to Cooke Maroney in October 2019.)

During her 2023 conversation with Kylie, Lawrence even jokingly referred to Kris as her mother while asking a question about Kylie’s relationship with her older sister Kendall Jenner. “You and me are Leos,” she told Kylie. “We’re happy, excited golden retrievers. And Kendall, of course, is a Scorpio like our mother, Kris.” She and Kylie ended their chat by exchanging I love yous.

Last month, Lawrence was mentioned on The Kardashians, with Kardashian revealing the actress had asked for Kourtney Kardashian’s contact information to say sorry for a remark she made during a Vanity Fair interview about Kourtney.

Kardashian said that Lawrence wrote in her apology email, “I just feel like I’m one of your sisters.”
