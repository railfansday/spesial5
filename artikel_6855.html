Rihanna Shares Rare Footage of Her Kids RZA, Riot, and Rocki on Set

Rihanna tapped her three children, 4-year-old RZA, 3-year-old Riot, and 1-year-old Rocki, for Savage X Fenty’s holiday campaign—and thoroughly documented their time on set. The entrepreneur shared behind-the-scenes footage of the shoot, giving fans a rare look at the family’s dynamic.

“Behind the scenes with the actual gifts!!! 🥹🎁,” she wrote. Her daughter, Rocki, is featured in several adorable close-ups, while her sons RZA and Riot have their own playful moments:

This isn’t the first time Rihanna’s kids have appeared in a shoot. The three siblings have all graced magazine covers, with the youngest, Rocki, making her debut on W with her mom in April.

Riot was the first of the three to have a solo cover. Earlier this month, his mom revealed the six covers he shot for Arena Homme+.

His dad, A$AP Rocky, teased Riot’s Arena Homme+ modeling job in an interview with The Cut in September.

“One of my sons has a modeling gig in like three days, you know, the day before my birthday, and it’s—I’m so proud of him; this is his first magazine cover, so I think it’s just fascinating to see these little people grow into personalities of their own,” he said. “It’s trippy, just in retrospect, thinking back when they were just so small and little. I mean, they’re still relatively little and small, but you know, they kind of speak up for themselves and have their own kind of swag and shit. It’s crazy.”
