Kate Middleton’s Awkward Moment as a Fan Encourages Her to Break Protocol Ahead of the Earthshot Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The royal had to stick to her guns and wouldn’t back down.

The Princess of Wales, best known as Kate Middleton, had a slightly awkward moment on her tour of India this week (where she’s raising awareness for her husband Prince William’s Earthshot Prize Awards, which reward innovative environmental solutions). While greeting an excited crowd gathered in Mumbai, the Princess sadly had to disappoint a cluster of children who were hoping to get her autograph.

Speaking to one child who had a pen and paper in hand for the Princess, Catherine reportedly had to say, “Sorry, I can’t!” as to sign her name would be a severe breach of royal protocol.

None of the royal family are allowed to give out autographs, owing to fears that their signature could be replicated and forged, or that they’ll simply be sold on for monetary gain.

In recent years, they have become more amenable to people requesting selfies, which previously was not common practice.

It’s far from the first time the Princess has had to navigate this type of delicate situation with a member of the public; earlier this year while on a visit to Wales, she was also asked to sign her name and had to offer up a consolatory handshake instead.

The trip to India is Kate’s first international visit since her cancer diagnosis in 2024, and her attendance was somewhat unexpected. She has missed the last three Earthshot Prize ceremonies, but given she’s traveled all the way to Mumbai, it’s not a surprise that she showed up alongside William for this year’s event, wearing a stunning Anarkali by Indian designer Mahima Mahajan.

The 2026 Earthshot Prize ceremony will not air live but will be available to watch on Amazon Prime from November 30.
