Lions, Patriots Week 10 inactives: Keith Abney won’t play in Germany

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Detroit Lions have declared their inactives ahead of their Week 10 matchup with the New England Patriots in Munich, Germany.

Following the addition of safety Andre Cisco, the Lions were rostering one more defensive back than they normally keep on game days, and with the starters set, the decision to make rookie defensive back Keith Abney inactive likely came down to special teams. Reserves like Cisco, Christian Izien, and Khalil Dorsey are all established special teamers, while players like Ennis Rakestraw and Nick Whiteside give the Lions depth at a position of weakness.
