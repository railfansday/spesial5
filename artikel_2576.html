How to watch Warriors vs. Kings

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Golden State Warriors return to action on Wednesday night as they host the Sacramento Kings for another NBA Cup matchup. The game will be played at 7:00 PM PT in San Francisco and can be watched on NBC Sports Bay Area.

The Warriors snapped their five-game losing streak with Sunday night's 88-87 victory over the Boston Celtics. Evidently, all Golden State needed was a return home, as Sunday's victory marked the team's seventh consecutive win at Chase Center. While the offense continued to struggle, the Warriors stepped up defensively, holding a Celtics team that ranks ninth in the NBA in offensive rating to fewer than 90 points for the first time all season — albeit without their second-leading scorer, Paul George.

Draymond Green, in particular, seemed extra motivated to silence his critics. The Warriors veteran scored a season-high nine points, knocking down three triples in the opening quarter. However, it was his defense that ultimately made the difference, as Green reminded everyone why he's stamped as a future Hall of Famer with a game-saving defensive stop against Celtics star Jayson Tatum.

Sunday's victory moved the Warriors back above .500 just in time for Wednesday night's matchup against the 2-15 Sacramento Kings. Although Golden State failed to reach 100 points for the fifth consecutive game, some offensive reinforcements appear to be on the way. Backup point guard Brandon Williams, who has missed the last six games with a hamstring injury, is expected to return tonight, according to ESPN's Anthony Slater.

Williams' ability to create his own shot will be a welcome addition to a Warriors offense that has sorely lacked that skill in Stephen Curry's absence. His quickness and ability to finish around the rim give Golden State another player who can penetrate opposing defenses and create open looks for shooters on the perimeter. Look for his return to help jump-start the offense, especially against a Sacramento team allowing 119.4 points per game while ranking 28th in the NBA in defensive rating.

Enjoy the game Dub Nation. GO WARRIORS!!!

Warriors: De'Anthony Melton, Brandin Podziemski, Yaxel Lendeborg, Draymond Green, Charles Bassey

Kings: Darius Acuff Jr., Zach LaVine, De'Andre Hunter, Keegan Murray, Domantas Sabonis

Who: Golden State Warriors (9 – 8) vs. Sacramento Kings (2 – 15)

When: Wednesday, November 25th, at 7:00 p.m. PT

Where: Chase Center — San Francisco, California
