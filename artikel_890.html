Cummins to remain captain after 'unanimous support' from Cricket Australia

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Pat Cummins confirms he will "100 per cent support" forum Cricket Australia has set up following Usman Khawaja's 'Sandpapergate' allegations; CA chair David Boon rejects notion Cummins' integrity has been "compromised" amid varying accounts from players of 2018 ball-tampering scandal

Pat Cummins will remain as Test and ODI captain after being given "unanimous support" by Cricket Australia, with the skipper confirming he will take part in the independent forum launched following Usman Khawaja's 'Sandpapergate' allegations.

Cummins' position had been under increasing scrutiny since Cameron Bancroft wrote a detail-filled account of the scandal in the West Australian newspaper, backing up Khawaja's claims that every member of the 2018 side was aware of the sandpaper plot.

Bancroft - the player caught on camera trying to alter the condition of the ball in South Africa eight years ago - said he was stirred to break his silence by Cummins' vehement denials of knowledge of the scheme in a press conference last week.

After Cricket Australia chair David Boon and CEO Todd Greenberg met Cummins in Sydney on Friday, a statement released on Saturday read: "In the light of the ongoing speculation surrounding the Australian men's team tour of South Africa in 2018, the CA board met to ensure measures are in place to protect the integrity of Australian Cricket and the welfare of all concerned.

"As part of this meeting, the board resolved to express its unanimous support for Pat Cummins as captain given ongoing speculation about his position."

Cummins, who is set to lead Australia in a four-Test series at home to New Zealand from December 9, with the first match in Bancroft's native Perth, said: "Being Australian captain carries with it an awesome responsibility.

"I have and never will take that lightly.

"David (Boon) and Todd (Greenberg) have a very tough job navigating cricket through all of this and I commend them for reaching out to me and to many of the players and staff from 2018.

"I support the forum 100 per cent. I will be there. All of the players and staff I have spoken with are very keen to have their say. There will be a lot of difficult conversations but that is what's needed.

"Otherwise, from this point, captaining the Australian team in Perth will be my focus and priority."

Boon added: "We've expressed our support to Pat and our concern for everyone impacted by the events of the 2018 tour of South Africa.

"We have learned from that experience and are proud of the team and the culture Pat and other leaders in the team have fostered over the past eight years and unequivocally reject any implication that Pat's integrity has been compromised."

"We are absolutely determined to steward Australian cricket through this very difficult time with diligence and patience and to listen and learn.

"That's what the independent forum we announced this week is about and the board encourages all players and support staff to tell and discuss their stories there. We will be publishing the terms of reference in due course."
