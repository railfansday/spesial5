DWTS' Jenna Johnson Breaks Bone Night Before Quarter Finals

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Dancing With the Stars pro Jenna Johnson—partnered with Harry Shum Jr. in season 35—revealed that she broke her nose while rehearsing for Grammy Night and how the injury could impact her performance.

Jenna Johnson can smell the finish line—well, at least metaphorically.

As the Dancing With the Stars pro salsaed one step closer to clinching the Mirrorball Trophy with celebrity partner Harry Shum Jr., she revealed that she injured her nose during a rehearsal ahead of season 35's quarter finals.

"So last night I definitely broke my nose during camera blocking," Johnson explained on Instagram Nov. 10, while giving fans a glimpse at her face, noting on the right side it was "a little swollen."

But the ballroom dancer was still in good spirits after the accident, quipping, "No black eyes today, which is awesome." She kept up her sense of humor with the caption, "Salsa: 1, Jenna’s Nose: 0, Happy Show Day."

Johnson, 32, and Shum Jr., 44, were preparing for Grammy Night, during which the couple—who currently hold the highest cumulative score of the season—will perform a salsa to Lady Gaga and Bruno Mars' "Die With A Smile."

As such, Johnson warned ahead of the romantic duet, "If my nose looks just four times the size, that's why."

The two-time champion—who won season 26 with Adam Rippon and season 33 with Joey Graziadei—is taking the unwelcome twist in stride, proving that she is still fierce competition. Johnson's third title would tie her with husband Valentin Chmerkovskiy and Mark Ballas for current pros with the most Mirrorballs. (Derek Hough is the all-time DWTS champion with six wins during his 17 seasons as a pro.)

And Johnson's well aware the competition can come with difficult moments. After a tense exchange with judge Carrie Ann Inaba over a critique of a dance move during Super Bowl Week, she clarified, "This is a competition. There are judges for a reason. They do need to give feedback. So, yeah, we will take it."
