Mel Andreatta sits down with Jane Lewis to discuss life on and off the pitch ahead of Scotland’s huge World Cup play-off with Sweden. How has the Australian made Ayrshire feel like home, and what has she learned since taking charge of the national team? She reveals the messages exchanged with former colleague Tony Gustavsson before they meet in opposite dugouts and explains why Scotland won’t be intimidated by Sweden’s reputation.
