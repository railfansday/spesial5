31 Best Luxury Gifts That Are Worth the Splurge in 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Though we don’t like to assign a price tag to a good gift, the best luxury gifts rise to those special occasions during the holidays or beyond when it’s appropriate to go all out for your nearest and dearest. So if you’re looking to spoil someone on your list (or maybe just yourself), we’ve rounded up some of the most carefully considered, no-holds-barred options for when money’s no object. As a baseline, it’s always important to ponder your recipient’s needs before dropping a ton of money on something they’ll never use. But thoughtful presents are our speciality if you’re racking your brain for investment ideas that will impress (and not just end up on someone’s eBay account). We’ve compiled a short list of some of the finest gifts out there that radiate luxurious energy, from a high-end espresso machine to a professional-grade massage gun, state-of-the-art pizza oven, and much more.

The perfect “just a little something” for your consummate host. Sterling silver knots serve as a sophisticated perch for olives, cherries, and other charcuterie-board fixings. Consider it an elegant addition to the next aperitivo and every one after.

To know your loved one is to know whether they’re a gold or silver type. If the latter is what they prefer, Jenni Kayne and Anastasio Home’s sleek trinket box will be their new favorite way to store precious baubles and heirloom jewels.

Throw blankets are one of the easiest ways to layer texture into your spaces, something this floral jacquard design by House of Lyria does especially well. The lucky recipient of this gift will appreciate that the textile’s wool-blend isn’t scratchy, making it perfect for enjoying holiday movie watch-a-thons in style.

For the ones still trying to nail down their signature scent, a candle set that runs the entire olfactory gamut is equal parts indulgent and educational. Personalize the labels with sweet sayings, their initials, or even anniversary dates to commemorate the occasion. Rumor has it that even Taylor and Travis received this as a gift for their wedding.

Indulge in a functional luxury gift this holiday season with an expertly crafted catchall by interior designer Alyssa Kapito and leather goods brand Savette. Made from 100% calf leather with a silver base, this tray will ensure any keepsake is held with care.

Each perfume in this trio is handmade with a grounding blend of long-lasting aromatherapy oils, and designed to set an intention. Each sculptural bottle is handblown in Barcelona, making them lovely objets d’art to adorn your dressing table or nightstand.

Picture this silver-plated fruit bowl giving your stone fruits the season they deserve. Red jasper accents only add to the sculptural glamour, as does the satin-finished interior. Fill it with peaches and artichokes, or let it remarkably elevate the mail pile on your entryway table.

A two-tone, Portuguese cashmere throw that will take them from season to season. Lightweight enough to toss over the arm of a sofa and long enough to cover their toes, what more could they ask for?

Crafted from historic silver brand Christofle in a durable stainless steel with a leather sleeve, this is one mug that won’t get lost in the back of your cabinet. The cup instantly elevates their morning commute, ensuring every moment of their day is wrapped in luxury.

Boroux’s water purification system effectively removes harmful chemicals and bacteria from your hydration routine. With the ability to clean up to 24,000 gallons of water, the four-filter filtration device has been backed by lab-driven results to guarantee you’re hydrating with only the purest water.

Inspired by ancient Chinese teapots, Balmuda’s moonkettle has a traditional look but comes powered by an electric heating system. Unlike standard kettles, this one features a precise temperature control and soothing tone (no annoying whistle) that signals when the water has reached a boil. The base can also keep the water warm for up to 30 minutes, in case you need a second cup.

Bestowing this set of playful designer plates on your favorite host will all but solidify your invite for future parties. Just don’t be surprised when they seem extra eager for the dessert course.

Gianfranco Frattini’s influential designs helped shape the 1950s and 1960s Italian design movement. Naturally, this glass table lamp’s commanding silhouette will bring midcentury style to a loved one’s interiors.

Raise a toast to the end of another year with a set of twisty champagne coupes. This hand-blown pair feels more like sculptures than drinking glasses, so much so that you’ll want to keep them on display for every special occasion. For those who prefer a complete set, there are coordinating wine glasses too.

Does anyone need a specialized aluminum travel case for their barware? No. Will it make your budding mixologist speechless and allow you to win the holidays? Yes. Rimowa’s classic cocktail case is modeled after their best-selling (and status-y) luggage, but this time with a foam-padded interior to comfortably hold everything you need to make a martini at 35,000 feet. It’s even TSA-approved.

If they’ll do just about anything to achieve the perfect cup of joe, this Dutch-designed coffee maker brews them strong and isn’t lacking in the looks department. Commerce writer Julia Harrison called its sister appliance, the Technivorm Moccamaster’s One Cup “an attractive, streamlined, functional device.”

Sabre is universally approved by design lovers from all over—including Hawkins New York founder Paul Denoly, who describes the flatware as a “rare kind of design that looks just as natural in a Parisian café as it does on your designer friend’s kitchen table.” Think of this full set as the gift of a more elevated tablescape.

Anyone who’s a serious caffeine enthusiast will enjoy this sleek stainless steel espresso machine with over 1,000 five-star reviews from Breville. It’s got features like an interactive touch screen for easy drink selection as well as a built-in grinder, steam wand, and an ultra-fast heating system to serve up steaming cups of café-quality java from the comfort of your own kitchen.

When we asked our editor-in-chief Amy Astley about one of her latest MVP purchases, she pointed us in the direction of this next-level candle from Loewe. “I love the chunkiness, the heft, [and] the jumbo size,” she says. Amy adds that when the candle has burned down, “you can keep the vessel for anything you want—and it’s nice that this one isn’t delicate and teeny.” (Looking for more candle inspo? Check out our guide to the best-smelling candles with faves from Diptyque, Voluspa, Boy Smells and more.)

The iconic Christofle egg screams all out luxury while embracing chrome home aesthetics to the highest degree. Inside the stainless steel orb lives 24 pieces of silverware that your gift recipient will cherish and eventually pass down to generations to come.

It won’t really matter what record they’re spinning, any vinyl—whether it’s The Cure, Olivia Rodrigo, or Kacy Musgraves—sounds (and looks) good on Marantz’s sculptural turntable. But just know that the device is also designed with superior audio quality in mind, with tech to help deaden vibrations and keep distortions at bay.

Put your best gifting foot forward with this Jonathan Anderson-designed bag that takes inspiration from a classic loafer silhouette. In vibrant rust, it’s a stylish way to bring some cheer to your winter ensembles, especially if neutrals dominate the majority of their wardrobe.

Cashmere socks are something they wouldn’t normally buy for themselves but you know they’ll adore. This Comme Si assortment has them set throughout winter, spanning both neutrals and vibrant hues that look equally attractive peeking out of sneakers or dress shoes.

Lauren Manoogian’s headscarf is the natural progression of summer’s bandana trend. The cotton wool blend protects your head from the winter chill but also comfortably tucks underneath a hooded jacket for added warmth.

Hand-carved and delicately detailed, JW Anderson’s latest bag charm was made in collaboration with—who else?—Mouseman. The British woodworkers and Dior creative director Jonathan Anderson have joined forces to bring a little whimsy to your work bag.

There are very few wardrobe scenarios where we think a gold bangle from David Yurman wouldn’t instantly elevate things. And if the golden girl (or guy!) in your life is already a collector, adding to their stack will make them feel so loved.

This pajama button-up is less mature and more casual, quiet luxury. Your giftee will look forward to spending off-work hours draped in its organic cotton silhouette, either relaxing and catching up on the latest shows or showing off in the Whole Foods produce section.

Ormaie’s 18-12 pays homage to a joyous day in December (with the date corresponding to December 18th). The scent is robust with floral and fruity notes—fresh rose, lychee, bergamot, and mandarin to name a few—for a vibrant blend that’s guaranteed to attract many compliments. We think Memphis Milano design lovers will be drawn to this attractive bottle, thanks to its cool geometric topper.

And for that special someone who's constantly hopping on and off flights, upgrade their old luggage to a sturdy Rimowa, the brand beloved by everyone from Rihanna to Robert Pattinson.

If they spend their weekends connecting with nature, a tent that’s both durable and design-forward will bring comfort to their outdoor adventures. Snow Peak’s Alpha Breeze tent comfortably fits up to four campers, includes an extended vestibule, and has a lightweight design that makes it easy to trek through even rocky terrains.

A trip upstate is always a welcome surprise. We recommend checking into the Six Bells Countryside Inn. The hotel is a favorite in our curated guide of fall and winter travel destinations, and it was designed in collaboration with New York design firm GrecoDeco and also named an AD Great Design Hotel Award winner in 2025.
