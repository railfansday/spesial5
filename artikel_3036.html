On Holding AG (ONON -7.54%) reported record quarterly net sales and its highest underlying gross margin to date, attributing results to the execution of its premium strategy across all regions and product categories. Management noted that the company is currently in the initial phase of its largest innovation cycle, highlighted by the rollout of new supercritical foam technologies and the introduction of its LightSpray manufacturing process. The company is actively diversifying into new athletic categories, including football and golf, while expanding its retail footprint and digital engagement with younger consumer segments. Strategic investments in technology and infrastructure are intended to scale operations for long-term growth targets through the end of the decade.
