If You Had Invested $10,000 in the Vanguard S&P 500 ETF (VOO) in 2016, Here's What You Would Have Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

It was a big decade for the Vanguard S&P 500 ETF, but there's one thing to keep in mind.

Ten years ago, a $10,000 investment made into the Vanguard S&P 500 ETF (VOO -0.38%) might have sounded pretty boring. Instead, that exchange-traded fund (ETF) has turned into one of the best things people could have put their money into. Because that $10,000 would have turned into roughly $41,900 over the past decade.

Here's the math. The Vanguard S&P 500 ETF's average annual return over the past decade is 15.3% with dividends reinvested. Plug that into the compounding growth formula, and when you account for rounding of the return, the answer is consistent with the $41,900 figure above.

You would have quadrupled your money over 10 years from a fund that simply tracks the S&P 500. Who said investing had to be complex?

But before you build a retirement plan around that expectation, keep in mind that a 15% average annual return is far above the S&P 500's historical performance. Over the past decade, the index has returned closer to 10% annually.

So if you rerun the math with a more typical return of 10%, the same $10,000 grows to about $25,900 over 10 years. Still a strong number, but much lower than the past decade.

The bottom line, however, is that Vanguard S&P 500 ETF remains one of the best and cheapest core holdings for long-term investors.
