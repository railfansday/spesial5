Hull City hero reveals unusual good-luck charm after dramatic win over 'excellent' Brighton

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Tigers scored twice in stoppage time at the MKM Stadium on Sunday (November 22) to secure their first win since August, with McBurnie netting in the 92nd and 95th minutes.

But while McBurnie grabbed the headlines with his late double, Hull's victory owed much to an outstanding performance from Tzolakis, who kept Brighton at bay during a difficult first half.

Jakirovic admitted his side had been made to suffer before the break, with the goalkeeper producing several important saves.

Speaking to Sky Sports, he said: "We played against an excellent team today, very high intensity. In the first half we survived and Konstantinos Tzolakis saved us four or five times.

"We had more stability in the second half. We won 2-0 and I'm extremely happy because we wait almost three months [for another win] which is unbelievable.

"Congratulations to my boys because it was not an easy game but in the end we won."

Jakirovic also felt his side had taken advantage of the opportunity presented by Costinha's 89th-minute dismissal.

"The second yellow card I think there is no problem about that and we used that moment," he said.

McBurnie was quick to acknowledge the contribution of his goalkeeper, admitting Hull had ridden their luck against a Brighton side he described as a top team.

Speaking to Sky Sports, he said: "We haven't lost a game at home. They are a top team and we knew it was going to be tough.

"We rode our luck and we have the big man [Tzolakis] to keep the ball out of the net so that's what he's done."

The striker also revealed the unusual good-luck charm he had taken onto the pitch – a toy rabbit belonging to his daughter.

"My daughter when I leave for work every game she makes me take something with me for good luck," he explained.

"Today it was her bun bun because that's what she had in her hand. It's all good fun and I'm sure she will be happy with that one."

McBurnie was equally full of praise for Tzolakis, jokingly referring to him as 'the cat' when speaking to Match of the Day.

"It's his job. That's what he's there for. The cat was brilliant today and we are lucky to have him," he said.

"Brighton are a top team and we knew we were going to be suffering and ride our luck at times, but with a bit of luck and with the cat in net we rode our luck and stayed in the game."

'The football for the goals was brilliant'

McBurnie also reflected on the importance of ending Hull's wait for a victory, although he insisted it had not felt like such a long time because of the team's strong home form.

"Buzzing for the boys because we haven't won for a while," he said.

"It doesn't feel like that because our home run has been really good. To get that three points today was massive."

The Scottish striker was particularly pleased with the way Hull worked the ball for both goals.

Regan Slater and Lucas Gourna-Douath combined to set him up for the opener before Mohamed Belloumi created the second with a run and pass across goal.

"You love those ones, especially the second one when you are passing it into an empty net. It's a lovely feeling," he said.

"If you look at the actual football for the goals it was brilliant football."

The manager also praised Tzolakis again in his BBC Match of the Day interview, describing his performance as ‘unbelievable’ and highlighting the importance of the clean sheet.
