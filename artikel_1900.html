Prediction: Vitoria vs Bragantino

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Hoping to move within touching distance of the Copa Libertadores spots on Thursday night, Bragantino will travel to Vitoria.

Ahead of Thursday’s Serie A showdown, you can find all of the Vitoria vs Bragantino H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, while Vitoria might have dragged themselves over the finishing line last season as they eventually clung to their Serie A status, Jair Ventura’s men have another major battle on their hands. Last seen heading into November’s international break with a nightmare display at Barradao as they slumped to a 3-0 drumming against Fluminense, the Bahia-based outfit are searching for a desperate boost here. However, signing off October with a 2-0 defeat away at Coritiba, Vitoria have claimed just two points from their previous five Serie A appearances. Opening the midweek fixtures holding just a one-point buffer over the drop zone, Thursday’s hosts have also put just a sole win on the board since the start of September. It should also be highlighted, Ventura’s camp have drawn back-to-back blanks for the first time since the end of August.

Both still recovering from respective long-term knee injuries, Vitoria will once again be without the duo of Nathan Mendes and one-time Braga youngster Fabiano on Thursday night. However, serving a one-match Serie A suspension prior to November’s international break, Argentinian defender Emanuel Britez will return to Ventura’s squad.

Despite notching just a sole strike across all competitions since the middle of July, former Fortaleza striker Renato Kayzer could be handed a full recall and the chance to spearhead Vitoria’s attack. Completing a loan switch from Bragantino over the summer, Luan Candido will feature against his parent club here.

As for the visitors, while Vitoria might have limped their way into November’s international break at the heart of a Serie A relegation scrap, Bragantino will simply be looking to carry on from where they left off prior to the fortnight interval. Last seen clinching a 2-1 victory at home against Santos, the Braga Boys could be smelling the chance to make another domestic statement on Thursday night. Likewise, signing off October with a dominant display in Sao Paulo as they cruised their way to a thumping 3-0 win against Chapecoense, Bragantino could move within touching distance of the Copa Libertadores spots here. Suffering just a sole defeat from any of their previous six consecutive Serie A appearances, Vagner Mancini’s side also secured a 2-0 victory when they met Vitoria for their reverse top-flight meeting on May 17th.

Shown what was a costly booking against Santos prior to November’s international break, Brazilian marksman Lucas Barbosa will serve a one-match suspension here. Both recovering from respective long-term knee injuries, Mancini’s camp will once again be without the duo of Davi Gomes and former Sao Paulo youngster Rodriguinho.

Bagging four goals in his back-to-back appearances prior to November’s international break, Wallace Yan could play a leading role in the final third. Now in his second campaign at the Cicero de Souza Marques, veteran goalkeeper Tiago Volpi will start between the sticks on Thursday night.

While Bragantino might be able to move within touching distance of the Copa Libertadores spots on Thursday night, we could be set for a nervy affair. With the Braga Boys registering just a sole strike in their previous three Serie A showdowns outside of Sao Paulo, chances could come at a premium here. It should also be highlighted, marking their return from November’s international break sat in the Serie A drop zone, Vitoria have also drawn consecutive blanks for the first time since the end of August.
