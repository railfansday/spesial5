Let’s take a look at why you should consider adding the Amex Gold to your wallet.

While not everyone will be targeted for the Amex Gold's top welcome offer of 100,000 points, it's the highest welcome offer we've ever seen on the card. Previously, the highest number of points you could earn on this card was 60,000 points (offer no longer available).

This offer is worth up to $2,000, based on TPG’s November 2026 valuations. To get the most value from your welcome bonus, you'll want to take advantage of Amex's 20 transfer partners, including standout options like Air Canada Aeroplan, Air France-KLM Flying Blue and ANA Mileage Club.

TPG credit cards editor Olivia Mittak previously saved thousands by transferring 60,000 points to Air Canada Aeroplan to book a lie-flat United Polaris business-class seat from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) to Frankfurt International Airport (FRA), plus $59 in taxes and fees.
