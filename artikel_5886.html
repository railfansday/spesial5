US launches unarmed ICBM with Trump in Asia in routine test

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

President Donald Trump arrives in Shenzhen, China, for historic talks with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin. Former CIA station chief Dan Hoffman joins 'Fox & Friends First' to analyze the trilateral talks. He also weighs in after the U.S. launches defensive strikes against Iran.

The U.S. military carried out a test launch of an unarmed Minuteman III intercontinental ballistic missile (ICBM) as President Donald Trump was traveling aboard Air Force One to China.

The Air Force Global Strike Command said the "routine test" late Tuesday at Vandenberg Space Force Base in California, outside of Los Angeles, "was scheduled years ago and is not in response to world events."

"The ability to conduct these rigorous, realistic tests relies on the dedication of our Airmen and is foundational to ensuring the safety and surety of the Minuteman system," U.S. Air Force Gen. S.L. Davis, commander of Air Force Global Strike Command, said in a statement.

"Test opportunities, including this most recent launch, assess the entirety of the ICBM enterprise and provide various government agencies data to inform ICBM sustainment, engineering, and lifecycle analysis efforts," Air Force Global Strike Command added. "AFGSC has completed over 300 Minuteman III test launches to date."

Following the test launch, Chinese President Xi Jinping greeted Trump as he arrived in Shenzhen, China, on Wednesday.

The Air Force describes the LGM-30G Minuteman III missile as the "land-based component of the U.S. nuclear triad, providing a responsive and survivable strategic deterrent.

"The current force consists of 400 Minuteman III missiles dispersed at F.E. Warren Air Force Base, Wyoming; Malmstrom Air Force Base, Montana; and Minot Air Force Base, North Dakota," the Air Force said.

"Missile combat crews, consisting of two officers, are on alert around the clock in underground Launch Control Centers. A robust and redundant communications network ensures the president and U.S. Strategic Command have reliable, direct contact with each crew," the Air Force also said, noting that the Minuteman III system was first deployed in 1970 and has "evolved to meet new security challenges through more than 60 years of continuous service."

The last unarmed Minuteman III test launch happened on May 20 at Vandenberg Space Force Base.

An unarmed Minuteman III intercontinental ballistic missile launches during a previous operational test at Vandenberg Air Force Base in California. (Defense Media Activity/Web.mil)

"This launch allows us to authenticate the complete performance profile of the Minuteman III," Lt. Col. Karrie Wray, commander of the 576th Flight Test Squadron, said at the time. "It confirms the precision of the system, ensuring the reentry vehicle can be delivered to its target with pinpoint accuracy."

Fox News' Lucas Tomlinson contributed to this report.
