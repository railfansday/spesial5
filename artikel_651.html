Netflix’s ‘The Murder of JonBenét Ramsey’ Haunting Trailer Shines Spotlight on Melissa McCarthy and Clive Owen

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The limited series from showrunner Richard LaGravenese releases its 10 episodes in December.

That’s the conclusion drawn by the trailer for the newest take on the infamous JonBenét Ramsey murder cold case.

Netflix’s The Murder of JonBenét Ramsey, which recruited Melissa McCarthy and Clive Owen for the roles of JonBenét’s parents Patsy and John Bennett Ramsey, respectively, will get its turn to tell the enduring, unsolved mystery of JonBenét Ramsey’s murder when the limited series releases all 10 episodes on Dec. 10.

“I was fascinated by the challenge of doing something that was a murder mystery without an ending, without being solved,” says showrunner Richard LaGravenese with the first look announcement, which included the trailer and new photos (below). There has been curiosity around how the latest screen take would handle adapting the true crime story of the devastating murder of 6-year-old Ramsey — the beauty pageant winner was found strangled in the basement of her Boulder, Colorado, family home on Dec. 26, 1996. Thirty years later, mystery around her death remains. Netflix’s recent doc Cold Case: Who Killed JonBenét Ramsey from filmmaker Joe Berlinger, in November 2024, pointed the finger at the Boulder Police Department as why the case remains unsolved.

The trailer (below) for The Murder of JonBenét Ramsey plays out like a haunting thriller mystery, as it traces the steps of the well-known-by-know investigation by the Boulder Police, which includes questioning Patsy and John, contaminated evidence and the case becoming a tabloid sensation. “The country is looking for an end to this story that makes sense to them,” says District Attorney Hunter, played by Shea Whigham in the trailer.

Owen and McCarthy are “unlike anything you’ve seen them do before,” said Netflix in its marketing for the series. Owen said of the series’ take, “It felt to me that if there was one thing that you could really hold onto as an anchor for the whole thing, it was the fact that it’s a tragedy. It’s not a whodunit; a little girl died.” McCarthy added, “We all forgot that these were parents and they loved their children. And one of them was taken in such an unimaginably violent, terrible way. That should be the crux of the story, and it became almost non-existent. It became like it didn’t even matter. We were just watching this exciting tale of who did it.”

The Murder of JonBenét Ramsey was greenlit back in 2024 at Paramount+ before Paramount’s takeover by Skydance. Production on the series wrapped in early 2025 under the title Unspeakable: The Murder of JonBenét Ramsey. The intent was to have it be the first installment of an anthology series, like Netflix’s Monster franchise, to examine unsolved crimes. But after Skydance took over Paramount, the series was quietly shelved. 101 Studios moved from Paramount to NBCUniversal earlier this year, following Taylor Sheridan, and the series then landed at Netflix this summer.

Here’s the logline for The Murder of JonBenét Ramsey: “The series centers on one of the most infamous unsolved murder cases in American history and the devastating personal and public reckoning that followed the death of JonBenét Ramsey on Christmas night in 1996.”

Rounding out the cast is Emily Mitchell in the role of JonBenét Ramsey and Angus Caldwell as her brother, Burke Ramsey. The additional cast includes a group of local and federal investigators, played by an ensemble including Garrett Hedlund, Will Patton, Whigham and Alison Pill.

Jeremy Bobb, John Billingsley, Josh Stamberg, Margo Martindale, Tzi Ma, Owen Teague, Clifton Collins Jr., Jaime Ray Newman, Rory Cochrane and Chris Bauer also star.

LaGravenese (Behind the Candelabra, Freedom Writers, A Family Affair) is the showrunner, with Anne Sewitsky (Presumed Innocent) as a director and executive producer. Harrison Query and Tommy Wallach, who co-wrote the series, also executive produce. Executive producers also include David C. Glasser, Ron Burkle, Bob Yari and David Hutkin for 101 Studios as well as McCarthy and Sewitsky.
