Bills news: No matter who is at QB, Buffalo’s defense is playing great

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Buffalo Bills had one of the league's worst defenses through the first seven weeks of the season, ranking 30th in scoring defense (28.5 points per game), 31st in yards allowed per game (386.8) and 31st in total defense.

Buffalo couldn't get off the field on third down and allowed teams to hang around by surrendering 1.5 touchdowns per fourth quarter heading into their bye week, the worst rate for any defense in any season going back to at least the 1977 campaign.

But since their Week 7 bye, the Bills defense has done a complete turnaround, allowing the fewest points and second-fewest yards among teams that have played three games.

Today's edition of Buffalo Rumblinks leads off by examining how Buffalo's defense has been able to pull off a Jekyll and Hyde, regardless of who has been the opposing quarterback.

Check out the news items below and then let us know your thoughts on the sudden improvement on the defensive side of the ball in the comments section.

Buffalo's defense has not allowed a touchdown over its last 33 possessions dating back to the first quarter of a 36-10 home win over the Baltimore Ravens in Week 8.

The Bills have surrendered only 28 total points over their last three wins, which have come almost exclusively against backup quarterbacks, a trend that will continue on Sunday when the last-place Miami Dolphins come to Highmark Stadium.

Regardless of who has lined up at quarterback, Jim Leonhard's defense is on a roll, with this recent run of success fueled by an increase in turnovers and sacks, a decrease in third-down conversions and improved communication in the secondary.
