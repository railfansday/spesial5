Paul George shares blunt gesture to 76ers fan amid Celtics rout

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Paul George made a memorable return to Philadelphia on Tuesday night just four months after being traded to the Celtics and it's clear there is no love lost between him and at least one Sixers fan.

The veteran forward appeared to flip off a person in the crowd at Xfinity Mobile Arena in the fourth quarter amid Boston's 123-101 blowout win over the 76ers.

The move came at the end of a superb scoring night for George as he erupted for a game-high 36 points against his former team after hearing boos from the Philadelphia crowd all night.

George spoke about the interaction that led to him flipping off the fan in the fourth quarter.

"Yeah, there was a fan being an [explicit]," George said. "The ball rolled to him and he looked me dead in my eyes, dropped the ball on the ground as if I'm a dog to go fetch or go get it instead of passing the ball to me like a normal fan would do. And so yeah, so then I rolled him the middle finger in return."

The Sixers traded George and two first-round picks to the Celtics for Jaylen Brown in July just two years after signing him to a four-year max contract in the summer of 2024. George had two underwhelming seasons in Philadelphia which were riddled by injury woes before the team opted to deal him away for a younger All-Star in Brown.

George spoke about his emotions on returning to Philadelphia before the game.

"Had I been here eight, nine, 10 years, it'd be a different story," George said. "I was there two years. Obviously, we didn't have the best of luck. We had a great partnership in terms of my ability to stay healthy and on the floor. So that kind of played into a lot of it. Obviously, it's a tremendous fan base. A great sports town to play in."

George and the Celtics won't face off against the 76ers again until January 21 in what will be Brown's first game as a visitor in Boston.
