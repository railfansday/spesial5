Lakers star keeps daughters close with heartfelt sweatshirt after big win

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Luka Doncic wore a sweatshirt to honor his daughters after the Lakers' 131-123 win over the Suns on Sunday, their eighth straight win.

The custom-made sweatshirt featured his daughters' names inside hearts, with 10-month-old Olivia's name written on a pink heart and three-year-old Gabriela's name on a purple heart.

Luka Doncic of the Los Angeles Lakers speaks to the media after a game against the Phoenix Suns in Phoenix, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

When asked about the sweatshirt, the Slovenian superstar didn't hesitate.

"Oh, it's just my two daughters," said Doncic, who had 43 points, nine assists, three rebounds and one steal against the Suns. "Just trying to represent them. Just trying to carry them with me."

Luka Doncic (77) of the Los Angeles Lakers shoots against the Phoenix Suns in Phoenix, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

Doncic was also wearing colorful beaded bracelets made by his eldest daughter, as he does whenever he's not playing.

Doncic is in a custody battle over his two daughters with his ex-fiancée, Anamaria Goltes. He filed a "Hague Convention" petition before the season to have his two daughters brought from Slovenia to the United States.

Luka Doncic of the Los Angeles Lakers looks on during a game against the Phoenix Suns in Phoenix, Arizona, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

After spending the summer in Slovenia alongside his daughters, including flying the Lakers to his home country for a mini-camp so he wouldn't have to be apart from them, he took to Instagram before returning to Los Angeles ahead of training camp to post a message in honor of them.

"G+O, no matter where in the world your Dad is, there is not a day when you wouldn't be in my heart," Doncic wrote in Slovenian. "Wherever I go, you are always with me. I love you!

Doncic, who's having an MVP-caliber season, entered Sunday's game leading the league in scoring with 38.6 points, 7.5 rebounds and 7.6 assists per game.

On Sunday, Doncic scored 40 or more points for the seventh time this season for the Lakers, who are tied for the best record in the league at 12-2.
