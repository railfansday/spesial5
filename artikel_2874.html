Player Grades: Cavs vs Knicks – Donovan Mitchell drops 37 points in loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Cleveland Cavaliers fell 109-97 to the New York Knicks on the second night of a back-to-back.

Grades are based on our usual expectations for each player.

Mitchell looks like he's finding the right balance between scoring and passing through the last two games. He's been aggressive first, allowing his playmaking to come as a result of the defense overreacting to his gravity. That's led to some efficient games passing the ball, but we were all waiting for Mitchell to have a scorching hot game shooting it.

Mitchell went off for a season-high 37 points, and he was the catalyst behind Cleveland's momentary comeback in the fourth quarter. Mitchell was one of the only Cavaliers who had a pulse on offense tonight.
