Bears have a chance to prove a key prediction wrong in Week 11 vs. Saints

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

With newly acquired defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons in the fold, the Chicago Bears' defensive outlook and philosophy have undergone a major shift for the better.

That's not just from an individual perspective, though Simmons has been great in that regard, posting three tackles for loss, two sacks, and two passes defended in his two weeks with the Bears. The unit as a whole has benefited from the increased double teams the All-Pro tackle has faced, allowing just 4.5 yards per play when he's been on the field and ranking in the top 10 in EPA/play and success rate allowed since Simmons got here.

But there's one aspect in which, while improved, the Bears have remained somewhat middling at: defending the run. That's why NFL.com's Brooke Cersosimo forecasts New Orleans Saints running back Travis Etienne might be a problem for Chicago on Sunday.

"Etienne returned to action last week after missing five games with a hamstring injury and looked rusty — to say the least — rushing for 14 yards on eight carries. He turns things around in dramatic fashion against the Bears on Sunday with his first 100-yard rushing performance and touchdown of the season," Cersosimo wrote for her bold prediction for this week in NFL action.

When healthy, Etienne is one of the better dual-threat backs in the league. But nothing about his performance in his first season with the Saints suggests he's either of those things (142 rushing yards on 38 carries). In fact, no one in New Orleans running backs room has done much worth writing home about: through nine games, quarterback Tyler Shough leads the team with 187 rushing yards. That's ugly.

The Saints have a true running-by-committee squad, ravaged by a combination of injuries and ineffectiveness. So it's unlikely any of them gets enough action on their own to approach 100 yards rushing unless one of them gets hot. From what we've seen, there's nothing to suggest that'll happen. Plus, if the Bears come out hot on offense and put Shough and the Saints in catch-up mode, you can forget about Etienne as a rushing threat. That, no doubt, is part of what the Bears hope to do.

But it's also important to note just how big a deal this Simmons acquisition has been for Chicago.

Because yes, the Bears defense has been pretty uninspiring against the run for the past couple of years, and a player with Etienne's skill set could definitely have given them trouble in a game like this before Simmons arrived.

These days, though, the Bears have the look of a squad bent on changing that reputation (as long as Simmons stays healthy and on the field himself). The presence of an elite defensive tackle has everyone playing better, especially speedy linebacker Devin Bush.

Again, there's nothing wrong with a bold prediction or two. But let's stash an Etienne 100-yard game in the "highly unlikely" category.
