Harden’s 51 points leads Cavs over the Pacers

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

James Harden put on a vintage performance, dropping an electric 51 points, Donovan Mitchell added 20 of his own, and Mario Hezonja put up a double-double off the bench to lead the Cleveland Cavaliers to a 128-119 victory over the visiting Indiana Pacers in an NBA Cup matchup. Cleveland sits just behind the Miami Heat in NBA Cup Group Stage play, while the Pacers fall to 1-2.

Harden finished 15-26 from the floor, including 7 for 11 from beyond the arc, to go with six assists and six rebounds. Oh, and in classic fashion, he was also 14-14 from the free throw line.

Cleveland now sits just over .500 at 9-8, beating an Indiana team that was still without star guard Tyrese Haliburton. The Cavs did not have forward Evan Mobley, who is undergoing treatment for a great toe sprain. Peyton Watson returned from a one-game absence due to a back injury, and provided 15 points, four rebounds, and three assists.

The two teams were neck and neck in the first half, with the Cavs holding a slight one-point advantage at the break. But it was the 37-year-old Harden who took over in the third quarter, helping Cleveland outscore Indiana 43-23 in the frame. Harden's 51-point effort, the 26th of his career, puts him past Kobe Bryant for third on the NBA's all-time 50-point game list.

"My idol, the person I watched growing up," Harden said in a postgame interview when told he passed Bryant on the all-time list.

Harden has been playing much better of late following his slow start, something that the former MVP has built into his conditioning strategy of playing into game shape. Mitchell's 20 points mostly came in the second half, but he helped apply pressure in that "Cavalanche" of a third quarter that allowed Cleveland to pull away.

While most of the attention will go toward the 50-point effort, Hezonja's ten points and ten rebounds off the bench continue a stretch of solid play. When the Cavs play well, and with energy, chances are that Hezonja is part of the reason why. He has made smart passes, rebounded the basketball well, and seems very comfortable on offense getting to his spots around the paint. It won't be the headline of this game, but Cleveland must be pleased with their signing of Hezonja this past offseason.

The Cavs also moved to 2-1 in NBA Cup play and put themselves in position to advance out of the Group Stage, just behind the Heat. Cleveland will not play this weekend, with their next game coming on Monday in a rematch against the Chicago Bulls. Tip-off is at 8:00 p.m EST from the Windy City.
