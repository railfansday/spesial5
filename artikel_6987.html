Coder Technologies Inc.: Demand Generation Program Manager (EMEA)

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Coder is the leading platform for AI development infrastructure, and we’re growing fast in EMEA. We’re looking for a demand generation marketer to help us build predictable, repeatable pipeline growth in the region.

You’ll own day-to-day execution of the EMEA programs that create, capture, and convert demand, across media sponsorships, paid campaigns, vendor channels, and in-region nurture. This is a hands-on role for someone who can manage multiple programs independently, keep them running on a consistent cadence, and turn performance data into clear recommendations on what to scale.

You’ll work closely with our International Marketing Lead to execute our go-to-market strategy in the EMEA market, and partner with the global Demand Gen lead, Paid Media Manager, Integrated Marketing, ABM, and SDR team to ensure every program reaches the right audience and turns into meaningful business results.

Run day-to-day execution of EMEA sponsorships, vendor programs, and paid campaigns against the regional plan.

Own vendor relationships and program logistics, including timelines, deliverables, budgets, launches, and follow-up.

Research and recommend new EMEA channels, sponsorships, vendors, and campaign opportunities to test.

Partner with the Paid Media Manager to launch and optimize EMEA paid campaigns, from awareness through conversion and retargeting.

Partner with marketing ops to execute lifecycle and always-on nurture programs that move EMEA leads from MQL to opportunity.

Support ABM programs for EMEA target accounts

Partner with the SDR team to build EMEA lead follow-up plays to improve conversion rates, and use their feedback to refine targeting and programs.

Track program performance against pipeline goals (MQLs, meetings, sourced and influenced pipeline, cost per opportunity) and recommend where to optimize, scale, or stop.

Document processes and learnings so successful programs become repeatable playbooks for the region.

4+ years of hands-on experience in B2B demand generation, digital marketing, paid media, or a related field.

Experience marketing in EMEA markets such as the UK and Germany.

Experience managing media sponsorships, paid advertising programs, or vendor-led campaigns across multiple channels, with a track record of getting more value from sponsorship budgets.

The ability to independently own programs from planning through execution and reporting.

Strong project management skills across multiple campaigns, deadlines, and stakeholders, and clear communication when things shift.

Comfort working with data. You can pull reports from tools like HubSpot, Salesforce, or HockeyStack, assess performance, and turn the numbers into actionable recommendations.

Experience partnering with sales or SDR teams to turn marketing programs into meetings and pipeline.

Curiosity about using AI tools and workflows to speed up processes, analysis, and execution.

Experience with ABM, programmatic advertising, or intent platforms such as 6Sense, LinkedIn, CommonRoom, Vector.

Experience managing programs and workflows in tools such as Asana.

Experience marketing to developers, platform engineers, or other technical buyers.

Experience in B2C marketing or industries outside traditional B2B demand gen. We value people who bring ideas from different playbooks.
