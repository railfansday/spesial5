Construction on the 20-acre site began June 25, 2024. The facility includes areas for charging, storage, maintenance, bus wash stations, parts storage and space for cleaning and detailing vehicles. It also provides bus dispatching and driver training.

“We’ve built a facility that gives our teams the technology to serve riders better today and the room to grow with Miami-Dade,” Department of Transportation and Public Works Director and CEO Stacy Miller said in a statement. “This is how we build a transit system worthy of the trust our riders place in us every day.”

The facility includes rapid charging stations and rooftop charging platforms, according to Hypower, the station’s utility contractor.

Because lithium-ion batteries pose a fire hazard, the structure will be heat-insulated to prevent damage should a fire erupt, WSP Senior Vice President and Alternative Delivery Project Director Angel André Chavarria told Smart Cities Dive in 2024. Sprinklers will activate in an area just around the fire location rather than the entire building, he said. WSP provided design and construction services for the project.

The site includes a reclaimed water system for landscaping.

The operations center can also serve as an emergency command center for DTPW during major storms and other emergencies.
