What next for Blackburn Rovers as Tony Mowbray steps back? New boss stance, impact on players and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It marks the second time Mowbray has done so, temporarily withdrawing from his position at Birmingham City in February 2024 after being diagnosed with bowel cancer.

The news has been met with an outpouring of support from across English football, and deservedly so. Mowbray is one of the most universally liked figures in our game, and all hope to see him recover speedily.

Blackburn Rovers fans have sent their best wishes and got behind Mowbray first, of course. However, the news has understandably led to questions over what happens next at Ewood Park.

At the time of writing, it appears Blackburn Rovers will not be appointing a new head coach – not permanently, anyway. The wording of the club’s statement confirming Mowbray’s situation said he is ‘stepping back’, not stepping down, leaving, or anything similar.

First-team coaches Damien Johnson and Mike Dodds are taking over duties for the time being, and no further statement will be made, the club says.

The situation played out similarly upon Mowbray’s withdrawal from duties at Birmingham City. His long-term assistant Mark Venus took charge in his absence before the boss took formal medical leave and Gary Rowett was appointed on an interim basis.

This is a new and developing situation, but Rovers’ statement indicates a new boss will not be coming in at this moment in time. Trusted local reporter Elliott Jackson has since stated on X that the club ‘are certainly not looking for a successor right now’.

Mowbray cuts a hugely popular figure at Ewood Park. After relegation in 2016/17, he led them back from League One and consolidated their place back in the Championship before moving on at the end of his contract in 2022.

There were some reservations over his return though. It felt like an easy and crowd-pleasing move, but some questioned whether Mowbray could replicate his previous success after his absence and ill-fated second stint as West Brom boss.

However, Mowbray quickly made his presence felt at Blackburn Rovers, and they have overachieved in the first 15 games of the campaign. A side widely touted as relegation candidates now sits 13th in the table, three points off the play-offs and with big wins over top sides like West Ham, Millwall and Middlesbrough.

Mowbray has brought forward-thinking back to Ewood Park. It has seen creative aces like Todd Cantwell and Dapo Afolayan thrive, and star striker Andri Gudjohnsen has benefitted massively. Furthermore, the boss’ track record with developing youngsters has helped Kristi Montgomery come on leaps and bounds.

The 62-year-old has given Rovers fans something to celebrate again after tough years with poor football and growing frustration towards the ownership. Of course, hostility towards the hierarchy has not waned, but the quality of the football on display and positivity surrounding Mowbray’s return have made for a welcome change.

This is an unforeseen development that Blackburn Rovers have no choice but to deal with. Losing Mowbray, even if only temporarily, is a big blow, and it will impact the players.

Johnson and Dodds will focus on offering continuity. It will be smart to stick to Mowbray’s methods and a similar tactical plan, but they must be prepared to make bold decisions and necessary changes if form does falter.

Then, there’s the responsibility of the club hierarchy. It appears an external successor is not going to be named, but if Mowbray can not return to the dugout, the likes of Balaji Rao, Steve Curwood and Rudy Gestede have to be prepared with a plan to ensure it doesn’t derail their season.

When Birmingham City were met with the same situation, their initial action to promote Venus as interim boss did not work. Then, they acted too late to bring in an external replacement, and Rowett could not save them from relegation.

It is an awful predicament for Rovers, but they must be prepared for all scenarios to protect the club.

In the short term, though, Johnson, Dodds, the rest of the coaching staff and the Blackburn Rovers squad will be determined to deliver a derby day win over rivals Burnley for Mowbray as he recovers.
