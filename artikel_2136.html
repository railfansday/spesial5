5 managers Blackburn Rovers must eye now after devastating Tony Mowbray news

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Tony Mowbray has stepped down as Blackburn Rovers head coach due to health issues, and the hunt for his replacement has begun at Ewood Park.

Just a couple of years after stepping down as the head coach of Birmingham City to battle bowel cancer, Mowbray has had to make the unfortunate decision to step down from his role as Blackburn boss due to health issues.

Mowbray issued a heartfelt message to Rovers supporters upon the announcement of his resignation, which can only be described as an incredibly difficult situation for everyone involved, as we at Football League World wish him the best.

Blackburn travel down the road to Burnley next for an enthralling encounter at Turf Moor, with Damien Johnson and Mike Dodds taking charge of first-team duties until a new head coach can be appointed.

As the hunt for a new boss begins, FLW has taken a look at five potential candidates who could realistically assume control at Ewood Park next.

Former Irish forward Damien Duff began his coaching career with Shamrock Rovers in his native country before working as an assistant coach for both Celtic and the Republic of Ireland men's national team.

He took his first steps as a head coach with Shelbourne in 2021, spending three and a half years in charge of the club and guiding them to a first league title in 18 years in 2024.

Duff, who made 220 appearances for Blackburn during his playing career, more than any other club he represented, joined Keith Andrews' coaching staff at Brentford in the summer of 2026, but would surely be tempted by the Blackburn job.

Someone who knows the club inside and out and can seamlessly take the wheel from Mowbray, Duff could be an excellent fit for Blackburn, and simply must be considered.

Austrian coach Gerhard Struber began his career on the touchline in his native country with RB Salzburg, their feeder club, FC Liefering, before joining Wolfsberger and then Barnsley in November 2019.

He would help to keep the Tykes in the Championship in dramatic fashion before leaving Oakwell for the Big Apple with New York Red Bulls just 11 months later, as spells back in Salzburg and then FC Köln preceded his move to Bristol City in the summer of 2025.

After starting bright at Ashton Gate, things quickly derailed, and he was dismissed in March 2026; he has been out of work since, and though likely not a standout candidate for the Blackburn job, he would certainly make for an interesting pick, to say the least.

A name that will be well-known to supporters of Middlesbrough, Nottingham Forest, and Birmingham City, Aitor Karanka is a fairly left-field suggestion, though his connection with Blackburn's current Head of Football Operations, Rudy Gestede, makes him an interesting candidate.

The Spaniard helped to guide Middlesbrough to the top flight in 2016, as spells with Forest, Birmingham, Granada, and Maccabi Tel Aviv followed, where he now works as a Director of Development for the Spanish national team, taking that role in 2025.

Whether he would be open to a return to management at this stage, especially in the Championship, remains to be seen, but his connection with Gestede during their time together with Boro makes him an interesting candidate.

Rob Edwards is a name that will be linked with almost all Championship jobs that become available until he eventually takes on another role, having been dismissed from his post at Wolverhampton Wanderers unexpectedly over the summer.

The Welshman has spent time in charge of the likes of Forest Green Rovers, Watford, Luton Town, Middlesbrough, and Wolves throughout his coaching career, helping to guide Luton to the Premier League in 2023, which remains his most notable achievement in the technical area at this stage.

Edwards reportedly turned down the opportunity to take the Burnley job over the summer, and should he decide that Ewood Park is his next destination, he will almost immediately have the backing of Rovers supporters for his previous decision to reject the Clarets.

Having taken charge of Luton in November 2022 before guiding them to the top flight, Edwards has the opportunity to do the same with Blackburn, and his name will surely be touted for the position.

David Wagner is a name that will put a smile on the faces of many Huddersfield Town supporters, having guided the Terriers to a historic promotion to the Premier League in 2016 before keeping them up against the odds the following campaign.

The German, who was the best man to Jürgen Klopp at the former Liverpool boss' wedding, would spend time with Schalke and Young Boys after Huddersfield before landing at Norwich City in January 2023, helping the Canaries to the Championship play-offs in 2024 before missing out to Leeds United.

He left shortly after the defeat and has been an academy manager at RB Leipzig since 2025, but with an excellent record in the Championship, he would make an interesting candidate and one Blackburn must consider.
