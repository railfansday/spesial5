‘Been written off’ : Blackburn Rovers vs Charlton Athletic predictions

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Despite Tony Mowbray stepping back from his role as head coach of Blackburn Rovers, the side has maintained form with four points in their last two games, including a 1-0 win over Watford on Tuesday night. The points have the East Lancashire side looking up rather than down, sitting in 12th and only two points off the Championship play-offs.

Club captain Todd Cantwell has stepped up impressively for Rovers, netting in each of the last two games to lead by example in a challenging time at Ewood Park. It’s not been easy for the 28-year-old over the last few years, but he is really showing his worth in his third season at the club.

Nathan Jones‘ Charlton Athletic returned to winning ways as they dismantled Lincoln City 4-0, a result that saw both Chris Cohen and Tom Shaw lose their jobs. There’s no hiding that the Addicks will be in and around the Championship relegation fight this season, so beating those sides that will also be down there is of the utmost importance.

The victory displayed just what the Addicks can do, and they will be eager to produce a similar performance this weekend. For Jones’, it is all about pace from the wings, with Tyreece Campbell leading most attacks and delivering six goals from only 2.24 xG in an impressive 2026/27 campaign.

“Blackburn Rovers have consistently been written off this season and have consistently hit back. After losing Tony Mowbray, many thought they would fall off, and instead they have continued on the same course. Rovers don’t have all the quality in the world, but they have composure and control, and they showed real maturity in seeing out their win over Watford.

“Charlton Athletic are an odd entity – completely hot and cold, yet picking up enough points to stay above water. It’s hard to know what they’ll do week on week but heading to Ewood Park is a challenge for any team, regardless of where they sit in the league.

“Even though the Addicks are unpredictable, this looks like a game the hosts can, and should, win.”

“Blackburn Rovers got back to winning ways on Wednesday night, defeating Watford after drawing with rivals Burnley. That will feel like an important win, as it was the first without Tony Mowbray in the dugout after he stepped back from his head coach role for health reasons.

“Rovers have got a pretty good home record this season too, winning five of their eight Ewood Park games. That combined with the form of star attackers Andri Gudjohnsen and Todd Cantwell, who has scored in back-to-back games, should mean they’re confident of beating Charlton Athletic.

“The Addicks will be in a confident mood themselves, though. They put Lincoln City to the sword with a welcome display of ruthless attacking and have now won two of their last three, easing some of the pressure on Nathan Jones and putting some distance between them and the bottom three.

“The issue for the visitors is that since beating West Ham in their first away league game of the season, they have taken just two points on the road from seven games. For that reason, I’m leaning towards another Rovers victory here.”
