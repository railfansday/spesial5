Sheffield Wednesday & Dave Challinor sent automatic promotion warning

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EFL pundits Callum Fowler and Tom Garratt have questioned whether Sheffield Wednesday can win automatic promotion from League One this season ahead of a tough run of upcoming fixtures.

As a new era begins at the club following David Storch's takeover, it has been a turbulent start to life back in League One for Wednesday, with inconsistent results forcing the club into an early season managerial change.

Wednesday parted company with manager Henrik Pedersen last month after a run of just one win from six games, and they did not take long to appoint his replacement, former Stockport County boss Dave Challinor.

The Owls were knocked out of the FA Cup in Challinor's first game in charge as they were beaten 2-1 by League Two side Oldham Athletic before a much-changed side were thrashed 4-0 by Accrington Stanley in the EFL Trophy, but the 51-year-old secured his first victory at the first attempt with a 3-0 win over Plymouth Argyle at Hillsborough on Saturday.

It was not the most convincing of performances from Wednesday, but they produced a clinical display against one of their promotion rivals, with goals from Billy Mitchell, Davis Keillor-Dunn and Di'Shon Bernard getting Challinor's reign up and running.

Ahead of the trip to face South Yorkshire rivals at Oakwell on Friday night, the Owls currently sit fifth in the League One table, and they are just six points behind second-placed Stockport County and leaders Huddersfield Town.

On the latest edition of The Pyramid podcast, Fowler and Garratt discussed Wednesday's automatic promotion hopes, and the pair claimed that it will be easier to assess their top two chances after their challenging upcoming run of fixtures.

When asked if the victory over Plymouth marked the start of a title push for the Owls, Fowler said: "No, but they're definitely finishing top six.

"I want to see what they're like on Friday against us (Barnsley).

"A game like that definitely has potential for them to slip up."

However, Garratt disagreed that the clash at Oakwell would be a test for Challinor's men, responding: "That's a tap-in for them.

"They've got Stevenage away on the Tuesday, that's a tough game, then Reading at home, then Oxford, then Leicester, then Luton.

"After the tap-in on Friday, they've actually got a run where you can start judging them.

"Barnsley away at the minute is like playing a secondary school team."

"I still think Sheffield Wednesday have definitely got a good chance of top two," Garratt added.

Fowler was less confident about Wednesday's automatic promotion prospects, replying: "I think Huddersfield and Stockport will definitely finish in front of them, so that wipes it out for me.

"I think Wednesday will at least finish where they are now."

With a tough fixture schedule and mounting injury list, it could be a tricky first few weeks in charge at Hillsborough for Challinor, but Saturday's victory over Plymouth has certainly got Wednesday supporters excited about what could be ahead under their new manager.

It is difficult to disagree with Fowler that Huddersfield and Stockport are the standout teams in the division at present, while Oxford United have made an impressive start to life under Aaron Ramsey, but there is still plenty of time for the Owls to force their way into the automatic promotion mix.

Given that Challinor led Stockport to two consecutive play-off finishes in League One before his departure from Edgeley Park this summer, Wednesday fans will be confident of at least securing a top six spot, and they may be hoping for even better than that.
