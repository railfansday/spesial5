Finance expert alerts Sheffield Wednesday fans to Dejphon Chansiri development

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Football finance expert Kieran Maguire has revealed an update on former Sheffield Wednesday owner Dejphon Chansiri's situation since being replaced at the club by David Storch and Arise Capital.

Dejphon Chansiri's turbulent ten-year reign at Sheffield Wednesday ended in October 2025 when severe financial mismanagement, unpaid wages, and mounting tax debts forced the club into administration. Following a six-month search for a buyer — and the collapse of an earlier bid — American consortium Arise Capital Partners, led by David Storch, emerged as the preferred bidder.

In May 2026, Storch’s group officially completed its takeover of both the club and Hillsborough Stadium, successfully persuading the EFL to waive a further 15-point penalty and closing the chapter on Chansiri’s divisive tenure. Now, more news Wednesday supporters will be keen to hear about has emerged.

Born into one of Thailand’s wealthiest families, Chansiri owes his fortune to the Thai Union Group, which is the world’s largest canned tuna producer. Though he held no executive role there, his personal ventures spanned Thai real estate, construction, and shipping — companies that largely struggled to generate significant profits.

Per the BBC, upon buying Sheffield Wednesday in 2015, Chansiri promised Premier League football within two years. He initially bankrolled heavy transfer spending through personal director’s loans and relied on external borrowing, including loans secured against Hillsborough.

However, after consecutive play-off heartbreaks, reckless overspending triggered Financial Fair Play breaches, points deductions, and relegation. Without sustainable income, his erratic, loan-heavy funding model eventually collapsed under mounting tax debts and unpaid wages.

The 25 top scorers in the Championship since the second tier was rebranded back in 2004/05, including Billy Sharp and Aleksandar Mitrovic.

It appears as though one of his business ventures has since collapsed a year on from his removal from Sheffield Wednesday after administration, with the bad news continuing for Chansiri after his energy drink company Blue Steel Beverages Ltd was dissolved, per Kieran Maguire.

For Sheffield Wednesday supporters, watching Chansiri’s post-Hillsborough business ventures unravel has to bring a strong sense of poetic justice.

After years of enduring unpaid wages, ticketing controversies, and the humiliation of administration, many fans have to openly savoured the recent news.

His UK-registered energy drink firm Blue Steel Beverages Ltd has finally been dissolved. The company’s quiet demise mirrors the fate of other elusive, Chansiri-linked brands, such as Elev8 and D Taxis, that famously adorned Hillsborough’s shirts and scoreboards without ever establishing a genuine commercial footprint.
