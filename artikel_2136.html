Blackburn Rovers player bombshell emerges ahead of Burnley clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Blackburn Rovers may have to go into Sunday's East Lancashire derby against Burnley without first-choice goalkeeper Balazs Toth, as they sweat the results of a possible broken finger while on international duty with Hungary.

It's been a hectic international break for Blackburn, who went into it sitting 13th in the Championship with 21 points from 15 games. They've punched above their weight all year and have exceeded plenty of pre-season expectations so far.

Toth has helped Rovers stay in games, and at Bristol City especially, win them, making seven saves at Ashton Gate and preventing 2.56 goals in a classic 1-0 away win. His contract is up at the end of the season, so his long-term future is up in the air, but in the meantime, he's an important player at Ewood Park.

So, the possibility of losing him for their biggest game of the campaign on Sunday at Turf Moor against a hapless Burnley side would be a massive blow.

Toth's performances in a Blackburn shirt have led to him nailing down the number one shirt for Hungary. He played every minute in their Nations League campaign, including Tuesday night's draw against Northern Ireland.

That point was enough to see ex-Rovers boss Michael O'Neill lead his side, filled with EFL talents such as Justin Devenny, who scored the equaliser against Hungary, Pierce Charles, Terry Devlin, Isaac Pirce and others, to top Group 2 of League B in the Nations League.

It was a big result for the Green and White Army, as it means they'll be playing in League A in the next Nations League phase. As for Hungary and Toth, his evening got worse as he revealed he was heading to hospital after the game to treat a possible broken finger.

"I most likely broke my finger. I’m on my way from the stadium to the hospital, hoping it might not be too serious," Toth said in an interview translated from Hungarian after the match.

"It’s hard to come to terms with the fact that we didn’t win. We got what we expected from Northern Ireland. We didn’t play that well; we couldn’t break them down; perhaps we lacked a bit of creativity."

Toth and his Hungary side can still earn promotion to League A of the Nations League, with their second-placed finish in their group securing a play-off spot. They'll battle Slovenia, Bosnia and Herzegovina and the Republic of Ireland in March.

It's not been the most encouraging week for Blackburn fans. They were initially worried after talisman Andri Gudjohnsen was substituted at half-time against Luxembourg, although a possible injury concern has since been debunked by Iceland boss Arnar Gunnlaughsson.

Then came the sad news that Tony Mowbray had to step back from his role as head coach due to health concerns. The 62-year-old is loved across the EFL, and has been the man largely credited with Rovers' perceived overachievement so far this season.

And now there's the possibility that Toth may miss the East Lancashire derby and further games due to an injury picked up on international duty.

"U have got to be kidding me, ☠️," one Blackburn fan commented on X. Another said that it was "time to end international breaks forever."

One other supporter claimed that "the universe doesn't want our club to succeed" while the word "cursed" was thrown around, too.

There were some optimistic fans believing that Toth could possibly play on with a broken finger. He did so for around half an hour during the game on Tuesday night, but adrenaline could have got him through that.

Ainsley Pears played on with a broken wrist at times last season while Toth was out himself, so perhaps the Hungarian can soldier on for the time being. At least, that's what some fans hope.

Blackburn's return to action against Burnley has plenty of anticipation. For the first time in what feels like years, Rovers head into a matchup at Turf Moor as the favourites. They haven't won an East Lancashire derby in over 16 years, but that could end on Sunday.

In each of the last four campaigns they've shared a division, the Clarets have been promoted out of the Championship. Burnley have won seven of the last eight meetings, but they head into this one inside the bottom three with one win all season.

More importantly, too, their winless league run at Turf Moor will have eclipsed 400 days come Sunday. Regarding league position and home form, Blackburn have a massive opportunity to earn some rare derby success.

However, these setbacks won't be helping at all, and the last thing Blackburn needs is to head into their biggest game of the season disjointed against a Burnley side who will be as desperate for the win as they ever have been.

Rovers will be hoping that Toth comes out of the hospital unscathed and without a serious injury, as losing him for Sunday could be detrimental to their chances of winning.
