Jay Robinson clause can affect Sam Edozie situation at Southampton

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Southampton have a decision to make regarding the future of winger Sam Edozie in January, although the winger has featured more regularly for Tonda Eckert of late.

Sam Edozie was brought to the club back in 2022 from Man City, but the winger has not always been involved at St. Mary's, having also been on loan to RSC Anderlecht during the 2024/25 season.

Southampton have utilised the 23-year-old sparingly, but in just 321 minutes of Championship action, Edozie has a goal and two assists to his name. He can operate as a winger on either flank and scored an important goal against West Bromwich Albion recently.

Louis Harris is FLW's Southampton fan pundit and he was asked if his stance on offloading Edozie has changed. He's starting to get more game-time at Saints now, so we asked if Saints should avoid moving him on or not.

He replied: "As it stands, he is probably the only realistic replacement when Scienza is injured or not fit. He has glimpses in games where he does well.

"However, it will probably be worth reviewing in January, considering we've got a recall option available for Jay Robinson at Monza.

"He seems to have started the season really well, so a lot could depend on whether Robinson gets recalled as well.

"So, we'll have to see what January looks like as well. Edozie has come in and he has helped.

"But, obviously, you can see the difference in the team when Scienza is playing compared to Edozie."

Southampton need to seriously rethink their attacking options in January, with too much responsibility currently falling on Cyle Larin, who remains their best natural centre-forward. He is also one of the best strikers in the whole of the Championship.

Divin Mubama has struggled to lead the line in his absence, while Lewis Dobbin has arguably been asked to perform a role that does not suit his skill-set, having been used as a focal-point number nine rather than a winger or support striker playing just off of a number nine.

That has at least allowed Edozie to feature more regularly out wide, but the overall balance remains questionable. Whether Southampton send Mubama back and sign a specialist striker or recruit another versatile forward, Larin and creative influences such as Leo Scienza and Finn Azaz need considerably more support.

Kuryu Matsuki is an excellent option for Eckert due to his versatility, but there needs to be a rebalancing of the attack soon, especially if they have serious ambitions of automatic promotion. At Larin's age, they cannot just rely on the Canadian to perform Saturday and Tuesday most weeks.

Edozie arrived at Southampton from Manchester City and showed considerable promise during his first Championship campaign under Russell Martin in 2023/24, registering six goals and three assists in 34 league and play-off appearances.

However, a subsequent loan at Anderlecht and injury problems stalled his progress. He is now beginning to feature more regularly under Eckert, even scoring recently for his first Saints goal since February 2024.
