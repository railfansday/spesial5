Arsenal 1-0 Hull City: Martin Ødegaard rescues Gunners with late winner at the Emirates

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Lucie Turner writes about Arsenal for Read Arsenal and Newcastle United for Read Newcastle. A long-time Arsenal supporter, she also follows rugby, snooker, darts and Formula 1. Her work covers match previews and reports, breaking news, player profiles and opinion pieces.

Arsenal needed a moment of quality from their captain to overcome Hull City and claim a narrow 1-0 Premier League victory at the Emirates Stadium.

Martin Ødegaard scored in the 72nd minute as the hosts struggled to break down a resolute Hull side. It was much like I expected in my match preview, as Arsenal had to work hard to win this game.

The result gives them an extended lead at the top of the table. Ahead of Manchester City’s clash with Nottingham Forest. However, Mikel Arteta will be aware that his team did not play well in this game. It was far from a walk in the park.

Hull arrived at London looking to play an attacking game. Jakirovic’s side were well organised and looked to launch quick counter attacks.

They did have some positive moments in the first half, but Arsenal struggled to get into their attacking rhythm. Kai Havertz couldn’t capitalise on one good chance created by Ødegaard.

The hosts were hopeful of taking the lead after Havertz fell to the ground in the box after being pulled back by Nobel Mendy.

However, referee Farai Hallam’s initial decision was overturned after a VAR review showed Havertz was offside earlier in the attack, so Arsenal’s penalty was cancelled.

Another frustrating moment for the home side, who failed to score during the first half, despite dominating possession.

Arsenal came out strong after the break. However, Hull were still causing problems. Lewie Coyle was also walking a fine line after a yellow card in the first half and a late challenge on Christos Tzolis.

Arteta made a three-man change on the hour mark, bringing on Viktor Gyökeres and Mikel Merino for Havertz and Declan Rice. Six minutes later he brought on Eberechi Eze and Piero Hincapié.

The breakthrough came from Madueke, forcing Konstantinos Tzolakis into action. The Hull keeper couldn’t hold his shot, and Ødegaard was quickest to react, scoring from the rebound.

The captain’s strike took the frustration out of Arsenal’s efforts and was a reward for their perseverance. It also provided another vital moment for the Norwegian. He’s had a tough campaign so far due to injuries.

Despite going behind, Hull never gave up in their search for an equaliser. However, Arsenal held on to their lead.

Arsenal dominated the game with 67% of possession and 21 shots, compared to just seven for Hull. Tzolakis put in a number of key interventions to keep the scoreline respectable.

Although they weren’t at their best, Arsenal were able to perform in the key moments of the game. Ødegaard’s goal meant that the Gunners earned three points and are sitting top of the Premier League heading into the international break.
