Mike Washington Jr. logs nine touches in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Las Vegas Raiders RB Mike Washington Jr. rushed eight times for 23 yards and caught one pass for four yards against the Seattle Seahawks in Week 10.

Why it Matters: Washington finished with one less carry than Ashton Jeanty, but Washington ended up leading the team in rushing. He seems to be splitting carries with Jeanty, and while Jeanty has the clear edge on receiving downs, Washington is being given enough touches to be in the RB3 mix. Expect more of the same when the Raiders face the Denver Broncos in Week 11.
