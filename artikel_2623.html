Bucketheads: After the Buckeyes loss to UConn, where does this team stand?

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

"Bucketheads" is LGHL's men's basketball podcast, hosted by Connor Lemons and Justin Golba. In every episode, they give you the latest scoop on the Ohio State Buckeyes and everything else happening in college hoops.

In Episode 225 of the "Bucketheads Podcast," Connor and Justin are back for this special recap episode as the Buckeyes lose 84-68 to UConn. There was some good, but ultimately the bad outweighed all that and the Huskies pulled away in the second half.

To open the episode, we recap the UConn game. What did the Buckeyes do well, what did they struggle with, and where did the game get out of hand?

With this loss, the Buckeyes are now 2-2 and 0-2 against ranked teams. We break down where the Buckeyes go from here and give a report card on each player through four games.
