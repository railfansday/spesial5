Arch Manning had 3 INTs vs LSU. His last-second TD lifted Longhorns to a road win

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

With one play and a single flick of the wrist, Texas potentially secured a spot in the College Football Playoff while killing LSU's postseason dreams.

Trailing No. 22 LSU on the road by five, 21-16, and facing a second-and-goal from the Tigers' 4-yard line with six seconds remaining, Arch Manning found an outbreaking Raleek Brown for a 4-yard touchdown pass with two seconds left to give the No. 8 Longhorns a dramatic 22-21 victory on Saturday, Nov. 14 at Tiger Stadium in Baton Rouge, Louisiana.

It marked the final play of a 12-play, 58-yard drive that helped coach Steve Sarkisian's team earn a win in a game in which they trailed by 11 with two minutes remaining in the third quarter.

For Manning, who was born and raised in nearby New Orleans, it offered a measure of redemption in what had been a difficult homecoming for much of the night. Even with the late touchdown pass, Manning threw three interceptions, completed only 54.3% of his passes and averaged just five yards per attempt.

With the win, Texas avoided a third loss that could have potentially knocked it from playoff contention.
