Kenneth Walker listed as out of Chiefs practice Monday

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Running back Kenneth Walker has missed the last two Chiefs games and Kansas City’s first practice report of the week doesn’t find him on an upward trajectory ahead of Thursday’s road game in Buffalo.

The Chiefs listed Walker as out of practice due to his groin injury. The participation level was an estimation because the Chiefs only held a walkthrough.

Right tackle Jaylon Moore (ankle) was also listed as out after missing Sunday’s win over the Cardinals. Right guard Trey Smith (concussion) also missed that game, but he was listed as a full participant.

Tight end Noah Gray (rib) and fullback Ben VanSumeren (knee) were listed as out. Defensive tackle Omarr Norman-Lott (knee) was listed as limited while cornerback Mansoor Delane (shoulder), quarterback Patrick Mahomes (knee, right elbow), wide receiver Rashee Rice (knee), and defensive lineman Peter Woods (neck) were all listed as full participants.
