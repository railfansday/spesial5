Nets vs. Timberwolves Preview: Julius Randle returns to Minnesota

The Brooklyn Nets followed up their embarrassing outing against the Chicago Bulls with a spirited effort against the Orlando Magic. Brooklyn had their chances late, but couldn't cash in. On the other side, Franz Wagner continued his All-Star level play and hit the game winner in the final seconds of the game. Altogether, it was the Nets' fourth straight loss.

The opponent tonight has managed some rocky moments, but are doing well despite them. The Minnesota Timberwolves are near the top of the jam-packed Western Conference and figure to be a formidable opponent in the playoffs. They helped their cause with a great 104-95 win against the Memphis Grizzlies on Saturday night.

Matchup: Brooklyn Nets (3-12) at Minnesota Timberwolves (12-5)

Location: Target Center (Minneapolis, MN)

OUT – Nolan Traore (right hamstring strain)

OUT – Jaylen Clark (right hamstring strain)

OUT – Donte DiVincenzo (right Achilles tendon repair)

Full NBA injury report for November 23 here.

Location matters a great deal. When you're in one environment, the things you normally do are frowned upon and minimized. But when you move, what was mocked becomes celebrated. That's the story of LaMelo Ball's run so far. He was loathed by many an NBA observer while with the Charlotte Hornets. But now that he's on a winning team, he's gotten a lot more praise and credit for his play. Even with that, there's still room for the young guard to grow.

"The key here, like with the whole offense, is LaMelo Ball. He's had some wild swings between offensive genius and being in way over his head during his first month running Chris Finch's offense.

It often looks like LaMelo is trying to play free jazz while McDaniels, Dosunmu, Kuminga, and Gobert are much more popular with the standard pop hits. It's clunky, but the Wolves are no worse for wear. The bug in the system hasn't affected their clutch-time offense, which ranks fifth in the NBA, and 66.7% of their clutch field goals are assisted.

The Wolves look like contenders a month into the season. All teams have some kinks to work out. The ball movement can always be better; just ask Gregg Popovich and Steve Kerr. But Minnesota's iso heavy offense is still strong enough to go deep into the playoffs, especially when Anthony Edwards returns to the lineup with a vengeance."

That's one of those things that'll get settled the more experience everyone has with each other. By the time the Nets see the Wolves in February, they should be a lot better on offense.

The Wolves have arguably the best defense in the NBA to start the season, and a lot of their success is due to their ability to limit three-pointers. Minnesota is only allowing 35 three-point attempts per game, fifth-fewest in the league. Even more impressively, they're holding teams to 32.8% from downtown, third best in the league. It's hard to get shots up against this team, and without Traore, scoring will be a bit harder for the Nets to come by tonight. Look for Egor Demin to return to the starting five and for Mikel Brown Jr to get all the minutes he can handle.

Brooklyn will need a big game out of Michael Porter Jr tonight. Porter Jr can heat up at a moment's notice and that skill will come in handy for Brooklyn tonight. Brooklyn needs wins in the worst way and if the defense can hold up long enough to keep it close late, maybe Porter Jr can make magic happen at the end.

A homecoming! Randle spent two seasons in Minnesota, helping the Timberwolves reach the Western Conference finals during his time there. He figures to receive a nice hand from the Target Center faithful this evening.

On the court, Randle will look to bounce back from a rough ending to Friday's game. The Nets had their chances to win late, but a Randle turnover and missed three -pointer at the buzzer doomed Brooklyn's chances of pulling off the upset. Randle should be amped up and with a chance to end the team's losing streak, he'll have even more motivation to play a great game.

Randle will be matched up with an old teammate of his. Jaden McDaniels has improved each season of his career and earned himself a nice extension for his efforts. As one of the more versatile forwards in basketball, McDaniels can take on a variety of roles and impacts the game in multiple ways. With Randle almost certainly looking to hunt for his shot tonight, McDaniels will play a bigger role than usual for the Wolves.
