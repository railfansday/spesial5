Jaguars open practice window for OL Patrick Mekari

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Jaguars are getting a key player back on the practice field.

Jacksonville has opened the 21-day practice window for offensive lineman Patrick Mekari, the team announced on Wednesday.

Mekari was placed on injured reserve with a designation to return at roster cuts in August. He had undergone offseason back surgery.

Mekari started 14 games at right guard for the Jaguars last season.

He joined the club on a three-year deal in the 2025 offseason after spending his first six seasons with the Ravens.
