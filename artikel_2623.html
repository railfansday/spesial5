Liverpool Women Near Lock for Players Cup Quarter-Finals Following Win

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

It's been a good week for Liverpool's women's team, with the reds recording a 3-0 victory over Brighton on the weekend that took them to move up to tenth in the FA Women's Super League before sweeping past Nottingham Forest 4-1 in the Players Cup last night.

On the women's side, the Players Cup is played using a Swiss-style league phase with two groups of 11 and every team playing six games, three home and three away. The overall best eight sides then advance, but seeding is based on where they've finished in their group.

Currently, Liverpool sit level with Everton in second with 12 points in the northern group, just behind Man United and with Brighton and London City topping Group B with 12 points apiece, which is a total that all but guarantees advancement to the quarter-finals.

"I liked some of the first-half performance," was head coach Gareth Taylor's muted reaction to the win. "I don't like the territory we gave them in the second half, I think we can manage that better. But we made changes at the half and that can disrupt the team a bit.

"But of course we've also got a quick turnaround to Saturday and were trying to manage that. I think it's nothing to be sniffed at. It would be good for us to progress in this competition. This is a competition we want to win and we can win, so hopefully that result takes us on."

Behind Liverpool in the combined table there's a three way tie for sixth with Tottenham, West Ham, and Aston Villa on 11 points. Ninth in the combined table are Crystal Palace with eight points—meaning they can only catch any of the three sides behind the Reds.

The only reason Liverpool can't say that they're mathematically through is Sunderland, who sit 11th on six points with two games left against Burnley and Everton. If they win both games and Liverpool lose with a combined 9-point swing, the Reds would miss out.

"Chelsea have been strong this season, you can see that obviously," Taylor added. "They've got seasoned pros who are ready to play three games a week. We expect the challenge to be really tough but we go in with good feelings, in good form, and we're looking forward to it."
