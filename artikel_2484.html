Spirit cruise past Thorns to reach NWSL semifinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Washington Spirit are heading to the NWSL semifinals after a commanding 4-1 victory over the Portland Thorns at Audi Field on Sunday.

The third-seeded Spirit made their intentions clear early, scoring three times in a blistering first-half performance before adding a fourth after Portland briefly threatened to make a game of it in the second half.

Mélissa Bethi opened the scoring just six minutes into the match, finishing off a cross from Trinity Rodman to give Washington the early advantage. The goal marked Bethi’s first in the NWSL playoffs, while Rodman recorded her ninth assist of the season.

Trinity Rodman steals the ball, finds Mélissa Bethi, and IT'S IN THE BACK OF THE NET 🔥 pic.twitter.com/UarMPezjmb

The Spirit doubled their lead in the 34th minute after a costly mistake from Portland goalkeeper Mackenzie Arnold. Taking too long to clear the ball after a back-pass, Arnold was punished by Clara Luvanga, who capitalized on the error to make it 2-0.

Clara Luvanga puts the pressure on and is rewarded 😗 pic.twitter.com/YuAUIABq0z

Portland’s afternoon went from bad to worse when Washington struck again just 90 seconds later. Luvanga turned provider, setting up Rodman to finish a quick attacking move and send the home side into the break with a commanding three-goal lead.

TRINITY RODMAN HITS TOP SPEED AND GETS THE GOAL pic.twitter.com/HgZsU00Fu2

Portland’s defensive struggles prompted head coach Robert Vilahamn to make two changes before halftime, with Reilyn Turner and M.A. Vignola replacing Marie Müller and Carolyn Calzada in the 38th minute.

Portland showed more urgency after the restart and found a breakthrough in the 51st minute, with Pietra Tordin getting the visitors on the scoresheet to cut the deficit to two.

But Portland’s hopes of building on that momentum were quickly dealt a finishing blow.

Rodman extinguished the Thorns’ momentum in the 58th minute, finishing from another Luvanga assist to complete her brace and restore Washington’s three-goal cushion.

2 TRINITY 2 RODMAN ✌️Rodman gets her second of the game! pic.twitter.com/4ieKlv4VI7

To add insult to injury, Thorns defender Jayden Perry was sent off after receiving a second yellow card in the closing minutes of the match.

The Washington Spirit will face the Utah Royals in the NWSL semifinals next weekend after Utah pulled off a 1-0 upset of the second-seeded San Diego Wave on Friday. Washington will look to carry its attacking momentum into the matchup as it continues its push for a third consecutive NWSL Championship appearance.
