UEFA Nations League: Olise and Upamecano start as France face Italy at San Siro

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Dayot Upamecano and Michael Olise are in France’s starting lineup for the game against Italy at the iconic San Siro stadium. The center-back returned to the side after missing the Belgium win because of suspension, while his FC Bayern teammate Olise played a huge part in that victory for Zinedine Zidane's side with a brace and an assist off the bench.

Here are the lineups for the two teams ahead of one of the marquee fixtures in this month's international break.

Italy: Donnarumma ©; Kayode, Scalvini, Bastoni, Calafiori; Barella, Tonali, Frattesi; S. Esposito, P. Esposito, Cambiaghi

France: Maignan; Gusto, Jacquet, Upamecano, Truffert; Cherki, Da Cunha, Rabiot ©; Olise, Doue, Barcola
