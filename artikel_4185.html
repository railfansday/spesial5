GTA 5 And Red Dead Redemption 2 Are Leaving PS Plus

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Even though tens of millions of us will be playing GTA 6 later this week, Rockstar wants to make sure the player count is as high as possible. That even means sacrificing player counts in their other games, as GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 are leaving PS Plus.

The two most recent Rockstar games have been available to play through PS Plus Extra for the past few months. The games are routinely added to Plus and then removed, with their latest arrivals - particularly when it comes to GTA 5 - Rockstar’s final push to get as many people playing its games before GTA 6 arrives.

Every game coming to Essential, Extra, and Premium and the dates you'll be able to play them for free.

GTA 5 and Red Dead 2 will be removed from Extra on November 17 at midnight, which means today, November 16, is your last chance to play them on Plus. Not only a chance to jump in and play them for a few hours if you don’t already own them, but also your last chance to play if Extra is how you’ve been playing them up until now.

That’s because, unlike Essential games which remain in your library and playable as long as you have an active subscription, Extra games come and go. When a game is removed from Extra, you can no longer play that game unless you pay for it, even if you have it installed on your console.

Games are removed from Extra’s library around halfway through every month, but GTA 5 and Red Dead 2 being a part of this batch is unquestionably a tactical move on Rockstar’s part. While there will undoubtedly be people who play GTA 5 for years to come - it has sold more than 200 million copies and has an online element that GTA 6 won’t have at launch - Rockstar will still want to do every little thing possible to ensure as many people as possible are playing GTA 6 from November 19.

There is an argument to be made here that the better approach would be to leave GTA 5 on PS Plus in an attempt to attract more people to GTA 6. It’s a tactic sports series, which release a new game every year, have been using for a while now. EA Sports FC, for example, will see its latest game added to PS Plus around nine months after release. That gets more people playing and, in theory, convinces new and lapsed fans to buy the new entry right away three months later because they don’t want to wait for it to be added to Plus a year later.

Rockstar is taking a different approach, and if you want to play a GTA after today, you’re going to have to pay for it. Odds are that GTA is going to be GTA 6, although it will be fascinating to watch sales numbers over the coming months to see if there’s a similar pattern to the way Mario Kart has sold. Despite the release of Mario Kart World, Mario Kart 8 Deluxe continues to sell incredibly well. Perhaps we will see the same thing happen to GTA 5 and GTA 6.
