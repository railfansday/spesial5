Madison Man Sentenced to 15 Years in Federal Prison for Armed Drug Trafficking

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Tallahassee, Florida – Frederick Allen Smith, Jr., 54, of Madison, Florida, was sentenced to 15 years’ imprisonment after previously pleading guilty in federal court to possession with intent to distribute 50 grams or more of methamphetamine and 500 grams or more of a mixture containing methamphetamine, possession of a firearm in furtherance of a drug trafficking offense, and possession of a firearm by a convicted felon.
