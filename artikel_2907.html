Brunicke’s Goal Proves Wilkes-Barre/Scranton Is Ready To Fly

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Harrison Brunicke just proved he belongs at the next level with a single play. The Wilkes-Barre/Scranton Penguins trailed the Lehigh Valley Phantoms 2-0 before a turnaround on Friday. Brunicke scored the go-ahead goal with a solo rush that left everyone in the arena staring. The Penguins won the game 4-2, and Brunicke was the main reason why.

In his previous four games, Brunicke had accumulated four goals prior to Friday's contest. He corralled the puck in his own zone and skated around three Phantoms players. With 4:15 remaining in the third period, he shot the puck top shelf to secure a 3-2 advantage for his team. Yahoo Sports noted that he has now scored five goals in his last five games. The Penguins also got two goals from Bill Zonnon, who tied the game early in the third period. Late in the final frame, Atley Calvert secured the victory by scoring an empty-net goal. The final score of 4-2 looks close, but Brunicke's goal was the difference maker.

Heading into their upcoming matchup, the Wilkes-Barre/Scranton Penguins hold a record of 9-6-3. They face the Rochester Americans on Saturday night. Before being designated the second star of the game, Brunicke recorded a goal and an assist. Zonnon took the first star spot for his two goals. The team is showing they can win even when they fall behind early. Brunicke has scored a point every single time he has played against the Phantoms. The statistic indicates he performs best against that team. If he keeps this up, a call to the NHL club will not be far away.

Brunicke's performance on Friday night was the kind of play that changes a roster spot.
