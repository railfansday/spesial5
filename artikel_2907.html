Panthers vs Ravens: Matchups to watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Carolina Panthers notched a key divisional win over the New Orleans Saints last Sunday. They sit alone at the top of the NFC South with a one game advantage over both the Saints and Bucs. Both of those teams have very difficult road games against NFC North foes. Meanwhile the Panthers are playing host to the Baltimore Ravens, who are having a season from hell and just loss to the previously winless Los Angeles Chargers. It was the Ravens' fifth straight loss. And now they're embroiled in controversy because their starting cornerback said his own team's city was "best at making criminals." He's been suspended for those comments and won't be playing on Sunday.

It's a great spot for the Panthers to create some space and the teams that are chasing them. There are some spots where they can really take advantage and help themselves get that much needed win.

The Ravens made a desperate trade for former Eagles center Cam Jurgens as they were ravaged by injuries at that spot over the course of the season. They apparently hadn't noticed that Jurgens had been struggling, and he has done little to alleviate the problems they had at that position. PFF currently ranks him 34th out of the 35 qualifying centers, and he's bottom five as both a run blocker and pass blocker. Lee Hunter hasn't been getting a lot of snaps, but he's made some huge plays when he's on the field. This is a matchup he can take dominate when he's on the field and the rest of the line can take advantage of play to play.

Jackson looks poised to make his return to the lineup, and he's walking into a tough spot. Zay Flowers is probably one of the more underrated receivers in the league. He's remarkably shifty and can turn any catch into a huge play. He's been credited with nine big plays this season, by far the most on the team. He's about the only explosive play threat on the offense right now. Keeping him under wraps and forcing Jacoby Brissett to sustain long drives is a recipe for this defense to succeed.

The Ravens suspiciously backed out of a trade for Maxx Crosby over the offseason and then conveniently able to sign Hendrickson to a free agent contract like the very next day. It hasn't paid off so far. He's been decent at winning as a pass rusher, but it hasn't translated into sacks, and sacks are pretty much all you pay him to do. Walker has had his fair of struggles at left tackle this season, and he's going to be the primary adversary for Hendrickson in this game. The Panthers need him to just hold up in the passing game, hopefully with some help. And they'll need him to move Hendrickson in the running game to give Chuba Hubbard space to work.

Last week, it was the Saints vs the first quarter. They didn't score any points in the entire first half, so it was a decisive win for the first quarter. The Ravens have a problem with the other end of games. Dating back to the end of the John Harbaugh era, the Ravens have blown more fourth quarter leads than anyone. Firing Harbaugh has done nothing to fix that. They've already blown two double digit leads in the fourth quarter this season. The Panthers just need to hang around, and they'll have a really good shot to make hay late if they need to.
