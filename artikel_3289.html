Bryan Cranston & Marianne Jean-Baptiste Heading To Broadway In Ivo Van Hove’s Acclaimed ‘All My Sons’ Revival

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Director Ivo Van Hove‘s acclaimed, Olivier Award-winning West End revival of Arthur Miller’s All My Sons starring Bryan Cranston and Marianne Jean-Baptiste will transfer to Broadway this spring, producers announced today.

The production, with Cranston and Jean-Baptiste reprising their roles of the play’s Joe and Kate Keller characters, will begin performances at Broadway’s August Wilson Theatre on Tuesday, March 30, 2027, with an opening night set for Tuesday, April 13, 2027.

“We are thrilled to bring this Olivier Award-winning production to Broadway following its acclaimed run in London, where audiences connected with Arthur Miller’s masterpiece in powerful and deeply resonant ways,” said producers Wessex Grove and Gavin Kalin Productions. “Led by the extraordinary Bryan Cranston and Marianne Jean-Baptiste and brought to life by Ivo Van Hove and an exceptional design team, this production offers a compelling examination of the American Dream and the cost of putting self-interest above collective responsibility.” Watch on Deadline

The producers’ statement continued, “At a moment when Miller’s questions of accountability, truth, and moral responsibility feel more urgent than ever, we look forward to sharing this timely and deeply moving production with New York audiences.”

The production will feature scenic and lighting design by Jan Versweyveld and costume design by An D’Huys, both longtime collaborator of Van Hove. Sound design will be by Tom Gibbons, last on Broadway with Every Brilliant Thing and attached to the upcoming Small Mouth Sounds.

Including its original 1947 production, All My Sons has been staged on Broadway four times, most recently in Jack O’Brien’s 2019 staging starring Tracy Letts and Annette Bening.

The official synopsis: “Respected, self-made businessman Joe prides himself on providing for his wife and their two sons. While wartime delivers profits for the family, it comes at a price when his partner is charged with criminal manufacturing deals, and his eldest son goes missing in action. Peacetime brings little peace of mind as Joe’s true involvement in the activity begins to surface, and he is suddenly confronted by the consequences of his actions. Arthur Miller skewers the dishonest promise of the American Dream in this disturbingly prescient play.”

Breaking Bad star Cranston was last on Broadway in Van Hove’s Network (2018), in which he played the disturbed and doomed TV news anchor Howard Beale. He played President Lyndon Baines Johnson in 2014’s All The Way, and was a producer on the 2015 musical Finding Neverland.

Jean-Baptiste, Oscar-nominated for 1997’s Secrets & Lies and a series regular on CBS’ Without A Trace, will be making her Broadway debut with All My Sons (though she has appeared in two 2010 Off Broadway productions for The Public Theater’s Shakespeare in the Park – The Winter’s Tale and The Merchant of Venice). Her UK stage credits go back to 1990 up to her performance in All My Sons.

The West End All My Sons production won the Olivier for Best Revival, as did supporting actor Paapa Essiedu, with Van Hove, Cranston and Jean-Baptiste receiving nominations.

The revival is produced by Wessex Grove (Benjamin Lowy, Emily Vaughan-Barratt, Tom Powis) and Gavin Kalin Productions.
