Apple’s head of camera and Photos software has retired, per report

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

According to a new report from Bloomberg, Apple’s head of camera and photo software retired last month. Jon McCormack spent eight years at Apple and “oversaw the Camera and Photos apps and the underlying technology that determines how users capture, view, and edit their pictures and video.”

Mark Gurman reports that Apple hasn’t named a new vice president to replace McCormack, who reported to software chief Craig Federighi. “Instead, his former deputies are assuming his responsibilities under other executives,” the report says.

In his role, McCormack oversaw “close coordination between software engineers and Apple’s camera hardware and chip teams.” He’s credited with last year’s redesigned selfie camera and other projects:

His work included the redesigned selfie camera introduced with the iPhone 17 lineup last year. This year, Apple added a variable-aperture camera to the iPhone 18 Pro lineup, giving users more control over lighting and depth of field. The company also introduced a customizable Camera app interface with professional controls.

Bloomberg describes McCormack as a “strong opponent of using generative AI to create or alter photographs,” a philosophy that created tension as Apple embraced AI tools in the Photos app. His retirement, however, “wasn’t related to a disagreement or clash with Apple over its AI strategy,” the report says.

McCormack spoke in depth about iOS 27 features like Clean Up, Extend, and Spatial Reframing in an interview with Tyler Stalman in June. He emphasized how the features “preserve the integrity of the moment.”
