‘You Can See Everything’ Sweeps Top Categories At Critics Choice Documentary Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim’s buzzy documentary You Can See Everything swept all the major categories at this year’s Critics Choice Documentary Awards. The film, which chronicles the last days in Elizabeth Holmes’ life before she went to prison, won every category in which it was nominated.

You Can See Everything took the top award of the evening, Best Documentary Feature. The film also Best Director for Nathan Fielder & Lance Oppenheim, Best Cinematography for David Bolen, Best Editing for Adam Locke-Norton and Best Score for Ari Balouzian.

The night’s other big winner was David Greaves’ Once Upon a Time in Harlem, which won Best First Documentary Feature for Greaves, Best Archival Documentary and Best Historical Documentary.
