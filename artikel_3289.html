Tanggal: 2026-11-13

LiqTech International, Inc. (LIQT -8.81%) reported a 15.5% year-over-year revenue increase and reaffirmed its full-year revenue guidance of $20 million to $23 million. Management stated that growth was led by the Marine and Diesel Particulate Filter segments, while Commercial Aquatics and Plastics experienced timing-related softness. The company is emphasizing the standardization of its filtration systems to scale operations and improve gross margins. Management indicated that the current opportunity pipeline supports a preliminary 2027 revenue growth outlook of 30% to 40% as the company works toward sustainable profitability.
