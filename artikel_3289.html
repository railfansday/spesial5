Netflix Animated Series ‘Living The Dream’ Gets Trailer & Release Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Here’s the trailer for Living the Dream, the animated Netflix series from Apple & Onion creator George Gendi.

The trailer shows best friends Des and Ray, a pair of guys who think they should have made it by now, but are stuck working at a save-the-planet company called Ecofood. They must navigate awkward workplace romance, HR blunders, directives from the L.A. office and Ray’s dad’s disapproval.

Netflix has given the show a December 18 release date globally.

The eight-part show stars the BAFTA-nominated Gendi, Javone Prince, Julia Davis, Tom Stourton, Ellie White, Ambika Mod, Chris Diamontopoulos and Stavros Halkias, and is animated by Ireland’s Boulder Media. Warner Bros. Animation’s Hanna-Barbera Studios Europe is the producer. Sam Register, Sarah Fell and Daniel Lennard, all of whom have worked on Cartoon Network’s The Amazing World of Gumball, are the exec producers. Watch on Deadline

Living the Dream creator Gendi is also behind Cartoon Network show Apple & Onion. He had originally joined Cartoon Network Studios Europe as a storyboard artist on Season 1 of The Amazing World of Gumballand then went on to develop his idea for Apple & Onion, which was picked up by Cartoon Network Studios out of the UK.

He moved to the U.S. in 2017 to make two seasons of the show before returning to the UK while developing Living the Dream with Hanna-Barbera Studios Europe. Netflix boarded the show and production began in 2024.
