This trick swaps one of Apple’s downloaded screensaver videos for a video saved on your computer.

First, open System Settings → Screen Saver, choose an animated aerial screensaver, and wait for it to download.

Next, locate its downloaded video file in Finder. Save a backup of the original, then give your own compatible video the same filename—including the extension—and replace the downloaded file. You may be asked to authenticate as an administrator.

Select that screensaver again in Settings. The option still looks like Apple’s original, but it now plays your video.

One catch: this is an unofficial workaround from the Sonoma era. Later macOS updates may block it or change where the files are stored, and playback can be unreliable.
