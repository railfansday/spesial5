Josh Allen is one score away from four straight seasons with 12 or more rushing touchdowns

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Bills quarterback Josh Allen has become something that few quarterbacks are — a rushing-touchdown machine.

In nine 2026 games, Allen has 11 rushing touchdowns. With only one more, he'll have his fourth straight season with 12 or more rushing touchdowns.

Via NBC Sports research, that would make him only the fifth player in NFL history to have four straight seasons with 12 or more rushing touchdowns.

The others were all running backs. LaDainian Tomlinson did it six times. Shaun Alexander accomplished it five times. Derrick Henry and Adrian Peterson did it four times each.

Henry, who has 10 this year, is two short of running his own streak to five seasons in a row.

Allen's next rushing touchdown will give him 91 for his career, putting him in a tie for 12th all time with Franco Harris and Jerome Bettis. Given that Allen is only in his ninth season, the all-time rushing-touchdown record of 164, held my Emmitt Smith, could eventually be in jeopardy.
