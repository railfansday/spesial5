Syracuse football: recap of Fran Brown’s press conference

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Another week in the 2026 season is in the books for Syracuse Football. With bowl aspirations on the mind of the Orange, they get an opportunity to get their 6th win of the season this Saturday after a come from behind 34-31 win over NC State.

With Boston College in sight, as usual game week starts on Monday, with Coach Brown's weekly press conference. The biggest news of this week's presser was Brown's announcement was his expectation of the return of wide receiver Calvin Russell in 2027.

Brown has stated before that he has had multiple conversations with players about returning next year. Brown did not say who, but did state those conversations have taken place. With this talking point today it opened up this question about Quarterback Steve Angeli and his future in Orange.

" I haven't heard that Steve was going to go anywhere else, I think he's going to be here… I just don't want to be saying "hey this guy is going to be here, he's going to be here, and then they have different thoughts in their minds." Brown said about Angeli and other members of his team.

Resilience was a big talking point in yesterday's presser for Fran, one player in particular Linebacker Chris D'Appalonia. D'Appalonia made the game sealing fumble recovery (unless you ask the people in the SU building, it's an interception) with a club on his hand." (D'Appalonia) had surgery on Monday, didn't practice on Tuesday, practiced Wednesday, He's tough." Brown said about his linebacker. D'Appalonia had broken his hand and had pins and needles in his hand. In terms of BC this upcoming weekend, Eagles Quarterback Mason Mackenzie will be the player to watch for as he has thrown for over 1,600 yards and leads Boston College with 686 rushing yards this season. "Whether we will be able to stop him or not, I'm not sure of that , this kid is dynamic…he's just all over the football field." Brown when talking about the BC Signalcaller.
