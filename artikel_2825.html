Eagles Injury Report: No practice for DeVonta Smith, Saquon Barkley, Tay Wicks

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Philadelphia Eagles issued their first official injury report of the week on Wednesday in advance of their Week 9 game against the New York Giants.

The Eagles listed seven players under DID NOT PARTICIPATE: Saquon Barkley, DeVonta Smith, Dontayvion Wicks, Jihaad Campbell, Michael Carter II, Britain Covey, and Jonathan Jones.

Barkley was listed under DNP on Wednesday and Thursday last week before being limited on Friday and playing on a pitch count on Sunday. The Eagles SHOULD just sit him down and rest him. Especially with the Week 10 bye coming up next. But we'll see if they actually do that or not.

Smitty and Wicks seem to be tracking to miss another game. Again, there's no need to rush them back ahead of the bye. The Eagles could be down to Darius Cooper, Makai Lemon, Hollywood Brown, and Elijah Moore once again.

It looks like Campbell will miss another game, putting Jeremiah Trotter Jr. in line to start again.

MC2 seemingly aggravated the abdomen injury that previously caused him to miss time. Not great for the Eagles' safety and nickel depth.

Covey could miss another game, leaving Cooper DeJean to return punts.

Jones could miss another game, leaving the Eagles without their top backup cornerback. But the veteran shouldn't really be playing over young guys at this point anyway.

The Eagles listed three players under LIMITED PARTICIPATION: Jake Elliott, Jonathan Greenard, and Jalyx Hunt.

Not good to see the Eagles' kicker on the report. But guessing the Eagles are managing his workload to get him to game day.

Greenard clearly isn't healthy. The Eagles are managing his reps. Ditto for Hunt.

The Eagles listed eight players under FULL PARTICIPATION: Tanner McKee, Landon Dickerson, Dallas Goedert, Zack Baun, Jordan Davis, Moro Ojomo, Markel Bell, and Kenny Moore II.

All of these players should be in line to play on Sunday.

McKee is a new addition to the report with a chest injury. This development comes at the same time that he's reportedly being benched for Joe Milton III. For some reason, the Eagles are once again being weird about the handling of their QB situation.

Man, imagine if Nick Sirianni didn't focus on competitive advantage so much. The Eagles might be a really bad team without all of this very necessary obfuscation.

Dickerson being listed with a hip injury is new. He was previously dealing with knee and pectoral injuries.
