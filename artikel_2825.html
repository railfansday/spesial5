Liverpool transfer news: Alisson Becker contract talks confirmed as Araujo stalls

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The November international break has arrived, but business continues at Liverpool as the club presses ahead with plans to strengthen under Andoni Iraola.

While the manager focuses on improvements on the training pitch during the two-week hiatus, the recruitment department will also be working diligently to identify external options capable of making a real impact on the team going forward.

That could encompass moves in the upcoming January transfer window or potential long-term targets for the summer window, while internal discussions will also center on several critical contract situations. With that in mind, here's a rundown of the latest Liverpool transfer news and rumors ahead of another busy day for the Reds.

Liverpool goalkeeper Alisson Becker has revealed that his agent is in negotiations with the club regarding his future, as the shot-stopper approaches the final few months of his deal, which runs out next summer. Speaking to Liverpool.com and a group of reporters following the weekend's match against Crystal Palace, the Brazilian stated: "I'm focused on playing every game, but in my mind I'm happy to stay. You know, that depends on a lot of aspects and different situations. But my will is to stay.", reports Liverpool.com.

"I'm happy here, my family is happy here. I'm still hungry for titles and for delivering good performances. I still want to play at the top level for as long as I can. So that's my thinking on that."

When pressed on whether discussions were ongoing with Liverpool, he said: "My agent is talking with the club."

Liverpool.com reports: Alisson's performances have been inconsistent this season, yet when called into action, the goalkeeper has largely delivered when it mattered most.

Goalkeepers can typically continue playing at a high level for longer than outfield players, suggesting there may be less risk involved in handing the Brazilian a new contract compared to other veteran stars within the squad.

There may be reasonable concerns regarding wage expectations versus on-field output, particularly if Alisson's standards decline significantly, but ultimately it will come down to negotiations. The only additional factor to consider may be the players behind him, with Giorgi Mamardashvili widely expected to step up around this period to claim the number one jersey.

With several months still remaining, Liverpool are under no immediate pressure to rush into offering a new contract, but early dialogue is an encouraging indicator that progress can be made.

According to Spanish outlet Sport, Ronald Araujo is holding off on any decision regarding his long-term future with Liverpool until the season concludes.

The Barcelona loanee has made a strong impression with the Reds so far, prompting reports that a permanent transfer could be accelerated to January, with a $61 million buyout clause included in his loan agreement. Nevertheless, it seems the Uruguayan international is inclined to hold off before reaching a definitive decision, while the Merseyside club would similarly likely delay until the summer, given the substantial financial outlay such a transfer would demand.
