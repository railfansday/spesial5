Broncos' order of 2027 NFL draft picks before trade deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Denver Broncos will have plenty of draft capital available as they approach the NFL trade deadline (Nov. 10).

In addition to holding their original selections in all seven rounds, the Broncos also hold a seventh-round selection from the New Orleans Saints following their 2025 Devaughn Vele trade. Denver is also projected to receive a fourth-round compensatory pick for losing John Franklin-Myers and a seventh-round comp pick for losing safety P.J. Locke during NFL free agency this spring.

Additionally, if defensive coordinator Vance Joseph lands a head coach job in 2027, the Broncos would receive a third-round comp pick in 2027 and another third-round selection in 2028 as part of the NFL's Rooney Rule that rewards teams for developing minority coaches and executives.
