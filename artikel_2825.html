Celtics faced with massive NBA Cup opportunity vs. Hawks

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Your Boston Celtics have the chance to win their Emirates NBA Cup group for the first time in the competition's young history.

Now, before you angrily scroll to the comments to tell me how you don't care about the Cup and that an NBA championship is the only thing you want to see this team win, take some deep breaths. You're allowed to want to see your team win every competition that they're a part of.

Through their first three Cup games, the Celtics are undefeated with a +68 point differential — 20 points better than San Antonio, who hold the second-highest differential, and over double the Pistons' +31 mark, which is second best in the East.

In short, they're in a good spot entering their Group C finale against the Atlanta Hawks on Friday night.

All they have to do is win and they'll not only win the group, but clinch the best record in the Eastern Conference, an accomplishment that would reward them with home-c0urt advantage through the semifinals.

Home-court is a sneaky perfect prize for the Cs. The team has spent November touring the country like they just dropped a platinum album. We've seen their play sputter a bit, presumably due to their strenous travel schedule. Guaranteeing themselves two extra home games while they try regain a sense of normalcy and routine would be huge.

Even if they lose, which wouldn't be super shocking against a good Hawks team that's riding a four-game winning streak into this one, they've still got the East's Wild Card to fall back on.

The league shared the full list of scenarios that would see Boston move onto the knockout stage. All you need to know is that they'll be fine as long as the avoid a catestrophic loss.

Now, advancing in the Cup is great. Frankly, it's what we all expected when the groups were announced over the summer and Boston's was clearly the weakest in the conference.

More than anything though, this presents the Celtics with an opportunity to lay a foundation for the remainder of the season. The NBA Cup is unique in that there's so little room for error if you actually want to win the thing. When I wrote about the team's chances earlier this month, I joked that it's harder to win the Cup than to win a title because you can really only lose one game if you want to make it all the way.

Some of you lost your minds in the comments and that's okay.

The competition is still an objectively difficult climb, and there's a reason why the Celtics, in one of the franchise's most successful eras in terms of winning percentage, have yet to make a deep run.

Lifting the trophy at Hinkle Fieldhouse next month would mean that this group rose to the challenge of must-win scenarios. Facing adversity and prevailing is objectively going to bring you closer together. Somewhere down the line when things inevitably get tough in the postseason, a Cup win could serve as a reflection point for Boston.

Let's not get too far ahead of ourselves though. Beating the Hawks tonight is the first step towards any of that.
