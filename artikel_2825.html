Kings reportedly signing veteran Gabe Vincent to be backup point guard

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

This had been rumored for weeks. Veteran point guard Gabe Vincent had worked out for the Sacramento Kings and had talked with them about coming in as their backup to rookie Darius Acuff Jr., he reportedly even had a standing offer.

Now, it's finally happening, a story broken by Shams Charania of ESPN and confirmed by other reports.

Vincent, 30, is signing for a veteran minimum contract, and the Kings had an open roster spot, so no other move is needed. Vincent was invited to Knicks training camp to compete for a roster spot, and reportedly also had conversations with Atlanta before the season, but chose not to join either team.

This is a full-circle moment for Vincent, who was born in Modesto (about 75 miles from Sacramento) and played his high school ball in Stockton, about 45 miles away from the Golden One Center where the Kings play. He went on to play his college ball at UC Santa Barbara.

The Kings have been seven points per 100 possessions worse this season with Acuff off the court, they needed a veteran backup point guard after losing Ben Simmons to an Achilles tear before the season. Vincent is being brought in both to stabilize the second unit and to mentor Acuff. Vincent, a seven-year NBA veteran, fills that role. Vincent isn't an elite scorer or shooter (he has averaged 6.7 points per game over his career and is shooting under 40%), but he understands how to play off talented teammates and could work well with Kings such as Domantas Sabonis and Zach LaVine.
