Report: Ravens to sign K Harrison Mevis to practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Ravens had a pair of kickers in for workouts on Wednesday and they’ll reportedly keep them both around for a while.

There was word on Wednesday that they will be signing former Dolphins kicker Riley Patterson and NFL Media reports on Thursday that they will also sign Harrison Mevis to their practice squad.

Mevis played in the first five Rams games of the season, but he was released after missing a pair of field goals in their overtime loss to the Bills last month. He was 5-of-9 on field goals this season and 12-of-13 in nine games for the Rams last season.

The Ravens put Tyler Loop on injured reserve ahead of Week 9 and elevated Jake Moody from the practice squad for their last game. Moody signed with the Falcons this week and the Ravens will now have a choice to make about who handles their kicking duties against the Chargers on Monday night.
