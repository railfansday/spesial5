Nolan Traoré set to miss 8-10 weeks, Nets announce

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Just as Nolan Traoré was starting to get hot for the Brooklyn Nets, he and they were dealt some unfortunate news.

After he left Friday night's loss to the Orlando Magic early with a right hamstring injury, Brooklyn announced on Monday morning that he has been diagnosed with a right hamstring strain. He will be reevaluated in eight to ten weeks, the team added.

Despite a 3-12 start to the season, Traoré had been one of the Nets' most promising young pieces. Through 14 games and 10 starts, the 2025 first-round pick is averaging 12.9 points, 1.6 rebounds, and 2.7 assists on 51.1% shooting, including a whopping 40.4% from 3-point range. He also had a career-high 23 points vs the 76ers last week.

One of the biggest areas for growth that Traoré has shown is finishing around the rim. Since entering the league, he'd been regarded as one of the fastest players in the sport, but in his rookie campiagn, he struggled to finish near the basket. According to Synergy, he converted just 44.6% of his shots at the rim—ranking dead last among 230 players with at least 130 attempts.

After an offseason where he bulked up and gained muscle, he has improved significantly in that facet of the game, and the Frenchman has finished 56.4% of his shots near the rim, a significant improvement.

It will be interesting to see how Jordi Fernández and the Nets adjust in his absence. One possibility, of course, is that Mikel Brown Jr. will see more action and perhaps even start. However, he remains on a minutes restriction, Another name worth keeping an eye on is Ben Saraf.

Another 2025 first-round pick and member of the Flatbush 5 has spent most of the season in the G League with Long Island. However, that could change with Traoré sidelined.

The 20-year-old, the team's second youngest player after Traore, has certainly made a case for himself. With Long Island, Saraf is averaging 16.4 points, 3.8 rebounds, and 8.6 assists per game. He is inside the top 10 in the G League in assists per game and near the top in total assists..

Nevertheless, Traoré was just hitting his stride, and now he is facing an extended absence. Not only is it unfortunate for the second-year guard, but it will also be a tough blow for Brooklyn in what has already been a difficult start to the season.
