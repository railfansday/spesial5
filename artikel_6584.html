Hailey Bieber Wore One of Carolyn Bessette-Kennedy’s Most Iconic Looks

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

CBK-mania swept New York earlier this year, but Hailey Bieber isn’t ready to hang up the headband just yet. Today, at the NY Times DealBook Summit, Bieber went further than ’90s minimalism, wearing a jacket identical to one of Carolyn Bessette-Kennedy’s most recognizable pieces. (Bieber’s rep tells Vogue that the Rhode founder’s jacket did not belong to Bessette-Kennedy.)

Hailey Bieber at the NY Times DealBook Summit.

John F. Kennedy Jr. and Carolyn Bessette-Kennedy at the 1998 Robin Hood Gala.

Bieber took the stage at Lincoln Center in a spring 1998 Yohji Yamamoto jacket replete with a raw mock-neck collar, a sheer back and sleeves, and a tight row of buttons up the front. Bessette-Kennedy wore the jacket with a white linen skirt to the 1998 Robin Hood Foundation benefit at the 26th Street Armory.

The Rhode entrepreneur took cues from Bessette-Kennedy, pairing the blazer with a white midi pencil skirt. But the two women diverged shoe choices; CBK styled the look with a pair of nude pointy-toe heels while Bieber opted for businesslike black pumps. She finished off her look with a black headband. (Justin Bieber, who cheered his wife on from backstage, declined to dress the part of JFK Jr., instead wearing a classic leather jacket and sporty SKYLRK sunglasses.

Bessette-Kennedy’s Yohji Yamamoto jacket hit the auction block earlier this year. While it was expected to sell for $2,000 to $3,000, it fetched $12,500 in March. Still, while Hailey Bieber is not its proud owner, she is certainly a student of the school of Carolyn Bessette-Kennedy.
