Your Exclusive First Look at the Official 'Cameron Winter at Carnegie' Hall Merch

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

“I will keep rolling down until my best shirt rolls off,” warbles Cameron Winter on “The Rolling Stone,” the opening track of his 2024 solo debut Heavy Metal. That album later became the basis of the Geese frontman’s historic December 2025 performance at Carnegie Hall, which was filmed and subsequently turned into a captivating concert film by Oscar-winning director Paul Thomas Anderson. A year later, Winter—and perhaps you—will now have a new best shirt to roll off.

PTA’s Cameron Winter at Carnegie Hall, which was shot on 35mm and 16mm film, will hit select theaters nationwide on Friday. As a reward to the Geeseheads and steadfast cinephiles who attend the 35mm screenings, distributor Janus Films produced a limited-edition run of merch (many such cases!) that can only be purchased IRL at select participating theaters. Which is to say, acquiring this merch…takes miles.

But if you’re looking at the Cameron Winter at Carnegie Hall T-shirts ($40) and baseball hats ($35) and thinking, “Wow, those look a lot like the maximalist, erudite wares from Boot Boyz Biz,” that’s because they were in fact designed by two of the Chicago brand’s current outposts, Now-Time and Mike’s Deli.

In an email, the Now-Time/Mike’s Deli team said that they drew inspiration from Carnegie Hall’s history and architecture. “We looked to the building itself as a visual archive to draw from, seeking forms that resonated with the performance across time,” they wrote. “Familiar architectural details took on a new heightened significance, knowing what was happening inside. In Carnegie Hall’s classic horseshoe layout we saw the camera’s panoramic intent. Outside on the 19th-century facade we found typographic elements that paralleled the energy of the event.” A very Winteresque explanation, in a way. Ideal for any enigmatic T-shirt enthusiast.

If you, too, would like to participate in the seeking forms and panoramic intent of Winter’s moving piano stylings on one winter’s night in Manhattan, you better start a-walking to the nearest 35mm-equipped cinema near you.
