George Karlaftis has been on a tear since the Chiefs’ bye week

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Kansas City Chiefs' defense has seemingly heated up as the season has gone on. The unit has certainly solidified since allowing 30 points to the Indianapolis Colts in Week 2, and the pass rush has generated more impact, especially over the last two games (8 sacks).

Sack Nation is typically led by its CEO, Chris Jones, but defensive end George Karlaftis is the company's top earner right now, specifically since the bye week.

Through Week 5, four games and the bye, Karlaftis had no sacks, two tackles for loss and two passes defended (including an interception against the Miami Dolphins).

Since then, Karlaftis has 5.5 sacks, eight tackles for loss and eight passes defended.

According to Chiefs Communications, prior to "Sunday Night Football" and "Monday Night Football," Karlaftis has the second-most sacks and tackles for loss in the NFL since Week 6.

Karlaftis' season has been defined by his ability to make a play in the pass game without sacking the quarterback. He leads the team in passes defended this year (10), joining former Houston Texans great J.J. Watt in his 2012 Defensive Player of the Year campaign as the only two defensive linemen to reach double digits in passes defended through nine games in a season since the turn of the century.

However, since the team's week off, Karlaftis has been as productive as any pass rusher in the NFL, on top of constantly getting in the way of quarterbacks' throws. He has defended a pass in seven consecutive games and has tallied a sack in four of the last five weeks.

With Kansas City's secondary looking sound, Karlaftis and the rest of the Chiefs' pass rush should keep getting chances to bring the quarterback to the ground, especially if the offense can keep playing with a lead.
