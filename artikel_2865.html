Louisiana playoff thriller produces 175 points, record-setting 10-TD performance

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

A Louisiana high school football playoff game produced a historic offensive explosion Friday night, Nov. 20.

Notre Dame (Crowley, LA) defeated Country Day (Metairie, LA) 91-84, with the teams combining for 175 points to set the LHSAA record for most points scored in a playoff game.

Country Day sophomore quarterback Jack Grace made history despite the loss, throwing 10 touchdown passes to break his own Louisiana state record for touchdown passes in a game.

The scoring barrage reached another level in the second half. Notre Dame led 63-56 with 4:18 remaining in the third quarter before the teams continued trading touchdowns.

By the end of the third quarter, Notre Dame held a 77-63 advantage. Country Day eventually pulled even at 84-84 in the fourth quarter before Notre Dame found the end zone again to secure the victory.

Grace's 10 touchdown passes capped a remarkable performance for the sophomore, who already owned Louisiana's single season record with 63 touchdown passes entering the game. He had also tied the previous single-season record of 62 during Friday night's game.

Grace connected with Curtis McAllister for three touchdowns, while KB Brewer and Justin Drago each caught two of his scoring passes.

Despite Grace's record-setting night, Notre Dame emerged with the victory in one of the most extraordinary games in Louisiana high school football history. The Pioneers' 91 points in Friday's win accounted for 20.7% of their point total all season, with their prior high this season being 50 points in a win over Basile.

Notre Dame will advance to take on Dunham in the Division III Select quarterfinals, while Country Day saw their season end at 7-5.
