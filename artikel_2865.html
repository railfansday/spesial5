What NFL games are on TV today? Channel, schedule for NFL Sunday games in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

When was the last time anyone could say the Cleveland Browns would be missed on a fall Sunday afternoon?

Say hello to success in the NFL. The 6-4 Browns get their bye this week and it's actually consequential for a change. They currently control their playoff destiny, holding the seventh spot in the AFC and they will receive the extra week to get healthier.

That means Browns fans will have to find another game to watch. Like that's tough given the rabid nature of football fandom.

There's little doubt, however, which game will likely be of most interest. It comes down to the Battle of Pennsylvania – the Pittsburgh Steelers vs. the Philadelphia Eagles, which will air at 1 p.m. on CBS (WOIO, Channel 19 locally) throughout Ohio, Pennsylvania, Kentucky, through the Washington D.C.-Baltimore area and down through the Carolinas.

The Steelers are one spot ahead of the Browns in the playoff picture and while the Eagles may be reeling this season due to the loss of quarterback Jalen Hurts, the Steelers are coming off a thumping by division rival Cincinnati Bengals. While the expectation of Eagles win may be too much to ask, it won't be difficult to predict who Browns fans will be pulling for.
