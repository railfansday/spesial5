Tennessee-Kentucky football live updates, score, stats, highlights

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

No. 16 Tennessee (6-2, 3-2 SEC) is hosting No. 7 Kentucky (7-1, 5-1 SEC) on Saturday in Week 10. Rankings reflect the US LBM Coaches Poll.

"Excited, November, best part of the year here playing meaningful football and get an opportunity to come home, dark mode homecoming," Tennessee head coach Josh Heupel said ahead of hosting the Wildcats. "First SEC night game for us and excited to get back into our our stadium with our fans and playing a really good football team that is playing extremely well together.

"You look at their ability to win multiple ways. Finish games, turnover margin, they've been elite. One of the least penalized teams in the league. Playing really smart all three phases, so will be a great test and one I'm looking forward to."
