‘Feeling good’ – Shams Charania provides Luka Doncic, Austin Reaves injury updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

After Luka Doncic and Austin Reaves both seemingly picked up injuries against the Milwaukee Bucks, Shams Charania provided an update.

Luka Doncic and Austin Reaves have been the NBA's highest-scoring duo so far this season. The two have been combining for a whopping 64.6 points per game, and that is without getting into the assist numbers. So when they both left the court in pain against Milwaukee, the fans were very concerned.

Austin Reaves picked up a heel issue following a bad landing and had to exit the game. With Collin Sexton also out during the fourth quarter, Doncic was forced to play. The MVP favourite then seemingly hurt his ankle while landing after a three-point attempt. Shams Charania shared the latest on both.

The Lakers have shown that they can win without Luka or Austin. However, if both were to go down somehow, it would almost certainly put an end to the winning streak they have been on. It seems those concerns are unfounded at the moment, though.

Charania provided an update on both stars' health before their next game against the Phoenix Suns, and it is a mostly positive one.

"Good news for Luka Doncic," Shams said. "One source told me today that Luka Doncic actually woke up with no pain. He's feeling good, I'm told and he's going to go through his normal treatment process in the lead-up to Sunday's game. So that's the news on Luka Doncic.

"As far as Austin Reaves is concerned, the expectation is that he is going to undergo tests on his heel injury at some point today. The Lakers landed back from Milwaukee in LA late last night. So I'm sure Austin Reaves slept in but at some point, we will get a re-examination on that heel injury."

It is not promising that the news on Reaves isn't out yet, but Doncic seems to be in the clear. LA has a slightly easier schedule in their next few games until they play San Antonio in two weeks. Having both stars fit and healthy by then will be extremely important for the team.
