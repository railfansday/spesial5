49ers praise ‘off the chain’ Mexican fans for unbelievable support

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The 49ers played in the first NFL regular-season game played outside the U.S. in 2005.

San Francisco lost to the Cardinals 31-14 in a historic matchup at Estadio Azteca in Mexico City that season. In 2022, the 49ers returned to the renowned stadium for a rematch with the Cardinals and earned a 38-10 victory.

San Francisco 49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan watches from the sideline in the first half of an NFL football game against the Las Vegas Raiders in Santa Clara, Calif., Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026. AP Photo/Kelley L. Cox

Although it was considered a road game for the 49ers, coach Kyle Shanahan recalled the overwhelming support from Mexican fans.

"It was definitely a home game that week," Shanahan said Wednesday. "I feel like our Mexican fan base is off the chain. They're unbelievable. … The last time we were there, it felt like almost the whole stadium (was filled with 49ers fans)."

San Francisco 49ers running back Christian McCaffrey answers a question after an NFL football game against the Dallas Cowboys in Arlington, Texas, Nov. 15, 2026. AP Photo/Julio Cortez

Running back Christian McCaffrey echoed a similar sentiment to Shanahan's, sharing the team is excited to return to Estadio Azteca — since renamed Estadio Banorte — for the highly touted NFC showdown against Minnesota.

"It was one of the coolest experiences playing there," McCaffrey said of the 2022 game in Mexico City.

"That many people being that loud for that long was something that was really special. You never know what to expect going into an international game and unfamiliar territory, but they made it so fun for us. The Mexican fans are unbelievable. So we can't wait to get down there."

San Francisco 49ers fans cheer before an NFL football game against the Dallas Cowboys in Arlington, Texas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. AP Photo/Jeffrey McWhorter

This time, the 49ers are the home team on paper, too. Sportsbooks have had San Francisco favored by three points against the Vikings.

Kickoff from Mexico City is set for 5:20 p.m. PT Sunday, Nov. 22, on NBC and Peacock.
