Review: Air France A350-900 Business Class Paris To New York

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Air France delivered one of the best business class flights I have taken in years on my journey from Paris to New York. The new A350-900 cabin is excellent, but what impressed me most was how well everything came together, from the comfortable seat to the delicious dinner, gracious service, and fast Starlink Wi-Fi.

I shared my first impressions of this flight earlier, calling it the best business class experience in Europe. Having had more time to reflect on it, I stand by that assessment…this flight left very little room for improvement.

I needed to return to the United States after taking my children to Germany for the summer. Flying Blue came through with a last-minute business class award from Basel to Paris to New York for 60,000 miles plus €453 in taxes and fees. The timing worked perfectly and I booked this about 36-hour before departure, so saver space can certainly open up via FlyingBlue.

This was a so-called “reverse redeye,” leaving Europe late in the evening and arriving in the United States even later that same evening. That suited my plans very well, particularly because I had an overnight drive to Pittsburgh ahead of me after landing.

My journey began in Basel with an Air France Hop! flight to Paris. I had time to visit the lounge before my connection and enjoyed a meal there, though I deliberately left room for dinner onboard.

Stepping onboard, I found my seat in 6L. Though approaching 8:00 pm, it was still very light outside.

I first reviewed Air France A350 business class in 2020, flying from Paris to Toronto. I enjoyed that flight very much, but the newer business class cabin represents a substantial improvement.

Far more important the gimmicky door was the room for your feet, which I found plentiful. Legroom is 43 inches when the seat is the upright position and it transforms into a bed that is 79 inches long. Seats are 21 inches wide.

There are no personal art vents, but thankfully the cabin was kept very chilly. I also loved the window darkening feature, where the window tinting could be adjusted simply by sliding your finger left or right.

I found this suite spacious and comfortable whether sitting upright, reclining, or lying down. The cabin looks so sharp. I particularly love the illuminated winged seahorse at each seat…a small detail, but one that gives the cabin an unmistakable Air France identity.

There was plenty of personal storage, and the wireless charging worked well, though there was also USB-C and universal A/C power.

After dinner, I converted my seat into a bed and slept for about four hours.

The Sofitel-branded bedding (duvet, mattress pad, pillow) was excellent, and I found the seat very comfortable for sleeping.

This is one of my favorite business class seats.

The catering was a highlight of this flight. I had eaten in the lounge, but was very glad I saved room for dinner.

Before takeoff, flight attendants offered Champagne and a warm towel, along with the familiar Gavottes savory biscuits.

Dinner began with an appetizer of pea, lemon, and basil cream accompanied by smoked salmon fillet and raspberry and grapefruit jelly.

A side salad accompanied the starter, though I saved mine until after the main course, as the French do. The bread was delicious.

I also tried the Quintessence cocktail, an Air France variation on a Negroni. It combined La Quintinye royal red vermouth, bitter Chambéry liqueur, a hint of fig, and ABK6 VSOP Cognac. I enjoyed it very much. It was not nearly as strong as a traditional Negroni, which suited me just fine on this flight.

For my main course, I chose veal with a creamy morel mushroom sauce, spelt risotto, and peas.

Admittedly, the presentation was not going to win any awards…yes, it looked like vomit. But it tasted delicious and I would happily order it again. I enjoyed a delicious glass of Chateau D’Arsac wine, which was so good.

A cheese course followed, then dessert. I enjoyed both ice cream and a crispy hazelnut tart, accompanied by hot chocolate.

This was a substantial supper and a very satisfying one.

About an hour before landing, flight attendants served a light meal of shrimp with a pickled cucumber salad.

It was excellent and just the right amount of food after the large dinner. I had two espressos with it, which helped prepare me for the late night ahead.

The entertainment system included a tail camera, which I particularly enjoyed during our departure from Paris (though not the fire that seemed to be burning near CDG). It was a gorgeous evening, and watching the aircraft leave Charles de Gaulle was a lovely way to begin the flight.

I did not watch any movies or TV on this flight, but there was large selection of movies, TV shows, games, and music.

Noise-cancelling headphones were provided, though you can also connect your own Bluetooth device.

Fast, free Starlink internet was available onboard. The connection worked from gate to gate and felt much like using the internet on the ground. I did not have any essential work to complete on this flight, but I appreciated being able to connect onboard. Starlink increasingly influences which flights I book. Air France already gives me plenty of reasons to choose it, but reliable internet makes the proposition even more attractive, especially for a daytime westbound journey when I might otherwise spend several hours working.

The Air France A350 has three lavatories in business class: one I the front (though typically reserved for crew use) and two in between the front and rear cabin.

Service was warm and attentive throughout the flight. I was even addressed by surname.

I have heard plenty of complaints over the years about service on Air France, but my own experiences have been very positive. This crew continued that pattern and the great service complemented the rest of the experience. I enjoyed a delicious meal, slept comfortably, and felt well cared for throughout the journey.

The new seat deserves attention, but a cabin can only do so much without a good crew in terms of leaving a lasting positive impression. On this flight, Air France delivered both.

We landed on time at JFK, and I quickly cleared immigration using Global Entry. The late arrival had been part of the appeal when booking, and the flight delivered exactly what I had hoped for: an enjoyable evening onboard, a few hours of rest, and an on-time arrival in New York.

Then it was time to collect a rental car and begin my overnight drive to Pittsburgh…

This was a tremendous flight on Air France…I have no complaints at all about anything.

I liked the older A350 business class product when I flew it in 2020, but the newer seat is a considerable improvement. Pair that with excellent bedding, delicious food, and a gracious crew, then add Starlink, and I struggle to identify much that I would change. Cappuccinos maybe that are not powdered?

The lack of individual air vents remains a drawback, though the comfortable cabin temperature thankfully meant it did not interfere with my sleep on this flight.

Air France reminded me how enjoyable a transatlantic business class flight can be when the airline gets the details right. I would happily book this product again and would go out of my way to find it.
