Breaking: FAA Issues Nationwide Ground Stop For All US Flights

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

* Last Updated: 6:00 PM EST November 13, 2026 by Paul Hartley

The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) ordered a rare nationwide ground stop at all US airports following communication failures affecting "multiple" air traffic control centers on Friday. Citing extraordinary circumstances and safety concerns, the FAA has suspended all departures until further notice. International arrivals will be handled individually while technicians investigate the disruption.

The exact cause of the ground stop is not yet known, but in a statement obtained by CNBC, the FAA said, "communication issues" impacted air traffic control (ATC) centers. Aviation watchdog JonNYC reported that the halt is not a “9/11 type of event”, which required flights to be forced to land at the closest airport available.

Currently, all airborne aircraft are being allowed to continue flying until further notice. According to FlightAware, more than 6,500 flights are currently halted.

"There has been a temporary telecommunications outage - out of an abundance of caution all flights have been halted. Every flight has been accounted for and international flights will be handled on a case by case basis."

FlightRadar24 is reporting that more than 1,000 flights had already been canceled by 6:00 PM. Cirium data indicates that more than 11,000 flights were still set to depart across the US on Friday after the ground stop was put in place, so cancellations are expected to increase significantly as the evening progresses.

Ground controllers will also face growing difficulty as aircraft continue to land and none depart, putting pressure on already stressed infrastructure and increasignly scarce gate availability.
