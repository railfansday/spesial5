Senior Hells Angels bikie charged with threatening to kill investigative reporter Nick McKenzie

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Victoria police allege man, suspected of being a chapter president of the gang, sent ‘threats and harassing text messages’ to Nine journalist

Get our breaking news email, free app or daily news podcast

A senior Hells Angel has been charged with threatening to kill Nine’s investigative reporter Nick McKenzie, the Victorian police taskforce investigating corruption in the building sector has confirmed.

Police said detectives from the taskforce had charged a man on Monday, after he “sent a number of allegedly threatening, offensive and abusive text messages” to the Age journalist on Friday.

“A 36-year-old Moorolbark man, believed to be a chapter president of the Hells Angels OMCG, was arrested this morning,” police said.

“He has since been charged with make threats to kill and use a [telecommunications] device to menace harass and offend.”

The man was bailed to appear at the Ringwood magistrates court in February.

The Age’s editor, Patrick Elligett, said: “Threats against journalists are becoming all too common.

“If we accept this as normal, it will have a chilling effect on public interest reporting and our society will be poorer as a result.

“We’re pleased to see Victoria Police acting on these allegations.”

The taskforce that charged the man was established in 2024 in response to reporting from McKenzie and his colleagues into alleged corruption on Big Build projects in Victoria.

“Police would encourage anyone who has been threatened or who has any information on concerning behaviours in the construction industry to come forward and speak to police,” police said.

“Taskforce Hawk is focused on assessing new intelligence and evidence relating to allegations of criminal behaviour linked to the construction industry.

“It also continues to proactively target organised crime associated with the sector, including a focus on any individuals employed within the construction industry who have known links to outlaw motorcycle gangs.”
