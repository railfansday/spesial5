Miguel Ángel Félix Gallardo, Mexican Drug Lord, Dies in Prison

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Miguel Ángel Félix Gallardo, who helped build the foundations of modern-day drug empires, was the subject of a major U.S. manhunt for his role in the murder of a D.E.A. agent.

Paulina Villegas reported from Mexico City.

Miguel Ángel Félix Gallardo, a notorious Mexican drug lord who helped build the foundations of the modern drug trade, died a prisoner at the age of 80 on Sunday, according to the Mexican authorities.

Mr. Félix Gallardo became internationally known in the 1980s after the United States Drug Enforcement Administration accused him of the kidnapping, torture and murder of an undercover agent, Enrique (Kiki) Salazar Camarena.

On Friday, after decades behind bars, Mr. Félix Gallardo was transferred from the Puente Grande prison complex to a private hospital in Guadalajara after a medical emergency, according to the Jalisco state secretary of public security. He died around 2 p.m. Sunday of multiple organ failure, the agency said in a statement, citing medical personnel who treated him.

A small religious ceremony will be held at the hospital in the presence of his relatives, according to Jorge Eduardo Montiel, a spokesman for the Secretary of Public Security of Jalisco state.

When Mr. Félix Gallardo was arrested in 1989, he was described by the Mexican attorney general, Enrique Alvarez del Castillo, as the “No. 1 narcotics trafficker in Mexico.”
