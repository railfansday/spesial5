Meet Kiren: The New Mobile Security App Built to Protect Your Decisions Before You Tap

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

F-Secure introduces new consumer security brand that brings protection to online decisions,including free tools to help people check what's real before they click

LOS ANGELES, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- For years, mobile security has focused on keeping devices safe. But today's biggest risks start with something that looks completely ordinary: it's a text that looks genuine, or a website that looks completely legitimate. One tap can be all it takes. Today, F-Secure introduces Kiren, a completely new kind of mobile security built for the moment before that tap.  Kiren brings world-class security into the decisions people make every day on their phones. It helps users check suspicious content before they engage with it, while providing constant protection from online threats. The driving idea is simple: protection should be there the moment people need to make a choice online, not after the threat has reached their device. Until now, consumer cyber security has focused heavily on protecting user devices from malware. This is still important, but the focus has changed, and users need protection in the digital moments when they act and make decisions online. Kiren provides confidence in these moments.   "For a long time, security has been built like a wall, with the device on the inside and the danger on the outside. Now, as people's lives unfold online, the question they're asking has changed from 'is my phone safe?' to 'is this real?'," said Timo Laaksonen, President and CEO at F-Secure. "Answering that question takes a different kind of protection, one that works alongside people at the exact point they take action or make a decision and tells them what's real and what isn't. That's where security is heading, and that's why we built Kiren."  "Spotting a fake used to be a skill. Now it's a coin flip, because AI has erased all of the obvious tells," said Dr. Megan Squire, Principal Threat Intelligence Researcher at F-Secure. "Kiren checks the texts, links, images, and everything else for you and gives you a straight answer. People should be able to use their phones the way they want to, without feeling the need to play detective to do it."  Kiren. For the Real.  Kiren is available now in two tiers: Free and Plus. The free version gives users a way to check something that doesn't feel right. They can assess links and online shops, scan suspicious messages, and check their device health. The Plus version adds automatic protection, including checks for messages from unknown senders on SMS and WhatsApp, as well as protection against harmful websites and copycat banking pages. Users can also submit an image or screenshot to look for signs of a scam or possible AI manipulation. Personal data monitoring alerts users when information they've chosen to monitor appears in a breach. Android users also get antivirus protection.

Kiren is a mobile security brand from F-Secure that protects the decisions people make on their phones. It checks what's in front of you before you act and tells you plainly whether it's real, so you can get on with your digital life with confidence. Developed and run by a dedicated team at F-Secure, Kiren is available on the App Store and Google Play. Learn more at kiren.com. Kiren. For the Real.
