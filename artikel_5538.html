Mortgage Rates Dip Ahead of a Big Week for Inflation Data

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

In A Nutshell:  Mortgage rates are coming down as investors dial back expectations for rate hikes. But that could change: Key inflation data comes out this week.

Two highlights from a busy election week:

This week’s economics highlight is the CPI inflation data coming Tuesday, coupled with Friday’s PPI data. Together, the two will form a complete picture of the core PCE inflation measure the Fed uses. The consensus forecast has core CPI coming in at 0.2% month-over-month, down slightly from last month. Forecasters are expecting a small statistical distortion in the data due to last year’s government shutdown. A very low read for inflation might convince the Fed to hold off on hiking rates at the December meeting, but they could just as easily write it off as one data point. They would evaluate how broader financial conditions have changed since the last meeting, including any further improvement in rates.

Many Fed officials are holding speaking engagements this week. The ones to watch are Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan on Tuesday; Vice Chair Michelle Bowman on Monday, Tuesday, and Friday; Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack on Thursday; and New York Fed President John Williams on Thursday.
