Anthropic's IPO closes at $93.5 billion after underwriters exercise greenshoe

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Anthropic received about $91.6 billion of the proceeds, and a concurrent $26.0 billion private placement lifts the total to $119.5 billion

Anthropic closed its initial public offering with gross proceeds of about $93.5 billion after its underwriters exercised in full their option to buy additional Class A common stock, the company said on Friday.

The offering priced at $83 per share. Before underwriting discounts, commissions and other expenses, Anthropic received about $91.6 billion of the proceeds. The company said it received nothing from stock sold by selling stockholders.

Anthropic also closed a concurrent private placement of Class N common stock at $83 per share, for an aggregate price of about $26.0 billion. The buyers were Advanced Micro Devices, Broadcom $AVGO, Google $GOOGL, Micron $MU Technology, Microsoft $MSFT, Nvidia $NVDA and TSMC $TSM Partners. The company said the IPO and the private placement together brought in about $119.5 billion before deductions.

Anthropic's Class A stock began trading on the Nasdaq $NDAQ Global Select Market on Thursday under the symbol "ANT." The stock slid by over 10% in the hours before Friday's opening bell.

The underwriters' overallotment option is known as a greenshoe, according to CNBC. The $93.5 billion total surpasses the amount raised in SpaceX's record IPO in June.

Goldman Sachs $GS and Morgan Stanley $MS were lead book-running managers for the offering. The company said a registration statement became effective on Nov. 18 and that the offering was made only by means of a prospectus.

Anthropic debuted on the Nasdaq on Thursday in the largest IPO on record. The stock opened at $111.50 and closed at $97.17, 17% above the IPO price. The major U.S. averages each dropped more than 2% that day.

Anthropic priced the offering on Wednesday above the $70 to $78 range it had guided. Its underwriters had a 30-day option to buy up to 147 million more shares. SpaceX's total reached $85.7 billion after its underwriters exercised their own overallotment option.
