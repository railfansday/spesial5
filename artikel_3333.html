Audible’s Black Friday Deal Gets You a Subscription to Bestselling Books for Only $.99 Per Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Until Dec. 28, Audible's biggest sale of the season gives eligible customers 90% off a Standard membership, bringing the monthly price down to just $0.99.

All products and services featured are independently chosen by editors. However, Billboard may receive a commission on orders placed through its retail links, and the retailer may receive certain auditable data for accounting purposes.

Sometimes the best holiday shopping purchases are something for yourself, especially if it means having a new stack of audiobooks ready for the holidays.

I recently took advantage of the deal during my travels from Los Angeles to Edinburgh this week. I listened to Straight from the Heart, Bonnie Tyler’s memoir, narrated by the singer herself. It’s an intimate yet often funny account of her life, including how using her voice too soon after vocal cord surgery contributed to her signature raspy tone. I downloaded it before travelling so I could listen offline on the plane and also saved Dolly Parton’s Star of the Show: My Life on Stage to listen to later on.

With Audible Standard, you can choose one audiobook each month to keep in your library, revisit whenever you like and purchase additional titles separately if you whizz through them like me.

Brandy’s memoir was a major hit when it came out, becoming a New York Times bestseller a month after its release. It’s a must-read for fans who’ve followed her career since the ’90s. From Moesha and her iconic Cinderella role alongside Whitney Houston to her chart-topping hits, there’s plenty of nostalgia in this book. Brandy is also set to reunite with Monica for their 10-show The Boy Is Mine Las Vegas residency in 2027, making this a particularly good time to revisit her story.

With the next instalment of Cher’s memoir coming in November 2027, now is the perfect opportunity to catch up on the first chapter of the Grammy winner’s story. Cher: Part One explores her childhood, extraordinary rise to fame and turbulent marriage to Sonny Bono. Among its most striking revelations are allegations of financial control, infidelity and Bono’s admission that he had contemplated killing her. There’s plenty to discover before the next volume picks up around 1980.

As a huge Liza Minnelli fan, this is high on my listening list. Narrated by the EGOT-winning entertainer herself, the memoir promises an unfiltered look at growing up as the daughter of Judy Garland and Vincente Minnelli. Liza explores fame, relationships, addiction, health struggles and her remarkable career.

Published just three years before her sudden death in 2026, Bonnie Tyler’s memoir feels especially poignant now. Narrated by the Welsh singer herself, Straight from the Heart is a chance to hear her tell her own story, from the highs of international stardom to the personal experiences behind that unmistakable raspy voice. It’s candid, funny and a lovely tribute to a music legend.

Published in 2025, Dolly Parton’s photo memoir includes a downloadable companion PDF featuring a selection of photographs from the hardcover edition. The book follows Parton’s remarkable seven decades as a live performer, taking fans behind the scenes of her legendary career on stage. Co-written and co-narrated by Parton and Tom Roland, the audiobook is a particularly special listen for Dolly fans, offering a chance to hear the country music icon reflect on her extraordinary life and legacy so soon after her death.

Former San Francisco 49ers quarterback Colin Kaepernick revisits the protest that changed his career and made him one of the most talked-about athletes in America. After taking a knee during the national anthem in 2016, he became a polarising figure and ultimately never returned to the NFL. This memoir offers his perspective on the controversy and the personal experiences behind his decision.

An instant No. 1 New York Times bestseller, Famesick is the latest from Lena Dunham, the creator and star of HBO’s Girls. Known for turning her personal life into compelling on-screen storytelling, Dunham takes a similarly candid approach here, reflecting on fame, relationships and the health struggles that have shaped her life behind the scenes. Expect plenty of honesty and her signature self-deprecating humour.

I love a good audiobook, but I also adore the feel and smell of a physical book in my hands. That’s why I make a point of enjoying both formats throughout the year at a 50/50 ratio. My annual goal is to read 15 physical books and listen to 15 audiobooks, a balance that has worked well for me for years.

As an avid audiobook listener, one of my favourite Audible features is the adjustable narration speed. I usually start at normal speed to get familiar with the narrator before cranking it up to double speed once I’m settled in. The ability to download audiobooks, podcasts and Originals is another benefit, especially when travelling and without Wi-Fi on a plane. I also love the sleep timer, chapter navigation and the ability to sync my listening progress across devices. Moreover, the Audible Read & Listen feature allows you to pair eligible audiobooks with Kindle editions to follow along as you read.
