If you need some new tools for your holiday DIY projects, or if you have a DIYer on your list, Black Friday deals are one of the best ways to save money on tools. DeWalt has been making cordless tools for over 30 years, and they have a reputation for making durable, reliable tools. Here are the best Early Black Friday deals on DeWalt cordless tools.

A good quality drill and driver is the foundation of a home tool set because they allow you to drive screws and bolts and drill pilot holes for basic projects like putting up shelves, as well as more advanced woodworking and maintenance tasks.

The DeWalt 20-volt MAX drill and driver set is on sale on Amazon for $129, 46% off its regular price. The set comes with a drill, an impact driver, two 1.5-amp-hour batteries, a charger, and a tool bag. This is a good starter set because it has everything you need to use it right out of the box.

The DeWalt 20-volt compact drill is on sale at Home Depot for $99, 45% off its usual price. It comes with a 20-volt, 2-amp-hour battery and a charger. This is the first basic cordless tool you need for most DIY home maintenance.

The DeWalt 20-volt compact impact driver is on sale at Home Depot for $99, 45% off its usual price. It comes with a 2-amp-hour battery and a charger. This is a good tool for an expanding home tool set, allowing you to drive screws and bolts into tougher materials with less effort.

Saws and trimming tools are a key part of a compact tool set if you plan to cut and shape your materials. They can be useful for DIY projects from fence repair to installing custom trim.

The DeWalt 60-volt Flex 7 ¼-inch circular saw is on sale at Home Depot for $299, 52% off its regular price. This saw comes with a 60-20-volt Flex 12-amp-hour battery. You’ll need a charger for the high-capacity battery. This is a good saw for working farther away from a power source because the battery is designed for longer run-time between charges.

The DeWalt 20-volt MAX cordless jigsaw and the DeWalt 20-volt MAX cordless router are on sale at Home Depot for $199 at Home Depot, 60% off its usual price. They come with a 4-amp-hour battery, a 6-amp-hour battery, and a charger. These are woodworking tools that can be used to shape trim and make specialty cuts.

The DeWalt 60-volt Flex compact cordless angle grinder is on sale at Home Depot for $299, 51% off its typical price. This tool comes with a 20-60-volt battery. You’ll need a charger for the high-capacity battery. This is a good tool for metalworking or for grinding off screw and nail tips if they poke through your material.

The DeWalt 20-volt oscillating multi-tool set is on sale for $99, 50% off its regular price. This set comes with the oscillating multitool, a 2-amp-hour battery, and sanding attachment and accessory wrench, and four sheets of sandpaper. This is a versatile tool that can be used for cutting and finishing projects.

Batteries are one of the most expensive parts of a cordless tool set, and having a few extras so you can always have a fresh battery on the charger to swap out helps keep your DIY projects moving.

A two-pack of DeWalt 20-volt MAX 2-amp-hour batteries is on sale at Home Depot for $79, 53% off its regular price.

A set of two DeWalt 20-volt MAX batteries and two 20-volt MAX 4-amp-hour batteries is on sale for $199, 45% off its typical price.

Yes, Home Depot has already launched its early Black Friday deals, offering deep discounts on major brands of cordless power tools. They also update their sales page on Thanksgiving day and include Black Friday in-store-only doorbusters as part of their seasonal sales.

Home Depot and Lowe’s often compete with each other for seasonal sales, so it’s always a good idea to check both sites before buying. Right now, Home Depot has the best deals on DeWalt and Milwaukee cordless tool sets, but Lowe’s has a better deal on Craftsman hand tool sets.

The best prices of the year on tools are during the holidays in November and December. This is because of the holiday shopping push, as well as making space for new tools that come out in the spring. The second-best time to buy tools is in May and June during Father’s Day sales.
