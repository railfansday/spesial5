John Mayer Transports Michael J. Fox Back to the Future With Chuck Berry Performance

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

John Mayer and Brad Paisley joined Michael J. Fox onstage Saturday night for a rendition of Chuck Berry’s “Johnny B. Goode,” the song Fox played in the climax of Back to the Future. Guitar World reports the performance was part of Fox’s A Funny Thing Happened on the Way to Cure Parkinson’s gala, which took place at New York’s Waldorf Astoria.

The performance, which also featured Steve Jordan and Super Soul Banned, came after Mayer’s cover of Tom Petty’s “Free Fallin'” (a duet with Paisley) and songs off Mayer’s Continuum and Battle Studies albums. Video of “Johnny B. Goode” shows Fox playing a Les Paul on stage left with Paisley in the middle and Mayer, who sings the lyrics, to his right. Paisley sings backup as Fox plays bluesy filigrees, the likes of which evoke “that new sound you’re looking for.” When they finished, Fox thanked the musicians with an emotional speech. Even if Fox’s performance in the film didn’t really inspire Berry’s revolutionary fusion of blues, R&B, and country music to create rock & roll (as Back to the Future suggests), it did inspire Mayer. In 2015, the singer-songwriter, who joined Fox for another go at the song two years earlier, called the scene “the big Rocky moment for a lot of kids who were into music” in an MSNBC interview. “That for me was the moment the nerd gets revenge.”

“[John Mayer] said he got into playing guitar because of that scene in Back to the Future,” Fox once said on Entertainment Tonight. “And now look at him – he’s one of the greatest guitar players in the world!”
