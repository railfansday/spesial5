'Outlander's 2-Part Spin-Off Officially Returns for Season Finale in 1 Day

After two months of expecting the worst and hoping for the best, Outlander: Blood of My Blood Season 2 ends in less than 24 hours. A prequel to the hit series Outlander, the historical fantasy drama explores the love stories of two couples who lived long before Jamie and Claire Fraser's romance began. Rather than adapting Diana Gabaldon's novels directly, the series expands the Outlander universe by focusing on the parents of its central characters: Jamie's parents, Brian Fraser (Jamie Roy) and Ellen MacKenzie (Harriet Slater), and Claire's parents, Henry Beauchamp (Jeremy Irvine) and Julia Moriston (Hermione Corfield).

Before Outlander wrapped up its eight-season run in May 2026, Blood of My Blood premiered on August 8, 2025. The first season concluded after ten episodes on October 10, 2025, but the network had already renewed the gripping drama for a second season in June, ahead of the finale. Season 2 premiered on September 18, 2026, and though it is already approaching its tenth and final episode, a third season has yet to be greenlit. This begs the question: Is this the end for Outlander: Blood of My Blood? Well, it's still too early to say, since TV shows often get renewed later than anticipated.

With that, the Season 2 finale of Outlander: Blood of My Blood, titled "Twelfth Night," is scheduled to premiere tomorrow, November 27, amid the Thanksgiving break. The episode airs on Starz at 8 p.m. ET, where new episodes have premiered weekly since the romance series debuted. This comes two weeks after a scheduled pause in the Season 2 schedule, as no new episode aired on November 13, after eight straight weeks of releases.

Four worlds and four completely different ideas of what a good life looks like. This is where you belong, what it would ask of you, and what you would get back in return.

You belong somewhere old enough that the mountains have names and somebody can still tell you who gave them. Middle-earth is for people who think the right thing is worth doing even when it will not be finished in their lifetime.

You want the sea, a crew, and absolutely nobody telling you where you are allowed to go. The Grand Line is the world for people whose main ambition in life is finding out what is over there.

You would rather be feared and useful than liked and useless. The Continent is a world where the monsters are the straightforward part of the job and the people are where it all gets complicated.

You want the myths to be real and loose in the actual world, and you want to find out at about thirteen that you were never anywhere near as ordinary as everybody kept telling you.

The penultimate episode, "Another Man's Shadow," leads directly into the Blood of My Blood finale, offering some much-needed answers while raising even more questions. A "Twelfth Night" preview has also been released, hinting at high-stakes danger and a festive titular gathering (referencing Shakespeare's Twelfth Night) that turns violent, setting the stage for a potentially shocking cliffhanger.

Speaking with Collider about the challenges their characters face this season on Outlander: Blood of My Blood, Corfield and Irvine teased that the finale will leave fans eager for a potential third season, thanks to that cliffhanger. However, Corfield admitted that she is uncertain about Julia's fate and what might happen next. "I don't know what's coming next, to be completely honest, from where the season ends," she said. "We have no idea. It could go anywhere … We're either hanging in the balance or being tortured."
