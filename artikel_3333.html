‘Much Ado About Nothing’ Starring Tom Hiddleston Joins Sell Outs ‘A Few Good Men’, ‘Other Desert Cities’ & ‘Hamilton’ – Broadway Box Office

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Broadway business was off about 13% last week (from the previous week and from last season at this time) – maybe Halloween Saturday pulled away some attention? – with total box office for the 33 shows hitting $29,572,284. Attendance for the week ending November 1 was 251,700, down 4% from the previous week and 9% year-to-year.

Most shows – 26 of the 33 – reported downturns, some considerable in the six figures. Both & Juliet and The Outsiders were down by more than $300,000 from the previous week (to, respectively, $610,232 and $605,946).

Still, four shows managed to buck the trend and sell out:

Other Desert Cities also topped the ticket price roster, with its average paid attendance at a hefty $301.23. Only two other productions topped the $200 mark: Much Ado About Nothing ($238.36) and Hamilton ($218.03). The overall average paid admission for the 33 shows was $117.49.

Three other shows were in previews last week:

Billy Crystal’s 860 had three heavily comped press performances but still pulled in a strong $1,353,611 for seven performances. Attendance was at 99%.

In the 23rd week of the 2026-27 season, Broadway has grossed $726,010,709 season-to-date, down about 8% from last year at this time, with total attendance of 5,929,169 down 3% year-to-year.

All figures courtesy of The Broadway League. For more box office information visit the League’s website.
