Raye, Rosalía, and Ella Langley Lead 2027 Grammy Nominees

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Raye, Rosalía, and Ella Langley led the nominees for the 69th annual Grammy Awards, which were announced Monday morning, Nov. 16.

Raye led all nominees with a staggering 11, which ties Beyoncé’s 2025 for the most nominations in a single year for a woman artist. She’s up for all three major prizes: Album for the year for This Music May Contain Hope, and Record and Song of the Year for “Where Is My Husband!” Raye also earned a nomination for Songwriter of the Year, Non-Classical.

Rosalía — fresh off her historic victories at the Latin Grammys — followed close behind Raye with nine nominations, with Lux up for Album of the Year, and “Berghain” with Björk and Yves Tumor nominated for Record and Song of the Year. And Langley notched seven nominations, including Record and Song of the Year looks for her smash, “Choosin’ Texas”; Album of the Year for Dandelion; and Best New Artist.

Langley was one of four artists to score seven nominations, alongside Phoebe Bridgers, Olivia Rodrigo, and Lambert (who co-wrote “Choosin’ Texas”). Other top nominees included Bruno Mars, Leon Thomas, Olivia Dean, Taylor Swift, and Geese.

For Record of the Year, “Where Is My Husband!”, “Berghain,” and “Choosin’ Texas” will compete against Dean’s “Man I Need,” Mars’ “I Just Might,” Rodrigo’s “The Cure,” Swift’s “The Fate of Ophelia,” and Tame Impala’s “Dracula” remix with Jennie.

Song of the Year will also feature “Berghain,” “Choosin’ Texas,” “The Cure,” “Man I Need,” and “Where Is My Husband!”, as well as Swift’s “I Knew It, I Knew You,” Mars’ “Risk It All,” and Ariana Grande’s “Hate That I Made You Love Me.”

And in Album of This Music May Contain Hope, Lux, and Dandelion will compete against Bridgers’ Lost Weekend, Mars’ The Romantic, Rodrigo’s You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl So in Love, and Swift’s The Life of a Showgirl.

There’s arguably no clear frontrunner for this year’s Grammys, with many of the major category contestants — including Raye, Dean, Langley, Rosalía, and Bridgers — competing in Album, Song, and Record of the Year for the first time. Of these four, Raye and Langley are the only artists without Grammys, while Bridgers has yet to win as a solo artist. (She does have several Grammys with Boygenius, and earned Album and Record of the Year nominations with the group, too.) Rosalía picked up Best Latin Rock Album Grammys in 2020 and 2023, while Dean won Best New Artist at the most recent Grammys.

This could also be the year Rodrigo finally wins a major prize, with her latest album You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl So in Love garnering the mix of critical accolades and commercial success that usually makes a Grammy favorite. Rodrigo previously won Best New Artist in 2022, and has a couple of rock prizes to her name, but has yet to win Song, Record, or Album of the Year.

Of course, all of these musicians will ultimately have to face off against two of the most established and decorated Grammy winners of recent decades: Mars and Swift. They have 14 and 16 wins, respectively, including several in the major categories. Swift could even extend her record as the artist with the most Album of the Year wins to five if The Life of a Showgirl takes home the prize.

But Swift might be even more keen on finally winning Song of the Year in 2027. Beyond the major categories, the nominations for 69th Grammy Awards feature what might be one of the best showings for new rock & roll bands in years. Brooklyn outfit Geese and Quebec duo, Angine de Poitrine both feature prominently in the Best New Artist category, and picked up several nods in the rock and alternative categories. Geese earned five nomination total, while Angine de Poitrine earned four; both acts are first-time Grammy nominees.

Slayyyter, who enjoyed a breakout year with her third album, Worst Girl in America, picked up her first Grammy nods, earning four total. And Don Toliver earned his first nominations as a solo artist, after previously scoring a look for his contributions to Kanye West’s Donda.

And then there’s all the fun, strange, and exciting stuff buried in the 100 categories that comprise the Grammys. Tinashe and Underscores both scored their first nominations, as did living legend John Cale, who picked up a look for Best Song Written for Visual Media for his work on “House” from Charli XCX’s Wuthering Heights soundtrack. (Charli, meanwhile, showed up in the Best Alternative Music Album for Music, Fashion, Film.)

Bo Burnham, a previous Grammy winner for his comedy song “All Eyes On Me,” picked up two nominations for his contributions to Bridgers’ Lost Weekend. And Dolly Parton scored three posthumous nods, including two in the Country Duo/Group Performance category, and one for Best Audio Book.

The 2027 Grammy Awards will feature five new categories as well. These include Best Asian Pop Music Performance, Best R&B Collaboration or Duo/Group Performance, Best Traditional Pop Vocal Performance, Best Traditional Folk Album, and Best Latin Song. Those nominees and more are below.
