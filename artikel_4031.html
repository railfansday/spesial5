Sea Of Thieves Is Getting A Massively Reduced File Size On Xbox And Other Platforms

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Anyone who's planning to play GTA 6 this week seems to be feeling the pinch in terms of storage space, so you might be glad to hear that Sea Of Thieves is getting a ridiculously large reduction in install size this Thursday.

On Xbox Series X, for example, the game is going from over 100GB down to 56.4GB, while players on Xbox One and Xbox Series S are only looking at a mere 25+GB to keep the game installed. An amazing improvement all round!
