Final Fantasy Resonance Devs Are Somehow Surprised That Fans Still Like Turn-Based Combat

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

"I expected opinions on it might be divided"

Final Fantasy Resonance was met with high praise when it launched on 22nd October 2026, earning a score of 8/10 from us and a critic average of 85 over on Metacritic.

Despite being an adaptation of a somewhat middling mobile title, Resonance's focus on turn-based combat mixed with the handsome HD-2D visuals made it an instant fan-favourite. The developers, however, seem somehow surprised at the positive reception, believing that opinions would be a bit more divided.

This comes via an interview at Game Spark with producer Keisuke Nakashima, co-producer Miki Kimura, and director Hiroto Furuya (thanks, Automaton), who all feel that Resonance's reception exceeded their expectations.

Nakanishi-san said, “I think the action-oriented and 3D Final Fantasy games are wonderful too, but I was surprised to see just how many people, not only in Japan but overseas as well, are eager for the kind of FF they remember from back in the day, with classic turn-based battles and pixel art.”

Development actually focused on appealing to more old-school fans of the series, with Nakanishi stating that the intended direction was for Resonance to feel almost like a follow-up to Final Fantasy 6. "We figured it was something we ourselves would enjoy, and that there would be players out there who’d feel the same way", he said.

With the series being so heavily focused on 3D visuals and action-oriented gameplay in recent years, the team simply didn't expect Resonance to have such mainstream appeal.

"I thought this was a rather unconventional title, so I expected opinions on it might be divided", said Kimura-san. "I think the fact that a game with such an unusual background has been embraced by so many people is a positive for the industry as a whole, and I’m personally very happy to see it.”

Hopefully the positive reception will lead to more original games being made that focus on turn-based combat and 'retro' visuals. There's clearly a demand for it!
