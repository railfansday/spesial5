Fantasy RPG 'Sovereign Tower' Brings Some Round Table Management To Switch Next Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Sovereign Tower launched on Steam earlier this year and quickly picked up its fair share of fans for its wacky round table management. Today, developer Wild Wits and publisher Curve Games have announced that we Switch fans will be able to get in on the fun in the new year.

That's right, this one is coming to Switch on 14th January 2027. In it, you play as the sovereign of the titular tower, thrust into power after the untimely demise of your predecessor and tasked with keeping the kingdom up and running. To do so, you'll have to recruit knights and match their unique skills to tasks across the land — nabbing stolen manuscripts, taking down evil beings, negotiating with vampires, you know the drill.

There's a rewind function for when things go south, and you can even romance some of those handsome knights you'll meet along the way.

Here's a brief rundown of its key features and a handful of screens so you can soak in the cool visuals:

Your Reign, Your Rules - Every choice you make defines your legacy and the fate of your kingdom. Recruit eccentric Knights - Manage a court of unpredictable heroes with big egos and even bigger problems. Manage Your Round Table - Send knights on epic quests where success or disaster hinges on your judgment. Bend Time and Tempt Fate - Uncover secrets, pursue forbidden romances, and when it all goes wrong, rewind time like they never happened in the first place. Expand Your Tower - Build new annexes, acquire upgrades, and outwit rival rulers in increasingly impressive displays of sovereignty.

January is already filling up with Switch releases, but this could just end up being one to look out for.
