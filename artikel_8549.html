The Coinbase One Card is Coinbase’s credit card, issued by First Electronic Bank and run on the American Express network, and only paying Coinbase One members can get it. Its bitcoin back depends on how much you keep on Coinbase and lands in your Coinbase account, usually as each purchase posts.
