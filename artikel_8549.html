Get the Alliant Cashback Visa® Signature Credit Card if you’re an Alliant member who wants a no-fee Visa for trips abroad, since it adds no foreign transaction fee to purchases there. At home, its flat 1.5% cash back earns less than no-fee cards paying 2%, so use one of those for everyday spending.

The Cashback Visa Signature is Alliant Credit Union’s flat-rate cash back card, with no annual fee and no spending categories to track. It’s open only to Alliant members of at least 90 days, though anyone can apply to join the credit union.

Alliant members who travel abroad get a Visa with no annual or foreign transaction fee, whose cash back can go straight into their Alliant account. Everyone else earns more at home from a no-fee cash back card paying 2% instead of 1.5%, and travelers who aren’t members get the same 1.5% with no foreign transaction fee from the Capital One Quicksilver.
