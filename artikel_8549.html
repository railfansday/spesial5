Open Barclays Tiered Savings for its $200 bonus if you can move $25,000 from other banks and keep it there 120 days. By our math, the bonus and its 3.50% rate earn about $90 more on that sum over six months than an account paying 4.40%. Once the bonus posts, move your savings to the higher rate.

Tiered Savings is the higher-paying of Barclays’ two online savings accounts, but its 3.50% annual percentage yield (APY) trails the 4.40% Pibank pays with no minimum balance. Pibank sends money out by wire, which your bank may charge to receive, yet on $50,000 Barclays’ lower rate pays $450 less a year by our math, more than twice the one-time bonus.

Savers with more than $25,000 earn the most by moving just the $25,000 the bonus asks and keeping the rest at a higher rate. Below $25,000 there’s no bonus, and Barclays’ 3.50% earns less from the first day than Pibank’s 4.40%.
