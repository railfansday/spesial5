US declassifies more UFO footage filmed in the Middle East

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The US government released an eighth tranche of records regarding Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAPs), marking the latest step in an effort to declassify government files on UFOs.

After US President Donald Trump called for UFO files to be released in an executive order earlier this year, the Pentagon has declassified hundreds of files. The first tranche was released 8 May 2026.

The footage in this video makes up three of the 43 files released in this tranche. All three clips were filmed from aboard US military platforms in the Middle East in 2020, 2023 and 2026.
