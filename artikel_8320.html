CDT Trail Town Warns Hikers of Suspected Rat Poison on Local Trails

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Authorities in the CDT trail town of Helena, Montana are warning the public that suspected rat poison has been discovered on trails in the area. Hikers and locals are being asked to keep a close eye on the ground while hiking local trails, especially if accompanied by dogs.

In a recent Facebook post, Helena police described the suspected poison as small, green tablets. Authorities say that accidentally ingesting the tablets poses a serious health risk, particularly to pets and small children.

For day hikers and trail runners who visit the CDT near Helena with their dogs and families, police urge extra caution and close supervision.

The first incident reportedly occurred on October 6th, when an individual reported finding a small quantity of the suspected rat poison on a local trail. With no information on a suspect or additional reports, several weeks passed with no updates.

However, over the weekend, police announced that more of the suspected poison has surfaced across multiple trails in the Helena vicinity.

If you come across small, green tablets or any other suspicious substances while hiking, do not touch them, keep your pets and children away from the area, and contact the Helena Police Department immediately to report the location.
