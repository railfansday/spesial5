The NWSL got bigger in 2026, with 16 teams, two new clubs in Boston and Denver, 30 games apiece, and a midseason break to make room for the men's World Cup. Somehow it got tighter, too. After eight months of soccer, the final playoff spot came down to the last hour of Decision Day, when the Seattle Reign slipped past the Orlando Pride 1-0 and sent both the North Carolina Courage and Denver Summit home. The eight teams still standing tell you a lot about where the league is now. Gotham went from No. 8-seed champion last fall to first-time NWSL Shield winners this year. Utah Royals are making their playoff debut. San Diego Wave finished right on Gotham's heels, and many others all have a real case that this is their year.
