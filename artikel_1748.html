Opinion: African midfielder is destined to join Liverpool, he could be the next Nyoni

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

An exciting Malian midfielder is destined to join Liverpool.

When you think of Mali and the Reds, you remember Djimi Traore. He might not have been one of the best players to have ever played for the club, but his contributions in Istanbul in 2005 on that fated May evening will be remembered forever.

Traore's blocks and the way he gave it his all to help inspire the greatest comeback in the history of the sport are indelible. They live on through the stories and moving images that have been recounted time and time again about that night.

Another Malian. This time a midfielder who was so good when he first arrived. At his best, he looked like he was going to be on par with the likes of Michael Essien.

But injuries hampered Sissoko's time at Anfield.

Liverpool need a midfielder this winterThe Reds could so desperately do with someone of Sissoko's profile in this winter market. What the Malian was known for was his brilliance defensively and his composure on the ball. Right now, Andoni Iraola's squad is lacking a lot of players capable of instilling that composure on the ball. Dominik Szoboszlai and Ryan Gravenberch are both erratic midfielders. Alexis Mac Allister has stepped into their shoes and has done really well. Trey Nyoni as a mere teenager, has right now also become pretty much the first name on the teamsheet when it comes to the midfield. Iraola clearly needs more in that department. He needs players who are capable of playing like Nyoni and progressing the ball from deep.
