Prediction: Manchester United vs Brentford

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Desperate to find a route out of the Premier League drop zone this weekend, Manchester United will welcome Brentford to Old Trafford on Saturday afternoon.

With a trio of top-flight showdowns pencilled in on Saturday, there are plenty of attractive Premier League betting promotions available.

With all eyes falling on Old Trafford this weekend, you can find all of the key Manchester United vs Brentford H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, officially getting Mauricio Pochettino’s tenure underway with an infamous 4-0 drumming away at bitter historic rivals Liverpool last weekend, the former Tottenham boss is more than aware that he has an almighty job on his hands at Old Trafford. Suffering even more public frustration earlier in the week as they were eventually forced to settle for a controversial 2-2 stalemate away at Portuguese giants Sporting Lisbon, the Red Devils have shattered a string of unwanted records this season. Remarkably opening Saturday’s return to Manchester sat in the relegation zone, United have collected just a sole win from any of their first 11 Premier League appearances. Completely blown away at Anfield last weekend, Pochettino’s new squad have gained a real reputation for their glaring defensive struggles. In fact, already shipping 23 top-flight strikes, only Crystal Palace (27) have conceded more Premier League goals so far this campaign.

While Brazilian international Matheus Cunha might have missed out in Portugal on Wednesday night after serving a Champions League suspension, the ex-Wolves star is free to feature here. However, picking up a thigh injury against Roma earlier in the month, big-money summer recruit Carlos Baleba remains on the sidelines.

Manchester United have confirmed that former RB Leipzig marksman Benjamin Sesko will miss the remainder of the calendar year due to an extended shin issue. Bagging a Champions League brace at his former stomping ground on Wednesday night, skipper Bruno Fernandes will once again play a leading role in the final third.

As for the visitors, marking their return from November’s international break with a sensational display in west London as they stormed their way to a 4-0 romp against Everton, Brentford have skyrocketed their way back into the European spots. However, despite enjoying a rugged start to proceedings when opening up the 2026/27 campaign back in August, Keith Andrews’ camp are searching for some recent stability. Heading into November’s fortnight interval with a 2-0 loss away at Brighton, Saturday’s guests have won just a pair of their previous seven appearances across all competitions. Watching their EFL Cup adventure come to an end last month as they fell to a rollercoaster 3-2 defeat against Sunderland, Brentford have also failed to find their feet outside of west London. In fact, continuing on from last season, Andrews’ men have registered just a single Premier League win on their travels since the end of February.

Shown a straight red card against Brighton prior to November’s international break, attacking talisman Igor Thiago remains on the sidelines through suspension. Sustaining a hamstring injury while away with Scotland over the past fortnight, former Bologna defender Aaron Hickey will miss out here.

Bagging a first-half brace against Everton on Monday night, the versatile Keane Lewis-Potter will start at left-back. Despite still waiting to open his Premier League account this season, summer recruit Callum Wilson should keep his spot leading the line at Old Trafford.

Suffering more frustration on Wednesday night as they were eventually forced to settle for a thrilling 2-2 stalemate away at Sporting Lisbon, Pochettino has endured a worrying opening week in the Manchester United dugout. Completely blown away at Anfield last weekend as they slumped to that woeful 4-0 drumming against Liverpool, Saturday’s hosts astonishingly find themselves sat in the drop zone and they have gained an unwanted reputation for their glaring defensive issues. Already conceding 23 Premier League strikes so far this season, Man United once again have a huge amount of pressure on their shoulders at Old Trafford.
