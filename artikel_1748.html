Opinion: Liverpool are getting back a world-class player, that's confirmed

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Liverpool are getting a world-class player back after the international break. That's just confirmed at this point.

The international break is nearly over. Liverpool are back in action at the weekend and it's a massive one to say the least - Manchester United at Anfield.

Not just any United side, either, but a new-look one under Mauricio Pocchetino. The Argentine has taken over from Michael Carrick and this, incredibly, will be his debut in the dugout.

It's difficult to know what Liverpool should expect as a result of that. United could be re-inspired or it could be their 14th consecutive false dawn - we just can't be sure.

What we can be sure of, though, is that the Reds look like they'll be returning from the break in good form. One player, in particular, looks world-class.

Florian Wirtz has been hit-and-miss since joining Liverpool. Mainly miss, if we're honest.

But there's no question that he's one of the world's most talented footballers and the problem has always been that Liverpool can't get the best out of him. Certainly, they view him as a generational talent in Germany and that's what the Reds thought they were buying.

They were right, too. Wirtz is exactly that - you've just got to get the most out of him to do that. It's the same for any elite footballer, too.

If you don't put them in an environment to succeed, you won't see their elite level. The margins at the top are too fine that it's virtually impossible to stand out if things aren't setup well for you.

This season, though, we're beginning to see more of Wirtz's best. He's had some brilliant moments and ran some victories for Liverpool. We just need to see it more consistently.

It seems obvious, too, that last season sapped Wirtz of his confidence. It was, after all, the first time in his career that he didn't exceed expectations. It's difficult to know how a player reacts to that and the German retreated into himself a bit.

It's telling, for instance, that Jurgen Klopp has made it his mission to build that confidence back up since taking over the German National Team.

“I only knew him from television. I was shocked when I saw him on the pitch for the first time,” said Klopp after Germany beat the Netherlands 4-0.

“An extraordinary footballer. We have also found the right position for Flo. It helps that he is so vocal. He is the first pressing player, can press two players without getting tired.”

Wirtz is reacting to that confidence building very well, too. He was outstanding against the Dutch, scoring twice and with both goals demanding elite quality.

It was the performance of a world-class player and we're confident that Klopp's plan is working. He's sending a player full of confidence back to Liverpool and with that, the Reds are getting a genuine superstar.

Now it's their turn to keep Wirtz's momentum going.
