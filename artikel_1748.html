‘The 22’ launch event info – November 6th 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

I just wanted to give you details of our London event for the launch of The 22, the limited edition book we told you about last week.

This will be taking place at Big Red on Holloway Road on Friday November 6th, from 4pm – https://maps.app.goo.gl/BVG9SCHXuYaKbhar9

If you’d like to come along, please register your interest via this link – https://arseblog-yumastudios.eventbrite.co.uk

We’ll have music, drinks, Arsenal fans, and of course a very special book celebrating the Premier League title win and the incredible celebrations that followed on the streets of North London.

On Monday, those registered will receive an email which will allow them to purchase the book ahead of the event, and collect it on the night. These purchases will be for collection only.

It will also be possible to take payment on the evening if you prefer to do it that way. Books are limited to a maximum of two per person.

Pre-sale for Arseblog Patreon members will now be on Friday October 30th at 3pm (Dublin/London). We’ll make a post on Patreon with a link to get your copy!

The remaining books will go on sale on November 7th, with worldwide shipping available.
