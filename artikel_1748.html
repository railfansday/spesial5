Kompany urges Bayern to embrace the challenge of Champions League

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Vincent Kompany did not delve too deep into Bayern Munich's defeat to Lille in the Champions League, instead urging his players to embrace the fight of reaching the knockouts.

Bayern were beaten for the first time in Europe this season at the Stade Pierre-Mauroy on Wednesday, with Aleksandar Pavlovic's own goal sealing a 2-1 victory for Lille.

Despite having both Harry Kane and Michael Olise out injured, Kompany's side took the lead in the 15th minute when Dayot Upamecano nodded in from Tom Bischof's corner.

However, Lille turned the game on its head in the space of three second-half minutes, with Ayase Ueda curling into the bottom corner after seeing an earlier goal ruled out for a foul on Joshua Kimmich, with his strike chalked off after a VAR review.

And Lille profited from some misfortune in the 65th minute. Ethan Mbappe's shot hit the right post, with Pavlovic comically turning into his own net under no real pressure.

The result saw Bayern lose a Champions League group stage game having been ahead at half-time for the first time since November 2014 against Manchester City (3-2).

Bayern were also beaten by a French side other than Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League for the first time since November 2009 (2-0 against Bordeaux), having won seven in a row in between against Lyon (three), Marseille and Lille (both two).

Kompany's side are now 15th in the 36-team standings and two points adrift of the top eight, with fixtures against Slavia Prague, Manchester United and Real Betis remaining.

"We tried, so I can't blame the energy. The second goal obviously shouldn't happen," Kompany told reporters.

"But like I said, I know these moments from my experience. We now have to fight for our own luck. Stick together, battle and also enjoy this fight a bit."

Bayern largely kept Lille at bay in the first half, with Ueda drawing a fine stop from Jonas Urbig – in for the injured Manuel Neuer – soon after Upamecano's opener.

Hiroki Ito flicked a header wide and Mbappe saw another chance saved, while Berke Ozer had to be on his toes to keep Konrad Laimer out at the other end.

"The first half was exactly how we imagined, in terms of our strengths and also our weaknesses," Kompany added. "But overall it was a very good first half.

"Then we just didn't get into our rhythm any more in the second half. That's part of football, you always have to play your game until the last minute."

There were just 213 seconds between Ueda's equaliser for Lille in the 62nd minute and Pavlovic's own goal in the 65th minute to put the French team ahead.

Lille's dominance in the second half was backed up by the statistics, with their expected goals (xG) total of 1.49 from their 10 attempts dwarfing Bayern's tally of 0.22 from their six shots, with only two of those on target.

"We played a very decent first half, where we implemented a lot of things in the way we wanted. We were aggressive and had good energy," Laimer said.

"We lost that a bit in the second half. Then we concede the two goals, which happened too easily. We no longer produced what we're capable of.

"If you don't reach your highest possible level in the Champions League, you'll come away with nothing.

"We stick together and try, with the players we have, to play our football. Now it's about recovering quickly because it continues already on Saturday."
