Maura Higgins Reportedly Postponed ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Rehearsals Leading to Semifinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

“Mark had to readjust some of the choreo.”

Maura Higgins has been battling a rib injury behind the scenes on Dancing With the Stars, and the reality star has reportedly had to postpone multiple rehearsals this week leading to the semifinals.

“Mark had to readjust some of the choreo for her on next week’s dance,” a source tells The Sun. “They also had to postpone two different rehearsals so she can rest. But she’s pushing through the pain—which is getting worse every week but she’s determined to not let it stop her.”

Here’s Maura arriving to rehearsals on November 15, so clearly she’s still in it to win it.

Viewers saw Maura experience rib pain during rehearsals for Grammy Week, and she told Entertainment Tonight after the show that she’d ended up in the hospital. “It was quite a traumatic week. I’m not gonna lie,” she said. “Like we lost so much rehearsal time. I ended up in the hospital. I couldn’t really breathe. The swelling was so bad. But I didn’t want to give up. I didn’t want to throw the towel in. And I literally was like I just need to fight through this. I’m just glad I’m still here. And I’m feeling so much better now.”

Maura also confirmed that while X-rays showed there’s no crack in her rib, muscle and cartilage damage would “feel the exact same” and that the only treatment is to wait and let it heal.

Here’s to hoping Maura gets a chance to rest up and heal ahead of the DWTS semifinals Tuesday night!
