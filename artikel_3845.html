Grand Theft Auto VI in-game animated series ‘Macca & Friends’ teaser trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Rockstar Games has released a teaser trailer for Macca & Friends, an animated series featured in the world of Grand Theft Auto VI.

The series follows the adventures of Macca the Gator and his wetland friends across the state of Leonida, where Grand Theft Auto VI is set.

Grand Theft Auto VI is due out for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series on November 19.
