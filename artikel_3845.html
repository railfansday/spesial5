Famitsu Sales: 11/16/26 – 11/22/26 [Update]

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Famitsu has published its estimated physical game software data for Japan for the week of November 16, 2026 to November 22, 2026.

Grand Theft Auto VI for PlayStation 5, which is sold as a code in a box at retail, was the highest selling release of the week, opening at 200,759 retail copies sold.

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for Switch 2 placed second with an additional 35,295 retail copies sold.

On the hardware side, the PlayStation 5 family sold 41,472 units, Switch 2 sold 36,817 units, the Switch family sold 4,362 units, and the Xbox Series family sold 751 units.
