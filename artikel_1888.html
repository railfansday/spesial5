Doncaster Rovers the latest Yorkshire League One side to have game postponed due to international call-ups

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Rovers lost both games in the three-week September/October break for the same reason. Thy had been due to play Oxford United and Luton Town. This is Doncaster's only game of this shorter international window. Their next League One match will now be at Bradford City on November 21. Doncaster are currently 20th in League One, a point above the relegation zone.

A new date for Orient's visit will be announced in due course.
