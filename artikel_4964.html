Morning Minute: Jensen Returns, Markets Soar (Except Crypto)

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Morning Minute: Jensen Returns, Markets Soar (Except Crypto)

Morning Minute is a daily newsletter written by Tyler Warner. The analysis and opinions expressed are his own and do not necessarily reflect those of Decrypt. And check out our daily news show 'FOMO HOUR' covering all of the top stories and market action.

Six days after Nvidia’s board chair told investors “the Nvidia board does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO,” the board voted unanimously to reinstate him as president, CEO and director, effective immediately.

Nvidia shares rose 33% to around $167, adding roughly a trillion dollars of market value in a session. Huang’s personal net worth climbed about $36.6 billion on his 3.5% stake, moving him to 13th on the Forbes global list. Short sellers took roughly $12.2 billion in paper losses, their worst single day since at least 2019, which wiped out the $10.5 billion they had booked the prior week.

The stock market absolutely ripped on the news. The Nasdaq closed up 6.51% at 24,089, its best day since April 2025. The S&P gained 4.7%, the Dow was +3%, and the VIX collapsed 25.8% to 26.62.

Bitcoin went along with the ride—for a few hours. It touched $63,929 intraday, up 5.1%, then spent the afternoon handing it back and finished near $61,000, barely up on the day. The ETFs used the pump to exit more, with BTC ETFs seeing another $570M in outflows on the day.

ETH and other majors also closed red. Alts gave back their gains. And the CMC top 20 index managed just 0.20% gain.

That’s a 19 to 1 ratio between what the Nasdaq captured and what Bitcoin kept, on the same catalyst, on the same day. Pain for the crypto bulls.

What makes it even more painful, is that crypto fell with AI stocks the whole way down, and the standard explanation was that both trade on the same liquidity and the same risk appetite. Monday tested that and the correlation fell through. The AI trade got a catalyst and markets bounced. Crypto didn’t bounce with it.

As for Nvidia’s reversal decision itself, questions remain to say the least. The board says it was satisfied with Huang’s “candor and ability to lead,” and that input from senior U.S. officials left it confident his return “poses no national security risk.” Trump publicly pushed for it and said the board had sought assurance on the espionage allegations. What actually changed between November 17 and November 23 hasn’t been explained. The independent directors’ investigation is still open, and Nvidia is now standing up a board committee for national security and export-control oversight plus recruiting a chief compliance officer.

Perhaps answers will trickle out over time. The AI trade may not need them anyway. And crypto, well, it's in its own world right now, waiting for its own catalysts (likely quantum-related). But it’s very unclear that catalyst is coming in 2026, and it’s becoming clear that many are not interested in waiting for the answers…
