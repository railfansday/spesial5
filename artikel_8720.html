Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Investors will turn their attention back to inflation and artificial intelligence in the week ahead, with October consumer prices due Tuesday and post-election volatility still hanging over technology stocks.

The CPI report is expected to show headline inflation running at 3.7%, with airfare seen posting another notable increase. Fed speakers are also scattered through the week, including Austan Goolsbee, Tom Barkin, Jeffrey Schmid, Lorie Logan and Mary Daly.

AI will remain firmly in focus. Anthropic (ANT) is expected to begin its IPO road show and could file an updated S-1/A with an initial price range and finalized valuation target. Adobe (ADBE) kicks off its three-day MAX event Tuesday, while Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM) is due to release monthly revenue.

Earnings are due from KKR (KKR), Rocket Lab (RKLB), Take-Two Interactive (TTWO), Occidental Petroleum (OXY), CoreWeave (CRWV), Cisco (CSCO), Applied Materials (AMAT) and Nebius (NBIS).
