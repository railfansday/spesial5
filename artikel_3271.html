Good morning! It's going to be a big day for anybody excited for GTA 6. Reviews are expected to drop, and the game will start going live (the lucky folks in New Zealand are up first). There's plenty to talk about, so let's dive into the latest GTA 6 updates.

Of course, the single biggest question today is when exactly GTA 6 reviews will appear. Rockstar hasn't officially confirmed when critics will start delivering their verdicts on the game. However, a recent article from Insider Gaming claims that GTA 6 reviews are embargoed until today (November 18) at 3 a.m. PT / 6 a.m. ET / 11 a.m. GMT. That would mean GTA 6 reviews in less than an hour. But without word from Rockstar, this reporting should be taken with a grain of salt. It's not confirmed.

GTA 6 reviews aren't even live yet, and already the game has been nominated for a slew of awards at the upcoming Game Awards 2026. It's up for eight awards, including Game of the Year. Other nominees for the event's top honor include The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, 007: First Light, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem and Control Resonant. That's great company to be in, and it would suggest that GTA 6 won't disappoint! You can check out the full list of nominees here.

I've seen a lot of online debate about GTA 6's Metacritic score. As of writing, the highest scored game of 2026 is the Ocarina of Time remake at 96. Meanwhile, GTA 5 scored an impressive 97 back in 2013, and GTA 4 got a stunning 98. Where do you think GTA 6 will land on the popular review aggregate site. Let me know in the poll.

If you're planning to play GTA 6 on PS5, there's an important update that you need to download first. Yesterday, PlayStation released a new PS5 system software update (version 26.07-14.23.00), and over on X, PlayStation Support US confirmed you will need to download it on your console before playing GTA 6 tomorrow. The post reads:

"Sony Interactive has issued a PS5 system software update that is required before playing Grand Theft Auto VI. Players should download this update prior to midnight on November 19 so that they’re ready to play on launch day, November 19."

So, even if you're already pre-loaded GTA 6, make sure to switch on your PS5 and get the update downloaded to your console ahead of the game unlocking at midnight.

A big decision when it comes to GTA 6 is which version to get, the standard or the Ultimate Edition, which costs an extra $20, but comes with plenty of extra digital goodies. Let me know which edition of the game you've gone for in the poll above.

After 13 years of waiting, it's a little surreal to say that GTA 6 arrives in just 10 minutes. If you live in New Zealand. Rockstar has opted for a staggered release time, with the game "unlocking" at midnight in your specific region. Because of timezones, this means the lucky folks in New Zealand can enter Vice City first (I'm not jealous...).

Reminder, it's also been reported that GTA 6 reviews will drop as the game goes live in New Zealand, so we might be minutes away from getting our first critical impressions of GTA 6 as well. Should (hopefully) be lots to discuss very soon.

GTA 6 reviews are going live, and the first one I've spotted is from Giant Bomb, their reviewer, Dan Ryckert, has given the game a full 5 stars, and said:

"Grand Theft Auto VI is Rockstar’s best game. In terms of inspiring awe at how far video games have come, I can only compare it to formative experiences of mine like playing Super Mario 64 or Metal Gear Solid for the first time. In the thirteen years between entries of one of the biggest franchises in entertainment, fans’ expectations grew to a level that felt unrealistic. Somehow, Rockstar has managed to overdeliver on the most hyped video game of all time."

VGC's Jordan Middler says, "Grand Theft Auto 6 is a modern masterpiece. Rockstar's developers have created a remarkable open world that excels in storytelling, gameplay, visuals, music and technology to achieve something truly unmatched. Minor performance issues don’t dull the sparkle of an absolute must-play experience. GTA 6 was more than worth the wait." VGC has given GTA 6 a full 5/5!

Over on review site OpenCritic, GTA 6 is off to a flying start; it's currently got a 99 average score, with 100% of critics recommending it. Rockstar looks to have delivered and then some!

"Unprecedented. Unbelievable. Unmissable." That's the strapline for IGN's GTA 6 review. Quite a way to open any review.

IGN's reviewer, Luke Reilly, says, "GTA 6 has not stopped astonishing me since I started it. I regularly find myself in love with video games, but it’s extraordinarily rare that I find myself in complete awe of them. Put simply, Grand Theft Auto 6 is the single most impressive video game I have ever played."

Wow. As of writing, Grand Theft Auto VI holds a near-perfect 99 score on Metacritic. This would tie it with the best reviewed game of all time, the original Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Of course, this score could fluctuate as more review scores pour in, but the game is off to one heck of a start. Could this be the best reviewed GTA in history?

Another GTA 6 review, another perfect score. This time from Destructoid. Their reviewer, Scott Duwe, writes, "Grand Theft Auto 6 is part criminal come-up tale, part love story, and part life sim all wrapped up in an impossibly deep and detailed Rockstar Games open world that may never run out of things to do. For the last three-plus weeks, I have been gleefully vacationing in Vice City: robbing banks, fighting drug dealers, stealing cars, and enjoying the view while not coming close to finishing everything it has to offer. GTA 6 is finally here now, and it was well worth the wait."

Our friends over at TechRadar, have plenty of positive things to say about GTA 6. In a 5-star review, TR's Rob Dwiar, says, "Grand Theft Auto 6 is an absolute triumph, and one of the most complete, most gripping, and downright coolest video games I have ever played, and is an essential must-play experience."

Another five star review comes from Gamesradar. Reviewer Josh West writes, "GTA 6 is the highest-fidelity sandbox of its generation. Rockstar has built an open world of truly astonishing scale, scope, and quality. There's a degree of sophistication to everything GTA 6 does, and there's no aspect of the series' gameplay that hasn't been improved over past instalments."

If you had any doubts about pre-ordering GTA 6, these reviews might have swayed you. The game is getting perfect scores across the board, and is being described as "the game of the generation." You can still lock-in your pre-order, and if you opt for the "code in box" edition you get a free poster as well (and £5 discount in the U.K.).

More reviews have gone live, and as of writing, GTA 6 is the highest-rated game on Metacritic. It's at an astonishing 99 after 146 reviews. While the original Ocarina of Time also boasts a 99 score, that's from a much smaller sample size of just 22 reviews (it's a game from 1998, before the explosion in online game reviews). This score could change as more reviews are published, and post-launch reviews could be more critical, but so far, it's holding. GTA 6 really is getting unprecedented review scores.

There was some controversy yesterday when GTA 6 was nominated for eight Game Awards, including GOTY, without it being available to the public. Those nominations certainly don't look undeserved now, based on the reviews that have just dropped. At this point, you would have to say that GTA 6 looks like the clear favorite to win GOTY.
