Denis Villeneuve Says ‘Dune: Part Three’ Is “Something Even Better” Than The First Two Films

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Denis Villeneuve is teasing Dune: Part Three, saying the third film in the trilogy is his best yet.

In an interview with GQ, the filmmaker explained why he thinks the third one is his favorite.

“The second one was much better than the first one because I had learned, because the storytelling was better and the first one was more contemplative,” Villeneuve said on the One More Question podcast.

He continued, “It was meant to be like that, but I wanted to drift into something a bit more muscular. Villeneuve added, “As an artist, I feel that I was able to do something that I was not able to achieve in the two first ones. There’s tension there. There’s an emotional journey that is strong, which I’m really happy with. How will it be perceived? How you will receive it? As a filmmaker, I’m satisfied that there was a progression.” Watch on Deadline

Dune: Part Three is set to open in theaters on December 18. The film, directed by Villeneuve, was co-written by him and Brian K. Vaughan and is based on Frank Herbert’s novel Dune Messiah.
