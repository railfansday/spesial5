Jamaica Revisited: A New Expression of Caribbean Luxury

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Sandals Resorts elevates its robust amenities across the properties of its home island.

There are places in the sun that are just a little more special than the rest. Jamaica is one of them, with powder-sand shores lined with luxury resorts; a dense, verdant interior laced with waterfalls and coffee farms; waters that range from the lightest turquoise to the darkest navy; and a distinctive cuisine layered with spice. The beaches are spectacular and the natural wonders such as the crystalline YS Falls and the winding Martha Brae River are just that—full of awe and adventure.

The Taíno name for Jamaica is Xaymaca, which translates to “the land of wood and water”—and few descriptions capture the island more distinctly. For Sandals Resorts, that ethos has inspired a homegrown luxury. Born in Jamaica more than four decades ago, the brand remains deeply rooted in the island’s familial spirit, personality, food, and topography, even as its properties continue to evolve beyond their birthplace. The result is an immersive travel experience shaped by beaches, lush mountains, local flavors, deep culture, and the people who make Jamaica an unforgettable destination.

From the energy of Montego Bay and Ocho Rios to the sweeping coastline of Negril and the more tranquil pockets of the South Coast, the seven Sandals resorts across Jamaica each offer distinct ways to settle into the island. Across the collection properties, accommodations function as private retreats within a bigger resort experience. Swim-Up Suites allow guests to slide directly from the terrace into the water. Private pools create an aquatic world unto themselves. And on the Caribbean Sea, the Overwater Butler Villas turn a quiet resting place above the water into a pampered vacation lifestyle.

This year, a trio of properties are entering significantly new chapters following gorgeous reimaginings. The iconic comebacks promise renewed accommodations, including new Swim-Up suites, and new dining options. Cocooned by nature thanks to its setting beside a 500-acre preserve, Sandals Jamaica South Coast reopens November 18, with Overwater Butler Villas and the brand’s first Two Queen Junior Suites—perfect for friend-group travel. One month later, December 18 sees the return of Sandals Jamaica Montego Bay, introducing a redesigned pool, Sandals Jamaica Caribbean Cay features newly refreshed accommodations, which are luxurious cocoons. Some days the hardest decision you make might be whether to head for the beach, dine at your favorite restaurant, or remain in your robe exactly where you are.

At Sandals Jamaica Montego Bay, the resort where the brand first began, renewed ocean-view suites promise an impressive vantage point to view the gorgeous ocean. The new Bay Roc Rum Club embraces the music and rum culture that’s part of the fabric of Jamaica and the Caribbean.

Nearby, the former Sandals Jamaica Royal Caribbean emerges as Sandals Jamaica Caribbean Cay with a unique resort experience centered around the sea and water. The new SkyPool Suites will feature a private plunge pool with an infinity edge that’s cantilevered from a large open air terrace, allowing guests to swim while enjoying uninterrupted views of the white sand and aqua waters below.

Further south, the secluded Sandals Jamaica South Coast offers ultra-luxury villas, including detached overwater villas arranged in a heart shape over the calm lagoon. Each comes equipped with see-through glass floor panels, outdoor overwater hammocks, and private boat dock access for the ultimate ocean-to-door service.

Jamaican food rarely arrives without presence. Scotch bonnets bring the heat, pimento adds the spice, and jerk carries the intensity of wood and smoke long before the plate lands on the table. That sense of heritage is part of the experience at Sandals Jamaica Caribbean Cay with Suppa, a new restaurant inspired by the island ritual of Sunday dinner. The menu celebrates familiar Jamaican flavors in savory dishes, stews, and curries served in a casual, supper-style setting made for sharing plates around the table. The adjoining Keynote Rum Bar features a soundtrack of the island’s legends in a laid-back lounge drenched in warm leather and rich tones, plus an extensive Caribbean rum selection.

The waterfront Buccan at Sandals Jamaica Montego Bay brings a different kind of fire. This open air space is where meats, seafood, and veggies are cooked over an open hearth and served at family-style tables set by the ocean. The well-received concept first debuted at the lush and secluded Sandals Saint Vincent, nestled in its own private cove, and is now arriving in Jamaica.

Visiting Sandals Jamaica South Coast, every beach day can start with Jamaica’s renowned Blue Mountain coffee and island pastries at BLŪM before afternoons stretch into hours at The Jerk Shack, enjoying must-have Jamaican jerk chicken, jerk pork, fish in foil, and other heritage-style comfort snacks. At the newly arrived Butch’s Island Chop House, excellent steak dinners and perfectly chilled martinis are on the menu.

Arguably one of the greatest luxuries while on a getaway is not having to weigh every decision. Sandals’ all-inclusive model is built around freedom. Not only are meals, snacks, and drinks included, but so are many of the resort experiences that turn a beach trip into an adventure, such as snorkeling, paddleboarding, kayaking, and daily diving for certified scuba divers, as well as classes to become PADI certified. Back on land, guests can move from the beach to an active day at the tennis and pickleball courts. For the golf lover, a day at Sandals Ochi, Sandals Dunn’s River, and Sandals Royal Plantation in Jamaica also include complimentary greens fees at Upton Estate Golf & Country Club in the green hills above Ocho Rios.

All-inclusive leaves room for every traveler to have their own version of the Caribbean holiday. One guest may spend the morning beneath the surface of the sea and the afternoon learning pickleball, then indulging in fresh sushi and sashimi. Another may decide the day’s only pursuit should be moving from a suite to a lounge chair, then being served jerk chicken or just-out-the-oven pizza beachside.

For all of Jamaica’s waterfalls, rivers, lagoons, beaches, and mountains, the element travelers often cherish most is a feeling of welcome. The ease of a conversation with the butler, the waiter who remembers your daily coffee order, the laugh with the bartender that extends a quick interaction into a memory, the recommendation to try a local dish, visit a secluded beach or a local shop or market that might have otherwise been missed is waiting in Jamaica.

That kind of Jamaican hospitality at Sandals cannot be engineered by simply refreshing a pool or designed into a new suite. It comes from people knowing how to welcome guests to their home. At Sandals Resorts, the flavor, the memorable landscapes, and the contagious Jamaican warmth makes visitors feel less like they simply arrived at a destination—and more like they’ll be welcomed back again and again.Book your Jamaican getaway today at sandals.com.
