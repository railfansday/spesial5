Penélope Cruz Says It’s Time for Women Directors to Be the Norm

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

With Bunker, The Invite, and La Bola Negra all out this year, ELLE Women in Hollywood honoree Penélope Cruz told three very different stories through her characters. She dedicated her speech at our Women in Hollywood event to the writers and directors behind those projects, along with her The Invite co-star Seth Rogen. She also called out the lack of women directors in Hollywood, saying it’s time for things to change.

“I wanted to share this recognition with all the people that over the years have given me the opportunity to make a living out of this profession. That was my prayer every night,” Cruz began. “I also want to share it with four directors and two writers. Florian Zeller [who directed Bunker], thank you for your talent and for your trust and for allowing me to express through a character my anger and sadness about the fact that a story where people are building bunkers to save themselves is not a story of science fiction anymore. Thank you, Javier Calvo and Javier Ambrosi, my new best friends, for giving me the treasure of a character that is Nené Romero in your incredible, genius film, La Bola Negra. And Guitarrica is here, and so [I’m] sharing [this honor] with you and all the cast of the film.”

“I want to thank the incredible Olivia Wilde. You are brilliant,” she added of The Invite’s director. “I really loved making our film. The only thing that I didn’t like about it is that once again, when you are being directed by a woman, you get to do all the press, that is still the number one question: Does it feel very different to be directed by a woman? And I feel really like after 30 years working, when are we going to get to the day where that is not worth [it] to ask anymore because it’s just a normal, common thing? That is truly a very strong wish that I have.

“Seth [Rogen], I don’t know where you went, but what a privilege it has been to sing Sade for you in this film. What a privilege to work with you, with Edward [Norton], with Olivia, to be part of a film that brings people to the theater and makes them laugh and cry at the same time for two hours. People are stopping us in the streets now to tell us all their secrets about their relationships. And others, and I’m not joking, are sending us letters to inform us about their very recent divorce—in a good way. So, the process of making this movie was interesting. Everything had to happen very quickly, but thanks to the generosity of the writers Will [McCormack] and Rashida [Jones] and the generosity of Olivia, it happened. It was one of those miracles where everything gets put together very quickly, and it happened. And truly, it was important for me today to name Will and Rashida because I haven’t met in my career people with that amount of generosity and zero ego, so this is also for them.”

Cruz ended her speech by shouting out her longtime agent, team, and her brother, who attended the event with her.
