Our Favorite Loafer Now Comes in Menswear’s Favorite Leather

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

A store I worked at in the 2010s used to stock dozens of styles of well-known shoe brands, and while our inventory was well-stocked, the rare cordovan loafers that popped up were almost mythical. There would be murmurs of a possible restock and by the time I showed up for my shift they’d be gone. In other words, cordovan loafers aren’t always easy to come by. I bring this up because Meermin’s 25th Anniversary Unlined Penny Loafer is now available in a gorgeous burgundy shell cordovan—one that, considering the nature of both the material and the build, should hold up for another 25 years of consistent wear.

First, a quick little primer on Meermin: the Mallorca-based, family-owned shoemaker started in, you guessed it, 2001. They decided to put decades of luxury industry experience into their own, less-marked-up, dexterously-made products. 25 years later, and the Unlined Penny Loafer is the catalog’s crown jewel—tenaciously timeless, super versatile and as comfortable as loafers come—and thus the bedrock the brand has riffed-upon for the anniversary.

We should also note that shell cordovan leather is very much not like the other leathers. For starters, it can only come from horsehide, and specifically from the underlayers of the rump, which is the (actual) technical term for the ass and also a very finite region of hide. Cordovan is notoriously sturdy. Like, damn-near-indestructible, will-outlive-the-cockroaches, kind of durable. And, like many long-lasting materials, it develops an inimitable character with age, such that one could argue it gets more beautiful with each scuff.

And if you’re worried about the bottoms of a pair of shoes designed to last decades—and millions of steps—the flexible chestnut bark-tanned leather outsoles Meermin employs are affixed with a resoleable Goodyear welt, so you won’t need to fret about the inevitably asymmetrical wear-and-tear. If there was a single nit to pick with the endeavor, it’d be that these are a limited release and don’t allow for a whole lot of waffling. So, if you’re in the market for some forever loafers, your search could be over in a matter of minutes. Head on over to Meermin to check ‘em out in all their glory.
