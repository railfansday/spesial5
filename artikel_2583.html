Cooper DeJean: DK Metcalf "just missed" poking me in the eye

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Eagles defensive back Cooper DeJean says Steelers wide receiver DK Metcalf didn’t poke him in the eye on Sunday, but came pretty close.

“Just missed,” DeJean said, via the Philadelphia Inquirer.

Metcalf was ejected from Sunday’s game after multiple incidents and two personal foul penalties, but DeJean said nothing that happened during the game could explain what had Metcalf so upset.

“We were just competing,” DeJean said. “Things like that happen in football. It wasn’t anything crazy, and no one was really saying anything. It was just two guys going at it. He was trying to block me, I was trying to get him off me, things like that, and then it obviously carried over to whatever that was when he got thrown out.”

Metcalf had just one catch for two yards before he was kicked out, and DeJean suspects that Metcalf is mostly upset with his own production.

“He wasn’t getting the ball a whole lot during that game,” DeJean said. “Some receivers, they let that get to them. Obviously he tried to take it out on me.”
