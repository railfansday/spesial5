Stripe: Briefing Content Strategist

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

The Global Executive Content and Experiences team creates experiences for executive audiences that build advocacy and trust, strengthen relationships, and generate revenue. We develop content and programs that help Stripe attract, grow, and retain our most strategic users.

As an Executive Briefing Content Strategist on the Executive Briefing team, you’ll turn account research, business insights, and Stripe’s product capabilities into tailored content for executive briefings. Your work will help executive audiences understand how Stripe can support their business objectives and create new opportunities for growth.

You’ll partner with Executive Briefing Managers and colleagues across Product Marketing, Product, Sales, Customer Success, and Stripe’s executive leadership to develop materials that educate and inspire existing and prospective users. Through compelling narratives and visual storytelling, you’ll help build trust in Stripe as a financial infrastructure partner.
