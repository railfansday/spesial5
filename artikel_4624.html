Healthgrades Announces Top Hospitals for Specialty Care, Outpatient Procedures

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Follow Healthline on our socials for all things health.

Hospital ratings have become an important resource for choosing where to receive care. They influence which doctors patients choose, how far they are willing to travel, and whether they seek care at a different facility.

On October 27, Healthgrades announced the recipients of the 2027 Specialty Excellence Outpatient Awards and Specialty State Rankings.

The annual awards recognize hospitals delivering outstanding patient outcomes in specialty care across the United States. Healthgrades is the leading platform used by Americans for finding a doctor or hospital.

New research from Healthgrades highlights that hospital quality is increasingly a factor in healthcare decisions.

Around 80% of patients report that when looking for a doctor, the quality of the hospital where they practice matters. Additionally, 7 in 10 patients (68%) say they’re more likely to seek care for a procedure or health condition from a doctor who works at a hospital with high quality ratings.

What’s more, around 71% of patients say they would switch doctors to gain access to a higher-rated hospital.

“Our specialty awards identify the nation’s top-performing hospitals across numerous medical specialties, helping consumers find high quality care tailored to their specific needs,” said Alana Biggers, MD, MPH, medical advisor at Healthgrades.

Biggers said that outstanding hospitals recognize that high quality care extends beyond just treating an illness.

“It involves listening to patients, understanding their individual circumstances, and ensuring they feel respected, informed, and involved in their healthcare decisions,” she said.

Patients want to find a hospital that excels in the procedures or health conditions relevant to them.

The recipients of the Specialty Excellence Awards are among the top 15% of hospitals specializing in these areas.

Healthgrades evaluated patient outcomes at roughly 4,500 hospitals across 31 common procedures and conditions to reach their decisions.

Among the list of Specialty Excellence Award recipients, Healthgrades also recognizes America’s 100 Best Hospitals for Specialty Care for 11 specialty areas andAmerica’s 50 Best Hospitals for Specialty Care for four specialty areas.

“An outstanding hospital prioritizes both patient care and the people delivering that care,” Biggers told Healthline.

“While clinical outcomes, patient safety, and access to evidence-based treatments are essential measures of quality, I believe hospitals must also invest in workforce development and support. This means ensuring physicians, nurses, and other healthcare professionals have appropriate staffing levels, adequate clinical and administrative support, and opportunities for continued professional development,” she continued.

Healthgrades awards U.S. hospitals that excel at Outpatient Orthopedic Surgery, Outpatient Joint Replacement, andOutpatient Prostate Care.

Outpatient care is one of the fastest-growing areas of healthcare. The number of orthopedic procedures performed in an outpatient environment has grown exponentially in recent years.

From 2020 to 2025, outpatient hip replacement procedures grew by 81%, while inpatient procedures dropped by 75%. Outpatient knee replacements increased by 71% while inpatient volumes decreased by 70%.

“I believe several factors are contributing to the growing demand for outpatient care, including advances in medical technology, a greater emphasis on preventive care, and the increasing need to manage chronic medical conditions,” Biggers said.

“Many services and procedures that previously required hospitalization can now be safely performed in outpatient settings. Patients also appreciate the convenience of receiving care closer to home and avoiding unnecessary hospital stays,” she added.

To meet the increasing demand for outpatient procedures, Biggers cautioned that healthcare systems must ensure that these services are adequately staffed and supported.

“Effective communication and coordination between outpatient providers, specialists, and hospitals are essential to maintaining continuity and quality of care,” she said.

State Rankings identify the top three or five hospitals in eligible states across 19 key specialty areas.

The rankings are based on clinical performance and build on the 2027 Healthgrades Specialty Excellence Awards Methodology.

State-level specialty care rankings help consumers find outstanding local hospitals for their condition or procedure.

The analysis used Medicare inpatient data from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) from 2023 to 2025, and assessed patient outcomes across 31 conditions and procedures.

These outcomes served as the foundation for determining Specialty Excellence Awards and state hospital rankings.

With consumer-facing awards and ratings influencing healthcare decisions, transparency about their methods and limitations remains essential.

Ratings can help patients ask better questions and compare options, but they should complement — not replace — clinical guidance and informed decision making.
