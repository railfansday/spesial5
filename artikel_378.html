Lego is giving away free holiday reindeer builds this Friday: How to claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

On Nov. 13 from 5-7 p.m. local time, participating Lego stores across the U.S. and Canada are hosting a holiday make-and-take event. Attendees can build a miniature Lego Reindeer model and take it home at no cost. It doesn't get any more festive than that.

The set is not available for purchase and is limited to one build per guest on a first-come, first-served basis while inventory lasts.

The event is aimed at builders aged 6 and up, making it a great low-stakes Friday evening pitstop whether you're bringing kids along or just want a quick five-minute breather after a long week of meetings.

The only real catch is the two-hour window with limited supply. That's a recipe for long lines and rapid stockouts, so we recommend getting to your local store early.

You should also be aware that free events like these are generally designed to pull foot traffic through the front doors before Black Friday. If you step inside to grab your Lego Reindeer, you will be surrounded by high-ticket holiday temptations. It's up to you to stay strong. There's no need to walk away with a Lego Death Star, right?
