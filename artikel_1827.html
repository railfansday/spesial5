Luis Enrique vows Doue will ‘come back even stronger’ after ‘serious injury’

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Luis Enrique confirmed Desire Doue suffered a "serious injury" during Paris Saint-Germain's victory over Nice, but insisted the winger will "come back even stronger".

PSG extended their winning streak to 12 matches in all competitions after prevailing 3-2 at Allianz Riviera, now the longest run of Luis Enrique's managerial career.

Mohammed Amoura had given Nice an eighth-minute lead before two goals in the space of a minute turned the game on its head for the defending Ligue 1 champions.

Senny Mayulu's 31st-minute penalty was followed up by Doue's sensational finish just 55 seconds later, the shortest gap between two PSG goals across Ligue 1 and the Champions League over the last 20 years.

However, PSG star Doue was then stretchered off the pitch just before the break after appearing to twist his right knee awkwardly in a challenge with Sofiane Diop.

The France international has been an influential figure for PSG this season with 10 direct goal involvements (six goals, four assists), but there is now a fear he could be set to join long-term absentee Khvicha Kvaratskhelia in the treatment room.

"We have to wait for the final exams to know exactly what the injury is, but it's a serious injury," Luis Enrique said of Doue.

"It's clear. We could see it on television. It's the life of high-level professional players, we can see situations like that on almost every matchday.

"You have to accept it. I'm sure he'll be able to recover very well and that he'll come back even stronger."

Doue's replacement Ferran Torres went on to score what proved to be the decisive goal in the 85th minute, sealing Luis Enrique's 300th top-flight victory as a coach.

Ferran has now scored four goals as a substitute in Ligue 1 this season, the most by any player in Europe's top five leagues, and he acknowledged the importance of the win.

"This was a very important win," Ferran said. "It wasn't our best display, but ultimately, we were competitive and got the three points.

"It's always important to win, especially after an international break, as it puts the team at ease. Ultimately, we got the three points which are our priority.
