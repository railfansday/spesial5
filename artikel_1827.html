‘Like him a lot’: Celtic star attracting interest ahead of January transfer window

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Celtic midfielder Benjamin Nygren is on the radar of Atalanta and Bologna, according to reports in Italy.

Nygren joined Celtic from FC Nordsjaelland in the summer of 2025 for a fee in the region of £1.5 million and made a big impact, scoring 21 goals across all competitions as the champions retained their Premiership crown and also lifted the Scottish Cup.

The 25-year-old also represented Sweden earlier this year at the World Cup and remains an integral part of Graham Potter’s international squad.

This season, Nygren has netted eight times in 21 games. And with the January transfer window approaching, it is understood that two Serie A clubs in Atalanta and Bologna are weighing up a bid for Nygren.

Respected Italian transfer journalist Gianluca di Marzio has claimed in an interview with Rekatochklart that a move could be made for Nygren on the back of his performances over the past 18 months,

“Nygren is a very good player and Atalanta like him a lot, but they haven’t made a move for him yet,” said Di Marzio. “He is a target and they are following him.

“Bologna like him too, but no negotiations have started yet. They follow him because he is doing very well both for Celtic and Sweden.”

Bologna have taken an active interest over the past few years in the Scottish market, signing defender Aaron Hickey from Hearts and midfielder Lewis Ferguson from Aberdeen.

Atalanta were also very interested in Matt O’Riley before the Celtic midfielder moved to Brighton and Hove Albion rather than Serie A in 2024.

Nygren admitted in September that there was interest in him after the World Cup this summer, although nothing materialised for the Swede.

“Yeah, there was interest,” he said. “Was it more than that? No, I play for a big club. When the right time is to move on, I cannot answer that. That’s up to me, the club and the potential club. So whenever the right time is, it is.

“I play for a huge club like Celtic. I really enjoy being here. I wanted to stay here to play in the biggest competition. Unfortunately, we didn’t make it to the Champions League, but we still play in the Europa League. I want to make it better here and then go further in the Europa League that we did last season.

“I wanted to stay here and continue to make it good and when the right time is, the right time is. But it’s not something I think of now. It’s not my focus. My focus is just to do as well as possible here and to win more trophies and then what happens in the future, we’ll see.”

Nygren is contracted to Celtic until 2030 and the Premiership leaders would likely demand a hefty fee for his services.
