Alex Baena urges Arsenal star to join Atletico Madrid: ‘He’s the second best in the world’

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Alex Baena has urged Atletico Madrid to sign Martin Zubimendi and believes the Arsenal midfielder is the second best in the world behind Rodri.

Zubimendi joined Arsenal in a £60 million deal from Real Sociedad last year and the 27-year-old played an integral role in the team’s Premier League title success under Mikel Arteta last season.

However, Zubimendi is yet to start a Premier League game this term and now faces increased competition for a place in Arsenal’s midfield following the arrival of Bruno Guimaraes in the summer transfer window.

Zubimendi has been linked with a move away from Arsenal ahead of the January transfer window and Chelsea are understood to have registered their interest.

Baena, who was also part of Spain’s World Cup success in the summer alongside Zubimendi, is keen to see his countryman return to La Liga.

When asked about the prospect of Zubimendi joining Atletico, Baena told Cadena SER: ‘I would love for him to come.

‘He is the second best defensive midfielder in the world after Rodri.

‘I hope he can come. He would be a great addition to the team and the dressing room.’

Baena also spoke out on Arsenal’s summer transfer target Julian Alvarez, who pushed for a move to Barcelona but ended up staying at Atletico and received hostility from the club’s supporters.

‘It certainly affects him,’ Baena said about the backlash Alvarez has endured since the start of the season.

‘He knows what he did back then. He’s already apologised. We also understand the fans’ anger at the way he acted.

‘But I think he’s working very hard, trying to get back to his old self. I think if he keeps this up, people will grow to like him more.’
