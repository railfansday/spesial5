Antonio Conte breaks silence on Manchester United links after Mauricio Pochettino hire

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Antonio Conte has hit back at the suggestion his intense managerial style is the reason he was passed over for the Manchester United job.

The Italian, formerly a Premier League winner with Chelsea, was one of several names linked with the United vacancy after Michael Carrick was sacked earlier this month.

He missed out on the opportunity to Mauricio Pochettino, though, with United paying around £7.5m to release him from his contract with the United States national team. Conte, meanwhile, remains available as a free agent.

Some reports suggested United opted against moving for Conte because they were “scared” of his “brutal” methods.

Conte, though, has hit back at those reports in a new interview with the Italian outlet Cronache di spogliatoio, saying that he boasts a proven track record for winning titles.

“First, let me address the question of my method,” he began. “If anyone is frightened by my method, then they are frightened of winning, because that methodology leads to victory. That is my answer.

“Manchester United has such a significant history that I find it strange to imagine anyone could be afraid of an approach aimed at restoring the club to its true stature.”

He continued, saying that he had met with Sir Alex Ferguson in an attempt to learn the secret behind his dominant Manchester United dynasty: “(United) last won the league in 2013, under Ferguson. I have immense respect for Sir Alex Ferguson.

“I’ve read his book, and when I was in London, I was fortunate enough to visit his home and spend a day with him. He has an incredible passion for talking about football, though, at times, his Scottish accent can be quite hard to follow! Through his own methodology and approach, he made Manchester United great.

“That is why I find certain remarks almost offensive toward United; it’s as if someone were placing a veto on the club’s return to its own history.
