Pochettino will face the media ahead of United’s meeting with Aston Villa as preparations continue.

Manchester United transfer target Yasin Ayari appeared to pour cold water on any potential move to Old Trafford - before implying that he is keeping his options open.

Ayari has been a key part of Brighton’s bright start to the Premier League campaign, hitting three goals in nine appearances.

Ayari is out of contract this summer leading to speculation regarding his future. United are said to be among the clubs keeping an eye on his situation.

They are far from the only club credited with an interest in the 23-year-old, though. Premier League leaders Arsenal, Serie A outfit Roma, and Turkish duo Fenerbahce and Galatasaray are all reported to be keeping tabs on the Swede.

But, speaking from the Swedish national team camp this week, Ayari appeared to play down reports linking him with a move away from the AmEx: “I’m at Brighton and I’m happy where I am. Things are going well for me right now. Then we’ll see what’s to come.”

JJ Gabriel has confirmed his decision to leave Manchester United in a statement, with Real Madrid expected to win the race to sign the highly-rated young winger.

The 16-year-old has played in United’s academy since 2020 and was expected to start appearing in the first team this season.

Until, that is, he made the shock decision to lodge a transfer request in September, amid reports he and his entourage were frustrated with his lack of first-team opportunities.

United worked hard to convince him to stay and offered him a new contract, but the player has not trained at the club for some time and is now expected to join Real Madrid. He will leave Old Trafford without having made a senior appearance.

After months of speculation as to where he might end up, Gabriel has now issued the clearest update yet on his situation, confirming his decision to leave United in a post to his Instagram page.

Speaking over clips of himself playing for the club, he said: “Dear Manchester United, I arrived in 2020 as a 10-year-old boy with big dreams.

“Here, I grew as a player and a person. From scoring at Old Trafford to receiving the Jimmy Murphy Award, you gave me moments I’ll always treasure.

“To every coach, every member of staff, to my team-mates and everyone who supported me, thank you for believing in me and helping me grow.

“As I begin my next chapter, Manchester United will always be a part of my story. Thank you for everything, JJ.”

QPR youngster Leon Scarlett is attracting interest from Eintracht Frankfurt and AC Milan after breaking into the first team this season, writes Sam Tabuteau.

Scarlett, who can play on the left and right wing, has made six Championship appearances for high-flying QPR after joining the club on a scholarship deal from Arsenal’s academy last summer.

The 17-year-old has already been capped by Wales at Under-21 level, and Standard Sport understands several clubs in England and Europe have registered an interest.

Manchester United are set to make history with the appointment of Silvia Tuya as part of Mauricio Pochettino’s backroom staff.

Man Utd have already agreed a £9m compensation package with the US Soccer Federation to extract Pochettino from his contract as manager of the United States Men’s National Team (USMNT).

Following the Argentine to Old Trafford will be long-serving lieutenants, including assistant manager Jesus Perez and goalkeeper coach Toni Jimenez.

But Pochettino will also bring newer additions to his coaching team, including strength and conditioning coach Tuya in what will be a first for Man United.

Manchester United should sign Chelsea youngster Estevao, says former defender Mikael Silvestre.

The 19-year-old has made 46 appearances for the Blues since arriving in London last summer from Palmeiras for an initial £29million deal.

Estevao, however, has been handed just one Premier League start by new Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso so far this season - coincidentally against Man United last month.

The Brazilian would go on to impress, helping his side seal a 2-0 win at Stamford Bridge, a result that increased the pressure on then-head coach Michael Carrick who has since been sacked and replaced by Mauricio Pochettino.

Declan Rice has agreed a new long-term contract with Arsenal, writes Matt Verri.

The midfielder began talks with the club over improved terms earlier in the year and those have accelerated in recent weeks.

It is understood that a verbal agreement has now been reached and Rice will soon put pen to paper on the deal.

Newcastle have no intention of selling Lewis Hall in January despite growing interest in the England international.

The 22-year-old has established himself as Thomas Tuchel’s first-choice left-back following impressive performances against Croatia and Spain, attracting attention from clubs across Europe.

Manchester United made an approach for Hall last summer but were told he was not for sale, before the defender signed a new long-term contract in September.

According to The Telegraph, Newcastle are likely to demand well over £80m for a defender contracted until 2031.

Barcelona, Real Madrid and Paris Saint-Germain could also emerge as potential suitors.

Arsenal are increasingly confident they can sign Real Madrid forward Vinicius Jr in 2027, despite the Brazilian recently extending his contract at the Spanish club until 2032.

According to FootballTransfers, the Gunners still view the 26-year-old as their leading target to strengthen the left wing, with senior figures believing a deal could be possible as early as next summer.

Vinicius has struggled for consistency this season and faces competition from new signing Yan Diomande for a starting place.

Arsenal reportedly believe they remain an attractive destination after the forward seriously considered their offer last summer. However, any move is unlikely before next summer, with the club prepared to be patient in their pursuit.
