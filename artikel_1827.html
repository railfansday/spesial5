Scaroni explains Cardinale’s ‘long-term’ project and why stadium is ‘fundamental’ for success

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Paolo Scaroni has insisted that AC Milan have a long-term project under Gerry Cardinale, for which the stadium is a vital component.

As we wrote about at length last night, Cardinale has big plans to help get Milan back towards the top end of European football. The new ‘MSE’ holding company is the latest development, while the Generali Stadium was recently unveiled, and other projects like the NBA Europe team are ongoing.

It is all part of a mission to try and make the Rossoneri brand more than just a football club. Scaroni has been the face of the stadium project for many years, and he was a guest at a sports talk organised by RCS and entitled ‘Attracting foreign capital and enhancing the system for a real relaunch’.

He spoke alongside Giovanni Carnevali (CEO and General Manager of Juventus), Giuseppe Marotta (President and CEO of Inter) and Ezio Maria Simonelli, (President of the Lega Calcio Serie A). His words are via MilanNews.

Serie A seems to be struggling compared to other Italian sports. What could be done to make football more competitive?

“You’re telling me about something we’re doing, Gerry Cardinale’s idea. He came to the world of Italian football after twenty-five years in sport.

“It’s about creating Milan Sport Entertainment, that is, creating other sports around Milan, combining other sports with football, providing entertainment, communicating, and using Milan and Milano – because he also places great importance on the city of Milan, which is one of Europe’s emerging cities – to create an attractive environment for other sports and other situations.

“I’ll give just one example: if you take the United States, which is a gigantic market for the world of sports, football comes in fifth among the sports that Americans love, after basketball, of course, and even after hockey.

“So, how do we make football – and, in our case, Serie A – attractive in the United States? Precisely by creating this concept of entertainment around football, this concept of Milan, of fashion, of spectacle, that is, creating an attractiveness that competes with the other sports we compete with every day.

“This is the idea that Gerry brought in Italy, coming from his vast experience in the world of sport, and therefore around this we look to our future. Gerry Cardinale comes to say that he will never sell Milan, precisely because his project is a long-term one.”

The Generali Stadium will be something more than a stadium and will help this…

“Let’s say it’s been quite a complex journey. A very long one that I’ve been on with Inter since 2018-2019. But we’ve reached, let’s say, some key milestones. The first was in November last year, when we bought the San Siro stadium.

“The Meazza belongs to Inter and Milan, and the entire surrounding area. So every now and then I read debates: ‘But should it be torn down, shouldn’t it be torn down?’. Well, this will be a decision we’ll make because it’s ours, so I want to say: we have a clear, notarial fact behind us that gives us ownership of the San Siro stadium.

“This was the first step. The second was the presentation of the stadium that we held a few weeks ago. I think the Milanese and the professionals liked this presentation of this stadium designed by two great architects: one who worked on the exterior of the stadium and another who was responsible for the division of the spaces inside.

“Building a great stadium like this is something extremely important. Technical. Because then, as always when you tackle new topics, you learn things. I, for example, learned that Manica – this company that designs our stadium – has built six hundred stadiums around the world.

“So, to say that building a stadium is a profession, it’s not something you improvise. And this was the second step. The third was that Generali seized the opportunity to own this new stadium and the old one as well, because the collaboration on the Generali Stadium begins now.

“They seized the opportunity to have the two great Milanese clubs in Milan, both of which have a history of victories, are known worldwide and have millions of fans worldwide, and to use this new stadium to promote their image, I would say especially in Europe – because Generali are a very European company – but I would also say worldwide.

“They seized a great opportunity because opportunities like this are just the ones you get. And so I must say the negotiations concluded very successfully.”

“We have all the authorisations in front of us by September 2027. Therefore, in principle, we could begin construction of the new stadium between the end of 2027 and the beginning of 2028. So, let’s say, we’re pretty close to the home stretch.

“Naturally, as always with these issues, there may be some discussion points. Furthermore, in Milan we’re heading towards an election campaign for a new mayor, and a topic like the construction of a new stadium is one that can naturally excite politicians.

“Let’s say we’re confident because the entire process we’ve taken so far protects us from these potential disruptions, and we’re fiercely determined to move forward. With the new stadium, among other things, we’ll have €100 million in additional revenue.

“This €100 million in additional revenue is a fundamental ingredient for the success of AC Milan, Inter Milan, and Serie A around the world, because ultimately, €100 million – to have great players, to pay great players – is a fundamental ingredient for success.”
