‘Baywatch’ Star Erika Eleniak Says Joining OnlyFans Saved Her From ‘Financial Stress’ and Being Evicted: It ‘Feels Really Good to Be in Control’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Erika Eleniak, who rose to fame playing “Baywatch” lifeguard Shauni McClain from 1989 to 1992, is opening up about how starting an OnlyFans account at 56 changed her finances.

On Rachel Uchitel’s “Miss Understood” podcast, the actress describes the “really difficult struggle” she’s had as career opportunities “sort of dried up.” “I’m in this position where I’m not really acting anymore,” she said. “It’s just a struggle, [it’s] really stressful. When you have that kind of financial stress too, it kind of depletes you in a way. She continued: “You get embarrassed, and also for me, you get yourself kind of caught into a situation where you know you have a certain overhead… and in order to pay that and get certain jobs, it’s not even that they’re beneath you or they’re too embarrassing. It’s just not going to pay the rent. And you find yourself in that position where it’s like, I need to make a certain amount of money. And so what do I do? Where am I going to make that kind of money?”

For Eleniak, the answer was to start an OnlyFans account, which she created in August and says has made her feel “empowered”: “I feel like I’m the one that is calling the shots, and that feels really good, to be in control.”

Eleniak received an eviction notice months before starting her account, which the “Baywatch” star said she was able to pay off with money from the pursuit in only a “couple months.” “The people that were in charge of my rental situation were really kind to me and really worked with me, so that was really a blessing,” she said.

She also added that she enjoys “interacting with the fans” on the platform, which was part of the initial appeal of joining. “I’ve had a diverse career, and I’ve had a lot of people stay loyal and follow my career over the years, so it was interesting to me to see if they were going to jump on board with OnlyFans as well. And they have, which has been really nice.”

In April, Variety announced that Eleniak will reprise the role of McClain in Fox’s “Baywatch” reboot, due out in January. Shay Mitchell, Brooks Nader, Hassie Harrison, Livvy Dunne, Noah Beck, David Chokachi and Thaddeus LaGrone star in the new show, among others.

With her success on the platform, Eleniak joins stars like Lily Allen, Shannon Elizabeth, Donna Mills and Jaime Pressly who have launched OnlyFans accounts in the past year.

“I’ve always believed in evolving with the times,” Pressly said in a statement to Variety at the time about her decision to join OnlyFans. “This is another way for me to connect directly with my audience, on my own terms, with creativity and intention. I’ve loved meeting fans at various Comic Cons, and the excitement of having those real face-to-face moments made me want to seek options like OnlyFans.”
