Meet the Creators Joining the World of <i><em>Cosmopolitan</em></i>

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Hearst’s newest initiative, Hearst Creator Studios, is bringing a new generation of voices to Cosmo.

Big news: Cosmopolitan’s creator community is growing. We are joining Hearst Creator Studios (HCS), a new platform for handpicked creators to co-produce and distribute original, editorial, and branded content across Hearst brands, including Delish, ELLE, Good Housekeeping, MotorTrend, and, of course, Cosmopolitan. Think of it as a mix of talent agency and creative studio, bringing together creator talent and editorial authority.

In light of the news, we called up nine of our favorite friends and creators to help kick this partnership off. Some are already very familiar to Cosmo readers, including beloved columnists Ella Snyder and Julia Mervis. Others are joining the Cosmo world for the first time, and each brings a strong connection with their audience and a strong POV on the things Cosmo readers really care about.

Get to know them below, and get excited because all nine creators bring something genuinely fresh to both your feed and ours.
