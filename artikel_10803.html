Darth Vader’s lightsaber sells for nearly $5 million to break Star Wars record

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Darth Vader’s lightsaber from The Empire Strikes Back and Return of the Jedi has sold for nearly $5 million, making it the most expensive Star Wars collectible ever sold at auction

Owning a piece of Star Wars history has never been cheap, but a prop used in one of cinema’s most famous confrontations has fetched a price that even other original trilogy collectibles haven’t matched.

On November 12, the lightsaber Vader famously used in his Cloud City duel with Luke Skywalker in The Empire Strikes Back sold for a record-breaking $4.875 million as part of Heritage’s Ultimate Star Wars Auction.

According to Heritage Auctions’ official announcement, the lightsaber was used in both The Empire Strikes Back in 1980 and Return of the Jedi in 1983.

Heritage describes it as a “screen-matched hero dueling lightsaber,” with the final sale setting records for both a screen-used Star Wars prop and any collectible from the franchise.

It beat the previous Star Wars auction record by $1 million, passing the $3.875 million paid for Tom Jung’s original poster painting for A New Hope.

Vader also overtook his own son in the prop rankings, as Luke Skywalker’s lightsaber and severed hand effects rig from The Empire Strikes Back sold for $3.75 million on July 15, 2026.

While Vader’s iconic weapon was the main attraction, the auction also featured 27 other lightsabers, including props used by Obi-Wan Kenobi, Anakin Skywalker, Darth Maul, and Rey.
