90 Day Fiancé Alum Sheila Mangubat's Cause of Death Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

After 90 Day Fiancé: Before the 90 Days alum Sheila Mangubat died at age 35, sign language interpreter Aimee Adiwang, who worked with the couple, shared her cause of death.

New details about Sheila Mangubat's private health battle have come to light.

After the 90 Day Fiancé: Before the 90 Days alum's former fiancé David Dangerfield confirmed she had died at 35, the cause has been revealed.

Mangubat and Dangerfield's sign language interpreter Aimee Adiwang shared with Page Six in a Nov. 24 statement that the reality star died suddenly after a battle with a gynecological cancer at age 35 in the Philippines.

Adiwang—who worked with the pair since Dangerfield was born deaf and used sign language to communicate—did not specify the cancer she was diagnosed with. Mangubat's niece had also told her that her aunt had suffered from a urinary tract infection (UTI) prior to her death.

The interpreter reflected on their time working together during season six of the 90 Day spinoff in an emotional Instagram tribute alongside screengrabs from the 2023 episode.

Even though these are one of the few photos we have together," Adiwang wrote on Instagram Nov. 23. "I'm holding onto them tightly as I remember where our friendship began. Honoring her life and the warmth she brought into mine as she rests peacefully with the Lord."

She concluded, "I will always be grateful for her presence in my life."

Dangerfield initially shared the news in an emotional Instagram message Nov. 22, noting that the family of Mangubat—whom he split from in October 2025 due to distance and K-1 visa issues, but eventually began dating again in June, according to TMZ—told him about her death.

"I am devastated and still trying to process this loss," he continued. "Please keep her son, her family, and me in your prayers. Thank you for all your love and support."

Although their romance had its ups and downs, the 45-year-old only had love for Mangubat, who leaves behind son Jhonreil from a previous relationship.

"I'm happy I never gave up because I found my love," he said on a June 2023 episode. "I have a girlfriend whose name is Sheila, and she's beautiful."

"When I first saw Sheila's picture, it got my heart beating," he continued. "I will have a new family with Sheila in the future. Sheila is better than any other woman I've dated because Sheila is a sweet person. She is the perfect match to me. I'll love Sheila forever."
