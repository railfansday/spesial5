DWTS’ Val Chmerkovskiy Looks So Different With Hair Transformation

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Dancing With the Stars pro Val Chmerkovskiy looked almost unrecognizable as he appeared without his signature slicked back hair on the Nov. 24 season 35 finale.

Val Chmerkovskiy is jiving with a new look.

The Dancing With the Stars pro appeared on the season 35 finale Nov. 24 sporting a completely new hairdo than viewers were used to.

Instead of slicking back his dark mane, Chmerkovskiy kept his hair flowing around him in a bob that swung around with his movements as he and partner Jenna Dewan—who were eliminated on the Nov. 10 episode—showed off their dance moves alongside their fellow eliminated contestants.

And it certainly took fans a minute to clock the 40-year-old when he waltzed onto the episode.

“I thought Val was Benny Blanco…,” one user wrote on Threads. Another joked, “Is that Val or Lil Dicky?”

One fan noted Chmerkovskiy also appeared to be channeling Bob Ross with his curly ‘do, sharing a photo of the famed painter with the caption, “Val tonight.”

After his elimination from DWTS, the dance pro shared how he got some much-needed encouragement from his and wife Jenna Johnson's son Rome, 3.

"It's OK to lose, right?" Chmerkovskiy asked the toddler in a Nov. 11 Instagram video, to which Rome replied, "Yeah, you try your best. If you don't win sometimes, you try to do it so many times."

And as Chmerkovskiy told his son, “You gotta learn how to do both. You gotta learn how to win. You gotta learn how to lose, and it's OK to be sad when you lose for a little bit."

Plus, he knew he was ahead of the pack having Rome in his corner, telling his son, “Even though I lost and I'm sad, I feel already better because I have you in my life."

And Rome emphasized just how many fans Chmerkovskiy—who faced a one-week suspension earlier in the season after comments made about judge Carrie Ann Inaba—and his celeb partner Dewan had accumulated before their week nine elimination.

"People want you to win because you're a great dancer," Rome said. "You win or you lose, you try again on a different day. If you wanna win, you need to move your body and do it so fast and then you win. You get [to be] the champion."

Chmerkovskiy captioned the sweet interaction alongside a heart emoji and a smiley face, writing, “Hard hitting advice from our 3yo."
