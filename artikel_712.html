Utah Jazz Rookie Darryn Peterson Signs With 3 Arts Sports (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The NBA athlete joins a roster that includes Travis Kelce, Mookie Betts and NFL stars Jayden Daniels and Myles Garrett.

Utah Jazz rookie star Darryn Peterson has signed with 3 Arts Sports for representation.

After being the No. 2 overall pick in the 2026 NBA Draft, Peterson is looking to 3 Arts Sports, the entertainment and talent management firm, to represent him off the court in partnership with his family-led agency, 727 Marketing Firm. As athletes increasingly become media personalities in their own right, 3 Arts Sports will look to expand opportunities for the NBA guard in high-profile entertainment ventures and unscripted content, while adding to his commercial endorsements and brand partnerships.

“Darryn is one of the most talented young players in the NBA. He has the charisma to connect with audiences beyond basketball and a clear vision for what he wants to build in business and entertainment,” Shey Olaoshebikan, executive vp at 3 Arts Sports, said in a statement. “Darryn’s father, Darryl Peterson Jr., and the Peterson family have laid the foundation for that work through 727 Marketing Firm. We’re excited to partner with them to develop new opportunities around Darryn’s interests and build long-term relationships with brands that share his ambitions.”

The new representation comes as Peterson continues to build his profile as a pro NBA athlete and as an entrepreneur looking to expand his business footprint with new opportunities away from the basketball court.

“I am excited to have 3 Arts Sports collaborating with our family-led 727 Marketing Firm. Their expertise in business and entertainment is exactly what we need to transform our innovative ideas into reality with the perfect partners. I look forward to building long-term partnerships with brands I believe in and seeing what we can create together,” Peterson said in a statement.

Peterson, 19, joined the NBA in October after a freshman season with the Kansas Jayhawks. He joins an elite roster at 3 Arts Sports that includes Travis Kelce, WNBA star Sophie Cunningham, L.A. Dodgers star Mookie Betts and NFL star Jayden Daniels.
