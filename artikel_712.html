How to Watch the CMA Awards (And Taylor Swift’s Performance) Live Online

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Ella Langley, Miranda Lambert and Kacey Musgraves are also set to perform at the 60th annual Country Music Association Awards on Wednesday night.

The 60th annual Country Music Association Awards, taking place Wednesday, Nov. 18, will be televised from Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena on ABC and Disney+, with next-day streaming on Hulu.

The evening will feature a live performance from Taylor Swift, marking her return to the CMAs’ stage for the first time in 13 years. Back in 2013, Swift graced the stage performing her song “Red” with Vince Gill and Alison Krauss after receiving the Pinnacle Award. She originally announced that she would attend the ceremony for the first time in 10 years after she was nominated for single of the year for her “Toy Story 5” hit “I Knew It, I Knew You.”

“LOOKS LIKE I HAVEN’T YEE’D MY LAST HAW,” she wrote in all caps in a message posted to her Instagram Stories. “THANK YOU CMA!!!! See you there Nov. 18.”

Other performers already announced include lead nominees Ella Langley, Miranda Lambert and Kacey Musgraves. The “Choosin Texas” singer nabbed nine nominations, with Lambert close behind with seven and Musgraves with five.

The list of artists taking the stage goes on with Cody Johnson, Tim McGraw, The Red Clay Strays, Zach Top, Tucker Wetmore, Luke Combs, Vince Gill, Megan Moroney, Mumford & Sons, Chris Stapleton, Lainey Wilson and Stephen Wilson Jr. Additionally, Brothers Osborne, Luke Bryan, Halestorm, Hardy and Marcus King will take part in a tribute to Brooks & Dunn. There will also be a salute to Dolly Parton.

Lainey Wilson returns as host for the third year. Show veteran Robert Deaton returns as executive producer, Alan Carter is back as director and Jon Macks is the telecast’s head writer.
