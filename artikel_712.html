‘The Bachelor’ Casts NFL Quarterback Sam Hartman as Season 30 Star

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

After two years off the air, “The Bachelor” is returning for its 30th season with a brand new face: NFL player Sam Hartman will help relaunch the famous reality dating franchise beginning on March 21.

Hartman is a quarterback for the Washington Commanders, who is also an alumnus of the Wake Forest and Notre Dame football teams, where he set the Atlantic Coast Conference records for most career touchdown passes and 300-yard passing games.
