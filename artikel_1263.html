Video: Curtis Jones tells Djed Spence to ‘get out of here’ over GTA claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

England stars Curtis Jones and Djed Spence were caught in a disagreement over the video game GTA in an amusing clip that has been doing the rounds on social media.

Inter were among the Serie A clubs to jump on the bandwagon ahead of the release of GTA 6, which officially comes out on Thursday, November 19.

On their way to the training pitch on Wednesday, the Inter stars were asked for their favourite instalment in the GTA franchise, leading Jones to tell Spence to ‘get out of here’ with his selection.

“GTA San Andreas, that was the first one I played, or five because it’s the latest one,” said former Liverpool midfielder Jones.

Spence, who has recently returned from international duty with England, followed a few steps behind him and added: “San Andreas, four, five, Vice City.”

“What?! Vice City? Get out of here,” his teammate shouted back in response.
