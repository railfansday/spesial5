Guide: These 11+ PS5 and PS Plus Games Are Coming Out This Week (23rd-29th November)

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Gothic! Spectrum Retreat! Station to Station!

It feels odd to be able to say it, but we're now in a post-GTA 6 world, and the release schedule once again reflects that game's imposing presence.

There isn't too much to speak of coming to PS5 this week, but it's always worth going over them in case you spot something you like.

We're looking at stuff like throwback RPG Gothic 3 Classic, relaxing train game Station to Station, and The Spectrum Retreat Redux, a souped-up version of the likeable puzzle adventure.

PS Plus Extra members are getting a handful of new games on the service as well, including Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii and MotoGP 26.
