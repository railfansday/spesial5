I'll Be Skipping Zenless Zone Zero's New Meta Unit in Favour of My All-Time Fave Waifu

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

I like to think my feedback in some small way contributed to Honkai: Star Rail’s Kafka being added as an S-Rank character to Zenless Zone Zero.

I’ve told various people at HoYoverse about my embarrassing admiration for the purple-haired, dual-pistol wielding waifu. But they’ve done me dirty by putting her in the same patch as the new Void Hunter!

For those who don’t know, in ZZZ lore, Void Hunters are basically demi-god units. This one, the Sunbringer, actually comprises three different characters – each ticking a different anime trope. (Don’t ask!)

But my pulls are limited, folks, so I’m going to have to hold strong for Kafka in the New Year and skip the OP Sunbringer. I can probably only afford to get one!

Anyway, this is a long-winded way of me saying ZZZ’s next update is out on 2nd December and will conclude the current Roscaelifer arc.

In addition to the main story quest, new content will include a Metal Gear Solid-style stealth minigame featuring Honkai: Star Rail crossover characters Robin and the aforementioned Kafka. The former you’ll be able to get for free as part of the update.
