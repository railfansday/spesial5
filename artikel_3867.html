The Streamer’s Alt Account Labyrinth launches January 29, 2027 for PC, later for Switch

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Dungeon RPG and visual novel The Streamer’s Alt Account Labyrinth will launch for PC via Steam on January 29, 2027, followed by Switch at a later date, publisher G-MODE Corporation and developer Acacia announced. It will support English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese language options.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:

Trapped in a maze of secret alt accounts—you wanna escape?

The owner of the alt is waiting for you somewhere within: find them and kill them.
