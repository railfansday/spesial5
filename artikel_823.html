Fury's manager says AJ called him to check fight was on

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Spencer Brown has reaffirmed Tyson Fury's fight against Anthony Joshua will go ahead on December 11; Paris Fury retracted comments made in a new Netflix documentary that her husband had Schizophrenia; the British Boxing Board of Control cleared Fury to fight on Friday

Tyson Fury's manager Spencer Brown revealed Anthony Joshua called him to check if their fight was still going ahead, after claims the 'Gypsy King' had been diagnosed with schizophrenia.

Paris Fury made the claims during a new Netflix documentary about her husband's life, but later retracted her comments, clarifying: "[Fury] doesn't have this condition and has never received a diagnosis."

While some had raised questions over whether the 38-year-old would be able to fight, the British Boxing Board of Control [BBBoC], on Friday, announced it had cleared Fury, adding he had passed pre-fight tests.

Speaking to Dev Sahni, Brown flatly dismissed claims that their December 11 fight was in jeopardy.

"Even Anthony Joshua rang me and said: 'Is this fight going on, because I've heard rumours that his licence…" Brown said.

"I said: 'One-million per cent [the fight is on], just turn up and be there. The fight's happening; it's unbelievable'."

Fury posted on Instagram on Friday night to explain how delighted he was about the launch of his new documentary TYSON FURY, urging fans to watch the film, before adding: "The journey continues, next stop December 11."

Netflix says it has worked to "update" the footage in light of Paris' clarification on Thursday.

The documentary explores Fury's experiences with depression, anxiety and bipolar disorder after they became acute following his 2015 world championship victory over Wladimir Klitschko.

Brown said the 38-year-old was more focused on his training rather than speculation about the fight.

"To him, it's a load of old rubbish from 10 years ago," Brown said.

"Obviously, it's reared its head. People have decided to make an issue of it, when there is no issue. And people panicked a little bit. So, there is no issue. The fight goes on."

Brown added: "It's been noted and everybody knows that Tyson has suffered from a mental illness. And we all know that. It's out everywhere.

"That was a long time ago, he's very happy, and I've never seen him in a better headspace, to be quite honest."

Fury's manager also revealed the lengths his team are going to to help the Wythenshawe-born fighter prepare for the bout.

"I speak to him nearly every day," he said.

"Some mundane things about, we have to make sure that he's travelled right and he's in his hotel and that wherever he's staying, every minute thing has to be done right, down to the windows being blacked out.

"Because he's on a different sleep pattern because they're fighting late at night.

"We have to make sure that he's got solitude, and he's happy, and he's smiling every day. His food is there, transport is there.

"His security are there. Everything has to be done to a specification."

Brown also issued a warning to Joshua ahead of the fight.

"If you're listening, Anthony Joshua, the fight's on," Brown said. "Get ready. Make sure that chin of yours is solid. Because it's going to need to be."
