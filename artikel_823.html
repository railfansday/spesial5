Kendall Jenner and Jacob Elordi, who started dating earlier this year, cozied up among autumnal foliage as they grabbed coffee in New York City Nov. 8.

Kendall Jenner and Jacob Elordi are keeping it cozy.

Meanwhile, Elordi looked equally toasty in a white hoodie, matching sneakers and dark blue jeans. The 29-year-old tucked his newly dyed blonde hair beneath a black beanie, which he covered with the hood of his sweater.

The couple, who first sparked dating rumors in April, matched in sunglasses as they sat on a stoop waiting for their drinks.
