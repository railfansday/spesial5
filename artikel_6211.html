British Airways Flight Attendant Hands Passenger Paper Towels And A Spray Bottle For Urine-Soaked Seat On 8-Hour Flight

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

A British Airways passenger says she boarded an eight hour flight from Miami to London Heathrow, sat down, and discovered her seat was soaked in urine. Her pants were wet, and she says a flight attendant told her there were no empty seats and she could have paper towels and a tiny bottle of antibacterial cleaning spray to deal with the mess – she couldn’t even have an extra blanket.
