Frontier Begins First Class Ticket Sales

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Frontier on Tuesday opened reservations for its upcoming First Class, which is set to debut in March 2027.

The new seats have up to 37 inches of pitch, the carrier said, and will be arranged in a two-by-two configuration at the front of the cabin. Installations are already underway. Reservations are available now for travel beginning March 5.

Frontier first announced plans to add a First Class product in late 2024.

The airline will also add extra-legroom seating, with up to 34 inches of pitch, in rows three through eight. These seats will be available with Frontier’s Premium bundle, which includes a carry-on bag and priority boarding.

Frontier plans to roll out two additional perks through 2027 – in-seat USB-C power at every seat in the first eight rows, and Starlink wireless internet service. The carrier decided on Starlink in July; it will be the first airline in the U.S. to use a new system managed directly by Starlink, officials said at the time.

“We’re transforming what customers can expect when they fly Frontier,” Bobby Schroeter, chief marketing officer at Frontier, said in a news release. “First Class seating, more room to stretch out, in-seat power, and fast, reliable Starlink Wi-Fi will bring a new level of comfort and convenience to flying with us.”
