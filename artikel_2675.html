Lakers share encouraging Austin Reaves injury update ahead of Jazz game

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Austin Reaves was a full participant at Lakers practice on Sunday after missing four straight games because of a left heel bruise.

The Lakers didn't do any live-action drills Sunday, but they did two sets of defensive breakdowns and two sets of offensive breakdowns.

"He did everything, making progress," Redick said.

Austin Reaves (15) of the Los Angeles Lakers shoots a free throw against the Milwaukee Bucks on Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, at Fiserv Forum in Milwaukee. NBAE via Getty Images

Redick said Friday that Reaves would "hopefully" return over the next two games. The Lakers play the Jazz on Monday at Crypto.com Arena and the Spurs on Friday in San Antonio.

Reaves, who suffered the injury on Nov. 11 and has been day-to-day since then, is averaging 24.7 points (51.3% from the field, 40.9% from beyond the arc), 3.3 rebounds and 4.7 assists for the Lakers, who are tied for the best record in the league (14-3) with the Spurs and 76ers.

Redick has called Reaves' injury a pain tolerance issue.

"He's made progress every day and they're doing a great job at figuring out like the most comfortable insert and the most comfortable shoe, finding that right combination," Redick said.

Austin Reaves (15) of the Los Angeles Lakers reacts after a late second half 3-pointer against the Detroit Pistons in Detroit, Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026. Getty Images

Redick can relate to Reaves' pain considering the coach suffered a bruised left heel when he was playing for the Clippers in April 2016.

"Mine was a different spot," Redick said. "Mine was on the bottom of my heel. Did it when I played for the Clippers, the last regular season game, and had to go play with it in playoffs, and was taking Toradol shots before the game, and it was super painful. His is in a different spot; it's on the medial side, so it's not on the bottom of the foot. But it's really just a comfort thing, and it's getting better every day."

That's great news for the Lakers, who have won 10 of their last 11 games, but have shown signs of fatigue without Reaves in their last two games, including a 109-97 loss to the Jazz on Wednesday and a 106-104 win over the Kings on Friday.

Over the last seven games, Doncic and Reaves have only played three-quarters of a game together after Reaves was out against the Hawks on Nov. 9 before suffering the heel injury, and Doncic was sidelined Nov. 8 because of lower back tightness on the front leg of a back-to-back.

Los Angeles Lakers' Austin Reaves drives to the basket against Milwaukee Bucks' Nate Ament during an NBA game Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, in Milwaukee. AP Photo/Aaron Gash

All things considered, Redick said he has been incredibly proud of his group over this stretch.

"When we put this team together, the number one priority for us was to re-sign AR and put complementary players around Luka and AR," Redick said Friday. "We've played three-quarters of a game with the two of them both available [over the last seven games], and we've gone 6-1. And so, I think it's just a credit to the group sticking with it."
