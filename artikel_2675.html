Fernando Mendoza starts first NFL game with a bang, leads Raiders on opening TD drive

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The first touchdown Fernando Mendoza scored in the NFL looked a lot like the defining moment from his college football career.

The Las Vegas Raiders rookie quarterback capped an 11-play drive, Mendoza's first as the team's starter, with a quarterback draw run play that went for a 10-yard touchdown to open the scoring Nov. 15 against the Seattle Seahawks.

It was eerily reminiscent of his 12-yard touchdown 10 months earlier during the national championship game, a fourth-down scamper as he secured the 2025 national title for his Indiana Hoosiers against the Miami Hurricanes.

Mendoza's "natty" run was certainly more physical – and didn't require a dramatic leap at the goal line – but the 2025 Heisman Trophy winner demonstrated his athleticism and ability to make plays with his legs.

Throwing the ball, Mendoza was 3-for-3 for 20 yards. The Raiders started in excellent field position after the defense forced a fumble on Seahawks quarterback Drew Lock.

Mendoza had spent the team's first eight games as the backup behind Kirk Cousins, who was traded to the Minnesota Vikings on Nov. 10.
