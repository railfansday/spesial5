Angel Reese’s ‘biggest flaw’ often fools executives into misunderstanding Dream star, says analyst

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

WNBA analyst Robin Lundberg has an explanation for why NBC executives do not like Angel Reese.

Angel Reese and the Atlanta Dream failed to win the 2026 WNBA Championship, but it wasn't a poor season by any means.

Despite that, many still seem to hate Reese as it was recently reported that one of the NBC executives believe that the Dream star 's____.'

Keeping that in mind, WNBA analyst Robin Lundberg has an explanation for the narrative, which exposes the lack of women's basketball knowledge of the aforementioned NBC executive.

Lundberg explained the NBC executive's hatred towards Reese during a recent episode of his show on YouTube. He believes that the executives simply do not understand Reese's game and are likely watching viral clips of her missed layups.

"Why would an NBC executive think Angel Reese s____? Angel Reese does not s___. Let's be clear about that. … But why would somebody think that?

"Well, I think a reason… someone who isn't watching WNBA games, who isn't necessarily in tune with some of the intangible impacts that Angel Reese might have, is that they could see a clip of Angel Reese missing 100 layups and think she s____," Lundberg said.

"Angel's biggest flaw leads to her getting clipped up all the time. And she gets clipped up all the time because that flaw is one of the harder things to fully understand.

"… Because that flaw is so significant, and it's such an easy fix as far as cause and effect goes. If she were to finish around the rim with consistency, where she ranks goes higher," he added.

There's no doubt that Reese is a dominant player, but her inability to consistently make layups has done a lot of harm to her image in the eyes of broadcasters.

As Lundberg suggested, if she can work on this flaw during the off-season, her stock will skyrocket and she'll be treated with more respect than ever by broadcasters, experts, and fans alike.
