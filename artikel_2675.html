AFC Playoff Picture: How standings, playoff odds look after Bengals blowout, Drake Maye injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

As the NFL season passes the halfway point, the AFC playoff teams are starting to take hold of their positions. There are seven spots in the AFC playoffs, and it would not be a shocker if the teams that currently own those positions would finish the season as postseason teams.

The Buffalo Bills have the best record in the AFC, and they are rolling with an 8-1 record. The Bills have been motivated all season under first-year head coach Joe Brady. The Bills were angry a year ago when they lost their grip on the division and the New England Patriots took advantage of a fourth-place schedule and stole it from them. They appear to be quite secure in their position as they have a 3.5-game lead over the Pats (5-5), who have lost two in a row and saw QB Drake Maye go down with an ankle injury.

The Bills are plus-110 to emerge with the No. 1 seed in the AFC playoffs, according to DraftKings. The Bills are the only team likely to make the playoffs from the AFC East. The Patriots still have a chance, but they will be sending Tommy DeVito out to lead the attack, and he struggled badly after taking over for Maye in the Week 10 loss to the Detroit Lions.

The Cincinnati Bengals (7-2) are on a major roll this season, and much of that is due to the health and dominance of QB Joe Burrow. The Bengals have won five games in a row, and Burrow has thrown for 2,795 yards with 27 TD passes and 6 interceptions. Based on his performance in the winning streak, Burrow looks unstoppable at this point. The Bengals are plus-600 to finish with the No. 1 seed in the AFC.

As dominant and exciting as the Bills and Bengals have been through the first half of the season, the Jacksonville Jaguars (7-2) have been nearly as good. The Jaguars have a 4.0-game lead over the Houston Texans (3-6) and they appear to be rolling to the AFC South title. Trevor Lawrence has reached elite status in the NFL, and he is having a brilliant year for head coach Liam Coen. Lawrence has completed 171 of 259 passes for 2,284 yards with a razor-sharp 21-5 TD-interception ratio.

The Jaguars are listed at plus-500 to end up with the No. 1 seed in the AFC playoffs.

The Bills, Bengals and Jaguars appear to have the best route to the playoffs and the top seed in the AFC. However, the Kansas City Chiefs and the Denver Broncos can't be counted out in the AFC West. Both teams are well on their way to making the playoffs and it's just a question of which of these 7-2 teams is going to emerge with the AFC West crown.

The Chiefs are demonstrating that last year's 6-11 finish was basically just a fluke The Chiefs have returned to excellent form and there is always confidence when Patrick Mahomes has the ball in his hands in a close game. The Chiefs are listed as minus-12500 to make the playoffs and plus-550 to earn the No. 1 seed in the AFC.

The Broncos appear to have a better shot at winning the AFC West crown over the Chiefs because their closing schedule is much easier. They play the Raiders twice, the Dolphins, the Jets and the Chargers, while the Chiefs have challenging games against the Bills, Rams and Bengals in consecutive weeks and another tough game against the 49ers. The Broncos are minus-8000 to make the AFC playoffs and plus-380 to take the No. 1 seed.

The Pittsburgh Steelers (6-3) and the surprising Cleveland Browns (6-4) are both in positions to take the final two seeds in the AFC playoffs. The Steelers suffered a 38-18 loss to the Bengals in Week 10, but head coach Mike McCarthy and QB Aaron Rodgers have no reason to panic. The Steelers are listed at minus-290 to make the playoffs. Their closing schedule features tough games against Denver and Jacksonville and winnable games against Philadelphia, Baltimore (2), Carolina and Tennessee.

The Browns currently have the No. 7 and final seed in the AFC playoffs. They have a 1.0-game lead over the Patriots, and while history says that it will be difficult for Cleveland to hold on to that playoff spot, first-year head coach Todd Monken has this team believing in itself even though this team is devoid of stars. The Browns are minus-130 to make the playoffs.

The Browns have been depending on Deshaun Watson at quarterback, and he has thrown for 2,118 yards and 7 TDs. He has also demonstrated that he can run away from pass rushers. He has 266 rushing yards and 2 TDs. The Browns are getting a lift from tight end Harold Fanin Jr. He has caught 44 passes for 544 yards and 4 TDs.

The struggling Patriots are the closest team that is out of the AFC playoff structure to gaining a position, but the absence of Maye means it is a major long shot Mike Vrabel's team to make the playoffs.

If the Browns are going to fall apart, it would seem that the Las Vegas Raiders or the Texans have the best shot at making a run at the postseason. The Raiders (4-6) have some winnable games against the Browns, Chargers, Titans and Cardinals, but their trade of QB Kirk Cousins to the Minnesota Vikings may have hurt their chances for success.

The Texans were expected to battle the Jaguars for the division title because they have a potentially dominant defense. They would suddenly have to hit their stride in order to make a run, but that seems unlikely because of the way DeMeco Ryans' team has played to this point.
