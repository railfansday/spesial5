Cam Skattebo on unretiring the backflip: When energy calls for it, it calls for it

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Cam Skattebo said during the week that he was retiring his celebratory backflip because he didn’t want to risk a similar injury to the one suffered by Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson in Week 10, but the retirement proved to be a very brief one.

After Skattebo iced the Giants’ win over the Jaguars with a first down run on Sunday, he flexed his muscles and then did a backflip to the delight of fans at MetLife Stadium. Skattebo told reporters in the locker room that he was swept up in the energy of the moment.

“When energy calls for it, it calls for it,” Skattebo said, via SNY. “My teammates — I do whatever I can to bring my energy for the team. I play this game with my heart and soul. When that happens, it’s second nature and it’s hard to hold back from.”

The backflip wasn’t the only callback to past games for Skattebo. He also lost a fumble in the second half, but head coach John Harbaugh did not bench him as he did after a fumble in overtime against the Commanders earlier in the season. That made it easier for the coach to chuckle about the way Skattebo punctuated the victory.

“He couldn’t help himself, could he? He just couldn’t help himself,” Harbaugh said, via a transcript from the team.

There was no injury and the Giants’ win leaves them on top of the NFC East, so there was little reason for Harbaugh to make either the fumble or the backflip the focus of attention after the game.
