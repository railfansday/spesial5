CFP rankings: Texas moves to No. 3 in latest Top 25

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Later Tuesday night, the College Football Playoff selection committee will release the fourth set of rankings for this year.

And following a wild week of action, we could see a lot of movement in the field.

The intrigue begins with UCLA at No. 4. The Bruins lost to Michigan on the road, and with UCLA potentially looking at third-string QB Madden Iamaleava for this week in a huge game against USC, how will the committee view UCLA? Starter Nico Iamaleava is already out for the season, and backup Ty Dieffenbach was hurt against the Wolverines and is questionable for this weekend's rivalry game.

Then there is Indiana. The Hoosiers lost to Washington to fall to 9-2, and could be on the outside looking in when these rankings are released.

Then there is the Group of Six. Boise State's loss to San Diego State — the Broncos were at No. 17 heading into Week 12 — likely drops Boise State out of the rankings. What team from the Group of Six will get into the hypothetical bracket?

The rankings will be released around 7:30 p.m. Eastern on Tuesday night. We'll be updating this as the rankings come in.
