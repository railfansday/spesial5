Vikings vs. 49ers odds: Vikings Underdogs for Big Mexico City Matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The stage has been set for a huge matchup on Sunday Night Football this week, and it will happen at about 7,000 feet above sea level in Mexico City, as the Minnesota Vikings and San Francisco 49ers will be meeting south of the border at Estadio Banorte as the NFL's International Series concludes for 2026. As the betting lines open for this heavyweight clash, the Vikings are starting the week as a slight underdog for what is, at least nominally, a San Francisco home game.

Our friends at the FanDuel Sportsbook have opened the betting lines for this week, and the Vikings have started as a three-point underdog to the Niners. The over/under for this one has started at 46.5 points.

The Vikings snapped their two-game losing streak this past Sunday at Lambeau Field, pasting the Green Bay Packers by a score of 30-10 to complete a sweep of the Border Battle for the 2026 season. The defense was dominant, sacking Jordan Love eight times and forcing three of the four turnovers committed by the Packers (the fourth came on special teams). But the big story was the Vikings' offense and the return of Kirk Cousins, whom the Vikings acquired at the trade deadline on Tuesday, arrived in Minnesota on Wednesday, and started on Sunday, making him the first player since at least 1950 to start games at quarterback for different teams in consecutive weeks.

Cousins' last start for his former team, the Las Vegas Raiders, came against these same 49ers, who come into this game tied for the league's best record at 8-1. Like the Vikings, the Niners got their season off to a 6-0 start before seeing their first loss, a 29-20 defeat to the Atlanta Falcons in Week 7. After the bye, they came back to beat Cousins and the Raiders by a score of 38-17, and this past Sunday they had a back-and-forth battle with the Dallas Cowboys that saw them eventually emerge with a 46-40 victory at Jerryworld. Brock Purdy is on the short list of potential MVP candidates for this season, and he showed why against the Cowboys, completing 22-of-28 passes for 389 yards and three touchdowns, two of which came on long passes to Christian McCaffrey, who had 161 receiving yards on the day.

This matchup looks to be another matchup of a very strong offense facing off against Brian Flores' #1 defensive squad. Unlike what we saw when the Vikings faced the Bills on Monday Night, however, now it appears that the Vikings have the offense to actually allow their defense to have a bit of a rest and not allow them to get worn down late in the game.

For the fourth consecutive week, the Vikings will go into a game as an underdog, ladies and gentlemen. What do you think about that? Let us know in the comments!
