Lakers get encouraging Austin Reaves update as star nears return

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

LOS ANGELES – Austin Reaves has been ruled out for a fifth straight game ahead of Monday's contest against the Utah Jazz, but there was an encouraging sight in El Segundo on Sunday afternoon.

Reaves stood at midcourt on the Los Angeles Lakers' practice floor at UCLA Health Training Center, took off his gold practice jersey and conversed with assistant coach Greg St. Jean as he walked over to the left corner. From there, the 6-foot-5 shooting guard was running drills, coming off screens and working with two-way center Carson Cooper.

"We didn't do anything live, but he was a full participant," head coach JJ Redick said.

Redick went on to say that Reaves' practice day consisted mostly of "cleaning up some stuff" followed by two sets of defensive and offensive breakdowns before finishing the day by getting shots up.

The Lakers star has been sidelined since landing awkwardly on his left heel midway through the third quarter of their road win against the Milwaukee Bucks on Wednesday, Nov. 11. X-rays came back negative that night and both Reaves and team medical staff opted against further imaging.

He's been considered day-to-day with a left heel bone bruise and contusion since, though Redick said Sunday that Reaves has been making progress in his recovery.

"They're doing a great job of figuring out the most comfortable insert and the most comfortable shoe," he said. "Finding that right combination."

Redick's insight is particularly valuable in this instance, considering he went through a similar injury himself when he suffered a bone bruise on the bottom of his heel in the last game of the regular season while playing with the Los Angeles Clippers.

"I had to play with it in the playoffs and was taking Toradol shots before the game. It was super painful," he said. "His is in a different spot, it's on the medial side so it's not on the bottom of the foot, but it's really just a comfort/pain issue."

The Lakers can't get Reaves back in the lineup soon enough. Quentin Grimes has had an impressive stretch stepping up in the Wichita State and Oklahoma product's absence, most notably a career-high 47-point game on 10 made 3s while leading the Lakers to an overtime comeback win over the Charlotte Hornets, their overall offense has suffered.

Luka Dončić, the early frontrunner for MVP, has struggled in particular the last two games and has looked downright fatigued at times to keep up his torrid start to the season. He's turned the ball over 20 times in the last three games. And collectively, the Lakers have shot the ball poorly. Dončić, Sandro Mamukelashvili and Jake LaRavia have all seen their 3-point percentages plummet over the last week after looking automatic from deep not long before then.

The stat that stands out the most to Redick: For the first 12 games, the Lakers had the top-rated halfcourt offense in the league. Reaves was on the floor for 70% of those possessions. Over the last seven, their halfcourt offense has dipped to fourth at 120.5 points per possession; Reaves has been on the floor for 9% of that time.

"Who would've thought that not having an elite offensive player, All-Star level player, would hurt your offense?" Redick prosed sarcastically. "That's crazy to me. We're gonna be fine."

The Lakers have been able to continue winning without Reaves, but now it looks like he's getting closer to his return, potentially at some point in their four-game road trip. That would be perfect timing as the Lakers are set to take on the San Antonio Spurs, Memphis Grizzlies and Houston Rockets in that span – all of which are currently top teams in the Western Conference.

Reaves has made the most of his new role as the unquestioned No. 2 in LA this season. In his 12 games played this season, he's averaged 24.7 points on 51.3% shooting (40.9% from deep) to go along with 3.3 rebounds, 4.7 assists and 1.2 steals per game. The Lakers have struggled to replace that production in the four games he's missed, even with Grimes' hot streak.
