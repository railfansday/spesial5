Manchester United reportedly closing in on deal with Mauricio Pochettino once team moves on from Michael Carrick

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

All indications point to USMNT coach Mauricio Pochettino joining Manchester United, but there's one significant roadblock preventing the move: Michael Carrick is still employed by the side.

That's expected to change Wednesday, as Manchester United brass will reportedly meet to discuss Carrick's fate. Once that meeting takes place — and Carrick is relieved of his duties — it's expected Pochettino will be hired by the team, per multiple sources.
