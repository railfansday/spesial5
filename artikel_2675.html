49ers secure a key piece of their future before kickoff in Mexico City

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The modern era of the San Francisco 49ers isn't going anywhere.

On Monday, NFL insider Jay Glazer reported that the 49ers and John Lynch agreed to terms on a new multi-year contract that would keep him as the team's general manager and President of Football Operations for the foreseeable future.

Glazer also reported that Lynch and 49ers team owner Jed York shook hands to agree on the deal on the sideline, before the team's 35-10 victory against the Vikings in Mexico City on Sunday night.

The San Francisco 49ers agree to a contract extension with John Lynch as general manager and President of Football Operations. AP Photo/Julio Cortez

Lynch came into this season on an expiring deal and was considered to be on the hot seat, due to the team's 41-6 loss to the Seahawks in the divisional round of the playoffs and was under pressure for a poor draft class from this previous year's NFL Draft.

Lynch has served as the architect of building the modern-era 49ers in 2017 and has been on a mission to capture the franchise's sixth Super Bowl.

One of the first moves he made in his role was finding the team a head coach.

Lynch landed on Kyle Shanahan, who was the AP NFL Assistant Coach of the Year winner in 2016 when he was the offensive coordinator of the Atlanta Falcons, who suffered a heartbreaking loss in Super Bowl 51, when they gave up a 28-3 lead in the second half.

With Lynch leading the way in the 49ers' front office and Shanahan calling the shots on the field, the 49ers have been to two Super Bowls and four NFC Championship Games, but have yet to win the Vince Lombardi Trophy.

Despite the 49ers' failed efforts of winning the Super Bowl over the last nine years, San Francisco's organization made a complete turnaround under Lynch's guidance, as the team snapped a six-year playoff drought during his second season.

John Lynch and Kyle Shanahan standing side by side during a team practice. AP

Lynch's front office has been responsible for drafting and developing players like George Kittle, Fred Warner, Deebo Samuel, Nick Bosa, Dre Greenlaw, Talanoa Hufanga, and their franchise quarterback, Brock Purdy — whom they selected with the final pick of the 2022 NFL Draft.

Lynch has also been responsible for trading for star talent, such as Trent Williams, Jimmy Garoppolo, Emmanuel Sanders, and Christian McCaffrey.

Garoppolo led the 49ers to the Super Bowl during the 2019-2020 season, while Williams became the team's franchise left tackle for the last seven seasons.

San Francisco 49ers cornerback Deommodore Lenoir (2) celebrates recovering a fumble in Mexico City, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Fernando Llano

McCaffrey changed the direction of the 49ers entirely in 2022 by reviving his career and being a key explosive piece of the Shanahan offense, and eventually winning the 2023 NFL Offensive Player of the Year, when he ran for 1,459 yards with 21 combined rushing and receiving touchdowns.

Lynch's extension comes at a time when the former Super Bowl-winning safety has responded to the pressure he faced over the offseason by creating one of the best teams in the NFL this season.

The 49ers are 9-1 to start the season and are in first place in the NFC and viewed as a contender to win the Super Bowl this year.

Purdy has played a vital part in San Francisco's success this season, completing 71% of his passes while having 23 touchdowns, for 2,624 yards and five interceptions.
