Colts vs. Texans live updates, inactives, where to watch: C.J. Stroud, Houston host Daniel Jones, Indianapolis in AFC South showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Indianapolis Colts and Houston Texans will open up Week 11 with an AFC South clash on "Thursday Night Football," with kickoff set for 8:15 p.m. ET at NRG Stadium in Houston. It's a must-win game as both teams look to keep their playoff hopes alive amid a less-than-ideal start.

The Colts (3-7) have continued to struggle this season despite a Week 3 win over the Texans to kick off their annual series. They managed to snap a five-game losing streak last week against the Miami Dolphins and will look to keep that momentum rolling against an AFC South rival.

The Texans got some tough news on the defense this week, officially placing linebacker Azeez Al-Shaair on injured reserve with a groin injury that will reportedly require surgery. It's the latest blow for the struggling unit, which blew a 17-0 lead to the Cleveland Browns in Week 10, officially falling to 3-6. They'll also be without running back Woody Marks, who was ruled out after suffering a concussion last week, though David Montgomery will return from a groin injury for the contest.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for live updates, highlights and more as the Colts face the Texans in Week 10, airing on Amazon Prime Video.
