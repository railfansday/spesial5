China’s tech bosses and Elon Musk head to Shenzhen for APEC CEO summit

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

CATL, Baidu, Unitree, BYD and Xiaomi bosses are on the APEC CEO summit agenda in Shenzhen. Musk travels with Trump, and US firms are the largest foreign group taking part.

Donald Trump boards Air Force One at Salina Regional Airport in Salina, Kansas, 1 November 2026.

The bosses of some of China’s biggest technology companies are on the agenda of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) CEO Summit in Shenzhen, which starts on Tuesday. The organiser released the agenda on Monday, Bloomberg and Reuters reported.

Robin Zeng, founder and chairman of battery maker CATL, and Baidu chairman and CEO Robin Li are among the speakers. Wang Xingxing, founder of humanoid robot maker Unitree, will also speak. BYD chairman Wang Chuanfu and Xiaomi CEO Lei Jun are due on panels, and the chairmen of ICBC, Bank of China and Agricultural Bank of China will attend, Reuters reported.

The US names include the CEOs of Visa, Mastercard and UPS, Boeing global president Brendan Nelson and Google’s head of government affairs, Karan Bhatia. PwC global chairman Mohamed Kande and Deloitte CEO Joe Ucuzoglu are also on the list, according to Reuters.

Xi Jinping will open the two-day summit at the Qianhai International Convention Center with a keynote speech. The organiser, the China Council for the Promotion of International Trade (CCPIT), announced it in a statement. Leaders of 11 other economies, including Russia, Canada and South Korea, will give speeches or join panels.

More than 2,000 political and business leaders are expected, from 115 Fortune Global 500 companies among others. US companies make up nearly 20% of the foreign participants, the largest group from outside China, the council said. More than 130 Shenzhen companies are taking part.

Chinese business leaders stayed away from Xi’s state visit to Washington in September, Bloomberg reported. Ahead of the summit, Shenzhen suspended drone deliveries and reduced robotaxi services, the Associated Press reported.

Elon Musk, the head of Tesla and SpaceX, will travel with President Donald Trump to Shenzhen. Senior US officials said so on a call with reporters on Friday, CNBC reported. One official said Musk could act as an adviser to the president.

“Obviously, he has tremendous business interests in the U.S.-China relationship,” the official said.

Musk will not join the three-way meeting of Trump, Xi and Russia’s Vladimir Putin, officials told the Associated Press. On the same call, officials said Trump did not plan to raise the Nvidia spy allegations with Xi.

“In all of our discussions and negotiations with the Chinese, we do not negotiate American export controls,” an official said.

On Monday, the Commerce Department issued a rule to stop Chinese companies renting AI chips remotely through overseas data centres. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick is joining the trip.

CATL, Baidu and Unitree are on the US Department of Defense’s Section 1260H list of “Chinese military companies”. Beijing has criticised the list. In July, Washington also tightened curbs on some foreign-made robots, citing possible supply chain vulnerabilities, Bloomberg reported.

The CEO summit runs on 17 and 18 November. The APEC leaders’ meeting follows on 18 and 19 November, where Trump is due to meet Xi.

Senior officials meeting in Shenzhen over the weekend prepared work on trade, connectivity and AI for the leaders, the APEC Secretariat said. It includes China’s proposed statement on the digital economy and AI, which calls for wider use of the technology in health care, education and energy and for safe and trusted AI development.
