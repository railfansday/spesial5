As a clothing-obsessed style writer, few things frustrate me more than a white tee that’s see-through, shrinks after one wash, or hangs awkwardly. So over the past few months, I made it my mission to track down the best white t-shirts for women. It might seem simple, but the details matter: the perfect tee should have just the right amount of stretch, a flattering drape, and be thick enough to wear on its own.

A white tee that ticks all those boxes is really the ultimate wardrobe essential, says celebrity stylist Julie Matos. It's the layering MVP that looks just as good with jeans as it does under a blazer. The tricky part is finding one that nails the trifecta of fabric, fit, and finish. Too often, white t-shirts are overly sheer, stiff, or cut in a way that doesn’t flatter the body. But after testing dozens, I can confirm: The perfect white t-shirt exists, and it’s worth the hunt.

So, are you ready to finally discover the perfect white tee that you can easily wear all year round? Start with this tried-and-tested guide.

After testing what feels like every white t-shirt on the internet, the Agolde Adine Shrunken Tee easily won my heart. It layers well and looks great on its own—it’s a true staple in my wardrobe that I wear about once a week. It has a relaxed silhouette and shorter (but not quite cropped) length that makes it super easy to style on its own with jeans or under a blazer or leather jacket. Oh, and it isn’t see-through at all.

It’s made from 100 percent organic cotton and has that thick, substantial feel I love in a t-shirt. Its weight gives it a luxe feel, even though the design is simple. That’s why it's thick enough to be worn with a nude bra, but still breathable and soft against the skin. Plus, it holds its shape well after multiple washes—I’ve been wearing and washing this tee at least once a week for the past year—no shrinkage, loose threads, or pilling in sight.

This tee nails that lived-in, vintage look that makes it look like you’ve owned it for years in the best possible way. Matos loves this t-shirt for that “fitted but not too tight” silhouette, and I totally agree. It’s structured enough to avoid looking sloppy, yet still relaxed and casual. The shrunken cut adds the right amount of shape, flattering my frame without too much extra fabric. The length seals the deal: it hits right at the waistband of my mid-rise jeans, so it looks perfectly balanced.

Between the fit, the feel, and that easy, slightly worn-in finish, the Adine Shrunken Tee truly is the white t-shirt gold standard. I’ve been wearing it nonstop—with jeans and a blazer to the office, layered under a sweater, or under a zip-up hoodie and sweats for laid-back errands.
