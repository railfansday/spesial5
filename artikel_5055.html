Stocks bounce on NVIDIA CEO news – Yet Bitcoin erases gains within six hours

Bitcoin [BTC] has slipped below the $60k mark for the second time within a week. According to CoinMarketCap data, the leading crypto was down 26.1% over the past month and 8.5% over the past week.

The rally past $80k in September fueled hopes of a bullish reversal, and it was widely accepted that the cyclical bottom might be in. Unfortunately for holders and investors, the rally didn’t last.

Relative weakness, like relative strength, is something market participants must be keenly aware of. An altcoin rallying while the rest of the market consolidates is a sign of relative strength and a good bet for continued momentum.

Equally, Bitcoin’s sell-off from the bounce to $64,603 on Monday, the 23rd of November, was worth noting.

Glassnode pointed out that stocks jumped on the news that NVIDIA reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO. Yet, within six hours of the 5% bounce, Bitcoin had retraced the move, and since then, has slipped below the $61k local support zone too.

The stock markets have also come under pressure. Nasdaq’s gains since the news came are gone. Investors are using any shred of strength to sell into, and Bitcoin was only the canary in the coal mine, wrote Glassnode.

Add to this a persistent lack of demand, and a BTC slide to $53k or even lower seems viable. In the past 20 trading days, U.S. spot BTC ETFs have shed $4.3 billion. Coinbase saw heavy selling pressure.

U.S. investors are wary of further drawdown, the data showed.

The short-term holder spent output profit ratio (STH SOPR) was at 0.99. Readings below 1.0 meant coins of this group are, on average, moving at a loss.

Crypto analyst Axel Adler Jr. observed that the LTH SOPR had fallen by 0.20 over the past week. It reached a 90-day low at 0.76, revealing LTH-moved coins were, on average, at deeper losses than the STH coins.

An SOPR return above 1.0 would signal the cohort’s profitability. Yet, the 22-day below-break-even LTH SOPR streak, alongside the BTC weakness and lack of demand, was a worrisome signal.

Alphractal data showed that the onchain realized price of Bitcoin was $53,104. If these bearish conditions persist, a price drop to $53k could materialize.
