Two enquiries arrive as Boniface nears winter exit from Leverkusen

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Out-of-favour striker Victor Boniface wants to leave Bayer 04 Leverkusen in January, is the latest report from Sky Germany.

The Rhineland club are prepared to let the Nigerian marksman go and are even willing to include on loan offer as a solution.

Sky understands that two enquiries have already been made and the situation is now becoming more concrete.

Boniface, who is under contract until summer of 2028, currently ranks as Leverkusen’s third-choice striker for Carles Martínez.

The 25-year-old has already fallen down the pecking order after he stormed down the tunnel during Leverkusen's 4-3 win over Mainz.
