Temu owner PDD Holdings stock jumps after profit beat ends string of declines

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Free daily briefing on global business news.

PDD Holdings' third-quarter net income rose to 29.5 billion yuan from 29.3 billion a year earlier as revenue held flat at 108.3 billion yuan

PDD Holdings, the owner of Temu, reported third-quarter net income of 29.5 billion yuan ($4.4 billion) on Thursday, up from 29.3 billion yuan in the same period a year earlier.

The results broke a run of falling earnings at the company, according to The Wall Street Journal. PDD stock rose close to 9% in premarket trading and has fallen about one third this year.

Revenue was 108.3 billion yuan ($16.2 billion), unchanged from a year earlier. Analysts projected 24.81 billion yuan in net profit and 115.67 billion yuan in revenue, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Operating profit rose to 31.2 billion yuan from 25.0 billion yuan. Total operating expenses fell 12% to 31.9 billion yuan, which the company attributed mainly to lower sales and marketing expenses. Those expenses were 25.3 billion yuan, compared with 30.3 billion yuan a year earlier.

Revenue from online marketing services and others rose 12% to 59.8 billion yuan. Revenue from transaction services fell 12% to 48.5 billion yuan.

Non-GAAP net income, which excludes share-based compensation expenses, was 31.0 billion yuan, compared with 31.4 billion yuan a year earlier. Diluted earnings per American depositary share were 20.04 yuan, up from 19.70 yuan.

Net cash generated from operating activities was 43.0 billion yuan, compared with 45.7 billion yuan a year earlier. Cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments totaled 455.4 billion yuan as of Sept. 30, up from 422.3 billion yuan at the end of 2025.

“We remain in a strategic investment phase, focused on strengthening supply chain capabilities and supporting our merchants,” Liu Jun, the company’s vice president of finance, said in a statement.

“As our global business adapts to an evolving trade and regulatory environment, we remain committed to deepening our supply chain investments,” Co-Chairman and Co-CEO Chen Lei said in the same statement.
