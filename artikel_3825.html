Lucky Luke: The Lonesome Cowboy Collection announced for PS5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, PS4, Xbox One, Switch, and PC

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Atari publishing label Infogrames and developers Graphite Lab and Implicit Conversions have announced Lucky Luke: The Lonesome Cowboy Collection, a collection of seven Lucky Luke games from Game Boy Color, Super Nintendo Entertainment System, PlayStation, and Game Boy Advance. It will launch for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Switch 2, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Switch, and PC via Steam on January 13, 2027.

Here is an overview of the collection, via Infogrames.

Lucky Luke: The Lonesome Cowboy Collection brings seven Lucky Luke titles from the 8-, 16-, and 32-bit eras to all modern platforms for the first time.

Lucky Luke: The Lonesome Cowboy Collection includes

Every game features quality-of-life improvements, including improved graphics, save states, achievements, visual filters, custom screen bezels, and rebindable controls. “How to Play” guides onboard unfamiliar players and help them solve difficult challenges.

The bonus Frontier Experience is an interactive first-person museum and shooting gallery that allows players to explore the history of the iconic gunslinger firsthand. Players can explore western locales, enjoy target-shooting minigames, search for collectibles and discover original comic and character art.

The legendary Lucky Luke originated in 1946 with a French-Belgian comic book about a street-smart gunslinger famous for shooting faster than his own shadow. Over the past 70 years the cowboy has gone on to feature in numerous comics, several animated television series, feature films, and most recently a 2026 live-action television series.

The Lonesome Cowboy Collection reunites Infogrames with the popular western franchise. Infogrames published several Lucky Luke titles in the late 90s and early 2000’s, including the incredibly rare Playstation Platinum edition of Lucky Luke: Western Fever.

The collection includes four games that were only released here in Europe and never made it to the United States, including the 1997 Super Nintendo Entertainment System release of Lucky Luke, the 2000 Game Boy Color release of Lucky Luke: Desperado Train, the 2001 PlayStation release of Lucky Luke: Western Fever, and the 2001 Game Boy Advance release of Lucky Luke: Wanted!
