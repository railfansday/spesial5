How to raid other players in ROB RUN

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

ROB RUN has many ways for you to expand your criminal empire, and raiding other players is certainly a good strategy. While the method isn’t as easy as breaking into NPC houses, you can master the art by following some simple tips.

First, you need to obtain Breach Decoders.

The first task is to get the Decoders, and there are different ways to get them. Uncommon Decoders are the easiest ones to get, and you can obtain them from cabinets, safes, and different containers. You can also find some from the Season 1 battle pass.

If you want to have the Epic Decoder, you can obtain it by exchanging the Uncommon variant. Go to Lenny’s Motel, and you’ll find a barter trader who will provide you with the item for $10,000.

When you’re about to raid a player base, you’ll need to have multiple Decoders. Unlike a house, a player base is a lot more complex when it comes to completing a breach. Each part requires a separate Decoder (or more than one) to gain entry.

In simple words, you’ll need to bring a Decoder for every base part that has a security system.

Completing a raid is pretty simple as long as you have the required Decoders. You have to use them to strip away points from a security terminal until it hits zero. The number of points you have to strip varies across different parts.

While the Fuse slots for a part don’t change, its tier will determine the number of Decoders you’ll need to open them.
