RPG Maker U2U launches January 28, 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Role-playing game creation software RPG Maker U2U will launch for PC via Steam on January 28, 2027, Gotcha Gotcha Games announced.

Availability of the “P2D Map Editor Demo,” which was originally planned to end today, has also been extended up until the software’s launch.

Here is an overview of the software, via Gotcha Gotcha Games:

RPG Maker U2U is the latest entry in the RPG Maker series of easy to use game development tools. With the addition of the new P2D system for adding depth and new light & shadow effects, create world of rich detail and immersion like never before!

RPG Maker U2U expands traditional 2D maps with depth, light, shadow, and a variety of beautiful effects with the new “P2D” (Perspective 2D) function. Create the world of your imagination and expression like never before in latest entry in the RPG Maker franchise!

P2D combine the intuitive simplicity of top-down mapping with true spatial elevation: add slopes, ledges, multi-story buildings, overpasses, and more with ease! Create towns, dungeons, interiors, and overworlds with a level of depth that exceeds traditional 2D map creation!

Out of the box you’ll have access to lots of visual effects. Camera effects like depth of field, weather effects like rain and fog, and dramatic lighting and shadows for interior lighting, street lamps, and other uses! When these effects are combined with the 3D verticality offered by P2D, creators will be able to make maps with strong aesthetics and mood for more immersive storytelling.

No coding required, so getting started is a snap! Create maps, game events, and configure your combat with U2U’s easy and intuitive editor.

And if you’re not an artist, don’t worry! U2U ships with a variety of different characters, monsters, visual effects, music, sound effects and more! Everything you need to get started with no additional downloads needed.

More than 100 pre-built P2D maps are included and ready for use!

Use the dedicated P2D map editor to quickly make place pre-made objects or make new objects from with provided blocks to create worlds tailored to your vision.

Re-use your existing RPG Maker MV and RPG Maker MZ assets as P2D map assets! See your familiar assets take on new life with the addition of P2D’s lighting and depth effects!

As the user interface and user experience are built around the RPG Maker MZ workflow, RPG Maker U2U should feel immediately familiar to series veterans.

A note of caution: conversion of project data and plug-ins created with previous RPG Maker titles is not supported.
