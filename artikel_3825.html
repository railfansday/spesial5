How to contact Slone on the Slone Phone in Plunder Port in Fortnite Treasure Hunters

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Weekly quests often challenge you to tackle fairly specific, strange tasks you’re not likely to attempt otherwise in Fortnite. One such mission asks you to contact Slone on the Slone Phone in Plunder Port.

Any mission that asks you to track down a specific item around the vast island is tough, as there are loads of unique locations to explore, and you’re looking for one very small item hidden around them. It’s a tough task to get done, so here’s how to contact Slone on the Slone Phone in Plunder Port in Fortnite.

You can find the Slone Phone in a brick building near the northeast end of the Plunder Port POI. This location is situated near the very southern end of the island. To find the Slone Phone, visit this spot and head over to the north end of the area, where there are a few different buildings you can explore, rather than the port, which has two ships docked at it.

At Plunder Port, look for the large, two-story brick warehouse building. It’s fairly close to the bright pink flamingo ship docked near the east end of this location. This building also has a large slurp tank slightly sticking out of it, so you can look for this to help you find the right spot.

Inside this building, walk around to the back of the large silver slurp tank. Turn right to find a small storage room. On the ground in front of the wooden shelves, you’ll find the Slone Phone.

Once you’re somewhat close to the right building, a white diamond icon with an exclamation point will appear, marking the precise location of the Slone Phone. You do have to be fairly close to it for this symbol to pop up, though, so finding the general region for it first is essential.

To complete the contact Slone on the Slone Phone in Plunder Port quest, you need to find and answer the Slone Phone at Plunder Port. The tough part of this mission is tracking down the small phone, but once you’ve found it, all you need to do is hold down the button that pops up next to the “Answer” text when looking at it. As soon as you do, this mission will be complete.

There are lots of other tricky missions you may want to tackle after checking off this one. Next, consider working on collecting a map fragment at The $.$. Rocky, using Sprite Wanted Posters to dig up and open Sprite Chests, and unlocking Solid Gold A$AP Rocky.
