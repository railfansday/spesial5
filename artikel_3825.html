Marvel fans are convinced Iman Vellani has been hiding her own Avengers: Doomsday casting in plain sight

After weeks of Marvel's Countdown to Avengers: Doomsday podcast airing on Disney Plus, Marvel fans have started to theorize that host and Ms. Marvel star Iman Vellani may actually be in the upcoming movie, and the evidence is hiding in plain sight.

One fan posted a screenshot of the podcast over on Reddit, pointing out that Vellani is sitting on a black director's chair in every episode. Why is that important? Well, you may remember that rather lengthy Avengers: Doomsday casting announcement, which was live-streamed on Instagram, which revealed the bulk of the cast by panning over to directors' chairs with names printed on them.

Now fans think that Vellani is going to turn her chair around and reveal her casting in the final episode of Countdown to Doomsday. "So this is obviously Iman's Doomsday chair and she will reveal it in the last episode, right?" said the original Reddit poster. You may be thinking why this theory has only just started to heat up when the podcast has been airing since August. But now that it has been confirmed that Hailee Steinfeld will return as Kate Bishop, Vellani's casting seems even more likely.

"Kathryn Newton revealed her own chair after she appeared in the first trailer, then when the ScreenX version of the trailer dropped, we noticed that Scott and Cassie's meeting was happening at Kate Bishop's apartment, and now Hailee Steinfeld revealed her chair before the next trailer, which comes out in a few hours," said the fan. "Since Kamala is the one who brought these characters together to form her new team, Kamala is definitely in that apartment with them, right?"

The plot thickens as back in April 2026, Lord of the Rings star Andy Serkis seemingly let Vellani's casting slip. When the Ms. Marvel star told Serkis that she is excited to see The Hunt for Gollum, Serkis replied, "I'm excited to see you in Doomsday." Many passed that off as a joke, but it may not have been. As we know, the full Doomsday cast has yet to be revealed, as co-director Anthony Russo has confirmed that he and Joe Russo still haven't announced the full cast.

Avengers: Doomsday hits cinemas on December 18, and December 16 in the UK. For more, check out our guide to the Marvel timeline, and keep up with upcoming Marvel movies and shows on the way.
