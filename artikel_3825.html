Girls und Panzer: All-Star Tank Carnival adds PS5 and PC versions, launches March 25, 2027 in Japan

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Tank battle action Girls und Panzer: All-Star Tank Carnival will launch for PlayStation 5, Switch 2, and PC via Steam on March 25, 2027 in Japan for 6,980 yen, D3 Publisher announced. A limited edition will also be available for 14,080 yen. The PlayStation 5 and Switch 2 versions will be available physically, with the Switch 2 version shipping on a game-key card. The PlayStation 5 and PC versions are newly announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via our previous coverage:

An Even More Evolved and Exhilarating Tank Action Experience from the Team Behind Girls und Panzer: Dream Tank Match

A fully-featured tank action game where you pilot tanks in combat!

Enjoy intense tank battles from the team that brought you the well-received Girls und Panzer: Dream Tank Match for PlayStation 4 and Switch.

The fast-paced action that fans loved in Girls und Panzer: Dream Tank Match returns for this game. Whether it’s close-quarters combat while drifting, angling your armor, or targeting tank treads to immobilize them, players will be able to enjoy authentic, Girls und Panzer-esque tank combat.

The graphics and presentation have also been improved. The reactions of the tank commanders peeking out of their tanks, along with their lively dialogue, add to the excitement of each match.

Explore Oarai, where the story of Girls und Panzer is set! Tour the streets virtually, taking in familiar sights one by one for a sightseeing experience that feels just like exploring Oarai!

Of course, you can also drive the Panzer IV around town.

Talk to friends scattered across Oarai to trigger game-original story quests (matches). The game progresses as you explore the town.

Intense Online Battles Against Rivals Across the Nation

Battle against players across the nation anytime in real-time tank combat! Use your favorite tank and crew combination in online battles with up to 10 players. Engage in intense tankery battles with rivals from across the nation online!
