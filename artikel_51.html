France: Protesters march for better salaries across the country

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Tens of thousands of people took part in protests across France on Tuesday after unions called a new nationwide public sector strike to demand pay rises. What started over two months ago as high school student protests has now morphed into a wider movement against the increasing cost of living.
