'Our culture and identity': Deaf Nigerians embrace indigenous sign languages

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

With a quick flick of the wrist, Umaru Zayyanu conveys the phrase "I am a Nigerian". But the 37-year-old is not signing, rather in the west African nation's official sign language. Instead, he's using Hausa sign language. Such indigenous sign languages abound in Africa's most populous country, where deaf people were communicating with each other long before the advent of formal sign language taught in deaf schools, in 1960.
