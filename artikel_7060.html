Paid Media Specialist at Kinsta

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Kinsta is one of the top providers of managed hosting for WordPress, with a growing club of 230,000+ businesses including startups, universities, and Fortune 500 firms. We're motivated by excellent quality and proud to have received a 97% satisfaction rating from our customers.

That's because, to us, each day is a chance to grow. As a team, we continuously inspire each other to learn new and better ways to make an impact. We own our jobs proudly - our leaders don't hold our hands, but they're in our corner whenever we ask for help - and our environment is flexible and remote-first.

As our new Paid Media Specialist, you'll plan, run, and optimize paid social advertising campaigns for Kinsta. You'll join our Performance Marketing team, report to the Paid Media Team Lead, and work closely with Demand Generation, Design, and RevOps to deliver a consistent volume of sign-ups and revenue that supports Kinsta's growth goals. It's a hands-on role for someone who is comfortable with data and enjoys testing new ideas.

Your focus is Kinsta's paid social presence across LinkedIn, Meta, Reddit, X, and other relevant platforms. You'll reach the businesses, agencies, and enterprises that run their sites on WordPress, through campaigns that cover the full funnel from awareness to customer acquisition.

You'll own campaigns from build to optimization, keep conversion tracking reliable, and turn performance data into clear recommendations. We work with teams across the globe, so you should be used to collaborating asynchronously and flexible enough to join the occasional early or late call. The ideal Kinsta Paid Media Specialist is analytical, detail-oriented, curious, proactive, and collaborative.

Plan, build, launch, and manage paid social campaigns across LinkedIn, Meta, Reddit, X, and other relevant platforms, covering the full funnel from awareness and demand generation to lead generation and customer acquisition.

Monitor performance and continuously optimize targeting, bidding strategies, budgets, ad creatives, and landing pages, scaling what works while keeping acquisition costs efficient.

Run audience research and competitive analysis to find new opportunities for growth.

Develop and run A/B tests across ad messaging, creative assets, audiences, and landing pages.

Own conversion tracking: implement, maintain, and troubleshoot it through Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4 (GA4), and platform-specific pixels and tags, and work with marketing and technical teams to keep conversion events and attribution accurate.

Analyze campaign performance using ad platform data, web analytics, and CRM reporting, and build reports and dashboards that share results and actionable insights.

Collaborate with content, design, product marketing, and sales on ad copy, creative concepts, and campaign assets that match our messaging and business priorities.

Support our paid media strategy: follow advertising trends and platform updates, and recommend ways to improve campaign efficiency, lead quality, and overall marketing ROI.

Use AI tools to streamline research, reporting, ad copy, and creative ideation, automate repetitive tasks, and apply critical thinking to AI-generated recommendations so decisions stay grounded in accurate data and business objectives.

2-4 years of hands-on experience independently building, launching, managing, and optimizing paid social campaigns, including on Meta, LinkedIn, and Reddit, ideally in hosting, B2B technology, or a related industry.

Practical experience setting up and troubleshooting conversion tracking with Google Tag Manager, GA4, and advertising platform pixels or conversion tags.

A strong understanding of core paid media metrics, including CPC, CPM, CTR, conversion rate, CPA, and ROAS, and experience analyzing campaign data and turning findings into actionable optimizations.

Familiarity with audience targeting, retargeting, and campaign testing methodologies, and an understanding of marketing funnels, attribution, and how paid media contributes to lead generation and customer acquisition.

Attention to detail, a proactive approach to problem-solving, and excellent organizational and communication skills, with the ability to manage multiple campaigns and priorities at once.

Experience running paid media campaigns for B2B SaaS, hosting, PaaS, or other technical products, and familiarity with WordPress or cloud platforms.

Experience with CRM, marketing automation, and attribution or reporting tools such as HubSpot, Salesforce, Looker Studio, Dreamdata, or HockeyStack.

Experience optimizing landing pages and collaborating on conversion rate optimization (CRO) initiatives.

Proficiency in other languages and experience managing non-English paid media campaigns

Experience using AI tools such as ChatGPT or Claude to improve campaign workflows, analysis, and creative development.

Annual professional development budget: available after one year with Kinsta.
