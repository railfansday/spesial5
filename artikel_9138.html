TEFAF Maastricht Names 277 Exhibitors for 2027 Fair

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

TEFAF Maastricht, the European Fine Art Foundation’s Dutch art fair, has lined up 277 international galleries for its 2027 edition at MECC, a convention center just outside the center of town, and the fair’s longtime home. TEFAF also runs a New York edition, at the Park Avenue Armory, since 2016.)

TEFAF is known for its rigorous vetting process, massive floral displays, and high-profile museum buyers, not to mention top-notch examples from 7,000 years’ worth of art history. Dealers at last year’s fair were “more than pleased” with sales and attendance, ARTnews reported. Newcomers to the 2027 edition’s main section include Galerie AB and Galerie Édouard Ambroselli, (France); Gallery Arte y Ritual (Spain); Galerie Bei Der Albertina (Austria); Butchoff Antiques (UK); Hazlitt (UK and US); Xavier Hufkens (Belgium); Thomsen Gallery and Martos Gallery (US); and Thaddaeus Ropac (UK, France, Austria, Italy, South Korea).

The fair will once again include two special sections: Showcase, featuring nine emerging dealers, all showing at TEFAF for the first time, and Focus, dedicated to seven galleries with booths that zero in on a single artist or curatorial concept. Three of this year’s Focus galleries are new to the fair: Daguet-Bresson from France, Edel Assanti from the UK, and Repetto Gallery from Switzerland.
