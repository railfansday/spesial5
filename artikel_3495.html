The Only Living Pickpocket in New York is an entertaining dramedy with some tributes to New York City. It’s also a very interesting take on heist movies. It isn’t the typical version of this movie, but it definitely incorporates elements of the best ones.

Additionally, John Turturro gives a fantastic performance. It may put him at the forefront of some Best Actor awards this year. He is one of the best things about The Only Living Pickpocket in New York, and that’s what helps this one important scene stand out so much.

Warning: The Only Living Pickpocket in New York spoilers are ahead. Proceed with caution.

Tatiana Maslany plays Kelly, Harry’s (John Turturro) long-neglected daughter. Before facing his death, Harry takes a detour to see Kelly one last time. In this scene, she pours out all her anger and frustration about her father for abandoning her all these years. Harry mostly allows his daughter to express her anger because this is the last time they will ever see each other.

However, he remains somewhat hopeful that this can end meaningfully and peacefully. He doesn’t really get that, but makes some amends before dying.

Maslany is one of my favorite actors. Therefore, I was once again mesmerized by one of her performances. Turturro truly is transcendent in this scene. He emotes so much with his facial expressions and the unspoken words. It’s always a privilege to watch two masters at their craft work and play off one another. I now wish they were a duo who appeared in more projects together. They’re so breathtaking together.

The first half of The Only Living Pickpocket in New York tells us a lot about Harry’s current life and career. However, we don’t learn much about his backstory. The full picture of how he developed this life and these relationships doesn’t come together until this pivotal scene. The confrontation between Harry and Kelly reveals so much backstory. We learn how he neglected his first family and a little bit of the story with his current wife.

Harry picks pockets for a living, but this is the first time you see how much of a deadbeat he used to be. It’s a stunning scene because it reminds us that he’s nefarious in many ways. However, it also shows his layers. He’s done bad things, but he’s not a terrible person, just a typical complicated human.

The Only Living Pickpocket in New York is about a man who is going to die. Unlike many movies with a similar concept, the main character doesn't discover they have a terminal illness; Harry will be murdered at the end of the movie. We know he’s going to die from early in it. Therefore, most of the movie is him trying to make amends and save those he cares about before it's over.

The scene between Harry and Kelly gets to the heart of that. This is a complex man trying to say goodbye before it's too late. It’s about doing something worthy of your legacy before you meet your end. There are many reasons to watch The Only Living Pickpocket in New York, but that scene is one of them. Add it to your must-watch 2026 movies list.
