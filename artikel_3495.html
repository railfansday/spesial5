Tatyana Ali Laughs Off 'DWTS' Partner Jan Ravnik's F-Bomb on Live Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Tatyana Ali has thoughts about hearing her Dancing With the Stars partner, Jan Ravnik, drop the F-bomb on live TV.

“I wasn’t that shocked,” Ali, 47, quipped while speaking with Extra after the Tuesday, November 24, finale. “He was just being real. It’s live TV, though.”

After Ali and Ravnik, 31, performed a stunning freestyle, the pro dancer was quickly censored when cohost Julianne Hough asked about his emotions. Ravnik said Ali “f***ing killed it” in the ballroom.

“It was just the perfect way to describe it, the last dance,” he told the outlet, defending his word choice.

Ravnik choreographed Ali’s freestyle to “Minnie the Moocher” by Cab Calloway and “Over the Rainbow” by Judy Garland.

“Jan told me to tell him what my dream world was like, and he created this beautiful dance that’s a metaphor for my experience here,” Ali tearfully said from the skybox on Tuesday. “So it was fitting that it would be the last dance that we did.”

Ali and Ravnik finished season 35 in fourth place, with Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach clinching the mirrorball trophy.

Despite narrowly missing a victory, Ali and Ravnik are grateful for their experience on the dance floor.

“There’s no goodbye, let’s stop talking about that,” he quipped to Extra, stressing they plan to remain friends.

Ali has previously been candid about her transformation in the ballroom.

“I was a different size when I came here, but I loved my body then too,” she exclusively told Us on November 17 after the semifinals. “I loved her. She helped me bring my babies into this world. She went through a lot of stuff [and] trauma. She got me through it.”

The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air alum continued at the time, “I liked my body before I got here, but it feels like I have — I don’t know how to describe it — I have room in my body. I don’t know how else to say it.”

“My advice for them is dance. Get your girls together and your mom friends because we’re not supposed to be momming by ourselves,” she stated. “I think that’s unnatural. We’re supposed to actually have a village and be doing it together. But y’all need to dance.”
