Below Deck's Chef Ben Robinson Defends Joao Franco After Arrest

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Below Deck’s Chef Ben Robinson is coming to the defense of his costar and friend João Franco after he was arrested in Turkey following a fatal fight.

“I’m so sorry to hear this,” Ben, 45, commented via an Instagram post on Monday, November 16. “Clearly, João was trying to de-escalate the problem. Now he faced five aggressors upon him, and it was clearly self-defense. So unfortunate that this minor lost his life.”

João, 36, and his brother, Karlos Franco, were reportedly arrested in Turkey following a brawl that left a 17-year-old dead.

The pair were seen engaged in a verbal dispute with the employees of a clothing store in the Bodrum district of Muğla on November 7, according to CCTV footage obtained by Page Six.

Although João seemingly attempted to break up the fight multiple times, he eventually appeared to swing a fist at one employee, causing the person to fall to the ground. The fight ended moments later.

The individual who fell to the ground was a 17-year-old employee who hit his head on some stairs, according to local outlets. He later died at a hospital, per the reports. (Us previously reached out to reps for Bravo and João for comment.)

Ben and João starred together on season 4 of Below Deck Down Under, which premiered in February on Bravo.

Colin Macy-O’Toole, who worked with João on season 3 of Below Deck Mediterranean in 2018, also came to his costar’s defense.

“Having seen the video footage, my impression is that João appeared to be trying to defend himself and de-escalate an already escalating situation,” Colin, who was not present for the incident, told Page Six on November 11. “From what is visible, it doesn’t look to me like he was looking to start a confrontation. It looks like he was reacting to what was happening around him.”

Colin, 39, continued, “Knowing João, I think it’s important that people take the full context of the footage into consideration before jumping to conclusions or assigning blame. This is an incredibly tragic situation, and I hope the facts are allowed to come out before anyone rushes to judgment.”

João first appeared on Bravo’s Below Deck Mediterranean season 3 in 2018 as a lead deckhand under Captain Sandy Yawn before returning to the show during season 4 in 2019 as a bosun.

He later appeared on Below Deck Down Under seasons 2 and 4 as a second officer/bosun under Captain Jason Chambers.

Despite the show’s success, João previously announced that he would not be returning to the franchise.

“The show neatly mirrored my real-world career progression. Across the four series, I moved from deckhand to lead deckhand, bosun and finally captain,” he told the Mallorca Daily Bulletin in May. “I never wanted to be a deckhand forever. The goal was always the bridge, so I spent every spare moment doing courses to move up.”
