MCU Star Teases Potential Avengers Return After Massive Character Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

WARNING: This interview contains MAJOR spoilers from VisionQuest season 1, episode 8, "Forever."EXCLUSIVE: One of the newest players in the Marvel Cinematic Universe bites the dust.

Since 2021, the MCU has continued to expand the franchise on the big screen, but also through the world of television, following Disney+'s launch, allowing new and established characters from the beloved superhero property to get their time to shine on the streaming platform. While the Avengers are about to enter their next era with Doomsday and Secret Wars, Marvel Television is officially ending one with the completion of the latest TV series.

In an exclusive interview with ScreenRant's Andy Behbakht for VisionQuest, T'Nia Miller, who boarded the WandaVision trilogy series as Jo Carrington/Jocasta, discussed the death of her character in the major finale of the Disney+ Original. While she became a big casualty in the Phase 6 drama, things may not be over for her just yet.

As the VisionQuest post-credits scene showed Ultron still having Jocasta's drive, the actress was asked if she had been told what the future may have in store for her, or if she, like audience members, is also waiting to learn her fate beyond the latest Marvel TV show. Miller commented, "Oh, I'm there with the rest of you. I'm in Ultron's hands, so let's see what happens."

If she gets the option to return to the MCU timeline, Miller shared how she would love to tap into some of Jocasta's larger mythology from the comics, as she has history with Earth's Mightiest Heroes themselves. The Free Rein alum commented that "I would like to see her starting to set about right what she started. I know in the comic book she works alongside the Avengers, and so I'd love to see some of that. That would be great. That'd be super cool, but yeah, I think I'd like to see what Jo Carrington started."

With the Avengers set to be the focus in the next upcoming MCU movies, Miller addressed whether or not she had maybe filmed something for Doomsday or Secret Wars. However, she has not filmed anything as Jocasta since VisionQuest, saying, "Oh no, no, no, no, no, no! [laughs] I wish! Some people pop up in those places, but not Jocasta, [not] this time."

As the WandaVision trilogy is completed, given that there are no plans currently for VisionQuest season 2, there is always the option for Marvel Studios to keep the spinoff era going. When asked if she would be down to headline an Ultron/Jocasta offshoot with James Spader, Miller was more than open to that, sharing, "It would be great, wouldn't it? Yeah, that would be great to see an Ultron and Jocasta spinoff of some sort. What would that look like?"

As of writing, it has yet to be revealed what the future has in store for several VisionQuest characters, excluding the big setup for one major hero in Avengers: Doomsday. Given that the focus is to finish the remainder of the Multiverse Saga, it may not be until Phase 7 that viewers learn about what will happen to some of these players.
