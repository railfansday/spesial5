Another Live-Service Game Is Going Extinct, And This One Has Dinosaurs

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Capcom has had a big year, but amid the hits comes news that its Overwatch-with-dinosaurs game, Exoprimal, will join the live-service graveyard next year. The game's servers will go offline next year, making it unplayable after that.

"Thank you so much for your continued use of Exoprimal, a statement on the Exoprimal site reads. "We regret to announce that service for Exoprimal will be discontinued as of Thursday, September 30, 2027. We cannot thank you enough for the love and support you have shown over the years, and we sincerely apologize for the gravity of this announcement."

Exoprimal season passes are no longer available to purchase through storefronts, and Capcom will slowly wind down the game over several months. Exoprimal and all its DLC will be delisted from stores starting March 30, 2027, but anyone who owns it can still download and play it before the September 2027 deadline. Despite a cool premise and fun gameplay, Exoprimal never truly took off after its 2023 launch.

"While Exoprimal’s early hours of repetitive and simple missions will likely drive some people away, its later surprises are a delight for those willing to give it the necessary time," James Carr wrote in GameSpot's Exoprimal review. "The escalation of difficulty and the wide variety of objectives introduced later on in the shooter gives it fresh life several hours in. Fighting through an ocean of dinosaurs is a ton of fun, and the variety of enemies keeps you from falling into a repetitive combat loop."

The writing was already on the wall for Exoprimal in 2024, as Capcom announced it was ending seasonal updates and would rotate through existing content on a monthly schedule. While that doesn't reflect the entire player community, Exoprimal numbers have largely cratered on PC via Steam: it peaked at 4,9995 concurrent players in November 2023 and nosedived the following month. At the time of writing, Exoprimal's 24-hour peak was a paltry 23 players.

2026 has been merciless for live-service games, with several high-profile casualties. Developer Wildlight’s free-to-play shooter Highguard quickly lost momentum after its January launch, Anthem was sunset this year, and New World: Aeternum began winding down operations ahead of its January 2027 closure.
