I slept on the Helix Midnight Luxe mattress for two years, and it's still the best mattress for side sleepers I've tested. The plush pillow top provides blissful pressure relief across the shoulders, hips and knees. Which means I can sleep on my side all night and not experience any of the hip pain I'm prone to. Happily, my back sleeping husband is also a fan. Beneath the pillow top, there are a further five layers, which include responsive foams and up to 1,000 individually wrapped coils for support. It's a combination of these coils and foams that hold your spine in healthy alignment with the rest of your body. Which is why it's one of the best mattresses for back pain you can buy.

Motion isolation is excellent, which means you're unlikely to feel movement elsewhere on the bed. Edge support is also strong, which means you can comfortably sit on or sleep right up to the bed's edge.

Temperature regulation is reliable, thanks to its breathable build and Tencel cover. I've never woken up hot on the Luxe, but very hot sleepers have two cooling mattress upgrades to explore. In short, the Luxe does everything you would expect from a premium hybrid mattress, and to a very high standard.
