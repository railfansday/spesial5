Pregnant Jen Affleck Bares Bump in Bikini During Solo Babymoon Amid Divorce

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Pregnant Jen Affleck is showing off her baby bump as she enjoys a solo babymoon amid her divorce from husband Zac Affleck.

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives star, 27, shared several bump-baring photos via Instagram on Sunday, November 22, from a recent trip before she welcomes her fourth child. In several snaps, Jen can be seen wearing a black lace outfit, while in another the reality star wore a polka dot bikini on a beach with daughter Nora.

Jen and estranged husband Zac, 30, announced in June that they are expecting baby No. 4. They are already parents to daughters Nora, 5, and Penelope, 16 months, and son Luca, 3.

“A babymoon I’ll always hold close to my heart. 🤍,” Jen captioned the post.

The Hulu reality star also shared some of the uploads to her Instagram Stories, alongside her thoughts about her impending divorce from Zac.

“If only you could read my journal entries from this trip. So many mixed emotions and so much I’m still trying to process,” Jen wrote in one post. “But even in the midst of grief and change, there’s still so much to be grateful for. God has blessed me in more ways than I can count, and I never want to lose sight of that. Happy Sunday, everyone.”

In a separate Instagram Story, Jen spoke about relying on her faith during this time.

“I still have a lot of questions about my faith, especially as I navigate my divorce and figure out what moving forward looks like. There’s so much I’m still trying to understand and make peace with,” she shared. “But even on my darkest days, I find myself turning back to Him. Through the hurt, love, change, and grief, one thing has remained constant. Nothing in this life has brought me more healing, peace, or strength than my Savior, Jesus Christ.”

Jen filed for divorce from Zac earlier this month after seven years of marriage.

“It was this fourth child. I had to ask myself very hard questions like, ‘Is this the type of relationship I want this child to look up to?’” the Dancing With the Stars alum exclusively told Us at the premiere of The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County. “When I realized it was a no, that’s when I realized that I needed to make a change. You’ll see a lot of that unfold in season [1 of the spinoff].”

“I think doing reality TV for sure sped up the process [of calling it quits] because when you’re filming and you’re rewatching your life unfold in front of millions of people, it’s the difference between looking in a mirror,” she continued. “It highlights things.”

Jen added, “But also it’s like one year of therapy feels actually like 20 years of therapy because it’s so much more magnified when you’re sharing it in front of the world. I feel like if I hadn’t done reality TV, I would have still made this decision. It just would have been way longer down the road.”
