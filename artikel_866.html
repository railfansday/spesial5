Allen to join Williams in interim Swans coaching team

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Joe Allen and Ashley Williams both played for Swansea in the Premier League

Former players Joe Allen and Ashley Williams are set to join a temporary Swansea City coaching team as the Championship club attempt to fill the void left by the departure of Vitor Matos.

Allen, 36, and Williams, 42, are poised to work alongside fellow ex-Swans Kristian O'Leary and Leon Britton as part of interim coaching staff.

Matos was named Strasbourg boss on Wednesday after just under a year in charge at Swansea, although the Welsh club are still to confirm his exit.

That leaves coaches O'Leary and Britton as the only remaining members of Matos' management team.

Swansea have therefore asked Allen and Williams to help out as they prepare for Saturday's home league game against West Bromwich Albion (15:00 GMT).
