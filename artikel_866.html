Who is close to a yellow card suspension in Champions League?

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Arsenal forward Kai Havertz is among the players to have received two yellow cards in this season's Champions League

Players will receive a one-match ban should they receive four yellow cards before the semi-finals of the Champions League.

This rule runs from the start of the league phase until the end of the quarter-finals. After then, all yellow cards are wiped out and players cannot be suspended for multiple yellow cards.

Yellow cards that result in a red card during a match do not count towards a player's tally, as a separate suspension will be served.

The same rules apply for both the Europa League and the Conference League.

During the opening three rounds of the Champions League, 16 players received two yellow cards, meaning they are now two cautions away from a one-match ban.

Shakhtar Donetsk's Marlon Gomes, Villarreal's Santiago Mourino and Inter Milan's Hakan Calhanoglu are each on three yellow cards, putting them on the brink of a ban.

Players can also be suspended if they pick up six, eight or 10 yellow cards. This would also result in a one-match suspension every time a player hits an even number of yellow cards above four.

Red cards result in a one-match suspension, with Bayern Munich's Michael Olise missing his side's earlier clash with Arsenal after being sent off against Viking in the previous round of fixtures.

Uefa may increase the length of a red card ban for serious offences such as violent conduct.

*denotes two yellows that resulted in a red card
