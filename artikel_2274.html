Burnley team news and predicted XI to face Blackburn Rovers as key player returns for derby day

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Fabio Celestini has inherited a sinking ship with the monumental task of keeping Burnley afloat, with the international break arriving at the perfect time to put a temporary halt to their struggle.

The Clarets’ 5-4 capitulation at Cardiff City, where they were leading 4-1 by the 62nd minute, was a brutal reminder of where they find themselves. Celestini’s squad are devoid of confidence and leaking goals at will.

As it stands, Burnley sit 22nd in the Championship table with just one win in 15 games. They are already five points from safety and lurking in the bottom three alongside Derby County and Preston North End.

Blackburn Rovers meanwhile will feel they can finally end their 16-year wait for a victory over their arch rivals, buoyed by a 2-1 victory over promotion-chasing West Ham last time out.

Anel Ahmedhodzic could return to the fold having missed the trip to Cardiff City due to illness, but Conor Roberts will be absent after a red card in South Wales and will serve the first match of his three-game suspension.

Injuries have been an unfortunate feature of Dastan Satpayev’s time at Turf Moor since arriving on loan from Chelsea and he remains out with a recurring hamstring issue. Tunisian international Hannibal Mejbri will be absent with the same injury.

The defeat at Lincoln City brought an end to Nicky Hayen’s time in charge, and it also brought Amando Broja’s season to a temporary standstill with a groin injury. Fellow frontman Ashley Barnes remains out, but is nearing a return after featuring for the U21s side.

Jacob Bruun Larsen is out due to a stress fracture and Jordan Beyer remains a long-term absentee and is yet to make a competitive appearance since December 2023 due to a serious knee injury. Among those also unavailable are Ben Amos, Mike Tresor, Shurandy Sambo and Michael Obafemi.

In better news, Bashir Humphreys has been back in training and is on the brink of a return. The Blackburn Rovers clash may come too soon, but he could return next week.

Coudert; Walker, Ahmedhodzic, Ekdal, Pires; Cullen, Hatate; Ramazani, Edwards, Banel; Vardy.

Due to injuries, Celestini’s side pretty much picks for itself. However, there could be one key change in midfield.

Gregoire Coudert has only kept two clean sheets this term, but will line up between the sticks.

The full-back flanks will be occupied by Kyle Walker on the right and Lucas Pires on the left, while the returning Ahmedhodzic could partner Hjalmar Ekdal at centre-back in place of Igor Julio.

Josh Laurent and Reo Hatate have been the midfield pivot for the last two outings, but after three appearances off the bench, club captain and star player Josh Cullen could return to the starting lineup for the first time since his ACL injury in December 2025.

Marcus Edwards plays in attacking midfield, and with two goals in three games under Celestini, the right-wing position will likely fall to the in-form Largie Ramazani. Jayden Banel plays off the left.

The centre-forward spot is the one position which is up for grabs, with Andreas Hountondji beginning to show signs of promise. However, the experience of Jamie Vardy could prove crucial in a game of this significance.

Burnley vs Blackburn Rovers kicks off at 12:00 on Sunday, and will be broadcast live on Sky Sports Football and ITV.
