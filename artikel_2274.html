Klopp's Germany midfield pecking order after first six games

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Six games into Jürgen Klopp's tenure as Germany coach, Bild has shed light on how the national team's central midfield options are currently ranked.

Perhaps unsurprisingly, given that they started together in a double pivot in the recent 3-1 win over Serbia and 5-0 thrashing of the Netherlands, Germany captain Joshua Kimmich tops the list ahead of Bayern Munich teammate Aleksandar Pavlović.

Another Bayern player, Tom Bischof, occupies third place after coming on as the first midfield substitute in both games. The 21-year-old has moved ahead of Felix Nmecha, who was a starter under Julian Nagelsmann at the World Cup but managed just nine minutes of playing time across the two November fixtures.

Celtic's Mika Baur is ranked fifth despite missing out on the latest squad. The midfielder was reportedly first on the standby list in case of an injury and is understood to be highly regarded by Klopp.

Finally, Leeds United's Anton Stach is named as the sixth option. Klopp tested the former Hoffenheim player as a substitute in two matches in September and continues to monitor his performances in the Premier League.
