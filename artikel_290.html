Stephen King slams Trump in wake of Nvidia scandal

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Stephen King is going after Donald Trump again.

This week started with yet more controversy for the U.S. president, after he admitted to helping get Jensen Huang reinstated as Nvidia CEO. Huang had previously resigned after news of multiple affairs — including one with an alleged Chinese spy — came to light. Trump himself has personal financial ties to Nvidia, owning a large amount of its stock, which shot up following the news of Huang's return.

Like many of America's late night hosts, King wasn't impressed.

"What Trump is doing is basically protecting the stock market (where he is heavily invested) at the cost of national security," King wrote on Threads. "He should be jailed."

This isn't the first time the horror author has criticised the president, and we're betting it won't be the last.
