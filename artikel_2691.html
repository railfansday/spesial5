Seahawks make series of roster moves ahead of must-win game vs. 49ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Fresh out of their bye week, the Seattle Seahawks have added a pair of players to the active roster.

Cornerback Trevon Diggs and tackle Bobby Hart were both signed from the practice squad to the 53 on Monday, with running back George Holani placed on injured reserve in a corresponding move.

Since Diggs signed with the Seahawks during training camp, the former All-Pro corner has bounced between the practice squad, game-day roster, and 53 on multiple occasions. On the season, he's appeared in five games for the team, playing 36 snaps on defense.

The 32-year-old Hart was called into action in Seattle's win over the Las Vegas Raiders, sliding in at right tackle after Abe Lucas left with an injury. With Charles Cross missing time with an ankle injury prior to Lucas's own issue, it was the third straight game in which Hart was elevated to the game-day roster.

Like Lucas, Holani left the Seahawks' win in Las Vegas and will now be out several weeks with the knee injury suffered last Sunday. It's his third time going to IR in as many seasons in Seattle. After Emanuel Wilson was a healthy scratch against the Raiders, he will presumably slide into the rotation behind Zach Charbonnet and Jadarian Price.

To fill the two vacated practice squad spots, the Seahawks re-signed quarterback Adrian Martinez and added running back Jacob De Jesus to the 16-player unit. Martinez was initially added to the practice squad as Sam Darnold dealt with his latest injury and Drew Lock was limited with tendonitis, but was released ahead of the team's bye week.

De Jesus, a 24-year-old UDFA out of Cal, was signed by the Kansas City Chiefs after the 2026 NFL Draft. Waived during the cut to 53, he was eventually re-signed to their practice squad and then waived again after he made his debut in the Chiefs' win over the Atlanta Falcons.
