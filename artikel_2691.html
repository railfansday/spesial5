Bulls rookie Caleb Wilson to undergo surgery on hand, reportedly out 4-6 weeks

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Chicago Bulls will reportedly be without rookie Caleb Wilson for a while. After suffering a fractured right hand on Wednesday, Wilson is set to undergo surgery and will miss four to six weeks, per multiple reports.

Wilson suffered the injury during the second half of Wednesday's loss to the San Antonio Spurs, heading to the locker room in the third quarter. He was ruled out of the game before the start of the fourth quarter.

It was unclear when Wilson sustained the fractured, though the Bulls broadcast highlighted a play where Wilson's right hand got jammed against Victor Wembanyama's elbow.

Wilson, who was selected No. 4 overall out of North Carolina in June, was touted as a young player to help revive the Bulls. A dominant showing in the NBA Summer League boosted Wilson's profile ahead of the regular season.

So far, he has shown a lot of promise, averaging 18.9 points, 6.2 rebounds and 1.9 assists per game as a starting power forward.

However, Wilson has a history of hand injuries, fracturing each of his hands — his left hand in February and his right hand in March before the NCAA tournament — during his lone freshman season with the Tar Heels.
