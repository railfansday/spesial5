Titans place Tyjae Spears on injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Tennessee Titans placed running back Tyjae Spears on injured reserve with a knee injury on Saturday. We might have seen the last of Spears in a Titans uniform. There were high hopes for what Spears could bring to the offense when he was selected as a third-round pick in the 2023 NFL Draft. Unfortunately, it just never played out.

This season, Spears has 22 carries for 94 yards and 16 catches for 75 yards. He was supposed to be a threat as a receiver. While we have seen glimpses of that, he really hasn't been able to stay healthy long enough to truly make an impact.

Michael Carter will see most of the work as the third-down back with Spears being out. Carter is a fine player but not a real difference-maker.

Tony Pollard was already the lead back for the Titans, but with Spears out, he should be the only back who gets meaningful carries for the rest of the season.
