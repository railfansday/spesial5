James Cook touchdown gives Bills 7-0 lead over Chiefs

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

There is some nasty weather in Western New York on Thanksgiving night.

The Bills have at least scored one early in the second quarter, with James Cook going in for a 5-yard score.

It took Buffalo 11 plays to go 49 yards, with the team getting great field positions as a result of one play on special teams.

Chiefs returner Josh Downs let a punt fall and roll instead of fielding it in his team’s territory, which led the offense to start its second drive at the 4-yard line. Kansas City then went three-and-out, and the punt went out of bounds at the Kansas City 49.

While Buffalo got down to the 1-yard line, a false start backed them up. but Cook then erased that with his touchdown run.

In three drives, Cook has 42 yards on 13 carries with one touchdown. Allen is just 3-of-6 passing for 30 yards.
