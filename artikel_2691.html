NBA players reportedly give Victor Wembanyama terrible new nickname

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

San Antonio Spurs star Victor Wembanyama is unique on and off the hardwood. He's the Spurs' leading scorer, averaging 29.9 points per game, and San Antonio sits at the top of the Western Conference. They are 15-3 and have won nine of their last ten games.

Wemby has gone viral for his introspective press conferences and what he says about different topics in the NBA.

The 22-year-old dropped 50 points in their victory over the New Orleans Pelicans earlier this week and shared comments about "ethical" hoops.

NBA players took notice and gave him a wild new nickname.

Victor Wembanyama isn't afraid to say what's on his mind and that wasn't any different when he dropped 50 points on the Pelicans. He said, "Unlike many of my peers, I'm not very good at stat padding."

Well, that caught the attention of some of those peers. ESPN's Michael C. Wright was on The Hoop Collective podcast, and he said this:

"I've heard players around the league talking about Vic being like Mr. Morality, Mr. High Morals. 'Moral high horse' is what it's been described as"

It's a terrible nickname, but it's also hilarious that players are upset about it.

It doesn't seem like a lot bothers the French big man, and this probably won't either. There is a saying: "If you have a problem, do something about it." Good luck to the rest of the NBA trying to stop him and his Spurs teammates.

Wemby has scored at least 25 points in four straight games. He's also excelling on defense. The Spurs' franchise player is blocking 3.9 shots per game and collecting 1.3 steals per contest.

San Antonio's next matchup is Wednesday night with the Portland Trail Blazers.
