Spurs overcome slow start in Sacramento for eighth road win of the season

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The short-staffed San Antonio Spurs handled the Kings in Sacramento, remaining perfect on the road, and they continue to allow the fewest points average in the Western Conference (105.4). It was the third of four matchups this season, and the Spurs did not get this one easy, while Stephon Castle did lots of the heavy lifting and the background players got extra burn.

After the game, coach Mitch Johnson gave props to some of the role players for staying ready. "This is what character is. This is what a program is, in terms of you don't necessarily know when and there's a lot of un-controllables throughout a long season…"

Despite the Kings' abysmal record, they had shown more fight recently, testing the Los Angeles Lakers in a close loss and handing the Boston Celtics their third-straight loss. Yet their luck wasn't the same on Monday as the Spurs were likely on high alert for a trap game. Domantas Sabonis was a force in the lane, but this was watered down by the squad's relentless hustle, and Castle later caused him to pick up his sixth foul, forcing him to sit.

It took some time for the Spurs to get settled as they had their worst opening quarter of the season (24 percent shooting). Aside from not having much horse power early, the horde did not have its usual synchronicity as they tried to key in on movement inside the lane.

Then Julian Champagnie stepped up, shooting the team out of the slump in the second quarter with five 3-pointers, and that breathed life into them on the other end. They finished the period, holding the hosts to 39.8 percent shooting and claimed a nine-point lead going into halftime after being down by a dozen points.

The Spurs were still still having trouble after the break forcing Darius Acuff Jr to his off-hand, yet the third quarter subsequently turned into the Castle and Devin Vassell show. They buried five 3-pointers, and Castle got the step on defenders. The rest of the team followed their lead, but the Kings had one run left in them, cutting the lead to eight points as the Spurs were cold to start the fourth.

It took a late surge before the Spurs could put the squeeze on them, and Castle plus Luke Kornet's fingerprints were all over it.

The Spurs won 123-107, extending their streak to three with one game left on their brief road trip.

Castle and Vassell were pressing early, making only two of their first 13 shots. They compensated for it by keeping their teammates involved and rebounding. Castle eventually found his forms and took the reins as the primary shot creator. He had his third double-double of the season of 28 points, 11 assists and eight rebounds plus set the tone with his defense, particularly, not picking up any cheap fouls. Vassell finished with 16 points, six rebounds and two assists.

The second quarter was the decisive moment of the game as the Spurs outscored them by 18 points (41-23) and logged a monstrous 157.7 offensive rating. Champagnie had 17 of his 22 points in that period.

The Spurs are usually one of the top transition teams in the league, and they only generated a few easy baskets as they didn't push the pace much. They made up for it with their pursuit of rebounds, which turned into 26 second-chance points, their most of the season. Kornet deserves most of the credit for those (9).

Without Wemby, the front-line rotation was Kornet and Tarris Reed Jr., so defensive communication was not the same, also relying more often on drop coverage and the Kings spent a good chunk of the game attacking without fear. Additionally, without Wemby's screening, Sacramento's low man only guarded one player instead of two on pick-and-roll plays. This made it a little harder for Castle to score at close range.

It takes a lot of talent to get things done in the NBA, and that still doesn't guarantee wins, so any victory with three starters missing matters. Forget that the Kings' record was 2-14 going into this game; there are still guys with pride on that team, and their eyes probably lit up when they saw the absences on the San Antonio side. They went for it and had the Spurs in a tough game for most of the first half. The Spurs were still able to score 127.3 points per 100 possessions, good enough for the 86th percentile. Not one Spur having more than two turnovers played a part in that.

Before this game, 19 3-pointers was the most the Kings had allowed this season. The Spurs had 16 and none came after the third quarter.

On a screen-roll play, Castle lobbed it perfectly to Kornet for a finish through contact.
