Game Preview: Suns look to solve their starting lineup problems against Denver

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Who: Phoenix Suns (5-7) @ Denver Nuggets (7-4)

Following a seven-game homestand, the Phoenix Suns hit the road for a three-game stint that begins on the second night of a back-to-back in the Mile High City. They'll take on a Denver Nuggets squad that already defeated them 127-120 earlier this season. And while the Nuggets haven't necessarily looked like the world beaters we've grown accustomed to seeing in recent years, they possess enough foundational continuity to remain a formidable opponent.

Meanwhile, the Phoenix Suns continue to search for answers surrounding a starting five that has struggled to establish any semblance of efficiency, cohesion, or stability through the first 12 games of the season. The bench has routinely kept Phoenix competitive and, in large part, is the reason the Suns have five victories to their name.

Perhaps getting away from home will do this group some good. For a basketball team still searching for its identity, maybe the process of finding itself begins on the road in Denver.

Mark Williams — OUT (Left Shoulder Surgery)

Ryan Dunn — QUESTIONABLE (Right Thumb Soreness)

Julian Strawther — OUT (Left Knee Sprain)

There have been two constants for the Phoenix Suns this season. The starting unit has been horrendous, and the rebounding has followed suit. So once again, what we'll be watching for tonight is whether that starting five can demonstrate any semblance of connectivity on the offensive end while possessing the ability to get stops defensively.

It's a dangerous combination right now. The starting unit struggles to generate efficient offense and has been equally ineffective at preventing opponents from doing so. Its most productive stretches typically coincide with Devin Booker putting the team on his back, and there's very little consistency in the production surrounding him. That's an unsustainable formula for success, particularly against quality opponents.

That's where my primary focus will be tonight. What can the Suns' starting five accomplish collectively on both ends of the floor? Can they establish an offensive rhythm that doesn't require Booker to shoulder the entire burden? Can they disrupt Denver's offense and finish defensive possessions by securing rebounds?

It would be nice to see the starting five establish the tone of a basketball game for once, rather than asking the second unit to come in and change it.

While the Suns rank 30th in the NBA in rebounds per game, the Nuggets rank 23rd, so rebounding might not be the primary determining factor in tonight's outcome, although it should always be an area of emphasis for Phoenix given its struggles this season.

The key to success tonight is taking care of the basketball. The Nuggets rank seventh in the NBA in steals per game, making them a pesky, feisty defensive team capable of generating extra possessions by forcing turnovers. Fortunately, one thing the Suns have done well this season is exercising discipline with the basketball, committing the sixth fewest turnovers per game in the league.

That trend needs to continue tonight in Denver. The Suns already surrender enough extra possessions through their inability to rebound. There's no need to gift their opponent additional opportunities by being careless with the basketball.

The remainder of this road trip presents some favorable matchups for Phoenix, but traveling to Denver on the second night of a back-to-back is a tough ask. Granted, the starters didn't play at all in the fourth quarter against the Lakers because the bench was busy carrying the load.

Can the starters return the favor tonight and give the second unit an opportunity to get some rest in their legs? Given what we've witnessed through the first 12 games of the season, I don't see it happening tonight.
