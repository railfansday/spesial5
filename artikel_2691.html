NFL Week 10 Survivor/Eliminator Picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The trade deadline is a weird time in the NFL. Some teams are making big changes, but football isn't like baseball — there is work to be done to incorporate guys into lineups and rotations. In baseball, sure, there might be some chemistry to form between pitcher and catcher or between shortstop and second baseman, but it's an individual game in a team sport. In football, you have to figure out how things work. That means that guys on new teams might not be excellent right away. And that makes this a particularly tough week making picks in NFL Survivor. Check out my Week 10 pick for Survivor contests. And while we're here, you can use our NFL Betting Model and our Bet Tracker to make a pick for yourself.

Every week, we have the same process. We eliminate teams in groups until we narrow it down to our real considerations. Start with all 32, whittle it down. And remember — this game is "once you use a team, they're off the board," so it's not always just a matter of taking the team you think will win. There has to be some strategy. I can't pick a team I've picked before, but you might not have tailed me, so previously used teams are eligible until the end.

My favorite category — picks I can't get wrong! If you don't play, you can't win, but also I won't pick you, so woohoo for easy. Chicago Bears Denver Broncos Philadelphia Eagles Tampa Bay Buccaneers That's four off the board.
