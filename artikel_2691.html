Are the Broncos playing today? NFL Week 10 schedule, next game, AFC West standings

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Denver Broncos can finally take a breath during the 2026 NFL season.

The Broncos are among four teams who have a scheduled bye during Week 10 of the NFL schedule, and they'll emerge from it in a strong position to chase the AFC's No. 1 overall seed coming off last year's AFC Championship game appearance. Denver currently owns a five-game winning streak and sits atop the AFC West standings, despite a point differential of just +12.

After a 26-21 victory over the Carolina Panthers in Week 9, the Broncos are now 6-0 in games decided by less than a touchdown this season. They have wins over the Jaguars, Chiefs and Seahawks that suggest Denver is poised for another deep postseason run.

The Broncos offense has endured rough patches as quarterback Bo Nix returned from the foot injury he suffered in the 2026 playoffs and coach Sean Payton was forced to take back play-calling duties from offensive coordinator Davis Webb. But Denver's defense remains potent thanks to its pass rush and several crucial turnovers have helped fuel the team's success in close games.
