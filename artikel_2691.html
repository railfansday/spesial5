Lakers make Austin Reaves injury decision ahead of Jazz rematch

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Austin Reaves is out for Monday's game against the Jazz because of a left heel bruise.

It will be the fifth straight game he has missed because of the injury.

Austin Reaves of the Los Angeles Lakers looks on during a game against the Utah Jazz in Los Angeles, Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

Reaves was a full participant in Lakers practice on Sunday, but it didn't include any live action.

The 28-year-old guard suffered the injury on Nov. 11 and has been considered day-to-day since, though Redick said Friday that Reaves was "hopefully" returning over the next two games.

Austin Reaves, Luka Doncic, Jaden Hardy, Jarred Vanderbilt and Jake LaRavia sit on the bench during a game in Los Angeles on Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026. Getty Images

With Reaves out Monday, that would make the target for Reaves' return Friday against the Spurs.

The Lakers, Spurs and 76ers are tied for the best record in the NBA at 14-3, and Luka Doncic and San Antonio's Victor Wembanyama are currently the frontrunners for the MVP Award.

Austin Reaves (15) of the Los Angeles Lakers shoots a free throw against the Milwaukee Bucks on Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, at Fiserv Forum in Milwaukee. NBAE via Getty Images

Reaves, whom the Lakers signed to a four-year, $180 million contract in July, is averaging 24.7 points (51.3% from the field, 40.9% from beyond the arc), 3.3 rebounds and 4.7 assists for the Lakers.

Redick said at Sunday's practice that Reaves is improving.

"He did everything, making progress," Redick said.

The Lakers' only loss over their last 11 games came against the Jazz on Wednesday, 109-97, snapping their nine-game winning streak.
