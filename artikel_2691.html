One offensive positive from Saints loss to Bears

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The New Orleans Saints didn't leave Soldier Field with the result they wanted against the Chicago Bears on Sunday, but there was at least one encouraging takeaway from Sunday's loss.

Despite another disappointing outcome, the Saints found success on third down against Dennis Allen's defense that had made it one of the most difficult situations for opposing offenses all season.

New Orleans converted eight of 17 third-down attempts, finishing with a 47% conversion rate. That was the highest third-down percentage Chicago had allowed in a single game all season.

Heading into Sunday's matchup, the Bears were allowing opponents to convert just 22.8% of their third downs, the best mark in the NFL. The Saints more than doubled that percentage, finding ways to extend drives and keep their offense on the field against a defense that had consistently held their own in that department.

For an offense that has struggled to find consistency, that kind of production is worth acknowledging and perhaps can lead to bigger developments.

Obviously, converting third downs didn't translate into the outcome the Saints needed. A strong performance in one area can't make up for the other mistakes that ultimately contributed to the loss.

That said, there is still something to build on from the performance.

The Saints have more than plenty of issues to address moving forward, but there were certainly still some positives to takeaway from the loss. Now, the challenge is turning that production into more consistent offensive performances and, ultimately, wins.
