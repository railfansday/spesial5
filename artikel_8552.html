Choose an Avant personal loan if your credit is fair and you need the money by the next business day. Its administration fee of up to 9.99% comes out of the loan, so compare its annual percentage rate (APR), which counts the fee, with a credit union’s before you accept.

On an $8,000, three-year loan priced like the sample loan Avant publishes, a 9.99% fee and a 29.95% APR, $7,201 reaches you and the payments total $10,998, while a no-fee loan with the same payments would deliver all $8,000. In return, Avant is open to fair credit, with most of its borrowers scoring 600 to 700, and can fund a loan by the next business day.

Avant is worth it for fair-credit borrowers who need money by the next business day and haven’t been offered a lower APR. Borrowers with good credit should shop lenders that charge no fee, and anyone a federal credit union will lend to should start there, since the National Credit Union Administration caps those loans at 18%.
