Use GasBuddy for its free, driver-reported gas prices, and add its Pay with GasBuddy+ card only if you pay for gas by debit. The card’s base discount is 3 cents a gallon, while a credit card paying 3% back on gas earns 12 cents on a $4 gallon, and you can’t use both on one fill-up.

That makes GasBuddy’s free app and website the part most drivers need, since they show the gas prices other drivers report so you can compare nearby stations before you fill up.

Debit payers gain at least 3 cents a gallon from the free card, and by our math Plus’s extra 17 cents a gallon over the free card covers its $89 yearly fee at about 44 gallons a month. Credit card payers should use the free map to pick the station and pay with a gas credit card that earns 3% or more.
