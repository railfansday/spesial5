Feid and Snoop Dogg Drop Thanksgiving Collaboration ‘Gracias x Todo’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The two stars previewed the song at the 2026 Latin Grammy Awards.

Feid and Snoop Dogg are spreading a message of gratitude this Thanksgiving.

On Thursday (Nov. 26), the Latin music star and hip-hop icon released their new collaboration “Gracias x Todo.”

“Happy thanksgiving for you all!!” Feid wrote on Instagram alongside the song’s music video.

The new single celebrates “gratitude, perseverance, and the importance of appreciating life’s journey,” according to a press release.

In the song, Feid and Snoop Dogg trade verses about life’s ups and downs, reflecting on how the toughest moments can lead to something better. Directed by Diego Patiño, the video pays tribute to the people and experiences that have shaped Feid’s life, including his family. The Fundación Good Kidz children’s choir also joins Feid toward the end of the clip.

Feid first previewed “Gracias x Todo” at the 2026 Latin Grammy Awards on Nov. 12, where Snoop Dogg made a surprise appearance alongside Divino, Lil Supa, Baby Ranks and DJ Premier.

During the ceremony, Feid won best reggaeton performance for “Verano Rosa,” his collaboration with Karol G.

The Colombian star has plenty more on his schedule. On Saturday (Nov. 28), he is set to participate in the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade, bringing Latin music to the centennial celebration.

Early next year, Feid will take his Ferxxo experience to arenas across North America with the Ferxxo – The Green Star Tour 2027. The Live Nation-promoted tour launches Feb. 5 at Orlando’s Kia Center, with stops in major cities including Miami, New York, Toronto, Chicago, Dallas and Los Angeles before heading to Mexico. The tour is scheduled to wrap up in Mexico City in late May.
