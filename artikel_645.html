Trump Posts Bizarre AI Meme of Jensen Huang, Holding Up a Bra and Riffing Off President’s 2024 Assassination Attempt, After Nvidia Board Reinstates CEO

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

After a head-spinning reversal by Nvidia’s board to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO, following revelations of the executive’s multiple affairs including one with a reported Chinese spy, President Donald Trump is exulting in the decision — and Trump posted a truly weird image to celebrate the turn of events.

On Monday, Trump shared an AI-generated image of Huang that echoed photos of Trump following an assassination attempt at his campaign rally in Butler, Pennsylvania, in July 2024. Related Stories Pink Announces 2027 Sphere Residency in Las Vegas

In the meme, Huang is triumphantly clutching a red bra with an Nvidia flag positioned in the background, as the executive is surrounded by Secret Service agents. In the image Trump shared, Huang has blood spattered on his face (as Trump appeared to also have after the Butler shooting).

The image Trump shared seems to imply that Huang was targeted for his sexual indiscretions in an attempt to figuratively “assassinate” his career — but that, like Trump, Huang has emerged bloodied but victorious.

“Congratulations to NVIDIA on having made, what I consider to be, an absolutely GREAT decision!” Trump wrote earlier on Monday, prior to the president posting the AI-generated image of Huang.

Trump had called for Huang’s reinstatement at Nvidia, including in a Truth Social post on Sunday night. Earlier Monday, Trump wrote on his social media platform that Huang would “ensure the Success and Prosperity of not only the Company, but the American Economy” as Nvidia’s CEO and that U.S. Attorney General Todd Blanche has confirmed the government did not possess evidence that Huang committed any crimes.

Nvidia’s board, in its statement before the market opened Nov. 23, said it had “voted unanimously to reinstate Mr. Huang following a series of discussions and meetings with Mr. Huang, after which the Board was satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang’s candor and ability to lead Nvidia.”

The statement from the AI chip giant’s board alluded to Trump’s intervention in the matter, saying: “In the process of reaching its decision, the Board received input from senior levels of the United States government, and based on that input, the Board is confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk.”
