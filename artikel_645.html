JO1’s ‘IGNITE’ Tops Japan Hot 100 as Hiromi Ohta’s 1975 Hit ‘Momen no Handkerchief’ Debuts

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Plus: BUDDiiS' "Mugendai LOVE!!!" jumps 48-2.

JO1‘s “IGNITE” soars to No. 1 on the Billboard Japan Hot 100 chart released Nov. 25.

The title track of the group’s 11th single “AAO,” released Nov. 18, serves as the opener for the TV anime Tokyo Revengers War of the Three Titans Arc. The song launched with 678,171 copies to lead sales while placing high across other metrics, including No. 2 for downloads and No. 6 for radio airplay. The song’s total points grew about 7.7-fold from the previous week.

BUDDiiS’ “Mugendai LOVE!!!” jumps 48-2. Also released Nov. 18, the track sold 251,610 copies to place at No. 2 for sales, with total points up about 6.2-fold week over week. EBiDAN’s “Yes! Tokyo” comes in at No. 3, leading video views and placing at No. 2 for streaming. Ayumu Imazu’s “Musubi” holds steady at No. 4, topping streaming and placing at No. 9 for downloads. Boku ga Mitakatta Aozora debuts at No. 5 with “Boku wa Mikata da.” The group’s ninth single, released Nov. 18, sold 94,282 copies for No. 3 in sales and placed at No. 4 for radio. Keisuke Kuwata’s “Kamigami no Tasuki” opens at No. 7. The theme song for the Nippon TV drama Oretachi no Hakone Ekiden arrived on digital platforms Nov. 16, topping both radio and downloads. The veteran singer-songwriter’s hit from 2007, “Ashita Hareru kana,” also re-enters the chart at No. 95.

Vaundy’s “Shiwaawase” lands at No. 6 with No. 3 for streaming. ILLIT places two songs back to back, with “You’re Under Arrest” at No. 8 and “It’s Me” at No. 9. The latter climbs from No. 10, placing No. 6 for streaming and No. 5 for video, with total points up 12.2% from the previous week. M!LK’s “That’s! Chojo” rounds out the top 10 with No. 4 for video. Just outside the top 10, M!LK’s “Bakuretsu Aishiteru” rises 13-11, coming in at No. 3 for video. INI’s “Present” moves up a notch to No. 13 with No. 5 for streaming.

FRUITS ZIPPER’s “Power of Love” surges 64-16. The track comes in at No. 4 for sales (37,289 copies), and total points grew 155.1% week over week. Lienel’s “Ai no Dabada” climbs 61-29 with No. 8 for sales (12,882 copies) and total points up 64.7%.

BESTie’s “Love Options” rises 43-35, with streams and downloads up about 20% from the previous week. Fujii Kaze, who held his Fujii Kaze Piano Recital on Nov. 20 and 21 and streamed it on YouTube, saw “Garden” climb 94-80 and “Hana” return to the chart for the first time in about a year. Hiromi Ohta’s “Momen no Handkerchief,” a vintage hit from 1975, debuts at No. 93. After reports of her passing on Nov. 12, the song soared to No. 24 for downloads, No. 11 for radio and No. 45 for karaoke.

The Billboard Japan Hot 100 combines physical and digital sales, audio streams, radio airplay, video views and karaoke data.
