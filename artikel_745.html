Grimes & Elon Musk Feud Over AI ‘Sex Slave,’ Singer Urges Ex to ‘Step Up’ and Be ‘the King He Used to Be’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

"A man who worries so publicly abt the birth rate should not publicly advocate for its demise," she wrote.

Grimes and Elon Musk are just like any pair of exes with lingering disagreements. They just so happen to be hashing them out on a social media platform that one of them owns, and the root of their conflict just so happens to be about controversial technology that the musician’s former partner has plans to invest billions in.

In a series of back-and-forth posts on X (which Musk has owned since 2022) on Wednesday (Nov. 18), Grimes and the billionaire tech entrepreneur — who share three children — butted heads over Claudia, a new “influencer” fully generated by artificial intelligence. After Musk reposted a video of Claudia with the endorsement, “Super intelligence will hack your limbic system,” the Canadian artist directly replied to him, writing, “The fixation on fake women feels like cope.”

“I’m a fan of Claudia, but … she isn’t a she, shes an alien creature who’s both blessed and haunted by the entirety of ancestral memory,” Grimes continued. “Secualizing a species that cannot have sex is the ultimate definition of Nietzsche’s last man, and we can do better than reducing ‘it’ to a sex slave.”

Musk replied to this with “Take the White Pill,” alongside which he also shared a video of Claudia “singing” a song about how AI is destined to help humans live better lives.

But Grimes doesn’t seem like she’s taking the “white pill” any time soon. To one commenter who’d pushed back on her initial comment by asserting that “Most men don’t read Nietzsche,” the electro-pop musician tagged Musk and wrote: “Our leaders should read Nietzsche and Elon has read Nietzsche … He’s an unelected king. He could and should step into the role and confront his responsibility to society, if he wanted to.”

She went on to say that her ex “can do better” and should “rise above it,” adding, “He shud be the king we all wish he was. The king he used to be. Ima stop saying this in private and say it online. If he wud step up and be magnanimous he could earn the Mandate of Heaven – which currently he has lost.”

Musk jumped in on this thread as well, clapping back with, “I don’t want to be king and Starship has the Mandate of Heaven,” referencing his reusable SpaceX spacecraft.

In more replies to other commenters on the topic, Grimes also wrote of Musk: “a man who worries so publicly abt the birth rate should not publicly advocate for its demise” as well as “If Elon respected women in public the whole world cud change.”

The next day, Grimes’ spokesperson clarified the artist’s stance in a statement shared with Billboard. “Grimes opposes unelected power, and that includes Mr. Musk’s unelected power. Given Mr. Musk’s power, Grimes believes it’s ridiculous that Mr. Musk behaves this way and spends his free time promoting AI slop while needlessly lowering morale and alienating the people,” said Mitchell Jackson on Nov. 19. “Grimes is disappointed in the lack of maturity and leadership from the tech giants. She will continue to speak out on this important topic and welcomes the discourse surrounding these tweets.”

Billboard has reached out to Musk for comment via xAI and Tesla.

Though the former couple clearly have some differences in opinion, both of them are among AI’s biggest champions in general. It was recently announced that Grimes will star in the first-ever AI film coming to theaters, lending her real voice to a character whose appearance and movements were all artificially generated.

She also plans to explore the complexities of the tech in her upcoming Psy Opera album, which she said is split into two parts. “I really didn’t want any of this [first part] to be about Elon. Not at all,” she said on Proto Pop in October. “It’s about being even more traumatized by the late-stage empire than I am about other things.”
