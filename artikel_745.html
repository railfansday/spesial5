From LISA of BLACKPINK & Gracie Abrams to the ‘GTA VI’ Album, Which Music Release Is Your Favorite This Week? Vote!

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

TOMORROW X TOGETHER, Tems and Zach Top also dropped fresh projects.

The last New Music Friday before Thanksgiving is full of treats.

Thirteen years after Grand Theft Auto V took over the gaming world, Rockstar Games has unleashed the sixth iteration of the wildly popular video game. To celebrate, Atlantic Records oversaw Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, a sprawling compilation album that brings the video game’s high-octane, Miami-set world to life across hip-hop, pop, country, alternative, dance, Latin, Afrobeats and more. While Cardi B, Morgan Wallen and Travis Scott headlined the compilation soundtrack with key pre-release singles, the full 34-track set also features contributions from SZA, Dua Lipa, Zara Larsson, Lana Del Rey, Robert Glasper, Camila Cabello, Omar Apollo, JENNIE, Fcukers, Burna Boy, Rauw Alejandro, Gunna, Young Thug, JT and Justice.

Gracie Abrams released the deluxe version of her latest album, Daughter From Hell, which includes the new single, “Here You Come, There You Go”; South Korean boy band TOMORROW X TOGETHER shared its ninth mini album, Perfect Storm; and Rema dropped E-Mortal Boy, a new EP bridging African and Caribbean music.

This week, we also got new projects from Toro y Moi (Unusua), NEEDTOBREATHE (The Long Surrender and The Fellow Passengers of Pain), YFN Lucci (Everything I Said I Am. / Everything She Said She Is.), Phoebe Bridgers (Anything), Conway the Machine (Otis), Fergie Baby (God Is From Harlem), Mez (Electric Future Gangster) and Take That (Dreamers).

LISA of BLACKPINK leads the way on the singles side with “Leave With Me,” the official end-credits song for her new YouTube Premium movie Always Lalisa. Notably, the song coincides with the kick-off of VIVA LA LISA, the first Vegas residency from a K-pop artist.

Tems shared “African Love Song,” the first single from the forthcoming Children of Blood and Bone soundtrack, which she executive produced; Myke Towers and Rauw Alejandro joined forces for “Tremendo Desastre,” and three-time Grammy-winning indie rocker Julien Baker dropped “Hound,” the first taste of their first new solo album in six years, This World or the Next, out next February.

This week, we also received new songs from beebadoobee (“No Punctuation”), MJ Nebreda (“P—y Pop”), Zach Top (“Moonshine”), Kane Brown (“Losing Hand”), Lainey Wilson (“Just A Woman”), ENHYPEN (“I Want You”), JISOO (“Sun Showers — from The Angry Birds Movie 3”), Chuckyy (“Don’t Get Caught Up”), Saweetie (“Details”), Chloe x Halle (“All I Think About on Christmas”), Gabriel Jacoby (“Be Alright”), GIVĒON (“Fast Asleep”), Paris Jackson (“Albatross”), Erika de Casier (“Whispers”) and AKIA (“Falling Star”).
