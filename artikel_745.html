DWTS' Brandon Armstrong Shows Off Cut From Ezra Frech’s Prosthetic Leg

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Dancing With the Stars pro Brandon Armstrong revealed his calf was cut after Ezra Frech’s prosthetic leg hit him during the track star’s performance on the Nov. 24  finale: “It split me.”

Brandon Armstrong was prepared to waltz into the fire for Ezra Frech.

The Dancing With the Stars pro showed off a cut on his calf where the track star’s prosthetic leg hit him during Ezra and partner Daniella Karagach’s freestyle performance on the season 35 finale Nov. 24.

After Brandon’s wife Brylee Armstrong said that “people are wondering what happened in Ezra's freestyle with his leg falling off, and if Brandon's okay” in a Nov. 25 TikTok video after fans noticed the dance pro was hit with the prosthetic during the performance, she revealed that “Brandon's here to give you guys a lowdown.”

“First of all, fine,” he said. “Just happy that he's fine. When he jumped off, his leg was not on properly or all the way, so I took a freaking shot.”

Brylee then zoomed into a fresh cut that stretched across the dancer’s calf.

“It's actually not too bad,” he said. “It split me, but it's not too bad.”

And while Brandon may have sustained an injury from the unforgettable performance, he was more than happy to take one for the team.

“I'm just happy that he is fine,” he said of Ezra. “That dance was unbelievable. Leg didn't get on right, but it got on right the second time, and he killed it. So everything's good. We're all good.”

Brylee added, “Our King Ezra, we love him.”

But the 32-year-old wasn’t the only one on the dance floor to shimmy away with a few war wounds. After all, Ezra raised eyebrows when fans spotted blood on his face after his freestyle performance—but it turns out he’d gotten scratched from one of Daniella’s costumes earlier in the show.

“I’m so freaking sorry,” Daniella told Ezra in her Nov. 24 Instagram Story. “Are you OK though?”

The 21-year-old assured her that he was indeed “fine,” joking that the cuts along his face and his ear were just “battle scars.”

After their performance, the Paralympian shed more light on what led to his prosthetic leg coming off during the performance.

“The leg just wasn't on correctly, and I felt it,” he told Variety following the episode. “Sometimes it'll readjust and I'll be okay. And so, as I was running up the steps, I'm like, ‘There's a 50 percent chance my leg will resuction and I'll be okay.’ And that wasn't the case.”

But Ezra wasn’t about to let the blunder stop him. In fact, he seamlessly reattached the prosthetic during the performance with the help of Daniella and dance pro Alan Bersten.

“She's drilled in me since the beginning that no matter what happens, we keep pushing through,” he said of his dance partner. “You never give up, and you fight for it. And in this case, regardless of the circumstance, I was gonna do whatever I can to fight for it.”

And it was that fighting spirit that ultimately led to him and Daniella being crowned as the season’s winners

"This is the most special moment of my life," he told the audience after their win. "Thank you, everybody, for voting. Thank you for believing in us. Oh my God."
