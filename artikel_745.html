‘Spider-Man: Brand New Day’ Set for December Rerelease With New Scenes

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Rosario Dawson's Night Nurse is among the highlights of the previously unseen footage for the extended cut, titled 'Spider-Man: [Brand New] Brand New Day.'

After a record-breaking summer, Sony Pictures is looking to soar even higher with its theatrical rerelease for Spider-Man: Brand New Day.

The studio announced Monday that the movie’s extended cut will appropriately be titled Spider-Man: [Brand New] Brand New Day and is set to return exclusively to theaters on Friday, Dec. 4. The new footage will run 10 minutes in total and feature previously unseen scenes, including one with Rosario Dawson playing the Night Nurse. The rerelease will also include a couple of other surprises that have not yet been disclosed. Tom Holland made his big-screen return as Peter Parker in filmmaker Destin Daniel Cretton‘s Spider-Man: Brand New Day, which was an absolute sensation upon hitting theaters July 31. It currently stands as the highest-grossing title ever at the domestic box office with $959 million, meaning that a rerelease could help push it beyond the billion-dollar mark for North America. Also starring Zendaya, Jon Bernthal, Mark Ruffalo and Sadie Sink, the movie ranks No. 3 on the all-time worldwide chart with $2.5 billion.

Also set for theatrical release on Dec. 4 are Universal’s David Harbour-led sequel Violent Night 2, the Pedro Pascal vehicle Behemoth! and Lionsgate’s Idris Elba-starring action film Above the Below. Spider-Man: Brand New Day hails from producers Marvel Studios and Amy Pascal.

The Hollywood Reporter reported back in late September that Brand New Day would be returning to theaters with previously unseen footage. That news broke days after Marvel Studios’ Avengers Endgame: Encore topped the weekend’s domestic box office, with the rerelease of the 2019 box office smash grossing $26 million to prevail over a slew of newcomers. Endgame’s rerelease was timed to drum up additional excitement for Disney’s Avengers: Doomsday, hitting theaters Dec. 18.

During a fan event in July, Dawson told the crowd that she had filmed a scene for Brand New Day as Claire Temple — the Marvel character she previously portrayed on the series Daredevil, Jessica Jones, Luke Cage, Iron Fist and The Defenders — but that it landed on the cutting room floor.

“My character is somewhere in that same universe, and I really love that,” the actress said at the time about her character, who is a version of the Night Nurse. “I hope I get to be Claire again.”

While speaking with THR shortly after the release of Brand New Day, Sony film chief Tom Rothman addressed the challenge of following the studio’s previous Spidey movie, 2021’s Spider-Man: No Way Home, which featured the high-profile returns of previous Peter Parkers in Tobey Maguire and Andrew Garfield.

“The strategic decision was — rather than trying what we all thought would be perhaps an empty effort of going quote, unquote bigger — we just went deeper,” Rothman explained. “That was a very conscious choice to focus on how we could make the audience feel.”
