Kourtney Kardashian Addresses Travis Barker Breakup Rumor

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Kourtney Kardashian set the record straight about her marriage to husband Travis Barker after she was asked to comment on a rumor that they "don’t live together anymore."

Kourtney Kardashian is keeping chatter at bay.

The Kardashians star called out "fabricated stories" about her marriage to husband Travis Barker, denying there's trouble in paradise after her publicist asked her to comment on breakup speculation.

"She is like, ‘The magazine is saying that you and Travis don’t live together anymore and you are splitting up,'" Kourtney shared on the Nov. 5 episode of her Hulu series. "I go, ‘Tell them to print it.’ Just see what happens. They know it is not true."

In fact, the 47-year-old believes the rumor was started by some "dumb" TikTok video.

"And people believe it!" she bemoaned. "It's so bizarre."

And the breakup rumor is far from the truth. After all, Kourtney and Travis—who share 3-year-old son Rocky Barker—have been renovating their home so that there's enough room for their family, which also includes kids Mason Disick, 16, Penelope Disick, 14, Reign Disick, 11, with her ex Scott Disick as well as Atiana de la Hoya, 27, Landon Barker, 23, and Alabama Barker, 20, from the Blink-182 drummer's past marriage to Shanna Moakler.

"My goal in renovating my house and all the changes we're making is so that we can all fit," the Lemme founder explained last year on The Kardashians, "and have it feel like a new space that we're all moving back into together, that feels like new energy and new vibes."

"I feel like blending, in the beginning, is a lot of adjusting for each person," she continued. "Us in their space and then us being out of our space and into a new space."

And though Kourtney admittedly feels "vulnerable" being temporarily displaced from her home due to construction, she's pooshing for new family memories.

"I can't wait to cook, have movie nights, barbecues," Kourtney raved while touring the home on the Nov. 5 episode. "I want to make it feel magical for everyone."
