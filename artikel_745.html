‘Avatar: Seven Havens’ Sets Season 2 Release Date at Paramount+

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Season 3 of the series will also launch in 2027

“Avatar: Seven Havens” has set its Season 2 premiere date, Variety has learned.

The second season, or Book 2 in the “Avatar” universe parlance, will debut on Paramount+ on May 21, 2027 with its first three episodes. Episodes 4-6 will then air on May 28, eps. 7-9 on June 4, and eps. 10-13 on June 11.

Furthermore, Book 3 will also debut in 2027 on a date to be announced later. As previously reported, the show has already been renewed through Book 4, which will bring the show to a conclusion. While Book 1 was titled “Survival,” Book 2 has been dubbed “Discovery.” The show is set over 200 years after the life of Avatar Korra, with the world having been devastated by an apocalyptic event dubbed The Cataclysm that Korra is blamed for causing.

The logline for the second installment states, “Pavi continues her chakra journey to the Floating Isles and learns more about what happened to Korra. Meanwhile, Nisha discovers vital information about the mysterious spirit energy Pavi released from Ellora. The girls are brought together in New Ba Sing Se, along with all their unresolved issues.”

The series stars Saheli Khan as Pavi, Aishu Devan as Nisha, Akshay Khanna as Karthik, Major Curda as Jae, Sakina Jaffrey as Agam, Darren Barnet as Daemin, and Dee Bradley Baker as Geet and Ruhi.

“Avatar” masterminds Michael Dante DiMartino and Bryan Konietzko are co-creators and executive producers for “Avatar: Seven Havens.” Ethan Spaulding is executive producer and Sehaj Sethi is co-executive producer. The series is produced by Avatar Studios and Nickelodeon Animation Studios.

Book 1 of “Seven Havens” debuted on Paramount+ in October, nearly 12 years since the conclusion of the last series in the franchise, “Legend of Korra.” “Seven Havens” received rave reviews upon its release, with the series holding a perfect 100% critical approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes.

In her review for Variety, Alison Herman wrote, “Combined with the imagery and an expanded pool of references, ‘Seven Havens’ successfully makes clear that it’s a new and different kind of Avatar series, while maintaining the transportive world-building that’s helped the franchise endure.”
