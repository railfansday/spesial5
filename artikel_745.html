Ella Langley Is Already a Winner at 2026 CMA Awards; Tim McGraw Sets a Record in Musical Event Category

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The awards were announced live on ABC's Good Morning America on Wednesday (Nov. 18).

Ella Langley’s expected sweep of the 2026 CMA Awards began early, with the announcement of her win for music video of the year for her megahit “Choosin’ Texas.” And Tim McGraw made CMA history, becoming the first five-time winner of musical event of the year for “McArthur,” a collab with HARDY, Eric Church and Morgan Wallen.

The awards were announced live on ABC’s Good Morning America on Wednesday (Nov. 18), with Lara Spencer sharing the news from Honky Tonk Central in downtown Nashville. GMA also took a look inside Bridgestone Arena with Lainey Wilson, who is hosting the show for the third year in a row.

Langley won music video of the year as both artist and director. She shares the award with co-directors Caylee Robillard and Wales Toney. Langley also won in this category last year (again as both artist and co-director) for “You Look Like You Love Me,” her sexy duet with Riley Green.

HARDY, Church, Wallen and McGraw share the award for musical event of the year with producer Jay Joyce. McGraw’s previous wins in the category are for “It’s Your Love” (with Faith Hill); “Find Out Who Your Friends Are (with Tracy Lawrence and Kenny Chesney); “Feel Like a Rock Star” (with Chesney); and “Highway Don’t Care” (with Taylor Swift and Keith Urban).

McGraw’s fifth win in the category allows him to break out of a tie with Brad Paisley, Urban, Chesney and Vince Gill, who have won four times each.

The 60th Annual CMA Awards is a production of the Country Music Association. Robert Deaton serves as executive producer, Alan Carter is director, and Jon Macks is head writer. The awards show will take place in Bridgestone Arena in Nashville on Wednesday night.
