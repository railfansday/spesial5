Instagram launches dedicated ‘Direct’ messaging app that basically no one can use yet

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Meta’s Instagram is launching a new “Direct” app that’s dedicated to direct messages, but it’s exceptionally limited at launch.

Available now on Android and iOS, “Direct” is a spinoff of Instagram’s direct messages. Instagram explains that the app is for “chatting one-on-one or in groups with friends and family.” It uses the same framework as normal Instagram DMs, but is in its own app and has a few features not found in the Instagram app including:

Meta says that “Direct” is available first in Türkiye and Thailand, and it doesn’t actually require an Instagram account. You can sign up with a phone number or email address, though this limits you to only being able to accept messages. Your contacts need to start the conversation with you, rather than allowing you to start conversations with others. Meta adds that “only your contacts and people you’re already connected to can start a chat with you or add you to a group on Direct,” and all users must be at least 13 years or older.
