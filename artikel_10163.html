Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold 9 lineup looks like it’ll include a new TriFold, leak suggests

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Following its short-lived Galaxy Z TriFold, Samsung appears to have a sequel on the way that’s set to arrive in the Galaxy Z Fold 9 lineup.

The Galaxy Z TriFold launched a few months after the Galaxy Z Fold 7, carrying the codename “Q7m.” GalaxyClub now reports that “Q9m” has surfaced, implying that Galaxy Z TriFold 2 will be based on the Galaxy Z Fold 9 series.

For one, don’t expect a new TriFold to arrive in the same schedule as the first one. The original Galaxy Z TriFold launched in late 2025 before expanding to other regions in early 2026. While it’s abundantly clear we’re not getting a new model before the year lets out, this codename effectively rules out a release before the Galaxy Z Fold 9 series launches which, assuming it follows the usual schedule, won’t happen until around July.

This also tells us that the Galaxy Z TriFold 2 will be based on the Galaxy Z Fold 9 series, likely including the latest chipset and Samsung’s Android 18 build.

There’s no guarantee yet about when the next TriFold might debut, how much it might cost, or where it might launch. GalaxyClub notes that the codename subtly suggests there’s no EU release in the plans, but it can’t be completely ruled out either.

Whatever happens with Galaxy Z TriFold 2, let’s just hope it lasts longer than the original.
