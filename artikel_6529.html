Costco Is Now Selling A Peanut Butter Instant Ramen From Jif

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The instant ramen aisle now features every flavor under the sun, from tonkotsu to carbonara. But another contender has entered the arena, and it might just be the wildest yet: peanut butter.

Costco is now carrying a six-pack of stir-fry-style noodles from Jif. Yes, you read that right. The peanut butter brand's instant ramen combines traditional noodles with a nutty, savory seasoning packet. Along with ingredients like soy sauce, garlic, and artificial beef flavor, the seasoning contains peanut flour.

Unlike your typical instant ramen, Jif's version uses less water to create more of a sauce than a broth. While peanut noodles are nothing new, seeing Jif's name on a ramen cup is certainly unexpected—and Costco shoppers can't decide whether the idea is unhinged or brilliant.

"Is this a joke? Is this AI? Tell me it's either one, PLEASE," one person wrote on Instagram. Another compared the product to "prison food," while someone else simply said, "Yuck."

But plenty of others can see the potential. "Anyone who thinks this sounds gross has never had Thai food, and it shows," one Instagram user wrote. "Peanut butter noodles are kinda awesome." Others admitted they were already adding peanut butter to their instant ramen, so this version simply skips the extra step.

Some shoppers were already brainstorming ways to make the noodles even better. Suggestions included scallions, chili oil, extra soy sauce, and even an egg to take things up a notch.

So far, the noodles have only been spotted at select Costco warehouses. But if you don't want to wait for your local store to stock them, shoppers have also reported finding Jif ramen at retailers like Dollar Tree and Walmart.
