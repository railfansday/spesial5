Northwood Temple pulls away late to defeat Ascend Leadership, 54-30

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Northwood Temple Academy used a strong fourth quarter to pull away from Ascend Leadership Academy for a 54-30 victory Thursday night in the Aviators' girls basketball season opener.

The Eagles jumped out to an early 6-2 lead, but the Aviators responded with aggressive play on both ends of the court to cut the deficit to 12-8 at the end of the first quarter.

Northwood Temple began to find its offensive rhythm in the second quarter, outscoring Ascend 12-6 to take a 24-14 lead into halftime.

The Aviators came out strong in the second half, using several scoring runs to keep pace with the Eagles. Ascend outscored Northwood Temple 11-9 in the third quarter, trimming the deficit to 33-25 entering the final period.

However, the Eagles took control in the fourth quarter, outscoring the Aviators 21-5 to seal the victory.

Ascend head coach Donna Taylor said injuries have made it difficult for the team to practice together and develop chemistry early in the season.

"We came in this season, we had several girls that we've been dealing with injuries and so we honestly didn't know who was gonna be able to play tonight, but they pushed through the injuries," Taylor said. "We just gotta find a way to get into the gym, get the whole team together and find a way to connect, and I believe we'll do better next week."

Judah Sibley led the Aviators with 17 points, while Claire Marshall added five. Harmony McLaughlin and Walker Alajah scored four points each.

Despite the loss, Taylor saw encouraging moments from her team but emphasized the need for greater consistency and attention to detail.

"At times we ran our stuff and we looked good. We just gotta focus on the details, we can't get sped up, we gotta play with pace," Taylor said. "So it's the little things that we just gotta work on."

Ascend Leadership will return to action Thursday when the Aviators travel to face Lee Christian at 5:45 p.m.
