Could Anthropic’s IPO Reverse the Market’s Sentiment on AI?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The AI trade is viewed bearishly right now.

Anthropic is going public on Nov. 19, and the timing couldn't be worse. The artificial intelligence (AI) world has been shaken over the past week by Nvidia (NVDA -4.12%) CEO Jensen Huang stepping down from his role and from the board of directors. Nvidia was an investor in Anthropic, though it played a smaller role than other financiers.

Even before this occurred, the AI world was viewed from a bearish perspective, as the market grew skeptical about the payoff of all this AI spending. Anthropic will be the first glimpse into what's possible, and I think investors should be fired up about what it brings to the table.

But can Anthropic's IPO reverse market sentiment around AI? Let's take a look.

Anthropic is a bit different from the AI hyperscalers that the market is so concerned about. There are question marks around companies like Alphabet (GOOG -3.85%) (GOOGL -4.07%)and Amazon (AMZN -3.34%) logging roughly $200 billion in capital expenditures this year due to heavy AI data center spending, and about what the payoff will be. Their current spending is aimed at securing market share in the future, as they expect AI usage to rise and thus need more computing power.

One way this computing power gets used is via Anthropic. Anthropic doesn't own any of its own data centers or computing chips. Instead, it rents them from cloud providers such as Alphabet's Google Cloud or Amazon Web Services (AWS). This keeps Anthropic streamlined, and really all investors need to worry about is AI usage. The more AI is used, the more tokens or subscriptions are purchased through Anthropic. Anthropic has committed to buy hundreds of billions of dollars of computing power over the coming years, so its revenue has to keep growing fast enough to cover that bill.

Right now, Anthropic is negative on the bottom line. However, it may not stay that way for long.

As a business grows, some costs remain fixed. So, the more revenue a company can generate, the closer it gets to profitability. That's the idea behind Anthropic's stock right now, as the company posted a nearly $700 million operating loss over the first six months of 2026 while growing revenue at a 1,305% pace to $16.3 billion. So, I wouldn't be surprised to see Anthropic flip the profitability switch in the near future.

That would be huge news for the AI market as a whole, showing there is serious money to be made from AI. Google Cloud and AWS have already shown their revenue is skyrocketing.

But there is still one outstanding problem.

Now that Huang is no longer CEO at Nvidia, there are still plenty of questions remaining about how Nvidia will perform. Nvidia led the AI investment sentiment for a long time, and has delivered incredible growth over that time frame.

While some parts of its business may be shifting, many of Nvidia’s arrangements to supply chips to hyperscalers will remain in place. Nvidia's Q3 conference call on Nov. 17 will clarify the situation, which could move the markets significantly in either direction.

By the end of this week, you will know a lot more about how the market is viewing AI. I expect good news from Nvidia on its Nov. 17 call, as none of the events over the past few weeks have affected its Q3 results. Furthermore, Nvidia already reported strong demand for next year, and that likely won't shift. Anthropic could have a well-received IPO, and by the end of the week, AI could be the market's top theme. Just stay patient and see, but I think the AI trade could be back in a positive light as early as this week.
