Tanggal: 2026-11-21

I don't use funds, I use ETFs. I feel it is a lot easier than funds, can be less expensive, and now that Schwab supports purchasing fractional shares there really isn't any reason not to do so in my opinion.

You could sell both funds and buy something like VTI. It would be an appropriate tax loss partner to SWTSX. It would also be a good partner for SWPPX. However, if you wanted something a little closer to an S&P500 fund, you might consider SCHK (a Schwab 1000 ETF) or VONE (A Vanguard Russell 1000 ETF). I like SCHK better for its lower ER and lower NAV price.

I believe that any account that is older than about 2 years defaulted to average cost. Schwab may allow you to change from average cost to another method for any shares that you purchased less than a year ago IF you haven't sold any shares during that time. You would need to call Schwab and ask them.

Otherwise, you could sell your entire positions in SWTSX and SWPPX and purchase an appropriate TLH partner or partners. I am OK with using funds in my retirement accounts, but not in my taxable.

An ACATS transfer would still result in the shares having their average cost I believe. However, I've never done it with a fund. ETFs are transferred with the spec-id information.
