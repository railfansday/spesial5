Yes, controlling income to get ACA subsidies is a perfectly plausible outcome. The big issue in controlling income for long periods of time is how to maintain sufficient cash flow to avoid pinching your lifestyle just to get a subsidy. It is helpful to have established the Roth account early and made some Roth Contributions. See the wiki on the various clocks that run that the IRS uses to test your ability to get to Roth money early:
https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/Roth_IRA#Distributions

From the perspective of risk management in retirement planning, I would make sure I could afford health insurance without a subsidy.

Another thing you can do to minimize the eventual need for Roth Conversions is try to slow the growth of your tax deferred accounts by placing funds tax efficiently, see the wiki:
https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/Tax-eff ... _placement
