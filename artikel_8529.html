Get HBO Max if you follow HBO’s series or want new Warner Bros. films about 11 weeks after they open in theaters, and pay for it only in the months you’re watching. At $10.99 to $22.99 a month, it costs too much to keep between seasons, and canceling still lets you watch until the period you paid for ends.

HBO Max carries HBO’s series and films, Warner Bros. and DC movies, Cartoon Network and Looney Tunes, and reality shows from HGTV, Food Network, TLC, and Discovery.

Its owner, Skydance (Paramount, renamed after buying Warner Bros. Discovery in October 2026), plans to bundle HBO Max with Paramount+ in early 2027 and later merge the two, but subscriptions, prices, and the app haven’t changed yet.

A yearly plan pays off only for viewers who’d keep HBO Max 11 months or more, since it costs about as much as 10 monthly payments. Everyone else should pay monthly while a show they follow airs, choose Basic with Ads unless the ads bother them, and rotate their streaming services between seasons.
