An early-payment discount is an expensive way to get paid sooner. Offering 2/10 net 30, 2% off for paying in 10 days instead of 30, costs you the equivalent of about 37% a year in interest. Offer one only when you need the cash and borrowing it would cost you more.

On a $10,000 invoice, 2/10 net 30 means collecting $9,800 on day 10 instead of $10,000 on day 30. You give up $200 to get your money 20 days sooner.

As an annual interest rate, that’s $200 ÷ $9,800 × 365 ÷ 20, or about 37%. To check any other offer, divide what you give up by the cash you get early, then multiply by 365 divided by the number of days early.

In the October survey by the National Federation of Independent Business, small business owners paid an average interest rate of 8.5% on loans of a year or less. At about 37%, 2/10 net 30 costs more than four times that 8.5%, so a line of credit near that rate is the cheaper way to cover a short cash gap. The same formula works on a factoring company’s offer to buy your unpaid invoices.

Before you discount anything, send each invoice the day the work is done, which costs nothing. The discount is worth its price in a cash crunch, when a large unpaid invoice would leave you unable to make payroll and you can’t borrow the difference. With a customer who usually pays on day 50, the discount brings the money in 40 days early instead of 20, and its cost drops from about 37% a year to about 19%.

Offer it only to the customers whose early payment you need. A printing company with no credit line, short of cash in its slow quarter, offers 1/10 net 30 to just its three largest accounts. They usually pay on day 30, so the printer collects $60,000 of invoices 20 days sooner for $600, about 18% a year, and everyone else still pays in full.

Write the deadline as a date and both amounts in dollars. On a $5,000 invoice dated March 2, that’s “2% discount ($100) for payment received by March 12. Full $5,000 due April 1.” The word “received” means a check mailed on March 12 doesn’t count. Bill the $100 to any customer who takes the discount after March 12.
