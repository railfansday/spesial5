While I mentioned above that we're next to Olympic National Park, the first thing that popped into my mind when I saw your post was actually what barnaclebob said: "Yellowstone/Grand Teton/ Glacier NP's (Most famous NP's)." About 14 days would be great. We spent about a week in Glacier N.P. last summer, exploring from the west and east. Also spent a half-day relaxing on Flathead Lake by renting a Tritoon, and took a full day whitewater rafting trip on Flathead River. A Red Bus tour is a great way to enjoy the Going to the Sun Road without the pressure of navigating the narrow and winding road (though we also drove it on one day to spend more time at certain stops). Early reservations definitely needed to stay anywhere in park.
