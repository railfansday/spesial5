Three Special Football Jerseys Go On Sale To Celebrate Halo's 25th Anniversary

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

I've always had a thing for video game edition sports tees. They're breathable, durable, a bit weighted, and usually feature something subtle from a game I like on the front.

Xbox and sportswear company Dyenamik have already teamed up in recent weeks with to ship a range of gorgeous Xbox 25th Anniversary shirts. Now they're back with a new collection of Halo Anniversary tees, and they're looking very tempting.

The Halo shirts seem to be on available now if you fancy popping one in your sports bag. Individually, they'll set you back £70, with worldwide shipping available. Alternatively, you can grab all three in a Limited Edition Anniversary collection box for £175. Just be mindful that only 200 boxes were made, so you'll want to be quick!

So, let's go through them - here's a look at all three:

The legend himself. A black jersey carrying a grey halftone print of the Master Chief's helmet, scaled up across the back and wrapping around the front, with crisp white piping, a white HALO wordmark and the Master Chief's helmet just below the collar on the back.

A tribute to the olive green MJOLNIR armour that made Spartan-117 the face of Halo. Black side panels, a black hexagonal pattern cascading across the shoulders and fading down the back, and chevron-striped cuffs, with 117 running down the left sleeve and the Master Chief's helmet and the UNSC eagle on the chest.

The AI who guided the Chief across the first Halo ring. The jersey fades from bright cyan to deep navy like her holographic form, with fine horizontal lines running through it like streams of data. White piping, Cortana's data chip and the UNSC eagle finish the front, with the Halo ring emblem just below the collar on the back.

A premium branded box finished in silver foil, with space to store and display all three jerseys together, kept the way a Spartan keeps their kit. Only 200 boxes are available, made for the fans who have been finishing the fight since 2001, and this collection is the only way to get one.

What do you think? Initially, I thought the black one was my favourite, but the blue Cortana jersey is growing on me. It is ultra vibrant and a perfect tone of blue.

£70 is no joke for a shirt, but it seems to be par for the course when it comes to sportswear these days. The Xbox 25th Anniversary collection is bleddy lush too, so worth checking those out whilst you're at it!
