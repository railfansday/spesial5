Star Trek: Voyager - Across The Unknown Final DLC Ventures To Switch 2 Next Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Year of Hell is right around the corner

Daedalic Entertainment and GameXcite are wrapping up the bonus content for Star Trek: Voyager - Across The Unknown with one final wave of DLC.

The 'Year of Hell' expansion launches on Switch 2 next month on 9th December, with its own mini-campaign inspired by the Voyager two-parter of the same name.

In it, you'll be up against Captain Annorax and his civilisation-destroying time ship. You can either follow the critical path from the show and help Janeway & co. take down this baddie, or you can try something new and attempt to strike a deal with him.

This is its own storyline focused on survival gameplay, so your main game save will remain untouched for its duration.

Here's a brief rundown of the expansion's key features and a handful of screenshots:

Manage the decline. Gameplay turned on its head. Begin with advanced systems and upgrades already in place, then fight to keep essential systems operational as the Krenim take that advantage away. A year of losses and difficult sacrifices. Attacks can destroy rooms, tear entire decks from the ship, and kill crew members. With less space and fewer resources, success means seeing how many of your crew survive the twelve months. Six ships. Shifting loyalties. Face four Krenim vessels, including Annorax’s timeship. The Mawasi Cruiser and Nihydron Warship can join your forces in certain timeline branches-or turn their weapons on you in others.

Those who already own the Across the Unknown expansion pass will land the DLC automatically at launch.

We had a great time with the base game earlier this year, calling it a damn good time "for resource management and survival nuts who like a bit of '90s Trek" in our 8/10 review.

@KillerIsDead It’s a simulation/resource builder using the show as a narrative framework. Each chapter is for the most part a “season” of the show and you can navigate the star systems you are in gathering resources as you make your way to the Alpha Quadrant.

The game has a mix of playstyles: point and click navigation as you send Voyager to planets or to the next star system with randomized encounters that are text based. Inside the ship you see an active blueprint where you build and destroy rooms/manage your resources view your population and handicaps (morale or illness) and craft with a zoomed out view. You have the option to go on away missions that are text based with outcomes and choices based on the skills of your team members. Finally there is combat, where you have a semi automated encounter against enemies where you control weapon aiming, shield frequency and special attacks (granted by assigned crew members from the cast). The gameplay systems all work well together and the only real drawback is that they couldn’t get the full cast back for voice acting and some bugs (devs have been good at patching so I have no complaints).

It is a lower budget game (who knows how much the license cost) so it doesn’t look like a graphical powerhouse but if you like simulations, and the Voyager series…. it is nothing but a good time. Game can be brutal on higher difficulties (but mostly feels fair). The trailers (and screenshots on the article) show the gameplay.

I have a wide palette for gaming and am a diehard Voyager fan, so this is a game I come back to often to try different decisions, ship config, or crew. If they had gotten Kate Mulgrew to lend her voice I would have been set. But Tim Russ’ Tuvok is my second favorite character so having him was great as well. I personally feel it is a diamond in the rough type game.
