Doncaster Rovers boss Grant McCann on Lincoln City loanee James Collins raising the decibels

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Signed on an initial loan deal until January from Championship club Lincoln City, Collins' long road back to first-team duty—after sustaining a serious knee injury at his parent club almost exactly 12 months ago—meant Rovers had to be patient regarding his availability.

The debut display of the senior striker, who turns 36 on December 1, when he came off the bench midway through the second half in Saturday's draw at Yorkshire rivals Bradford City, also preceded a bit of chatter among Rovers followers.

Collins showed what he was all about, demonstrating his nous to set up Rovers' first equaliser - a brilliant overhead kick from Jordan Thomas - and leading the line in canny fashion.

Given the summer departure of Billy Sharp – a player who similarly brought leadership and nous to the table in the final third – Collins will fill a void in that regard.

The former Republic of Ireland forward has been promoted seven times in his career from the lower leagues with five different clubs – Lincoln, Derby County, Shrewsbury Town, Northampton Town and Luton Town.

There was plenty of noise in the build-up to Saturday from Collins too.

Rovers chief Grant McCann revealed: "We've just lacked a little bit of character at times. He's certainly not been quiet on the training ground.

"You can certainly hear the noise levels lift. We did a session (on Friday) on the training pitch and had an 11 v 11 game and Collo was in the team that we were preparing against and he scored against us. And we were like: 'here we go'.

"I could feel the group going a wee bit quiet when we conceded the first goal (at Bradford) and Collo just picked them up and went: ''Lads. I've done this against loads of clubs, so don't be worried'."

Another welcome returnee was experienced Matty Pearson, who cast aside a booking in the first 25 seconds to stay on message at Valley Parade in only his second league outing of the campaign following injury.

Rovers host Pearson's former club Huddersfield Town on Thursday.
