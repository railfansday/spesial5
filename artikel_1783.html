Tottenham to pay tribute to beloved broadcaster Danny Kelly during Ipswich clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Tottenham will pay tribute to renowned journalist and broadcaster Danny Kelly during their Premier League clash with Ipswich after he passed away last week at the age of 69.

Kelly, a lifelong Spurs fan who edited the NME and Q magazine, and launched the Football 365 website before going on to be a presenter and pundit on the BBC and TalkSport, passed away following a battle with oesophageal cancer.

Spurs paid tribute to Kelly in an emotional statement last week and will follow that up by honouring him at today’s game at home to Ipswich.

“The entire Tottenham family mourns the loss of one of our own, broadcasting legend Danny Kelly,” the statement read.

“Our thoughts and love are with his family and friends at this incredibly sad time. Rest in peace, Danny.”

A tribute feature has been included in Spurs’ matchday programme, while members of Kelly’s family have been invited to the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, with the club planning to pay tribute to him at half-time.

Kelly’s family have also organised a round of applause in the 69th minute to mark his age.

Spurs’ head of communications, Anthony Marshall, opened Roberto De Zerbi’s pre-match press conference at Hotspur Way on Friday afternoon by offering condolences and confirming the club’s plans to celebrate Kelly.

“On behalf of everyone at Tottenham, I want to offer our condolences and love to the family, friends and colleagues of Danny Kelly, as well as everyone who read his work or listened to him on the radio,” Marshall said.
