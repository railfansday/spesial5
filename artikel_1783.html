Less than three weeks ago, Spurs sat bottom of the Premier League without a victory. Three games later and they are into the top half thanks to three straight wins.

This game may, therefore, be coming at just the right time for the Londoners, who have seen their relegation odds drift on betting sites following their recent resurgence.

Leeds’ good start is in danger of fading away. It is five without a win now for them, with only two goals scored in those matches.

The potential absence of Dominic Calvert-Lewin here due to a “setback with his foot” doesn’t help matters on that front, although the England international is considered more likely to be fit than Anton Stach (foot) and Noah Okafor (adductor). Both are major doubts. At least defender Joe Rodon is fit again.

Spurs will have Rodrigo Bentancur back from a ban, although the Uruguayan may find it tough to get back into the team given their form.

Lucas Bergvall and Tosin Adarabioyo are also available again, while James Maddison and Pedro Porro will be pushing for a starting spot having only been on the bench for last week’s win over Crystal Palace.

Put simply, Spurs are on the up at present with Leeds going in the opposite direction. The hosts also have some injury concerns over big players in Anton Stach and Dominic Calvert-Lewin.

Admittedly, Leeds do have a strong home record – they are unbeaten in their last nine at Elland Road – so perhaps you may want to consider the safety net that the draw-no-bet market provides.

However, Spurs have a strong record in this fixture, winning on their last three visits by a combined score of 10-2. With Leeds light on goals at present, let’s take a chance on the visitors making it four wins out of four.

Leeds vs Tottenham Tip: Spurs to win – 8/5 (Betfred)

There looks to be a bit of value available in the shot markets in the shape of Tonali. While viewed as primarily a defensive midfield player, his role at Spurs has allowed him to get forward to hit shots.

He’s managed to land this bet in five of his nine games since his big-money summer move from Newcastle, including four of the last six.

Spurs, overall, are fourth for shots taken in this season’s Premier League, while Leeds are in the top half when it comes to shots faced. All things considered, a price of 19/10 looks very much worth taking.
