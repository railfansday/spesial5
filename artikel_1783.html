RB Leipzig set to pay Brighton up to €30m for Brajan Gruda

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

RB Leipzig will owe Brighton between €25 million and €30 million for Brajan Gruda when the obligation to buy him takes effect in early 2027, according to Sky Sport Germany. The broadcaster says the clubs set that date for accounting reasons.

Gruda joined Brighton from Mainz in 2024 and made 45 appearances before returning to Germany on loan in February. He scored three goals and set up three more in his first spell at Leipzig. The clubs extended the loan in July, keeping him in the Bundesliga for the 2026/27 season.

Against Augsburg last Saturday, Gruda turned a 1-0 deficit into a Leipzig lead in the space of a minute. He beat three defenders to set up Ezechiel Banzuzi’s 34th-minute equalizer, then lifted the ball into the box for Christopher Nkunku to volley home. Leipzig went on to win 5-2.
