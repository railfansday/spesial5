Verdict: Flamengo to score in both halves

Starting with the hosts, making real history last season as they got their hands on an eighth Serie A crown, Flamengo are leading the chase to defend their domestic title over the next fortnight. Marking their return from November’s international break holding a three-point buffer over bitter title rivals Palmeiras, Leonardo Jardim’s men will simply be looking to carry on from where they left off prior to November’s fortnight interval. Last seen securing an impressive 3-1 victory at home against Gremio, Flamengo will be dreaming of a repeat performance on Wednesday night. Signing off October with a thumping 4-1 win away at Vasco, the reigning Serie A champions have won each of their previous five consecutive top-flight appearances. Likewise, the South American giants have gained a real reputation for their clinical displays in the final third. In fact, notching 71 Serie A strikes in their 34 matchups this season, Jardim’s side hold the best-attacking tally among Brazil’s elite.

In what will be a huge boost for Flamengo on Wednesday night, Flamengo are expected to welcome back Brazilian international and midfield talisman Lucas Paqueta. Likewise, Jardim’s side are hopeful that former Juventus star Alex Sandro will return to his squad here.

While ex-Arsenal midfielder Jorginho might be set to turn 35 next month, the Italian international will start in the middle of the park. Bagging consecutive Serie A braces prior to November’s fortnight interval, leading marksman Pedro will spearhead Flamengo’s charge in the final third.

As for the visitors, last seen heading into November’s international break with a nightmare 3-1 defeat at home against Vasco, Athletico Paranaense found themselves at the heart of a public crisis prior to the fortnight interval. Now suddenly holding just a three-point buffer from sacrificing their Copa Libertadores spot, Odair Hellmann’s downbeat squad couldn’t have asked for a tougher examination on Wednesday night. Signing off October with a routine 2-0 loss away at Gremio, the Hurricanes have now registered just a single point from their previous four consecutive Serie A appearances. Likewise, putting just a sole top-flight victory on the board over the past two months, Athletico Paranaense should also be concerned by a worrying record at the Maracana. Including a 1-0 defeat when they last travelled to Flamengo back in September 2024, they have also lost four of their previous five trips to the reigning Brazilian champions.

Shown a straight red card against Vasco prior to November’s international break, Colombian striker Kevin Viveros will miss out on Wednesday night through suspension. Likewise, Brazilian youngster Dantas will also not feature in midweek as the 22-year-old will serve his own respective one-match Serie A ban.

Completing a permanent switch to Parana over the summer, former Benfica defender Gilberto should be handed an immediate full recall. Completing his own respective switch to the Arena da Baixada this year, one-time Bayern Munich star Luis Gustavo will start in the middle of the park.

While Flamengo might have been forced to settle for a single point when they travelled to Parana for their reverse Serie A showdown back in May, the reigning Serie A champions are rightfully installed as a landslide contender here. Marking their return from November’s fortnight interval holding a three-point buffer at the top of the table, Flamengo have won each of their previous five consecutive top-flight battles by an aggregate score of 14-4. Likewise, winning four of their last five meetings against Athletico Paranaense at the Maracana, it is no surprise that the South American icons will be looking to make another public statement.

Verdict: Flamengo to score in both halves
