The Secrets Behind Brittany Snow’s Press-Tour Glow

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The actress is embracing a more intentional beauty routine with a little help from RéVive Skincare.

Though her recent red-carpet looks may beg to differ, Brittany Snow is no longer chasing perfection—at least when it comes to her appearance. Instead, the actress, who turned 40 this year and is starring in one of the moment’s most deliciously bingeable series, now in its second season, is focused on aging well and nourishing her skin with intention. “There’s something really freeing about realizing you don’t need to look perfect to feel confident,” Snow says. “I spent so much of my younger years unfortunately being aware of how I looked. Now, I’m much more interested in just looking like myself, just hopefully a little more hydrated.” That’s where RéVive comes in.

Snow credits her makeup artist Charlie Riddle with introducing her to the luxe skin care brand and helping her evolve her relationship to beauty. “He has always focused on making my skin look like skin—healthy, hydrated, and luminous,” she says. “He understands that the better you take care of your skin, the less you need to cover it up.”

Throughout the current press tour, Riddle has been prepping Snow’s skin with RéVive; the brand has quickly become a staple in her personal routine, too. “I was initially drawn to how beautiful my skin felt when Charlie used the products on me,” she says. “There was this sense of hydration without feeling like I had a million things sitting on my face. Charlie has taught me that the edit matters; I used to be someone who thought more products meant better results, and now I realize that consistency and quality are much more important.”

In addition to the products’ elegant feel and innovative, science-based formulas, Snow also appreciates RéVive’s brand story. “RéVive was founded by a plastic surgeon whose research began with helping burn victims heal,” she says. “There’s something really meaningful to me about a beauty brand that was born out of wanting to help people.”

Whether she’s preparing to film a scene, gearing up for a marathon press day, or decompressing before bed at home, she relies on RéVive’s Renewal Moisturizing Night Cream and Renewal Rescue Elixir Oil to hydrate, care for, and rejuvenate her skin.

Both products are powered by exclusive peptides enhanced by RéVive RVGF Technology—a breakthrough delivery system that targets skin concerns with greater precision to maximize efficacy. The complementary formulas work to renew skin overnight to reveal a healthier-looking, more radiant, and glowing complexion by morning.

“My skin goes through so much between filming, travel, and wearing makeup for hours,” she says. “By the end of the day, I really want something that feels rich and helps my skin look refreshed. After using them, my skin feels like glass.”

Glowing from the inside out, her recent appearances—be they in character on our TV screens or in late-night talk show clips on our phones—are proof both of RéVive’s results and the confidence that comes from feeling good in one’s own skin.
