Taylor Swift Is at Travis Kelce’s Chiefs-Cardinals Game—and Made It a Family Affair

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Taylor Swift has been spotted at all of Travis Kelce’s home games this season, and today’s matchup against the Arizona Cardinals was no exception. The singer was photographed with her parents, Andrea and Scott Swift, as she cheered on the Kansas City Chiefs. She wore a red and black jacket.

Swift has been out of the public eye since Wednesday, when she performed at the CMA Awards for the first time in 13 years. She attended the ceremony with Kelce’s teammate Patrick Mahomes’s wife, Brittany Mahomes.

Neither Kelce nor Patrick was with them at the ceremony, as the two NFL players are in the middle of their season.

Kelce has also been recovering from an ankle injury that sidelined him for part of the Chiefs’ game against the Atlanta Falcons last Sunday.

After the game, he told USA Today that he was doing fine. “I’m all good,” he said. “I probably could have done more than I did when I came back, but it was the direction the team wanted to go, so I was right there with them. We had a lot of young guys step up, so it was really nice.”

In March, the 37-year-old tight end spoke on The Pat McAfee Show about how Swift inspired him to play another season.

He said that “without a doubt [watching her be on top of her field motivated me to continue playing]. We share the same love for what we do and fortunately, we’ve had this desire since we were kids in our selective professions.”

“It’s just amazing to see her keep going to the table,” he said of her music career. “Keep finding new things to write about. Keep finding new melodies and things like that. And on top of that, still seeing her have that love and joy in what she does, of course that’s motivating. That’s motivating for anyone to see, let alone you know, [me watching] my fiancée and knowing that I’m going through something where I’m trying to figure out exactly what the future holds for me. Something like that definitely motivates me to say, ‘You know what? I’m not done either. I’ve still got some ideas in the back of my mind. I’ve still got some juice left to play this game.’”
