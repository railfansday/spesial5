Chrissy Teigen Reveals Her Go-To Date Night Food With John Legend—And It’s Surprisingly Simple

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Chrissy Teigen and John Legend might be one of Hollywood's most recognizable couples, but apparently, even they struggle to find time for a date night.

On the latest episode of Déjà Food, the cookbook author opens up about her favorite meals with Legend, revealing that between their busy schedules and raising four children, finding time for a night out isn’t always easy. But when they do manage to sneak away, there’s one type of food they almost always gravitate toward.

“We don't often get date nights, but when we do, it's Italian,” Teigen says.

And considering the couple’s history, that actually makes perfect sense.

Italy has played a pretty significant role in their relationship. The two tied the knot in Lake Como in 2013 and returned to the same destination to celebrate their 10th wedding anniversary.

During the interview, Teigen recalls enjoying an Italian feast at their anniversary celebration and reveals that their wedding dinner featured pasta served from a large cheese wheel.

Italian food also seems to be the couple's preferred choice for birthdays. Teigen recalls celebrating one of her birthdays in Italy, where she believes she ate porcini mushroom risotto, which she describes as one of her favorite dishes.

And for Legend's birthday, she organized a dinner with his New York friends at Casa Cipriani.

“I surprised him with his New York friends, and we went to Casa Cipriani and had an incredible meal,” she says. “Everything they have there is incredible.”

Of course, not every date night has been quite so effortless.

Teigen also shares a story about one of the first times she splurged on dinner for Legend early in their relationship. After months of him treating her, she decided it was finally her turn to pick up the check at a restaurant from Chef Daniel Boulud.

But she was so nervous about her card potentially being declined that she brought a large amount of cash and spent the entire meal mentally calculating the bill.

Things got especially stressful when she ordered a margarita that cost approximately $42.

“I was so nervous, but I had enough cash,” she recalls, before admitting that she thinks Legend ended up paying the tip.

Thankfully, their restaurant dates seem to have become a little less stressful over the years.

And while the couple clearly appreciates an elaborate Italian dinner, Teigen also reveals that Legend recently made her a Kirkland Signature Costco Chicken Bake at home.

“It was like a Hot Pocket, but huge,” she says.

So apparently, the secret to keeping date night interesting is a little Italian food, an occasional fancy restaurant, and sometimes a really big Costco Chicken Bake.
