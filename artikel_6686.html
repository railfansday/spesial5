These 8 Long-Lasting Perfumes Won’t Ghost You by Lunchtime

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Get ready to smell good all damn day, no re-spritzing necessary.

We may earn commission from links on this page, but we only recommend products we love. Promise.

I know I’m unforgettable because of the scent trail I always leave in my wake. It’s so romantic to think about my fragrance lingering in rooms I’m no longer in, or letting a friend borrow a piece of clothing only for them say it still smells like me. Few perfumes live up to my longevity expectations—which is why I made it my mission to find the best long-lasting perfumes that will stick around for 12+ hours. It wasn’t an easy task, but someone’s nose had to put in the work!

Below, find eight perfumes that Cosmo editors tested and can confirm stick around longer than the average juice. These fragrances make a statement—many of them are one-spritz wonders—and have the potential to make you the most memorable person in a room. (Who doesn’t want that?)

One Cosmo editor says: “Vanille Planifolia is often regarded as the best vanilla of all time, and I 100% agree. This is raw, captivating, and not like your standard sugar-laced vanilla. This is as close as you’ll get to sniffing it straight from the pod...with the addition of spicy-ambery undertones. Also, this may be the strongest perfume I own. All I need is one spritz max and it lingers for days. I’ve even smelled it on clothing weeks after spraying. It’s an investment, sure, but it will become the crown jewel of your fragrance collection.”—MARY HONKUS, CONTRIBUTING BEAUTY EDITOR

One Cosmo editor says: "The first time I tried this, I spritzed it once on my wrist and immediately left for CorePower class. I kept getting whiffs of the scent throughout workout, which surprised me because I was drenched in sweat. Afterward, multiple people came up to ask me what perfume I was wearing because it made the room smell so good. 'I thought they were pumping something good through the vents,' one of them told me. Hours later, when I got home and rinsed off, I could still some some remnants of this patchouli-based, slightly sweet scent."—BETH GILLETTE, BEAUTY EDITOR

One Cosmo editor says: "I’m obsessed with the original Delina, and I didn't think the scent could get any better—until I tried the exclusif. It's a little more elevated than the OG, making it perfect to wear from day to night. The notes lean warmer, and the florals have more depth to them rather than overt brightness and sweetness. Just one or two spritzes is all I need for all day wear."—JASMINE HYMAN, ASSISTANT BEAUTY EDITOR

One Cosmo editor says: “I promise you don’t have to drop $$$$ for a perfume to last longer than 20 minutes. Summer Fridays made sure of it! This cheery gourmand reminds me of savoring a vanilla ice cream cone during golden hour by the sea. Despite being loaded with indulgent notes—including caramel, vanilla, tonka, and amber—it maintains an airy breeziness courtesy of coconut and bergamot. When I wear this I smell like a sweet treat all damn day.”—MH

One Cosmo editor says: “The scent trail this produces will have people following you down the street, I swear! Ambre Latte combines all my favorite cold weather elements for a creamy, unforgettable potion reminiscent of enjoying a snow storm from inside my favorite coffee shop. Warm, spicy woods are swirled with creamy almond milk and a dash of glowing golden amber for one of the coziest scents ever created. I love wearing this on its own, but it’s also great for layering.”—MH

One Cosmo editor says: “This smells incredibly ritzy—like super rich and deep but not in a cloying way; much more balanced. I definitely smell the oak in there, and the cognac essence is not overpowering or boozy. There's a little bit of cinnamon spice on the initial spray, but then it settles into a nice woody, slightly sweet scent.”—JAYNE LEMIRE, CONTRIBUTOR

One Cosmo editor says: "BR540 reminds me a lot of how my skin would smell after taking a shower. There's a clean, subtle AROMA that just sits right against my skin. It makes me feel powerful, but not because it's strong and intense. Rather, it just feels like an enhanced version of my own skin. To me, that's the sexiest way to smell."—BG

One Cosmo editor says: "Of everything I've ever worn, this has the most intense sillage ever. One spritz will project throughout practically our entire office for pretty much the entire day. It’s sultry and luxurious, a true one-of-a-kind scent."—BG

Mary Honkus is a beauty contributor for Cosmopolitan with over eight years of experience researching, writing, and editing beauty stories. She is an authority in all beauty categories, but has a sweet spot for fragrance with a collection of over 600 scents. While researching for this story, she tested dozens of strong perfumes to bring you the most captivating options that linger for hours.
