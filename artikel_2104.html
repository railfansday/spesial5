Two days or two weeks – Michael Carrick future decision rolls into the international break

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Manchester United will use the November international break to decide whether Michael Carrick stays on as head coach, after a three-game reprieve that delivered only one win.

According to The Guardian, Carrick came close to being sacked after the 3-0 home defeat to Bournemouth on 25 October.

Director of football Jason Wilcox and chief executive Omar Berrada discussed his position at the training ground the following day and agreed to give him three matches to turn things around before the break.

Those games brought a 2-0 defeat at Chelsea, a 3-2 win over Roma and a chaotic 4-4 draw with Aston Villa at Old Trafford on Sunday. United conceded eight goals across the three fixtures, and defensive frailty remains the biggest worry.

Wilcox is now leading a review of Carrick’s position. He will consult Berrada before making a recommendation to Sir Jim Ratcliffe, who oversees the club’s sporting operations. Talks are expected to begin on Monday and continue through the break.

The league table makes grim reading. United sit in the Premier League relegation zone with just one league win in their last 10 matches.

If he does win a stay of execution, results will need to follow. Manchester United cannot be languishing in 17th place.

Carrick remains defiant. Speaking to Sky Sports News after the Villa draw, he admitted his side “haven’t won enough games” but insisted he expects to move forward.

“I’m quite clear with what direction the club needs to go in, and it’s got to win games, said Carrick after the late Villa draw.

“I think there’s more than enough [to turn things around].”

If United do make a change, former Newcastle manager Eddie Howe is reported to be Wilcox’s preferred candidate. Howe has been out of work since leaving St James’ Park during pre-season, after four and a half years in charge.

In the meantime, there are no plans to strengthen Carrick’s coaching staff.

For now, Carrick is expected to be in charge for United’s next match, against Liverpool. Whether three games were enough to convince the hierarchy should become clear before then.

The trip to Anfield could be do or die for the decorated former United midfielder.

He’s not likely to survive a heavy defeat at the home of the club’s fierce rivals, but a galvanising win would surely buy him time and goodwill.

That’s if he makes it through the next fortnight before then. A decision or indication could be two days or two weeks away.

The only certainty at Old Trafford is uncertainty.
