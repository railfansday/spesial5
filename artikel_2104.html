Omar Berrada breaks silence on Bruno Fernandes contract as Pochettino makes feelings clear

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Shumail Sajid writes about Manchester United for Read Man Utd and Crystal Palace for Read Crystal Palace, with occasional pieces on other Premier League clubs. He has also written for FanSided's Red Devils Dispatch, Yardbarker, Anfield Central and The Sports Side.

Bruno Fernandes has been at the center of contract talks this season and is yet to sign a new deal at Manchester United.

Various reports have indicated that Bruno Fernandes has turned down two contract offers. He reportedly wants a 4-year deal but United are wary of such a deal as he is already 32 years old.

Manchester United chief executive Omar Berrada has confirmed the club are discussing a new contract with Bruno Fernandes, but has refused to reveal any details about the negotiations as the midfielder approaches the final months of his current deal.

Fernandes’ contract expires at the end of the season, leaving Manchester United with an important decision to make over the future of their captain.

Speaking to Sky Sports, Berrada praised the Portuguese international for his contribution to the club and made clear how highly he is valued at Old Trafford.

“Listen, I’ve said before, we have a tremendous amount of respect for Bruno,” Berrada said.

“We’re very fortunate to have him as a captain.

“What he’s done for this team over the last six, seven years is absolutely incredible. Last year he won, I think, all the individual awards that you can win.

“And we’re very proud to have him as captain. It’s true that we’re having conversations about his new contract, but I prefer to keep that private.

“I think it’s something that needs to be discussed behind the scenes between him and the club.”

Although contract talks are taking place, there is no indication of how close the two parties are to reaching an agreement. United will be keen to avoid losing one of their most influential players throughout these years, but the terms and length of any new contract remain speculative at the moment.

New manager, Mauricio Pochettino, has backed his captain ahead of his first game against Liverpool. He was asked about Bruno Fernandes, whose critical comments about Michael Carrick’s tactics became a talking point during the former manager’s final weeks at Old Trafford.

The new head coach did not get drawn into the controversy, instead stressing the importance of Fernandes to the team and the need for every player to feel valued.

“Bruno is our captain and a very important player but all the players need to feel they are important from the bottom to the top,” Pochettino said.

The Bruno Fernandes contract situation comes at a difficult time for United, who are 18th in the Premier League with eight points from their opening 10 matches. They have managed just one win and five draws during that run.

Pochettino has inherited a struggling side, and keeping Bruno Fernandes committed to the project could prove important as he is one of the few performing stars in the fragile Manchester United team.
