Birmingham City, West Brom & West Ham will lick their lips at major Crystal Palace update

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

As we head into the November international break, it is shaping up to be a competitive battle for automatic promotion in the Championship.

With just six points separating the teams currently sitting in first and ninth place, it remains incredibly tight towards the top of the Championship, but events over the next fortnight could have a big impact on the promotion race.

Birmingham City hit the top of the Championship table after they recorded their third consecutive victory with a 2-1 win over Swansea City on Saturday, and Chris Davies' men look to be building strong momentum.

The Blues replaced Middlesbrough at the summit on goal difference after a disappointing week for the Teesside outfit, who followed up their midweek defeat at Wrexham with an underwhelming 0-0 draw at Watford.

Surprise package Stoke City are level on points with Birmingham and Boro after their impressive run continued with a 2-1 victory at Portsmouth, while Midlands duo Wolverhampton Wanderers and West Bromwich Albion are just a point behind the trio.

Swansea, Southampton and Queens Park Rangers complete the play-off places, with West Ham United dropping out of the top eight after their turbulent start to life under new head coach Scott Parker continued with a 2-1 defeat at Blackburn Rovers.

While the aforementioned nine teams appear to be the most likely automatic promotion contenders as things stand, it is difficult to ignore a resurgent Norwich City, who recorded their fourth consecutive win as they hammered Bristol City 5-0 to move within three points of the play-offs.

The international break will give many of the promotion hopefuls a chance to rest after a busy period, but recent developments at Crystal Palace may have Middlesbrough and Swansea feeling a little uneasy.

There are yet to be any managerial sackings in the Premier League this season, but that appears certain to change during the international break as Pierre Sage's future at Crystal Palace hangs in the balance.

Sage only took over at Selhurst Park in the summer when he replaced Oliver Glasner, but the Eagles have won just two of their opening 10 league games, and they currently sit bottom of the table, two points from safety.

Palace's poor start continued as they were beaten 3-1 at home by Liverpool on Sunday, and, after weeks of speculation over Sage's position, it was reported that the club were preparing to part company with the Frenchman.

It seems the Eagles have already drawn up a shortlist of potential replacements for Sage, with Middlesbrough's Kim Hellberg and Swansea's Vitor Matos said to be two of the names under consideration, along with Thomas Frank, Frank Lampard, Sean Dyche, Vitor Pereira, Tony Popovic, Jens Berthel Askou and Ole Werner.

Former Brentford and Tottenham Hotspur boss Frank is reportedly a leading contender to succeed Sage, which may offer some reassurance to Middlesbrough and Swansea, but news that Hellberg and Matos are attracting top flight interest will be music to the ears of their promotion rivals.

Given that Middlesbrough stole Hellberg from under Swansea's noses last November before the Welsh side turned to Matos, there is a certain irony to the fact that the pair are now both on Crystal Palace's radar.

While Hellberg inherited a strong foundation at the Riverside Stadium after the work of his predecessor, Rob Edwards, the Swede has taken Boro to another level over the past year, building one of the most dangerous attacking sides in the division, as well as implementing an attractive brand of football.

After an 11th-placed finish last season, the Swans have emerged as a surprise promotion contender this time around under Matos, and they led the Championship during the early weeks of the campaign before a recent run of four defeats in five games saw them drop down to sixth.

There is no doubt that Hellberg and Matos have been integral to the success of their respective clubs this season, and it would be a devastating blow for Middlesbrough and Swansea were either to lose their highly rated coaches, while simultaneously providing a huge boost to the likes of Birmingham, Wolves, West Brom and West Ham.

Given that Boro have assembled one of the strongest squads in the Championship after their summer spending spree, it seems unlikely that Hellberg would be tempted to make the move to Selhurst Park, but Matos could consider jumping ship if the Eagles come calling.
