Todd Cantwell drops emotional reaction to Tony Mowbray news at Blackburn Rovers

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Against all the pre-season odds, Blackburn Rovers have been making a real go of things in the Championship under Tony Mowbray in 2026-27.

The Lancashire outfit just about staved off the threat of relegation under Michael O'Neill last season, but the experienced manager decided to snub a full-time deal at Ewood Park before returning to his post as Northern Ireland boss, paving the way for Mowbray's second stint in charge of Rovers.

Despite a very quiet summer of transfer activity, in which the Blackburn playing squad was barely strengthened, they find themselves sitting 13th in the Championship after 15 matches, some 11 points ahead of the relegation zone and bitter rivals Burnley, and only three points off the newly-expanded play-off spots.

A huge curveball has emerged though in the lead-up to this Sunday's East Lancashire Derby at Turf Moor, with the shock news coming on Tuesday morning that Mowbray had 'stepped back' from his role as Blackburn boss.

Health issues are the reason for the 62-year-old's decision, and whilst his official statement reads like a goodbye to the job on a permanent basis, it's not entirely clear whether Mowbray still holds the head coach role.

Blackburn's Arni Gudjohnsen continued his excellent run of form with a hat-trIck for Iceland ahead of the derby against Burnley next weekend.

Lancashire Telegraph reporter Elliott Jackson has claimed that Mowbray is only 'stepping back' fron the role for now and there is no managerial vacancy to be filled, with his assistants Mike Dodds and Damien Johnson taking interim charge, whilst fellow journalist Alan Nixon has reported that he's 'stepping down' instead.

What is known for sure though is that it's not the first time Mowbray has had health issues that has impacted his job - he had to step away from his role as Birmingham City manager in 2024 just over a month after taking the job as he needed medical treatment.

That absence lasted until the end of the season, where Blues were relegated under Gary Rowett's management, before Mowbray tendered his resignation, revealing later in 2024 that he had been battling bowel cancer.

Given how well Blackburn had been performing under Mowbray's management, it is an untimely blow for the whole club, especially ahead of such a big game.

And one player in particular who has showcased improvement is Todd Cantwell.

Blackburn's skipper has somewhat divided opinion in his two years at the club, and there were fitness issues last season that kept him out for important games, but Cantwell has steered clear of the treatment table so far in 2026-27 and has been incredibly influential in the middle of the park.

The news of Mowbray's health issues will clearly hit hard for the whole Rovers squad, but Cantwell has led the way with his own tribute for the manager via his Instagram account.

Given the way he's managed to transform Blackburn's squad from what was a relegation-calibre one last season to pushing them up into the mid-table spots in such a short period of time is testament to Mowbray's management and coaching abilities.

Now, it is up to Johnson and Dodds to steady the ship, with a busy period of football coming up, with no international breaks now until the month of March.

With Mowbray only officially 'stepping back', it's likely that Dodds and ex-Rovers midfielder Johnson will be in charge until Mowbray's health improves, or he officially leaves and a new manager is perhaps sought if the duo do not get positive results, and that's going to bring big pressure.

A trip to Burnley for their first game is certainly an acid test, but there's a huge motivation now for Cantwell and co to push even further up the standings given what Mowbray has done for them.
