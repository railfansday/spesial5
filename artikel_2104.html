Arsenal Could Benefit as PSG Step Away from Ibrahim Maza Transfer Race

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Arsenal could have been given a helping hand in their quest to sign Ibrahim Maza. Paris Saint-Germain reportedly pulls out of talks with Bayer Leverkusen’s attacker.

The Gunners are just one of several clubs keeping an eye on the exciting young talent in Germany. However, with some of the biggest clubs in Europe keen on the prospect of adding the player to their attack, it might have made it difficult for them.

PSG looked like genuine contenders for the signing of Maza. The French champions having reportedly had talks with both Maza and Leverkusen.

It seems that PSG are not interested anymore, according to Fichajes. They have ended negotiations with the player and Leverkusen because they were unhappy with how the player’s representatives and Leverkusen have conducted themselves during the talks.

This could be great news for Arsenal, but it doesn’t mean they have full control of the situation.

The withdrawal of a club of PSG’s calibre is a benefit for Arsenal. They had seemed well on their way to landing Maza. Now, they will pursue him without the pressure of competition.

Maza has emerged as a good signing for clubs looking to improve the flair in their attacking third. Leverkusen will have noticed this and may be tempted to sell him if they get a decent offer.

Arsenal might also determine if Maza would be a valuable asset for their team in the long run. They must ensure that he can adapt to their attacking strategies.

They will not be the only one interested in Maza. Although PSG may be willing to let him go, there are many other clubs interested in the player.

Leverkusen will also want to get the best deal out of the situation. If they are willing to negotiate, Arsenal need to think if it would make financial sense to do so.

Finally, it will come down to what the player wants. Arsenal would need to persuade the player that the Emirates is the right place for his progression and that they can provide the right route for his aspirations and ambitions.

This may be a step in the right direction for Arsenal.

If Maza is still a target for the Gunners, then they may have a better chance of getting it done given PSG’s new stance. But they will still need to come to an agreement with Leverkusen and convince the player to move to north London.
