"I can't see Southampton doing it" - West Ham transfer plea made for Aaron Ramsdale

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Despite being a former Championship-winning goalkeeper, Mads Hermansen hasn't earned any glory back in the division at West Ham United, and some Hammers fans want to see him replaced altogether.

The Dane was solid for the London Stadium-based side in the Premier League, replacing Alphonse Areola when Nuno came in and keeping seven clean sheets in 18 league games.

So far this season, though, he's conceded over four more than his expected goals against tally suggests he should have, with a save percentage of just 54%. West Ham fans were heard booing his every touch at Blackburn Rovers, where Scott Parker's side fell to a third defeat in four league games.

Unfortunately, though, with Areola suffering an injury in the warm-up at Ewood Park, Hermansen seems to be Parker's only senior option in between the sticks until January at least, when it's almost universally agreed that a new keeper will be on the shopping list.

Aaron Ramsdale's name has been floated around Hammers fans as a potential January recruit, with the 28-year-old firmly third-choice at St Mary's following a dud loan spell at Newcastle United last season.

Ramsdale has played at the top level for the majority of his career, but his stock has fallen so far after leaving Arsenal for the South Coast that the ex-England international will be looking anywhere to help revive it.

FLW's West Ham fan pundit, Ryan Horn, hopes that his side looks for a goalkeeper in January, and wouldn't mind seeing Ramsdale pursued. They're arguably the only Championship side that could afford his top-flight-level wages, and he could rediscover some form back in the capital.

"We should be looking to bring in a keeper, and I wouldn't mind Ramsdale," he said. "I know he's had issues in the past, like at Arsenal and in the past couple of years, but I'd definitely give him a go."

Horn did express some doubt, though, about whether Southampton would want to send Ramsdale to the London Stadium, as that could strengthen a promotion rival, even if the fan pundit admits his side doesn't look like challenging for the top two just yet.

The 28-year-old may be far from first choice at St Mary's, but there's no doubt about the quality of the keeper if he can revive his career elsewhere. Perhaps a move to West Ham would be too risky for them.

"Hopefully we can do a deal, but I can't see Southampton doing it," he explained. "I don't think they'd want to strengthen a promotion rival. As much as we don't look like one at the moment, I do feel that we'll get there."

In the meantime, though, Horn hopes that, given Hermansen's poor form, Scott Parker will take a chance on young goalkeeper Finley Herrick, who has just three appearances for West Ham, none of which have come in league action.

With Areola out injured, the fan pundit doesn't want it to feel that Parker has no choice over whom to play, as he doesn't feel that the Dane has done enough to warrant starts.

"I would bring Ramsdale in, try and move Hermansen on, and at the moment, with Areola out injured, I would like to see Herrick get game time," the fan pundit continued.

"You can't keep rewarding Hermansen with game time when he isn't putting the performances in. What would that show to our younger players? Just because you may be the best on paper, shouldn't mean that you get to come in and have game time every week.

"I'd rather Parker take the risk and reward game time to players who deserve it."

It would be a risk, giving Herrick minutes, given his lack of senior experience. However, with some West Ham fans losing complete faith in Hermansen, perhaps it would be wise to take the 26-year-old out of the firing line for a week or so.

If Herrick performs well in the meantime, then perhaps he'd be worthy of a more extended period in between the sticks.

On the Ramsdale move, Southampton are in a difficult situation. The 28-year-old would be on such large wages that it would be wise to move him on and not have to pay him each week to not play.

However, very few clubs would be open to paying what Saints, or Ramsdale himself, may want for the goalkeeper. Yes, the majority of his career has been at Premier League level, but he's been relegated from the division three times.

There may be little interest in the 28-year-old come January, like there was this past summer, and if that's the case, Southampton may be forced to accept what they get to help get him off the books.

If that deal proves to involve West Ham, they may have to grit their teeth and bear it, while hoping that it doesn't turn out to bite them later on in the campaign.
