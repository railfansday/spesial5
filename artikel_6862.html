Bella Hadid Buys Rustic Colorado Retreat for $5.5 Million

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Victoria’s Secret angel recently started dating someone with strong family ties to the area.

The rugged allure of the Mountain West continues to attract celebrities. Tom Cruise and Oprah Winfrey both own properties in the ski mecca of Telluride. Kevin Costner glamorized multigenerational homesteading with his hit show Yellowstone and, in real life, oversees Aspen’s sprawling Dunbar Ranch. Now, model Bella Hadid—who recently opened the 2026 Victoria’s Secret runway show—just acquired an alpine retreat of her own in a remote town called Crested Butte.

Nestled deep in Colorado’s Rocky Mountains, a roughly three-hour drive from Aspen, Crested Butte has been known as the “Wildflower Capital of Colorado” since 1990, when the state legislature bestowed the honorary title on the Gunnison County town (population: 1,639).

Hadid has reportedly been living in the tiny village for “several months,” per The Wall Street Journal, which was the first to report the sale. The news also comes just days after the model went public on Instagram with new boyfriend Nathan Lacy, a construction manager who has ties to the region.

“I’ve always loved hockey living in NYC,” she wrote, alongside several snaps of her and Lacy at a Colorado Avalanche game. “And being a Colorado girl at heart, growing up here since i was young, I guess it was only a matter of time before I found my way to the @coloradoavalanche.”

Her new creekside compound hit the market over the summer for nearly $7 million with Channing Boucher of LIV Sotheby’s International Realty. Hadid reportedly got it at a discount, paying $5.5 million.

Built in 2001, the property sits on a 0.2-acre parcel with 200 feet of frontage on Coal Creek. Wrapped in weathered lumber, the three-bedroom, three-bathroom retreat was partially constructed out of two “original 100-year-old coal sheds,” per the listing. The rustic interiors, meanwhile, come with 30-foot beamed ceilings and hardwood floors reclaimed from a Virginia barn.

Rounding out the estate is a creek-front dining terrace plus a hot tub at the edge of the water.

This appears to be Hadid’s first real estate transaction since 2021, when she offloaded her New York City penthouse for $6.35 million.
