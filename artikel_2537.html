Confirmed lineups: Crystal Palace vs. TSG Hoffenheim

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

With kick-off in the English capital scheduled for just over an hour's time at 18:45 CET, both coaches have released their starting lineups which can be viewed in full below.

Crystal Palace (3-4-2-1): Henderson – Richards, Ahanor, Canvot – Khalaili, Timber, Wharton, Mitchell – Kamada, Pino – Mateta

TSG Hoffenheim (4-3-1-2): Baumann – Abraha, Kabak, Machida, Rots – Prass, Avdullahu, Burger – Kramaric – Asllani, Lemperle
