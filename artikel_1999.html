Darren Beckford remembered after former Hearts striker dies aged 59

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Former Heart of Midlothian forward Darren Beckford has sadly passed away at the age of 59.

Beckford enjoyed a brief spell in Gorgie between August and December 1996, making 12 competitive appearances and scoring twice for the Jambos.

The most memorable of those goals was the opener against Dundee in the League Cup semi-final at Easter Road.

Beckford began his professional career with Manchester City before becoming a firm favourite during a memorable spell at Port Vale, where he established himself as a prolific goalscorer.

His well-travelled career also included spells with Norwich City and Preston North End before his short stint in the capital with Hearts.

The striker arrived at Tynecastle during Jim Jefferies' time in charge, joining a Hearts side that would go on to enjoy a memorable period in the late 1990s.

His stay in Edinburgh proved short-lived, but Beckford still managed to make his mark during his few months in maroon, with his League Cup semi-final strike among the highlights.

It was at Port Vale, however, where the forward enjoyed some of the most memorable years of his career and became a popular figure with supporters.

Beckford's passing will be mourned by fans of the many clubs he represented during a career that took him across English and Scottish football.
