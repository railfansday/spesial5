Goring CC FC 4 v Hunston Community Club 0

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Goring hosted previously unbeaten Hunston Community Club and came away with a decisive 4-0 victory to retain their position at the top of the West Sussex League Division One South.

Goring started really strongly with the midfield trio of Todor Bankov, Dave Wilcox and Nathan Madgwick dominating play and in the 7th minute Connor Pomeroy fed Ben Rubano who made no mistake from the edge of the box to give Goring an early lead. The next chance came when a fine run and pass by Alex Staines saw a fierce shot from Jacob Pippen go just wide. On 14 minutes, Rubano found space for a whipped shot which was very well saved by the visiting keeper only for it to fall to Pomeroy who made no mistake from close in for a 2-0 lead.

Goring were now completely dominant and shots rained in from Pomeroy, Charlie Sparks and Daniel Wilson only to be foiled by good saves from the Hunston keeper, before Goring added a third after 28 mintes when Pippen fed Pomeroy who produced an unstoppable shot which whistled in from fully 20 yards. A well deserved fourth goal came just before half time when Goring won a free kick and it was Ben Crack's clever pass into Pippen which drew a foul inside the box. Up stepped Captain Todor Bankov with a trademark penalty for a 4-0 half time lead.

Goring continued to boss the game in the second half with Hayden Briggs, Connor Harvey and Pomeroy all being denied further goals by excellent saves from the Hunston goalkeeper. The game ended 4-0 with Goring putting in their best performance of the season. Men of the match were Nathan Madgwick and Dan Wilson.
