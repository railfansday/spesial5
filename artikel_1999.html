Ashley Barnes reacts to huge moment for ex-Burnley star Maxime Esteve

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Maxime Esteve made his Champions League debut for RB Leipzig against Lens on Tuesday night, and one of his former teammates was cheering him on.

One of the biggest costs that comes with relegation from the Premier League can be the loss of players that comes with it. Even relegated sides have some players who will be wanted elsewhere, and many will have clauses in their contracts entitling them to leave in the event that their teams go down by the end of a season.

This exodus took place at the end of the 2025-26 season for Burnley, just as it routinely does for clubs relegated from this level, and in the case of defender Maxime Esteve the offer was an appealing one. The 24-year-old centre-back signed for RB Leipzig in the summer, and although he's primarily been used as a squad player by the Bundesliga outfit through the opening weeks of this season, he was included in their team for their Champions League match against RC Lens on Tuesday night, his debut in the competition.

It turned out to be a successful night for both the player and his new club. RB Leipzig won the match 3-1, and Esteve scored strongly for his performance before being withdrawn midway through the second half with cramp. And after the match, when he posted to social media about his delight at making his Champions League debut, one of his former Burnley teammates was there in the replies with a show of support for him.

Posting to the social media platform Instagram after RB Leipzig's 3-1 win over RC Lens in the Champions League on Tuesday night, Maxime Esteve couldn't contain his delight at having made his first appearance in Europe's premier club competition, saying, "First Champions League game, at home, and a win to go with it."

Esteve was clearly very pleased to have been given the opportunity in such a prestigious tournament: "A night I won’t forget. Thanks to the team and to our fans for the atmosphere. Let’s keep going."

Among those congratulating Esteve in the comments was one of his former teammates. Ashley Barnes has played over 300 games for Burnley, but his first spell ended before Esteve arrived at the club. But the former Brighton and Norwich striker returned to the Lancashire club in January 2025 and extended his contract for a year at the end of the 2024-25 season, meaning that the two were in the same Clarets squad for a season and a half.

And it's clear from the reactions to this post - not only from Barnes - but also from a number of Burnley fans, that the central defender is still thought of fondly at Turf Moor, almost half a season on from his departure from their club.

Maxime Esteve has been tipped for the top after excelling for the Clarets this season

Maxime Esteve's move to RB Leipzig hasn't gone completely according to plan for the 24-year-old defender. He has only made five appearances for his new club since arriving in the summer, of which three have been starts. But an assured performance will only have strengthened his case for a more regular berth in their starting eleven.

This is an exciting time to be at the club. RB Leipzig are in fourth place in the Bundesliga, and although Bayern Munich remain the clear favourites to win the league title, Leipzig are only three points behind current leaders Borussia Dortmund.

It's all a long way removed from Burnley's dismal 2025-26 Premier League season, which ended with the Clarets relegated in 19th place having picked up just 22 points all season, leaving them 19 points short of safety.

Ashley Barnes, meanwhile, stayed at Turf Moor following relegation after agreeing another contract extension with Burnley during the summer. But Barnes has not been a central part of the Burnley team since his 2025 return to Turf Moor. He only made seven appearances for them in the Premier League last season, all of which came from the bench and which amounted to just 41 minutes of game time.

This season has seen Barnes get even less game time for Burnley. He has yet to make an appearance for them in the Championship in 2026-27, although at 37 years of age, the striker may be preparing for a post-playing career in coaching rather than being fully focussed on playing. It's already been reported that he'll be joining the club's coaching staff at the end of this season.

The only sighting of him that Burnley fans have had on the pitch this season came with a 45-minute cameo appearance in a Premier League 2 fixture against West Ham United towards the end of October. But the fact that he should be giving such support to one of his former teammates is a positive sign for what Burnley fans can expect from him as a coach.
