Crystal Palace stars 'to leave' in droves as club bring in new manager - Expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Crystal Palace have been warned their next manager will suffer the same fate as Pierre Sage amid behind-the-scenes issues.

While no confirmation has been forthcoming just yet, sources say Sage will be sacked by Crystal Palace within days if not hours after what has been a nightmare start to the season.

Work is ongoing to identify his replacement behind the scenes, and contact has been made with multiple potenital targets, but a change of manager may not be enough.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Crystal Palace have bigger problems than Sage, who was set up to fail by a series of poor decisions.

The Palace board failed to provide Sage with the options he needed to strengthen his squad in the summer, particularly following the departures of Maxence Lacroix and Daniel Munoz.

Meanwhile, there is a growing negative feeling amongst the supporters which has had a direct impact on the pitch, with Dean Hednerson insisting they need the fans' support.

Brown told Football Insider on 28 October that Sage has largely been thrown under the bus by the Crystal Palace hierarchy, who must shoulder some blame for their poor start.

While Sage cannot be blameless as his tactical setup often left his side exposed defensively and he failed to address their key weaknesses, he is also not entirely at fault.

The Crystal Palace board and recruitment team largely hung the boss out to dry during the summer, something that has reflected on the pitch, and the new boss could suffer the same fate.

"Unfortunately for Crystal Palace, I don't see their issues being resolved by a change in manager," Brown told Football Insider.

"They might sort things out on the pitch, but it's not as simple as that, because the new manager will have to work with these same players who haven't been performing.

"He will have to contend with the same issues that Sage did, in terms of the squad maybe not being good enough, especially not defensively.

"He'll have to deal with the board and the transfer team, the way Palace like to operate, which was also clearly a problem for Oliver Glasner before Sage came in.

"Key players are always bound to leave, we've seen them let the likes of Lacroix, Munoz, Guehi all leave, and there are more players who are likely to follow them.

"But Crystal Palace can't afford to keep letting their best players go without proper replacements coming in, and that's another thing that contributed to their issues this season.

"There's a bit of a toxic atmosphere amongst the fans at the moment as well, lots of them aren't happy, and that rubs off on the players too.

"So whichever manager they choose to come in and replace Sage, he will face all of the same issues at Crystal Palace, and it may not be a quick fix in the dugout."

With Sage expected to be sacked in the very near future, talk has pivoted towards who his replacement could be, with a number of high-profile bosses on Palace's radar.

The likes of Sean Dyche, Rob Edwards, Vitor Pereira and even Eddie Howe have been linked, although there are questions about who would be willing to take charge.

Football Insider revealed on 9 November that, with Sage now on the verge of being dismissed from his role, Thomas Frank has emerged as the clear front-runner.

Sources say Crystal Palace have already held talks with the former Spurs boss, notably following the 1-1 draw against Hoffenheim last Thursday and likely since then as well.

Frank has a number of admirers on the Crystal Palace board, and given his availability and willingness to return to management, he is expected to be the man approached.
