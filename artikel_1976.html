Sean Neave was experiencing a mixed start to life at Wolves after joining on loan from Newcastle United in the summer.

In his first seven Championship appearances, he had just one assist to his name and sparked concerns with three cautions in as many games.

At just 19 years of age, Neave arrived at Molineux Stadium with quite a lot of expectation on his shoulders. He hasn't delivered, at least until now.

Against Bristol City on Wednesday, 4 November, Scott Twine scored the opener at Ashton Gate, and it looks like the Old Gold were set for defeat.

That was until Cesar Peixoto rolled the dice, throwing Neave on for the final 25 minutes, netting just nine minutes after replacing Fer Lopez.

One point away from home would have been nothing to be ashamed of. But Wolves wanted more, and they got more, thanks to the young loanee.

He did incredibly well with the first goal, staying on his toes to latch onto the goalkeeper's parry, and fire home at the back post.

Then, in the 89th minute, Neave's diving header, again at the back post, sealed all three points in rather dramatic circumstances.

It was an amazing moment for the 19-year-old, who scored his first and second professional goals, and he enjoyed every single second of it.

It's Neave again! 🤯What a late comeback from Wolves! pic.twitter.com/M8HNya7pXu

There has been no mention of a recall clause in his current contract, meaning that Newcastle may have to wait until next season to bring him in.

But if there is any possibility of a mid-season loan termination, then the Premier League club should pull out all the stops to get that done.

Just a matter of days ago, it was assumed that Peixoto was just hours away from being sacked by the Wolves hierarchy.

Since then, the Old Gold have gone an incredible run, avoiding defeat in five games on the bounce, and even winning four of those.

Wolves are sitting second in the Championship table, one point behind Middlesbrough, with Wrexham up next at the SToK Cae Ras.

Many fans are not quite convinced by the manager just yet, but he's slowly turning things around at Molineux this season.
