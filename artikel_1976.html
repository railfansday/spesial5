Liverpool fans have Alexander Isak concerns for all the wrong reasons as data shows

Alexander Isak is doing the job he was brought to Liverpool to do, and that is scoring goals.

However, there are plenty of people out there who are becoming concerned with his lack of involvement throughout games.

Supporters spent many years seeing the work of Roberto Firmino leading the attack, while even the likes of Darwin Nunez were often hailed for his work rate too.

A striker pressing from the front has been synonymous with the Anfield side.

But, truthfully, that is not Isak's style, and it is no longer how Liverpool wants to play.

And it is working wonders for Andoni Iraola's side this season, with fans needing to take a step back from their concerns.

Liverpool are having a strong season under Iraola, but questions over Isak continue to be brought up.

Supporters growing frustrated with Isak's lack of involvement in games are completely missing the point.

This is no longer Jurgen Klopp's Liverpool, but rather how Iraola wants his team to play.

As per FotMob, Isak is on the lower end of touches per 90, coming in at the 11.4th percentile with 25.2.

But he is the second-top goalscorer in the Premier League with seven goals.

His job is to occupy positions and defenders, and ultimately make the most of the few touches he gets.

Not every striker needs to be heavily involved in build-up play, and there are different ways for Liverpool to play in a way that unites the fans.

Just because he is not touching the ball often does not mean he is not involved.

Ultimately, for Isak as well, Liverpool are not putting as much focus into their attacks down the middle of the pitch.

As WhoScored shares, the Reds frequently go down the left side much more often, with 39 per cent of their attacks on that wing.

The right-wing sees 32 per cent, while the centre is the lowest at 29 per cent.

This means that Isak is better off simply staying central, occupying defenders as opportunities for crosses are more likely to arrive.

So, the question truly is: would you rather a striker be involved in games, but get fewer goals, or have limited touches but still impact matches with his finishing?
