Italian chef fired after refusing to put pineapple on pizza

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

An Italian chef has been fired from a pizza restaurant in Australia after refusing to customize a pizza for a customer and put pineapple on it for their order.

There have been some divisive pizza toppings over the years, but the debate around putting pineapple on pizza is the fiercest. Some pizza restaurants see it as sacrilege and refuse to do it, while others are happy to serve up a Hawaiian slice to customers.

Over in Queensland, Australia, the La Costa restaurant – which is now closed – fired chef Davide Tamburella following a string of complaints from customers. One complaint, however, has caught attention more than others.

As per the BBC, reporting on a case from Australia’s Fair Work Commission, the chef refused to provide ham and pineapple pizza for guests at a private function.

According to the report, Tamburella refused to take direction and make the pizza as it “need not be followed when it is offensive to his culture”.

The Fair Work Commission was told that “he considers, as an Italian, that putting pineapple on a pizza is an insult to his culture” and had previously told his boss to “check how important it is for Italians not to put pineapple on a pizza”.

Tamburella lost his unfair dismissal claim as it was filed too late in the day, as per the BBC, but also “acknowledged use of inappropriate language and escalation of the situation during service.”

Elliot Ingram, managing director of La Costa Restaurant and Bar, told the Daily Mail there was “more to this story than a pizza topping” too, hinting at other complaints being made about the chef.

It wasn’t the first time that the chef had refused to make the custom pizza either. As per the report, he’d actually done it three times in four years.
