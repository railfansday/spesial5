Jordan Walsh continues to play himself into Boston’s lineup

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Going into Sunday's game against the Dallas Mavericks, I saw what Joe Mazzulla had in store for the Boston Celtics starting lineup. It was nothing out of the ordinary, but I found myself questioning why Jordan Walsh was still a part of it.

This was Walsh's eight start of the season out of 13 games, tied with Neemias Queta for the fourth most starts this season. The two of them trail only Jayson Tatum, Paul George, and Derrick White, the obvious core of the team.

Clearly, Mazzulla sees something in Jordan. Why else would he continue to trust him night in, and night out?

In Sunday's rout of the Mavericks, a 131-94 victory for the Celtics, Walsh showed exactly how he earned the trust of his coach. He's not going to be a big box score guy on a nightly basis, but he will still make his presence felt through grit, hard-nosed defense, and a whole lot of hustle.

Boston took a historic lead against Dallas in the first quarter, ending the period up 42 to 9, the second-largest first quarter scoring differential in NBA history. Jordan had his fingerprints all over the game as the Celtics built their lead.

From the jump, Boston's defense was hounding the Mavericks, with Jordan Walsh being at the center of several possessions. Though the Celtics defense relies on plenty of switching, Jordan mostly found himself picking up Cooper Flagg and Naji Marshall, the two primary ball-handlers for Dallas.

Walsh's matchups weren't bold enough to take a single field goal attempt in the quarter while he was the primary defender. Not only that, but Jordan was able to force two turnovers. He picked off a Marshall pass for Flagg which he took back for a tough transition bucket with a foul on one possession, and drew an illegal screen from Daniel Gafford not long after.

In addition to his on-ball defense, Walsh was incredibly active off the ball, stunting drives and showing his hands to disrupt the Mavericks possessions while giving his teammates an extra second to get back in step with their matchups.

When Jordan ultimately went to the bench for the first time, Boston held a 22-4 lead. All four of Dallas's points came at the freethrow line. Meanwhile, Walsh was responsible for eight of Boston's 22 points, tying his career-high for most points in the first quarter.

All of that being said, Jordan is far from a finished product. What he does have is an unbreakable spirit, and a desire to improve. He's still a few months away from turning 23 years old, so even though it feels like he's been around for a while, there's plenty of untapped potential in there which we can see shine through on nights like this.

Walsh made a rare podium appearance after the game, and provided us with some insight into how he views his role at this point. He explained how his value isn't defined by the box score, saying "it's never really about shots and points. It's about 'what can I create for the team?' Can I create a situation where they can't help off me? Can I create a situation where Cooper Flagg isn't bringing the ball up the court and it's somebody else? That's how I find my way into the game."

When asked about his confidence as a defender he said "I think I'm the best defender. Every game is guarding the best player, and obviously the team, the organization believes I can do that every night so that's why they put me on that guy. That in and of itself gives me the confidence to go out there and try to do the best I can."

Mazzulla gave some encouraging words to compliment that sentiment as well, saying "I think Jodan's continuing to develop the mindset that we need him to have versus great players. And a great team has to have a group of guys that they can rely on to make it difficult for the team's best player every single day… Jordan's on that list."

Walsh even got a shoutout from Tatum in the press room, who added that Jordan, along with Baylor Scheierman, Hugo Gonzalez, and Ron Harper Jr., is more than capable in the starting spot. "Whether it's on the defensive end, or knocking down shots, or crashing, those guys have been really, really good."

Clearly, the team is behind Walsh, and believes in his ability to contribute to the team. Don't be surprised while we continue to see him in the starting lineup, or even when he has a big night off the bench.
