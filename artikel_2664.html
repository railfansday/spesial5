Cowboys star Quinnen Williams has bizarre press conference with drama building

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Quinnen Williams might not have enough cachet to channel his inner "I'm just here so I don't get fined" or "We're onto Cincinnati."

The Cowboys' prized defensive lineman refused to answer questions from the media Wednesday, repeatedly replying to questions about Dallas' bottom-of-the-league defense with "I'm gonna keep that to myself" as they change defensive play-callers from Christian Parker to Mike Pettine.

Williams was asked 12 questions about Pettine and how the defense can improve and he gave the same answer all 12 times as he stood in front of reporters.

It is unclear if Williams is unhappy with the decision to switch from Parker to Pettine, but the media strategy certainly raises questions about why Williams appeared to be irritated by these questions.

He refused to answer questions such as: "What can this team accomplish the rest of the season?"; "What gives you confidence that with [Pettine] calling the plays that you guys can turn it around?"; and "So, nothing at all on Pettine today?"

Williams did answer some questions, just none about Pettine.

When asked about this upcoming week, Williams responded, "That's the most important thing is to win, and nothing else matters."

Williams answered another question related to Parker and how much responsibility of the defense falls on the players rather than the play caller.

"Everything is on us. We on the field. No matter what play get called, no matter who call it, we got to execute the play," Williams said in his longest response of the session.

"Once the play get called, no matter who call it, the players on the field got to execute it," Williams said. "And that's, that's what we haven't been doing. We got to do it going forward."

Dallas Cowboys senior defensive assistant Mike Pettine, left, confers with defensive coordinator Christian Parker, Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026, in Frisco, Texas. AP Photo/LM Otero

We've seen variations of this media playbook before, but this one raises eyebrows about Williams' relationship with Pettine.

Marshawn Lynch refused to answer questions at the 2015 Super Bowl Media Day by saying, "I'm just here so I don't get fined," and legendary Patriots coach Bill Belichick infamously said "We're onto Cincinnati" after an early 2014 loss that raised questions about quarterback Tom Brady.

It's a strange strategy from the Cowboys' interior defensive lineman, who has had a solid season despite playing on a terrible defensive team after being traded from the Jets midseason.

Williams has six sacks and three forced fumbles in 10 games this season for a unit ranked No. 31 in defensive DVOA this year.

Pettine was added in Week 5 by coach Brian Schottenheimer to be a "senior defensive assistant" as Parker's defense began to struggle.

Pettine's presence hasn't helped yet, as Dallas is allowing 6.1 yards per play, the second-highest number in the league.

The Cowboys face the Titans on Sunday in a must-win game against the No. 32 defensive DVOA squad.
