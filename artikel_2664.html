Green Bay Packers News: Micah Parsons is ready to win

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

For the second time in as many years, we excitedly await the arrival of Micah Parsons.

In 2025, Parsons was a surprise addition to the Green Bay Packers. Acquired in a late-summer trade, nobody knew what he'd bring to the Packers after sitting out virtually all of training camp. He didn't start, but he did contribute, immediately energizing the Packers' pass rush and taking down Jared Goff late in the Packers' season-opening win over the Detroit Lions.

Now, in 2026, we await Parsons under much different circumstances. He tore his ACL a little less than a year ago, scuttling the Packers' season and setting himself on a long road to recovery. The idea is that he can still energize the Packers' pass rush, but there are different questions at hand.

What kind of player will he be, post-ACL? Will his knee hold up? How many snaps will he play? Will he ever be the same as he was before he hurt his knee?

Nobody knows the answers, but they're still crucially important questions, and the process of finding out will determine a lot of the rest of the Packers season.

Or will it? That's the other thing at play: as transformational as Parsons is, he doesn't solve the Packers' biggest issue. The offensive line is still in shambles, and I don't think the plan is to put Parsons there.

That'd be the ultimate irony: Parsons returns and is every bit the player the Packers hoped he could be, even after his injury, but it doesn't matter. If that's how things go, it won't be a surprise, but it still would be a disappointment.
