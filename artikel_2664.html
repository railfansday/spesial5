Packers add 2, drop 1 on Tuesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

As expected, the Green Bay Packers signed receiver Coleman Owen back to the practice squad after he cleared waivers unclaimed on Monday. Owen was the first player the Packers have cut from the 53-man active roster this season, and they opened a practice squad spot on Monday by releasing defensive lineman David Blay Jr.

Beyond just that move, though, the Packers also made a change with their international exemption slot on the practice squad. Gone is kicker Lenny Krieg, who has been with the team this entire regular season. The team has added Haggai Ndubuisi, a defensive lineman from Nigeria, to replace Krieg.

Ndubuisi, a former offensive lineman, has previously been on the rosters of the Arizona Cardinals, Denver Broncos, San Antonio Brahmas (UFL), Washington Commanders, New England Patriots, Houston Texans and Tampa Bay Buccaneers. Earlier this season, he worked out for the Indianapolis Colts, Minnesota Vikings, Buffalo Bills and Las Vegas Raiders, prior to his visit to Green Bay on Monday.

According to a source, Ndubuisi was measured at 6'7", 323 pounds and had 36" arms at the IPP's pro day back in 2023. He was active for the Texans in one game (Week 14) last season.

Ndubuisi's time in New England also overlapped with DeMarcus Covington's time there. Covington, now the Packers' outside linebackers coach/run game coordinator/assistant head coach – defense, was the defensive coordinator of the Patriots at the time.

After these three moves, here's what the Packers' practice squad looks like going into their bye week:

WR: Coleman Owen, Isaiah Neyor and Quincy Skinner Jr.

DL: Mazi Smith and Haggai Ndubuisi (international exemption)

ED: Nyjalik Kelly, Arron Mosby and D.J. Johnson
