Sixers single out VJ Edgecombe for impact in other areas after the win

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

NEW YORK -- It's been documented a number of times throughout the 2026-27 season, but VJ Edgecombe has continued to make an impact for the Philadelphia 76ers despite not being involved as much in terms of his usage percentage as a rookie in the previous year.

Edgecombe had 14 points, nine rebounds, four assists, and two steals in Monday's win over the Brooklyn Nets and he was impactful on the defensive end of the floor. He set the tone in the rebounding department and did a number of great things to contribute to winning for Philadelphia.

"I was most impressed with the way he was getting through and over screens," coach Nick Nurse said of Edgecombe. "He just was constantly getting screened and he was just bodying through them, keeping his man right there, just sticking to him really closely. And I thought that was a good tone setting part of our defense by him."

Getting through and over screens is something that will not be shown in the box score. It's not a contribution that can be measured by any type of numbers, but Edgecombe was able to get over screens and bother the Nets out on the perimeter. His point of attack defense has been something that has been noticeable on a nightly basis while also giving Philadelphia a big boost in the rebounding department.

Edgecombe had five rebounds in the first quarter as the Sixers were able to set the tone against a young, but scrappy Nets team. He has been a real help in that area and Philadelphia has benefited from his improvement in that area.

"That's an emphasis area for me," Edgecombe explained. "I just try to get as much rebound as I can kind of try to speak selfishly. I'm able to obviously start the break or push the ball, do whatever I got to do, or just go coast to coast. So, selfishly, I like getting rebounds."

The Sixers will continue to push Edgecombe forward as they grow as a team and continue to build chemistry and nail down roles. Their next chance will be on Wednesday against the Orlando Magic to finish a road trip.
