The World of Hyatt personal and business credit cards can help you save money and enjoy valuable perks on your next Hyatt stay.

Right now, the personal card is offering its most lucrative bonus since February, making now a good time to apply.

The World of Hyatt program is known for offering some of the best redemption rates in the industry, along with a diverse portfolio of distinctive hotel brands.

Let's dive into these offers and help you decide whether to apply.

The (see rates and fees) is currently offering new cardholders four free nights (at Category 1-4 Hyatt hotels) after spending $5,000 on purchases in the first three months from account opening.

Per Hyatt's award chart, Category 1 Hyatt hotels can cost as few as 3,000 points per night, while Category 4 properties can cost as many as 25,000 points per night.

That means the value of this welcome offer depends on how you redeem the free nights. If you use all four certificates at Category 4 properties that would otherwise cost 25,000 points per night, you'd "use" 100,000 points. Based on TPG's November 2026 valuations, that puts the bonus's potential value at up to $1,650.

While this isn't the strongest welcome offer we've seen in the card's history, it does meet our threshold for when to apply.

Why we like it: This card has valuable ongoing perks, the best of which is an annual free night certificate at a Category 1-4 Hyatt property, which you’ll receive after every account anniversary. You’ll also get a second free night after spending $15,000 in a calendar year.

Both the World of Hyatt personal and business cards offer plenty of benefits for Hyatt loyalists. In fact, the Hyatt personal card ranks among the top picks of TPG staffers.

Having either card automatically grants you Discoverist status, which includes benefits such as late checkout, preferred rooms and a 10% points bonus on eligible purchases. Spending on either card can also earn you additional elite-qualifying nights.

You’ll also enjoy several travel protections, such as baggage delay insurance, lost luggage reimbursement and trip interruption and cancellation insurance.

Plus, the personal card comes with a free night certificate every anniversary year (valid at Category 1-4 properties), which is extremely valuable for any Hyatt enthusiast.
