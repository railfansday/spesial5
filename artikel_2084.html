Stefan Borson can't believe Man City could re-sign James Trafford from Leeds

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

James Trafford is unlikely to want another Manchester City return after a positive start to life at Leeds United this season.

That is according to former Man City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that rumours about another return are "ridiculous".

The Citizens sold Trafford to Leeds in a £40million deal during the summer, but also included a sell-on fee and a right of first refusal in case of a sale.

Some supporters are already urging the club to trigger a move for the 24-year-old shot-stopper, as concerns around Gianluigi Donnarumma emerge.

Borson labelled the Italy international as a "big" and "very expensive" problem after a handful of costly errors in between the sticks.

His latest came in the 3-2 win over RB Leipzig, being beaten at his near post by right-back Ridle Baku, sparking cries for Trafford to be re-signed.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson is doubtful that Trafford is the answer to Donnarumma, and doesn't believe that a return is on the cards anyway.

"Anything's possible in the future, but nothing's going to happen until the summer, that's for sure," he explained.

"I'm not sure Trafford will want to come back again. It's starting to get a little bit ridiculous to come back three times. But it's not impossible.

"I would think there's so much uncertainty around the City situation, it's not really worth worrying too much about what the squad looks like next season.

"And I'm not sure Trafford is necessarily the answer. I think it's a bit more complicated than that."

Trafford boasts one of the highest save percentages in the Premier League this season, but he isn't without his faults.

At 24 years of age, though, the England international has plenty of time to iron out his errors, which were on show against Bournemouth.

Leeds continued to play out from the back against the Cherries on Saturday, 31 October, and that was eventually punished by Evanilson.

Trafford loves to play with his feet, and in the modern world, that's needed in a goalkeeper. But he needs to know when to just clear the danger.
