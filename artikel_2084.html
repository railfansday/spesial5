Consistent Middlesbrough player lauded by reporter for ‘another brilliant display’ against Swansea City

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Middlesbrough beat the Swans 2-1 at the Riverside Stadium on Tuesday night in their latest Championship outing. Myles Peart-Harris and Will Lankshear got their goals as they secured another impressive three points.

Brittain, 28, is a key player for Boro and has been ever-present for Kim Hellberg‘s side so far this season. He has made 20 appearances in all competitions, 17 of which have come in the league, and he has chipped in with one goal and two assists from the back.

Journalist Johns was impressed with his performance against Swansea City and he has delivered his verdict, as per a report by TeesideLive: “Another brilliant display, doing both his defensive and attacking work. His ball through for Peart-Harris unlocked the defence for Boro’s second goal.”

Middlesbrough are positioned top of the table above Wolves by a point after their first 17 fixtures of the campaign and they have lost only two matches. They are back in action on Friday night with an away trip to St Mary’s to take on Southampton as they aim to keep their momentum going for as long as possible.

Middlesbrough signed Brittain back in 2025 and he has since done a steady job for them. Prior to his move, he had spells at MK Dons, Barnsley and Blackburn Rovers.

The right-back, who is an ex-England youth international, is a dependable option for Boro to call upon. He has kept summer recruit Sam Byram out of the team in this campaign so far.

Brittain is an experienced operator in the EFL who has played over 350 games. He will be a useful individual to have in and around the dressing room.

The Bedford-born man has never played in the top flight before. Therefore, he will be eager to go up with Middlesbrough this term and test himself against some of the best players in the world.

Brittain helped Middlesbrough reach the play-off final last season. They lost to Hull City at Wembley back in May though.

Nevertheless, they have bounced back from their defeat to the Tigers well though. Boro look well on the way to going up automatically this time around.

Hellberg’s men need to do all they can to avoid the play-offs and go up via the top two route. They are finding ways to win games at the moment and in Lankshear, they have a proper number nine who knows where the back of the net is.

Middlesbrough lacked that cutting edge up top in the past campaign. Their decision to bring in Lankshear from Tottenham Hotspur has turned out to be a masterstroke.
