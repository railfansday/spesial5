Talk SPORT reveal comes amidst Scott Parker’s darkest days | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

There’s talk, behind the scenes, at West Ham regarding the Nuno era that came before and the differences that’ve taken hold under new coach Scott Parker. If anything, on the pitch West Ham have got worse under their former player-turned-head-coach as the slide down the second division continues.

Even with former Hammers legend Mark Noble on the coaching staff, the players look lost and at times without fight or character. Which comes as a shock to supporters who had expected the opposite would be the case with the hugely popular duo at the helm.

As footballers they excelled – but they’re being tested like never before. They still deserve the fans’ support. In my humble opinion. Disagree? Go for it, That’s what we have a ‘comments’ section for.

However, the talk is, apparently, not all doom and gloom.

TalkSPORT’ sAlex Crook, reporting during the Portsmouth v West Ham game, claimed his own exclusive:

“I was speaking to a couple of West Ham staff and they were saying, even if the results aren’t coming, the general atmosphere is a lot better than it was under Nuno.”

If that is the case and there’s progress off the pitch then we as fans will just have to suck it up and get behind this group. They’re the only 26 available until January and whilst they aren’t very good, the majority are at least, trying.

Noble and Parker have to pick the eleven who will try their hearts out and run over hot coals for their manager and their supporters.

At least then the players won’t hang back for fear of being abused amidst away-end ‘ugly scenes‘ after the final whistle as the BBC reported.

That surely has to stop, however frustrated supporters feel. It won’t make the players perform any better. The opposite, in fact.
