Femi Azeez signs: What West Brom’s perfect January transfer window looks like

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

When the January transfer window opens up, West Brom will be hoping they are still in the promotion mix to attract some desired targets.

The Baggies have been excellent under James Morrison so far this season, going into the latest international break unbeaten in their last six league games and securing four victories in that time.

Their latest 3-0 romping of Millwall last weekend leaves Albion fifth in the second-tier standings, sitting just one point off the summit in such a compact top eight.

It’s been a remarkable turnaround under Morrison, particularly when you factor in that the Black Country outfit underwent somewhat of a revamp this summer, recruiting 10 new players, many of whom had never played a minute in the Championship before their moves to The Hawthorns.

And with the Baggies setting their sights on ending a six-year exodus from the Premier League, showing ambition in the January window could be what gets them over the line in that quest.

All connected with Albion will be hoping it’s a statement window for bolstering key areas of the squad, with the attacking departments in particular still needing to be strengthened.

So, with this in mind, FLW has detailed three players West Brom should sign to make it a perfect January transfer window.

During the January transfer window, a department West Brom must address is on the flanks.

Starting off on the right-hand side, Femi Azeez could be a signing of intent given his previous Championship exploits with Millwall.

His heroics at the Den last season earned him a move to Premier League outfit Brighton, signing on the dotted line for a reported fee of £12.5m.

However, a hamstring injury means he is yet to feature for Fabian Hurzeler’s side, although Azeez is believed to be closing in on a return to full fitness.

However, with Brighton flying high in the Premier League, it’s hard to see where Azeez will fit in once he comes back, with Hurzeler not wanting to disrupt a team that is in full flow and playing so well.

Meanwhile, Yankuba Minteh is now back involved after his own injury issues, and, given his quality, he will presumably stay ahead of Azeez in the pecking order. Not to mention Kaoru Mitoma is scheduled to return this month as well, so it’s difficult to see the 25-year-old getting much, if any game time.

Therefore, a loan move may well be on the cards in January, and a return to the Championship could be exactly what Azeez needs to get back playing regularly and rediscover his old form.

After scoring 11 goals and registering eight assists from 35 Championship games last season, Azeez has proven to be such a livewire in the second tier.

And with the Baggies needing players to hit the ground running, Azeez is more than capable of doing that given his previous pedigree.

Azeez is a player who could add an extra gear to Albion’s attacking line, while the Black Country are in need of reinforcements there given Kareem Tunde is the only recognised right-winger on the books at this moment in time.

Over on the opposite wing, George Hemmings could be an ideal player to come in and share minutes with Mikey Johnston.

The Republic of Ireland international is yet to play a minute in the Championship so far after suffering a reoccurrence of his ankle problem that ruled him out of the majority of Morrison’s caretaker reign towards the end of last season.

He’s expected to return to the squad imminently but during the Christmas period and certainly over the new year, Albion are going to have to be careful with his minutes as they build up his fitness.

And a younger understudy could be the perfect arrival to come and take the burden off Johnston, with West Brom credited with interest in Hemmings by the Express & Star back in July.

The Villains ultimately didn’t sanction any exit due to Hemmings’ regular role in the first team at the beginning of the campaign, but further summer signings at Villa Park have since settled in and pushed him down the pecking order.

Hemmings came off the bench for 19 minutes of Villa's dramatic 4-4 draw with Manchester United last weekend, but hadn’t featured for the team in their previous four league matches, so the 19-year-old has a chance of heading for the exit door on loan in 2027.

While the Baggies haven’t had the most prosperous partnership with Aston Villa when it comes to loaning in their players, Hemmings has the potential to be a star in the Championship, with Unai Emery’s trust to throw him in for their UEFA Super Cup clash with Paris Saint-Germain and the beginning of their Premier League campaign speaking volumes about the talent he possesses.

Such a sharp and technically gifted player, Hemmings is another wideman who could have a massive impact on the potency of Albion’s forward line, and given their previous interest in securing his services, it’s a loan deal that could well progress when the January window opens.

As well as bolstering out wide, the arrival of another striker is what West Brom didn’t manage to do in the summer and something they must rectify in January.

The Baggies were busy during the summer window revamping some of the forward line, replacing the outgoings of Josh Maja and Daryl Dike with Barney Stewart and Jimmy-Jay Morgan.

Morgan in particular has been a roaring success so far, grabbing eight goal contributions in his opening 15 league matches for the club, but is not a conventional centre-forward, drifting over to the left-hand side while a proper number nine stays central.

Pundit Tom Garratt has urged the Baggies to do all they can to sign Andri Gudjohnsen in January.

In contrast, the more traditional centre-forwards, like Stewart, have found opportunities hard to come by, netting just once for his new club so far, while Aune Heggebo in his second season at the club has also found himself in and out of the side, with most of his success coming after being introduced from the bench.

Thankfully, much of Albion’s early success has been down to players in other positions chipping in with goals, but over time, that’s at risk of drying up, so Albion must do all they can at the turn of the new year to bring in a forward who has an eye for goal and can make a real difference when leading the line.

And a name that could well be on West Brom’s shortlist is Ellis Simms, who himself has struggled to gain regular minutes for Coventry City in the Premier League.

It won’t be a name that generates a lot of excitement among the Albion faithful, but after scoring 13 times in the second-tier last season, Simms has shown he’s a player who contributes goals in the Championship at a steady rate and could keep the Albion team ticking over with enough strikes to keep them in the promotion conversation.

At the same time, it’s a realistic deal for the Baggies to achieve, with the club likely to be eyeing up a loan deal for any striker in January after much of their budget went towards some of their summer rebuild.

Simms has only featured four times in the top-flight for the Sky Blues so far this term, and with a move to The Hawthorns not far away from his current employers, it would be a surprise to not see the former Everton frontman linked with a move to B71.

Proven at Championship level and physically suited to the division, Simms is a player Albion could depend on for the second half of the season, with the team needing that extra bit more at the top end of the pitch to keep up their high-flying start.
