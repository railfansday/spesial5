God of War (2018) Now I understand why people talk about it so much and why it's held in the highest regards. Everything blew my expectations out of the water. There's so much more to the game, multiple troops onscreen, chainswords, boltguns, bit-boxes, paint pots, power-ups and more dangerous enemies.
