Zero Hour for PS5, Xbox Series launches December 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series versions of tactical first-person shooter Zero Hour will launch on December 3 for $24.99, developer AttritoM7 Productions announced.

Zero Hour first launched in Early Access for PC via Steam on August 12, 2020, followed by a full release on September 9, 2024. It is also available via Epic Games Store.

The console versions will feature full cross-play support, as well as include six premium Weapon Aesthetics Packs—Gold, Platinum, Titanium, Redline, Serpentine, and Savanna—at no additional cost.

“Zero Hour has come a long way since we first released it in Early Access back in 2020, and then the full release four years later,” said Zero Hour director Meheraj Maruf in a press release. “Seeing our game finally make its way to PlayStation and Xbox honestly feels a bit unreal. It’s something we could only dream of when we first started. We have a lot to thank our community for, especially those who have supported us throughout the years and continue to play the game to this day. We wouldn’t have reached this point without them. A huge thanks to Skymap for helping us make this possible, and I really hope everyone has a great time with the game.”

Here is an overview of the game, via AttritoM7 Productions:

Jump in and experience true online tactical gameplay. We want to thank our community for the tremendous support and patience everyone has shown. Join our discord and get closer to the community and the game’s development. We hope you enjoy the game!

Welcome to Zero Hour, a tactical first-person shooter with online team-based action gameplay that is set across various locations in Bangladesh with real-life scale and resource management. Join forces with up to 10 players in intense cooperative missions, or go solo with up to nine AI squad mates. Experience hostage rescue and bomb defusal missions that require calculated strategies in the dynamic five-versus-five player-versus-player mode. Shape your route through compelling situations by interacting with story-driven NPCs and engaging with various objectives.

Engage in intense five-versus-five player-versus-player battles across various game modes, including bomb defusal, hostage rescue, and deathmatch. Experience competitive gameplay with a comprehensive leaderboard and tier system. Choose your loadout wisely, as limited guns and gadgets add a strategic layer—you’ll lose them upon dying, making every decision critical.

Zero Hour presents serious slow-paced, and methodically intense gameplay. The game offers authentic gunplay and scenarios, where quick thinking and strategic execution are essential for victory. Utilize the planning table to overview operations and formulate your approach before taking action. Remember, every move counts, and success demands teamwork, strategy, and precision.

Innovative Gameplay Elements and Weapon Customization

Explore over 18 diverse maps set across various locations in Bangladesh, featuring unique elements like functional elevators, bulletproof glass, and dynamic shutters for strategic advantages. Strategically utilize the breaker switch to control building lights, adding tactical depth to your missions.

Choose from over 23 distinct weapons and 18 utilities, emphasizing resource management and team coordination. Fine-tune your arsenal with detailed weapon customization, modifying rails, sights, and foregrips for optimal performance. Immerse yourself in realism by checking magazine bullet counts and repacking magazines for efficiency.

Doors are your lifeline and your barrier. Infiltrate with caution as terrorists set traps and alarms. Meanwhile, attackers have an arsenal of over 4 breach options: kick, door rammer, C2 explosives, and back kick combined with grenades, ensuring a variety of strategies to navigate these vital entry points.

Face a variety of suspects, each with distinct behaviors. Some even wield suicide bombs, while others utilize sniper tactics in long-range maps. They even have the ability to sense incoming fire and strategically time their advances ensuring maximum safety. The dynamic nature of suspects keeps you on your toes, with no guarantee of their next move.

The K9 Attack Dog feature brings an immersive and emotional experience to your missions. As the game starts, you’re assigned as a dog handler, initiating your journey with a small, nameable puppy. Initially resting in a cage, the puppy soon becomes interactive and starts playing around, following the player through the training facility. After successfully completing three missions, the canine companion becomes available for mission deployment.
