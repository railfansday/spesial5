Deborah Ann Woll Reprising Role As God Of War’s Faye In Prime Video’s TV Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Deborah Ann Woll, who plays Faye in the upcoming God of War Laufey, is coming back to play the character again in Prime Video's God of War show.

She will star in the show alongside Dave Bautista as Kratos. Sons of Anarchy actor Ryan Hurst was originally signed on to play him, but he suffered a terrible injury and the role was recast with Bautista.

Woll was the leading choice to play Faye, Deadline reported., though other names that might have been in the mix are not available.

Ólafur Darri Ólafsson (Severance) will play Thor in the God of War TV show, taking over for Hurst, who played Thor in the latest God of War games.

The God of War cast also includes Teresa Palmer as Sif, Max Parker as Heimdall, Mandy Patinkin as Odin, and Alastair Duncan as Memir, reprising his role from God of War Ragnarok. Callum Vinson plays Atreus.

The God of War TV show will follow some of the events of 2018’s God of War and 2022’s God of War Ragnarok, telling the father-son story of Kratos and Atreus.

As for the game series, God of War Laufey was recently delayed to May 2027. A new Kratos-led game is coming after that, and it will pick up where Laufey left off.
