Save $70 on the Kindle Paperwhite in Amazons huge Black Friday sale

SAVE $70: As of Nov. 19, the 2024 Kindle Paperwhite (16GB) is on sale for $129.99 in Amazon's Black Friday sale. That's $70 off its full cost of $199.99.

If a Kindle is on your holiday wishlist this year, Black Friday sales are the perfect time to track one down for less.

This is a $70 price cut, which is good to see after the model was hit by a price bump this year. And while a new version of this Kindle may have dropped this year, we still have a lot of love for the 2024 release. Mashable's Samantha Mangino said it's "as good as an e-reader can get" in her review, thanks to its "responsive interface, quick-turning pages, and lightweight design."

The Kindle Paperwhite features a seven-inch, glare-free display with adjustable brightness and warmth, so your eyes can stay glued to your favorite books while you're out in the sun or late at night. Its thin design is a huge bonus for travelers, making it easy to pack in a bag to take your digital library on the go, and it's even waterproof for the pool or beach.

There's no need to worry about it running out of battery. This model can last up to 12 weeks on a single charge, which is fantastic news for avid readers. Plus, with 16GB of storage, you have plenty of space available to fill up with your favorite titles (check out some of the best books of 2026, according to Amazon editors).
