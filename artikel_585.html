Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie Settle Winery Lawsuit That Revealed Abuse Allegations

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Brad Pitt has settled his legal battle with ex-wife Angelina Jolie over the sale of her stake in the couple’s winery in France.

Pitt sued Jolie in 2022, accusing her of selling her 50% share in Château Miraval to a subsidiary of Stoli, the Russian spirits company, without Pitt’s approval. The litigation drew attention to Jolie’s allegation that Pitt abused her and the couple’s children on a private plane in 2016, precipitating the couple’s divorce. “We have settled out of court in the best interests of our family and will not be commenting further,” representatives for Jolie and Pitt said in a statement on Wednesday. “Any comments in media stories attributed to ‘sources’ are in fact not coming from either of us, or anyone close to us.”

Terms of the agreement were not disclosed. Pitt has also sued Yuri Shefler, the Russian-born billionaire who owns the Stoli Group. That portion of the litigation is ongoing.

Pitt and Jolie bought the Miraval estate in 2008, and were married there in 2014. They invested tens of millions of dollars in the 1,300-acre property in order to turn Château Miraval into a profitable rosé brand.

Jolie filed for divorce in 2016, and the fate of the winery has been in dispute ever since. Pitt has argued that the company was his “passion,” that he poured his energies into making it a success, and that Jolie has forced him into an unworkable arrangement with Stoli, a competitor. In a cross-complaint filed in 2022, Jolie accused Pitt attempting to seize control from her and of “looting” it for other ventures.

“Pitt is an actor, not a winemaker. He deals in illusions, not dirt and grapes,” Jolie’s lawyers wrote. “While he no doubt visited the vineyards to admire the work of the French laborers who actually made the business successful, Pitt is no vigneron.”

According to her cross-complaint, Jolie initially planned to sell her half of the winery to Pitt. But in 2021, the deal fell apart. According to her, Pitt conditioned the buyout on her willingness to sign a non-disclosure agreement about the domestic abuse incident that led to their divorce.

The cross-complaint alleged that during a flight from France to the U.S., Pitt drunkenly grabbed her by the head and pushed her into a wall of the plane. The suit also claimed that Pitt lunged at one of the couple’s children, poured beer on Jolie and the children as they tried to sleep, and then refused to let the family leave the plane for 20 minutes after it landed.

Her complaint alleged that Pitt “sought to use Château Miraval as leverage to coerce Jolie into keeping quiet about Pitt’s behavior. This new demand essentially imposed a poison pill, all but ensuring that no deal could ever be reached.”

Rather than accept that condition, Jolie sold her stake to Stoli. She alleged that the couple had no agreement not to sell to a third party.

A judge denied Jolie’s demurrer in November 2024, finding that Pitt’s claims were adequately pled.

The parties have since been engaged in disputes over access to financial records and over jurisdictional issues. Pitt has accused Stoli and Shefler of attempting a “hostile takeover” of the wine business. In June, an appellate court revived Pitt’s case against them, finding that Shefler, a Swiss resident, can be sued in California.
