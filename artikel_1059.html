Swansea investigate after naked image of player is shared on social media ahead of grudge match with bitter rivals Cardiff

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Published: 12:15 EST, 26 November 2026 | Updated: 14:05 EST, 26 November 2026

Swansea City have launched an internal investigation after an inappropriate image of one of its players was shared on social media.

The image, which emerged on X, formerly Twitter, on Wednesday night, appeared to show a Swansea City player naked standing beside a bed.

The photo, first taken on Snapchat, has since disappeared online after violating the social media site’s rules.

A Swansea City spokesperson confirmed to Daily Mail Sport that the club are investigating the photo internally but no further comment will be made at this time.

The photo, which was first shared by a Cardiff City fan, has emerged just days before this Sunday’s South Wales derby between Swansea and Cardiff, with tensions between both fanbases high now that they are back in the same division.

While Swansea plan to speak to the player to establish the key facts behind the image, there is also a legal side that cannot be ignored.

In England and Wales it is a criminal offence to share naked or intimate photos without consent under Section 66B of the Sexual Offences Act 2003.

Should an investigation determine the image has been tampered with, including AI-generated or manipulated 'deepfakes,' which was one of the allegations on social media on Wednesday night, that too is a criminal act under the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.
