Child Dies of Measles in Wisconsin's First Such Death Since 1990

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

— Four measles-associated deaths confirmed by CDC this year, but states report higher numbers

An infant in southwestern Wisconsin has died after contracting measles, the state's first death from the disease in 36 years, state officials announced Thursday.

The coroner listed the cause of death as measles, state health officials said.

"My heart is broken for the family," said Kirsten Johnson, MPH, secretary of the state health department. "Any death of a child is a tragedy and even more so when it is related to a vaccine-preventable disease."

The U.S. stopped the domestic spread of measles in 2000 by maintaining high vaccination rates, but they have fallen in recent years to below the 95% coverage needed to prevent outbreaks of the viral disease.

The CDC has confirmed four measles-associated deaths in the U.S. this year, but state health departments have reported higher numbers. In Pennsylvania alone, health officials have reported five deaths this year and are investigating a possible sixth.

As of Thursday, Wisconsin reported 431 cases of measles, all but three in unvaccinated people. Nine people had been hospitalized.
