Stop Porch Theft – Route Packages to a Locker or Pickup Counter for Free

Package thieves can’t take a box that never sits on your porch. Have your orders held at a locker, a store’s pickup counter, or your Post Office. Choose the pickup point at checkout or in each carrier’s free online account. For boxes that still come home, tell the driver where to leave them out of sight.

A $300 order left at your door at 10 a.m. sits in view of the street until you get home from work. Sent to a locker two blocks away, the same box waits locked up until you collect it.

You can make that switch yourself. Amazon offers lockers at checkout, and UPS, FedEx, and USPS each let you send a box to a pickup point through a free online account.

UPS and FedEx pickup locations hold a box up to seven days. When a box has to come home, each carrier’s free account lets you tell the driver where to leave it, such as a side door or your building’s office. FedEx also delivers between 5 and 8 p.m. for $5.55 a package.

Report a stolen box to the seller right away. UPS pays claims to the shipper, not to you, so the seller is the one who can replace or refund the order. Then check the card you paid with for purchase protection, which can cover a stolen purchase.

Set the UPS preference once, and redirect each FedEx or USPS box the day its tracking number arrives. USPS refuses Delivery Instructions for a package already out for delivery, and FedEx says a box redirected by midnight the day before delivery should be ready for pickup on delivery day.
