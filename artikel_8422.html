Choose Consumer Cellular if you’re 50 or older, mostly call and text, and want help from a person by phone. Check AT&T’s coverage at your address, and give your AARP number at sign-up for 5% off monthly service.

Consumer Cellular sells no-contract service on AT&T’s network, with plans and phones aimed at people 50 and older. In the American Customer Satisfaction Index’s 2026 study, its customers gave it 84 out of 100, the highest score of any wireless carrier rated.

Consumer Cellular is worth it for someone 50 or older who mostly calls and texts and wants a U.S.-based person to call for help. Heavy data users 55 and older should compare T-Mobile’s 55+ plan, whose first 50GB a month gets priority on a busy network, and anyone wanting a button that reaches 24/7 response agents should look at Lively’s Jitterbug Flip3.
