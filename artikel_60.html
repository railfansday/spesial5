Deutsche Bank leaks: German lender's dealings with Russian oligarchs under scrutiny

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Germany's finance minister Tuesday urged "full transparency" from Deutsche Bank after international media outlets published reports based on millions of leaked documents that shine a light on questionable past business practices at the banking giant. They include ties to Russian oligarchs, the convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein, and Donald Trump before he became US president. France 24's Douglas Herbert takes a closer look.
