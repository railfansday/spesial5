English village votes to "leave UK"

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Residents of the small English village of Barnham voted on Saturday in a symbolic referendum to leave the UK, in protest against government plans to house 931 asylum seekers at a nearby former air force base – more than the village’s own population of 584. The vote follows a similar protest in another village in September, highlighting growing tensions over immigration across the country. Earlier this week, the leader of a far-right anti-immigration group was jailed, but supporters have vowed to keep the movement alive. Jack Pownall reports.
