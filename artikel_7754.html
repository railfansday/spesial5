Attorney General Todd Blanche Honors 10 District of Hawaii Assistant U.S. Attorneys, Staff, and Agents at 2026 Director’s Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

HONOLULU – United States Attorney Ken Sorenson announced that Assistant U.S. Attorney Michael D. Nammar, Assistant U.S. Attorney Aislinn K. Affinito, Victim Assistance Specialist Leilani Uganiza, Supervisory IT Specialist Cheryl Tagaca, and Supervisory Paralegal Specialist Kari Sherman, all of the U.S. Attorney’s Office for the District of Hawaii, as well as Homeland Security Investigations Special Agent Murray Acosta, IRS Criminal Investigation Special Agent Shaun Morita, Environmental Protection Agency Special Agent Jan Yukumoto, FBI Special Agent Thomas Palmer, and FBI Special Agent Joshua Parker were among 183 Department of Justice employees recognized by Attorney General Todd Blanche and Executive Office for U.S. Attorneys (EOUSA) Director Francey Hakes at the 2026 Director’s Awards Ceremony held October 28, 2026, in the Great Hall of the Robert F. Kennedy Department of Justice Building in Washington, D.C.
