Motorola confirms list of devices getting Android 17 updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Motorola has posted an updated list of devices getting its Android 17 update, with a few updates already available and many more left to go.

With the update now rolling out to select devices, most recently the Razr Fold, Motorola is confirming an official list of devices getting Android 17.

The list was posted by Motorola Brazil, so this includes devices Motorola doesn’t sell in US markets, and also lists Razr devices without their year naming scheme, rather their international branding – i.e. “Razr (2026)” is “Razr 70.”

As per usual, Motorola’s update rollouts may see timing vary by region. The Razr Fold update, for example, is currently rolling out in some international markets but has yet to land in the US.

Motorola explains what’s new in Android 17 as follows:

Among the new features of Android 17 are improvements to the system interface, with more customization options, new icons, and changes to the layout of buttons and widgets. The Settings menu has been redesigned to make navigation more intuitive and facilitate access to the smartphone’s main functions.

With the update, Circle to Search now offers real-time translations, allowing you to understand content in other languages ​​more easily, without having to interrupt your current activity on your smartphone.

Android 17 enhances connectivity with Instant Hotspot , which simplifies sharing your internet connection between compatible devices. Quick Share has also received improvements to facilitate file transfer, including compatibility with iOS smartphones via QR Code.

Security remains a top priority for the system. The Authentication Failure Lock , a security feature in case of theft, locks the screen after unsuccessful authentication attempts. Meanwhile, Message-Based Fraud Detection enhances the identification of potential threats and fraud. Furthermore, access to emergency calls has become easier and faster.

The update also improves HDR brightness control, which adjusts the screen intensity to highlight bright details in compatible photos and videos. Users can adjust this brightness for more comfortable viewing, or even disable the feature.

The Motorola Signature 27, the company’s new flagship, ships with Android 17 out of the box.
