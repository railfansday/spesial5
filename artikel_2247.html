Why West Brom’s £2m summer addition is becoming a smart signing under James Morrison

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

West Brom missed their chance to move into the top two as they drew 1-1 with Southampton on Tuesday night. As they prepare to host Millwall this weekend, James Morrison‘s WBA are serious about fighting for promotion this season, winning three of their last five including an impressive 3-1 win over West Ham at the London Stadium.

The start that they have had comes off the back of a successful summer window. Under Morrison, WBA made a real effort to revamp the squad with more youth and several new additions were made with that in mind.

Jimmy-Jay Morgan has taken a lot of the plaudits after all his goalscoring exploits. However, another one of their additions is starting to break into the side, Rabby Nzingoula.

The Baggies spent a reported £2m on the 20-year-old after a decent second half of the season on loan at Nurnberg. He has been a hugely important player over the past five games where West Brom have gone unbeaten.

The 20-year-old arrived from Strasbourg with plenty of potential but relatively little experience of English football. However, three months into his Hawthorns career, he is adapting to the Championship quicker than many would have expected.

Nzingoula has gradually moved from an impact substitute to a regular part of James Morrison’s midfield, making 14 appearances and accumulating 710 minutes. Seven of those outings have been starts, while his average rating of 6.73 suggests he is providing a dependable contribution despite his young age.

There is also more to his game than simply breaking up opposition attacks. Nzingoula has recorded 41 line-breaking passes and created nine chances in his Championship minutes so far. His 1.14 chances created per 90 puts him in the 75th percentile, while his shot attempts rank in the 80th percentile.

These figures suggest West Brom have a midfielder capable of contributing further up the pitch rather than simply protecting the defence.

At 20 years old, Nzingoula has a good level of experience at decent levels. The WBA man has played 37 times in Ligue 1 as well as featuirng in Europe for Strasbourg.

His versatility is another positive. Comfortable operating as either a six or an eight, Nzingoula gives Morrison different options in central and wider midfield areas. His recent performances, including a 7.3 rating against Southampton on November 3, suggest he is beginning to find his feet.

There are still areas for improvement. He has yet to register a goal or assist, his aerial numbers are relatively modest and three yellow cards highlight the occasional risks associated with his aggressive approach.

Nevertheless, West Brom should be encouraged. Nzingoula is only 20, has already demonstrated Championship-level defensive qualities and is showing signs of becoming a more complete midfielder. For a reported £2m, the Baggies could have secured a very smart long-term investment.
