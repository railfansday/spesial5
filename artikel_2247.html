Birmingham City player was ‘absolutely dominant’ against Millwall, says reporter

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Birmingham City won 3-0 at home against the Lions on Tuesday night to boost their Championship promotion push. Alex Cochrane, Max Bird and Jay Stansfield got BCFC’s goals as they stormed to another impressive three points.

Klarer, 26, has been rock solid for the Blues this season and is one of their key players under head coach Chris Davies. He has made 16 appearances in all competitions in this campaign, 14 of which have come in the league, and he has chipped in with one assist from the back.

Journalist Dicken was impressed with his performance against Millwall and has delivered his verdict, as per a report by BirminghamLive: “(Lyndon) Dykes might have won the first aerial challenge of the night, but Klarer won the rest. The Austrian was absolutely dominant to win 12 aerial duels in the match. He stood up to Millwall.”

Birmingham City have risen up to 2nd in the table after 14 fixtures behind Middlesbrough above the likes of Stoke City, Swansea City and West Brom. They are back in action this weekend against the Swans as they aim to keep their momentum going.

Birmingham City have the best defensive record in the division at the moment ahead of West Brom and Southampton. BCFC have conceded only 13 goals.

Klarer has been excellent alongside Neumann in the heart of the Blues’ backline. The duo have kept summer recruit Dael Fry out of the team.

The Austrian is an imposing figure for the Midlands outfit and is tough to break down. He was snapped up by his current club back in 2024 and has since become one of their most prized assets.

Birmingham City landed Klarer when they were in League One and helped them win the third tier title in his first year. He has since adapted well to the step up a league.

Klarer will surely want to test himself in the Premier League at some point. Birmingham City are hoping to claw themselves into the top flight this term and are doing well at the moment.

The Blues need to stay grounded though and will want to quietly go under the radar. BCFC boss Davies has done a steady job over recent times and his team are set up well.

Klarer is under contract until June 2029 meaning he still has three years left to run down on his deal. It wouldn’t be a surprise to see other teams sniffing around him and Neumann though ahead of January with the way things are going but Birmingham City are under no pressure to sell.
