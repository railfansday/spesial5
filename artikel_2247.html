Cardiff City lurk as Swansea City begin negotiations over loan deal termination

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Swansea City signed Jeremy Monga on loan in the summer, bringing in the 17-year-old from Manchester City shortly after his permanent move to the North West from Leicester City. However, the teenage winger has struggled for minutes, and Vitor Matos has faced questions over his future.

Now, the Swansea Independent reports that the South Welsh side’s chief executive Tom Gorringe is in negotiations with Manchester City over the financial terms of Monga’s loan termination. It is said that the initial £1m loan fee would be reduced to £500,000, and the contribution of around £20,000 towards his weekly wages would obviously end.

No agreement with another club has been reached yet, it is added. However, Cardiff City are among the Championship sides interested in signing Monga if his loan is terminated as expected.

After making 27 appearances in the Championship for Leicester City last season, Monga has not made a single appearance for Swansea City. He was not in the matchday squad for Saturday’s defeat to Birmingham City.

Following relegation from the Championship, Monga’s move away from Leicester City felt inevitable. The youngster is among the country’s brightest prospects having broken into senior football at only 16.

He joined the Swans despite not being a regular starter for the Foxes. The youngster was behind the likes of Abdul Fatawu and Stephy Mavididi in the pecking order, and understandably so given his age.

Monga often featured for a couple of minutes at the end of games, so while his talent and potential is clear to see, he is still developing and an unknown quantity in senior football. Perhaps a stint with a top Championship side like Vitor Matos’ with Joseph Opoku, Ji-sung Eom and Moussa Yeo as wide options was not the wisest of moves.

It is clear that Swansea City are already well equipped on the wings, but Monga will find more options further down the Championship. That could bring a move to rumoured admirers Cardiff City to the table.

Ollie Tanner has been a key player on the wing for the Bluebirds this season, but Chris Willock has struggled for a similar impact on the opposite flank and depth is not particularly strong. Furthermore, the presence of a former Manchester City youth coach in first-team boss Brian Barry-Murphy could make Cardiff an ideal next destination.

The Bluebirds will be eager to add to Jack Moylan‘s star power in attack and someone of Monga’s promise could help. Under Barry-Murphy, he will be playing for a manager who can carefully oversee his development and show the faith he needs to make a mark on the senior stage.
