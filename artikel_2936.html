Cost of Man Utd hiring and firing managers nears £100m

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Manchester United borrowed another £10m three days after confirming Mauricio Pochettino as their new head coach.

In an extended Stock Market filing to go with their first-quarter financial results that were released on Thursday, United said they would be liable to pay £7.5m in compensation to US Soccer to hire the Argentine.

United announced Pochettino had agreed a two-and-a-half-year deal on 13 November. They borrowed £10m on 16 November.

The loan is one of three confirmed after the 30 September first-quarter reporting date, which takes the overall amount owed on their rolling credit limit to £240m.

The amount paid to release Pochettino from his USA deal takes the sums paid hiring and firing managers since Sir Alex Ferguson retired in 2013 to £95m. However, that figure is likely to top £100m once the cost of hiring Pochettino's staff and sacking Michael Carrick is added.

Carrick was sacked last week with the club in the relegation zone and enduring their worst start to a season in 40 years. The former midfielder only signed a two-year contract in the summer after a successful six-month spell in caretaker charge last season.

Pochettino is the ninth full-time manager to be appointed since Ferguson, including Ralf Rangnick in November 2021. United have changed their manager in the middle of the last three campaigns.

The club also confirmed they had spent £5.7m to either acquire or extend registrations of "certain players and members of coaching staff".

The filing also revealed they owe £417m in outstanding transfer fees, £181m of which is due after one year. United said on Thursday their substantial $750m debt was £577.4m when converted to sterling on 30 September.
