Commanders Roster Moves: Jer'Zhan Newton activated, Dyami Brown to IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

UPDATE: The Commanders made the Jer'Zhan Newton activation from injured reserve on Saturday afternoon, formally making him available for Sunday's game against the L.A. Rams. That move was expected after he practiced in full all week leading up to this game.

As you can see in the Commanders' tweet above, that wasn't the only IR move of the day. Backup receiver Dyami Brown was placed on injured reserve after suffering a calf injury during Thursday's practice. Head coach Dan Quinn said on Friday that the injury was concerning.

He won't be eligible to return until Week 13. Jaden Bradley was a practice-squad elevation for Sunday's game, with fellow receiver Treylon Burks already ruled out with a foot injury. The Commanders have five receivers on the active roster — Van Jefferson was added earlier this week — and could manage with that number.

The Washington Commanders are expected to announce a few roster moves before they host the Los Angeles Rams on Sunday afternoon. One move has already been reported, DT Jer'Zhan Newton (pec) is expected to be activated from injured reserve. His 21-day practice window was opened last week, but he didnt get activated to play vs the Eagles. He rejoins a team that just lost both their starting defensive tackles to season-ending injuries that required surgery this week. Newton was a full participant in practice this week and should make his return on Sunday.

The Commanders released their injury report on Friday and ruled out WRs Treylon Burks (foot) and Dyami Brown (calf). They're expected to elevate rookie Jaden Bradley from the practice squad later this afternoon.
