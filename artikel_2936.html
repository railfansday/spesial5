Grading the Browns defense: New faces, similar results at the bye week

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Going into the 2026 season, the Cleveland Browns defense was undergoing some changes that were seen as major from the outside. Reigning Defensive Player of the Year Myles Garrett was traded to the Los Angeles Rams, and defensive coordinator Jim Schwartz chose to leave the team after being passed up for the head coaching job.

In the Myles Garrett trade, the team received defensive end Jared Verse from the Rams (who won defensive rookie of the year in 2024 and is a two-time Pro Bowler), who isn't necessarily the same caliber player as Garrett but has shown signs of being a really good edge rusher while in LA. The Browns hired Atlanta Falcons passing game coordinator Mike Rutenberg to be their defensive coordinator.

Analysts and fans were concerned about the defense taking a step back after losing Garrett and Schwartz, but it appears that the defense is doing just fine. Just like we did with the offense earlier this week, it is time to size up the Browns defense at the bye week and give it a grade.

Outside of losing Garrett and Schwartz, not much. After losing linebacker Devin Bush in free agency, the team replaced him with linebacker Quincy Williams. Cornerback Martin Emerson also left, but outside of those minor subtractions, the team has essentially returned most of their group from last season.

Defensive tackle Mason Graham has emerged as the best player on the defensive line. Linebacker Carson Schwesinger might be a top 10 linebacker in this league, maybe even Top 5. Extending cornerback Denzel Ward and safety Grant Delpit were no-brainers. Ward, who is playing at a top-5 level, will likely be an All-Pro this season, and Delpit has been one of the more underrated safeties in the league. After being acquired in a trade with the Jacksonville Jaguars last season, cornerback Tyson Campbell has fit in well in his second full year with the team.

When Schwartz wasn't retained as the defensive coordinator, many wondered how the scheme would look.

Rutenberg, also known as Rudy, decided to not change the scheme but instead tweak it. Schwartz's defenses weren't complex in terms of how the scheme looked. Not much creativity in terms of mixing up the coverages and disguising looks. Schwartz also failed to make the necessary adjustments, and his defenses were easy to decipher against better offensive minds. Rutenberg has made tweaks to the scheme but is still keeping the core principles that made the Browns defense elite last season.

Overall, going from a top 5 defense to a top 10 is not that bad. One of the more surprising stats is how the team is still top 10 in pressures despite losing Garrett. It speaks volumes as to how talented their defensive line is. Players such as defensive end Isaiah McGuire and tackles Adin Huntington and Maliek Collins have contributed to the pressure.

In terms of the play on the back end, the Browns play man coverage more than anyone in the league, and they are really good at it. Rookie safety Emmanuel McNeil-Warren has started to stand out, and it's not long before he becomes a full-time starter because of how well he's played.

Slot corner was considered a weakness going into the season, but cornerback Myles Harden might have fixed it. Harden has shown his effectiveness on blitz packages, but his coverage is what stands out. Harden had a rough training camp, but he was able to bounce back and show his contributions at a high level. Teams will regularly throw in his direction, but that is a testament to Ward and Campbell more than anything else.

It's not a major concern, but the run defense has taken a step back from last season. As good as the Browns defensive line is, they lack someone big along the interior. Cleveland's defensive line is small but quick in terms of their get-off, but against teams that run trap/wham run concepts, they are susceptible to giving up chunk yards.

The tackling also needs to improve, but some of the tackling woes can be due to the team's attacking style they play. In terms of who needs to improve from a player standpoint, it's Jared Verse. Verse hasn't had the season he's wanted, but he has to find a way to convert pressures into sacks. Last Sunday against the Texans, Verse played one of his best games as a Brown, but it can only get better if he builds off that performance.

Despite losing Garrett, the Browns defense is still a top 10 unit in the league. Rutenberg has been an upgrade over Schwartz. The team is still missing that alpha in the defensive end room, but overall, for a unit that many people thought would fall off, the fact that this unit has been top-10 has been a surprise, but not to those who pay close attention.
