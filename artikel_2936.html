Josh Allen held mostly in check by Miami in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Buffalo Bills QB Josh Allen completed 27 of 45 passes for 304 yards with one touchdown and two interceptions in Week 11 against the Miami Dolphins. He did not run the ball and was sacked three times.

Why It Matters: Allen surpassed his season high for pass attempts by 11 against a stingy Miami defense but was limited to just one total touchdown for the second time in three weeks. His 304 yards passing were his most since the opener against Houston, but he was limited to just one TD pass for the third consecutive game and fifth time in the last seven contests. It may not be typical Josh Allen but his dual-threat ability (11 rush TDs) keeps him a QB1 in all formats, although his upside may be a bit capped in Week 12 as he faces a tough task on Thanksgiving night against Kansas City.
