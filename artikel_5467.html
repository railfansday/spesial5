Tech stocks poised to rebound after Tuesday's losses, helped by CoreWeave

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Nasdaq-100 futures climbed 1.9% and Nvidia stock jumped 5% after the major averages ended Tuesday lower

CoreWeave stock jumped 13% in premarket trading on Wednesday after the cloud provider boosted its 2026 outlook for both revenue and operating income, lifting U.S. stock futures. Nasdaq $NDAQ-100 futures rose 1.9% and S&P 500 futures added 0.9%, while contracts tied to the Dow Jones Industrial Average were up 76 points, or 0.2%.

CoreWeave's third-quarter revenue topped analyst expectations, according to CNBC. The company provides data center capacity on lease to major technology firms like Microsoft $MSFT, and a recent spike in its borrowing costs has unsettled investors, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Nvidia $NVDA stock jumped 5% after dropping nearly 10% at the start of the week. The drop followed Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s admission of an affair with a woman suspected of spying for China, according to CNBC.

The pullback in Nvidia dragged on the wider market Tuesday, and the major averages finished the session in the red. Equities also faced pressure from inflation data. The consumer price index rose 0.4% in October from September. That topped expectations, according to CNBC.

The odds that the Federal Reserve will raise rates by a quarter point in December climbed to roughly 90% once the data came out, according to CNBC, which cited CME $CME Group's FedWatch tool. The U.S. bond market is shut Wednesday in observance of Veterans Day.

CoreWeave’s results fueled a 2.6% gain in South Korea’s Kospi index at the close, according to CNBC. SK Hynix rose 5.4% and Samsung Electronics added 3.6%. Japan’s Nikkei 225 traded 0.9% higher, and Australia’s S&P/ASX 200 added 0.2%. Hong Kong’s Hang Seng Index fell 0.7%, and the CSI 300 slipped 0.4%.

European tech names also moved higher. Infineon advanced 6.3%, ASMI rose 5.5%, STMicroelectronics gained 4%, and ASML $ASML rose about 3%. The pan-European Stoxx 600 was up 0.3%, Germany’s DAX rose 0.7%, France’s CAC 40 added 0.4%, and the U.K.’s FTSE 100 gained 0.3%.
