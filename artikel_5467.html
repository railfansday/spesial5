Lyft is replacing its Lyft Pink subscription with a new plan that bundles DoorDash

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Lyft Go with DashPass costs $9.99 a month and gives riders 8% back in credits for Lyft rides or DoorDash orders

Lyft $LYFT announced a new Lyft Go membership on Thursday that bundles a DoorDash DashPass subscription with credits riders can spend on rides or delivery orders.

Lyft Go with DashPass costs $9.99 a month or $96 a year, Lyft said. Members earn 8% back in Lyft Credits on rides, from Wait & Save to Lyft Black, and can redeem the credits on Lyft rides or DoorDash orders. The rollout across the U.S. begins Nov. 5, and riders can join in the Lyft app with a one-month free trial.

The plan takes over from Lyft Pink, a membership at a comparable price, according to Bloomberg. Lyft said existing Lyft Pink members will receive notice and be moved to Lyft Go with DashPass at no change in price. They can activate DashPass in the app or cancel before their renewal date. Lyft Pink All-Access is not changing, and those members keep their current benefits, Lyft said.

That tier stays at $199 per year and is the sole option that gives members free unlocks on bikes and scooters, according to Bloomberg. Lyft Go gives members no complimentary use of Lyft’s fleet of bikes and scooters.

DashPass carries $0 delivery fees, reduced service fees and savings on eligible orders, which Lyft valued at $120 a year. Lyft Go members also get three relaxed ride cancellations a month and a waiver of the fee for returning a lost item, with unlimited uses. Lyft matches 100% of donations made through its Round Up & Donate feature for members who opt in.

New and existing DashPass members can link their accounts to get Lyft Go at no added cost. DoorDash said existing DashPass members can claim Lyft Go through a DoorDash page beginning Nov. 5.

Lyft said it will also offer Lyft Go Core for $4.99 a month. The tier returns 5% of spending as credits that can be used only toward rides, and it includes relaxed cancellations and donation matching, but no DashPass.

Lyft said any rider can browse nearby DoorDash restaurants in the Lyft app on select trips and start an order before the ride ends, with no membership required.

“With Lyft Go, your ride to work or your ride home from the airport can help cover the cost of dinner,” Siddharth Patil, Lyft’s chief rideshare officer, said in a statement.

Rob Edell, DoorDash’s vice president of DashPass, said in a statement that the partnership turns “everyday rides into everyday rewards.”

Lyft and DoorDash have offered discounts on each other’s services since 2024, according to Bloomberg. Uber $UBER One, which also costs $9.99 a month, returns 5% of ride spending as Uber credits and waives delivery fees. Lyft does not run a food delivery service.

Lyft reported record second-quarter gross bookings of $5.50 billion in August. The company said partnerships, including DoorDash and United Airlines, accounted for roughly 30% of North American rideshare volume in the quarter, a record share.
