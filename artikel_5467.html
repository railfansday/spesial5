LOWELL, Mass., Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Markley's Lowell data center campus has been accredited as a Level I arboretum by the ArbNet Arboretum Accreditation Program, the world's only international accreditation program specific to arboreta. According to ArbNet, it is the first time a data center has earned the designation.

Seven landscapes, one living collectionWorking with Stantec, a global design and engineering firm, Markley turned the site cleanup into a designed landscape of about 1,500 trees and more than 400 plant species. Stantec describes the project as starting as just a corporate campus landscape that has evolved into a curated collection of trees and diverse plant communities, chosen for form, texture and ecological value.  The campus access loop, once purely utilitarian, now leads visitors through a sequence of seven distinct outdoor rooms that change with every season.

The collection spans maples, deciduous ornamentals, Japanese cultivars, weeping specimens and evergreens. Together, the seven rooms show that meaningful habitat, urban forestry and critical technology infrastructure can share a single, seamless landscape.
