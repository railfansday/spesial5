The launch gives companies with APAC operations direct access to senior practitioners based in the region.

With the addition of Asia Pacific, Lean Focus now operates one business system across four regions: Lean Focus North America, Lean Focus Europe, Lean Focus Nordic, and Lean Focus Asia Pacific. Whether a client needs to improve delivery, release cash from inventory, increase capacity, or strengthen leadership, Lean Focus starts with the client's business priorities and works alongside its people to deliver measurable results. The approach combines hands-on Kaizen with the leadership routines and capability building needed to sustain improvements.

The region is led by Tim Niewand, Vice President and Managing Director of Lean Focus Asia Pacific. Trained by former Toyota plant managers and Danaher Business System leaders, Niewand has facilitated more than 100 Kaizen events across 25 companies in 10 countries.

"Companies operating in Asia Pacific want improvement that shows up in their results, not just in a report," said Niewand. "Our role is to partner with their leaders and our global team to turn business priorities into focused action on the ground, and to build the capability that keeps those gains in place."

Organizations interested in improving the performance of their APAC operations can contact Lean Focus Asia Pacific to discuss their highest-priority challenge. Existing Lean Focus clients can contact their current relationship leader to explore extending their partnership to APAC operations.
