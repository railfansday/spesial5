Jensen Huang's exit has investors asking if Nvidia will keep bankrolling the AI boom

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Investors are concerned that Nvidia could scale back its financing of data centers under new leadership, which would slow the AI boom

David Paul Morris / Bloomberg via Getty Images

Nvidia $NVDA named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO on Thursday after founder Jensen Huang resigned, and the board has begun a search for a permanent successor.

Nvidia stock fell as much as 14% in Thursday trading before paring losses and closing down about 4%. The stock is still en route to a sixth consecutive week of losses, the longest run of that kind since 2012, according to CNBC. The company will post its next earnings figures on Tuesday.

Kress will keep her CFO role while she serves as interim CEO. The board is looking at both insiders and outsiders for the permanent job. Kress said in a statement that she plans to expand on the base Huang spent 33 years laying.

Kress joined Nvidia as CFO in 2013, after finance roles at Cisco $CSCO, Microsoft $MSFT and Texas Instruments $TXN, according to CNBC. Kress has held the post for more than double the typical length of service among CFOs at S&P 500 and Fortune 500 companies, according to Bloomberg.

Dan Ives, an analyst at Yorkville Ives & Co., told CNBC before the announcement that Huang "didn't do anything without her." Ives called Kress "a Swiss Army knife for Nvidia."

Boards frequently look to their finance chief to lead temporarily when trouble hits, according to Bloomberg. "In a time of crisis, you want to convey the message of stability and confidence, even though there's tremendous instability right now," said Cathy Logue, a managing director at executive search firm Stanton Chase, according to Bloomberg.

Huang resigned after reports of two extramarital affairs, one of which involved a businesswoman that American spy officials suspect works for China's intelligence services. Huang admitted infidelity in a note to employees on Sunday, and the board opened an independent investigation the same day. He also left the board, effective immediately.

The board named Harvey C. Jones, a former CEO and executive chairman of Synopsys $SNPS, as its first chair. "We expect the company's operations, customer commitments and product roadmap to continue without interruption," Jones said in a statement.

Investors are concerned that Nvidia could scale back its financing of data centers under new leadership, which would slow the AI boom, according to Reuters. In its latest earnings report, Nvidia disclosed $164.5 billion in financial commitments.

Shares of CoreWeave and Naver, both Nvidia customers, fell 6% or more, while shares of Broadcom $AVGO and AMD $AMD rose.
