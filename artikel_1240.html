Arsenal Fans Slam ‘Abysmal’ Dud in Borussia Dortmund Draw

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

In their fifth Champions League game of the season, Arsenal hosted Borussia Dortmund. They had to settle for a point on the night as the match ended 1-1.

The Gunners had won all but one of their games in Europe so far this term, and carried their impressive form into the early stages of this encounter. Having been trusted to start ahead of Kai Havertz, Viktor Gyokeres started brilliantly, smashing the ball in from 25 yards to give Arsenal a lead after just 10 minutes.

That was enough to separatethe teams at the break, but Dortmund were able to draw level 10 minutes into the second half. Maxi Beier showed great composure to find the bottom corner after being set up well by Joane Gadou.

That was enough to see the game end all square at 1-1.

Leny Yoro was sent off in the eighth minute, ruining United's chances from the offset.

Arsenal signed Christos Tzolis from Club Brugge on a five-year deal in the summer, as the winger was brought in to help fill the void left by the departing Leandro Trossard and Gabriel Martinelli. There were also hopes of signing another high-profile attacker, like Vinicius Junior, one of the best wingers in the world, but no elite forward arrived.

This meant Tzolis came straight into the starting XI, and he looked bright early on, getting two assists on debut in the Community Shield win over Manchester City. He then picked up assists against Napoli and Chelsea but is yet to score for the Gunners – or register a goal contribution since August.

Against Dortmund, he was poor, struggling to make a big impact. Mikel Arteta preferred Eberechi Eze in the Premier League draw against Newcastle United, and he lost patience with an ineffective Tzolis after 65 minutes in the Champions League, hooking him for the Englishman.

His 6.3 SofaScore rating was the lowest of any starting Arsenal player, as the winger finished the match with 64% passing accuracy, 0/2 attempted crosses, 1/3 shots on target, and lost the ball 16 times.

Fans watching on were far from impressed with Tzolis' efforts. Making their feelings clear, one wrote: "Tzolis had a good 3 games and been mediocre ever since, if we being real."

Another added: "Tzolis is genuinely so bad." A third wrote: "Tzolis, I’ve been holding my tongue but he really can’t hang."

Another wrote: "Should have taken Tzolis off at half time. He has been abysmal."

One more summed up the mood, writing: "Tzolis plays like a pitch invader." While a final fan said: "Tzolis just isn't on the same wavelength as the rest of the team man."
