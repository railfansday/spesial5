Seahawks' team doctor OK after getting hit in the face by a Fernando Mendoza pass

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Seahawks team physician Dr. Ed Khalfayan took a Fernando Mendoza fastball to the face on Sunday, but he emerged OK.

With the Raiders backed up deep in their own territory, Mendoza dropped back into his own end zone and fired a bullet toward the sideline. It sailed out of bounds near the 20-yard line, where Khalfayan was standing on the sideline. Khalfayan didn’t see it coming, and it hit him hard in the head.

Khalfayan dropped to the ground and for a moment it appeared that he was knocked unconscious, although he was able to get back up quickly.

Reporters asked Seahawks coach Mike Macdonald after the game, and Macdonald said the sideline was alarmed by the sight of it.

“I was worried about him,” Macdonald said. “That was scary, so we’ll check on him. Thanks for asking.”

Fortunately, Khalfayan told the Seahawks’ website he was doing well after the game.
