Seahawks sign CB Trevon Diggs, OT Bobby Hart to active roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Seahawks returned from their bye week by adding a pair of veteran players to their 53-man roster.

Cornerback Trevon Diggs and offensive tackle Bobby Hart were both signed off of the practice squad. The Seahawks had one open roster spot and created another by placing running back George Holani on injured reserve. Holani has a knee injury.

Diggs has bounced between the active roster and the practice squad this season. He's played in five games and has made one tackle.

Hart has been called up to play in two games. He played 44 snaps in Week 10 after right tackle Abraham Lucas suffered an ankle injury.
