Eagles RB Will Shipley: Emotions were running high, nothing but love for Nick Sirianni

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Eagles head coach Nick Sirianni ended Thursday’s win in Dallas by jawing with fans near the field and it wasn’t the first verbal exchange he got in over the course of the afternoon.

Cameras showed Sirianni and running back Will Shipley having words with one another during the first quarter of what turned out to be a stunning 39-38 Eagles win. After the game, Shipley declined to say what he was discussing with Sirianni but said that all that transpired between that conversation and the game-winning field goal made it water under the bridge.

“Emotions were running high. Like on Thanksgiving, all families are arguing about politics and the drunk uncle,” Shipley said, via Tim McManus of ESPN.com. “I’ve got so much love for Coach Nick and him as the leader of this football team.”

There wasn’t a lot of fun to be had around the Eagles during their seven-game losing streak and it looked like Thanksgiving was going to end poorly for them, but a furious comeback left them with the last laugh and the ability to sweep any early bumpiness under the rug.
