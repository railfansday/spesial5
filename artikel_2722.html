David Bailey among several Jets who achieve milestones during Chargers win

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Sometimes, a win can calm even the most devastated of fan bases.

Sunday's New York Jets victory over the Los Angeles Chargers was a welcome change of pace.

The win won't change the national perception of the Jets (3-8), nor will it erase the struggles that have defined their season. However, it gives fans and the media something positive to discuss for a few days, particularly with several players reaching personal milestones during the contest.

A handful of Jets accomplished notable feats in Week 11. All of which, made the victory even more enjoyable.

Breece Hall rushed for 86 yards, bringing his career total to 3,749 yards. He passed Bilal Powell's 3,675 yards to move into eighth place on the Jets' all-time rushing list.

Garrett Wilson caught three passes, pushing his career reception total to 368. He passed Curtis Martin for seventh place on the franchise's all-time receptions list.

Geno Smith threw three touchdown passes, bringing his total with the Jets to 43. That ties him with Ryan Fitzpatrick for 10th in franchise history.

David Bailey recorded two sacks and forced a fumble. He became the first NFL player since sacks were officially tracked in 1982 to record at least 9.5 sacks and six forced fumbles through his first 10 career games.

The Jets scored on each of their first five possessions, making their sixth drive a simple matter of running out the clock and securing the victory. There was no need to give the Chargers another opportunity to make things interesting, and New York finished the afternoon in victory formation.

There was also an unusual statistical footnote. The Jets-Chargers game featured 12 combined drives. That's the fewest in any game since at least 1978, the beginning of the play-by-play era, according to ESPN Research.

For a team that has spent much of the season searching for positives, Sunday offered several, perhaps even a reminder that even in a frustrating year, there are still reasons to celebrate. The Jets will take the win, the milestones, and a much more enjoyable flight home. Whether the performance proves to be a turning point or simply a brief reprieve, the Jets at least gave their fans something to enjoy for a change.
