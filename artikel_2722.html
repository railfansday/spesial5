AT&T Stadium has become a house of horrors for Cowboys

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

It was hardly the most decorated time in Dallas Cowboys franchise history. Nobody is saying that a banner was deserved.

But tell me you would not immediately trade the current version of the Dallas Cowboys for what we saw from 2021-2023. That group played stable football over a 1,000-day period, and while they fell short of the ultimate goal after each season, "stable football" is so far away from what the current group is offering on a weekly basis.

What's more is that group took care of business in their house, something the current group doesn't do either.

This was true before a snap had been taken. Well before kickoff it was obvious that the Cowboys had absolutely no home field advantage to speak of on Sunday as 49ers fans had full well taken over AT&T Stadium.

Make of this what you will. Some will offer that it is a result of ticket prices being so high. Others will claim that the 49ers have a massive fanbase, like the Cowboys, and that it travels well. The reality is that this is all likely the result of a combination of things, and one of them is that Dallas has hardly been stellar when playing football in the building as of late.

Consider that during the three-year span we started this conversation with the Cowboys played 25 games at home. They went 21-4 in them and notably, infamously, were undefeated there through the 2023 regular season before the Green Bay Packers broke the franchise in a way they are still not recovered from.

This discussion is sort of an actual measurement of that point, one that many people claim. Dallas has played, from 2024 to present, 22 games in the same building. They are a moribund 7-14-1 in them.

It is important to note that AT&T Stadium was hardly ever a football fortress. It was never Seattle, Kansas City, or even other notable NFL home environments like Green Bay, Buffalo, or Philadelphia. Again this is likely the result of several factors, but the point here is that at the very least the home team made sure that people got what they paid for way more often than not.

Like with everything involving the Cowboys since 2024, that has withered away. All that is left to show for the work that the franchise did from 2021-2023 (again not saying it was serious work, but just in a relative-to-context way) are the uniforms and logos.

Nothing about who this team is right now is recognizable even compared to a part of franchise history where nothing serious was achieved. This team pales in comparison to those from '21-'23 and that group is among the least accomplished in franchise history at large! The current group is simply so moribund that even that is way out in the distance.

You aren't imagining things when you feel like the Cowboys have become a more difficult proposition. This is true in more ways than we can even count.

Lately it has borne itself out in the building they call home, though. And now they have two more games in the building through Thanksgiving Day.
