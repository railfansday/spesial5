Report: Ravens made trade offer for Colts' Daniel Jones before deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Indianapolis Colts reportedly had the opportunity to trade Daniel Jones prior to the trade deadline.

According to The Ringer's Tom Pelissero, the Ravens offered the Colts a mid-round pick swap. Had Baltimore made the playoffs, the trade "would've returned a pick to Indianapolis," Pelissero wrote, via Bleacher Report.

But instead of trading Jones to Baltimore, the Colts decided to keep him. And not even two weeks after the trade deadline passed, Indianapolis released Jones -- getting nothing in return for him.

ESPN's Jeremy Fowler recently highlighted a few reasons why the Colts ended up moving on from Jones at this time.

By releasing Jones, the Colts are taking on a good amount of dead cap for the remainder of this season and into 2027. After signing a two-year, $88 million deal this offseason, Jones earned nearly $50 million for 11 games.

This season, Jones has completed 58% of his passes at a near league-low 5.6 yards per attempt with seven touchdowns to 13 interceptions, which leads the NFL.
