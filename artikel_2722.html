LeBron James denies telling Philadelphia to be patient as he finds rhythm

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It all started with this paragraph, written by ESPN's Dave McMenamin, in a story about what is working and not working for the Philadelphia 76ers as they start the season.

A Sixers source told ESPN that James has asked coaches to be patient with him as he expects to find his rhythm and get his conditioning in line by December as he ramps up.

ESPN's social media team clipped that quote into an Instagram post — then LeBron James saw it and denied ever saying that.

It's an interesting reaction to a report that wasn't so much critical of James as it was explaining his slower statistical start. LeBron is averaging a career-low 15.1 points per game (his previous low was 20.9). He's doing that while shooting 44.3% overall and 22.5% from 3, with a 51.8 true shooting percentage, all numbers that are career lows or close to it. That said, he has been the glue for this star-studded roster in many ways, and he leads the team in assists at 6.9 per game — he has been especially critical for them at the end of clutch games, where the 76ers are 9-0.

It's also worth noting that McMenamin wasn't directly quoting his source, it read more like his source was relaying the essence of a conversation. LeBron didn't like the interpretation used.

With the 76ers at 12-3 and on top of the East through 15 games, LeBron and Philadelphia can afford to be patient, whether he asked for that or not. If the 76ers are going to be playing in late May and June as they hope, it's going to take a healthy and efficient LeBron — as well as Joel Embiid, Jaylen Brown and Tyrese Maxey — to make that a reality.
