Cowboys news: Joey Porter Jr. provides update on his recent injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Dallas Cowboys squeaked by the Indianapolis Colts in week nine, but they will need to be on their absolute "A-game" on Sunday against the NFC favorite San Fransisco 49ers.

With one of the toughest games on their schedule quickly approaching, all eyes are on Joey Porter Jr. and George Pickens to see if they will return in week ten. After missing last week's game due to an ankle injury, Porter gives an update on his injury status and how quickly he plans on getting back in a heated NFC East division.

Joey Porter Jr. has been everything and more when on the field for the Cowboys since they acquired him early in the season from the Pittsburgh Steelers. The only issue is he has rarely been on the field and Porter gives an update on his injury ahead of week 10.

It's all Porter can do to contain his aggravation, wanting to be on the field for the Cowboys equally as much as they need him there.

"I mean, it's definitely frustrating," he said on Thursday. "You know, I'm on a new team, and I want to show these guys what I can do, and I feel like I have been, for the most part, when I'm on the field. So when I can't play football, it's frustrating for everybody. I'm just trying to keep the main thing, the main thing, and get back as fast as possible."

Cowboys turn to Barham as EDGE injuries pile up

Fast forward to Week 10. The Cowboys are gearing up to face the 7-1 San Francisco 49ers, and they're looking to Barham to help pull them out of a terrible situation.

Barham is about to play outside linebacker. According to Cowboys defensive coordinator Christian Parker via Nick Harris (Fort Worth Star-Telegram), the rookie will get increased work at the position this week because that group is battling injuries. Multiple reporters also indicated Barham worked with the outside linebackers during the media viewing portion of Wednesday's practice.

The 49ers have been one of the Cowboys many boogeymen over recent years. Can the Cowboys put up a fight on Sunday? Or will it be more of the same?

The Cowboys play the 49ers Sunday at AT&T Stadium, and it's sad to see this thing on life support now. The 49ers, who, like Dallas, own five Lombardi Trophies, used the Cowboys as title game stepping stones for two of them — the "Catch" in 1981 to get to their first and then Dallas' somewhat messy start but near-magical comeback at Candlestick after the 1994 season.
