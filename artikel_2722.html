Shedeur Sanders or Deshaun Watson, who would you pick? Browns Reacts Survey

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Welcome to SB Nation Reacts, a survey of fans across the NFL. Throughout the year we ask questions of the most plugged-in Browns fans and fans across the country.

The NFL trade deadline has passed, and the Cleveland Browns still have all the same players on their roster. At 5-4, they stayed with their young core and made no additions from other clubs, like they did a year ago with CB Tyson Campbell. Now, all eyes turn to who you would or would have started at quarterback for the Browns this Sunday against the Houston Texans?

Forgive the odds phrasing of "should or should have," but I'm expecting Todd Monken to announce the starter on Wednesday, and this poll will run through Thursday to gauge overall who you would've picked, regardless of what Monken decides. Cleveland still can be a contender in the playoff race with their 'easy' schedule, but after losses to the Jets and Saints, they've squandered a lot of their margin for error already. Do you trust Deshaun Watson, who has five-game winning drive, or Shedeur Sanders, who looked pretty bad for three quarters until he started taking risks out of necessity?

And then our other question is the usual confidence meter for the direction of the team. Be sure to also let us know what you think in the comments section!
