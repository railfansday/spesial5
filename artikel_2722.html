Boys soccer: Conrad Weiser’s Cash Stout is the BCIAA player of the year

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Conrad Weiser and Tulpehocken are playing in the quarterfinals in their respective classes in the state tournament so it should come as no surprise that each team placed three players on the BCIAA All-Conference boys soccer team.

The all-conference squad is led by Conrad Weiser senior Cash Stout, who was named the player of the year by the conference's coaches. He's joined on the all-conference team by senior teammates Luke Callahan and Owen Meredith.

Tulpehocken's three players named to the all-conference team are senior Leshane Christ, junior Van Koch and sophomore Brady Woll.

Stout heads into Saturday's Class 3A quarterfinal match against Susquehannock (2 p.m. at Hershey High School) with 22 goals and 6 assists. Meredith has 12 goals and 10 assists.

Christ leads the county in scoring with 27 goals and 9 assists, and Koch has 19 goals and 15 assists.

Tulpehocken, the District 3 runnerup, plays District 4 champion Lewisburg at 2 p.m. Saturday at East Pennsboro High School in the 2A quarterfinals.

District 3 Class 2A champion Wyomissing placed one player on the all-conference team in senior Nate Flowers, who has 15 goals and 6 assists.

Wyomissing plays District 12 champion Devon Prep in the 2A quarterfinals at 11 a.m. Saturday at Perkiomen Valley High School.

Exeter placed four players on the all-conference team, including junior Sam Hartman who led the Eagles with 16 goals and 6 assists.

Conrad Weiser's Derek Long was named the coach of the year, the second year in a row he earned the honor.

Conrad Weiser won the BCIAA championship, beating Exeter 3-1 in the title match.

The teams were decided by a vote of the conference's coaches.
