Rams roll to 34-6 victory over Cardinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Dealing with numerous injuries on offense, the Rams understandably got off to a slow start in Sunday’s game against the Cardinals.

But Los Angeles got going late in the second quarter and went on to roll to a 34-6 blowout victory.

The Rams have now won four in a row after starting the season 2-4, with their last loss coming on Oct. 18 to the Cardinals at home.

Starting late in the second quarter, the Rams scored four consecutive touchdowns to make the game a laugher. Quarterback Matthew Stafford hit Tyler Higbee with a 5-yard touchdown to get things started with just over a minute left in the second period. Then after a Kam Kinchens interception, Stafford connected with Xavier Smith for a 22-yard touchdown on a screen pass to give L.A. a 17-6 halftime lead.

Blake Corum kept the roll going to open the second half, scoring on a 1-yard run. Then Stafford hit Davis Allen for a 19-yard TD to make the score 31-6 with 5:55 left.

Brandon McManus closed out the scoring with a 43-yard field goal.

The Rams finished the game holding the ball for the final 12:15 of the contest, with Ronnie Rivers getting 81 yards on 15 carries to close it out.

L.A. ended the game leading time of possession 42:23 to 17:37. The Rams had 28 first downs, 436 total yards, and were 7-of-12 on third down. Arizona had just six first downs, 123 total yards, and the team was 0-of-6 on third down.

Corum led Los Angeles with 100 yards on 17 carries with a touchdown. He also caught three passes for 19 yards.

Matthew Stafford was 18-of-26 for 249 yards with three touchdowns and an interception.

Making his debut, rookie Carson Beck finished 11-of-18 for 108 yards with two picks.

The Rams will now go into their abbreviated Week 11 bye at 6-4. They will next host the Packers on Wednesday of Week 12.

Arizona falls to 3-7 on the season. The club will be on the road to face Kansas City next Sunday.
