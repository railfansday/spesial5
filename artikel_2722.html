Lakers rookie makes most of biggest opportunity he’s gotten so far

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Lakers coach JJ Redick has emphasized for weeks that every player on his roster is going to get an opportunity to play.

The nature of an 82-game regular season necessitates flexibility with playing rotations.

Cameron Carr of the Los Angeles Lakers drives to the basket. NBAE via Getty Images

During Friday's 106-104 home win over the Kings, rookie Cameron Carr got his most significant opportunity with the Lakers to date.

And his performance showed why he's deserving of more playing opportunities moving forward.

With Austin Reaves sidelined for the fourth consecutive game because of a bruised left heel, Carr was in the rotation off the bench, playing 11 minutes.

Unlike some of his previous playing time, a good amount of which has come when the game's results were already decided, the former Baylor Bear's minutes had a direct impact on the Lakers' chances of winning.

And the rookie made the playing time count, having five points on 2-for-5 shooting (1-for-4 on 3-pointers), a steal and a rebound.

"He looked great," Walker Kessler said. "He hit some big shots, played with physicality. He's got some long arms, I can tell you that. That layup he made — he's just got some long arms. He looked good tonight. I was happy. It was good to see."

Cameron Carr of the Los Angeles Lakers speaks to the media. NBAE via Getty Images

The stats don't matter as much as the comfort Carr showed when he was on the court.

He didn't hesitate to shoot the corner 3-pointer that momentarily gave the Lakers a two-point lead midway through the second quarter. And didn't shy away from contact on a layup later in the quarter.

Carr looked ready for the defensive physicality needed to hold his own on that end of the floor.

"That was huge," Quentin Grimes said. "I mean, as a rookie, you got to go out there and kind of earn minutes. Everybody kind of has to do that at one point some time in their career. And he got thrown in there, hit a big 3, hit a tough layup and had a couple good closeouts and got some stops. So, those are the things that earn trust from a coach when they throw you out there even more later in the season, for sure."

Redick has said Carr and second-year forward Adou Thiero's development over the next couple of years could raise the team's collective ceiling.

But before they can be ceiling-raisers, Carr and Thiero need to establish a floor for themselves.

Jaden Hardy #7 and Cameron Carr #43 of the Los Angeles Lakers smile during a game against the Sacramento Kings. NBAE via Getty Images

With Reaves expected to make his return to the floor next week, playing time won't be available as it has been with him sidelined.

But Carr showed, at least for one game, he's ready to contribute more when his number is called.

And it's on the Lakers to find more chances for him so he can develop into the ceiling-raiser they're looking for him to be.
