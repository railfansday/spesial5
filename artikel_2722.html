Bears add CB Isaac Yiadom off waivers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Chicago has claimed cornerback Isaac Yiadom off waivers, the team announced on Tuesday.

The cornerback appeared in three games for the Commanders this season, playing exclusively on special teams.

Bears defensive coordinator Dennis Allen has plenty of familiarity with Yiadom, as the cornerback played under him when Allen was New Orleans' head coach in 2022 and 2023.

A Broncos third-round pick in 2018, Yiadom has played 121 career games with 34 starts for the Broncos, Giants, Packers, Texans, Saints, 49ers, and Commanders.
