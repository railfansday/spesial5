When will Steph Curry return? Latest injury update on Warriors star

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Golden State Warriors have struggled to keep their heads above water in the absence of Stephen Curry.

The Warriors have won only two of their seven games without their veteran superstar, including a narrow 88-87 victory against the Boston Celtics on Nov. 22.

Curry has been ruled out with an oblique abdominal tear he suffered against the Houston Rockets on Nov. 11, around the 6:25 mark in the first quarter. The Warriors await his return, and anticipate every update concerning the 38-year-old.

A team spokesperson told USA TODAY Sports that Curry will be re-evaluated on Friday. An additional update on his return status is then expected to be released.

Through 10 games, Curry was averaging 27.8 points, 3.9 assists and 3.7 rebounds, shooting 50% from the floor and 45.7% on 3-point attempts.

Curry has been around the team, whether in practice, in the locker room or on the bench. His presence has helped to keep the team morale high while providing leadership by lending an extra voice.

"That's definitely helpful," Warriors head coach Steve Kerr told reporters about Curry's presence after a Tuesday practice. "He's just always a breath of fresh air. The humor and the confidence that he has. You saw the other night, Draymond (Green) hits a three from the corner, and (Curry is) flying all over the place on the bench. There's just a different vibe when Steph is in the building, and it's good to have him here, even if he's not playing."
