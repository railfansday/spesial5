3 Elite Prime Video Series to Binge This Week (Nov 23-27)

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Two weeks after premiering its third season, The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power is still the number one show on Prime Video. An epic fantasy drama inspired by J.R.R. Tolkien’s Middle-earth canon, the show is one of the most ambitious TV shows ever made, with the latest season earning a lot of praise from critics so far. But that said, it’s hardly the only great series you could watch on the platform. Here’s a look at three great shows that we think you should binge this week on Prime Video.

For more recommendations, check out our list of the best shows and movies on Prime Video.

Developed by Peter Harness and Guy Ritchie, Young Sherlock is a series adaptation of Andrew Lane’s books that reimagines Sir Arthur Conan Doyle’s legendary detective as an unruly 19-year-old, years before he became famous. The series follows Sherlock as a student at Oxford University, where he befriends James Moriarty, and they set out to solve his first-ever murder mystery tied to a global-level conspiracy. Hero Fiennes Tiffin stars as Holmes, Max Irons as his brother Mycroft, and Dónal Finn as Moriarty, with Natascha McElhone and Holly Cattle as notable characters.

Created by Matthew Parkhill, Young Sherlock is a youthful, Victorian-era reimagining of English literature’s most iconic detective and a fun pastiche of the Sherlock Holmes canon with a touch of that characteristic Guy Ritchie flair. The show excels as an independent story and is commendable for how it never attempts to recreate Conan Doyle’s original work or draw directly from the original canon. Since its premiere in March 2026, Young Sherlock has been positively received by fans and critics alike, earning praise for Tiffin and Finn’s performances, with the latter cited as a breakout star of the show.

A great detective is really just a personality with a method attached, which is why we keep remaking the same four of them. Here is yours, what it says about you, and the case for them being the best in the business.

You let people think you are not a threat and then you simply never go away. Columbo is the only major detective show that tells you who did it in the first ten minutes, because the pleasure was never the puzzle, it was watching a rich man slowly realise he is in trouble.

You are the most interesting person in the room and you know exactly how to use it. Rian Johnson built Blanc as a gentleman detective who is also an outsider everywhere he goes, which lets him be charming at people who would never otherwise talk to him.

Everybody tells you everything, and you have never once asked them to. Jessica Fletcher is a crime novelist from a small town in Maine who is so warm and so obviously trustworthy that suspects confess to her essentially as a social courtesy.

You expect to be lied to and you are almost never disappointed. Chandler wrote Marlowe as a man with a rigid private code in a city that has none, which is why he takes the case, solves it, gets nothing out of it and takes the next one.

A cult hit teen sports drama created by Peter Berg, Friday Night Lights is a series adaptation of Berg’s 2004 film of the same name, which in turn was inspired by a 1990 book of immersive journalism by H.G. Bissinger. Set in a small fictional town in West Texas, the five-season series follows a high school football team, exploring how their highs and lows affect the close-knit community. The show stars Kyle Chandler, Connie Britton, and Aimee Teegarden in central roles.

During its run, Friday Night Lights became a critical favorite and built quite a following, but it never quite received the widespread attention it deserves. The series has since amassed an enduring fan base and is considered one of the most well-written television dramas ever. Praised for the compelling performances and grounded portrayal of contemporary Americana, Friday Night Lights is easily one of the finest football shows of all time.

Developed by Scott Neustadter and Michael H. Weber as a musical drama adaptation of the novel by Taylor Jenkins Reid, Daisy Jones & The Six follows the rise and fall of the rock band in 1970s Los Angeles, led by the titular singer-songwriter. The series chronicles their phenomenal growth within the LA music scene, becoming one of the most famous bands in the world, before they split at the height of their success. Riley Keough and Sam Claflin play the lead roles and perform original songs, with Camila Morrone, Suki Waterhouse, Will Harrison, Josh Whitehouse, and Sebastian Chacon starring as the other band members.

Presented in a documentary style with footage of interviews, concerts, and recording sessions, Daisy Jones & the Six feels more realistic than many of its real counterparts. Even though it alters several character arcs, plotlines, and character relationships, the series successfully brings life to the New York Times bestseller and is a well-made modern show set in the '70s. Critics have praised the show’s original songs and the musical performances, especially the duets by the leading duo.
