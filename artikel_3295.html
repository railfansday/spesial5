Lucy Hale Notes “Everyone Is Willing” To Reunite For ‘Pretty Little Liars’ Revival: “There’s Gotta Be A New Version Of ‘A'”

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Nearly a decade later, the cast of Pretty Little Liars is apparently ready to take another secret to the grave.

Lucy Hale, who starred as Aria Montgomery on the Freeform series, recently teased that everyone from the cast is “willing and would want to” reprise their roles for a revival of the teen mystery series based on Sara Shepard’s YA novels.

“I don’t think it would take much. I think everyone is willing and would want to,” she told Entertainment Tonight. “I think it’s just a matter of schedule and getting the right script. I think the script has to be right, the storyline has to be right.”

Hale continued, “And where is Aria? What’s Aria doing? Especially all the girls that came out of Rosewood, I feel like they’re not ever able to escape it. So, I feel like they somehow maybe left town but found their way back.” Watch on Deadline

After the show’s 2017 series finale, which saw her character Aria end up with Ezra Fitz (Ian Harding), Hale predicted where they would be in the revival. “I do believe Aria and Ezra are still together. I believe they have kids. I believe Aria has written some books,” she said.

Harding, who reunites with Hale in Hulu’s The 12 Dates of Christmas, said that he’d want a revival to see everyone “in a place that is kind of what they wanted but maybe not quite, and then something brings them all back together.”

Hale agreed, adding, “Like, a tragedy brings them all back to Rosewood or something. There’s gotta be a new version of ‘A’ as well.”

The pair starred in the series alongside Shay Mitchell, Ashley Benson, Troian Bellisario, Sasha Pieterse, Keegan Allen, Tyler Blackburn and Janel Parrish.

Following Pretty Little Liars‘ seven-season run from 2010 to 2017, Freeform released the spin-off Pretty Little Liars: The Perfectionists for one season in 2019, followed by HBO Max’s offshoot Pretty Little Liars: Original Sin in 2022, which had ended in ’24 with Season 2, entitled Pretty Little Liars: Summer School.
