Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Management lifted full-year guidance but flagged a housing slowdown tied to elevated mortgage rates.

Management increased full-year earnings guidance based on year-to-date performance and constructive regulatory developments in several jurisdictions. The company executed multiyear rate plans and settlement agreements in Washington, Oregon, and Texas while continuing to invest in utility infrastructure. Strategic initiatives focused on expanding the natural gas storage footprint and integrating water utility services into existing growth markets. Financial results for the period included the effects of customer additions across all utility segments and disciplined operations and maintenance spending.
