Celebrating the 2026 Builder’s Choice Design Award Winners

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Trent HancockManaging Director, Camelot HomesAmy AlperPrincipal, Amy A. Alper, ArchitectJesse RobesonAssociate Principal, KTGY

Residential design is as diverse as the people and places it serves. This year’s 16 Builder’s Choice Design Award winners reflect the breadth of unique work happening across the industry, from production and custom homes to townhomes, build-to-rent developments, and master plans.

The honorees, spanning one Project of the Year, nine grand awards, and six merits, showcase creativity across architecture, floor plan efficiency, interior design, landscaping, and sustainable strategies.

These nine grand winners highlight the ambition, innovation, and design excellence pushing residential architecture forward.
