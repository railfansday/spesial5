A venture capitalist warned Anthropic's IPO lockup could spark an AI correction

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Copper Sky's Jack Selby said a weak Anthropic offering and later lockup expirations could pressure the AI trade

Jack Selby, the head of the venture firm Copper Sky, warned that Anthropic's initial public offering could trigger a correction in the AI market, according to Bloomberg.

Selby, who also helps run Peter Thiel's family office as a managing partner, said the end of Anthropic's lockup periods could weigh on its stock. He named SoftBank Group and Oracle $ORCL as early warning signs, adding that Oracle is the more vulnerable of the two because it carries so much debt relative to its size.

"An underperforming Anthropic IPO has to be a very prominent player in what will be a pretty massive correction in the market with respect to AI," Selby told Bloomberg Television on Wednesday. "If the most anticipated AI company's IPO falls very short of its projected valuation, and just imagine where it's going to go after the lockups expire."

Anthropic hopes to bring in up to $100 billion from a private placement, the stock offering itself and any overallotment shares that may be sold, and early indications suggest demand is strong, according to Bloomberg. Selby is skeptical.

He said over 80% of what Anthropic earns does not repeat from period to period, which leaves it exposed whenever competitors get better. He pointed to DeepSeek and other Chinese models as another threat. "If they're six months or nine months behind what Anthropic does, what does Anthropic do that is so proprietary that it is better or has to be a national security interest versus these Chinese models?" Selby said.

Selby also pointed to the heavy outlays required for chips, data centers, power and data, and noted that interest rates remain well above zero. "It's a real business, but the business is still in its early innings and the idea that it is a trillion-dollar business seems exceptionally far-fetched," he said.

Anthropic launched the roadshow for its IPO on Tuesday, with its Class A common stock expected to price at $70 to $78 a share. Pricing is scheduled for Nov. 18, and trading is set to begin the next day. The company has applied to list on the Nasdaq $NDAQ Global Select Market under the symbol "ANT."

Anthropic is targeting a valuation near $1.33 trillion, according to The Wall Street Journal, and the top of the range implies a fully diluted valuation as high as $1.405 trillion, according to CNBC. Investors had expected a valuation near $1.5 trillion before AI stocks sold off last Thursday, according to The Wall Street Journal.

In an amended filing, Anthropic detailed lock-up agreements tied to the offering that bar covered holders from selling, pledging or hedging their stock without the underwriters' written consent. Anthropic has agreed to sell $26 billion of Class N common stock in a private placement to Advanced Micro Devices, Broadcom $AVGO, Google $GOOGL, Micron $MU, Microsoft $MSFT, Nvidia $NVDA and Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company. Those shares are exempt from the underwriter lock-up agreements.
