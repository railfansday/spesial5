Blue Owl is preparing to sell $513 million in bonds tied to an Amazon data center

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The securities would be backed by a mortgage on a 54-megawatt Virginia facility that Blue Owl acquired for $779.5 million

Blue Owl is lining up a $513 million offering of commercial mortgage-backed bonds to help pay for a Virginia data center it bought that Amazon $AMZN leases, according to Bloomberg.

Blue Owl Digital Infrastructure Trust, through a subsidiary, completed the acquisition of the 54-megawatt facility in Gainesville, Virginia, on Nov. 10 for $779.5 million, the company said in an SEC filing. The filing said the property is 100% leased to a hyperscaler customer and that the trust paid with its available cash and a new property-level mortgage loan.

Citigroup $C is running the bond sale, according to Bloomberg. The deal includes a $375 million portion of bonds, averaging five years in life, which Fitch Ratings and Kroll Bond Rating Agency are expected to grade AAA. Initial pricing guidance for that tranche is a spread of 1.95 percentage points above the benchmark.

A $112 million tranche that likewise has a five-year average life is expected to receive an AA rating from Kroll, with guidance of a 2.6-percentage-point spread to the benchmark, according to Bloomberg. A $26 million slice that Kroll is expected to rate AA- is being sold in a private placement.

The securities are secured by a mortgage covering the property, known as Project Crossing III, according to Bloomberg. Amazon's lease started in March, extends to 2041, and gives the tenant a pair of five-year renewal options. Amazon had no role in putting the financing together. Representatives for Blue Owl and Citigroup declined to comment, Bloomberg said.

In the filing, Blue Owl said the property owner entered a loan agreement on Nov. 10 with Citibank and Wells Fargo $WFC for a $512.6 million floating-rate mortgage loan. The loan matures in December 2028, with one 12-month extension option. It carries interest at one-month Term SOFR plus 2 percentage points through Nov. 9, 2027, rising to 2.25 percentage points after that through the initial maturity date.

Blue Owl Digital Infrastructure Operating Partnership provided a non-recourse guaranty for the loan, the filing said.

The property ranks as the third facility built within the Gainesville Crossing Data Center campus, according to Bloomberg. The campus is projected to grow to five buildings with a combined capacity of 326 megawatts.
