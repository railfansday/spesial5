Thieves crash stolen SUV into video game store before fleeing in getaway vehicle

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Police in Canada are searching for at least three suspects after thieves crashed a stolen SUV through the front of a video game store before escaping in a waiting getaway vehicle.

The incident occurred in the early hours of November 26, just one week after the release of GTA 6, at We Got Gamez in Whitby, Ontario, leaving the business temporarily closed as its owners assess the damage.

According to Durham Regional Police, officers responded to reports of a break-in at approximately 2:55 AM.

Two suspects exited a black SUV after driving it through the storefront before fleeing in a second vehicle. Police later confirmed that the SUV used in the break-in had been stolen.

The store was unoccupied at the time, and no injuries were reported. Authorities have not disclosed what merchandise was taken or its value.

In a series of Instagram posts, We Got Gamez shared its devastation over the incident but promised customers that the business would bounce back.

“Folks this is getting out of hand. Last night our Whitby Location was violated and broken into,” the store wrote.

A post shared by We Got Gamez (@wegotgamez1)

The owners estimate that the location will remain closed for 10 to 14 days as they clean up, account for missing merchandise, replace fixtures, and ensure the building is structurally safe for customers.

“Mike and I have had our emotional moments and now it’s game time. We promise you We Got Gamez Whitby will be back and better than ever,” the store added.

The owners also thanked the local gaming community for visiting the damaged store and offering their support following the break-in.

“Means the world and is our secret power to get back up and running.”

A post shared by We Got Gamez (@wegotgamez1)

We Got Gamez’s other locations in the GTA, Ajax and Mississauga will remain open while repairs are underway in Whitby, but the store is warning customers not to literally crash into those stores for their Black Friday door-crasher deals.

Meanwhile, police are asking anyone with information, surveillance footage, or dashcam recordings of the incident to come forward.
