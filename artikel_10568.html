McDonald’s tests major chicken menu overhaul with five hand-breaded items

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

McDonald’s has begun testing hand-battered chicken in select US markets as the fast food giant explores a potential nationwide rollout of the menu items.

The company has continued to expand its chicken offerings in recent years, with the McCrispy sandwich and McCrispy Strips becoming part of its permanent menu.

Now, McDonald’s is experimenting with a different preparation method that would see employees bread chicken by hand inside its restaurants, alongside the introduction of a new spicy chicken sandwich.

On November 23, McDonald’s franchisee account McFranchisee revealed that the chain is testing its hand-breaded chicken menu in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, and the Tucson, Arizona, area. A similar test is also taking place in Ireland.

All five items are also available as meals, with prices ranging from $8.19 to $9.99 at the location shown.

According to McFranchisee, the McSpicy marks the first time McDonald’s has offered a spicy breaded chicken sandwich of its kind in the US since a short-lived rollout in 2006. The sandwich differs from the version sold in Asian markets, which uses thigh meat.

The test is designed to assess customer demand, kitchen complexity, and the financial viability of introducing hand-breaded chicken across the country.

Some franchisees have reportedly raised concerns about the additional labor hours, kitchen operations, and potential remodeling costs associated with the preparation method.

McFranchisee previously shared footage in June showing employees preparing hand-breaded Spicy McWings, alongside comments attributed to McDonald’s CEO about introducing hand-breaded chicken in the US.
