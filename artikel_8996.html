Biarritz Building / Ariel Jacubovich | Oficina de Arquitectura

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Read the original version in Spanish here.

Text description provided by the architects. Biarritz is a 35-unit residential building in the district of San Martín, Province of Buenos Aires, developed alongside a cooperative of teachers and education workers. It is the result of a participatory process initiated in 2017 that spanned from searching for the land to the actual construction, including training workshops, debates on ways of living, and even policy proposals for democratic housing development created in collaboration with local organizations.
