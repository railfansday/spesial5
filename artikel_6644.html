Pumpkin Cheesecake Muffins

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Loaded with pumpkin seeds, canned pumpkin purée, and pumpkin pie spice, these big streusel-topped muffins are every fall lover’s dream come true. And to make things even better, the inside of each muffin is stuffed with a soft, lightly sweetened cream cheese mixture. Make the cheesecake filling and the streusel ahead of time to pace things out if it’s helpful. Don’t, even for a minute, consider using anything other than Libby’s 100% pure pumpkin purée (not pie mix). Nothing is better. We promise.

This recipe is part of Pumpkin Week 2026, Bon Appétit’s annual celebration of autumn’s most magical pantry staple.

Whisk ⅓ cup coarsely chopped raw pumpkin seeds, ¼ cup (31 g) all-purpose flour, 2 Tbsp. light brown sugar, ½ tsp. pumpkin pie spice, and a pinch of kosher salt in a small bowl to combine. Using your fingers, rub in 2 Tbsp. unsalted butter, room temperature, until moist pea-size lumps form. Chill until ready to use.

Do Ahead: Streusel can be made 1 day ahead. Cover and keep chilled.

Vigorously whisk 6 oz. cream cheese, room temperature, ¼ cup (50 g) granulated sugar, 2 tsp. all-purpose flour, 2 tsp. vanilla extract, and ½ tsp. Diamond Crystal or ¼ tsp. Morton kosher salt in a medium bowl until combined and smooth.

Do Ahead: Filling can be made 1 day ahead. Cover and chill.

Place a rack in middle of oven; preheat to 375°. Line cups of a standard 12-cup muffin pan with paper liners. Whisk 2 large eggs, 1½ cups (packed; 300 g) light brown sugar, 1 cup canned pumpkin purée, ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil, 4 Tbsp. unsalted butter, melted, slightly cooled, ¼ cup whole milk, 1 Tbsp. pumpkin pie spice, 1 Tbsp. vanilla bean paste or vanilla extract, and 1½ tsp. Diamond Crystal or 1 tsp. Morton kosher salt in a large bowl until smooth, about 1 minute. Whisk 1¾ cups (219 g) all-purpose flour and 1½ tsp. baking powder in a small bowl to combine. Add to pumpkin mixture and fold in with a rubber spatula until just incorporated and no large dry streaks of flour remain (don’t overmix; batter will be lumpy).

Bake muffins until streusel is crisp and muffins spring back when gently pressed, 24–27 minutes. Let muffins cool in pan 5 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack and let cool until just warm or completely.

Do Ahead: Muffins can be made 2 days ahead. Store airtight at room temperature.
