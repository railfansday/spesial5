Gelt Peanut Butter Blossoms

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

We started with a peanut butter blossom cookie recipe from Tammie Merrill of Wake Forest, North Carolina, and turned it into a Hanukkah treat! These gelt peanut butter blossoms, made with chocolate coins, are perfect for the holiday. For an even more festive version, roll the peanut butter cookie dough in blue and white sprinkles before baking. —Taste of Home Test Kitchen
