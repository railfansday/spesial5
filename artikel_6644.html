Do You Recognize These Past GQ Breakouts?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

After a seven-year hiatus, we’re thrilled to announce the return of GQ’s Breakouts portfolio. First launched in 2014, the series has highlighted (often young) stars just as they hit. Our group in 2026 includes Belmont Cameli of Off Campus, Owen Thiele of Adults, and Rosalind Eleazar of Misty Green. We also expanded our scope beyond actors: There’s the electronic musician duo Fcukers, the Spanish director duo Los Javis, Pablo Torre—a longtime podcaster and journalist who won the Pulitzer Prize—and many more. We suspect you’ll be seeing them around for years.

The people we’ve featured in past breakouts have often gone on to become beloved household names. For instance, when this portfolio first began, we included Shailene Woodley, Jack O’Connell, and the late Chadwick Boseman. In 2017, we featured Timothée Chalamet, now a bona fide movie star. Jack Lowden, also included that year, is a frontrunner to play James Bond. And so on.

As we bring back this portfolio, it felt only fitting to take a trip down memory lane. Everyone, after all, has to start somewhere. Here are some stars who were once GQ Breakouts:

A mansion in LA. The best party clothes of the season. An electric musical duo. A model turned actor. A comedian turned director. At least two podcasters. Come on in and meet the 13 people who just had the biggest, most-spotlight-stealing year of their lives.
