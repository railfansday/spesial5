The Mercedes-AMG GT 4-Door is back in a radically different form, packing two levels of electric performance across two variants.

The Mercedes-AMG GT 4-Door will be available in both GT55 and GT63 flavors.

The GT55 uses three electric motors to produce 805 horsepower.

The GT63 turns up the wick to 1153 horsepower and 1475 pound-feet of torque.

Given all of that motive force, the GT63 should reach 60 somewhere around the 2.0-second mark.

What’s more impressive than its straight-line performance, though, is its charging performance. It can juice up at a rate of up to 600 kilowatts when connected to the right hardware.
