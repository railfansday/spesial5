Tanggal: 2026-11-27

We're closing in on the biggest shopping day of the year. While most people will do the bulk of their shopping online for Black Friday 2026, some still prefer the in-store experience. There's something nostalgic about heading to a big box store at the crack of dawn to do some discount shopping.

If you're gearing up for that classic Black Friday in-store shopping experience, knowing when retailers close for Thanksgiving and reopen the next morning is key to planning your route. Nearly all major big-box and department stores will keep their doors locked on Thanksgiving Day (Thursday, Nov. 26), though their websites and apps will be running Black Friday deals all day long.
