Daily Dose Of Internet reveals he’s “semi-retired” after 11 years on YouTube

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Daily Dose Of Internet has revealed that he’s now “semi-retired” after more than a decade on YouTube, marking a major milestone for one of the platform’s most recognizable creators.

The YouTuber, whose real name is Jason Gryniewicz, launched his channel in 2015 and has since built an enormous following by compiling some of the funniest, strangest, and most interesting videos from across the internet.

However, after 11 years of creating content, Jason says he’s reached a point where he considers himself semi-retired.

In a November 13 post on X, Daily Dose Of Internet revealed his semi-retired status, although that doesn’t necessarily mean fans have seen the last of his popular compilations.

“I have been semi-retired since the start of 2026,” he said. “I have a team of 20+ employees that handle all the hard work. I still help look for videos and do the voice overs. Been living in Miami for the past 3 years and going on adventures with my girlfriend.”

According to the YouTuber, the past year has been the best of his life.

Interestingly, this isn’t the first time Jason has considered stepping away from the platform.

Back in 2023, Daily Dose Of Internet revealed that he nearly sold his YouTube channel for more than $10 million.

During an interview with Anthony Padilla, he explained that he had received offers of $10 million and $12 million from companies interested in acquiring the channel.

“I was really thinking about selling out and just quitting YouTube,” he admitted at the time.

Jason joins a growing number of veteran creators who have reconsidered their relationship with YouTube after years of consistent uploads.

In 2024, fellow creator Tom Scott stepped back from weekly videos after a decade, although he later returned to the platform with new projects.

In 2025, MoistCr1TiKaL revealed how he made an astronomical $35M on YouTube after disabling monetization on the platform.

For Daily Dose Of Internet, semi-retirement represents a significant change in how he describes his career, even if it doesn’t signal the end of the channel itself.
