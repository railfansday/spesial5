Spain boss denies Lamine Yamal gets 'special privileges'

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Lamine Yamal became the youngest ever player to win the European Championship when Spain triumphed at Euro 2024

Spain head coach Luis de la Fuente has denied claims Lamine Yamal received "special privileges" after being allowed to join the national team later than the rest of the squad.

The 19-year-old did not travel with Spain for Thursday's Nations League match against the Czech Republic because of discomfort and inflammation in his right knee following Barcelona's La Liga victory over Atletico Madrid, remaining in Barcelona to work with the club's medical staff.

"I decided that, to avoid the trip, it would be better for him to stay at home and continue treatment with his club," De la Fuente said on Wednesday.

"Lamine [Yamal] doesn't have any special privileges. We would have done this for any other player.

"What hurts me most is the lying, the lack of rigour, the absence of truthfulness and the fabrication. I can't stand people who try to deceive you and lie."

Real Sociedad captain Mikel Oyarzabal is also expected to join up with the squad late while recovering from flu and having just welcome his second child.

Reports in Spanish media suggested Lamine Yamal had been given special treatment because he was not required to report to the national team immediately.

De la Fuente's comments come amid continued focus on Lamine Yamal's workload, with the winger playing a leading role for both Barcelona and Spain despite still being a teenager.

Lamine Yamal has played 16 times for his club this season, scoring 13 times and producing 12 assists.

He played 45 matches last season and has played 14 times for Spain in 2026.

Lamine Yamal is expected to reunite with the Spain squad before Sunday's match against England (19:45 GMT).

De la Fuente said: "The recovery is going well and we believe he will be with us on Friday."
