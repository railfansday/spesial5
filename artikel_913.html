Van Gerwen 'preparing for the worst' as he faces Sykes in Grand Slam quarters

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Michael van Gerwen expected to face Luke Humphries in the quarter-finals but will now take on Tom Sykes and knows he needs to be prepared for a real contest; you can watch the Grand Slam of Darts in Wolverhampton live on Sky Sports until November 22

Michael van Gerwen is through to his first premier ranking quarter-final in over a year but he is up against an opponent he probably did not expect.

Van Gerwen put in a stellar display in a battle of two darting juggernauts when he defeated James Wade 10-8 to make it through to the final eight at the Grand Slam of Darts.

The 'Green Machine' battled hard against everything Wade threw at him and, with a late surge and some superb finishing, he got the victory over the line.

Now, he is preparing for a quarter-final in Wolverhampton but instead of the predicted clash with world No 2 Luke Humphries, he is up against the 72nd-ranked player on the Tour in Tom Sykes.

Sykes pulled off a stunning final-leg victory over Humphries to make the quarter-finals on his debut at the major.

The Dutchman was impressed with what he saw from the 'Castleford Tiger' and knows that if he can beat Humphries, then he needs to expect the unexpected.

"Tom did really well against Luke. I didn't expect that, I think no one expected that, because this is the first time he's played such a long format on such a big TV appearance," he said.

"A win is a win and moving forward I think this gives me a lot of confidence.

"I have to face Tom who had a fantastic game against Luke, 100 average, loads of 180s, so I need to make sure I am prepared for anything.

"He is a fantastic player; he's been doing really well lately on the Tour, but now he has to deal with the fact that he's going to be first to 16. He's never been in this situation, so it will be new for him as well.

"But don't get me wrong, if you can beat Luke, he can beat anyone, so I need to make sure I prepare myself for the worst."

Van Gerwen has had well-documented ups and downs in his darting career over the last two years but has had glimpses of the levels everyone knows he can reach, his massive win against Wade the latest.

He admitted that the victory, and the manner of it, helped him overcome some demons about his own game and he is now looking to turn that into consistency.

"Yes, I did (overcome some demons). Of course, when you're not doing what you are capable of, it can be a really frustrating game," Van Gerwen added.

"But then to finally do it and also in the way how I did it, I think that gives me energy also towards the next game.

"I always believe in my own ability, otherwise I wouldn't be here. If I think I can't believe in myself anymore, that's the day I have to stop playing darts.

"But that day is not there, and it won't be there in the next few years, it is as simple as that.

"My consistency is starting to get better, my belief in my own ability starts to get better, if you also look towards the Pro Tours and everything, my game starts to get somewhere, and that's what you want, that's where you put the work in and the effort.

"That is how darts is, and darts can be a really cruel game, but at the moment I am still in so that's a good thing."
