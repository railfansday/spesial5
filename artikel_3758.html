Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair: Definitive Edition announced for PS5, Switch 2, and PC

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Playtonic Games has announced Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair: Definitive Edition, an enhanced re-release of the 2019-released side-scrolling platformer. It will launch for PlayStation 5, Switch 2, and PC via Steam on December 15 as a free upgrade for existing owners.

Here is an overview of the game, via Playtonic Games:

One of the best platforming adventures of its generation is back, enhanced for PlayStation 5, Switch 2, and PC. Yooka and Laylee return for a full-scale 2.5D adventure, with a 3D Overworld that’s an adventure in its own right, levels you can alter to create new ways to play, and Capital B’s Impossible Lair waiting to test your skills.

Explore vibrant worlds packed with enemies, secrets, and collectables. Complete objectives, uncover hidden paths, meet a colorful cast of characters, and rescue the Royal Beettalion as you prepare for Yooka and Laylee’s biggest test yet.

Optimized for PlayStation 5, Switch 2, and PC

Enjoy sharper visuals, smoother performance, enhanced visual effects, and more!

All the bees in the Royal Beettallion have been bee-napped! Every level beaten is a bee rescued (and one more hit you can survive in the Impossible Lair itself!). Do you beat every course to rebuild the Beettallion or take on the Impossible Lair early to see how you fare?

Run, jump, roll, and dodge your way through the many levels, then take them on again in radically altered forms!

Dash through turret-filled towns, spider-infested forests, trap-happy factories, and even airships soaring amongst the clouds.

Find secret T.W.I.T. Coins to bypass the nefarious Trowzer’s paywalls scattered across the Overworld and unlock new areas with more levels to discover.

The more you explore the adventure-filled Overworld, the more levels you’ll find. Solve its many mysteries, open up new routes, and complete its challenges to help rescue every last member of the Royal Beettalion.

Think you’ve got a level figured out? Think again.

Out in the Overworld, find ways to flood levels, freeze them, or even flip them upside down to unlock alternative routes, layouts, and even crazier surprises.

Discover over 60 hidden Tonics to unlock new skins, visual effects, challenges, skills, and perks. Mix and match them to create crazy play styles and OP combos!

The toughest platforming challenge your skills can take on!

Tackle the Impossible Lair at any time! Of course, the more levels you beat, the easier it gets. You choose how challenging you want it to be(e)!
