All boss locations in Fortnite Treasure Hunters

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Around the Fortnite Treasure Hunters island, there are quite a few bosses you can find and take on in battle, with new ones arriving on a fairly consistent basis. Various quests challenge you to seek out these foes and take them out in battle, which means you need to know where they spawn.

Tracking down any specific character around the island is tough, even on a mini-sized island like the one present in Treasure Hunters, as there’s lots of ground to explore and plenty of spots they could be hiding. If you’re having trouble tracking them all down, here are all boss locations in Fortnite Treasure Hunters.

As of the time of writing, there are three bosses you can find around the Fortnite Treasure Hunters island. All of them offer a special weapon from the loot pool, alongside some basic drops like ammo and healing items. Here’s a full breakdown of all key details for each one.

Additional bosses may arrive on the island as the mini-season progresses, as there are big weekly updates with loads of new content. If any others are introduced, we’ll add them here.

The A$AP Rocky boss only ever spawns at The $.$. Rocky location. This key POI is situated near the middle eastern section of the island. At this spot, you can find him wandering around slightly north of the boat, standing on the beachy area nearby.

You can find the Lil Uzi Vert boss hanging around Lil’ Uzi’s Stronghold. This location can be found near the upper northeast corner of the island. He wanders around the area packed with buildings at this spot, so he can be a bit tough to pinpoint.

Buggy spawns at Buggy’s Beachhead, which was previously known as Loot Lagoon prior to his arrival. You can find this POI near the northern middle section of the island. He spawns outside of the large, shark-themed boat parked at this location.

If you visit any of the boss spawn spots and don’t see them there, this means another player found and defeated them first. Each boss can only be fought once per round, so to ensure you don’t miss out on one of these battles, it’s a good idea to head for one of the boss spawn locations straight out of the battle bus.
