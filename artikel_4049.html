How To Unlock The Key Cloner In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Your first step to getting better cars in Grand Theft Auto 6 is the Key Cloner. Here's how to unlock it.

Stealing cars is one of the most important parts of any GTA game, but Grand Theft Auto 6 puts more focus on it than ever before, with more rules and regulations that you need to be aware of.

While basic cars can be stolen with no more than a broken window and some hotwiring, anything slightly nice will take a lot more work, particularly the unlocking of a device called the Key Cloner.

If you try to steal any nice cars in Grand Theft Auto 6, you're incredibly likely to see a greyed-out message telling you to clone a key. This is because the car has more advanced security that won't let you simply hotwire it and take it on the run. Similarly to the advanced tracker, you'll need to make some progress in the story to unlock the Key Cloner and be able to steal expensive cars.

You can easily see if a car needs a Key Cloner by using the WaINk app to scan it. You'll see "Key Cloner" under the access category.

You'll really need to put in the work if you want GTA 6's best vehicles. Here's how it's done.

Luckily, you won't have to make it nearly as far to get the Key Cloner as you do to get the ability to remove advanced trackers. The Key Cloner is unlocked during Chapter 3, and you'll need to have met Randy as part of the main story. Once you've completed the "Crosstown Traffic" mission for Raul, you'll be able to "Steal the Zorrusso for Randy", which is part of the Clonin' Keys and Makin' G's mission.

During this mission, Randy will send you the Key Cloner app, which you'll have access to from then on and works similarly to hotwiring by finding a sweet spot with the right stick. Of course, being able to drive the fancy cars doesn't quite fix all your problems, as you'll need to get much further to unlock the advanced tracker removal. Still, you'll be able to take expensive cars and sell them to Randy with ease now, which is a great way to make money at that point in the game.

Fast and Furious fans have an extra special easter egg to find in GTA 6: Paul Walker's Mitsubishi Eclipse from the first film.
