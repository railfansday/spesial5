GTA 6 Gets Its First Unboxing Video As Shipping Begins For Xbox & PS5

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Here's a look at what's inside the physical edition

While Rockstar Games mentioned a date of November 12th for shipping the physical edition of GTA 6 for Xbox and PS5 the other day, we noticed other retailers stating the 11th instead, so it's no surprise that it's showing up in the wild.

The first unboxing video for GTA 6 seemingly arrived overnight, racking up over 1 million views already on social media since it was posted. This specific video is for the PS5 version, but we expect no major differences for the Xbox version.

Since this was posted, other unboxing videos have also started to hit the web, like you see in the YouTube video at the top of this article. It clearly shows that GTA 6 comes with a download code, a separate code for Ultimate Edition content where applicable, and a double-sided poster as had been confirmed previously.

It also now affirms that the poster contains the GTA 6 cover art on the front, and a piece of artwork for a female character on the back - not Lucia or Jason, but presumably a character that's heavily involved in the story regardless.

We've obviously talked a bit about the physical edition of GTA 6 already this week, and you can vote in the poll below if you didn't get a chance the first time, but we thought an actual unboxing video was worth a bit of extra coverage - an opportunity to see exactly what you'll be getting inside the physical edition if you've opted for it.
