How To Get Your Free Snom Ornament From The Pokemon Center

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Between games, plushes, and trading cards, being a Pokemon fan can get pretty pricey. That’s why it’s always welcome when The Pokemon Company gives its fans something for free, and if you’ve got more merch to buy heading into the holidays, then there might be a new ornament waiting for you when you head to the Pokemon Center checkout.

The promotion kicked off on November 23, 2026, which means it’s live right now, and it’ll bag you a free Snom ornament to hang from your Christmas tree - or wherever else you might want to hang it - provided you meet all of the criteria for a qualifying purchase.

The first thing you need to do is make sure you try to get your Snom before it’s too late. The promotion will run until 11:59 pm PST on December 25, 2026, so you have plenty of time. However, that’s only if Pokemon doesn’t run out of Snom ornaments. This is a “while stocks last” situation, and there’s no indication of just how many Snom Pokemon has to go around.

Don't hang around if you missed them the first time. They'll be gone again before you know it.

As long as the deadline hasn’t passed and Pokemon still has them in stock, you will qualify for a free Snom ornament when you spend $50 or more in a single transaction at the Pokemon Center. The only caveat is pre-order and digital products don’t qualify, so no buying stuff that isn’t out yet, and digital versions of games won’t count towards your $50 total.

There is no code to enter at checkout. The Snom ornament should simply be added to your cart when you go to pay and be shipped along with the rest of your order. Just make sure it’s there before you actually check out. The ornaments are limited to one per customer, meaning you won’t get two if you spend over $100, and if you want a refund on anything in your order, the ornament has to be returned along with it.

Pokemon’s holiday collection is now live, so you can shop from that to get your free ornament, and you can also buy all of Pokemon’s Lego sets so far through the Pokemon Center. That includes the Iconic Trainer Moments Poke Ball set which has Red & Blue easter eggs hiding inside. You can get that set for less on Amazon right now, but buying it there means you won’t get your Snom.
