Danganronpa Creator Says Physical Game Production Is Reliant On Day-One Patches

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

"We barely pass platform certification requirements"

Kazutaka Kodaka, creator of Danganronpa and The Hundred Line, has made a pretty incredible admission regarding the production of physical games.

His comments were made during a roundtable discussion at the SKY31 Convention Seoul (thanks, Kotaku) during which he explains how developer Too Kyo Games is able to meet ongoing demand for physical games, particularly in Japan.

Essentially, the production of physical games requires the original master to be submitted much earlier than digital titles. This is to account for the extended time required to pack the items, especially if it's a collector's edition.

So Kodaka-san confessed that the studio often submits builds that, in his words, shouldn't be played by anyone. He operates under the assumption that everyone has access to the internet these days and is therefore able to download a day-one patch to bring the game up to an acceptable standard.

“Honestly, we approach it with the mindset of finalizing the master via patches. Collector’s editions in North America or Europe take an enormous amount of time to pack with figures, so the master submission deadline is absurdly early.

"To be frank, we sometimes submit builds where we think, ‘No one should ever play it in this state.’ We barely pass platform certification requirements and release it while believing no one plays games without an internet connection these days.”

He goes on to jokingly say "I’m not sure if I should be making such risky confessions in a venue like this". To be fair though, it sounds like Kodaka is merely being honest and open about a practice that is widely adopted by the industry at large.

Hell, even Ocarina of Time received a day-one patch. Granted, this was primarily to enable connection to Zelda Notes, but if you bought the physical and couldn't update it for whatever reason, you wouldn't be able to use this feature.

Kodaka's next project is Danganronpa 2x2 for the Switch and Switch 2. In somewhat related news, it was revealed earlier this year that the collector's editions on Switch 2 would no longer come with a full cartridge, with Spike Chunsoft instead opting for a Game-Key Card.
