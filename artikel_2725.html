Lakers honor longtime PA announcer Lawrence Tanter

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Los Angeles Lakers honored longtime public address announcer Lawrence Tanter by unveiling a plaque at Crypto.com Arena on Monday, Nov. 23.

Tanter spent 43 seasons in that role for the franchise before his death at 76 on Sept. 23. Tanter had officially retired in June.

He had called more than 2,100 games, including 14 NBA Finals series and four NBA All-Star Games, during his tenure.

The Lakers played a tribute video during the game that included remarks from Lakers legends Michael Cooper and Mychal Thompson.

Fans were also given the opportunity to write messages and memories in honor of Tanter in the Star Plaza outside of the Lakers' home arena.

Lakers coach JJ Redick also shared some thoughts about Tanter before the game against the Utah Jazz on Monday.

"He's an iconic voice," Redick told reporters. "He made a mark not only on the Lakers but the NBA."

"The first time I ever got to play in Staples Center, the curtains dropped from the scoreboard and the music came on and you hear his voice. …  It just felt like I made the NBA. He was an integral part of what made this place so special."
