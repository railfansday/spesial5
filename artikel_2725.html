Game Recap: Suns bench pours in 81 points in blowout win over Clippers, 133-110

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Do you see what happens when you go from Victor Wembanyama to *checks notes* Brook Lopez? (with all due respect to the vet)

The Suns' second unit took this one over early and never gave it back. Phoenix beat the Clippers 133-110 Tuesday night, improving to 4-6. The bench poured in 81 points, Phoenix hit 22 threes to LA's 15, and the Suns led for 44 of the 48 minutes. Seven Suns finished in double figures.

It was a much-needed bounceback win for the Suns. We are now 10 games deep into the NBA season, and Phoenix sits at 4-6 after a brutal schedule to open up. All things considered, it could be worse.

Jordan Goodwin led Phoenix with 18 points in under 14 minutes, going 6-of-7 from the field with all six makes from three. Devin Booker added 17 points on 6-of-14 shooting with 5 assists. Luke Kennard scored 14 on 5-of-7 shooting, and Jalen Green had 13 on 5-of-7, including 3-of-4 from deep, with 5 rebounds. Collin Gillespie scored 12 points, all on threes (4-of-9), and dished out a game-high 8 assists. Man Man finished with 11 points on 4-of-5 shooting, 7 rebounds, and 3 assists. Ryan Dunn added 10 points on 4-of-5 shooting and 8 rebounds, and Oso Ighodaro grabbed 8 rebounds and finished +18.

Darius Garland and Rui Hachimura each scored 13 for Los Angeles. Garland shot 5-of-12 with 6 assists, and Hachimura went 4-of-10 with 3 threes and 5 rebounds. Baba Miller also had 13, all in the fourth quarter, on 4-of-4 shooting. Max Strus added 12 points, and Derrick Jones Jr. and Keaton Wagler each scored 11.

The Suns jumped out to an early 6-2 lead, led by 4 points from Devin Booker.

This quarter was tight for most of the first quarter. Los Angeles took an 18-16 lead behind 8 points from Rui Hachimura, who hit two of his three attempts from deep. Then the Phoenix bench took over.

The Suns closed the period on a 13-0 run. Collin Gillespie went 2-for-2 from three, Ryan Dunn was perfect at the rim, and Jordan Goodwin added a triple.

The Phoenix bench outscored LA's reserves 13-0, and the Clippers' second unit went 0-for-4 from the field.

Man Man didn't attempt a shot, but he still had 3 rebounds and 3 assists in under five minutes. Phoenix won the glass 17-8 and the paint 12-4. Devin Booker led all scorers with 8, though he turned it over three times.

Phoenix led after the first quarter, 29-18.

Jordan Goodwin turned the second quarter into his own shootaround. He went 4-for-4 from three for 12 points in just over six minutes, and Luke Kennard added two more triples off the bench. Phoenix shot 8-of-14 from deep in the quarter.

The Clippers found more offense this time, shooting 50 percent with eight different players scoring. Isaiah Jackson and Max Strus gave LA's second unit some life after it went scoreless in the first. Phoenix still stretched the lead to 17 at 48-31, its biggest of the half.

Devin Booker went 0-for-4 from the field in the quarter, and his only point came at the line. Ryan Dunn did not play in the second half after his strong first quarter. Collin Gillespie drilled a deep three at the buzzer to close out the half.

The Suns opened the second half with a bang.

Miles Bridges finally got going after a scoreless first half. He scored 8 points in the third on 3-of-4 shooting, including 2-of-3 from deep.

Devin Booker played all 12 minutes and found his rhythm with 8 points on 2-of-4 from three. Phoenix hit 7 threes in the quarter, while LA went 4-for-15 from deep.

The Suns did damage on the offensive glass. They scored 16 second-chance points in the quarter, compared to 7 for the Clippers, and the lead hit 32 at 91-59.

Man Man added 7 points on 2-of-2 shooting and a perfect 3-of-3 at the line.

With a 27-point cushion, both benches took over. No Phoenix starter played in the fourth, and the Suns closed with an all-reserve group.

Luke Kennard led the way with 8 points. Jamaree Bouyea made all five of his shots, going 2-of-2 from the field and 3-of-3 at the line for 7 points, and added 3 assists. Koa Peat chipped in 5 points, and Rasheer Fleming knocked down his only three. Phoenix shot 12-of-16 in the quarter.

Baba Miller made the most of his minutes for Los Angeles, scoring 13 points in the fourth on 4-of-4 shooting and 4-of-6 at the line. The Clippers took the quarter 38-34, helped by six Phoenix turnovers.

The Suns' never-ending homestand continues as they take on the Hornets on Thursday night.
