Chicago Bears vs. Detroit Lions – Keys to a Chicago victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Happy Thanksgiving! It's shaping up to be a great weekend for watching football while eating and spending time with family or friends.

Things got off t0 a good start last night, as the Green Bay Packers lost another game, taking a massive hit to their playoff chances.

Now it's up to the Chicago Bears to take control of business against the Detroit Lions this afternoon.

The clash of 8-2 teams looks to be a fantastic game and will give the winner control of the NFC North heading into December.

In case you haven't heard, Ben Johnson and Caleb Williams have yet to beat Dan Campbell and the Lions in their careers.

Sam: Ben Johnson surely wants to avenge the two losses to his coaching mentor from a season ago. The Bears NEED to win to stay in the thick of the division. This game is, perhaps too simply, a game in the trenches. Whichever defensive line and offensive line wins is going to win the game. Stop Jahmyr Gibbs and pressure Goff, establish the run with Swift and Monangai. "Easy" as that.

Jacob: The Bears need to hold up in pass protection to allow the intermediate-to-deep passes — a staple of their passing attack — time to develop. Not having to face Aidan Hutchinson should make things easier, but the Lions could take on a blitz-heavy approach to dial up the pressure against Caleb Williams. A lot of the Bears' struggles on offense have come from the trenches, and that will need to be fixed in a critical game within the NFC North.

Josh: Chicago needs to be more physical. That doesn't just mean they need to impose their will at the line of scrimmage, but that is definitely true. They also need to make the hard tackles and they need to extend plays after contact. Dan Campbell is going to want to take Ben's lunch money, and Chicago needs to say enough is enough and just win out of pure cussedness.

TJ: The Bears need to maintain composure in all aspects of the game in order to leave with their first division win of the year. They can't succumb to penalties, turnovers, or other dumb mistakes. On defense, stay disciplined and limit the damage done by St. Brown and Gibbs. On offense, lean on the ground game and hopefully keep time of possession in Chicago's favor.

DGooch: The key to the game, as usual, starts with the run game. The Bears need to establish the run with Guy and Swift, something they struggled to do with the Saints this past Sunday, and really for the last five weeks (3 yards per carry average). I think they should try out Jones over Bradbury, who has been pretty awful, but I doubt the Bears go that route on a short week. So – run to the Wright (see what I did there) and Jackson side–the Bears dominant run direction and where the Lions will be missing Hutch. The Lions secondary has Branch and Joseph back, but it is still their primary weakness, a weakness Caleb needs to exploit while protecting the ball. On defense, I think Allen needs to increase his blitz calls – yes he can get pressure without it now that Big Jeff is in the middle – but making Goff uncomfortable in the pocket is, well, key. And run blitzing Gibbs may limit his production.

Mongo Peanut: OL play needs to be better, especially on the interior. The linchpin to this team's success in 2025 was how good we were there. Obviously, losing a center like Dalman has been hard to replace, but the Bears are averaging under 4 yards per carry since Week 5; much of that is due to the decline in play on the interior. Caleb's pressure rate has also spiked, largely in part due to the same issue. Fix that, and a lot of the nagging offensive annoyances go away. On defense, we just need to keep doing what we've been doing up front, pressure the QB, and make his life as difficult as possible. Jeffery Simmons allows a bit more leeway with how to attack Goff, which equalizes things a bit when it comes to their WRs and their ability to generate YAC.

JB: Roast lion – You want to make sure that you pre-heat the oven first thing in the morning so that you're ready to go out of the gate. No slow starts. Next, use short to intermediate cuts, letting the knife Rome through the length of the meat to unBurden its defenses. Next, take your Monangai mallet and pound the lion chunks with Swift movements until flattened and tenderized. Finally, place the lion into the oven at a Sweat-inducing 550 degrees to broil, making sure to turnover at least twice. On the stovetop, bring your gravy to a Simmonser and top your lion for a satisfying win on Thanksgiving that the whole family will love.

ECD: Offense – Get ready for a shootout and don't let off the gas until the clock reaches 00:00. The Bears' supporting cast on offense matches up fairly well against a Lions defense that no longer has Aiden Hutchinson. Just keep DT Alim McNeill in check as much as possible.

Defense – Take the ball away any time the opportunity presents itself. You're not going to win a foot race against Jahmyr Gibbs, and tackling has to be sure-handed with Amon-Ra St. Brown looming large.

Special Teams – Do not give standout ace Trevor Nowaske any chances to make plays on the ball. Also be on alert for fake punts or field goals as they have a crafty SPC in Dave Fipp.

What are your keys to the game? Drop 'em in the comments and let us know your prediction, too.
