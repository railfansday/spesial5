Virginia lands a commitment from in-state 2028 OL Alvin Holland

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Suffolk (Va.) King's Fork offensive tackle Alvin Holland tells Rivals that he has committed to Virginia.

The in-state Cavaliers offered the 6-foot-4, 267-pound Holland back in June. He is commit No. 2 for head coach Tony Elliott and his staff in the 2028 cycle.

"The relationships I have with the coaches," Holland said. He has spent a lot of time on campus. "Going down to the facilities, seeing the campus, everything in Charlottesville makes it a legit home."

Holland chose to play for Virginia over an offer list that also included Wake Forest, Penn State, Pittsburgh, South Carolina, Syracuse and Duke. He joins a class that also includes quarterback Grayson Clary.

"I know that Coach Elliott will have everything turned around very soon," Holland said. "I see a possible win out for the last few games."
