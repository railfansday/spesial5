Packers receive huge boost on Josh Jacobs’ recovery timeline after finger surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Green Bay Packers received positive news on Josh Jacobs' recovery Saturday. The Packers' veteran running back successfully underwent surgery to repair his fractured right ring finger and is expected to be back on the field within the next few weeks.

Tom Pelissero broke the news of Jacobs' successful surgery through a Saturday post on X. The update comes just ahead of Green Bay's Week 10 meeting with Minnesota, giving fans a better idea of when the Packers could have their starting running back back in action. The 6-2 Vikings visit Lambeau Field on Sunday for a 1 p.m. ET kickoff, with Fox airing the game.

"Packers RB Josh Jacobs had successful surgery on his fractured right ring finger Friday in Los Angeles with Dr. Steven Shin, the noted hand and wrist surgeon, and should be back in the next few weeks," wrote the NFL insider.

Jacobs hurt his finger in the opening quarter of Green Bay's 45-27 win against New England last Sunday. Before leaving the game, the three-time Pro Bowler managed only four carries for five yards. Head coach Matt LaFleur had not ruled out an injured reserve stint, a move that would sideline the running back for at least four games. The latest update, however, points to a potentially shorter absence.

The Packers running back has played only three times this season after sitting out Green Bay's opening six games on the commissioner's exempt list. The 28-year-old has totaled 80 rushing yards and a touchdown on 36 attempts, averaging 2.2 yards per carry. He has also added 11 catches for 68 yards and two scores through the air, giving him 148 yards from scrimmage overall.

Green Bay Packers running back Josh Jacobs (8) runs against Detroit Lions during the first half at Ford Field in Detroit on Sunday, Oct. 25, 2026.

Green Bay will need its other running backs to shoulder more of the workload while Jacobs recovers. Kaleb Johnson made a strong case for an expanded role against New England, finishing with 68 yards and a touchdown on 14 carries. Pierre Strong Jr. also contributed, gaining 22 yards on eight rushing attempts.

The Packers head into Sunday's NFC North matchup against the Minnesota Vikings looking to carry their recent success into another divisional test. With Jacobs out of the lineup, Green Bay will turn to its remaining running backs to help sustain that momentum while the team waits for its starter to recover.
