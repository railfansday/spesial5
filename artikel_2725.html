The first 21 chapters of Quarterback of the Future are available

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

For the 25th anniversary of the launch of PFT, I decided to post the rewritten Quarterback of the Future — one chapter per day.

Three weeks in, 21 chapters are available. The full menu is here, with a button at the bottom of each chapter to quickly move to the next.

The chapters are short. Which means there are plenty of them. But each one reads quickly and, ideally, tempts the reader to read the next one.

It’s the writer’s job to make the reader want to do that. If you haven’t started, here’s Chapter One. If you like it, click to Chapter Two.

I may accelerate the process as we get into the holiday season, possibly increasing to two or three per day.

If you want to wait until more are posted, that’s fine. All chapters will remain up until all of them are posted. After that, we’ll leave it up for a while, if you want to be able to read the whole thing without getting to the end of what’s been released and don’t want to wait.

The whole thing is a thank you to those of you who have been out there since the early days, when we didn’t know what we were doing and it looked like it.
