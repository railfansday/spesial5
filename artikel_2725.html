The Colts playing Daniel Jones is indefensible

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Why? Just why? Why is Daniel Jones out there for the Indianapolis Colts? It literally doesn't get any worse, so maybe that's the reason. Maybe it's the $50 million Chris Ballard gave him this year. Maybe he has something on Shane Steichen? I don't know what it is. All I know is Jones shouldn't see the field again this year.

Jones started his career with the Colts in the most incredible fashion. It is ending that way too. Incredibly. It is incredible how he is playing right now. A 1.7 QBR is the worst of any starting quarterback this year. That's worse than Baker Mayfield completing zero passes. That's worse than Carson Wentz on Monday Night Football against the Bills. Both of those performances were dreadful statistically. Jones beat them both.

This wasn't against the Vikings' defense. This wasn't him trying to keep pace with the best offense in the league. This was against the 1-7 Dolphins who ran a quarterback out there for his first start. Jones threw two picks and had 108 yards. Not all picks are fully on the quarterback, but the one returned for a touchdown could have been thrown better. Everything this year for Jones could be better. Not that the options to replace him are glowing, but Shane Steichen sticking with him at this point is insanity. Nothing tells you he is the guy.

Wanting a change at quarterback isn't just whiny fandom. It is a legitimate concern. Daniel Jones was horrid against one of the worst teams in the league. Things don't get any easier than a home game against the Dolphins. Yes, talent has been depleted due to trades, but Jones has to be better. If he can't be, then he needs to be done. The Colts have to make the change.
