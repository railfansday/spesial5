Cowboys vs. Titans: Offensive, defensive impact players in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

If there's such a thing as a cakewalk victory in the NFL, this Week 11 matchup with the Tennessee Titans could be it for the Dallas Cowboys. The Titans currently holds the No. 1 overall pick in the 2027 NFL Draft, and as such, are considered one of the worst teams in the league. That should equate to an easy "W" for the Cowboys.

Since there's no such thing as a guaranteed victory in the NFL, the Cowboys still need to remain vigilant this week and do everything within their power to secure their fifth victory of the season. In order to do that they need everyone to play at their best. Some players have a way of impacting the game more than others, which we've identified below.

Okay, it's somewhat cheating to pick the starting QB as the offensive impact player this week, but that's exactly what we're going to do anyway. Dak Prescott should have a huge game in this Week 11 matchup against the Tennessee Titans. The Titans secondary currently ranks dead last (32nd) in the league in passing yards allowed (266.9) and No. 4 should be able to pick them apart at will.

With the weapons at his disposal, Prescott should be able to slice and dice Tennessee's secondary. George Pickens could have another big game after last week's 145-yard, three-touchdown performance and CeeDee Lamb should hit the 1,000-yard receiving mark (currently sitting at 976 yards). Throw in Ryan Flournoy's clutch catches when needed and Dallas' aerial attack should be lethal this week.

The last two games have been interesting for Caleb Downs, the Cowboys first-round pick this year. He played arguably the best game of his young career in Week 9 against the Indianapolis Colts, but maybe one of his worst last week against the San Francisco 49ers. Experiencing ups and downs for a first-year player is be somewhat expected, but we believe he will bounce back in a big way this week against the Titans.

With Christian Parker losing his playcalling duties we believe No. 13 will be put in better position to play to his strengths in Week 11. Mike Petine will likely have him playing more around line of scrimmage, quite possibly using him as a blitzer more this week than we've seen in past weeks. Against a second-year QB and a talent deprived offense, we fully expect Downs to make several impact plays to help secure the "W" for the Cowboys.
