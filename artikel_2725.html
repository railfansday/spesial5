Cowboys injuries: Caleb Downs, Jake Ferguson DNP

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Nowhere to go but up. After the Dallas Cowboys squandered another late-game lead to fall to 3-5, they are back to the drawing board. The Cowboys take on the Indianapolis Colts this week. The Colts have fired their general manager and traded multiple former franchise cornerstones, waving the proverbial white flag on the 2026 season.

The Cowboys open the week in questionable physical shape after last week's game after having some players leave the contest. After the first practice of the week, here is today's injury report.

For Dallas, Caleb Downs (Concussion) did not practice as he sat out in the league's concussion protocol. Also in the protocol was safety P.J. Locke.

KaVontae Turpin (Ribs) was also cited as a DNP as he hopes to return this week after a two-game absence. Jake Ferguson (Ankle) did not practice today. He left the game with an injury last week and did not work into team activities today. Edge rusher Malachi Lawrence (Ankle) was also did not practice today. The team expects him to miss a few weeks.

Dee Winters (Calf) was limited in practice today. Meanwhile tight end Luke Schoonmaker (Groin) and George Pickens (Hamstring) were also limited.

As for Indianapolis, they gave their veterans the day off. Keenan Allen and Charvarius Ward took days off. Running back Jonathan Taylor was limited but also take a lighter load that was not related to injury.
