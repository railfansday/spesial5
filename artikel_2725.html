Drake Maye reportedly could miss remainder of season with 'significant lower leg injury,' Pats fear ankle fracture

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

New England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye reportedly could potentially miss the remainder of the season with what is described as a "significant lower-leg injury," NFL Network reported early Monday.

The Patriots reportedly fear Maye could be dealing with a fractured ankle, per ESPN.

Maye did not travel with the team to the United States after the contest, instead staying in Germany to undergo additional testing. The injury could require surgery and Maye is looking at a lengthy absence, if not a season-ending one.

Maye was knocked out of Sunday's loss against the Detroit Lions in Munich following the team's first offensive drive. Maye, who was sacked to end the drive, appeared to sustain an ankle injury on the play. When the Patriots took the field for their second offensive drive of the day, Maye went to the locker room.

He was replaced by backup Tommy DeVito, who finished 12-for-24 passing for 81 yards, 1 touchdown and 1 interception. He was sacked four times in Detroit's 35-10 victory.

The Patriots (5-5) quickly ruled Maye as questionable to return due to an ankle injury. He was eventually ruled out for the contest.

New England has a bye next week before facing the Los Angeles Chargers on Nov. 29.

Maye wasn't the only player injured on the play. Detroit Lions star Aidan Hutchinson, who sacked Maye on the play, sustained a knee injury after celebrating the sack. He walked off the field under his own power and visited the blue medical tent on the sideline before walking to the locker room. Hutchinson was later ruled out.
