Dolphins news: Week 8 position grades, trade rumors, and loss of a former player

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Miami Dolphins are preparing to face the Detroit Lions in Week 9, but their Week 8 loss to the New England Patriots is still being dissected. What went well and what did not work as Miami fell to 1-6 in the 2026 NFL season?

Tyler Carmona, Miami Dolphins on SI writer, took a look at the performance from each position group during the game, providing a grade for each. The wide receivers, defensive line, and linebackers all received the top grades for the week, a B for each of those groups. About the defensive line, Carmona wrote:

Josh Uche has pretty much defined serviceable this season.

He might not be keeping offensive tackles awake at night, but he generally does his job.

On a misdirection run, much of the defense followed Corey Kiner's jet motion. Uche stayed home and was able to corral Rhamondre Stevenson. He also finished with four tackles and a third-down sack that dropped Maye for a 10-yard loss.

Chop Robinson added two sacks and showed some versatility beyond rushing the passer. On third-and-5 with 9:22 left in the first quarter, he bluffed a rush before dropping into the middle of the field, helping take away an option as Maye threw incomplete.

Robinson also had a chance to bring Maye down before DeMario Douglas' 48-yard touchdown and couldn't finish it.
