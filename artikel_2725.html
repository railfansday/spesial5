PSG midfielder Fabián Ruiz reveals he ‘would like to end’ his career at Real Betis

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Fabián Ruiz (30) has never made a secret of his attachment to Real Betis. Having come through the Andalusian club's academy, the Spanish midfielder cut his teeth there, eventually making his professional debut in 2014, at a time when Los Béticos were still playing in the second division.

After moving to Napoli in 2018, Ruiz made a name for himself there, before truly coming into his own whilst playing for Paris Saint-Germain, where he won every possible title, and establishing himself in the national team, with whom he notably won the World Cup last summer.

Now aged 30, the Los Palacios y Villafranca native has clearly not forgotten his roots. And eight years after leaving the club where he spent 14 years of his life, Ruiz hopes one day to have the opportunity to wear their colours once again. "I'd like to finish my career there, spend my final years at Betis, back home," he told Spanish media outlet Okdiario. "I hope that will happen one day and that I'll be able to wear the Betis shirt again."
