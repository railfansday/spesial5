Russell Wilson says he’s only ever seen two NFL players who can match what Joe Burrow is doing now

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Joe Burrow is putting up elite numbers for the Cincinnati Bengals that are unmatched in the NFL this season.

Cincinnati Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow is firmly in the conversation for the 2026 MVP Award, an honor he has never won before.

Through Week 10, Joe Burrow leads the NFL for touchdowns with 27 in total. He scored four against the Steelers, following Peyton Manning in 2004 by becoming only the second quarterback to throw five in four consecutive games.

Former NFL quarterback Russell Wilson is in awe of Burrow's numbers – and says that in his opinion, only two players have had such an electric start to the season. Manning was not one of the two players he named.

He cited Aaron Rodgers and Patrick Mahomes as the two players who remind him of Burrow's prolific play.

Wilson told NFL Today: "When I think about what Joe Burrow's doing, I mean, guys, there's only been a couple of guys to ever do it.

"And we talked about Patrick Mahomes, how great he's been all these years. We know how "great that's been. You know how dominant he is. And when he won MVP one of those years, it was special, the touchdown rates and all that.

"He had, I think, 28 touchdowns in one season, and at this point, right through Week 10, Aaron Rodgers had around 23 touchdowns."

Russell Wilson is right in his assessment, that Joe Burrow's numbers are such a rarity that he is in elite company.

But there are three NFL quarterbacks in history who have scored more touchdowns than Joe Burrow through the opening 10 weeks.

Peyton Manning, Denver Broncos 2013: 33 touchdowns

Tom Brady, New England Patriots 2007*: 31 touchdowns

Patrick Mahomes, Kansas City Chiefs 2018: 28 touchdowns

*Brady had 27 through 10 weeks, but as one of those was a bye week, his 10th game bumps his numbers up to 31.

Wilson should not have forgotten his Broncos predecessor Peyton Manning, who ended the 2013 season with 55 passing touchdowns.

Burrow is incredible – but even those numbers may be beyond him.
