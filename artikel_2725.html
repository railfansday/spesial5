Stat dump: Key numbers and observations from the Seahawks Week 8 loss to the Bears

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

(Editor's note: The following is a guest post by Ryan Woodall, assistant coach at Edmonds-Woodway High School in Edmonds, Washington)

Here is an advanced stats dump from the Seattle Seahawks' Week 8 loss to the Chicago Bears on Monday Night Football. These stats include some season-long observations, tendency breakers, and comparisons to other stretches of the season. There's also a stats round-up from across social media that may be of interest to you at the end of this article!

Most 11 personnel (59%) this season (11p = 1 RB, 1 TE), 2nd lowest 12p (18%) by the Seahawks this season, Most 21p (18%) since week 2.

Bears allowed 4.5 YD/PL, their avg Weeks 1-7 was 6.3.

Red zone struggles continued, TD on 1-4 trips. Bears averaged 65% allowed W1-7

Average drive distance (22ish) and plays/drive (5) stayed the same as season. Bears DEF reduced their yards allowed by 7 (30 -> 23).

Didn't get into a 3rd and 3 or less all game. Went 1/11 on 3rd down overall. 8/11 3rd downs were 7+

Lowest dropback success of season (35%). 2nd game with < 40% Dropback success. The 49ers (38%) was the other game.

2nd lowest explosive pass (6%), DEN was lowest (4%). 4th game < 10%.

2nd highest shotgun usage (63%) of season. Up 14% from week 1-7

Play action usage rate has fallen off a cliff.

You heard all night on the broadcast about the Bears not needing to blitz with the addition of Jeffery Simmons. 4-man rush Weeks 1-7 60% | Week 8 vs. SEA 80%. 5 man W1-7 24% | W8 vs. SEA 15%. 6 man W1-7 16% | W8 3%

Bears Man/Zone numbers remained the same. Their shells changed, 2-High: W1-7 45% | W8 67%, 1-High: 54% | W8 33%, Safety Rotation: W1-7 31% | W8 18%

JSN (63%) had his 3rd game of 60%+ of the passing yards. 5th with 50%+, 7th with 40%+

2nd highest runs from Shotgun (37%), 3rd highest Gun YPC (5)

Highest YD/Before Contact since W1, still only 1.6 yards.

39% pressure allowed. 7th game with 39%+ pressure allowed. Last week (33%) was the only game < 40%.

3 sacks allowed. 4th game with 3+ sacks allowed. Haven't had a back to back game with 0 sacks. 0

Allowed 4/5 RZ trips to score a TD. Bears scored TD on 52% of RZ trips W1-7.

Seahawks DEF reduced Bears Yards/drive by 6 (36 -> 30), but increase their own by 9 (21 -> 30)

Tied for highest success allowed (49%), 49ers the other game. Only games > 42%.

7/15 3rd downs were 3 or less. With 1 4th & 3 or less.

Slowest Time to Pressure this year with 2.75 . Weeks 1-7 was 2.52 (13th)

Highest passing success allowed (53%). Only 2nd game > 46%.

2nd highest short (<10) passing success (55%). 3rd game > 44%.

Highest intermediate (11-20) passing success allowed (63%) & YD/ATT (11.3), & 2nd most attempts (8)

Most deep (21+) attempted (6), completing only 2 of them. Only 2nd game with 4 or more attempts.

2nd highest rush success allowed (46%), 49ers was highest (54%). 2nd game > 45%

Most rushing attempts faced (35) but only allowed 3.6 YPC
