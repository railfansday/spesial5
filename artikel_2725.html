Matthew Stafford’s wife Kelly makes nostalgic Lions admission before Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Los Angeles Rams are facing the Green Bay Packers on Netflix Wednesday night, in what is the first-ever Thanksgiving Eve NFL game.

There have been countless Thanksgiving Day games. In fact, Rams star quarterback Matthew Stafford played in 12 such games during his Detroit Lions tenure, as Detroit always plays a home game on the holiday.

Matthew Stafford's wife, Kelly, speaking on an Instagram post. Instagram/@kbstafford89

Matthew's wife, Kelly, expressed her nostalgia for those Lions Thanksgiving Day games on her Instagram story Tuesday.

"I love Thanksgiving football. Got to watch him do it for 12 years and then head home and eat a monster plate of sides while watching the later game. I miss it," Stafford wrote in the post's caption after reposting the clip of her husband's previous comments on the subject.

Earlier in the week, Stafford spoke about his team's upcoming game and the Thanksgiving game tradition on "The Let's Go Podcast" with Jim Gray.

Los Angeles Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford warms up before an NFL football game against the Los Angeles Chargers in Inglewood, Calif., Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026. AP Photo/Caroline Brehman

"It's a lot of fun. This is a little bit different, in the fact that we don't have this game every single year. And when I was in Detroit, man, for 12 years — the city, the team, the organization — we took so much pride in being one of those games that was on Thanksgiving every single year," Matthew Stafford said.

"Like you said, with family gathered around the table, giving thanks to all the things that they love about each other, life; one of those things being football," he continued.

"It was a really special game to be a part of for 12 years. And then getting a little bit of a taste of it Wednesday night on Netflix, it will be a lot of fun. I think a ton of people will tune in."

While Kelly may miss those games, she should get a nice Thanksgiving at home with family this year. If the Rams are able to pull out a victory against the Packers on Wednesday night, it will likely make that sweet potato that much sweeter, too.
