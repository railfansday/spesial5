No. 3 UConn men’s basketball falls at No. 12 Arizona, 85-79

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

UConn men's basketball suffered its first loss of the season Wednesday night at the hands of No. 12 Arizona. The Wildcats came out swinging from the opening tip and put the Huskies on its heels, and while UConn managed to find its bearings in the second half, it was too little, too late as Arizona held on for an 85-79 victory.

It's hard to imagine a worse possible start for UConn (4-1) in the first half. Arizona opened up scoring with a kill shot, going up 10-0 before a Jayden Ross three put the Huskies on the board with 16:33 to play. Big men Na'jai Hines and Oskar Giltay faced arguably their biggest test of the season with Arizona center Motiejus Krivas, but both UConn bigs picked up a foul before the under-16 timeout, limiting their ability to play physically with Krivas.

On top of all of that, Arizona was red hot from three. The Wildcats were 7-14 from deep in the first half after entering Wednesday's matchup shooting 30.1 percent from beyond the arc as a team. While UConn struggled defensively with Hines and eventually Ross in foul trouble, Arizona was taking and making good shots. And when Zona wasn't making shots, Ivan Karchenkov and Krivas were reeling in rebounds, helping the Wildcats out-rebound UConn 24-11 in the first half.

Karchenkov was magnificent all night against the Huskies, thanks in part to an incredible first half. The sophomore had 14 points, five assists and three rebounds in the first 20 minutes, setting the tone on both ends of the floor. When UConn did finally manage to get close late in the first after five-straight points from Braylon Mullins to get within 13, it was Karchenkov who hit a three with two minutes left in the half to swing the momentum back to Zona. The Wildcats would lead by as much as 19 in the first half and took an 18-point lead into the break — the Huskies' largest halftime deficit since 2019.

Arizona stretched the lead to 20 out of halftime, but UConn finally delivered a counterpunch of its own with the help of Mullins. The sophomore shooter scored nine of the Huskies' first 11 points to start the half, and managed to get the lead down to 13 with 16:47 left after another Mullins three.

Colben Landrew emerged after a quiet first half, scoring all 12 of his points in the second half. He partnered with Mullins and Hines to spur a 9-0 second run that finally brought the Zona lead to single digits. With the Wildcats up just eight with 10:42 to play, Karchenkov hit a pair of free throws to stop the bleeding once again.

UConn would cut the lead to eight four more times, but the Wildcats always had an answer. The Huskies made a concerted effort to fight through screens and close out on the perimeter to prevent the Wildcats from getting as many open looks from three. It worked — Arizona made just three threes in the second half, and didn't make its first until there was 8:06 left to play, but the Wildcats adjusted and leaned on Krivas, who had 10 second-half points.

The Huskies trailed by as little as six with 48 seconds to play, but Arizona was able to hold on and keep UConn at arm's length. The six-point win is a little deceptive at face-value — the Wildcats played well for 40 minutes in a relatively comfortable win, while the Huskies came out flat on the road and fought back like crazy to make it interesting in the second half. After shooting 57 percent from the field in the first half, Arizona shot 45.5 percent from the field in the second half and finished the night averaging 1.23 points per possession.

The teams tied in the rebounding battle at 37 each, but it really was a tale of two halves that was indicative of the momentum of the game. Arizona out-rebounded UConn by 13 in the first, while UConn out-rebounded Zona by 13 in the second. The Huskies had 20 offensive rebounds on the night, 15 of which were in the second half.

Mullins led all scorers with a career-high 29 points and shot 6-15 from three. He scored 19 of those points in the second half to fuel UConn's comeback chances and now has 20 or more points in four of five games this season. Hines (10 points) and Silas Demary Jr. (15 points) joined Landrew in double figures for UConn. Landrew also led the Huskies with 10 boards for his first collegiate double-double.

Karchenkov led the way for Zona with 20 points, but freshman Caleb Holt wasn't far behind with 19. Three other Wildcats hit double figures in the win, and Krivas finished with 18 points, 12 rebounds and four blocks after dominating the paint for all 37 minutes he was on the floor.

Up next, the Huskies are off for a full week before heading to Las Vegas to take on No. 5 Duke. With the Blue Devils and UConn both suffering losses this week, it's unlikely that it stays a top-five matchup, but it still figures to be one of the most highly anticipated games of the season as a rematch of last year's Elite Eight matchup. The game will be broadcast on Amazon Prime, with tip-off set for 7:00 p.m. ET.
