Xbox Game Pass, a CNET Editors’ Choice award pick, offers a wide selection of games you can play on your Xbox Series X, Xbox Series S, Xbox One and PC or mobile device for as little as $10 a month. With a higher-tier Game Pass Ultimate subscription ($23 a month), you can access hundreds of games, including new ones the day they’re released, each month.

Here are all the games Microsoft is adding to Xbox Game Pass between now and early November.

In this award-winning horror game, you play as one of five crew members aboard the stranded spaceship, the Tulpar. Food and medical supplies aboard the ship are running low, and the crew are beginning to crack under the stress. What follows is an unsettling experience that grips you in its maw and doesn’t let you go, even after the credits roll.

This remake of the award-winning Final Fantasy 7 retells part of the original game’s story, but this time it has updated visuals, faster gameplay and additional story elements. Whether you’re new to this classic roleplaying game or have played the original, there’s something new here for you.

You play as mercenary-turned-eco-terrorist Cloud Strife. Your goal is to stop the megacorporation Shinra from draining the planet of its energy source, and work toward that goal with a team of folks from all over the world. Like in other Final Fantasy games, each party member has their own skills to fit your play style, giving you different avenues to tackle a range of enemies and problems.

This classic tower-defense game is back and in high definition! So you can see the cartoony plants and zombies in all their goofy glory. This version also includes new levels and challenges for both old and new players.

Those are some of the games coming to Game Pass between now and early November. Microsoft is adding these other games to the service in that time frame, too.

While Microsoft is adding those games to Xbox Game Pass, it’s also removing 11 titles soon. So you’d better hurry and finish your campaigns before you have to buy these games separately.
