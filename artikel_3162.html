Tsakos Energy (TEN -1.81%) reported financial results for the third quarter and first nine months of 2026, driven by elevated tanker freight rates resulting from global geopolitical tensions and supply-demand imbalances. Management reported that the company maintained high fleet utilization despite an increased number of drydockings and repositioning activities. The firm is actively pursuing a fleet modernization strategy, selling older vessels and acquiring energy-efficient newbuildings, including dual-fuel and shuttle tankers. Additionally, the company is exploring a dual listing on the Singapore Exchange (SGX) to expand its investor audience in East Asia. The board has prioritized shareholder returns through a doubled semi-annual dividend and a $100 million share repurchase program targeting both preferred and common stock.
