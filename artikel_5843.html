GOP senator 'not likely' to mount presidential bid, but continues leaving door open to possibility

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Capitol Hill security hearings focus on massive national tech disruptions. Sen. John Kennedy, R-La., joins 'The Story' to analyze testimony from FBI, DNI and DHS leaders. Security officials testify they are 98% confident that the recent widespread outages are not the work of foreign adversaries, despite intense congressional scrutiny.

Sen. John Kennedy, R-La., is continuing to leave the door open to the possibility of mounting a 2028 presidential bid, though he said that such a move is "not likely" and that he plans to seek re-election.

"My plan right now is to run for re-election," Kennedy told Kristen Welker during an interview that aired on NBC's "Meet the Press" on Sunday.

"If I get fed up with things and catch a wild hair and have a, I don't know, a spontaneous moment of ... questionable genius, if I get really fed up I could run. But it's not likely," he said, noting that he plans to pursue re-election.

Kennedy has served in the Senate since early 2017. His current term ends in early 2029, so if he wants to seek a third Senate term, he will need to run in 2028.

President Donald Trump, who is currently serving his second term, is constitutionally barred from being elected president again.

During a "Meet the Press" interview that aired last month, Kennedy also told Welker that he planned to seek re-election.

"But as I've said before," he told Welker last month regarding the idea of a presidential run, "if I don't think I can be effective in the Senate anymore I would consider it."
