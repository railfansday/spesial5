England 2-1 Croatia: Highlights, stats, man of the match as Harry Kane hits winner on his special night to secure quarter-final spot

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

By Matt Law | 12 Nov 2026 21:38, Last updated: 12 Nov 2026 21:43

Harry Kane sent England into the quarter-finals of the 2026-27 UEFA Nations League on Thursday night, with the Three Lions captain scoring in 2-1 win over Croatia at Wembley.

The Bayern Munich striker officially broke the England men's appearance record in the League A Group 3 fixture, pulling clear of the legendary Peter Shilton courtesy of his 126th cap.

Petar Sucic had cancelled out an opener from Morgan Rogers to leave the two teams level just before the hour, but a penalty from Kane restored England's advantage soon after.

England are second in Group 3, three points behind Spain ahead of Sunday's fascinating battle between the two sides, and first position is still on the line.
