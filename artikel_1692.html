Renee Slegers: Arsenal confirm manager exit as statement issued

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Renee Slegers has left Arsenal by mutual consent after the club’s poor start to the Women’s Super League season.

The Gunners sit sixth in the table and are already 12 points off the pace set by Chelsea, having drawn all six of their league matches on home soil.

A goalless draw with West Ham last weekend has proved to be Slegers’ final match in charge.

Slegers took charge as interim head coach in October 2024 and was then given the permanent job three months later.

That season ended in glory for Arsenal, who won their second Champions League title in May 2025. Earlier this year, the club won the inaugural FIFA Women’s Champions Cup.

However, results have dipped significantly this season and Arsenal have used the international break to make the change in head coach, with Slegers’ assistant Aaron D’Antino also leaving the club.

“It was an extremely difficult decision to part ways with Renee, who has led the team with dedication and commitment throughout her time here,” said director of women’s football Clare Wheatley.

“In leading us to glory in the Champions League and the inaugural FIFA Champions Cup, Renee has made a significant and lasting contribution to the history of Arsenal Football Club, with close support from Aaron.
