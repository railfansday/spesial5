Opinion: Julian Ward could now make dream Michael Edwards transfer happen for Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Michael Edwards always wanted to break into the Brazilian transfer market, and now Julian Ward can.

The Americas remain a market Liverpool have yet to fully conquer. Since Brexit, changes to the work-permit system have made it considerably easier for South American players to move to the Premier League. The points-based system favours footballers competing in leading CONMEBOL competitions, opening up an opportunity that several clubs have been quick to exploit.

Brighton are arguably the best example. They identified the potential of Moises Caicedo and Alexis Mac Allister early, bringing both to England before eventually selling them for substantial profits.

Their recruitment strategy has shown just how lucrative the South American market can be when approached correctly. And just how many exciting young talents can be found on the continent.

Liverpool's attempts to break into the South American marketNottingham Forest have also started making significant moves in that part of the world, while Chelsea continue to invest heavily in South American talent, particularly at youth level. Manchester City another club who have bought exciting gems and sold them on for profit from the continent. Liverpool, by comparison, have been relatively slow to catch on. But that could be about to change. The Reds recognise there is plenty of untapped potential across the Americas and are looking to expand their scouting network over the coming years. They have tried in the past. Michael Edwards was a big advocate of exploring the market. A move for Rodrygo Goes fell through a couple of times with the Brazilian having set his heart on joining Real Madrid. The likes of Joao Gomes, Andre and Rayan were also targeted in the past. It's an area where Liverpool could find their next big talent before the rest of the Premier League catches up.

Liverpool could finally break into the Brazilian marketBut Liverpool's woes might be over. That is because Sport Witness are reporting that Julian Ward and the Reds have initiated talks and enquires with Palmeiras over the signing of 19-year-old Brazil U20 international, Erick Bele. It is believed Palmeiras would be open to selling their young star for around €30m (£25m). Bele is a talented player who can also play as a forward. He hasn't played too many games at senior level for his club side though. In total he has featured just 260 minutes in Palmeiras' first-team. Considering he is turning 20-years-old in January, a figure of £25m does seem a little steep for a player with such little experience. But at the same time, Liverpool's recruitment team are known for being able to spot a gem, so maybe they have confidence Bele can be special.
