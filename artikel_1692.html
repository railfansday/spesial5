Chelsea: Xabi Alonso breaks silence on Wesley Fofana interview controversy - ‘It won’t be repeated’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Chelsea defender Wesley Fofana has apologised for an interview not authorised by the club that caused controversy this week.

The French centre-back was strongly criticised by fans following comments made about missing a crucial penalty during Chelsea’s Carabao Cup last-16 defeat by Liverpool last month.

Fofana spoke for two-and-a-half hours with YouTuber Zack Nani, who has since insisted on social media that “several quotes were taken out of context or plainly made up in England”.

Asked about the situation at a press conference held before Friday night’s trip to Nottingham Forest in the Premier League, Chelsea head coach Xabi Alonso said: “We have addressed that with him [Fofana]. He has apologised.

“It was not right, he has said it is not going to be repeated, these types of things.”

Fofana missed the decisive penalty that saw Chelsea’s Carabao Cup run end in the fourth round with a 6-5 shootout loss at Anfield following a 1-1 draw, blazing his spot-kick over the crossbar after Emmanuel Emegha had seen an effort saved by Giorgi Mamardashvili and Valentin Barco hit the woodwork.

He had also given away a penalty in normal time for handball that was converted by Cody Gakpo just three minutes after Morgan Rogers had given Chelsea the lead from 12 yards, continuing what has been a difficult season for him so far.

“The Liverpool match was tough to take, but I moved on quickly because that’s just part of football,” Fofana was quoted as saying during the interview with Nani.

“I took responsibility for the penalty - I missed it - and, well, I knew I wasn’t a good penalty taker to begin with, you know [laughs]?”

He continued: “So, I just thought, ‘Okay, I’ll own it.’ But I ended up conceding a penalty during the match - it was a handball - but that’s just part of the game. I could have avoided it; it’s the kind of thing we talk about - you have to be consistent, and you have to keep improving in those areas.

“But after the penalty, I stepped up, because I’m one of the most experienced players. Out on the field, there were a lot of young guys. And, well, you had to get out there, you know?

“You’ve got to go for it. And the way I look at it, if I fail, well, I’ve been through things. I’ve lived through a lot. If I fail, it’s not that it’s not a big deal - sure, it sucks, obviously - but as for me, if I’m the one being attacked, it’s no big deal.

“It’s not the first time I’ve been attacked. It’s fine; it doesn’t matter. I couldn’t care less. I know there’s another match in three days.

“In three days’ time, you win, and people forget. They’ve already forgotten about your penalty. That’s just how football works. I stepped up, took it, and missed.

“I sent a real beauty of a shot way over the bar. Did you see my shot? I really blasted it high [laughs]. I remember a fan, way up high, he actually managed to catch the ball. But yeah, I missed, you know.

“Now, I laugh about it because it’s just part of football - that’s how it goes. And, well, there you have it.

“Besides, as I said, we kept the momentum going, we got the win [against Manchester United three days later], and it sort of faded into the background. We actually saw a great response in the second half.”

Fofana also revealed in the interview that he had considered retirement under previous boss Enzo Maresca, having suffered with repeated injury issues since joining Chelsea from Leicester for £70million in the summer of 2022.
