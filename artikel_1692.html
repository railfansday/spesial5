‘We almost had a heart attack!’ – Frank hopes first win will bring calm for Palace

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Thomas Frank knows victories are the only thing that will settle Crystal Palace down, after a nerve-shredding finale resulted in his first win against Real Sociedad on Thursday.

The Premier League's bottom club recovered from a goal down to claim a 2-1 win at Selhurst Park in the Conference League, having been boosted by Jon Martin's 71st-minute red card.

A Jean-Philippe Mateta penalty got Palace level in the aftermath of Martin's dismissal, before Dwight McNeil prodded home to complete the turnaround.

But the Eagles were almost pegged back in a dramatic finale, as Yeremy Pino passed up the chance to score a second penalty for the hosts, being thwarted by Unai Marrero, before Dean Henderson made a vital save to deny Orri Iskarsson at the other end.

Having avoided losing his first two games in charge, Frank was able to breathe a sigh of relief at the full-time whistle, expressing his hope that things will get more straightforward in the coming weeks.

"It's okay to bring energy and confidence, but what really moves the needle is when you get over the line and you get some wins," Frank told TNT Sports.

"So, in the last five minutes, we were all almost about to get a heart attack because it was end to end!

"And that's part of the confidence; we should control that game 11 versus 10. But that's football – its emotions, its everything."

— Crystal Palace F.C. (@CPFC) November 26, 2026

Asked if he expected the result to provide a major confidence boost after Palace's poor start to the campaign, the former Brentford and Tottenham boss said: "I surely hope so.

"You can't underestimate what a win brings to a group, the feeling.

"And again, there were more positives than negatives from this performance, but still a lot we need to work on."

Palace go up against Hull City in their next Premier League game on Sunday, when a victory could lift them out of the relegation zone, depending on the results of Manchester United, Sunderland and Nottingham Forest.
