Trump says Sen Cornyn is doing a 'tremendous disservice' by blocking Texas judicial pick

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Sen. Rick Scott, R-Fla., joins ‘Saturday in America’ to discuss President Donald Trump’s clash with Sen. John Cornyn over a blocked judicial nominee, Sen. Thom Tillis’ opposition to a lobbying bill and the Senate’s recess schedule.

President Donald Trump told Fox News' "Saturday in America" that outgoing Texas Sen. John Cornyn is doing a "tremendous disservice" by blocking his pick to fill a judicial seat in Dallas.

"We're going to have a radical left lunatic get in there, and we don't want that," the president said.

Trump's comments come after Cornyn told The Associated Press he has no intention of returning the "blue slip" for T. Elliot Gaiser, a Department of Justice senior lawyer who was nominated by the president to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Texas.

TRUMP TAKES AIM AT SENATE 'BLUE SLIP' TRADITION AS GOP RESISTS CHANGE

The blue slip tradition allows for home state senators to weigh in on judicial nominees, giving them a say on who does and doesn’t move forward.

Returning a blue slip is the equivalent of giving a thumbs up to the nominees moving forward, while keeping the slip effectively blocks the process.

"Everybody tells me he's outstanding, and he's being held up by John Cornyn because John Cornyn's feelings were hurt," said Trump, who endorsed Cornyn's challenger, Ken Paxton, in the Texas Republican Senate primary.

Sen. John Cornyn, R-Texas, speaks with reporters after votes in the Senate at the U.S. Capitol in Washington, D.C., Nov. 9, 2026. (Anadolu via Getty Images)

"And, by the way, John Cornyn would have lost. It was just a point in time. John Cornyn would have lost by a lot. You know, one of those things. But he was beaten in the primary. And he's. He feels badly about it," the president continued.

"He's a nice guy, John Cornyn, but he's doing a tremendous disservice because Gaiser is fantastic."

Cornyn wrote on X later Saturday, "If President Trump wanted to fill a judicial vacancy in Dallas, why did he wait until after the midterms to nominate someone?

"The seat has been vacant since 2025. Then, without any consultation, he nominates a non-Texan with little connection to the Lone Star State, with no trial court experience and who has not been appropriately vetted for a lifetime job. The Senate has a constitutional responsibility to provide 'advice and consent,' which is impossible without consultation," Cornyn added.

"Still, I would like to work with the President to fill the seat while we still have a Senate majority. I have forwarded an outstanding candidate who was highly rated by Senator Cruz's and my bipartisan Federal Judicial Evaluation Committee, and who has now been interviewed by White House Counsel.

"All that is needed is an FBI background check (which) they stand ready to do on a timely basis," Cornyn also said. "Back to you, Mr. President. Time is of the essence."

CORNYN MEETS WITH SEN-ELECT TALARICO, DESCRIBES HIM AS 'BASICALLY ... A GOOD PERSON'

Sen. Ted Cruz, R-Texas, called Cornyn's move "really frustrating," describing to Fox News' "Saturday in America" a tense encounter he had with Cornyn this week in Washington.

"I came to him on the Senate floor, and I asked him to please let this judge go. And I tried to make the case on the merits that he'd make a fantastic judge. And I asked him, 'Will you just meet with him?' Because Cornyn refused even to meet with him," Cruz said.

"He wouldn't talk to him. He just said, 'No, no, no.' And I was asking him, I said, 'Look, you're going to give this seat to the Democrats. Why would you want to do that?' And, unfortunately, he got very angry. He began screaming. He began cursing at me," Cruz added.

Cruz said, after the Republican losses in the midterm elections, "All the Republicans agreed, 'Let's do everything humanly possible to confirm every judge we can before the end of the year, because we know the Democrats are going to come in January,' and I expect they're going to block all judicial confirmations."

TRUMP'S SUPREME COURT MAJORITY FACES LOOMING THREAT AFTER DEM SENATE TAKEOVER: 'STUCK'

Assistant Attorney General T. Elliot Gaiser is interviewed in his office at the Department of Justice on Sept. 21, 2026, in Washington, D.C. Gaiser previously clerked for Supreme Court Justice Samuel A. Alito Jr. (The Washington Post via Getty Images)

"We had two vacancies in Texas, one in Austin that we're moving forward with. And we'll have that judge confirmed by the end of the year. We had a second vacancy in Dallas, and the problem was that we did not have time to have a brand-new nominee go through the paperwork, the FBI background check," Cruz told Fox News.

"That takes weeks and weeks and weeks, and we simply didn't have time between now and the end of the year to get that done. And, so, the White House proposed a different idea, which is to go with a senior lawyer at the Department of Justice, a man named Elliot Gaiser, an incredibly qualified judicial nominee.

"This is someone who was a law clerk at the U.S. Supreme Court for Justice Sam Alito. He was a state solicitor general arguing cases before the United States Supreme Court," Cruz said.

"And he's right now the head of the Office of Legal Counsel, which is the senior constitutional advisor to the president and the senior constitutional advisor to the attorney general."

Cruz also told Fox News he interviewed Gaiser and called him "incredibly principled, smart, capable, ready."

Cruz added that Gaiser "was a fantastic nomination. I was enthusiastic. Unfortunately, my colleague John Cornyn decided to block the nomination, and any one senator can block a district court nomination from your state.

"And, so, Cornyn did what's called — he refused to return the blue slip, which basically means he vetoed it — and the result of that is the seat is going to remain vacant. The people of Dallas are not going to have a judge in that seat. And he basically gave the seat to the Democrats," Cruz said.

Fox News Digital's Alex Miller contributed to this report.
