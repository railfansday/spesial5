Nets projected to take UConn C Na'jai Hines in 2027 NBA Draft

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Brooklyn Nets (3-12) are still trying to turn their season around after getting off to a less-than-ideal start for a team that was hoping to play better than what the NBA space expected from them. Brooklyn has been trying to improve the chemistry of the team while incorporating multiple new players in the rotation, but there could an interesting player for them to bring in for next season.

"On a per-minute basis, no player in the country combined better shot-blocking and offensive rebounding rates than Na'jai Hines last season," Jonathan Wasserman wrote for Bleacher Report. Wasserman has the Nets selecting UConn center Na'jai Hines with the No. 18 overall pick in the 2027 NBA Draft that the team would be receiving from the New York Knicks due to the Mikal Bridges trade.

"Despite lacking any real creation or scoring skills beyond finishing and post-ups, his paint presence and defense are strong enough for scouts to look past the limited offensive versatility," Wasserman continued on Hines. "He appeared to be in good shape and lighter on his feet during Connecticut's preseason, which could lead to more transition and easy baskets."

Hines, who is in the midst of his sophomore year after spending his freshman season at Seton Hall, is averaging 11.8 points, 6.8 rebounds, and 1.6 blocks per game while shooting 69.0% from the field and 82.6% from the free-throw line. Hines is a big-body center who does all of the damage inside the paint thanks to his size, strength, and ability to protect the rim against dribble penetration.

As of right now, the Nets have Day'Ron Sharpe and Mo Wagner as their centers, but both players have options for next season so if one of them are not on the team next year, Hines could fill that void. There is still plenty of team for general manager Sean Marks and the front office to evaluate the players that would like to add to the team for next season, but Hines could be a guy that fills a potential need for them.
