Bills benefited from extra seconds during Chiefs' final drive

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

We saw it on Monday night, when the clock operator didn’t press the button to save a second or two for the Bengals to try to pull out a win over the Commanders.

It apparently happened again on Thursday night, in more subtle fashion.

By our count, the Buffalo clock operator managed to preserve four seconds during what was the Chiefs’ final drive of the game.

I’ve watched it, several times. The Chiefs had first and 10 on their own 11. Quarterback Patrick Mahomes dropped the shotgun snap. By the time he recovered the ball, was touched, the whistle blew, and the Bills called a timeout (assuming they did it instantly), four seconds had elapsed. The clock, however, ultimately moved by only one second, from 26 to 25 — not 22.

Then, after running back Emmett Johnson gained five yards on second and 15, 19 seconds remained when the whistle blew. The clock in the NBC score bug at one point shifted from 19 seconds to 20, presumably to match the official time on the stadium scoreboard.

After the third-down incompletion, the clock showed 15 seconds. It should have been 11 seconds.

Following the punt, which was partially blocked, and the return by Greg Dortch, there were four seconds to play. Four seconds. The same four seconds that apparently were preserved through creative clock operation.

Of course, if the clock had shown 11 seconds and not 15 before the punt, the Bills may have not tried to block it. They may have opted to attempt a fair catch followed by a free kick.

Regardless, there was more time on the clock than there should have been. And those extra seconds ensured that the Bills would have just enough time for Tyler Bass to make a walk-off 57-yard field goal.

With so many games decided by such tight margins, there needs to be a better effort to ensure complete accuracy with the game timing, especially as the final seconds of a game tick, tick, tick away.

So when it was time to divvy up the postgame turkey legs, here’s hoping the Bills saved a drumstick for the timekeeper.
