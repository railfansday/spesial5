Tom Brady backs Bill Belichick, urges NFL Hall of Fame voters to reconsider

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Tom Brady is sticking by Bill Belichick as he urges the Pro Football Hall of Fame to reconsider their position on his future induction.

On Sunday, Nov. 22, Brady spoke about his former coach on Fox's pregame show after Patriots owner Robert Kraft and Belichick were announced as this year's finalists.

"You know what? Congrats to everybody," Brady said. "What an honor for all those guys, and I'm glad all the voters seem like they need to come to their senses on this one. These guys were just architects of what a two-decade dynasty was all about."

He added, "[Kraft], what he's done for this league has just been exceptional. Bill [Belichick], c'mon, there's nobody that I'd rather play for than him. I'm expecting August in Canton to be a big Patriot party."

It's not surprising that Brady has decided to back up Belichick once again after their long-standing history in the NFL. Together, Belichick and Brady won six Super Bowls together during their time with the New England Patriots. The former Patriots quarterback campaigned to have him inducted during his first ballot for the Hall of Fame. Last year, both Kraft and Belichick were nominated but failed to be inducted due to lack of votes.

"I don't believe that any coach in the history of the game has done more to earn his place in the Hall of Fame than Bill Belichick," Brady said in his statement at the time. "To say that he was my greatest coaching influence would be an understatement. His tremendous leadership, vision, and daily discipline allowed our team to excel in nearly every area for almost two decades. We were consistently competitive at the highest level every week as he challenged us to grow from the day that season started to the day it ended. That's the role of the head coach and that's Bill Belichick."

The Hall of Fame has a mandatory five-year waiting period from the a player's retirement. Since Brady retired for the second and final time in 2023 — he previously retired in February 2022 and later reversed his decision to return to play the 2022 season — is eligible for the Pro Football Hall of Fame 2028 class. He has since already been inducted into the Patriots Hall of Fame in June 2024 after the team waived its four-year waiting period.
