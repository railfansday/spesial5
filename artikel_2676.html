Felix Okpara ejected from G League game after punching Rob Dillingham

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Washington Wizards two-way forward Felix Okpara was ejected after throwing a punch at Westchester Knicks guard Rob Dillingham during the Capital City Go-Go's 125-104 loss on Friday.

With eight minutes remaining in the fourth quarter, Okpara wound up and threw a punch that struck Dillingham in the head and neck area. Okpara quickly backed away toward his team's bench as Dillingham pursued him.

Okpara and Dillingham were ejected from the game.

The Wizards are aware of the incident but declined to comment at this time.

Washington selected Okpara with the 46th pick in the 2026 NBA Draft. He's appeared in just three NBA games this season, mainly at the end of blowout wins or losses, while spending most of his time in the G League.
