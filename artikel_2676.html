Here’s what Broncos QB Bo Nix said about Aaron Rodgers before Steelers benched him

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The 11th-hour announcement from the Pittsburgh Steelers that Aaron Rodgers would be a healthy scratch for the first time in his 22-year NFL career rocked the NFL world on Tuesday.

However, before the news broke in the evening, Denver Broncos players and coaches lauded the future Hall of Famer, who has said that 2026 will be his final NFL season. Denver's set to face Pittsburgh on Black Friday.

Quarterback Bo Nix credited Rodgers as someone he's always looked up to. Nix only faced Rodgers once, in 2024, when Rodgers played with the New York Jets. Nix and Rodgers scored a combined 19 points in a 10-9 Broncos victory that day.

"With Aaron Rodgers, he's just one of those guys who I've watched and looked up to for so long," Nix said. "He's going to be a Hall of Fame quarterback. He's one of the best quarterbacks to ever play. In my opinion, the best thrower of the football to ever play. It's just special to be able to share the field with a guy like that. He's been doing it for a lot longer than I have. He's set the standard for what it's like to be a quarterback in this league."

Coach Sean Payton similarly praised Rodgers' tremendous skillset as a passer on the field.

"He's a first-ballot Hall of Famer," Payton said. "He's a first-ballot thrower. Just the arm angles, the rhythm, so many things that he's exceptional at. It was Green Bay for a number of years. [The years] kind of run together, like I said, but the ball comes out quick. He's got a great throwing motion. There are just so many things that he's accomplished in his career. Nothing but respect for his skill set and also his staying power. It's not easy to do."

Cornerback Pat Surtain has been on the other end, also facing Rodgers in 2024. He spoke about the challenge it presented, going best-on-best.

"He's a future Hall of Famer," Surtain said. "Just growing up watching him play the quarterback position, one of the most talented quarterbacks to ever play the position. So just to face up against him is going to be pretty awesome in another conference matchup. I just think [he's a] all-world talent for 20-plus years. It doesn't get any better than that."

Tuesday's benching could very well be an ignominious end to Rodger's storied career. He's certainly left an impression on those he faced throughout his tenure.

The Broncos travel to Pittsburgh to face the Steelers on Friday at 3:00 p.m. ET (1:00 p.m. MT) on Prime.
