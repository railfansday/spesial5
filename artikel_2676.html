Javonte Williams makes a pair of visits to the end zone in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Dallas Cowboys RB Javonte Williams carried 18 times for 67 yards and a touchdown and caught all three targets for 24 yards and another score in Week 11 against the Tennessee Titans.

Why It Matters: Dallas hasn't been able to run the ball as effectively as last season, but Williams remains a workhorse and touchdown machine. He's now up to 12 total TDs (10 rushing) – one shy of last season's total - despite averaging only four yards per carry this season after 5.1 yards last season. Williams has carried the ball 103 times over the last five weeks, so fantasy managers should be wary of the possibility he sees a few less reps in Week 12 against Philadelphia on Thursday. That said, he's the only show in town in the backfield, so continue to treat him as a solid RB1 option in fantasy.
