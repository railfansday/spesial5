Who Stephen A. Smith picked to win the Super Bowl before 26/27 season began

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

With just over half of the NFL season now past, the potential Super Bowl contenders are starting to become clear.

The San Francisco 49ers and the Buffalo Bills both went into Week 10 having lost just one game from eight.

The likes of the Chicago Bears, the Denver Broncos and the Jacksonville Jaguars have also had solid campaigns so far. But none of them were Stephen A. Smith's pick to win the 2027 Super Bowl before the season began.

Speaking to ESPN on the eve of the 2026/27 season getting underway, Smith instead picked the Los Angeles Rams to face the Broncos in the Super Bowl and come out as winners.

Back in September, he said: "The Los Angeles Rams meet the Denver Broncos for the Super Bowl at SoFi Stadium in La-La and right there, the Super Bowl champions: Sean McVay, Aaron Donald, Myles Garrett, the monster, the crew. Matthew Stafford going to show up, too. He's going to outdo Bo Nix.

"The Los Angeles Rams will win the Super Bowl for a second time, at SoFi Stadium."

However, the Rams have since endured a pretty tough 2026/27 season. They went into Week 10, facing the Arizona Cardinals on November 15, with a record of 5-4.

They also began the latest round of action third in the NFC West behind the 49ers and the Seattle Seahawks.
