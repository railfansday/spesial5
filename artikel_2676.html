Where the Dallas Mavericks stand in the NBA cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

After Tuesday night's win over the Denver Nuggets, the Dallas Mavericks are in great position to play for the third annual NBA cup.

The Mavericks are 3-0 in group play, with a point differential of 36, by far the highest in their group.

They also hold the head-to-head tiebreaker over the Denver Nuggets and Houston Rockets. The only team that could jump the Mavericks is the Phoenix Suns, who play the Rockets tonight, and the Mavericks on Thursday.

With a Rockets win, the Suns would be eliminated from contention, and the Mavericks would be in regardless of Friday's result.

If they do advance to the tournament, it would mean a matchup against another western conference contender. This would give the Mavericks another opportunity to show their mettle, and establish themselves as a real threat within the West.

"It means a lot," said Caleb Martin in his post-game interview with Jeff Wade. "Just to have an oppurtunity to play in these types of enviroments… to test where we're at."

While the NBA cup isn't the same as real playoff basketball, it's much closer than the regular season.

It would be especially helpful for a young Mavericks team that has limited experience in the postseason; Cooper Flagg being the player with the most to gain.

And for those questioning the value of cup games, look no further than last year's Cup final, which featured the eventual NBA finals matchup.

It also gives the Mavericks a chance at redemption for their recent 3-game losing streak against other contending teams, and gives them a chance to add more proverbial skins on the wall.

Their seeding within the tournament will come down to Friday's game against the Suns, but they will likely have a home game in the quarterfinal due to their record and point differential.
