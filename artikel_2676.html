Pacers rumors: Indiana on trade watch as Tyrese Haliburton injury, other woes ruin start to season

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Heading into the season, optimism was high for the Indiana Pacers. With Tyrese Haliburton back in action, along with Ivica Zubac and Obi Toppin, and the solid addition of Kelly Oubre Jr., the Pacers were poised for a resurgent campaign.

But now, skepticism has surrounded Indiana, with Haliburton sidelined anew by a hamstring injury and Zubac, Toppin, and Oubre all struggling with consistency.

The offense has been anemic, averaging just 112.1 points per outing, which is in the bottom 10 of the NBA. The Pacers are the fifth-worst rebounding team with 41.6 caroms per game.

Amid Haliburton's extended absence and the on-court issues, The Stein Line's Marc Stein said Indiana,  which has a 5-9 record, could retool the roster.

"Indiana, meanwhile, is increasingly described as a team weighing its trade options after it made an unsuccessful run at trading for (Nique) Clifford," wrote Stein.

"The Pacers are known to be seeking an upgrade on wing, but one of the key variables for them, of course, is determining how soon Tyrese Haliburton can come back from the hamstring injury he sustained after playing in just four games and how effective the 26-year-old is projected to be after missing all of last season recovering from an Achilles tear sustained in Game 7 of the 2025 NBA Finals."

Clifford went to the Oklahoma City Thunder, who sent two second-round picks to the Sacramento Kings in exchange.

The Pacers control all of their first-round picks from 2027 through 2033. They could dangle them to attract a key acquisition.

Trey Murphy III of the New Orleans Pelicans and Michael Porter Jr. of the Brooklyn Nets could be prime targets for Indiana, which could also ship Toppin, TJ McConnell, Aaron Nesmith, Jarace Walker, and Ben Sheppard.
