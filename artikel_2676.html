CB Mike Jackson says he's ready to return to Panthers lineup

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Panthers are set to get a key piece of their secondary back for Sunday’s game against the Ravens.

Mike Jackson has been out since having core muscle surgery after Week 3, but has been back at practice for the last couple of weeks. On Wednesday, Jackson proclaimed himself ready for a return to the lineup as the Panthers try to improve to 5-5 on the season.

“I’m game ready, so I’ll be out there on Sunday,” Jackson said, via Mike Kaye of ESPN.com.

The Panthers also lost Jaycee Horn to a quad injury in Week 3 and the dual injuries thinned out the team’s cornerback corps. Getting both of their starters back for the stretch run would be a shot in the arm to Carolina’s chances of a second straight division title.
