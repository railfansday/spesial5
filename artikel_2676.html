Sixers aim to extend win streak to six vs. Trail Blazers

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Sixers just keep winning. It was too close for comfort in Saturday's contest, as their matchup against the last-place Bucks came down to the wire, but the Sixers still managed to come away with a 132-129 win. After a sluggish start, losing their double-digit lead late, and facing Tyler Herro going off for 50 points, they improved their record to 14-3.

Next, as they look to extend their win streak to six, the Sixers are at home against the 10-6 Trail Blazers.

Philadelphia are also likely getting some reinforcements back too. Jaylen Brown (hamstring), Dean Wade (hamstring) and Adem Bona (neck) are all listed as probable.

The Blazers have had a rock solid start to the season. They sit at seventh in the Western Conference and rank seventh in both offensive and defensive rating. They have, however, been hit with a couple of big absences. New addition Ja Morant got to play just four games and is now out with a hamstring injury, and Shaedon Sharpe is out until March after suffering a torn meniscus in the summer.

The Blazers are running lineups with plenty of size, from quality 6-foot-7 defender Toumani Camara and 6-foot-8 Deni Avdija on the wings, to the 7-foot-2 Donovan Clingan at center. That size and mobility is a key factor in their stout defense, and they complement that at the other end of the floor with their mix of creators and shooters, ranking fourth in team three-point attempts per game at 43.1 (making a decent 35.8 percent).

Avdija will be the top assignment for the Sixers' forwards. He's the Blazers' leading scorer at a career-high 24.2 points per game on an impressive 61.8 percent true shooting. He hasn't been too efficient at the rim so far this year, but he still has good driving ability, an effective mid-range game, and is shooting a career-high 38.6 percent from three.

LeBron James is certainly up for the task with the way he's been defending this year.

The backcourt matchups could also be fun in this one. Damian Lillard is fittingly back in Portland as his career approaches its end, and while his efficiency has dropped to start to the new season so far, he's still starting and putting up 20.6 points and 4.0 assists per game. He's leaning into three-point gunning being his main weapon more than ever, taking 61.9 percent of his field goal attempts from three (by far the highest mark of his career). Tyrese Maxey and VJ Edgecombe will need to stick close to Lillard at the arc and guard closely as he comes off screens.

Jrue Holiday's excellent perimeter defense will be another good test for Maxey and Edgecombe (the latter in particular). The 36-year-old Holiday is playing well as Portland's sixth man with 26.8 minutes per game, putting up 13.9 points, 3.5 rebounds, 4.3 assists, 1.2 steals and shooting 43.5 percent from three, all while delivering his quickness, physicality, strength and versatility on defense.

It'll be fun to see how Edgecombe fares when he's matched up against the Blazers' bigger wings and Holiday. Can Edgecombe deliver another performance like his big night against the Magic?

Clingan is a huge presence in the paint at both ends of the floor, and will give the Sixers some real challenges whenever they're running smaller lineups with Jabari Walker or Dominick Barlow (who, to his credit, played well immediately in his return on Saturday) at center. Clingan is averaging 10.9 rebounds (3.7 offensive) and 2.1 blocks in only 26.1 minutes per game, using his massive frame, 7-foot-7 wingspan, strength and timing as a rim protector and rebounder to be in the right spots at the right time.

Joel Embiid will need to bring his physicality both defensively and on the boards in this one to lead the Sixers' battle in the paint. Embiid can't switch off on Clingan as a floor spacer either. Portland's center is now taking 2.5 three-pointers a game and making 40.0 percent of them. The Blazers are also seventh in total rebound percentage as a team, so rebounding/boxing out by community is going to be needed to help keep Clingan and Co. in check to avoid giving up easy second chances. Bona will also need to be at his best if he returns and is going to hold up against Clingan, but having Bona's size and explosiveness off the bench could be needed — as good as Walker and Barlow have been.

The Sixers clearly weren't at their best against the Bucks on Saturday. An early tip-off against a 1-15 team after an exciting win against Orlando felt like a trap game, and it's one the Sixers just about survived. If they can snap back to their best, hold their own on the boards, and ideally stop the Blazers going off as they inevitably fire off a ton of threes, the Sixers should have a good shot at extending their win streak to six.
