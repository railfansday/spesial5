Steelers worked out veteran safety

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

After giving up 406 passing yards versus the Cincinnati Bengals on Sunday, it seems the Steelers have some interest in bolstering their secondary.

On Tuesday, the Steelers worked out veteran safety Jordan Whitehead, NFL writer Aaron Wilson reported.

Earlier in the day, Pittsburgh reportedly worked out former Chiefs cornerback Nazeeh Johnson, which Wilson also reported. The team also officially signed former Seattle Seahawks and Los Angeles Chargers safety AJ Finley to its practice squad.

Although Whitehead has never been on the Steelers, he should be a familiar name as a former Pitt Panther in college. Whitehead has also been linked to the Steelers a number of times, including visiting the team in July earlier this year.

Whitehead last played in 2024, when he started 12 games for the Tampa Bay Buccaneers, recording 79 total tackles and two passes defensed. He suffered a season-ending injury that year after suffering a serious neck injury in a car accident. He was cleared to resume his football career in July.

A fourth-round pick in 2018, the 29-year-old has appeared in 105 career NFL games, starting 101, with 11 total interceptions.
