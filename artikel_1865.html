Martinez insists Leverkusen must be ‘strong at the back’ against Paderborn

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Carles Martinez insisted his Bayer Leverkusen side must be "strong at the back" if they are to defeat Paderborn on Saturday.

Leverkusen have lost two of their last three Bundesliga matches, but defeated Koln 2-1 before the international break.

The Werkself lost 4-2 at home to Stuttgart following a 3-0 defeat at Hoffenheim, leaving Leverkusen fifth in the Bundesliga, five points behind Bayern Munich and Borussia Dortmund.

Paderborn, meanwhile, are 13th in the league after nine games, three points clear of the relegation playoff places, but also just six points behind Leverkusen.

Paderborn (W1 D3) are one of only three teams, alongside Bayern and Dortmund, to remain unbeaten in their last four Bundesliga matches.

This four-game unbeaten run equals Paderborn's club record in the Bundesliga, previously achieved only in their first four matches in the competition in 2014.

Martinez is wary of the task ahead in Paderborn, but hopes his Leverkusen players will perform in front of their home fans following the international break.

"The changes to the international break schedule are new for everyone," Martinez said.

"We've adapted well. After those gruelling weeks, we've used the time to recover and think carefully about exactly where and how we can improve.

"We can't wait to experience that special [home] atmosphere again – we've really missed it.

"Paderborn are having a good season, have a clear identity and try to dictate the play themselves. That's why we need to be particularly strong at the back. I expect the best from us."

Patrik Schick has scored eight goals in the Bundesliga this season, finding the net in all four of his home appearances.

Eight goals after nine Bundesliga matchdays equals the club record for Leverkusen, previously achieved only by Schick himself (2021-22) and Patrick Helmes (2008-09).

The Czech forward has now scored as many league goals on his own as the entire Paderborn team.

Santiago Castaneda is the only Paderborn outfield player to have played every minute in the Bundesliga this season.

The American has also recorded the second-most interceptions in the league (25, Kaishu Sano: 27) and has covered the second-most distance (108.4 km, Hennes Behrens: 109.2 km).

Leverkusen have not lost any of their seven previous professional meetings with Paderborn in all competitions (W6 D1), winning each of the last six.

Against no other current Bundesliga side have Leverkusen played as many professional matches without suffering a single defeat.

Leverkusen lost 3-2 away to Elversberg on the opening matchday of the Bundesliga season and could now lose consecutive Bundesliga games against promoted sides for the first time since 2017.

However, the Werkself are unbeaten in their last 10 home Bundesliga matches against promoted teams (W7 D3).

Paderborn are one of three teams without an away win in the Bundesliga this season, alongside Koln and Union Berlin.

Paderborn have drawn three of those matches (L1), including two 0-0 draws, and have conceded only five away goals – only Dortmund have conceded fewer on the road (four).
