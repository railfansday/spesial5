DramaBox Ties Knot With Toyota’s Indian Wedding Microdrama ‘3 Days Till I Do’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

EXCLUSIVE: DramaBox‘s latest microdrama original will be set around an Indian wedding.

3 Days Till I Do is now playing on the vertical video app. Commissioned by car manufacturer Toyota and its Asian American advertising agency, Intertrend Communications, this marks the latest example of a brand commissioning a full production to hit its consumer base.

The microdrama also marks L.A.-based DramaBox’s first Indian culture-themed original, with both cast and crew hailing from those with Asian heritage. DramaBox’s U.S. team wrote, developed and shot the show over a three-month period. Sarang Sharma is directing a script from Mayanka Goel, with Anja Vasa starring as Priya, Aman Jaisinghani playing Dev, and Fateh Singh cast as Arjun. The series was developed by Romil Sharma, produced by Roberta Sparta, with Sikandar Sidhu serving as executive producer for DramaBox. Watch on Deadline

Intertrend’s team collaborated closely with DramaBox’s director and production team to help shape the storytelling, guide cultural authenticity and integrate Toyota into the narrative. The production also brought together costume designer Charu Gupta, production designer Armaan Pujani, choreographer Kavita Rao, and henna artist Neha Assar.

Per the synopsis, “Three days before her wedding, Priya has everything she was raised to want: Arjun, a successful, dependable fiancé her father adores; a family bursting with pride; and a celebration full of music, color, and tradition. Then Arjun’s best man pulls into the driveway: Dev, the first love who broke her heart six years ago, is back, and he’s no longer the young man her father once dismissed. As the mehndi, haldi, and sangeet [pre-wedding celebrations] unfold, old feelings and unanswered questions resurface, and Priya begins to wonder whether being the perfect bride is what she really wants.”

DramaBox has been expanding internationally, and recently launched its first productions from Brazil, Colombia and Mexico, as we were first to report in August.

“DramaBox feels like the natural place for Toyota’s Asian Indian microdrama,” said Marcela Kartaszewicz, Head of Communications at DramaBox. “Global stories with local flavor are what we do best. Our creative team integrates brands organically into the script, and our worldwide reach opens a unique avenue for partners to meet new fans inside the stories they love.”

“At Toyota, we believe connecting with multicultural audiences starts with cultural understanding and meaningful engagement,” added Tiffany Shido, General Manager of Marketing, Toyota Motor North America. “Our collaboration with DramaBox and Intertrend brings together cultural insights, creativity, and entertainment to tell a story that celebrates Asian Indian wedding traditions while creating new ways to connect with the Toyota brand.”

The commission came as Omdia predicted social video advertising would account for almost half of the global TV and video revenues, which are set to hit $1.2T, by 2031. During data released at MIPCancun in Mexico, Omdia once again predicted the microdrama market would hit $14.1B in 2026, with 25% now being made outside of China, where it is a dominant form on consumption.

Omdia’s presentation noted most new microdramas are now AI productions, though 3 Days Till I Do is an entirely human creation, and pointed to evidence that brands such as Dr Pepper and JCPenney seeing the medium as a huge content opportunity.
