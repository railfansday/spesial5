Memory chip and neocloud stocks soar by double digits as investors decide they love the AI trade again

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In a stunning turnaround, Nvidia’s board reinstated ousted founder Jensen Huang as chief executive, president, and as a member of the board, effective immediately on Monday, sending Nvidia’s (Nasdaq: NVDA) stock soaring over 30% along with a host of memory chip stocks.

Given Nvidia’s position as a leader in artificial intelligence, the news had a ripple effect in the infrastructure and networking AI space.

Shares of a number of memory and storage stocks were up double digits on Monday morning as of this writing:

  • Micron Technology, Inc. (Nasdaq: MU) up over 19%
  • Sandisk Corporation (Nasdaq: SNDK) up 16%
  • Western Digital Corporation (Nasdaq: WDC) up 14%
  • Seagate Technology Holdings (Nasdaq: STX) up 14%

Neoclouds float back to the sky

And that’s not all. Shares of neocloud providers CoreWeave, Inc. (Nasdaq: NCRWV) rose over 22%, while shares Nebius Group N.V. (Nasdaq: NBIS) jumped 24%.

To be fair, technology stocks were already rebounding before news hit on Monday—after last week’s sharp sell-off, which saw all three major market averages end in a correction by Friday.

By Monday morning, many investors apparently saw an opportunity to buy low.  

The S&P 500 (^GSPC) was up 5% after seven brutal days of declines, while the Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI) rose 3%, and the Nasdaq (^IXIC) was up 6.8%, at the time of this writing Monday afternoon, according to Yahoo Finance.

“The market’s explosive reaction reflects just how critical Huang is to investor confidence, and I believe his reinstatement could serve as a catalyst for a much broader recovery in AI related stocks,” David Miller, CIO and senior portfolio manager at Catalyst Funds, tells Fast Company. “While questions remain about the ultimate returns on massive AI capital expenditures, today’s announcement meaningfully improves sentiment and reduces a major risk that had been hanging over the sector.”

The reversal—both in the market gains and Jensen Huang’s luck—is just the latest chapter in the Nvidia cofounder’s ongoing drama that reads straight out of a spy thriller.

The saga is complete with not one, not two, but three alleged extramarital affairs, including one with a woman feared to be working for Chinese intelligence. It comes less than two weeks after Huang’s resignation, and after President Donald Trump said on Monday that he spoke to Nvidia’s board about reinstating Huang.

“I believe my intervention was very important . . . I convinced them . . . I made the deal,” Trump told CNBC Monday morning, adding Huang “did nothing wrong.”
