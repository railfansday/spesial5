What’s happening with banks today? Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo customers report widespread outages

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

If you’re having trouble accessing your bank account today, you’re not alone.

Customers at several U.S. financial giants reported problems accessing their accounts on Monday as widespread service disruptions appear to have hit Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, and other financial institutions.

The problems started Monday morning, November 16, when thousands of customers began reporting trouble logging in to their Chase accounts, checking their balances, and completing transactions.

According to outage-tracking website Downdetector, Chase appeared to be the hardest hit, with more than 4,000 reports by early Monday afternoon. But Chase wasn’t alone: Bank of America and Wells Fargo also saw spikes in customer complaints, along with U.S. Bank and payment service Zelle.

Reached for comment by Fast Company, a Chase spokesperson said that a technical issue impacted not only Chase but also other banks it does business with.

“JPMorgan Chase has experienced a temporary issue related to a software bug in on-premise hardware systems—there is no evidence this is cyber-related,” the spokesperson said in a statement. “It is taking longer than usual to service some customers. This is not a systemic issue—other banks may have been impacted by slowness in transactions only to the extent that they were a party to a transaction with us. Customers still have access to their accounts and funds. We’re working diligently to resolve this by the end of the day today.”

Fast Company has also reached out to the other banks for more details.

The banking troubles come after multiple separate major technology disruptions in recent days, including a widespread Microsoft Outlook outage on Thursday and a nationwide Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) ground stop Friday, disrupting air travel across the country.

There’s no confirmed connection between those incidents and Monday’s banking problem.

Is Chase down?

Chase customers began reporting problems around 9:48 a.m. ET Monday, according to Downdetector.

Logging into the website was among the most common complaints, although some customers also ran into issues with the mobile app and viewing their account balances and transaction histories.

And the problems are not limited to online banking.

Some customers on Reddit reported trouble accessing services at physical Chase branches, suggesting the disruption could extend beyond the bank’s website and app.

At one New York City branch visited by Fast Company on Monday, Chase associates were alerting customers as they entered the door that systems were down.

Issues at Bank of America, Wells Fargo, and other financial giants

While possibly the worst hit, Chase wasn’t the only bank having a rough Monday, though Chase indicated that those issues were related to its own technical issues.

Downdetector also recorded a jump in outage reports involving Bank of America, Wells Fargo, U.S. Bank, and Truist. Some customers said they were unable to view transactions or complete payments and transfers.

Zelle, the popular person-to-person payment service used by many major banks, also saw more outage complaints.

It’s worth keeping in mind that Downdetector relies on reports submitted by users. A spike in complaints doesn’t necessarily mean a bank’s entire system is down, and the numbers don’t reflect the total number of customers affected.
