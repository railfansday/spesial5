Japanese Charts: No Prizes For Guessing What Has Overtaken Zelda & Switch 2 This Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The latest Japanese charts are in from Famitsu, and in perhaps the least surprising debut in recent memory, Grand Theft Auto VI is a hit.

Rockstar's code-in-a-box release had a staggering debut week in Japan, pulling in over 200,000 physical sales and kicking Ocarina of Time's comparatively measly ~35,000 into second. It's shy of the 537,000 Link's latest put up in its debut week, of course, but for a box with no disc in it, it's not bad going.

The rest of the chart is a Switch-fest, mind you, with the likes of Nintendo Switch Sports Resort and Rhythm Heaven Groove still performing well, and last week's silver medallist, Metaphor: ReFantazio, hanging in there at ninth.

All of that GTA love means that Switch 2 has been dethroned as champion of the hardware charts this week. The PS5 Digital Edition sneaks into the top spot, with a perfectly respectable 40,362 sales to its name, while Nintendo's latest follows behind with ~36,000 of its own.

You don't need us to spell it out for you, but that means the PS5 wins the SKU race over the Switch 1 this week. Sony's three systems put up 41,472 total sales, while the Switch, Lite, and OLED combined for 4,362.
