How To Get All Achievements In Dice Hoarder

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

While you're organizing all those dice, why not collect all the achievements too?

Dice Hoarder is a collectathon game where the goal is to organize a sea of tabletop dice scattered all over the floor of a small shop. The game is simple enough, but it has plenty of achievements to collect that might not be very clear.

Luckily, even though you have to sort through thousands of dice, you only need to earn sixteen achievements over the course of the game. You can claim them all in a single playthrough of the campaign, but you'd have to do it the hard way. Here are all the achievements in Dice Hoarder and how to get them.

In a regular playthrough of Dice Hoarder, you should be able to get every achievement, but one will make it a bit more challenging. Here is a breakdown of all the game's achievements and how to get each one.

Left-click the cat and drag your cursor back and forth to pet it.

Place any die into its slot in the store.

When you find the first floppy disk, install it onto the computer to learn the skill.

Place seven corresponding dice into the correct slot in the store until the slot glows.

After unlocking the resin caster, mix, pour and TKTKT the dice before pulling them out of the slots.

Fill any 50 slots in the store with the correct dice.

Fill any 100 slots in the store with the correct dice.

In any nine-slot display, fill all slots with the correct dice until all slots glow.

Fill a display slot with seven dice, each from a different set.

Take a d20, d12, d10, d8, d6, d4 and percentile die from different sets and put them all in the same slot.

Fill any 200 slots in the store with the correct dice.

Take any d20 in the store and keep tossing it into the rolling tray until you roll a natural 20.

Install every floppy disk on the computer as they show up over the course of the campaign.

Complete the campaign by putting every die in the store in its correct slot

Eat all the hidden edible dice in the shop.

Eat the ten hidden dice throughout the shop that don't have a slot, that are stuck in corners and on shelves.

After unlocking each skill, go into the computer and use XP to level them all up until you reach the max level in each.

Without using True Sight, Dice Summons, Guidance or Critical Sort, finish sorting out all the dice in the store.
