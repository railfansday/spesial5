Dark visual novel PRIMA MATERIA announced for PC

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Publisher Top Hat Studios and Synergia developer Radi Art have announced PRIMA MATERIA, a dark visual novel coming to PC via Steam in 2027.

Here is an overview of the game, via Top Hat Studios:

PRIMA MATERIA is a dark surreal visual novel set in a sterilized wasteland ruled by Angels, where consciousness is harvested, brewed, and sold. Mana, a Mind Digger who survives by eating minds, takes one last job: fixing a demi-angel worth keeping whole.

“I must reject morality because it serves those in power; this is a mechanical world where I am nothing but a mechanical device and so is everyone else. Everything’s real, nothing’s in your head.”

In the Concrete Sea, an endless grey wasteland beneath a sun that never sets, consciousness is a resource. Angels farm humans for it, brewing it over centuries into Prima Materia, a luminous orange liquid that can be distilled and most importantly, consumed. Mana is a Mind Digger, someone who carves psyches for a living, extracting and eating what others don’t want to carry anymore: guilt, fear, memories.

Her self-contained existence collapses with the arrival of Girin: the rebellious daughter of an ancient Angel, dropped at Mana’s door with a simple request to “fix” her, in exchange for an infinite supply of Prima Materia. Girin’s untamable nature doesn’t make things easy, but it’s what lies deep inside her mind that scares Mana. Something old and protective is waiting, something that recognizes Mana for what she is and that has no interest in letting her anywhere near Girin’s core.

What begins as a transaction quickly becomes something stranger, more intimate, and far more dangerous. Because the deeper Mana digs, the more she finds the memory of the person she was before she carved herself down to appetite and survival.
