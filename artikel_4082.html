AMD, Sony Mark PS5's 100 Million Unit Milestone with Signed Consoles

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

PS5 is the fourth Sony system to officially ship over 100 million units during its lifespan, and it may be the manufacturer’s most impressive achievement yet.

The firm has nimbly navigated a global pandemic, semi-conductor shortages, US electronics tariffs, a slumping global economy, and now AI-induced price increases to achieve the feat.

It remains within touching distance of the PS4 life-to-date, despite the latter costing less than half the PS5’s price at the same point in its lifecycle.

Celebrations are in order, then, and current PlayStation CEO Hideaki Nishino caught up with AMD’s Jack Huynh to sign some consoles fresh out of the factory.

AMD is obviously a longtime partner of Sony, and is currently working extremely closely with system architect Mark Cerny on bespoke solutions for the PS6.

Even amid the current component cost crisis, it’s going to be fascinating to see what the two companies concoct. PlayStation hardware typically punches well above its weight thanks to smart optimisation and great product design.
