City of Benicia Wins Statewide Award for Cutting Red Tape and Modernizing Permitting

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

BENICIA, Calif., Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- What once required paper applications, multiple trips to City Hall and time-consuming steps can now be completed online in Benicia. This transformation has streamlined permitting for residents, contractors and businesses, earning the City a 2026 Helen Putnam Award for Excellence from the League of California Cities.

Selected from 161 submissions statewide, Benicia was one of 12 cities recognized for innovation in local government. The City received the award in the Internal Administration category for its Permit Services Approach, a five-year initiative that transformed its paper-based permitting process into an electronic system.

Today, 74% of Benicia's permits are processed through automated Express Permits or SolarAPP+. Permits that required hours of in-person processing can now be approved automatically.

Making Permitting Easier for Residents, Contractors and Businesses

Previously, obtaining a permit required in-person visits during limited hours, paper applications and physical plan sets. Development Services staff managed permit counter traffic, data entry and an average of 30 to 40 phone calls daily.
