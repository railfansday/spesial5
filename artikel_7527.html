Federal Jury Convicts Crucian Man For Possessing “Glock switch” Machine Gun

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

St. Croix, USVI – U.S. Attorney Adam F. Sleeper announced today that a federal jury convicted Ervin Noel Jr, 22, of St. Croix, for Possession of a Machinegun, 18 U.S.C. § 922(o), and Reckless Endangerment, 14 V.I.C. § 625(a). The defendant faces a sentence of up to 10 years imprisonment. The Chief District Court Judge, Robert A. Molloy, will schedule a sentencing hearing to determine the sentence to be imposed.
