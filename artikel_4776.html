Repeatable laser measurements reveal 'forbidden' vibrations in single molecules without destroying them

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The possibility of controlling "forbidden" excitations of vibrations in molecules could form the basis of extremely accurate clocks, quantum bits and precise tests of the fundamental laws of physics. Researchers at the University of Basel have now identified and controlled such "forbidden" transitions in single isolated nitrogen molecules without destroying them.
