Sigmetrix Announces the Release of VariSight 3.0: New Analytics and Comparison Capabilities for Mechanical Variation Management

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

VariSight's latest release delivers deeper insight into variation drivers, enhanced visualization of statistical results, and improved management of tolerance analysis data

MCKINNEY, Texas, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Sigmetrix today announced the launch of VariSight 3.0, the latest release of its cloud-based platform for managing, collaborating, and comparing tolerance analysis results generated with CETOL 6Ï. Designed for manufacturing organizations responsible for product quality, dimensional control, and variation management, the new release adds advanced comparison capabilities, improved statistical visualization, and enhanced support for maintaining analysis data across the product life cycle.

VariSight serves as a centralized repository for CETOL 6Ï studies, enabling engineering teams to evaluate analysis results, track design changes, and collaborate across design, manufacturing, and quality disciplines. Version 3.0 introduces several enhancements intended to provide greater visibility into the factors driving variation and to streamline the review of analysis results.

VariSight â Mechanical Variation Management Platform

VariSight is Sigmetrix's enterprise platform for managing tolerance analysis and variation simulation data. By centralizing CETOL 6Ï results and enabling detailed comparison of engineering studies across revisions, VariSight helps organizations improve design decisions, accelerate root-cause analysis, and maintain visibility into the factors that drive product variation.

Detailed Sensitivity and Contribution Comparisons

A key enhancement in VariSight 3.0 is the ability to compare sensitivities and contributions between revisions of an analysis model. Engineers can now evaluate how design modifications affect critical variation drivers and identify the root causes of changes in assembly performance. This capability supports more effective design reviews and enables teams to focus corrective actions on the parameters that most strongly influence product variation.

VariSight 3.0 expands access to statistical results through the addition of distribution plots within detailed measurement views. Engineers can now examine the underlying distributions associated with analysis outputs without leaving the application. Additionally, distribution plots from baseline and comparison revisions can be displayed side-by-side within the Results Comparison summary, making it easier to assess shifts in process capability, variation spread, and measurement performance between design iterations.

To improve communication across multidisciplinary teams, VariSight now supports the association of images with measurement definitions. Users can upload image sets that provide additional context for measurement locations and requirements, helping reviewers correlate statistical results with physical product features and inspection characteristics.

VariSight 3.0 introduces a new service that automatically upgrades supported legacy CETOL 6Ï datasets to the officially supported release during upload. This feature simplifies the management of historical analyses and helps organizations maintain consistency when comparing results generated across multiple CETOL versions. The release officially supports CETOL 6Ï v12.3.2 while automatically up-versioning data created in versions 11.5.4 through 12.3.1.

Productivity and Collaboration Improvements

The release also includes several usability enhancements designed to improve engineering workflows. Application URLs now capture the complete page configuration, enabling engineers to create permanent bookmarks and share direct links to specific analysis views. Additional workflow improvements include direct analysis upload capabilities from the CAD Model Details page and simplified revision management from the Analysis Model Details page.

Supporting Modern Engineering Environments

VariSight 3.0 supports the latest releases of Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Firefox on Windows 11. The release also includes support for the VariSight PLM Connector with PTC Windchill 12.0, helping organizations integrate variation analysis data into broader product lifecycle management processes.
