Bitcoin was trading at $64,768 on Tuesday morning, up 1.27% over the past 24 hours, after the price of the cryptocurrency touched $62,000 over the weekend. Ether rose to $1,810, and Solana gained 3.92% to $73.18.

Exchanges liquidated $279.23 million in positions over the past 24 hours, and the amounts liquidated in longs and shorts were almost equal, according to CoinGlass.

Binance co-founder Changpeng Zhao blamed the drop to $62,000 on a combination of ETF outflows, high interest rates, and the Anthropic IPO. Anthropic is set to go public on Nov. 19 with a $1.4 trillion valuation.

VanEck's head of research, Matthew Sigel, also ties Bitcoin's pullback to liquidity flowing into the Anthropic IPO. Sigel pointed to SpaceX, where the market bottomed 18 days after the listing in June. He also mentioned the risk of a $500 billion cut in Nvidia's AI funding after Jensen Huang stepped down as Nvidia CEO on Nov. 12. Nvidia's quarterly report on Nov. 17 is the company's first since Huang's exit.

XRP was trading at $0.966 on Tuesday morning, up 1.73% over the past 24 hours. According to CryptoQuant analyst Darkfost, whales have withdrawn more than $127 million worth of XRP from Binance in net terms since Nov. 10.

Darkfost said that between $12 million and $33 million worth of XRP left Binance every day last week, and on Nov. 16, whales withdrew another $21.4 million. Since July 2025, whales had mostly been sending XRP to Binance, according to Darkfost.

Darkfost said that during the August rally, inflows of XRP to Binance reached $75 million, and on some days, deposits went up to $270 million. Some of the latest withdrawals may still be internal transfers by Binance, which was moving funds between its own wallets after the hack.

U.S. spot XRP ETFs recorded net outflows of $19.1 million between Nov. 9 and Nov. 13, according to SoSoValue.

Ripple-backed Evernorth filed its first quarterly report with the SEC on Nov. 13, ahead of its Nasdaq listing under the ticker XRPN. As of Sept. 30, the company had written down its XRP holdings by $505 million, or almost 60%.

The market value of the 473 million XRP held by the company stood at $516 million on that date, and Evernorth plans to use the reserves for yield strategies and ecosystem development.

Developer Jameson Lopp reminded Bitcoin holders of the core principle: "Focus on your hatred of governments and central banks rather than your love of wealth. Bitcoin will always be easy to hold if you prioritize sovereignty over price." — Jameson Lopp
