Roland DG's "Make Your Mark" Global Brand Refresh Wins Three Drum B2B Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Global Brand Refresh Recognized with Two Silver Awards and One Bronze at The Drum B2B Awards 2026

IRVINE, Calif., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Roland DG Corporation, a leading provider of digital printing solutions, has won three awards at The Drum B2B Awards 2026 for its global brand refresh and Make Your Mark campaign.

Announced at The Drum B2B Awards ceremony in Miami, Roland DG received:

The recognition follows the global launch of Make Your Mark in April 2026, which introduced a refreshed brand message, visual identity and creative platform designed to reflect both Roland DG's more than four decades of engineering excellence and the evolving ambitions of its customers around the world.

More than just a visual evolution, Make Your Mark represented a strategic shift in how Roland DG presents its global business, positioning the company around the complete solutions it provides, including high-performance hardware, intelligent software, supplies, and service. At its heart is a simple idea: empowering people to turn their ideas into reality and make a positive impact through what they produce.

The award-winning creative brought this positioning to life through a richer and more expressive use of photography, color and real-world applications, placing customers and their work at the center of Roland DG's global communications.

Dan Wilson, VP of Global Marketing at Roland DG, said: "Make Your Mark was built around what we heard directly from our customers: that creating great work is about more than the technology itself. It's about the confidence and impact that comes from what you produce."

Wilson added: "To see that thinking recognized across photography, corporate positioning and brand positioning is incredibly rewarding. These awards are a testament to the work that went into creating a global brand platform that feels true to Roland DG's heritage, while giving us a distinctive way to tell our story as the business continues to evolve."

Make Your Mark was informed by in-depth customer interviews and surveys with thousands of Roland DG users around the world. The research highlighted that customers value not only product performance, but the confidence and pride that comes from the work they create.

Those insights shaped Roland DG's new visual and verbal identity, bringing greater consistency across the company's global communications while creating a more expressive platform designed to resonate with print professionals, manufacturers, entrepreneurs, and makers.

Since its launch, the refreshed identity has been rolled out across Roland DG's global digital channels, product communications, and major international industry events, supporting the company as it continues to expand into new applications and market segments.
