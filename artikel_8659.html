Thank you for taking the time to join us today. I am Matsumoto. Building on our review of the Fifth Mid-Term Plan, I will explain how, in the Sixth Mid-Term Plan, we will integrate sustainability with our management strategy and connect it to value creation.

First, I will review our sustainability initiatives under the Fifth Mid-Term Plan. Under the Fifth Mid-Term Plan, we steadily advanced the creation of innovative pharmaceuticals and the improvement of global access to health care, while also strengthening the foundations that support our business, such as decarbonization, compliance and our human resource base. As a result, we further strengthened the foundation for achieving both social and economic value and delivered steady results toward realizing our purpose.

Among our key achievements, on the business side, we launched 10 indications with our 3 ADCs, and the cumulative number of priority review designations from fiscal 2021 to fiscal 2025 reached 51. Access to health care for patients also expanded, and ENHERTU has been launched in 99 countries and regions. On the business foundation side, we reduced Scope 1 and 2 CO2 emissions by 48.5% compared with fiscal 2015. We also maintained 0 significant compliance violations and improved the positive response rate in our engagement survey, strengthening each area.
