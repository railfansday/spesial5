Here's What a $1,000 Investment in Tesla Stock Could Be Worth in 5 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Recognized as a market-beating portfolio addition over the long term, Tesla shares have actually fallen 16% in the past half decade.

Investors view Tesla (TSLA +4.23%) as a perennial winner. Over the last decade, this is true. Shares have soared 2,630% since early November 2016 (as of Nov. 5, 2026).

But the story takes a dramatic turn if you zoom in. This "Magnificent Seven" stock has actually dropped 16% in the past five years. That performance means that it has significantly underperformed the broader market. Investors are left wondering if the company's shares are staying out of the fast lane permanently.

Here's what a $1,000 investment in Tesla could be worth in five years. There is, without a doubt, significant upside. However, uncertainty is sky-high.

One of the core tenets of Warren Buffett's investing philosophy is to own what you know. Investors must be able to confidently figure out what a business will look like in the future. Naturally, this limits the opportunity set to stable and predictable companies.

Tesla is the opposite. It's like the business is always undergoing some sort of strategic restructuring. This makes it extremely difficult to assess what the future will bring.

Five years from now, Tesla's operations could look very similar to what they are today. During the third quarter (ended Sept. 30), 73% of the company's revenue came from selling electric vehicles (EVs). This business continues to struggle, though, as competition diverts consumer spending to rival offerings, which has forced Tesla to lower pricing. Deliveries dipped 2% year over year in Q3.

It also doesn't help that car demand is exposed to changing economic winds, adding volatility to the mix. Just ask the legacy mass-market auto manufacturers how much their profits can fluctuate based on interest rates and other macro variables.

Tesla is working on exciting projects that attract the market's attention. While the Robotaxi service is only running in select markets right now, with Cybercab only in Austin, Texas, the future of the company rests on autonomous vehicle technology. The ultimate goal is to run a global fleet that collects recurring revenue at tremendous scale.

Optimus, the humanoid robot, is another important part of the story. These won't be available for sale until the end of 2027, at the earliest. However, Elon Musk said in 2024 that this product could drive Tesla's market capitalization to $25 trillion one day.

Tesla's future rests squarely on its ability to bring artificial intelligence (AI) capabilities into the physical world. Self-driving EVs and robotics are the avenues. Their market opportunities appear to be massive. But there are an unlimited number of questions surrounding technical advancements, scaling production, regulatory developments, user adoption, safety and reliability, and timelines.

If you view them at a high level, it's easy to assume that shares are egregiously expensive. The belief is that this has always been the case. Investors can buy Tesla stock right now by paying a forward price-to-earnings ratio of 151.

That multiple is more than 11 times higher than what Nvidia trades at. The dominant artificial intelligence infrastructure business is forecast to bring in $190 billion of free cash flow in fiscal 2027. That's almost double what Tesla will report as revenue this year.

The Elon Musk premium is real. The investment community prizes what he says and does. And the general view is that he's the greatest entrepreneur and visionary ever.

This feeds into the positive sentiment. Tesla's strongest bulls will argue that the company's optionality signals that shares are severely undervalued. Robotaxi and Optimus will usher in a new era of financial prosperity that will contribute to unprecedented profit growth.

The bears point to a track record of over-promising and under-delivering on key milestones and deadlines. It seems that Tesla has always been working toward some ambitious goal that it never fully achieves. And investors are always left having to buy the narrative and ignore the current reality of the business.

An extreme valuation coupled with sky-high uncertainty lead me to believe that the stock will largely stay flat over the next five years, leaving investors with the $1,000 that they started with.
