Every Playable Video Game in GTA 6, Ranked Worst to Best

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Why rob a bank when you can play Time Jackers?

Grand Theft Auto 6 is so big that it’s actually five games in one. Like previous games in the series, there are several games-within-the-game that you can play while hanging out in Leonida. These aren’t just quick arcade games that you can play for a few minutes, but full-on console games that are actually pretty fun in their own right.

It’s a playful meta joke from Rockstar, and I would be remiss not to play into it. Where there are games, there must be a video game website ranking those games in an arbitrary list. Here’s how the four games featured in GTA 6 stack up. Even if they aren’t all winners, they’re all at least high-effort jokes that deserve some praise.

As a parody of the kind of hunting games you find in dive bars, Bang For Your Buck is a pretty one-note gag. It’s an on-rails shooter where you simply have to gun down bucks while sparing does that populate three stages. The only real twist is that your money ticks down along with the timer, so you need to kill deer rapidly to keep playing. That mechanic is reused for a much better game that you’ll see higher on this list, so Bang For Your Buck doesn’t have much going for it beyond the obvious hunting satire. But as a piece of worldbuilding? It’s pure Florida, and I’m glad that it exists in Leonida.

Street Trash 3: Dezombifier is GTA 6’s very own Vampire Survivors clone. It’s a top-down twin-stick shooter where the goal is to gun through dense waves of zombies (and skeletons, for some reason) as you hunt for gears. As a send-up, it’s a little hit-and-miss. The roguelite is fun enough with its reliable choose-three level-up system, though it could have benefited from adopting Vampire Survivors’ more intuitive auto-firing action. It’s a little less effective as satire than some other entries on his list, since it feels more like a parody of AAA action games, complete with its quippy narrator. Still, it’s a fun little distraction that even has its own unlock system to keep you playing and find new weapons.

Arguably the funniest and most unexpected gag in all of GTA 6, Tenebrae Lux is Rockstar at the height of its satirical powers. The black-and-white puzzle game is a send-up of arthouse indie games created by auteur developers. More specifically, it’s a parody of Jonathan Blow, the creator of The Witness and Order of the Sinking Star. The convoluted experience (created solely by fictional developer Johannes Zuch) sees you exploring a mysterious island as a pompous narrator waxes poetic about philosophy. Funny enough, it’s a pretty well-designed puzzle game with galaxy-brained solutions. While it’s debatably a little too cerebral to enjoy casually, it’s an undeniably great spoof that respects this kind of game enough to lampoon them so perfectly.

The better of GTA 6’s two twin-stick shooters, Time Jackers Rewind is an over-the-top sci-fi game. A wisecracking soldier’s attempt to take down DominiCorp hits a snag when he accidentally travels through time. He gets some time powers in the process, but the twist is that he will die if he runs out of juice. You can only top it off by killing mutants, which basically makes Time Jackers Rewind a Crank video game in my book. It's a fast-paced shooter that pushes you to perfect your route through the city as you strategically acquire enough power to stay alive. It’s just a great mechanical premise, even if you take away the loudmouthed hero who is constantly tossing out hilariously hammy one-liners.
