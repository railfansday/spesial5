These Are the Best Early Black Friday 2026 Deals on Samsung Devices

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Black Friday might be a couple of weeks away, but Samsung is rolling out the discounts early, with deals on its entire lineup of devices, including smartphones, tablets, laptops, and wearables. While not all of these early Black Friday offers are true discounts, you can find ways to save money on some devices through these promotions. Here's what Samsung's offering ahead of the official Black Friday discounts.

Samsung is offering some decent discounts on both its flagship smartphones and its midrange and budget lines. However, as part of its early Black Friday sale, the company is also offering increased trade-in credits towards these devices. While all of the devices here have trade-in credit options, only some have optional discounts for early Black Friday. Here's what you can expect:

Samsung's premier flagship, the S26 Ultra, is getting a price cut ahead of Black Friday. You can save up to $350 off Samsung's MSRP without trading in another device, or up to $720 if you bring something to trade in. That brings the cost down from $1,399.99 (256GB) to $1,049.99.

Galaxy S26 Ultra: Up to $720 trade-in credit ($250 off without trade-in)

Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold 8 is the company's new "passport-style" foldable. This isn't Samsung's premier foldable—that title belongs to the Z Fold 8 Ultra—but the Z Fold 8 offers a similar experience in a smaller footprint and price tag. Ahead of Black Friday, you can take $350 off the Z Fold 8 without trade-in, or get up to $800 when trading in another device.

Galaxy Z Fold 8: Up to $800 trade-in credit (or $350 off without trade-in)

Here are the other deals Samsung is offering this year:

Galaxy S26 FE: Up to $400 trade-in credit ($150 off without trade-in)

Galaxy S26: Up to $340 trade-in credit ($150 off without trade-in)

Galaxy S26+: Up to $440 trade-in credit ($250 off without trade-in)

Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra: Up to $800 trade-in credit (or $350 without trade-in)

Galaxy Z Flip 8: Up to $400 trade-in credit (or $200 off without trade-in)

Galaxy A27 5G: Up to $120 trade-in credit ($100 off without trade-in)

Galaxy A37 5G: Up to $205 trade-in credit (or $100 without trade-in)

Galaxy A57 5G: Up to $205 trade-in credit (or $100 without trade-in)

Samsung's wearable deals are much like its smartphone deals: mostly trade-in offers, but some traditional discounts if you don't have a trade-in as well.

Samsung's Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 is the company's latest model aimed at athletes and power users. Right now, the company will take $100 off the watch without trade-in, or up to $250 off depending on the device you choose to exchange.

Galaxy Watch Ultra 2: $599.99 down from $699.99 (without trade-in)

Samsung's latest flagship smartwatch is (sort of) on sale for Black Friday. Like the Galaxy Watch Ultra 2, you can take $50 off the watch without a trade-in, or up to $250 when trading in with another device:

Galaxy Watch 9: $329 down from $379 (without trade-in)

Samsung also has deals available for the following watches and wearables:

Galaxy Watch 8 Classic: Up to $150 trade-in credit

Galaxy Watch 8: Up to $150 trade-in credit

Galaxy Watch 7: Up to $100 trade-in credit

Galaxy Ring: Up to $150 trade-in credit (out of stock as of this article)

Galaxy XR: Up to $1,030 off (plus three months of YouTube TV at $1 per month)

For some of its tablets this early Black Friday, Samsung is advertising that it will take off a certain amount in "total savings." That's a wiggily way of saying that to get the best deal, you'll need to stack multiple offers at once: certain trade-in values, per-device discounts, as well as discounts from the Samsung Offer Program.

That "total savings" deal applies to Samsung's Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra. This tablet typically starts at $1,399.99, but with the right trade-in, you can take off $550. Samsung says you can take another $100 off with code "PAYPAL100," and stack another discount at checkout, which is as high as 15% for certain products. According to Samsung, all of those combined will bring your Tab S12 Ultra down to $304—of course, if your trade-in isn't valued at $550, the tablet's total will be higher.

Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra: Up to $1,095 in "total savings" ($550 trade-in credit)

Samsung is running promotions on these tablets as well:

Galaxy Tab S10 Lite: Up to $75 in trade-in credit

Galaxy Tab S10 FE: Up to $110 in trade-in credit

Galaxy Tab S10+: Up to $205 in trade-in credit

Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra: Up to $305 in trade-in credit

Galaxy Tab S12+: Up to $995 in "total savings" ($550 trade-in credit)

Galaxy Tab A11+: Up to $75 trade-in credit

Galaxy Tab A11+ Kids Edition: Up to $75 trade-in credit

Samsung has early Black Friday deals on both laptops (specifically Galaxy Book 6 devices) and external monitors, particularly ones meant for gaming.

Samsung's 49-inch OLED G9 gaming monitor is currently $720 off, bringing the price from $2,039.98 to $1,179.99. Plus, you can take another $50 with Samsung's "upgrade credit," bringing the price down to $1,129.99. Samsung says it'll throw in a 24-inch "Essential" S3 monitor as well.

Odyssey OLED G9 (49-inch): $859.99 off, plus $50 upgrade credit

Samsung has a confusing deal with its Book 6 laptop. The company says it'll offer up to $335 (or more) in trade-in credits, but it'll take off $500 if you don't have a trade-in. I'm not sure what the incentive for trading in a device would be, but if you don't, you can add another $165 to the savings. Samsung says you can also take $100 off with "PAYPAL100," and stack another 15% discount on top of that:

Galaxy Book 6: Up to $500 trade-in credit ($335 trade-in credit); $100 off with code; 15% off at purchase

Galaxy Book 6 Edge: Up to $335 trade-in credit

MovingStyle Essential Smart Monitor (43-inch): $200 off, plus $150 upgrade credit

Odyssey OLED G8 (27-inch): $300 off, plus $50 upgrade credit

Samsung doesn't have as many TV deals as it does for its other devices, but there are some good options here—particularly for the company's more expensive offerings:

Right now, Samsung is taking up to $1,500 off its Micro RGB R95H TVs. That brings the price of the 85-inch model from $6,499.99 to $4,999.99.

Similarly, the company is taking up to $1,500 off its S95H line of OLED TVs. That means the 83-inch model has the same price drop: From $6,499.99 to $4,999.99.

Samsung is taking up to $1,200 off the company's NEO QLED QN80H TVs. The 85-inch model drops from $2,999.99 to $1,799.99.

Finally, Samsung is cutting its Mini LED M80H line by up to $500 for early Black Friday. That brings the price of its 85-inch model from $1,599.99 to $1,099.99.

In short, yes, Black Friday still offers discounts that can be rare throughout the rest of the year. If there’s something you want to buy, or you’re shopping for gifts, it’s a good time to look for discounts on what you need, especially tech sales, home improvement supplies, and fitness tech. Of course, if you need to save money, the best way to save is not to buy anything.

Prime Day and Black Friday are the two best times of the year to get the best deals on most purchases. Of course, depending on what you're shopping for, you might find other opportunities throughout the year, but in general, Prime Day and Black Friday have the best sales.
