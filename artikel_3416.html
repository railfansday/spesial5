GTA 6 May Actually Play Better on Xbox After All

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

PlayStation 5 might not be the best place to play Rockstar’s latest

In the lead-up to the release of Grand Theft Auto 6, Sony and Rockstar Games have stated that the highly anticipated game "plays best on PS5." For some gamers looking to purchase a console solely to play GTA 6​​​​​​​, this statement may be the deciding factor in getting an Xbox or a PlayStation. Yet, about a week after the game was released, it now appears that Xbox gamers may not actually be at as great a disadvantage​​​​​​​ as Sony would like you to think.

The tech aficionados at Digital Foundry have published a comprehensive comparison of how GTA 6 runs on all available platforms. While the details of the report are fascinating, let's cut right to the shocking results. It turns out GTA 6 actually plays better on Xbox... in the right circumstances. When comparing the base PlayStation 5 to the Xbox Series X (which are relatively equivalent), Digital Foundry discovered that while the visuals are basically the same on the two consoles, there is a clearer distinction when comparing performance​​​​​​​. Digital Foundry stated that "PS5 suffers substantially more frequent frame-rate drops than Series X, which is compounded by the fact that the Xbox machine also benefits from 120 Hz VRR support to smooth out minor dips," concluding that "Ultimately​​​​​​​, Series X is meaningfully better off."

It is important to note that Digital Foundry's study is strictly focused on technical performance, which ignores a big reason GTA 6 has been stated to play best on PS5: the controller. Sony's DualSense offers unique features like haptic feedback and adaptive triggers tuned to every little detail in the game, such as the weight of a gas pedal in a car or the buzz of a phone. It makes the experience more immersive.​​​​​​​ To the average gamer, the slight difference between Xbox Series X and PS5 may not be as noticeable as the difference the DualSense makes.​​​​​​​ But tech heads will be glad to know this.​​​​​​​

Even with the surprise that Xbox Series X plays somewhat better than the base PS5, Digital Foundry still asserts that PS5 Pro ​​​​​​​delivers the definitive GTA 6 experience​​​​​​​ thanks to its larger GPU, Sony's PSSR upscaling, and more. All of this allows for the most stable version of GTA 6. Given the price​​​​​​​ of every console on the market right now, individual gamers will have to decide whether the technical benefits justify the ever-growing cost of hardware​​​​​​​.

Xbox gets another win with the budget Xbox Series S. ​​​​​​​Despite being significantly less powerful than every other Xbox and PlayStation console on which GTA 6 is available​​​​​​​, the Series S still manages to put up a good fight. This is accomplished by lowering visual quality​​​​​​​ and taking advantage of upscaling from 720p to 1080p. While less stable than the Series X, Digital Foundry noted that the image quality is still decent.
