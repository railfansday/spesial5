One Of Our Favorite Action Games From Gamescom, Showa American Story, Has Been Delayed To 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Showa American Story is set in a world where Japan's 1980s economic prowess was strong enough to purchase America, and so it did. Taking place in Neo Yokohama, formerly Hollywood, this raunchy, zany action game was one of our favorites at Gamescom 2026, but unfortunately, we'll have to wait a little longer than expected to experience more of this adventure.

That's because developer Nekcom Entertainment and publisher 4Divinity have revealed that Showa American Story has been delayed to next year. Originally due out sometime this year (though it became clear that wasn't going to happen given the radio silence as we near the year's end), Showa American Story will now launch on PlayStation 5 and PC on October 28, 2027.

"Reaching this milestone after five years of development means a lot to our entire team," the studio writes in a statement. "With core game content now complete, we're dedicating our full focus to polishing and optimizing the experience so that when you step into Showa American Story, it feels cohesive, responsive, and complete. Thank you for your continued support, enthusiasm, and patience along the way. We can't wait to welcome you on October 28, 2027."
