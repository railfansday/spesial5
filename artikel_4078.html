Lords Of The Fallen 2 Launches April 2027, Final Date Coming Next Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Publisher CI Games has announced that Lords of the Fallen 2 will launch in April 2027.

This comes via a social media post from founder and CEO Marek Tyminski (thanks, Gematsu), who simply said "Lords of the Fallen II: April 2027. Exact date in December". So yes, we'll keep an eye out for a further update and let you know when exactly you can expect the game to drop.

The game was previously delayed into 'Q1 2027' earlier this year, with Tyminski explaining that the stacked 2026 schedule – which includes Zelda: Ocarina of Time and a little game called GTA 6 – led to a desire to give Lords of the Fallen 2 a bit of space to shine.

"This updated release window also strategically positions Lords of the Fallen II outside of a highly competitive holiday period, ensuring the game receives the dedicated attention it deserves."

It's honestly not a bad choice at all, and we're thankful to see at least one game escape the avalanche of releases in 2026. Hell, we've still got a bunch of big-hitters coming in December, including Rayman Legends Retold, Monster Hunter Wilds, Dragon Quest Monsters: The Withered World, and Professor Layton and The New World of Steam.
