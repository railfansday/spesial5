Mouse Work, The Switch 2 Game Using Joy-Con Mouse Controls, Arrives This December

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Update [Tue 10th Nov 2026, 11:35pm]: Nitrome has now officially announced Mouse Work will be arriving for Switch 2 on 7th December 2026.

It's described as the ultimate showcase for the system's mouse controls, and will include a "massive 8-10 hour campaign solo" or you can bring a crew with you for a co-op experience. This includes 1-4 players locally and online via GameShare.

This title will set you back $19.99 / £15.99 and pre-orders are now live on the eShop. Nitrome has also shared a new trailer, which you can check out above.

Original Story: Remember Mouse Work? It was announced by developer Nitrome for the Switch 2 just a few months before the system's launch and requires you to use mouse controls to complete all sorts of fun and challenging jobs.

We've not got an update about this title since last year, but it looks like it's finally on the way. According to a listing that has recently appeared on the Japanese Nintendo Store and eShop, it will be arriving this holiday season on 7th December 2026 for 2,200 yen.

The translated description notes how the campaign is 10 hours across more than 50 unique stages. You'll also be able to participate in local and online multiplayer, including GameShare support. When we hear more about the local release, we'll provide an update.

Nitrome has previously released games such as Bomb Chicken and Shovel Knight Dig on the original Switch. Are you excited for its new game on Switch 2? Let us know in the comments.
