Kim Kardashian Rocks Plunging Leather at F1 Academy Pre-Lewis Hamilton Race

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Kim Kardashian’s paddock style is already unmatched.

Kardashian, 46, rocked a plunging, fitted black leather long-sleeved blouse with coordinating skintight pants to the F1 Academy finale on Saturday, November 21.

F1 Academy is Formula One’s all-women racing series, launched in 2023 to help prepare aspiring female drivers to move up the ranks in the high-speed sport. Kardashian attended alongside managing director Susie Wolff and special guest Venus Williams, who had the honor of waving the checkered flag at the race’s starting line.

One day earlier, the Kardashians star attended the F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix qualifying session on Friday, November 20, in a fitted red leather bodysuit in support of her boyfriend, Lewis Hamilton.

Kardashian has been a fixture in the Ferrari garage since she started dating longtime friend Hamilton, 41, earlier this year.

“I was really single for five years, and that felt great,” she told The Times of London in an October profile, making a rare comment about her dating status before finding love with Hamilton. “I could focus on my kids and projects that I really wanted to be involved with. I really felt no rush. Like, I really didn’t feel empty.”

Kardashian, who shares four kids with ex-husband Kanye West, explained how she wanted to “completely heal” before diving into a new relationship. (Following her divorce, the Skims mogul was briefly romantically linked to Pete Davidson and Odell Beckham Jr., respectively.)

“I felt like I needed my time,” she noted to The Times. “I wasn’t ready to open up. I really wasn’t even thinking about it. The universe works in your favor when you just allow it to.”

Kardashian, however, has declined to speak about her romance with the British racer at length.

“In the most respectful way, I’d probably rather not,” she quipped to the outlet when pressed for further details about her blossoming romance.

Even so, Kardashian and Hamilton’s romance has continued to heat up.

“They are really in love. Kim may want to have more kids with Lewis,” a source exclusively told Us in August. “They just get each other, they get each other’s quirks. She is really happy with him.”
