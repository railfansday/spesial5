A pre-start version guard for self-hosted services (after the LNbits 1.7.0 advisory)

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

On 8 November the LNbits team told operators to shut their servers down after attackers created unauthorized accounts and withdrew funds. One operator reported losing 2,285,545 sats. On 10 November, version 1.7.0 shipped as an urgent security update, with a clear instruction: upgrade before you restart.

That instruction is easy to break by accident. A systemd unit restarts after a reboot, a container comes back with an old image, and the vulnerable version is serving requests again before anyone checks.

Here is a small guard you can put in front of any self-hosted service: it reads the installed version, compares it with a minimum you set, and exits non-zero if the version is too old, so the service never starts.

If ExecStartPre fails, systemd doesn't run ExecStart. The unit fails, and you see it in systemctl status and your alerts.

Run it from cron and diff the output against your pinned minimum. A change means it's time to read the release notes.
