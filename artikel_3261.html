Apple releases first iOS 27.3 public beta with redesigned Health app and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Apple has released the first iOS 27.3 public beta for iPhone. The update brings back the redesigned Health app that briefly appeared during iOS 27.2 testing. It also adds vertical tabs in Safari, gives Siri AI more to do across Apple’s apps, and makes the iPhone Duo easier to use with one hand.

Apple released the first iOS 27.3 developer beta on November 3. The biggest change is the redesigned Health app, which didn’t make it into the final iOS 27.2 release.

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, the new Insights tab brings together trends from sleep, activity, and more. The Longevity tab includes Health Age, which uses Apple Watch data to show how your health metrics compare with what’s typical for your age.

For more changes, check out our full iOS 27.3 beta 1 roundup.

Back up your iPhone, then sign up for the free Apple Beta Software Program with the same Apple Account you use on your iPhone.

Go to Settings > General > Software Update > Beta Updates and select iOS 27 Public Beta. Return to Software Update and tap Update Now when iOS 27.3 appears. If you’re already on the public beta track, you can check for the update directly in Software Update.
