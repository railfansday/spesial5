Warwick Thornton, Teona Strugar Mitevska & Myroslav Slaboshpytskiy Set For Rotterdam’s Cinemart

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The International Film Festival Rotterdam (IFFR) Pro has unveiled the 20 projects selected for the 44th edition of its co-financing platform CineMart as well as work-in-progress showcase Darkroom and the Safe Harbour stand, for filmmakers who have experienced forced migration.

All three strands fall under the umbrella of IFFR’s Co-Production market running from January 31 to February 3, 2027.

The Cinemart selection includes upcoming films by Warwick Thornton (The New Boy), Teona Strugar Mitevska (Mother), Myroslav Slaboshpytskiy (The Tribe), Damien Hauser (Memory of Princess Mumbi) and Guido Hendrikx (Stranger in Paris). Dedicated to projects by filmmakers who have presented work in the Tiger Competition or Bright Future in the last 10 years, Darkroom will showcase ten projects looking for completion funding, sales agents and festival selections. The projects are also in the running for IFFR Pro Awards. Watch on Deadline

They include working titled Tahmina by Aboozar Amini, who previously made waves with Kabul, City in the Wind, and Elisa’s Miller Legítima about a rape victim charged with murder after she kills one of the attackers in self-defence. Nicolás Celis who took credits on Emilia Pérez is producing

“From contemporary reinventions of the Western and working-class musical fables to personal and urgent stories of displacement and identity, this year’s CineMart, Darkroom and Safe Harbour selections unites an extraordinary range of cinematic voices – filmmakers reclaiming histories, subverting familiar genres and finding fresh ways to tell stories about our world,” said Marten Rabarts, Head of IFFR Pro.

“That we can bring together celebrated auteurs and festival alumni working in independent cinema with filmmakers embarking on their first features is something quintessentially Rotterdam. It is the strength, relevance and originality of the cinematic vision, rather than the stage of a filmmaker’s career, that draws us to a project,” he added.

A number of films presented at CineMart or Darkroom are also confirmed for the upcoming edition of the festival. Four Seasons In Java by Kamila Andini and Elephants in the Fog by Abinash Bikram Shah will play in the Limelight section. Kaktarua by Yudhajit Basu and Prithvijoy Ganguly will world premiere in the Bright Future lineup. El Mundo es Nuestro by Francisco Márquez and The Station by Sara Ishaq will screen in the Harbour sidebar.
