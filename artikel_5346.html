FF EAI Robotics Ecosystem (FFR) Officially Launches "High Performance, Surprisingly Affordable" Black Friday Sale with Discounts of Up to 50% Across the Robot World Product Lineup

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

(NASDAQ: FFR) ("FFR" or the "Company"), a U.S.-based Embodied AI robotics company, kicked off its Black Friday Season of Savings, offering discounts of up to approximately 50% on select robots, additional post-purchase cash-back rebates on five models ordered through FF Mall, and a free developer toolkit valued at $390 with select robots. The promotion spans multiple products from FFR's lineup including humanoid, quadruped and wheeled-arm platforms, with options for families, educators, developers, researchers, and enterprise customers.

From a first hands-on robotics project to advanced research and industrial applications, the campaign offers more ways to explore Physical AI at a lower cost. Customers can choose compact robots for coding and creative learning, humanoids for development and competition, or professional platforms for security, inspection and manipulation projects.

About FF EAI Robotics Ecosystem Inc. FF EAI Robotics Ecosystem Inc. (NASDAQ: FFR) is a U.S.-based Embodied AI robotics company focused on the research and development, manufacturing, commercialization and deployment of intelligent robotic technologies, products and industry solutions. FFR is building a "Four-Core Full-Stack" AI ecosystem covering the full lifecycle of robotics, consisting of Physical AI Brain & Developer Platform, Physical AI Devices, Physical AI Industry Productivity Solutions, and Physical AI Data Factory. Guided by the technology and product philosophy of "One Brain, All Robots, Multi-capabilities," FFR aims to empower humanoid, biomimetic, and other robotic form factors through a unified Physical AI Brain, while continuously expanding their multi-task and multi-scenario capabilities. The ecosystem is designed to support the full robotics lifecycle, including R&D, deployment, data collection and training, operations, and commercial applications. FFR also operates RoboShare, a robot-sharing and services platform. To learn more, visit https://www.ff.com.
