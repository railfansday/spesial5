Now entering the debate on whether Bruno Guimaraes is returning Toon hero or Toon turncoat

I have been reading lately, with interest, the conversations surrounding the return of Bruno Guimaraes to St James’ Park this coming weekend (Saturday 21st November)

Opinion seems to be split as to what kind of reception our former captain should receive if he is selected to face us on Saturday evening.

Since leaving Newcastle United, Bruno Guimaraes hasn’t been an automatic starter, so we may not have the chance to show him our collective feelings either way.

From what I am reading the camp seems to be split directly down the middle between “Bruno Guimaraes is a hero who led us to our first silverware in decades”, to “Bruno Guimaraes is a traitor who went all out to manufacture a move away from the club.”

Looking back now at the summer dealings it’s easier to reflect on what happened and why the players who left, did.

In my opinion , it was only a matter of time before Sandro left us, his heart never seemed in the Northeast, neither does it in North London to be honest, the only time I think we will see him truly happy is when he is plying his trade back in Italy, unfortunately for him it doesn’t look as if Italian football could subsidise that kind of financial arrangement.

Again, in my opinion the same can be said about Anthony Gordon, I liked the lad but also got the impression he thought he was destined for a bigger club, something which was proven to be true.

I just got the impression he thought he was too big a name for the club and it was a matter of time before he left. The fact he went abroad seemed to make the move slightly more palatable as a Newcastle fan.

And on to Bruno, I love(d) the bloke, I was stood in the away section of the Stadium of light, with 7,000 others, 3-0 up and watched him slide tackle on our own goal line and celebrate as if he had scored.

I loved his passion and enthusiasm for the game and the Toon. He could drive and win a game single handedly by grabbing the team by the scruff of the neck and pushing the team forward.

I loved his speech at the presentation in Leases Park and the way he appreciated the character of the Northeast.

Will I boo him if he plays on Saturday? 100%. This is not out of spite; I take this decision more so on a few factors.

Bruno Guimaraes is a confidence player; we have seen this ourselves over the years. We know he feels bad about leaving, he has made a few statements leading up to the game about the toughest decision he has had to make-blah blah blah etc.

But, he went out of his way to hire a super-agent in Kia Joorabchian to help get a move away. Not the actions of a player in love with the glorious Northeast.

He had opportunities to soften the blow leaving, many times over the course of the 2026 World Cup, but chose the cowards way out with total silence, despite the obvious affect it would have on the fans

Let’s get at him, rattle him, affect his confidence, and take the roof off every time Dan Burn cuts him in half, maybe that will be enough to unsettle him and his teammates.

Newcastle United are in a transitional period as a team, but I still feel on a Saturday evening kick off, with a crowd fuelled with day drinking we can beat anyone..

Maybe spare applause for Bruno Guimaraes at the end for his service to the club, I will.
