How much money Wayne Rooney will earn for I'm A Celebrity appearance

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Former Derby County, Plymouth Argyle, and Birmingham City manager Wayne Rooney is entering the I'm A Celebrity jungle, with details emerging over how much he will be paid.

Wayne Rooney has not held a managerial position since leaving Plymouth Argyle in December 2024, following a disappointing seven-month spell in which he oversaw just four Championship victories in 23 league matches.

The former England captain had previously endured difficult spells at Birmingham City and D.C. United, with his managerial career failing to replicate his playing achievements thus far, albeit there were some encouraging signs when he was coaching Derby County.

He has since spent considerable time working in television since that Plymouth role, becoming a pundit for the BBC's Match of the Day and making appearances on Amazon Prime Video's football coverage frequently as well.

However, on Monday, he will be in the jungle alongside Ade Adepitan, AJ Odudu. Chloe Burrows, Claire Sweeney, Jack Skipper, Joanna Page, Leigh Francis, Leomie Anderson, Mariella Frostrup, Ricky Wilson, and Sharky.

The show will run from Monday, 16 November and will last around three weeks. However, Rooney is arguably the standout name in the lineup. The former England and Man United captain is a legend of English football, following the likes of Ian Wright, Jimmy Bullard, and Wayne Bridge to enter the jungle.

According to SuperTV247 on X, as relayed by GiveMeSport, Rooney will be paid £800,000 for his appearance on the show. That is considerably more than any other guest, with artist Ricky Wilson earning £175,000 to be present in Australia.

There may be some viewers who question why Rooney is reportedly earning so much more than his fellow contestants, with reports suggesting his fee dwarfs the amount offered to the next highest-paid star.
