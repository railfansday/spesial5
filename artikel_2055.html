3 in, 3 out: How Birmingham City's dream January transfer window looks

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Birmingham City are flying high in the Championship at present, with Chris Davies' side leading the division across the November international break.

Blues are ahead of Middlesbrough and Stoke City as a result of their superior goal difference after 15 matches, with it now remaining to be seen if they can match or better such performance levels.

Such feats come after an interesting summer of activity, during which fewer additions and sales were made at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park than in years gone by, whilst newly-appointed director of football, John Murtough, is set to oversee his first recruitment drive.

With that said, Football League World have looked at what an ideal January window could look like for Birmingham amid their promotion hopes.

Getting things underway would be an attempted pursuit for FC Lorient's Arthur Avom, with FLW exclusively revealing Birmingham's interest in the 21-year-old alongside that of Middlesbrough.

Despite being relatively young, Avom's impressive performances for the French side have attracted the attention of several Premier League sides, as well as other top-flight sides across Europe, with valuations as high as £17.8m touted in the meantime.

He hasn't let such speculation impact his early-season performances for Lorient either, and with his contract expiring next June, Birmingham could eye a potential cut-price deal for his services, should Avom's current employers not seek to tie him down to fresh terms in the coming weeks.

Another cut-price deal which may arise for Davies and his recruitment team is one for Canadian international, Liam Millar, who has certainly made his mark already at St. Andrew's on loan from Hull City.

Blues fended off interest from the two sides directly below them in the table to land the winger's services for the season, and after netting for the Tigers in the Premier League against Coventry, Millar has since produced four goals and three assists in his 11 Championship appearances so far this term.

Those in East Yorkshire have also been left confused by the 27-year-old's recent admissions in interviews, and whilst Hull have the option to extend his contract until June 2028, it could make sense for all parties to wrap up a permanent deal as early as January.

Birmingham's two main attackers, August Priske and Jay Stansfield, have caused plenty of havoc for defences so far this season, but there are question marks beyond the duo.

Having been linked with a return last January, as well as being the subject of interest from local rivals, Wolves, in the summer, Birmingham could explore a reunion with Scotland international, Che Adams, seven years after his departure for Southampton.

However, how feasible this deal would be remains to be seen, as the experienced striker is now in his third season with Torino, and has already scored four goals in his first nine Serie A outings this season under Ignazio Abate.

Middlesbrough's Kim Hellberg and Swansea City's Vitor Matos are on the radar of Crystal Palace, which could be good news for their promotion rivals.

Lee Buchanan has become rather forgotten about at St. Andrew's, with the last two years being a tale of injury misfortune for the Birmingham defender.

The 25-year-old hasn't featured competitively for 23 months, with a serious knee injury followed by further setbacks, and he is now firmly behind Alex Cochrane and Kristoffer Lund in the pecking order.

Buchanan attracted interest from Luton Town and Reading in the summer, and it would be no surprise to see transfer links surround him in January, with it appearing that an exit could be the best decision for his long-term career.

Marvin Ducksch's immediate future at St. Andrew's has been up in the air for some time, and it wouldn't be the greatest of surprises to see last summer's £1.75m arrival find a new club this winter.

Ducksch hit double-figures last season, but after being charged for causing a three-vehicle collision in April, the two-time German international has been the subject of heavy exit talk.

Indeed, it was suggested that the forward came mightily close to joining Valencia on Deadline Day before a deal between the two clubs collapsed, and since then, he has started in none of his nine Championship appearances so far this season.

Scott Wright was recently inserted back into Birmingham's 25-man squad following Kanya Fujimoto's loan move to Tokyo Verdy, but a swift departure for the 29-year-old winger also seems inevitable.

After featuring heavily in all competitions two seasons ago after signing from Rangers, Wright suffered a serious injury against Reading last February, and only made a full recovery in the latter weeks of 2025.

Since then, an underwhelming loan with Dundee FC and limited minutes with Blues have followed, meaning that if the Scottish youth international wants to source regular minutes, it would be elsewhere rather than being behind Carlos Vicente and Patrick Roberts in Davies' pecking order.
