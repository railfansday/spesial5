Parker’s vote of confidence designed to calm London Stadium audience | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

After a two week break it is wonderful to be getting back to the serious business of Scott Parker trying to reinvigorate this West Ham squad after just over s year of Nuno Espírito Santo, relegation and all.

Few West Ham United fans will feel uplifted tomorrow if the team sheet reveals an unchanged goal keeper-  after Mads Hermansen has had such a public all from grace and been the subject of booing by his own fans -twelve goals in four games is a pretty big condemnation of the ‘keeper or his defence. or both.

However without the option of starting Alphonse Areola tomorrow who is still not fit, Parker has come out with words designed to calm Hermansen’s fragile confidence and the mood of supporters: Whilst admitting there are problems with the Dane’s game, West Ham’s manager has given Hermansen his backing.

Mixed fortunes for Denmark means Hermansen’s confidence is probably still fragile

In truth, unless he’s prepared to bench the Dane in favour of twenty year old Finn Herrick, Hermansen it will have to be:

“There’s a lot going on, we can’t deny that, and I can’t sit here and say anything different. He’s an honest boy [Mads Hermansen] who works tirelessly on his game.

He’s made a couple of mistakes, or he’s just not quite at it, but, as I said, he has mine and our support.” (whufc.com)

Few of the 60,000 supporters will relish another 90 minutes with Hermansen between the posts : The last thing he or the team need is anther outbreak of home fan booing.
