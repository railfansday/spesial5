Get the Business Advantage Customized Cash Rewards Mastercard® Credit Card if one of its six 3% categories, like gas or travel, takes more than a quarter of your business’s card spending. It pays 3% there on up to $50,000 a year with no annual fee, and members of Bank of America’s Preferred Rewards for Business earn up to 75% more.

It’s a business card, but a freelancer or side business owner can apply as a sole proprietor with a Social Security number instead of a business tax ID. Whoever applies, as the owner or an authorized officer, is personally liable for the balance along with the business.

By our math, owners who put more than a quarter of their spending, up to $50,000 a year, in one of its categories earn more here than on a flat 1.5% card, and Preferred Rewards members gain the most. Owners whose spending is spread evenly do better with the flat 1.5% Business Advantage Unlimited Cash Rewards, and those who want travel points with the Business Advantage Travel Rewards.
