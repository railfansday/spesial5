Forget 'Bosch,' Netflix's 5-Part Detective Drama Is a Perfect Binge

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Prime Video's Bosch was one of the most exciting police procedurals on television. For seven seasons, from 2014 to 2021, we got to follow along with Los Angeles Police Department Detective Harry Bosch (Titus Welliver) as he investigated some seriously fascinating cases. The series has inspired two spin-off shows, including the three-season Bosch: Legacy and Ballard starring Maggie Q. MGM+ will be capitalizing on Bosch's devoted fanbase with another spin-off, a prequel called Bosch: Start of Watch, which is set to arrive in January. If you're looking for more in the vein of Bosch, there's another compelling detective drama, and it's one you might not even know about.

There have been a lot of cop shows in the history of television. But few of them have been as well-made as Southland. The underrated series, which ran for five seasons from 2009 to 2013, is a captivating look at law enforcement in Los Angeles. The drama, created by Ann Biderman (Ray Donovan), is a brutally realistic portrayal of the many complicated aspects of working in the LAPD. We gain entry into this high-stakes world by following the story of several LAPD officers, including rookie Officer Ben Sherman (Ben McKenzie). Ben is brand-new to the force, and is completely in the dark about the danger he's just walked into. His training officer, John Cooper (Michael Cudlitz), tries to show him the ropes, but as a closeted gay man, John is desperate to keep his personal life hidden from his co-workers. We also meet two inspirational women in the LAPD: Detective Lydia Adams (Regina King, pre-Oscar win) and Officer Chickie Brown (Arija Bareikis), who are both facing an uphill battle to make their way in a male-dominated field. Each character provides valuable insight into the complex life of these public servants.

Unlike some other cop shows, Southland creates an altogether authentic picture of the LAPD. There are the typical car chases and fight sequences, but these moments of high-intensity action serve the storytelling in a realistic way. The drama explores everything from gang violence and corrupt cops to the somber mental health and addiction struggles the officers face. The gritty style of Southland ensures that none of the storylines ever feel cheesy or predictable. The series also digs into the lives of these characters, so that they become fully dimensional people. The writing allows the viewer to feel the depth of their sorrow and traumas, as well as to celebrate some of their wins. It's easy to feel drawn into the world of Southland from the very first scene, which means the series is perfect for a binge-watch as you'll want to zoom ahead to catch every twisty episode.

Before he was Dr. Abbot, Shawn Hatosy was Detective Sammy Bryant.

The writing in Southland ensures there are zero bad episodes, but one of the most remarkable elements of the series is its extraordinary cast. McKenzie is spot-on as the naive Ben, and his scenes with Cudlitz crackle with tense electricity. King is a perfect addition to the cast, and her performance as Lydia provides a certain grounded feeling to the series. Shawn Hatosy, who recently won an Emmy for his work in The Pitt, brings a necessary frantic energy to his role as Sammy Bryant, an officer who can't seem to get out of his own way. C. Thomas Howell is also a bright spot in the cast as Bill "Dewey" Dudek. Howell provides much of the comic relief for the otherwise tonally dark series. In Season 4, Lucy Liu joined the cast as Officer Jessica Tang, delivering a riveting performance. The entire cast serves as a strong foundation that allows the storytelling to flourish, even when the subject matter is incredibly heavy.

For a while, it seemed like Southland would have a short run. Despite a second season renewal, NBC decided to cancel the show after Season 1. Luckily, TNT swooped in, and aired several more seasons. In 2013, TNT announced that Southland would be cancelled after five seasons, with no reprise in sight. Despite the fact that the show could have carried on for much longer, there are still 43 brilliant episodes for you to binge as a Bosch replacement.

Southland is available to stream on Netflix in the U.S.
