Travis Kelce Details Perfect Christmas Day W/ Taylor Swift

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Travis Kelce needs an Advent calendar, 'cause he's counting down the days to his first Christmas as a married man ... and describing what his perfect day looks like!

Spoiler alert: it includes Mom Donna Kelce in the kitchen, baking up her blueberry muffins.

The 37-year-old Chiefs tight end laid it all out in a Tommy Hilfiger ad for their winter campaign, "The Perfect Holiday," which dropped Thursday.

And this year's a big one for the future Hall of Famer ... 'cause Kelce says he's "excited for my first holiday as a married man!"

So, what does the ideal day look like for him? Time with the family, and then a Christmas Day night game with the Chiefs ... oh, and he wants a Tommy onesie!

Lucky for TK, his wife loves Christmas, too! Taylor Swift grew up on a Christmas tree farm in Pennsylvania. Back in 2013, she said that's why she's "obsessed with Christmas" and wishes it lasted all year, later turning the whole experience into a holiday song, "Christmas Tree Farm."

Start preheating that oven, Donna ... it's going to be a VERY Merry Christmas in the Kelce-Swift house!
