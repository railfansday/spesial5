Inde Navarrette Credits Actresses Who Gave Her ‘Permission to Be Free’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

For ELLE Women in Hollywood honoree Inde Navarrette, playing Nikki in Obsession was about far more than landing her first leading role. In her acceptance speech at our annual event at the Four Seasons Hotel Los Angeles at Beverly Hills last night, the actress spoke about putting everything that she had into playing the character. “I needed to break through every glass ceiling either school, peers, society, or even I put on myself,” she said. “I needed to be connected with that rage that I used to find solace in. I needed to be reminded that my voice, whether soft or loud, holds weight and creates tension that I get to be in control of. I can play a girl who has an internal world—crazy!—whom is the lead of a film and whom is not the perfect victim. Nikki was my opportunity to show the world what I knew I could do and to that, I have to say thank you.”

She went on to thank Obsession’s writer and director Curry Barker, along with all actresses who inspired her early on (including Bryce Dallas Howard, who presented Navarrette with the award). “Thank you not only to Curry Barker for writing a role that allowed me that chance, but thank you to all of the women that took that chance before me,” she said. “Thank you to Jessica Lange for showing me and all of her work that there is beauty in madness and to just give it to the world. Thank you to Natalie Portman for Léon: The Professional. It was the first time I got to see a kid in a movie being more than a kid. She was me. Thank you to Sally Field and Steel Magnolias for daring to portray grief honestly and the hardships of motherhood gracefully. Thank you, Bryce Dallas Howard and The Help, for portraying how intersectional womanhood can divide us and that women who look different than ourselves are no different than ourselves. Thank you to Florence Pugh, Carrie Coon, Julianne Moore, Elizabeth Debicki, Samantha Morton, Rosalind Eleazar, Penélope Cruz. Thank you for daring to be wild. Thank you. Incredibly, you have all given me permission to be free. Let’s all shine a light in each other.”
