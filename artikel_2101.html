Newcastle boss Matthias Jaissle could be first Premier League manager sacked - Sources

Newcastle United boss Matthias Jaissle could become the first Premier League manager to be sacked this season.

The 38-year-old has come under intense pressue at St James' Park and has now lost the backing of large sections of the fans after a poor run of form across all competitions.

Other bosses in the frame to be sacked in the coming weeks include Crystal Palace's Pierre Sage, with the Eagles already believed to be weighing up his future.

However, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Sage has an advantage over Jaissle as he has retained the support of his players.

Crystal Palace captain Dean Henderson spoke ahead of their 1-1 draw against Hoffenheim, as he insisted the players are looking internally and still back the manager.

At Newcastle, however, recent reports elsewhere have suggested there is discontent in the dressing room which has piled the pressure on Jaissle and could force a decision.

Football Insider revealed on 23 October that Newcastle are not planning to sack Jaissle in the near future, and hope to give the manager time to turn things around.

However, the Magpies have won just three of their last nine games across all competitions and have sunk to 13th place in the Premier League, adding to the doubts over his role.

With growing discontent in the crowd and allegedly in the dressing room, Jaissle may not have long left to reverse Newcastle's fortunes, and could find himself out of a job.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "There's now reports coming out of Newcastle from elsewhere, suggesting that the players aren't particularly happy.

"There does seem to be a few different issues there. It's interesting when you compare that to other managers who may be under pressure.

"Especially Pierre Sage, let's use him as an example, Dean Henderson came out and said the players are still behind him, they like what Sage is doing, and they have to look at themselves.

"That does feel like a big contrast to the noise that seems to be coming out of Newcastle at the moment, where there have been suggestions that those players aren't behind Jaissle.

"At the moment it is all sort of rumours and speculation, but it's interesting to see the difference there when we're looking at which Premier League manager could be sacked first.

"It seems that, at least in that respect, Sage has a bit of an advantage that Jaissle may not have at Newcastle."

Despite the pressure on Jaissle's position at Newcastle, it does seem Sage is the most likely candidate to be the first manager sacked in the Premier League this season.

Similar to the Magpies, large sections of the supporters have grown frustrated with the boss, and recent results have done little to ease the pressure on his position.

Former Man United coach Mick Brown told Football Insider on 2 November that Sage still has the support of his Palace squad, but that may not be enough to save him.

However, those players have now been called upon to step up on the pitch, with many having let Sage down and contributed directly to the pressure via their performances.

Crystal Palace have won just one of their last seven games across all competitions, and have done little to resolve their clear weaknesses, raising fresh doubts.
