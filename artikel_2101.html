Norwich City are sitting on a cash goldmine with 'future Harry Kane'

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

For Norwich City, it will feel as though the international break came at the completely wrong time as Philippe Clement's side began to hit their stride in the Championship.

Although nowhere near the levels of last season's dire start under Liam Manning, the Belgian was still the subject of intense criticism before the start of a current four-game winning run in which 15 goals have been netted.

The Canaries are now 12th in the table as a result, just four places and three points behind the expanded play-off places, which is exactly where many outsiders predicted them to finish in pre-season.

The experienced head coach has battled against many injuries to key players, but the aforementioned run to form has come slightly after Jovon Makama's return to full fitness following two frustrating setbacks.

In a short sample size of minutes, the 22-year-old has reminded everyone of his qualities, and it has drawn particularly high praise from a Carrow Road legend.

Makama was one of several summer arrivals during Manning's only transfer window in Norfolk, penning a five-year contract after a £1.2m deal was struck with Lincoln City.

Having amassed 15 goal contributions for the Imps in 2025/26, many were intrigued to see how the attacker fared in the Championship, although, like many of his teammates, it took until Clement's arrival for his season to kickstart.

Despite netting three times under the Belgian's predecessor, Makama's first purple patch at Carrow Road came between November 2025 and January 2026, in which he netted eight times in 10 Championship outings, with Norwich winning four of those encounters.

Norwich won three of the following four games with Makama as one of their attacking focal points, but it then transpired that the highly-rated forward would miss the remainder of the season.

One of two foot injuries was the root cause of his absence, which has since led to Clement adopting a cautious outlook on the forward's minutes at the beginning of the current campaign.

Philippe Clement has deployed Lucien Mahovo further forward, despite emerging as a left-back, which is a mirror image of Thomas Meunier's career

Makama's first game back after seven months injured came in the 2-1 victory over Birmingham City on September 9th, but just one start so far this season has limited his impact to just 347 minutes' worth of action.

Makama recently hailed Clement for the impact he has had on the striker's performances despite such injury woes, saying: “I think Philippe Clement coming in has helped me develop massively as well, and there’s still a lot more to come."

In those 10 appearances, the England youth international has scored his first three goals of the season, including a stunning effort in the 4-0 rout at Derby County on November 3rd, before also netting against Bristol City last time out.

As such, the striker was called up to Lee Carsley's England Under-21s squad for the first time for friendlies against Portugal and France, and whilst his involvement in said matches remains to be seen, it has seen the Nottingham native receive high praise indeed from Canaries cult hero, Iwan Roberts.

Discussing who could be in line to replace the Three Lions' all-time record goalscorer, appearance holder and Ballon d'Or winner, Harry Kane, the Welshman said: “But who's next on the conveyor belt? Who's next in line? Obviously, people are talking about Will Lankshear, after his £10m move to Middlesbrough. He got 13 goals this season and he's the name on the end of everybody's tongue at the minute.

“But don't forget Jovon Makama," Roberts stated.

“They're very different players, but I think Makama's got more of a presence about him."

Makama's return to fitness will certainly have been welcomed by all of a Norwich persuasion, not only due to his qualities but the unfortunate news that Mo Toure will be sidelined for some time after sustaining a shoulder injury.

The Australian international scored nine goals in his first 11 Championship appearances last season, many of which came during Makama's own time on the treatment table, as well as a further five in seven games this term.

Norwich have shown in recent games that goals can fly in from all angles, having scored 32 in 15 games overall, but the prospect of Toure and Makama fronting an attack when at their maximum capacity is a frightening prospect for opposing defenders to have to contend with.

Not only this, but given Makama still has three years left on his contract at the end of the current campaign, if the goals continue to flow, the Canaries will be aware of the potential cash windfall that could come their way in the near future.
