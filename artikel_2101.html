Crawley Town face January battle to keep star player with League One clubs interested

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Crawley Town signed Jonny Russell from Northern Irish outfit Glentoran in January, and he has been a bright spark in their tough start to the season. The Red Devils are currently rooted to the foot of the League Two table, but the 22-year-old has impressed.

Now, Pete O’Rourke states on X that Russell’s performances have drawn interest at a higher level with a number of unnamed League One teams interested in a potential winter move.

The Northern Ireland U21 international has been deployed at both centre-back and left-back this term with Kevin Blackwell’s arrival resulting in his upturn in form. Russell has been integral to Town’s defensive improvements, helping secure their first clean sheets of the season against Oldham Athletic and Barnet as the club aim to avoid relegation to non-league for the first time since 2011.

Russell was a relatively unknown quantity when he touched down in West Sussex at the beginning of the year. The move brought an end to his 11-year association with boyhood club Glentoran as he headed to the League Two strugglers.

The move was a gamble for Russell given his settled position in Glentoran’s side and Crawley Town’s precarious position towards the bottom of the table. However, the switch has ultimately paid off for all parties.

He played on 19 occasions as the Red Devils avoided the drop. Russell showed signs of inconsistency with a handful of mistakes, something which has been eradicated under Blackwell this term.

Russell has grown into his time with Crawley Town and it’s unsurprising teams higher up the pyramid are starting to take note. The 22-year-old ranks as one of the division’s best defenders with a 100% successful dribble rate and winning 4.76 of his aerial duels per 90 (FotMob).

Although Crawley Town have shown encouraging signs of shoring up at the back, with Russell at the heart of that. However, the precarious position they find themselves in could prompt them to cash in on their star defender in January, and he may be tempted by a move as well.

Town reportedly forked out £100,000 for his services, and they will undoubtedly make a profit.

If they were to hold on to Russell until the end of the season in the hope they retain their League Two status, they could risk losing a large sum of money in the summer if they drop down into the National League.

Russell has certainly proven his credentials in a struggling side so he would only benefit from better players around him, with a step-up to League One being the perfect platform to continue his development.

Nonetheless, his impact for Crawley Town cannot be overstated, and losing him in the upcoming transfer window would significantly hamper their survival hopes.
