Sources: Crystal Palace keen on Conor Gallagher reunion

Crystal Palace have joined a host of Premier League clubs keeping tabs on Conor Gallagher, sources have told Football Insider.

The 26-year-old's future at Tottenham has been called into question in recent weeks, with his recent reduction in playing time leading to claims he could be made available.

The midfielder has a number of admirers behind the scenes at Palace, and they could be tempted to reignite their interest as they eye further additions to their squad.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, the Eagles have been keen on a reunion with Gallagher since he left the club in 2022.

Leeds United and Everton are also believed to be strong admirers of the England international, and could be prepared to pounce if he is allowed to leave Spurs in January.

However, as things stand, Gallagher is unlikely to be allowed to leave Spurs as he remains a big part of Roberto De Zerbi's squad and his plans for the long-term future.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider before Gallagher's move to Tottenham last January that Crystal Palace were hopeful of agreeing a deal.

The Eagles eventually missed out on their former loanee, but remained keen amid a belief that the midfielder enjoyed the best spell of his career at Selhurst Park.

Palace could be prepared to reignite their interest if he becomes available at Tottenham, but are unlikely to be alone in their pursuit with other clubs eyeing his situation.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "It is interesting to see the clubs who have been linked with Conor Gallagher if he is allowed to leave Tottenham.

"I've heard Leeds and Everton both mentioned in the last couple of weeks, and I know that Crystal Palace are obviously long term admirers of him as well.

"I think probably still it would be fair to say that he played his best football during that loan spell at Palace from Chelsea a couple of years ago.

"They wanted to sign him before he made that move to Tottenham, so it would surprise me if that interest has gone away, because he is very highly thought of there.

"So it'll be interesting to see if any of those clubs do try and tempt him away. But like you said, it is a bit of a surprise because he does definitely seem to still have a role at Tottenham."

Speculation about Gallagher's long-term future at Tottenham has come after he was pushed down the pecking order by other options in De Zerbi's starting XI.

The midfielder has started just four games so far this season, including just one of Tottenham's last six Premier League games, alerting clubs to his potential availability.

However, former Everton CEO Keith Wyness told Football Insider that Gallagher is unlikely to make a fuss over his playing time, while Spurs are under no pressure to sell.

Everton, Leeds and Palace are among those clubs who have been placed on alert by his situation, however, and could look to tempt him away if he is made available.

For now, though, it appears Gallagher still has a role to play at Tottenham and is still regarded as part of De Zerbi's squad as they aim to push higher up the Premier League.
