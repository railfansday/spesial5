Eagles’ trade of star might not be their last: 3 more big names they could trade to shock NFL

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Eagles traded defensive tackle Jordan Davis on Monday night to the Jacksonville Jaguars for a 2027 first-round pick and a 2029 second-round pick.

Davis was not expected to be traded ahead of Tuesday's NFL trade deadline or at any point in the near future. The Eagles signed Davis to a three-year, $78 million extension in March after he had a breakout 2025 season.

The 2-7 Eagles sent a clear message to the rest of the NFL with this blockbuster move: They are rebuilding and could be open to trading star players for the right price.

The Eagles were expected to make minor moves, such as trading players on expiring contracts.

But the Davis trade changes the thinking. The Eagles could be tempted to trade another star player if they can land more first- and second-round picks.

The Eagles now possess two 2027 first-round picks and two 2028 first-round picks. They also have two second-round picks in 2028 and 2029.

The Eagles have the ammunition to build another Super Bowl contender. They could use these picks to land a new franchise quarterback or to acquire the next superstar player who wants out.

Here are three more surprising players the Eagles could trade to add to their draft capital, ranked from most likely to least likely. These are hypothetical scenarios, and none of them may happen.

Trading Smith would not be juicy, but the Eagles could receive a good draft pick for their fourth-year edge rusher.

Smith's tough and physical playing style gives the Eagles' defense an edge. He's not a flashy pass rusher, but he applies pressure by pushing the pocket. He's also a stout run defender.

Before the Eagles have to guarantee Smith's fifth-year option of $13.752 million next year, they could look to trade the 25-year-old pass rusher for a Day 2 pick. Super Bowl contenders always look to add more pass rushers around the trade deadline, and Smith could be one of the best options available.

The Eagles traded a 2026 third-round pick for current Panthers pass rusher Jaelan Phillips last November. They could receive a similar haul in return for Smith. Maybe they could even manage to get a second-round pick?

Baun could land the Eagles another first- or second-round pick.

Two years after earning his first All-Pro selection, Baun remains one of the NFL's best off-ball linebackers.

Baun has recorded 65 tackles and two sacks during the first nine weeks of the 2026 NFL season. He single-handedly wrecked the Commanders' offense in the Eagles' Week 1 victory, recording 12 tackles and locking up the middle of the field by providing elite coverage.

After the 2025 season, Baun agreed to a three-year, $51 million contract. That's a reasonable contract for the Eagles to move on from and for another team to take on.

Baun is an excellent player, but he turns 30 years old in December and could be exiting his prime in the next few years. By the time the Eagles are competing for another Super Bowl, he might not be elite anymore.

Still, it would take a significant return to make a deal worthwhile.

DeJean has not played at an elite level this season, but some NFL teams still might regard him as one of the NFL's best defensive backs. The Eagles still might think that, too.

DeJean earned a first-team All-Pro selection last season after a dominant second year.

He capped off an incredible rookie season in 2024 by snagging a pick-6 in the team's Super Bowl victory over the Kansas City Chiefs.
