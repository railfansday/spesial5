Trinity Rodman out to prove she is true NWSL MVP as Washington Spirit aim for other side of Champinship hump

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Trinity Rodman out to prove she is true NWSL MVP as Washington Spirit aim for other side of Champinship hump originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Trinity Rodman has been the face of the NWSL for nearly her entire professional career.

Drafted by the Washington Spirit as the youngest player in NWSL history in 2021, and signed as the highest-paid player in the league later that year, Rodman is used to being a trend-setter.

Now 24 years old and still thriving as one of the biggest — if not THE biggest — star on the U.S. women's national team, Rodman has led the Spirit to the 2026 NWSL Championship, hoping to secure their first league title since her rookie season.

Just a few days before the match, Rodman was in attendance at the NWSL postseason award gala, having been nominated for the league's MVP award.

"It would mean the world," Rodman said before the ceremony of potentially winning league MVP. "That's one that I've always wanted, I think everybody wants that one. It would mean a lot to me, and I would love for that to happen."

It didn't happen, as Rodman lost to a deserving Ashley Sanchez. The North Carolina Courage forward enjoyed a spectacular season, finishing as the NWSL leading goal scorer while equaling the league's single-season scoring record. While Rodman didn't win the award this year, she found the time and grace to record and post a TikTok dance with Sanchez, a close friend, proving she has no ill will towards the victor.

Sanchez absolutely put forth the best individual performance through the 2026 NWSL season, which MVP awards typically honor. But if you're looking for the true "most valuable player" in the NWSL, it's undoubtedly Rodman. With 12 goals and eight assists this season, her goal contributions were instrumental in the Spirit's push to the title match. Meanwhile, Sanchez's Courage didn't even qualify for the postseason.

This year, though, feels different. While other top NWSL teams such as Shield winners Gotham faded towards the end of the season due to injuries and dip in form, Rodman and the Spirit are peaking at the perfect time. After scoring in the regular season finale against basement-dwelling Chicago, Rodman has bagged two goals — a brace against Portland in the quarterfinals – and two assists in their two playoff games to this point.

"Going into [a championship game] not 100 percent isn't the greatest feeling because you know that you're still going to play, but you don't know if it's going to be a great performance," Rodman told reporters on Thursday at a pre-match event in D.C. "So to be healthy and to be able to give everything that I have is really exciting for me."

You see, Washington has made the title match in each of the last three seasons now, but they lost both of the previous two. Last season, Rodman suffered a back injury that ruined the second half of the campaign, and even though she came back to play in the final, she wasn't quite the same. In 2024, Rodman failed to register a goal or assist in the last seven games of the season, including any of their three playoff games, as the Spirit lost to Orlando.

"I think it's just like, full-circle moment obviously with it being in D.C." Rodman told ESPN's Futbol W show on Thursday. "just starting my pro career here and then re-signing to stay and play here, everything that I've been through just with the fans here, the community, everything, and how much the fan base has grown. To be able to do that in this city will be the most rewarding thing and very celebratory."

Rodman has already won an NWSL Championship, her very first year in the league, and she has already lost the 2026 NWSL MVP award. And yet, there is still so much on the line in Saturday's final. Winning the 2026 title would elevate Rodman's club resume from good to great, even without the individual honor.

Trinity Rodman doesn't need an NWSL MVP award to prove she's the league's most valuable player. She's done that all season long, and she can put on the finishing touches Saturday on her home turf, with a Championship win capping off a truly remarkable campaign and cementing Rodman as the biggest superstar the league has to offer.
