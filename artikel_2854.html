Brandon Ingram set to make Clippers season debut Monday against Rockets

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

An NBA All-Star is coming OFF the injured list. That is a refreshing change of pace.

Brandon Ingram will return to the Clippers on Monday night when they travel to face the Houston Rockets, a story broken by Clippers reporter Ohm Youngmisuk and ESPN's Shams Charania.

Ingram has been out since May, when he underwent heel surgery, which revealed a partially torn Achilles tendon. This wasn't a full-blown rupture, his return to the court was going to be quicker, but it did cause him to miss training camp and the start of the season.

Ingram had a bounce-back season in Toronto before his injury, averaging 21.5 points a game, shooting 38.2% from 3, grabbing 5.6 rebounds a night, and earning an All-Star selection. He was traded to the Clippers in September as part of the Kawhi Leonard deal.

Ingram should be a floor-raiser for a Clippers offense that is 23rd in the NBA this season — Ingram is a bucket-getter. Paired with point guard Darius Garland and with solid role players around them in Derrick Jones Jr., Rui Hachimura, Brook Lopez and Kris Dunn.

The Clippers also got center Yanic Konan Niederhauser back following foot surgery on Thursday. However, they remain without Bradley Beal (right knee injury management) and Jordan Miller (shoulder surgery), and there is no timeline for either of them to return.
