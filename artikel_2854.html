Corey Heim will drive No. 67 in 2027 NASCAR Cup season

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Until Tuesday, 23XI Racing's full-time lineup for 2027 was missing one thing -- the car number for Corey Heim.

When Heim was announced as Riley Herbst's replacement, the original plan was for him to take over the No. 35 car. Michael Jordan wore the number in his brief baseball career.

However, as a part-time driver in the No. 67, Heim managed to win two races, taking victories in the inaugural San Diego Street Course race as well as the Brickyard 400 at Indianapolis Motor Speedway.

When Heim won in San Diego, he became the first driver to ever win a Cup race in the No. 67, which was making its 650th appearance in a Cup Series race that weekend. Joe Harrison was the first to use the number at the Daytona Beach road course back in February, 1950.

Heim then collected a crown jewel victory, making more history as the first part-time driver to win multiple races in the same season since 1987. He was also the first driver to earn two Cup wins within their first 15 starts since 1965, the first part-timer to win a crown jewel race since 2011, and also the youngest Brickyard 400 winner since the inaugural running in 1994.

All of that success made people want to see more of the number, and of course, it was further fueled by the '6-7' meme as well. But there's also some great connective tissue between running the No. 23, No. 45, and No. 67 for a race team.

Well, 23XI has finally confirmed in a mock press conference that Heim will continue with the No. 67 into next season. The car number hasn't been used by a single driver on a (near) full-time basis since Buddy Arrington in 1987, though he only ran 20 of 29 races that year. He used the number more than any other driver, taking credit for nearly 500 of its 657 races run so far.
