If Brian Keefe were fired, who would be the Wizards’ interim head coach?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

In my poll last week, I asked what you think the Washington Wizards should do if they continue to play sub .500 basketball through December. A majority believe that head coach Brian Keefe should be fired.

That sounds easy: firing a head coach. And I get why. Washington is now 4-10 this season and on a seven game losing streak.

The harder part: who becomes the Wizards' interim head coach if they make that decision?

This is where we need to take a look at the Wizards' coaching staff. Ultimately, here are the names who stand out as possibilities:

Steve Clifford: Clifford was an NBA head coach for a decade from 2013-24 which include two stints with the Charlotte Bobcats/Hornets and the Orlando Magic. His cumulative record is 340-461. Clifford could be an interim right away should Washington make a mid year change. He is currently a coaching advisor for the Wizards.

Adam Caporn: He is the lead assistant coach for the Wizards. Caporn is also the head coach of the Australia men's national basketball team, the Boomers. He was the Long Island Nets head coach in the G League from 2021-22. If Washington is looking for a younger coach who also leads one of the world's best men's national teams, Caporn could be on that short list.

Cody Toppert: Toppert was the Capital City Go-Go's head coach and led them to three straight playoff runs in the G League.

Patrick Ewing: The former Georgetown men's basketball head coach spent six years in D.C., leading the Hoyas to the 2021 NCAA Tournament. However, the Hoyas weren't a Big East contender and he had a sub .500 record coaching them. Before returning to the Wizards for his second stint as an assistant coach, the Naismith Hall of Fame Center helped develop post players for multiple teams.

None of this implies the Wizards are about to fire Keefe. But I'll admit this team, after a solid start, has more talent on paper and is still winning games at a similar rate to… the last three years. It's one thing for the Wizards to be losing more than two out of three games. It's another matter when Anthony Davis and Trae Young are starting games and that record isn't much different than the last three years.
