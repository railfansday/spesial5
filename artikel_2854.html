Keys to the GameDefense – Defense is always the number one key to winning games. There is no truer statement than "Defense wins Championships." The Mavs have an offensive rating of 112.5 (16th) while the Celtics have an offensive rating of 117.4 (6th). The Mavs are 12th with a defensive rating of 111.7 while the Celtics are 2nd with a defensive rating of 105.6. When the Celtics struggle on offense, as they sometimes do, they have to be able to stop the other team from scoring. The Celtics especially need to defend the paint as the Mavericks average 48.3 points in the paint per game. The Celtics have to continue to make defense their identity.

Rebound – Second to defense, rebounding is the key to winning. One of the few times I have agreed with Pat Riley was when he said "No rebounds, no rings." The Celtics are averaging 44.8 rebounds per game (8th) while the Mavs are averaging 44.0 rebounds per game (15th). The Mavs are 6th with 16.5 second chance points per game. The Celtics need to put out extra effort on the boards to keep the Mavs from getting extra possessions and second chance points and to give the same to themselves.

Be Aggressive – The Celtics must be the more aggressive team. They have to be more aggressive in going to the basket, in diving for loose balls, and on defense. They have to aggressively crash the boards. They have to be the team that wants to win the game more. They absolutely can't let the Mavericks play harder than them if they want to win this game. Hard work beats talent when talent doesn?t work hard and when talent works hard, they are very hard to beat.

Move the Ball Carefully – The Celtics are at their best when the ball moves and they make the extra pass. When the ball sticks and players try to do too much, the Celtics struggle. But they have to make careful passes and not get sloppy because the Mavericks average 18.4 points off turnovers and 17.2 fast break points per game. The Celtics need to be careful with their passes and ball handling because if they aren't the Mavericks will make them pay.

They will have the distractions of travel and playing in front of a mostly hostile crowd,although I expect a lot of cheers for hometown Cooper Flagg. Fatigue could be a factor for the Mavs while the Celtics have had 3 days off and should be well rested. The Celtics need to get motivation from the home crowd and defend their home court with energy and effort.

Officiating – Officiating is always an x-factor in every game. Every crew officiates differently. Some call it tight, others let them play.
