ASU basketball sees stakes rise with LSU looming in Palm Springs

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Arizona State men's basketball team did what it set out to do by winning five straight games on its home floor to begin the season. Now coach Randy Bennett's team will try its hand on the road against power conference opponents.

The Sun Devils will compete in the Acrisure Series in Palm Desert, with two games against SEC foes. The games will be played at Acrisure Arena, which opened in 2022 and serves as home to the Coachella Valley Lakers, of the NBA's G League and the American Hockey League's Coachella Valley Firebirds.

First up for the Sun Devils is LSU (6-0). Then comes South Carolina (5-0), which will open the event against USC the same night ASU and LSU play. Most national projections had LSU and South Carolina in the bottom of the conference.

Time/Site: Noon, Nov. 25, Acrisure Arena, Palm Desert.

About the matchup: This will be the first game against a power conference opponent for both schools. The two have one common opponent, though. Both have played Louisiana-Monroe, with LSU beating that opponent 79-67 and ASU doing so 92-62. This will be the second meeting between the schools. Arizona State defeated LSU 71–52 on Nov. 27, 2009, in the Dick's Sporting Goods NIT Season Tip-Off in New York. LSU has been a team in the middle of controversy this season. Coach Will Wade brought in a player in R.J. Luis, who was in two NBA camps and actually appeared in three preseason games with the Celtics. Court debate over his eligibility followed. He got a preliminary injunction and played in a game on Nov. 18, getting 11 points and nine rebounds against Louisiana-Monroe. The school president issued an order to his coach not to play Luis, fearing retribution from the SEC. The player, through his lawyer, then came out with a statement saying he would not move forward as part of the team.

About the Sun Devils: The Sun Devils are coming off a 68-65 win over Oakland, marking the first time this season they had been challenged. The team was bolstered by the return of two players, who had been out with injuries in 6-11 senior forward Jonathan Griman and defensive stopper Emmanuel Innocenti, both of whom were pivotal in the Sun Devils rallying from a 14-point defecit last time out. Paulius Murauskas (26 ppg) ranks second in the nation in scoring and 23rd in rebounding (9.2 pg). Sophomore point guard Joel Foxwell is 16th in assists per game (6.8). As a team, the Sun Devils rank 16th in assist-to-turnover ratio. Sophomore Dillan Shaw (10 ppg, 9 rpg), who came to ASU from Saint Mary's along with Murauskas, is close to averaging a double-double.

About the Tigers: LSU hasn't made the postseason since 2022. Wade is back as head coach after spending 2017 to 2022 in Baton Rouge, where his teams were a combined 105-51. He was fired from the job after the NCAA formally served a notice of allegations based on reports of recruiting violations. Last season, he served as head coach at North Carolina State. The roster icludes two players who previously played at UCLA: Donovan Dent (15.3 ppg) and Skyy Clark. The sharp-shooter is 6-7 senior wing Abdi Bashir (13.8 ppg), a transfer from Kansas State who is 245-for-614 from 3-point range in three college seasons. LSU's best win this season was an 81-80 victory over Gonzaga.
