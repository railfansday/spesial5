Commanders sign DT Ricky Barber to their active roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Commanders have lost a pair of defensive tackles to season-ending injuries and they added one to help fill out the ranks on Wednesday.

They announced that they have signed defensive tackle Ricky Barber off of the practice squad. Javon Kinlaw and Daron Payne both landed on injured reserve due to season-ending injuries this week.

Barber signed with the Commanders as an undrafted free agent in 2025 and appeared in two games. He had five tackles and a sack in that action, but has not been elevated to the active roster yet this year.

The Commanders also announced that they have signed defensive tackle Daniel Ekuale to their practice squad.
