Sutton's predictions v world champion boxer Lauren Price

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Manchester United start life under new boss Mauricio Pochettino on Sunday but will they get their first Premier League win since August when they face Liverpool at Anfield?

"My worry for United is that they have one of the worst back fours in the Premier League," said BBC Sport football expert Chris Sutton.

"Their defensive record is something Pochettino will try to address but, whoever he picks, they are just not very good."

Sutton is making predictions for all 380 Premier League games this season, against AI, BBC Sport readers anad a variety of guests.

For week 11, he takes on boxer Lauren Price, who is an Arsenal fan.

She will defend her unified welterweight titles against Michaela Kotaskova on Friday, and has a shot at becoming Wales' first undisputed world champion after the vacant WBO welterweight title was added to the fight.

Price returns to home soil at the Utilita Arena in Cardiff for a fight that will be shown live on BBC Two and BBC iPlayer, from 21:00 GMT.

Price, the first Welsh boxer to be an amateur world champion and win Olympic gold, said: "When I turned pro it was my dream to become 'undisputed'. To achieve that and make history again for Wales on Friday, and in front of my own fans too, would be a dream come true."

Before turning to boxing, Price was a world kickboxing champion and international footballer - she captained the Wales Under-19 side, and played for the senior team as well as Cardiff City.

"My football days have helped me massively to get where I am in the ring today," the 32-year-old told BBC Sport.

"I honestly believe my footwork, and how fast my feet are, comes from when I played football.

"I am really proud of everything I achieved in football, especially playing for my country. It is the same now in boxing - to get in the ring and represent Wales is always an honour."

Price said the support of her grandparents helped her become successful at so many sports - and she followed her grandad, Derek, by becoming an Arsenal fan too.

"When I was a kid I was absolutely besotted with football and Arsenal were THE team when I was growing up," she explained.

"It was just the way they played under Arsene Wenger, and how they passed the ball - it was incredible. I loved Robin van Persie but Thierry Henry was my icon, my hero."

Chris Sutton and Lauren Price were speaking to BBC Sport's Chris Bevan.

The AI predictions were generated using Microsoft Copilot Chat - we simply asked the tool to 'predict this weekend's Premier League scores'.

The wheels have come off for Enzo Maresca at Manchester City with three Premier League defeats in a row.

It's not just City's results which have been alarming, either - they were well beaten by Nottingham Forest last time out, and their performance would have been the biggest concern for their manager.

In contrast, Fulham's results have improved of late and they are unbeaten in four league games. They've done all right whenever I've seen them this season, so I'm not surprised to see them picking up points.

Still, the international break probably came at a good time for Maresca to sort things out. Erling Haaland is scoring again too - not that he ever really stopped for very long - and I'm expecting City to be much better here.

I'm even going for them to keep a clean sheet, for the first time in eight games. I'm always brave... which is why I am bottom of the predictions table.

Lauren's prediction: City are having a tricky time of it at the moment but they should still win this. 2-0

I was at Sunderland's win over Chelsea and they thoroughly deserved it. They had been on a dreadful run of results before then, but it was a reminder of what they are about.

Brian Brobbey was just phenomenal for the Black Cats in that game but, like his captain Granit Xhaka, he is injured and will miss out here.

That just makes me think Aston Villa will have the edge. I always fancy them at home anyway with the way they go after teams, although I don't think this will be easy for them.

Villa boss Unai Emery was fuming after his side threw away a two-goal lead twice in their 4-4 draw at Manchester United, but they will hold on this time while Sunderland go down fighting.

Lauren's prediction: Villa are going really well. 2-1

Bournemouth have won eight games in a row in all competitions and it is incredible how Marco Rose has turned things around after a slow start.

The Cherries were absolutely flying before the international break - but will the two weeks off affect their momentum?

Forest did not need the break either, after their brilliant win over Manchester City.

That was such an important result for their manager Oliver Glasner but I think they will find it difficult to follow it up at Bournemouth.

Lauren's prediction: Forest shocked City last time out but I am going with Bournemouth here, they are on a brilliant run. 2-1

Chelsea were so lethargic when I watched them lose against Sunderland, and I don't think they were hard done by in that game - they looked flimsy at the back, and were deservedly beaten.

I had a bit of a pile-on from some Chelsea fans for saying that after the game, but then Xabi Alonso came out and was also heavily critical of the way they had defended - so he and I are on the same page there.

If Joao Pedro is fit then they will at least have more physicality up front this week, but I still have a feeling Leeds will get something out of this.

Daniel Farke's side got a great win over Tottenham last time out and they do not give much away - Arsenal and Brighton are the only top-flight teams to have conceded fewer league goals this season.

Leeds have definitely benefited from signing keeper James Trafford and what a move it has been for him personally as well.

I admire him for that, because he could have hung around at Manchester City as Gianluigi Donnarumma's number two, but he wanted first-team football. Now he is battling it out with Jordan Pickford to be England's number one.

Lauren's prediction: I don't think Leeds will keep Chelsea quiet. 3-1

This is the battle of the Franks... Lampard versus Thomas.

Thomas Frank's first conundrum as Crystal Palace's new manager is to stop them leaking so many goals - they have conceded 24 in their first 10 games, which is concerning.

I still think Palace have got some really good players, like Ismaila Sarr, Jorgen Strand Larsen and Jean-Philippe Mateta, and it's just a case of them getting fit and finding some form.

The way Coventry play, the Eagles are going to get some chances - but can they take them?

Frank Lampard's side counter-attack with such vigour, so I definitely think they will score... but I can see Palace's forward players having some joy too.

Lauren's prediction: I'm standing next to a Palace fan now, Ed from Boxxer, so I've got to go with them here! 0-2

We've seen Palace change their manager after making a disastrous start but they are still only four points below Ipswich and they will certainly see that as retrievable.

The Tractor Boys are not in the relegation zone but they are still in a relegation fight so, it is important for them to keep picking points up here and there. The trouble for them is that I don't see them getting anything at Tottenham.

Spurs lost to Leeds last time out but I still think they have turned a corner with their performances before that defeat.

I actually think this will be a routine win for Roberto de Zerbi's side, and I'm going for them to keep a clean sheet too.

Lauren's prediction: Spurs will take this one. 2-1

I am covering this game and I think it is a huge one for Newcastle boss Matthias Jaissle.

The Magpies have picked up as many points - 12 - in their first 10 games under Jaissle as they did when Eddie Howe was in charge last season, but I still don't think their fans are overly convinced by him.

Some of their unease is because Newcastle don't seem to have a defined style of play, and can be a bit kamikaze going forward.

Because of that, I just think Arsenal will pick them off, and win comfortably.

The Gunners have conceded only five league goals so far and have made an incredible start to their title defence with nine wins out of 10.

The worry for their rivals is that it feels to me Mikel Arteta's side have not even got going yet, but I still don't see anyone being close to them.

Lauren's prediction: No problems for Arsenal here! 0-4

Hull are going to scrap and fight - they have not won for eight games now, but they have still picked up some points because of their battling approach.

You know they will get in Brighton's faces but I don't think it will bother the Seagulls too much.

I want to apologise to Hull fans because I keep writing the Tigers off every week, but I am going to do it again here because Brighton are such a good team going forward and defensively.

Lauren's prediction: Brighton to edge this one. 0-1

Good luck to Pochettino. I like him as a manager and he seems a decent guy too.

He will need some luck as well, because it is going to be incredibly difficult for him to succeed at Old Trafford.

The constant for Manchester United is their managers keep getting the sack, when the club's problems seem to be deeper-lying than that.

Pochettino will get time because of what he has taken over, but it is amazing and also alarming to think United are in the bottom three, and could be bottom by the time this game is played.

As well as being so poor at the back, they have a midfield without any great athleticism and, stylistically, they did not really have an identity under Michael Carrick.

If you are Pochettino, you are probably going into the dressing room and putting your arm arm around Bruno Fernandes, to get the old sulker onside.

He really needs to, because Fernandes is an outstanding player and played a key role in the club's form with his numbers last season.

United need to see that version of him more than ever now, because so far this season he has not got going at all.

As for Liverpool, well I think they will get there under Andoni Iraola. It is just taking a bit of time for his players to adapt to his style.

They are not particularly fluid most of the time, and it feels like Iraola is not quite sure what his best midfield is.

Liverpool are still a work in progress, and United have picked up some results at Anfield recently - two draws and a win in their past three visits - but I can only see one result here, and it is a home victory.

Pochettino will probably look to defend and play on the counter-attack, and they might get some chances that way, but Liverpool are going to win. The only question is how many they will score.

Carrick began by beating Manchester City and Arsenal in his first two games in January. Pochettino might start by being on the wrong end of a pummelling.

Lauren's prediction: Liverpool are at home and I am going with them to win it. 2-1

Brentford beat Everton 4-2 at the Hill Dickinson Stadium last season but, in three of their past four meetings, the points have been shared.

This has got a draw written all over it, too.

Everton are so solid under David Moyes, and so well organised, that I just don't see them losing. Brentford will keep things tight as well.

Lauren's prediction: Both these teams are doing well and this should be pretty even - I haven't gone for a draw yet, so let's finish with one. 1-1

That means they miss out on the weekly win, which ended up in a tie between the BBC readers and AI. They both got six correct results with no exact scores, for a tally of 120 points.

There was only one game where everyone picked the right outcome - Liverpool's 3-1 win over Crystal Palace.

Chris, FM 27, Miles, AI and the BBC readers all backed the Reds to win, but AI was the only one to get the score spot on.

There were two games where no-one got the correct result - Nottingham Forest's win over Manchester City, and Sunderland's victory against Chelsea.
