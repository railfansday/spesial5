Penélope Cruz Details Family Life in Madrid With Javier Bardem & Kids

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Invite's Penélope Cruz explained why it's "easier" for her and her husband Javier Bardem to live with their kids Leo, 15, and Luna, 12, in Madrid.

"All our families are there," Cruz said in an interview with The Perfect Magazine published Nov. 5, "and the friends from childhood and from our teenage years; it’s easier to have a normal life, you know?"

"You have to make an effort to keep a part of your life private," she continued. "But I think when you have children, it’s really important. And they deserve that, they completely deserve that. Later they can decide what they want to do in terms of their work and everything. But they need to have that freedom and not be exposed to things when they are little."

"I feel like I'm still learning [in my relationship], you know?" Cruz told People in July. "And I am with somebody that I've known for 33 years."

“I really know that person, but it's like, is there a day where you really get to know yourself?" she added at the time, alluding to the complex relationship between her and Edward Norton's characters in The Invite. "No. So the same with your partner."

Another key element to making their relationship work? Picking and choosing when to work together, with Cruz explaining that why she prefers to collaborate with her husband—who she met while filming 1992's Jamón Jamón and became romantically involved with after filming 2008's Vicky Cristina Barcelona—only "once in a while."

"Obviously we can't choose parts just for logistical reasons, like 'Oh let's work together because it's easier,'" she told Marie Claire in 2019. "No. In fact, it's not something we want to do that often, partly out of desire to protect what we have."
