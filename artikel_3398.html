Netflix Officially Confirms ‘The Witcher’ Will Not End in 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Hannah covers horror, sci-fi, and games at Collider, where she writes news and features, reviews new releases, and interviews the people who make them. She’s helping build out Collider’s gaming coverage, with Pokémon as one of her specialties. If she isn’t writing about a monster, she’s probably watching one on a tape from her VHS collection.

Geralt of Rivia made up the “of Rivia” part because he thought it would get him more work. Four seasons into The Witcher, a queen finally makes the name official, and the person who’d appreciate that story most is Ciri. Back at Kaer Morhen, Geralt had already once let her in on a secret that’s a little embarrassing for a man who makes his living killing monsters: when he was a boy, he wanted to be a knight. By the time he gets his wish, he’d trade it for an afternoon with his daughter.

It’s been more than a year since Liam Hemsworth took over for Henry Cavill, and one of the stranger consequences of the timing is how little of his run he’s gotten to spend with Freya Allan, because Geralt and Ciri were already separated when Hemsworth picked up the sword. With Netflix filming the final two seasons back-to-back, the expectation was that viewers who saw his first season in October 2025 would get the rest of that story this year.

Netflix has officially slated The Witcher Season 5 onto its release schedule for 2027 or later. In its November 16 announcement from the Texas Book Festival, the streamer listed Geralt’s final outing among the book adaptations coming in “2027 and beyond,” alongside Bridgerton Season 5 and Narnia: The Magician's Nephew. Netflix has yet to announce an exact premiere date for The Witcher's fifth and final season.

Four worlds and four completely different ideas of what a good life looks like. This is where you belong, what it would ask of you, and what you would get back in return.

You belong somewhere old enough that the mountains have names and somebody can still tell you who gave them. Middle-earth is for people who think the right thing is worth doing even when it will not be finished in their lifetime.

You want the sea, a crew, and absolutely nobody telling you where you are allowed to go. The Grand Line is the world for people whose main ambition in life is finding out what is over there.

You would rather be feared and useful than liked and useless. The Continent is a world where the monsters are the straightforward part of the job and the people are where it all gets complicated.

You want the myths to be real and loose in the actual world, and you want to find out at about thirteen that you were never anywhere near as ordinary as everybody kept telling you.

Geralt has spent the better part of a season looking for Ciri, and even if somebody handed him a map with her whereabouts marked on it, he’d still be working with bad information. The girl he remembers training at Kaer Morhen has fallen in love, found a home with the Rats, and watched Leo Bonhart (Sharlto Copley) murder every last one of them, ending with Bonhart taking Ciri alive. Whatever Geralt imagines he’ll need to do for his daughter when he finds her, he has no idea yet what happened to Mistle or what Ciri had to see.

Then there’s Ciri’s biological father, who has come to an ugly conclusion about the man who raised her. Emhyr (Bart Edwards) once owed Geralt his life. By the end of Season 4, his advisers have convinced him that the bond between Geralt and Ciri is the reason he keeps failing to find his daughter. The emperor closes the season by walking up to a cell and giving the creature behind the bars Geralt’s name. After four seasons of people hunting Ciri for what her blood can do, someone has finally figured out what makes her father vulnerable. Everybody on the Continent knows who he’ll come for.

The first four seasons of The Witcher are streaming on Netflix. Stay tuned with Collider for future updates on the release of Season 5.
