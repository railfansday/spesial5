Arsenal handed William Saliba boost as Arteta considers defender’s return

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The William Saliba Arsenal boost comes at a crucial time. Saliba can use the November international break to build up his fitness after missing out on France’s latest squad.

Arteta confirmed that the defender had returned to full training ahead of Arsenal’s Champions League meeting with Slavia Prague. He also completed 45 minutes in a behind-closed-doors-friendly last week as he stepped up his recovery.

However, Saliba did not feature in the 2-1 victory. Arteta included him in the matchday squad for the first time this season, but the Frenchman remained an unused substitute.

It means he was not selected by Zinedine Zidane for France’s Nations League games against Italy and Turkey. This gives Arsenal a chance to continue working on his recovery. He will not be travelling for international games or playing in competitive matches.

Saliba has not featured since he suffered a back injury during France’s World Cup semi-final against Spain in July. Arteta has had to find alternatives in central defence in the early stages of the campaign.

Since transferring to Arsenal from Aston Villa in August, Konsa has played a part in filling the gap left by Saliba. His presence in the centre of defence complements Gabriel Magalhães and gives Arteta more options.

The England defender is also expected to be fit for the match against Hull City on Saturday. According to Arteta, the player’s replacement in the game against Slavia Prague was just a precaution after he reported some discomfort.

The fact that he has made a recovery means that Arsenal have the option to control how Saliba returns. Arteta has the possibility of bringing Saliba back gradually, as Konsa is ready to play and Mosquera is also available.

But Saliba’s return still matters. He needs to get back in shape before the fixtures start getting tougher after the international break.

Arteta has made it clear he will respect whatever decision Zidane makes regarding Saliba’s availability but acknowledges that he needs some game time after being out for so long.

France will play Italy on November 12 and then Turkey three days later. This means Saliba will be missing those matches, as Zidane named his 23-man squad on Friday.

This means he will be able to continue to be monitored by the club at their London Colney training ground. He won’t be able to return to play in international games ahead of his first appearance for the club.

Arsenal will be hoping that he can use the extra time to get himself ready for the next set of games, including a trip to Newcastle before playing Borussia Dortmund and Manchester City at home.

Arteta will be keen to have Saliba back in contention but will be focused on getting him back at the right time.

There is some immediate optimism on Konsa’s fitness for the game against Hull. But more importantly, Saliba has now progressed to a point where he can be included in the planning process.
