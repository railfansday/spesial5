Southampton urged to sign 'pricey' Andri Gudjohnsen from Blackburn Rovers

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Southampton have been urged to make a move for Blackburn Rovers' Andri Gudjohnsen in the January transfer window, with support for Cyle Larin becoming a clearer need.

Will Lankshear is the Championship's top scorer with 13, while Jack Moylan and Andri Gudjohnsen have 10 in joint-second. Cyle Larin has seven for Southampton thus far but neither Lewis Dobbin or Divin Mubama have shone in his absence.

The Man City loanee has one goal and one assist in his 14 appearances, with Southampton needing more in that area of the pitch for when Larin is out of action. That has become more evident for Tonda Eckert in recent weeks.

Louis Harris is FLW's Southampton fan pundit and, with the January transfer window fast approaching, we asked him to name one player he would absolutely love the club to make a move for, while keeping it realistic but being free to be ambitious as well.

He said: "The January transfer window is always a tricky one, I think. Lots of panic buying and clubs usually looking to over-inflate prices compared to a summer transfer.

"In regards to where we need to strengthen in January, I absolutely think we need to look at our striking options. It's quite clear that Mubama isn't working.

"I can imagine City would want to look at recalling him to get him gametime elsewhere. Dobbin has been handy, playing as a striker, but he can't play as a lone striker. I prefer him as a number 10.

"Larin is Larin. And I don't think he has a 46-game season in the tank. Well, he quite clearly doesn't.

"So, for me, I'd really like to see us go for Andri Gudjohnsen from Blackburn [Rovers]. It could be a bit of a pricey deal to do but he looks a robust centre-forward.

"He's a really good age. I think he'd offer lots of competition for Larin. When you see his goals, he's been able to score them with real number nine finishes, and poachers goals, and he looks to be a bit of a threat aerially as well.

"Which is something Dobbin doesn't offer when he comes in for Larin. On top of that, he looks to have better movement around the box compared to Mubama. I'd really like to see us go for him.

"But I could imagine Blackburn will ask us to cough up a lot of money, so we will see."

Blackburn are positioned to demand around £15 million for Gudjohnsen in January, pricing out fellow Championship clubs. Having signed a long-term deal running until 2028 with a 12-month club option, Rovers face zero pressure to sell.

The 24-year-old Icelandic striker has been sensational in the 2026/27 campaign under Tony Mowbray, scoring 10 goals in 14 Championship appearances alongside an EFL Cup strike and a recent international hat-trick in the Nations League.

Consequently, promotion rivals like Southampton must rethink their winter recruitment plans. Mid-season deals carry steep premiums, and spending possibly £20 million or more on one forward is financially unfeasible for second-tier sides.

Unless Premier League suitors join the race, Rovers can comfortably retain their talisman for a late play-off push. They may always feel like they need to sell eventually, but January should not be a consideration for Blackburn regarding Gudjohnsen.

Born in London as the son of former Chelsea and Barcelona star Eidur Gudjohnsen, the Blackburn forward developed in Spain’s elite youth systems at both Barcelona and Real Madrid.

Seeking senior football, he moved to Sweden’s IFK Norrkoping before thriving on loan at Danish side Lyngby, where a prolific 15-goal campaign earned him a permanent move to Belgian club KAA Gent in 2024. After a quieter spell in Belgium, he joined Blackburn in August 2025.

His debut season at Ewood Park showed clear promise despite hamstring and ankle injuries cutting it short, yielding seven goals in 25 Championship appearances and a new long-term contract. Now fully fit in 2026/27, he is the division's standout marksman.

Only 24, and with plenty of room to develop further, Gudjohnsen already has 15 international goals. Only five players have more, including his father and Gylfi Sigurdsson. He is well on the way to topping that list given his current trajectory.
