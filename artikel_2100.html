Stoke City won’t regret selling 27-year-old in the summer transfer window: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Stoke City cashed in on the attacker on 1st September and he penned a four-year deal at the MKM Stadium running until June 2030. Since leaving SCFC, he has made six appearances for the Tigers in all competitions and has chipped in with one assist.

Thomas, 27, was a key player for the Potters during his time at the Bet365 Stadium and was one of their key players under Mark Robins. He made 52 outings for the Staffordshire outfit in all competitions, scoring 10 goals laying on 15 assists for his ex-teammates.

Robins’ side haven’t missed him at all though and they are sat joint-top of the Championship table along with Birmingham City and Middlesbrough. They are positioned in 3rd on goal difference behind Boro and they are in the play-offs along with Wolves, West Brom, Swansea City, Southampton and QPR after 15 fixtures.

Stoke City won 2-1 away at Portsmouth last time out before the international break after goals by Jack McGlynn and Ben Wilmot. They are back in action next weekend at home to Birmingham City.

Stoke City have flourished since Thomas’ departure. SCFC are less reliant on one player and it has become more of a team effort.

The Wales international, who has 31 caps to his name, hasn’t been able to make the impact he would have wanted to at Hull City. He missed out on their recent squads against Brentford and Ipswich Town and was left on the bench last Saturday against Arsenal.

Thomas faces a tough battle to get into the Tigers’ starting XI in the top flight. They are positioned in 13th in the league and have adapted pretty well to the step up a division against the odds.

Stoke City signed the London-born man back in 2025. Prior to his switch, he had permanent spells at Boreham Wood and Huddersfield Town, as well as loans at Blackburn Rovers and FC Nantes.

Thomas was a great player for Stoke City and his numbers were very impressive. He deserved a big move to Hull City off the back of his form at SCFC.

However, the Potters are better off without him now and have others players stepping up. Robins’ men will be full of confidence at the moment and will be aiming to keep their momentum going against Birmingham City next Saturday.
