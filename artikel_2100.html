Mark Noble Set For New West Ham Role Alongside Scott Parker

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Scott Parker’s impending arrival at West Ham United could be accompanied by another interesting appointment, with Mark Noble reportedly set to join his former teammate’s coaching staff. According to The Guardian, Noble is expected to form part of Parker’s backroom team, although the precise role has yet to be officially confirmed.

It’s a development I find particularly encouraging. As Claret & Hugh has already reported, Parker is set to return to the club following the dismissal of Nuno Espírito Santo, bringing with him an impressive record of three promotions from the Championship.

But the prospect of Parker and Noble working together offers something else entirely. We could have a manager and a senior member of his coaching staff who completely understand West Ham’s identity, what the club means to its supporters and the importance of developing our own players.

For me, this would be a really shrewd move, both politically and in terms of developing West Ham’s younger players. Noble has already been closely involved with the club’s footballing operations as sporting director, giving him valuable knowledge of the youngsters coming through the academy.

There has recently been discussion within West Ham’s recruitment department about improving the pathway between the academy and the first team. Having Noble directly involved in the coaching set-up could be an excellent way of putting that ambition into practice, particularly given his familiarity with the players and their development.

I’d imagine the likes of Lewis Orford, Ollie Scarles and Golambeckis would welcome the prospect of somebody who knows them so well being involved in first-team decisions. That doesn’t guarantee any of them a place, of course, but it should at least help ensure their abilities are properly understood and considered.

There is also something reassuring about the prospect of two former Hammers working together at a time when the club desperately needs a change of direction. Following Nuno’s dismissal, West Ham need to restore confidence and establish a clearer identity on the pitch.

Of course, knowing the club isn’t enough on its own. Parker and Noble will ultimately be judged on results, and the immediate priority remains getting West Ham promoted back to the Premier League.

Nevertheless, I think this would be an excellent appointment. Parker brings his Championship experience, Noble brings his knowledge of West Ham and its young players, and together they could provide exactly the sort of connection between the first team and academy that we’ve been missing.

There’s still the question of Noble’s precise job title and how his sporting director responsibilities would change, but the prospect of the pair working together is one I find genuinely exciting.
