EY announces alliance with Stuut to help organizations modernize order-to-cash processes

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

LONDON, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The EY organization today announces an alliance between Stuut, an AI coworker for order-to-cash, and Ernst & Young (EY US) to help organizations redesign finance processes, transform operating models and introduce agentic AI into complex accounts receivable environments.

As organizations look to reduce days sales outstanding (DSO), improve cash flow visibility and streamline collections and dispute management, the EY-Stuut Alliance brings together Stuut's agentic AI platform and EY US's finance transformation, process redesign, technology implementation and change management capabilities. Together, the Alliance will help organizations modernize accounts receivable operations, improve efficiency and enable more connected, intelligent finance processes.

Through the Alliance, EY US and Stuut will pursue order-to-cash and working capital transformation opportunities, integrate Stuut's platform into EY-led finance offerings and develop industry-specific use cases and solution playbooks. EY US will lead the business and functional aspects of client engagements, while Stuut will provide its platform, solution configuration, technical support and ongoing product innovation and enhancement.

Shawn Ryan, EY-Stuut Alliance Leader, EY US, says:

"Organizations are navigating increasingly complex accounts receivable environments while considering how emerging AI capabilities can support their finance priorities. The combination of EY US's transformation experience and Stuut's AI-native platform provides a strong foundation for addressing these evolving needs. Formalizing the Alliance identifies a clearer framework for bringing our complementary capabilities to organizations more consistently and at greater scale. It also creates opportunities to expand the application of agentic AI across finance operations while maintaining a strong focus on business outcomes."

Ben Winter, CBO and Co-Founder at Stuut, says:

"The value of AI in finance has to show up in the P&L. Stuut does the work to get companies paid, carrying forward each account's history and learning from every interaction. Our AI, paired with EY US's deep order-to-cash experience, helps enterprises accelerate collections, unlock working capital and scale their finance operations beyond what their teams could previously manage."

For more information, visit ey.com/alliances.
