Wholesale prices are up 6.5% from a year ago, the biggest annual rise since November 2022

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The Producer Price Index for final demand rose 0.3% in October from September, after a 1.4% gain the month before

The Bureau of Labor Statistics reported that producer prices for final demand rose 6.5% in the 12 months ended in October, on an unadjusted basis. That was the largest 12-month rise since a 7.4% increase in November 2022, according to the agency.

On a seasonally adjusted basis, the producer price index for final demand rose 0.3% in October. Prices advanced 1.4% in September and 0.4% in August, according to the BLS.

The index for final demand goods rose 0.5% in October after a 2.5% increase in September. Final demand food prices rose 1.3%, while energy prices edged down 0.1%. Prices for basic organic chemicals advanced 3.9%, and diesel fuel prices fell 7.9%. Over the 12 months ended in October, final demand goods prices rose 10.2%, the largest 12-month rise since a 10.5% gain in October 2022.

The index for final demand services moved up 0.2%, its fifth consecutive advance. Prices for transportation and warehousing services rose 2.0%, and truck transportation of freight rose 3.9%. Margins for final demand trade services decreased 0.2%.

The index for final demand less foods, energy and trade services rose 0.5% in October, the same as in September. That measure was up 4.9% from a year earlier.

Within intermediate demand, prices for processed goods rose 0.4% in October. They were up 14.6% from a year earlier, the largest 12-month rise since a 17.2% jump in July 2022. Prices for unprocessed goods fell 3.1%, the largest decline since a 6.6% drop in June, as crude petroleum prices decreased 10.3%. Unprocessed goods prices were up 16.9% from a year earlier.

Prices for services for intermediate demand rose 0.6% in October and 6.8% over 12 months. That was the largest 12-month advance since a 6.9% increase in November 2022.

The BLS said figures for June through September were revised to reflect late reports and corrections by respondents. The agency scheduled the November producer price index release for Tuesday, Dec. 15, at 8:30 a.m. ET.
