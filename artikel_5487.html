Annexus Health Awarded Gold in 2026 Merit Awards for Healthcare

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Gold recognition for Best Healthcare Information System underscores Annexus' contributions to improving patient access and financial assistance delivery

CRANBERRY TOWNSHIP, Pa., Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Annexus Health, the infrastructure behind patient financial assistance, today announced that it has been awarded Gold for Best Healthcare Information System in the HealthTech category of the 2026 Merit Awards for Healthcare.

Merit Awards recognize achievements across industries including healthcare, technology, business, and communications. In healthcare, the awards honor organizations addressing industry needs through their technology, services, and innovations.

This recognition highlights Annexus' work to help patients receiving specialty therapeutics access and afford treatment, while reducing the operational complexity healthcare providers face in securing and managing financial assistance.

Through connected technology, automation, and intelligence, the Annexus Platform helps providers identify available financial assistance, improve financial performance, and maintain support throughout treatment. The Platform also helps life sciences organizations extend patient support programs into provider workflows, connecting more eligible patients with available assistance.

"Financial assistance can make a meaningful difference in a patient's ability to access treatment, but the complexity of securing and managing that support creates challenges across healthcare," said Brad Frazier, Co-Founder and CEO of Annexus Health. "We've spent years developing the technology and operational expertise to make that process more connected and effective at scale. There's more work ahead, and we're focused on bringing these capabilities to more providers so they can connect more patients with the financial assistance available to them."

Annexus has helped secure more than $11 billion in financial assistance and supported more than 400,000 patient enrollments. Its nationwide network spans more than 735 healthcare organizations and 32,000 sites of care.
