GTA 6 Fans Think They’ve Spotted Gay Tony Cameo In New Trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

With pre-loads going live, Rockstar just shared 30 seconds of new GTA 6 footage. And one character from the teaser is making a bit of a stir.

Featured is a man with a goatee in a blue tank top who has a New York accent (pictured above). Considering that Rockstar hasn't even confirmed who plays Jason and Lucia, minor characters like this are a total mystery. But fans think he might be Tony Prince, the protagonist from the iconic GTA 4 expansion, The Ballad of Gay Tony.

"Why does this guy actually kinda look and sound like Tony?" popular YouTuber TGG said. "I'm losing my mind, similar glasses, beard and very similar voice. There's no way he let himself go and moved to the keys right? The trailer is also based around nightclubs so you know what? Hell yeah I think it might be him."

Tony Prince, played by D. B. Cooper (not the guy who jumped from the plane with $1.6 million) is the owner of several nightclubs in Liberty City, and returned in GTA Online to expand his business to Los Santos.

As TGG said, Tony Prince is synonymous with nightclubs in the HD games. But going beyond that, commenters have pointed out what appears to be a GTA 5 postcard on his wall, meaning the character has visited Los Santos—just like Tony.

However, Tony Prince was born some time between the 1950s and early 1960s, which would make him almost 70-years-old today. The character in the trailer looks a lot younger, even if the voice is similar. There's also nothing to suggest the man in the blue tank top has anything to do with nightclubs, other than the teaser centering on them.

62 items, and you don't need to visit the stores in-person to get them.

There are plenty of posts on X and Reddit suggesting that Tony might have moved to Leonida and put on a few pounds. And these theories have gotten mixed up with speculation that Rockstar has somehow hidden the entirety of Liberty City from its marketing, reusing scrapped ideas from GTA 5. However, leakers have full access to GTA 6 and haven't said anything about Jason and Lucia visiting Liberty City, so it's highly unlikely.

This character, whoever he is, looks and sounds like Gay Tony, but that's probably where it begins and ends. He certainly doesn't appear to be in his late 60s, he isn't dressed how Tony usually dresses, and there's nothing connecting the two beyond a goatee. Still, it's weirdly nostalgic to have farfetched speculation a week out from launch. It really brings you back to the moon cycle days, eh?
