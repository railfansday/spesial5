Do a violin's physical characteristics really improve with regular playing?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

There is a widespread notion among violinists: A violin "opens up" as it is played, which is to say its sound gradually improves. However, an experiment conducted by a team led by a CNRS researcher has challenged this belief in a study published in Acta Acustica. After six months, the professional soloist in charge of playing the violin judged the process complete, reporting that the instrument had become easier to play and its tone had improved. Yet, no physical or acoustic measurement confirmed that this feeling resulted from such a mechanical change in the instrument itself.
