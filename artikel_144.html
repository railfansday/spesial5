A Mexican Artist Sang Some of His Hits. Authorities Stopped the Show, Then Arrested Him.

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

A concert by the singer Gabito Ballesteros was suspended after he sang songs that supposedly glorify cartel culture. The action has renewed a debate over culture and free speech.

A popular Mexican singer’s concert was halted on Friday night because he performed songs considered to be glorifying cartel culture, making him the latest and most high-profile artist to be ensnared in a government crackdown on the genre known as narcocorridos.

He was later arrested by the federal authorities in relation to an unrelated investigation.

Gabito Ballesteros, 27, whose songs have billions of streams, was performing at a fair in Querétaro State in central Mexico when authorities escorted him off the stage.

Before the show, his set list was reviewed and approved by state officials and event organizers, according to a statement issued by the fair. Songs that glorified criminal activities were explicitly excluded, in accordance with state regulations, the fair said.

Last year, the state joined several other states and municipalities throughout Mexico that effectively prohibit the public performance of so-called narcocorridos, a version of traditional Mexican ballads that celebrate drug cartels instead of historic events and everyday life. Violations were punishable with fines or even jail time.

The actions — and the genre itself — have sparked much debate in Mexico, which has been wrestling with both the effects of organized crime, particularly amid pressure from the Trump administration to crack down on cartels, and its identity as defined through popular culture. The discussion also has became a referendum on free speech.

On Friday night, Mr. Ballesteros performed an unauthorized song and local authorities took him offstage to remind him of his prior agreement, the fair said. When he returned, the fair said Mr. Ballesteros started to perform another unauthorized song, “AMG.” The 2022 hit, which references the luxurious lifestyles and fancy cars of cartel members, has nearly one billion streams on Spotify.
