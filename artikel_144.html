BBC correspondents discuss what Trump, Xi and Putin got out of historic talks

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Presidents Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin and Xi Jinping have concluded their historic 90 minutes of trilateral talks by issuing a "joint declaration of peace", but what did each leader actually get out of the meeting?

The highly anticipated meeting of three of the world's global superpowers held in China's south-eastern city of Shenzhen, marked the first time that the three had met together.

The BBC's Russia editor, China correspondent and North America correspondent got together to discuss the outcome of the meeting.
