Creative Media (CMCT) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Multifamily and hotel gains drove results as asset sales came under review

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Creative Media & Community Trust Corporation (CMCT +6.61%) reported a 55% increase in total segment net operating income during the third quarter, driven by performance gains in multifamily, office, and hotel assets. Management stated that the company remains focused on improving funds from operations, strengthening the balance sheet, and exploring strategic asset sales to narrow the gap between share price and portfolio value. The company has reduced its corporate debt to $27.1 million while continuing to fund renovations and leasing initiatives. Management indicated that fundamental recovery in the Bay Area multifamily market has led to record-high rent growth and declining vacancy rates.

In-place rents at our Bay Area multifamily properties are approximately 17% below current asking rents, providing a meaningful opportunity for continued growth as new leases roll to market.
