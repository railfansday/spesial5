The DJ's voice feature can now be disabled but what's the fun in that?

Music streaming service Spotify has unveiled new abilities for its popular AI-powered DJ feature, including new hosts — speaking in Spanish and English — and an on/off announcer toggle. The update rolls out to existing DJ markets starting Tuesday.

The lineup includes seven new English voices with British, American, Irish, Australian and Indian accents, as well as two new Mexican-accented voices that speak in Spanish.

The personalities also differ by announcer, too — for instance, Spotify says American Tess is “direct but inviting, has a bit of an edge. In-the-know” while Australian Sam is “gregarious, fun, likes to keep things not-so-serious.”

You can also turn the announcer off, but it would then be hard to tell how a Spotify DJ mix differs from a random mix. The company says you’ll still hear a “brief acknowledgment” when you start the DJ or press the DJ button to switch modes.

Spotify’s DJ was introduced in 2023 and is a way to set up your own radio station with a dynamic announcer. In addition, Spotify is the most popular music streaming service, with over 100 million tracks and a plethora of features including audiobooks, podcasts and the end-of-year Spotify Wrapped. Subscriptions are available starting at $13 a month.
