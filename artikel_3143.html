‘The Trip To The Northern Lights’: IFC Acquires Latest In Michael Winterbottom, Steve Coogan & Rob Brydon Franchise

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

EXCLUSIVE: Independent Film Company (IFC) and Sapan Studio have taken U.S. rights to The Trip to the Northern Lights, the fifth installment in Michael Winterbottom, Steve Coogan and Rob Brydon’s The Trip franchise. An April 9, 2027 theatrical release has been set.

IFC has been the home of the previous four chapters in The Trip series which has spanned the duo’s journeys throughout the UK, Italy, Spain and Greece.

More than five years since their Grecian escapades, 2x Oscar nominee Coogan and 5x BAFTA Award nominee Rob Brydon reunite to tour the rugged beauty of northern Europe. What follows is a trek through Scandinavia in search of fine food, wry conversation and a glimpse of the elusive Northern Lights. The duo soak up northern Europe’s wildest reaches, from sculptural Swedish hotels to remote Norwegian islands in what is equal parts travelogue and midlife reckoning. Said BAFTA winner Winterbottom, “We are very happy to be back on the road with IFC. They have been brilliant partners ever since the first Trip all those years ago. The Trip to the Northern Lights takes Steve and Rob from Malmo in southern Sweden through Norway’s spectacular fjords to the Lofoten Islands in Norway’s Arctic circle. I hope the audience enjoys the journey as much as we did.” Watch on Deadline

“The Independent Film Company is thrilled to continue our longstanding relationship with this world-class group of filmmakers on the latest installment of this beloved franchise. Few comedic partnerships have the enduring appeal of Steve Coogan and Rob Brydon, and under Michael Winterbottom’s inspired direction, each new trip remains the comedic gift that keeps on giving. This time around, we can’t wait for audiences to join us on the road to the Northern Lights,” said Ayo Kepher-Maat, Independent Film Company’s Acquisitions and Production VP.

“Michael Winterbottom, Steve Coogan and Rob Brydon have created a franchise filled with taste, wit and belly laughs. Independent Film Company has brought each one to audiences with characteristic care and capability. The Trip to the Northern Lights advances the history with the delight of something completely fresh and tasty,” said Josh Sapan of Sapan Studio.

The Trip to the Northern Lights is produced by Melissa Parmenter and Josh Hyams for Revolution Films. The deal was negotiated by Ayo Kepher-Maat on behalf of Independent Film Company and Tristan Whalley MD of Goalpost Films on behalf of the filmmakers.

EPs are Winterbottom, Coogan, Rob Brydon, Alex Moody, Josh Sapan, Tristan Whalley, and Paul Wiegard. The pic is a Revolution Films, Baby Cow, and Small Man Production in association with Sky Studios.
