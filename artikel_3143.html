Sabra Health Care REIT, Inc. (SBRA +3.19%) reported a strategic shift toward its Senior Housing Operating Portfolio (SHOP), which is projected to reach 40% of the portfolio by year-end 2026 and 50% by the end of 2027. Management indicated that this transition will result in the senior housing portfolio outweighing the skilled nursing portfolio for the first time in company history. The company reported $747.2 million in year-to-date investments and an additional $425 million in awarded deals, which are expected to support a year-over-year earnings growth rate of 7% to 8% at the midpoint of 2026 guidance. Management is also focused on optimizing its skilled nursing segment through fair market rent resets upon lease expirations between 2027 and 2031. Leverage continues to trend downward, reaching 4.54x as the company utilizes its at-the-market equity program to fund a robust pipeline of value-add and stable assets.
