Logistic Properties of the Americas: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The shares trade below book value at around $8.86 per share, so the Mexico bet is being priced with some skepticism.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 21, 2026 at 5:54PM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 11, 2026, Logistic Properties of the Americas (LPA -4.59%) announced three major real estate sales totaling over $300 million to fund its expansion into Mexico. The company is selling mature real estate assets in South America at a premium to balance sheet values and shifting that equity into higher-yielding logistics parks.

Management sold properties in Peru, Costa Rica, and Colombia to recycle capital into higher-yielding logistics hubs in Mexico. This shift lets Logistic Properties of the Americas expand without taking on new debt or diluting equity.

The company's recent property sales closed at premiums to appraised balance sheet values, proving the real cash value of its real estate. Even after these transactions, the stock trades below both its book value per share and its cash per share.

Reinvesting sales proceeds into new projects at capitalization rates (the annual rental yield on property value) above 8% widens the return spread over older, lower-yielding assets. Institutional partnerships allow Logistic Properties of the Americas to control large developments with small equity stakes while earning ongoing management fees.

Capital deployment into Mexico's freight corridors is accelerating as asset sales unlock cash for higher-yielding developments. The company's regional platform and capital-light institutional partnerships give it structural cost advantages, enabling consistent pricing power across key Latin American distribution routes.

Management is pacing its acquisitions to match tenant demand while keeping debt leverage low, focusing on execution along primary supply chains to serve multinational tenants.
