Klarna (KLAR +4.70%) maintained its trajectory toward full-year profitability by achieving its fourth consecutive quarter of adjusted operating income. Management indicated that the company is transitioning from a period focused on global market expansion to a strategic phase centered on increasing average revenue per active consumer. This shift is being executed through the scaling of high-margin products including the Klarna Card, Fair Financing installments, and premium subscription services. The company also integrated its payment solutions with major payment service providers and established a multiyear partnership with Apple to broaden its reach within the U.S. retail sector. Additionally, the company is deploying artificial intelligence to transform its mobile application into an autonomous shopping assistant capable of facilitating agentic commerce across its global merchant network.
