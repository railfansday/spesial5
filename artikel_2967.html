Telefônica Brasil S.A. (VIV +5.32%) reported total revenue of BRL 16 billion for the third quarter, representing 7.2% growth year over year. Management stated that performance was driven by a 7.4% increase in mobile services revenue and a 26.6% rise in handset and electronics sales. The company indicated that profitability outpaced top-line growth, with EBITDA rising 10% to BRL 7.1 billion. The strategy remains focused on high-quality customer acquisition in the postpaid and fiber segments, supported by expanding digital services and integrated connectivity solutions. The company also confirmed it remains open to M&A opportunities in the fiber segment while maintaining a low leverage ratio of 0.5 times EBITDA.
