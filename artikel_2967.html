Zach LaVine takes bizarre shot at Lakers after Kings’ embarrassing defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Los Angeles Lakers' defense did more that enough to stifle the Sacramento Kings on Wednesday night, as evidenced by the Lakers' 116-103 road win.

But that final score didn't stop Kings guard Zach LaVine from taking a shot at Los Angeles' defense after the game.

"They've got some bad defenders on their team, but so do we, so they exploited them," LaVine said about the Lakers, per an X post from Brenden Nunes.

Sacramento Kings guard Zach LaVine speaking about the Los Angeles Lakers' defense. (X/@Brenden NunesNBA).

LaVine, who finished with 21 points on 6-for-14 shooting from the field, also spoke about how Luka Dončić shredded the Kings' defense, and how the Lakers make it hard when they funnel their offense through Walker Kessler down low.

"It's just frustrating when you play hard, and you give it your all. No one is gonna be perfect, regardless of how much you try of whatever. But it's just unfortunate sometimes. But we're still competing," he added.

The Lakers are absolutely more of an offensive juggernaut than a defensive-minded team, at least on the individual level.

But it still didn't feel like the right time for LaVine to take this shot at Los Angeles, even though he admitted his own squad struggles in the same way.

Los Angeles Lakers guard Luka Doncic reacts during the second half of an NBA basketball game against the Sacramento Kings Wednesday, Nov. 4, 2026, in Sacramento, Calif. AP Photo/Scott Marshall

Zach LaVine of the Sacramento Kings looks on during a game against the Los Angeles Lakers on Wednesday, Nov. 4, 2026, at Golden 1 Center in Sacramento, Calif. NBAE via Getty Images

The Lakers' opponents are averaging 113.9 points per game this season, which is No. 17 in the NBA. Sacramento is allowing 123.3 points per game, coming in at No. 28.

Perhaps LaVine should spend more time looking in the mirror.
