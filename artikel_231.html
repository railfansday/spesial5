Trmnl, a company best known for its highly customizable E Ink dashboards that can function as anything from a desktop calendar to a stock tracker, has introduced a new display that’s also well suited for showing artwork and photos. It’s not the company’s first color E Ink device, but instead of being limited to just four colors, the new Frame can display thousands of shades using a 31.5-inch E Ink Spectra 6 panel, similar to what you’ll find in products from Aura and Pocketbook.

The Frame is now available for preorder for $1,299 in a handmade wooden body and you can choose from four different types of wood: white oak, cherry, sapele, and walnut. The first batch will be a limited run of around 470 units that’s already more than half sold out, and delivery of most of the displays is expected to happen in early 2027. Optional add-ons include a matching wooden kickstand if you don’t want to hang it on a wall, and a larger 20,000mAh battery. However, the Frame should run for months between charges with the standard 10,000mAh option.

What sets the Frame apart from other large format E Ink art frames and posters is that it’s open source and can do more than display images. Through plugins you can build yourself, or download from a marketplace featuring over 1,400 at this point, the Frame can also be used as a dashboard showing the weather, to-do lists, or whatever info you want hanging on your wall.

If you do use it for displaying imagery, Trmnl developed Lazarus, a color conversion engine the company believes provides the “best color matching on the market for any Spectra 6 display.” If you disagree, because the Frame is open source, you can adjust the color tuning to match your own preferences.

As with Trmnl’s other E Ink products, the Frame can be connected to the internet to dynamically display whatever information you need it to. But to further extend its battery life, you can also fill a microSD card with a folder of images files and use the Frame offline. You can customize how frequently a new photo is displayed, or set up schedules so that your favorite piece of art stays on screen longer than others. While the Frame is more expensive than other E Ink Spectra 6 products currently available, the appeal here is the flexibility to use it as more than just a digital poster.
