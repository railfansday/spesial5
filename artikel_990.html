Man City boss Enzo Maresca says Erling Haaland had goal wrongly disallowed

Enzo Maresca had no doubt Erling Haaland’s disallowed goal in Manchester City’s 1-1 draw with Fulham on Saturday should have stood.

The prolific Norwegian had an effort chalked off in the Premier League encounter at the Etihad Stadium when he was deemed to have kicked the ball out of Fulham goalkeeper Bernd Leno’s hands.

Haaland had not connected cleanly with a volley but Leno spilled the ball attempting to gather his attempt and the striker prodded in from close range.

Referee Thomas Bramall, however, ruled that Leno – because the ball was still between his hands – had not lost control and VAR Sam Barrott upheld the decision.

Maresca said: “I reviewed the goal after the game. From my point of view, it’s quite clear that it’s not a foul, but I’m not the guy that decides, unfortunately.”

Asked how Haaland viewed the incident, Maresca added: “When they were reviewing the VAR, he was absolutely sure that it was a goal, because I asked him from the touchline. He was completely sure that it was a goal but the referee decided for different things.”

With that effort ruled out in the 25th minute, City had to wait until just before the hour to cancel out Rodrigo Muniz’s 16th-minute opener.

Elliot Anderson scored their equaliser with a fine volley – a sweet moment for the midfielder after being sent off playing for England last weekend.

Maresca said: “It was a nice moment for him, a nice moment for us because it gave us the chance to come back in the game.

“We used him today for the first time as an attacking midfielder. He was playing a little bit more deep with us. We are trying to find different solutions, the right balance, and to be more consistent.”

Anderson’s equaliser ended City’s run of three successive Premier League defeats but it was nevertheless another frustrating result for a side that opened the campaign with seven straight wins.

Maresca said: “For sure we don’t like this kind of moments, in terms of four games in a row without a win.

“But we also know that, unfortunately, it’s normal or it’s part of your path that you go through these kind of moments.

“It’s important that we find a way to keep a clean sheet. Sometimes you need to win games 1-0.”

For Fulham, the result ended a sequence of 20 successive losses to City in all competitions and extended their unbeaten run to five games.

Manager Alvaro Arbeloa said: “We came here trying to win this match.

“Obviously we knew that we had lost the last 20 times against Man City, but I told them that we will have the chance to beat them if we play with courage, with character, if we press them so high, play the way we like to play.

“I think we played really, really well. We could have won the game, but for me the most important thing was the performance and the character of my team.”
