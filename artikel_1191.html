Only one club has had a better start to their first Champions League season than Como

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Como currently sit in third place in the Champions League table with four victories from their first five games in 2026-27, and only one team has had a better start to their first season in the competition after the Champions League rebrand in 1992.

Como defeated AEK 1-0 at home on Tuesday evening to pick up their fourth win in their first five Champions League matches; Luis Milla providing the only goal of the game.

Cesc Fabregas’s side have also beaten RB Leipzig 4-1 this season, Manchester United 4-1 and Lens 9-1 away from home, the biggest winning margin by an away side in Champions League history. Feyenoord are the only team to have got the better of Como in Europe so far.

Sitting comfortably in third place with 12 points after five matches, and now targeting a top eight and a guaranteed spot in the round of 16, Como have started their first ever season in the Champions League in impressive fashion.

In fact, since 1992, only one team has enjoyed a better start to their first season in the Champions League: That was Leicester City back in 2016-17, off the back of their against-all-odds Premier League victory the season prior.

Under the guidance of Claudio Ranieri, the Foxes won four and drew one of their first five matches in the Champions League, which was enough to secure qualification to the knock-out rounds with one group stage match to spare.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
