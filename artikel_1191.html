Jack Grealish is EXILED by Everton: Ex-England star is still being left out, confirms boss David Moyes, after boozy nights at pubs and karaoke

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

By HARRY BAMFORTH, LIVE SPORTS COVERAGE REPORTER

Published: 08:52 EDT, 5 November 2026 | Updated: 08:54 EDT, 5 November 2026

David Moyes has confirmed that Jack Grealish will be absent for Everton's clash with Coventry City on Friday following disciplinary action last week.

The 31-year-old was dropped for back-to-back matches against Newcastle following a ‘breach of club discipline’ and will not be part of the Toffees' matchday squad for the fourth game on the spin tomorrow night.

'No, Jack is not available,' Moyes said. 'I wouldn't give you the plan, but we will be working with Jack again soon.'
