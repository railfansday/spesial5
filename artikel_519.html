Tanggal: 2026-11-27

TL;DR: Get the foldable Aero Volt Explorer Drone with dual cameras for $79.99 (reg. $199.99), saving $120.

Bulky camera drones tend to stay home when you pack for a trip, but the Aero Volt Explorer Drone folds small enough to fit in a daypack. During Black Friday, this carbon fiber drone drops to $79.99 (reg. $199.99).

Folded, the Explorer measures 7.09 by 4.92 by 2.76 inches and weighs under 0.55 pounds with the battery. Its front camera tilts up to 90 degrees from the remote, and a bottom camera shows the ground directly below. That downward view works well for overhead shots of a campsite or a shoreline. You can switch between the two views during a flight and watch the live feed on an iOS or Android phone.

The companion app lets you trace a flight path on your screen for the drone to follow, and gesture capture takes a photo with a hand signal. Four brushless motors and optical flow positioning help the drone hold a steady hover. Sensors help detect nearby obstacles, but you should still keep the drone in sight while you fly. A 360-degree flip function is there for fun once you’re comfortable with the controls.
