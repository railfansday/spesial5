Back at 100%, DeVonta Smith defends playing in Panthers game

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Back at 100%, DeVonta Smith defends playing in Panthers game originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

DeVonta Smith was back at practice on Wednesday afternoon as the Eagles returned from their Week 10 bye. The 28-year-old was a limited participant but said he's 100% after missing the last three games with a hamstring injury.

Of course, it's probably too little, too late.

The Eagles have lost seven straight games and, at 2-7, find themselves in the basement of the NFC East. Smith took over as the Eagles' WR1 in 2026 but has played in just four of nine games this season.

But Smith on Wednesday defended the decision to play against the Panthers back in Week 6. If you remember, he missed Weeks 4 and 5 with a hamstring injury and then ended up aggravating it against the Panthers and missed the next three games.

Smith claims he was 100% healthy going into that Panthers game, but still aggravated the injury.

"I know someone asked Nick (Sirianni) about shutting me down during the season," Smith said. "I didn't bust my ass this whole summer, I didn't continue to get up at 4:30 in the morning every day, to come out here and not play. My focus has been to getting where I was supposed to be.

"I felt 100% that game. I feel 100% now. Like I say, if something happens, it's out of my control because I know I do everything I can do for my body to make sure I'm in tip-top shape."

Smith was on a pitch count in that Panthers game and ended up playing 42 snaps, which he said was right on target. But he clearly tweaked the hamstring during that game and missed the three division losses against the Cowboys, Commanders and Giants.

In hindsight, the Eagles probably could have waited for Smith to return for those crucial NFC East matchups. Maybe it would have preserved him and the season, but maybe it wouldn't have mattered.

Smith said he has given up on being frustrated about the injury.

"I do everything I can do," Smith said. "I've been doing everything I can do. That's why, myself, I kind of got to the point where I was like, 'Maybe this is what God wanted' for whatever reason it is. Because I did everything I can do and if it happens, then it's out of my control. There's nothing I can do about it."

The one bright spot of Smith and Dontayvion Wicks missing the last three games is that rookie Makai Lemon made the most of his extra opportunities. The first-round pick had the three best games of his young career.

Lemon had 7 for 67 and a touchdown against the Cowboys, 6 for 57 against the Commanders and 6 for 61 against the Giants. Smith has spent his time off the field trying to help his young teammates as much as possible and was impressed by those performances from Lemon.

"The biggest thing is just going out there and knowing what to do," Smith said. "I think he's settled down a lot into knowing what he has to do and things like that. He's going out there, he's thinking less, he's playing a lot faster and I think that's the biggest thing. You can go out there and play fast and play free, you're going to be alright."

The last time we saw Smith in Week 6, the Eagles' starting quarterback was Tanner McKee. So Sunday against the Steelers will be his first game with Joe Milton under center.

Smith said he is using this week to learn as much as he can about the way Milton operates. He also admitted that Milton is still learning the offense.

This is Smith's six NFL season and he has been to the playoffs in the previous five. That streak is very likely going to end in 2026 as the Eagles enter the second half of their season with a 2-7 record.

Smith admitted has been a frustrating season from a team perspective.

"But at the end of the day, it's not over," Smith said. "Especially in this division. I feel like there's still a chance in our division right now but we can only take it one week at a time so focusing on this week. Going out there and getting a win would be the biggest thing right now. Getting back on track."
