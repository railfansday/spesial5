Breakfast News: What NVDA’s Earnings Call Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Nvidia’s first earnings without Jensen Huang in the spotlight, cybersecurity threats ahead of Anthropic IPO, and more…

By TMF Breakfast News – Nov 18, 2026 at 7:30AM EST

Everything is in place at Nvidia (NVDA -0.76%) on the operational and financial front. Its revenue more than doubled in the third quarter, and operating income and earnings per share hit all-time highs. Shares rose around 4% in pre-market trading.

Few investors dial into a company’s earnings call live. This time, it was different. The Q&A was their first chance to get answers following co-founder Jensen Huang’s sudden exit.

Chair Harvey Jones opened the Q3 earnings call and got right to the point, stating that the board “does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO.”

Then he handed the call to CFO and interim CEO Colette Kress.

Kress didn’t give us all of the answers, but she laid out the blueprint for a post-Huang Nvidia.

She closed the call with four words: “We have got this.” Long-term investors should hold steady, because only time will answer some questions.

Salesforce (CRM -3.87%), recommended by Team Rule Breakers, fell nearly 9.5% on Tuesday, ahead of Thursday’s Anthropic IPO, as Business Insider reported another round of layoffs tied to restructuring and fresh worries that AI could squeeze traditional software companies.

Anthropic is set to go public at a valuation of up to $1.4 trillion. Keep an eye out for our deep-dive tomorrow!

At a time when cybersecurity is making headlines, digital security specialist Palo Alto Networks (PANW +1.61%) unveils first-quarter earnings, and data storage systems and software provider Everpure (P -1.35%) reports its third quarter. Both are after market close.

Team Hidden Gems’ five-year price prediction for Kinsale Capital (KNSL +0.32%) is $628.42, nearly 60% above today’s price of about $398.

Kinsale insures the odd business risks that standard carriers turn away, and a $1,000 stake from our first Team Hidden Gems rec in April 2018 is worth about $8,100 today. We look for leaders who spend every dollar with purpose, and Kinsale’s choices during this year’s property insurance price war show that discipline in action.

Kinsale can keep growing its earnings while property prices slide, and a rebound in insurance prices would give our position a second wind.
