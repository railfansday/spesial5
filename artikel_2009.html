Dave Challinor confirms tweak to Sheffield Wednesday training - is it helping?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Sheffield Wednesday boss Dave Challinor has revealed that he has made training more ‘competitive’ after his positive start to life in charge of the club.

The Owls made the decision to sack Henrik Pedersen last month, with Challinor named as his successor at Hillsborough.

Whilst it’s still early days, the ex-Stockport chief has enjoyed a fine start at Wednesday, having followed up a 3-0 win over Plymouth with a hard-fought 2-1 success at Barnsley on Friday night, which makes it consecutive league victories.

Even though there have been defeats in cup competitions, Challinor knows that he will be judged on whether the team can win promotion this season, so he will be pleased that the team have moved into the play-off places.

Of course, the challenge will be to maintain these levels over the course of the campaign, but fans are happy with how Challinor has made an instant impact.

And, speaking to the Sheffield Star, the new boss has opened up on the tweaks he has made since his arrival, as he explained that he has called for an increase in intensity in training in a bid to improve ‘standards’.

“We've tried to make training a lot more competitive. Every game that we play, whether it's possession, whether it's a passing drill, whether it's a small-sided game, it's about winning. It's about being competitive. It's about driving standards. They've been great with that. I would hope that's played a part in what we've done tonight, and it will certainly help us moving forward.”

Firstly, the Sheffield Wednesday fans will love to hear this from Challinor, as there was a feeling that Pedersen lacked authority at times.

Despite their struggles last season, the Owls managed to build a very talented squad by League One standards, and the aim for Wednesday must be to win promotion.

Challinor is aware that it’s a big ask to do that, and you win promotion by the work you do every day on the training ground. So, the quality is there, but the players must show that they have the mentality and drive to bring success to the Yorkshire outfit on a daily basis.

The manner of the win at Barnsley on Friday was impressive, as Wednesday were ruthless early on, and then they saw the game very well, as they limited the Tykes to few clear opportunities.

There were great scenes at the end of the game as the players celebrated with the away supporters, and it’s victories like that which help strengthen the bond at the club.

Now, the challenge for Wednesday is to keep building momentum, and they could be difficult to stop if they do get on a roll.
