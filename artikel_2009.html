Arsenal were dealt an injury concern in the final game of the international break.

They will be sweating over the fitness of a midfield star, but worries were eased over Ezri Konsa, who returned to action in England’s 3-2 win in Spain.

The defender scored the second goal on a historic night for Thomas Tuchel’s men in Madrid, but one man was forced off with a problem in the second half.

With that in mind, here is the latest Arsenal injury news and return dates…

The midfielder hobbled off at the Estadio Metropolitano with what looked like a hamstring problem.

He was pictured grimacing and rubbing the back of his leg as he took his place on the bench after being replaced by Nico Williams.

It is unclear what the extent of his injury is, but it will be a worry for Mikel Arteta ahead of the trip to Newcastle.

Potential return date: Saturday November 21, vs Newcastle (A)

French international Saliba missed out on an international call-up, but stepped up his return from a back injury during the break.

He featured for Arsenal’s Under-21 side in a 5-2 win against Aston Villa having travelled to Prague for the Champions League clash against Slavia Prague.

He was substituted at half time, with head coach David Horseman saying that that had been the plan all along.

“He’s fine, by the way, it was all pre-planned, so nobody needs to panic,” Horseman said.

Saka has not featured for his boyhood club for the last five matches.

He picked up a quad issue against Bayern Munich in the Champions League, missing out on England’s Nations League finishers over the past week.

It had been first feared that the winger would be out for eight weeks, but Arteta has confirmed that the forward is recovering well.

He is still expected to be out for a number of weeks, however.
