Newcastle United played out an entertaining game against Arsenal in the Premier League on Saturday.

The Gunners were all over the hosts in the first half, but Matthias Jaissle's team turned things around.

It was astonishing to see the difference in performance from the Magpies in the second 45 minutes.

Eberechi Eze gave Arsenal the lead, but Dan Burn was left reeling after it at St James' Park.

After a disappointing first half from the hosts, Jaissle made three changes at the break, and they had an immediate impact as Harvey Barnes scored in the 49th minute.

Malick Thiaw and Noni Madueke also got on the scoresheet as the game ended 2-2, and here Football Insider look at some of the moments you may have missed from the game.

St James' Park was bouncing in the opening minutes against Arsenal and Newcastle players made sure to use that energy.

In the 16th minute, with momentum largely in favour of the Gunners, Amar Dedic decided to get the crowd going.

After a crunching challenge on Eze, which went out for a throw-in, the right-back got up and pumped his fists to the crowd, raising the decibels inside St James' Park.

Jurrien Timber and Riccardo Calafiori were Arsenal's right-back and left-back on paper against Newcastle, but Mikel Arteta seemed to have a surprising tactic up his sleeve.

The two full-backs often switched wings, causing mayhem for the hosts' defence.

In the 23rd minute, the Dutch defender was deep on the left-wing during Arsenal's attack.

Calafiori was also seen playing off the right on occasions, with Timber making overlapping runs from right-back.

Bruno Guimaraes was a clear target for the homes fans at St James' Park, with boos ringing out whenever he was on the ball.

However, in the 28th minute, as Declan Rice played a cross into the Newcastle box and it almost found the Brazil international, the boos were at their most intense.

There clearly seems to be no love lost between the Magpies' supporters and Guimaraes.

In the build-up to Arsenal's opening goal, Lewis Hall had the chance to clear the ball, but instead chose to get it under control.

Kai Havertz stole the ball away from the left-back and played Eze in behind, who made no mistake beating Hornicek.

Dan Burn screams at Lewis Hall, "head it, head it". Hall instead takes long ball on his chest, loses possession & that allows Havertz to thread Eze clear & he finishes first time. A soft concession but ruthlessly punished by Arsenal.

Daily Mail journalist Craig Hope shared on X that Burn screamed at Hall to head the ball, but the Englishman decided against it.
