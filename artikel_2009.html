Celtic contracts 26/27: Here are all 8 players out of contract at Parkhead in the summer

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Here are all 8 Celtic players who will see their current deal run out at Parkhead in summer 2027.

Celtic will be aiming they can keep their place at the top of the Scottish Premiership table and seal a sixth consecutive title win come the end of the current campaign.

Currently four points ahead of Rangers at the summit, Martin O’Neill’s men were pushed to the final day by Hearts last season, but sealed a dramatic title win with two late goals securing a 3-1 win victory on an emotional day in May. However, with the battle at the top almost as tight this season, they’ll be hoping they can pull ahead in the weeks to come.

It was a busy summer window at Parkhead, with the club shelling out over £20million on the likes of Kasper Hogh, Camilo Duran and Haissem Hassan. There were outgoings too, though, with Daizen Maeda, Arne Engels and Kasper Schmeichel all departing the club.
