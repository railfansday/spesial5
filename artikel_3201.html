South Bow Corporation (SOBO -7.17%) reported third quarter results that included an upward revision to its full-year distributable cash flow guidance and continued progress on its deleveraging targets. Management stated that normalized EBITDA reached $248 million for the period, with the company reaching a leverage ratio of 4.3x normalized EBITDA. The company indicated it is advancing the Prairie Connector project toward a mid-2027 final investment decision while maintaining focus on achieving regulatory certainty and permit durability. Management reported that Western Canadian oil production growth is creating increased demand for infrastructure expansions across the existing corridor.
