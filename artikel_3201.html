The Show Must Go On: Broadway Will Perform Saturday In Macy’s Thanksgiving Parade

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

New Yorkers woke up this Thanksgiving morning to drizzly, gray skies along with winds that had sidewalks covered in dead, yellow leaves, though the weather throughout the pre-noon hours was hardly the fearsome nor’easter blast that prompted the postponement till Saturday of Macy’s 100th Thanksgiving Day Parade.

Still, with wind levels of up to 23 mph – and gusts considerably stronger – the postponement appears to have been justified, and the worst of the storm is yet to come.

The impact of the postponement on performances that had been scheduled for today – marching bands, celebrity appearances, the big Broadway medley involving nine different shows – was still be dealt with as of this morning. Coss continued, “Our performers, our talent, our performance groups, our bands, we’re all coming together to try to figure this out, and we’re optimistic that we’re going to have a really successful parade on Saturday morning.” Watch on Deadline

While Coss didn’t specify which performances are on schedule, Deadline can confirm that the Broadway medley that was planned in lieu of the traditional individual show performances is a go for Saturday. What Macy’s is calling the “All-Star Broadway Thanksgiving Day Celebration” will go on as planned, with performers from The Lost Boys, The Rocky Horror Show, Wicked, Schmigadoon!, Aladdin, The Book of Mormon and Chicago confirmed. (Hamiilton and the 30th-anniversary tribute to Rent have not weighed in yet, but sources say they are expected to participate).

Paddington the Musical is confirmed to participate in the Saturday broadcast as a separate float, as originally intended.

On Instagram today, Sherri Shepherd, currently portraying Genie in Broadway’s Aladdin, wrote, “Winds won’t stop our participation in the Macys 100 Thanksgiving Day Parade, so tune in Saturday at 8:30am on NBC to catch myself and the cast of Aladdin perform!!”

On the Schmigadoon! IG page, Brad Oscar, dressed and in character as the Mayor, said in a video, “As mayor of Schmigadoon, I have a proclamation: The Macy’s 100th Thanksgiving Day Parade will be postponed until Saturday at 8:30 a.m. on NBC. See you there.”

The Book of Mormon had a simple message on its IG page about the date switch: “Ready and psyched.”

As for other non-Broadway performers originally scheduled for Thursday, reports are filtering in piecemeal on whether they’ll make the move to Saturday. The New York Post has reported that Smash Mouth will perform, and Holly Robinson Peete and Rodney Peete are expected as well, as are the Radio City Rockettes. Other announcements should be coming in over the next day or two.

As for the marching bands, again, piecemeal reports. Texas’ Pride of Pearland Marching Band reportedly raised an extra $350,000 in community donations to cover the additional housing and transportation costs to stay until Saturday, while other marching bands were either still scrambling or had made the decision to bow out of the event due to logistics. In the latter group are the Campo Verde High School Marching Band and some members of the Bowling Green State University Marching Band that were participating in a all-star band group.
