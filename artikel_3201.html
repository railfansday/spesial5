‘Saturday Night Live’ Taps Amanda Seyfried, Robert De Niro, Timothée Chalamet as Holiday Hosts

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Saturday Night Live has announced the schedule for the holiday run-in, with Amanda Seyfried, Robert De Niro, and Timothée Chalamet set to host, and Don Toliver, Slayyter, and Charli XCX performing as musical guests.

Seyfried and Don Toliver will anchor the Dec. 5 show, with both making their SNL debuts. For Seyfried, it’s a particularly unique gig, coming not in the middle of a traditional press tour, but as she earns rave reviews for her surprise turn in Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim’s You Can See Everything. The actress both appears as herself and reprises her role as the documentary’s subject, Elizabeth Holmes, in several memorable recreations.

As for Don Toliver, the star musician is enjoying a breakthrough year thanks to his hit album, Octane, and his upcoming LP, Nitrous, which is set to arrive on Dec. 4. Toliver was just nominated for five Grammy awards, including Best New Artist, Best Rap Album for Octane, and Best Rap Song for “E85.”

One of Toliver’s fellow Best New Artist nominees, Slayyyter, will make her SNL debut on the Dec. 12 show. Slayyyter will be paired with De Niro, an SNL vet who’s hosted the show three times before. While De Niro — who’s starring in the Meet the Parents sequel, Focker-In-Law — hasn’t hosted since 2010, he did make numerous cameos in 2018 and 2019, portraying Robert Mueller during the investigation into alleged Russian interference in the 2016 election.

Lastly, SNL’s final show before Christmas, Dec. 19, will feature Chalamet and Charli XCX, both making their fourth appearances. Chalamet will be promoting Dune: Part Three, and he last appeared on the show in January 2025, when he was both host and musical guest while promoting the Bob Dylan biopic, A Complete Unknown. Coincidentally, the last time Charli was on SNL was November 2024, when she was also serving as host and musical guest.
