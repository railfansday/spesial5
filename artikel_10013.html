Vanessa Formicola shares how technical leaders can move beyond "wishful architecture" by practicing holistic socio-technical engineering. Vanessa Formicola is an Engineering Leader and Sociotechnical Architect with 15+ years of experience across big tech at Microsoft, consulting at Thoughtworks, and Flo Health. Specializing in sociotechnical approaches to software delivery and talent growth, she is a skilled technologist and leader comfortable bridging the gap between complex system design and high-performing team dynamics.
