Bobby Bones Claps Back at Claims He Made Ezra Frech's 'DWTS' Win About Himself

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Former Dancing With the Stars winner Bobby Bones is addressing claims he made Ezra Frech’s season 35 win about himself.

Bones, 46, took to Instagram on Wednesday, November 24, to respond to a social media user who wrote, “I’m usually not a negative person, but why are you making this about you. History was just made.”

“My bet is you are usually a negative person,” Bones replied in his video. “However, for those that didn’t see, I posted a picture last night as the champion was crowned of me holding up my trophy with the joke, ‘Hey, I’m still the worst ever.’ A joke. A nod to all the people that voted for me … By the worst — I mean change how the show, the rules were made. Like, we did something no one’s ever done in the history of that show.”

He continued, “And congratulations to Ezra. But if I want to post a picture of me on my page of me holding the mirrorball, that’s cool. You are usually a negative person, I bet. I’m also making it about me because it’s my page. It’s my page. That’s like you coming into my house, being like, ‘Why are you making this place about you?’ It’s my house, freaking loser.”

Bones clarified that he didn’t think the person was “a loser,” but likely “just having a bad day.” Or, he speculated, “one of those people that you go, ‘the internet’s so toxic,’ and then you’re the toxic one.’”

“But congrats to Ezra. Great job. Also, yeah, still the worst dancer,” he concluded, captioned the video, “I got thoughts! Big dancing with the stars day.”

Bones famously won the mirrorball trophy with Sharna Burgess in 2018, a controversial victory that has continued to be discussed through the years after receiving the lowest combined judges’ score of the four finale couples. This year, Frech, 21, took home the season 35 title with partner Daniella Karagach and made history as the first amputee and youngest man to win DWTS.

“Last night was crazy. … I can’t believe this happened,” Frech said on Good Morning America on Wednesday. “I can’t believe we got that opportunity to represent [the disabled community].”

While taking the ballroom floor for the last time, Frech and Karagach, 33, performed a judges choice dance, instant dance and the fan-favorite freestyle — earning a combined judges score of 90 from Carrie Ann Inaba, Bruno Tonioli and Derek Hough. During the freestyle, the Paralympian struggled to get his prosthetic blade onto his leg and briefly danced without it — which was not part of the plan.

“We started our freestyle without the prosthetic, that was a creative choice, and then things went a little off script,” Frech said on GMA. “I wasn’t able to suction my leg on and I knew it wasn’t on. Sometimes when that happens, it’ll re-suction, but it didn’t. It came off. She told me to put it on mid-dance, I kinda blacked out. When it came off, I was like, ‘I’m doing the rest of the dance without my leg on.’ And I heard [Daniella] go, ‘Put your leg on!'”

Karagach added, “I saw his face, I was like, ‘If I don’t tell him to put his leg on, he’s gonna do the whole dance without it.'”
