Miley Cyrus Getting Married to Maxx Morando in Palm Springs This Weekend

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Miley Cyrus will be tying the knot with fiancé Maxx Morando this weekend in Southern California ... and TMZ has learned the nuptials are going down in Palm Springs.

Sources with direct knowledge tell TMZ ... Miley and Maxx have chosen the desert destination for their big day, where they'll exchange vows surrounded by family and friends.

As we first reported ... the couple was planning to tie the knot somewhere in Southern California this weekend, but the exact location had remained under wraps ... until now.

Miley's no stranger to the desert ... she's spent time in Joshua Tree and Palm Springs over the years, including attending the Palm Springs International Film Festival in January.

Miley and Maxx are already celebrating ... the couple was spotted arriving at their rehearsal dinner Friday night alongside her mom, Tish Cyrus, and sister Noah Cyrus.

And guests were eating pretty well ... photos of the dinner menu showed filet mignon and Maine lobster ravioli among the options.

As you know ... Miley -- who was previously married to Liam Hemsworth from 2018 to 2020 -- got engaged to the rocker late last year.

Here's an interesting twist ... back in 2013, Miley and Liam were reportedly planning to get married in Palm Springs before putting their wedding plans on hold that year.
