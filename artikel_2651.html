Arizona women’s basketball signs top 15 class for 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Arizona head coach Becky Burke welcomed a top 20 class this season. The class she signed for next season is even better.

Burke signed a class of four on Wednesday, Nov. 11. The 2027 group is ranked 10th by ESPN and 15th by 247sports. ESPN gives all four signees four-star ratings.

The 2026 class, which was ranked No. 17 by ESPN and No. 20 by 247sports, had a heavy focus on athletic guards. The three top-ranked recruits—Jasleen Green, Makayla Presser-Palmer, and Simba Finley—are all listed as either a guard or guard/forward on this year's roster. Next year, the focus is on size.

"I think we we addressed that with the 2026 class," Burke said. "I think we addressed it even more with the 2027 class, and then just people that give us different looks. We didn't sign another Jas in the 2027 class. We signed a player that could complement her very well. We didn't sign a Bre [Williams]. We signed another big that will complement her well. So, really getting a variety of skill sets. You know, getting people that are going to complement one another versus just tacking the same players on top of each other. But we were 6, 6-1, 6-3, 6-4. I mean, with those four, so yeah, we definitely did address the the length and size for sure."

There is disagreement about which signee is the headliner of the class, although the majority leans towards 6-foot small forward/wing Brieana Brown. Others put 6-foot-3 post Celsy Colombo in that position. The other two members of the group are 6-foot wing Corinne Lomax and 6-foot-2 forward Ashley Vande Ven.

Brown comes in as the 40th-ranked player in the ESPN Sportscenter Next 100. That's the highest-ranked recruit of Burke's career.

Rivals is the next service that has Brown as Arizona's top recruit, but there is conflict about the rankings on that website. On the main recruiting rankings, Rivals has her at No. 46 overall, No. 11 at the small forward position, and No. 4 in the state of California. On her individual recruit page, the service places her at No. 57 overall, No. 12 at the small forward position, and No. 5 in the state of California.

Prep Girls Hoops also puts Brown atop the list of Arizona recruits. That service places her No. 74 overall.

While Prospects Nation doesn't have Brown as the top player in the class, she isn't far behind one of her teammates. She is the No. 47 player overall and No. 16 forward according to that service.

247sports and Prospects Nation both have Colombo as the top incoming recruit. ESPN does not rank her among the top 100, but she does have a four-star rating on the site.

Prospects Nation has the post as the No. 36 player in the class. She's listed as the No. 11 power forward.

247sports has Colombo No. 38 nationally, No. 10 at the power forward position, and No. 2 in the state of New York in its internal rankings. The composite, which is an average of her rankings across several services, has her at No. 48 overall, No. 11 at her position, and No. 2 in the state. Colombo is the only player in the class ranked by 247sports.

Lomax is second on the list of Arizona recruits according to ESPN. The wing is ranked No. 65 overall. While Prospects Nation does not rank her among its top 60 players, she is one of its Elite 150 which includes players who don't receive individual rankings but are considered among the best prospects in the class.

Vande Ven comes in at No. 82 according to ESPN. The Canadian is also listed in the Elite 150 of Prospects Nation.

With two strong high school classes back-to-back and a transfer class that included several players with multiple years of eligibility, Burke is looking towards the future. She knows it's tough to keep a group intact these days, though.

"I was telling Bre a couple weeks ago, if this group wants to stick around to build something special here in Arizona, they have that opportunity to do that," Burke said. "This thing could get really fun and really scary in a hurry…but these days, it takes a committed group that care about the right things and really, really, truly want to build this thing and stick around."
