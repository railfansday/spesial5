LeBron James just played his best game as a 76er with 25-point triple double

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

No Joel Embiid. Jaylen Brown leaves at halftime with a hamstring injury. Philadelphia was facing a red-hot Miami team that had won seven straight behind Giannis Antetokounmpo.

LeBron had a 25-point triple-double — including scoring 14 straight in the second quarter — to lift the 76ers to a 111-104 NBA Cup win at home. That was LeBron's first triple-double as a 76er, coming one month before he turns 42.

"Father Time always wins. But right now, he's giving Father Time hell," Heat coach (and former LeBron coach) Erik Spoelstra said before the game.

This has been an impressive start from the 76ers, who are 15-4 and comfortably atop the Eastern Conference. They're also 2-1 in NBA Cup Group B play and can win the group and advance to the knockout round by beating Indiana on Friday night.
