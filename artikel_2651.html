Predicting Week 13 Big Ten Football Game Times

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Illinois was unable to pull off the upset in Los Angeles on Friday night losing 22-14. The Illini showed promise late in the game but the offense was not good enough to beat the No. 4 ranked team in the nation. Week 13 will be the final game of the season for the Illini. Illinois is 6-4 and clinched bowl eligibility but winning their last two games would give them momentum going into the bowl game.

There will be nine Big Ten games in Week 13, with two happening on Friday night. The Big Ten slate will be carried across FOX, FS1, NBC, CBS, Peacock and the Big Ten Network, as usual.

Minnesota at Wisconsin — 6:30 p.m. on NBC

Michigan at Ohio State — 11:00 a.m. on FOX (Big Noon Kick Off)

Penn State at Maryland — 11:30 a.m. on Big Ten Network

Michigan State at Rutgers — 2:30 p.m. on FS1

Illinois at Northwestern— 3:15 p.m. on Big Ten Network
