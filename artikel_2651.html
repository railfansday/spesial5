Rams upgrade RB Kyren Williams to full participant on Monday's injury report

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

While any injury news about the Rams has largely been bad this season, there was a positive development on the team’s Monday practice report.

Running back Kyren Williams (toe) was upgraded from a limited to a full participant in the day’s session.
