Jayden Daniels passed for 146 yards in fourth quarter after totaling 143 in first three

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

When the fourth quarter started on Monday night, the Commanders trailed the Bengals 17-13. By the end of the fourth quarter, Jayden Daniels had led three scoring drives, and the Commanders won 22-20.

Daniels passed for more yardage in the fourth quarter than he had in the first three quarters combined on Monday night, delivering in the clutch in a season when he has frequently been criticized for failing to do so.

In the first three quarters, Daniels was 13-for-19 for 143 yards. In the fourth quarter, Daniels was 10-for-16 for 146 yards.

Daniels got better as the game went on. The Commanders punted on their first four offensive possessions, then scored on their last six. Their three fourth-quarter drives went 13 plays for 78 yards, 11 plays for 66 yards, and finally a sensational 64-yard drive that consisted of a 22-yard pass from Daniels to Antonio Williams, a spike, a 42-yard pass from Daniels to Williams, another spike, and the game-wining field goal as time expired.
