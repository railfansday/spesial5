NFL betting, odds, lines: Betting trends to know for Week 12's biggest games

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

A week after serving up one lopsided matchup after another, the NFL has cooked up a mouthwatering Thanksgiving Week feast. Which means we're dishing out a heaping platter of NFL betting trends targeting all five midweek games on the Week 12 menu.

The festivities begin with a clash of two NFC playoff hopefuls in the first Thanksgiving Eve contest. That's followed by back-to-back Thanksgiving Day battles between NFC division rivals (including one with first place on the line).

Then, just as the second slice of pumpkin pie hits your plate Thursday night, two AFC teams with a combined 16-3 record square off in Western New York. Capping the midweek football frenzy (and our Week 12 NFL betting trends report): another matchup of AFC playoff contenders, this one on Black Friday.

• The Week 12 slate kicks off Wednesday night with a pair of teams that are 6-4 SU and ATS and coming off a bye.

Green Bay entered its bye off a surprising 30-10 blowout loss to division rival Minnesota as a 2.5-point home favorite. It was just the Packers' second loss in their last seven games and also snapped a 6-0 ATS run.

Meanwhile, the Rams emerge from their week off riding a season-best four-game winning SU/ATS streak. Three of those were road victories (35-21 at Las Vegas, 20-13 at Washington and 34-6 at Arizona), with the other a 24-9 home rout of the Chargers.

• The Packers have cashed in each of their last three games as an underdog (2-1 SU).

Also, since starting the season with non-covers in road games at the Vikings (39-22 loss as a 2.5-point favorite) and Jets (20-17 win as a 3.5-point road chalk), Green Bay has gone 3-0 ATS as a visitor (2-1 SU).

The Packers haven't covered the spread in four consecutive road contests since late in the 2009 campaign.

• This is the fourth time in as many seasons in which Los Angeles has enjoyed a 4-0 ATS run.

During this time, the Rams' lengthiest point spread hot streak was five games in 2023 (Weeks 12-16). It's their only 5-0 ATS heater since early in 2003.

Another NFL betting trend worth nothing: The Rams were favored in all four games during their current 4-0 ATS surge. They haven't covered five in a row as a favorite since the 1999 Super Bowl-winning team went 7-0 ATS from Weeks 10-16.

• These teams squared off in five straight seasons from 2020-2024 (including a 2020 playoff matchup). The Packers were favored in four of the games and went 5-0 SU and ATS.

In fact, since 2007 and including the postseason clash in 2020, Green Bay is 9-1 SU and 10-0 ATS against the Rams, including 5-0 SU and ATS on the road.

• The under is 6-1 in the Packers' last seven and 3-1 in their last four on the highway. What's more, six of Green Bay's last eight overall — including three of four on the road — have fallen shy of 40 combined points.

Meanwhile, the under is 3-1 this year when L.A. plays at SoFi Stadium. Final scores in those four contests: 28-6 (Giants), 37-34 (Bills in overtime), 18-16 (Cardinals) and 24-9 (Chargers).

• Chicago (8-2 SU, 6-4 ATS) ran its winning streak to five in a row with Sunday's 21-13 victory over the visiting Saints. However, the Bears failed to cover as an 11.5-point favorite, their second straight point-spread setback (following a 5-1 ATS run).

Detroit (8-2 SU, 5-4-1 ATS) picked up its sixth straight victory — and improved to 5-0 at home this season — in Week 11, dusting the Buccaneers 31-21 as a 3.5-point home chalk.

The Lions have followed a 1-4-1 ATS start with a four-game spread-covering streak. They haven't covered in five straight games since a 6-0 ATS run from Weeks 3-9 in 2024.

• Detroit has won seven of the last eight meetings with the Bears (5-3 ATS), including an ongoing four-game winning streak (3-1 ATS).

This NFL betting trend also favors the Lions: The underdog has covered the spread in nine of the last 13 in this rivalry, with five outright victories.

• Since Dan Campbell took over as head coach in 2021, Detroit has gone 1-5 SU and 2-4 ATS on Thanksgiving Day (including three non-covers in the last three years).

The Lions' lone SU victory under Campbell: 23-20 over the Bears as an 8.5-point favorite in 2024. That's one of four Thanksgiving Day meetings between these teams since 2018.

Over this stretch, Chicago is 3-1 SU and Detroit is 3-1 ATS, while the underdog covered the spread in each of the last three meetings. Underdogs went 3-0 SU and ATS on Thanksgiving Day in 2025.

• Going back to the end of last season, the Lions' last four division games have stayed under the total (2-0 under this year). None of the four reached 38 combined points.

The under has cashed in each of the Bears' last four against NFC North foes. All failed to clear 38 total points.

• Since the 2022-23 season, the over is 8-4 in Thanksgiving Day games.

Also, three of the last four Turkey Day games at Ford Field have cleared 50 total points. The one exception: the Lions' 23-20 win over Chicago in 2024.

• Philadelphia halted a seven-game losing skid — its longest since an eight-game slump in 2012 — with a 19-16 Week 11 upset of instate rival Pittsburgh as a 3.5-point home underdog.

Not only did the Eagles also snap a four-game ATS drought, it was just their second spread cover in 10 games this season. At 2-8 ATS, Philadelphia remains the second least-profitable team in the NFL, ahead of only the Chargers (1-8-1 ATS).

• Dallas rebounded from a 46-40 Week 10 loss to San Francisco as a 3.5-point home underdog with a 51-30 blowout of Tennessee as a 7.5-point home favorite. It's the first time since Sept. 1971 that the franchise put up 91 points in consecutive games.

The Cowboys have been spinning their wheels all season, going 5-6 on the field and 5-5-1 in the wagering department.

They've alternated SU wins and losses in their last six contests, and haven't recorded back-to-back wins or spread covers since a 3-0 SU/ATS run from Weeks 12-14 last season.

That's also the last time Dallas — which snapped an 0-3 SU/ATS home slump against the Titans — won or covered consecutive games at AT&T Stadium.

• With their blowout of Tennessee, the Cowboys ended an 0-7 ATS slump as a favorite of five points or more (one playoff game included). They lost five of those seven games outright.

Meanwhile, the Eagles have been an underdog of more than a touchdown just once in the regular season since the start of 2021: Back in Week 5 of this season, they went to London as a 7.5-point pup and lost 19-17 to Jacksonville on a last-second 57-yard field goal.

Dak Prescott of the Dallas Cowboys was feeling it against the Tennessee Titans.

• Dallas traveled to Philadelphia in Week 7 and cruised to a 38-21 victory as a 4.5-point favorite.

The Cowboys have won the last two head-to-head meetings and covered the spread in the last three. Going back to 2012, Dallas is 17-12 SU and 18-11 ATS in this NFC East rivalry, including 7-1 SU and ATS at home since 2018.

• The Cowboys are 34-22-1 all time on Thanksgiving Day, which includes an active four-game winning streak.

Dallas is 3-1 ATS during this recent stretch, including comfortable wins over division rivals New York (27-20 in 2024 and 28-20 in 2023) and Washington (45-10 in 2023).

Oddly, the Cowboys and Eagles have only hooked up two previous times on Thanksgiving. Philly recorded lopsided victories in both (27-0 in 1989 and 33-10 in 2014).

• A totals-related NFL betting trend to consider: All six of Dallas' Thanksgiving games this decade have cleared the total. Combined points scored: 57, 69, 48, 55, 47 and 59.

The over is also 26-9-1 in the Cowboys' last 36 games overall (8-3 this season), including 11-5-1 at home (4-1 this season) and 10-3 in divisional games (3-0 this season).

On the other hand, the under has hit in three straight Eagles-Cowboys clashes in Texas. The teams combined for 46, 40 and 45 points in those contests.

• The NFL's three-course Thanksgiving meal concludes with a delicious dessert, as two perennial powerhouses vying for the AFC's top seed meet in the regular season for the sixth year in a row.

Kansas City (8-2 SU, 6-4 ATS) arrives in Buffalo having followed its only two defeats with a trio of blowout victories. In successive weeks, the Chiefs pasted the Jets 35-12 (home), Falcons (35-13 road) and Cardinals (34-7) — easily covering in all three games.

Buffalo won its sixth straight game in Week 11, pounding the Dolphins 26-9 as a 16.5-point home favorite. The Bills' 9-1 SU record matches San Francisco for best in the NFL, and their 8-2 ATS mark is tied with Denver for tops in the league.

During their current winning streak, Buffalo is 5-1 ATS. The last four victories were double-digit triumphs by the combined score of 116-37.

• Kansas City's current 3-0 ATS run is its longest since Weeks 6-8 last season. The franchise hasn't covered four straight games since going 6-0 ATS from Weeks 10-16 in 2021 (bye week included).

Also, with QB Patrick Mahomes under center, K.C. is 12-5 SU and 12-4-1 ATS all time as an underdog (four postseason games included).

However, one of those defeats occurred five weeks ago, when the Chiefs went to Seattle and got mauled 20-3 as a 3-point pup.

This week, K.C. faces a Buffalo squad that is 6-1 SU/ATS as a favorite of 3.5 points or fewer since last season kicked off (4-0 SU/ATS this year).

• The Bills are 4-1 SU/ATS in brand-new Highmark Stadium. The lone blemish: a 29-26 loss to New England as a 7-point chalk in Week 4.

Meanwhile, Kansas City's only two losses were as a visitor. It followed the 17-point defeat at Seattle with a 28-24 setback at Denver as a 1-point favorite.

Otherwise, the Chiefs are 3-0 SU and ATS on the road with a plus-55 point differential.

• Since 2021, the Bills have won and covered five straight regular-season meetings against Kansas City.

That includes consecutive home wins the last two years (30-21 as a 2.5-point favorite in 2024 and 28-21 as a 2.5-point underdog in 2025).

However, come must-win time in January, the Chiefs have owned Buffalo, going 4-0 SU and ATS since 2020.

Josh Allen of the Buffalo Bills runs onto the field before the start of the game against the Miami Dolphins at Highmark Stadium on Sunday.

• The over cashed in five of the Bills' first six contests this season, but the under has hit in each of their last four.

Also, the under is 16-5 in the Chiefs' last 21 outings, including last year's 28-21 loss at Buffalo that fell short of the 52.5-point total.

In fact, each of the last three Kansas City-Buffalo battles in the regular season have stayed under, with the teams tallying 37, 51 and 49 points.

That's in sharp contrast to the four postseason meetings this decade, which saw final scores of 38-24, 42-36, 27-24 and 32-29. All four hurdled the total.

• Except for the 2020 COVID season, the Thanksgiving Night contest has been played every year since 2006. And the related NFL betting trends are eye-openers.

First, favorites are 15-4 SU and 14-5 ATS, including 8-1 SU and ATS in the last nine. The lone outlier: Cincinnati's 32-14 rout of the Ravens as a 7.5-point underdog last season.

The under has cashed in 13 of the 19 prime-time Thanksgiving games, including nine of the last 11. And only three games eclipsed 48 total points: Philadelphia's 48-20 pounding of the Cardinals in 2008; New England's 49-19 rout of the Jets in 2012; and Minnesota's 33-26 win over the Patriots in 2022.

• Pittsburgh's playoff hopes have taken a hit the last two weeks, as it followed a 4-0 SU and ATS run with losses at Cincinnati (38-18 as a 4.5-point underdog in Week 10) and Philadelphia (19-16 as a 3.5-point favorite in Week 11).

The Steelers haven't suffered a three-game SU or ATS skid since dropping their final four games of the 2024-25 season (1-3 ATS).

• While Pittsburgh limps into the NFL's fourth straight Black Friday game, the Broncos are humming, having won and covered six in a row. The latest: Sunday's 38-24 post-bye-week victory over the Raiders as an 11.5-point home favorite.

With that, Denver improved to 8-2 SU (tied with the Chiefs atop the NFC West) and 8-2 ATS (tied with Buffalo for the best point spread record).

Also, since dropping their first two road games, the Broncos are 3-0 SU and ATS on the road.

• The Steelers are 4-0 SU and ATS at home this season. They also have won all three head-to-head meetings with the Broncos this decade, including a 13-6 upset as a 2.5-point road underdog early in the 2024 season.

However, this negative NFL betting trend hovers over the six-time Super Bowl champs coming into this matchup: Even with the 2024 upset at Denver, they're mired in a 5-12 SU and 6-11 ATS funk as an underdog (including two playoff games).

Oddly, the winner covered the spread in 16 of those 17 contests.

• Pittsburgh has followed a 4-0 over stretch with a 3-1 under run. All three unders ended with fewer than 36 total points.

Conversely, Denver's last five games have hurdled the closing total with 46, 52, 47 and 62 combined points. Compare that with the Broncos' first six contests, five of which failed to eclipse 41 total points.

The Broncos haven't had a 5-0 over run since Weeks 5-9 in 2024.

• In the NFL's inaugural Black Friday game in 2023, the Dolphins traveled to New York and spanked the Jets 34-13. Miami easily covered as a 9.5-point road favorite, and the game flew past the 40.5-point total.

However, the underdog covered in the two most recent Black Friday battles (one outright upset). Both stayed under the total.
