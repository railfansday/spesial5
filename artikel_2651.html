Just another average Sunday in Buffalo? The NFL record books disagree

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Buffalo Bills added another win to their record Sunday. Once again, it was a very awkward, weird game day where Josh Allen was sacked too many times, threw a pair of interceptions, but still managed to set a brand-new NFL record. Greg Rousseau turned in another multi-sack performance, and the Bills' defense still hasn't given up a touchdown. Yet, somehow, another backup quarterback managed to find openings and move his offense down the field enough to make things uncomfortable.

But, much like the week before when the Bills handled business against the Jets, the outcome never really felt in question. Stalled drives were more of an annoyance than a problem. Scores were more of an expectation than a celebration. And, in the end, the Bills walked off the field with a 9-1 record and woke up Monday morning as the best team in the NFL.

So, with another win in the books, it's time to hand out a few more Shout!outs.

_____________________________________________________________________________

It's been 35 years since the Bills started a season this well. And, with Sunday's win over Miami, the 2026 team has officially matched a mark that hasn't been reached in Buffalo since the days of Jim Kelly, Thurman Thomas, and company.

Buffalo Bills — Consecutive winning seasons

It's not just this season that's worth celebrating. The Bills have now put together eight consecutive winning seasons, extending their own franchise record. That kind of consistency is hard to come by in the NFL, and right now, nobody else in the league has managed to sustain it for as long.

Joe Brady just keeps winning, and the list of coaches who can say they started their careers the way he has is getting pretty short. The Bills' rookie head coach has joined some impressive company, and he's only 10 games into the job.

Buffalo Bills offense — Consecutive scoring quarters

The Bills' offense hasn't always been pretty, and there have definitely been a few frustrating stretches along the way. But even when things aren't clicking, Buffalo has found a way to put points on the board.

Josh Allen has made a habit of putting up impressive numbers against the Miami Dolphins. While Sunday's game wasn't exactly one of his cleanest performances, he still managed to extend a streak that no other active quarterback has matched.

James Cook is having himself quite a season. He's finding the end zone every week, piling up rushing yards, and putting his name alongside some of the best running backs to ever wear a Bills uniform. He's doing a pretty good job of rewriting the NFL record books with Josh Allen, too.

One sack just isn't enough for Greg Rousseau anymore. The Bills' edge rusher has been making a habit of bringing down opposing quarterbacks multiple times a game, and nobody in the NFL has done it more often this season.

You don't have to be a starter to make a big impact, and Joe Andreessen has been proving that for two seasons now. The Bills' linebacker has quietly become one of the NFL's most productive special teams players, and Sunday's performance added a couple more tackles to his growing total.

Who had Tommy Doman throwing one of the longest passes of the afternoon on their bingo card? The Bills' punter got his chance to show off his arm Sunday.

Buffalo Bills — An unusual passing record

Speaking of unusual passing statistics, Sunday's game put the Bills in a rather exclusive club. It isn't often that a quarterback throws for more than 300 yards and gets a little help through the air from his team's kicking unit. In fact, it's almost never happened.

_____________________________________________________________________________

The last few weeks may have felt a little average to Bills fans. But when you look at all these records, milestones, and accomplishments, it's worth remembering that what we've come to consider an average Sunday in Buffalo is anything but average around the rest of the NFL.
