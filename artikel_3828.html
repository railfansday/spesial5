Star Wars: Galactic Racer studio "didn't dream" Biddy Blas would be as popular as he is

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

"He's always known exactly who he is, and he wears it proudly."

Fuse Games, the developer of Star Wars: Galactic Racer, has said it "didn't dream" the pod racing game's background character Biddy Blas would become as popular as he has.

Since Galactic Racer's release in October, Blas - who resembles a short, humanoid crocodile (officially a crocin) - has become a viral sensation. The combination of his unexpected swagger and ridiculous high-pitched Huttese monologues - performed irresistibly by actor Richard Summers-Calvert - have made him a fan favourite, especially his catchphrase "scrib doon" (which means "write it down").

"Here's a name for you, amateur!" Blas tells the player upon meeting them, albeit in Huttese. "Biddy Blas. Write it down. Tattoo it on your arm, and someday your arm will be worth millions!"

Fuse Games shared a comment from Biddy Blas creator and Galactic Racer narrative designer Ren Alex-Rose about the character's success. "We always suspected Biddy Blas would be a popular member of the Galactic League, but we didn't dream of just how popular he'd become," Alex-Rose said.

"He was an incredibly enjoyable character to build, from our early ideation of a small, angry non-human character (who we lovingly referred to as our cast's 'chaos goblin') to recording his sometimes-absurdist dialogue with Blas' voice actor, who took the crocin to a whole new level.

"It's been great watching Biddy Blas emerge into his assured self, as well as seeing the community embrace him for it. He's always known exactly who he is, and he wears it proudly."

Blas' breakout success was praised by Lucasfilm producer Craig Derrick, who said his social feeds were filled with posts about Galactic Racer, though mostly about Biddy Blas. And the enthusiasm actor Richard Summers-Calvert was greeted with during October's MCM Comic-Con in London, which I attended, was palpable. There's a nice video of him doing Blas' voice on Instagram.

Star Wars: Galactic Racer was awarded a full five stars in Eurogamer's review. "Both a surprisingly fun Roguelite campaign and a racing treat for Criterion fans, this is just wonderful stuff," wrote Christian Donlan.
