All Fishing Rods, Upgrades, and how they work in DREADMOOR

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

DREADMOOR is a mix of horror and fishing, and there are different rods to craft. These rods also offer various upgrades to improve their potential. The key is to first get the recipes from the trader and then craft them.

Let’s find out everything to know about the recipes, how they work, and how much will you need to shell out for them.

Once you’re past the opening area of the game, you can then visit the shop as your first piece of business. Scroll down to the consumables section, and you’ll find all the applicable purchases related to the Fishing Rod.

The first rod is available for free, and you’ll get it from the main campaign. However, the rest of the upgrades have to be unlocked and crafted from its respective recipes.
