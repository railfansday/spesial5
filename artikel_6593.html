Candied Sweet Potatoes

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Candied sweet potatoes are a Thanksgiving classic that you just have to have on your table—we don’t make the rules! Their syrupy goodness makes for the perfect side that’ll have all your guests asking for seconds. But these roasted potatoes are also too delicious to just have during the holidays. While they might be extra-sweet on their own (they're practically a dessert), they pair perfectly with savory mains like roast chicken, pot roast, or prime rib, too! Trust us, with a brown sugar, apple cider, and bourbon glaze, you'll want these sweet potatoes with everything.

Start by preheating your oven to 400°. Grab a medium baking dish and give it a good brush with butter so nothing sticks.

Melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Next, whisk in the brown sugar, apple cider, cinnamon, and the bourbon if you’re using it. Let everything come to a gentle boil, then turn the heat down a bit and let it simmer for about 2 minutes until it thickens up just a little.

Layer your potatoes in that buttery dish, sprinkle them with some salt and pepper.

Then pour the warm syrup right over the top. Cover the dish with foil and pop it in the oven for 30 minutes. Take off the foil, give everything a good stir, and put it back in the oven.

Bake until the sweet potatoes are tender and coated in that delicious sauce, about 40 more minutes.

Let the candied sweet potatoes cool completely, then transfer them to an airtight container and refrigerate for up to 4 days.
