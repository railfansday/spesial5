27 Luxurious (and Truly Over-the-Top) Beauty and Wellness Gifts

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Some people are fans of practical gifts. Others want an experience, like a beach getaway or ski trip. One of my favorite gifts to receive? The ability to be a voyeur into your holiday season.

I live for social media posts of ornament-laden trees sparkling over piles of color-coordinated packages or perfectly curated table settings. Ditto Instagram stories featuring people unwrapping presents with nary a care for their delicate camellia bows or designer ribbons. (“Save them!” I silently scream, proving my grandmother is still with me in spirit.) Every time a holiday rolls around, I’m waiting to see what the Kardashians will get up to—matching golf carts for all the kids on Christmas? Hermès china, ultimately returned? A suite of identical puppies? Show me now.

In the off season (aka January to October), I fantasize about what will be next—what are the greatest gifts to give if there were no budget restrictions? Once, I was told there was “no budget” when it came to a shopping experience (and if the person who uttered the phrase to me unwisely assumed I would be at least sensible, no dice)—I applied the same mantra to these beauty and wellness items in this story.

The following gifts are a short and sweet selection of beauty and wellness offerings of Kardashian-level caliber—so fanciful, delightful, extravagant and extreme that any beauty lover will be wowed … along with anybody who is following along on social media, too.

There is, in fact, a gift for your sardine-obsessed, protein-pilled family member. This very cute, very heavy advent calendar from Los Angeles-based Fishwife has everything from their classic Sardines (filled with vitamin D, B12, and omega-3 fatty acids, as well as essential minerals including iodine, selenium, iron, calcium, and potassium, phew!) to an adorable keychain and the collaboration with Bag Balm.

If The Glucose Goddess’s decision to take a hunk of parmesan cheese to Fashion Week inspired anybody in your life to double down on this high-protein, low-lactose cheese, why not give them what I imagine could be … well, at least a few months’ supply? It’s 80 pounds, which means there’s about 13,000 grams of protein inside the rind.

You want to touch grass. You also don’t want to touch grass with your bare feet, because you live in New York. Instead, try a pair of barefoot shoes that are fashion-forward and made of luxurious cashmere.

Poor fruitcake, it’s always the butt of the joke when it comes to holiday sweets. But this one from perennially chic Flamingo Estate will not only wow visually, but perhaps even change people’s tune. Created in collaboration with the actually quite Santa Claus-looking Shannon Swindle, the pastry chef at trendy LA hotspot Funke, there are plenty of fruits in this cake (as the name would imply). But more importantly? It’s soaked in a lot of dark rum.

A collab between Nike and fitness tech company Hyperice, these slides were made to help athletes recover from long days on their feet—and yes, that includes women wearing heels all day, too. There’s a tiny pod inside each slide that warms and vibrates, helping to relax tired muscles, though if you just have chronically cold feet, we imagine these would be nice, too.

Lymphatic drainage was one of 2026’s buzziest beauty trends. This is the gift for the man or woman who has been dry-brushing since Gwyneth Paltrow first uttered the word on Goop. Made in France, the palm-sized lacquered brush (which comes in two shades—aubergine and powder pink) is totally vegan and top-shelf worthy.

This tabletop water purifier removes 80 potential contaminants from your drinking water (like microplastics and PFAS) as well as tracks your daily ounces.

Yes, it sort of looks like that creature from Stranger Things or a pop-art object. But Dyson’s newest air purifier is here to do the lord’s work (especially as the EPA continues to face budget cuts). It can work across multiple rooms (great for those of us who live in a tiny New York apartment), heats and cools, and reports back what’s in the air at any given moment.

Is it sexy to gift an alarm clock? Maybe not. But you know what the sexiest thing of all is? Ditching your phone in another room and getting an incredible night’s sleep. This alarm clock can actually help with those things, along with making waking up easier (it has a two-phase alarm that will gently wake you up, not startle).

Fitness trackers can be ugly, it’s true. But if you’re going to truly commit to wearing it every day (ahem), might as well spruce it up a bit. This cover is made of 14k gold and step-cut diamonds (that’s my favorite kind!), but the Henri Noël team can also do custom orders if sapphires, emeralds, or rubies are more your vibe.

One of the most ubiquitous beauty products in the world is a nail cutter—but that doesn’t mean it has to be basic or utilitarian. Instead, any nail lover will love this gold and monogrammed five-piece kit from Louis Vuitton, which has everything you need to fix a manicure emergency (scissors, tweezers, nail clipper, and nail file) and could probably help you survive if stranded on a desert island, too.

The Sisters of the Valley are Brides of Christ … who love weed. This handmade salve is made with hemp-based CBD that is harvested during the moon cycle (to give it peak potency), along with coconut oil, vitamin E, and calendula. Hail Mary!

Red light therapy is scientifically proven to reduce redness, inflammation, fine lines, and more—and while the face devices are great, why stop there? Instead, work smarter, not harder, with this combo: The Celluma shield mask is lightweight and flexible so you can bend it to cover your face and scalp, while the Bon Charge mask covers you from neck to toes. I like how this combo truly forces you to slow down (try doing TCM meditation or listen to a podcast) for at least 20 minutes a day.

You loved your trip to Fiji. The sensible next step? A fragrance that reminds you of the divine time. The juice inside this frosted glass bottle smells just like a giant arrangement of incredible white flowers—jasmine, white gardenia, and frangipani.

Mrs. Prada’s sustainable spirit has made its way to your lunchbox, too. The stainless steel pail, made in collaboration with Black + Blum, can be divided into two portions, and comes with a set of utensils, so you can eat anywhere with style. Perfect for your bean salad, wheat meat, or retinol-rich diet.

For the beauty lover who is obsessed with logomania—the Hermès lash curler, which has an orange pad so you can go all-in on the brand’s signature shade.

Halle Berry loves her Eight Sleep (even saying that it helped her through menopause), and I do, too. The latest version of the cooling-heating-moving-sleep tracking mattress pad is smaller than ever—perfect for Manhattan apartments—and can help you get a great night’s sleep.

If you’re going to Korea for any type of beauty treatment, a translator is a must-have (especially if you’re interested in doing work on your face!). Jennifer Seo’s team at Aneue Concierge not only books the appointments for you and sends along all the details in a schedule, but they also tag along to make sure you know exactly what you’re getting.

My willpower to stop doomscrolling is more kindergartener who just tried T-ball for the first time, less Olympian. So I’ve outsourced the job for me. Brick is a physical social media blocker that you have to tap to unlock—though I’m thinking about leveling up to the dumb smartphone Wise, which has all the necessities like we did when the phone first came out, none of the brain-sucking frills.

If somebody you know (or you yourself) asks to always see the photos from their colonoscopies, wants to keep pulled teeth, and is really curious about every marker of their health, one of these kits—which measure your skin biome, hormones, or gut sensitivities, respectively—will be a hit.

Taylor Swift used this bra to help keep her posture in place while training for The Eras Tour. It’s a compression-like garment that holds everything from your shoulders to your chest in place, so it keeps you both secure and straight-up.

Functional can be fun (and fab!)—like this caviar-tin-inspired mirror compact, one of Estée Lauder’s limited-edition holiday designs.

For the person in your life who loves to carry multiple drinks. While this fridge may be mini, its perks are mighty: it can hold 77 cans of your favorite sparkling water or healthy soda (or 27 bottles if you’re trying to up your antioxidant intake), is controlled by your iPhone, and can even tell you what’s stocked inside (or if you need to re-up on kombucha while at the grocery store).

I’m constantly searching for a better way to organize all my daily medications (antidepressants! thyroid meds! migraine meds!), along with the handful of supplements I take daily. And while a gum ball machine would be my first choice, I’m on too many medications for that. Why not this solid Calacatta marble pillbox instead?
