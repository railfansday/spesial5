3 Things Everyone Should Know About the 2027 Social Security COLA

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

We're just a few weeks from a major change to Social Security.

We're just a few weeks away from the 2027 Social Security cost-of-living adjustment (COLA), which will provide much-needed financial assistance to seniors who have been struggling with rising costs throughout the year. The first payments with the new COLA won't arrive until January, but it isn't too early to start understanding how they will affect your budget.

Here are the three most important things to know about the 2027 Social Security COLA when preparing for next year.

The 2027 Social Security COLA came in at 3.6%. This is above average compared to the last 50 years of COLAs, and it's the largest COLA since 2023. However, it still might not be enough to cover all your rising costs.

Since the COLA is a percentage, everyone sees a slightly different dollar-value increase, depending on what their checks are now. Those who are currently receiving large benefits will see bigger increases from the COLA.

The typical retired worker received $2,092 per month from Social Security as of October 2026. A 3.6% COLA will add roughly $75 to this benefit amount. However, some of this will be offset by rising Medicare Part B premiums for those enrolled in this program. Spousal benefits will see a more modest average increase -- up to $1,024 per month from $988 per month in October 2026.

You should receive a personalized COLA notice from the Social Security Administration giving your exact benefit increase in early December. If you have any questions about how the COLA will affect your checks, wait until after you've received this notice to reach out to your local Social Security office to ask.
