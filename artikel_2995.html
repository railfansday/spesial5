Thinking About Buying the SpaceX Dip? Here Are 3 Things to Know First.

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The company has grand ambitions beyond just space flight these days.

By Brett Schafer – Nov 19, 2026 at 7:48AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Space Exploration Technologies ›

Elon Musk has massive expectations for the future of Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX -0.20%). The founder of the rocket-flight disruptor is now turning it into a fully fledged artificial intelligence (AI) player and making moves to grow revenue rapidly.

After going public through an initial public offering (IPO) in June, SpaceX shares initially soared before falling back to earth. Right now, the stock is down 31% from highs, even though revenue grew well north of 100% year-over-year last quarter. Does that make the stock a promising opportunity right now?

If you are thinking about buying the dip on SpaceX stock, here are three things you need to know before adding it to your portfolio.

SpaceX was made famous by its awe-inspiring commercial rocket launches, including recent tests of the massive Starship rocket.

Connectivity revenue grew to $5.25 billion last quarter, up 70% year-over-year. Importantly, these satellite internet services are highly profitable, generating $2.37 billion in operating income last quarter alone.

There is still good runway for growth in Starlink's core customer base in rural areas, as well as in commercial services for boats and airplanes. However, in the long term, SpaceX has much grander ambitions to disrupt the entire telecommunications sector with direct-to-device internet capabilities worldwide. It will take many innovations to get there, but that is the thesis you are buying today: that Starlink can reach hundreds of billions in revenue a decade from now.

In the immediate term, a bet on SpaceX is a bet on the company's aggressive ambitions to pivot to AI infrastructure investments.

After acquiring Musk's xAI start-up in February 2026, SpaceX has begun building massive data centers to sell as an AI neocloud. This year, it has signed large deals with the likes of Anthropic and Alphabet, among smaller players. AI revenue was up to $7.56 billion last quarter, or more than 9x from a year ago.

SpaceX expects even more rapid growth in the quarters ahead. It spent $16.4 billion on AI infrastructure capital expenditures last quarter. Overall company capex is expected to double in the fourth quarter. By the end of next year, Musk forecasts overall annual recurring revenue (ARR) will reach $500 billion or higher. To be clear, that $500 billion is not a typo, but it also sits well above independent revenue estimates.

On quarterly revenue terms, that would mean $125 billion in Q4 2027 revenue compared to $7.56 billion today. This is what an investment in SpaceX shares is betting on today.

Forward growth is going to be massively important for buying SpaceX right now. Why?

Because the stock trades at a massive premium to its trailing financials. Right now, SpaceX has a market cap of $1.9 trillion and will likely continue to take on debt and sell new shares of common stock, diluting shareholders.

That gives it a trailing price-to-sales ratio (P/S) over 50. A P/S ratio over 50 makes it one of the most expensive stocks on the market, especially if you only include large-cap stocks.

This means an investor buying today needs to have supreme confidence in SpaceX's growth trajectory. Its valuation will decline quickly if connectivity and AI revenue continue to grow at this rapid pace, but these are optimistic assumptions that Musk and the business are putting out there. A lot can go wrong when executing on a product vision.

Unless you have high confidence that SpaceX can rapidly grow to hundreds of billions in revenue, the stock is probably one you want to avoid today.
