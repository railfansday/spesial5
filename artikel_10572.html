Buffalo Wild Wing’s reveals pickle menu and it even has a pickle margarita

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Buffalo Wild Wings is going all in on pickles with a new limited-time menu featuring pickle-flavored wings, fried pickle chips, and even a pickle margarita.

The restaurant chain is introducing the lineup on November 10, just days before National Pickle Day on November 14, giving customers several new ways to enjoy the popular dill flavor.

The launch follows a growing trend of pickle-inspired fast food offerings, with KFC previously introducing its own pickle-themed menu featuring a pickle-flavored Pepsi soda.

Leading the lineup is the new Pickle Sauce, a tangy, dill pickle-inspired glaze that customers can order tossed on the chain’s signature wings.

The menu also introduces a $6 Pickle Margarita, made with Lunazul Blanco Tequila, triple sec, pickle juice, and lime sour. The cocktail is finished with pickle garnishes and a Tajín rim.

Customers looking for something to eat alongside their wings can order hand-battered dill pickle chips, which are fried until golden and coated in chipotle BBQ seasoning.

“Pickle fans are passionate, and we knew if B-Dubs was going to get into the pickle game, we had to go all in,” said Scott Nelson, Chief Marketing Officer at Buffalo Wild Wings.

“We’re always playing around with new flavors to find our next craveable sauce, and Pickle Sauce is our latest swing. It’s bold, unexpected and a fun one to try with friends while you watch the game or enjoy a Tuesday night hang.”

Alongside the individual menu items, Buffalo Wild Wings is offering a $17.99 pickle-themed bundle exclusively through its app and website.

The deal includes 20 boneless or bone-in wings tossed in the new Pickle Sauce, a large order of fries, and four dips.

The new Pickle Menu will be available starting November 10 at participating Buffalo Wild Wings restaurants for a limited time. The company has not announced an exact end date for the promotion.
