Canary Technologies Named to the Deloitte Technology Fast 500™ for a Third Consecutive Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Canary ranks among North America's fastest-growing technology companies as hospitality's demand for AI accelerates.

SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Canary Technologies, the leading AI platform for hotel and guest management, today announced that it has made the Deloitte Technology Fast 500â¢ for the third year in a row. Now in its 32nd year, the annual list ranks the 500 fastest-growing tech companies in North America.

Canary's three-year run reflects hotels' sustained demand for technology that automates guest and staff workflows. With AI guest communication, guests get faster answers and better stays: in a Canary study of more than 10 million AI-powered interactions, those who engaged with AI were 11% more likely to rate their stay five stars. Hotel teams, meanwhile, spend less time on repetitive requests and more on the moments that need a human.

"AI is reshaping hospitality at remarkable speed, and Canary is excited to be leading the transformation," said Co-founder and CEO Harman Singh Narula. "We're pleased to be named among North America's fastest-growing tech companies, a credit to our team and the results they deliver for hoteliers every day."

Canary's growth has been driven by the more than 20,000 hotels it serves worldwide, from boutique, independent properties to global brands such as Marriott, Four Seasons, Wyndham, Choice, IHG, Hyatt and Best Western.

Canary Technologies is modernizing the hotel tech stack with its award-winning agentic AI platform for hotel and guest management. Digitizing guest touchpoints from discovery to post-stay, Canary manages tens of millions of guest and staff interactions end-to-end autonomously each year, improving operational efficiency, increasing revenue and elevating the guest experience. Canary has built the most robust artificial intelligence interaction layer designed specifically for hotels, staff and guests.

Trusted by over 20,000 hotels in 125+ countries, Canary powers hospitality at the world's most renowned brands, including Marriott International, Four Seasons, Choice Hotels, Wyndham Hotels & Resorts, BWH Hotels and IHG Hotels & Resorts. Recognized as the #1 Guest Experience Platform by HotelTechReport for multiple years, Canary has earned recognition from Deloitte, Business Insider, Fast Company, the American Hotel and Lodging Association, TravelTech Breakthrough, Hospitality Technology and more.

For more information, visit https://www.canarytechnologies.com.

In order to be eligible for Technology Fast 500 recognition, companies must own proprietary intellectual property or proprietary technology that significantly contributes to the company's operating revenues. Companies must have base-year (fiscal year 2022) operating revenues of at least US$50,000, and current-year (fiscal year 2025) operating revenues of at least US$5 million, with a growth rate of 50% or greater. Additionally, companies must be in business for a minimum of four years and be headquartered within North America (United States and Canada).
