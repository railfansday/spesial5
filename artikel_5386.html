FF EAI Robotics Ecosystem Inc. (FFR) CEO YT Jia Shares Weekly Updates on Robots Delivery to Central Michigan University, Brain Blocks AI Module, and Industrial Scenario Validation

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

LOS ANGELES, Nov. 15, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- FF EAI Robotics Ecosystem Inc. (NASDAQ: FFR) ("FFR" or the "Company"), a California-based Physical AI robotics ecosystem company, today shared a weekly business update from YT Jia, CEO of FFR.

"Welcome to Weekly Report 81. Let me start with another update on our Hundred-City Initiative for robot deployment. FFR has delivered two Aegis Ultra robots to Central Michigan University, our first customer in Michigan. This marks our first sale and delivery to a university lab in the state. This delivery means more than adding one more university customer. It expands our business on three levels.

First, we're moving beyond Kâ12 schools into universities and research labs, broadening the customer base of FFR's Education and Research Ecosystem. Second, we're expanding from California into other states, building experience for taking the Hundred-City Initiative across regions. Third, we're going beyond delivering robots and into applications in specific academic disciplines. CMU's Department of Recreation, Parks, and Leisure Services Administration has a long history and is one of its signature strengths. We believe Aegis will bring unique value to Physical AI teaching, research, and hands-on practice in this field. It will also help us build experience for developing use cases and rolling out solutions to more colleges and universities.

Going forward, we'll launch a customer success tour across our four industry ecosystems, visiting customers to see our robots at work in real-world settings. Through videos and written updates, we'll share more feedback and firsthand experiences straight from users on the front lines.

In our Industrial and Logistics Ecosystem, the team recently completed a simulation-based validation of a label-based parts-sorting scenario using Faber U, our wheeled mobile manipulator. This lays out the groundwork for testing on real robots and proof-of-concept projects in real-world settings. Faber combines autonomous mobility, precise manipulation, and dual-arm coordination. Building on those strengths, we'll validate it on real robots and on site, and step by step develop Skills and Industry Productivity Solutions for sorting, machine loading and unloading, and logistics transfer. Now let me turn to our Four-Core Full-Stack AI products and business.

Today I'll focus on our Physical AI Brain and Developer Platform Core. Brain Blocks, one of the six development tools on our Developer Platform, has launched an AI module along with motion recording and playback. It can recognize gestures, body poses, objects, and sounds, and use those results in block-based programs, with no coding required. This makes developing robot perception and motion far more intuitive and greatly lowers the barrier to programming. In turn, it speeds up the adoption and application of Physical AI.

On November 6, FFR hosted a developer workshop in Silicon Valley on vibe coding, where even beginners could program using natural language. The event produced a batch of new Skills, adding to our Skills Store. It also gave us experience as we explore ways for FFR and developers to share in the revenue together.

This week, FFR released its first financial results since completing the acquisition of the robotics business. For the first nine months of the year, the acquired robotics business generated unaudited standalone combined revenue of approximately $2.3 million and gross profit of approximately $593,000, a gross margin of about 26%. As we continue to advance our four industry ecosystems and Four-Core Full-Stack AI business, we reaffirmed FFR's 2026 guidance for the robotics business on a standalone basis: approximately 2,000 units sold and shipped, with continued deliveries at a positive unit gross margin.

In the next phase, we'll focus on advancing the Hundred-City Initiative, RoboShare's Ten-City Strategy, and the "Built in USA" Acceleration Program. We'll keep expanding sales and deployment and improving our operating and financial performance.

We also plan to launch our Physical AI Cloud technology platform during CES, together with our Productivity Token standards, which will define how Productivity Tokens are measured, calculated, and used. Together, they will bring together the capabilities of our Physical AI Brain and Developer Platform and establish a way to measure and settle robot productivity. This will provide a foundation for pricing and transacting robot-sharing services, and make transactions across the industry more efficient.

Given the time, I won't go through everything in our Q3 results here. For details, please refer to FFR's public filings.

At FFAI, our strategic and business upgrades are also moving forward. This includes plans of building a Physical AI Device manufacturing, testing, and validation center for FFR robots at our Hanford factory. We're also in active discussions on capital partnerships based on complementary technologies, and we plan to announce major progress in early December. All right, see you next week!"

FF EAI Robotics Ecosystem Inc. (NASDAQ: FFR) is a U.S.-based Embodied AI robotics company focused on the research and development, manufacturing, commercialization and deployment of intelligent robotic technologies, products and industry solutions. FFR is building a "Four-Core Full-Stack" AI ecosystem covering the full lifecycle of robotics, consisting of EAI Brain & Developer Platform, EAI Devices, Industry Productivity Solutions, and EAI Data Factory. Guided by the technology and product philosophy of "One Brain, Multi-forms, Multi-capabilities," FFR aims to empower humanoid, biomimetic, and other robotic form factors through a unified EAI Brain, while continuously expanding their multi-task and multi-scenario capabilities. The ecosystem is designed to support the full robotics lifecycle, including R&D, deployment, data collection and training, operations, and commercial applications. FFR also operates RoboShare, a robot-sharing and services platform.
