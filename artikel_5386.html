Workday is raising its full-year outlook as AI products drive revenue beat

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Workday set fiscal 2027 subscription revenue guidance at $9.958 billion, while fiscal third-quarter revenue rose 11.2% to $2.705 billion

Jakub Porzycki / NurPhoto via Getty Images

Workday $WDAY raised its fiscal 2027 subscription revenue guidance to $9.958 billion, representing 13% growth, as it reported fiscal third-quarter results on Tuesday. The company also increased its fiscal 2027 non-GAAP operating margin guidance to 31.5%.

Total revenue for the quarter ended Oct. 31 was $2.705 billion, up 11.2% from a year earlier. Subscription revenue was $2.532 billion, up 12.8%.

Analysts expected revenue of $2.69 billion, according to The Wall Street Journal. The previous subscription revenue outlook was a range of $9.94 billion to $9.95 billion.

"Our Q3 results reflect ongoing adoption across our AI portfolio, reinforcing our conviction in the compelling opportunity ahead," CFO Zane Rowe said in a statement.

"The past few quarters have been our strongest on the innovation and invention front, and in Q3, our AI products drove nearly $150 million in new ACV [annual contract value], more than three times what we delivered a year ago," said Aneel Bhusri, co-founder, CEO and chair of Workday. "Our focus now is on accelerating customer adoption, moving more agents into production, and helping customers realize the value of our AI products faster."

More than 2,200 core customers have purchased at least one AI product, the company said.

Net income was $237 million, or 96 cents per diluted share, compared with $252 million, or 94 cents per share, a year earlier. Operating income was $329 million, and restructuring expenses of $51 million weighed on the quarter.

Non-GAAP diluted earnings per share were $2.82, compared with $2.32 a year earlier. Analysts forecast $2.74, according to The Wall Street Journal. Non-GAAP operating income was $850 million, a margin of 31.4% compared with 28.5% a year earlier.

Workday reported operating cash flows of $735 million, up from $588 million. Free cash flows were $691 million, compared with $550 million. The 12-month subscription revenue backlog was $9.216 billion, up 12.3%. Workday repurchased $750 million of its Class A common stock during the quarter.

For the fiscal fourth quarter ending Jan. 31, 2027, Workday expects subscription revenue of $2.6 billion, representing 10% growth, and a non-GAAP operating margin of 31.7%.
