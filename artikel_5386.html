Splendid Tianfu, Leisurely Sichuan: A Journey into the Ancient Culture of Bashu

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

BEIJING, Nov. 27, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- A news report from China Global Television Network Corporation:

From intangible cultural heritage experiences in the streets and alleys of ancient towns to the ancient Shu civilization showcased in museums, from everyday life in rural communities to ecological conservation in giant panda habitats, this year's "Splendid Tianfu â¢ Leisurely Sichuan" global cultural and tourism promotion project invited international youth from several countries to visit Sichuan. Through on-site visits, hands-on activities and face-to-face exchanges, they experienced the profound historical heritage and vibrant vitality of life in Sichuan. In the form of people-to-people cultural exchanges, the project promoted mutual learning among civilizations and presented to the world a credible, lovable and respectable image of Sichuan, China.

"Splendid Tianfu â¢ Leisurely Sichuan" is Sichuan Province's cultural and tourism brand. In June 2025, the Seventh Plenary Session of the 12th CPC Sichuan Provincial Committee called for efforts to develop the brand, using a distinctive regional identity to showcase Sichuan's natural beauty and cultural heritage and promote closer integration of culture and tourism.

"Splendid" evokes the fine craftsmanship of Shu brocade and Shu embroidery, as well as Sichuan's beautiful landscapes and long-standing cultural heritage. "Tianfu" carries the historical legacy of Sichuan. "Leisurely" captures the local people's love of life and their optimistic, easygoing outlook. It reflects a way of life that balances an enterprising spirit with a relaxed approach to life. From its landscapes to its everyday culture, the brand offers international visitors many ways to discover Sichuan.

The project focused on Three Kingdoms culture, Three-Su Culture, the ancient Shu civilization and giant panda conservation. It turned cultural resources into engaging experiences that visitors could take part in and share, bringing to life the natural beauty, cultural richness and everyday charm that the brand represents.

In the ancient town of Langzhong, the young visitors wandered through its historic streets, learned about Three Kingdoms culture and took part in traditional craft activities. Historical stories and traditional crafts came together, giving participants a chance to see how local traditions have been kept alive over the years.

In Zhongyan Village, Qingshen County, the guests explored sites linked to Three-Su Culture, sampled local food and chatted with residents. They saw how cultural traditions remain an integral part of everyday life in the village, and how they continue to be shared from one generation to the next.

In Guanghan, the Sanxingdui Museum and the Sanxing Village Archaeological Exploration Base introduced the young visitors to the ancient Shu civilization. They appreciated ancient artifacts, tried making bronze objects and took part in simulated archaeological excavations. They also asked questions and shared their thoughts as they explored this ancient civilization. Among the visitors were Anna and Ye Zimu, two international students from Russia, who saw the Bronze Sacred Tree at the Sanxingdui Museum and tried their hand at bronze casting. They said the artifacts at Sanxingdui were unlike anything they had seen before. Making bronze objects themselves gave them a firsthand experience of this ancient civilization, and they hoped to take their creations back to Russia as gifts for their families. Anna also said she was impressed by how Chinese museums bring history and culture together in their exhibitions.

In Dayi County, the guests visited the Dasuanping Protection Station in Giant Panda National Park to learn about local conservation efforts. They also visited Taiping Community in Xieyuan, where they tried traditional bamboo weaving and observed everyday community life. Together, these experiences offered visitors another perspective on Sichuan, showing how ecological conservation and rural development can go hand in hand.

Su Zhiyao, an international student studying in China, accompanied park staff to the protection station. In the mountain forest at an altitude of 2,140 meters, she watched real-time footage from infrared cameras to observe giant pandas moving in the wild. She said that visiting pandas in their natural habitat and using infrared camera footage to observe their activities gave her a more direct understanding of how people and nature can live in harmony.

The visitors' experiences were also featured in television programs and short videos, which followed them as they took part in activities and shared their experiences. The programs are being released across different platforms, with content precisely targeted to different platforms and audiences.

From ancient towns to rural communities, and from cultural heritage to ecological conservation, "Splendid Tianfu â¢ Leisurely Sichuan" continues to send invitations to the world, inviting friends from around the world to visit Sichuan and understand Sichuan. By building bridges for cross-cultural communication through face-to-face dialogue between people, it enhances understanding in encounters among civilizations, shares development achievements and a better life, and continues to tell vivid and lively Bashu stories overseas.
