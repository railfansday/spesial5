SAN JOSE, Calif., Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Payactiv, Inc., the pioneer of Earned Wage Access infrastructure and a Certified B Corp, today announced a DeFi and blockchain initiative designed to expand financial inclusion to frontline workers through a payroll-connected financial ecosystem.

In collaboration with PACT and the USAâ® ecosystem, Payactiv will expand how working people access, move and use earned money by connecting existing payroll services with digital wallets, stablecoins and programmable tokens.

The initiative advances Payactiv's mission to improve financial lives by providing digital finance tools to working people too often excluded from financial innovation like DeFi. Through payroll-connected services, Payactiv aims to reduce barriers to participation, making digital finance tools available to workers who choose to use them.

"Connecting stablecoins with EWA and consumer banking brings digital finance to the services people already use," said Safwan Shah, founder and CEO of Payactiv. "More than $10 trillion moves every year through highly reliable payroll systems, a powerful foundation for meeting growing demand for digital-dollar services. This initiative is a practical path to financial inclusion and an extension of our mission to improve lives through access to earned money."

A Digital Finance or DeFi Layer for Workforce Finance

Payactiv's payroll and HCM relationships reach more than 50 million eligible U.S. workers. Because HCM platforms connect employers, workers, payroll data and financial benefits, they provide a practical channel for digital-finance or DeFi capabilities to reach workers at scale. The initiative will  enable employers and their platforms to offer workers impactful products and features  without requiring them to become blockchain operators. Offerings would be optional and subject to eligibility, disclosures, terms and compliance requirements.

The planned infrastructure is designed to manage the movement, conversion, routing and reconciliation of value across traditional and digital payment systems, with enterprise controls for identity verification, authorization, transaction monitoring, risk management and compliance. Applications include non-custodial digital wallets, stablecoin-enabled movement of earned money, programmable tokens and connectivity between traditional and blockchain-based payments .
