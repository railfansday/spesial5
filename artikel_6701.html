The Best Veterans Day Food Freebies, From Dunkin’ Donuts To Shake Shack Burgers

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

A guide to the restaurants thanking veterans with free meals and treats.

Every November 11, Americans celebrate and thank those who have served in our military. Veterans Day marks the anniversary of the 1918 armistice that ended World War I, but it wasn’t until 1954, after both World War II and the Korean War, that it changed nationally from Armistice Day to Veterans Day.

To mark the occasion, many of our favorite national food chains are dishing out free meals, drinks, and treats to both active-duty military and vets. Here’s where you can score big.

Aroma Joe’s is offering veterans free handcrafted coffee or signature AJ’s Rush Energy Drink in any size up to 24 ounces at all locations with a valid ID.

Veterans and active-duty military members can get 10 free Boneless Wings + Fries with valid proof of service at participating BWW sports bars for dine-in only and GO locations with a minimum $5 purchase.

All active military members and veterans can enjoy a free All-American Single Smash or Americana Big Dog in-restaurant.

All active-duty service members and veterans with proof of service can enjoy a free Red, White, and Blueberry Pancake Combo that includes two buttermilk pancakes topped with glazed strawberries, blueberry topping, and whipped topping, along with two eggs, hash browns, and a choice of two strips of hickory-smoked bacon or two pork sausage links.

Active and retired military members can receive a free Original French Toast during operating hours for dine-in orders.

Active-duty, retired military members, and veterans can enjoy a complimentary order of Bang Bang Shrimp and a nonalcoholic beverage when dining at any Bonefish Grill location.

All active and retired military can enjoy a free donut.

All active military and veterans can receive a free single ShackBurger with a valid military ID or proof of service.

All active-duty and retired military members can enjoy a free meal from the chain’s special Veterans Day menu, with the purchase of a beverage when dining in at participating locations. The menu includes the option of 10-Piece Original Wings, Cheeseburger, Buffalo Chicken Sandwich, or Chicken Caesar Salad.

The bagel chain is offering veterans and active military a free coffee and bagel with schmear all day long. The offer includes any size hot or iced coffee and any bagel with any schmear and is only available in the bakery.

Gold Star Families, active military, veterans, and first responders can get a complimentary 5-Course Teppanyaki Entree, including a choice of Hibachi Steak, Chicken, or Salmon. The offer is available for dine-in only and requires the purchase of an appetizer or starter.

Veterans and military members can order a free Spaghetti with Marinara or Meat Sauce with proof of veteran or military status by using the code VET26.

All current and retired military members can enjoy a complimentary buffet and beverage at Golden Corral locations nationwide.

Active-duty military and veterans can enjoy a complimentary choice of 10 Bone-In or Boneless Wings, Beer-Battered Fish & Chips, Steakburger, Chicken Caesar Salad, Buttermilk Crispy Chicken Sandwich, or Chicken BLT Tacos.

Veterans and active military personnel can enjoy a free small hot or iced coffee and a free donut of their choice.

Service members and veterans can receive a complimentary entrée and beverage from a special prix fixe menu, plus a Buy One Get One card, good for a pizza, pasta, or salad with the purchase of one on a return visit between November 12 and 25.

Veterans with a valid military ID can receive a complimentary meal at participating locations when dining in the restaurant between 11 a.m. and 7 p.m.
