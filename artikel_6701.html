Jordan Ngatikaura Sent ‘SLOMW’ Fans a Private Video Defending Zac Affleck Amid His Divorce

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Meanwhile, Jen is out here struggling with the split.

Just when it got a little too quiet in the realm of DadTok drama, Jordan Ngatikaura made his way back to Cameo to spill some Secret Lives of Mormon Wives tea.

In a private video sent to fans—or…haters? Who’s to say, really—Jordan weighed in on Zac and Jen Affleck’s recent divorce.

When asked whether Zac was really a “little bitch” or if he’s only portrayed that way on TV, Jordan said, “Yes, that is scripting and editing. The entire show is scripted and edited to make the men look like they’re dumbasses and they’re pieces of shit to elevate the women.”

Unsurprisingly, the self-proclaimed DadTok leader came to Zac’s defense, adding, “Zac is a really good guy. Has he made mistakes? Absolutely. But I consider Zac a very good husband and probably even a better dad.”

Other SLOMW fans reacted to Jordan’s message in the comments section of the clip, and for the most part, they agree Zac could have treated Jen better.

“Coming from piece of ish number 1 ? Riiigghhhtttttttt,” one person commented, while another shared, “I can admit when I’m wrong - I wasn’t hating on Zac until I saw Jordan defending him.”

Jordan’s comments are circulating online as Jen admitted that she’s “struggling” with her split from Zac after eight years together.

“It’s been really, really hard, but I’m trying my best to just stay positive,” Jen, who’s expecting her fourth child with Zac, told Page Six’s Virtual Reali-Tea podcast on November 10.

The SLOMW: OC star explained that she’s “doing what’s best” for their young children, adding, “I wouldn’t wish divorce on anyone. Honestly, if you can avoid it, you should avoid it. All I can say is, I gave it my all.”

According to court docs obtained by TMZ, Jen filed for divorce from Zac in Utah on Tuesday, November 3. The outlet reports that she also “submitted a proposed parenting plan, though the details of the filing are private.”
