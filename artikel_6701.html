Who Is Keira Knightley’s Husband, James Righton?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

All about the English musician married to the Black Doves actress

Keira Knightley has always kept her personal life private. While the actress’s many on-screen romances are hard to forget—from her passionate relationship with Mr. Darcy in Pride & Prejudice to her tragic affair in Black Doves—far less is known about her real-life love story with her husband, James Righton.

The British musician, best known as the former keyboardist for the indie rock band Klaxons, has been married to Knightley since 2013. Together, they've welcomed two daughters and built a family life largely out of the spotlight. So, who exactly is the man at the center of one of Hollywood's more low-key love stories? Here’s everything to know about James Righton and his relationship with the Love Actually star.

Like Knightley, Righton is from England. Born in 1983, he grew up in Stratford-upon-Avon, Warwickshire, before moving into the London music scene. In 2005, while Knightley was earning an Oscar nomination for her performance in Pride & Prejudice, Righton was making a name for himself as a founding member of Klaxons, an English indie rock nu-wave band.

The group, which also included Jamie Reynolds and Simon Taylor-Davis, became known for its energetic sound and dance-inspired take on indie rock. Their debut album, Myths of the Near Future, was released in 2007 and won the prestigious Mercury Prize that same year. Klaxons went on to release two more albums, Surfing the Void (2010) and Love Frequency (2014), before disbanding in 2015.

After the Klaxons’s split, Righton began a new chapter as a solo artist and composer. In 2017, he launched a project called Shock Machine, releasing an album of the same name before putting out his first solo record, The Performer, in 2020.

He has since expanded his work into film and television scoring, composing music for projects including Simon Amstell’s 2018 film Benjamin, the comedy series Daddy Issues, and the Netflix documentary Untold: The Liver King. He’s also collaborated with an impressive roster of artists, including Rihanna at the 2008 BRIT Awards, The Chemical Brothers, and Arctic Monkeys, and has composed music for fashion projects, including runway shows for Erdem.

In a 2020 interview with The Independent, Righton opened up about the difficult period following Klaxons’ breakup, revealing that he had temporarily lost his love for making music. He credited Knightley with helping him get back his creative confidence, saying that she encouraged him to share his early solo demos with Klaxons producer James Ford. That support eventually helped lead to his Shock Machine project.

Righton and Knightley were introduced through a mutual friend at a dinner party in 2011. In a 2014 interview with The Guardian, Knightley clarified that they met through a friend named Tim—not Alexa Chung, as some reports had suggested. “We were very drunk,” she recalled.

Righton has also offered his take on their first meeting. Speaking to The Independent in 2020, he said they first connected at an Oscars screening party in London’s Soho. “Luckily, I was really, really drunk,” he said. “So I was fearless. I don’t know what I did or said that connected. Maybe she took pity on me.”

He also remembered taking Knightley to Thorpe Park for one of their early dates, despite being terrified of roller coasters himself. “I thought that would do it,” he joked. “I was terrified of rollercoasters, but I thought I needed to do something that was not the usual.”

The couple’s relationship quickly became serious, and they announced their engagement in May 2012, just over a year after meeting.

Knightley and Righton officially tied the knot on May 4, 2013, in an intimate ceremony in Mazan, a village in the south of France. The couple exchanged vows at the town hall before celebrating with a reception at a nearby estate owned by Knightley’s mother.

For the occasion, the bride wore a pale gray Chanel Couture dress. She paired the tulle mini with a white tweed Chanel jacket and ballet flats, keeping her bridal look effortlessly understated.

In a 2024 interview with The Times, Knightley offered some rare insight into her marriage to Righton. Commenting on their 11-year-long relationship at the time, she jokingly remarked, “It’s good for an actress and a musician, isn’t it? We really annoy each other. But, f*ck do we laugh.”

She also revealed that the couple can be complete opposites—Righton is the extrovert of their relationship, while she struggles socially at industry parties and events. “If James comes, they’re fun,” Knightley said. “James believes it is his right to have a good time and he will create it anywhere he goes. Unless I’m really drunk, and you can’t be if you’re at one of those events, I find it quite intense and quite difficult. James manages to break formality with people, and I can’t do that.”

She echoed those same sentiments in an earlier 2014 interview with The Guardian, saying, “He’s the sort of person who will be the brightest light in any room, whereas I’m the one who sits in the corner. And he’s much nicer than me.”

Knightley and Righton have two daughters: Edie, born in May 2015, and Delilah, born in September 2019. The couple has been intentional about keeping their children largely out of the public eye, rarely sharing details about their family life or bringing them to public events.

During a 2023 appearance on The Drew Barrymore Show, Knightley mentioned the couple’s 10-year wedding anniversary, joking, “We have two children, and we’ve made it a decade. It’s a miracle.”
