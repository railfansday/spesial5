Cornerstone of Kretinsky’s new era is soon to be revealed | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

As the summer boardroom turmoil raged upstairs at West Ham United following David Sullivan’s resignation, one of the first promises made by the then-Kretinsky – Gold alliance was that the intensely disliked club crest (the badge to you and me) would be replaced once a consultation had been undertaken with supporters.

Which was well received by all. The ‘London’ variant of the Crossed Hammers crest still remains tainted by the David Sullivan – David Gold – Karren Brady inspired enforced move to the old Olympic Stadium, ditching years of heritage and and what felt like a pretty shabby attempt to cash in on tourist trade by linking the club’s new signage with ‘London’.

Through the years..what’s next? 1975 all over again?

The consultation was duly undertaken and now there’s progress towards an unveiling of a shortlist, as ExWHUEmployee reports on thewesthamway.com:

“We understand that the club have now a set for 4 new crest designs from the selected design agency, which will soon be made available to fans to vote on.

“As of yet we don’t have the exact timetable but we believe the final 4 designs will be revealed soon and then the next phase of selecting the new preferred design will commence.”

No doubt they’ll all be ‘revealed’ ( i.e. ‘leaked’) ahead of the official club communication – some things don’t change at West Ham United – and we’ll let you now the designs as soon as they’re in the public domain. Exciting stuff.
