Stefan Borson shares bleak Newcastle United stadium verdict after secret meetings

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Newcastle United's owners will struggle to make back the money spent on a brand-new stadium over the next few decades.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that PIF will be lucky to see a return for 50 years should they move away from St James' Park.

New plans have emerged for the Magpies to build a new £1.3billion super stadium in Leazes Park, which has turned some heads on Tyneside.

The latest update is that Newcastle chiefs have already been in secret meetings with Wall Street money men after successful talks with Goldman Sachs.

There have been discussions around potentially staying at St James' Park, but it would appear that moving away is seen as the best course of action.

It would be an extremely expensive project, however, and one that the owners will have to be patient with if they want to see a return on their investment.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson has warned PIF about the long wait for profit on a brand-new "super stadium".

"The new Newcastle stadium, if it's well designed, is a stadium for quite a big catchment area in quite a big area of the country," he said.

"It should have good capacity for additional events. So I can see some business plan around it, but it's still super expensive. To get a return on it, even over 50 years, it's not going to be straightforward.

"But because of the way the rules are constructed, you need the revenue. So that's what they're probably focusing on. They need the extra £30m of gate receipts.

"A lot of that will go to the bottom line, because that helps them compete. There aren't that many other ways to do it."
