We asked Davey Hat-Trick to give us his match ratings and player comments after Newcastle 2 Arsenal 2 in the Premier League.

Out of the 1,000+ games I have watched in person, the 4-4 draw with Arsenal 15 years ago is the match that most deserves the ‘game of two halves’ label. This one – against the same opponents – wasn’t far off.

A blind man could have seen the gulf in class between the two sides in the first half. Arsenal outclassed Newcastle without trying. Newcastle looked petrified, and it looked like no one wanted the ball. Having said that, Arsenal only scored a single goal, and didn’t really come that close again. Our centre halves were good.

The second half could not have been more different. Newcastle tore into Arsenal from the get-go, and the Gunners looked shell-shocked. By the time Arsenal had recovered their composure, Newcastle were 2-1 up. In my opinion, we were playing as well as we had all season.

Arsenal were bound to come back into it, and they did. They scored an equaliser, but that’s not to say Newcastle were overwhelmed. We were giving as good as we got, and continued to do so.

Having conceded, most Geordies were fearing the traditional last-minute winner for our opponents, and when the officials came up with nine minutes of injury time, it seemed nailed on.

But Newcastle held firm, and got a deserved point against the reigning champions, and this season’s most likely winners. It was a triumph of belief, effort, teamwork and quality, and it had a galvanising effect on the black and white faithful. St James’ Park was rocking to a degree I haven’t witnessed since our opener against Liverpool.

It’s hard to give the players a sensible mark. Everyone was poor in the first half. Everyone was excellent in the second.

Lukáš Horníček 5 Not at fault for Arsenal’s first, may have done better for their second, but he always looked nervy and unsure with the ball at his feet. His distribution was poor and he never looked confident.

Amar Dedić 4 Good player, bad game. Stranded for Arsenal’s first and took a pointless yellow card way before half time. Subbed off at half time, a bold move by the manager, but the correct one.

Malick Thiaw 8 If it wasn’t for our centre halves, Arsenal could have been 3-0 up at half time. Their challenges, blocks and clearances kept Newcastle in the game. Just. The second half saw Thiaw liberated to pick his passes, get up the pitch and score Newcastle’s second from all of four inches.

Dan Burn 8 A captain’s innings. Kept the Gunners from tearing us apart in the first half and made a nuisance of himself in attack in the second. I thought he wouldn’t get much game time this campaign. Now he’s one of the first names on the team sheet.

Lewis Hall 6 People – me included – were calling him the best left back in the world this week. I still think he is…but he wasn’t anything like that in that first half. Was mugged for Arsenal’s first and made a few other errors. He typified Newcastle’s second half – a much improved performance. Got up the pitch and had Arsenal on the back foot. Went off with what looked like a strain. Let’s hope it’s nothing serious.

Sean Steur 5 The game passed him by in the first half and he didn’t appear for the second. I don’t think he was bad, just not particularly effective.

Lewis Miley 6 A mate of mine thinks he was our best player. I just thought it was a decent game from Lewis. Not exactly dominant, but decent on the ball, and another wiho improved in the second half. Had a run at goal which nearly came off.

Jacob Murphy 5 I only remember Newcastle having one attempt in the first half and it was Murphy who had it. It was on target as well, except it hit his own team-mate. Other than that, Jacob offered little else and was hooked at half time.

Joe Willock 6 He’d be on a 7 or 8 if he got his head up and passed the ball instead of taking on ridiculous long distance shots. I thought he had a decent game, to be fair, and was full of energy. It looked to me like he played deeper in the second half and it worked. I think he got a dodgy assist for Thiaw’s goal.

Harvey Barnes 8 Another who seemed like a bystander in the first half but who came to life in the second. Scored the goal that gave us belief and even went past his man a few times on the wing. Well played lad.

William Osula 6.5 There will be some who say Osula ‘did nowt’ in the first half but I think that was due to his team-mates being pinned back in their own half. He was isolated, although he showed his usual willingness to run. Second half was a different game. His colleagues actually got up the pitch to support him and he looked much better. A good assist for our first goal and it became a more confident display.

Tino Livramento 7 I’d forgotten what an excellent footballer Tino can be. He came on for the second half and looked like the player we saw two seasons ago. Quick, intelligent and good on the ball. It’s fair to say the half-time substitutes improved the team; none more than Tino.

Anthony Elanga 5.5 To be truthful, he didn’t do a lot with the ball that I remember, but he did inject pace and energy, qualities that were non-existent first half. We looked a different team. We were a different team.

Jacob Ramsey 6 We looked more secure when Jacob entered the fray. Suddenly our midfield looked better than Arsenal’s. Played some good balls but skied a shot that could have won us the game.

Yoane Wissa 4 On for a tiring Willock. Found it difficult to get into the game.

Sven Botman 5 Came on at a time when most fans’ hearts were in their mouths – Arsenal were looking dangerous. But Sven held his own and looked the part. Relief all round.
