Blackburn Rovers 2- 1 West Ham United | The morning after

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Scott Parker’s words at full time pretty much summed up the remainder of this Championship season for West Ham United following the side’s first away defeat of the campaign. The new manager bounce failed to materialise as his squad thew away chance after chance against their solid opponents who twice capitalised on the now-traditional lacklustre Irons’ defending.

Speaking after the game to BBC Radio London, a hoarse Hammers head coach declared :

Not only at Ewood Park, but in the 31 remaining games of this season. A six point gap between the Hammers in ninth and the league leaders is not yet insurmountable. But it is, certainly, a mountain to climb for Parker to turn West Ham’s dreadfully mediocre-looking squad into Championship contenders.

With so many leaks, it is difficult to know which hole to plug first in the slowly sinking good ship West Ham United. Goalkeeper, defence, midfield, attack – all are areas crying out for urgent post-Nuno repair.. But none are yet beyond salvage.

A tough watch – Scott Parker will have learned a lot about his West Ham squad

As many have commented, though, it’s going to be a long season.

Right now, half a season of the dour, drab, boring but solid wins for which Scott Parker received so much criticism from his time at Burnley would be pretty well received in east London.

Usually we curse the international break but I have a feeling this time around it comes as a huge relief to the manager, the squad and supporters, for very different reasons. Amongst supporters, optimism will no doubt resurface at some stage during the next fortnight as we convince ourselves that if nothing else, we have the right man for the job at the helm.

Whatever Scott Parker is getting paid, if he somehow manages to transform this group into his trademark mean, lean, tough side capable of grinding out one-nil victories week in, week out, frankly he’ll be worth every penny.
