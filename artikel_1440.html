Endrick threatens Real Madrid with January departure

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

While FC Barcelona fears every minute played by Raphinha with the Brazil jersey, Real Madrid has the opposite position: they want Endrick to play as much as possible with their selection. Obviously, this is linked to the status of the young Brazilian within Casa Blaca, where he has to make do with crumbs. Since the last break, Endrick has benefited from 136 minutes of playing time at Real, compared to 139 during the 3 matches played with Brazil, which means he needs his selection to stretch his legs.

Endrick therefore joined his selection, which will face Japan then Singapore during this break, with the ambition of playing as much as possible, which will allow him to show himself, in the eyes of his coach José Mourinho, but also of the clubs interested in his services. Because it is one of the major issues for Real during the next winter transfer window, since Mourinho has already declared that he wants to keep him. “He’s a player that I adore, a player that I love deeply. He has great potential and he is very important for us. Anyone interested in Endrick will have to fight me. I don’t think he needs to be loaned out; he will do very well here”he said.

But does Endrick have the same opinion? He spoke about his future during a short mixed zone upon his arrival at the Brazilian rally. “I don’t know what will happen in January. It’s in God’s hands. I only ask what is best for my career. Come what may. I will keep my feet on the ground and do my best. Madrid is a beautiful city, but I will always do everything possible to advance my career”he explained, making it clear that a departure was possible on his side, even if he will not take unnecessary risks.

“I am still very young. I don’t need to throw myself headlong into the void. I’m only 20 and we’ll see. I just want to play, be happy, help my teammates… Score goals, make assists, like I did in Lyon, which remains one of the best experiences of my life and one of the happiest moments of my existence.”he recalled, he who is under contract until 2030. His time in Lyon had left him with good memories, and vice versa. It remains to be seen whether by January, he will have skyrocketed his playing time at Real Madrid.
