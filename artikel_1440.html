Pedro Neto is sent off for two yellow cards in under two minutes before bizarre touchline clash with Craig Bellamy - as Portugal beat Wales with Bruno Fernandes on target

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Published: 17:34 EST, 17 November 2026 | Updated: 17:49 EST, 17 November 2026

Chelsea star Pedro Neto lost his cool while playing for Portugal in their win over Wales as he received two yellow cards in as many minutes before clashing with opposing manager Craig Bellamy.

The Blues winger had in fact scored in the game, netting his side's second goal at the Cardiff City Stadium, but saw his game fall apart in the second-half.

He was first booked for a high challenge on the back of Brennan Johnson's leg – a tackle which some may argue could have been a red in itself.

One minute and 59 seconds later, he was shown his second yellow when he slid in on Johnson again. He didn't appear to make too much contact, if any, but left the Wales man sprawling to avoid it.

German referee Sven Jablonski showed no hesitation in sending Neto off, but the winger wasn't going to take it lying down.

He had to be held back by captain Bruno Fernandes as he tried to confront Johnson, and seemingly managed to shout in his direction before reluctantly making his way off the field.

Pedro Neto was sent off while playing for Portugal against Wales after two yellow cards in under two minutes

He also had a confrontation with Wales manager Craig Bellamy (right) as he left the field of play

Before that, though, he had a conversation with Wales boss Craig Bellamy, with officials initially trying to separate the two, with Neto looking angry.

Bellamy, though, spoke to Neto and embraced him before finally walking down the tunnel.

The 26-year-old will now serve a one-game suspension, meaning he will miss the first leg of Portugal's Nations League quarter-final, which will be played in March.

Jorge Jesus's side secured top spot in their group with the win over Wales, which was achieved by Carlos Forbs's 70th-minute goal.

Fernandes had opened the scoring for his side in the 11th minute, but David Brooks and Sorba Thomas gave Wales an unlikely lead.

That was until Neto levelled things up just before half-time, and Forbs scored the winner, with ten-man Portugal able to hold on for all three points.

Wales finished bottom of the group with one win – against Norway – and five defeats from their six matches.

Captain Ben Davies said after the game: 'We showed a lot of character and we've shown good effort in these games where we've just come up short from a results perspective.

After expressing his frustration, the Chelsea star had to be directed off the field by captain Bruno Fernandes (right)

He had in fact scored for his side in the game, bringing them level before the half-time interval

'There is a lot to take from this going into the games in March and we have to feel confident because we've been playing well.

'We know the boys up top can be so dangerous, and if teams want to come here and play man-for-man against us, we've got a lot of speed and we have to be good on the ball. That comes with risk sometimes, like you saw with the chances that they had, but I'd rather play this way and go toe-to-toe with these teams and have a go.

'We competed in the campaign and we were not a million miles off like the table might suggest. I thought we put in some very good performances, we just wished we had some better results.'

Neto, meanwhile, will now return to his club side Chelsea ahead of a busy Christmas period.

He has started all but one Premier League game so far and has been a key player under new manager Xabi Alonso.

The Blues will take on Leeds at Stamford Bridge this weekend as club football returns.
