Gasperini: “I’m happy and proud of my team. Castro deserved a chance, Hermoso was brilliant.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Speaking to Prime Video, Gian Piero Gasperini commented on Roma’s 2-2 draw at PSG.

“I left Malen out. Castro played because I wanted to give him a big chance. Malen has been playing a lot, including for the national team, so it was my duty to do so.”

The Giallorossi coach commented on Luis Enrique’s comments, who reportedly said Roma played very well: “I could say the same about PSG. We played against a fantastic team, both in terms of role interchange and pace. I’m very happy this evening; we faced a team that was going at full speed, and despite struggling, we managed to put in the performance: I’ll have to watch it again. It’s a shame we conceded from a corner at a difficult moment, but we were ahead: it wasn’t the kind of goal we should have conceded.”

Gian Piero Gasperini expressed his satisfaction with tonight’s performance: “I’m satisfied with the spirit, with the technical and tactical growth. We’ve always climbed the ladder well, and whenever the opportunity arose, we offered our quality. We’re on the right path, but we must always set ourselves higher goals. Now we must be proud. I’m already thinking ahead to Sunday because these matches are demanding and some players have been called up for reserve duty. I’ve seen high-level performances from many players. Luckily, we’re playing on Sunday and not right away.”

The Giallorossi coach concluded by commenting on Hermoso’s desire to be competitive on all fronts: “This is what we all want to do, and he’s an extraordinary performer in this regard. Not just for his mentality but also for his quality. Today he had three chances to score, and he scored one: it was extraordinary.”
