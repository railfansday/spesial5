Silly Polly Beast coming to Switch 2 in November

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Publisher Top Hat Studios and developer Anji Games will release a Switch 2 version of psychological survival action horror game Silly Polly Beast in November, the companies announced.

Here is a brief overview of the game, via Top Hat Studios:

A story-driven shooter filled with aggressive gunfights and terrifying monsters, the game follows a girl who, by the will of fate, must face the beast and understand its nature in order to become free. Players explore eerie realms, confront haunting figures from Polly’s past, and master forbidden spells in a fight for freedom.

Silly Polly Beast first launched for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, and PC via Steam on October 28, 2025.
