Oil prices slide as election results dim Iran escalation fears

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Brent crude fell 2.5% to $101.02 as Trump said he has no immediate plans to strike Iran and Democrats took Congress

President Donald Trump said the United States has no immediate plans to strike Iran, and oil prices fell on Wednesday after Democrats won control of Congress in Tuesday’s midterm elections, according to Reuters.

Brent crude futures slid 2.5%, to $101.02 per barrel, amid choppy trading. U.S. West Texas Intermediate futures lost 2.6% and traded at $93.61.

Dennis Kissler, an analyst at BOK Financial, wrote in a note that Democratic control of Congress could restrain American defense funding for operations in the Middle East and make a peace agreement more likely in the near term, according to The Wall Street Journal. He said larger Middle East crude exports and Trump's comments on Iran are "keeping the pressure on crude oil prices for now."

Middle Eastern flows of crude oil, condensates and refined fuels, not counting LPG, came to an average of 17.1 million barrels a day in October, compared with 18.9 million in September, according to Reuters, which cited Vortexa ship-tracking data. That level is close to three-quarters of pre-war levels.

Traders still face mixed signals on the conflict. A U.S. official said the military conducted defensive strikes on Iran's Qeshm Island on Tuesday, aimed at anti-aircraft missiles that had been fired at American assets, according to Reuters. Meanwhile, a senior Iranian official said Tehran and Washington are still a long way from an agreement to end the war, while Qatar has intensified its mediation efforts.

"President Trump's conflicting signals, with military strikes continuing alongside statements downplaying further escalation, have made it difficult for traders to build strong directional conviction," Priyanka Sachdeva, head of market insights at Phillip Nova, said, according to Reuters. She said that after October's steep rally, it was reasonable for traders to book gains and trim their speculative bets on higher prices.

Democrats won majorities in both the House and the Senate, taking each chamber from Republicans. Trump acknowledged that the conflict with Iran contributed in part to Republican losses, though he said he had no regrets about it. He said he was willing to cooperate with Democrats but would use his veto if necessary.
