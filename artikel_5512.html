Healthcare Triangle Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results: Nine-Month Revenue Up 162% to $28.2 Million and Nine-Month Gross Margin Up 354%

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

AI-Powered Customer Engagement Segment Delivered $6.53 Million of Q3 Revenue at a 27.9% Gross Margin

PLEASANTON, Calif., Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Healthcare Triangle, Inc. (Nasdaq: HCTI) ("HCTI" or the "Company"), a technology company delivering AI-powered customer engagement and digital transformation services, today reported financial results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026. Revenue for the nine months rose 162% to $28.16 million, while gross profit grew 354% to $6.54 million, lifting gross margin to 23.2% from 13.4%. In the third quarter, revenue increased 161% to $9.12 million and gross profit increased 237% to $2.08 million, while loss from operations narrowed to 21.9% of revenue from 74.3% a year ago. The Company's AI-powered Customer Engagement Services segment delivered approximately 72% of third quarter revenue and was the primary driver of the increase in gross margin to 22.8% from 17.7%.

Building on the momentum from the first half of the year, the Company's results reflect the transformative impact of its subsidiaries Teyame 360 S.L. ("Teyame 360") and Datono Mediacion S.L. ("Datono," and together with Teyame 360, "Teyame"), which contributed to a third consecutive quarter of triple-digit revenue and gross profit growth.

Third Quarter and Nine-Month 2026 Highlights

Revenue growth: Nine-month revenue of $28.16 million, up 162%. Third quarter revenue of $9.12 million, up 161%, marking a third consecutive quarter of triple-digit growth.

Gross profit outpacing revenue: Nine-month gross profit of $6.54 million, up 354%, with gross margin expanding 9.8 points to 23.2%. Third quarter gross profit of $2.08 million, up 237%, with gross margin of 22.8%, up from 17.7% a year ago and 22.5% in the second quarter of 2026.

Customer Engagement Services: Segment revenue of $6.53 million, up from $6.31 million in the second quarter, at a 27.9% gross margin, up from 26.1%, contributing $1.82 million of gross profit.

Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026 Financial Highlights

Customer Engagement Services (Teyame): Scaling Revenue and Expanding Margins

Teyame remained the primary driver of HCTI's financial performance in the third quarter. The acquired businesses, which operate as an integrated AI-powered omnichannel customer experience, marketing, and financial and insurance distribution platform and are reported as the Company's Customer Engagement Services segment, contributed $6.53 million of revenue during the quarter, up from $6.31 million in the second quarter and representing approximately 72% of the Company's total Q3 2026 revenue.

The segment generated $1.82 million in gross profit at a gross margin of approximately 27.9%, above the Company's historical margin profile and up from approximately 26.1% in the second quarter. The continued strength of this segment remained the key driver behind the Company's overall gross margin expansion, from 17.7% in Q3 2025 to 22.8% in Q3 2026 and from 13.4% to 23.2% for the nine months. Management expects gross margin to continue to improve as new contracts are negotiated at higher margins.

David Ayanoglou, Chief Financial Officer of Healthcare Triangle, said:

"Our third quarter and nine-month results show that the performance we saw following the Teyame and Datono acquisition continues to improve. Revenue has more than doubled, gross profit has grown 354%. The Customer Engagement Services segment contributed $6.53 million in revenue and $1.82 million in gross profit during the quarter, and our Company-wide gross margin has expanded to 23.2% for the nine months from 13.4%. This is why we believe we are well positioned to continue building a diversified, AI-driven technology portfolio."
