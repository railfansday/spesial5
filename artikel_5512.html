Cathie Wood's ARK Invest bought $90 million of Anthropic on its IPO debut

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Anthropic stock rose 17% on its first day and fell in premarket trading Friday; the purchase spanned six ARK funds

ARK Invest bought about $90.8 million of Anthropic stock across six of its funds on Thursday, the day the AI company began trading on the Nasdaq $NDAQ, according to CNBC. ARK, founded and run by CEO Cathie Wood, had a pre-IPO position in Anthropic through its ARK Venture Fund.

The firm bought roughly 935,000 shares of Anthropic stock in total, according to CoinDesk. ARK Innovation ETF accounted for 556,777 of those shares, CoinDesk said, citing an emailed trading update from the firm.

Anthropic makes up 0.3% of the $6.1 billion ARK Innovation ETF, according to CNBC. Tesla $TSLA and SpaceX are the fund's largest positions, and combined they make up over 21% of its assets. Anthropic ranks outside the fund's top 10 holdings, according to CoinDesk.

ARK Innovation is down more than 10% this year, while the S&P 500 is up 4.8%. According to CNBC, Wood often adds to positions in favored names gradually, over extended periods.

Anthropic stock opened at $111.50 on Thursday and finished the day at $97.17, which is 13% under that opening price. The close was 17% above the $83 IPO price, according to CNBC. It traded lower in premarket trading Friday.

Anthropic priced its initial public offering at $83 a share on Wednesday, selling 980 million shares of Class A common stock. The offering totals $81 billion, topping the $75 billion SpaceX collected in its IPO before its underwriters exercised their overallotment option.

Of the shares sold, Anthropic is selling 956,351,354 and certain selling stockholders are selling 23,648,646. Anthropic receives no proceeds from the selling stockholders' portion. The company gave its underwriters a 30-day option to buy up to 147 million more shares of Class A common stock. The offering is expected to close on Friday, subject to customary closing conditions.
