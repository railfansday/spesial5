Prime Video’s God Of War Officially Confirms Major Game Actor’s Return

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Dave Bautista will go face-to-face with an iconic game actor for God of War.

Marking the first-ever live-action adaptation of the hit action game franchise, the Prime Video series is specifically adapting the 2018 game in which Kratos and his son Atreus venture through Midgard and ancient Scandinavia to spread the ashes of their wife and mother, Faye, all while Kratos struggles to keep his son's demigod nature a secret. Headed by For All Mankind's Ronald D. Moore, the God of War series infamously hit a snag when original Kratos actor Ryan Hurst was injured, leading to Dave Bautista taking over the part.

As filming gears up to resume in the near future, Prime Video has announced that the God of War series has officially added Deborah Ann Woll to its cast. The star will be reprising her role of Faye from the recent games, making her the first major game actor to take part in the series after Ryan Hurst was injured and had to depart his role of Kratos.

Adapted from the PlayStation video game franchise, God of War is an action-adventure drama series in development under Amazon. The series will center on series protagonist Kratos, but no other information on the series has been divulged.
