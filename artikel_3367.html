‘NCIS’ Spin-Off Series “Cannot Go Out Like This” Amid Renewal Uncertainty

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Since the latest seasons premiered, the Tuesday night NCIS lineup has given CBS a lot to be happy about this fall. All three of the franchise's current titles premiered as the most-watched programs in their respective time slots, earning a collective 39% viewership increase year-over-year for the long-running procedural name. While the flagship led the night at 6.45 million viewers and remains a favorite in its 24th season, the newcomer NCIS: New York has similarly been performing well, starting with a strong 5.62 million viewers on average as fans flocked to see the return of LL COOL J's Sam Hanna. Last but not least was NCIS: Origins at 4.39 million viewers, with an increase of 20% from last year; yet, of the three, it has easily enjoyed the most eventful fall run.

Origins Season 3 has split its focus between the past and present this season, bringing Mark Harmon back in front of the camera for a mission with a direct connection to his past. After being attacked at his remote cabin in Alaska in the premiere, he's been on the hunt for answers as to exactly who the men were, why they targeted him, and who exactly was the "one to go" they mentioned after believing they killed Gibbs. To learn the truth, he's turned to some old friends, meeting with Michael Weatherly's Tony DiNozzo for some information and linking up with the also off-the-books Ellie Bishop (Emily Wickersham) for some extra help in the field, among others. For as entertaining as it has been in the eyes of critics and audiences, the end is coming soon thanks to a shorter ten-episode order.

All the twists and turns seem to be leading to something big and revelatory that could forever change Gibbs in the modern day, which could make for a fitting series finale on January 19. However, Kyle Schmid, the series' young Mike Franks, doesn't want this to be the end. Speaking to TV Insider about the current season, he teased what's bound to be an action-packed final few episodes to close out Season 3 and reflected on how Origins has always delivered on the drama with its finales. In his eyes, the current run ends on a note that simply begs for a Season 4. Beyond just the finale, he fully believes the procedural prequel has fully found its groove and is delivering on the promise of digging into Gibbs' time at Camp Pendleton, saying:

“The show is exceptional this year. I’m so proud of the writing team, our cast, our crew, everybody that I had the pleasure of working with for the past four or five months. I think we found our thing this year. I think the show’s found its shape. I think the characters have found their meaning. And let me tell you, when I say I don’t want the show to end like this, the show can’t end like this, and I mean that with all my f---ing heart. So when you get to the finale of the show, you will know damn well what I mean when I say it cannot go out like this, so there better be a Season f---ing 4.”

When the series returns from its Thanksgiving break on December 1, Gibbs will continue trying to unravel what happened to Lala (Mariel Molino). Season 3 delivered a bombshell by confirming that the former Major Case Response Team leader's old love interest never died in a plane crash, as he once believed. Episode 7, "Don't Let the Days Go By," will see him and Bishop linking back up with some of his few remaining Camp Pendleton colleagues to see what they know, including Vera Strickland, played once more by Roma Maffia. Their search will run alongside an investigation in 1993, where Gibbs (Austin Stowell) and the team find a murdered Marine with his tongue cut out, putting them on the trail of a dangerous Russian serial killer. Caleb Foote, Diany Rodriguez, and Tyla Abercrumbie also star.

NCIS: Origins Season 3 airs Tuesdays at 10 p.m. ET/PT on CBS. The Season 3 finale will wrap up Harmon's arc on January 19 and make way for NCIS: Sydney on January 26.
