Turn ChatGPT into your new literary curator

There’s such a gratifying feeling that comes from finishing a really good book, closing it up, reflecting on its quality and placing it back on your bookshelf.

I spend a good deal of time flipping through autobiographies written by my favorite wrestlers, musicians and media personalities. Visual history books dedicated to the video games, comic books and anime I love also keep me entertained for hours. I’m always searching for the next great book that’ll keep me from staring at my phone and reading rage-baiting tweets in my spare time.

ChatGPT is capable of stepping up to be everyone's personal book curator. It has certainly put me on the right path to finding even more quality reads based on what it knows about the genres I like, the authors I’m a fan of and even the sort of books that I’m not really all that into.

These prompts have helped expand my collection of books, and I’m sure they’ll do the same for all my fellow bookworms reading this right now. Try any of these out and see where ChatGPT leads you next on your journey for more quality literature.

Inputting any of the following seven prompts into ChatGPT, while also providing it with your literary tastes, favorite authors, preferred styles of writing that compel you the most and current mood, will help it highlight a list of books you may end up adoring next.

The first prompt works quite well in commanding ChatGPT to examine the deeper literary qualities attached to your favorite books. The second prompt works to showcase the type of books that deliver the same type of emotional experiences you derived from your last read. The third prompt is perfect for book hunters who are on the lookout for less celebrated books that you may not have known about beforehand. The fourth prompt is the ultimate mood-book matcher, as it takes note of how you’re currently feeling and shows you the type of books that could get you out of a mental funk or put you in a more relaxing mood.

The fifth prompt puts ChatGPT in the mindset of a book recommendation engine that gives you the sort of books that explore genres you may have overlooked. The sixth prompt goes even further to help ChatGPT suggest what books you should read, as it mentions both your literary likes and dislikes.

As for the seventh and final prompt, it works as a survey that gives ChatGPT everything it could possibly know about you to give you the best book recommendations possible.

And as an extra follow-up response, this next prompt can help ChatGPT do another round of evaluation to see which books truly match your tastes and weed out any made-up books it may have suggested:

Now challenge your own recommendations. Which [number] books on this list are the weakest matches for my tastes, and why? Replace them with better alternatives. Don't invent titles or authors, and tell me where you're uncertain.

Running to ChatGPT to tell it to recommend you a bunch of new and old books worth reading without being as descriptive as possible about your tastes is a waste of time. The chatbot needs every piece of information about your interests across the literary spectrum to tailor its book recommendations to match everything you enjoy (and don’t enjoy as much).

Hopefully, by the end of each ChatGPT session that kicks off with any of these prompts, you’ll leave it with one or a bunch of strong suggestions that could keep you and your book club occupied for months on end. Let me know how you get on in the comments below.
