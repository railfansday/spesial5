Tottenham: Roberto De Zerbi issues Savio and James Maddison injury update as Mathys Tel timeline confirmed

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Savio, James Maddison, Destiny Udogie and Lucas Bergvall are all ready to return for Tottenham, Mathys Tel has been ruled out for up to three week with an Achilles injury.

Summer signing Savio has been ruled out since the 1-1 draw at Manchester United in October, missing the last four games with a muscular issue, while Udogie was left out of De Zerbi’s squad as Spurs lost 1-0 to Leeds before the international break.

Maddison has not trained consistently since the start of the season and came off midway through Spurs’ 2-0 win over Chelsea with a suspected groin issue. The 29-year-old has not featured since that historic victory at Stamford Bridge, despite being available for selection.

Bergvall is also expected to be in contention for minutes against Ipswich, having returned from a knee injury sustained against United. The Swedish midfielder was not called up for international duty by Graham Potter, but could make his first appearance since the start of October.

The fit-again quartet were all pictured in training at Hotspur Way this week and are now in contention to face Ipswich on Saturday, but Tosin Adarabioyo and Tel will miss out.

Spurs manager Roberto De Zerbi told reporters on Friday: “Bergvall, Maddison, Savio are back, [but] Tosin and Mathys Tel, no.

“Tosin is already okay, but it is not the right moment to take any risks. Mathys Tel we need another two, three weeks.”

Asked if Udogie was available, De Zerbi said: “Yes.”

Elaborating on Maddison, who was named in two matchday squads before the international break without getting on the pitch, De Zerbi said: “He is working very well since last Wednesday, working very well every day.

“Before Leeds, from Chelsea to Leeds game, he worked just three times on the pitch. If you don’t work during the week, you risk injury. In any way, you lose the normal physical condition you need to play in Premier League.”

While De Zerbi has been boosted by the return of several key players, the Spurs boss is set to be without French winger Tel until the middle of December after the French forward suffered an Achilles injury against Leeds.

The 21-year-old was on the end of a heavy challenge from Leeds defender Jayden Bogle and left Elland Road with a protective boot on before withdrawing from international duty with France’s Under-21s.

Mykhailo Mudryk is also still absent due to the ankle injury picked up in training in September

The original prognosis was two to two months, but De Zerbi says the loanee from Chelsea is still a few weeks away from a return.

He continued: “[The] plan was to play Ipswich or Sunderland, and then he felt no pain, but not comfortable last week. They take two weeks more. Step by step.”
