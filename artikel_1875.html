Jurgen Klopp gushing in praise for Arsenal star: ‘He is a complete package’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Jurgen Klopp has talked up Arsenal’s Kai Havertz as ‘a complete package as a top player’ as he hopes the forward can have a big impact for Germany.

The 27-year-old has won 64 caps for his country, scoring 25 goals, and was in action as Germany beat Serbia 3-1 on Friday.

The former Chelsea man will be in the team on Monday night for the crucial Nations League clash with Netherlands, as Klopp looks to take his side into the quarter-finals.

The Germany boss has looked to build confidence in Havertz by talking up the forward’s qualities, which he feels can match up to almost anyone in the game.

‘I have often faced teams defended by Kai, and I know very well in tactical preparations that facing Kai is not an easy task. He is a complete package as a top player,’ said Klopp.

‘Mikel Arteta relies heavily on him because Kai is always reliable to put in an extraordinary performance thanks to his technical capacity and intelligence.’

He added: ‘Kai and I have agreed that there is still huge potential that we want to develop further together in the national team.’

Havertz has not been in good goal-scoring form of late, netting just once in his last 13 appearances for club and country.

That was a crucial goal in a 1-0 Arsenal win over Liverpool, but finding the back of the net has not come easily for the Gunners star.

He will look to improve his recent record in Berlin on Monday night as Germany try and topple the group-leaders, although Klopp says the long-term project is more important than short-term success.

‘We want success right now, but the process we have set in motion takes precedence over everything else,’ he said.

‘We want to put in a top performance, then we will see how that measures up against the Netherlands.

‘We are really looking forward to this game. We wanted this opportunity, now we have got it and we want to make the most of it.

‘[Netherlands have] basically got the same squad that played at the World Cup, that is the difference between us. We are in a period of transition right now.’
