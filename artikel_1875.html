Sources: Scott Parker sets sights on 'proven goalscorer' as West Ham priority clear

West Ham United are set to prioritise a move to bring in a regular goalscorer in the January transfer window, sources have told Football Insider.

New boss Scott Parker is keen to make several changes to the squad at the London Stadium, with the team slowly falling away from the promotion race in the Championship.

Parker has already made his desire for an overhaul clear to the recruitment team, who are assembling a list of targets ahead of the window.

Sources say that the boss feels that the addition of a "proven goalscorer" is a necessity if West Ham are to get back to the Premier League at the first attempt.

The new boss remains winless after his opening four games in charge, and the East London outfit are now sitting in 12th.

They have quickly fallen 10 points adrift of the automatic promotion places, and know that they must get January right to get back into contention.

West Ham are the Championship's leading goalscorers so far this term, but their tally of 38 league strikes has been inflated somewhat.

Their pair of 6-0 victories over Wrexham and Lincoln City have helped to inflate that figure a little, and perhaps hide some of the issues that have been brewing.

Despite the Hammers' lofty goal tally, just six of their strikes have come from their recognised strikers.

Taty Castellanos and Pablo both have one goal apiece, while Joel Piroe is not faring much better on four.

That is a situation that Parker is hoping to see rectified in January, given the sustained issues that the trio are experiencing.

However, West Ham are also aware that goalscoring strikers often come at a premium in the middle of a campaign.

Football Insider revealed on Tuesday that West Ham feel that selling Castellanos in January is a move that will "suit all parties."

The Argentine has made it clear that he is keen to leave the club, and West Ham have been hugely disappointed with his performances since their relegation.

The Hammers are prepared to sell the 28-year-old at a loss, knowng that it will be "very difficult" for them to recoup the £26million they paid for him last January.

Castellanos netted six Premier League goals from just 18 games last term, but he has struggled to find any sort of form so far in 2026-27.
