Tottenham: Roberto De Zerbi lifts lid on James Maddison injury woes as long-standing issue revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Roberto De Zerbi has revealed that James Maddison is still suffering from the same hip issue he sustained during pre-season.

The Tottenham midfielder has started just two games in all competitions this season and has not featured since coming off injured midway through Spurs’ 2-0 victory over Chelsea last month.

Maddison is available for selection against Ipswich this weekend and was also named in the matchday squad in each of Spurs’ last two league games against Crystal Palace and Leeds.

However, De Zerbi’s decision not to bring him off the bench against Palace or Leeds has raised concerns that Maddison is still not fully fit.

The 29-year-old returned from an anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) injury at the end of last season, and he had hoped to use pre-season to build his way back to full fitness and force his way into De Zerbi’s starting lineup.

Maddison’s summer was, however, disrupted by setbacks, with a hip issue restricting his ability to train or play with the rest of the squad, and De Zerbi has now revealed that, four months into the season, the same problem is continuing to hamper the England playmaker.

“After Chelsea he had the same injury he felt in the Chelsea game and not a big injury but he was not able to work. He had a problem with his hip,” De Zerbi said in his pre-match press conference on Friday afternoon.

“It’s not a big problem, but if you don’t work consistently, you lose the physical condition, and to play in this level of competition you have to stay in the top physical condition.

“He is still young, 29 years is not old, and he has to push and demand of himself the top in terms of physical condition, because if he is 80 or 90 per cent of his physical condition he makes the difference for us, he changes the games.”

Maddison, who has been unable to train consistently this season, saw his professionalism called into question after Spurs’ defeat to Leeds earlier this month when pictures surfaced of him drinking with friends.

The incident in question occurred on the first of three days off for Spurs’ first-team squad, and De Zerbi says he has no issues with Maddison’s conduct, instead joking that he was “very angry” that he was not invited.
