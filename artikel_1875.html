Liverpool injury update: Virgil van Dijk, Giovanni Leoni, Jeremie Frimpong latest news and return dates

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Liverpool have been handed a double defensive boost ahead of the Premier League clash with Manchester United.

It has been a mixed international break for Andoni Iraola’s side on the injury front, with concerns over Virgil van Dijk and Dominik Szoboszlai.

However, there was some positive news, as star striker Alexander Isak was rested by Sweden on Tuesday night, meaning he should be fully fresh and raring to go on Sunday afternoon.

With that in mind, here is the latest Liverpool injury news and return dates…

Leoni is one of two Liverpool players reported by The Athletic to have returned to first team training after a long injury lay-off.

The Italian has missed a full year of action since suffering an ACL injury, with further issues in his knee. Originally pencilled for a September return, the complications have pushed Leoni’s comeback to December.

Head coach Iraola explained: “It’s taking more time than we wanted. The planning was to train with the team in this past international break [but] probably it’s going to be in the next international break, so we’ve lost one month in the process.

“Giovanni’s injury, I wasn’t here but talking to the medical department, it was not just an ACL – it was a bad injury, a lot of things around the knee were involved apart from the ACL.”

However, despite a return to training, it is expected that Leoni is still a few weeks away from being in contention for some playing time.

Potential return date: Saturday December 12, vs Leeds (H)

Conor Bradley is the other long-term absentee back in first-team training. The Northern Ireland international suffered bone and ligament damage in his knee against Arsenal in January.

Bradley was predicted to resume training with the first team during the international break, implying that he is keeping pace with his expected recovery.

Potential return date: Wednesday, December 2 vs Sunderland (H)

Van Dijk dropped out of the Netherlands’ squad for Nations League clashes with Greece and Germany, with the defender’s absence explained as fitness reasons.

“Virgil van Dijk [and Brian Brobbey] have been dropped from the Dutch national team's upcoming international matches. Both players did not return fit from the past weekend's matches and are therefore unavailable,” a statement read.

“The squad thus currently consists of 23 players.”

Iraola will give a full update in his pre-Man United press conference, though speaking before the international break, Van Dijk admitted to plaything through a ‘little niggle’.

After the win over Crystal Palace, the Liverpool captain said: "Mentally I’m in a good place. Physically I’ve been struggling with a little niggle the last couple of weeks but I do everything in my power to be out there.”

Potential return date: Sunday November 22, vs Manchester United (H)

Dominik Szboszlai’s availability for both club and country has been impacted by a knee problem this season.

At the start of the international break the Hungarian FA stated they would be running tests on Szoboszlai, before the midfielder played the full 90 in both of Nations League matches.

Unless the issue has been re-aggravated by travel and those games, Szoboszlai should be available to face the Red Devils on Sunday.

Potential return date: Sunday November 22, vs Manchester United (H)

Ryan Gravenberch was another Dutch international that did not feature in games with Greece and Germany, having also missed Liverpool’s last two matches due to a heel fracture.

Unlike Van Dijk, Gravenberch was not named in Dutch boss Xavi’s initial squad for the Nations League double header. The midfielder had been slated to return against Crystal Palace, but instead missed out.

Iraola’s most recent comments on Gravenberch implied that with two weeks of rest, he would be available for the Manchester United game.

“The only doubt we will have is Ryan,” the Spaniard said. “The others will be the same as we have been.

“The Ryan Gravenberch thing is a day-to-day thing, it’s a matter of pain. Let’s see if he can deal with the pain and he can help us.”

Potential return date: Sunday November 22, vs Manchester United (H)

One of four Dutch players in the Liverpool squad, Jeremie Frimpong also missed out on representing his country during the latest international break.

There was small hope that the right wing-back would have been available for the Crystal Palace, but another two weeks of recovery and Frimpong might be able to make his return against United.

Iraola said:“Frimpong is obviously a new muscle injury and it happened, I think, in the second or third minute he was playing, difficult to predict and we will have to wait until he recovers.

“But it’s going to be difficult, I think, before the [November international] break. We will try but it’s going to be difficult to have him.”

Potential return date: Sunday November 22, vs Manchester United (H)
