Kyle Shanahan: 'Nothing has changed' with injured rookie WR

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The San Francisco 49ers still don't have a clear timeline for rookie receiver De'Zhaun Stribling's return. When asked by reporters Wednesday, head coach Kyle Shanahan said there's no update on Stribling's ankle.

"I don't know. I haven't asked," Shanahan said. "I mean, nothing's changed, so I think it's a couple weeks away."

Stribling suffered a serious ankle injury in Week 1 that required surgery. He has been on injured reserve since and was expected to miss at least 10 weeks. That time has since passed, and it appears Stribling is no closer to coming back to the field.

Shanahan's answer suggests the rookie is still working his way back, although it's unclear if Stribling is on the same timetable he was previously, or if his timeline has been pushed. The most recent update around Stribling from mid-October was that he wouldn't come back until after Week 12.

By the coach's estimate, Stribling remains a couple of weeks away from returning.

The injury cost Stribling most of his first NFL season. The 49ers are 9-1 and have managed without him, despite dealing with other injuries at receiver, but his return would give the receiver room even more depth down the stretch. The current depth chart includes Deebo Samuel, Mike Evans, Demarcus Robinson and Jordan Watkins.
