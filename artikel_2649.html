Marlon Humphrey apologizes for saying Baltimore is "best at making criminals"

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Ravens cornerback Marlon Humphrey found himself in some hot water for comments he made about Baltimore on Wednesday.

During a since-deleted episode of his podcast, Humphrey said “they’re best at making criminals” when asked what the city is best at making. Humphrey’s words were met with an angry response in the city and he tried to walk them back with an apology while speaking to reporters after practice.

“As you guys know, I’ve been here my whole career,” Humphrey said, via Jeff Zrebiec of TheAthletic.com. “Spent lots of offseasons here, welcomed a son here. I’m an investor in the city, an investor in different areas (and) parts of the state. Obviously, I do know the effect that words have, and just would like to apologize for those comments.”

Humphrey’s apology was noted by Baltimore Mayor Brandon M. Scott, who wondered in a social media post how Humphrey would respond “if prominent Baltimoreans used their platform to imply that Baltimore once produced great defensive backs but we have recently lost our way.” Scott added that “a verbal apology is not enough” for those who look up to Ravens players and invited Humphrey to meet with people in the city working on “preventing others from making the mistakes they made.”
