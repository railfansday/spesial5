Commanders at New York Giants 'Thursday Night Football' betting odds, picks and predictions: Best bets, player props

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Bright lights will shine down on MetLife Stadium on Thursday, when the New York Giants host the Washington Commanders in a crucial showdown between NFC East rivals.

The Giants enter this meeting with their all-too-familiar foes fresh off a workmanlike, 19-11 road victory over the beleaguered Philadelphia Eagles. Quarterbacks Jameis Winston and J.J. McCarthy combined under center to lead New York (5-2-1) to its second consecutive win.

As for their opponent, this is the most important game of the season for the Commanders. The struggling franchise enters this affair fresh off a 20-13 home loss to the Los Angeles Rams.

It might sound cliche, but if there were ever a must-win situation for Washington (2-5-1), this game is clearly it. A defeat would put the Commanders — 0-3 on the road — four games behind the Giants in the division and deliver a serious blow to their chances of reaching the postseason.

When it comes to the betting market, you can find the Giants favored anywhere from 1.5 points to 2.5 points, depending on the book. As for the total, the number currently ranges from 40.5 to 41.5 points.

With plenty on the line for both teams, let's take a deep dive into this contest and see where we can find some betting value.

The Giants have a pretty impressive record, but whom have they beaten? I will give New York and its blue-bleeding faithful a checkmark for what I think was a quality road win against the struggling Houston Texans.

Other than that, the Giants have victories against Dallas, Tennessee, Arizona and Philadelphia. The combined record of those teams? An ugly 10-25. Not one of those teams is above .500 in the standings. Throw in Houston, and New York has five victories against teams with a combined 13 wins.

On the flip side, I don't know what to make of the Commanders. They handed Seattle a 33-31 defeat on Sept. 27 at Northwest Stadium. They played the Rams and San Francisco 49ers tight in close defeats. Yet what sticks out are the loss to the Indianapolis Colts and that unforgivable tie against the Giants in their previous meeting.

To sum it up, Washington is mediocrity defined.

When it comes to the respective offenses, they're very much alike. The Commanders rank 11th in rushing yards (118.3 yards per game), while the Giants check in at 17th (112.1 YPG). As for the aerial attacks, they're also similar: New York sits 16th in total passing yards (221 YPG), with Washington not far off in 21st (207 YPG).

I don't trust QB Jayden Daniels and his banged-up offense. And I don't like this team's chances of getting its first win on the road in a prime-time confrontation with an opponent trending upward.

I'm jumping on New York to get the job done against Washington and take a firm grip on the NFC East under first-year head coach John Harbaugh.

WR Malik Nabers – 70+ receiving yards (-104) | 80+ receiving yards (+146) | 90+ receiving yards (+205) | 100+ receiving yards (+300)

Nabers had a relatively subdued showing against Philadelphia, finishing with just five receptions (on eight targets) for 43 yards. However, those paltry stats by his standards were nothing compared to what he did prior to that game.

In his four contests before the clash with the Eagles, Nabers went off:

10/25 at Houston: 7 catches for 111 yards

10/18 vs. New Orleans: 12 catches for 165 yards

10/11 at Washington: 11 catches for 121 yards

10/04 vs. Arizona: 6 catches for 112 yards

Now, he faces that same Washington side against which he was targeted a stunning 20 times in their previous meeting.

I'm taking a swing on Nabers via this ladder play for receiving yards. McCarthy is going to target Nabers plenty throughout the night, giving me optimism the wideout will go off against the Washington secondary again.

LB Arvell Reese to record a sack (+182, FanDuel)

The rookie linebacker, selected fifth overall in this year's NFL Draft, has been so good that he's currently the even-money favorite to earn Defensive Rookie of the Year honors at sportsbooks. The Ohio State product leads the Giants' defense with 68 total tackles (49 solo and 19 assists) and has a team-best 4.5 sacks.

Reese is coming off his most productive game of his young career, finishing with 12 tackles (10 solo) and a whopping 3.0 sacks in last week's road win against Philadelphia.

Now, Reese and the New York defense face a Washington outfit that has been terrible protecting Daniels of late. The Commanders have allowed 15 sacks in their past four contests.

We're getting close to 2-to-1 odds on Arvell getting to Daniels, so I can't pass up this angle tied to a red-hot defender facing a subpar Washington offensive line.

As a Baltimore Ravens fan, it has been bittersweet watching Likely absolutely light things up for New York.

The New York brass knew what it was doing when it signed the 26-year-old to a three-year, $40 million deal in the offseason. His season résumé – 46 receptions for 448 yards and 6 touchdowns – has put him among the elite at the TE position, and I don't see a decline coming his way.

Just how good are those numbers compared to other tight ends? Likely ranks first in touchdowns, second in targets (66), third in receptions and sixth in receiving yards. Now, he faces a Washington defense allowing just under 70 receiving yards to tight ends (68.8 yards, to be exact), which is fourth-most among NFL defenses.

Earlier this season, Likely put together his best showing against Washington. He finished with 11 catches (on 13 targets) for 94 yards and two touchdowns. The Commanders' defense couldn't stop him then, particularly in the red zone. That same defense is going to have problems containing him in the rematch.

Back Likely to find the end zone at this nice price.

McCarthy averaged almost 5.0 yards per carry in 10 games last season, finding the end zone on four occasions. Despite a limited sample, the Giants' quarterback has gotten busy with his legs on designed plays or when flushed out of the pocket this campaign, to the tune of nine carries for 51 yards and a 5.7 YPC.

New York running back Cam Skattebo is set to return to the lineup, but I think McCarthy will have a couple of opportunities to get loose on the ground deep in Washington territory and get in the end zone for the first time this season.
