Oklahoma State football coach Eric Morris receives contract extension, per report

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

STILLWATER — Oklahoma State football coach Eric Morris is staying put in Stillwater.

The first-year coach received a six-year contract extension, basically extending his contract by two years, with a "significant pay raise", ESPN's Pete Thamel reported Wednesday morning.

Morris' new contract will run through 2032, according to Thamel.

Morris made a base salary of $3.8 million this season, according to his original contract obtained by The Oklahoman last December after his hire. He was set to earn $20 million in base salary over the term of the five-year contract he signed. That agreement ran through the end of the 2030 season.

Morris already locked up a $150,000 bonus when the Cowboys, who are 7-4, locked up a bowl appearance earlier this season.

Morris had already become a popular name around the country for other program openings. But this keeps him with the Cowboys for the immediate future.
