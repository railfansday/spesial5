Cowboys place RB Tyler Goodson on injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Cowboys said this week that running back Tyler Goodson will miss multiple weeks due to knee and ankle injuries and we learned on Saturday that he’ll be out for at least the next four games.

Goodson was placed on injured reserve as part of a series of roster moves the Cowboys made ahead of Sunday’s game against the 49ers. Those moves also included elevating running back Israel Abanikanda from the practice squad to join Javonte Williams in the backfield.

Cornerback Derion Kendrick was also elevated for Sunday’s game. Joey Porter Jr. and DaRon Bland are both listed as questionable.

In addition to those moves, the Cowboys waived defensive end Tyrus Wheat while signing tight end James Mitchell off the practice squad and activating defensive tackle Jonathan Bullard from injured reserve.
