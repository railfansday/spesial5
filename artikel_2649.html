Giants put Greg Newsome on injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Giants head coach John Harbaugh said on Monday that cornerback Greg Newsome will miss multiple weeks with a high-ankle sprain and a clearer picture of just how many weeks came on Tuesday.

Newsome was placed on the injured reserve list, which means he’ll be out for at least the next four games. Newsome signed a one-year deal with the Giants in the offseason and signed a three-year extension earlier this month.

Paulson Adebo is expected to see more playing time with Newsome out of the lineup.

The Giants filled Newsome’s roster spot by signing linebacker Dawuane Smoot off of the practice squad. He has appeared in three games and had three tackles and a sack in those appearances.
