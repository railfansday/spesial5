Activision Dons Itself The Home Of Banjo-Kazooie In New Job Listing

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

"and now… Halo, Age of Empires, Sea of Thieves, and Banjo Kazooie"

Back in September, Xbox officially announced its reorganisation plans for legendary studio Rare, moving it under Activision alongside Halo, Age of Empires, Sea Of Thieves, and Banjo-Kazooie.

At the time, Rare was reportedly working on "other projects" beyond Sea of Thieves, but decisions about their future were now in Activision's hands. The two companies have collaborated pretty well in the past, considering how well Activision handled Crash Bandicoot and Spyro.

However, a new job posting on Activision's LinkedIn page suggests that another Rare IP, Banjo-Kazooie, has found a home amongst its new overseers. The job is for 'Product Manager, Emerging Franchises' with responsibilities to lead market planning, publishing strategies, drive business performance, and a whole bunch of other corporate fluff. What we found interesting is the accompanying mission statement.

Here's a look at the 'Your Mission' segment:

"Activision is home to iconic gaming franchises including Call of Duty®, Crash Bandicoot™, Spyro the Dragon™, and now… Halo®, Age of Empires®, Sea of Thieves™, and Banjo Kazooie®. Become part of the team working on building, growing, and shaping many of these franchises."

It's been a good 18 years since Banjo-Kazooie: Nuts & Bolts, already longer than GTA VI's 13-year wait, so you've gotta feel for Banjo fans. According to the job posting, both Sea of Thieves and Banjo-Kazooie now fall under Activision, with a team working on "building, growing, and shaping many of these franchises".

What happens next for Banjo-Kazooie has been a hot topic of conversation for donkeys now, and if this job listing is to be taken at face value, could we finally be looking at a new game in the near future? According to former Rare employee Grant Kirkhope, at least three studios have already pitched a new Banjo-Kazooie game to Microsoft. So, the interest is out there. Let's just wait and see what Activision does.
