Guide: Here's How To Claim All The Xbox 25th Anniversary In-Game Items

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

That translucent green do be looking fire

Xbox's 25th Anniversary translucent green look is killer, and I'm a big fan of the cosmetics that Xbox's first-party titles have whipped up to support the milestone.

We thought we'd do a quick guide on how to get all Xbox Anniversary items across Forza Horizon 6, Halo: Campaign Evolved, Gears of War: E-Day, Minecraft, Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024, and Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4.

Most of these are free to claim through in-game stores or redemption codes, but it's worth saying the Modern Warfare 4 M4 skin - arguably the best anniversary cosmetic of the lot - will require some sort of spending.

So, here's a look at all the Xbox 25th Anniversary in-game items and how to claim to them:

On this page: Here's How To Claim All The Xbox 25th Anniversary In-Game Items

How to claim: Find it in the Gifts tab of the Message Center

We are celebrating 25 years of XBOX the Horizon way with a new livery for Forza Horizon 6‘s cover car, the 2025 GR GT. This new livery features a unique design that calls back to the original XBOX’s colors and materials. Find it in the Gifts tab of the Message Center!

How to claim: Go to Halo Waypoint's Redemption page and enter this code - GWT9-DNBT-7X9S-DJCX-8KH3

Embrace your inner “X factor” with the Damascus Prototype Armor Style, available to all Halo: Campaign Evolved players. Click this redemption link to unlock it on Halo Waypoint with code GWT9-DNBT-7X9S-DJCX-8KH3

How to claim: Go to the in-game Store and redeem the Xbox Anniversary Weapon Skin Set

To celebrate 25 years of XBOX, players will be able to claim the XBOX Anniversary Weapon Skin Set for free in the Gears of War: E-Day in-game shop. Once our shop rotates the following Tuesday, November 17, the weapon will enter the player barracks where it can be acquired with our free-to-earn grind currency (Coins) or purchased with premium currency.

How to claim: You can claim the creator item from the Minecraft dressing room

Celebrate 25 years of XBOX in blocky style by claiming your free OG Green Character Creator item! This base player skin covers you from head to toe in the iconic XBOX green. You can wear whatever you want over it or keep it as is, the green translucent glow will let you shine either way! Claim it for free in the Minecraft dressing room between November 10 at 10 AM PT and December 10 at 10 AM PT.

*Requires Minecraft: Bedrock Edition (sold separately). 1 Character Creator item per person/account, must be redeemed by December 10, 2026 at 10 AM PT.

How to claim: Automatically added to Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 owners

Microsoft Flight Simulator pilots, get ready to celebrate a major milestone in gaming history. To mark the 25th Anniversary of XBOX, we’re excited to announce the release of four brand-new commemorative liveries that all throw back to the XBOX original logo and designs. Available automatically to all Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 owners, these commemorative liveries are available in the Aircraft Livery menu for the Boeing 737 MAX, XCub, Hot Air Balloon, and Skyship 600.

How to claim: By purchasing Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 Vault Edition, or through the Vault Edition Upgrade on Xbox Series consoles

Players who pre-ordered the game digitally on XBOX Series X|S will view the Inner Circuit Blueprint in a translucent OG Green visual treatment. Designed as a tribute to 25 years of XBOX, the Inner Circuit Blueprint takes cues from classic translucent Xbox hardware. Its transparent green finish exposes the weapon’s internal systems, turning the technology beneath the surface into part of the visual identity.

Celebrate 25 years of Xbox with an appropriate pose for your pirate. The X25 Emote is available as an Xbox Game Pass In-Game Benefit until Nov 30th.
