Star Wars: Galactic Racer Gets Local Splitscreen Multiplayer Next Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Star Wars: Galactic Racer has been one of 2026’s breakout titles, but much of the game’s praise stems from its exceptional roguelike single-player mode. If you want to race against human players, you can only do so online. Thankfully, for fans of couch multiplayer, that’s going to change.

Developer Fuse Games announced yesterday that local splitscreen multiplayer is coming to Star Wars: Galactic Racer early next year. The mode lets you race up to three other players on the same screen. That's pretty much all there is to it, but given that local multiplayer was the game's biggest omission and, as a result, the most requested addition, there's probably no better news for Galactic Racer fans.

We’ve heard you! Local split-screen multiplayer racing is coming in early 2027. A dedicated team at Fuse Games has been working hard to develop a new mode: Free Play. Choose your vehicle, character, planet, and track to race alone or with up to 3 other players in local splitscreen action.[image or embed]

Star Wars: Galactic Racer is available now on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, and PC. Read our review of the game here.
