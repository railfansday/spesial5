All vault locations in the Fortnite Chapter 7 mini season

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

A guaranteed location to find rare chests

Vaults are locked locations around the map that contain rare chests and a sprite chest in Fortnite Chapter 7's mini season.

To get into a vault, you need to find a vault key, which can be found randomly as chest loot. They're a bit rare, but they're worth holding onto if you're near a vault. However, vaults are a highly sought after commodity, and there are only so many of them.

Here's the location of every vault in Fortnite Chapter 7's mini season, so you know where to go as soon as you get a vault key.

There are nine vaults in Fortnite Chapter 7's mini season. To open one, you need to have a vault key. With the vault key equipped, the closest unopened vault that is in the storm will appear as a lock on your map. However, vaults in the storm might still be unopened, so if you have a ton of healing items, they still might be worth going to. Inside each vault, you'll find four rare chests and one rare sprite chest.
