Pregnant Savannah Chrisley Defends Not Wanting to Vaccinate Her Baby

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Savannah Chrisley made a decision against vaccinating her first baby upon his arrival.

“I am not, but again, that is my choice,” Chrisley, 29, said on the Tuesday, November 17, episode of her “Unlocked” podcast. “I have friends who are having babies around the same time … [and] they do believe in all the vaccines. Great, like, you have to do what’s best for you and your family. What I love is I have found a pediatrician who is not a one-size-fits-all.”

Savannah, the daughter of Todd and Julie Chrisley, announced in October that she is expecting her first baby with husband Jason Galanis. (Savannah and Galanis, 56, have been together since 2025, secretly tying the knot in the Cayman Islands this summer amid the podcaster’s pregnancy.)

Before giving birth, Savannah specifically sought out a doctor who’d accept her baby boy as a patient no matter his vaccination status.

“Unfortunately in today’s day and age, that’s what you have to look for because the majority of pediatricians won’t accept you if you don’t vaccinate,” she claimed. “And then, if you choose not to vaccinate while you’re with them, they will drop you as a patient.”

The CDC states that vaccines administered to millions of children in the U.S. each year are safe and effective against infectious diseases such as hepatitis B, chickenpox and measles. The Pan American Health Organization announced on Tuesday that America has lost its measles-elimination status after 26 years in a historic setback under the leadership of Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr., a vaccine skeptic.

Savannah stressed that she has yet to make a decision about her child’s entire life.

“That’s not to say, ‘OK, I’m never going to do this.’ I don’t like the pressure of, ‘All right, as soon as they come out of the womb, you’ve got to do this, this and this,’” she stated. “The pediatrician, she was like, ‘75 percent of my patients don’t vaccinate.’ She said there are a lot of them who will say, ‘All right, once they reach their major milestones, then we maybe do one and then we wait and we do another one or if you’re going somewhere and there’s an outbreak of something.’”

Savannah has made her peace with her choice, arguing that no parent “should be shamed” for how they choose to raise their family.

“That’s the decision that you choose to make when it comes to vaccinating,” she stated. “I’ve made my decision, you make yours, and that’s where we’re at.”

Savannah’s decision initially raised eyebrows from Todd, 57.

“I pushed back on that because I vaccinated all of my children,” the father of six recalled. “I don’t think that me using the fact that I vaccinated all of my children is actually a raving review over the way some of my kids have acted.”

Todd has since come around, stressing that he respects Savannah’s decision.

“That’s your choice, and we live in a country where you should have your choice,” the Chrisley Knows Best patriarch noted. “If you’re a Democrat, you should love that she’s exercising her right to choose, and if you’re a Republican, you should love the fact that as a Christian woman who has her moral compass and her values aligned with her lord and savior that she’s making that choice. Remember the Republicans believe pro-life, the Democrats believe in love and acceptance. You should be covered in the blood of both parties.”
