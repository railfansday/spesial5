Apple Podcasts 2026 Show of the Year is ‘Baby, this is Keke Palmer’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Each year, Apple Podcasts names its Show of the Year, and the 2026 winner is Baby, this is Keke Palmer. Apple said the podcast consistently ranked as one of the most popular shows, and praised it for its “exceptional quality and cultural impact” …

For anyone unfamiliar, the show is an interview format, but aims to dig a little deeper than most.

Palmer invites some of the biggest names in entertainment and culture to share the experiences that have shaped them. From exploring self-worth with Oprah Winfrey to discussing creativity, reinvention, and fatherhood with Anderson .Paak, she moves effortlessly between laugh-out-loud exchanges and thoughtful reflections on life, relationships, and success […]

The show pairs Palmer’s quick wit with genuine curiosity, striking a balance between humor and thoughtful inquiry that makes even the most recognizable guests feel like close friends. Her natural ability to connect with people draws audiences into every conversation, while her candor and energy bring a refreshing and unique authenticity to the interview format.

Palmer said that authenticity was a key goal for the show, both for herself and for her guests, and she encourages other creators to take the same path.

Don’t try to manufacture a personality for the camera. Develop a relationship with yourself, get comfortable expressing what is already there, and let the camera capture that […]

Preparation is supposed to support the conversation, not control it. I’m always listening for that moment when somebody says something that opens up an entirely new question.
