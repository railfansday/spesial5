Witcher 4 Has Fewer People Working On It Than It Did 3 Months Ago, But Just Barely

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

As part of CD Projekt's latest earnings report, the Polish developer revealed new information about its projects and headcount.

For the quarter that ended on October 31, The Witcher 4 was the company's biggest project by headcount, with 513 people working on it. That's down from 519 people for the quarter that ended on July 31.

For comparison, the number of developers on Cyberpunk 2 rose from 184 to 199 and staffers on the new IP codenamed Hadar jumped from 33 to 34. The codename Sirius game, believed to be a multiplayer The Witcher game, saw its development staff shrink from 84 to 60; the project has undergone numerous shifts throughout its development as CD Projekt reworks the experience.

CD Projekt's total development headcount rose from 1,045 to 1,051.

In terms of money, CD Projekt reported Q3 revenue of 208 million PLN ($51 million USD) and net earnings of 109 million PLN ($26.8 million). CD Projekt spent a lot--more than 177 million PLN ($43.5 million) on development costs during the quarter--but the company's total cash reserves grew by 31 million PLN ($7.6 million USD).

CD Projekt has a number of big projects in the works, including The Witcher 3: Songs of the Past in 2027 and The Witcher 4 in 2028. Cyberpunk 2 is also on the way, but it doesn't have a release window yet. The company is also working on a game with Scopely, the mobile gaming giant owned by Saudi Arabia.

Cyberpunk 2 was in the news recently, as the game's creative director joked that the RPG is now canceled because GTA 6 is so good.
