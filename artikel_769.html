Shutters Opens Doors to Annual Membership Club

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The oceanfront property, long a favored destination of entertainment industry insiders, has launched Shutters Reserve, a membership program featuring exclusive access and a slew of Shutters-style perks.

Los Angeles isn’t hurting for private membership clubs but Shutters on the Beach just launched a new way to entice potential VIPs.

The popular entertainment industry hideaway, an oceanfront property located just feet from the sand at 1 Pico Boulevard, has followed in the footsteps of luxury hotels like The Ned by launching a membership program.

For an annual fee of $25,000 for couples or $15,000 for individuals, Shutters Reserve members receive exclusive access to the hotel and a slew of Shutters-style perks: a two-night stay in an ocean view room, monthly spa credit (totaling $2,400 for the year), unlimited, year-round gym and pool access, one pool cabana reservation per month, daily use of beach chairs, umbrellas and bikes, complimentary ocean view yoga classes and valet parking, a 10 percent discount on food and beverages, and signing privileges throughout the property.
