Dubois vs Parker announced for WBO heavyweight title clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Daniel Dubois will box former world champion Joseph Parker on January 30 at Manchester's Co-op Live arena; Dubois will be defending the WBO heavyweight world title he secured defeating Fabio Wardley; Parker has had his suspension lifted

Daniel Dubois will defend the WBO heavyweight world championship against Joseph Parker on January 30 at Manchester's Co-op Live arena.

Dubois and Parker were due to box in 2025 for the IBF title, only for Dubois to fall ill and withdraw just three days before the fight.

Parker knocked out short-notice replacement Martin Bakole and then lost a WBO Interim title bout to Fabio Wardley.

In August he announced that he could continue his career after a suspension was lifted following a failed drug test in October 2025.

Dubois lost the IBF strap to Oleksandr Usyk last year but stormed back to form when he twice halted Wardley, winning and retaining the full WBO world championship.

He will now be back in action early in the new year against Parker, who's also recorded standout wins over Deontay Wilder and Zhilei Zhang in recent years.

A former holder of the belt, the New Zealander lost the WBO championship to Anthony Joshua back in 2018 at the Principality Stadium when he became the first professional opponent to go the distance with Joshua.

Frank Warren said: "This heavyweight showdown will deliver a blockbuster beginning to 2027.

"It's a fight everyone wanted to see, and I'm thrilled we can now make it happen. Daniel is entering his prime and when he is on his game it is hard to picture anybody beating him.

"But Joseph has long promised to become world champion again, and now he gets his chance."
