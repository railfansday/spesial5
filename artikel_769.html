Stray Kids Exclusive: Their Thoughts On Enlistment & What Fans Can Expect From RUN IT Tour | Billboard News

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Stray Kids sits down with Tetris Kelly for an exclusive interview about how they feel about enlistment and how the band is getting through it, what fans can expect from their new RUN IT tour, and more!

Hey what’s up Stray Kids? Long time no see! It’s good to see you guys again. I do want to start off by asking something really quick. What is something that made each one of you smile today?

Thanks for the compliment guys and we all love breakfast, but we got to talk about this tour, RUN IT, over 40 cities. Asia, America, Europe. What’s going to be different about this tour than everything else you guys have done?

Just like the tour name RUN IT, we will run a lot, and it will be fast-paced.

Okay, well I can’t wait to see all the running around guys. I will definitely be there. And with you guys having so much touring behind you, what is something that you guys look back on that’s really memorable?

What we do really cherish a lot is being able to see fans have fun, and performing is what keeps us going and receiving that energy and seeing the expressions of being able to see everyone have fun. I think it’s… I think being able to see everyone’s expression is what’s very memorable. It’s a key moment for us.

And you’re talking about the passion of fans, you know they’re going to be so excited about the setlist. So what songs are you preparing, what must you sing, and what you might want to leave behind?

The song I really want to do is “RUN IT.” That’s the song when we perform really unifies and brings everyone together, and we really want you to come see for yourself.
