It's the second expansion of streaming options on Fubo since Hulu + Live TV and Fubo merged in 2025. Fubo's Pro and Elite plans cost $89 and $100 a month, respectively.

This means that within your Fubo app, you'll now have access to original Disney Plus and Hulu programming, including blockbuster Marvel movies and Hulu's burgeoning list of comedy specials. For sports fans you'll still have access to ESPN Unlimited and Fox One, which was already part of the program.

The Fubo and Hulu integration was announced in January of 2025 but didn't go through until October 2025 when it was approved by the Department of Justice. Now, we're finally starting to see the fruits of the merger.

Within the platform, you should see those apps as options now, plus more than 200+ live channels. If you don't, you should reach out to Fubo for more information.
