Airbnb vs. Booking: Quarterly Revenue Scale and Historical Growth

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Booking has out-earned Airbnb in every quarter of the past two years, yet the gap may be narrowing as Airbnb's third-quarter peaks grow.

Airbnb (ABNB -2.82%) primarily generates its revenue by managing a global digital marketplace that connects individual property hosts offering private rooms, primary residences, and vacation homes with travelers seeking unique lodging experiences, custom local activities, and tailored vacation rentals worldwide.

It recently entered an exclusive property booking partnership integrating over one million listings into the American Express platform, and it formally introduced updated operational policies restricting hosts from assigning specific professional checkout chores to visiting guests.

Booking (BKNG -1.95%) functions as a global provider of digital travel and dining reservations, earning revenue through a diverse portfolio of consumer platforms that offer extensive accommodation bookings, airline tickets, rental cars, vacation packages, cruise itineraries, and online restaurant reservations across international markets.

It recently introduced a new artificial intelligence-powered personal travel agent tool to enhance consumer booking experiences, while simultaneously navigating a Federal Trade Commission investigation regarding specific marketing and billing practices at its Priceline brand.

Revenue here refers to the data provider's standardized income-statement revenue line item, which serves as a fundamental financial tracking metric that shows individual investors exactly how much total money a business brings in from its core operations before any administrative, marketing, payroll, or operational expenses are deducted by the company's management.

Normally, the first thing to note when examining a company's revenue trend is seasonality: some times of the year have stronger buying patterns than others. In normal times, seasonality holds true for consumer-driven stocks like these. Airbnb and Booking Holdings get exceptional volume for summer months in the Northern Hemisphere, which translates into third-quarter revenue being booked.

To properly evaluate the revenue trend of companies with strong seasonal trends, compare each quarter to its comparable prior-year quarter. Note that Airbnb's 2026 third quarter rose sharply over Q3 2025, adding $800 million in revenue. Interestingly, Booking's revenue actually dipped year-over-year, suggesting some weakness could be appearing. Despite Booking's large international business, third-quarter results felt no effect, for better or worse, due to foreign exchange considerations.

Revenue is the simplest metric investors use to judge a business. Ideally, on a chart like this, a company would show sequentially rising bars, left ot right. But even when seasonality exists, a trend is visible.
