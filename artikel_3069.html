The best streaming services hit their lowest prices of the year

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

Black Friday is here and that means it's time to save big on bingeing the best shows and movies. Many of the best streaming services have slashed prices to their lowest of the year (and some are the lowest of all time).

Right now you can save 79% on some of the most popular platforms. My favorite Black Friday streaming service deal is the Disney+/Hulu bundle at $4.99/month (was $11.99). Other great deals include HBO Max for $5.49/month for 12 months and Paramount+ Premium for $2.99.

I'm digging through all the best Black Friday streaming deals, so you can enjoy a huge wealth of content without crushing your wallet that's probably already strained around the holidays.

The Disney+ and Hulu bundle is a solid savings all year long, but for Black Friday, it's an even bigger bargain at $4.99. For less than $5 a month, you get two streaming services, offering the best of Disney, Marvel and Star Wars, alongside Hulu's excellent library of more grown-up content. Even better, if you lock in this discounted price today, you'll get Disney+ and Hulu at almost 60% off for a full 12 months.

Paramount+ has discounted every tier to $2.99, so the choice is obvious: get Paramount+ Premium. With this top-tier package, you get full access to the Paramount+ library with ad-free streaming, Showtime originals, and your local CBS station. There's plenty to watch, from the final season of "Yellowjackets" to live sports, like NFL and Champions League soccer. The only downside is that this offer is only valid for 2 months, which feels stingy compared to rivals.

Apple TV is a streaming service you shouldn't overlook. It packs one of the best original libraries of any streamer, with a wealth of must-watch TV shows you can't find anywhere else. Ahead of Black Friday, Apple TV is 50% off, giving you six months of access for $6.99 a month. That's enough time to binge a wide selection of the very best Apple TV Originals from "Severance" to "Slow Horses".

HBO Max is offering 50% off its Basic with Ads plan. Half price on our favorite streaming service is a deal we're eager to shout about. HBO Max offers a near-unbeatable library with top-tier television and plenty of blockbuster movies. Sweetening the deal, the discounted price lasts for 12 months, so you can stream the best of HBO Max for an entire year. Get it now ahead of "Harry Potter" season 1 arriving in December.

Peacock skipped Black Friday last year, but has returned with a respectable discount for 2026. It's not the most headline-grabbing saving at 31%, but plans start from $2.99 per month for the Select tier. However, I recommend the Premium tier for $8.99, which gives you access to the full Peacock library, including shows, movies, live sports, and Peacock Originals.

Horror fans, this one's worth a look. You can currently add MGM+ to your Prime Video subscription for just $1.99 per month for two months, down from $7.99, saving 75%. It's home to brilliant originals like "From" and "The Institute," plus a great selection of movies, including "Project Hail Mary."

Prime Video add-on subscriptions: deals from $0.99Prime Video add-on subscriptions offer some of the cheapest Black Friday streaming deals of the season. Discounts start from $0.99 and include Paramount+, Apple TV, HBO Max, Starz, AMC+, MGM+, BritBox and Hallmark+. Upgrade your Prime Video library with these great deals.

Starz add-on subscription via Prime Video (3 months): was $11.99/month now $2.99/month @ AmazonThe Starz add-on is worth it on Prime Video if you want consistently bingeable shows and great movies without hunting around. You get sweeping dramas like "Outlander," the endlessly addictive "Power" universe, and on the movie side, there are recent favorites including "The Housemaid" and "Shelter." You'll need to clear your summer schedule to watch everything that Starz has to offer.

If you're a fan of British TV and don't normally have access to it, a BritBox subscription might be for you. Right now, you can jump in and try it for $2.99 a month for the first two months. You'll get "Downton Abbey," "Death In Paradise," "Vera," "Father Brown", "Ludwig" and many more.
