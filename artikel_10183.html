Website owners thumb their noses at EU privacy rules, researcher finds

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

From October 1 to 3, 2026, Hanff took a clean Google Chrome browser profile and measured what happens when the browser loads the 100 most visited English-language websites for five minutes, without any consent banner interaction.

All 100 sites produced what the report characterizes as Tier 1 events – interception, exfiltration, or high-entropy fingerprinting (a total of 14,675). Tier 2 consists of storage on the device, capturing device characteristics, and script loads without consent (284,463 events recorded). Tier 3 includes protocol mechanisms and passive reads used for tracking (219,632 events).

The median website triggered 2,695 events, for a total of 28,695 events deemed high or critical. And 92.4 percent of the servers contacted during this process were on networks outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Most of these servers – 1,247 out of 1,400 – were in the US, and 1,034 belonged to third-parties.

The report, released in conjunction with a presentation at the Field Talk conference, also calculates the estimated environmental impact to load all this third-party content on websites without consent: 1,271,444 kWh per year, or enough to power 353 homes in Europe at 3.6 MWh per home annually.
