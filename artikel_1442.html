The top goalscorers for club & country in 2026 so far: Kane, Yamal, Mbappe, Raphinha…

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Big names from Real Madrid, Barcelona, Arsenal and Bayern Munich feature alongside some surprise names in the most prolific goalscorers of the calendar year so far.

Some big names are surprisingly absent from the most prolific goalscorers of 2026 to date.

Drawn from Europe’s five major leagues, here are the top 10 goalscorers in 2026 across club and international competitions.

Schick has had reason to celebrate in 2026. Earlier this year, he reached the milestone of 100 goals for Bayer Leverkusen.

He’s been on fire this season, with 11 goals from 13 games for the German side.

There won’t be any more international goals from Schick, though. He retired from Czech Republic duty after the World Cup.

The jury is still out on Gyokeres, who remains a perplexing forward.

This calendar year has seen him look borderline anonymous and increasingly sidelined at Arsenal.

And yet Gyokeres also fired Sweden to the World Cup with a hat-trick against Ukraine and a late winner against Poland. He’s basically the Ikea Gerd Muller at this point.

Dembele has scored 20 goals in 36 PSG appearances this year, including several crucial strikes in Europe – none bigger than the equalising spot kick he coolly dispatched against Arsenal in the Champions League final.

His goal in France’s World Cup win over Iraq was, incredibly, his first in major tournament football. That was swiftly followed up with a rapid hat-trick against Norway, before another in the quarter-final against Morocco.

He is largely taking it easy right now, once again frequently rested during PSG’s domestic campaign. But he popped up with a goal against Manchester City.

What a year it’s been for Malen. Started it as a benchwarmer at Aston Villa. Set to finish it as one of Europe’s most lethal strikers at Roma.

Malen made an immediate impact after his January move to the Italian capital and his scoring rate has barely slowed down since.

For some reason, his form hasn’t quite translated into international football; he hasn’t scored for the Netherlands at all this year.

But high-flying Roma are laughing. Malen almost won the Serie A golden boot last season despite arriving midway through and he’s a leading contender for the gong this season.

A fairly recent entrant into the top 10 after heading into the international break on the back of a hat trick against Union Berlin.

Olise was the Bundesliga’s best assister last season but has been more than capable of finding the net himself a fair few times too.

He didn’t score any goals at the World Cup but netted the winner in Zinedine Zidane’s second game in charge of France. The best Fantasy Footballer around right now.

Yamal has long been talked up as a Ballon d’Or contender, and slowly but surely he’s getting the numbers to back it up.

The teenager’s goalscoring output has grown season on season, and 2025-26 was no exception. February’s hat-trick against Villarreal was evidence of a player about to go truly stratospheric.

Unfortunately, the final furlong of the campaign was hit by a hamstring injury, or else we suspect he’d be a few places higher up in this list.

He needed just 10 minutes in his first World Cup start to find the back of the net, although that would be his only goal in Spain’s triumph out in North America.

Now he’s looking fit and hungry – at least based on the evidence of his exceptional form of late.

The Brazilian has an exceptional tally of 33 goals for Barcelona so far this calendar year (plus one for Brazil), which is especially impressive when you consider he’s missed quite a lot of action due to injury.

He averages a goal every 49 minutes for Barca this season – to put that in context, Kylian Mbappe averages a goal every 84 minutes for Real Madrid.

Raphinha has scored in all but three of the Barca games he’s played in in 2026-27, scoring hat tricks in two, and currently leads the Europe-wide goalscoring charts with 15 in the league.

The top three are the last three winners of the European Golden Shoe. This list has taken a while to take shape, but the frontrunners are the usual suspects.

Haaland’s four goals against Iraq and Senegal in the group stages finally saw him climb up into the top 10, from which he was surprisingly absent in the latter half of the 2025-26 club season, before he added to his World Cup tally against the Ivory Coast. Then there were two goals against Brazil.

He finally drew a blank as Norway exited the World Cup at the hands of England at the quarter-final stage. He earned a well-deserved rest before spearheading Man City’s new era under Enzo Maresca.

Same as it ever was with nine goals in nine Premier League appearances for City this season, plus braces against Porto and PSG and another Champions League goal against his favourite opponents, RB Leipzig.

Like his rival Haaland, it somehow took Mbappe until mid-June to enter the top 10.

Mbappe kicked off the year in superb style, scoring in six successive starts for Real Madrid (nine goals in total) before dipping considerably with just one goal in his next seven appearances.

By the nature of his 10 goals at the World Cup, we don’t expect to see the Real Madrid superstar dropping back out. His Champions League record is pretty sensational.

Arguably the finest player in world football right now. How often have you been able to say that about an Englishman?

The only player in Europe’s five major leagues to average a better-than-goal-a-game output so far this calendar year, the England captain has 41 goals in 40 appearances for Bayern Munich in all competitions in 2026 – including home and away against Real Madrid and PSG.

He capped off his superb season with a hat-trick in the DFB Pokal final. Two goals against Croatia showed Kane carried his form into the World Cup, where he eventually scored six times.

Unbelievably, the Norway game was Kane’s first knockout appearance for club and country in 2025-26 in which he didn’t score. Which, of course, he followed with a blank against Argentina.

Surprisingly enough, Kane failed to add to his tally by drawing a blank in his first three Bayern appearances of the 2026-27 campaign.

But it’s back to normal service with his Bayern tally standing at 10 goals in 14 games. The Czechs and Croatia have been on the receiving end in the Nations League too.

Still some way to go before catching Lionel Messi’s calendar year record, mind.
