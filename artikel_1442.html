Hermoso: “We played without fear, that’s what matters.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

At the end of PSG-Roma, Mario Hermoso spoke to Sky Sport.

“Everyone knows what Gasperini brings to the team. This is his identity, this is his way of playing. We defenders also have the opportunity to be protagonists.”

“I’m very happy to arrive in a stadium like this and see a Roma team that plays in a special way, even without fear. We’re on the right path and we continue to make great strides, which are important for us.”

“We’re all big time players. This is us, this is the team, and we’re all in a position to be key players. We have a lot of games to play, so we’re all important. Donyell, Paulo, and even Bryan, all those who perhaps played more minutes at the start of the season, came in with the right mentality, also to help their teammates and bring home an excellent result.”

“The coach is the one who gives you this confidence, this belief, this mentality of never giving up, of reacting to those difficult moments we may have experienced or felt in past years. This is the mentality the coach has brought, he has brought this identity here to Rome, and we are happy to continue doing this, which is a great thing, an important thing even for a club like Roma.”

“I’m especially happy to help with these goals and also to see a solid Roma, a Roma that also supports the guys who are starting to be important within this team. This speaks very well of the idea, the way of playing, and also of all the teammates. So I’m happy to be able to help like this.”
