Harry Kane: “I have always been a big admirer of Karim Benzema”

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

French Football Weekly: entering the very closed circle of Ballon d’Or winners allowed you to feel fully established among the greatest strikers of all time? Do we need to win this trophy to have the recognition we deserve in the world of football?

Harry Kane: I am extremely proud to have won the Ballon d’Or, and receiving this award in my hometown made the moment even more special. Seeing my name alongside players like R9, who actually gave it to me that evening, Cristiano Ronaldo, Messi and Gerd Müller is an immense source of pride, especially given my journey with loans at the start of my career which forced me to show self-confidence and resilience. Since arriving at Bayern, I have clearly taken a new step, and I feel like I am at the top of my game. I think winning the Ballon d’Or has changed the way people perceive me around the world. I probably underestimated the enthusiasm and the reaction of the fans. When I brandished the trophy in front of the supporters who were waiting for me in the street as I left the ceremony, I understood that I was experiencing a magical moment that I will never forget. This victory is the reflection of an exceptional season that I experienced between the goals, the titles and the great moments. Winning the Ballon d’Or at 33 just shows that if you continue to work hard and believe in it, you can reach the top.

FM: Karim Benzema was Ballon d’Or in 2022, Harry Kane was Ballon d’Or in 2026. The full number 9s who participate in the game are highlighted. Do you find any similarities between you and Karim Benzema in your football?

HK: Karim is an incredible player. I have always been a big admirer of the player. What he achieved at Real Madrid in 2022 was truly impressive. He has this ability to drop into midfield and play almost like a number 10, dictating the play, while arriving in the box at the ideal time. We have slightly different physical characteristics, but in terms of playing intelligence, ability to find spaces and desire to help the players around us, I clearly see a lot of similarities in our football. Both of us showed that it is not always necessary to be in the box to score goals. For me, I have more opportunities when I go lower, because I’m not marked up close. The goals come from me going down and the game developing. I find myself in a number 10 role and, when the opposing block moves back, I shoot from the edge of the area, which is one of my great assets. Last season was the best example: I scored 73 goals, which is almost double the number of goals I scored in any other season.

FM: after winning a Ballon d’Or, what is the priority objective? Go get a second one to make even more history?

HK: of course, I would love to win another Ballon d’Or, but I know there are many other great players who have their chances. But there are still many trophies I would like to win, like the Champions League with Bayern and, obviously, a title with England.

FM: Last year, your journey in the Champions League ended in the semi-final against PSG after a very high-level double confrontation. Do you think that, this season, Bayern Munich is capable of going for the big-eared cup?

HK: the Champions League is a major goal for me and for the team. We have a strong squad, made up of incredible players. Last season, the matches against PSG were crazy, it was decided by a hair, and being eliminated by the team which won the title twice in a row was nothing to be ashamed of. Spending another year with the coach and the team helps us become even stronger. We approached this season telling ourselves that this had to be our year. We have to find a way to achieve this, find that little extra that will make the difference to get through the rounds and do everything we can to win the Champions League. This is our main objective.

FM: You are the first Bayern player to win the Ballon d’Or in over 45 years. Can a player like Michael Olise be your successor, the next Munich player to win this Grail?

HK: Michael is a fantastic player. We see, every day in training, his qualities and what he is capable of doing on the pitch. He undoubtedly has the means to bring a new Ballon d’Or to Munich, that’s for sure.
