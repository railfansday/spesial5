A Complete Timeline of Justin and Hailey Bieber’s Relationship

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The singer and supermodel-turned-skin care mogul are one of Hollywood’s leading power couples.

Justin Bieber and Hailey Baldwin dated on and off long before they became husband and wife. Hailey first met Justin in the late aughts, and they eventually struck up a casual romance.

Though they decided to keep it out of the public eye, this didn’t stop fans from obsessing over their relationship. After a breakup and surprise reconciliation-turned-whirlwind engagement two years later, Justin officially made Hailey his Mrs. during a September 2018 New York City courthouse ceremony. They got married in front of friends and family in a more formal wedding a year later. In May 2024, they announced they were expecting their first child. They welcomed their son, Jack, in August 2024.

From their highly publicized South Carolina nuptials to their adjustment to parenthood, here’s the couple’s full history and how their relationship has evolved.

Hailey has her father, Stephen, to thank for introducing her to her future husband. There’s even footage of their first meeting, which took place backstage at The Today Show. Hailey and Justin—then in their teens—shared a brief handshake and the rest, as they say, is history.

Chaperoned by her father yet again, Hailey attended the New York City premiere of Justin’s documentary Never Say Never. Later that month, Justin confirmed his relationship with Selena Gomez when the couple made their red carpet debut at Vanity Fair’s Oscar After Party.

Hailey officially declared herself a member of the Jelena fan club. The model took to Twitter, sharing her support for the couple. “I don’t care what anyone says but Justin Bieber and Selena Gomez together is the definition of a teenage dream! #word,” Hailey wrote.

After calling it quits with Gomez, Justin was photographed attending church services at Hillsong alongside Hailey and fellow supermodel Kendall Jenner. It wasn’t long before fans began speculating that Hailey and Justin were an item.

Hailey denied any romantic connection between her and Justin. She set the record straight during an interview with E!. “I’ve known [Justin] since I was like 13—and we’ve just been good friends over the years,” Hailey said. “We have just stayed close, and there’s nothing more to it than that.”

Justin backed up Hailey’s statement in an Instagram post. The singer shared a snap of himself and Hailey, writing, “I’m super single and this is my good friend u would know otherwise.”

Hailey joined Justin and his family on vacation in Anguilla, fueling rumors that their friendship was actually something more.

Justin made his romance with Hailey Instagram official. Shortly after New Year’s Day, he shared a photo of them kissing.

Hailey and Justin revealed that their relationship was casual. “We are not an exclusive couple,” Hailey told E!. Meanwhile, in an interview with GQ, Justin called Hailey “someone I really love,” but he added that he didn’t “want to be held down by anything.”

Hailey and Justin ended their brief fling. According to People, Justin also unfollowed Hailey on Instagram amid his new relationship with Sofia Richie.

Following their split, Hailey and Justin completely lost touch for almost two years. In 2020, Hailey shared her thoughts on their time apart: “Our lives seemed to be going in very different directions,” she told ELLE. “I actually think—now that I look back at it being married—that it was a good thing for the two of us, very healthy.”

During Justin and Hailey’s rift, the singer rekindled his romance with Gomez in November 2017. However, by March 2018, Jelena was over for good.

Hailey revealed that she was speaking to Justin again. “We went through a long period of time when we weren’t friends,” she told The Times U.K.. “We’ve moved past that.”

Justin and Hailey appeared as if they were back together. After sharing some PDA at a religious conference, they were spotted together at LIV nightclub in Miami. Just days later, they were photographed holding hands and kissing in New York City.

After rumors of the couple’s engagement began to swirl, Justin confirmed the news on Instagram. He posted two portraits with his new fiancée and penned a lengthy caption devoted to her. “Hailey I am soooo in love with everything about you,” he wrote. “So committed to spending my life getting to know every single part of you.”

Hailey joined the love fest, tweeting, “Not sure what I did in life to deserve such happiness but I am so utterly grateful to God for giving me such an incredible person to share my life with!”

Just two months after their engagement, TMZ ran photographs of the couple walking into New York City’s Marriage Bureau. The next day, People claimed that Hailey and Justin obtained their marriage license, officially making them husband and wife.

Meanwhile, Hailey dismissed the gossip on Twitter: “I understand where the speculation is coming from, but I’m not married yet!”

The couple finally confirmed their marriage status. Justin shared an Instagram photo of him and Hailey with the caption “My wife is awesome.” The following day, Hailey changed her last name to Bieber on all of her social media accounts.

TMZ broke the news that a second wedding was imminent after the date was moved several times. The outlet acquired a photo of a save the date card, which revealed that the ceremony would take place on September 30 in South Carolina.

Mr. and Mrs. Bieber officially tied the knot…again! People disclosed the details of their ceremony, which took place at Somerset Chapel in Bluffton, South Carolina. Vows (and Tiffany & Co. wedding bands) were exchanged “as the sun set.” The star-studded guest list included Kendall Jenner, Camila Morrone, and Jaden Smith, among others.

A day later, Justin basked in wedded bliss as he shared photos from the couple’s reception.

The Biebers continued to share photos from their big day. Hailey put her Off-White by Virgil Abloh wedding dress on full display, including her elaborate train, which read “’till death do us part.” Justin wore a Celine tuxedo.

Commenting beneath JustJared’s Instagram post, she wrote, “please stop with this nonsense. There is no ‘response.’ This is complete BS.”
