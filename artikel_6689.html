All About Bonnie-Chance Roberts, Beanie Feldstein’s Wife

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Beanie Feldstein just shared a major life update: She and her wife, Bonnie-Chance Roberts, quietly welcomed their first child. “She is genuinely beyond my wildest dreams. I really did dream her,” Feldstein told People of their baby girl in November 2026.

In May, the actress announced her pregnancy on Instagram, writing, “Limited Edition Scouse Beanie Baby coming soon!! 🎀🍼.”

The announcement comes nearly two years after the couple married in a camp-themed celebration in upstate New York.

Feldstein often keeps the spotlight on her work, but when it comes to Roberts, she doesn’t hold back. Over the years, she’s shared glimpses into their relationship, from how they met on set to building a life together.

So who is Bonnie-Chance Roberts? Here’s everything to know about Feldstein’s wife and their life as parents.

Originally from Liverpool, Roberts works behind the scenes in the entertainment industry as a producer.

Her credits include projects like Mary Queen of Scots, Bridget Jones’s Baby, and How to Build a Girl—which she worked on as a producer while Feldstein starred in it.

Feldstein and Roberts first connected while working on How to Build a Girl. Feldstein told Vogue in June 2023 that they initially met over Skype during the casting process. “Bon had been developing Caitlin Moran’s book How to Build a Girl into a movie for over five years when they finally started the casting process for Caitlin’s fictional proxy, Johanna, a 16-year-old girl from Wolverhampton, England,” Feldstein said. “With a dash of luck and a push from the universe, they ended up being open to meeting a Jewish girl from Los Angeles.”

Their working relationship quickly shifted. As Feldstein recalled to Vogue, “From the moment we first kissed, we both knew we would get married and months after that, Bon had announced that when the time came, she wanted to be the one to propose to me.”

The relationship grew steadily from there. By 2020, Feldstein publicly celebrated Roberts on Instagram, writing in a birthday tribute, “I’m pretty convinced you’re actually made of magic. The girl with the warmest heart, the most expansive imagination, and the most exceptional brain. I love you so much. Happy birthday, bubba!!!”

The couple didn’t keep things private for long. In September 2019, they stepped out together at the Toronto International Film Festival for the premiere of How to Build a Girl, marking their first red carpet appearance as a couple.

Roberts later posted about the experience on Instagram, writing, “They say it takes a village to build a girl, and they are right!”

Roberts took the lead when it came time to propose. Feldstein announced their engagement in June 2022, sharing photos from the moment on Instagram and captioning them, “I do, Bon.”

Roberts later described designing the ring during a long period when the couple lived apart due to the pandemic. “The dream was for the ring to look as if a shard of magic was frozen in time,” she told Vogue in 2023.

The couple tied the knot on May 20, 2023, at Cedar Lakes Estate in New York’s Hudson Valley.

The theme felt personal. Feldstein told Vogue, “I grew up going to summer camp for 10 years, and my parents and both sets of my grandparents met at summer camp, so camp is a lineage of love through the generations of my family. Even though we met in London and fell in love on a film set, to get married at a camp was a truly beautiful emotional homecoming.”

Roberts wore a tailored bridal suit for the ceremony, while Feldstein opted for a vintage-inspired Gucci gown.

Looking back on the day, Feldstein shared on Late Night with Seth Meyers in February 2024, “[My wedding] went so swimmingly,” she said. “Like, I’m such an anxious girly. And I was more calm at the wedding than I am, like, brushing my teeth, at the supermarket. I was so calm. I just got to look at her and hear her vows, and it was perfect.”

“We wrote our own vows, and it actually rained a little the day of our wedding, and we handwrote the vows and the trees sort of shook and dripped on my vows,” she continued. “And I was like, ‘Oh, I hope I know them by heart.’ But it was fine. It was actually all going perfectly.”

In May 2026, Feldstein and Roberts announced they were expecting their first child.

The couple shared the news with a joint Instagram post featuring a series of photos, including one of a cake decorated with pink bows and the message “B+B are having a baby!”

The announcement marked a new chapter for the couple—and a years-long dream realized for Feldstein. In 2021, the actress told Bustle, “Sixteen years from now I hope to be living in New York City or London, married to my girlfriend, and have babies and puppies running around while we both make movies, shows, and plays that feel meaningful and exciting and profound.”

Feldstein quietly welcomed her and Roberts’s baby girl. In a November 4 interview with People, she opened up about parenthood for the first time. She did not share her child’s name or birth date but marveled about life with her.

“She is genuinely beyond my wildest dreams. I really did dream her,” Feldstein said. “I always wanted to be a mom, and I always wanted a daughter. I look at her every day, and I’m like, ‘You’re just beyond anything I could have even tried to imagine.’”

“I love getting to watch her take in the world; it’s just the greatest gift. And getting to be a mom alongside my wife is the greatest gift,” she added. “She’s such a good mom, and it’s so special to watch. Motherhood is unbelievably beautiful.”

She reflected on one milestone she saw a few weeks before her interview. “There’s nothing like the first smile,” she said. “I could feel her soul from the moment she was born. I remember people told me newborns are little blobs and they don’t really come alive until 6 weeks. I disagree with that completely. I could feel her soul from the moment I met her, but when you get to see that externalized in the smile, it’s so magic.”

Feldstein doesn’t shy away from expressing how much Roberts means to her.

Speaking to People in February 2024, she said of married life, “My favorite part of married life is everything, but I think the big thing that felt different was saying ‘my wife.’ [It] has more power than like ‘my girlfriend’…you know, it’s like ‘my wife’ is like ‘oh, this is legit.’”

She added, “Oh my god, I just want her with me every second of every day.”

In her November 2026 interview with People, she touched on her gratitude raising a child with Roberts. “I don’t think anyone that has had a baby can look at their child and not just feel the most gratitude to the universe,” Feldstein said. “I feel very grateful every day that I get to be her mom and mother alongside my wife and watch her with my whole family.”
