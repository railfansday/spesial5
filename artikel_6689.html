The Vogue Business Funding Tracker

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Introducing the Vogue Business Funding Tracker, a running list highlighting the most notable and intriguing investment and M&A activity in fashion and beauty. From emerging disruptors to legacy giants undergoing major changes, we spotlight the deals that are shifting the dynamics of the sectors we cover, including fashion, beauty, tech and sustainability.

Company description: Founded in 2023, Counsel Health is an AI-powered primary care platform that combines medical AI with a network of physicians to deliver personalized medical guidance and treatment. The company has agreed to be acquired by Finnish smart ring maker Oura for $625 million, as Oura looks to move beyond biometric tracking toward more holistic, tailored health insights and actionable medical care. The acquisition will integrate Counsel’s AI and on-demand physicians with Oura’s continuous health data collection, enabling users to access medical advice, prescriptions, lab result interpretations, and chronic care support through the Oura app.

Funding and round: Acquisition, $625 million.

Investor comments: “Oura helps members understand their physiology and, with Counsel, they can take action on their health across a range of needs, from quick questions and prescriptions to lab results, chronic care and referrals,” said Oura CEO Tom Hale. “Together, we can create a new model of care that extends the current healthcare paradigm by delivering an experience that’s more immediate, personalized, and proactive.”

Company description: Founded in 1889, Lee has made its marks as one of the biggest heritage names in denim. The American brand generates an average of $1.5 billion in annual sales across 73 countries, of which almost 40% comes from outside the US and Canada. New York-based Authentic Brands Group has successfully acquired Lee from Kontoor Brands in an effort to expand Lee into adjacent categories, including activewear and workwear, as well as lifestyle offerings.

Investor(s): Authentic Brands Group (portfolio includes: Guess, Juicy Couture, Reebok).

Investor comments: “We are thrilled to welcome Lee to the Authentic family,” said Jamie Salter, founder and executive chair of Authentic Brands Group. “Lee has the heritage, recognition and consumer loyalty we look for in a brand. By connecting Lee to Authentic’s global platform and the right partners in key markets, we see a significant opportunity to expand its reach through content, experiences and new product categories.”

Company description: Touted as London’s first functional smoothie bar, Elevate launched at the Royal Exchange in June 2025, before opening in Selfridges in April 2026. Founded by Julia Baldet, its bestselling smoothie, The Glow, made with collagen and hyaluronic acid, has grossed close to half a million pounds since rollout. Elevate has collaborated with brands such as Nike x Skims, Adanola, Ouai and Medicube, as wellness continues to crossover into beauty and fashion. The new funding will be used to open additional London sites next year, after finishing its first full year with over £1 million in sales.

Investor(s): JamJar (portfolio includes: Innocent Drinks, Deliveroo, Humantra), Active Partners (portfolio includes: Tala, Healf, Salad Project) Cassius, Soho House founder Nick Jones.

Investor comments: “Elevate’s proposition is perfect: delicious smoothies that look great while meeting real nutritional and wellness needs. It’s clearly working; loyal customers love the brand. But most of all, we’re backing Julia. She’s focused, ambitious, creative and commercial, and we’re thrilled to support her as she builds Elevate into a category-defining brand,” JamJar partner Adam Balon said in a statement.

Company description: Frasers Group has successfully taken control of Hugo Boss. Following a rejected takeover bid of €2 billion (€38 per share) in June, the British retail conglomerate has raised its stake to approximately 50.08% — an additional purchase of 198,666 shares, equating to €7.63 million, according to the stock market filing. The German fashion company will now become a subsidiary of the Mike Ashley-owned business.

Hugo Boss believed the June takeover proposal to be “financially inadequate”, urging its shareholders to reject. Though Frasers continued its efforts, gradually raising its holding once again to 48% in mid-August (up from 30.3% in June, alongside the initial takeover bid). Frasers Group CEO Michael Murray assumed the Hugo Boss chair position last month, after Stephan Sturm stepped down.

Investor(s): Frasers Group Luxury (portfolio includes: Flannels, The Webster, Harvey Nichols, Matches).

Company description: Frasers Group has adjusted its stake in Burberry from 7.4% to 6%. This comes after the growing British retail group raised its position in the company from 6.3% last week, after upping it from 4.2% at the end of September. This marks a potential pullback from its current luxury brand spending spree.

Investor(s): Frasers Group Luxury (portfolio includes: Flannels, The Webster, Harvey Nichols, Matches).

Company description: Frasers Group has acquired a 8.78% stake in US-based sports brand Under Armour, continuing a string of cross-category investments in recent months. Earlier this week, Frasers upped its investment in Burberry to 6.3%, having also taken on ownership of esteemed department store Harvey Nichols in August. The Frasers portfolio spans discount retailer Sports Direct through to The Webster, House of Fraser, and Flannels, as well as a strong stake in Hugo Boss — upped to 47.89% in August. Under Armour’s share price has risen since the SEC filing was made on October 1.

Investor(s): Frasers Group (portfolio includes: Harvey Nichols, Sports Direct, The Webster)

Company description: Frasers Group has upped its economic interest in Burberry from 4.2% to 6.3%, with the British brand now part of a new luxury division launched in early October.
