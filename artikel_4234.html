Grand Theft Auto VI: New Original Song 'Pusher' Out Now, Full Score Album Arrives This December

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Rockstar Games has released a new original song in Grand Theft Auto VI called "Pusher" by the game's composer duo, Justice. You can listen to it now on all major streaming services, including Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube. Alongside this sneak peek at the game's original score, Rockstar says the GTA VI original score album, aptly titled Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score, will be released on all major streaming services, as well as on double CD and triple vinyl, on December 11.

The original score will feature 30 tracks from the band's full suite of songs composed for GTA VI, including the previously released "Flower in a Hurricane" by Justice, featuring Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard, plus a number of digital bonus tracks included across all formats.
