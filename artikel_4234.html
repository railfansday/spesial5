GTA 6 has a ton of secrets to find throughout the large map of Leonida. Some examples include the Kraken deep underwater, UFOs, and even a Mario and Luigi reference.

One of the coolest easter eggs in the game is the secret Satan boss fight. Not only is it a really trippy mission, but it also rewards the player with one of the best weapons in the game.

If you are looking to take down Satan himself, this guide explains everything you need to know. Here’s how to find the secret Satan boss fight in GTA 6.

To find the secret Satan boss in GTA 6, you must travel to the northern side of Port Gellhorn and visit the house with “Welcome to the Hellhole!” written on it. You can find the exact location of this house in the image of Port Gellhorn below.

When you arrive at this house, the garage will either be open or closed. The Satan boss fight event can only be triggered if the garage is open, so if it happens to be shut when you arrive, then you’ll have to come back another time. This event all comes down to RNG, but there are ways to increase your chances.

First, the garage has a higher chance of being open late at night. For example, it was open for me around 10:00 PM, so I recommend only visiting the Hellhole house around this time.

Keep in mind that the event won’t trigger if you just wait outside the house. Instead, I recommend using the RydeMe app to fast travel to another location and back to the house multiple times until the garage opens. This worked for me after five attempts.

Once the garage finally opens, walk inside to find two men having some drinks. They will ask you if you want a beer, which you should grab from the cooler next to the couch.

For the next couple of minutes, make sure you don’t do anything to piss these guys off. You can talk to them, but don’t choose the “provoke” dialogue option or throw a bottle to mess up the event. Instead, wait patiently until their van arrives.

When the van arrives, the men will ask you to join them for their upcoming ritual. You should agree here and climb into the back of the van, which will then transport you to another location with a bonfire.

Next, get out of the van and make your way to the bonfire to celebrate with the two Satanists, and wait until they tell you to look into the fire. Once the prompt “look into the fire” pops up, hold down the button to do just that.

Looking into the fire will teleport you to hell, where you’ll have to fight a wave of Satan’s demons and Satan himself.

Satan is not very difficult to defeat, but the fight can be a bit confusing. What you want to do here is shoot the exploding enemies that are running toward you, which will turn Satan’s health bar red.

Once the health bar is red, you want to shoot at Satan to drain his health. When the health bar turns back to black, you want to repeat the process and kill the surrounding enemies to turn the bar red again.

Doing this a few times will have Satan going down fairly quickly. Once you kill Satan, you will teleport back to the real world.

When players respawn back to the real world after fighting Satan in GTA 6, they will be granted a permanent health increase. Additionally, they’ll be able to claim the Hellspawn Cumshot 12 Shotgun next to the large throne.

This shotgun is extremely powerful and is one of the best guns I have found in the game so far, so I recommend equipping it for any upcoming missions you have.
