Alphonso Davies sustains injury in action for Canada

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Injury-plagued Bayern Munich star Alphonso Davies suffered another setback while on international duty with Canada on Saturday.

The left-back was forced off just six minutes into Canada's 3-2 victory over Guatemala after picking up an injury.

“It's a lower body injury.. we'll get the full extent of it once the doctor takes a look at it and then make a decision on what the next steps are," Canada assistant coach Mauro Biello said, via iMiaSanMia.

The full extent of Davies' injury remains unclear at this stage, with medical examinations set to determine whether the 26-year-old will return to Munich early.
