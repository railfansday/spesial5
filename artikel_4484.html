The plug-in hybrid 2028 Audi Nuvolari is the brand’s return to the supercar world, and there’s plenty to love about it.

The Nuvolari’s body panels are constructed of carbon-fiber-reinforced plastic.

A twin-turbo 4.0-liter V-8 lives in the rear and can spin up to 10,000 rpm. An electric motor is connected to its crankshaft.

Two additional electric motors drive the front axle and provide torque vectoring.

The engine and motors combine for 987 horsepower.

The Nuvolari uses Brembo’s CCM-R Plus carbon-ceramic brake system.
