In neuroscience, there is no definition for human intelligence in brain, no mechanistic theory or even postulates on improvement.

But it is known that intelligence uses memory, and memory is a part of the mind.

The mind is conceptualized to be electrical and chemical signals.

So it is possible to explore a digital health display to explain labels of intelligence to know how it works, conceptually and shape how to think about how it is improved.

There are several labels associated with human intelligence. They include creativity, innovation, reasoning, analysis, curiosity, observation, logic, imagination, decision-making and so forth.What does it mean that the brain is having an imaginative process to result in an intelligence outcome? The same question applies to other labels.This question not mean that what circuits are active or what lobe is likely, but that what are the components of mind that are operating intelligence and what is their specific mechanism, in contrast to others?Human intelligence can be assumed to be a factor of the human mind, because it is tied to human memory. Memory is a subset of the mind.So, to establish what human intelligence is, it is important to establish what the mind is, and the components to be seek out mechanisms that result in outcomes.In neuroscience, it is established that neurons are dominant across functions of the brain for human life and experiences. But neurons do so with their electrical and chemical signals.In psychiatry, antidepressants, antipsychotics, and anxiolytics target chemical signals. Electrical, magnetic, and ultrasound stimulations target electrical signals.Even when psychiatric medications target enzymes or intracellular signaling molecules, or have metabolic, hormonal, neuroinflammatory or receptor-trafficking as targets, they all influence the environment for which neurotransmitters are made, released or are broken down.So, it is possible to assume that because what must be influenced for a good mental health are mostly electrical and chemical signals, they are the candidates of the mind, since the mind and mental are synonymous.Conceptually, electrical and chemical signals interact, in sets. Those sets are found in clusters of neurons. The extents of their interactions are determined by attributes. Attributes include sequences, splits, thick sets, overlap and so forth.Human intelligence in the brain can be definedas the relays of electrical signals, with attributes like sequences, splits and intensity, to sets [of interactions] where memories are obtained, for advantageous experiential outcomes.In general, human intelligence is defined as the use of memory for desired, expected or advantageous outcomes. So, the use of memory for specific outcomes is intelligence.Intelligence is mostly about relays for memory, which is at destinations.There are two major types of intelligence, operational intelligence and improvement intelligence. Both of them have mild, mid and complex ranges.Operational is for routine, it includes most aspects of nurture, like passing exams, using tools, cooking, driving, participating in activities and so forth, while improvement intelligence ranges across problem-solving, creativity, innovation, advancement and so forth.It is possible to use electrical and chemical signals to explain labels of intelligence, presenting a key nosology that is based on conceptual brain science.
