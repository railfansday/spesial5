Gallery: Hollow Knight & Silksong's Switch 2 Physicals Are A Delight For Map Fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Fangamer announced earlier this year that it had two swanky new Switch 2 physical releases in the works for both Hollow Knight and Silksong, each complete with a couple of bonus goodies inside the box.

With the release date finally upon us, the good folks over at Fangamer have sent us a copy of each, and let it be known, they are both really rather sexy.

Both boxes boast wrap-around cover art (for some particularly detailed spines) and reversible sleeves so you can opt for a design without all of the Switch 2 T&C text down at the bottom.

All of the DLC for the first game is included on the cart, too, and while Silksong's Sea of Sorrow expansion obviously isn't on there at the moment (we're still waiting for a firm release date on that one), it will come as a free download to all those with a copy when it eventually drops.

Inside, both copies contain some bonus booklet goodies, too. Each has its own instruction booklet, containing introductions for everything from both the Knight and Hornet's movesets and upgrade abilities, to points of interest in the world. Gosh, we didn't realise how much we had missed physical manuals.

A physical copy of each game's map is included in the box as well, which folds up into a little wax-stamped envelope — a very neat touch, if we've ever seen one. This gives you a detailed rundown of each location for both games, and could prove as a valuable route tracker for those still trying to uncover Hallownest/Pharloom's secrets.

Both the Hollow Knight and Silksong Switch 2 physicals are now available on the Fangamer website.
