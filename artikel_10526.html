Influencer uses his 1.1M followers to track down stolen $500K McLaren

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

A Detroit influencer managed to recover his stolen $500,000 McLaren after using his 1.1 million social media followers to track it down, but not before thieves were caught doing donuts in the luxury supercar.

Businessman and content creator Ali Sobh was left stunned when his rare McLaren disappeared from the driveway behind his home in Detroit’s Boston-Edison neighborhood.

However, things took an even stranger turn when footage surfaced online showing someone driving his stolen vehicle at a street takeover, performing donuts in front of a crowd of spectators.

Rather than simply waiting for police to locate the car, Sobh decided to put his massive social media following to work.

According to ClickOnDetroit, Sobh shared footage of the stolen vehicle with his 1.1 million followers and asked for help identifying its whereabouts.

The footage, also highlighted by FOX 2 Detroit, showed the McLaren being driven recklessly at a street takeover, with the driver spinning the expensive vehicle around in front of onlookers.

A post shared by Ali Sobh (@alisobh_official)

Fortunately, Sobh’s followers came through with tips that helped narrow down the car’s location.

The influencer passed a potential location near Six Mile and Telegraph to Detroit police, who subsequently recovered the $500,000 supercar.

Sobh explained that the vehicle was particularly special because of its rare paint job.

“I’m the only one in the country that has this color as well,” he told ClickOnDetroit.

Although he managed to recover the vehicle, Sobh was understandably frustrated that the thieves had been brazen enough to show it off at a public gathering.

The incident is another bizarre addition to the growing list of social media stars having problems with expensive vehicles.

Previously, YouTuber WhistlinDiesel helped recover a fan’s stolen truck, while influencer Duke Dennis had his Lamborghini broken into and personal belongings stolen.

Detroit police are continuing to investigate the theft and review footage from the street takeover to identify those responsible.

No arrests have been announced, and Sobh says he plans to increase security around his home to prevent another incident.
