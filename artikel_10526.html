GTA 6 made real gyms empty out as players chose in-game workouts instead

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Grand Theft Auto 6 caused a pretty big drop in real-life gym workouts, with new data revealing just how much people focused on working out in-game instead.

On November 19, Grand Theft Auto fans finally got to get their hands on GTA 6, quickly diving into Jason and Lucia’s story, as well as exploring the wider state of Leonida.

Even though the game has been out for a week, plenty of players have not progressed past Chapter 1. Many have lapped up the enjoyable vibes of Key Lento, playing pool at the Rusty Anchor, focusing on fishing off the coast, and working early missions for Brian Heder.

One key part of the first chapter, and those there after, are workouts. Jason’s deck has a partial gym setup, and there are fully fledged gyms elsewhere in Vice City.

Well, as it turns out, plenty of people decided to focus on Jason’s workouts rather than their own following the game’s release.

As per stats from Gymverse and Fitness22, workouts logged by Gymverse users dropped in big ways. The biggest drop came in Saudi Arabia, as numbers dropped by 17%. In the UK and United States, the drop in logged workouts came in at 12% in the first 24 hours of the GTA 6 launch.

Over the first three days of the launch week, though, worldwide workouts dropped by 10% compared to the weeks before.

“Our data suggests that for three days, many people chose to work out in Vice City rather than in the gym,” said Yossi Tarablus, VP of Marketing, Fitness22.

It wasn’t just workouts and gym numbers that were impacted by the launch. An estimated 1.5 million people in the United States took at least one day off to play GTA 6. That was said to cost the economy around $1 billion.

Unlike in GTA, there is no real cheat code to bulk up in real life other than putting in the hard work.
