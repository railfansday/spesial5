Trump floats an unexpected name to be his successor as powerhouses Rubio and Vance stand by

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Chief of staff Stephen Miller praises President Trump's record on deregulation and the economy, and Trump jokingly asks if he wants to run for president.

During a lighthearted Friday afternoon moment with the media inside the White House press briefing room, President Donald Trump floated a new name to succeed him as president, while two expected 2028 hopefuls stood by.

The president asked the media if they'd like to continue with the press conference — where Trump provided updates on his trip to China where he met with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin. This prompted laughs from Vice President JD Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio, who flanked him on either side during the briefing.

Then, he asked newly-minted Chief of Staff Stephen Miller, whom he called the "most powerful man in the world," to speak with members of the press.

Miller took the podium and launched into a more than two-minute speech listing Trump's accomplishments from the first two years of his second term in office.

"I just wanted to say that what President Trump has achieved over the last two years is more than any president has achieved by far in American history. We can stand up here for four hours and go through achievement by achievement by achievement," Miller said.

He then rattled some of them off, including:

"Under his leadership, the next two years are going to be the greatest two years in American history," Miller said. "And I'm so proud to be his chief of staff to oversee on his behalf the staff in this building to keep delivering wins for this extraordinary American leader."

Trump was clearly impressed with Miller, who stepped in to replace Susie Wiles earlier this month following sweeping midterm losses for Republicans.

"We have breaking news! Breaking news!" Trump said. "He was better than these two guys," he added, turning towards Vance and Rubio, who both also spoke at the press conference.

"Do you want to run for president?" Trump asked Miller as he was leaving the podium, prompting laughter from the group.

No major names from either party have yet to announce their intentions to run for president in 2028. But with the midterms finished, rumors have swirled about who will toss their hats in the ring.

Vance and Rubio are widely viewed as the two most viable options for Republicans.

Miller's name has not been previously floated as a contender for launching a White House bid.
