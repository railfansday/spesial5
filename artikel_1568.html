Liverpool initiate sensational transfer to sign next Jude Bellingham

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Liverpool are ready to sign the next Jude Bellingham, and he dreams of joining the Reds.

It was Julian Ward who ultimately pulled out of the move to pursue the England international back in 2023. Still working as the club's sporting director, Ward though the deal would be too costly for the Reds - especially as they needed a complete midfield revamp.

It was Ward who then set in motion the policy of Liverpool buying not one but two and three for the price of Bellingham that summer with Alexis Mac Allister, Dominik Szoboszlai and Ryan Gravenberch arriving at Anfield.

Merely two years later, all three would become Premier League winners while starting in the same midfield under Arne Slot.

So, it's fair to say Ward probably doesn't have an regrets about that decision.

Julian Ward is targeting another no.10 for LiverpoolNow back at the helm once again, Ward's task is a little different this time around. The club doesn't just need a midfield overhaul, you could argue that in its entirety there needs to be an overhaul. Even if there are some exciting players, and the spine of the team does look promising. Andoni Iraola is still having to deal with first of all not enough jigsaw pieces to complete the puzzle, and in some cases, he has pieces that are not quite the right fit. It's the former issue that Ward is trying to address with his decision to pursue a no.10 for Liverpool in the January window. The club's sporting director knows that there's a reliance on Wirtz at the moment, and he doesn't want the German to be over-exhausted. Ward realises that Liverpool need a back-up in order to preserve the longevity of Wirtz's career.
