Pavlovic admits he liked Conceicao despite ‘Hitler-like’ methods: “Such a circus”

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

AC Milan defender Strahinja Pavlovic has admitted that he was a fan of Sergio Conceicao, even if his management was rather totalitarian.

It’s fair to say that Conceicao is a divisive figure within the Milan sphere. The Portuguese arrived and won the Supercoppa Italiana and there were glimpses of the Rossoneri becoming a team under him.

However, there were far too many incidents which turned most against him – including some members of the squad – and that gave him little hope of continuing. So, when he lost the Coppa Italia against Bologna, he was all but done, if he wasn’t before then.

He’s called it unfair since, but logically there was no way he could’ve continued.

Speaking to the Serbian podcast SS Calcio, Pavlovic admitted that he was fond of Conceicao, despite some of his strange methods, as MilanNews relays.

“Conceicao banned Nutella, and since then they haven’t allowed it [he laughs]. He was… He’s a good guy, I like him, but he had Hitler-like methods. We go away for a match and at the hotel we split up; the players stay together, everyone else stays outside.

“Knee and elbow pads are mandatory in training, whether the temperatures are below zero or 35 degrees; they have to be there.

“Three days before a match, some players went to a concert until 11 p.m.… He had assistants, or spies if you like, who reported to him, and he made such a circus out of it that in the next match, none of those five or six players were allowed on the pitch.”

Another anecdote about Conceiçao, when he had just been appointed coach of Milan…

“He was 40 minutes late to the first meeting; all the players and management were waiting for him, and he was in his office. He just wanted everyone to know who was in charge. He did it on purpose.”

It’s certainly a strong comparison to make; that’s for sure…
