Cameraman Beaten and Equipment Stolen in Attack on Journalists at French Protests

A cameraman was badly beaten and his camera equipment stolen while covering protests in France this weekend.

Warning: The following video contains content that some may find upsetting.

On Saturday, a team of journalists from French television and radio network BFM TV was attacked by protesters in Lyon. Demonstrations have taken place across France over the past two months over school funding, social inequality, and rising living costs.

According to a report by Le Monde, the television crew consisted of two journalists, including a cameraman, and two security guards. Local government officials in France say protesters “threw themselves upon them” in an attack that was “very violent.” Riot police then launched “an offensive” to protect the journalists.

In an open letter published on X (the platform formerly known as Twitter), one of the BFM TV journalists who was attacked says she “truly feared for (their) lives.” According to a report by French news outlet 20 Minutes, the BFM TV journalist says her cameraman was thrown to the ground and beaten, with some protesters shouting threats before stealing his camera. She also says he spent five hours in the emergency room. The extent of his injuries was not specified.

“My cameraman was violently thrown to the ground and then beaten. Some even shouted ‘Burn him!’ before snatching his camera. He spent five hours in the emergency room,” she recounted.

“You can dispute what journalists say. You can criticize their work. You can even hate them. But you don’t hit them,” she added.

Another freelance journalist who witnessed the incident says the cameraman was hit several times and that his camera was smashed and stolen. The journalist also says he had to remove his press badge to avoid being targeted and was struck twice in the foot during the incident.

“It was very, very violent. One of them was hit several times. His camera was thrown to the ground, broken, and then they picked it up and stole it. He was in shock,” the freelance journalist, who publishes under the name Medave Prod on social media, tells Le Monde.

“I had to take off my press badge myself, otherwise I would have been lynched too,” the journalist says, adding that he had been hit “twice in the foot.”

An investigation into “intentional violence committed by a group and with faces concealed” was opened, the Lyon prosecutor’s office announced on Sunday. The investigation also covers “damage to private property,” referring to the journalists’ equipment.
