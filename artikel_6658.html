Big, Fat Lapels Are All Over the GQ Men of the Year Party Carpet

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Andrew Garfield is on GQ’s plush 2026 Men of the Year red carpet, his sandy mane slightly cropped, two beefy lapels spread across his chest. The Artificial protagonist, wearing Savile Row stalwart Edward Sexton, is one of the three co-hosts—alongside his fellow MOTY cover stars, Matt Damon and Chris Rock—of this year’s party at the Chateau Marmont, and his ultra-sharp lapels made him look every bit like the Hollywood powerhouse he is.

Garfield’s Artificial co-star, Yura Borisov, was also sporting a fat, wooly set of wings, courtesy of Dior. And then there was Belmont Cameli, one of our 2026 Breakouts, who shot to fame as the romantic lead in yet another steamy hockey drama to take over our screens this year, Off Campus. While the actor wasn’t in skates and protective gear, the pinstripes and lapels on his Vivienne Westwood suit were so bodacious they were practically jutting out of his chest.

Another one of our anointed Breakouts, Owen Thiele of FX’s Adults, wore a slick, hickory-brown Valentino blazer. Ever since creative director Alessandro Michele took over in 2024, the Italian heritage brand has catered to a certain flavor of ’70s swank, with zeitgeisty talent—new friends of the house like Sombr and Alexander Skarsgård—bringing those pieces to life. Thiele let the double-breasted jacket serve as his outfit’s centerpiece, styling it with a high-neck, knotted top and a pair of straight-leg black trousers. Proof that sometimes the difference between a good suit and a great one is larger-than-life lapels.
