Katy Perry Shares Personal Photos of Her and Justin Trudeau From Their Baby2Baby Gala Date Night

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

On Saturday, Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau made a rare red carpet appearance at the Baby2Baby Gala. But Perry later shared her own photos from their night out supporting the charity. She posted several romantic shots of her and Trudeau in their matching looks on Instagram and tucked in one shot of them at the event with Zooey Deschanel and Jonathan Scott.

“Always a joy to sparkle for @Baby2Baby and celebrate the work they’ve done over the past 15 years ✨,” she wrote. “ICYMI: Every year, Baby2Baby reaches 1.5 million children in need across all 50 states, through homeless shelters, domestic violence programs, foster care, hospitals, and school districts, as well as children who have lost everything in the wake of disaster. I am so proud to be a small part of this incredible organization and to partner with them on @fireworkfoundation ♥️”

Perry previously shared photos with Trudeau to mark her 42nd birthday, revealing they went on a trip together. “Climbed to level 42 this week!” she wrote on October 29. “Was feeling indifferent about the inbetweeness of age 42…Then it was explained to me that 42 is actually the answer to everything. Though I still don’t understand, I’m ok with turning 42 as long as I can turn 42 with you ♥️ back to living…”

Perry and Trudeau have been dating for more than a year. In September, the singer called him the love of her life in an interview with People.

She looked back on her past year of healing, telling the outlet, “I learned how to step back into my purpose, and I got the greatest gift from this [Lifetimes] Tour: I got to meet the love of my life.”

Reflecting on her feelings just before meeting the former Canadian prime minister, she said, “I always wanted this epic, legendary love, and I was talking to my therapist about it: Everything in my life is epic, but this is kind of the missing puzzle piece—then it showed up.”

She also opened up about her boyfriend’s support of her music career, saying, “He knows the words, he’s supportive, and he’s in awe; it’s super healing. He is really heart-centered, and I think that we align in that way. I’m so grateful.”
