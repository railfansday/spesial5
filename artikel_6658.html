Zendaya Revives a 2000s Golden Gown at the 2026 Governors Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Zendaya is no stranger to shutting down a red carpet—so did you really not expect her to deliver a major fashion serve at the 2026 Governors Awards tonight?

The annual ceremony serves as the official kickoff to the 2027 awards season, so it’s no big surprise that the superstar and her stylist, Law Roach, started things off with a bang this evening—by cultivating a headline-worthy fashion moment worthy of the red carpet. The actor went for gold at the ceremony, choosing a shimmering metallic gown with three-dimensional padding at the hips. And better yet, the striking frock is vintage, too—from the late 2000s. Can you guess the brand and season?

Zendaya and Roach unearthed the fringed column gown from Giles Deacon’s fall 2007 collection, and it feels just as fresh and contemporary today (despite being almost a decade old). Of course. Zendaya and Roach are long known for turning throwback designs into modern-day hits: It’s one of their best fashion tricks. In the past, she has embraced everything from priceless archival Galliano to ’90s Versace to flashy Bob Mackie creations—and she has a knack for always making them feel just as relevant for today.

The big question is: Is this a preview into how Zendaya will approach red carpet dressing for the 2027 awards season ahead? There’s more than one feature to look forward to if so—her dress has all the correct notes to it, including it’s vintage, it’s eye-catching, and best of all, it’s bold. Safe or boring is simply not a word in her style vocabulary (and thank god for it).
