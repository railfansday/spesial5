Meghan Markle’s Two Favorite Staples Survived Her Move to the UK

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

A change of country needn’t necessitate a change of personal style. Having traded Montecito for the English countryside, Meghan Markle is reacquainting herself with British life, catching up with old friends, and reacclimatizing to a less balmy climate. Two tenets of her California capsule wardrobe, however, remain firmly intact.

The Duchess of Sussex’s longtime hairstylist and friend George Northwood took to Instagram to share some photographs from their recent reunions, offering a little insight into Meghan Markle’s new life in England. In one, the pair embrace in the grounds of her home, Meghan barefoot in a billowing, midnight-blue Anine Bing slip dress. Diaphanous with a lustrous finish, it’s just the sort of piece we’ve become accustomed to seeing the duchess wear in her Montecito mom era. (It wouldn’t look out of place among the abundant vegetable patches and flower beds of With Love, Meghan either.)

Another photograph, taken over lunch at Jeremy King’s London restaurant The Park in October, sees Meghan in another familiar sartorial guise. The duchess opted for a black peak-lapel tuxedo blazer from British high street favorite Marks & Spencer, worn with cropped blue jeans and neutral-colored heels. She added a trusty gold watch and glossy, loose brunette waves that brought a little California polish to the long lunch proceedings. Meghan has long been a proponent of the blazer-and-jeans combination, whether attending an Invictus Games engagement or heading out to dinner with friends. The latest addition to her tailoring arsenal comes courtesy of the British high street.

Northwood, of course, has been instrumental in the evolution of Meghan’s beauty aesthetic. The London-based hairstylist was responsible for the artfully undone chignon she wore to her wedding reception in 2018, accompanying her on royal tours of Ireland and Australia that same year. His knack for creating expensive-looking hair with a more effortless finish dovetails neatly with Meghan’s personal approach to getting dressed.

Since relocating to England with Prince Harry and their children, Archie and Lilibet, at the end of the summer, Meghan has gradually returned to public life. There was a dinner with Malala Yousafzai, a visit to Nottingham in coordinating burgundy cashmere separates from Joseph, and, earlier this month, a trip to Brussels with Harry for the unveiling of Archewell Philanthropies’ Lost Screen Memorial. Last week, the Sussexes also attended a holiday fair with their children.

And while Meghan has been reacquainting herself with the British high street—Boden, M&S, and Joseph have all featured in her recent wardrobe—her overarching fashion philosophy hasn’t changed all that much. A few strategically deployed cashmere layers aside, the duchess’s transatlantic capsule wardrobe appears to be holding up rather nicely.
