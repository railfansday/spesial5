Countdown to Christmas with Snoopy! The Best Peanuts Advent Calendars

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Lenox Peanuts Advent calendar is both a beautiful luxury gift for loved ones and a nostalgic yearly tradition for families.

Here at Taste of Home, we know all too well that Advent calendars are no longer just for tots. In fact, some of the top-selling picks among our readership over the last few years include wine calendars and other boozy options. Not to mention the bestselling Bonne Maman Advent calendar, which has been voted one of our favorites for three years running! But when it comes to collectible calendars that double as unforgettable gifts, nothing impresses us quite as much as the Lenox Peanuts Advent calendar.

That’s right! The brand behind the iconic Spice Village now has Advent calendars. And yes, the Peanuts Advent calendar is just as gorgeous and nostalgia-filled as you’d imagine. Here’s my experience unboxing this assortment of adorable ornaments. Warning: spoilers ahead!

Boasting 25 ornaments crafted in Lenox's signature porcelain, the Lenox Peanuts Advent calendar is a luxury Advent calendar unlike any other.

The Lenox Peanuts Advent calendar isn’t just your average Christmas countdown. It’s a luxury Advent calendar that unfolds to reveal 25 doors, each housing its own adorable ornament. Because these are made from Lenox’s signature  premium porcelain, they’re meant to be enjoyed season after season. That makes this Advent calendar an extra special gift for Peanuts fans. It also makes a fun yearly tradition for families! Don’t forget to put on A Charlie Brown Christmas as you hang them on your Christmas tree!

This collector’s calendar comes with 25 different tree hangers, each boasting either an iconic wintery scene from the comic strips or one of our favorite classic characters. In total, there are 13 characters and 12 scenic snowflakes, all of which sit at around two inches tall. And these bite-sized heirlooms aren’t found anywhere else—you can only get them inside the Advent calendar, making them a thoughtful gift for collectors.

My experience unboxing the Lenox Peanuts Advent calendar was truly magical. Even before I opened the first door, I was struck by the packaging. While simple, the shiny red box looks classy and expensive. It also feels nice and hefty—a nod to the precious porcelain ornaments nested inside. Once you loosen the gold clasp, the calendar folds open like a book to reveal 25 doors, and each opens to reveal a figurine or snowflake nestled in a protective foam cushion. I love that the box is protective enough to reuse for storage once the season ends, too!

From Lionel to Peppermint Patty to Charlie himself, each of the character ornaments offers extraordinary detail. Despite the small size, they’re slightly heavy, so you can tell they’re also high-quality. Among the figurines are also several iterations of fan favorite puppy dog Snoopy partaking in holiday traditions and lounging on his light-wrapped doghouse.

Even the snowflake ornaments offer their own holiday charm. Each one is carefully painted with a miniature scene from the wintery comic strips. Among the art are ageless, captivating sequences of the gang gathered around a lighted tree, Lucy performing on ice skates and mischievous Snoopy tangled up in a string of vibrant Christmas lights among many others. Adorning each hanging loop is a festive tassel, giving the ornaments a little added flair.

Whether you plan on giving this one as a gift or want to keep it as a holiday heirloom, it’s truly a special collection that the whole family can enjoy. It’s also the perfect way to signal the most wonderful time of the year is here!

While we absolutely love the Lenox Peanuts advent calendar as a yearly tradition, it’s not exactly child-friendly (without parental assistance, that is). For folks seeking an alternative Advent calendar for kids that still offers the same Peanuts charm, we love this Peanuts puzzle Advent calendar. Deputy Editor Katie Bandurski tested it last year and praises its adorable prints, which cover all of the classic characters.

Want something a little sweeter? Sure, you can always opt for a classic chocolate Advent calendar. But for fans of Snoopy, this adorably delicious Advent calendar from Sugarfina makes a showstopping gift. It’s brimming with 24 of the brand’s popular sweet treats, including gummies in fun holiday shapes, peach hearts, sparkling stars, sparkle pops, chocolate biscuits, confetti caramels and so much more.

“This calendar gets major points for its design even before you get into the good stuff,” raves Katie, who enjoyed last year’s edition. “It’s shaped like the iconic red doghouse. It gets even better when you open the doors to the calendar and find a pop-up paper Snoopy.”

This gorgeous Advent calendar will quickly become a yearly family tradition.

Absolutely smitten with the limited-edition Lenox Peanuts Advent calendar? We don’t blame you. Folks wanting to get their hands on one ahead of the holidays can find them on the Lenox website, Wayfair and Macy’s. The calendar retails for around $300, but offers an incredible $500 value, making it a great choice for gifting or adding to your yearly holiday traditions.

One note: We recommend investing in one as soon as you can, because they’re bound to sell out well ahead of Christmas time.
