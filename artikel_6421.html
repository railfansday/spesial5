Low oxygen at high altitudes may protect honey bees from deadly parasites

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Honey bees (Apis mellifera) are the most important managed pollinator of agricultural crops globally. A study published Nov. 19 in the open-access journal PLOS Biology by Huoqing Zheng at Zhejiang University, Hangzhou, China, and colleagues suggests that parasitic mites causing honey bee colony collapse have less reproductive success at high altitudes.
