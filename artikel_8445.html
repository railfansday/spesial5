To be straight, I don't really have this all figured out just yet. I'm leery about the idea of "not knowing what I don't know", as well as the fact that having been sanguine about the market plunges during dotcom bust, GFC, Covid, Tariffs, etc. does not necessarily translate to remaining sanguine once the Megacorp W-2 paychecks stop rolling in. Can't know what that's truly like until I retire.

The main issue right now is that I'd like to get a TIPS ladder going but have no place to put it -- and so the plan is to put one together after retirement when the 401K is rolled over into an IRA and I can build a ladder out in that. Until then, stuck with "total bond funds" in the Megacorp 401k.
I'm creating a 6 year rolling TIPS ladder with defined maturity TIPS ETFs. I admit that it is kind of a random stab at having some inflation mitigating, reasonably stable, assets in my portfolio. In my effort to fool myself into thinking it isn't just a random shot-in-the-dark, I have each rung targeted to cover the RMD amount that I expect to need to come out of my IRA 6 years in the future. 6 years was chosen because that would be roughly 10% of my Portfolio. The idea is that I'll always have the cash in my IRA to take the RMD. My AA is around 85/15 and will likely drift down to 90/10.

Not entirely rational, but I just felt like doing it.
