Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: Cold feet on a $950K house. Would you walk away from a $45K deposit? If you’d close, how much would you put down?

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

OP, it sounds like your alternatives are 1) keep renting indefinitely, or 2) find either a different house for roughly the same price that doesn’t need any work or a cheaper house that does need work. You say you haven’t been able to get a bid accepted on a house in 3 years. Is the market likely to suddenly get better where you live? Are new houses being built in the neighborhoods where you want to live?

I’m guessing the answer to both of those questions is no. So, if you reject this house, it sounds like you’d better resign yourself to renting indefinitely.

Personally, I wouldn’t put that much money down. It’s not as liquid as other investments if you need to get some of that money out.
