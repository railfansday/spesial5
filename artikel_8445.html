Personal Consumer Issues • Re: New computer

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

When I'm away from home, including on vacation, my smartphone provides all the computer access and functionality I need. At home we've got a few Dell desktops and they've always been great. We seem to get between 7 and 11 years of use from each of them due to Windows upgrade limitations and hardware capacity. Great service for low cost. Our work laptops seem to always get replaced every 2 or 3 years if they don't conk out earlier than that due to a hardware failure. I've avoided laptops at home because of the hassle to take them somewhere for repair. Of course a desktop is more cumbersome to take in for repair but I've never had to do that. There are plenty of web references and youtube videos that tell you how to fix things, particularly for Dells which are all over the corporate world. I'm really sold on Dell products. Other brands, like HP, can be less expensive but their models can change so frequently it's hard to find a repository of solutions for problems that may develop the model you have.
