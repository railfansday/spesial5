From Cooper Flagg to Ben Simmons, a look at Montverde Academy’s top NBA draft picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Montverde Academy (FL) has established itself as one of the premier pipelines for basketball talent in the country, producing NBA stars and some of the sport's most recognizable names.

From No. 1 overall selections to first-round picks who have gone on to become franchise cornerstones, the Central Florida powerhouse has built an impressive résumé at the professional level.

With three players selected first overall in the NBA Draft, here is a look at the 10 highest NBA Draft picks to graduate from Montverde Academy.

The third Montverde Academy product to be selected No. 1 overall, Flagg was the centerpiece of the school's undefeated 2023-24 national championship team. After one season at Duke, he was selected first overall by Dallas in 2025 and won NBA Rookie of the Year in 2026. He is now emerging as the face of the Mavericks' franchise

Cunningham became Montverde's second No. 1 overall selection after leading the Eagles alongside Scottie Barnes and Moses Moody. Following one season at Oklahoma State, he was drafted first overall by Detroit. He has since become a two-time NBA All-Star and led the Pistons' resurgence, including a 60-win season in 2025-26

Simmons became Montverde's first No. 1 overall NBA Draft pick after starring for the Eagles before playing one season at LSU. He won NBA Rookie of the Year in 2018 and became a three-time All-Star with Philadelphia and Brooklyn. His NBA career has since been derailed by injuries, and he is no longer an established NBA roster player.

Russell helped lead Montverde to a national championship before playing one season at Ohio State. He was selected second overall by the Lakers and has gone on to play for several NBA franchises, including Brooklyn, Golden State, Minnesota and the Lakers.

A former five-star recruit, Barrett starred at Montverde before playing one season at Duke. He was selected third overall by the Knicks and spent four-plus seasons in New York before being traded to Toronto in 2023. He remains a key member of the Raptors.

Barnes was part of Montverde's loaded 2020-21 team alongside Cunningham and Moody. After one season at Florida State, he was selected fourth overall by Toronto. He won NBA Rookie of the Year in 2022 and has since become a two-time All-Star and franchise cornerstone.

Duren spent his final high school season at Montverde before playing one year at Memphis. Originally selected 13th overall by Charlotte, his draft rights were traded to Detroit. He has developed into one of the Pistons' key frontcourt players and earned his first NBA All-Star selection in 2026.

Queen was part of Montverde's undefeated 33-0 national championship team in 2023-24. Following one season at Maryland, he was selected 13th overall by Atlanta before being traded to New Orleans. He is now beginning his NBA career with the Pelicans.

Moody helped Montverde capture a national championship during the 2020-21 season before playing one year at Arkansas. He was selected 14th overall by Golden State and has developed into a rotation player for the Warriors. He is currently recovering from a ruptured patellar tendon suffered in March 2026.

Achiuwa graduated from Montverde Academy in 2019 before playing one season at Memphis. He was selected 20th overall by the Miami Heat in the 2020 NBA Draft. He has since played for Miami, Toronto and the New York Knicks.
