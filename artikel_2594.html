WNBA makes announcement as Brittney Griner rewrites league history

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Brittney Griner just wrapped up a historic season with very little fuss.

Brittney Griner's 2026 season with the Connecticut Sun was a rough ride. The veteran center put up 13.2 points, 5.8 rebounds, and 2.5 assists a night.

Unfortunately, her individual play wasn't enough to lift Connecticut, as the Sun limped to an 11–33 finish and missed the postseason.

Looking ahead, Griner hasn't inked a deal for 2027 yet, after playing on a one-year contract, she enters February's free agency window completely open to signing anywhere.

During #WNBADefenseWeek, the official WNBA account hopped on X to give Brittney Griner her flowers, posting "THE BLOCK LEADER" alongside a crown emoji.

The league celebrated her history-making season, noting that she finished the year with a staggering 891 career blocks. By hitting that mark, Griner officially passed the late Margo Dydek, whose record of 877 blocks had stood untouched since 2008.

Even at 36, Brittney Griner still gives a contender plenty to work with. She protects the rim, scores easily in the paint, and brings real title experience.

And with chatter linking her to Indiana, the 6-foot-9 vet could anchor the Fever's defense and give their young core a steady post presence, which Caitlin Clark has called for.
