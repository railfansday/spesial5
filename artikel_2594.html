NFL news, live updates: Aidan Hutchinson reportedly suffers torn ACL on sack celebration; Patriots QB Drake Maye out indefinitely with ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Another NFL Sunday is in the books, with just one game left in Week 10 as the Baltimore Ravens host the LA Chargers on "Monday Night Football."

Sunday kicked off with a bang as the Detroit Lions and New England Patriots faced off in Munich, Germany, with injuries to two stars that will have all eyes drawn as the new week plays out. Lions star defensive end Aidan Hutchinson suffered what is feared to be a season-ending ACL tear while celebrating a sack against Patriots quarterback Drake Maye … who suffered an ankle injury on that very sack, which knocked him out of the game and could cost him the season.

Week 10 also offered us our first look at Las Vegas Raiders No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza as a starter. He led scoring drives on each of the Raiders' first two possessions and entered halftime with a 10-7 lead over the Seattle Seahawks, but Drew Lock rallied with two passing touchdowns in the third quarter to take the lead, improving to 3-0 as a starter for Seattle this season. Raiders star tight end Brock Bowers exited the loss with an ankle injury.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for the latest NFL news, injury updates and more coming out of Week 10.

NFL Network's Ian Rapoport gave an update on some injuries to watch on Tuesday. It is still early in the practice week, but Rapoport mainly spoke about Kenneth Walker III and Baker Mayfield.

Walker missed last week's game due to a groin injury, but rookie Emmett Johnson balled out, so the Chiefs may not be rushing Walker back to the field.

Mayfield is coming off a bye, but was spotted in a walking boot last week at the facility. Rapoport said that he thinks Mayfield could miss this week's game, in which undrafted rookie QB Jalon Daniels would be back under center for Tampa Bay.

The Tennessee Titans are adding wide receiver Wan'Dale Robinson to injured reserve due to an ankle injury, per team reporter Jim Wyatt.

In a corresponding move, the team is signing wide receiver Xavier Restrepo to the active roster.

Robinson suffered the ankle injury in the team's Week 10 loss to the Jacksonville Jaguars and could not return to the game. Restrepo was activated from the practice squad on game day and finished with 31 yards on three receptions.

Robinson has played in all nine games for Tennessee this season. He has brought in 38 receptions for 272 yards and a team-high three receiving touchdowns.

Head coach Robert Saleh has not revealed the extent of Robinson's ankle injury. If the wideout returns this season, he would first be eligible to be activated off IR in Week 15, ahead of the team's game against the Indianapolis Colts.

Tennessee will be without Robinson for at least the games against the Dallas Cowboys, Jacksonville Jaguars, Washington Commanders, and Detroit Lions.

Miami Dolphins defensive tackle Jordan Phillips suffered a torn biceps in last week's loss to the Indianapolis Colts that will sideline the 2025 fifth-round pick for the remainder of the season, according to ESPN's Marcel Louis-Jacques.

Phillips is the third Miami DT to sustain a season-ending injury in the past two weeks, per Louis-Jacques.

The Dolphins are 1-8 under first-year head coach Jeff Hafley. That said, their young core has flashed promise, especially on the defensive side of the ball.

The Commanders announced Tuesday that they are opening the 21-day practice window for running back Jacory Croskey-Merritt.

He has been sidelined due to a groin injury since Week 5. The running back remains on IR and must be activated before he can play in a game for Washington.

With the injury to Patriots QB Drake Maye, and the Chargers' 1-8 start, the NFL has moved the game out of primetime.

New England-Los Angeles will now kick at 4:05 p.m. ET. Moving the Titans and Jaguars game into a 1 p.m. ET kick.

The new SNF kick will feature the Las Vegas Raiders taking on the Cleveland Browns.

Cronin also added that Kyler Gordon is considered "day-to-day" by head coach Ben Johnson due to a shoulder injury also suffered against the Buccaneers.

The Texans are on a short week this week, taking on the Colts on Thursday night in an AFC South divisional matchup.

It's a tough time for Houston to not have many days to get some much-needed rest, as the Texans are looking like they could be down some impactful players.

The Cleveland Browns have been through this type of season before. It has just been a long time.

In 1979, the Browns had seven fourth-quarter drives to win games. In the 1980 season they were 11-5 and nine of the wins came by one score. That group got the nickname of the Kardiac Kids. Brian Sipe became a Cleveland legend, and won the 1980 NFL MVP award.

The 2026 Browns aren't at that level yet, but they're getting close. After rallying to beat the Houston Texans on a touchdown with 53 seconds left, the Browns are the first team in NFL history to win each of its first six games by four or fewer points, according to ESPN Insights. Every one of those wins has included a game-winning drive in the fourth quarter. It has been a magical, improbable run.

Detroit Lions head coach Dan Campbell won't let one freak injury change how he approaches the game. Following star pass rusher Aidan Hutchinson tearing his ACL during a celebration, Campbell said he will not discourage other Lions players from celebrating big plays moving forward.

Campbell, who made those comments during an appearance on Detroit's 97.1 The Ticket, explained that Hutchinson's injury was "just one of those things," and that's not enough for Campbell to establish a "nobody celebrates anymore" rule.

"I hate it, I know he hates it. But I mean, the number of moves that I've seen Hutch make and the number of plyometrics, explosive movements, the spins, the one-leg lateral movements, lateral balances, I'm just like, it was one of those things, man," Campbell said. "I hate it. I think we all understand what it is, and let's be smart, but no, I'm not gonna say, 'Nobody celebrates anymore.'"

When asked whether he felt he would need to address the issue with the team, Campbell shot down that idea. He then revealed that he once sustained an injury while celebrating a big play.

The Raiders have some good news on star tight end Brock Bowers, who exited Sunday's loss to the Seahawks with an ankle injury. Though the injury, which occurred during the first quarter, initially appeared concerning, Las Vegas head coach Klint Kubiak said Monday that things were better than originally feared.

In a press conference, Kubiak said that the team was "still evaluating" Bowers, but that the tight end will be day-to-day with the injury.

"It's not as bad as we thought, but still is a significant injury, so we'll keep monitoring him," Kubiak said.

Bowers, who missed the first two games of the season after undergoing minor knee surgery, leads Las Vegas in receiving with 676 yards and six touchdowns in eight games.

New York Jets head coach Aaron Glenn was hired by the team thanks to his excellence as a defensive coordinator. But after the team struggled on defense during Glenn's first year as head coach, he decided to take over defensive play-calling duties in 2026.

That change lasted just nine games. Glenn told reporters Monday he gave up defensive play-calling ahead of the team's 31-12 loss to the Buffalo Bills in Week 10, per ESPN.

Glenn said the move was made to help the team win games. He added that while Jets defensive coordinator Brian Duker called plays Sunday, Glenn was "very involved" in the team's scheme.

"I've said this and I will continue to do this: Anything I can do as a head coach to help us win games, I'm willing to do it," Glenn said. "I'm not afraid to do that. So I gave him the lead, but I was very involved in what we ran."

The change appeared positive initially, as the Jets (2-8) gave up just 10 points in the first half Sunday. But Buffalo's offense got rolling in the second half, allowing the team to pull away.

The Jets ultimately gave up 31 points in a loss to the Bills. Though, in Duker's defense, facing Josh Allen in your first game as a defensive play-caller is among the toughest asks in the NFL. The Bills rank first in the NFL with 31.9 points scored per game. In that sense, Duker's defense actually held the team under its season-long average … barely. The Jets still allowed 31 points, so the team shouldn't start popping the champagne just yet.
