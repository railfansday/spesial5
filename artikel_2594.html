Arizona Cardinals Week 11 defensive, special teams snap counts vs. Chiefs

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In the Arizona Cardinals' 34-7 loss to the Kansas City Chiefs, 20 players participated in at least one of the 72 defensive snaps, and 33 players participated in at least one of the 24 special teams snaps.

How was the playing time divided? We have the numbers for everyone below.

The first number after each name is their snap count and the second is the percentage. Players in bold were starters.

NT Roy Lopez (37/51), L.J. Collier (36/50), Walter Nolen III (35/49), Darius Robinson (31/43), Dante Stills (30/42), Jared Harrison-Hunte (11/15)

Injured reserve: Zach Carter (practicing), Kaleb Proctor

Nolen had a sack for seven yards and a quarterback hit, while adding four tackles (one for loss/two solo). Playing the most defensive snaps after practicing only one day because of a toe and ankle injury, Lopez tied for the lead in tackles among the group with five and a pass defensed. Robinson (one solo) and a QB hit and Stills (two solo) also had five. Collier had two tackles (one solo).

ILB Mack Wilson Sr. (72/100), ILB Jack Gibbens (53/74), OLB Josh Sweat (38/53), OLB Baron Browning (36/50), OLB Zaven Collins (34/47), OLB Eku Leota (28/39), ILB Cody Simon (19/26)

Did not play: ILB Karson Sharar (played only on special teams)

Injured reserve: OLB Jordan Burch (eligible to return, but won't)

Gibbens was the team leader with 10 tackles (six solo) and also had a QB hit. Wilson had eight tackles (four solo). Simon and Collins each had two tackles (one solo). Simon played only 19 snaps because of a calf injury. Browning had one solo tackle and Leota one assist. Leota had been inactive for the previous three games. Sweat had two QB hits, but nothing else.

CB Denzel Burke (72/100), CB Kei'Trel Clark (72/100), S Wydett Williams Jr. (72/100), S Budda Baker (69/96), CB Kalen King (34/47), CB Garrett Williams (10/14), S Dadrion Taylor-Demerson (3/4)

Did not play: S Kitan Crawford, CB Max Melton (both played only on special teams)

Injured reserve: S Andrew Wingard (eligible to return after Week 15), S Joey Blount, CB Will Johnson (eligible to return, but won't)

Baker had six tackles (five solo). Burke and Clark each had five tackles with four solo, with Burke also having a pass defensed. Wydett Williams Jr., getting the start after Wingard was placed on injured reserve, had four tackles (one solo). King had two solo tackles

LB Karson Sharar (23/96), S Kitan Crawford (23/96), CB Max Melton (23/96), S Dadrion Taylor-Demerson (17/71), LB Cody Simon (15/62), TE Elijah Higgins (14/58), LB Eku Leota (14/58), TE Teagan Quitoriano (13/54), LB Zaven Collins (12/50), LB Jack Gibbens (11/46), WR/RS Devin Duvernay (9/38), LB Baron Browning (9/38), WR Simi Fehoko (8/33), RB Evan Hull (8/33), P/Holder Blake Gillikin (7/29), LS Casey Kreiter (7/29), DL Dante Stills (7/29), NT Roy Lopez (7/29), TE Hunter Long (6/25), DL L.J. Collier (6/25), CB Kei'Trel Clark (6/25), K Chad Ryland (3/12), CB Garrett Williams (3/12), RB Bam Knight (2/8), DL Jared Harrison-Hunte (2/8), CB Kalen King (2/8), C Hayden Conner (1/4), G Isaac Seumalo (1/4), G Isaiah Adams (1/4), T Jayden Williams (1/4), T Elijah Wilkinson (1/4), T Jedrick Wills Jr. (1/4), T Josh Fryar (1/4)

Melton, who didn't play any snaps on defense, was busy on special teams. He had three kickoff returns of 30 or more yards with a long of 37 and 34.3-yard average. Duvernay averaged 27.0 yards on three kickoffs and had a long of 32. Melton also caught a punt at the 3-yard line.

Gillikin had a rough day, partly because of short-field punts, but he also had one for 23 yards from the Cardinals 24-yard line. That resulted in an overall average of 40.2 and 36.8-yard net.

Ryland's day consisted of one extra point and two kickoffs.
