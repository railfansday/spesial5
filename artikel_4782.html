This time of year, my shopping cart is primarily filled with gifts for others, but when I saw that lululemon was hosting a rare Black Friday sale, I had to treat myself. What can I say? It’s an amazing time to stock up on wardrobe staples like this Evolve Half-Zip Hoodie while it’s only $84 in select colors. Lululemon doesn’t run a lot of official sales, so I’m excited to see rare savings on several best-selling items. Merry early Christmas to me!

Of course, this is also a fantastic opportunity to buy gifts for your fellow lululemon-loving friends and family. I snagged an Everyday Belt Bag for my daughter last week when they were under $20, and while that size and deal sold out wildly fast, the larger Belt Bag still has a few colors left on sale. I’ve rounded up the most notable deals below (including plenty of winter-friendly workout gear), which are slated to last through Cyber Monday. I wouldn’t wait, though—as I mentioned, popular pieces and sizes have already sold out since the sale started last week.

The Steady State Joggers and Dance Studio Mid-Rise Pants are both designed for lounging or wearing as an extra layer to head to or from the gym—we love a double-duty buy! It’s worth repeating that I’ve watched popular Lululemon sale items sell out in the past, so I wouldn’t wait until the last minute to shop for a certain size or color.
