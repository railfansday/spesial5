Xabi Alonso issues new Chelsea injury update as Joao Pedro return timeline revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Xabi Alonso has provided a fresh update on Joao Pedro’s recovery from injury after he was quizzed whether the Chelsea striker could return for the Crystal Palace game next week.

The Blues head to the City Ground on Friday night to face Nottingham Forest as they bid to get back to winning ways after successive Premier League defeats to Sunderland and Leeds.

While Alonso has confirmed that Moises Caicedo is available to play, neither Maxence Lacroix nor Joao Pedro have returned from their injuries in time to be involved against Forest.

Chelsea then play midweek next week, hosting Crystal Palace on Wednesday night.

Alonso was asked whether there is a chance of the Brazilian No9 returning from his thigh injury to feature in that London derby.

The Spanish manager remained coy, replying: “We talk on Tuesday”, indicating that he will provide an update in his press conference ahead of the Palace game.

Earlier on Thursday, he was asked whether his side would have any players returning from injury for the Forest game.

“No,” he replied, before going on to reveal good news about Ecuador captain Caicedo being back available following a medical assessment after being forced off with a knock in the first half of the Leeds match.

“Yes, he is going to be okay for tomorrow,” Alonso said of the former Brighton midfielder.

Alonso’s men have endured a difficult run of form in recent weeks, winning just one of their last five matches.

Last time out, they were thrashed 4-1 at home by Leeds United, leaving them eighth in the Premier League table.
