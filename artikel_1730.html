Man Utd hero Nicky Butt snubs Man City in naming Arsenal’s only Premier League title rivals

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Nicky Butt believes that Liverpool will prove to be Arsenal’s main title challengers over the likes of Manchester City this season.

Defending champions Arsenal extended their lead at the top of the table to six points after a hard-fought draw away at Newcastle.

Brighton had the chance to cut that lead to just three points, but a late Oli McBurnie brace saw them lose 2-0 to Hull City on Sunday.

Manchester City did snap their three-match losing streak, but a 1-1 draw on Fulham means Enzo Maresca’s side are now winless in four Premier League matches and also six points off the league leaders.

City will have the opportunity to eat into Arsenal’s lead at the top of the table when the two sides meet at the Emirates next week.

However, Butt does not expect them to last the course in this season’s title race, instead talking up Liverpool’s chances after their impressive 4-0 win over Manchester United on Sunday.

‘Any win for Liverpool against Man United or vice versa is massive,’ Butt said on The Good, The Bad & The Football podcast.

‘Forget the ten men or the level of the team they are playing against at the time. When Liverpool beat Man United or the other way around, it’s a massive result, especially for a new manager.

‘It was only a couple of years ago that they won the league, so they’re not a bad team, and they’ve added well.

Paul Scholes, meanwhile, was less certain about Liverpool’s title chances this season, but admitted he was impressed by their start under Andoni Iraola.

‘He’s started off well, hasn’t he?’ Scholes said. ‘I think it’s going to be gradual, but they have got the makings of a very good team.

‘And they haven’t half spent a few quid, they’ve got three £100m-plus players so you would expect them to be alright.’

Liverpool will look to make it three league wins on the spin when they return to action next Sunday against local rivals Everton at the Hill Dickinson Stadium.
