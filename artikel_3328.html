BBC Studios Hires Former Amazon Exec Bree Frank In Production Role

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

BBC Studios has named Bree Frank as SVP, Production.

The Dancing with the Stars producer has hired Frank from Evolution Media, the Amazon MGM Studios-owned producer, where she worked on series including The Real Housewives of Rhode Island, The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills and The Real Housewives of Orange County.

In the role, she will work across BBCS’ unscripted slate, which in addition to the ABC dancing series, includes Fox’s The 1% Club, Celebrity Weakest Link, and Nation’s Dumbest, and Netflix’s Outlast. She will oversee production operations.

Before Evolution Media, she was SVP, Physical Production, Unscripted at Hello Sunshine, which was behind Apple’s My Kind of Country, where she built out the company’s physical production services, and before that she was Executive in Charge of Production at Matador Content. Watch on Deadline

Additional credits include Wife Swap, Surf Girls and Meet Me in Paris.
