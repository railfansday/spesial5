BeIN Media Group To Bring ‘Michael’, ‘Hamnet’ & ‘Jurassic World Rebirth’ To MENA Audiences With Fresh NBC Universal Deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Qatar’s beIN Media Group and NBCUniversal Global are reinforcing their long relationship with a new multi-year output agreement including recent box office hits Michael and Jurassic World Rebirth as well as awards winner Hamnet.

The deal with NBCUniversal Global TV Distribution, announced on the sidelines of the Film Committee at Media City Qatar’s Doha Industry Days 2026, gives beIN exclusive rights to a broad portfolio of NBCUniversal content across the Middle East and North Africa (MENA), including Pay-1 films and first-run series.

The deal will provide high-end content for beIN’s entertainment channels, beIN ON DEMAND, beIN CONNECT, and TOD by beIN. It covers regional rights to NBCUniversal’s latest films following their theatrical release, as well as new series premiering for the first time in the region, Peacock Original content, and a vast collection of NBCUniversal’s beloved library film and TV series. Watch on Deadline

As well as Michael, Jurassic World Rebirth, and Hamnet, the deal also includes The Super Mario Galaxy Movie, and Wicked: For Good as well as movies from the franchises Fast & Furious, How to Train Your Dragon, Minions, Shrek, and Downton Abbey.

Dramas series covered by the accord include recently released legal drama War, starring Sienna Miller Dominic West, as well as the complete sets of library shows such as The Office and Suits

Michael Bonner, President, NBCUniversal Global TV Distribution added that the new agreement builds on the studio’s on existing “valued” relationship with beIN and also showed their shared commitment to bring high-end content to audiences across the Middle East and North Africa.

“With beIN’s reach and deep understanding of the region, we look forward to continuing our work together and offering their viewers an exceptional range of NBCUniversal films and television series for years to come,” he said.
