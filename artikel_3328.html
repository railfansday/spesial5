Willie Geist To Depart MS NOW’s ‘Morning Joe,’ Will Expand NBC News Role And Continue To Host ‘Sunday Today’

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Willie Geist will focus full time on NBC News’ Sunday Today and expand his presence on Today and the network, while departing MS NOW‘s Morning Joe at the end of the year.

Guest was the last remaining on-air talent to work for NBC News and MS NOW following the latter’s split from Comcast into a new company, Versant.

Geist made the announcement on Morning Joe on Monday. He has been a regular on the show for nearly 20 years. He joined Today in 2012 as co-host of the 9 a.m. hour, and has hosted Sunday Today since 2016. “We knew this was coming, but it makes it no less difficult,” Geist said on Morning Joe on Monday. He said it “was never intended to last forever.” Watch on Deadline

He said that there was an understanding that his stint on both networks would last through the midterms. He has split his time between Morning Joe‘s Times Square studios and Today‘s Rockefeller Center studios.

“There would not have been a Morning Joe over over the past 20 years without you and Mika and me, you two, because you’re so patient with me, and I appreciate it,” co-host Joe Scarborough said on the show, next to Mika Brzezinski.

Scarborough added, “I will say, too, to the corporate overlords: I’m not thrilled. You’re not thrilled. Mika’s not thrilled. But we are really grateful … They cared about this show, and I will say at both networks, they cared about this show and what we had built so much that they allowed you to be the only hybrid employee.”

He said that Geist will be able to come back for the show’s 20th anniversary in May.

Geist recalled that 20 years ago, he was a freelance producer at MSNBC “and Joe Scarborough, don’t ask me why, called me one time and said, ‘Hey, we’re going to do a political show in the morning, and I’d love for you to be on it.’ And I said, ‘Me? Did you want me to connect you to someone else that you’re looking for?’ He said, ‘No, no, I’ve seen you do a couple of things here and there. I think it would really work.'”

“Joe had a vision way before any of us did, way before I did,” Geist said.

Morning Joe has averaged 761,000 viewers this year, up 24% from the same period a year ago, with a heavy viewership of New York and Beltway insiders.

The Comcast split meant that a number of personalities who had dual roles had to land either at NBC News or the rebranded MS NOW. Figures like Steve Kornacki chose to stay at NBC News, while Stephanie Ruhle and Jacob Soboroff were among those who went to MS NOW. Peter Alexander, chief White House correspondent and co-host of Saturday Today, was at NBC News after the split, but departed for MS NOW earlier this year, and he now hosts a weekday 11 a.m. ET news show.
