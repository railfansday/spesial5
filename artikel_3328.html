Watch Harry Shum Jr. Honor ‘Glee’ Costar Naya Rivera on ‘Dancing With the Stars’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Harry Shum Jr. paid tribute to Naya Rivera, his late Glee costar, during a performance on Dancing With the Stars last night.

Shum Jr. and his professional dance partner Jenna Johnson performed a quickstep to “Valerie,” a hit song popularized by Amy Winehouse. Shum Jr. and Rivera sang the number together during Glee‘s second season. Rivera died in 2020 at age 33.

“I’m excited because that song was just so meaningful to a lot of Glee fans,” Shum explained on the show. “Glee definitely changed my life. Gave me the opportunity to not only dance, but act, and I never thought I would be able to dance to that song again. This is where we just really crank it up.”

During the dance, Shum pointed up at the sky and blew a kiss, seemingly to Rivera. He told the judges, “I feel like Dancing With the Stars is synonymous with Glee, and to be able to dance to Naya’s version, it means so much to so many people, including myself. We miss you, baby girl.”

Later on the show, Shum Jr. and Johnson performed the pasa doble to “We Found Love” by Rihanna and Calvin Harris.

Rivera drowned during a boating trip with her four-year-old son in July 2020 on Lake Piru in California. She was initially reported missing, but later confirmed dead. At the time, Shum Jr. shared an emotional letter to his costar on Twitter, writing, “You gave life your all and I hope all the good that you have given to the world will be returned in abundance when you reunite with our brother in the heavenly skies.”

Shum Jr. has not confirmed whether he plans to join the upcoming reboot of Glee, which was announced earlier this year. “I’m currently really, really busy now,” he said in October, adding that he would “love to keep continuing” if possible.
