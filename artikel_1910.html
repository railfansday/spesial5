Thomas Frank appointed new Crystal Palace manager on two-and-a-half year deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Thomas Frank has been confirmed as Crystal Palace’s new manager, with the Dane in post for the trip to Coventry this weekend.

He replaces Pierre Sage, who was sacked last week with Palace bottom of ther Premier League table.

Frank, who has been out of work since leaving Tottenham in February, was on Palace’s shortlist over the summer as they looked for a replacement for Oliver Glasner.

The Eagles ultimately agreed a three-year deal with Sage, but a disastrous start to the season saw the Frenchman relieved of his duties on Thursday.

The Dane has penned a two and a half year contract with the south Londoners, with club chairman Steve Parish hailing his “proven track record”.

“I’m delighted to be able to welcome Thomas to Crystal Palace,” Parish said in the club announcement.

“He has a proven track record of both creating winning teams and improving players, making him the perfect fit for our club.

“I’m confident Thomas’ extensive experience in the Premier League will help us achieve our goals for the rest of the season.”

In his first words as Palace boss, Frank called his new side an “iconic club”.

"It's an honour to sit here and be in charge of this fantastic club. I'm really, really looking forward to it.

"Crystal Palace is an iconic club in England and in the Premier League. I will do everything I possibly can to help and support the players and everyone around the place, and I'm really looking forward to meeting them.”

Frank endured a difficult spell at Spurs, who had been dragged into a relegation battle by the time he was sacked, but there remains a good deal of appreciation for his work at Brentford.

During his time in west London, Frank earned promotion to the Premier League before stabilising Brentford as a top-flight club and getting them challenging for Europe on one of the smallest budgets in the division.

Frank’s immediate task will be to drag Palace out of the relegation zone, starting with back-to-back fixtures against newly promoted Coventry and Hull.
