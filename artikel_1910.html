Mauricio Pochettino wants Jason Wilcox replaced as Manchester United's sporting director, sources have told Football Insider.

The 54-year-old has been appointed as Man United's new manager and will take charge of his first match in eight days against Liverpool at Anfield, but he wants further changes behind the scenes.

Football Insider can reveal that Pochettino wants someone he can trust in charge of recruitment at Old Trafford.

Discussions have already taken place between the Argentine and the United hierarchy over potential candidates for the key role.

It is understood that a potential change in sporting director was key to the former Tottenham Hotspur manager coming to Old Trafford this season.

The hierarchy recently made the decision to sack Michael Carrick and lure Pochettino from the USA national side in the wake of Sunday's draw against Aston Villa that left United 18th in the Premier League.

That may be one of Wilcox's last acts, though, with Pochettino wanting further changes behind the scenes after agreeing a two-and-a-half-year contract.

If Pochettino is hoping for huge investment during the January window, and that's what he's been promised, he might be utterly disappointed.

Football Insider understands that if there are to be additions, the club will focus on strengthening the defensive third, rather than anywhere else.

Bruno Fernandes turning down a new contract has certainly thrown a spanner in the works, and replacing him will be incredibly difficult.

The Glazers and INEOS are not expected to stick their hands in their pockets, but either way, that will cause outrage amongst the fans.

Changes behind the scenes might be able to keep some fans onside, but keeping Pochettino happy will be much more important.

Only time will tell if sacking Carrick for Pochettino was the correct decision, but something had to change with Man United sitting in 18th.

Mick Brown, a former coach at Old Trafford, has already raised concerns over the move, suggesting that people are forgetting about some of his spells.

Undoubtedly, Pochettino achieved more than expected during his time at Tottenham, but PSG and Chelsea were completely different stories.

Fans will be hoping for an immediate bounce-back against Liverpool after the international break, but improvements might come much slower than that.
