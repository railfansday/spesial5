Ruben Dias is surely finished, rotten stats expose his downfall

Ruben Dias is having a poor season, with the Manchester City defender's woes continuing away on international duty.

Although the Portuguese defender has started all 10 Premier League games, his performances have left a lot to be desired.

Manchester City have lost their last three Premier League games, conceding six goals over that period.

Dias has been a mainstay in those losses, playing 90 minutes across all three.

Dias' struggles have also followed him onto international duty, raising further questions about his current form and confidence.

Enzo Maresca is likely to hope this is a rough patch, and not the start of a continuing trend.

Dias' defensive statistics are making for a worrying read, and his displays are becoming hard to ignore.

Once the Premier League's player of the year, his standards have dropped quite a bit this campaign.

His inability to win duels and tendency to concede unnecessary fouls are now costing both club and country.

As per FotMob, Dias' incredibly poor duel rate is clear, with him often coming second best.

Over the 10 league games this season, he is in just the 14th percentile for duels won percentage with 46.4 per cent.

Additionally, he has been sloppy when defending, giving away countless fouls, and ranking in the 2nd percentile for fouls committed with 14.

Those same struggles were then evident as Portugal took on Denmark in a 4-1 loss, with Dias giving away numerous fouls, four in total - the highest individual rate of the game.

Within those, the centre-back conceded the penalty that allowed the Danes to take the lead.

In terms of duels, he won 0 per cent of his ground duels, proving that his performances this season are becoming a consistent pattern.

While having a mixed performance in the air, with two out of four aerial duels won.

If Dias' form continues to go down this negative trajectory, Man City manager Maresca will have to change things up.

Defensive partner Marc Guehi has also had his troubles, leaving the possibility of Abdukodir Khusanov or even Josko Gvardiol as options in the middle of defence.

This season, Khusanov has been utilised as a versatile option, with games at centre-back as well as at right-back.

Gvardiol has not featured at centre-back since the 3-0 defeat in the Community Shield to Arsenal.

Maresca may have little choice but to consider alternative defensive partnerships if Dias and Guehi continue to struggle for consistency.
