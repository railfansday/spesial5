A Reds statement read: “We know supporters will want reassurance as we head into a busy and important period on and off the pitch, so we want to be clear about how the club will be run in the interim, and what happens next.

"Bobby Hassell will take charge of the footballing side of the club on an interim basis.

A former Reds captain who made almost 300 appearances for the club, Bobby knows Barnsley Football Club inside out. He will work closely with head coach Daniel Stendel and his staff, and will lead our preparations for the January transfer window alongside the wider recruitment team.

"Commercial operations and governance will be overseen by the club's existing senior staff.

"The process to appoint a new Chief Executive Officer is well underway with a leading recruitment firm, and has attracted strong interest. We will update supporters as soon as we are in a position to do so.

"Nothing changes in the day-to-day running of the club. The focus now is firmly on what matters most: the games ahead, starting with Sheffield Wednesday at Oakwell this Friday night, live on Sky Sports, and our preparations for the January window.

Meanwhile, chairman Neerav Parekh has confirmed that head coach Stendel has the club's ‘full support’ in his quest to turn around the club’s alarming first third of the 2026-27 campaign.

The second-from-bottom Reds, kept off the bottom of the League One table on goal difference alone, are winless in nine League One matches.

Parekh said: "I want to reassure our supporters that the club is in good hands in the interim. Bobby has lived and breathed this club as a player and a member of staff, and he will lead our football operation alongside Daniel and his staff through the January window.

"He performed a similar role during the January 2024 window, and has a strong working relationship with Daniel.

"I would also like to reiterate that Daniel and his staff have our full support. Stability matters, and continuous change will not build strong foundations.

"The process to appoint a new chief executive is well underway, and we will ensure we get the right person. In the meantime, our focus is where it should be: on the pitch and on our preparations for January.

"At the end of the day, we all care about this club and want it to succeed, and that can only happen if we come together as a collective."
