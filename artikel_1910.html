“Superb” – Parker’s delight over reversing Nuno’s biggest mistake | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

24 hours on from West Ham’s  tepid point against the bottom side in the league and whilst the justified criticism is flying around, there are definitely positives to pull out of a fixture in which West Ham dominated all the important statistics save the most important one: The points tally at the final whistle.

Sadly, again the Hammers have lost ground on the leaders and now sit eight points adrift. And yet, from Scott Parker’s comments, there are definite signs that he’s learned from at least some of the mistakes made by his predecessor. Reversing Nuno’s decision to cast out Edson Alvarez from the senior team proved a massive success.. Who knew.

Yesterday’s re-integration of ‘El Machin’ into the starting line up was his first league appearance in a West Ham shirt since May 2025.

This was an Alvarez playing more like his first season as a Hammer.

As Scott Parker summed up, Alvarez did not let him down:

“He brought every bit of what we needed. It’s a young team, and his experience today in terms of organising and his general presence on the pitch was very, very good for us for large aspects.

“It was a big ask and I knew that going into the game, he hasn’t played at all this year and probably hasn’t had a lot of training in and around the first team. But I just felt his experience and know-how around it was needed, and for sure he was a massive part today of why we performed the way we performed” (Justin Allen- YouTube)

The Mexican clearly cares: He was in tears before kick off, so tough has his time in the football wilderness been.

Having been such a critic of him during his card-strewn Lopetegui era, credit where it is due. Edson Alvarez was, indeed, excellent yesterday and it is a testimony to his professionalism that he played a full ninety after not playing competitive football since August.

If he can maintain that kind of focus and form, Parker may well have found the missing piece in West Ham’s defensive jigsaw puzzle, that proved beyond his predecessor who simply wouldn’t extend the olive branch to any of his ‘frozen out’ players.
