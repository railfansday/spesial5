Leeds United player ratings: ‘Chief tormentor’, ‘feisty with flair’ - Trio of 9/10s in win over Chelsea

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Leeds United’s players rated following their Premier League clash with Chelsea

Leeds United made it back-to-back Premier League wins as they exacted revenge on old rivals Chelsea.

Daniel Farke’s side were beaten 6-3 by the Blues in the Carabao Cup in September, but it was a different story as the sides met for a league battle at Stamford Bridge.

After Emmanuel Emegha fired the Blues ahead, Leeds roared back with Noah Okafor goals either side of a Dominic Calvert-Lewin penalty.

Misery was piled upon Chelsea in the dying embers, when substitute Brenden Aaronson weighed into wrap up a remarkable 4-1 win for the visitors.
