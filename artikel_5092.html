Aave [AAVE] V4 deposits have reached a new record high of $1.75B, an all-time high for this version of the DeFi lending protocol. V3 still has around $31B in deposits, but the V4 scaling has been substantial thus far.

Meanwhile, the protocol’s token prices have plummeted below key support zones following the market-wide selloff in recent days.

In the past 24 hours, AAVE has bounced by 3.6%, while Bitcoin [BTC] was only at breakeven. The Monday New York session would likely set the tone for the rest of the week’s price action.

AMBCrypto reported that Aave’s V4 platform was making new highs in active loans, even amidst the DeFi liquidity crunch. Aave’s move to add support for using Bitcoin as collateral offered a new source of liquidity, but also faced a risk from increased speculative activity.

The retracement in October and the consolidation within a range in early November were part of a bullish price structure on the daily timeframe. The $113-$123 zone had been identified as a bullish order block and a key demand zone for AAVE.

A daily session close below this area, as well as the swing low at $113.19, confirmed a bearish structure shift. Moreover, the CMF was below -0.05 in recent weeks to show sizeable capital outflow.

The 4-hour chart was also firmly bullish. The technical indicators were neutral, but the price structure was bearish. The short-term range (purple) was lost, and a bounce to $115 was swiftly halted.

The $111-$115 zone was the short-term supply zone to watch. Traders can look for a chance to sell into a bounce here.

In the short term, the sentiment was firmly bearish. The Open Interest was up 3.76% in 24 hours, following the 3.5% price bounce. Yet, the funding rate was firmly negative, and the spot CVD showed steady selling.

The negative funding rates do not guarantee a short squeeze, but traders need to be wary of a price bounce to hunt down overleveraged short positions.
