Simple: Scott Parker’s secret weapon in West Ham reboot

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

West Ham return to action tomorrow with a home tie against visitors Preston North End – a team who sit rock bottom of the Championship but are not without their recent triumphs after turning over promotion-chasing Southampton just before the break. So West Ham need to be wary, despite their opponents’ lowly league table position.

With Scott Parker having enjoyed an unbroken two week training period during the break, West Ham fans will now surely get to see the ‘Parker XI’ rather than Nuno’s inherited lin-up with which he began the first two games of his West Ham tenure.

The first visible difference will surely be to see -shock, horror – players used in their correct positions: Nuno because infamous for using square pegs in round holes – Morato at left back being the case in point.

Morata’s days at left back are surely over

Despite being signed as a centre back and having played all but 33 of his last 177 games as such, Nuno insisted on using the 6’3 Brazilian – hardily the most mobile – as a left back who was frequently found to be out of position, often allowing wingers to get the better of him: Scott Parker hooked him at half time after a pretty inept left-back performance against Cardiff.

With centre back Maximilian Kilman suspended for tomorrow’s clash there should be no more Morato at left back – incredibly West Ham fans have never seen the loanee in his preferred specialist position until now. Ezra Mayers has already done enough to start at left back leaving Parker free to slot Morato into the role for which he was signed, alongside Mavropanos.

Square pegs into square holes: Scott Parker’s secret weapon in his West Ham reboot. Who’d have thought.
