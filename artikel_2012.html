Watch: Pulisic and Rabiot score in quick succession to put Milan 2-0 up vs. Inter

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

AC Milan drew first blood in the Derby della Madonnina, and it was the man who has just renewed who dealt the opening blow.

Inside four minutes, Milan took the lead and it was the derby talisman Christian Pulisic who struck once again, just 24 hours after his contract extension was announced.

It was quite a simple move down the right side in the end, with Diego Moreira checking back inside onto his left foot and whipped in a simply perfect cross for Pulisic to nod in from a few yards out.

Then, just a few minutes later, Milan doubled their advantage from a set-piece. A low delivery from Moreira found the run of Rabiot towards the near post, who somehow managed to divert it in via the far post to make it 2-0.
