If the ball falls to a Newcastle United player in the penalty area, who do you want that player to be?

Harvey Barnes is such a natural finisher, one of the very best.

His strike against Arsenal on Saturday night was his 100th career goal.

However, when you drill down, his Premier League direct goal contributions (scoring and assisting) are even better than that bare stat of a century of career goals.

Down below I have detailed the Harvey Barnes Premier League record these last seven and a bit seasons.

From the start of the 2019/20 Premier League season onwards, the first four for Leicester and then three and a bit for NUFC.

My only surprise is how surprised so many people are, at just how good and productive Harvey Barnes has been across so many seasons.

This season for Newcastle United, Harvey Barnes has scored or assisted a third (five) of United’s 15 Premier League goals. Whilst if you add his two Carabao Cup goals, that makes it seven direct goal involvements already in all competitions this season, scoring five and two assists.
