Enzo Maresca is holding Jeremy Doku back at Man City, new details actually show why

Enzo Maresca looks to be Jeremy Doku's biggest problem as he shines away from Man City for Belgium.

Doku has struggled for form and fitness so far this term since Maresca's arrival at Man City.

The Belgian winger enjoyed the best season of his career up to now in 2025-26, with 10 goals and 11 assists in 50 club appearances.

He has long threatened to become one of the best forwards in the world, but this campaign is imperative for him to show that he can impress over an extended period of time.

His latest exploits on the international stage prove that Maresca may well be the main issue stopping him from reaching his full potential with the Citizens.

Doku demolished Turkiye in the Nations League on Thursday with a complete attacking performance that has not yet been seen this season with his club.

Injury issues have restricted Doku's availability this season, but even when fit, he is yet to start three consecutive matches for the Citizens under Maresca.

Just one goal and assist each from 10 appearances so far is a poor return, but maybe the 24-year-old is not the main issue behind that lack of output.

He took to the international stage with real determination on Thursday, putting in a man-of-the-match performance in Belgium's 2-1 Nations League win in Izmir.

Doku did superbly well to beat a man and cross for ex-City teammate Kevin De Bruyne to level the score in the 27th minute, and ended the game with the most dribbles (7) and most touches in the opposition box (7) of any player.

The Citizens star was also fouled more times (6) than any of his teammates, and he won the most duels (13) of any player too.

It was a superb performance which showed just how lethal he can be in the final third and how impactful he can be across the pitch, so now the onus should be on Maresca to coax that out of him at club level like Mark van Bommel has internationally.

Most of the criticism of the Belgian playmaker in recent years has been aimed at his lack of end product, with just 23 goals to his name in 141 appearances for City since 2023.

They will need him more than ever after the international break following a run of three straight league defeats, and Doku has previously admitted that he knows where his faults lie.

Speaking in April, he said: "I have to be more in the areas where you can score easy goals, tap-ins and stuff like that. Sometimes in the game...you don't realise that you're not in that position where you should be to just score the easy goals. That makes a big difference.

"Obviously if I score goals, this is a different conversation. A winger needs to score. If I have those goals, then I believe that I can (be one of the best wingers in the world) for sure, 100 per cent."

Doku can clearly be proficient in attack, with 10 goals last term, but maybe Maresca needs to tweak things slightly to get the best out of his star man.
