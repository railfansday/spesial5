EXCLUSIVE: RB Leipzig not looking to sell Arsenal target Antonio Nusa this winter, could demand more than €80m in the future

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Antonio Nusa has been linked with a move to Arsenal, with reports suggesting the Gunners could make a move for him in January. However, according to our information, RB Leipzig have no intention of letting the 21-year-old leave during the winter transfer window. Meanwhile, the Bundesliga outfit could demand a fee in excess of the reported €80 million to part ways with the Norwegian international in the future.
