Real Madrid just received a crazy offer for Endrick!

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The winter transfer window should be rather calm on the Castellana side. After a somewhat sluggish start to the season, which earned early criticism from Mourinho and a good number of players in the squad, the Merengues found their cruising speed and caught up with Barça in La Liga, in addition to achieving a perfect run in the Champions League. Everything seems to be going well, and, a priori, no need to strengthen this winter.

The Merengue leaders are not very fond of winter recruitment in any case. On the other hand, in terms of departures, there could be movement, with players who could leave on loan, like the young Thiago Pitarch, barred by competition in midfield. But if there’s one name that’s going to be talked about in January, it’s Endrick. It is difficult, at present, to know the player’s plans. What is certain is that he does not seem to totally matter to José Mourinho, who has only started him once this season (for 8 TCC appearances in total).

A difficult situation for a player who needs playing time to continue his progress. Especially since there is no shortage of suitors, with a good number of Premier League and Serie A clubs dreaming of welcoming him. It is AC Milan which seems the hottest on the file, and the Cadena SER has just confirmed the interest of the Lombards in the 20-year-old striker. Better, the media indicates that an offer of 70 million euros has already been made, and that it has therefore already arrived on the desk of the management of Real Madrid. AC Milan have even already spoken with Endrick to express their interest.

For the moment, José Mourinho does not wish to part ways with the player. For his part, Endrick is not necessarily leaning towards leaving but the former Palmeiras’ entourage could push him to pack his bags. In the event that a transfer is not possible this winter, the Milanese team has already planned to go on the attack again this summer. A sign that Ruben Amorim and the Milanese decision-makers are really counting on him. On the Merengue side, we would even make a small added value on the player, who had cost 35 million euros fixed + 25 million euros in the form of bonuses which have not yet been reached.
