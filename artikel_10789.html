EA FC 27 adds AI-generated commentary from iconic commentator

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

EA FC will be adding AI-generated commentary from iconic football commentator John Motson, after gaining permission from his family.

If you’re an EA FC (FIFA) player of a certain generation, then you grew up with Martin Tyler and Andy Gray providing the soundtrack to your games. In more recent years, that has shifted with Derek Rae, Stewart Robson, Guy Mowbray, and Sue Smith commentating on games.

However, before all that, the BBC’s John Motson actually commentated on games for players. ‘Motty’, who is synonymous with Match of the Day, was the voice of FIFA games from 1996 to 2006, before being replaced by Clive Tyldesley.

Now, the late commentator is set to return in EA FC 26, with AI-generated commentary lines coming from tools that have been “trained” on some of his iconic lines.

“My father adored being the voice of the game, and we are so grateful to the team at EA SPORTS FC for this touching tribute and preserving his memory in-game,” Fred Motson said.

“His voice inspired millions of players across generations every time they picked up a controller, and now a whole new set of fans will get to experience his legacy and commentary.”

EA SPORTS called it a “digital tribute” to Motson, with the option to start hearing the new commentary lines coming in the November 26 update.

As per the BBC, it is the first time that EA’s iconic series has included commentary lines from a deceased commentator.

“It’s not there to replace commentators in any way shape or form… there’s certainly no future plans to replace anyone with AI,” EA SPORTS’ Mat Prior also told them.
