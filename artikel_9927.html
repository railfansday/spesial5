As AWS re:Invent is around the corner, we’re proud to recognize a new group of AWS Heroes. Every Hero finds their own way to give back, whether through sharing what they know, bringing people together, or building tools that help others succeed. What they have in common is a genuine commitment to supporting the builders around them learn, grow, and lead.

Serverless Hero Andres Moreno leads the Cloud del Norte AWS User Group and is a former AWS Community Builder. On his blog, And More Dev, and at conferences and community events, he explains serverless, cloud native architectures, generative AI, and agentic systems in ways that make new technology easier to try. Andres puts special energy into supporting builders across Latin America and helping them explore what’s possible on AWS.

Community Hero Geethika Guruge is a Principal Consultant at Mantel Group whose work spans serverless, DevOps, cloud infrastructure, and agentic AI. Geethika has shared real lessons from building agentic applications and leading large migration and modernization programs at AWS re:Invent, AWS Summits, and AWS Community Days. At home in Auckland, Geethika organizes local AWS meetups that encourage grassroots innovation and mentors the next generation of cloud architects and leaders.

Data Hero Jéssica Cristina Coelho is a Database Reliability Engineer and a former AWS Community Builder with more than 15 years of experience in databases, cloud, and data engineering. She leads the AWS User Group in Minas Gerais, hosts DataTalks Brasil, teaches MBA courses, and supports Women in Cloud, DevOpsDays, and AWS Community Day events. She speaks at events such as AWS Summit São Paulo and Web Summit Rio on database modernization and generative AI. At AWS re:Invent 2025, she was recognized as a LATAM Diversity & Inclusion Champion.

DevTools Hero Kazuho Cryer Shinozuka is an AWS CDK Top Contributor and Trusted CDK Community Reviewer. With 200 merged pull requests, he ranks 18th among more than 1,700 contributors. He has reviewed more than 120 pull requests, has been involved in about 2% of all CDK commits, and has published 10 construct libraries, along with tools such as cfn idle cost. Through talks at JAWS DAYS and CDK Conference Japan and through hands on contribution workshops, he helps more developers in Japan start contributing to open source.

To connect with these Heroes or find one in your region, explore the AWS Heroes webpage, and join the conversation on AWS Builder Center.
