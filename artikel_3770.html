World of Warcraft: Forever has a lot of changes from the classic version of the game, but one of the chief ones is that its items are different—and while I'm only just now starting to see actual, potential game-changing bits of kit (I've gotten into trinket-juggling between pulls), there have been some pretty flavourful pieces. Like Rockslicer, an axe that lets you mine ore.

In a recent episode of the official WoW: Forever podcast, senior game designer Aidan Moon spoke a little on his design philosophies, and items that players are yet to see: "There's a lot that they have not seen that I'm very happy with and I think is a great direction for the game."

Moon's entire philosophy seems built around flavour over form, using the example of the Ogre Loincloth, a pair of cloth casting pants with strength, as a north star of sorts: "I just want to do something different that adds texture and crunch to the game. People will see things like the Ogre Loincloth, and they'll be like: 'Why does this cloth thing have strength on it?'

"That's because, when we say we're a fantasy-forward game, that means that before optimisation, before it being good, it needs to be flavourful, it needs to feel like it belongs in the world and that the stats are essentially a storytelling mechanism for items."

Ultimately, Moon thinks that interesting items that are memorable are far better for the game than a purely optimal numbers upgrade: "I would rather there be an Ogre Loincloth in the game that people talk about and get upset over, than just another strength belt that people go 'oh, that's just the mail strength belt in the dungeon'."

As for what we're yet to get to, Moon, laughing, remarks: "I've done some stupid stuff.

"There may be an item that is master looter BiS—I'll just put it that way … Maybe you get extra stats if your bags are completely full." Finally. An item for people who are allergic to inventory management! Me. I'm talking about myself. My bags are so full please send help.
