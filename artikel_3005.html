Kalshi to Offer Stock Trading After Buying Brokerage

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Kalshi Inc. is preparing to offer stock trading on its app after acquiring a brokerage firm called FB Securities. The company is trying to expand beyond just prediction markets. It will also offer exchange-traded funds. This story was first reported by the Wall Street Journal on Monday. (Source: Bloomberg)
