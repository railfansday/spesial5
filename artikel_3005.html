Nvidia Board Faces a Governance Reckoning

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

On Bloomberg This Weekend, Bloomberg opinion columnist Gautam Mukunda tells hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini that Jensen Huang’s departure from Nvidia has exposed broader corporate governance problems, as the board faces outside pressure to bring him back while investigations and potential shareholder litigation loom. Mukunda argues that Silicon Valley’s tendency to concentrate power around founder CEOs can weaken oversight, while Huang’s exit also complicates the debate over whether the US should allow advanced Nvidia chips to be sold to China.  Nvidia has publicly said its board does not intend to reinstate Huang as CEO. (Source: Bloomberg)
