FINAL: Boston College Takes Down Syracuse 27-15

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Boston College leaned on its running game and a bend-but-don't-break defense to beat Syracuse 27-15. Bo MacCormack supplied the explosive runs with 236 yards on 30 carries, while the Eagles repeatedly turned Syracuse big-play scoring opportunities into field goals, or nothing at all.

It was not always comfortable, but BC made the plays that mattered. MacCormack broke loose for a 56-yard touchdown in the third quarter, then delivered another long run to set up a crucial fourth-quarter score. On the other side, the Eagles surrendered too many big passing plays but repeatedly held their ground near the goal line. It's been a problem for this Syracuse team all season, and reared its ugly head again. Nice when it's not us!

BC could hardly have scripted a better opening possession. Mason McKenzie found Javarius Green for 22 yards, MacCormack ripped off a 47-yard run, and McKenzie capped the four-play, 81-yard drive with a six-yard touchdown pass to tight end Zeke Moore.

The defense had a solid start as well despite allowing a big play to Calvin Russell III. The freshman helped get them to the BC five but the defense held strong and forced a field goal.

That inability to finish drives became a recurring theme. Syracuse's receivers created problems downfield, and BC let the Orange escape several third-and-long situations. The Eagles had their own missed opportunities. A Syracuse targeting penalty extended a possession, but McKenzie missed an open Moore for a potential touchdown, leaving BC to settle for a field goal and a 10-3 lead. Syracuse then appeared to answer with a touchdown, only for the play to be wiped out, and the BC defense got the full stop on fourth down.

The Eagles finished the half with a well-executed late possession, setting up a field goal on the final play to take a 13-3 advantage into the locker room.

Syracuse chipped away with long third-quarter field goal, to cut the lead to 13-9. That came after quarterback Steve Angeli got knocked out of the game after a hit by Demetrius Ballard and Malachi Nelson took over

For a stretch, both teams struggled to generate much of anything. Stalled possessions and poor punting slowed the game down and kept things tight.

Then MacCormack broke it open. His 56-yard touchdown run gave BC a 20-9 temporary cushion. Syracuse responded with a long touchdown pass to Umari Hatcher. The superstar wideout had over 200 yards in the game. BC stopped the two-point attempt, keeping the margin at 20-15. Another big play to Hatcher put Syracuse in position to cut further into the lead, but a missed field goal now kept it at five. MacCormack promptly seized the moment, breaking another long run to the Syracuse 20 and setting up the touchdown that brought back BC's two-score advantage.

From there it was one more Eagles red-zone stop to finish the job. BC did not need a flawless afternoon to get a badly neede win. BC heads to Miami to close out the season, with the longest of long shots to get a bowl game (unless an academic 5 win bowl has already been achieved).
