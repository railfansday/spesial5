2026-27 NBA Minutes Report: How injuries to Austin Reaves, Alperen Sengun shake up two contenders

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Welcome to the NBA Minutes Report. Every week, I will review each team's updated minutes per game to see which players are seeing the court more or less than in previous weeks and what their impact has been. That can help you with any fantasy basketball decisions you may need to make, but also help you get a lay of the land on what's happening around the NBA and which players are gaining or losing value.

The charts below are also great for exploring on your own. You can track the minutes over the last three, five, ten games, and for the entire season, to see which trends stand out to you. All of this data was made accessible by Kyle Bland, who is incredibly talented and also extremely generous. Be sure to follow him to check out all of his baseball data as well.

The Hawks announced that CJ McCollum would miss at least a month with a calf injury. With Kingston Flemings also battling a back injury, Aaron Wiggins started on Wednesday and scored 14 points with three rebounds in 26 minutes. Lu Dort also played 31 minutes off the bench. We'd expect to see Flemings' minutes tick up once he's back, potentially later this week.

No injuries for the Celtics right now, so this is their rotation, with Mitchell Robinson still looking like a poor signing based on how they've been unable to figure out how to use him.

Egor Demin has returned to his larger minutes allotment now that he has been back for over a week. That, and the emergence of Nolan Traore, has led to fewer minutes for Keon Ellis and Mikel Brown Jr. There's just not a lot to get excited about here.

Kon Knueppel and Dennis Schroder remain sidelined, and Grayson Allen missed Tuesday's game with concussion-like symptoms. Those injuries are tricky because you never know how long they'll linger. Coby White was back and stepped into a larger role on Tuesday, playing 26 minutes while scoring 19 points with four assists, three rebounds, and three steals. Grant Williams also started and played 30 minutes in that one, but the Hornets went big with Williams, Moussa Diabate, Naz Reid, and Brandon Miller all in the starting lineup. I'd expected Williams to move back to the bench when Allen returns.

Caleb Wilson is going to miss up to six weeks with a fractured hand, which has been a real bummer for the Bulls. Buddy Hield has started to play more minutes off the bench, while Isaac Okoro has moved into the starting lineup. I'm surprised Jalen Smith continues to start and play more minutes than Nic Claxton, but maybe they are still ramping Claxton up. On Wednesday, Claxton played one more minute than Smith despite not starting the game.

Evan Mobley has missed a week with a big toe sprain and will likely be out until the end of next week. That has led to slightly more usage and production from Jarrett Allen, and also more minutes from Mario Hezonja, who started on Wednesday and played 28 minutes. That was also because Peyton Watson was out with a back injury in that game. Jaylon Tyson also missed Wednesday with an ankle injury, which led to more minutes for Meleek Thomas. Both he and Tyrese Proctor have impressed in their minutes this season.

Daniel Gafford started on Wednesday but played just 24 minutes, while P.J. Washington played 27 minutes off the bench. Santi Aldama had seemingly joined the rotation, but fell back out of it once Morez Johnson Jr. returned. Dusty May has inserted Caleb Martin into the starting lineup as a screener/passer alongside Cooper Flagg and Kyrie Irving, and while Martin himself isn't putting up big numbers, he has been a productive member of the lineup and should continue to see good minutes.

Denver is starting to find their groove, and their rotation has been pretty locked in. In a win over the Celtics on Wednesday, the five starters and DeMar DeRozan all played at least 25 minutes, and Spencer Jones was the only other player to see more than 11 minutes. We should expect that to continue.

The Pistons are still dealing with injuries to Jalen Duren and Duncan Robinson, who could both return sometime after Thanksgiving. On Wednesday, they were without Cade Cunningham (knee) and Taurean Prince (knee), but that likely had to do with their opponent being the Pelicans. Kevin Huerter has picked up more minutes as a floor-spacer with Robinson out, but Isaiah Joe is also back from injury and played 16 minutes on Wednesday, shooting 3-for-5 from beyond the arc. His minutes should continue to tick up.

The Warriors are adjusting to life after the injury to Stephen Curry, which I wrote about on the website last week. Brandon Williams also suffered a hamstring injury, but it has not been De'Anthony Melton who picked up the slack, as I expected. LJ Cryer has stepped into a bigger role and even started on Wednesday, although he played just 19 minutes. Gary Payton II returned and played 16 minutes while scoring eight points and grabbing eight rebounds, so he could be the one playing a larger role. Charles Bassey has also been playing more minutes of late and had 10 points and eight rebounds in 22 minutes on Tuesday.

Alperen Sengun suffered an eye injury on Wednesday and only played 14 minutes. It remains to be seen how long that will keep him out, but Steven Adams is back and played 16 minutes off the bench, so he and Tari Eason would likely fill whatever minutes they needed in Sengun were to miss time. Otherwise, Reed Shepard and Marcus Smart are the only two other bench players who are really seeing consistent minutes.

With Tyrese Haliburton (hamstring) and Andrew Nembhard missing Wednesday's game with a hip injury, we saw 23 minutes from Kelly Oubre Jr. He likely would have played more, but Houston was up 16 points at the half, so both teams went to their bench a lot in the second half. Aaron Nesmith continues to play a lot but has been inconsistent, and T.J. McConnell figures to see more action as long as Nembhard is out.

The Clippers lost to the Jazz on Tuesday, which should tell you everything about how their season is going. Brandon Ingram, Bradley Beal, and Jordan Miller all remain out, and so we're seeing the same mess of a deep rotation. Keaton Wagler scored 15 points in 18 minutes on Tuesday and probably needs more minutes in a lost season.

Austin Reaves has been working through a heel injury, which I covered in a video this week, but he seems likely to return over the weekend. That could push Matisse Thybulle to the bench and limit usage for Quentin Grimes, who has been really good for the Lakers lately and played 35 minutes on Wednesday. Collin Sexton played 20 minutes off the bench in that one, and Jake LaRavia also came off the bench with Ziarie Williams starting, even though LaRavia still played 30 minutes.

Memphis just won their 11th game with a victory over the Cavs on Wednesday without Ty Jerome, who was dealing with a hip injury. Isaiah Stewart also missed the game with an illness, which allowed Olivier Maxence-Prosper to play 16 minutes. Cam Spencer also played 29 minutes without Jerome and scored 26 points with seven assists. He has been playing himself into a much larger role of late.

The Heat are starting to figure some things out, but this is really a three-man team with Giannis Antetokounmpo, Bam Adebayo, and Davion Mitchell, who has been unreal this season, as I covered this week. They will consistently play 30+ minutes, and then the rest of the rotation will be mixed in behind them.

Ryan Rollins has been out with an illness, so Kasparas Jakučionis has picked up extra minutes off the bench and scored 20 points in 26 minutes on Wednesday. Gary Trent Jr. has also entered the rotation, which is nice with all that money they're paying him, while Kel'el Ware can't get consistent run and played just 17 minutes on Wednesday while Jericho Sims started at center and played 18 minutes. OK.

The Timberwolves are playing a very tight rotation without Anthony Edwards, Jonathan Kuminga, and Donte DiVincenzo. On Tuesday, only nine players saw the court, and just seven of them played more than 17 minutes, with Bones Hyland and Cody Williams being the other two. Kuminga shouldn't be out long, which will likely push Williams fully out of the rotation, so you know what you're getting here.

Dejounte Murray has demanded a trade and won't play until that demand is satisfied, which I covered in a video this week. This will likely now be Jeremiah Fears' team, alongside Zion Williamson, of course. Bennedict Mathurin has battled a thumb sprain and also a foot injury, and Trey Murphy III missed Wednesday's game with a knee injury that has kept him sidelined. Jordan Poole is now back and in the mix, playing 30 minutes on Wednesday, so he'll continue to operate as an inconsistent volume shooter off the bench.

Mikal Bridges has started to pick up his production a bit, but Josh Hart is struggling to get his shots to fall, which I covered in a video this week. Landry Shamet has continued to play a big role off the bench, but Mike Brown is playing the hot hand with Miles McBride, Tyler Kolek, and Jose Alvarado as the backup PG, which has made their minutes a muddled mess.

The good news is that Ajay Mitchell returned from his sprained ankle without missing much time, but the Thunder lost Alex Caruso to a hip injury in Wednesday's loss to the Hawks after just nine minutes. That could lead to more minutes for Bennett Stirtz or Nique Clifford. Aday Mara has been starting alongside Isaiah Hartenstein and continues to deliver in his rookie season.

Jalen Suggs returned and came off the bench briefly before returning to the starting lineup. He played 31 minutes on Wednesday and should be climbing back to his normal workload. That will mean fewer minutes for Jevon Carter and Jase Richardson, who didn't play at all on Wednesday. This team is going to rely heavily on his starting lineup for now, with Tristan Da Silva and Goga Bitadze being the only consistent bench players.

The 76ers beat the Magic on Wednesday without Tyrese Maxey (foot) and Jaylen Brown (hamstring). Kentavious Caldwell-Pope started and played 32 minutes, with Anfernee Simons also starting and playing 38 minutes. Both of those guys will see their minutes and usage increase as long as Maxey and Brown are out, which might not even be another game.

I keep asking the Suns to play Collin Gillespie more, but he had just 17 minutes on Wednesday. Right now, Devin Booker, Jalen Green, Dillon Brooks, and Miles Bridges seem locked into 28+ minutes a night, and then the rest of the rotation shifts around them. Oso Ighodaro has been playing more minutes as the starting center, and Luke Kennard has now muddled the wing minutes off the bench.

Deni Avdija is battling a foot injury, and the Trail Blazers are still limiting minutes for Robert Williams III. With Avdija out, Jayson Kent has stepped into the starting lineup and played 27 minutes in a loss to the Timberwolves on Tuesday. Jrue Holiday continues to play big minutes off the bench with Ja Maront (hamstring) out, while Scoot Henderson is the only other consistent bench player.

The Kings are the worst team in basketball, so there's not a lot to say here. Precious Achiuwa has seemingly fallen out of the rotation and played eight minutes on Wednesday. Yet, Dylan Cardwell played only 12. Who knows what's going on here.

The Spurs lost to the Pistons on Tuesday and also lost Dylan Harper to a hip injury. As of now, he is out on Thursday, but he might miss more time after that as well. He was hurt in the final minute on Tuesday, so we didn't see how the Spurs would respond, but it would likely be Julian Champagnie moving back into the starting lineup, and Keldon Johnson getting more minutes off the bench.

Kawhi Leonard (knee) has started strength training, but remains out; RJ Barrett is dealing with a hamstring injury, and Scott Barnes is on an injury management plan after his groin injury, so he played 34 minutes against the Knicks on Tuesday and then sat out Wednesday's loss to the Wizards. We'd have to assume Barnes could be back to his normal allotment of playing time next week, but until then, Jamison Battle will continue to see more minutes (he played 33 on Wednesday), and Allen Graves will also play a bigger role off the bench.

The Jazz just won their third game, which is an expectedly low number considering they're playing without Lauri Markkanen, Jaren Jackson Jr. and Jusuf Nurkic. Jaxson Hayes also missed the last two games with a knee injury but returned against the Lakers. His minutes will probably tick back up, which could mean fewer minutes for Mo Bamba, who played 31 minutes against the Clippers and had a good game, scoring 15 points with 10 rebounds. He added 16 rebounds in 23 minutes against the Lakers, so he will remain in the rotation while he is producing.

With Justin Champagnie sidelined, the Wizards are playing only two players consistently off the bench: Khris Middleton and Bilal Coulibaly. Tre Johnson played just eight minutes in Wednesday's win, and did you know Deandre Ayton was on the Wizards?
