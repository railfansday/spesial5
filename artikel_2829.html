Greg Newsome, Giants agree on three-year extension

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Cornerback Greg Newsome signed a one-year deal with the Giants as a free agent this offseason, but his stay with the team won’t be done once the 2026 season comes to an end.

Adam Schefter of ESPN reports that Newsome has agreed to a three-year extension with the NFC East club. The extension is worth $46 million with $32 million in guaranteed money.
