Joel Embiid listed as doubtful for 76ers' NBA Cup matchup vs. Miami Heat on Wednesday due to illness

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Joel Embiid is listed as doubtful due to illness for Wednesday's NBA Cup matchup between the Philadelphia 76ers and Miami Heat.

Embiid also missed Monday's 128-111 loss to the Portland Trail Blazers. The Sixers center will have missed five of the team's 18 games this season.

Prior to Monday, he had appeared in Philadelphia's past three games and played arguably his best basketball of the season, averaging 30 points. That stretch includes Saturday's 132-129 win over the Milwaukee Bucks during which he nearly notched a triple-double with 26 points, 17 rebounds and 9 assists.

Embiid was involved in a crucial play in the closing seconds, taking an inbounds pass from LeBron James and stepping out of bounds at the baseline. However, the officials failed to notice Embiid's left foot going out and the Bucks were out of replay challenges to contest the call.

Embiid is the 76ers' third-leading scorer at 20.4 points per game while leading the team with 8.9 rebounds. His 4.1 assists rank fourth among Sixers players.

The 76ers currently have the top record in the Eastern Conference at 14-4 and are tied for second in the NBA behind the 15-3 San Antonio Spurs and Los Angeles Lakers. Philadelphia has won seven of its past 10 games.

Following Wednesday's contest, the Sixers have back-to-back games on the road, visiting the Indiana Pacers (6-12) on Friday and facing the Houston Rockets (13-5) on Saturday.

Embiid seems likely to miss at least one of those games, if not for illness than perhaps the right foot plantar fascia management that has sidelined him for a game when the Sixers play a back-to-back.
