Cowboys add LB Jaishawn Barham to injury report, list him as questionable

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Cowboys added linebacker Jaishawn Barham to the injury report on Friday with hip and groin injuries. He is listed as questionable for Sunday’s game against the Titans.

The team ruled out cornerback DaRon Bland (ankle), right guard Tyler Booker (ankle), tight end Jake Ferguson (knee/ankle), safety P.J. Locke III (concussion) and wide receiver Jonathan Mingo (ankle).

Cornerback Devin Moore (hip), tight end Luke Schoonmaker (illness) and safety Jalen Thompson (hamstring) are questionable. Moore and Thompson remain in their 21-day return-to-practice windows.

Cornerback Joey Porter Jr. (ankle/foot) was a full participant all week and has no status.

Outside linebacker Donovan Ezeiruaku (ankle) and cornerback Shavon Revel Jr. (illness) returned to full participation on Friday. Ezeiruaku was limited Thursday and Revel missed practice.
