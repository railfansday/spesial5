Raiders add quarterback to program

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Las Vegas Raiders have been expected to add to their quarterback room and Monday they did.

Las Vegas announced they have signed veteran quarterback Brett Rypien to the practice squad. He joins new starter Fernando Mendoza and backup Aidan O'Connell. The need for another quarterback came last Tuesday when the Raiders traded then starter Kirk Cousins to the Minnesota Vikings for a third-round draft pick.

Rypien, 30, entered the NFL in 2019 and this is the 12th NFL program (he was with Minnesota twice) he has been part of. Rypien played in four games for the Denver Broncos when Raiders' head coach Klint Kubiak was their passing game coordinator and quarterbacks coach in 2022.

Rypien last threw a pass in a regular-season game in 2023.

To make room for Rypien on the practice squad, defensive end Bralen Trice was released. He spent less than three weeks in the Raiders' program.
