3 things we learned about the Cowboys from the 49ers game

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Dallas Cowboys lost 46-40 to the San Francisco 49ers on Sunday. Here's what we learned.

Over the last three weeks coming into this game, the Cowboys pass rush has picked up the pace, racking up nine sacks over that time. The trend continued this week with Cowboys exploding for five sacks against a stout 49ers offensive line that's only allowed eight on the season. Four of those sacks came in the first half.

The defense continued to apply pressure on Brock Purdy throughout the game, especially from the defensive interior. Unfortunately, at times Brock Purdy was able to clear the rush and make great plays downfield to Christian McCaffrey, George Kittle and others. Still, the effort from the pass rush was a bright spot for the fourth straight week and should continue to improve as the season continues.

Even with the sacks, however, Dallas was gashed on way too many big plays to be competitive for an entire game.

As well as the defense played in the first half (minus one big broken play), they fell apart in the second. Misdirection plays from an offense known for misdirection plays shocked absolutely nobody watching, yet Dallas bit hard time after time. A big end around touchdown from Deebo Samuel seemed to be the play that broke the Cowboys. After that point, the 49ers never looked back, scoring touchdowns on nearly every possession.

The defense couldn't muster much until halfway through the fourth quarter when they finally forced a 49ers punt before giving up the game sealing touchdown on the following 49ers drive.

The defense continues to prove to be one of the most untrustworthy units in professional football.

Dak Prescott and the offense put up 16 points in the first half behind an efficient rushing attack and a strong passing game. George Pickens grabbed two touchdowns in his return back to the field in the first half in a game that should have gone into the half tied if not for a missed Brandon Aubrey extra point. Pickens would go on to finish the day with three touchdowns in total.

Despite the success, however, Dallas would put up just nine points through the second and third quarters until they broke through with 21 points in the fourth. At one point, the 49ers went on a 28-3 scoring stretch. If the defense is going to disappear, the offense needs to stop the bleeding and somewhat keep pace. Fair or not, it's the position this team found themselves this week and oftentimes this season.

At the end of the day, however, any team that scores 40 points should easily win the game. We can talk about the 49ers defense playing a bit soft with a big lead, but that point total should always result in a win. For the 2026 Cowboys, however, losing with massive offensive numbers is extremely unsurprising.
