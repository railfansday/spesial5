AP Overreactions: The Chiefs assembled the greatest RB room ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

After the Kansas City Chiefs' win over the Atlanta Falcons in Week 10, we checked the comments on X to find the strongest reactions to the game. The question is: Are they overreactions or not?

I'm with you. I even tweeted that this was the most fun I've had watching a Chiefs game in a long time. We got vintage Patrick Mahomes in a 35-13 win over Atlanta, with 348 passing yards, five touchdowns and zero picks.

Okay, calm down with the "greatest ever" talk. I'll go this far, though. Give me Kenneth Walker III and Emmett Johnson over any running back duo in the NFL right now, especially after Johnson put up 171 yards from scrimmage and caught two touchdowns with Walker out.

Absolutely. When Josh Simmons is on the field, I think he's a top-10 left tackle in football. Kahlil Benson deserves credit for holding it down, but Simmons takes this offense to another level because of what he gives Mahomes in pass protection.

I get why the Chiefs eased Josh Downs in for his first game. He still caught four passes for 51 yards and a touchdown on just 10 offensive snaps. I'd expect that snap count to go up every week as he gets more comfortable in the offense.
