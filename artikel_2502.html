Orlando Pride appoint Yolanda Thomas as head coach

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Orlando Pride have named Yolanda Thomas as head coach. The former assistant coach served as interim head coach for the last two months of the 2026 season after Seb Hines was let go. Thomas is the first Black woman to be a head coach in National Women’s Soccer League history. The Pride and Thomas have agreed to a multi-year contract.

Thomas, a Swedish international, joined the Pride as an assistant for the 2023 season, winning the Shield and Championship with the team in 2024. Before joining the Pride, she had multiple front office and coaching positions for two Women’s Premier Soccer League sides, Oklahoma State University, the University of Tulsa, and Rogers State University. She led two United States Women’s under-23 National Team camps in 2025 and serves as a scout for U.S. Soccer.

Orlando finished in 11th place in the regular season standings this year. Thomas let the team to a 3W-4L-1D record as interim head coach, bumping them up one place in the standings.

“She is an exceptional coach who understands the importance of culture and what people need in different moments, and she has earned the trust and respect of our players and staff through her work over the past several years.” Caitlin Carducci, the Pride’s general manager, said of Thomas in a statement. “While her familiarity with our organization is a strength, what stood out throughout our global search process was her vision, her depth of experience and her ability to bring out the best in those around her. We are confident she can build on the foundation we’ve established and lead this club to continued success.”

“I am honored to lead a club that I care so deeply about and a group of players I believe in wholeheartedly. What we’ve built together over the last several seasons is just the beginning of something special. We have established a strong foundation and a championship standard, and I believe the potential of this club goes far beyond what we’ve already achieved,” Thomas said. “We see an opportunity for the Orlando Pride to become a global leader in women’s football, and I look forward to working with Caitlin and our entire program to help lead us there.”
