Picture this: You’re halfway through a 20-mile day when the sky opens up, dumping sheets of rain on you and your hiking partners. You pull out your shell, zip it up, and keep crushing. An hour later, you notice you’re starting to feel awfully damp. Your shell isn’t leaking, but it might as well be: Your exertion has turned it into a sweatbox.

The best rain jackets are next to useless if you end up sweating them out. If you’re the kind of hiker who always steams up your shell, picking one that has the breathability and venting options to keep you cool and dry is a must. We went through our testers’ favorite rain shells of 2026 and chose the best picks for hikers who always overheat.

First, understand you may have to compromise. While waterproof-breathable membranes are almost ubiquitous, most jackets prioritize waterproofing over breathability, or vice versa. Jackets that can weather a downpour without so much as wetting out may not breathe well, and those that breathe well may be better for scattered showers than the kind of rain that makes you start thinking about building an ark.

Pay attention not just to the breathability of the jacket itself, but to the venting options, too. Even the most breathable fabrics can’t dump heat as well as a set of pit zips or mesh-backed pockets that expose your underlayers to fresh air. Packability is more important than you might think, too: If you’re consistently hot, having a jacket that you can ball up and stick inside your pack without a second thought is a better move than carrying a heavy, bulky one.
