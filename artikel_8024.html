If you ran into me at any point over the last couple of months, I was probably wearing the same half-zip: the gray Smartwool Second Cut Fleece 1/2-Zip. Walking my dogs on a brisk morning? The Second Cut. Rushing from the climbing gym to my car? The Second Cut. Hiking with my one- and four-year-old in the mountains of Northern New Mexico? You guessed it: the Second Cut.

That’s because my Smartwool half-zip is perfect for just about every brisk-weather activity.

Made from Smartwool’s reclaimed Merino wool scraps blended with recycled polyester, with a 100-percent recycled polyester overlay, the entire top is sourced from rescued material. And, important for wearers like me who rarely take it off, the material offers that classic no-stink wool perk. After months of regular washes, I haven’t noticed any pilling, either.

Right now, my number-one fleece is on sale for 25 percent off at Backcountry, marking down the price from $160 to $120.

The high neck is long enough to protect against cold mountain gusts but not so high that it rubs my chin too much. It’s also extra wide, so it doesn’t feel constricting as some turtlenecks do.

In fact, the entire cut feels roomy in the best way. The Second Cut is large enough to fit more than just a base layer under it for those extra cold mornings. Plus, its boxy fit is Gen-Z cool without making this millennial feel like she’s trying too hard.

I own the charcoal color, which matches any pair of pants in my closet—jeans, colorful track pants, or even workout leggings. And it’s a good thing this fleece so versatile, because at this point, it’s basically attached to my body.

The Second Cut got me through the fall, and now, as snow starts to dust the peaks near my home, I know I’ll keep wearing it: cross-country skiing with my family, under a shell to skin up the local ski hill, and for every errand and outdoor mission in between.
