GM just made it way easier for EV drivers to charge at Electrify America stations

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Chevrolet, Cadillac, and GMC EV drivers enrolled in GM’s Energy Pass can now simply plug in to an Electrify America station to start charging. No apps or credit cards needed.

Public EV charging is getting easier and faster, with more DC fast-charging ports available and services like Plug & Charge that streamline payment.

The technology lets you start a charging session by simply plugging in the vehicle. The session is then billed through a linked payment method, so you don’t even need to swipe a credit card or use an app.

Eligible GM electric vehicle drivers enrolled in Energy Pass can now use the service at compatible Electrify America chargers.

The capability is now available for Chevrolet, Cadillac, and GMC EVs with a native CCS port (pre-2027 model year). Newer models, with a native NACS port, will gain the capability in early 2027, GM said.

“We’re making it easier for drivers to start a charging session and get back on the road, with an experience that has fewer steps than swiping a credit card to get gas.” Wade Sheffer, vice president of GM Energy, said.

Based on GM’s internal testing, the energy begins to flow within about 45 seconds of plugging the vehicle into the charger.

The charge session is then billed through your preferred payment method with Energy Pass. You can view session details and receipts in the myChevrolet, myGMC, or myCadillac app.

During the rollout, GM said drivers will still be able to use Charge by App and other charging methods.

First, you’ll need to enroll in GM’s Energy Pass in the myChevrolet, myGMC, or myCadillac app.

From there, you can add your preferred payment method, and the app will guide you through setting up Plug and Charge.

Once the profile is complete, you can plug your electric vehicle into a compatible Electrify America charger to begin the session. The vehicle and charger will automatically authenticate and begin the session.

The new capabilities come after GM integrated the Electrify Network into the myChevrolet, myGMC, and myCadillac apps in January, adding more than 6,200 fast-charger options, most of which can deliver 350 kW.

GM said EV drivers now have access to over 300,000 public chargers across the US and Canada.
