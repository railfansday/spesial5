BYD Offices Raided in Legal Probe Over Land Rover Defender Similarities

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The BYD Ti7 bears more than a passing resemblance to Land Rover’s current off-road champ, but is that enough to make a case in court?

American buyers may not be familiar with the BYD Ti 7, a Chinese-made three-row hybrid SUV with a boxy shape and an unusual square of painted surface floating above the beltline. They will, however, be familiar with the Land Rover Defender sold in the U.S., a three-row SUV with a boxy shape and an unusual square of painted surface floating above the beltline. And according to a report from British newspaper The Times, Land Rover may have noticed some similarities between the two SUVs.

Last August, according to The Times, a court-ordered raid of BYD’s Dutch offices occurred on behalf of Land Rover company JLR. This was, reportedly, to find documents surrounding how a Land Rover Defender was used in the Ti 7’s development—evidence that could be key in a potential legal case over similarities between the models.

The BYD Ti 7 starts at the equivalent of $27,000 below the MSRP of the most affordable three-row Defenders on the U.K market. It also gets a plug-in hybrid powertrain alongside its seven-seat layout, while Land Rover’s plug-in Defender is only available in England with six seats.

Notably, Land Rover has already been involved in one lawsuit over another automaker attempting to build a product that looks astonishingly similar to a Defender. That model, the Ineos Grenadier, was cleared for sale in 2020 despite looking, well, much like an original Defender.

In the case of the BYD Ti 7, the visual similarities with Land Rover’s larger Defenders are obvious. The question is how much the two SUVs actually have in common, and whether or not it is enough to build a winning legal argument. Top Gear reports that a Dutch court will decide whether a case will proceed later this week.
