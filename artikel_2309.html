Klopp admits he was “shocked” by Wirtz after seeing Liverpool star up close

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Germany head coach Jürgen Klopp has admitted he was “shocked” by Florian Wirtz's quality after watching the Liverpool star in person for the first time.

The 59-year-old spoke highly of Wirtz following Monday's 5-0 win over the Netherlands in the 2026/27 UEFA Nations League, in which the 23-year-old scored twice.

“I only knew him from watching him on television. I was shocked when I first saw him on the pitch. An extraordinary footballer,” Klopp said at the post-match press conference.

Wirtz has been in excellent form for Germany since Klopp took charge, scoring four goals in five UEFA Nations League appearances.

Klopp explained that finding the right position for the 23-year-old has helped bring out the best in him, while highlighting that his contribution goes beyond his technical ability on the ball.

“It helps that he is so vocal. He is the first player in the press and can press two players without getting tired,” Klopp added.
