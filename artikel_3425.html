First Look At New Batman Series Confirms It's Going To Be A Modern Classic

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Batman is easily one of the most popular characters and valuable properties in comic books. He’s beloved for his ability to bend but never break, and he does it in a city where it seems to have a constant dark, cursed cloud over it. What’s worse is that as time goes on, it seems like Gotham City isn't getting better. That's bad for the citizens of Gotham, but great for readers.

During Batman: Bad Seeds, the Dark Knight stopped the Parliament of Trees from taking over Gotham and eventually destroying all human life on Earth. It took a team-up with the help of Victor and Nora Fries to get the job done, but the day was saved.

This is only the most recent of the nearly apocalyptic events that Batman has stopped over the years. This particular one showed that Bruce would do whatever it took to save the day. Thanks to the combined might of the Absolute Martian Manhunter creative team, comic book fans will see how far Batman will go to protect the people of Gotham City.

The Eisner Award-winning creative team of Deniz Camp and Javier Rodriguez is bringing their talents to Gotham City to create a story that will rock DC Comics. The ongoing series is called Batman: Shadow of the Bat and, unlike Batman: Dark Patterns, will run with DC’s regular continuity. On X (commonly known as Twitter), Deniz Camp said, “When a neighborhood is drenched in the same chemical waste that created the Joker, Batman moves to contain the madness!”

One of the many things that people should be looking forward to in this series is Deniz Camp's storytelling. Anyone who's read Absolute Martian Manhunter knows that he (and Javier Rodriguez) know how to create chaos. All twelve issues showed the worst of the world. There was death, destruction, and, at times, the city was on fire. Sometimes the flames were literal. Now, imagine all of that, but in the hellscape that is Gotham City.

"Our ongoing, in-continuity series tries to do new stuff with one of our oldest icons!" -Deniz Camp

Camp says he’s treating this as if this is the first time readers have heard of Batman and as a "creator-owned project." This means readers will get a new perspective on the Dark Knight. Many have done this before, but this is different. Now Bruce Wayne is dead (this happens in Matt Fraction's Batman #15), leaving the Caped Crusader to work more freely. With Ace Chemicals being a problem, he’ll need independence.

As brutal as everything in Absolute Martian Manhunter was, Camp and Rodriguez had a way of ending issues or storylines with some hope. Sometimes it took a while for the sun to come out, but it always did. When it took a long time, Javier Rodriguez came to the rescue. His artwork and bright colors made a grim situation less bleak.

Deniz Camp has mastered telling stories where good people turn evil, and the bad ones get worse. He did this in The Ultimates and Assorted Crisis Events. With Gotham City being one of the darkest places in comic books, his style fits perfectly. Not just because of the darkness, but because he will bring sunshine to a city that not even the Devil wants any part of.

The Black Mercy strikes again as Batman's investigation of an alien crime exposes old wounds once more.

When this series eventually ends (and hopefully not for a long time), DC Comics fans will look back at this and call Batman: Shadow of the Bat one of the best. Considering how many classics are in the Dark Knight's lore, that says a lot.
