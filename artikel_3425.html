Netflix Rolls Out More Than An Hour of ‘KPop Demon Hunters’ Bonus Material, Including Playlists, Short-Form Stories and Games

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

KPop Demon Hunters Bonus Content features HUNTR/X dance rehearsals and kids shorts spread across 22 new episodes.

Netflix dropped a ton of bonus KPop Demon Hunters content on Monday (Nov. 2) as part of a roll-out of 22 new episodes inspired by the wildly popular, Oscar-winning animated film. The 78 minutes of fresh content ranges from stop-motion adventures to games, official dance and choreo videos and kids shorts. The new material includes KPop Demon Hunters PLAYlist, a 13-episode, 49-minute collection of shorts that kicks off with HUNTER/X Backstage Pass, which follows Rumi, Mira and Zoey as they battle with rival idols and demon janitors, confront writer’s block, fashion emergencies and face off against rivals the Saja Boys in a promo photo shoot face off. The bonus episodes also include stop-motion animation toy versions of the movie’s characters acting out some of the original film’s biggest moments and interactive games focused on the character’s backstage lives. There is also a nine-episode bundle of dance videos that run just under 30 minutes that includes animated Kpop girl group HUNTR/X going through a dance rehearsal for “How It’s Done” featuring choreo not seen in the movie, as well as a live-action dance performance video of the song. Fans can also watch full, live-action choreo videos for “Golden,” “Takedown,” “Soda Pop” and “Your Idol,” in which choreographers Leejung Lee, Nail and K-Tigers break down how and why Mira, Rumi and Zoey move the way they do.

The massively popular animated movie that launched on Netflix in 2025 has been streamed a record 325 million times, as well as screened in movie theaters across the world, with producers confirming that a sequel to the streamer’s most-watched title of all time is on the horizon. While no details are available now, directors Maggie Kang and Chris Applehans are set to return.

The original featured the eight-week Billboard Hot 100 chart-topper “Golden,” which won the best original song award at the 2026 Academy Awards, Critic’s Choice Awards, Grammys and the Golden Globes.
