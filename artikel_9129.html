Shahzia Sikander Confronts British Colonial Legacy in High Line Video Work

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

With work by Shahzia Sikander and Hylozoic/Desires, “Between Salt and Smoke” simultaneously probes and defies the violent and extractive legacies of British imperialism across Asia.

A new joint video exhibition has opened above 14th Street on the High Line, featuring three moving-image works that illustratively dissect and defy the economic legacies of the British Empire's grip on South Asia and China. Screening daily until January 11, Between Salt and Smoke presents two animated videos by Pakistani-American artist Shahzia Sikander and one documented performance by artists Himali Singh Soin and David Soin Tappeser, who make up the duo Hylozoic/Desires.

The artists examine British colonial extraction and violence in Asia through the lens of the East India Company's monopolies on salt, opium, and other highly valued goods. A new work by Sikander, “3 to 12 Nautical Miles” (2026), consists of hand-painted images that the artist digitally animated in a research-driven cinematic exploration of trade history between the declining Mughal Empire, the East India Company, and the Qing Dynasty.
