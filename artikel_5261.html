The SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust (SPY) fell 0.35% in premarket trading on Monday to $751.76, down $2.61 from its last close of $754.37, as AI-linked stocks stayed under pressure at the start of a busy week. AMD led the declines, dropping 4.64% to $509.26 before the open after already falling 8.75% to $534.06 in the previous session. Alphabet’s Class C shares were nearly flat at $292.39 following a 0.96% drop to $292.52. Investors are bracing for several major events. Nvidia reports third-quarter results on Tuesday, its first since Jensen Huang’s resignation, while the Federal Reserve releases minutes from its October meeting on Wednesday that could hint at a December rate hike. Anthropic is set to price its IPO on Wednesday and begin trading on Thursday, the same day Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin meet in Shenzhen. Concerns over Nvidia’s financing of customers and suppliers also weighed on chip and AI infrastructure stocks.
