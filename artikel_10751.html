Mysterious 3,300-year-old sword found at bottom of lake in “excellent” condition

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A 3,300-year-old sword was found at the bottom of a small lake in Italy, and it looks like it is ready to be used.

Over the years, plenty of pretty old items have washed up on shores around the world. However, some of the most unusual and sought-after discoveries are still hidden deep below the earth’s surface.

In Central Italy, experts have uncovered a sword from the Bronze Age that appears to be at least 3,300 years old. The sword had been submerged in Lake Mezzano, which can be found in the province of Viterbo and Southern Etruria, and was brought to shore in October.

“The artifact, preserved in excellent condition, still featuring a sharp edge, represents an important addition to our understanding of the communities that frequented the lake basin over three thousand years ago,” said Italy’s Superintendence of Archaeology, Fine Arts and Landscape for the Province of Viterbo and Southern Etruria.

They noted that it is the third sword found at similar sites since the 1970s, and that it is of “extraordinary interest” for experts.

“The new discovery further confirms the exceptional scientific significance of Lake Mezzano and the importance of this site for the study of the protohistory of central Italy,” the Superintendence added.

“The new evidence contributes to enriching our knowledge of the populations who inhabited Tuscia over three thousand years ago, opening up further research avenues into the economic, social, and cultural dynamics that characterized the region in prehistoric times, well before the emergence of the Etruscan civilization.”

They also noted that research “will continue in the coming years” as they want to uncover further items linked to “ritual and cult practices connected to water.”

This isn’t the first age-old sword that has been dug recently. In May, a six-year-old boy uncovered a 1,300-year-old sword from the Viking age during a field trip in Norway.

Elsewhere, archaeologists in Turkey believe they’d uncovered the real grave of a professional wrestler who lived between 475 and 300 BC. Ancient coins had been found buried in the grave too.

Also, in early October, scientists in Dublin, Ireland, uncovered “ancient DNA” in a 1,000-year-old book linked to “disease outbreaks in animals” from the past.
