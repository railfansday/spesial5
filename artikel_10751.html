GTA 6 screenshot hidden in San Andreas back in 2021 was real and the house has a dark secret

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A mysterious GTA 6 screenshot discovered in San Andreas Definitive Edition back in 2021 has finally been found in Rockstar’s new game, confirming a theory that predates even the infamous 2022 hack.

Five years ago, players exploring GTA Trilogy: The Definitive Edition noticed something unusual inside the Lil’ Probe’Inn, a UFO-themed bar in San Andreas.

Among the photographs on the wall was a picture of a suburban house with palm trees and a UFO flying overhead. Unlike the other images, which came from existing Rockstar titles, nobody could identify where it was from.

Dexerto reported on the discovery in November 2021, with fans theorizing that Rockstar had secretly included the first-ever GTA 6 screenshot.

Now, with GTA 6 finally here, it turns out they were right.

The same day the game was released in New Zealand, a player named Jared from the GTA 6 mapping community discovered the house in Belville, located in Leonida’s Leonard County.

The building is almost identical to its 2021 appearance, right down to the garage, palm trees, and surrounding vegetation.

All credit goes to Jared from the GTA VI Mapping server.The house is located in Belville. (https://t.co/hWt1Koz3uz) pic.twitter.com/t2ZsSRPQFI

However, players who break into the garage will discover something even more bizarre.

Inside is the hideout of a conspiracy theorist who has seemingly figured out that he’s living inside a video game.

Notes scattered around the garage reveal that the resident has been trying to escape Leonida, but can’t purchase a plane ticket to Los Santos or drive beyond the state’s borders. He’s even considering fleeing by boat.

The conspiracy theorist has also noticed NPCs who look identical, neighbors unable to enter their own homes, people glitching through walls, and buildings with nothing behind their locked doors.

There’s even a graph showing an enormous spike in violent crime alongside numerous references to the number six.

The photo Rockstar added to GTA San Andreas Definitive Edition in 2021 was a real GTA 6 photo.You can visit that house to find more conspiracy theories hidden inside. pic.twitter.com/5vxNFBmsSQ

Perhaps most interestingly, the resident identifies two individuals, known only as J and L, who appear immune to the strange limitations of his world. These are seemingly references to GTA 6 protagonists Jason and Lucia.

We’ll have to see if there’s even more to this discovery as players continue to explore Leonida and see everything the world has to offer.

The find is especially remarkable because the original screenshot surfaced in November 2021, nearly a year before the massive September 2022 Rockstar hack exposed early GTA 6 gameplay.

It’s unclear whether Rockstar planned the garage’s fourth-wall-breaking Easter egg all those years ago or added it after seeing the community obsess over the house.

Either way, the developer managed to hide a glimpse of GTA 6 in plain sight years before its official reveal.
