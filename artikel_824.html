Cynthia Erivo to Host Rockefeller Center Tree Lighting Starring Kelly Clarkson, Alanis Morissette & More

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Alex Warren, EJAE & Wynonna Judd are also on the bill.

Cynthia Erivo is set for a wickedly festive Christmas tree lighting at Rockefeller Center, with NBC announcing Tuesday (Nov. 17) that the Tony winner will host its annual televised special.

The two-hour program — broadcast live from the network’s flagship building in New York City — will also feature performances from a star-studded list of musicians, including Kelly Clarkson, Kirk Franklin, Wynonna Judd, JYT, Brad Paisley and Alex Warren. Additionally, Alanis Morissette will sing amid the rollout of her new Christmas album, Good Weather for Immaculate Conception, as will Kpop Demon Hunters star EJAE, whose schedule of seasonal gigs also includes the Macy’s Thanksgiving Parade this year.

“I can’t wait to spend Christmas in Rockefeller Center with you,” Erivo excitedly wrote on her Instagram Story.

Carson Daly, Craig Melvin and Al Roker will make appearances as well, along with the Rockettes.

Viewers can tune in at 8 p.m. ET on Dec. 2 on NBC and Peacock to watch the iconic NYC evergreen shine for the first time this Christmas season.

This will mark Erivo’s first time hosting Christmas in Rockefeller Center. The news comes right after she received an early Christmas gift, picking up a Grammy nomination alongside Ariana Grande for their “For Good” duet in Wicked: For Good.

Erivo is also riding high after her fabulous performances at the 2026 Rock & Roll Hall of Fame induction ceremony and her Monday (Nov. 16) benefit concert at Lincoln Center, where she shot her shot at recording the next James Bond theme following Robert Pattinson’s casting announcement in the franchise.

The Rockefeller tree lighting is one of NYC’s most treasured traditions, dating back to the Great Depression. This year’s tree — a Norway spruce cut from West Edmeston, N.Y. — measures at 76 feet tall and 50 feet wide, weighing approximately 11 tons. It will soon be decked out in tens of thousands of lights and its 900-pound Swarovski star topper.
