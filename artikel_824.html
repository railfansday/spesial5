Roberto De Zerbi hails Tottenham for tying down wonderkid Luca Williams-Barnett

Roberto De Zerbi has hailed Luca Williams-Barnett’s new contract as a clear sign of Tottenham’s ambition.

Williams-Barnett marked his full debut for Spurs with a goal and assist in Saturday’s 2-0 win over Ipswich to justify the growing hype around the highly-rated 18-year-old.

It was followed by a new long-term contract for the teenager on Tuesday, which is understood to run until 2031, and ahead of Sunday’s trip to Sunderland De Zerbi stressed the importance of keeping hold of him.

“It’s great news for us, the extension of his contract, because if you see other big teams, big clubs, they are losing young talent in the same situations,” De Zerbi said.

“We have to say thanks to the club, because I think it was an important step for us, for the pitch, for the football, but also for the fans, because I think we gave a great signal with this extension of the contract.

“And if the agent of Luca, if the family of Luca, accept to stay and to extend the contract, it’s because they believe in the Tottenham project. So I think the credit is for the club.”

Williams-Barnett looks to be in line for another start at Sunderland due to the absence of several attacking players.

De Zerbi added: “You have to analyse many things.

“I think he is ready to play because he is a mature guy, he has the personality to play in every stadium, every game, but we have to analyse everything he has done this week.

“The debut in the first XI, the goal, the assist, the extension of his contract. Everything is for the best. For us, for him, for the club.”

De Zerbi used Dejan Kulusevski as an example for Williams-Barnett to follow and the Sweden playmaker could start one of Tottenham’s three matches over the next week after a 17-month absence with a serious knee injury.

“I am thinking to start maybe not now, but I think we are very close to start a game,” De Zerbi added.

“Deki is working well. I watched his games in the national team just to understand the physical condition.

“He played the first game as a winger and the second game as a number 10 and especially in the second game he played very well, but we have to be good to understand the right moment to put him on the pitch.

“I think medical staff, physical staff, they are working very well with him and we can’t lose any time, but we can’t take any risk because he is a special player.”

While Kulusevski is close to a start, Tottenham are set to be without Savio for the rest of 2026 and beyond due to a hamstring issue sustained late on against Ipswich.

De Zerbi said: “I don’t know, 50 days, 60 days, 80 days, I don’t know. When he comes back I think he will be crucial for us for the last step of the season.”
