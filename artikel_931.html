‘Fragile’ Chelsea must toughen up after Leeds humiliation, admits Xabi Alonso

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Xabi Alonso is straying towards crisis territory. Here’s a mind-bending statistic: Chelsea are eighth but have now lost more league games than Manchester United. They were bullied by a rampant Leeds United, who fully merited their first win at Stamford Bridge since going top with a 2-0 victory in December 1999, and can look forward to another season with no European football if they do not sharpen up soon.

It is not really worth dwelling on the fact that the second Leeds goal, a Dominic Calvert-Lewin penalty converted early in an astonishingly one-sided second half, came after Morgan Rogers was harshly penalised for handball. That was a minor detail given the wider picture. Leeds, superbly coached by Daniel Farke and up to sixth after two clinical strikes from Noah Okafor, Calvert-Lewin’s sixth goal of the season and a late effort from Brenden Aaronson, were so dominant that they had no real need for a helping hand from the officials.

Farke’s side were tactically superior against flimsy opposition. For Chelsea, who were jeered off after their fifth defeat in 11 games, the indignities piled high. Records are falling, but not in a good way. This is the first time in 67 years they have lost grudge matches at home to Leeds and Tottenham in the same season, leaving Alonso on shaky ground with supporters, and there was further alarm when Cole Palmer appeared to clash with Willie Isa after the side’s “cultural architect” urged Chelsea’s No 10 to acknowledge the crowd at full-time.

Palmer cut a disgruntled figure throughout and did nothing to make Thomas Tuchel rethink his decision to leave him out of the England squad. The one mitigating factor is that few players will thrive in a directionless team. What does Alonso want Chelsea to be? No one knows. There are no patterns of play and the main takeaway from this surrender is that the only sign of an identity was an addiction to calamitous defending.

Calvert-Lewin followed the lead of Sunderland’s Brian Brobbey by tormenting Levi Colwill and Wesley Fofana. Alonso was appointed in the summer but the pressure is mounting already. The Spaniard’s control even let him down when a ball dropped towards the technical area in the second half.

It was some turnaround from the visitors, who trailed when Emmanuel Emegha marked his full debut by scoring his first goal since joining Chelsea from Strasbourg last summer. Farke continues to excel. The best that can be said of Alonso is that he needs time. Losing Moisés Caicedo early on was a blow. Even then it is impossible to understand Alonso’s intended style of play. There is an emptiness to Chelsea. They faced 21 shots and failed to up the physicality after crumbling against Sunderland.

Admittedly Alonso is dealing with João Pedro’s absence in attack. He has toyed with a false nine in recent weeks, but used a target man this time and the early signs were promising. Emegha gave Chelsea a focal point and finished well when an error from James Justin allowed Rogers to send the striker through on goal.

Leeds were on top when they conceded. Chelsea had lost Caicedo, who made way for Jordan Henderson after injuring his back in a collision with Justin, and were already wobbling. Calvert-Lewin spurned three excellent headed chances and Leeds levelled after 28 minutes.

Chelsea had no defensive leaders. Fofana is too easily ruffled in the air and was involved in the equaliser. He sent a routine header back into dangerous territory and Chelsea panicked. Josh Acheampong mistimed his jump as the ball bounced around and Calvert-Lewin was able to find Okafor, who cut inside and slipped a low shot past Emiliano Martínez.

Farke was rightly disappointed that Leeds were not ahead at half-time. Tarik Muharemovic charged forward from defence, away from Palmer’s feeble challenge, and crossed for Calvert-Lewin to flick wide. Ethan Ampadu ran midfield, outshining Henderson and Romeo Lavia, and the Leeds wing-backs were wonderful. Ao Tanaka went close and Muharemovic grimaced after having a goal disallowed for offside.

Alonso looked bewildered. Colwill, low on confidence since returning from a knee injury, kept misplacing simple passes and Estêvão Willian was ineffective on the right. Chelsea were jeered off and made a dreadful start to the second half. Martínez should have conceded a penalty for a challenge on Calvert-Lewin. Michael Oliver then pointed to the spot when Rogers was adjudged to have handled Jayden Bogle’s shot.

• Leeds have won their first game against Chelsea at Stamford Bridge in all competitions since December 1999, ending a run of 10 away games against the Blues without victory (D2 L8), while they’ve scored 4+ goals away at Chelsea for the first time since January 1970 (5-2).

• Leeds have won their first Premier League game in London since May 2022 (2-1 against Brentford), ending a run of 15 matches in the Capital without victory (D4 L11), while it’s their biggest winning margin in London since April 2003 (6-1 against Charlton).

• Leeds’ Ao Tanaka (pictured with Cole Palmer) committed three fouls inside the first six minutes against Chelsea; no player has ever fouled more often inside six minutes in a Premier League game on record (since 2003-04). Opta

It looked harsh given Rogers’ arm was close to his body and the ball hit him close to the shoulder but the decision was upheld after a VAR check. Calvert-Lewin stepped up to score and the result was never in doubt once Leeds led. Chelsea, who were mentally fragile, crumbled. Leeds were rampant. Chelsea could not cope with Ampadu’s long throws. One was half cleared but Sean Longstaff worked the ball back to Ampadu, who crossed for Okafor to make it 3-1.

“We are in a fragile moment mentally,” Alonso said. “When we receive a punch we didn’t recover. The fans were disappointed because the expectations are high. I understand their disappointment. There were moments that we were not competing at the level required.”

The closing stages were miserable for Chelsea, who have already conceded 20 goals. Leeds cruised and completed the humiliation when Aaronson, a late substitute, turned past Malo Gusto before sliding a shot through Martínez’s grasp.

The home sections had already emptied out. Those who stayed soon made their displeasure clear.
