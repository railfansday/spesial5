Manchester City's seven-match winning run to start the Premier League campaign feels like a very long time ago. Three successive defeats before the international break have left Enzo Maresca's side six points behind leaders Arsenal, while they also trail early-season surprise package Brighton & Hove Albion.

The good news for City is that Fulham appear to be the perfect opponent to end their losing streak. They have beaten the Cottagers in each of the last 20 meetings in all competitions, while their unbeaten run stretches back 26 games to a 3-1 home defeat in April 2009 — the first season under the Abu Dhabi United Group ownership.

A win would see City narrow the gap to Arsenal ahead of the Gunners' trip to Newcastle United later in the day, giving them a psychological boost for their clash with Mikel Arteta's side at the Emirates Stadium next weekend.

Winger Antoine Semenyo remains absent with a knee injury, while defenders Marc Guehi and Abdukodir Khusanov are doubtful after withdrawing from international duty due to fitness issues.

Fulham will feel like this is as good a time as any to change their appalling record against City, on account of their opponents' recent vulnerabilities and their own encouraging run of form.

The Cottagers followed up wins over Hull City and Coventry City with draws against Aston Villa and Newcastle United before the international break, easing the pressure on manager Alvaro Arbeloa.

Fulham's unbeaten run has lifted them to 15th in the Premier League and a rare win at the Etihad Stadium would propel them all the way up to 10th, for a few hours at least.

Midfielder Tom Cairney could return for the Cottagers, but goalkeeper Benjamin Lecomte is out for the season with an Achilles injury. Emile Smith Rowe also looks set to miss today's game with a hip issue.

The international break should have allowed City to reset so we are backing them to return to winning ways.
