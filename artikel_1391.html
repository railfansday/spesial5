Joao Neves: “Roma are one of the best teams we’ve faced. They press really hard and have lots of quality.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

“Roma are one of the best teams I’ve ever faced.” Joao Neves said so, making no secret of his admiration for Gasperini’s team after the 2-2 draw at the Parc des Princes.

Did the Portuguese expect a different match? The Giallorossi certainly put PSG under pressure, ending a 12-game undefeated streak. And especially without a draw. “We were used to always winning,” Neves admitted with a hint of regret.

Then Roma arrived and the script changed. A performance of character, intensity, and quality, which could have led to a different result. The Parisian midfielder himself acknowledged this: “Roma played a very, very good, positive match. These are the teams we enjoy playing against and competing against. I think it’s precisely games like this that help us grow, learn, and gain experience in this competition.”

Neves then went on to elaborate on Gasperini’s style of play: “They’re a team that presses man-to-man. I think it’s one of the few times we’ve faced an opponent with this kind of pressure and so much quality.”

Gasperini’s trademark, in short, didn’t go unnoticed. Neves, however, also emphasized his team’s reaction: “I think that, despite everything, we were superior to them. In the end, though, what matters is putting the ball in the back of the net more often than our opponents, and we did that, scoring goals.” The final result was a draw, but the feeling is that Roma left their mark. And the Portuguese’s words confirm this: “It was a very good match, hard-fought, and with room for improvement.”
