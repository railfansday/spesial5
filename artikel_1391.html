Pollock at heart of scuffle that inspires Leicester to thumping win in feisty derby

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Henry Pollock was at the centre of an East Midlands derby flare-up that sparked a second-half Leicester onslaught as Northampton were overpowered at a sodden Mattioli Woods Welford Road.

The England back row, never knowingly far from controversy, escalated a brawl at the end of the first half that Leicester prop Joe Heyes revealed “added fuel to the fire” of a Tigers side who roared impressively when they returned after the break.

The incident fired up a sell-out crowd and Leicester brought what had up to that point been a drab game to life with a storming second-half performance.

Three tries in a pulverising 13-minute spell overturned a half-time deficit and put the hosts on course for a bonus-point win over their arch-rivals that took them to second place in the Prem table.

Pollock, withdrawn after 52 minutes, may wish to reflect on the wisdom of riling the Tigers in their own lair.

“That was a proper Tigers second half and a really pleasing performance,” Geoff Parling, their director of rugby, said.

“We got on the front foot and showed our power. The way the guys problem-solved was brilliant and I’m just really pleased with the emotional energy they threw into that game.

“The way we were physically for each other – I’m really pleased.

“The challenge for us, if we want to be a title-winning team, is to play like that when it isn’t Northampton Saints. We have to play with that heart – the fire we showed in our chests – but also the calmness we showed too.

“I think if we play our game to the best of our ability we can knock over anyone.”

It was a chastening afternoon for the champions who last won at Welford Road in January 2023.

Phil Dowson, Saints’ director of rugby, felt his side were in the contest at the interval but bemoaned the fact that they were unable to handle Leicester’s second-half power surge.

“It was a scrap. The conditions were going to dictate that. And they had some set-piece dominance which hurt us,” he said.

“That gave them a ton of energy and we didn’t seem able to respond, which was probably the most disappointing thing.

“In the second half we didn’t seem to have that same buzz about us.”

Dowson was reluctant to blame the tunnel flare-up for the turnaround.

“The fracas at the end of the half was just one of those things that happens in a derby – that happens every derby. I don’t think that’s necessarily something that triggered anything,” he said.

“It’s not as if anyone got filled in. Well, you just can’t anymore, can you? These games are going to be hostile and in the current game you can’t throw anything. It’s just push and shove and talk c--p, so I don’t think that had any impact.”

Northampton stand-off Fin Smith was withdrawn late on with a dead leg but he is expected to join up with the England squad for training on Monday.

Hello and welcome to our live coverage of Leicester Tigers v Northampton Saints.

The Prem’s derby weekend continues today at Mattioli Woods Welford Road, where Geoff Parling’s side go head-to-head in with their East Midlands rivals.

Both Leicester and Northampton have two wins from the opening three rounds of the season and with both sides involved in a number of high-scoring encounters already in the new campaign, we could be in for a real treat.

Tigers started the season with a 38-40 loss at home to Saracens, but bounced back quickly with wins over Gloucester and Newcastle Red Bulls. They will be itching to right a few wrongs after suffering a chastening Champions Cup defeat in Pau last week.

Northampton, meanwhile, have notched up wins over Newcastle Red Bulls and Bath either side of loss to Bristol Bears.

A win today for Saints would be their first on the road since defeating Exeter Chiefs at Sandy Park in April.

“It (the East Midlands derby) means a lot to everyone, we know it will be a great atmosphere at Mattioli Woods Welford Road,” said Parling.

“I don’t think with this sort of game either team has to build it up too much emotionally because that just comes, so I think it’s actually about being really really calm in the moment.”

“Hopefully we can put the best of ourselves out there and I’m sure Saints will be able to do exactly the same.”

Saints are without a victory in Leicester since January 2023, when a single point decided the 18-19 result.

Since then, the last five meetings between the sides have been won by the hosts on the day – with Saints last taking home the bragging rights when a Tom Litchfield hat-trick helped Northampton to a 45-31 win on home soil in last season’s league play-off.
