The Internet’s Favorite Preppy Brands Are Back for a New Parke & Stoney Clover Lane Drop

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Rolling Stone may receive an affiliate commission.

Some fashion collaborations feel unexpected. Others make so much sense you wonder why they didn’t happen sooner. Parke and Stoney Clover Lane fall firmly into the latter category, and lucky for fans of both brands, they’re doing it again.

The latest Parke x Stoney Clover Lane collaboration arrives Friday, Nov. 6, at 10 a.m. ET, with another round of cozy apparel, colorful accessories, and customizable pieces that practically have TikTok written all over them.

It’s not hard to see the appeal. Parke has built a devoted following around its oversized sweatshirts, vintage-inspired denim, and relaxed silhouettes, while Stoney Clover Lane has made a name for itself with customizable bags, pouches, and patches.

The brands first joined forces in September 2025, bringing those two aesthetics together. Now, they’re trading some of their usual streetwear influence for a cozier, outdoorsy direction, complete with sherpa, suede, and fall-ready textures.

Parke’s signature oversized mockneck gets a preppy update with Stoney Clover Lane branding, and we wouldn’t be surprised if it’s one of the first pieces to sell out.

The latest drop finds common ground between Parke’s casual, vintage-inspired aesthetic and Stoney Clover Lane’s penchant for personalization.

Standout pieces include a $250 suede and denim combo jacket, a $188 wide-set tote, and $108 large varsity pouches. Accessories range from $24 decorative patches to $88 plush bear bag charms, with additional pouch styles and cozy pieces rounding out the lineup.

Made from a cozy cotton-blend fleece, these boxer sweatpants have a relaxed, baggy fit and a co-branded elastic waistband. Pair them with the matching mockneck for the full look.

This oversized canvas tote comes with dual carrying straps, fuzzy varsity-style Parke lettering, and plenty of room for everything from gym clothes to other essentials.

Parke and Stoney Clover Lane’s take on the weekender comes in rich brown suede, complete with leather trim and custom co-branded details.

The collection drops Friday, Nov. 6, at 10 a.m. ET on Stoney Clover Lane and Parke.

If there’s something you’ve already spotted in the previews, don’t wait until launch morning to make a decision. Check sizing, compare colors, create an account, and save your payment details ahead of time.
