GTA 6’s Lucia Actor Revealed, And It’s The Person Everyone Already Guessed

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The actor playing Lucia in Grand Theft Auto 6 has been revealed, and it's Manni L. Perez, the person everyone expected it to be for a long time now. In fact, fans discovered that Perez was Lucia all the way back in 2023.

The news didn't come from Rockstar Games or the actor herself, but instead via The Game Awards. That's because Perez was nominated for Best Performance. GTA 6, meanwhile, was nominated for overall Game of the Year this year, and that's sorta awkward because reviews for the game aren't live yet.

Perez was nominated for Best Performance alongside Patrick Gibson (007 First Light), Angela Sant'Albano (Resident Evil Requiem), Liam McIntyre (Marvel's Wolverine), and Rahul Kohli (Saros). The actor playing Jason in GTA 6 remains a mystery, but people widely believe it's Dylan Rourke.

GTA 6 leads the way this year at The Game Awards with eight nominations. These include:

  • Game of the Year
  • Best Game DIrection
  • Best Narrative
  • Best Art Direction
  • Best Score and Music
  • Best Audio Design
  • Best Performance (Manni L. Perez)
  • Best Action/Adventure Game

GTA 6 launches on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a PC release reportedly on tap for early 2027.

In addition to Perez as Lucia and someone else as Jason, we've learned that veteran actor Stephen Root will play Brian Heder. People believe Saturday Night Live actor Bobby Moynihan is playing Cal Hampton, but this is not confirmed yet. For more, check out all the GTA 6 confirmed and rumored actors.
