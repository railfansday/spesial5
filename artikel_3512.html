Alexander Skarsgård’s Fantasy Romance Movie Officially Passes Major Box Office Milestone

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Following his major breakout as vampire Eric Northman in the hit series True Blood, Alexander Skarsgård has been part of some of the biggest and best movies and shows. This week, it was confirmed that he would join his Pillion co-star Harry Melling, Sunrise on the Reaping's Maya Hawke, and Blade Runner 2099's Hunter Schafer in The Confessions, an upcoming project described as a "cautionary psycho-dramedy."

This news comes as another Skarsgård performance is proving a quiet favorite at the box office. The Succession star is in another elaborate costume in Wicker, a new bonkers fairy tale based on the short story The Wicker Husband by Ursula Wills. After making $5.7 million in its opening weekend, the film has maintained a presence among the domestic top ten, placing eighth in the most recent daily ranks with $581,000 from just over 2,000 locations.

This latest daily return has helped Wicker pass its biggest box office milestone yet. Globally, the film has now returned $30 million, which is three times its reported $10 million budget. The film has become Black Bear's highest-grossing self-distributed release in North America, after the company purchased the film's distribution rights for $2.5 million. Last weekend, Wicker scored a haul of $3.4 million and finished inside the top ten. The highest-grossing film of the weekend was writer/director Takashi Yamazaki's Godzilla Minus Zero, which earned $30 million in a hugely impressive opening gambit.

Written and directed by Alex Huston Fischer and Eleanor Wilson, Wicker doesn't just boast the talented Skarsgård in a typically quirky performance. Academy Award winner Olivia Colman, Peter Dinklage, Elizabeth Debicki, and more also feature in this fantasy hit, which earned rave reviews from critics. This included Collider's Therese Lacson, who called it "an immensely entertaining watch" and "a fun and quirky story about love and overcoming loneliness" in her review. A synopsis reads:

"On the outskirts of a village by the sea, lives a Fisherwoman; smelly, single and perpetually ridiculed. One day, fed up with her stuffy, small-minded neighbors, she commissions herself a husband to be made from wicker. In an otherwise conservative town, this unconventional romance sparks outrage, jealousy and chaos."

Wicker is available in theaters. Stay tuned to Collider for the latest box office news.
