AEW Wrestler Dustin Rhodes Announces Death of 3-Year-Old Grandson

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

All Elite Wrestling star Dustin Rhodes and his ex-wife, WWE legend Terri Runnels, have announced the death of their 3-year-old grandson, Rowan.

Her former spouse Dustin (real name Virgil Riley Runnels III), 57, also posted the tragic news on behalf of his daughter Dakota, 32, who works with her dad as a photographer and administrative assistant for All Elite Wrestling. (Dakota and Bleau also share son Duncan, who was born in May 2025.)

“Guys my grandson Rowan has passed. Please keep us all in your prayers during this time. I am here with @DakotaRunnels22 now,” Dustin tweeted on Wednesday, adding, “Please pray for my family. For @DakotaRunnels22 and her husband Bleau.”

He later shared a photo of Dakota with her late son, adding, “I love u Dakota and Rowan.”

No additional details about the circumstances surrounding Rowan’s death have been publicly disclosed as of publication

The wrestling community has come together to support Dustin and his family in their time of grief.

“I’m sending you and your family my love and condolences,” WWE star R-Truth replied to Dustin via Twitter.

WWE Hall of Famer Rob Van Dam sent his longtime friend three “prayer hands” emojis while Alexa Bliss wrote to him, “I’m so so sorry – sending so many prayers to you & your family.”

Dustin’s former Ring of Honor World Tag Team Championship partner, Sammy Guevara, passed along “love and prayers” from himself and his wife, former AEW wrestler Tay Melo.

“Prayers for your family,” WWE’s Dominik Mysterio tweeted to Dustin.

Dustin is part of a legendary pro wrestling family. His father, the late “American Dream” Dusty Rhodes, held the NWA World Heavyweight Championship on three occasions while his younger brother, Cody Rhodes, is a three-time former WWE Champion. (Dusty died from kidney failure at age 69 in June 2015.)

Nicknamed “The Natural,” Dustin has had a near 40-year career of his own in professional wrestling, having started out in 1988. He held the WWE Intercontinental Championship three times as well as the TNT Championship on one occasion in All Elite Wrestling. (Dustin joined AEW in 2019 as both an active wrestler and a behind-the-scenes coach for younger talent.)

Terri and Dustin tied the knot in 1993 after working together in World Championship Wrestling. They joined the WWE in 1995, where they portrayed the iconic characters Goldust and Marlena, respectively, during the company’s influential “Attitude Era.”

The couple divorced in 1999. Dustin was later with his second wife, Milena Martelloni, from 2002 to 2003 and has been married to Ta-rel Marie Roche since 2012.

Terri and her second husband, David Essel, were married from 2001 to 2002.
