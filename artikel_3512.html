I’m a Deals Writer, and These Are My Top 10 Tech Deals This Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

As Lifehacker's deals writer, I'm always looking for the best bargains on TVs, speakers, laptops, and other tech. Now that it's Friday, I've gathered together this week's sales highlights, all of which I've vetted using my favorite price-tracking tools to make sure they are actually good deals.

This week, my favorite travel headphones are an incredible bargain, plus there are sweet deals on a TCL QLED TV, a great robot vacuum, and more.

The Sony WH-CH730N are $128 (originally $179), the lowest price they've ever been. They use the same Sony Connect app as the premium headphones; as a result, you get a 10-band slider on the EQ menu, a battery care feature that extends the longevity of your battery life, 50 hours of juice, ANC, Ambient Sound (transparency mode), Multipoint to connect to two devices at once, and Bluetooth 6.0.

The CMF Watch 3 Pro is Nothing's latest smartwatch, and it incorporates AI features powered by ChatGPT; you can currently pick it up at a major discount: Originally $99, you can get the CMF Watch 3 Pro for $59 at Amazon. This is the lowest price this smartwatch has been.

The JBL Tour One M3 Smart Tx have replaced my Sony 1000XM6s as my go-to airplane headphones. The Tour One M3s are down to $199.95 (originally $449.95), their lowest price ever. This is a great deal for these headphones, which I deemed my favorite travel headphones in my review.

I got the Roborock Qrevo CurvX because it seemed like the perfect robot vacuum for me, as I have a dog, a mix of different floor types, and a gym with a workout mat, and it excels at handling them all. Amazon currently has it for $749.99 (originally $1,499.99), a 50% discount, and the lowest price it has ever been.

The Sennheiser Momentum 4 have been around since 2022, and they were only succeeded this summer by the Sennheiser Momentum 5. However, they are still great headphones, and with their $199.95 price (originally $299.99) after a 32% discount, they're a great opportunity to get top-quality headphones at a more than reasonable price.

Amazon has dropped the 46mm Apple Watch Series 10 (GPS + Cellular) to $679 from $799, taking $120 off this titanium model. Although it's no longer Apple's newest smartwatch, the Series 10 still has plenty going for it, with PCMag giving it an "outstanding" rating. Much of its appeal comes down to the design, which is thinner and more comfortable than the Series 9, with a larger always-on OLED display that gives you more room to read notifications and messages without making the watch feel particularly bulky. The titanium case also gives it a more polished appearance than most smartwatches, especially if you plan to wear it beyond workouts.

Samsung’s HW-Q600F soundbar is down to $379.97 at Amazon from $499.99, a $110 discount that brings it to its lowest price ever. This 2025 model sits in the middle of Samsung’s Q-Series lineup and pairs a sleek, angular bar with a subwoofer in a 3.1.2 setup. Dolby Atmos also brings a modest sense of height, and effects track cleanly from left to right. You get a graphic equalizer and sound presets for adjusting the audio to your preferences through the included remote.

Right now, the 65-inch QM8L is $999.99 (originally $2,499.99), its lowest price since it launched in April. The QM8L has a “Zero Border” bezel design, which gives it a modern, minimalistic look. When you turn it on, you'll likely note how bright this TV gets. TCL claims it reaches 6,000 nits; no OLED can come close to this unit's brightness. Another important spec to note here is the local dimming zones: TCL claims this TV has up to 4,000, which means it gets top-tier backlight control, limited light blooming, and incredible contrast levels that some might not be able to distinguish from an OLED.

The Ultimate Ears Miniroll is down to $49.99 (originally $79.99), the second-lowest it has sold for so far. It’s a small, flat, puck-shaped Bluetooth speaker that weighs just over half a pound, with a removable rubber strap you can attach to a backpack, bike handlebar, or shower caddy. Despite its size, the Miniroll can get surprisingly loud—which PCMag confirms in its review—and at moderate volume, it has enough sound for a kitchen or backyard without sounding thin. As for its battery life, you get about 12 hours of playback before it needs a charge over USB-C, while its IP67 rating protects it from dust and a brief dunk in water.

If you're looking for a premium model, Samsung’s 65-inch S90H OLED TV is down to $1,497.99 from $2,697.99 at launch, saving you over $1,000. The sale price is easier to justify when you consider that this is a new 2026 model. If the 65-inch model does not suit your space, Samsung also sells the S90H in 42-, 48-, 55-, 77-, and 83-inch versions, and those sizes are discounted as well.
