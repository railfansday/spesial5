The Best Dividend Stock to Invest $1,000 in Right Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Invitation Homes has a 4.5% dividend yield -- and it's growing.

I'm familiar with lots of great dividend-paying stocks, and I own a bunch of them, too. Every now and then I stumble upon one that's new to me, and that happened recently with Invitation Homes (INVH +0.75%). It's moving to the top of my watch list, as I wait to have $1,000 or more available to invest in more stock.

I'm super intrigued by the stock because:

That dividend is significant in part because Invitation Homes is a real estate investment trust (REIT) -- a company that owns lots of real estate, leasing it to tenants. REITs are required to pay out most of their earnings to shareholders, in the form of dividends.

While some REITs focus on retail buildings and others focus on warehouses, medical facilities, or other niche(s), Invitation Homes specializes in renting out homes to families and individuals. The company has noted that the average resident age is 39 and 54% have at least one child at home, as of 2025.

The company is growing well, with its third quarter featuring total revenue up 6.3% to $731 million. Its occupancy rate for homes it has rented out for a year or longer was 96.4%. It sold 658 wholly owned homes, bought 216 more, and used the proceeds from sales to fund share buybacks -- another way companies can reward shareholders. (When buybacks are factored in, the stock's total yield is 4.85%.)

Those kinds of yields are quite attractive, and the fact that payouts are growing is even better. The company's current quarterly payout per share is $0.30, and that's up from $0.22 in 2022 and $0.15 in 2020.

Invitation Homes' business prospects appear solid, in part because interest rates are relatively high these days, putting a damper on homebuyers' hopes and leaving more as renters. Housing shortages, meanwhile, have kept many homes priced high.
