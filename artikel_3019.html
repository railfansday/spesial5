Why Compass Minerals International Stock Rocketed Higher Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The latest earnings report shows Compass Minerals is headed in the right direction.

By Scott Levine – Nov 24, 2026 at 5:46PM EST

Compass Minerals International (CMP +14.51%) reported fourth-quarter 2026 financial results yesterday after the market closed, and investors expressed their approval of its recent performance -- and its outlook for fiscal 2027 -- during today's market session.

Shares of Compass Minerals ended today's trading session 14.5% higher than yesterday's closing price of $20.33.

Reporting Q4 2026 sales of $250.9 million, Compass Minerals surpassed the consensus among analysts that it would post $219.1 million on the top line.

Towards the bottom of the income statement, Compass Minerals showed progress toward profitability. The company reported fiscal 2026 adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) of $235.1 million, an 18% year-over-year increase.

Management's auspicious outlook provided additional fodder for the bulls to feast on today. For fiscal 2027, Compass Minerals expects stronger salt pricing, a better customer mix, and further reductions in production costs to drive the company closer to profitability, with adjusted EBITDA forecast at $250 million to $290 million. Should the company achieve the midpoint of this guidance, it will represent year-over-year growth of 17%.

Compass Minerals provides customers with a variety of important minerals, such as salt products used to keep roads safe in winter weather, and its plant nutrition products help improve crop quality and yield while supporting sustainable agriculture.

After encountering operational challenges several years ago -- including its decision to cancel its lithium project in Utah -- Compass Minerals is showing signs of success in its efforts to get back on track. Plus, the company ended fiscal 2026 in better financial health, reducing its adjusted net debt-to-trailing twelve-month adjusted EBITDA ratio to 3.2 as of Sept. 30, 2026, down from 3.9 at the same time last year.

With shares of the materials stock trading at 6 times operating cash flow, a discount to its five-year average cash flow multiple of 6.5, it still seems like a good time to load up on shares even with their recent rise.
