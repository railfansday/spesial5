Nvidia’s Biggest Risk Is Product Pace: Windsor

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Radio Free Mobile founder Richard Windsor says the immediate damage to Nvidia is reputational, with its products, contracts and financial outlook still intact. He says the critical test for Jensen Huang’s successor will be maintaining the rapid product-development cycle that underpinned Nvidia’s dominance and warns that any slowdown could cost the company market share and margins. Windsor was speaking to Tom Mackenzie on the latest episode of Bloomberg Tech: Europe which explored the risks and rewards for Europe following Jensen Huang's departure as Nvidia CEO. (Source: Bloomberg)
