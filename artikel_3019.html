If You Want To Trade Like Anthropic, You Better Look Like Anthropic, Says Buyer

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Lise Buyer, Founder of Class V Group and one of the chief architects of Google’s 2004 auction IPO, discusses Anthropic’s stronger-than-expected IPO demand, share count and pricing after reports had suggested a possible downsizing. She cautioned investors not to rely on market chatter, emphasizing that a company’s value is ultimately what investors are willing to pay. She speaks with Romaine Bostick & Ed Ludlow on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
