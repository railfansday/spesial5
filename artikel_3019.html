Huang's Ties to Trump Under Scrutiny After Spy Claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Bloomberg's Mike Shepard said that actions taken by the Trump administration and by Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang are coming under sharper scrutiny by Congress amid investigations into the affair Huang had with a women alleged to be a Chinese spy. Trump had denied that he knew anything about the national security risks posed by Huang's affair, but recent New York Times reporting reveals that Trump had been briefed about the risk before his trip to China earlier this year. (Source: Bloomberg)
