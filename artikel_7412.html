Newsmax Sues Fox Over Roku Deal, Alleging Antitrust Violation

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Newsmax is suing Fox Corp., alleging its recently completed $22 billion purchase of Roku unlawfully consolidates control over America’s rapidly growing free streaming television market.

The antitrust lawsuit was filed Wednesday in the United States District Court for the Northern District of California. In a shared statement, Newsmax said Fox owning Tubi and The Roku Channel, the nation’s two largest FAST platforms, gives it approximately 71% of the U.S. FAST television market as measured by viewing time.

Newsmax is seeking injunctive relief to stop further integration of the companies, preserve Roku as an independently managed business, and ultimately require Fox to divest either Tubi or The Roku Channel.

Newsmax founder and CEO Christopher Ruddy said in a statement, “This merger represents an extraordinary threat to competition, consumer choice, and the ability of independent news organizations to reach the American public.”

For its part, Fox Corp., in a statement shared with TVNewser, said, “Newsmax’s complaint, which comes after a rigorous review process by regulatory agencies, is wholly without merit. Upon closing, we commenced actively integrating this approved transaction for the benefit of consumers, partners, and other stakeholders.”

Earlier this month, the Rupert Murdoch-owned company completed its purchase, combining Fox Corp.’s broadcast, news, sports, streaming, and local news portfolio with Roku’s open connected TV platform and first-party data from more than 100 million global streaming households.

As part of its agreement with the Department of Justice to take over Roku, Fox Corp. committed to keeping Roku open and neutral for at least three years, including continuing to carry non-Fox content, avoiding search or placement discrimination, providing third-party data products, and maintaining contractual firewalls.

This is not the first time Newsmax has tussled with Fox; in Sept. 2025, a Florida judge dismissed another Newsmax lawsuit against Fox News without prejudice. That lawsuit alleged the Rupert Murdoch-owned network engaged in an extensive, unlawful campaign to block competition in the right-wing pay-TV market.

Fox News responded to the dismissed lawsuit by saying, in a statement to TVNewser: “Newsmax cannot sue its way out of its own competitive failures in the marketplace to chase headlines simply because it can’t attract viewers.”
