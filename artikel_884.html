Rangers great Brand dies aged 89

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Rangers say Ralph Brand will "forever be remembered as a great of the club"

Rangers have paid tribute to Ralph Brand, one of the most prolific goalscorers in the club's history, who has died aged 89.

A Hall of Fame forward at Ibrox, the former Scotland striker, who scored eight times in eight caps, netted 222 goals in 347 appearances for the Govan side.

Brand made his Rangers debut as a 17-year-old in 1954 and remained with the club until 1965.

He was part of four league-winning sides and won three Scottish Cups and four Scottish League Cups during his time at Ibrox.

After spells in England with Manchester City and Sunderland, Brand returned to Scotland to finish his playing career with Raith Rovers and Hamilton Academical.

"Ralph Brand's contribution to Rangers was vast and, through his Hall of Fame recognition, he will forever be remembered as a great of the club," the Ibrox club said in a statement.
