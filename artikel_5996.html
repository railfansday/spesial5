After shopping for everyone else over Black Friday and Cyber Monday, it's your turn on Travel Tuesday. As you plan something for yourself, Capital One wants to help you book it for less.

Beginning Dec. 1, Capital One will offer eligible cardholders limited-time savings on hotels, rental cars, flight-and-stay packages and premium vacation rentals through Capital One Travel.

Some of these deals are more useful than others. Here's who can use each one and how to tell whether the savings are worth booking now.

Capital One is highlighting five offers this year:

The Washington, D.C., hotel offer ends Dec. 7 or when supplies run out, whichever comes first. The Hertz offer runs through Dec. 15, while supplies last.

The other offers each carry their own booking window, travel dates and terms, which you'll find in your Travel Wallet once the deals go live.

This year's deals are a perfect example of "when it works, it really works."

Some offers can save hundreds, but only if they match a trip you already wanted to take.

The hotel discount could save you $150 off $500, for example. That's meaningful savings if Washington, D.C. or Florida are already in your travel plans. But if you're traveling anywhere else, that discount is irrelevant.

The complimentary fourth night sounds valuable, particularly if you were already considering one of Capital One's curated collections. But it's worth noting that Capital One Travel already offers the fourth night free at select Premier and Lifestyle Collection hotels — this isn't a new deal for Travel Tuesday.

Similarly, the vacation rental offer appears to be a version of a targeted offer already in my account. Without waiting for Travel Tuesday, I already have a $200 discount available toward premium rentals in my Venture X account. There's no indication that the upcoming deals will raise my offer, though Capital One advertises that some cardholders may be targeted for up to $500 off.

Additionally, Capital One's collection of vacation rentals skews toward higher-end properties. Even with a discount, budget travelers may do better searching a wider selection of properties on Airbnb or Vrbo.

Lastly, the 15% discount on qualifying Hertz rentals is great when Hertz is already competitively priced, but it might not be high enough to change your plans if another brand is offering a better deal. Keep in mind that the Venture card family includes Hertz elite status after enrollment: Eligible Venture X cardholders can enroll in President's Circle status*, while Venture and VentureOne cardholders can enroll in Five Star status*.

The flight-and-stay package discounts require more comparison shopping because Capital One hasn't shared a standard discount. You'll need to check the cost of booking the same flights and hotel separately.

*Upon enrollment, accessible through the Capital One website or mobile app, eligible cardholders will remain at that status level through the duration of the offer. Please note, enrolling through the normal Hertz Gold Plus Rewards enrollment process (e.g. at Hertz.com) will not automatically detect a cardholder as being eligible for the program and cardholders will not be automatically upgraded to the applicable status tier. Additional terms apply.

The Venture X is the biggest winner. Cardholders may have access to the widest range of promotions. The card earns 10 Capital One miles per dollar spent on hotels and rental cars plus 5 miles per dollar spent on flights and vacation rentals booked through Capital One Travel.

Capital One’s Travel Tuesday deal lineup has some winning deals...and a few that likely won't be useful for most shoppers. The free fourth night is a standing perk, and the vacation rental discounts may already be in your Travel Wallet. Use this as your opportunity to re-familiarize yourself to card benefits you may have available year-round.

The genuinely new deals are the hotel discounts in D.C. and Florida, the Hertz savings and the flight-and-stay packages. If any of these fit your plans, even one well-matched offer can make a difference in your travel budget.
