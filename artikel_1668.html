Emmanuel Emegha: New details emerge as Chelsea striker ‘robbed’ after Leeds game

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Chelsea striker Emmanuel Emegha has revealed that he was “robbed” following Saturday’s Premier League match against Leeds.

Sources close to the Dutchman say that while his phone was not among the possessions stolen, personal belongings and several work-related items were taken. The incident took place outside a hotel in Mayfair on Saturday night.

Emegha was unhurt, with Chelsea understood to be aware of the situation and providing their full support to the player.

The Metropolitan Police are also aware of the situation.

Emegha scored against Leeds on what was his first Premier League start for Chelsea since arriving from BlueCo sister club Strasbourg in the summer, but his goal made little difference in the end as Leeds won 4-1 against Xabi Alonso’s side.

The 23-year-old took to Instagram on Sunday evening to reveal that he had been robbed in London shortly after the match.

“Unfortunately after the game I was robbed,” Emegha wrote. “I am okay and thankfully no one was hurt. Thanks for the messages of support.”

The post started: “First goal, disappointing result, we’ll be back stronger on Friday [against Nottingham Forest].”

Emegha’s transfer to Chelsea was agreed in September 2025 but he remained at Strasbourg last season and arrived at Stamford Bridge in the summer.

He scored 31 goals in 78 total appearances for Strasbourg across all competitions and opened his account for Chelsea on his third outing.

After nursing a hamstring injury at the beginning of the season, he made his first competitive appearance off the bench in Chelsea’s Carabao Cup last-16 defeat by Liverpool last month before then being handed a league debut in the 1-0 loss at Sunderland before the international break.
