US has 'unbalanced approach' on DR Congo deal, Rwanda's foreign minister says

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

In an interview with FRANCE 24, Rwandan Foreign Minister Olivier Nduhungirehe said the Trump administration had "an unbalanced approach" in implementing the DR Congo peace deal it brokered. He also said that the recent row over the Organisation Internationale de la Francophonie's top job was "manufactured in Paris and Kinshasa". Finally, he defended a deal for Rwanda to host failed asylum seekers from five European countries.
