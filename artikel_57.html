'A masterpiece of the gaming industry': GTA 'most expensive' entertainment production in history

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

François Picard is pleased to welcome Olivier Mauco, President of the European Video Game Observatory and Lecturer at Paris Dauphine University. For Spotlight, he discusses the extraordinary anticipation surrounding GTA 6, the game's enduring popularity and the economics of blockbuster video game production. “It's kind of the masterpiece of the game industry,” says Mauco of Grand Theft Auto, whose next instalment has generated considerable anticipation following a 13-year hiatus.
