A tale of stars and struggles: Player ratings for Olympiacos-Milan from Italian papers

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

AC Milan recorded their second win in the Europa League by beating Olympiacos, and it came in dramatic circumstances.

When Jota Silva put Olympiacos 2-0 up after Lorenzo Pirola’s header cannoned off Mike Maignan to produce the opening goal, it looked like being another miserable night of continental football for Ruben Amorim’s men.

Yet, they roared back thanks to 15 minutes of madness. Omari Hutchinson inspired it, scoring a wonderful solo goal and then setting up both Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic to complete the comeback, before Diego Moreira sealed it late on.

This morning’s edition of La Gazzetta dello Sport starts us off with an 8 out of 10 rating for the team’s overall performance, and a 7.5 for Ruben Amorim after he made the right changes to inspire the comeback.

Hutchinson was the man of the match, getting an 8.5, while Koni De Winter struggled the most and scored a 5. Cisse and Pulisic each got a 7.5, while Moreira, Ardon Jashari, Yunus Musah and Fikayo Tomori received a 7.

Mario Gila, Adrien Rabiot and Filippo Terracciano were all praised (6.5s). Mike Maignan got a passing score of 6, while Sankhoun Diawara (5.5), Pervis Estupinan and Goncalo Ramos (5s) all struggled.

Corriere dello Sport gave a 7.5 to Hutchinson, a 7 to Pulisic, then 6.5s to Tomori, Musah, Moreira and Cisse. Maignan, Diawara and Ramos (5.5s) were below par, while Estupinan got a 5 and De Winter was even scored a 4.

Tuttosport awarded De Winter a 4.5 while they were not as impressed by Gila, who got a 5.5 along with Diawara. Estupinan scored a 5, as did Ramos. Praise instead went to Tomori (7), Musah (6.5), Moreira (7), Musah (6.5), Jashari (6.5), Rabiot (6.5), Cisse (7), Hutchinson (8) and Pulisic (7).
