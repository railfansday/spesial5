PSG: there is danger for David Boly!

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

It’s no secret now: the Paris Saint-Germain training center has nothing to envy of the best academies in the world. Long little used by the various coaches who have succeeded one another at the head of the first team, the Parisian Titis now benefit from more opportunities with Luis Enrique. The Spaniard does not hesitate to call on them, particularly during Ligue 1 matches, where he wishes to rotate his squad. Senny Mayulu or even Ibrahim Mbaye, before his departure, are good examples.

And if we ask Paris Saint-Germain specialists who will be the next young player to have a fairly interesting playing time in the first team, many will answer David Boly. Used against Le Havre in the second period for the first time this season on Friday, the 17-year-old right back is considered one of the big nuggets, if not the biggest nugget, that PSG has in its ranks.

Obviously, it is also a somewhat complex issue to manage for Parisian decision-makers, and one on which they will not want to make a mistake. The situation is not obvious: the player is at the end of his contract in 2027, and has still not signed his professional contract. Obviously, a lot of clubs, including European big names, want to take advantage of the situation to snatch it from the European champions as the newspaper explains. The Team. Recently, for example, there was talk of serious interest from FC Barcelona for his services.

The sports daily indicates that the management of PSG made him a very interesting offer on a financial level, testifying to its confidence and the desire of the club to see him register for the long term in Paris. Positive point for the Parisians: for the moment, the player’s choice seems to fall on PSG. At least, it is the Ile-de-France team which is at the top of the list of the main concerned. On the other hand, he sets one condition: to already have a little more playing time in the championship. Let us hope, for PSG at least, a positive outcome in this matter…
