Pablo Pagis reveals behind the scenes of his call-up to the French team

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

He is the chef’s surprise. Author of a good start to the season with Paris FC, Pablo Pagis was summoned by Zinédine Zidane for the two Nations League matches in Italy, on November 12, then in Bordeaux against Turkey, on the 15th. If his presence in the list is mainly the consequence of the absence of Maghnes Akliouche, package, the Parisian will have the opportunity to highlight himself in the eyes of the coach, who is very complimentary of him. After Paris FC’s victory against Auxerre this Saturday (0-1), Pagis revealed behind the scenes of his call-up.

“ I knew that I was pre-selected, so obviously, you look at the list and tell yourself that (that you have a chance of being called up, editor’s note). But I had already been pre-selected before, so I think the fact of having experienced it earlier meant that I expected it less, even though there were more chances. I had less of that excitement from the first list, but I was very happy. My girlfriend asked me in the morning: “But do you believe it?” And I told him: “I don’t really know, we’ll see.” In fact, I didn’t want to worry about it and concentrate on the Auxerre match. It’s not easy to be on the bus and learn about this (the Blues call-up, editor’s note) while you are preparing for a match. It was crazy (the atmosphere on the bus, editor’s note), and yes, my dad called me right away, and the whole family. These are good times “, confided the 23-year-old player.
