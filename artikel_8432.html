We got the PFF and window tinting on our 2026 Y, along with front mudflaps.

PFF for the long-term look, some chip protection, and allow using the car wash without swirl concerns

The tinting was for heat/cold protection, and cosmetics

No PFF in the past, although I think the Lexus paint on our existing SUV is pretty sturdy. I got the PFF this time based on the recommendations of “Car guy” friends who have it on their black BMW’s and were materially motivated by not having to hand wash their vehicles.

Seems like the arguments against it from a financial perspective are accurate. The financial portion wasn’t a motivator for us.

Just a sidebar… I did get full OEM glass replacement on my insurance policy with no deductible as I understand the front windshield replacement on Tesla will be pricey, and we do get a fair amount of rock chips hitting windshields around here.

Have fun. We are retired, age 67, and I haven’t like to drive for a long time, and I just love the fsd. It’s 100% the reason we bought the vehicle.

The car wash made swirl/light scratches on our Toyotas, so I will be either hand washing or touch free carwash on the Tesla when it arrives.

I do find benefit to tint. And will add the sun shade if it gets too hot next summer.

Good tip on that sidebar. Noted.

As for FSD, I test drove the S, 3, and Y about 3-4 years ago. I love the speed and tech. But FSD in 2026 is miles ahead of anything I've ever experienced. My commutes are getting longer and they are a bit stressful. I've also been the victim of not at fault accidents which I believe FSD may have been able to avoid 2 out of 3. That alone makes FSD worth it for me.

Enjoy retirement and the Tesla!
