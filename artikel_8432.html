New Details Emerge About Amicalola Falls Machete Attack

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A man accused in an October 12 machete attack at Georgia’s Amicalola Falls State Park allegedly slashed a male hiker who had intervened to help a woman, according to new testimony.

Benjamin Robinson,32, of Kentucky, appeared for a court hearing on Tuesday in Dawson County, GA, where he stands accused of aggravated assault. According to investigators’ testimony at the hearing, Robinson was allegedly attacking the woman he was dating when a bystander stepped in to help around 8 p.m. on October 12. Robinson allegedly slashed the man with a machete. “If you’re going to leave me … you’re going to leave me with nothing,” Robinson reportedly said to the woman during the incident.

The identities of the woman and the injured hiker have not been publicly released, and authorities have not provided details on the extent of the man’s injuries or his current condition.

Robinson allegedly fled the scene after the incident and was arrested in McCracken County, Kentucky the following day during a traffic stop. He was later extradited to Georgia, where he faces 7 charges, including aggravated assault.

Amicalola Falls State Park is a hiking destination in northern Georgia where many Appalachian Trail thru-hikers start their journeys, as the park includes the Approach Trail to the AT’s southern terminus on Springer Mountain.

Dawson County Sheriff’s Office did not immediately respond to a request for comment for this article.
