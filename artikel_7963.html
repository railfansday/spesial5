“There’s no place like home” rings even truer for the homebodies in your life. If you’re shopping for someone who prefers cozy nights in over wild nights out, the right gift will make their time at home even more restful. From soft blankets to top-rated fire pits, we’ve rounded up the best gifts for anyone who simply loves staying home.
