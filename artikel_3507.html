Man Flew From France to Nebraska to Kill Woman He Once Went on a Date With

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

A man has been accused of flying from France to Nebraska to kill a childcare worker he had gone on one date with three years earlier.

Terry Kanmacher has been arrested after he allegedly stabbed Lauren Becker to death. Kenmacher, 21, was caught while trying to board a plane at a Minnesota airport to head home to his native country of France.

Omaha Police Chief Todd Schmaderer said during a press conference that Kanmacher allegedly attacked Becker, 22, outside Kids Can Community Center in Omaha on Tuesday, November 24, around 9:15 a.m.

Kanmacher arrived in Omaha on Sunday, November 22. Schmaderer said Kanmacher left his Airbnb two days later and waited in the childcare center’s parking lot for about 45 minutes before attacking Becker as she walked to work.

Becker and Kanmacher previously connected online, and they met only one time in prison when he flew to Omaha in the summer of 2023. Becker ended their communication in December 2023 and reportedly made it clear she did not see a future with him.

“The victim was very clear in 2023 that the relationship was over,” Schmaderer said, per Nebraska Public Media. “She was afraid of him at this point. She came to the police department and she reported her concerns. Her concerns were very detail oriented, and a lot of her concerns mirrored what took place, quite frankly.”

Three years later, Kanmacher allegedly traveled to Omaha with plans to kill Becker.

Kanmacher was arrested on Tuesday at the Minneapolis–Saint Paul International Airport while trying to board a flight back to France. He had originally been booked to fly out of Kansas City on November 30, but he changed his itinerary to fly out of Minneapolis a few days early.

The Omaha police worked with Homeland Security Investigations and Minneapolis–Saint Paul Airport Police to track down Kanmacher and arrest him. Schmaderer said that investigators made the “tactical decision” not to release suspect details before he was arrested.

“He didn’t know we were onto him,” Schmaderer said of the defendant. “We had his flight information, and his vehicle was put into all law enforcement systems. We did not want to alert him and scare him into changing vehicles and abandoning his travel plans.”

Schmaderer also noted that authorities used Becker’s 2023 report to help identify Kanmacher as a suspect.

The attack happened in front of a glass classroom window, though it was not immediately clear whether any students witnessed the killing.

Becker started working at Kids Can Community Center in August, where she led STEM activities for school-age children.

Kanmacher is currently being held in Hennepin County, Minnesota, and is scheduled to attend an extradition hearing on Monday, November 30, according to jail records viewed by Us.

It’s not currently clear whether Kanmacher has entered a plea or retained legal counsel following his arrest. The investigation into the case remains ongoing.
