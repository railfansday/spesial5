Brad Pitt and GF Ines de Ramon Hold Hands During Rare Red Carpet Appearance

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Brad Pitt and his girlfriend, Ines de Ramon, couldn’t contain their smiles while walking the red carpet.

The couple stepped out on Monday, November 16, to attend the premiere of Netflix’s The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth in Los Angeles, which Pitt, 62, stars in as the titular character. Pitt and de Ramon, 33, held hands on their date night.

For the event, Pitt opted for a navy sweater and pants with a double-breasted coat and accessorized with a pair of sunglasses. De Ramon, for her part, stunned in a cream dress with cutouts at the waist and a plunging neckline. She styled her hair down in loose curls as she beamed with pride at the world premiere.

Pitt and de Ramon have been linked since 2022 when they were first spotted joining friends Cindy Crawford and Rande Gerber at a Bono concert at the Orpheum Theatre in Los Angeles. Two years later, Pitt and de Ramon debuted their romance at the British Grand Prix. Pitt, for his part, has denied that the 2024 appearance was part of a planned promotional event for his 2025 film F1.

“No, dude, it’s not that calculated,” he recalled to GQ in a May 2025 profile. “If you’re living … oh, my God, how exhausting would that be? If you’re living with making those kinds of calculations? No, life just evolves. Relationships evolve.”

The pair have since given glimpses of their relationship, with Pitt opening up about how he and de Ramon enjoyed a date-night at Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce’s star-studded wedding in July.

“You mean, the wedding of the century? Let me tell you, that was such an amazing night,” Pitt told Entertainment Tonight in September when asked about the phone-free event. “I think we were there [for] 10 hours, maybe 12.”

Pitt and de Ramon matched in coordinating black formalwear at the event, which was held at New York City’s Madison Square Garden.

“It was so thoughtful and well-planned and really moving and great fun,” Pitt gushed. “But it was big, and at one point, I lost her in the melee on the dance floor.”

But the pair reunited quickly. “Not long. There’s always someone to go, ‘That way,’” he said, pretending to point in opposite directions.

Prior to his relationship with de Ramon, Pitt was previously married to Jennifer Aniston from 2000 to 2005 and later to Angelina Jolie, with whom he shares six children, from 2014 to 2019.
