GTA 6: How To Go To Hell And Fight Satan

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Grand Theft Auto 6 gets downright weird when you go off the beaten track, and one secret mission will see you travel to the bowels of hell to fight Beezleboss himself, Satan. This detour is worth it because you'll get cool cosmetics and a powerful shotgun, plus a nice little bump to your health. Here's how you can start this Doom-inspired mission in GTA 6.

How to go to Hell in GTA 6

You can find this mission in Port Gellhorn, Kelly County, and it's generally recommended that you tackle it once you've gotten your bearings in Chapter 2 of GTA 6. You can access it with either Jason or Lucia, and you'll need to follow these steps to find it:

  • Start the mission at night, preferably around midnight.
  • Go to the northern section of Port Gellhorn (as indicated on the maps) and look for the Circles of Hell, a house with an open garage.
  • Inside the garage, you'll find two NPCs rocking out. Press L2 to highlight them and start talking.
  • Select the dialogue options "Having fun?" and "It's good" when prompted.
  • Follow the NPCs into a van that appears shortly afterward, and get inside.
  • After being transported to the camping grounds, press L2 to stare into the campfire.

Once you've done all that, you'll be taken to Hell, and you'll have to face off against an army of demons. Don't worry! You'll be equipped with a powerful shotgun, so you can mow down the demons and fight your way to Satan. The Prince of Darkness will occasionally summon reinforcements to defend him, but the denizens of hell are no match for a few well-placed shotgun rounds. Finish off the devils, and you'll wake up at the campsite. For your efforts, you'll be rewarded with the following items and upgrades:

  • Circles of Hell top
  • New tattoo design
  • Hellspawn Comshot 12 shotgun (collect it next to the throne)
  • Permanent health boost
