Small Local Scholarships Draw Far Fewer Applicants than National Ones – Apply to Several

Apply for small local scholarships through your high school counselor, community groups, local employers, and your college’s departments. Each is open only to students from one area or group, so far fewer people compete than for a national award. A few hours on these applications can pay more than one long national essay.

The Coca-Cola Scholars Program gives $20,000 each to 150 high school seniors a year, and it picked its 2026 class from more than 107,000 applications.

A local award is open only to students from one county or school, or to the children of one company’s workers. Far fewer people compete for it than for a national one. The Scholarship Foundation of Santa Barbara serves a single California county. For the 2026-27 school year, it received 3,481 applications and gave more than $8.4 million to 2,288 students. Santa Barbara’s awards work out to about $2,400 for every application it received, against about $28 per application at Coca-Cola.

Small checks add up, too. Say a senior wins four awards of $500 to $2,000 from her credit union, a Rotary club, her mother’s employer, and her high school’s booster club, for $4,500 toward her first year.

Write one core essay about a goal or a setback you handled, then rework its opening and closing for each prompt. Keep every deadline, prompt, and recommender in one spreadsheet, and ask teachers for recommendation letters at least three weeks before your first deadline.

Tell your college’s aid office about every award, since outside scholarships can shrink the aid the college gives you. The College Board notes that some colleges cut their own grants and others cut your loans, so ask which one your college cuts first. And skip any service that charges a fee to find scholarships for you, since the Federal Trade Commission warns that some hand over nothing at all once you’ve paid.

Ask your high school counselor or your college’s aid office for a list of local awards this week, then apply to every one you’re eligible for.
