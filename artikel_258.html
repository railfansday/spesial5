DOJ just launched something called the Super Intelligence Task Force

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The unit is tasked with ensuring that the technology is not “manipulated by bad actors at home or abroad.”

The Department of Justice (DOJ) just announced the creation of something called the Super Intelligence Task Force, also called the DOJ-SITF. The unit is tasked with making sure that US-based artificial intelligence technology isn't compromised by "foreign state and state-sponsored actors, transnational criminal organizations and individual or corporate actors engaged in fraud."

It's being led by Stanley Woodward and Stephen Weiss, two people who report to Attorney General Todd Blanche. I bring Blanche up because he has been accused of fraud on several occasions, once via a crypto scheme, and now he's involved with an agency that will investigate it in the AI sector.

"SI development will continue to be powered by American innovators," Blanche said in a press release announcing the unit. "The SI Task Force at the Department of Justice will facilitate our partnership with SI Force Chair Jay Clayton as we ensure critical SI technologies serve the American people and are not manipulated by bad actors at home or abroad."
