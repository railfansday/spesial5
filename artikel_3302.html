As I look out onto my backyard, fall is in full swing. My lawn is covered in leaves, and my trees are starting to look bare. While I don’t mind the golden and russet colors scattered on my lawn, I know I need to clear the leaves to keep my grass healthy.

That’s why I’ve been looking for the best deals on yard tools to remove leaves, and I’ve spotted some excellent discounts on Amazon. Whether you want to blast through the mess with a leaf blower or get the kids involved with a fun leaf-scoop set, I've rounded up the best Amazon finds to get the job done.

These large leaf grabbers make grabbing leaves a cinch. The oversized claws measure 15 x 12 inches and allow you to grab large quantities of leaves, grass clippings or even mulch in one swoop. For ease of use, the handle can be adjusted to suit the user.

This narrow metal leaf rake has 25 steel tines and is designed to clear leaves and debris without clogging. It has an adjustable handle with four length settings (36 to 73 inches), making it easy for the whole family to use. Made of high-strength steel with a rust-resistant construction, it's heavy-duty yet lightweight to use and store.

These three reusable lawn and leaf bags are ideal for collecting leaves and lawn clippings. Each bag includes a removable support hoop to keep it upright in use, plus top and bottom handles for easy maneuvering. What’s more, with a 132-gallon capacity, each bag can hold a stack of leaves or other yard debris.

Be kind to your back when raking leaves with this long-handled leaf grabber. With a 17-inch leaf grabber, you can grab more leaves at once. Its dense metal tines provide a strong grip, making cleanup easy. At just 3.4 lbs, it’s ultra-light and easy to handle.
