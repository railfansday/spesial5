‘Vengeance Is Mine’ Director Edwin Reveals Cast For ‘Dancing With My Shadow’ Based On Generation-Defining Album

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

EXCLUSIVE: Indonesian filmmaker Edwin has revealed the five young leads for his upcoming feature, Dancing With My Shadow (Menari Dengan Bayangan: Evakuasi), inspired by the album of the same name from Hindia.

Produced by Palari Films, the coming-of-age drama marks a return to youth-focused stories for Edwin following horror film Sleep No More (2026), which premiered in Berlinale Special Midnight earlier this year, and darkly comic action romance Vengeance Is Mine, All Other Pay Cash, which won the Golden Leopard at Locarno film festival in 2021.

Based on the album by singer-songwriter Baskara Putra (a.k.a. Hindia), Dancing With My Shadow will star a mix of rising talent and familiar faces, including Rafly Altama, known for Riri Riza’s Rangga Cinta, and Hyori Mika, who stars in Palari Films’ recent Tokyo prize winner Jani Be Good. Also joining the cast is Muzakki Ramdhan, a former child actor from well-known genre films such as Gundala and Satan’s Slave 2: Communion, who already has more than 30 films under his belt.

Newer faces in the young cast include Daffa Saputra, an actor and fashion model for whom this is his first significant film role, and Freya, a member of the JKT48 idol girl group, who started her acting career in horror hit Dominion Of Darkness and family drama A Brother And 7 Siblings.

The cast is being revealed tonight at the final of the DBL Jakarta Series, Indonesia’s biggest high school basketball league, the world where the story takes place.

Released in 2019, Hindia’s Menari Dengan Bayangan became the soundtrack of a generation, touching on subjects that young Indonesians rarely talk about openly: anxieties around growing up, pressure from home, and the fear of being left behind. It holds the record as the most-streamed Indonesian-language album on Spotify, and still regularly appears on the platform’s weekly Top Albums chart in Indonesia.

The team behind the film explain that the album’s themes have been reworked into an original story, “with the music serving as an emotional map rather than a script”. Written by Winnie Benjamin, the story follows a quiet teenager, Agra, still grieving his father, who becomes the photographer for his high school’s rowdy basketball supporters club. Behind chants and chaos, he finds friendship, first love and a group of kids quietly learning how to survive.

Produced by Palari Films’ Muhammad Zaidy and Meiske Taurisia, the film will be executive produced by Baskara Putra, Mira Lesmana and Dian Sastrowardoyo, with music by Petra Sihombing and Enrico Octaviano.

Baskara will also appear in the film as himself performing on a concert stage. Commenting on the project he said: “This album feels like my own child. I’ve always wanted my albums to take on new forms, but handing it to someone else was scary. Then I realized the film shouldn’t be my life story. It should be a new story born from the same feelings. Edwin has always filmed young people honestly, without looking down on them. That’s what these songs needed.”

For Edwin, the project marks a return to youth stories a decade after 2017 coming-of-age drama Posesif, which won a Citra Award for Best Director. “Agra spends most of the film behind a camera, deciding what to frame and what to leave out. I think that’s how many of us lived our teenage years,” Edwin said. “The stands are full of noise and bodies, but everyone there is carrying something they don’t talk about. I wanted to stay close enough to feel it.”

Zaidy said: “From Posesif to Ali & Ratu Ratu Queens and Dear David, and now Menari Dengan Bayangan: Evakuasi, we keep coming back to coming-of-age stories. There’s always a fascinating crossroads in them. They feel familiar to so many people, yet there’s always a sense of liberation and empathy for people going through a transition in their lives. That stays relevant, even for adult audiences.”

Dancing With My Shadow is being lined up for an Indonesian theatrical release in summer 2027.
