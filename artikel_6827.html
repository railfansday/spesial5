We are seeking a Director, Global Strategic Partnerships, responsible for managing our AWS (Amazon Web Services) partnership reporting to our global partner ecosystem team. You will be responsible for shaping and delivering on our strategy to build mindshare and use of Siemens Xcelerator portfolio with AWS and their clients. Your responsibilities will include developing business, GTM, and technical relationships with AWS and their client base to identify where we can unlock significant joint business potential.  Key business metrics for success include joint solutions, pipeline development, lead generation, and deal closure influenced by AWS.

The ideal candidate will possess a strong Partner management background along with an understanding of all the elements that are required to enable and grow our AWS partnership.  This role is senior in nature and will seek bring a consolidated approach to this partnership by aligning our teams to one strategy and enabling consistent execution.
