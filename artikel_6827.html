The 6 Best Places to Find Early Black Friday Handbag Deals

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Tory Burch, Balenciaga, and Chloé are already up to 40 percent off.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

You don’t have to hold out for Black Friday to shop for a new handbag—fashion sales have already begun. Retailers like Amazon, Nordstrom, and Revolve marked down dozens of styles from popular labels like Tory Burch and Mansur Gavriel, to name a few. And with discounts up to 60 percent, many of these designer finds will sell out before Thanksgiving dinner even hits the table, so we don’t recommend waiting until the holiday weekend to shop.

To help you beat the rush, we’ve shortlisted the six best sites for early Black Friday handbag deals. Whether you’re looking to upgrade your everyday work tote, pick up a new party bag before the holidays, or check a few stylish people off your gifting list early, these online destinations are worth your time.

If you’re planning to shop Amazon’s massive Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale, it’s worth breaking your trip up into a few smaller, more manageable purchases. In years past, we’ve seen early fashion deals sell out (or, arguably worse, return to full price) before the event officially begins. Everyday bags from entry-level designers like Marc Jacobs, Kate Spade, and Michael Kors are already deeply discounted. Plus, the affordable, celeb-loved label JW Pei is offering 15 percent off an assortment of styles, including Anne Hathaway’s Lucia bag.

Michael Kors Bryant Convertible Shoulder Bag

Nordstrom already rolled out hundreds of early Black Friday deals, including handbags from in-demand brands like Tory Burch, Reformation, and AllSaints. The retailer has yet to release details about its official sale, so it’s worth saving full-priced items to your wish list and checking back during the holiday weekend. Warning: Stock of discounted designer items is already running low, so don’t leave that Balenciaga bag lingering in your cart for long.

Those looking to invest in a designer bag should start their search at Mytheresa, where big names like Chloé and Valentino Garavani are currently up to 40 percent off. On sale for under $300, Taylor Swift’s Vivienne Westwood Granny Frame bag makes a fashionable gift for Swifties. We wouldn’t wait until Thanksgiving weekend to shop, though—it’s likely to sell out before then.

Revolve is where cool girls shop for IYKYK labels like Verafied, By Far, and Margesherwood—all of which are included in the retailer’s pre-Black Friday markdowns. Neutral-colored shoulder bags and totes are going fast, and we don’t expect prices to drop further. Finalize your handbag purchase now, then check back again on Black Friday for deals on holiday party dresses, winter coats, and other seasonal items.

You can count on Saks Fifth Avenue to have great handbag deals, no matter the time of year. Ahead of Black Friday, totes and shoulder bags from Veronica Beard, Staud, Vince, and Polo Ralph Lauren are 30 to 60 percent off. Keep an eye out for short-lived flash sales and promo codes that offer extra discounts on existing markdowns—the department store is famous for them.

Fashion insiders flock to Net-a-Porter for its rare handbag deals from sought-after designers. Invest wisely, and you can easily save over $1,000 on Alaïa, Toteme, and other luxury labels. Often, only one or two of each style are in stock, so you’ll need to finalize your cart fast to avoid missing out.
