A Burgundy Bomber Cardigan Is a Wardrobe Essential

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

When we wave goodbye to summer and refresh our wardrobes accordingly, it’s often all too easy to abandon color altogether: to reach for blacks, browns, grays. But that needn’t always be the case—an easy way to keep your neutral outfits looking lively throughout the darker fall months is to reach for earth tones. The standout color for this season? A rich, wine-hued burgundy.

Seen all over the street style scene during London and Milan Fashion Weeks, and across the high street after appearing prominently in the accessories of fall/winter 2026 collections for the likes of Miu Miu, Chloé, and Gucci, a pop of burgundy adds a sense of depth to any outfit—and one of the easiest ways to incorporate the shade into your wardrobe is via careful layering.

Burgundy has been taking the form of bomber cardigans recently, and we can’t get enough of it. There’s a plethora of styles from many of our favorite brands. Adanola makes a sophisticated collared knit while Lafayette 148 New York delivers an understated, elegant crewneck in a cotton-silk fabrication. No matter the burgundy bomber cardigan you choose, it’s the piece in your wardrobe you’re going to turn to time and time again.
