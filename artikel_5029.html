Strategy (MSTR) Adds 457 Bitcoin for $29.6 million

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Strategy (MSTR) bought 457 bitcoin last week for $29.6 million at an average of about $64,898 per coin, an 8-K filing shows, lifting its total to 851,305 BTC as the bitcoin price trades about under the company’s average cost.

The purchase ran from Nov. 16 to Nov. 22. Strategy said it paid $64.23 billion for its stack, an average of $75,446 per coin including fees and expenses. That is $6 below the $75,452 average it reported a week earlier, so the new coins barely moved the cost basis.

The buy is also smaller than the 485 bitcoin Strategy added the week before. Strategy shares were trading near $83.50 in premarket on Monday, up about 7% from Friday’s close near $78.

Strategy funded the purchase entirely with common stock. It sold 1,925,676 MSTR shares under its at-the-market program for net proceeds of $176.7 million, and no preferred stock was sold during the week.

Only $29.6 million of that cash went into bitcoin. The rest went to buy back Strategy’s own STRC preferred stock, which is the pattern the company has followed for several weeks.

Strategy repurchased 2,183,747 STRC shares for $211.8 million. It paid $147.0 million of that with MSTR proceeds and $64.8 million from its USD Cash balance. The preferred buyback was roughly seven times the bitcoin spend.

The board also lifted the size of its digital credit securities repurchase program from $3.0 billion to $4.0 billion. After the increase, $1.12 billion remains available. About $17.9 billion of MSTR stock is still available under the ATM.

The 457 coins were bought at an average of $64,898, which is above where bitcoin trades now. They also continue a slowdown: Strategy added 1,036 bitcoin in its Nov. 9 filing, 485 in its Nov. 16 filing and 457 in the latest one, while the preferred buybacks have grown.

The stack equals about 4% of bitcoin’s 21 million supply cap. Last week’s $29.6 million bitcoin spend was about 17% of the $176.7 million the ATM raised.

Bitcoin hit a weekend low near $55,100, according to Bitcoin Magazine Pro data. At that level, Strategy’s coins were worth about 27% less than the $75,446 it paid on average.

The price has since recovered to around $61,000. At that level the 851,305 bitcoin are worth roughly $52 billion, about $12 billion below what Strategy paid. That is a paper loss only, since the filing shows no bitcoin sales during the week and the coins are still on the balance sheet.

The slide has hit the stock hard. Strategy shares tumbled for a sixth straight day to about $86 on Thursday, and they fell again on Friday. A week ago the company said it was holding bitcoin modestly below cost, and the gap has widened since.

Strategy also changed how it labels its dollar cash. The board authorized management to reallocate up to $1.0 billion from the USD Reserve, which backs dividends and interest, to USD Cash, which the company can use for bitcoin purchases, buybacks and other purposes.

As of Nov. 22, after that reallocation, the USD Reserve stood at $3.73 billion and USD Cash at $1.09 billion. Strategy used $17.6 million of the reserve last week to pay preferred dividends.

That gives Strategy more room to keep buying bitcoin or retiring preferred shares without selling more MSTR stock. It also leaves the reserve smaller, at $3.73 billion, than the $4.75 billion the company reported on Nov. 15.

At the time of writing, the bitcoin price is near $61,000.
