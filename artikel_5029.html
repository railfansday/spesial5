Fidelity Crypto Adds AAVE, LINK, UNI, Expanding Asset List to Eight

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Home » Blitz news » Fidelity Crypto Adds AAVE, LINK, UNI, Expanding Asset List to Eight

Fidelity Investments adds three DeFi tokens to its retail lineup.

Fidelity Investments has added AAVE, Chainlink (LINK) and Uniswap (UNI) to its Fidelity Crypto trading service, expanding its supported digital asset list from five to eight. The addition lifts the number of supported assets from five to eight and brings three DeFi tokens to eligible US customers.

Fidelity announced the update on its official website and in an X post. The platform now supports Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), Litecoin (LTC), AAVE, LINK, UNI and the Fidelity Digital Dollar (FIDD), a US dollar-pegged stablecoin the company announced in January 2026.

Each new token is tied to a different part of DeFi. AAVE is the token for Aave, a lending protocol that mediates cryptocurrency lending and borrowing.

UNI is the governance token for Uniswap, a decentralized exchange. LINK is used in Chainlink, an oracle network that delivers external information such as price data to smart contracts.

Trading and custody are handled by Fidelity Digital Assets, a subsidiary licensed as a U.S. National Trust Bank. The service is available to eligible U.S. customers aged 18 and over residing in states where Fidelity provides the service.

Fidelity launched Fidelity Crypto for individual investors in 2022, initially offering Bitcoin and Ether. The latest addition follows Charles Schwab’s November 9 listing of Solana, Avalanche and Chainlink on its spot crypto platform.

Fidelity’s addition of AAVE, UNI and LINK extends a major US brokerage’s retail crypto offering to DeFi infrastructure tokens. It also adds to competition among traditional brokerages for crypto-active customers, following Schwab’s recent expansion.
