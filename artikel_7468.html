Woodland Hills Accountant Sentenced to Nearly 3½ Years in Federal Prison for Embezzling More Than $1.7 Million from Indie Films that Employed Him

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A film production accountant from the San Fernando Valley was sentenced today to 41 months in federal prison for embezzling money from the independent film productions that employed him, moving the more than $1.7 million in illicitly obtained funds into an account he called “Fun Fun Fun,” and using the money to fund his extravagant lifestyle.
