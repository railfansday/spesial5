As Manager, Corporate Security - Platform Engineering, you'll lead a small, distributed team of security engineers and own programs that span Corporate Security teams. You'll define clear team boundaries, run security tooling through GitOps-based engineering practices, and bring program priorities, budgets, and vendor decisions to Security leadership. This is a hands-on technical leadership role: you won't be the primary implementer, but you'll write code, review designs and merge requests, make architecture decisions, and support incident response. This role is remote within North America, reports to the VP, Corporate Security, and is at job level M3 (Manager, Security Engineering).

Platform Engineering owns shared security tooling, non-production environments, and department-wide programs within Corporate Security. The team works alongside Endpoint Engineering and Identity Engineering, with clear boundaries around each team's responsibilities. Its focus is to make security services easier to operate and use through automation, self-service, and written documentation, while keeping ownership, costs, and audit evidence clear. You'll lead a small, distributed team and collaborate asynchronously across time zones with technical, security, and business partners.
