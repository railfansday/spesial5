Inside AWS&#8217;s Bid to Be a Brand for Everyone, Including AI Agents

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Amazon Web Services is no longer marketing primarily to developers, but to a wider cohort of knowledge workers, and even AI agents. Its recent campaign for AWS Quick, the company’s AI-centered workspace, included ads featuring visuals that humans could see, undergirded by text for AI agents to read.

It’s indicative of the cloud company’s wider strategic pivot, which aims to win over more non-technical customers and help AWS reach CEO Matt Garman’s goal of becoming a $1 trillion revenue business within five to 10 years.

As part of a push to get more kinds of professionals to consider AWS, the company is updating its brand to look more like its parent company, it told ADWEEK exclusively.

Kendra Barnett is Adweek's senior tech reporter.
