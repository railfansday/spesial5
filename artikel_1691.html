Journalist confirms Euro giants stance on Liverpool star

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Liverpool made a lot of changes to their team in the summer.

In fact, even last year a lot of money was spent on trying to improve Liverpool's ranks.

Most people thought that the Reds would be unstoppable last term after bringing Florian Wirtz and Alexander Isak in.

But, that was far from the case with the club finishing fifth in the Premier League, just a season after winning the title.

This forced Fenway Sports Group to make a change to their club and appoint Andoni Iraola.

Since then, things have got better but, as has been the case for a few years now, more players need to be brought in to bolster the Reds' ranks.

But, for them to do this, some of their own players may need to be moved on with many decisions still needing to be made.

Barcelona are not going to recall Ronald Araujo from his loan early

Ronald Araujo has probably been one of Liverpool's best signings this season.

If the defender was a permanent purchase, fans would have been very thankful that their club splashed the cash on him.

But, for now, he's on loan until the end of the season with the Reds having a €55 million option to buy in their favour.

Talk of Liverpool already making his stay permanent has emerged with some links with a return to Barcelona even arising.

However, thanks to an update from Ekrem Konur, the future of the Uruguayan has now been made a little more clear.

"Barcelona are not considering recalling Ronald Araújo from his loan spell at Liverpool in January," he wrote.

"The Catalan club continue to monitor the Uruguayan defender's development and performances closely.

"Barcelona are understood to be satisfied with Araújo's progress and value the regular playing time he is receiving at Liverpool.

"As a result, the club currently have no plans to activate a recall option during the January transfer window."

Liverpool need to weigh up their options before committing to Ronald Araujo

Even though Araujo's addition to Liverpool's starting XI has helped them out massively, the Reds can't just focus on him as their only option.

They're one of the biggest teams in the world and, with the way that things are going at the moment, they're also going to qualify for the Champions League.

Several different players such as Murillo, Pierre Kalulu and Nico Schlotterbeck have all emerged as potential options for Liverpool.

So, with this amount of talent available to them, why would they stick with just one option?

Obviously, if he continues with this purple patch of form he finds himself in, it'll be a no-brainer but, as has been the case since the start of the season, the €55 million option to buy does feel quite steep.

Here's hoping that a decision is made soon so that fans can understand what Liverpool's squad will look like next term.
