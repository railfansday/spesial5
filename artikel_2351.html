Ajax, AZ and NEC all win in European football

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ajax, AZ and NEC all won their European matches on Thursday evening, with NEC moving into the Europa League’s automatic qualification places.

Ajax beat Swiss side FC Thun 4-0 at the Johan Cruijff Arena in the Conference League. It is the Amsterdam club’s third win in a row after an opening defeat at Hajduk Split, and it lifts them to third place for now, with other matches still being played.

The top eight after six rounds go straight through to the last 16.

AZ came back from 2-0 down at half-time to win 4-2 at Sparta Prague in the Europa League.

NEC beat French side Stade Rennais 3-1 at De Goffert in Nijmegen. NEC are now eighth in the Europa League table with 10 points, a position that would give them a direct place in the last 16.
