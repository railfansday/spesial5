Anthropic’s Claude completes a 16-hour human research job in under 3 hours

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Epoch AI found that Claude produced contractor-level research at a lower cost, prompting the organization to reconsider how it uses human workers.

Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 completed a research assignment in under three hours that typically takes human contractors 16 hours, according to Epoch AI.

The AI research organization reported on Nov. 17 that the model produced work comparable to that of its professional contractors, raising the possibility of automating parts of its research operations.

The assignment involved researching EcoDataCenter 1 for Epoch's data center database, a process requiring researchers to examine news reports, regulatory filings, and satellite imagery to establish details about the facility.

Epoch provided the AI models with its contractor guidelines, research tools, and evaluation criteria before assessing their work against the standards applied to human researchers.

Claude Opus 5.5 scored 87.5% on the assignment, completing it in just under three hours at a lower cost than hiring a contractor. Another Anthropic model, Claude Fable 5.2, delivered an even stronger result, scoring 97.5%. However, it required approximately six and a half hours and cost more than a human contractor.

Both models met Epoch's standards for core research, although their submissions still required revisions to equipment classifications, image annotations, and written descriptions.

The higher-scoring Claude run cost more than a contractor in Epoch’s comparison.

Epoch’s analyst judged both first passes comparable to contractor work and identified revisions for each.

The findings have prompted Epoch to reconsider how it allocates work between AI models and human contractors.

The organization said it is considering shifting some contractor time away from gathering and compiling research toward reviewing AI-generated submissions and performing other data center analysis.

Such a transition could let AI systems handle much of the initial research while human workers focus on checking findings, correcting errors, and preparing the material for publication.

However, the results also exposed differences in the economics of using AI for professional work.

While Opus completed the assignment faster and cheaper than a contractor, Fable's higher-quality output cost more, despite also finishing considerably faster than a human researcher.

The models also struggled with parts of the assignment that required visual interpretation, particularly labeling satellite images and identifying equipment.

These shortcomings help explain why Epoch has not proposed eliminating human involvement entirely, even as its models approach contractor-level performance.

Instead, the organization is considering a workflow in which AI produces the initial research and contractors refine the results, potentially reducing the human labor required for each database entry.
