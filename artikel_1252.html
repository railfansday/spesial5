Official: Carnesecchi to undergo knee surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Atalanta confirm that Marco Carnesecchi will undergo surgery on Tuesday due to a ‘tear of the medial meniscus in his left knee.’

Italy international Carnesecchi will undergo knee surgery tomorrow, his club Atalanta said in a statement on Monday.

“Following the injury sustained during the pre-match period ahead of Atalanta-Inter, footballer Marco Carnesecchi underwent diagnostic imaging, which revealed a tear of the medial meniscus in his left knee,” La Dea said.

“The player will undergo surgery tomorrow for surgical debridement of the meniscal tear.”

Carnesecchi was meant to start in a 4-4 draw against Inter on Sunday, but had to withdraw during warm-ups and was replaced by Marco Sportiello.

It remains unclear how long the 26-year-old will be out of action.

Carnesecchi has been a regular in the Italy squad over the last few years, but has yet to make his senior debut with the Azzurri.

If he recovers in time for the next international break in March, he might have a chance to start the first game, as Gigio Donnarumma will serve a one-game ban with the Italy national team.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
