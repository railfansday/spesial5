Trump’s Ukraine-War Realpolitik Isn’t Realistic

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

His policy has the trappings of realism yet is too personal and too hostile to an embattled ally to constitute a true exercise in hardheadedness.

From left: Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky, President Donald Trump, and Russian President Vladimir Putin

Guglielmo Mangiapane, Jonathan Ernst, Sputnik/Alexander Kazakov/Pool via Reuters

There’s a place for realpolitik in American foreign policy, but it needs to be based on a correct appraisal of our interests.

President Trump’s policy toward the Ukraine war has the trappings of realism yet is too personal and too hostile to an embattled ally to constitute a true exercise in hardheadedness.

Rich Lowry is the editor in chief of National Review.
