Luka Doncic told Lakers front office to get Walker Kessler this offseason

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Walker Kessler has been everything the Lakers needed him to be this season: 14 points a game on 66.9% shooting, plus 10.8 rebounds and 2.1 blocks per night (third in the league). He's been setting picks and rolling hard to the rim on offense, and on defense he's been the backstop in the paint the Lakers needed to allow their wing defenders to be disruptive and force turnovers.

Luka Doncic said he told Lakers GM Rob Pelinka this offseason to get Kessler, in an interview with ESPN's Dave McMenamin.

"I told them after the season I wanted Walker," Doncic told ESPN of an offseason conversation he had with Rob Pelinka, Lakers president of basketball operations and general manager, and the front office.

"I think he's a really good player," Doncic continued. "Obviously, last season he didn't play. He played like five games. Obviously [Utah] kept him out, but just playing against him in the previous years, it was hard to score. He was protecting the rim, he was dominating the paint. So just after the season, I went to them and said, 'I want Walker.'"

The price to get Kessler out of Utah was steep: two first-round picks (2031 and 2033) and two pick swaps (2028 and 2030), but it's one the Lakers had to pay to best reshape the roster around Doncic. The biggest risk from the Lakers' perspective was health, Kessler played just five games the previous season due to shoulder surgery and had not played in 65 or more games since his rookie season.

It has worked. The Lakers have the best record in the West and sit atop the conference heading into a showdown with San Antonio on Friday night. Both the Lakers' offense and defense are more than six points per 100 possessions better when Kessler is on the court, and the team's biggest challenge right now is non-Kessler minutes (look for them to be active heading toward the trade deadline).

This has all worked out just as Doncic has envisioned.
