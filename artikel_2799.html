Jesse Palmer narrated couple's intense convo in Wake Forest-Georgia Tech stands

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Saturday's Wake Forest-Georgia Tech in Atlanta didn't really offer fans much in terms of competitive football, but the stands featured a heck of a detour from the game.

The ESPN broadcast caught a young couple having an intense conversation during the game, with commentator and Bachelor host Jesse Palmer providing a very funny breakdown of what may have been going on. He crushed it.

Look, this football game offered very little for football fans. Finding a passionate conversation in the stands and getting a Palmer narration is a blessing from the broadcast gods for the crew and the folks at home.

Just in case you were worried this seeming lover's quarrel might end on a low note, they seemed to make up later in the game with a few smooches. The ESPN crew loved to see it.

This was really, really funny, but can you imagine how that couple's night is going to go once they check their phones and see the whole world was watching them? The wonder of live television!
