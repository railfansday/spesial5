Patriots trade deadline tracker: News and rumors ahead of the Nov. 10 deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Even though they have already matched their total losses from last year, the New England Patriots once again look like a legitimate contender for a playoff spot in the AFC. However, in order to get there, they will have to find a way to overcome the injuries that have ravaged their roster over the first two months of the regular season.

While the expectation is that they will get several players back from injured reserve at some point, they also could go for a more radical approach in hopes of improving their team and gearing up for a stretch run in 2026. Like they have done multiple times in 2026 already, they could look at the trade market.

Will there be additions to a list that already includes the likes of A.J. Brown, Jaylen Reed, Corey Kiner and Walter Rouse? Or could they move on from some of their players, like they did in 2025?

We will know soon: the NFL trade deadline is coming up on Tuesday, November 10, at 4 p.m. ET. Until then, please make sure to bookmark and regularly revisit our Patriots trade deadline tracker for all the latest developments.

The Patriots are "lurking" in the wide receiver trade market ahead of the Nov. 10 deadline. They currently have four wide receivers on their active roster, but with A.J. Brown nursing a knee injury could benefit from more depth. Some of it will come in the form of Mack Hollins, with Romeo Doubs expected to return from IR after the Week 11 bye. | Report

The Patriots are not actively shopping Christian Barmore or Carlton Davis. That does not mean they won't listen to offers, but they have not put up the "for sale" signs just yet. | Report

The Patriots are conducting "business as normal" with less than a week to go until the NFL trade deadline. | Report

The Patriots are looking to buy at the trade deadline, with edge help a particular focus. | Report

The Patriots are reportedly looking for a fourth-round draft pick in a potential Christian Barmore trade. | Report

Cornerback Carlton Davis is speculated as another trade candidate. | Report

Patriots defensive tackle Christian Barmore reportedly "could be had" via trade. | Report

Wide receiver Josh Down has requested a trade from the Colts. | Report

"Wide receiver-needy teams" have reportedly called the Colts about fourth-year man Josh Downs. Downs, of course, is a former college teammate of Patriots quarterback Drake Maye. | Report

Quenton Nelson will not be coming to New England. The Colts, who recently fired general manager Chris Ballard and are in full fire-sale mode, have decided to trade the All-Pro guard to Seattle. The Seahawks invested a 2027 third-round draft pick and 2028 fifth-rounder in Nelson. | Report
