Bills’ Week 11 win never in doubt, but they must play better vs. tougher teams

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Buffalo Bills (9-1) beat the Miami Dolphins (1-9) at Highmark Stadium, their sixth straight win and the third game in a row without allowing a touchdown. Buffalo finished with 405 total yards to Miami's 185.

Here are my notes from the game, taken as it happened.

Bills get the ball to start the game again (10th straight game opening with the possession). Start with their usual run first/PA offense. They certainly believe they came to impose their will today.

Several play-action rollouts from Josh Allen in this first drive. The run game has been contained so far (Buffalo finished with 95 rushing yards on 27 carries, 3.5 yards per carry).

That run from James Cook from the FB position is a good addition to the playbook. Tough play to stop. Blocks in the interior remind me of the way they block for the tush push (Cook: 22 carries, 89 yards, 1 TD).

Josh Allen is sacked on 3rd down. Free rusher from inside, really? Tyler Bass' FG is good, 3-0 Bills (Allen was sacked 3 times in the game).

Bills defense starts hot again, containing the Dolphins' run game and leaving the field on 3rd down. Davison Igbinosun is playing great ball at the moment (Miami punted on every first-quarter drive).

Another offensive drive and the heavy PA game continues. However, players aren't getting open downfield, and Josh is forcing it.

A fake punt pass by Tommy Doman, great completion for a 1st down. Keleki Latu with the reception. From their own 20, I don't like it. Unnecessary and very risky (30-yard completion; the Bills never punted all game).

Josh forces a deep pass outside and flirts with an INT. I like it, though. A high pass to Keon Coleman? It's supposed to be a bigger chance of a big play than an INT.

Another FG with the run game not working and the pass catchers not being able to create separation. Bills 6-0 (Bass: 54-yard FG).

The defense continues to play really well. Another impressive drive. Got lucky on 3rd down, though. Chris Bell has to catch that perfect pass by Kyle McCord, deep. Damar Hamlin and Cole Bishop clearly beat on the play, but the rookie bubbled it (McCord: 12-of-33, 102 yards, 36.4% completion rate).

Josh Allen starts to take the checkdowns available to him, and it makes all the difference in the world. The offense is moving downfield easily (Ty Johnson: 7 catches on 8 targets, 55 yards).

DJ Moore is so important. No hesitation by Josh throwing to him (Moore: 8 catches, 91 yards, 1 TD).

Josh misses Dawson Knox on a check-and-release route, already in the red zone. It would've been a walk-in TD to the veteran TE. He had nobody ahead of him.

Very next play, TD Bills. Josh makes up for the previous misfire, finds Moore on a crossing route in the back of the endzone (4-yard TD, capping a 12-play, 79-yard drive). XP is good. 13-0 Bills.

Dolphins finally can move down the field a bit, starting with great field position from their kick return. Run game could move the sticks a bit and put them in FG range. 13-3 Bills (Daniel Carlson, 52-yard FG; Miami had 6 kick returns for 159 yards).

Good to see Tre'Davious White back for some snaps. Wanna see the same with Shaq Thompson. Those vets can be valuable depth pieces late in the season or even in the playoffs.

Also great to see rookies like Kaleb Elarms-Orr and Davison Igbinosun playing better and better as the year progresses.

DJ Moore is so reliable. Great catch on 3rd and 12. 1st down.

Keon Coleman with a catch-and-run on a quick slant, getting very physical with the YAC. Love to see it.

After forcing it and almost getting intercepted, Josh quickly recognizes Ty Johnson open in the flat and delivers it, the RB does the rest. 1st and goal Buffalo.

James Cook punches it in. 20-3 Bills (1-yard TD, capping an 11-play, 57-yard drive; Cook went over 1,000 rushing yards for the season).

Another 3-and-out by the defense. The opponents they're facing are questionable for sure, but we can't deny how good they've looked recently. Deone Walker is a nightmare for IOLs, and Ed Oliver is too quick on one-on-ones. Greg Rousseau has really become a top edge rusher, and the miscommunication issues are gone. Impressive (Bills defense: 2 sacks, 8 QB hits, 5 tackles for loss, and 3 three-and-outs forced; Rousseau had 2 sacks, 10 on the season, a career high).

Great play by the Dolphins' young duo. Chop Robinson beats Spencer Brown inside and gets to Josh's face, hitting his arm as he throws. The ball goes up, and Jacob Rodriguez intercepts it. Worse than the INT is DJ Moore getting hurt on the play, with what looked like a nasty ankle sprain.

Despite great field position, the Dolphins offense can't generate anything. DeWayne Carter, Greg Rousseau, and Deone Walker with incredible plays in a row. FG is good. 20-6 Bills (Carlson, second 52-yard FG; he went 3-for-3 on the day).

DJ Moore is back and makes a huge catch after Allen shakes Chop Robinson off his shoes. Bills close to FG range.

3rd and 10, Josh throws an awful INT in the middle of the field. The safety was sitting there in the cover 3.

The Dolphins give the Bills the ball back, great play by Damar Hamlin. INT, Bills get the ball back in the middle of the field (Hamlin's first interception of the season).

What is Josh doing here? Forces it deep, and it's intercepted again. A foul on the play gives the Bills an extra chance. Screen from Josh to Moore gives them a few yards, but that's it. End of the 1st half. 20-6 Bills (Allen finished with 2 interceptions officially).

Miami starts well. Good run by Ollie Gordon II, and a good completion by McCord to Caleb Douglas put them in the red zone (the Dolphins gained more yards on that first drive of the 2nd half than in the entire first half).

Igbinosun was turned around by Douglas on that slant. Luckily, the Dolphins WR dropped it. Would've been a TD.

Another stand by the Bills D. Dolphins FG is good. 20-9 Bills (Carlson, 27 yards).

Bills D in a historic run without allowing TDs right now. More than 35 consecutive drives doing so (third straight game without allowing a touchdown; the Dolphins finished with 185 total yards and 3.4 yards per play).

The run game starts to work better, but the passing game continues to struggle. Josh doesn't look good, and nobody other than Moore can get open (Allen: 27-of-45, 304 yards, 1 TD, 2 INT).

Tyler Bass' FG is no good. 20-9 Bills and, with the way they've played, they're giving these Dolphins a chance to believe (Bass finished 4-of-5 on field goals).

Buffalo's D continues to dominate. Great play by Cole Bishop on 3rd down here. Punt.

Khalil Shakir with some good plays for the Bills on this drive (Shakir: 5 catches, 101 yards).

Penalties are killing the offense right now. Josh escapes a sack and completes a pass to Shakir to, at least, put Bass in better FG range. His FG is good. 23-9 Bills. End of the 3rd (Bills: 7 penalties for 48 yards).

Delay of game on the kickoff. This is new for me. Did they declare going for an onside kick here? Weird.

Ollie Gordon II continues to find some success running the football (Miami: 20 carries, 99 yards, 5.0 yards per carry).

Shaq Thompson in for Terrel Bernard in this drive.

Dee Alford, after getting beat by the same route early on another 4th down in this same drive, makes the play to close this drive on a 4th down. Buffalo ball (the Bills defense got 2 fourth-down stops).

Good offensive drive to (practically) close the game. Buffalo has almost 15 more minutes of ball possession than the Dolphins. It hasn't been pretty or exciting, but it has been efficient (time of possession: 36:45 to 23:15; 25 first downs to 10).

4th and goal, 2 min warning. Bass FG is good. 26-9 Bills (28 yards).

Bills' D forces 4th down but can't get out of the field there.

Great play by Tre'Davious White on 3rd and 8. Nice performances by him and Shaq Thompson. Reliable vets on PS.

Shaq almost intercepts it on 4th down. Ball game. Bills win, 26-9.

Buffalo did its homework. A lot of margin for improvement, but I can't complain when their bad game against poor competition is a 26-9 win where they never felt in trouble during the game (Bills 9-1, matching the best 10-game start in franchise history, with five of their drives ending in field goal attempts instead of touchdowns). They need to play better against better competition, but we know they can do that.

The next stretch of this schedule should determine their seed 1 chances, starting with next Thursday's matchup against the Chiefs. After this stretch, we should have a real picture of who the 2026- 27 Buffalo Bills are and their true chances at a SB appearance.
