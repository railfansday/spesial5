Stats Rundown: 3 numbers to know as the Mavericks defeat the Los Angeles Clippers, 110-100

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Dallas Mavericks (9-7) defeated the Los Angeles Clippers (4-11), 110-100, in a Saturday night matchup at the American Airlines Center. The win makes the Mavericks' second straight and follows their 116-94 blowout win over the Golden State Warriors. The Mavericks were able to overcome the Clippers down the stretch and move to 4-1 in clutch games this year.

PJ Washington, Dereck Lively, Tarik Biberovic, and Sergio de Larrea were out for the Mavericks. The Clippers were without Bradley Beal and Brandon Ingram.

Kyrie Irving and Cooper Flagg combined for 47 points in the Mavericks' win, but shot a combined 14-of-40 from the field. Brook Lopez led the Clippers with 21.

Here are three numbers to know from Saturday night's win.

Dallas finished with 42 bench points in the win over Los Angeles. In Washington's absence, the Mavericks needed someone to answer the call, and Max Christie did so, finishing with 13 points on 5-of-7 shooting from the field. Christie has struggled to start the season as he has dealt with minor injuries, and his performance against Los Angeles should give him the boost of confidence he needs to continue contributing to the Mavericks.

Christie joined Walter Clayton in leading the Mavericks bench, who finished with 16 points and five rebounds. Clayton has carved a consistent role for himself on the Mavericks after signing a two-way contract with the team. Dallas will need to find a way to make a roster spot for Clayton if he continues to be a reliable guard for the Mavericks.

Naji Marshall had a performance to forget, finishing with 4 points and shooting 0-of-5 from the field in just 14 minutes on the floor. Marshall's offensive woes slowed down a Mavericks offense that typically struggles to find reliable shooters on a nightly basis. Marshall mentioned at media day that he was targeting a goal of making 45% of his three-pointers, but is sitting far from that at 31%.

It felt like every time Marshall had the ball, even in favorable situations, he failed to capitalize. The Mavericks need more from Marshall, who was the recent recipient of a multi-year contract extension over the offseason. Performances like his against the Clippers beg the question of whether he can fit in a future built around Flagg, and why there was a need to extend him last summer.

Cooper Flagg made three shots from behind the arc tonight for the second straight game. He finished with 22 points, six rebounds, and eight assists in the Mavericks' win. He left some on the table, missing four mid-range shots, but was able to contribute offensively regardless. Flagg's three three-pointers aided in the Mavericks' 39% shooting from behind the arc. The Mavericks needed shooting to take this one home, and Flagg was able to contribute that tonight.

The Mavericks are back in action Sunday night as they hit the road to conclude their back-to-back in Utah. Catch the action against the Jazz at 6 p.m. CT on KFAA-TV and MavsTV.
