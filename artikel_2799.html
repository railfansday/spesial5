Future Broncos: 3 Prospects to Watch in Week 12 of NCAA Football Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Welcome back to another installment of Future Broncos. We are getting down to the final weeks of the collegiate football season before the playoffs and bowl games begin. Another Saturday, another big slate of games for fans to enjoy. Here are three prospects of interest Broncos Country should keep their eye on today.

The Broncos tried to make a splash prior to the NFL trade deadline making a deal for Los Angeles Chargers linebacker Daiyan Henley. Unfortunately, the medical review of Henley's knee wasn't favorable and the trade wound up being nixed. That saved the Broncos a fourth and fifth round selection in the 2027 NFL Draft. However, it's clear they are hoping to upgrade the position long-term given their interest in Henley.

I haven't covered too many inside linebackers in this year's class. However, UCLA prospect Sammy Omosigho is one that is skyrocketing up draft boards. Omosigho started his collegiate career at Oklahoma back in 2023 and spent his first three seasons there.

He never earned a full-time starting role in their defense but was a quality contributor in the snaps he played. In 36 games with the Sooners, he amassed 96 tackles, 10 tackles for loss, 2 sacks, 1 forced fumble, and one fumble recovery. For his final season, he transferred to UCLA and has become one of the best playmakers in their defense.

A ten-game starter for the Bruins, Omosigho (6'2", 239 pounds) has been on an absolute tear as a senior. He already has 72 tackles, 10.5 tackles for loss, 5.5 sacks, and a whopping five forced fumbles. Whether it's stuffing the run, getting after the passer, or doing well in coverage—he touts some of the best numbers of any draft eligible prospect.

Given his versatility, I think he is a player Defensive Coordinator Vance Joseph and the Broncos' scouts are going to be keeping a close eye on throughout the draft evaluation process. You can catch Omosigho in action today when the undefeated and fourth ranked Bruins take on the Michigan Wolverines on CBS. If he keeps playing at a high level, he should easily be a Day 2 selection next April.

This past week, the Broncos opted to extend boundary cornerback Riley Moss. The $67 million deal will keep him in orange and blue for the next for seasons. While Denver's boundary duo is set for the foreseeable future, the long-term outlook at safety is far less certain.

The reason is simple; Brandon Jones is a free agent and has regressed this season. Moreover, Talanoa Hufanga's contract is up after the 2027 season. Keep in mind most all of their depth players are on one-year contracts as well. There is no doubt in my mind Denver will be addressing the long-term need there in the 2027 NFL Draft.

One player that is having a tremendous senior season is LSU safety Ty Benefield. Benefield is the son of Daved Benefield, who was a thirteen-year linebacker and All-Star player in the Canadian Football League. Ty was a backup player and pinch starter as a freshman for the Broncos in 2023 but earned a full-time role as a starter for his sophomore and junior campaigns.

In three seasons at Boise State, he played in 41 games with 33 starts and put up some eye-popping numbers: 235 tackles, 18 tackles for loss, 3 sacks, 5 interceptions, 4 fumble recoveries, and 2 forced fumbles. This year, he transferred to LSU to see if he could improve his draft stock and battle much tougher competition in the SEC.

So far, so good for Benefield (6'3", 216 pounds). In my opinion, has been one of the best defensive backs in the nation. In ten games, he has 49 tackles, 4.5 sacks and 3 interceptions. With some injuries in their secondary, he has seen time at both safety and nickelback. That positional versatility will be valued by NFL scouts and general managers and should be of interest to Denver giving the ambiguity at both positions moving forward.

His physical profile is off the charts, and he has consistently produced at a high level. I think he is one of the most well-rounded defensive backs in this class and a great fit for Joseph's aggressive defense. If he keeps dominating, I don't think being picked in the Top 50 is out of the question for him come spring. You can catch Benefield in action this afternoon when the Tigers take on the ninth-ranked Tennessee Volunteers on ABC.

I haven't covered too many running backs in Future Broncos. I remain quite bullish on the potential of Jonah Coleman and RJ Harvey as the future thunder and lightning duo for the offense. However, both have had some injuries this season and J.K. Dobbins has not looked as good as he did in the first half of 2025 prior to injury.

I kind of treat running back in drafts like MLB teams approach pitching. It's always worth pursuing and better to have and need than need and not have. Strong rushing attacks are what get you through the late half of the season and are extremely important come the playoffs.

A particular underclassman that has caught my eyes in a few games this season is Utah Utes junior Wayshawn Parker. Parker started his career out at Washington State. In twelve contests as a freshman, he had 137 carries for 735 yards and 4 touchdowns. He added 11 receptions for 108 yards and a touchdown as a receiver.

For his sophomore season, he transferred to Utah and saw an uptick in playing production. Parker had 981 yards and 6 touchdowns on 149 carries. His 6.6 yard per carry average was the best in the Big 12. He caught 13 passes for 185 yards and 3 touchdowns and had 1,166 all-purpose yards.

In 10 games this season, Parker (6'0″, 199 pounds) has 764 rushing yards and 9 rushing touchdowns. He also has been more involved in their passing attack under the new coaching regime and sports 29 catches for 269 yards and 4 touchdowns.

Based on what I've seen, he is one of the best draft-eligible backs when it comes to forcing missed tackles (49 last season) and getting yards after contact. He is a dual threat player that offers a lot of value as a runner and receiver out of the backfield. For those of you who are wondering about his pass protection? It's quite good for just a junior player.

I'm not sure Parker ends up declaring, but he is definitely on my radar. I think he would add a lot of juice and playmaking ability for the Broncos. He has been a big reason for Utah's success on offense this season. You can catch Parker and the second-ranked Utes in action when they take on TCU tomorrow evening.
