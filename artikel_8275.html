Top 10 Resorts in the West for Lodging (2027)

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

A memorable ski trip isn’t just about what happens on the mountain. Where you stay matters, too—whether that means rolling straight out of bed and onto the slopes, unwinding in a full-service hotel, or settling into a cozy condo with room for the whole crew. And the best ski destinations offer plenty of ways to make yourself at home, whatever your budget or vacation style.

These 10 Western resorts earned the highest marks from SKI readers for their lodging in our annual Reader Resort Survey. From slopeside stays to full-service retreats and everything in between, here’s where readers say you’ll find the best home base for your next ski vacation.
