Whether it’s the soft nugget-style ice or the large clear cube that looks great in a cocktail, there are likely some strong opinions on what makes the perfect ice and which ice makers can make just that. Ice makers—and especially countertop options—offer a convenient solution for RVing, camping, and tailgating. They can even act as substitute appliances for a broken ice machine in your fridge.

Now, thanks to better technology and plenty of demand, there are more choices than ever for producing restaurant-quality ice. We researched numerous options to find the best ice makers so shoppers can make their preferred shape of specialty ice for use in cocktails or for late-night crunching.

The GoveeLife Smart Nugget Ice Maker Pro is a high-capacity option for households that go through plenty of chewable ice. It starts producing ice in just 6 minutes and can make up to 60 pounds per day, keeping it cold in the attached 3.5-pound-capacity basket. The portable water tank holds 4.9 liters to reduce the frequency of refilling, and the unit offers Wi-Fi connectivity, app scheduling, and Alexa and Google Assistant compatibility so you can control production remotely. It also has two cleaning modes and app reminders that simplify maintenance.

Get the GoveeLife ice maker at Amazon, Walmart, or Govee.

The Frigidaire countertop ice maker is a budget-friendly option that still offers decent ice production and convenient features. This compact machine churns out 26 pounds of ice per day and starts producing ice within 6 minutes—rivaling more expensive models. Its 1.5-pound basket is on the smaller side, but the fast production helps make up for it. Operating at just 38 decibels, it’s quieter than many “quiet” dishwashers, and it has a bottom drain plug that makes releasing excess water quick and easy during cleanup.

Get the Frigidaire compact ice maker at Amazon, The Home Depot, Walmart, or Frigidaire.

The GE Profile Opal nugget ice maker sets the standard for countertop nugget ice. It starts producing ice in just 10 minutes and makes up to 38 pounds per day (1.6 pounds per hour), making it great for entertaining or anyone who enjoys crunching on ice throughout the day. Compatible with Alexa and Google, the Opal features Wi-Fi connectivity and smart controls, allowing you to monitor or start ice production remotely. While some reviewers note it can be noisy, its true nugget shape, fast production, and thousands of rave reviews make it an excellent pick.

What our tester says: “This ice maker has been a game changer for my family. When our built-in ice maker cannot keep up with constant smoothies, water jug refills, and iced coffee needs, the GE Profile Opal 2.0 steps in. The ice machine makes consistently great ice at a fast rate.”—Stephanie Cronk, Executive Editor of Product Reviews

Get the GE Profile ice maker at Amazon, Walmart, or GE.

The Easyera nugget ice maker is a great option for those who love nugget ice but don’t have room for a large machine. Taking up less than 1 square foot of space, it makes a whopping 33 pounds of ice in a day—enough for gatherings or several days of regular use. Its intuitive control panel lets you start production or cleaning with the push, and lights indicate when the bin is full or when to add water. While it doesn’t offer multiple ice sizes, the Easyera consistently delivers a generous amount of quality nugget ice.

The Euhomy Dart H1 is a compact, grab-and-go bullet ice maker ideal for small kitchens, offices, RVs, and dorms that need reliable ice without sacrificing counter space. It produces nine cubes in about 6 minutes and up to roughly 26 pounds per day, with a 1.3-pound basket and 1.2-liter tank that balance capacity and footprint. Operation is simple—fill the reservoir and press the Ice/Clean button—and indicator lights and a transparent lid make it easy to monitor. A 30-minute auto-cleaning function helps maintain hygiene and extend the machine’s life.

Get the Euhomy Dart ice maker at Amazon or Euhomy.

Igloo’s automatic self-cleaning ice maker is a compact countertop model with a built-in handle for easy portability between kitchens, patios, dorm rooms, RVs, or offices. It produces up to 26 lb of ice per day and makes nine bullet-shaped cubes in about 7 minutes. Its removable basket holds 1 to 3 pounds at a time, while its LED-lit control panel, size-selection button, and automatic five-cycle cleaning mode keep daily use simple.

The Frigidaire stainless steel ice maker is a freestanding countertop ice maker built for households that need a steady supply of ice without installing a built-in unit. It produces up to 26 pounds per day and delivers its first batch of bullet ice in about 6 to 8 minutes, with two size options to suit different drinks. A clear lid, simple status lights, large water reservoir, and self-cleaning function help keep daily use and maintenance easy.

Get the Frigidaire stainless steel ice maker at Amazon.

Perfect for fans of chewable “Sonic-style” ice in small spaces, the Euhomy Pearl H1 produces up to 34 pounds of nugget ice per day, with the first batch ready in about 6 minutes. Its compact design and built-in handle make it portable, while intuitive controls, status LEDs, a removable ice basket, and an automatic cleaning cycle simplify use and maintenance—keeping your nugget ice fresh for daily drinks and small gatherings.

Get the Euhomy Pearl ice maker at Amazon or Euhomy.
