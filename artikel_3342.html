Taylor Sheridan’s Landman Season 3 Is Officially Changing One Of Its Most Divisive Characters

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Landman season 3 will be changing one of its divisive characters.

It was announced in October that Landman season 3 was officially starting production in Fort Worth, Texas. Along with the confirmation that cameras were rolling on the hit Taylor Sheridan drama, several casting changes were reported. Colm Feore (Nathan), Mustafa Speaks (Theodore “Boss” Ramone), Bobbi Salvör Menuez (Paigyn), and Francesca Xuereb (Cheyenne) have all been upgraded to series regulars after previously recurring in varying capacities.

This means that these previously supporting characters will get a bigger spotlight moving forward, including Paigyn as the non-binary roommate of Michelle Randolph's Ainsley Norris, but it also suggests that Ainsley herself may be getting more personalized stories when the Paramount+ breakout finally returns.

In comments to WWD, while promoting her new Prime Video romantic comedy Clashing Through the Snow, Randolph offered a small preview of what audiences can expect from Ainsley once Landman returns. In particular, in the quote below, the actor talks about how she finally has a chance to play Ainsley entering college life and away from just being the daughter character:

Michelle Randolph: I love this season. Ainsley started out as 17, now she’s 18, and she’s now in college for this season. And so I’ve gotten to actually figure out who Ainsley is not around her family. As an 18-year-old, she’s figuring out her identity and I’m having a lot of fun playing with that instead of just playing the daughter. I don’t have to say mama and daddy this year. I’m happy about that.

Randolph's comments may be brief, but they point back to a major issue that viewers have had with Ainsley since the start of Landman. In those early season 1 episodes, Ainsley is seen bantering with her father, Tommy Norris (Billy Bob Thornton), about her sex life in some very blunt terms. Male characters would often leer at Ainsley even though, as Randolph says, the character is only 17 years old.

The approach was divisive. Some pointed to it as an example of Sheridan's weak spot in terms of writing female characters. Others argued that some of the behavior around Ainsley was true to life, and that the depiction didn't necessarily point to an endorsement of the behavior. Both sides tended to agree that it wasn't the best fit for Randolph.

The ending of Landman season 2 already demonstrated that the series is taking steps to move Ainsley away from being just the daughter of Tommy and Angela (Ali Larter). She finds common ground with Paigyn, despite their frosty introduction. Paigyn helps Ainsley with her cheerleading ambitions, and Ainsley stands up for Paigyn against some bigoted bullies.

That relationship is poised for continued examination in season 3. But it's not the only one. As seen in the table above, Feore is newly part of the Landman main cast as Nathan, an oil company lawyer and treasurer of Tommy's new venture. Speaks has also been upgraded as Theodore “Boss” Ramone, a seasoned member of Tommy's crew, as has Xuereb in the role of Cheyenne, a stripper Tommy hires to help rehabilitate his father, T.L. That signals a bigger role for Sam Elliott.

Landman season 3 doesn't yet have a premiere date announced. But for those hoping to see Randolph in a different project, her romantic comedy movie Clashing Through the Snow is currently streaming on Prime Video. She stars opposite The Summer I Turned Pretty alum Christopher Briney. They play competing publishers eager to secure a buzzy book.
