Jonny Greenwood and Dudu Tassa Return With New Album and Lead Single

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Jonny Greenwood and Israeli rock singer Dudu Tassa have reunited for Jalsa, their new collaborative album. The album is set for release on Dec. 4, but the pair unveiled Tuesday the lead single “Ya Hala Bid-Dayf” assisted by Emirati singer Zeina.

Jalsa is the second installment in the duo’s Jarak Qaribak project, which aims to celebrate Middle-Eastern music and artists through collaborative works across the region. The first album, Jarak Qaribak (“Your Neighbor Is Your Friend”), was released in 2023.

Lead single “Ya Hala Bid-Dayf” reimagines the Lebanese song with featured vocals from Zeina, who hails from the United Arab Emirates. “Singing this song was a special honor for me,” she said in a statement. “I feel that bringing such a timeless melody to a new generation is a beautiful way of keeping our musical heritage alive. Music has no borders, and this project is a wonderful celebration of that.”

“I’ve loved this music for 30 years, and I am happy to be finally working with people devoted to it,” Greenwood notes. Beyond his work with Tassa, Greenwood has kept busy since Radiohead’s most recent album A Moon Shaped Pool. Greenwood, Radiohead frontman Thom Yorke, and Tom Skinner formed the Smile in 2018 and have released three records under the side project. Last year, he continued his collaborative relationship with director Paul Thomas Anderson by scoring his Best Picture-winning film One Battle After Another; Greenwood was nominated for Best Original Score, but lost to Ludwig Göransson’s Sinners. Tassa released his most recent album, אי, this February.
