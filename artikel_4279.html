Hill Climb Racing 2 gives you new adventure tracks, lore and more to celebrate ten years

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

If you're a fan of wacky, physics-based racers, more than likely you've spent plenty of time with Fingersoft's Hill Climb Racing franchise. Now, you can celebrate ten years of Hill Climb Racing 2, one of their most popular releases, with an entire month of exciting in-game events for you to sink your teeth into, kicking off today!

The celebrations in Hill Climb Racing 2 kick off with Adventure Week, which adds two new tracks, The Great Below and The Climb (no prizes for guessing what the gimmick of these tracks is). Shocking week follows right after, and promises both a new event type and 'special anniversary surprises' we're not yet privy to. All this, and a new mode too, but things don't stop there!

Finally, things wrap up with Conspiracy week, and it's a double-header here with both a UFO-themed event and a purported sneak peek at what Hill Climb Racing 2 has in store going forward through the rest of 2026 and beyond.

And of course, an anniversary event means there'll be plenty of free goodies to grab throughout the week. Hill Climb Racing 2 presents a pretty great way to celebrate a decade, and the Conspiracy event particularly grabs my attention. After all, who doesn't like the fun of UFOs and all that entails?
