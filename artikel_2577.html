Nasty weather expected for Bills-Chiefs Thanksgiving night showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Plan your Thanksgiving fantasy football decisions accordingly.

Nasty weather is expected Thursday night at Highmark Stadium in Orchard Park, N.Y. for the Chiefs-Bills showdown that will have great bearing on the AFC playoff picture.

There is a 90 percent chance of rain that will accumulate to roughly a half inch, along with expected winds of 15-25 mph with "higher wind gusts possible," per weather.com, as of Wednesday morning.

Buffalo Bills quarterback Josh Allen (17) walks off the field after an NFL football game against the Miami Dolphins in Orchard Park, N.Y., Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Matt Rourke

The projected weather in Western New York falls in line with other expected poor atmospheres Thursday, including New York City where the Macy's Parade will be affected.

The Bills have certainly hosted their fair share of weather-affected games in franchise history but it's usually snow, not rain, that leaves its mark.

The slippery conditions certainly add a unique layer to a game that could have Super Bowl ramifications.

Buffalo (9-1) enters the game as the AFC's No. 1 seed, one game ahead of the AFC West-leading Broncos (8-2) and the Chiefs (8-2).

The Chiefs and Broncos have the same division record and split their two meetings this season, but Denver holds the edge due to its record against common opponents.

The Bills are going to cruise to the AFC East title with the Dolphins and Jets not contending and Drake Maye's potential season-ending injury crushing the Patriots' chances, but this game and their Week 16 road game against the Broncos could determine their playoff seed.

Kansas City Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes (15) rolls out to pass against the Arizona Cardinals in Kansas City, Mo., Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Ed Zurga

Beyond the known incentive of hosting a playoff game, Buffalo also has only hosted Kansas City in the playoffs one time in their four postseason meetings this decade — and haven't won any of the matchups.

A common refrain heard in Western New York is how those games play out in Buffalo instead of Missouri.

How the Chiefs fare against the Bills could also determine if they can outlast the Broncos in the AFC West, especially since the Broncos get to host Buffalo later in the year.

The weather could also potentially affect the status of Kansas City star running back Kenneth Walker III after he missed the last two games due to a groin injury.

The Chiefs could potentially hold him back in awful conditions to prevent worsening an injury, instead allowing him an extra 10 days to heal before returning.
