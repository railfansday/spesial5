Varsity Voices breaks down Section V football championship weekend

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Only five Section V football teams remain.

Democrat and Chronicle high school sports reporters James Johnson, Justin Ritzel and Jeff Uveino recap everything they saw across an action-packed weekend of football at St. John Fisher University.
