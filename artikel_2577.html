Clock operator robbed Bengals of one final play

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Bengals-Commanders game carries a small but not insignificant asterisk.

As ESPN rules analyst Russell Yurk explained in the postgame show, the clock operator incorrectly ran the remaining four seconds off the clock during the game-winning field goal from Washington.

Yurk said two seconds remained when the ball hit the net. It looks more like it should have been one second.

Regardless, Yurk said the replay process can’t restore time when the next play isn’t a scrimmage play. (There’s also a rule regarding restoring time when there are fewer than two seconds left, and — with all due respect to Yurk — it appears that there was only one second when the ball hit the net.)

The bottom line is that the clock operator was slow to get to the button, and there wasn’t anything that could be done about it.

What would have happened if the Bengals had gotten one last chance? We’ll never know, because the person hired to keep track of the time handled it incorrectly.

Or, depending on that person’s rooting interests, he handled it perfectly.
