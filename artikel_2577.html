Women's basketball AP poll: South Carolina remains No. 1 as No. 7 UConn prepares for critical week

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

This will be a huge week for Geno Auriemma and the Huskies.

And while it is still just the first month of the season, back-to-back losses for UConn this week — something that is absolutely on the table over the Thanksgiving holiday — would put the Huskies in a position not often seen from Auriemma-led teams.

The Huskies will first go up against No. 1 South Carolina on Tuesday night. The Gamecocks, still a perfect 6-0 on the season, remained the top-ranked team in the country in the latest Associated Press poll on Monday.

Auriemma revealed on Sunday that he hasn't spoken to South Carolina coach Dawn Staley since their heated incident at the Final Four last spring. The two coaches got into a wild exchange while approaching for a postgame handshake after the Gamecocks' win, which sent them into the national title game.

"I'm not playing, so I'm just trying to coach my team because they're the ones playing," Auriemma said, via ESPN's Alexa Philippou. "And that's what people are coming to watch, I think. They're coming to watch South Carolina and Connecticut play against each other. … I don't coach against other coaches. I coach against other players.

UConn, still No. 7 in the latest poll, will then have to take on No. 5 Duke just four days later at TD Garden. The Blue Devils are also entering this week at an undefeated 6-0, though their schedule has been far less daunting than either UConn's or South Carolina's. Duke has yet to play a ranked opponent, and has won every game easily by double digits. The closest game so far was a 16-point win over Green Bay.

But that doesn't mean Duke isn't capable of beating UConn, especially a UConn team with three losses, should it not be able to get past Staley and the Gamecocks. Blue Devils star Toby Fournier is averaging 15.8 points while shooting 70% from the field, which is the fourth-best output of anyone in the country.

Michigan remained at No. 2 in Monday's poll. Louisville held at No. 3 and USC came in at No. 4. The top five was actually unchanged from last week, as nobody in that group lost once.

UConn fell first to the Wolverines in the opening week of the season, and then got blown out by the Cardinals on the road a few weeks later. If things don't go well this week, the Huskies could be a shocking 4-4 entering December. But if Auriemma can turn things around and get through the week unscathed, he will have reminded the country that his group is still very much a national title contender.
