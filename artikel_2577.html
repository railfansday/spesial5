How Jimmy Coenraets adjusted his coaching to lead Utah to historic playoff berth

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

“If having fun, having trust in each other and the plan has led us to reaching the playoffs, let’s keep doing that”

The Utah Royals are in the National Women’s Soccer League playoffs. It is a sentence many did not expect to say at the start of this season. Head coach Jimmy Coenraets led his team’s turnaround by learning a different way to lead and building a team that believes it belongs.

Coenraets arrived in Utah with a detailed vision for how the Royals should play. He had a presentation of about 150 slides covering tactics, game plan and pretty much everything he had learned from coaching OH Leuven in Belgium, where he masterminded turning a struggling team into one of the country’s top sides.

He was initially hired by the Royals as an assistant coach but was elevated to interim head coach a month later, in July 2024. Just 20 days after taking over the lead mantle, he threw his detailed plan in the trash.

Days before Decision Day, Coenraets sat down with The Equalizer, sun on his face and blue skies stretching above him. He appeared relaxed, although there was a pensive way in how he spoke about what his team had accomplished and the challenge ahead in the playoffs.

The Royals were preparing to face Gotham FC, with the possibility of a win ensuring quarterfinal hosting rights. They went on to lose 0-2, as Gotham clinched a historic Shield at home. But Utah had already secured their first playoff qualification in franchise history.

“Overall, I think everyone’s happy,” Coenaerts said, describing the mood around the club. “But I do feel like there is a next feeling as well in the building.”

The next feeling is ambition. Utah was not expected to be anywhere near the playoff spots. At the beginning of the season, predictions from NWSL experts placed them last or second-to-last. After clinching their place, those predictions became a source of amusement in the team’s group chats.

The greatness of the achievement, though, for the ambitious coach is partially in the question of what’s next.
