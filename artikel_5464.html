Pixelworks Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

PORTLAND, Ore., Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Pixelworks, Inc. (NASDAQ: PXLW) ("Pixelworks" or the "Company"), a provider of innovative cinematic and enhanced visualization solutions, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

"During the third quarter, we continued to execute on our strategy of building and scaling a pure-play technology licensing business centered on TrueCut Motion and our visualization solutions," stated Todd DeBonis, Chairman and CEO of Pixelworks. "This included steady progress toward our near-term objectives of growing the TrueCut Motion ecosystem of premium large-format exhibitors and expanding the availability of cinematic TrueCut Motion-graded content.

"Most recently, we achieved a major milestone with Meta becoming our first major device manufacturer customer for TrueCut Motion device certification. This multi-year agreement, which is ahead of the original timeline for our device licensing program, will bring unmatched cinematic clarity and highly immersive content to Meta's VR devices. We also believe this partnership will meaningfully accelerate the pipeline of new TrueCut Motion-graded titles. Importantly, we remain well capitalized to execute on our strategy and continue driving growing momentum for our differentiated TrueCut Motion content across both theatrical and device experiences."

Pixelworks has more than 20 years of delivering image processing innovation to leading providers of consumer electronics, professional displays and video streaming services.

As previously announced, on January 6, 2026, the Company completed the transaction to sell its shares in Pixelworks Semiconductor Technology (Shanghai) Co., Ltd., a subsidiary of Pixelworks ("Pixelworks Shanghai"), to a special purpose entity led by VeriSilicon Microelectronics (Shanghai). The contribution from the Pixelworks Shanghai semiconductor subsidiary to the operating results of the Company for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 was determined to be immaterial. Therefore, the Company's reported financial results contained in today's press release do not include discontinued operations activity from the first several days of January 2026 before the sale closed.
