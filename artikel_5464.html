Innovaccer expands Gravity Voice AI agents with SpaceXSI’s Grok Voice model

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Healthcare artificial intelligence company Innovaccer Inc. today announced that it has expanded its Gravity Voice AI agents with Grok Voice, the speech-to-speech model from Elon Musk’s artificial intelligence company SpaceXSI, formerly xAI.

The integration is aimed at the routine calls that make up much of a health system’s phone traffic, such as booking a visit, refilling a prescription or asking about a bill.

Grok Voice is a speech-to-speech model from SpaceXSI that is said to offer subsecond time to first audio, native tool use and the ability to reason while it speaks. According to Innovaccer, the model was trained on real phone audio with interruptions, corrections and background noise in it. More than 25 languages are supported. A business associate agreement is available for Grok Voice, which is Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act-eligible.

Coming into the expansion, Innovaccer’s voice business has grown quickly. Patient voice interactions on the platform have gone from roughly 250,000 to more than five million in the six months since the agents went live. Customers using them are said to be booking significantly more appointments, with referral intakes tripled and documentation time per case down 90%.

“Healthcare Voice AI is moving from answering questions to doing work,” co-founder and Chief Executive Abhinav Shashank said. With Grok Voice, he said, the company is extending where its agents work across the healthcare journey.

Innovaccer’s Agents of Care on Gravity approach is designed so that software handles the bulk of administrative calls, such as scheduling and refills. A human stays in the loop on clinical conversations.

Existing customers will get Grok Voice first through upgrades, along with new customer deployments. Innovaccer plans to use it in scheduling, referrals, prior authorizations, refill requests, chronic care check-ins and annual wellness visit outreach, among other tasks.

The integration is set to extend beyond voice over time. Innovaccer says it will bring Grok models to its healthcare data through the Grok API, with plans for SpaceXSI’s Grok Build coding agent and Grok Bot AI agent as well. Healthcare-specific training and evaluation are to follow once the voice integration is live.

The integration comes about six weeks after SpaceXSI released Grok Voice 3.0, the latest version of the model. At the time, SpaceXSI said Starlink uses Grok Voice to answer about 20,000 customer calls a day, with roughly 70% resolved without a human. The SpaceXSI name was adopted earlier in October, following SpaceX’s acquisition of xAI in February and a rename to SpaceXAI in July.

Innovaccer is a venture capital-backed company that has raised about $675 million to date, including a $275 million Series F round in January 2025. Notable customers include Orlando Health, Adventist HealthCare and Banner Health.

Gravity Voice AI agents with Grok Voice capabilities are available now.
