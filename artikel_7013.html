Hailey Bieber and Bella Hadid Make a Case For “Coyote Chai” Colored Fur Coats

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Chocolate brown’s cooler cousin has officially entered the chat.

Move over, chocolate brown: There's a new shade taking over the celebrity style scene, and it's honestly the perfect shade for transitioning from fall to winter. Enter what I’m coining Coyote Chai, the deliciously rich, multi-tonal brown fur coat trend that Bella Hadid and Hailey Bieber are making a serious case for right now.

If winter dressing had a moodboard, these two would be running it. And while their personal aesthetics might differ, they've seemingly landed on the same outerwear obsession: fluffy, statement-making coats in a gorgeous mix of earthy browns, caramel, taupe, and deep brown.

Bella stepped out in a fuzzy, sandy-brown jacket paired with dark-wash denim and heels, alongside a brown Coach purse. Meanwhile, Hailey took a similarly tonal approach in New York, wearing a cropped, textured fur-effect jacket with warm brown, honey, and gray undertones, styled with classic jeans and barely-there black heels.

While it feels like chocolate brown has been the star of the show, this shift into what I’m calling Coyote Chai feels like the rightful move as we head closer to winter, brightening the sartorial vibes as it gets gloomier outside. This shade of brown is the cooler, blended cousin of espresso and caramel shades. As a non-coffee drinker and chai latte warrior, this light brown shade blended with hints of darker spots is the perfect layering style coat to be bundled up in. And as Bieber and Hadid show, the coat makes the simplest of outfits all the more interesting.

Consider this your sign to grab a good coat for winter, and maybe make it in this fun Coyote Chai shade.
