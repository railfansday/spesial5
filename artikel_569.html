Gerald R. Molen, Oscar-Winning ‘Schindler’s List’ Producer, Dies at 91

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Gerald R. Molen, the Oscar-winning producer behind Steven Spielberg’s “Schindler’s List,” has died. He was 91.

Molen’s death was announced by his great-niece, Rachel Lorraine Pullan, in an Instagram post on Sunday.

“This morning my great-uncle, Gerald R. Molen, passed on to join my great-grandma and his dear wife Pat on the other side of the veil. I am so happy for him, and also so very sad,” she wrote. “Uncle Jerry was a man of great moral character and high integrity. Molen frequently collaborated with Spielberg throughout his career. He served as producer on “Jurassic Park,” “Minority Report,” “Hook” and “The Lost World: Jurassic Park,” as well as “Schindler’s List,” which won best picture in 1994. Molen shared the Oscar with Spielberg and Branko Lustig. Molen also worked on several Amblin Entertainment films, such as “The Flintstones,” “The Little Rascals,” “Little Giants,” “Casper” and “Twister.”

Molen’s working relationship with Spielberg began in 1985 when he worked as a unit production manager on “The Color Purple.” That job led to an associate producer role on 1987’s “*batteries not included,” on which Spielberg served as executive producer.

Molen’s other producer credits include “Rain Man,” “Days of Thunder,” “Just Like Heaven,” “The Meg,” “Meg 2” and “The Best Christmas Pageant Ever.”

Born in Montana in 1935, Molen moved to North Hollywood in 1947. His parents, Gerald Sr. and Edith, owned and operated a cafe next to the Republic Pictures lot in Studio City.

Molen’s wife of almost 70 years, Pat, died in January 2024 at 86 years old.
