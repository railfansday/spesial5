Danny Boyle Backs Jack O’Connell as a Bond Villain, but Says He ‘Would Have Been Great’ as 007

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Danny Boyle has thrown his support behind his “Ink” star Jack O’Connell as the man to battle Robert Pattinson‘s James Bond in the next 007 movie.

The director — who himself was due to direct the last Bond “No Time to Die” before leaving over creative differences — has acknowledged that with the lead role now cast, people had been “beginning to talk about Jack O’Connell as a possible villain.” Related Stories Mike Newell Recalls Being Shot at in Colombia While Filming ‘Love in the Time of Cholera’ With Giovanna Mezzogiorno: ‘She Was Like, “F— All This!”’

Speaking on the Alchemy with Anthony Mason podcast, he claimed that both O’Connell and Pattinson possessed a rare quality. “They have that thing [where] you want to watch them,” he said. “And it’s impossible to quantify what it is, otherwise we’d all buy it for ourselves. But it’s just there and you want to follow them.”

However, he noted that O’Connell, who was believed to be among those considered for the role of 007, “would have been a great Bond as well.”

As it turns out, back in 2019, less than a year after leaving the 007 franchise, Boyle actually suggested that Pattinson should replace Daniel Craig, having seen his performance in Claire Denis’ “High Life.” Speaking to The Guardian at the time, he said “It was so bizarre, because I was sitting there thinking: ‘Oh my God, they should get him to be the next Bond.’”

Seven years on and with Pattinson finally anointed as the latest to join His Majesty’s Secret Service, Boyle noted that he could sense his capabilities for the role even further back.

“He’s a movie star, and I could see that from the first ‘Twilight’ films. Him and Kristen Stewart, the two of them. You thought: they’re movie stars. It’s like a given,” he said. “So there’s not that many of them out there. And having a British pedigree will always help him in that world as well.”
