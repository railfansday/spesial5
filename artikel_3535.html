H3 Podcast's Ethan Klein Breaks Silence on Affair, Apologizes to Wife Hila

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

H3 Podcast cohost Ethan Klein is breaking his silence after his wife and costar, Hila Klein, accused of having a six-month affair.

In a statement posted via his Instagram Stories on Tuesday, November 24, Ethan, 41, admitted to his infidelity, apologizing to wife Hila, 38, for the end of their marriage, as well as the end of their long-running podcast.

“Let me begin by apologizing for my long silence. I’ve wanted to take my time to make my comments as thoughtful and sincere as possible,” he wrote. “Truthfully, I still have a lot to process and I still don’t fully understand many of the decisions I’ve made, but suffice to say this for now: I am sorry for the pain I’ve caused. It was selfish, pathetic, surreal… It was the ultimate betrayal to my wife, myself, and you, the audience.”

Ethan continued, “The disappointment and despair I’ve caused is immense, and I have so much to reckon with in coming to terms with what happened. I’m most sorry to Hila, to whom the word ‘sorry’ likely has the weight of ash, and so I will refrain from getting too deep into apologizing to her here. I could never say or do enough to make up for what I have put her through. We are coparents now, and even in her righteous indignation, she has been beyond graceful in her approach to me as her children’s father, and I’m so grateful to her for that.”

“Regarding the affair, my explanation and involvement will come in time as I feel ready to maturely and thoughtfully address it with the seriousness it deserves,” he shared.

Ethan went on to address the future of the “H3 Podcast” after Hila said earlier this week she would be stepping away from the show amid her divorce from Ethan.

The podcaster said that “after the crew learned of the shocking and painful revelation of my infidelity, they had all decided, understandably so, that they would not continue to work with me. And so they, along with Hila, decided to take a shot at producing the show without me.”

“I supported this fully and stepped away, and from this moment I have been uninvolved in the politics, decision-making, or show planning that took place,” he added.

Ultimately, Hila announced on Monday, November 23, that she would not be going forward with a new version of the “H3 Podcast,” effectively ending the show.

“I want to be completely clear: the show ending is entirely my fault, the responsibility of this rests on no one’s shoulders but my own,” Ethan wrote. “The affair was entirely my fault. I was the boss and the elder, it was always my responsibility to stop it before it escalated. None of this would be occurring without my catastrophic failure as a husband and boss. I owe everyone an enormous apology. My wife, the crew, the audience, my family… I’ve let so many down. It is, without a single reservation, entirely my fault.”

Ethan concluded his statement by thanking fans who have followed the “H3 Podcast” since it launched in 2016.

“To the fans: I’ve let you down in a way you never expected possible,” he wrote. “To be perfectly frank, in a way I never thought possible, either. Emotionally, I am still reeling and I wish I had some explanation for you to make the pain and disappointment go down easier, but the sad reality is I don’t. Not for you or for myself. I was selfish, dishonest, shallow, treacherous, hypocritical, callous, reckless, insecure, desperate and pathetic.”

He added, “I’m facing someone new in the mirror, a stranger, someone who is capable of lying and cheating and sneaking.. is he new or was he always here, dormant? I don’t know. But the love and dedication I’ve had for you guys all these years has always been real, and I miss you all very much. I’ve been swept away on something I never saw coming. And it’s taken me really far away from myself in my own sort of midlife crisis Odyssey.. castaway far from home and unsure if I’ll ever make it back.”

Hila, who shares three children with Ethan, filed for divorce from her husband earlier this month.
