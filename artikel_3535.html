Critical Role Returns With the Mighty Nein Season 2 in 2027, but Fans Aren’t Happy

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

More Mighty Nein is never a bad thing, but this latest news has left Critters despondent

It's always a great time to be a Critical Role fan, and 2027 will be no different, with several new live-play shows and series planned. However, things are about to get even better, thanks to a new announcement made by Critical Role during TwitchCon 2026: The Mighty Nein season 2 will arrive on Prime Video on March 24, 2027.

The news was announced during a special live Lord of the Rings one-shot, when Game Master Liam O’Brien asked fans to raise a cup to celebrate The Mighty Nein’s return.

However, the good news came with a worrying catch. According to the official news release, the episode release schedule for The Mighty Nein will be very different from that of its first season — and Critters are concerned about what this could mean for the show's future.

Season 2 of The Mighty Nein will consist of eight episodes, all of which will be released at once when the season debuts on Mar. 24, 2027. This contrasts sharply with the release schedule for season 1, which released its first three episodes on Nov. 19, 2025, followed by weekly episodes through Dec. 22, 2025.

The change has left Critters speculating whether Prime Video — the publisher and partner behind The Mighty Nein and Critical Role’s other animated series, The Legend of Vox Machina — has lost faith in Critical Role’s animated tales set in Exandria.

“God, I hope not,” one user posted on social media platform X, referring to all episodes of The Mighty Nein dropping at once. “Publishers have to know by now that dropping everything at once instantly kills a series online presence?”

“[It] makes me sad and I hope also isn't a choice that's at all indicative of Amazon's faith in the series or a sign s1 underperformed,” another user posted. “Really hope it means nothing at all and they just thought it'd do better bc tm9 is genuinely so good so far.”

It’s impossible to know what Prime Video is thinking about its binge release of The Mighty Nein, but Critters aren’t wrong. This type of release rarely sparks discussion the way a week-to-week release does.

Primve Video’s news release for season 2 of The Mighty Nein included a plot summary, which you can read below.

“In Season Two, The Mighty Nein set off on a pirate adventure across the high seas as they face their complicated pasts and hurtle toward a treacherous future. While Fjord’s burgeoning powers tempt him down a dark path, the Beacon reveals its own enticing possibilities, and the group must choose between newfound magic and their friendship.”

The first season of The Mighty Nein already featured plenty of changes, including a revised backstory for Aasimar barbarian Yasha (Ashley Johnson). There seem to be even more changes this time around, with the group’s pirate arc taking center stage earlier than expected. We wonder whether this means certain characters will stick around longer than they were originally established in the actual-play Dungeons & Dragons campaign from which The Mighty Nein is adapted. Only time will tell.

The Mighty Nein airs exclusively on Prime Video. Season 1 is available to watch now, with season 2 set to debut on Mar. 24, 2027.
