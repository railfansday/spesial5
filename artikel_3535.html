Will Josh O’Connor Be The Next James Bond?

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Let’s clear up any potential misunderstandings: there are no official announcements on who will play James Bond at this time. But a British tabloid says that a conversation at a pub has revealed the top-secret casting—if an anonymously sourced rumor is to be believed.

A long list of stars are reportedly auditioning for the 007 role, but even former James Bond Pierce Brosnan says the casting process is a mystery. “I’m just the man in the street who is eager to see who is going to play this epic role of cinematic history,” Brosnan tells Vanity Fair. “All the men who are on the list now are actors I admire.”

According to UK publication The Sun, the decision is rumored to have been made: a recent colleague of Josh O’Connor, a 36-year-old actor from Cheltenham, Gloucestershire, says Disclosure Day star has been cast in the role. If true, Turner beat out likely contenders including Callum Turner and Aaron Taylor-Johnson.

Josh O'Connor at UK premiere for Disclosure Day on June 04, 2026.

The news comes as a bit of a game of telephone. Until last week, O'Connor was in the case of Golden Boy, Clifford Odets' 1937 drama about a violin player turned boxer in Depression-era New York City. Citing the claims of one of O'Connor's cast mates, an unnamed source tells The Sun “I bumped into one of Josh’s Golden Boy cast mates at The French House."

“Out of nowhere, and oiled by a few glasses of lager, they blurted out that Josh was the next James Bond."

When the source pushed back, the thespian grew more insistent. “They said that he had confided in them about getting the role when the cast of Golden Boy had celebratory drinks after the play finished its run on Halloween," a claim that implies the decision has been made for over a week.

Josh O'Connor, winner of the Outstanding Lead Actor In A Drama Series award for The Crown, at the 73rd Primetime Emmy Awards on September 19, 2021.

If this rumor comes to fruition, it would not be the first time O'Connor took on an iconic UK role. In 2021, he took home the outstanding lead actor Emmy for his role as then-Prince, now-King Charles III, in Netflix series The Crown. While in his role as the future king, he was encouraged to tap into a side that one might not expect.

“It’s Prince Charles as Walter White," he told The Guardian in 2020, making a reference to crime series Breaking Bad. "Our job was to make people feel sorry for [Charles], so that in season four, when he turns into this at times monstrous character, we understand how he got there.” A heel turn that isn't unBond, especially the darker Bond we grew used to with Daniel Craig. (Craig, in fact, has a direct connection to O’Connor, as the pair co-starred in Knives Out: Wake Up Dead Man.)

Speaking with the BBC, O'Connor did not confirm the casting rumor—but he didn't deny it either. (Vanity Fair has reached out to O'Connor's representatives for comment, but has not yet received a response.)

"To be considered in that same sort of bracket is extraordinary," O'Connor said this week.

"It's an incredibly bizarre pinch me moment...even being uttered in that same sentence and being considered for something like that. But I am afraid, there is nothing to say beyond that."
