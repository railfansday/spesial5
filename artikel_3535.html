Gabriel Iglesias' Rep Gives Update After Comic Fell Ill, Needed Help Off Stage

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Gabriel “Fluffy” Iglesias‘ spokesperson is addressing the comedian’s condition after he fell ill and had to be helped off stage during a Raleigh, North Carolina, show.

“Gabe is doing much better today,” his rep told Us on Monday, November 23.

Hours earlier, a viral video circulated via social media, showing alarming scenes at the comic’s Sunday, November 22, performance at the Lenovo Center. Iglesias could be seen hunched over a stool on stage, calling for opener Martin Moreno to help him away. (TMZ was the first to report the news.)

An edit in the clip cut to Iglesias, 50, receiving oxygen just off stage moments later as Moreno, 58, closed the show by explaining that the headline act wasn’t feeling well. Iglesias had earlier mentioned near the beginning of his set that he was under the weather.

“[Gabriel] has been sick the last week or so,” the spokesperson confirmed to Us. “He didn’t want to cancel on his fans, so he tried to pull through to give them a great show.”

Iglesias nor Moreno have yet to personally address the incident from Sunday’s live show.

Fellow comedian Dane Cook publicly reached out to Iglesias via Instagram on Monday, revealing that he’d heard about the concerning viral video while offering his fellow comedian his support.

“Just heard the news about Gabriel Iglesias,” Cook, 54, said in an Instagram Reel. “Fluffy, we love you. I hope everything’s OK. Please, everybody, send nothing but positive thoughts and prayers.”

Cook encouraged his longtime friend to take the necessary time to recover once he is feeling better.

“Gabriel Iglesias is one of the good guys,” Cook added. “This dude just sold out SoFi Stadium. He took standup comedy to unbelievable heights, and we want him around for a long time. Fluffy, get better, and then take a vacation! OK? I’ll go with you. And I don’t even like to vacation either, but we’ll go together. Cook and Fluffy, the Do Nothing Tour — on a beach!”

As Cook mentioned, Iglesias achieved a rare feat for a standup comic by selling out SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, alongside fellow comedian Jo Koy for a special “One Night Only” event in March.

Iglesias has since embarked on his 1976 Tour: A Fluffy Celebration, named in honor of the year of his birth. He is scheduled to perform next at the FedEx Forum in Memphis on Saturday, November 28, before wrapping up his 2026 live dates in Melbourne, Australia, at the Rod Laver Arena on Saturday, December 12.

The comic is scheduled to hit the road for more 1976 Tour: A Fluffy Celebration gigs in the New Year, starting with four nights at El Paso’s Plaza Theater from Thursday, January 7, 2027 through Sunday, January 10, 2027.

Iglesias has been performing live comedy since 1997, having parlayed his stage success to supporting roles in the Magic Mike movies and his own Netflix sitcom, Mr. Iglesias. He lent his voice to Disney’s Oscar-winning animated hit Coco and portrayed Looney Tunes character Speedy Gonzales in 2021’s Space Jam: A New Legacy.
