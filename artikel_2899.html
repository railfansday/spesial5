Why wild 49ers anomaly ought to put fright into Vikings fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The 49ers have seemingly been hit with injuries more than any other NFL team in recent years.

So it's notable any time they have a relatively clean bill of health.

Dre Greenlaw was scheduled to practice Friday. Getty Images

That was the case on Friday as Kyle Shanahan's team had something extremely rare happen: All 53 players on the active roster participated in practice ahead of their key game against the Vikings, with the winner earning the No. 1 seed in the NFC through the first 11 weeks.

That includes star receiver Mike Evans, who is officially listed as questionable for Sunday night's key game in Mexico City after missing the last three contests with Achilles tendinitis.

Having all active players at practice doesn't mean the 49ers are completely healthy, but it's a step in the right direction. They didn't rule any players out for the Vikings game on Friday.

The team is still listing Evans, linebacker Dre Greenlaw (neck), defensive tackle C.J. West (knee), cornerback Nate Hobbs (adductor), cornerback Upton Stout (shoulder, neck), pass rusher Khalil Kareem (ankle), backup quarterback Mac Jones (illness) and linebacker Luke Gifford (chest) as questionable.

San Francisco 49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan looks on during the second quarter against the Washington Commanders at Levi's Stadium in Santa Clara, California, Monday, Oct. 19, 2026. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

There remains some uncertainty regarding West and Hobbs, who remain on injured reserve. They could be activated before the game. The 21-day practice window for Hobbs closes on Wednesday, while West has until Dec. 2.

Additionally, the 49ers have three of their eight return designations from injured reserve available. Candidates for those include rookie receiver De'Zhaun Stribling, second-year linebacker Nick Martin, running back Isaac Guerendo and pass rusher Sam Okuayinonu.
