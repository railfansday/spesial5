New details on Keyonte George’s injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Utah Jazz are in the midst of a 12-game losing streak, and they're struggling under the weight of their losses. There have been many reasons the Jazz haven't been winning, but one of the biggest is injuries. On a nightly basis, the Jazz are without important, core players, and it's usually 3-4 of them every game.

That's why, when Keyonte George went down with an ankle injury, it felt like an absolute dagger to the Jazz's chances of winning any game anytime soon. After missing one game, Keyonte George returned and didn't look quite right, but it was clear he was battling through something.

Ben Anderson wrote about the toll losing was taking on the team, especially Keyonte George.

It's a great read with a lot of information about the team and how they're handling the losses. But this note from the article about George's ankle stood out to me.

He suffered a sprained ankle in the team's November 8 loss to the Minnesota Timberwolves, unforgivingly on his 23rd birthday. The injury forced him to miss only one game, though his absence should have been longer.

The fourth-year guard has struggled since returning, averaging 17.7 points on 33 percent shooting over his last three appearances. Prior to the injury, George had averaged 21.1 points per game in 10 appearances while connecting on 45 percent of his attempts.

Clearly, George is battling through ankle pain, and it's making it tough for him to be efficient on offense and defend effectively. That said, you have to respect the toughness to stay on the floor and go for a win. Hopefully, George can get back to 100% and his incredible numbers from the first few games, and Utah can overcome what has been a terrible start to the season.
