LIVE | Week 11 Texas high school football scores across Texoma

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The final week of the Texas high school football regular season has arrived, with district championships and playoff positioning on the line across Texoma.

Week 11 is headlined by the final Wichita Falls city matchup of the season, as Memorial faces Legacy on Friday at Memorial Stadium.

Graham hosts Iowa Park in District 4-4A Division II, while Burkburnett travels to Bridgeport and Brock visits Mineral Wells. Vernon hosts Peaster in District 4-3A Division I, Jacksboro welcomes City View and Henrietta hosts Breckenridge in District 4-3A Division II.

The smaller-school schedule includes Munday at Windthorst, Electra at Archer City, Olney at Muenster and Nocona at Tioga.

Six-man action features Newcastle at Bryson, Savoy at Saint Jo, Chillicothe at Crowell, Benjamin at Rule and Gold-Burg at Harrold.
