Affiliated Managers Group, Inc. (AMG +5.56%) reported record quarterly results as the company continued its strategic evolution toward alternative investment strategies. Management reported that assets under management reached an all-time high, supported by record inflows into alternatives and strong investment performance. The company stated that alternatives now account for approximately two-thirds of EBITDA on a run-rate basis, up from approximately one-third seven years ago. Management indicated that its business model generates significant unencumbered cash flow, which the company intends to deploy toward new affiliate investments, supporting existing affiliates, and share repurchases. CFO Dava Ritchea noted that organic growth in the alternatives segment was driven by four specific secular trends: secondaries, infrastructure, absolute return, and tax-aware strategies.
