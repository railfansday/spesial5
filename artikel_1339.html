Who would Italy have faced in 2026-27 Nations League quarter-finals?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Italy will not be competing in the Nations League quarter-finals, but they have recently learned which opponents they would have come up against had they managed to avoid defeat against Belgium in Brussels on Sunday.

Heading into the final round of group stage fixtures, Italy had sat at the top of their group thanks to a 3-1 victory over France in San Siro last week. Their hard work against Les Bleus was undone, however, by a 4-1 defeat away against Belgium on Friday, which saw the Azzurri drop from top spot down to third place by the end of the night.

Italy would have qualified for the Nations League quarter-finals with a draw and would have finished at the top of the standings with a victory against Belgium.

The draw for the 2026-27 Nations League quarter-finals has recently been conducted, and Italy have learned that they would have gone up against Germany had they drawn against Belgium and finished the group in second place. The Azzurri would have had to play World Cup winners Spain had they beaten Belgium and topped their group.

Instead, Italy will begin their qualification campaign for the 2028 Euros in the next international break, which officially kicks off on March 22, 2027.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
