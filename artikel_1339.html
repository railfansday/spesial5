Pochettino's Transfer Solution: Man Utd Academy Star Harry Amass

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Mauricio Pochettino is the new manager of Manchester United, and he already knows that he has some major issues to solve within his squad.

The Argentine has signed a two-and-a-half-year deal to leave the USMNT after their Nations League quarter-final second-leg clash with Haiti and make the switch to Old Trafford, where his first game will come on Sunday afternoon when they travel to Anfield to face off against arch-rivals Liverpool.

The boss will only have a number of days to assess his squad before picking his first starting lineup, but there are areas of the squad that are already short of quality and options, and the club will be looking to address that in the January transfer window.

In the summer the club were looking to add a forward and a left-back to the squad, but after spending £155m on Youri Tielemans, Andrey Santos, and Carlos Baleba, the club opted against bringing in any further additions.

Benjamin Sesko has since been ruled out for several months with a recurrence of his shin injury, meaning the striker position is short on quality, while at left-back both Luke Shaw and Patrick Dorgu have been dealing with injury issues throughout the campaign.

But while the striker situation has got the depth across the attack to survive for a few more weeks before any deals can be done, the left-back position is not so lucky, and Pochettino must start to look at options to solve the problem between now and then.

The 54-year-old has never been shy of giving young players an opportunity in the first team. It was he who gave Luke Shaw his debut at Southampton as a 17-year-old, while he also gave players like Dele Alli, Philippe Coutinho, Harrison Reed, Kyle Walker-Peters, Xavi Simons, and Harry Kane their big breaks at the top level.

Now at Old Trafford, where he has the chance to revolutionise Man Utd, he will have the responsiblity of ensuring that the famous pathway from Carrington to the first-team doesn't disappear.

The academy at Man Utd has been legendary in the Premier League era, producing top stars like Ryan Giggs, David Beckham, Paul Scholes, Marcus Rashford, and Paul Pogba. The biggest talent now is JJ Gabriel, although the 16-year-old is set to depart the club for Real Madrid after a fallout between the club and his family over his involvement in first-team games.

But there is another youngster who is ready to make an impact in the first-team, and Pochettino could solve a major issue by giving him his chance.

Youngster Harry Amass has been one of the most impressive young players to come out of the academy system at Man Utd in recent years, but he has found opportunities in the first-team hard to come by so far.

The 19-year-old spent last season out on loan at Sheffield Wednesday before joining Norwich in January, where he suffered a season-ending injury after just a handful of appearances.

But he returned to Old Trafford in the summer and was part of the pre-season squad, even scoring in the win over Rosenborg. Despite the injuries suffered by Shaw and Dorgu, however, Carrick still handed him just 18 minutes of action before he was sacked, preferring to use Diogo Dalot, Noussair Mazraoui and Ayden Heaven out of position instead.

Pochettino will see a lot of what he saw in Shaw at Southampton in Amass. He is quick, powerful, excellent on the ball, and good defensively. In one-on-one situations, he can hold his own too, and while he looks small, he is almost 6ft tall.

Whenever he has been given a chance to shine he has taken it, too. So much so, that former teammate Charlie McNeill described him as 'ridiculous'.

“He’s a joke, honestly. He’s so good, on the ball he’s ridiculous and he’s not shy of putting a tackle in. He's good enough to have a future there [Man Utd."

Pochettino has a chance to give him his opportunity and figure out whether a big signing is really needed in January, or if the club have enough to get by until the summer when Shaw will likely leave, and a new first-choice arrival can join to compete with Amass and Dorgu.
