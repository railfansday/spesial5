Sunderland Want Former Crystal Palace and Southampton Hero to Replace Le Bris

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Sunderland manager Regis Le Bris is under pressure at the Stadium of Light as they sit bottom of the Premier League table.

They picked up just one league win all season, and they are already four points away from safety.

The Black Cats also suffered a disappointing defeat at home to Dynamo Zagreb in the Europa League on Thursday, putting further pressure on manager Le Bris.

Sunderland have produced some strong performances and results in Europe so far this season, but it's their Premier League form that will be a huge concern to the decision-makers at the Stadium of Light.

Sunderland face Chelsea at home on Sunday afternoon, which is a real opportunity for Le Bris to turn things around.

Despite that, it's certainly not going to be easy with the attacking quality that Chelsea possess.

According to a report from journalist Alan Nixon, Sunderland are already considering who is going to be their next manager due to the performances of Le Bris.

It's claimed that Portuguese coach Jose Fonte, who joined the backroom staff last summer, is highly regarded by club chiefs.

Nixon suggests that Fonte is the man they will turn to if they do decide to sack Le Bris in the near future.

Fonte doesn't have managerial experience in the Premier League, but he did play in England for years, including with Crystal Palace, Southampton, and West Ham.

Nixon adds that if Le Bris is sacked or even quits, then Fonte will be on hand to step in for the Black Cats.

Le Bris is feeling the heat in the Premier League sack race after a disappointing start to the season, and it certainly wouldn't be a surprise if he were relieved of his duties in the near future.

It'll be interesting to see whether the result against Chelsea has any bearing on Le Bris's future, as it could be perfect timing to make a change with the international break approaching.

Manchester United, Sunderland, and Crystal Palace are all drastically underperforming in the Premier League this season.

With the international break just around the corner, it is time for Sunderland to make the difficult decision to sack Le Bris.

There were signs last season that they perhaps overachieved to finish in the European places, and they've reverted to where many would expect them to be this season.

Sunderland are in serious danger of being relegated if they are unable to turn things around, and at the moment, it's looking difficult for Le Bris to do so.

A win over Chelsea could change things and kickstart their season, but if they lose, it's going to be a long old international break for them.

Now is the ideal time for Sunderland to make a change, especially if they are defeated by Chelsea.
