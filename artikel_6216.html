American Grows Maintenance Footprint in Miami

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

A new deal with Miami-Dade County will help American Airlines solidify and eventually expand its maintenance operations at Miami International Airport.

The county announced this week that it will buy a hangar and maintenance space at Miami International from a third-party owner and lease it directly to American for its MRO operations. The acquisition includes Hangar 890, Building 891, 10.2 acres of land, and a 2.2-acre parking lot.

According to the Miami Herald, American already leases the property and uses it for maintenance work, but a change in ownership from private jet operator Aero Miami to the county will give American more leeway to develop the space as it sees fit. The new lease with the county will run for 20 years and cost American $67.8 million, the newspaper said.

The airline plans to keep more than 650 aircraft maintenance workers at the facility. It has also agreed to complete about $13.9 million in building repairs.

“Miami is a cornerstone of American’s international network and a consequential part of our long-term growth strategy,” American Senior Vice President of Miami Operations Juan Carlos Liscano said in a news release. “This agreement reinforces our dedication to our Miami team and preserves our ability to continue growing our network to more destinations around the world for our customers.”

American is the largest airline at Miami, carrying more than half of all passengers there.
