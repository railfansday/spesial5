'I'm Telling You This Because It's True' - Reporter On Xbox's Release Window For The Elder Scrolls 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Yesterday, we were treated to the official name of The Elder Scrolls 6 - it's called The Elder Scrolls VI: Sentinel — and we also got a very brief teaser trailer to accompany it. Still, as things stand, very few details have been revealed.

One of the big things that everyone wants to know is when TES6 will actually release, and according to reliable Bloomberg reporter Jason Schreier, Xbox is targeting a release window that's "more than" two years away.

Schreier initially reported this as soon as the announcement went live yesterday:

I think a lot of people forget that The Elder Scrolls VI was only announced in 2018 (along with Starfield) to soften the blow of Fallout 76 — "Don't worry, even though we're making a multiplayer game we've got single-player stuff coming too!" Sorry to say this game is still more than 2 years away!

It's not a prediction. I know the time frame the game is targeting

This appears to have rubbed some Elder Scrolls fans the wrong way, as Schreier has issued a lengthy post over on the official TES Reddit page highlighting various instances in which he's been correct about games and dates in the past.

He uses that history to justify why he's not trying to annoy the fans, but simply provide the facts:

"So here we are again. You have to understand: I'm a reporter. I am not telling you this because I enjoy watching people get mad about having to wait for sequels to their favorite video games. I am telling you this because it is true, and because my job is to report accurate facts and clear up misinformation. TESVI: Sentinel is more than 2 years away."

Jason is almost always correct with the stuff he reports, so we're inclined to believe that Xbox is currently targeting a release window of 2028 or later for The Elder Scrolls VI: Sentinel. He does specifically say "more than" though, so that appears to suggest 2029 or 2030 is the most likely timeframe as things stand.

That seems a very long way away for a game that's been so highly anticipated for so long, and it comfortably pushes TES6 into Project Helix territory - there's a good chance it won't launch on Xbox Series X or Xbox Series S at all.

As with any future release though, it's impossible to predict exactly when it'll come out and what the industry will look like when it does, so who knows whether it could end up getting pushed forward (or held back). We'll see!

We did actually publish a poll on this yesterday before Jason's comments began gaining traction, so if you haven't voted on it yet, you can do so below. The most popular vote yesterday was actually 2028, which might be a bit too ambitious.
