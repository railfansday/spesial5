NBA 2K27 Brings A Slice Of GTA 6's Vice City To Switch 2 This Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A little-known curio called Grand Theft Auto 6 is launching this week; you may have heard of it. It's not releasing on Switch 2, of course, but that doesn't prevent any crossover content from making it our way. And so, welcome to the closest we'll get to GTA 6 on Switch 2, via NBA 2K27, of all places.

2K has just announced the forthcoming season of 2K27's online The City game mode, which'll feature an official crossover with Rockstar and GTA 6. Season 3 is all about Vice City, with Rockstar's already infamous take on the Miami streets making its way into online hooping sessions.

The season kicks off on 27th November, and includes a new Vice City-inspired Park and a bunch of new clothing and accessory items for your MyPlayer, like Vice City and Los Santos jerseys, and Burger Shot and Cluckin’ Bell Employee Outfits.

Here's a handful of screenshots of the event — none of which come from the Switch 2 version, obviously:

The open 'The City' mode is available in NBA 2K27 on Switch 2 (after the console missed out on the larger space in previous entries), though 2K still hasn't graced the hybrid with crossplay, so expect things to still be very quiet.

2K is marking the occasion with the digital release of the NBA 2K27 Vice City Edition on 27th November. This one features Miami Heat's own Bam Adebayo on the cover, and comes bundled with a handful of extra perks (Season 3 Pro Pass, Vice City T-Shirt, 30 Skill Boosts, and 15 Gatorade Boosts).

So no, we won't be diving into GTA 6 on our Switch 2s any time soon, but hey, this is... something?
