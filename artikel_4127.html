Nintendo's Delightful 'My Mario' Range Is Now Available Online (US)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Last year, Nintendo revealed its 'My Mario' merch line. The collection of toys, plushies and books were all designed to bring a little Mario into the lives of our littlest family members, and this week, Nintendo has made it even easier to do so.

The collection is now available online via the Nintendo Store in the US. With many of the items being exclusive to Nintendo's physical storefronts up until now, it's a prime time to get your hands on some of the merch and make sure that your little ones are up to date on their Mushroom Kingdom knowledge from day dot.

A bunch of the cutest items in the collection are included in the online drop, too. Those wooden blocks that double as amiibo? They're on there. The adorable fleece overalls featuring familiar faces? Yep, they're there. The bath toys shaped like warp pipes? You guessed it...

For those who have missed it up to this point, the collection also encompasses a series of stop-motion shorts on YouTube and the Hello, Mario! Switch app, which lets you squish and poke Mario's face in a way that's vaguely reminiscent of the Super Mario 64 start screen.

It's all laser-focused on bringing your toddlers into the world of Mario from the earliest point possible, but it's still extremely cute and online purchasing makes things that bit easier as we approach the Holiday season.
