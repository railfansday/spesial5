Xbox One Players Share Their First Impressions Of GTA 6 Via The Cloud

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Here's what it's like using Xbox Cloud Gaming to play on XB1

If you want to play GTA 6 natively on any console right now, you can only do so on Xbox Series X, Xbox Series S or PS5. However, if you're willing to use the cloud, it's possible to play the game on Xbox One.

To do this, you have to buy the game on the Xbox Store first, and then acquire Xbox Cloud Gaming time via one of two means - you either purchase time on the Xbox Store, or you get a subscription to Xbox Game Pass that includes a certain amount of time per-tier (and you can top it up later if you need to).

There is also the option to play "free with ads" using Xbox Cloud Gaming, but we haven't verified that it works with GTA 6 yet (although there's seemingly no reason why it wouldn't), and it only gives you 30 minutes of game time before it kicks you off. Some of GTA 6's missions can last longer than 30 minutes, so that's not really ideal.

Anyway, while we're still in the process of dusting off our Xbox One consoles and trying GTA 6 on there for ourselves, we thought we'd first share some impressions from Xbox fans across the web on what it's been like for them so far.

Let's take a look at what some Xbox One owners are saying about it:

Overall, it seems like most people are enjoying their time with GTA 6 on Xbox One so far, although that's not to say there haven't been issues in some cases. The biggest complaints seem to be that some players think it looks "blurry" (while others don't really see a problem), and there are also a small number of reports that it appears to disconnect for no reason sometimes.

There's also a bit of worry that the servers might not be able to handle the load during "peak" hours, and considering we're only on the first day of GTA 6's official launch, it's hard to say how things are going to go in that regard.

Meanwhile, every other version of Xbox Cloud Gaming officially runs at up to 1080p and 12mbps bitrate.

If you want any further feedback about playing GTA 6 on the Xbox One, we'll aim to get some "hands on" impressions to you ASAP. Based on these initial day one impressions though, it seems to be working fairly well right now.

Keep in mind that you obviously need a strong and consistent internet connection in order to play games via Xbox Cloud Gaming, otherwise you may experience heavy lag, disconnects and all sorts of other problems.

Are you playing GTA 6 on Xbox One? Please let us know your impressions of it in the comments below!
