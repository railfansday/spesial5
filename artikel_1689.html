Capello pays tribute to Baresi and is ‘waiting for results’ after Cardinale’s bold claims

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Fabio Capello recalled what Franco Baresi meant to him as a player and captain, while commenting on Gerry Cardinale’s bold declarations too.

Few players mean more to a club – if any – than Baresi meant to Milan. The centre-back reinvented the role and became a symbol of one of the most dominant eras in the history of the sport, as well as an icon in the eyes of the fans who adored him.

Capello was lucky enough to coach the No.6 and he spoke about Baresi’s greatness on Andrea Longoni’s YouTube channel ‘Milan Hello’. His words were relayed by MilanNews, and the emotion was clear in his voice.

“He was important for what he conveyed to the other players when they came to play for Milan. I’m talking about champions, important players, foreigners, of all qualities. They saw in him the man who conveyed the history of Milan,” he said.

“Franco didn’t talk much in the dressing room, but when he did, he was listened to because you could feel his words came from the heart, you could feel they were something important for everyone.”

Last night, Capello was interviewed by Sky and he commented on Cardinale’s words, who has been making some quite bold pledges about Milan’s future in recent weeks.

“He’s promising a lot with his words, I read everything he says. Now I’m waiting for him, I’m waiting for the results. I want to see if he’ll do what he’s saying, if he has a plan and a real idea of ​​where he wants to get to.

“Berlusconi had also promised great things, but then he surrounded himself with people who knew about football. Besides the players, you also need to create a staff that follows you and understands your ideas.

“Now I don’t know if this staff has already been chosen, he keeps talking about new sporting directors, about new people who will join Milan. Let’s see what he can do. The promises are great, we’ll see what the results will be.”
