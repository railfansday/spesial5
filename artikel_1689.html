TNT Sports Brazil: Milan prepare €70m Endrick offer to Real Madrid – loan ‘off the table’

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

A report in Brazil has claimed that AC Milan have made an offer to Real Madrid for Endrick, and it is for a permanent deal.

In the past few weeks, there has been a lot of speculation suggesting that Milan are lining up a winter move for Endrick. He seems to be a player that the owner Gerry Cardinale personally likes, and the funds are available to at least make an attempt.

According to Vitor Sergio of TNT Sports Brazil, Milan have begun talks with Madrid and Endrick’s representatives and are expected to submit a total offer of €70m for him. This would be €60m in fixed money, and €10m in add-ons. He adds that a loan is ‘off the table’.

In addition, the Rossoneri know they need to substantially increase the Brazilian’s salary. He currently receives around €3.2m net per season. Milan are aware of the figures involved in this deal and believe they have the financial capacity to pursue it.

To seal the deal, Real Madrid would also reportedly ask for two clauses with the future in mind. The first would given them the option to buy Endrick back for €85m (with only the length of the buy-back window still to be decided).

The second would allow Real to match a future offer, with the aim of reducing the chances of Endrick joining Barcelona or another elite European club in the future.

Although Los Blancos are willing to negotiate with the Diavolo, the Spanish club remains uncertain about selling the 20-year-old, as Mourinho still wants to count on him.
