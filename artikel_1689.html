Miller ‘converting potential to being a performer’, says Revs boss Mitrovic

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

New England Revolution head coach Marko Mitrovic believes winger Peyton Miller is "converting from being potential to being a performer".

Miller made a decisive impact in the Revs' first-round, Game 1 victory over the Philadelphia Union in the MLS Cup playoff.

After forcing an own goal from Nathan Harriel, the 19-year-old scored in the second half to secure a 2-0 success at home.

Miller has scored eight goals and provided nine assists in MLS play this season, and Mitrovic praised the teenager's shift in mentality this term.

"He's converting from being potential to being a performer, and that's what a performer does," Mitrovic said of Miller.

"If I have to track 90 yards or 90 metres back and protect my goal, I have to do it. And if I have to attack, I have to do it.

"I think that level of maturity is actually the biggest progression that we've seen from Peyton this year."

Ahead of their must-win game at Subaru Park, Philadelphia will be hoping to rely on a youngster of their own in Cavan Sullivan.

Sullivan was named 2026 MLS Young Player of the Year, and Union interim head coach Ryan Richter was delighted for the 17-year-old.

"All the good things that come to him are a reflection of the hard work and the mentality that he has," Richter said of Sullivan's award.

"The one thing that you know with him is that he has big ambitions and he has big dreams of what he wants to do in his career.

"This is a great honour for him, but I know he's not going to take it and get complacent. He's onto the next thing. He's driven to take another step after this."

Miller (19 years, 13 days) became the first teenager to score a playoff goal for New England last weekend.

Prior to Miller, the youngest Revs goalscorer in the MLS Cup playoffs was Tajon Buchanan (21 years, 290 days) in 2020, also against the Union.

Philadelphia have lost just once at home against an MLS team since the start of April (W8 D5), but the loss came versus New England on October 24.

The Revs have won consecutive road matches against the Union once, winning both their away games in this fixture in 2015.

The Union have been shut out in three of their last four games, after not being held scoreless in any of their previous 20 matches against MLS foes.

The Game 1 win was New England's first shutout playoff victory since a 2–0 win over Philadelphia in the 2020 first round.

The Revs have posted consecutive shutout wins once in their playoff history, with 1–0 victories over the New York Red Bulls and Chicago Fire in 2007.
