Flekken urges Leverkusen to ‘go on the attack’ despite Dinamo win

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Mark Flekken believes Bayer Leverkusen are "well on their way" in the Europa League after a 2-0 win over Dinamo Zagreb.

Malik Tillman opened the scoring just 10 minutes in before Jarell Quansah made sure of the result on the stroke of half-time.

It was Leverkusen's fourth win from five games in the Europa League, after they bounced back from a 1-0 loss to Marseille last time out in the competition.

The result moved them up to fourth in the table, leaving them three points behind pacesetters Bournemouth, boosting their chances of earning an automatic spot in the knockout stage.

However, it was not a vintage attacking performance from Leverkusen, who scored with both of their shots on target while only having nine attempts worth 1.3 expected goals (xG) overall.

And while Flekken was thrilled to get the victory, he noted how important it will be to put a bigger emphasis on attacking more in their next games.

"We did well in the first half; in the second, we were a bit weaker," he said.

"We'll certainly take one or two points away to analyse, but overall, it was a good match.

"We're now well on our way with twelve points. But of course, the motto for the next match is: go on the attack!"
