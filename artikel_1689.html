Mainoo: No one’s coming to save Man Utd amid relegation fears

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Kobbie Mainoo has demanded Manchester United show fight, character and togetherness after warning that "no one's coming to save us" amid the squad's latest setback.

Mainoo's comments came following United's 4-0 defeat to rivals Liverpool last Sunday, leaving the Red Devils sitting 19th in the Premier League after 11 matches of the season.

Leny Yoro's eighth-minute red card – which has now been overturned – did not help Mauricio Pochettino's cause in his first game in charge, with Dominik Szoboszlai and Florian Wirtz scoring before the break.

The Reds sealed the win after goals from Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz in the second half, condemning Pochettino to the joint-heaviest defeat by a United manager in his first game at the helm, with supporters continuing to vent their frustrations.

Accounting for three points for a win all-time, United's eight points this season are their fewest after 11 games of a league campaign since 1972-73 (seven).

That is also the previous season in which the Red Devils only managed one win through their first 11 league matches, and Mainoo urged his team-mates to show a response.

"It's all about character now," Mainoo told MUTV. "We're at the lowest we've ever been, and no one's coming to save us.

"There's no helping hands coming, so we've got to work together and lean on each other and keep working, and hopefully it'll come good.

"We're all committed. No one is going anywhere, and we're all leaning on each other.

"We all know what we have to do, and we all know with the new manager we need to get behind him, keep working in training. That's all we can do."

United have the chance to respond in the Champions League on Wednesday, with a trip to Estadio Jose Alvalade to face Sporting CP up next.

The Red Devils are, however, winless in their last six Champions League away games (D2 L4), losing both this season.

Indeed, they last had a longer such run in the competition between February 2004 and December 2005 (eight matches – D3 L5).

Sporting and United are meeting for the fifth time and the first since the 2007-08 Champions League. The Portuguese side have won just one of those four prior matches (L3), a 5-0 home victory in March 1964.

Pochettino's side are 17th in the 36-team standings after dramatically beating Roma last time out, and despite coming into the game off the back of another damaging defeat, Mainoo is relishing another European matchday.

"Of course we're looking forward to it [the Sporting game]," Mainoo added. "It's coming quick, so we have to pick ourselves back up and go again."
