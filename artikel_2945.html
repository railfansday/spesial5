Why Nvidia Rehiring Jensen Huang Is Making Sandisk Stock Skyrocket Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Jensen Huang is back at the helm of Nvidia, and all's well with AI again.

By Rich Smith – Nov 23, 2026 at 10:43AM EST

Sandisk (SNDK +15.69%) stock bounced back in a big way Monday, soaring 20.5% through 10:30 a.m. ET -- and we know exactly why.

This morning, Nvidia (NVDA +32.52%) announced its board has voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO. Shares of Nvidia ripped 35.5% higher as shorts were forced to burn their bets against the stock and buy it back in apparently massive quantities.

With the future of AI looking suddenly secure, other AI stocks -- Sandisk included -- are soaring alongside Nvidia.

Nvidia's strategy of investing in its own customers, giving them the cash they need to buy Nvidia's chips, has been described (by detractors) as creating a "circular economy" -- but also (by fans) as turning Nvidia into the "Central Bank of AI." A sick Nvidia (and Nvidia was looking pretty ill without Huang) could bring the whole house of cards tumbling down -- but healthy Nvidia can once again provide the cash to keep the AI perpetual motion machine churning.

Apologies for the mixed metaphors -- but you get what I'm saying. With Huang back in charge, investors think Nvidia looks healthy again. Sales of Nvidia AI GPUs will continue to grow. And because those GPUs need memory chips to do their AI magic, Sandisk's sales of NAND memory chips will continue growing as well.

And that means investors may once again be able to rely on analyst forecasts for Sandisk's sales and earnings growth -- estimates originally made back when all was well with Nvidia, called into question when Huang was fired, but now valid once more.

It means that Sandisk stock, trading under 5x trailing earnings but expected to grow earnings at nearly 40% per year over the next five years, looks very cheap again this morning.

And that's why investors are buying Sandisk stock.
