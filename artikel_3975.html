Minecraft Dungeons II First DLC 'The Humblers Awaken' Arrives Next Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Mojang hosted a surprise Minecraft Dungeons II broadcast at TwitchCon San Diego this year, and alongside this, it's announced the first paid DLC will be arriving very soon.

The Humblers Awaken will launch on 1st December 2026 and brings a fresh adventure to the action-RPG with new quests, unexplored environments, colourful cosmetics and an expanded arsenal of gear. Here's a bit more about what you can expect:

"The Sift is in danger once more, and the humblers – an ancient threat that has slumbered for ages – are beginning to wake up. Turns out they are not particularly happy about it, and if left unchecked, they will spread across the dimension, or worse – stir a much more dangerous foe. It falls on you and your trusty allies to venture into the dimension’s deeper reaches and investigate this strange unrest.

"The journey will take you through the sprawling fungal sporefields and winding underground burrows, with new quests shedding light on the mystery behind the humblers’ rude awakening."

This DLC will also add "three new artifacts that provide active abilities" and "four new talismans offering passive bonuses". Additionally, there's some cool new equipment including The Pearl skin, the Baby Nuzzle pet and Nacreous cape.

Alongside this DLC will be a bonus free update - this will add new challenges, more weapons and armour to Minecraft II. It will also reunite heroes from the original Minecraft Dungeons (Hal, Hex, Adrienne and Valorie) as player skins. There'll also be the new trial dungeons, where you can earn powerful gear:

"Take on the new trial dungeons to earn powerful gear. Available through Brave Haven, trial dungeons offer daily and weekly challenges that feature a combination of rule-bending modifiers and increased difficulty. Enormous spiders, constant TNT explosions, and soul projectiles are just some of the modifiers you will encounter in these dungeons!"

Last but not least, it's been confirmed the DLC 'Illusions of Grandeur' will launch at some point in Spring 2027.
