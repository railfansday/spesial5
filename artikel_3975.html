Oh, and as for Mura. It's basically like looking through a cheese cloth. It's always present, but you really start to notice it in dark/black sections in a game. PSVR2's 'pentile' based OLED display not only does it have Mura, but it's less clear than RGB displays, like Quest 3, Steam Frame's and more, because its sub pixel count is lower. Plus sony used a disfusion layer(anti-SDE filter) to their OLED to try and hide or mitigate it's screen door effect. 🤭

Nobody uses Fresnel Lenses(Softer clarity, tiny sweet spot, color fringing, more glare), or Pentile OLED anymore. It's usually Pancake, with RGB MicroOLED, LCD or sometimes QD Mini-LED. Neither RGB LCD or MicroOLED have mura, and they're clearer. Pimax's RGB QD Mini-LED which is being used has very mild mura, if anything in their upcoming Crystal Pro.

Meanwhile, Quest 3 has clarity in the bag. it's almost like a generation ahead of PSVR2, but it faulters with it's milked out LCD black levels, weaker 3D depth perception, no pcvr display port, no DFR etc.
