Complete DREADMOOR Walkthrough: Part 1

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

DREADMOOR is now out in early access, and it presents an interesting tale for you to explore. Set in a dark, murky world, you have to capture different fish, explore locations, and upgrade your boat.

The campaign of DREADMOOR begins with a tentacle knocking you in the water. The story is broken into multiple quests that you can complete.

The opening will lead you to safe piece of land, and your main objective to get your boat. To do so, you’ll have to power up the generator with the fuel. Once you reach the generator, go to the hut right from there. You’ll find the fuel to power up the generator.

Once the generator has been powered up, you’ll unlock the boat, and then start exploring the world of DREADMOOR. Make sure to pick up the Hook Gun as well along the way.

First, we have to head to the Church Settlement. This place will provide you with your first weapon that allows you to clear blockages. Moreover, you’ll also find the first bed where you can sleep for the night to be over.

Next, we set sail for the Trading Post. Here, we will meet the trader and he will task us with some quest. The trader will also provide us with different blueprints to purchase, but that’s something you can ignore for now.

From there, go upwards to patch the hole in the wall in your shelter. Here, you’ll find the Simple Rod, and you can now fish in the waters. But first, sleep on the bed to survive the night.

Once we get the fishing rod, the next task is to open the door to the Dry Dock. Get the required resources, and then speak with the Engineer to open the doors. This will then allow you to clean and upgrade your ship.

Before you can install the upgrades, you’ll also need to clean the polyps from your ship using the water pipe. Then, you can install the upgrades after crafting them with the required resources. To do so, you’ll have to unlock the blueprints first.

This will complete the first part of the game and get you up to date with all the basics.
