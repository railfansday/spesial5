ArcheAge Chronicles is set in the world of Auroria, which was once forgotten in the legends. Rediscovered, you’re one of several pioneers taking their first steps in the world as a member of an established trading company.

You’ll explore ancient ruins during your adventures where dangers await at every turn. However, you can use your fighting skills to defeat the enemies. You get to pick from a wide variety of weapons and skills that ultimately lead to unique player-driven builds. Whether you want to fight close-quarters or use range, the option is in your hands.

As you level up, you’ll get to learn new recipes, master trade with other players, and enhance essential skills like hunting, gathering, and more. You have complete freedom when it comes to world exploration and engaging in different trades.

This is an MMO, you can always band up with other players to turn your adventures in Auroria into a memorable one.
