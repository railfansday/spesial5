‘Disgrace’: Manchester United legend lets rip after Champions League controversy

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ex-Manchester United star Ashley Young labelled the performance of referee Szymon Marciniak during Wednesday’s chaotic Champions League draw with Sporting as a “disgrace”.

Marciniak was at the centre of multiple controversial decisions in a dramatic 2-2 league phase tie in Lisbon, awarding three penalties, ruling out two Man Utd goals and sending off midfielder Youri Tielemans in stoppage time.

New Red Devils boss Mauricio Pochettino claimed that his side had been “punished” by the officials, going on to say that it was “impossible” to understand how the decisions were reached.

Young held a similar position, with the former winger left searching for words when speaking about Marciniak’s display.

The ex-United captain said on BBC Radio 5 Live: “The referee’s performance was just simply… wow.

“The decisions that he gave… Youri Tielemans getting booked, to eventually getting sent off later because you booked him when it should never have been a booking, and it should have been an equalising goal.

“The third goal that should have stood because he’s saying Patrick Dorgu fouled the ‘keeper.

“The ‘keeper comes out and flaps at it before he is even touched. How that has gone to VAR is beyond me. His decision-making today is nothing more than a disgrace.”

United fans would have been further irked by the decision to rule out their potential late winner, as Sporting’s equaliser came in a similar fashion. Goalkeeper Senne Lammens dropped a high ball under light pressure, allowing Luis Suarez to convert with an improvised bicycle kick.

However, talkSPORT pundit Gabby Agbonlahor believes the reaction to the refereeing controversies has been overblown.

He said on Thursday morning: “Manchester United fans, just get on with it. You didn’t deserve to win the game.

“Did the referee make a couple of mistakes? Yes, he did. But come on. Manchester United fans - first they’re moaning about the owners, next they’re moaning about chief executives, [Omar] Berrada, [Jason] Wilcox. Now it’s referees every game, it’s VAR every game.

“How about - you didn’t deserve to win the game? The referee didn’t make Lisandro Martinez make a stupid challenge to give away the first penalty that gets saved.

“The referee didn’t make [Noussair] Mazraoui come in so tight to the centre-half and allow the [Sporting] left-back flying forward.

“The referee didn’t make Lammens come out to no man’s land. I don’t know what he’s doing. I don’t know what he’s going to achieve running out there.

“The referee didn’t make Lammens have chocolate wrists and drop the ball when no one is on him.”

The draw with Sporting leaves United 19th in the Champions League table, with three games remaining in this season’s league phase.

Tielemans’ red card means the Belgian will miss their next European fixture - a home tie with Bundesliga outfit RB Leipzig on December 8.
