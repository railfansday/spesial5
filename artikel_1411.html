Players we can’t believe were never sent off after James Tarkowski’s first Premier League red

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

James Tarkowski picked up the first red of his Premier League career on Monday night and let’s be honest, we all thought he had more than that.

Tarkowski was shown the red for the follow-through on a tackle on Brentford’s Yehor Yarmolyuk which saw his name crossed off a list of players who have surprisingly never received a red.

A career of 323 games and not a single red card is an impressive feat from King.

The former Tottenham defender’s struggles with injuries are well known with him only twice playing more than 30 Premier League games in a single season but when he was on the pitch, he was as composed as can be.

Not only did King never get his marching orders, he only got nine yellows in every match he played.

King’s numbers are impressive, but the record belongs to Hughes.

The former Newcastle, Fulham and Aston Villa man played 455 Premier League matches but never received a red card.

In total, the Northern Irishman played 675 games in his career and was never sent off, earning 19 yellows in that time.

Considering how composed he often looked, perhaps we could not be too surprised that Lahm features on this list.

He played in eight different positions in his career but never once was so out of his depth that he got himself sent off.

Lahm primarily operated as a full-back with 269 appearances on the right and 219 on the left before Pep Guardiola converted him into a defensive midfielder.

He did quite like a yellow though, getting 49 in his career.

Considering he played the majority of his career as a sweeper, it is particularly impressive that Italian legend Scirea was never sent off.

The World Cup and European Cup-winning defender played 621 times and yet was never given his marching orders.

To make it even more impressive, in his long career, which featured 551 games for Juventus, Scirea got just four yellow cards.

The former Match of the Day host is well known for having not only never received a red, but never even got booked either.

Lineker played 468 games, the majority of which were in the First Division, and yes he was a striker but we could show you a long list of number nines who have picked up a red.

The closest Lineker ever came was in Euro 1992 when he stamped on Basile Boli’s toe. The ref missed it and the pre-VAR world meant Lineker was in the clear.

Not only did Barnes never receive a red, but he was also the first player to make it past 100 Premier League games without a card of either colour.

Barnes played most of his football pre-Premier League, getting 349 appearances in the old First Division before making 201 in the Premier League, but never got a red.

He ended his career with 696 games played and not a single entry in the referee’s book.

Iniesta getting a red card just feels wrong, doesn’t it? Well, the good news is it’s never happened.

Iniesta played 885 games in his career, 674 of which were for Barcelona, but never received a red card.

He did pick up a fair few yellows, having got 74 in his time.

Considering he was quite a robust winger, Duff being on this list is surprising.

He played 629 games, starting off with Blackburn before moving to Chelsea in 2003, yet never got a red card.

Instead, he got 42 yellows, the majority of which came with Rovers, but he was largely a clean player.
