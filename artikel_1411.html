Bid For Signed Everton Match-Worn Poppy Shirts

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Signed match-winning shirts from Everton's Premier League victory at Newcastle United on Monday night are now up for auction in order to raise funds for the Royal British Legion.

Evertonians are able to bid on match-worn shirts from two Men’s and Women’s fixtures this month, all individually signed and bearing the poppy in memory of the fallen and Remembrance.

In addition to the clash at St James' Park, the shirts worn by the Men's team at home to Coventry City on Friday 6 November will be up for grabs, while Everton Women’s players will wear poppies for the trip to Chelsea in the Barclays Women’s Super League on Saturday 7 November and at home to Durham Women in the Subway Players Cup on Wednesday 11 November.   The Men’s game against Coventry and Everton Women’s meeting with Durham will be the Club’s two dedicated Remembrance fixtures.   Match-worn and match-issued shirts for all the above games will be signed by the respective players and auctioned online via matchwornshirt.com.

Proceeds from the auctions will go to the Royal British Legion’s Poppy Appeal to boost their year-round support of the armed forces community, including serving personnel, veterans and their families.     The first auction, for shirts worn by the Men’s team at Newcastle United, is now OPEN and will run until 5pm on Saturday 14 November.

Auction runs from 8pm (GMT) on Friday 6 November to 2pm (GMT) on Saturday 21 November. Make your bid HERE*

Auction runs from 12.45pm (GMT) on Saturday 7 November until 12.45pm (GMT) on Saturday 14 November. Make your bid HERE *

Auction runs from 7pm (GMT) on Wednesday 11 November to 7pm (GMT) on Wednesday 18 November. Make your bid HERE *

If they don’t already have one, supporters will need to create a free MatchWornShirt account to bid.

Founded in 1921, the Royal British Legion is England’s largest Armed Forces charity with 180,000 members and 110,000 volunteers.

Its support offering includes befriending services for those who are isolated, help with finances and housing, and recovery programmes after injury and illness.

Last season, Everton’s shirt auctions raised £29,097 for the Royal British Legion.

*Auctions will only commence at the start times listed.
