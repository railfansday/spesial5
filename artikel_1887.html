Frank embracing life ‘back in the circus’ ahead of Palace bow

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Thomas Frank is relishing the chance of being "back in the circus" as he prepares to take charge of Crystal Palace for the first time in the Premier League on Saturday.

Palace make the trip to the Coventry Building Society Arena as they aim to lift themselves off the bottom of the table after parting ways with Pierre Sage in the international break.

Frank has been out of work since being sacked by Tottenham after just eight months in charge, with the Dane leaving the club 16th in the Premier League in February.

The 53-year-old, who has penned a deal through to the end of the 2028-29 season, had been linked with the Palace job after Oliver Glasner left for Nottingham Forest.

After also managing Brentford, Frank will be the third manager to take charge of three different London clubs in the Premier League after Alan Pardew (Charlton Athletic, Crystal Palace, West Ham) and Harry Redknapp (Queens Park Rangers, Spurs, West Ham).

Frank is aiming to use his tumultuous spell at Spurs, along with his successful stint at Brentford, to try and turn Palace's fortunes around, starting against Frank Lampard's side.

"I can actually, honestly say I would have been fine if it was three, four, five, six, seven months more, because it's good to be back in the circus, but it's also unbelievably good not to be in, especially if you enjoy life, which I do," Frank said.

"I was spending time with family and friends and doing all that, but I think the club, the way Palace is, how well Steve Parish especially spoke about the club and ideas.

"Matt Hobbs and Iain Moody and these guys, too, helped convince me it was a good idea. I'm a little bit surprised by this feeling I have in the body, how good it feels."

Palace have conceded 24 goals in 10 Premier League games this season, their most after 10 league games in a season since 1980-81 under Terry Venables (also 24).

Indeed, 10 of the 15 sides to have conceded that many goals in their first 10 Premier League games have been relegated, though Wolves did it most recently in 2024-25 (27 conceded) and ended the season in 16th.

Only two of Palace's 13 previous permanent managers in the Premier League have won their first game in charge (D4 L7 – 1st spells only), with Pardew beating Spurs in January 2015 and Glasner beating Burnley in February 2024.

This is the first meeting between Coventry and Palace in any competition since March 2012, a 1-1 draw in the Championship.

And despite the way his time at Spurs ended, Frank said he remains unbruised by that experience and has firmly put his time across the capital in his rearview mirror.

"I think we all have an ego," Frank added. "Some are better at controlling it than others.

"For me, it's not the critics, it's more what I can learn, what I can prove, how I can get better.

"There's a lot of clever people out there, but the interesting thing is they only know 10 per cent of the truth.

"That's a tenth of the truth at Brentford, there's a tenth of the truth at Tottenham, and that's just the way it is.

"If you can't handle that as a coach or a player, then you shouldn't be in this business, and I'm very happy to be back in the game."
