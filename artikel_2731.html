Miami vs. Wake Forest ACC Championship Game Setting up as Top 10 Matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Miami and Wake Forest remained in the top 10 in the Associated Press Top 25 and AFCA/LBM Coaches polls released on Sunday. The Demon Deacons and Hurricanes are in position to set up an ACC Championship Game with two top-10 teams for the first time since 2020. Number four/three Clemson defeated second-ranked Notre Dame 34-10 in the 2020 ACC Championship Game.

Miami rallied from a 14-0 first-quarter deficit to beat Virginia Tech on Saturday. The Hurricanes dominated the second half. Miami held Virginia Tech to 55 yards in the second half and six in the fourth quarter. The Hurricanes outscored the Hokies 14-0 in the fourth quarter. Wake Forest won its eighth consecutive game and seventh straight blowout with a 50-29 win at Georgia Tech.

Miami is fourth in both polls this week. The Hurricanes moved up one spot in the AP Top 25. Miami received 1,491 points in the AP Top 25. Notre Dame is one spot ahead of Miami in the AP Top 25 with 1,591 points. Texas is one spot behind Miami in the AP Top 25 with 1,392 points. Georgia is first in the AP Top 25 for the third consecutive week with 1,711 points.

Miami moved up two spots in the coaches' polls this week. The Hurricanes received 1,569 points in the Coaches Poll. Miami was seventh in the College Football Playoff rankings last week, fifth in the AP Top 25, and sixth in the Coaches Poll. With UCLA losing at Michigan on Saturday, Miami is poised to move up at least one spot in this week's College Football Playoff rankings.

Wake Forest earned its highest ranking in program history with the release of this week's polls. The Demon Deacons received 1,264 points in the AP Top 25 this week. Tennessee is seventh, one spot ahead of Wake Forest in both polls. The Volunteers received 1,317 points in the AP Top 25 and 1,345 in the Coaches Poll.

Texas Tech is ninth in both polls. The Red Raiders received 1,166 points in the AP Top 25 and 1,230 in the Coaches Poll. Wake Forest is the only team Miami has beaten this season that is currently ranked. The Hurricanes won 33-20 at the Demon Deacons on September 18. Beating Wake Forest a second time in the ACC Championship would be a huge win for the Hurricanes in College Football Playoff seeding.

Texas, Alabama, and Tennessee at 9-2 are between Miami and Wake Forest in the Week 13 top 25. Georgia has clinched an SEC Championship Game berth. The competition for the second SEC Championship Game berth between Texas, Alabama, and Tennessee is complicated. One of those three will make the SEC Championship Game.

If Miami wins out, it should be poised to pass the SEC Runner-up in the final College Football Playoff rankings. Even if Alabama, Tennessee, or Texas beat Georgia, Miami would have one loss as the ACC Champion if it beats Boston College on Saturday. Alabama remained ninth in the College Football Playoff rankings after losing 28-7 in the 2026 SEC Championship Game.

The ESPN Football Power Index is nearly identical to the polls. Georgia is first, Texas second, Notre Dame third, and Miami fourth in the ESPN Football Power Index. Utah is the aberration in the polls compared to the metrics. The Utes are 11th in the ESPN Football Power Index. Miami is fourth in the ESPN Football Power Index Strength of Record and 33rd in Strength of Schedule.

The Hurricanes are second in the ESPN Football Power Index Game Control and 10th in Average In-Game Win Probability. Utah is first in Strength of Record, Arkansas has played the toughest schedule according to the ESPN Football Power Index, and Georgia is first in Game Control and In-Game Win Probability.

Miami is fifth in the ESPN SP+. Georgia, Notre Dame, Alabama, and Texas are ahead of Miami in SP+. The Hurricanes are first in SP+ offense, 25th in defense, and 15th in special teams. Georgia is second in SP+ offense, first in defense, and 32nd in special teams. Vanderbilt, 87th in SP+, leads the metric in special teams.

Wake Forest is 10th in the ESPN Football Power Index. The Demon Deacons are eighth in Strength of Record, 58th in Strength of Schedule, fourth in Game Control, and third in Average In-Game Win Probability. Wake Forest is sixth in SP+, fourth in offense, 20th in defense, and 13th in special teams. Miami and Wake Forest are two of the seven SP+ top-25 teams in all three phases.

How Miami performs against Boston College and Wake Forest plays against Duke in the regular-season finale on Saturday will greatly influence the point spread and metric projections in the ACC Championship Game. ESPN Analytics projects Miami with a 61.9 percent chance to win the ACC Championship Game over Wake Forest.

The winners of each of the Power Four Conference Championship Games will all earn berths in the College Football Playoff. Miami is on the cusp of earning a first-round bye in the College Football Playoff if it beats Boston College on Saturday and Wake Forest in the ACC Championship Game.
