NFL Week 10 Predictions: Picks for Every Sunday and Monday Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Week 10 of the NFL season starts its Sunday slate early with New England and Detroit meeting in Munich before a full afternoon of games with major playoff implications.

PFN's Cam Mellor and Jacob Infante went through every Sunday and Monday matchup, weighing the point spreads, roster changes, injuries, and recent performance before running the results through PFN's NFL Playoff Predictor.

Infante stayed with several favorites but wasn't willing to simply take the chalk across the board. He likes Minnesota to win at Lambeau Field, Carolina to win in New Orleans, and the winless Chargers to finally break through Monday night.

Note: Point spreads below reflect the numbers discussed when the video was recorded. Lines can move before kickoff.

Recording line: Lions -2.5Recording total: 51.5 pointsPick: Lions -2.5

Detroit enters the Munich game at 6-2, and Infante believes the recent improvement on defense is enough to separate the Lions from a 5-4 New England team dealing with injuries throughout its offense.

The Lions held Minnesota to 15 points in Week 7, Green Bay to 17 the week before that, and Miami to nine in Week 9. That doesn't completely erase the defensive issues from earlier in the season, but it gives Infante more confidence in a unit that doesn't need to carry the team with Jared Goff, Jahmyr Gibbs, and Amon-Ra St. Brown on the other side.

New England is also entering the game shorthanded. TreVeyon Henderson has been ruled out, Rhamondre Stevenson did not practice Wednesday, and A.J. Brown remains limited.

Infante expects a competitive game but sees Detroit's offensive personnel and improved defense producing a one-score victory that clears 2.5 points.

Recording line: Chiefs -3Recording total: 46.5 pointsPick: Chiefs -3

Atlanta has shown enough this season to make Infante wary of treating this like an easy road game for Kansas City.

The Falcons have beaten strong opponents and played several others close. Their problem has been getting good offense and good defense in the same game.

Atlanta was shut out by Tampa Bay in Week 8, then allowed 34 points to Cincinnati in Week 9. Infante sees that inconsistency as a bigger issue against a Chiefs team coming out of its bye.

Both teams were active at the trade deadline, but in very different directions. Kansas City added Josh Downs to its receiving corps, while Atlanta sent Jessie Bates III to Jacksonville.

Mellor doesn't expect Downs to immediately dominate the game plan, but Bates' absence is easier to feel immediately in the Falcons' secondary.

Infante expects Atlanta to keep the game competitive before Kansas City pulls away enough to cover the field-goal spread.

Recording line: Texans -4.5Recording total: 40.5 pointsPick: Texans -4.5

Cleveland owns the better record, but Infante prefers the direction Houston is heading.

C.J. Stroud and the Texans' passing game have been much more explosive lately, with Nico Collins surrounded by enough receiving depth to keep Houston functioning when injuries hit elsewhere.

Deshaun Watson is officially returning to Cleveland's lineup after clearing concussion protocol and sitting out the Week 9 loss to New Orleans.

The matchup against his former team creates an obvious storyline, but Infante isn't treating it as a reason to move away from Houston.

Cleveland can create explosive plays with Denzel Boston and KC Concepcion, but the Texans have shown a higher offensive ceiling. Infante expects Houston's passing attack to eventually force the Browns into a game they aren't equipped to chase.

Recording line: Jaguars -10.5Recording total: 46.5 pointsPicks: Jaguars moneyline, Titans +10.5

Jacksonville is the easy pick to win. The spread is where Infante becomes more cautious.

The Jaguars are 6-2 and just added Jordan Davis, Jessie Bates III, and Davon Godchaux to a defense that was already one of the NFL's better units.

Tennessee is 1-7, but several of its losses have remained competitive. The Titans pushed Cincinnati to overtime in Week 8 and have repeatedly stayed within one score even when the overall results haven't been there.

The Titans are also coming off their bye, giving them additional time to prepare for a division opponent.

Infante expects Jacksonville to win but views 10.5 as a large enough number to take Tennessee. A Jaguars victory by seven or 10 points would cash both sides of his prediction.

Recording line: Colts -3.5Recording total: 38.5 pointsPick: Colts -3.5

Infante initially wanted to find a reason to take Miami. Malik Willis' injury changed the decision.

Willis was placed on injured reserve with a groin injury, putting Kyle McCord in line for his first NFL start. McCord joins a Miami offense that has already struggled to generate consistent production.

Indianapolis isn't exactly entering from a position of strength. The Colts are 2-7 and moved several veterans around the deadline, including Josh Downs, DeForest Buckner, and Quenton Nelson.

The difference is that Indianapolis gets the game at home against an inexperienced quarterback.

Infante isn't particularly high on the Colts. He simply trusts their current situation more than Miami's.

Recording line: Bills -8.5Pick: Bills -8.5

Buffalo is one of the teams Infante is willing to lay a large number with.

The Bills are 7-1 and continue to operate as one of the NFL's best offenses. Their defense has also delivered encouraging results after being a concern earlier in the season.

Buffalo held Baltimore to 10 points in Week 8 and Minnesota to six on Monday night.

The Bills also added Amani Hooker at the trade deadline. Buffalo has not formally guaranteed a Week 10 role for him, but Hooker said Wednesday that he expects to be ready to play against the Jets.

New York doesn't have the offensive firepower Infante believes is necessary to keep pace with Josh Allen and Buffalo for four quarters.

He's comfortable taking the Bills to cover more than a touchdown.

Recording line: Packers -3Recording total: 41.5 pointsInfante's pick: Vikings moneylineMellor's lean: Under 41.5

This is Infante's first notable outright upset of the slate.

Green Bay is 6-3 and has played some of its best football recently. Under normal circumstances, Infante would have preferred the Packers at Lambeau Field.

Minnesota acquired Cousins from Las Vegas after the Vikings' quarterback situation collapsed in a 23-6 loss to Buffalo. Minnesota hasn't formally announced Cousins as the Week 10 starter, but Packers coach Matt LaFleur said Green Bay is preparing with the expectation that Cousins will play.

The Vikings already have PFN's highest-rated defense. They didn't need an elite quarterback as much as they needed competence from the position.

Infante believes Cousins can provide that immediately because of his previous experience in Kevin O'Connell's offense.

Mellor also liked the under. Minnesota's defense has consistently kept scoring down, while Green Bay now has to prepare for an offense whose exact shape with Cousins is difficult to project.

Recording line: Saints -2.5Pick: Panthers moneyline

Carolina has been one of the league's most difficult teams to predict because its offensive ceiling and floor have been so far apart.

The Panthers have lost three straight, but Infante is willing to bet on the better version of Bryce Young showing up against New Orleans.

Carolina has already won twice on the road, including a blowout of Atlanta and a decisive victory in Philadelphia.

Young also has enough around him with Tetairoa McMillan and Jalen Coker to attack a Saints defense Infante doesn't consider particularly intimidating.

New Orleans has been more competitive recently and gets the game at home, but that hasn't meant much in the NFC South this season. The division has struggled collectively in home games.

Infante is taking the Panthers outright as a short underdog.

Recording line: Seahawks -6.5Picks: Seahawks moneyline, Raiders +6.5

Seattle should win the game. Infante doesn't think it will be comfortable.

The Raiders officially turned the offense over to Fernando Mendoza after trading Cousins to Minnesota. The No. 1 overall pick will make his first NFL start against Mike Macdonald's defense.

That's a difficult assignment, but Las Vegas has already been one of the league's harder teams to define.

The Raiders started 3-0, played Buffalo and Kansas City competitively, and have also been blown out by New England and San Francisco.

Mendoza adds another unknown to that range of outcomes.

Infante trusts Seattle's defense and coaching enough to pick the Seahawks, but 6.5 points is more than he's willing to lay on the road against a quarterback whose NFL tendencies haven't yet been exposed on film.

Recording line: Rams -9.5Recording total: 39.5 pointsPick: Rams -9.5

Los Angeles is dealing with significant injuries and still owns one of the clearest matchup advantages of the week.

Puka Nacua is expected to miss significant time, and Davante Adams remains on injured reserve. That leaves Matthew Stafford operating with a heavily depleted receiving group.

Gardner Minshew is on injured reserve, Michael Wilson is done for the season, and rookie Carson Beck is in line for his first NFL start.

Infante's biggest concern for Arizona is how Beck handles pressure. The Rams can create it without needing their offense to score 30 points.

Los Angeles should also be able to lean on Kyren Williams and Blake Corum, control possession, and avoid asking Stafford to carry the game through the air.

Infante expects a low-scoring matchup but still sees enough separation for the Rams to cover the large number.

Recording line: 49ers -3.5Recording total: 55.5 pointsPick: 49ers -3.5

The highest recorded total of the week also produced one of Infante's more straightforward picks.

Dallas has enough offensive talent to turn this into a shootout. The problem is its defense.

The Cowboys have allowed at least 20 points in every game, and Infante doesn't expect that streak to end against Brock Purdy and Kyle Shanahan.

San Francisco has continued to produce despite a long list of injuries. Christian McCaffrey and George Kittle remain high-end options, while the 49ers added Keenan Allen from Indianapolis at the trade deadline.

Allen's exact Week 10 role remains uncertain only days after the trade, and Mike Evans was not practicing to begin the week. Infante still trusts Purdy and Shanahan to find enough answers.

Dallas can score. Infante simply doesn't believe its defense can get enough stops to win the game.

Recording line: Bengals -3.5Pick: Bengals -3.5

Pittsburgh already beat Cincinnati once this season, winning 30-27 in Week 3.

Infante expects the Bengals to answer at home.

Both teams are 6-2, making this one of the most important divisional games on the Week 10 schedule. Pittsburgh has the stronger defense, while Cincinnati owns the much more dangerous offense.

Joe Burrow has the Bengals rolling. Cincinnati has scored at least 31 points in four consecutive games, and Infante sees that ceiling as the difference.

Pittsburgh can keep the game competitive if Jaylen Warren gives Aaron Rodgers enough support and the Steelers prevent Cincinnati's interior defensive line from consistently collapsing the pocket.

Rodgers has still shown flashes of high-level play, but Infante isn't willing to trust that happening often enough against this opponent.

He's taking Cincinnati to split the season series and cover 3.5 points.

Recording line: Ravens -4.5Recording total: 38.5 pointsPick: Chargers moneyline

The final prediction is Infante's biggest upset call.

Los Angeles is 0-8. Baltimore has lost four straight.

The Ravens have been particularly poor over their past three games, getting outscored 101-33 while failing to reach 14 points in any of them.

Baltimore is also preparing to start Jacoby Brissett after acquiring him from Arizona. Infante believes Brissett can eventually stabilize the offense, but he isn't convinced that happens immediately.

The Chargers have problems of their own, obviously. They're the league's only winless team and have watched the season unravel around them.

One thing that changed after the recording is their linebacker situation. Los Angeles agreed to send Daiyan Henley to Denver at the deadline, but the deal was later called off. Henley remains a Charger.

Infante's argument is partly mathematical. Winless seasons are exceptionally rare, and Baltimore is currently vulnerable enough to give Los Angeles a realistic opportunity to finally get one.

It won't be pretty. He's still calling for the upset.

Mellor ran Infante's selections through PFN's NFL Playoff Predictor after the picks were complete.

The model agreed with much of the card. It also liked Detroit over New England, Houston over Cleveland, Indianapolis over Miami, Minnesota over Green Bay, Carolina over New Orleans, Seattle over Las Vegas, and Cincinnati over Pittsburgh.

The simulation produced Atlanta over Kansas City, New York over Buffalo, Dallas over San Francisco, and Baltimore over the Chargers.

The Jets beating the Bills was the result Infante had the hardest time buying. He acknowledged that any single simulation can produce an upset, but Buffalo is playing too well on both sides of the ball for him to move off the Bills laying 8.5 points.

Atlanta and Dallas were easier to envision. The Falcons have already beaten good teams this season, while the Cowboys have enough offense to turn the San Francisco game into a shootout if their defense can find a few stops.

The predictor also sided with Baltimore on Monday night. Infante is sticking with Los Angeles and its first win of the season.
