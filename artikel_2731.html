Bills and Chiefs both favored by 16.5 on Sunday, biggest point spreads of the season

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Bills and Chiefs are scheduled for a Thanksgiving night showdown, but before that they each have a game on Sunday. And both games are expected to be blowouts.

The Bills are favored to beat the Dolphins by 16.5 points, and the Chiefs are favored to beat the Cardinals by 16.5 points. Those are the two biggest point spreads of this NFL season.

The previous biggest spread of this season also featured the Dolphins, when they were 13-point underdogs against the 49ers in Week 2. (The 49ers covered that spread, winning 35-13.)

The Dolphins and Cardinals are expected to be mere speed bumps on the way to one of the marquee games of this NFL season on Thanksgiving night. With the Chiefs likely to be 8-2 and the Bills likely to be 9-1 heading into that game, it has major implications for the No. 1 seed in the AFC.
