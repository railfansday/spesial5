Ousmane Dieng to undergo surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Milwaukee Bucks are set to be without forward Ousmane Dieng for an extended period, with the 23-year-old due to undergo surgery to repair a fractured finger on his non-shooting hand, per Milwaukee Bucks Radio Network and Locked On Bucks host Justin Garcia:

The Milwaukee Journal Sentinel's Jim Owczarski also wrote about the news, stating that "While there is no firm timeline for a return, [Dieng] believes he may be able to get back on the court within a couple of weeks." According to Owczarski, Dieng suffered the injury in the opening minutes of the Bucks' loss to the Orlando Magic, his first start of the season, but he "wasn't sure when or how."

The injury comes at a particularly frustrating time for both Dieng and the Bucks. After starting the season in Taylor Jenkins' rotation, Dieng quickly lost his role to rookie Nate Ament before recently reentering the rotation. Prior to the game against the Magic, Dieng had featured in three straight contests, playing his best basketball of the young season, averaging 9.0 PPG, 2.0 RPG, and 1.3 APG on the back of 6/10 shooting from deep.

The Bucks, meanwhile, having lost to the Atlanta Hawks on Sunday, are in the midst of the worst start to a season in franchise history and need all the help they can get.

In Dieng's absence, look for Gary Trent Jr., Nate Ament, or AJ Green to pick up some additional minutes.
