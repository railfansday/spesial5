Bucks vs. Hawks, Milwaukee trying to snap nine-game losing streak

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Milwaukee Bucks are looking to snap a nine-game losing streak as they host the Atlanta Hawks on Sunday, Nov. 15 at 7 p.m. at Fiserv Forum.

The Bucks are 1-12, tied for the worst record in the NBA with Utah. Milwaukee is on the verge of tying the 1974-75 team for the worst start in franchise history as that team began the year 1-13 as Kareem Abdul-Jabbar missed the first 16 games due to injury. This is the first season the Bucks are playing after trading Giannis Antetokounmpo.

The last time Milwaukee lost 10 straight games was Nov. 9-30 during the tanking 2013-14 season, which was Antetokounmpo's rookie year.

Atlanta is 7-6 and has lost two straight.

At some point you are what the record says you are, and while there is belief in the Bucks locker they could've won more than one game to date, with 15% of the season gone the numbers say the team is indeed one of the worst in the NBA.

Heading into the game against Atlanta, Milwaukee is:

28th in offensive rating (105.7 points per 100 possessions)

28th in defensive rating (120 points allowed per 100 possessions)

30th in net rating at minus-14.2 (two entire points worse than Sacramento)

The areas that Milwaukee aims to be good at, or at least better than most at, are mediocre at best:

14th in pace (100.31 possessions per 48 minutes)

18th in offensive rebounding percentage, or how often a player secures available offensive rebounds (30.5)

29th in turnovers forced (13.1) and steals (6.5)

By the raw numbers, Milwaukee is the No. 6 team in offensive rebounding (13.3 per game), but they are just tied for 12th in second chance scoring at 15.7 points per game.

They are No. 7 in 3-pointers made (14.8) and No. 4 in attempts (43.2) but are shooting an almost-impossible 26.2% on "open" 3-pointers [defined as the nearest defender being 4-6 feet away], 2.3 percentage points worse than the No. 29 Charlotte Hornets.

Milwaukee is generating 17.0 "open" threes per game, which is third best in the league. But at this point, it might be fair to wonder if teams are just leaving players open because the Bucks aren't making these attempts.

The game will be broadcast on MY24 with Lisa Byington, Marques Johnson and Melanie Ricks on the call.

Atlanta is a 7.5-point favorite over Milwaukee and the over/under is set at 234.5 points, per BetMGM.
