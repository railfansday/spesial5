Luka Dončić says fatigue can’t be an excuse for Lakers loss to Jazz

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Playing their third game in four nights, the Los Angeles Lakers saw their nine-game winning streak come to an end Wednesday when they fell at home to the Utah Jazz, 109-97. Plenty of players struggled, including the Lakers' franchise superstar, Luka Dončić, who tallied 29 points on 10-of-28 shooting (2-of-12 from deep), seven assists and nine turnovers.

Following the loss, Dončić took accountability for his lackluster game and refused to let tired legs be the reason behind Los Angeles' poor performance.

"That can't be an excuse. All NBA team plays 82 games, so that can't be an excuse," Dončić said. "I got to do way better. I know that. I feel like we had some open shots we didn't make, but it's one game. We got to move on."

Dončić's 10-of-28 shooting (35.7%) marked his worst field goal percentage of the year while his 29 points were his fewest since Oct. 23 and his nine turnovers were his second-most this season. If the Lakers are going to contend for a championship like they aspire to, their best player, who rarely has this year, cannot sink to that level — and he knows it.

"I mean, honestly, I just got to be way better than this, Dončić said. "This is unacceptable for my part. I've got to lead the team in better ways. I look at this game, got to move on and learn from this."

Dončić's head coach, JJ Redick, refused to single out one player for the loss Wednesday. Dončić may be their superstar and most impactful contributor but he was far from the lone guy to play significantly below the standard they've set for themselves this season.

"I asked the guys, 'Who in here feels like they played well tonight?' Nobody raised their hand. Everybody could be better," Redick told reporters postgame. "We can sit here and nitpick every single guy. Nitpick me. It's whatever. Could my rotations have been different? Maybe [could have] gotten a little more offense, maybe played a little more size at the five when Jaxson [Hayes] or [Mo] Bamba was [out there]? Yeah. We'll do all that, but we didn't play well. Nobody played well. It's not Walker, it's not Luka, it's not Q, it's not Sandro. Nobody had a great game."
