Cowboys injuries: Dallas had 7 players who did not practice today

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Some big developments today coming from the Dallas Cowboys. It was announced that first-year defensive coordinator Christian Parker will no longer call the defensive plays. Mike Pettine, who was brought in out of retirement as an advisor to Parker now takes over the calls for the team.

As for the personnel, the Cowboys need to get healthy and in a hurry. The secondary has taken some bumps and bruises. The offense has some key injuries as well. Here's the latest after today's practice.

The Dallas injury list is a long one. Donovan Ezeiruaku (Ankle), Tyler Booker (Ankle), Jake Ferguson (Knee), DaRon Bland (Ankle), Jonathan Mingo (Ankle), P.J. Locke (Concussion) did not practice today. Juanyeh Thomas (Ribs) was limited. Jalen Thompson (Hamstring) had his 21-day practice window opened as he hopes to return from injured reserve. He was limited today. Joey Porter Jr. (Ankle/Foot) was a full participant.

Tennessee is going to be altered in their passing game after Wan'Dale Robinson (Ankle) was placed on injured reserve. Rookie receiver Carnell Tate (Shoulder) also was a limited participant at practice today. Furthermore, fellow rookie Anthony Hill Jr. (Neck) was listed as limited on today's report. Finally, Tony Adams (Concussion) was sidelined today along with running back Tyjae Spears (Knee).
