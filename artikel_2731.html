Here’s where the Steelers sit in the AFC playoff standings after Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Pittsburgh Steelers have ended their brief two-game losing streak, defeating the Denver Broncos handedly 27-5. With the win, the Steelers jump up in the AFC playoff picture, which you can see in full below:

1. Buffalo Bills (10-1)2. Denver Broncos (8-3, better record vs KC common opponents )3. Jacksonville Jaguars (7-3, win over CIN)4. Cincinnati Bengals (7-3, loss to JAX)5. Kansas City Chiefs (8-3, worse record vs DEN common opponents)6. Pittsburgh Steelers (7-4)7. Cleveland Browns (6-4, AFCN 2-1)8. New England Patriots (5-5)9. Houston Texans (4-6)10. Las Vegas Raiders (4-7, 3-4 AFC)11. Baltimore Ravens (4-7, 2-5 AFC)12. New York Jets (3-8, 3-5 AFC)13. Indianapolis Colts (3-8, 2-6 AFC)14. Los Angeles Chargers (1-9, 1-6 AFC)15. Miami Dolphins (1-9, 1-6 AFC)16. Tennessee Titans (1-9, 1-6 AFC)

In the AFC West, the tiebreaker is the record vs common opponents now that they have the same division record. The Broncos are 4-0 against the Raiders, Chargers, Seahawks, and Cardinals. The Chiefs are 3-1 against the same opponents.
