Jared Goff throws for 170 yards in Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Detroit Lions QB Jared Goff went 17-for-25 for 170 yards, zero touchdowns and one interception against the Miami Dolphins in Week 9.

Why It Matters: Goff was held without a passing touchdown for the first time this season, but the team was still able to pick up the victory. He has been held to under 200 yards in each of the last two games but will look to be more successful next week against the New England Patriots. He can be considered a No. 1 quarterback in fantasy leagues.
