Running up the score & what else to watch as Chiefs play host to Arizona Cardinals | The Kansas City Star

Chiefs player to watch: defensive end Ashton Gillotte

George Karlaftis and Chris Jones aren't the only Chiefs who have collected sacks in recent games.

Gillotte has picked up a sack in each of the past two outings, and his takedown of Michael Penix Jr. in the red zone last week might have been the biggest defensive play of the game in the Chiefs' road victory over the Falcons.

Chiefs coach Andy Reid has noticed, too: "You've got Gillotte, who's playing out of his mind right now at both tackle and end."

NFL Week 11 predictions: Upsets, fantasy sleepers, stat lines, TD scorers, more | ESPN

George Karlaftis, Edge, Kansas City Chiefs: 2.5 sacks, 7 pressures, 31% pass rush win rate

It's hard to imagine a better matchup to record a sack than this. Karlaftis typically lines up opposite the left tackle, which means he'll face Arizona's Jayden Williams — who ranks dead last in pass block win rate at tackle but has missed the past two weeks with a knee injury — or Williams' backup Josh Fryar.

Karlaftis will be chasing down rookie QB Carson Beck in his second career start — Beck took five sacks (at over a 20% sack rate) in his debut against the Rams — or veteran journeyman Josh Johnson. Plus, the Chiefs are 15.5-point favorites, so the Cardinals almost certainly will be behind and need to pass.

Titans DE Jaylen Harrell accused of assaulting rideshare driver, hospitalized after crash | The Athletic

Harrell was riding in the back seat of a Kia Sorento rideshare around 8:46 a.m. Friday when "he reportedly assaulted the driver while the vehicle was in motion," according to a Tampa Police Department preliminary report. The driver, identified by police as a man in his mid-30s, then swerved, struck a Toyota Highlander and hit a utility pole.

Harrell was thrown forward upon impact, the police report stated, hitting the windshield and losing consciousness. The 24-year-old was then taken to a nearby hospital with serious injuries, where police said he was still receiving medical care as of Saturday. Police said the rideshare driver suffered "minor injuries" and was treated at the scene, while the driver of the Highlander was uninjured.

Sources: Rams' Puka Nacua out 4-5 months after knee surgery, will miss playoffs | ESPN

Puka Nacua's recovery time from MCL surgery is considered to be four to five months, sources told ESPN on Saturday, meaning the Los Angeles Rams star wide receiver will miss the rest of this season, including the playoffs.

Sources told ESPN that three doctors recommended that Nacua undergo the procedure, which is expected to have him healthy for the 2027 campaign.

Nacua's right knee checked out clean aside from the MCL, according to a source. The operation was performed by team doctor Neal ElAttrache, a source told ESPN.

Texans WR Nico Collins slapped with $14,926 unsportsmanlike conduct fine for 'violent gesture' in Week 10 | Yahoo! Sports

The NFL has cracked down on gun-related celebrations over the past few years, with finger guns, grenade or rifle gestures and similar celebrations leading to big fines. Collins is the fifth player this season to be fined for a "violent gesture."

First-time offenses for unsportsmanlike conduct, like with Collins, result in a $14,926 fine; a second offense will cost a player $20,897.
