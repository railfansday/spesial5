Raiders sign QB Brett Rypien to the practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Raiders needed another quarterback after trading Kirk Cousins to the Vikings. They accomplished that Monday by signing Brett Rypien to the practice squad.

Rypien joins Fernando Mendoza and Aidan O’Connell in the quarterbacks room.

The Raiders released defensive end Bralen Trice from the practice squad to make room for Rypien.

Rypien was most recently with the Buccaneers, signing with their practice squad in September, when Baker Mayfield dislocated his thumb.

Rypien, the nephew of Super Bowl XXVI MVP Mark Rypien, has appeared in games with the Broncos, Rams and Bengals. He has played 11 games, going 2-2 as a starter, but has not appeared in a game since 2023.
