So much for a Spurs fan to be thankful for in 2026 and beyond

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

My first Thanksgiving post for Pounding the Rock was on November 27, 2014, about a month after I joined the team. (The Pounding the Rock team, not the Spurs.) I didn't write about being thankful for the Redemption Finals, even thought we were all VERY thankful for, and the joy that win brought all basketball fans. Instead, I wrote about a conversation I had with NBA great Rudy Tomjanovich earlier that month:

"Especially in light of everything going on in the sports world, I loved to hear Rudy T's comments about the team we all root for. He used the words "good people". Among all the things to be thankful for this Thanksgiving, the list includes the ability to cheer on a Spurs team that is a group of good people. Not everyone can say that, but we can. Happy Thanksgiving, Spurs Nation."

I feel the same way about the present Spurs team. Watching the players interact with each other, the coaches and the staff, and in their interviews with the media, the Spurs continue to be "good people". The way the guys on the bench support the players in the game is truly wonderful. I remain thankful to root for a team with good people.

Things changed a lot for the Spurs between 2014 and 2019. In my short Thanksgiving post on November 28, 2019, I tried to find some things to be thankful for in a season that ultimately ended with the COVID lockdown, the Spurs at 33-39 and missing out on the Bubble Playoffs:

"At this point in the season, it may be difficult for Spurs fans to find something to be thankful for. But remember we still have: (1) the memory of 22 straight years of winning; (2) likable and exciting players during those 22 years, including the Big Three (3), who won more regular season games and more playoff games than any threesome ever; (4) the best NBA coach ever; (5) an excellent front office, and (6) being part of a passionate and knowledgeable fan base. And, of course, the Coyote. So Happy Thanksgiving, Spurs fans."

Four years later, a portion of my 2023 Thanksgiving post remains as true now as then:

"The ex-Spurs. I am very thankful that the Spurs of our (relative) youth are still around and invested in the team. It is always great to see David Robinson in the stands at a Spurs home game, except for the fan who has to sit directly behind his 7'1" self. The Big Three also seem to be around a lot. Manu Ginobili in particular, I am confident is haunting Spurs' practices teaching the youngsters how to throw long one-handed bounce passes through traffic."

I remain thankful every time they show Manu, the Admiral or the Great Duncan it attedance for games in 2026. I am sure they enjoy seeing this team carry on the Spurs tradition.

Part of that tradition is drafting well, when the Spurs have the fop pick in the draft (the Admiral and TD), late first round picks (Tony Parker), and second round picks (Manu!). But no team can match the three consecutive drafts that produced Victor Wembanyama, Stephon Castle and Dylan Harper (plus Carter Bryant as a bonus). We really need a name for those three drafts – "Big Three" is already copyrighted. Those young players were key to last season's Thanksgiving column, written in late November 2025. That was early in the season, and thus well before anyone even suspected that the young Spurs would win the Western Conference and make the NBA for the first time in over a decade. I wrote:

"The main thing Spurs fans are thankful for: we finally have a good team again."

By contrast, this season's buzz around the league, and the team, is completely different. Even without Fox for the start of the season, and Victor's recent absence, the team has remained competitive. I am very thankful to once again turn on the game and expect a Spurs win – a feeling we haven't had in years. I am also thankful to hear the pre-game commentators on the various networks describe the team in glowing terms, also something we have not been hearing for a while."

Now we are in November 2026, with Thanksgiving upon us. Time to mention some things I am thankful for, and for this year, a few things which I am not thankful for.

As mentioned above, we can push repeat on some things to be thankful for: The Spurs are good again, remain good people while winning games, and are blessed to have the ex-Spurs out supporting the team. I will add something that his year's team has that the Big Three 'teams did not — youth! When the 2014 team won it all, it came with a sense that the Big Three era was nearing its end. Even with the disappointment of the 2026 Finals, we can be thankful to have the hope that the Spurs' latest appearance in the Finals might just be the first of many.

I am also thankful that Selena Gomez is a Spurs fan! Who knew?

But back to good things. I have said many times that the key thing to playing defense is caring — a team with players that really care about stopping the other team from scoring will do all the right things to make that happen. This Spurs team clearly cares about defense, and this former coach is thankful to see how hard the team defends.

I am especially thankful to being able to look at the NBA standings every morning and see San Antonio on the top line. We will all be especially thankful if the Spurs stay on that top line through the regular season and playoffs. If they do, I might just make a big Thanksgiving dinner in late June 2027 and invite all my friends. Even those who are Knick fans.
