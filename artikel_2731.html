NASCAR taps Busch Light and Glen says 'May I have another?' Bye-bye bowlers

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

If you reflexively answered "NEVER!," you're not wrong. Of course you're not wrong.

But then again, you've never had to sell sponsorship of a major automobile race. Not all decisions are an either-or scenario, but when it comes to naming something, someone has to speak up, and guess who has the loudest voice: That's right, Mr. Dollar.

Just over a century ago, President Calvin Coolidge famously said, "The chief business of the American people is business."

Granted, "Silent Cal" also never had to sell sponsorships, but he surely would understand why the good folks in New York's Finger Lakes region have given up bowling for the refreshing flow of Busch Light.

Busch Light's NASCAR takeover has seen the opening of a new front: Watkins Glen, where the Bowling Proprietors of America have been replaced by the Boys in Marketing — St. Louis Division.

Anheuser-Busch, already one of NASCAR's four "Premier Partners," already the "Official Beer of NASCAR," and namesake of every Saturday's "Busch Light Pole Award," has now introduced the "Busch Light at the Glen," the newly christened Cup Series race slated for next September's postseason.

Except in rare instances (yes, generally in auto racing), there's only room for one title sponsor. So Busch Light's takeover ends Watkins Glen's bowling partnership, which means we say goodbye to one of the more unique race names in NASCAR: "Go Bowling at the Glen," which ran from 2018-2026.

With the length of the sponsorship contract concluded, and with the price likely increasing due to the Glen moving to the postseason Chase next year, the Bowling Proprietors unlaced their shoes, grabbed their ball bags and made way for the deep pockets of Anheuser-Busch and its expansionist Busch Light brand.

A nine-year title-sponsor run was a long one in any NASCAR era, so don't weep for the bowlers. And frankly, be happy the new presenter is a household name. Looking back at some of the Glen's past title sponsors, you see quite a mix …

"Helluva Good! Sour Cream Dips at the Glen" (2009-11).

While everyone loves a Cheez-It, Busch Light definitely has more of a NASCAR feel. And guess who's happy about all that — yep, the Glen's leadership team.

"With The Glen returning to The Chase in 2027, we're proud to build on our road-racing tradition together and welcome fans to the Finger Lakes for a fall weekend with championship stakes," said Watkins Glen track president Dawn Burlew — and I know what you're thinking, and you're right.

You can't spell Burlew without the b-r-e-w.
