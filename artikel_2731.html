Vintage Paul George drops Kyshawn George to the floor with ankle-breaking crossover in NBA Cup win

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Paul George is in the midst of a career renaissance in his first season with the Boston Celtics.

Washington Wizards guard Kyshawn George learned the hard way Tuesday night. With the Celtics leading in the second half of their NBA Cup matchup in Washington, Paul sized up Kyshawn in isolation.

He gained some separation with an arm extension on the perimeter, then crossed Kyshawn over as he drove to his right. Kyshawn did not stay with him.

Instead, Kyshawn fell on his backside on to the court and into the legs of his teammate, Anthony Davis.

Paul picked up his dribble and waited a beat while shooting a glance at Kyshawn — just to make sure Kyshawn was watching. Only then did Paul pull up for a jumper from just inside the 3-point arc that sank through the net to extend Boston's lead to 90-65.

George, 36, entered Tuesday's game doing an admirable job of making Celtics fans forget about Jaylen Brown, whom he was traded for. He delivered agains Tuesday night with 28 points, 9 assists and 4 rebounds while shooting 6 of 9 from 3 as the Celtics held off a late Wizards rally for a 131-116 win.

George entered Tuesday averageing 19.9 points, 5.6 rebounds, 3.8 assists and 2.2 steals as the clear No. 2 option behind Jayson Tatum. He remains one of the game's best shooters with a 40.7% success rate from 3. And as Kyshawn learned Tuesday night, he's still got some moves.
