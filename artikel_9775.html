I built a Windows app to explore and compare Instagram data exports locally

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Instagram gives you a ZIP when you request a copy of your data. I built InstagramTracker to make that archive easier to browse and to compare two exports from the same account.

The Windows preview lets you open the ZIP on your computer, see which categories it contains, and compare follower and following lists across two exports. You can do this without signing in to Instagram or uploading the ZIP. A missing entry shows a difference between the exports, but it doesn’t tell you why it disappeared.

The project is MIT-licensed. The Windows x64 preview is portable and the executable is unsigned.
