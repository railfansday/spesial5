The Bambu Lab P1S Combo 3D printer hits record-low price on Amazon — save $50 this Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

SAVE $50: As of Nov. 11, the Bambu Lab P1S is on sale for $349 at Amazon. That's a 13% discount on the list price.

If you've been eyeing up a 3D printer for custom creations or personalized gifts, you might not have to wait until Black Friday to find a decent deal. Early discounts are already appearing, including this latest deal on the Bambu Lab P1S, a ready-to-use FDM 3D printer designed to make creating your own objects from digital designs more straightforward.

This 3D printer is designed to make creating detailed, multi-color models quicker and easier, with printing speeds of up to 500 mm/s and an acceleration of 20,000 mm/s². It supports up to 16-color printing when connected to a compatible Bambu Lab AMS system, giving you more flexibility when working on different designs.
