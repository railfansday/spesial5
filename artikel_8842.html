30 Relatable Things That Are Perfectly Normal for Yoga Teachers to Do

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

“Have I never had an original experience?”

Chances are you’ve seen this exact question written in the comments section on social media. There’s relief in being able to relate to other people’s silly, honest, or otherwise unfiltered life moments, because you start to realize that you’re not alone. You’re human.

You could say that yoga teachers have their own version of the “perfectly normal” trend. It’s perfectly normal for yoga teachers to be completely imperfect. And isn’t embracing your mistakes exactly what yoga encourages us to do, anyway? If you’ve had your fair share of slip ups, quirks, and completely unserious moments as a yoga teacher, consider yourself part of the club.
