Archohm: Indian Contemporary Architecture

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

This publication compiles 25 years of work by Studio Archohm, highlighting their evolution into key players in Indian nation-building through large-scale urban, infrastructure, and civic projects, alongside private-sector experimentation.The studio's multidisciplinary portfolio spans design research, city branding, education, and experience design, connecting scales from small objects to entire master plans. The featured projects seek to unlock space potential while balancing indigenous practices with global perspectives to reimagine a contemporary Indian design identity. Richly illustrated with plans, diagrams, and photography, the volume includes project breakdowns and essays from principals and international critics, capturing the firm's core philosophy to celebrate life through design.
