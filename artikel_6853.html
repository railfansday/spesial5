Pamela Anderson’s Cranberry Nails Are the Most Elegant Fall Manicure: How to Recreate

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Not only is the shape practical, but the color is timeless.

I said it before, and I’ll say it again: Pamela Anderson has become one of the most unexpected beauty muses. From her pared-back approach to makeup to her ever-changing hairstyles, the actor has spent recent years setting trends that span generations. And her latest manicure is making a rather convincing case for swapping your usual autumnal burgundy for something a little juicier.

Taking to Instagram, Anderson recently shared a video reel documenting her recent evening spent gardening, reading, and simply living her best life. "Fall is the season of the soul… 🤍🍂," she wrote to caption the post. While the wholesome content had me questioning my own screen-time report (spoiler: it’s an unhealthy amount), it was her nails that left a lasting impression on the beauty editor in me. And she’s been rocking a versatile fall hue everywhere from her backyard to the CFDA Awards.

A far cry from her signature French manicure, Anderson debuted a glossy, cranberry-red shade painted onto short, squoval-shaped nails. Not only is the shape practical and universally flattering, but the color is timeless and an autumn manicure classic.

To recreate Pamela Anderson’s cranberry squoval nails, you’re going to want to invest some time into the prep. I’m talking trimming your cuticles, filing your nails down, and rounding the corners off for that signature square-meets-oval shape.

Then comes the polish. A bright-toned red like essie’s Forever Yummy or OPI’s Big Apple Red is going to be your best bet. Finish with a generous helping of a high-shine top coat for that freshly picked, almost wet-gloss look, and you’re good to go!

Who knew a trip down the fruit and vegetable aisle could double as a nail appointment? I’ll definitely be channeling the duality of Anderson both gardening and attending a chic red carpet with this fall-ready manicure.
