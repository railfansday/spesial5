Julia Garner Just Randomly Reunited With Anna Delvey 4 Years After ‘Inventing Anna’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

No, you’re not seeing double. Julia Garner really reunited with her Inventing Anna muse, Anna Delvey, four years after she played a fictionalized version of her on TV.

It all went down at a double-feature fan screening of Netflix’s The Altruists and showrunner Graham Moore’s 2014 movie The Imitation Game at the Paris Theater in New York City on November 16. During a Q&A, Julia noticed a familiar face in the crowd, which turned out to be the real Anna.

According to fan-captured footage, the Weapons star invited Anna to join them on stage shortly after she spotted her in the audience. The pair shared a hug before Anna sat down on the panel, and after all these years, I still can’t help but do a double take.

Of course, the internet had a lot to say about their impromptu reunion.

“Because celebrating scammers and thieves is cool now,” one person sarcastically commented, while another added, “I can’t…. Not with a con artist.”

Others were fully here for Anna’s surprise appearance, with someone else chiming in, “Icon meets Icon.”

This marks Julia and Anna’s first public appearance together since Julia first met Anna in prison while preparing for her role. The Netflix series, loosely inspired by The Cut’s 2018 article “Maybe She Had So Much Money She Just Lost Track of It,” received awards buzz when it dropped in 2022.

Julia’s portrayal of Anna landed her several award nominations, including a Primetime Emmy, Critics’ Choice Award, a Golden Globe, and a SAG Award.

As for Anna, she still resides in a luxury apartment in New York City while serving her ongoing legal and immigration restrictions.

Seeing these two together in the big 2026 was not on my bingo card, but here we are!!
