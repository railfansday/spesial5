‘Dancing With the Stars’ Celeb and Pro Plan to Reveal Secret Relationship After the Season Ends

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Rumor has it that Dancing With the Stars’ front-runner Ezra Frech and pro Hailey Bills are secretly dating, and while neither of them have confirmed...literally anything (in fact, Ezra claimed he was “single” just this week!), a source says they plan to come clean about their relationship once the show ends.

“Hailey and Ezra are getting even flirtier and more serious as each week goes on,” an insider tells The Sun. “As the season continues, they are getting more comfortable sharing their feelings about each other with the cast. They still want to keep it on the DL publicly so Ezra can focus on the last few weeks of finishing this season strong. So they won’t be posting together anytime soon. But if they’re still together after the season ends, they’ll proudly show off each other then.”

They added that “Hailey is so smitten by Ezra and he treats her with so much respect and is in awe of her. They both share a similar sense of humor and just laugh and laugh and laugh together backstage.”

Meanwhile, Ezra recently told Emma Slater and Sharna Burgess during a TikTok Live on Disney Night, “I’m single. I’m locked in on dancing.”

FYI, a source previously told The Sun that “They are being really careful not to post each other right now because they don’t want the chatter about them potentially dating to overtake everything else. Ezra is really serious about making it to the finals and even winning this show. At the end of the day, he has the mindset of an Olympian. So he’s not letting a crush get in the way of winning the show. So they’re being strict about not posting together until the season is over.”
