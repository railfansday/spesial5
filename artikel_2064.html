Former Leeds United and Sheffield United boss will be in charge of Crawley Town for the rest of the season

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The 67-year-old was appointed interim head coach after the sacking of Colin Kazim-Richards in October with the club bottom of League Two.

A club statement read: “Over the past few weeks, the club has been working hard to make the right appointment and has been carefully weighing up the options to ensure that the appointment is the right one for the team and the club as a whole.

“During this process, Kevin Blackwell made a strong impression on club leadership, making him a strong candidate for the role as the weeks progressed.

“Kevin’s leadership skills, work ethic and desire have made a strong impression on club staff and the first-team squad, and the club’s leadership team are committed to ensuring the positive mentality remains in the building as we approach a run of crucial games.

“Kevin has the full support of everyone at the football club, as we look to turn over a new page on our season and climb back up the table.”

And Blackwell was pleased. He said: “I'm delighted to stay here. I have to say, I've been so impressed with what I've seen around the place. As I've said before in my press conferences, Raph [Raphael Khalili] and the ownership have supported everything that I've asked them to do. We're bringing in new staff, the club's taken a whole new vamp.

“I have to say, the thing that really persuaded me at the end was the players. I think their attitude has been absolutely fabulous. I can honestly tell you we've done some really hard sessions here, as hard as I've known.

“While they've found it tough, not one of them has complained about it and they've worked hard. I'm really here because the players have really helped make my mind up. I was, as you know, 50-50 what I was going to do, but let's say two weeks here, I've liked what I've seen."

In the coming weeks, Kevin will be making further appointments to his coaching team, following the recent arrival of Jordan Tait.

A statement from ownership will follow shortly.
