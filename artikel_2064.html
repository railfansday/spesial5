West Brom must accelerate £5m Barnsley deal to secure promotion

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

West Bromwich Albion head into the international break on a high after defeating Millwall 3-0 at The Hawthorns, as James Morrison's side launch themselves well and truly into promotion contention.

West Brom have continued where they left off last season under Morrison, with the Scottish head coach turning the Baggies from a club on the brink of relegation to League One to one seriously challenging for a return to the Premier League.

The 40-year-old, who is in his first head coaching role of his career, has performed admirably both on and off the pitch, and if his performances as a player didn't have West Brom supporters in awe, his as a coach surely are.

If the Baggies can maintain this level of form through until January and remain in the Championship promotion conversation, the winter window becomes a real opportunity to strike while the iron is hot, and preparations for the new year must already be underway.

West Brom recruited brilliantly over the summer, shifting some ageing, high earners off the books such as Jed Wallace, Josh Maja, Daryl Dike, Alex Mowatt, and Krystian Bielik, whilst bringing in hungry, fresh blood such as Jimmy-Jay Morgan, Brayann Pereira, Rabby Nzingoula, Kareem Tunde, and Felix Horn Myhre.

The Baggies squad, across the board, has strength and depth in almost every position, with few notable weaknesses throughout. However, that doesn't mean they should ignore the opportunity to strengthen even further.

If promotion looks a serious possibility for the club, they must throw everything they have at the winter window to ensure they have the firepower to get over the line, and ought to sanction the return of a player who was never given the opportunity to show what he is capable of.

Starting on the left flank more often than not for West Brom this season has been Northern Ireland international Isaac Price, and with eight goals and three assists in 17 appearances across all competitions so far this season, he is amongst their most important players.

The 23-year-old has made that position his own, though he is capable of operating anywhere across the final third, as Morrison must consider sanctioning the return of former youth sensation Reyes Cleary this winter.

Cleary, 22, came through the ranks at West Brom before spending time out on loan with Walsall in League Two and Hartlepool United in the National League, to varying degrees of success.

He would join Barnsley for a minimal fee and a reported 20 per cent sell-on clause in the summer of 2025, before going on to have an incredible debut year in the third tier under former Tykes boss Conor Hourihane.

In his first year at Barnsley, Cleary netted seven and grabbed 13 in 40 League One games from the left flank as the Tykes stumbled to a 15th-place finish.

Over the summer, the 22-year-old was linked with an array of Championship clubs, as Mark McAdam reported via X that the likes of Millwall, Bristol City, Blackburn Rovers, Stoke City, and Derby County were all interested in his services, as the club rejected bids of around £3 million for the winger, demanding a guaranteed £5 million to allow for his departure.

With six goals and three assists in 17 appearances across all competitions this season so far, Cleary has maintained a decent level of form despite Barnsley's struggles, and is ready to make the jump to the Championship ASAP.

Interest in the England youth international will surely reemerge sooner rather than later, and when it does, West Brom must make sure they are towards the front of the queue and not let him slip through their fingers once again.

The winger could be the catalyst that helps send West Brom back to the Premier League once again, and if they are involved in the promotion conversation come January, Cleary must be made a priority.
