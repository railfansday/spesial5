Governors Awards 2026: Fashion—All of the Best Looks From the Red Carpet

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Tonight, the 2026 Governor Awards in Hollywood serve as the official kickoff to the 2027 awards season. (Yes, it starts this early!) Presented by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, the ceremony is being held at the Ray Dolby Ballroom, and will celebrate key figures in the film world—including special awards for actor Glenn Close, director Ridley Scott, and animator Floyd Norman, among others.

Before all the accolades are handed out on stage, however, the Hollywood elite will hit the red carpet in their finest formal wear—choosing custom or fresh-off-the-runway gowns that are made for shining in the paparazzi flashbulbs. The annual red carpet affair, after all, is known to deliver plenty of glamour. Just see last year’s striking array of gowns and tailoring.
