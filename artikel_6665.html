21 Easy Weeknight Dinners for November

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

As daylight dwindles and the November chill settles in, dinner calls for something cozy. Think chicken thighs nestled into caramelized onions, pasta slicked with brown butter, and hearty soups that practically demand a hunk of crusty bread. It’s time to lean into comfort food.

The good news? You don’t need to spend your whole Sunday cooking to make dinner feel special, so you’ll have plenty of time for that When Harry Met Sally rewatch. These 21 easy weeknight dinners deliver all the comfort without the commitment. There’s a three-ingredient pumpkin soup, gochujang chicken that gets a quick blast under the broiler, and a smoky one-pan pasta made with pantry staples. Plus a few Thanksgiving-adjacent flavors—sage, stuffing, squash—because the holiday is right around the corner.

Greek yogurt tenderizes the chicken quickly, but the real weeknight win is the broiler: just 15 minutes under high heat, and you’ve got dinner.

Juicy chicken meatballs and crisp-tender sesame green beans roast on opposite sides of the same sheet pan, streamlining both dinner prep and cleanup.

This speedy stir-fry recipe brings together thin strips of boneless pork shoulder and crisp-tender bok choy for a satisfying, weeknight-ready dinner.

Savory ground beef, fluffy scrambled eggs, and crisp vegetables turn a bowl of steamed rice into a satisfying Japanese dinner that’s ready in 35 minutes.

Give boxed stuffing mix a new lease on life with this clever, holiday-inspired crust. Mixed with Parmesan, lemon zest, parsley, and butter, it gives tender fish a golden, crispy topping. Genius.

Brushed with a sticky, sweet teriyaki sauce, then hurled under the broiler, this salmon develops a glossy, caramelized finish in a matter of minutes.

This easy sheet-pan dinner pairs tender cod with a savory chorizo topping. Big flavor, minimal effort, and only one pan to wash.

Canned chickpeas and frozen spinach make this richly spiced saag quick to prepare—and any leftovers taste even better the next day.

Inspired by satay, this saucy, lip-smacking tofu dish comes together in 30 minutes in just one skillet—so cleanup is a breeze.

Cheesy, creamy, and deeply comforting, these fondue-inspired beans bring a little alpine indulgence to an ordinary Tuesday. Serving them over crusty bread is a must.

Nutty brown butter, crunchy pecans, and hot Italian sausage make this an easy dinner bursting with fall flavors. One reader reports, “Made me feel like a 5-star chef.”

Buttery, garlicky shrimp scampi meets pasta in this weeknight-friendly dinner. A splash of white wine and a pinch of red pepper flakes make the sauce sing.

Canned butternut squash purée (find it by the canned pumpkin!) makes this cozy, cold-weather recipe come together in a snap. Vegetable broth and certified vegetarian parm keep it meat-free, but chicken broth works if that’s what you’ve got.

Four ingredients are all you need for these grown-up buttered noodles. Toasted almonds and parsley are totally optional, but add welcome texture and zing.

Pumpkin purée adds richness, body, and a gentle orange hue to this speedy ground beef chili, letting the flavors of the spices and aromatics shine.

Roasted squash, crispy bacon, and crunchy walnuts turn chopped salad into a proper fall dinner. Use pre-cut butternut squash to streamline prep.

This comforting chicken noodle soup is ideal for chilly evenings or sick days. Chicken breast, kabocha squash, and tiny ditalini cook right in the broth.

A vegetarian spin on the classic Reuben swaps in hearty kale for the meat but keeps the melty cheese and tangy sauerkraut. One reader reports, “I didn’t miss the pastrami one bit.”

Made almost entirely from pantry ingredients—S&B curry powder, red lentils, crushed tomatoes, and coconut milk—this cozy soup is, as one reviewer puts it, “way more delicious than it should be.”

When dinner needs to get on the table pronto, a tuna melt is hard to beat: pantry-friendly, cheesy, and comforting.

This fiery soup has a shockingly trim ingredient list: curry paste (we like Maesri), a can of pumpkin purée, and coconut milk. Perfect for busy November weeknights.
