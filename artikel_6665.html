All About Phoebe Dynevor’s Husband, Cameron Fuller

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Phoebe Dynevor became a fan favorite during the first season of Bridgerton. The Netflix show focused on the love story between her character Daphne and Regé-Jean Page’s Simon. Their romance ended with a happily-ever-after, and Dynevor has continued to build her career in Hollywood. Off camera, her romantic life has been flourishing as well. In September 2026, the actress married Cameron Fuller, an actor himself and someone very familiar with the entertainment industry as the son of a famous producer.

Here’s everything to know about Dynevor’s relationship with Fuller so far.

Fuller is the son of producer Brad Fuller, who specializes in horror films like The Purge franchise, A Quiet Place, and A Nightmare on Elm Street. The younger Fuller studied performing in college, receiving a bachelor of arts in theater from the University of Southern California in 2017. He has since appeared on shows like Insecure, Into the Dark, The Goldbergs, and The Last Ship.

In March 2023, Dynevor and Fuller were seen leaving a hotel in New York City together. A month later, they were photographed holding hands in London, eventually meeting up with Fuller’s father for some shopping. A few pics showed some PDA where Fuller’s arms were wrapped around the actress.

In July, they were photographed enjoying Wimbledon.

E! broke the news of their engagement on May 7, 2024, confirming that Dynevor unveiled her engagement ring at the 2024 Met Gala.

In 2022, Fuller was romantically linked to Pretty Little Liars’ Lucy Hale briefly. He also dated influencer Lauren Elizabeth for a few years. In 2021, Dynevor dated Saturday Night Live alum Pete Davidson for five months.

Yes. Dynevor and Fuller left the Corinthia Hotel together and headed to the BAFTAs on Feb. 18, 2024.

The Bridgerton star was nominated for EE Rising Star and was dressed in a white gown with a low-cut halter neckline by Louis Vuitton and a pair of gold sandals. Fuller wore a classic black tux.

Dynevor and Fuller were spotted at the US Open on Aug. 26, 2024, making their first public appearance since revealing their engagement. The pair looked smitten and stylish as they watched tennis matches together. The Bridgerton star wore a navy pinstripe vest with jeans, accessorizing with a small white purse. Meanwhile, her partner opted for a laid-back look in a white T-shirt, gray cardigan, and blue pants.

On April 8, 2025, Dynevor and Fuller posed together at the Los Angeles red carpet premiere of Drop. Dynevor stood out in a bridal white gown, while her partner was sharp in a black suit. Her long-sleeve gown featured a surprise leg slit.

In mid-December, Dynevor and Fuller made another rare public appearance together in Los Angeles, attending the after-party for The Housemaid following its premiere. The couple stepped out at TAO, where they were photographed with Sydney Sweeney.

Dynevor wore a black top paired with a matching skirt, while Fuller also opted for an all-black look.

Yes. Dynevor confirmed her marriage to Fuller on Sept. 17, 2026, nearly two weeks after the couple reportedly wed in Provence, France, on Sept. 5, 2026. She shared two black-and-white wedding photos on Instagram: one showed the newlyweds walking together after the ceremony, while another captured them kissing beneath a floral arch.

“Married my best friend🌙,” Dynevor captioned the post.

Earlier in the month, Fuller’s close friend, actor Sam Lerner, also shared photos from the wedding weekend on Instagram. The festivities included several of the couple’s famous friends, including Logan Lerman, Cole Sprouse, and Kiernan Shipka.

On Nov. 11, 2026, Dynevor was photographed wearing her slim gold wedding band with her engagement ring while running errands in London’s Notting Hill neighborhood.

In April 2026, Dynevor shared how she first met Fuller during an appearance on The Drew Barrymore Show.

“I did a film called Fair Play,” she said, “and I went to Sundance, and it was a big high in my career, it was like one of the first movies I’d done, and it was getting all this hype so I was feeling good, and there he was.”

She added, “It was at TAO, the pop-up. We were at the club.”
