Artist Quentin Jones Launches a Surrealist Jewelry Collection

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

With Nocturne, the House of Quentin Jones founder brings the playful, sculptural language of her homewares to a collection of wearable art.

Above: Artist Quentin Jones wears pieces from her new jewelry collection while posing in front of tapestries Circe’s Garden and Western Moon.

British mixed-media artist Quentin Jones has quite the varied resume—one that spans artistic disciplines and collaborators as varied as Chanel, Apple, and Miley Cyrus. Now based in Brooklyn, her practice blends filmmaking, photography, collage, and painting.

Last year, Jones turned her eye towards interiors, launching a homeware line and design studio called House of Quentin Jones. The brand debuted with a collection of tapestries and ceramic sculptures that are both architectural and playful, leaning into Jones’s moody, dreamy look. Now, after a year of HOQJ, Jones is releasing new styles—and adding jewelry to the mix.

Nocturne, a collection of sculptural jewelry, translates Jones’s eclectic design language into wearable art. She pulled motifs like eyes, hands, and celestial forms from her tapestries and ceramics and incorporated them into the pieces. The collection features earrings, pendants, chains, and cuffs that balance black and white, pearl and onyx, organic and sculptural shapes.

“I’m interested in jewelry as sculpture,” Jones tells ELLE Decor. “A cuff wraps around an arm, a collar frames the neck, a tassel moves. The body becomes part of the composition. I want the pieces to have a presence of their own, while leaving room for the personality of the person wearing them.”

As she enters this new category, Jones is also beefing up her existing offerings. She’s adding new quilted tapestries to the studio’s repertoire and iterating on previous designs. With these new pieces, she says, “The palette has become softer and earthier—ivory, sand, and burgundy—but the compositions still have that surreal, graphic quality that runs through my work.”

But she isn’t stopping there. Jones has big dreams for the future of her eponymous studio, a “House” built larger with each new release. Next up, she’d like to expand the accessories category further, hoping to add bags, scarves, and other wearable pieces to her lineup.

“I’ve spent years working between art, film, and fashion,” she says. “I’m building towards a collection of things that can accompany you through your life—from what hangs on your wall to what you choose to wear.”
