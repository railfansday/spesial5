Here's what's on their wish list... for now, at least.

Oh, you think shopping for a teenager is rough these days? Try figuring out what’s still in style among the 9- to 12-year-old set. While you could play it safe and stock them up on so much viral TikTok stuff, you run the risk of picking something that’s no longer “cool”—tween whims seem to change with zero notice to anyone over the age of 21. But never fear: our gift guide editors on top of it!

We spend our days poring over beauty samples, viral tech gadgets, and the latest fashion trends that have captured the attention of the average tween. If she's seen it on TikTok, we've probably tried it too. With the holidays around the corner, use our edit to surprise her with a special gift.

Whether she's redecorating her room for the third time or amassing a beauty collection that's bigger than yours, we found the gifts that’ll make her think you're a mind reader.
