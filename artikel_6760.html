Lights, Camera, Action! An Exclusive Look Inside ELLE’s 2026 Women in Hollywood Celebration

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Florence Pugh, Julianne Moore, Penélope Cruz, and more were honored at our annual event.

Los Angeles has no shortage of awards shows, but ELLE’s annual Women in Hollywood event has always been about something more: putting the spotlight on the women whose work makes the industry worth watching. On Monday night, eight of film and television’s most talked-about talents came together to celebrate their collective achievements, with plenty of oohs, aahs, and well-deserved gushes along the way.

This year’s honorees—Carrie Coon, Elizabeth Debicki, Rosalind Eleazar, Julianne Moore, Samantha Morton, Inde Navarrette, Florence Pugh, and Penélope Cruz—gathered at the Four Seasons Hotel Los Angeles at Beverly Hills for an evening hosted by ELLE editor-in-chief Nina Garcia.

Presented in partnership with Ralph Lauren and Bulgari, with support from Evian, the soirée drew familiar faces from across Tinseltown. Matt Damon, Seth Rogen, Bryce Dallas Howard, Alexander Skarsgård, and Louisa Jacobson were among those who took the stage to present the honors, while Morton accepted hers remotely from her home in London, where she was sick with the flu.

Beyond the eight honorees, the guest list included Lily James, Ashley Park, Elsa Hosk, Madison Beer, Jackie Tohn, Molly Sims, and more.

Champagne and yuzu mocktails flowed as guests mingled before making their way into the ballroom, where a three-course dinner awaited: beet salad, Chilean sea bass, and chocolate tortino.

Garcia kicked things off by celebrating a banner year for moviegoing, while reminding the room that there’s still work to be done when it comes to creating opportunities for women. “We need roles for women that are messy, complicated, funny, and real—and sometimes even older than 50!” she said.

She pointed to the eight honorees as a reminder of what’s possible when those roles make it to the screen. “You are all proof of what happens when these roles are written—you knock it out of the park. These women we are honoring? The characters they have played? They are not decorative. They’re not supporting a man’s journey. They are women in full.”
