Costco’s “Cove” Console Table Gives You That Pottery Barn Aesthetic for Half the Price

This is the perfect piece to maximize your storage space, too.

There’s really no place like Costco because, let’s be honest, where else can you buy gigantic jars of mayonnaise and home decor in one fell swoop? Not only does the wholesaler sell everything from sweet sheds to small space-friendly furniture, but you can also find any type of design aesthetic for a fraction of the price. Case in point: Northridge Home’s Cascade Accent Console, which looks like a dead ringer for something you’d spot at Pottery Barn.

This storage unit’s hardwood frame and white oak veneers add rustic flair, while the curved wooden handle pulls offer a touch of modernity. Of course, there’s more to this console than good looks. The 68-inch-long structure has plenty of room to stow lots of blankets, books, and games, while two adjustable shelves will keep your stash organized. In fact, there are a few cutouts along the back so you can plug in a gaming console or Wi-Fi extender and hide your tech in plain sight.

Meanwhile, adjustable levelers and two pairs of soft-close Euro hinges keep squeaks, creaks, and clanks to a minimum.

While design enthusiasts love Pottery Barn for its elevated traditional pieces, if you’re on a budget, this is a good alternative. At $600, the Cascade Accent Console offers a similar look for half the price. For content creator Kelsey Taylor, who almost purchased several consoles three times the price before settling on this Costco find, it lives up to the hype.

“It exceeded our expectations,” she tells House Beautiful. “It’s even more gorgeous in person, and looks like something you’d find at Pottery Barn.” As a mom of three young children, Taylor loves how this piece blends with the storage and functionality needed for everyday family life.

Sure, $600 isn’t exactly chump change, but it’s almost impossible to find a good table for that price. So to add smart, stylish furniture to the mix for under triple digits is nothing short of a shopping miracle. “It feels like such a high-end piece without the price tag,” Taylor says. We couldn’t agree more.
