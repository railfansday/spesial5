Week 11 heavily favors the home Bills, who are 8-1 and first in the division, over the 1-8 Dolphins, who are in contention for the first overall pick in the 2027 NFL Draft. Who could Miami select with such an early pick in the spring?

Do the Dolphins have a chance against the Bills? How will quarterback Kyle McCord, making his second career start, play against a Bills team that has feasted on backup quarterbacks over the past few weeks?

Will Miami see the return of injured starters like center Aaron Brewer? Is rookie wide receiver Chris Bell poised to break out?
