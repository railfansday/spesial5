Pacers get good news on guards Andrew Nembhard and Braden Smith vs. Cavs

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

CLEVELAND -- Pacers point guards Andrew Nembhard and Braden Smith will both be available for Friday's game against the Cavaliers, coach Rick Carlisle said in his pre-game press conference.

Nembhard missed the last two games with a left hip sprain. Smith suffered an ugly ankle sprain in Wednesday's loss to the Rockets and had to be carried off the floor. Both players were listed as questionable on the injury report.
