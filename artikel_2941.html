MotoGP Qatar GP: Full starting grid

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Aprilia's Marco Bezzecchi will start the Qatar Grand Prix from pole position after breaking the lap record in qualifying.

Marc Marquez will line up second on the grid and will have a chance of sealing his 10th world title - and eighth in MotoGP - on Sunday.

Bezzecchi and Marquez collided during the sprint on Saturday, leaving the former out of the race and the title fight.

Bezzecchi's team-mate Jorge Martin still has an outside shot at the title, but he will start down in 11th place. The Spaniard blamed Pedro Acosta for his poor showing in qualifying.

The front row will be completed by VR46's Fabio di Giannantonio.

Only 17 riders are expected to take the start, with Yamaha withdrawing the entire Losial weekend due to the geopolitical situation in the Middle East and Ducati's Francesco Bagnaia breaking his foot on Saturday.
