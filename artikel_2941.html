Bhayshul Tuten remains out of practice for the Jaguars

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Jaguars listed a key offensive player as out of practice for the second straight day on Tuesday.

Running back Bhayshul Tuten (ankle, foot) remained on the sideline after being listed as out on the team's estimated practice report on Monday. Tuten had 16 carries for 104 yards and a touchdown along with two catches for 55 yards and a score, so his presence would be significant against the Ravens.

Defensive end Travon Walker (rib) took a more positive step as he was upgraded to the limited category. Walker left Sunday's win over the Colts because of his injury, but the 42-7 blowout meant there was little reason for him to push for a quick return.

Defensive tackle DaVon Hamilton (quad) and safety Eric Murray (elbow) also missed practice and both players are going to miss the rest of the season.

Guard Ezra Cleveland (knee, ankle), safety Antonio Johnson (ankle), wide receiver Jakobi Meyers (hamstring, ankle), running back Chris Rodriguez (hand), linebacker Branson Combs (shoulder), and cornerback Jarrian Jones (ankle) were the other players listed as limited.
