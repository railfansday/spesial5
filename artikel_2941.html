Hoban vs. Walsh Jesuit, OHSAA high school playoff football score, live updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

It'll Hoban-Walsh Jesuit VIII for the seniors of Walsh Jesuit and Hoban football when the teams square off in a Division II, Region 5 quarterfinal at 7 p.m. Nov. 6.

The winner between the No. 10 Knights (4-6) and No. 2 Warriors (7-2) will play either No. 3 Nordonia (9-1) or No. 6 Eastlake North (10-1) in a regional semifinal next week.

Walsh has a 32-27 series edge on Hoban. The last time the two played, the Knights won 30-0 on Sept. 25. ... This is the 22nd time Hoban has made the playoffs. The Knights have reached the state semifinals 11 times, won titles in 2015-18 and 2020 and were runners-up in 2021-23. Hoban has a 64-16 postseason record. The Knights beat No. 7 Villa Angela-St. Joseph 38-7 on Oct. 30 to advance. The Warriors are making their 24th playoff appearance. They've reached the semifinals six times, have won a title in 1999 and were runners-up in 1998. Walsh has a 38-22 record in the playoffs.

The infusion of Kahlil Diming into the lineup was big for Hoban last week in a 31-point win over No. 7 Villa Angela-St. Joseph. Diming, who rushed for 96 yards and a score on 24 carries, played for the first time since Week 5. The 38 points were the second-most this season for the Knights, who beat Cincinnati Woodard 46-0 on Oct. 2. Diming has 505 yards and six TDs on 96 carries. Major Cribbs (119 carries, 873 yards, six TDs) leads the way in rushing with Jaiden Daniels (35 carries, 195 yards, four TDs) behind both of them. Cribbs is 103-of-181 passing for 1,155 yards and six scores. John Johnson (40 catches, 480 yards, three TDs), Cameron Chaney (19 catches, 192 yards, TD), Mario Manningham Jr. (16 catches, 191 yards) and Malachi Cook (11 catches, 160 yards, two TDs) are his main targets.

The Warriors come in behind freshman quarterback Kinzy Shea, who took over as for an injured David Ternosky in Week 7 as stater. Shea has offers from Penn State, Pitt, Kent State and Miami (Ohio) and interest from Ohio State, Notre Dame, Purdue and Illinois. Shea is 29-of-51 passing for 375 yards and seven TDs. … Cayden LeVert (28 catches, 426 yards, three TDs) and Cam Brunson (22 catches, 379 yards, four TDs) are his main targets. Dom Courtad (108 carries, 637 yards, eight TDs) and Mason Rodgers (47 carries, 226 yards, TD) man the rushing attack. … Dario Jeras (28 tackles, three for loss, 1.5 sacks), Emmit Tobin (63 tackles, seven for loss, six sacks), Caden Mariola (59 tackles, three for loss, two sacks, two caused fumbles, two fumble recoveries), Ben Biel (46 tackles, three for loss, two fumble recoveries) and Drew Wollschleger (50 tackle, caused fumble) are the dogs on defense.
