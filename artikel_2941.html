Josh Jacobs misses another practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Green Bay Packers RB Josh Jacobs (finger) did not participate in practice Thursday, Nov. 12.

Why It Matters: Jacobs is expected to miss at least Week 10 because of the injury and will likely miss more time. MarShawn Lloyd and Kaleb Johnson could share playing time while Jacobs is sidelined and can be flex options in fantasy leagues.
