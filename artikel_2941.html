Steve Kerr: ‘Lineup combinations are going to change’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

It was somewhat understandable — albeit, still a bitter pill to swallow — that the Golden State Warriors were shellacked by the league's best team in the San Antonio Spurs without Steph Curry being there to save the day. Scoring 14 points in the first quarter against a defense anchored by perhaps the best defender in the world in Victor Wembanyama doesn't need much explanation, especially when Curry — a proven hard counter to Wembanyama and his defensive abilities — is back in the Bay Area rehabbing a torn right oblique muscle.

However, scoring fewer than 14 points in the first quarter against the New Orleans Pelicans — heading into tonight, the fourth-worst defense in terms of points allowed per 100 possessions — is great cause for concern. The Warriors managed a paltry 11 points against the Pelicans in the first quarter, while giving up 35 in turn. It was a hole they never managed to climb out of, too insurmountable for even one of their patented fake comebacks to occur.

It's clear that without Curry — and to a certain extent, backup guard Brandon Williams — lineups have become wonky. Combinations that normally wouldn't see the light of day are being deployed. Such is the nature of a team that is without their best player, their second-best player, and a couple of key role players that connect everything together (not to mention, a center that virtually just up and left the team) — all combining for hundreds of millions of dollars in salary missing.

But that is the reality right now for the Warriors, who will have to find ways to tread water. One of those ways is finding combinations that do work — a task for Steve Kerr and his coaching staff to figure out.

The Warriors had perhaps their most winnable game of the road trip tonight — and did things that were far from winning basketball. The schedule isn't going to get any easier, with Dallas up next on Thursday, followed by Memphis the following night to complete a tough back-to-back slate. Three days after, they will thankfully return home — but against the Eastern Conference's best team in the Boston Celtics.

The NBA is a league without mercy — and the Warriors will just have to find ways to cope with that fact.
