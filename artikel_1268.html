Lamine Yamal Named Ballon d’Or Rival as His Toughest One-on-One Opponent Ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Lamine Yamal terrorises defenders all over the world, no matter the competition. Whether it's on the way to World Cup glory with Spain or a Champions League at Camp Nou for Barcelona.

The 19-year-old is already one of the best players in world football, having come third in the 2026 Ballon d'Or rankings, behind only Harry Kane and Kylian Mbappe.

Tracking the leading contenders to win the prestigious Ballon d'Or award in 2027.

Yamal is viewed as the long-term Lionel Messi successor in football, and it's clear to see why. His fearless dribbling and unwavering confidence are reminiscent of the greatest footballer in history.

Yamal won his first major international honour back in 2024, when he played a starring role in Spain's European Championship triumph. During that tournament, the teenager came up against his toughest opponent, who will surprise many.

He named Germany defender David Raum as the hardest left-back he'd come up against at that point. Raum has been at RB Leipzig since 2022, and was labelled a 'solid defender' by the best young player in the world.

He said (per Bundesliga): "David Raum has been the toughest opponent I've had to face. In the Euros, against Germany, he was tough to beat. A solid defender."

However, Yamal now has over 200 appearances to his name for club and country and has since changed his toughest opponent. According to ESPN, he now believes Portugal and Paris Saint-Germain star Nuno Mendes is the hardest player he's faced one-on-one.

Mendes is undoubtedly one of the finest defenders on the planet as an all-action member of PSG, the back-to-back Champions League winners.

He finished 23rd in the Ballon d'Or rankings this year, just 20 places behind Yamal. Mendes made the top 10 in the 2025 Ballon d'Or, showing just how impressive he's been for club and country.

Yamal and Mendes have met several times, with the latter getting the better of his opponent in a Champions League tie between Barcelona and PSG last year.

Mendes was named Player of the Match as the French giants won 2-1 at Camp Nou. He was humble after shutting down Yamal and even grabbing an assist going the other way.

Speaking after the match, Mendes said: "In every match, I try to do my best and help the team as much as possible. We know that in big games, with top-level players, people will talk about these duels.

"The most important thing is that I helped the team and stayed consistent after picking up a yellow card, because it's tough to defend against a player like Lamine when you're on a booking. I managed that well. I think it was a good match for the team, and also individually."
