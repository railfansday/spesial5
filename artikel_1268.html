Endrick price tag set as Real Madrid star targeted by Roma and Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Roma and Milan are on high alert after Endrick confessed he doesn’t ‘know what will happen in January’ due to a lack of regular playing time at Real Madrid, but the asking price is over €60m.

The Brazilian talent was a major investment for Real Madrid to poach him from Palmeiras for up to €60m including add-ons in 2024.

However, despite flashes of pure brilliance, the 20-year-old has consistently struggled to get into the starting XI.

“I just want to play, to be happy and help my team-mates,” said Endrick at a press conference while on international duty with Carlo Ancelotti’s Real Madrid.

“To score goals, provide assists, like at Lyon, one of the best things to have happened to me and one of the happiest times I’ve had.

“I don’t know what will happen in January. That’s in God’s hands. I only ask to do what’s best for my career. Whatever happens, happens. I’ll keep my feet on the ground and do my best. Madrid is a wonderful city, but I’ll always do everything I can to advance my career.”

There was a six-month loan spell with Olympique Lyonnais from January to June 2026, which helped Endrick secure his spot in the Brazil squad at the World Cup.

Roma and Milan were the most interested, along with Aston Villa, for a potential loan move over the summer, but Jose Mourinho was determined to keep the youngster in Madrid.

According to The Athletic, Real Madrid are now even less prepared to let him go out on loan, but could be open to a sale for over the €60m invested to purchase him two years ago.

Notably, Endrick came off the bench and transformed the game when Roma were leading Real Madrid 3-1, contributing a goal and an assist in the 4-3 comeback victory.

Since that night, he has only played a total of 36 minutes across four appearances in the Merengues jersey.
