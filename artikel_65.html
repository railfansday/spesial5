From leadership crises to tech outages, can corporations rebuild public trust?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

From cyberattacks to network failures to leadership crises, tech corporations are showing signs of vulnerability. The dramatic resignation and return of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has underscored the potential risk of depending on a single leader, both for the company and the entire AI industry. Meanwhile, global outages at Microsoft and a number of US banks have showcased declining public trust in institutions.
