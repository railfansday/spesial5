Myanmar's Rohingya case: how does the ICJ define genocide?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The ICJ is preparing to rule on whether Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, in a case brought by The Gambia after the 2017 crackdown. The key legal test is proving intent to destroy a group, which is notoriously hard, and the court’s reading of it could also weigh on South Africa’s case against Israel.
