Trump warns of new strikes against Iran after new year, calls negotiators ‘professional tap dancers’

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Fox News Chief Congressional correspondent Trey Yingst joins 'Fox News Live' to weigh in on President Trump's remarks after new Iran aerial attacks targeted U.S. military facilities.

President Donald Trump is warning that he is inclined to order military strikes against Iran after the new year if the two countries fail to reach an agreement, dismissing ongoing diplomatic talks with Tehran as meaningless.

In exclusive comments Sunday to Fox News, the president accused Iranian negotiators of stalling while continuing to escalate hostilities following the U.S. elections.

TRUMP KEEPS SIGNALING AN END TO IRAN WAR, BUT EXPERT WARNS A CRUCIAL PIECE OF HIS STRATEGY IS MISSING

If negotiations with Iran collapse, the U.S. is likely to move quickly to degrade Tehran’s military capabilities — a campaign analysts say would begin with missile systems, naval assets and command networks before escalating to more controversial targets. (Yuri Gripas/Abaca/Bloomberg via Getty Images:  Sasan / Middle East Images / AFP via Getty Images)

"They’re more aggressive now than before the election," Trump said, characterizing Iranian officials as "professional tap dancers."

"They talk all the time, all they do is talk, but it’s all b------," he added.

The remarks come as the U.S. maintains a naval blockade of Iranian ports amid broader conflict in the Strait of Hormuz, and as Trump predicted that tit-for-tat strikes will continue between American and Iranian forces.

PROTESTERS CALL FOR REGIME CHANGE IN IRAN FOLLOWING DEADLY CRASH THAT KILLED 13 OIL AND GAS WORKERS

Trump also dared Tehran to challenge the U.S. naval siege.

"I hope they do, they’ll all be at the bottom of the sea like the rest of the navy," Trump said regarding the possibility of Iranian forces testing the blockade.

Meanwhile, anti-government demonstrations have flared up again in several Iranian cities. Asked about the renewed unrest inside the country, Trump offered a direct message to demonstrators on the ground: "Keep protesting."
