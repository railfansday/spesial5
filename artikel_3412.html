One of the most talked-about reality TV spinoffs is finally making its way to your screens. The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County premieres Thursday, Nov. 12 (Wednesday evening for West Coast viewers), introducing a new cast of nine women, including Jen Affleck (from the original SLOMW series), Bobbi Althoff, Aspyn Ovard, Avery Woods, McCall DaPron, Ashleigh Pease, Salomé Andrea, Madison Bontempo, and Jordan McClanahan.

DirecTV includes access to Hulu through its MyEntertainment Genre Pack and a few of its bigger streaming packages. These subscriptions start with a five-day free trial, giving you a chance to stream the new series without paying upfront.

Disclaimer: The brand notes that the free trial is available to new customers who sign up for residential DIRECTV streaming without leasing a device, such as a Gemini.

Social media is already buzzing about the cast, particularly Jen Affleck (the new Queen Bee), who is currently navigating a very public divorce from her husband, Zac Affleck.

The two have shared videos about their relationship on social media that have amassed millions of views, although some of that footage appears to have since been removed. The series will also feature Aspyn Ovard, another social media star whose current polyamorous relationship has left fans curious about the details of her love life.

All eight episodes premiere Thursday, Nov. 12 on Hulu.
