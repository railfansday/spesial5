Henry Cavill’s ‘Voltron’ Officially Abandons Theatrical Release Plan

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Voltron may be one of the biggest robots around, but he won't be defending the big screen. The new live-action adaptation of the classic animated series is set to go straight to streaming, without getting a theatrical release. The film will star Henry Cavill, Rita Ora, and Sterling K. Brown.

Voltron began life as the Japanese anime series Beast King Go-Lion; it was brought over to North Americs, re-edited and re-dubbed into Voltron: Defender of the Universe. It centered around a team of five humans, each the pilot of a massive mechanical lion. In times of trouble, the five lions could unite to form the enormously powerful super robot Voltron. With Voltron, they valiantly battled against the evil alien King Zarkon, the wicked witch Haggar, and their marauding Robeasts. The series was a success on both TV and in toy stores, and proved sufficiently popular that another anime series with a combining mecha, Armored Fleet Dairugger XV, was appended to it; fans often refer to it as "Vehicle Voltron." The series has subsequently been revived several times in animation, most recently as Voltron: Legendary Defender, a DreamWorks Animation series that became a cult hit on Netflix.

And now, while Voltron is coming to live-action for the first time ever, it will only be found on Prime Video. As reported by IGN, the film will not get a theatrical roll-out akin to the one fellow 1980s animation adaptation Masters of the Universe got earlier this year. However, a theatrical release was apparently never on the table, as it was intended as a streaming-only release from the very beginning. That may be a wise decision: while Masters of the Universe disappointed at the box office, it's had the power of Grayskull on streaming, regularly topping Prime Video's charts.

Voltron is directed by Rawson Marshall Thurber (Dodgeball: A True Underdog Story); he also co-wrote the script with Ellen Shanman. Cavill will play King Alfor, ruler of the peaceful planet Altea. The villainous Zarkon and Haggar will respectively be played by Brown (Paradise) and Ora (Fifty Shades of Grey). The film also stars Daniel Quinn-Toye (Sterling Point), John Harlan Kim (The Librarians), Alba Baptista (Warrior Nun), and Samson Kayo (Our Flag Means Death). The film's plot and visuals are being kept under wraps for now.

Voltron will be released on Prime Video in 2027. Stay tuned to Collider for future updates.
