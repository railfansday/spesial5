This Six-Pack of Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Alarms Is $60 Off for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A smoke alarm going off in the basement isn’t all that useful if you can’t hear it when you’re sleeping upstairs. That’s one big reason why wireless interconnected smoke alarms are worth looking into. This becomes all the more important if you live in a larger or multi-story home.

If you’re looking to add an additional layer of security and some peace of mind to your home safety setup, this Black Friday deal on a six-pack of X-Sense SC07-W smoke and carbon monoxide alarms makes upgrading a lot easier on the wallet. Right now, the bundle is down from $270 to just $210 on Amazon, saving you $60 through Nov. 30.

The SC07-W does more than just detect smoke. This combination alarm system includes carbon monoxide monitoring, giving you protection against two types of household hazards in a single device. The biggest selling point here, however, is the ability to wirelessly connect up to 24 alarms.

If one alarm detects smoke or CO in—for example—the basement, every connected alarm sounds off together, making it much less likely for you to miss an alert. You also don’t need to mess around with additional wiring, making this an easy retrofit for an older home.

The product is designed for longevity and X-Sense has clearly paid a lot of attention to detail. Each alarm ships with a 10-year sealed battery, so you won’t have to keep track of battery replacements across multiple rooms. The built-in LCD displays carbon monoxide levels and alarm information, giving you all necessary information about what’s happening in your ambient environment instead of leaving you guessing.

Additionally, the alarm runs an automatic self-check every minute, which is a reassuring feature in a device as critical as this. Moreover, the X-Sense SC07-W is ETL certified and meets the latest US safety standards for smoke alarms.

If your current setup consists of standalone alarms scattered around your home, or no alarms at all, this Black Friday deal is a solid opportunity to cover multiple rooms and get interconnected protection at an affordable price.
