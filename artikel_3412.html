Sam Rockwell’s New Spy Movie Nearly Doubles Its Theater Count

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

As next year's 99th Academy Awards draw closer, the race for the Best Actor trophy is heating up. Who will follow in the footsteps of Michael B. Jordan and his literally two-faced, Oscar-winning performance in Sinners by taking home the Best Actor prize? Some of the contenders include Robert Pattinson for his affecting role in Primetime, Sebastian Stan for his detailed performance in Fjord, Matt Damon for his starring turn in Christopher Nolan's adaptation of Homer's The Odyssey, and Andrew Scott for the tearjerking drama Elsinore.

Scott currently sits a close second in the running, with the brilliant John Malkovich the slight favorite at the time of writing. Malkovich stars alongside Sam Rockwell as CIA agents Lee Chesterfield and Chris Carlisle in Wild Horse Nine, a dark comedy from Martin McDonagh that follows two spies on a trip to Easter Island. McDonagh graced the Oscars three years ago with the Best Picture-nominated The Banshees of Inisherin, which somehow walked away without a Golden Statue, despite receiving nine nominations.

Wild Horse Nine will be hoping to go one step further and win an Oscar, and is currently increasing its exposure in theaters. Debuting in 1,100 locations in North America, the film expanded to 2,175 locations last weekend and held strong despite tough debuting competition. Wild Horse Nine earned $4.6 million last weekend, marking just a 22% drop from its opening frame. In total, the film has made $28 million worldwide to date, split between a domestic haul of $13.3 million and a further $14.7 million from overseas markets.

Following its debut at the 83rd Venice International Film Festival earlier this year, Wild Horse Nine quickly became a favorite of critics. Aidan Kelley awarded the film 9/10 in his review, calling it an "instant Oscars favorite" and writing, "It's another stellar entry in McDonagh's repertoire, but above all else, it is an extremely enjoyable ride." On review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes, the film is "certified fresh" at 94%, with audiences awarding a 92% rating. Steve Buscemi, Tom Waits, and Parker Posey also feature in this co-production between the UK, the U.S., and Chile.
