All Upcoming Owen Wilson Movies & TV Shows, Including Focker-In-Law

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Owen Wilson is one of the most popular actors and comedians of the last 25 years. He’s appeared in countless classics, from the Meet the Parents franchise to Disney’s iconic Cars films. Wilson always tends to incorporate his classic comedic charm into every role, all while making each character truly stand out.

Wilson is showing no signs of slowing down, either. The actor is currently working on a number of projects, some of which are follow-ups to his classic films (such as the upcoming Focker-in-Law, which releases in theaters this holiday season).

Interestingly, Owen Wilson has branched out in recent years, appearing in more serious roles as well as comedies. That’s different from Wilson’s early career, which saw him appear mostly as the “funny man,” (with a few exceptions) in projects like Wedding Crashers and Starksy and Hutch.

Moving forward, Wilson continues that streak, with some projects you’d expect from the actor, while also having some bolder moves lined up, too. The last time the actor appeared on the big screen was in Avengers: Endgame Encore (out now on Disney+), in which he showed up in one of the four additional post credits scenes added to the film.

As of now, it’s not clear if Wilson will appear in the cast of Avengers: Doomsday, or if the scene from Encore was filmed specifically for the film’s rerelease. Regardless, the actor has a number of exciting movies and shows coming up for his fans to look forward to.

Owen Wilson’s longest running role in his career is, perhaps surprisingly, not Lightning McQueen from the Cars franchise. Instead, that title belongs to Kevin Rawley, the character that Wilson plays in Ben Stiller and Robert De Niro’s family comedy franchise that began with Meet the Parents.

Wilson is set to appear in Focker-in-Law, which comes out in theaters this holiday season. Wilson once again plays Rawley, an unusual figure who is a favorite of Jack Byrnes (De Niro) and was once engaged to Greg Focker’s wife, Pam. All of these are back in the new movie, and they’re joined by two new characters — Skyler Gisondo’s Henry Focker and Ariana Grande’s Olivia Jones.

As stated, there is no official confirmation on whether or not Owen Wilson will appear in Avengers: Doomsday, the highly anticipated Marvel Studios film which premieres next month. However, his presence in one of the the post-credits scenes of Avengers: Endgame Encore provides strong evidence that Wilson might be making another MCU appearance.

The post-credits scene was directed by Joe and Anthony Russo, who are helming Doomsday, which serves as further evidence of Wilson’s potential appearance next month. Not only that, but another TVA member is in Doomsday, too — Tom Hiddelston’s Loki. And, like Owen Wilson, Hiddelston appears in one of the added post-credits scenes in Endgame: Encore, which shows him arriving at Steve Rogers' house in the 1950s.

Wilson will reprise his role as Lightning McQueen next year in Cars: Lightning Racers, which is currently in post-production. Lightning Racers is a different kind of Cars project, however, and will release on Disney+ next year. The new series will be primarily geared towards young school-aged children, and has a unique animation style to boot, too.

The visual style of Lightning Racers is similar to that of the Cars films, so classic characters like Lightning McQueen and Mater will still be recognizable. Both are returning for the new series, as are other characters like Pipes, Miles, and Ms. Blinker.

Cars: Lightning Racers will take an episodic approach, with each episode focusing on a new adventure in the lives of Lightning McQueen and co. Along with McQueen and the returning characters, there are reportedly a number of new heroes created for the new show that will make their Cars franchise debut next year.

This show marks the sixteenth different project that Owen Wilson voices Lightning McQueen in. The actor helped to launch the franchise in 2006 with the first Cars film, and has since gone on to play the iconic character in a number of follow-up movies and shows. While there hasn’t been a Cars film since 2017’s Cars 3, in which Lightning McQueen serves as the mentor to Cruz Ramirez, Wilson has also appeared in several specials and shows since then.

For example, Wilson appeared in the 2022 Cars miniseries Cars on the Road, which premiered on Disney+. He also reprised his role briefly in 2024s LEGO Pixar: BrickToons, in which he appeared in the episode Trust Yer Ol’ Pal, Mater. Wilson has lent his voice to a number of Cars video games, too, along with recording original lines for Cars Land at Disney California Adventure theme park.

2026 marks the twentieth anniversary of the Cars films, so it’s only fitting that Wilson will soon be joining the fold once again for Cars: Lightning Racers.

Owen Wilson is also set to appear in an untitled film from writer/director Nancy Myers, which is slated for release on Christmas Day in 2027. Wilson is one of many A-list actors to be cast in the film, which is currently in post-production, having completed principal photography earlier this year.

Along with Wilson, this untitled film also features Penélope Cruz, Kieran Culkin (a recent Academy Award winner), and Jude Law, although few details on his character have been revealed at this stage. Audiences can expect more information to be released about this film, including its title, at some point in 2027, thanks to its current expected release date.

Prior reports detailed a plot wherein a young filmmaker becomes romantically involved with a producer, before splitting up only to be reunited for another project. However, more recent updates suggest this may not have been the actual story, and that the plot and script similarly may have changed in the years since these reports emerged (as per The Hollywood Reporter). This means that the film's actual plot is for now unknown, and we'll only know more about what to expect from Owen Wilson and the rest of the cast in the movie closer to the film's release date.
