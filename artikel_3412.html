If you want to see Brunson in action, the episode airs at 9 p.m. ET, and you’ll need one of two things: Access to NBC or a subscription to the network’s streaming platform, Peacock. But if you find yourself without either, we’ll show you where you can stream the show for free.

The two first crossed paths in 2024, with Brunson having stated in past interviews that he is a forever fan of the show. Since then, the pair have done everything together, from standing in Brunson’s Knicks parade float to going out to dinner.
