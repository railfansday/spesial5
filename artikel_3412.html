Abdul El-Sayed Handily Beats Mike Rogers in Michigan Senate Race

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Abdul El-Sayed has made history, becoming the country's first-ever Muslim senator ... after he handily defeated Republican Mike Rogers in Michigan.

NBC News reported overnight that El-Sayed was the projected winner in the hard-fought race for the Michigan Senate seat, clocking in with 52.3 percent of the vote, compared to Rogers' 45.7 percent with 94.4 percent of votes counted.

In total, El-Sayed has so far raked in 2,431,112 votes to Rogers' 2,123,522 votes, with the third candidate, Lydia Christensen, only getting 1 percent of the vote.

We've reported on all the drama surrounding the tightly contested race ... with Rep. Haley Stevens pulling her endorsement of El-Sayed over the weekend ... even though she's a Democrat and Rogers is a Republican.

Stevens officially endorsed Rogers on Sunday ... hours after publicly stating she was sticking by El-Sayed. She tried to explain her decision during an interview with Anderson Cooper on CNN -- a move which backfired.

Democratic strategists like James Carville and Lis Smith both ripped into Rep. Stevens for backing Rogers ... while Governor Josh Shapiro of Pennsylvania didn't slam her -- but didn't back her up either.

Stevens penned a heartfelt letter on Election Day in which she further delved into the reasons for her defection ... and criticized the attacks on her by Dems.
