Pundi X is a protocol that intends to make cryptocurrencies available to everyone. It is conceiving a new era of decentralized technologies to enhance users’ everyday lives. The project makes use of point-of-sale (POS) devices.

Pundi X intends to distribute point-of-sale smart devices to retailers to accept payments in the form of PUNDIX. Post the redenomination from NPXS to PUNDIX, it is yet to ignite a run to the higher cliffs.

Are you keen on investing at the dips for the peaks, but doubtful of its price prospects? Look no further, as this write-up decodes the plausible price prediction for 2026 and the years to come.

Pundi X (New) is down 0.60% to $0.0986 over the past 24 hours, despite a broader market rise. The decline comes amid low trading activity as the token recorded around $967,000 in 24-hour trading volume against a market cap of $25.5 million. This low activity makes PUNDIX more sensitive to small trades, allowing modest selling pressure to affect its price.

PUNDIX is now testing support near $0.095. If it holds, the token could trade between $0.095 and $0.100. A break below this level could send it toward its 30-day low of $0.080. On the upside, a move above $0.105 would need stronger trading volume.

Pundi X is focusing on making Web3 payments easier by combining AI, cross-chain trading, and real-world payment solutions. After closing Pundi X Chain, the project moved its operations fully to the Ethereum-based PUNDIX token.

Its partnership with ChimpX brings AI-powered Solana tools that allow users to complete swaps and payments using simple text commands. This could make crypto payments easier for everyday users.

Pundi X is also looking to expand its XPOS payment terminals into new markets, including Vietnam, which could increase real-world PUNDIX usage.

Meanwhile, its partnerships with Prom and other platforms could support token demand through transaction-fee burns and merchant-driven buybacks. These developments could reduce circulating supply while increasing network activity, potentially supporting PUNDIX’s price in 2026.

A sustained move above $0.12–$0.15 would strengthen the recovery structure and could allow PUNDIX to move toward the first major resistance at $0.2508. If buyers successfully break and hold above $0.2508, the next major resistance sits at $0.3766.

The chart also suggests a possible step-by-step recovery toward these levels, with a pullback potentially occurring after the first resistance before another upward move.

However, this remains a projected bullish path rather than a confirmed breakout. A failure to hold the current base near $0.07 would weaken the setup and could send PUNDIX back toward its recent lows.
