Ethereum price has staged a strong rebound, climbing more than 7%, reclaiming the $2,400 mark as buyers return to the market. The rally comes amid a broader recovery across cryptocurrencies following the initial volatility triggered by the US midterm election results. The key question now is whether Ethereum (ETH) can turn this sharp rebound into a sustained recovery.

Ethereum’s latest rally comes as the broader cryptocurrency market recovers from its initial reaction to the US midterm election results. Democrats’ victories in both chambers of Congress initially raised concerns about delays to crypto-friendly legislation, putting pressure on digital assets.

However, the selling pressure eased as markets reassessed the potential policy impact. Bitcoin also recovered from its earlier decline, helping improve sentiment across the crypto market. Ethereum followed the broader rebound, with its gains accelerating as bearish positions came under pressure.

Another factor is the changing derivatives positioning. As ETH moved higher, traders betting on further declines faced forced liquidations, adding buying pressure to an already recovering market.

Liquidation data shows that approximately $236.47 million in Ethereum positions were liquidated over a two-hour period. Short liquidations accounted for $132.15 million, representing 55.9% of the total. Long liquidations made up the remaining $104.33 million, or 44.1%.

The larger share of short liquidations suggests that bearish traders faced greater pressure during the rally. When short positions are forcibly closed, exchanges effectively buy back the underlying asset, potentially adding momentum to an ongoing price increase.

However, the sizeable liquidation of long positions also signals elevated volatility. The move is not a one-sided squeeze, and traders on both sides of the market have faced significant losses.
