Traders on prediction market Kalshi have sharply raised their bets on a SpaceX and Tesla tie-up. The odds of the two Elon Musk companies merging before March 2027 have more than doubled since Thursday, climbing from 27% to 62%. The contract will be settled using news reports and filings on the SEC’s EDGAR system. Confidence was boosted by Walter Isaacson, who wrote a biography of Musk, telling CNBC on Friday that a merger makes sense and is likely. He pointed to Terafab, the chipmaking project shared by Tesla, SpaceX and xAI, as a key reason to combine. Isaacson added that Tesla’s annual shareholder meeting on December 3 could offer more clues. Political resistance is building, though. Texas Attorney General-elect Nathan Johnson, a Democrat, told Bloomberg any deal would face heavy scrutiny. Jefferies’ Philippe Houchois acknowledged the higher political risk after the midterms but said the strategic case for a combined company still holds. Tesla shares fell 2.2% and SpaceX lost 1.5% on Monday.
