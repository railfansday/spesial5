Cadillac confirm Bottas to stay with team for 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Cadillac have confirmed Valtteri Bottas will stay on at the team for the 2027 Formula 1 season, ending speculation about the Finnish driver's future.

Bottas returned after a year's absence from the grid for this season, Cadillac's first in the sport, as one of two hugely experienced drivers for the all-new American team's first line-up alongside Sergio Perez, who signed a contract extension last month until at least 2029.

But, Bottas' future has been up in the air recently as Cadillac pushed for American Formula 2 driver Colton Herta to earn a Formula 1 super licence, which he secured two weeks ago, by running him in practice sessions.

The team have committed to Bottas for another year though, citing the 37-year-old's "extensive Formula 1 experience, technical insight and race-winning pedigree to the team".

"To whom it may concern, I'm staying!" said Bottas, in reference to his team radio after winning the 2019 Australian Grand Prix with Mercedes.

"I'm excited to continue this journey with Cadillac into 2027. We knew building a new Formula 1 team from the ground up wasn't going to be easy, but that's exactly what makes this challenge so special.

"We've laid the foundations, we're learning and improving, and there's still plenty more to come, I'm hungry for more, and I believe in what we're building together. The story is only just getting started."

Newcomers Cadillac are yet to score a point in their maiden campaign but Bottas is ahead of Perez in the Drivers' Championship due to countback, as his 13th place result at the Chinese Grand Prix is the team's highest result.

But, Perez has generally outpaced his team-mate which, with the links of Cadillac potentially keen for an American driver in Herta, led to speculation about Bottas' future.

Cadillac F1 CEO Dan Towriss said: "Valtteri's experience, perspective and commitment are important strengths for our team.

"Continuity gives us a strong platform as we continue developing the team and working toward our ambitions in Formula 1. On the eve of the Las Vegas GP, everyone in the team is pleased to have Valtteri locked in for 2027 alongside Checo and look forward to the next stage of our journey together."

Cadillac say they will announce their 2027 F1 reserve and test drivers in "due course".

With Bottas' future secured and Racing Bulls confirming last week that Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad will be their line-up once again in 2027, only Haas are yet to complete their driver pairing for next year.

Last month, Haas announced Rafael Camara will drive for the team in 2027, having already made an early F1 debut in Brazil and will compete again this weekend in Las Vegas, after Esteban Ocon was dropped following a dispute at the Mexico City Grand Prix.

But, Oliver Bearman has not been confirmed, although is expected to keep his seat once a "few bigger-picture things that we're working on" have been resolved according to the British driver.

Haas team principal Ayao Komatsu furiously dismissed reports claiming that contract extension talks with Bearman are being held up by the British driver demanding a £5m annual salary.

"I have no idea where that comes from," he said in Mexico.

"I've experienced this sometimes this season as well. People say there's no smoke without fire but really some people will invent a story."
