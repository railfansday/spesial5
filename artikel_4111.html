All Heists Ranked in GTA Online

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Shane is a Contributor for DualShockers writing guides and other materials. He has a degree in Game Design and Criticism.

With the newest installment of GTA on the horizon, some players are wondering what they might be able to expect from a new GTA Online mode if it comes about. The biggest and best draw for GTA Online was the major heists that made players really feel like a career criminal.

While some players may enjoy simply running a criminal enterprise in San Andreas, many are hoping to see some new and engaging heists appear in GTA VI. It may not be far off but it can always be important to remember where you started when you get where you’re going.

For that reason this list will encompass all eleven heists, counting the Doomsday Heists as three individual heists, and rank them from worst to best. A lot of what makes heists so much fun is how different they are and how they can appeal to some very different styles of play.

Of all the heists in GTA Online The Prison Break Heist is arguably the odd man out compared to a lot of the others. As one of the original heists added during the heists update back in 2015, it’s definitely been played enough for players to come to a decision about it.

Unless you were coming prepared with a team who knew what they were doing this heist could get very annoying very quickly. While prison breaks may be a popular trope in movies it seems that it’s not quite as popular to participate in.

Added to the roster of heists in 2017 this is the first part of the three part heist in which players are introduced to the Doomsday storyline and meet the eccentric cast of characters. As contractors the players will attempt to stop an attack on government servers.

The heist is pretty good, but being the first of the overall storyline means things really only get better from there. This puts a bit of a damper on the heist itself as it's really a precursor to some much better and more interesting heisting.

When your very favorite insane person shows up and asks you for help establishing a drug running operation you’re obligated to help. That’s pretty much the whole premise for this heist and even with Trevor’s wild personality it doesn’t exactly hit the highs of the other heists.

The biggest issue is that the finale simply isn’t as interesting as many others, since it’s entirely about defending a position from waves of attackers. Many players enjoy what Trevor brings to the table but the heist doesn’t close itself out well enough to satisfy.

The very first original heist opened up the floodgates for players to enjoy an all new way to play GTA V. With just one other player, you were tasked with helping Lester on a small job to rob Fleeca Bank before making your getaway in the Kuruma.

Since it is the introductory heist, it stays relatively grounded and simple. That isn’t a bad thing, but when compared to the other heists it just doesn’t stand up despite the feeling of nostalgia the heist gives players.

As the second part of the Doomsday Heist things start to get a little more interesting with this heist. Players will find themselves investigating a Russian submarine to uncover a crazy conspiracy and engage in some underwater infiltration.

The memorable setting and story do a lot of the heavy lifting for this heist before the inevitable finale which earns it a stronger reputation. Unfortunately, the prep for this one isn’t as engaging as some putting it a bit lower on the list.

The most recently added heist having been added in 2026, The Kortz Center Heist is a classic old school Art Job. Scout the museum, figure out the best way past security and make off with some very pricey paintings.

One of the few heists that you can complete solo gives it a major boost, but also becoming an art thief is incredibly cool. The big issue here is that you won’t get even close to being able to do this heist until you’ve managed to wrack up enough cash for a mansion with an Art Studio.

Another of the original heists, The Humane Labs Raid is an intricate and entertaining heist that sees players engage in a bit of serious Seal Team Six work. Players will infiltrate a secret research facility to steal data after activating an EMP to go in under the cover of dark.

One of the standouts of the original heists, it really nails the feeling of infiltrating a secret facility. The finale is pretty incredible but many would argue that the setup missions are tedious though that doesn’t stop it from being a favorite amongst the originals.

The final culmination of everything the Doomsday Heist has been building and the payoff is well worth it in every sense. Players will be attempting to stop the true Doomsday Scenario, but with a bit of a twist that we’ll leave for now just in case you haven’t played it yourself.

Arguably one of the most challenging finales of all of the heists, this one really puts you in the shoes of an action hero as you fight your way through the heavily armored enemies. That’s also what makes it a little divisive given how difficult the finale can be.

This heist, introduced in 2020, was the first heist to allow players to complete it entirely solely earning it a lot of leeway in terms of appreciation. Breaking into a fortified private island of a drug lord in a lot of different ways certainly helped too.

Many people adored it for being completeable solo, and earned it a good reputation since you no longer had to rely on others for help. Some did have things to say about the prep missions and overall stealth mechanics of the heist.

The Pacific Standard Job is the culmination of the first line-up of heists added to GTA Online. Players prepare themselves to rob the Pacific Standard Bank in a true and classic heist that really encapsulates what GTA Online heisting is all about.

There’s no denying that of all the heists in the game, this one evokes that sense of being a career criminal better than any other. With the actual job being an incredible experience and the getaway being a particular high point.

At the very top of this list sits the most interesting heist in the whole game added way back in 2019, The Diamond Casino Heist. Players break into the Diamond Casino in a few different ways and make off with tons of cash from the casino in a truly epic fashion.

With three different kinds of entries, all of which are incredible, there’s a good amount of flexibility in how to approach it. Each entry has its own perks but all are memorable and really make you feel like you stepped into a casino heist movie.
