Wolfenstein 3 Is Now "Officially In Production" At MachineGames

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Earlier this year in January, we heard how a third major Wolfenstein game was reportedly in the works, and it's now been confirmed.

Xbox's CEO Asha Sharma has announced Wolfenstein 3 is "officially in production" at the Swedish developer MachineGames. Here's what she had to say, alongside of the game's script, which name-drops some characters:

Asha Sharma: "Wolfenstein 3 is officially in production at MachineGames. One of gaming's most important franchises, and a team that has done remarkable work bringing it forward. Looking forward to sharing more."

Actors Brian Bloom (B.J. Blazkowicz) and Debra Wilson (Grace Walker) will also apparently be returning to their roles. Details such as platforms and a release window have not been revealed at this early stage, but when we hear more, expect an update.

As previously noted, MachineGames has released Wolfenstein II: The New Colossus and Wolfenstein: Youngblood on the Switch. Earlier this year in May, it also released Indiana Jones and the Great Circle on the Switch 2.
