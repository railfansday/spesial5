GTA 6: Where to Find Strip Clubs

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Adam Beck is a Canadian who has covered the gaming industry for over twenty years. He has a passion for RPGs, action, stealth and horror games.

Who doesn’t like to party a little in games? Well, Grand Theft Auto 6 certainly has various means to do so. Whether it’s a club, a fancy VIP experience, or a traditional strip club, Vice City has everything you’re looking for.

This has been a staple of the series ever since Grand Theft Auto 3, as going into these clubs not only facilitated story missions but also allowed the characters (and the player) to get an NSFW experience. There are a handful of different options in Rockstar’s Grand Theft Auto 6, but finding them can be a bit tricky. Most of them are centralized, and some you won’t see until much later in the campaign.

Players already have a list of 61 cheats to look forward to.

The first strip club you’ll find is in Chapter 2 upon meeting Vicki who will introduce you to her boss at the Delights Cabaret. This is a traditional strip club, but one that’s more in line with being out in the middle of nowhere. It’s more of a bar atmosphere with girls in the back.

With that said, you’ll be able to find Delights Cabaret in the Port Gellhorn section of the map, all the way to the west.

The first strip club you’ll come across in Vice City is part of the story between Jason and his old buddy Dre’Quan. He will invite you out to his club, where he will introduce you to Boobie, who will be putting Jason and Lucia up in his apartment.

This is a traditional strip club, with dancers on poles. It’s also one of the more expensive clubs, with VIP dance treatments costing $1,000. Jack of Hearts is located in central Crosstown, just south of your apartment. This is the go-to spot for adult entertainment.

This has more of a club vibe with a bit of stripping. It’s far fancier than Jack of Hearts, but has just as many options, if not more, with dancing on the dancefloor. The best part is that the VIP dances will only set you back $300 in-game.

NINE1NINE is south of Jack of Hearts, at the top of the Downtown section of the map. Even when you roll up to the place for the first time, you’ll notice just how much nicer it is, with a more exclusive feeling.

This is less of a strip club and more of a dance club. At least one main mission takes place here, and it’s probably the best place to go dancing with Lucia. It features multiple rooms with different types of music and DJs, and its aesthetic is built around an old vault, with a giant vault door at the entrance.

It’s definitely somewhere to check out in Vice City, and thankfully, it’s right across the street from NINE1NINE, just to the west.

Last but not least is VYB. It’s located in the northeast portion of Ocean Beach, directly south of your Act 4 home in Washington Beach. Similar to The Vault, it has a more traditional club atmosphere, with dance floors and high-class bars in the back.

Unfortunately, VYB isn’t accessible to the public, as it’s part of Dre’Quan’s lengthy story and is only used during a main mission in Act 4. With that said, it’s probably the nicest club in the game.

Grand Theft Auto 6 delivers an enormous open world, a gripping story, a vibrant Vice City and an astonishing range of activities.
