This article contains spoilers for VisionQuest.

VisionQuest has brought Vision's quest to rediscover himself to its climactic end.

Starring Paul Bettany and James Spader, VisionQuest follows the storyline started in WandaVision and carried into Agatha All Along. The series was created by Terry Matalas, and it also stars Ruaridh Mollica, Todd Stashwick, Emily Hampshire, Orla Brady, James D'Arcy, Henry Lewis, Jonathan Sayer, and T'Nia Miller.

Instead of following the original Vision first introduced in Avengers: Age of Ultron, VisionQuest tells the story of how S.W.O.R.D.'s white Vision recreation rediscovers himself through bonding with his son, Tommy. By the series' end, he has come to accept that he is the true Vision and assumes a new, red form more reminiscent of the original Vision. Recently, Bettany discussed this new shift in appearance with The Playlist.

"My understanding and the idea that I was playing with is that now all of those versions of Vision have coalesced and found space," he explained. "I mean, White Vision, of course, was the original Red Vision–it’s his body. And then there is Hex Vision and all of those memories. He has to accept the truth of what happened in the Hex, which I love."

To Bettany, the visual change is meant to symbolize Vision's growth as well as the melding of the prior forms of the character audiences have seen thus far. He further indicated that this is in line with the show's exploration of self-worth and fatherhood. He stated:

One of the scenes with Ruaridh in the swimming pool that was very important to me was because I think it’s very redolent of father-and-son relationships, where the father admits, "I haven’t been listening to you. You’ve told me who you are, and you’ve told me it’s real, and I haven’t been listening well enough to you. You are who you think you are." And for me, it was really important for the show to also have a sort of really positive, warm male energy in a world where it’s needed."

VisionQuest ended on an optimistic note; our hero made peace with his father figure Ultron and reconnected with his sons, Billy and Tommy Maximoff. Similarly, he accepted himself and assumed a new true form. While these developments end Vision's post-Infinity War arc, they also set up a future for him and his children in the MCU.

Despite this, Vision is not confirmed to return in any upcoming projects. Though his love interest, Wanda Maximoff/Scarlet Witch, appearing in Avengers: Doomsday could mean a surprise role for him and/or the boys. "Whether Vision continues at all is sadly a decision that is made further up the food chain than I sit," Bettany said when asked about the fate of Vision in the MCU.
