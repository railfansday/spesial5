‘Supernatural’ Star Officially Lands in Netflix’s Next Mega Hit

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Hannah covers horror, sci-fi, and games at Collider, where she writes news and features, reviews new releases, and interviews the people who make them. She’s helping build out Collider’s gaming coverage, with Pokémon as one of her specialties. If she isn’t writing about a monster, she’s probably watching one on a tape from her VHS collection.

One of the perks of being married to Jared Padalecki is that when your book club finds a romance you’d like to see him star in, you can take it up with him at home. In 2022, Genevieve Padalecki, who played Ruby opposite her future husband on Supernatural, picked up a book by fellow Texan Katherine Center. Four years later, the movie adaptation starring Padalecki is just hours from premiering on Netflix.

It’s easy to see what she had in mind for him. Between fifteen seasons of saving people as Sam Winchester and four more carrying a Texas Ranger’s badge on Walker, Padalecki looks like a pretty safe bet to get somebody home in one piece. This time, however, he plays the other half of that dynamic, and the person qualified to keep him alive is Leighton Meester.

Padalecki and Meester star in Guarding Stars, which premieres Wednesday, November 18, on Netflix. Based on Center’s 2022 novel The Bodyguard, the holiday rom-com follows action star Jack Stapleton (Padalecki), who has picked up a stalker just in time for Christmas at his family’s ranch. Bringing a bodyguard home would mean telling his family why he needs one, but bringing home a girlfriend buys him a perfectly good excuse to keep Hannah Brooks (Meester) close, provided nobody asks the happy couple too many questions.

Four love stories, four completely different ideas of what it means to be somebody's person. This is the one that matches how you actually fall, and what it would ask of you.

You do not fall for strangers. You fall for the person you have already been talking to for years, which is the slowest and by some distance the most durable way anybody has found to do this.

You want a partner, not an audience. Somebody who wants their own life as badly as you want yours, and who would never ask you to be smaller to make the relationship easier.

The intensity was never the problem. The secrecy is. You are drawn to a love that lives entirely in private, and to the particular ache of wanting somebody you are not supposed to want.

You do not do this cautiously. When it happens for you it is immediate and total and it rearranges everything, and you would not accept a smaller version of it.

Jack makes the mistake of questioning whether Hannah can protect him while they’re both still standing on the airport tarmac. By the time she’s finished answering, only one of them is still standing. Director Elizabeth Allen Rosenbaum told Swooon she hadn’t read Katherine Center’s book when the screenplay came her way, but she knew she wanted the job the moment Hannah tossed Jack over her shoulder. “I mean, how often does the romantic heroine get to throw her leading man over her shoulder? I was in.” Rosenbaum had directed Meester on Gossip Girl, where Blair Waldorf could do considerable damage to a man without having to lay a hand on him. For Guarding Stars, Meester learned how to flip a stunt performer, ride a horse, and jump clear of an explosion.

Padalecki, meanwhile, gets to put a considerably less lethal skill to use: he sings with country artist Walker Hayes on a Christmas parade float. Hayes originally auditioned to play Jack’s older brother, and when the filmmakers decided to make the brother younger, Rosenbaum rewrote the part of the town sheriff to keep him in the cast. Giving the sheriff a duet with Padalecki was also her idea.

Guarding Stars premieres November 18 on Netflix.
