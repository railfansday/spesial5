Taylor Sheridan’s New Yellowstone Sequel Series Returns To Disappointing Rotten Tomatoes Audience Score

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Taylor Sheridan's new Yellowstone sequel series just got a disheartening update.

One of the latest in Sheridan's Yellowstone spinoff series has returned with a disappointing Rotten Tomatoes audience score as new episodes continue to be released. Following the end of the original series, the franchise was split into two paths of continuation, both of which tell very different stories. Dutton Ranch, airing on Paramount+, sees Beth and Rip trying to build a new life on a ranch in Texas. The other show, Marshals, airs on CBS, and sees Kayce join a group of US Marshals to hunt down threats across Montana.

Now, Rotten Tomatoes reports that Marshals has scored a disappointing update due to its audience score for season 2. While season 2 began on October 4, 2026, it's not until now, November 23, 2026, that it's earned enough user reviews for it to get a score. Unfortunately, that score isn't very good, with season 2 earning a 52% based on less than 50 ratings. It's a small sample size, but one that indicates viewers are generally disinterested in the series.

However, Marshals season 2 reviews are much better than season 1, which saw audiences give it a 28% based on over 500 ratings. While the series' score is still relatively negative, it's also almost double the score from season 1, indicating more viewers enjoyed this second outing with Kayce than the first. However, despite having better reception on paper, many find the writing weak and the characters uninteresting, with some calling it Sheridan's worst show. Even with a higher score, it's not impressing most people.

Marshals season 2 offered a major change in contrast to the first season. The episode count has expanded to 20 episodes this season, the most of any show in the Yellowstone franchise, or under Sheridan's belt in general. Every week focuses on a new case for the Marshals to tackle, while also expanding upon ongoing storylines for a multitude of characters. While not exactly procedural, it's a very different approach from the original series, and one that makes it quite different, even from Dutton Ranch.

Despite the lack of enthusiasm in reviews, viewership for Marshals has been particularly strong. Season 1's premiere originally drew the largest viewership of any series not bolstered by an NFL game, while the series has averaged around 8 million live viewers per episode across season 2. As the show continues to air weekly on CBS, it seems likely it'll continue to retain this level of success. Even with poor reviews, there's still plenty of investment in the series from audience members.

Although Marshals didn't end up attaining a positive audience score with season 2, the show has continued to impress viewership-wise, which might be exactly what it needs to guarantee its future. The series may not be as well-regarded as Yellowstone was, but it seems to have retained people who enjoy it nonetheless. Unfortunately, the negative score does indicate that the show needs to improve on the writing front to ensure viewers are satisfied with the way Kayce's story is being handled.
