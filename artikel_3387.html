Taylor Sheridan’s ‘Tulsa King’ Officially Returns With Major Season 4 Episode

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Considering the entire premise of Tulsa King was that Sylvester Stallone's Dwight Manfredi was being sent somewhere that his New York associates considered to be of little importance, things have worked out rather well for the General in Oklahoma. Having spent 25 years in prison, Dwight emerged to find that even buying a cup of coffee was a rather different experience from the one he remembered, yet he's managed to put together a fairly lucrative business with a bunch of guys who probably wouldn't have given each other the time of day had they met under different circumstances. His attempts to become a slightly more legitimate businessman have been complicated by the fact that some of Tulsa's supposedly legitimate businessmen aren't much better than him, and we've still got another four episodes to see how that all plays out this year.

The seventh episode of Tulsa King Season 4, titled "Black Diamonds," will officially premiere on Paramount+ tomorrow, Friday, November 27, at 3 a.m. ET. There are ten episodes in the fourth season, with the finale, "Exit Interview," scheduled to arrive on December 18, so we'll have wrapped things up with Dwight for the year by Christmas.

There's no need to worry about whether we'll be seeing Stallone again next year, either, given that Paramount+ renewed the series for a fifth season before the fourth had even premiered. Interestingly, production on Season 5 is moving to New York, the city Dwight was effectively banished from when we first met him.

Created by Taylor Sheridan, with Boardwalk Empire creator Terence Winter serving as head writer, the series features Stallone (Rocky) as Dwight Manfredi, Martin Starr (Silicon Valley) as Bodhi, Jay Will (The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel) as Tyson Mitchell, Garrett Hedlund (TRON: Legacy) as Mitch Keller, Dana Delany (Body of Proof) as Margaret Devereaux, Frank Grillo (The Purge: Anarchy) as Bill Bevilaqua, Neal McDonough (Justified) as Cal Thresher, and Gretchen Mol (Boardwalk Empire) as Amanda Clark.
