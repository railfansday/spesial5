28 Years Later 3 Confirms Cillian Murphy's Status As Sequel Hits Major Milestone

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The hit horror franchise 28 Years Later just received a huge update about another sequel.

28 Years Later is a post-apocalyptic horror film franchise. Serving as a follow-up to the 28 Days Later movie series, the first film of the new trilogy featured a massive ensemble cast that consists of Jodie Comer, Aaron Taylor-Johnson, Jack O’Connell, Alfie Williams, and Ralph Fiennes. The franchise is set in a world that has been destroyed by the Rage Virus.

The third chapter of 28 Years Later has received some very encouraging news from its director, Danny Boyle. He revealed that Cillian Murphy is definitely willing to return and join the cast of 28 Years Later 3. The director added that the project hit another milestone as Alex Garland has already completed the screenplay. However, despite these developments, the film still faces one major obstacle.

In an interview with Deadline, Boyle recently discussed the future of the horror franchise. The director expressed his enthusiasm for the script and Murphy's willingness to return for the sequel. He also explained that the next hurdle that 28 Years Later 3 needs to get past is funding.

Boyle further elaborated on Murphy's involvement, stating that the Academy Award-winning actor is "up for" reprising his role as Jim. He originally played the character in 2002's 28 Days Later. Garland has reportedly written the screenplay specifically with Murphy in mind, implying that he could potentially play a big role in the sequel.

While Murphy's interest is an exciting development, it doesn't necessarily mean the actor will return for 28 Years Later 3. Although Boyle said Murphy would be happy to return, nothing has been finalized or confirmed by the actor, his representatives, or any production companies at the time of this article's publication.

The director also offered some insight about the upcoming chapter, which is supposed to be the final installment of the 28 Years Later trilogy. He explained that Garland's screenplay brings the overall narrative to a close. Boyle believes the final film will connect the events of the franchise's other movies and will offer viewers a conclusion to a decades-long story.

Despite the creative team's progress, the financial aspect of producing 28 Years Later 3 is still up in the air. It was reported in December 2025 that Sony Pictures was allegedly moving forward with the third film before The Bone Temple debuted in theaters. However, the sequel suffered at the box office, earning $58.5 million worldwide on a budget of $63 million. The major financial loss could cause a lot of problems for the final movie.
