Southwest Airlines Now Runs Late Nearly A Third Of The Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Southwest Airlines was late nearly a third of the time in October, and its cancellation rate was more than twice Delta’s. It finished last for punctuality among the ten North American airlines in aviation analytics company Cirium’s new monthly report, while Delta led on both. For the past two months, I’ve pointed out that Southwest’s poor on-time rankings were offset by canceling fewer flights than anyone else. They weren’t on-time but they reliably got passengers to their destinations. In October, though, Delta, United and even Frontier cancelled a smaller share of flights than Southwest.
