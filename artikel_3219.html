Apple Notes adds a powerful new advantage in iOS 27.3

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

There’s no shortage of note-taking apps on the App Store, but Apple Notes has long been my favorite, and in iOS 27.3 it’s gaining a new advantage: extensive app controls via Siri AI.

Voice-driven computing is on the rise, with AI assistants—including Siri AI—now more capable and intelligent than ever.

Siri AI is already a big upgrade over the old Siri, but it’s getting even better in iOS 27.3.

As we reported last week, iOS 27.3 gives Siri AI many new capabilities across built-in Apple apps on your iPhone, iPad, Mac, and more.

The one I’ve been testing the most is Apple Notes. Siri has offered basic Notes-related functionality for years, but it gains a whole new set of powers with the new update.
