Tanggal: 2026-11-05

CLO debt reset cut financing costs while extending the reinvestment period.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Palmer Square Capital BDC Inc. (PSBD +0.91%) reported third quarter results focused on portfolio optimization and a disciplined capital allocation strategy through share repurchases. Management reported that the company executed a reset of its collateralized loan obligation debt, which reduced the weighted average cost of financing while extending the reinvestment period through 2031. The company prioritized share buybacks during the period, utilizing its expanded repurchase authorization to address the stock's discount to net asset value. Management stated that the investment strategy currently favors secondary market opportunities in broadly syndicated loans and direct private credit investments due to prevailing spread dynamics. The portfolio maintained a high concentration of senior secured positioning across diverse industries, with interest coverage ratios remaining stable despite heightened interest rate volatility.
