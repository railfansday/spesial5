Next year’s iPhone Pro redesign sounds like exactly what I’ve been wanting

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Rumors about next year’s 20th anniversary iPhone Pro models have been ramping up, and they suggest Apple is designing an ‘iPhone 20 Pro’ that combines traits from two current models.

Apple keeps expanding the iPhone line to include new models. And while it’s great that there are more models for differing preferences and needs, it’s also left me missing the days of having one model that was the clear “best.”

That problem isn’t going away, since the iPhone Duo represents a brand new type of iPhone that some users will love, while others find it isn’t for them.

With my failed iPhone Duo experiment over, I’m back to using iPhone 18 Pro as my default model. But I still have an iPhone Air around for work, and every time I pick it up, I’m struck by how impressive it remains.

I used the iPhone Air all of last year and loved it. The main reason I upgraded to an iPhone 18 Pro is the camera system. I have young children at home—including newborn twins—and want to capture the best possible photos before they get older.

iPhone 18 Pro’s Main camera has been fantastic, and it’s great having the Telephoto again too.

But I still sorely miss the overall design of the iPhone Air. And it sounds like next year, I could get the best of both models.

Rumors indicate Apple’s 20th anniversary iPhone Pro models will have a unique new design featuring a quad-curved display that makes it look bezel-free.

The new models are expected to also be a bit slimmer, use curved edges, and possibly even bring back titanium. Leakers say the new design borrows a lot from the iPhone Air’s look.

This sounds like exactly what I’ve been hoping for.

While the ‘iPhone 20 Pro’ certainly won’t—and shouldn’t—be as thin as iPhone Air, if it can slim down a little and adopt several other Air design traits, I suspect it will evoke the same affection the Air has.

But unlike the Air, the new Pro model will still offer the best camera system in an iPhone (not to mention better battery life).

As a huge fan of the design of the Air, and the functionality of the 18 Pro, Apple’s 20th anniversary iPhone model could prove the perfect model for me. And for anyone else not opting for iPhone Duo 2.
