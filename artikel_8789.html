Tanggal: 2026-11-18

This November the Kingston Urban Room is inviting residents to explore local tall buildings and help shape the conversation about Kingston's future:

The exhibition's central question is: As Kingston grows, what should we value, what should we protect, and what should we change?

The exhibition's central question is: As Kingston grows, what should we value, what should we protect, and what should we change?

Tall Buildings, Shared Futures follows the previous What If? installation, held at Penny School Gallery in September 2025 as part of the London Design Festival and Kingston 2025 celebrations. That programme brought residents, designers, artists, architects and innovators together to explore Kingston's past, reflect on present challenges and imagine possibilities for its future.
