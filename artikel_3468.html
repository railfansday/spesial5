This Portable JBL Speaker Is a Lifehacker Gift of the Year, and It's 36% Off for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

While tech can get pretty expensive, especially in our current "RAMageddon" era, there are still plenty of affordable devices out there. The problem, of course, isn't quantity, but quality: Buying a tech item for under $50 is risky, since many of these products are made as cheaply as possible. If you're looking for a portable Bluetooth speaker, for instance, you don't want to buy something with poor sound quality, or that doesn't easily connect to your devices, or that breaks unexpectedly.

You don't need to worry about any of that with the JBL Go 4. This is a solid portable speaker that just so happens to have an MSRP of $49.95. It's so good, I named it Lifehacker's best gift under $50, as its price, excellent sound quality, and feature set make it a great option for most anyone on your list. It's an even better buy during Black Friday, too: Right now, you can pick up the JBL Go 4 for as low as $31.95, 36% off the original retail price.

JBL's Go 4 is a small speaker, but it gets surprisingly loud—especially for a speaker at this price point. Like other JBL speakers, it places a particular emphasis on bass. This is a tiny speaker, so don't expect a subwoofer on the go, but not only will it let you hear the bass in your music, it is noticeably punchy. JBL advertises seven hours of playtime on a single charge here, which is often plenty for taking the speaker out and about for the day. You can net an additional two hours of if you engage "Playtime Boost." JBL doesn't specify how this works, but it likely cuts some of the frequencies here to save on battery life. And, since it's 1P67-rated against dust and water, it's a great choice for outdoor use.

Perhaps my favorite thing about the JBL Go 4 is that it supports Auracast. That means you can easily connect to any other Auracast-compatible JBL speaker using the Auracast button on each device. I've used this before at parties and gatherings for an impromptu multi-speaker setup. Since JBL is so popular, it's not impractical to assume one or two other people at the function might have a similar speaker you can connect to, or if you know friends or family members have JBL speakers of their own, you can coordinate these setups ahead of time.

This discount is only available on specific colors of the Go 4, at least on Amazon: Black, White, Blue, Purple, Red, and "Squad." Black and Orange, Pink, and Sand are all still $49.95. Again, the JBL Go 4 is absolutely worth the $50 MSRP, but unless you're married to one of those three colors, it's even better at a discount.
