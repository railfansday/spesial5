Slow Horses officially moves beyond the books with 2 more seasons

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Seems like Jack Lowden isn’t going to be James Bond

In an interview with The Times ahead of the UK release of Anton Corbijn’s thriller A Talent for Murder, star Olivia Cooke spoke about her role on Apple TV’s hit spy show Slow Horses. Cooke said that shooting had begun for the series’ eighth season, which would be its last, and that she was so depressed by the news she was crying in her makeup chair. “We’re all melancholy, distracted,” she said. It turns out there’s no reason for tears.

Apple announced today that Slow Horses has been renewed for two more seasons. Season 6 ended in October, and production has already been completed for season 7, which is expected to be released in fall 2027. The series keeps to an exceptionally tight production schedule – the writers’ room for season 7 opened while season 6 was still being filmed. That’s allowed Apple to be extremely consistent with the release schedule, dropping at least one six-episode season annually since the show premiered in 2022.

Cooke’s report seemed believable because Slow Horses is running out of material. The series is a faithful adaptation of Mick Herron’s bestselling Slough House series, with each season typically corresponding to a single novel. Season 6 compressed Herron’s sixth and seventh books, Joe Country and Slough House. Season 7 will adapt Herron’s 2022 book, Bad Actors, and season 8 will adapt his 2025 book, Clown Town. Herron hasn’t said that he’s finished with the series, but given how far ahead Apple works, it is going to run out of material unless the author delivers a new book soon.

Without Herron’s tightly written John le Carré-style spy stories to provide the plot, further seasons of Slow Horses could decline in quality. The risk seems especially high given that series creator and showrunner Will Smith left Slow Horses after season 5. Slow Horses is one of the best shows on TV, so hopefully there’s a good plan in place for further adventures of the misanthropic spymaster Jackson Lamb (Gary Oldman) and his squad of failed MI5 agents. I would hate to see the series fall apart like Game of Thrones did when showrunners David Benioff and D. B. Weiss moved past what George R.R. Martin had written.

Cooke revealing that she’s on set for season 8 is a bit of a spoiler given how season 6 ended with her character Sid Baker fleeing MI5 custody. River Cartwright (Jack Lowden) went to find Sid, and was seemingly poisoned. Assuming the show continues to closely follow the plot of the books, neither River nor Sid will be part of the main cast of season 7, but they will return for season 8. That means Slow Horses’ renewal probably came with a bit of professional bad news for Lowden.

River thinks he’s James Bond even though he’s just as much a screw-up as the rest of his colleagues. Ironically, Lowden is considered a frontrunner for the upcoming Bond movie directed by Denis Villeneuve. Other lead contenders include Josh O’Connor, Callum Turner, Nicholas Hoult, and Robert Pattinson. While it’s possible that Slow Horses will actually kill River off and leave Lowden free to join the Bond franchise, it seems more likely that Villeneuve is going with another actor and River will just keep irritating Lamb for years to come.
