‘Godzilla Minus Zero’ Officially Delivers Record-Breaking Box Office Debut

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Two overseas titles secured spots on the top-five list of the domestic box office this weekend, with Godzilla Minus Zero claiming the number-one spot and Ramayana: Rise of a Legend finishing fifth. While the top-five list was bookended by the two international movies, three vastly different homegrown titles took the remaining three spots. Among them were two new releases, the animated offering The Cat in the Hat, and the period musical Drummer Boy.

Ramayana: Rise of a Legend is the most expensive Indian film of all time, with a reported production budget of approximately $200 million. The movie is the first part of a planned duology, with the second installment currently slated for a 2028 release. A grand international publicity tour was mounted to promote the mythological epic, necessitated by its massive production cost. As a rule of thumb, a film this size needs to gross twice its reported budget to break even. Ramayana got off to a lukewarm start, with $55 million globally and around $12 million domestically this past weekend.

Godzilla Minus Zero, on the other hand, had built-in buzz thanks to the positive word-of-mouth earned by its predecessor, Godzilla Minus One. Released in 2023, the previous installment grossed more than $110 million worldwide against a reported budget of $10 million. The movie broke box-office records for international titles in North America, having grossed more than $55 million in its theatrical run. Godzilla Minus One rode a wave of positive buzz to win the Best Visual Effects Oscar, beating out mega-budget behemoths.

The 2023 film was set in post-war Japan, serving as an allegory for the bombing of Hiroshima and Nagasaki. Many viewed it as a companion piece to Christopher Nolan's biographical thriller Oppenheimer, which was released the same year. Like Nolan's film, Godzilla Minus One was critically acclaimed. It now holds a near-perfect 99% score on Rotten Tomatoes.

Godzilla Minus Zero has also received positive reviews. The film is currently sitting at a "Certified Fresh" 90% critics score and a "Verified Hot" 96% audience score on the aggregator website, where the consensus reads, "Keeping its epic action rooted in human interest, Godzilla Minus Zero solidifies director Takashi Yamazaki's take on the King of Monsters as one of the mightiest yet by upping the ante without losing sight of its lovable characters." Buoyed by positive reviews, the film grossed nearly $32 million in its domestic debut, nearly tripling the $11 million opening-weekend haul of its predecessor.
