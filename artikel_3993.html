Winter Whiskers launches December 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Winter Whiskers, a “simple cleaning sim that turns the chore of snow-clearing into a relaxing and satisfying game,” will launch for PC via Steam on December 9, developer Mango Leaf Games announced.

Here is a overview of the game, via Mango Leaf Games:

An unexpected blizzard just rolled through town, and right before the big Winter Festival! Winterlight Harbor needs you and your fancy snow tools to help clear away all the snow so that the Winter Festival can go ahead as scheduled. ​

At the heart of this adventure is your trusty snow vacuum and the broken steam system that usually powers the town. Vacuum up all the snow and load it into furnaces that convert it into steam, turning back on the power in homes, lighting the streets, and activating vents that melt icy paths. Your cleanup efforts literally warm up the town and make it safe for your friends.
