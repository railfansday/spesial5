"We've finally reached this milestone": Cyberpunk 2077 multiplayer mod CyberMP kicks off open beta after around six years preparing for cybermayhem

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

"Let's see how the project handles real player traffic"

After a couple of years of regular testing behind closed doors, Cyberpunk 2077 multiplayer mod CyberMP is now out in open beta, giving anyone who fancies a bit of multi-merc action a chance to give it a go. Though, if you do, be prepared for bugs and crashes, as work on the mod is far from over with the release of this "true beta".

As shown off in the video above, CyberMP's not about being able to play through the base game's missions with friends, but rather cruise around Night City as a gang getting into races and PvP shootouts. In that regard, it seems more akin to GTA 4's online mode than GTA 5's, with classic improvised hijinks the main draw over being able to pull off gigs with a crew. Though, there do also look to be roleplaying servers which could scratch the itch of those more interested in pretending to be a merc than blowing up other people's rides.

"After almost six years of development, countless reworks, and code rewrites, we're finally launching the public beta," the modders wrote on Discord. "Anyone can jump in and play now — no invites or applications needed. Fair warning: this is a true beta. Crashes, desyncs, and server drops under heavy load are completely normal for now. That's exactly why we need testers. We welcome everyone: regular players, future server admins, and those who just love breaking game mechanics."

"Right now, our absolute priority is stability and fixing crashes," they continued later in the post. "New features are planned, but we need to polish the foundation first. A huge thank you to everyone who has been with us since the early versions, helped with closed testing, and patiently waited. We've finally reached this milestone. Let's see how the project handles real player traffic!"

According to the modders, CyberMP's "client doesn't have a digital signature yet", which could cause antivirus software not to be a fan of it. They're also anticipating "problems with the anti‑cheat system", so it may be worth holding off on giving the mod a go if you're not keen on being trolled by someone with the sort of destructive power usually reserved for megacorps and AIs from beyond the blackwall in the Cyberpunkverse.

For info on how to get CyberMP up and running, as well as create a custom server dedicated to the likes of roleplay or races, head over to its website. As you might expect, the mod requires a legit copy of Cyberpunk bought on either Steam, GOG, or the Epic store.
