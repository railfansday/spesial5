Everything Included In GTA 6's First Post-Launch Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Grand Theft Auto 6 has only been out for less than a week at this point, and Rockstar already seems hard at work on refining the experience closer to its full immersive potential. While GTA 6 has already blown past global records for its current sales in the entertainment industry with glowing review scores from critics, there have still been plenty of reports regarding frequent technical issues and other complaints with the gameplay experience.

While some of these problems, like the 30fps lock on consoles and complaints over GTA 6's gameplay design, aren't ones that can be fixed anytime soon, some of the other bugs contributing to its current review-bombing are already being addressed.

As shared by the GTA 6 Countdown on X, Rockstar has officially released the first post-launch gameplay patch for the studio's long-awaited open-world RPG that fixes a few miscellaneous performance issues like crashing, weather changes, autosaving, and more broken down in the following points on Rockstar support:

The response to the update by fans has also been surprisingly positive so far, with players most relieved by the fact that they'll finally be able to see the variety of GTA 6's weather as opposed to the open-world being stuck in a perpetual "always sunny" state, while also no longer worrying abotu the game crashing as frequently and soft-locking progress at certain missions.

The update will also drastically increase the chances of getting a storm as a result, which many fans were confused about why they haven't experienced even once yet.

While some eager players have already started achievement hunting at 100 percenting everything GTA 6 has to offer, Rockstar's post-launch updates are helping make the experience more seamless for those still powering through the game or taking their time exploring its world.

Rockstar leadership vowed in a new interview with Bloomberg that the studio's next game won't take another 8 years to come out.

Given that there are still a few issues like object clipping, collision, and vehicle behaviour, this will certainly be far from the first official patch for Grand Theft Auto 6 either, with more fixes to come while we wait for the eventual Online expansion and PC release.
