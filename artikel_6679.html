If you can remember what a modem sounds like, these under-$20 picks are for you

We all have different expectations for our foundation: Some prefer sheer coverage, others full; some want a dewy finish, and others a natural finish. But when you’re 50+, perhaps the most important quality is finding a hydrating formula that won't exacerbate wrinkles, spots, and texture. Although there are excellent foundations for mature skin for all budgets, don't sleep on what's right at the drugstore.

“You can absolutely find a high-quality foundation that caters to the unique needs of mature skin, such as hydration, anti-aging benefits, and a smooth, even finish that isn’t cakey—even at the drugstore,” professional makeup artist Allie Renee tells Harper’s Bazaar. “Many foundations are formulated specifically with mature skin in mind and can provide a beautiful result without breaking the bank.”

To prove it, we've gathered the best drugstore foundations for mature skin, with expert picks from Renee and top-reviewed products. Find your new go-to below.

What Should I Look for in a Foundation for Mature Skin?

Renee recommends keeping an eye out for “a balanced foundation that allows the skin to feel comfortable without emphasizing texture or fine lines on the complexion, as this will ultimately achieve a smooth, natural-looking finish.” The key to striking that balance, she says, is finding one that satisfies both personal preferences while addressing the specific needs of mature skin. “Remember, the goal is to enhance, not cover, your unique features,” she adds.

Are Matte Formulas a Dealbreaker for Mature Skin?

Matte foundations can make mature skin appear drier and exacerbate the look of fine lines. Instead, “Look for a dewy, radiant, or satin finish,” recommends Renee. “These types of finishes will give your skin a youthful glow without emphasizing texture or dryness.” That being said, if you have mature skin that still leans oily, you may be able to wear a matte-finish foundation.
