6 Flannel Shirt Outfits to Tackle the Winter Season in Style

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Plaid and checks are here to stay for another season. The spring-summer 2027 shows made that abundantly clear, with Dior, Loewe, The Row, and Chanel all incorporating grid-like patterns into their collections. Once a staple of the grunge and club scenes of the ’90s, the fabric takes on a more refined form this season, fashioned into tailored silhouettes and reimagined in unexpected colorways.

A great layering piece no matter how you style it, flannel moves effortlessly through shifting temperatures and settings. This past fashion month, It girls and models off-duty proved its versatility, tucking it into skirts, wearing it over jeans, or peeking it out from under a suede jacket. For ways to wear flannel right now (plus our favorite styles to shop), keep scrolling for six outfit ideas built around this cold-weather essential.

The flannel shirt pops when surrounded by autumnal hues and rich textures: Think khaki cords, brown leather loafers, and a gold-buckle belt.

For a take on the groutfit that still incorporates the print, try Leset’s button-down, neatly tucked into COS’s pencil skirt. The result is a polished ensemble that will carry you through your 9-to-9—no outfit change required.

In bright red, the flannel shirt brings a touch of Western flair to city dressing. Style it with worn-in jeans, your favorite cowboy boots, and a classic wool coat wrapped on top.

Contrast a brown flannel shirt and supple leather skirt with sharp white pumps. The overall silhouette scans polished, but the darker palette and mix of textures toughen it up.

The best off-duty outfits are rooted in relaxed silhouettes with a pop of personality. Leset’s wide-leg satin pants are a wardrobe staple, while a flannel shirt and bright red tee add a playful touch.

We love the way a flannel tied around the waist looks. Here, it stands in for a belt, giving shape to a black knit dress. Layer the look underneath a trench coat, and finish with a black handbag. Add a pair of biker boots, and you’ve practically traveled back in time.
