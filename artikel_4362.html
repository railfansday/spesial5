10 Legendary Grenades and Ordnances You Should Have in Borderlands 4

There are various types of legendary items in Borderlands 4, and it goes without saying that they are some of the best items in the game. Because of this, you will want to fill your endgame build with legendary items galore.

The main items you will be using across your builds are weapons, shields, and grenades. Once you have some powerful legendary weapons and have acquired a shield, you will need to focus on getting either a strong grenade or a strong ordnance. For a primary weapon, consider the best legendary assault rifles.

There are numerous legendary grenades and ordnances in Borderlands 4, and several of them are must-have items at this point in the game to refine your build with.

There are some grenades that you have to be careful about where you throw them, and then there are grenades that many refer to as “throw and forget” grenades. This is because you don’t need to worry about where you throw them. Instead, you can toss it in a general direction and go.

The Swarm is a grenade you can forget about because it homes in on targets. Its ability, Swoosh, makes it spawn three homing grenades on impact, and those spawn additional homing grenades for a total of nine.

If you happen to get bonuses on this build that increase your grenade damage and explosion size, you will be living the dream. You can get the Swarm from Sidney Pointylegs in Hungering Plain.

If you are someone who wants to use ordnances, your options are somewhat limited, as there aren’t as many of them in the game, but they are quite powerful. One of the more powerful ones is the Disc Jockey.

This heavy launcher has the ability Bouncing Biscuits, which makes it so it launches energy discs that bounce off surfaces. Each time one of these discs bounces, the damage of the disc increases by 20%.

This is a weapon that is great in small rooms, as the bounces can rack up some serious damage on groups of enemies. You can get this weapon from Sidney Pointylegs in Hungering Plain.

It is interesting whenever a grenade doesn’t deal grenade damage and instead gets all its power from another source. In the case of the Barb’ara, it gets its damage from your skill damage buffs, making it perfect for those who are running skill builds.

This grenade has the ability Tentacles, which causes it to create a tentacle explosion on impact. The explosion created by this impact deals skill damage. These tentacles will spawn two additional grenades that create more tentacles, also dealing skill damage.

Since every Vault Hunter in the game can run a skill build, this is a great grenade to have whenever you decide to switch up your build. You can farm this grenade from Crazed Earl in the Vault of the Damned DLC.

There are plenty of weapons in Borderlands 4 that have been added in DLC. Which ones of them are the best?

Sometimes you just want to fire a rocket launcher and let it delete your problems. In this case, the problem is usually a group of enemies or a massive boss that is blocking your path. The Loiter Sploiter is designed to take care of these problems quickly, and is great if you have an ordnance-focused build and amp gear.

The Loiter Sploiter’s ability, Sploiter, makes it so this weapon fires high-damage rockets. These rockets then spawn additional rockets when they explode, causing massive amounts of damage.

If you are running gear with the amped ability, this will power up these rockets, making them capable of one-shotting bosses with ease. You can get this weapon Infamous Dave in the Murders & Acquisitions DLC.

There are some grenades in Borderlands 4 that don’t just explode. Instead, they have some quirky effects such as shooting lasers or a machine gun at enemies before they explode. Either way, you get an explosion.

The Alignment is one of those grenades, as it has the ability Coordinated, which makes it so the grenade seeks out an enemy when thrown, hovers above them, shoots them with a beam of elemental energy, and then divebombs them.

It is an interesting effect that makes that weapon great for dealing elemental status effects, and therefore, is good for those who want an elemental status effect build. You can obtain this grenade from the Warped & Weird Vault Card.

Speaking of the weird and quirky, the Heimdahl is perhaps the weirdest of the ordnance weapons in Borderlands 4. This is because the weapon shoots Dahl soldier heads that attack enemies. They are out for revenge, and will kill anyone in their way.

The ability of this weapon is called Rig. It fires soldier heads that fly around the battlefield, track down enemies, and vomit on them. This deals significant damage, and is a weapon that you can fire and forget since it tracks enemies.

This weapon is perfect for Vault Hunter builds where you don’t need to worry about ordnance ammo, as you can just keep firing it and clear out a room in no time. You can obtain this weapon from Captain Willis in The Maelstrom in the Vault of the Damned DLC.

There are plenty of legendary assault rifles in Borderlands 4, and we will show you how to get them all.

The Blockbuster grenade is one that will clear an area with ease, but you better make sure to stay out of the area or you will get killed. This grenade is designed to do one thing, which is to cause numerous explosions.

The ability of this grenade is called Got You Covered, and it does give you plenty of cover. This ability makes the grenade split into four grenades on impact. These grenades then spawn an additional two grenades. This sounds normal until you realize that these grenades all bounce in place, creating explosions as they do.

It will cover the field in explosions, and you just need to stay out of the way so you don’t get killed. You can get this grenade from Vile Lictor in Terminus Range.

The Firepot is similar to the Swarm grenade, but it focuses on dealing fire damage and it loves to stick grenades to enemies. Torgue returns with one of the finest grenades in Borderlands 4, and it packs a serious punch.

The ability of this grenade is called Spicy, and it makes the grenade spawn homing Torgue sticky grenades that attach to enemies. In total, nine grenades will spawn from this bad boy, and they will each explode after 13 seconds, igniting the enemies that are unfortunate enough to be hit by them.

It is another throw and forget style grenade that does an amazing job covering the field without you needing to focus too much on what you are doing. You can get this grenade from Leader Willem in Grindstone of the Worthy.

The Waterfall was one of my first legendary grenades in Borderlands 4, so it holds a special place in my heart, and it might be a bit weaker than some of the other weapons on this list. However, this doesn’t mean that it isn’t worth getting, as it packs a serious punch.

This grenade has the Waterfall ability, which makes it explode multiple times whenever it hits an enemy. Throw it in a group, and each enemy damaged by the grenade will cause another explosion, cascading the damage over the surrounding area.

It can wipe out groups of enemies easily, but it is noticeably not as effective as the Swarm. You can get this grenade by farming Bio-Bulkhead in the Dissected Plateau.

There are some grenades and ordnances that can clear out entire rooms, and then there are some that are focused on one thing, and that is killing bosses. The Draupner is basically a delete button for life’s problems, and it is amazing.

Return Wave is the name of the Draupner’s ability, and it makes the weapon fire out three saw blades in a horizontal pattern from the weapon. These saw blades return to you after two seconds or upon impact, effectively hitting enemies twice over.

This ordnance effectively diminishes the damage numbers of every other ordnance in the game, making it a must-have weapon. You can obtain it by running the Takedown at the Hadron Abyss.

There are plenty of legendary weapons in Borderlands 4, but these are the weapons that you should have at this point if you want a strong build.
