Hyundai’s new Mercury EV Concept will launch as the IONIQ M in 2027 [Images]

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Hyundai unveiled the Mercury Concept, a preview of its upcoming entry-level electric SUV set to launch as the IONIQ M in 2027.

Following the IONIQ V, which went on sale in September, Hyundai offered a look at its upcoming IONIQ lineup for China at the Guangzhou Auto Show on November 27.

Hyundai’s Mercury Concept stole the spotlight. The concept previews its upcoming “B-segment premium smart SUV,” set to launch in China in 2027.

According to Hyundai, the IONIQ M will be “the most accessible entry-level model in the IONIQ universe.” Unfortunately, it’s designed specifically for China.

Like the IONIQ V, the electric SUV was developed by Hyundai Motor’s Chinese design and engineering team. As such, it features a unique style and advanced tech you won’t find in Hyundai’s vehicles sold in the US, Europe, or other overseas markets.

The SUV’s futuristic low-slung design is inspired by Mercury’s orbit around the sun. Up front, the shark-nose design and slim, edgy lights form Hyundai’s “H” badge, while an integrated rear spoiler adds to the compact, sporty look.

Hyundai said the production version will feature Zhuoyu L2++ ADAS and a high-tech cabin powered by ByteDance’s Doubao AI model.

A battery-swap version is also set to launch through Hyundai’s partnership with China’s CATL following the production IONIQ M.

The Mercury follows the Venus, now on sale as the IONIQ V, and the larger Earth SUV, as Hyundai’s third IONIQ Planet name designed for China. It will go on sale in 2027 as the IONIQ M, alongside the IONIQ V EREV.

In 2028, Hyundai will launch the production version of the Earth SUV, while two new energy vehicles will join the lineup in 2029.

While Hyundai didn’t reveal battery or powertrain specs, it will likely feature a similar setup to the IONIQ V.

The Hyundai IONIQ V is available in China with two CATL lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery packs: A 53.5 kWh battery paired with a 140 kW (188 hp) rear electric motor that offers a CLTC range of up to 540 km, or a 66.8 kWh battery paired with a 168 kW (225 hp) motor with a CLTC range of up to 650 km (404 miles).

Hyundai said the IONIQ M will be “the most accessible entry-level model in the IONIQ universe.” Given the IONIQ V starts at 109,900 yuan ($16,500), the production IONIQ M could be priced around 99,000 yuan ($15,000) in China.
