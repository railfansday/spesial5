How to Navigate Amazon’s Black Friday Sale Like an Editor

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

From fashion to beauty, these are the deals worth shopping

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Amazon’s Black Friday Sale is one of the largest you’ll ever see. This year, the retailer extended its usual five-day promotion into a 12-day event called the Turkey 12, which runs through November 30. With so many markdowns to sort through, it can be hard to know where to start, so we’ve shortlisted the best Black Friday deals Amazon is offering across key categories like fashion, beauty, and tech.

Black Friday is a great time to grab big-ticket items like Apple iPads, headphones, and Dyson products. Then, circle back on Cyber Monday to sort through any last-minute deals that you might’ve missed. Ahead, shop the best Black Friday Amazon deals we found on handbags, sweaters, skincare devices, hair tools, and much more.

Amazon’s fashion department has a treasure trove of handbag deals. Totes and shoulder bags from Tory Burch, JW Pei, Rag & Bone, and Vince are all on sale for under $300.

Amazon’s current fashion deals can help round out your cold-weather wardrobe. Gap, Madewell, Lacoste, and more currently have sales on sweaters, button-down shirts, and jackets to make layering this winter even easier. Plus, Rag & Bone’s viral sweatpant jeans are on sale for 25 percent off, but we don’t expect them to stay in stock for long.

In Amazon’s shoe department, shop markdowns on popular labels like Adidas, Ugg, and Stuart Weitzman. Whether you’re after workout sneakers, work-appropriate flats, or everyday winter boots, you’ll have no trouble finding something you like.

Restock for your skincare routine with deals from Biodance, La Roche Posay, and other editor go-tos. Then browse markdowns on red-light masks and other skincare devices that make excellent holiday gifts for beauty tech obsessives.

Black Friday is a smart time to upgrade your hair tools. Editor-approved brands like T3, Ghd, and Wavytalk have markdowns on flat irons, blow-dry brushes, and curling irons that’ll revamp your daily routine.
