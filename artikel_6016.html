She Lived to the Sound of God’s Music

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Remembering God’s gift to New York City girls and beyond.

From left: Sr. Christine Cosgrove, O.P., the author, Sr. Martha Kunesh, O.P., and speaker Kristen Hanson, an advocate against assisted suicide, at a National Review Institute/Sheen Center event on the virtue of hope.

Could anything sound more exotic today? This opening line comes from a 16th-century prayer by the Carmelite reformer St. Teresa of Avila. She carried it in the breviary she used for daily prayer. It also captures the quiet steadiness of Sister Martha Kunesh, O.P. (the initials stand for “Order of Preachers”), who entered a Dominican convent just before the opening of the Second Vatican Council. She took the religious name Sister Teresa Avila (“the ‘of’ was silent,” it was said). She eventually returned to her birth name, but she never lost the contemplative spirit of her first patron.
