Gasperini, D’Amico to hold transfer meeting this week

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Gian Piero Gasperini is convinced that the real step toward the top of the table can be made primarily through transfer market additions.

The international break will be a good time to recover injured players and review strategies ahead of the January transfer window, which aims to raise Roma’s level.

The transfer window (opening early on December 28th) is just around the corner, and Roma doesn’t want to be caught off guard to make the leap in quality.

Roma plans to use the upcoming transfer window to improve Gian Piero Gasperini’s squad.

As Il Tempo reports, a meeting is scheduled for the next few days between various officials at the Giallorossi club to plan moves ahead of the winter transfer window.

D’Amico, before the match against Sassuolo, made it clear that the primary objective will be to find a winger capable of counterattacking with pace, a trait Gasperini’s squad lacks. The manager is awaiting further developments.
