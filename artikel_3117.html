The CEO behind GTA VI doesn’t play games. Analysts say he bet up to $1.5B

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Strauss Zelnick says being a gamer doesn't make a better boss, and his biggest bet launches on Thursday.

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has run the company since 2011.

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick does not play the games his company sells, Fortune reported, as GTA VI launches with an estimated $1B to $1.5B budget. Take-Two has not confirmed the cost, but the game has tied Metacritic’s record score and pre-orders beat expectations.

Strauss Zelnick has run Take-Two Interactive, which owns GTA studio Rockstar, since 2011. He does not play the games it sells, Fortune reported in a profile republished on Wednesday, the eve of Grand Theft Auto VI’s launch.

“I don’t think being consumer-in-chief really helps a CEO be effective in this business,” Zelnick told Business Insider.

Analysts cited by Business Insider estimate GTA VI cost $1B to $1.5B to make. Take-Two has not confirmed a figure, and Zelnick has only said the game “was expensive”.

The bet looks set to pay off. GTA VI tied Metacritic’s all-time record with a score of 99, and Take-Two said pre-orders beat expectations. Sensor Tower data puts pre-orders at 17 million, according to Fortune.

The game costs from $80 and comes without a physical disc, as Sony plans to stop making discs for new games from 2028. Its predecessor, GTA V, has sold more than 215 million copies since 2013.

Under Zelnick, Take-Two’s shares have risen more than 1,700%, and its revenue grew roughly fivefold to $5.6B in 2025. He has also said generative AI played no part in making GTA VI, which Rockstar built by hand.
