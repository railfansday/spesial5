Are You Considering Buying Anthropic Stock on Day 1? Here’s What I’m Doing.

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

IPO stocks can be quite volatile during their first few weeks on the market.

By Keithen Drury – Nov 18, 2026 at 4:17PM EST

Anthropic will be going public on Nov. 19, and there are many investors who are looking to get in on the action right off the bat. However, some are content sitting on the sidelines, waiting for more information to come out about the company. Staying patient has the added benefit of allowing the market to settle on what a fair price for the stock is, as it could be much higher (or lower) than what the first trading price is.

So, what am I doing? I am going to stay patient, but keep a watchful eye.

Nobody knows what will happen when Anthropic starts trading. While the company sets an initial price, the demand for shares could be so great that it results in a big first-day pop.

On the flip side, the market is fairly bearish on AI right now. Anthropic is about to be the only pure-play AI stock available on the market, and that may not go well depending on investors’ risk appetite. I think the odds of investors sinking the stock on Day 1 are low, but the market has done stranger things.

If the stock does pop on Day 1 and then drop, I might be willing to pounce on the stock and buy then. But what if it doesn't sell off in the near future and drop to a price I like? I’ll still be OK missing the action on Day 1. I'm not planning on buying Anthropic stock for what it will do over a few weeks or months. I'd buy it for what it could do over multiple years.

Anthropic has the leading AI model family in Claude right now, and the bounds of its business are currently unknown. The market has yet to see what steady-state AI usage looks like for companies of all sizes, and that means that Anthropic's growth potential is uncapped right now (Although there is a limit somewhere).

Because I'm planning on investing in Anthropic over the long term, I'm OK staying patient and seeing how the company does over multiple earnings reports. That will give me a better idea regarding how the business is run and what challenges it faces.

In Anthropic's S1 document filed with the SEC, it had some impressive figures, including revenue growth over 1,000%. However, Anthropic is currently not profitable, and I'd like to see what its pathway to profitability shapes up to be. As of now, a little over half of its expenses go to compute costs, so it will never be able to have a high profit margin.

That reality may become more apparent as the company is on the public markets, and that view could shift its valuation. I don't know enough about Anthropic, the company, to be able to make an investing decision right now. As a result, I'm OK staying patient and waiting for a bit to see what happens before deciding to buy the stock or not.
