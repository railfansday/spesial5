Chris Pratt Shares Holiday Plans With Wife Katherine Schwarzenegger and Kids

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Chris Pratt is counting down the days until he’s at home with his family for the holiday season.

“I’m really excited for the holidays. Man, I love the holidays,” Pratt, 47, exclusively told Us on Saturday, November 21, while attending the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix. “Getting the families together. You know, watch football.”

The actor teased that fans can watch his latest venture Way of the Warrior Kid, written by Jocko Willink, when it releases on Thursday, November 25, a.k.a. Thanksgiving day on Apple TV.

When it comes to his own family, the Jurassic World star reiterated that he loves everything about the holidays.

“I look forward to it all year round,” Pratt shared, noting that he “always knows” that he’ll “be home for the holidays” despite his sporadic filming schedule.

Pratt — who shares three children with wife Katherine Schwarzenegger and one with ex-wife Anna Faris — explained, “Usually in my job, I might be gone at any given time. But typically you get Thanksgiving and Christmas off.”

The Parks and Recreation alum beamed with excitement, adding, “I always know I can be with my family and that means the world to me.”

Pratt has prioritized his family from the moment he became a father in August 2012 when he and Faris, 49, welcomed their son, Jack. The exes were married nine years before finalizing their divorce in 2018, but have maintained a good coparenting dynamic.

“I think it’s just the general idea of just making sure that he’s surrounded by a lot of love and happiness,” Faris exclusively told Us in January 2018 of the former couple’s ability to put their son first. “We’re really good at that and we have amazing friends and incredible family and we have, like, just the most amazing resources to make sure that he feels safe and protected and happy.”

Following his split from Faris, Pratt found love again with Katherine — Arnold Schwarzenegger and ex-wife Maria Shriver’s eldest child — whom he wed in 2019.

Pratt has since welcomed three kids with the Gift of Forgiveness author. Their daughter Lyla was born in August 2020, followed by daughter Eloise in May 2022. The Guardians of the Galaxy actor and Katherine, 36, rounded out their brood in November 2024 with the birth of their son, Ford.

When it comes to what values Pratt hopes to instill in all four of his children, he pointed to a strong faith above all else.

“I’m, for the most part, always gonna be a man of faith,” Pratt, 47, told Fox News Digital last month. “My faith is how I start my day, it’s how I end my day. It’s the most important thing that I’ll be able to pass on to my kids.”
