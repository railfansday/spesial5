5 Years Later, 'Yellowjackets' Officially Solves One of Its Last Huge Mysteries

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Yellowjackets season 4 is already providing answers

Yellowjackets has plenty of mysteries that have yet to be answered as the twisty survival thriller reaches its final season. Is the show supernatural or just a psychological thriller? Are there more adult Yellowjackets still alive? What does the symbol the teen Yellowjackets keep finding in the wilderness mean?

Yellowjackets season 4 is series creators Ashley Lyle and Bart Nickerson’s last chance to answer those questions. However, the new season’s first episode, “Hivemind,” has already solved one big mystery.

The very first episode of Yellowjackets is memorable for raising several big questions. There was the tease of the Antler Queen and the mystery of which member of the stranded Yellowjacket girls soccer team had been skewered in a pit by her classmates. Both of those mysteries were solved in season 3, but another big one remains. Where exactly did the girls get stranded when their plane crashed in the remote wilderness 19 months ago?

Over the years, fans have speculated about the exact location the Yellowjackets could have crashed. Because the girls were based in New Jerseyand were heading to Seattle for a national championship tournament, the general consensus is that they landed somewhere in Canada. Canada has many beautiful cities and towns, but it also has plenty of wilderness they could have flown over if the plane had diverted due to poor weather.

Fan speculation seems to have paid off. In the opening sequence of “Hivemind” in 1998, we see a young Jeff Sadecki (Sam Braun) trying to sell furniture to a customer. However, he is interrupted by a news broadcast revealing that the survivors of the Wiskayok plane crash have been found.

Turning the volume up, the news reporter states, “It's believed they [the Yellowjackets] were located in a remote stretch of the Canadian Rockies."

Although no exact location is provided, confirmation that the girls were in a remote part of Canada supports the idea that it would have been utterly hopeless for all of them to set out in search of help.

For one thing, the Rocky Mountains stretch for over 3,000 miles, and the lack of food and water would makeit impossible for them to trek through the mountains without encountering danger, as happened when they tried and were attacked by wolves.​​​​​​​

Lastly, the plane’s transponder had already been sabotaged by Misty (Samantha Hanratty), meaning that even if people were looking for the girls at that location, they wouldn’t have been able to pick up the signal.

With so many factors working against the girls being found, it’s clearer than ever that they made it back to civilizationonly because some frog scientists found them in season 3 and had a satellite phone.

That’s one big Yellowjackets mystery finally solved, but will there be others? We can’t wait to find out.

Yellowjackets streams exclusively on Paramount Plus. Seasons 1-3 are available now, with season 4’s episodes airing through Jan. 22, 2027.
