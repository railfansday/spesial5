At the moment there is just the one World of Stands code available, though this will unlock an extra Stand Storage Slot and some Haunted Arrows for summoning Stands. To redeem World of Stands codes, you need to tap the Menu button at the top of the screen, then from the next menu hit the Player icon, and finally select the Settings icon. You'll then see an Enter Code text box at the bottom of the screen, so use that to type in a promo before using the REDEEM button to claim your rewards. Codes for World of Stands are not case-sensitive, though many of them have a certain level requirement you need to reach before you can redeem them.
