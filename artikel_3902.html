Elements Destiny launches December 8 for PS5, Xbox Series, PS4, Xbox One, and PC; December 15 for Switch

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

2D retro-inspired fantasy RPG Elements Destiny will launch for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, and PC via Steam and GOG on December 8, followed by Switch on December 15, publisher Deck13 Spotlight and developer ShrooMoon UG announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via Deck13 Spotlight:

Step into the world of Solitas in this epic 2D fantasy adventure.

What begins in a quiet, peaceful hamlet carries the lingering weight of past tragedies and ancient sacrifices. Players will control Erika, a young huntress whose troubling nightmares hint at a far greater fate. You won’t carry this burden alone, Lia, Maline, and Karen join Erika on her quest, each bringing their own strength to the struggle for Solitas.
