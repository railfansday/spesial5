Birmingham City push to sign La Liga forward who is 'fed up’ - it may impact Carlos Vicente

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Birmingham City will head into the month of December as automatic promotion contenders in the Championship, having finally found their spark under Chris Davies.

A 10th-placed finish in the second tier last year, just 12 months after their 111-point haul in League One, was respectable enough, but there was always the scope for more to come from Davies' squad, and they're really showing it now.

Whilst Blues look to have depth all over the pitch, one area that they could look to bolster in the upcoming January transfer window is at the top end.

He had a slow start to life in the Midlands since his £6 million move from Djurgardens in January, but August Priske has finally come alive in the Championship.

The 22-year-old Dane found the back of the net just once from his 17 Championship appearances in the second half of 2025-26, but now acclimatised to English football, he's scored seven goals in 13 outings so far in the current campaign.

Priske has forged a good understanding with Jay Stansfield in the number 10 role, but his understudies have made little impact, with German veteran Marvin Ducksch and summer recruit Luis Vazquez both failing to fire off the bench.

It leaves Davies with a dilemma ahead of January - do City let one of the pair go and bring in a new striker to battle with Priske in the second half of the season as they push for the Premier League?

Blues may do just that, as reports have been emerging about a potential striker transfer target that is based in Spain.

According to a report from Ficherío, Sevilla forward Isaac Romero is on the club's radar ahead of the window's opening at the start of 2027, with the 26-year-old Spaniard growing frustrated with life at Los Nervionenses due to a lack of game-time this season.

It's claimed that Birmingham are already in contact with the agents of Romero in regards to a deal, and that a 'significant salary' has been put on the table for him to make the move to the second tier of English football - albeit it's also reported that Romero would like to stay at Sevilla if he gets more chances of minutes.

Romero was plucked from lower league Spanish outfit Antoniano in 2019, having started his youth career with Sevilla, making his first-team debut in 2024 for the club.

Starting in just four of his 11 La Liga appearances, Romero is behind Lucas Stassin and Robbie Ure in the pecking order, but he's scored more goals than the pair put together so far in the Spanish top flight, netting three times compared to Stassin and Ure's two goals combined.

If Birmingham make good progress with a move to sign Romero from Sevilla, then it could spark a departure from St. Andrew's - but not necessarily from their striker unit.

Reports from earlier in the week claim that Sevilla have an interest in signing Blues winger Carlos Vicente, who hasn't found his best form since signing from Alaves for £7 million last January.

Vicente worked under current Sevilla boss Luis Garcia Plaza whilst he was the manager of Alaves, and he's reportedly keen to re-unite with the 27-year-old just a year after he departed for England.
