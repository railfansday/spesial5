Why Jules Ahoka can save Regis Le Bris' job, Sunderland stat proves it

Jules Ahoka was handed a rare opportunity for Sunderland against Chelsea.

After Granit Xhaka went down injured in just the second minute of the Black Cats' 1-0 win, Regis Le Bris turned to the 20-year-old as his replacement.

It was certainly a significant call from the Mackems manager, as Ahoka had only one Premier League appearance this season prior, and had yet to start a game.

And it would have been a nerve-wracking moment for the summer signing.

However, the DR Congo youth international proved his worth with an exceptional showcase for the Northern English side.

And following the international break, he must be a regular in the side to help Sunderland stave off relegation.

Ahoka delivered a performance that should give Le Bris plenty to think about when deciding his midfield options.

The youngster showed he could compete physically and technically against elite Premier League opposition.

As per FotMob, Ahoka ended the game against Chelsea as the player with the most defensive contributions, with a total of 14.

A particularly impressive feat when you consider Moises Caicedo was also on the pitch.

Ahoka also showed plenty of bravery on the ball too, as he completed two out of three attempted dribbles, while also completing three out of six long passes.

He won 75 per cent of his duels, proving that his lack of experience was no issue for him against one of the Premier League's top sides.

Even if Xhaka's injury does not keep him out for a sustained period, Ahoka must be confident that he has done enough to warrant a string of starts for Sunderland.

If so, he can be the midfield presence Le Bris needs to turn Sunderland's season around.

Despite Sunderland's poor form before the weekend, Le Bris is likely the manager who least wanted this international break.

Following the win against Chelsea, the Frenchman could have used that to galvanise his squad ahead of a tough run of fixtures coming up.

Instead, the manager will see a break in play in the English top flight, potentially threatening any momentum earned on Sunday.

After the break, Sunderland face a run of five games against Aston Villa, Tottenham, Liverpool, Newcastle and Nottingham Forest.

Each game is likely to prove tough to secure all three points.
