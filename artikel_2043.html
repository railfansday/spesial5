‘Another defeat’: Wolves vs Derby County predictions

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Wolves come into Tuesday night’s clash with Derby County after extending their Championship unbeaten run to seven games with a 2-1 win over Charlton Athletic. Cesar Peixoto’s side started the second half with a flurry as Adam Armstrong and Rafiki Said netted in the 51st and 54th minutes, both set up by Mateus Mane.

The victory moves Wanderers into 3rd in the table, behind only Stoke City and Middlesbrough. They have lost just once in their eight home league games this term too, winning all of the last three.

As for Derby County, they make the trip to Molineux off the back of a 2-0 loss away at Southampton. It marked a tough fixture for DCFC as they looked to build on a 3-1 win over Bolton Wanderers before the international break.

It has been a tough season for the Rams, who remain 23rd in the table with just 10 points from 16 games. John Eustace will hope form can start to turn with the return of some key players though, with Patrick Agyemang, Matt Clarke and Eiran Cashin all closing in on comebacks.

“Wolves are in great form and in a good place in the Championship table. Cesar Peixoto had to weather a bit of a storm in the early stages of his tenure but their seven-game unbeaten streak has seen them collect 17 points while conceding only five goals.

“Mateus Mane was excellent against Charlton Athletic and it will be hoped he can really kick on after a fairly quiet campaign to date. Rafiki Said looks like an exciting player, and Adam Armstrong can be relied on to score goals at Championship level.

“I think Old Gold will have too much for the Derby County defence. DCFC would have hoped to build on that Bolton Wanderers win, but away games at Southampton and Wolves mean they don’t have a great chance of building momentum and clambering out of the bottom three.

“I’ll be going for a home win here, and in fairly routine fashion.”

“Wolves look like serious top two contenders and they will see their upcoming clash as another opportunity to pick up three points. WWFC have won their last three home games without conceding a goal.

“Cesar Peixoto’s men haven’t lost since their defeat to Bolton Wanderers in mid-October. They are well organised and have dangerous threats at the top end of the pitch.

“Derby County continue to struggle for regular points and they are at serious risk of slipping into League One this season. DCFC have won only two of their 16 matches in the league this term which is a massive worry.

“The Rams’ injury issues have been well documented and pressure continues to grow on John Eustace. I think they will slump to another defeat on their travels here.”
