Rangers & Simon Jordan won’t like what’s happening at Leicester City right now

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Leicester City and Russell Martin are in far better form, with Rangers and Simon Jordan now part of an interesting story developing in recent months now that things are finally looking up for the Foxes.

Their 1-0 away victory over Huddersfield Town was arguably one of the most impressive results anywhere in League One this season, suggesting Russell Martin's ideas are beginning to take hold with this Leicester City squad.

The former MK Dons, Swansea City, Southampton, and Rangers boss has now taken charge of five clubs, with Leicester representing his latest challenge. After a difficult start, he is beginning to show why the Foxes appointed him.

Martin's appointment at Leicester was always going to divide opinion, particularly after his disastrous 123-day spell at Rangers, where he won just five of his 17 matches before being dismissed.

He then endured a difficult start in the East Midlands, winning two and losing three of his first eight league fixtures. The pressure was considerable given Leicester's expectation of bouncing straight back to the Championship, with another season in League One representing a disaster for a club of their stature.

Simon Jordan was among those questioning Martin's position at Leicester, with things looking particularly bleak after the 3-1 defeat to Peterborough United and former Leicester player Robbie Savage. The warning was there that he would be sacked unless results improved.

The 25 top scorers in the Championship since the second tier was rebranded back in 2004/05, including Billy Sharp and Aleksandar Mitrovic.

However, the Foxes have since won four of their next six league games, including that outstanding Huddersfield victory. Rangers supporters and some of the divisive coach’s other critics could be eating their words.

They have always piled on Martin for last season, with neither they nor Jordan perhaps enjoying seeing him turn things around. Leicester are looking considerably more positive, easy on the eye, and results are following.

Martin's Southampton side also took time to get going, suffering four consecutive defeats early in his first season before eventually embarking on a remarkable 25-match unbeaten run.

George Elek, an EFL expert and Not The Top 20 podcast pundit, has argued that Martin's reputation has become a footballing culture war, with his failures often attracting considerably more attention than his achievements.

In further replies on X (formerly Twitter), Elek added: "Tough ask to expect him to come on board a sinking ship and turn it around straight away. League position at this stage is pretty meaningless, they’re clearly playing well now. Why do you think chucking money at it will solve the clear rot that had set in? His playing style is extreme. Is he not entitled to have some time to implement it?"

There is a reasonable argument that Leicester simply needed to give him time to implement his ideas, particularly given the scale of the rebuilding job. The Foxes could now be ready to climb the table rapidly and become genuine automatic promotion contenders rather than simply hoping to sneak into the play-offs.

There is, however, a cautionary tale from Martin's Southampton tenure. Despite that extraordinary unbeaten run, Saints eventually fizzled out during the closing stages of the 2023/24 campaign, losing three games in one damaging April week and missing out on automatic promotion.

They entered the play-offs with questions surrounding their form, although they ultimately overcame Leeds at Wembley. Leicester would happily take that outcome, but should be wary of allowing their standards to drop once the initial momentum arrives.

Martin has the midfield quality to dominate League One for the foreseeable future, with John Lundstram, Sammy Braybrooke, Will Smallbone, and Louis Page offering an excellent blend of technical ability, energy and composure.

That engine room could become the foundation of a serious promotion challenge from this point forth. Rangers fans and Jordan would not enjoy that turnaround, should it come to fruition.
