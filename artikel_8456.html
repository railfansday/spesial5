And there lies the problem with LTCi. It pays out a few hundred thousand and that’s it. Here is a plan I found for Illinois (not where I live.)

For a 60 year old male I would pay about $5k a year for a plan that will pay out $400k with a 2% inflation rider. There is some longevity in my family. Let’s say I need care starting at 85. I would pay $5k a year for 25 years which adds up to $125k. If instead I invested that money in a 60/40 portfolio and we consider the historical average return of 8.7% minus the same 2% inflation (6.7%) that portfolio would be $302k in today’s dollars. How is missing out on $302k in my portfolio worth the chance of getting $400k in possible long term care payments.

The whole thing doesn’t make sense. I can afford the $98k difference. I would expect most bogleheads can afford the difference.
