This is my thinking as well. Transactions are friction to be avoided as much as reasonably practical.

I am in full time decumulation, and I have a reasonably sophisticated to AA tracker for my portfolio. I normally take withdrawals to the extent possible that address whichever portion of the portfolio is "over target", so I am constantly moving toward the perfect, but only as much as I need to live my life.... That means outside deep dives like March 2020, nothing really happens except taking regular withdrawals.

I am way past the years for many reasons where buying the dips or looking for a rebalance alpha/bonus even gets passing consideration.
Since all my accounts are tax-advantaged, transactions cost me nothing but the few seconds to implement them. And since I check my AA anyway every couple of weeks and when I see a big change in the markets, there’s no real extra effort.
100% agree that tax advantaged accounts are where the small rebalancing band works for me. I would never rebalance frequently if there were big tax consequences.
