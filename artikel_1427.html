Ramos sees Olympiacos clash as an ‘opportunity’ but admits: “It’s now or never”

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Goncalo Ramos spoke to the media ahead of AC Milan’s game against Olympiacos, admitting that it’s now or never for the Rossoneri.

After clinching just three points in the first four games, Milan are well aware that a win is needed tonight. A top-eight finish is now out of reach, so we are talking about getting to the knockout play-offs, for which at least nine points will likely be needed.

It’s no surprise, therefore, that Ruben Amorim has opted for a little less rotation for the game against Olympiacos tonight. Goncalo Ramos is one of the players keeping his spot in the line-up from the weekend, hoping to build on the goal he scored against Frosinone.

Speaking to Sky Italia, as transcribed by MilanNews, the No.9 admitted that Milan are in a ‘now or never’ situation when it comes to the Europa League. He also said that they are improving week by week, which hopefully means they will finally put in good European displays tonight.

You are more cheerful after the goal against Frosinone…

“I’m happy. Tonight is another opportunity to play well, so we are all excited.”

Are you feeling more and more at ease here at Milan?

“I think that’s a natural part of our job. During the season, we feel better week by week, and we work to improve day by day.”

Do you think you can be decisive in such an important match?

“We will try to win this match. It’s very important. We want to reach the knockout stage, so it’s now or never.”
