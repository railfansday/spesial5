Bitcoin rose 4% over the past 24 hours to $63,470.62 on Monday, with most of the gain coming in the last hour after Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive. The move lifted risk assets across the board. Nvidia shares surged as much as 30% in premarket trading, adding close to $1 trillion in value within minutes, while Nasdaq futures briefly halted after a 7% jump. Since crypto has been trading closely with tech stocks, the rebound quickly spilled over. Ether climbed 4.7% to $1,812.43, Solana rose 7.6% to $77.43 and BNB gained 3.4% to $579.26. XRP rose 5.9% to reclaim the $1 level, making it the only major token, along with Solana, in the green for the week. Tether and USDC held their dollar pegs. Bitcoin is still down 1.4% over seven days, after last week’s slide below $56,000. Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse had predicted a crypto bounce once the Anthropic IPO cleared, and Monday’s move brings some of that recovery.
