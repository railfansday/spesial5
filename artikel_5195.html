Binance Halts Luna Classic Burns: What’s Going On?

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Home » Altcoins » Binance Halts Luna Classic Burns: What’s Going On?

Terra Luna Classic’s community got used to the monthly burning ceremonies, but this time it’s postponed.

A few weeks after Binance’s $6.4 billion hack following an employee’s laptop breach, the largest digital asset exchange across the globe is resuming most of their regular services. However, the Terra Luna Classic (LUNC) community has missed the monthly LUNC burn, coming from 50% of fees garnered from Binance’s LUNC-related trading pairs.

Typically done on the first of the month, this time Binance’s LUNC burn didn’t arrive at all, chain records show. That’s the first break in a pattern running since March 2023, when Binance pledged to help restore the downtrodden Terra Luna Classic community’s hope in LUNC’s long-term restoration efforts via continuous token burns.

On-chain records show 50 batches of LUNC burns being carried out by Binance on an earlier note. The last one, which was on October 1, 2026, eliminated 335 million tokens, pushing the exchange’s cumulative Terra Luna Classic burn count towards 91 billion.

That’s roughly 19.6% of all LUNC burns to this date, a considerable contribution in the deflationary campaign.

Some LUNC community members are floating the main reason for the delay to be apparent changes in wallet security, but Binance confirmed no such action. Naturally, the internal wallet reshuffling can be a normal practice following the multi-billion dollar hack and the day-by-day withdrawal restoration efforts done with diligence.

Back in August, the community behind Terra Luna Classic revisited the proposal 12223 to raise the on-chain transaction tax to 1.5%. Of that, 1.2 percentage points go towards burning even more LUNC coins. Meanwhile, strong voices in the crypto community have been advocating for more exchanges beyond Binance to burn fixed-fee allocations.

KuCoin & OKX were among the ones named in negotiations. The game-tested altcoin still has a massively over-minted supply of 5.5 trillion tokens in circulation, a figure normally ascribed to meme coins.

However, the LUNC chain restoration efforts have torched nearly 500 billion out of the original pile, showcasing what years of strict coordination can do for an embattled community.

Aside from the 19% portion claimed by Binance, the on-chain transaction tax and direct LUNC community burns make up the rest of the historical total. Regardless, the biggest hurdle for a proper Terra Luna Classic (LUNC) restoration point remains the stagnant trading volume.

Since the hard fork in May 2022, the cumulative burn efforts crunched 6.7% of total supply so far.

Token burning is a considerable deflation measure for long-term holders to ensure the asset remains at relatively bearable market value.
