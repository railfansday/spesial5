My Dream is Finally Happening

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

I am so excited to be writing this right now. It feels like a long time coming and it’s finally happening. My name is Annie and I have, potentially an unhealthy, obsession with the outdoors and hiking. I grew up with an amazing mom and dad that pushed me out the door to explore, rather than sit in front of a tv or screen. Thank God. My parents took my sister, Aubrey, and I on lots of family hikes to places like Tallulah Gorge, Pine Mountain, and Red River Gorge. My dad eventually started to hear about the Appalachian Trail, and decided to take me on some backpacking trips when I was in highschool. Our first trip was to Max Patch (when it was open for camping) and that really set the bar high. We quickly fell in love with the AT and the idea of completing it all the way through. Sooo…

In 2027 I will be thru-hiking the Appalachian Trail Northbound with my (new) fiancé, Nolin. Nolin recently proposed in Zion National Park during our Big Five Utah trip. Absolutely incredible. He is the first man I’ve met that actually wants to do the trail because he wants to do it. Granted, I introduced him to it, but he actually has his own motivations for completing it. If you follow my blog on trail, you’ll probably see and read a lot about him since we’re going together.

Speaking of the blog, I am excited to share my journey with family, friends and strangers through this platform. I have been in the outdoor recreation sphere for a really long time and I feel like I always end up back on The Trek’s site to get advice, information, etc. My dad and I did several hikes during my college years as well, and that is where I learned so much about gear and whatnot. Since then, a lot of things have changed and improved, so it’s been a whirlwin trying to get basically all new gear for a reasonable price. Reaching out to brands has been extremely helpful in that regard! More to come on that process in a future post.

Anyway, thanks for tuning into my first post as a 2027 Appalachian Trail Trek Blogger. I am so happy and honored to be here. Can’t wait to get started.
