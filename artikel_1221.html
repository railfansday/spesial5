Picture: Leaked Inter 2027-28 home shirt appears online

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Images of the projected Inter home shirt for the 2027-28 season have begun to emerge online on Thursday: Take a look below!

Kit experts Footy Headlines have shared images of the leaked Nerazzurri 2027-28 home kit, which featured larger vertical stripes compared to the current strip being worn in 2026-27.

The leaked shirt for 2027-28 features white detailing, as opposed to the gold sponsorship logos on the current home shirt. As is usually the case, the Inter home strip is set to feature black shorts and black socks.

The kits will be produced by Nike, who have partnered with the club since 1998. Their current agreement runs until 2031.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
