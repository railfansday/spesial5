2027 Grammy Nominations: Raye, Rosalía Lead a Women-Dominated General Field

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Raye earned 11 nominations and Rosalía got nine, while Olivia Rodrigo, Ella Langley and Phoebe Bridgers tied with seven each. Olivia Dean earned six.

Raye is the top nominee heading into the 2027 Grammy Awards — with Rosalía, Ella Langley, Olivia Rodrigo, Phoebe Bridgers and Olivia Dean not far behind — as women dominate the noms in the major categories for this year’s awards, revealed during the Grammys‘ livestream Monday morning.

Raye earned 11 nominations, including for record and song of the year with “Where is my Husband!,” and for album of the year for This Music May Contain Hope. Rosalía got nine nominations with her heavily lauded Lux, which also got an album of the year nod, while single “Berghain” is up for record and song of the year. The Grammys themselves will take place Feb. 7 at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, with this year’s ceremony airing live at 7 p.m. ET / 4 p.m. PT on the Disney-owned ABC and streaming on Disney+ and Hulu. The 2027 Grammys will be the first in more than 50 years not to air on the music awards show’s longtime home of CBS.

“It’s a privilege to congratulate all the incredible music creators recognized in this year’s Grammy nominations,” Harvey Mason Jr., Grammys CEO, said in a statement. “From first-time nominees to artists reaching career milestones, it’s inspiring to see musicians at every stage of their careers represented. Together, they reflect the extraordinary diversity of genres, crafts and backgrounds that make up our community. We look forward to a remarkable season of celebration, and I’m grateful to our voting members for their continued commitment to honoring the music that moves us year after year.”

Langley, Rodrigo, Bridgers and Miranda Lambert all tied for the third-most nominations this year with seven each. Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas” got record and song of the year nods, while her second album Dandelion is up for album of the year, and she’s also up for best new artist. (Lambert earned four of her nominations, including producer of the year, for her work with Langley on Dandelion and “Choosin’ Texas.”) Rodrigo’s You Seem Pretty Sad For a Girl So in Love is up for album of the year, while “The Cure” is up for record and song of the year.

Bridgers’ Lost Weekend earned an album of the year nomination, her second time nominated in the category after Boygenius’ The Record was nominated in 2024. Olivia Dean, last year’s best new artist winner, earned six nominations, with The Art of Loving up for album of the year while “Man I Need” is up for record and song of the year. Taylor Swift got five nominations, as The Life of a Showgirl is up for album of the year, while “The Fate of Ophelia” was nominated for record of the year and her Toy Story 5 track “I Knew It, I Knew You” is up for song of the year.

Bruno Mars is the lone male artist in this year’s album of the year race with The Romantic, his first solo album since 2016. The album’s lead single “I Just Might” nabbed a record of the year nomination, while “Risk It All” is up for song of the year.

In the the record of the year race, 2027 Coachella headliner Tame Impala rounds out the category with “Dracula,” his hit with K-Pop superstar Jennie. Meanwhile in the song of the year race, Ariana Grande took the last spot with “Hate That I Made You Love Me,” her first-ever nomination in the category and her first nomination in the general field for her solo work since Thank U, Next was nominated for album of the year back in 2020.

In the best new artist category, Langley, whose “Choosin’ Texas” broke the record for most-ever weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100, is joined by Sienna Spiro, Angine de Poitrine, Geese, Slayyyter, Don Toliver, Remi Wolf and the Womack Sisters.

For producer of the year, Lambert is up against Tony Berg, Noah Goldstein, Mike Sabath and Dernst “D’Mile” Emile. For songwriter of the year, Raye was nominated in the category for the second time in her career. She’s joined by Edgar Barrera, Joybeth Taylor, 2024 winner Theron Thomas and Amy Allen, who’s looking to win in the category for a third consecutive year.

Outside of the major categories, Leon Thomas, one of the top nominees of last year’s Grammy season, nabbed six more noms this year, all across the R&B categories. The pop and rock/alternative categories look particularly competitive, with Grande, Raye, Rodrigo, Dean and Mars all up for pop vocal album.

For best rock album, The Rolling Stones’ Foreign Tongues, is up against Jack White’s Frozen Charlotte, Angine de Poitrine’s Vol.II, Foo Fighters’ Your Favorite Toy and Muse’s The Wow! Signal. For alternative, Bridgers’ Lost Weekend and Rosalía’s Lux are joined by Geese’s Getting Killed, Gorillaz’s Mountain, Alabama Shakes’ I Must Be Dreaming and Charli XCX’s Music. Fashion. Film. (Charli has two albums up for Grammys this year, as her Wuthering Heights soundtrack album is up for best compilation soundtrack for visual media.)

Rap was underrepresented in the general field this year compared to last, when three rap albums made it to the album of the year race. Toliver is the lone hip-hop act in the general field this time.

As for what’s not included, BTS is the most notable holdout of the season, deciding not to submit amid the newly-introduced Asian pop music performance category, with the group’s members saying in statements that they hope “music can be heard and loved for what it is, rather than being divided by region or language.”

Meanwhile, Harry Styles was arguably the biggest snub of the Grammy season, as he and his latest album Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally were completely shut out this year. Madonna, whose Confessions II was widely regarded as her strongest work in decades, managed to net her four nominations across the pop and dance categories, but none in the general field. Noah Kahan’s The Great Divide, which garnered notable Grammy buzz heading into voting, had a lighter showing than expected too, getting a single nomination for best contemporary folk album.
