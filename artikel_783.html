Pregnant Brittany Mahomes Attends Chiefs Game After Sharing Baby News

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Brittany Mahomes made her first appearance since revealing she's pregnant with her and Patrick Mahomes' fourth baby by supporting her husband at the Kansas City Chiefs game Nov. 15,

Brittany Mahomes is entering her pregnancy season once again.

Five days after the wife of Kansas City Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes revealed she’s expecting the couple’s fourth child, she made her first public appearance since sharing the exciting news, stepping out to cheer on her husband in his NFL game against the Atlanta Falcons in Atlanta Nov. 15.

For the occasion, Brittany—who shares kids Sterling, 5, Bronze, 3, and Golden, 21 months, with Patrick—wore a Chiefs red jacket and oversized black sunglasses, styling her blond locks in a wavy ponytail.

And, of course, the 31-year-old brought her winning team to support Patrick on the field.

After all, Brittany shared fun videos of her kids enjoying some family fun before the Chiefs and the Falcons faced off.

In an adorable clip posted to her Instagram Stories Nov. 15, the Kansas City Current co-owner carried Golden through the stadium tunnel out onto the field, noting her daughter didn’t arrive empty-handed.

“This child literally walked into the stadium with [a] waffle in hand,” Brittany shared as her youngest ate her snack. “No waffle left behind!”

And just like her mom, the toddler arrived at the game in style, wearing a white jacket that featured her dad’s name and jersey number in red on her back, along with matching red ruffled leggings.

Golden matched with her older sister Sterling, who also sported Chiefs red leggings under a pink dress.

And as the Mahomes crew huddled up to watch Patrick score another win for the Chiefs, they are continuing to celebrate the exciting news of adding another player to their team.

On Nov. 10, Brittany and Patrick announced they’re expecting their fourth baby together.

“Another blessing from God,” the couple wrote in a joint Instagram post alongside sweet family photos, including a shot of their kids with their hands on Brittany’s baby bump. “& then there were 6.”

Though the pair—who tied the knot in 2022—haven’t shared whether they’re expecting a boy or girl, Patrick joked that he has one play he may prefer.

As the 31-year-old told reporters during a Nov. 11 press conference, “I’m hoping that me and Bronze can even up the score.”

But Patrick emphasized that all that matters is that his and Brittany’s new arrival is “healthy.”

“Obviously, it’s a blessing to have another baby on the way,” he shared. “I’m excited for it.”
