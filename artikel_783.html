Miss Universe Vietnam 2026 Stripped of Title After 2 Months

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

After Miss Universe Vietnam crowned Nguyen Quynh Anh its 2026 winner on Oct. 2, the beauty queen's title was revoked following a controversy that began with a leaked group chat.

Nguyen Quynh Anh's reign as Miss Universe Vietnam has officially come to an end.

The beauty queen was initially suspended in October when private messages speaking negatively about other pageant participants became public, and now the organizers have revoked her title due to Nguyen's conduct while under suspension.

The issues occurred while Nguyen was barred from performing in all "artistic performance activities" related to Miss Universe for six months, the show’s organizers, Five6 Entertainment, announced in a Nov. 18 statement, according to Tuoi Tre News.

In addition to being suspended from events for the group text—which included runner-up Trinh My Anh—containing derogatory remarks about past winners, fellow contestants and judges, the model was ordered to pay a fine, the outlet said.

However, when the influencer attended a community event while banned from representing the pageant, the production company, per Tuoi Tre News, said her appearance caused issues that "affected the image of Miss Universe Vietnam and its organizers."

In addition to promoting her title while suspended, the organizers stated that the 27-year-old delayed signing her management contract in order to prioritize a "mental health condition" amid the transitional period. Still, the team said they learned Nguyen signed an exclusive contract with a competitor management agency and "continued accepting performance engagements and attending events without reaching an agreement with Five6."

In response to the initial backlash, the competition's runner-up, Trinh, apologized on Instagram. Meanwhile, Nguyen released an Oct. 13 statement transcribed by the outlet where she apologized "to the women concerned and everyone affected by this incident, especially the audience."

"I understand that holding a beauty queen title does not make me incapable of making mistakes," her statement continued. "However, the title requires me to be more mindful and careful about my words, conduct and responsibilities, even in seemingly minor matters."

E! News has reached out to Nguyen Quynh Anh regarding the recent conduct allegations but has not yet heard back.

The release noted that her title was formally revoked after reviewing the conditions of holding the crown, the outlet said.

And given the unexpected exit, Vietnam will not send a competitor to the global Miss Universe finale on Nov. 24 in Puerto Rico.
