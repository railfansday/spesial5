Isak and Wirtz power Liverpool to easy victory at Club Brugge

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Club Brugge vs Liverpool. UEFA Champions League Round 1.

Jan BreydelstadionAttendance: Attendance27,286.

Report as Liverpool overpowered Club Brugge in Belgium with two goals from Alexander Isak and a fine strike from Florian Wirtz; Andoni Iraola's side rise to fifth in the Champions League phase standings, in prime position to finish inside the automatic qualifying spots

Liverpool recovered from their shock defeat to Fenerbahce in their last Champions League outing to beat Club Brugge 3-0 and improve their chances of a top-eight league phase finish.

Andoni Iraola's side have had no problem scoring goals in Europe, but faced issues keeping them out. Brugge were most obliging hosts in that respect, a far cry from the regularly free-scoring side that have so far averaged 2.75 goals per game and beaten Inter Milan at the San Siro.

Felix Lemarechal was the home side's greatest threat. But Alexander Isak was far more decisive, netting the opener following Yann Sommer's misplaced pass before rounding off the scoring in the second half with a deft chip to beat the goalkeeper. Sandwiched between was a superb strike from the in-form Florian Wirtz, set up by a surging run from Rio Ngumoha.

Brugge got better, Lemarechal twice forced Alisson into saves and Nicolo Tresoldi, who has scored the same number of Champions League goals as Erling Haaland this term, also called the Brazilian into action. But Liverpool were creatively superior. Momentum is building.

The only blemish of an otherwise perfect night for Andoni Iraola concerned an injury to Victor Munoz in the first half after clashing with Jorne Spileers. The coming together initially looked innocuous but the winger was unable to continue and immediately replaced by Rio Ngumoha. He walked off the pitch unaided despite the stretcher being called for.

Isak has now scored in six consecutive away outings for Liverpool while Wirtz continues to grow in stature and influence. The German has scored six times for club and country in the month of November alone. 12 points from five games gives the Reds a favourable chance of finishing inside the automatic qualifying positions, while Brugge drop to 13th - the first time they have tasted defeat in 11 games.

Florian Wirtz was, very briefly, the most expensive transfer in Premier League history when he joined Liverpool nearly 18 months ago. Only now is the German playmaker showing he was worth the record outlay.

His goal in Brugge marked a small but significant milestone, the first time he has scored in three consecutive appearances as a Liverpool player. It's been two years since the 23-year-old managed to find such consistency of output.

Wirtz's brilliance extended beyond his goal, linking play through the thirds and igniting attacks without relinquishing control out of possession. Finally, Iraola appears to have found a formula that brings the best out of the midfielder's qualities.

Here is a moment where the headline numbers have finally caught up with his growing influence.

Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola to TNT Sports:

"It was very positive. It puts us in a good position. We want to go to the top eight. We were efficient, a more difficult game than it looks now.

"It was difficult to expect a clean sheet, we expected an open game, man to man, so overall a very good performance. I appreciate a lot this win."

On Isak: "As a team, I value the first goal, but the second goal was also very nice."

Liverpool forward Alexander Isak to TNT Sports:

"I feel good, I'm in a good place, the team is in a good place most importantly.

"That's where we want to be, and hopefully it just gets better from here."
