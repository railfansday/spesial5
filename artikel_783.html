Antonio Sabato Jr. Asks for Privacy After Son Jack Arrested in Fatal Alaska Hit-and-Run

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The 32-year-old son of Sabato and Virginia Madsen is accused of striking and killing a pedestrian in Anchorage and driving away.

Jack Sabato, 32, was arrested Nov. 9, the same day 42-year-old pedestrian Carl Simmonds was struck and killed by a Chevrolet Tahoe. Investigators allege that Sabato hit Simmonds and kept driving. He was arraigned on Nov. 10 and now faces a felony charge of leaving the scene of an accident without assisting the injured and a misdemeanor charge of leaving the scene of an accident resulting in injury or death. According to Anchorage police, patrol officers and Anchorage Fire Department medics responded at about 5:48 a.m. on Nov. 9 to the intersection of Airport Heights Drive and Debarr Road on a report of an unresponsive man. Simmonds was pronounced dead at the scene. Police said he had been struck by a vehicle that then fled. Officers found the Tahoe later that day and arrested Sabato.

Charging documents say Sabato contacted police, saying that he’d hit an animal and didn’t stop to check because he was scared since he did not have insurance.

Sabato remains held at the Anchorage Correctional Complex.
