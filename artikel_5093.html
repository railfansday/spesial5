XRP has completed a short-term golden cross as the cryptocurrency stages a recovery during the Thanksgiving holiday period. The 50-period moving average has risen above the 200-period moving average, completing a golden cross on the 1-hour chart.

Risky assets tracked a rebound in tech equities, pushing crypto markets higher as they headed into a period of thin holiday trading. Global equities staged a tentative recovery following an AI-driven sell-off as investors wrapped up a turbulent, shortened trading week due to the Thanksgiving holiday.

A number of crypto assets were trading in the green; XRP was up 0.70% in the last 24 hours to $0.95. XRP began to rebound from a low of $0.888 on November 25, 2026, rising to a high of $0.985 early Friday as the recovery entered the third day.

If XRP's recovery is sustained and a decisive move above the $0.99 support level, now turned into resistance, is achieved, XRP might aim for a return to the $1 range. On the other hand, support is expected in the $0.86 to $0.88 range in the event of price drops.

Ripple's plan to bring bank-style lending to the XRP Ledger is about to materialize as the XRP lending protocol amendment has reached a majority, surpassing 80% validator support.

The lending protocol allows on-chain fixed-term, uncollateralized loans from pooled funds in a Single Asset Vault.

Lending would give stablecoin holders another way to earn a return on the network. The setup includes Vaults, which are pools where lenders park one asset, like XRP or a stablecoin and receive share tokens representing their share.

The 'SingleAssetVault' amendment sets the stage for multiple depositors to pool assets. This amendment is intended to be used with the on-chain Lending Protocol. Also, the SingleAssetVault and LendingProtocolV1_1 amendments have received majority support and are entering the two-week window prior to mainnet activation.
