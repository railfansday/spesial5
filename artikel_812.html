Liverpool fan found not guilty of racial abuse after Antoine Semenyo withdraws from case

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Man accused of abusing former Bournemouth winger

Alleged incident happened at Anfield game in 2025

A Liverpool fan accused of racially abusing Antoine Semenyo has been found not guilty after the Manchester City forward withdrew his support for the prosecution.

Semenyo was due to give evidence at Liverpool magistrates court on Friday in the trial of Mark Mogan, 47, who was accused of shouting abuse during the Premier League match between Liverpool and Semenyo’s former club Bournemouth at Anfield on 15 August last year.

But Simon Jones, prosecuting, said: “Following recent contact made with Mr Semenyo, he has indicated he is no longer supportive of proceedings and the prosecution do not consider it is appropriate to compel him. In those circumstances we offer no further evidence in the case.”

District judge Mary Dowrick recorded a verdict of not guilty to the charge of racially aggravated behaviour.

Semenyo, who left Bournemouth for Manchester City in January, had been due to give evidence last month but the trial was adjourned halfway through when Mogan was taken to hospital after a seizure.

The court had heard Mogan, in a wheelchair, shouted racial abuse at Semenyo as he went to take a throw-in about 30 minutes into the game. He was alleged to have said: “Hurry up and take the throw-in, you black bastard.”

Opening the case last month, Mr Jones said: “That racial abuse was shouted directly at Antoine Semenyo, who was close to him. Mr Semenyo briefly froze, as he couldn’t believe what the male had said.”

Mogan then shouted: “I’m talking to you, you cunt,” the court heard. Witnesses also said they saw him throw his chewing gum at the footballer.

Semenyo reported the incident to the referee and Mogan was removed from the stadium at half-time. Mogan, who had an intermediary with him in court, told police in interview that Semenyo had called him a cripple after he shouted at the player to “hurry up”.

Mogan declined to comment as he left court.
