Cranberry Pistachio Shortbread Cookies

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Cranberries and pistachios are a classic winter pairing—and not just for their red-and-green Christmas hue. Sweet, tart cranberries balance the nutty richness from crunchy pistachios, and the flavor pairing shines with the buttery shortbread backdrop.

In a large bowl, cream the butter and confectioners’ sugar until they’re blended. Beat in the vanilla and almond extracts until combined.

In another bowl, whisk together the all-purpose flour, baking powder and salt. Gradually beat the flour mixture into the creamed mixture just until no flour streaks remain. Fold in the dried cranberries and chopped pistachios.

Editor’s Tip: Use a hand mixer or stand mixer to make creaming the butter and sugar easier.
