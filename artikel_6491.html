Loaded Mashed Potatoes

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Mashed potatoes are a classic side dish that can make almost any meal better. They’re creamy, fluffy, and nearly perfect…nearly. When thinking of how to improve on a dish already so close to perfection, I decided to go back to the basics. What’s the best kind of potato? A loaded baked potato. With this in mind, I combined the two superstar spuds into one glorious dish. In it, light, airy mashed potatoes are topped with all of the classic baked potato fillings—cheddar cheese, chives, bacon, and sour cream—for a dish that truly embodies the best of both worlds.

The beauty of these potatoes is that they can go with any number of dinners. Serve them alongside your favorite protein—chicken, steak, pork chops, the world is your oyster—or alongside a few lighter sides like any of our green salads, caramelized Brussels sprouts, or roasted green beans.

Any leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the fridge for 3 to 4 days. If you haven’t already added the toppings, store them separately. Whatever you do, don’t let leftovers go to waste. We’ve got plenty of leftover mashed potato recipes to make sure you get the most out of the special spuds.
