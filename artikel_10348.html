We Got A Grand Theft At Disney World Before GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The "We got X before GTA 6" meme continues as a Grand Theft has been reported at Walt Disney World's Epcot theme park just days before Rockstar's open-world crime game releases, and the meme comes to an end.

The Walt Disney World: Active Calls account, which tracks police reports in the Disney World area, said a Grand Theft call at Epcot came in at 9:52 PM on November 14.

There are no further details to go on here, but Grand Theft, known as Grand Larceny in some states, involves stealing something valuable. Epcot and Disney in general has lots of items that would be considered valuable.

This is the closest that Disney World might come to GTA 6, as Rockstar already confirmed that GTA 6 won't have its own version of the park. That makes sense, too, considering Walt Disney World is located in Orlando and GTA 6 takes place in its own version of Miami, which is more than 200 miles away.

Calls for Grand Theft are not uncommon at Disney World. In October 2026 alone, the County Sheriff's Office received at least seven Grand Theft calls.

One of the most infamous heists at Walt Disney World took place at Epcot. In 2018, thieves nabbed the multi-hundred-pound Buzzy animatronic from the Cranium Command attraction, which was housed at Epcot's shuttered Wonders of Life pavilion.

It has never been recovered. A documentary, Stolen Kingdom, was released in 2025, chronicling the heist and the wider black market for Disney props.

More recently, the Paddlefish restaurant at the Disney World entertainment district, Disney Springs, was robbed by someone who swam up to the waterside restaurant in frogman gear, forced two workers into a corner, obscured the security camera with foam, and made off with cash. This case has not been solved.

In other Disney news, the entertainment giant is teasing "major" announcements for The Game Awards next month. As for GTA 6, the game finally releases on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

The "We got X before GTA 6" memes have been among the most popular and hilarious over the years as fans waited and waited for the game to release. Even Take-Two's own CEO, Strauss Zelnick, is aware of the memes.
