1alt HD: Here’s Why NEAR’s Higher-Timeframe Bullish Structure Holds Firm Despite 50% Drop From October Peak

NEAR Protocol’s [NEAR] token rallied by 7.4% in the last 24 hours, making it one of the biggest winners among the top-20 crypto assets by market capitalization. The altcoin’s daily trading volume hiked by nearly 45% too.

This boded well for the altcoin’s bulls, especially as NEAR reacted from the key $2.81-weekly support and rebounded higher.

As with most other altcoins, the question with NEAR is — Can the buyers convert the short-term momentum into something lasting? Or will the uncertainty around Bitcoin [BTC] and Ethereum [ETH] snuff out any bullish hopes for NEAR?

From August to October, NEAR rallied by 290%, from $1.53 to $6. Surging cross-chain swap volume on NEAR Intents, a cut in the maximum annual token issuance from 2.6% to 1.5%, and the confidential perpetual futures roll-out helped boost bullish sentiment.

The subsequent price rally was met with short liquidations, driving further gains.

After hitting its $6-high, the altcoin underwent a 50% correction and sunk to $3 once again.

The 3-day timeframe’s price structure was bullish at press time. The altcoin’s price approached the 78.6% Fibonacci retracement level at $2.49, climbing to $3.04 at the time of writing.

Now, although the RSI was below neutral-50, the OBV has held up well in recent weeks’ selling. This may be a sign that after the 50% correction, buyers might be able to push the trend back in their favor.

On the contrary, the 4-hour price structure was bearish. A trading session close above $3.28 is needed to flip the internal structure bullishly. A sustained move above $3.47 is required to confirm this timeframe’s bullish swing structure shift.

And yet, the first bullish sign has already occurred. The imbalance (white box) at $2.75 has been respected on the charts, kickstarting the gains made in the last 24 hours.

Given that this is a good start, bulls can keep an eye on NEAR token‘s progress. $3.47 will be the pivotal level that would signal a credible bullish trend shift.
