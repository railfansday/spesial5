Zcash holders just got a new update as the major crypto exchange Gemini has announced support for Shielded Zcash deposits.

Scott Merkel, 'Wolf of All Streets,' shared this update in an X post, highlighting the milestone as the first of its kind for any major exchange. According to Merkel, Gemini now lets customers deposit Zcash (ZEC) to shielded addresses, saying it is the first major exchange with both shielded deposits and withdrawals.

Gemini co-founder Tyler Winklevoss shared the milestone in a recent X post, adding that the recent move builds on the launch of shielded Zcash withdrawals on Gemini this summer.

In July 2026, shortly after the Ironwood upgrade was activated, Gemini launched support for shielded Zcash withdrawals to the new Ironwood pool, allowing users to send Zcash from Gemini to their Ironwood deposit address. Ironwood (NU6.3) went live on July 28, 2026, at block height 3,428,143, bringing a new shielded pool for Zcash.

With the recent launch of support for Shielded Zcash deposits, Gemini users can make Shielded Zcash deposits and withdrawals to and from Ironwood, with Winklevoss remarking that the private movement of money was now complete.

Zcash's total shielded supply now sits at around 4.93 million ZEC (around 28.94% of total ZEC supply) as of November 25, 2026.

November has brought in key updates for the Zcash network. Zcash's NU7 upgrade went live earlier in November 2026, slashing ZEC's target block time to 25 seconds from 75 seconds. The new 25-second block target lets miners add a batch of transactions to the chain three times as often.

None of this prints extra ZEC. Each block pays a third as much and halvings come every 5,040,000 blocks instead of 1,680,000, so the issuance schedule holds at roughly four years per halving.

Days after the NU7 upgrade, the Zcash Foundation followed up with the release of Zebra 7.1.0, which fixed ten security advisories, most of which closed off ways in which another node on the network (a peer) could stop a Zebra node from catching up with the chain or make it run out of memory. It also added sync and memory diagnostics, automatic peer recovery, and clearer errors.
