SWIFT Weighs XRP & ALGO Benefits vs. Quantum Threats

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Home » Ripple (XRP) » SWIFT Weighs XRP & ALGO Benefits vs. Quantum Threats

PQC algorithms deliver the same core functions like regular cryptography, but absorb the quantum-computing hazards.

SWIFT, the largest European financial conglomerate, has just released their latest perspective on quantum computing threats, a topic the crypto community, including the XRP Army, has eagerly debated over the past few months.

The financial community is uniting towards building the post-quantum cryptography (PQC) service, which would replace the algorithms prone to breaking by quantum computing. This aligns with the roadmap of several DLT-based altcoins, including Ripple (XRP) & Algorand (ALGO).

Both of these Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) based chains are preparing for signatures that erases those threats, potentially fully implementing it by 2028. “Today’s cryptography is deeply embedded across systems, applications & processes”, – the paper states, carving out a transitional path.

Transitioning to new standards requires SWIFT compliant organisations to understand where they are exposed and manage change in a controlled, resilient way. Blockchain adoption comes as a no-brainer: SWIFT’s new ledger acts as a plug between multiple popular chains, including Ripple’s XRP.

SWIFT’s statement urges companies not to wait for complete inventory of cryptographic assets, but rather prioritise their most critical and exposed systems, beginning the PQC transition. That’s where Algorand (ALGO) & Ripple’s XRP Ledger stands out from the regular crypto crowd. Algorand’s is leading the pack.

The chain’s workable quantum-proof mechanics are being gradually implemented from 2022, targeting broad quantum resilience by the last quarter of 2027. This includes more account features, multisig & solid research into a post-quantum VRF and consensus messaging. First Falcon-protected transfers hit the mainnet in 2025.

It already protects historical state and offers native quantum-resistant accounts that people can use today. On XRP’s part, the Ledger sports a well-documented, institution-oriented plan with a practical migration path (keeping the same address, dropping the old key) with a mid 2028 goal. However, that’s still pre-development aspirations.

Algorand’s better matched with SWIFT POC aspirations for now. XRP currently uses classical elliptic-curve signatures, known as secp256k1 / Ed25519. No post-quantum signatures are live on the Ledger’s mainnet. The high-standard approach is going to be tested in 2027, while SWIFT is already piloting a roster of Ripple-linked banks.

Santander, Standard Chartered, HSBC & Deutsche Bank are the highlights of a double-digit list, but that doesn’t necessarily mean XRP coin is used as collateral or settlement. The chain’s On-Demand Liquidity (ODL) provides an additional liquidity layer via XRP, but it’s optional.

The bigger question is whether XRP can become a standard settlement layer on cross-border corridors.
