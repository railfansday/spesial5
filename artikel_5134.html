Kraken says trades stand after Bitcoin sold at $24,250 Canadian

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Kraken said heavy selling drove its Canadian-dollar markets below prices elsewhere. Its Bitcoin pair climbed back above C$80,000 by early afternoon.

Bitcoin hit C$24,250 on Kraken’s Canadian-dollar market on Saturday, Nov. 21. The next 15-minute trading interval came within C$1.40 of that low. Kraken said executed trades would stand after heavy selling pushed its Canadian-dollar pairs below prices elsewhere.

The C$24,250 low was about 71% below the Canadian-dollar conversion on CryptoSlate’s Bitcoin price page at 09:40 UTC. The comparison puts an earlier Kraken trade alongside a later reference value. CryptoSlate displayed a price of US$57,809 and a converted value of C$82,708 at that check.

Kraken’s Canadian-dollar pair had already reached C$61,880 during the 15 minutes starting at 21:00 UTC on Friday. The trading intervals beginning at 06:00 and 07:00 UTC Saturday reached lows of C$50,000 and C$33,750, respectively.

The interval starting at 08:00 UTC opened at C$32,000, ranged from C$24,250 to C$49,999 and ended back at C$32,000. Kraken recorded 976 trades totaling about 22.38 BTC across those 15 minutes. How much Bitcoin changed hands at the lowest price remains unclear.

The next interval reached C$24,251.40 before closing at C$37,000. Kraken’s 09:40 UTC ticker still showed C$24,250 as the 24-hour low. The best bid was C$40,900 and the best ask was C$42,557. Kraken reported a high of C$88,964 and volume of about 658.51 BTC for the rolling 24-hour window. The bid and ask show where buyers and sellers were quoting at the time of the check.

In the 09:40 UTC ticker, Kraken’s USD/CAD last price was C$0.7304 per U.S. dollar. Multiplying the exchange’s BTC/USD last price of about US$57,796 by that rate gives about C$42,214 per Bitcoin. That was close to C$42,557, the last price Kraken reported for its BTC/CAD pair in the same response.

The comparison shows how different the prices looked using Kraken’s own currency markets. A U.S. dollar traded for fewer Canadian dollars on Kraken, so using Kraken’s rate in place of the broader market conversion brought the implied Bitcoin price much closer to the direct CAD trade. The last trades in the three markets may have occurred at different times. The calculation gives an indicative price, and any actual order would depend on the price and quantity available at execution.

The low prices extended beyond Bitcoin. Kraken’s ticker at 09:40 UTC showed a 24-hour low of 30 Canadian cents per U.S. dollar. Over the same window, USDC fell as low as C$0.49 and USDT reached C$0.0022. Ether and Solana reached lows of C$796 and C$32 on their respective CAD pairs.

Traders watching the CAD pair reported different prices as it moved. At 06:21 UTC, the X account @grebby described an equivalent of about US$39,000 and said a deposit attempt failed. In a 07:15 UTC update, @grebby put the equivalent at US$27,000 and said they had bought and withdrawn Bitcoin. The purchase, execution price and withdrawal remain unverified.

At 08:19 UTC, @alistairmilne described a price of about C$30,000.

As of 09:40 UTC, Kraken’s status page listed no CAD spot-market incident. The incident log showed scheduled U.S. futures maintenance ending at 02:03 UTC, and a separate Banking Circle notice concerned euro and sterling transfers.

Kraken Support said heavy selling pushed its CAD pairs below other markets from around 20:00 UTC on Nov. 20. It said orders were processed correctly, its security team found no sign of a breach or compromised accounts, and executed trades would stand. At 13:06 UTC, the last BTC/CAD trade in Kraken’s ticker was C$82,099.
