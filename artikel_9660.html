Running Autonomous Pentest Agents for About $1 (What Our Benchmarks Taught Us)

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

When an AI agent runs for 200+ turns, you are not paying for fresh prompts. You are paying to re-read the same growing conversation over and over.

We hit this lesson twice: first in our ARC-AGI-3 agent benchmark (FL-2026-006), and again running real open-source pentest agents against isolated lab apps on Nodecode Cyber v1.

Here is the simple takeaway and the real numbers.
