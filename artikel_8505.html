Hiker Who Was Reported Missing on the AT Found Safe

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

An Appalachian Trail hiker reported missing yesterday was found safe, the Washington County, VA Sheriff’s Office confirmed to The Trek around noon on Friday.

Family members had reported the hiker, whose name is Shay, missing near Damascus, VA after she failed to check in and reportedly missed a planned resupply stop. A multi-agency search effort ultimately found her unharmed. According to the Sheriff’s Office dispatch, she told searchers she did not need assistance. The hiker’s family also separately announced that she had been located.

The confirmation follows several conflicting reports circulating on social media, including premature claims that the hiker had been found that were subsequently disputed by the family earlier on Friday.

No additional details are available at this time.
