Put your holiday shopping on the card that pays the most at the store where you’ll shop most, such as the Prime Visa at Amazon for Prime members or the Target Circle Card at Target. Then pay the January statement in full, because a few months of interest cost more than the rewards earn.

Banks charged an average interest rate of 22.36% in August on card accounts that paid interest, the Federal Reserve reports. That works out to about 1.9% of the balance for each month it’s carried, more than the 1% to 1.5% most of these cards pay outside their bonus categories. Three months of interest at that rate, about 5.6% of the balance, cost more than even a 5% bonus earns. Paying in full is also what makes a store card’s discount worth taking.

Each card here pays extra where holiday spending goes, and some add purchase protection, which covers a new purchase against theft or damage for 90 to 120 days. The Discover it Cash Back’s rotating 5% categories this quarter are restaurants, entertainment, and utilities, so this year it pays 5% on dinners and outings, not gifts.

Prime members who buy gifts on Amazon earn 5% on them, and purchase protection covers each new item up to $500 against theft or damage for 120 days (90 in New York).

For more information, read our full Prime Visa Review.

Target shoppers get 5% off Black Friday sale prices in stores and online, and restaurant and travel gift cards bought there as presents get the 5% too, though Target GiftCards don’t.

For more information, read our full Target Circle™ Credit Card Review.

Walmart+ members earn 5% at Walmart in stores and online, and new cardholders who apply and are approved by December 31, 2026, earn 3% outside Walmart for their first 90 days.

For more information, read our full OnePay CashRewards World Mastercard® Review.

Department store and electronics shoppers who enroll in those two categories this quarter earn 5% on their first $2,000 combined, at stores such as Macy’s, Kohl’s, and Best Buy.

For more information, read our full U.S. Bank Cash+® Visa Signature® Card Review.

Shoppers buying gifts from U.S. online retailers earn 3% on up to $6,000 a year, and purchase protection covers each eligible purchase up to $1,000 for 90 days.

For more information, read our full Blue Cash Everyday® Card from American Express Review.

Online shoppers can pick online shopping as its 3% category for November and December and switch back in January. New cardholders earn 6% on it the first year.

For more information, read our full Bank of America® Customized Cash Rewards Credit Card Review.

Holiday hosts earn 5% on up to $1,500 at grocery stores (not Walmart or Target) and on dining from Oct. 1 through Dec. 31, once they activate by Dec. 14.

For more information, read our full Chase Freedom Flex® Review.

Holiday rewards come down to matching one card to the store that will get most of your holiday budget. Use it from Black Friday on, and set autopay for the full statement balance so January’s bill carries no interest.
