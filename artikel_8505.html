To save on groceries, start with your store’s own app if it has digital coupons, which cost nothing and load in a few taps before each trip. Then add Ibotta for the name brands you buy, and Flipp if you shop more than one store’s sales. Stop there, since every extra app asks for its own taps each trip.

Grocery prices rose 1.8% over the 12 months through September 2026, the Bureau of Labor Statistics’ Consumer Price Index shows. Its Consumer Expenditure Survey puts the average household’s grocery spending at $6,491 for 2025, which works out to about $540 a month. The apps here cut that bill in different ways, and we weighed each by what it saves, how many taps it asks of you, and how soon the savings reach you.

Receipt scanning apps that pay points on any receipt you photograph, whatever you bought, are a separate kind from these.

Each app’s digital coupons work only at its own company’s stores, and they come off at checkout once you load them to your account and enter your phone number or scan your card.

Buy the average household’s $540 a month of groceries at Kroger or one of its other chains, and the points come to about $5 off groceries or $7.50 off a 15-gallon fill-up, by our math.

For more information, read our full Kroger Review.

If you buy most groceries at Safeway, Albertsons, Vons, or a sister chain, the points from the average household’s $540 a month reach $15 off groceries about every two months, by our math.

For more information, read our full Safeway forU Review.

Add its offers before each trip, and it pays cash back at more than 2,600 retailers. Link your Walmart account and pay with the card saved there, and Walmart trips need no receipt.

For more information, read our full Ibotta Review.

Shoppers who buy name brands at more than one store can photograph the receipt to claim its offers wherever they bought the item, unless an offer names a store.

For more information, read our full Checkout 51 Review.

Anyone who shops two or more stores’ sales can find an item in nearby stores’ weekly ads with one search, then buy it wherever the sale price is lowest.

For more information, read our full Flipp Review.

Upside says its gas offers average 11 cents a gallon, so a 15-gallon fill-up earns about $1.65 by our math, on top of your card’s rewards, if you claim the offer before you pump.

For more information, read our full Upside Review.

Cooks who’ll use or freeze food before it expires can choose produce and meat at up to 50% off, then pay in the app and pick it up at the grocery store.

For more information, read our full Flashfood Review.

A surprise bag of a store’s or restaurant’s unsold food costs half price or less, a deal if you’ll cook with whatever’s inside and can pick it up at the time the store sets.

For more information, read our full Too Good To Go Review.

Your store’s app earns points on nearly everything you buy there, while each of the others saves only on the items or fill-ups it covers. Set up your store’s app first, then add the one or two others that fit how you already shop.
