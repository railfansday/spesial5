Exec: Time for Banks to Reclaim Mortgage Biz From Non-Bank Lenders

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Mortgage rates recently reached 7.51% on the 30-year fixed, almost a full percentage point higher than last year. Despite that, the CEO of Chase Home Lending plans to aggressively expand its mortgage business to reclaim market share from non-bank lenders.

In a recent report on CNBC, Sean Grzebin noted that while banks had to recover consumer confidence following the great financial crisis of 20-some years ago, he thinks that’s a done deal.

“The non-banks are all very formidable competitors, and I think they’ve had an advantage while the banks were distracted through several decades,” he said. “Now you’re starting to see the resurgence of banks. A lot of banks are actually going to grow their mortgage business through this.”

Some of the major non-bank lenders in the U.S. include Rocket Mortgage, United Wholesale Mortgage (UWM), PennyMac Loan Services, loanDepot, Better Mortgage, to name a few.

Non-banks currently dominate originations with a 66.3% share by dollar volume, up from just 27% before the 2008 financial crisis, according to CNBC. However, large banks with $50 billion or more in assets are now increasing originations by 34% annually, outperforming competitors.

“We’re going to hire a bunch of lending advisors across the United States,” said Grzebin. “We’re going to focus on areas where we haven’t had a lot of coverage, but there’s a lot of mortgage activity and transactions taking place. And it really matches the expansion that we’re having within the branch network on the consumer bank side.”

Non-banks had a 66.3% share of originations by dollar volume in the first half of this year, an increase over all of 2025, according to Inside Mortgage Finance. That compares with just a 27% share in 2000 before the financial crisis. But large banks, those with $50 billion and more in assets, are now increasing originations by 34% on an annual basis, outperforming both competing banks and non-banks.”

Michael Catarevas is a senior editor for RISMedia.
