China's economy stumbles as retail sales, factory output, and investment all miss

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Retail sales rose 0.4% in October and industrial output 4.8%, while fixed-asset investment fell 8.1% in the first 10 months

China's National Bureau of Statistics released October data on Monday showing retail sales up 0.4% year on year, industrial output up 4.8%, and fixed-asset investment down 8.1% over the January-to-October stretch.

Each reading missed analyst forecasts, according to CNBC, which put expectations at 0.8% growth for retail sales, 5.3% growth for industrial output and a 7% decline for investment.

The bureau said investment in real estate development fell 21.6% in the first 10 months. Infrastructure investment declined 5.1%, manufacturing investment fell 2.9% and private investment dropped 11.4%. The value of newly built commercial building sales fell 14.3%. Looking at October by itself, investment was down 17% against the same month a year earlier, according to Bloomberg.

Retail sales of consumer goods in urban areas rose 0.2% in October, while rural sales rose 1.5%. Exports of goods rose 28.7% in yuan terms. The urban surveyed unemployment rate was 5.1%, down 0.1 percentage point from September. Consumer prices rose 0.7% from a year earlier, and producer prices rose 5.7%.

The bureau described the economy as "generally stable." It also said the domestic imbalance between strong supply and weak demand is acute and that some enterprises face operating difficulties.

“We’re still in the waiting period for the stimulus impact to kick in,” Helen Qiao, chief economist for greater China at BofA Global Research, told Bloomberg Television.

Beijing announced mortgage subsidies and targeted rate cuts in late September. The economy grew 4.5% in the third quarter from a year earlier. September retail sales rose 1.1% and industrial output grew 5.5%. Fixed-asset investment had contracted 7.2% through September, with real estate development investment down 20.6%.
