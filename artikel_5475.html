Exclusive: ICE Mortgage Technology appoints AI strategy, product leaders

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

ICE Mortgage Technology appointed Kiran Sahota as vice president of AI strategy and Leslie Peeler as vice president of product.  AI Summary

ICE Mortgage Technology has appointed Kiran Sahota as vice president of AI strategy and Leslie Peeler as vice president of product, the company announced Thursday.

The two executives will help guide how ICE applies artificial intelligence, automation and other technology across its mortgage platforms, including origination, servicing, and data and analytics. The leadership moves come as lenders and servicers face persistent pressure to reduce costs while managing rising compliance and performance requirements.

“Kiran and Leslie have been at the forefront of major technology and operational change in our industry,” Bob Hart, president of ICE Mortgage Technology, said in a statement. “They have applied emerging technologies to some of the toughest challenges facing lenders and servicers, and their expertise will be instrumental as we continue investing in technology that helps our customers succeed.”Peeler joins ICE from mortgage subservicer Cenlar, where she most recently served as executive vice president and chief operating officer. Cenlar is in the process of being acquired by Pennymac. Her background also includes senior leadership roles at IBM and a stint as senior vice president of credit portfolio management at Fannie Mae.

At ICE, Peeler will oversee servicing product development, including the MSP loan servicing system.

“Mortgage servicing is no longer just about administering loans. It’s about helping clients adapt to changing market conditions, operate more intelligently and build stronger relationships with homeowners,” Peeler said.Sahota will lead the application of AI and intelligent automation across ICE’s mortgage technology business. Her role will focus on identifying high-impact use cases where AI can address operational and customer experience challenges for lenders and servicers.

She previously served as chief information officer at Freedom Mortgage and later as president of Xpanse, a mortgage technology company launched by the founders of Freedom. Earlier in her career, Sahota held vice president roles at Rocket Mortgage and Rock Ventures.

“Having worked on the lender and servicer side has given me a deep understanding of the challenges they face every day,” Sahota said. “The question shouldn’t be where we can add AI, but what problems matter most and whether AI is the best way to solve them.”
