McDonald's is testing new value bundles as U.S. restaurant traffic slips

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

U.S. comparable sales fell 0.2% in the third quarter as the chain tests two formats for a rollout set for early 2027

McDonald's $MCD is testing two new value menu formats at U.S. restaurants, CEO Chris Kempczinski said on the company's earnings call Wednesday.

The company reported third-quarter results on Wednesday showing U.S. comparable sales fell 0.2%, as negative comparable guest counts were mostly offset by positive check growth. Global comparable sales rose 1.5%. McDonald's stock finished Wednesday up by over 6%.

One format uses mix-and-match combinations, and the other uses fixed bundles of set items, according to MarketWatch. The eight-week test runs in about 1,900 locations spread over a few U.S. regions. The format that ends up winning over both franchisees and shoppers will go national in early 2027, according to CNBC.

"In the U.S., we're encouraged by improvement as the quarter progressed following our intervention on value," Kempczinski said in a statement.

Chief Financial Officer Ian Borden said on the call that same-store sales rebounded in September, having declined through July and August, according to CNBC. Borden added that he had not claimed the fourth quarter would bring better U.S. same-store sales.

To address the decline, McDonald's put its marketing weight behind Extra Value Meals and revived promotions, both regional and nationwide, in its mobile app. Promotions have also featured breakfast sandwiches at $2 and complimentary fries for purchases of at least $1, according to Reuters.

According to Reuters, citing Placer.ai data, the number of customers visiting McDonald's restaurants dropped 4.6% in the third quarter compared with a year before. This was the first drop in U.S. comparable sales following a five-quarter run of increases.

Franchisee participation has been an issue. Executives said in August that a third of franchisees did not take part in the under-$3 menu, which hurt its execution. Kempczinski said the corporate side and U.S. franchisees are "in a good spot with the exception of this entry-level value."

The test follows a second quarter in which same-store sales grew 0.8% as guest counts declined. McDonald's outlined an $8.5 billion franchisee support package at an investor day in September, and U.S. executives acknowledged falling short on consistent execution. Skye Anderson became president of McDonald's USA in August.

Over the past two years, McDonald's has introduced a menu of items priced under $3 and a $4 breakfast meal.
