Old foes Taylor and Van Barneveld to reunite for Ally Pally charity pro-celeb

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Phil Taylor and Raymond van Barneveld set to collide almost 20 years since their iconic world championship final; you can watch the Grand Slam of Darts in Wolverhampton live on Sky Sports up until the final on November 22

Phil Taylor and Raymond van Barneveld will renew their iconic rivalry as non-playing captains at a special pro-celeb event at Alexandra Palace next month.

Two of darts' greatest names will lead teams featuring world champions and celebrity guests on Thursday December 10, with £5 from every ticket sold donated to Prostate Cancer UK.

Taylor and Van Barneveld famously contested the 2007 final at the Circus Tavern in Purfleet, one of the sport's most memorable matches.

Almost 20 years on, they will bring that competitive spirit to the captaincy as their teams battle for the Rivals Reunited trophy.

The evening will serve as a test event ahead of the World's move into Alexandra Palace's larger Great Hall, giving fans an early opportunity to experience the new setting before the venue hosts the tournament for the 20th time.

Phil Taylor said: "Raymond and I have had some great battles over the years, and the competitive instinct never goes away. It should be a good laugh, but I'll still be making sure my team know we're there to win!"

Raymond van Barneveld added: "Phil says it's a bit of fun, but I know what he's like - he'll want to win just as much as I do! It will be great to be back at Ally Pally together, and hopefully my team can give the fans plenty to cheer about."

Two-time world champion Adrian Lewis, radio presenter Jordan North and social media personality Woody, will feature for Team Taylor.

Team Barney will include three-time world champion John Part, radio presenter Chris Stark and comedian Lloyd Griffith.

Sky Sports remains the home of darts with the Grand Slam of Darts (November 14-22) and World Darts Championship (December 11-January 3) all live. Stream darts and more through NOW with no contract.
