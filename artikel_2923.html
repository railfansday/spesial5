Ime Udoka calls Rockets 'mentally fragile' after loss to Nuggets

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

After a 7-1 start to the season, the Rockets have gone 5-4 — including a loss to Denver on Friday night — with the 13th-ranked defense in the NBA.

After that loss to the Nuggets, Rockets coach Ime Udoka called his team "mentally fragile" and had more to say (quote via Varun Shankar of the Houston Chronicle).

"There's no effort or any attention to detail on the defensive end at all.... They had four 30-point quarters... you're not going to win anything and that just shows a lack of effort, attention to detail or fight on that end. And so, doesn't really matter what we did offensively. Play that poorly, you're not going to win any games."

It seems a little early in the season to call your team soft, but Udoka is an old-school, defense-first, outwork-your-opponent kind of coach, and Houston's effort lately is clearly frustrating him. Also frustrating him was the fact that Nikola Jokic scored 24 points in the second half (although he has done that to a lot of teams).

Things are not dire in Houston, the Rockets had won 4-of-5 before Friday night and still sit as the No. 4 seed in the West with the second-best offense in the NBA. It's a long regular season where the Rockets can work out their defense and get it on point, this is a team that can make a deep run in the West. Which is why Udoka expects more from them than he has seen of late.
