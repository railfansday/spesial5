DICK'S Sporting Goods vs. Etsy: Which Consumer Stock Is a Better Buy for 2027?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

DICK'S trades at a fraction of Etsy's sales multiple, but the real question is whether its Nike dependence and Foot Locker integration are worth the discount.

By Jake Lerch – Updated Nov 19, 2026 at 9:55AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Dick's Sporting Goods ›

As physical stores stage a comeback against the digital wave, investors are weighing the stability of retail giants against specialized e-commerce platforms. Choosing between DICK'S Sporting Goods (DKS -2.45%) and Etsy (ETSY -2.11%) requires evaluating two distinct growth paths.

DICK'S Sporting Goods is a leading destination for athletes and sneaker fans, with a massive physical and digital footprint. In contrast, Etsy operates a global marketplace for unique, creative goods, connecting millions of independent entrepreneurs with buyers. This comparison highlights the difference between a traditional retail powerhouse and a high-margin digital platform.

DICK'S Sporting Goods focuses on being an omnichannel leader among retail stocks by offering apparel, footwear, and equipment through its namesake stores and expanding banners such as Foot Locker. The company serves a wide range of athletes and sneaker enthusiasts across North America, Europe, and Asia, aiming to provide a premium shopping experience both in-store and online. A significant partnership with Nike (NKE -2.81%) accounts for nearly 31% of merchandise purchases, and customer concentration like this adds a layer of risk to the business.

In the 12 months ended Aug. 1, 2026, revenue reached nearly $21.1 billion, representing a growth rate of approximately 53.5% over the previous 12-month period. This significant expansion helped the company achieve a net income of close to $838.8 million during the same time frame. The substantial increase in revenue reflects the ongoing integration of new retail locations and strong consumer demand for high-performance athletic gear.

As of its January 2026 balance sheet, the current ratio is approximately 1.5x, indicating the business's ability to cover its short-term debts with assets that can be quickly converted to cash. The debt-to-equity ratio is roughly 1.4x, which compares total debt to shareholders' equity. In the fiscal year ended Jan. 31, 2026, free cash flow was nearly $400.2 million, providing the company with capital to reinvest in its growing store base.

Etsy operates a unique marketplace model focused on creative, personalized items that are often difficult to find in big-box retail stores. The platform connects roughly 5.6 million active sellers with about 86.5 million buyers globally, creating a vibrant ecosystem for independent entrepreneurs. Most of these sellers are small-scale businesses run by one person working from home, helping the company maintain an asset-light profile while serving a massive global audience.

In the 12 months ended Sept. 30, 2026, revenue reached approximately $2.9 billion, representing a growth of roughly 2.1% compared to the prior twelve months. Net income for this period was close to $987.5 million, reflecting the efficiency of its marketplace-driven business model. While revenue growth has been modest, the company continues to maintain significant profitability by providing essential infrastructure for millions of creative entrepreneurs to sell their goods.

As of its December 2025 balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio is approximately -2.8x, indicating that total liabilities exceed shareholders' equity. The current ratio, which measures short-term liquidity, is approximately 1.4x. In the fiscal year ended Dec. 31, 2025, free cash flow was close to $638.8 million. Note that stock-based compensation accounted for roughly 35.3% of operating cash flow, inflating reported cash generation, as SBC is a non-cash expense added back to cash flow.

DICK'S Sporting Goods faces significant risks due to its heavy reliance on its primary vendor, which supplies nearly a third of its inventory. Macroeconomic conditions, such as inflation, can also hurt discretionary spending on athletic gear, especially during periods of economic uncertainty. The company is currently working through the integration of the Foot Locker business and must navigate potential legal challenges related to inventory management and securities disclosures.

DICK'S Sporting Goods appears to be the more conservatively valued option based on its lower Forward P/E and P/S ratio. The Forward P/E measures a stock's price relative to future earnings estimates, while the P/S ratio compares the market cap to sales over the past twelve months.

Valuation metrics include those sourced from Financial Modeling Prep (FMP) and may differ from those of other data providers.

When comparing Dick's and Etsy, investors should consider a few key factors. Let's have a look at them and see what that tells us about each stock.

Firstly, we should consider revenue growth. Since 2021, Dick's has generated average year-over-year revenue growth of 22%. Etsy, by contrast, has delivered average revenue growth of just 2%. That gives Dick's an edge in this category.

Another factor to consider is each company's profitability. Here, Etsy shows its strength. The company's operating margin now stands at 18.1%. That's well above Dick's, whose operating margin has fallen in recent quarters to 6.4%.

Finally, there's valuation. Since both companies have been consistently profitable, we can examine their respective price-to-earnings (P/E) multiples. Dick's P/E multiple is 15x, while Etsy's is only 10x.

To sum up, investors may want to consider both of these consumer stocks. However, in my opinion, Etsy comes out on top in this head-to-head matchup. Its combination of higher profitability and lower valuation makes it more appealing than Dick's, despite that company's faster revenue growth.
