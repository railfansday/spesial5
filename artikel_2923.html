Report: Dominick Barlow trending towards season debut vs. Bucks

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

More frontcourt reinforcements! Tony Jones of the Athletic has reported that Dominick Barlow is trending towards making his 2026-27 season debut for the Sixers this Saturday against the Milwaukee Bucks. Jones reported earlier in the week that Barlow was set for one last evaluation that would help him get cleared if all went well. Barlow himself tipped off that the evaluation went well, posting this Instagram story.

Barlow will provide a much needed center option for Nick Nurse off the bench and can also play the four in plenty of lineups as well. He missed the first 16 games of the season with a back injury.

The Sixers also provided brief updates to reporters at practice on the others who missed their most recent game against the Orlando Magic. Tyrese Maxey, Dean Wade, and Adem Bona were all full participants in what was reportedly a light practice. Maxey and Wade were late additions to the injury report in Orlando. Bona, who's now missed seven straight games, still needs to go through a full contact practice. This is courtesy of SixersAdam.

Jaylen Brown participated in individual work only. ESPN's Kris Rhim reported that Brown will be reevaluated ahead of the Bucks game before the team determines a timeline for his return. Brown suffered a hamstring strain at the end of a win over the Brooklyn Nets on Nov. 16. So it may still be a little bit before the Sixers are back to full strength, but Barlow's return will be a much needed boost for the forward rotation.
