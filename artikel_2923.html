South Korea’s Celebrity Shark Raises Bigger Questions About Climate Change And Ocean Conservation

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

A dusky shark named Bukangi has become an unlikely celebrity after swimming into a canal in Busan, South Korea, attracting more than 1.6 million curious visitors. But while the world has fallen in love with her, scientists are increasingly concerned about her health and why she refuses to return to the ocean.
