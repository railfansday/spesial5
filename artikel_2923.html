Wisconsin basketball vs Delaware live updates, highlights, how to watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

MADISON – Wisconsin men's basketball will be trying to keep quite the streak alive in its Nov. 6 game against Delaware.

The Badgers have won their last 20 nonconference home games in a streak that dates back to Nov. 17, 2023. It is the fifth-longest streak in program history and tied for the longest active streak in the Big Ten.

Those 20 wins include a 2023 win over then-No. 3 Marquette and a 2024 win over then-No. 9 Arizona. The Blue Hens present much less of a challenge than those teams after going 10-21 and finishing last in Conference USA in 2025-26.

Tipoff is set for 6:30 p.m., and the game will air on Big Ten Plus, which requires a subscription.
