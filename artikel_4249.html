Game of Thrones: Dragonfire now lets you build your own team of capable Commanders

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

With the airing of House of the Dragon, Game of Thrones is roaring back to popularity in a big way. So it should be no surprise that Warner Bros. Games is giving Game of Thrones: Dragonfire, the mobile spinoff, the lion's share of attention. And now, your journey to dominate Westeros is getting assistance in the form of Commanders.

If you've ever played a mobile RTS/RPG mashup, you'll know what to expect from Commanders. These individual characters can be collected and assigned to lead your force, with their distinctive abilities either buffing your own units or debuffing those of the foe, as well as synergising with the signature dragons of Dragonfire.

Speaking of which, the starting roster features plenty of fan favourite characters to pick from, with Rhaenyra Targaryen, Alicent Hightower, Corlys Velaryon, Daemon Targaryen and Ser Criston Cole all taking up the role.

Commanders offer a great niche to interact with fan-favourite characters, without overshadowing their roles elsewhere in Game of Thrones: Dragonfire. And doubtless there'll be plenty of you out there already preparing to create some truly devious synergies between their abilities and your dragons, all with the aim of conquering Westeros.
