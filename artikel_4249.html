Reading the temperature fingerprints of electrocatalytic reactions

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Electrocatalysis is key to turning renewable electricity into fuels and chemicals, and industrial electrolyzers typically operate at moderately elevated temperatures. Measuring how reaction rates change with temperature is also a powerful, yet underexplored, way to probe what controls them. Such measurements yield two quantities: an apparent activation enthalpy, reflecting the energetic cost of the reaction, and a prefactor, which is linked to the activation entropy.
