Thanksgiving is a full week away, but early Black Friday sales are already here. I've been keeping an eye on all the best fitness trackers and smartwatch deals so that you can snag the best possible price from now until the end of Cyber Monday on Nov. 30.

If you're looking for a sports watch, Garmin sets the standard. Right now, my favorite Garmin, the Forerunner 970, is $100 off, bringing the price down to $649.99. While this is one of the best running watches money can buy, I know it's a far cry from a budget option when you're shopping deals this holiday season. The 44mm Garmin Instinct 3 is 249.99 (down from $449.99). The 1.2-inch color AMOLED display makes the screen more readable in bright daylight conditions, and battery life is best-in-class—a must for those going off-grid.

For most color combinations, the Garmin Fenix 8 is discounted 25% to $749.99 (down from $999.99). If you opt for slate gray with a black band, you can snag this premium watch for as low as $719.89. The Garmin Venu 4 is $449 (down from $549). If you're looking for something not so rugged or sport-specific, the Venu 4 is one of Garmin's best daily health options. It is still a great sports watch, coming with training status, training readiness, and Garmin Coach.
