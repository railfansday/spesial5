/Film Holiday Gift Guide 2026: The Best Movies & TV Shows To Buy On Physical Media

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

We may receive a commission on purchases made from links.

It's the holiday season, with the whoop-de-do and dickory dock, and don't forget that physical media is the way to go if you want to truly own your favorite movies and TV shows in the age of streaming and digital media.

That's right, we're kicking of /Film's 2026 Holiday Gift Guide by diving into our bread and butter: cinema and television. This year saw plenty of great movies and TV shows grace our screens, and thankfully, a lot of them are available to bring home on disc. We're talking about some of the biggest movies to hit theaters, the most watched shows on TV, and plenty of titles that might have fallen under the radar. Plus, there are always classic titles and old favorites arriving on 4K for the first time or getting complete series box sets.

So, from "The Odyssey" (which might be saving physical media) to "Tom & Jerry" and everything in between, let's take a look at all the great physical media offerings that came out in 2026 and are worth adding to your holiday shopping list.

"Spider-Man: Brand New Day" and "The Odyssey" were the two biggest box office hits of 2026, and the rest of the top earners weren't even close. "The Odyssey" is available now, and "Spider-Man: Brand New Day" will be arriving on physical media on December 15.

As for the rest of the movies, we've got a great mix of blockbusters, horror, comedy, sci-fi, animation, and beyond, offering some of the biggest and best films of the year, even if some of them bombed at the box office. Plus, there's a new Collector's Edition of "Avatar: Fire & Ash" that's available.

There are plenty of movies that fell by the wayside in 2026, either because they didn't get the marketing push they needed, or they were overshadowed by the bigger releases of the year. Thankfully, you can catch them when they arrive on home media.

Plus, there are some late physical media releases from movies that arrived in 2025, such as "Twinless" and "The Last Showgirl." Oh, and "Kill Bill: The Whole Bloody Affair" was finally released on 4K, too.

The Criterion Collection is one of the finest physical media labels, and this year, it released The Complete Kubrick collection (above). But there's also an eclectic mix of old standbys, new favorites, and (of course) "KPop Demon Hunters."

Kino Lorber knows how to give movies the high-definition treatment they deserve, and these are some of the releases worth highlighting from 2026.

Arrow Video appreciates movies from the past, especially those that might not immediately feel like they need a 4K release. It also released an incredible 4K box set of "Stranger Things" on physical media.

Shout Factory isn't quite the haven for movies that it once was, but it's still releasing some great 4K library titles, ranging from silly comedies to stellar action and more.

Vinegar Syndrome likes to get down and dirty with its releases. While many of its picks skew towards the strange, there are some more easily accessible picks that get outstanding collector's edition releases on 4K, and these are the best from 2026.

Plenty of movies have gotten released on 4K for the first time in 2026, from classics like "Doctor Zhivago" to comedy hits like "Bridesmaids" and "Mrs. Doubtfire." Action hasn't been forgotten, either, with "Con-Air" and "Speed Racer" joining the mix. Oh, and "Barbarian" finally got a 4K release for the horror hounds out there too.

There are a handful of movie box sets out there worth snagging, if only to reduce the shelf space taken up by entire franchises. There's even a cool 30th anniversary release for the original "Scream" that includes a cordless phone replica. But the best of the bunch here is the "Nirvanna The Band The Show The Movie" box set with both the movie and the TV show.

Even in the age of streaming, TV shows are still hitting physical media season by season. Don't sleep on those "Looney Tunes" releases, folks, and make sure to snatch those streaming shows, because you never know when they might disappear.

For the TV completist, you can't go wrong with these complete collections of entire series, ranging from classic cartoons to the latest hits.
