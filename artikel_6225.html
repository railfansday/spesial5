Allegiant Air Cancels 13 Routes To Major Vacation Destination: See Full List Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Michael Take | Shutterstock, Simple Flying

Allegiant Air has flown to Myrtle Beach since 2009. Cirium Diio data shows that it was the ultra-low-cost carrier's third destination in South Carolina. It came after Columbia, the capital, and Greenville/Spartanburg, both first served 20 years ago. All flights to Columbia have now ended.

Myrtle Beach is renowned for being a family-friendly beach destination. It has tens of miles of white sand, along with being the so-called golf capital of the world. It is not a surprise that it had held much appeal for Allegiant for so long, at least until now, just as it is increasingly doing for Breeze.

Comparing the airline's schedule in 2026 with what is planned for 2027 shows that all of these routes are no longer scheduled. They were cross-referenced with what is listed on Allegiant's website, and all the routes were removed. The 13 markets are in nine states. While only two of the removed destinations are now served by another carrier, all nine states will still have flights, whether by Allegiant or a different operator.

US Department of Transportation data shows that Allegiant flew between Myrtle Beach and Atlantic City between March and August 2026. It had inherited the route from the now-defunct Spirit Airlines, which had served it for 29 years until early 2026. Allegiant transported 11,453 passengers and filled 80.5% of seats. While fare information is unavailable, the extremely short period shows that the route performed very poorly. Despite this, Breeze is keen, as flights started in October.

The cuts outlined above are only one part of Allegiant's story at Myrtle Beach. The ultra-low-cost carrier's booking engine currently extends to mid-May 2027. Between January and then, the airline plans to fly to nine places from Myrtle Beach: Akron/Canton, Albany, Allentown, Cincinnati, Columbus Rickenbacker, Harrisburg, Pittsburgh, Stewart, and Syracuse.

217 departures will be available on those nine routes through May 2027, down by 31% compared to January to May 2026. Seven of the markets will have fewer flights than they did, none more so than Akron/Canton, which Allegiant partly serves because Cleveland is only around an hour away. Departures in the first five months are down from 26 to just eight. In 2026, Allegiant served the market from March. Now it'll do so from May.

All of these routes begin in 2026 or 2027. Find them all here!

Most of the nine states will, of course, have flights to other places, rather than those dropped by Allegiant. Myrtle Beach will have flights to Illinois with American (Chicago O'Hare), Southwest (Chicago Midway), and United (O'Hare), while Indiana will be served by Southwest (Indianapolis).

Then there's Kentucky, with Allegiant flying to Cincinnati (the airport is in Kentucky, but the city is in Ohio), Maryland is linked with Southwest (Baltimore), Michigan's in the hands of Delta (Detroit), and New Jersey's served by Breeze (Atlantic City) and United (Newark).

New York has many routes from Allegiant (Albany, Stewart, Syracuse), American (LaGuardia), Breeze (Albany, Buffalo, Islip, Rochester, Stewart, Syracuse, Westchester), and Delta (LaGuardia). Ohio is served by Allegiant (Akron/Canton and Columbus Rickenbacker), Breeze (Akron/Canton and Columbus John Glenn), and Southwest (Columbus John Glenn). Finally, West Virginia will have flights by Breeze (Charleston) and Contour (Beckley and Parkersburg).
