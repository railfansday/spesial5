The Best Airbnbs in Amsterdam, From Canal Houses to a Boat on the Amstel River

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Amsterdam today looks remarkably similar to how it did on my first visit as a high schooler over a decade ago, with its maze of canals, steady flow of bikes, and gabled façades—many of which have stood there since the 17th century. To a history-loving teenager visiting Europe for the first time, the Dutch capital was a wonderland of Van Goghs. It felt so different from my hometown, yet still approachable, and sparked my lifelong love of travel.

What has changed since then is the crowds. Amsterdam is now one of Europe's most visited cities, so you'll need to book tickets to the major sites well in advance, especially during summer. The same goes for accommodations. On more than a dozen return trips (many by train from Belgium, where I used to live), I have stayed everywhere from youth hostels to boutique hotels to Airbnbs. And over the years, I've learned that renting an Airbnb offers a completely different way to experience the city.

Whether you're looking for a classic canal house in the Jordaan, a design-forward houseboat along the Amstel, or a multi-level family home near Vondelpark, the rentals on this list can help you settle into the city in your own way. Read on for a few of my favorite Airbnbs in Amsterdam, whether you're here for the art, the nightlife, or to explore by bike.

What are the best neighborhoods to stay in Amsterdam?

Book a stay in the Grachtengordel (canal ring) for the quintessential Amsterdam experience. The Jordaan has a similarly atmospheric feel with slightly quieter surroundings, while the Museum District puts the city's famous art museums right at your doorstep.

When is the best season to visit Amsterdam?

Spring is one of the best times to visit. It's when the city wakes up from the long winter and the Netherlands's colorful tulips burst into bloom (plan a day trip to the famous Keukenhof Gardens, about 40 minutes away). Come for King's Day on April 27, when the entire city turns bright orange with street parties and canal boat parades, or visit on the brink of summertime, in late May or June, and you'll enjoy much longer days, with daylight sometimes lasting past 9 p.m.
