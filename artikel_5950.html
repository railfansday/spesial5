Election Day 2026: Photos from around the country

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Against a backdrop of deep political division and with the control of Congress at stake, voters across the country are heading to the polls today to cast their ballots for the 2026 midterm elections. From lines at polling stations to the anticipation and reactions as the results come in, NPR station photographers are documenting the election in their communities.

Voters fill out their ballots at Chicago's McKinley Park Library. Anthony Vazquez/Sun-Times and WBEZ hide caption

An election official tapes "VOTE" signs to a wall to direct voters to the polling room in the Fairfax County Government Center in Fairfax, Va. Tyrone Turner/WAMU hide caption

Voters take to the polls at Wild Carrot, an event space in St. Louis' Shaw neighborhood. Brian Munoz/St. Louis Public Radio hide caption

Tyler Prehn, 5 (right), inserts his father's ballot into the tabulating machine at Lutie Lewis Coates Elementary School in Herndon, Va. His brother, Levi Prehn, 3, looks on. Tyrone Turner/WAMU hide caption

Peg Ihinger takes a photo as she leaves her early voting sticker at the memorial for Alex Pretti in Minneapolis. "I just want this nightmare to end for our city and our country," Ihinger said. "And damn it, they shouldn't have died." Pretti and Renée Good, both 37 and residents of Minneapolis, were killed in separate incidents by U.S. Customs and Border Protection officers and Immigration and Customs Enforcement agents during the Trump administration's Operation Metro Surge in January. Ben Hovland/MPR News hide caption

Voters drop off ballots outside the City Hall Voting Center in San Francisco. Beth LaBerge/KQED hide caption

A large "VOTE" sign hangs from a balcony outside of Hartford City Hall in Hartford, Conn. Raquel C. Zaldívar/New England News Collaborative hide caption

A voter collects their "I Voted" sticker after casting their ballot at Manchester High School in Manchester, Conn. Tyler Russell/Connecticut Public hide caption

Voters cast their ballots at electronic voting stations at a polling station in Hastings City Hall in Hastings, Minn. Kerem Yücel/MPR News hide caption

"NATL GUARD 50 FT BACK" is marked on a sidewalk in Washington, D.C.'s Columbia Heights neighborhood. Ben Ackman/WAMU hide caption

Voters wait in line to cast their ballots at University Presbyterian Church in Austin, Texas. Patricia Lim/KUT News hide caption

A "VOTE HERE" sign is posted at the entrance of the voting station at the Lyon Upper School in Boston's Allston neighborhood. Robin Lubbock/WBUR hide caption
