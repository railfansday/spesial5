How Donnarumma reacted to Napoli fans’ insults in Man City’s ‘theatre-like’ stadium

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

La Gazzetta dello Sport reports that Napoli fans’ insults towards Gianluigi Donnarumma were clearly audible in the quiet stadium, prompting the Italy captain to respond during and after the game.

Napoli fans at the Etihad Stadium insulted Italy captain Donnarumma last night.

Around the hour mark, with Napoli in the lead, away supporters in the third tier started targeting the Italy national team captain with repeated insulting chants.

Gigio reacted by raising his hand, as if to say he had heard it but didn’t appreciate it. He made no rude gesture, but the chants continued.

When Napoli scored the second goal, going 2-1 up, chants became even louder.

The Gazzetta correspondent noted that chants were “easy to hear in Manchester City’s theatre-like stadium, where football tourism is commonplace on European nights.”

As Napoli fans continued to insult him and his mother, Donnarumma sarcastically invited them to keep going, suggesting that the chants didn’t really bother him. Gigio once again made it clear he had heard them, but kept his cool and avoided any rude gestures.

The Azzurri captain, however, didn’t hide his frustration as he left the stadium through the mixed zone later on, telling Napoli reporters he was upset by insults aimed at his mother.
