Ronaldinho day earns Ravenna €199,000 in ticket sales

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Ravenna have announced their sold-out Serie C match featuring Ronaldinho’s return to the field earned them €199,000 in ticket sales alone at the Stadio Benelli.

The match against Forlì in the third division of Italian football turned into a global event that was transmitted live in many different countries, an entirely unusual occurrence for a club in the lower leagues.

The Stadio Benelli was even expanded for the event, with a new temporary stand set up and named Tribuna R10 in honour of the Brazilian legend.

The Ravenna press office informed Football Italia that they raked in €199,000 in ticket sales on Sunday, thanks to a 6,743 sold-out crowd at the Stadio Benelli.

Ticket prices were inevitably raised for the big occasion, rising to a maximum of €150 for the hospitality section, but starting at €15 for season ticket holders, and under-14s able to attend for just €10.

Ronaldinho started the match and played the entire first half, using the break to salute the crowd and perhaps bow out for good at the age of 46.
