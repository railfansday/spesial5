Klopp hails Germany squad after dominant Netherlands win: "This is a very special group"

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Germany rounded off 2026 in stunning fashion in Berlin, as they dismantled the Netherlands 5-0 to seal top spot in their Nations League group.

Derry Scherhant opened the scoring shortly before half-time, before a Florian Wirtz brace and goals from Nico Schlotterbeck and Brajan Gruda rounded off a memorable evening.

Following the final whistle, boss Jürgen Klopp reflected on his first six matches in charge, offering an assessment of the expectations that came alongside his summer arrival.

“I had a bit of a feeling in the summer that people thought the new coach, which was me, would have to drink himself into believing the German national team was good,” he said in his post-match interview with ARD.

But he was keen to emphasise just how impressed he has been by the players involved, suggesting that the public perception does not reflect the quality within the German ranks.

"They are wonderful footballers, wonderful, fantastic characters. But the perception from outside isn't like that, because apparently we're constantly chasing something.

"I'm not chasing anything. I'm at the beginning. I don't feel as though I've already been through three tournaments. I simply feel that this is a great team.

“We have pretty good quality, and if we get these highly talented players going, then it won't be much fun playing against us.”

That was most certainly the case for the Netherlands, who saw the game continue to drift out of their sight as Germany romped home in the second half.

“This is a very special group at the moment,” added Klopp. "I know people have heard that a few times before, and our responsibility is to make that visible and keep it visible.

“We can't change the world, but we can show a positive face, and that's what we did today. I think everyone who was here today will go home with a smile on their face and won't question the German national team for at least three or four months.”

The new boss also spoke on the privilege of representing his country after his remarkable club career.

"I've had the great fortune of coaching three big clubs, and I could always identify with those clubs very easily. During my time at each club, there was no other club for me. I loved working there.

“And now I have the great fortune of working for my home country. There's nothing greater, and that's exactly how it felt.”

He was though, adament that Germany cannot get carried away after the result, acknowledging that further improvement will be needed when his side return to action in March.

"Now we have the opportunity to play in the quarter-finals. I've had a look, and there are only really tough opponents in there. I'm already looking forward to it.

“But to say we've suddenly solved all our problems would obviously be nonsense. We've shown something, though, and that was important.”
