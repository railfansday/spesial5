ThreatBook Named a "Strong Performer" in the Gartner® Voice of the Customer for NDR Report for the Fourth Consecutive Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

SINGAPORE, Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Recently, Gartner released its 2026 "Voice of the Customer for Network Detection and Response (NDR)" report. On the strength of TDP's user reputation and product capabilities, ThreatBook has been named in the "Strong Performer" quadrant for the fourth consecutive year.

Gartner Peer Insights "Voice of the Customer" is based on genuine reviews from enterprise users and provides a peer-perspective reference for technology and service procurement across two dimensions: "User Interest and Adoption" and "Overall Experience."

According to Gartner: "The Voice of the Customer covers vendors whose products fit this market and that had 20 or more eligible published reviews during the 18-month evaluation period ending August 31, 2026. Reviews submitted by vendor partners and by companies with revenue of less than US$50 million are excluded. In the Network Detection and Response market, Gartner Peer Insights published a total of 1,245 reviews and ratings during this evaluation period."Of these, ThreatBook TDP received 84 reviews from users worldwide in the finance, manufacturing, energy, IT and retail industries, with a five-star positive review rate of 100% and a willingness to recommend of 97%, and multiple metrics, including product experience, are industry-leading.
