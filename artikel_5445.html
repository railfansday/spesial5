Bronwyn Newport Gives Estranged Husband Todd Bradley a Tour of Her New Salt Lake City Home: ‘It’s Time To Move On’

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Bronwyn Newport has a new Utah home—and she just gave fans and her estranged husband a tour of the dwelling.

On the Nov. 25 episode of “The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City,” the fashion blogger received a visit from Todd Bradley, her estranged husband. The couple announced their separation in December 2025 after nine years of marriage.

The tech investor initially said he “just wanted to come by and see” Newport and her new home, but soon admitted that he had stopped by to apologize after an explosive conversation the pair had shared over dinner in the previous episode.

In a confessional interview, Newport admitted that Bradley had reached out to smooth things over after their last dramatic conversation, but that she had not yet been ready to talk.

Instead, she extended an invitation to her new property.

“I invited him to the house because it’s a smaller, nicer way for me to say, ‘This is what I’m doing, and this is the direction I’m going,’ without saying things that are hard for him to hear,” she explained.

Bradley had not yet seen the home in person, although he said it looked nice in the photographs he had looked at. Newport offered him a tour of “some of it.”

“The whole basement is going to be Gwen and Logan’s domain,” she explained, referring to her 20-year-old daughter and her daughter’s boyfriend.

The ground level of the home—which was empty at the time of the impromptu tour—featured hardwood flooring, an open-concept plan, a fireplace, and plenty of windows. There were also recessed lights and a glass sliding door, which led to an outdoor space.

“Upstairs is really Muzzy’s space,” Newport said as she walked up the steps of the yet-to-be-furnished home with Bradley.

She said that the upstairs space—in which her mother, who goes by Muzzy, will reside in—includes a kitchen and essentially functions as an apartment.

Finally, Newport brought Bradley into her primary squite, a light-filled space lined with expansive windows. The room features an en suite bathroom with a walk-in shower and tub, which she said she’s very excited about, as she and Bradley had previously discussed a remodel of the home they shared, which would have included a lavish bathtub.

There’s also a well-organized walk-in closet, though fans of the show will understand that it’s likely not going to be enough space for Newport’s massive wardrobe.

In a Sept. 30 interview with Vulture, she did a photo shoot in a storage unit she has in the nearby suburb of Millcreek, UT, which serves the sole purpose of storing all of her clothes and accessories.

“It’s very weird to give Todd a tour of a house that’s going to be mine and not his,” she said during the episode. “There is a part of me that feels I need to be totally free and separate of Todd, but there’s a part of me that is scared to death to not have Todd be a part of my life at all.”

Newport and Bradley had moved into a sprawling Salt Lake City home not long before she joined the cast. In a September 2024 tour of the previous abode, she said they had been living there for a year and a half after moving from nearby Park City.

In the video, Newport revealed she came across the home in a viral listing. It had originally featured a sunken floor with a "psychedelic carpet,” which she said she “liked and really wanted to keep.”

Per Bradley’s wishes, they upgraded the space to have sleek hardwood floors.

“I love a project, so I wanted this old, haunted house in Salt Lake that we could remodel,” she said. “When we first came in, I was obsessed with this living room, the view of Salt Lake and the mountains, all the light, and a big space for me to have tons of parties all of the time.”

The home was boldly decorated, with a royal blue kitchen, rainbow couches in the living room, and a color-drenched lavender office space, with built-in shelving filled with art and designer accessories.

After their separation, Newport and Bradley sold the home for an undisclosed amount in an off-market deal.

The transaction has become a storyline this season of “The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City,” with friend of the cast Britani Bateman flexing her real estate expertise to suggest that the home was priced too high when it was on the market, and that Newport must not have received the 50-50 split from the sale that she had claimed to get.
