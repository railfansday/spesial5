Cypris Introduces the First AI Agent Workforce for R&D

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

New agent workforce platform enables R&D organizations to deploy specialized AI agents across technical, scientific, commercial, and regulatory workflows, with persistent memory, performance reviews, and enterprise governance.

NEW YORK, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Cypris today announced the launch of Agent Fleet, a new capability enabling R&D organizations to build, deploy, and manage their own workforces of specialized AI agents.

Rather than relying on AI as a tool for individual research tasks, R&D teams can now orchestrate fleets of specialized agents capable of executing complete workflows across technical, scientific, commercial, and regulatory intelligence. Each agent operates under the direction and governance of the organization's subject matter experts, with defined responsibilities, persistent memory, performance feedback, and the ability to improve its execution over time.

The launch marks a shift toward a new operating model for corporate R&D, where professionals increasingly act as orchestrators of AI agents, directing specialized digital workers to accelerate discovery, identify new opportunities, and reduce uncertainty across the innovation lifecycle.

Purpose-Built Agents for Chemicals and Advanced Materials

Unlike general-purpose AI assistants, Cypris agents are designed to execute specialized R&D workflows, with a particular focus on the complexity of chemicals, advanced materials, and industrial innovation.

These agents work across Cypris's integrated intelligence ecosystem, connecting patents, scientific literature, chemical compounds, regulatory information, and commercial signals to deliver actionable research outputs.

Unlike traditional AI tools designed for isolated tasks, Cypris Agent Fleet enables organizations to develop a workforce that improves through continued use and human guidance.

Agents can adapt to organizational preferences, incorporate feedback, and become more effective with experience.

Over time, organizations can cultivate specialized agents that increasingly reflect their technical standards, research priorities, and institutional knowledge.

Enterprise Governance for a New Class of Digital Workers

As AI agents take on more complex responsibilities, organizations need the ability to manage and scale their deployment with confidence.

Agent Fleet provides enterprise-level governance, allowing organizations to manage agents across teams while maintaining consistent standards and human oversight.

The result is a new operating model in which R&D professionals increasingly direct, evaluate, and coordinate AI agents rather than manually execute every research task.

Agents That Work Across Applications and Deliverables

Cypris agents are designed to operate across the environments and workflows enterprise R&D teams already use.

Whether within Cypris or connected applications such as Microsoft Copilot, Claude, and electronic laboratory notebooks (ELNs), agents can execute research assignments across multiple environments.

Agents can deliver work in various formats, including technical reports, presentations, spreadsheets, and interactive research outputs, allowing organizations to integrate AI agents into existing R&D processes.

Building the Agentic Workforce for Corporate R&D

"The next generation of R&D professionals won't spend their days manually gathering and synthesizing information. They'll be orchestrating fleets of specialized AI agents that carry out this work on their behalf," said Steve Hafif, CEO and Co-Founder of Cypris.

"What's important is that these agents aren't simply one-off assistants. They have responsibilities, memory, performance expectations, and the ability to improve through feedback. We're building the infrastructure for R&D organizations to manage AI agents much like they manage teams today."

Agent Fleet represents the next step in Cypris's broader vision of building the agentic workforce for corporate R&D. The company plans to introduce additional agent capabilities, deeper workflow integrations, and expanded workforce management functionality as the platform evolves.

Cypris is the AI-powered R&D intelligence platform purpose-built for innovation, IP, and R&D teams. The platform aggregates more than 1B data points across global patents, scientific literature, chemical compounds, regulatory sources, and corporate activity, giving research organizations a single source of truth for technical and strategic decision-making. Cypris is trusted by thousands of enterprise R&D teams, government laboratories, and innovation teams worldwide.
