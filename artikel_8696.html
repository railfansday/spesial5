Search Remote OK for free to find remote jobs in tech and other office work, but skip Premium, which costs $14.95 a month for 12 months to show the salaries most listings hide. Set the free minimum-salary filter to the lowest pay you’d accept instead, and confirm on the employer’s own page that each job is still open.

Seeing pay first lets you skip roles that pay less than you need before you spend time on an application. Remote OK attaches a salary range to its listings, either the employer’s or an estimate based on similar jobs, and its minimum-salary filter screens by that range even when the figure is hidden from you.

Remote OK suits job seekers in programming, design, marketing, and support who will confirm on each employer’s site that a job is still open and hires where they live. Anyone who wants listings screened for scams needs a board that vets every post, and anyone unsure which remote jobs fit their skills should choose a field before searching.
