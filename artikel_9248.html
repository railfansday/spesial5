Rare Hilma af Klint Painting Sells for Staggering $12.7M

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The 1920 painting smashed its original sale estimate and the previous benchmark for a work by the Swedish forebearer of abstraction.

Hilma af Klint's painting “Ser III No. 5” (1920) sold for a record $12.7 million (SEK 128.9 million) at Bukowskis's modern art and design auction in Stockholm today, November 19, fetching over 16 times the upper end of its estimated price.

The first abstract painting by the Swedish artist and "mother of abstraction" to appear on the open market in 30 years, "Ser III No. 5" was one of the 85 geometric paintings the artist produced during a fervent months-long process in 1920. Theosophical symbols, triangles, and circles in "Ser III No. 5" provide insight into af Klint's last engagement with geometric abstraction before she turned to more naturalist watercolors in the last years of her life.
