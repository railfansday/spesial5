Cavs vs. Pacers: How to watch, odds, and injury report

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Cleveland Cavaliers will look to rebound from a frustrating loss on Wednesday as they welcome the struggling Indiana Pacers for an NBA Cup game.

This is essentially a must-win game if the Cavs are going to advance out of the group stage of the NBA Cup. Cleveland is a game behind the Miami Heat, who they lost to last week. For them to advance, the Cavs will likely need to win out, have one of the New York Knicks or Philadelphia 76ers defeat the Heat, and then they would need to have a better point differential than the other teams in the division (provided it's a three-way tie).

Alternatively, the Cavs could make it as a wild card if they don't win the group. For them to do that, they would need to have a good point differential and likely finish with one NBA Cup loss.

The Pacers have been in the wilderness without the services of Haliburton for most of the season. They've won only three of their last 10 games. Winning this one by a substantial margin would go a long way toward helping the Cavs advance out of the group stage of the NBA Cup for the first time.
