GDS (GDS) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Utilization dipped as new capacity came online, and management's 2028 outlook raised questions.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 25, 2026 at 10:07AM EST

GDS (GDS -10.15%) reported a 10.1% increase in third quarter revenue and updated its capital expenditure guidance for the full year. Management indicated that demand for AI infrastructure in China remains robust, driving the company toward a 1 gigawatt annual bookings target. The company is currently executing on a strategy to recycle capital through its Shanghai-listed C-REIT and is in the final phase of repricing legacy contracts. Financial projections for 2028 indicate a projected expansion of billable capacity and EBITDA based on the existing backlog and contracted move-in schedules.

GDS is making good progress towards achieving all of these objectives. We stepped up our bookings, taking on high-quality, low-risk business, which will feed through to higher billings as we cycle through next year and into 2028.
