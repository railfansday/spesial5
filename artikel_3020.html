Vodafone: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The 1 billion-euro cost program through fiscal 2030 is a bet that margins can outpace mobile price pressure.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 21, 2026 at 5:31PM EST

On its first half of fiscal 2027 earnings call, held on Nov. 10, 2026, Vodafone (VOD -1.14%) upgraded its full-year financial guidance following accelerated revenue growth across European and African markets. The business isn't sitting still as it trims operational complexity and expands enterprise digital services to fuel long-term cash generation. Three things management said stand out.

Management upgraded its full-year outlook to reflect 21% organic growth in adjusted free cash flow and 7% growth in adjusted EBITDAaL at the midpoints of its guidance ranges, or earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization after leases. Acceleration in European and African markets provides the baseline for this higher cash generation.

To widen margins, management's executing structural expense reductions across its footprint, including 2,300 headcount cuts in Germany. The broader initiative aims to reduce addressable costs by more than 30% to capture over 1 billion euros in annual net OpEx, or operating expenses, savings by fiscal 2030.

Expansion into cloud hosting, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence tools is transforming the B2B, or business-to-business, segment into a core engine for growth. Enterprise digital products now account for over 2 billion euros in revenue, helping offset traditional mobile price pressures.

Service revenue gains across primary markets provide room to execute a German turnaround. Full U.K. ownership and pricing discipline support structural margin expansion.

Growth across Europe, Africa, and the B2B business underpins management's long-term cash flow outlook.
