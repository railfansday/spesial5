All of the frustration, none of the magic | West Ham’s latest enigma

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Watching last night’s woeful performance against Portsmouth unfold, I was struck by the similarity in playing style of one current West Ham winger with a previous playing enigma from whom West Ham could never seem to coax the best.

Triggered by the  irritation generated from watching Manor Solomon constantly check back, slow down play and then inevitably cut inside, I found myself comparing him with another West Ham winger who repeated the same trick ad nauseam.

Even on the one occasion when he was put clean through beyond his full back, by a brilliant pass from Ezra Mayers, Solomon got to the by line and – infuriatingly, checked back instead of trying to cross with his weaker foot.

And at that moment I thought “Saïd Benrahma” all over again.

The enigma that was Saîd Benrahma: at least he scored a few, created a few, and smacked a mean penalty past Fiorentina’s keeper in the summer of 2023.

‘Benny’ could at least create chances (and score a mean penalty) whereas Manor Solomon just runs down blind alleys, head down, inevitably slowing and cutting inside to use his right foot.

Exactly as, on numerous occasions in each game, the Algerian would check back, infuriatingly, and slow up play instead of pressing on to beat his full back on the inside or out. In that respect Solomon seems identical, disrupting West Ham’s flow, allowing defenders to reset and frustrating the bejesus out of supporters.

At least Benrahma could create and occasionally score: Solomon has, by the reckoning of westhamworld.co.uk, now played “1141 minutes for two goals and two assists, averaging a goal every 285 minutes”.

For whatever reason the Israeli clearly is a shadow of the player who helped fire Leeds to promotion and West Ham need to consign him to the same ‘rest and recuperation’ home in which Mads Hermansen has been sent to recapture his form.

Austbo at least has a shot on him and seems to be able to beat players inside and out: With the Norwegian there is the feeling that there’s more to come. With Solomon, exactly as Tottenham fans suggested, the fear is that his best days are long in the rear view mirror. Much like Saîd Benrahma, but without the trophy to show for it.
