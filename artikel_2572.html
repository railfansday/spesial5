Urban Meyer says there is 'no chance' Ryan Day will get fired if Michigan wins

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Former Ohio State coach Urban Meyer believes current Buckeyes coach Ryan Day has job security, even if OSU loses to Michigan and falls to 7-5.

"There's justified heat," Meyer said on "The Triple Option" podcast Nov. 24. "I mean, I took heat all the time. You lose one game, you take heat. The chance of Ryan Day getting let go is no chance. Stop with that. I think there's a difference between criticism and someone making a change, and there's zero probability."

Ohio State's 31-28 loss to Nebraska on Nov. 21, was widely critiqued, including by former OSU players. That loss almost certainly eliminated Ohio State's chances of making the 12-team College Football Playoff bracket and made it practically impossible to reach the Big Ten Championship.

With many calling for changes to Day's staff in the future, Meyer said all the focus this week should be on Ohio State-Michigan rather than outside noise.

"I'm hopeful that you walk in the Woody Hayes facility right now and there's nothing but six straight days of how do you beat this team, because you have to beat this team," Meyer said.

There was a very specific group of players he worried about losing focus this week.

"You have 51 new players that people are driving into their ears right now," Meyer said. "The infamous third uncle, the idiot that right now is telling the player, 'Hey, should we stay?' Shut up. Go beat the team up north and then have that conversation after."

Meyer went 7-0 against Michigan in his Ohio State tenure. Day led the Buckeyes to a 27-9 victory last year, but is 2-4 against the Wolverines in his coaching career.
