Skipper Bryce sets new Scotland run record in win over UAE

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

United Arab Emirates 138-4 (20 overs): Oza 64, Hotchandani 43; Gordon 2-24

Scotland 142-3 (18.1 overs): K Bryce 51, S Bryce 43; Dharnidharka 2-22

Captain Kathyrn Bryce became Scotland Women's all-time leading run scorer as she hit an unbeaten half century to Scotland to a seven-wicket win over host nation United Arab Emirates at the ICC Women's Emerging Nations Trophy.

The skipper hit seven fours on her way to 51 not out as Scotland reached 142-3 with 11 balls to spare.

It takes her international run total to 3562, one more than Kari Carswell.

Her sister, Sarah Bryce, made 43, batting at number three after Darcey Carter's departure in the first over.

Katherine Fraser added 15, with Pippa Sproul on 16 not out.

Batting first, captain Esha Oza led the way with 64 not out as UAE reached 138-4 in their 20 overs.

Heena Hotchandani added 43 before she was one of two batters to be stumped by wicketkeeper Sarah Bryce from the bowling of Kirstie Gordon.

Having previously beaten Papua New Guinea and lost to Sri Lanka, Scotland sit second in Group A and are assured of a place in the Super Six stage.

Scotland conclude their group games against Ireland on Wednesday.
