Monday Night Football: Bengals lead 10-7 at halftime

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

It took more than a quarter and a half for any points to get scored in Washington on Monday night, but the Bengals and Commanders finally got going in the final minutes before halftime.

After a 15-play drive resulted in a field goal midway through the second quarter, the Bengals found more explosive plays on their next possession. Running back Samaje Perine broke free on a short pass for a 44-yard gain and Joe Burrow then went deep to wide receiver Tee Higgins for a 31-yard touchdown on the first play after the two-minute warning. The catch bore some resemblance to the one by Jaguars wideout Parker Washington that was ruled incomplete on Sunday, but there was no review.

That score put the Bengals up 10-0 and left the Commanders with a last chance to find their first points of the evening.

Jayden Daniels ran for one first down and threw for two more before hooking up with tight end Chig Okonkwo for a 32-yard gain that moved the Commanders to Cincinnati’s 6-yard-line. A pass to wide receiver Treylon Burks in the end zone drew a pass interference flag on cornerback Dax Hill and running back Jacory Croskey-Merritt punched the ball in on the next play to make it 10-7 going into the break.

Daniels is 8-of-11 for 85 yards, but has been sacked three times by Boye Mafe. The last of those came on a third down and led to an animated Stefon Diggs appearing to yell about throwing the ball on the sideline while the Bengals had possession. Diggs’s only catch of the half came on the late touchdown drive.

Burrow is 16-of-23 for 179 yards and has been sacked a couple of times. The Bengals also saw safety Bryan Cook leave just before halftime with an ankle injury and he’s called questionable to be back on the field when the Commanders get the ball to start the second half.
