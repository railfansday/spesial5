Recent opponents reportedly spill truth about Patriots 'soft' defense

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The perception from some of the New England Patriots' recent opponents is that the defensive unit is "soft."

That's a major turnaround for a unit that was considered one of the best in the NFL last season. With that said, the Patriots have given up 99 points in total over the last three games. That includes 35 points against the Detroit Lions and 45 points against the Green Bay Packers.

There really is no sugar-coating the fact that it has looked like a disaster at times against top-tier offensive teams. Even worse, those other teams are starting to talk about it.

"I've had conversations with people that have played the Patriots of late, and that is a word that has come up about their defense is soft," Perry said on Zolak & Bertrand. "And softer than what they were anticipating I think, too, because you have some big names, especially up front there like in the interior and the way they were moved in that Lions game I think surprised a lot of people."

The defensive front has struggled to stop the run and get home consistently enough to impact opposing quarterbacks. However, one of the more disappointing problems for the Patriots has been their defensive backfield. Star cornerback Christian Gonzalez hasn't played up to his potential, and Carlton Davis Davis hasn't been available due to injury.

Kevin Byard has also struggled at safety.

There is also the fact that defensive coordinator Zak Kuhr hasn't been as effective as a play-caller as he was a year ago. Overall, it has been a frustrating season for a defensive unit that came into the year with high expectations.

The good news is that there is still time left to turn things around with the hardest stretch of football still ahead.
