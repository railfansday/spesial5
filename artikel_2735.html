Weekly Stat Review: Post Week 8 edition

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Welcome to the newest edition of the Weekly Stat Review! Another week, another painful viewing experience for fans of the Carolina Panthers. As is par for the course for Thursday Night Football, it was sloppy and potentially the least entertaining game of the week.

That said, it also marked the last game as the Defensive Coordinator for Ejiro Evero, so this week's stat review will be interesting to look back upon a few weeks from now to see if the trends shift at all after Evero's departure and through the beginning of Todd Wash's tenure.

Percentage of plays that qualify as an explosive play. Explosive plays being a carry of 10 or more yards or a pass attempt gaining 20 or more yards.

Percentage of dropbacks with a pressure or sack allowed

Rate of 3rd downs that converted to 1st downs.

Percentage of coverage snaps spent in a zone defense.

Percentage of pass rush opportunities that resulted in a sack or pressure.

Rate of 3rd downs allowed to be converted into 1st downs.

Will Lee III has been called for 4 pass interference penalties that cost a -8.17 expected points added (EPA). This is the 3rd highest amount of cumulative EPA cost by a single player in the NFL so far this season.
