After earning their first win, Chargers are favored to win their second vs. Jets

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Chargers finally won a game on Monday. The betting odds say they should win another on Sunday.

It’s not often that a 1-8 team is favored, but the Chargers are 3.5-point favorites on Sunday against the Jets.

That’s not an indication that anyone is buying into the Chargers after they beat the Ravens on Monday night, as the Chargers opened as favorites even before they won a game. It’s more a reflection of how bad the 2-8 Jets are, that they’re not a safe bet to beat anyone.

The Chargers were favored in the first two weeks of the season, by 9.5 against the Cardinals in Week 1 and by 6.5 against the Chargers in Week 2. They lost both games by 12, and since then they’ve been underdogs every week.

This is the ninth time this season the Jets have been underdogs. They were last favored in Week 7, when they were favored by four points over the Dolphins but lost 28-25.

In a game between two of the worst teams in the league, someone has to win, and the betting odds say that someone will be the Chargers.
