QB Kedon Slovis returns to Arizona Cardinals via waiver claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Arizona Cardinals have added a fourth quarterback to their roster. On Wednesday, they were awarded Kedon Slovis on a waiver claim. The Baltimore Ravens waived him earlier this week.

This means Slovis returns to the Cardinals after spending last season on their practice squad and active roster and then this past offseason and preseason.

The Cardinals' quarterback room now includes Carson Beck, Josh Johnson, Joey Aguilar and Slovis. This move likely signals one of two things. Either Aguilar will be waived next week (teams commit three weeks to a player they sign off another team's practice squad) or Beck's back injury is bad enough that he will be placed on injured reserve this week.

Beck did not practice on Wednesday, but, according to head coach Mike LaFleur, he is doing better than he did last week.

Slovis has not played this season. His only NFL regular-season action was last season for the Cardinals when he was their backup quarterback after Kyler Murray was placed on injured reserve. He appeared in two games, played five snaps, and he completed 1-of-2 passes for minus-two yards.

The Cardinals did not have to make a corresponding move, as they had a vacancy on the 53-man roster.
