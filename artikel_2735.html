Brian Schottenheimer on confidence in Christian Parker: We're still working through the process

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

One constant of the 2026 Cowboys season has been a focus on defensive coordinator Christian Parker’s job security in the wake of losses and things aren’t any different in the aftermath of Sunday’s loss to the 49ers.

The 49ers piled up 584 yards and scored three touchdowns that covered at least 66 yards in their 46-40 win, which led head coach Brian Schottenheimer to call their tackling unacceptable in his postgame press conference. Executive vice president Stephen Jones used the same word to describe the team’s performance on Monday and Schottenheimer’s press conference later in the day began with a question about whether he remains confident in Parker.

“We’ll still working through the process,” Schottenheimer said. “Talking to the guys is the first step. At the end of the day, there’s a lot of things we need to clean up as a football team. That starts with me. You look at this game, the positives. Five sacks team that had been sacked eight times — you get five sacks. Probably missed another three or four, could have been almost double digits. But at the end of the day, the tackling and some of the coverage stuff led to the explosives. We’re taking a deep dive, we’re looking at everything.”

The answer notably didn’t include an affirmation of confidence in Parker, which led to a followup asking if there’s a chance that they could make a change involving Parker.

“I’m not saying that. I’m saying we’re still in the process of going through all the film and evaluation of where we stand today,” Schottenheimer said.

The Cowboys may not choose to make a change, but it’s hard to feel confident that there’s going to be a major difference between their play over the first 10 weeks and their play over the rest of the season if everything about the defensive approach remains the same.
