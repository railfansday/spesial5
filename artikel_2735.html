Clippers interim GM given full power to blow up roster at trade deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Earlier this week, Marc Stein reported the Clippers are viewed as a "certain seller" with the NBA trade deadline approaching in February.

Jack Fischer of The Stein Line said certain teams have expressed skepticism about what the Clippers might do.

Fischer described the term "skepticism" as "misplaced" and said sources told him that interim general manager Trent Redden has been given the power to make "any moves he sees fit."

Brook Lopez playing in an NBA game for the Clippers against the Mavericks. NBAE via Getty Images

Redden was the same member of the Clippers' front office who finalized the decision to move Kawhi Leonard from Los Angeles to the Raptors over the offseason.

According to Fischer, the Clippers have a few veteran pieces who could be dealt, including center Brook Lopez and wing Derrick Jones Jr.

Fischer added the club wants to assess what its roster will look like with Brandon Ingram staying healthy.

Ingram was acquired in the trade with Toronto for Leonard. He came over to Los Angeles with Gradey Dick, two first-round draft picks, including a first-round pick swap, and two second-round draft picks.

Brandon Ingram making his season debut for the Clippers on Monday night against the Rockets. Getty Images

Ingram missed the first month and a half of the season due to a partially torn Achilles tendon he suffered with the Raptors last season. He made his season debut on Monday.

Ingram was the Clippers' third-leading scorer in their 116-104 loss to Houston on Monday, but he gave the team a glimmering hope about his health. Ingram was on the court for 27 minutes and tallied 16 points, seven rebounds and four assists.
