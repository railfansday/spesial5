Sixers take care of business without LeBron, secure 10th vs. Raptors

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Maybe not the blowout people are looking for, but still a pretty decent win. The Sixers erased a 17-point first half deficit to defeat the Toronto Raptors 123-112 Thursday night.

Tyrese Maxey led the Sixers with 27 shooting 8-of-13 from the field along with eight assists. Joel Embiid didn't quite break out of his shooting slump, but looked much better as he avoided an injury scare early in the game. He finished with 20 points going 7-of-17 from the floor along with four rebounds.

Jaylen Brown had an up-and-down night, going 8-of-15 from the field for 18 points and six rebounds. VJ Edgecombe had another great swiss army knife game, finishing with 15 points, eight rebounds and eight assists. R.J. Barrett led all scorers with 31.

LeBron James was out for this one with lower back management, as well as Adem Bona (neck) and Dominick Barlow (back). Toronto was without Kawhi Leonard, Scottie Barnes, and Trayce Jackson-Davis.

The Sixers opened the game generating good shots. Brown shook free of CMB for a pull-up jumper, Dean Wade and Edgecombe both stepped into open threes, and Embiid got a jumper from the free throw line, but none of these looks fell. Maxey finally got the Sixers their first field goal of the game getting the ball off a broken play and getting to the basket.

Embiid set a screen for Maxey to help him get open on the three-point line and appeared to bang knees with Ja'Kobe Walter as Walter was trying to fight through the screen. Embiid went down in a heap of pain immediately and the Sixers stopped the game so he could head back to the locker room.

The Sixers' offense continued to struggle after the stoppage. Brown was trying attack off the dribble but was getting bottled up. They still couldn't hit a three off the catch either — the rest of the team started the game 0-of-4 from deep. The Raptors ripped off a 12-0 run during this stretch.

For the second game in a row, Edgecombe had a really nice spin move to split defenders and get to the basket. Darko Rajakovich called timeout after he did so, which allowed Embiid to emerge from the locker room and return to the game. Edgecombe did just about the same thing coming out of the timeout.

Maxey ran quick off a miss to create a fast break layup, but Toronto was able to snuff out the momentum quickly. The Sixers went into a zone for a couple of possessions, but the Raptors shooters had gotten into a rhythm and they were able to get open pretty easily. Two threes from Barrett and another from Jamison Battle helped them score 13 more unanswered points.

Embiid nearly passed up a wide open layup near the basket, but did eventually put it up once he realized the 6-foot-1 Jamal Shead was guarding him for his first field goal of the game. Maxey was able to hit a ridiculous layup in transition, and Anfernee Simons drilled a three at the buzzer to pull the Sixers to within nine.

Simons drilled a stepback three with a hand in his face to open the quarter. Embiid, looking a bit more comfortable, blocked a layup and got himself an easy bucket going to the paint. Brown wasn't able to bury his three after making Kyle Anderson fall with a crossover, but he finally got a midrange to go that capped off a 15-0 Sixers run going back to the first.

That seemed to do the trick as Brown drilled his next two pull-ups in the midrange. Edgecombe was the best Sixer on the floor for much of the first half. His intercepting of passes from the weak side was the best defense they had going for them, and it was creating plenty of transition opportunities. On top of a big fast break dunk, Edgecombe also hit a very nifty up-and-under layup.

It probably gave everyone a good scare, but Embiid was able to get by Jakob Poeltl and throw down a dunk, albeit one that did not look like the smoothest landing. The Sixers' offense started scorching, but a lack of stops prevented them from building a lead. Nick Nurse called a timeout to regroup after they allowed two offensive rebounds on one possession, ending with a putback.

That timeout also gave Nurse a chance to put Maxey back in the game, who immediately went straight to the basket and got fouled. After teams traded threes, Maxey got to the hoop again, but Embiid picked up his third foul of the night trying to grab a defensive rebound and had to sit for the last two and a half minutes of the half.

Brown took up the mantle offensively, nailing another pull-up and driving for a layup. Jabari Walker, who didn't have much of an impact in his first shift, picked up an offensive rebound and drew a foul pulling down another. His putback was answered with another three from Barrett. Wade missed another open three on the ensuing possession, keeping the game tied at 60 at the break.

Both teams stayed hot coming out of the break as Wade and Edgecombe each drilled a corner three. On the other end of the floor the Raptors drove right to the basket for three easy layups. Immanuel Quickley also drilled a very deep, very contested pull-up three. Embiid missed a fadeaway over Poeltl, but got himself another easy dunk playing high-low with Edgecombe.

The Sixers were still giving up plenty of easy baskets off simple actions, but they were guarding one-on-one better when the Raptors isolated. Brown blocked a Barrett layup and jumped a passing lane for a pick-six. Screens ended up being risky plays on the night as Kentavious Caldwell-Pope came up favoring his shoulder trying to fight through one, but he didn't have to leave the game.

Despite throwing a post-entry pass for Brown a little too far, Embiid seemed to finding his comfort offensively. He was confident in his physical advantages over Poeltl and was aggressive because of that. It did seem like he was trying to dunk as much as he could, which did lead to him fumbling away an attempt at the basket.

Embiid is still waiting on that midrange game to come back around as he was long on another free throw line jumper. It remained a hot Simons game, something the team desperately needed with how they've been shooting lately.

Maxey was a consistent source of scoring to close the quarter, but stops on the other end continued to come at a premium. The Sixers were finally able to get some space after Quickley missed a pretty open layup after beating Maxey through the paint. Simons got it up the floor for a transition basket. The Raptors called a timeout to regroup, but CMB got called for a moving screen on the first play coming out of it. Maxey got to the line, which Barrett responded to by backing him down in the post for a layup, bringing the Sixers lead back down to nine after three.

Walker opened the quarter with a corner three and just missed a second a few possessions later. He did scoop up another putback after Edgecombe's attempt to dunk on two Raptors came up empty, and Simons swiped the ball away from Barrett for a fast break bucket to start the quarter strong.

At some point in the first half, the ball got stuck behind the backboard and the crowd let Poeltl hear it as he struggled to knock it free with a broom. When the ball got stuck again in the fourth, a referee brought the broom back to Poeltl on the bench for a chance at redemption. The crowd got a kick out of it, but Poeltl did not, and refused. The crowd shared a sarcastic celebration with KCP after it took him a few attempts to dislodge it.

The Sixers' offense seemed to hit a bit of a lull midway through the fourth. Brown missed a pair of free throws and was blocked again at the rim by CMB. Edgecombe tried to hit Brown with a lob but that was broken up by CMB again, but Edgecombe was able to pick it back up and finish it himself. After giving up second chance points on the following possession, Nurse called a timeout to put Embiid and Maxey back in the game.

The Raptors got another basket inside, a trip to the line, and a corner three to cut the lead down to six with plenty of time left. Simons hit a much needed three, as was the case for much of the night. Embiid finally knocked down a midrange after a couple of stops, but Quickley walked into an open three off a screen on the next trip down the floor.

Maxey got to the line again as the Raptors started to run out of time. Embiid got past a couple of defenders and nearly had another self lob off the backboard, but that was also broken up by CMB. Embiid was still able to secure the ball and get it to KCP, who buried a three right before the shot clock expired. Embiid hit a midrange on the following possession, wrapping up this game quite nicely for both himself and the team.

The double-digit lead gave the fans enough confidence for loud "We Want Tacko" chants, and Nurse obliged, putting Tacko Fall in for the final 54 seconds of the game. Poor Jabari Walker got a round of boos when he went to the scorer's table on account of not being Tacko Fall.
