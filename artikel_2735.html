‘These Rules Are Straight Trash,’ ‘The NFL Absolutely Sucks’ — Hall of Famer Terrell Owens Rips Officials To Shreds Over Nick Bosa Roughing Call

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Terrell Owens was watching Sunday Night Football with his son when a flag in Mexico City set him off.

The Pro Football Hall of Famer tore into the NFL and its rules after San Francisco 49ers defensive end Nick Bosa was called for roughing the passer on Minnesota Vikings quarterback Kirk Cousins.

The penalty handed Minnesota a fresh set of downs on the drive it rode to a 7-7 tie late in the first quarter, and Owens didn't hold back, insisting, "That was NOT a roughing the passer on Bosa!!" However, his frustration went beyond the one flag.

Owens also aimed his anger at the league and its rulebook. "Watching the game with my son and the NFL absolutely SUCKS!! That was NOT a roughing the passer on Bosa!! These rules are straight TRASH!!!" he wrote.

The Rate The Refs account watching had the same complaint about the flag, writing, "NFL, you've done it again!"

Once the game kicked off, the 49ers took a 7-0 lead on Brock Purdy's 17-yard touchdown pass to George Kittle. After a missed field goal and a 49ers punt, Minnesota started a drive at its own 10-yard line with 6:01 left in the first quarter and worked its way deep into San Francisco territory.

That's where Bosa got home, landing on Cousins on a pass intended for T.J. Hockenson that fell incomplete and would have brought up third down. Instead, officials flagged Bosa for roughing the passer, ruling that he had come down on Cousins with his full body weight, according to Athlon Sports.

The penalty gave the Vikings an automatic first down and moved them closer to the goal line, and Aaron Jones Sr. finished the job with an 11-yard touchdown run. That score capped a nine-play, 90-yard drive and tied the game 7-7 with 1:13 left in the first quarter.

The doubts weren't limited to fans. Sports Illustrated NFL insider Albert Breer also questioned the flag, writing, "That roughing call on Bosa … I'm not sure what he could've done."

San Francisco scored 21 of the next 24 points after the tie and ultimately wrapped up a neat little 35-10 win, improving to 9-1 and solidifying its case as the best team in the league this season.

Both the Niners and the Vikings return home for Week 12 on Nov. 29, with San Francisco hosting the Seattle Seahawks at Levi's Stadium and Minnesota welcoming the Atlanta Falcons to U.S. Bank Stadium.
