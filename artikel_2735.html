Joe Burrow: Sunday night was great, but you're not winning anything after nine games

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow entered some rare company in Sunday night’s romp over the Steelers.

Burrow’s four touchdown passes gave him 20 over the last five games and that’s something that only Tom Brady and Peyton Manning have accomplished over the history of the league. Both of those players have won MVP awards and Burrow is now the frontrunner for that award for the current season.

The crowd in Cincinnati picked up on that and chanted “MVP” for Burrow during the 38-18 win and Burrow acknowledged the run he’s been on during a postgame press conference. Burrow said “a great one” when asked what kind of quarterback he was on Sunday, but noted it’s “a long season” when asked about the MVP conversation and that the team’s goals are equally unrealized at this point in the year.

“No one is winning the division with seven wins,” Burrow said, via a transcript from the team. “Nobody’s making the playoffs with seven wins, and we’re certainly not satisfied.”

Burrow may not want to look ahead at this point, but others will do it for him and the Bengals’ schedule is set up for them to continue stacking wins as long as they remain in the groove they’ve been in on their current five-game streak. If they do that, there will likely be a lot of people who believe Burrow can join Brady and Manning as both MVPs and Super Bowl champs.
