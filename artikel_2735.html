Jeffery Simmons picks up 3.0 more sacks, Bears beat Saints 21-13

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

When the Saints look back on their Week 11 loss to the Bears, they’ll likely lament several missed opportunities.

While the Bears looked rusty coming off of their Week 10 bye, they still were able to make enough plays to defeat New Orleans 21-10 on Sunday.

Chicago got out to a 16-0 lead midway through the second quarter with a 41-yard field goal and two touchdown passes from Caleb Williams, though one of them featured a missed extra point. But the Saints narrowed that lead to six points at halftime with a 1-yard pass from Tyler Shough to Alvin Kamara paired with a 56-yard field goal from Charlie Smyth.

That, however, didn’t make up for an egregiously wide right missed field goal from 47 yards out.

The Bears then extended their lead with a 35-yard field goal from Cairo Santos midway through the third quarter. And the Bears got a safety early in the fourth quarter to close out their scoring, with Montez Sweat bringing Shough down in the end zone for a sack.

The Saints got down to Chicago’s 10-yard line after a Bears fumble in the fourth quarter, but weren’t able to connect, turning it over on downs.

With no timeouts due to failed challenges, New Orleans could only manage a 51-yard field goal late in the fourth quarter before attempting an onside kick.

As most have this season, the Saints’ attempt failed.

As noted by the Fox broadcast, this was the Bears’ fifth consecutive game without trailing. It’s the franchise’s longest streak since 1959, when they had six such contests.

Williams ended the game 15-of-23 for 181 yards with two touchdowns and an interception.

The Jeffery Simmons trade continues to pay off for the Bears, as he registered 3.0 sacks on Shough. Via NFL research, Simmons is now the first Bears player to have at least 4.0 sacks in his first three games with the franchise since Khalil Mack in 2018.

Shough was 25-of-42 for 222 yards with a touchdown and a pick. Chris Olave led with 102 yards on nine catches.

Now at 8-2, the Bears will have a quick turnaround as they’ll face the Lions on the road on Thanksgiving.

The 3-7 Saints will be on the road to face the Bengals next Sunday afternoon.
