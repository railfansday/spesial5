Eagles News: Jeremiah Trotter Jr. and Jihaad Campbell lead inspired defensive effort

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Welcome to The Linc, the daily Philadelphia Eagles links roundup at Bleeding Green Nation!

With Zack Baun now on injured reserve, the Eagles will get a look at the duo of Jeremiah Trotter Jr. and Jihaad Campbell together over the next three games … if not longer. Baun has been a rare bright spot for the Eagles this year … but maybe they can trade him in the offseason and move forward with their youth at off-ball linebacker? Something to consider.

Let's get to the Philadelphia Eagles news and links …

Spadaro: 10 takeaways from the Eagles' 19-16 victory over the Steelers – PhiladelphiaEagles.com

Jeremiah Trotter sets the tone, and dominates With Zack Baun sidelined and on Injured Reserve, the Eagles went with Jeremiah Trotter Jr. and Jihaad Campbell (7 total tackles, 1 for loss) at linebacker and both played extremely well. Trotter set the tone on the first possession, knifing into the hole and tackling running back Jaylen Warren for no gain, and the Eagles' 40-yard line. Trotter ended up with 13 tackles, including 9 solos, and set the tone for a terrific defensive performance on that fourth-down stop. "I give the credit to my guys up front," Trotter said. "They did their jobs and I did mine. I had a clear path to the ball and just did what I was supposed to do. We knew we had to stop the run today. We knew they ran the ball well, so we tackled well and got off blocks." A note on the run defense: Pittsburgh came into the game averaging 116 yards per game on the ground and gained just 73 yards on 20 carries, including 29 from the not-nimble Rodgers on scrambles. Great job.

10 Eagles things I think I think following the win over the Steelers – BGN

If the Eagles were facing a competent offense against a quarterback that wasn't well past his prime, the outcome may have been different. Give credit to the offense for getting the field goal when they needed it and icing the game, but they had a stretch where they went 24 plays for 44 yards (1.8 yards per play). The ineptitude just happens way too often on offense to win games consistently. The Eagles were fortunate the Steelers never got anything going on offense until they were down 10 points in the final minutes.

Joe Milton, Saquon Barkley and the Eagles offense did just enough to get the job done. Join Curry, Fran Duffy, EJ Smith, Brandon Lee Gowton and Bo Wulf for the lowdown.

DK Metcalf has become the Steelers' eye-poking disaster – SB Nation

There's something in the water when it comes to wide receivers in Pittsburgh that turns them into clowns. It's been this way since the halcyon days of Hines Ward, as the team has managed, time and time and time again, to be saddled with problematic guys and know exactly when to part ways before the ticking time bomb explodes. Until DK Metcalf arrived. This is the organization's big mistake, the whiff that is setting them back, and Sunday was a perfect example of how much of a disaster Metcalf has been for this team. It isn't simply about losing to a hopeless Eagles team, or Metcalf getting ejected for poking Cooper DeJean in the eye, or even losing pace in the AFC North — it's all of these things in concert, and the fact that they're paying through the moon for this guy to actively hurt their team. Perhaps the worst part of all this is that anyone could see this was a HORRIBLE move for the Steelers when they made it.

Steelers offensive woes continue, drop second straight game in loss to Eagles – Behind The Steel Curtain

The Pittsburgh Steelers have now lost consecutive games for the first time this season, losing to the Philadelphia Eagles by the score of 19-16, dropping their record to 6-4 on the season. For the second straight week, the Steelers' offense completely did them in. Their first four drives ended without points, the first of which saw a turnover on downs that led to an Eagles field goal. Their subsequent two drives were a pair of three-and-outs and then a six-play drive that ended in a punt. In total, they ran 16 plays for 17 yards in their first four drives. Meanwhile, the Eagles put together a 13-0 lead that the Steelers would never surmount. The Steelers entered halftime down 13-3, and Aaron Rodgers averaged 0.5 air yards per attempt over the first two quarters, the lowest such number with at least 20 attempts since ESPN began tracking air yards in 2006, per Brooke Pryor.

From top-down, angry Eagles defensive front 'whoop' Steelers to halt losing streak – PHLY Eagles

Moro Ojomo said it felt like winning the Super Bowl. He would know. To be clear, the Eagles' 19-16 win didn't come with any trophy. There wasn't confetti falling from the sky or stanchions dragged out to midfield or cigar smoke weighing down the air of the postgame locker room. What did exist was the weightlessness of a group adding to the win column for the first time since the Phillies were still playing baseball and the residual anger that it took that long to achieve it. That anger was palpable amidst the defensive line in the moments before the game. Ojomo broke his teammates down after pre-game warmups with a message he tried — and failed — to put "politely" in his locker stall a few hours later. "Whoop a motherfucker's ass," he said.

An update on the Eagles' projected compensatory pick for the loss of Jaelan Phillips – PhillyVoice

However, there was always a possibility that the value of the compensatory pick for Jaelan Phillips could fall into a lower round tier if he didn't hit playing time benchmarks. And certainly, that danger was higher for an oft-injured player like Phillips, who missed Weeks 7, 8, and 9 with a hamstring injury. If you look at OverTheCap's compensatory formula chart, Phillips needs to play around 23 percent of the Panthers' regular season defensive snaps to qualify for a third-round compensatory pick. Through 10 games, Phillips has played 336 of a possible 684 defensive snaps, or 49.1 percent. The Panthers are on pace to play 1,163 defensive snaps this season. If Phillips doesn't play another snap and the Panthers stay on that pace, he will have played roughly 29 percent of the Panthers' defensive snaps. There's no moment where Phillips will "clinch" a third-round pick, but he should now safely be within that third-round tier at this point, even if he doesn't play another snap this season.

Roob's Observations as Eagles' defensive statement sparks win over Steelers – NBCSP

This offense is so bad in the second half it's hard to comprehend. They did manage a couple field goals Sunday – one in the third quarter, one in the fourth – but they have still scored an astonishing 46 points after halftime through 10 games. That's their fewest since 1972, when they had 26 through 10 games and second -fewest since 1942. That's all coaching. Not that they're setting any scoring records in the first half, but give defensive coaches a chance to make adjustments at halftime and the Eagles don't have a prayer in the second half. They have four 2nd-half touchdowns, only two of them since Jalen Hurts was lost for the season. They won a game Sunday because the defense was really good, but it's just hard to fathom an offense that is averaging 4 ½ points per game in the second half. That's almost impossible to do.

NFL Week 11: Big takeaways, questions and fantasy spin from Sunday's games – ESPN

Can the defense lead the Eagles on a mini-run? The defensive line has rounded into form the past two games. They sacked Aaron Rodgers five times after posting five sacks and nine QB hits against the Giants two weeks ago. Edge Jonathan Greenard has been part of that surge with two takedowns in two games after having none before Week 9. It will be a step up in competition on Thanksgiving against Cowboys QB Dak Prescott, but with the Cardinals and Colts up after that, Philly could make things interesting in a down NFC East if the defense continues to shine.

Eagles' first win in 2 months 'felt like the Super Bowl.' Is it too late to matter? – The Athletic

The question is whether it's too late to make anything meaningful out of the victory. In the locker room during the week, the message from DeVonta Smith and Nolan Smith was that the Eagles are not out of it yet. Look at the division, DeVonta Smith said. They're still far behind, but this is not a team playing out the string of a forgettable campaign. Barkley said he felt more togetherness this week than before. There was clear energy and emotion on the field and the sideline. "A lot of ball left," Jordan Mailata said. "And it's pride, man. It's a pride thing. It's not even about turning it around. It's about having pride." "I mean, if you have a sense of pride … you ain't just gonna sit there and take a beating," Ojomo said. "What type of man are you? Who do you represent? Who are the people that you're setting an example for? Your family members, your kids, whatever it may be. You want to be able to look those people in the eyes and know that you did your best." That was especially the case for the defense. Entering the season, it looked like it would compete to be one of the NFL's best units. The defense was inconsistent for a chunk of the first nine weeks, unable to mask the offense's issues. Vic Fangio's group had a strong performance in Week 9, even in a loss to the New York Giants. Then Jordan Davis was traded, and Zack Baun's back injury forced him to injured reserve.

10 thoughts on the Cowboys win over the Titans – Blogging The Boys

Unstoppable offense The offense was phenomenal on Sunday. Yes, it came against a bad Titans defense, but you really couldn't have asked this unit to do much more than it did. Dallas scored on every single offensive possession of the afternoon, minus the final kneel down, totaling an eye-popping 51 points.
