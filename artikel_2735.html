CSC Reacts Survey: What’s the biggest obstacle facing the Saints?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Following Sunday's 21-13 loss to the Chicago Bears, the New Orleans Saints have fallen to 3-7 on the season. While the team has shown flashes of potential under head coach Kellen Moore, their inability to consistently put together a complete performance on both sides of the ball remains a serious concern.

Through ten games, the Saints are averaging just 21.5 points per game while allowing 25.7. Offensive struggles, defensive inconsistencies, numerous injuries, lackluster playcalling, and costly turnovers have all contributed to the team's disappointing season.

With seven games still remaining, the Saints have opportunities to address some of their shortcomings and build a stronger foundation for the future. However, there are still several areas that need improvement before New Orleans can establish itself as a legitimate playoff contender.

So, in this week's reacts poll, we asked our readers: What's the biggest obstacle preventing the Saints from becoming playoff contenders?
