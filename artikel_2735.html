Dolphins news: Injuries, roster changes, and preparation for Colts in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Miami Dolphins are preparing for their Week 10 game against the Indianapolis Colts, which kicks off at 1 p.m. Eastern on Sunday. The game features two teams in contention for the first overall pick in the 2027 NFL Draft, though the Colts do not hold a first-round pick this year. Instead, as part of their trade for cornerback Sauce Gardner last year, that pick belongs to the New York Jets. Could this game decide who holds the keys to the draft in the spring?

This game will also feature the Dolphins making multiple roster adjustments after injuries to quarterback Malik Willis and defensive tackle Zach Sieler. Kyle McCord is expected to start in Willis's place, his first regular-season opportunity after the Philadelphia Eagles selected him in the sixth round in 2025. Willis is out for at least four weeks, though season-ending surgery is still possible if his goin injury does not respond to rest.

Sieler tore his biceps muscle on the first play of the team's Week 9 game against the Detroit Lions, but played through the injury for the remainder of the game. He will need surgery and around six months of recovery time before he is cleared to return to football activities.

The Dolphins may also be without starting center Aaron Brewer, who missed Wednesday's practice with a concussion. He will have to clear the NFL's concussion protocol before he is cleared to play.
