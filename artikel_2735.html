Winners and Losers from the Denver Broncos 27-5 loss to the Pittsburgh Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Broncos were dominated by the Steelers. The offense had its worst overall effort in a long time; Nix had three turnovers, with two of them leading to Steelers touchdowns, and they only managed 5 points, with two of those points coming from an Alex Singleton safety.

It was all bad. The defense gave up plays to Mason Rudolph, Lutz missed a field goal, receivers dropped the ball, the offensive line was dominated, the run game was nonexistent, and the Broncos simply embarrassed themselves on the national stage once again.

Here are your losers from the Broncos' 27-5 loss to the Steelers.

Nix was bad. Like, really bad. Three turnovers (as of this writing) and two of them went for touchdowns. He just dropped the ball, and it was scooped up for a Steelers touchdown; then he threw an ugly pick-six and later threw another on third and forever.

Awful showing. Your least favorite Bo Nix hater is going to be loud.

It wasn't just Nix; the entire offense was bad. The offensive line was dominated all game; they had no run game, and receivers were dropping balls.

This falls on everyone. Sean Payton, the entire offensive coaching staff, Nix, and the entire offense.

Embarrassing and unacceptable. Worst showing from the Payton/Nix era by far.

A loss like this falls on the coaching staff.

Short week, road game, and against the team going through a lot on and off the field. You need to be prepared and not fall into a trap game, and yet that is what happened.

They looked unprepared, played sloppily, were dominated in the trenches, had no answer for their blitz plays, and looked like the team that imploded earlier in the week.

One of the worst and most embarrassing losses in the Sean Payton era.

Bolles gave up a sack, Peart isn't it, Meinerz was flagged, McGlinchey was owned by Watt. Pressure instantly got into the backfield multiple times; they had no room to run the ball, and certainly did not look like the the elite unit they were billed as.

The WRs continued to drop passes. Can't have it. Waddle had one, Sutton had at least one, and it has been a consistent and annoying trend for a few seasons now.

Nix had three turnovers, and the entire offense was shit.

Surtain II was burned for a touchdown and later flagged.
