Mavericks vs Clippers Preview and Injury Report: Dallas looks to build at home

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Dallas Mavericks (8-7) look to continue their string of strong play at home when they host the Los Angeles Clippers (4-10) on Saturday night. It's the first game of another weekend back-to-back. Dallas is coming off of a nice win off the floundering Golden State Warriors on Wednesday. The Clippers lost to the San Antonio Spurs on Thursday night as they swing through Texas. Here's the main things you need to know.

WHO: Dallas Mavericks vs Los Angeles Clippers

WHERE: American Airlines Arena, Dallas, Texas

The Mavericks once again have a long injury report. Tarik Biberovic, Sergio De Larrea, Dereck Lively, and Tobi Lawal are all out with various injuries. Walter Clayton Jr. is questionable with Patellar Tendinitis. PJ Washington is doubtful with an illness. The Clippers will be without Bradley Beal, Brandon Ingram, and Jordan Miller.

This is another game Dallas should win easily. The Mavericks won't have to deal with Ingram, who is playing his first game Monday vs the Rockets. Mavs fans will get their first look at Keaton Wagler, a player who many fans hoped fell to Dallas in June's NBA Draft. Matt wrote a longer preview here.
