George Kittle tallies 58 receiving yards in Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

San Francisco 49ers tight end George Kittle registered four receptions for 58 yards on seven targets in Week 9 against the Las Vegas Raiders.

Why it Matters: It wasn't the best game for Kittle, but he led the team in targets. In fact, nobody else had more than four targets, so he is the top banana in the passing offense right now. He remains an unquestioned TE1 in all fantasy leagues.
