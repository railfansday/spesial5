Report: Pedro Marques is at it again, Liverpool now ready to sign South American phenom

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Pedro Marques is at it again as he eyes a move for a South American phenom.

Liverpool are on a mission, and it's as exciting of a mission as you can possibly get.

The Reds want to build the greatest possible team on the planet. In order to do that they want to assemble an empire. Possibly the best empire that has ever been.

Julian Ward is leading the operation, but behind him one of the brains behind the project, is the aforementioned Marques.

He was poached from Benfica in order to bring some of the best young players to Liverpool and he has already done a phenomenal job at that.

Liverpool are already bursting to the brim with exciting young talent and there is more to come.

Liverpool's mission to dominate the youth marketIt all started with Armin Pecsi last year. Liverpool won the race for the Golden Ball nominee's signature and brought him to Anfield. Since then Liverpool have signed Mor Talla Ndiaye, Ifeanyi Ndukwe, Samuel Martinez, Lucca Brughmanns, Djylian N'Guessan. They have taken Sheriff Bah and Musa Jatta on trial and are also expected to sign Sidy Barhama Ndiaye when he turns 18-years-old. It's already a spectacular cohort of talent but there will be more on the way. Liverpool have a strong scouting presence across the globe and they are keeping a close eye on the U17 World Cup at the moment where they will be eyeing several candidates ahead of the January and summer window - just as they did in the past with Ndukwe and Ndiaye.
