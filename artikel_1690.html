Di Marzio: Cagliari could give Milan a ‘buy option’ for Mendy to secure Camarda loan

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Francesco Camarda could join Cagliari on loan in January and as part of the deal, AC Milan could obtain an option for Paul Mendy, according to a report.

There has been a lot of speculation regarding Camarda’s future in the last few weeks. The 18-year-old has played just 119 minutes so far this season and it’s clear that his development isn’t moving in the right direction as a result.

A loan seems to be the most likely option, especially if Milan were to bring in another striker, but the big question is where he should go. After a so-so loan spell at Lecce, the Rossoneri must evaluate carefully before making their choice.

According to Gianluca Di Marzio, Cagliari have expressed a concrete interest in securing Camarda on a loan with an option to buy, leaving a buy-back in favour of Milan. In other words, it would be the same formula that was used when he moved to Lecce.

There is one big difference, though. As per the journalist, the Cagliari striker Paul Mendy could also be involved in the deal. The idea would be to add a ‘buy option’ for Milan, presumably giving them priority to sign the player for a pre-agreed fee.

The 19-year-old has bagged two goals and two assists for the Sardinian side so far, attracting the interest of several clubs (and not just Milan).
