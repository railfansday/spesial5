Georgia’s Kirby Smart shares special moment with his mom after beating South Carolina

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Georgia's Kirby Smart shares special moment with his mom after beating South Carolina appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Kirby Smart and his Georgia football team clinched a record sixth straight trip to the SEC championship game with a sound 34-6 beating of South Carolina. It was their fourth win in a row, all over ranked teams. Following that historic win, reporter Caroline Soro was able to catch Smart sharing a wholesome moment with his mother on the field.

Smart walked up to his mother and gave her a hearty embrace and kiss before going up to the Georgia fans who made the trip to Columbia and celebrating the win. Georgia fans had a lot to celebrate once again as their team was outstanding on both sides of the ball.

The Georgia offense, led by quarterback Gunner Stockton, cut the Gamecocks open. Stockton completed 19 of 23 passes for 281 yards and one touchdown, while running back Nate Frazier had 94 yards and two touchdowns. Frazier is having a fantastic end to the season. Over his last four games, he has 340 yards on 65 carries (5.2 yards per carry) with six touchdowns.

Meanwhile, the defense continued its absolute dominance, limiting LaNorris Sellers and the SC offense to 218 total yards of offense. The Georgia defense has now held its opponents to under 10 points for the third time during their four-game win streak.

Georgia will close out the regular season against state rivals Georgia Tech before heading to the SEC championship game the following week. Then it's on to the College Football Playoff, where the Bulldogs figure to be the overwhelming favorite given how dominant they have been in recent weeks.
