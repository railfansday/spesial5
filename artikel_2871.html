Steelers sign former Seahawks safety

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Pittsburgh Steelers have signed former Seattle Seahawks and Los Angeles Chargers safety AJ Finley to their practice squad, the team announced today.

Finley signed with the Chargers in 2023 as an undrafted free agent out of Ole Miss, where he would go on to appear in 12 games as a rookie. He was waived in November, 2024 and immediately picked up by the Seattle Seahawks. Ever since, he has been on the Seahawks practice squad, appearing in four games in 2025 and three games in 2026. In 29 career games, he has 19 tackles and one forced fumble.

Pittsburgh has been thin at safety all season due to the ongoing injury issues with DeShon Elliott, who missed Week 10 after hurting his knee in warmups. That has left Jaquan Brisker, Robert Spears-Jennings, and Jalen Ramsey in a hybrid role as the only safeties at the Steelers' disposal.
