Dolphins quiet through NFL trade deadline 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Miami Dolphins were projected to be sellers at the NFL's 2026 trade deadline, but with the 4 p.m. Eastern deadline now come and gone, Miami stood pat with their roster. While most teams that start a season 1-7 would be considered a disappointment and in need of a rebuild, Miami has already embraced the restart the franchise has needed and shed most of their veteran players before the season. Those that are on the roster were retained for a reason and, with the deadline now passed, they will hold those roles throughout the remainder of the year.

Speculation had focused on Jordyn Brooks. The All-Pro linebacker could have provided a team with a much-needed upgrade, especially if they need an injury replacement. Teams like the Detroit Lions and Dallas Cowboys were believed to be interested in Brooks.

Defensive tackle Zach Sieler was another potential trade asset, but both the Dolphins and Sieler seemed to want to keep the veteran in South Florida. News on Tuesday that Sieler sustained a torn biceps muscle during the Dolphins' Week 9 loss to the Detroit Lions ruled out any possible trade. Miami will now have to decide if they are going to keep Sieler beyond this year, especially with a salary cap number expected to balloon to $20.8 million next year compared to this season's $11.0 million.

The Dolphins could be stingy with any offer they received this year, putting a premium on all of their players and expecting high draft picks in return. The veterans that could be targets for other teams seem to be players Miami plans to keep beyond this year. There was no pressure to move on from a player, agreeing to every trade offer sent, and, ultimately, Miami chose not to make any deal.

The day was not completely silent in Miami, however, as a groin injury for quarterback Malik Willis and a torn biceps muscle for defensive tackle Zach Sieler landed both veterans on injured reserve.
