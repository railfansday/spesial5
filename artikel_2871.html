Potential landing spots for Daniel Jones after $50 million Colts disaster

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Colts announced the release of quarterback Daniel Jones on Friday following another disastrous performance in a 26-3 loss to the Texans on Thursday night.

But he might not be waiting all that long to land his next gig.

The eight-year pro will hit waivers Friday afternoon with the opportunity to sign with any team if he's not claimed by Monday.

Will Anderson Jr. #51 of the Houston Texans sacks Daniel Jones #17 of the Indianapolis Colts for a safety in Houston, Texas, Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026. Getty Images

Jones, alongside All-Pro running back Jonathan Taylor, was one of two key figures who were the subject of trade deadline speculation as the Colts begin their latest rebuilding process.

According to ESPN's Adam Schefter, the Ravens made an offer to acquire the signalcaller around the deadline before turning to Jacoby Brissett. Brissett threw for 340 yards and one interception without a passing touchdown in a 23-20 loss to the Chargers, and it remains to be seen if Baltimore would want to bring in another veteran passer.

The Vikings also expressed interest in Jones before bringing back Kirk Cousins.

Teams whose quarterback rooms need help include the Cardinals and Patriots.

If Jones wants to be a starter in the NFL this year, Arizona may be his best bet. The Cardinals are in dire need of quarterback help, with Carson Beck questionable for this week's matchup and the team preparing to start 40-year-old Josh Johnson against the Chiefs.

The Patriots may also be looking for a new starter under center. The team announced Friday that Drake Maye is out indefinitely with a fractured fibula, leaving former Giant Tommy DeVito as the likely starter going forward.

Indianapolis Colts quarterback Daniel Jones talks to the media following an NFL football game against the Houston Texans in Houston, Nov. 19, 2026. AP Photo/David J. Phillip

The release comes at a premium as Jones signed a two-year, $88 million deal heading into the season. The historic deal also included nearly $50 million guaranteed and a maximum value of $100 million.

In his final game with Indianapolis, Jones completed 12 of 27 passes for 144 yards and an interception as the Colts (3-8) have now lost six of their last seven games.

Through 11 appearances this season, Jones has thrown for 1,974 yards, seven touchdowns and 13 interceptions, an NFL high. He also posted a 65.4 passer rating while completing 58.4 percent of his passes, both career lows.
