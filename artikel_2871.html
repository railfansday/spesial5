Daniel Jones makes unwanted NFL history no quarterback has matched in 45 years

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Indianapolis Colts quarterback Daniel Jones has put together the kind of season the NFL hasn't seen in 45 years, and the latest dud in a 26-3 loss at Houston left his starting job in doubt.

Doug Clawson of CBS Sports pointed out that Jones is the only quarterback in that span to start each of his team's first 11 games without a single game of more touchdown passes than interceptions.

Jones completed 12 of 27 passes for 144 yards and an interception on Thursday Night Football and was pulled for Anthony Richardson Sr. with under 10 minutes to play. The Colts finished with a season-low 167 yards, failed to score a touchdown for the first time since 2024 and fell to 3-8.

OptaSTATS found that the Colts are the only NFL team in the Super Bowl era to allow a safety, throw an interception, miss multiple field goals, allow multiple sacks, score zero touchdowns, finish under 200 total yards and convert under 10% of their third downs in the same game. Texans edge rusher Will Anderson Jr. sacked Jones for a third-quarter safety. Colts kicker Spencer Shrader missed from 56 yards, and his 62-yard try was blocked before halftime. Indianapolis also went 1 of 11 on third down.

"I understand where we are as a team. I understand it's an issue to score three points," Jones said after the game.

Jones has seven touchdown passes and an NFL-high 13 interceptions through 11 starts. His 58.4% completion rate and 5.6 yards per attempt are both career lows, and Thursday marked his fifth straight game under 200 passing yards.

Even the Week 10 win over Miami offered little comfort. Jones went 11 of 22 for 108 yards with two interceptions, including a pick-six, and completed one pass beyond the line of scrimmage in the second half. His QBR in that game was the lowest by any starting quarterback this season.

"I think, obviously, I've got to do a better job taking care of the ball, keeping us on track and finding more completions," Jones said afterward.

The slide is stark for a passer who threw 19 touchdowns against eight interceptions in 2025 and helped Indianapolis to an 8-2 start before a fractured fibula and a torn Achilles derailed his season. The Colts rewarded that run with a two-year, $88 million contract in March, but they're getting the version of Jones the Giants gave up on.

The empty possessions look familiar to anyone who watched the former Duke star in New York. He threw zero touchdowns and eight interceptions over six straight home starts between 2023 and 2024, the first starter to go six consecutive home games without a touchdown pass since Trent Dilfer in 1995-96.

He also entered an October 2024 Monday night game at Pittsburgh with a 1-15 record in prime time and eight straight prime-time games without a touchdown pass.

Colts coach Shane Steichen said, "Yeah, right now Daniel Jones is our quarterback," after the Miami game. After Houston, when asked about benching Jones, he said, "We'll evaluate everything."

The Giants visit the Colts on Nov. 29 at Lucas Oil Stadium in what would be Jones' first game against the team that drafted him.
