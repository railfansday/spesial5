Recap: Wizards mercifully win 103-99 vs. Raptors

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Washington Wizards finally got back on the win column on Wednesday in a 103-99 contest versus the Toronto Raptors at Scotiabank Arena.

The Wizards ended their losing streak before it reached nine. However, it was hard to feel good about the performance, given how shorthanded the Raptors were coming in. Toronto played without Kawhi Leonard, Scottie Barnes, and RJ Barrett, and also lost Collin Murray-Boyles before halftime due to illness.

The Wizards started things the way most fans would have wanted. A pair of AJ Dybantsa dunks helped Washington race to a 12-6 lead early. Anthony Davis feasted in the interior, scoring 10 early points and grabbing 7 rebounds. The good guys led 29-23 after one.

Washington kept showing a lack of discipline on defense. The team repeatedly made the right rotations for 15 to 20 seconds before allowing timely cuts or extra passes to burn them. Our guys were putting in the work but clocking out before the shift was over.

The Wizards also gave up 9 offensive rebounds and committed 10 turnovers through two quarters. The last giveaway came via a sloppy pass from Trae Young to Davis that led to a Raptors three-pointer to beat the halftime buzzer. The team was fortunate to be up 49-47 entering the break.

Young was invisible for most of the game. He didn't score his first field goal until he connected on a 32-foot three-pointer at the 1:11 mark of the third quarter to give Washington a 76-73 lead.

The Wizards also failed to take advantage of the Raptors committing their fourth team foul with over 8 minutes left in the third. Washington only earned bonus free throws in the final minute. The Wizards trailed 79-78 entering the fourth.

If there's one positive in this game, it was seeing how much fight Dybantsa displayed to get the victory. He scored 9 emphatic points in the final period, flashing the top-shelf athleticism that made him worthy of the No. 1 overall selection.

The Wizards built up a 10-point lead with 2:49 to play, finally pulling away from a Raptors team far inferior to them on paper. A sloppy finish to this one gave Toronto a chance to tie the game in the final seconds, but Washington survived for the 4-point win.

I'd advise any Wizards fan considering watching the game on delay to steer clear of the final three minutes for your own sanity. Stick to Dybantsa highlights on YouTube.

AJ finished with a career-high 23 points and notched his first NBA double-double with 12 rebounds. Davis led all scorers with 25 points, while Kyshawn George poured in his usual 20 points and 4 threes.

The Wizards head to Chicago next for a rematch with the Bulls on Friday night.
