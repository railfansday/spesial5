Arsenal&#8217;s Six-Point Lead Survives as Brighton Stunned by Hull

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Arsenal may have been left frustrated by Saturday's 2-2 draw against Newcastle United, but the weekend's results have ensured that Mikel Arteta's side remain six points clear at the top of the Premier League.

Manchester City and Brighton both failed to capitalise on Arsenal dropping points, leaving the defending champions in a commanding position after 11 league matches.

Manchester City had the first opportunity to put pressure on Arsenal, hosting Fulham at the Etihad Stadium in Saturday's early kick-off.

However, City were held to a 1-1 draw after Rodrigo Muniz gave Fulham the lead before Elliot Anderson equalised for the hosts. Despite having time to complete the comeback, City were unable to find a winner.

Arsenal then travelled to St James' Park knowing victory would extend their advantage to eight points.

Eberechi Eze put the Gunners ahead in the 33rd minute, but Newcastle responded strongly after half-time.

Harvey Barnes equalised before Malick Thiaw gave Newcastle the lead, leaving Arsenal facing defeat until Noni Madueke struck to secure a 2-2 draw.

Arteta was frustruated with the result, particularly after Arsenal's first-half dominance, but City's failure to win meant the Gunners had nevertheless maintained their advantage over one of their closest rivals.

Attention turned to Brighton on Sunday afternoon, with Fabian Hürzeler's side knowing victory at Hull City would reduce Arsenal's advantage to just three points.

Instead, Brighton suffered a dramatic 2-0 defeat at the MKM Stadium.

As reported by Reuters, Brighton dominated large periods but were reduced to ten men when João Costinha received a second yellow card.

Hull then struck twice in stoppage time through Oli McBurnie, securing a memorable victory and leaving Brighton six points behind Arsenal.

The results leave Arsenal top with 28 points, ahead of Brighton and Manchester City, who both have 22.

Liverpool are another side who have joined the conversation at the top. They began Sunday's meeting with Manchester United on 18 points, ten behind Arsenal.

Liverpool beat United 4-0 – after United were reduced to ten men when Yoro received a red card early on – moving them up to fourth, on 21 points, seven behind the Gunners.

Arsenal now face Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League on Tuesday before next Sunday's huge Premier League meeting with Manchester City at the Emirates.

As we discussed ahead of the Newcastle fixture, the Gunners entered this demanding run of matches with an opportunity to strengthen their position at the top.

Despite dropping two points on Saturday, their six-point cushion remains intact.
