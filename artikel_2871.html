Trent McDuffie, Colby Parkinson listed as questionable for the Rams

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The biggest injury news for the Rams this week is that defensive end Myles Garrett is set to make his first appearance since Week 1 of the regular season, but it is not the only injury news.

Cornerback Trent McDuffie is listed as questionable to play against the Commanders due to a back injury that kept him out of practice all week. The Rams had already announced that they are going to be without cornerback Jaylen Watson due to a shoulder injury.

Tight end Colby Parkinson (ribs) and safety Kam Curl (foot) both returned to practice on Friday as limited participants. They are both listed as questionable to play as well.

Linebacker Grant Stuard (hamstring) and wide receiver Jordan Whittington (back) round out the players in that category. Long snapper Joe Cardona (ankle) is considered doubtful and head coach Sean McVay said that tight end Terrance Ferguson (ankle) will be activated from injured reserve along with Garrett.
