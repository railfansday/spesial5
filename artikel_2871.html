3 bold predictions for Indiana football at Washington

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Indiana football is set for the big trip out west to take on Washington on Saturday afternoon at Husky Stadium in Seattle, Washington.

The Hoosiers, 9-1 on the season, are looking to lock up bids in the College Football Playoff and Big Ten Championship Game with games against the Huskies and Purdue on deck. Indiana remains in great shape should it take care of business.

Khobie Martin showed something the running backs room really hasn't in quite a few weeks and that's multiple, substantial chunk carries against USC. With Turbo Richard out against Washington, someone's going to need to take over as the lead back.

Lee Beebe Jr. has been reliable this season and helped Indiana beat Northwestern, but Martin really showed what he can do against the Trojans, rushing for 6.8 yards per carry and a touchdown with a long of 37 on the afternoon.

Indiana's had enough trouble in the run game lately even before the injury to Joe Brunner. If Martin can produce in spite of that, he's the guy in the backfield.

Throwing the ball to Charlie Becker is, by far and away, Indiana's best offense right now.

The Hoosiers have to rely on an explosive passing game more than ever with a lack of efficiency through the air or on the ground, and Becker is as good as it gets at that. He finished with three receptions for 75 yards against USC, the first game Indiana had to tweak its offense to account for a change in quarterback for.

I think Wilson ends up looking his way downfield pretty often, either hoping he hauls it in or forcing the Washington defensive backs to stay honest to try to create some additional room for the running game.

Which brings us to this. Washington is going to know the deep ball is coming.

Ryan Walters' tenure at Purdue was obviously very, very ill-fated, but he's still a bright defensive mind who knows how to put together a strong defense, particularly on the back end. Defensive backs have played well under Walters before, and this year's Huskies have already caught a few interceptions.

If Wilson, who had a few near-misses against USC, has to test that group, I think they come down with one, at the very least. Indiana has dealt with this before and emerged relatively unscathed, but it's going to be a factor in the game today, I think.
