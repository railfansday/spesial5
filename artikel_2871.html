NFL markets measure different outcomes, from the winner to an individual player's statistics. Confirm the market and scoring period before comparing prices. These examples use hypothetical lines.

Moneylines back a team to win outright. At -150, a winning $15 cash wager earns $10 profit and returns the $15 stake.

Point spreads apply a scoring handicap. A team at -3.5 must win by four or more points. A three-point win at -3 generally produces a push, returning the stake.

Game totals concern the teams' combined points. Over 44.5 requires at least 45. Check whether the market covers the full game, a half or a quarter.

Team totals concern one team's points. A team total over 23.5 requires that team to score at least 24, regardless of its opponent's score.

Player props concern individual statistics, such as passing yards or receptions. Over 250.5 passing yards requires at least 251. Participation and injury rules affect settlement.

Futures concern outcomes decided later, such as a division winner or Super Bowl champion. Read which games count toward season win totals.
