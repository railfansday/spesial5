‘Game of Thrones’ Creator’s Box Office Bomb Officially Makes Streaming History

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Best known for his work writing A Song of Ice and Fire, the series of novels that inspired the hit HBO series, Game of Thrones, George R. R. Martin is also involved in plenty of other important projects. Martin wrote the acclaimed series of short stories around the characters known as Dunk and Egg that inspired the second Game of Thrones spin-off, A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms, which is set to return in the first half of 2027 with its second season. He even penned A Song of Fire and Blood, which was the inspiration behind the first Game of Thrones spin-off, House of the Dragon, which is returning in 2028 with its final season. He even produces the hit AMC+ Western series, Dark Winds, which has quietly become one of streaming's most-watched shows in the last few years.

Video game fans also recognize Martin's name as one of the writers behind one of the best games ever made, Elden Ring, which is being adapted into a feature film in 2028 by Alex Garland. It will be far from the first time that one of Martin's stories has made it to the big screen, though, but fans can only hope that it will be received more warmly than his 2025 fantasy bomb, In the Lost Lands, starring Dave Bautista and Milla Jovovich. In the Lost Lands came into theaters carrying a $55 million budget, meaning it needed over $100 million to break even, but it grossed only $6 million during its entire run. It has since found a streaming home on Hulu in America and on Prime Video globally, where it has now been in the top 10 for over 100 days.

In the Lost Lands star Dave Bautista has taken on some important roles over the years, including Drax in the Guardians of the Galaxy universe. News broke earlier this year that he had been cast as Kratos in Prime Video's God of War series, and he's been training for the role, posting that he's added 20 pounds of muscle since being cast. Ryan Hurst was previously set to star as Kratos in the show, but after he suffered an injury during a stunt on set, production was halted and Bautista was brought in to replace him.

Check out In the Lost Lands on Hulu in America and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of George R. R. Martin's future projects.
