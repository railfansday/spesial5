Does Marvel's VisionQuest Have A Post-Credits Scene? A Spoiler-Free Guide

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The end of a trilogy that started back in 2020 is upon us. The "WandaVision" trilogy is one of the most significant pieces of Marvel's Multiverse Saga, focusing on Wanda Maximoff and Vision's complicated relationship and the characters who are connected to them. With "VisionQuest" reaching its conclusion, the trilogy has been capped off. But is it all over once the credits start to roll on the final episode? Or is there more to it?

This series brings back Paul Bettany as Vision for what is presumably one last ride. It also brings back James Spader as Ultron more than a decade later. How exactly is Ultron alive in "VisionQuest?" That's one of many questions the show has answered during its run.

The Marvel Cinematic Universe is notorious for including post-credits scenes in its movies and TV shows. Some of them have been very good and consequential, such as Thanos arriving in "The Avengers." Some of Marvel's worst post-credits scenes? Not so good. Whatever shape they take, it's always reasonable to wonder whether or not a given MCU project contains a credits scene or two.

That brings us back to the "VisonQuest" season finale. We're here to offer up a spoiler-free guide to the show's credits scene situation. Do you need to stick around? Or can you find something else to watch once the credits roll? Seriously, we're not going to spoil anything significant. We're just here to arm viewers with necessary information. With that out of the way, let's get into it.

Yes, "VisionQuest" does have a post-credits scene that fans should be aware of. In fact, there are two scenes. One of the scenes is important enough that viewers who made it all the way through the series shouldn't skip it. It helps put a button on the events that transpired in the show. Without spoiling anything, it's not a throwaway gag or anything like that. In fact, go ahead and watch both scenes. It's worth it.

This shouldn't come as a huge surprise. "WandaVision" also had a pretty significant credits scene. The "WandaVision" post-credits scene was sneakily altered by Marvel Studios, though that's another conversation entirely. Still, it stands to reason that the finale to this trilogy would include a credits scene as well.

This show had a lot to accomplish and was rather ambitious. "VisionQuest" explained the biggest plot hole from "Avengers: Age of Ultron," and also had to serve as a satisfying end for a character who has been with the MCU since its earliest days. No pressure.

Christopher J. Byrne, Gandja Monteiro, Vincenzo Natali, and Terry Matalas directed episodes of the series. The cast also includes Ruaridh Mollica (Tommy Maximoff), Todd Stashwick (Paladin), T'Nia Miller (Jocasta), Lauren Morais (Lisa Molinari), Orla Brady (F.R.I.D.A.Y.), Diane Morgan (The Handler), Polly Frame (Fiona Guthrie), Henry Lewis (D.U.M.-E.), Jonathan Sayer (U.), Emily Hampshire (E.D.I.T.H.), and James D'Arcy (J.A.R.V.I.S.).

"VisionQuest" is streaming now on Disney+.
