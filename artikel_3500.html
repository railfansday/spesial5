GTA 6‘s First Post-Launch Update Fixes 7 Bugs

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Four days after launch, Grand Theft Auto 6 is getting its first post-release update. The patch is aimed at fixing some crashes on console and various bugs players have encountered. It’s small but no doubt appreciated for anyone who’s encountered the issues in question.

Fixed several issues that caused the game to crash

Fixed an issue that resulted in the weather not changing for extended periods

Fixed an issue that resulted in the game not autosaving correctly

Fixed several issues that resulted [in] phone calls not triggering correctly including those that progressed the story or unlocked mission content

Fixed an issue that resulted in the players being unable to lose their Wanted Level

Fixed an issue that resulted in the player being unable to use the deadlift bar in the Gym

Fixed [an issue] that resulted in partners not handing over bags so that players could sell them to fences

Rockstar Games doesn’t call it out by name, but some players on PlayStation 5 were reporting getting crashes with the error code CE-108255-1. It’s unclear if those will be resolved now. Another pesky issue had to do with autosaves. Some players were finding that the game wasn’t autosaving as often as they expected. It seems like at least some of that was due to a glitch Rockstar has now addressed. Other fixes are clearly for much more isolated issues, like unresponsive weights at the gym.

This is the first of what will likely be a stream of post-launch patches tweaking the game. We’ll see how much the updates in these first couple of weeks stick to bug fixes, or whether Rockstar will start re-balancing the underlying game or begin to address some of the player complaints around things like ATMs and the wanted profile interactions.
