Get the Spark 1.5% Cash Select if your business needs 0% for 9 months on a big purchase or puts less than about $19,000 a year on its card. It pays 1.5% back with no annual fee. Spend more than that, and a Capital One card paying 2% earns more even after its fee.

Capital One aims the card, which its pages also call the Spark Cash Select, at business owners with excellent credit who want a flat cash back rate on everything they buy. Owners who’d rather earn travel miles get the same earning rates from the VentureOne Business card.

It suits owners who’ll pay off a big purchase during the interest-free intro period, and owners who spend less than $19,000 a year on purchases that earn 1.5%. An extra 0.5% earns $95 on $19,000 a year, which covers the Spark 2% Cash’s fee, and $150 on $30,000, which covers the fee on the Spark Cash Plus, for owners who pay in full each month.
