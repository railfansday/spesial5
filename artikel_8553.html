Choose Travelex’s Ultimate plan for a family trip. It covers one child 17 or younger at no extra cost with each insured adult and lets you add upgrades such as cancel for any reason. If you travel alone and want the lowest price, compare quotes from several insurers.

With Ultimate’s kids-included pricing, two parents and two kids pay the base premium for the parents alone, as long as each child’s trip cost is no more than a parent’s. Travelex shows prices only in a quote, but travel insurance generally costs 4% to 8% of a trip’s total cost, according to the U.S. Travel Insurance Association.

Families with children 17 or younger get the most from Travelex by buying Ultimate within 21 days of the first trip payment, which its pre-existing condition coverage and cancel for any reason both require. A solo traveler who wants the lowest price should first check whether the card they booked with already covers a canceled trip.
