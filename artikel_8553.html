That's $237k more than the $1.5m you estimated in your first post, and if you're actually able to sell the house for $3m, it would be even more. And are 6% commissions really standard in your area? In many areas (especially places where houses are $3m), commissions demanded by realtors are less, and with some savvy you can negotiate it down further, and/or use a discount agency. Also there might be a way to keep your capital gains under the 15% and NIIT thresholds via an installment sale. The way I understand it, an insurance company acts as an intermediary in the sale. They take the cash from the buyer and issue you a fixed-term annuity that pays out over 2 or more years. The capital gains are therefore spread out over multiple tax years. I have no idea how complicated or risky or expensive this is, but it might be something to look into if you do decide to sell.
