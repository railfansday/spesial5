Canada’s National Bank Discloses XRP Position Through Bitwise

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Despite the slowed performance witnessed across the XRP ETF market in recent weeks, it appears that interest in the investment product remains intact especially through its largest issuer, Bitwise.

In a recent 13F filings with the U.S. SEC, the national bank of Canada, revealed it recently joined the XRP ETF ecosystem, showcasing its exposure to XRP through the Bitwise XRP ETF.

Although the bank did not disclose the specific amount it had invested in the asset, the filing has triggered discussions as it highlights XRP’s growing exposure among institutional investors.

Notably, the Form 13F the bank filed with the SEC covers the third quarter of 2026, which ended on September 30. This suggests that it had recently joined the XRP ecosystem despite the prolonged market weakness.

Unlike regular investors who purchase XRP directly from exchanges and hold in their wallets, the bank invested in XRP through Bitwise's fund.

With its XRP ETF offering, Bitwise allows traditional investors to gain exposure to XRP without having to manage the crypto asset themselves.

Although XRP has continued to see major institutions and banks join its ETF ecosystem, participation in the XRP funds have slowed significantly following the ongoing market volatility.

While the exposure showcased by the major Canadian bank shows growing XRP ETF adoption, it doesn’t change the fact that institutional traders are beginning to take caution as they have just recorded the highest weekly withdrawals since March 2026.

The bank’s XRP exposure has triggered discussions as it shows that it has retained its position till September 30 despite the persisting market correction.
