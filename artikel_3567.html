Grandma Shot Dead by Daughter's 'Stalker' After He Broke Into Family's Home

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

A grandmother was fatally shot inside her Tennessee home after her daughter’s alleged stalker broke in while demanding to know where her daughter was.

The Tennessee Bureau of Investigation announced on Wednesday, November 25, that Bradley Jackson was charged with first-degree murder in the death of Susan Burton, as well as aggravated assault and aggravated burglary.

Jackson, 35, was booked into the Trousdale County Jail, where he is currently being held without bond.

The incident took place on Tuesday, November 24, around 9 a.m. At the time, Burton, 62, was at her home in Hartsville, Tennessee, with her daughter, her son, her son’s girlfriend and her grandchildren.

“It is with the heaviest heart a child could ever have that I am making this GoFundMe for my mom who was murdered,” Burton’s daughter, Amanda Goode, wrote in a GoFundMe page set up following the tragedy. “My stalker, whose order of protection just expired, broke into my mom’s home, terrorized my family and murdered my mom.”

Goode said that Jackson allegedly pulled into the family’s driveway “and blocked their only escape before making his way around the back of the house.”

Jackson then reportedly went into Goode’s little brother’s room, and his girlfriend “woke up to her dogs barking just to open the door and see a strange man.”

The defendant allegedly “demanded to know” where Goode was, and he “pointed the gun” in the girlfriend’s face. Jackson then allegedly suggested he could shoot her “in the eye” if she didn’t cooperate.

Goode said that her younger sister heard dogs barking and “ran quietly to wake up our mom and told her she heard a man over there who said he had a gun.”

Burton then reportedly confronted the man. When Jackson told her he had a gun, Burton reportedly responded, “So?”

Burton – who was unarmed – then turned to leave the room as Jackson pulled out a gun and shot her in the back.

“She was laid on the cold hard ground where she fell out of the back door,” Goode wrote, adding that Jackson would not let the girlfriend help Burton “while she laid there and bled out.”

As one family member called 911, Jackson allegedly took Burton’s phone to try to locate Goode.

Police later arrived at the home and arrested Jackson after they found evidence that he was responsible for “shooting and killing Burton and threatening another woman in the home.”

It’s not currently clear whether Jackson has entered a plea or retained legal counsel following his arrest. The Tennessee Bureau of Investigation had no additional updates to share when contacted by Us about the case.
