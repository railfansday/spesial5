Investing - Theory, News &amp; General • Re: Market-cap weighting and company-specific risk

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

It is good to wonder when one philandering CEO resigns today and one of the largest AI sector leading companies stock loses 100's of Billions in value in a day... dragging down Total market holding ETFs that are very heavily weighted in these tech companies.

Could it be portending of an AI induced bubble based on too much speculation?

May be a fair warning as to why not to blindly put the majority of your investments into a lopsided basket, i.e. Total market funds weighted with so much percentage of tech, perhaps.
A total market fund is always weighted toward the biggest companies and sectors. Isn't being lopsided in that way just the nature of the market? I'm not blind to, or uncomfortable with, that reality regarding my US equity allocation, and I wouldn't want to underweight tech given the world that we're living in. Company and sector leadership has changed more times than I can remember since I started investing over forty years ago, and I'm always happy owning the cap weighted market. Even as big names that have fallen out of favor, other companies have risen to replace them. Owning them all has worked out well for me.
