Advantage Checking is the highest-paying checking account at Presidential Bank, a Bethesda, Maryland, bank that opens accounts online nationwide. The seven electronic withdrawals it requires each month can be bill payments and automatic debits, not only debit card purchases and ATM withdrawals, so it suits someone who pays most bills online and rarely swipes a card.
