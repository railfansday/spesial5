Democrats won control of Congress. So what does that mean for the Supreme Court?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Type to search articles, cases, and authors.

Democrats won big in Tuesday’s midterm elections, flipping at least 40 seats in the House and at least seven in the Senate. Come January, the Democratic Party will control both houses of Congress, which will allow it to set policymaking priorities and play a lead role in shaping the future of the Supreme Court.

Most immediately, the power shift in the Senate will complicate (if not effectively eliminate) President Donald Trump’s ability to appoint another justice to the Supreme Court, should a vacancy arise after the new Congress is seated. The Democratic Party will have enough votes to reject a nomination – if it even allows confirmation hearings to proceed.

Kelsey Dallas is SCOTUSblog's managing editor and the primary author of the SCOTUStoday newsletter.
