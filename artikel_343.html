Pornhub traffic plunged on GTA 6 release day

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Given that Grand Theft Auto VI broke streaming records, it only makes sense that players are spending their time gaming rather than on other hobbies. You know, like gardening, crafting, or watching porn.

At least with the latter, we have some data to prove it: Pornhub's U.S. traffic nosedived 15.5 percent just an hour after the game dropped last Thursday.

Just hours before, at 10 p.m. ET, the tube site's traffic was up 16.5 percent compared to an average day. Maybe players were bored and trying to kill time before the anticipated release. It started to drop an hour later, to 9.9 percent above average, and by midnight it dipped 7.5 percent below average. That night and into the morning, Pornhub traffic fluctuated — perhaps a time zone thing, perhaps gamers needed a break.

At 8 a.m. Thursday morning, traffic was 6.4 percent below average, but later in the day it cruised just above average again.

GTA 6 also proves that Rule 34 (if it exists, there's porn of it) is going strong. Searches for GTA-related terms skyrocketed 16,196 percent on Nov. 19, with a staggering 15 million searches since Nov. 18, according to Pornhub.

What exactly are viewers searching? The term "gta 6" itself is far and away the top, followed by "gta 6 lucia" (the female protagonist) and "gta 6 strip club / tacon" (slang for strip club).

It seems you can take the gamers out of Vice City, but you can't take Vice City out of the gamers.
