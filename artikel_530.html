The Pokémon TCG Delta Reign Booster Display Box is down in price at Amazon — buy 36 packs for $250

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Delta Reign Booster Display Box is available to preorder for $249.99 at Amazon, down from the $269.99 price we first spotted in October.

The Pokémon TCG Delta Reign expansion is only a few days away from release, and after several weeks of fluctuating preorder deals, collectors can once again grab 36 packs for less than $7 each before the new Mega Evolution set drops.

As of Nov. 3, the Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Delta Reign Booster Display Box is available to preorder for just $249.99 at Amazon. That's exactly $20 less than the $269.99 price we covered when preorders first went live on Oct. 8, and one of the lowest-ever deals for the box (confirmed via price tracker camelcamelcamel). It works out to $6.94 for each of its 36 booster packs.

Sold and fulfilled by third-party seller House of Cards and Games, free delivery is included, although your box isn't expected to arrive until Nov. 16. That's 10 days after Delta Reign launches on Nov. 6.

Although this Amazon price is a new low for the Delta Reign display Booster Box, we need to let you know you can spend even less with Walmart and TCGplayer.

Compared with the box’s estimated $227.76 market price, Walmart is selling its own for $229.29. Meanwhile, the dedicated trading card marketplace TCGplayer has listings for slightly less, with the lowest at $227.98 — only 22 cents above market price.

Delta Reign introduces over 140 cards, with Mega Rayquaza ex leading the expansion alongside Mega Golurk ex, Mega Malamar ex, and Mega Golisopod ex. Each booster contains 10 random Pokémon cards, giving you 360 to open across the complete Display Box, excluding Energy and code cards.
