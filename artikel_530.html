The Pokémon TCG Delta Reign Elite Trainer Box is down to market price on release day — shop the best-ever Amazon deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Delta Reign Elite Trainer Box is on sale for $103.23 at Amazon, down from its $137.95 list price.

The Pokémon TCG's Delta Reign expansion has officially arrived, and Amazon sellers have marked the occasion by giving its Rayquaza-themed Elite Trainer Box the biggest price reduction we've ever seen.

As of Nov. 6, the Pokémon TCG’s Delta Reign Elite Trainer Box is available for just $103.23 at Amazon, chopping $34.72 off its typical $137.95 price. That's a huge saving compared to the $140 preorders we spotted in October. It is indeed the lowest price we’ve seen for this ETB (confirmed by our go-to price tracker, camelcamelcamel).

This new price by third-party seller Hero Collect means you’re only paying $11.47 per booster pack before factoring in the accessories. In addition to being $1.72 cheaper than Walmart’s lowest listing of $104.95, this heavily discounted price is only $4.23 above the estimated $99 market price on TCGplayer.

You get nine booster packs from the Pokémon Trading Card Game expansion, an exclusive full-art foil Kyogre promo card, 65 card sleeves, 40 Energy cards, seven dice, a plastic coin, six dividers, a collector's box, a player's guide, and a Pokémon TCG Live code card.
