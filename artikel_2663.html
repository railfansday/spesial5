Dak Prescott on facing Joe Milton: He's still a Cowboy, I hope he helps us out

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

After the Cowboys beat the Titans 51-30 on Sunday, their quarterback Dak Prescott was asked about the team’s Thanksgiving Day opponents.

Prescott was specifically asked about former teammate Joe Milton quarterbacking their NFC East rivals to a win over the Steelers and how he feels about the chance to face his former backup. Milton spent last season in Dallas and was on their practice squad before signing with Eagles in October.

“Very excited. It’ll be fun,” Prescott said. “Joe knows how I feel about it. He’s still a Cowboy and I’m hoping he helps us out.”

Milton probably has a different view on whether or not he’s still a Cowboy and it’s a safe bet that neither he nor the Eagles will be in any mood to help out their longtime adversaries on Thursday afternoon.
