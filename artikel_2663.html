Social media reacts to Michigan's failed Trev Alberts AD hire

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Just as the college football world was starting to turn its attention to LSU, given that the SEC plans to hold proceedings to potentially oust the Tigers from the conference, the University of Michigan said, 'hold my beer.' And this just as it had appeared as if the Wolverines were finally getting their affairs in order.

It's been one dumpster fire after the next in recent years in Ann Arbor, and with the football program struggling under first-year head coach Kyle Whittingham, Michigan fast-tracked a hire of former Nebraska and Texas A&M athletic director Trev Alberts, supposedly in hopes of bringing order from chaos. However, the brass at U-M decided against bringing in Alberts after all, and now, the Wolverines have egg on their face in multiple ways.

First, they'll reportedly owe him upwards of $17 million, just to never set foot in Ann Arbor. But also, the reactionary move indicates that Alberts was presented with a memorandum of understanding and a contract without the school having done due diligence.
