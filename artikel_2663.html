For the Seahawks this year, Drew Lock's numbers blow Sam Darnold's away

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

When Seahawks coach Mike Macdonald confirmed that Sam Darnold will start in Week 12 against the 49ers, assuming he is medically cleared, it was no surprise: The Seahawks signed Darnold to a three-year, $100 million contract last year and won the Super Bowl with Darnold at the helm. Quarterbacks like that don’t often get benched.

But Drew Lock has made it reasonable to question whether Darnold should get benched.

That’s because Lock has played three full games, and most of a fourth, while Darnold has missed time with injuries this season. And Lock hasn’t just been better than Darnold. Lock has been so much better that there’s no comparison between the two.

The Seahawks are 4-0 in the games when Lock was their primary quarterback. They’re 2-4 in the games with Darnold as the primary quarterback.

Of the 37 NFL quarterbacks who have thrown at least 90 passes this season, Lock ranks first with a passer rating of 125.6. Darnold ranks 31st with a passer rating of 79.5.

Lock has still not thrown an interception this season. Darnold has thrown nine.

Lock has completed 73.1 percent of his passes. Darnold has completed 60.2 percent.

Lock is averaging 9.0 yards per pass. Darnold is averaging 7.0 yards per pass.

Lock has been sacked on 5.1 percent of his dropbacks. Darnold has been sacked on 7.5 percent of his dropbacks.

With seven games left in the season, the 6-4 Seahawks are going to have a battle to get back to the playoffs. Macdonald believes Darnold is the best quarterback for the job. The numbers call that into question.
