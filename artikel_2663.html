Browns work out 4 linemen, including 2 former 3rd-round pick Super Bowl champions

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

As the Cleveland Browns begin their preparations for Week 12's Sunday Night Football matchup with the Las Vegas Raiders, their roster continues to have news and, potentially, is in flux. Not only could the 53-man roster see RB Raheim Sanders return from injured reserve, but GM Andrew Berry continues to look at ways to improve different units. On Tuesday, the Browns had four players in for workouts in Berea:

Nnadi and Saunders were both third-round picks, playing multiple years for the Kansas City Chiefs, including Super Bowl victories. Nnadi played 130 games, including 98 starts, with the Chiefs, accumulating 249 tackles, nine QB hits, seven tackles for loss, and five sacks.

Saunders spent four years with Kansas City, two with the New Orleans Saints, and split last year between the New York Jets and Jacksonville Jaguars. In seven seasons, Saunders has 193 tackles, 18 QB hits, eight tackles for loss, and 7.5 sacks.

Stille has spent four seasons with five teams, including six games with the Browns in 2022. In 19 games, the former undrafted free agent has had 17 tackles, three QB hits, two sacks, and one tackle for loss.
