Steelers suddenly have a major mess

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Last night, Steelers fans who weren’t already familiar with the name “Burt Lauten” became instantly acquainted with him, when his name appeared in large, bold print as the person announcing the benching of quarterback Aaron Rodgers. And Lauten will be earning every bit of pay and then some in the coming hours.

Lauten serves as the team’s senior director of communications. He’s basically in charge of P.R. And the Steelers have a P.R. mess unlike any they’ve ever experienced.

The benching of Rodgers has prompted a predictable reaction. Rodgers went straight to the McAfee show and pressed “play” on his various complaints and concerns about coach Mike McCarthy.

It was specific. It was detailed. It was damning. The things Rodgers said created the clear impression that McCarthy should not be coaching the team.

This becomes a problem that goes beyond P.R. strategy. Owner Art Rooney II needs to get involved, determine whether Rodgers’s version is accurate, and take action.

It’s simple. If what Rodgers is saying is remotely true, he should not currently be the coach of the team. If Rodgers isn’t telling the truth, the Steelers need to set the record straight immediately.

However it goes, it’s a mess. A gigantic mess. An unprecedented mess for the Steelers and perhaps for any NFL franchise.
