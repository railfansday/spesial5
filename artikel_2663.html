Jahmyr Gibbs scores again, Lions lead 26-17

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Lions have been thankful to have running back Jahmyr Gibbs in their backfield for many years and he’s giving them more reasons for gratitude this Thursday.

Gibbs scored his second touchdown of the game with 5:45 to play in the third quarter and the Lions moved their lead over the Bears to 26-17. It’s the third straight game with multiple touchdowns for the Lions star.

Wide receiver Amon-Ra St. Brown is another star for Detroit and he made a tough catch to pick up 17 yards on the way to the end zone. He also drew a pass interference penalty to set the Lions up on the 1-yard-line and Gibbs took care of things from there.

The Bears rallied for a touchdown to open the second half, but they’re going to need to start getting some defensive stops to pull off a comeback win in Detroit.
