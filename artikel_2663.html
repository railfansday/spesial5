Houston Texans vs Indianapolis Colts: Predictions

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Here we have 2 teams both racing for the basement – the 3-7 Indianapolis Colts and the 3-6 Houston Texans. The Colts traded away most of their key players to start a rebuild, while the Texans trotted out a failed offensive scheme and altered the winning defensive scheme from 2025. 1 team wants to lose, the other wants to win – but loses anyway.

The baby horses currently lead the all-time series, 33-15-1 thanks in great part to the Peyton Manning/Marvin Harrison Sr. years. However, Houston has won 5 of past 6, thanks to the Colts no longer showing interest in winning football.

On paper, as of this writing, the Texans should absolutely dominate this game – and very well may. H-Town QB C.J. Stroud is having an MVP season. The Houston wide receiver corps is one of the best in the league – including the son of the aforementioned Harrison Sr.. Their run game ran away when Joe Mixon suffered the mystery of the ages injury in 2024, but Stroud has still managed to find ways to complete 201 passes for 2462 yards, 19 touchdowns and only 3 INTs. The offensive line remains offensive, mostly, but has had just enough get-in-the-way-ness to give Stroud time to throw. Currently, the Texans passing attack is 4th overall.

Defensively, Houston has the horsepower to compete at an elite level, but due to off-coverage, injuries and what looks to be confusion with the gameplan, they're currently ranked 20th. Some believe it's a little too much "read your own press coverage", others blame coaching and play calling. In some ways it seems a desire to change up from last season to keep opponents guessing has taken this defense away from what they do best. If you're great at zigging, but decide to zag all the time cause you think your opponent is expecting the zig, then you need to be ok with ignoring your own strengths…

We won't talk about the special teams… #ulcerinducing

Meanwhile, over in the baby horse stable, the Colts are clearly trotting down the rebuild trail. So far in 2026 Indy has:

A new owner who has taken direct control over the coaching staff

An interim general manager (after firing their previous one late last month)

Quenton Nelson: Traded to the Seattle Seahawks.

DeForest Buckner: Traded to the San Francisco 49ers.

Josh Downs: Traded to the Kansas City Chiefs.

Keenan Allen: Traded to the San Francisco 49ers.

And managed to win 1 game since all these team-affecting moves. Their current goal seems to be the #1 draft pick, if the Los Angeles Chargers don't beat them to it.

In true Houston Texans fashion, the Texans will beat an opponent who doesn't want to win. Just as they did to the aforementioned Chargers. The 2026 brand of Texans football doesn't defeat winning teams (exception: the Jacksonville Jaguars cause, #Glitter). They do, however, lose the battle to not win – meaning when an opponent wants to lose, they freely allow H-Town to go to town on them. Which will happen again tonight. Stroud will look like an MVP candidate on a national stage. The Colts will show all their dysfunction. And the talking heads will ignore the apparent incoming mutiny on Kirby Drive.

Those with a superficial knowledge of the Texans will beat the drum that they still have a shot at the playoffs, and how they've fixed their issues. Until the following week when they lose against the Baltimore Ravens at the end of the month.

Meanwhile, those who do lift the hood and look beyond the check engine light glaring on this franchise's dashboard will see a different prediction:

The end game pot is on the stove, simmering away. Chances are tonight will turn the burn down just long enough to have a nice Thanksgiving.
