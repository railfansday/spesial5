Celtics announce starting lineup against Denver Nuggets

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

DENVER — The Celtics will start Derrick White, Jordan Walsh, Paul George, Jayson Tatum, and Neemias Queta when they face the Denver Nuggets on Wednesday.

Queta is coming off his first rest night of the season, a Monday night Celtics loss to the Orlando Magic, which snapped the team's 6-game win streak. The Celtics struggled without him, with Luka Garza starting and Mitchell Robinson and Chris Cenac Jr. playing backup minutes.

But, Monday was more of an anomaly than a typical Boston performance; the Celtics have the fourth-best offensive rating and second-best defensive rating in the league. Only the Minnesota Timberwolves (104.0) have a better defensive rating than Boston (106.1).

And, before tip-off, Nuggets head coach David Adelman praised the Celtics' defensive versatility.

"I think it's been the most underrated thing about them," Adelman said. "Queta is a very active big…Obviously Derrick's always been a good defender. All those guys, they bought into what they do."

Adelman also said he thought people overlooked how good the Celtics might be this season after Jaylen Brown was traded to the Philadelphia 76ers in exchange for Paul George and draft compensation.

"I thought quietly what was maybe misunderstood was: everybody else is coming back. Tatum was back, all that shooting's back. It's not like Pritchard's in the trade, and there's a lot of really good players. Jaylen Brown's an all-time great player, but their team core is back."

The Celtics are 11-3 entering the season; the Nuggets are 7-5.

The Nuggets will start Jamal Murray, Christian Braun, Cam Johnson, Aaron Gordon, and Nikola Jokic.

Pregame, Joe Mazzulla was asked about limiting Nikola Jokic — and explained there's no easy way to do it.

"You have to defend them with your offense," Mazzulla said. "You can't play inefficient offense. You can't allow them to get more shots than you. You can't turn the ball over. If you get into a close game, you have to pick your poison. He is a very good player, and he has the ability to put pressure on every single coverage. He's seen every single coverage. So it's not about stopping him. It's about containing him and winning on the margins. We can't put him on the free throw line. You can't give up offensive rebounds. You can't give up back cuts, and you have to play efficient offense. Guys like that test the margins, and you have to play two-way basketball. You have to be able to attack them so that you can defend them. And you wouldn't guard them the same way down five as you would tied, as you would up five, because of their ability to put stress on multiple coverages. So you have to play well-balanced basketball against a team like this tonight, and the margins are super important. You start giving up back cuts, offensive rebounds, fouls; you're not going to be able to guard them in the half-court, so you've got to be disciplined on both ends of the floor."
