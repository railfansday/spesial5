James Monroe volleyball

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

LINDSIDE — The James Monroe volleyball team returns to state level play when the WVSSAC State Volleyball Championships are played this week at the Charleston Coliseum and Civic Center.

The No. 3 Mavericks (37-11-2) will begin play at 3 p.m. on Tuesday, taking on South Harrison (32-7-1) in a Class AA state quarterfinal match.

If James Monroe wins, it will advance to face either No. 2 Phillip Barbour or No. 7 Wayne in Tuesday's 8 p.m. semifinal match. The No. 2 Colts (45-9-1) and the No. 7 Pioneers (23-15) play one another on Tuesday at 1:30 p.m.

In the other side of the bracket, No. 1 Wheeling Central Catholic (26-4) plays No. 8 at Independence (14-22-2) at 10 a.m. on Tuesday and No. 4 Oak Glen (26-12) plays No. 5 Charleston Catholic (20-15) at 8:30 a.m.

The winner of those two quarterfinal matches will meet in a 6:30 p.m. semifinal on Tuesday. Both of Tuesday evening's semifinal winners will advance to Thursday's 12:30 p.m. Class AA state championship match.
