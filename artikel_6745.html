Should I Be Manifesting Love?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Sometimes, it feels like you’ve tried it all when it comes to love: downloading every dating app available, letting your friends set you up with their “nice” cousin, and even aimlessly going to a Home Depot and pretending to look confused. But when nothing seems to be working, it may be time to consider some less orthodox options, like manifesting love.

If your immediate reaction to hearing about manifestation is to roll your eyes, I get it. Manifestation can sound like a bunch of “woo-woo” for someone who doesn’t find comfort in “trusting in the universe.” But we are living in the era of the Etsy Witch, baby! And opening yourself up to love may just be on the other side of a mindset change—which is essentially all manifestation really is.

Contrary to what you might believe, manifestation is not magic. When stripped of its mystical claims, the practice is grounded in neuroscience, psychology, and behavioral alignment.

A 2025 study showed that people who more strongly believed in manifestation tended to perceive themselves as more successful, have bigger aspirations, and believe they were more likely to achieve future success. And because they believed these things about themselves, they were more likely to go after their goals and take bigger risks—which can all translate to your love life.

“Manifestation gives people hope, especially after heartbreak and reminds them that their past experiences don't have to determine their future in love,” Noah, psychic advisor at California Psychics tells Cosmopolitan.

So you can’t exactly just light a candle, declare “I want love” out loud and then move on with your day—that’s not exactly how manifestation works. In practice, it takes a lot of thought about what it is you truly want out of a relationship.

“Manifesting love is about getting clear on the kind of relationship you want, not just the person you hope to attract,” Noah says. “I encourage people to think about how they want to feel in a relationship, whether that's emotionally secure, appreciated, respected or genuinely loved.” Sometimes, doing things like visualizing, saying affirmations, meditating, and journaling—all things you’d be advised by a therapist—can help you figure out what it is you’re looking for.

And then you have to take stock of your life and your attitude. If you’re someone who’s been closed off to romance (totally fair), it’ll be hard to let something new and exciting in your life.

“You have to be willing to make room for the kind of love you're asking for,” Noah says.

You also have to be careful to not mix up manifestation with wishful thinking. If you’re already into someone or dating them and want them to be “the one,” manifestation isn’t really going to do much to change someone else’s behaviors.

Noah explains that healthy manifestation should bring you a sense of peace, confidence, and clarity. Wishful thinking, on the other hand, can keep you attached to a situation that isn't fulfilling your needs. This is why it’s important to really understand your situation before you try to manifest love out of it.

“Manifestation should never require you to ignore reality or compromise your self worth,” Noah says.

Plus, manifesting a specific person rather than the kind of relationship you want can end up being counterproductive to your love life. Noah says that when someone becomes hyperfocused on manifesting one specific person, they sometimes close themselves off to other possibilities. And manifestation won’t make someone love you. Love involves two people and everyone has their own free will.

“I don't believe manifestation should become an excuse to wait indefinitely for someone who isn't showing up for you,” he says. “Sometimes releasing that attachment is what allows you to become open to something healthier.”

But of course, any kind of love—manifested or otherwise—requires you to feed into yourself, first and foremost. And manifestation can play a role in self love, too.

Noah explains that taking the time to understand what you want can help better inform what you look for (and ask the universe for). You need to start with the relationship with yourself.

“Set your intentions, trust your intuition, and remain open to how love might enter your life without trying to control every detail,” Noah says. “And when someone does come along, pay attention to their actions because chemistry may bring two people together but consistency, communication, and mutual respect are what help love last.”

And once you’ve successfully manifested the love of your life, you can use positive thinking and reinforcement—AKA manifestation—to show up better everyday in your relationship.

A study as old as 1996 found that couples who had more positive perceptions of each other were more likely to stay together, experience increases in relationship satisfaction, and report fewer conflicts and doubts. By manifesting the type of person you want to be in a relationship (while actually in it), your mind will work to make it a reality.

Unfortunately, there is no secret method to landing the love you want. But if changing your outlook on things and keeping your mind open might help you find what you’re looking for, why not try?
