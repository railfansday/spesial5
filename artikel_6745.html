Olivia Dean Goes Backless in Straight-Off-the-Runway Conner Ives

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The pop star brought a pop of pink to the Los40 Music Awards

Last night, Olivia Dean attended the Los40 Music Awards in Barcelona, celebrating a year of global music. And working with stylist Simone Beyene, she selected a striking look for the evening—bringing a pop of pink to the blue carpet.

The dress in question debuted at New York Fashion Week earlier this fall, during Conner Ives’s grand Spring/Summer 2027 return to his hometown. The American designer, who got his start in London after graduating from the city’s Central Saint Martins, crafted the piece-work number from panels of ivory satin, dyed with pink and coral gradients to create a whimsical, airbrushed fish-like pattern.

Delicate spaghetti straps and a square neckline left Dean’s shoulders bare. In the back, the straps dropped into billowing satin, creating a plunging open back silhouette. And from the knees, the shape relaxed into a floor-length skirt that pooled at the singer’s feet.

Hair stylist Sophie Jane Anderson curled Dean’s long locks into a bouncy Old Hollywood blowout with a deep side part, and makeup artist Emily Engleman topped her cheeks with rosy blush.

In addition to performing hit single “Man I Need” at the ceremony, the Art of Loving artist took home the coveted Best International Artist trophy. Accepting the award, she admitted she “wasn’t expecting” it, telling the crowd she was “quite surprised and feeling overwhelmed” with the honor.

“I guess I just want to say thank you. I feel so proud to be an artist and to be an artist that travels the world and be received in other countries,” she continued. “I’m speechless, so I’m just going to go back to my seat now.”
