Kraft Mac &amp; Cheese Is Taking A Big Swing With 2 Unexpected New Flavors

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The latest additions are taking the brand in very different directions.

Delish editors handpick every product we feature. We may earn commission from the links on this page.

I'm as adventurous as the next person when it comes to food, but I firmly believe Kraft Mac & Cheese is one of those things that should remain as classic as classic gets. I can’t say I’ve ever felt the need to question a perfectly good bowl of cheesy pasta.

But the mac and cheese aisle is a competitive place, and sometimes you have to bring out the big guns to stand out. Apparently, Kraft found its latest ammunition somewhere between the spice cabinet and the bakery aisle.

The company is introducing two new limited-time Kraft Mac & Cheese flavors: Chili Crisp and Cinnamon Roll—and yes, you read that second one correctly. Kraft is bringing cinnamon into the cheese sauce for a combination that's supposed to be creamy, a little tangy, and reminiscent of the beloved pastry.

Chili Crisp, on the other hand, is something I can absolutely get behind. People have been adding hot sauce to mac and cheese forever, so giving the familiar blue-box dinner a little extra heat feels like a natural thing to do.

Of course, Kraft hasn't exactly been playing it safe with its flavors lately. There was Pizza Mac & Cheese, along with Jalapeño, Ranch, and Smoky Barbecue. And who could forget last year's Apple Pie Mac & Cheese, which had plenty of people questioning whether dessert and dinner really needed to share a bowl.

But even with that track record, the latest announcement has people feeling all sorts of ways.

"Yall are out of your minds," one person declared, while someone else was apparently ready to try both, writing, "Uhhhh 👁️👄👁️ I need these in my life." Even SpaghettiOs got involved, chiming in with "Honestly, I’m down 🔥".

Chili Crisp seemed to have an easier time winning people over. "Chili crisp actually 100% yea!" one person commented. And at least one fan was willing to give the more questionable Cinnamon Roll flavor a chance, thanks to a surprisingly positive experience with Kraft's previous dessert experiment.

"Everyone thought the apple pie one a year or two ago was weird, but it honestly was pretty good. I’ll give cinnamon roll a shot! 🤷‍♂️ (Chili crisp sounds amazing)"

Meanwhile, some people were less concerned with what Kraft was introducing and more interested in what the company had taken away.

"please bring back garlic parmesean, that was my favorite and i miss it dearly!!" one person pleaded. Another wanted the pizza version back, writing, "I miss your pizza one, it needs to come back." And apparently, the Alfredo fans haven't forgotten, either. "Alfredo bring back!!!" someone demanded.

For anyone ready to put their taste buds to the test, Chili Crisp is available for a limited time at Meijer, Walmart, Amazon, and other retailers, while Cinnamon Roll can be found at Walmart. Kraft hasn't shared exactly how long either will stick around, but the company is already teasing more flavors for 2027.
