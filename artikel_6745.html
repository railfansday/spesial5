Nike’s Black Friday 2026 Sale Is a Layup

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Nike’s Black Friday Sale has always had some perennial favorites and seasonal hitters, but in the past the true tentpoles were nowhere to be found. We’re in a whole new era, however, and Nike’s Black Friday Sale this year is absolutely nuking: a minimum of 25% off nearly everything one could hope to find on sale.

And this isn’t the dregs, either: all-time classics, current-season crushers, catalog deep-cuts (shoutout the Skylines) and even some styles—Royal 1’s, True Blue 3’s—that would send a denizen of 2004 NikeTalk into a certifiable tailspin. As such, and because there are almost too many BF sales for me to keep track of and I’m literally paid to do so, I thought I’d find some of the best and brightest of the massive selection, which you can find below. Considering the styles, and no guarantee of lasting inventory, if you see something you’re into, it’s probably best to just pull up and let it fly.

The biggest clothing company in the world made its name putting athletics first, but the brand's impact in the fashion arena extends far beyond its reputation for record-breaking performance gear. From near-singlehandedly birthing modern sneaker culture to highfalutin collabs with the likes of Louis Vuitton, Nike's evolved into a bona fashion fide force. So if you don’t already have a Swoosh somewhere in your closet (that seems a little improbable, but we'll take your word for it), now's the time to rectify your mistake.

This year in the US, Black Friday is on Friday, November 27, a day after Thanksgiving. While that’s the official date, many retailers roll out deals throughout November. You can expect the biggest discounts to happen between Thanksgiving and Cyber Monday, but be on the look out for retailers to launch sales early.

Cyber Monday lands on November 30 this year, the Monday after Thanksgiving. Most brands keep the deals rolling through the week—but don’t bank on it.
