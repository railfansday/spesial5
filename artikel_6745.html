Kendall Jenner and Jacob Elordi’s Complete Relationship Timeline

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Kendall Jenner started her 29th year with a major decision: to stop actively dating after having what felt like “a lot” of relationships in her twenties. “When I turned 29, I really made like a minor commitment to myself,” she told Owen Thiele during a January 2026 episode of In Your Dreams, “where I was like, ‘I’m not going to reject anything…I’m going to stay open to anything that feels like it would suit me.’ But I really wanted to have like a me year where I chose myself.”

Fast-forward to spring 2026, and Jenner has seemingly found someone who suits her very well: Australian actor Jacob Elordi.

The two stars first sparked dating rumors at Coachella, but in May 2026, the speculation intensified. Jenner and Elordi were photographed on a Hawaii trip and then out in Los Angeles with Jenner’s sister, Kylie, and her partner, actor Timothée Chalamet, days later.

Sources spoke to People and Entertainment Tonight around that time, revealing that Jenner and Elordi had been hanging out in private for months. Their history goes back far further than that, though.

Here, the complete rundown of Elordi and Jenner’s friendship and new romance.

Elordi and Jenner were first photographed hanging out in Paris that February. They appeared with a group of friends in photos that surfaced on social media.

At the time, Jenner was romantically linked to Devin Booker, while Elordi was in an on-off relationship with Olivia Jade Giannulli. (In February 2026, a source told Entertainment Tonight that they had broken up for good, saying that the influencer “has moved on from Jacob Elordi and is ready for a new chapter.”)

People reported that Elordi attended Jenner’s birthday party that November.

Jenner and Elordi were photographed talking at the Vanity Fair Oscar Party on March 15.

Elordi and Jenner sparked dating rumors at Coachella, with reports emerging that they were seen making out at headliner Justin Bieber’s after-party during weekend 1. No photos surfaced of them together, however.

A source told People that Jenner and Elordi had privately been spending time together for weeks leading up to the festival. “They’ve been hanging out and getting to know each other the last couple months,” the insider said.

Entertainment Tonight got intel from its own source, who revealed Jenner and Elordi have romantic interest in each other.

“Kendall and Jacob have run in similar circles for years, but in the last couple months, they’ve developed an attraction and flirtation,” the insider said. “It’s been fun for Kendall to have a new crush.”

It has been “easy” for the two stars to connect, the source added. “They get along because they’re both low-key and take their careers seriously.”

Photos came out of Jenner and Elordi getting breakfast together at Nourish Hanalei in Kauaʻi, Hawaii, during a private trip. People reported that they were seen in Los Angeles one day before the photos were posted, signifying they had since returned home.

Jenner and Elordi joined her sister, Kylie Jenner, and her boyfriend, Timothée Chalamet, for a night out in Los Angeles. The group and their friend Renell Medrano were photographed leaving a Fanatics party on Saturday night. Elordi was driving, and Jenner sat beside him in their car. Chalamet, Jenner, and Medrano were seated in the back.

TMZ obtained a photo of Jenner and Elordi sitting together on a private beach in Kauaʻi during their Hawaii trip. The outlet reported that the photo was from the previous weekend, with an eyewitness saying they appeared to be on a “cute little date” as they spent time there. There was no PDA, the source added, but “the chemistry between the two was obvious,” the outlet wrote.

Later that day, a source spoke to People about Jenner and Elordi’s relationship with Kylie Jenner and Timothée Chalamet. The insider said that the two “couples” are on good terms: “They have a lot of fun together,” the source said, noting they have “similar personalities and lifestyles, [so they] all really get along.”

The source noted that Kylie had spent time with Elordi at industry events, as both Chalamet and Elordi were Oscar nominees in 2026. “[She] really likes him,” the insider said, adding, “She’s excited Kendall is spending time with him.”

A source told Entertainment Tonight that Elordi pursued Jenner, who has come to really like him.

“When Jacob started pursuing Kendall romantically, she was open to it right away,” the insider explained. “He’s been very sweet to her.”

The source clarified that their relationship is still “new and casual,” but Jenner finds Elordi to be “incredibly hot and smart.”

Her family is also supportive, the source added. “Kendall’s told all her sisters about him, and they’re all for it—especially Kylie. Kylie has been encouraging Kendall and Jacob’s relationship, and it’s been a lot of fun for them to go on double dates [with Kylie and Timothée Chalamet] and spend time together as a group.”

Jenner and Elordi quietly took a trip to Tokyo, where they dined at Udon Shin. The restaurant shared an Instagram of the couple there, writing in Japanese, “You won’t believe it....We were honored to have Kendall Jenner (@kendalljenner) and Jacob Elordi visit our restaurant! ✨ Thank you so much for coming to Udon Shin. We hope you enjoyed your time with us.”

Tokyo pizza restaurant Seirinkan shared a photo of them there during their trip. “Thank you for visiting our restaurant. 🍕,” the pizzeria wrote on Instagram. “We sincerely appreciate your patronage and look forward to serving you again.✨”

Jenner and Elordi were photographed walking a dog together in Byron Bay, Australia, with Jenner wearing a cobalt blue jacket, black pants, a patterned headscarf, and narrow sunglasses. Elordi coordinated in a bright yellow hooded jacket, dark pants, and sunglasses. It marks the pair’s first time together in Elordi’s home country.

A source told People that the Wuthering Heights actor still hasn’t been introduced to all the Jenners and Kardashians in the model’s extended family.

“They’ve been spending more and more time together,” the insider shared. “He hasn’t been brought around the family yet.”

However, everyone seems to be very supportive so far.

“The family is hearing Kendall is happy, but the family doesn’t know him well yet,” the source added.

On Fourth of July weekend, Kendall and Elordi were seen in Idaho with Justin and Hailey Bieber. TMZ shared footage of the two couples enjoying some time in a speedboat on a lake together.

A source told Entertainment Tonight Kendall thinks Elordi might be the one.

“Kendall has been telling her closest friends that she’s in love with Jacob,” the insider said. “She feels like Jacob is everything she’s been looking for, and the past few months have felt like something out of a movie. She’s happier than she’s been in a long time. He brings out a side of her that even surprises her.”

“With him, everything feels different,” the source continued, adding, “He’s thoughtful in all the little ways. He’s always carrying her bag, opening doors, and making sure she feels taken care of.”

And her sister is particularly excited about the union.

“Kylie is thrilled to see Kendall like this, and she’s really hoping it lasts,” the source said.

On July 24, Jenner and Elordi were spotted at a Chris Stapleton concert in George, Washington. In a video shared on TikTok, the couple can be seen taking in the music from the VIP section. Jenner was sipping a drink while leaning toward the actor to share some conversation.

Jenner and Elordi were photographed holding hands after a dinner date at China Tang at the Dorchester in London. The couple was in the city for the premiere of Elordi’s new film, The Dog Stars.

For the evening, Jenner wore a sleeveless light dress with black heels, a clutch, and sunglasses. Elordi opted for a dark pinstripe suit with a white shirt, black tie, and tinted sunglasses.

The pair was seen leaving The River Café in Hammersmith, West London, after a dinner. While they appeared to be trying to pass the paparazzi cameras, Elordi tenderly led Jenner through the restaurant door as she grabbed his wrist. They then held hands. The actor wore a white T-shirt and black pants while Jenner was in a black scoop neck shirt and jeans. They both wore sunglasses.

Jenner and Elordi were photographed on another date night, this time in West Hollywood. The couple was seen leaving Manpuku Japanese BBQ, with Jenner walking a few steps ahead of Elordi as they made their way out.

For the outing, Jenner wore an ivory cami with lace detailing, dark-wash jeans, black mules, and sunglasses. Elordi paired loose dark jeans with a white T-shirt, adding a plaid shirt draped around his shoulders, a navy baseball cap, and sneakers.

Kendall’s older brother Brody Jenner weighed in on a report about her relationship with Elordi. After a social media post recirculated claims that Kendall’s family was concerned about the relationship and that she was paying for the pair’s travels, Brody responded in the comments, “I can confirm that this is clickbait. 👎”

Jenner and Elordi were photographed together during a lunch date in Los Angeles. Both dressed in black for the casual outing: Jenner wore a fitted top and jeans, while Elordi opted for a T-shirt and pants with a backward baseball cap.

Elordi supported Jenner at Bottega Veneta’s spring/summer 2027 show during Milan Fashion Week. He was seen photographing her on a film camera as she wore Louise Trotter’s closing look on the runway.

The couple was photographed walking together in Paris after Jenner’s appearance at L’Oréal’s fashion show during Paris Fashion Week. At one point, Elordi intervened with paparazzi, asking for them to just let the pair walk home. The video was posted on social media.

In early November, Jenner and Elordi spent a weekend in New York City. On November 7, they were photographed seeing Paranormal Activity on Broadway. The next day, they were snapped cuddled up with coffee while outside.
