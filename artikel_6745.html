Looking for a New Winter Perfume? These 4 Zara Vanilla Scents Smell So Expensive

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

We may earn commission from links on this page, but we only recommend products we love. Promise.

There’s a reason vanilla perfumes are so popular. They’re no longer the sickly-sweet, sugar-loaded compositions you might remember. Today they’re much more complex—the creaminess of the note is balanced with more sophisticated whiffs of florals, woods, and amber. This new generation of vanilla-forward fragrances feels rounded, full-bodied, and, perhaps most importantly, expensive.

Sure, many of these complex scents come with a hefty price tag. But luckily, Zara has an entire collection dedicated to these deep vanilla perfumes, and there is an array of options that are all ideal for a comforting winter scent. With every spritz, you’ll smell how distinct each one is. In some, vanilla sits quietly at the base, gently rounding off sweeter or floral notes. In others, it takes center stage, creating a creamy, decadent throw.

Whether you like your vanilla subtle and soft for an everyday spritz, or bold and woody for after-hours, there’s a budget-friendly Zara perfume calling your name.

Notes: Chantilly cream, jasmine, sweet tonkaMood: A gourmand scent that’s indulgent and warm but never sickly

The truest vanilla of them all, Dark Romance is the place to start if you’re after a traditionally sweet, creamy—but not sickly—vanilla fragrance. The base note is sweet tonka, which creates that toasty, caramel-y base that sits on your skin, while notes of jasmine and Chantilly cream linger with a velvety, milky sillage. It’s the perfect everyday fragrance; one we envision misting on a cashmere sweater on a chilly night.

Notes: Apricot blossom, jasmine sambac, vanillaMood: A floral vanilla that’s creamy and comforting

Zara’s Hypnotic Vanilla smells exactly as it sounds. Decadent from the first spritz, it instantly envelops you in this golden, buttery cloud of vanilla. Initially, it’s sweet, but then the apricot blossom and jasmine sambac come through, giving it a floral and slightly powdery edge. If Dark Romance is the one you’d wear for a cozy night in, Hypnotic Vanilla is its older sister you’ll reach for for a night out. It’s a great option if you can never choose between gourmands and florals.

Notes: Salty coconut, jasmine, vanilla bloom, amberMood: A full-bodied gourmand that’s creamy, sweet, a little spicy, and seductive

With Immortal Vanilla, you’ll be surprised it only costs $39. While Zara’s perfumes have impressive sillage, this one truly does linger, so you’re bound to receive many compliments. On paper, it has notes of coconut, jasmine, amber, and vanilla. But IRL, they come together to create something that’s comforting, sensual, and ever so slightly creamy and salty. After one spritz, you’ll want to douse yourself in it again, and again, and again.

Notes: Wild orchid, black amber, vanilla Mood: A deep, sophisticated scent that balances vanilla with a woody base

If you want your vanilla perfume to smell like an old-fashioned, New York-style jazz bar, Daring Vanilla is for you. Of the four scents, this is the one that comes closest to a traditional oud fragrance, with naturally smoky, woody notes balanced by wild orchid and vanilla, which lend a warm, subtly sweet throw. It’s the kind of fragrance you’d slip over an LBD, slide into some killer high heels and head out for the night.
