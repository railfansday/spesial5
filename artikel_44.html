'Their lives, their rights, their history': Not the end of accountability for the Rohingya people'

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Oliver Farry welcomes Sonya Sceats, Director and CEO of the British Institute of International and Comparative Law. The case brought by The Gambia against Myanmar before the ICJ, and the court ruling, both have great significance for the Rohingya people, the interpretation of the Genocide Convention and the possibility of future criminal proceedings. The case could also have important implications for South Africa's proceedings against Israel, where there are “many similarities between the two cases.”
