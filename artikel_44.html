California braces for flooding threat from huge tides, El Nino

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Californians are preparing for a confluence of exceptionally high tides and El Nino-fueled weather that threatens coastal areas with extreme flooding during the busiest US travel week of the year. The San Francisco Bay Area is expected to get hit hard with a one-two punch of inclement weather and monster tides, which forecasters warned could push seawater into low-lying areas on Wednesday and Thursday.
