French police hold Paris rally after weeks of protests

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

French police staged a surprise demonstration in front of the French interior ministry in Paris on Wednesday, including members of the specialist riot control unit the CRS. It comes after weeks of huge protest marches around the country that have often descended into violence and led to criticism of police over heavy-handed tactics.
