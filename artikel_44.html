'Tribal loyalty' works on right: 'Heart on the left' sensitive to racism, anti-Semitism

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

As French far-right leader Jordan Bardella denies fresh allegations concerning anti-Semitic remarks he allegedly made as a teenager, how much damage could the affair do to Marine Le Pen's presidential prospects? Ana Navarro Pedro, correspondent for Portuguese media, believes its electoral impact may be limited, despite Le Pen's defence of Bardella and her choice of him as a future prime minister. "What they most regret in this whole episode is that Bardella is denying it," she says of prospective Le Pen voters.
