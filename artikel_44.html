Authorities take over Turkey's last pro-opposition broadcaster Sozcu TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Police raided the Istanbul headquarters of Sozcu TV on Tuesday, dealing another significant blow to Turkey's pro-opposition media. This is the third such seizure of pro-opposition media in just over a year. It comes amid public uproar over a multi-billion investment fund scandal that has affected the savings of nearly half a million people and piled pressure on President Recep Tayyip Erdogan's ruling AKP party ahead of elections in 2028. FRANCE 24's Jasper Mortimer reports from Ankara.
