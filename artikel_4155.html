10 Best Games That Showcase The Xbox Brand

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Eric Warner is a Journalist and Multimedia Producer based in New England with over 7 years of experience producing stories for multiple publications

Microsoft’s gaming brand, Xbox, has finally reached the momentous milestone of existing for 25 years, with all four generations of Xbox console greeting gaming memories that will last lifetimes for those who played them throughout the 21st century.

These days, however, in the wake of poor direction during the Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S eras, Xbox is struggling to retain a healthy gamer population, with many gamers choosing instead to play games on Nintendo or PlayStation due those platforms’ vast variety of exclusive titles.

With Xbox having recently reached its 25th anniversary and the next generation of Xbox consoles, Project Helix, expected to debut in a few years, here’s a look back at 10 exclusive games that best showcase the Xbox brand and may potentially entice people to try an Xbox console.

Many fans of the British game developer, Rare, likely recognize them for creating Nintendo-centric titles like Banjo-Kazooie or Donkey Kong Country but under Microsoft’s ownership, one of the best games they ever released was Viva Piñata.

A life simulation game, Viva Piñata is set on the wondrous Piñata Island and has players restore a garden there with the goal of attracting, raising, and defending the local population of living piñata animals so that they can be eventually shipped off across the globe to become the center of attention at parties.

Viva Piñata encapsulates how creative, fun, and unique Xbox games can be and, while there hasn’t been a new Viva Piñata game in over 18 years, people today can still easily play it and other classic Rare games via the compilation Rare Replay.

One of the best games that showcases the absolute power and capabilities of modern Xbox consoles is Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024, the latest entry in the long-running Microsoft Flight Simulator series.

Like many flight simulator games, Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 allows players to learn how to fly planes in both first and third-person but this game is the most advanced flight simulator yet as players can learn how to fly other real and fictional aircraft in numerous locations across the globe.

For example, people can fly Halo’s Pelican dropship over Yosemite National Park, the Goodyear Blimp over London, a DG Flugzeugbau DG-1000 over Mount Fuji, a Airbus Helicopters H225 over the Stilt Villages of Papua New Guinea and more.

From Minecraft Legends to Gears of War: E-Day, these 10 great games defined the Xbox Series X|S and showcased Xbox's ever-changing modern identity.

Viva Piñata was very memorable but it wasn’t the most popular video game Rare made under Microsoft’s ownership, with that title instead going to the hit cartoonish pirate multiplayer game Sea of Thieves.

Played primarily in first-person, Sea of Thieves allows players to create and customize their very own pirate and ship as they set sail alone or with a crew of friends into monster-infested waters in search of treasure and glory across the sea.

Sea of Thieves might have had rough start when it first launched in 2018 but in the years since, it has truly shined to become the definitive pirate multiplayer experience thanks to its comedic gameplay and eventful updates adding new areas to explore and supernatural sea creatures to fight.

For a while, Xbox was the best platform to play western RPGs on and, during the original Xbox era, one of the best exclusive games to showcase this status was Bioware’s legendary Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic.

A third-person round-based RPG, Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic is set roughly 4,000 years before the events of the Star Wars films and follows an amnesiac warrior as they set out to stop the Jedi turned Sith Darth Malak from conquering the galaxy with the mysterious Star Forge.

While KOTOR’s gameplay hasn’t aged perfectly in the 23 years since its launch, its story, characters, and world designs have remained immaculate, with its characters going on to influence many other Star Wars stories and it still featuring one of the greatest gaming twists surrounding the character of Darth Revan.

Since the days of the Xbox 360, Microsoft has often invested and supported small game developers with immense potential, leading to the release of games like Braid, Ori and the Blind Forest, and the subject for this entry, Cuphead.

Studio MDHR’s debut title, Cuphead is a 2D run and gun game with visuals inspired by rubberhose-style cartoons of the 1920s and 30s, similar to Steamboat Willie, following the brothers Cuphead and Mugman as they set out to collect soul contracts from various bosses in order to save their souls from the Devil.

While Cuphead was only a timed exclusive on the Xbox One, it represents the brand’s ability to support small game development studios and uplift experimental titles into gaming stardom as Cuphead has gone on to sell millions of copies and become a highly successful multimedia franchise.

From PUBG: Battlegrounds to Cuphead, these 10 great games defined the Xbox One and helped entertain players through Xbox's darkest era.

Racing has always been at the core of Xbox gaming from the early days of Project Gotham Racing to the more modern Forza Motorsport titles and while none of these games have been as popular as Mario Kart, one has managed to become the most advanced, realistic racing game yet: Forza Horizon 6.

The latest entry in the ongoing Forza Horizon series, Forza Horizon 6 has players take to the urban and rural streets of Tokyo, Japan in over 500 cars as they strive to qualify for the Horizon Festival or simply explore the many beautiful landscapes of Japan alone or with friends.

In a similar way to how Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 became the premier modern flight simulator, Forza Horizon 6 has become the premier realistic racing game thanks to its hyperrealistic visuals, vast automotive and track customization options, and dynamic environments.

KOTOR is one of the best RPGs to ever debut on an Xbox console but it’s not the brand’s premier exclusive RPG, with that honor instead going to 2004’s Fable and the greater Fable franchise.

Originally pitched to be a fantasy RPG that would have allowed players to have kids in-game, and witness plant life grow among other promises, the original Fable wound up being a very charming, British humor fantasy RPG following a young boy as he set out to become the hero of Albion.

While the first Fable didn’t meet all of its creator, Peter Molyneux, aspirations, it sequels did slowly start to achieve all of Molyneux’s promises and more, becoming the source of many fond memories to the point that Forza Horizon 6’s own Playground Games is rebooting the series with 2027’s Fable.

One of the most recent games that best showcase the Xbox brand’s storytelling capability and multiplayer potential is The Coalition’s Gears of War: E-Day, the sixth mainline entry in the Gears of War series and prequel to the first Gears game.

Like its predecessors, Gears of War: E-Day is a third-person cover shooter with updated maneuverability but what made it really shine is its emotional story following COG soldiers Marcus Fenix, Dominic Santiago, Mags Carter, and Lucas Reyes as they face the first horrifying Locust invasion on Emergence Day.

Gears has always been known for its gory, action-packed gameplay as well as its heartfelt story following people face seemingly insurmountable nightmarish forces and E-Day proves that Gears can still deliver horrifying, emotional yet hopeful gaming narratives 20 years after its first debuted in 2006.

From Kinect Star Wars to Halo 3, these 10 great games defined the Xbox 360 and the era when Xbox was at the top of the gaming industry.

The Xbox 360 era was arguably Xbox at its best and most successful largely in part due to Microsoft investing in several permanent and timed exclusive titles alike, such when Microsoft and Mojang worked together to make the Xbox 360 the first console home for the global sensation that was Minecraft.

While Minecraft was already highly popular on PC, Minecraft: Xbox 360 Edition helped introduce millions of console players to the blocky survival game for the first time via Xbox Live Arcade in 2012 and led many to grow up with the game as it evolved and expanded with every update.

Minecraft: Xbox 360 Edition’s success ultimately led to Microsoft acquiring Mojang in 2014 and since then, the two companies have worked together to make Minecraft one of the most successful multimedia franchises of all time through the release of numerous Minecraft updates, spinoffs, and adaptations.

The Halo franchise is as integral to Xbox as Sonic the Hedgehog is to Sega and Super Mario is to Nintendo and while there have been many fantastic Halo titles released over the years, I would argue that the best entry in the series to showcase the Xbox brand is the FPS prequel, Halo: Reach.

Bungie’s last Halo title, Halo: Reach has players embody the customizable Spartan-III Noble Six as they join the Spartan-II and III squad of Noble Team in defending the human military world of Reach from an alien Covenant invasion a few months before the events of Halo: Combat Evolved.

While Reach’s campaign is a ton of fun on its own, I believe Reach is the best Halo title to represent the Xbox brand because it remains the most complete Halo title to date as it launched with four-player campaign co-op, playable Elites, Firefight, Forge, Theater, Infection, Grifball, and more fun modes and features.

From Project Gotham Racing to Halo: Combat Evolved, these 10 great games defined the original Xbox and set the groundwork for Xbox's future.
