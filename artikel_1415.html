Rui Costa airs PSG in the Prestianni file

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Gianluca Prestianni file seems on the verge of a positive outcome for Benfica. While the young Argentinian is attracting the interest of Paris Saint-Germain in particular, the Lisbon club is working to extend his contract in order to secure the future of one of its great hopes. Present this Friday at the inauguration of the Casa do Benfica in Cantanhede, President Rui Costa was particularly optimistic about the negotiations.

Asked about the progress of the file, the Portuguese leader assured: “ everything is almost ready and everything is going smoothly. » In other words, Benfica should keep its sultry and coveted winger. A statement which suggests an imminent extension and which testifies to Benfica’s desire to keep Prestianni despite the interest shown by PSG.
