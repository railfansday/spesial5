Boox is giving away my favorite e-reader during Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The adorable and tiny Picco launched just a few weeks ago, and Boox is already giving the new e-reader away during its Black Friday deal. Even if it isn’t a price cut, it’s still a neat add-on.

At its core, the Boox Picco is just a very small e-reader – 3.97 inches, small. It doesn’t run Android, so you don’t get any of the third-party app support that comes with the rest of Boox’ e-readers. But that doesn’t matter as much when the transfer app and OS do their job phenomenally.

The Boox is around $99, depending on where you buy it. That’s what makes it such a cool little gadget. It’s affordable, easy to use, and gives you access to your digital library wherever. Genuinely, wherever.

Right now, Boox is giving them away with the purchase of another item on its website. The caveat is that the deal means a Picco comes with an order of another e-reader at $800 or more.

That’s admittedly more than the price of a Picco, but if you were already in the market for an Android-powered e-reader and looking for a deal, the rest of its lineup is worth recommending if a gifted add-on is worth it to you. I’ve used a number of Boox tablets over the last few years, and genuinely love using them for reading and sketching.

Boox is also adding a free Tappy page turner and $30 off with a $600 purchase.

The Boox Picco is also sold on Amazon for $109, and there are some other awesome e-reader deals running now.
