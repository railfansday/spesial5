There's been speculation for years about whether Airbnb would launch flights. Such small-minded speculation: Airbnb intends to use specialized AI agents to do every travel vertical. Airbnb Cruise, anyone?

Airbnb CEO Brian Chesky said the company plans to develop specialized AI agents for integrations with numerous partners to handle bookings for "every vertical in travel" in the Airbnb app.

Chesky said partnerships are a key opportunity for Airbnb, which grew for many years mostly organically without such collaborations.

Airbnb CEO Brian Chesky said on the company's third quarter earnings call that Airbnb plans to build specialized AI agents and integrate with partners across "every vertical in travel," with the goal of eventually booking all travel natively inside the Airbnb app. Chesky pointed to car rentals, currently handled through a CarTrawler partnership as a "link off" that sends users to another site, as the model Airbnb intends to replace with native booking on Airbnb. He said the company will sign a large number of partnerships to reach scale, and that native integrations would give Airbnb a greater share of revenue along with responsibility for customer service. Airbnb's shareholder letter said a specialized AI agent will roll out to guests in the first half of 2027. Chesky declined to put a timeline on the broader goal and said he did not want to reveal more of the product roadmap, but he said specialized AI agents will win over generalized ones.
