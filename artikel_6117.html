Boeing Weighs More Layoffs As 737 MAX Output Boost Slows

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

On November 19th, Boeing declined to tell one of its largest unions whether further layoffs will follow a round planned for next month, even as the company struggles to lift 737 MAX output from 42 jets a month to 47, a rate it had expected to reach this past summer. The company announced this month that it would cut 5% of the roughly 50,000 employees in its commercial airplanes division, and now expects to reach 47 jets a month in early 2027, according to Reuters.
