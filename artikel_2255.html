Di’Shon Bernard fit but three players still out as Dave Challinor issues Sheffield Wednesday injury latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Sheffield Wednesday face Oldham Athletic in the FA Cup on Sunday as Dave Challinor takes charge of his new side for the first time. The new SWFC boss has a squad full of talented players to pick from, but he has injuries to consider too.

And, speaking to the press ahead of his maiden game, Challinor confirmed that trio Liam Cooper, Joe Lumley and Ali Al-Hamadi are set to remain out. Cooper is more of a precautionary decision, while Lumley and Al-Hamadi are targeting returns to training next week.

In better news, Di’Shon Bernard is fit and available having trained all week. Challinor and the Owls will remain cautious with his load management after a long time on the sidelines.

Cup matches against opponents in lower divisions often offers teams the chance to rotate, giving fringe players the chance to come in and impress. It will be intriguing to see just what sort of approach Challinor takes though, as he gets to grips with his Sheffield Wednesday squad.

Early on, Challinor has to determine his strongest starting lineup. The most efficient way to do so will be by playing as strong a side as possible in this FA Cup clash with Oldham Athletic.

Alternatively, Challinor will have done his research before taking over at SWFC, and he watched the Blackpool draw. If he already has a good idea of his favoured XI, he can take this game to give chances to those projected to be on the fringes to get a clearer picture of the full squad at his disposal.

It is an interesting dilemma for Challinor, and it will be interesting to see how he approaches it.

Lumley’s continued absence means Ashley Maynard-Brewer will likely keep his place in goal. Cooper being on the sidelines gives Bernard a good chance of coming straight into the side as well.

Challinor had a strong squad at his disposal at Stockport County, but it feels like this Sheffield Wednesday group is a level up. There are experienced pros like Cooper, Barry Bannan and Liam Palmer, alongside players who could arguably be at a higher level, such as Callum Slattery, Billy Mitchell and defender Bernard.

Pair this with the youthful exuberance of Ernie Weaver, Will Grainger and Harry Gray, and SWFC have a squad built for the short and long-term. There’s a good balance of profiles and characters, making up a recipe for success at Hillsborough.

Challinor faces plenty of selection headaches in deciding his best lineup, and he will be excited to find it out.
