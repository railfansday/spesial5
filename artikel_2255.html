Frank McAvennie apologises after criticising Celtic star's grief absence

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Former Celtic striker Frank McAvennie has apologised after criticising Auston Trusty for missing games after the death of his father.

The defender went public two weeks ago after sadly losing his dad, paying tribute to him online on October 21.

He sat out games against Celta Vigo, Hibs, Dundee United and Falkirk. In the build-up to the latter, manager Martin O'Neill revealed Trusty was heading back to America to "celebrate his dad's life."

McAvennie was critical of how much football the defender was missing, saying on the Let Me Be Frank podcast: "I've got every respect for that, but how long do you want off? Martin (O'Neill) says he's away to celebrate the life of his dad.

"Celebrate the life? You've got football. You're getting paid and we're in trouble. How long is it going to be?

"It's nonsense. My dad was the best man, the only man I looked up to when he died, but you've got to get on with it."

The comments prompted outrage online, with the pundit accused of being insensitive.

That has prompted an apology in the form of an online statement.

McAvennie said: "I’ve seen some of the reaction to comments I made on the podcast about Auston’s absence from football following the death of his father.

"I can understand why people feel my comments were insensitive, and looking back, I should have chosen my words more carefully.

"Everyone deals with grief differently, and I certainly meant no disrespect to Auston or his family. Celtic need the big man back, and that’s what I was clumsily trying to say.

"I wish him and his family all the very best."
