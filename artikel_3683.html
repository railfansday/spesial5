Booby-Trapped Video Game Console Explodes At Rival Gaming Store

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A video game console exploded last month at a repair shop in Santo AndrÃ©, Brazil, injuring two of the people who worked there. Police are calling it an attempted murder, and say a rival business owner was trying to eliminate his competition. The man they think was responsible was arrested over the weekend as part of a separate investigation.

G1 Globo reports (via Dexerto) that the original incident took place on October 23 at around 5:30 p.m. A delivery person dropped off a gaming device for some sort of repair, but inside the retro gaming console, police say there was a concealed timer and two cylinders filled with flammable liquid. The store owner reportedly put the console inside a drawer where it later ignited, putting a hole in the ceiling as well as damaging other parts of the store.
