Valve push on with their Steam improvements by revamping the Community Market home page and adding a watchlist to it so you're bookmarks will be a lot lighter

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Another day, another Steam update! Earlier this year, Valve made some tweaks to Steam's Community Market as part of their ongoing widening of the digital storefront. The maybe Half-Life 3 developer is back with another update for the Community Market, once again bringing some fresh UI/UX bits, as well as some quality of life adjustments that should make it a bit nicer to use.

For starters, there's a new Market home page! "We've rebuilt the Market Home to load faster and present you with the most relevant items from your games," a blog post explains. "Instead of the Popular Items section, which was a small and static slice of millions of items, Trending Items will give you a glimpse into what’s new in the games you’re currently playing. The game links move up to the top spot and are now personalized, so rather than a flat and unchanging list, you get quick access to the items you're most likely to be interested in."

Next up in the update is the addition of a watchlist feature. Before, if you wanted to follow a number of items in the marketplace you'd need to bookmark everything, but now you can just save things to your watchlist; this includes the likes of specific listings, particular item types with any filters you've applied, and even specific searches, again with filters and search text. You can also apply custom names to watchlist entries in case two of them end up looking particularly similar.

There were a few other smaller bits to this update too. You'll find a new section that highlights Steam community items, i.e. trading cards and emotes, from games you've played. If you have anything listed on the marketplace, you can now change the price without having to relist it. There's a list view option while browsing items. And market pages now load faster and are more responsive. A convenient sounding update for the Steam Community Market connoisseur! Which is already live, by the way.
