Unannounced Nioh 3 Complete Edition has been rated in Korea

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

A rating for the unannounced Nioh 3 Complete Edition has popped up on Korea's Game Rating and Administration Committee (GRAC).

As Insider Gaming spotted, the board gave Nioh 3 Complete Edition a 19+ rating on 17th November after it was submitted on 2nd November, even though Koei Tecmo hasn't announced a Complete Edition for the game yet.

The listing, translated by machine, describes it as "a compilation of additional content from the dark Sengoku action RPG Nioh 3", and gives the rating for "excessive expression of violence", noting that "red blood and body amputation frequently occur during combat".

The listing also notes an overseas rating, which Insider Gaming says is a 17+ rating from Japan's CERO.

Unlike some rating boards, GRAC publishes its ratings as soon as they're decided, which is why game listings often surface there before they're officially announced.

If Koei Tecmo follows the same pattern as Nioh 2, there may not be long to wait. GRAC's records show Nioh 2 was rated in January 2020, and its Complete Edition followed in November that year. Nioh 3 was rated on 14th November 2025, almost exactly a year ago.

Complete Editions typically includes the base game and all of its DLC. It could even launch alongside Nioh 3's second DLC, Bloody Insurrection, which is due in February 2027. Eurogamer has contacted Koei Tecmo for comment and we'll let you know if/when they come back to us.

Back in April, an update introduced "multiple high-difficulty Battle Scrolls" (side missions), accessible from shrines, the addition of a Stone of Penance item "which increases accessory +Values and rarities in exchange for higher difficulties", and new skills given as rewards for completing the aforementioned high-level Battle Scroll missions.

Eurogamer's review of Nioh 3 called the threequel a new high for the 'masocore' series and "the crucible in which all of Team Ninja's experience has been siphoned into." It's sold over one million copies (and counting) since its 6th February launch.
