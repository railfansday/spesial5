Macaulay Culkin to Star in 'Home Alone' Sequel Written by Seth Rogen: Details

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Macaulay Culkin might find himself home alone once again.

Multiple outlets reported on Friday, November 20, that Culkin, 46, is in talks to return to the Home Alone franchise in a sequel developed by Disney and 20th Century Studios. Seth Rogen and his producing and writing partner, Evan Goldberg, are reportedly attached to pen the script, which is based on an idea that Culkin himself pitched to Disney in recent months.

The plot centers on Culkin’s character, Kevin McCallister, as a father, according to Deadline.

Us reached out to reps for Disney for comment.

In July, Culkin was reportedly spotted at Walt Disney Studios in Burbank, California, to discuss the potential film.

“I got a tip the other day that there was a very interesting sighting on the Disney lot: Macaulay Culkin was there,” veteran entertainment journalist Matthew Belloni shared on his “The Town” podcast. “He’s talked about having an idea for a new Home Alone, and Disney is, like, salivating.”

He continued, “They’re offering him another movie — like, he could do his own passion project if he could also do another Home Alone — and they have a good idea. I’ve talked to people who have heard it, and it’s a really good idea for a Home Alone reboot with Macaulay Culkin.”

As a child, Culkin starred in the original 1990 comedy directed by Chris Columbus. The classic holiday movie follows 8-year-old Kevin, who is accidentally left alone at his parents’ home in Chicago while his family heads off on a Christmas vacation in Paris. Kevin must fend for himself and defend his home when a pair of burglars attempts a robbery.

Culkin reprised his role in the 1992 sequel, Home Alone 2: Lost in New York, which finds Kevin alone in New York after he accidentally boards the wrong plane.

Home Alone and Home Alone 2, also directed by Columbus, grossed a combined $836.3 million. The franchise spawned a 1997 threequel without Culkin, instead starring Alex D. Linz and directed by Raja Gosnell. The third film grossed $79.1 million worldwide.

A fourth made-for-TV installment, Home Alone 4, recast Kevin (Mike Weinberg) and was directed by Rod Daniel in 2002. A 2012 made-for-TV film, Home Alone: The Holiday Heist, starred Christian Martyn as Finn Baxter. The installment was directed by Peter Hewitt.

The franchise’s most recent installment, Home Sweet Home Alone, arrived on Disney+ in 2021, directed by Dan Mazer and starring Archie Yates as Max Mercer.

“It’s been revisited with really bad sequels. Sorry to insult anybody, but they’ve completely f***ed it up,” Columbus, 68, told The Hollywood Reporter in December 2025 while celebrating the original film’s 35th anniversary. “It started with Home Alone 3, and then it just went downhill from there; Home Alone 3 is sort of the best of the bunch of the bad movies.”

Culkin also shared his idea for a reboot.

“I like the idea that maybe Kevin’s older, that he’s like a widower or something like that. He’s raising his kid and they don’t really get along, he’s working all the time. … it’s almost like a Liar, Liar kind of thing,” he told the outlet. “There’s one of two ways you can do it. One, he actually leaves the kid behind by mistake; he calls up his mom like, ‘So sorry, I get it now.’ Or I leave him behind on purpose, like, ‘Oh, that made me the man I am today.’”
