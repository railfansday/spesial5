The cordless Vitamix Ion can be used anywhere.

Just over two months after Vitamix launched Nano, its first personal blender, the company has released its first portable blender, the Ion. Announced on Monday, the cordless blender is designed for blending smoothies, shakes and frappés anywhere you crave a blended beverage.

Between the Nano and Ion, Vitamix’s 2026 focus seems to be on expanding its line with compact blenders. Laura Ostenkamp, Vitamix’s vice president of sales and marketing, said in a press release that the Ion was developed to give people more flexibility in how and where they blend, whether they’re at the gym, in the office or generally away from their own kitchen.

The Ion features a 24-ounce, BPA-free container with a handle and a flip-top. To blend your ingredients in that same container, all you have to do is double-click the Ion’s single button and the aluminum-enclosed, removable motor base and stainless steel blades will get to work.

With USB-C charging, you get 15 blends per charge. If you drink a smoothie a day, that’s over a week of drinks without needing to recharge.

Priced at $150, the Ion comes with a two-year limited warranty and is now available on Vitamix.com and at select retailers, including Amazon.
