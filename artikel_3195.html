Despite higher fuel prices, revenue growth outpaced the increase in costs, resulting in gross margin expansion of 130 basis points to 49.5%. We remain focused on reducing fuel consumption through technology and operational improvements while preserving the flexibility for our captains and expedition leaders to adjust itineraries in real time to optimize the guest experience. These efforts are delivering tangible results.

Selling and marketing costs increased $7.7 million, or 25.5%, primarily due to the final royalty rate step-up under our National Geographic agreement. General and administrative costs, excluding stock-based compensation, transaction-related expenses, and reorganization costs, increased $2.9 million, or 10.2%. Excluding this prior year benefit, G&A as a percentage of revenue declined approximately 1% year-over-year, reflecting disciplined cost management and improving operating leverage as we scale the business. Despite higher fuel costs and the final royalty rate step-up under our National Geographic agreement, we maintained Adjusted EBITDA margins of 23.8%, consistent with the prior year quarter.
