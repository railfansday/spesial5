Here's How Much You Can Expect $10,000 in Corning (GLW) Stock to Pay You in Dividends Next Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Corning is not the most impressive dividend payer -- but is very impressive (and promising!) in other ways.

So you're thinking of investing in Corning (GLW -0.80%), or you've already bought shares -- and you're wondering how much dividend income you can look forward to over the coming year. Let's take a look.

First of all, know that Corning is an established giant in the realm of glass, ceramics, and optical fiber -- supplying such widely used things as TV sets, smartphones, telecommunication networks, and even data centers. The stock was recently trading for about $120 per share and paying a dividend of $0.28 per quarter per share -- totaling $1.12 for the year. That's a dividend yield of about 0.93%.

Let's assume that you've plunked $10,000 of your hard-earned dollars into Corning. That's enough at the current price to get you 83 shares. And with each share generating $1.12 in annual dividend income, you're looking at a total annual income of ... $93.
