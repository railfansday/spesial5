Mount Logan Capital Inc. (MLCI -1.98%) executed three strategic transactions during the third quarter to transition its asset management base from legacy noncore vehicles toward scalable permanent capital structures. Management completed the Yieldstreet transaction and the merger of the Alternative Credit Income Fund into BC Partners Lending Corporation, while announcing a pending agreement to manage the AFA Asset Based Lending Fund. The company also reached an operational milestone at its Ability Insurance subsidiary by launching direct origination of multiyear guaranteed annuities following a new credit rating from AM Best. Management reported that these structural changes are intended to improve operating leverage and drive GAAP profitability as the benefits of these initiatives flow through the financials in late 2026 and 2027.
