Opinion pollster Ipsos 'fesses up to 'cybersecurity incident'

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Market research company brings in external specialists to grok cause and scope

Market research giant Ipsos says it's currently investigating a "cybersecurity incident," and has reported the matter to the authorities.

In a statement issued on Monday from its Paris headquarters, the company  confirmed it filed a complaint over the attack on November 11, but gave few details because its internal investigation is ongoing.

"Upon discovery, we promptly activated our incident response procedures, took steps to contain the incident, and engaged external cybersecurity specialists to support our investigation," it said [PDF].

"The investigation is ongoing to determine the nature and scope of the incident. We have reported the matter to the relevant authorities."

Ipsos confirmed the attack had no impact on its operations or customer service delivery, however, it did not comment on the nature of the attack or whether any data was compromised.

"We take protection of the information entrusted to us very seriously," Ipsos added. "Should the investigation establish that information relating to clients, projects or business partners has been affected, we will contact those concerned directly.

"As the investigation is ongoing, we are not in a position to comment further at this stage."

The company declined our requests for additional data, as it does not want to release unverified information to the market.

Founded in France in 1975, Ipsos is one of the leading market research companies in the world, employing nearly 20,000 people and operating in 220 cities across 90 markets.

It is routinely engaged by businesses for its market research data, as well as by public sector organizations for its opinion polling, often responsible for providing data regarding political views and satisfaction surveys.

Tasked with procuring a wealth of data on myriad topics, Ipsos also offers data analytics services that turn the information it gathers through research, polls, and surveys into insights that inform organizations' business strategies. ®
