West Ham interested in 22-year-old goalkeeper with minimum $15m transfer fee demanded

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

West Ham have encountered a goalkeeping dilemma, with Mads Hermansen struggling for form and Alphonse Areola now injured. Attention has turned to the transfer market and potential solutions, and according to Argentine news outlet TyC Sports, WHUFC have eyes on River Plate’s Santiago Beltran.

It is claimed that the Hammers are among the clubs to have shown interest in the 22-year-old, but Spanish giants Atletico Madrid and Premier League side Aston Villa also have eyes on the goalkeeper. River Plate have been keen to extend Beltran’s contract, which expires in 2027, but they are willing to sell if $15m (£11.2m) is tabled.

The Argentine club would be willing to sell in January too, which will suit the Hammers as they look to resolve their goalkeeping issues. Alongside Hermansen and Areola, academy graduate Finlay Herrick is an option available to new boss Scott Parker.

Beltran is a product of the River Plate academy and has broken into the first team setup over the past 18 months. He made his international debut for Argentina in the summer but is also eligible to represent Italy.

Over the course of 2026, Beltran has locked down the starting berth with one of his country’s most prestigious sides. Given that his contract runs out at the end of 2027, it is little wonder that interest from West Ham, Atletico Madrid and Aston Villa is emerging.

In Liga Professional, Argentina’s top flight, Beltran has recorded 50 saves and an xG prevented of 3.93. It shows that while he isn’t necessarily highly active in terms of shot-stopping behind a tight defence, he is doing a lot of individual work to deny goal-bound shots when he is called upon.

He is solid in terms of his short-distance distribution with 259 successful passes, but he has a long ball accuracy of only 27.8%. This is an area of his game to improve, but at only 22, the WHUFC target has plenty of time to grow in this area (all stats via FotMob).

Beltran’s success in Argentina indicates he would be an upgrade on West Ham’s current options. Interest from Atletico Madrid and Aston Villa emphasises the high regard he is held in too.

Importantly, Beltran’s age means that he could become the Hammers’ long-term No. 1. He has both time and room to grow, mainly in his distribution, so he can become an important player off the bat while growing into a valuable financial asset in the years to come.

At around £11m, he wouldn’t be a cheap signing. But, at that price, WHUFC would be signing a goalkeeper upgrade who can only grow in value as he progresses. Beltran feels like a good target and a smart option in the effort to tighten up in goal.
