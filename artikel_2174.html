EFL expert Jobi McAnuff backs ‘fantastic’ Middlesbrough as Championship promotion favourites

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Middlesbrough are winless in their last two games against Wrexham and Watford. They drew 0-0 with the latter last time out at Vicarage Road before the international break.

Boro are sat joint-top of the Championship table after 15 fixtures along with Birmingham City and Stoke City. They are behind the Blues on goal difference.

McAnuff, who played for the likes of Cardiff City, Watford and Reading, has delivered his verdict on Kim Hellberg‘s side on BBC’s 72+ Podcast: “Middlesbrough are a fantastic team. I love what Kim Hellberg has done. It’s amazing isn’t it, for such a good team, so well functioning around the striker (Will Lankshear), you take him out and he’s been phenomenal this season, what an impact that can have.

“That was a little bit the issue last season, not having that real killer at the top end of the pitch to take those half chances, getting in that position. They’ve missed him the last couple (of games). I still have them for sure as my favourites. I think anyone who is above them this year will be getting automatically promoted.”

Middlesbrough sold key man Hayden Hackney to Everton in the summer. Boro also got money from a sell-on clause they had on Morgan Rogers from Aston Villa to Chelsea.

They reinvested heavily into their playing squad following their play-off final defeat to Hull City at Wembley back in May. The likes of Will Lankshear, Ashley Phillips and Radek Vitek came through the entrance door at the Riverside Stadium.

Lankshear, 21, has been prolific under the guidance of Hellberg and has scored 15 league goals already. However, he has missed the last two outings against Wrexham and Watford due to injury and his presence has been missed.

The ex-Tottenham Hotspur will be hoping to return after the international break against Bristol City at home. His team haven’t managed to score without him which is a big worry.

Middlesbrough can’t just rely solely on Lankshear. They need others like David Strelec and Kyle Joseph to step up.

The latter was snapped up from Hull City in early July. He hasn’t managed to score yet in the league and has found it hard to find his shooting boots.

Boro have been strong overall this term and will fancy their chances of finishing in the top two. Hellberg is an impressive young manager and as McAnuff alluded to, his team are well drilled and tough to play against.

They have some solid players like Aidan Morris, Amario Cozier-Duberry and Riley McGree and on their day, they are a match for anyone in the second tier. Hellberg’s men will be eager to return to winning ways against Bristol City next up and their upcoming opponents have been struggling for form of late.

Middlesbrough will get the chance to freshen up their squad in January with some new faces. They will also have the opportunity to let some players leave if needed.
