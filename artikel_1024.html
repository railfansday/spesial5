Hull stun Brighton with stoppage-time double as Seagulls reduced to 10 men

Wild scenes erupted at the MKM Stadium as Oli McBurnie scored a stoppage-time double to snatch a late victory for Hull over title challengers Brighton.

The Tigers had not won since they beat Coventry in late August, drawing five times since, but they withstood a barrage of attacks - with home goalkeeper Konstantinos Tzolakis producing an immediate display - and stole a 2-0 win against the run of play.

The MKM Stadium has become something of a fortress and Brighton had never enjoyed success on Humberside.

But the Seagulls have been in immense form, with many talking of a serious title tilt, and arrived in Hull second in the table and with the chance to close to within three points of leaders Arsenal.

Fabian Hurzeler’s side were dominant but could not find a way through, thwarted on several occasions by Tzolakis, and failed to distance themselves from third-placed Manchester City, who dropped points at home to Fulham on Saturday.

Brighton push for an eighth victory of the season was halted by Joao Costinha’s dismissal a minute from time for a second yellow card and, from there, the wheels came off.

McBurnie bundled home a fine Hull move two minutes into stoppage time and the Tigers were not done there as the striker buried his fourth goal in as many matches to lift his side up to 11th in the table.

A quarter of an hour in Tzolakis was forced into a deft save as Diego Gomez picked up a dangerous Maxim De Cuyper cross and nearly made in 1-0, with the keeper blocking a shot from Georginio Rutter with his legs on the half-hour.

As the first half drew to a close, Yasin Ayari hit the bar and Rutter got to the rebound, but Tzolakis produced another impressive save to ensure all was level going into the break – with a late Brighton penalty shout dismissed as Pascal Gross felt he had been pushed in the goalmouth.

Hull went close to breaking the deadlock after 64 minutes when Mohamed Belloumi darted through Brighton’s defence but fired what was a valuable chance wide.

Just over 10 minutes later Tigers’ hopes were raised again as substitute Sorba Thomas breathed new life into the game by hitting the woodwork, after which Hull looked dangerous again when Thomas pushed forward but was foiled by Dunk.

Costinha was shown a second yellow card for pushing Thomas and it was the break Hull needed. Regan Slater played in Lucas Gourna-Douath, who picked out McBurnie to prod into the bottom corner.

Before the dust had settled, the Tigers were on the move again with McBurnie the man at the forefront once more as he linked up with Belloumi to find space in front of an open goal in the 95th minute to seal the win.
