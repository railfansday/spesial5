Manchester United CEO Omar Berrada has claimed that five summer signings have helped make the club’s squad “fundamentally better” despite the club sitting inside the relegation zone.

United have hired their fourth permanent manager under the current ownership model in less than three years since Ineos bough a minority stake from the Glazer family at the start of 2024.

Mauricio Pochettino has succeeded Michael Carrick, who followed Erik ten Hag and Ruben Amorim in failed tenures at Old Trafford, with his first game in charge on Sunday at Liverpool.

United spent almost £150m in the summer and despite disastrous form and outrage from many fans, Berrada insists the group of five signings under Carrick last summer helped bolster the squad.

"You have to take it in the context of the last few years," Berrada told Sky Sports. "The summer of 2025, I think the recruitment team was widely praised with the four players that were signed. And they did very well. They helped the team achieve Champions League qualification. When we look at this summer, we went in knowing what we needed to do.

“Of course, there have been different dynamics at play. It's probably been the summer or the transfer window where there's been the highest inflation in transfer fees.

"And we've said publicly and we've said consistently in the last few years that we need to stay disciplined. We need to take the right decisions. We need to buy the right players for the right value at the right time in terms of what the squad needs. And when we look at the summer transfer window of 2026, I know we're receiving a lot of criticism for it, but we're actually happy with the players that we bought.

"We think that Carlos Baleba, Youri Tielemans, Andrey Santos, and Karl Darlow - and let's not forget that we've also welcomed back Marcus Rashford, who had a very good season on loan at Barcelona - these are five additions that we think have made the squad fundamentally better.

"It's true that we lost Casemiro, who is a top player and gave a lot to the club. We also lost Manuel Ugarte to, unfortunately, an ACL injury. So our focus was very much about straightening the midfield. When we started the transfer window, we really only had Kobbie Mainoo as our midfielder. So we wanted to reinforce that, and we did it."
