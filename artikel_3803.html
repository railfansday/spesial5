If you're playing GTA 6 on Xbox, you should turn off your console's automatic upload feature or you could risk having your Xbox account suspended. An Xbox user on Reddit reported receiving a two-day, unrepealable ban for automatically uploading a 28-second clip of GTA 6 characters at the beach. According to the Reddit user, sangobar, the clip was almost immediately flagged by the Xbox Network for either violating the Xbox Community Standards or Microsoft Services Agreement terms (the email they received from Xbox doesn't specify which). However, the user claims the clip only showed characters in beachwear and the clip was therefore incorrectly flagged.So, to avoid the risk of an Xbox ban it is better to be safe than sorry, to disable your Xbox automatic upload feature, and to understand what Xbox's guidelines are around the content you can upload to the Xbox Network.

GTA 6 clips could potentially violate the 'Keep your content clean' term of the Xbox Community Standards, specifically the 'Make stuff that's suitable for all audiences rule', which states: "The Xbox community must be friendly and accessible to everyone, this isn't the appropriate place for sexual content. Keep your public posts and messages suitable for all ages and audiences."

According to Xbox, some examples of content that could violate this term include sharing or distributing non-consensual intimate imagery, links or imagery that displays pornography, screenshots or game clips that include mature sexual content, custom gamerpics that contains nudity, lewd messages to other players, and distasteful or inappropriate pictures. In other words, be careful before sharing images or clips of GTA 6's more mature content, like GTA 6 strip clubs.
