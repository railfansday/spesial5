Desktop productivity game renal spring: Focus as a Kidney announced for PC

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

BOKURA developer Tokoronyori has announced renal spring: Focus as a Kidney, a desktop productivity game where you play as a dog’s kidney. It will launch for PC via Steam in early 2027.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:

When you get to work, so does the dog’s kidney.

Start a timer as you tackle everyday tasks. Your work becomes the kidney’s work, filtering toxins and helping the dog regain its energy. Don’t worry: the dog won’t die if you leave the game unattended.
