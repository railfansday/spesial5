Why Rockstar Games Includes Bigfoot And UFOs To Find In Its Big Open-World Games: ‘We Love That Stuff’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

BigfootGrand Theft Auto 6GTA 6Rob NelsonRockstar Games

For nearly as long as Rockstar has been making big open-world games, like the recently released Grand Theft Auto 6 and 2018’s Red Dead Redemption 2, players have been exploring these worlds for strange secrets, supernatural events, and cryptids like Bigfoot and Mothman. And sometimes they actually find things along these lines, which made me curious as to why Rockstar includes these oddities.

I spoke to Rob Nelson, Rockstar North’s head of production and the de facto boss on GTA 6, ahead of the massive game’s launch. And during our interview, I asked him why so many of its open-world games contain otherworldly or hard-to-explain mysteries and creatures. According to him, there are a few reasons why Rockstar added things like the Skunk Ape, which players have already found lurking in the swamps of Leonida, to GTA 6. For one thing, they have the “team size” to be able to work on all of this extra content in a way they couldn’t long ago. The other reason: “We love it.”

“Also, the fact that Florida is so fucking weird and crazy, we love that stuff,” said Nelson. “We love sort of playing with players. You never know whether it’s real paranormal stuff or it’s phony paranormal stuff. Is it really Bigfoot? Is it a guy wearing a Bigfoot mask? We just love it.”

Nelson explained that the team also wanted the map in GTA 6 to feel completely full and packed with things to do and see. They wanted it to be “constantly surprising you.” It also helps that, according to Nelson, the team working on GTA 6 has been together for “such a long time” and that there are entire groups within the studio made up of people who in the past were responsible for a lot more. Now, they can focus on smaller details and specific bits of each game, like adding ghosts in GTA V or a vampire in Red Dead Redemption 2.Â
