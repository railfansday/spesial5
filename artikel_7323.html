Amazon Advertisers Can Now Target Shoppers That Buy Elsewhere

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Advertisers can now target display and video ads to Amazon users who also shop at Walmart, Dollar General, Kroger, Walgreens, or Sephora.

This capability comes from an integration that data firm Attain has with Amazon’s ad buying tool Amazon Ads DVA+. Advertisers can build audience segments based on purchase behavior across tens of thousands of merchants and brands in Attain’s network, and activate them across Amazon’s inventory, including Prime Video as well as third-party inventory in Amazon’s network.

Attain CEO Brian Mandelbaum told ADWEEK this is “really bridging the gap between what happens on Amazon to what happens at retailers like Target and Walmart, and local grocery, etc.”

Mitchell Parton is ADWEEK's senior commerce reporter, covering retail media as well as the rise of agentic and social commerce. Before joining ADWEEK in 2026, he was a reporter at Digiday Media's Modern Retail and before that wrote for several regional publications in Texas.
