From Brasil: Roma inquire about Cruzeiro’s Kevin Arroyo

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Roma are once again looking to Brazil to strengthen their attack.

After scouting him this summer, the Giallorossi have once again set their sights on Cruzeiro winger Kevin Arroyo.

As reported by Brazilian portal RTI Esporte, the 2006-born player is also facing competition from Benfica, who have long been eyeing the jewel in the crown at the Belo Horizonte club, having enjoyed a season with 8 goals and 8 assists in 50 appearances.

Cruzeiro’s asking price is high for Kevin Arroyo, valued by the Brazilians at a staggering €45 million.

The blue-and-white club has no intention of negotiating below this figure. Arroyo’s contract with Cruzeiro, which expires in December 2029, also includes a monstrous €100 million release clause, in addition to a 50% resale fee to Independiente del Valle.
