Balde offered to Roma: Giallorossi not considering him a priority

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

There’s been a lot of talk about Alejandro Balde, Barcelona’s left-back.

According to Fabrizio Romano, the player has also been offered to Roma.

But the Giallorossi are currently cold on the matter and have other priorities for the January transfer window.

Among other things, there’s talk of significant fees, especially for Italy.

The Catalans wanted to sell him in the summer but were asking for at least €50 million.

Mendes is currently in Barcelona to figure out how to proceed in the coming months. The door remains open for his arrival in Italy, it’s emphasized, only if the format were to change.
