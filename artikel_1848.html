Arteta confirms Saliba return for Arsenal, Saka out for four more weeks

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Mikel Arteta revealed Arsenal defender William Saliba is "in good condition" and could feature against Newcastle United on Saturday.

Saliba is yet to play for the Arsenal first team this season after suffering a back injury during France's World Cup semi-final defeat to Spain in the summer.

In the 2025-26 campaign, when Arsenal won their first Premier League title in 22 years and reached the Champions League final, Saliba won possession more often than any other Gunners defender (177).

The Frenchman also won the second-most duels (198), won the third-most tackles (35) and recorded the third-most interceptions (29).

Saliba played 45 minutes for Arsenal's Under-21s before the international break and has featured in a behind-closed-doors friendly.

"He's ready," Arteta said when asked if Saliba could start against Newcastle.

"He's in good condition, we want to ramp up his minutes.

"He's actually played two games, one [behind] closed doors, and the other one more official. He did very well and he's in good condition."

However, Arsenal are set to be without Bukayo Saka for another four weeks after he suffered an injury in their Champions League clash with Bayern Munich.

"We had another test, he's evolving really well, but it looks like it's going to be an eight-week injury, more or less," Arteta said on Saka.

"I think he's four [weeks] at the moment, so it's still a while for him to be back with the team."

Arsenal are looking to become only the fifth side to reach 10 wins in as few as 11 games from the start of a Premier League season, after Chelsea in 2005-06, Manchester City in 2011-12, City again in 2017-18, and Liverpool in 2019-20 (all 10 in 11) – all of whom went on to win the title.

The Gunners have only won more Premier League games against Everton (41) and West Ham (39) than they have against Newcastle (38), while their 32 clean sheets against the Magpies are the most one side has against another in the competition.
