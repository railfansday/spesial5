England squad announced: Isobel Goodwin handed first call-up for crunch World Cup play-offs

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

London City Lionesses’ Isobel Goodwin and Chelsea goalkeeper Katie Cox have been named in the Lionesses squad for the first time as Sarina Wiegman’s side bid to reach next summer’s World Cup.

Wiegman named a 25-player squad on Tuesday morning ahead of the final round of World Cup qualifying play-offs, where England face Ukraine in Murcia, Spain and then at Wembley.

The Lionesses defeated Greece 8-1 on aggregate in the first round of the play-offs and now face Ukraine.

England were finalists at the last World Cup in Australia and New Zealand back in 2023 and will be hoping to add their third major title under Wiegman next summer if they qualify, having also won the European Championships in 2022 and 2025.

The London City Lionesses forward Goodwin and Chelsea goalkeeper Cox, on loan at Nottingham Forest, have been rewarded for strong form at club level with maiden call-ups for the Lionesses at senior level as they prepare for their meetings with Ukraine on December 1 and December 5.

Sarina Wiegman said: “We know what’s at stake in these games and our focus is on producing two strong performances to secure our place at the World Cup.

“We have worked hard to put ourselves in this position and now we have to finish the job,” Wiegman said.

“Ukraine will provide a challenge and we will prepare with the same focus and determination that we always do.

“We know what Wembley can be like for our big games and that special connection we have with our supporters. We've shared many happy moments with them in the stadium and hopefully this will be another.”

England finished second in their qualifying group to world champions Spain, while Ukraine finished bottom and were beaten 6-1 and 3-0 by the Lionesses, who will draw confidence from those results as they prepare to face off with the Ukrainians for a place at the World Cup.
