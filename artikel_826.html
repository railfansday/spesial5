Julia Garner Reunites With Anna Delvey After Inventing Anna Role

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Four years after Julia Garner played disgraced criminal socialite Anna Delvey in Netflix’s Inventing Anna, the actress and her muse reunited at a screening for The Altruists in New York.

VIP is always better, but this reunion is simply the best.

Four years after Julia Garner’s Emmy-winning portrayal of Anna Delvey in Inventing Anna, the actress reunited with the fake socialite at a fan screening for The Altruists.

At the Nov. 16 event for the Netflix series—which chronicles the rise and fall of the now-incarcerated FTX founder Sam Bankman-Fried and exec Caroline Ellison—Garner and Delvey shared a warm moment together on the red carpetat the Paris Theater in New York. In fact, the convicted con artist (real name Anna Sorokin) even joined in on the question and answer segment with the Ozark alum and Altruists creator Graham Moore on stage.

Delvey, for her part, was looking as chic as ever, sporting a black skirt suit with a fitted blazer, tights and her blonde hair styled into a swoopy lob. Of course, the 35-year-old also sported a required accessory with her ankle monitor wrapped around her left leg. (Delvey is currently under a flexible house arrest from the Immigration and Customs Enforcement following her 2021 release from prison.)

Garner, for her part, matched Delvey’s color palette in a halter knee-length dress with an attached long-sleeved cover.

And while it’s been years since Garner portrayed Delvey on screen in the Netflix series, the duo’s history dates back even further to when Garner visited Delvey during her nearly four-year prison sentence following her conviction of grand larceny.

“She was very nice,” Delvey said of Garner’s visit during a 2022 appearance on the Forbidden Fruits podcast. “She came to see me and she’s a very sweet girl.”

Nevertheless, Delvey had one problem with Garner’s take on her: her iconic accent.

“It's just so weird,” she admitted, “because the way you hear yourself—like your voice is just completely different when you hear yourself on TV.”

Still, Delvey defended Garner’s artistic liberties.

“She was portraying me from like, 2015, 2016, 2017,” the Dancing with the Stars alum explained. “So now I've spent the past four years just in the States. I was surrounded by Americans so maybe my accent was different.”

To Delvey, her voice has evolved even since her prison sentence.

“I barely even speak German these days,” she said. “All I speak is English. Back in the day, I would speak French, German, English on a daily basis.”
