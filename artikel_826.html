What are rules on loan players in Champions League?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Arsenal teenager Ethan Nwaneri is currently on loan at Bundesliga side Borussia Dortmund

Ethan Nwaneri could play against parent club Arsenal in this season's Champions League for Borussia Dortmund, where he is currently out on loan.

English winger Nwaneri, 19, is currently on a season-long loan with the Bundesliga outfit.

His loan and parent clubs face each other in this season's Champions League league phase on Tuesday, 24 November.

Unlike league rules in the English football pyramid, Uefa does allow players who are out on loan to play against their parent clubs when the two sides meet in European competitions.

That means Nwaneri could feature for Dortmund when they take on the Gunners.

Nwaneri is one of 12 players in this season's Champions League who is on loan between two clubs competing in the competition.

Of those 12, just three will initially be in the situation of potentially playing for their loan team against their parent club, though that number could grow in the later rounds of the competition.

And Nwaneri is the only one of those three whose match against their parent club is still yet to take place.

Dutch defender Lutsharel Geertruida featured for loan club PSV Eindhoven against parent side RB Leipzig earlier this month, while Lask's Robert Ljubicic played against parent club AEK Athens in the opening round of league-phase fixtures.

Liverpool defender Ronald Araujo is among the players on loan from another club in the Champions League. Should Liverpool and Barcelona meet in a later round in, it would pit Araujo's permanent and loan employers against each other.

It has been a long-standing rule in Uefa competitions that players on-loan players can face the side they have temporarily left and it has seen led to some high-profile reunions.

In 2004, Spanish striker Fernando Morientes scored in both legs of the Champions League quarter-finals for Monaco against parent club Real Madrid. Morientes would go on to win the competition's Golden Boot award that year.

In 2014, Belgium goalkeeper Thibaut Courtois helped Atletico Madrid beat parent club Chelsea in the semi-finals of the competition.
