Republic of Ireland captain Collins to miss Kosovo game

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Collins picked up a knee injury in the closing stages against Austria on Saturday

Republic of Ireland captain Nathan Collins has been ruled out of Tuesday's must-win Nations League game against Kosovo with a knee injury.

Defender Collins picked up the issue in the closing stages of Saturday's 1-0 defeat by Austria in Linz and has returned to his club Brentford for treatment.

Queens Park Rangers' Jimmy Dunne will replace Collins - who also missed all four games of the September/October window - in the matchday squad for the Kosovo fixture.

Head coach Heimir Hallgrimsson has opted against drafting in Seamus Coleman, who is currently with the squad in a playing/coaching hybrid role.

"No, Jimmy Dunne will take Nathan's spot but we're really happy with what Seamus is giving to us," said Hallgrimsson.

"We obviously need to see the session today, that's the first session after the game, to see how players are but given everything I think we'll have the same squad including Jimmy."

Hallgrimsson rues 'painful' defeat for Republic of Ireland

The Republic of Ireland must win Tuesday's game to finish second in Group B3 and seal a place in March's promotion play-offs.

Hallgrimsson's side fell to a late 1-0 defeat by Kosovo in September before bouncing back to take four points from two matches against Israel.

"We need to get the same mentality, the same characters on the pitch against Kosovo," added Hallgrimsson.

"It is going to be as tough a game as it was in Austria, so just transfer that same attitude and if we do that, we will have a good byproduct, which is a win.

"It is clear we need to win the game, a draw will do nothing for us, and that is our mentality going into the game."

The Republic of Ireland's ultimate aim is securing a place at Euro 2028, which they will co-host alongside England, Wales and Scotland.

While goalkeeper Caoimhin Kelleher said playing in a tournament at home would be "amazing", he insisted the players are not looking that far ahead.

"Yeah, it would obviously be hard to describe, it would be an amazing experience, to play in a major tournament at home would be special," said the Brentford keeper.

"But I don't think we can think too far ahead into that. Obviously we've got to focus on tomorrow and trying to get a good result and we'll look towards that then when it comes.

"But we all know the opportunity that's there for us if we can perform, so it'll be a great opportunity."
