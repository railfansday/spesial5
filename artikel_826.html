Lisa’s ‘Viva La Lisa’ Las Vegas Residency Setlist: Every Song From Night 1

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Lisa performed all six songs from her new EP, "Press Play," while making history on the Las Vegas Strip.

Lisa wrote a new chapter in her career Thursday night (Nov. 12), becoming the first K-pop artist to headline a Las Vegas residency as she kicked off “Viva La Lisa” at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace.

Packed with fire, leather and lingerie moments, the BLACKPINK rapper, singer and dancer brought her solo catalog to the Strip for the first of six performances scheduled through Nov. 29. Originally announced as a four-show engagement, the production expanded after the initial dates sold out in less than 10 minutes. Additional performances are scheduled for Nov. 13-14 and 27-29.

Divided into five chapters, the 90-minute, perfectly choreographed “Viva La Lisa” draws heavily from her debut solo album, Alter Ego, released in February 2025, while putting her new six-track EP, Press Play, front and center.

Released Oct. 23, the project is represented in its entirety, from the cinematic “Tarantino,” which opens the show, to “SaWaDiKa,” the Thai-inspired single that closes the evening. The remaining four tracks—“V12,” “Cobra,” “Cut U Off,” and “Sweat”—are sprinkled throughout.

The setlist embraces all the major hits from her still-new but momentous K-pop culture run, including “Money,” her breakout solo single from 2021, and “Rockstar,” along with collaborations “New Woman,” featuring Rosalía, and “Born Again,” with Doja Cat and Raye.

Her first solo album, Alter Ego, debuted at No. 7 on the Billboard 200, establishing her career outside BLACKPINK. Performing in Vegas signifies another milestone for the Thai-born performer, who made her solo Coachella debut in 2025 and expanded into acting with HBO’s The White Lotus.
