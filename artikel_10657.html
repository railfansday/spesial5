Parent who pooped in school hallway after losing football game 42-0 investigated by police

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A parent in Michigan has come under fire after deciding to poop in the hallway of an opposing school after their child’s school lost a football game by 42-0.

There are plenty of people out there who don’t act too graciously after losing a sports matchup or game. Some might decide to blame everyone else but themselves for the loss, while others rage and get angry about it.

In the case of a parent in Bedford, Michigan, they decided to leave a pretty smelly message after losing 42-0 to Grosse Pointe South High School in a game of football.

As per The Detroit News, the parent – who has not been named – was caught by Grosse Pointe South High School’s CCTV system as heading into the hallway and defecating on the floor, seemingly as a protest against the defeat.

“This behavior is wholly unacceptable and is inconsistent with the standards of conduct and respect that the District expects of anyone entering its school facilities,” the school system told The Detroit News.

“Regardless of the outcome of a game, all individuals are expected to demonstrate appropriate conduct and respect for our students, schools, and communities.”

Additionally, a police report was filed against the unnamed parent, with Bedford Public Schools noting they will be “fully cooperating” with law enforcement and the other school district’s investigation.

Back in August, a man was warned by police after he tripped a nine-year-old during a football game just as he was about to score a touchdown.

The 41-year-old, from Racine, Milwaukee, admitted he got “upset” by the fact that the youngster was going to score.
