Better signs agreement with mortgage servicer to grow HELOC volume

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Better signed an agreement with an unnamed mortgage servicer to offer HELOCs and home equity loans to existing borrowers. The Week 7 scorecard also reported $38.97M in annualized corporate savings since Nov. 17 as part of its $64.2M savings target.  AI Summary

Better Home & Finance Holding Co. signed an agreement with an unnamed mortgage servicer to offer home equity lines of credit (HELOCs) and home equity loans to existing borrowers, expanding its lead-generation channels as the company continues its turnaround efforts.

The agreement, announced Tuesday in the company’s Week 7 Better 2.0 scorecard, is expected to provide a lower-cost customer acquisition channel.

Since its Week 6 scorecard on Nov. 17, Better has identified additional efficiencies across its corporate organization that are expected to generate a total of $38.97 million in annualized savings. The company also reported $770,000 in additional annualized savings from mortgage operations.

Better said it plans to pursue another $1.5 million in vendor-related reductions by Dec. 1. The company is working toward a broader cost savings target of $64.2 million.

“HELOC volume with our partners continues to grow each week, four more brokerages are lined up to join our wholesale platform, and two new partnerships are set to launch. At the same time, we’re moving toward a signed agreement to sell Birmingham Bank, which will let us focus fully on our U.S. business,” said Vishal Garg, Better’s founder and head of product, platform and innovation.

The company has received five term sheets for about $50 million in debt financing tied to the proposed sale of the bank and is continuing discussions with prospective financing partners.

The company said its AI-powered HELOC partnership with a large existing customer is live, with volume increasing weekly. Better is also preparing a multichannel campaign to expand the product through its wholesale platform and onboard additional brokerages.

Four more mortgage brokerages are in the pipeline to join the platform. Two previously announced partnerships — with a rental credit management platform and a home inspection company — are expected to launch early next week.

Better is also advancing discussions with three other major partners and a previously revived large-scale partnership, its release stated.
