Investors Are Throwing Their Weight Behind Ethereum—Details

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Investor confidence in Ethereum appears to be holding firm according to a recent analysis from Cryptoquant, even as the cryptocurrency’s price has come under pressure. A clear divergence has opened up between Ethereum’s market performance and its staking activity, with the staking ratio continuing to climb despite the recent decline in ETH’s value, a trend that could help support the network’s supply dynamics over time.

The Ethereum 2.0 staking rate has risen by roughly 4.32% since May, meaning a growing portion of the total ETH supply is now locked in staking. This shift reduces the amount of ETH freely available for trading and introduces a potentially positive supply-side effect. While ETH’s price on Binance has dropped from about $2,800 in October to $2,132, the staking ratio has continued to rise, pointing to sustained investor commitment amid the weakness.

Around 36.32% of Ethereum’s total supply is currently staked, which limits the liquid portion that can be readily sold. Greater participation in staking may ease selling pressure and help stabilize prices if demand remains solid.

However, staked ETH is not permanently locked, since validators can begin withdrawals at any time. Meanwhile, total staking inflows have fallen by 40.11%, underscoring the need to separate longer-term growth in the staking ratio from shorter-term daily flow data.

The standout pattern is the negative correlation between ETH’s price and its staking ratio. As prices have moved lower, staking participation has continued to rise, suggesting investors have not meaningfully dialed back their commitment.

Conclusively, Cryptoquant’s report indicates that Ethereum still carries a potentially supportive supply-side dynamic for the medium to longer term. If the market can absorb current selling pressure and draw in enough demand during this correction, the conditions could be set for a firmer upward move ahead.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​
