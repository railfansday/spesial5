My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic Is Back, And It's Better Than Ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

BOOM! Studios’ November 2026 My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic series opens with a story that understands what makes the Mane Six an entertaining and beloved group. Their personalities are funniest when they collide, but their differences also give them room to help one another. Written by Seanan McGuire, illustrated by Amy Meberson, and colored by Joana Lafuente, issue #1 reunites the familiar cast for a messy, friendship-focused adventure.

Rather than introduce a sweeping threat or a new corner of Equestria, the premiere starts with a party gone wrong. Pinkie Pie’s attempt at a prank leads to an accident involving something Twilight Sparkle treasures, and the friends have to work out how to make things right. The setup is light, but the damaged object gives the issue a question to explore.

The comic establishes its comedic rhythm through Pinkie and Rainbow Dash’s antics in Twilight’s castle. Pinkie’s contraptions and confidence repeatedly create problems, while Rainbow’s impatience adds another layer of chaos. Applejack gets caught in the mess, and the frosting-covered mishaps turn the opening pages into a lively showcase for the cast’s timing. Meberson’s expressive poses and Lafuente’s vivid frosting splashes make the chaos easy to read.

That slapstick is not just background decoration. The accident leaves the castle library in disarray and damages a book Twilight had been studying. The Mane Six clean up together, with Rainbow using her speed, Applejack pitching in, and Rarity bringing her standards to the task. Their teamwork gives the opening a recognizable Friendship Is Magic feel, while keeping the pace moving.

The story becomes more interesting when Twilight explains why the ruined book matters. It is her only known copy, and its author disappeared after completing it. The book is a friendship story, but not a simple celebration of companionship as it examines how a friendship can fracture. That premise gives Twilight a personal reason to recover it, beyond its rarity.

McGuire connects Twilight’s interest in the book to Fluttershy’s news that she has made a new friend. The parallel is gentle rather than heavy-handed. While the Mane Six prepare a surprise party for Fluttershy, the comic shows how good intentions, distraction, and Pinkie’s enthusiasm combine into an unintended disaster. The conflict suits the series’ focus on relationships.

The comic’s history adds a wistful note. Its author, Posey Pan, once lived in Seaward Shoals with her best friend, Merry Weather. The panels show the two fillies sharing books before their futures pull them apart. Twilight wants to understand what happened and preserve the lesson, making the missing volume more than a rare collectible. The flashback gives the adventure an emotional mystery.

The issue turns that emotional thread into a concrete adventure. The friends learn that another copy might be found at Rainbow Falls Trader’s Exchange, then head toward Picca-Filly Circus, a gathering place for traders. Because the search has barely begun, the debut feels more like a promising setup than a complete adventure; issue #2 of the My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic comic is advertised for December 2026.
