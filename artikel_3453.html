Whether your favorite recent mystery has been one of the Knives Out movies, or one of the twisty seasons of Only Murders in the Building, everyone can agree that a good, well-developed crime mystery makes for some gripping TV. After introducing all its players, a sudden death spirals the characters into despair and/or purpose, with the leading detective (or trio of detectives) tracing every clue, talking to every suspect, and eventually uncovering the real killer. A mystery like this is certainly formulaic, but must-watch TV nonetheless.

Among the recent releases to deliver a mystery like this is Netflix's The Final Problem. The Spanish series, released on September 25, 2026, is based on the novel by Arturo Pérez-Reverte, and delivers the perfect blend of period drama and nail-biting mystery.

Much like Steve Martin's Charles-Haden Savage in Only Murders in the Building, The Final Problem centers on a semi-retired actor whose acting days are likely behind him, but he is determined to relaunch his career. In the case of The Final Problem, the protagonist is Benjamin Basil (José Coronado), an actor who rose to fame playing none other than Sherlock Holmes onscreen. The series starts off as he travels to a small island near Mallorca in the hopes of relaunching his career and instead has to step into his character's shoes when one of the guests, a quiet English tourist named Elisa Mander, is found dead. What is initially seen as a suicide quickly turns out to be something far more disturbing: a murder.

Set in the spring of 1959, the series then sees the hotel go into lockdown as a storm isolates them more than ever, and Basil begins investigating all the guests and hotel staff. Along the way, Basil relies on Adela Figueroa (María Valverde) and Foxá (Martiño Rivas), as they together search the halls of the luxurious hotel for clues, and test the composure of the hotel's manager, Mrs. Ausländer (Maribel Verdú). "In a place no one can leave and no one can reach, every guest and every member of staff becomes a suspect in a crime that grows more complex by the minute," the official description reads.

The Final Problem has been out for a little over a month, and it's already making waves. During its first week, the series amassed a total of 2.4 million views, with a second week increase to 3.2 million views. In addition to the massive streaming numbers, The Final Problem has also scored a flawless 100% score on Rotten Tomatoes among critics. Among the raving reviews are critics calling the series "enjoyable" and highlighting Coronado's performance as the lead. "José Coronado sparkles as the actor who made his name playing Sherlock Holmes, trying his hand at solving a classic locked-room mystery," wrote one review. "The Final Problem is a classic whodunnit, and that’s a good thing, thanks to a lavish setting and a fine lead performance from Coronado," echoed another.

The show holds a 92% score on Rotten Tomatoes.

With all that said, while mystery shows aren't exactly sparse on streaming these days, The Final Problem is proving its place in the genre. With a chic period setting, a full international production, and a mystery that takes viewers into the lives of Spain's crème de la crème, the series has quickly become a must-watch in the genre, in addition to a major hit for Netflix.
