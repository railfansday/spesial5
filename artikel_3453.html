Forget ‘Twilight,’ Netflix’s 6-Season Series Is Leaving Streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The YA supernatural genre gained its popularity due to the global success of Twilight. Fans around the world found themselves fascinated by the existence of vampires and werewolves, eagerly wanting more monster content for young adult audiences. A slew of new shows and movies released in the years after Twilight. The Vampire Diaries, True Blood, and The Originals gave viewers similar content to enjoy, but one show focused on the underrated side that Twilight didn't share enough about.

Teen Wolf truly feels like its own story. Loosely based off the 1985 film of the same name, starring Michael J. Fox, the show takes the story in countless unique directions. When Scott (Tyler Posey) is bitten by a werewolf, his whole life changes in a single moment, and he finds himself thrown into a world full of supernatural threats, dangerous endeavors, and unlikely allies. The show feels reminiscent of other supernatural media of the time, but doesn't limit itself to the mold Twilight created.

Twilight centers around the existence of vampires with werewolves serving as secondary supernatural forces. While werewolves are present in the saga, they aren't fully explored in the same way vampires are. Instead of focusing on vampires, Teen Wolf breaks from the mold and dedicates itself wholly to exploring what becoming a werewolf is like. The show feels like it could be from Jacob Black's (Taylor Lautner) perspective as a werewolf. Balancing high school and the experience of being a supernatural creature is truly explored in this show, giving audiences a look into what further development into Jacob's werewolf experience might have been like.

As Twilight centers heavily on vampires, the werewolves often feel less developed or focused on. Jacob is reduced to an alternative option within Bella's love triangle. Teen Wolf gives attention to werewolves in a way that Twilight would have benefited from. Scott could be seen as a version of Jacob that fans never got to see, giving them a glimpse into another side of Jacob's character that could have been explored.

In every generation, there is an iconic line that changes history.

As he withholds the truth of his double life from his family and friends, Scott's personal life becomes very complicated. His friendships become embroiled in his werewolf activities, often pushing his friends too far as they try to stop supernatural threats. Scott's relationship with Allison is directly impacted by his transformation. Audiences rooted for the pair to get together, but when they did, new challenges arose that viewers and Scott weren't expecting. Allison's family and hunting background presents a direct and personal danger that Scott has to skirt around.

Outside his personal life, Scott finds himself thrown into the middle of a supernatural disaster. With his town serving as a beacon for paranormal activities, Scott becomes a protector of his town. The weight of the role impacts Scott and puts real pressure onto this young character who never expected to be a hero. As he grows in his own abilities, Scott finds a way to haphazardly balance his two lives, but it's never the same as before. Becoming a werewolf isn't an easy transition, and the show doesn't shy away from all the issues Scott deals with after being bitten.

Teen Wolf could have followed in Twilight's footsteps. It would have taken the easy route and presented Twilight with some small alterations, but the show decided to push further. The series introduces new monsters and threats that keep the show feeling unique and unlike anything else in the YA supernatural genre. Its intense plot lines and myriad of entertaining characters make for the perfect binge for fans of Twilight looking for more werewolf content.
