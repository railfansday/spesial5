Margot Robbie’s Answer to ‘The Mummy’ Officially Takes Over the World

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

There has been a lot of talk about an Ocean’s Eleven prequel, with the project seemingly getting stuck for several years. Thankfully, earlier this year, it is starting to take shape, as Margot Robbie has been announced to star opposite Bradley Cooper, who is also directing, and the film has been scheduled for release on June 25, 2027. The film has also tapped a recent Oscar nominee in a villainous role, as Wagner Moura joins Robbie and Cooper in a film that is still kept tightly under wraps.

As more details about this Ocean's prequel filter through, Robbie fans are searching for her work on streaming to keep them entertained. One film to benefit is The Legend of Tarzan, a 2016 action-adventure film starring Robbie that was intended to set the wheels in motion on a new blockbuster franchise. Alas, this wasn't to be, with the film struggling to make the desired impact at the box office. By earning just $350 million at the worldwide box office against a reported budget of $180 million, the film undeniably underperformed, and not just commercially.

The Legend of Tarzan, which was directed by Harry Potter's David Yates and featured the likes of Samuel L. Jackson, Alexander Skarsgård, Djimon Hounsou, and Christoph Waltz in the cast, also flopped critically. The film scored just 35% on the review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes, with Collider's review at the time saying, "The Legend of Tarzan plays it so safe at every turn that it forgets that a daring adventure should be daring." Despite all this, the film has become an unlikely streaming hit ten years later. At the time of writing, The Legend of Tarzan is the most-watched movie on HBO Max in the world.

You belong somewhere old enough that the mountains have names and somebody can still tell you who gave them. Middle-earth is for people who think the right thing is worth doing even when it will not be finished in their lifetime.

You want the sea, a crew, and absolutely nobody telling you where you are allowed to go. The Grand Line is the world for people whose main ambition in life is finding out what is over there.

You would rather be feared and useful than liked and useless. The Continent is a world where the monsters are the straightforward part of the job and the people are where it all gets complicated.

You want the myths to be real and loose in the actual world, and you want to find out at about thirteen that you were never anywhere near as ordinary as everybody kept telling you.

Although she's never far from a box office hit, Robbie is also no stranger to a divisive film that splits both audience and critical opinion. She found both with her 2026 adaptation of Emily Brontë’s novel, Wuthering Heights, alongside Jacob Elordi. "Emily Brontë is absolutely rolling in her grave," wrote Therese Lacson in Collider's review of the movie, which was one of many entirely underwhelmed by this misjudged interpretation. However, the film was a big box office hit, earning almost $250 million.
