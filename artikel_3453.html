Snoopy's Adorable New Holiday Igloo Collection Is Officially Here

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The good news keeps coming for Peanuts merchandise collectors eager to get the next piece of fun memorabilia. The added benefit to some collaborations, though, is that they can have practical use with just as much potential as a cute new showpiece for their display, with Snoopy at the center of each of them. In the case of the Igloo brand's newest products, they have the benefit of keeping your cold drinks cool, hot drinks warm, and with the Igloo Peanuts Christmas KoolTunes cooler, have a party for virtually any occasion. The best part is, for those looking to shop ahead of the holidays, each of these products are in stock now.

As shown on the Igloo website, the Peanuts Christmas KoolTunes cooler is easily the biggest draw of a set of three new releases. Accompanying it are two 15oz travel stainless steel travel coffee mugs, each with different Christmas-themed designs perfect for getting into a festive mood either on the go or while relaxing at home. It's one of the most striking Peanuts collabs of the year, and while the decals on the Igloo cooler certainly shout Christmas carols from the rooftops with Charlie Brown and the gang, it's got just the right added perks to juice up any home Christmas party.

Measuring 14.57" x 10.91" x 13.9", able to hold 26 12-ounce cans, and the added perk of connecting via Bluetooth, one can easily use Igloo's new Peanuts Christmas KoolTunes cooler to play relaxing tunes perfect for the occasion like "Christmas Time Is Here". Lay out the cool beverages at the party in this iconic company's delightful new product while gathering friends, family, and other loved ones to celebrate warmth and togetherness. However, given the premium quality and added features, these latest additions don't come without a hefty price tag.

As seen, these premium products are certainly tailored to the holiday season, but can reasonably find use throughout winter, or for those who adore Peanuts, something to potentially use year-round within reason. Igloo's never been shy about releasing product collabs for any number of franchises, including a striking Naruto set back in 2023. But when it comes to Peanuts, like with many brands, Igloo seems eager to pull out all the stops to release something truly special to celebrate one of America's most believed comic strips ever made.

With the recent Hockey Halloween event, Starbucks collab, and some of the cutest Crocs designs ever seen, Peanuts is finishing out 2026 with some truly stellar products alongside impressive brands and franchises. This means fall Peanuts collectors have gotten their Great Pumpkin-themed goodies, and for those who missed out on the special Starbucks paraphernalia, they can pick up their new travel mugs and welcome fellow fans while sharing their freshly-baked Charlie Brown-ies by Duncan Hines. Bobbleheads, watches, footwear, and cups for all seasons have resulted in a pretty great year for the Peanuts brand.

Celebrating the 60th anniversary of It's the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown, a massive new Starbucks drop lands on September 15.

But Igloo's is possibly the most striking and interesting product lineup yet, a perfect way to celebrate the series and season by sharing with others and brightening the mood with some choice tunes. Peanuts is a special series with heavy significance across multiple generations worldwide, and it's hard to deny the charm of this latest collab, so be sure to consider the price first, and place orders sooner rather than later. As the holiday season approaches, the Christmas rush will gobble these products up like the Kite-Eating Tree trying to ruin Charlie Brown's day.
