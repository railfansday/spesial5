Alibaba is proposing to spin off its in-house AI chip unit T-Head in Hong Kong

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The Hong Kong exchange has cleared Alibaba to proceed; the size and structure of the offering have not been finalized

Alibaba Group Holding announced in an exchange filing on Tuesday that T-Head, its in-house chip design unit, has applied to list in Hong Kong, advancing a planned spin-off, according to Bloomberg.

Alibaba said the Hong Kong bourse has given it approval to go ahead, and T-Head filed its listing application on Sept. 11. The offering's scale and format remain undecided.

T-Head is fully held by Alibaba and, according to the filing, is expected to stay within the group as a subsidiary once it is listed. Alibaba characterized the unit as a supplier of data-center chips spanning the full technology stack, which power computing systems at the system level for customers in China.

Alibaba said the spin-off would give investors a way to price T-Head on its own, apart from the wider group. It also could draw in investors who want a pure-play stake in the chip business, the company said.

A standalone listing would let T-Head turn directly to capital markets for both equity and debt, Alibaba said. The company said it would also make it easier for the unit to obtain loans from banks.

The filing follows a series of chip announcements from Alibaba. The company unveiled a new AI chip, the Zhenwu V900, in September at its annual Apsara Conference in Hangzhou. CEO Eddie Wu said the chip delivers three times the performance of the previous Zhenwu M890, with mass production and commercial release targeted for the first quarter of 2027.

Alibaba released the Zhenwu M890 in May, and its Zhenwu chips were already in use by more than 650 customers across sectors including automotive, finance, energy and manufacturing. The company has committed to spending more than $53 billion over three years on cloud and AI infrastructure.

U.S. export controls that cut off Chinese companies from Nvidia $NVDA's top-tier chips have accelerated efforts by firms such as Alibaba to build their own semiconductor capabilities.
