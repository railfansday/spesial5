Super Micro Computer stock pops after quarterly sales more than tripled

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Supermicro expects net sales of $72.0 billion to $82.0 billion for fiscal 2027 and $19.5 billion to $21.5 billion next quarter

Super Micro Computer reported first-quarter net sales of $19.9 billion for the quarter ended Sept. 30, up from $5.0 billion a year earlier, and its stock gained 6.4% in extended trading on Tuesday.

Net sales in the prior quarter were $11.1 billion. Net income was $1.59 billion, compared with $168 million a year earlier. Diluted earnings per share were $1.75, versus $0.26 a year earlier, and non-GAAP diluted earnings per share were $1.81, versus $0.35.

Gross margin was 12.7%, up from 9.3% a year earlier and down from 17.5% in the prior quarter.

"Supermicro's transformation into a Total AI and IT Datacenter Solution provider continues to accelerate, delivering record revenue of $19.9 billion for the first quarter fiscal 2027," Charles Liang, the company's founder, president and CEO, said in a statement.

Chief Financial Officer Mark Long told MarketWatch that a "high-quality customer base" is behind the company's revenue growth.

For the second quarter, which ends Dec. 31, the company expects net sales of $19.5 billion to $21.5 billion. It expects GAAP net income of $1.59 to $1.83 per diluted share and non-GAAP net income of $1.66 to $1.90 per diluted share.

For fiscal 2027, the company expects net sales of $72.0 billion to $82.0 billion.

Operations used $434 million in cash in the quarter. As of Sept. 30, the company held $6.9 billion in cash and cash equivalents and had $8.6 billion in total bank debt and convertible notes.

The company said the figures are unaudited and preliminary because its financial closing procedures for the quarter are not complete.

Supermicro will hold a live audio webcast to review the results at 5 p.m. ET on Tuesday.
