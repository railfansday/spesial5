For years, researchers and investors have been searching for a treatment that’s as effective in treating sickle cell disease as gene therapy — but without the many drawbacks.

Biotech entrepreneur Romesh Subramanian thinks he might have one. On Tuesday, he announced the founding of new startup Marrow Therapeutics to pursue it, with $173 million in Series A and B funding from Scion Life Sciences, venBio, OrbiMed, and others.

“We expect it to be as good as [gene therapy] in patients,” Subramanian said, “but to be proven for sure.”
