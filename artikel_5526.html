CVS Health is splitting into two companies, separating its pharmacies from insurance

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

CVS Health expects the separation of Aetna and Caremark from about 9,000 pharmacies to be completed in the first half of 2028

CVS Health $CVS announced plans to split into two independent public companies on Wednesday, separating its retail pharmacies from Aetna and CVS Caremark. The decision came after the board conducted a strategic assessment of the business.

One company will hold Aetna, CVS Caremark, CVS Specialty, Oak Street Health and Signify Health. It will keep the CVS Health name in the interim. The second company, called CVS Pharmacy, will hold about 9,000 pharmacies, the mail pharmacy, MinuteClinic (including virtual care) and the Health100 technology services business.

CVS Health Chairman and CEO David Joyner will hold the same roles at the healthcare solutions company once the split is complete. Prem Shah, group president of CVS Health, will become CEO of CVS Pharmacy. The company said it will announce other leaders and board members at a future date.

“From a position of strength, and a focus on the long term, we plan to create two focused companies to improve consumer engagement, affordability, and care coordination,” Joyner said in a statement, according to CNBC.

Joyner told Bloomberg that cutting the retail unit loose from the rest of the company would give it room to grow more rapidly than it could under the existing umbrella.

CVS Health expects the separation to be completed in the first half of 2028. It is intended to be tax-free to the company and its shareholders for U.S. federal income tax purposes. CVS Health shareholders will own stock in both companies.

Completion is subject to final approval by the board, effectiveness of a Form 10 registration statement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission, and confirmation of the tax-free treatment. CVS Health said it expects both companies to hold solid investment-grade ratings and to pay a dividend.

CVS Pharmacy plans to expand services from its nearly 30,000 pharmacists and to pursue partnerships with payers, pharmacy benefit managers and manufacturers, the company said. Until the split is complete, CVS Health will operate as it does today. Customers, patients and clients will continue to receive their medicine, care and benefits without interruption, the company said.

In a separate release on Wednesday, CVS Health reported third-quarter results and lifted its full-year 2026 earnings forecast, which now stands at a minimum of $8.20 per share versus the earlier $7.90 to $8.10 range.

The plan would partly unwind the 2018 deal, valued at $78 billion, in which CVS bought Aetna and formed the combined company, according to CNBC.

Lawmakers in Arkansas and Tennessee have enacted measures over the past two years intended to stop a single company from both administering drug coverage and owning storefront and mail-service pharmacies, according to The New York Times.
