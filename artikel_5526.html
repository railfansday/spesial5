Blackstone agreed to buy a majority stake in Amazon data centers for about $5 billion

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Free daily briefing on global business news.

AWS will keep a 25% stake and lease back all 13 powered shells, and the deal is expected to close in the fourth quarter

Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust, or BXDC, signed a definitive agreement on Monday to take a 75% stake in a pair of joint ventures formed with Amazon $AMZN’s cloud unit, Amazon Web Services, in a deal valued at about $5 billion. BXDC is externally managed by an affiliate of Blackstone.

The price reflects an initial capitalization rate of 7.0%, the company said. The transaction is BXDC's first investment.

The joint ventures will own 13 newly constructed powered shells across two AWS data center campuses. AWS will hold on to the remaining 25% of the ventures and rent the whole portfolio back, starting with a 12-year term that can be extended to as long as 32 years.

BXDC stock rose more than 6% in premarket trading on Monday.

BXDC CEO and President Nick Pell said in a statement that the quality, basis and tenant profile of the assets make them a match for deploying the trust's IPO proceeds. The company said the purchase suits its approach of holding stabilized data centers that cloud providers rent under long-term agreements.

“We’re focused on delivering long-term local benefits, from creating good jobs and free workforce training programs to supporting local priorities,” Prasad Kalyanaraman, vice president of AWS Infrastructure, said in a statement.

The transaction is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026 and is subject to certain closing conditions, the company said. Morgan Stanley $MS, CBRE Capital Markets, Citi, Goldman Sachs $GS, J.P. Morgan and Wells Fargo $WFC are serving as financial advisers. Simpson Thacher & Bartlett is legal adviser to Blackstone.

The announcement also appears as an exhibit to an SEC filing.
