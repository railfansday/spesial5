Real Estate Expert Matt Nuzie Explains Buying and Selling a Home at the Same Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The article explains how homeowners can plan their finances, decide whether to buy or sell first, and coordinate closing dates when moving from one home to the next.

TRUMBULL, Conn., Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ --

How can homeowners manage buying and selling a home at the same time? A HelloNation article provides the answer, featuring insights from Matt Nuzie, Real Estate Expert with REMAX Right Choice in Trumbull, Connecticut. The newly published article explains how financial planning, mortgage options, and closing dates affect both sides of the move.

The article explains that selling your current home first gives homeowners certainty. Once the sale closes, they know exactly what they have for a down payment and a price range.

Selling first also removes the risk of carrying two homes. The trade-off is that homeowners may need temporary housing, storage, or two moves.

Buying a new home first lets homeowners move on their own schedule. The article notes that it can mean covering two mortgage payments, property taxes, insurance, and utilities, so a conversation with a lender should come before any offer.

How Do Mortgage Options Affect Buying and Selling?

According to the article, mortgage options often decide the order. Some buyers need the equity from their current home for a down payment, while others qualify for financing that lets them buy before selling.

Matt Nuzie recommends reviewing income, savings, and the current mortgage with a lender before touring homes.  A home sale contingency makes a purchase dependent on selling the buyer's current home. It offers protection, but many sellers prefer offers without one when they have several to choose from.

Matt Nuzie notes that scheduling both closings close together lets homeowners move directly from one home to the next.

Closings can be delayed for ordinary reasons, such as a lender's request for documents or an inspection issue, so building in some breathing room is wise.

A rent-back agreement can let sellers stay in the home for an agreed period after closing. Short-term temporary housing is another option when the dates don't line up.

How Can Homeowners Prepare for Unexpected Costs?

The article recommends starting early on cleaning, small repairs, and listing photos, since they often take longer than expected.

Moving, storage, repairs, and temporary housing can add up quickly, so setting aside extra savings helps.

Matt Nuzie also emphasizes keeping the real estate agent, lender, and attorney informed of deadlines, and reading contract terms closely before signing.

How to Buy and Sell a Home at the Same Time features insights from Matt Nuzie, Real Estate Expert of Trumbull, Connecticut, in HelloNation.

Is It Better to Buy or Sell a Home First?

According to Matt Nuzie, it depends on your finances, your moving plans, and the local real estate market. Selling first brings financial certainty, while buying first offers more flexibility with the move.

Matt Nuzie explains through insights featured in the article that a home sale contingency makes a purchase dependent on selling an existing home. It offers protection, though many sellers prefer offers without one.

Can You Close on Two Homes at the Same Time?

The article explains that homeowners can schedule both closings close together. Financing or inspection delays can still shift the timing, so some cushion is wise.

What Happens if Closing Dates Do Not Match?

Based on insights from Matt Nuzie, Real Estate Expert of Trumbull, Connecticut, homeowners may consider a rent-back agreement or short-term temporary housing. Both can provide extra time between transactions.
