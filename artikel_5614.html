Is Trump Media Selling Bitcoin? Democrats Prepare Crypto Probe

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Trump Media has moved $343 million in Bitcoin to Crypto.com, raising fresh questions if Donald Trump’s media company could be selling its crypto holdings. The transfer comes as Democrats prepare investigations into Trump’s family crypto ventures following their midterm election victories.

The company sent 4,259 BTC in two transactions, according to blockchain data tracked by Arkham. The coins came from wallets labeled as collateral for convertible notes, adding another layer of uncertainty around the Trump Media Bitcoin transfer.

The timing is difficult to ignore. Trump Media faces a potential $1 billion debt repayment deadline later this month, while incoming Democratic lawmakers are preparing to scrutinize Trump’s crypto businesses. A sale has not been confirmed, but this move puts the company’s Bitcoin treasury under pressure.

Arkham data showed Trump Media-linked wallets transferring 4,259 BTC to Crypto.com just a few hours ago. The coins were moved in two batches of more than 2,000 BTC each. The transfer represents around $343 million at recent prices. It also accounts for 30% of the 14,139 BTC Trump Media previously held, including pledged coins.

However, the destination does not confirm a sale. It is known that Trump Media has used Crypto.com for custody and trading-related transfers before, including transactions in May and August.

The latest movement is particularly notable because the Bitcoin came from wallets associated with convertible-note collateral. Trump Media’s previous filings identified 4,260.73 BTC as collateral for its notes.

A November 2 filing also disclosed that noteholders can require cash repayment on November 30. If all eligible holders exercise that option, the company could face approximately $1 billion in repayments.

Trump Media warned that refinancing or other measures might not be sufficient. In that scenario, the company could be forced to sell Bitcoin or other digital assets to meet its obligations.

This disclosure gives the Crypto.com transfer a potentially more serious explanation than routine custody management. The company may be positioning assets ahead of a major liquidity decision. Although the reason remains unconfirmed, Trump Media could be selling its Bitcoin.

The transfer also arrives as Democrats prepare to investigate Trump’s family crypto businesses after winning control of Congress in the midterm elections.

Incoming lawmakers have identified World Liberty Financial, the Trump family’s crypto venture, as a potential focus. Investigators are expected to examine foreign investment, business profits, and possible conflicts of interest involving the administration’s crypto dealings.

Senator Elizabeth Warren has pledged scrutiny of Trump’s crypto business relationships, while Representative Robert Garcia, expected to lead House Oversight, warned Trump-linked interests to prepare for investigations.

Democrats are expected to gain subpoena power when the new Congress begins in January. Reuters previously reported that investigations into Trump family businesses could begin as early as February.

The current political environment could complicate the outlook for Trump-linked companies. Congressional scrutiny may increase pressure on financial relationships, crypto holdings, and business operations connected to the president’s family.

So, is Trump Media selling its Bitcoin? There’s no clarity as of now, but a confirmed sale would raise questions if Trump Media is reducing its crypto treasury to meet financial obligations.

The next major date is November 30, when noteholders can exercise their repayment option. Investors will also be watching if the Bitcoin remains at Crypto.com, moves again, or appears in transactions that indicate an actual sale.
