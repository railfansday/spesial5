Blood Test Predicts Dementia; Diabetes and Alzheimer's; Parkinson's Data Retracted

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

— News and commentary from the world of neurology and neuroscience

In a cohort of cognitively unimpaired individuals, blood levels of phosphorylated tau 217 (p-tau217) were linked with 5- and 10-year risks of developing mild cognitive impairment and dementia. (JAMA Neurology)

Waves of cerebrospinal fluid that help remove debris from the brain during sleep were significantly reduced in a study of older adults. (Nature Aging)

Electroencephalography data showed that sleep-like slow waves during wakefulness were more frequent in people with Parkinson's disease and correlated with hallucinations and impaired cognition. (Science Translational Medicine)

People with type 2 diabetes had a 53% higher risk of Alzheimer's disease in a systematic review and meta-analysis. (Alzheimer's Research & Therapy)

The rare APOE Christchurch mutation reduced brain alpha-synuclein pathology in a mouse model of Lewy body dementia. (Cell Reports)

A plasma immunoassay for endogenously cleaved microtubule-binding region tau (eMTBR-tau) identified advanced tau pathology in Alzheimer's disease across four cohorts, with results comparable to those seen with mass spectrometry. (JAMA)

Fluid eMTBR-tau containing the residue 243 also may be a potential diagnostic biomarker for late-stage chronic traumatic encephalopathy (CTE) and symptomatic frontotemporal lobar degeneration, a case-control study suggested. (JAMA)

Neural hubs with more network connectivity were vulnerable to atrophy in multiple sclerosis, an analysis of more than 2,000 MRIs showed. (Science Advances)

Citing data manipulation, Cell Reports retracted a 2025 study of cell and mouse models of neurologic diseases like Parkinson's from Denali Therapeutics scientists. (Retraction Watch)

As part of the BRAIN Initiative, researchers mapped millions of cells in non-human primate, marmoset, and mouse brains to create a shared basal ganglia reference atlas, the NIH said.

Compared with neurotypical adults, people with autism had greater variability in encoding patterns in nearly all brain networks when watching videos, functional MRI data showed. (Journal of Neuroscience)

Irish boxer Lee Reeves died at age 31, 3 months after sustaining a head injury during a fight. (The Telegraph)
