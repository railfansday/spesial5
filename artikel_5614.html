Meta's longtime finance chief is leaving to become CFO of Fireworks AI

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Dave Wehner, Meta's chief strategy officer and CFO from 2014 to 2022, will join the AI startup valued at $17.5 billion in July

Dave Wehner, Meta $META's chief strategy officer and its chief financial officer from 2014 to 2022, is leaving the company to become CFO of Fireworks AI, according to CNBC. Wehner announced the move on X $TWTR on Friday.

Fireworks CEO Lin Qiao told CNBC that Wehner's job will be to build out the firm's financial and operational capabilities as it grows. Fireworks operates a service where developers can tailor open AI models to their own business needs and deploy them.

Wehner joined Meta, then called Facebook, in 2012, according to CNBC. Meta COO Javier Olivan said in a statement to CNBC that Wehner "brought disciplined thinking to some of our hardest company-level decisions" during his 14 years at the company.

"Fireworks has the right mission for the moment," Wehner wrote on X, according to CNBC.

Qiao wrote in a LinkedIn post on Friday, according to CNBC: "Dave told me he wanted to get back to a smaller company where he could build toward a big impact again." She added that Wehner regards AI as the technological change that has most defined his lifetime.

Meta had a market capitalization of about $1.56 trillion at Friday's close.

Fireworks AI raised $1.505 billion in a Series D round in July, valuing the company at $17.5 billion. Atreides Management, Index Ventures and TCV led the round, and Nvidia $NVDA also took part.

At the time, annualized revenue had crossed $1 billion, up fivefold from a year earlier. The company had roughly 200 employees and planned to triple that number before the end of the year.

Fireworks competes with Together AI and Baseten in the AI inference cloud market. Its customers include Uber $UBER, Shopify $SHOP, Doximity, Elastic, GitLab and MongoDB $MDB.
