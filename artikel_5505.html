Nvidia's board is preparing to oust Jensen Huang over two affairs

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Huang has said he is opposed to succession planning, and it remained uncertain who would take over if he is ultimately removed

Nvidia $NVDA's board is getting ready to push out CEO Jensen Huang following two extramarital affairs, according to The Wall Street Journal. The board convened Wednesday to consider Huang's future, and Nvidia had yet to disclose any decision on Thursday morning.

Nvidia stock fell 8% in after-hours trading Wednesday to $159.95, after closing at $173.73. The stock was still off almost 8% ahead of Thursday's open.

Huang told employees in a Sunday letter that he had cheated on his wife. That affair was with Alice Huang, a former executive at Megaspeed, a data company based in Singapore. U.S. intelligence officials think she is employed by Chinese spy agencies.

The second affair was with Kimberly Powell, who runs Nvidia’s healthcare business, according to The Journal and other outlets. Powell did not respond to the Journal’s requests for comment.

Megaspeed, a major Nvidia customer, has spent months under scrutiny from U.S. investigators over suspected chip smuggling, according to Bloomberg. Megaspeed and Nvidia have denied wrongdoing. The Justice Department is heading several probes that touch on Alice Huang, according to The Journal.

Huang, 63, has said he is opposed to succession planning, and it remained uncertain who would take over. He is Nvidia's biggest shareholder, holding roughly 3.6% of its stock.

Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation on Sunday, overseen by a committee of independent directors. The independent directors retained former acting U.S. Attorney Joon H. Kim and the law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to conduct it, the company said Tuesday. The board said it had no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised and that it had confidence in Huang.

China’s Foreign Ministry has called the espionage allegation unsubstantiated. Nvidia is scheduled to report fiscal third-quarter earnings on Nov. 17. Three House Republican committee chairs have asked Huang to appear for a private session with lawmakers on Nov. 18.
