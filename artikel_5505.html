La Gorce Island Point Lot Overlooking Biscayne Bay Is Listed for an Eye-Watering $170 Million

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A spectacular waterfront mansion on Miami’s guard-gated La Gorce Island has hit the market for a jaw-dropping $170 million.

Built in 2015, the legacy estate is reportedly owned by Betsy Sherman, wife of George Sherman, the late former chairman of Campbell’s. If the property fetches anywhere near its asking price, it would rank among Miami’s most expensive home sales ever, according to The Wall Street Journal.

The Shermans purchased the property for just $17 million in 2006 and custom-built the sprawling 18,213-square-foot estate, which now claims the top spot on the Realtor.com® weekly list of the most expensive homes.

Boasting 320 feet of Biscayne Bay frontage, the residence was designed to capture spectacular sunset views from nearly every room.

Interior highlights include an expansive great room with sliding glass doors that open to the outdoor living area, a formal dining room, a modern kitchen, and a bar.

A sculptural, curved glass staircase leads to the sleeping quarters, where a lavish primary retreat features a private terrace overlooking the bay.

Additional amenities include a fitness center, bunk room, pool, four-car garage, and deep-water dock designed to accommodate yachts.

More spectacular mansions to make the week's most expensive homes list include a Utah mountain retreat under construction, an oceanfront estate in Hawaii with sweeping Pacific Ocean views, and a rebuilt Palm Beach, FL, sanctuary.

Gold Coast: This 50-foot-wide double lot on the north side of Broadway has panoramic views from San Francisco Bay and the Golden Gate Bridge to Alcatraz and the Marin Headlands.

Built in 1908, the seven-bedroom estate was reconstructed in 2007 and has 9,001 square feet of reimagined space. The four-level home has herringbone-patterned floors, arched doorways, built-in bookshelves, custom millwork, seven fireplaces, and an elevator to each floor. A stained-glass skylight illuminates the sculptural staircase.

The formal living room is surrounded by floor-to-ceiling windows framing the waterfront views. The professional-grade kitchen, with a butler’s pantry and breakfast bar, flows into the dining room, which has patio access. The lower level has a wine cellar, a secondary laundry room, service areas, and storage space.

A private sport court is surrounded by mature greenery.

Neal Ward of Compass is the listing agent.

Teardown opportunity: This 2.33-acre property offers a rare chance to “create a dream estate on a truly grand scale.”

Built in 1959, the existing nine-bedroom home has 16,290 square feet of space. Offered “with the freedom to build from the ground up,” the property is in the country’s most expensive ZIP code and near the tech hubs of Silicon Valley. No interior photos are included with the listing, but it does show a swimming pool.

The expansive lot has room for gardens and large-scale entertaining.

Ken Deleon of Deleon Realty is the listing agent.

Fjell House: This 11,632-square-foot mountain retreat will be the largest new home in the gated Stein Eriksen Estates in Deer Valley when it is completed in 2027.

The seven-bedroom home will also have 3,567 square feet of outdoor living space. Interior highlights will include a great room with a 12-foot ceiling, an entertainer’s kitchen with a quartzite waterfall island, a spacious dining room, a home office, and dual primary suites, each with its own deck and fireplace.

A white oak floating staircase will descend to the lower level, which will have a lounge, a bar, a 172-bottle wine storage system, a golf and sports room, a fitness center with a teak sauna, three guest suites, and a bunk room with access to a patio with an eight-person hot tub and fire pit.

The home will also have an oversized three-car garage with EV-charging capabilities.

Kristen Barber of Stein Eriksen Realty Group is the listing agent.

Old Marsh Golf Club: This legacy compound, set on 1.77 lakefront acres, has a primary residence, a guest cottage, a separate apartment, and a seven-car garage.

The eight-bedroom estate has marble and wood flooring, coffered ceilings, four fireplaces, a commercial elevator, built-ins, and French doors that open to spacious patios.

Other amenities include four fully equipped kitchens, a primary suite with dual dressing rooms and separate baths, a 3,000-bottle wine gallery, an entertainment room, and a bar. Covered patios overlook mature banyan trees, manicured gardens, and a swimming pool.

The property also has access to a Pete Dye championship course, regarded for its no-tee-time policy and renowned caddie program.

Jim McCann of Premier Estate Properties is the listing agent.

The Bluffs at Mauna Kea Resort: Set in an enclave of just 11 oceanfront residences, this five-bedroom estate sits on a promontory with more than 200 feet of ocean frontage.

Overlooking Mauna Kea and Hapuna beaches, the nearly 1-acre lot on Hawai’i’s Kohala Coast is surrounded by Pacific Ocean views. Built in 2004, the 9,909-square-foot home was “refined over six years” by Mexican architects Ricardo and Victor Legorreta, with living spaces designed around light, color, and water.

Glass walls and lanais connect the indoor living areas with the coastal setting. A sunken courtyard with a black stone water feature is surrounded by patios for entertaining and enjoying the views.

The property also has a two-car garage and access to nearby golf, beaches, and dining.

Harold X Clarke Private Listings Hawaii, co listed with Carolwood Estates is the listing agent.

The Light House: This newly built waterfront estate is owned by powerboat racer and real estate investor Ross Romash and his wife, Caroline Romash, who bought the property for $2.45 million in 2021.

Located in the double guard-gated Belle Meade Island, the shimmering six-bedroom home sits on a corner lot with 120 feet of frontage overlooking Biscayne Bay. The double-height great room has disappearing glass walls that transform the space into a waterfront pavilion. The 11,358-square-foot interior includes a “showpiece” kitchen with a waterfall island and a hidden pantry, a primary suite with a water-facing terrace, and a glass-enclosed fitness center with a private poolside entrance and a bay-view sauna.

The nearly half-acre lot also has a summer kitchen, an air-conditioned eight-car auto showroom with glass garage doors, an infinity pool, a spa, a cold plunge, and a private boat dock.

Chad Carroll of Compass is the listing agent.

Intracoastal Waterway views: This seven-bedroom waterfront estate offers 1.88 acres of seclusion, complete with a private beach area.

Built in 1962, the 7,958-square-foot residence also offers the potential to expand by an additional 2,486 square feet, according to the listing. Oversized windows, retractable glass walls, built-in cabinetry, French doors, vaulted ceilings, and private guest suites are featured throughout the home.

Multiple patios provide picturesque views of the pool.

Adrian Reed of William Raveis Real Estate is the listing agent.

Under construction: This six-bedroom Old Cutler estate spans 10,260 square feet of luxurious waterfront living and is slated for completion in fall 2027.

According to The Real Deal, the property is owned by an LLC managed by Mocca Group’s Alex Pirez and Alicia Nussdorf, founder and CEO of hair removal company Completely Bare.

Floor-to-ceiling glass walls frame sweeping views of Biscayne Bay, while formal and casual living, dining, and entertaining spaces are found throughout the home. Upstairs, an ornate primary suite features a private bay-facing terrace, accompanied by four en suite bedrooms.

The property also boasts a wellness center with a gym, sauna, steam room, and cold plunge. Covered terraces overlook the pool and private boat dock.

Elyse Taylor of Mocca Realty is the listing agent.

Estate Section: This rebuilt lakefront residence was sold just two years ago for $51,631,500 to an undisclosed buyer. The sellers were Jeanne S. Siegel and her late husband, investor and businessman Herbert J. Siegel.

The nine-bedroom estate, designed by Marion Sims Wyeth, was reconstructed by the Siegels, who were also involved in a water-damage lawsuit alleging that the company that sold them the home failed to disclose its knowledge of mold caused by water intrusion.

The extensively restored interior features a bright, airy open floor plan framed by sweeping waterfront views. Outside, formal gardens and expansive lawns lead to 165.9 feet of Intracoastal Waterway frontage.

A pool, a guest house, and a deep-water dock are also located on the 1.04-acre property.

Suzanne Trapani- Frisbie of The Corcoran Group is the listing agent.

La Gorce Island: This eight-bedroom waterfront estate is reportedly owned by Betsy Sherman, wife of George Sherman, the late former chairman of the Campbell’s Company.

Built in 2015, the 18,213-square-foot mansion occupies a prominent point lot at the tip of the island, with 320 feet of open Biscayne Bay frontage. Designed to capture waterfront views from nearly every room, the property was customized for both grand entertaining and intimate waterfront living.

Highlights include a primary retreat with a bayfront balcony, a living room with retractable glass walls, and a chef’s kitchen with access to a covered terrace. A floating circular staircase leads to an upstairs primary suite overlooking the bay and pool.

The 1.23-acre property also features a four-car garage and a deep-water dock capable of accommodating large yachts.

Jill Hertzberg of Coldwell Banker Realty is the listing agent.
