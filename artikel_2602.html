Raiders tight ends quiet with Brock Bowers out

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Here is a look at how the Las Vegas Raiders' 2024 draft class fared in a 38-24 road loss at the Denver Broncos on Sunday and what to look for Sunday at the Cleveland Browns:

The first-round pick didn't play due to an ankle injury that kept him to just 11 snaps in Week 10 against Seattle. In Bowers' absence, Las Vegas tight ends Ian Thomas and Michael Mayer combined for just two catches for 23 yards and a touchdown. The Raiders are hopeful Bowers can play at Cleveland and he's clearly needed.

The second-round pick remained on the bench for the sixth straight game. He played four snaps on special teams.

The third-round pick started at right tackle as usual.

The fifth-round pick from Ohio State played just four defensive snaps. It was the second straight game he didn't play much after playing extensively for three straight games. He did play 29 snaps on special teams.

The sixth-round pick had had six kickoffs for 134 yards and he had a punt return for 13 games. He is averaging 29.9 a kick return, which is second in the NFL. He had been leading the league for several weeks.
