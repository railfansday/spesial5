Curt Cignetti breaks down Indiana decision to start Grant Wilson over Josh Hoover vs. USC

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Indiana football turned to Grant Wilson behind center before facing No. 18 USC. Coach Curt Cignetti even had a healthy Josh Hoover available following his injury against Minnesota.

But the decision paid off to roll with Wilson, as IU dismantled the Trojans 27-13 in Bloomington Saturday.

Still, Cignetti addressed why he went with the 25-year-old over the original starter Hoover. Turns out he, not the coaching staff or players, made the decision. And he made the final decision before he went to sleep the night before.

But there was more to Cignetti's decision to start Wilson. Yes, Hoover's health played a role. But "Cig" wanted to pivot back to Indiana stressing defenses down the field. So making the change to Wilson became necessary to him.

"We've been kind of playing in a tent, so to speak, the last two weeks, conservative, and we all hated it," Cignetti said to reporters. "We wanted to attack [USC] and came out early and made some plays… we wanted to play to win and attack, not play with any fear."

Indiana reverted back to attacking down the field with Wilson. The backup tallied 305 passing yards and tossed two touchdowns with one interception. And Wilson dropped the dagger sticking bomb to wide receiver Charlie Becker with under 3:30 left, which covered 42 yards and extended the lead to 14.

The move to choose Wilson became quite the gamble for "Cig." But it keeps IU in the race for the Big Ten Conference title and the College Football Playoff picture. Especially with fans in Bloomington visualizing a title repeat.
