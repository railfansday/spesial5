When will Dylan Larkin make Canadiens debut? Live updates after Red Wings trade

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Longtime Detroit Red Wings star Dylan Larkin is a member of the Montreal Canadiens after a blockbuster trade on Sunday, Nov. 22.

Larkin had requested a trade, and the Canadiens landed him for forward Kirby Dach, goaltender Samuel Montembeault, the rights to forward Michael Hage, a conditional first-round pick in 2027, a second-round pick in 2027, a fourth-round pick in 2028 and a conditional first-round pick in 2029. The Canadiens also acquired the Red Wings' 2028 second-round pick and their 2027 third-round pick.

But what comes next? When does Larkin make his Canadiens debut? What will the Red Wings say about the trade? When does Larkin return to Detroit?

Follow along for news and notes on the day after the major Red Wings-Canadiens trade:

The Canadiens announced that Larkin will play against the Los Angeles Kings on Monday night. That gives him a chance to start in front of the home fans who once chanted, "We want Larkin," when he played there in October.

The Canadiens hit the road for three games afterward.

Larkin last played on Oct. 26 with the Red Wings. He informed the team on Oct. 29 that he wouldn't play for them again. He was suspended and lost more than $1 million in pay.

Dylan Larkin will wear No. 89 with Montreal.

Dylan Larkin's willingness to go to a Canadian team provided the break the Red Wings needed to pull off a trade.

"Montreal was a team that [had] intrigue from I would say the beginning, not June, but September or so, once we started looking." Larkin's agent, Pat Brisson, told the Free Press. "Once the season started, the problem is early September, most teams are set, most rosters are set, you can't move an asset around, it's so hard.

"The right time to do this is May and June, June at the draft, because at that point they had really good deals, and it's easier to operate. But then once it gets to to camp, right around camp, it's so hard to get general managers to move a player, especially once they're all set pretty much But now, after 15 to 20 games in, you've got teams doing better than expected or vice versa, then things are opening up a little bit." - Helene St. James, Detroit Free Press

The Canadiens play in Detroit on Feb. 18. They also play there on March 20. Larkin was booed during the Red Wings' home opener.
