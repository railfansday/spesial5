Cowboys vs. Eagles: Offensive, defensive impact players in Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

With a victory over the Philadelphia Eagles this week the Dallas Cowboys have a chance to get back to .500 on the season. They've already beaten them once the season, and this time around they are the ones with the home-field advantage.

To secure the victory this week the Cowboys are going to need some impact plays on both sides of the ball. Today, we identified one offensive and defensive player we believe will make such an impact, helping to sway the game in Dallas' favor.

The Cowboys aerial attack has been lethal the past two games and a lot of that probably has to do with George Pickens' uptick in production. He's coming off back-to-back 100+ receiving yard games and has been much more involved in Dallas' passing game during that time. In the last two weeks alone he's caught 18 passes for 247 yards and four touchdowns. We believe we will see more of the same in the Thanksgiving Day matchup with the Eagles.

The Eagles are currently ranked 17th in the league allowing 222.8 receiving yards per game. They held George Pickens to just four catches for 57 yards in Week 7, but did allow him to score a touchdown in that matchup. That was then and this is now. No. 3 is playing his best football right now and QB Dak Prescott is looking his way more often because of it. Another 100-yard receiving game isn't out of the question.

Dee Winters exited the game early with a knee injury last week against the Titans and didn't return. The Cowboys surprisingly chose to replace him in the starting lineup with LB Justin Barron, who they signed as an undrafted free agent out of Syracuse last year. He's predominantly been a special teams player thus far in his young career with the Cowboys, but ended up leading the team in tackles last week with nine as an injury fill-in.

With it being a short week, No. 45 has a good chance to get his first NFL career start on Thanksgiving against the Eagles. He was impressive last week while filling in for Dee Winters and could get rewarded because of it. He seems to have the coaching staffs trust and has been productive when given the opportunity. He could have a big impact on the game helping to contain Saquon Barkley as both the run and receiver this week.
