Tom Brady weighs in on Aaron Rodgers drama: 'Nobody that wins when the family feuds'

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Aaron Rodgers isn't playing on Thanksgiving this year, but he is certainly a big topic of discussion.

Broadcasters, former players and coaches have all weighed in on the situation that marked the end of the Rodgers era in Pittsburgh. The now-former Steelers quarterback was released by the team after airing his grievances with head coach Mike McCarthy on the Nov. 25 episode of "The Pat McAfee Show."

The explosive interview could be the footnote to a 22-year career for Rodgers, with the final images of him on the field coming after a loss in Philadelphia to the Eagles on Nov. 22. And that could be a sad way to mark the end of a career, prompting the NFL on Fox's Kevin Burkhardt and Tom Brady to discuss the situation during the Thanksgiving game between the Eagles and Dallas Cowboys.

Brady, who played under Bill Belichick with the New England Patriots for 20 seasons, didn't seem to enjoy the approach Rodgers took.

"The sanctity of an NFL locker room is a very sacred place and we shouldn't ever lose track of that as players," Brady said. "It's a privilege to be in that locker room. You're fighting with your teammates, you're fighting with your coaches, you're always acting as a team together. (The locker room) shouldn't always be the place for private things to become public. That's what I'll say."

Rodgers and Brady were always connected as two of the greatest quarterbacks of their generation. Brady had the Super Bowl rings and Rodgers had the stats, making for some intense debate amongst football fans and analysts over the years.

Brady didn't dispute anything about Rodgers the player, but appeared to take issue with Rodgers the teammate.

"Certainly Hall of Fame career and he's a tremendous player. It has nothing to do with his play style," Brady said. "I was fortunate to be around a lot of great teammates over the years. What made a great teammates to me was you cared about your teammates and your coaches. And you cared about what the team was trying to accomplish. And that supersedes any individual aspect of your game that you wanna promote."

"There's nobody that wins when the family feuds."

Burkhardt pointed out that Brady and Belichick were together for a long time, prompting a comparison of the two situations. The former Patriots quarterback noted that he wasn't a perfect player and Belichick wasn't a perfect coach, but the on-field success is secondary in his post-playing days.

The seven-time Super Bowl champion said the relationships matter to him more now in retirement than anything else.

Some former teammates have taken issue with Rodgers in the aftermath of his explosive comments. Whether they have any lasting impact remains to be seen.

If nothing else, Rodgers has always been one that beats to his own drum, so it's unclear if he'll regret those comments in the future.

At this point, the only regret he seems to have is vouching for McCarthy to get an interview in Pittsburgh.
