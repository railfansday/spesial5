“We needed to make a move” – Moncada explains Milan’s multi-year pursuit of Cisse

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Geoffrey Moncada, today, has given a bit of insight into how long AC Milan were trying to sign Alphadjo Cisse.

When Milan signed Cisse, there was a bit of a mixed reaction. Although he was seen as a talent for the future, the money could’ve been spent on a first-team acquisition, with the squad lacking in certain areas.

Now, though, everyone would be glad that the decision was made to sign the young Italian. To say he’s exploded this season would be an understatement and he’s perhaps the biggest gem at the club right now.

Speaking to Calcio e Pepe, as Francesco Nasato relayed on X, Moncada went through the process of the Rossoneri’s scouting of Cisse.

“We had a scout who worked near Verona and he immediately pointed out this boy to us, Alphadjo Cisse, who played for the Under-17s and then for the Primavera, and then for the Under-20s. So we went to see him and he was very interesting.

“Two or three years ago, we launched Milan Futuro. Our idea was to sign him for that team a year earlier, but we couldn’t reach an agreement with Hellas Verona. They wanted to keep him and decided to send him on loan to Serie B.

“We followed him very closely in Serie B, too, and he literally exploded there in the first six months. We told ourselves he’d made such a good transition from the Primavera and Verona’s bench to Serie B that we needed to make a move.

“It was winter and we had to hurry. If he had gone to PSV and you had wanted to buy him later, perhaps after playing in the Champions League, the price would have risen to €30 or €40 million. Sometimes you have to speed things up a bit.”

Considering the club had followed him since he was in the Under-17s, it’s a good thing they eventually managed to sign him, especially with the way he’s started at San Siro.
