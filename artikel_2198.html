Who stays and who leaves Manchester United under Mauricio Pochettino, Gary Neville reveals list

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Shumail Sajid writes about Manchester United for Read Man Utd and Crystal Palace for Read Crystal Palace, with occasional pieces on other Premier League clubs. He has also written for FanSided's Red Devils Dispatch, Yardbarker, Anfield Central and The Sports Side.

Manchester United hired Mauricio Pochettino as their next manager to replace Michael Carrick, who managed just one win in 10 games.

His appointment makes him Manchester United’s eighth permanent manager since Sir Alex Ferguson retired in 2013.

Pochettino takes charge of Manchester United sitting in an unfamiliar 18th in the Premier League table with only one win from 10 matches. He has a massive job ahead of him, with many pundits and former players terming his situation the toughest in the Premier League.

Gary Neville believes the Argentine will need several transfer windows to get the squad where he wants it, if he gets that much of time at Manchester United.

The former captain shared his views on which players he expects Pochettino to keep, and who could be heading for the exit.

Neville believes Senne Lammens should remain part of the plans, although he expects Pochettino to bring in another goalkeeper to give him some competition for his place. At the back, he backed Luke Shaw, Harry Maguire and Leny Yoro to stay, while suggesting Ayden Heaven needs a loan move to the Championship.

“Heaven, I’d loan him out, get some experience in the Championship. He’s nowhere near it, it’s unfair to put him in the situation he’s in,” Neville said on Stick to Football.

In midfield, Neville wants Pochettino to build around Bruno Fernandes, Kobbie Mainoo and Youri Tielemans. He also believes Andrey Santos could have a future at the club because of his age, mobility and aggression.

Mason Mount, however, is not part of his plans. Neville said: “Mount would have to go” and identified him as the player to leave from that midfield group.

Neville also has his doubts on the forward line, with much of the front line seeing a change. Gary Neville expects Joshua Zirkzee to leave and believes United cannot continue paying Marcus Rashford’s wages.

“Zirkzee definitely to go. I think Marcus 100 per cent to go because he’s on massive money and that story has been well told. They can’t afford to pay him that money, you have to get rid of that wage at the earliest opportunity.”

He expects Pochettino to work with Amad Diallo, Bryan Mbeumo, Matheus Cunha and Benjamin Sesko. Patrick Dorgu could also be useful in the short term, although Neville thinks he may leave within the next couple of years. Manuel Ugarte, meanwhile, was another player he was certain would go.

Talking about the exits, Gary Neville has singled out many United stars to be shown the door in the first wave of squad clearance by Mauricio Pochettino. Neville identified Lisandro Martínez, Matthijs de Ligt and Noussair Mazraoui as further candidates to leave in the first wave of departures, alongside Mount, Rashford and Zirkzee.

Neville believes another group of exits could follow a year later, with Shaw, Maguire, Diogo Dalot and Fernandes among the players whose futures may not be at the club in long term. Pochettino would also have to decide whether Santos and Carlos Baleba fit into his long-term plans.

Neville does not expect United to rebuild the squad in a single summer, even if Pochettino makes a positive start.

“But the earliest Mauricio Pochettino can unravel this squad is two seasons if he does well. It could take three to get this squad unravelled.”

Pochettino’s first game comes against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday. His immediate priority will be to get some results for Manchester United but beyond the immediate games and results, Manchester United are expected to make multiple exits from the club as Pochettino settles into the job.
