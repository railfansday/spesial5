What the 'next evolution' of Rangers looks like this season

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Alan Hutton reckons the next "evolution" of Rangers is the squad starts taking their chances and finishing games off quicker.

The Light Blues are second in the Scottish Premiership table with a game in hand over Celtic after beating St Mirren on Saturday evening. Gers had 17 attempts on goal with seven on target and all the big-hitters missed chances.

And that, says Hutton, has been an issue that has plagued the Ibrox outfit at times this season.

The former defender does feel, however, that the next step in the Rangers process - and how they improve by taking things to the next level - is by beginning to take thieir opportunities and stop wasting them.

"I think there's a lot they need to brush up on," Hutton said. "They created a lot of chances against St Mirren, they need to be clinical.

"I think that's the next evolution of this team, turning the good play within this area of the pitch, Minsu, Bouanani, Kelsy, Naderi, finishing it off and getting the games done as soon as possible."
