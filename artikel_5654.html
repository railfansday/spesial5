A psychedelic drug candidate from Helus Pharma significantly helped people with major depression in a Phase 3 trial, the company said Monday, but a number of serious adverse events in the study raised questions about the safety of the treatment.

Participants took one dose of the drug, called HLP003, on the first day of the trial and a second dose three weeks later. Six weeks into the study, people taking the drug in addition to their standard antidepressants experienced a reduction of 19.5 points on a depression rating scale called the MADRS, while the placebo group had a 11.9-point reduction.

At 12 weeks, the effect was largely maintained, with the treatment group seeing a 16.9-point reduction and the placebo group experiencing a 9.9-point reduction. The differences between the two groups were all statistically significant.
