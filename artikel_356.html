Home Depot Black Friday sale: Doorbusters, free tools, appliances on sale, and everything to know

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

At the Home Depot, it's been Black Friday since the beginning of November. But things are really heating up now that the big day is here.

Whether you're prepping your home for holiday gatherings, tackling an essential home upgrade, or trying your hand at a DIY project, Home Depot is where you go for the right gear. You'll find tons of deals and discounts across every department this season, but holiday decor, power tools, and appliances are the major standouts.

To help you navigate Home Depot's Black Friday 2026 sale online and in person, here's everything you need to know, including store opening times, in-store doorbuster deals, free tools, and more.

If you want to take advantage of the home improvement store's annual holiday sale, these are the key dates to know:

Early Savings Event: Nov. 5 to Nov. 25 in-store and online

Thanksgiving Day: The Black Friday sale began online on Nov. 26 and runs through Dec. 2

Black Friday: Shop one-day-only in-store doorbusters and the opening of the Holiday Tree Lot on Nov. 27

Wondering if your local Home Depot location will be open? Always check local store hours or call ahead, but in general, the rule of thumb is closed on Thanksgiving, open on Black Friday:

Thanksgiving Day (Nov. 26): All Home Depot stores were closed for the holiday

Black Friday (Nov. 27): Doors open at 6:00 a.m. local time, but closing times may vary by location

The home improvement mega retailer hosts a free tool deal, which has become a holiday tradition. When shoppers buy a qualifying tool, they automatically qualify for a free bonus tool or similar value. The famed Home Depot free tool deal is still going, with tons of BOGO (buy one, get one) deals on select Milwaukee, DeWalt, Ryobi, and Ridgid tools and combo kits. For instance, if you buy the Milwaukee Cordless Hammer Drill and Impact Driver Combo Kit, you can get a free bonus tool.

If you're willing to make the trek to your local Home Depot store, you'll get in-person doorbusters on select items while supplies last. Some of those include holiday decor, a Ring home security camera kit, and more. These doorbuster deals are available at stores starting at 6 a.m. (local time) on Black Friday in limited quantities, so when they sell out, they sell out.
