Juiced Bikes has launched an early Black Friday sale: Score $345 in free gear

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

TL;DR: Juiced Bikes kicked off seasonal sales across its Scrambler e-bikes, dishing out up to $345 in free bundled gear through Nov. 22 and an extra $100 price cut for Black Friday and Cyber Monday.

Juiced Bikes just rolled out its Black Friday sale for the Scrambler lineup, giving away hundreds in free gear and knocking down prices through the end of the year.

If you have been looking at moto-inspired e-bikes, the Scrambler platform is built around that classic vintage moped stance without sacrificing modern power. Both bikes pack serious punch: a 750W motor delivering up to 1,764W of peak power, 90 Nm of torque, and a 28 mph top speed, backed by a 52V battery that delivers up to 60 miles of range.

You have two different setups to choose from: the Scrambler Hardtail and the Scrambler Full-Suspension. The Hardtail runs an inverted KKE front fork with a rigid rear frame. Skipping the rear shock means zero bounce eating up your pedal or motor stroke, delivering sharp street handling and a 300-pound payload capacity for heavy hauling.

The Full-Suspension version takes everything on the Hardtail and bolts on an adjustable KKE rear shock. That extra travel lowers payload capacity to 225 pounds, but it pairs with wider 20x4.5-inch knobby CST tires and switchable torque or cadence sensors to keep both wheels planted over potholes, gravel, and dirt.

Juiced Bikes is rolling out the savings across three separate sales windows between now and the end of the year:

Beat the Sellout Pre-Sale (Nov. 2-22): Buy either model at regular retail and get $345 in free bundled gear automatically at checkout. The free package includes front and rear fenders, a rear utility rack, a bar bag, a bar pad, a front headplate, a personalized nameplate, and a sticker pack.

Black Friday and Cyber Monday (Nov. 23 to Dec. 2): The biggest cash discount of the season. Both bikes get an instant $100 price drop ($1,599 for the Hardtail, $1,799 for the Full-Suspension), plus $315 in free accessories, for a total of $415 in savings.

Grab your gear bundle now, or hold out until Thanksgiving week if you want the extra hundred bucks in your pocket.
