Matthew McConaughey's 3 Kids Look All Grown Up on the Red Carpet

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The apples don’t fall far from the tree in Matthew McConaughey’s household.

The Oscar winner and wife Camila Alves’ three children looked all grown up and every bit at home as they made a rare red carpet appearance together on Thursday, November 19, at the Los Angeles premiere of Way of the Warrior Kid.

The couple’s eldest son, Levi McConaughey, was supported by his younger siblings, sister Vida, 16, and brother Livingston, 13, at the premiere of his latest movie, which costars Chris Pratt.

The sibling trio were all smiles as they posed for photos together. Levi looked suave in a dark suit with a light blue shirt, while Livingston opted for a light blue suit with a white T-shirt. Vida looked elegant in a white dress.

Levi has been following in his famous dad’s acting footsteps in recent years. He first appeared alongside Matthew, 57, in The Lost Bus, which was released in 2025.

In a joint interview with his dad for People last year, Levi admitted he was beginning to feel the pressure of being Matthew McConaughey’s son.

“Yeah, it’s just starting,” Levi shared. “And as much as people can say, ‘Oh, you got this because of your father or your mother,’ they can open the door, but then it’s going to be up to me to do my thing once I get in there. That’s on me.”

Levi said “you got to have some chops” to make it as an actor, adding, “And so, yes, it’d feel like it would be a disservice to them because of how hard they’ve worked to get to the position that they’re at to not take advantage of it, but also put in the work and get after it and do it.”

In October 2025, Matthew said that his son earned his role in The Lost Bus on his own merit.

“I’m not putting that pressure on him either way. But he’s off to a great start, and he’s in the door,” Matthew said on Sunday Today With Willie Geist.

“As far as nepotism goes for me, Camila and I always say, ‘Don’t you ever feel entitled,’” he continued. “When he read for this role, I sent it to the casting director. She said, ‘I think it’s good enough to send to the director.’ And I went, ‘Pull his last name.’”

Matthew also opened up about the pride he felt seeing Levi on set.

“There was an extra sort of soul to it for me,” he admitted. “I said, ‘I can help coach you, I can help teach you what I can, but once we show up on the day, I’m not there as a safety net.’ And he showed up. He was not looking at me like, ‘Hey, is it OK?’ Uh-uh,” the actor said. “He worked with the director, and I sat back as a proud dad going, ‘There we go!’”
