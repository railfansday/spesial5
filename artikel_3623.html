GTA 6 Finally Explains Why You Can Hear The Police Radio Chatter

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

If you’ve played any of the mainline Grand Theft Auto games, you’ve likely committed a crime and heard a police radio communication reporting your naughty behavior. It’s been a part of the series since the first game. And yet, Grand Theft Auto 6 is the first game in the series to actually explain how your character can hear this police chatter.

Early on in Grand Theft Auto 6, Jason’s hacker friend and stoner buddy Cal does the character a solid. He grabs his phone and installs a custom, homebrew police radio scanner program. From then on, Jason can hear the police reporting on his actions, crimes, and location, as was the case in (nearly) every previous GTA game. Later on, when GTA 6 lets you free-roam around as Lucia, she, too, has the same police scanner app installed on her phone. The game doesn’t show Cal installing it, so I’ll just assume it happened off-camera.

Now, to be clear, I don’t think Rockstar needed to dedicate any time or effort to explaining how players could hear police radio chatter in GTA. Sure, sometimes I’d think about how it was possible for characters like CJ or Tommy Vercetti to hear the police reporting on their crimes, but then I would just remind myself it’s a video game, and games are filled with stuff that doesn’t make sense. Characters die and then respawn. Protagonists never age. And you can hear the police radio chatter despite not having a scanner. Just usual video game-y nonsense.

Still, I think it’s fun that for GTA 6, Rockstar took the time to explain how Jason and Lucia can hear the police radio chatter. And it also factors into gameplay. Our protagonists know if the police have spotted their car or have reported their clothing. They even mention it during chases as they try to make their escape, so it’s important we understand that the characters, and not just the player, can hear the chatter. In past games, you could just assume it was a gameplay mechanic, and the protagonists didn’t hear the radio messages. Not so in GTA 6.
