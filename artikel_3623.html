Ella Langley Beats Out Taylor Swift for Single of the Year at 2026 CMAs

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Taylor Swift’s return to the 2026 CMA Awards was all the talk in Music City — but in a surprising twist, she didn’t take home any trophies.

Ella Langley beat out Swift, 36, on Wednesday, November 18, for Single of the Year, the first award handed out during the celebratory evening.

Swift was nominated for “I Knew It, I Knew You” from Toy Story 5, while Langley, 27, won for “Choosin’ Texas,” produced by the singer herself, as well as Miranda Lambert and Ben West.

Langley, who was nominated in the same category for her song, “Be Her,” also bested powerhouse singers Lainey Wilson, who hosted the awards show, and Cody Johnson.

“I love this song,” Langley said during her acceptance speech. “We just sat in a room and did our best one afternoon and it is the gift that just keeps on giving.” (Langley walked the red carpet solo after Us previously confirmed that she is dating country singer Tucker Wetmore.)

She gushed, “I’m just grateful to be a part of it. I truly am. I can’t believe this is my life and what’s going on is absolutely insane. I just can’t believe it’s me this is happening to.”

Swift, for her part, has never won a CMA in the category but she was previously nominated in the award in 2013 for “Highway Don’t Care,” recorded with Tim McGraw and Keith Urban.

Earlier this month, producers announced that Swift would be taking to the stage for her first performance at the ceremony in 13 years. (The 14-time Grammy winner first attended the CMAs in 2006.)

The special night came after Swift recently traveled to Tennessee and celebrated making music history with her induction into the Nashville Songwriters Hall of Fame.

The “Patient Zero” singer officially became the youngest inductee in the prestigious music organization during its annual ceremony on October 26.

“There is no music community like this music community. I know. I checked all the communities. There’s no one doing it,” she said in her speech via Rolling Stone. “Nashville is the place that honed and polished the thing about myself and the thing about my life that I am the most proud of and the most passionate about.”

Swift continued, “To be here with you and to be getting to have this moment, I am truly grateful, and it just feels like one of those amazing Nashville moments I’m going to revisit on a rainy day.”
