Personal Investments • Re: Roth Conversion at 71?

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

My wife and I are both 71 and we have been Roth converting up to top of 24% tax bracket for years - our IRAs are larger than yours and once RMDs start in two years, our marginal tax bracket will be 24% or higher depending upon markets and account values. One never knows the future, but the 24% bracket is a fairly safe bet for anyone with tax-deferred assets of $2 million or higher.

As with many responders, I believe you can likely, beneficially Roth convert to the top of the 24% tax bracket (a few hundred thousand dollars per year), but conversions of as much as $600K into the 32% or 35% tax bracket may or may not be beneficial based on your and spouse's life span, future tax rates, account growth (dependent upon stock\fixed income asset allocation) estate plans, QCD's, IRMAA impacts etc.

I also agree with many responders that for tax-efficiency, after-tax accounts should be invested with a tilt toward capital gains, and income producing assets are preferentially best held within tax-deferred accounts
