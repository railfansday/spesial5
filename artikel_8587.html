The two steps work together because gas apps do different jobs. Each pick here does one of them best for a particular driver. The other apps cut what you pay at the station you choose, with cash back on an offer you claim before you pump or a discount at one brand’s stations.

An average car or SUV burned 423 gallons in 2024, by the Federal Highway Administration’s count. That makes every 5 cents off a gallon worth about $21 a year. Claim one cash back offer per fill-up, since Upside pays nothing on a fill-up that Checkout 51 or GasBuddy already paid cash back on.
