I love the look of a perfectly layered outfit, but actually putting one together? That’s a different story. Between button-down shirts bunching under sweaters, collars that never sit right and sleeves that feel too bulky, layering is sometimes more trouble than it’s worth. That’s why I’m obsessed with two-in-one sweaters, aka my genius lazy-girl hack to look like I spent way more time getting dressed than I actually did.

Luckily, Amazon has plenty of styles that make faking the layered look surprisingly easy. I found my 17 favorites, including preppy collared sweaters, cozy oversized knits and sophisticated V-necks that create the illusion of two perfectly coordinated pieces. The best part is that prices start at only $18, so looking polished doesn’t have to be expensive.

1. My Favorite: This V-neck long-sleeve was bought over 400 times in the last month, so clearly I’m not the only one into the lazy girl hack. The top-seller features a buttoned neckline, ribbed hems and a soft knit fabric that’s both elevated and cozy.

2. Oversized Perfection: This oversized knit style is one I’d pick for chilly fall days when comfort is the top priority. The faux ‘under layer’ looks like a lightweight ribbed hem sweater, while the top has a V-neck, buttoned feature. To make the look extra cozy, style the top with leggings and knee-high boots.

3. Expensive-Looking: This henley-neck sweater channels that expensive, rich-mom layered look . . . without paying the high price tag. Made with a quality knit fabric, ribbed hems and an effortlessly relaxed look, it’s surprising the pullover rings in at $23.

I especially love the black-and-blue combination, but it also comes in brown, burgundy, green and several other colors.

6. Pretty in Pink: Who says the layered look has to be all business? This pink-and-cream sweater gives the preppy trend a feminine makeover with a soft rosy hue, contrasting polo collar and gold-buttoned cuffs. Pair it with white jeans and ballet flats for a polished outfit that looks like it took effort to put together.

7. Flower Power: This daisy-print sweater reminds me of something hiding in a cute little boutique — except it’s just $30. Tuck it into high-waisted trousers for a tailored finish or wear it untucked with straight-leg jeans on casual Fridays.

10. Minimalist Moment: Sometimes, the most luxe-looking outfits are the ones with the fewest details.
