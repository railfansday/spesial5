An apology from RPS

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Yesterday I made a significant error in judgement. In writing an apology to our readers, I made a target of one of our writers and I won't be able to undo that. What I can try to do is apologise to that writer, who I'm addressing now.

My failures towards you were several. Firstly, in not explaining that we are able to step in by replying to and moderating comments when a discussion is becoming heated or aggressive, I never made it clear there were protections available to you that you weren't receiving.

Secondly, for mentioning you at all in the apology. While I hoped to make it clear to readers we wouldn't disrespect them in future, I laid the blame for that on your feet in public. The responsibility of an editor is to protect their writers, and I failed you profoundly.

Thirdly, that I didn't speak to you ahead of the post. As far as you were concerned the matter was settled, and I then put it in public without any warning.

Fourth, while we often talk of RPS being a collection of different writers with different viewpoints, where no one member of the team has the definitive view on a subject, this creates a vacuum for our contributors to operate in. This means new writers have very little direction when it comes to how they approach any topic and the response their work may receive. Again, any failings here are ultimately my responsibility.

Finally, this failure is all the more significant because it has happened across lines of gender privilege. RPS has a full-time staff of cisgender men, which inevitably gives rise to bias and ignorance we must actively confront, while we work towards being in a position to address our lack of gender diversity, beyond commissioning the occasional marginalised freelance writer. Non-cisgender writers are both much more exposed to abuse and much less favoured in terms of steady work. As such, a public rebuke from your own editor is far more damaging than it would be if the freelancer were a cisgender man. On this count, I have been extremely thoughtless in ways that would likely not have been the case when this site had a majority female editorship.

Ashley, I am deeply sorry that I let you down in these ways.
