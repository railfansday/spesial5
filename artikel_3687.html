Square Enix Is Making Final Fantasy 14 Free For 30 Days For A Ton More People

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

If you are like me and haven’t played Final Fantasy XIV in a minute, now might be the time to jump back in and finally get through Dawntrail before the new expansion launches in 2027. Starting November 16, players who haven’t logged in to the paid MMORPG in the last five months (150 days specifically) are eligible for 30 days of free playtime.

Players have from November 16th to January 14, 2027 to claim their free 30 days, which means that, if you time it right, your free period will overlap with the launch of the $45 Evercold expansion on February 23. Square Enix has all the specifics here.

Digging into the details, it seems like Square Enix knows a lot of people (including me) lit out after Endwalker and haven’t been back to play the latest expansion, Dawntrail. In addition to offering free game time, Square Enix is also offering a new $10 Tales of Adventure item which lets players auto-skip main story quest content. It’s designed specifically to leap-frog players over Dawntrail stuff, taking them from the end of Endwalker to the beginning of “In Fate’s Footsteps,” this year’s Evercold-introducing 7.5 event patch.

It is powerfully seductive to pay $10 bucks to take the coward’s way out of Dawntrail. I’ve heard that expansion is just okay, and possibly even worth skipping. But I play Final Fantasy XIV for its story. It can’t always be Heavensward or Shadowbringers, sometimes there has to be a Stormblood in there and that’s okay. As I try to take advantage of the free game time, I’ll finally see Dawntrail for myself.
