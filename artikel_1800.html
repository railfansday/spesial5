Crawley Town boss: Post-match celebrations are for players – not for me

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Since taking over, the Reds are unbeaten in four in the league, and despite dropping out of both cup competitions in the space of three days, there has been a mentality shift in the players – and the fans have seen that the players are giving their all.

Despite Christmas just being around the corner, the club hope Reds fans travel in their numbers to Exter to cheer the boys on in an early kick off. But it has been noted that Blackwell doesn’t celebrate with the players like past managers have.

Speaking in his pre-Exeter press conference, Blackwell said one or two fans asked him why he didn’t go and thank the support: “I thank the fans all the time, but after the game, it’s important that the players get the credit, not the manager.

“That’s not my job. My job is to try to prepare the team, and the players go out and give everything. If they do that, that’s what the fans want. They don’t want me walking up and down the pitch clapping and whatever. Get on, do your job Blackwell, do your job. And if that’s the case, the players are doing well.”

He added: “It’s not my style. I enjoy them enjoying it and I enjoy that the fans enjoy it. I’ve had a great career, managing big clubs and been a long way in big competitions, been all over the world, and I’ve never wanted the adulation.

“I just enjoy other people having it and seeing my players get that and the fans enjoying it. Sending them home after a week’s work, seeing the players give everything. That’s what they want. If we can keep doing that, I think we keep them on-side.”

Talking about the bond between players and fans, Blackwell said “I want the players to take the credit because the bond is between the players and the fans, and that’s the bond.

"As an ex-player, I knew there was a great bond between me and the fans. I’d hate to think that the manager walked up and started taking my thunder after having a man of the match performance and he walks up and takes all the credit. No, I want the players to have the credit.”
