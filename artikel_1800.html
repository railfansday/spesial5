Rose: Bournemouth cannot think about records

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Marco Rose urged his Bournemouth side not to "think about records" after they recorded five straight Premier League wins for the first time in their history.

Evanilson and Justin Kluivert sealed the three points as Bournemouth overcame Nottingham Forest 2-0 at the Vitality Stadium.

Bournemouth's victory saw them move up to fourth in the Premier League table, before Liverpool, who are now fifth, face Manchester United on Sunday.

The Cherries are now unbeaten in all nine of their Premier League games against Forest (W5 D4), marking both the most number of times they have faced a side without losing and the most Forest have faced a side without winning in the competition.

Speaking to BBC Match of the Day, Rose said: "We don't think about records, we just think about the next game.

"We took the right approach. We knew it would be tough and we would have to stay patient, and that's what we did. It was a top performance and a deserved win."

Rose was also impressed with in-form Evanilson, who scored in a sixth straight Premier League match, extending what was already the longest scoring streak by any Bournemouth player, or any Brazilian in the competition.

"The way he works for us out of possession, works as a link player and goes deep. The way he scores is quite impressive," said Rose.

Meanwhile, it was a day to forget for Oliver Glasner, who, on his 100th Premier League game, saw Forest muster a tame expected goals total (xG) of 2.07 from 18 shots.

Saturday's result also means Forest are now winless in their last seven away league games against Bournemouth (D2 L5), since a 2-1 victory in the 2014-15 Championship campaign.

"I think it's a deserved defeat. We have to blame ourselves for the first half. We made too many mistakes, especially when I see the two goals we conceded," said Glasner.

"The second half, we tried everything, so credit to the lads for everything, as they tried until the end. We got punished, that was more or less the result of this game."
