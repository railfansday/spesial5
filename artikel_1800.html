Richarlison grabs last-gasp winner for 10-man Spurs in fiery Chelsea friendly

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Tottenham concluded their tour Down Under with a 2-1 victory over London rivals Chelsea at Accor Stadium in Sydney.

Sandro Tonali put Tottenham in front with a deflected effort before Estêvão equalised for Chelsea minutes later. Tottenham’s cause was not helped by a red card for Austrian defender Kevin Danso, who lasted just three minutes after coming on at half-time.

After making a mistake, Danso brought João Pedro down and was given his marching orders for denying a clear goal-scoring opportunity. Chelsea came close to taking the lead with their one-man advantage only for Tottenham goalkeeper Antonin Kinsky to make some crucial saves.Just as the game appeared to be heading to penalties, Richarlison scored on the follow-up after Jamie Donley hit the post in the final few seconds of added time.

Tottenham will take on Getafe and Hoffenheim back on home soil before taking on Brentford on the opening weekend of the new Premier League season.

Chelsea have four more pre-season games, including fixtures against Juventus and AC Milan, before travelling to Fulham in the opening Premier League game of the Xabi Alonso era.

There is the final whistle and the 10 men of Tottenham snatch victory at the death with Richarlison scoring with seconds remaining.

In the final few seconds of the game, 10-man Tottenham win it. Donley’s shot from just outside the box hits the post and Richarlison follows up to score. It would have been a close call on offside if VAR was in use but it is not and Tottenham will take victory in Sydney.

Tottenham are being forced into a late change as Kyerematen, who come on for Robertson and nearly scored that wonder solo goal, is being forced off injured and is replaced by Tingey.

Delap is found in the centre of the Tottenham box with a cross from the right but the striker’s header from around the penalty spot goes wide and does not really trouble the target.

Initially, after Danso’s red card, Chelsea were in the ascendancy with a man advantage but Tottenham have dealt pretty well down a man.

Kyerematen nearly scores a quite sensational solo goal from inside his own half before driving forward. As he approaches the Chelsea penalty area, he tries to pick out the top corner and his effort comes off the top of the bar.

Xabi Alonso is making seven changes, yes seven, at once! That includes the likes of Delap and Tosin, who takes the captain’s armband.

The changes keep coming as Williams-Barnett replaces Tel for Tottenham.

A reminder that if the game is level after 90 minutes there will be a penalty shoot-out.

Roberto de Zerbi is making a double substitution as Tye Hall and Kyerematen replace Bergvall and Robertson.

17-year-old Satpaev, who hails from Kazakhstan, is replacing Estevao for Chelsea. Satpaev, who was at Kairat and turns 18 in 11 day’s time, scored the other day for Chelsea against Western Sydney.

Moore, who was on loan at Rangers last season, is replacing Solomon for Tottenham.

Robertson is so, so lucky. He is under no pressure as a cross comes over from the left and he should clear it with ease. However, he tries to be too clever and chests it back looking for his goalkeeper. There is not enough on it, allowing Pedro to nip in but his effort from inside the six-yard box is saved. Pedro probably should have scored and Robertson is a relieved man. Kinsky at the moment is the reason why Tottenham are not a couple of goals behind.

Tottenham have found it tough to get forward a lot since Danso’s red card but they do counter now and it ends with Tonali having a shot from just outside the box but his effort is easily saved by Sharman-Lowe.

Chelsea make a change as Anselmino comes on for Hato.

Palmer plays a ball down the right channel to pick out the run in behind of Palestra, who then returns it back to Palmer inside the Tottenham penalty area. Palmer’s first-time effort from the edge of the box is blocked.

Kinsky makes another big save to deny Chelsea. Tonali, who thought he had been fouled, loses the ball deep inside his own half, before Estevao has a great chance to put Chelsea in front from inside the Tottenham penalty area but Kinsky makes the save. It would have been a touch soft to give a free-kick to Tottenham but in the Premier League you suspect Tonali would have been awarded a free-kick there.

Tottenham are going to make a change at the back in light of Danso’s red card. Hardy is going to come on for Gallagher, who is booed by some of the Chelsea fans inside Accor Stadium. The England Under-18 international Hardy has come on at right centre-back in place of Danso.

Chelsea hit the frame of the goal. Pedro cuts it back from the byline to the penalty spot. Estevao gets to it first and his shot takes a deflection off Robertson. Kinsky makes a brilliant reflex save to push it onto the bar before Colwill blazes over.

Roberto de Zerbi is slumped in his seat distinctly unimpressed, shaking his head in frustration and perhaps disgust.

Palmer takes the free-kick but his effort is always curling wide of the far post.

The Austrian defender came on at half-time but has lasted just three minutes before being given his marching orders. Danso makes an absolute mess of a pass back to him from Gray and lets the ball run past him. Pedro gets around him and looks destined to drive towards the Tottenham penalty area but is brought down. Danso is shown a red card as Davies was probably not going to get across in time and it was the denial of a goal-scoring opportunity. Why do that in a pre-season friendly? Stupid.

Tottenham are making two changes at the break as Richarlison and Danso come on for Solanke and Van Hecke. Chelsea have also made a substitution as 16-year-old Nicoll-Jazuli comes on.

It appears the more times you see the incident involving Wesley Fofana and Conor Gallagher right at the end of the first half the more it looks like a penalty. Fofana did not appear to get the ball and instead catches Gallagher so Tottenham can probably feel a little aggrieved not to be given a penalty.

The first half is brought to an end and it is level at 1-1 at the break in Sydney.

Should Tottenham have been awarded a penalty? Robertson whips the ball in from the left to pick out the run of Gallagher into the box. Gallagher appeared to get to the ball ahead of Fofana, who seemingly then took Gallagher out but the referee does not point to the spot. That was a risky challenge to say the least from Fofana and on first viewing probably should have result in a penalty.

Palmer plays the ball forward to pick out the run in behind of Pedro, who has a two-on-one with Kinsky but the offside flag quickly goes up. Pedro was miles offside.

That was a decent opportunity for Tottenham. Tel finds the underlapping Davies with a pass towards the byline and the latter plays it across the six-yard box but Solanke was not there to meet it. Had Solanke read that, it would have been a simple tap-in.

Tel comes close to putting Tottenham back in front. It would have been a great solo goal as he drives inside from the left wing and moves into the Chelsea penalty area. He gets the better of Essugo on the corner of the six-yard box but is denied by a decent save from Sharman-Lowe.

Pedro has a chance to put Chelsea in front but his effort goes wide. Chelsea steal possession inside the Tottenham half after a poor pass from Robertson and the ball is then played forward to Pedro, who shoots from just inside the Tottenham box but his effort goes wide of the far post. Davies did just enough to put Pedro off.

Tottenham have two penalty appeals in quick succession turned down. First was for a potential handball by Essugo and then a push by Palestra on Tel. The handball would have been very harsh and the second incident was never a foul on Tel, who was losing his balance anyway.

Chelsea gift possession away deep inside their own half and Tel’s eyes light up. The ball rolls across to the Frenchman in a central position just outside the box and he opens up his body, trying to curl one into the top corner, but gets his first-time effort all wrong and his shot misses the target by a mile.

Like London buses, two goals come in quick succession and Chelsea are back on level terms. Chelsea counter from deep inside their own half and the ball ends up with Gittens on the left. From the corner of the box, he crosses towards the far post. It takes a touch off a Tottenham defender and ends up at the back post where Estevao, one of the smallest men on the pitch, beats Robertson and heads into the opposite corner.

Tottenham take the lead in Sydney courtesy of £100m signing Tonali. Solomon lays it back to Tonali who, from just outside the box, hits it first time and his shot takes a big deflection off Colwill, taking it into the far corner and giving Sharman-Lowe in the Chelsea goal no chance. That was probably Tottenham’s first proper foray forward in the game.

It has been a pretty stop-start game as play continues to be halted by fouls. The two managers might enjoy seeing the passion of their teams but they will also want to see their sides actually play, which is quite difficult when fouls are being committed left, right and centre.

Van Hecke has already fouled his former teammate Pedro once and he bundles into him again. Van Hecke has a wry smile as he walks away and Pedro tells the referee that is now twice he has been fouled by Van Hecke. I think it is a fairly safe assumption that Van Hecke and Pedro do not like each other that much!

We are not even 10 minutes into this friendly but there is absolutely no love lost between these two sides and the challenges are flying in. Former Chelsea man Gallagher slides in on Essugo, which infuriates the Chelsea players. The referee takes his time to make his decision as both sides come to blows before giving out the first booking of the game to Gallagher.

Pedro is caught by Van Hecke out on Chelsea left and the latter is absolutely furious with that decision, believing he got the ball. However, replays show that he did not get the ball and a free-kick was the right decision. Those two were teammates at Brighton and supposedly were involved in a training ground altercation just over a year ago!

The free-kick is whipped in towards the back post and the last touch is deemed to have come off Chelsea, much to their chagrin.

Lavia catches Tonali late and eventually the referee blows his whistle to bring play back for the free-kick. In a Premier League game, that may well have been a booking for Lavia. It might be a pre-season game but there will be no love lost between these bitter rivals.

The pitch in Sydney is on the bobbly side, which will make slick passing more challenging, dare I say it probably not really possible or realistic.

Both sides emerge from the tunnel, with Tottenham led out by Archie Gray and Chelsea by Levi Colwill. There is a lights show inside Accor Stadium as the teams making their way onto the pitch.

It is a Premier League encounter Down Under as Chelsea take on Tottenham at the 82,000-capacity Accor Stadium in Sydney. Chelsea won both meetings in the league last season, winning 1-0 at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium before a 2-1 victory at Stamford Bridge in the final few weeks of last season.

It is the start of a new era at Chelsea, with former Bayer Leverkusen and Real Madrid midfielder Xabi Alonso taking over. They began their tour down under with a crazy 6-4 win over Sydney Wanderers, with Joao Pedro scoring a hat-trick. Chelsea have broken the British transfer record to bring in Morgan Rogers from Aston Villa. Rogers is not on this tour as he enjoys time off following England’s World Cup exploits, with the likes of Reece James, Enzo Fernandez and Malo Gusto also not involved.

It has been a busy week for the club off the pitch as they secured the signing of French centre-back from Maxence Lacroix from Crystal Palace and are closing in on deals for both Danny Welbeck and Jordan Henderson. Chelsea’s opening game of the new Premier League season will be a west London derby as they travel to Craven Cottage to take on Fulham.

Tottenham have already played two games on their tour down under. A 2-0 win over Auckland FC in New Zealand was followed up by a 1-1 draw against Sydney FC on Wednesday. Tottenham have been very active in the transfer market this summer. In particular they have targeted Premier League players including Andy Robertson, Sandro Tonali and Jan Paul van Hecke. Mohammed Kudus, Xavi Simons, Dejan Kulusevski and Wilson Odobert have remained in the UK as they continue their rehabilitation from injuries.

Goalkeeper Guglielmo Vicario has also stayed at home with a minor knock but the Italian has been linked with a move away from Tottenham this summer. Djed Spencer, Pedro Porro and Cristian Romero are not on the tour following their World Cup exploits. They begin their Premier League campaign with a London derby as they take on Brentford at the Gtech on the opening weekend.
