FA Cup draw in full: Second round fixtures confirmed as Leyton Orient and non-league sides learn fate

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Wimborne Town will face Bradford in the second round of the FA Cup, while Leicester have been handed an away tie against lower league opposition.

Seventh-tier Wimborne are one of the lowest-ranked teams left in this season’s competition after causing a major upset in round one by beating League Two strugglers Crawley Town.

As such, the Magpies’ reward is another home tie in the second round as they have drawn League One side Bradford - who will face Manchester City in the Carabao Cup quarter-final.

Leicester, the 2021 winners, must overcome non-league opposition in Dover Athletic to reach the third round, while Leyton Orient will face last season’s quarter-finalists in Port Vale.

Elsewhere, 2013 winners Wigan take on Barnsley in an all-League One showdown and Jack Wilshere’s Luton Town have been handed a trip to AFC Fylde.

Plymouth Parkway have overcome Berkhamsted after extra-time in the final first round tie and will be at home in round two against Barnet.

FA Cup second round ties will be held across and around the weekend of December 5, 2026.
