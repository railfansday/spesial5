Igor Jesus favors Premier League stay amid Flamengo and Botafogo interest

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Igor Jesus wants to stay in the Premier League amid interest from Flamengo and his former club Botafogo, according to Sport Witness. The Nottingham Forest striker has scored once in seven appearances this season.

ESPN Brasil puts Forest’s valuation at €40 million and reports that clubs have already inquired about the 25-year-old ahead of January. Juventus and Porto also asked about him during the summer, according to Sport Witness.

Botafogo won the Copa Libertadores and the Brazilian league title with Igor Jesus leading its attack in 2024. The following June, he scored the only goal against Paris Saint-Germain at the Club World Cup. Forest signed him to a four-year contract the next month.
