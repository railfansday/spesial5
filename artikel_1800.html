Calafiori enjoying ‘best moment of my career’ at Arsenal

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Riccardo Calafiori believes he is having the "best moment of my career" for Arsenal after being honoured by Rome Mayor Roberto Gualtieri on Tuesday.

The full-back was presented with a special plaque in his hometown for "the outstanding results achieved" in 2026, which included Arsenal's first Premier League title since 2003-04.

Calafiori made 36 appearances in all competitions last season, with 26 of those coming in the Premier League.

He also helped Arsenal to reach the EFL Cup and Champions League finals, though the Gunners suffered defeat on both occasions, to Manchester City and Paris Saint-Germain respectively.

Calafiori, who joined Arsenal in July 2024, has also impressed at the start of 2026-27, scoring two goals and registering two assists in 12 appearances so far.

"I want to thank Mayor Gualtieri. I felt quite emotional walking in here," Calafiori told reporters via Football Italia.

"Thank you for this recognition, which fills me with pride. I always try to represent my Roman roots abroad. Whenever I come back, it's always a great emotion."

"I think it's the best moment of my career," he added about his spell at Arsenal.

"I feel like a leader in the dressing room, and I try to help my team-mates and the younger players."

A post shared by Riccardo Calafiori (@richycala)

Calafiori started his career at Roma, coming through the academy and making his first-team debut in 2020 before leaving to join Basel in August 2022.

He made just 10 appearances for Roma, and the Italian has confirmed he would like to return to the Giallorossi at some point in the future.

"I hope to win as much as possible over the coming years," he added.

"Then, in the more distant future, as I've always said, I would love to return to Roma.

"Everyone speaks highly of [Gian Piero] Gasperini and his ability to get the best out of players. I hope he can achieve the best possible results by the end of the season."
