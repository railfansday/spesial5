Editor-in-chief mailbag: Sixers are 12-3 and haven’t played their best ball yet

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

With their win over the Brooklyn Nets Monday, the Sixers swept another back-to-back and moved to 12-3, their best start since the 2000-01 season.

If you were told before the season started they'd be 12-3 after 15 games, you would've done cartwheels. There are obviously things to figure out. The half-court offense has been ugly at times, though it's trending upwards, ranking 14th in offensive rating. The defense has gone the other way, but remains a top-10 unit.

The most notable thing about the Sixers through 15 games is how they're valuing possessions. Rebounding has been a constant pain point under Nick Nurse, but they're currently fourth in defensive rebounding percentage. They're also second in turnover percentage and lead the league in loose balls recovered per game. They're also undefeated in clutch games … though they sort of created their own clutch game Monday.
