Jets OL fined nearly $15,000 for infraction vs. Chiefs in Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

New York Jets offensive lineman Armand Membou was assessed a $14,926 fine for unsportsmanlike conduct in a matchup against the Kansas City Chiefs in Week 9.

Membou — a Kansas City-area native turned Missouri Tigers star who was selected by New York with the No. 9 pick in the 2025 NFL Draft — removed his helmet during the Jets' loss to the Chiefs, which is a prohibited act under league rules.

A PWFA All-Rookie Team selection last season, Membou is usually more restrained in his on-field reactions and is sure to correct his behavior after the league's latest disciplinary action against him.

None of Kansas City's players were fined for their actions in Week 9, and the Chiefs will look to continue playing a clean and penalty-free brand of American gridiron football during their matchup against the Atlanta Falcons on November 15.
