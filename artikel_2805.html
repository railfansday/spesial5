Borussia Dortmund president Hans-Joachim Watzke concerned by Harry Kane’s injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The injury blows keep coming for Bayern Munich. During Friday night's 1–1 draw with FC Köln at the Allianz Arena, Bayern lost both Harry Kane and Michael Olise to injury in the first half, though Kane came off much worse for wear than Olise.

After the match, the club officially revealed that the England skipper suffered a syndesmosis ligament tear in his left ankle and faces a considerable spell on the sidelines—possibly up to three months. It's a massive blow for the German Rekordmeister, as the striker joins an already long list of key injuries in the squad. Manuel Neuer, Serge Gnabry, Ismael Saibari, and Alphonso Davies are all out injured, while Luis Díaz is just getting set to mark his return after a lengthy absence. Meanwhile, Jamal Musiala returned off the bench in the second half against Köln after missing significant time due to injury and a health condition.

Thankfully for Olise, while he was seen leaving the Allianz Arena on crutches on Friday, that proved to be more of a precaution; the French winger avoided any serious injury beyond an ankle contusion. Even so, Bayern noticeably missed both Kane and Olise in the attacking line during the second half against René Wagner's deep block.

For Vincent Kompany and Bayern, making do without Kane for several months will be a tall order—especially since the club's supervisory board recently insisted there was no need to make any January signings during the winter transfer window. Compounding the long-term injury loss and dropped points at home to Köln, Bayern were overtaken by Borussia Dortmund, who moved top of the Bundesliga table with a 1–0 win over Borussia Mönchengladbach at Borussia-Park. Dortmund now lead the league by two points after 10 matchdays.

For what it's worth, Dortmund president Hans-Joachim Watzke is delighted that his side sits atop the table, yet he expressed grave concern over Kane's injury. "This might sound strange to you, but what concerned me on Friday was Harry Kane's injury. Harry Kane is not only an impeccable sportsman and a great footballer – he has also brought enormous prestige to our Bundesliga, which we all love so much. And that's why I believe it's not wrong to convey our heartfelt wishes for a quick recovery, even from a members' meeting of his rival Borussia Dortmund," Watzke told SID this weekend (via @iMiaSanMia).

From a rivalry perspective, Bayern being without Kane for an extended stretch would normally be seen as a positive for Dortmund, but Watzke highlighted how much the Englishman has elevated the Bundesliga and German football as a whole. Winning the Ballon d'Or was a fitting crowning achievement for everything he has accomplished since joining Bayern from Tottenham in the summer of 2023. While Watzke surely wants Dortmund to win the title, he'd rather they do it with Kane still in the race—for the sake of the Bundesliga as a whole.
