South Carolina coach Shane Beamer says he made decision for star QB LaNorris Sellers to not participate in senior day activities before Georgia loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Saturday night's loss to Georgia could have been LaNorris Sellers' final game at South Carolina's Williams-Brice Stadium. However, the fourth-year QB did not participate in senior day festivities.

Sellers, who is listed as a senior on South Carolina's roster and is set to graduate, was asked why he wasn't a part of the pregame ceremonies after Georgia's 34-6 win. As soon as Sellers started to answer, his head coach Shane Beamer jumped in.

"I made that decision," Beamer said. "Seniors walk. He's not a senior. Now, he's graduating, but he's not a senior. So we talked about it, if I'm the bad guy, I'm the bad guy but there's a lot of guys who have been here since 2023 who are not technically seniors. So that's all me, sorry."

Beamer is correct; Sellers has another year of eligibility because of both his 2023 season and the NCAA's five-in-five rule. Sellers played in three games as a freshman and redshirted that year. If the NCAA hadn't granted players a fifth season of eligibility this summer, he'd still be able to be at South Carolina in 2027.

But it's still a bit odd that a star quarterback about to graduate with NFL Draft aspirations didn't partake the pregame pomp and circumstance.

"I think LaNorris is listed as a redshirt junior, or how is he listed? … I don't even know, so I just — they gave me the list of who was walking and he wasn't on it and we talked about it and chose not to, or I said not to, so again, that's on me. I got a lot more things to worry about, hell, than who was walking for senior night. And I've got a group text with all the seniors that I've had since August and everybody knows who's on the group text and LaNorris hasn't been in the group text."

While Sellers' production hasn't matched the top-10 hype that had surrounded him in disappointing 2025 and 2026 seasons for the Gamecocks, there's no denying that he's an incredibly talented quarterback with many of the skills that NFL teams covet.

Saturday night wasn't pretty, however. Sellers was just 10-of-18 passing for 87 yards and an interception in the loss while rushing nine times for 24 yards. Sellers injured his right ankle two weeks ago in a win over Texas A&M — he hobbled off the field after sprinting for the game-winning touchdown — and missed last week's loss at Arkansas. While he wasn't on South Carolina's injury report ahead of the Georgia game, it's reasonable to think that Sellers wasn't 100% against the Bulldogs.
