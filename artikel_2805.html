Commanders upgrade WR Treylon Burks to limited practice participation

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Commanders wide receiver Treylon Burks missed last Sunday's loss to the Rams with a foot injury, but he's moving toward a return to the lineup.

Burks was upgraded to limited practice participation on Tuesday. The Commanders only held a walkthrough, so that's an estimated level of work but it's still a positive sign for Burks's chances of returning against the Giants on Thursday night. Burks has 12 catches for 233 yards and two touchdowns this season.

Center Lucas Patrick (concussion) and tight end Colson Yankoff (knee) were listed as out for the second straight day. Head coach Dan Quinn said earlier in the day that Nick Allegretti will start at center on Thursday.

Tight end John Bates (shoulder), quarterback Jayden Daniels (left elbow), center Matt Gulbin (knee), tight end Chig Okonkwo (hip), cornerback Amik Robertson (ankle), and defensive lineman Shy Tuttle (shoulder) were all listed as full participants.
