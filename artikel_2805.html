Tim Cowlishaw's Cowboys-Titans report card: A smooth ride into Thanksgiving rivalry game

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Cowboys are this close to owning the AFC South. Check that, Jacksonville remains on the schedule in December so perhaps a "not so fast" is in order. Still, Sunday's 51-30 destruction of the Tennessee Titans gave Dallas a 3-0 record against the South, a 5-6 record overall and by far its most one-sided victory to date. A look at the highlights:

There's only one way to get better than scoring seven touchdowns and a field goal on your eight possessions, and that would be, of course, eight touchdowns. Had the Cowboys needed that eighth one, I have little doubt they would have found a way. Dak Prescott threw for more than 300 yards with just five incompletions, and they gained 155 yards on the ground including two more touchdowns. At 7.6 yards per play with zero punts and zero turnovers, they were masterful against — admittedly — a really poor Titans defense.

Giving up 30 points to Tennessee (the final touchdown came with 31 seconds to play) is not bad at all. The Titans had averaged 27.7 points in their last three losses to winning teams (Jacksonville, Cincinnati, Cleveland). Markquese Bell had a big fumble return on a takeaway created by Quinnen Williams and the Cowboys sacked Cam Ward five times. Williams had a pair of them. Hats off to linebacker Justin Barron with nine tackles.

Dallas Cowboys quarterback Dak Prescott celebrates as he leaves the field following the Cowboys victory over the Tennessee Titans in an NFL football game at AT&T Stadium on Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026, in Arlington. (Smiley N. Pool/The Dallas Morning News)

Probably should go with the incomplete grade when Aubrey goes 1-for-1 on field goals and Bryan Anger has no punts. It's really more of an A for your team when special teams play this tiny of a role (especially on punts). On eight returned kicks, the Cowboys kept the Titans from having a 30-yarder.

This was a no doubter almost from the start, and just because the Titans are bad, an easy afternoon was not a given, considering recent performances against Arizona and Indy. Coach Brian Schottenheimer had the offense in tune all day, and Mike Pettine and Christian Parker, both walking the sideline, seemed to find the right touch with a downtrodden defense.

I think Tennessee would have to be held below 30 to go any higher with this grade. But the Cowboys got the smooth ride into the short week of Thanksgiving that they were seeking and seemed to steer clear of major injuries before getting ready for a tougher game with the Eagles.
