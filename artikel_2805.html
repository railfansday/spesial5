Chiefs-Jets Madden Simulation Week 9: Get Right Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

On Sunday, the (5-2) Kansas City Chiefs are back at Arrowhead taking on the (2-6) New York Jets. Let's see how the game plays out in Madden NFL 27.

Note: Madden simulation is 6-1 this season, predicting the outcome.

If you want to watch the simulation without spoilers, start this video — or if you can't see it, click here.

The Kansas City Chiefs received the opening kickoff and wasted no time getting the offense going.

At midfield, Patrick Mahomes lobbed a pass down the left sideline to Kenneth Walker, who was running a wheel route. Walker hauled it in and took it all the way to the Jets' four-yard line.

The New York Jets defense attempted to stand tall, but on third-and-four, Mahomes found Travis Kelce on a slant route in the end zone for a touchdown.

The Jets could potentially welcome back running back Breece Hall after his quad injury. On their first five plays, Hall had his hands on the football. His biggest play came on a 16-yard run straight up the middle.

Felix Anudike-Uzomah got to Geno Smith for a sack, forcing the Jets to punt.

The Chiefs picked up one first down but couldn't get past midfield. Matt Araiza came on to punt the ball back to the Jets.

The Chiefs defense quickly got the ball back as Chris Jones and George Karlaftis teamed up to sack Geno Smith on third down, forcing a three-and-out.

Mahomes connected with Kelce twice for 37 yards, moving the Chiefs inside the five-yard line. After a play that resulted in a 10-yard loss, Mahomes went right back to Kelce in the end zone for his second touchdown of the game.

At their own 24-yard line, Hall took a handoff straight up the middle, broke a tackle, and made several defenders miss on his way to the end zone. A 76-yard touchdown run brought the Jets back into the game.

Rashee Rice picked up a first down on a drag route to midfield. On the very next play, Mahomes delivered a strike to Xavier Worthy, who ran a corner route before cutting upfield and racing into the end zone for a 50-yard touchdown.

Jadon Canady intercepted Smith with 49 seconds remaining at the Jets' 44-yard line.

The Chiefs failed to capitalize on the opportunity before halftime.

The Jets quickly went three-and-out. During the drive, Mansoor Delane came screaming off the edge on a blitz and brought down Smith for a sack.

The Chiefs controlled the majority of the quarter, putting together a lengthy drive deep into Jets territory. At the 10-yard line, Mahomes kept the ball on a read option and took a massive hit before being brought down at the two-yard line.

On third-and-goal from the two, Walker caught a screen pass from Mahomes and reached the end zone for a touchdown, giving the Chiefs a three-score lead.

Karlaftis got to Smith for another sack, forcing the Jets to punt once again.

On the first play of the quarter, Walker broke free and reached the Jets' 16-yard line.

Worthy followed it up by catching a touchdown pass in the end zone, giving the Chiefs a four-possession lead.

The Jets managed to put together a 12-play scoring drive, capped off by an Adonai Mitchell touchdown.

New York attempted an onside kick, but Jalen Royals had other plans. Royals recovered the football and returned it all the way to the four-yard line.

However, Mahomes threw an interception, giving the Jets possession once again.

Facing fourth-and-goal from the nine-yard line, Smith lobbed a pass toward the corner of the end zone, where Tim Patrick hauled it in for a touchdown.

Patrick Mahomes: 26 for 30 passing, 320 yards, 5 touchdowns, 1 interception, 5 rushes, 21 yards.

Kenneth Walker: 9 rushing attempts, 78 yards, 8 receptions, 88 yards, 1 touchdown.

Travis Kelce: 6 receptions, 87 yards, 2 touchdowns.

Xavier Worthy: 4 receptions, 71 yards, 2 touchdowns.

Geno Smith: 15 for 23 passing, 175 yards, 2 touchdowns, 1 interception.

Breece Hall: 12 rushing attempts, 119 yards, 1 touchdown, 4 receptions, 30 yards

Adonai Mitchell: 4 receptions, 53 yards, 1 touchdown

Next week, the Chiefs are back on the road heading to Hotlanta to visit the Atlanta Falcons. Until then, we will see how it is played out inside the EA Universe.
