Cowboys news: Jerry Jones tries to walk back ‘paying coaches’ comments

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Jerry Jones has been criticized for not being willing to write a huge check for a championship type of coach, but he doesn't feel that it's an issue whatsoever.

The NFL trade deadline is rapidly approaching. If Dallas was to indulge, there's one area that's likely to get the most attention. The Cowboys have a talented offense that more than capable of being productive, but hiccups at the wrong times have caused some confusion.

Jerry Jones got ridiculed after he mentioned that there was no connection between the highest-paid coaches to winning championships. Now, he's saying spend top dollar for a coach hasn't been a problem in Dallas.

"The facts are I have been out there, and I have been trying to lure very successful coaches away from the teams they were on and knew full well that I was going to pay them for their success," he said. "So that has been done. No one knows that, but I do. And so it is absolutely ridiculous. And I actually grin when somebody says, 'Jerry's not willing to pay the money for what it would take to get the right kind of coach out there.'"

The Cowboys are facing a huge game against the Colts as they try to stay within striking distance in the NFC East. They'll have to do so with multiple starters out.

On Friday, the Cowboys hit the practice field for the last time before boarding the flight to Indianapolis on Saturday, and they did so without two starters. Wide receiver and kick returner KaVontae Turpin (ribs) and rookie outside linebacker Malachi Lawrence (ankle) did not practice this week and have been ruled out for Sunday's game.

The NFL deadline is next Tuesday, and the Cowboys have been talked about as a team that could make a move. The specific position that they could make a play for was revealed.

Q: Which position or positions do you think could help the team the most right now?

Jones: "Yeah I think it's one everybody talks [about], obviously a pressure player could help us. After that, we took care of business earlier in the year with [Joey Porter Jr.]. It's unfortunate he's been missing some time. No excuses, because everybody has injuries, but no question it hurt us not having him or Caleb [Downs] in the game there in that quarter as well."
