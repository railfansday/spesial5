Commanders add RB Rachaad White to the injury report as limited with a heel injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Commanders added running back Rachaad White to the injury report Friday. He has a heel injury that limited him in practice.

The Commanders play the Bengals on Monday night.

The team downgraded wide receiver Treylon Burks (foot) from a full participant on Thursday to a non-participant on Friday.

The rest of the injury report remained the same as from Thursday.

Safety Quan Martin (shoulder) was limited a second consecutive day.

The other nine players on the injury report all were full participants, including quarterback Jayden Daniels (left elbow), running back Jacory Croskey-Merritt (groin), tight end John Bates (shoulder) and tight end Chig Okonkwo (back).
