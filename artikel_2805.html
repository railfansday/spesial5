The greatest Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) women's fight of all-time (not my words) has finally arrived …

Bantamweight legends Kayla Harrison vs. Amanda Nunes are finally set to collide tonight (Sat., Nov. 14, 2026) inside Madison Square Garden in New York, N.Y., at UFC 334.

Harrison talked the talk while running the PFL table (aside from that "best-ever loss" to Larissa Pacheco). And since crossing into the Octagon, she's walked the walk. Oddly, Harrison has only competed twice as a UFC fighter coming into tonight (here's why); however, she has finished two former champions and captured gold in the process.

That was seemingly enough to lure "Lioness" out of self-imposed retirement.

Nunes dominated UFC's 135-pound division for most of a decade, walking away on top just a couple of years ago. If anyone can return from retirement and immediately reclaim the throne, it is Nunes (and Georges St-Pierre, of course!). Harrison, however, represents a super tough task at any point in Nunes' career … and there are plenty of questions heading into this showdown about how Harrison will perform following neck surgery.

We are about to get some awesome answers.
