‘For Sure’ - Romano Has Just Shared New Nico Paz To Man Utd Transfer Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Manchester United will have to wait until next summer if they want to sign Nico Paz from Como, according to a new update from Fabrizio Romano, and even then the chances of signing him are unlikely.

Paz, 22, one of the best young players in world football, has been in blistering form for the Italian side this season, scoring six goals and providing eight assists in 14 appearances across all competitions.

He found the net TWICE in Como's historic 4-1 win against United last month, and was awarded the Player of the Match for an outstanding individual performance.

The Tenerife-born forward has been impressive for Argentina as well, and continued his excellent form with a brace during his nation's 7-0 win against Costa Rica on Tuesday.

Due to all this, speculation has intensified around Paz's future. Real Madrid have a £66.7million buyback clause, valid next summer, after selling the Argentine to Como in 2024, while there are a number of other clubs interested in a potential deal, including Arsenal and Paris Saint-Germain.

United are another that could make a move for Paz, with the Reds desperate for some attacking impetus at the top end of the pitch as they plan their 2026/27 transfer business.

With Mauricio Pochettino sharing the same agent as Paz, a move could be possible, though United would have to wait until next summer if they wanted to land him, as he is expected to remain at Como for at least the rest of the 2026/27 season.

Delivering an update on Paz's future, Fabrizio Romano maintained that Real Madrid are in the driving seat to re-sign him due to the fact they have a buyback clause inserted. But United have become a prominent name talked about regarding the 22-year-old talent in recent days.

Speaking on his YouTube channel, Romano said: “We need to discuss Nico Paz. What a player! This boy is performing at an incredible level for club and country. As I told you, Real Madrid have an €80 million buyback clause valid in summer 2027, and Real Madrid are already having internal discussions about Nico Paz because Real Madrid loved Nico, loved his attitude, loved his quality.

"Obviously, they didn’t expect the player to have the kind of position that Nico had in the summer. Usually, players want to jump into the Real Madrid squad as soon as possible, but Nico said ‘Let me stay at Como one more season’. That was a direct conversation with [Jose] Mourinho, but Real Madrid still have the plan and the option to bring back Nico Paz in summer 2027.

“So, why are we getting links between Nico Paz and Manchester United? Because the agent of Mauricio Pochettino is Argentinian. Mauricio Pochettino is Argentinian, and the agent of Mauricio Pochettino is the same agency taking care of Nico Paz. That’s why the story is about Nico Paz and Manchester United. Of course, they appreciate the player. Who doesn’t appreciate Nico Paz? I think there is not one single club in the world you could ask today and say ‘Do you like Nico Paz?’ and them answering no, Nico is one of the best around, and so, obviously, the appreciation is very clear, and the fact that several clubs around Europe like him, it’s absolutely normal.

“But between Man United and Nico Paz, there is nothing concrete because we cannot even mention Nico as a dream for United. The future of Nico Paz is in control of Real Madrid, and it’s important to say not in January. Forget about Nico Paz for the January transfer window for Man United, for Premier League clubs, for Real Madrid, for anyone.

"Nico Paz is going to finish the season at Como for sure.”
