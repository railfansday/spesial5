Thomas Frank announced as Crystal Palace boss as he eyes raid on Chelsea set-piece guru marginalised under Xabi Alonso

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

By BOBBY MANZI, FOOTBALL REPORTER and KIERAN GILL, MAIL SPORT REPORTER

Published: 10:07 EST, 16 November 2026 | Updated: 13:08 EST, 16 November 2026

Thomas Frank has been unveiled as Crystal Palace’s new manager on a two-and-a-half-year deal.

Frank has been out of work since being sacked by Tottenham Hotspur in February and has taken a significant pay cut to take over at Palace, who languish at the bottom of the Premier League.

However, he will earn more than his predecessor, Pierre Sage, who was sacked last week after winning just two of his ten league matches in charge.

New Palace boss Frank said: ‘It's an honour to sit here and be in charge of this fantastic Football Club. I'm really, really looking forward to it.

‘Crystal Palace is an iconic club in England and in the Premier League. I will do everything I possibly can to help and support the players and everyone around the place, and I'm really looking forward to meeting them.’

Across his spells at Brentford and Tottenham, Frank has managed 178 Premier League matches with a 34 per cent win rate.

Palace are set to approach Chelsea about Bernardo Cueva, who worked with Frank at Brentford before being poached to become the Blues' set-piece specialist.

But Cueva may now be elevated back to the forefront of football if Palace can strike an agreement to bring him in to join Frank’s backroom staff. It is said Chelsea paid Brentford around £1million in compensation to hire him in 2024 at a time when his stock was high.

Cueva arrived at Chelsea in 2024. However, Daily Mail Sport revealed in the last summer how the 39-year-old Mexican’s role was changing after two seasons of work as Xabi Alonso’s side hired Austin MacPhee from Aston Villa as their new lead on set-plays.

Cueva has not been seen since then, either in training at Cobham or at matches at Stamford Bridge, but continued to work for Chelsea in more of an analytical role behind the scenes.

Club sources stressed at the time that it should not be seen as a demotion, and he is still listed on the first-team staff section of the website under the title ‘technical analyst’.

Chelsea are due to play Palace in two weeks' time at the Bridge on Wednesday, December 2.

Chelsea previously identified Cueva as a prime candidate for a role which had been vacant since Anthony Barry exited for Bayern Munich. Cueva, who also holds a UEFA Pro Licence through the Football Association, was regarded as an impressive acquisition in 2024.

Before joining Brentford in 2020 and helping them secure promotion to the Premier League, Cueva was at Chivas in his homeland of Mexico. He has also had a role with Norway men's national team.
