Marco Materazzi Criticised For Comments About Jesus de Miguel’s Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

It's been just two weeks since the tragic death of CD Tenerife forward Jesus de Miguel, who passed away following an on-pitch collision with FC Andorra goalkeeper Jesus Owono.

The 30-year-old was rushed to hospital after the incident, which saw Owono's knee make contact with his chest. However, the Spanish Segunda Division club announced his passing the next morning.

Jesus Owono has broken his silence after the tragic passing of CD Tenerife forward Jesus de Miguel.

Owono has since been handed a 12-match ban and is contemplating whether he ever wants to return to football. The Spanish police opened an involuntary manslaughter investigation following the tragic events.

Marco Materazzi played just under 500 professional matches during his 25-year career. Of those appearances, 41 came for Italy.

His most significant moment in football was lifting the 2006 World Cup with his country. However, he's not best remembered for the goal he scored in the final against France or his overall contribution to the match itself.

Instead, the first thing that comes to mind for the majority of fans who hear his name is that Zinedine Zidane headbutt. Zizou reportedly took exception to a comment Materazzi made to him during the match and responded by planting his head into the Italian's chest, ending his playing career on a sour note.

Referee Horacio Elizondo has opened up on one of the most iconic moments in World Cup history.

Materazzi has been in the headlines again recently. This time, for comments he made relating to the death of De Miguel. The 53-year-old compared his physical collision with Zidane to the challenge that saw Di Miguel lose his life.

Speaking to Gazzetta dello Sport, he said: "The death of Jesús de Miguel made me thank God that nothing had happened. They're talking about manslaughter for a gambling tactic: what if it had happened to me?"

Those words have been criticised by football fans on social media and have been labelled 'tone-deaf'. One supporter said: "It's so tone-deaf," while another added: "How can I make this about me?"

Another questioned: "Is this not an insane thing to say? Seriously. How self-centred and tone-deaf can you be?" One Reddit user said: "Read the room, Marco."

Another World Cup winner who has given his thoughts on the situation is Gerard Pique. The former Spain and Barcelona defender is now the owner of FC Andorra, Owono's club.

Tenerife manager Alvaro Cervera has responded to the sanction given to Jesus Owono for his role in the incident that caused Jesus de Miguel's death.

Responding to the 12-game suspension handed out to the goalkeeper, Pique claimed the sanction placed blame on Owono. He said:

"Why did this happen? Players, former players, coaches, goalkeepers... they’ve all called it an accident. I truly believe that imposing the maximum penalty on Jesús for this incident puts him in a situation he doesn’t deserve, because he isn’t to blame for any of this. My take on all this is that the 12-game suspension for Jesús Owono seems to me to be placing the blame on Jesús for consequences that weren’t his fault. I believe the penalty should be based on the action itself, not on the consequences."
