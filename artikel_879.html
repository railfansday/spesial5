Alcaraz into Paris Masters quarter-finals after Berrettini withdraws

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Carlos Alcaraz has been handed a free passage through to the quarter-finals of the Paris Masters following the withdrawal of Matteo Berrettini; watch the ATP Paris Masters live on Sky Sports this week, with the WTA Finals in Indian Wells starting on Sunday

Carlos Alcaraz made it through to the quarter-finals at the Rolex Paris Masters without taking to the court after his scheduled opponent Matteo Berrettini withdrew from their third-round match.

The pair had been due to play on Thursday evening in the French capital but Berrettini suffered another injury setback, pulling out due to pain in his arm.

Alcaraz, who is chasing his fifth tour-level trophy of the season in the French capital, will face Taylor Fritz in the last eight at the ATP Masters 1000 event on Friday after he beat home favourite Arthur Fils 6-3 6-4.

Fritz must beat Alcaraz to qualify for the ATP Finals in Turin, with the last spot currently held by Spaniard Rafael Jodar, who lost in the second round earlier this week in Paris.

Alcaraz will be bidding to reach the semi-finals on the indoor hard courts of Paris for the first time in six appearances.

An official social media announcement from the tournament read: "Matteo Berrettini has to withdraw from the Rolex Paris Masters. Wishing you a speedy recovery Matteo. Carlos Alcaraz is qualified for the quarter-finals."

Berrettini posted a statement of his own in Italian on Instagram stories, which translated as: "Hi all, unfortunately the pain in my arm that I've had for a few days won't allow me to take the court tonight.

"In the next few days I will have all the necessary tests to better understand what this is. Thank you all for the support and affection you show me every day on and off the court. Mat."

American Tommy Paul beat Frenchman Quentin Halys 7-6 (7-3) 6-4 to keep his ATP Finals hopes alive but will need to at least reach Sunday's final to stand a chance of being in the all-important top nine, with world No 2 Jannik Sinner out for the rest of the season.

Paul will next face compatriot Ben Shelton, who fought back to defeat Arthur Rinderknech 3-6 6-4 7-6 (7-5) in a thrilling match.
