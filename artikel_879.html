Former Bromley boss Andy Woodman joins Aaron and Jobi. He lifts the lid on life after leaving Bromley and what’s next for him. They discuss the top of the Championship, with just 1 point separating the top 5. At the other end of the Championship, what’s happening with Bristol City, who currently lie 17th in the league under former Lincoln manager Michael Skubala – but when is the right point for a manager to make the jump? In League 2, it’s been a less than desirable start for Crawley Town who are bottom of the football league and out of the FA Cup after being beaten by 7th tier Wimborne – where do they go from here? Elsewhere in the FA Cup, Plymouth Parkway assistant manager Matt Godfrey drops by after their win – and with Barnet on the horizon. And after Shrewsbury Town’s new ownership, what’s next for them?
