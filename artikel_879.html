Calvert-Lewin signs new Leeds deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Dominic Calvert-Lewin has signed a new deal until the summer of 2029 at Leeds; the striker joined the club in 2025 from Everton; he has scored six goals in all competitions this season; his form earned him a recall to the England squad

Dominic Calvert-Lewin has signed a new deal at Leeds, keeping him at the club until the summer of 2029.

Sky Sports News has been reporting that he was expected to commit his future to the club, which has now been confirmed.

Manchester United had shown interest in the 29-year-old.

Calvert-Lewin joined Leeds on a free transfer in the summer of 2025 after leaving Everton. He scored 15 goals across all competitions last season, and has scored six this season. He scored in a 4-1 win against Chelsea on Saturday.

Since he joined Leeds, Calvert-Lewin is the highest-scoring Englishman in the Premier League (19) - three ahead of his next closest countrymen, Morgan Gibbs-White and Ollie Watkins.

His form for Leeds saw him return to the England set-up in March, and he also featured in the September/October and November squads. Calvert-Lewin was not selected for the World Cup.

Ao Tanaka is also close to signing a new contract with Leeds, Sky Sports News understands.

The midfielder is understood to have agreed terms on a deal that will keep him at Elland Road until 2029. The 28-year-old's current contract was due to expire in the summer of 2028.

Tanaka has grown to become a key figure in Daniel Farke's side this season, starting in nine of Leeds' 11 Premier League matches. They have lost just 10 of the 66 games Tanaka has started since then.

Calvert-Lewin follows in the footsteps of Ethan Ampadu, Joe Rodon, Jayden Bogle and Brenden Aaronson, who all signed new deals in the summer. Leeds are also reportedly set to trigger Lukas Nmecha's one-year extension.

Leeds are seventh in the Premier League, four points from the Champions League places. They face Coventry next in the league on Saturday.

Ahead of the weekend's match, Daniel Farke has refused to shed any more light on his own new contract talks. He signed a four-year contract when appointed in 2023 and that is set to expire at the end of the season.

He was reported to have parted company with his agent in October while reaching an agreement with Leeds in principle to extend his contract until 2029.

Farke said: "I'm the manager of Leeds United. I sign players. I don't sign managers. Since I've been here, I fell in love with the people here, and I fell in love with the club.

"And the only thing I care for is our supporters because they had to suffer really for such a long time. I want to bring success to them. I care for the players, I care for the staff and this is the way I work.

"I think this is also the reason I don't like to talk about my contract at all because I have to lead the people here.

"I'm a leader of men and women at this club and and no one wants a leader who talks about his future, about himself, or whatever. And this also not my style."
