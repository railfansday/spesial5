Did ex-Arsenal star Nicolas Pepe really need eye surgery after staring at solar eclipse?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Villarreal have rubbished a bizarre online rumour claiming Nicolas Pepe required eye surgery after he filmed himself taking in the solar eclipse.

Reports circulating on social media over the weekend suggested Pepe had sustained damage to his retina during the lunar event back in August, which had supposedly forced the forward to miss five matches.

A resurfaced video, which Pepe himself had uploaded to Snapchat, showed the 31-year-old winger observing the eclipse in Spain without the use of protective glasses.

‘To all the idiots on my Snapchat, that is a solar eclipse! In France, all they got was a little croissant from the bakery,’ Pepe joked at the time.

Unsurprisingly, the wild rumour quickly spread online amongst fans, many of whom mocked Pepe for apparently going against the advice of experts and wilfully exposing himself to intense visible light, ultraviolet and infrared radiation.

But on Sunday evening, Pepe’s club Villarreal released a statement vehemently denying these baseless claims on social media.

‘Villarreal CF, in response to reports published in various media outlets recently, clarifies that it is completely false that Nicolas Pepe’s eye surgery has any connection to observing the solar eclipse,’ the statement from the La Liga outfit read.

Villarreal confirmed that Pepe had indeed undergone surgery but stressed that it was ‘entirely unrelated’ to the solar eclipse.

‘The surgery had been scheduled in advance for reasons entirely unrelated to this event,’ the statement continued.

‘The club urges accuracy and responsibility when reporting on matters related to its players’ health.’

The surgery Pepe underwent meant the ex-Arsenal attacker was sidelined for Villarreal’s games against Real Madrid, Napoli, Elche, Liverpool and Valencia last month.

The Ivory Coast international made his return in Villarreal’s Copa del Rey first-round tie with Manacor, in which he scored his side’s final goal in a thumping 5-0 victory.

He has gone on to feature in all of Villarreal’s next four matches, although he has failed to complete 90 minutes in any of those contests.

Since joining Villarreal from Trabzonspor back in 2024, Pepe has made a total of 86 appearances for the Spanish club, chipping in with 14 goals and 18 assists in that time.

Villarreal surrendered an early two-goal lead to lose 3-2 at Barcelona on Sunday and sit tenth in La Liga after 13 matches.

Pepe became Arsenal’s club-record signing when he signed from Lille for a fee of £72million back in 2019 – and the move remains the third most expensive deal in the Gunners’ history, behind those involving Declan Rice and Bruno Guimaraes.

Despite enjoying an encouraging start, Pepe badly struggled to live up to expectations at the Emirates and has often been branded one of the worst signings of the Premier League era.
