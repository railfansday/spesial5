Mauricio Pochettino learns shock size of Manchester United transfer budget as first targets revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

New Manchester United boss Mauricio Pochettino will be kept to a tight transfer budget in January as he fights to avoid relegation.

The Argentine was made well aware of his new club’s woes at Anfield on Sunday, losing 4-0 to arch-rivals Liverpool in his first match on the job.

If Leny Yoro’s eighth-minute red card hadn’t stressed the need for reinforcements, being hit for four certainly did, but any big-money moves may need to be put on ice.

Per reports in the Daily Mail, Pochettino will have less than £50m at his disposal in the January transfer window as United navigate Premier League financial rules.

A busy summer brought Youri Tielemans, Andrey Santos and Carlos Baleba to Old Trafford for a combined £148m in transfer fees, though this expenditure could not keep Michael Carrick in a job.

The former midfielder, who lifted the 2008 Champions League under Sir Alex Ferguson as a player, was sacked this month with his side in the drop zone.

Now, his replacement will not be able to count on similar cash as Manchester United seek to stave off a first relegation since 1974.

Reports indicate the Red Devils will only bring two new recruits to Manchester, while The Times claim strikers are the Argentine’s top priority.

With Benjamin Sesko injured and Joshua Zirkzee underwhelming, Pochettino’s striker wishlist reportedly features United States international Folarin Balogun, Dominic Calvert-Lewin and Ivan Toney.

Pochettino’s latest test will come in Europe on Wednesday, as United travel to Sporting, the former club of beleaguered captain Bruno Fernandes.
