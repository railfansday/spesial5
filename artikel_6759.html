These Are the Body Products ELLE Editors Can’t Stop Recommending

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

These game changers converted even the skeptics among us.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

For this year’s Future of Beauty Awards, we went in with skepticism. Can a hand sanitizer break new ground? Is it possible to reinvent the wheel for body wash? Will a self-tanner ever be good? As it turns out, yes to all of these questions, since our testers found true breakthroughs in each category. A whopping 130 body products were submitted, and we chose to try the 25 that seemed most promising. Out of those, our testers whittled the list to 11 that felt like true beauty game changers. Ahead, you’ll find the most innovative body products on the market, with commentary from ELLE editors who tried each and every one.

The sleek packaging and fresh scents only add to the experience. “It’s such an elevated take on an everyday essential, from the packaging down to how nice the formula feels on my hands,” our tester said. “I’d happily keep one in every bag I own.”

Plus, the coconut-vanilla scent creates the perfect gourmand base layer for post-shower spritzing.

But this body wash is 50 percent serum and oil, meaning the formula helps keep your skin hydrated, even in the dead of winter after the hottest shower. “It had a smooth texture, almost like a jelly cleanser, and my skin felt moisturized after rinsing,” our tester said.

Our tester used this on a camping trip and said that they were a welcome respite after a day of hiking—and even felt downright luxurious.
