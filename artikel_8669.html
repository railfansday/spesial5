Several records were broken at the IPF Sheffield V Powerlifting Championship on Nov. 20, 2026, as 12 men and 12 women gathered to put their all into one of the most prestigious events on the circuit. Making history once again was Cranon Worford, who smashed three records to dominate in the 76kg weight class.

Warford came to Sheffield with a ton of momentum after becoming the first Black woman to win the IPF World Championship in the 76kg weight class this past Juneteenth weekend. At that event in Lithuania, the lifter hit a total of 625.5 pounds. The powerhouse representing the U.S. had previously broken national records, but her showing in Sheffield has now put her on top of the world.

“Being the first isn’t about being the last,” said the inspirational athlete in a recent interview with Ebony. “It’s about leaving the door open. I hope somewhere a young black girl sees this and understands that she belongs in every room, every arena, every stage, and every conversation she chooses to enter.”
