Popeyes celebrates GTA 6 launch with ‘Spice City’ sandwich packed with double chicken

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Popeyes is getting in on the GTA 6 hype with a new ‘Spice City’ sandwich featuring two crispy chicken fillets, arriving just in time for Rockstar’s biggest launch in years.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally set to release on November 19, 2026, and the excitement has reached the point where millions of Americans are reportedly planning to call in sick just to spend more time in Vice City.

Of course, anyone planning to spend their entire day playing GTA 6 is going to need something to eat, and Popeyes has decided to capitalize on the occasion.

The fast food chain has announced its new Spice City Sandwich, a limited-time offering inspired by the game’s fictional city and designed for players who have absolutely no intention of leaving their consoles.

The Spice City Sandwich features two of Popeyes’ signature crispy chicken breast fillets stacked on a buttery toasted brioche bun, complete with barrel-cured pickles and a choice of classic or spicy mayo.

Essentially, it’s the chain’s iconic chicken sandwich with twice the chicken, which should help players survive a few extra hours of causing chaos in Leonida.

“This new sandwich pays homage to the one you’ll be grabbing in the game, with a crave-worthy duo of chicken breast fillets packed in one sandwich. Between missions, order the Spice City Sandwich online or on the Popeyes app, and have it waiting at your door when you put your controller down,” the chain says.

According to a press release shared with Dexerto, the sandwich will be available nationwide in the US from November 19 through November 22, giving fans four days to get their hands on one.

Interestingly, Popeyes isn’t the only fast food chain getting involved in the GTA 6 festivities.

As we previously reported, Rockstar has brought GTA’s fictional Burger Shot and Cluckin’ Bell restaurants to life in Miami, giving fans the chance to eat at the same restaurants they’ve been visiting in-game for years.

The promotion has already sparked a bizarre resale market, with fans paying hundreds of dollars for Burger Shot bags and other merchandise.

Between companies giving employees time off to play GTA 6 and restaurants preparing to feed them, November 19 is increasingly starting to look like an unofficial national holiday.

And if you’re planning to spend four straight days in Vice City, at least you won’t have to visit Cluckin’ Bell to get your chicken fix.
