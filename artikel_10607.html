How to watch your Xbox Playback recap for the 25th anniversary

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Xbox flung open its archives ahead of its 25th birthday with a recap built to dredge up every hour you’ve sunk in.

That recap filled a gap players noticed last year, when Xbox quietly skipped its annual Year in Review and left fans leaning on third-party trackers. A late-October leak hinted at a comeback, and it finally arrived in a packed anniversary week, a day before the $899.99 Series X25 lands on November 13.

Only this time, Xbox went bigger with the launch of Xbox Playback, a personalized recap that covers every console generation instead of a single year, on November 12. It goes back as far as the Duke controller era and serves up a smorgasbord of stats, including your first Xbox game, total time played, your most-played game, your top years and your longest streak.

To qualify, your profile needs at least one Gamerscore, one achievement and one minute of qualifying gameplay. Xbox is rolling the feature out gradually throughout the day, so it might not show up right away. Plus, because it relies on available account data, parts of older histories may also be incomplete or take time to appear.

In any case, there’s time. Playback runs until December 31 in 15 countries, including the US, UK, Spain, Brazil, and Australia.

Xbox started recap season early, while Sony has usually followed in December. If you play on both consoles, our guide to the 2025 PlayStation Wrap-Up explains how last year’s version worked.
