Vigilante called “The Templar” wears medieval armor to fight crime after home robbed 3 times

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A man in Brazil has taken neighborhood watch to medieval extremes, dressing as a knight to patrol the streets after his home was broken into three times in just six months.

The mysterious vigilante, who goes by “The Templar,” has been roaming neighborhoods in the northern part of São José do Rio Preto, a city in Brazil’s São Paulo state.

According to Brazilian outlet g1, the man originally purchased his medieval armor two years ago for a Halloween party. However, after repeatedly falling victim to burglars, he decided to put the costume to a more practical use.

“My house was burglarized while I was at work. In a six-month period, there were three burglaries,” he explained.

“It’s an area where everyone goes to work during the day, and the houses end up becoming easy targets.”

Among the stolen belongings was a television that he hasn’t even finished paying off.

The Templar now patrols three neighborhoods, José Bernardes Coelho, Edson Baffi, and Campo Belo, primarily during late-night hours and on weekends.

Although he has completed security guard training, he has never worked professionally in the field.

Despite his intimidating appearance, The Templar hopes his presence alone will discourage potential thieves while encouraging residents to report suspicious activity to police.

In a video shared by G1, he encouraged others to begin documenting patrols in their own neighborhoods.

“I am a symbol of hope, and I represent people who are tired of losing, even though we are the majority of good people,” he declared.

The Templar claims most residents have supported his unusual approach and says he’s already noticed an increase in police patrols.

“I’m not a rebel without a cause, and I don’t want to be a security force or take the place of the police,” he added. “I’m fighting for my own cause because the number of thefts here had become unbearable.”

Interestingly, police statistics show that reported thefts in São José do Rio Preto actually decreased by nearly 11% during the first nine months of 2026 compared to the same period in 2025.

The armored crimefighter isn’t the first unusual vigilante to make headlines this year.

Back in June, a mysterious figure dubbed the “Batman of Lagos” went viral in Mexico after suspected motorcycle thieves were discovered beaten and tied to poles.
