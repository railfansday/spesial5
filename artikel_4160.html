Nintendo Adds More Dates For The Zelda 40th Anniversary Concert Tour

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Following high demand for the Zelda 40th Anniversary concert tour, Nintendo has added fresh dates in Europe with even more coming soon for Spain.

Tickets will go on sale this Friday, 13th November (unlucky for some..?) at 1pm CET / 12pm GMT, however dates for Paris and Frankfurt will be available at a later date. No specific information has been provided for those in Spain just yet, but we suspect this will be released very soon.
