Xbox Brings Halo's Iconic Pelican To Microsoft Flight Sim 2024 As Limited-Time Freebie

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Act fast and claim the combat dropship for free!

Asobo Studios is commemorating the 25th anniversary of Halo by introducing the iconic UNSC Pelican into Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024.

Over the years, the combat dropshop has seen a plethora of talented pilots don its cockpit: Carol 'Foehammer' Rawley, the reluctant Fernando Esparza, and now you!

"Designed and built by Misriah Armory, the D77 Pelican has a typical crew of three (pilot, copilot, and crew chief) and can carry up to 14 fully outfitted infantry and their equipment. A vehicle can be carried in the external hardpoint clamp for immediate deployment. It has a lifting body design powered by four high-efficiency fusion thrusters with secondary positional / stability augmenters. The wings have three hardpoints each, which can hold sensors, cargo pods, or weapons."

Halo turns 25 alongside Xbox this Sunday, on November 15. So, we've seen iteration after iteration of the sci-fi shooter's most recognisable ship, which has traditionally only been pilotable in the previous MSFS and Halo 4's 'Shutdown' mission. There are other, more difficult means, to cheese your way into flying the Pelican across other Halo games, but now you don't have to.

The D77-TC Pelican in Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 offers:

Personally, even though MSFS isn't my cup of tea, getting to roleplay what it'd been like to ferry Master Chief across the ring is something I'm definitely interested in trying. I really do enjoy when Halo vehicles show up in other games. From time to time, I stick on Halo 2's scorpion mix, hop into Forza Horizon 5 and take the Warthog for a spin. Just for the vibes.

If you're keen to fly the Halo Pelican in Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024, you'd best act quick, because it's only free between November 10-17, and then $9.99 afterwards.
