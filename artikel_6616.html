Glove Shoes Are Having a Major Fashion Moment

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The ultra-soft style is showing up everywhere, from ballet flats to polished heels and boots.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Glove shoes are certainly having a moment. Made from soft, pliable leather that molds to the foot, they often feature a higher vamp than traditional shoes and come in everything from flats to heels and boots. The style showed up across both the fall and spring runways, with high fashion designers giving it plenty of attention. Naturally, that makes us pay attention to it, too. Ahead, dive into the trend and shop it for yourself.

Surely you’ve heard of the jazz shoe trend by now, right? Some such styles are glove-like, but you can also find an abundance of “regular” ballet flats in a glove style. Personally, I can’t stop wearing the pair I have from Steve Madden.

If you really want to be the most fashion-forward person in your office (without looking like a clown), wear a pair of glove heels to your next board meeting. It’s a subtle swap that fashion insiders will immediately recognize. My personal favorites are from Bottega Veneta and 3.1 Phillip Lim.

Proenza Schouler Glove 70MM Leather Pumps

Now, when it comes to glove boots, your options are a bit more limited. That said, if any of these pairs speak to you, I recommend jumping on the chance to make them yours. Whether you go for a flat or heeled rendition, these are a sleek alternative to your average winter boot.
