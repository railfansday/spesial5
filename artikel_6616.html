Starbucks Red Cup Day 2026: Everything to Know to Snag the Freebie

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Starbucks Red Cup Day 2026 is just around the corner—but how do customers snag the free reusable cup that’s made this day famous? Let’s take a closer look at the holiday tradition, when it is and how to participate.

Each November since 2018, coffee fans have swarmed Starbucks on Red Cup Day, when the company gives away reusable hot-beverage cups. Customers simply need to purchase a qualifying holiday beverage to get their free cup, while supplies last. Although their drink will be served in a separate cup, coffee lovers can take their collectible cup home, wash it, and keep using it all season long.

In 2026, Starbucks Red Cup Day is Tuesday, Nov. 17. The coffee chain announced the news on its website.

Customers should plan on waking up early to ensure they make it to Starbucks before the free cups run out. They must order a handcrafted holiday beverage—Starbucks will likely share a complete list of qualifying drinks as the date gets closer—and then they will automatically receive a reusable cup.

Starbucks hasn’t officially revealed the design (or designs) for its 16-ounce grande 2026 Red Cup Day cup, as of writing. However, that hasn’t stopped food bloggers and fans from speculating on social media. Some of the top contenders: an argyle design, something botanical and a two-tone red cup.
