Frank agrees deal in principle to become new Crystal Palace head coach

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Thomas Frank is set to return to the Premier League with Crystal Palace after being sacked by Tottenham in February; he will replace Pierre Sage at Selhurst Park, who departed last week after a difficult start to the season; the Eagles are currently bottom of the Premier League

By Lyall Thomas, Chris Reidy and James Savundra

Crystal Palace have agreed terms in principle with Thomas Frank to become their next head coach, Sky Sports News understands.

Formalities are now being concluded so Frank can get to work as soon as possible and he is at the Crystal Palace training ground putting the final touches on his appointment.

An announcement is expected on Monday or Tuesday if everything progresses smoothly.

Frank will replace Pierre Sage, who was dismissed on November 12 following a seventh league defeat of the season to Liverpool.

Frank was among a list of names that Palace considered in the summer.

Other candidates that Palace were interested in included Frank Lampard, Sean Dyche, Vitor Pereira, Kim Hellberg, Vitor Matos, Tony Popovic, Jens Berthel Askou and Ole Werner.

Frank had great success at Brentford during his seven-year spell between 2018 and 2025, getting the Bees into the Premier League and then establishing them as a top-flight club, reaching a ninth-place finish in 2023.

He then moved to Tottenham last season to replace Ange Postecoglou as head coach, but was sacked after less than eight months in charge with the club in 16th place, five points above the relegation zone.

Spurs only sealed survival from relegation on the final day of last season, after Igor Tudor and Roberto De Zerbi replaced Frank as head coach.

Frank failed to win over the Tottenham faithful, with his short tenure littered with setbacks, including drinking from a coffee cup which carried the badge of rivals Arsenal.

Crystal Palace are bottom of the Premier League with seven points, two from safety. They face the team in 17th place - Coventry - when the league resumes on Saturday November 21.

They are also 21st in the Europa League table - which would currently see them through to the knockout play-off phase - with one win, two draws and one loss so far. The Eagles face Real Sociedad - a place ahead of them on goal difference - at Selhurst Park in their next European fixture on Thursday November 26.
