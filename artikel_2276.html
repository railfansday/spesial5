West Ham fans given 'dire' Burnley warning after Scott Parker appointment

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

West Ham United fans have been served a warning from a Burnley perspective following the club's decision to appoint Scott Parker in place of Nuno Espírito Santo.

The Hammers dismissed the Portuguese on Tuesday morning, with Parker swiftly installed in the East London hotseat ahead of Wednesday's dramatic draw at Cardiff City.

The Irons found themselves three goals down at half-time through Jack Moylan's hat-trick, but a comeback sparked by Divine Mukasa, Arne Engels and Max Kilman's strikes saw them remain eighth, five points behind leaders Middlesbrough.

Parker's return to the club who he played 129 times for was met with mixed opinions given his managerial record, which is largely impressive at Championship level on the surface, although supporters of Fulham, AFC Bournemouth and Burnley can attest to post-promotion woes.

Football League World's Burnley fan pundit, Aaron Wright, is firmly in this camp, believing that the natives could soon get restless once more in E20, even if promotion is achieved.

Parker has won promotion to the Premier League on three occasions at his aforementioned clubs, twice finishing second whilst winning the 2020 play-off final with the Cottagers.

His immediate success at Turf Moor was met with criticism over the style of play, even if a concession of just 16 goals in 46 games in an all-time EFL record.

All three of his promotions have also come with newly-relegated teams, so when asked for his immediate thoughts on Parker's move to East London, Wright told FLW: "It's the most unsurprising news there could ever be that he's got the West Ham job. It's the biggest job he could get by an absolute mile.

"Let's be honest with his record, there was only one other job in the league he was going to take and that would be Wolves, because he's scared of every non-year one parachute team in the Championship.

"He has the best squad in the league and no doubt they will get promoted. No doubt. Whether it's winning the league, that's on Scott Parker. He's yet to do that. Yes, granted one on goal difference - it wasn't for a Plymouth defensive mistake to give Manor Solomon the ball, he would have won the Championship last time," Wright added.

"But there is also the caveat of if we hadn't have drawn 13 games, we'd have won the league. If we'd won one of them instead of drawing 13. It boggles my mind.

"You can't discredit him for also winning the number of games he had to win to cover for 13 0-0's and a few 1-1's in there. Marvellous consistency.

The new Hammers boss has generated plenty of talk for his fashion sense over time

Wright went on to admit that Parker is likely to guide West Ham back to the Premier League at the first attempt, such is the strength of the squad at his disposal, but also fired a warning to the supporters of Burnley's divisional rivals.

"West Ham will probably end up, maybe not walking this league, because I think Middlesbrough and Southampton both stand very good chances and I wouldn't want to face Birmingham in the play-offs currently. But they'll get promoted," he claimed.

"As a Burnley fan, I my thoughts are way past Scott Parker. We're having a relegation fight. I don't care what Scott Parker does now.

"But this is the biggest job he could get, if he can't get them promoted slash win the league, and if he can't then keep them in the Premier League, he needs to rethink pretty much everything. He is never getting a bigger job than this and his reputation as a player has helped him get this one. It's the most guaranteed promotion you will ever have.

"The West Ham fans won't enjoy it, but I think there'll be a good respect to it if it turns out how Burnley's did. 33 games unbeaten is something else.

"Let's not forget, this is a fanbase that saw 22 under Kompany and 23 under Dyche. To reach 33 is remarkable. We didn't lose a game from 5th November. That is insane mentality.

"But when you go up and you beat one team who was in the Premier League the year before, one, away from home because of a mad 6 minutes, we'd barely looked in a game past the end of October. We should have got better results," Wright stated.

"The football's dire. He will bore you to death, and he will cause a fanbase to turn through little snide little comments. He will cause the West Ham fanbase to turn on it. And that has happened at every single club consistently.

"There's a reason why Fulham fans and Bournemouth fans don't like him despite promotion. Burnley fans have a begrudging respect for the way he got promoted. 33 unbeaten.

"I don't know how many teams have done that at second tier level, but our defensive three and fullbacks- that three especially of Esteve, Trafford and CJ Egan-Riley -probably will go untouched for many decades in this league. And his football contributed towards that in both positive and negatives."

Given the fact West Ham are already the top scorers in the Championship, it is defensive improvements which need to be made first and foremost, with Parker's aforementioned feats at Turf Moor suggesting he could be the man to bring them to the London Stadium.

The 46-year-old was also a part of the last Hammers side to be relegated in 2011, so is also more than aware of the pressures that come with the fact a club of such stature is in the EFL to begin with.

However, that isn't the only pressure he is facing, as it was recently reported that if promotion isn't achieved in May, the board have the ability to relieve him of his duties at no cost.
