Fan notices something remarkable about Scott Parker’s first 24 hours at West Ham

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

West Ham United began the Scott Parker era with a dramatic 3-3 draw against Cardiff City in South Wales on Wednesday night, and much has already been made of the 46-year-old's first days in charge.

After successive home defeats, the hierarchy at the London Stadium opted to fire Nuno Espírito Santo, before Parker was swiftly drafted in as the Portuguese's replacement, 15 years after controversially departing the Hammers for Tottenham Hotspur as a player.

The three-time Championship promotion winner will have already learned plenty about his new chargers at the Cardiff City Stadium, as they found themselves staring at a third straight loss through Jack Moylan's hat-trick.

However, the Irons were able to rally themselves to a point through strikes from Divine Mukasa, Arne Engels and Max Kilman.

It has been well-documented that Parker's team talk inside the dressing room was the catalyst for such a dramatic on-pitch transformation, but that wasn't the only talking point.

The former England international has previously gained notoriety for his fashion sense, and that came to the fore once again on social media.

Given the nature of Parker's appointment, the new West Ham boss has firmly been in the spotlight this week.

This included interviews with the club's in-house media team as well as the national press as he set his stall out for the future, but some eagle-eyed supporters have noticed a remarkable pattern in the process.

Indeed, it was noted by online personality, Huey Taylor, on Instagram, that Parker had been spotted wearing three different jackets in the space of '21 hours', including images of him applauding the travelling fans on Wednesday evening.

When explaining his observations, Taylor commented: "I'm genuinely convinced this is some sort of paid advertisement.

Referring back to some of Parker's previous clothing choices on the touchline at Fulham, AFC Bournemouth and Burnley, he added: "Those three stripes still remain.

He then joked: "It's like if Adidas did jackets for people from London with money."

Parker's favoured fashion brand on the touchline is that of Thom Browne, who founded his apparel business in New York City 25 years ago.

During his spell at Bournemouth, it was noticed that Parker sported a cardigan produced by the brand, which reportedly cost £1,350. It was then claimed, over the course of a 90-minute game, the cost-per-minute ratio worked out at £4.50.

Ironically, Taylor's reference to the brand's similarities with Adidas cannot be ignored. In January 2023, the German sportswear giants lost a legal dispute with Thom Browne over their usage of the four-stripe design - a trademark feature of what Parker has sported on the touchline.

Adidas planned to claim Thom Browne were required to pay approximately £6.4m in damages, but were unsuccessful.

Ultimately, what Parker dons in the dugout will not be his highest priority at the London Stadium, as West Ham are desperate to return to the Premier League instantly.

The Hammers are currently eighth after 14 games played despite scoring the most goals out of any side in the Championship, having won just two of their last six games.
