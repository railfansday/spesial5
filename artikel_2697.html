Cowboys point/counterpoint: Eagles are going to be much more of a test

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

At least all hope is not lost. After the Dallas Cowboys delivered a dominating victory over the Tennessee Titans, there is still a path to the playoffs.

But there were some warts that still emerged in the game. Now the Thanksgiving Day game against the Philadelphia Eagles has become one more crucial step if Dallas hopes to overtake the surging New York Giants, who took down the Jacksonville Jaguars, one of the top teams in the leagues. Our Tom Ryle and Sean Martin cast their skeptical eyes on things.

Tom – Normally, you take a 21-point win without quibbles. Sadly, I am unable to do that. There was too much in that game that points to potential problems on Thursday.

Let's start with something obvious. Tennessee is in any conversation about the worst franchise in the league this year. And for the most part, that's exactly how they played. Outside of a few rather freaky plays, their offense was mostly impotent, especially their passing game.

Cam Ward managed to deliver a respectable 248 yards passing and two touchdowns without an interception, but his offensive line failed him. He was sacked five times for 28 yards lost, and endured eight QB hits. Kudos to the Cowboys' pass rush, but you have to look at what they were up against. This game was just too easy for them to penetrate and get to Ward.

Flipping it around, Dak Prescott saw very little pressure, with no sacks and only one hit. That is the main reason he had such a magnificent performance. He just had all the time he needed to keep leading the team down the field and into the end zone.

Both CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens eclipsed 100 yard receiving and scored a touchdown, showing that not only was the Titans' pass rush not much of a threat, their secondary was badly overmatched. Prescott set records in the game.

We have to expect that the Eagles are going to present much more of a challenge. And this season, Prescott has been significantly less effective when pressured. Don't forget that Philly is coming off their own win, a close one over the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Sean – As unrealistic as it is, obviously in some sense the offense showed they can do what it takes to win every game. Can we expect a scoring drive on every possession though? Of course not. The defense however played much more like the type of defense that should be supporting a high powered offense.

The splash plays like five more sacks and the fumble nearly returned for a touchdown are exactly what they should be after – instant drive-killers that get the ball back in Dak Prescott's hands. Mike Pettine seemed to understand this, and I'm not discounting the fact that maybe Schottenheimer's splendid day calling plays had something to do with less pressure in the week of game prep worrying about the defense because of Pettine's steady hand.

Is the most realistic, grounded, and likely outcome still that this team has dropped too many winnable games early to parlay another November run into a playoff berth? Unfortunately, yes. But the ultimate gift of this lousy division is one the Cowboys are going to firmly stay in the hunt for, and the Giants are far from locks to win it just yet.

Also, we were so starved for even average defense last year that when the team won these November games, Quinnen Williams making a few plays here and there was seen as enough to get excited about. Now, we're seeing more and more each week that Williams will be someone to build this defense around in the offseason.

That's still a tough consolation prize should this team actually miss the playoffs for a third straight year, but building blocks need to be put in place on defense at some point, and there's enough runway left for that to happen through December I hope.

Tom – At least the defense seemed to be more consistent. That pressure speaks for itself. The secondary held up most of the game, with just a few notable breakdowns. One was a pass interference call the announcers were not so sure about, and the other just a bit of being asleep at the wheel when they failed to make sure the receiver was actually on the ground when they touched him.

I think those are more related to the discipline issue than anything, but take some hope from how things looked outside those plays and the wild touchdown throw against the second-stringers at the end.

Now it comes down to stringing together two at least acceptable defensive performances paired with the offense not slowing down. Again, this should be a much harder task against our beloved NFC East rival from Philadelphia.

The key here may just be something that is applicable far beyond the football field. Take care of the things that are in your control and don't get distracted by what isn't. Dallas can't do a thing about the Giants until the penultimate game of the season. They need to keep improving and just win as many as they can over the final six contests, and things should work out.

Sean – The Eagles are coming to Arlington off a win that looks to be something of the polar opposite of how yet another fairly wild Cowboys home game played out on Sunday. A game where both teams score less than 20 points is a super rarity these days, but when the Eagles had lost seven straight coming in, that's probably the fluky type of game they needed.

The most concrete take to have is probably that old friend Mike McCarthy's offense is sputtering badly in Pittsburgh, but given how the Cowboys have dropped every chance to show us fans they can string together wins, another strong Eagles defensive performance to make Thanksgiving a nail-biter can't be ruled out either.

Then there's the fact Joe Milton will know this defense well, and is a hard QB to bring to the ground in pass rush. If that element of the defense which Mike Pettine has working is neutralized at all, anything can happen on a short week for these rivals.

Anyway, here's hoping for a great turkey day, Tom! As our colleague RJ Ochoa knows well, and many other Cowboys faithful in the Lone Star state as well, I've dipped my toe into the very popular hobby of smoking meat. So with that said, I'm throwing my bird on the smoker in the early hours of what should be a fairly cold morning in Montana and going to see how it comes out.

It should be fun, and likewise for the game, with not much to say about the Bobcats this week thanks to earning a bye in the FCS playoffs. My college team earned that bye by yet again beating our bitter rivals at Montana, which was an awesome Saturday before the Cowboys dropped 51 on the Titans.

Fun fact: 51 is actually the record for most points the Cowboys have ever scored in a Thanksgiving game, doing so in 1980 against the Seahawks to win 51-7. I guess Cowboys like hunting birds on Thanksgiving…
