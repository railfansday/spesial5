Severity of Drake Maye injury suggests Patriots QB is done for season

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

It appears that the New England Patriots may need to navigate the remainder of the 2026-27 NFL campaign without star quarterback Drake Maye under center.

Maye, who suffered an ankle injury against the Detroit Lions in Munich, remained in Germany following Sunday's game to undergo further testing. Immediately, there were concerns that the star quarterback could miss extended time.

Now, ESPN insider Adam Schefter is providing the latest. According to Schefter, the Patriots "are concerned that Drake Maye fractured his ankle."

The team and doctors are determining whether the star quarterback will need to undergo surgery, which would effectively end his season. But for now, he is out "indefinitely."

New England is currently in the mix for an AFC Wild Card bid, but if they are forced to go through the remainder of the season with Tommy DeVito under center, it's a grim outlook for the team.

The Patriots have games against the Buffalo Bills, Minnesota Vikings, and Kansas City Chiefs coming up in three of the next four weeks, which is not an ideal situation for a team with a backup quarterback. That stretch could doom New England's season.

It has been a down year for Maye, who came into the year with high expectations, throwing for 2,330 yards, 18 touchdowns, and 11 interceptions. An official update from the team should be coming sooner rather than later.
