Oregon Ducks unveil green and chrome uniforms for Washington rivalry

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Oregon Duck fans are always interested in seeing what their favorite team is going to wear in any given week, but that's no more the case than during rivalry week when the Washington Huskies are on deck. Over the years, we've seen a number of great uniform combinations in this matchup, whether it's retro yellow, white, and green combos, or purple vs. yellow color-rush games.

On Wednesday night, the Ducks unveiled what's in the closet this week for the rivalry game against UW, breaking out a green and chrome combo that features chrome helmets, green jerseys, and green pants.

There's a ton riding on this game, with Oregon keeping their postseason hopes alive should they get a victory, whether that's via the Big Ten Championship Game or an at-large bid into the College Football Playoff. Meanwhile, the Huskies would like nothing more than to ruin the Ducks' season once and for all.
