Alex Singleton tackles Jaylen Warren in the end zone for a safety as Steelers lead 14-5

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

On Black Friday in Pittsburgh, the defenses are outplaying the offenses.

The latest big play on defense came via Broncos linebacker Alex Singleton, who tackled Steelers running back Jaylen Warren in the end zone for a safety. That made the score 14-2 Steelers. On the subsequent possession, the Broncos kicked a field goal to make it 14-5.

More bad news for the Steelers came when linebacker Jack Sawyer was ruled out for the rest of the game with a knee injury.

Pittsburgh has two touchdowns, one of which came from the defense when T.J. Watt recovered a Bo Nix fumble in the end zone. The Steelers’ other touchdown, a Mason Rudolph to DK Metcalf touchdown pass, was the game’s only offensive score.

Rudolph has been better than Nix so far, which few would have expected heading into the game. But this game has been less about the quarterbacks than about the defenses.
