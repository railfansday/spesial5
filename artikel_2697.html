Miami Hurricanes vs Virginia Tech Hokies Week 12 game placed on 6 day hold

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

On Monday afternoon, the ACC announced kick times and TV coverage for games played November 21st.

And for your Hurricanes, coming off the Homecoming game against Duke, that means another home game against the Virginia Tech Hokies. But when exactly will that game be played? We'll find out late Saturday or Sunday.

A quick reminder: conferences can invoke the 6-day hold provision to delay their decision on game times and TV coverage to ensure the best games get the best slots for visibility. Miami has been the subject of 6 day holds in the past, but this is the first such occurrence this season.

Previously, Miami was a no brainer choice for every available primetime TV slot. 8 of Miami's first 9 games were played in Primetime. Fully understandable since Miami has been a top-10 team this whole season, and every opponent treats their game against the Canes as the biggest game in their season.

So, we'll have to wait until after this week's homecoming game against Duke (and likely many others later) to know when next week's Virginia Tech game will be played. No problem. We'll focus on the game in front of us and worry about the rest later.
