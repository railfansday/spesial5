Patriots vs. Packers Wednesday injury report: Hunter Henry among four sidelined

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

While the 21-day return windows opened for wide receiver Mack Hollins and guard Greg Van Roten, the New England Patriots practiced without an offensive captain on Wednesday.

Hunter Henry was among four non-participants in Foxborough as Green Bay Packers work got underway. After sustaining a foot injury last weekend, the tight end stayed sidelined alongside running back TreVeyon Henderson, wide receiver A.J. Brown and defensive tackle Christian Barmore.

Here's the first injury report ahead of Sunday's 4:25 p.m. ET kickoff at Gillette Stadium.

Henry injured his foot before halftime last weekend against the Miami Dolphins. After heading to and from the locker room, the tenured tight end tested things out yet was ultimately downgraded to out in the final quarter. His outing spanned eight offensive snaps as well three catches for 30 yards. That sent rookies Eli Raridon and Tanner Arkin up the depth chart along with August waiver claim Cameron Latu.

Elsewhere for New England's 53-man roster, illnesses kept Henderson and Barmore away on Wednesday. The running back had been ruled out for the AFC East meeting at Hard Rock Stadium after missing three straight practices last week, while the defensive tackle started and split a sack.

As for the No. 1 wide receiver, Brown missed back-to-back sessions before the road trip with a knee issue in addition to the high ankle sprain that landed him on injured reserve in September. The three-time All-Pro was downgraded from questionable on the eve of the 4:25 p.m. ET divisional kickoff as DeMario Douglas, Kyle Williams, Efton Chism III and standard elevation Ryan Miller made up the wideout room.

Hollins landed on injured reserve due to a calf injury in early October, while a hamstring injury sent Van Roten to the same destination in late September.
