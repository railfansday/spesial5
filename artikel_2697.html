NFL playoff picture Week 10: Bengals, Chiefs move into first place; Lions now second in NFC

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Every week for the duration of the 2026 regular season, USA TODAY Sports will provide timely updates to the NFL's ever-evolving playoff picture − typically starting Sunday afternoon and then moving forward for the remainder of the week (through Monday's and Thursday's games or Saturday's, if applicable. And, when the holidays roll around, we'll be watching then, too).

What just happened? What does it mean? What are the pertinent factors (and, perhaps, tiebreakers) prominently in play as each conference's seven-team bracket begins to crystallize? All will be explained and analyzed up to the point when the postseason field is finalized on Sunday, Jan. 10.

Here's where things stand with Week 10 nearly complete:

Week 3: Indianapolis Colts kicker Spencer Shrader (3) celebrates with long snapper Luke Rhodes (46) after Shrader kicked a 58-yard field goal against the Houston Texans at Lucas Oil Stadium. The Colts debuted their "Anvil Strike" uniform - featuring an unfamiliar gray uniform and glossy, metallic blue helmet with the familiar horseshoe logo in white. The Colts won, 19-17, for their first win of the season.

Week 3: Buffalo Bills quarterback Josh Allen (17) hands off to running back James Cook III against the Los Angeles Chargers at New Highmark Stadium. For the game, the Bills debuted their "Nickel City" alternate uniforms - the most striking element being the blue "Charge Helmet," adorned simply with an oversized cutout of the team's familiar logo. The Bills won the game, 24-16.

Week 2: Los Angeles Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford (9) hands the ball to running back Kyren Williams (23) during a 28-6 win on "Monday Night Football" against the New York Giants at SoFi Stadium. The Rams debuted their new "Classic Sol" uniforms, an homage to the team's look during its 1951 championship season.

1. Buffalo Bills (8-1), AFC East leaders: They've never had home-field advantage during the Josh Allen era. May as well get it in the first year they're in their glorious new stadium. Remaining schedule: vs. Dolphins, vs. Chiefs, at Patriots, at Packers, vs. Bears, at Broncos, at Dolphins, vs. Jets

2. Kansas City Chiefs (7-2), AFC West leaders: After splitting with the Broncos, their 3-1 record in division games and 6-1 mark in intraconference games give them a strong start in the tiebreaker department − vaulting them back into first place Sunday and also ahead of Jacksonville. Remaining schedule: vs. Cardinals, at Bills, at Rams, at Bengals, vs. Patriots, vs. 49ers, at Chargers, vs. Raiders

3. Jacksonville Jaguars (7-2), AFC South leaders: They've been as dominant as any team this season, but their Week 2 loss at Denver does and could loom large. But a roster reloaded ahead of the trade deadline and a weak division should keep the Jags on the fast track to a second straight divisional crown. At minimum. Remaining schedule: at Giants, vs. Titans, at Bears, vs. Steelers, at Texans, at Cowboys, vs. Commanders, at Colts

4. Cincinnati Bengals (7-2), AFC North leaders: They moved atop the AFC North on Sunday night by blowing out the Steelers and earning a season split with them. Remaining schedule: at Commanders, vs. Saints, at Browns, vs. Chiefs, at Panthers, at Colts, vs. Ravens, vs. Browns

5. Denver Broncos (7-2), wild card No. 1: Statistically, they have the league's easiest remaining schedule, which could mean a second straight No. 1 seed as things unfold. Remaining schedule: vs. Raiders, at Steelers, vs. Dolphins, at Jets, at Raiders, vs. Bills, at Patriots, vs. Chargers

6. Pittsburgh Steelers (6-3), wild card No. 2: They dropped out of the AFC North lead Sunday night in the Queen City but will try to get healthy on the other end of Pennsylvania next weekend. Remaining schedule: at Eagles, vs. Broncos, vs. Texans, at Jaguars, vs. Ravens, vs. Panthers, at Titans, at Ravens

7. Cleveland Browns (6-4), wild card No. 3: QB Deshaun Watson continues to pull wins out of the fire ... and pulled the Dawg Pound back into a postseason position with Sunday's comeback against Houston. And given they don't play on the road again until Dec. 20, this is a stretch to take advantage of. Remaining schedule: BYE, vs. Raiders, vs. Bengals, vs. Falcons, at Giants, at Ravens, vs. Colts, vs. Bengals

8. New England Patriots (5-5), in the hunt: The bye comes at the right time given their rampant injury issues. But they're running out of time to make a run, especially with the opponents they're about to face. Remaining schedule: BYE, at Chargers, vs. Bills, vs. Vikings, at Chiefs, at Jets, vs. Broncos, vs. Dolphins

9. Baltimore Ravens (4-5), in the hunt: Can new QB1 Jacoby Brissett keep them afloat long enough to turn the reins back over to Lamar Jackson? Maybe, given Baltimore isn't exactly facing a murderers' row ... at least not until it faces division foes each of the final four weeks. Remaining schedule: vs. Chargers, at Panthers, at Texans, BYE, vs. Buccaneers, at Steelers, vs. Browns, at Bengals, vs. Steelers

10. Las Vegas Raiders (4-6), in the hunt: Rookie QB Fernando Mendoza didn't look ready for prime time on Sunday. Whether he's ready to play in Denver remains to be seen. Remaining schedule: at Broncos, at Browns, BYE, vs. Chargers, vs. Broncos, vs. Titans, at Cardinals, at Chiefs

1. San Francisco 49ers (8-1), NFC West leaders: A surprisingly comfortable margin in what was the league's best division last year could be tested in the coming weeks ... but not Sunday in North Texas. Remaining schedule: vs. Vikings [at Mexico City], vs. Seahawks, at Giants, vs. Rams, at Chargers, at Chiefs, vs. Eagles, at Cardinals

2. Detroit Lions (7-2), NFC North leaders: They're actually in a three-way tie with Minnesota and Chicago. But the Lions, who haven't played the Bears, currently have a 1-0 record against those teams, putting them in first. For now. Remaining schedule: vs. Buccaneers, vs. Bears, at Falcons, vs. Titans, at Vikings, vs. Giants, at Bears, at Packers

3. New York Giants (5-3-1), NFC East leaders: While they remain in relative control of the division, they let a win slip away against the Washington Commanders on Thursday night − one that would have been a chokehold on the NFC East. The Giants also have the most difficult remaining schedule in their division, though the Cowboys have yet to prove they can take advantage of their opportunities this season. Remaining schedule: vs. Jaguars, at Colts, vs. 49ers, at Seahawks, vs. Browns, at Lions, at Cowboys, vs. Eagles

4. Carolina Panthers (4-5), NFC South leaders: Sunday's win at New Orleans gave them outright possession of the division lead. Still, the Buccaneers and Saints remain just a game off the lead. Remaining schedule: vs. Ravens, at Buccaneers, at Vikings, vs. Saints, vs. Bengals, at Steelers, vs. Seahawks, at Falcons

5. Minnesota Vikings (7-2), wild card No. 1: Kirk Cousins' return was a smashing success ... through one game. A Week 2 victory at Chicago currently puts Minnesota into the fifth seed. Remaining schedule: vs. 49ers [at Mexico City], vs. Falcons, vs. Panthers, at Patriots, vs. Lions, vs. Commanders, at Jets, vs. Bears

6. Chicago Bears (7-2), wild card No. 2: They lost some ground during their bye week, but they'll be rested and ready to make it up in Week 11. Remaining schedule: vs. Saints, at Lions, vs. Jaguars, at Dolphins, at Bills, vs. Packers, vs. Lions, at Vikings

7. Green Bay Packers (6-4), wild card No. 3: Is this really going to be the fourth straight season they finish as the NFC's final wild-card entry? A 4-4 record in NFC games currently confers the tiebreaker over Seattle, which is 2-3 in that department. Remaining schedule: BYE, at Rams, at Saints, vs. Bills, vs. Dolphins, at Bears, vs. Texans, vs. Lions

8. Seattle Seahawks (6-4), in the hunt: The defending champs have been beset by various issues − a new offensive coordinator, a series of QB injuries, having to use their entire running back depth chart. If most of those resolve, maybe they can ultimately break through the NFC North logjam ahead of them. Remaining schedule: BYE, at 49ers, vs. Cowboys, vs. Giants, at Eagles, vs. Rams, at Panthers, at Rams

9. Los Angeles Rams (6-4), in the hunt: Losing their first two NFC West games further hinders a team that's failed to live up to its own hype this season. The Rams also own the league's second-toughest remaining schedule. Remaining schedule: BYE, vs. Packers, vs. Chiefs, at 49ers, vs. Cowboys, at Seahawks, at Buccaneers, vs. Seahawks

10. Dallas Cowboys (4-6), in the hunt: They're now two games back of the Giants after getting steamrolled Sunday by San Francisco. And winning the NFC East is probably Dallas' only hope of making the field. Remaining schedule: vs. Titans, vs. Eagles, at Seahawks, BYE, at Rams, vs. Jaguars, vs. Giants, at Commanders
