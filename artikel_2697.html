Stats Rundown: 5 numbers to know from the Mavericks 120-101 win over the Nuggets

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Dallas Mavericks (11-7) took on the Denver Nuggets (10-6) in Emirates Cup action on Tuesday night. Carrying a three-game winning streak into Denver, Dallas continued the trend with a 120-101 victory.

The Mavericks were leading by 13 points with 1:16 remaining in the second quarter. Jamal Crawford, announcing on the Peacock broadcast, stated that the final minute-plus was crucial – the score could easily balloon to 17, or be cut to 8. The Mavericks reeled off six more as part of a 15-0 run to bump the lead to 19 points.

Perhaps the Mavericks had the release of Grand Theft Auto VI on their minds, as they were stealing everything in sight. Dallas had nine steals in the first half – five of which came from Caleb Martin – which they turned into 17 points. This was a textbook example of turning defense into offense. Turning the ball over limited the Nuggets' offense in a big way and the Mavericks doubled the damage by scoring on the other end.

Throughout the first half, the Mavericks' three-point defense left a bit to be desired, but they weren't badly hurt due in part to the Nuggets missing their attempts. The third quarter flipped that script in a significant way. Dallas' lack of close-outs often left shooters wide open and Denver shot themselves right back into the game. The Nuggets hit six threes in the frame, with Christian Braun alone hitting three in a row in under a minute.

The Mavericks showed a very well-balanced offense, which was critical on a night where both Cooper Flagg and Kyrie Irving struggled a bit with their shot. All five Mavericks starters – Irving, Flagg, Martin, Zaccharie Risacher and Daniel Gafford – were joined by P.J. Washington and Naji Marshall in scoring double figures. Morez Johnson, Jr. was a single point shy of joining the club.

Coming into the night, Flagg needed to connect on two three-pointers to achieve the rare milestone of scoring 1,500 or more points and hitting 100 or more three-pointers before turning 20-years-old. With only a single made three, he temporarily fell short of joining Kobe Bryant as the only teenagers in league history to achieve the feat. He has just under a month to get there, with his 20th birthday arriving on Dec. 21.
