Heidi Klum Shocks In Severed Hand Halloween Costume

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Heidi Klum has done it again. The Project Runway host exceeded all our Halloween expectations by transforming herself into a giant, severed human hand nearly two meters tall for the latest edition of her annual party, held Saturday night in New York City. The costume, which required hours of work by her makeup and prop team, left no detail to chance: hyper-realistic veins modeled after her own anatomy, wrinkles, red-painted nails, and an exxagerated version of the rings she typically wears.

“Mike [Marino, from the special effects team ProRenFX] has all of these scans of my body, so he was able to use those to create an exact replica of my hand,” Klum explained to Vogue. “We wanted everything to be as realistic as possible—the anatomy, the veins, the muscles, all the little details that make a hand look like a real hand.”

The costume’s structure used the model's legs to form two of the fingers, while her right arm provided mobility for the thumb. Only her eyes and mouth were visible amid the bloody wounds on her palm, from which—to top off the gruesomeness of it all—Heidi Klum would occasionally spit out the fake blood that a team of assistants injected into her mouth. The most macabre part was at the wrist, where a horrific gash exposed a jumble of blood, bones, and torn flesh.

Heidi Klum (L) recieves an injection of fake blood to augment her 2026 Halloween costume.

Other guests at Klum's party included Kyle MacLachlan, who arrived dressed as his character from The Flintstones; Neil Patrick Harris and his husband David Burtka, dressed as the girls from The Shining; and Heidi Klum and Flavio Briatore's daughter, Leni Klum.

Before making her grand entrance, the host had built up anticipation on social media by posting clues about this year’s costume, such as a photo of her real hand that showed her wedding ring alongside the small tattoo of husband Tom Kaulitz's name. It, too, was part of her Halloween costume. “Nothing was overlooked! I love that Mike pays attention to things that most people might not even notice at first. The details make these costumes so special. It’s not just any giant hand: It’s my giant hand!” Klum said.

Heidi Klum 2026 Halloween costume, as seen from the back.

This is the 25th annual Halloween party thrown by the former Victoria’s Secret Angel: The event began in 2000, and has only continued to expand in scope. “ “Can you believe it? Twenty-five years of Halloween, costumes, incredible transformations, and so many amazing memories,” she said.

Last night’s “amputated hand” joins Klum's collection of Halloween costumes that ranges from last year’s mythological Medusa (with a petrified Kaulitz by her side) to E.T., to Shrek’s Fiona, to a disgusting giant worm inside which she feared she might suffocate. We're already looking forward to what she comes up with for next year.
