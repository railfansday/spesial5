Best Mitya build in Genshin Impact: Weapons, artifacts, and teams

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Capable of excelling as both a meta main and sub-DPS, Mitya arrived as a godsend for all looking for an Electro Stellar-Conduct specialist in Genshin Impact.

But as he also comes with one of the most convoluted kits the game has ever produced, building him can feel like a tall task at first. Here are the best main and sub-DPS Mitya builds in Genshin Impact.

Mitya is a flexible DPS capable of dealing high on-field and off-field Electro/Stellar-Conduct DMG. The first pillar of his kit lies in his Detection Core. Generated by his Elemental Skill, the core follows and deals Electro damage to nearby enemies when in Steady State, allows Stellar-Conduct reactions to generate Gradient Beacons in Overloaded State, and provides the following:

His final Combat Passive then sets all into motion by allowing Mitya to turn all of the party’s Superconduct reactions (and their DMG) into Stellar-Conduct, now generating Gradient Beacons and putting him into Radiance. The beacons stay by the on-field character while applying the Polestar Field effect, increasing Mitya’s CRIT Rate by 5% (20% max) and boosting the team’s Stellar-Conduct DMG based on his maximum EM. Also generated via his Burst, up to four beacons can be active simultaneously.

Mitya’s signature 5-star catalyst, Starsealed Edict, is his best weapon and provides both multiple Elemental Mastery buffs and a massive amount of CRIT DMG. The latter is huge here, as Stellar-Conduct DMG can crit to maximum effect.

With his recommended weapons already providing plenty of CRIT DMG, a 4-piece Disenchantment in Deep Shadow is the best option for main DPS Mitya, with a full Gilded Dreams set feeling handmade for the sub-DPS role.

As an on-field DPS, Mitya relies on his Overload-charged attack combos to deal most of his DMG, something he can only do if paired with a Cryo enabler/main buffer (ideally Odette). Valeriy is then his ideal secondary support/shielder, with Yae Miko being the perfect fourth slot pick via her high DMG and energy generation. If you wish to use Mitya as an EM-focused reaction sub-DPS, he will usually take Yae Miko’s place as the main Electro applier in Sandrone’s best team, although both can work in tandem when paired with Wriothesley. As you can see below, he can also be a key component in one of Cyno’s most F2P-friendly teams.

Thankfully for all saving for Anastasya, Mitya is not a must-pull in Genshin Impact. After all, even if a premier Electro/Stellar-Conduct main DPS, he can be challenged in the role by other 5-stars within his type, such as Cyno. He is also not irreplaceable in the sub-DPS role, with a C1 Yae Miko being able to perform just as well and even surpassing his value in most meta compositions.
