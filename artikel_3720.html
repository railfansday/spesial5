Scheana Shay Has a Morbid Reason for Wanting Another Baby

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Vanderpump Rules alum Scheana Shay and her husband, Brock Davies, have been discussing whether to expand their family.

“This is something we’ve been talking about a little more lately,” Scheana, 41, said on a recent episode of her “Scheananigans” podcast. “I’ve been back and forth with if we want another kid, if we want to do a surrogate and all of that.”

Scheana and Brock, 36, welcomed daughter Summer Moon in 2021. (Brock is also the father of two older children from a previous relationship, from whom he is estranged.)

“There was a friend of mine’s dad’s funeral that we were at [in] the beginning of this year,” Scheana tearfully recalled on her podcast. “Seeing her stand up there with her two siblings, I just knew when that moment comes for Summer, I want her to have someone. I don’t want her to be up there alone.”

The former Bravo star and Brock have yet to officially make plans to have another child.

“However, I’m not necessarily ready to take that next step yet,” Scheana noted. “Summer also, right now, is in this only-child phase where she does not want to share me.”

Should Scheana and Brock decide to move forward with expanding their brood, the reality TV alum has stated that she wouldn’t carry their child after complications from her last pregnancy.

“HELLP [Hemolysis, Elevated Liver enzymes and Low Platelet count] syndrome can be fatal. It’s something most people don’t know about,” she exclusively told Us in her July 2025 cover story. “I certainly didn’t until I was transferred to the high-risk ICU. When Summer was born and not making a sound, I was terrified. I thought she wasn’t going to make it. It was the scariest minute of my entire life.”

At the time, Scheana admittedly hadn’t yet “fully come to terms with” using a gestational carrier to potentially welcome another baby.

“I felt [Summer] kick, I felt her hiccup, I breastfed her, and it’s going to be a very different experience this time,” she told Us. “I worry that I might not have the same connection to the baby, but we are slowly taking steps in that direction.”

Scheana and Brock’s marriage was also tested during her pregnancy. She revealed in her My Good Side memoir last year that Brock had an affair with an unidentified woman from his gym and later apologized.

“It takes work every single day. I will forever have insecurities,” she told Us. “Just the other day, I saw him swipe and delete a text, and I’m like, ‘What was that?’ And he’s like, ‘Seriously, Scheana?’ I genuinely believe he would never do anything like that again. I do trust him. I have forgiven him.”
