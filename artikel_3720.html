How to get Solid Gold Hatsune Miku in Fortnite Treasure Hunters

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

There are many special loot items up for grabs around the Fortnite Treasure Hunters island, including a rare collection of gold skins. One such outfit you might be on the hunt for is Solid Gold Hatsune Miku.

Finding this rare skin is quite a daunting task, and there’s only a limited amount of time when you can earn her, so acting fast is essential. If you’re wondering how to add this outfit to your locker, here’s how to get Solid Gold Hatsune Miku in Fortnite Treasure Hunters.

Solid Gold Hatsune Miku is a random Treasure Chest loot item, which means you have a chance of finding her whenever you’re looting around the Fortnite island. There’s no guaranteed way to get her, and instead, it all comes down to luck and chance.

She is one of the rarest items featured in Treasure Chests, as are all other Solid Gold skins like A$AP Rocky and Lil Uzi Vert. These outfits are the overall rarest loot drops available on the island, so finding them is incredibly difficult.

Whenever you open a loot container, you have a shot at finding special Treasure Chest bonus items in addition to the regular goodies you get from the container. The featured items rotate on a weekly basis, with Solid Gold Hatsune Miku being part of the loot pool for week three. All of the items you can potentially find for this week are as follows.

With such an expansive pool of potential items you may be granted while looting, actually finding Solid Gold Hatsune Miku is a tough process, as all other loot drops have much higher odds than she does. Because of this, all you can do is loot as much as possible and hope luck ends up favoring you before she leaves.

Solid Gold Hatsune Miku can be found in any Treasure Chest around the island. This includes both regular chests and Rare Chests, so loot all containers you come across for a chance at finding her. There are no fixed spawn locations for the Solid Gold Hatsune Miku Outfit.

The standard odds and drop rate for Solid Gold Hatsune Miku are 1 in 1,562,500, which translates to roughly 0.000064 percent. This is the baseline chance for all Solid Gold weekly skins.

The baseline odds and drop rates for Treasure Chest loot sometimes change to improve the odds. Keep an eye out for the next Treasure Hours event to have a boosted chance of finding this skin.

Solid Gold Hatsune Miku is available to find from Nov. 16 to 21. You have just under a week to find her before she leaves the active loot pool and is replaced by the next weekly Solid Gold skin.

After this period ends, it’s unknown whether this skin and all other weekly ones will be available again, or if they are only exclusive to their initial runs. They may be added back in the loot pool near the end of the season, introduced as assets you can buy from the shop using V-Bucks, or instead vaulted, which would mean only players who found them during their featured week would ever have them.
