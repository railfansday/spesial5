Swagbucks Review – Most Surveys Pay About $1 to $3, Screen-Outs Pay Nothing

Use Swagbucks to earn in idle minutes and to get cash back on online shopping you’d do anyway. Most of its surveys pay about $1 to $3, and one that screens you out pays nothing, so expect spare change rather than an hourly wage.

Swagbucks is a free rewards site you can join from age 13, owned by Prodege, which also owns InboxDollars. It pays points called SB, with 100 SB worth $1 in gift cards, for surveys, shopping through its links, sign-up offers, games, and receipt scans.

Swagbucks pays off for idle minutes and for shopping you’d do anyway, where the only extra step is starting the purchase from its Shop page. Assuming no screen-outs, a $2 survey that takes 20 minutes pays $6 an hour, less than the $7.25 federal minimum wage, so put any hour you could be paid for into paid work.
