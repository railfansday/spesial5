The Bureau of Labor Statistics counted 11.9 million independent contractors in their main job in July 2023, 7.4% of U.S. workers, and 1.9 million in a second job. If you work for yourself without forming a company, you’re a sole proprietor, and you can open each account here with just your Social Security number and pay no monthly fee.

No employer withholds tax from your payments, so you set it aside yourself. Self-employment tax is 15.3%, charged on 92.35% of your profit, according to the IRS. That works out to about 14% of profit below Social Security’s yearly earnings cap. Add your federal income tax rate and any state rate, and set aside the total from each payment, about 26% in the 12% federal bracket with no state income tax. Each pick can move that share aside automatically, most on a free plan. They’re all business accounts, since a bank can bar business use of its personal accounts, as Ally’s deposit agreement does.
