Villa hires former Century Communities CFO Messenger as CEO

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Villa has named homebuilding and real estate veteran David Messenger as CEO to lead the offsite homebuilder’s next growth phase. Villa says its integrated offsite model, which has delivered more than 425 homes since 2019, positions it to support larger communities. The move signals a push toward disciplined, community-scale growth and deeper partnerships with institutional housing players.  AI Summary

Villa, a San Francisco-based manufactured homebuilder, has appointed homebuilding and real estate veteran David Messenger as CEO, the company announced on Monday.

Founded in 2019, Villa says it has delivered more than 425 homes using an offsite construction model that spans accessory dwelling units (ADUs), multifamily projects and new neighborhoods. The company manages feasibility, design, permitting, site preparation and installation, and partners with a network of factories for production, according to the announcement.

Messenger brings decades of financial and operating experience in homebuilding and multifamily real estate, having spent more than 10 years as chief financial officer of Century Communities. During his tenure, Century expanded from about 150 to 1,800 employees across 18 states, grew to $4.5 billion in annual revenue and more than 10,000 annual closings, completed seven acquisitions and raised more than $3 billion in debt and equity capital, the company said.

He also led Century’s initial public offering and helped launch its mortgage, title and insurance subsidiaries, giving him direct experience with the vertically integrated models many large homebuilders use to manage costs and capture more of the housing value chain. Earlier in his career, Messenger spent roughly a decade at UDR, a publicly traded multifamily real estate investment trust, where he advanced through senior finance roles to become CFO.

Messenger’s appointment comes as Villa looks to expand beyond individual ADUs and small projects into larger communities in partnership with land developers and homebuilders. The company is already working on new neighborhoods including the 25-home Acacia Village in Santa Rosa, California, Alpine Village in Westminster, Colorado, and homes in Altadena, California, for families rebuilding after the Eaton Fire, according to the release.

Investors say his background in scaling a public homebuilder and in capital markets was a key factor in the decision.

“Villa has demonstrated its capabilities across ADUs, multifamily properties and new neighborhoods, and Dave is the right leader to build on Villa’s success and advance its growth strategy,” said Aaron Marsh, partner and CFO at Unless, an investor in Villa and member of Villa’s board of directors. “He brings deep housing experience, a track record of scaling businesses nationally and a clear understanding of what land and development partners need to move projects forward.”

Messenger said he sees an opportunity to apply processes from large-scale homebuilding to Villa’s offsite platform.

“This team has done what much of our industry has only talked about: delivering quality homes through offsite construction across individual properties, multifamily communities and new neighborhoods,” Messenger said in the announcement. “I see great potential to build on that foundation by bringing a large-scale homebuilder’s operating discipline to Villa, with an emphasis on predictable execution and profitable growth.”

Villa’s model aims to give homeowners, multifamily operators and developers a single partner from concept through completion. The company handles site feasibility, site and home design, permitting and on-site work and uses factory partners to build homes offsite, aiming for more predictable timelines and costs than traditional stick-built construction, according to the release.

“Dave has an exceptional track record spanning homebuilding, multifamily real estate, capital markets and land development,” said Heather Miksch, chief operating officer at Villa. “As we build on Villa’s momentum and pursue development opportunities, his experience will be invaluable in helping us grow our business while continuing to serve both homeowners and development partners.”
