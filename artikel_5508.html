LCH2 Awarded Additional $1 Million to Advance Lewis County Clean Energy Campus

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Total grant support reaches $1.4 million as the "From Wood to AI" project advances site, engineering, permitting, commercial and financing work.Supported in part by a grant from TransAlta Centralia Generation LLC.

CENTRALIA, Wash., Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The Lewis County Hydrogen Alliance, Inc. (LCH2) has been awarded an additional $1 million from TransAlta Centralia Board Funding, LLC, acting through its Energy Technology Board under the Centralia Coal Transition Grants program, to advance development of the Lewis County Clean Energy Campus. Total grant support now reaches $1.4 million.

The new funding supports site control, engineering, permitting, feedstock-supply and product-offtake agreements, and project-financing work. The initial assessment examined feedstock and logistics, the site-selection pathway, concept-level process engineering and permitting, supporting a recommendation to advance.

The full-campus vision would use regional forestry residuals to produce hydrogen, clean carbon coproducts and dispatchable electricity, with a next-generation AI Advanced Compute Complex alongside the energy infrastructure.

At full buildout, the current planning case targets approximately 1,000 wet metric tonnes per day of forestry residuals and a 50-megawatt advanced-compute IT load. LCH2's management target of more than 220 family-wage jobs is a project target, not an independently completed economic-impact forecast.

"Centralia's Coal Transition Grant program was created to help communities build durable economic and energy opportunities through change. LCH2's next stage brings together Lewis County's forestry strengths, emerging clean-energy technology and new industrial demand in a way that is aligned with that long-term purpose. We are pleased to see locally grounded projects continue moving from concept toward execution, supporting the region's next industrial chapter," said Chris Fralick, Executive Vice President, Generation and Chief Operating Officer of TransAlta.
