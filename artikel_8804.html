Y2K Running Shoes Are Back, and These Nikes Will Put You on Trend

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

They’re also a great gift for this holiday season.

Need a new pair of knockaround sneakers? Skip the Stan Smiths or the Vans. Right now, Y2K-era running shoes are the trendy choice. Shoe brands across the market are reviving early ’00s designs as casual sneakers, but Nike is leading the pack, and Google just named its V5 RNR a Top 100 holiday gift this year. Whether you’re wrapping them up for someone else or gifting to yourself, these sneakers are a solid pickup, especially since Nike just put them on sale with a huge markdown.

The Nike V5 RNR normally retails for $95, but several colorways are marked down to $77, and you can use code EARLYBF to take an extra 25% off the sale price—bringing them down to just $58. This shoe comes in unisex sizing, and plenty of sizes are in stock. Nike’s prices beat DSW and Zappos, too.

The V5 RNR features all the hallmarks of early ‘00s running shoe design. The biggest tell is the multilayered upper: synthetic mesh dressed up with swooping stitched overlays, all in matching tonal colors. Underneath, a sculpted foam midsole (Nike doesn’t specify the compound, but it’s likely basic EVA) provides a cushioned ride, and large patches of outsole rubber deliver reliable grip and good durability for everyday walking.

These kicks come in a wide variety of neutral and pastel colorways, which makes them easy to style. Choose black, gray, or white for a classic sporty look that pairs well with any athleisure outfit, or opt for a pastel shade for a subtle pop of color.

At just $58, the V5 RNR is a serious bargain, too. Snag your pair today while there are still plenty of sizes and colorways to choose from.
