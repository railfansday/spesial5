Metaphor: ReFantazio Switch 2 Patch To Resolve Colour Glitch

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Metaphor: ReFantazio arrived on the Switch 2 this week, and while the port has been well-received by fans and critics alike, there's one issue you may encounter.

Sega (and Atlus) have acknowledged this an official Japanese post about the game's release. Fortunately, it's not a game-breaking bug or anything like that, it just changes the colours of objects and characters. Here's a rough translation:

Atlus: "We have confirmed that after playing for a certain period, some objects and characters temporarily display colors different from their original colors. This bug does not affect game progress.

"We are preparing an update patch to fix this bug. When the stream begins, we will announce it on the official X account and official website."

As noted, a patch to address this issue is already in the works. This bug apparently can appear after 30 hours of gameplay.

Alongside this news, Atlus has also shared a special illustration to commemorate the release of this title on the Switch 2 this week. The artwork is from the game's character designer Shigenori Soejima.

If you aren't already playing this game on the Switch 2, there's a demo you can download from the eShop (save data also carries over), and you can find out more information about this version of the title in our review here on Nintendo Life.
