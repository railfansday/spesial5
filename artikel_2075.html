Lisandro Martinez to Barcelona: Deco gives verdict on the move for defender

Manchester United signed Lisandro Martinez in 2022 from Ajax for £57 million but at the end of his contract, he might leave United for free.

Martinez is now in his fifth season at Old Trafford after joining from Ajax for £57 million in 2022. He has made 121 appearances in all competitions for the club, but his long-term future remains unclear.

Manchester United are not expected to stand in his way if a suitable opportunity to leave comes, especially following his public criticism of the club’s transfer strategy last month.

The defender is out of contract at the end of the season, although United have the option to trigger a further 12 months. Whether the club chooses to use that clause remains to be seen.

A departure from Manchester United could give Martinez the chance to find a new challenge after four seasons in Manchester. Barcelona had looked like a possible destination, but the latest reports suggest they are not currently pushing for his signature.

Lisandro Martinez’s hopes of joining Barcelona appear to have suffered a major setback, with reports from Spain claiming that neither sporting director Deco nor manager Hansi Flick is convinced by the Manchester United defender.

Atletico Madrid had also been mentioned among the interested clubs but there weren’t any further reports on a possible transfer, leaving Martínez’s options in Spain uncertain.

The latest claims suggest a switch to the Camp Nou may be unlikely.

Journalist Pedro Bravo on El Chiringuito reports that Martínez’s profile does not appeal to either member of Barcelona’s sporting leadership.

“Lisandro, as far as I’m aware, neither Deco nor Flick are keen on him,” Bravo was quoted as saying.

The journalist also claimed to have spoken directly with Deco, who is also ruled Martinez out.

“He’s a good player, but we don’t like him. He doesn’t fit in with us.”

With United not expected to sell him in the winter transfer window, United may be looking at extending his deal to recoup some transfer fees by selling him in the summer transfer window.
