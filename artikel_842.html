'You question everything' - Haaland lays City's frustrations bare

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Erling Haaland said Manchester City's form is 'not good enough' after a run of one win in seven games; Next up for Manchester City is a trip to the Premier League champions Arsenal on Sunday, live on Sky Sports

Erling Haaland has admitted Manchester City's poor form is affecting the players, admitting "when you don't win, you question everything" after they were held 2-2 at home by Napoli in the Champions League.

Rayan Cherki's late strike salvaged a draw but Enzo Maresca's side again struggled to find their fluency.

The draw leaves City in sixth spot in the Champions League table but they are likely to drop out of the top eight by Wednesday evening and have now gone 10 games without a clean sheet. Maresca's men look vulnerable ahead of the trip to Arsenal on Sunday, live on Sky Sports.

Haaland speaking to TNT Sports said: "We tried to push but we couldn't. It's not good enough. We let them in too easily and were unable to control or attack the game as we want. Football can be so difficult and easy. When you win games everything is easy and you know exactly what to do, when you don't win you start to question everything. The perfect thing would be to don't think, but it's difficult in these moments.

"We have so much quality. It's about getting that quality out of every single player. We have to regroup to stay positive and bring the energy on the pitch.

"We go to the champions next. The most difficult game of the season. We need a top, top performance."

Ruben Dias was given the night off against Napoli as it was Marc Guehi and Josko Gvardiol that started together as the centre-back pairing for the first time this season.

Stability is proving elusive for City. Maresca has used eight different back four combinations across City's last 10 games in all competitions, changing his defensive personnel from one game to the next throughout that run.

Not a single clean sheet has been forthcoming and they have conceded 20 goals in that period.

Maresca said: "It's a big game on Sunday. The players have confidence. We don't like these moments when we don't win games, we all want to win games. But unfortunately we need to be patient, to learn and move on.

"We are conceding too many goals. It's quite clear. It's something we need to adjust. We are able to adjust it."

"Move the ball quicker," yelled one fan from behind the press box. "Press them," screamed a youngster in front. They were not wrong. Manchester City still have the individual quality to entertain the Etihad Stadium but they are also a frustrating watch at present.

City have picked up just one point from their last four Premier League games and while they did defeat RB Leipzig 3-2 in their previous Champions League outing, they let in two more in this draw with Napoli. It is 10 games now without keeping a clean sheet.

"Off the ball, the team was good from my point of view," said Maresca in the press conference afterwards, presenting a positive spin. "We did not concede a lot. The problem is that they were clinical. We created many chances." Not everyone agreed.

Haaland's assessment that this was "not good enough" and that City were "unable to control or attack the game" chimed more with the feeling inside the stadium. The line that "when you do not win you start to question everything" will resonate.

Maresca is being questioned. He has stressed that he is months into his time here, not years and it is evident that he is still searching for the solutions. Finding the best role for new signings Elliot Anderson and Enzo Fernandez is something that he is still tweaking.

Against Napoli, it was Jeremy Doku's spark off the bench that helped to bring the first equaliser and Rayan Cherki's class that delivered the second. But it was all the bits in between, with and without the ball, that will concern the Manchester City supporters.

Rasmus Hojlund got the better of Marc Guehi and Gianluigi Donnarumma as Napoli were rewarded for pressing City high up the pitch. Noa Lang converted Leonardo Spinazzola's right-wing cross on the counter-attack - highlighting another weakness.

Maresca turned on his heels and looked to the skies in exasperation at that one because it is a recurring issue for City. They remain susceptible to the breakaways even when he loads the midfield in attempt to prevent it. Losing duels is another theme.

Sluggishness is an easy accusation. Haaland was largely anonymous, unable to get into the game. But that is because City are yet to find a pressing structure that works for them. Even if Haaland does close down, his team-mates are not necessarily following.

As had happened against Fulham at the weekend, Napoli were able to have good possession of the ball, something that was once unheard of for visiting teams at this stadium. That aura was already fading under Pep Guardiola but it is truly absent now.

Maresca is an excellent coach and the structure at Manchester City remains such that he can expect to be given time to figure all this out. But as he himself has previously acknowledged, there is not just one thing that is undermining this team right now.

The loss at Aston Villa was not the same as the home reverse to Brighton. Fulham approached it very differently to Nottingham Forest. Both got results. And now Napoli, in just one game, showed two different ways to undo Maresca's side.
