Rob Kardashian Restores Cousin’s Vintage Car in Rare TV Appearance

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

While Rob Kardashian is mostly absent from his family’s Hulu series The Kardashians, his recent camo on a Prime Video series has a sweet nod to dad Robert Kardashian Sr.’s family.

Rob Kardashian is kicking his latest rare reality TV appearance into gear.

While his sisters are fixture of pop culture with their Hulu series The Kardashians, Rob has managed to stay mostly out of the spotlight in recent years. However, he made a rare exception for the latest episode of Fully Torqued Unleashed.

In the third episode of season three of the Prime Video series, which dropped Nov. 7, Rob makes a brief cameo to ask host Steve Pazamy to assist in a surprise for his father Robert Kardashian Sr.’s cousin Cici Bussey. The 39-year-old expressed that he wants to fix up Cici’s 1963 Mercedes, which she has owned over 20 years.

Later, Cici is floored by the gift and expressed her gratitude on camera.

“If it wasn’t for Rob, I wouldn’t have met Steve,” she said in an interview. “And if it wasn’t for Steve, my car wouldn’t look beautiful.”

But while Rob was willing to put himself on camera for the sake of his cousin once removed—as well as to celebrate his sister Kim’s 46th birthday last month—he has previously shared why he prefers to keep to himself.

“It just has everything to do with myself,” he told his sister Khloe Kardashian during a 2025 episode of Khloe in Wonder Land, emphasizing he remains tight with Khloe, as well as Kourtney Kardashian, Kim Kardashian and mom Kris Jenner, “There's definitely nothing wrong between any of us.”

For Rob—dad to daughter Dream, 10, with ex Blac Chyna—he simply doesn’t want to put himself in a situation where he’s not “comfortable.”

“Like, I'm not comfortable in my skin, so why would I wanna go be on camera and just be vulnerable and let people in?” the Arthur George founder continued. “That’s not what I wanna do.”

However, there is one part of filming that Rob does enjoy—enough to even bring him back from time to time.

“I love filming if it's positive and if it's natural and I'm feeling good about myself,” he expressed. “I like being around my family, so that stuff is good to me.”

Nevertheless, he admitted, “The reason I don't is because I'm a homebody and I just choose, like I said, my peace and happiness.”
