Manchester United have triggered their option to extend captain Bruno Fernandes’ contract for another year to keep him at Old Trafford to 2028.

The 32-year-old was in the last year of his deal, but United had built in the chance to add a further 12 months, which they have now done.

United chief executive Omar Berrada said last week that the club was holding talks to give Fernandes a new deal but the midfielder promptly said he had put them on hold to concentrate on getting results.

“The club offered me a new contract so I think that, from their side, shows that they ⁠want me to stay,” he said then.

Fernandes has attracted interest in the past from Saudi Pro-League clubs and from Galatasaray but has chosen to stay at United.

If United had not taken up the option, Fernandes could have discussed a summer move to a foreign club from 1 January.

A 2020 signing from Sporting CP, he has scored 115 goals in 343 games for United, including the first two goals of new manager Mauricio Pochettino’s reign in a 2-2 draw with his former club on Wednesday.

However, he missed training on Friday due to a virus and is a doubt for Saturday’s match against Brentford.

United have three other senior players out of contract next summer, in Luke Shaw, Harry Maguire and Lisandro Martinez, though they also have an option to extend the Argentinian’s deal for a further year.
