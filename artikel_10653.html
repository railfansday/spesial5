Amazon customers have a few weeks to claim share of $309 million settlement

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Amazon customers who experienced problems receiving refunds for returned products could be eligible for compensation from a $309.5 million class action settlement, but the deadline to submit a claim is approaching.

The settlement follows a lawsuit accusing Amazon of improperly handling customer returns, including failing to issue refunds, providing incorrect refunds, and charging customers again after they had returned items.

Amazon has denied any wrongdoing but agreed to a $309.5 million settlement, which impacted customers can claim their share of.

The company also began issuing refunds in 2025 after identifying transactions where payments had not been completed or it could not verify that the correct items had been returned.

According to court documents, Amazon has already issued approximately $570 million in refunds, with additional payments expected as part of the agreement.

Customers may qualify if they purchased a physical product through Amazon.com, received it in the United States, and initiated a return or requested a refund between September 5, 2017, and February 12, 2026.

The settlement divides eligible customers into two groups, with different requirements for receiving compensation.

Customers in Subclass A, whose qualifying transactions have already been identified through Amazon’s records, will automatically receive payments and do not need to submit a claim.

Those in Subclass B must submit a valid claim before December 1, 2026, to receive compensation. Customers can check their eligibility and file a claim through the official settlement website.

The amount customers receive will depend on their individual refund issues and the settlement’s distribution process, so there is no fixed payout.

The December 1 deadline also applies to customers wishing to exclude themselves from the settlement or submit an objection.

However, payments are not guaranteed yet. A final approval hearing is scheduled for March 16, 2027, and compensation will only be distributed once the court approves the agreement and it becomes final.

Amazon has maintained that it did not violate consumer protection laws, and the court has made no finding of wrongdoing.
