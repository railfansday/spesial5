Roma not expected to invest in January midfield signing

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Gian Piero Gasperini’s second season as Roma coach is making Roma fans dream: 27 points after 11 games, 31 goals scored and 10 conceded. The Roman club is two points behind top-seeded Lazio and one behind Chivu’s Inter.

D’Amico is studying the most effective strategy to strengthen the team for the Grugliasco-born coach, who has publicly stated he doesn’t want to sign for second place.

What’s missing from the manager’s squad? Certainly a fast-paced attacking winger, the real priority of the upcoming transfer campaign. Will there be other signings? It doesn’t seem so…

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, Roma will focus solely on the winger, ignoring other positions as rumored in recent weeks.

No other midfielder will be signed, so the squad will remain with four midfielders until the end of the season: Cristante, Koné, De Roon, and Pisilli. They will await Lorenzo Pellegrini’s recovery.
