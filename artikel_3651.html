A price drop always stands out more when the product in question already has a reputation for delivering above its size, and that’s the case with the Bose SoundLink Plus. It hasn’t been on the market for long, but it’s already earned strong reviews—CNET labeled it “the Goldilocks of Bose Bluetooth speakers.” And right now, ahead of Black Friday, it’s down to $179 from $269, which price trackers show is the lowest it’s ever been.

Bose positions it right in the middle of the SoundLink lineup, and you feel that balance in how it’s built. It’s compact enough to clip onto a backpack using its built-in nylon strap, and tough enough to handle getting knocked around, and sealed against dust and full water submersion with an IP67 rating. Battery life can last up to 20 hours, depending on the volume, which is sufficient for full-day outings. On the connectivity side, there’s Bluetooth 5.4 with AAC and AptX Adaptive support, as well as the ability to keep two devices paired for smoother switching. What you don’t get is a microphone, an aux port, or any voice assistant support, which is worth considering if you prefer wired options or use speakers for calls.

As for its sound profile, it leans into mid- and high-bass, which gives electronic and pop tracks a lively pulse, though at higher volumes, you may notice some compression (which is common in speakers this size). It also doesn’t reach into true low-bass territory, so anyone who wants thumping sub-bass may not get it here, but the punch it does deliver feels clean for its size, notes this PCMag review. And while you won't get the width and separation of a stereo mix since the SoundLink Plus plays audio in mono, its directivity is good—meaning its sound remains clear from different listening positions, so you don't have to sit directly in front of it to get a good experience.
