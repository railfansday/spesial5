Despite some early concerns that Phantom Blade Zero would be easy, it's not. Players have flocked to certain corners of the internet to express which enemies are giving them trouble, and things have gotten so bad that the developer, S-Game, has already had to nerf one boss, with more expected.

If waiting for nerfs isn't your style, or you're stuck and can't move on, one player has you covered. They've created a complete cheat menu with auto-parry, auto-block, and unlimited consumables.

A few games have received cheat menus over the last few weeks. One came to Baldur's Gate 3 on console in September, and last month, The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered got one. Now it's Phantom Blade Zero's turn.
