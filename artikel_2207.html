Will Lankshear set for England audition as Thomas Tuchel’s scouts prepare for Middlesbrough trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Middlesbrough signed Will Lankshear in a deal potentially worth up to £20m in the summer, and the striker has been one of the Championship’s stars of the season so far. The 21-year-old’s return of 15 goals in 16 Boro games has raised questions over a potential call-up to the England senior squad.

Now, a report from talkSPORT states that Thomas Tuchel is keeping a keen eye on Lankshear, and scouts are set to watch the striker in action when Middlesbrough face Wrexham on Wednesday night. The Boro star will be looking to score in a sixth consecutive league match.

Tuchel’s next England squad will be named on Friday as UEFA Nations League games against Spain and Croatia approach.

It is a real rarity for Championship players to earn call-ups to the England squad. The last player to be capped by the Three Lions while at a second-tier club was Sam Johnstone in 2021, with Wilfried Zaha, David Nugent, Jay Bothroyd and Jack Butland among other past examples.

Middlesbrough man Lankshear has been a regular in the Young Lions setup, picking up caps at U19, U20 and U21 levels over the past three years.

There can be no denial of Lankshear’s form – 15 goals puts him clear of all other English strikers, including new Ballon d’Or winner Harry Kane. He heads into the Wrexham game off the back of back-to-back braces and he leads the way in the Championship for goals, xG, non-penalty xG, shots and shots on target (Fotmob).

It comes down to the competition for a place, with the last England squad containing captain Kane and Dominic Calvert-Lewin as the only natural strikers. Ivan Toney has 14 goals in 15 games over in Saudi Arabia this season, while Ollie Watkins has six goals in 10 games in the Middle East too.

English strikers Dominic Solanke and Liam Delap have struggled for Premier League form and seem unlikely to come into contention.

If Tuchel would prefer to pick a domestically-based player over Toney or Watkins, Lankshear is arguably the third-best option behind Kane and Calvert-Lewin. It would be a huge step for the Boro star, but it is certainly feasible that he earns a call.

At only 21, Lankshear is emerging as one of England’s brightest young prospects. The Middlesbrough man has already drawn comparisons to Harry Kane, and his form in front of goal is scintillating.

The most exciting thing is that, at his young age, the best is yet to come from Lankshear. It means that while there is a case for him to be called up for the next England internationals, it is not the end of the world if Tuchel overlooks him for now.

Lankshear will be widely backed to make the jump some day. His form means he has a better chance than any of his recent Championship compatriots, but Tuchel is yet to call up someone from England’s second tier, so he may opt to wait until the Boro star has experience at a higher level.
