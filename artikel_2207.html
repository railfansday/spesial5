Sheffield Wednesday vs Plymouth Argyle predictions with opinion split over Dave Challinor’s first League One game

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Sheffield Wednesday face Plymouth Argyle in Dave Challinor‘s first league match in charge of the club. The SWFC boss is tasked with righting the ship, with a four-game winless run in the league leaving them 9th in the table and six points off the automatic promotion spots.

The Owls were beaten in the FA Cup by Oldham Athletic in Challinor’s first game, before a youthful side lost 4-0 to Accrington Stanley in the EFL Trophy on Wednesday night. The new boss will be determined to get off the mark in an important game in the third tier.

As for Plymouth Argyle, they are three points better off than the Owls having played one more game. They sit up in 5th as it stands, winning seven of their 14 League One matches to date.

Unlike their upcoming opponents, the Pilgrims were victorious in their cup matches with wins over Alvechurch and Swindon Town in the FA Cup and EFL Trophy respectively. In their last league game, Tom Cleverley‘s men played out a 0-0 draw against Reading.

“Dave Challinor has led two Sheffield Wednesday games so far, but this feels like the first one that really means something. Points are up for grabs in the bid to climb back up the League One table and after poor results in the cups, he will be determined to get a tune out of his side and put a first win on the board.

“The SWFC boss played a side strong enough to beat Oldham Athletic and he was not impressed as they came up short. It means you have to anticipate changes for a tricky game against Plymouth Argyle.

“The Pilgrims are a good side, but they’ve got some notable absentees through injury. Aribim Pepple and Alex Mitchell are major doubts for the trip to Hillsborough and they will be big misses against an Owls team hungry for all three points.

“Argyle’s away record is strong though, with four wins in the last five League One games on the road. However, I do think their injuries will weaken them here, so I lean towards a valuable win for Challinor and Wednesday.”

“Dave Challinor needs to get off to a winning start with Sheffield Wednesday at Hillsborough this weekend. If SWFC lose, he will be on the back foot already after back-to-back cup losses to Oldham Athletic and Accrington Stanley. In fairness, he played a weakened team against the latter.

“The Owls’ recent form has been dire and their new head coach has a big job on his hands as he looks to get them on track. They have won only one in their last eight matches in all competitions.

“Plymouth Argyle should fancy their chances of getting something in South Yorkshire. PAFC are unbeaten in their last four and will see their upcoming opponents as a promotion rival.

“It is important that the Pilgrims don’t lose on Saturday and they need to be solid at the back. I think Tom Cleverley’s side will have enough to bag themselves a point.”
