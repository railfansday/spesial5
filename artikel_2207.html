Premier League club tracking Middlesbrough star Will Lankshear amid historic form

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Middlesbrough only signed Will Lankshear in the summer, but the striker’s stunning form has brought excitement over his next steps. England boss Thomas Tuchel has eyes on the Boro star, and now, The Sun states Newcastle United are closely watching his performances.

Scouts were set to watch Lankshear in action against Wrexham on Wednesday, but injury kept him sidelined for that game and the 0-0 draw at Watford on Saturday. Kim Hellberg‘s side failed to score in both games, highlighting how much they are missing their star striker.

Lankshear has 13 goals in 13 Championship games, and 15 goals in 16 matches for the Teesside outfit across all competitions. No Boro player has started a season in such prolific form since club record goalscorer George Camsell in 1926.

Lankshear has been viewed as an exciting prospect for some time. Spurs snapped him up from the Sheffield United academy at a youth age and he really announced himself on the senior stage on loan at Oxford United, where he scored 12 goals and provided three assists in 47 games across the 2025/26 season.

Despite such success and encouragement over his future, there were eyebrows raised when Middlesbrough struck a deal worth £20m with add-ons to sign him in the summer. However, Lankshear has already justified the hefty fee.

Lankshear has scored four Championship braces on his way to the top of the goalscoring charts. He was on a run of seven goals in five games before injury hit and ruled him out of Boro’s last two matches.

The 21-year-old’s lethal finishing and trajectory have drawn early comparisons to England captain and Ballon d’Or winner Harry Kane. Lankshear is currently averaging a goal every 78 minutes for Middlesbrough, so it is little surprise to see the England national team and Premier League club Newcastle United tracking his performances.

They will not be the only ones with eyes on Lankshear, who is viewed as one of the country’s most promising young attackers.

Lankshear only moved in the summer and is under contract until 2031. It means Boro aren’t under pressure to cash in.

However, to keep him long term, if Middlesbrough are to have a chance of fending off top interest, they have to ensure their trajectory matches that of Lankshear. The only way to do so is by winning promotion to the Premier League this season.

If not, there will be no shortage of clubs ready to spend big on bringing him in. Newcastle United are the only concretely reported admirer at present, but he is sure to have alerted more sides.

Middlesbrough sit 2nd in the Championship table, despite failing to win either of their last two games.
