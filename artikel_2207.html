Barry Bannan reacts as Davis Keillor-Dunn drops Sheffield Wednesday message

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Davis Keillor-Dunn's opening goal for Sheffield Wednesday last Sunday against Blackpool was a big moment for both the Owls and the 29-year-old.

It marked the unofficial first goal of the Dave Challinor era. The 51-year-old may not have been in the dugout, but he was in attendance at Hillsborough, having agreed on Saturday evening to become the new Wednesday boss.

The goal was also the culmination of some long periods of first-half pressure and, without the red card to Sil Swinkels five minutes out from half-time, the Owls would likely have gone on to score a few more and return to winning ways after picking up just one point from their previous three.

In the end, that didn't happen. Blackpool got level late on through ex-Wednesday loanee Jerry Yates, and the points were shared. That didn't detract from the milestone Keillor-Dunn surpassed with his opening goal, though.

Keillor-Dunn's effort to open the scoring was his third league goal of the campaign, and the 100th league goal of his career.

The 29-year-old has netted at least 10 league goals in each of his last six seasons, but it has been since he joined Mansfield Town in January 2023 that he has begun to really become an established marksman, first in League Two and now at League One level.

He's now netted 39 times in just 97 third-tier outings, the majority of which came at Barnsley before his switch to Wrexham back in January. A real struggle to get into the side at the Racecourse Ground meant he was stuck on 97 career league goals for quite some time.

But Keillor-Dunn has now surpassed the century, netting a brace against AFC Wimbledon and then the opening goal on Sunday. He took to Instagram to react to hitting that milestone, stating, "Proud to have scored my 100th league goal, and what a place to do it at Hillsborough. Hungrier than ever for more."

Numerous ex- and current teammates reacted to the post, giving supporting comments. Wednesday midfielder Barry Bannan was the pick of the bunch, stating, "Go on the boy 👏."

The likes of Liam Palmer, Harry Gray and Jamal Lowe all joined Bannan in praising Keillor-Dunn for crossing that 100-goal milestone in Wednesday colours. Evidently, they all have the same opinion as those in the stands: they want the 29-year-old to stay all season.

Keillor-Dunn's loan spell from Wrexham runs out in January, meaning that a decision will have to be made about his future come the turning of the calendar.

With the Red Dragons set to be without Sam Smith for nine months due to a knee injury, Phil Parkinson may opt to play Keillor-Dunn back at the Racecourse once his spell ends. Wednesday will hope that's not the case.

The versatile attacker practically wasted six months of his career after making the mid-season switch to Wrexham in January. After netting 16 times in all competitions for Barnsley during the first half of the campaign, he only played 89 minutes from January to May.

Now, he's clearly loving his football again, and the phrasing in his Instagram caption, "what a place to do it," shows that he feels happy at this historic club. Wednesday fans want him to see out the season, so do his teammates, and by the looks of things, so does he.

Challinor was in the stands observing and making notes during Wednesday's 1-1 draw with Blackpool, and you'd expect he had some good things to write about after Keillor-Dunn put the Owls ahead.

The ex-Stockport County boss knows exactly what the 29-year-old brings to the table, having battled against him both while he was at Mansfield in League Two and then Barnsley in League One.

Keillor-Dunn has scored four times in seven appearances against Challinor's Stockport outfit, so the new Wednesday boss must be pretty happy to have him on his side, at least for the time being.

The fact that the former Barnsley man has played for Wrexham already this season makes January's decision a lot more straightforward. He won't be able to play for a third side, so it'll be either a return to the Racecourse or a full season at Hillsborough.

If it's the latter, you get the feeling that he'll be set for a starring role in Challinor's side as the Owls strive to get back into the Championship at the first time of asking.
