Bridge House / Studioshaw

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Text description provided by the architects. Studioshaw announces the completion of Bridge House, an eight-story, 6,250 sqm mixed-use development at the heart of Hackney Wick. Delivering 40 apartments alongside dual-level commercial and community spaces, and set beside a new public square, the project marks the practice's largest scheme to date. Its completion coincides with Studioshaw's tenth anniversary and the delivery of its 100th project.
