Talking Point: What Are You Playing This Weekend? (November 14-15)

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

It's the penultimate weekend to get stuck into the games you'd like to finish before November 19th rolls up.

It's WAYP time, people! You know the score by now, so let's hear what you're playing this weekend! This is your opportunity to have a chat with one another about what's good, what's worth playing, why you're vibing, or maybe, not vibing with certain games. It's an open floor, PX'ers.

Alrighty then, let's get into it. Here's what we're playing on Xbox this weekend:

Recently, I haven't been trying to complete anything, grind online, or chase a Mythic cheevo. I'm just vibing with my Xbox. Honestly, I think GTA 6 has me in a bit of a chokehold, keeping me from starting anything I might not finish. With that in mind, Hozy has been the perfect lil' game to sit down and chill with on a rainy day.

Lots of games are getting little bits of my attention, including Silver Pines, TCG Card Shop Simulator, and Gears of War: E-Day, to name a few. But, my main game at the moment is It Takes Two. I've absolutely loved playing it with my wife. The best bit is how into it she is, that and the bonded trauma it inflicts!

Something I'd like to get back into is Marathon. That new Symbiosis trailer looked awesome, and I'm hoping to lock-in and get that Mythic. I think all I need is to grind to level 100 and it'll be mine. Shouldn't be too much of a time commitment, right?

But I'll be real. I'm playing a lot of Zelda: Ocarina of Time right now. It's a perfect remake of a childhood favourite, and it's feeding my inner kid. He's a happy boy.

GTA 6 is right around the corner, and you better believe it's going to take up the rest of the year in terms of dominating my free time. That means it's not really wise to get stuck into anything too meaty right now, although I'm keen to check out Shrek 2 Remastered and see whether Aspyr's new port does the OG Xbox title justice or not.

I might even play a bit of GTA: Chinatown Wars on my 3DS. I finally started playing that game about a year ago, and even though it's rough around the edges by today's standards, I'm enjoying how unique it is compared to other entries.
