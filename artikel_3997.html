Base PS5's QSSR Enhancer Coming to 6 More Games, in Spider-Man 2 Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Control Resonant, Arc Raiders, and more upgraded

Sony has announced it is expanding support for its QSSR graphics enhancer on base PS5 to six more PS5 games in the coming weeks.

Through a free update, the feature is live right now in Marvel's Spider-Man 2. In addition, the following titles will receive support for QSSR soon:

More games will receive QSSR support in the future too, as Sony continues to work with developers in implementing the visual enhancement technology. "Developers are just getting started," Sony states. with the PS5 Pro has had PSSR since launch, which was recently upgraded via new version, the QSSR tech is designed for the base PS5 system.

Ghost of Yotei and Marvel's Wolverine also support the feature.

"It's great to be improving the PS5 this far into the generation"

QSSR support in Marvel's Spider-Man 2 is live now, and head of technology at Insomniac Games, Mike Fitzgerald, told the PlayStation Blog you should spot improved image stability and sharpness across both the Fidelity and Performance modes in the game.

Remedy expects to have QSSR support added to Control Resonant in December, at which point the PS5 title will have a "more stable and detailed image". Here's a comparison video:

Embark Studios is supporting QSSR across both its multiplayer games, The Finals and Arc Raiders. Once the PS5 patch is applied, expect "increased sharpness, better environmental clarity and overall visual improvements". Here is another comparison video for Arc Raiders specifically:

When No Man's Sky is updated with QSSR support, it'll "render the universe in sharper, cleaner and more beautiful detail, while delivering the best possible framerate". The patch should become available in the next few weeks.
