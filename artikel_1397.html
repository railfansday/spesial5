Soule shines: 10 goal contributions in 17 matches

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It wasn’t Hercules, but Matias Soulé who lit up the Parc des Princes.

The Argentine talent took just five minutes to become the star of yet another great European night: a decisive assist for Hermoso after dancing to the ball, skipping past opponents.

Gian Piero Gasperini expressed his satisfaction at seeing a player continually improving: “Besides being strong, he’s very positive, he’s receptive, and he wants to grow. He’s 23, and he’s in a period where he can grow, as he is doing. He’s more complete than before; today he’s played on the left, whereas until last year he was stuck on that pitch. He’s reaching extraordinary technical and physical maturity. I’m happy for him; in these last two months, he’s been doing something very positive.”

Matias Soulé shows no signs of slowing down his unstoppable growth. The Argentine winger has already been involved in 10 goals this season, one every 114 minutes: four goals and six assists in 17 appearances.

The Argentine talent’s performances this year are rising in proportion to the quality of his opponents: he has rarely been without a hand in big matches.

In fact, Soulé scored one goal and provided three assists in the Champions League matches against Real Madrid, Manchester United, and PSG.

In the league, he was instrumental with a goal that allowed Roma to a last-gasp 2-1 victory against Atalanta, adding to his assist for Hermoso that temporarily tied the game 1-1. On October 24, he lit up the Maradona stadium with a total performance from start to finish, further enhanced by the goal that sparked the comeback to secure a 3-3 draw at Napoli.
