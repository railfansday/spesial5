Enseo Introduces Managed Services Portal, Giving Hoteliers Business Intelligence to Improve Technology Performance and Guest Satisfaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

MIAMI, Nov. 5, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Enseo, the leading provider of personalized digital guest experience technology for hotels, launched its new Managed Services portal at The Hospitality Show. The business intelligence platform brings together real-time technology monitoring, operational analytics and guest engagement data, giving hoteliers a comprehensive view of performance across individual properties and entire portfolios.

Built around the needs of hotel operators, Managed Services provides the visibility and business intelligence to identify potential issues earlier, make more informed operational decisions and uncover opportunities to improve the guest experience and financial performance.

"Hotel technology touches nearly every aspect of the guest experience, but operators often have to rely on multiple, disconnected systems to understand how it's performing," said Kevin Sweeney, Enseo's Chief Operating Officer. "Managed Services brings technology health, service activity and guest engagement together in one place. Operators stop chasing data and start making the decisions that move the property forward."

Managed Services builds on Enseo's proprietary SITEÂ® product, which continuously monitors technology performance through a 3-D model of each hotel. The new portal extends these capabilities with real-time analytics at the property and portfolio levels, enabling hoteliers to evaluate current performance, identify recurring issues and use historical data to guide future decisions.

Through a single, centralized portal, hoteliers can monitor in-room entertainment and WiFi performance, track support activity, access billing information and analyze trends in guest engagement and revenue. This comprehensive view helps operators better understand what's happening across their properties and where attention may be needed.

"Hotels generate an enormous amount of data through the technology their guests use every day, but that information is only valuable if operators can put it to work," said Brian Gurley, Enseo's Chief Executive Officer. "Managed Services gives our customers greater visibility into what's happening across their properties, from technology performance to guest engagement and revenue. By turning that information into actionable business intelligence, we're helping hoteliers make smarter decisions that benefit both their guests and their bottom line."

For more information, visit www.enseo.com.

Enseo provides the technology for modern hospitality, helping hotels deliver personalized digital guest experiences while simplifying operations behind the scenes.
