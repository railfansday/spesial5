loanDepot’s Q3 loss grows while it chases 2027 break-even

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

loanDepot reported a $29 million net loss in Q3 2026, larger than the prior quarter, as high mortgage rates weighed on originations. The lender is pivoting toward higher-margin products like home equity loans and purchase mortgages, which helped lift gain-on-sale margins even as volume fell sequentially.   AI Summary

loanDepot widened its net loss in the third quarter of 2026 as it sold mortgage servicing rights (MSRs), repurchased debt and leaned into higher-margin products.

The California-based nonbank lender reported a $29 million net loss in Q3, compared with a $7 million loss in the prior quarter, according to its earnings release Monday. Founder and CEO Anthony Hsieh told analysts that the quarter was challenged by unexpectedly higher interest rates but said the company remains focused on gaining market share despite depressed volumes.

“Getting over the seasonality of Q4 and Q1, and of course not knowing where the market will end up next year, but if it stays at this level, we are very confident that we’ll get to a break even of profitability by first half or mid next year,” Hsieh said.

loanDepot originated $7.05 billion in mortgages in the third quarter, down 12% from the prior quarter but up 7.8% year over year. Purchase loans made up more than 61% of total volume.

On a unit basis, originations rose 7% quarter over quarter, helped by the company’s expansion into home equity products. Management said loanDepot estimates it ranked among the top three nonbank home equity lenders in the third quarter based on volume, a shift that helped push gain-on-sale margin to 399 basis points, up 54 basis points from the second quarter.

“Since my return last year, we increased origination volume by 8%, grew unit volume by 33%, increased purchase volume by 10%, improved return on marketing by 14%, and reduced our cost to produce by 16%,” Hsieh said. “These results reflect the success of our ongoing efforts to diversify revenue sources, increase operating efficiency, and improve the scalability of our platform.”

The company now expects its productivity and efficiency initiatives to deliver $25 million in annualized cost savings through the remainder of 2026, more than double the previous estimate of $12 million.

loanDepot continued to shrink its balance sheet and reduce debt through MSR sales in the quarter. The company closed the sale of $10 billion in agency MSRs and agreed to sell an additional $9 billion in government MSRs, a transaction expected to close in October.

After these moves, loanDepot’s servicing portfolio stood at $115.7 billion at the end of the quarter. Proceeds were used to repurchase $87 million of senior notes at an average price of 88% of par.

Chief Financial Officer David Hayes said the combination of MSR sales and debt repurchases reduced net debt obligations by about $114 million, strengthened liquidity and improved financial flexibility.

Even with those steps, leverage remains elevated. Debt obligations, net to total equity, were 6.5x at the end of the quarter, up from 4.8x a year earlier. Hayes said the company is working “actively” to address nearing debt maturities and is pursuing financing options to refinance or extend with financing counterparties ahead of 2027 bond maturities. He did not provide more details during the calls with analysts.

Looking to the fourth quarter of 2026, loanDepot guided to origination volume between $4.5 billion and $6.5 billion, implying potentially lower production than Q3. However, it expects gain-on-sale margin between 315 basis points and 355 basis points.

Management framed the outlook as a continuation of the pivot toward more profitable products, a larger purchase footprint and a leaner cost base in the face of persistent rate pressure.

“While elevated interest rates remain a headwind for mortgage demand, we believe the actions we have taken to diversify our product offerings, expand our purchase market presence, improve efficiency, strengthen margins, and optimize our balance sheet have positioned loanDepot to emerge from this cycle as a stronger and more competitive company,” Hsieh said in a statement. “We remain focused on growing market share, improving profitability, and creating long-term value for our shareholders.”
