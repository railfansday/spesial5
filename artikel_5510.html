Kraken's parent Payward posted earnings down 67% from a year earlier

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Total platform margin fell 12% to $487 million, while funded accounts rose 37% to 7.1 million from a year earlier

Payward, which owns the crypto exchange Kraken, reported that its adjusted earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization fell 67% in the third quarter, according to a shareholder letter reviewed by Bloomberg. Adjusted EBITDA was $60 million.

Total platform margin, which the company said it now uses in place of adjusted revenues, dropped 12% year over year to $487 million. Total platform transaction volume shrank by 30%, landing at $353 billion. Funded accounts, meaning ones that hold any positive balance, grew 37% to reach 7.1 million.

Results improved from the second quarter. Adjusted EBITDA rose 163% from that period, and total platform margin climbed 12%.

The decline came amid a downturn in cryptocurrency prices, according to Bloomberg. Bitcoin climbed roughly 40% from the middle of August to the start of October. Its price then retreated as fears about inflation and concern over how U.S. midterm elections might affect the industry weighed on the market. Many exchanges depend on trading fees for the largest share of their revenue, and activity tends to slow when prices slide, Bloomberg said.

Payward is pushing into tokenized stocks and similar businesses as a way to broaden its revenue base, according to Bloomberg. The company has said it intends to offer onchain perpetual futures to its clients in the U.S., pending sign-off from regulators. Payward agreed in October to purchase Saldo Bank, and Kraken intends to enter the UAE once Dubai's regulators grant it complete authorization.

Payward plans to enter prediction markets and issue a token of its own, according to Bloomberg. The shareholder letter did not give timing for either, Bloomberg said.

Nasdaq $NDAQ's venture capital arm agreed in September to invest $100 million in Payward to build infrastructure for tokenized equities. The investment values Payward at $21 billion. Nasdaq and Payward expect to launch Nasdaq Equity Tokens in the second quarter of 2027, and Kraken's platform will serve as a distribution channel for tokenized versions of Nasdaq-listed stocks.

Payward's initial public offering may not take place before next year, according to Bloomberg.
