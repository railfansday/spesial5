What Retirement Buyers Want From Their Next Home, According to PulteGroup

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Warm weather remains a major draw for retirement-age home buyers considering a move, but a new PulteGroup survey suggests the decision is becoming about more than just climate.

The survey of 1,330 prospective home buyers at or nearing retirement age found differences between what working and already-retired respondents prioritize when considering an out-of-state move. Among those still working, 62% selected weather and climate as one of their top two reasons to relocate, while 47% cited affordability and cost of living, and 35% selected proximity to family or friends.

For respondents who were already retired, family connections carried more weight. Forty-five percent selected proximity to family or friends, tying weather and climate as their leading considerations for moving out of state.

“For many buyers, retirement isn’t just about where they want to live, but how they want to live,” said Sean Strickler, president of PulteGroup’s West Florida division and brand champion for Explore by Del Webb. “Warm weather will always have its appeal, but buyers are also thinking about how their next home will support the lifestyle they envision, whether that’s spending more time with family, building friendships, or enjoying new experiences.”

When respondents were asked what they prioritized in their next home, 59% selected a low-maintenance lifestyle, making it the most commonly cited consideration. Affordability and financial peace of mind followed at 48%, while 39% selected luxurious, resort-style amenities.

The findings show that builders targeting active-adult and retirement buyers may need to balance amenity-rich communities with homes that reduce the day-to-day responsibilities associated with homeownership.

Community programming also ranked prominently. When asked what matters most in selecting their next community, 62% chose built-in activities, social opportunities, and a sense of community. Safety and peace of mind followed at 42%, while 40% selected proximity to quality health care.

Those priorities align with expectations for retirement itself. Nearly half of respondents, 48%, said they were looking forward to more travel and new experiences, while the same share cited a stronger social life and sense of community. And 96% expect their next home to be where they spend their retirement years.

Family proximity extended beyond respondents already in retirement. Among all surveyed prospective buyers who have children, 74% said proximity to children or grandchildren was either the primary reason or an important factor in where they would purchase their next home.

The Southeast continues to stand out among respondents contemplating an out-of-state move. Florida was the leading destination, selected by 25%, followed by South Carolina at 19%, and North Carolina at 17%. Together, the three states accounted for 61% of potential destinations selected by respondents considering relocation.

The preferences overlap with PulteGroup’s expansion of its active-adult and lifestyle offerings in the region. The company has more than 80 active Del Webb and DiVosta communities in Florida and recently launched a Panhandle division. It is also developing two Tampa-area communities under its Explore by Del Webb brand.

In the Carolinas, PulteGroup has additional projects underway, including the 670-acre Carolina Ridge near Charlotte and the 500-acre Del Webb Point Hope near Charleston.
