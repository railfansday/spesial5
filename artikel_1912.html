Premier League panel confirm ruling on Jayden Bogle incident in Leeds United’s win over Tottenham Hotspur

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The decision not to send Jayden Bogle off in Leeds United’s win over Tottenham Hotspur has been supported by a Premier League panel.

Leeds defeated Spurs 1-0 at Elland Road on November 7 and the losing side felt the hosts should have been reduced to 10 men.

Marauding wing-back Bogle caught the back of the ankle of winger Mathys Tel, which forced the wideman out of action.

VAR (video assistant referee) did not call referee John Brooks over for another look and Bogle was not carded for the challenge at all.

The incident sparked plenty of controversy but Brooks’ call has been backed by the Premier League’s key match incidents panel.

The panel said: “This was a split decision with the majority supporting the referee's on field call of no red card. The panel considered this to be a clear foul but the majority felt Bogle was looking to challenge with his right leg and his left leg came down on the back of Tel's ankle without the speed, force or intensity to be serious foul play.

“They felt that the contact made was very clearly reckless, but below the threshold for a red card. Two panellists felt that the challenge was sufficiently dangerous to merit a red card.

“The non-VAR intervention was also supported, 3:2, on the basis that the majority considered the challenge to be reckless, therefore not a clear and obvious error.”

Spurs signalled their intention to appeal the decision and former Premier League referee Graham Barber felt Bogle should have been sent for an early bath.

He said: “I thought it was a very, very poor challenge, it was reckless at best but you could describe it as dangerous and endangering the safety of the opponent, and did it endanger the safety of the opponent? Well, the answer is probably yes, because he had to leave the field of play about five minutes later.”
