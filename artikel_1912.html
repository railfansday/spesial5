Mikel Arteta discusses new Arsenal contract for Martin Odegaard

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Mikel Arteta wants Arsenal to agree a new contract with his “instrumental” captain Martin Odegaard.

The Gunners have made a push to tie down the core of their Premier League-winning squad to long-term deals.

Declan Rice, David Raya and Jurrien Timber are all understood to have agreed terms in recent weeks, while Piero Hincapie has also been rewarded with an improved contract.

They follows a group including Bukayo Saka and William Saliba in committing their futures to the club, along with Arteta himself.

Odegaard is one of Arteta’s most trusted players and he is close to entering the final 18 months of his contract, which runs through to 2028.

His role and my opinion on how important he is to continue to do what we’re doing is critical

The Norwegian revealed over the international break that he has not started talks with the club over a new deal but Arteta has made it clear that he wants those to take place.

“Martin is an instrumental player for us, for me,” Arteta said.

“I think what he’s done for the club and for the team in the last years since he joined has just been [on] an uplifted trajectory with some moments of incredible consistency and magic, and some others that are a bit more difficult.

“But I think his role and my opinion on how important he is to continue to do what we’re doing is critical.”

Asked if that meant he wanted Odegaard to stay at Arsenal beyond the duration of his current contract, Arteta added: “Yes, definitely yes.”

However, Arteta was reluctant to be drawn into why there had been no talks with Odegaard up to this point, despite the progress made with several of his team-mates.

“You know that I never talk about these things,” Arteta said.

“I can tell you the opinion I have and I think that’s a very general and unanimous feeling that we have, everyone at the club.”

Odegaard has been back close to his consistent best this season, with three goals in the first ten Premier League matches.

He scored only once in the league last season, during a campaign in which he was hampered by shoulder and knee injuries, and Arteta hailed Odegaard for how he responded to those challenges.

“Sometimes injuries can really stop the momentum of a player,” Arteta said.

“That affects everything. It affects performances, it affects the confidence, it can affect the future of the player.

“But Martin has always reacted incredibly well to any kind of situation like this because he loves the game so much, because he loves this club so much.

“He does absolutely everything that he can to get back on track to his very best. You can see that he’s enjoying his game so much now.”
