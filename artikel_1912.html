Naivety cost us two points at Scunthorpe: Meeney’s verdict after Worthing’s late heartache

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Rebels looked on course for another notable away National League win when they led 1-0 at Glanford Park courtesy of Jordan Young’s fine finish in the first half.

But the Iron levelled three minutes into time added on, leaving bosses frustrated, with Adam Hinshelwood shown a red card at full-time.

Worthing were denied the chance of a quick return to winning form when Tuesday night’s Sussex Transport Senior Cup tie at Wick was rained off. Now the seventh-placed Reds tackle another double-game week in the league – at home to Harrogate on Saturday and away to Woking on Tuesday.

Assistant boss Jon Meeney told club media after Saturday’s draw: “It was a really good first half away from home – the lads carried out everything we desired.

"But we showed our naivety in the second half and made it a lot harder than it needed to be, hence why we conceded a late goal.

“First half we were present in duels… dealt with their direct balls and showed a real intensity against the ball.

"We passed the ball and got up the pitch to suffocate the opposition at times.

"But we stopped playing and stopped finding solutions in the second half. We lost a bit of bravery and went longer and the game opened up.

"We didn’t arrive on enough second balls, we were disconnected, which gave them space – and that’s the naivety of the group, and you’re going to get that with a young group.

"We’ve got to learn and we’ve got to be better at seeing out games of football in that manner.”

Meeney praised scorer Jordan Young, saying: “We know he can be a game-changer.

"He creates but what we like about Youngy is we know he has that quality and it’s what he does off the ball.”

He said the whole squad needed to learn the off-the-ball side of the game from the likes of Young. Meeney said there was a lot to like about the team’s overall display: “The lads have shown an honest performance, they’ve shown grit and determination, honesty to defend the box, stopping crosses, marking in the box.”

The assistant boss described Worthing’s travelling support as ‘outstanding’ and added: “They were with the boys from minute one. It was a fantastic showing after the game applauding the boys off, and vice-versa.

"But we’re just really disappointed it was a draw – it should have been a win."
