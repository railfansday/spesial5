Chelsea 'unhappy' with Levi Colwill as Leeds expose 'huge weakness'

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Chelsea have grown concerned about the performances of Levi Colwill as alarm bells are ringing at Stamford Bridge.

The 23-year-old has struggled during a nightmarish run of form under Xabi Alonso, which has seen the Blues win just three of their last nine games across all competitions.

Defensive issues have been a problem for Chelsea throughout the season so far, and their centre-back's displays have now exposed a major weakness in their side.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Chelsea's inability to deal with physical strikers has ruled out any chance of success this season.

First, it was Sunderland's Brian Brobbey who caused constant problems for the Blues during their 1-0 win, before Dominic Calvert-Lewin posed a constant threat for Leeds.

Colwill's lack of physical prowess has done little to ease Chelsea's concerns, and there are now growing doubts about whether he is at the level required at Stamford Bridge.

Former Tottenham scout Bryan King told Football Insider back in September that defensive issues are becoming a huge problem for Chelsea that Alonso must address.

However, they have now kept just three clean sheets in 14 games this season, and recent results have sparked new concerns about the displays in their back line.

Colwill has been singled out for criticism after being bulled in back-to-back games, but he is not the only concern at Stamford Bridge, as something has to change.

"Chelsea are so weak defensively and that is now becoming clear every single week," Brown told Football Insider.

"They conceded four goals at home against a Leeds side who have struggled to score much at all this season, and that has got to be a concern for everybody involved.

"But when you look at the players individually, they have got to be unhappy with what they've seen from Levi Colwill because he has been nowhere near good enough.

"It's a huge weakness when you have centre-backs who can't deal with physical strikers, like we've seen with Brobbey and Calvert-Lewin now.

"It sets up a blueprint, if you want to beat Chelsea, use a big, physical centre-forward and you'll cause them problems because their defenders cannot cope.

"There's no way Chelsea are going to be able to compete for any major silverware if they have such clear issues at the back.

"I've never been massively convinced by Colwill, to be honest. He's got some good assets, but he's slow, he can't compete physically, and now that's becoming a problem."
