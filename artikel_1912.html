Why Anthony Elanga can reshape Matthias Jaissle's Newcastle future, new data is telling

Anthony Elanga has not had many minutes at Newcastle United this season.

That has in large part been due to the Swedish international picking up an injury, handing Matthias Jaissle quite the blow.

Without him, results for the Geordie side have been poor, with the German manager now under immense pressure.

Jaissle's time at Newcastle could end before the season ends, with just three wins out of the opening 10 games.

The Magpies will want to qualify for Europe, and right now they are far off that pace.

But the return of Elanga could begin pushing them back in that right direction.

Elanga has only started four games in the Premier League this season, with just 329 minutes of the 900 available.

The winger did miss four games through injury, and since returning, he has only started one of three.

To start the season, he was a vital member of Jaissle's plans, scoring in the opening two games.

Now, Elanga looks to be back to full fitness, with his latest showing for Sweden seeing him produce the goods.

As per FotMob, Elanga was one of the biggest dangermen for his national side against Bosnia and Herzegovina.

While he was taken off after just an hour, by that point, he had the most touches in the opposition box (seven), and managed to pick up an assist too.

Additionally, he was constantly looking to beat the man in front of him, attempting three dribbles and completing one of those.

No other Swedish player attempted more by the time he had been subbed out.

That bravery to get into the box and beat a player is exactly the profile Jaissle needs for vertical-style football.

So, once the international break ends, he must be a regular once again for Newcastle.

With Jaissle under pressure, the next four games could make or break his job at St James' Park.

The fixture run looks incredibly difficult with Arsenal, Brighton, Man United and Sunderland all to come.

If they end that string of games with few points, the German will be looking at the axe.

Especially if they cannot get the bragging rights in the Tynewear Derby.

Elanga can help them get points over the line, and his manager will need him to help save his job.
