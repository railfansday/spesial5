Is Sheffield Wednesday v Plymouth Argyle on TV? Channel and streaming details for League One clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Sheffield Wednesday are set to host Plymouth Argyle in Dave Challinor’s first league game as Owls boss.

Since Challinor picked up the baton from caretaker Matt Hamshaw, he has overseen FA Cup and EFL Trophy losses to Oldham Athletic and Accrington Stanley.

This weekend will mark a return to the club’s bread and butter, with Plymouth due up on the blue side of the Steel City.

Owls legend Chris Waddle has warned Challinor must “get to grips” with his players in order to reingite Wednesday’s promotion push.

He said: “He needs to get to grips with the players in his squad and their best roles on the grass and he needs to do it very, very quickly. The trouble with Sheffield Wednesday is that he's got players who don't actually play in their proper positions, apart from the back four.

“In midfield, do you play Barry Bannan as a six, an eight, or a 10? You have other players who just seem to run around. He's got the nucleus of a squad if he can get the balance right. That's what's letting Sheffield Wednesday down - the shape of the team.”

Here are the key TV and streaming details ahead of Wednesday’s clash with Plymouth.

Kick-off at Hillsborough is scheduled for 3pm tomorrow (Saturday, November 14).

Yes - the match will be shown live on TV. It has not been given top billing on any of the main Sky Sports channels but can be viewed as an alternative broadcast via the Sky Sports+ service. Subscriptions can be purchased online.

The 3pm blackout can only be enforced when half of the matches across a country’s top two tiers are played at the same time and there are none scheduled to take place.

Supporters can stream the action via the official Sky Sports app or on NOW TV with a valid subscription.
