The latest on Apple Wallet ID support in three long-promised states

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Support for storing your driver’s license in Apple Wallet has gradually been expanding across the United States. Today, we have a status update on when the feature will go live in Connecticut, which Apple has touted as a planned partner since September 2021.

Last week, Connecticut released its own mobile ID app for iPhone on the App Store, but without Apple Wallet support. As spotted by MacRumors, however, the app’s developer says that Apple Wallet support is “coming soon.”

While the state and developer haven’t shared any more specific information recently, a press release in May from Connecticut Governor Ned Lamont said the goal is to expand digital wallet ID support to Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, and Samsung Wallet by August 2027.

Connecticut residents have been waiting a long time for the state to launch Apple Wallet ID support. This is the first notable update we’ve had in a while.
