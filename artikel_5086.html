EVERY HOLIDAY SEASON for the past decade, REI has shut its doors and ceased online order processing on Black Friday. Instead of holding a Black Friday sale, the outdoor retailer encourages customers (and its employees) to #OptOutside by getting in nature and on an adventure. This Black Friday marks 11 years of that. But, the good thing for you and your holiday shopping is that REI still holds a Holiday Sale, which just began on November 13 and runs through November 23.

I combed through and found top-tested picks, some year-after-year stalwarts, and some rare savings you should scoop up. Almost everyone who loves the outdoors knows that REI is ground zero for gearing up for hiking, biking, kayaking, climbing, fishing—pretty much any type of adventure, really. REI Outlet is the retailer's dedicated discount section, a roundup of last season's gear and its famous flash sales. It's the single best place to shop if you're looking for the absolute best deals on the world's best outdoor brands. You'll find great deals year-round, but the deepest discounts are almost always during REI's holiday sale events. Don't forget to check back early and often as REI Outlet's inventory changes frequently. Sale prices can drop suddenly and things sell out fast!

REI offers many of its best perks and sale event access to REI Co-op members. Even if you're not a frequent REI shopper, it's worth the one-time $30 fee to join. For the Holiday Sale, REI members get a 20% off one eligible full-priced item and an extra 20% off one eligible Outlet item with the code HOLIDAY2026. That's not all though.

REI members also receive a 10% dividend on most in-store and online purchases, as well as receiving discounts on gear rentals and during REI's best annual sale events (like the holiday sale). That $30 can easily pay for itself in just one visit!
