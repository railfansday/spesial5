Senate Dem challenging Sen Chuck Schumer for leadership post backs Hakeem Jeffries for House speaker

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Sen. Chris Van Hollen, who is running against Sen. Chuck Schumer for the Senate Democratic leadership slot, supports House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries for House speaker. (Credit: Youtube/ POLITICO)

Sen. Chris Van Hollen, D-Md., who is currently challenging Sen. Chuck Schumer, D-N.Y., for the Senate Majority Leader role, has backed current House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries, D-N.Y., in his bid for the speaker's gavel.

Asked by Politico's Will Steakin whether he supports Jeffries for the post, Van Hollen said that he does.

The senator noted that Jeffries has "been the minority leader, but he's not served as speaker before," adding that he thinks the congressman "should be given an opportunity to bring the caucus, uh, together in this moment."

Van Hollen's office did not immediately respond to Fox News Digital's comment request on Monday.

Schumer, the current Senate Minority Leader, is aiming to retain his party's leadership position next year when the Democrats will be in the majority. Last week, Van Hollen threw his hat into the ring to challenge Schumer for the post.

Schumer, who has been a senator since 1999, has been either the Senate minority or majority leader since 2017, which is the entire time that Van Hollen, who took office in 2017, has been serving in the Senate.
