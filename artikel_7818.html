Yarbo’s Black Friday pricing drops up to $2,500 off its robotic snow blowers through Nov. 30, right as winter settles in. The Yarbo Y40 Robot Snow Blower for $4,899 (was $6,199) is an autonomous electric snow blower that plans its route, clears driveways, walkways, and sidewalks around the clock, and heads back to its dock to recharge when the battery drops to about 20%.

For long driveways, the Yarbo Y40P Robot Snow Blower for $6,199 (was $7,799) clears up to 7,000 square feet of 1-inch snow per charge, compared with 6,000 for the Y40, and maps zones faster. Homeowners who want one smart outdoor robot for every season can start with the Yarbo Y40 Snow Blower and Mower Bundle for $6,499 (was $8,199), which swaps to mowing when the snow melts.

This autonomous snow blower runs a 2-stage, 24-inch-wide module that takes in up to 12 inches of snow and throws it up to 40 feet. At about 20% battery, it docks, recharges, and picks up where it left off.

The robot snow blower for long driveways uses the same snow module as the Y40 but clears up to 7,000 square feet per charge. Brushless hub motors give it smoother handling on ice and uneven ground, and it maps zones faster.

This robot snow blower and mower combo clears snow all winter, then switches to the Lawn Mower Pro module from spring through fall. One robot handles both jobs, with one dock and one app.

For anyone who’s cleared a long driveway by flashlight before work, Yarbo makes the case as a smarter investment in winter convenience. It clears during snowfall, keeping accumulation below its 12-inch intake instead of fighting a foot of packed snow afterward. Off-season, the same robot mows, blows leaves, and trims edges with add-on modules, so the bundles earn their keep year-round.

Both the Y40 and Y40P use the same Snow Blower Module Gen 2, so they clear the same snow in the same conditions.

The Y40 is the lower-cost way into a Yarbo, with the same clearing width, depth, and throw distance as the Y40P.

The Y40P adds brushless hub motors, faster mapping, and up to 7,000 square feet per charge, and those upgrades carry over to every module.

Yarbo also sells the Y40 and Y40P robot snow blowers on Amazon.
