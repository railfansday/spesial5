Sources: Celtic's chances of signing Jadon Sancho revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Celtic are holding back on pursuing a move for Jadon Sancho despite his ongoing availability as a free agent, sources have told Football Insider.

The 26-year-old left Man United upon the expiry of his contract in June and has yet to find a new club, despite having held talks with a number of potential suitors.

Martin O'Neill's side have become the latest to be linked with a move for the winger as they aim to bolster their side ahead of the January transfer window opening.

However, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Celtic are highly unlikely to make a move for Sancho at any stage in the near future.

Part of the reason the former England international has failed to find a new club is as a result of his lofty wage demands, having earned around £250,000 per week at Man United.

Celtic would not be willing to match Sancho's demands, while there are also concerns about his fitness levels after months out of the game, which could see them look elsewhere.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider during the summer that Aston Villa pulled out of a move to sign Sancho due to his wage demands.

The same issue saw the winger miss out on a move to Chelsea following his loan spell at Stamford Bridge in the 2024-25 season, despite the Blues agreeing terms with Man United.

Celtic are not likely to be willing to offer more than their Premier League counterparts, let alone to match his lofty demands, and therefore are prepared to look elsewhere.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Again, I would be surprised if Celtic were to make a move for Jadon Sancho any time soon.

"He's a free agent so I'm sure there will be number of clubs who will be looking at Jadon Sancho, because on his day we know what a top player he can be.

"I think for Celtic, again, he was on very sizable wages at Manchester United as well, so maybe his demands would make any move to Celtic unlikely.

"I don't think Celtic probably are making a new winger out of top priority for them right now. I would say probably Martin O’Neill would prefer to strengthen his squad elsewhere."

Sancho has been a free agent since leaving Man United in the summer, and remains so despite interest from clubs across world football in the last few months.

Most recently, the winger held talks with La Liga outfit Getafe, but was unable to reach an agreement, while clubs in Turkey, the USA and the Premier League have been keen.

However, Brown told Football Insider that Sancho's most likely destination will be a club in the Middle East, whether that's in the Saudi Pro League or elsewhere.

A number of clubs in that part of the world have shown an interest, and would be the most likely candidates to meet his lofty wage demands.
