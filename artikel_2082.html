The confirmed Newcastle United team v Arsenal has now been announced.

Saturday bringing the fourteenth match of the season for NUFC.

A record so far of five wins, three draws and five defeats.

Exactly nine weeks ago Newcastle beat Hull City 2-1. Since then there have been only two more home matches and they both ended in defeat, against Aston Villa and Everton.

Matthias Jaissle and his players looking to build some confidence and momentum but up against a ruthlessly efficient Arsenal team that have won nine of ten Premier League matches so far this season.

Hornicek, Dedic Thiaw, Burn, Hall, Miley, Steur, Barnes, Willock, Murphy, Osula

So it is two changes from the team that started two weeks ago against Fulham.
