Photos: Milan players react to Inter win on social media – “Milan is red and Milan is my home”

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

AC Milan enjoyed perhaps their biggest high of the season on Saturday night as they beat city rivals Inter 3-1 under the San Siro lights.

Milan went into the derby on a run of seven unbeaten against the Nerazzurri, and they extended the run to five wins in a row in a convincing display. Christian Pulisic, Adrien Rabiot and Diego Moreira all scored in the first half to establish a commanding lead that was enough to down the champions.
