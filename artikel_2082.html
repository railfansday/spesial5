Divine Mukasa set for Man City breakthrough after West Ham latest - verdict

Divine Mukasa's chances of becoming a big part of Man City's long-term future have improved after his latest West Ham showing.

West Ham managed to avoid embarrassment in Scott Parker's first game in charge of the club against Cardiff on Wednesday.

The Irons dramatically fought back from 3-0 down to draw 3-3 in the Welsh capital and stay just inside the top eight of the second tier.

Mukasa, 19, impressed for the first time under his new manager as he continues to shine on loan from Man City.

He has been tipped for big things at the Etihad Stadium and his latest showing is as good a sign as any that he could be key to his parent club's fortunes in the coming years.

City may well end up facing a season in the Championship after a full verdict on their financial charges is reached, and Mukasa is already a standout player in the division.

Mukasa scored his fourth goal of the campaign in his 12th league appearance for the Hammers to kickstart their comeback against Cardiff.

He displayed outstanding willingness, composure and technical ability to burst through the middle of the pitch, pluck Jarrod Bowen's long pass out of the air and chip the ball over Nathan Trott for 3-1 just before the hour mark.

Mukasa also completed the most passes (56) of any player on the pitch in South Wales, created the second-most chances (3), had 100 per cent dribble success rate and produced three ball recoveries.

The 19-year-old is turning into one of the first names on the Hammers' teamsheet, no matter the manager, with two goals and an assist in his four starts over the last month.

He is among the best-performing Championship midfielders for several key metrics this season, including shots on target per 90 (0.65), accurate long balls per 90 (2.38), successful dribbles per 90 (0.54) and touches in the opposition box per 90 (1.84).

Mukasa could end up being key to their promotion push under Parker, proving his worth as a playmaker who has the potential to be key to Man City in the future if they are forced to sell off their best players.

Mukasa was handed his senior City debut just days after his 18th birthday last year against Huddersfield Town.

Guardiola had already sensed his potential, and sung the teen's praises after his professional bow in the Carabao Cup.

He said: “Divine played unbelievably, he’s an incredibly top player in the pockets, in the striker [position], has a good sense. Divine is top, top. He has a sense of touch, quality with the ball, and he fought a lot. He played really good!"

Mukasa is now showing what he can do on a regular basis in senior football and could soon be ready to stake his claim at City too.
