Man Utd make four changes as Pochettino names starting XI for Sporting clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Manchester United have confirmed their starting lineup for Wednesday night’s Champions League meeting with Sporting Lisbon, with Mauricio Pochettino making changes after Sunday’s heavy defeat to Liverpool.

The Red Devils travel to Portugal looking to respond after a difficult start to life under their new head coach. Manchester United were beaten 4-0 by Liverpool at Anfield in Pochettino’s first match in charge, with Leny Yoro sent off inside the opening eight minutes.

Yoro’s dismissal disrupted United’s plans but the defender is available for tonight as his suspension only applies to domestic football.

Pochettino has selected a 4-2-3-1 formation for the trip to Lisbon, with Senne Lammens starting in goal. Noussair Mazraoui, Harry Maguire, Yoro and Lisandro Martinez make up the defensive line.

Kobbie Mainoo and Youri Tielemans start in midfield, while Amad, Bruno Fernandes and Bryan Mbeumo are named upfront behind Joshua Zirkzee, who gets his start this season.

United have several players unavailable or struggling with fitness issues ahead of the game. Benjamin Sesko and Manuel Ugarte remain sidelined with longer-term problems, while Carlos Baleba and Marcus Rashford are also dealing with issues.

Matheus Cunha is another absentee. The forward is suspended for the game after seeing a red against AS Roma.

Those absences have limited Pochettino’s options as he looks to find a response in his second match at the helm.

United face a difficult test against a Sporting side that have gone nine matches without defeat. The Portuguese side have no major injury concerns, with all key players available.

The pressure is already building on Pochettino after the defeat at Anfield. A positive result in Lisbon would offer United a chance to put that disappointment behind them, but they will need a far more disciplined performance to get anything from the game.

Lammens; Mazraoui, Maguire, Yoro, Martinez; Mainoo, Tielemans; Amad, Fernandes, Mbeumo; Zirkzee.
