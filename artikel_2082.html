Kris Boyd hits back at Rangers penalty moans and groans with blunt verdict

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Kris Boyd insists Rangers were correctly awarded their penalty against St Mirren and has backed referee Nick Walsh amid the controversy surrounding the decision.

James Penrice went down inside the penalty area after a challenge from Samuel Ramos, with Walsh immediately pointing to the spot.

'Surprisingly enough I thought it was a penalty' Kris Boyd on Rangers' penalty against St Mirren on Saturday night. #BBCFootball https://t.co/yBVvoy5fLZ

— BBC Sport Scotland (@BBCSportScot) November 9, 2026

The decision sparked debate, but Boyd believes the Rangers full-back was impeded and insists there should be little argument over the call. Speaking to the BBC, Boyd said: “Surprisingly enough, I thought it was a penalty!

"I think you can understand why people are moaning about it, but personally, I feel that Penrice was obstructed; he had to move his body to get out of the way. Nick Walsh was standing right in front of it, and he thought it was a penalty.

"He is the best referee in the country, and we moan about referees not making decisions on the pitch, and we're still moaning when they do. We are a nation that are never happy.

"For me, I thought it was a penalty at the time.”

Rangers capitalised on the decision, converting from the spot on their way to a 2-0 victory in Paisley.
