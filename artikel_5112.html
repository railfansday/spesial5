Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Russia will impose on Russian buyers of Tether’s USDT stablecoin an additional 22% value-added tax (VAT) when Moscow Exchange begins cryptocurrency trading on December 1, adding a high cost to transactions involving the dollar-pegged asset.
