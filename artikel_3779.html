How to get all bait recipes in DREADMOOR

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

As you will quickly learn while trying to angle your first Legendary Fish, unlocking the right baits early on can save you a lot of time in DREADMOOR.

But contrary to what you might imagine, you cannot unlock them via trial and error, with each only becoming craftable after you acquire its recipe. Here’s how to get all bait recipes in DREADMOOR and an overview of what each option offers.

You can get a total of six bait recipes in DREADMOOR, with the Wind-Up Tin and Rainbow Fin baits being unlocked automatically via the main story. The other recipes can then be purchased from Gill and Bozur, with their prices being directly tied to their rarities.

Most of the baits can also only attract one type of fish (Predatory or Peaceful), with the Clinging Pincer being the only exception. As you can see below, the pincer is also your best option when looking to deal with the end-game legendaries.

With all baits taken care of, don’t forget to also take a look at all Fishing Rod upgrades and how they work in DREADMOOR. After all, even the best bait will be worthless if you cannot put up a fight against your catch.
