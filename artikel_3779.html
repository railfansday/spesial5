Jamie Dornan speaks out about taking over from Viggo Mortensen in The Hunt for Gollum

New Aragorn actor Jamie Dornan has reacted to getting Viggo Mortensen's approval ahead of The Hunt for Gollum.

Speaking on Watch What Happens Live with Andy Cohen, Dornan revealed that while he hasn't spoken "directly" to Mortensen, he has heard from a recent Andy Serkis interview clip on Happy Sad Confused that the OG Strider/Aragorn actor is "thrilled" with the casting choice.

"He gave the stamp of approval, and that’s all I care about," Dornan said. "They released a thing that he sort of agreed to, to say that he was thrilled that I’d be taking over for him."

The Hunt for Gollum, which sees a band of heroes come together to search for Smeagol and the Ring prior to the events of The Fellowship of the Ring, has now finished filming.

Speaking to The Morning Mosh Pit after revealing the news, Serkis – who is both starring as Gollum and directing the new Lord of the Rings movie – says he is "very excited" about returning to the role which put him on the map.

He added, "He is one of Tolkien's most complex characters, and it definitely bears further examination."

The Hunt for Gollum also stars returning actors Elijah Wood, Sir Ian McKellen, and Lee Pace, alongside newcomers Jamie Dornan, Kate Winslet, Anya Taylor-Joy, and Leo Woodall.
