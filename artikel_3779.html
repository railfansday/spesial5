All weapons and loot in Fortnite Treasure Hunters

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

One of the most exciting aspects of each new Fortnite season is the updated loot pool, which consistently brings fresh items to the island while also rotating back in old favorites. In Fortnite’s Treasure Hunters season, the loot pool has plenty to discover.

Discovering and keeping track of everything available to find around the island is essential, as certain items are sure to work better for your combat style than others. If you want to keep up with everything that’s up for grabs, here’s all of the weapons and loot you can find around the island in Fortnite Treasure Hunters.

The loot pool for Fortnite Treasure Hunters primarily features an array of One Piece and anime-themed items to complement the overall theme of the season. It also has plenty of unvaulted items from past seasons, though, so some previous favorite items of yours may have returned this time around.

As the season progresses, the existing loot pool will change. This includes both the addition of fresh items to use and the removal of others. Because of this, it’s a good idea to use any items you really like while you can, as there’s no guarantee they’ll stick around forever.
