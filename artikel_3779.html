Motherboard manufacturer Biostar has just announced the BIPTL-IHT, a mini motherboard complete with an integrated Panther Lake-H processor. It's the first desktop Panther Lake motherboard to be released in retail.

As spotted by VideoCardz, the motherboard range has support for up to 128 GB of DDR-7200 MHz SO-DIMM memory and can come with the following chips:

All of these chips come with Xe3 graphics with four Xe cores, and are lacking the punchy integrated B390 iGPU (and its 12 Xe3 cores) that some mobile Panther Lake chips have. You can still do some light gaming on these integrated graphics, but you won't be making yourself a mini gaming PC with Biostar's latest.

Instead, it seems like the purpose of this motherboard is to support industrial PCs, but given Xe3 graphics support hardware-accelerated AV1 encoding and decoding, one could feasibly use the motherboard in a mini media server PC instead.

You are getting two M.2 Key-M slots here, with PCIe 5.0 x4 and PCIe 4.0 x2 connections. This allows for NVMe storage, and the memory is dual-channel too. You also get two HDMI ports, two DisplayPort 1.4 ports, and the motherboard can support up to four displays at once.

As it's a 'Thin' Mini-ITX motherboard, its dimensions are 170 mm by 170 mm. That's very small. We don't yet have a release date or price point for this motherboard range.

Still, it's a bit of a shame we don't have any mini motherboards with gaming-worthy integrated Panther Lake mobile chips yet. Especially as GPU prices are so high. I managed to get the chance to test out the Intel G3 Extreme in the Acer Predator Atlas 8 handheld, and it's pretty incredible for a chip with integrated graphics. It rocks both gaming and productivity tasks, given the size and heat.
