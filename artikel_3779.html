November 19 marked the highest-traffic day in the ISP's history.

In a post to X, "The Everything App," US entertainment and communications conglomerate Comcast revealed that Grand Theft Auto 6's historic launch drove record-breaking internet traffic. According to Comcast, only a quarter of "the entire internet" was downloading Rockstar's latest cozy game phenomenon.

"GTA 6 didn't just break records in gaming," Comcast wrote. "Its launch drove the biggest internet traffic day in our network history, with traffic climbing 31% above an average day. At peak, GTA 6 accounted for nearly a quarter of all network traffic."

Comcast shared a video of a line graph showing the massive relative spike in activity on November 19. The chart is unlabeled except for the date, but it presumably corresponds to the volume of data being downloaded across Comcast's entire network.

And that last but does add a caveat to the numbers Comcast cited: Its network and "the entire internet" are two different things, unless I'm missing something here or Comcast's data accounts for other ISPs and other countries, not just its own services.

Regardless, it's still a significant bit of fun with statistics, another example of the totalizing footprint of what may be the largest commercial launch in entertainment history. Imagine how much bigger it would have been launching on PC as well.
