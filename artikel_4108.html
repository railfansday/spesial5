Resident Evil Requiem Wins GOTY As Xbox Takes Home Two Awards At The Golden Joysticks 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Wednesday night was a celebration of all the best games from the past 12 months, with the Golden Joystick Awards taking place in London's Troxy venue.

What a night it was for Capcom, as not only did they come away with Ultimate GOTY, and Console GOTY for Resident Evil Requiem, but the developer won another five awards including Studio of the Year.

In terms of Xbox's own studios, it's a familiar story to last year's Joysticks with Minecraft taking home the "Still Playing" award for consoles and PC, and Doom: The Dark Ages Revelations claiming "Best Game Expansion".

Altogether, there were 12 games all vying for the Golden Joysticks' most covenanted Ultimate Game of the Year award. Xbox's Forza Horizon 6 was in the running but it was a Resident Evil kinda night.

We'll leave you to look over the full list of winners and nominees from the Golden Joystick Awards 2026 below.
