Black Panther Actor Says Marvel 1943 One Button Rumor Is “Ridiculous”

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Debunking reports that Marvel 1943 had been cancelled, Kotaku claimed on November 11 that Amy Hennig's Black Panther and Captain America-centric game was originally designed for Netflix before transitioning to console and PC, meaning that it's light on gameplay and mostly uses one button. However, Black Panther himself, Khary Payton, claims otherwise.

"I've seen stuff about this game like it's not happening or some people — I think the latest thing is it's a one-button game. I don't know how that could be possible," Payton said in a new interview with Brandon Davis. "I have no idea what that means, but I have literally played the game. I know that's not true. It's ridiculous.

They're going to be blown away by this game.

"This game is so — not just beautiful, people don't understand the level of genius on every level," he continued. "Amy Hennig has put together a team that is phenomenal. And they're going to be blown away by this game. You know what? I look forward to those same guys who say these things on the internet having to eat a little crow. I know that they're going to love it."

While it was reported that Marvel 1943: Rise of Hydra underwent serious turmoil behind the scenes after transitioning from a narrative-focused Netflix game to a more traditional console title, Kotaku also claimed that production was "close to done," something Khary Payton corroborated. He told Davis that "we have finished the majority" and are now "cleaning up," and while he doesn't have a release date to share at this time, he teased that "it's coming soon."

“I wanted to remind my kid and people like him that they are magic."

With that being said, we know exceedingly little about Marvel 1943. The game is set in occupied Paris during World War 2 and is based on the limited Marvel Comics series Captain America/Black Panther: Flag of Our Fathers, with Howling Commandos' member Gabriel Jones and Wakandan Spy Network leader Nanali serving as playable characters alongside Steve Rogers and Azzuri (Black Panther), but otherwise, we don't know how it'll play or even what it looks like in its current state. Beyond the initial 2021 reveal, little has been shown.

Hopefully, that will change soon — maybe at The Game Awards (where Iron Man appears to be suiting up for a trailer). Right now, there are a lot of contradictory reports and rumors circulating, and it'd be reassuring to finally have the record set straight.

Paramount want to ensure that Amy Hennig's new team is "Set up for a great future," and that means not rushing Marvel 1943 out the door.
