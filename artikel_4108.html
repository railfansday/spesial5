Intergalactic PS5 in the 'Last Stage of Production', Gameplay Coming Next Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Neil Druckmann thinks the game will "surprise people"

Since the announcement of Naughty Dog's next big game, Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet, we really haven't seen or heard much about it. In 2027, though, that's set to change.

In a brief appearance on a live show at Comic Creators Block Party 2026, director Neil Druckmann has teased a few things about the upcoming sci-fi adventure.

You can see all the Intergalactic chatter in the post below:

Druckmann says the game is at the "last stage of production", meaning "we can play it, we're refining and polishing it". He says he "can't wait to show it to the world."

It sounds like 2027 will be big for Intergalactic, as Sony and Naughty Dog will "show a lot of it next year" including "gameplay" and "more of the story". Whether the game actually launches next year is another question, but at the very least we'll be seeing plenty more of it.

"I think it'll really surprise people, what they think the game is and what it actually is," Druckmann says.

He also talks about how Naughty Dog has been making "relatively grounded" games for a long time with Uncharted and The Last of Us, and Intergalactic presents an opportunity for the team to explore something more fantastical.

"Something we've been really hungry for is to go sci-fi," he says, referring to the studio's previous series, Jak & Daxter.

"We really wanted to take everything we learned from that franchise, which is sci-fi/fantasy, the pulp action adventure stuff we learned with Uncharted, the more serious drama we got from The Last of Us... [Intergalactic] really is a combination of those three franchises."

Druckmann continues, talking about how the aim is to combine Naughty Dog's cinematic storytelling chops with "deeper gameplay systems" in Intergalactic.

I'm super excited to finally learn more about this game in 2027; fingers crossed its release won't be too far behind.
