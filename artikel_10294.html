Google Antigravity adds ‘Knowledge Work’ interface for non-coding work

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Following last week’s big update, Google Antigravity is rolling out a “Knowledge Work” user experience.

Antigravity launched with a coding focus, but Google has seen how people, including employees internally, are using it for “far more than code.”

Alongside software engineering, more and more daily sessions center on deep research, data analysis, drafting documents and presentations, prototyping designs, and automating everyday workflows.

The company shared that “tens of thousands of Googlers outside software engineering (across sales, finance, legal, product, design, research, marketing, and operations) now rely on Antigravity every week alongside engineers to get their work done.”

As such, Google Antigravity now has an optimized Knowledge Work experience that joins the existing Software Engineering interface. The underlying Antigravity agent is unchanged “with full access to frontier models, local files, plugins, skills, rules, custom agents, and MCP servers.”
