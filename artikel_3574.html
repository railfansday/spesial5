Dolly Parton's Niece Honors Country Star on 1st Thanksgiving Since Her Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Dolly Parton’s niece Danielle Parton is honoring her late aunt this Thanksgiving.

Danielle, 50, paid tribute to Dolly on the first holiday since the “Jolene” singer’s death in August via a touching video shared on social media on Thursday, November 26.

“This year looks a little different from my family,” Danielle said in the video uploaded to Instagram. “We’re missing someone very special at the table. But Aunt Granny taught us to count our blessings, and I have so many.”

Danielle — who is the daughter of Bobby Lee Parton and Doris Maxine Noland — said she was grateful for her loved ones and well-wishers sending the family support after enduring a tough year.

“I’m thankful for my health, for all the people in my life, and for every one of you who sent so much love my way this year,” Danielle added in her message. “So hug your people a little tighter today, and from my home to yours, happy Thanksgiving.”

One day prior, U2 honored the memory of Dolly with a posthumous duet at the NFL’s Thanksgiving Eve game.

The Irish rockers projected archival footage and audio of Dolly for a hybrid live performance of their collaborative track “Torn” at the Green Bay Packers and Los Angeles Rams’ game on Wednesday, November 25.

The “I Will Always Love You” singer died on August 25 after a brief battle with an undisclosed form of cancer. She was 80.

“Global icon and humanitarian, Dolly Parton, known to the world as a beloved singer, songwriter, philanthropist, actress, playwright and author died peacefully today in Nashville, Tennessee,” read a statement from Parton’s rep at the time.

The statement continued: “A rhinestone life that shone bright enough for the world to see, Dolly will forever stand as an inspiration not only through her timeless music and prolific songwriting, but also her wit, warmth and kindness that made us all feel like family.”

Last month, Danielle spoke candidly about her own health struggles in the wake of her aunt’s death.

“I’m going through a little bit of a cancer battle myself right now,” she shared in an interview with Extra published on October 22. “I’m OK.”

Although she did not share further details, Danielle added that she’s “stage zero,” meaning her doctors “caught it early.” She also divulged that Dolly took the news of her cancer “really heavy.”

“I didn’t know, like anybody else, what she was dealing with. I knew she was sick,” Danielle recalled. “And so she was just devastated that I was going through it, and I remember telling her, I’m like, ‘Look, you’ve gotta get it together. It ain’t that deep. I just wanted you to know ‘cause I was having a biopsy.’”

She added, “She worried more about everybody else before she would ever let you know anything was wrong with her.”
