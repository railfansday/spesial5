Miley Cyrus' Stepsister Seemingly Shares Maxx Morando Rehearsal Dinner Pic

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando are reportedly inching closer to walking down the wedding aisle and saying “I do.”

Cyrus’ stepsister Lily-Rose Purcell appeared to offer a glimpse of the pair’s traditional pre-nuptial celebration, sharing images of a dinner plate to her Instagram Stories on Friday, November 20.

In one snap, a place setting for her sister Audrey Purcell was visible alongside a printed menu with “M+M 11.20.26” shown at the top. Us has reached out reps for Cyrus, 33, and Morando, 27, for comment.

Cyrus is reportedly set to marry Morando this weekend, per TMZ. (Miley’s mom, Tish Cyrus, married Prison Break alum Dominic Purcell, a father of four from a previous relationship, in 2023.)

Miley and the drummer, who have not publicly shared any wedding plans, have been together since 2021. They got engaged in December 2025.

“We were in Kyoto, Japan. For some reason, you’ll find this as crazy as I did, she’d never been to Japan before. Neither had I, and we’d both wanted to go for a really long time,” Morando recalled to Wonderland magazine in July of popping the question. “There were talks before we went about me popping the question, but I was trying to keep it secret. She was not trying to keep it a secret, if you know what I mean.”

He added at the time, “I had to be really sneaky about it. We went to Tokyo first for a couple days and then on to Kyoto. I think she had a sense that it was coming, so I was waiting until the very last moment of the very last day to throw her off the scent as much as I could.”

Attempting to keep his proposal plans under wraps, Morando merely asked Miley to go on a seemingly innocent walk around the hotel grounds.

“There was this little outer garden that you had to cross a plank to get over. There was a sign that said you couldn’t cross. I was like, ‘That’s crazy, let’s go over there,’” he quipped. “She’s like, ‘No, it’s blocked.’ Eventually, I persuaded her. We walked in, and it was a little dead end, this beautiful Japanese forest-y situation. She was taking videos and was turned away. That’s when I got on one knee and tapped her on the shoulder.”

The Hannah Montana alum was admittedly stunned by her beau’s proposal.

“I am not easy to surprise because I love to control every situation, and I had completely surrendered,” Miley told Good Morning America late last year. “I’m telling you, I was so, so surprised.”

Miley was previously married to her The Last Song costar Liam Hemsworth from 2018 to 2020.
