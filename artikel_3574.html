Stephen Baldwin Opens Up About ‘Life-Changing’ Heart Disease Scare

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Stephen Baldwin gained a new perspective on life after he experienced a frightening medical emergency over the summer.

“I was at home, and I got this kind of fluttering on my chest, directly over my heart,” Baldwin, 60, told People in an interview published on Wednesday, November 18. “It was almost like if I took a feather and I tickled the back of your hand. I felt this weird kind of tickling sensation on my skin, directly under my pectoral muscle.”

The Usual Suspects actor said he experienced the sensation again multiple times throughout the night, approximately every four hours.

“I woke up the next day, and immediately it was there again,” he recalled. “Nothing intense, no pain. Just a strange fluttering.”

Baldwin said he became alarmed when the fluttering increased to every two hours.

“I’m not a doctor, but I’ve played one on TV. And common sense would say, you know, my body’s saying something and I don’t know what it is,” he continued. “So, ​​I’m getting a little nervous.”

Thankfully, Baldwin already had an aortic specialist after his daughter Hailey Bieber encouraged him to keep an eye on his heart health four years ago. (Baldwin’s father had an aortic aneurysm, a balloon-like bulge in the aorta, before he died from lung cancer at age 55. Aortic issues can be hereditary.)

Baldwin recalled texting his specialist, who called him right away.

“He wastes no time. He just literally says to me these life-changing words: ‘Listen to me, and do exactly as I tell you. When you hang up this phone…’ And that’s when I knew something was wrong,” he continued.

Baldwin was referred to a cardiologist, who told him that he needed to go to the hospital. After blood tests, an ultrasound and a CT scan at Lenox Hill Hospital in New York City, he was diagnosed with coronary artery disease (CAD).

According to the Mayo Clinic, CAD is a common type of heart disease that “affects the main blood vessels that supply blood to the heart, called the coronary arteries,” and reduces blood flow. A complete blockage of blood flow can lead to a heart attack, making the condition very serious.

Baldwin underwent a diagnostic procedure called a cardiac catheterization, which led doctors to discover that his left anterior descending (LAD) artery had a 90 percent blockage. They performed an angioplasty to open the blocked blood vessels and placed a stent.

Baldwin admitted that he got “pretty choked up” when he learned about the severity of his CAD.

“You just start to go through all of those thoughts in your head, like, what could have happened? That was kind of life-changing because I thought I was fine,” he told the outlet. “And that block didn’t just happen overnight. The calcification of the block was something that took years to develop.”

Baldwin recalled his doctor telling him, “‘You’re a very lucky guy. God has plans for you.’ For a surgeon, a guy who goes in and does this every day several times a day, to say that … it’s life-changing.”

Baldwin said the health scare led him to feel “born again” with a “new lease on life.”

“When you face your mortality in this way? That’s a wake-up call that now has become a bit of a mission for me,” he shared. “Hopefully I can be a part of communicating the importance of plaque tests, cholesterol checks, all of that.”

Baldwin concluded, “You have to do the research on your own body to make sure you’re OK. There’s enough information and enough science about how to be heart healthy. Once you turn 50, 55, 60, you have to do something to protect yourself so that your quality of life in your 70s, 80s, 90s is what it could potentially be. You’ve got to do what you have to do to live and stay alive and be healthy.”
