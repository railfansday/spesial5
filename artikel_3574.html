GTA 6 Players Are Officially Spending an ‘Absolutely Bonkers’ Amount of Time with the Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Launch day playtime stats for Grand Theft Auto 6 are off the charts

Grand Theft Auto 6 players weren't kidding about wanting to spend the game's entire launch day in Leonida. Much hullabaloo was made about people requesting time off from work or faking sick to spend the entire day playing GTA 6, and plenty of companies tried to capitalize on that conversation with marketing stunts.

Mat Piscatella, senior director at market research firm Circana, shared data on Friday about just how much time players spent with GTA 6 on its launch day, Thursday, Nov. 19. "PS5 GTA VI players averaged 513 minutes that day, Xbox players 494," he said in a post on Bluesky. That's an average of 8.55 and 8.23 hours for PS5 and Xbox players, respectively. That means GTA 6 players effectively spent the length of a workday stealing drugs, beating up rednecks, driving around Fake Florida, kicking bodega cats, and playing GTA 6's in-game video games.

You could fly from New York City to Seattle in under 8.55 hours. You could watch the entirety of the original Star Wars trilogy in under 8.55 hours. You could binge The Office season 4 in under 8.55 hours. You could finish Resident Evil 2 in under 8.55 hours, and probably still have enough time to finish it again. You could get an honest-to-god good night's sleep for under 8.55 hours, on average. All I'm saying is, 8.55 hours is a long time, especially for an average.

The average playtime stat is certainly eye-catching, but other numbers Piscatella shared are perhaps even more staggering. 61% of US-based PlayStation 5 players and 56% of global Xbox Series X players "engaged" with GTA 6 on its launch day, figures Piscatella called "absolutely bonkers." He used Fortnite to put that in perspective: Epic Games' battle royale was the second-most played game on Nov. 19 with 10% of PlayStation and Xbox players engaging with it.

Those numbers are as unbelievable as GTA 6's sales figures. According to Take-Two Interactive, GTA 6's launch is now the biggest entertainment launch in history with $3.79 billion generated in revenue.
