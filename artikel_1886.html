Alexis Mac Allister must fix his Liverpool weakness, Argentina performance exposes it

Alexis Mac Allister has been in dazzling form for Liverpool this season and looked back to his best following a rotten 2025-26 campaign.

Last season was far from ideal for the Argentine, who struggled to have the same impact he previously had on this Liverpool side.

The arrival of Andoni Iraola has seemingly lit a new fire under the midfielder, who has generally been accepted as his team's best player this season, but away on international duty, a weakness has appeared.

One that, if fixed, could send him to an even higher level than he is already playing at.

Mac Allister routinely gets on the ball, but when it comes to influencing things in the final third, he could do a lot more.

And that has been a common issue for the South American in the Premier League this season.

Mac Allister is comfortable keeping possession and dictating the tempo, but he is also capable of creating

However, that is something he has not been doing enough of this season, with that evident while away on international duty as well.

As per FotMob, Mac Allister had 66 touches against Uruguay, but he was unable to translate those into chances created.

He created just one chance, which did not equate to a big chance for his nation.

That is an issue that has been arising at Liverpool, as with Mac Allister being a technically sound midfielder, you would expect him to create more.

And this campaign, the Argentine has yet to create a single big chance for his side.

It is a weaknein to his game that he must address, as becoming more influential in that regard, he can still go up another level.

Mac Allister may be operating in a two-man pivot that may be limiting his ability to create chances.

But he is sitting in a deeper position, mostly just before the final third.

That is an optimal place to be on the pitch to send a decisive pass into the box or through the defensive lines.

Perhaps he is asked to be safer on the ball, and at that, he has been performing well.

But, with adding those decisive actions, there would be no argument over who Liverpool's best midfielder is, as that is all he needs to add to his game.
