‘I don’t see there’s any need’ - Ex-Leeds United and England goalkeeper preaches James Trafford patience

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Former Leeds United goalkeeper Paul Robinson does not believe there is “any need” for Three Lions boss Thomas Tuchel to immediately replace Jordan Pickford with James Trafford.

Game time has been evenly split between Everton’s Pickford and Leeds’ Trafford across the last two international breaks, with the latter pushing to dislodge the former.

Trafford has made a stellar start to life at Leeds since his summer switch from Manchester City and shone in England’s 3-2 Nations League win over Spain at the weekend.

The 24-year-old has been showered with praise but Robinson, who also represented England in his playing days, has preached patience.

Writing for The i, he said: “At the moment, I don’t see there’s any need to replace Jordan. Without a doubt, the next major tournament he will be playing, but he’ll be 34.

“It just goes to show Tuchel sees this as a long-term project. He’s not in overnight thinking, how do I win the next game? He’s preparing for life further down the road – and life after Jordan Pickford.”

Robinson may not be advocating for an immediate change between the sticks, yet is a strong admirer of Leeds’ club-record signing.

He has suggested Trafford - who cost the Whites a hefty £40m - has the potential to enter the elite goalkeeper bracket.

The 47-year-old said: “As English goalkeepers, we haven’t had one quite in the bracket of Ederson, Alisson, Thibaut Courtois at their peaks. The bracket underneath, we’re flush with.

“Trafford has the potential to enter that top bracket, while Pickford has improved as a person and a player. There was a lot of talk about his antics on the pitch – he’s understood who he is, and he’s channelled the best part of him in the right way.”
