Sources: Leeds set sights on Dion Beljo after what he did vs Sunderland

Leeds United have set their sights on a deal to sign Dinamo Zagreb striker Dion Beljo, sources have told Football Insider.

The 24-year-old is enjoying an outstanding campaign both domestically and in Europe, where he has scored 19 goals and provided nine assists in 20 games across all competitions.

A goal in Zagreb's 2-0 win against Sunderland at the Stadium of Light is believed to have alerted a number of English clubs to his talents, with a January move on the cards.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Beljo ticks a number of boxes for Leeds as they continue to assess the striker market.

Daniel Farke is determined to strengthen his attacking options in the mid-season transfer window, with the Zagreb forward now high on their list of potential targets.

Beljo's goalscoring exploits so far this season have placed him on the Whites' radar, and they are set to assess his performances ahead of the transfer window opening.

Football Insider revealed last month that Farke is pushing the Leeds recruitment team to sign a new striker as he seeks support for his ambitious season plans.

Dominic Calvert-Lewin remains the first choice striker at Elland Road, but the lack of strength in depth behind him has emerged as a concern over the last 12 months.

The addition of a new centre-forward is seen as a priority for the manager, and Beljo ticks a number of boxes, meaning he could be the target Leeds have been looking for.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I'm sure it's not just Leeds who looking at Beljo because of his prolific goalscoring form.

"He's got 19 goals in 20 games in all competitions. Obviously, he enhanced his reputation by scoring against Sunderland in the Europa League earlier this month as well.

"That caught the attention of a number of Premier League clubs, and we know Leeds are going to be in the market for striking options in the next couple of transfer windows.

"They want cover and competition for Dominic Calvert-Lewin, and there are long-term doubts over the future of Lukas Nmecha as well.

"Beljo is a player that Leeds are monitoring, and if he continues in this vein of goalscoring form, there will be clubs across Europe trying to sign the Croatia international."

Beljo's performances for Dinamo Zagreb this season have sparked high-profile interest not just from Leeds, but from a number of clubs across European football.

His goalscoring exploits have placed a number of clubs on alert, with his Europa League displays proving he can perform at a high level outside of Croatian football.

Football Insider revealed last month that Everton have been keeping a close eye on Beljo's progress and could be tempted to step up their interest in the January window.

Much like the situation at Leeds, the Toffees are short of squad depth in attacking areas, with Thierno Barry the only senior striker on the books at the Hill Dickinson Stadium.

Beljo's performances have placed him firmly on their radar, and they may be prepared to go head-to-head with rivals clubs to complete a deal when the window opens.
