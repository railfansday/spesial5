‘We were not bothered’ - Daniel Farke salutes Leeds United response after remarkable comeback against Chelsea

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Leeds United manager Daniel Farke saluted his players for the remarkable manner in which they responded to going a goal down at Chelsea.

Emmanuel Emegha rifled the hosts ahead on the 25-minute mark but Leeds were back on level terms within three minutes.

Noah Okafor levelled proceedings and the Whites did not stop there, striking again through Dominic Calvert-Lewin, Okafor and Brenden Aaronson to claim an emphatic 4-1 victory.

“[It was a] great result,” said Farke. “Obviously I think the first win here at Stamford Bridge for us as a club in this century, so it says a lot about how it ranks more or less in the history books of Leeds United.

“[It was a] great performance. I would have been disappointed even with a draw today. I was a bit disappointed even at half-time when it was just a draw because I think we were, from the first and last, the better side.

“Somehow you concede a goal, but what I liked a lot is that we showed the mentality, we were not bothered. We played further on on the front foot and didn't doubt ourselves and wanted to play exactly the way we have played. I think a great reaction and also to equalise and to play further on.

“The boys came out flying and it seemed like they listened. There were some great team moves and great chances. We were then also clinical in front of the opponent's goal and could have scored perhaps even a few more, but I can't complain too much with a 4-1 win here away at Chelsea.”

Leeds registered a staggering 21 shots against Chelsea’s star-studded side, outfighting and outclassing an expensively assembled side.

Statistics show Chelsea enjoyed the lion’s share of possession, but control was very much with the Whites.

“We dominated this game,” Farke said. “We made sure that our plan for this game works.

“We wanted to play on the front foot, we wanted to press Chelsea high, we wanted to make sure that they can't show their world-class potential.

“They have unbelievable players and we didn't want these players in the mood to enjoy the game. I still think that Chelsea came out motivated today and also played with intensity.”

Leeds have risen to sixth in the Premier League table, leapfrogging Chelsea with the victory.
