England vs Croatia: Nations League prediction, kick-off time, TV, live stream, team news, h2h results, odds

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

England face Croatia in the penultimate game of the Nations League group stage, which will see captain Harry Kane become the most-capped player in Three Lions’ history.

Fresh from winning the Ballon d’Or, captain Kane is set to make England history with his 126th cap to move clear of long-time record-holder Peter Shilton.

Croatia head to Wembley, with their mauling at the hands of the Three Lions’ in Rijeka last month still fresh in the memory.

Thomas Tuchel’s side put seven past their hosts without reply, as Harry Kane and Bukayo Saka bagged braces. There were further goals from Jude Bellingham, Anthony Gordon, and Morgan Rogers as England wiped the floor with Croatia.

England are poised to advance to the next stage of the Nations League but currently via a second-placed finish, which would set up a quarter final with a big hitter such as France or Portugal.

Victory for England at Wembley would likely set-up a last-game decider with Spain, the winner of that match topping the group.

England vs Croatia is scheduled for a 7.45pm GMT kick-off on Thursday, November 12, 2026.

TV channel: In the UK, England vs Croatia in the 2026/27 Nations League is being televised live and free to air on ITV, with coverage of the match beginning at 7pm BST on ITV1 ahead of a 7:45pm kick-off.

There should be another landmark reached for Ballon d’Or winner Kane on Thursday, with the England skipper set to overtake Shilton as the most capped player for the Three Lions.

Injuries for Marc Guehi and Ezri Konsa have led to the late call-ups of Dan Burn and James Hill, with the latter hoping to make his England debut.

Other potential debutants include Fulham's Josh King and Borussia Dortmund midfielder Jobe Bellingham, younger brother of England star Jude.

England are a bit light on the right with Arsenal duo Bukayo Saka and Noni Madueke out injured, with Chelsea attacker Morgan Rogers and Liverpool youngster Rio Ngumoha likely replacements.

For Croatia, ex-Tottenham Hotspur winger Ivan Perisic is suspended after his red card last time out against Spain in Split.

After last month’s chastening 7-0 loss to England, many in Croatia are writing this game off, with calls to rest legendary 41-year-old Luka Modric for the crunch clash with Czechia later in the International window.

As mentioned above, this fixture has already produced plenty of goals. Croatia have been leaky at the back throughout the Nations League campaign, conceding 14 goals in four games. A 4-1 drubbing at the hands of Spain in their last away game indicates that England should have plenty of chances to score at home.

While the Three Lions were able to keep Croatia quite away from home, their guests on Thursday night usually score at least once. It would not be a surprise to see England match Spain’s latest result against Croatia.

England and Croatia have crossed paths in three of the last four major tournaments, with the Three Lions victorious on two occasions. However, Croatia had the last laugh - beating England in the semi-finals of the World Cup to reach a maiden final.

Overall, this is a fixture England usually win - but Croatia have inflicted major upsets before, preventing England from qualifying for the 2008 Euros with wins home and away.
