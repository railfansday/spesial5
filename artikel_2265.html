British football's top 25 most demanding football fanbases ranked by AI

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The United Kingdom is the birthplace of football and is widely acknowledged as the home of the world's most popular sport, and, naturally, some fanbases are proud, passionate, and above all, demanding.

The first laws of the game of football were established by the FA in 1863, and in the more than 160 years since, the sport has developed and refined drastically into the spectacle we all know and love today.

Certain towns and cities across the UK live and breathe football, as how their team does at the weekend will largely affect their moods for the rest of the week until they play again, and, as a byproduct of that, some teams' supporters demand and expect more from their side.

Whether you're a fan of a League Two club or one in the Premier League, your expectations and demands will naturally vary depending on who you support, as Football League World has asked AI to rank the 25 most demanding clubs in the United Kingdom right now.

AI says: Wolves supporters expect the club to be an established Premier League side, competing towards the top half rather than simply fighting to stay up.

One of the founding members of the EFL, Wolverhampton Wanderers are engulfed in a rich history dating back to 1877, and in the near-150 years since their inception, the Old Gold have taken home 17 major domestic trophies, including 11 league titles, four FA Cups, and two league cups.

Success hasn't found its way to Molineux often in modern years, but still a regular in the Premier League until recently, the Old Gold unsurprisingly snuck their way into this list with some of the most demanding fans in the UK.

AI says: As former Premier League champions, Blackburn supporters expect their club to return to the top flight and compete at a much higher level.

Blackburn Rovers are another one of the founding members of the EFL, and established in 1875, the Lancashire-based outfit is the most successful club in Lancashire in terms of major honours, with 11 in their cabinet, including the 1994/95 Premier League title.

Although the club has spent the bulk of the last decade or so in the second tier, their rich history has naturally meant their fanbase demands and expects more than what has been afforded to them in recent times.

AI says: Bolton's successful Premier League years have left supporters expecting a return to the upper reaches of English football.

Founded in 1874 and another founding member of the EFL, Bolton Wanderers have spent a large chunk of their recent history bouncing between the bottom three divisions, but have spent a good amount of time in the Premier League since its reformatting in 1992.

The Trotters have four FA Cups in their trophy haul, and though three came before World War 2, their fanbase remains proud of their history and demands more as one of Greater Manchester's biggest clubs.

AI says: Derby are a historically significant club, and their supporters expect regular promotion challenges and, ultimately, a return to the Premier League.

As one of four EFL founding members in a row, Derby County boast an extremely proud and passionate fanbase dating back to 1884, and with three major honours in their trophy haul, they have the history to back it up.

The Rams have spent just seven years in the Premier League since its inception in 1992, and given the club's status in England, supporters would surely have been demanding more.

AI says: Coventry supporters increasingly expect the club to establish itself in the Premier League rather than settle for occasional success in the Championship.

Coventry City are a Premier League club once again, but go back less than a decade, the Sky Blues were dwindling in League Two on the brink of existence.

With one FA Cup in their major honours list, Coventry don't have as rich a history as other clubs on this list, but boast an incredibly proud and passionate support few clubs in the country can rival, as AI believes they deserve a spot in this ranking.

AI says: Everton's history and stature mean supporters expect European qualification and considerably more than mid-table mediocrity.

Since the Premier League's inception, Everton have never spent a single season outside of the top tier, despite a few close calls, and with 15 major honours in their history, the Toffees have one of the UK's proudest and most loyal supporters.

Largely in the shadow of their noisy neighbours Liverpool throughout most of their history, Everton have held their own, though having seen the red side of Merseyside boast the majority of success over the years, supporters have naturally demanded more from their club.

AI says: West Ham supporters expect European football and believe the club has the resources, history and fanbase to achieve more.

West Ham United are back in the Championship right now, but Hammers supporters will be hoping, and demanding, that it won't be for the long haul, and Scott Parker can take them straight back to the Premier League.

With average attendances rivalling the very best in the entire UK, and even the rest of Europe, despite their second tier status, West Ham's fanbase is something to be marvelled at, and with five major honours to boot, it's hardly a surprise their fans demand more than struggling in the second tier.

AI says: Middlesbrough supporters expect their club to be challenging for promotion virtually every season, given its history as a regular Premier League side.

Middlesbrough have spent nearly a decade in the second tier, and though they will be hoping to change that under Kim Hellberg this year, the Teesside outfit can still boast 15 years in the top flight, as well as one League Cup.

A consistent presence in the Championship promotion picture, Middlesbrough fans often demand their side to be fighting towards the top of the league year after year, as AI gives them a big ranking in this list.

AI says: West Brom are traditionally viewed as a top-flight club, meaning anything short of a serious promotion challenge can be considered disappointing.

Established in 1878 and another founding member of the EFL, West Bromwich Albion are the other side of the Black Country to Wolves, and like their Old Gold neighbours, boast a rich and proud history consisting of seven major honours.

The Baggies have spent the bulk of the millennium between the Premier League and Championship, with their support demanding to, at the very least, be challenging for a place in the top flight year-on-year if they are not already in it.

AI says: Sheffield United supporters expect Premier League football and believe their club belongs among England's established top-flight sides.

Now to the city of Sheffield, as Sheffield United take their place on this list with five major honours across their history.

Their demanding support largely stems from wanting to be ahead of bitter city rivals Sheffield Wednesday at all times; if the Blades are behind the Owls at any point, supporters are quick to point that out and demand better.
