Sheffield United man to attract transfer ‘interest’ in January window, says reporter

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Sheffield United could face a battle to keep hold of the England youth international in the upcoming January transfer window amid potential attention from other clubs. He is under contract at SUFC until June 2028 meaning he still has just under two years left to run down on his deal at Bramall Lane.

Peck, 22, has become a key player for the Blades in the Championship under the guidance of head coach Chris Wilder. He has made 16 appearances for the South Yorkshire outfit in all competitions this season, 14 of which have come in the league, and he has chipped in with four goals and three assists.

Oxley has shared this update on his situation, as per a report by BBC Sport: “Peck’s stock is high, and rising. He has become a regular in the England under-21 side, he’s one of the first names on the Blades team sheet, and his underlying shooting, passing, possession and defending statistics show he ranks as one of the Championship’s best players in his position.

“He was coveted by Premier League clubs last summer, and you’d imagine he will be the subject of interest once again in January, especially after United sold a trio of stars pre-season to help balance the books.”

Sheffield United won’t want to lose Peck anytime soon, especially halfway through this campaign. He is a valuable member of SUFC’s squad and has the capacity to grow and develop even further down the line.

West Ham were said to be keen on him in the summer after their relegation from the Premier League. However, nothing materialised in the end and he stayed put beyond the last deadline in September.

Peck, who is from Worthing, has enjoyed plenty of game time with the Blades over recent times. He battles it out with the likes of Kalvin Phillips, Oli Arblaster and Joe Rothwell, among others, for minutes in Wilder’s starting XI.

He joined Sheffield United as a youngster back in 2021 after rising up through the academy at Arsenal. He has since played over 100 matches and was also loaned out to Oldham Athletic back in 2022 to get some more experience under his belt down in League Two.

Sheffield United’s chances of retaining the services of Peck in the long run will likely depend on whether they can reach the top flight. If they go up this term, he would probably stick around at SUFC.

If they stay in the second tier though, it would be a surprise if he stayed beyond next year. The Blades need to take advantage of his ability whilst they can.

Wilder’s men have been in good form recently and are genuine contenders for the play-offs if they can stay consistent. They beat Preston North End 3-2 last time out on Tuesday night with Peck scoring a hat-trick at Deepdale against the Lilywhites.

Sheffield United are now unbeaten in their last four and they are starting to build a bit of momentum now as they aim to get into the top eight. They are sat in 9th position in the table and they are only a point behind West Ham in 8th ahead of their upcoming match against Charlton Athletic away.
