Rangers boss Derek McInnes brands Falkirk win over Celtic 'not really a shock'

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Rangers manager Derek McInnes has stated Falkirk's win over Celtic was "not really a shock".

The Ibrox boss commented on the weekend result when he appeared on talkSPORT on Monday morning, as he hailed the work done by John McGlynn.

Ally McCoist had singled out McGlynn for praise for the job he has done at Falkirk - and McInnes joined in on heaping praise on the manager.

And he insisted it wasn't a huge shock to see Falkirk beat Celtic.

On talkSPORT, McInnes said: "The likes of John, you like to see good managers doing good jobs.

Clearly they lost a few good players, and that's normally the way. It's difficult to keep them - especially at a club like Falkirk.

"You just see a good manager doing his work well.

"Although it seems a shock Celtic getting beat there, but it's not really a shock when you see it's Falkirk that win the game.

"Falkirk are capable of that, and they did that a couple of times last season as well."

McInnes then backed his former club Hearts to be in the title hunt this season.

He said: "I do think Hearts will be title contenders again with the investment and size of squad they have got and the level of player they have got.

"I think Hearts will be up there again. It will be more of a surprise if they're not."
