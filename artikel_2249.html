Why Martin O'Neill has made a Kasper Hogh and Henrik Larsson Celtic comparison

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Martin O'Neill has compared Kasper Hogh's situation at Celtic to that of Henrik Larsson.

Hogh has not scored for his new club since a hat-trick against Kilmarnock and has been in and out of the team after some flat performances.

After drawing a blank against Killlie this time, while having a goal disallowed, O'Neill feels the crowd are right behind him just as they were a certain fellow Scandinavian who went on to become a club legend.

The Celtic manager told BBC Radio Scotland: “I didn’t want to bring this into the melting pot.

“But a few years ago Henrik Larsson stepped onto the field here and couldn’t do anything wrong (in the eyes of the supporters).

“And that’s a lovely feeling to have, regardless of the number of times he might have misplaced a pass – which wasn’t that often.

“The crowd were very forgiving of a fantastic footballer.

“Now I’m not saying Kasper has reached that height. But I think the crowd appreciate what he’s doing and that he’s trying to help the team.”

The 74-year-old felt that if Hogh's goal had been allowed to stand, he would have gone on to get another.

"We thought Kasper had got that goal and then it was chalked off," he added.

“Then a few minutes later he had another chance. And I think if the first ‘goal’ had counted then I think perhaps he then gets a second goal.

“It’s maybe a lack of confidence in front of goal at this minute.

“But I thought he worked exceptionally hard for us during the game – as he did on Thursday night. The goals will come.

“I think it’s a great thing when a player steps onto the pitch and think that the crowd is with them.

“That gives you a boost of confidence for a start – and to know that you can maybe make a couple of mistakes and might be forgiven.
