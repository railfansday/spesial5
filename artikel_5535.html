Jennifer Lopez Celebrates ‘Freedom’ as an Empty Nester Since Her Twins Left California Home for College

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Jennifer Lopez has admitted that she is enjoying the "freedom" that comes with living alone after her 18-year-old twins, Max and Emme, left their California home for college.

The 57-year-old singer and actress—who is attempting to sell her and ex-husband Ben Affleck's $50 million marital mansion—once again opened up about the realities of being an empty nester while appearing on "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon."

The singer, who shares the twins with ex-husband Marc Anthony, admitted that she "cried for six months straight" when her twins headed off to college.

Although Lopez confessed it has been "emotional," she insisted it has also been an "amazing" journey.

“It’s all the feelings. It’s like one of those moments in life where you’re just, you’re sad, you’re melancholy, but you’re excited and you’re hopeful—there’s just so many feelings that go with [it]. I cried for, like, six months straight, like, two months before they graduated until I dropped them off and then a few weeks after that," she said.

“It’s crazy ... like, I still have on my phone them on my lap, as babies," she added. "It goes so fast, just like everybody says.”

The A-lister admitted that she is now getting comfortable with the "freedom" she has living alone.

“It’s the first [time] since I was, I don’t know, in my early 20s that I haven’t been with someone, had my kids. I’m alone, I’m free—I can do whatever the f--- I want! And it’s kind of amazing, but also weird," she joked.

In October, Lopez said she was channeling that “devastation” into her music.

Speaking to Variety, the hitmaker revealed that living alone has inspired a burst of songwriting, producing material she believes could become some of her biggest hits.

“They take up such a big part of your brain for so long, and when they’re gone, which is devastating and sad and scary, you’re facing a life that’s totally different now, and you’re like, ‘What am I going to do with this space and this time?’" the hitmaker said. "For me, I just went back in the studio. I’ve written some of the best songs that I’ve ever written, and I’m really excited.

“It’s a very happy-era album. It’s some of the most joyous, beautiful songs that have just a tad of a tear in them," she added.

Her comments come mere weeks after she got candid about her kids going off to college in an emotional social video.

"My kids are going away to college," she said while going through her morning skincare regimen.

The "On the Floor" singer revealed that she made a point of soaking up as much time as possible with Max and Emme over the summer, including traveling with them before they began their new lives away from home.

"I was thinking about this summer and what a great summer it was. We got to go away together. I spent as much time with them as I could," she recalled.

Lopez revealed she was facing the reality of returning to a much quieter home—and admitted that it hasn't been easy.

"When they go away and I'm sitting here being an empty nester, and crying and thinking about them and looking at baby pictures of them and all of that stuff, these routines are the things that are going to keep me kind of sane," she shared.

For Lopez, those routines include exercising, taking care of her skin, and making an effort to prioritize herself as she navigates the newfound free time that comes with having two college-age children.

"Working out, doing my skincare routine, caring for myself, with the newfound kind of space that's in your life," she said.

But the superstar also acknowledged that the milestone comes with conflicting emotions. While she is thrilled to watch Max and Emme begin their adult lives, their departure also marks a major change for her.

"You're so excited for them to step into this new era of their life, but it's also, we have to remember, it's a new era of our lives, too," Lopez explained.

She encouraged other parents experiencing a similar transition to look at the change as an opportunity to reconnect with interests or ambitions that may have taken a back seat while raising children.

"Maybe it's time to revive some old dreams that you had," she suggested, adding that parents might return to something they "put aside during the time that you were raising your kids" or simply pursue activities they previously didn't have time for.

Still, Lopez didn't shy away from admitting that embracing such a significant lifestyle change can be daunting.

"It's a scary time. It's a transitional time. It's a new beginning, in a lot of ways, for them, certainly, but also for you," she said.

She ended her message on an optimistic note, urging others entering the empty-nest phase to continue looking after themselves and finding purpose in their new routines.

"Keep your routines going. Stay hydrated. Stay happy, stay joyful, stay productive," she said. Lopez has been preparing for the milestone for months.

Earlier this year, she revealed during an interview with "Extra" that both Max and Emme had been accepted into all five colleges they applied to, with each also receiving a scholarship offer.

The proud mom also spoke about how hard the twins had worked to reach that point, explaining that both have ADHD and have had to find approaches to learning that work for them.

Lopez also made sure to carve out plenty of time with her children before their departure. Over the summer, she documented a road trip through Italy with Max and Emme.
