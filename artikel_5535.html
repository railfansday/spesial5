Polymarket's annualized revenue has topped $1.8 billion

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The prediction market's annual revenue run rate was above $1 billion in June, after it opened its U.S. exchange

Thomas Fuller / SOPA Images / LightRocket / Getty Images

Polymarket told CNBC on Tuesday that its annualized revenue has surpassed $1.8 billion, up from a run rate above $1 billion in June.

The growth is coming from Polymarket's U.S. exchange, a standalone marketplace that runs independently of the company's international DeFi platform, according to CNBC. In October, the domestic exchange processed notional volume of more than $14.5 billion, compared with $4 billion in June, according to CNBC, which cited TickerTracker data.

CNBC credited the jump in volume to high-traffic events such as the summer's FIFA World Cup and the NFL season kickoff in September. Combo event contracts, which operate much like sportsbook parlays that bundle several legs together, made up over 70% of the platform's trading activity on Sunday, according to CNBC.

Polymarket has started raising $1 billion in new funding, a round that puts its valuation at $21 billion, according to CNBC.

Polymarket had said in June that its annualized revenue had surpassed $1 billion, six weeks after its U.S. exchange opened to the public. Access widened in mid-May when the company removed the waitlist for mobile app users. The U.S. exchange had been in a restricted period since its December debut.

The company faces regulatory and political questions, according to CNBC. The Commodity Futures Trading Commission is running a wide-ranging investigation of Polymarket, examining its advertising practices and whether it breached an earlier settlement requiring it to keep U.S. residents off its international exchange.

Polymarket could also draw heavier scrutiny under a Democratic Congress, in part because Donald Trump Jr., the son of President Donald Trump, advises the company, according to CNBC.

A Polymarket spokesperson told CNBC the company intends to cooperate with legislators while they get up to speed on the prediction market industry. "We welcome scrutiny," the spokesperson said.
