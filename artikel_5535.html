U.S. flight cancellations from FAA router outage top 17,000, worst tech-related in 35 years

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The total tops the 16,900 flights Southwest Airlines canceled in 2022, while the FAA and L3Harris investigate the root cause

U.S. airlines canceled more than 17,000 flights from Friday through Sunday after the Federal Aviation Administration’s nationwide ground stop, the largest total for a technology-related disruption in 35 years, according to Bloomberg. The count passes the 16,900 flights Southwest Airlines canceled during its 2022 systems meltdown.

The FAA said it issued the ground stop at about 3:30 p.m. ET on Nov. 13 after a technical problem, and air traffic resumed at 8:15 p.m. that day. The agency said operators canceled approximately 12,462 flights on Nov. 13, including 9,082 by major airlines. It said not all cancellations were directly caused by the ground stop.

The FAA said its legacy equipment became unstable when data flooded its network at a rate of 1,000 connection attempts every 4 to 5 seconds. The initial investigation points to a radar site in Amarillo, Texas, and a radio communications site in El Dorado, Arkansas. L3Harris, the FAA's network service provider, had been working at the El Dorado site, including deploying a new router. The FAA said it has no indication of a cyberattack, and it and L3Harris are still investigating the root cause.

Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said at a press conference with FAA Administrator Bryan Bedford that officials have not determined whether contractor error or a faulty router caused the flood. Duffy said that as crews shifted from copper wire to fiber, the router being replaced started inundating the rest of the routers on the air traffic control network.

Cancellations dropped on Sunday, with more than 2,220 scrubbed flights at 11:15 p.m. ET, according to Bloomberg, citing FlightAware data. More than 10,100 flights were delayed. Delta Air Lines and Alaska Air Group both said they are counting on operations returning closer to normal, or on far fewer scrapped flights, on Monday.

American Airlines Group had the most cancellations, with more than 5,300 since Friday, according to Bloomberg, citing Cirium. JetBlue Airways suffered the steepest disruption relative to the size of its schedule.

President Donald Trump on Sunday blamed the Biden administration for an "old and broken" FAA system and urged Congress to provide money. Duffy said Congress gave the department a partial payment of $12.5 billion for air traffic control modernization.

Bedford said the router replacement was routine maintenance of the existing telecommunications network and not part of that modernization effort. He said the FAA spends $1 billion a year maintaining the network.

Nationwide ground stops are rare. The previous two followed the Sept. 11 attacks and a brief FAA computer failure in January 2023.
