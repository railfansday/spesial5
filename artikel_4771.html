The 10 Best Face Washes for Men in 2026, Tested by Grooming Editors

EVERY GOOD SKINCARE routine starts with the same thing—a quality face wash. Before you layer on serums, moisturizers, and eye creams, you need to clear the canvas. A face wash does exactly that by clearing away dirt and grime that accumulate on your skin while also helping prevent clogged pores that can trigger breakouts. That’s why it’s crucial to find the best face wash for you, no matter your skin type.

Wash your face every morning, night, and after heavy workouts to remove bacteria and sebum before they build up, says dermatologistDr. Anthony Rossi, MD. The best face washes for men go beyond cleaning: they hydrate, fight acne, and control excess oil. This first step in your routine is especially important if you live an active lifestyle. While cardio workouts boost circulation and support collagen production, sweat can leave your skin vulnerable to breakouts and irritation.

With so many products out there, finding the right one for your skin type and preferences can feel impossible. That’s why I spent the last two years testing every cleanser I could get my hands on to find the ten best face washes for virtually any skin type and price point.

A face wash is the cornerstone of any skincare routine—without it, the rest of your products won’t work as well. Choose one that’s gentle yet effective because even if your entire routine is just washing your face, you don’t want a cleanser that strips away your skin’s natural oils.

Matching your face wash to your skin type ensures it works with your skin’s natural needs, clears and balances your complexion, and primes your face for the products that follow.

We’ve been testing face washes and cleansers since this story was first published in 2021. As Men's Health's Grooming Editor, I’m also constantly trying out the latest releases to determine which are worth adding to your rotation. For this update, I considered picks from previous iterations of this roundup, as well as current favorites from fellow Men's Health writers and editors. This ultimately led to me testing over 50 face washes, evaluating their ingredients, feel, consistency, and effectiveness. Below is the culmination of this extensive hands-on research: the ten best face washes for men with any skin type or budget.

Formulated with hydrating glycerin and squalane—and free of parabens and fragrances—it thoroughly cleanses without stripping moisture. Lightweight, gentle, moisturizing, and nourishing, it’s one of the most versatile cleansers I’ve tried—perfect for any skin type. At $16 for 2.5 oz, it isn’t the cheapest option, but it’s still on the affordable side of the market. Because it foams so well, I only need a small amount for a full wash, and one container lasts me about two months with daily use. Overall, Kiehl's Ultra Facial Cleanser remains my go-to everyday face wash, no matter your skin type or budget.

Wash your face twice a day—morning and night. Just as important, pay attention to the products you use, avoiding harsh chemicals like sulfates and parabens. Choose a gentle cleanser for everyday use and keep an exfoliating scrub for two or three times a week. Apply the scrub with light pressure—fast, hard, and rough isn’t the answer. Overdoing it can damage your skin and create new problems. And always remember: everyone’s skin type is different. What works for one person might not work for another.
