Nikocado Avocado fears lifelong pain after surgery leaves him feeling “burned alive”

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

YouTuber Nikocado Avocado has hit back at viewers who believe he deserves to suffer from potentially lifelong nerve damage because of how he made money on YouTube.

Nikocado Avocado, whose real name is Nicholas Perry, famously built a YouTube empire through his excessive mukbangs and outrageous on-camera meltdowns before revealing his 250-pound weight loss in September 2024.

However, his physical transformation has come with complications. Earlier in November 2026, Perry revealed that a skin removal surgery had allegedly caused nerve damage, leaving him in constant agony.

In a new YouTube video, the influencer revealed that his neurologist had given him a 50/50 chance of the pain being permanent.

“It feels like I’m being burned alive every day nonstop,” he said. “And it’s been something that I’ve been going through for months.”

Perry took issue with comments suggesting his suffering was deserved because of the content he made throughout his YouTube career.

“Do I deserve to have lifelong chronic pain that prevents me from sleeping and having a normal life? Because of the way I made money on the internet,” he questioned.

“Because I bounced around and gained weight and acted like a silly idiot, because I cracked jokes, that this is a fitting punishment? I would never wish this on my worst enemy.”

The YouTuber also revealed that he had been planning to surprise fans by performing on Broadway and returning to his roots as a violinist.

However, the pain in his thumb and index finger has left him unable to even hold his instrument, putting his music career and upcoming content projects on hold.

Perry stressed that he wasn’t looking to blame his surgeon, explaining that the doctor had been supportive throughout his ordeal. Instead, he hoped someone watching the video with expertise in neurology could offer a new perspective.

The creator said that the money wasn’t worth all the pain he’s going through now.

“This is my number one biggest regret of my entire life,” he admitted. “I was trying to fix one complication and I got a worse complication.”

“I feel that something from my life has been taken away from me. I am devastated.”
