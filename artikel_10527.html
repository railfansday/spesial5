How Women’s Super League Football rebranded—and scored

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The English soccer organization previously known as the Women’s Professional Leagues Limited announced a rebrand last year to Women’s Super League Football.

Founded in 2011, the league had used the same visual language as English football’s crown jewel, the Premier League, but that had become an issue: It conveyed that the women’s game was merely a subset of the men’s.

“They were exactly the same,” says Ruth Hooper, WSL Football’s chief marketing officer. “The same colors, pretty much the same fonts, the same art direction and composition.”

Creative agency Anomaly, which led the effort, turned motion-capture data gathered by EA Sports tracking how female athletes actually move into graphics evoking players in motion. Typography featured strong slants.

The rebrand builds a distinctive visual identity and hits at a moment of explosive growth: WSL Football said in January that it tripled its revenue in two years, and it added two new teams for the 2026–27 season.

WSL Football’s new visual identity has been seen widely; the league generated a record 669 million social video views during the 2025–26 season. Meanwhile, the league featured heavily in season 4 of Ted Lasso: Jason Sudeikis’s titular character coaches the fictional Lady Greyhounds, the newest WSL2 club.
