What Architecture Brings Together: Encounters at Open House Valencia 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Architectural mediation is also a form of spatial and urban production. Open House Valencia offers a way of understanding it as a relational practice capable of introducing new dynamics into the everyday structure of the city. Its urban dimension takes shape through a temporary infrastructure that redistributes attention, access and urban rhythms, bringing together spaces that usually remain outside the scope of shared experience.

Architectural mediation is also a form of spatial and urban production. Open House Valencia offers a way of understanding it as a relational practice capable of introducing new dynamics into the everyday structure of the city. Its urban dimension takes shape through a temporary infrastructure that redistributes attention, access and urban rhythms, bringing together spaces that usually remain outside the scope of shared experience.

Through this infrastructure, buildings enter into a wider network of relationships shaped by different temporalities, scales and forms of urban life. Architecture enters circulation as knowledge, memory and experience; buildings acquire new positions within a network of urban references, while collective attention shifts towards spaces, histories and relationships revealing connections that may otherwise remain dispersed across the urban experience.
