'Big Short' investor warned AI cloud stocks are in trouble after Nvidia turmoil

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Steve Eisman said he thinks neoclouds are part of Nvidia's "vertical holding company" and are about to get ejected from it

Photo Illustration by Matteo Della Torre/NurPhoto via Getty Images

Steve Eisman, the investor known for "The Big Short," said on CNBC on Tuesday that the Nvidia $NVDA scandal now unfolding will hit AI neocloud stocks harder than any others.

Eisman described Nvidia as a "virtual vertical holding company and it's unwinding" during an appearance on "Squawk Box." "I think neoclouds are all in big trouble though because they are part of that vertical holding company and I think they're about to get ejected," he said.

Neoclouds borrowed money to build data centers. CoreWeave stock and Nebius stock have each fallen more than 50% over the past three months.

Nvidia founder and CEO Jensen Huang stepped down last Thursday after a controversy erupted over an affair with a woman suspected of spying for China, according to CNBC. The Commerce Department has since acted to end a loophole that let China access data centers remotely, and Nvidia is due to report fiscal third-quarter earnings after the close on Tuesday, Nov. 17.

Anthropic is expected to go public on Thursday, according to CNBC. Eisman said the offering has lost its significance for the AI trade now that investor hopes for it have cooled. "I don't think anybody really cares," he said.

Eisman said he is focused instead on how severely Democrats will limit data center construction after they assume power in Congress and in a number of governors' offices in January.

"I think the biggest issues by far are how much are, how much are local and state governments going to slow AI data center construction? Is it going to be 100% moratorium? That would obviously be — that would put the country into recession overnight. Or is it something like 10% to 30%?" he said.

If construction slows by 10% to 30%, Eisman said, hyperscalers would fare best among AI-trade companies. "The group that makes out the best are the hyperscalers because they go cash flow positive again," he said, according to CNBC.

Eisman had said on Oct. 29 that he had cut risk in his own portfolio because he sees the AI trade nearing a tipping point. He said then that he had shorted against a number of his positions but was not net short any of them.

Elon Musk, the CEO of SpaceX and Tesla $TSLA, predicted on Oct. 25 that the more than 200 neocloud companies will see "guppy-level death and consolidation." Nvidia holds stakes in a number of neoclouds, and Huang had said the week before that demand for them is "off the charts."
