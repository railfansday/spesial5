My Packing Style: Tastemaker TyLynn Graham

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

When it comes to mastering the art of the travel wardrobe, it’s no surprise that our favorite fashion insiders also moonlight as packing pros. In My Packing Style, we tap some of the most well-traveled people we know for a peek inside their travel bags and vacation wardrobes to see how they take their style on the road. You’ve bookmarked the trip, you’ve screenshotted the outfits; now, a look at how it all comes together.

For those on the East Coast, there are still a couple precious weeks of fall to take advantage of before winter hits. The apple orchards are pouring the last of the hot cider, and leaf-peeping season is at its tail-end. For TyLynn Graham, tastemaker and curator of Vette storefront TyLynn.shop, this means one thing: It’s time to hit the road for a cozy weekend in Upstate New York.

“For a road trip, my packing strategy is different because I don’t have to worry about luggage restrictions,” says Graham of her planning process—though she still plots her outfits ahead of time, the key, she says, to stress-free travel days getting ready in the morning. “I also keep a few essentials, like a scarf, my beauty bag, and an extra sweater, within reach in the car. There’s something really lovely about having the freedom to bring a little more of home with you.”

Her fall travel uniform is as cozy as you might expect. There’s flannel, of course, alongside other tactile materials like corduroy and wool; a palette of browns and oatmeal hues feels especially apropos for the destination. “It was the perfect place to spend a few days in November,” she adds.

Ahead, here’s everything Graham packed for a long fall weekend Upstate—along with her tips for creating easy outfit formulas on the road.

I start brainstorming and visualizing what I want to wear about a week before a trip, but I pack the night before. Everything stays in my closet until then—I write down my looks in a notebook, so the packing starts on paper.

After everything is in the notebook, I do pack in outfits. I gravitate toward natural tones—black, white, cream, and navy—with good patterns and plaids mixed in. That palette helps everything feel cohesive while still leaving me room to play. Because this was a road trip, I had the luxury of bringing a few extra things; I still planned my looks, but found room for another sweater or jacket.

For this trip, I gravitated toward Guest in Residence, Agolde, and Jeanerica. Their knits, denim, and easy layers felt right for a few autumn days in the countryside.

For this road trip, I brought my REI Co-op Trailgate Gear Bin. It fits everything! I love being able to bring the extra layers and familiar things I want for a few days away. I also brought my one-of-a-kind Bode equestrian bag—the equestrian references fit the feeling of this trip beautifully.

Jeans, a tee, a great sweater, and good boots. Simple and comfortable, but still put together. For this trip, I brought my Blundstones—perfect with denim, corduroy, and all the cozy layers.

My Louis Vuitton makeup bag always comes with me, whether I’m flying or driving. It keeps my beauty essentials together and brings a little of my home routine with me wherever I’m staying.

Both, actually. I love a good sweater and a scarf—I’m always happy to have an extra layer. For this trip, I packed my Toteme scarf along with plenty of knitwear.

Lesse face spray—it’s a staple in my travel beauty bag.

I keep accessories pretty minimal. My Tiffany & Co. watch is my go-to for every day, so it comes with me everywhere. I also brought a black leather belt with silver hardware that I know works well with both jeans and corduroy pants.

No—I gravitate toward fabrics that need very little maintenance. I like being able to unpack, get dressed, and enjoy wherever I am without too much fuss.

My Blundstone boots! They’re the pair I reach for for a few days in the countryside. I love them with corduroy, a plaid shirt, and an oversized sweater.

My Toteme scarf. I wear it around my head or neck, either draped open or tied in a knot. It’s really beautiful, and changing how I wear it gives the same outfit a different feeling. I love it with a simple tee and sweater or tucked into a jacket when it’s particularly cold.

I like to leave a little room for things I find along the way—plus, a road trip gives me a bit more freedom to bring something home.

Bring the things you always wear. For me, that means familiar denim, a white tee or henley, a favorite sweater, and good boots; pieces that make it easy to get dressed in combinations that already feel like me.
