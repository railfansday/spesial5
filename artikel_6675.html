The Official<b><strong> </strong></b>Guide To Last-Minute Friendsgiving Appetizers, Based On How Much Time You Have

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Consider us your official party-planning committee.

The holiday season is one of the busiest times of the year, so it’s no surprise that Friendsgiving can sneak up on you. If you put your name down to bring an appetizer and put off the planning until crunch time, don’t worry, last-minute appetizers are kind of our thing. Whether you’ve got days to plan ahead or minutes to whip together a party-worthy app, we’ve got you covered with these 10 Friendsgiving appetizers to bring to the party, based on how much time you have. Plus, these apps are so impressive, nobody will ever realize you almost showed up empty-handed.

Looking for more Friendsgiving recipes? Check out the rest of our last-minute Friendsgiving recipes and our top-rated Friendsgiving recipes.

You Want To Get A Jump Start The Night Before: Turkey Cheese Ball

This cheese ball is not only the most eye-catching appetizer you can add to your holiday menu, but it’s surprisingly easy to create. You’ll just have let it chill for about 3 hours in the refrigerator.

You Woke Up Early & Have Plenty Of Time: Sweet Potato Rolls

These rolls use sweet potatoes to make them even softer and fluffier, similar to our classic potato rolls. The tops are brushed with a honey butter and sprinkled with salt to play up both the savory and sweet side of a sweet potato.

You Still Have A Couple Hours To Spare: Cranberry Walnut Cheese Log

Salty, crunchy, and sometimes sweet, this pre-dinner treat is easy and infinitely adaptable for any season and occasion. Here, dried sweetened cranberries, walnuts, and fresh thyme conjure a holiday feeling, while a little honey complements the sweet-tart fruit.

You’re Still In Your Comfy Clothes: Green Bean Casserole Bites

Baked in crescent roll cups with crispy fried onions, a rich, creamy mushroom sauce, and tender-yet-snappy green beans, these handheld wonders hit every note. Have them as an appetizer or even on your plate as a biscuit substitute!

You’re Feeling Good With An Hour To Go: Sausage Stuffing Bites With Cranberry Sauce

Baked stuffing, inside the turkey or out, is a Thanksgiving staple for many families, although the particulars of the go-to recipe can vary. The only thing better than any of these stuffing recipes? MORE. STUFFING. Enter a bite-sized, stuffing-centered appetizer with a tangy, three-ingredient dipping sauce.

You’re Starting To Keep An Eye On The Clock: Brie & Green Bean Bundles

Perfect for a Thanksgiving potluck, these bundles of joy turn the classic green bean casserole into a handheld delight, all wrapped in flaky crescent dough. Wherever you choose to serve them, we guarantee one thing—these flaky and cheesy apps will go fast.

You Just Got A Last-Minute Text From The Host, Asking For Another App: Pumpkin Spice Hot Honey Brie Bites

Sweet and savory with a kick of heat, these pumpkin, Brie, and hot honey bites are the perfect fall appetizer. The honey provides the perfect balance for the sweetness of the pumpkin and the savory, melty Brie. Add a bit of fresh thyme and some roasted pecans, and you’ve got yourself a certified crowd-pleaser.

You’ve Got 30 Minutes To Make Some Magic Happen: Turkuterie

This Friendsgiving, keep your kitchen free of filching fingers by creating the most adorable charcuterie—err, turkuterie—board your friends and family will have ever seen. We’ve packed this cheese board with fruits and cheeses appropriate for an autumnal snack that everyone will want to gobble up!

You’ve Got One Shoe On Already: Cranberry Whipped Feta Dip

A holiday cheeseboard is great and all, but why go to all the trouble when you can make this sweet, salty, creamy, and tart dip with a fraction of the time and effort Fresh feta cheese is combined with herbs, garlic, and cream cheese, whipped until super-smooth, then topped with tart homemade cranberry sauce, toasted pistachios, and honey.

Oh No, Weren’t You Supposed To Bring Something?!: Antipasto Bites

When we need a last-minute appetizer for any party, we always turn to these antipasto skewers. They're ready in just 15 minutes, are perfectly shareable, and have all of the varied flavors and textures of an antipasto spread.
