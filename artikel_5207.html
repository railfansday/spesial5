Anthropic Stock Trails the SpaceX IPO: ANT Up 6% in Three Days vs SPCX’s 57%

Anthropic stock closed its third trading day on Monday, November 23, at $87.64, just 5.6% above its $83 IPO price. SpaceX’s shares (SPCX) were 56.6% above their IPO price at the same point in June.

The two debuts started out alike. Both closed their first day up 17% to 19%. Then they split. SpaceX kept climbing for two more sessions, while ANT slid from a $111.50 open to $87.64.

Monday showed the gap clearly. The S&P 500 jumped 4.7% and the Nasdaq Composite 6.5% after Nvidia brought back Jensen Huang, yet Anthropic stock fell 1.4%. In pre-market trading on Tuesday, it was near $85, about 2% above the IPO price.

Wall Street now heads into a short Thanksgiving week. So did Anthropic’s IPO really do worse than SpaceX’s, and will the holidays help or hurt?

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 on November 18 and raised a record $81.3 billion, above SpaceX’s $75 billion. We lined up each stock’s daily closes against its IPO price, session by session.

The market helped SpaceX. The S&P 500 rose 1.6% over its first three sessions. Over Anthropic’s three, it fell 1.2%, with a 3.7% drop on Friday.

So, yes, Anthropic stock has done much worse so far. But SpaceX’s lead did not last. Its shares fell below the $135 IPO price on July 16, their 23rd session. They hit a low close of $108.27 on August 5 and closed Monday at $133.35, slightly under the IPO price.

US stock markets are closed on Thursday, November 26, and close early, at 1 p.m. ET, on Friday. Fewer traders usually means thinner trading and bigger swings.

Seasonally, the holidays tend to be kind to stocks. We checked the S&P 500 every year since 1990:

The pattern has weakened lately. December rose in only 6 of the last 10 years, with an average gain near zero. The worst case was 2018, when stocks were already selling off in November and fell another 5.1% to year-end.

ANT has no holiday history yet, so SpaceX is the best guide. Its first market holiday was Juneteenth, on Friday, June 19. When trading resumed on Monday, June 22, SPCX fell 16.4%, still its worst day as a public company.

That day also fell 10 days after the listing. Under FINRA Rule 2241, that is when the banks that sold an IPO can start publishing research on it. For Anthropic, that window opens around Monday, November 30, the first session after Thanksgiving.

Trading in ANT is already thinning out. Volume fell from 857 million shares on day one to 254 million on Monday. A half-day session on Friday could mean sharper moves on less news.

Anthropic stock trades 24 hours a day as a perpetual futures contract on Hyperliquid, a crypto exchange. On the hourly chart, xyz:ANT sits at $84.61, right on support and below its 50-hour exponential moving average (EMA), an average that gives recent prices more weight, at $88.27.

Anthropic stock is losing the race with SpaceX for now, but SpaceX’s lead faded within a month. The short holiday week rarely hurts stocks in general. For ANT, the bigger tests are holding above $83 and the first research from its own banks after Thanksgiving.
