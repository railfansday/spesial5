Gate Launches Taiwan Stock Trading, Initially Supporting Popular Stocks Listed on Taiwan Stock Exchange Including TSMC and MediaTek

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Gate has officially launched Taiwan stock trading, further expanding its global stock trading business. The initial offering includes 19 stocks listed on the Taiwan Stock Exchange (TWSE), covering a range of sectors, including semiconductors, artificial intelligence, electronics manufacturing, telecommunications, financial services, and traditional industries. Featured stocks include TSMC (2330), MediaTek (2454), Hon Hai Precision Industry
