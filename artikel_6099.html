I’ve had first hand reports of this as well – the Trump administration has been refusing ESTAs to former U.S. citizens, requiring a full visa, even when they are citizens of and residing in visa waiver countries. The fact alone that they are ex-citizens keeps them from a smooth visit path, independent of any risk factors, and even if they’ve visited using ESTAs for 15 years.

This is a big deal for anyone thinking about renouncing US citizenship.

Many assume they can keep visiting the US after renunciation on ESTA based on their other citizenship. But there are more and more reports of former citizens being denied ESTA and told to apply for a visitor visa instead.

A visitor visa, unlike ESTA, generally requires a consular interview and proving that the applicant will leave after the visit. With family, businesses, or assets in the US, that could mean delays and refusals. This has to factor into the decision on whether or not to renounce.
