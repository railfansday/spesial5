We had a Jura C9 for many years and were very happy with it. I had it apart a few times to make repairs (parts are readily available online), but it was definitely a bit of a faff to get it all apart and back together again. When it finally died we replaced it with a Z10 and an E4. Of the two, my wife prefers the Z10, but I think it was on the order of 3-4x more expensive than the E4.
