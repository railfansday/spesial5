Solving Decades of Enterprise Fragmentation

"For decades, automotive software has been fragmented, leaving dealerships with disconnected data and workflows," says Albert Thompson, Founder and CEO of IDP. "Adding standalone AI agents to this broken infrastructure only compounds the chaos. Instead of more disjointed tools, IDP's Agent-Native System of Action connects existing investments through a universal execution layer."

From AI Conversations to Completed Business Outcomes

For automotive retailers, the difference between conversational AI and operational execution is particularly visible in service scheduling.

An AI agent may successfully answer a customer's call, but completing the request requires access to customer records, dealership policies, scheduling availability and operational systems.

When those systems remain disconnected, employees must manually complete or verify the work.

"Automotive AI providers often fail to connect customer conversations with execution," Worthington noted. "Our previous platform couldn't schedule appointments or generate tasks, forcing employees to manually review every interaction. Instead of eliminating work, it just created another system for them to babysit."

IDP's approach prioritizes completed business outcomes, including confirmed appointments, coordinated follow-up, exception handling and operational visibility.

Systems of record preserve business information. IDP's System of Action coordinates the work.
