By Elizabeth Koh and Liz Kowalczyk — Boston Globe

Despite all the setbacks, Boston Scientific won’t stop pushing the Watchman.

The medical-device giant committed hundreds of millions to acquire the tiny heart implant more than a decade ago, and then endured a years-long debate at the Food and Drug Administration over whether to approve it.

When that approval finally came through, for a limited pool of patients, Boston Scientific was prepared with a plan for something much larger.
