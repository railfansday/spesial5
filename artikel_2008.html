8 players set to miss Wolves vs Derby County

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Wolverhampton Wanderers could go top of the Championship on Tuesday night when they host Derby County at Molineux, whilst the Rams aim to get a much-needed three points on the road amid a scrap towards the foot of the table.

Wolves have leapfrogged Birmingham City in the Championship to go 3rd, just one point behind Middlesbrough and Stoke City ahead of them, as depending on how Boro get on at home to Swansea City, the Old Gold could go top for the evening.

Cesar Peixoto's side are undefeated in their last seven league games, with their most recent defeat coming at home to Bolton Wanderers, as Derby will hope to follow in the footsteps of the Trotters on Tuesday.

Pressure on John Eustace is increasing, with just two wins in 16 games this season, and with only Preston North End beneath them in the table, a win would be huge for the Rams.

Wolves certainly come into this clash the favourites, but Eustace will do everything in his power to give his Derby side the best possible chance of a result, as Football League World takes a closer look at the absentees of this fixture.

Mexican striker Raul Jimenez is set to miss the rest of the calendar year after going in for knee surgery in mid-October, in a huge blow for Wolves, having re-signed the 35-year-old on a free transfer over the summer.

With three goals and one assist in six Championship games this season, Jimenez has displayed he is still a capable goalscorer despite his age, as Wolves head coach Cesar Peixoto will be eager to see him return to action in the new year, with Adam Armstrong, Sean Neave, and Tawanda Chirewa each vying for a place up top as things stand.

Following Peixoto to Wolves this summer was Ivorian left-back Ghislain Konan, with the 30-year-old adding another option in that area of the pitch following his release from Gil Vicente.

However, after just three appearances in all competitions, the defender has been sidelined until late December after picking up an MCL injury, and will certainly play no role in this clash.

A big blow for Derby, as star attacking midfielder Sammie Szmodics remains on the sidelines until near the end of the year after suffering a knee injury in the Rams' 4-0 home defeat to Norwich City.

Signing a permanent deal at Pride Park in the summer from Ipswich Town after a successful loan spell, Eustace will have to make do without the 31-year-old, with his attacking output a crucial miss for his side.

Danish striker Henrik Meister joined Derby on loan from Italian side Pisa late into the summer window to provide another option up top alongside Carlton Morris and Lars-Jorgen Salvesen.

The 23-year-old, who has yet to really show what he is capable of since arriving at Pride Park, replaced Mohammed Fuseini just on the hour mark in the Rams' 2-0 defeat away to Southampton, but had to be taken off 20 minutes later after injuring his shoulder.

The extent of the injury, at this stage, remains unclear, but he's not expected to feature in this one.

Veteran full-back Craig Forsyth has been out of action since the beginning of September, coming off in the latter stages of Derby's 2-0 away victory to Portsmouth, which remains their only three points on the road this season.

The 37-year-old was expected to be out for at least a couple of months, and now approaching three, it appears as though this setback has taken longer than hoped, as he is not expected to feature in this one.

Joining Derby from Dutch outfit Utrecht in January 2026, English full-back Derry Murkin has yet to make a single appearance this season after suffering an injury back in April.

Further damage to his hamstring during pre-season set him back for a further four months, and upon his return to action, he will be a welcome addition to the side.

Norwegian defender Sondre Langås compiled misery for Derby in their 4-1 away defeat to Queens Park Rangers back in mid-October, suffering a groin injury which keeps him out of action until the new year.

The 25-year-old was taken off just 12 minutes into the clash, and if Derby want to avoid a relegation battle this season, his return to fitness ASAP is of the utmost importance.
