TMW: Stuttgart star Stiller the ‘favourite’ as Krösche begins search for Modric’s successor

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

AC Milan are looking at possible options to come in and replace Luka Modric, with one name emerging as the preference.

As TMW report, Gerry Cardinale is ready to invest further in Milan. The goal is to build an increasingly stronger team, taking advantage of the various transfer windows to grow gradually. The ultimate goal must be to return to fighting for a Scudetto, which the Rossoneri have not won since 2021-22.

The priority remains a central defender, with Gonçalo Inacio in pole position. An investment of at least €40m will be required, assuming the Portuguese club is willing to part with him mid-season. Alternatives include Union Saint-Gilloise’s Sylla, Lens’ Ganiou and Borussia Dortmund’s Reggiani.

However, the report goes on to mention a task that will need to be tackled ahead of next season, given that Modric’s deal is set to expire and it is currently unknown if he will retire.

Milan must think about who the Croatian should hand the reins to, and the ‘favourite’ is VfB Stuttgart star Angelo Stiller. The plan is to acquire a top midfielder who is similar in mould but can still grow.

The task of acquiring him will be entrusted to the new Head of Football Markus Krösche, who will officially be in office starting from January, but in reality is already planning future moves.

He seems gravitated towards a target that Milan’s scouts have had in their sights for at least a couple of seasons anyway, and that is Stiller. The 25-year-old is also a key member of the German national team currently coached by Jurgen Klopp.

The big temptation also comes from the fact he has a €40m release clause, and Krösche knows him well given his past in the Bundesliga. That said, other ideas such as Brighton’s Ayari and Bournemouth’s Scott shouldn’t be overlooked yet either.
