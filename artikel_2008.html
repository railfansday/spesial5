Wolves fans will love how Raul Jimenez reacted to what Adam Armstrong did v Derby

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Raul Jimenez could be seen joining in the celebrations after Adam Armstrong’s excellent volley for Wolves sealed an emphatic 3-0 win over Derby County.

Cesar Peixoto’s side are starting to hit their stride, as the victory against the Rams made it eight unbeaten for Wolves ahead of a weekend trip to struggling Lincoln City.

However, perhaps the most encouraging aspect of the three points against Derby was the performance, as Wolves battered the visitors, recording 39 shots on goal.

Despite the overall dominance, John Eustace’s men actually started brightly at Molineux, as they put the hosts under pressure and had a few chances to take the lead.

But, Wolves were always a threat, and they took the lead when Armstrong slotted home after good work from Mane.

Wanderers missed chance after chance in the second half, but the talented teenager put the game to bed as he got his first goal of the campaign with a side-footed finish.

Armstrong then made it three, which reflected Wolves’ superiority, with a great volley that wowed the home fans - and it also got Jimenez on his feet.

That’s after footage was shared on social media of the Mexican applauding his teammate as he looked very impressed with Armstrong’s effort.

It’s great for Wolves fans to see Jimenez in the stands like a fan even though he is out injured, as it shows what the club means to him.

Of course, his legendary status at Molineux is already secured, but the fact he is out watching the team on a cold night in November means a lot to the supporters, and he has also been present at various club events in recent weeks.

Jimenez and his family have bought into what Wolves and the city are all about, and even though he hasn’t played for months, Peixoto was keen to point out the influence that the striker has had on the dressing room even in his absence.

And, seeing his reaction to Armstrong’s goal, it is an example of a team with a good spirit and togetherness, which will be crucial if Wolves are going to win promotion.

Following on from that, Wolves will feel they are starting to click into gear as they chase an immediate return to the Premier League, especially when you consider they will have Jimenez back in January.

Even though they’ve been very good in terms of their points return all season, the win over Derby felt like a statement one in the sense that they really looked devastating going forward for the first time in a while.

Armstrong has been key to that with his movement, intelligence and excellent finishing, but the prospect of adding Jimenez to this team is one that should worry Wolves’ promotion rivals.
