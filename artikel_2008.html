Nick Woltemade was left out of the Juventus starting eleven on Sunday.

After a woeful goalless opening half Juventus were booed off the pitch.

Nick Woltemade introduced for the start of the second half and the rest is history.

Within 14 minutes Juventus had the game won.

Nick Woltemade scoring the opening goal within eight minutes of entering the pitch.

It was then 2-0 within 14 minutes as the Newcastle United loan star got the assist for Teun Koopmeiners.

Juan Cabal a third goal, before then Nick Woltemade passed up the chance of scoring his second when he handed the ball to fellow striker Randal Kolo Muani to score from the penalty spot.

A goalless first half shocker transformed into a thumping second half 4-0 Juventus triumph.

Supersub Nick Woltemade handed the man of the match trophy after the final whistle.

When Sports Dunia reported on the match. they waxed lyrical about the contribution of Nick Woltemade: ‘Came on and instantly changed the game. His close-range finish for the opener broke Venezia’s resistance and shifted all momentum. We noticed his intelligent movement between lines, dragging defenders out of shape. He also assisted Koopmeiners, showing composure in the box. From our perspective, a superb super-sub performance.’

Nick Woltemade has still only started seven games in all competitions for Juventus but now has eight direct goal contributions, scoring five and getting three assists.
