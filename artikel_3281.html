Apple is building an AI agent that codes and controls apps, Bloomberg reports

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A job listing describes an Apple AI agent that browses the web, writes code and works across apps, with a launch eyed for late 2027. Rival agents now face extra steps on the Mac.

Apple is developing its own autonomous AI agent, Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman reported on Thursday. It would write code, browse the web, control apps and move around devices for users. The report is based on job listings Apple added to its careers site this week.

One listing seeks a senior engineering manager for a proactive, agentic AI assistant. Apple has eyed a launch as soon as the end of 2027, according to Bloomberg. Development is still at an early stage.

“Imagine an AI agent that can use a computer the way a capable person does,” the listing reads.

The agents would plan and carry out multistep tasks across apps, browsers, command lines, code and APIs. They could also coordinate specialised subagents, the listing says. It also names “general-purpose computer-use agents”. That kind of tool already lets ChatGPT and Meta’s Muse operate a Mac.

Agents that run code, use credentials and act for users need “strong permission models, sandboxing and transparency”, it adds.

Apple appears to have pulled the listing, Digital Trends reported. A screenshot shared on X matches its role number and its 17 November posting date. The role did not appear in a search of Apple’s careers site on Friday. The hire would work with Apple’s Siri, UI Frameworks and Apple Foundation Models teams, Digital Trends reported.

Apple’s new chief executive, John Ternus, has been pressing Craig Federighi to move faster on AI. An agent is central to that push, according to Bloomberg.

Apple is also making it harder for other companies’ agents to control a Mac. In the macOS 27.3 beta, turning on ChatGPT’s computer-use support takes 14 steps, up from four, Bloomberg reported. New warning screens ask users to reconsider granting third-party agents that control.

Apple presents the change as a way to protect users. Some AI companies and users see it as an attempt to slow competitors, according to Bloomberg. In October, Apple said AI agents were the reason for new Full Disk Access controls on Macs.

OpenAI launched its Dots agents in September, and Anthropic previewed Duet in October. Gemini on the Mac gained computer use on Thursday.

Meta shares fell nearly 2% and Anthropic’s more than 3% in late trading after the report, Bloomberg said.
