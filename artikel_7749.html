Four Law Enforcement Agencies in Arizona to Receive DOJ Funding to Hire Officers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

WASHINGTON — Through the Office of Community Oriented Policing Services (COPS Office), the Department of Justice yesterday announced it has awarded nearly $150 million under the 2026 COPS Hiring Program (CHP). CHP is a competitive grant program that provides funding directly to state, local, tribal, and territorial law enforcement agencies to pay for the entry-level salaries and fringe benefits of newly hired and rehired full-time sworn officers/deputies to make America safe again.
