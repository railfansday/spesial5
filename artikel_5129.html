Bitcoin has entered a bearish phase again, with weakening demand and broken technical support levels raising the risk of further declines, according to Julio Moreno. In a November 19 post on X, Moreno said both spot and speculative demand have contracted sharply since late October, while Bitcoin has lost nearly all major on-chain and technical support levels. Market data showed BTC trading around $62,853, down 2.7% over 24 hours and 15.1% over seven days. Moreno added that market sentiment among the research firms he tracks has turned the most bearish since November 2025. Although some long-term holders have started realising substantial losses, he warned that additional selling pressure remains possible. He also said that Bitcoin has entered a historical value zone, but previous market bottoms have taken months to develop, meaning short-term rebounds may not signal a sustained recovery.
