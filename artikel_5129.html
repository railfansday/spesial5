Flop Labs Pushes Back Testnet Launch Amid Final Preparations

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Flop Labs has announced a minor delay to the launch of its public testnet, pushing back from its previous goal of 15:00 (UTC+8) on November 24.

It's unclear what the reason for the delay is, although the team does expect to launch the testnet before 18:00 (UTC+8) the same day.

Possible reasons for the delay could include conducting final checks, calibrating nodes, validator onboarding, load testing, etc. (although Flop Labs has not confirmed any of these to be the reason for the delay).

It does not appear that Flop Labs has any plans to move the testnet launch to another day.

Flop Labs is building FLOP Network, a blockchain serving as a settlement layer for what it calls the "agentic economy". This would enable AI agents to make payments for computation (e.g. model inference) and memory services, and provide rewards to GPU miners that contribute their computational power to fulfill these requests.

Computation is verified via Flop Labs' "Proof of Useful Inference", which entails miners providing evidence that they've carried out the desired AI computation, and validators checking this evidence.

Why does this matter? FLOP Network would enable agents to make payments for computation, memory, etc. Flop Labs' main competitors include Akash Network and io.net (both of which facilitate access to compute/gpu resources in a decentralized manner), as well as Render Network (which started out facilitating decentralized graphics rendering but has since expanded into AI/compute more broadly).

While Flop Labs' competitors also facilitate access to compute, Flop Labs' main differentiating factor appears to be its focus on facilitating agent-to-agent commerce and computation-verification.

Flop Labs is led by Arthur Hayes, co-founder of crypto exchange BitMEX and current chief investment officer at Maelstrom. The rest of Flop Labs' team includes Sergey Vidyuk (ex-Deutsche Bank/BitMEX) as CTO, and Shu Duan (with experience in investment banking and crypto asset management) as chief strategy officer.

The testnet would be an opportunity to see if Flop Labs can successfully build out its envisioned agentic economy.
