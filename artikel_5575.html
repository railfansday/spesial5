HaystackID launches Trade Fraud Investigation Discovery Services as federal enforcement intensifies

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Defensible discovery offering helps corporations and outside counsel respond to government requests, grand jury subpoenas and internal reviews as trade fraud exposure converges from several directions

CHICAGO, Nov. 10, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- HaystackIDÂ®, a trusted partner focused on managing complex data and workflow challenges in legal, compliance, regulatory and cybersecurity environments, today announced the launch of HaystackID Trade Fraud Investigation Discovery Services. The offering helps corporations and their law firms identify, preserve, analyze, review and produce complete and defensible evidence in response to civil investigative demands, grand jury subpoenas and qui tam matters, and during internal investigations of potential trade fraud.

Trade fraud has become a top civil and criminal enforcement priority for the U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) and its partners at the U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS), including U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) and Homeland Security Investigations. DOJ and DHS launched the cross-agency Trade Fraud Task Force in August 2025, and on July 14, DOJ reported that the task force had surpassed $1 billion in civil and criminal recoveries, penalties, forfeitures and publicly charged losses in less than one year.

Whistleblowers continue to drive False Claims Act (FCA) enforcement. In fiscal year 2025, about 78% of FCA recoveries came from qui tam matters, underscoring the role of whistleblower suits brought by current and former employees, brokers, competitors and other insiders.

On Oct. 1, Assistant Attorney General Colin M. McDonald issued Directive 26-12, "Corporate Enforcement in the Fight Against Fraud," directing prosecutors in the DOJ's National Fraud Enforcement Division to take an "all-tools approach" to the division's health care, government, tax and trade fraud priorities. The directive lists "fraud schemes involving tariff evasion, importation of goods or services, or forced labor" among its corporate enforcement priorities.

Investigations can extend across the supply chain and involve substantial volumes of commercial, customs, financial and communications data. The quality of a company's discovery response can affect its credibility with investigators, the government's assessment of the matter and the terms of any resolution.

"Enforcement agencies are applying data analytics and AI to millions of entry records to find anomalies, and the companies they investigate need the same capability on their side of the table," said Chad Pinson, chief executive officer at HaystackID. "Trade Fraud Investigation Discovery Services brings our forensics, Global Advisory, AI and managed review teams into a single investigation process that gives counsel the findings and the source record behind them."

Scoping, preservation and analysis: Defensible from the start

HaystackID works under the direction of outside counsel, supporting the investigation while helping legal teams maintain appropriate privilege and work-product protections. Each engagement is staffed with Global Advisory experts, project managers, certified forensic examiners and managed review teams, and HaystackID serves as the data hub for the forensic accountants and compliance experts on the matter. At the outset, the team turns the government's request into a data plan and gives counsel validated burden and cost estimates to support scope negotiations with the agencies.
