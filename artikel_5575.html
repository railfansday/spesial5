Mortgage Calculator: Here’s How Much You Need To Buy a $430K Home at a 7.11% Rate

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Mortgage rates continued their slow retreat for a fourth consecutive week, mirroring the trajectory of bond yields, although this week's hotter-than-expected inflation print suggests the relief will be short-lived.

The average rate on 30-year fixed home loans decreased to 7.11% for the week ending Nov. 12, down 7 basis points from 7.18% the week before, according to Freddie Mac. Even with this steady month-long decline, borrowing costs remain significantly higher than they were a year ago, when rates averaged 6.24% during the same period in 2025.

So what does this mean for homebuyers? Using the Realtor.com® mortgage calculator, we can look at how the math works out for the median-priced home in the U.S.

All examples assume a 30-year fixed mortgage and include principal and interest only, excluding property taxes, homeowners insurance, and mortgage insurance.

For a homebuyer eyeing the median house price of $430,000, a 20% down payment results in a loan amount of $344,000.

At today's 7.11% rate, the monthly principal and interest payment is approximately $2,314. This reflects a $16 monthly reduction from the previous week’s payment of $2,330.

Compared to the 6.24% average from November 2025, which required a $2,116 monthly payment for a home at this price, today’s buyers are paying $198 more every single month.

The monthly costs have also risen markedly for those using FHA loans with a 3.5% down payment.

On a $430,000 home, an FHA borrower would finance roughly $414,950. At today’s 7.11% rate, the monthly principal and interest payment comes to approximately $2,791.

This reflects a $20 decrease from last week's monthly cost of $2,811. Compared to the 6.24% rates of November 2025, where the monthly payment for this loan amount sat at $2,552, today’s FHA borrowers are paying an extra $239 in interest every month.

However, looking back at the October 2023 peak of 7.79%, where the payment for a home at this price reached $2,984, today's monthly payment still offers $193 in relative relief.

The long-term financial picture shows how this continued downward drift improves overall borrowing costs over a 30-year term.

A buyer with a 20% down payment at today’s 7.11% rate will pay a total of $833,080 in principal and interest over the life of the mortgage. While current financing costs remain elevated compared to year-ago levels, this total still represents a distinct contrast to the October 2023 peak of 7.79%, when the total cost for that same $344,000 loan would have reached $890,630.

By securing a mortgage at today’s rate instead of that peak, a homebuyer effectively avoids $57,550 in interest charges over the 30-year loan life.

FHA borrowers see a similar trajectory of long-term figures. Financing the current median-priced home at today's 7.11% rate results in a lifetime payment of $1,004,902 for principal and interest. If that same loan had been locked in at the 7.79% peak in late 2023, the total cost would have climbed to $1,074,323. This represents a total long-term savings of $69,421 for FHA buyers.

While stubborn inflation may limit how much further rates can fall in the weeks ahead, this four-week pullback has helped trim monthly expenses for buyers jumping in the late-fall market.
