Opinion - Liverpool must be brave: Reject Barcelona star

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Liverpool have got to be brave. It's time to fully reject a Barcelona star and move on to something new.

Liverpool have some major decisions to make over the next few months. They've got to lock in their transfer targets, not just for January but for next summer, too.

And this is a squad that needs transfers. That's the sad reality of things - there's just too much uncertainty for Liverpool to stick with what they've got, even after spending record-breaking amounts.

Centre-back is a problem, with Virgil van Dijk and Joe Gomez both out of contract. Ronald Araujo is only on loan, too. Jeremie Frimpong doesn't appear to have a future at right-back, either, and Conor Bradley is an unknown quantity post-injury.

Left-back is a difficult one, too. Milos Kerkez is still establishing himself, and Kostas Tsimikas is out of contract. This is the area where difficult decision no.1 can be made, though.

The Alejandro Balde problemLiverpool will need to sign a left-back and they've been consistently linked with Alejandro Balde. The player isn't getting proper game time at Barcelona and is viewed by many clubs as the best possible option on the market. After all, he's still young at just 23, has incredible experience, and his attacking numbers stack up very nicely. However, we're going to argue that this is one Liverpool should pass on. Balde would apparently cost €40m. It's a fairly large amount of money for a left-back - Kerkez is the only player Liverpool have spent more than €15m on in the position.

After all, he's still young at just 23, has incredible experience, and his attacking numbers stack up very nicely. However, we're going to argue that this is one Liverpool should pass on.

Balde would apparently cost €40m. It's a fairly large amount of money for a left-back - Kerkez is the only player Liverpool have spent more than €15m on in the position.

It's especially a large amount of money for someone who isn't even playing regular football at this point. It's also far more than most clubs are actually paying for their left-backs.

Bournemouth, for instance, spent just €14m signing Adrien Truffert to replace Kerkez in 2025. He's now a left-back that everyone wants - but it isn't like he was hiding anywhere. He was one of the best-rated in France.

So given the investment in Kerkez already, it seems a little mad to spend a substantial amount on a new left-back. Surely the risk this time should be on a lower-cost player? Especially as Balde would command risk anyway as a player Barcelona aren't all that keen on.
