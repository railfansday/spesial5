Investing - Theory, News &amp; General • Re: 2026 Hedge Fund Contest

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Post the Anthropic IPO and the big AI selloff following the somewhat unexpected midterm results and their anti-AI sentiment, I thought it would be a good time to see how well our "hedge" fund managers are doing in this violent market downturn. Short version - not well, if they were long! The broad market, as measured by out total market index (VTI) is now down -1%, having been up moderately earlier this year.

Current Standings - 2026 Hedge Fund Contest

Unsurprisingly, short biased funds have taken the lead in the market crash, with Tanelorn in 1st at +100% for his bankruptcy short pick. Random Musings is barely holding 2nd place with +77% ahead of Darth Xanadu's +73%, whose short basket has all big winners ranging from roughly +50% to +90%. Interestingly, both 2nd and 3rd place managers were short Beyond Meat (BYND), the fake meat company. However, while Random Musings cashed out his profits at +77%, Darth Xanadu is still holding and short BYND is his biggest winner as it continues to fall, now -87% YTD.

Looking at the statistics, the average long pick is down -16%, heavily underperforming the less risky market index at -1%, with a range from +130% (DELL) to -93% (MVST, an EV battery company). That corresponds to a negative alpha of ~15% among the long stock picks in aggregate. I attribute this to popular long picks being both more risky, and more likely to be in the previously "hot" AI / tech sectors which have been particularly hard hit in this downturn. The average short pick is up +16%, a considerably better showing with +15% alpha, and probably benefiting from the opposite of those same factors / trends. The shorts chosen range of +100% (Luminar Technologies, LAZRQ, wiped out in bankruptcy) to -476% Moderna (MRNA), the company known for their covid vaccines and has had good trial results applying that technology to cancer treatments.

Good luck with the rest of the year!
