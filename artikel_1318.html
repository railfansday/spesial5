Today’s Papers – Inter, Como and a Scudetto fight

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Inter-Como super in una maxi domenica (Inter-Como headlines a huge Sunday)

The Pink Paper leads on tonight’s clash between Inter and Como at San Siro, with Marcus Thuram and Nico Paz on the front page. Gazzetta describes the game as the strength of champions against a wave of attractive football, and says there’s a sense of a Scudetto race between Cristian Chivu’s side and Cesc Fàbregas’ Lariani.

At the top of the page, Roma a tutto gol, Gasp secondo (Roma go all out for goals, Gasp second) celebrates Saturday’s emphatic win over Sassuolo, which moves Gian Piero Gasperini’s side up to second. Teenager Rodrigo Mora is pictured, and the paper notes that the capital now leads the way in Serie A.

Milan are in Genoa this afternoon, and Ritorna il big Milan (The big Milan return) reports that Ruben Amorim will select his first-choice players, from Luka Modrić to Gila, so the Rossoneri don’t slip up again.

The lunchtime game between Napoli and Lazio is billed as Un duello sottosopra (An upside-down duel), with Gennaro Gattuso top of the table and Aurelio De Laurentiis putting the silencer on Massimiliano Allegri.

Juventus are at Fiorentina this evening, and La Signora vuole il 3 (The Old Lady wants three) says Kerim Alajbegović’s Juve are chasing a third consecutive victory.

Elsewhere, Saturday’s round-up is summed up as Vola il Bologna, cade il Frosinone, Toro frenato dal palo (Bologna fly, Frosinone fall, Toro denied by the woodwork).

In Formula 1, Kimi da 10 (A perfect 10 for Kimi) reports that newly crowned world champion Kimi Antonelli starts on the front row in Brazil as he goes for his 10th win, behind Lando Norris on pole, while the Ferraris have been demoted.
