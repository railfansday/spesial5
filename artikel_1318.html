Newspaper names six players Italy should build around for 2030 World Cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Despite a 4-1 hammering away against Belgium on Sunday evening, Italy have been urged to look for the positives after a largely positive Nations League campaign and La Gazzetta dello Sport has named six players that Roberto Mancini should build around in the years approaching the 2030 World Cup.

Italy were defeated 4-1 in Brussels by Belgium on Sunday night despite opening the scoring after 10 minutes through Pio Esposito. Maxim De Cuyper, Nathan Ngoy and Jeremy Doku scored three goals within 11 minutes in the first half and Milan’s Diego Moreira added a fourth 10 minutes before time.

Despite starting the night at the top of their group, the 4-1 defeat saw both Belgium and France overtake Italy, who finished the group in third place and will not be progressing to the Nations League quarter-finals.

Still, many pundits and analysts have urged Italy to look for the positives after picking up 10 points from their last five matches in a tough group that also contained France and Turkey.

La Gazzetta dello Sport suggests that Italy’s mix of emerging youngsters and experienced core players will stand them in good stead heading into the next few major tournaments.

The Pink Paper wrote that everybody knew that Gianluigi Donnarumma was a top-quality player well before the latest set of Nations League fixtures, but that it is encouraging to now be able to include Pio Esposito in the same category at the other end of the pitch.

La Gazzetta dello Sport suggests that these are the two players that Italy must build their future and upcoming qualification campaigns around, along with the core of Nicolo Barella, Sandro Tonali and Alessandro Bastoni. Michael Kayode also appears a lock-in at right-back for the foreseeable future.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
