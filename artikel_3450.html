Macaulay Culkin Officially Revives Home Alone With New Sequel

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Macaulay Culkin officially revives Home Alone.

Culkin stars in a new sequel to the classic film that comes from one of the most unlikely of places. Culkin's best roles involve a number of TV shows and movies across the board, especially as his career has seen a resurgence in recent years. But his role as Kevin McCallister in the first two Home Alone movies will forever remain a classic part of both his career and cinema history. He's even reprised his role across various ad campaigns, lending his talent to nostalgic approaches to the movie and its cinematic importance.

Now, Collider has confirmed Culkin is back as Kevin McCallister for a McDonald's holiday campaign, advertising a limited-time Home Alone meal. The meal, which arrives in the US on December 1, 2026, consists of either a Sausage, Egg & Cheese McGriddles for breakfast, or a Big Mac for the rest of the day. It comes with movie-inspired Little Nero's Pizza McShaker Fries and a Lovely Cheese Pizza Dipping Sauce, useable for both fries and a customer choice of McNuggets or McCrispy Strips.

In addition to the food, each meal also comes with a collectible sock featuring McDonald's characters pulling inspiration from the Home Alone movies. There's also the Everything Sundae McFlurry, a separate dessert item that comes equipped with Oreos, M&M's, and hot fudge.

As for Culkin's role, he stars as Kevin in an ad wherein he's a McDonald's employee manning an empty restaurant during a snowstorm. The advertisement shows off the food, which he makes for himself in a comedic montage. Check out the full advertisement featuring his return as Kevin below:

In addition to the Home Alone-inspired food and collectible socks, customers in Los Angeles can sign up to participate in the Home Alone challenge pop-up on December 4 and 5, happening from 5 p.m. to 10 p.m. PT. The free, first-come, first-serve event requires online registration, with reservations limited to groups of eight people or less. It's an additional element to the food-centered campaign, and one that puts the movie first with plenty of promising surprises.

The Home Alone meal is the second time Culkin has returned as McCallister for an advertisement role. His latest rendition was in a 2018 Google Assistant commercial, where he utilized the technology for a more modern take on the movie. According to the report, Culkin was drawn by the idea of associating McDonald's "comfort food" with the movie, having "no notes" about the meal when it was presented to him.
