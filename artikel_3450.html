Taylor Sheridan Star Officially Addresses That Devastating Season 3 Finale Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Editor's Note: The following contains spoilers for 'Lioness' Season 3If there's one thing Taylor Sheridan has demonstrated throughout his television career, it's that getting attached to his characters can be something of a poisoned chalice because, whether it's Yellowstone, 1923, or Lioness, Sheridan isn't exactly a fan of happy endings, and that's especially true when his characters all find themselves caught up in situations where survival is anything but certain. And one of the main characters in his spy thriller felt the sharp end of his pen.

Elizaveta Neretin, who portrayed Oleksandra in Lioness Season 3, has officially spoken out on her character's death in the season finale, saying that she understood Sheridan's decision even though she obviously hoped it wouldn't happen. Speaking about the storyline, Neretin also discussed the decision to have the character die off-screen, a creative choice that left viewers confronting the aftermath rather than witnessing the moment itself. Speaking with Fox News Digital, Neretin explained:

"I thought it was going to happen. So when he called, and he told me, I was like, 'Yeah, I get it.' I'm sad for Oleksandra and what Oleksandra represents, but I understand the choice. It does serve this. It serves to show how the whole system is. Like American or not American, the whole system and the people that work in the system. It's all sharks, you know, so it just demonstrates how people are."

Surprisingly, we don't even see the murder. Instead, the finale shows two Delta Force operators removing her from a safehouse before a later scene reveals that a jogger has discovered her body near a trail. For a series that has never been particularly shy about depicting violence, it's an odd choice but Neretin believes there may have been a good reason for leaving the character's final moments to the imagination. Speaking with Decider, she added:

"We didn't film her actual murder, but I wonder if that's more of a question for Taylor. He writes some pretty gruesome things... Good writing means you don't write the things that are obvious. The viewers already knew she was going to die. So, what would be the purpose of showing her being murdered? Every scene has to have a purpose. Again, I don't want to speak for Taylor, but perhaps that's why the audience only saw her dead body. That's all they needed to see."
