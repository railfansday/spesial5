'The Acolyte' Star's Upcoming Fantasy Adaptation Officially Gets Stunning New Trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

As 2026 winds down, the big-budget fantasy adaptations have only continued rolling in. Last month saw the release of LAIKA's long-gestating stop-motion rendition of Wildwood to overwhelming acclaim from both critics and audiences, while November has marked the arrival of the Indian epic Ramayana: Rise of a Legend, the first part of a duology that clocks in as the country's most expensive undertaking ever. On Christmas Day, HBO will also give the gift of its much-anticipated Harry Potter series. Even as the calendar flips over, the streak will stay alive with a much-anticipated, if controversial, epic starring The Acolyte lead, Amandla Stenberg.

On January 15, Children of Blood and Bone will finally get its time in the sun with Gina Prince-Bythewood's fantasy epic adaptation in theaters. Paramount has been slowly building up buzz for the star-studded film, based on one of the most popular and acclaimed YA novels of the past ten years from bestselling author Tomi Adeyemi. Last month, the cast, led by Thuso Mbedu, Damson Idris, Tosin Cole, and Stenberg, appeared at New York Comic Con to tease the painstaking work to bring the world of Orïsha to life, from the elaborate costumes to the dazzling effects and extensive fight training. Now, Zélie Adebola's (Mbedu) story is getting another look with two months remaining until release.

Paramount has shared a new trailer showcasing Children of Blood and Bone's magic, African mythology-inspired world in stunning fashion. At the center is Zélie, a young woman who witnessed the magic of her kingdom be violently ripped away by its ruthless king, Saran (Chiwetel Ejiofor), leaving her without a mother. Saran created a society where the magically-inclined divîners have no power, and the few allowed to survive are forced into lives of poverty and oppression. To restore what her people lost and save Orïsha, she and her brother join forces with the King's own son and daughter to oppose his reign of terror.

Four worlds and four completely different ideas of what a good life looks like. This is where you belong, what it would ask of you, and what you would get back in return.

You belong somewhere old enough that the mountains have names and somebody can still tell you who gave them. Middle-earth is for people who think the right thing is worth doing even when it will not be finished in their lifetime.

You want the sea, a crew, and absolutely nobody telling you where you are allowed to go. The Grand Line is the world for people whose main ambition in life is finding out what is over there.

You would rather be feared and useful than liked and useless. The Continent is a world where the monsters are the straightforward part of the job and the people are where it all gets complicated.

You want the myths to be real and loose in the actual world, and you want to find out at about thirteen that you were never anywhere near as ordinary as everybody kept telling you.

Alongside the main stars standing against King Saran, the fantasy epic also features Cynthia Erivo, Lashana Lynch, Zackary Momoh, Richard Mofe-Damijo, Regina King, Idris Elba, Viola Davis, and many more among its massive ensemble. All three of Mbedu, Davis, and Lynch also worked with Prince-Bythewood on her last film, The Woman King, in 2022. All the award-worthy firepower on Children of Blood and Bone is finally poised to get the ambitious fantasy adaptation across the finish line after years of the project languishing in development hell and changing hands from the Disney umbrella to Paramount. Despite co-writing the script, however, Adeyemi ultimately distanced herself from the production this summer, saying she would not continue "being hurt and attacked behind the scenes" and citing a rift with Stenberg specifically.

Children of Blood and Bone takes over theaters on January 15. Check out the new trailer in the player above.
