How to get a key cloner in GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The key cloner in Grand Theft Auto 6 allows you to steal keyless cars. As the most expensive cars in the game tend to be keyless, unlocking the key cloner is probably high on your priority list, but beware that it'll take a while to meet Randy, the key cloner expert.

Here's where to find a key cloner in GTA 6 and how to use it.

In GTA 6's first chapter, you can only steal cars by smashing a window, which may set off an alarm. After meeting Moses in Chapter 2, you'll obtain a slim jim, which allows you to circumvent the alarm as you break in. Neither carjacking method, however, is enough to steal a keyless car. For that, you will need the key cloner.

If you try to steal a keyless car before you've unlocked the key cloner, you can still break in, but you won't be able to drive away. It's therefore important to check the car's security system first; you don't want to smash a window and set off the car's alarm system, only to find that you can't use the car.

To obtain the key cloner and steal keyless cars in GTA 6, you must complete the following steps:

To determine whether a car is keyless or not, use the WAiNK scanner on your phone (open the WAiNK app or hold the upward arrow on the D-pad for a shortcut). You'll see the words "key cloner" listed as the car's access type in the bottom right of your screen.

To steal a keyless car in GTA 6, interact with one of its front doors and select the "clone key" option (which appears instead of the "slim jim" option for non-keyless cars). Now, move the right stick in a circular motion until the center circle turns pink, and then hold the right stick in this position to unlock the car and drive off.

Beware that most keyless cars are protected by a standard or advanced tracker. The standard tracker isn't much of a problem (it just means you can't modify the car unless you've registered it), but the advanced tracker, as you've experienced during "Steal the Zorrusso for Randy," is actively tracked by the police. The only way to stay ahead is to drive very fast. So, while you can steal both types with the key cloner, stealing cars with standard trackers is far easier.
