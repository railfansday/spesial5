Krakow Shooting Leaves at Least 6 Killed in Poland After Gunman Drove Onto Square

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

11:45 AM PT -- Małopolska Police have said in an update that four women and two men are among those killed in today's attack and that officers responded to the incident within 23 seconds.

At least 6 people are dead after a gunman opened fire from his car at a busy marketplace in Krakow, Poland.

Around a dozen people -- including police officers -- were treated for injuries after the deadly shooting that ended with the gunman being shot ... the Małopolska Police shared on social media.

The unidentified suspect was then taken to a nearby hospital ... it's unclear what his current status is.

In an update, police confirmed that the suspect is a Polish citizen and denied reports of a second suspect.

The shooting happened around 3 PM ... when the suspect drove onto Rynek Glowny square before opening fire on the crowd and a tram ... according to Krakow’s public transportation operator, MPK Krakow.

MPK wrote on X ... "According to the information we have, neither the passengers nor the driver were injured."

This story is developing ... we'll keep you posted as we learn more.
