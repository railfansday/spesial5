Meghan Markle's Father Says He's Not Suicidal in Jail Amid Child Porn Case

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Meghan Markle‘s father, Thomas Markle Sr., told psychological evaluators that he was not suicidal as he awaits trial for allegedly possessing child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

“Mr. Markle reported no current psychiatric symptoms other than memory loss,” a psychological report obtained by Us reads. “Near the end of the interview (after discussing the allegation against him), I asked again, and he said, ‘I don’t think so. I am getting depression now!’ He reported no thoughts of suicide.”

Markle, 82, was arrested on November 6, 2026, in Lakeland, Florida, and subsequently charged with possession of child pornography. While he was initially released on bond, Markle was re-arrested days later and held in Clearwater, Florida’s Pinellas County Jail after allegedly trying to cut off his court-mandated ankle monitor with a small knife at eldest daughter Samantha’s house after being kicked out of an assissted living facility.

A judge ordered that Markle undergo an independent competency evaluation by a psychiatrist to help determine whether he is competent to stand trial.

The subsequent report mentioned that Thomas’s face was “flushed” and he “stuttered often” during a recent competency evaluation, though he also showed that he’s capable of “a logical, linear, and goal-directed thought process,” per staff at USF Health.

“He reported no auditory or visual hallucinations, and there was no behavioral evidence of hallucinations,” the psychiatrist noted. “This was no evidence of a thought disorder or paranoia. He did not express delusional thinking. He reported no self-injurious thoughts, self-injurious behavior, suicidal thoughts, or homicidal thoughts.”

In light of the psychiatrist’s findings, an independent competency evaluation reportedly determined that Thomas has “sufficient ability to understand the nature and consequences of the charges against him,” making him competent to stand trial.

Federal investigators pursued charges against Thomas after two of his rented storage containers were raided in Los Angeles, where authorities allegedly found images depicting child sexual abuse.

“Mr. Markle allegedly targeted innocent children and sought to steal their innocence through his criminal actions,” FBI director Kash Patel said in a November 6 statement. “Working with our Justice Department partners, our agents moved quickly, identified and located the explicit material and arrested Mr. Markle.”

Patel, 46, continued, “This FBI will use every single resource at our disposal to protect innocent kids — any predator who targets children will have zero safe haven in this country or anywhere they try to hide. Thank you to FBI Los Angeles, FBI Tampa, our Mexico, Manila, and Marshall Islands Law Enforcement Attaches, and our partners who executed quickly in this case.”

Thomas is facing a separate investigation in the Philippines, where he lived until May 2026 after having a portion of his left leg amputated in December 2025. His son, Thomas Markle Jr., allegedly found CSAM images at his father’s Cebu property and turned over evidence to local police.

On October 23, Thomas Sr. denied any wrongdoing, insisting that his son’s accusations were “sick lies.”

“I’m shocked, I’m hurt and I’m very upset that my son is making these accusations against me,” he told the Daily Mail at the time.
