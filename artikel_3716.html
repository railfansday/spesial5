You can keep playing GTA 6 after the credits, but there's some things you may want to know first

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

No matter how far you're through the main GTA 6 missions list, you are probably starting to get a sense of just how massive Leonida really is – and how stacked it is with curiosities, distractions, objectives, and side-activities. So naturally you must be wondering if you can free-roam after beating GTA 6, or whether you need to pack everything in before hitting a point of no return.

I'm going to answer this question for you and, because I'm nice, I'm going to do so in the most spoiler-free way that I possibly can.

You can continue free-roam after beating GTA 6, albeit with a few caveats. Because there are numerous GTA 6 endings, with a wide range of different variables and impacts for all of the characters depending on your relationship status, your criminal profile, whether you paid into family savings, and plenty more besides, Rockstar has decided that it would be far too difficult to simply drop you back into Vice City in a world in which there are no more main missions daunting your GTA 6 Index.

This means that once you've completed the main story in GTA 6, the game will drop you back to an auto-save just before the point-of-no-return. When the game does this, I recommend that you be prepared to make a new save in GTA 6 – as that auto-save won't exist forever, and can be lost if you decide to load an old save or start the campaign over from scratch.

From this point, you're free to do whatever you want to do. Your relationship status will be locked, so there's no need to worry about maintaining this if you don't want to, but you can still mess around with your Criminal Profile. You can also, at any point, restart the final mission and go through it again.

The GTA 6 end-game is where the world really lets you be free. You'll have loads of GTA 6 challenges to complete, which contribute to the unlocking of GTA 6 Cheats that are now available in the Progress menu. So, long story short, you can free-roam after beating GTA 6, but expect to have that final mission sitting on your menu screen for the long haul.
