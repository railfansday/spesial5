So Is The $100 GTA 6 Ultimate Edition Actually Worth It?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Before Grand Theft Auto 6 even launched, there was some controversy online over the announcement of a $100 “Ultimate Edition” version of the game that would include access to a few exclusive shops and in-game cosmetics. So, now that GTA 6 is here and I’ve spent over 150 hours with it, I can share what’s in the Ultimate Edition, how you access this stuff, and if it’s worth upgrading from the standard $80 version of GTA 6. The short version is…eh, probably not.

In GTA 6, there are five stores that are locked behind the Ultimate Edition paywall. These stores include an exclusive tattoo parlor, street clothing shop, and hair salon. Now, at first, I thought it was pretty shitty of Rockstar to hide stores behind a paywall. And to be clear, it still does suck that a few shops are only accessible if you pay $20 more. But after playing GTA 6, I discovered that the world is filled with over 600 stores. There are already so many clothing, car, hair, make-up, and nail options in the base game. So the Ultimate Edition’s extra few stores don’t feel that necessary. And after actually visiting these shops and seeing what they have to offer, I don’t think people are missing out on that much.

First up, there’s 305, a clothing store specializing in urban, skater-inspired clothing and gear. There’s some alright stuff in here, including some nice-looking jeans for Jason and a cool graphic shirt or two. And a really wild fuzzy hat for Lucia. But the options in here aren’t something you need to have. You can create plenty of great and cool outfits without stopping by this Ultimate Editionâonly retailer.

Next up is the tattoo shop Electric Fang. Of all the Ultimate Edition stores, this is the coolest one to enter. And I do think some of Electric Fang’s tat designs are solid. But again, there are so many other options in the game for ink that I’m not sure these are very necessary or worth the $20 upgrade.

Sara’s Unisex Salon might be the best of the bunch as it offers some stylish hairdos for both Jason and Lucia. But on top of that, it offers some really nice makeup options for Lucia and cool nail art, too. I ended up using some of these options during my time as Lucia because I liked them so much. Still, again, there are so many other good options for haircuts and nail art already in GTA 6 that I’m not sure you need to spend $20 more on this version.

There are also two exclusive car mod garages in GTA 6‘s Ultimate Edition. One of them, Rideout Customs, offers some really gaudy and exotic paint and accessories options. If you wanted a neon-pink car with stripes painted to look like cheetah print, well, this is the place for you.

The other mod shop, One-Eyed Willie’s, is more useful. It offers up some really nice off-road options. There aren’t nearly as many off-road modding options in GTA 6 as there are for street cars, so losing out on access to this in the base game is a shame. I also love that One-Eyed Willie’s offers up a random paint job for every car in the game, including non-off-road vehicles, that you can only see once it’s applied. I brought my Banshee into the shop, and it received this pretty pink design that I loved.

The Ultimate Edition also includes some unique free clothing items based on characters from an in-universe cartoon you can watch. These clothes are fine. I guess if you like the show a lot, they might be fun to wear. But…eh.

Beyond the stores and cosmetics, there are some exclusive vehicles you get access to in the Ultimate Edition, but some of these are only available during chapter one for story reasons. The only thing from the Ultimate Edition I used regularly and still use often when I play is the Vapid off-road truck. It’s fast as heck, a great option for driving on the dirt, and can be upgraded into a beast that can hold a lot of stolen loot. There’s also a classic car scavenger hunt side mission hidden behind the paywall, but after 150 hours I’ve barely interacted with that thing.

Answering the big question of “Is the Ultimate Edition worth it?” is tricky, because it depends entirely on what you value and your taste for fashion and cosmetics. I’ve provided some samples of what’s on offer and how it fits into the larger GTA 6 world.

My opinion is that if you can afford the extra $20, a few bits of the Ultimate Edition are nice and worth the upgrade. But for the most part, if you just want to play Grand Theft Auto 6 to enjoy the open world and its story, you don’t need this upgrade. You’ll have a great time with the base game and never think to yourself, “Boy, I wish there were 30 more shirts and a few more cars in this!” GTA 6 is already big. Don’t worry about making it a bit bigger.
