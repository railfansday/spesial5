Giants earn their most impressive win of the season with upset of Jaguars

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The NFC East-leading Giants made a statement today against the Jaguars.

In a game that few expected the Giants to win, New York took a 10-3 first quarter lead and never relinquished it, turning in an impressive performance and beating the Jaguars 23-18.

The Giants were aided by two highly controversial touchdown calls: Giants quarterback Jameis Winston got a fourth-and-goal tush push touchdown despite his helmet appearing to be down before the ball crossed the goal line, and Jaguars wide receiver Parker Washington was denied a touchdown catch despite appearing to have possession with his feet down in bounds.

But the two controversies shouldn’t overshadow that this Giants team is far better than anyone expected. They’re 6-3-1 and in total control of the NFC East, and today they earned their most impressive win yet.

The Jaguars fall to 7-3, and despite the loss they’re still the overwhelming favorites to win the AFC South. This loss will hurt in the AFC playoff seeding, however, as they’re now long shots to earn the No. 1 seed.

Both the Giants and the Jaguars look like playoff teams. This was a hard-fought game between two opponents that expect to still be playing well into January. And maybe February.
