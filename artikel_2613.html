Bengals-Commanders officials miss Treylon Burks' facemask Monday night

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Commanders receiver Treylon Burks got away with grabbing Bengals defensive back Dax Hill's facemask as Hill defended Burks on a pass during the third quarter of their NFL Week 11 game at Northwest Stadium on ESPN's Monday Night Football.

"Looked like Burks had a hold of (Dax Hill)," MNF analyst Troy Aikman said during the broadcast.

Earlier in the game, Aikman said rainy conditions during the first half were benefitting the Commanders by keeping Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow from trying to throw downfield.

During the Bengals-Commanders preseason game in 2025, MNF analyst Troy Aikman criticized Bengals defensive back Jordan Battle for not hitting Daniels on the QB's first-quarter touchdown run.

Weeks later, Aikman disagreed when Bengals center Ted Karras was called for a crucial false-start penalty during the first quarter of the team's NFL Week 4 loss to Denver at Empower Field at Mile High.

Aikman also called the Bengals' offense a mess during the Denver game.

In December 2024, Aikman said his NFL MVP vote would go to Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow. And Buck and Aikman agreed that officials missed an obvious penalty when the Dallas Cowboys' Marist Liufau grabbed Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow's facemask during the fourth quarter of Cincinnati's NFL Week 14 win at AT&T Stadium on Monday Night Football.

The Bengals and Commanders met on Monday night during the 2024 regular season. Buck said it was a must-win game for Cincinnati after an 0-2 start. Washington won 38-33.

During the Saints-Chiefs Monday Night Football game in 2024, Buck trolled the Bengals and punter Ryan Rehkow's errant hold in a loss against Baltimore.

Buck and Aikman also called the Bengals' overtime win against the Jacksonville Jaguars in December 2023.
