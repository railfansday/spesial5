Recap: Rockets rout Wizards, 147-119

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Washington Wizards fell to the Houston Rockets, 147-119, on Sunday night at Capital One Arena, extending their losing streak to seven games. The Wizards trailed by as many as 37 points and were it-rebounded by 19.

Coming off a 30-point performance against the Detroit Pistons on Thursday, Alex Sarr scored eight quick points on 3-of-3 shooting, including a three-pointer, to give Washington an early 13-12 lead.

The Wizards closed the first quarter trailing 36-31, with Sarr leading the way with 10 points. Anthony Davis, who also scored 30 against Detroit on Thursday, had a much quieter start, missing all three of his field-goal attempts, including several looks around the rim.

Houston opened the second quarter with a 7-0 run and eventually stretched its lead to 12. Sarr briefly halted the momentum with another three-pointer, but the Rockets continued to pull away. Houston's lead reached 17 with 6:29 remaining in the half, as Amen Thompson and Kevin Durant helped fuel the offensive surge. Thompson started 3-for-3 from beyond the arc, while Houston shot 56% from three-point range in the first half.

Washington went into the break trailing 73-54 despite Sarr's 15 points, four rebounds and two assists. The rest of the Wizards' starting lineup — Trae Young, AJ Dybantsa, Kyshawn George and Davis — combined for just 18 points.

Houston picked up right where it left off to start the third quarter, opening the period with a 12-4 run, and the game wasn't competitive from that point forward.

Thompson (26 points), Durant (25), Tari Eason (25) and Alperen Sengun (21) all scored 20+, while Sarr (25) was the only Wizard to finish with more than 20 points. Dybantsa scored 16 and grabbed seven boards, Davis finished with 14 points and eight rebounds, and George tacked on 11 points and four assists. Will Riley knocked down two three pointers and scored 14 points off the bench.

The Wizards will turn around to host the Memphis Grizzlies on Monday night at 7:00 p.m. ET at Capital One Arena.
