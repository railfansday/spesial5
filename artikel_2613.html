Brock Bowers injury update as Raiders TE leaves Week 10 game with ankle issue

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Las Vegas Raiders were getting a look at their new-look offense. That new look is coming without a key piece though.

Brock Bowers, the star tight end, exited in the first half of the Week 10 game against the Seattle Seahawks with an ankle injury. The 23-year-old initially departed after he was rolled up on in the first quarter.

Bowers went back to the locker room with an ankle injury. His return is questionable.
