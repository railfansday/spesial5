How former Cyclones performed in Week 10 of the NFL

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Ten former Cyclones made appearances in Week 10 of the NFL and played a combined 343 snaps on offense, defense and special teams.

In a 31-12 loss to the Buffalo Bills, Hall recorded 11 receptions for 41 yards and four receptions for 67 yards. He appeared on the field for 41 offensive snaps.

In a 27-24 loss to the Cleveland Browns, Hummel recorded two tackles on special teams. He appeared on the field for 21 special teams snaps.

In a 27-24 loss to the Browns, Hutchinson started and recorded four receptions for 58 yards and a touchdown. He appeared on the field for 51 offensive snaps.

In a 23-20 win against the Baltimore Ravens, Kolar started. He recorded two receptions for 16 yards. Kolar played 41 snaps on offense and 15 on special teams.

In a 31-12 loss to the Bills, McDonald did not start. He recorded a sack and two quarterback hurries – he played 30 defensive snaps and four on special teams.

Montgomery sustained a groin injury in Week 5. He was a full participant at practice on Monday (Nov. 16).

He is out for the remainder of the season with a turf toe injury that requires surgery.

In a 31-12 loss to the Bills, Nwangwu appeared on the field for six special teams snaps.

In a 30-10 win over the Packers, Orange did not start. He recorded two tackles played seven defensive and three special teams snaps.

In a 21-13 win against the Seattle Seahawks, Darien Porter did not start. He recorded 30 defensive snaps.

In an 46-40 win against the Dallas Cowboys, Purdy went 22-of-28 through the air for 389 yards and three touchdowns – the longest pass of the day, 74 yards, went to Christian McCaffery. He also had two carries for 21 yards. Purdy played all 65 offensive snaps.

In a 23-20 loss to the Chargers, Tampa did not start. He recorded one tackle. Tampa played three defensive and 26 special teams snaps.

Travis sustained a knee injury during the Week 9 matchup versus the Dallas Cowboys. He is expected to be out two to four weeks.

The Broncos had their bye during Week 10.
