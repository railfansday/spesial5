How does Jadarian Price’s season compare to other 2026 rookies?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

With the Seahawks in their bye week, this makes for a good benchmark to compare how the team and their players are doing in comparison to the rest of the league. Let's do that with Seattle's rookie running back, Jadarian Price, and see how he compares to the rest of the NFL rookie running back class.

Price, who scored his first NFL touchdown last week against the Las Vegas Raiders, is off to a slower start than most Seahawks fans likely envisioned entering the 2026 season. However, a lot of that can be thanks to a chest injury he suffered earlier in the season, one which put him on injured reserve, causing him to miss four games.

When comparing Price to other rookie running backs, he comes in as the sixth most rushing yards among rookie running backs, with 208 yards. Ahead of him are Kaelon Black (San Francisco 49ers) with 356 yards, Mike Washington Jr. (Las Vegas Raiders) with 298 yards, Seth McGowan (Indianapolis Colts) with 285 yards, Jeremiyah Love (Arizona Cardinals) with 268 yards, and Emmett Johnson (Kansas City Chiefs) with 212 yards.

For rushing touchdowns, Price only has one touchdown which ties him for fourth most, behind the likes of Jonah Coleman (Denver Broncos) with three touchdowns, and Kaelon Black and Seth McGowan, both with two touchdowns each.

For yards per carry (amongst rookie running backs with at least 45 carries) Price also comes in at sixth with an average of 3.7 YPC. Ahead of him are Seth McGowan with 5.2 YPC, Kaelon Black with 4.5 YPC, Emmett Johnson with 4.4 YPC, Jonah Coleman with 4.1 YPC, and Mike Washington Jr. with 3.8 YPC.

On the receiving end, Price is tied with Jeremiyah Love for third most receptions by a rookie running back (11), behind Emmett Johnson (20), and Jonah Coleman (13). Price is also third for most receiving yards by a rookie running back (91), behind Emmett Johnson (307) and Jonah Coleman (128). Price has no receiving touchdowns.

All of these stats show that Price has not yet hit the expectations many have for the rookie. However, after coming back from injury, Price has shown a lot more explosiveness and vision that the Seahawks saw in him coming out of Notre Dame. Most notably, Price has also not fumbled in his three games back.

It will be interesting to keep an eye on these stats as the season goes along to see if Price is able to rise in these statistical rankings. One could argue that Price should shoot up these rankings as he will get more carries than the other running backs listed above. However, with Zach Charbonnet also back from injury and impressing, Price at best will be in a running back committee situation. Price has gotten 28 carries over the last three weeks. That's far less than Zach Charbonnet's 42 carries over that same time span.

In those three games back from injury, Price has averaged 9.3 carries per game, for an average of 29.7 rushing yards per game. If you were to project the rest of Price's season based on that 9.3 carries for 29.7 yards/game, that would leave Price with 65 carries for 208 rushing yards for the rest of the season. Add that on top of his current season stats, that projects Price to finish 2026 with 416 rushing yards on 121 carries. These projections of course don't account for other factors such as injuries, weather, game situations, etc., all of which can seriously alter any of these projections.
