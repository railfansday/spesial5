Patriots Power Rankings: Pats plummet heading into Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The league expressed it's confidence in the Patriots recovering from their two-game blown-out streak, by removing them from the Week 12 Sunday Night Football lineup in favor of Raiders at Browns. Did you get that? A Patriots game has been replaced by the Raiders and Browns. In the immortal words of Dunder Mifflin's Michael Scott, "Well, well well, how the turn tables…"

The experts not only turned the Pats in the rankings after last week's debacle of a game, but dumped them on their head. New England now sits at 20th and could conceivably fall lower after the Bye week, depending on the results of Week 11.

With Drake Maye out, my guess is that the team will move forward with the quarterbacks on their roster: Tommy DeVito and the backup's backup, Behren Morton. The coaching staff has two weeks to figure out how to stanch the bleeding on both sides of the ball. Maybe some of the walking wounded will heal up enough to contribute. I still think this team can potentially still win some games, even as banged up as they are. The Chargers (Week 12), Jets (Week 16) and Dolphins (Week 18) are still bad enough to be beatable. We'll see.

My expectations have dropped lower than the rankings, but I still want to watch to see how Mike Vrabel and his men handle the situation.
