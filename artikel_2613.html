Joel Embiid doubtful for Sixers’ NBA Cup showdown vs. Miami Heat

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Sixers are getting dangerously close to full strength, which means in true Sixers fashion, calamity is right around the corner. Lo and behold, Joel Embiid came down with an illness that caused him to miss Monday's game against the Portland Trail Blazers, and he's still dealing with it a day later.

Embiid is doubtful to suit up for the Sixers' NBA Cup game against the Miami Heat on Wednesday, according to the NBA's injury report. The good news is that aside from Embiid and Ariel Hukporti (Achilles), no one else is even listed for the Sixers. So much for Jaylen Brown's hamstring injury!

On Miami's side, two-way signees Tre Donaldson and Vladislav Goldin are out on assignment in the G League, while Myron Gardner is available. The big question mark is Giannis Antetokounmpo, who's listed as questionable with right groin tendinitis. He missed Miami's win over the Oklahoma City Thunder on Sunday with the same injury, and in his absence, Bam Adebayo messed around and got a triple-double.

If Giannis is able to suit up and Embiid misses the game, the Sixers will have a hell of a time stopping him and Bam down low. None of Jabari Walker, Dominick Barlow or Adem Bona have the size to combat the Greek Freak in transition. But if Giannis does sit out, it'll be more of a fair fight.

The Heat sit atop East Group B with a 2-0 record, while the Sixers are 1-1. If the Heat beat the Sixers, they'll be all but guaranteed to win the group, as they'll have the head-to-head tiebreaker over both the Sixers and Cleveland Cavaliers. But if the Embiid-less Sixers can take down the Heat, they'll have a chance to win Group B as long as the New York Knicks beat the Cavs later Wednesday.

The Sixers have never advanced to the knockout stage of the NBA Cup, but they've also never had NBA Cup MVP LeBron James on their roster before this year. And since LeBron is playing on a veteran-minimum contract this year, the prize money for winning the Cup would be more than 10 percent of his salary. That's assuredly a huge motivation for someone with an estimated net worth of $1.4 billion.

Embiid's likely absence will give the Sixers a chance to continue experimenting with their backup options as they inch closer to the start of unofficial trade season. They largely went with a Walker-Barlow combo against the Blazers—Bona played only five minutes in his first game back from a cervical spine sprain—but it's worth keeping an eye on whether the Sixers change that up against the Heat. They should still be in the intel-gathering stage of the season, so the more they mix it up, the better. (Could we even get some brief Tacko Time?)

As someone whose two-year-old is just getting over hand, foot and mouth disease, it's that time of the year for parents. Here's hoping the rest of the Sixers stay healthy and Embiid feels better soon.
