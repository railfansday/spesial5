Inter have new injury worry with Bastoni limping off

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Inter have a new injury concern, as Alessandro Bastoni was forced off with a muscular problem during the first half of this evening’s Champions League game against VfB Stuttgart.

You can follow all the action as it happens in that match and Paris Saint-Germain vs. Roma on the Liveblog.

The Nerazzurri were already missing Hakan Calhanoglu, Ange-Yoan Bonny and Piotr Zielinski for this fixture in Matchday 5 of the European tournament.

Bastoni only lasted 40 minutes before limping off the field clutching his left thigh, and it is safe to say this will be another worry for coach Cristian Chivu.

He was replaced by Federico Dimarco, meaning that Carlos Augusto shifted from his original role as left wing-back to drop into the three-man defence for the rest of the game.

It remains to be seen how bad the damage is, or if Bastoni managed to stop early enough to avoid anything more serious.

Muscular injuries have been the bane of Inter’s campaign so far, as Lautaro Martinez only just returned to the field, with John Stones and Curtis Jones also still not fully fit.
