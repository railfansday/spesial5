Judge Allows Trump’s Defamation Lawsuit Against the BBC to Proceed

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

President Trump is seeking damages from the British broadcaster over a documentary it conceded was misleadingly edited.

A federal judge in Florida on Tuesday allowed President Trump’s defamation lawsuit against the BBC to proceed, rejecting the British broadcaster’s bid to dismiss it.

Mr. Trump sued the BBC over its documentary “Trump: A Second Chance?” which aired a week before the 2024 presidential election. The program spliced together two portions of remarks that Mr. Trump delivered roughly 55 minutes apart on Jan. 6, 2021, giving the impression that he was encouraging his supporters to storm the U.S. Capitol.

Mr. Trump’s legal team has accused the BBC of “intentionally, maliciously and deceptively doctoring his speech” in an effort to interfere with the election, and is seeking $10 billion in damages.

The BBC has apologized, acknowledging that its editing gave “the mistaken impression that Trump made a direct call for violent action,” a controversy that prompted the resignations of its director general, Tim Davie, and news chief, Deborah Turness. But it says it was not evidence of a fundamental bias in the BBC’s news reporting and did not constitute defamation.

On Truth Social, Mr. Trump described Tuesday’s ruling as a “A Huge Victory against Fake News BBC.”

A spokeswoman for the BBC said in an emailed statement: “We are surprised by this decision but respect the court’s authority and note the case will now proceed to the next stage. We remain confident in our position and will continue to respond through the proper legal process.”

Lawyers for the BBC had sought to have the lawsuit dismissed, in part because the program was never aired in the United States.
