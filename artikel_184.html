Police arrest teacher who missed sentencing after indecent assault conviction

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Sally-Anne Bowen was found guilty of having sexual relationships with two pupils at a boys’ school in the 1980s

A chemistry teacher who had sexual relationships with two pupils at a boys’ school in the late 1980s has been arrested by police in Hampshire after she failed to turn up to her sentencing hearing.

Sally-Anne Bowen, 65, could not be found at her home by Metropolitan police officers on Friday morning after a warrant was issued for her arrest, a court heard.

The former teacher, who was in her 20s when she had sex with one pupil up to 30 times and kissed a second boy when they were aged about 14 to 15, was convicted in August of seven counts of indecent assault and granted bail before her sentencing.

Her trial heard she wore short, tight skirts and flirted in class, with one former pupil comparing her behaviour to “a Sabrina Carpenter video”.

After her conviction she was granted bail and was due to be sentenced at Harrow crown court on Friday.

But Bowen, from Hemel Hempstead, Hertfordshire, did not turn up to court in the morning, and the judge, Karim Ezzat, issued a warrant for her arrest, “not backed by bail”.

Neelam Gomersall, prosecuting, said Bowen’s partner of 36 years, Darren Scotchbrook, was at the address when officers arrived, but Bowen was not.

Gomersall had earlier told the court there was “no evidence” that Bowen could not attend the sentencing, and that information from Scotchbrook was “suggestive of refusal rather than inability” to attend.

Bowen’s barrister, Mark Kimsey, said: “Our instructing solicitor has tried to contact her and the phone rings but doesn’t answer.”

He had earlier said that Bowen was in contact with solicitors on Thursday and had engaged with a pre-sentence report, adding there had been “concerns as to her welfare due to the content of the messages” from her.

In an update on Friday afternoon, the Met said: “On Friday November 20, officers were made aware that a 65-year-old woman had failed to appear at Harrow crown court for sentencing.

“A warrant was issued for her arrest and officers promptly launched inquiries to locate her.

“At around 3pm, officers from a neighbouring force found the woman at a location in Hampshire and arrested her on suspicion of failing to appear at court.

“She has been taken into police custody. A new sentencing date will be set by the court in due course.”

Bowen’s trial heard that she was in her mid-20s when she taught at Christ’s College in Finchley, north London.

One victim had told jurors he had had sex with her “about 20 or 30 times” over several months, considered her his girlfriend and kept it secret. He said it had led to “the destruction of my education”.

The second victim described spending lunchtimes with Bowen in a cafe, where he was “goaded” into kissing her by other boys and recalled touching her breasts.

Bowen denied the allegations as “total fabrication” and claimed a medical condition made sex difficult and painful. She denied flirting, saying: “I didn’t flirt with students, I was just friendly.” Defending her clothes, she said she “wasn’t going to dress like Margaret Thatcher”.
