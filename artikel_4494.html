They know what you want, and it's bloody disgusting: Love and Deepspace's latest Myth introduces Vampire Sylus

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

One year after Sylus got his second Myth, the third one arrives to make good on all those vampire fantasies.

Love and Deepspace has kicked off its latest Memory Pair event today: Where Silverwings Rest, the new chapter for dragon boy - and now vampire boy - Sylus. There are new Memories to explore, a new Myth - and, of course, a new companion form for him.

This latest limited-time event is live now until Tuesday, December 16, making for a two-week runtime.

The highlight in Where Silverwings Rest, of course, are the new 5-Star Memories. There’s a new, fittingly themed tale about restlessness. Players are getting Sylus: Crimson Rapture, and Sylus: Crimson Departure, two 5-Star Solar-Slot Memory Pairs.

You’ll find them in the Wish Pool throughout the event’s duration. You can do a maximum of 150 pulls before you’re guaranteed to unlock the two of them, and earning the Sylus: Silverwing Fiend Companion. That Companion Form is also new for Sylus.

If you’re a fan of reading, you’ll find the new Crimson Spirit Myth, which is very much a vampire tale about the tormenting reality of being an immortal being. Beyond all the lore and visuals, there’s also the Crimson Feast event that runs alongside it.

As part of its progression, you can claim Sylus: Ivory Thirst, and Sylus: Ivory Prudence, two 4-Star Solar-Slot Memory Pairs. You can also look forward to getting 10 Deepspace Wish: Limited, 500 Diamonds, and other free bonuses for those who log in regularly.
