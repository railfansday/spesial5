Microsoft outage brings fax machines back into action across Japanese offices

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Ongoing Microsoft outages have led companies across Japan to dust off fax machines, with employees now using the old-school tech to complete tasks during the widespread blackout.

A widespread Microsoft 365 outage has disrupted email and workplace services around the globe, leaving many without access to the everyday tools they need for work.

While some services have started to recover, local governments and firms across Japan have turned to a familiar sight in offices during the 1980s and 1990s: fax machines.

These chunky devices are used to send copies of documents over telephone lines, allowing staff to communicate and confirm transaction details while email remains unavailable.

So, despite the tech being outdated compared to more modern devices, it turns out that sometimes relying on old-school equipment can be the perfect modern solution.

On November 13, companies across Japan began using fax and other alternatives to keep communications moving. Disruption has also reached government offices, with some asking people to contact them by telephone or fax while their email systems remain unavailable.

Since then, Japanese users across social media have been praising fax machines and other analogue tech for enabling them to continue communicating throughout the outages.

“Unbelievable, faxes are saving the day yet again…it reminded me of when faxes were getting renewed attention after the recent cyberattack. When you’re in a bind, you can always count on analog,” wrote one user on X.

“Our company wasn’t affected by the Outlook issue, but since other companies were, emails were only going one way to our phones. We used to exchange photos by texting them to each other, but in the end, even in this day and age, fax was just unbeatable,” added another.
