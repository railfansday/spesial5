Get a portable screen for travel, or second (or third) screen for your desk for less.

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

If you ever travel with your laptop, want to game on a console in places where you don't have access to a TV, or just want a second (or third) screen for your desk, a portable monitor is a must-have. And with many screens at all-time lows, veering into impulse-buy territory, there's never been a better time to pick one up. But how do you know what to buy when there are dozens of brands (especially on the low end) that you've probably never heard of (I haven't, and I've covered tech for 20 years)? Below are a few quick portable monitor shopping tips, followed by four of our favorite current deals.

This 16-inch IPS portable monitor sports a 1920 x 1200 resolution, a built-in kickstand, is just 0.3 inches thick and weighs 1.58 pounds. You'll want to get a case for it, but this is a great screen for productivity on the go.

The VE-1 isn't quite the cheapest portable screen we've seen, but I'd argue it's the biggest bargain. It's a little larger than most, thanks to its 16:10 display, which also gives it more pixels for getting work done. It's also rated to 300 nits of brightness and is slim and light for travel. It comes in different colors if you're willing to pay a few dollars more. I like this deal so much that I bought one to replace my aging portable monitor from 2018.

This 1080p portable monitor supports refresh rates up to 144 Hz, is rated to 106% of the sRGB color space, and has a built-in kickstand, a big upgrade over screens with floppy foldble cover stands.

This FHD, higher-refresh model is better for movie watching and games, as it's 16:9 and the screen is more colorful and responsive. It's rated to 300 nits of brightness and sports two USB-C ports and a Mini HDMI.

This dual-screen 14-inch setup pairs two 1920 x 1200 14-inch IPS panels in a clamshell design with a kickstand. It weighs 2.6 pounds and is rated to 350 nits of brightness.

What's better than a portable monitor? How about two in a fold-up package that you can connect to your laptop with a USB-C cable (or HDMI) and prop up with a built-in kickstand? There are cheaper dual-screen portable screens available. But most are from no-name brands and/or cost nearly as much as this one from Asus. And this Asus model has a higher brightness rating than most dual-screen alternatives.

This 15.6-inch OLED touch monitor is rated for 400 nits of brightness and sports a pixel-dense 4K resolution as well as packing its own battery that's rfored to four hours of use. While it sadly lacks a kickstand, it does come with a protective case that also functions as a stand.

Yes, the Innocn PU15-Pre is more expensive than some other deals here. But it's also a 4K OLED panel, with deep blacks, vibrant colors, and a 282 PPI pixel density that's probably better than your laptop screen. It also has a rated 400 nits of brightness that puts it ahead of most portable monitor competition. On top of that, it brings its own battery, so you won't be quickly running down your laptop's cells to keep it going.If you're a photo or video editor on the go or you just want a pixel-dense screen that will look good next to your laptop's OLED display, this is one of the best premium options we've seen so far during this deals season. The main downside seems to be that this model lacks a kickstand, so you'll have to use the included wrap-around sleeve as a stand, and those tend to be fiddly or flimsy (and sometimes both).
