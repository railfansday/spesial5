So for something more robust, I think I have a lot of light reading to do this weekend. I get the feeling "delimited continuations" are actually a lot less scary than they sound.

I highly recommend the paper Direct implementation of shift and reset in the MinCaml compiler for the basic idea around implementing delimited control "directly". I found this paper to be the most straightforward of all the possible resources. Of course, in practice, you may wish to use a segmented stack (cactus stack) as, say, OCaml does (as of version 5).

Of course, you can cut many corners if your implementation is not compiling to machine code. For example, you can effectively write an interpreter in CPS and use the "meta continuation" of the interpreter for delimited control.
