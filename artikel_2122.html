Duo escape criticism after West Ham slow start shocker against Baggies | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Two names seemed – deservedly – absent from the long list of players singled out for culpability amongst the anger and consternation felt across fan channels and the wider football media this weekend, as pre-season favourites West Ham slumped to their third home defeat of the season.

Jarrod Bowen, without saying, put in a shift and dragged the Hammers back once with a trademark run and low shot: But two of West Ham’s youngsters both put in solid shifts, despite the result, and are deserving of warmer words than the rest of the starting line up.

Edvin Austbø, starting in place of Manor Solomon, injected pace and directness on the left hand side that was deserving of praise -yet his efforts were largely lost amongst the justifiable criticism of the team’s performance.

West Ham’s Edvin Austbø – another one who escaped criticism amidst a crap team performance

This Norwegian does have an exciting future providing Nuno doesn’t decide to evict him from the starting line up after one bad result that wasn’t of the youngster’s making.

Similarly Airidas Golambeckis, in just his second start of the season – only eighteen years old, remember, deserves special praise despite an error and part-culpability in at least one of the Baggies’ strikes.

Playing alongside the tentative Maximilian Kilman would tax the abilities of any decent centre half and it is vital for West Ham’s future that he doesn’t find himself banished back to the u21’s after one start and a result that reflects badly on the whole team.

There was considerable fan encouragement for the youngster and I’d certainly pick him ahead of Max Kilman on present form.

These young players are the future of the club and need an arm around their shoulders after both put in a shift: The pair should certainly hold their heads up high and expect further first team outings, after neither of the duo let their manager, their team mates or the supporters down.
