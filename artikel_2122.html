Lewis Ferguson insists Rangers move 'will happen' in big transfer declaration

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Lewis Ferguson has revealed he expects to play for Rangers one day as the Scotland star opened up on his future.

The Bologna captain has established himself as one of the leading Scottish players abroad after moving to Italy in 2022, with his performances regularly prompting speculation over what could come next.

But the former Aberdeen midfielder has admitted a move to Ibrox is something he believes will eventually happen.

Asked about the prospect of following in the footsteps of his father and uncle, both former Rangers players, Ferguson said: “Ah, I know... Rangers? Let’s just say that sooner or later, I’ll go there; one day I know it’ll happen. [Gazzetta]

"The rest is about today: we’ve bounced back now, and we have to ride this wave.”

For now, though, Ferguson remains firmly committed to Bologna. The Scotland international revealed he received interest over the summer but ultimately decided to remain in Italy after reflecting on everything the club had given him.

He explained: “I’d taken a bit of time out. I’d had some enquiries, yes, but I wanted to figure out exactly what to do with my life, whether to change direction or carry on as I was.

“Then I thought: Bologna has given me so much. On top of that, the Champions League, the Coppa Italia won and lifted as captain, the Europa League which... if we hadn’t come up against Aston Villa, we’d have reached the final, then the moments of friendship, even the tough times like the injury.

“In short: I took my time and then said just four words: Bologna is my home. And I want to stay here for at least another year.”

Ferguson later reiterated his affection for the Italian club while refusing to put a definitive timescale on his stay. He added: “I’m certainly not thinking about how long I’ll stay, but I’m definitely thinking about how I’ll stay.

"Bologna has been home since day one, I’ll say it again, and I’m having a brilliant time here.”

The midfielder has also embraced the responsibility of captaining Bologna, an achievement he described as a major source of pride as a Scotsman playing abroad.
