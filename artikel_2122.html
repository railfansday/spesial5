Portsmouth transfer can help Birmingham City unlock Premier League millions

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Birmingham City topped the Championship table heading into the international break, as the mood around St Andrews is sky-high right now, though head coach Chris Davies cannot afford to rest on his laurels.

Birmingham have had almost two weeks sitting at the top of the Championship table following a recent run of excellent displays and results, as under Davies, the Blues look like serious automatic promotion contenders.

With eight wins, five draws, and just two defeats after 15 games, Birmingham supporters couldn't have wished for much more at this stage, and having transformed from a free-flowing attacking side into one increasingly difficult to beat, their squad hasn't left much room for improvement looking ahead to the January window.

However, it would be a shock if Davies wasn't already lining up some potential moves to give his side the best possible chance of getting across the line and back to the Premier League, as another attacking midfielder wouldn't go amiss.

Following the departures of Kyogo Furuhashi and Kanya Fujimoto, Birmingham were expected to bring in another body in the attacking midfield position to rival Jay Stansfield for minutes.

However, despite their best efforts, they couldn't get a deal over the line, as both Stansfield and, more recently, Max Bird have occupied that position to varying degrees of success.

Though neither has done a bad job per se, Bird is certainly used to playing slightly deeper and Stansfield slightly further forward, as Birmingham should look to rekindle their interest in a former summer target this winter.

Although Birmingham couldn't get a replacement for Kyogo or Fujimoto over the line, it wasn't for lack of trying, with Ipswich Town's Anis Mehmeti being heavily courted by the Blues.

The Albanian would decide to stay put at Portman Road and fight for his place, as Birmingham sought fresh targets and landed on Portsmouth's Croatian forward Adrian Segecic.

Segecic, 22, enjoyed an exceptional debut year in the Championship with Pompey following his summer move from Sydney FC in 2025, scoring ten and assisting five in 38 league appearances under John Mousinho, and emerged as a potential option for Birmingham.

Capable of playing anywhere across the final third but best through the middle, Segecic hasn't quite hit the heights he has proven he is able to this time around as yet, but remains one of the hottest forward prospects in the division.

With either Liam Millar or Demarai Gray off the left, Carlos Vicente or Patrick Roberts off the right, and either August Priske, Luis Vazquez, or Jay Stansfield up top, Birmingham could really do with another star attacking midfielder to complete their forward line.

Although Stansfield is perfectly capable of playing at the number ten position himself, the Englishman could do with some fierce competition, and Segecic would offer just that.

Still only 22, the Croatian has ample time to improve his craft and to develop into a top player, as Birmingham should look to invest early in the forward by striking a deal with Portsmouth this January.

Pompey will certainly have no intention of parting ways with Segecic anytime soon, but with a contract running into the summer of 2028, he could enter the final 12 months of his deal at the end of the season, which would significantly weaken Portsmouth's stance on the matter.

The South Coast outfit will do everything in their power to tie him down to fresh terms sooner rather than later and avoid a sale to a division rival, though Birmingham have the financial muscle to get a deal done; for the £200 million jackpot of Premier League promotion, he will be worth every penny and then some if he can get the Blues over the line.

Davies will have few complaints over his current Birmingham squad as things stand, but this doesn't mean he shouldn't be eyeing up potential additions the second the January window rolls around.

If Blues can sustain this level of form and performance into January, few could deny that they are serious Championship promotion contenders, and with an added little sprinkle of stardust, it could turn into a special year at St Andrews.

Segecic could be exactly what Birmingham needs to get over the line, and Davies must reignite his interest in the Croatian ASAP to ensure this potential bargain doesn't slip through the cracks.
