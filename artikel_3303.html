‘Dìdi’s Sean Wang To Direct ‘How To Rule The World’ For Warner Bros.

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: In 2023, Warner Bros. and Amy Pascal snatched up rights to a Theo Baker bestseller, How to Rule the World: An Education in Power at Stanford University, in a competitive auction. We broke that story, and now, we can tell you that the project has a director in Sean Wang, the Oscar nominee and Independent Spirit Award winner behind Focus Features’ Dìdi.

Wang and Matthew Libby are adapting the screenplay, with Pascal and Rachel O’Connor set to produce for Pascal Pictures.

Published in May, Baker’s How to Rule the World is both a coming-of-age memoir and a critique of some of our most powerful institutions. The book recounts his freshman year at Stanford, when the aspiring coder wound up working on investigative reporting as part of the student newspaper. His coverage sparked an investigation into allegations of research misconduct involving then-Stanford president Marc Tessier-Lavigne and his earlier work at Genentech, the biotechnology corporation based in San Francisco. Tessier-Lavigne resigned in the wake of this reporting, which made Baker the youngest-ever recipient of the prestigious George Polk Award. He graduated from Stanford just this year and has served as Senior Silicon Valley Reporter for The San Francisco Standard since October.

An instant New York Times bestseller, Baker’s book has been named a Tom Wolfe Literary Prize finalist, one of Amazon’s Best Books of 2026, one of Time magazine’s 100 Must-Read Books of 2026 and a Best Book of the Year (So Far) by the New York Times, Barnes & Noble and Apple Books.

Wang made waves with his first narrative feature, Dìdi (弟弟), which premiered in competition at the 2024 Sundance Film Festival, where it won the U.S. Dramatic Audience Award and the Special Jury Prize for Best Ensemble Cast. Dìdi is a semi-autobiographical coming-of-age film following Chris, a 13-year-old Taiwanese American coming up in Fremont, California during the summer of 2008. Along the way, he navigates friendships, crushes, and questions surrounding his identity in the early days of social media.

Wang’s film was shaped by his participation in the 2023 Sundance Screenwriters and Directors Labs. Even before it, though, he clearly demonstrated his promise with Nǎi Nai & Wài Pó (Grandma & Grandma), a documentary short that won both the Grand Jury Prize and an Audience Award at SXSW 2023 before going on to an Academy Award nomination.

In 2024, Wang was named a BAFTA Breakthrough Artist and received the Sundance Vanguard Award for Fiction. He later earned a Directors Guild of America nomination for Outstanding Directorial Achievement of a First-Time Theatrical Feature Film, while Dìdi won Independent Spirit Awards for Best First Feature and Best First Screenplay. Also a Google Creative Lab Five alum, a Young Guns 19 winner and a member of the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, Wang is repped by WME and Hansen, Jacobson, Teller.

Baker is repped by CAA and published by Penguin Press.
