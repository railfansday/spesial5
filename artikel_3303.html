Anthropic shares fall more than 10% on day two as the AI sell-off spreads

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Underwriters’ extra shares lifted the total raised to $119.5bn, as Anthropic shares traded below Thursday’s close. Global stocks are heading for their longest losing streak in more than three years.

Anthropic logo, Guilherand-Granges, France, March 2025

Anthropic shares fell more than 10% in premarket trading on Friday, as of 7:25am in New York, Bloomberg’s Carmen Reinicke and Sangmi Cha reported. The stock rose as much as 39% on Thursday before closing 17% up, its first day of trading.

The fall came as the underwriters exercised their over-allotment option, known as a greenshoe, Bloomberg reported. The option lets the banks sell extra shares after trading starts. Anthropic granted them up to 147 million more shares, according to its pricing announcement.

That lifts the total raised by Anthropic and its selling co-founders to $119.5bn, up from the $107.3bn at pricing. The IPO accounts for $93.5bn and the private placement for $26bn.

“We’ve raised almost $120 billion. We think that positions us really well,” chief financial officer Krishna Rao told Bloomberg Television’s Ed Ludlow on Thursday.

Global stocks are heading for their longest losing streak in more than three years, Bloomberg reported. MSCI’s Asia Pacific index fell 3.3% to its lowest level since April, and Nasdaq 100 futures slipped 1.8%.

In Tokyo, SoftBank fell 14.5%, its worst day in more than two years, The Guardian reported. Taiwan’s market dropped 6.7%, with TSMC down about 6%. Britain’s FTSE 100 hit a five-month low.

Investors have spent weeks worrying about Nvidia’s role as a backstop for the AI industry after Jensen Huang’s ouster, Bloomberg reported. The Democratic sweep in the midterm elections could also make new data centres harder to approve. Bloomberg named Apple’s plans for its own autonomous AI agent as another blow to sentiment.

Anthropic could be “draining market liquidity”, IG’s chief market analyst Chris Beauchamp said, according to The Guardian. Investors are selling other shares to pay for its stock, he said.

“What’s important for markets now is Anthropic is the most transparent real-time signal of the AI risk trade,” Jordan Watson, managing director of investments at Tandem Investment Advisors, said, according to Bloomberg.

ARK Invest bought $90.8m of Anthropic shares across six funds after the debut, according to a trade notification from the firm, CNBC’s John Melloy reported. ARK has held private shares through its venture fund since early 2023, Wood said. Anthropic is a 0.3% stake in the $6.1bn ARK Innovation ETF.

“We’re climbing a wall of worry,” Wood told CNBC’s Squawk Box on Friday.

More demand may come from index funds. If Anthropic qualifies for early inclusion, it could join the Russell 1000 and the Nasdaq 100 as soon as December, according to Bloomberg.

Anthropic reported a $567m operating loss in the third quarter, Bloomberg reported. The main driver was $5bn of computing costs under short-term commitments. There were also $2bn of non-cash charges tied to its donation-matching programme.

Goldman Sachs analysts expect Anthropic to reach positive annual free cash flow only in 2030. They model its revenue at about $685bn that year.

“A large part of the value is in the future, which is uncertain, so the share price movements will reflect that,” Vey-Sern Ling, managing director at Union Bancaire Privée, said, according to Bloomberg.
