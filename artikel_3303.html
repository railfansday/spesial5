Thomas Markle Facing New Child Sex Abuse Charges in the Philippines

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Thomas Markle Sr., the estranged father of Meghan Markle, is facing new child sex abuse charges in the Philippines.

The Philippine National Police have filed three charges with prosecutors in the city of Cebu, where Markle reportedly lived until May, according to ITV News. These include two counts of offering, distributing, selling, exporting, or importing child sex abuse material, and one count of possessing child sex abuse material.

The prosecutors in Cebu will evaluate the charges before deciding if they will issue a subpoena. Police are also still investigating the case, which came about after Markle’s son, Thomas Markle Jr., reported to authorities that he discovered “hundreds, if not thousands” of child sexual abuse images at his father’s home.

The news comes just weeks after Markle was arrested in Florida and charged with one count of possession of child pornography. He was initially released on bail and placed under house arrest, but was taken into custody a few days later after he cut off his ankle monitor. Markle has yet to enter a plea and is waiting to undergo a court-ordered medical evaluation.

A lawyer for Markle did not immediately return a request for comment.

While the investigation into Markle originated with his son’s claims in the Philippines, American investigators picked it up after learning that Markle rented two storage units in Los Angeles. From those storage units investigators said they seized approximately 78 items, including slides, film negatives, and books that contained suspected child sex abuse material.
