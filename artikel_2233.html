‘All of a sudden’: Sheffield United in strong position ahead of next three fixtures, says EFL expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Sheffield United have risen up to 10th in the league after their first 13 fixtures and they are three points off the play-offs behind West Ham in 8th. SUFC won 1-0 at home against Wrexham last time out with Ben Brereton Diaz getting their goal.

The Blades are back in action on Tuesday evening with an away trip to Deepdale to face rock bottom Preston North End. They then face struggling Charlton Athletic and Watford.

Maxwell has delivered his verdict on the South Yorkshire outfit on the Not The Top 20 Podcast on YouTube: “Maybe the most impressed I’ve been with them this season (against Wrexham). That is quite a lot bar to clear I will say. (Romelle) Donovan looked good. I think increasingly (Michael) Cooper in goal, (Japhet) Tanganga and (Liam) Kitching at centre-back, (Sydie) Peck and (Kalvin) Phillips in midfield, it’s quite a nice spine.

“All of a sudden they’re up in 10th Sheffield United having had what felt like a really poor start to the season. They’ve got Preston next away and then Charlton away, after the break Watford at home so 10th right now with three fixtures on paper against teams right down at the bottom.”

On his day, Sheffield United goalkeeper Michael Cooper is one of the best players in his position in the division. He is then backed up on the bench by the experienced Adam Davies.

The Blades have been solid defensively so far in this campaign. Cooper has kept five clean sheets along with Stoke City’s Viktor Johansson and Birmingham City’s James Beadle, with only Swansea City’s Lawrence Vigouroux keeping more.

As Maxwell has alluded to, Japhet Tanganga and Liam Kitching have formed a solid partnership in the heart of defence. The latter joined from Premier League new boys Coventry City in the summer and he has made a positive impression.

Sheffield United have useful full-backs like Femi Seriki and Harrison Burrows who like to get up and down the flank. Further up the pitch, Kalvin Phillips, Sydie Peck and Oli Arblaster are all good options to have in the middle of the park.

Patrick Bamford is a proven goal scorer up top for Wilder’s side. They also have dangerous attackers including Brereton Diaz, Romelle Donovan, Tom Cannon and Tyrese Campbell at their disposal as well so the competition and depth is decent.

Despite not being able to spend much money in the last transfer window, Sheffield United still have a strong squad. SUFC also have an experienced manager in Wilder.

There is no reason why the Blades can’t push for the play-offs this term. They have a nice blend of youth and experience in their ranks.

They will need to be at their best to get a win against Preston North End, regardless of the Lilywhites’ position and poor form. No game is easy and Wilder will be aware that it could be a potential banana skin for his players.
