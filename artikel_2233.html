Jim Goodwin says Dundee Utd are eager to keep momentum going against Motherwell

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Jim Goodwin insists Dundee United are raring to go against Motherwell following their unwanted free weekend.

After a morale-boosting 2-2 draw with Celtic at Parkhead which extended their unbeaten run to six games, the Tannadice side had to miss their next William Hill Premiership fixture against Hearts as the Jambos had a Premier Sports Cup semi-final match against Hibernian.

Ahead of the visit of the Steelmen to Tannadice on Saturday, Goodwin told DUTV: “We’ve had a really good week training, obviously with no football at the weekend. That can be a little bit frustrating because, especially off the back of such a good performance against Celtic, you’re desperate for the next game to come around quick.

“But we’ve had a solid week’s training. The boys have done seven or eight sessions over the four days that we’ve been in. We’re having to drag them off the training pitch at times because they’re so keen to stay out there.

“Hopefully, there should be a lot of fresh legs there and hopefully we can go and give a good account of ourselves and maintain this unbeaten run that we’re on.”

United’s last defeat, coincidentally, was a 3-0 loss to Motherwell at Fir Park in September where Goodwin’s side were reduced to 10 men in 14 minutes when Emmanuel Agyei was sent off for a foul on Alex Lowry.

Goodwin is reading little into that defeat. He said: “For the first 15 minutes I didn’t think there was a great deal between the two teams. But then we get the red card to Emmanuel Agyei and that changes the whole game.

“They’ve got good pace. With the man extra they can really find pockets of space all over the pitch and they punished us on the night. But it’s a very hard game for me to analyse because of the fact that we were down to 10 men for 75 minutes.

“So I think tomorrow, if we can keep 11 men on the pitch, that would certainly be a start. I think 11 v 11, it makes for a very entertaining game of football. Two really good teams, two technical teams.

“They’ve got that attacking threat and that bit of firepower at the top with the pace they have. But we also believe we’ve got real firepower ourselves and real quality in the middle of the pitch where hopefully we can get that bit of control that we want.”
