WeatherBug Ushers in a New Season for Weather Apps With a Radically Helpful New Forecasting Experience

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Reimagined app and website make personalized weather easier to understand, turning complex data into clear, actionable insights for everyday life

NEW YORK, Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Americans are constantly checking the forecast. Nearly three-quarters of Americans 1 (73%) check the weather at least once a day, and a whopping 90% say the weather impacts their mood, plans or routines, according to a new WeatherBug survey conducted by The Harris Poll.

But for all the weather information at our fingertips, the forecast doesn't always make the next decision easier. More than half of weather app users (53%) say they're often unsure whether to change their plans after checking their weather app, while 39% say their weather app alerts often feel more stressful than helpful. The problem isn't a lack of weather data. It's understanding what that data means for your day.

Today, the sun is rising on a new day for weather apps. WeatherBug unveils the biggest evolution of its digital experience in 15 years, with a completely reimagined app and website designed to make local weather easier to understand, faster to navigate and more useful in the everyday moments that matter most.

"Rather than simply putting more meteorological information in front of people, we designed the new WeatherBug to put the forecast into context and make it more useful in everyday life," said Gundeep Hora, President of WeatherBug. "Checking the weather is really just the beginning. People want to know: How close is that storm to my house? Is the air quality good enough for my run? Should I grab an umbrella on my way out? WeatherBug humanizes the forecasting experience, offering clearer guidance for whatever the day has in store."

The update represents a major evolution for WeatherBug as it shifts from a traditional weather data provider toward a more human-centered weather companion.

Taking the Guesswork Out of the ForecastAt the heart of the new experience is a simple idea: less decoding, more understanding.

The redesigned home screen puts the hourly forecast front and center, while simplified navigation makes key features easier to access. Across the app and website, major weather experiences have also been upgraded.

From Data to DecisionsTogether, these advancements reflect a broader shift in the weather forecasting category, spearheaded by WeatherBug.

"People don't necessarily need another number, chart or alert. They need to understand how the weather may impact their day," said Zeeshan Khan, Head of Product Design at WeatherBug. "We put data into context, whether that means understanding pollen levels in your neighborhood, knowing when outdoor conditions will improve or putting today's temperature in perspective. It's about moving from simply forecasting what the weather will be to helping people understand how it will impact their day."

After 30 years of helping people keep an eye on the sky, WeatherBug is looking toward the next season of weather forecasting, with the core belief that understanding the forecast is just as important as seeing it.

Available on iOS and Android, with a web experience at WeatherBug.com. WeatherBug holds a 4.8-star rating based on more than 2.2 million ratings on the Apple App Store, making it one of the most recommended and trusted weather brands.
