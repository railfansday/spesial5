Nvidia's board weighs Jensen Huang's future as Megaspeed pledges U.S. cooperation

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Nvidia's board was meeting Wednesday evening on the CEO's future, and no outcome had been reported as of early Thursday

Megaspeed, the Singapore-based data center company tied to Nvidia $NVDA CEO Jensen Huang's affair, vowed Thursday to assist American probes of possible threats to national security.

Nvidia's board was meeting Wednesday evening to discuss Jensen Huang's future, according to NBC News, which said the White House was aware of the meeting. There was no word on an outcome as of early Thursday, according to The Associated Press.

In a statement, its first since Jensen Huang acknowledged the affair, Megaspeed said compliance with U.S. export control laws is a serious obligation. The statement carried the signature of Chairman and CEO Tan Yong Pong, who is also known as James Tan.

Megaspeed said it has taken part in inspections carried out by the U.S. Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security and is willing to assist any other American agency with a relevant role. It said it has brought on a U.S. law firm to oversee a count of its graphics processing units and to audit how it complies with export controls. The company said it "welcomes appropriate government scrutiny."

The statement said nothing about the claims involving Alice Huang, according to NBC News. She is a former Megaspeed executive, and U.S. spy agencies concluded she was romantically involved with Jensen Huang and was working as an operative for Beijing’s intelligence services, NBC News said. China has denied the allegations. A spokesperson for China’s Foreign Ministry said Thursday that Washington should quit inventing spy stories to justify blocking China’s access to technology.

Last year, Megaspeed was accused of covertly funneling high-end Nvidia chips to buyers in China. The company maintained in December that there was “no evidence” any such shipments had occurred, according to the AP. Singapore says it is looking into the claims, and Megaspeed is the subject of a criminal probe in the U.S., according to NBC News. President Donald Trump has announced that the FBI is investigating, according to the AP.

Nvidia's board announced an independent investigation on Sunday, to be overseen by a committee of independent directors. The board said it had no reason to believe that Nvidia's proprietary information had been put at risk or that U.S. security had been breached. The directors retained former acting U.S. Attorney Joon H. Kim and the law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to conduct the inquiry, the company said Tuesday.

Nvidia is scheduled to report fiscal third-quarter earnings on Nov. 17. Three House Republican committee chairs have asked Jensen Huang to appear for a private session with lawmakers on Nov. 18.

Sen. Elizabeth Warren, a Massachusetts Democrat, and Sen. Chris Coons, a Delaware Democrat, wrote Wednesday to Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick asking that records on the matter be preserved, according to NBC News. Nvidia stock has fallen since Jensen Huang acknowledged infidelity in his marriage.
