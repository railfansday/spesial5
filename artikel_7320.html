Pernod Ricard USA Hires VML as Commerce Agency of Record

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

One of the largest spirits makers, Pernod Ricard USA, has named its next commerce agency of record.

WPP’s VML will handle U.S. shopper and in-store marketing for the Jameson, Absolut, Kahlúa, and Malibu brands in addition to the company’s ready-to-drink offerings.

The companies’ first work will begin with Jameson’s partnership as the official spirits sponsor of the NFL. Other work includes Kahlua & Absolut’s holiday espresso martini and Jameson’s St. Patrick’s Day program in 2028.

VML said it plans to work on creative development, point-of-sale materials, retailer-specific adaptations, digital commerce assets, and sales enablement tools. The appointment followed a review that began in March, according to VML.
