Grand Theft Auto 6 launch day - all the news and rection as GTA 6 arrived around the world

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Actors revealed! Viewership records broken! Lots of honorary New Zealanders!

The day is finally here: Grand Theft Auto 6 is available to play worldwide. It's destined to become the largest video game release of all time, and probably one of the biggest entertainment releases of all time. So we're doing a launch day live blog to chart its arrival in the world.

Have you played it yet? I'm curious how many people stayed up until midnight in their region to give the game a go. I couldn't manage it but my college-age son was contemplating it. And if you have played it, what do you think - what are your early impressions? Is this what you were waiting for?

More broadly, I wonder about the impact GTA 6 will have on the wider world. I've already seen nearly all mainstream news and media outlets attempt to cover the game - and many other kinds of businesses - so will GTA 6 make people reappraise video games?

Moreover, exactly how big will the launch be? The record books are ready to be written in, but what exactly will be written? And how will other games fare in GTA's wake - better, or worse? There's lots to consider as the ripples spread around the world.

We'll pull-in stories and developments, and your comments, as we chart the arrival of Rockstar's game around the world. Let's get to it.

Rockstar employment tribunal verdict could take weeks

The din of release is in danger of drowning out the Rockstar employment tribunal that came to a close this week in Scotland. Former Eurogamer video producer Chris Bratt - now co-owner of People Make Games - has been following the case closely, and reported yesterday that a verdict in the case could take "weeks".

He quotes Judge Lucy Wiseman as saying: "I cannot give a time frame for producing the judgement in this case. What I will say is that I will use my best endeavors to get that judgement out as quickly as I possibly can."

GTA 6 is Metacritic's highest rated game ever

A notable moment yesterday was GTA 6 being crowned as the highest-rated game ever on review-averaging site Metacritic. The game currently holds a 99 score there, averaged from 156 critics' reviews. The previous champion was the original Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, from 1998, with the same 99 average critics score. Will it retain the title as the days and weeks unfold?

Chris' expansive GTA 6 review is of course one of those contributing to that score. "A game of opposites and awe, minute detail and impossible scale, Rockstar pushes back the horizon of video games once again with GTA 6. An irreducible mix of silliness, intimacy, grand ideas and rock 'n' roll action - and a load more after that."

More ridiculous GTA 6 launch piggybacking and this time it's an email with a therapist talking about how the game might help teens with their dating outlooks.

Says Natalie Buchwald, therapist and founder of Manhattan Mental Health Counseling:

"Psychologists sometimes talk about 'relationship scripts': the unspoken expectations we absorb about how relationships work, what partners owe each other and what success looks like. We pick these up long before we have much experience of our own, from family, friends, online spaces and the stories we see in entertainment.

"No single film, game or influencer shapes someone's values on its own. What matters more is the overall mix of messages a person encounters, and whether they have the space to question them. That's particularly relevant now that dating advice is so widely available online, where some content frames relationships around status, control or getting something in return, rather than mutual respect.

"That's why the most useful thing parents can do isn't to hand over a set of rules. It's to help young people think critically about the scripts they're picking up. Being curious rather than critical, and asking what they've seen, what they think of it and what they believe makes a good partner, tends to keep conversations open.

"Entertainment can play a helpful role here. A story built around a partnership, like Jason and Lucia's, can give parents a natural, low pressure starting point for conversations that might otherwise feel awkward."

Chris referenced some of this in his review, praising Jason's tender and caring side, and his not being a toxic asshole. GTA 6 is also the first game to not only feature a couple but a female playable lead, having very much revolved around male fantasies in the past.

GTA 6 developers fear for their jobs now the game has shipped

Rockstar and Take-Two stand to make billions of dollars from GTA 6, but there's an enormous workforce - 4,711 people at last count - working at the GTA studio. Will they keep their jobs now the game has come out? That staff-count more than doubled after Red Dead Redemption 2 was released but will it grow again now?

Fears of lay-offs reach top volume both as the game is released and as Rockstar workers vote wheter to formally recognise a workers union there - a union that's proved so problematic in the past. Sherif has the full story below.
