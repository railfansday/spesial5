Japan making AI app to learn Japanese by talking to anime characters

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Japan is developing a new language-learning app that will let anime fans practice their Japanese by having AI-powered conversations with characters from their favorite shows.

Anime and manga have become hugely popular around the world, introducing millions of people to Japanese culture and encouraging many to learn the language themselves.

Now, the Japanese government is hoping to capitalize on that popularity with an app that makes learning Japanese feel more like interacting with your favorite characters.

As reported by The Japan Times, Japan’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs has asked the Japan Foundation to develop an AI-powered language-learning app aimed primarily at younger audiences overseas.

Rather than simply studying vocabulary or completing exercises, users would be able to practice speaking Japanese by having conversations with characters from popular anime and manga.

While no specific characters or franchises have been announced just yet, it would likely mean users could converse with the likes of Goku from Dragon Ball Z or Monkey D. Luffy from One Piece.

The Japan Foundation has previously developed language-learning tools featuring original characters, but licensing restrictions have prevented it from using recognizable faces from existing franchises. For the new app, it plans to negotiate with copyright holders to secure permission to use famous characters.

The government hopes the app will encourage more people to learn Japanese while also making lessons accessible in countries where qualified teachers and dedicated language schools are harder to find.

It could also give anime fans a new way to engage with the characters they love, although they’ll have to wait to find out which ones will be available.
