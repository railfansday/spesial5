Muse to Headline BST Hyde Park & Roundhay Festival in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The British rockers will headline both events next June.

BST Hyde Park’s lineup is continuing to grow with another huge act set to play: British rockers Muse. The Devon-formed band will perform at the iconic London park on June 26, 2027 and head to Roundhay Festival in Leeds the following day. Both performances will be joined by Nothing But Thieves, with CHVRCHES on the bill for BST Hyde Park only. Muse are the first headliners to be revealed for Roundhay Festival, which launched its first edition in 2026 with performances from Pitbull and Lewis Capaldi. Roundhay Festival is operated by AEG, and often shares performers with BST Hyde Park.

Presales begin at 10 a.m. (GMT) on Nov. 9, followed by a general sale on Nov. 13 at the same time. Get tickets here.

BST Hyde Park has already announced a number of headliners for its upcoming 2027 edition, with Bon Jovi set to kick off the festival run on June 24. On July 10, RAYE will perform a homecoming show at the festival supported by Sienna Spiro.

Additional names for 2027 edition of BST Hyde Park will be announced soon, following a 2026 season that featured Garth Brooks, ATEEZ, Maroon 5, Mumford & Sons, Duran Duran, Pitbull and Lewis Capaldi.

Muse has scored eight consecutive No. 1s on the U.K.’s Official Albums Chart, including latest LP The Wow! Signal, which peaked at No. 41 on the Billboard 200. The band is currently on a European arena tour, with a pair of shows at London’s O2 Arena and Manchester’s Co-op Live set to take place later this month.
