Aaron Paul is joined by correspondent John Murray, former England internationals, Matt Upson and Rob Green and Spanish football expert Guillem Ballague to react to a thrilling 3-2 win for England against the world champions Spain in Madrid. The result means England top their Nations League group. The panel discuss England’s impressive opening hour which had them 3-0 up – as well as the important saves made by goalkeeper James Trafford as England held on. We also hear from goalscorers Harry Kane and Ben White, as well as manager Thomas Tuchel. TIMECODES: 07:32 - Harry Kane interview 20:06 - Ben White interview 22:07 - Thomas Tuchel interview
