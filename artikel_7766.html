Former district attorney sentenced to prison in bribery scheme

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Jody Owens, the former Hinds County, Mississippi, district attorney, at a legislative hearing in 2023. Owens was sentenced to more than three years in prison after being convicted of bribery. (Photo by Rogelio V. Solis/The Associated Press)

The former district attorney of Mississippi’s largest county was sentenced Thursday to more than three years in prison after being convicted of bribery.

Jody Owens, the former Hinds County, Mississippi, district attorney, was accused of soliciting $250,000 in bribes in exchange for awarding city contract bids to certain people in a scheme that also ensnared other city officials, the Associated Press reports.

“He took an oath to uphold the law, and yet he solicited $250,000 in bribes to rig what should have been a fair and open bidding system for city contracts,” said U.S. District Judge Daniel P. Jordan III of the Southern District of Mississippi.

In addition to his prison sentence, the court also ordered Owens to pay a $100,000 fine.

Owens ran for office as a criminal justice reformer, promising to focus on prosecuting violent crime while looking for alternatives to incarceration for nonviolent offenders.

“I can’t begin to tell you the shame I feel,” Owens said in court. “I betrayed myself, my family, my friends and my colleagues by my foolish and repugnant actions.”
