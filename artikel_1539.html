Exclusive: Strong belief at Liverpool they've signed midfielder to replace Alexis Mac Allister

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Liverpool believe they have done it, they've found their Alexis Mac Allister replacement.

For now, the Argentine is staying at Anfield. That has been decided largely by Andoni Iraola and Mac Allister's resurgence under the Spaniard.

This time last year, Mac Allister looked like a shadow of himself. Approaching 30-years-old, it seemed that he had lived his best days as a footballer

A parting of ways seemed natural. Especially with Dominik Szoboszlai and Ryan Gravenberch both earning new deals at the same time when Mac Allister wasn't even in negotiation with the club.

There was supposedly interest in him during the summer window as well. But no club made a concrete offer. Had anyone been willing to meet Liverpool's asking price, Mac Allister would probably not have stayed at Anfield.

Liverpool in talks to extend Mac Allister's contractHow the tables have turned. The 28-year-old has now become the most important midfielder in Iraola's system, and at this stage it feels like letting him go would make no sense. The Liverpool hierarchy are of the same belief. Julian Ward has begun talks with Mac Allister and his representatives over a new deal. Mac Allister's current deal expires at the end of the 2027/28 season. It means he's going to be entering the last 18 months of his deal. Now at this stage, there is no agreement yet. There isn't even an assurance from Liverpool that they will extend his deal. But the desire is there for all parties to reach an agreement, which means Mac Allister will likely stay at the club in the long-term.

Liverpool already believe they have Mac Allister's replacementKeeping Mac Allister is the perfect scenario for Liverpool. Especially because there's a strong belief at the club that the Argentine's replacement has already been signed. Anfield Watch can exclusively reveal that Liverpool believe Samuel Martinez, who is arriving at the club next summer, is a box-to-box midfielder who has what it takes to step into Mac Allister's shoes in the future. Many sites and analysts have claimed Martinez to be an attacking midfielder or a no.10. However, Liverpool's scouts have identified his future role to be as a no.8, similarly to Mac Allister. Martinez is currently catching the eye at the U17 World Cup with Colombia. Despite entering the tournament only recently recovering from an injury, Martinez has a goal and an assist in about 45 minutes played for his team. He has caught the eye for his vision and creativity, something Iraola is going to enjoy nurturing in the coming years.
