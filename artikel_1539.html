Bayern Munich communicates on Manuel Neuer’s injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Bayern Munich will have to do without their captain. Manuel Neuer was injured during the Bavarian victory (1-2) on the lawn of Mainz on Saturday in the Bundesliga. The German goalkeeper suffers from a partial tear of a tendon in his right foot, an injury which will keep him away from the field for an as yet undetermined period. A setback for the 40-year-old goalkeeper, whose experience and leadership remain valuable in Vincent Kompany’s collective. This absence represents a new headache for the Belgian technician, who will have to find a solution to ensure the interim in the Bavarian cages.

Bayern, however, has not communicated a precise timetable regarding the return of its captain, who will now begin his convalescence period.
