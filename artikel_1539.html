Mercato: OM, a big return announced for this winter

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

An astonishing avenue is mentioned in Marseille for the next winter transfer window. According to La Gazzetta dello Sport And Il Mattinoa former OM player, André-Frank Zambo Anguissa, would like to make his comeback with Ciel et Blanc.

The Cameroonian international midfielder (31), under contract with Naples until next June, has still not extended with the Italian club. The Neapolitan leaders would be ready to let him go in January in exchange for a check for 6 million euros.

Feasible? Given the finances of the Marseille club, this seems unlikely. And after Foot Mercatothis scenario is even completely excluded! The specialized media affirms that the Indomitable Lion did not “no intention” to leave the Partenopei during the season.

Possessed by OM from Stade de Reims in 2015, André-Frank Zambo Anguissa was sold three years later to Fulham for nearly 25 million euros. A great deal. The ex-Olympian then wore the colors of Villarreal, on loan, before landing in Italy.
