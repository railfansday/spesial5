Police Race to Madison Beer and Justin Herbert’s Los Angeles Home After Burglary Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Hollywood Hills home of Madison Beer and Justin Herbert was swarmed with police officers after a break-in was reported.

The Los Angeles Police Department rushed to the sprawling property after receiving a call about a break-in. However, after speaking to the caller, the 911 dispatcher upgraded the incident to a burglary.

Upon arrival, law enforcement discovered the songstress, 27, and the Los Angeles Chargers quarterback, 28, standing outside the home.

According to the LAPD, officers on the scene reported that no crime had occurred, as first reported by ABC.

The suspected break-in came just one day after tennis star Sloane Stephens' Sherman Oaks dwelling was burglarized.

Beer and Herbert sparked relationship rumors back in 2024 when he athlete was seen on set of one of her music videos.

They then confirmed their romance in October 2025 when they shared a passionate kiss on the sidelines of a Chargers game.

Herbert got down on one knee and asked Beer to marry him on July 26, 2026, which was their one-year anniversary of becoming an official couple.

Although it is unclear whether the property of Beer and Herbert was targeted, it wouldn't be the first celebrity home to be hit by thieves.

In September, Kim Kardashian's custom Hidden Hills estate was targeted when authorities arrested a 27-year-old man on suspicion of burglary after responding to the property on Aug. 9. The reality star and her four children were not living at the residence at the time because it is undergoing renovations. A security guard alerted authorities to the incident.

Reality star Morgan Stewart also said her home was burglarized after a security guard fell asleep, declaring, "Everyone I know has gotten robbed."

And Herbert isn't the first athlete who has had their property targeted.

In Feb. 2025, a group of Chilean men were arrested in Ohio in connection with a series of burglaries at high-value properties, including for the break-in at a mansion belonging to Cincinnati Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow.

In 2024, Travis Kelce's Kansas home was also hit, with thieves taking off with $20,000 in cash and several luxury jewelry pieces.
