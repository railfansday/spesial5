Johnson & Johnson agreed to buy eye disease biotech Ollin for $2.2 billion upfront

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Blackstone Life Sciences will pay $750 million to fully fund Phase 3 development of Ollin's lead drug candidate, OLN324

Johnson & Johnson $JNJ agreed to acquire Ollin Biosciences, a clinical-stage drugmaker focused on treatments for serious eye diseases, in a deal worth $2.2 billion upfront, the company said on Monday.

The agreement also provides for as much as $1.125 billion in additional payments if certain regulatory and commercial targets are met. J&J expects the transaction to close before the end of 2026, subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions.

The acquisition would add OLN324 to J&J's pipeline. The investigational bispecific antibody targets two pathways in retinal disease: vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) and angiopoietin-2 (Ang2). It is being developed for conditions including wet age-related macular degeneration and diabetic macular edema, which J&J said affect more than 40 million people worldwide and are leading causes of vision loss.

Kip Connor, vice president and ophthalmology disease area stronghold leader at J&J, said in a statement that OLN324 could reduce retinal fluid more effectively and allow more time between treatments. He said the data generated to date point to that potential.

J&J will enter a separate development funding agreement with Blackstone Life Sciences, contingent on the acquisition closing. Blackstone will provide J&J a total of $750 million, spread across several years, to cover the entire cost of Phase 3 development of OLN324. In return, Blackstone could receive royalties in the single digits, plus certain payments tied to regulatory and sales goals.

J&J said it will communicate the accounting treatment on or before the close of the transaction.

The Ollin deal follows J&J's June agreement to acquire Firefly Bio for $1 billion in cash. That transaction is also expected to close before the end of 2026, pending regulatory approvals and standard closing conditions.
