CargoSprint Announces Patent-Pending Dynamic Stack Management, Bringing Terminal Gate and Yard Operations Closer Together

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

eModal Pre-Gate feature coordinates gate appointments with yard operations to optimize import delivery, increase throughput, improve cargo fluidity, and reduce truck turn times.

ATLANTA, Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- CargoSprint, a technology company serving the global cargo industry, today announced that Dynamic Stack Management (DSM), a patent-pending module of its eModal Pre-Gate appointment platform, is now live at multiple major marine terminal operators (MTO) in the United States. DSM's novel solution works in conjunction with CargoSprint's payment and appointment platforms to optimize import delivery processing, increase terminal throughput, improve cargo fluidity, and reduce truck turn times.

DSM uses expanded container lifecycle data to promote optimal import appointment timeslots. Ongoing communication with terminal systems to update container status and yard position enables closer coordination between gate and yard operations while improving the overall terminal experience. Less time spent at the terminal for the trucker and fewer container rehandles produce a better experience for terminals and truckers alike â and ultimately the shippers they serve.

"Marine terminals are being asked to move more cargo with the same footprint and equipment, creating a gap between gate and yard operations is where velocity is lost," said Caro Krissman, CEO, CargoSprint. "Dynamic Stack Management (DSM) brings the two together, so appointments and yard operations work as one. The results our terminal partners are seeing show what's possible when scheduling and yard operations work together. I'm proud of our team for innovating in this space on behalf of our customers and the community."

This patent pending module is now available as an add-on to eModal Pre-Gate and configurable to fit each terminal's operational needs. By coordinating the appointment timeslots offered to truckers with yard conditions as they change, DSM helps terminals optimize import delivery processing and improve cargo fluidity while reducing truck processing and turn times. It builds on the integration already supported by the terminal operating system and is currently available for rubber-tired gantry (RTG) yard operations, with a design built to extend to other operating models.
