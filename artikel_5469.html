Mortgage Applications Rise to Their Highest Level in 5 Weeks Despite Rise in Rates

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Mortgage applications increased last week, despite high interest rates, inflation concerns, and a wobbly stock market.

For the week ending Nov. 20, the Mortgage Bankers Association's Market Composite Index—a measure of total mortgage loan application volume—increased 6.1% on a seasonally adjusted basis from one week earlier.

The Purchase Index, which measures the volume of mortgage applications to purchase a single-family home, increased 5% from one week earlier after seasonal adjustment, though purchase applications are down 16% from a year ago.

The average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed mortgages with conforming loan balances jumped to 7.38% this week, an increase from last week's rate of 7.31%, according to MBA calculations, and it's the highest level in five weeks.

A separate survey from Freddie Mac showed mortgage rates rose to 7.28%, up from  7.11% last week. A year ago at this time, the 30-year fixed-rate mortgage averaged 6.26%.

"While mortgage rates increased slightly on a weekly average basis, rates declined over the course of the week, which likely spurred purchase application activity,” said Joel Kan, MBA’s vice president and deputy chief economist.

“Purchase applications reached their strongest weekly pace since September but remained well behind last year’s pace," he added.

Kan also noted that the share of borrowers relying on riskier adjustable-rate mortgage loans jumped to 11.5%—its highest level in more than a year.

"At 6.39%, the ARM mortgage rate was nearly a full percentage lower than the 30-year fixed rate last week," he said.

The share of FHA loan applications decreased to 15.9% from 16.1% the prior week. The VA share of total applications decreased to 12.4% from 12.8% the prior week. And the USDA share of total applications remained unchanged at 0.5%.

The refinance share of mortgage activity decreased to 40.1% from 41.1% the previous week, but was 51% lower than the same week one year ago, as homeowners remain locked in to their lower interest rates.

"Refinance activity was flat over the week with conventional applications showing a small increase while VA refinances dropped more than 11%," Kan said. The latest mortgage application data comes following a week of stock market volatility. On Friday, the Dow Jones Industrial Average and S&P 500 briefly plunged into correction territory, defined as a 10% decline from their last peak.

The sell-off, driven by a wipeout in tech and AI stocks, came a day after Anthropic raised a staggering $81.34 billion during its IPO on Thursday, lifting the company's valuation to $1.414 trillion.Adding to the economic stress was an announcement on Wednesday from President Donald Trump that he's proposed new tariffs of up to 20% on China, the EU, and a dozen other major U.S. trading partners to take effect next year.

Mortgage rates are calculated based on several economic factors, while your loan term and credit score also influence the mortgage rate you qualify for. On the economic side, rates tend to track the 10-year Treasury note, since most 30-year mortgages are paid off or refinanced in roughly eight to 11 years.

That makes the duration on the loans roughly comparable, and mortgage lenders use the 10-year Treasury as a benchmark for setting rates, adding on a risk premium.

Long-term yields for Treasury notes are determined by a number of factors, including the supply of and demand for U.S. government debt, and investor expectations for inflation over the life of the bonds.
