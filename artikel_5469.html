Qualcomm's push into new markets drives a sales forecast that beats expectations

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Qualcomm's earnings-per-share outlook came in below analyst expectations, and its stock fell about 5% in extended trading

Qualcomm $QCOM reported fiscal fourth-quarter results on Wednesday and forecast revenue of $10.4 billion to $11.2 billion for the current quarter, a range that includes its data center business.

The company guided to non-GAAP earnings of $2.15 to $2.35 a share. Analysts had projected revenue of $10.55 billion, according to Bloomberg. The midpoint of the earnings range landed below the average analyst estimate of $2.32 a share. Qualcomm stock dropped roughly 5% in after-hours trading.

For the quarter ended Sept. 27, Qualcomm's GAAP revenue fell 8% to $10.3 billion. Non-GAAP earnings were $2.17 a share, down 28% from a year earlier. Analysts had forecast $10.1 billion in revenue and per-share earnings of $2.16.

Handset revenue fell 26% to $5.14 billion. Automotive revenue rose 89% to a record $1.99 billion, and revenue from internet-of-things products rose 4% to $1.89 billion. Analysts had projected handset revenue of $5.25 billion, according to Bloomberg, which said the automotive and internet-of-things figures beat estimates.

"We are pleased to deliver fourth quarter non-GAAP revenues at the high end of our guidance," CEO Cristiano Amon said in a statement. Amon said non-handset revenue is projected to grow more than 60% in fiscal 2027, and the company reiterated its fiscal 2027 data center revenue target of $5 billion.

Qualcomm said the semiconductor industry is facing a broad increase in input costs across wafer fabrication, assembly, test, advanced packaging, memory and other materials. The company said it is raising product prices to reflect the higher costs and expects the full benefit in chip-segment gross margins in the second half of fiscal 2027.

For all of fiscal 2026, Qualcomm reported GAAP revenue of $43.1 billion, down 3%, and non-GAAP earnings of $10.54 a share, down 12%. The company also said it renewed long-term patent license agreements with Apple $AAPL, Huawei and Transsion and announced a multi-generational product collaboration with Amazon $AMZN for AI data centers.

Qualcomm raised its fiscal 2029 non-handset revenue target to $40 billion at its investor day in June. That plan includes a data center revenue target of more than $15 billion, along with $10 billion in automotive revenue and more than $14 billion in internet-of-things revenue.

At the same event, Qualcomm said Meta $META Platforms would use its Dragonfly C1000 server processors, which are expected to be available in 2028. Qualcomm also agreed to acquire AI software startup Modular in an all-stock deal valued at $3.92 billion.
