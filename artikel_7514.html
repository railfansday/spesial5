Top 50 Most Subscribed YouTube Channels Worldwide • Week Of 11/08/2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

SASHA SHOW was the top dog in our subscriber charts during the month of October, and his hot streak continued into November. The Turkey-based creator picked up 1.4 million weekly subscribers during the first measurement period of the month. That brought his total YouTube following up to 17.2 million.

Though a creator based in Turkey is dominating the Global Sub Top 50, India is still the most ubiquitous country in the ranking. 14 of the top 50 channels hail from that nation, and a few of them have discovered a science experiment that reliably delivers millions of views each day.
