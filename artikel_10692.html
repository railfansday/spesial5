Pokemon Center is selling customizable keychains so fans can show off their full team of six

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Pokemon Center is giving trainers a way to show off their favorite team beyond the game with new customizable keychains that can hold six Pokemon at once.

The official Pokemon account in Japan teased the products on November 3, showing a red keychain display filled with individual Pokemon pieces and extra charms. The post has already attracted more than 900,000 views, making the surprisingly practical merch line a hit with fans.

The collection is called My Pokemon Party and is designed around the idea of building your own party from the franchise’s 1,025 Pokemon. Fans can choose between three six-slot keychain designs, including a Pikachu version, a Pokedex-inspired version, and a basic design.

Each Pokemon piece is sold separately for 88 yen, allowing fans to mix and match their favorites instead of being locked into a pre-selected lineup. The six-Pokemon keychain base costs 1,320 yen.

The display can also be customized with additional charms based on Poké Balls and held items. Available options include the standard Poke Ball, Great Ball, Ultra Ball, Master Ball, Quick Ball, Love Ball, Moon Ball, Dream Ball, and many others.

Held-item charms include familiar competitive staples such as Leftovers, Focus Sash, Choice Scarf, Choice Band, and Choice Specs.

The My Pokemon Party collection launches at Pokemon Center stores in Japan on November 7. Pokemon Center Online will begin selling the products on November 5 at 10 a.m. local time, while the official announcement currently lists Japanese stores and online sales.
