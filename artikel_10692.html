Papa Johns is giving away free pizza to customers who take pictures of their orders

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Papa Johns is offering customers a free pizza simply for taking a picture of their order and sharing their honest feedback with the company.

The pizza chain has launched a new promotion called “Snap a Pizza, Get a Free Pizza,” encouraging customers to photograph their food and review their experience in exchange for a reward.

The two-week promotion is part of Papa Johns’ broader effort to improve its food and customer service by collecting direct feedback from people ordering its pizzas.

To participate, customers must be enrolled in Papa Rewards, the chain’s loyalty program, and opt in to receive text messages. New customers can also create an account to take advantage of the offer.

Once enrolled, customers need to place an order worth at least $15 through the Papa Johns website or mobile app.

After ordering, eligible customers will receive a text message from Papa Johns prompting them to take a picture of their pizza before eating it.

The photo must be real and unedited, and customers will also be asked to provide feedback on the pizza’s bake, crust, and overall quality.

Once the photo is confirmed, participants will receive an offer for a free large one-topping pizza in their Papa Rewards account.

However, there is one additional requirement. The free pizza can only be redeemed with a minimum $5 purchase on a future order, meaning customers will need to spend money again to claim their reward.

“We see the pizza pics, the unboxings and the hot takes online every day, and we know our customers aren’t shy about sharing what they think,” said Chris Lyn-Sue, Global Chief Marketing Officer at Papa Johns.

“With Snap a Pizza, Get a Free Pizza, we’re inviting that feedback directly. Every photo and every review helps us better understand what customers love and where we can improve as we continue our journey to deliver a better experience every time they choose Papa Johns.”

The promotion also marks the first initiative under Papa Johns’ new “Unbox Better” strategy, which was announced in November 2026.

According to the company, the strategy focuses on improving product quality, value, restaurant operations, and customer service.

By collecting photos and reviews directly from customers, Papa Johns hopes to identify areas where its pizzas and overall ordering experience can be improved.

Customers interested in participating can sign up for Papa Rewards and place an eligible order through the Papa Johns app or website during the two-week promotional period.
