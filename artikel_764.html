Jeff Bezos Says He Was “Heavily Involved” in Casting Robert Pattinson as James Bond

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Amazon founder told a conference Tuesday that he and the film leadership were initially biased against Pattinson before realizing they found their man.

The names Bezos, Jeff Bezos. And he just chose the next James Bond.

The Amazon founder and chairman told a conference Tuesday that he “was heavily involved” in selecting Robert Pattinson as the new James Bond, working alongside the Amazon MGM leadership team, producers Amy Pascal and David Heyman, and director Denis Villeneuve.

“We ran a really tight process, and we made the final decision on Friday,” Bezos said at Semafor’s Silicon Valley & The World conference. “There were a big group initially, and we selected down to five finalists, and then we did real old-fashioned screen tests of those five finalists.”

By now, those finalists are well known: Callum Turner, Josh O’Connor, Jack Lowden, Nicholas Hoult, and Pattinson, the man who ultimately secured Bond’s tuxedo.

Bezos says that they ran an elaborate screen test, with each actor performing three scenes with full costumes and sets.

“They were all amazing, but there was something special about Rob and his performances, and it was kind of immediately un-debated. Denis, the director was, we all sort of gravitated to him,” Bezos recalled. “By the way, initially, I would say we were slightly biased a little against him just because he’s already so famous. We sort of thought, maybe we should have somebody a little more unknown. But in the screening tests, it was so obvious: He is James Bond.”

The casting of Pattinson is without question the biggest move yet by Amazon since it acquired MGM and since it bought out the Broccoli family for control of the franchise.

The irony is not lost on people that the Bond franchise is filled with tech tycoons and media industrialists as villains, from Hugo Drax in Moonraker to Max Zorin in A View to a Kill to Elliot Carver in Tomorrow Never Dies.

Now James Bond is firmly controlled by one of the world’s richest man, and a mogul whose empire spans tech, media, entertainment and even rockets into space. And this time he was the one helping to call the shots.
