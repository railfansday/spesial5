Joan Smalls Says Tom Brady Was "Catalyst" for Candice Swanepoel Feud

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Joan Smalls addressed her and fellow Victoria's Secret Angel Candice Swanepoel's friendship fallout, confirming that the South African model's new romance with Tom Brady was the reason.

Joan Smalls is strutting away from relationships that don't respect girl code.

One month after rumors emerged that the Victoria's Secret supermodel was unhappy with fellow runway star Candice Swanepoel for dating her former fling Tom Brady, she confirmed the New England Patriots alum was at the center of their friendship breakup.

"Unfortunately, that was the catalyst," Smalls said during the Nov. 25 episode of The Cutting Room Floor podcast. "I was seeing this person for a period of eight months."

E! News has reached out to reps for Swanepoel and Brady for comment and has not yet heard back.

Though Smalls doesn't consider Brady to be an ex, she clarified that they casually dated the former NFL star on and off between 2023 and 2024. Swanepoel went on to spark romance rumors with Brady in June.

And for her part, Smalls emphasized that before ending her and Swanepoel's 15-year friendship—which she confirmed took place in April—they were each other's confidants about love and heartbreak.

"You've seen the relationships, situationships and people I've dated, and I've confided in you in these spaces and we never had a crossover," she reflected. "All of a sudden, I stopped seeing this person, and three months later, you text me and say, 'Oh, we have to speak about something,' which is usually not a conversation we normally have."

The Puerto Rican-born model—who had confirmed their fallout in scathing comments under her own Instagram posts last month—had a feeling that Swanepoel, 38, was planning to name him her MVP.

"I called her and I asked her, I said, 'Is this about him?' She said, 'Yes,'" she recalled. "We spoke on the phone very briefly, and I was just perplexed and lost for words."

The confrontation was especially devastating because Smalls said Swanepoel—who was previously linked to model Andrés Velencoso in 2022—had been a guiding light during her romance with the Fox Sports commentator.

"She helped me write text messages to this person," Smalls, also 38, said. "She knew intimate things. Potentially to my detriment, I overshared. I was just being open with a friend that I thought was a safe spac

As for how the conversation went? Smalls accused her former friend of committing a cardinal girl code sin: Dating a guy your friend was romantically involved with.

"She came across as justifying her actions," she explained. "She started diminishing my experience to make herself feel better about the situation, which I think is what infuriated me."

Smalls believed that Swanepoel—who shares two sons with ex Hermann Nicoli—refused to take accountability for being a bad friend.

"It was this whole attitude of 'I'm an adult. I don't need permission,'" she posited. "Both are actually true: you are an adult and you don't need permission. But as a friend, you should consider me in your choices. Don't consider me in your apologies."

"Then she was like, 'Well, I'm sorry, but I'm going to explore this and so is life,'" she continued. "It was just this attitude of entitlement and lack of care towards someone who's your friend and something that I would never do to you."

Looking back at the conversation, Smalls felt that Swanepoel was looking to ease her own mind.

As she put it, "She wanted me to comfort her about her bad decision so that she didn't feel guilty by her choice and then she would constantly justify it."

It was especially difficult for the model since she prefers to keep her inner circle small.

"I rarely allow people into my life, and I'm very private," Smalls added. "So, if I let you into a space that I thought you would protect me as my friend, that's the only one thing I asked for you to do. Create a safe space for me to be myself, to open up, and you can't do that."

Following initial speculations about their fallout, Swanepoel's team detailed her perspective on their friendship.

"Candice has always been exceptionally private about her personal life, with her focus remaining on raising her children," her rep Amanda Ruisi told E! News in October. "She and Joan share a longstanding history and a friendship that Candice will always respect."
