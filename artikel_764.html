Southampton sink Middlesbrough in ill-tempered first meeting since 'Spygate'

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Report and free match highlights from the Sky Bet Championship match between Southampton and Middlesbrough at St Mary's on Friday night; goals from Ryan Manning and Finn Azaz earn Saints victory in first meeting since last season's 'Spygate'

Southampton's unbeaten start to the season at St Mary's continued as they beat leaders Middlesbrough 2-0.

Six-and-a-half months on from 'Spygate' - which dominated the headlines during last season's play-offs - Saints emerged victorious, as they did in the semi-final second leg, in an ill-tempered affair that saw 10 yellow cards brandished to players and two to coaching staff.

Had it not been for the four-point penalty imposed on them as a result, Friday night's win would have sent them to the top of the Sky Bet Championship.

But goals from Ryan Manning and Finn Azaz - who goaded his former club's fans as he celebrated - put them within touching distance of the summit.

Boro's third defeat of the season opens the door to Stoke and Wolves to leapfrog them into top spot if they win on Saturday.

In the build-up, Tonda Eckert and Kim Hellberg insisted they had buried the hatchet - but neither fanbase had forgotten it. Both sets of players were booed when they came out to warm up, while chants referencing the saga echoed around St Mary's. The atmosphere was electric.

Meaningful chances came at a premium in the first half, but they were high quality when they did come.

The home fans fell silent for a split-second when Will Lankshear ghosted away from his man and moved one-on-one with Saints goalkeeper Daniel Peretz, who denied the Championship's top scorer.

And at the other end, Radek Vitek had to be at his best, first to tip Azaz's fierce strike onto the crossbar, then to push a Cyle Larin header over.

The first goal came just before the hour. Kuryu Matsuki's cross flew over everyone in the box, but James Bree was there to collect and fizz in a low ball that Manning ran onto and side-footed high into the net.

Twenty minutes later, Azaz capitalised on a defensive mistake to calmly slot home the second, before running straight towards the visiting supporters and making a sarcastic crying gesture, sparking a heated reaction.

This is not a rivalry that is going anywhere any time soon.

Southampton's Finn Azaz speaking to Sky Sports:

"They sang about my family home and away last season, so I was just waiting for my moment to get one back.

Southampton head coach Tonda Eckert in his press conference

Southampton head coach Tonda Eckert admitted he "didn't like" a social media video posted by the club ahead of the first meeting with Middlesbrough since 'Spygate' last season.

The video included footage of Oli McBurnie's Championship play-off final winner for Hull against Boro at Wembley in May.

It was also played on the big screens at St Mary's in the lead-up to kick-off.

"I didn't like the video this morning. No, I didn't like it," Eckert said in his post-match press conference.

"I haven't even seen the video, I've just heard the information about what was in there and I didn't like it.

"I think, if we look back a couple of months, the reality is that both clubs have lost. One club has lost off the pitch, the other has lost off the pitch. I think it's an episode that is good to close for both clubs and to move forward.

"I've apologised to Kim, I've apologised openly. For me, there is no need to warm that up again."

It was pointed out to Eckert that a box of tissues had been placed on the table behind which managers sit, seemingly mocking Hellberg after his emotional reaction to the defeat in the play-off semi-final at St Mary's in May.

After a few seconds of looking at the box, he said: "You can take this one [box of tissues] off." He then placed them under the table.

"I just love to talk about football and I hope everyone who came here, especially the Middlesbrough staff and players know that," he said.

"I hope they know these things don't come from me."

Hellberg said: "They are always trying to fire everything up. I saw there were some posts at the end where they tried to mock me - or whoever they were trying to mock - so I feel a little bit small about that.

"Otherwise, there's a very good football side, so many good players, the staff are good. There's no need for that, but it is what it is."
