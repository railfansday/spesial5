Ryan Seacrest Shares His New Fitness Journey After Turning 51

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Ryan Seacrest shared that he started practicing yoga while showing off some of his new moves for co-host Vanna White during a Nov. 18 episode of Wheel of Fortune.

Ryan Seacrest is stretching his fitness routine.

The Wheel of Fortune host revealed that he’s taken a new hobby to keep him active in his 51st year of life: yoga. He even put some of his new moves on display for costar Vanna White on the Nov. 18 episode after she suggested, "Show me something."

"I'm not so good. I just started," Seacrest confessed. "But I have this mat in the office upstairs. Between our meetings, I like to get down and do some things."

The American Idol star wasn't afraid to wrinkle his sleek blue ensemble as he dropped into cobra pose, supporting his torso with his arms while curving his back. He then shifted into the pigeon position, where one leg bends toward the chest while the other extends long to bring relief to the hips.

And the burgeoning yogi shared that his inspiration for taking up the wellness activity was to maintain his mobility as he gets older. In other words, "I feel like it's important that at my age, I try and stretch it out."

Still, the radio DJ's enthusiasm for learning yoga hasn't turned him into an expert just yet.

"This is my cobra," Seacrest narrated his flow while quipping, "This is my other insect. I'm not quite sure the names of them yet."

To that end, after his humble tutorial for White, he had no choice but to divulge, "I've only done it twice," while also teasing his next spiritual pastime: "I'm taking up sound bowls next."

And it's been a year of firsts for Seacrest, who also recently received his very first airbrush tan.

"My debut spray tanning was last night. Never done it before," he admitted during a taping of On Air With Ryan Seacrest Nov. 10. "I was nervous because they told me if I go too far, you just can't really downgrade it."

As such, the Wheel of Fortune host played it safe by opting for a level two bronze and skipped tanning his face, admitting to his radio co-hosts, "I don't want that stuff on my face to cause a zit."
