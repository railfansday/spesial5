Underscores Is First Transgender Woman Nominated for Best Dance/Electronic Recording Grammy

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The 2027 nominations in this category are also significant for the number of women nominated.

The Grammys are making history this year, with first-time nominee Underscores now the first out transgender woman to be nominated for best dance/electronic recording. The artist got the nod for “Music,” from her March album U.

Many transgender women have paved the way for this accomplishment, with SOPHIE, Kim Petras, Arca, Honey Dijon, Wendy Carlos, Jackie Shane and Teddy Geiger all receiving nominations over the years. In 2023, Petras became the first transgender woman to win a Grammy, when she got the award for best pop duo/group performance for her Sam Smith collab “Unholy.”

In her acceptance speech, Petras acknowledged this achievement, saying, “I just want to thank all the incredible transgender legends before me who kicked these doors open for me so I could be here tonight. SOPHIE especially, my friend who passed away two years ago who told me this would happen and always believed in me… Madonna for fighting for LGBTQ rights. I don’t think I could be here without Madonna. My mother — I grew up next to a highway in nowhere Germany and my mother believed me that I was a girl, and I wouldn’t be here without her and her support.”

Trans women are also making history elsewhere at the 2027 Grammys, with Geese’s Emily Green being the first out trans woman nominated for best new artist, best rock performance, best alternative music performance, best alternative music album and best rock song.

The 2027 dance/electronic recording category is also significant for the number of woman nominated, with Madonna’s “I Feel So Free,” Slayyyter’s “Crank” and Tinashe’s “Melatonin” all nominated alongside Underscores and fellow nominees Fred again.. (for “Lights Burn Dimmer”) and Tame Impala (for “My Old Ways”).

This marks the first time four solo women acts were nominated for best dance/electronic recording since 2000, when the nominees were Gloria Estefan for “Don’t Let This Moment End,” Jennifer Lopez for “Waiting For Tonight,” Donna Summer for “I Will Go With You (Con te Partirò),” Fatboy Slim for “Praise You” and Cher for “Believe,” with this latter song winning the award.
