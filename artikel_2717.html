Raiders Ballers & Busters Week 11 vs Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

I wouldn't exactly call Sunday game in Denver a rollercoaster. Those go up and down. Which would suggest the Raiders were up and some point. Which they weren't. Sorry to be doom and gloom, because I know it's a nice story that Fernando Mendoza threw for three touchdowns. But it's just the truth. The best you can say is the drop flattened out for a couple minutes before resuming its free fall.

Las Vegas was down 17-0 before the comeback in the third quarter. And they were down 31-17 a few minutes later, never getting closer than seven points in the fourth quarter and allowing the Broncos scoring a season-high 38 points and win by two touchdowns.

So, all in all, it's pretty much what we had come to expect when Kirk Cousins was still behind center.

The Raiders got on the board for the first time in the second quarter. And they did so because Jeanty found a hole and was shot out of a cannon 46 yards. The offense fell apart at that point, but they got three points out of it. Next drive, Mendoza saw Jeanty was being covered one-on-one by a linebacker and threw a perfect ball over his shoulder in-stride and he was gone 68 yards for the score. He would finish with 79 yards rushing and 90 yards receiving for 169 total yards and a touchdown.

Early in the third quarter, with the Raiders down 17-10, the Broncos lined up to go for it on fourth and one. Crosby moved to between the guard and tackle and then at the snap, burst through to keep Bo Nix from getting past the line of scrimmage to give the Raiders the ball back that the Denver 39-yard-line. Five plays later, the Raiders were in the end zone to tie it up at 17-17.

Late in the fourth quarter, with the Raiders back to a two-score deficit, Crosby batted down a Bo Nix pass on third down to help stop the Broncos with a three-and-out to give the offense a shot to cut the Denver lead in half. And they did. But in the end, they needed to do it twice and could not.

That drive for the score late in the fourth was almost all Mendoza to Bech. A 39-yard catch by Tre Tucker put the Raiders in scoring range. From there, Mendoza and Bech connected on five consecutive passes for 41 yards and the touchdown at the left pylon from seven yards out. Bech finished with career highs in catches (eight) and yards (88). And most importantly, he finally was able to step up in the absence of Brock Bowers while showing some chemistry with his new quarterback.
