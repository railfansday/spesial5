Embiid and James listed as questionable for Sixers vs. Raptors

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Embiid and James listed as questionable for Sixers vs. Raptors originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Sixers listed both Joel Embiid and LeBron James as questionable for their Thursday night game vs. the Raptors.

As has been the case on previous early-season injury reports, James' official listing was "low back/sciatica management" and Embiid's was "right foot plantar fascia injury management."

While Thursday's contest isn't part of a back-to-back, it's the sort of situation in which the Sixers have load management matters in mind for the 32-year-old Embiid and 41-year-old James. They'll host the Pacers on Sunday at 1 p.m. ET and then visit the Nets the following night.

James logged 29 minutes Tuesday in the 9-3 Sixers' loss to the Celtics and Embiid went 31. Embiid said postgame that he rolled his ankle late in the second quarter, but he was fine to play the second half.

With the Raptors facing Boston on Wednesday night, Toronto's complete injury report won't be available until Thursday afternoon. The Raptors announced Tuesday that superstar Kawhi Leonard will be sidelined for at least one more week because of his left knee injury.

Adem Bona (cervical spine sprain), Dominick Barlow (lower back stress reaction) and Ariel Hukporti (right Achilles tendon tear) will all remain out for the Sixers against the Raptors.
