Sean McVay hypes up Rams’ OC, possibly for head coaching job?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Los Angeles Rams have not had a smooth season, and the offense has under-performed relative to expectations, but Sean McVay was quick to credit offensive coordinator Nate Scheelhaase with helping Matthew Stafford stay on track in Week 10's win over the Arizona Cardinals. The Rams scored 34 points, had 436 yards of total offense, and their +22.9 EPA was the second-best total of the season for L.A.. McVay told reporters after the game that Scheelhaase deserved all the credit for Xavier Smith's 22-yard touchdown just before the half, allowing the Rams to take a 17-6 lead.

It's a perfect example of how McVay has been able to expand his coaching tree faster and wider than virtually any other head coach now or before him. The Rams are always quick to let other teams know: "We got talent on our staff" and Scheelhase could be the next to find himself running one of L.A.'s rivals.

It won't hurt that by hiring Scheelhaase, the Rams would receive third-round compensatory picks in 2027 and 2028.

The Rams hired Scheelhaase in 2024 as a passing game specialist after he spent three years at Illinois and six years at Iowa State. Among the players on those Cyclones teams was quarterback Brock Purdy.

Scheelhaase was promoted to passing game coordinator in 2025, then offensive coordinator when Mike LaFleur was hired as head coach of these very same Cardinals. Perhaps seeing LaFleur inspired McVay to brag about the possibility that he might like his new offensive coordinator even better.

But how much longer will Scheelhaase be working for McVay…and not against him?

Not a single head coach has been fired during the 2026 season. It's the latest the NFL has gone into a season without firing a head coach since 2017. That year, a coach wasn't fired until Ben McAdoo in Week 13.

Among the first teams that you would think might fire their head coach soon:

It is unlikely that Tampa Bay fires Bowles in the middle of the season because the Bucs might actually be the best team in the NFC South. It's just a really bad division. But seeing how badly Baker Mayfield needs a good play caller, Tampa Bay might love to replace Bowles with Scheelhaase in 2027.

Other teams that could get closer to making a change at head coach include the Eagles, Saints, and Panthers. It doesn't sound like the Chargers would fire Jim Harbaugh even if they go winless, instead putting the axe to Mike McDaniel. Would the Titans fire Robert Saleh after one year? Will the Colts blame Shane Steichen?

Whatever happens, Scheelhaase is sure to be among the first phone calls by teams that need a head coach and want to focus on offensive-minded play callers, similar to the success that Liam Coen is having with the Jaguars. Scheelhaase was popular last time, interviewing with the Ravens, Browns, Steelers, and Bills.

Pittsburgh reportedly thought he had a "Mike Tomlin vibe".

It's certain that Scheelhaase isn't long for the Rams. Can he stick around long enough to help right this ship in time to make the next Super Bowl?
