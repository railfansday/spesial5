Game Recap: Saints visit Bears in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The New Orleans Saints were away from home for the first time this month when they faced the Chicago Bears on Sunday. Here's a recap of everything that went down at Soldier Field.

The Bears received first, and D'Andre Swift found success on the ground and through the air as Chicago crossed midfield.

Caleb Williams completed his first three passes, including a 19-yard connection with Rome Odunze, but was incomplete on 3rd down.

Cairo Santos' 41-yard field goal gave the hosts a 3-0 lead.

Tyler Shough found Chris Olave for 14 yards with his first pass of the day, but the Saints had to punt soon after as Shough was sacked twice.

The Bears continued to run the football successfully on the ensuing series, with Williams also finding Sam Roush for 19 yards.

But electing to go for it on 4th-and-3 in field goal range, Williams' pass was incomplete, and the Saints took over.

Shough threw short to CJ Donaldson early on the next series, though the running back would fumble after the catch.

Williams took advantage of the short field. He found Odunze for 16 yards before throwing a 3-yard touchdown pass to Swift to establish a 10-0 lead.

The Saints fared no better as they got the ball back, with Shough throwing an interception near midfield early on the drive.

Zavion Thomas then broke off a 30-yard run for the hosts, helping set up a 15-yard touchdown pass from Williams to Colston Loveland.

Santos missed the extra point wide right, leaving the score at 16-0.

From there, the Saints offense found some life as Shough connected with Olave for 24 yards before Travis Etienne broke off a 46-yard run to set up 1st-and-goal.

Three plays later, on 4th-and-goal, Shough found Kamara for the 1-yard touchdown pass to get the black and gold on the board.

Trailing 16-7, the Saints quickly had the ball back again as Carl Granderson tipped Williams' first pass of the ensuing possession. Danny Stutsman secured the interception.

But the offense failed to move the ball far, and Charlie Smyth's 47-yard field goal attempt missed badly.

Chicago then went three-and-out and punted before a 30-yard scramble from Shough helped matters in the two-minute drill.

Smyth was back out for a 56-yard attempt as time expired, which he nailed this time to cut the lead to 16-10.

The Saints then went three-and-out to begin the second half.

Two runs from Swift for a total of 17 yards began the ensuing series before Williams connected with Thomas for 40 yards.

More short runs from Swift followed before the Bears came up short on 3rd down.

Santos converted from 35 yards to push the lead to 19-10.

A 10-yard pass from Shough to Olave helped the black and gold as they moved the ball past midfield, but they elected to punt on 4th-and-9 from the Bears' 40-yard line.

Chicago then only found one first down as they soon punted themselves, pinning New Orleans inside their own 10-yard line.

Shough was then sacked by Montez Sweat in the end zone for a safety.

The Saints had to punt, but they quickly had the ball back following a fumble by Swift.

Shough connected with Devaughn Vele for 16 yards and Olave for 11 more. But on 4th-and-2 at the 10-yard line, the Saints went for it and couldn't get it, turning the ball over down 21-10.

Chicago were then forced into another punt with 5 minutes left.

Shough put together a 47-yard drive from there, but on 3rd-and-19 was incomplete to Noah Fant.

Smyth's 51-yard kick made it a one-possession game, but New Orleans wouldn't see the ball again.

After an unsuccessful onside kick, Williams knelt out the clock to secure the 21-13 win for Chicago.

Attention now turns to Cincinnati, where the Saints face the Bengals on Sunday.
