GTA 6 boss sends warning to pirates who make AI-generated PC version before official release

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has responded to the idea that unofficial, AI-assisted PC ports of Grand Theft Auto 6 could start popping up shortly after it is released.

Once November 19 hits, a 13-year-long wait for Grand Theft Auto fans will finally come to an end, as GTA 6 releases for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series S/X. However, the wait will go on – at least for an unannounced period – for PC players.

Rockstar Games have often taken up to a year before they release a PC version of their games, with GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 being two big examples of that. However, there have been suggestions that, with AI assistance, PC players could find themselves with unofficial ports of the game.

Former Rockstar Games dev Obbe Vermeij said it would cost “tens of thousands of dollars” to achieve, but didn’t rule it out.

“I don’t think it’s a financial matter, in terms of a threat to our business. I do think it’s a creative matter. It’s important to everyone, our developers and our consumers alike, to deliver the experience Rockstar envisions,” Zelnick told IGN. “And at the same time, of course, we protect our intellectual property vigilantly. So we are focused on these concerns.”

It isn’t the first time he has mentioned security concerns or enhancements either.  “We are utterly focused on enhancing our technical defenses and enhancing our legal activities appropriately. And at the same time, without in any way being cavalier, every entertainment business that exists is subject to some element of piracy,” Zelnick told investors at a Q&A.

“So this is likely to invade our business to a greater or lesser extent. I think it will be manageable and we are focused on it.”

As noted, GTA 6’s PC release hasn’t yet been confirmed. There have been reports that it is slated to drop in the first quarter of 2027, which would be a rapid turnaround.

Additionally, there are questions surrounding the release of GTA Online in GTA 6, too. The multiplayer side of the game isn’t set to release alongside the single-player, but it should release “next year,” according to Twitch CEO Dan Clancy.

Clancy revealed that he had spent “a lot of time” talking to Rockstar about that, seeing as GTA RP is pretty big on Twitch.
