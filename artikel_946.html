'We feel the pain': Pochettino on 4-0 defeat at Liverpool to start Manchester United era – video

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Liverpool thrashed 10-man Manchester United 4-0 at Anfield on Sunday to climb to fourth in the Premier League. But the defeat left United 19th, only one point above bottom-placed Crystal Palace. There was no new manager bounce for Mauricio Pochettino, who saw Leny Yoro sent off in the eighth minute for a last-ditch foul on Alexander Isak.

Pochettino accepted full responsibility for the loss, but claimed the dismissal was wrong and United should have had a penalty moments earlier for a Jérémy Jacquet challenge on Bryan Mbeumo. 'It was a desperate take,"' writes Andy Hunter at Anfield.
