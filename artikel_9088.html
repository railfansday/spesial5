How do Artworks Acquire Value? New York Art Dealers David Nolan and Anton Svyatsky Weigh In.

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

How do artworks acquire value? I discussed this complex question with two New York art dealers during Art Week NYC, a citywide festival organized by ARTnews, Artforum, and Art in America.David Nolan, whose eponymous gallery has been open since 1987, gave the view of an art world veteran; Anton Svyatsky, who opened Management in 2023, provided a newer perspective.

Along the way, we discussed the role of the collector in sustaining value, the challenges of pricing artworks by emerging artists, the ways in which influence and market value have become decoupled, the casino that is the auction house and the zoo that is the art fair.At our talk on Friday, November 13 at the PMC Studio in Midtown Manhattan, Svyatsky insisted that collectors need to see themselves as responsible for an artist’s value, rather than depend on dealers and institutions for that function. This shift in perspective, and a return to collecting artists in depth, may well be the key to ridding the art world of speculators. Nolan recalled the immense changes that took place during the 1980s, and the increasing attention paid to artworks’ financial value.Speaking of casinos, a flask emerged at the mention of the new partnership between Artnet and Kalshi—you can catch that moment and other choice ones in the video of the talk above.
