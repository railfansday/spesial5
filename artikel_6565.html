Jeans and a Sweater Are Classic for a Reason—Shop Our Favorite Combos

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Jeans and a sweater are hardly a new combination, but classics are classics for a reason. After all, nothing says fall quite like a cozy knit (there’s a reason they call it sweater weather!) and the perfect pair of jeans. The pairing might be as timeless as it gets, but there are endless ways to make it feel fresh. With a world of denim silhouettes and sweater styles to choose from, the possibilities are practically limitless.

This season, we have our eye on zip-front cardigans à la Miu Miu’s sporty girl, which pair particularly well with straight-leg jeans, sneakers, and wrap shades. Fair Isle knits are another favorite, whether you’re leaning into classic Americana from Ralph Lauren or something a little more offbeat from Bode. We’re also partial to Khaite’s argyle cardigan worn with dark-wash denim for a polished take on cozy. Of course, you can never go wrong with a classic navy crewneck: The Row’s oversized Ophelia sweater remains a fashion favorite for its perfectly relaxed slouch.

The jeans and a sweater formula may be simple, but the styling possibilities are anything but. Ahead, our favorite ways to wear the combination this season, built around our favorite knits.

You can’t go wrong with a classic navy crewneck and blue jeans. Toss on a pair of sturdy black books and a leather belt for an easy, everyday uniform you can wear all season.

Take cues from Miu Miu’s sport-forward aesthetic with a striped zip-front cardigan and straight-leg jeans. Retro sneakers and wrap shades finish the look.

Khaite’s argyle cardigan is a strong statement piece already, so keep the rest of the outfit simple with dark-wash denim and leather extras. Sleek, lace-up flats continue to be on trend.

A bright red sweater is an easy way to inject some color into your fall wardrobe. Pair it with relaxed, light-wash jeans and classic black loafers for an easy off-duty look.

Bode’s colorful Fair Isle sweater offers an offbeat take on classic Americana. Add cream colored denim and suede boots for a fashion-forward fall look.

Faded pink jeans become surprisingly wearable when worn with an oversized charcoal half-zip and white ballet flats.

A chunky v-neck in electric blue livens up a casual jeans and sneakers combo. Finish the look with an of-the-moment statement belt.
