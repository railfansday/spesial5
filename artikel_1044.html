Jack Grealish issues public apology to Everton fans after disciplinary breach

Jack Grealish has apologised to Everton supporters after returning to action during a behind-closed-doors friendly with Derby.

The player has missed four matches and had not featured for the Toffees since 17 October following a breach of team discipline.

Boss David Moyes was reluctant to discuss any details, with the club only confirming the 31-year-old former England winger was "not match-fit" ahead of a trip to Newcastle on November 2. However, Grealish featuring at Hill Dickinson Stadium on Thursday suggests a line has now been drawn under the issue.

"Nice to be back out there with the boys today after a few good days training," Grealish wrote on his Instagram story.

"I want to apologise to the fans and everyone at the club for not being able to help the team the last few weeks.

"I love playing for this club and can’t wait to be back playing in front of you guys in an Everton shirt. See you all soon."

Grealish spent the first half of the 2025-26 season on loan at Everton before injury disrupted his spell, but he secured another temporary deal on transfer deadline day.

In his absence, Everton have won three of their last four matches to move up to sixth in the Premier League table, while also earning their spot in the quarter-finals of the Carabao Cup.

Following a 2-0 loss to Arsenal, the Toffees picked up successive wins over Newcastle before beating Coventry in their final game before the international break.

Everton are due to return to action on 23 November when they travel to Brentford. Grealish could be set to make his first competitive appearance in over a month in that game as he looks to get his season back on track.

Grealish has earned 39 caps in his England career, but has not played for the Three Lions since October 2024.
