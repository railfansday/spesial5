The Spectrum Retreat: Redux coming to PS5, Xbox Series, and PC on November 26

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Ripstone will release The Spectrum Retreat: Redux, a remastered version of the 2018-released first-person puzzler The Spectrum Retreat, for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC via Steam on November 26 for $12.99 / £9.99 / €12.99.

The Spectrum Retreat: Redux first launched for Switch 2 on October 29.

Here is an overview of the game, via Ripstone:

The Spectrum Retreat: Redux is an atmospheric first-person puzzle thriller set inside The Penrose—an art-deco hotel cut off from the outside world. Unsettlingly quiet corridors make its endlessly attentive android staff seem all the more uncanny. Guided by the voice of a mysterious stranger, you begin to search for the truth of why you’re there.

Your progress hinges on exploring the hotel and completing a series of logic and spatial challenges. You’ll absorb and swap colors to slip through security barriers, redirect energy, and attempt to rethink the rules of each space.

Beneath the hotel’s facade lies a story of memory, grief and loss—one that reckons with a family failed by systems meant to protect them. It’s a prescient narrative that resonates even more sharply today than it did on release.

Redux also includes Express Checkout, a new puzzle speedrun mode built for players chasing the fastest possible run. Shave seconds off every puzzle room clearance, with clean lines and quick thinking rewarded throughout.
