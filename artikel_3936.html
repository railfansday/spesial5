Archetype go all in on the Mass Effect vibes with a FemJun reveal, but I'm just left hoping they'll call the alternative option MascJun to balance it out

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

I know it, you know it, hell, I'm sure developer Archetype Entertainment know it: Exodus is kind of just Mass Effect again. That's in part because the DNA of the game has been built by series vets like Drew Karpyshyn, Chad Robertson and James Ohlen. But it's also because, well, they're putting FemShep in the game. Yes, I'm being a tad facetious here, but when the latest trailer for your game is literally titled "Female Jun Reveal," well, comparisons will be made!

To top it all off, a blog post from the devs literally refers to the alternate gender for the protag as FemJun. They know what they're doing. In any case, first up, her voice actor! She's being played by Carina Conti, an up and comer last digitally heard in Mafia: The Old Country as Isabella Torrisi. Male Jun (who hasn't been given a nickname, which couldn't possibly serve to other female Jun (and MascJun is right there!)) has also had his voice actor confirmed as Stephen Oyoung, who you'll have heard as Mister Negative in Insomniac's Spider-Man games and Red Hood in Gotham Knights.

It's been previously confirmed that no matter which gender you pick for Jun, you'll be able to romance whoever you like, so, happy smoochin'! The story will stay the same too, as will dialogue options, though I'm imagine some pronoun switcheroos will take place somewhere along the line.

I'm mostly just left feeling that this doesn't really seem like it'll fix the game's boring hero problem. Edwin made note of Jun's quite lacklustre vibe earlier this year, aptly tearing down MascJun's Instagram visage, though this was prior to the face model and voice actor change the character has undergone. Personally, I'm not sure that'll be enough, and FemJun kind of matches that Instagram look; the perfect bun and bangs that pop out when she takes her helmet off is quite silly too. Ah well! A game isn't done until it's done (which, in theory, will be April 7th next year).
