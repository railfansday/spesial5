Star Trek: Voyager - Across The Unknown Final DLC Ventures To Switch 2 Next Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Year of Hell is right around the corner

Daedalic Entertainment and GameXcite are wrapping up the bonus content for Star Trek: Voyager - Across The Unknown with one final wave of DLC.

The 'Year of Hell' expansion launches on Switch 2 next month on 9th December, with its own mini-campaign inspired by the Voyager two-parter of the same name.

In it, you'll be up against Captain Annorax and his civilisation-destroying time ship. You can either follow the critical path from the show and help Janeway & co. take down this baddie, or you can try something new and attempt to strike a deal with him.

This is its own storyline focused on survival gameplay, so your main game save will remain untouched for its duration.

Here's a brief rundown of the expansion's key features and a handful of screenshots:

Manage the decline. Gameplay turned on its head. Begin with advanced systems and upgrades already in place, then fight to keep essential systems operational as the Krenim take that advantage away. A year of losses and difficult sacrifices. Attacks can destroy rooms, tear entire decks from the ship, and kill crew members. With less space and fewer resources, success means seeing how many of your crew survive the twelve months. Six ships. Shifting loyalties. Face four Krenim vessels, including Annorax’s timeship. The Mawasi Cruiser and Nihydron Warship can join your forces in certain timeline branches-or turn their weapons on you in others.

Those who already own the Across the Unknown expansion pass will land the DLC automatically at launch.

We had a great time with the base game earlier this year, calling it a damn good time "for resource management and survival nuts who like a bit of '90s Trek" in our 8/10 review.
