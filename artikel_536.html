Black Friday deals on self-emptying robot vacuums: Dreame, Roborock and Eufy lead the pack

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

If you’re buying a robot vacuum, it should be self-emptying. It just wouldn’t make sense to buy one without that feature, given that the whole point is to make vacuuming as hands-off as possible.

I have two beagles, and I vacuum twice a day. That’s why I have what I like to call my Dreame Team: the Dreame L60 Pro Ultra, the Aero Pro Steam, and the N20 Steam Portable Carpet Cleaner. I also have a lone Shark upright. Clearly, I have enough cleaning to do without emptying a robot’s dustbin, too.

Luckily, almost every robot vacuum at Amazon seems to be on sale for Black Friday, and Mashable has tested so many robot vacuums. But when everything has a flashing red "Black Friday deal" label, figuring out which deals are genuinely good takes a little more work. That’s what Mashable's shopping team is here for.

Throughout Black Friday, I’ll be double-checking MSRPs, comparing prices across retailers, and watching for new deals from our favorite robot vacuum brands. As always, Mashable is here to get you the best value for your money this holiday season. So, without further ado, here are our picks for the best Black Friday self-emptying robot vacuum deals.

The Roborock Q10 S5+ is a Mashable fave. Not only is it an affordable option, but Leah Stodart, our former vacuum expert, found it reliably performed in her cleaning tests, including wiping up a variety of spills and splatters in her kitchen.

It’s also super quiet, even when emptying its dustbin. Stodart liked its accurate mapping and sonic mopping, which actually scrubs rather than just dragging a damp pad across your floors. At $229.99, you’re getting more than a basic robot that picks up crumbs and calls it a day.

The only downside? You'll have to wash the mop pad yourself, and Stodart noted that it gets dirty quickly. It also isn’t the most thorough around edges. But if you want a self-emptying robot vacuum without spending $500 or more, this is a good entry-level pick. If washing the mop pads yourself is a dealbreaker, the Roborock Qrevo S Pro is on sale for $379.99, down from $699.99. That’s $150 more than the Q10 S5+, but you’re getting a dock that does more than empty the dustbin. It also washes the mop pads with hot water, dries them with warm air, and refills the robot’s water tank. That may feel like an unnecessary feature, but after testing a lot of robot vacuum and mop combos, we can tell you firsthand that mildewy robot mop pads really stink.

And on a personal level, if I’m buying a robot to mop my floors, I’d prefer not to be responsible for washing its dirty pads afterward.

If you're going to splurge on a robot vacuum, make it the Dreame X60 Max Ultra Complete. Mashable's reviewer Leah Stodart called it the "most well-rounded premium robot vacuum" and the "best robot vacuum in 2026," period. Its 35,000 suction power is only topped by Roborock's Saros 20 line, but Stodart found the Dreame to be "much more of a beast" than the basic Saros 20. And while we haven't tested the Saros 20 Sonic yet, it would cost you an extra $360. So the Dreame X60 it is!

Besides its impressive suction power, it also features a livestream pet camera, dual spinning mopping pads that are more precise around edges than flat mops or roller mops, and a design that is just over three inches tall. If you have low-clearance furniture, it should glide underneath without issue.
