Tracker Quietly Makes Colter’s Shaw Family Tragedy Worse

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Warning! SPOILERS ahead about Tracker season 4, episode 7.Colter’s case in Tracker season 4, episode 7 revealed just how much worse Colter is poised to take Russell’s secret from Tracker season 3’s finale. The episode’s case already introduced a new variable in Tracker season 4, episode 7 by breaking Colter’s success rate, as Colter and Luke saved Mika in the end, but Colter still couldn’t reach John in time to save him. However, the most interesting revelations came from the juxtaposition of Colter and Luke’s (Josh Holloway) entirely different systems and values.

Before Luke, the only other rewardist Tracker had introduced and fleshed out was Billie Matalon. Colter and Billie’s relationship was fraught too, due to past misunderstandings, a romantic connection, and both their tendencies to put their job first. Still, when Colter was in danger, Billie always came through, proving that their personal relationship was more important than any given case. With Luke, Tracker season 4 presented a rewardist in complete antithesis of what Colter stood for, be it methods, as Luke immediately started shooting at Colter, or principles, as Luke blindly trusted his employers about John.

There was no doubt about Luke’s abilities as a tracker in Tracker season 4, episode 7. Indeed, it was probably because of his exceptional skills in tracking and delivering John to Hiro that Colter couldn’t save John. Still, their exchanges over the type of life they chose inevitably enriched them and gave Luke a new perspective on the job. Simultaneously, they clearly defined what drives Colter and revealed why he chose to be a rewardist.

Up until Tracker season 4, episode 7, Colter’s love for his job was evident, but his motives had never been clearly spelled out. Luke asking in his hospital bed whether a number existed that would have convinced Colter to leave his rewardist days behind forced Colter to admit the truth that he not only loves his job, but that it is his purpose, and how he serves his community.

Some details in Tracker season 4, episode 4 reveal a great deal more of Colter Shaw, resolving a problem plaguing the CBS drama for a long time.

Luke and Colter’s discussions about their past and what drives them in their jobs also explained why they came to be trackers, and in doing so, Tracker season 4 revealed how Ashton Shaw’s teachings inspired Colter to become a rewardist. This went beyond his survivalist skills, which Tracker’s earlier seasons already revealed Ashton had taught Colter and Russell as teens.

Instead, Colter opened up in Tracker season 4, episode 7 about how Ashton showed him to really look for the details of something, thus inspiring him to go beyond the surface when accepting a job. Colter even shared how he made his Ashton’s notion of doing something well or not at all, effectively disclosing how what drives him as a rewardist is everything that Ashton taught him. This inevitably sets up how difficult it will be when Colter discovers what Russell learned in Tracker season 3’s finale.

Tracker season 4 has yet to reveal what Russell saw in McIntyre’s file on Colter in season 3’s ending, but it must be damning enough for him to want to work for McIntyre in order to protect Colter from the truth. Given that the file prompted Russell to leave without saying goodbye to Colter or letting him know there was anything to worry about, it’s clear that whatever Russell saw in the file will eventually find its way to Colter.

Considering how lies separated Russell and Colter in the first place, making honesty imperative in their relationship, we already know that Colter learning about the secret Russell is keeping will hurt him and negatively affect the trust Colter has in his brother. Russell’s appearance in Tracker season 4, episode 5 already proved that Colter sensed something was wrong, making this secret potentially lethal in the long run.

Learning how Ashton’s teachings greatly impacted Colter, to the point that he follows them daily in his mission as a rewardist, nevertheless points to Russell’s secret having the potential to completely destroy Colter beyond the simple fact that Russell hid it from him. Indeed, learning what Ashton did to Colter could shake the foundations of Colter’s identity, effectively making Russell’s secret simultaneously Tracker season 4’s most important development and its most tragic.
