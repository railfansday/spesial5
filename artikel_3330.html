Star Trek Actors Reveal Big Change To Starfleet Academy Season 2 Episodes & Filming Difficulties

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Star Trek: Starfleet Academy's actors reveal a big change to season 2 and talk about their filming challenges.

Bella Shepard and Karim Diané return in Star Trek: Starfleet Academy's second and final season, which premieres in March 2027 on Paramount+. Diané plays Jay-Den Kraag, Star Trek's first gay Klingon, and Shepard reprises Genesis Lythe, her overachieving Dar-Sha cadet. All 10 episodes of the divisive, young adult Star Trek series headlined by Holly Hunter wrapped filming in early 2026.

TrekMovie's Laurie Ulster reported from Creation Entertainment's ST:PHL: Trek to Philadelphia 60th Celebration, where Karim Diané and Bella Shepard were featured in a Star Trek: Starfleet Academy panel moderated by Syfy Sistas' Tamia Harper.

The pair of Star Trek's youngest actors dropped hints about season 2, revealing that the episode order changed. A later episode that has Shepard's favorite moments with Jay-Den, Captain Nahla Ake (Holly Hunter), and The Doctor (Robert Picardo) was moved up to be the second episode of Starfleet Academy season 2. See their quotes below:

Bella Shepard: “You have my favorite moments… Your moments with Bob [Picardo] and Holly [Hunter] in that episode. You guys won’t know what I’m talking about, unfortunately. Like I really want to talk about it.”

Karim Diané: “You know, it’s episode 2 now.”

Bella Shepard: “Yeah, they switched it. Yeah, it comes earlier. It’s funny because it’s… See, we’re talking in code now. I’m sorry. I don’t want you guys to feel like that. But it was originally supposed to be in a different order, and then they switched around. So season 2, episode 2 is what I’m trying to talk about.”

Star Trek series changing the release order of episodes from how they were produced is not uncommon, going back to Star Trek: The Original Series season 1. Star Trek: Strange New Worlds season 4's first few episodes changed their release order, and it was noticeable in how some of the character arcs played out at season 4's onset.

As two of the actors who play alien Star Trek: Starfleet Academy cadets, Bella and Karim also delved into how wearing Klingon and Dar-Sha prosthetics left them "in tears... so desperate..." with their "skin going crazy..." and suffering from "rashes." Shepard also discusses how she coped with Genesis' Star Trek technobabble, which required her to have "full debriefs with our writers and other people who have been on Star Trek shows":

Karim Diané: “I would say physically, there’s nothing I’m not gonna be able to do. Physically, me and Bella were in prosthetics next to each other every single day… There are days I would be like in tears, like so desperate… Our skin was going crazy … I remember after two weeks, I took my prosthetics off, and I just had like a row of bumps all over my face and I started crying.”

Bella Shepard: “I had rashes on my eyelids and the alcohol that they have to use to be in my skin above my eyebrows…”

Karim Diané: “After this job, there’s nothing that we won’t be able to do because [of] the physicality of it. We’re on set 20 hours a day for like five days a week. We had rehearsals on the weekends.”

Bella Shepard: “Even dialogue-wise, for me, the stuff that I’ve had to say? I have had to do full debriefs with our writers and other people who have been on Star Trek shows before and be like, ‘I need this broken down because if I can’t understand what my character is saying, I can’t deliver it, and I can’t deliver it well at all.’ So I feel like I could be thrown into some crazy medical drama, and I’d be able to tell someone how to perform surgery or something because I said words I didn’t even know existed.”

Star Trek: Picard alumna Isa Briones' experience helps her handle complex medical jargon. After Picard, Briones went on to play Dr. Trinity Santos in HBO's Emmy-winning medical drama, The Pitt. Perhaps Bella Shepard will also be cast in a medical show now she has wrapped Starfleet Academy. Meanwhile, Diané does have to deal with medical technobabble, as he becomes The Doctor's protégé in Starfleet Academy season 2.

Star Trek: Starfleet Academy's second and final season brings the cadets into their sophomore year, with new relationships, new missions away from San Francisco, and new characters played by Ruaridh Aldington, Bill Nighy, and Rosemarie DeWitt. The final 10 episodes cut off the cadets' story at the halfway point, but perhaps there will be some sort of denouement that reveals what happens to Starfleet's youngest heroes.

Star Trek: Starfleet Academy season 1 is streaming now, and season 2 premieres March 2027 on Paramount+
