Roku Teams With LeBron James & Fulwell 73’s Uninterrupted Label For Harlem Globetrotters 100th Anniversary Special

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

EXCLUSIVE: Roku is teaming up with LeBron James and Fulwell 73’s Uninterrupted label on a Harlem Globetrotters 100th Anniversary Special, which will celebrate 100 years of the legendary basketball team.

The one-hour documentary-style special will feature archival footage and have exclusive behind-the-scenes access to former and current Harlem Globetrotter stars, who will share their experiences traveling the world, as well as NBA talent, comedians, and historians who will speak to what the team meant to America and beyond. The special is set to premiere early 2027 on Roku. The basketball troupe is famous for its athleticism, larger-than-life personalities, and on-court fun, having entertained more than 148 million fans across 123 countries and territories. It is also known as an important cultural organisation that has helped break down systemic barriers for athletes of color. Watch on Deadline

The special is executive-produced by LeBron James, Maverick Carter, Gabe Turner, Ri-Karlo Handy, Christopher Dillon, Jamal Henderson, Keith Dawkins, Bronwen O’Keefe, Tiffany Cumbry, and Matt Rissmiller, with Khadija Bush serving as co-executive producer. The project is overseen by Sean Boyle on behalf of Roku.

“The Harlem Globetrotters pioneered the fusion of sports and entertainment, and after 100 years, they’re still a true cultural institution,” said Sean Boyle, Roku Originals. “We’re thrilled to partner with the Harlem Globetrotters, LeBron James, Maverick Carter, and the Uninterrupted team to explore this iconic legacy and the present-day influence of this special team.”

“The legacy of the Harlem Globetrotters is special, and we are honored to bring it to life in this film celebrating the 100 years of excellence and joy they bring to the world of sports. Their impact on culture and the game of basketball is tremendous, and we are excited to partner with the Roku team to share this film widely,” said Jamal Henderson executive producer of the film.

“In partnership with Roku and LeBron James and Maverick Carter’s Uninterrupted Productions, we’ve created a love letter to millions of Harlem Globetrotter fans around the world,” said Keith Dawkins, President, Harlem Globetrotters & Herschend Entertainment Studios. “The significance of having arguably the greatest basketball player ever involved in a project showcasing a team that broke barriers and set the stage for the global game that we consume today is not lost upon us. It inspires us to continuously elevate our impact, serve as global Ambassadors of Goodwill, and bring love and joy wherever we go.”
