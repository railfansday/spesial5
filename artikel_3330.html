Gael Cameron Says Her ‘Below Deck’ Romance With Nathan Gallagher Was Abusive: ‘I Am Still Scared’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Almost a year after Below Deck star Nathan Gallagher was arrested for domestic abuse in Australia — and 12 weeks after the news went public — Gallagher’s former partner and cast member Gael Cameron is finally speaking out about their relationship.

Cameron broke her silence on an episode of the podcast We Need to Talk, hosted by Paul C. Brunson, where she publicly accused Gallagher of abuse for the first time and described a toxic relationship that’s left her in fear to this day.

“I know that he is not afraid to do anything to throw me under. He’s not afraid to lie.… He’s always had this idea that he has ownership or control over me,” Cameron said tearfully. “I still feel that. And I am still scared of him. And that’s why I’ve not said anything, even though he’s thrown all these accusations out there.”

Gallagher and Cameron met while filming Season Nine of Below Deck: Mediterranean in 2023, both working on the superyacht’s deck team. Over three seasons, their relationship evolved from flirty co-workers to welcoming a son together last June. But this August — halfway through a season where Gallagher struggled to juggle his bosun duties and be a present father — news broke of Gallagher’s December 2025 arrest on domestic-violence charges. According to Australian police, Gallagher attacked Cameron at her apartment in Sydney, resulting in his arrest on Dec. 27, 2025. During the physical altercation, Gallagher “placed both of his hands around the victim’s neck, applying enough pressure which made her unable to breathe,” according to police records. He was charged with assault occasioning actual bodily harm, common assault, and intentionally choking a person without consent.

Although Gallagher previously told Rolling Stone he had proof that would clear his name, he failed to appear at a mandated court hearing in September. As a result, he was convicted by default on charges of assault. Australian authorities issued a warrant for his arrest, noting Gallagher was suspected to have fled the country. Both Bravo and Gallagher declined to comment further to Rolling Stone.

During the podcast episode, Cameron charts the timeline of her relationship with Gallagher, including the behind-the-scenes struggles that kept her silent and afraid. Cameron first met Gallagher while filming Season Nine of Below Deck: Mediterranean in 2023. At the time, she was dating another man but began a friendship with Gallagher. Their working relationship was often “playfully flirty” when the cameras were rolling, but they didn’t start seeing each other until filming wrapped.

In 2023, the two were in a long-distance relationship, but Cameron said there were early signs of anger issues in Gallagher. When they were able to meet up, Cameron described his “love bombing,” showering her with gifts and affection. When she found out she was pregnant, Gallagher convinced her to keep the baby — the first of many moves which she claims he did in order to isolate her.

“If I ever said, ‘I don’t know if I can do this. I don’t think it’s the right time,’ he would make me feel very bad about myself. He would make me feel very guilty and just terrible about myself,” she said. “I have quite a conservative mum. I couldn’t talk to anyone. So he was kind of my only sounding board because I was so afraid to tell anyone.”

But the pregnancy only aggravated Gallagher’s behavior, according to Cameron. She said that she spent close to “90 percent” of her pregnancy in tears, often feeling like any small mistake would trigger Gallagher’s anger. In one instance, she describes that while drunk, Gallagher jumped out of a moving car rather than talk to her. When Gallagher flew to Croatia to film Below Deck: Mediterranean Season 11, Cameron didn’t initially want to go. She appeared on the series as a guest star and lived off of the boat with her infant son. But she believes Gallagher pushed her to come to Croatia as another form of control. “It felt as though he was very worried about who I spoke to or who I was around when he wasn’t around,” she says. “There was this element of control that he would lose over me if I wasn’t in the same country as him.” She also describes incidents during heated fights, when Gallagher would record on his phone, pretending that she was striking him or hitting him unprovoked. Things came to a head on Dec. 27, 2025, when Cameron said Gallagher choked her while their son was in her arms, which led to Gallagher’s arrest and subsequent conviction. Cameron appeared visibly emotional when she recounted the night.

“When I was in the bathroom, and he had his hands around my neck and attempted to lift, my son was in my arms. All I could think is, ‘If I fall, so does he.’ From that moment, I should have known there’s no return,” she says. “When he had me pinned down and he was striking me on the jaw, I actually sat there and I asked if he was going to kill me. According to Cameron, she has not spoken to Gallagher or been in contact since he fled the country. But she says that his departure has changed her life for the better.

“Him leaving was the biggest weight off my shoulders. All of a sudden, I could have my family over to help me look after my son when I went to work. I could breathe again,” she says. “I had been living this hell. So life felt so bright.”
