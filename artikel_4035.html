Fable's Latest Showing Didn't Exactly Blow Our Socks Off

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

It's the first time the Xbox RPG didn't wow us

Here at Pure Xbox, we're rather stoked about Playground Games' revival of Fable. We were picking jaws up off the floor in June, then again at gamescom in August, and even left London FanFest feeling pretty jazzed with what we got to play of the upcoming RPG.

So, let it been known for the record, I fully expect Fable to be a brilliant RPG. Top class in execution, full of joy and wow moments aplenty, but what on earth was Xbox thinking promoting Fable with that four-minute gameplay video? C'mon gang!

The footage was revealed during Seattle FanFest as part of Xbox's 25th anniversary jollies, although most of it was stuff we'd sorta seen before, and the choice of location was questionable at best. See for yourself in the video above.

"Both myself and Craig had a chance to play this demo last weekend, and we both came away pretty darn impressed with what we'd played. That said, we also remarked to each other that we wished we'd had a chance to explore Albion and interact with NPCs, which is something we'll have to wait a little bit longer for."

Feature We've Finally Played Xbox's New Fable Reboot, And Here's What We Think So Far

We went hands on at the recent Xbox FanFest

Where we'd previously been gobsmacked by vibrant castle towns and lush wilderness, this latest setting felt rather basic and lacking in character or detail. It was just some generic cliffside. It's not necessarily bad, but it's hardly the sort of environment I'd think to stuff into a trailer meant to turn people onto Fable.

As the video goes on, we get a little combat featuring swords, archery, and a sprinkling of magic. It all looks good, and having played the combat myself, it feels fun. But it doesn't last long, nor does it show us anything new or particularly interesting. The quest itself is cut short and lacking, as though it's just been ripped from a spoiler-free section of the game.

Just to reiterate, I reckon Fable will come good. Every gameplay reveal up until this point has been great. Big fan. This one just feels a little odd, almost as if it was cobbled together last minute. That's not ideal when you're trying to convince players to pick up Fable when it launches on Xbox Series X|S, PC, and PS5 on February 23, 2027.
