21 Original Xbox Games Are Coming To PC Via Backwards Compatibility Today (November 12)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Woah, woah, woah! As part of Xbox's 25th anniversary celebrations, it's been announced that 21 original Xbox games are coming to PC via backwards compatibility today, joining the four that were made available earlier in the year.

If you already own any of these games for your Xbox console, you'll also now get a PC version for free, and each title apparently also supports mouse and keyboard in addition to a traditional controller.

"We’ve also improved performance and stability for the first four games and every game now supports mouse and keyboard too."

It's also worth noting that four original Xbox games have added support for achievements today, with those being the two Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic titles along with Blinx: The Time Sweeper and Conker: Live and Reloaded.

Microsoft says that more original Xbox games are coming to PC backwards compatibility in the future, and achievements will be added to other OG Xbox titles as well.
