Witcher 3 Remastered Gets Its Next Update On Xbox & PC, Full Patch Notes Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

"Two things are certain in a witcher's life: the Path never sleeps, and the work is never done."

Here comes another new patch for The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Remastered. The latest update arrived today (November 26) for Xbox Series X|S, PC, PlayStation 5, and Nintendo Switch 2, focusing on "Photo Mode improvements" alongside a hearty batch of fixes.

"Patch 5.02 for The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Remastered is now available across PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2! From gameplay and balance and visual adjustments to Photo Mode improvements, to plenty fixes across the Continent and more, this update brings a host of changes to make your adventures on the Path even better."

Most of the fixes revolve around issues with lighting, New Game+, the UI, quests, and Photo Mode. There aren't too many Xbox-specific fixes, but a couple are worth mentioning. These include a fix for achievements not unlocking after transferring PC saves via Cross Progression, as well as intermittent freezing on Xbox Series X|S.

In addition to CD Projekt Red's official fixes, cross-platform mods have just gone live for the Witcher 3 Remastered too! These feature more than 40 mods from community modders, such as boat races, glowing eyes for Geralt, hairstyles and more.

Those are the highlights. We'll leave you with the full patch notes down below.
