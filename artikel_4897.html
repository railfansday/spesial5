Nvidia turmoil tests Bitcoin miners’ AI funding plans

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Nvidia’s leadership upheaval and Texas permit restrictions sharpen the divide between miners with funded projects and those still seeking capital and approvals.

Jensen Huang’s departure from Nvidia comes as Bitcoin miners are committing billions of dollars to risky AI infrastructure pivots.

For investors, questions remain around whether large AI contracts can produce cash flows that justify construction costs and support debt service and lead to a sustainable business model. The pivot replaces exposure to Bitcoin (BTC) with construction, financing and customer credit risks. Companies with already-funded projects, usable power and enforceable payment commitments could therefore deliver sharply different returns from peers whose expansion still depends on additional capital and regulatory approvals.

Huang’s departure reaches miners through Nvidia’s overlapping roles as supplier, customer and financier. IREN signed a five-year, $3.4 billion cloud services contract with Nvidia in May, while Nvidia invested $2 billion in CoreWeave in January.

IREN and Applied Digital fell during the Nov. 12 AI selloff following Huang’s departure. For companies seeking equity financing, lower share prices mean issuing more shares to raise the same amount.

JPMorgan credit analyst Erica Spear said “new deals could take longer or require more compensation.” For miners, a slowdown in Nvidia investments, guarantees or capacity purchases could raise financing costs and delay construction. Tighter funding for their cloud-provider tenants could also weaken demand for additional capacity.

A financing retreat has not been confirmed. Nvidia’s board said it expects operations, customer commitments and its product roadmap to continue uninterrupted.

It is also important to recognize that the AI data center industry is in fact a diverse set of entirely separate business models with their own risk exposures. Leasing powered land, operating colocation facilities, and owning GPUs to sell computing services have different construction requirements, operating responsibilities and equipment replacement exposure.
