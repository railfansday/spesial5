Agoda’s COO on AI Booking: ‘Nobody Knows’ the Business Model Yet

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Agoda wants to win AI agents' business the way it wins travelers': on price, speed and reliability. What it can't say yet is what those agents will eventually cost it, and that answer will matter more to its margins than any integration.

AI booking is "definitely disruptive to our industry," Agoda's chief operating officer Ittai Chorev said at a media roundtable in Bangkok on Thursday. But "in terms of how the business model or commercials will look, nobody knows." For now, he said, that works in Agoda's favor: the AI platforms are "in the growth stage, so everything is free, which is great for us."

The Booking Holdings-owned online travel agency was one of the first travel partners in Asia to integrate with Meta's Muse, an AI agent that can search and book on a user's behalf, and it works with OpenAI and Microsoft on its internal tools.

How well Agoda uses AI matters beyond its own app. Booking Holdings is building its new B2B unit mainly on Agoda's partner technology, Skift reported in August, so Agoda's AI work will shape more of the group's business.

Agoda's chief operating officer Ittai Chorev said AI booking is "definitely disruptive to our industry," but that nobody yet knows how the business model or commercials will look — and for now the uncertainty works in Agoda's favor, because the AI platforms are "in the growth stage, so everything is free." The Booking Holdings-owned online travel agency was one of the first travel partners in Asia to integrate with Meta's Muse, going live in Singapore and Japan in October, and it works with OpenAI and Microsoft on internal tools. Agoda's strategy is to be the supplier AI agents choose: Chorev said the agent will decide based on the quality and speed of technical integration, and that Agoda views ChatGPT and Gemini as an opportunity rather than a threat. Agoda is also building its own assistant, tied to live inventory so prices and hotel names come from the same data used for booking. The stakes extend past Agoda's own app, since Booking Holdings is building its new B2B unit mainly on Agoda's partner technology, and CEO Omri Morgenshtern is moving to New York to also run the new Booking Partner Division. Volumes remain small: Booking Holdings CEO Glenn Fogel said at Skift Global Forum in September that AI referrals were under 1% of Booking.com room nights in the latest data he had seen.
