Google Health 5.12 rolling out with manual blood pressure & pregnancy logging, more

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Following a sizable release last month, Google Health is rolling out another big update with version 5.12.

Google Health 5.12 on Android and iOS will let you “log your blood pressure readings manually or sync your readings from another app via Apple Health or Health Connect.”

Go to the Health tab > Heart to see blood pressure graphs.

This release also introduces a “new glucose visualization that overlays logged and tracked events (exercise, sleep, nutrition) on the graph.”

You can also log events directly from the graph. Check this out in the Metabolic Health section of the Health Tab.
