Wunderkind: Manager, Concierge Partnerships

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

About Wunderkind:Wunderkind is a performance marketing solution that uses AI and data to help brands acquire and retain customers through one-to-one, personalized messages across websites, email, text, and ads, guaranteeing a lift in revenue. Founded in 2010, Our goal is to scale a brand’s ability to create and grow customer relationships through websites, email, text, and ads. We believe individuals should have the freedom to choose the kind of internet they want. An internet that fosters genuine relationships with brands. An internet that treats humans like real people instead of “web traffic.” We also believe in empowering brands to treat their customers as individuals, satisfying their unique needs with tailored experiences and thoughtful communication. By expertly personalizing, automating, and scaling beautiful one-to-one experiences, Wunderkind helps brands acquire new customers at scale and keep them loyal for life.

We’re hiring a Concierge Partnerships Manager to sit on our Partnerships team and grow Concierge, Wunderkind’s referral partner program. You’ll recruit and activate a network of e-commerce operators, former clients, and industry insiders who make warm introductions to brands, then help carry those introductions through to closed-won revenue. You’ll work alongside our existing Concierge lead to scale the program. This is a commercially-oriented role focused on revenue and new deal flow, not just leads or partner sign-ups.

The base salary range for this role is $85,000.00 to $95,000.00. The compensation package includes variable earnings. Actual compensation packages are based on several factors that are unique to each specific candidate; including but not limited to skill set, depth of experience, applicable certifications, and geographic work location. Our pay bands include different levels for different USA states, so salaries may vary depending on living location and market rate.
