Nations League quarter-final draw simulated: England get favourable tie as Spain handed France rematch

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The draw for the Nations League quarter-finals is now just days away, and England's route to potential international glory will become clearer.

Following the conclusion of an exciting and unpredictable group phase, the stage has been set as seven teams aim to dethrone two-time winners Portugal next summer.

The holders are safely through to the knockout phase as group winners, but they only confirmed their position on the final matchday after beating Wales in a five-goal thriller in Cardiff.

Joining Portugal in progressing from Group A4 are Norway following a remarkable comeback against Denmark on Tuesday night.

Trailing 2-0 after just 14 minutes, a brace from Erling Haaland and Martin Odegaard gave the World Cup quarter-finalists a 3-2 win as they pipped the Danes to the runners-up spot by virtue of a superior head-to-head record.

There was also final matchday drama in England’s group. Thomas Tuchel’s side travelled to Madrid needing to beat reigning world and European champions Spain to top Group A3 and did exactly that, despite playing the last half hour with 10-men.

Netherlands lost out on top spot to Germany in Group A2 after being thumped 5-0 by Jurgen Klopp’s side, but squeezed through on goal difference having finished level on points with Greece.

France and Belgium have qualified from Group A1, meaning seven of the top 11 ranked teams in the world have made it through to the Nations League quarter-finals.

So ahead of Friday's draw, who will be paired with who? Well, the four seeded sides each have three possibilities as teams from the same group cannot be paired with each other.

As a result, a knockout stage simulator has determined their potential opponents in the quarter-final and it's safe to say the results are quite fascinating.

In this simulation, England have been paired with the Netherlands in what would be a repeat of the semi-final during the inaugural season of the Nations League.

While the Three Lions were beaten on that occasion, it would be a favourable draw given they have won two of the last three meetings against the Dutch - including the Euro 2024 semi-final.

The simulator hasn't been as kind to France as they've been paired with Spain, a team they have lost to three times in a row since victory in the 2021 final.

In addition, the simulator has thrown up two other eye-catching fixtures with Norway taking on Germany, and Portugal continuing the defence of their crown against Belgium over two legs.
