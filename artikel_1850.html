Arsenal handed major new Christos Tzolis injury update after MRI scan

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Arsenal winger Christos Tzolis is expected to be involved in Greece’s Nations League clash against Serbia after an MRI scan came back all clear.

Tzolis played 73 minutes in Greece’s 7-0 defeat to the Netherlands on Friday, as his Arsenal team-mate Jurrien Timber scored twice from left-back.

There were concerns over Tzolis’ fitness after that match, with the 24-year-old feeling some muscular discomfort when Greece returned to training.

Tzolis was sent for an MRI scan over the weekend and he was initially viewed as a doubt for the match at home to Serbia on Monday night.

Victory in that game, against a side without a single point in this Nations League campaign, will confirm second place in the group for Greece.

They have now been handed a major boost, with Tzolis’ scans confirming that there is no injury issue.

He has been passed fit and is set to be in the squad against Serbia.

It will be a relief to Arsenal that Tzolis has not picked up an injury for the second international break in a row.

Tzolis missed a month with a hamstring issue he picked up while on international duty earlier this season.

He has made only three Arsenal appearances since suffering that injury at the start of October, but Tzolis was still trusted by Mikel Arteta to be thrown straight back into the side when he started against Liverpool at Anfield.

However, it has been a fairly quiet start to life at Arsenal, even if he had established himself as first-choice on the left wing before his injury.
