Ex-Manchester United defender names Chelsea star as dream first Mauricio Pochettino signing

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Manchester United should sign Chelsea youngster Estevao, says former defender Mikael Silvestre.

The 19-year-old has made 46 appearances for the Blues since arriving in London last summer from Palmeiras for an initial £29million deal.

Estevao, however, has been handed just one Premier League start by new Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso so far this season - coincidentally against Man United last month.

The Brazilian would go on to impress, helping his side seal a 2-0 win at Stamford Bridge, a result that increased the pressure on then-head coach Michael Carrick who has since been sacked and replaced by Mauricio Pochettino.

While Pochettino has agreed to take over at Old Trafford, he will do so after leaving his post as United States Men’s National Team head coach [USMNT] following the CONCACAF Nations League quarter-final clash with Haiti on Wednesday.

With that said, attentions have already turned to the upcoming transfer window and potential reinforcements to improve Man Utd’s fortunes as Pochettino’s task will be to steer the club away from relegation danger.

Silvestre, who made 361 appearances for the Red Devils, believes Estevao is a player his former club should target if Chelsea were willing to do business.

“Well, Estevao played against United for Chelsea, and I think he’s shown why he should feature more,” Silvestre told Greek outlet Betarades.gr.

“It looks like he’s going to fight for his place in the team, so I don’t anticipate United making a move for him.

“But he’s a good player for sure. He’s playing for the national team of Brazil, and I like his style. If he were available, he would be a good signing for United.”

Man Utd have frequently been linked to Newcastle left-back Lewis Hall, who ended speculation in the summer by signing a new contract at St James’ Park.

Silvestre, though, also name-dropped the England international as a potential signing for his former club.

He continued: “Lewis Hall had an impressive season with Newcastle, and he’s been on the club’s radar for sure.
