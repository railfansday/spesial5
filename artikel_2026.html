Mikel Arteta watched on as his Arsenal team dominated Newcastle United in the first half.

United fortunate to be only one down after the opening half.

A completely different second half though, Newcastle United by far the better team and Arsenal lucky in the end to grab a point.

Arsenal going six points clear but Brighton with a game in hand.

Mikel Arteta asked about Bruno Guimaraes making an emotional return to St James’ Park and how he thought he dealt with it: “Very good. We spoke about that before the game. Because inevitably, emotionally, you have so much going on, especially in the way that he feels connected to this club, having been the captain and everything that they did together. But I think he was very composed and he was very, very, very focused on what he had to do in the game. I think for the time that he was on the pitch he had a really good performance.”

Suggested to Mikel Arteta that a point at St James’ Park is usually a good result but that he won’t feel like that is the case tonight: “My feeling is that we lost two points today. We were exceptional in the first half, from the beginning to the end. The intensity, the level of aggression, the intensity that we put on the defensive aspects, the quality that we showed. Thee clarity to understand the jobs, the composure that we have with the ball, the aggressiveness that we have to attack the right spaces, we should have been two, three, or four up clearly. When you don’t do that then the opponent is going to try to react, we play at the same level but we still have to increase the level to cope with that. Then right at the beginning of the second half we aren’t at the races, we start to lose duels, we start to lose second balls, we give the ball away, really poor circulation goals against their press and their waves. When that happens, you give them belief. One thing is to give them belief, and another thing is to give them a goal. We give them a goal from a throw, that is obviously very, very, very disappointing. On top of that we give them a corner, we give them the best player that they have on the pitch on set-pieces free.”

Mikel Arteta on Newcastle United and the fans: “You have to give credit to Newcastle United as well for that period of time [when they were on top], the quality, the aggression, the conviction that they showed that they could get back in the game. The same with the stadium, which we know that when it comes to these evenings, they believe that they can do it, it’s another push. We have won football matches playing much worse than we have done here. But it is not enough because when the margins are so small, everything matters.”
