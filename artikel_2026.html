Liverpool told to pay '£60m' to complete Bart Verbruggen deal - Transfer expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Liverpool may need to aside up to £60million to sign Brighton goalkeeper Bart Verbruggen next summer.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the Seagulls will charge "serious money" for their number one.

Verbruggen is out of contract on the South Coast in 2028, and that has alerted the likes of Liverpool and Real Madrid to a potential move for him next summer.

Wyness believes that Brighton won't make major attempts to try and keep the 24-year-old, and will instead look to create an "auction" to drive his transfer fee up.

Fabian Hurzeler's side have become the masters of developing players and cashing in on them at the right time, and it looks set to be the same story once again with Verbruggen.

The shot-stopper has already kept seven clean sheets in the Premier League so far this season, a tally which is only matched by Arsenal's David Raya.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that Liverpool are likely to be in direct competition with Real Madrid for Verbruggen's services.

The Spanish giants have identified the Brighton ace as an ideal long-term replacement for Thibaut Courtois, who is out of contract in 2028.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how Liverpool will have to splash the cash to win the race.

"Well, we're talking about Brighton, who are excellent at managing to get a bonus for their players, as we know. And I think in this case as well, they'll create an auction," he said.

"You already alluded to interest from other clubs for him. You know, Brighton will make some serious money out of this player.

"From memory, I think he was about £16m when Brighton bought him. I expect him to go at an auction up to £50m or £60m."

Former Everton scout Bryan King told Football Insider earlier this week that Ryan Gravenberch is "not happy" with his lack of football at Anfield of late.

The midfielder was left as an unused substitute for the recent defeat to Arsenal, with Andoni Iraola preferring youngster Trey Nyoni in the middle of the park.

King believes that Gravenberch's performances must improve if he wants to get his starting spot back, with Nyoni making waves at the moment.

The ex-recruitment man labelled the starlet as a "breath of fresh air" and believes he is ready to make a big impact for the Reds this term.
