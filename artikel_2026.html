Ex-Man City adviser shares 'crazy' Liam Delap claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Liam Delap is absolutely worth the £50million that Nottingham Forest paid to take him off Chelsea's hands in the summer.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that the striker should not be classed as an "expensive mistake".

In his first eight appearances for Forest, the 23-year-old had received more yellow cards than he had goals scored, only netting against Aston Villa.

However, against his old club Man City on Saturday, 7 November, he inspired Oliver Glasner's side to a 3-1 at the City Ground, grabbing two of the goals.

Supporters have seriously questions if splashing £50m on his signature was a mistake by the Reds, but those concerns have now been squashed.

Of course, he could go the rest of the season without hitting the back of the net again, but right now, he appears to be worth his weight in gold.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson remains a huge fan of the 23-year-old striker, believing that £50m is simply the going rate for a young striker now.

"Well, I didn't think he was an expensive mistake anyway, because if you look at the price Chelsea paid, they were able to because of the release clause," he said.

"He was obviously worth £50m when he came out of Ipswich Town, because that's what young strikers who can score cost.

"And I think there's enough there to say that in this market, as crazy as it is, with the rarity of strikers, he's worth in the ballpark of £50m.

"I always felt that. Now, obviously he looks a lot better after he scores those two goals. But as I say, what quality striker are you going to get for less than £50m these days in the Premier League?

"It's just very rare, very rare to find. And what is he, 22, 23 maybe? Physically very strong, showed great pace against City and finished well."

It would be fair to say that Delap simply took his chances against the Citizens. The same could not be said for Erling Haaland at the other end.

He had just 25 touches, completing just four passes in 87 minutes, but with two shots on target, that was enough to secure all three points [FotMob].

It was more than just goalscoring for the centre-forward, though. He worked his socks off, recording the fourth-highest speed of anyone, and also the ninth-highest distance covered.

Delap entered into 12 total duels, won three fouls to relieve pressure, made two recoveries, and even made three line-breaking passes.
