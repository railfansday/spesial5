Here Are the Results from the 2026 Philadelphia Marathon

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Both the men’s and women’s winners set course records on Sunday morning.

The 2026 Philadelphia Marathon kicked off on Sunday, November 22 on a humid morning. Victor Shitsama dominated the men’s race, finishing in 2:09:23 and winning by more than nine minutes. In the women’s race, Dayna Pidhoresky handily won, running 2:26:34. Both times are course records.
