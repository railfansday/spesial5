Rob Gronkowski, vet DT make opinions known on Patriots-Aaron Rodgers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

There wasn't much optimism about the idea of the New England Patriots pursuing legendary quarterback Aaron Rodgers, who was shockingly released by the Pittsburgh Steelers on Wednesday.

The Patriots are preparing to start backup quarterback Tommy DeVito against the Los Angeles Chargers on Sunday. Drake Maye is currently sidelined indefinitely with a fractured right fibula, and New England is currently seeded behind the Steelers and Cleveland Browns in the wild-card race.

So, should the Patriots make a run at the future Hall of Famer?

Patriots veteran defensive tackle Milton Williams admitted it was "crazy" that Rodgers was benched (before his eventual release), but he is riding with DeVito as the starting quarterback in New England.

"I don't know about all that. We like who we got," Williams said on WEEI. "We were riding for Drake when he was in there for us. ...We got all of the confidence in the world for [DeVito] to go out there and take care of business."

Patriots Hall of Famer Rob Gronkowski took things a step further by mentioning the potential distractions that would come along with signing a player like Rodgers.

"I don't see him going to another team. I don't think anyone wants to sign him, even if he does get released," Gronkowski said on Up & Adams. "There's too much of a distraction and too much to bring on with him signing to the organization. I think this is possibly it, and all we can do is just celebrate his career."

Rodgers, a former Super Bowl champion and four-time NFL MVP, joining the Patriots would turn the Boston sports world upside down. But there's also the harsh, yet very real reality that he's 43 years old and way past his prime. There's also the inescapable drama that seems to follow everywhere he goes.

Before getting released, Rodgers went on The Pat McAfee Show and aired out the Steelers' dirty laundry, including accusations of head coach Mike McCarthy missing and falling asleep during meetings. Through 10 games, Rodgers has thrown for 2,241 yards, 16 touchdowns and six interceptions.

The Patriots absolutely need saving while Maye is on the shelf. But Rodgers is no longer Superman, and the team could run the risk of losing the locker room, along with the season, by bringing the polarizing legend into the mix.
