How long it takes to play GTA 6: Playthrough and achievements

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Grand Theft Auto VI is almost here. The highly anticipated video game will finally come out in the U.S. on Nov. 19, more than 13 years after its predecessor.

If you patiently waited more than a decade for GTA 6, you're probably hoping for a long, immersive experience that makes it worth the wait.

Good news! Reports are now coming in from game reviewers, and it seems like you're in for a treat. You'll likely be playing GTA 6 for a long time.

According to Insider Gaming, GTA 6 will take around 50 hours to complete the main campaign.

That estimate covers nearly 100 missions across the five chapters of the game. Of course, your playtime may vary depending on your skill level. But, that seems to be the average based on reports.

However, that's just the amount of time to complete GTA 6's main campaign. If you want to fully complete everything in the game, you're looking at a much longer gameplay time. Much longer.

Aside from GTA 6's main campaign, there are also side quests and an array of interactions, events, and locations to explore. Gamers will likely want to unlock the reported 55 achievements, including the Platinum Trophy and the Mythic achievement.

If you want to do all of that, you're looking at a reported 200 hours of gameplay for GTA 6.

That's not even counting any time spent just wandering the streets of Vice City or cruising around in a car while listening to the stacked GTA 6 soundtrack.

So, lock in, gamers (and please take plenty of breaks). More than 200 hours of GTA 6 await.
