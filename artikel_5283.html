Jeff Bezos donated $198.9 million in Amazon stock in two days, including shares he’s held since 1994

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Jeff Bezos gave away nearly $200 million worth of Amazon stock over two days this month, bringing his disclosed charitable stock donations this year to more than $600 million.

The Amazon founder and executive chair donated 674,088 shares on Nov. 12 and another 97,404 shares the following day, according to a filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission on Nov. 16. Forbes valued the 771,492 shares at $198.9 million based on Amazon’s closing price that day of $257.75.

The Bezos Earth Fund Foundation, which Bezos founded, received the smaller gift, worth about $25 million, and filed a notice of its intent to sell the shares. Bezos acquired those shares on July 5, 1994, the day he incorporated the company that became Amazon and nearly three years before its 1997 IPO.

The gifts come with the Earth Fund’s deadline about four years away. Bezos launched the fund in 2020 with a pledge to give away $10 billion by 2030. As of late September it had granted about $2.4 billion, meaning it would need to average roughly $1.8 billion a year to hit the target, Fortune reported.

The November gifts follow two earlier rounds. Bezos donated more than 1.25 million Amazon shares in May and 415,580 shares in August, worth about $323 million and $107 million, respectively, at the Nov. 16 close.

Altogether, Bezos has donated at least 2.44 million Amazon shares this year, worth approximately $629.1 million at that price.

Day 1 Academies, the nonprofit that runs Bezos Academy, his network of tuition-free preschools, received at least 450,837 of those shares: 220,200 in May and 230,637 in August. Together, the two nonprofits he founded account for about 22.5% of this year’s donated shares. The filings describe the rest as gifts to nonprofits without naming the recipients.

Even after the donations, Bezos directly held more than 878 million shares of Amazon, No. 1 on the 2026 Fortune 500, valued at approximately $226.4 billion as of the Nov. 16 close, per Forbes. This year’s gifts amount to less than 0.3% of that stake.

On Nov. 19, the Earth Fund told Fortune exclusively that it was committing more than $100 million to protect the Amazon rainforest through 15 new grants across South America. Cristián Samper, the fund’s managing director and leader for nature solutions, said it would use grantee reports and independent satellite analysis to track whether forests remain standing and deforestation declines.

Day 1 Academies runs 38 tuition-free, Montessori-inspired preschools in underserved communities across Arizona, Florida, Hawaii, Kentucky, Texas, and Washington. It enrolled 2,456 students for the 2026–27 school year, according to its annual report.

The preschools grew out of the $2 billion philanthropic commitment Bezos announced in 2018 to support early childhood education and families experiencing homelessness.

Through the other arm of that commitment, the Day 1 Families Fund, Bezos funds local organizations that help families move from shelters or the streets into stable housing. In 2025, the fund awarded $102.5 million to 32 groups in 20 states, the District of Columbia, and Guam.

Bezos and his wife, Lauren Sánchez Bezos, had given away an estimated $4.7 billion through the end of 2025, placing them 10th on Forbes’s list of America’s top philanthropists. Forbes put their giving at about $600 million in 2025.

Bezos told CNN in 2022 that he planned to give away most of his fortune during his lifetime. He said “the hard part is figuring out how to do it in a levered way,” drawing a comparison to building Amazon.

“So we’re building the capacity to be able to give away this money,” he said.

His ex-wife, MacKenzie Scott, whose net worth Forbes estimated at $27.1 billion as of Nov. 20, gave away $7.2 billion in 2025 alone. That brought her total since her 2019 divorce from Bezos to $26.4 billion, according to Forbes.

That single year of giving was more than Bezos and Sánchez Bezos have donated over their lifetimes.
