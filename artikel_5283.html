Crypto Hack Losses Hit $8.74 in 2026, More Than Triple 2025, Yet Without Binance the Year Ran Quieter: How Did Bitcoin and Ethereum React After Major Hacks?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Crypto hack losses hit $8.74B in 2026 vs $2.51B in 2025. Here’s how Bitcoin and Ether moved a day, a week and a month after major hacks.

Crypto hack losses reached $8.74 billion in 2026 by October 31, more than three times the $2.51 billion lost over the same ten months of 2025, after the October 29 Binance breach alone added $6.36 billion.

Take Binance out and the picture flips. The rest of 2026 comes to $2.38 billion, about 5% less than 2025 at the same point, even though attackers hit 325 targets against 120 a year earlier. More attacks, smaller hauls, and then one enormous one.

The market has its own pattern. Across the ten largest hacks since January 2025, Bitcoin’s move on the day of the attack ranged from -4.1% to +1.9%. A month later, for the nine with a full month of data, the range ran from -15.5% to +19.9%, and it followed whatever the broader market was doing.

The 2025 hacks landed in falling markets, and every one was followed by a lower Bitcoin price a month later. Most of 2026’s landed in rising ones.

So how bad has 2026 really been, and what does history say about the weeks after Binance?

The size of the biggest theft. Through September, 2026 was running behind 2025: $2.24 billion lost against $2.48 billion. Then Binance lost $6.36 billion across ten blockchains in one night, more than all of 2025’s hacks put together.

The two years share a culprit. The FBI tied 2025’s largest crypto hack, the $1.4 billion Bybit theft on February 21, to North Korea’s TraderTraitor group, and Chainalysis now ties Binance to the same unit. The biggest hacks of each year:

The rest of 2026 looks different from 2025 too. There were 325 incidents through October, almost three times 2025’s 120, and five 2026 hacks topped $290 million, against one in 2025. Stolen keys and cross-chain bridges did most of the damage outside Binance.

The gross number also overstates what stays lost. CertiK’s October tally counts $3.03 billion frozen or returned, mostly Binance funds caught on-chain, which cuts the month’s adjusted losses to about $3.47 billion. Trackers also disagree on the year: CertiK puts 2026 at $9.2 billion gross and 2025 at $3.35 billion, while DefiLlama has $8.74 billion and $2.72 billion.

Bybit is the closest match to Binance: a North Korean exchange theft over $1 billion. Bitcoin lost 14.2% within a week of it, with US tariff fears weighing on markets that week too, and it was still 12.4% lower a month on.

After Bybit, Bitcoin lost its floor within a week. After most of 2026’s hacks, it was higher a month later. The line between the two is clear on the chart: the 200-day EMA below, and the 50-day EMA at $80,293 above.

The crypto hack record says the first day rarely decides much. What Bitcoin does at the 200-day EMA over the next few weeks will show which path this one takes.
