Dem eyeing Homeland Security committee's oversight subcommittee gavel aims 'to dismantle DHS from the inside'

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Rep. Brandon Gill, R-Texas, criticizes Rep. Delia Ramirez's, D-Ill., proposed legislation to abolish I.C.E. and CBP and replace DHS, calling the 'reimagining safety' policy dangerous, on 'The Will Cain Show.'

A Democrat, who noted that she is seeking the House Homeland Security Committee's Oversight, Investigations and Accountability Subcommittee chair, said that she aims "to dismantle" the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) "from the inside."

"I introduced a bill to dismantle DHS, and I intend to dismantle DHS from the inside. Every investigation we do — whether it’s about detention, who is profiting at the expense of children who are literally in cages, or the expansion of these 287(g) agreements where local police [work] with federal agents to hunt down people — is critical to provide the evidence necessary to be able to bring the most justice," she said Tuesday to Mother Jones, which noted that the discussion with the lawmaker had been edited for the sake of length and clarity.

Ramirez reportedly said that "there’s no other agency that has consolidated the amount of power that DHS has with the amount of money that DHS has in order to terrorize the American people and literally rob us from our civil rights, while at the same time saying that they’re doing it under the guise of protecting us from terrorism. There is no democracy if DHS continues to operate the way it does."

'SQUAD' DEMOCRAT FORMALLY INTRODUCES BILL TO ABOLISH ICE, BORDER PATROL AND DISMANTLE DHS

Rep. Delia Ramirez, D-Ill., holds signs as President Donald Trump delivered his address to a joint session of Congress in the House Chamber of the U.S. Capitol on Tuesday, March 4, 2025. (Tom Williams/CQ-Roll Call, Inc via Getty Images)

"For me, investigations must lead into reimagining something new, where we protect cybersecurity infrastructure, where we protect FEMA, where we protect TSA, where we protect the customs functions at the border, and where we abolish ICE and CBP and reinvest funds towards healthcare, community safety and first responders," she said, according to the report.

Ramirez, who has served in the House since early 2023, just won re-election during the 2026 midterms.

Democrats will have the majorities in both chambers of Congress next year, and the congresswoman noted to Mother Jones that she is "pursuing the Homeland Security Oversight Subcommittee gavel."

'SQUAD' DEMOCRAT SLAMMED BY TRUMP ADMIN FOR MOST 'RADICAL' PLAN TARGETING ICE YET

Rep. Delia Ramirez, D-Ill., speaks during the People's State Of The Union Rally And Boycott Outside The Capitol on the National Mall on Feb. 24, 2026 in Washington, D.C. (Jemal Countess/Getty Images for MoveOn)

She asserted that "there is no affordability without accountability. Point-blank. If you want to actually talk about why people are living and experiencing the challenges they are right now, whether it’s that they can’t afford to buy groceries, they can’t afford to drive to work or pick up their kids, or they can’t afford the rent or buy their home, or why they’re living in the shadows and their businesses are shutting down, there has to be accountability to the actors that have made that happen. And it also means accountability to those that have terrorized them," she told the outlet.

"Yes, we should be passing Medicare for All. Yes, we need to pass a bill that increases wages in this country. Yes, we should abolish super PACs and tax the rich. But we also have to understand that, as we fight for an affordable country, we have to hold every fascist in this administration accountable," Ramirez added.

The White House, DHS and Ramirez's office did not immediately respond to Fox News Digital's requests for comment.
