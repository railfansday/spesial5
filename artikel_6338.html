Mullin: Wave of Tech Failures May Be Linked to AI

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Artificial intelligence could be playing a role in the cascade of high-profile technology failures seen over the last week, U.S. Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said Monday.

Mullin told FOX News’ Sean Hannity that he spoke with someone in the tech industry, “one guy who is very smart with tech,” who identified AI as a possible culprit.

“He said, ‘I believe that it’s the SI, because the programs aren’t designed to work as fast as SI is,'” he said.

The individual suggested that the technology’s ability to move fast and fix itself created “ripple effects” that overwhelmed older conventional systems. The secretary said that theory “kind of makes sense to me.”

The Trump administration uses the term “super intelligence,” or SI, in place of AI.

Mullin said his source did not want to be identified but ruled out at least one famous personality from the tech sector.

Multiple large, U.S.-based networks have crashed over the past week, including the FAA’s air traffic control communications system, United’s internal applications, Microsoft’s business services, and software used by banks such as JPMorgan Chase and Wells Fargo.

Mullin said there is no indication from the intelligence and cybersecurity communities that the outages are part of a cyberattack.

The FAA has blamed Friday’s nationwide ground stop on a faulty or improperly installed router, which flooded other routers with data and ultimately crashed the system.
