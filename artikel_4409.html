This is the new, eighth-generation version of the Lexus ES, and this mid-size luxury sedan introduces some major changes to the lineup.

The Lexus ES sedan was previously offered with either various gasoline engines or as a hybrid, but it's now offered as either a battery-electric model or this hybrid.

The hybrid version is called ES350h, and it has a total output of 244 horsepower.

The ES350h comes standard with front-wheel drive and offers all-wheel drive as an option.

This color is called Wavelength, and it's a new addition to the lineup.

The ES is longer, taller, and wider than before.

The ES350h uses the latest version of Toyota's hybrid setup, which the company calls its sixth-generation system.

All ES350h hybrid models come with 19-inch wheels.

The best way to tell the difference between the hybrid and the EV is the front end. If it has this slot-shaped opening under the logo, it's the hybrid.

The gasoline engine is a 2.5-liter inline-four.
