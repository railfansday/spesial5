Huckberry's Annual Cyber Week Sale Is Its Biggest of the Year—Save Over 60% on Editor-Approved Gear and Outerwear

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Gear-obsessed editors choose every product we review. We may earn commission if you buy from a link. Why Trust Us?

It’s the most wonderful time of the year—at least if you shop at Huckberry. Its annual “Cyber Week” sale runs through this weekend, and it’s easily the retailer’s biggest savings event of the year. It’s technically also the only sitewide sale of the year (with some exclusions).

From now through Sunday at midnight, you can take 15 percent off basically everything on Huckberry. This includes already-marked-down gear and apparel in its sale section, which is also particularly loaded right now. The only thing missing from this year’s sale is Flint and Tinder’s best-selling flannel-lined waxed jacket—it’s the only exclusion I found after hours of digging. But plenty of its other waxed jackets are a part of the sale, as well as virtually every other piece of outerwear, gear, and apparel you can think of. This includes editor-approved non-Huckberry brands as well, like Leatherman, Osprey, Peak Design, and more.

We rounded up some of our favorites below, but you should definitely check out the full sale if you have the time. You won’t regret it.

Flint and Tinder’s Flannel-Lined Quilted Waxed Rancher Jacket and the James Waxed Snap Jacket are both on sale and solid, if not spectacular, alternatives to the flannel-lined Waxed Trucker. Its best-selling All-American Stretch Denim Jeans are also 30 percent off, plus an additional 15 percent savings. These jeans are an absolute go-to for me; they’re soft and stretchy, yet hard-wearing.

There are a couple of Leatherman multitools part of the sale as well, including the editor-tested Leatherman Wave Plus (30 percent off) and the Skeletool CX (20 percent off). Getting the Wave Plus for under $100 is always a win; that’s the case here after factoring in the additional 15 percent off.

A couple of outdoor/travel bags are deeply discounted too. Take 40 percent off Osprey’s weather-resistant Campcore Transporter Duffel and 15 percent off one of my favorite commuter bags, Peak Design’s 30L Everyday Backpack.

Some of our other favorites include 35 percent off the Huckberry x One of These Days Custom Engraved 664 Fixed Blade Knife, 55 percent off the Classic Cuff Beanie, and 55 percent off the stylish yet rugged Flint and Tinder Utility Cargo Pant. Time is of the essence here.
