Why Nvidia Stock Fell Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Tuesday's earnings report now has to do more than hit big numbers, because the company also has to reassure investors that the Huang story isn't about to get worse.

Nvidia Corporation (NVDA -4.12%) stock fell once again, finishing Monday, Nov. 16, down 4.1%, as investors continued to digest the ouster of its co-founder, Jensen Huang.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC -1.92%) and the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -2.49%) sank 1.9% and 2.5%, respectively.

Jensen Huang, Nvidia's co-founder and CEO of 33 years, resigned last Thursday after reports broke of multiple extramarital affairs, including one with a woman reportedly suspected of spying for China.

Then, over the weekend, the Financial Times reported that he had discussed a comeback with allies. A petition asking for Huang to be reinstated had reached more than 180,000 signatures -- including Elon Musk's -- as of Monday morning.

But earlier today, CNBC reported that the board is not in talks to bring Jensen back and is instead focused on an investigation into whether Huang's conduct compromised national security or company secrets.

Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress is serving as interim CEO while the board searches for Huang's permanent replacement. She will lead the company's earnings call after the bell on Tuesday.

To say the stakes are high for this one would be an understatement. Wall Street is looking for revenue of $109 billion, or 91% growth. The market was already expecting incredible numbers, but now the company has to reassure investors that the Huang story isn't about to get worse.
