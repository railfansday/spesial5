Thanks to their all-foam layers, the Siena Signature and the SweetNight CoolNest both appear in best memory foam mattress guide (as our best cooling and best soft pick respectively). The EGOHOME Black 12" Foam Mattress is also made entirely of foam.

The SweetNight is 12" tall and features the most sophisticated design of the three, with six internal layers of foam. One layer is infused with cooling Phase Change Material (PCM), which has been proven to improve human heat dissipation by up to 25.6% compared with non-cooling mattresses, and the mattress itself is encased in a cool-to-the-touch knit cover.

The Siena Signature is the shortest at 10" tall, and has the simplest design with just two internal layers: one layer of memory foam for comfort and one stability foam layer for support. These layers of topped by a soft, breathable cover.

While the EGOHOME Black Foam has the same 12" height as the SweetNight CoolNest, it has half as many layers. These layers are enveloped in a graphene-infused cover for both temperature and moisture control.

I conducted hands-on testing on the Siena Signature Mattress, following our thorough mattress methodology. I also lab-tested the CoolNest and the EGOHOME Black, in addition to sleeping on them at home for subjective feedback.

I'm a side and back sleeper, and I loved sleeping on the CoolNest during my SweetNight CoolNest mattress review. It balanced medium-firm support and body-cradling comfort well, and the wider testing panel agreed that it has the right amount of pressure relief for side and back sleeping.

However, my fellow testers and I agreed that it isn't firm enough for stomach sleepers, even if is one of the best mattresses for side sleepers. Still, its responsive feel is great for moving around and switching sleep positions without feeling "stuck" in the mattress' foam.

The Siena Signature, on the other hand, is one of the best mattress for stomach sleepers. When I was part of the hands-on testing panel for the Siena Signature, the moment I felt this firm memory foam bed I immediately knew it offered tons of support — and that it was too firm for a side sleeper like me to test meaningfully.

Instead, a front-sleeping tester became the lead tester for our Siena Signature review, and she loved the lumbar support it offered to her preferred sleep style.

For my EGOHOME Black 12" Foam mattress review, I slept on the EGOHOME mattress for over seven months (for reference, we usually test mattresses at-home for three weeks), and I always felt supported no matter what position I slept in. However, the wider testing panel (along with our subjective tests) found that its body-hugging foam is great for side and back sleepers, but too soft for stomach sleepers.

Both the Siena Signature and the SweetNight CoolNest performed surprisingly well in our temperature regulation tests, where we used a thermal imaging camera to measure how well the mattresses absorb and dissipate heat.

As one of the best cooling mattresses on the market, the SweetNight has a rapid cooling rate of 2.1°F per minute. The Siena, meanwhile, displayed a slower cooling rate of 1.52°F per minute; however, it absorbed less heat in the first place.

Subjectively, too, the SweetNight wins, as my fellow testers and I were immediately impressed with its cool-to-the-touch feel. I also slept on it during summer heatwaves in a building with no AC, and I never felt overheated.

The Siena also kept its lead tester comfortable, but provided a temperature-neutral feel rather than an actively cooling feel — this may be because it's designed with a focus on breathability rather than featuring any specific cooling tech.

In contrast, the EGOHOME Black didn't impress me with its cooling when I slept on it during hot weather. I often woke up feeling overheated and could feel patches of heat build up under my pressure points. Testing panelist Ruth Jones, agreed, reporting that she felt the mattress warm up when she was lying on her stomach.
