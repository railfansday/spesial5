Ariana Grande Sets Peacock Double Play With ‘Eternal Sunshine Tour’ Concert Film & Making-Of Doc

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Ariana Grande hit the concert trail this past summer for the first time in seven years, and she is giving fans a close-up look at the show and how she and her team prepped for it.

The three-time Grammy winner has set two projects to stream on Peacock this month: documentary The Eternal Sunshine Diaries: The Making of the Tour on December 11 and concert film The Eternal Sunshine Tour the next day. She had teased a project on Monday.

The Making of the Tour follows Grande during the 30 days leading up to the opening of her 41-show arena tour as she and her team race to bring the ambitious production to life. At the same time, the Wicked Oscar nominee is putting the finishing touches on her eighth studio album, giving audiences an intimate window into her creative process during a pivotal moment. On December 12, Peacock will show The Eternal Sunshine Tour, which, per the streamer, journeys through the collapse of her reality, a cosmic rebirth, a reconnection with her inner child and the memories that shaped her. She transforms the Eternal Sunshine Tour into a cinematic celebration of healing, connection, and the music that has defined her life.

The singer’s fans already were on alert and suspected the she was working on something after Grande posted a photo on social media last week alongside director Christian Breslauer and editor Andrew Morrow. Breslauer worked with Grande on videos like “Yes, and?” and “We Can’t Be Friends (Wait for Your Love,” while Morrow is known for editing Beyoncé’s Renaissance: A Film by Beyoncé.
