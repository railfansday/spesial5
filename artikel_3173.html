Orion Energy (OESX) Q2 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

LED lighting growth offset EV charging weakness this quarter.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Orion Energy Systems, Inc. (OESX -7.67%) reported a 9% revenue increase for the second quarter of fiscal 2027 and reiterated its recently upgraded full-year revenue guidance. Management attributed the growth primarily to the LED Lighting segment, which offset declines in EV charging and maintenance revenue. The company reported its eighth consecutive quarter of positive adjusted EBITDA, supported by increased project activity with major retail and data center customers. Financial performance in the quarter was influenced by a mix shift that temporarily lowered gross margins, though management expects these rates to return to levels above 30% in the second half of the year.
