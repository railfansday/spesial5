iPad Air vs iPad Pro: Which should you buy?

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Apple’s iPad lineup is incredibly strong, and two standout options are the iPad Air and iPad Pro. If you’re trying to decide whether the iPad Pro is worth the extra cost this holiday season, here’s everything you need to know.

Bonus pick: The all-new iPad mini is up to $100 off for Black Friday as well.

Apple updated the iPad Air earlier this year, bringing the M4 chip for improved performance along with 12GB of RAM, up from 8GB in the previous generation.

The iPad Air is available in 11-inch and 13-inch screen sizes, and uses an LCD display with a 60Hz refresh rate. This means it doesn’t offer OLED or ProMotion, unlike the iPad Pro. It uses Touch ID for biometric authentication, supports the Magic Keyboard and Apple Pencil Pro, and offers the full iPadOS multitasking experience.

The iPad Air is available in four colors: blue, purple, starlight, and space gray. It starts at 128GB of storage and can be configured up to 1TB.

The iPad Pro is the best iPad money can buy. It’s powered by Apple’s M5 chip and offers many advantages over the iPad Air.

The biggest difference is the display technology. The iPad Pro is available in 11-inch and 13-inch sizes and uses Apple’s Ultra Retina XDR display with tandem OLED technology. This means it offers deeper blacks, better contrast, and higher brightness than the LCD screen found in the iPad Air.

The iPad Pro also supports ProMotion with adaptive refresh rates up to 120Hz. This means scrolling, animations, Apple Pencil interactions, and more will be significantly smoother.

Contrary to what the naming implies, the iPad Pro is also noticeably thinner and lighter than the iPad Air. The 11-inch iPad Pro measures 5.3mm thick, while the 13-inch model is even thinner at 5.1mm.

The iPad Pro offers Face ID instead of Touch ID, Thunderbolt connectivity, a four-speaker audio system, and nano-texture display options with the higher-end configuration.

For most people, the iPad Air is the right choice. It delivers the entire iPad experience, excellent performance, and comes in four fun colors and two different screen sizes.

The iPad Pro is the best iPad you can buy, but it’s overkill for most people. Don’t get me wrong, I love my iPad Pro. If it’s in your holiday budget, it’s a fantastic choice. I can’t overstate how impressive the tandem OLED display is. It’s significantly better than the LCD screen on the iPad Air. It’s also great having Face ID in the iPad form factor.

The iPad Pro is slightly more tempting at its current sale price, with the mid-range 13-inch model at an impressive $350 off.

Still, both the iPad Air and iPad Pro run the same iPadOS experience, and that’s why I think the iPad Air is the better value for most people. What do you think? Let us know down in the comments.
