Netflix's Spy Thriller Black Doves Season 3 Hopes Officially Addressed By Creator

After making waves with its exciting second season, it appears Black Doves is already gearing up for more of the series whether Netflix has ordered it or not.

Netflix's Black Doves, a spy thriller starring Kiera Knightley and Ben Whishaw, has developed a distinctive tone throughout its two short seasons, leaving viewers waiting in anticipation for more of the exciting series. According to the show's creator, Joe Barton, Black Doves season 3 may be closer than audiences anticipate. In an interview with Variety, Barton explained that he's "in a writers' room for [Black Doves season 3] at the moment, and we're hoping." While this doesn't confirm that Netflix has ordered another season, it definitely strengthens the possibility.

While Barton's answer doesn't necessarily mean that Netflix will move forward with another season, Black Doves season 2's ending left viewers yearning for more of the hit spy series. Knowing that Barton and his team are in a writers' room breaking down what would be next for Knightley's Helen Webb, especially after the shocking character death of the previous season, makes it clear that the creatives are interested in continuing the story. Now, the ball is in Netflix's court on whether or not the series moves forward with more.

Barton also discussed some of the major character developments made throughout Black Doves season 2, specifically discussing the decision to kill Agnes O'Caswey's Dani. While Knightley originally expressed interest publicly in Helen being the one to kill Dani after Black Doves season 1, the decision to have Gabrielle Creevy's Eleanor do the job threw a wrench into the larger story of the series. Barton shared that O'Casey's scheduling issues pushed the creative team to make a quicker than expected decision on who would be behind Dani's demise, and how it would happen.

Sharing that both Dani's death and Cecile's (Neve Campbell), were meant to be shocks to the system, and Black Doves season 2 succeeded on both fronts. With viewers still reeling from the fallout of the second season of the Netflix series, it's clear that there are still stories that need to be explored further before the show can truly come to a natural close. With Helen's marriage to Wallace (Andrew Buchan) facing a major turning point, Sam's departure from Helen also leaves them on uncertain terms.

With far too much left to explore, Black Doves season 3 is less of a question and more of a necessity. Thankfully, the reception of Black Doves season 2 along with Barton's answers about his desire to keep going feels like a great indicator of things to come.
