Steelers Coach Mike McCarthy Breaks Silence on Aaron Rodgers' Allegations

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Mike McCarthy has broken his silence after Aaron Rodgers was shockingly cut from the team after he publicly slammed the Pittsburgh Steelers head coach.

“It was a process that went on throughout this week,” McCarthy, 63, told reporters on Wednesday, November 25, when asked what initially led to Rodgers, 42, being benched as the starting quarterback ahead of the Steelers’ highly-anticipated Friday, November 27, matchup against the Denver Broncos.

McCarthy, who previously coached Rodgers during the quarterback’s time at the Green Bay Packers, explained, “We got to a point, we’d talked about some things, communication, things like that, and there was an ultimatum made [that was] clearly out of his job description. So it was my decision to bench him.”

After having “private conversations” with Rodgers, he was informed that backup quarterback Mason Rudolph would be stepping in for Friday’s game.

Rodgers addressed the coach’s decision during an appearance on the The Pat McAfee Show on Wednesday, where he proceeded to claim that McCarthy is “unhealthy.”

The four-time NFL MVP alleged that McCarthy had fallen asleep during team meetings, missed multiple team walkthroughs and was unwilling to connect with the team’s defensive players.

Rodgers also claimed that his longtime coach had yelled, “One, two, three, Packers!” when breaking a huddle during the Philadelphia Eagles game, hinting that McCarthy wasn’t aware of what team he was coaching at the time.

“The Philadelphia situation at halftime was very emotional,” McCarthy said in his Wednesday press conference, referring to Rodgers’ allegations against him. “There’s conversations prior to that. [It was an] embarrassing moment, but I just think we work a lot of hours, so definitely a difference of opinion from what I was told about the comments.”

When asked why he was the right person to keep leading the Steelers, McCarthy replied, “I know how to win.”

Shortly after Rodgers’ controversial interview, the Steelers announced they “released” the athlete while adding that he is no longer part of the organization.

McCarthy admitted on Wednesday that he was “disappointed” over Rodgers’ comments, but said he was “proud” of the team’s leadership and “urgency” to move forward as a unit.

“Aaron and I have a long history,” he explained, speaking about what led to Rodgers’ firing. “He has a lot of opinions, and those are things we always talked about. Once again, I consider those private conversations.”

McCarthy was fired from the Packers in 2018 after 12 years with the team for 12 years. Rodgers was the quarterback during his tenure, and they had their public ups and downs before McCarthy accepted the Steelers’ head coach job in 2026, where Rodgers had been playing since 2025.

When asked why he thought teaming back up with Rodgers for the 2026 Steelers season would be a good idea, McCarthy said, “We had a number of conversations early in the process of him returning.”

McCarthy hinted that those talks went well, but something recently changed.

“Unfortunately, this is where we are today,” he concluded.
