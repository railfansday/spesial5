Musician Ryan Adams' Relationship History Before Kate Farr Engagement

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Ryan Adams has spent nearly three decades in the public eye as an alt-country singer, a tabloid fixture and the subject of serious misconduct allegations.

In fall 2026, the musician announced his new engagement to writer and producer Kate Farr amid a 30-year age gap, almost 10 years after his divorce from actress Mandy Moore.

Adams dated Posey from 2003 to 2005, telling The Guardian in 2003 that she brought “normalcy and structure” to his life.

“[She’s] kind of frenetically artistic, as I am,” he told the outlet. “I think, if anything, some kind of comfort and artistry and a lot of just great feelings have been exchanged between us, and I think that, if anything has changed, it’s that I can dive in and do anything now and be less afraid. It’s as if in the past I’ve been jumping off bridges and been lucky enough to walk away unscathed, but I have a tow-rope now and I can go out to the middle, dive as deep as I like and know that I can come back up.”

Adams and Moore began dating in late 2007, when the actress was nine years his junior. Adams initially denied the relationship in March 2008, telling Daily Intelligencer: “I can’t believe you guys would print lies like that. You said Mandy Moore and I were dating! Which we’re not.”

Adams proposed in February 2009, and the couple married in Savannah, Georgia, the following month. They split just two months shy of their sixth anniversary in January 2015, telling Us in a joint statement that it was “a respectful, amicable parting of ways.”

Moore, who settled her divorce in June 2016, later accused Adams of abuse. The This Is Us actress was one of six women who came forward with allegations of emotional abuse or sexual misconduct against Adams.

“Music was a point of control for him,” Moore told The New York Times in February 2019. “He would always tell me, ‘You’re not a real musician, because you don’t play an instrument.’ … His controlling behavior essentially did block my ability to make new connections in the industry during a very pivotal and potentially lucrative time — my entire mid-to-late 20s.”

Adams, through his lawyer, called Moore’s depiction “completely inconsistent with his view of the relationship.”

“I am not a perfect man and I have made many mistakes. To anyone I have ever hurt, however unintentionally, I apologize deeply and unreservedly,” he told Us in a statement. “The picture that this article paints is upsettingly inaccurate.”

In June 2020, Adams issued a broader apology, stating, “There are no words to express how bad I feel about the ways I’ve mistreated people throughout my life and career.”

Moore responded the following day on the Today show, claiming it is “curious that someone would make a public apology but not do it privately.”

Adams confirmed his relationship with Farr in October 2026.

“Just stopping by to tell you I love you so very much and I hear you rattling around upstairs. ❤️,” he replied to an Instagram post, to which she responded, “WEIRDO!!! jk I love you 💛.”

Adams revealed the next month that he proposed to the writer.

“I’ve never been happier in my life. Kate is the most fascinating, kindest nerd I’ve ever met,” he told TMZ in a statement. “She’s my best friend, and I’ve never been happier. Here’s to love,” he told the outlet.
