Anderson beaten in Italia T10 league comeback after Lancashire exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

James Anderson was released by Lancashire at the end of the County Championship season and has joined Surrey in 2027; Anderson is among the players in action for Napoli Knights, a T10 franchise league in Italy

James Anderson’s franchise cricket return ended in defeat as Napoli Knights lost to Rome Gladiators by six wickets in their Italia T10 league opener.

Anderson is co-owner of Napoli Knights, with the 44-year-old's appearance his first since being released by boyhood club Lancashire at the end of the season.

Napoli Knights are one of six teams in the nine-day tournament at Roma Cricket Ground, running until November 28, with Anderson's side restricted to 73-9 after losing the toss and batting first.

Anderson finished nought not out from one delivery faced, scampering a bye from the final ball of the innings, before taking 0-14 from two overs during Rome Gladiators' successful chase.

England's history-making Test bowler conceded just three from a tight opening over of Rome's chase, before being hit down the ground by Singh for a maximum - during an 11-run over - when returning for a second stint later in the innings.

Anthony Mosca fired an unbeaten 45 from 27 deliveries and Jaspreet Singh ended 15 not out from nine, as Rome Gladiators claimed a five-wicket win with two balls remaining.

Wayne Madsen, captaining the Napoli Knights: "It's fantastic to have Jimmy [Anderson] here. He's inspirational to so many of us.

"To be playing with him, and for him to share his knowledge with this group, is amazing. I think, as we've seen, he has still got the skill to go out there and perform at the top level."

Napoli Knights have further matches against Brescia Lions (Saturday), Venice Livewire (Sunday), Milan Icons (Monday) and Bologna Hurricanes (TBC).

Anderson has joined Surrey ahead of the 2027 season in the Rothesay County Championship, having been left "sad" and "quite shocked" by his contract not being extended by Lancashire.

Upon the announcement of his move to the Kia Oval, Anderson said: "It's an honour to join a club like Surrey and I can't wait to start a new chapter in my career. I still feel like I have a lot to offer, taking wickets, trying to help the team win games of cricket.

"I also think that I can bring a lot to the team off the field and help those young players who are looking to develop their skillset and go on and represent their country."

Anderson, who turns 45 in July of next year, will get the opportunity to add to his haul of 1,193 first-class wickets and perhaps more silverware to a glittering CV.
