Chargers Injury Report: Ladd McConkey, Brenen Thompson DNP for 2nd consecutive practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

If Mike McDaniel's offense needs legitimate speed to be at its best, then we never get to see the offense in its fastest state with injuries plaguing the team's swiftest wideouts.

For the second day in a row, wideouts Ladd McConkey and Brenen Thompson did not take the field. McConkey continues to deal with back spasms that kept him out of last week's loss to the Texans and Thompson remains unable to practice despite having already missed the previous six games.

Some positive news on the wideout front is that Tre' Harris improved from a limited participant to a full on on Thursday.

Defensive lineman Justin Eboigbe saw some progression from Thursday's practice, going from DNP to limited. Same goes for cornerback Deane Leonard who went from limited to full as he comes back from a concussion.

Despite having his activated window opened, offensive guard/tackle Trey Pipkins missed his second consecutive practice with a knee injury/personal reason.

Linebacker Denzel Perryman and tight end David Njoku continued their path back to the field, as well. Perryman was a full participant on Thursday while Njoku logged his second consecutive day as limited.
