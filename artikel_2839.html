Baltimore Ravens plan to load up on Derrick Henry fades away vs. Jaguars

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Baltimore Ravens quickly showed their hand against the Jacksonville Jaguars on a wet night in Charm City.

Really, Derrick Henry left, right and center was the only hand they could play.

Henry, the Yulee native and 11-year veteran running back who has made a career of turning Jaguars defenses into mush, touched the ball on five of Baltimore's first seven offensive snaps and 10 of their first 16 in the Ravens' 33-10 loss to the Jaguars at M&T Stadium on Nov. 5 in a Thursday Night prime time game.

Henry's combination of runs from scrimmage, both taking handoffs and direct snaps and even a pass (which lost 3 yards) got the Ravens (4-5) only a 53-yard field goal by Jake Moody on their first possession of the game and not much else as the Jaguars (6-2) dominated the balance of the night.

With an undrafted free agent rookie quarterback in Joe Fagnano, who took his first NFL snaps only five days before in a loss to Buffalo, the Ravens had little choice but to put the game in Henry's hands and hope their defense kept the game close.

But when the Jaguars took a 17-3 halftime lead and the rain kept pouring down, there was little hope Fagnano would start slinging the ball around the field.

Henry gained 57 yards on 17 carries in his first game against the Jaguars since the 2023 season and his first in a Baltimore uniform. It matched his second-lowest total against the Jaguars since a 238-yard, four-touchdown performance in 2018.

When he played for the Tennessee Titans from 2016-2023, Henry had seven 100-yard games against the Jags and two more with 200 or more yards. He entered the Thursday Night game with 1,584 in 15 starts against Jacksonville, scoring 16 touchdowns and averaging 5.4 yards per carry.

He wouldn't come close to that kind of production against a Jaguars team with a far better defense than most of the units Henry faced during his banner years with the Titans, and with no threat of Fagnano making anything happen in the passing game — certainly not the fault of Henry, the former Yulee High and Alabama star who was trying to carry the load with an beat-up offensive line in front of him, against the NFL's No. 1 rush defense.

Fagnano finished with 10 completions in 18 attempts for 60 yards and a 55.6 passer rating.

The Jaguars locked in on Henry in the second half and held him to 6 yards on five carries. He had a rare attempt for a loss, with Travon Walker making the tackle for minus-2 yards.

Henry took a direct snap 10 times in the shotgun. He ran six times for 25 yards and threw a pass to Zay Flowers, who was snowed under by Travon Walker and Ventrell Miller for a 3-yard loss.

Henry pitched or handed the ball off twice to Flowers after taking shotgun snaps, with Flowers gaining a total of 8 yards. Henry also pitched the ball to Fagnano for no gain.

Even the loss last week of nose tackle DaVon Hamilton didn't deter the Jaguars in putting the clamps on Henry.

Henry was reasonably effective in the first half when Baltimore had a big edge in time of possession but with Jaguars quarterback Trevor Lawrence throwing passes of 52 yards to Nate Boerkircher to set up the first score and then hitting Parker Washington in stride for a 50-yard score less than a minute left in the second quarter.

Henry's 51 yards on 12 carries still didn't make a dent in the first half bottom line save for Moody's field goal.

The Jaguars started Matt Dickerson and Arik Armstead on the inside and rotated in Ruke Orhorhoro and rookie Albert Regis. Linebackers Foye Oluokun, Miller and Branson Combs had solid games and in the second half Henry and the Ravens simply wore down.

Fagnano, who threw only one interception last season with UConn, didn't help matters when he was picked in the second half by Montaric Brown and Jourdan Lewis. Later in the game, LaJohntay Wester fumbled the kickoff after Cam Little's fourth field goal, with Jalen McLeod recovering.

Justice Hill scored on a 65-yard run for the Ravens in the fourth quarter, the longest run of the season against the Jaguars' defense. It also ended the Jaguars' streak of holding running backs to 70 yards or less at 24 games.

Hill had 85 yards on nine carries, giving the Ravens 172 yards as a team, the most against the Jags defense since Jonathan Taylor gained every yard for the Colts (177) in the final game of the 2024 season.

Hill scored against a Jaguars defense on the field that had only one player with a start this season, cornerback Jarrian Jones, and two other players who see frequent action, Combs and defensive end Danny Striggow.
