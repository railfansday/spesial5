Stephen A Smith insists LeBron James ‘ruined’ one part of NBA All-Star Weekend

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

ESPN analyst Stephen A. Smith believes LeBron James is directly responsible for ruining the NBA All-Star weekend's premier event – The Slam Dunk contest.

The four-time NBA champion has nearly achieved everything in the sport and is playing in Year 24 for the Philadelphia 76ers in a bid to add another championship.

However, he has never participated in the Slam Dunk contest, despite being athletically gifted. James is a 22-time All-Star and has won the All-Star Game MVP award three times. Despite his active participation in the All-Star weekend, James has refused to participate in the Slam Dunk contest, which is a decision Stephen A. Smith is not a fan of.

James has some of the most explosive dunks in the history of the game, and seeing him in the Slam Dunk contest would definitely be a joy to watch. Although it seems impossible now, James had previously stated that he was an in-game dunker, and enrolling in a contest to dunk was something he found hard.

Smith believes that James' refusal to participate in the dunk contest has led to its slow decline over the years.

"I hold LeBron James directly responsible for ruining the Slam Dunk Contest. It started with him that stars began not participating, cause they follow LeBron's lead. Kobe, Vince, Tracy McGrady everybody who were sky walkers participated in the Slam Dunk Contest but LeBron James, I hold that against him," said Smith on The Stephen A. Smith Show.

Out of the players Smith named, Kobe Bryant and Vince Carter both won the Slam Dunk contest. Tracy McGrady participated in the competition, but was beaten by Carter.

To Smith's point, stars have hesitated to participate in the Slam Dunk contest. Zach LaVine and Aaron Gordon put on a show for the fans during the 2016 All-Star weekend's Slam Dunk contest, but the stars Smith is talking about include the likes of Russell Westbrook, who was a ferocious dunker and one of the best players in the league, but never participated in the dunk contest.

In recent years, the competition has regressed, and fans have asked for improvements to be made to it. However, the simple reason it doesn't have the same attraction now is the lack of superstars participating in it. It will be interesting to see if there will be any changes to the competition or the format, which may encourage more stars to be a part of the event.
