Eagles-Steelers matchups to watch in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Eagles-Steelers matchups to watch in Week 11 originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Eagles (2-7) are hosting the Pittsburgh Steelers (6-3) at the Linc on Sunday afternoon at 1.

The biggest move Howie Roseman made before the trade deadline was sending Jordan Davis to the Jacksonville Jaguars for a first-rounder and a second-rounder. So this will be the Eagles' first game without their run-stuffing nose tackle and they're facing a team that is averaging 4.4 yards per carry on the ground — the fourth-highest average in the NFL.

The Steelers are 14th in overall rushing but have been efficient this season. Their ground game starts with running back Jaylen Warren, who has 126 carries for 719 yards and 2 touchdowns this season. That 5.7 yards-per-carry average is the best in the NFL among qualified players. Of Warren's 719 yards this season, 533 of them have come after contact. He's averaging 4.2 yards after contact per attempt, which is the fourth-highest average in the NFL. So it'll be important for the Eagles to wrap up in this game.

The good news for the Eagles is that they had their best run defense game of the season before the bye week against the Giants, allowing just 80 yards on the ground. It was the first time all season they held their opponent under 100 rushing yards.

Eagles LBs and S vs. Pat Freiermuth, Darnell Washington

The Steelers' leading receiver this season is DK Metcalf, who has 432 yards and 2 touchdowns on 35 catches. He is also leading the team in targets with 70. So maybe this is another good week to have Quinyon Mitchell travel with a top receiver. But the Steelers also have two tight ends who each see a ton of action in Pat Freiermuth and Darnell Washington.

Freiermuth: 38 targets, 27 catches, 270 yards, 5 TDs

Washington: 31 targets, 20 catches, 259 yards, 3 TDs

The Steelers have 15 passing touchdowns this season and 8 of them come from these two players. So the Eagles have to be aware of them in the red zone.

The Eagles have actually been very good against tight ends this season. They have allowed 38 catches for 386 yards and 3 touchdowns to opposing tight ends this season. Just two teams (Titans and Jaguars) have allowed fewer yards to opposing tight ends. But this will be a big test for their safeties and linebackers, especially if Zack Baun (back) is unable to play.

Aaron Rodgers is one of the greatest QBs in NFL history but he's obviously past his prime. Rodgers will turn 43 on Dec. 2, but he's having an OK season. He has completed 59.9% of his passes for 2,014 yards with 15 touchdowns and 6 interceptions. But he has already been sacked 26 times in nine games. The only QBs who have been sacked more this year are Deshaun Watson (37), Justin Herbert (33), Geno Smith (29) and Bryce Young (29).

Despite that, the Steelers are allowing a pressure rate of just 31.4%, which is the seventh-best QB pressure rate in the NFL. The 49ers are tops at 27.9%. While Rodgers is getting sacked quite a bit, he's also getting the ball out quickly. His average time to throw is 2.75 seconds, which is eighth in the NFL.

The Steelers are going to be without starting left guard Mason McCormick, who suffered an ankle injury in the loss to the Bengals.

The Eagles' defense finally broke out before the bye week with six sacks against the Giants after having just 10 entering the game. But with 16 sacks, they still rank 28th in the NFL this season and their pressure rate of 32.6% ranks 26th.

The plan this season was to have third-round pick Markel Bell sit and learn behind all-time great Lane Johnson but that plan got thrown into a blender earlier this year when Johnson abruptly retired. While Bell has had some rough moments, it'll be his job for the rest of the season. That became abundantly clear when the Eagles released Fred Johnson late last week. In six games, Bell has given up 5 sacks and 19 pressures. There have been ups and downs as the Miami product adjusts to the NFL.

This won't be an easy week. T.J. Watt leads the Steelers with 7 1/2 sacks and is second in pressures on the team with 23. (Don't sleep on Alex Highsmith or Nick Herbig; this is a dangerous group of edge rushers.) Watt, 32, is a future Hall of Famer and a former Defensive Player of the Year who is fourth on the active sack list with 122.5. He's behind just Von Miller (139.5), Cameron Jordan (132) and Myles Garrett (128.5). Oh yeah, and he plays most of his snaps on the defensive left, which sets up this matchup against Bell. The Eagles will need to help the rookie in this game.

Before the bye week, Saquon Barkley was dealing with hamstring and back injuries that were limiting him. Barkley looked good in limited action against the Cowboys but the last two games had 26 carries for 52 yards. Barkley has looked good when healthy this season but he's also running behind an offensive line that has really struggled this season. He'll see a tough test in Week 11.

The Steelers this season have the NFL's No. 3-ranked run defense. They have given up just 83.2 yards per game on the ground in 2026 and have held opponents to 43.3 rushing yards per game over their last three games. They have given up just 0.71 yards before contact per attempt.

DeVonta Smith vs. Asante Samuel Jr., Jamel Dean

In the Steelers' Week 10 loss to the Bengals, they had no answer for Jamar Chase, who ended up with 11 catches on 14 targets for 231 yards. Based PFF's data, Chase caught a pass against six different Steelers defenders but did most of his damage against their top two corners:

Chase vs. Samuel Jr.: 4 targets, 2 caches, 96 yards

Chase vs. Dean: 5 targets, 4 catches, 53 yards

The Steelers have been playing Jalen Ramsey at nickel corner and safety. He's no longer a full-time outside cornerback, so we won't see him on Smith in this game. At one point, the Steelers had a cornerback who would have had a better shot against Smith but they were unable to reach a contract agreement with Joey Porter Jr. and traded him to the Cowboys in late September.

Smith has missed the last three games with a hamstring injury but if he's healthy in this one, it could be a big opportunity for him in his first game with starting QB Joe Milton.
