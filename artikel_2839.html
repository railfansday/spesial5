Cowboys vs Eagles national anthem singer: What to know about Lauren Daigle

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Dallas Cowboys and Philadelphia Eagles will meet on Thanksgiving Day at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas, for an important NFC East battle.

The Cowboys (5-6) are coming off a dominant win over the Tennessee Titans, while the Eagles (3-7) will be looking to build on last week's 19-16 victory over the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Since it's the middle game of the NFL's three-course Thanksgiving menu (4:30 p.m. ET, Fox), viewers shouldn't have to miss any of the pregame pageantry. Just before kickoff, two-time Grammy Award-winning singer Lauren Daigle will perform her rendition of "The Star-Spangled Banner."

Instead of a touchdown, Jones was tackled after a 73-yard return. The Ravens went on to win the game, 22-20.

Contemporary Christian singer Lauren Daigle will be the featured performer and sing "The Star-Spangled Banner" before the Cowboys and Eagles kick off at AT&T Stadium on Thanksgiving Day.

Daigle first reached No. 1 on the Billboard Top Christian Albums chart in 2015 with her debut "How Can It Be." She achieved crossover success with the single "You Say," which peaked at No. 29 on the Billboard Hot 100 chart in 2018. That song, and the album "Look Up Child" earned her a pair of Grammy Awards in 2019 for Best Contemporary Christian Music Album and Best Contemporary Christian Music Performance/Song.

Daigle is no stranger to performing in front of NFL fans.

She sang "America the Beautiful" alongside Trombone Shorty before Super Bowl 59 in her home state of Louisiana.

In addition to her two Grammy wins, she was also nominated in 2016, 2017 and 2024. She has won four American Music Awards as well.
