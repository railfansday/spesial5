LaMelo Ball injury update: Timberwolves star hurt late in win vs. Thunder

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Minnesota Timberwolves were already without their biggest star due to injury. Now they may have lost their second-best player, at least for the short term.

Late in Minnesota's game Wednesday, Nov. 25 against the Oklahoma City Thunder, with 2:29 left in the fourth quarter, point guard LaMelo Ball was defending a play in the middle of the paint, when he jumped and landed awkwardly, rolling his left ankle. Ball immediately grabbed at his foot and writhed on the floor in obvious discomfort. He remained on the floor for several moments before being helped toward the bench.

Trainers assessed Ball's foot while he sat on the end of the bench. After some minutes, he eventually got up and limped toward the locker room.

"No," Timberwolves coach Chris Finch told reporters after the game when asked if he had an update on Ball. "It looked pretty bad, but I'm not sure."

The Athletic reported, however, that Ball was in good spirits in the postgame locker room and was laughing and joking with his teammates.

Ball scored 19 points on 7-of-18 shooting before leaving the game, adding 8 assists and 7 rebounds.

Ball is an absence the Timberwolves cannot afford. The team has been without All-Star Anthony Edwards, the most dynamic shooting guard in the NBA, since the fifth game of the regular season. Edwards underwent an arthroscopic procedure on his knee to clean up the area and reduce swelling.

Despite Edwards' absence, the Timberwolves have thrived, sprinting out to a 14-5 record, good for third place in the Western Conference.

Guard Ayo Dosunmu and forward Jaden McDaniels have also had a big offensive impact with Edwards sidelined, and if Ball is forced to miss some time, even more will be asked of the pair.

The Timberwolves would eventually beat the Thunder in their game, 113-106.
