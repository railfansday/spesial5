Lakers optimistic about Austin Reaves’ availability after star guard’s latest step

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Austin Reaves' left heel injury sidelined him for the fifth consecutive game on Monday.

But he also took an important step in his return-to-play process before the Lakers hosted the Jazz again at Crypto.com Arena, leaving the team optimistic about his availability to return later in the week.

Austin Reaves has been sidelined for five games because of his heel injury. NBAE via Getty Images

Reaves participated in a "stay-ready" game on Monday after the team's shootaround at their practice facility as he recovers from his bruised left heel.

The star guard was a full participant in Sunday's practice, but it didn't include any live action.

The Lakers ruled him out for Monday's game Sunday evening after coach JJ Redick said Reaves was "making progress."

Austin Reaves (15) of the Los Angeles Lakers shoots a free throw. NBAE via Getty Images

"We want to be consistent with our [return-to-play] stuff," Redick said. "I think [Monday] was a big step just in terms of getting a stay-ready game in. And he played great, moved great. We're optimistic for the rest of this week going forward."

The 28-year-old guard suffered the injury on Nov. 11 and has been considered day-to-day since.

Redick said Friday that Reaves was "hopefully" returning over the next two games.

Austin Reaves of the Los Angeles Lakers smiles during a game against the Utah Jazz. NBAE via Getty Images

The Lakers' next game after Monday is against the Spurs on Friday in San Antonio.

Reaves has averaged 24.7 points (51.3% from the field, 40.9% from beyond the arc), 4.7 assists and 3.3 rebounds in the 12 games he's played.
