The Wizards move up in the NBA power rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Washington Wizards have moved up to No. 19 in the 2026-27 NBA.com power rankings by John Schuhmann. Last week, they were 23rd.

Right now, the Wizards' overall offensive and defensive ratings still are in the 20s. However, during their current three game win streak, the defense in particular has been very good.

Here's Schuhmann's blurb on Washington's jump in the rankings.

The Wizards are the team whose success has been most dependent on the strength of their opponent. They're one of three teams (the Nets and Bucks are the others) that don't have a win (they're 0-8) against the 18 teams that are currently over .500. But they're now 7-3 within the bottom 12 in the league, with four of their seven wins having come against the Bulls and Magic. The much bigger difference between their 10 games against losing teams (106.2 points allowed per 100 possessions) and their eight against winning teams (126.1 allowed per 100) has been on defense.

Their bottom-five offense has shown some improvement, scoring 115.0 points per 100 possessions (12th) over their last six games. Trae Young continues to really struggle inside the arc, but Anthony Davis has been pretty consistent on that end of the floor and Kyshawn George has taken another step forward in his third season.

Coming up: The Wizards will host the Magic again this week. First they'll complete Emirates NBA Cup action against the Celtics, to whom they've lost 11 straight games, with the last nine of those losses having come by an average of 22.3 points.
