Ashbrook football scores dominant NCHSAA 6A Round 1 win. Here's how.

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Home losses for Ashbrook football's 2027 class have been an extreme rarity the past three seasons. With its Friday, Nov. 6 matchup against Central Cabarrus potentially the senior class' last game in front of home fans, the Green Wave made sure not to disappoint.

Ashbrook is on the Round 2 for a third year in a row, the No. 12 Green Wave advancing in the NCHSAA 6A West playoffs with a 47-21 win over 21-seed Central Cabarrus. Ashbrook will next travel to Winston-Salem for a Friday, Nov. 13 matchup against fifth-ranked Mount Tabor.

"I feel like this whole season we haven't really been playing to our standard," said Ashbrook senior quarterback A.J. McClain. "This week, I told myself and I told my team: 'We're going to have to turn it up a notch to get to where we want to go."

Ashbrook scored touchdowns on each of its five possessions in the first half, including four TD passes by McClain.

It only took two plays for Ashbrook to draw first blood, running back Savion Lindsay scoring on a 12-yard TD run. Following a defensive stop deep in its territory, the Ashbrook offense went 92 yards the other direction to up its lead to 13-0. The 11-play drive was capped by McClain's first scoring toss of the night, a 32-yard catch-and-run to Zyere Dixon for a TD.

Central Cabarrus provided a sign of life on its following drive, cutting the Green Wave lead to 13-7 on a Dillon Ryan TD pass to wideout King Howard. Ashbrook seized momentum back on its following drive, McClain hitting standout wide receiver Braylon Smith for a short TD.

McClain connected with Dixon for another TD pass, in addition to one in the direction of fellow senior Jakelan Littlejohn for a 33-7 Green Wave halftime lead.

"A lot of people don't know about (Dixon), but he's a dawg," said Ashbrook football coach Darius James. "But we have four or five guys who, when the ball is in their hands, can do special things. We just have to be consistent about getting them the ball."

Messiah Paterson added a short TD run late in the third to up Ashbrook's lead to 40-7. Backup quarterback Bobby Edwards Jr. also got in on the act, hitting Littlejohn on a TD pass with just over six minutes left.

Howard's second TD reception, as well as a LaQui Mitchell-Moore TD run in the fourth made the score somewhat more palatable for Central Cabarrus, whose season ends at 3-8.

As for Ashbrook (6-5), its senior class likely put a bow on its time at William N. Eccles Field in Gastonia. Over the last three seasons, the group has compiled a 19-1 home record with its only setback a Week 1 defeat at the hand of Palisades.
