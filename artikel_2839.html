Jaguars list Bhayshul Tuten, Travon Walker as questionable for Thursday night

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Jaguars will be making gameday calls about a pair of starters before facing the Ravens on Thursday night.

They listed running back Bhayshul Tuten (ankle, foot) and defensive end Travon Walker (rib) as questionable to play in the game. Tuten was listed as a non-participant in practice all week while Walker moved up to limited participation the last two days.

If Tuten cannot play, Chris Rodriguez, Ameer Abdullah, and LeQuint Allen will be the running back options for Jacksonville. Tuten is coming off a 159-yard, two touchdown performance against the Colts in Week 8.

Walker has 18 tackles, three sacks and seven quarterback hits this season. Josh Hines-Allen, Bryan Thomas, and Danny Striggow also saw action at defensive end against Indianapolis.
