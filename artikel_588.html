NBC Plans to Give Thanksgiving Parade Ads Extra Run After Macy’s Postponement

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

NBC has a Thanksgiving Week present for advertisers scheduled to appear in the now-postponed Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade.

The network plans to run commercials from sponsors of the event twice this week — once when the 2026 parade airs on Saturday and once on Thursday, when the network will run a repeat of last year’s Macy’s extravaganza. In short, Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade advertisers get two runs on the network for ther price of one. The details were confirmed by an NBC spokesperson. Macy’s and NBC decided to postpone the parade to Saturday, November 28, citing extreme wind conditions and inclement weather expected on Thursday. Many of the signature balloons used in the parade cannot be controlled properly during high wind speeds. The network said it is “committed to ensuring this remains a high-priority opportunity for our clients with both the reach and scale of tomorrow’s re-airing and the original live telecast on Saturday.”

NBC has many reasons to keep advertisers in the parade happy. The cost of a 30-second commercial in the parade is up 15% to 20% over last year, according to Guideline, a tracker of ad spending, when NBC was able to charge between $900,000 and $1 million per unit. Those figures would indicate that a 30-second spot in the 2026 parade could cost between$1.03 million and $1.2 million. Ad spend on the event is pacing about 25% to 30% ahead of last year, according to Guideline. The parade snared more than $88.7 million in ads last year, the company says.

The parade is also part of a time-honored lineup that includes the National Dog Show and a primetime NFL game. The Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade telecast was the opening event in a extended schedule of holiday programs that the network uses to lure marketers eager to court holiday shoppers.

Geico had plans to run a two-minute commercial that uses stop-motion animation featuring many of its trademark characters, such as the Geico Gecko. Starbucks had signed on to promote a new holiday album, “The Holiday Blend,” and is the sponsor of a takeover of an NBC livestream of the parade on TikTok.

NBC isn’t the first TV network to confront delays tied to a massive live event. CBS in 2013 gave some of the advertisers in that year’s Super Bowl an extra run of their commercials after a 35-minute power outage brought a game between the Baltimore Ravens and the San Francisco 49’ers. Bud Light and Subway were among the marketers that enjoyed an extra airing of ads that had multi-million-dollar price tags.
