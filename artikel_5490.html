FHFA Increases Conforming Loan Limit by $22k for 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Federal Housing and Finance Authority (FHFA) has announced a new conforming loan limit (the maximum dollar amount for a conventional mortgage) for Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac mortgages in 2027. The new limit for one-unit properties will be $855,400, up $22,650 from 2026’s limit.

The Housing and Economic Recovery Act (HERA) requires FHFA to adjust the Enterprises’ baseline conforming loan limit each year to reflect the change in the average U.S. home price, according to a release. As the organization’s research found that home prices were up 2.72% on average from Q3 2025 to Q3 2026, the FHFA said that the conforming loan limit has increased on the same baseline.

In addition, high cost areas (where 115% of the local median home value exceeds the baseline conforming loan limit) have a higher limit due to HERA, according to a release. The high cost area limit sits at a ceiling of 150% the baseline limit, which in 2027 will be $1,283,100.

The FHFA also noted special statutory provisions establishing different loan limits for Alaska, Hawaii, Guam and the U.S. Virgin Islands. In these areas, the baseline loan limit and the ceiling loan limit for one-unit properties will be $1,283,100 and $1,924,650, respectively.

Due to rising home values, the FHFA stated that the conforming loan limit will be higher in all but 24 U.S. counties or county equivalents.
