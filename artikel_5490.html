Unanet's AI-First Innovations Drive GovCon and AEC Firms to Switch from Competing ERP and CRM Systems

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Nearly half of Unanet's new customers in 2026 came from competing platforms as firms seek modern technology to improve efficiency, profitability and growth

DULLES, Va., Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Unanet, the leader in AI-first ERP and growth software for the government contracting (GovCon) and architecture, engineering and construction (AEC) industries, is seeing continued momentum as firms replace their existing ERP and CRM systems with modern technology designed to reduce manual work, connect workflows and help teams operate more efficiently.

In 2026, almost half of Unanet's new customers migrated from another ERP or CRM solution, reflecting growing demand for technology that simplifies everyday work and connects critical business processes. When prospective customers evaluate solutions, outcomes such as easier day-to-day workflows, real-time visibility into project and financial performance, less manual reporting and a better total cost of ownership make Unanet the clear choice. Increasingly, they also cite the pace of Unanet's innovation and its ability to bring actionable intelligence directly into the work their teams do every day.

This momentum continues as GovCon and AEC firms face mounting pressure to compete more efficiently, strengthen financial visibility and leverage AI to move work forward. Unanet's 2026 AEC Inspire Report found that 75% of AEC firms are using AI, but only 29% have high confidence in the underlying data. Additionally, the 2026 GAUGE Report found that 70% of GovCons are using AI. Together, these findings underscore that firms need quality, structured data to use AI effectively, along with modern, integrated business systems that connect that data, streamline workflows and turn AI investments into measurable business results.

"As firms face tighter competition, growing compliance requirements and pressure to deliver more with existing resources, we're seeing more GovCon and AEC leaders recognize that their current platforms are holding them back," said Jeff Davison, Unanet's Chief Customer Officer. "They want software that's easier for employees to use and easy-to-adopt AI capabilities that give them real-time visibility into pipeline, project and financial performance, so teams are working smarter."

Continuous innovation is underpinning customer choice

Unanet's continuous innovation is a key differentiator in customers' decisions to switch from their current solutions. In 2026, Unanet introduced new capabilities across its portfolio and customer experience, including ChampAI and KnowledgeAI, which make it easier for customers to implement, adopt and get support for Unanet, with compliance built in.

Two examples show how Unanet's innovation is reaching day-to-day work:

"Champ is a tool to automate anything you're looking for in the ERP system. It will automate it and bring efficiency to you," said Brian Bartholomew, Vice President of Operations at AGEISS, a leading defense contractor and loyal Unanet customer. "You don't have to be a programmer. You don't have to be a database guru. You just say, 'I want to see this,' and it does it."

Unanet's ERP and CRM enhancements also include automated accounts payable and receivable, streamlined spend management, vendor invoice automation, assisted bank reconciliation, a modern timesheet experience, Champ Assist within CRM and new integrations with leading companies like Procore and ADP. Together, these help finance teams move through billing reviews faster and get invoices out the door sooner, while simplifying approvals, data imports and proposal development so business development and marketing teams can spend more time moving opportunities forward.

"AI is only as useful as the data behind it, and our customers' project, financial and pipeline data already lives in Unanet," added Davison. "That's why Champ Agents is built directly on our core systems. Agents automate real workflows with consistent, deterministic results and follow the same security model our customers already trust, so firms can adopt AI with confidence instead of adding risk."
