Aravind Sviniras Perplexity AI Predicts a Mega-Bullish XRP Price Move

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Aravind Sviniras’ Perplexity AI predicts that even with the crypto market currently in freefall, XRP could still see an insanely bullish move between now and the end of the year.

The broader market currently feels heavy, and XRP has slipped into the $1.08–$1.11 range. However, a highly unlikely yet possible catalyst could dramatically change the narrative almost overnight.

Imagine a scenario in which a major global payments consortium, comprising large banks, a dominant cross-border messaging network, and multiple central bank digital currency pilots, officially designates the XRP Ledger as the preferred settlement rail for high-value international transfers. In this scenario, XRP would need to serve as a liquidity and bridging asset.

In this speculative scenario, Aravind Sviniras’ AI model believes that new capital and liquidity regulations could incentivize, or even require, participating institutions to maintain substantial XRP balances as collateral and working capital.

This sudden surge in demand, combined with an expanding suite of institutional products, could propel XRP into a true price-discovery phase and potentially surpass previous cycle highs.

XRP has been under pressure, recently trading around $1.08–$1.11 after failing to hold higher levels in the $1.20–$1.30 area. The chart shows a series of lower highs and a test of support near the $1.05–$1.10 zone.

Volume has been elevated on down days, reflecting distribution, yet longer-term holders note that the price remains well above the deeper 2026 lows near $0.99.

On higher timeframes, the structure still retains a higher-low sequence from the broader cycle base. A sustained reclaim of the $1.30–$1.40 region would be the first meaningful technical confirmation of a trend change, opening the path toward the previous cycle high near $3.65.

In an extreme demand-driven scenario, where institutional liquidity requirements force large-scale accumulation, measured-move projections and multi-year Fibonacci extensions from the multi-year base could theoretically support far higher targets.

Momentum indicators currently sit in oversold-to-neutral territory on intermediate timeframes, leaving room for a sharp expansion once selling pressure exhausts and buyers step in with conviction.

Current weakness may simply be the final shakeout before a stronger narrative takes hold. While short-term price action looks bearish, accelerating real-world adoption, regulatory milestones, and the lingering potential for a structural demand catalyst offer a more hopeful longer-term case.

Markets often bottom when fundamentals improve but sentiment remains depressed. XRP may be approaching that zone.

These developments show real-world utility and regulatory progress that many token holders argue is not yet reflected in the price. In a risk-on environment, the gap between rising institutional adoption and current market valuation could close violently.
