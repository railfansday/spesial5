Mortgage Rates Dip Ahead of Holiday, Application Activity Increases as Rates Fluctuate

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Real estate housing market decline concept. Wooden house model standing next to falling red arrow on floor with window blind shadows. Decreasing property value and mortgage rate.

Even while lingering above 7%, slight dips in the average mortgage rate were enough to spur home-buyer activity this past week.

The latest Market Composite Index from the Mortgage Bankers Association (MBA)—its measure of mortgage loan activity volume and includes purchases and refinances—shows mortgage application activity increasing 6.1% on a seasonally adjusted basis, a notable increase from last week’s 2.3% decrease. On an unadjusted basis, the Index increased 3% compared to a 5% decrease the previous week.

This week’s increase follows weeks of fluctuations as the 30-year fixed mortgage rate still remains over 7%–this past week decreasing to 7.21% from 7.28% the previous week, according to Freddie Mac and currently sits at 7.29%, according to Mortgage News Daily.

“Mortgage rates moved higher, with the 30-year fixed rate rising to 7.28 percent (last week),” said Joel Kan, CMB, MBA’s vice president and deputy chief economist. “While mortgage rates increased slightly on a weekly average basis, rates declined over the course of the week, which likely spurred purchase application activity.”

“Purchase applications reached their strongest weekly pace since September but remained well behind last year’s pace,” he added.

The latest Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey® released Wednesday ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday, also shows the 30-year FRM is still up year-over-year more than a full percentage point from 6.23% last year. The 15-year FRM averaged 6.66%, up from last week’s 6.45%. A year ago at this time, the 15-year FRM averaged 5.51%.

“Mortgage rates currently are about a percentage point higher than a year ago, while home prices are also still rising in many local markets,” said Bright MLS Chief Economist Lisa Sturtevant. Affordability is the biggest constraint on housing market activity, and until affordability eases, the pace of home sales transactions is going to be far below average. Existing home sales are on pace to come in below 4 million for 2026, which will be the first time sales have tracked below that level since the mid-1990s.

“Inflation continues to exceed the Fed’s target raising expectations for a December Fed rate hike, placing a floor under interest rates, including mortgage rates,” said Realtor.com Chief Economist Danielle Hale. “Despite this week’s decline, mortgage rates remain roughly a percentage point above where they were at this point in 2025, adding another headwind for buyers contending with high home prices.”

Kan pointed to a rise in adjustable rate mortgages (ARMs) as most notable this week.

“A larger share of borrowers relied on ARM loans for lower monthly payments, which accounted for 11.5 percent of applications, the highest share in more than a year,” he said. “At 6.39 percent, the ARM mortgage rate was nearly a full percentage lower than the 30-year fixed rate last week.”
