Replit Awarded 2026 Microsoft for Startups Partner of the Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Replit, the agentic software creation platform used by builders in 85% of the Fortune 500 companies, today announced it has been named the 2026 Microsoft for Startups Partner of the Year. The company was honored among a global field of top Microsoft partners for demonstrating excellence in innovation and implementation of customer solutions based on Microsoft technology.

"Replit's mission is to give everyone the ability to build software without learning to code, and we're honored to receive this recognition from Microsoft," said Jeff Burke, VP of Partnerships at Replit. "Our work together is making it easier for companies to build and deploy software using the cloud infrastructure and enterprise tools they already rely on. It's an important step toward our vision of the self-driving company where people set direction and AI agents handle more of the execution."

Replit's collaboration with Microsoft combines AI-powered software creation with Microsoft's cloud infrastructure, enterprise data and tools, enabling businesses to build applications while maintaining security, governance and control.

The Microsoft Partner of the Year Awards celebrate partners delivering exceptional customer outcomes through Microsoft Cloud, AI, services, and devices. This year's winners and finalists were selected from more than 4,500 nominations submitted worldwide across a diverse range of categories and geographies. Replit was recognized for providing outstanding solutions and services in Microsoft for Startups.

"Congratulations to the winners and finalists of the 2026 Microsoft Partner of the Year Awards," said Nicole Dezen, Chief Partner Officer and Corporate Vice President at Microsoft. "This year's honorees are harnessing the power of Microsoft Cloud and AI to deliver transformative solutions, accelerate customer success, and expand what's possible. Their ingenuity and impact inspire us, demonstrating what can be achieved when bold vision and technology come together to empower organizations around the world."

The 2026 Microsoft Partner of the Year Awards are announced ahead of Microsoft Ignite, which will be held in San Francisco from November 17-20.

About ReplitReplit is the agentic software creation platform that enables anyone to build applications without learning code. Founded in 2016, the company has more than 60 million users worldwide, including users at 85% of the Fortune 500. Teams at enterprises including Atlassian, Accenture, Adobe, and Databricks use Replit to build apps, and the company has partnerships with Stripe and Slack, among others. Replit is headquartered in San Francisco with offices in London and Tokyo.
