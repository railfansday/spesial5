Semperis updates Lightning identity security platform to defend against AI agent attacks

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Enterprise identity protection and cyber resilience company Semperis Ltd. today released the latest version of its Semperis Lightning identity security platform, which is designed to help organizations defend against identity attacks by artificial intelligence agents and conventional attackers alike.

Delivered through a single console, the new version of Lightning is said to combine exposure management, attack-path analysis and detection-and-response workflows. Along with Microsoft Corp.’s Active Directory and Entra ID, the new release supports Okta Inc.

Semperis says organizations using Lightning can adopt AI tools for productivity while reducing the risk of agents compromising critical resources. According to Chief Executive Mickey Bresman, identity systems have become “the main control plane” in the agentic age.

Though AI agents move at machine speed through the enterprise, Bresman said, they still depend on the identity fabric for access. With Lightning, organizations are said to be able to create, enforce and monitor what he called “agentic execution boundaries.”

Chief Product Officer Alex Weinert, who joined Semperis in April 2025 after serving as vice president of identity security at Microsoft, said security teams “do not need another flat list of identity findings.” Context on what can actually reach the identity control plane is what they need instead, he said. Agentic identities, he added, are opening new routes to privileged systems.

On the exposure side, Lightning, which now comes with a growing catalog of more than 220 security indicators, continuously scans identity infrastructure for exposures in human and nonhuman identities. The results are then scored, according to Semperis, before being triaged in a single workflow.

The platform’s live identity graph, which Semperis describes as a map of relationships and attack paths, covers routes to domain administrators, privileged accounts and other critical assets. According to the company, remediation guidance from the graph is prioritized by risk so that teams can respond faster.

Nonhuman and agentic identities are discovered automatically, Semperis says, and added to a searchable inventory of every identity in an environment. The company claims Lightning can scale to the largest multitenant, multiforest environments.

Built-in rule templates are designed to detect suspicious activity in Active Directory and Entra ID, such as unusual logons over Kerberos and NT LAN Manager. The company is also offering what it calls generative assistance alongside the templates.

Semperis introduced Lightning in April 2024 with Lightning Identity Runtime Protection, a machine learning-powered identity threat detection service. The company extended its resilience offerings to Okta and Ping Identity Corp. environments in February, when it acquired MightyID Inc.

More than 1,200 organizations, including over 25% of the 100 largest U.S. companies, are said to use Semperis. The company last raised $125 million in growth financing from JPMorgan Chase & Co. and Hercules Capital Inc. in June 2024. Earlier investors include Kohlberg Kravis Roberts & Co. LP, Insight Partners, Ten Eleven Ventures and Paladin Capital Group.
