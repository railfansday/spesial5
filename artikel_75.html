Gender pay gap: French women work "for free” from today

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Women in Europe are still paid less than men, with the gap falling from 15.1% in 2016 to 11.1% in 2024, according to Eurostat. In France, women symbolically start working for free from 10:51 am on Thursday, 19 November, and at the current pace equality would not be reached until 2062. Experts warn that AI could make things worse by eliminating administrative and legal jobs where many women work, while childbirth and short paternity leave continue to slow women’s careers.
