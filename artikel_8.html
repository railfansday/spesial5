Middle East crisis poses 'largest threat' to energy security in history, warns IEA

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The International Energy Agency said on Thursday that the Middle East crisis triggered by US and Israeli strikes on Iran in February posed the “largest threat” to energy security in history. It warned that consumers were bearing more of the costs as Iran’s blockade of the Strait of Hormuz disrupted oil shipments.
