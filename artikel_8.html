What's next after no deal at COP31 climate talks?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The COP31 climate summit in Turkey has officially come to an end after countries failed to come to a consensus on a new deal.

The European Union and some other countries rejected a fourth draft agreement over its failure to mention a need to phase out fossil fuels, the largest driver of global climate change.

An EU representative accuses some oil states of hijacking talks, as Russia defends opposing the inclusion of such a commitment and levels direct criticism at the EU in its speech.

The talks have been the longest-running in the summit's history, with negotiations breaking down two days after they had been due to end.

BBC climate and science reporter Esme Stallard reports from Antalya, in Turkey.
