Senegal reinstated as 2025 Africa Cup of Nations winners

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Senegal ​were reinstated as 2025 Africa Cup of Nations winners ​on Tuesday after the Court of Arbitration for Sport upheld their appeal against being stripped of the title. Senegal contested the ​Confederation ‌of African Football's decision to declare ⁠Morocco champions, despite Senegal beating the hosts 1-0 after extra time in ‌January's final in Rabat. Morocco announced that it would “pursue all necessary legal procedures” in response to the decision.
