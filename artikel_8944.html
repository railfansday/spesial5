hoor al qasimi appointed curator of the venice art biennale 2028

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

the sharjah art foundation founder will lead the 62nd international art exhibition, bringing her experience in cross-cultural exchange and international exhibition-making to venice

Hoor Al Qasimi has been appointed curator of the 62nd Venice Art Biennale, taking on the role of Director of the Visual Arts Department at La Biennale di Venezia ahead of the 2028 edition. The appointment places the Emirati curator in charge of the international exhibition, which opens to the public on April 8 and runs through November 26, 2028. Al Qasimi brings to Venice nearly two decades of experience directing the Sharjah Art Foundation, an institution she established in 2009 to support contemporary art and cultural exchange in the United Arab Emirates and internationally.

Her appointment comes with a curatorial practice shaped by an interest in different cultural geographies, historical narratives, and artistic perspectives. Since founding the Sharjah Art Foundation, Al Qasimi has developed an extensive program of exhibitions, commissions, and public initiatives, positioning Sharjah as an important meeting point for contemporary art. Among her recent projects is Sharjah Biennial 15 (2023), alongside the international touring retrospective of Hassan Sharif (2017–2018). She has also curated exhibitions dedicated to artists including Jorge Tacla, Leda Catunda, Bouchra Khalili, William Kentridge, Monir Shahroudy Farmanfarmaian, Rasheed Araeen, Simone Fattal, and Yayoi Kusama.

Beyond Sharjah, Al Qasimi has held leading roles in several international art events. She curated the second Lahore Biennale in 2020 and served as artistic director of the sixth Aichi Triennale, A Time Between Ashes and Roses, in 2025. In 2026, she took on the artistic director role for the 25th Biennale of Sydney, titled Rememory. Her institutional responsibilities also extend across art, architecture, education, and cultural research. She serves as president of the International Biennial Association, the Sharjah Museums Authority, and the Africa Institute in Sharjah, among other positions. She is also the director of the Sharjah Architecture Triennial.

In announcing the appointment, La Biennale president Pietrangelo Buttafuoco highlighted Al Qasimi’s attention to cultural memory, the complexities of the present, and the relationships between places and their communities. He described her curatorial approach as one that brings different geographies and perspectives into dialogue, qualities he considers closely aligned with the institution’s international vocation. Al Qasimi, in turn, described the Venice exhibition as an important platform for artists and curators around the world. Recalling her first visit to the Biennale in 2003, shortly after beginning her curatorial work at the Sharjah Biennial, she said she was honored to take on the responsibility and looked forward to collaborating with the institution’s team.

The appointment establishes the curatorial direction for the 2028 edition, although its exhibition title, central theme, and participating artists have yet to be announced. Al Qasimi’s previous work offers some indication of the breadth of perspectives she may bring to Venice, particularly her sustained engagement with artistic practices and cultural histories that extend beyond established Western narratives.
