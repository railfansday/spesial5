Crack the case in the Hit Avery Pawn mission.

Chapter 2 of GTA 6 finally gives you full control over both Jason and Lucia, and one of the first missions you'll undertake as a criminal couple involves committing a robbery in one of three locations.

One of your options is Avery Pawn Shop, where you'll be able to make a tidy sum of money if you can smash-and-grab enough loot and, more crucially, find the safe code to crack it open for additional cash and jewellery.The easiest way to do that is to grab the code for the safe, which is thankfully pretty easy to do.

Head inside Avery Pawn with your partner in tow—don't forget to pop on a mask, along with a hat and sunglasses to hide your identity—and begin holding up the store by pulling out your gun with L2 and pointing it at the clerks.

You should be able to threaten one of the staff members with Square and, with the other, the same input should instead change to "Safe Code?" if you look at the bottom-right corner. For me, it was the one standing behind the counter on the left.

The clerk should give up the safe code with little resistance. Once you have it, head behind the counter and into the back room. There'll be another staff member there, but you can point your gun and threaten him as well. Head into the office to the right, input the safe code, and grab your goods.

It looks like, at least where pawn shops are concerned, the code given is random. In my case, the code was 5683, but yours will most likely be different. This method should work for every pawn shop though, not just Avery Pawn!
