Phasmophobia ‘Resonance’ update launches November 24

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Kinetic Games will release the “Resonance” update for Phasmophobia on November 14, which introduces new investigation equipment, technical upgrades, and several gameplay improvements.

“We want to ensure every item in our Ghost Hunters’ kits can add as much value as possible in investigations, and the ‘Resonance’ update has been an exciting opportunity not only to freshen up Sound Sensors and Voice Recognition, but to add a completely new piece of equipment with the Dowsing Rods,” said Kinetic Games CEO and game director Daniel Knight in a press release. “We’re looking forward to hearing how the community get on with them. ‘Resonance’ also brings Phasmophobia‘s move to Unity 6—while this transition is a technical upgrade first and foremost, it has allowed us to implement major lighting improvements to make the game even more immersive. It’s been a huge undertaking for our whole team, and we hope our players enjoy everything ‘Resonance’ has to offer.”

Here is a brief overview of the update, via Kinetic Games:

The “Resonance” update also marks Phasmophobia‘s transition to the Unity 6 engine, bringing technical improvements in the form of more realistic lighting to enhance immersion, and improved stability. Additional settings are being introduced to the game too, with expanded graphics and quality options, VR Eye Adaptation, and a suite of colour-blindness accessibility settings.

Addressing recent community feedback, “Resonance” will also be bringing numerous fixes for several prominent bugs. The update will also introduce a new player conduct warning system that will warn players before receiving an account ban, except in cases of severe violations. Full details are available in the blog notes here.

Phasmophobia is currently in Early Access for PlayStation 5 with PlayStation VR2 support, Xbox Series, and PC via Steam, and is coming to Switch 2. The game has surpassed five million copies sold on consoles and 28 million copies sold overall.
