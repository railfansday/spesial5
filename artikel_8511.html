You probably are looking at the bond search results screen. That shows the minimum quantity for the best quote. If you click on the depth of book (DOB) link for one of the TIPS shown in the search results, you almost always will see quotes for smaller minimum quantities when the market is open.

lso, if you just click Trade, you'll see at least a partial DOB in the Trade screen. If you click Trade in the search results screen, then enter a quantity in the Trade screen, the price will auto-adjust to the price for that quantity, as long as there's an ask quote with a suitable min qty, which is what I usually do.
