Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle announced for iOS, Android

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Square Enix has announced Dragon Quest RPG: The Cursed Tree and the Chosen Isle, “the next adventure in the Dragon Quest series” for iOS and Android.

Further information about the game, including the announcement trailer, will premiere on November 10 at 3:00 a.m. PT / 6:00 a.m. ET / 20:00 JST.
