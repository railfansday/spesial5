James Talarico’s Political-Powerhouse Girlfriend, Brianna Menard, Is Also a Master Thrifter

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The 2026 midterm elections brought a blue wave of record-breaking wins. Among the most monumental was James Talarico, who became the first Democrat to win a Senate seat in Texas since 1988.

But his historic win is not all the internet has been talking about. At his celebratory Election Night event in Austin, Texas, the 37-year-old senator-elect was joined by his girlfriend, Brianna Menard, who was previously his chief of staff. Days later, the two attended a University of Texas football game—to which Menard wore a corduroy minidress she thrifted the week before—and the moment went viral across social media. (Even better, Talarico’s mom was the one who shared the photo of the couple at the game that quickly got traction on X.)

Chalk it up to her political prowess, clear sense of style, or the fact that she looks like an Instagram baddie, but some fans have even gone as far as to declare the pair this generation’s John F. Kennedy and Jacqueline Kennedy.

The couple first met in 2022, when Menard joined the politician’s team as chief of staff, and their relationship eventually turned romantic. Menard has been by Talarico’s side throughout his Senate campaign, accompanying him to the polls on Election Day in November and later joining him onstage to celebrate his historic victory.

So, who is Brianna Menard? Ahead, everything to know about Talarico’s accomplished and stylish girlfriend.

Menard is a born-and-raised Texan. She attended and graduated from the University of Texas at Austin, where she studied government.

In 2022, Menard was hired as Talarico’s chief of staff. According to reports, the two didn’t begin dating until a year later, after which Menard left her role working for the politician. In August 2026, Talarico’s campaign confirmed the couple’s relationship. “Four years ago, after he and his then-chief of staff developed feelings for each other and started dating, she left the office to pursue other opportunities,” a Talarico campaign spokesman told the New York Times.

After leaving Talarico’s office, Menard worked on the campaign for Rep. Salman Bhojani, the first Muslim member of the Texas legislature, and eventually became his chief of staff.

Menard is currently the director of TEXPAC and political education at the Texas Medical Association. According to her bio on the organization’s site, before stepping into the role, she had a decade of experience working in the Texas Legislature and previously served as executive director of the Early Childhood Caucus of the Texas House, where she led bipartisan efforts to “advance early learning, childcare access, workforce development, and healthcare affordability.”

Menard’s bio also lists her as the Board Secretary for Wheatsville Food Co-op and a member of the City of Austin’s Early Childhood Council.

According to a now-deleted bio on the Wheatsville Food Co-Op site, where she currently serves as Board Secretary, Menard called herself a “foodie and a committed vegan” who loves cooking. In the welcome post, she also revealed her love of yoga, dancing, and cuddling her cat named Zia.

“I’ve spent my career serving in the Texas Legislature and working on political campaigns across the state with the goal of making Texas a healthier and more equitable home for all of us,” she wrote in the post.

Menard joined Talarico at the polls to vote early in the midterm elections. On November 3, she also joined Talarico’s Election Night event in Austin, where she congratulated the Senator-elect on stage following his historic win. The couple shared a sweet hug and kiss to celebrate.
