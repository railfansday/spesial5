Husband of ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Contestant Was Allegedly a “Nightmare” on Set, Tried to Say “Certain Dance Moves” Were Off-Limits

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

“He said there were certain costuming things she couldn’t wear.”

Jen Affleck and Zac Affleck are getting divorced after seven years of marriage, and now a Dancing With the Stars source is out here spilling on what their vibe was like when Jen was on the show last season. To put it bluntly: not great!

Jen was partnered with Jan Ravnik during season 34, and an insider tells The Sun that “Zac was a nightmare on set. He didn’t allow Jan to fully do his job and made things very uncomfortable.”

Meanwhile, “Jan was a total professional and dealt with it but Zac was much more controlling than any other partner that season. Even crew members felt they had to tip-toe around him because he was constantly upset over something.”

If you want specifics on what Zac was upset about, apparently it came down to Jen’s dance and wardrobe choices. “He asserted his dominance and tried to dictate certain dance moves that were off limits for Jen, and he said there were certain costuming things she couldn’t wear,” the source explains. “Poor Jan was trying to do his job well as a first-time pro and he had a very stressful experience, largely because of Zac.”

Reminder: Jen wasn’t the only cast member from The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives on Dancing With the Stars last season. Whitney Leavitt was also on the show, and the insider says interacting with her husband, Connor, was a drama-free experience: “It was the complete opposite experience with Whitney and Conner—who couldn’t have been a more supportive husband.”

Zac hasn’t commented on any of this, but he and Jen have been going back and forth about their relationship since she filed for divorce, so more on that whole thing below.
