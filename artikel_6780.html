Which Couples Broke Up (and Who Got Married) on ‘Love Is Blind’ Season 11?

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

A brand-new batch of aspiring reality-star singles gathered in Boston for the annual social experiment known as Love Is Blind, and the final installment of season 11 is officially out on Netflix. This week’s finale saw multiple couples break up and just one pair make it down the aisle—here’s where things stand going into the reunion!

Despite managing to work through some of their communication issues, Aneha’s parents refused to attend her wedding to Cam—and her sister made it abundantly clear she wasn’t on board either. Ultimately, Aneha turned down Cam at the altar, but also seemed to want to stay together? Meanwhile, Cam was less sure about going forward in the relationship, so guess we’ll see where they stand at the reunion! (Spoilers this way if you can’t wait.)

Currently: still split...but kinda vibing?

Jax might have ghosted Stew immediately after their engagement, but they reunited with the cast at a bar and spent the entire time joking around with each other at a table and watching the mess unfold. I swear, they had chemistry. Weird chemistry, but still chemistry! Though, for what it’s worth, Jax insists nothing is going on.

Wilnie and Wilson ended things after their bachelor/bachelorette parties! During a tense couch convo, Wilson called out Wilnie for not...cooking enough (?), and Wilnie pointed out Wilson’s lack of affection. Ultimately, the pair were on the same page about not being right for each other, but they didn’t exactly end on good terms.

Sure, they had some tough political conversations, but Sarah and John said “I do” in front of their friends and families! Just remember that this show was filmed over a year ago, and things can change. Spoilers this way.

Woof. Michaella broke up with Rob after he went off during a group bar hang, telling everyone he didn’t care about their relationship, pointing out they’d only known each other for a few weeks, and acting generally messy. Michaella told Erika that Rob had left her at home that night, and while he did call to make amends, she ended the relationship.

Much like Wilnie and Wilson, Erika and Blake had a pre-wedding couch convo and decided to part ways. The pair ended things very amicably, and seem to still genuinely love each other. They just couldn’t get past their different lifestyles thanks to Blake living much of the year on a boat. Understandable!
