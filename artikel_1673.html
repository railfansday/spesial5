Man City 1-1 Fulham: Highlights, man of the match, stats as Anderson, Muniz score in stalemate

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

By Oliver Thomas | 21 Nov 2026 14:35, Last updated: 21 Nov 2026 18:39

Manchester City’s winless run in the Premier League has been extended to four games after they were held to a frustrating 1-1 draw by Fulham at the Etihad Stadium on Saturday afternoon.

Enzo Maresca’s “main target” was to secure all three points against the Cottagers following a dismal run of three consecutive losses to Aston Villa, Brighton, and Nottingham Forest prior to the international break.

However, the Citizens fell behind inside the opening 16 minutes when Fulham striker Rodrigo Muniz – starting his first PL game of the season at the expense of Gonzalo Garcia – curled a sweet strike into the top corner following a sweeping counter-attack.

Erling Haaland thought he had equaliser less than 10 minutes later, but he was controversially deemed to have fouled goalkeeper Bernd Leno before prodding the ball over the line.

Elliot Anderson did restore parity for the hosts, though, with a superbly-struck volley just before the hour mark, but in a second half that saw both teams create decent openings, the visitors admirably held on to claim a share of the spoils, ending an English record 20-game losing streak against the Sky Blues.

The draw represents Man City’s first in the top flight this season and leaves them sitting third in the Premier League table, five points behind leaders Arsenal – who face Newcastle United later tonight – and just behind second-placed Brighton on goal difference, with the Seagulls to take on Hull City on Sunday.

As for Fulham, a third successive draw has seen them extend their unbeaten run in the division to five matches, with Alvaro Arbeloa’s men sitting 14th in the top-flight standings and three points above the relegation zone.

Oliver Thomas live from the Etihad Stadium

After winning his first seven Premier League games in charge of Man City, a feat no other manager has ever achieved in the division, Enzo Maresca has since seen his team stumble and hand Arsenal a significant title boost even before entering the month of December.

One of the biggest concerns for Maresca is how frail his defence has looked for the majority of this season, with 18 goals conceded across their last nine matches in all competitions, while City have failed to keep a single clean sheet in each of their last seven matches at the Etihad.

Maresca has acknowledged that finding the right balance is key for his new-look Citizens side and it’s ‘normal’ for teams in transition to have moments of struggle, but concerns over his defence, team structure and the performances of individuals are growing heading into a hectic fixture period.

Regardless of the outcome regarding their well-documented guilty verdict appeal, Man City would face an uphill battle to stay in the Premier League title race if they lose at Arsenal next weekend, with a fourth league defeat in five games leaving them trailing the Gunners by a daunting margin before the festive schedule even begins.

As for Fulham, Arbeloa has now steadied the ship following a challenging start to life in Premier League management, with nine of their 11 points accumulated across the last five gameweeks.

The Cottagers are by no means out of trouble just yet, but this positive run of form, extended by a hard-earned point at Man City, has given the club’s supporters genuine hope that a protracted relegation battle can be avoided.
