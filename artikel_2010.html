West Ham striker has history on his side ahead of Preston North End clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

West Ham’s rejuvenated striker Joel Piroe, fresh from a successful international break with Suriname, could hardly have picked a better opponent to face as West Ham prepare to resume their Championship campaign against Preston North End tomorrow and the Hammers’ on-loan striker seeks a return to front line duties under new boss Scott Parker.

Piroe hasn’t featured regularly under Nuno and he will be hoping for a change of fortunes under the Hammers’ new head coach: The Dutch-born Suriname striker has something of a happy history against Saturday’s visitors, having scored four goals in eight previous appearances against the Lilywhites.

Proving he’s bang in form, Piroe’s two goals for Suriname last week will surely give Scott Parker the nudge needed to promoting him ahead of Taty Castellanos into the starting eleven.

The former Leeds striker has demonstrated his ability to find the net in the Championship, unlike West Ham’s often-moody Argentine forward who has but one goal to his name this season in what can only be seen as a massive disappointment.

For a side desperate to build some momentum, a striker returning from international duty with two goals under his belt and a proven record against tomorrow’s opponents can’t be overlooked.

Opportunity knocks – if Piroe can extend his Preston scoring record tomorrow he could cement a place in the starting eleven for the rest of the year.
