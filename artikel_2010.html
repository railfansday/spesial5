Sources: Ismaila Sarr 'keeping options open' as Crystal Palace delay explained

Crystal Palace's contract talks with winger Ismaila Sarr have been delayed by their change of manager, sources have told Football Insider.

The Eagles spent several weeks deliberating whether to sack Pierre Sage, and that has seen the move to tie Sarr down to a new deal put on the back burner.

Talks with the Senegal international began at the end of October, but it is understood that limited progress has been made since then.

Sources say that Sarr is also "keeping his options open" for as long as possible ahead of the upcoming January window.

The contract talks are not a "top priority" for the wideman and his camp right now, after he was denied a move away from Selhurst Park in the summer.

He was heavily linked with moves to both Liverpool and Galatasaray, but was ultimately forced to stay put in South London.

Palace were hugely reluctant to lose Sarr in the summer, and their stance has only hardened further since then.

With the capital club now facing a potential relegation scrap, they have become even more determined that their star man won't be allowed out of the door.

Palace know that they cannot lose Sarr in the middle of the campaign, as it would provide a huge setback in their push for survival.

Injuries have limited him to just 15 minutes of Premier League football so far this season, but he is still expected to be a key player when he returns.

Sarr's stock rose dramatically after he hit 21 goals in all competitions last term, and it won't be easy for Palace to continue to fend off interest.

However, that remains their intention for now, even if the talks over a new deal have taken a back seat of late.

Football Insider revealed on Sunday that the delay behind Palace's expected appointment of Thomas Frank centres around his desired backroom team.

The Eagles are still working on a deal to appoint the Dane as their new boss, but are trying to ensure that he can bring his staff with him.

Sources say that is the reason behind the delay over the appointment, despite a deal reportedly being close over the weekend.

Frank has been out of work since he was sacked by Tottenham back in February, but will be expected to produce the goods immediately at Selhurst.
