Satanic Temple can host holiday celebration at state Capitol building, judge rules

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Satanic Temple of Iowa coloring pages. The temple will be allowed to host a family-friendly holiday celebration in the Iowa Capitol building, a federal judge has ruled. (Photo courtesy of Magick Moth Media via the American Civil Liberties Union)

Iowa state officials must allow the Satanic Temple to host a family-friendly holiday celebration in the Iowa Capitol building without conditions or restrictions, a federal judge has ruled.

Chief U.S. District Judge Stephanie Rose of the Southern District of Iowa granted a preliminary injunction Monday ordering the state to approve the Satanic Temple’s application, which has been denied for the past three years despite other religious organizations being approved, Courthouse News Service reports.

The application was denied this year because a state official said the event’s costume contest—featuring a figure from European folklore known as Krampus who carries a stick—would harm children.

Rose reasoned that whether a costume may frighten a child is not a permissible basis for excluding speech, and the entire event cannot be denied simply because someone might carry a stick.

The American Civil Liberties Union and the temple filed the lawsuit in August.

“Congregants of the Satanic Temple have every right to live their authentic religious identity in public and are entitled to the same dignity and the same access to public spaces as members of more familiar traditions,” said temple attorney Matt Kezhaya in a Nov. 2 press release.
