Dropbox: Application Security Engineer

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

As part of the Product Security Engineering team, you’ll focus on reducing risk at scale by building the security infrastructure, automation, and tooling that empowers engineers to ship secure products with confidence. We work closely with engineering and product teams throughout the software development lifecycle (SDLC), embedding secure-by-default practices and delivering scalable solutions.

Product Security Engineers create impact by implementing security tooling, writing custom security rules, and building frameworks that address broad classes of vulnerabilities. In addition to proactive development, we support teams through threat modeling, documentation, and education to uplift security culture across Dropbox.

Our Engineering Career Framework is viewable by anyone outside the company and describes what’s expected for our engineers at each of our career levels. Check out our blog post on this topic and more here.

Many teams at Dropbox run Services with on-call rotations, which entails being available for calls during both core and non-core business hours. If a team has an on-call rotation, all engineers on the team are expected to participate in the rotation as part of their employment. Applicants are encouraged to ask for more details of the rotations to which the applicant is applying.
