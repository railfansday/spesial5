Liverpool have their new Thiago, it's official.

There was a time when we all just wanted to see him on the pitch. Thiago in his prime was the kind of player who turned football into art.

It's just a shame his career at Liverpool was so hampered by injuries. Because when he was on the pitch and pulling the strings, there really wasn't anyone quite like him.

Thiago now sets the ultimate example of what a Liverpool midfielder should be. The bar is extremely high and it's very difficult to live up to it.
