From Gangs to Grizzlies

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

How a former gang member found a different kind of vigilance in the Montana wild.

One summer evening, I was hiking a trail near Marias Pass in Glacier National Park, returning to the car after a few hours of wildlife photography at a lake— nothing but deer and mosquitoes to show for it—when I noticed something blondish-brown moving toward me. It barely registered. I felt relaxed, though, truthfully, I’m never fully relaxed.

A grizzly cub, big as an overgrown Saint Bernard. I was fascinated—until reality hit. The mother wouldn’t be far behind.

“Hey, bear,” I shouted, my voice cracking. The cub woofed an alarm and took off to my left, a dark blur swallowed by the forest.

I turned toward the trail ahead. And—boom—there she was. Running at me, big and fast and flat to the ground.
