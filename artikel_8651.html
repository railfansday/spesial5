Creatine Is Moving Beyond the Weight Room, Especially Among Adults 55 and Older

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

MyFitnessPal logging data shows the sharpest year over year rise in creatine use among its oldest users. The supplement has potential benefits for strength when paired with resistance training, but it is not a shortcut to healthy aging.
