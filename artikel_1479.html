Gasperini eyes Ruben van Bommel as Roma’s top target

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Roma is looking to the Netherlands to find the attacking winger requested by Gian Piero Gasperini.

At the top of the list of possible reinforcements is Ruben van Bommel, the 22-year-old striker for PSV Eindhoven and the Dutch national team. Journalist Piero Torri revealed the news this morning on Manà Manà Sport Roma.

The son of a talented player, he’s one of the players the Giallorossi are most confidently targeting to strengthen their attack.

Van Bommel perfectly fits the profile of the winger Gasperini is seeking: he plays primarily on the left flank, is right-footed, has an imposing build, stands nearly 1.90 meters tall, and combines speed, power, and the ability to beat his opponent. He loves to start wide and then cut inside to shoot, but he’s also capable of attacking deep.

He’s a player with particularly interesting characteristics for the Roma coach’s aggressive, vertical style of football.

Van Bommel is being supported by Team Raiola and has a contract with PSV until June 2030. The Dutch club invested around €16 million to acquire him from AZ and would be unlikely to let him leave on particularly favorable terms. This is an aspect that Roma will eventually have to evaluate, along with the deal’s formula and the financial margins for January’s move.
