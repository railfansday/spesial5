Liverpool fan found not guilty of racially abusing Antoine Semenyo as Man City star withdraws support for prosecution

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Published: 05:49 EST, 20 November 2026 | Updated: 08:12 EST, 20 November 2026

A Liverpool fan accused of racially abusing footballer Antoine Semenyo has been found not guilty after the player withdrew his support for the prosecution.

Semenyo was due to give evidence at Liverpool Magistrates' Court on Friday in the trial of Mark Mogan, 47, who was accused of shouting abuse during the opening match of the Premier League season at Anfield on August 15 last year.

But Simon Jones, prosecuting, said: 'Following recent contact made with Mr Semenyo, he has indicated he is no longer supportive of proceedings and the prosecution do not consider it is appropriate to compel him.

'In those circumstances we offer no further evidence in the case.' District Judge Mary Dowrick recorded a verdict of not guilty to the charge of racially aggravated behaviour.

Semenyo, who was a Bournemouth player at the time of the incident but later signed for Manchester City, had been due to give evidence last month but the trial was adjourned halfway through when Mr Mogan was taken to hospital after suffering a seizure.

The court had heard Mr Mogan, in a wheelchair, shouted at Semenyo as he went to take a throw-in about 30 minutes into the game.

Mark Mogan (right) has been found not guilty of racially abusing Antoine Semenyo (left) after the player withdrew his support for the prosecution

He was alleged to have said: 'Hurry up and take the throw-in, you black b*****d.'

Opening the case last month, Mr Jones said: 'That racial abuse was shouted directly at Antoine Semenyo, who was close to him.

'Mr Semenyo briefly froze, as he couldn't believe what the male had said.' Mr Mogan then shouted "I'm talking to you, you ****", the court heard.

Witnesses also said they saw him throw his chewing gum at the footballer.

Semenyo reported the incident to the match referee and Mr Mogan was removed from the stadium at half-time.

Mr Mogan, who had an intermediary with him in court, told police in interview that Semenyo had called him a cripple after he shouted at the player to 'hurry up'.

Mr Mogan, of Templehill Close in Dovecot, Liverpool, declined to comment as he left court.

Semenyo, who scored a brace in the game, had brought the incident to the attention of match referee Anthony Taylor and the match was briefly halted while both managers and captains were spoken to.

At half time an announcement was made to the crowd asking for no racist abuse and pictures on social media showed a man in a wheelchair being escorted along the touchline and out of the ground.

Mogan, pictured with Liverpool legend Robbie Fowler, would have been banned for life if he'd have been found guilty

Merseyside Police later announced the man had been arrested and then was bailed for three months pending further investigations.

Pictures on social media showed Mogan playing wheelchair basketball for a team called the Greenbank Meteors while other images show him posing with Liverpool legends Robbie Fowler and Emile Heskey.

After he was charged, Liverpool confirmed he would be banned for life if found guilty but Judge Dowrick recorded a verdict of not guilty on Friday morning.
