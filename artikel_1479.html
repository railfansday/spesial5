OL: AC Milan is pushing for the return of Zachary Athekame!

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

On October 30, Gerry Cardinale, the boss of AC Milan, was very clear in the columns of the Gazzetta dello Sport on the Rossoneri’s strategy for the next winter transfer window. “In January, in the transfer market, we will continue to be aggressive, opportunistic, and I will continue to strengthen our bench. My style is to act with determination and speed. For me, what matters is getting what we want, players worthy of Milan. I won’t waste money, but when I set out to sign a player, I do everything I can to make it happen. »

This Thursday, the pink paper newspaper confirms that the Lombard club intends to enlist at least two, even three recruits in January. Targeted recruitment in defense, since an axial and a lateral are hoped for. In the central hinge, Matteo Gabbia and Fikayo Tomori are unavailable for several weeks. To compensate for their absence, Milan would think of Gonçalo Inácio (Sporting CP) as well as Massiré Sylla (Union Saint-Gilloise). Two elements estimated respectively at €35 million and €5 million by the specialized site Transfermarkt.

And that’s not all: Milan also want to attract a full-back. Last month, this rumor caused a stir in the Rhône, since the transalpine press assured that the Rossoneri wanted to break the loan of Zachary Athekame (21), currently sold to Olympique Lyonnais (2 goals, 3 assists in 10 L1 matches) until the end of the season. Information which had been qualified as “fake news” by the man who is no longer the coach of Les Gones today, Paulo Fonseca. Nothing to dampen the Milanese enthusiasm.

The daily ensures that the idea of ​​repatriating the Swiss international (4 selections) is indeed current and that “if the “Diavolo” (nickname of AC Milan, editor’s note) put pressure on the French, an agreement on the immediate return of Athekame could still be found, although questions of form and relations are also at stake”. Will Milan try to get to the end of this matter, even if it means clashing with OL? Among Gones observers, it seems unthinkable to see Athekame packing up after only six months spent in the capital of Gaul. But Fonseca’s sudden departure to Sporting CP before mid-season once again proved that the football world can question any certainty.
