Roma to pursue Kvernadze in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Roma continues to monitor Giorgi Kvernadze, the Georgian striker currently playing for Frosinone.

The player was reportedly in the Roman club’s sights last summer and will continue to be monitored ahead of the upcoming transfer window.

This interest could become more concrete in the coming months, especially given the player’s financial terms.

The 2003-born striker is tied to Frosinone by a deal valid until June 30, 2028, but his contract reportedly contains a specific release clause.

The fee required to release the striker is reportedly €4 million, with the option to exercise the option next summer.

This valuation could make the Georgian an attractive prospect for Roma, who are keen to monitor various prospects to strengthen their attacking line.

However, the potential deal would present another financial aspect to consider.

As reported by Gazzetta Regionale, in addition to the €4 million stipulated in the release clause, a significant percentage would need to be considered for the player’s agent.

This detail could impact the overall cost of the deal and the Roma management’s assessment.

Kvernadze’s agent is the same one representing Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, another Georgian talent who has established himself in our Serie A. At the moment, Roma’s interest remains to be monitored, and no official negotiations have been initiated to bring Kvernadze to the capital.
