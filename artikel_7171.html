Instacart: Business Intelligence Manager II

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

At Instacart, we invite the world to share love through food because we believe everyone should have access to the food they love and more time to enjoy it together. Where others see a simple need for grocery delivery, we see exciting complexity and endless opportunity to serve the varied needs of our community. We work to deliver an essential service that customers rely on to get their groceries and household goods, while also offering safe and flexible earnings opportunities to Instacart Personal Shoppers.

Instacart has become a lifeline for millions of people, and we’re building the team to help push our shopping cart forward. If you’re ready to do the best work of your life, come join our table.

There’s no one-size fits all approach to how we do our best work. Our employees have the flexibility to choose where they do their best work—whether it’s from home, an office, or your favorite coffee shop—while staying connected and building community through regular in-person events. Learn more about our flexible approach to where we work.

Instacart's Commercial Analytics team sits at the intersection of the industry's richest grocery data, our most strategic retail partnerships, and the CPG category intelligence that powers growth across the platform. As our retailer relationships scale and the complexity of our business deepens, we need someone who can support the data and analytics layer for the entire Commercial organization. We're hiring a Business Intelligence Manager II to build the data and AI foundation the Commercial org runs on: the models, semantic layer, dashboards, and AI tools that let analysts go deeper and let partner managers get trusted answers. This individual contributor role is the first hire in a new Data & AI function for Commercial Partnerships, with room to grow as it scales. The role will partner closely with our Commercial Insights and Partnerships teams, and the work will shape how Instacart grows its most important partnerships.

You sit between data and the business. You're as comfortable modeling data as you are sitting with an account manager to understand what they actually need, and you measure success by how many people can answer their own questions because of what you built.
