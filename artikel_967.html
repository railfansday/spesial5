Szoboszlai fitness call to come at 'last minute'

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Dominik Szoboszlai has made eight Premier League appearances for Liverpool this season

Hungary say they will do "everything possible" for Liverpool midfielder Dominik Szoboszlai to feature in Nations League fixtures against Georgia and Northern Ireland over the next eight days.

The 26-year-old was replaced after 69 minutes of his club's 3-1 Premier League victory over Crystal Palace on Sunday and was described by Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola as "not 100%" because of a knee problem.

On Tuesday, the Hungarian Football Federation said in a statement on X that their "national team's medical staff will do everything possible over the coming days to ensure that the team captain is available to the coaching staff as soon as possible", adding that any decision will "almost certainly only be made at the last minute".

Hungary are second in Nations League Group B2, one point behind leaders Northern Ireland who they play in the concluding fixture of the campaign in Budapest on 17 November.

Before that potential decider, Marco Rossi's side travel to Georgia on Saturday.

Speaking after the win over Palace that lifted the Reds up to fourth in the Premier League table, Iraola said he appreciates the player will do all he can to be available for his country.

"I know he will do everything to go because he loves to play with the national team, but he was not 100% when he finished the game.

"I suppose they will have to analyse, the medical staff, and see what it is.

"I know what I want, but I also have to understand the national teams. I'm sure that Dom, if he is fine he will want to go, but I'm not sure if he's going to be fine."

Szoboszlai, along with Alexander Isak and Cody Gakpo, missed Liverpool's 3-0 defeat by Manchester City in October directly following a four-game international window with Iraola keen to avoid any further stress on his playing squad.

"The last thing we need now is to lose players in the international games," he said.
