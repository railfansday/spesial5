Las Vegas GP: UK schedule and how to watch on Sky Sports

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Find out the race-week schedule, weather and how to watch the Las Vegas Grand Prix at The Strip Circuit; watch every session live on Sky Sports F1, with qualifying at 4am on Saturday November 21 and the race at 4am on Sunday November 22

Formula 1 heads for the bright lights of Las Vegas with both 2026 titles settled, but the competitive picture at the front of the grid tighter than it has been at any other stage of the season.

The Las Vegas Strip Circuit opens the triple header of events that conclude the campaign with the race on the streets of Sin City quickly followed by a fortnight in the Middle East for the Qatar and Abu Dhabi Grands Prix.

Last time out at the Sao Paulo Grand Prix, Mercedes completed a title double by sealing the Constructors' Championship a week on from Kimi Antonelli becoming F1's youngest ever world champion at the Mexico City Grand Prix.

Despite closing out both championships, Mercedes are actually enduring their most difficult period of the season, with their rivals regularly outperforming them.

McLaren's Lando Norris claimed his third win of the season in Brazil, while Ferrari may well have claimed a one-two the weekend before in Mexico had Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc not clashed on the first lap.

Max Verstappen has featured on the last eight podiums, a run which has moved the Red Bull driver 17 points clear of Antonelli's team-mate George Russell in the battle for second in the standings, with Hamilton just a point further back.

The race for third in the constructors' standings is also very much on, with Red Bull just 17 points clear of McLaren. The duel for seventh is also tight with Haas only three points ahead of Audi.

That will ensure there is plenty to play for as F1 makes its fourth appearance at the stunning street circuit since Las Vegas returned to the sport's calendar in 2023.

With all track sessions running in the late afternoon or evening local time in Vegas, which is eight hours behind the UK and Ireland.

Sky Sports F1 viewers need to set their alarms - or, in the case of first and final practice, perhaps just stay up late - to watch the weekend's sessions live. Qualifying and the Grand Prix itself both start at 4am GMT.

Full F1 session re-runs will also be shown on Sky Sports F1 and Main Event each morning, while there will also be highlights available to watch on the Sky Sports website and app.

Considered F1's most ambitious project to bring racing to the heart of Las Vegas and the city's world-renowned Strip, the street track runs to 3.8 miles in length and features 17 corners.

The layout winds past some of Vegas' most famous landmarks, such as the Venetian, the Bellagio Fountains and the Eiffel Tower at Paris, and features a 1.2-mile flat-out blast down the Strip.

With an average speed of 150mph across Russell's dry-weather pole position lap in 2024 (2025's qualifying was wet), Vegas represents F1's second-fastest street track after the Jeddah Corniche Circuit in Saudi Arabia. It has also proved itself to be good for racing, with plenty of overtaking and action over the first three years.

With the race again taking place late in November, the 2026 edition is set to follow the pattern of the first three events in terms of weather conditions.

Day-time temperatures in the mid-high teens are set to be followed by chilly evenings as temperatures drop towards single figures in Nevada's Mojave Desert. No rain is currently forecast during track days.
