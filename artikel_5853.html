Biden book sales lag way behind Obama, Clinton memoirs in first week

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Fox News' Lara Trump discusses former President Joe Biden's new memoir as he defends his 2024 debate performance with president Donald Trump. Trump details how Democrats allowed for Biden's decline to go on behind the scenes.

Former President Joe Biden's new memoir, "Promise Me, America," sold only 21,000 copies in its first week of publication, falling well short of memoirs by former presidents Barack Obama and Bill Clinton, according to market research firm Circana.

In 2020, Obama's "A Promised Land" sold more than 800,000 copies within the first 24 hours of publication, reaching 1 million copies sold by the end of its first week.

Clinton's "My Life" hit the 1 million copies benchmark within two weeks of its release.

Despite its apparently low sales numbers, Biden's book still topped the New York Times Best Sellers list for nonfiction.

The new book, "Promise Me, America," by former U.S. President Joe Biden is displayed at Skylight Books Nov. 17, 2026 in Los Angeles. (Justin Sullivan/Getty Images)

The book's publisher, Little, Brown and Company, said in a statement it was "immensely proud" of the book reaching the top of the chart.

Biden's promotion of the book had been limited by his relatively scant appearances to promote it, an effort hampered by the former president's 2025 prostate cancer diagnosis.

"Some days are better than others. It hasn't stopped me. But sometimes it slows me down. What that means is, unfortunately, I won't be able to do as many conversations about my book as I'd hoped. However, I'm hopeful the words on the page speak for themselves," Biden wrote in a Nov. 8 post on X.

The book's diminished sales numbers compared to Biden's Democratic predecessors appear to follow a trend of the public's relative disinterest in the Bidens' bibliography.

President Joe Biden talks with son Hunter Biden upon arrival at Delaware Air National Guard Base in New Castle, Del., June 11, 2024, as he travels to Wilmington, Delaware.  (Andrew Caballero-Reynolds/AFP via Getty Images)

Former first lady Jill Biden's June memoir "View from the East Wing," also underperformed compared to the published works of other former first ladies.

Biden's book sold approximately 46,000 copies total, according to Circana. Those numbers are dwarfed by former Sen. Hillary Clinton's "What Happened" and Michelle Obama's "Becoming," each of which surpassed 1 million copies sold.

President Joe Biden, former President Barack Obama and former President Bill Clinton during a memorial service for Ethel Kennedy at the Cathedral of St. Matthew the Apostle in Washington, D.C., Oct. 16, 2024. (Jim Lo Scalzo/EPA/Bloomberg via Getty Images)

Former President Joe Biden's post-vice presidential memoir, "Promise Me, Dad," did relatively well compared to the Biden family's other offerings.

The book sold over 300,000 copies, according to Forbes.

Biden appeared at more than 30 public promotion stops while touting that book compared to none for his most recent release.
