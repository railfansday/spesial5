Everything Hilary Swank Said About Having Kids Amid Pregnancy With Baby No. 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Hilary Swank has opened up over the years about her motherhood journey.

In 2006, the actress noted that she was “so optimistic” when it came to her future as a parent. “I definitely would like kids someday. That’s definitely something that I’ve always thought about as a very, very young girl,” she told reporters while at a movie premiere. “I just love kids and that would be an important thing to happen for me someday whenever the time is right.”

The film producer found love with Philip Schneider after they were set up by mutual friends. Following two years of dating, the duo quietly tied the knot.

In October 2022, Swank shocked fans when she announced that she and her husband were expecting twins. “This is something that I’ve been wanting for a long time, and my next thing is I’m gonna be a mom,” she gushed during an interview with Good Morning America. “And not just of one, but of two. I can’t believe it.”

Swank and Schneider welcomed twins Ohm and Aya in April 2023. Three years later, the Freedom Writers actress revealed they’re expecting baby No. 3 in December 2026.

“For me, my career is like, for lack of a better word, a baby,” the Alaska Daily star told Marie Claire in 2009, noting that she was "thinking about the day" she would become a mom. “When the time is right, I’ll know [I am ready]. "I actually do want to talk about [my thoughts on motherhood]. I feel that I’m very maternal in the people that I care for and all the sentient beings I have around me — my dogs, my horses or just the human beings," the Nebraska native shared with Daily Mail in August 2020. "And I feel like so much of the time women who either choose not to or can’t have children are somehow seen as not maternal or not mothering and it is a conversation that needs to be had." Swank continued: "I’ve heard a lot of women say that they’ve been told, 'Oh, you’re kind of a failure.' Or, you know, 'Didn’t you come here to procreate?; There are so many different ways to procreate!"

Swank reflected on welcoming her first two children at 47 in an interview with Women’s Health.  “I would have had kids earlier. I’m not saying I actively waited until 47,” she said. “But that was a magical time in my life, when it all culminated."

After announcing her pregnancy with baby No. 3 at age 52, Swank clapped back at those who judged her for having babies later in life.  “There’s a lot of backlash women get for having children older but not men, which is interesting,” she said on The Drew Barrymore Show in October 2026. “There’s one story that I found really interesting, and I think I was attracted to this particular story because this one woman said, ‘How dare you. You’re too old, and you won’t be old enough when your kids are older, and my dad was my favorite person, and he died when I was in my 20s, my early 20s, and it’s just devastation.’” She continued, “I related to it because my dad has also passed, but he was one of my favorite people on Earth, and so I was drawn to that, and I thought to myself, ‘Well, I would rather be born and have that person that was my favorite person and experience that than not at all.’ Do you know what I’m saying? So that’s how I took what she said.”
