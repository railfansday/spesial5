GTA 6’s Mandy Actor Reacts To The Online “Weirdos”

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

One of the most talked-about characters in GTA 6 outside of Jason and Lucia has been Mandy, a woman who tries to seduce Jason early in the game. She may also be a serial killer. The actor who plays Mandy, Lexie Shoaibi, has now discussed how she got the part and her reaction to people being weird online.

Speaking to Vulture, Shoaibi said she's able to see "more good than bad" thanks to her experience being a content creator.

"There are weirdos out there, and they’re going to try to bring you down, so it’s important to stay around the people and the places that aren’t doing that," she said. "It’s easy enough to do, which I’m very grateful for. There’s enough support in my real-life community and online that I can say, 'I could just hang out over here. I don’t actually need to go to the scary place.' I’m living in the positive, for sure."

People have been, to put it mildly, thirsting over Mandy since GTA 6 came out. However, Shoaibi had no idea she was going to be in GTA 6 at all because when she auditioned, she signed what she described as an "incredibly" strict NDA. In fact, she didn't even know what she was auditioning for.

Her commercial agent in New York brought it to her, and she thought it would be "just like any other commercial audition." From the time she auditioned until the game launched on November 19, Shoaibi did not know she was going to be in GTA 6. Instead, she did the audition and recorded her scenes for a project she knew as "Project Bubblegum."

Shoaibi said the notes she received included being told to play a “sexy ex-girlfriend" and to "be seductive.” 

When Shoaibi finally saw Mandy in GTA 6, she said it was "very exciting," in part because it meant that Rockstar didn't cut it. She did the recording for Mandy in 2023 or early 2024, Shoaibi said, and now that GTA 6 is finally here and her role as Mandy confirmed, Shoaibi said, "I feel like I'm living in a dream. It's crazy."

Shoaibi said she hopes her role as Mandy in the biggest video game ever can help grow her profile and get more jobs. "There’s definitely momentum that I wasn’t feeling before," she said.

GTA 6 launched on November 19 and had a gigantic debut, selling 32.5 million copies by launch and generating around $4 billion on day one.
