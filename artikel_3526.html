J.K. Rowling’s 6-Part Hit Crime Series Officially Confirms 2026 Release Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The controversial author, Rowling, finally published Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone in 1997 after many rejections, and it led to it becoming the biggest book series in history. Since the seven novels concluded, the author has continued writing, whether that be to expand her Harry Potter universe, write other children's novels, write screenplays, or write under pseudonyms to venture into entirely new genres.

Rowling's new crime thriller adaptation is set to be released in one month, just weeks before the upcoming HBO Harry Potter series on December 6. Strike: The Running Grave will be returning for its seventh season, with Tom Burke returning as Detective Cormoran Strike and Holliday Grainger portraying his partner, Robin Ellacott. It will be adapting the seventh novel in Rowling's crime series written under the pen name Robert Galbraith.

"When Sir Colin and Sally Edensor approach Cormoran Strike and Robin Ellacott, they are desperate to reconnect with their estranged son, Will. The Edensors enlist the detectives to gather evidence to discredit the Universal Humanitarian Church, a religious cult who have indoctrinated Will and siphoned off his trust fund."

The seasons include adaptations of The Cuckoo's Calling, The Silkworm, Career of Evil, Lethal White, Troubled Blood, The Ink Black Heart, and now, The Running Grave. The most recent installment of Strike, titled The Ink Black Heart, averaged over 7.1 million viewers, becoming the BBC's most-watched drama. Burke and Grainger lead a guest cast including James Fleet (known for Bridgerton) as Sir Colin Edensor, Keeley Forsyth (known for Happy Valley) as Mazu Wace and Nichola McAuliffe (known for British soap opera EastEnders) as Shelley Heaton.
