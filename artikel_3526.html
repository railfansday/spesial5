Russell Brand's Wife Will 'Honor' Prison Rules After 'Not Guilty' Message

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Russell Brand’s wife, Laura Brand, said she will “honor” the rules of his prison after controversy over a message she read out from the incarcerated comedian declaring his innocence.

A spokesperson for U.K. Prime Minister Keir Starmer confirmed last week that Brand’s message — read out by Laura on the November 15 episode of their “Sunday Service” podcast — was being investigated to determine whether it had violated U.K. laws that ban prisoners from making statements that may cause distress to victims.

“Last week’s episode drew a considerable amount of attention, and now that I understand the parameters set by the brilliant and professional staff at HMP Littlehey [in Cambridgeshire, England], I will absolutely honor that,” Laura, 39, said at the beginning of the latest episode of the “Sunday Service” podcast on Sunday, November 22.

Just days after Brand, 51, was sentenced to 10 and a half years behind bars for rape and sexual assault, the disgraced star declared his innocence in a missive read by his wife on their YouTube podcast.

“Beloved brothers and sisters in Christ, God bless you for your support. As you know, I am not guilty for the offenses I have been jailed for, so something strange is happening,” Brand declared in the message.

“This is clearly appalling and is being urgently investigated. We expect action to be taken if he is found to have broken the rules,” a spokesperson for Starmer said in a statement on November 16.

“Prisoners are strictly banned from making statements that cause distress to victims, and speaking to the media is only permitted in exceptional circumstances,” the spokesperson added. “Prisoners cannot get around these rules by having someone else publish their comments.”

On the latest episode of her podcast, Laura explained why she is continuing forward with “Sunday Service,” which she once cohosted with her husband.

“I’m choosing to continue ‘Sunday Service’ because I really enjoy it,” she said. “It continues to be a great education for me, and I enjoy reading the supportive comments, and I am so heartened by the many of the messages that I got, and that is what inspired me to do it this week and look at the ways I can continue to and move forward with this.”

Laura also declared, “I will continue to be proud to be a wife, a mother and a woman defending her family at a time when the media’s image of what kind of a woman I am is under so much scrutiny.”

Brand was convicted of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault at London’s Southwark Crown Court on November 5. He was sentenced on November 9.
