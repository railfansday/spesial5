Who Will Be The Next Bond Girl?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Since the beginning of the week, the James Bond frenzy has shown no signs of stopping. On November 16, new Bond owners Amazon MGM Studios finally revealed the name of the actor who will succeed Daniel Craig as 007: Robert Pattinson. After years of speculation and months of intense rumors, the next James Bond now has a new face. Now, the next question on everyone’s mind: Who will be the next Bond girl?

The exception in thie great debate is Léa Seydoux. The actor, who seems to be everywhere this year, played Madeleine Swann in Craig's final Bond films, Spectre (2015) and No Time to Die (2021). Her magnetic performance made Madeleine an unforgettable Bond girl, especially since her character plays a decisive role in the arc of Craig’s character: at the end of No Time to Die, the famous spy sacrifices himself to save her and their daughter, Mathilde. For the first time since its creation in 1953 by British author Ian Fleming, the secret agent in Her Majesty’s service was gone.

Léa Seydoux and Daniel Craig pose as they arrive on the set of No Time To Die on September 09, 2019 in Matera, Italy.

In a recent interview on Vogue's The Run-Through podcast, Seydoux was candid about how little interest she had in her successor. “I don’t really care,” she said with a laugh. “I don’t really care, guys. How can you?”

When pressed on who Pattinson's female lead might be, Seydoux expressed mixed feelings about the British spy's latest iteration. With the purchase of the Bond IP by Amazon, “It became American," Seydoux said. “But I’m sure the movie will be great because it’s directed by Denis Villeneuve, who’s a very talented director. I’m sure he will do something great.”

The online megamart took control of the storied franchise in 2023, after which Amazon founder Jeff Bezos controversially sought feedback on the title character's casting via X. The announcement of Villeneuve as the next film's director brought things back from the brink.

Bezos, who confirmed that he was “very involved” in the film's casting process, also seems to give Seydoux pause. In an interview with Variety, she admitted “was a bit sad when I heard that it was sold (…) but now that it’s Denis, I was like, Oh, at least it’s him, so it will be cinema.”

Denis Villeneuve, who directed Seydoux in Dune: Part Two, will release the final installment of this epic science-fiction trilogy will be on December 16. After that, he'll be free to focus on his next major project: reviving the world’s most famous secret agent. It remains to be seen who he will choose to star opposite Robert Pattinson as his female companion—but we know that despite her fondness for the director, Seydoux will not return: Just last month, the French actress told People “I really loved being on this franchise, but now it’s ... done.”
