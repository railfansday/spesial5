Binance’s Co-Founder Says Crypto Market Pullback Is Temporary

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Yi He, the co-founder of the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange, Binance, has sparked discussions across the crypto market after urging retail traders to stay calm even as major crypto assets continue to post massive daily losses.

Yi He’s consoling statement has come as crypto traders grow increasingly weary and the prolonged market volatility begins to cause even bigger losses, with Bitcoin retesting $63,000.

In her post, Yi urged investors holding major crypto assets that have been substantially affected by the severe market volatility to remain calm amid the latest market downturn.

She described the ongoing crypto market pullback as ‘temporary’ while assuring spot traders that they have little reason to worry.

Alongside the post, Yi He shared a link to the Binance app showcasing a 30-day asset change of approximately -$101.31 million in her account.

The figure indicates the amount of losses the pro-crypto investor has suffered on her holdings over a period of about a month, from October 17 to November 16, 2026.

Although the showcased portfolio shows a decline in the founder’s holdings, it does not establish whether the decline represents realized trading losses, unrealized losses, withdrawals, or other changes in Yi He’s account.

It appears that some investors may have found comfort in Yi He's message as they expressed support, noting that turbulent conditions like these don’t necessarily last long.

However, other commentators have questioned whether wealthy industry figures like Yi He can fully understand the financial pressure facing retail traders at a time like this.

Some of the arguments questioned how meaningful such reassurance could be for retail investors, arguing that losing 100 million yuan might not significantly affect someone with substantial wealth like her.

Meanwhile, a loss of 20,000 or 30,000 yuan could wipe out an ordinary investor's savings.
