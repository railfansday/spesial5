The XRP Ledger lending system is getting closer to deployment, but don’t confuse a countdown with a working loan market. The SingleAssetVault amendment is projected to activate on December 9, provided validator support holds. The separate LendingProtocol amendment still needs approval.

Meanwhile, XRPL stablecoin supply has reached $836.90 million. RLUSD accounts for 89.35% of that total, according to November 27 figures. That’s a sizeable balance, but it isn’t money already committed to lending.

The SingleAssetVault amendment would establish infrastructure for pooling a single asset and issuing depositors shares representing their interest. The LendingProtocol amendment performs a different job: it enables brokers to arrange fixed-term loans and record repayments on the ledger.

XRPL amendments need more than 80% support from trusted validators for two consecutive weeks. If support falls short, the waiting period can restart. So December 9 remains a projection, not a guaranteed launch date.

The proposed system supports uncollateralised loans, making borrower assessment a serious issue for depositors. Underwriting and risk management happen offchain, according to the lending specification.

Brokers may also contribute optional first-loss capital in the same asset as the loan pool. That can absorb some missed-payment losses, but it isn’t a blanket guarantee.

The XRP Ledger Foundation has published an open-source reference application demonstrating the system on Devnet. It combines vaults and lending with credentials, permissioned domains and batch transactions.

Still, a working demonstration isn’t commercial adoption. XRPL DeFi total value locked stands at about $30.65 million, a separate measure from the $836.94 million stablecoin balance.

The XRP Ledger lending system now needs amendment activation, production markets and funded loans before stablecoin supply can translate into actual lending activity.
