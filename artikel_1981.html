Why North Macedonia star is confident they can stun Scotland at Hampden

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

North Macedonia star Luka Stankovski reckons they can stun Scotland on Friday...because the pressure is off them.

Scotland face the group's minnows, who have lost all four games in the section, including a 2-0 defeat to the Scots in Skopje.

They have failed to win in their last 12 games since a victory against Liechtenstein and haven't even scored in their past eight games.

Macedonian boss Goce Sedloski dropped Rangers striker Bojan Miovski from his squad and he was their last goalscorer....in a 7-1 defeat to Wales last September.

However, midfielder Stankovski, who plays for Ukrainian side Kolos Kovalivka, believes they can start to show what they can do because everyone has written them off.

He said: "Unfortunately, we no longer have a chance to advance from this group, so maybe we will be more psychologically relaxed and be able to play better.

"We have two tough matches ahead of us, but we hope that we will manage to achieve a positive result in both of them.

"Our ambition in every match is victory and I can promise that we will give 100 per cent to make Macedonian fans happy.

"It's hard to forget defeats, but believe me, we the players, experience them the hardest.

"We give our all, not only on the pitch, but mentally too.

"I hope that it will be easier for us now, that we will play with more freedom, without pressure.

"I hope we will finally score this goal that we need. I believe we can win both matches if we perform to our best."
