“The Bloke’s a Warrior” West Ham’s Souček Update Could Be Bad News For Stoke

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

West Ham United have received a timely boost ahead of Saturday’s Championship clash against Stoke City, with Tomáš Souček back in training at Rush Green. The Czech midfielder suffered a nasty cut near his left eye during Tuesday night’s defeat at Portsmouth but is hoping to be involved against the Potters.

Speaking to the club’s official website this morning, Souček revealed that he was feeling good despite requiring stitches and expects to wear a bandage if he plays tomorrow. Hardly a surprise really, the bloke’s a warrior and there’s not a West Ham fan in the world who didn’t expect big Sou to make himself available to play.

The 31-year-old also highlighted the importance of matching the opposition’s intensity when West Ham are without the ball. Having impressed during his substitute appearance at Fratton Park, Souček has certainly made a strong case for a return to the starting line-up.

Personally, I can’t think of a more important player than Tomáš Souček in West Ham’s current situation. Make no mistake about it, if Stoke boss Mark Robins sees the big Czech on the Hammers’ team sheet, he’ll be worried and will have to make defensive provision for him.

With West Ham’s strikers continuing to struggle, Souček must start and, if he does, he has as good a chance of scoring as anyone on that football pitch.
