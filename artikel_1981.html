Nottingham Forest could 'cash in' on Murillo as 'major concerns' emerge

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Nottingham Forest have not ruled out the possibility of cashing in on Murillo in 2027 if his recent performances do not improve.

The 24-year-old remains a key part of the starting XI at the City Ground, but like a number of his teammates, has failed to impress this season since Oliver Glasner's arrival.

Glasner recently called on his players to step up after a shocking start to the season, but concerns have grown about the centre-back's displays, and his future could be questioned.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Forest could be tempted to sell Murillo in an attempt to maximise his value given his downturn in performances.

There has been no shortage of interest in the Brazil international for almost two years at this stage, with a number of the Premier League's biggest clubs still keen.

Given the lofty price tag Nottingham Forest have placed on Murillo, the club could be tempted to let him move on should a significant offer arrive from one of his suitors.

Football Insider revealed last month that Nottingham Forest have placed a "huge" price tag on Murillo amid growing interest from the likes of Man United in recent weeks.

A number of Premier League clubs are looking to sign a centre-back, and while a January move would likely be ruled out, there is a possibility he could be available next summer.

Murillo has long been on the radar of top clubs, and Forest may feel there is value in letting him leave soon, rather than risking his price tag dropping due to his performances.

"Nottingham Forest could find themselves in an interesting situation with a few players," Brown told Football Insider.

"When they're not hitting the levels they've shown in the past, you start to question, was it a flash in the pan? Was he playing above himself? Or is this just a dip in form?

"Murillo, for me, is one of those players at the moment because so far this season he hasn't been anything like the player we've seen in the last couple of years. That's a major concern.

"Everybody knows there is this interest in him, but that could start to fade if clubs get a feeling that maybe he's not still playing at that high level.

"From a Nottingham Forest point of view, the difficult decision is about whether they cash in now to maximise his value while they can still demand a high price.

"Because if he has a poor season, it could mean clubs aren't willing to pay so much in the future, and they'll have missed out on potentially a big fee.

"It's because of those worries that I don't think they'll rule out letting him go, as much as they would like to keep him and want him to get back to his best."

Murillo has no shortage of interest from rival clubs in the Premier League, as previously mentioned, who could be tempted to lure him away from the City Ground soon.

Man United are believed to have been long-term admirers of the centre-back, and will be looking to strengthen their defence in January and heading into next summer.

Meanwhile, Football Insider revealed last month that Liverpool and Chelsea are both interested in signing Murillo and are keeping tabs on his recent performances.

Liverpool are seeking a long-term replacement for Virgil van Dijk, while Chelsea remain determined to bolster their squads, and both clubs could be ready to spend big.

That interest could tempt Nottingham Forest into a sale, but regardless of whether they are concerned about his performances, any deal will have to come at a huge fee.
