Mixed week for Haywards Heath Town – but one to forget for Hassocks

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Lukas Franzen-Jones led the rout with a treble and Mark Goldson, Ryan Warwick, Connor Webb and Henry Watson added one each.

It was a sixth straight league win for Kev Green’s side, who have scored 20 goals in that spell.

Hassocks slipped up, though, with their own unbeaten run coming to an end at Newhaven, where they lost 2-1.

James Westlake’s side remain third and have been boosted by the signing of goal ace Dan Perry from East Grinstead Town.

Heath continue their league campaign at sixth-placed Roffey on Saturday when Hassocks go to Billingshurst.

The two local SCFL Premier sides turned their attentions to the Sussex Senior Cup on Tuesday night – and both crashed out at the third round stage.

There was another goal-fest at Haywards Heath but this one was in favour of Broadbridge Heath, who ran out 6-2 winners.

Charlie Meehan and Jack Barnes were Town’s scorers against a Bears side who play one division higher in the Isthmian South East.

At Hassocks the home side found Chichester City, who play two divisions above them in the Southern League Premier South, in clinical mood, scoring three in each half to win 6-0.
