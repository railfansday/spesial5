Middlesbrough must be shaking at one thing Tonda Eckert is doing at Southampton

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Championship has already provided some dramatic encounters this season, and many will expect nothing less when Southampton host Middlesbrough on Friday night in front of the Sky cameras.

The past six months tell you there is no love lost between Saints and Boro supporters, which will only add to the atmosphere normally generated when two sides towards the top of the table face off under the lights.

Tonda Eckert's side are just seven points behind the league leaders after 17 matches, and could narrow that gap down to four whilst, in turn, piling the pressure on Wolves, Stoke City and Birmingham City.

There is much to like about the way the German and Kim Hellberg set up their sides, with Boro the undisputed top scorers ahead of the encounter, largely through Will Lankshear's remarkable form.

However, Saints will not be fearful of the quality oozing through Hellberg's ranks, with Eckert also able to call upon several technically gifted individuals in a bid to attack the game.

Eckert is approaching the one-year anniversary of his permanent appointment as Saints head coach, and plenty has gone on since then.

Given the club's recent Premier League past, there is always an expectancy for Saints to be in and among the Championship's promotion hopefuls, and that has remained the case this term despite a four-point deduction.

Home form is seen as a key barometer in the division, and Southampton's displays at St Mary's under the 33-year-old have been almost perfect.

Friday night's clash will be the 30th home fixture Eckert has overseen, with Southampton losing just two of the previous 29 against Hull City on January 17th and to West Ham United in August.

Although Middlesbrough have accumulated one more point and played more times at the Riverside Stadium, Saints, strictly speaking, boast the division's best home record, taking 22 points from a possible 24, scoring 22 goals in the process.

Eckert, who was recently rewarded with a fresh contract, revealed in September that reconnecting with the club's fanbase has been a key reason behind such an impressive run.

As impressive as Middlesbrough have been under Hellberg, the North East outfit aren't flawless, and they were exposed in disappointing away performances and results at Wrexham and Watford.

In both encounters, Boro continued to dominate possession and created 30 openings across 180 minutes, but a lack of cutting edge, somewhat down to an injury sustained by Lankshear, was punished by Phil Parkinson's side, whilst, on another day, a side in better form than the Hornets could have performed a smash-and-grab.

Will Lankshear has sent a message to Middlesbrough fans ahead of Friday evening's hotly-anticipated clash with Southampton

Southampton have averaged 57.1% possession this season, but certainly have the firepower to expose any defensive frailties through the likes of Leo Scienza, Finn Azaz and Canadian hotshot, Cyle Larin, who was back on the goalscoring trail against Derby County.

Boro have also taken just 12 of their 35 points in away matches thus far, which could give travelling supporters further nerves heading into Friday's contest.

With both sides known for a high-tempo game, a fascinating encounter was already on the cards, but then you factor in the passion from the stands which is likely to be channeled by the 22 starters, substitutes and head coaches after May's Spygate debacle.

Hellberg revealed in October that he'd received an apology from Eckert after Southampton were found guilty of breaching EFL regulations and the German was found to have done similar against FA guidelines, but after what ensued in the play-off semi-finals, which were effectively void as Middlesbrough went on to play and lose the final to Hull City in emotionally draining circumstances, the two sets of fans are likely to be partaking in intense 'back-and-forth'.
