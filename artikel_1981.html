‘Exposed the world champions’: Spanish press react to England win in Nations League

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

10 man England secured a miraculous 3-2 win against world champions Spain in Madrid to top their Nations League group.

The Three Lions raced into a 3-0 lead at the Metropolitano Stadium, before Elliot Anderson conceded a penalty and was shown a straight red card for bringing down Lamine Yamal inside the area.

Mikel Oyarzabal scored the resulting spot-kick, and substitute Nico Williams set up a nervy finish as he slotted the ball home after a quick counter-attack.

Despite late pressure from Spain, England held on thanks to a superb double save from James Trafford, ending La Roja’s 43-game unbeaten run on a memorable night for Thomas Tuchel’s side.

Sunday’s result will go down as one of the most remarkable nights in England’s recent history, with Tuchel admitting his team reached the “highest level football-wise in all aspects of the game”.

Meanwhile, captain Harry Kane, who opened the scoring from the penalty spot at the end of the first half, said the victory was “as big as it gets” outside of a major tournament.

England entered the international break still carrying the scars of the World Cup, with Tuchel facing significant criticism for his in-game management during their semi-final defeat to Argentina.

However, victory over Spain means England have now won five of their six matches since returning from America, while Tuchel can point to having got his substitutions right as his side held on with 10 men to beat the reigning world and European champions in their own backyard.

England made the perfect start in the Spanish capital, with Declan Rice claiming post-match that, “The first 10 minutes was the best we’ve played” as Luis de La Fuente’s side struggled to live with England’s pressing.

ITV pundit and former Manchester United midfielder Roy Keane was similarly impressed by England’s intense start, describing their first-half performance as “perfect” after Kane gave the visitors a deserved lead just before the break.

Keane’s ITV colleague, Ian Wright, was particularly pleased for Arsenal right-back Ben White, who was subjected to boos during England’s 2-1 victory over Croatia but started and scored against Spain after a flowing passing move.

“Can I just say, I’m really pleased for Ben White and the way things have turned around for him. It’s just brilliant for him to get back in, and he was magnificent tonight,” Wright said.

“Not just with the goal, but he was making tackles, niggling the Spain players. I’m so pleased it’s ended that way for him.

“There was a lot to be excited about for England. They were 3-0 up and cruising against the world champions before the red kind of changed the game.”

Spanish outlet AS also heaped praise on England’s performance, declaring the result as “the biggest setback of the De la Fuente era” with Tuchel’s men “completely neutralising Spain.”

“England exposed the world champions in what represents the biggest setback of the De la Fuente era, a bitter end to a dream 2026. Goals from Kane, Konsa, and White rewarded a superior opponent,” a report in AS stated.

“Tuchel’s men had managed to cut off both of their opponents’ cables, the red and the blue, completely neutralising Spain.”

Fellow Spanish outlet MARCA also chose to celebrate England’s dominance rather than attack Spain’s performance.

“It’s better to lose against a formidable opponent like England, who dominated the match until they were reduced to ten men,” MARCA said before continuing by lavishing praise on Tuchel.

“Credit goes to Tuchel, though his performance has been questioned because he seemed afraid to be the hero in the World Cup match against Argentina. It’s been a long time since an opponent has so thoroughly dismantled Spain.”
