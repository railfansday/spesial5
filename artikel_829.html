Jeers, injuries and doubters - White's journey back to the top

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Ben White has made eight appearances for England.

Ben White's crucial goal against world champions Spain on Sunday will not be forgotten by England fans for a long time.

A smart finish to cap a flowing Three Lions move gave Thomas Tuchel's men an almost unbelievable 3-0 lead in Madrid. They hung on - just - meaning White will always be remembered as the player who scored that winner.

But, for White, the moment represented more than just a goal. It signalled a dramatic turnaround in fortunes for his international career - just four years after many thought his England days were over.

The Arsenal defender has had a tough time on the international stage to say the least.

He left the World Cup in 2022 after a disagreement with then assistant manager Steve Holland and made himself unavailable during the rest of Gareth Southgate's time in charge.

Since returning to England duty in March under Tuchel, the 29-year-old has now established himself as arguably England's best right-back - despite facing jeers from sections of his own fans for his decision to leave that camp.

The defender, who initially arrived at Arsenal from Brighton as a centre-back, has had an excellent start to the season, having played a crucial part in the Gunners' first title win for 22 years last time out.

It hasn't been straightforward for him at Arsenal either though. White has had to navigate his own injury problems and at one point when the defender was struggling for fitness, there was talk that the Gunners should look at moving him on.

But White has battled back and even dealt with competition from Jurrien Timber to remain one of Arsenal's key players.

"I think just obviously people don't know the full story, because I never said anything and won't put anyone under the bus", White said before the England win over Spain.

The 29-year-old is keen to put the well-covered departure from the World Cup in Qatar behind him.

After his long absence from playing for England, some supporters did not believe that White should return.

His first appearance at Wembley since that incident in a friendly against Uruguay in March was booed by some supporters, loud enough for Tuchel to be asked about it after the match.

That game in itself was dramatic for White, who scored the opening goal for England before giving away the penalty that saw the Three Lions draw 1-1 with Uruguay.

White, who missed the World Cup with injury, had been expecting the reaction from some fans. But he is used to proving himself and changing the perception around him.

Tuchel was keen to back his player and said in the build-up to the Spain game that he hoped that the chapter was closed on White being booed by his own supporters.

"Obviously a bit tricky and not straightforward, but yes, it's obviously amazing to be here now." White said of his return to England.

White said in that same interview that he would make the same decision again that he did at the World Cup, but it was now time to move on.

His displays show exactly where his mindset is for his country.

He played the final 12 minutes in England's win over Croatia at Wembley before being brought into the starting line-up for England's toughest match of the November international break against Spain.

This new era of England is being dubbed Tuchel 2.0 and White firmly seems to be part of it.

Chelsea defender Reece James is out with injury. He is a big favourite of Tuchel's but cannot be relied upon for every game due to injuries.

White is a key player for the Premier League champions, reliable in defence and a threat in attack while being physically imposing. There is a lot for Tuchel to like.

And with three of his Arsenal team-mates on the pitch as England won the game, and Bukayo Saka to return, White brings the positives of the cohesion of a club side to Tuchel's team.

Arsenal supporters will not be surprised by White's excellent performances for England.

Since signing for the Gunners in 2021, not many players can say they have played more of a crucial role in Arsenal's rebirth under Mikel Arteta.

Such is the importance of White, the Gunners attack does not flow as freely when he is not in the side to link up with Martin Odegaard and Saka as he relentlessly produces overlapping runs - a trait he showed when he scored England's third in Madrid.

His team-mates regularly say he is one of the fiercest competitors in training, and he has regularly shown his mentality when it looks as though things are going against him with his club side.

Arsenal signing Timber and a number of injury issues looked to have had White nearing an exit but, such is his determination, each time a challenge comes up he manages to turn it around.

White is a unique character within football, and has even said he doesn't watch the game away from the training ground, but his passion for his profession cannot be doubted.

Gunners fans will know that the level of passion he displayed in Madrid is something he shows every time he is on the pitch for his club side.
