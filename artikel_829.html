Millennials Can Exhale: Low-Rise Jeans Are Out, This Y2K Style Is In

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

You heard it here first—bootcut jeans are back! And we found Black Friday deals on the style that's forecasted to take over 2027.

It's clear that Y2K fashion isn't a temporary trend, it's a lasting resurgence. That's particularly true when it comes to jeans.

While several early aughts denim designs have made their way back into our closets, from rhinestone styles to baggy silhouettes and low-slung fits, one has remained underrated—the bootcut jean.

Based on runways and street style pics alike, bootcut jeans are poised for a potential 2027 takeover. The best part? You can save on the rising trend now because we found tons of pairs on sale for Black Friday, from $50.

Before skinny jeans stepped onto the scene, bootcut jeans were the denim of choice throughout the early to mid aughts.

While bootcut jeans can technically be low-rise, we're mostly referring to its silhouette, which is somewhere between a flare and straight leg jean. With a slim fit through the hips and thighs, bootcut jeans boast a leg-elongating, curve-defining fit compared to the effortless laidback feel offered by baggy jeans with low-slung waists.

Bootcut jeans were specifically designed to accomodate, you guessed it, boots. That's why even today, they look best skimming the tops of a pointed-toe bootie or heeled mule.

To modernize the denim trend, try pairing them with chunky oversized knits, timeless trench coats and button-downs, or silky lace-trimmed camisoles.
