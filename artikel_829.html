Renee Slegers leaves Arsenal after disappointing start to WSL season

Renee Slegers has left her role as head coach of Arsenal following a difficult start to the Women’s Super League season for the north London club.

The Gunners occupy sixth place in the WSL standings, sitting 12 points behind leaders Chelsea after 11 matches, and remain winless at home in the league this term.

The Dutch manager assumed the role from Jonas Eidevall, initially on an interim basis, two years ago before guiding Arsenal to Champions League glory in May 2025.

A statement on arsenal.com read: "We can confirm Renee Slegers has left her position as head coach of our women’s first team by mutual consent.

"Renee departs following a disappointing start to the 2026-27 season, with assistant coach Aaron D’Antino also leaving the club.

"The process of appointing a successor is under way and we will update in due course. Assistant coach Melissa Clarke-Reed will take charge of the team in the interim period.

"Everyone at Arsenal would like to thank Renee and Aaron for their contributions to the club and wish them every success in their future careers."

Pressure had been mounting on Slegers, particularly with Arsenal struggling to convert in front of goal, and her final match ended in a goalless draw against West Ham last Sunday.

Gunners director of women’s football Clare Wheatley said: "It was an extremely difficult decision to part ways with Renee, who has led the team with dedication and commitment throughout her time here.

"In leading us to glory in the Champions League and the inaugural Fifa Champions Cup, Renee has made a significant and lasting contribution to the history of Arsenal, with close support from Aaron.

"After careful reflection, it is clear that results have been below the expected level and change is necessary."

Arsenal have two weeks until their next fixture due to the international break, with England set to face Ukraine in a two-legged playoff as Sarina Wiegman’s side aim to secure their place at next year’s World Cup.

Once the international break finishes, Arsenal will return to action without Slegers when they travel to Everton on 11 December.
