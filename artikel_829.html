Tyra Banks Looks So Different With Blonde Hair Transformation

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Tyra Banks, 52, showed off her long platinum locks in New York City as the supermodel celebrated the rerelease of her fantasy novel Modelland.

Tyra Banks is in the running for America’s next top blonde.

Thanks to switching up her signature honey-brown look for flashy platinum blonde locks, the America’s Next Top Model host was able to shine bright and shine far.

As the 52-year-old stopped by SiriusXM Radio studios Nov. 11, she showed off the daring blonde ombre, balanced with a timeless all-back ensemble.

The supermodel’s day out in New York City also included the first stop on her book tour for the rerelease of 2011’s Modelland, for which she recently recorded the audiobook. In the dystopian fantasy, aspiring model Tookie De La Crème chases her dreams at a dangerous boarding school for models despite her awkward appearance.

And the Project Runway judge is taking a page out of her protagonist’s book as it pertains to bravely facing a new chapter. Banks recently shared she is embracing single life following a breakup with her partner of seven years, Louis Bélanger-Martin.

“I’m a single woman now,” Banks revealed on the Hot 97 Radio Show Nov. 10. “I’m 52 years old and I was kind of over it. Like, I’ve got my mom, I’ve got my son and I’m good.”

That said, Banks—mom to Yorks, 10, with ex Erik Asla—has been smizing into the new era of rediscovering her roots.

“I didn’t even have, like, a drive,” she confessed. “But now I’m like, you know what? It is not too late. Now, I feel feminine.”

The ice cream entrepreneur added, “I’m living in my feminineness right now.”

But that doesn’t mean Banks will stay on the market for long. After all, she has been enjoying the dating experience at this stage in her life.

As she put it, “I’m dating. I’m back to that. And there is no such thing that, ‘Oh it has to be over because of an age or because you’re just done.’”
