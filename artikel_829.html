Justice, Pretty Lights, Honey Dijon & RL Grime Lead Lightning In a Bottle 2027 Lineup

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The festival returns to SoCal on May 26-30.

Lightning In a Bottle has dropped the lineup for its 2027 fest, and it’s major.

Leading the bill is Justice, who will play LiB for the first time in the festival’s 23-year history, performing a DJ set. Pretty Lights will return to the event for the first time since 2011, with house icon Honey Dijon, trap legend RL Grime, soul/funk leaders Jungle, techno titan Amelie Lens, UK pioneers Basement Jaxx, dance-pop favs Sofi Tukker and multigenre standout Whethan also getting top billing.

Also gracing the lineup are DJ Habibeats, Carlita, Chloé Caillet, DJ Minx, Andy C, The Glitch Mob, Eliza Rose, Jackie Hollander (who was named a Billboard rising dance artist to watch earlier this year), a DJ set by Fcukers, Mary Droppinz, HAAi, TEED, Carré, Cain Culto, DJ_Dave, esteemed L.A. party brand A Club Called Rhonda returning to host a night in one of the tents and more. Lightning In a Bottle 2027 will happen at its regular home at Buena Vista Lake near Bakersfield, Calif., on May 26-30. Tickets — available as either three- or five-day passes — go on sale Friday, Nov. 20 at 12 p.m. PT.

In addition to the music, LiB will, as always, host a sprawling lineup of talks, yoga classes, wellness sessions and other programming. The lineup for that component will be announced in the coming months.

Prior to LiB 2027, the independent festival’s organizers The Do Lab will also host their annual stage at Coachella, with the lineups for that festival forthcoming.
