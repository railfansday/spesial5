Get the Citi® Diamond Preferred® Card if you’re moving a credit card balance you’ll need well over a year to repay. Transfers completed in your first four months get 0% for 21 months from account opening, for a 3% fee, so move yours as soon as you’re approved. Purchases get only 0% for 12 months.

The card is built for paying down a large, high-rate balance moved from another bank’s card. Whatever is left when the 0% ends is charged the regular rate, so plan to clear the balance in time.

It’s the card for a balance that needs every month of its transfer intro, which runs longer than the Citi Simplicity’s or the Citi Double Cash’s. Readers wary of a missed payment still do better with the Simplicity, which charges no late fees or penalty rate, and readers who want rewards do better with the Double Cash, which pays 2% back.
