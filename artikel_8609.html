When you compare your portfolio returns to a benchmark, do you apply the same cash flow to the daily prices of the benchmark or do you compare to the reported YTD/annual returns of the benchmark? I used to do the former when I was judging my trading strategy against the S&P since the timing of the cashflow in a volatile benchmark can change the actual returns...XIRR assumes a constant return over the period, like a fixed rate savings account, which certainly isn't the case.

FWIW, isn't MS Money no longer supported? I use Quicken which also has similar reports on returns and runs on my Mac.
I have very little cash flow to account for. We used to own VSMGX and AOR in IRAs. They served as internal controls. MSMoney reports the same performance numbers that Vanguard and iShares report which of course include reinvested dividends. If there are any differences, then they are quite small. That is, I think the YTD returns for the benchmarks are legit for my purposes.

Yes, MSMoney is no longer supported, but still available for installation on the old Windows laptop that I have.

I suppose many readers might be thinking that livesoft (me!) makes a trade or two every day. That is NOT the case. I rarely make any transactions at all excepting for reinvesting dividends manually. That's because RBDs are very rare, right?
