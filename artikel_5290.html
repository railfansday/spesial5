Oracle’s Larry Ellison has fallen from the top 10 richest billionaires in the world after his cloud company’s stock tanks—he’s lost $10.9 billion

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Like many other tech billionaires, Oracle cofounder Larry Ellison is no stranger to how a stock market swing can make or cost him billions—and he just took a  nearly $11 billion hit that knocked him from the top 10 richest billionaires.

Ellison’s net worth dropped $10.9 billion at the close of markets last Friday, according to the Bloomberg Billionaires Index, as his cloud company’s shares continued to drop.

Oracle’s stock fell around 7.5% to around $102 at the end of last week, the lowest it’s been since January 2024, as the company’s shares have slid 47% year-over-year.

It’s a far cry from when the business’ stock hit a peak of $345 in September 2025 amid surging demand for AI computing power; but in the following year, investors have grown concerned over Oracle’s massive AI infrastructure spending, mounting debt, and negative free cash flow. As Ellison owns around 40% of the company, those losses hit his pocket hard, sending him tumbling down the ultra-rich list past Walmart heirs Jim Walton, Rob Walton, and Alice Walton.

However, Ellison still has a whopping $139 billion to his name—enough to put him thousands of times above the estimated $62 million net-worth threshold for America’s wealthiest 0.1% of households.

Even a multibillion-dollar loss barely makes a dent in a fortune of that size. And this isn’t the first time the 82-year-old has had his wealth swing dramatically from one week to the next.

Fortune reached out to Oracle for comment.

For most billionaires whose fortunes are tied to company stock, a single rally can send their net worth soaring by billions. However, a sudden sell-off or market change can wipe out just as much wealth almost overnight.

In September 2025, Ellison briefly took the throne as the richest person in the world, outstripping Tesla billionaire Elon Musk, who had steadily held the spot for nearly a year straight. Following better-than-expected quarterly results, the Oracle cofounder subsequently enjoyed a $101 billion surge in wealth overnight, hitting a $393 billion net worth—which placed him ahead of Musk’s $385 billion fortune at the time.

But Ellison’s ranking as the world’s richest person last year was short-lived: His estimated net worth fell by $34 billion in the two days following Oracle’s stock surge, according to Bloomberg’s index. J. Bradford DeLong, a U.C. Berkeley economist, told Fortune that the sharp downfall was triggered by “second thoughts” around Oracle’s cloud deal with OpenAI.

Michael Dell, the founder and CEO of pioneering tech company Dell Technologies, watched his wealth double this September thanks to the AI boom. The 61-year-old—who owns 40% of his tech company—gained around $122 billion between September 2025 and 2026, nearly doubling his wealth to a whopping $262 billion, according to Bloomberg’s Index.

His wealth has since fluctuated alongside the stock market, and today, he’s the sixth richest person alive with $176 billion to his name.

Moderna cofounder Robert Langer also saw his fortune rebound as he re-entered the billionaires club earlier this year following promising cancer vaccine trials that sent the company’s stock soaring.

The “Edison of Medicine” witnessed his equity in the biotech business double in value, shooting his net worth up to roughly $1.7 billion, according to an August Forbes analysis.

His wealth surge followed news that Moderna and pharmaceutical company Merck’s mRNA cancer vaccine hit its goals in a Phase 3 trial targeting melanoma, sending Moderna’s stock soaring more than 100%.
