Wall Street’s Stunning Post-Election Rebound Puts Bitcoin and Crypto Markets in Focus

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Wall Street staged a sharp recovery following the U.S. midterm elections, reversing earlier losses as investors reassessed the implications of Democrats taking control of both chambers of Congress.

Notably, the turnaround has also drawn attention to Bitcoin and the wider cryptocurrency market as traders weigh the political outcome against its potential impact on crypto regulations.

According to analyst Bull Theory, the recovery was particularly striking given the scale of the initial sell-off. NASDAQ futures had dropped more than 2% as election results pointed to a Democratic sweep, while Japan’s Nikkei and South Korea’s KOSPI also suffered significant losses.

However, major indices subsequently rebounded from their session lows. The S&P 500 recovered 2.18%, while the NASDAQ gained 3.13%. The Dow Jones and Russell 2000 advanced 2.16% and 2.31%, respectively. In Asia, Japan’s Nikkei 225 climbed 5.09%, while South Korea’s KOSPI rose 4.71%.

Bull Theory attributed the reversal partly to investors reassessing what a Democratic-controlled Congress means for President Donald Trump’s remaining two years in office.

Although Democrats are projected to control both chambers from January 3, 2027, Republicans retain control until then. Furthermore, Trump’s presidential powers remain intact, including his ability to veto legislation passed by Congress. Overriding a veto would require a two-thirds majority in both chambers, a threshold Democrats cannot reach on their own.

Notably, the election outcome could make it harder for Trump to advance his legislative agenda without necessarily allowing Democrats to push through major policy changes of their own. Investors may therefore be adjusting their expectations rather than treating the result as an immediate threat to financial markets.

Bitcoin also fell during the election-related sell-off, briefly dropping into the $75,000 range as a Democratic victory in both chambers became increasingly likely. However, the cryptocurrency recovered as uncertainty about the political outcome eased.

TDX Strategies founder Dick Lo explained that the recovery followed a reduction in election-related uncertainty.

“Bitcoin dropped initially in response to the Democratic sweep of the midterm elections, but has since recovered as a clear outcome resolves political uncertainty,” he told CoinDesk.

He added that a Democratic-controlled Congress is generally viewed as less favorable for pro-crypto legislation, while divided government makes major, market-unfriendly policy changes less likely.

Meanwhile, analytics platform 10x Research warned that Bitcoin and Ethereum remain at an important technical turning point despite easing election-related risks.

In a post on X, the firm wrote, “While our systematic trend models remain technically short both Bitcoin and Ethereum, fading consensus to trade against established signals demands exceptional risk/reward skew.“

The firm explained that traders should look beyond political developments when assessing whether the recent correction is a temporary setback in a bull market or the start of a more significant shift in market direction.

Its framework focuses on longer-term market indicators, trading-volume support levels, and key price thresholds to determine whether investors should remain defensive or consider taking bullish positions.

Nevertheless, the post-election recovery does not eliminate other risks facing digital assets. TDX Strategies pointed to recent exchange security breaches as a potential source of pressure on investor sentiment, even as declining political uncertainty creates room for market recovery.

That said, with Bitcoin and Ethereum still facing technical resistance, traders will be watching whether the broader market rebound translates into sustained buying interest across cryptocurrencies.
