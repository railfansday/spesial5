United Airlines experiencing widespread system outage just days after FAA grounds planes

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Update 7:24 p.m. Nov. 16: United Airlines says it has resolved the system outage that delayed some departures on Monday, Nov. 16. A United spokesperson told CNN the outage lasted about 30 minutes and affected fewer than 150 flights.

Just days after a telecommunications and data issue prompted a nationwide FAA ground stop, one major airline is experiencing a system outage of its own.

On Monday, Nov. 16, United Airlines announced that it's experiencing a "widespread system outage." The outage is affecting departing flights, but not flights already in the air.

"We are troubleshooting a technology issue affecting departing flights," United Airlines said in a reply on X. "There is no impact on flights in progress, or on United Express flights." According to United Airlines, the company is "working hard to get things back up and running as soon as possible."

It's unclear exactly what the issue is, but this isn't the first major system outage to affect a large company in recent days.

Last week, Microsoft suffered a multi-day system outage that affected Outlook and other Microsoft 365 services. On Friday, as previously mentioned, a telecommunications issue forced the FAA to issue a rare ground stop for all air travel in the U.S. for hours. On Monday, Chase bank reported a software issue that resulted in major downtime for its banking customers.

United Airlines' official account on X is currently responding to numerous complaints from customers whose departing flights have been delayed due to the issue.
