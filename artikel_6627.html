Taylor Swift’s Matching Mani-Pedi Is a Perfect Pour of Fall’s Favorite Nail Color

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

From the rainbow of album-inspired colors she wore throughout her Eras Tour to the subtler, sometimes cryptic nail choices that have sent Swifties into detective mode, Taylor Swift knows that the smallest beauty details can make a big statement. Her latest? A perfectly coordinated, deeply autumnal mani-pedi.

Returning to the CMA Awards in Nashville on Wednesday night for the first time in a decade, Swift made quite the entrance in a sunshine-yellow Oscar de la Renta gown from the house’s spring 2027 collection. It was a textbook Taylor Swift look: a strapless bodice and exuberantly gathered, high-low skirt, paired with some gold strappy sandals—and, more importantly, a glossy wine-red pedicure. That same rich, black-cherry-adjacent shade appeared on her neatly shaped, short fingernails. Against the electric yellow, the contrast was especially lovely. Taylor tends to favor a brighter mode of red—especially when it comes to her pout—but this took things several shades moodier.

It’s a particularly good moment for the merlot manicure. As the temperatures drop, the usual scarlets and tomato reds are giving way to deeper, more decadent shades: think Bordeaux, oxblood, claret, and black cherry. Jennifer Lopez recently took the color-coordination brief to its a most glamorous conclusion, pairing a head-to-toe burgundy ensemble with glossy wine-colored nails and matching makeup. Selena Gomez has also been integrating the shade into her own rotation, including a long, almond-shaped merlot manicure punctuated by tiny gold piercings and hardware, courtesy of nail artist Tom Bachik. Anne Hathaway has also dabbled in rich mulberry, while Naomi Watts and Uma Thurman have brought wine-stained nails to the fashion front row.

For Swift, though, nail color has often been about more than following the seasonal mood. During the Eras Tour, she famously painted each fingernail a different shade to represent her first 10 albums, from Fearless gold and Speak Now purple to Reputation black and Midnights navy. At the 2024 Grammys, her white manicure, revealed beneath black opera gloves, seemed an early clue to the monochromatic world of The Tortured Poets Department. Swift has long enjoyed experimenting with polish, often doing her own nails, while nail artist Lisa Peña-Wong has worked with her on several major red carpet appearances.

This time, there were no elaborate embellishments or multicolored Easter eggs to decipher. Just a beautifully matched set with a high-shine finish, showing off a shade that felt perfectly in tune with the season.
