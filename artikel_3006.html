Roman Wilson, Michael Pittman Jr. questionable vs. Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

No Steelers are out, but six are listed as questionable ahead of Pittsburgh's Sunday clash against the Philadelphia Eagles.

On Friday's injury report, the Steelers listed wide receivers Michael Pittman Jr. (foot). and Roman Wilson (hand), safety DeShon Elliott (knee), outside linebacker Nick Herbig (hip/ribs), linebacker Patrick Queen (hand), and guard Gennings Dunker (knee) as questionable.

Pittman, Wilson and Dunker remain on injured reserve but in their 21-day return windows. The Steelers currently have two open spots on the 53-man roster ahead of Sunday.

Herbig was a non-participant every day ahead of Week 11, but the Steelers' other five questionable players finished the week with a full practice.

Veteran safety Jalen Ramsey, who missed Wednesday's practice for personal reasons, does not carry a game day designation.

Pittsburgh's Week 11 opponent, the Eagles, have yet to release their Friday injury report or game designations at the time of this writing.
