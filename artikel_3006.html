Trump, Xi, Putin To Meet as Jensen Huang Reportedly Eyes Nvidia Comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Pressure is mounting on Nvidia days after it ousted Jensen Huang as CEO with Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent urging the company to make a “dramatic” leadership shakeup and come clean about its compliance with export controls on its prized AI chips. Hours after Bessent’s comments, the Financial Times reported that ousted CEO Jensen Huang has been discussing the idea of returning to the company he co-founded more than three decades ago. A selloff in technology stocks carried into a new week as traders shunned risky bets ahead of Nvidia’s high-stakes earnings and Anthropic’s expected market debut in the coming days. The firm and some of its backers seeking to raise as much as $90.4 billion in the share sale and a separate private placement. Donald Trump is arriving at an unprecedented summit meeting with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin politically weakened at home, yet perhaps even more eager to flex his power abroad. (Source: Bloomberg)
