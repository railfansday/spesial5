Missed the Anthropic IPO Allocation? Here’s How to Buy ANT on Thursday.

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Anthropic stock will be a hot commodity on Day 1 of its public listing.

By Keithen Drury – Nov 18, 2026 at 7:21PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Anthropic, PBC ›

With Anthropic (ANT +0.00%) set to roll out its initial public offering (IPO) on Thursday, not everyone will be able to get in on the expected initial list price of $83. To get that, you had to be a large entity or have access to a brokerage that received some allocation of shares that were priced at the initial price. When a stock goes public, it rarely starts trading at its initial list price due to high demand.

So, if you missed out on the allocation, you'll have to purchase shares on the open market, likely at a higher price than the initial list price.

Will this be worth doing? Let's take a look.

Some stocks execute their IPO without a lot of fanfare. Others get plenty of attention and tend to see their stock skyrocket by the end of the first day of trading. The majority of IPOs follow a common trend: They rise during the first week, then sell off.

Something similar happened with Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX +1.27%) earlier this year in what was then the largest IPO in history based on funds raised (Anthropic is projected to surpass it). When measured by valuation, SpaceX is likely to retain the “largest” title. Anthropic was projected to go public at a $2 trillion valuation earlier this year and set a new record, but that won't happen now due to lower interest in AI investments compared to earlier this year.

The lower valuation forecast could end up being a reason why Anthropic doesn't follow the typical initial rise-and-fall pattern, as interest in AI investing has already taken a bite out of Anthropic's initial estimated price tag.

If you want to invest in Anthropic on Day 1, you’ll have to buy shares on the open market, which could mean paying an inflated price compared to what those who got in at the IPO price paid. Still, if Anthropic continues its tremendous growth pace (its revenue increased 12 times year over year to $17 billion in Q3), that price tag could be worth it in a few years.

If you do buy Anthropic shares, just remember that you should be buying for the long term, not to take advantage of a one- or two-day gain. You should be buying the stock to own the company, not to rent it short-term. Anthropic has real potential to be a fantastic business that is rapidly growing and pioneering the AI-first world. It also has risks associated with it that need to be factored in. It has a huge runway, and with the world just scratching the surface of what's possible with AI, now could be a great opportunity to own a winner.

Just because you missed out on the initial allocation of Anthropic stock doesn't mean you have to miss out completely on the IPO.
