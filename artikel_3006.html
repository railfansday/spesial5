Why Oracle Stock Slumped Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

There are growing doubts about the balance sheets of weaker players in the hyperscaler market.

By Lee Samaha – Nov 25, 2026 at 3:20PM EST

Oracle (ORCL -5.13%) stock was down by more than 8% at midday today. The slump comes on a day of weakness for AI -related stocks. In a nutshell, there are increasing concerns around the costs of AI enterprises developing frontier AI models and, in turn, their ability to pay AI infrastructure companies like Oracle.

The weakening sentiment is evident in the credit default swap (CDS) pricing on its debt. CDS pricing reflects thre price of insuring against a debt default. It's measured in basis points (bps), whereby 100 basis points equals 1%. So, for example, insuring $1 million in debt with a CDS at 100 bps (1%) would cost $10,000 per year.

In the middle of 2025, the CDS on Oracle's 5-year debt stood at just 35 bps, but it closed out 2025 at 154 bps. But here's the thing. Yesterday it spiked to 360 bps, according to data from S&P Global Market Intelligence.

To put that number into context, the bond market typically assumes that 40% of a defaulted bond is actually paid out, so the implied annual hazard rate can be calculated by dividing the CDS price by the 60% loss in default. In other words, .036/0.6=.06, or 6% a year.

Over 5 years, the cumulative rate equates to almost 26%. While it's important to note this is just the bond market pricing, there is still a lot of fear in the market over Oracle. Those fears are centered on its soaring capital expenditures to fund its AI build-out, which have turned its cash flow negative.

The market is clearly worried about the sustainability of AI investment, and in such conditions it makes sense to focus on stronger players like Alphabet on weakness, rather than chase Oracle stock.
