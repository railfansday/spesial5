CTA positioning in U.S. equities hits lowest level since March, Citadel Securities model shows

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Citadel Securities model estimated aggregate positioning in U.S. equities by commodity trading advisers (CTAs) at its lowest level since March 31, as the gauge fell to -3.3 standard deviations, according to Citadel Securities' Scott Rubner.
