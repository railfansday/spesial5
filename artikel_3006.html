“We Put A Gun To Her Head” – Coco Gauff Jokes About Elena Rybakina Joining Aryna Sabalenka & Her In WTA Finals Video Drop

Coco Gauff jokingly explained how she and Aryna Sabalenka convinced Elena Rybakina to join them in a fun reel ahead of the 2026 WTA Finals.

Ahead of the season-ending tournament, the eight finalists appeared for media day and posed for photos. Meanwhile, Sabalenka and Gauff made a fun reel featuring Australian Open and US Open champion Rybakina.

Gauff has been drawn alongside Elena Rybakina, Jessica Pegula, and Linda Noskova in the WTA Finals.

Coco Gauff is set to compete at the WTA Finals for the fifth consecutive time. The tournament will be held in Indian Wells, California, from November 8 to 15. She will take on compatriot Jessica Pegula in the first group match.

Gauff made her debut at the event in 2022, but fell in the round-robin stage. The next year, she advanced to the semifinals before losing to Pegula.

The American won her first WTA Finals title in 2024, defeating Zheng Qinwen in the final. She exited early in 2025.
