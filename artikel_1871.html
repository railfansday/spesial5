New date set for Walsall v Crawley Town

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The fixture at the Bescot Stadium – originally scheduled to take place on Saturday, October 3 – was postponed as a result of the EFL’s approval to the Reds following a number of international call-ups in a recent international break.

A rearranged date of Tuesday, December 15 was agreed between all parties and publicised to supporters.
