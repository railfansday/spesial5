Tottenham 2-0 Ipswich: Luca Williams-Barnett overtakes Sol Campbell in Spurs goalscoring charts

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

By Ben Knapton | 21 Nov 2026 17:00, Last updated: 21 Nov 2026 17:08

Tottenham Hotspur youngster Luca Williams-Barnett repaid Roberto De Zerbi's faith in him with a goal and assist in Saturday's 2-0 Premier League victory over Ipswich Town.

The 18-year-old was handed his first Premier League start against the Tractor Boys in place of the injured Mathys Tel, having shown glimpses of his potential off the bench against Leeds United and Crystal Palace.

With 15 minutes gone at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, Williams-Barnett muscled his way beyond Abdoul Ouattara to meet Pedro Porro's cross and bundle the ball into the back of the net for the opening goal.

The 2008-born youngster followed up his maiden Premier League goal with a fine assist in the second period, driving down the left-hand side and crossing for Dominic Solanke to crash home.

Williams-Barnett made way for Savio in the 64th minute, having been booked shortly after Solanke's goal, but not without becoming Tottenham's third-youngest Premier League scorer ever.

At 18 years, one month and 20 days old, Williams-Barnett is only older than Andy Turner and Gareth Bale when they scored their first Premier League goals for Tottenham.

Turner holds the club record, having scored at just 17 years and five months against Everton in 1992, while Bale was just four days younger than Williams-Barnett is now when he netted against Fulham in September 2007.

Furthermore, Williams-Barnett has overtaken Tottenham traitor Sol Campbell on the list, as the latter was 18 years and two months old when he scored his maiden Spurs Premier League goal.

Aaron Lennon, Reto Ziegler and Dele Alli are the only other players in the top 10 to have scored their first Tottenham Premier League goals in the 2000s.

While Williams-Barnett stole the spotlight at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, the teenager did not quite manage to break either a club or Premier League record.

Despite both scoring and setting up a goal in North London, Williams-Barnett is only the second-youngest player to both score and assist in a Premier League game, behind Michael Owen.

The former Ballon d'Or winner was just 17 years and 364 days old when he scored and assisted for Liverpool against Crystal Palace in 1997, before also doing so against Leeds United at 18 years and 12 days.

Nevertheless, Williams-Barnett has made his case to also start next Sunday's clash away to Sunderland, as well as the first North London derby of the season on December 6.

Spurs' victory over Ipswich has lifted De Zerbi's side up to ninth in the Premier League table on 15 points from 11 matches, just three points worse off than fourth-placed Bournemouth.
