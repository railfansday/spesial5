Josh King: Fulham star reveals England mentor after shock Nations League call-up by Thomas Tuchel

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Fulham’s Josh King has revealed that Jude Bellingham has taken him under his wing for England, and insists he is willing to play wherever Thomas Tuchel needs him.

The attacking midfielder was called up to take part in the Three Lions’ pre-World Cup training camp in Florida in June but on Friday received his first proper call-up to the senior squad ahead of Nations League matches against Croatia on Thursday and Spain on Sunday.

While only 19, King has been given plenty of responsibility for his club under Alvaro Arbeloa this season and has been rewarded now with the chance to earn his senior international debut over the coming week.

“There were a lot of people I learned from [in the pre-World Cup camp].” King said.

“Jude was a big person for me. He took me under his wing and tried to make me feel welcome. There were a lot of players like that. Harry Kane as well, he was one person who really made me feel welcome and tried to help me through my time there.

“But it's a credit to what England have going here, everyone is a really good person as well as good player on the pitch.”

King added: “Playing for England is the dream so to be that one step closer is an amazing achievement, something I've been working towards for a long time. I'm grateful to the manager for putting his trust in me.”

Tuchel said on Friday that he had King as one of his options on the right wing this month, but sees him as able to play in a variety of attacking positions.

“I'm happy to play anywhere for the manager,” King said on Monday. “I have to add other parts to my game, in wider positions you might have to cover defensively or make runs in behind.

“I'm capable of playing lots of different positions, which is probably one of the reasons why I'm here.”

In an interview with Standard Sport in April, King said he wants to become “one of the best midfielders in the world” and dreams in time of being a “leader” for club and country.
