‘I probably didn’t realise’ - Sam Winnall reflects on Barnsley FC to Sheffield Wednesday move ahead of derby

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Sam Winnall has conceded he “probably didn’t realise” how intense the rivalry between Barnsley and Sheffield Wednesday was when he swapped the Reds for the Owls in 2017.

The now retired 35-year-old was a talismanic figure in his Barnsley days, scoring 48 goals across 155 appearances for the club.

He was lured away from Oakwell in January 2017, making a controversial move to their South Yorkshire neighbours Wednesday.

The move sparked anger among Barnsley supporters and Winnall received a frosty reception when he faced the Reds as a Wednesday player.

“A lot of what happened was actually well out of my control,” he told The Star. “And I think you look back with not so much regrets from what I can control, but more so just wishing it could have happened at a different time, if I'm going to be totally honest.

“At that time I probably didn't realise how big the rivalry was, being from somebody that didn't come from Yorkshire. There were a lot of things that went on behind closed doors that have never been disclosed.

“It was sloppy in how it was dealt with from all parties and I think there could have been a far more professional way of dealing with it.

“And I look back on it with fond memories, actually, because I think it was a transfer that brought me a few difficulties but I came through it and came out the other side. I grew a level of resilience with what had happened. I don't look back on it with any regrets of how I acted personally.

“People do move between rival clubs. Mine was obviously at a lower level but look at Sol Campbell, Carlos Tevez, it’s football. But I do definitely look back and think it could have been done more professionally.”

Winnall added fuel to the fire when he returned to Oakwell just months after departing, scoring in a 1-1 draw and celebrating in front of the home supporters.

“It's probably been my most asked question since I've retired,” he said. “And do you know what? I look back and if I could have the chance again, I'd do exactly the same.

“That's because it had been quite a difficult time. I'd had quite a lot of stick from different fans and even some former teammates that had spoken about me in the press. I felt a little bit let down by that. I will say this, it was never meant to kind of upset or create any kind of animosity towards any of the Barnsley fans at all.”

Barnsley and Wednesday are set to renew their rivalry in League One tonight (November 20).
