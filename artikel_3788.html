How to complete the Schwarzenegger challenge in BitLife

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The latest weekly challenge has landed in BitLife, and this time around, you’re challenged to check off quite a few goals inspired by Arnold Schwarzenegger’s life and career. There’s quite a lot to get done in the Schwarzenegger challenge, with a few tricky goals to check off.

Every challenge you can tackle in BitLife is unique, with lots of tasks you’ve likely never attempted before. If you’re struggling to get through this one, here’s how to complete the Schwarzenegger challenge in BitLife.

There are five key goals to complete in BitLife’s Schwarzenegger challenge:

To get started, begin a new life. Choose male as your gender and Austria as your country when creating this life. You also may want to consider rerolling your character until you end up with fairly high looks stats, as this will make your later goal of becoming a famous movie star much more attainable.

The next goal in this trial is to emigrate to the United States. When you’re ready to make this happen, select the Activities tab in the bottom right corner, locate and select the Emigrate option, choose the United States as your desired country, and request approval.

The third goal in this trial is one you should prioritize working on early on, as you must be consistent with it to get it done. This task asks you to go to the gym 40+ years in a row.

To visit the gym, select the Activities tab, find the Mind & Body section, and choose the Gym option. Before you age up each year, make sure you head to the gym at least once. You need to go for 40 years in a row to get this quest done, so consistency is key.

The fourth goal in this challenge is one of the toughest ones, as you now need to work on becoming a famous movie star. To succeed at this task, it’s a good idea to focus on it early on in life, as there’s a lot you can do along the way to make success more likely.

First, try to increase your looks if you don’t have a fairly high score for this stat. Work on tasks listed under the Mind & Body category, or consider getting plastic surgery to try and raise this number.

Proceed through school, and once you’re old enough, apply to college and major in political science to make becoming a governor later on easier. It’s technically possible without this degree, but it’s much easier to get done if you properly prepare for it.

When you’re ready to get a job, look for a job in the acting field, ideally something like a Voiceover Actor or Extra. If you don’t see any available listings, close and reopen the app or age up to get new options to appear.

Once you land a job in the right industry, give it your all. Consistently select the “Work Harder” option, build strong bonds with your co-workers and boss, and otherwise put in all the work you can. Age up, and eventually, your hard work will be recognized, leading to you becoming a full-on actor.

Keep at it for a few more years, and once you get enough promotions and fame from working hard at this profession, you’ll become a famous movie star. Once you’re in the right field, this goal really just takes time, patience, and a bit of luck, so keep at it even if it ends up taking a bit to achieve fame.

The very last goal in this challenge is to become governor. This goal is a tough one to check off, as you must have the Politician Job Pack purchased to get it done. Unfortunately, there’s no way to enter this career without it. You used to be able to join this career for free, but the option to do so was removed.

When you’re ready to get going on this goal, start off with a lower-level position, like a school board director, to get your foot in the door. Then, move on to a higher one, like the mayor. And once you succeed at that, become governor.

To successfully get elected and run, you’ll need lots of money and smarts. Put in a lot of work earlier in life to ensure the process is easier when you do finally reach this point.

If you’re looking for other tasks to tackle after this one, there are loads of challenges available to choose from in the vault. Some good ones to consider are the Mean Girls challenge, the Gold Digger challenge, the Demon Slayer challenge, the Jolene challenge, and the Manic Mother challenge.
