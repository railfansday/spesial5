terminal design solutions cuts through urban clutter with vivid aluminum street furniture

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

by combining crisp machined aluminum with vibrant anodized finishes, the studio creates benches, lean bars, and shelters that act as bold, functional anchors in the public realm.

John Smith edited by christina vergopoulou November 24, 2026 2 min

Founded in 2026 as a family-run practice working across industrial design, urban planning, and architecture, Terminal Design has introduced a collection of street furniture engineered to regulate circulation and occupation in public spaces. Developed in collaboration with civic partners and refined through full-scale prototype testing, the line encompasses slatted benches, staggered lean bars, barriers, and cantilevered shelters produced in limited runs for municipal plazas, corporate courtyards, commercial streets, and transit hubs. Each component is proportioned around calculated inclines, intervals, and facets that support brief stops while discouraging prolonged lounging, maintaining pedestrian flow in congested zones.

Fabricated from machined aluminum with a directional brushed grain and finished in anodized tones of red, pink, and deep violet, the pieces pair orthogonal geometry with weather-resistant durability. Beyond visual contrast, the studio utilizes the thermal conductivity of uninsulated aluminum as a passive behavioral mechanism: by absorbing and radiating seasonal heat in summer and cold in winter, the metal surfaces naturally limit seating duration without active enforcement.
