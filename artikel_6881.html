Who Is Nicholas Galitzine’s Girlfriend, Cameron Valentina Eyre? Here’s What We Know

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

As an actor, Nicholas Galitzine wears many hats. He’s been the Prince Charming to Camila Cabello’s Cinderella, a himbo jock in Rachel Sennott’s Bottoms, and recently played Prince Adam/He-Man in Masters of the Universe. Another role you may not know about? Nicholas is someone’s boyfriend IRL, as he was photographed walking hand-in-hand with a mystery woman in New York City on November 3.

The woman in question is dancer and model Cameron Valentina Eyre, who he’s been quietly dating for a minute. While neither of them has publicly disclosed the nature of their relationship, I must admit they look really cute together (and this definitely isn’t the first time they’ve been seen together, either—please see their London outing in March 2026). Here are all the facts on Cameron so you can alert your group chats that Nicholas is off the market.

…why wouldn’t she be? Cameron is signed to The Industry Model Management in London and NEXT Management in Paris. According to her portfolio, she’s been featured in Vogue Italia and has modeled in campaigns for Tom Ford, ASOS, and Anastasia Beverly Hills.

Honestly, the lore runs deep. Cameron was part of a girl group called CuteBad, which was only active from 2022 to 2023. They went on an indefinite hiatus when they dropped their last single, “Provocateur,” in April 2023.

According to her IMDb profile, Cameron was a dancer in the movie. The more you know!

Cameron was born on April 16, 2000, making her an Aries. Oh, and fun fact: She shares a birthday with Pope Benedict XVI and Charlie Chaplin, according to this Instagram post. Cool-cool-cool.

While the actor has kept his love life pretty private, he shocked fans when he randomly dropped a photo of him holding someone’s hand on his Instagram Stories in March 2024.

Eagle-eyed fans on Reddit theorized that the mystery hand could belong to Cameron, as she wore the same sweater in an Instagram Story she shared that same day. (Again, this was never confirmed by the pair.)

However! According to an interview with The Cut published that same month, Nicholas confirmed he had been dating someone for about six months.

“I thought the love affairs of my youth had quashed the hopeless romantic in me, but I think that will always remain,” he said at the time, adding that his girlfriend is a “flower person,” and that he realized flowers were “fucking great” that Valentine’s Day.

During a June 2026 appearance on the Therapuss podcast with Jake Shane, Nicholas made rare comments about his relationship with Cameron after he had accidentally posted a pic of her on his main Instagram Story in May.

“That was kind of supposed to be to my Close Friends,” he explained. “Look, anyone who follows me at all knows that I’ve had a girlfriend for a few years, but we like to be private, which I think is just easier in this day and age.”

Private or not, they’re still a really cute couple!
