Ella Rubin on Her Banner Year: ‘It’s Out of Body in the Best Way’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Sterling Point star shares her hopes for season 2 and the watch she’ll have “forever.”

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

It’s a good time to be Ella Rubin. The Sterling Point star has had the breakout year actors only dream of, after her role as Annie Jacobson in the hit Prime Video series launched her to viral heights. “It’s out of body in the best way,” she tells ELLE. “It’s a one-in-a-million chance to get to work as an actor, especially on a show that you love and you’re proud of.” Also on her agenda? An intimate holiday dinner with Chanel to celebrate its watches and fine jewelry.

On an unseasonably warm Tuesday night in New York, the French fashion house hosted ambassadors, friends, and more at Daniel Humm’s new, already hard-to-reserve restaurant, HUMM. As she got ready for her outing, the 25-year-old was as relaxed as ever. In a downtown hotel room, a scene unfolded that any 20-something woman would recognize: “Wide Open Spaces” by The Chicks played on a speaker (a song Rubin calls her “Annie anthem”), a light girl dinner of French fries and soda splayed across a table, and a friend on hand for moral support.

Rubin, an Upper West Side native herself, knows that for a Manhattan night on the town, sometimes less is more. The actress opted for a black tweed jacket—in true Gabrielle Chanel fashion—from look 40 of the Métiers d’art 2026 collection. Paired with a simple white tank and perfectly fitting Levi’s, “it’s my ideal dinner look,” she says.

In terms of accessories, Rubin wore a Chanel Première Ribbon watch, with diamonds elegantly placed around the face. “A beautiful gift,” she says, “and also something you’ll have forever, which is sort of the cornerstone of why I love Chanel jewelry so much, as it’s pieces you’ll know and love, and hopefully, give to someone someday.”

Despite not having her ears pierced, Rubin’s accessories game remains strong. The simplicity of Rubin’s outfit allowed for her jewels to truly shine as she finished off her look with stacked Chanel Fine Jewelry Coco Crush ear cuffs and rings in 18K white gold and diamonds. For glam, makeup artist Shayna Goldberg kept things natural and dewy, starting with a seamless base of Chanel Beauty Les Beiges Healthy Glow Foundation, topped off with the Les Beiges Healthy Glow Natural Eyeshadow Palette and Les Beiges Mascara. The finishing touch? Rouge Coco Longwear Satin Lipstick in 124 Marie and Le Crayon Lèvres Precision Lip-Defining Pencil in Nude Brun.

Below, Rubin reflects on her busy year, plots her holiday gifting list—Chanel included, of course—and reveals who she’d most like to guest star on season 2 of Sterling Point. Someone get Julia Roberts on the phone, stat!

I’m in a really lovely structured jacket, with this high kind of collar, and really beautiful buttons. They’re just so ornate and detailed. I find the jacket to be incredible, [and] my favorite kind of thing to wear. And then, I’m in just some Levi’s and a heel. Because of the high neck, we went with a ponytail, sort of like, a 60’s influence. And then the jewels, part of the reason for the ponytail is because I’m wearing some ear cuffs, which…I don’t actually have my ears pierced. So, I’m really glad that I’m getting to explore that side of jewelry. And then, I love a watch, and I love rings. Those are sort of my staples, my favorites.

It almost feels too late for me. I did it when I was a kid. And they closed up immediately, because I played soccer, and I just wasn’t committed. Now we’re just rocking with it, and it almost feels like, “Okay, this is just where we're at, and where we're staying.” But because Chanel makes such wonderful clip-ons, I get the opportunity to wear lovely earrings now.

Oh, my God. All of them. So many of them. Some of the cuffs I’m wearing tonight, honestly, luckily, they don’t need me to have my ears pierced for them, but if I had to, I would.

Something that’s comfortable, that feels like a part of you, and a part of your outfit. Something where you feel like yourself, it’s an extension of you.

There is this Coco Crush ring that I have now, very, very luckily. It’s on one side, just metal. And then, on the other side, it’s diamonds. And it’s just shiny and beautiful, and lovely, and it feels both, really simple and casual, and also, a party in the back.

That’s a great question. I think a watch is an incredible gift. I’ve had the pleasure of wearing a Chanel watch tonight, and on the show I’m on. It feels just like an incredible staple that, of course, also has utility, and it’s really classic and useful, and a beautiful gift.

It’s just so wild. It’s very surreal. It’s out of body in the best way, but also, I feel unexpectedly grounded, because I’m surrounded by wonderful people, but also, it means I get to work more, hopefully, and be more committed to the work I am doing, and feel more self-assured within it. So that's really the ultimate goal. I just feel unbelievably fortunate to be in the position to keep working. So, I guess what I make of it is just trying to really not let it get old in any way, and keep enjoying myself, and feeling as grateful and in my body as I possibly can, which I do. So it's pretty wonderful. It's solely wonderful, yeah. I’m just going to choose to keep enjoying it, and sharing it with the people I love.

Always Sex in the City, of course. I watch a lot of movies. I recently saw Wild Horse Nine. I think, for fall and winter, going to the theater is the best activity. It’s my favorite thing to do in the world, but especially as it’s getting colder, it’s just an incredibly cozy, wonderful time to go do that kind of thing. I’ve been in the theater almost every single day. Catch me at any AMC or Film Forum.

I’m obsessed with Alison Oliver. Maybe we could discover another sister, and it’s a biological sister, and it’s Annie’s sister. I’m just desperate to work with so many actors. So I’m thinking of Julia Roberts, Viola Davis. I’m naming all my favorite actors in the world. Oh, my God. Susannah Perkins. I just did God of the Woods with them. They’re fantastic. I would do anything to work with them again.

Get ready for more laughing and crying. Season two is still being figured out, so there's so much I don't even know, but I will say I think we're going to only become more intimately close with these characters, and know them more deeply.
