The Beverly Hills Hotel Is Getting Its Biggest Update Since the 1940’s

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Old Hollywood glamour meets contemporary panache in the hotel’s landmark refresh.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE Decor editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Above: The script logo was designed by Paul R. Williams in the 1940s.

“I love the romance of the past,” says Ken Fulk, mastermind of the most significant updates to the Beverly Hills Hotel since the ’40s, when Paul R. Williams created many of its beloved spaces and its pink and green palette. Fulk has refreshed terraces and devised what he calls “the prettiest dining room in all of L.A., a very 1920s library bar, and an over-the-top Copacabana-style supper club,” opening early next year.

An unabashed Hollywood fanboy, Fulk aimed for old-school glamour with contemporary panache. “In my mind,” he says, “Marlene Dietrich and Cary Grant could walk through the lot.”
