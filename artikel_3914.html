There are 80 challenges in GTA 6 split across eight different categories to complete

GTA 6 Challenges are a vital way to reach 100% completion. The challenge system is a key way for you to unlock all of the GTA 6 cheats, and you'll need to have at least 70 of these wrapped up if you want to unlock the GTA 6 Platinum Trophy or 1000 GS too.

The Challenges in GTA 6 are split into eight separate categories, designed to test your abilities and skills in a variety of different ways as you progress through GTA 6. You can complete challenges in any order, and you can wait until you finish all of the GTA 6 missions to pursue these as well. Use these jump links to leap between all of the different categories:

You can check your progress through all GTA 6 Challenges by pressing the touchpad / menu button to open the GTA 6 Index and then using R1/RB to scroll to the Progress tab. In there, you'll find the option for Challenges. When you've completed one, you'll get a tick in the relevant checkbox.
