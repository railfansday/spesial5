Ex-Nvidia boss Jensen Huang offers to return if that's best for the company and America, Nvidia board essentially say 'nah, we're all good'

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

New rulers impervious to highly persuasive message of 'come on, please give me my job back, go USA'

Nvidia's board have indicated that they aren't about to do a big u-turn and reappoint recently resigned CEO Jensen Huang, following his departure after multiple alleged instances of extramarital cavorting were reported by the press. Since his exit last week, Huang has indicated he'd be open to making such a return, were it deemed best for both the company and AMERICA.

"On November 12th, our board of directors announced that after disclosure of personal behavior inconsistent with the company's code of conduct, standards, and cultural norms, Jensen Huang voluntarily resigned his positions as president, CEO, and board member," Nvidia chairman Harvey Jones said during an investors call on November 17th (thanks, Percieving Calls Gamer). "The Nvidia board unanimously accepted his resignation and does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO."

That last bit seems likely to have been said with the intention of quieting down some of the speculation around a Huang return that's been bubbling away since he removed his bum from the black and green throne and that the exec embraced in a statement put out yesterday. "I did not live up to the standards expected of everyone at Nvidia, and I have made serious mistakes in my personal life," he wrote. "I created problems for the Board that I deeply regret. I own my mistakes completely, and I am committed to cooperating with the board’s independent investigation.

"Since my departure, I’ve seen an outpouring of calls from Nvidia’s employees, investors, business partners, and customers for me to return as CEO. Many continue to reach out to me directly as the company navigates complex technology, market, competitive, and geopolitical dynamics. Nvidia is a once-in-a-generation company. If the board of directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honoured to answer these calls and return as CEO."

For now at least, it seems leather jacketed GPUperman will not be triumphantly flying back into his former post amid a wave of corporate and patriotic fervour. Likely as part of continued efforts to reassure moneyfolk that they won't be making negative headlines again soon following Huang's departure, Nvidia's board announced yesterday that they're establishing a "new standing committee responsible for board-level oversight of the company’s national security policies, export control compliance and global trade risk management". One of Huang's alleged affairs was with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy. The Chinese government have denied the accusations of espionage.

Chief financial officer Colette Kress is currently serving as Nvidia's interim CEO while the company's board searches for a permanent sexy leather jacket man replacement.
