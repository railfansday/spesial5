Pikmin 4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy ‘Overview’ trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Nintendo has released a four-minute overview trailer for Pikmin 4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy.

Here is an overview of the game, via Nintendo:

Guide plantlike Pikmin on an enhanced expedition!

Meet Pikmin: small, plantlike creatures you can grow, pluck, guide, and send into battle with creatures multiple times their size! These loyal, curious helpers come in different types, including fire-resistant Red Pikmin, Ice Pikmin that can freeze bodies of water, and Glow Pikmin that can light the way forward at night. The fate of your Pikmin is yours to decide!

Create and customize your own pint-sized explorer and traverse a world that is teeming with life—and visually enhanced with high-resolution graphics for the Nintendo Switch 2 system!

Dandori is the art of commanding your Pikmin effectively, and sometimes explorers like to get in on some friendly competition.

The Nintendo Switch 2 Edition of the game features Dandori Academy, an extensive series of Dandori exam stages for you to test your skill and earn in-game medals! A total of 72 stages will be released over time—and they vary in difficulty, so you can choose the ones that match your skill level.

Pikmin 4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy is due out for Switch 2 on November 12.
