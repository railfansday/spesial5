Fuse Games Reveals What's Coming To Star Wars: Galactic Racer In December Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Why don't you come to my private party? 🎶

The first Star Wars: Galactic Racer update is on the way! Fuse Games has today revealed what'll be included in the Speed Legends update when it arrives in December.

In a post on the official Galactic Racer Twitter account, Fuse Games teased that Speed Legends will bring a whole host of new features to the roguelike racer, including both free and premium content.

Whilst the announcement is quite light on proper details, the key highlights include new characters, events, scenarios, multiplayer ranks and customisation options. Here's the full roundup of new features and free content:

In addition to all the freebies, premium paid content will be included in the update. This looks like the M-68 Landspeeder Icon Vehicle featured in the Solo: A Star Wars Story movie, and three premium speeders inspired by The Mandalorian flick.

All of this sounds good, and to be honest, we're just happy to see a bunch of stuff added so we've got an excuse to hang out with ex-professional model and all-round great guy, Biddy Blas.

If you're liking the sound of Speed Legends but want even more, here's a quick reminder that Fuse Games has already begun working on local split-screen multiplayer, which it's targeting for an early 2027 release.
