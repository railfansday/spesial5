Samsung supplier leak suggests mystery ‘bezel-less’ Galaxy 27 display

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

There are rumors of a Samsung phone with an “extremely bezel-less” display coming from one of the company’s suppliers, and it might be on one of the next Galaxy S27 models.

The information comes from a related supplier for Samsung and was brought up by a known leaker, Lanzuk. Their Naver post specifically mentions an “extremely bezel-less” display. What that means is up for debate, and machine translation does not clarify the original post in Korean.

The big takeaway might be that Samsung is looking to launch one of these bezel-less display options within the Galaxy S27 lineup. An early adoption might indicate that even though this supplier planned on developing these displays for an upcoming Galaxy device, Samsung may want to push the timeline forward and fit it to one of the Galaxy S27 series phones.

Which device would debut a new “extremely bezel-less” display is up in the air. Odds are it would be the most expensive option’s display that gets updated – the Galaxy S27 Ultra. It’s also possible it could show up on both the Pro and Ultra variants, to stretch the premium tag across the upper end of the lineup.

This leak is probably best taken with a grain of salt. The author seems to have doubts, and this seems like a 50/50 shot. But even if Samsung doesn’t shave the bezels off of one of the Galaxy S27 models, this leak still indicates a future device will get a similar display.

That previously planned iteration may even be a better version than anything the Galaxy S27 series could have. More than a proof-of-concept.

The Galaxy S27 series is expected to launch early next year. In the meantime, the Galaxy Z Fold 8 is nearly bezel-less, folds, and is marked down at its best price for the holidays.
