Serbia's student-backed MPs take seats in parliament after election win

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Serbia's Student ⁠List lawmakers entered parliament ​on Tuesday, cheered on by hundreds of supporters, after their October ​election victory ended 14 years of rule by the Serbian Progressive Party (SNS). The Student List, a group that includes academics, professionals, farmers, actors and activists ​chosen ‌by student assemblies, secured 140 seats in the ⁠250-member parliament in October 25 polls, while the SNS gained 104.
