Follow Daily Mail Sport's live coverage for the latest updates of Wednesday's Champions League fixtures, with Liverpool, Arsenal and Manchester City all in action.

You'd expect City to at least get top 16, but they will really be eyeing up top eight to save that play-off round.

And they have some tough fixtures still to come.

Hard to predict what will happen at the minute.

Slavia Prague and the Champions League are a match made in... hell.

By suffering defeat to Arsenal tonight the Czech side have equalled a rather bad record.

They are now on a 23-game winless run in the Champions League (and European Cup). Both AEK Athens (1989-2006) and FCSB (2006-2013) share that record with Slavia Prague.

Will they ever win a match in this tournament?
