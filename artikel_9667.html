Meta's bart-large-cnn was downloaded 1.04M times on Hugging Face in the last 30 days, nearly 4x the next model, distilbart-cnn-12-6 (263.7K). Below are all 120 summarization models in the TextModels directory, ranked by downloads: BART and its distillations, Google's PEGASUS and T5 variants, long-document models and dozens of domain fine-tunes.

Abstractive summarization was the first job transformers took off NLP teams' hands, and the models from that era still do it cheaply. HackerNoon's comparison of crowdsourced summaries against a BART-large model shows why teams keep them around: they're predictable, small and easy to evaluate. Most of this list is BART, PEGASUS or T5 fine-tuned on one dataset, from news (CNN/DailyMail) to dialogue (SAMSum) to science papers.

Everything comes from the TextModels directory as of November 23, 2026, filtered to summarization models (120 entries). Hugging Face download counts are the last 30 days from the Hugging Face API. "Verified" means the directory fetched and confirmed that model's metrics. Hosted models have no public download counts, so they're listed by price or provider, and historical models link to their papers.
