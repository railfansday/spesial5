Juventus, Newcastle and Brentford among clubs chasing Lazio’s Taylor

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Reports in Italy claim that a growing list of clubs are beginning to show an interest in Lazio midfielder Kenneth Taylor, including Juventus, Newcastle United, Brentford and AFC Bournemouth.

The Netherlands international has been a key player and has started in every Lazio match so far this season in a campaign that sees the Biancocelesti sit alone at the top of the Serie A standings, still unbeaten after 11 games of the 2026-27 season.

Tuttosport describes the 24-year-old as a ‘complete player, with characteristics and numbers worthy of the major European stage’.

Tuttosport claims that Juventus are ‘by far the most interested’ club in Taylor as things stand. The Old Lady have been linked with a move for a new central midfielder in January, with reports suggesting that Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg and Sassuolo’s Darryl Bakola could be among the options as well.

Tuttosport adds that several Premier League sides, including Newcastle, Bournemouth and Brentford, are interested in Taylor as well.

Lazio’s stance, for now, is clear, though. The club do not wish to sell key players in January after such a promising start to the 2026-27 season, despite suggestions that Taylor could fetch a fee in the region of €40m. Lazio spent a fee of €17m with bonuses included to sign Taylor from Ajax less than a year ago.

Even though selling Taylor would bring a significant capital gain, Tuttosport suggests that the financial benefits do not outweigh their ‘sporting needs’.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
