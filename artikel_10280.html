Madden NFL 27 and NBA The Run are now free on Amazon Luna

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Amazon Luna is getting another big freebie, as Madden NFL 27 is available today on the cloud gaming platform at no extra cost.

EA’s Madden NFL 27 launched in August and, starting today, is free on Amazon Luna, and also joined by NBA The Run.

Both games are available for free as part of Luna’s Amazon Prime perks, so you’ll need an active Amazon Prime membership to play.

Amazon touts the addition alongside its “MADDEN” movie (starring Nicolas Cage) coming to Amazon Prime Video on November 18.

With the NFL season in full swing and the NBA heating up, there has never been a better time to be a Prime member. Today, EA SPORTSTM Madden NFL 27 and NBA THE RUN join Amazon Luna, bringing the live action and excitement of the gridiron and the basketball court to players at home.

…Now, with Madden NFL 27 and NBA THE RUN joining the Luna lineup, fans have even more opportunities to turn their favorite sports moments into interactive play.

Starting November 18, football fans can also tune-in to the new movie, MADDEN on Prime Video. Inspired by the life of a beloved American icon, MADDEN chronicles John Madden’s incredible journey alongside best friend and true original Al Davis – from a Super Bowl-winning partnership with the Raiders, to becoming football’s most recognizable voice, to building the worldwide Madden NFL video game.

Last month, Amazon Luna added Star Wars: Galactic Racer for free.
