Alisa Viejo, California, November 10th, 2026/CyberNewswire/--One Identity today unveiled Identity Manager 10.0 LTS, modernizing the identity governance experience with browser-based administration, AI-assisted insights and streamlined automation for everyday identity operations.

Of the three products, Identity Manager 10.0 LTS provides the clearest UI modernization story. Its new browser-based experience provides administrative functionality without requiring a traditional local desktop installation, helping create a more accessible and unified cross-platform experience. See the Identity Manager 10.0 LTS feature upgrades.

The experience goes beyond a new look and feel. Administrators can perform core synchronization work for connected third-party systems through the browser and create, edit and monitor automated-task schedules, reducing the amount of repetitive administration required in everyday identity operations.

Identity Manager 10.0: AI and Automation Make Complex Governance More Accessible

The UX modernization extends into how users interact with identity data. AI-assisted reporting lets authorized users query identity information using natural language, reducing dependence on complex SQL when accessing information required for reviews, compliance and audits. [oneidentity.com]

Identity Manager 10.0 also brings risk-score integrations, behavioral-driven governance and custom Identity Threat Detection and Response playbooks into the broader experience, simplifying how teams move from identifying identity risks to remediation.

These developments reinforce Identity Manager's position among top Identity Governance and Administration solutions.

#1 Peerspot Ranking and Kuppingercole Recognition Validate the UX Direction

PeerSpot currently ranks Identity Manager by One Identity#1 in its IGA category, with an 8.4/10 rating across 194 reviews. Its assessment highlights lifecycle management, onboarding, access requests, role-based access, automated provisioning and de-provisioning, customization and SAP integration.

That user-experience direction also aligns closely with the KuppingerCole 2026 Identity Governance and Administration Leadership Compass, which identifies One Identity as a Market and Product Leader. Particularly relevant here, highlighted strengths include a“unified user and admin experience,” alongside mature analytics, workflow capabilities, extensive API coverage, risk-aware access governance and broad connectivity.

Feature in focus: Browser-first administration with Identity Manager 10.0 LTS

Identity Manager 10.0 LTS is the strongest true UI/UX modernization story across the three products, combining a modern browser experience with more accessible synchronization, scheduling, automation and AI-assisted reporting.
