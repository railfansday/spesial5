Your Thanksgiving Turkey Might Be Free If You Shop At These Stores

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A free turkey could be just one shopping trip away.

Delish editors handpick every product we feature. We may earn commission from the links on this page.

Turkey Day is coming, and as the name implies, you’re gonna need a turkey. You’ll want to try our classic oven-roasted turkey recipe for the ultimate Thanksgiving centerpiece. But while getting a hulking 15-pound bird onto the dinner table is one thing, getting it into your home in the first place can be another challenge these days. Luckily, a few companies are trying to make it easier this fall to feed your family the turkey dinner they deserve. I scoured the internet to find you the three best deals on the bird.

Instacart is giving away 50,000 turkeys this November, dropping 10,000 birds at noon each day from Wednesday, November 18, through Sunday, November 22. And the best part? They're basically free.

Instacart is offering $40 off a whole turkey (fresh or frozen), which should cover the cost of the bird. All you have to do is spend $50 on your Thanksgiving basket, turkey included. So, essentially, you’re getting a turkey with your weekly grocery haul.

All you have to do is redeem the promotion on Instacart’s Thanksgiving landing page and get to shopping!

ShopRite has been giving away free turkeys since the ’90s, so they’re pros at making sure you have a bird to put at the center of your Thanksgiving table. And they’re back this year with a Free Holiday Favorite promotion!

If you’re a Price Plus Club card member, you can get a free turkey weighing up to 21 pounds. All you have to do is spend $400 on qualifying purchases between October 25 and November 26, 2026. Just shop for your usual groceries, rack up your savings, and redeem your free bird! And if turkey isn’t your thing, you can also redeem your rewards for other holiday favorites, including ham or chicken.

If you stopped by BJ’s Wholesale Club to shop in the past few weeks, you might already qualify for a free turkey. Check your receipts to see if you spent $150 in a single transaction between October 31 and November 9, either in-store, online, or through the app. If you did, you can head to your local BJ’s and redeem a free Butterball turkey valued at up to $25. You can pick up your bird between November 14 and November 25.
