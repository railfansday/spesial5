PlayStation’s Black Friday sale is officially live until 30 November, and I’ve been comparing prices across PlayStation Direct, Amazon, and Smyths to find the best savings on controllers, headsets, and VR gear.

If you only pick up one thing, the standout deal for most players is the standard DualSense controller at £43 from Amazon — which undercuts Sony’s own £44 sale price. Below are the five PS5 accessory deals I think are actually worth buying right now to beat the Black Friday rush.

Sony’s standard PS5 pad remains one of the best controllers around thanks to its responsive haptic feedback and adaptive triggers. Standard colourways, including white, midnight black, cosmic red, and galactic purple, are down to £43 here, beating PlayStation Direct’s sale price.

Fresh off Robert Pattinson being named the new James Bond, Sony’s 007 First Light collector’s pad feels well timed. Finished with a golden sheen and gun-barrel motif framing Bond’s codename, it packs the same adaptive triggers and haptic feedback as the regular DualSense.

Available to new sign-ups or existing members upgrading their tier, Sony’s top-level subscription unlocks online multiplayer, monthly games, cloud streaming, and hundreds of PS5 and classic PlayStation titles. Just note it auto-renews at £119 fter the first year unless you cancel.

Built with planar magnetic drivers for crisp, detailed 3D audio, this over-ear headset connects to your PS5 over low-latency PlayStation Link while pairing to your phone via Bluetooth at the same time. A retractable, noise-rejecting boom mic rounds out the package.

Sony’s virtual-reality headset plugs straight into the PS5 with a single USB-C cable and includes eye tracking, headset feedback, and two Sense controllers. This bundle throws in a download code for Horizon Call of the Mountain alongside the £90 discount.
