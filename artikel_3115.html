Claros (CMTG) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Loan resolutions and deleveraging took center stage amid lingering macro headwinds.

Claros Mortgage Trust, Inc. (CMTG +66.67%) reported third-quarter results focused on the resolution of watchlist loans, the repositioning of real estate owned assets, and the deleveraging of its balance sheet. Management reported resolving $3.8 billion of loans and reducing indebtedness by $2.9 billion since Jan. 1, 2025. The company is directing proceeds from recent asset sales toward paying down its secured term loan to reduce its highest cost of capital. The company reported that its current portfolio has been reduced to 21 loans and eight REO investments, which management stated increases transparency and reduces variability in book value calculations. While macroeconomic headwinds and elevated interest rates persist, the company has ring-fenced its multifamily exposure and is executing lender-driven price discovery processes to turn over the portfolio.
