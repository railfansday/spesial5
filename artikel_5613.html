Cision Report Reveals the Consumer Trends Hidden Between Search, Social, and AI

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Drawing on an analysis of more than 564 million social media posts and thousands of search queries and AI prompts, the report explores what brands miss when signals tell different stories

CHICAGO, Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Cision, a global leader in consumer and media intelligence, today released The State of Search and Social 2027, a new report examining how connecting search, social, and AI insights can help brands better understand consumer interest, identify emerging business opportunities, and make more informed decisions about where to invest.

"Search can show where interest is changing. Social helps explain why. And AI answers reveal the narrative reaching consumers. Connecting those perspectives helps brands understand not just what's happening, but how to respond, and when to act." - Amy Jones, Chief Marketing Officer at Cision

Drawing on more than 564 million social media posts and nearly 5,000 search queries and AI prompts, the report distinguishes passing attention from sustained interest and explores changing consumer perceptions. It also reveals gaps between what consumers discuss and how AI presents brands and topics.

For marketing and communications teams planning for 2027, the findings highlight why timing matters as much as understanding what is driving consumer attention. Recognizing a trend only after it peaks can mean arriving too late. Connecting signals while interest is building can help brands identify opportunities to act as well as understand when to move and when to change course.

"Some of the most useful consumer insights emerge when you look at the space between channels," said Amy Jones, Chief Marketing Officer at Cision. "Search can show where interest is changing. Social helps explain why. And AI answers reveal the narrative reaching consumers. Connecting those perspectives helps brands understand not just what's happening, but how to respond, and when to act."

Connected signals reveal what's really behind consumer attention

The report examines consumer trends across health and wellness, food and beverages, automotive, AI, and online culture. The findings challenge assumptions that can shape investment decisions, campaign strategy, and reputation management:

What AI answers mean for brand visibility and consumer demand

Brands need to understand not only what audiences are saying and searching for, but what information they encounter in AI answers as well. In the automotive analysis, the most-cited sources were YouTube creators, editorial brands, and individual journalists, not the carmakers themselves. The coverage, reviews, and community conversation a brand earns are now the raw material for the answers its buyers receive. Connecting those perspectives helps teams identify unmet needs, understand potential barriers to purchase, and make better decisions about where to invest and how to communicate.

Move beyond tracking attention to understanding it

The report includes five questions to help teams connect what people search for, what they discuss on social, and how AI answers frame a topic. Teams that continue to measure these signals separately will increasingly misunderstand what consumers are asking, discussing, and being told. Connecting them helps identify gaps in understanding and make more informed campaign, investment, and communication decisions.
