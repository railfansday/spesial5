Crawley Town Fan column: This was a good point, but it was touch and go at times in that second half

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Player to Watch: Vito Mannone (Crawley Town)

Well, that was a tense watch. We definitely rode our luck at times, but I’ll happily take a point from a 0-0 draw with Barnet at the Broadfield Stadium. We put in an impressive defensive display, setting up with a back four with Charlie Barker sitting just in front, which allowed Josh Flint and Ben Heneghan to put in some massive clearances and blocks, which really frustrated Barnet.

Going forward, Kellan Gordon was whipping in some great crosses from the right, with  Dylan Morgan powering through midfield to cause problems, as we looked lively for most of the first half. However, it was Barnet who had the best chances, and if it wasn’t for Vito Mannone, who was great throughout the whole game, as he made some outstanding saves onto the post and crossbar to keep us in the game.

Barnet did turn the screw in the second half and showed why they are near the top of the league, showing their quality with us being unable to get out of our own half as they really pressed us hard. But it was clear they were missing Kabongo Tshimanga up top, as they had plenty of chances that they should have put away.

But that’s 8 points from 12 now for Crawley, with the Red Devils still unbeaten, with this result drawing us level on points with Rotherham and leaving us only two points off Rochdale in 22nd. Next up is Exeter, and hopefully we can keep this run going and climb out of the relegation zone before the week is out. It was a nervy watch, but I'm buzzing with this result.
