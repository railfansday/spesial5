Serie A sensation Gustavo Varela responds to Arsenal transfer links

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Monza star Gustavo Varela has distanced himself from speculation that he could be set for a January transfer move and insists he is ‘paying no attention’ to incessant rumours linking him with Arsenal, Barcelona and Napoli.

Varela, 21, was deemed surplus to requirements at Benfica and completed a switch to newly promoted Italian outfit Monza for a fee of just €2million (£1.7m) – plus performance-related bonuses – in the summer.

It has proven a tough start to the season for Monza on their return to Serie A, with the club picking up just three wins and languishing down in 14th place in the table.

Varela has proven a shining light up front, though, scoring 11 goals in his opening 11 top-flight appearances, and the striker’s barnstorming form in Lombardy has begun to attract the attentions of European football’s elite.

According to recent reports emanating out of Varela’s native Portugal, Barca, Napoli and Arsenal are all actively pursuing a deal for the forward on the back of his goalscoring exploits in the first three months of the campaign.

Premier League champions Arsenal are said to be among a host of Premier League clubs that have ‘expressed an interest’ in Varela and were ‘one of the first to enquire’ about the player’s potential availability.

It’s understood the Gunners are in the market for an attacker of Varela’s profile, with Viktor Gyokeres struggling to live up to expectations following his £64m arrival from Sporting last year.

On Monday night, Varela ensured he remained in the headlines with a terrific brace in Monza’s 3-0 victory over Fiorentina – in doing so, becoming only the third player in the last 50 years to reach the double figures in their first 11 Serie A games.

‘I can’t believe what I’m doing. I go home, close my eyes and I can’t believe it. Football is made of moments,’ Varela told DAZN Italy following Monza’s win.

On his brilliant flicked effort to complete his double, Varela added: ‘It was pure instinct, it’s not something I practice.

‘A very beautiful cross came in, I was close to the goal and I decided to try my luck. If you don’t try, you can’t do it, and it happened.’

Varela was then asked for his reaction the rumoured interest he was receiving from some of Europe’s most high-profile teams, to which he responded: ‘I continue to thank Monza for allowing me to be here. It has all been incredible.

‘I feel lucky to be part of this reality and I only hope to continue on this path.

‘I pay no attention to the news. I only focus on what I have to do on the field. It’s normal, it’s part of football.

‘I’m going through a great moment but a less good moment may arise tomorrow.

‘That’s why I try to simply enjoy the present without paying attention to the news.

‘Sometimes they speak well of me and sometimes they speak badly, but this is the world of football and I only think about what I have to do on the field.’

Monza are believed to have slapped an asking price on Varela in the region of €40-50m (£33-42m).

Though they did not insert a buy-back clause in their deal with Monza for Varela, Benfica will be entitled to 20 per cent of any future transfer fee for the sought-after youngster.

The Primeira Liga club also have the right of first refusal should a bidding war break out for the Portugal Under-21 international, who was named the Serie A Rising Star of the Month for October.

Varela’s current contract with Monza runs through to June 2030.
