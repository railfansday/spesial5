Celtic suffer Baur injury blow

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The 22-year-old is expected to be out for at least 10 weeks; he injured his ankle in Celtic's win against Kilmarnock before the international break; Martin O'Neill's side return to Scottish Premiership action on Saturday at home to St Mirren

Football Journalist @AlisonGConroy

Celtic midfielder Mika Baur faces a lengthy period on the sidelines with an ankle injury.

The German midfielder, who signed from Paderborn in the summer, is facing around ten weeks on the sidelines with the injury.

He left Celtic Park on crutches after he was forced off during their 1-0 win against Kilmarnock before the international break.

Manager Martin O'Neill said: "He'll be out for some considerable time. It's a real shame, he was playing great for us."

"There's talk about ten weeks, but I spoke to Mika yesterday and he's a quick healer. There are things he can't do for a while; his foot's in a boot.

"He was on fire, he was doing great for us. It is a blow and it seemed so innocuous as well.

"I thought he'd been kicked, but it was the other foot he landed on and he's just gone over on it."

Celtic could also be without Benjamin Nygren for Saturday's Scottish Premiership return against St Mirren.

The midfielder missed the second of Sweden's recent double-header, with O'Neill confirming his absence from club training.

He said: "Nygren didn't train today, but we'll see how he is in the morning - he's got a sore heel. He's been feeling it for a couple of weeks."

Celtic face a gruelling five-match stretch of games over the next two weeks, with a visit to Portugal to face Torreense in the Europa League after St Mirren.

They then travel to Hearts, live on Sky Sports, before taking on Dundee at Dens Park, then a home game against St Johnstone.

The defending champions are four points ahead of Old Firm rivals Rangers at the top of the Premiership, having played a game more.
