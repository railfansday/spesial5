Sky Sports News presenter Fulton becomes Kent director of cricket

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Sky Sports News' David Fulton will take over as Kent's new director of cricket at the start of next month; A former Kent captain he will return to the club after representing his home county in a playing career that spanned two decades

Sky Sports News presenter David Fulton will become Kent's new director of cricket next month.

Fulton is a former Kent captain and will take up this new role on December 1.

He will return to the club in a full-time role after representing his home county in a playing career that spanned two decades, and will oversee both Kent men's and women's talent pathways, from age group to first-team level.

Fulton made his Kent first-class debut against Cambridge University in 1992, and went on to make over 300 appearances for the county in all formats, scoring over 14,000 runs.

He was named as the PCA's Cricketer of the Year in 2001, and was appointed as club captain in 2003, after previously leading Kent's County Championship side in the 2002 season.

Following retirement from playing, Fulton embarked on a successful career in the media with Sky Sports, while he also coached within the Kent Talent Pathway and men's first team as a batting consultant, as well as with England Lions.

"I'm extremely honoured to take on the role of director of cricket at my home county," Fulton said.

"Kent has always been a huge part of my life as a fan, a player and a captain, and I can't wait to take up this new challenge.

"I want Kent members and the wider fanbase to be proud of our club and the way we play our cricket. I'm excited to get to work with our men's and women's coaching staff heading into the 2027 season and beyond."
