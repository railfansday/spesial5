Liverpool Confidential squad audit: The NINE stars who could leave Anfield, the latest on each of their futures and what Reds should do with them

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Published: 12:00 EST, 26 November 2026 | Updated: 12:08 EST, 26 November 2026

Liverpool's dressing room has felt like a revolving door of uncertainty in the last five years, with constant doubt about certain players’ futures.

Even when the team won the Premier League title two seasons ago, there was a never-ending subplot about the futures of Mohamed Salah, Virgil van Dijk and Trent Alexander-Arnold.

Now there is ambiguity over Van Dijk again, plus Alisson, Alexis Mac Allister and others, with the depth of Liverpool's squad still an issue to be addressed.

In this week’s Liverpool Confidential, we take a look at all nine players with uncertain futures, reveal what we are hearing about them, and reach a verdict on what we would do if we were sporting director Julian Ward.

Owners Fenway Sports Group have a long-standing unwritten policy not to offer big deals to players over the age of 30, which led to the likes of Gini Wijnaldum and Andy Robertson leaving on free transfers. But they broke this rule two years ago to offer Van Dijk a new deal. Will they do it again?

Liverpool's owners FSG broke their rule on offering 30-somethings big deals two years ago to tie down Virgil van Dijk. They should do it again

What we’re hearing: Van Dijk would be more than open to staying. Sources in Italy say AC Milan have approached him over moving there – but whether he would want to is a completely different story.

Van Dijk is yet to be offered a new deal at Liverpool but this is not a reason for fans to panic. They always bide their time with these things.

Liverpool are carefully managing Van Dijk’s workload for the first time, as evidenced by him being a substitute in Bruges this week. The squad have had a day off on Thursday ahead of returning to training on Friday for the Merseyside derby.

Our verdict: There is a narrative that Van Dijk is starting to show his age. Maybe he is not quite at the extraordinarily high level he maintained in previous years but the skipper is still integral to everything the Reds do. If the two parties can agree on a one-year extension, it would suit everyone. But the Reds must also prepare for life without the formidable Dutchman, whenever that actually happens.

What we’re hearing: The Brazilian wants to stay and is in talks to do so. There are murmurs that he could ask for a big financial package.

Our verdict: Alisson is still in the top three goalkeepers in the Premier League. Like Van Dijk, a short extension might suit everyone.

Liverpool are well stocked in the goalkeeper department, with the club believing Giorgi Mamardashvili is better than he has shown so far. They will soon welcome Belgian 18-year-old Lucca Brughmans, who is rated as one of the best up-and-coming keepers in the game.

But are they better than Alisson right now? No.

Alisson wants to stay and remains one of the top three goalkeepers in the Premier League

The situation: The Argentine has publicly declared he feels let down by the club after he was informed they would not yet extend his contract, and the 27-year-old's deal also runs out at the end of next season.

What we’re hearing: His father and agent Carlos Mac Allister is expecting to hold talks with Liverpool in the coming weeks to set out his demands and may reach a compromise.

Our verdict: Mac Allister has been Liverpool’s best midfielder this season. He has been so good, in fact, that the club should rethink their stance, which they may do with Ward now installed as sporting director.

But there are still 18 months until his deal expires. What if his form falls off a cliff again as it did last season? No one was desperate for a new deal then. Liverpool should bide their time.

He came close in summer 2024 when he was left out of the first matchday squad of the Arne Slot era, and again in 2025 when Marc Guehi was inches away from signing for Liverpool.

Federico Chiesa has rarely been fit while at Liverpool and it feels like it is in everyone’s best interests for the Italian to move on

The situation: The 51-cap Italy winger is not in Liverpool’s Champions League squad and young players like Lewis Koumas and Jayden Danns are ahead of him in the pecking order. The 29-year-old has had just two minutes of competitive action this season.

What we’re hearing: There is a constant background noise of ‘Italian clubs are circling for Chiesa’. An exit seems likely in January, though Chiesa has come close to leaving several times before now and not done so.

Our verdict: Chiesa and Liverpool has just not worked out, despite the Italian becoming a bit of a cult hero last year thanks to some late winners off the bench. He has rarely been fully fit and it feels like it is in everyone’s best interests for the Italian to move on.
