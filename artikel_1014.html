Michael Carrick is clinging onto his job by a thread as he takes charge of Manchester United’s meeting with Roma in the Champions League, desperate for a change of fortunes to save his skin.

United lost again on the weekend as they slumped to a 2-0 defeat at Chelsea, leaving them 17th in the Premier League, hovering above the relegation zone on goal difference.

After surviving initial club crisis talks at Carrington last week, every game now feels like it could be Carrick’s last unless he can manage to turn the tide, with United winless in nine games for the first time in 40 years.

United have just two more matches before the international break - with the visit of Aston Villa following on Sunday – which could bring about judgement day for Carrick.

Follow all the action from Man United vs Roma in our live blog below:

Here’s Richard Jolly inside Old Trafford:

Yesterday, Michael Carrick said it might sound crazy but he was really looking forward to this game. It sounds even crazier now.

The half-time whistle is met with boos inside Old Trafford. Deeper into the pit of despair Manchester United go.

45+2 mins: CLOSE! Soule sets himself and goes for the curler into the top left corner, but it’s just wide. Ever so close to a second for him.

45 mins: SAVE! Lammens gets down well as Malen threatens to make it three, aiming for the far corner.

Two minutes added on. Not entirely sure what Michael Carrick could possibly say to pick his players up here.

44 mins: CHANCE! A first decent opportunity for United is squandered by Fernandes, who spoons over from the pull-back inside the box.

It was a scruffy pass back to the United captain in fairness, and Fernandes is angry that the service wasn’t better.

42 mins: Manchester United have a free-kick in shooting range... maybe they can finally call Vazquez into some sort of action.

Bruno Fernandes steps up and fires into the wall. It comes out to Baleba who then passes it behind his team-mate and out for a throw. That sums up this United side about right.

39 mins: Yoro makes a diving challenge to deny Mora a shot at goal as Roma broke forward after a spell of United possession.

United were in the Roma half but offered absolutely nothing in terms of actual danger to Devis Vazquez’s goal. Vazquez, a reminder, is Roma’s third-choice keeper. He’s been a spectator for nearly 40 minutes.

Here’s Richard Jolly inside Old Trafford:

Manchester United are 24th in the live table, just above the dotted line. Although half the teams have a game in hand and if they go three down here, they drop below Sabah in the standings.

32 mins: Only the away fans can be heard inside Old Trafford at the moment as they watch their side pop the ball around 10 red mannequins.

There is a lack of energy and movement all-round. No wind under these sails.

26 mins: United are trying to produce some kind of response.

On each flank, both Dalot and Amad break into the box and go down claiming a penalty, but the referee isn’t having it. Dalot’s claim was soft and Amad’s just wasn’t a penalty - it was a superb recovery challenge by Wesley.

Baleba also had a crack from outside of the box but leans back and it flies well over.
