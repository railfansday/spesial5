Man Utd to appeal Yoro red card

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Manchester United lost 4-0 to Liverpool in the Premier League on Sunday, with Leny Yoro sent off inside the first eight minutes for denying a clear goalscoring opportunity; the club have informed the FA of their plans to appeal decision; Dermot Gallagher agreed with the onfield decision

Manchester United have notified the FA of their intention to appeal Leny Yoro's red card against Liverpool.

Mauricio Pochettino's side lost 4-0 at Anfield in the Argentine's first game in charge, and his plans went up in smoke as Yoro was dismissed inside the first eight minutes for bundling over Alexander Isak as the striker ran through on goal.

There was some debate as to whether the foul was a clear denial of the goalscoring opportunity, as it was unclear whether Isak was in control of the ball as he made his way towards the penalty area.

Under the laws of the game, one of the considerations regarding a red card for Denying of an Obvious Goalscoring Opportunity (DOGS), is the likelihood of the attacking player "keeping or gaining control of the ball".

United boss Pochettino felt that not only did Isak lose control of the ball with his touch, but also that United should have been given a penalty for Jeremy Jacquet's challenge on Bryan Mbeumo at the opposite end just moments before.

However, former Premier League referee Dermot Gallagher backed the onfield decision in both instances.

"If you look at it from the referee's angle, is it a foul? That's the first step. Yes. Is he in possession of the ball? From that angle, yes.

"I think most people would have expected a red card. It would have been a surprise during the game if a red card hadn't been given.

"I don't know if he would have got to the ball before Lammens. You watch it play out. Do you think it's a red card? Gary Neville's immediate reaction was red card."

On Mbeumo's penalty appeal: "When players are side by side, you can do that. They are side by side. Jacquet was even in front of him."

"The ball was for the goalkeeper. In that situation, if you review the VAR you can see for sure, [Yoro] never had the intention to connect with Isak.

"When the control was long with the striker, the ball was going to the goalkeeper, he runs and goes down. In Leny's case, it wasn't intentional.

"If you look before, on the sixth minute. Jacquet on Mbeumo. Similar? No. If you compare the actions, that was a penalty."

Manchester United midfielder Kobbie Mainoo on the red card:

"The game's been taken out of our hands by a red card that I don't think can be a red card," he said.

"No chance. The ball's already with the goalkeeper. They're both running full speed. What can we do? It is what it is.

"It's 10 vs 11, you come to a place like Anfield, it's a tough game anyway. Then you lose a man, it makes it hard. I can't fault the boys for their fight in the game. We take the pain and move on.

"I wouldn't say it all goes out the window. You can still take the things you did in training into the game, but it makes it 10 times harder with one less man in a situation like that."

Sky Sports' Roy Keane speaking at half-time:

"Isak's not getting the ball. There's no way he's getting the ball.

"It's clumsy from United. Obviously it's one straight ball over the top. You shouldn't be in so much trouble.

"But there's no way he's getting that ball. Absolutely not. I know Isak is quick and he's sharp, but we've looked at it again.

"It's really poor defending. Players not smelling danger. But Isak is not getting that ball. Absolutely not."
