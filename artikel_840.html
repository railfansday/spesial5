Declan Rice agrees new Arsenal contract making him club’s joint highest-paid player

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Deal will run to 2031 and worth about £300,000 a week

Jurriën Timber also agrees fresh contract extension

Declan Rice has agreed a new five-year contract at Arsenal, with the England midfielder poised to match Bukayo Saka’s status as the highest-paid player at the Premier League champions.

Representatives for Rice, who won his 82nd cap in England’s thrilling win over Spain on Sunday, have been in talks for months about extending the deal he signed in 2023 after a record £105m move from West Ham. That is believed to be worth about £250,000 a week and is due to expire in 2028, although Arsenal have the option of a further 12 months.

Rice’s new contract will expire in 2031 and includes a substantial pay rise, rewarding him for his status as one of Mikel Arteta’s key players and taking his salary to about £300,000 a week. Saka signed his new five-year contract last season.

Rice was a driving force in Arsenal ending their 22-year wait to be champions in May, although the second half of his campaign was hampered by back and hamstring injuries. The 27-year-old admitted last week he had an “average” World Cup as a result, although he looked back to his best against Spain.

Arsenal have also agreed a contract extension for Jurriën Timber, whose deal is due to expire in 2028. The Dutchman, recently back from a groin injury that forced him to miss the World Cup, has been one of Arteta’s most consistent performers since joining from Ajax in 2023.

The Arsenal manager committed his future by signing a new four-year deal last month that could take him beyond a decade in charge. Arteta and the sporting director, Andrea Berta, have prioritised securing their top players on long-term contracts over the past year and talks are believed to be ongoing with David Raya and Piero Hincapié.
