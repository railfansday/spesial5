Stuttgart shot-stopper's contract extended

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

VfB Stuttgart have announced that Fabian Bredlow’s contract has been automatically extended by a further year after the goalkeeper reached a specific appearance threshold.

The 31-year-old’s previous deal was due to expire next summer, but now runs until the summer of 2028.

“I’m delighted to be extending my contract by a year,” Bredlow said.

"“Everyone knows how comfortable I feel at VfB and how keen I am to continue contributing to the club’s success in the future.

"All of us in the goalkeeping group drive one another on to perform as best we can while at the same time supporting each other, and I love this kind of environment.”

Bredlow joined Stuttgart from 1. FC Nürnberg in 2019 and has made nine appearances so far this season.
