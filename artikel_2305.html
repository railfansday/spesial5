Scherhant elated after rout of the Netherlands: "It was so much fun"

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Netting a goal in his home city of Berlin, Derry Scherhant could hardly put Germany's 5-0 win over the Netherlands into words.

“What can I say? I don't think it could have gone any better,” the SC Freiburg winger said in his post-match interview with ARD.

"You couldn't have dreamed of anything better, really. It's just unbelievable. It was so much fun and I'm just absolutely delighted.”

Having turned 24 last Tuesday but busy with the DFB squad, Scherhant revealed he felt this game would be the perfect birthday present.

"Absolutely, yeah, there are plans. I'd already manifested beforehand that things would go well, and I've organised something.

A nice meal with all my friends and family, and I'm looking forward to it. I mean, it couldn't have gone any better here in my city.

“To score the opening goal as well, I'm very happy, very proud. And it's just so enjoyable playing football with the lads,” he added.
