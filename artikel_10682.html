Musician responds as countless YouTubers hit with copyright strikes on GTA 6 videos

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Colombian reggaeton artist Frank El Santo has responded to backlash from YouTubers who claimed that their GTA 6 videos have been hit with copyright strikes from him.

After 13 years of waiting, Grand Theft Auto fans have finally been able to jump into GTA 6 and, naturally, YouTubers and streamers want to share their experiences with everyone else.

However, a number of them – including theRadBrad, HollowPoiint, machodgdon, and DarkViperAU – have all been hit with copyright strikes on their videos that claimed they’d been using footage from the reggaeton artist’s own videos of him playing the game.

As DarkViperAU showed, El Santo, otherwise known as Andrés Arango Flórez, has seemingly uploaded his videos to YouTube’s Content ID system, hence them being flagged.

“Yo my dude, you have put your GTA 6 playthrough in the Content ID system, and so are claiming GTA 6 gameplay livestreams. Please fix. Thanks,” he said.

A number of YouTubers claimed it was “deliberate” and that the artist had been “laughing about it” in different comments where he was tagged.

However, as per Polygon, Arango Flórez responded to the situation in the comments of one of his videos, passing the blame onto YouTube’s automated moderation system.

“To everyone who got a copyright claim on their GTA 6 video or stream: I’m really sorry. I’m not filing these claims manually, and I don’t own any rights to GTA 6,” he said, urging creators to dispute the claims.

“Thank you for your patience, and again, sorry for the trouble.”

While GTA has a streamer mode so that creators can avoid strikes on copyrighted music, this situation is different, given the musician has played the game himself.

YouTube’s content moderation system has routinely come under fire from creators, especially as appeal processes are also largely moderated by AI.

The platform has made changes over the years, but the same problems still come up.

“If it wants to use AI for moderation, it should only be able to flag channels internally and then put that up the ladder for a human being to look at,” YouTuber MoistCr1TiKaL said back in December 2025.
