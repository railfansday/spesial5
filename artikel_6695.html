Shop the Best Hoop Earrings—No Jewelry Box Is Complete Without a Pair

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Few jewelry pieces have quite the staying power of a good hoop earring. It’s one of the most essential pieces in one’s jewelry collection, complements almost any outfit, and never goes out of style. Whether you prefer gold, silver, huggies or jumbo hoops, this classic go-to style holds the power to pull together any look. Its appeal lies in the versatility—there’s an appropriate version for every occasion. Pair a simple gold Jennifer Fisher Samira hoop with a white button-down and jeans to polish off your everyday uniform, or add sparkly pavés to your evening look to make a statement.

Jewelry designer Jennifer Fisher, hoop aficionado, has said it’s the one jewelry piece her clients rely on most when wanting to feel more polished. Fisher would concur–there’s no denying the power of a good accessory. She’s played a part in accessorizing some of society’s most influential, from Jennifer Lopez to former first lady Michelle Obama in her coveted hoop earrings. Who better to discuss building a hoop earring collection with than the Queen of Hoops herself?

Fisher recommends starting with small hoop earrings. “Your first pair should be something clean that you can wear morning, noon, and night.” From there, perhaps you gradually add some flair. “Your fifth pair of hoops should be something that has texture and maybe a larger diameter. Something that rounds out your collection.”

According to Fisher, hoop earrings are the blue jeans of the jewelry world–timeless, comfortable, and a wardrobe piece everyone should own. “I always say that hoop earrings are like the perfect pair of jeans–you need a few styles to round out your collection,” Fisher continues. “Your foundation is normally something pretty classic, and then as you grow your collection, your selection should become more diverse. A great example: For your first pair of jeans, you probably aren’t going to buy a wide-leg, long version. You’re probably going to go with a classic straight-leg version, so I suggest the same idea when you’re buying hoops.” Following those building blocks, start with a classic 18k gold huggie as the foundation. Then maybe you branch out to a medium size, perhaps go chunky, try some two-tone mixed metals, or consider a drop earring with gemstones…the list goes on.

No jewelry collection is complete without at least one (or two…or three) gold hoop earrings. Whether you’re building your personal edit from the ground up, contemplating a jewelry gift to self, or simply looking for a few new variations to add to your jewelry box, below is Vogue’s guide to the best hoop earrings on the market.

Huggies are the tiny versions of hoop earrings that sit really close to the earlobe, wrapping around just like a hug (hence the name). In 14K yellow gold, huggie hoop earrings are the perfect low-effort, never-take-off pieces you can shower, swim, and sleep in without much notice or care. They’re also great if you, like myself, love to layer. Add them to your second, third, or fourth hole, or all of the above for a really cool look.

These are the in-between styles that are a bit larger than the huggie hoop style we just discussed, but smaller than the oversized stunner pieces we’ll touch on later. Go for a solid gold everyday hoop or choose something more elaborate—we love the intricate fine jewelry creations from Lucy Delius and Tabayer.

An oversized hoop earring is a classic statement piece and can really add drama to your look. Those with sensitive ear though may want to opt for a thinner pair like these Anita Ko diamond hoop earrings rather than something chunky. If you’re anything like me and want to make a statement, try this silver-plated Rachel Comey pair.

Jewelry is often referred to as art. Some of the most fun hoops aren’t just a circle in a piercing–but perhaps include a twist, a turn, or a spiral that loops around the ear in suspend motion. This sparkly Swarovski pair are amazing for a night out; for everyday wear, we love Charlotte Chesnais’s architectural silver hoop earrings, which curve gracefully the lobe.

A little color goes a long way. Whether it’s a fun mustard yellow leather like this So-Le Studio hoop, or a vibrant emerald-adorned Anita Ko pair, you can comfortably skip a necklace when you’re rocking a statement hoop.
