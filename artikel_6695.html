Office Hours: Saie’s Laney Crowell on the Beauty of Doing Good

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

For Laney Crowell, “feel good, do good” is not just the core philosophy of her beauty brand, Saie, but how she lives her life. “So much of beauty is not only who you are on the inside, but the actions that you take,” she says, emphasizing “the importance of taking action, especially in things that you are excited and passionate about.”

The driving force behind Saie was Crowell’s growing desire to make beauty better by creating formulas that are not just safe for your skin but also good for it. She wanted to create products that spoke to her as a self-professed “expert consumer.” “I’ve always struggled with really sensitive, acne-prone skin,” she says. “I had an aha moment where I realized that my makeup was what was making me break out. I started a blog [called The Moment] to have the conversation. It was niche, but at the same time, I was one of the only people having this conversation.” Saie was born from this community building seven years ago.

The brand has steadily expanded since then, without ever straying from its mission. Each new product follows the same principles: It’s formulated to nourish the skin, is noncomedogenic, and is clinically tested and dermatologist tested. “When you try Saie, you immediately feel the textures and how hydrating they are. Then as you continue to use them, you realize how happy your skin is. You’re really seeing benefits over time.”

Saie’s innovations, however, haven’t come without growing pains. Working with a long list of ingredients to avoid, including silicones, the team has found that developing long-lasting products takes a long time. “I only ever want to launch something if it’s perfect,” Crowell says. Take its City Set setting spray, for example. When it debuted in December 2025, no setting spray claimed 16-hour wear.

“I make beauty products for women who get ready in five minutes, just like me,” says Crowell, who is gearing up to solve another customer request. Saie’s next launch, CityStick, took two years to perfect and is set to debut later this year. “I’m always on the go, so I wanted something I could throw in my bag, swipe on for a bit of radiance, and know I was also doing something good for my skin. That’s CityStick—my makeup and skin care in one quick step.” It’s 50% makeup, 50% skin care; expect the sheer, soft-focus, radiant coverage of a skin tint with the benefits of nourishing skin care.

Helming a cosmetics brand—not to mention pioneering an entire category—requires vision and tenacity, two things Crowell has in equal measure. The last seven years have shown her just how resilient she is. “The most important attribute of a founder and entrepreneur is the ability to keep going,” she says. The other thing she’s learned? How much she loves to improve. “I am always thinking about how I, the brand, and everything that we do get 1% better every day.”

Beyond formulating, leading, and championing a host of climate initiatives (the brand has Planet Beautiful, an annual sustainability and plastic-recovery campaign in partnership with Sephora and RePurpose Global), Crowell is also a mother of two. She’s as passionate about Saie’s commitment to our planet (read: making systemic change regarding the billions of units of plastic the beauty industry generates each year) as she is about showing up for her two little girls.

“My weekdays are crazy, but I love it,” she says with a laugh. Days at her home upstate start with a 6:30 a.m. matcha (Rocky’s ceremonial-grade matcha with steamed oat milk and simple syrup she makes herself) and a workout (weight training or Pilates) before the kids wake up. After getting them ready and taking them to school (her favorite part of the day), she’ll make another matcha, then settle in for a marathon of Zoom calls before spending the evening with the kids. She comes into the city once a week to meet with her team in person; The Mercer is her home away from home.

As an entrepreneur, she’s keen to take care of herself by consciously putting herself first, including upholding her boundaries. “When you do something that you really love, I think that is also a really beautiful commitment to yourself,” she says.

Having started her business at the tail end of 2019, Crowell worked tirelessly during the pandemic, when she also gave birth to her second child, Rosie. “I think back on the success of Saie, and a big part of it is that for two years I basically worked the amount that most people do in, like, six years,” she says. Her days were a balance of work and breastfeeding, and she only really left the house to go for walks. “After that, my immune system was a mess,” she adds. Working with holistic coach Jill Ketner Villa was her saving grace. Years later, Crowell says Villa is her “secret weapon” as a founder. Through her work with the practitioner, she’s developed her supplement ritual; she takes approximately 15 a day. Her assortment includes magnesium, zinc, iron, and vitamins C, A, and D—all housed in a beautiful leather pill case.

She’s also been meditating for 12 years. Nowadays, she loves Gabrielle Bernstein’s guided meditations, which last 10 to 20 minutes. “I get a lot of clarity out of it, and I find it to be the best way to calm anxiety.” Sleep, of course, is another tranquil practice; practice is the operative word because Crowell does a lot to ensure she gets a good night’s sleep. She takes magnesium or CBD before bed and uses the Truvaga device to stimulate her vagus nerve system and regulate her circadian rhythm. “Then I have my humidifier, face mask, earplugs, always a thick lip balm—that usually does it.” (The humidifier also makes its way to her weekly stays at The Mercer.)

Otherwise, she says going on a walk in nature helps her be her best self. “If I can fit a walk in between Zooms, that is a good day. I have my best ideas on walks.”

Crowell’s skin philosophy is appropriately minimalist, largely to suit her sensitive, dry, acne-prone skin. “I don’t use a ton of products, and I don’t use anything that’s super active. Generally, ‘less is more’ is what’s right for my skin, with a heavy focus on hydration.” As someone so knowledgeable and passionate about formulation, she’s gotten her skin-care regimen down to a science and has a couple of brands she knows work well for her skin.

First is Medik8, which she turns to for a suite of products: cleansers, a hydrating serum, and its Crystal Retinal. “Their retinol is the only retinol I can use. I use either level one, two, or three. I don’t really go above that. I also really love Sofie Pavitt’s line—it’s really wonderful if you have acne-prone skin.” Specifically, she gravitates toward the Omega Rich Moisturizer, Clean Clean Cleanser, and Mandelic Clearing Serum to spot-treat breakouts. Otherwise, she loves the Dr. Barbara Sturm toner for extra hydration, Alloy’s estrogen-powered face cream to boost skin’s plumpness, and Colorescience’s tinted mineral sunscreen if she’s skiing or in the sun, though her daily SPF is Saie’s Slip Tint. For extra protection, you’ll likely find her in a hat.

Crowell likes to keep her makeup routine quick. “I am not a makeup artist,” she says. “So all of our formulas are easy and effortless. I really love looking like myself.” Her five-minute regimen always starts with Saie’s Glowy Super Gel, which “gives [her] the most beautiful lit-from-within glow,” followed by Slip Tint for moisturizing coverage with sun protection. After a bit of Slip Tint concealer and a thoughtful dusting of the collection’s setting powder, she moves on to blush. At night she might layer the liquid Dew Blush and the powder SuperSuede blush, but for daytime it’s one or the other paired with the SuperSuede Baked Bronzer. Next, she brushes up her brows with gels by Kristie Streicher or Rare Beauty. Then she adds a mist of City Set setting spray, a few coats of Mascara 101, and her go-to lip combo (lip liner in shade Flirt, plus Glossybounce lip oil in shade Dip) for the finishing touches.

Hydration is key to keeping her thick, long brown hair, which can sometimes be dry, looking its best. “I don’t get a lot of haircuts, so I’m always hydrating the roots,” she says. Roz’s Milk Hair Serum comes with her everywhere, as does the brand’s Santa Lucia Styling Oil. “I really love her formulas,” she says. K18’s post-shower leave-in delivers molecular repair, while Arrey’s Not Today, Grey supplement slows early graying and supports fullness. “It’s 100% made a difference. When I stopped taking them, I noticed it.”

Another product she doesn’t leave home without is Dae’s styling wand, which comes in handy for taming flyaways. For her beloved beach waves, she swears by T3’s curling iron (specifically the 1-inch and 1.5-inch barrel sizes) and a bit of Dae’s styling cream.

Crowell is as thoughtful with her style as she is with her skin care and makeup. “I definitely believe in quality over quantity, so I wouldn’t say I buy a lot,” she says. When she does shop, however, she always asks herself if the item fits her “’90s, a little sporty, and minimalist” ethos. That said, she gravitates toward pieces from The Row, Khaite, and Kallmeyer or really special vintage finds. Most days, you’ll find her in jeans, a T-shirt, and Adidas sneakers—or Manolos to dress things up.
