The Best Celebrity Watches From the GQ Men of the Year Red Carpet 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Tonight, GQ hosts its annual celebration of the biggest, most impactful, and superlatively stylish Men of the Year at the Chateau Marmont in Hollywood. In 2026, of course, no great outfit—and no great man—is complete without a wristwatch. Naturally, for the liveliest menswear party of the year, attendees are bringing out their very best timepieces.

The dress code is “modern red carpet,” and attendees have found creative ways to interpret that on their wrists. Performer of the Year Andrew Garfield, who’s been aggressively auditioning new wristwear recently, is wearing the brand-spanking-new Breguet Classique in platinum, a watch that leans all the way into simplicity with just a single hand to read both the hour and minutes. Skyler Gisondo threw down with the ultimate-classic Rolex Submariner. Michael Rider, always attuned to what’s trending in the watch world, is in a dainty Cartier Tank.
