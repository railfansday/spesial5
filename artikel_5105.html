Solana to $300B? Bitwise CIO Says Tokenized Stocks Could Drive 7.5X Growth

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Bitwise CIO Matt Hougan says Solana could reach a $300 billion valuation in five years if it captures 10% of global stock trading through tokenization.

Bitwise Chief Investment Officer Matt Hougan is betting that tokenized stock trading will make Solana one of the largest networks in crypto. In a Real Vision interview published Tuesday, he said Solana could be worth $300 billion within five years if it handles 10% of global stock trading.

He called that “a completely reasonable valuation.” Solana’s market value today is about $40 billion, so his target is roughly 7.5 times higher.

Solana’s own fees show how far the network still has to go. It collected $229 million in transaction fees over the past 12 months, about 1/33 of the revenue in Hougan’s scenario.

SOL trades near $67.81, down 77% from its January 2025 record and 2.7% lower on the day.

A tokenized stock is a blockchain token that tracks a share such as Nvidia or Tesla. It can trade around the clock and settle in seconds.

Hougan was less upbeat on Ethereum, which still holds more tokenized assets overall. He said some ETH holders are “probably going to step out of the asset,” adding: “We’ll see which ends up being more valuable.”

For Hougan’s forecast, the size of today’s market matters. It is growing, but it starts small. The rwa.xyz stocks dashboard counted $3.57 billion of tokenized shares in investors’ hands on Wednesday, held across 5 million wallets.

That last number puts Hougan’s goal in perspective. Ten percent of global stock trading comes to about $2.5 trillion a month. Even counting every token transfer as a trade, tokenized shares on all chains are at about 4% of that.

The key test for Hougan’s case is fees, because his math rests on them. We added up the base and priority fees users paid on the network, using DefiLlama’s Solana fee data.

January 2025 alone, the month of the TRUMP memecoin launch, brought in $241 million, and that memecoin frenzy has faded. Trading in tokenized shares would need to replace it many times over for Hougan’s math to work.
