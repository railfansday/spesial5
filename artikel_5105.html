Kraken’s Dave Ripley: “That Was the Bear Market” and Bitcoin Is “On Our Way Out of It”

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Kraken’s Dave Ripley explains why Bitcoin’s rebound past $80K hints the bear market is over, marking the softest macro pullback in the asset’s history.

Is the Bitcoin bear market already over? Kraken co-CEO Dave Ripley says the move back above $80K makes him think “that was the bear market,” and that it may be the softest bear market in Bitcoin’s history. He explains how retail and institutional customers are approaching Bitcoin right now on the Bitcoin Magazine Podcast with Spencer Nichols.
