Veteran Analyst Sounds Alarm Over XRP Price Manipulation, But Sees a Monster Bull Run Coming — Here’s Why

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

XRP’s price action may be frustrating investors, but popular crypto analyst Dan Gambardello believes the current market turmoil could be hiding a much bigger opportunity.

While warning that repeated manipulation is keeping the Ripple-linked crypto trapped in a cycle of familiar price movements, Gambardello maintains that the broader crypto market is eventually heading toward an explosive bull run. His latest assessment raises questions about how long XRP holders may have to endure the current market games before bullish momentum finally takes over.

In his latest commentary, Dan Gambardello pointed to XRP’s hourly price chart as evidence of what he considers repeated market manipulation. According to him, the token’s short-term price movements have become increasingly predictable, with similar patterns playing out repeatedly rather than allowing a sustained directional move to develop.

Describing the situation as a case of “rinse and repeat,” the analyst suggested that market participants are dealing with recurring price tactics that continue to frustrate XRP traders. This pattern, in his view, reflects the challenging conditions crypto investors face as they wait for broader bullish momentum to return.

Gambardello’s remarks come on the heels of XRP’s notable rebound on November 23, when the cryptocurrency briefly reclaimed the $1 mark after enduring several consecutive sessions of downward pressure.

The rally initially gathered momentum from around $0.94, with buyers pushing XRP toward $0.99 before a fresh wave of buying lifted it above $1.02. However, the upward momentum quickly faded. XRP struggled to hold above the psychological $1 threshold as renewed selling pressure dragged the token toward $0.95 within hours. At press time, XRP had slipped to approximately $0.9176, marking a 10.04% drop from its intraday peak of $1.02.

Notably, his warning about XRP’s current price behavior does not undermine his longer-term optimism.

Despite his concerns about XRP’s recent market movements, Gambardello remains firmly optimistic about the crypto market’s long-term prospects. He believes the industry is eventually heading toward a meteoric bull market that could launch a powerful rally across digital assets.

The pundit’s outlook suggests that the current wave of volatility and alleged market manipulation should not be mistaken for the end of the broader bullish opportunity. While investors may have to contend with repeated price swings and frustrating market conditions in the near term, he expects the crypto market to eventually regain momentum and deliver a much stronger upside move.

For XRP holders, this presents an interesting contrast between the token’s current struggles and the analyst’s longer-term expectations. Nonetheless, Gambardello has not provided a specific timeline or price target for when the anticipated bull run could commence.
