XRP’s Native Chain First To Clear CertiK’s GL1 Verification

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Home » Ripple (XRP) » XRP’s Native Chain First To Clear CertiK’s GL1 Verification

XRP Ledger’s node operators’ self-attestations were assessed using a new gold standard in blockchain security.

CertiK has completed independent technical verification of self-attestations submitted by the XRP Ledger Foundation and Partior under the Global Layer One (GL1) Market Infrastructure Toolkit.

The toolkit supplies a common set of controls for assessing digital financial infrastructures. It draws on international standards and industry practices and was developed under the GL1 initiative involving financial institutions and policymakers.

The next phase is now being advanced by the Global Layer One Organisation, a Singapore-based non-profit founded by HSBC, Kinexys by J.P. Morgan, MUFG Bank, Societe Generale-FORGE, and Standard Chartered.

CertiK reviewed all 112 controls in the toolkit to determine which ones could be checked through independent technical methods. It then examined the network operators’ self-attestations against supporting evidence that included code, configurations, logs, and test results.

The work focused on the Architecture and Resiliency principles.

As tokenized-asset activity increases, clearer visibility into how infrastructure controls are actually implemented can help institutions evaluate operational and risk-management fit. Independent verification adds a layer of evidence beyond the operators’ own statements.

“Institutions developing and expanding tokenized asset services need to understand how the controls supporting their infrastructure have been implemented,” said Esme Pau, CertiK’s Head of Capital Markets and Policy.

“Independent technical verification helps operators substantiate their self-attestations and gives institutions evidence they can weigh in their own infrastructure assessments.”
