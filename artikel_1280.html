Serie A | Juventus 4-0 Venezia – Super-sub Woltemade inspires Bianconeri to victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Nick Woltemade had an immediate impact for Juventus, helping the Bianconeri secure a 4-0 home win over struggling Venezia with one goal and one assist after coming on from the bench.

Juventus beat Venezia 4-0 in Turin on Sunday, scoring all four goals in the second half.

See how the game unfolded on the live blog.

Edon Zhegrova was the big surprise in the Juventus XI as the Kosovo international started a Serie A match for the first time in his career. The ex-Lille star was arguably the most dangerous Juventus player in the first half, but Venezia had the best chances before the break.

The first clear chance fell to summer signing Akor Adams. Just five minutes in, the striker found plenty of space after receiving the ball just behind the halfway line and drove towards Guglielmo Vicario’s goal. However, a heavy touch as he prepared to shoot allowed Gleison Bremer to recover, with the Juventus defender tracking back brilliantly to block Adams’ effort.

Zhegrova had a good chance a few minutes later, nutmegging USMNT international Gianluca Busio on the left flank and trying to score with a curler at the near post, but Filip Stankovic saved it. Busio later earned a corner kick for Venezia when Vicario deflected his shot from distance.

Juventus’ best chance came in the final minute of the first half, when Weston McKennie headed a Douglas Luiz corner kick from the left, but didn’t find the target.

Juventus were booed by their fans when the referee blew the half-time whistle, but it triggered the team’s reaction after the break.

Nick Woltemade was introduced to replace the injured Kerim Alajbegovic, and the Newcastle loanee made an immediate impact, scoring and assisting in 15 minutes.

The German broke the deadlock in the 53rd minute, pouncing on a McKennie shot deflected by Juan Jesus inside the box and beating the onrushing Filip Stankovic to score his second consecutive goal in Serie A.

Just five minutes later, Teun Koopmeiners won the ball high up the pitch, linked up with Woltemade inside the box and found the net with a curler at the far post.

Juventus’ two goals entirely changed the energy inside the Allianz Stadium. Juan Cabal scored the third goal for the home side, outjumping the Venezia defence from a short corner performed by Koopmeiners and Zhegrova.

Five minutes later, Randal Kolo Muani earned a penalty by outsprinting Halhal on the left flank; the Venezia defender slid in inside the box, but didn’t get the ball. Woltemade took the ball in his hand and seemed ready to take the spot-kick, but then gave it to Kolo Muani, who scored his second Serie A goal this season.

Venezia had one final chance when John Yeboah struck the crossbar with a curler from the edge of the box, but Juventus had already put the game to bed.

The victory allows the Old Lady to climb to sixth, still five points below fourth-placed Como, their next opponent in Serie A.

Meanwhile, Venezia remain 19th with just six points from 12 matches and one win in their last five.
