Skydance Cable Shakeup: Betsy Sanner Ayala, Jason Sarlanis, Tina Perry & Michael Ouweleen To Oversee Programming; Howard Lee Exiting

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: Skydance has restructured its cable network division and named four executives to oversee programming, weeks after bringing together Warner Bros Discovery and Paramount.

The company has formed Skydance Global Cable Media, which will include such brands as Adult Swim, BET, Comedy Central, Discovery, Food Network, HGTV, ID, MTV, TLC and Nickelodeon.

This comes after Channing Dungey was handed an expanded role to oversee all of the cable networks, having previously overseen the WBD networks in addition to the television studio. She now is Chair, Skydance Global Cable Media in addition to running Warner Bros Television Group.

The new structure will see the programming teams divided into four: Unscripted Lifestyle; Broad-Based Entertainment; Talent, Music and Live; and Comedy and Animation. Watch on Deadline

Betsy Sanner Ayala, who is President and Head of Content for Food Network, will oversee Unscripted Lifestyle and now also Discovery, HGTV and Magnolia, with Joe Boyle continuing as Head of Content for Discovery, reporting to Sanner Ayala.

Jason Sarlanis, President and Head of Content at TNT, TBS and ID, will oversee Broad-Based Entertainment and also take on oversight of TLC, with Alon Orstein continuing as Head of Content for TLC reporting to Sarlanis. Sarlanis has been with the company for around a decade and actually started as SVP Development at TLC.

Tina Perry, President and Head of Content at OWN, will oversee Talent, Music and Live. She is adding MTV/VH1 and BET to her portfolio, coming a day after BET President Louis Carr stepped down.

Sitarah Pendelton will continue as Head of Content for MTV/VH1, Brian Rikuda will serve as Head of BET, and Aisha Summers-Burke will continue as Head of BET Content. All report into Perry.

Adult Swim boss Michael Ouweleen will oversee the Comedy and Animation group.

He will oversee Skydance Television Animation — which includes Nickelodeon and Nickelodeon Animation Studios, Adult Swim, Cartoon Network, Warner Bros Animation, Cartoon Network Studios and Hanna-Barbera Studios Europe — and also will have day-to-day oversight of Cartoon Network and Adult Swim. Peter Girardi and Alec Botnick will oversee the studio assets of Nickelodeon Animation Studios, Warner Bros Animation, Cartoon Network Studios, and Hanna-Barbera Studios Europe and report to Ouweleen, with Botnick also overseeing programming on Nickelodeon along with Laurel Weir.

The animation divisions will continue to sell to third-party broadcasters and streamers.

Comedy Central now also will report to Ouweleen, with Ari Pearce running The Daily Show network day-to-day.

As part of the restructure Howard Lee — who was Chief Creative Officer, U.S. Networks — is exiting the company. He has been in that role since the end of 2024, but his recent day-to-day focus was overseeing HGTV.

Dungey just informed staffers of the news in a memo, seen by Deadline. Read it in full below.

“The breadth and quality of programming this team delivers is truly unmatched, from Adult Swim’s Rick & Morty to TLC’s 90 Day Fiancé franchise, from Discovery’s Puppy Bowl and Shark Week to The Soul Train Awards on BET and The VMAs on MTV, our brands are bigger than television ,” she wrote. “They are part of our audiences’ daily lives in meaningful and unique ways, and I look forward to stewarding their legacies into the future alongside you”.

Hi Team,As we bring together our renowned cable brands across Warner Bros. Discovery and Paramount, I’m pleased to share an organizational update that will help us leverage the full strength of our combined portfolio and position us for future success.Our newly formed group will be called Skydance Global Cable Media and will encompass a premier roster of 50+ cable networks, including such iconic brands as Adult Swim, BET, Comedy Central, Discovery, Food Network, HGTV, ID, MTV, TLC, and Nickelodeon, among many others. Together, these brands deliver culture-defining entertainment, compelling kids and family programming, and leading lifestyle content to audiences around the world.The breadth and quality of programming this team delivers is truly unmatched, from Adult Swim’s Rick & Morty to TLC’s 90 Day Fiancé franchise, from Discovery’s Puppy Bowl and Shark Week to The Soul Train Awards on BET and The VMAs on MTV, our brands are bigger than television. They are part of our audiences’ daily lives in meaningful and unique ways, and I look forward to stewarding their legacies into the future alongside you.As we look ahead, our focus is clear: create exceptional stories, unlock the full potential of our programming, franchises and brands, and deepen our relationship with audiences. The quality and reach of our programming, together with the extraordinary talent across our organization, give us a strong foundation for what comes next.To help achieve these strategic goals, we have created four distinct segments within the group. I’m pleased to announce our new structure and creative leadership for Skydance Global Cable Media:Unscripted Lifestyle: Betsy Sanner Ayala, currently President of Food Network, will continue in that role and also oversee Discovery, HGTV, and Magnolia. Joe Boyle will continue as Head of Content for Discovery, reporting into Betsy.Broad-Based Entertainment: Jason Sarlanis, currently President of TNT, TBS, and ID, will continue in that role, and will also oversee TLC. Alon Orstein will continue as Head of Content for TLC, reporting into Jason.Talent, Music, and Live: Tina Perry, currently President of OWN, will continue in that role and add BET and MTV/VH1 to her purview. Sitarah Pendelton will continue as Head of Content for MTV/VH1, Brian Rikuda will serve as Head of BET, and Aisha Summers-Burke will continue as Head of BET Content, all reporting into Tina.Our fourth network group will be comprised of Comedy and Animation. Skydance Television is now the home of some of the most incredible animation networks and studios in the industry, including Nickelodeon and Nickelodeon Animation Studios, Adult Swim, Cartoon Network, Warner Bros. Animation, Cartoon Network Studios, and Hanna-Barbera Studios Europe. I’m pleased to say that all these beloved assets will now be together as part of Skydance Television Animation, and this group will be overseen by Michael Ouweleen, President. Michael will also continue with day-to-day oversight of Cartoon Network and Adult Swim. Reporting to Michael and leading the work overseeing the studios assets (Nickelodeon Animation Studios, Warner Bros. Animation, Cartoon Network Studios, and Hanna-Barbera Studios Europe) will be Peter Girardi and Alec Botnick. Alec will also continue to oversee programming on Nickelodeon along with Laurel Weir. In addition, Comedy Central will roll up to Michael, with Ari Pearce continuing day-to-day oversight of the network.I’m excited about this new structure and feel confident that this incredible team of leaders will help defend our core business while building out a multi-platform strategy for these brands that will allow us to reach audiences wherever they may be. We are continuing to work through the process and will share more about the international business in the coming weeks.One of the hardest parts of any transition is saying goodbye to talented leaders who have contributed so much and, just as importantly, have been trusted colleagues, friends, and mentors. I want to express my sincere thanks to Howard Lee for his excellent leadership. I’m deeply grateful for the immense talent, commitment, and passion he brought to Warner Bros. Discovery’s US Networks over the years and wish him the best in his future endeavors.I know we have a lot of work ahead and I am excited to get started. With our unrivaled portfolio of brands, incredibly talented teams, and shared passion for making great TV, there is nothing we can’t achieve together.
