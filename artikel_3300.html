Will Packer Launches Microdrama Studio June With NBCU Backing

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Will Packer is launching a microdrama studio, with NBCUniversal (NBCU) among the backers.

June is being billed as an “entertainment and content” business that will develop, produce, finance and acquire original scripted microdramas designed for Black audiences. Culturally-relevant storytelling for mobile users will be the focus.

Sherrese Clarke’s HarbourView Equity Partners and NBCUniversal are investing in June, and will provide resources and strategic support. Will Packer will serve as CEO and his son, Dom Packer, will serve as President, overseeing strategy, operations and growth. This is the latest example of a Hollywood studio backing a vertical video venture, with Fox Entertainment Group owning a stake in Ukraine-based Holywater and influential independent players launching their own businesses, as the U.S. microdrama boom deepens. Watch on Deadline

Will Packer has produced movies with a combined gross of over $1B. He’s worked with Universal Pictures several times on the likes of Girls Trip, Night School and the Ride Along franchise, as well as exec-producing Universal Television’s Fight Night: The Million Dollar Heist, theb Peacock’s original series.

“Will has a seasoned instinct for stories that resonate with audiences and June is an opportunity to pair that creative vision with a format built for how viewers are engaging today,” said Liz Jenkins, Chief Business Officer, NBCUniversal Entertainment.

“Our strategy isn’t about chasing a format, we always start in the same place: with the story, the storyteller and the audience. This is exactly the kind of creator-first partnership we want to build, working with storytellers to evolve thoughtfully, learn quickly, and aim to define the future of mobile-native storytelling.”

“I’ve always believed in telling authentic stories that meet audiences where they are, and with June, we’re building a home for those stories alongside Sherrese Clarke, her expert team at HarbourView and my longtime trusted partners at NBCUniversal,” said Will Packer.

“The name captures the warmth and romance of summer and celebrates Black freedom and culture through Juneteenth and Black Music Month. That spirit of joy, connection and pride is at the heart of what we’re creating.”

“Vertical entertainment offers an intimacy and immediacy that can make culturally resonant storytelling especially powerful,” said Clarke, founder and CEO of HarbourView, which will primarily. “Will and Dom’s vision for June puts the experiences and perspectives of an audience that has helped shape music, entertainment, and culture around the world at the heart of this category, and HarbourView is proud to help bring it to life, empowering a studio that can create stories and connect them to the brands that want to reach this audience.”

June’s first slate is being developed with Black women at its core, pairing the romance, heightened drama and cliffhangers common to microdramas with culturally-resonant storytelling that Will Packer’s film and television work includes.

June flagged several reports to back its launch, including a Pew Research Center survey that showed 51% of Black adults in the U.S. use TikTok, compared with 37% of all U.S. adults, and a Nielsen report from 2025 that found Black adults spend more than 23 hours a week on smartphone apps and websites.
