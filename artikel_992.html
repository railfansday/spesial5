Florian Wirtz stars as Germany hammer Netherlands to top Nations League group

Wirtz scored twice in a thumping 5-0 win as Germany, Netherlands and Greece finished level on 11 points in Group A2

Florian Wirtz scored twice as Germany hit four goals in 18 second-half minutes to thrash the Netherlands 5-0 in Berlin and reach the Nations League quarter-finals as group winners. Germany, the Netherlands and Greece all finished on 11 points in Group A2 after substitute Giorgos Masouras scored in the 69th minute to give Greece a 1-0 win over Serbia at home.

The Dutch advanced as runners-up and Greece finished third while Serbia ended the campaign without a point. It was the Netherlands' ​first ⁠defeat since Xavi Hernandez took charge in August. The Dutch had beaten Greece 7-0 three days earlier and needed a draw in Berlin to win the ⁠group.

Jurgen Klopp’s side entered Monday’s match third in the Group A2 standings and Derry Scherhant scored his first international goal with a stunning top-corner finish to put the hosts in front at the break. Germany then put in a ruthless second half display as Wirtz bagged a brace, while Nico Schlotterbeck and Brajan Gruda also got on the scoresheet to complete a stunning victory.

"We always hope we can give the fans a lot of enjoyment. And we enjoyed it very, very much ourselves, especially in the second half," Wirtz said.

Donyell Malen had a chance to put the visitors ahead but headed over from close range. Scherhant hit the post at the other end before cutting in from the left in the 41st minute and firing into the top corner for his first international goal. The 24-year-old Freiburg winger grew up ‌five minutes from the Olympiastadion, where he made his ‌Bundesliga debut for Hertha Berlin.

"What can I say? I don't think it could have gone any better. I couldn't even have dreamed of a better outcome. It's truly unbelievable. I had an incredible time out there and I'm just absolutely delighted," Scherhant said.

Wirtz doubled the lead on the hour with a left-footed drive from outside the box that went in off the far post. Schlotterbeck headed in Gruda's free kick five minutes later before Gruda set up Wirtz for a close-range finish in the 71st.

Gruda turned in a rebound in the 77th minute after Scherhant struck ​the post.

Germany's fifth goal secured top spot on head-to-head goal difference. ‌

The Dutch had last trailed by five ⁠goals in an international in 1959, when West Germany beat them 7-0 in ⁠Cologne.
