Tommy North seals Hearts contract extension as starlet stays until 2028

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Heart of Midlothian have confirmed that Tommy North has penned a one-year contract extension.

The youngster has been on loan with Edinburgh City as part of the two clubs' cooperation agreement. Now he has been rewarded with a new deal at Tynecastle with a view to continuing his development upon his return.

The club released a statement revealing the news regarding the signing from Livingston last season who has found the net eight times for the Hearts B team before his six goals for at Meadowbank this term.

"Tommy North has signed a one-year contract extension until the summer of 2028," Hearts confirmed this afternoon.

"Having previously been involved in the Hearts Academy at foundation level, North returned to the Jambos and signed his first professional contract with the club last summer.

"The striker spent the end of last season on a cooperation loan at Montrose, having scored eight goals in 24 games for the Hearts B team earlier in the campaign.

"This season, North made the move to Meadowbank, joining Edinburgh City on a cooperation loan, where he has provided three goals and three assists so far for the Citizens.

"Tommy has now put pen to paper on a contract extension until the summer of 2028 as the 18-year-old looks to continue his development with the men in maroon."
