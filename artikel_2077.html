Manchester United linked with Nico Paz as Real Madrid retain buy-back option

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Manchester United have been linked with Como midfielder Nico Paz, although Real Madrid’s buy-back option and the absence of reported negotiations leave considerable uncertainty over a potential move.

The 22-year-old Argentina international has impressed in Italy this season, scoring six goals and providing eight assists in 14 appearances. According to The Sun, United have been monitoring Paz ahead of a possible summer transfer, with Mauricio Pochettino’s appointment potentially helping their chances.

Paz joined Como from Real Madrid in 2024 and has become an important player under Cesc Fabregas. He scored twice and provided an assist in Como’s 4-1 Champions League victory over United last month.

However, Madrid confirmed in June that they retain an option to re-sign the midfielder for the 2027-28 season. The clause is reportedly worth €80m (£67.6m), although the Spanish club have not publicly confirmed that figure.

Transfer journalist Fabrizio Romano has questioned the strength of United’s reported interest, saying there has been no contact or serious negotiation. Romano explained that the link had emerged because the same agency represents Paz and Pochettino.

He also suggested that Madrid remain central to the player’s future and that a January move is not being considered. United could assess their attacking midfield options next summer, with captain Bruno Fernandes’ contract situation still unresolved.

For now, Paz remains a reported target rather than a player involved in active transfer talks.
