Date and time of Celtic AGM revealed as 18 resolutions on table

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Celtic have announced the date of the club's AGM for 2026.

The annual general meeting will take place on Tuesday, December 1. It will be held at Celtic Park with a start time of 12pm. The notice for the plans for the AGM is available on the club website.

The AGM agenda makes clear that there will be 18 resolutions.

The resolutions to be considered are as follows:

13.That, pursuant to section 551 of the Companies Act 2006 and Article 6 of the Company'sarticles of association ("Articles"), the directors be and are generally and unconditionally

This authority is in substitution for all existing authorities under section 551 of the Companies Act 2006 which authorities, to the extent unused at the date of this resolution, are revoked with immediate effect.

14. That, subject to the passing of Resolution 13 and pursuant to section 570 of the Companies Act 2006 (and Article 6 of the Articles), the directors be and are generally empowered to allot equity securities (within the meaning of section 560 of the Companies Act 2006) for cash pursuant to the authority conferred by Resolution 13 as if section 561(1) of the Companies Act 2006 did not apply to any such allotment, provided that this power shall be limited to:

14.1 the allotment of equity securities in connection with an offer (whether by way of a rights issue, open offer or otherwise):

14.1.1 to holders of ordinary shares (within the meaning of section 560(1) of the Companies Act 2006) in the capital of the Company in proportion (as nearly as practicable) to the respective numbers of ordinary shares held by them; and

14.1.2 to holders of other equity securities in the capital of the Company, as required by the rights of those securities, or subject to such rights, as the directors otherwise consider necessary,

but subject to such exclusions or other arrangements as the directors may deem necessary or expedient in relation to treasury shares, record dates, fractional entitlements or any legal or practical problems under the laws of any territory or the requirements of any regulatory body or stock exchange;

14.2 the allotment of equity securities in place of a cash dividend pursuant to any authority conferred upon the directors in accordance with and pursuant to Article 40.8 of the Articles; and

14.3 the allotment of equity securities otherwise than pursuant to sub-paragraphs 14.1 and 14.2 above, up to an aggregate nominal amount of £47,577.62;

and (unless previously revoked, varied or renewed) this power shall expire on the earlier of 1 March 2028 and the conclusion of the next annual general meeting of the Company after the passing of this resolution, save that the Company may make an offer or agreement before the expiry of this power which would or might require equity securities to be allotted for cash after such expiry and the directors may allot equity securities pursuant to any such offer or agreement as if this power had not expired.

This power is in substitution for all existing powers under section 570 of the Companies Act 2006 which powers, to the extent unused at the date of this resolution, are revoked with immediate effect.

Resolutions 15, 16, 17 and 18 are ordinary resolutions, requisitioned on behalf of members of the Company in accordance with section 338 of the Companies Act 2006 and are not proposed by the directors:

15. That, the members of Celtic plc, noting the number of shareholders who have become untraced and whose dividend and other entitlements remain unclaimed, request the Board to appoint Georgeson, the asset reunification service operated by the Company's registrar, Computershare Investor Services PLC, to carry out a shareholder tracing and asset reunification programme on the customary contingent fee basis under which that service is provided at no upfront cost to the Company, and to report to shareholders on the programme at the next Annual General Meeting.

16. This AGM instructs the Board to prepare a strategic review and a published report with a football, financial, and strategic plan (medium term I.e. 3-5 years). The plan should contain a clear stated strategic aim and measurable football and financial goals for the Board to target. Remuneration should be linked to achieving those goals.

17. This AGM instructs the Chairman of the Board of Directors of Celtic PLC to:

17.1 Undertake, in cooperation with elected representatives of the Celtic support, a full review of the security operations at Celtic Park. The aim of such review to examine, among other things:

17.1.1 The training and management of security staff to assess their understanding of how best to ensure the safety and security of supporters.

17.1.2 The training and management of security staff to assess the extent to which medical emergencies are responded to appropriately.

17.1.3 Any systems intended to capture lessons from any incidents that do take place.

17.1.4 The recruitment of senior staff inside the security department; the extent to which they understand their role and the extent to which they are able to work with other departments (chiefly supporter liaison) to ensure the safety of supporters and their respectful treatment.

18. This AGM instructs the Chairman of the Board of Directors of Celtic PLC to:

18.1.1 Issue a full and unreserved apology to the shareholders and supporters of Celtic FC for giving a platform at the 2025 AGM to an individual, who holds no role within the governance of Celtic PLC, which he then abused to insult and denigrate our shareholders and our supporters.

18.1.2 To publicly commit to ensure that such an abuse of the AGM is never allowed to happen again.

The confirmation of the AGM plans come after fan groups called on Dermot Desmond to attend the AGM in person for the first time in 20 years.

A Celtic Fans Collective statement read: A Celtic Fans Collective statement read: "For twenty years, Dermot Desmond has not attended Celtic's Annual General Meeting, despite wielding considerable influence over the direction and decision-making of our club.

"Although only a minority shareholder, Desmond appears to exercise influence far beyond his shareholding. With that influence comes a responsibility to be accountable.

"We call on Dermot Desmond to publicly commit to attending the 2026 AGM in person and answer questions about his role in the running of Celtic Football Club.

"Sending his son, another representative or a prepared statement in his place is no substitute for attending personally.

"The decision by Peter Lawwell to prematurely conclude last year's AGM left a clear impression among supporters that the Board had taken an opportunity to avoid scrutiny. Responsibility for how that meeting was conducted and ultimately concluded rests entirely with the Board.

"This year, however, the focus must be firmly on scrutiny rather than protest. There is a clear understanding across the support that the AGM must provide the opportunity for important questions to be put directly to those running the Club, without allowing scrutiny to be evaded once again.

"Celtic supporters have repeatedly sought meaningful dialogue and a more constructive relationship with the Club.

"At a time when fans want to see decisive leadership, a public commitment from Dermot Desmond to attend the AGM in good faith would be an important demonstration of his willingness to engage directly with supporters and shareholders."
