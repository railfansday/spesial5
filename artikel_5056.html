OKXICE is in the news right now after nearly all the companies it first planned to list pushed back. This, after the joint venture between OKX and Intercontinental Exchange first pitched tokenizing shares of 62 companies.

Most of those objections showed up in public notices in the last 45 days. A few more came in right before launch. That includes companies that weren’t even on the original list.

Only six tokenized stocks are available at launch: Coinbase, Robinhood, Strategy, Circle, Securitize and BitGo.

The bigger plan hasn’t gone anywhere though. The platform has lined up 113 assets, including ETFs, for a gradual rollout.

Fidelity, Direxion, eToro, Strive and Vida Global have also come onboard as issuers.

Pushback or not, the demand is still hard to ignore.

The tokenized stock market is now worth $4.8 billion, as per data from Token Terminal. A year ago, it was just $527 million. A near 9x jump in 12 months!

Holders grew too, from 150K to 5.9 million. This hinted at a 40x hike.

For context, stablecoins were in a similar position in March 2020. We all know how that story went. The category went onto spread the dollar to those outside the country.

Tokenized stocks could do the same for U.S equities. And, they’re being to put to use too.

And there’s very little of them that are actually being put to use.

By late September, only about $80 million in tokenized equities were being used as collateral for loans!

The point is to borrow against your stocks and keep your exposure, instead of selling them. This is already an accessible TradFi option; the DeFi avenue can make it available round the clock.

Kamino, Morphom Jupiter Lend, Lista DAO and Maple are already building on it.
