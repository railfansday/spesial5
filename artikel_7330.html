LinkedIn Taps Former Trade Desk Exec to Lead Global Ad Sales

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Anders Mortensen, who left The Trade Desk in June after about seven months as chief revenue officer, has taken the role of vice president of global sales for LinkedIn Ads.

A LinkedIn spokesperson confirmed the hire and said he reports to Matthew Derella, svp of LinkedIn Ads and Microsoft Advertising.

Mortensen announced the move in a LinkedIn post late Wednesday evening, saying he was three days into the job and had already been onstage flipping pancakes with LinkedIn CEO Daniel Shapero.

“AI is changing how people discover brands and make decisions, but the importance of trust, relationships, and human connection isn’t going anywhere,” Mortensen wrote.

Trishla is an Adweek staff reporter covering AI and tech.
