I spend a lot of time outside. Unfortunately, a remarkable amount of the outdoors comes home with me.

Between my muddy hiking shoes, ski boots, camping gear, and two dogs who seem personally committed to bringing the entire Wasatch Range indoors, my floors take a beating. Dirt, gravel, pine needles, dog hair, the occasional stick. You name it, I’ve probably tracked it across my living room.

For years, I accepted this as the price of admission. I swept. I vacuumed. I swept again. And then one of the dogs would come barreling through the door, and we’d start all over.

Which is why the best piece of outdoor gear I’ve bought this year has never actually been outside.

The Shark Navigator Robot Vacuum with Self-Emptying Base is currently on sale for $280, down from $450. It’s nearly identical to the model I’ve been using for the past six months, but with a larger self-emptying base that can hold up to 60 days of debris. With the holidays coming up, I think it might be one of the best gifts you could give someone who spends a lot of time outside, especially if they have dogs.

I spent three years wavering over buying one. Surely I could manage to clean my own floors? Did I really need to spend hundreds of dollars on something I could do myself?

For the past six months, my Shark has been dutifully making its rounds, picking up the dirt, debris, and dog hair that accumulate between adventures. It maps my house, works on carpet and hard floors, and returns to its dock to empty itself. I can schedule cleanings from my phone, so my floors get vacuumed while I’m outside doing something considerably more fun.

It’s not perfect. Occasionally it gets stuck, and once it even threw itself down the stairs. (I see you, adrenaline junkie!) But overall, it’s held up well to six months of living with two dogs and two humans who aren’t much better about tracking dirt inside.

The part I didn’t anticipate was how much joy I’d get from checking the collection bin.

I’m apparently so deeply into my mid-30s that one of the highlights of my day is seeing just how much dirt this little robot has collected.

And now it’s not? And I didn’t have to clean it?

This thing isn’t going to put away my ski boots or haul me up the skin track. But it does mean I can spend less time cleaning up after my adventures and more time having them. And if that isn’t a great gift for someone who’d rather be outside, I don’t know what is.
