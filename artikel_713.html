Non-league player injured falling down badger hole

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Cav Miley has made 17 appearances since joining Chelmsford in the summer

Footballers may be among the fittest athletes in the world of sport, but they are not immune to bizarre injuries.

Dave Beasant famously severed a tendon in his big toe when he dropped a bottle of salad cream, while Rio Ferdinand strained tendons in his knee by lying in one position on the sofa for too long watching television.

The latest inductee into the strange injury hall of fame is Chelmsford City captain Cav Miley.

The 31-year-old midfielder missed the 2-2 draw with Walton and Hersham in the National League South on Saturday after twisting an ankle in training by falling down a badger hole.

Miley returned for the 1-0 home victory over Maidstone United on Monday, after which manager Mark Cooper shed more light on the incident.

"Everyone is telling me it's gone viral, but I told the truth - he fell down a badger hole," Cooper told the club's YouTube channel.

"I was told at the start it was a fox hole, but then the guy at where we train told me it's a badger hole.

"We need to make sure we fill all of them in before we train now."

Miley joined Chelmsford in the summer after spells in the National League with Southend United and Eastleigh.
