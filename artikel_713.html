From Bad Bunny to Sexyy Red, Here’s Every Music Cameo in ‘Grand Theft Auto VI’

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

GTA VI sales are nearing $4 billion after just one day.

After 13 long years, Grand Theft Auto VI is finally here.

The game everyone has been talking about since it dropped at midnight has already pulled in almost $4 billion in sales and features some of music’s biggest names on the soundtrack and in the game.

Bad Bunny, Trina, Trick Daddy, Anitta, Burna Boy, TLC, N.O.R.E., The Alchemist, Charlamagne tha God, Morgan Wallen, Keith Richards, Seth Troxler, Lana Del Rey, Gloria Estefan and more, including record producers and DJs, all host their own shows on the game’s legendary radio stations. Kodak Black, Sexyy Red, Freddie Gibbs and others make appearances in the game as either themselves or a character that you interact with. Comedian, actor and rapper Zack Fox helped with TV ads and contributed to some of the writing.

The Grand Theft Auto series has become more than just a video game; it’s part of the pop culture zeitgeist. This version’s radio stations includes podcasts, traditional talk radio, and channels dedicated to Southern rap classics, pop music, classical music, Latin rap, U.K. hits, reggae and Afrobeats, which makes it that much more fun as you cruise around creating havoc in a fictional version of South Florida. You never know who’s going to pop up and how. Some are playing themselves or are putting on a character as they try to play along with the game’s satirical tone.
