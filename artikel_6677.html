Colored Tights Are Taking Over—Here’s How Fashion Girls Are Styling Them Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Consider this your sign to swap basic black for cobalt, red, lavender, and more.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

’Tis the season to cozy up, which means it’s also the season to have a little fun with tights. This year, try swapping out the traditional black hose and elevate your outfits with a pop of color instead. The colored tights renaissance was alive and well on the fall 2026 runways, with Chanel, Marc Jacobs, and Valentino leading the charge.

Off the runway, the street style crowd has been just as enthusiastic, livening up their looks with bold tights. If you need outfit inspiration, we’ve rounded up the best ways fashion girls are styling colorful hosiery right now.

And when in doubt, let the tights do the talking: pair a bold, colorful pair with a neutral base of black, gray, or brown, and you can’t go wrong.

Below, check out some elevated ways to style colorful tights this season.
