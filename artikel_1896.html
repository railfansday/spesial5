‘That was very silly’ - Brighton player ratings at Hull City as 3/10 defender has a torrid time

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

A hugely frustrating afternoon for Brighton saw them suffer a painful 2-0 loss at Hull City on Sunday.

The Seagulls played out the final minutes with 10-men as Costinha was sent-off after receiving his second booking on 89 minutes and they were made to pay as a late brace from Oli McBurnie sealed the three points.

Brighton actually played well for long periods but tired as the match progressed. Fabian Hurzeler’s men started as the team more likely to score with Yasin Ayari, Costinha and Georginio all going close in the first half.

Ayari then rattled the bar from a Diego Gomez cross and Georgino's follow-up effort was well saved by Hull keeper Konstantinos Tzolakis.

Brighton players were clearly frustrated as they went down the tunnel at halftime and started fast after the break. Pascal Gross saw a left footed effort blocked by the excellent Tzolakis and Gomez fired wide when well placed on 51 minutes.

Jack Hinshelwood, Charalampos Kostoulas and Yankuba Minteh were introduced but Hull came closest as Sorba Thomas’ mis-hit cross deceived Bart Verbruggen and hit the bar.

Kostoulas then saw a chipped effort cleared off the line but Brighton were punished after Costinha’s dismissal. McBurnie’s late double - both from close range - was a painful one to take.

They remain second in the table on 22 points from 11 matches, six behind leaders Arsenal.

Bart Verbruggen - 6: He conceded five for the Dutch against the Germans during the international break and another two here. Neither of McBurnies were his fault to be fair.

Costinha - 3: Missed a golden chance on 18 minutes as he sidefooted wide of the post. Booked for a cynical foul on Thomas and then another booking on 89 minutes saw him dismissed. Quite silly really and a poor game for the Portuguese.

Lewis Dunk - 5: Caught in possession in the early stages and passing not as accurate as usual.

Luka Vuskovic - 6: Booked on 34 minutes for a foul and then kicking the ball away. He had a tough battle with McBurnie.

Olivier Boscagli - 5: Maintained his ever-present record and delivered a steady performance at left back with some nice distribution. Replaced by Minteh on 67.

Pascal Gross - 5: The German should have had another assists to his tally as he set-up the Costinha chance on 18 minutes. Saw a left footed effort beaten away by Tzolakis after the break.

Chema - 6: The young Spaniard does the basics well and was good for the most part. Ran out of gas in the later stages. He was booked for a foul on Coyle.

Diego Gomez - 7: Fizzed a delightful cross into the box on 13 minutes which somehow just eluded everyone. He also supplied the cross that saw Ayari rattled the bar. Should have scored on 51 when he flashed an angled effort wide of the far post. Replaced by Kadioglu on 80.

Yasin Ayari - 6: Saw his first-time sliding effort well saved by Tzolakis. He probably should have scored. Rattled the bar with a brilliant first time effort on 36 minutes. He got into the positions but just not ruthless enough. Replaced by Hinshelwood on 66 minutes.

Max De Cuyper - 6: Delivered an accurate cross to tee-up Ayari's big chance on 14 minutes. Lacked the cutting edge needed to breakthrough this stubborn Hull team.

Georginio - 6: Selected ahead of Kostoulas and forced a sharp save from Tzolakis on 31 minutes with crisp right footed effort. Tzolakis then denied him on 38 minutes with an even better save. A solid outing for the Frenchman and his start since the opening day of the season against Villa. Replaced by Kostoulas on 66.
