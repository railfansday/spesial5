Police conduct 'knife sweep' around Crawley Town stadium

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Operation Sceptre is a national police campaign aimed at reducing knife crime and serious violence.

Police confirmed the ‘knife sweep’ took place in a Facebook post on Wednesday (November 18).

A force spokesperson said: “As part of ongoing activity during Op Sceptre week, officers have been conducting a knife sweep around the Broadfield Stadium area, no weapons were found during the search.

“Knife crime has devastating consequences for individuals, families and communities.

“These proactive searches play an important role in removing weapons from public spaces, reducing opportunities for violence and helping to keep our neighbourhoods safe.

“Everyone has a role to play in preventing knife crime.

“By working together, reporting concerns and supporting young people to make positive choices, we can help ensure our community remains a safe place for everyone.

“If you have information about knife crime, please report it to Sussex Police online or by calling 101.

“In an emergency, always call 999. or you can report anonymously to Crimestoppers @ https://orlo.uk/Hgay2.”
