Cash certainly comes in handy. I haven't heard of thumbstack of taskrabbit but handymen typically prefer cash.

I just tipped an uber driver in cash. He had a 4.99 star review out of thousands of trips but did not have venmo or cashapp. The trip was paid for by dealership and there was no other way for me to tip.

We keep a cookie jar with cash and wife always has over $100 cash in her purse. Cash allows for another avenue to make purchases should multiple systems go down and is far quicker and easier than the aforementioned venmo or cashapp. Many still live off cash tips.

This is actually a nice segue from multiple posts about having those bogleheads with too much money and not sure what to do with it. A nice way to help others is to give cash to those you come in contact with. The appliance delivery guys, the mechanic, the handymen, local servers, lemonade stand, school car wash or fundraisers, hair salon, now that we are approaching the holidays, the mailman, UPS/FedEx, etc.

Quick, easy, and impactful.
