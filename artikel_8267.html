Getting Going With Two 20’s

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

“Feels like forever even if forever’s tonight” –Bazzi (From Mine)

This morning at 6 am Gabrielle, the trail angel, picked us up from the Days Inn in Tucson for an 18 mile start day from Colossal Cave. If you haven’t read my blog in the past you wouldn’t know that this is our 3rd first day on the AZT. October of ‘25 we did 380 miles on the trail from the South Rim of the Grand Canyon to Superior. We didn’t get off early, but fit in as much as we could in the three weeks we had at the time. March ‘26 we did 185 miles. We were going to finish then, but it was too damn hot. (Highs in the high 90’s while hiking through the desert with no shade is intense.) So here we are finishing the 115 miles we need to get to the southern terminus.

The saguaros were so pretty in the morning light. After about 5 miles in we took a breakfast break. I swung my pack off and looked around the ditch before squatting down to pee. A man came over the hill and I was in plain sight. My reflexes pulled my pants up but he had still seen my lady bits. He awkwardly said hello and kept on walking. I had a Truvani bar to give me some energy.

Honestly desert hiking isn’t really my thunder, but it is my mom’s (Landslide Lizard). She never forces me to come. I agreed that I’d come to AZ to hike with her, because…

A. I don’t want her solo-hiking in the land of pricklies and Gila Monsters.

B. I think it would look good on college applications. “I hiked the AZT at 11 years old.” Does sound kinda good. 😃

C. The Trek is awesome and I don’t want to take too long of blog writing breaks, so I needed an excuse to hike.

As we walked I thought about my cat and what he’s up to. I thought about Tucson and how it’s a lovely city. I like it 100 times better than Phoenix. I thought about how sad it is that the Dodgers didn’t go to the World Series.

The saguaros faded into barrel cacti (my all time favorite.) It was not super steep but I kept stubbing my toes on rocks.

At lunch I had tuna and bread. On trail I eat fish, sometimes you got to make exceptions. Another one is I eat crackers in the wilderness. Not at home. I forgot how good they can taste. NutThins here I come!

We stocked up on water from the cache and my bag was heavy. So heavy. I tried to chug my electrolytes but it didn’t make my load much better.

My mom and I chatted about what we are going to give people for the holidays. I wish I could say what but I don’t think that’s such a good idea.

When we came to the camping spot we realized it was a 20 miler! First day on trail isn’t usually this high. So we gotta celebrate! I ate my Mac. It’s yummy but also heavy. So win win.

As we were setting up our Durston tent we heard voices. A dude walked by followed by a lady. *Drum roll please!* It was PegLeg!!! 🥳 I was SO excited that I got to meet a hiker-celeb on the first day on trail. We talked for 3 minutes before she and her friend kept on. They are doing big miles, so they had to leave. If you don’t know who PegLeg is, she is famous in the hiker world for doing the  calendar year border to border triple crown (CYB2BTC). In other words, PegLeg set the record for being the first female to hike 10,000 miles in a year last year. She is super cool and it was by far the best part of my day! PegLeg also is a Trek Blogger, see here.

Hi, I’m Fluff the Hiking Fox. I’ve done all the thru-hikes PJ has done. Not trying to brag but I’ve been up Mt. Whitney! Not many foxes if any have done that. I get it easy, just bouncing along on the pack. Today I got in close quarters with a water jug. The cache was really cool. Lizard Lady and PJ like them a lot and always talk about how grateful they are to the trail angels, so I do too! ‘Night my friends, talk tomorrow.

Last night it was windy and I couldn’t get comfortable. Too many nights on a Tuft & Needle mattress back home. It was 11 before I drifted off. Just to awake at 3 am and be up a while. 🙄 My watch tells me I got 2 hours and 45 minutes of sleep last night, no REM or deep. Starting off complaining, I guess. 😉

We hit the trail by 6:36. An entire 4 minutes earlier than yesterday. As we walked I thought about a number of different things. Like how my legs were getting toasty in my Kühl leggings. So we stopped and I ditched ‘em super quick so we could keep on the trail to Kentucky Camp. (It’ll be a solid 20 miles just like day one.)

Just before we stopped for lunch I popped in my ear buds and listened to some Anna of the North, but then realized I needed a bit more of a rock and roll song. I put on my Bazzi Mix! That’s when I decided Mine had the perfect quote for this post.

Lunch time we filtered water and I had tuna and bread. 🥖 🍣! I chose tuna again because jam for lunch is not very good I’ve learned. Sweet food in the middle of the day isn’t tasty due to the FACT that you need salts if you’re burning calories like the river is on fire.

After lunch we hit a little ascent for a couple miles. The terrain was gorgeous. 😍 We were cruising until afternoon break. After that we were going like .7 in 30 effing minutes. We usually average about 1 mile in 24 minutes.

At dusk we got to Kentucky Camp. The host and hostess were so chatty and nice. They even brought us Linder chocolate later. We have a sink with soap, outhouse, toaster and microwave. So it’s the royal treatment. 👑  There’s a big historical building. We went in and the floor boards creaked and there were like 1,000 doors. I don’t watch horror movies but it seemed right out of one. I was prepared to get a jump scare, but luckily I didn’t.

Then I snuggled up for a hopefully restful sleep.

I took a long nap while Lizard and Peej walked. Their conversations are boring for a 2 inch fox. At lunch Lizard let me chill on her Yosemite hat. It reminded me of the park. GTG. 😘
