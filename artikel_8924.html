Call for Submissions: Deborah Norden Travel Fellowship 2026-27

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Deborah Norden Travel Fellowship offers grants to support imaginative, original travel projects rooted in the direct experience of buildings, landscapes, spaces and places.

The Deborah Norden Travel Fellowship offers grants to support imaginative, original travel projects rooted in the direct experience of buildings, landscapes, spaces and places.

The grants, comprising two streams ("Open Stream" and "Community College Stream"), are given in memory of Deborah Norden, an architect and arts administrator who passed away in 1994. Each stream funds one or two proposals annually. Applicants for the Open Stream may seek up to $6,000; applicants for the Community College Stream may seek up to $2,000. 2026-27 is the inaugural year of these Localoci grants. Between 1995 and 2025, The Architectural League of New York issued travel grants from their now sunset Deborah J. Norden Fund. This Localoci project builds on that initiative's legacy.
