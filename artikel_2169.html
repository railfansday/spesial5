Zero goals in seven games: Ex-Sheffield Wednesday man struggles for form after stern ‘good riddance’ exit message

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Sheffield Wednesday rebuilt their squad in the summer, with a brand-new group of players put together after multiple departures. Among those to move on was striker Ike Ugbo, who joined Cypriot side Apollon Limassol on an initial season-long loan after two goals in 62 games as a permanent SWFC player.

Ugbo’s move was viewed as a much-needed one for all parties, and former administrator Kris Wigfield didn’t hesitate to make his feelings known upon the deal. He wrote in a since-deleted X post: “When even in the bleakest of financial circumstances, a high wage earner shows such entitlement for the money they receive and such contempt for the supporters that were literally paying his wages – you have no chance. Good riddance.”

Ugbo defended himself before turning his attention to the next chapter in his career. A move to Cyprus marked a chance for the 28-year-old to rediscover his past best, but he has struggled to hit the ground running.

Ugbo scored only twice in two seasons after earning a permanent move to Sheffield Wednesday. His initial loan under Danny Rohl was a major success as his seven goals helped inspire the Owls to Championship survival.

Unfortunately, such success has proven tough to emulate, and that has been the case with Apollon Limassol too. Ugbo has made seven appearances – all from the bench – and failed to find the back of the net.

He played in their UEFA Conference League third-round qualifier and his other six outings have come in the league. Apollon Limassol were unbeaten in the first eight matches before a 1-0 loss to Olympiakos Nicosia last weekend.

Ex-SWFC man Ugbo’s battle to break into the starting XI has been made tougher by the form of fellow summer signing Alen Ozbolt, who has seven goals in 11 games from striker. Wingers Brandon Thomas (five goals, three assists) and Garry Rodrigues (four goals, five assists) have impressed too.

Ugbo is still searching for form in Cyprus, and Sheffield Wednesday are hoping a new talisman emerges too.

Jamal Lowe has seven goals in 15 games having made a strong start to the season, but form has waned in recent weeks and he served a three-match suspension for a red card against Notts County on October 20th.

Davis Keillor-Dunn has found more minutes and now has five goals in 13 appearances. Among those hoping to come up with more consistent goalscoring returns for new SWFC boss Dave Challinor are Mason Burstow, Ali Al-Hamadi and Harry Gray.

The unlocking of a truly prolific goalscorer will be key if the Owls are to cement themselves as strong promotion candidates in League One.
