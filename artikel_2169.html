Jake Cooper interview after Millwall's loss to Birmingham City sparks reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Millwall captain Jake Cooper has sparked a reaction through his post-match assessment of the Lions' 3-0 defeat against Birmingham City at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park on Tuesday night.

Having romped to a 4-1 success over lowly Derby County on Saturday, there was hope that Alex Neil's side could continue an upsurge in the Championship standings.

Instead, they were the side who found themselves three goals behind in very little time, with all of Birmingham's strikes from Alex Cochrane, Max Bird and Jay Stansfield coming ahead of the half-hour mark.

Blues climbed into the automatic promotion places as a result, with Chris Davies' men winning four of their last five matches, scoring 14 goals during that timeframe.

Having finished third last season, Millwall have started the current campaign indifferently, winning five of their opening 14 encounters, whilst a sixth loss of the campaign saw them drop to 13th.

Cooper, 31, knows the Championship inside out, with his outing on Tuesday taking him nine away from 450 appearances in the division for the Lions and his previous club, Reading.

Although Birmingham found themselves cruising to victory by the time Bird made it 2-0 on 16 minutes, it was Millwall who created the first-half's only 'big chance' whilst the hosts still forged 15 attempts before the interval.

Speaking to Millwall's in-house media post-match, Cooper believed that the scoreline didn't reflect the overall balance of the game.

"Obviously, the first feeling is disappointment, like you say," he said. "I don't think they did enough to score three goals against us.

"I think we had some good opportunities to get back in the game, at important times, that we couldn't take.

"It's important at this level, both boxes are what makes the difference. They were clinical and we, unfortunately, were lacking in that area today," Cooper added.

Davies' side have developed a habit of putting those who visit St. Andrew's to the sword, with it being the third successive home outing in which Birmingham have netted at least three goals.

Millwall only tested Ryan Allsop with two of their 16 attempts, though, which tells its own story by and large, and Cooper's assessment certainly baffled some of the home support.

"My head would be on Mars if we’d got thumped 3-0 and my centre half who were playing the way he did said this," wrote one Blues supporter.

"Funny every team we’ve beat this season say the same thing, yet here we are second in the league, sour grapes" said another Bluenose.

"Commentators last night said "this is a very un-millwall like performance" different commentators said the same on a few occasions and remember it being said on Saturday as well, maybe we just know how to nullify teams and make them look a bit average?," said another.

It has also been claimed that this is a general viewpoint held by teams who have lost in B9, with another verdict reading: "Funny every team we’ve beat this season say the same thing, yet here we are second in the league, sour grapes."

"How can he say Birmingham didn’t do enough to score their 3 goals? It was a joy to watch how fluid Birmingham were. Game was done inside 20 odd mins," backed up the general consensus from Blues fans.

There were also some Millwall fans not on board with Cooper's stance, with one stating: "lol they looked like scoring every attack in that first half. Could have been 5 or 6."

If Birmingham's fans were bemused by Cooper's stance, then the likelihood is they will also have felt the same way when reading Neil's post-match thoughts.

"The fact we have created the chances is a good thing because we looked threatening, but the lack of clinical edge in the final third was a big problem for us," he said.

"Goals change games. The lack of discipline in the first half didn't help us, albeit they have had four shots and three of them go in the back of our net.

"We are really disappointed that we didn't manage to build on the weekend. It is disappointing the manner we have lost the game."

Millwall will look to respond against West Bromwich Albion at The Hawthorns on Saturday, whilst Birmingham host Swansea City.
