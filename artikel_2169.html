Blackburn Rovers confirm extent of Ryan Alebiosu injury blow with return timeline set

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Blackburn Rovers have seen Ryan Alebiosu become a key player since his signing in the summer of 2025. He was their Player of the Season in the 2025/26 campaign and has retained his starting berth under Tony Mowbray this term too.

However, Alebiosu was forced off in the first half of the 2-1 defeat to Stoke City at the start of the month and missed the win over West Ham as a result. Now, Rovers have confirmed the 24-year-old has undergone surgery on his knee and will be sidelined until 2027.

Alebiosu will now work through a rehabilitation programme with the Rovers medical staff in the hope that he can recover swiftly and get back to action over the second half of the campaign.

Alebiosu is ideal as a wing-back or attack-minded full-back. He’s got the athleticism to deal with the demands of getting up and down the right-hand side, and the energy to recover and see to his defensive duties.

He offers great assets in both thirds too, recording six assists across his Blackburn Rovers career to date. The former Arsenal youngster is a great tackler too, anticipating his opponent’s movements well and showing patience and timing to nick the ball.

He’s a big asset on the right for Rovers when attacking and defending, and his presence will be missed. The hope is that surgery reduces the risk of Alebiosu suffering a worse issue further down the line.

Losing Alebiosu is a blow, but one man who will relish the chance to impress is Gady Beyuku. Signed from Modena FC in the summer, the 20-year-old had only made four substitute appearances for Blackburn Rovers before his fellow right-back was cast to the sidelines.

The Frenchman will be determined to prove he’s up to the task of Championship football, as he is the only other natural right-back on the books. It marks a big chance for Beyuku to earn his place in Mowbray’s side.

Youngster Matty Litherland is working his way back to fitness and could come into consideration. Kristi Montgomery has covered on the right as well, but he has progressed well in central midfield, and it would be a blow to break up his partnership with Sam Morsy.

Opportunity knocks for Beyuku while Alebiosu recovers, so it will be intriguing to see just how he fares.
