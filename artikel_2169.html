West Ham can sack Scott Parker for free on one condition

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Scott Parker is expected to become the latest head coach of West Ham United following the club's decision to dismiss Nuno Espírito Santo on Tuesday, but there is a catch to the looming appointment of the former midfielder.

Nuno saw the pressure on his shoulders grow even further on Saturday when the Hammers suffered a second straight Championship loss at the London Stadium at the hands of high-flying West Bromwich Albion.

The defeat against James Morrison's side also made it two games in succession where the Portuguese's side had shipped three goals in E20, having lost 3-2 to Southampton on October 24th.

Results elsewhere saw the East London side slide down to eighth in the table after 13 games played, with concerns raised about the fact the pre-season promotion favourites find themselves six points behind leaders Middlesbrough.

The timing of Nuno's decision did cause some form of surprise, though, with it being confirmed in Tuesday's early hours, less than 48 hours before the Hammers take on newly-promoted Cardiff City in South Wales.

Since then, it has been widely reported that Parker is to be who takes over, but a revelation about the deal he is set to sign has emerged.

Parker may have less than a decade's worth of managerial experience under his belt, but the former West Ham midfielder has achieved promotion in all of his three full seasons in the Championship with Fulham, AFC Bournemouth and Burnley.

However, he has also been unable to preserve the league status of two of those clubs, whilst he was dismissed by the Cherries shortly after promotion was achieved following a 9-0 hammering at Liverpool.

It has been reported by Hammers-related website, Claret and Hugh, that the 46-year-old is set to sign a two-and-a-half year contract with the club which would run until the summer of 2029.

However, there are serious short-term conditions which need to be met for Parker to fulfil the length of said contract, with the publication revealing that if a first-time promotion is not achieved, West Ham's board of directors have the capacity to relieve him of his duties at no cost.

If the club does return to the top-flight, though, Parker will be given the opportunity to stabilise the Hammers' position amongst English football's elite.

The Hammers have parted ways with the Portuguese head coach but are looking to act swiftly when sourcing his replacement

Parker's appointment would make him West Ham's 21st permanent manager, 14 months after Nuno signed a three-year contract in East London shortly after leaving Nottingham Forest.

The 52-year-old had won the Championship previously with Wolves, but despite winning 6-0 twice so far this season, sections of the fanbase had often voiced their frustrations at the Portuguese's tactics and overall performance levels.
