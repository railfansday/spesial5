Source Speaks on ‘SLOMW’ Stars Jen Affleck and Zac Affleck Navigating Divorce and 4th Pregnancy at the Same Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Jen announced her pregnancy just a few months ago.

Jen Affleck just filed for divorce from her husband Zac Affleck after seven years of marriage, news that comes just four months after the couple announced that they’re expecting their fourth child together. So, how are they planning to navigate a breakup and a pregnancy at the same time?

A source tells People that Zac “is doing okay” and that “He and Jen are staying focused on preparing for the baby and supporting their kids.” Judging from Instagram Stories, the pair seemingly celebrated their oldest daughter’s birthday together yesterday—the same day news broke.

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives stars have a 5-year-old, 3-year-old, and 1-year-old, and announced the incoming arrival of their baby back in June:

Zac and Jen’s marriage problems have been chronicled on The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives, but Jen recently praised her ex while talking with People about the tough early months of her pregnancy. “I could not be there for my kids, and so Zac really had to show up. I mean, he didn’t really have a choice,” Jen said, before alluding to them having some issues. “There’s a lot that we’ve been navigating behind closed doors, but I do have to say, I wouldn’t have survived the first trimester without him.”

Reminder: Jen and Zac are appearing on the upcoming The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County, so presumably we’ll find out specifics on what caused their split soon.
