Gigi Hadid’s Guest In Residence Is Taking Its Signature Knits to the Slopes With Halfdays

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Like peanut butter and jelly or macaroni and cheese, cozy knits and skiwear just go together. After all, bundling up in layers is of the utmost priority when you’re hitting the slopes—and even more so when you’re taking part in some après-ski. So it only made sense that the female-founded and cool girl-approved ski brand Halfdays would team up with one of the coolest girls of our generation: Gigi Hadid—and more specifically, Hadid’s luxury knitwear brand, Guest In Residence.

“Most of my favorite winter memories are tied to being outside, getting dressed for the cold, and staying out until the last run,” Hadid shares in a press release. “With Halfdays, we wanted to bring that feeling into the Guest In Residence world, pairing our signature cozy warmth with technical pieces you actually want to wear on the mountain. The collection mixes sporty, vintage, nostalgic and playful influences, and is absolutely at home on the slopes.”

The retro-inspired collection—which is available to shop now—consists of eight pieces, including a corduroy ski bib, a coverall, a puffer jacket inspired by cable-knit sweaters, a Fair Isle turtleneck, and waffle-knit base layers. Combine with a color palette reminiscent of the ’70s alpine scene, and you have what is sure to be a statement on the slopes—and off. “As we think about how Halfdays can live beyond the slopes, knitwear felt like a natural next step,” Ariana Ferwerda, Halfdays’s co-founder and CEO, shared. “Guest In Residence brought its expertise in warmth and knitwear, while we brought our technical approach to mountain dressing. Together, we were able to create something that feels fashion-forward without compromising the performance women need on the mountain.”

Whether you’re the type who’s on the slopes until the ski lift closes or you prefer to stay at the lodge sipping on a hot toddy (or maybe you’re the girl who does both), this collection will keep you looking cute no matter how you prefer to spend your days on the mountain.
