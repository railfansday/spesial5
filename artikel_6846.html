Jennifer Lawrence Is Committed to Oversize Layers

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

She doesn’t need to compromise comfort for style

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Skinny jeans may be sneaking back into the mainstream and cinched silhouettes may be all over the red carpet, but Jennifer Lawrence is sticking to her oversize layers this season.

On Tuesday, the Oscar-winning actor was seen heading to a meeting in New York City, where she combatted the city’s chill in a collection of stacked, multicolored layers that gave her plenty of room to breathe.

Up top, Lawrence embraced her workwear side in a garment-dyed denim barn jacket from Auralee, which featured a black shearling collar and two large cargo pockets in front.

She paired the faded-effect jacket with a set of baggy black sweatpants with white pinstripes on the sides, presumably from Adidas, and she slipped into a set of black leather flats to match. (If I had to wager, I would bet they’re from one of her beloved brands: the Row).

Last week, Lawrence was seen wearing the same denim jacket, which she left hanging open to reveal a Sesame Street-themed T-shirt underneath, and bright-red joggers, while a blue-and-red plaid flannel was tied around her waist.

This time around, she added another pop of color courtesy of a multi-patterned pink-and-tan scarf that was draped around her neck, Olsen-style. Finally, she added a striking finish with a set of black visor sunglasses from Tory Burch, which, like everything else on her body, were oversize.

From her floral jacquard Dries Van Noten puffer to her large lime-green Hermès Bolide bag, it seems Lawrence’s motto this fall is “the bigger, the better.”
