Nones makes little piece of Oval history – and Eastbourne United make it three wins in a row

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Common have had a poor start to the season, United quite the opposite, sitting in second place at the start of a day on which the latest addition to the ground was unveiled – an electronic scoreboard at the Seaside Road end.

Early on, there seemed to be a somewhat frantic attempt by most in the United team to be the one who registered the first goal on the new board, with Joe Overy looking like he had new batteries installed.

Four times he opened up the Common defence, but Callum Barlow, Ryan Blunt and Overy himself all shot agonisingly wide, and Jorge Nones had one well saved.

With 25 minutes gone, Barlow was hauled down in the box but the referee inexplicably waved play on. The ball was loose, everyone dived on it and Brett Patton’s sidefooted effort won a corner. Cometh the hour, cometh, well the boy - 35 minutes were showing on the new scoreboard as Nones kept a cool head to drive past Matt Crutwell to make it 1-0 to United. He will forever be the first scorer to have his name on the new board.

The second half proceeded with remarkably little incident – there was somehow an inevitability that if Common did too much, United would go and score, so a kind of detente broke out.

Deep into the second half, Alieu Secka had one of those moments that goalies specialise in, terrifying the crowd as a mix-up led to him scrambling to push the ball round for a corner.

The pressure was increasing and both teams probed more as United boss Anthony Storey rang the changes – Charlie Ball and James Stone had already joined the fray when James Hull replaced Nones. And 47 second later, Hull made it 2-0, breaking Common’s resistance completely and sending the Oval crowd home happy.

Nearly 250 braved the cold, and in a great atmosphere, football was the real winner as United prepared for Tuesday night’s visit to Bognor in the last 16 of the Sussex Senior Cup.

This Saturday Eastbourne United’s visitors are fourth-placed Guernsey, and it’s a 2pm kick-off at the Oval Arena.
