Incredible scenes at the Strawberry Arena.

Saturday night in Solna, just north of the centre of Stockholm.

Bosnian fans taking over the Strawberry Arena.

An estimated 30,000+ of them in a 51,409 sold out capacity crowd.

A Nations League match between Sweden and Bosnia & Herzegovina that didn’t disappoint.

Sweden blew the visitors away in the first half with Newcastle United’s Anthony Elanga playing his full part, always a threat as he repeatedly ran at the opposition defence. Not always successfully but Elanga a key role in forcing Bosnia back and that pressure leading to goals.

The first one arriving inside five minutes, a great cross from the right delivered by Anthony Elanga and Svensson’s flick header seeing the fingertips of the keeper unable to stop the ball nestling in the far bottom corner.

Arsenal’s Gyokeres then adding two more, the second of which was a penalty just before the break that made it 3-0 at the halfway point.

It looked like a humiliating night in prospect for the visitors but the 30,000 plus Bosnian fans stayed with their team and inside two minutes of the restart Gigovic brought it back to 3-1.

Elanga was one of two substitutions made by Sweden on the hour mark but within a minute Bosnia had another goal, Kolasinac scoring to make it 3-2.

Brilliant scenes inside the Strawberry Arena as the away fans celebrated.

The final half hour saw Bosnia roared on by their supporters but despite throwing everything it wasn’t to be, as the final score was 3-2 to Sweden.

However, for all of those inside the Strawberry Arena, a night they will never forget with the incredible scenes on the terraces.

Newcastle United’s Amar Dedic played the full match and on a night that wasn’t a stellar one for either defence, he didn’t have the best of games.

After the 3-2 defeat, Amar Dedić said his Newcastle United teammate couldn’t believe the crowd, with 30,000+ Bosnian fans making up the majority of the 51,000 attendance: “My brother Elanga told me that this is truly special, that he’s never seen anything like it. I told him, ‘These are our fans; for us, this is normal.'”

Sports Dunia gave the Newcastle United pair the following ratings out of 10 and write-ups:

Anthony Elanga – 7: ‘Elanga’s first half was electric. He grabbed an assist with a pinpoint cross for Svensson’s opener. We also saw him test Martin Zlomislic with a driven shot from range. His dribbling always felt threatening even if not every move came off. Ten possessions lost show the risk in his game, but that chaos unsettled Bosnia’s defence. From our perspective his impact justified a strong rating despite an early substitution.’

Amar Dedic – 5.5: ‘We expected more from Dedic on both sides of the ball. He did get forward, producing shots and attempting a cross, but his end product was inconsistent. Defensively he struggled against Sweden’s transitions, often arriving late or getting dragged inside. One or two efforts from range flew harmlessly off target. His 88% passing was neat yet mostly safe and backward. Overall we felt he never imposed himself fully on the game.’
