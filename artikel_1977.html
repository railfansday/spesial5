‘A comfortable fight’ - Harlem Eubank pokes fun at Conor Benn ahead of Brighton Centre return

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Brighton boxer Harlem Eubank expects another "special event" as he returns to the Brighton Centre on February 5.

Eubank, 32, will be up against big-punching American Greg Outlaw in a 12-round welterweight contest, which will also be screened live on DAZN.

A win against Outlaw - who is ranked No 13 by the WBA - could tee-up some lucrative fights in 2027 against the likes of American boxers Devin Haney, Teofimo Lopez, Ryan Garcia and Britain's Conor Benn.

Eubank lost on points last time out against Frenchman David Papot in an entertaining clash at the Cooper Box last July but remains unbeaten in his three previous fights at the Brighton Centre.

Timo Schwarzkopf, Tyrone McKenna and Josh Wagner have all been beaten by Eubank at the Brighton Centre and the lifelong Brighton and Hove Albion fan expects to make the Outlaw his fourth victim on the south coast.

"I'm excited for this one," said Eubank to Sussex World. "Returning to the Brighton Centre, I love fighting there.

"It's really become my home venue and there's a great energy there. People love coming out to witness a show, and that's what we're ready for on 5 February.

"The Outlaw has a solid record. He's top 15 in the WBA, top 20 in the IBF. A highly ranked guy. I expect him to come and really fight. I'm expecting a good test, and I'm excited to challenge myself once again."

Eubank will have his hands full with the Outlaw on February 5 but the Brighton boxer - who is the nephew of former two-weight world champion Chris Eubank Sr - can‘t resist looking ahead to some big fights.

“Devin Haney, Teofimo Lopez, Ryan Garcia,” said Eubank to Sussex World. “I like them fights, the American guys that have a bit of flair to them.

“I feel like I've got my own style and own flair, and I always think when a Brit goes up against top American fighters, you really engage the British boxing public.

“I'd love to represent Great Britain against one of the top Americans.”

He also wants to renew the Eubank-Benn rivalry and believes a fight with Conor Benn is always an option and a “comfortable” night’s work. Eubank Snr had two epic battles with Nigel Benn in the 1990s and Conor Benn (Nigel’s son) has also had two fights with Chris Eubank Jnr (Harlem’s first-cousin).

Conor Benn is currently plotting his route back into top level boxing after he was blasted in less than two rounds by Garcia last September.

Eubank said: “Yes, definitely. I would love that fight. If he rebuilds, he obviously just got taken out at world level.

“But I think that fight will always be there. It's a fight that I've wanted for a number of years. It's a comfortable fight.

“I've fought many better guys in my previous fights. It's a fight that can happen anytime really.”

Another Eubank v Benn is a very sellable fight for the future but Eubank is also working his way back from his loss to Papot. It was a fast-paced and entertaining bout that saw Eubank down twice and Papot once.

The Brighton boxer displayed tremendous courage against the accomplished Frenchman in an topsy-turvy and bloody battle for both men.

“It was a very entertaining night,” Eubank said to Sussex World. “ Very closely contested fight against a great fighter.

“I feel like it was a fight that showed a lot of different parts of my game, not just skill set, but mentally, physically.

“All the components that you can't teach. All the components that I know I have in my game, but it takes certain experiences for you to show the public

“So I can take a lot away from it. I feel like I was just scratching the surface in terms of how good I can be.

“Maybe it [the loss] was required for me to introduce that side of me and show people different parts of my game.”

Tickets for Eubank vs Outlaw at the Brighton Centre on February 5, go on sale on Monday, November 23 at 10am.
