Duct-Taped American Airlines Passenger Who Allegedly Made Racial Slur Has State Charges Dropped

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The American Airlines passenger who had to be duct-taped to his seat during an allegedly belligerent in-flight meltdown has gotten off scot-free ... at least with his state charges.

Arthur Lundeen saw his second-degree assault and disorderly conduct charges dropped by Anne Arundel County prosecutors on Nov. 16 ... a month after he was federally charged for his shocking outburst.

As you know ... Lundeen was indicted by a federal grand jury on Oct. 13 for interfering with flight crew members and attendants, and assaulting those individuals by striking, beating, and wounding in connection with the incident.

A spokesperson for the Anne Arundel County State's Attorney's Office said ... "Based on the fact that the alleged conduct currently charged took place in the air during flight, the State believes the matter falls under Federal jurisdiction."

We reported earlier ... Lundeen previously claimed in court that he didn't remember "any of what happened" after he allegedly shouted anti-gay comments and called a Black flight attendant the n-word.

Lundeen was duct-taped to his seat by flight attendants and fellow passengers after allegedly striking and pushing other travelers on their Sept. 3 flight ... and even as his head was restrained, he apparently yelled at someone that they had a "little, tiny d***."
