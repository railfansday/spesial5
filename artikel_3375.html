GTA 6: How To Get Into The Laptop In The Vice City Apartment

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

GTA 6 has an inordinate amount of things to uncover. Part of that is due to the gargantuan budget, which allowed Rockstar to create one of the densest open-world environments to date.

No matter what part of the greater Vice City area you're in, there's something unique to find, including major activities like racing or smaller pastimes like fishing. But every once in a while GTA 6 will scratch that puzzler itch some people have, albeit in a sparing and relatively simple way. Here's how to get into the laptop in Chapter 3 of GTA 6.

During Chapter 3 of GTA 6, Jason and Lucia move from the motel to Vice City proper, thanks to a temporary apartment gifted by one of Jason's friends. The apartment belongs to Boobie, whom you'll be meeting shortly after entering Vice City and getting your bearings.

In the master bedroom at the back of the apartment, you'll find an inconspicuous laptop that's locked. It's easy to miss, sitting on a table in the corner of the room, and it requires a password to unlock. Thankfully, all the answers are in the apartment, and you won't need to memorize anything out of the ordinary or solve a massive cipher puzzle to access the laptop.

If you want to discover the password for yourself, walk around the apartment and take a look at the doodle by the nightstand. Then, head over to the main hallway (near the kitchen) and look at a plaque dedicated to the dog Muffins.

Notice the clues (Muffins, a King's crown, and the number 15 on the back of the drawing), as these are the keywords for the password. Although you can investigate and access the laptop sooner, I ended up only digging into it later in Chapter 3.

So you have ample time to check it out, just make sure to try and do it in Chapter 3, as you'll have a completely new living situation in Chapter 4, and you won't always be waking up next to it.

There also doesn't seem to be any negative repercussions for accessing the laptop, and I never noticed any major dialogue from characters or any punishment for digging into the messages therein.

For the most part, exploring the laptop and checking in on Boobie's crew is for flavor, but there is one bonus reward you can pick up thanks to a DM from "Unknown:"

"Go to LaPerle right next to Auntie's Place and use 92665 to get it. I think you're really going to love it."

GTA 6 is full of little secrets like this, and although the rewards vary, it's easy to uncover twists and turns to discover. After all, the game has a huge number of collectibles, including over 100+ animals to identify (and hunt), and tons of unique weapons and vehicles. Now you're well on your way to completing one of them.
