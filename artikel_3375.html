Sombr Softly Pleads With Fan to Stop Flipping Him Off: ‘It’s Making Me Sad’

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

If you’re thinking of spending hundreds of dollars to purchase a concert ticket that would get you in Sombr’s sightline only to flip him off, Sombr would like you to think twice. On Sunday, he singled out a supposedly rude concertgoer mid-gig, one who may have spent hundreds of dollars multiple times just to flip Sombr the bird, to say he has feelings, too.

“[This guy] right here just keeps flipping me off and flipping me off every night,” Sombr, whose shirt seemed to have flipped right off him, told the crowd in video posted by Pop Crave. “Man, I don’t know why you’re coming to my concert if you’re gonna keep flipping me off.” He then seems to finger the man to security but then takes a softer touch. “It’s all love, but just please, please don’t flip me off at my show. It’s this guy with the shades and the leather jacket right here, that guy. “Please just don’t, don’t flip me off, man,” he continued. “It’s really making me sad, man. I don’t appreciate it. This is a safe space. I want everyone here to be kind to each other.”

Although Ticketmaster’s page for the Columbus show no longer has any data on it, tickets in and around the pit area at the front of the stage for Sombr’s arena tour, including his upcoming show in Cleveland on Friday the 13th, cost well over $100. So this fan must have some pretty deep pockets to store his middle fingers in. If the fan has the money, Sombr has eight more North American dates left on his You Are the Reason Tour before the fan has to put a little Santa hat on his finger for Sombr’s upcoming commitments to the iHeartRadio Jingle Ball tour.
