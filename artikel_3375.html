Jill Scott, Grammy Award-Winning Singer & Actress, Signs With Independent Artist Group

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

EXCLUSIVE: Three-time Grammy Award-winning singer, songwriter, actress, and producer Jill Scott has signed with Independent Artist Group for representation across film and TV, branding, literary, and international touring.

The news arrives in the same week that Scott received five nominations for the 2027 Grammy Awards, recognizing her sixth studio album, “To Whom This May Concern.”

While perhaps best known for her music, Scott has an extensive list of film and TV credits, including Tyler Perry’s Why Did I Get Married? and its sequel Why Did I Get Married Too?, HBO’s The No. 1 Ladies’ Detective Agency, the James Brown biopic Get On Up, and BET+’s First Wives Club. Most recently, she portrayed jazz legend Ella Fitzgerald and served as an executive producer on the short film Ella, which won Outstanding Short Form (Live Action) at the 57th NAACP Image Awards. Beyond entertainment, Scott is a New York Times bestselling author of The Moments, the Minutes, the Hours: The Poetry of Jill Scott and a philanthropist whose Jill Scott Arts & Community Fund supports educational and artistic opportunities for underserved communities in Philadelphia. In 2026, Scott was inducted into Billboard’s R&B Hall of Fame in recognition of her contributions to the genre. Her honors also include four NAACP Image Awards, a Peabody Award and honorary doctoral degrees from Temple University and Berklee College of Music.

Scott is represented by her business partner Shawn Gee and lawyer Stephen Barnes.
