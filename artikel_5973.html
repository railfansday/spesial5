Trump tangles with reporter on beautifying White House: ‘You’re so pathetic’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Before departing for Asia Monday, President Donald Trump tangled with a reporter who pressed him on White House construction projects.

President Donald Trump beat back questions from reporters Monday morning suggesting he was spending too much time on making improvements to the White House while the Federal Aviation Administration struggled with remaking its outdated communication systems.

"You're so pathetic," Trump told a reporter when asked about the management of his time.

"I have a responsibility to make sure the symbol of our country is in decent shape," Trump said, noting he spends "very little time" on beautification projects.

As the reporter attempted to interject regarding federal funding priorities for the aviation system, Trump dismissed the cost of the White House repairs as "peanuts," asserting that he pays for the upgrades himself and trading personal barbs with the journalist.

"This doesn’t cost money," Trump said. "This is peanuts. This is called talent. Talent. You don’t have talent, you know. You don’t know what you don’t know."

When pressed later in the gaggle if he bears "responsibility" for the FAA's nationwide ground stop Friday night, the first full stoppage of flights since 9/11, Trump fired back and expressed support for Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy.

"I don't take any responsibility," Trump said. "They did a great job, Secretary Duffy. They acted incredibly. Everybody was safe. Nobody was hurt. No crashes, no nothing."

Trump also repeated placing the blame on Congress for failing to pass the necessary funding to upgrade the FAA's telecommunications system to modernize the U.S. airline infrastructure.

"I've already informed Congress, you know, we've been working with Congress to try and get the money," Trump said. "I think the one good thing that comes out of it, I'm sure Congress will now give us the money to fix [it].

"We want to have the best system in the world, and for that, we need Congress," he continued. "We've been trying to get the money for two years to fix it."

The fixes from copper to fiber optics are long overdue, Trump stressed.

"For 40 years, they should have done this with this system that's probably, on average, 50 years old," he concluded.
