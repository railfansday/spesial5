The Hunger Games Officially Takes Over Streaming Before New Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Marvel and Star Wars have earned more at the box office than most other franchises since the start of the 2010s, but there haven't been many franchises with as dedicated a fan base as The Hunger Games. Inspired by Suzanne Collins' series of books, The Hunger Games movie franchise proved to be a worthy investment in the 2010s, grossing billions of dollars at the box office and inspiring Collins to write more books that could be turned into movies.

A few years ago, The Hunger Games officially left Jennifer Lawrence's Katniss Everdeen behind with the release of The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes, which starred Rachel Zegler opposite Tom Blyth. After a few years away from the screen, The Hunger Games is officially returning soon with a new tale inspired by one of the most iconic chapters in Collins' novels.

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is coming to theaters around the world on November 20, the same weekend that Rockstar Games releases Grand Theft Auto VI. It's safe to say it's going to be a big weekend for fans of the world's most popular entertainment franchises.

At the time of writing in mid-November, The Hunger Games movies are available to stream on Hulu in America, as well as the free streaming platform Tubi. Not only have the films surged into the top 10 on Hulu before the release of Sunrise on the Reaping, but they've also found success on Tubi as well. Sunrise on the Reaping is set up to be a massive hit at the box office, but there's no word yet on when the film will join streaming; it will likely be a while.

Jennifer Lawrence and Josh Hutcherson are confirmed to reprise their roles in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, which is set 24 years before the events of the first Hunger Games movie. Additional cast members include Mckenna Grace as Maysilee Donner, Joseph Zada as Haymitch Abernathy, Jesse Plemons as Plutarch Heavensbee, Ralph Fiennes as President Snow, Elle Fanning as Effie Trinket, Glenn Close as Drusilla Sickle, Kieran Culkin as Caesar Flickerman, Ben Wang as Wyatt Callow, and Maya Hawke as Wiress. Original Hunger Games director Francis Lawrence, who also worked on The Long Walk in 2025, is back in the director's chair for Sunrise on the Reaping.
