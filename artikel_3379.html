8 Years Later, George R. R. Martin’s ‘Fire & Blood’ Still Has No Sequel

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Being a George R.R. Martin fan requires a level of patience that would make even the most dedicated Maester of the Citadel proud. It's been 15 years since A Dance with Dragons arrived in bookstores, and readers are still waiting for The Winds of Winter to continue the main A Song of Ice and Fire series. In the meantime, Martin has kept himself busy expanding the history of Westeros, particularly the rather complicated family tree of House Targaryen. Unfortunately, one of those other books has now developed a lengthy wait of its own.

Martin's Fire & Blood will officially celebrate its eighth anniversary on November 20, 2026, without the publication of its promised second volume. The book was released on November 20, 2018, with publishers announcing from the beginning that it would be the first of two volumes chronicling the history of House Targaryen. Well, it looks like the television series that's based on Martin's work is going to end up concluding before Martin manages to get the words on the page because, eight years later, its follow-up still has no confirmed publication date.

Set roughly 300 years before A Game of Thrones, Fire & Blood begins with Aegon the Conqueror's invasion of Westeros and follows generations of Targaryen rulers. It also covers the Dance of the Dragons, the brutal civil war that forms the basis of HBO's House of the Dragon.

There's a big one coming up, alongside the final season of House of the Dragon and the next seasons of A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms. Earlier this year, Warner Bros. Discovery officially confirmed that Aegon’s Conquest will be released as a theatrical event movie, marking the first feature film set in the Game of Thrones universe.

“Beyond 2027, the slate will continue to leverage iconic intellectual property, including the theatrical event film Aegon’s Conquest from the Game of Thrones universe, the next installment of The Matrix, the next Batman film, and a live-action Jetsons film starring Jim Carrey.”

Aegon’s Conquest tells one of the most pivotal stories in George R.R. Martin’s mythology. It's also probably the coolest, because it's set 300 years before the events of Thrones and follows Aegon Targaryen alongside his sisters Visenya and Rhaenys, as they invade Westeros with their dragons Balerion, Vhagar, and Meraxes.
