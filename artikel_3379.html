Rosalía Calls Taylor Swift a ‘Master Songwriter,’ Reveals Her Favorite Swift Track

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Rosalía gushed that it was an “honor” for Taylor Swift to hail her Grammy-nominated Lux as a “perfect album” and said that she considers the pop star a “master songwriter.”

“To me, she’s a master, honestly,” Rosalía said during a red carpet interview for the Los40 Music Awards in Barcelona on Friday. “I think she’s incredible.” The Spanish singer also revealed her favorite Swift track. “I prefer individual songs rather than albums,” she added. “There’s one I really love, “Wildest Dreams.” It’s one of the songs I’ve listened to the most in my entire life.”

“Two perfect albums that I love and that I kept coming back to are from Olivia Dean and Rosalía,” Swift shared about what will likely end up on her Spotify Wrapped this year. “For Rosalía, she has created an ambitious and expansive concept album that could’ve only come from her vital life experience. Those albums are marvelous because they’re human, they’re made for people and are about the human experience; that’s why humans connect with them.”

The women’s camaraderie and mutual respect comes ahead of the 2027 Grammys where both are going up against each other in several categories, including Album of the Year for Rosalía’s classically experiment Lux and Swift’s glitzy The Life of a Showgirl, as well as Record of the Year for “Berghain” and “The Fate of Ophelia.”
