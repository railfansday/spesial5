Former NBA Champ David M. West Scripting ‘The Neighbor’; Myles Truitt & Stonebwoy Among Cast With Filming Underway In South Africa

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

EXCLUSIVE: Former NBA champion and All-Star David M. West is stepping into the world of filmmaking as screenwriter and executive producer of The Neighbor, a psychological thriller inspired by true events and directed by Kyle Scott.

Principal photography began November 4, 2026, in Cape Town, South Africa, where production is currently underway.

The international cast includes Myles Truitt (Stranger Things), Joseph Steven Yang (G20), Hana Kim (Mother/Android), and Ghanaian Afropop and dancehall musician Stonebwoy. West, who spent 15 seasons in the NBA and won two championships with the Golden State Warriors has scripted the project which is set in the San Francisco Bay Area. The movie follows “a veteran professional basketball player and his wife whose hopes for a peaceful new beginning unravel when an unsettling presence in their neighborhood draws them into a dangerous web of obsession, manipulation and buried secrets. As their sense of security begins to crumble, the couple discovers that the greatest threats may be hiding closer to home than they imagined.” Watch on Deadline

Kyle Scott’s credits including upcoming action-comedy Boyz Trip with Rome Flynn, Algee Smith, Desi Banks and Sivuyile Ngesi. The film’s production team includes lead producer Ama K. Abebrese (Beasts Of No Nation), producer Ty Walker (Die Hart), producer Sarsha Venord and Emmy-winning production designer Warren Gray, with David M. West, Kyle Scott and Jamez T. serving as executive producers.

The film brings together production companies across three continents, including In Peace Productions (Raleigh, North Carolina), KeyScott Entertainment (Brisbane, Australia), Blue Ice Africa (Cape Town, South Africa) and Braveheart Entertainment (Atlanta, Georgia).

West said: “Basketball has been such a big part of my life, and I’m grateful for the platform and opportunities it’s given me. As I step into filmmaking, I’m excited to expand that impact through storytelling and spark meaningful conversations. The Neighbor is a project I’m proud to be part of, and as production continues, I look forward to collaborating with our talented team to bring this story to life and create something audiences will remember.”

“The Neighbor represents an exciting evolution in my career as a filmmaker, marking my first psychological thriller and third major production in less than a year,” added director and executive producer Kyle Scott. “Through my collaboration with David M. West, I’m focused on building a diverse slate of films that challenge convention, transcend cultural and geographical boundaries, and deliver powerful storytelling to audiences around the world. Great cinema has no borders.”

Legal representation for The Neighbor is being handled by The Perry Law Group.
