Elon Musk Feels “Pretty Foolish” For Donating Mega-Bucks To Losing MAGA In Midterms

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Executive Editor, Legal, Labor & Politics

In a rare instance of humility and insight, Elon Musk admitted today he has expensive egg on his face out of MAGA‘s results in the midterm elections.

“I feel pretty foolish having spent so much money and not seeing much for it,” the world’s sometimes richest man said Wednesday at the New York Times‘ Dealbook conference. “It certainly shows that American elections are not just about money. If I could rewind the clock I would have spent less.”

When the SpaceX boss says he could have spent less on the likes of now ex-Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton in his unsuccessful Senate bid and the GOP’s Senate Leadership Fund, he is talking about a whooping nearly $500 million in donations. At the end of a race that saw the Democrats taking control of both houses of Congress and the Texas governorship, MAGA-linked Musk was the single largest donor of the off-year election. In fact, Musk is now the largest individual donor in a specific election in American history. Ironically, the previous record holder was Musk himself for the money he doled out to help get Donald Trump back in the White House in 2024. Watch on Deadline

Appearing virtually from China where Musk is accompanying Trump to the APEC Summit, the X owner’s return to the Andrew Ross Sorkin hosted shindig saw an often rickety and rambling interview on a wide range of topics. Facing tough-ish questions about his role in Russia’s now four-year long invasion of Ukraine, Musk boldly alleged that President Volodymyr Zelenskyy is “not always truthful” over his intentions with the South African born entrepreneur’s Starlink tech in pushing back Vladimir Putin’s forces.

Alternating between insights and inarticulation, Musk also touched on the fate of ex-Nvidia CEO Jensen Hueng, his desire to explore the stars, his need to scale up his companies, where AI company Anthropic is going next and where AI will lead us

Politics, however, in all senses dominated the conversation.

As did a reality distortion field, as they used to say of Apple’s Steve Jobs.

Insisting he is “not MAGA,” Texas-based Musk offered hope he could work with newly elected Gina Hinojosa when she takes over as governor. Stating “there are positives and negatives in both parties,” former DODGE leader and Trump advisor Musk remarkably said “I’m not sure what MAGA means, but if that means sort of blind adherence to a set of policies by the Republican Party, then I certainly don’t have that.”

This is the individual who at an October 2024 Trump rally told the crowd (as he was wearing a black MAGA ball cap): “As you can see, I’m not just MAGA. I’m Dark MAGA.”

Today, with the new realities of power in DC and many once totally red states now run and won by the Dems, Musk added: “I do think that it’s good to have some balance of power. You know, like not have as a general rule have not not have the presidency, senate, and House, all be of the same party.” He continued: “What was my actual goal here …which failed, by the way, obviously, was to have the Senate be 51 Republican …obviously, the Republicans were routed in this in this election.”
