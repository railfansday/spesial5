Google Gemini for CarPlay now available

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Google’s Gemini AI chatbot app for Apple CarPlay is now available. The company confirmed that it is bringing the full Gemini Live conversation experience to CarPlay on Wednesday, and the app update arrived on Thursday.

Google bringing Gemini to CarPlay is especially notable for one reason. Gemini was the last major AI chatbot holdout.

CarPlay has supported AI chatbot apps and other conversational apps for a while now.

iPhone apps including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot, Meta AI, and Grok all shipped CarPlay support already. Siri includes a standalone app in iOS 27, which also supports CarPlay.

Google has previously supported Gemini features through Google Maps on CarPlay, but not the Gemini Live experience through the dedicated chatbot app.

“Gemini on Apple CarPlay is rolling out globally starting today to all Gemini users on iOS across every supported Gemini Live language,” Google says.

“To get started, make sure you’ve updated to the latest version of the Gemini app on your iPhone, enabled microphone permissions, and used Gemini Live on your phone at least once. The next time you connect your phone to your car, tap the Gemini icon on your CarPlay display, then start a new conversation or pick up a recent one.”

Google Gemini for CarPlay is now available through the iPhone App Store.
