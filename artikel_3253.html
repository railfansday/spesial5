‘Schmigadoon!’ and ‘Widow’s Bay’ land Apple TV two Grammy nominations

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Apple TV today celebrated two Grammy nominations, one of which further establishes a canceled show as an unlikely awards success story. Here are the details.

Earlier this year, the Broadway adaptation of Apple TV’s ‘Schmigadoon!’ won four Tony Awards, including Best Musical, making Apple TV an EGOT winner by completing its collection of Emmy, Grammy, Oscar, and Tony awards.

‘Schmigadoon!’ premiered on Apple TV in 2021 and ran for two seasons, ending in 2023. In January 2024, show creator Cinco Paul confirmed that, although the third season had already been written with 25 new songs, Apple decided not to move forward with it.

Even before the Broadway adaptation’s Tony wins in June, a May report had raised hopes for the show’s return, revisiting an earlier interview in which ‘Schmigadoon!’ creator Cinco Paul revealed that there had been discussions about reviving it.

While there have been no further updates on a potential revival since then, fans got another reason to celebrate today, as the Broadway cast recording of ‘Schmigadoon!’ has been nominated for Best Musical Theater Album at the 2027 Grammy Awards.

It will compete against CHESS the Musical, The Lost Boys, Ragtime, and Two Strangers (Carry a Cake Across New York).

Additionally, ‘Widow’s Bay‘ earned a nomination for Best Score Soundtrack for Visual Media, putting composer David Fleming up against the soundtracks of ‘The Odyssey,’ ‘One Battle After Another,’ ‘Project Hail Mary,’ and ‘Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu‘.

The 2027 Grammy Awards will take place on Sunday, February 7, 2027. To check out the full list of nominees, follow this link.

Apple TV is available for $14.99 per month or as part of the Apple One bundle. It features hit TV shows and movies like Ted Lasso, Severance, The Studio, The Morning Show, Shrinking, and Silo.
