Bain Capital Crypto leads $110M Variational round at $1.5B valuation

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Variational raised $110 million at a $1.5 billion valuation to expand Omni’s derivatives markets and increase trading limits.

Onchain derivatives trading platform Variational has raised $110 million in a funding round led by Bain Capital Crypto, valuing the company at $1.5 billion as it seeks to challenge traditional brokerages by connecting crypto traders with liquidity from established financial markets.

The Series B round also received backing from Dragonfly, HSG, Peak XV and 50T Funds, according to the company’s Wednesday announcement.

Variational powers Omni, a platform offering leveraged exposure to more than 550 crypto and traditional markets. Unlike Hyperliquid, which maintains separate order books for its markets, Variational aggregates liquidity from onchain and off-chain sources, including Hyperliquid itself, a company spokesperson told Cointelegraph.

“Our unique model allows us to bridge off-chain liquidity to on-chain traders,” the spokesperson said, adding that liquidity from its unnamed tier-one market maker remains off-chain.

Since launching in early 2025, Omni has processed more than $475 billion in trading volume across over 100,000 accounts, the company said. Traditional markets now account for more than half of its daily trading volume.

Variational also launched its swaps product in September, which has since generated more than $80 billion in trading volume, according to the company.

Despite traditional assets accounting for most daily trading volume, Variational’s customers remain primarily crypto-native retail traders, a company spokesperson told Cointelegraph. Asked about competing with traditional brokerages such as Robinhood and Interactive Brokers, the spokesperson pointed to the need to make the platform more accessible to users unfamiliar with cryptocurrency.

“The platform needs to abstract away the ‘crypto’ feel,” the spokesperson said, adding that the company is developing email-based account registration and alternative deposit methods to remove the current requirements for crypto wallets and USDC.

Variational faces stiff competition in onchain derivatives trading. Hyperliquid recorded approximately $274 billion in reported trading volume over the past 30 days, compared with $82 billion for Lighter and $62 billion for Variational, according to DeFiLlama data at the time of writing on Wednesday. Variational also ranked third in open interest at approximately $688 million, behind Hyperliquid’s $5.7 billion and Aster’s $1.2 billion.
