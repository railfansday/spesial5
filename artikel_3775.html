Amazon has dropped the price of the 65-inch Toshiba Z670R to $549.97 from $748.99, taking nearly $200 off and bringing it to its lowest price ever, according to price trackers. The deal also includes free delivery to a room of your choice, which normally costs $19.99, though it does not cover unpacking, setup, packaging removal, or haul-away services. You also get a 90-day free trial of Amazon Music with your purchase.

Beyond the discounts, the Z670R has quite a bit going for it, starting with a 4K Mini-LED panel that combines QLED color with local dimming to help bright highlights stand out without washing out darker scenes. It also supports Dolby Vision IQ and HDR10+, so you can enjoy compatible HDR content across a wide range of movies, shows, and streaming services.

Gaming is another area where this TV makes a strong case for itself—its panel can run at up to 144Hz, while Variable Refresh Rate, Auto Low Latency Mode, and AMD FreeSync Premium are there to keep fast movement looking smoother and cut down on screen tearing with a compatible console or gaming PC, making it well-suited for racing games, shooters, and sports titles. You also get four HDMI inputs, with support for 4K at 144Hz, so there is room for a console, PC, soundbar, and another device without constantly swapping cables.

Fire TV is built in as well, which means Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, and other major streaming apps are available without adding a separate streaming stick, while Alexa handles voice searches and basic controls. The built-in audio is also more substantial than what you get from many slim TVs, with a dedicated bass woofer and Dolby Atmos support, so you may be able to live without a soundbar at first if you are setting up a bedroom or smaller living room. One potential downside is that Mini-LED does not give you the perfect blacks of OLED, so movie fans who watch mostly in a dark room may still prefer to pay more for one. For someone who splits time between movies, sports, and gaming, though, the Z670R gives you a lot of flexibility for about $550 without immediately requiring extra equipment.
