All Cooking Station Recipes in Loftia

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Loftia is now out, and setting up the Cooking Station will be one of your first tasks. Just like in the demo, this workstation allows us to prepare different food items based on the recipes you obtain.

While the number of recipes you have will be limited at first, you’ll be able to get more by grinding the in-game content (quests, research stations, and more).

In Loftia, cooking recipes appear across three levels (based on your workstation’s own). Naturally, several recipes will remain locked until you increase the level of your Cooking Station.

I will be adding new recipes to the tables above as I unlock and discover them on my adventures.
