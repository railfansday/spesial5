PSV raise Van Bommel’s pricetag to 50 million

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Roma is searching for strikers ahead of the January transfer window to improve Gian Piero Gasperini’s squad.

The coach is looking for a pacey winger who can add some of the qualities lacking in the attack. In addition to Endrick and Gittens, the Giallorossi are also pursuing Ruben Van Bommel.

The 192cm-tall, 2004-born player would inevitably bring the physicality that’s lacking in the Roma attack. However, the Dutch winger is performing well, boosting PSV’s valuation.

PSV is putting up a wall for Ruben Van Bommel. As reported by Tuttosport, the Dutch club has announced that the 2004-born player’s value is the same as Saibari, whom they sold to Bayern Munich: approximately €50 million.

The winger, formerly of AZ Alkmaar, has played 1,032 minutes this season, scoring four goals, half of which came in the last two league games, and providing five assists. PSV’s valuation is pushing the player away from Roma.
