Gallery: ‘Back in the hunt’, ‘The Devil wears Hutchinson’ – Today’s front pages in Italy

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

AC Milan produced a stunning second-half comeback to beat Olympiacos 4-2 and boost their chances of reaching the Europa League knockout stage.

After 50 minutes in Greece it looked like being another miserable European night for Milan, who trailed through a Mike Maignan own-goal and a strike from Jota Silva. However, Omari Hutchinson inspired a comeback, scoring after a solo run then setting up Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic.

Diego Moreira finished off a counter-attack in the closing seconds to make sure of the three points, which boost the Rossoneri back into the top 24 with three games to go.

Below are how the sports newspapers in Italy look, with each of them mentioning Milan’s comeback in Piraeus.

La Gazzetta dello Sport: “Comeback. Milan rally and gets back in the hunt. Trailing by two goals, the Rossoneri win in Athens (4-2), inspired by Hutchinson. Amorim eyes the play-offs.”

Corriere dello Sport: “Milan’s time. They turn the tables on Olympiacos: coming back from 0-2 to win 4-2 in just 39 minutes. A nightmarish start in Piraeus, but the Englishman Hutchinson proved decisive with his moments of brilliance: a goal and two assists. Cissé (his 10th goal of the season), Pulisic, and Moreira also got on the scoresheet. Amorim’s side now sit in 20th place, within the play-off zone.”

Tuttosport: “The Devil wears Hutchinson. From 0-2 down to a 4-2 win against Olympiacos: the Englishman orchestrates the turnaround. A big-club response from Milan following a horrific first 50 minutes.

“The playmaker scores with a spectacular coast-to-coast run, provides assists for Cissé and Pulisic, and finally launches the counter-attack finished off by Moreira. Amorim finds a glimmer of light in Europe, too: ‘It’s the beginning of something’.”

QS: “A comeback for the Diavolo. Amorim, a display of play-off-worthy pride. In the Europa League, after falling two goals behind, Milan fought back, unleashing a lethal four-goal salvo – courtesy of Hutchinson, Cisse, Pulisic, and Moreira – to overwhelm Olympiacos and put the Rossoneri back in the hunt for a spot in the round of 16.”
