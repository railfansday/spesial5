qwen 4 max for Automation Builders: Price, Limits and a Simple Routing Setup

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

qwen 4 max is now out of preview. Alibaba made the qwen4-max model generally available on 16 November 2026, five days after the preview went up. If you build automations for a small business or your own creator workflow, the headline is simple: it's a flagship-class multimodal model with a 1M-token context window at a lower list price than many top models. Caching matters a lot here. OpenRouter's page says the price people actually pay is often well below list because of caching, and it shows a weighted average input price under $0.30 per 1M tokens at the time I checked.

Example arithmetic (my own numbers, not a benchmark): a task with 20,000 input tokens and 2,000 output tokens costs about $0.03 + $0.009 = $0.039 at list price. If 15,000 of those input tokens are a repeated system prompt that gets served from cache, input drops to about $0.0086, so the task costs roughly $0.018. Your real bill depends on your prompts, cache hits and how much the model thinks.
