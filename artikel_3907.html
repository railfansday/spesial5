Grand Theft Auto VI pre-load now available, PS5 Pro support confirmed [Update]

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Rockstar Games has released a new trailer for Grand Theft Auto VI to commemorate the start of pre-loading ahead of the game’s November 19 release, as well as confirmed that the game will support PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution and ray-traced shadows on PlayStation 5 Pro.

Grand Theft Auto VI has been fully developed from the ground up to provide the best possible experience for the current generation of consoles, targeting 30 frames per second while pushing both platforms to their absolute limits to bring the vast and rich world of Leonida to life—while maintaining the density and visual fidelity of all its environments and inhabitants.

The PlayStation 5 Pro offers breathtaking visuals, with improved clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows only available on PlayStation 5 Pro.

Grand Theft Auto VI for PlayStation 5 includes high-fidelity haptic feedback fully integrated across the game on DualSense controllers and adjustable via accessibility settings, improved spatial audio rendering so all in-game sounds take full advantage of Tempest 3D Audio, and improved load times, making Grand Theft Auto VI play best on PlayStation 5 consoles.

The game will also be available via Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One consoles, utilizing Xbox Series S graphics settings. And as with all cloud streaming, connection speeds and other technical factors may influence game performance in ways that would not impact local gameplay.

Grand Theft Auto VI will also fully support Remote Play on PlayStation and Xbox.

Grand Theft Auto VI is due out for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series on November 19.

Update 10:55 a.m.: An additional “PlayStation 5 Features” trailer has also been released.
