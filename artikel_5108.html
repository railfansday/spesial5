LIQD, a crypto casino and sportsbook, takes deposits in 12 cryptocurrencies and keeps each balance in the coin the player sent. USDT can arrive over seven networks, so the cashier asks for a network as well as a coin when depositing.

Dollar, euro and Canadian dollar amounts are shown for reference in your account only, and move with the market price.

The lobby covers slots, blackjack, roulette, baccarat, live casino and game shows from external providers. Next to those titles, LIQD Casino offers its own games under the LIQD Originals name, including Plinko, Mines and Blackjack.

Football is listed first in the sportsbook menu, followed by basketball, tennis and other sports. The football competitions include the UEFA Champions League, the Premier League and the Bundesliga.

LIQD accepts BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC, SOL, LTC, XRP, DOGE, TRX, BNB, TON and POL, and the two stablecoins can be sent on the widest range of networks.

Because crypto transfers have both an asset and a network, USDT on Tron and on Ethereum are separate choices in LIQD’s cashier. For USDT held on Tron, the player selects TRC20 and sends the tokens to the deposit address the cashier shows.

The cashier also shows a minimum for each coin and network, and LIQD’s terms say a deposit below it is not credited. A transfer to the wrong address or network may not be recoverable, since a blockchain transfer cannot be stopped once it has been sent.

LIQD takes deposits in crypto only, and USD, EUR and CAD serve as display currencies. The figure shown in those currencies moves with the coin’s market price.

At a Bitcoin price of $80,000, a balance of 0.01 BTC shows as $800. If the price drops to $76,000, the same 0.01 BTC shows as $760 without a single bet in between.

To withdraw, players select a coin and network in the cashier and enter their wallet address. LIQD’s terms do not specify a fixed processing time. Withdrawals are subject to verification and regulatory review, and larger amounts undergo manual review.

Each Originals round combines a server seed, a client seed that the player controls, and a nonce.

Once the server seed is revealed, the player can reproduce the result with the Seeds and Verify controls built into the games. LIQD states that its Originals have been audited by the Provably Fair Foundation. Slots, live tables and other third-party games run on their providers’ own systems and fall outside this verification.

LIQD’s VIP program has 31 levels across ten tiers. Progress depends on the total amount wagered, measured in US dollars. The starting tier, Water, includes instant rakeback. Later tiers offer benefits such as weekly and monthly bonuses, subject to the terms of each offer.

LIQD Ltd is registered in Belize under company number 000052980. Its licence, ALSI-202603048-FI2, was issued by the Government of the Autonomous Island of Anjouan, Union of Comoros, and Return to Player Ltd in Cyprus acts as payment agent.

The terms list the US, the UK, France, the Netherlands, Spain, Austria, Cyprus, Comoros, and FATF high-risk call-for-action jurisdictions as restricted. LIQD runs risk-based AML and KYC checks, and verification may be requested.

Players must be 18 or older and can set wager and loss limits. Self-exclusion periods start at three hours, and permanent exclusion is also available. During an active exclusion, the player can still log in and withdraw the available balance, and live support is listed as open 24/7.

For anyone who starts chasing losses or staking more than planned, Gambling Therapy offers support. A loss limit set early is already in place from the first bet.
