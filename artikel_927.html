The 2026 NWSL season began in March and is now into the playoff matches to determine which teams will reach the Championship match on 21 November.

After the quarter-finals last weekend, just four teams remain: Gotham FC, Washington Spirit, Kansas City Current and Utah Royals.

With plenty of live streaming options for the semi-finals, including FREE coverage in the UK, read on for all the information on how to watch the 2026 National Women's Soccer League.

The NWSL has its very own streaming platform, NWSL+, which is totally free of charge.

All that's required is a simple email registration. Geo-restrictions apply, so the game will be blocked for those in the US or anywhere that has a broadcaster for the matches.

Fans in the USA can watch the semi-final between Washington Spirit on CBS and its streaming service, Paramount+ ($8.99/month).

The match between Gotham FC and Kansas City Current will be available on ABC and ESPN ($13.99/month).

Fans in the UK can watch both play-off semi-finals on TNT Sports.

TNT Sports 1 will show Spirit vs Royals while TNT Sports 2 is the go-to for Gotham vs Kansas, while HBO Max (£30.99 a month) hosts the live stream.

TSN are the official broadcasters of the NWSL playoff semi-finals in Canada.

Streaming access is available from CA$24.99/month.

The reigning NWSL champions finished top of the regular-season standings to claim the NWSL Shield for the first time. Led by head coach Juan Carlos Amorós, they are aiming to become the first team to win back-to-back Championship titles since the league's introduction of the current playoff format.

Kansas City enjoyed a strong regular season, finishing fourth in the standings before edging past Angel City 1-0 after extra time in the quarter-finals. The 2024 champions will be looking to reclaim the title, with back-to-back MVP Temwa Chawinga and Croix Bethune key to their attacking threat.

The 2025 Championship runners-up finished third in the regular season and made a statement with a 4-1 quarter-final win over Portland Thorns. With Trinity Rodman leading the attack, they will be looking to go one step further this year after falling short in last season's final.

Utah are the surprise package of this year's playoffs, finishing seventh in the regular season before causing an upset and knocking out second seeds San Diego Wave. Having reached the semi-finals for the first time, they will be hoping to continue their impressive run and reach their first-ever Championship final.
