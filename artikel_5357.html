U.S. Treasury yields rise as Nvidia's board reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Nvidia stock jumped more than 25% in premarket trading after the board voted unanimously to bring Huang back

Nvidia $NVDA reinstated Jensen Huang as president and chief executive on Monday, less than two weeks after he left the role. Huang returns to the company's board, and the change takes effect Monday. The company said the board's vote was unanimous.

Nvidia stock jumped more than 25% in premarket trading, according to CNBC. U.S. Treasury yields rose after the announcement, according to The Wall Street Journal. The 10-year yield was up at 4.98%, while the 2-year sat close to 4.72%.

The vote followed several rounds of talks and sit-downs with Huang, the company said. The board heard from top U.S. government officials and concluded on that basis that bringing Huang back creates no risk to national security, according to Nvidia. The company filed the announcement with the SEC.

"The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead Nvidia, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers," Board Chair Harvey C. Jones said in a statement. Jones will remain chair. He thanked Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress for her service as interim CEO, a role she held alongside her CFO duties.

"I am honored to return to Nvidia and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution," Huang said in a statement. "I can't wait to get back to work."

The board set up a panel of independent directors to examine reports that a foreign intelligence operation had targeted Huang. The panel hired outside counsel, and the board said the inquiry will be carried through to its end. Huang supports the investigation and is committed to cooperating with it, the company said.

The board formed a National Security and Export Control Committee of independent directors to oversee trade compliance and national security risks. Nvidia is searching for a chief compliance officer, a new position that will report to the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee. The company said it will continue to cooperate with government investigations and inquiries.

President Donald Trump wrote on social media that the board made "the correct and proper decision," according to CNBC. Trump said the U.S. government has "no reason to believe" Huang's return endangers national security and that officials have seen nothing showing he broke the law.

Huang resigned on Nov. 12 amid reports of multiple affairs. He had been unfaithful with at least two women, and U.S. officials suspect one of them of relaying information to China's intelligence services. Beijing has dismissed the spying allegation, according to Bloomberg. Nvidia named Kress interim CEO. Jones said on Nvidia's earnings call last week that directors had accepted Huang's decision to step down and had no plans to bring him back.
