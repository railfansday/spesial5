Deka Lash Pooler Offers 50% Off Lash, Brow and Facial Services Guests Haven't Tried Before

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

wo-week "New to You" promotion runs November 23 through December 6, 2026

POOLER, Ga., Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Deka Lash Pooler is inviting guests to explore a new addition to their beauty routine with its "New to You" promotion. From November 23 through December 6, 2026, guests can receive 50% off any lash, brow or facial service they haven't tried before.

The two-week offer gives residents of Pooler, Savannah and surrounding communities an opportunity to try a different service ahead of holiday gatherings, seasonal celebrations and year-end events. Whether guests have been considering a lash lift, a brow treatment or a facial, the promotion makes it easier to explore a new look or enjoy time dedicated to skincare.

Located at 1 N Godley Station Blvd in Pooler, Deka Lash offers customized eyelash extensions, lash lifts and tinting, brow lamination, brow tinting, brow shaping and personalized facials. The studio's approach emphasizes individual preferences, with consultations designed to help guests choose services that complement their style and desired results.

Guests interested in enhancing their natural lashes can explore lash lift and tint options, while those seeking a fuller-looking, more defined brow appearance can consider brow lamination or tinting. Personalized facials provide another way to refresh a beauty routine, with options focused on cleansing and hydration.

The "New to You" offer centers on trying a service for the first time. Guests who already visit Deka Lash Pooler for one treatment can ask the studio about applying the promotion to another lash, brow or facial service they have not previously experienced.

To explore services or schedule an appointment, visit Deka Lash Pooler. Guests should confirm eligibility and any additional promotion details with the studio when booking. The offer ends December 6, 2026.

Deka Lash Pooler provides personalized lash extensions, brow services and facial services in Pooler, Georgia. Its certified lash artists help guests explore customized beauty options, including eyelash extensions, lash lifts and brow enhancements, in a welcoming studio environment.
