NestWorks C500, the Desktop CNC Named in TIME's Best Inventions of 2026, Available to Order

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Precision metal machining, automated from tool change to chip evacuation, and CAM software that takes a design from model to machined part in four steps. The NestWorks C500 brings serious manufacturing capability to compact workspaces.

SHENZHEN, China, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Following its inclusion in TIME's Best Inventions of 2026, the NestWorks C500 desktop CNC machine is now available to order through the company's official online store at US$6,499, its lowest price of 2026. Designed for engineers, product developers, makers and small businesses, the C500 embodies the NestWorks promise: a faster way to make, turning digital designs into precision-machined parts without the wait, the outsourcing or the learning curve of conventional CNC.

Serious Metal Cutting, Sized for Your Workspace

Turning a digital design into a functional metal part usually means specialized equipment, complex machining processes or weeks spent waiting on external manufacturing services. For independent creators and small engineering teams, these barriers slow down prototype validation, product development and small-batch production. This puts real manufacturing capability just out of reach.

The NestWorks C500 closes the gap between desktop CNC machines and full-scale industrial equipment. A desktop CNC built for serious metal cutting, it combines a highly rigid machine structure, automated machining workflows and proprietary CAM software to deliver precision manufacturing in a compact workspace.

Its inclusion in TIME's Best Inventions of 2026 reflects the NestWorks mission: make precision manufacturing accessible to all, bringing design and production closer together.

The C500 features an 800W electric spindle running at up to 18,000 RPM, mounted in a rigid cast-aluminum structure built for stable, repeatable machining performance.

A five-position automatic tool changer, integrated tool setter and automated workpiece centering system minimize manual setup and keep jobs running without interruption. Minimum quantity lubrication (MQL) cooling and a tri-auger automatic chip removal system support continuous machining with minimal supervision, so the machine keeps working even when you step away.

NestStudio, NestWorks' in-house CAM software, takes a design from model to machined part in four steps: import the model, let the software recognize tools and materials, generate optimized machining strategies, then simulate and preview the full toolpath before cutting. Built-in material and cutting-tool libraries remove the need for manual parameter configuration. No steep learning curve, no third-party software required.

Under appropriate machining conditions, the C500 supports a wide range of materials, including aluminum alloys, steel, wood and plastics. Typical applications include functional prototyping, custom mechanical components, robotics projects and small-batch manufacturing.

By bringing production in-house, users shorten development cycles, accelerate design iteration, and keep full control over their projects.

Manufactured by NestWorks, in Our Factory

Every C500 is built in NestWorks' wholly self-owned factory, where machining, assembly, and final inspection are all controlled in-house. NestWorks guarantees consistent quality at volume and reliable delivery as orders scale by owning the entire production line, rather than outsourcing.

The NestWorks C500 is now available to order through the company's official online store at US$6,499, its lowest price of 2026. Orders are expected to ship approximately one month after purchase.
