XRP Ledger Posts 479% Payment Surge as XRP Heads For Recovery

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

XRP appears to be starting the new month on a stronger foot as its network activity and price movements over the past day have both turned out to be promising.

Latest onchain data from XRPScan shows a substantial increase in the asset's payment volume on the XRP ledger after closing October with weaker figures.

According to the data, the amount of XRP transferred between accounts on the XRP ledger has surged from around 229 million XRP on October 31 to over 1.33 billion XRP on November 1st.

This marks a substantial increase of over 479% in the metric within the 24-hour period of crossing into November, suggesting that the network is starting the new month with stronger momentum.

While the surge in the XRP payment volume has come at a time when the asset is beginning to show signs of a potential recovery after several weeks of high volatility, it appears that the XRP ledger is also seeing a rebound in its network usage.

Furthermore, the data also suggests that the impact of the recent enormous Binance heist on the network is gradually fading, as activities on the XRP ledger slowed following the incident.

The massive surge in the XRP payment volume could sometimes provide a bullish outlook for XRP's price as it typically signals an increase in the amount of XRP transferred from one account to another, which shows that traders are actively engaging.

While this does not guarantee an instant rally and XRP is yet to show any major price recovery as it is still showing mixed price action, market analysts are hopeful that stronger network activity could rebuild momentum and help the asset restore the gaining streak broken in October.
