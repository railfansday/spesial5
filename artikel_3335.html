‘The Hunger Games: Sunrise On The Reaping’ Album Boasts 29 Songs; Releases Ahead Of Film

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

UPDATED, Nov. 20: The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is finally here, full of both bleak torturous scenes and also hopeful ones.

With a full companion album complete with many original songs inspired by the film and some “Arena Remixes” of certain artists’ hits, the songs in the film narrow down to a sparse five. Find them below:

PREVIOUS, Nov. 13: The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping (The Album) is now available to stream and for purchase a week ahead of the film’s arrival in theaters Nov. 20.

The full tracklist on the soundtrack double album for the upcoming prequel, based on Suzanne Collins’ fifth book in the series, was unveiled at the start of November. Watch on Deadline

Along with the three already-announced tracks — Noah Kahan’s “Draw You Out,” Gigi Perez’s “Favor” and The Smashing Pumpkins’ “Black Stars” — The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping (The Album) from MCA Records also features the likes of Stevie Nicks, Ella Langley, The Cure, Depeche Mode, actressJennifer Lawrence and others. Star Mckenna Grace, who portrays the fierce Maysilee Williams in the film, has contributed to the soundtrack, and previous star Jennifer Lawrence’s rendition of “Deep in the Meadow” is on the album as well. Some songs have been remixed for the arena, like Kacey Musgraves’ “Ruthless” and Stevie Nicks’ “Doing the Best That I Can.” Still more artists like Noah Cyrus and Ella Langley have written new songs for the companion album.

PREVIOUSLY, OCTOBER 30: Gigi Perez is the latest artist to contribute a single to the companion album for The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.

Perez’s new single, “Favor” is now out as of Thursday night.

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping (The Album) is executive produced by Grammy-winning producer Dave Cobb (Chris Stapleton, Sturgill Simpson) and brings together 29 cross-genre artists.

“It is truly an honor to have created a song for The Hunger Games. I’ve been greatly impacted by stories told through The Hunger Games since I was 12 years old,” the Cuban-American singer said in a statement. “Writing this song allowed me to share another part of who I am, drawing from my own experiences with grief and the longing to reconnect with the ones I love who are no longer on this earth. It is a privilege to share something so personal within a story that has meant so much to me for so long.”

The Smashing Pumpkins’ song “Black Stars,” came out October 15.

The band’s frontman Billy Corgan was working on a solo album when The Smashing Pumpkins were invited to contribute an original song to the film. Corgan tapped producer Butch Vig to help pick the song that best fit into the world of Panem during the 50th Hunger Games and second Quarter Quell, in which Haymitch Abernathy (Joseph Zada) competes.

The music video that accompanies the song contains purple smoke, which corresponds to the purple and yellow smoke that fill the arena in the trailers for Sunrise on the Reaping.

“Black Stars” joins Noah Kahan’s “Draw You Out,” the first single announced off the album.

The album compilation for the latest Hunger Games film comes as no surprise to readers, viewers and fans who have kept track of the musical movements in the franchise since the original trilogy of books was published and served as the basis for a quartet of films starring Jennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson, Liam Hemsworth and more.

Taylor Swift wrote “Safe and Sound (feat. Civil Wars)” featured in the first film which came out in 2012 as well as “Eyes Open” for the companion soundtrack. Lorde wrote “Yellow Flicker Beat” for Mockingjay Part 1. Olivia Rodrigo wrote “Can’t Catch Me Now” for The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes. Coldplay also wrote “Atlas” for Catching Fire.
