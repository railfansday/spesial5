Hayden Panettiere’s Mother Blames Brian Hickerson For Star’s Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

While the entertainment world continues to mourn the death of Nashville and Heroes star Hayden Panettiere, her mother, Lesley Vogel, is placing blame squarely on Panettiere’s former boyfriend, Brian Hickerson.

On Monday, Vogel gave her first public interview since Panettiere’s Aug. 16 death, speaking with Inside Edition about the often estranged relationship she had with her daughter and her emotions following her death. “Sadly, she allowed Brian to reel her back in,” she said. “Hayden had clearly let Brian somehow take over many aspects of her life.” (Hickerson has denied any invovlement with Panettiere’s death and has not been charged with a crime.)

Panettiere was found in her Greenville, South Carolina home, after police responded to calls for an unresponsive woman. She was 36 years old. In a public statement provided by her representatives, her father Skip Panettiere called her an “incredible light and a force of nature who brought immeasurable love and joy to all who knew her — and to the millions who watched her onscreen.” In Sept., the Greenville County Coroner’s Office revealed her cause of death as the “toxic effects” of fentanyl and other drugs. Tests found traces of Xanax, the muscle relaxant Methocarbamol, and Quetiapine, a drug that is prescribed for schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, or depression, but the coroner said there were no signs of trauma or foul play.  On Oct. 28, the Greenville Police Department closed their investigation, ruling Panettiere’s death as “accidental.”

Throughout her time in the spotlight, Panettiere was open about her struggles with mental health; including postpartum depression following the birth of her daughter but also anxiety and substance abuse issues tied to her early brushes with fame. She also had a publicly contentious relationship with Hickerson, who remained her on-again-off-again boyfriend until the time of her death. In 2020, she requested a restraining order against him. And in her May memoir, This Is Me: A Reckoning, Panettiere described their relationship being fraught with emotional abuse and physical violence. At the time of her death, Panettiere had recently finished a stint in a rehab facility. And according to a police report obtained by NBC News, Hickerson and his brother Zach were both in the home at the time of the actresses’ death. (Brian Hickerson’s lawyer declined to comment on Vogel’s claims. Zach Hickerson did not immediately respond to Rolling Stone’s request for comment.)

When news of Panettiere’s death was first released, Vogel told NBC News that she had been extremely concerned about her daughter’s relationship with Hickerson. “This person in her life that we have been trying to get rid of for quite some time was with her at her death, and that was Brian Hickerson,” she said at the time. “There is a lot of history in the past that Brian has enabled Hayden many times, and this is why her father and I had been concerned for a long time about him.”

But in her most recent interview with Inside Edition, Vogel placed the blame firmly on Hickerson. When she was asked if Hickerson was responsible for Panettiere’s death, she responded, “Absolutely. Vogel claims Panettiere’s treatment for substance abuse was “going well,” but blamed Hickerson for their estranged relationship. She also claimed that Hickerson sent “harassing emails” to her after Panettiere’s death, threatening “action” if she kept making statements.

During the interview, Vogel also dismissed rumors that she was not invited to Panettiere’s celebration of life, instead saying she chose not to attend to keep the peace. ”I knew that some of the people there wanted to believe that I’m the horrible mother because Hayden was angry and made statements,” she said. “I just didn’t want to rock the boat either.
