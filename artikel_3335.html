These Stylish Meta Oakley Smart Glasses Are $120 Off for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

There's is no shortage of smart glasses on the market right now, but many of them have an awkward, clunky aesthetic that doesn’t exactly blend into your everyday style. If you’re looking for an alternative that won’t stick out like a sore thumb, the Oakley Meta HSTN smart glasses are a fun, sporty option, and during Amazon’s early Black Friday sale, they're discounted to $359 (originally $479).

While they have similar core features to the Meta Ray-Ban Gen 2 (also 25% off ahead of the holiday), these frames are sportier, and unlike the Ray-Bans, they shoot video in 3K rather than 1080p. They also have a bigger battery designed to last all day (up to eight hours on a charge, and five additional charges from the case, for a total of 48 hours).  In addition, the two open-ear speakers have been repositioned, allowing for audio to be directed more effectively towards the ears with less sound leakage.

Available in a range of stylish frame and lens colors, PCMag dubs these the best “smart glasses for Instagram addicts” given their social-focused design; users can use the embedded cameras to snap POV videos or photos and upload them seamlessly to Facebook or Instagram. They have a 12MP camera and come with 32GB of flash storage.

If you want a pair of smart glasses but have been on the fence due to mixed feedback about sound and battery life, the Oakley Meta HSTN smart glasses are a more advanced model that solves both of those problems with upgrades that outdo the Ray-Bans while allowing you to film and shoot POV videos on the go. Now $120 off, they're also at an all-time low price.
