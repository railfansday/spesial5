Here Are the 2027 Grammy Nominations in Top Categories: Updating Live

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Recording Academy is revealing the nominees in all 100 categories. We'll show the top categories right here as soon as soon as they're announced.

It’s Grammy Nominations morning! The Recording Academy is announcing the nominations for the 69th annual Grammy Awards in all (gulp) 100 categories in a livestream. It’s the first time they’ve done that. In recent years, the academy usually announced the nominations in the six “General Field” categories, and then a representative sample of about 12 more categories. This year they’re going all in.

A roster of past Grammy winners and nominees will help announce the nominees. They include reigning best new artist winner Olivia Dean, who is widely expected to sweep the nominations; Kehlani, who won two Grammys in the R&B field earlier this year; and Amy Allen, who has won songwriter of the year, non-classical the last two years running;

We’ll post the full list of nominees (and much more) the moment the academy announces the last nominee in the last category on the livestream. In the meantime, we’ll show 18 of the biggest categories here as soon as they’re announced.

There’s a reason media focuses on the six “General Field” categories – album, record and song of the year; best new artist; and producer and songwriter of the year, non-classical. Every voting member of the academy (there are currently about 16,000) is permitted to vote in those six categories without using up any of the limited number of categories in which they may vote.

Record, album and song of the year have been presented every year since the first Grammy ceremony in 1959. Best new artist was added for the second ceremony, later in 1959. Producer of the year, non-classical was first presented in 1975. Songwriter of the year, non-classical was first presented in 2023.

The 69th annual Grammy Awards will be presented on Sunday Feb. 7 at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles. After airing on CBS every year from 1973-2026, the show will move to ABC, Disney+ and Hulu.
