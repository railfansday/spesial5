Watch Taylor Swift Surprise Brenda Lee With an RIAA Diamond Plaque for This ‘Incredible Song’

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Lee's "Rockin' Around the Christmas Tree" is only the second holiday song to reach the milestone.

Taylor Swift had an early Christmas present for Rock & Hall of Fame and Country Music Hall of Fame member Brenda Lee.

Swift surprised Lee, 81, with the news that Lee’s holiday classic “Rockin’ Around the Christmas Tree” has been certified Diamond by the Recording Industry Association of America for more than 10 million units in the United States, and presented her with the Diamond plaque on Nov. 16 in Nashville. The sweet moment was shared in a video on Monday (Nov. 23).

The milestone makes “Rockin’ Around the Christmas Tree” only the second holiday song to reach the milestone, joining Mariah Carey’s “All I Want for Christmas Is You.”

“I’m one of the many, many fans of your iconic song ‘Rockin’ Around the Christmas Tree.’ It is such an incredible song, and I’m not the only person who feels that way, because I’m very honored to get to present you with your Diamond certification for more than 10 million copies,” Swift tells Lee in an Instagram video of the moment on Lee’s social media accounts. “You recorded this when you were only 13 years old, and it is one of only two Christmas songs to ever reach this milestone. You’re in very good company with Mariah Carey.”

Swift adds, “This is yours now and will be yours forever. … On behalf of all the fans and all the families and people whose Christmases this song has absolutely added dazzle and sparkle and warmth to, thank you so much for your incredible work.”

“It means the world to me that ‘Rockin’ Around the Christmas Tree’ is Diamond,” Lee said in a statement. “Johnny Marks, Owen Bradley and all the amazing musicians would be so proud. I am so thankful to the fans around the world who continue to love this song. Taylor surprising me was the cherry on top!”

This isn’t the first time Swift has honored Lee. When she was 18 years old, the pop superstar wrote an essay about Lee titled “Rare Peer,” which was published as part of the 2017 book Woman Walk the Line: How the Women in Country Music Changed Our Lives, from author and journalist Holly Gleason.

“Rockin’ Around the Christmas Tree” was written by Marks, also known for holiday classics such as “Rudolph, the Red-Nosed Reindeer” and “A Holly Jolly Christmas.” Bradley recorded and produced the song in October 1958, with a 13-year-old Lee doing only three takes of the song in one session. “Rockin’ Around the Christmas Tree” was released in 1958 and became an annual holiday favorite. The song reached No. 3 on Billboard‘s Christmas Singles chart in December 1965, and then experienced a resurgence in 1990 when it was featured in the movie Home Alone.

In 2023, “Rockin’ Around The Christmas Tree” reached a new milestone, topping the Billboard Hot 100 for the first time, 65 years after it was released. The song spent three weeks at No. 1, which included a return to the top of the charts in late December. Her song became only the third holiday No. 1 in Hot 100 history.

“Rockin’ Around the Christmas Tree” was inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame in 2019.
