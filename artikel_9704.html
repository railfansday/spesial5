Zeabur alternatives: where to move before the December 31 shutdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Posted on Nov 25 • Originally published at ownkube.io

Zeabur is shutting down. Running services on its shared clusters pause on December 1, 2026, dedicated servers stop on their own expiry dates during December, and everything stops with its data deleted on December 31. The closest alternatives are Railway, Render, Fly.io, DigitalOcean App Platform, Ownkube and a self-hosted panel such as Coolify on a VPS.

Zeabur announced the wind-down on October 17, after its second security incident in two months. For most apps the move is the same: redeploy the repo, restore the database from a dump, rotate every secret, then point the domain.

Dates come from Zeabur's shutdown notice and its migration and refunds post. If your app runs on a dedicated server, its expiry date can come before December 31, so check that first.

We priced a 1 vCPU / 1 GiB web app averaging 40% CPU, a 1 GiB Postgres with 10 GB of storage and the smallest cache each platform offers. The figures come from our cheapest way to host a web app with Postgres comparison, from list prices as of October 2026.

A VPS with Coolify can come in under all of these if you already run servers, but the backups, upgrades and security patches become your job, and a breach on your own server is yours to find.

Railway is the closest match to how Zeabur felt: connect a repo, add a database from a template, and get a URL. It meters CPU and memory per second, Hobby is $5 a month with $5 of usage included, and it has a Southeast Asia region in Singapore. It bills after the fact, so a busy month shows up on the invoice. See Railway alternatives and Railway pricing explained.

Render runs web services, workers, cron jobs and Postgres at flat instance prices, from $7 a month for 0.5 CPU and 512 MB, and Singapore is one of its regions. Flat prices suit an app that is busy all day. See Render pricing explained.

Fly.io runs Machines in many cities, including Tokyo, Hong Kong and Singapore, which matters if your users were close to Zeabur's Asia regions. Small Machines are cheap. Its Managed Postgres starts at about $38 a month, which is why it lands at the bottom of a stack with a database. See Fly.io alternatives.

App Platform has flat container prices from $5 a month and regions in Singapore and Bangalore. It is the pick here if your Zeabur project used MySQL or MongoDB, since DigitalOcean runs both as managed databases next to Postgres. See DigitalOcean alternatives.

Ownkube is a cloud for personal software. It runs web apps, workers, cron jobs, managed Postgres and a Valkey cache side by side on Ownkube Compute, built from your GitHub or GitLab repo, a Dockerfile or a public image. Apps meter CPU and memory per minute at about $18 per vCPU and $6 per GiB a month, or sit on a flat box from $5. Postgres runs on a database box from $4 a month plus $0.25 per GB of storage, and caches start at $2.

Billing is a prepaid wallet. Pay as you go starts with a $5 top-up, Personal ($5 a month) and Team ($20 a month) load credit every month, and unused credit never expires. If you ran n8n from Zeabur's template list, it is a one-click install in the Marketplace with its own Postgres.

Apps run in the Mumbai and Germany regions. If your users are in Tokyo or Taipei and latency matters, Fly.io or Render's Singapore region will be closer. Ownkube runs Postgres only, so MySQL and MongoDB projects belong on DigitalOcean or Railway.

If you want your own server, Coolify and Dokploy give you a Zeabur-like dashboard on a VPS you rent. You pay the server bill and nothing else, and you own patching, backups and uptime. See Coolify alternatives.

The steps below assume a web app with Postgres. Start now: after December 1 a shared service has to be resumed by hand before you can back it up.

Zeabur's October incident exposed more than 3.2 million environment variable records across about 320,000 accounts. Treat every API key, database password and token that sat in Zeabur's variables as known. Issue new ones at each provider and put only the new values on the new platform.

Pick another platform if your project runs on MySQL or MongoDB, if your users sit in East Asia and need low latency today, or if you need GPUs. Railway, DigitalOcean and Fly.io cover those cases.
