This 1948 Movie Was Once Voted the Greatest Film of All Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

On November 24, 1948, Vittorio De Sica's Bicycle Thieves was released in Italy—a landmark of Italian neorealism that topped the first Sight & Sound poll in 1952 and won a Special Foreign Language Film Award at the 1950 Oscars.
