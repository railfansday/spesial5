Instagram chief shares Muse tips for social media managers

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

Adam Mosseri told Marina Mogilko, aka Silicon Valley Girl, that the artificial intelligence agent can help with ideation and trend discovery.

As Meta continues to expand access to its Muse artificial intelligence agents, which were made available to Australian users last week, social media managers may be wondering how they can use the tool within their process.

Instagram creator Marina Mogilko, who goes by the handle “Silicon Valley Girl,” recently interviewed Instagram Chief Adam Mosseri to get his insights on how creators and social media managers can use Meta’s AI agent to grow Instagram reach.

Mosseri said that Muse is good for ideation and coming up with resonant content concepts.

As per Mosseri: “If you’re trying to come up with more ideas, telling [Muse] what your goal is, then asking it for 10, 20, 30, 50 ideas, telling Muse which ones you like and which you don’t and iterating, it’s a great way just to get, sometimes, the creative juices going.”

Mosseri added that Muse can be helpful for evaluating content performance, and can assess what’s worked and what hasn’t in posts. He said the AI agent can also help categorize content.

Finally, Mosseri said Muse can be helpful for understanding trends.

“So for your audience, or for your industry, what are the trends that are happening right now on social media, not even just on Instagram,”Mosseri said. “Because that might surface conversations that you might want to jump in, for instance.”

Mogilko also asked Mosseri about Muse’s capacity to analyze followers, and provide assessment on the most influential followers in any sector. Mosseri said that Muse can provide a level of assessment of a profile’s audience, but Instagram is working on making this more valuable and effective.

Also interesting: Last month, Meta ran an ad for Muse which promoted the fact that the agent can alert Instagram users to people who have unfollowed them. Though as reported by Business Insider, it quickly took down the promotion after realizing that it goes against Instagram’s ethos.

Wired also reported that some people have been using Muse to unfollow accounts that don’t follow them back.

The latter two use cases are probably not what Meta wants to promote, but they do provide additional considerations for how the agent could be used to assist in automating some aspects of digital marketing.
