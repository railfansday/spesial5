Russell Westbrook shows there’s no hard feelings in Los Angeles with heartfelt statement

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Russell Westbrook spent four seasons in Los Angeles.

In 2021, the nine-time All-Star joined the Lakers via a trade with the Wizards. After two seasons with the Purple and Gold, Westbrook signed with the Clippers and spent another two years in Tinseltown.

Russell Westbrook hands out Thanksgiving meals in Los Angeles. Instagram/@whynotfoundation

This holiday season, Westbrook has kept a close connection to the Los Angeles community.

Three years ago, Westbrook and the Clippers opened a community basketball court at Jesse Owens Park in South Los Angeles where he grew up. On Thursday, Westbrook returned to his hometown to hand out Thanksgiving meals and groceries.

Not long before Westbrook joined the Clippers, his 18-month tenure with the Lakers featured a fractured relationship with superstar LeBron James, locker room tension and organizational fallout.

But the franchise moved on and so did he.

Russell Westbrook hands out Thanksgiving meals. Instagram/@whynotfoundation

In August, the 2016-17 MVP announced his retirement after 18 seasons in the league. It was a shocking decision given many of his former teammates believed Westbrook could have continued his NBA career.

While Westbrook's time with the Lakers seemingly ended in dramatic fashion, it's evident he still holds the city near to his heart.
