Chase, Jefferson lead Week 10 fantasy football wide receiver rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

MIAMI, Nov. 11 (UPI) -- Ja'Marr Chase and Justin Jefferson lead my Top-50 fantasy football wide receiver rankings for Week 10.

CeeDee Lamb, Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Amon-Ra St. Brown are among my other Top-5 options at the position. Nico Collins, Tee Higgins, Rashee Rice, Parker Washington and D.J. Moore are among the other Top-10 plays in my rankings, which are listed below.

Players from the Chicago Bears, Denver Broncos, Philadelphia Eagles and Tampa Bay Buccaneers, who are on bye in Week 10, were removed from my rankings.

Players from Thursday's New York Giants-Washington Commanders game also were removed from my rankings.

Continue to monitor news at the position before throwing the injured wide receivers into starting lineups. My Week 10 waiver wire/free agent targets can be found here. My Week 10 quarterback and running back rankings also are available.
