What can recent signings tell us about the impending lockout?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

There have only been a handful of signings since the World Series. The Braves have added a reliever, Evan Phillips. There's been perpetual Braves-linked starting pitcher Sonny Gray getting 55 million dollars over 2 years. Dustin May has been grabbed by the Twins at 52.5 million dollars over 3 years. And Jake Bauers got four years from the Red Sox. Ivan asked us two or three weeks ago how many top 50 MLB free agents will be signed before the impending lockout on December 1st. I guess a pretty low number like many of you. It may pick up soon, holiday or not.

Three of the four players I mentioned got more money than was projected by the public sources. Bauers had a lower AAV, but did snag another season. So can these signings tell us anything about the impending lockout? Do we have enough of a sample size? Or does the low number of signings tell us anything? We can only attempt to read the tea leaves and sift through the social media posts from MLB and the players. The signed players getting more money might mean a more favorable result for the players. But then again, each of the signing teams can afford to work around these contracts anyway.
