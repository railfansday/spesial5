FIFA presidency candidate Philippe Diallo believes he has Emmanuel Macron&#8217;s support

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

FFF president Philippe Diallo has said that he believes he can count on the support of Emmanuel Macron as he runs against Gianni Infantino in the FIFA presidential campaign.

UEFA confirmed on Monday that the FFF president would be their chosen candidate to stand against Infantino in March's election. The governing body disavowed the incumbent after the revelation of a project to sell FIFA World Cup shares to private enterprise.

In a press briefing in Paris on Tuesday, Diallo confirmed his candidacy, which came after Arsene Wenger's decision not to run in the upcoming elections.

Diallo said that he would bring a "vision of open governance", whilst he also criticised Infantino, not only for the World Cup shares project, but also for the revoking of Folarin Balogun's red card during this summer's tournament. "All of these decisions, often taken alone, have shocked the football world," he told reporters.

As UEFA's candidate, he will already receive 55 of the 106 votes needed to win the presidential race, which will take place in Rabat, Morocco, on 18th March. He will need support from the other continents. Internally, at least, Diallo has the support of the President of the French Republic, Macron. "I think that I can say that I have the suppport of the President. In order to lead this campaign internationally, you have to already be supported internally," he said.
