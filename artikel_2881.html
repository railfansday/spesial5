3 key stats from Indiana men’s basketball’s 90-76 win over Kentucky

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Indiana men's basketball earned its second statement win of the week on Friday night, beating Kentucky 90-76 at Lucas Oil Stadium in Indianapolis.

The Hoosiers moved to 6-0 with the win and rose even further in KenPom's efficiency metrics. Darian DeVries, in his second year leading the program, has built up a good deal of good will in just one week and will likely have a ranked squad on his hands after this weekend.

It's been said so very many times, here and elsewhere, but DeVries built this team to be big and win on the glass. He emphasized it to the roster itself and in media when asked about it. It's realized that vision pretty early on, multiple times.

Indiana beat Kentucky largely because the Hoosiers out-rebounded the Wildcats 35-27, with a 14-6 advantage on the offensive glass. Indiana turned all of those offensive rebounds into 18 second chance points, which is just over the difference in the game.

Aiden Sherrell led the way with seven rebounds and Samet Yigitoglu, despite being limited to just 17 minutes, was right behind him with five. Sam Alexis tied Yigitoglu's effort from the bench with five of his own while two different Hoosiers, Darren Harris and Trevor Manhertz, each pulled down four.

It's a team effort for these guys and it shows up on the box score and on the floor.

Kentucky was in this game, even taking a halftime lead, thanks to the efforts of Milan Momcilovic. He looked unstoppable in the first half, going 5-6 from 3-point range and scoring 22 points, 12 more than the next high scorer on the Wildcats.

He ended up looking pretty stoppable in the second half. Indiana made some adjustments, getting into Kentucky's ballhandlers a bit more to keep Momcilovic from getting the ball at all in advantageous situations. He finished the second half adding just six points to his total, going 2-6 from the floor and 0-4 from deep.

That was a necessary adjustment and a great one from the Hoosiers, who neutralized the Wildcats' lone scoring threat and took control early in the second, retaking a lead they'd never relinquish.

Part of the appeal of adding Aiden Sherrell, alongside the size advantage he gives Indiana's jumbo frontcourt, is that he can step out and space the floor from beyond the arc.

Sherrell hit 33.8% of his attempts from 3-point range for Alabama in 2026-27 and is performing better for Indiana this season, connecting on 36.4% of his 3s up to this point. That shooting was crucial tonight, when he finished 4-6 from deep and just kept punishing Kentucky's willingness to leave him uncovered.

If Sherrell shoots it like that, this offense really, really opens up. Indiana already has a great movement guy in Darren Harris, but adding Sherrell's gravity just makes the Hoosiers that much more difficult to guard.
