Week 11 NFL Survivor strategy and picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Welcome to Week 11! Thanksgiving is a week away, which should be a wild one for Survivor picks. In the meantime, we're back for a fun week of football.

Vikings-49ers in Mexico City should be the highlight of it, but there's some other interesting matchups on the slate. Jaguars-Giants in the Meadowlands is a fascinating one. Both teams are in first place, but Jacksonville is considered a Super Bowl favorite after a big trade deadline, while the Giants are benefiting from a brutal division.

I'm back with my Week 11 Survivor picks ahead of Colts-Texans on Thursday Night Football. Survivor involves picking one team each week to win. If they win you advance, if they lose you're out in most cases. I'm in one pool with four entries, but it's also a double-elimination pool. I'm down to one entry that has one strike against it, but I'm hanging around for now.

Below I've got a look at the safest choices, best value picks, and then trap picks. I'll post my own picks at the end. I'll update on Sunday once I lock in my three picks for the weekend.

I've included odds for the favorite in each game, courtesy of FanDuel.

Chiefs (-15.5) over CardinalsBills (-16.5) over Dolphins

Maybe the Chiefs and Bills don't cover the spread, but they're going to win these games. The Cardinals and Dolphins aren't just circling the drain at this point; their seasons are pretty much a wrap.

Bills (-16.5) over DolphinsCowboys (-7.5) over Titans

We're getting to the point where it's a little more difficult to find true value. If you haven't used the Bills, now is as good a time as any. Their Week 13 matchup just got more interesting with Drake Maye's ankle injury, but beyond that, you wouldn't use the Bills until Week 17 at the Dolphins or Week 18 hosting the Jets.

The Cowboys are not a particularly good team, but they're facing a really bad Titans squad. Tennessee will show some fight, but the Cowboys are a good play this week or next week when they host the Eagles.

Texans (-12.5) over ColtsBroncos (-10) over Raiders

I'm confident in the Bills over the Dolphins, but these two divisional games are tougher sells. Houston and Denver should win given how Indy and Las Vegas have traded away talent, but I think they'll put up decent fights. Add in Texans-Colts being an island game on Thursday and it's an easy call to stay away.

I haven't used the Bills yet and this is the week for me. I have already used the Chiefs and Cowboys, so that leaves me Buffalo this week.
