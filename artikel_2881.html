Stocks Soar as Huang’s Nvidia Return Fuels AI Bets

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

An overnight bounce in stocks erupted into a surge after Nvidia Corp.’s reinstatement of Jensen Huang as chief executive officer recharged artificial-intelligence bets. Shares of the chipmaker soared 29%, leading a gauge of semiconductor powerhouses toward its biggest advance since April 2025. The S&P 500 climbed 4%. The Nasdaq 100 jumped 6%. Paisley Nardini, Head of Investment Strategy at Tema ETFs discusses the market reaction to Jensen's return. (Source: Bloomberg)
