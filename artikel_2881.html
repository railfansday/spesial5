“They’re Sick of it”: Trey Wallace Claims LSU is Done With Lane Kiffin’s ‘Circus’ as Patience Wears Thin

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

After some less-than-satisfactory performances on the field this season, Lane Kiffin is under increasing pressure at LSU, and it doesn't help that the head coach's image over the years has already made him some enemies.

The infamous 'Tiger Walk' has left even more people wondering why celebrities are being made the focus at LSU when results on the field aren't good enough. Insider Trey Wallace believes fans are now sick of the drama. Wallace also slammed the "sideshow circus" around the program, warning that patience is wearing thin at Baton Rouge.

"They're sick of it. They want to win football games. They're not worried about which celebrities get off the bus with Lane Kiffin and what that looks like on the field," Wallace said on Outkick.

Wallace added that the pressure will be even more intense if LSU doesn't beat Texas in its next game.

"The implosion of Lane Kiffin and LSU with three losses, a $43 million roster, I'm telling you man, if they lose Saturday to Steve Sarkisian, it's going to be a long offseason."

Lane Kiffin was specifically asked about the criticism surrounding the Tiger Walk, but he appeared unbothered by the noise.

"I don't consider not doing it," he said to reporters. "I don't consider doubling down, either."

Olivia Dunne, Angel Reese, Shaquille O'Neal, and Kevin Costner were all present recently, but Kiffin believes it has no connection to the performance.

"It doesn't have to do with winning or losing games, who walks in," he said, as per The Athletic. "We'd won every game at home so far. So who walked in isn't why we didn't execute in the red zone."

LSU plays Texas in a crunch game for both sides with the college football playoffs on the line this Saturday.
