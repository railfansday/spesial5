Sarah Michelle Gellar and Freddie Prinze Jr. Find ‘Crazy’ Family Connection

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Sarah Michelle Gellar and her husband Freddie Prinze Jr. have more in common than they they bargained for

Speaking to E! News on Friday, November 21, Gellar, 49, revealed the couple recently uncovered a surprising family connection through Ancestry that has them rethinking just how small the world really is.

“We always joke that the closest connection I had to my husband’s side of the family was that for a small time I went to the same high school that my husband’s dad went to, and that was, like, sort of our one thing,” the Buffy the Vampire Slayer alum told the outlet.

She continued, “Then through Ancestry, we found out that our grandparents lived less than two miles away from each other in the Bronx at one point, which they could have potentially known each other. That’s kind of crazy.”

Although Gellar is a New York native, Prinze Jr., 49, was born in Los Angeles and raised in New Mexico. His late father, comedian Freddie Prinze, was originally from New York, making the family connection even more surprising.

The couple first met while filming 1997’s I Know What You Did Last Summer, but their romance wasn’t immediate. Instead, their relationship began with a series of road trips that ultimately sparked a friendship.

“She didn’t have a driver’s license. She didn’t know how to drive,” Prinze Jr. exclusively told Us in April 2020, recalling how they first connected.

“We were shooting in Southport, North Carolina, which was an hour’s drive from Wilmington, which was the biggest city that was closest to us. … So, I started driving her, and that’s when her and I became friends,” he explained.

The She’s All That actor said the pair would talk about “life” and their “completely different philosophies on just about everything.”

“By the end of the movie, I just wanted to be friends with her because I thought she was too skinny and I wanted to cook for her. Because that’s what my family does,” Prinze Jr. recalled.

He added, “And so, we started this weekly barbecue thing at her house, and me and my cousins would come over and cook food for her and her friends. And then we’d make sure everybody ate, and that was kind of how our friendship began.”

That friendship eventually turned into a romance, with Gellar and Prinze Jr. tying the knot in 2002. The pair welcomed daughter Charlotte in 2009, followed by son Rocky in 2012.

Over the years, the couple have also reunited onscreen several times, including in Scooby-Doo in 2002 and its sequel, Scooby-Doo 2: Monsters Unleashed, in 2004. Gellar also made a brief cameo in Prinze Jr.’s 1999 rom-com She’s All That, while the pair appeared in separate cameos  in Clerks III in 2022.
