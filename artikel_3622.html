25 Things You Don’t Know About Dorinda Medley

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Dorinda Medley may have famously warned, “Say it, forget it. Write it, regret it” — but some stories are simply too good to keep to yourself.

Between reuniting with her former Real Housewives of New York City castmates in West Palm Beach for The Golden Life, stepping into scripted TV with a cameo in Hulu’s All’s Fair and releasing her new memoir, I Know a Thing or Two About a Thing or Two, on November 17, the reality TV star has plenty of more chapters to unveil.

In her exclusive 25 Things You Don’t Know About Me with Us, Medley dishes on rubbing elbows with royalty, a last-minute iPhone audition and the tiny creatures that wouldn’t dare make themselves at home at Bluestone Manor.

1. I wanted to write my new book because I am truly in my “crone” era and filled with wisdom from living a full life with many twists, turns and pivots.

2. Something about shooting Real Housewives that would surprise people is that it was completely authentic and never, ever scripted or produced. It’s sometimes very overwhelming!

3. My most starstruck moment was meeting Princess Diana at the Harbour Club in London.

4. My most vivid childhood memory is coming home from school after lacrosse practice and finding my mother cooking something hearty and delicious, like lasagna.

5. My favorite audition story is All’s Fair! I was on set for The Golden Life, mic’d and ready to go, and I did a quick iPhone recording and sent it off!

6. My best fan interaction was when I was Sarah Paulson’s surprise guest on Watch What Happens Live.

7. My phobia is musophobia — a terrible fear of mice or rats. Yuck!

8. Widow’s Bay was the last show I binged. It is the best combination of horror, comedy and great acting and felt familiar, as I grew up going to Cape Cod.

9. My go-to karaoke song is “Spice Up Your Life” by the Spice Girls. It’s always fun!

10. God’s Love We Deliver is an amazing charity. They cook nutritious, medically tailored food with a fantastic group of volunteers.

11. Something that I love about my Traitors time is being able to dress in these amazingly creative outfits. It’s always about the fashion for me!

12. Hardest I laughed filming The Golden Life was in the mornings with Luann [de Lesseps]. She lived in the apartment above mine in West Palm Beach. We’d have coffee and go for a walk.

13. The trickiest part about writing a memoir is being vulnerable.

14. My favorite comfort meal is lasagna. I always make a bunch at a time and have some frozen at Bluestone Manor.

15. My first car was a yellow Volkswagen Beetle with a red fire stripe my dad put down the side. I still love that car!

17. My favorite movie is Big Fish. I’ve watched it so many times, and I love when the son says about his father, “A man tells a story so many times that he becomes the story.”

18. Peter Frampton was my celeb crush growing up. I had a huge poster of him on my wall.

19. “Our House” by Crosby, Stills, Nash & Young brings back the best memories. It reminds me of being young and driving to their concerts in Saratoga.

20. I was obsessed with Madonna growing up.

21. The proudest moment of my career, to date, is being on All’s Fair.

22. Still on my bucket list is going to Alaska, going on a larger DJ tour, more scripted work, writing more books and living my best Golden Life!

23. To decompress, I go for a walk in Central Park with the dogs at that magical time at dusk.

24. When I need to get out of a funk, I call my mom to this day. And then my BFF, Greg.

25. I hope my legacy is that I was a woman who lived life to its fullest, a survivor who reinvented herself many times and who made laughter a priority. And could make a great dirty martini!
