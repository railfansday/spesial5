Open Fidelity Go, Fidelity’s robo-advisor, if you have less than $25,000 to invest and want your funds picked and rebalanced. Below $25,000 you pay nothing, since there’s no advisory fee and its funds have zero expense ratios, the yearly cost of owning a fund. From $25,000, you pay 0.35% a year on the whole balance, $175 on $50,000.

Like most robo-advisors, Fidelity Go asks about your goal, your timeline, and your comfort with risk, then picks a mix of Fidelity mutual funds and buys and sells to keep that mix on target. It suits beginners opening an IRA, an HSA, or a first taxable account.

Fidelity Go costs nothing on $20,000 and $175 a year on $50,000, which stays a fair price in an IRA or HSA, where selling Flex funds to leave costs no tax. A taxable account you expect to grow past $25,000 is better opened at Betterment or Wealthfront, whose exchange-traded funds can move to another broker without being sold.
