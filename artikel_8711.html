Get the Chase Sapphire Preferred if you want a credit line of at least $5,000 for a $95 annual fee, and add an American Express card with no preset spending limit once you need more room. Your income, debts, and credit still decide how much room you get on either one.

Under the federal ability-to-pay rule, a card issuer has to weigh your income or assets against your current debts before it opens an account or raises your credit line. It can also look at your credit reports and scores. So a premium card can raise the smallest line an issuer will give you, but it never promises a specific one.

Visa’s rules require a Visa Signature credit card, one of the network’s premium tiers, to let you carry a balance of at least $5,000. Some issuers, PenFed among them, put you on a lower tier of the same card when they approve less than $5,000. Every card here either has a minimum line, set by its issuer or by Visa, or has no preset spending limit, which means no fixed line at all. To get more room on a card you already hold, ask the issuer for a credit limit increase. If rewards matter more to you than the size of the line, choose from the best credit cards for excellent credit.
