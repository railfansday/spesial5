Katy Perry Plans Tell-All About 'Dark Marriage' to Russell Brand

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Katy Perry is reportedly planning a tell-all autobiography and documentary series about her 14-month marriage to Russell Brand, following his rape and sexual assault conviction. Sources say the pop star deliberately waited until his trial concluded to share previously undisclosed details about what she calls a 'very dark period' of her life, with Netflix and Hulu reportedly in discussions.
