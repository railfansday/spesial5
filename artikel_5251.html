Google's Genie 4 Is Here, Letting Ultra Subscribers Build AI Worlds in Real Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The company announced Wednesday that its latest world model is rolling out through Project Genie, a browser-based experiment for generating interactive environments. Genie 4 was unveiled in October, but access until now had been limited to trusted testers.

Instead of rendering a finished video from a prompt, Genie generates the scene as someone explores it. With the fourth model, users can type instructions midway through a session to change the weather, introduce a character, or steer what's happening without starting over.

Genie 4 also creates sound alongside the visuals, from wind and footsteps to dialogue that reacts to events in the scene. Google says the model improves the way people move and objects respond to physical forces, though those claims come from its own demonstrations rather than independent testing.

Genie has come a long way from the side-scrolling experiments Google showed in 2024. The first version learned from more than 200,000 hours of gameplay footage and could turn a single image into a simple playable 2D environment, as Decrypt reported at the time.

Those early sessions lasted just 60 seconds, and character controls or physical behavior could be unreliable. Google is now keeping Genie 3 available alongside the new model, with sessions extended to three minutes, support for 360-degree panorama images, and the ability to connect multiple generated worlds into longer journeys.

That three-minute limit applies to the updated Genie 3 experience; Google did not specify a comparable session length for Genie 4 in Wednesday's announcement. Users can choose between the models depending on whether they want Genie 4's stronger visuals and audio or Genie 3's longer explorations.

The technology is not the same as a traditional game engine that builds a stable level with fully defined rules. Genie predicts what a world should look and sound like as it changes.

Google acknowledges that objects can drift or change between views, complex physics remain difficult, and generated speech or text may be inaccurate. It says Project Genie's audio and video carry SynthID watermarks and that inputs and outputs undergo safety screening.
