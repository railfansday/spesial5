Tottenham lineup vs. Leeds: Predicted XI for Premier League clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

By Oliver Thomas | 05 Nov 2026 16:05, Last updated: 06 Nov 2026 16:04

Tottenham Hotspur will be without five players for Saturday’s Premier League clash with Leeds United at Elland Road.

Xavi Simons is believed to be making positive progress in his recovery from an ACL injury and could return soon than anticipated. However, an appearance against Leeds is not on the cards.

Savio (muscle), Lucas Bergvall, Wilson Odobert (both knee) and Mykhaylo Mudryk (ankle) also remain out injuried, but Tosin Adarabioyo is available having recovered from a calf issue, while Rodrigo Bentancur is back from a three-game ban.

Head coach Roberto De Zerbi is not expected to make wholesale changes to a side that won their third Premier League game in a row last weekend, beating Crystal Palace 2-0 on home soil.

However, Pedro Porro is a strong contender to return at right-back at the expense of Arche Gray, potentially joining Jan Paul van Hecke, Micky van de Ven and Andrew Robertson in a four-man defence.

While Sandro Tonali and Mateus Fernandes are set to continue their central midfield partnership, playmaker James Maddison could be recalled to play an advanced role after watching on as an unused substitute against Palace.

The potential return of Maddison could see Omar Marmoush drop down to the substitutes’ bench, with Mohammed Kudus, Mathys Tel and Dominic Solanke all linking up in a three-man frontline.

Kinsky; Porro, Van Hecke, Van de Ven, Robertson; Fernandes, Tonali; Kudus, Maddison, Tel, Solanke
