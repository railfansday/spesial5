Mitro's 43: Can you score a record-breaking amount of goals? (Play)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Few strikers have ever owned the Championship quite like Aleksandar Mitrovic did in 2021-22.

The Serbian scored 43 league goals as Fulham stormed to the title, setting a record for a Championship season and leaving defences across the division wondering how to stop him. Headers, tap-ins, penalties and thunderbolts from the edge of the box: if a chance came his way, it usually ended up in the net.

Now it’s your turn to step into his boots. Mitro’s 43 puts you in charge of the Fulham hitman, and your job is simple: score.

Swipe up from the ball to shoot. A longer swipe sends it higher, a faster swipe hits it harder, and curving your swipe bends it into the corner. On desktop, use the arrow keys to aim and the space bar to shoot.

There are 15 levels, from pre-season penalties to a final-day record decider, with headers from crosses, tight angles, free kicks over the wall, wind, rain and defenders closing you down along the way. You only get three lives for the whole run, but clear levels five and ten to win one back.

Every goal counts towards your season tally. Can you match Mitro’s 43? Have a go below, then share your score and challenge your mates to beat it.
