Gary Lineker revealed how Sheffield Wednesday icon shaped Spurs decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Gary Lineker has a Sheffield Wednesday legend to thank for shaping a significant part of his playing career and identity.

The now 65-year-old first came through with Leicester City in the late 1970s before playing for Everton and Barcelona.

The striker came close to breaking the record for goals scored for the England men’s national team, finding the back of the net on 48 occasions from 80 caps.

And in 1989, he returned to English football with a move to Tottenham Hotspur, and he credited a surprising figure for the decision to sign for the London outfit.

Speaking on the Match of the Day Top 10 podcast in 2021, the former BBC presenter revealed that getting the opportunity to play with Chris Waddle was a major factor in his decision to sign for Spurs.

However, he claimed that it was only after he’d agreed to the deal that his agent informed him that the former Sheffield Wednesday star had already signed for Marseille in a big-money move.

“He didn't look like a footballer, but I loved playing with him,” said Lineker, via the BBC.

“He was one of the main reasons I signed for Spurs when I left Barcelona.

“I did the deal and I was on holiday in the Caribbean and I got a phone call from my agent saying that he'd been sold to Marseille for £15 million.

“It was like someone just snatching 15 goals a season out of my pocket.

“But he is loved in Marseille, he's a proper cult hero there, he's one of their most popular players in their history.

“And he was the only person who could share a room with Gazza!”

Lineker still went through with the move to White Hart Lane, where he spent three years before departing English football again for a move to Japan with Nagoya Grampus.

During his time with Spurs, the forward scored 67 times from 105 appearances, winning the FA Cup in the process.

Lineker cited Waddle as a major inspiration for his decision to sign for Spurs, but perhaps it would’ve gone differently had he known about his move to Marseille.

The 65-year-old revealed in 2015 that he also had the option to join Manchester United in 1989, who were then managed by Alex Ferguson.

The Scot wanted to partner Lineker with Mark Hughes, with the club targeting a first league title since 1967.

Of course, the Red Devils went on to become a juggernaut in the 1990s, and the former England star must wonder what could’ve been had it not been for Waddle.

It was only after his time at Marseille that Waddle cemented himself as an icon at Sheffield Wednesday.

After three years in France, the playmaker made the switch to Hillsborough ahead of the first ever Premier League season in 1992.

The now 65-year-old spent four seasons with the Owls, where he made 109 league appearances, contributing 10 goals and 22 assists, with the club finishing seventh, seventh, 13th and 15th in the table.

Even by the standards of someone in the latter stages of his career, Waddle’s performances for the Yorkshire outfit remained quite impressive, and he had a strong final year at Hillsborough, scoring two and assisting six in the Premier League at the age of 34.

While Lineker was unable to enjoy a spell at Spurs with Waddle as his teammate, the pair did, of course, feature together at international level.

In particular, the 1990 World Cup in Italy was a highlight of their time together, with the former Sheffield Wednesday player enjoying a strong tournament that ultimately ended up in disappointment in the penalty shootout in the semi-finals.

That tournament especially highlighted to Spurs fans what could’ve been with Lineker and Waddle working together, as it was England’s best ever showing in the competition on foreign soil until this year’s edition in North America.
