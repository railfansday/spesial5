Hearts pay tribute as former captain Don Murray dies aged 80

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Former Heart of Midlothian captain Don Murray has died at the age of 80.

The central defender spent almost two years at Tynecastle during the 1970s and captained the Jambos during the inaugural Scottish Premier Division season.

It is with great sadness to announce the passing of former captain, Don Murray, at the age of 80. An iron-hard central defender, Don spent less than two years at Tynecastle, but he made a fine contribution, assisting Hearts to qualify for the initial Scottish Premier Division campaign, as well as reaching the Scottish Cup Final in May 1976. Hearts announced that Murray died on Monday at Llandough, near Cardiff, and paid tribute to a player who made 71 appearances for the club.

The club said: “It is with great sadness that the club learned on Monday of the death of former captain, Don Murray, at Llandough, near Cardiff. He was 80 years of age.”

Born into a farming family at Duffus, near Elgin, in January 1946, Murray began playing with Hopeman Junior School before progressing to junior football with Burghead Thistle.

He signed for Cardiff City in January 1963 and went on to become an influential figure at the Welsh club.

Murray made 527 appearances for Cardiff and helped them reach the League Cup semi-finals during the 1965-66 season and the European Cup Winners’ Cup semi-finals two years later.

His performances also earned international recognition with Scotland at under-23 level.

After a brief loan spell at Swansea City, Murray joined Hearts for £15,000 in December 1974.

Don Murray (Image: Colorsport / Shutterstock)

His arrival helped bring stability to a difficult campaign as Hearts secured their place in the newly formed Premier Division for the following season.

Murray was subsequently appointed captain and led the Jambos during that historic first Premier Division campaign, when they finished fifth.

Hearts also reached the Scottish Cup final in 1976, although Murray was denied the opportunity to lead the team out against Rangers at Hampden because of a calf injury.

He remained captain for the club’s subsequent tour of Norway and Australasia, but continued to struggle with the injury at the beginning of the following season.

Murray left Hearts for Newport County in October 1976 after making 71 appearances for the Edinburgh club and later played for Barry Town.

Following his retirement from football, Murray worked as a social worker.

Hearts added: “Don always led by example.”

The club extended its condolences to Murray’s family and friends following his death.
