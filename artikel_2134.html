This is proving some week for Sean Neave.

The exciting teenage Newcastle United striker getting his first ever goals in senior football in midweek.

Wolves trailing 1-0 at Bristol City but loan star Sean Neave coming off the bench to score two late goals that totally turned the match around.

That win put Wolves second top of the Championship.

They play away at Wrexham on Saturday with a 12.30pm kick-off, knowing that a Wolves win will take them top of the table.

Sean Neave will be desperate to get more minutes as he’ll be in such a confident mood following his first ever goals in first team senior football.

Another huge boost on Friday as well for the Geordie striker from Winlaton, as the lifelong Newcastle United fan has been called up for Eilte League games with the England Under 20s.

The young lions face Czechia and Portugal in November and Sean Neave has been named in the squad by head coach Darren Moore.

The Young Lions play Czechia in Mansfield on Thursday 12 November (7.30pm GMT), they then head to Portugal for the second match of the window on Monday 16 November (4pm GMT) at the Estadio Municipal de Mafra.

Goalkeepers: Finlay Herrick (West Ham United), Nicolas Michalski (Crawley Town, loan from Blackburn Rovers), Oliver Whatmuff (Stockport County, loan from Manchester City)

Defenders: Harry Amass (Manchester United), Airidas Golambeckis (West Ham United), Ayden Heaven (Manchester United), Christian McFarlane (Leicester City, loan from Manchester City), Brooklyn Nfonkeu-Feuba (Manchester City), Kian Noble (Manchester City), Souleymane Sidibe (PSV Eindhoven), Olutayo Subuloye (Chelsea)

Midfielders: Jesse Derry (Sporting Lisbon, loan from Chelsea), Jack Fletcher (Manchester United), George Hemmings (Aston Villa), Chris Rigg (Sunderland), Jay Robinson (Monza, loan from Southampton), Jim Thwaites (Manchester United)

Forwards: Benjamin Broggio (Stevenage, loan from Aston Villa), Shea Lacey (Manchester United), Brian Madjo (Aston Villa), Shumaira Mheuka (Celtic, loan from Chelsea), Jimmy Jay Morgan (West Bromwich Albion), Sean Neave (Wolverhampton Wanderers, loan from Newcastle United)
