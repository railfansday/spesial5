Clorox Purell ProCare™ Recognized for Healthcare Disinfection Innovation and Industry Leadership at ISSA Show 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Clorox Healthcareâ¢ Quat Alcohol Disinfecting Wipes win 2026 ISSA Innovation Award for Cleaning Agents, while three Clorox leaders receive industry honors

AKRON, Ohio, Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Clorox Purell ProCare capped a milestone week at ISSA Show North America 2026 with recognition for both breakthrough healthcare product innovation and the leaders helping shape the future of the cleaning and facility solutions industry.

Recognizing newest disinfecting wipe innovation

The week was highlighted by Clorox Healthcare Quat Alcohol Disinfecting Wipes winning the 2026 ISSA Innovation Award in the Cleaning Agents category. The ready-to-use, one-step cleaner and disinfectant kills healthcare-associated infection-causing pathogens, including C. auris, norovirus and multidrug-resistant organisms, in 1 minute when used as directed on hard, nonporous surfaces. The patent-pending triple-active formula contains 40% less alcohol and has a contact time twice as fast as the leading healthcare quat alcohol wipes,1 helping keep surfaces wet for the full contact time and supporting compliant use.

The ISSA Innovation Program showcased new solutions from across the cleaning industry, with entries evaluated by industry judges on impact, practicality, sustainability, competitive edge and originality.

"ISSA was an exciting opportunity to showcase what is possible when we bring together the science, customer knowledge and innovation capabilities of our combined business," said John Lerner, general manager of Clorox Purell ProCare. "Winning the Innovation Award is tremendous recognition for the teams behind Clorox Healthcare Quat Alcohol Disinfecting Wipes and reinforces our commitment to developing superior innovations that solve real challenges for our customers."

Celebrating leadership across the industry

Clorox Purell ProCare's presence at ISSA also included recognition of teammates whose leadership and contributions are making an impact across the cleaning and facility solutions industry.

Jen Sherman, regional sales director, received the ISSA Hygieia Rising Star of the Year Award in recognition of her leadership, professional accomplishments and commitment to advancing women in the cleaning and facility solutions industry. An active member of the Hygieia Council, Jen continues to make a meaningful impact by creating opportunities for others to connect, learn and grow.

Kevin Affeldt, vice president, head of sales, received the ISSA Hygieia Ally of the Year Award, recognizing his advocacy and support for advancing the Hygieia Network's mission of empowering current and future industry leaders.

Matt Meridieth, sales vice president, public sector and national accounts, was named to the ISSA Emerging Leaders Class of 2026, a select group of 30 professionals chosen from nearly 100 nominations. The program recognizes rising professionals under 40 for their leadership, innovation, professional accomplishments and commitment to advancing the cleaning and facility solutions industry.

"These recognitions speak to something that goes well beyond products," Lerner added. "Everything we do is in service of our purpose: We save lives and make life better by championing a cleaner and healthier world. We're incredibly proud of Jen, Kevin, Matt and the many teammates whose expertise, leadership and collaboration were on display throughout ISSA. Together, they're helping us build the future of Clorox Purell ProCare and contribute to the future of our industry."

The awards and strong customer and distributor engagement throughout ISSA underscored the opportunity ahead for Clorox Purell ProCare as it brings together trusted brands, science-led innovation and more than 180 years of combined experience across hand and surface hygiene.
