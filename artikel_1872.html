When is FA Cup draw? Date, start time, second round ball numbers and TV channel

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The FA Cup continues apace this weekend with the draw for the second round.

League One and League Two clubs have joined the action, with one of the pick of the ties seeing Robbie Savage facing off against his son, Charlie, as Peterborough were thumped by Reading.

The FA Cup first round ties got underway on Friday night with 2021 winners Leicester seeing off Oxford City. Elsewhere, 2013 winners Wigan took care of business against Boreham Wood.

The dream is over for AFC Rushden & Diamonds after they were beaten Burton Albion, leaving eighth-tier Stafford Rangers as the lowest-ranked teams remaining in the competition as they face Grimsby Town, hoping to cause a major giant-killing.

The weekend’s action will see 80 teams whittled down to 40 for the second round, with Championship and Premier League teams entering the fray in the third round.

Here’s everything you need to know about the FA Cup draw for the second round...

The draw for the FA Cup second round will take place on Monday, November 9, 2026, before the clash between non-league clubs Plymouth Parkway and Berkhamsted.

Television coverage of the game starts at 6:30pm GMT, with the draw itself expected to begin shortly afterwards.

TV channel: In the UK, the draw will be televised live on TNT Sports 1.

Live blog: You can follow all draw via Standard Sport’s live blog.

The ball numbers are to be confirmed closer to the second round draw.

Kidderminster Harriers 3-0 Doncaster Rovers

Altrincham 2-2 Notts County (Notts County win 3-2 on penalties)

Stafford Rangers 3-3 Grimsby Town (Grimsby win 3-2 on penalties)

Cray Wanderers 2-3 Stockport County (AET)

The second round ties will be played across the weekend of December 5-6, 2026.
