First November 2026 PS Plus Extra Game Available to Download Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

One of the very few benefits to Sony's new experiment of delaying the release of PS Plus Extra games in select regions is that you do get one new game the day after the reveal.

Rather than having to wait until next week, Hell Let Loose is already available for UK and USA members. Those in Japan will get access tomorrow, 12th November, and then the rest of the world can play starting Tuesday, 17th November.

The game should be live now in the UK, and then availability rolls out across the USA later in the day.

A 100-player online PvP game, Hell Let Loose is pitched as a "hardcore" WW2 shooter with loads of classes, vehicles, and a "dynamically shifting front line". The title is still being supported and iterated on to this day, with the latest content roadmap teasing new maps, Canadian forces, and another game mode.

It's generally seen as a strong multiplayer offering, with favourable critic reviews and "very positive" user scores on Steam, while PS5 users rate it 3.99/5 on the PS Store. It sounds like you're in for a good time if you invest in Hell Let Loose.
