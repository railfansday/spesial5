Sea of Thieves Nearly Halves Install Size in Latest Update on PS5

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Developer Rare is preparing its next major update for its nautical multiplayer adventure, Sea of Thieves, and it makes a pretty big change to the game's storage footprint.

The update, which arrives this week on Thursday 19th November, makes a significant reduction to the title's install size across all platforms.

On PS5, it's moving from about 102.7 GB to a much more palatable 57.4 GB — roughly a 45% decrease.

When the update goes live, you'll need to redownload and install the game, but at its smaller size, so it hopefully won't take you too long.

That's not the only thing coming in the update, of course; the video above goes through numerous additions, like new cosmetics, shanties, December events, and various improvements and fixes.

The timing of the update is almost certainly a coincidence, but if Sea of Thieves has been eating up SSD space on your PS5, the reduction should make installing GTA 6 — a 212 GB monster — a bit easier.
