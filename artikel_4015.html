Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival live event set for November 21

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Rockstar Games and Sony Interactive Entertainment will host “Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival,” a special live event at Mana Wnywood in Miami, on November 21, the companies announced.

The event will feature a “star-studded lineup” of artists from the game and official soundtrack, including two stages of “non-stop music” with performances by Cactus Jack presents Travis Scott, Don Toliver, Kodak Black, and Sofaygo; Cardi B; Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso; Justice (DJ set); Metro Boomin; Nick Leon B2B DJ Python; Prospa B2B Skream; Rauw & Friends; Suero; Tainy; The Dare B2B Fcukers (DJ set).

A pre-sale lottery for a chance to purchase tickets will begin on November 11. Tickets are limited to two per pre-sale lottery. The lottery closes on November 10 at 1:00 p.m. PT / 4:00 p.m. ET. Users can sign up for the lottery here.

Grand Theft Auto VI is due out for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series on November 19.
