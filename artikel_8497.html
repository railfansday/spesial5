Perhaps one reason for using a revocable living trust is the lack of flexibility regarding beneficiary designations by some investment companies. We have most of our investments with Vanguard. Would we be able to designate the following using their beneficiary designation form? I'm not sure. Would a trust be a better way to get it done?

When the last of us is gone we want to specify the following: Siblings and their kids on my wife's side, and siblings (but not their kids) on my side will receive an equal percentage. (We have no children.) When married siblings pass away we want their portion to go to their current wives. (We want those wives listed by name, not just "spouse".) When single childless siblings pass away on my wife's side we want what those sibling's would have received spread evenly among the remaining beneficiaries. In contrast, when my siblings pass away, we want charities to receive what they would have received if they were still alive.

We also want a trust for our pets to make it more likely that they will be treated humanely after we are gone or incapacitated.
