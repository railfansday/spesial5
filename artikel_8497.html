Pick the credit card that pays the most where your household already spends. That’s a warehouse club’s card if you buy groceries at Costco, Sam’s Club, or BJ’s, the card for Walmart, Target, or Amazon if one of them is your main store, or the Blue Cash Preferred for supermarkets. Put the rest on a flat 2% card.

The average household spent $6,491 on groceries and $2,503 on gas in 2025, the Bureau of Labor Statistics reports. Earning 5% on those groceries rather than 1% is worth about $260 a year, so the cards here are grouped by where a family shops.

A partner or teen added as an authorized user gets a card on your account, and their purchases add to your rewards, but only you owe the balance. A joint credit card makes both of you owe it.
