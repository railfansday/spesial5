Travis Kelce returns after injury scare as Chiefs dominate Falcons with 35-13 win

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Travis Kelce headed to the Chiefs' locker room at the end of Kansas City's first drive after suffering an ankle injury against the Atlanta Falcons. Kelce had caught Patrick Mahomes' first pass of the afternoon for 23 yards, but landed hard on his hip after a diving tackle from Atlanta safety DeMarcco Hellams. Kelce appeared frustrated with the tackle, but no flags were thrown.

On the sideline, cameras caught Kelce grimacing in pain as the Chiefs moved downfield.

The Chiefs later scored on the drive when Mahomes found Emmett Johnson for a 12-yard touchdown pass. Kelce left the field soon after and was listed as questionable to return with an ankle injury.

He eventually did return to the field midway through the second quarter, finishing the game with two catches for 30 receiving yards as the Chiefs marched on to a dominant 35-13 win over the Falcons.

While Kelce's stat line left something to be desired, Mahomes had an elite showing, going 22-of-35 passing for 348 yards, 5 touchdowns and no interceptions. He took just a single sack for a nine-yard loss, finishing the day with a 135.5 NFL passer rating.

Two of Mahomes' touchdowns went to rookie running back Emmett Johnson, making his first career start in place of star Kenneth Walker III, who was ruled out with a groin injury suffered in practice this week. Johnson finished the day with a whopping 171 scrimmage yards, leading the team with seven receptions on the day.
