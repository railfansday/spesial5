Lions, Bears Week 12 injury report: Sam LaPorta, Chuck Clark upgraded on Tuesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Detroit Lions (8-2) and Chicago Bears (8-2) will square off in this year's Thanksgiving Day battle, and for the second straight day, the Lions and Bears have opted for walkthrough practices.

A walkthrough practice means that all participation levels are estimates from the coaching staff based on how the player would have participated in a traditional practice. So, with that in mind, let's look at Tuesday's injury report and get you up to date on what we know.

Note: Any changes from the most recent injury report will be bolded

DT Skyler Gill-Howard (knee) — Update: His season is over

Gill-Howard's knee injury is significant enough to require surgery and will end his rookie season. A staple of the Lions' three-man interior defensive line, the Lions will need more production from their depth players beyond starters Alim McNeill and Tyleik Williams.

"Sky is out for the year. He has a knee injury that will require surgery, so he'll be out. I hate that for him. He's had a heck of a season up until this point," Lions coach Dan Campbell said on Tuesday. Later adding, "He's done a really good job for us. But we've still got Mac (McNeill), we still have Tyleik, we've still got Levi (Onwuzurike), we've got (Tyler) Lacy that can play in there. Could see (Mekhi) Wingo. We've got options there, but you hate to lose a player like him. He's had a heck of a rookie season."

Wonnum played a full allotment of snaps last Sunday, but his injury appeared to pop up afterward and is significant enough to make him a game-time decision on Thursday, per Campbell. New injuries can be brutal on a short week, but there's still room for optimism.

Rakestraw's injury kept him out of last weekend's game, so starting the week with continued restrictions isn't a great sign for his availability. Campbell didn't say it directly, but he'll likely be a game-time decision, too.

S Chuck Clark (hamstring) — Upgraded from No Practice

Clark, like Wonnum, didn't have a notable injury during Sunday's game but was estimated to have missed practice on Monday. While it's an encouraging sign to see him upgraded for Tuesday's practice, he is also expected to be a game-time decision.

TE Sam LaPorta (ankle) — Upgraded from Limited

LaPorta was estimated to have been limited on Monday, but would have gotten in a full practice on Tuesday if it had been a traditional practice day. That's a great sign for his availability on Thursday.

Reed continues to improve, but with the Lions taking a slow approach to his recovery, despite the estimated full practices, he's still likely to be a game-time decision, per Campbell:

"Yeah, he's improving. I mean, I thought he ran around pretty good yesterday. But I can't give a definitive answer on that just yet. Probably come down, could be game time decision."

Dorsey was injured in the game but returned and played snaps on special teams. With an estimated full practice under his belt, he appears to have a good chance to play on Thursday.

RB Roschon Johnson (ankle) — injured last game

CB Kyler Gordon (shoulder) — missed last game

LT Theo Benedet (quad) — Upgraded from did not practice on Monday
