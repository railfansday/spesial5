There are a couple of things worth noting about this game between the Jets and the Chargers. First of all, it is on FOX. While I'm sure many of you know this already, AFC games are no longer automatically shown on CBS, and NFC games are no longer automatically shown on FOX. With the Giants playing on CBS in the late timeslot, this game has to be shown on FOX.

Also note that kickoff time is scheduled for 4:05 pm Eastern Time. Many late games kick off at 4:25, but this one is scheduled 20 minutes earlier at 4:05.

Adam Amin and Drew Brees will be on the call for FOX.

Given the quality of the teams and FOX only showing a single game on this Sunday, you might imagine this game will not have a wide distribution. FOX affiliates based in cities in New York State and Southern California will show the game. So will the FOX affiliates in Hartford, Pittsburgh, and Kansas City. To see whether Jets vs. Chargers will be shown in your area along with the other Week 11 action, go to 506sports.com.

If this game is shown by your local FOX affiliate, you can stream it on FoxOne and NFL+. If the game is not shown by your local FOX affiliate, you can stream it on NFL Sunday Ticket through YouTube and YouTube TV. Separate subscriptions are required for all of these options.

The entire nation will see Vikings vs. 49ers from Mexico City tonight on NBC and Bengals at Commanders tomorrow night on ESPN.
