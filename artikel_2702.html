Eagles sign Zach Ertz to 53-man roster in flurry of moves

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Eagles sign Zach Ertz to 53-man roster in flurry of moves  originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

It looks like Zach Ertz is going to have a chance to break the franchise record for receptions after all.

The Eagles on Monday afternoon made a flurry of roster moves headlined by signing Ertz from the practice squad to the 53-man roster. There are eight games left in the 2026 season and Ertz is just seven catches behind Harold Carmichael for the franchise receptions record.

Here's an overview of the roster moves from Monday afternoon:

• TE Zach Ertz and OT John Ojukwu signed to 53-man roster

• RB Will Shipley's 21-day practice window opened

• RB Jaydon Blue, LB Chance Campbell, TE Hayden Rucci signed to practice squad

• DB Shaun Wade released from practice squad

The Eagles brought back Ertz to their practice squad on Sept. 22 but used up his three elevations against the Bears, Rams and Jaguars. He has been stuck on the practice squad for the last four games. But coming off the bye week, Ertz is finally on the roster and will have a chance to break that record.

1. Harold Carmichael: 5892. Zack Ertz: 5823. Pete Retzlaff: 4524. Dallas Goedert: 4405. Brian Westbrook: 4266. DeVonta Smith: 4117. Brent Celek: 3988. DeSean Jackson: 3799. Pete Pihos: 37310. Keith Byars: 371

Earlier this season, Ertz played in those three games and had 3 catches for 22 yards. Ertz, 36, is obviously past his prime but did have 50 catches for 504 yards with the Commanders last season before tearing his ACL.

By signing Ojukwu, the Eagles are signaling that he's their new swing tackle. The Eagles released veteran Fred Johnson late last week so Ojukwu is now the next guy up behind Jordan Mailata and Markel Bell. Ojukwu went undrafted out of Boise State in 2023. He played in 16 games with 9 starts with the Titans from 2023-25. Ojukwu joined the Eagles on a futures deal in January and has been on the practice squad all year.

Shipley has been on IR since Oct. 16 with a foot injury. With his practice window now opened, the Eagles will have 21 days to activate Shipley or shut him down for the season. Right now, the only running back on the 53-man roster is Saquon Barkley.

As far as the practice squad transactions, both Blue and Campbell have been with the Eagles earlier this season, but Rucci is a new face. The 25-year-old went undrafted out of Wisconsin in 2024 and has been with a few teams. Earlier this season, he played in six games with two starts with the Chargers.
