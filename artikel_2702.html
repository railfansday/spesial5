The Lions, despite their resurgence, are 1-8 in their last nine Thanksgiving games

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Lions commenced their ongoing resurgence in 2022. That hasn’t translated to sustained success on Thanksgiving.

They’re 1-8 over the last nine Thanksgiving Day games.

The one win in that stretch came two years ago, against the Bears. That day, Chicago pocketed a timeout as the final 30 seconds or so ticked away, leaving the Bears without enough time to attempt a field goal that would have forced overtime in a 23-20 game.

The next day, the Bears fired coach Matt Eberflus. It paved the way for the eventual hiring of Lions offensive coordinator Ben Johnson, who returns to Ford Field today for the second time.

Last year’s game didn’t go very well. Detroit scored 52 points. The Bears lost the Soldier Field rematch, too.

This year, history takes a back seat to the immediate future. Both the Bears and the Lions are 8-2. The Lions have won all six of their home games. The winner takes control, for now, of the NFC North — and stays within striking distance of the 9-1 49ers for the No. 1 seed.

It’s not hyperbole to call it the most significant early-window Thanksgiving game ever. In many years, the Lions were the punching bag on turkey day. They’re now the ones doing the punching.

Which makes the 1-8 trend even more surprising.

For the Bears, the challenge is to prove that they’re Super Bowl contenders. They entered the discussion with their Week 8 Monday night win in Seattle. Today, the Bears have a chance to control the narrative.

However it goes, the numbers should be eye-popping. For last year’s Packers-Lions game, 47.7 million tuned in. It was a new all-time regular-season record audience, until Chiefs-Cowboys later that day shattered it with 57.23 million viewers.

All three games could be compelling today. Eagles-Cowboys got a boost from both teams winning on Sunday. And Chiefs-Bills continues to be one of the top rivalries in the league — even if cold and wind and rain could take more than a little steam out of the offenses.
