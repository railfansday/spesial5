Celtics announce new starting lineup against Kings in wake of injuries

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

SACRAMENTO — The Celtics will start Payton Pritchard, Hugo Gonzalez, Sam Hauser, Paul George, and Neemias Queta when they face the Sacramento Kings.

That comes as the team is without Jayson Tatum, Mitchell Robinson, and Derrick White; Tatum is out to rest on the first night of a back-to-back, while Robinson is out with illness. White is out with a shoulder contusion that Mazzulla said he doesn't expect will be a long-term concern.

Mazzulla said he enjoys nights like Saturday, when the team will be shorthanded, and other guys will see more opportunities.

"Who's in, who's out — the standard and expectation stays the same," Mazzulla said. "You gotta be ready to play. Games like this, I enjoy because it validates the locker room and the roster, and guys work hard, put themselves in position to impact winning, and that's what we expect tonight."

Brad Stevens weighed in on the logjam at the wing position at shootaround on Saturday.

"I said at the start of the year, the biggest challenge for the coaching staff is the wing differentiation," Stevens said. "That's been the hardest part, and I think they've managed it beautifully, because nobody has really differentiated themselves, and they're all good players. And so that's just kind of the hard part about having depth, which in this league you need now. It's really important, but it also comes with the challenges of — not everybody's going to get exactly what they want on every given night. Is it easier to have seven or eight guys that you know are going to play every night? Yeah, until two of them get hurt, then you're in trouble, right? And so that's part of the challenge in this day and age with how the league is going and how hard it is to play the full 82, and certainly game to game with what's required on both ends."

Mazzulla said he didn't give players a heads-up on who will start or who will get more opportunities in the wake of the absences.

"We just need everybody to come in with the professionalism, the preparation that at any point in time they could [get] minutes to impact winning," he said. "So, guys do a great job of staying ready. It's on everybody to be prepared. We don't know what we're going to have to get to, so guys got to be ready. They do a good job of that, and we try to stay consistent in that."
