Lakers guard Austin Reaves available for NBA Cup game vs. Spurs

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Austin Reaves has been listed as available for the Los Angeles Lakers on Friday, Nov. 27.

The Lakers will face their toughest opponent of the season against the San Antonio Spurs at the Frost Bank Center in NBA Cup play.

Reaves missed multiple games due to a left heel bruise. He managed to be a full participant in practice on Nov. 25, the first time he was able to do so in two weeks.

Reaves landed awkwardly on his left heel after missing a transition dunk attempt in the Lakers' 123-112 victory over the Milwaukee Bucks on Nov. 11.

"I didn't think I broke my foot," Reaves told reporters at practice on Nov. 25. "But it sure felt like I broke my foot."

Reaves' return could help the Lakers finish the month of November in a similar fashion to the way it started. The shooting guard was playing for the Lakers during the start of a nine-game winning streak that was snapped by the Utah Jazz on Nov. 18.

He's missed the last five games for Los Angeles, but the Lakers went 4-1 without him in the lineup during that stretch.

Reaves provides some relief despite the success, adding another proven scorer to the lineup. It could also allow for the team to be less reliant on Luka Doncic, who has averaged 36.2 minutes per game, the second-highest average in his career.
