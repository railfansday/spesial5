Atlanta United Falls To New York in Season Final Match

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Atlanta United FC saw its 2026 campaign come to a hard stop on Saturday. The team fell 3-0 to Red Bull New York at Sports Illustrated Stadium in New Jersey. This loss marked the final match of the regular season for the 5-Stripes. The scoreline reflected a night where the home team controlled the pace from the start.

New York took charge early and never looked back. The visitors scored three goals while Atlanta failed to find the net. Managing Editor Adam Jasper of Atlanta United FC reported that goalkeeper Jayden Hibbert made important saves but could not stop the flood of chances. The team conceded too many opportunities to stay in the game.

The match began with a chaotic first 20 minutes. Both teams bypassed the midfield to attack quickly. Miguel Almirón hit the post in the 7th minute during a two-on-one break. The Atlanta captain shot at the near post, but the ball rattled off and away. Despite the early scare, the hosts had the better of the chances in those opening moments.

Red Bull New York struck first in the 31st minute. Adri Mehmeti opened the scoring after Paulo Díaz fell trying to make a stop. Mehmeti slipped behind the defense and fired a shot that Hibbert saved initially. The young forward then avoided another dive from the keeper to score on the rebound. Red Bull New York stated that Mehmeti scored his second goal during the 2026 MLS season.

The home side nearly doubled their lead before halftime. In the 35th minute, Devin Padelford struck the crossbar from close range. By the break, the hosts had generated 2.3 expected goals compared to Atlanta's 0.6. That gap showed just how much control New York held over the game.

The second half did not bring the turnaround Atlanta needed. The Red Bulls took a 2-0 lead in the 54th minute. Cade Cowell snuck behind the backline and dribbled past Hibbert. Tomás Jacob deflected his shot, which appeared to be wide, into the net. Cowell's fourth goal of the season stands as his highest total in an MLS campaign since 2021.

Atlanta tried to respond but could not finish. Almirón had a chance in the 61st minute when the keeper spilled the ball, but the shot was stuffed. The 5-Stripes kept the initiative, yet New York countered well. Substitute Rafael Mosquera missed an open net in the 74th minute, dribbling too far left and hitting the post.

Red Bull New York sealed the win with a free kick in the 82nd minute. Julian Hall was fouled just outside the box, and Jahkeele Marshall-Rutty sent a direct kick into the top right corner. This goal marked Marshall-Rutty's first career goal with New York. The team finished with a clean sheet thanks to Ethan Horvath and the defense.

The final stats tell the story of a season that ended on a low note. Atlanta United finished with nine wins, 18 losses, and six draws for 33 points. They sat 13th in the Eastern Conference. Red Bull New York ended with 11 wins, 16 losses, and six draws, totaling 39 points. The gap between the teams was clear on the field.

New York's young stars made a big impact. In MLS history, this squad equaled the Columbus Crew's total of single-season goal contributions from players aged 21 and under. Julian Hall tied the record for the highest number of goals scored by a teenager in one MLS regular season. Ronald Donkor tallied his 12th assist, leading the team in that category.

For Atlanta, the loss highlighted issues with transition defense. The team allowed too many direct attacks through the middle of the pitch. The midfield was bypassed quickly, leaving the back line exposed. Before this final game, the 5-Stripes secured only one win across their last five MLS matches.

Red Bull New York ends the 2026 season with a friendly versus Club América on Saturday, November 14. Kickoff is set for 7:30 p.m. ET at Sports Illustrated Stadium.
