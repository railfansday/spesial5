Josh Jacobs not on practice field Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Green Bay Packers RB Josh Jacobs (finger) did not participate in practice Wednesday, Nov. 11.

Why It Matters: Jacobs is expected to miss some time because of the injury, so MarShawn Lloyd and Kaleb Johnson will likely see more playing time in upcoming games. Both can be considered flex options in fantasy leagues, but fantasy players should see if one of the backs receives more work in Week 10.
