Adam Schefter shares what he knows about Puka Nacua’s LA Rams future after latest injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Puka Nacua's long-term future with the Los Angeles Rams is up in the air at the moment and Adam Schefter has now provided his insight on the situation.

Nacua was drafted by the Rams in 2023, signing a four-year rookie contract worth $4.08 million.

That means he is currently on track to become a free agent in 2027 and after suffering a season-ending injury in a Week 9 game against the Washington Commanders, there is a chance he may never play for the Los Angeles outfit again.

However, when speaking to ESPN this week, Adam Schefter has revealed that the 'Rams would like him back' while adding that 'it's a mistake' to suggest he will not sign another deal with them.

He said: "Will they actually keep him? We'll see. In the off-season, there's a lot of things that come up. Puka obviously is serving a suspension now. He had off-field issues last off-season. Now he's got a knee injury that was operated on."

Schefter continued: "So he's coming off the knee surgery. He's a free agent. There's been the suspension. The Rams would like him back. He's going to want to get paid. We saw what happened with Jaxon Smith-Njigba, so how is this all going to work out?

"Right. I think it's a mistake to say he's not going to be back in LA, and I think it's a mistake to say he's automatically going to be back. We don't know. There are a lot of questions that are going to sort themselves out this off-season with Puka Nacua."

This is the third injury the wide receiver has suffered in the 2026 season, previously picking up a groin problem in September and a calf strain in October. His current issue means Nacua will be out for around four to five months, which is a big blow for the Rams given he already had 715 receiving yards and five total touchdowns in seven games this year.

He is part of an injury list that also includes Terrance Ferguson, Coleman Shelton and Steve Avila, all of whom are set to be out in Week 12 against the Green Bay Packers.

But despite the growing injury issues, the Rams have won their last four games and are now 6-4, level with the Seattle Seahawks in the NFC West.
