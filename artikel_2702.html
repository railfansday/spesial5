Tennessee volleyball sweeps Georgia

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

No. 11 Tennessee (19-5, 10-4 SEC) defeated Georgia (15-9, 7-7 SEC), 25-22, 25-14,25-13, Friday at Stegman Coliseum in Athens, Georgia.

The victory was the Lady Vols' 15th sweep of the 2026 season. Tennessee has recorded sweeps in its last three matches.

The Lady Vols totaled 49 kills and recorded a .375 hitting percentage against the Bulldogs. Tennessee also had six aces. Defensively, the Lady Vols recorded 40 kills and seven blocks, while holding Georgia to an .074 hitting percentage.

Kennedi Rogers reached a career milestone as she eclipsed the 500-kill mark in her sophomore season. The outside hitter recorded a match-high 14 kills. She also totaled seven digs and one block.

Maggie Dostic recorded nine kills, six digs and one ace, while Caroline Kerr finished with 43 assists, four blocks, four digs, three kills and two aces for the Lady Vols, who committed 14 errors on 94 total attacks.

Freshman middle blocker Anni Tang tied her season high with eight kills, while leading Tennessee with five blocks. Jalyn Stout finished with 16 digs and seven kills, while having a .479 hitting percentage. She also recorded two assists.

Chelsea Sutton totaled eight kills and one block for the Lady Vols, who will end the regular season Nov. 15 at South Carolina. First serve between the Lady Vols and Gamecocks is slated for 1 p.m. EST and the match can be watched on SEC Network+.
