Protesters Throw Projectiles at Biden During Dublin Trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Video showed demonstrators from a pro-Palestinian protest throwing projectiles at former President Joseph R. Biden Jr. after he attended an event at Trinity College Dublin on Tuesday.

Protesters threw projectiles at former President Joseph R. Biden Jr. after he attended an event at Trinity College Dublin on Tuesday, as the police scuffled with demonstrators attending a pro-Palestinian rally, according to video of the incident.

An Garda Síochána, the Irish national police force, said three men had been arrested on Tuesday in relation to a public order incident. Two of the men were later released, and one was charged, the police said in a statement on Wednesday.

Video of the episode showed Mr. Biden being shielded from the projectiles by black umbrellas held by members of his entourage.

Mr. Biden had been attending a ceremony honoring his son, Hunter Biden, by a debating society that praised him for speaking publicly about politics and his struggle with addiction. Hunter Biden and Jill Biden, the former first lady, also attended the event, according to Ireland’s national broadcaster, RTÉ.

“A protest took place in Trinity College Dublin’s Front Square on Tuesday, 10 November during a ceremony in the Exam Hall,” said Catherine O’Mahony, a spokeswoman for the college. “The protest escalated while the Bidens were leaving” the event, she said.

Videos show groups of people gathered in the square, some waving Palestinian flags and chanting “blood on your hands.” As Mr. Biden was leaving the area, footage shows objects and white liquid, which appears to be milk, being thrown at him.
