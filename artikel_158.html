Huang reportedly admitted to more than two affairs to Nvidia’s board

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

In a complete U-turn, Nvidia’s board on Monday reinstated Jensen Huang as the CEO and president of the chipmaker, just a fortnight after he resigned.

During a meeting before his reinstatement, however, Huang admitted to more affairs beyond the two that have been reported in the news, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Early in November, Huang admitted to an extramarital affair in a letter to employees. At the time, he was ostensibly talking about his relationship with Megaspeed executive and alleged Chinese spy, Alice Huang (not related to Jensen). Later, it was reported that he’d also had a relationship with Nvidia’s VP of health, Kimberly Powell.

Afterwards, Reuters reported that he also had an affair with another employee.

It’s not clear which affairs Huang admitted to during this recent conversation with Nvidia’s board of directors.

Huang’s return was spurred by a personal intervention from the U.S. government, Nvidia’s board said in a statement on Monday, and President Donald Trump apparently called the board to bat for Huang’s reinstatement.

The Wall Street Journal reports that over the weekend, U.S. Attorney General Todd Blanche told Trump and Vice President JD Vance that the Justice Department wasn’t investigating Huang and there was no evidence that he was a national security risk, after which both contacted the company’s board.

Huang received a “hero’s welcome” at Nvidia’s HQ, with several employees applauding his return.
