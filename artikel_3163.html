Stock Market Today, Nov. 16: Tech Selloff Deepens as AI Leadership Crisis Rattles Nasdaq

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Investors weighed a Nvidia leadership shakeup, rising Treasury yields, and fresh chip export curbs as tech stocks slid across the board, today, Nov. 16, 2026.

Falling 2.49% to 24,510, the Nasdaq Composite plunged as technology sentiment soured, while the S&P 500 slipped 1.92% to 7,416 and the Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI -1.41%) lost 1.41% to 51,608 as selling pressure expanded across the market.

Gold fell 0.88% to $3,852.10 as of U.S. market close, while the 10-Year Treasury yield rose to 5.15% (+0.05%) as Real Estate outperformed relative to laggards in Communication Services and Technology.

Tyson Foods, Inc. shares surged 8.72% after the company exceeded quarterly earnings expectations and provided a positive outlook for its beef segment, but the broader market was weighed down by Nvidia Corporation, which fell 4.12% amid a leadership crisis. Elsewhere, Intel Corporation dropped 7.02% during a volatile session for semiconductors, and Dell Technologies Inc. slid 6.96% following warnings about industrywide debt.

Today was a challenging day for tech stocks, with the Invesco QQQ Trust (QQQ -2.67%) falling 2.66% to $661.84 per share. This drop occurred as negative sentiment hit the sector on multiple fronts.

Aside from the ongoing struggles with Nvidia's leadership crisis, AMD stock suffered due to new export restrictions on advanced AI chips to Southeast Asian countries. Consequently, the stock fell by 5.58% to $504.28 per share.

Moreover, Federal Reserve Governor Michelle Bowman turned hawkish on corporate debt levels. This is concerning to hyperscalers like Meta Platforms, which depend heavily on debt financing to fund their AI build-outs.

Furthermore, the tech sector faces further tests this week when Nvidia announces its earnings for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 on Tuesday after the bell. This is Nvidia's first earnings call without CEO and co-founder Jensen Huang, and the announcement could offer hints on the company's direction going forward.

Additionally, Anthropic's IPO is set for Thursday. The company is set to price its IPO on Wednesday night, and how it performs Thursday and industry sentiment could influence its performance.

Indeed, investors face an unusual amount of uncertainty, though it is possible Nvidia's earnings report and Anthropic's IPO could ease some of those doubts. Knowing that, watching and waiting may be an AI investor's best strategy for now.
