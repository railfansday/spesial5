Gane, meanwhile, has taken the long route to the top table, earning plenty of respect along the way, but his victory over Alex Pereira and his no-contest against Tom Aspinall have cast an unflattering light over the Frenchman's fighting style.

The 36-year-old ascended from the interim title to the title proper because Aspinall had to vacate it due to eye issues stemming from a poke by "Bon Gamin" (translation: "Good Kid").
