Maximus: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Federal and state rules now require the eligibility checks and work-requirement reviews that Maximus' program integrity business handles.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 19, 2026 at 3:20PM EST

On its fiscal 2026 fourth quarter and year-end earnings call, held on Nov. 19, 2026, Maximus (MMS -5.00%) reported full-year adjusted earnings per share of $8.24. Operational productivity and strategic acquisitions are expanding profit margins while reducing reliance on individual contracts. Three key takeaways from management's comments stand out.

Management acquired Agile Defense to expand its footprint in federal defense spending, elevating the national security segment from under $100 million to nearly 10% of total revenue. The deal gives the company immediate access to technology services like artificial intelligence and cloud modernization without waiting years to build organic credentials.

The business relies on multi-year government relationships that often span decades. High contract retention allows management to raise prices on renewals while preserving predictable cash flows across state and federal agency programs.

Implementation of HR-1, also known as the OBBBA, federal legislation governing state benefit programs, requires states to enforce eligibility redeterminations and work requirements for benefit recipients. Management expects these mandatory administrative tasks to generate durable organic growth across state-level programs.

Core operations are expanding across federal and state markets, and management isn't hiding its satisfaction about the momentum. Strong contract retention provides operational leverage, while state demand for program integrity keeps cash conversion steady. Management is watching the integration of defense technology assets and the timing of state-level program implementations.

Looking forward, management expects capital allocation to focus on debt reduction and share repurchases while navigating upcoming federal contract solicitations.
