Airbnb vs. DoorDash: Which Stock Is a Better Buy?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Airbnb posts strong net income from an asset-light model, while DoorDash's revenue is climbing fast.

By Jack Delaney – Updated Nov 21, 2026 at 2:25PM EST

Investors often weigh growth potential against current profitability when comparing Airbnb (ABNB -1.41%) and DoorDash (DASH -2.88%). Both companies have transformed how consumers spend on experiences and daily conveniences, but their financial trajectories remain distinct.

Airbnb focuses on the global travel market by connecting hosts with guests seeking unique accommodations. DoorDash operates a local commerce platform that facilitates delivery from restaurants and retail merchants. While both benefit from digital shifts, their cost structures and capital requirements differ significantly.

Airbnb operates a two-sided global marketplace that connects more than 5 million hosts with guests across roughly 220 countries. The company focuses on expanding its brand through strategic partnerships with cultural and entertainment organizations to attract a diverse range of travelers. This positioning within consumer discretionary stocks allows it to capture spending on both short-term vacations and long-term stays.

In the 12 months ended Sept. 30, 2026, revenue reached nearly $13.9 billion, representing growth of approximately 16.7% over the prior year. The company demonstrated significant bottom-line strength by generating a net income of close to $3.2 billion during this same period. This level of profitability is a hallmark of its asset-light business model, which avoids the costs of owning physical properties.

DoorDash operates a local commerce platform that connects merchants, consumers, and delivery couriers across more than 40 countries. The company has evolved beyond restaurant delivery into grocery and convenience sectors, maintaining agreements with over 600,000 merchants. Growth is further supported by over 56 million monthly active users and a proposed $17 billion acquisition of Instacart to solidify its market position.

In the 12 months ended Sept. 30, 2026, revenue reached nearly $17.1 billion, which is a significant 35% increase compared to the previous twelve-month period. The company also reached a milestone by reporting a net income of approximately $1.1 billion.

Airbnb faces intense competition from established online travel agencies like Booking Holdings (BKNG -1.64%) and Expedia Group (EXPE -1.77%), as well as traditional hotel chains. The company is also subject to evolving legal and regulatory environments, particularly restrictive short-term rental laws in major markets like New York City. Additionally, reliance on third-party payment processors and other threats present ongoing operational risks.

DoorDash contends with fierce competition from other delivery platforms and merchants who develop their own direct-to-consumer ordering systems. The business model faces regulatory scrutiny regarding the classification of couriers as independent contractors, which could significantly impact labor costs. Furthermore, the company relies on third-party service providers like Amazon (AMZN -4.10%) Web Services for its platform stability and faces integration risks from its large-scale acquisition of Instacart.

Airbnb appears more attractive based on future earnings estimates, while DoorDash carries a lower valuation when compared to its sales.

DoorDash has a strong presence in the delivery space, but between DoorDash and Airbnb, I'd lean more toward Airbnb as an investable opportunity.

One reason is that Airbnb is simply more profitable. While DoorDash's net income in the third quarter of 2026 climbed 91% to $466 million, it still pales in comparison to Airbnb's net income in the same quarter, at $1.9 billion. The other reason is that Airbnb's opportunities for expansion and revenue growth look more appealing. While DoorDash is acquiring Instacart, its platform is still more about restaurants, groceries, and broad delivery services. In comparison, Airbnb has more opportunities for expansion and integration that can lead to meaningful revenue growth by bundling car rentals, travel planning, and experiences into its offerings.

To get an idea of what to expect from Airbnb stock over the near term, analysts generally have a favorable outlook. According to CNN-tracked data, of the 46 analysts who cover the stock, 57% rate it a buy, 41% a hold, and 2% a sell. Of those analysts, the median one-year stock price forecast is $211, representing a gain of 23.8% from the price as of this writing, $170.36. The group's highest target is $245, representing a 43.8% gain, while the lowest target, $161, would be a 5.4% loss. While stock price forecasts aren't guarantees, the lowest projection indicates a 5% loss, so the risk-to-reward framework looks favorable.
