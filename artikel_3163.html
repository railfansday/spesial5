Retail buying in Anthropic’s IPO could double SpaceX’s debut, CNBC reports

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Vanda Research expects about $250m of net retail buying when Anthropic starts trading on Nasdaq on Thursday.

Anthropic expects to price its IPO on Wednesday evening and start trading on Nasdaq on Thursday under the ticker ANT. It is offering 825 million shares at $70 to $78 each, according to CNBC’s IPO guide.

At that range, the offering would raise more than $64bn and value the company at about $1.4tn, CNBC reported.

Up to 20% of the shares could go to retail investors in the US and other countries. That is far more than the 5% to 10% that small investors usually get in an IPO. Brokers taking part include Charles Schwab, Fidelity, Robinhood, SoFi and E*Trade.

That has put some people off. Joe Pak, a former macro portfolio manager, told CNBC he would skip the IPO, partly because allocations are likely to be small.

Others are keen. One retail investor told CNBC that Anthropic’s focus on safety had drawn his interest.
