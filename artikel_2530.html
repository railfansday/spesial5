Auburn football puts QB Byrum Brown on first Iron Bowl injury report

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Auburn football's starting quarterback is considered 50-50 four days out from the Iron Bowl, as Byrum Brown was listed as questionable on the contest's first SEC availability report.

It marked Brown's first appearance on the report, and it comes after he played just two snaps a week prior in a blowout win over Samford. Brown also spoke to the media Tuesday, Nov. 24.

Here's a look at the final availability report for Auburn vs Alabama, which was posted Wednesday, Nov. 25:

QUESTIONABLE: QB Byrum Brown; LB De'Marcus Riddick

OUT: OL Casey Poe; LB Cayden Jones; DL Jeremiah Beaman; DB Jireh Edwards; TE Josh Ford; DL Mhari Johnson; DB Nick Sherman;

PROBABLE: LB Caleb Woodson; LB Desmond Umeozulu; DL Fatutoa Henry; LB Luke Metz

The SEC posts multiple availability reports throughout the week heading into conference matchups, with initial reports required to be posted by 7:10 p.m. CT Wednesday. Additional reports arrive no later than 7 p.m. Thursday and Friday evening, with a final pregame availability report no later than 90 minutes before kickoff, meaning this week's final report will come no later than 5 p.m. CT Saturday.

According to reporting policy, "probable" describes a player with at least 75% expectancy of playing, "questionable" means a player has a 50% chance of playing and "doubtful" means a player is unlikely to play or has a 25% chance of being available.

Availability reports don't include details on injuries, nor additional context surrounding a player's status.
