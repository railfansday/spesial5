B/R grades Michael Penix Jr.'s performance vs. Chiefs

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

To some extent every quarterback in the NFL is streaky - getting into a rhythm and maintaining it is a constant battle at this level, where defenses force adjustments practically every week.

That said, some quarterbacks are a bit more volatile than others, and right now Atlanta Falcons starter Michael Penix Jr. has as wide a range of potential outcomes as any other QB1 in the league.

From Weeks 3-7 Penix was on fire, culminating in two straight three-touchdown, zero-interception performances against the very tough defenses of the Chicago Bears and the San Francisco 49ers. At that point it looked like Penix was ready to establish himself as a top-10 starter.

Unfortunately, since then Penix's performance has been in free-fall mode. Over the last three games, he has just one touchdown to go with six interceptions, and Atlanta lost all three by big margins. For his latest effort against the Kansas City Chiefs, Penix has earned a C- grade from Brent Sobleski at Bleacher Report.

"The Falcons were particularly awful Sunday during critical downs and working in the red zone. Atlanta finished a combined five for 18 on third and fourth downs. Meanwhile, the Penix-led offense scored one touchdown despite five red-zone trips. The performance led to a dreaded vote of confidence in Penix from head coach Kevin Stefanski after the contest. However, the possibility of a change at the game's most important position still exists as Atlanta enters its bye week."

The Falcons are now on their bye week, which gives them some time to try to fix what's wrong with Penix. However, once they return to action they'll immediately face a high-level test against the Minnesota Vikings and defensive coordinator Brian Flores, who might be the best defensive play-caller in the game right now.

If Penix comes out with a strong game against the Vikings it would be a very encouraging sign, but the odds are against it. No matter how Week 12 goes, Penix is going to have to bounce back at some point in the second half of the season and start playing well again - or else the Falcons may go looking for another starter in 2027.
