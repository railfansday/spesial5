Kellie Pickler Has Settlement With Late Husband Kyle Jacobs’ Parents

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Kellie Pickler reached a settlement with the parents of her late husband Kyle Jacobs after they accused her of refusing to hand over his property—including guns and jewelry—following his 2023 death.

Kellie Pickler is putting a tough chapter behind her.

The "Red High Heels" singer reached a property settlement with her late husband Kyle Jacobs’ parents, Reed and Sharon, following his 2023 death, according to documents obtained by People on Nov. 4.

The terms of the settlement are confidential, though the docs stated that Pickler, 40, and her in-laws “have fully resolved all claims, disputes, and requests for relief by and between them" and that the parties are “satisfied with the distribution of assets” of his estate.

After Jacobs died by suicide at age 49, Pickler and his parents became engrossed in a legal battle over his property. His parents alleged that Pickler refused to give them Jacobs' belongings—such as his guns, jewelry and musical instruments—according to May 2024 documents obtained by People.

Among the items they requested were his Japanese samurai sword, Rolex watch, Garmin watch, baseball card albums, school awards, seven pistols, work laptop and iPhone, per their subpoena obtained by InTouch.

As for the American Idol alum, she said her in-laws "entered [her] home and obtained items of personal property after [Jacobs'] death," according to court docs obtained by the outlet.

Jacobs, a songwriter who worked with Garth Brooks and Tim McGraw, was found dead in the Nashville home he shared with Pickler in February 2023, with the Davidson Country Medical Examiner later ruling it a suicide.

Toxicology tests determined Jacobs did not have any drugs in his system at the time, though it noted he had a history of "pseudoseizures, gastrointestinal bleeding, elevated liver enzymes, and chronic alcohol use," per an autopsy report obtained by Taste of Country.

Pickler remained out of the spotlight in the wake of his passing, breaking her silence on her grief six months later.

"One of the most beautiful lessons my husband taught me was in a moment of a crisis, if you don't know what to do, ‘do nothing, just be still,'" the country star told People in August 2023. "I have chosen to heed his advice."

"Thank you to my family, friends, and supporters, for the countless letters, calls, and messages that you have sent my way," she continued. "It has truly touched my soul and it's helping me get through the darkest time in my life. As many of you have told me, you are all in my prayers."

The couple—who began dating in 2008 and married in 2011—weren't just partners in life, but also in music. They collaborated on several songs together, including 2008’s "One Last Time," 2013’s "The Woman I Am" and 2015’s "Feeling Tonight.”

"Yes, it's true, I'm sleeping with my producer!" she joked to People in 2015. "He is so good at what he does. I'm so excited to be in the studio again recording a new album, and working with Kyle has been amazing. He has watched me perform for years, so it was great to have a front row seat as an artist and see him in his element."

The "Best Days of Your Life" artist also credited Jacobs as her “saving grace.”

"My grandmother really showed me how to be a wife, a good wife," she told CMT Radio in 2011, per The Boot. "I never thought I'd be one though, because I never thought there would be a man out there who could love me. There's more flaws than smooth edges. They're mostly jagged, tattered. I'm really tattered at the seams. I'm very blessed."
