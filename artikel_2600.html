A Thanksgiving thank you to the community that changed my life

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

**While this article mentions Phoenix Suns experiences, this piece is about the gratitude that comes with covering the team. So if you're looking for a piece analyzing the team's hot streak or Jalen Green's trade value, feel free to check out other content on Bright Side. **

It is well known in sports media circles that SB Nation sites serve as an incubation lab for young journalists. Being able to write about professional sports, and maybe even get paid to do so, helps so many people achieve their dreams much earlier than they expected while gaining real-world experience in what it's like to work in this profession full-time.

From covering the Super Bowl, the NBA Draft, or Hall of Fame ceremonies, what you put into your time at SB Nation is what you get out of it. But to earn those opportunities, you need people to trust you. And on this 2026 Thanksgiving, I'd like to thank the Bright Side community for helping me land my first full-time job in sports media.

First and foremost, thank you to every reader. Whether you're a frequent post commenter or this is the first Bright Side piece you've ever read, I appreciate you. Knowing there are always people to serve and that we get to share the unique access we have at the NBA Draft, playoff games, Summer League, and many other events keeps our purpose strong: serving passionate Suns fans.

To the late, great Dave King, Harrison Faigen, and John Voita, thank you for taking a chance on a 20-year-old and allowing me to fly out to Philadelphia to cover Kevin Durant and reigning MVP Joel Embiid. Your trust in me is what has allowed me to conduct one-on-one interviews with Khaman Maluach and Rasheer Fleming after they were drafted, sit down with Amar'e Stoudemire and Mike D'Antoni before their Hall of Fame inductions, and cover dozens of NBA events across the country. I've met so many of my media idols and learned so many lessons about the industry at a much younger age than I expected.

This is the wordy DM I sent to John Voita when I was 19, looking for a chance. ANY chance to write about the NBA.

The relationships, experiences, and lessons I've gained in the first few years of my media career helped me stay sane and confident during my job search. It took me about six months to find my first full-time job in sports media after graduating from Syracuse in May (where the voice of the Suns, Jon Bloom, also went!). While many people have had to wait much longer to find their first job, and plenty spend more than six months between gigs, the process still took a toll on me. But I knew that, thanks to my experience at Bright Side, I'd eventually end up somewhere doing what I love.

So, on a day centered around thankfulness, thank you, Bright Side, for helping me land my first full-time job in sports media. I'm now working as an on-air host at WeTheHobby, where I broadcast, livestream, and podcast about the latest happenings in the NBA collectibles space.

My Bright Side coverage is still the same as it's been since I joined the staff, but now I get to do it knowing that I'm a full-time sports media professional and that what I've learned from being a part of this community has played a major role in getting me here. I'm truly grateful.

Happy Thanksgiving, everyone! If only there were NBA games on today!
