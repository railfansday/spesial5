Patrick Mahomes sounds off on 5 TD performance, blowout win vs Falcons

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Patrick Mahomes put together his best performance of the 2026 regular season during the Kansas City Chiefs' Week 10 win over the Atlanta Falcons, completing 22 of his 35 passes for 348 yards and five touchdowns en route to a 35-13 victory at Mercedes-Benz Stadium.

Kansas City's record advanced to 7-2 after the stunning road win, and Mahomes' marvelous outing against the Falcons has kept the Chiefs in prime position to earn a spot in the AFC playoffs.
