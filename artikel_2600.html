49ers-Cowboys cranks out 27.13 million viewers

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Fox's Sunday tripleheader culminated in the national telecast of the 8-1 49ers at the record-doesn't-matter Cowboys.

On average, 27.13 million watched the 46-40 barnburner, which ultimately underscored the fact that: (1) the 49ers are elite; and (2) the old "Doomsday Defense" nickname has a much different meaning now.

The performance of the game reconfirms the fact that 4:25 p.m. ET has become the most valuable weekly window. It currently rotates between CBS and FOX, with multiple simultaneous games per year.

It's hard not to imagine the NFL considering breaking that window away from the standard Sunday afternoon packages and selling it to one network. Given the size of the audiences, it would be the most lucrative package the NFL could offer.

This week, the NFL moved Steelers-Eagles from the late-afternoon window on CBS and shifted Jaguars-Giants into the spot. Raiders-Broncos also starts at 4:25 p.m. ET.
