Tanggal: 2026-11-25

At the Broad Street team store, retail workers began folding away 76ers jerseys and hats, replacing them with Flyers Hometown Remix sweaters and Gritty memorabilia. Banners of Tyrese Maxey and VJ Edgecombe were quickly replaced with Trevor Zegras and Porter Martone. Cleaning crews swept through the seating bowl, collecting discarded cups and spilled popcorn. One worker called out for a broom, warning that she'd fall behind schedule if someone didn't find her one soon. One floor below, catering crews led by executive chef Anthony Campagna began loading refrigerators with food for Flyers front-office staff, family and friends and corporate suites. It's often a race to the main elevators, Campagna said, which could become bottlenecks during doubleheader changeovers. Cold air also began pumping into the building, lowering the temperature across the arena's more than one million square feet. The NBA requires its games to be played at around 68 degrees Fahrenheit, according to Allen, while the NHL requires a cooler temperature of 58 degrees.
