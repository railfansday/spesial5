Bitdeer: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Bitdeer's 3.1 gigawatts of power can earn Bitcoin mining income today while sites await long-term data center leases.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 10, 2026, Bitdeer (BTDR +9.67%) announced an 89% year-over-year revenue increase to $320.7 million as it transitions power assets into high-performance computing data centers. The company is pivoting from volatile Bitcoin mining to long-term contracted artificial intelligence (AI) cloud infrastructure. Three things management said stand out.

Bitdeer is securing multiyear contracts for its high-performance computing (HPC, data centers built for intensive AI tasks) infrastructure and cloud services, giving the business visible cash flows that don't depend on cryptocurrency price swings.

Management is backing data center construction with asset-level debt and customer prepayments that cover more than 50% of graphics processing unit (GPU) costs, keeping share dilution low.

With 3.1 gigawatts of total power capacity, Bitdeer monetizes available electricity through Bitcoin mining while preparing sites for long-term data center leases.

Demand for artificial intelligence compute outpaces the available power supply, and Bitdeer relies on pre-energized sites, low-cost power, and proprietary hardware to convert mining locations into data centers, while maintaining financing execution and capital discipline.

The company aims to maximize returns by deploying power where economic value is highest, balancing short-term crypto earnings with long-term data center revenue.
