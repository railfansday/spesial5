Opinion: Liverpool should replace Alisson with 'immense' goalkeeper

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Alisson Becker has been one of the best goalkeepers the Premier League has ever seen.

Since his arrival at Anfield in 2018, the Brazilian has consistently delivered at a truly world class level.

Now 34, the shot-stopper is into the final year of his current deal and a potential move away from Merseyside is beginning to look more likely.

The 'keeper has made his desire to stay with the Reds clear, but concerns over his age, wage demands and injury record may lead the Liverpool hierarchy to move on.

Should the club choose to move in a different direction, the perfect Alisson replacement is waiting for them in the Premier League.

Several goalkeepers from across Europe have been touted as potential heirs to Alisson, including some already on the books at Liverpool.

Giorgi Mamardashvili has at times been viewed as the natural replacement for the Brazilian, whilst new signing Luca Brughmans is considered a long-term option for the Reds.

The perfect replacement for Andoni Iraola's number one is already playing for another Premier League side however, and on Sunday he showed just how good he can be.

Konstantinos Tzolakis has been Hull City's standout performer on their return to the Premier League, and pretty much single handedly kept them in Sunday's clash against Brighton.

The Tigers went on to win 2-0 with two injury time goals, but Tzolakis' display was the real highlight.

The Greek international made seven saves, six coming from inside the box, preventing 1.46 xG, and earning himself the man of the match award.

Tzolakis' performance earned him plenty of praise from around the football world, including from Henry Winter, who described the Greek shot-stopper as 'immense'.

Sunday wasn't the first time the 'keeper has been instrumental in earning points for Hull either, and it marked his fourth Premier League clean sheet of the season.

At just 24-years-old, Tzolakis could be the perfect long-term heir to Alisson at Liverpool, and the Reds should be doing everything they can to get the 'keeper to Anfield this summer.
