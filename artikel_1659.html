The World Cup RETURNS to America! FIFA announces 2031 tournament hosts after historic four-country bid

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

By ISABEL BALDWIN, US ASSISTANT SPORTS EDITOR

Published: 10:23 EST, 23 November 2026 | Updated: 13:19 EST, 23 November 2026

The World Cup is set to return to America after FIFA awarded the 2031 women's tournament to a historic four-nation bid.

Following the success of the men's tournament this summer, the United States was announced as one of the hosts of the women's edition in five years' time during a virtual meeting of FIFA's national associations on Monday.

The US will co-host the global soccer extravaganza alongside Mexico, Costa Rica and Jamaica after the four countries' joint bid was awarded uncontested the 2031 tournament.

The selection marks a milestone for the Women's World Cup with the tournament being hosted across four nations for the first time in its history.

The last World Cup was co-hosted by multiple countries for the first time with the 2023 edition taking place across Australia and New Zealand.

This summer's men's World Cup was also co-hosted by the US with games taking place across the States, Canada and Mexico.

The World Cup is set to return to the United States for the 2031 women's edition of the event

Spain are reigning champions after winning the Women's World Cup in 2023

The United States Women's National Team are four-time winners in 1991, 1999, 2015 and 2019

The 2031 tournament will also be the largest in the competition's history, featuring an expanded 48-team field and 104 matches for the first time.

'We are incredibly honored and excited to welcome the world to the United States, Mexico, Costa Rica and Jamaica for the 2031 FIFA Women's World Cup,' said US Soccer President Cindy Parlow Cone.

'This is a historic moment for our four countries, but the opportunity is much bigger than any one of us. Together with FIFA, we want to deliver the biggest and most impactful Women's World Cup in history and use its success to accelerate the growth of women's soccer around the world. Our ambition is to create a tournament that not only inspires millions of players, but helps give them the opportunities to play, grow and pursue their dreams.'

The selection marks the third time that the Women's World Cup has been awarded to the US with the country previously hosting the landmark 1999 and 2003 editions alone. The national team's tournament win in 1999 was the second of its four titles.

Meanwhile, Mexico, Costa Rica and Jamaica will become first-time hosts at the historic tournament.

'This milestone furthers the continued belief that the best days of soccer in America are still ahead,' said U.S. Soccer CEO and General Secretary JT Batson.

'We have an opportunity to use the momentum from the 2031 FIFA Women's World Cup to accelerate our ambitions for the future of soccer, from expanding access and participation to strengthening the pathways that develop the next generation of players. We are excited to work alongside our partners across the region to make this tournament a defining moment for women's soccer.'

The U.S.-led 2031 project across the CONCACAF regional soccer body bid offers FIFA a choice of stadiums in 20 cities: 14 in the U.S., four in Mexico plus San José in Costa Rica and Kingston, Jamaica.

US Soccer President Cindy Parlow Cone said the federation was honored to be selected

The host selection of the 2035 Women's World Cup was awarded to a joint bid between England, Northern Ireland, Scotland and Wales

The host selection of the 2035 Women's World Cup was also announced on Monday with the tournament being awarded to another joint bid between England, Northern Ireland, Scotland and Wales.

It marks the first time that a World Cup has been hosted on the British Isles since the men's tournament in 1966 in England.

The United Kingdom bid joins England with Northern Ireland, Scotland and Wales that offers a choice from 19 venues, including Wembley Stadium in London.

The winning candidates and FIFA president Gianni Infantino promised commercially successful tournaments - the first editions with 48 teams and 104 games - and to reinvest all that money in women's soccer.

FIFA wanted these heavyweights of women's soccer to face no opposition without rival bids and so lead hosting the first expanded tournaments. That will bring parity with the men's World Cup which expanded to 48 teams this year in the US, Canada and Mexico.

The 2027 Women's World Cup will have 32 teams in Brazil.
