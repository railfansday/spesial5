Athletic: Man Utd trigger Fernandes option – exit not ruled out amid Milan and Nico Paz links

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Manchester United have activated an option to extend the contract of captain Bruno Fernandes, but it does not rule out an exit.

Fernandes was in the final year of his deal with United and he recently revealed that he had opted to shelve the talks over extending it. However, the club always had a 12-month option to push the deadline back to June 2028, and they activated that on Friday as per The Athletic.

The decision is one that protects the Red Devils, given that the Portuguese playmaker was set to be free to agree a pre-contract foreign teams from January 1. Despite this, the report adds that the extension ‘does not rule out an exit in 2027’ and ‘would just enable United to command a fee’.

Fernandes ‘remains the subject of interest from Europe and Saudi Arabia’, while United too are starting to imagine life without the former Sporting CP star. For example, The Athletic are also reporting that Como playmaker Nico Paz is among the replacements they admire.

Fernandes will still feel that he has a lot to give at the highest level, having broken the Premier League assist record last season (21). He earned the Football Writers’ Association Footballer of the Year and Professional Footballers’ Association Player of the Year awards.

In recent weeks, there has been talk of a potential reunion with Ruben Amorim at Milan, which Fabrizio Romano suggested he would welcome. The pair worked together in Portugal and there could be an ideal role for his compatriot in the 4-2-3-1/3-4-2-1 system that Amorim plays.
