Romano: Balde expected to leave Barcelona – Milan’s position after summer talks

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

AC Milan have been linked with a move for Alejandro Balde, but Fabrizio Romano claims some flexibility is needed from Barcelona.

In the past couple of weeks there has been more talk about Milan strengthening in the left-back role. Pervis Estupinan has not been convincing since his arrival, while Davide Bartesaghi and Diego Moreira have been used further forward in recent weeks.

Yunus Musah was even adapted to playing as a left-sided defender in the recent win over Genoa, so it’s clear that Ruben Amorim might welcome a more natural player for the role, one that fits his ideas of how to play.

In a video on his YouTube channel, Fabrizio Romano touched upon a number of different topics connected to Milan, from Francesco Camarda’s fate to the possibility of a young midfielder arriving. He also spoke about Balde – another recent link – and his words are via MilanNews.

“Balde is a name that can be a key player in January. Then, to be completely honest, he’s a foreign transfer name, being realistic. He earns more than €5 million net per season,” he began.

“Barcelona were willing to let him go in the summer, but wanted a permanent deal worth €50-55 million. As for Italy, despite Mendes also trying to see if there was any room for negotiation with Milan, there were never any real negotiations. Also because the costs are decidedly high.

“Balde still isn’t playing and will speak with Mendes today, who is on a mission to Barcelona to meet with the player and his family and figure out what to do in the January transfer window.

“I’d keep an eye on the possibility if the formula were to change, because these are currently foreign figures. But he’s a player to keep an eye on. We’ll see if he goes to Italy or abroad.”
