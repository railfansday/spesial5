GdS: Predicted XIs for Olympiacos vs. Milan – Amorim opts for strong team in must-win

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

AC Milan travel to Greece to face Olympiacos in the Europa League this evening in desperate need of a victory.

Milan have won just one of their opening four European fixtures, which has them in the bottom 12 positions and currently set to crash out. Their opponents, meanwhile, have won three and drawn one which plants them firmly in the top eight spots.

What doesn’t help the must-win mission is the amount of absences for Ruben Amorim to deal with. Pietro Terracciano, Ruben Loftus-Cheek, Luka Modric, Samuel Chukwueze, Strahinja Pavlovic, Davide Bartesaghi, Matteo Gabbia, Alexis Saelemaekers and Francesco Camarda are all out.

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, Amorim will go with a four-man defence of Mario Gila, Koni De Winter, Sankhoun Diawara and Pervis Estupinan in front of the goalkeeper and captain Mike Maignan.

Ardon Jashari will get a chance in the double pivot alongside Adrien Rabiot. Diego Moreira, Omari Hutchinson and Christian Pulisic will then support the centre-forward Goncalo Ramos in a 4-2-3-1 formation.

It will be a young bench due to the injuries, featuring the likes of Pittarella, Vladimirov, Comotto and Ossola. Cisse and Musah are also ready to lend a hand during the game.
