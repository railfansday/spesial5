Arsenal lose second successive pre-season friendly after parading Guimaraes

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Arsenal suffered back-to-back pre-season defeats as they lost 3-2 to Borussia Dortmund in the Emirates Cup on Sunday.

The loss to the German side followed a 3-1 defeat by Real Betis on Wednesday night in Dublin.

Before kick-off, Bruno Guimarães was paraded to the Arsenal faithful after completing his £75m move from Newcastle on Saturday.

But enthusiasm about the Brazilian’s arrival was dampened when Dortmund took the lead at the Emirates inside the opening 10 minutes. Felix Nmecha’s incisive pass into the penalty area was met by the run of Samuele Inácio, who made no mistake with a clinical finish into the far corner.

The Bundesliga side doubled their lead just before the half-hour mark as 18-year-old Greek international Konstantinos Karetsas curled one in from the edge of the box.

Ten minutes after half-time, Arsenal pulled a goal back through Ethan Nwaneri, who scored from Christos Tzolis’s cross. However, moments later Dortmund restored their two-goal advantage as Joane Gadou smashed home from a corner.

Arsenal kept their hopes alive through a Viktor Gyokeres penalty after Tzolis was brought down, but Dortmund were able to hold on to claim victory. After the full-time whistle, there was a penalty shoot-out, which Arsenal won 5-4.

Arsenal still have a number of players to return to action following their World Cup exploits, including Declan Rice, Bukayo Saka and Noni Madueke. England and Real Madrid midfielder Jude Bellingham was in attendance at the Emirates to watch his younger brother Jobe in action for Dortmund.

As well as Guimarães, there was another addition on display at the Emirates: Arsenal have added last year’s title win to the list of honours displayed inside the stadium.

Arsenal play Cesc Fàbregas’s Como on Wednesday, before taking on Manchester City in the Community Shield next weekend. Mikel Arteta’s side then start the defence of their Premier League crown at home against newly promoted Coventry City.

When Bruno Guimarães signed for Newcastle United from Lyon in January 2022, much was made of the idea that he had rejected Arsenal in favour of a move to St James’ Park. The Brazilian played along with it, posting a video on his social media pages which featured multiple newspaper headlines about Arsenal wanting to buy him.

The Newcastle fans, still delirious about the recent takeover by Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund, understandably lapped it up. Their dream was to overthrow the established powers in English football, and the capture of Guimarães was regarded as a sign that they were truly ready to challenge teams of Arsenal’s stature and wealth.

Even the lyrics to the midfielder’s chant referred to the north Londoners, as Newcastle fans sang that Guimarães “could have signed for Arsenal” but chose instead to play “in the famous black and white”.

Yesterday Arsenal announced the signing of Brazilian midfielder Bruno Guimaraes from Newcastle for £75m on a four-year deal, with the option of a further year. Here is what the Brazilian had to say:

“I feel good, I feel amazing. I am glad for the opportunity. Since the first time I spoke to (Andrea) Berta and Mikel (Arteta) I was excited. I just felt inside of me that I need a new challenge in my life and I think to be an Arsenal player is going to be an exciting challenge in my life.

“I think after playing two World Cups, I am at the point of my life, I think I need a challenge like this. I want to win trophies, I want to make history and I think I am in the right place to do it. I am so excited to start. We have tough games to play this season so the squad is going to be very, very important and I am hopeful we can achieve our dreams.”

Premier League champions Arsenal continue their preparations for the new season as they take on Borussia Dortmund in the Emirates Cup. Arsenal beat Spanish side Athletic Bilbao 3-0 in the 2025 edition, with goals from Viktor Gyokeres, Bukayo Saka and Kai Havertz sealing victory. These sides met during the 2014/15 Champions League group stages, with both games being won 2-0 by the home side.

Arsenal beat Girona 4-1 just over a week ago but fell to a 3-1 defeat to Real Betis in Dublin on Wednesday night. Reflecting on that defeat, Mikel Arteta expressed his frustration at the way his side conceded the goals.

“I am disappointed with the result,” Arteta told Arsenal’s in-house media. “We are here to win football matches. We had some fantastic moments in the first half, the way we played, the way we opened them up. We have to score first, clearly, in the two chances that we had before the score.

“I am very disappointed in the manner that we conceded the first goal, the two giveaways leading to the other two goals. It is true that the finishing quality from them was exceptional, but to win a football match, you have to compete at a different level, especially in those areas of the pitch and that is the learning from today [last Wednesday].”

This is in fact Arsenal’s first game at the Emirates since being crowned Premier League champions, as they sealed the title with a game to spare, with that final match of last season being away at Crystal Palace. After today, Arsenal have two more games, including the Community Shield in a week’s time, before they kick off the defence of their Premier League crown at home against newly-promoted Coventry. The likes of Declan Rice, Bukayo Saka and Martin Zubimendi are still yet to return after their World Cup exploits.

Dortmund, led by Niko Kovac, finished second in the Bundesliga last season, 16 points off winners Bayern Munich. They have played four pre-season friendlies thus far, winning twice and losing twice. The likes of Karim Adeyemi, Julian Brandt and Niklas Sule have left the club this summer.
