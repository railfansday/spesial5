If AI Makes Us Faster, Why Aren't the Apps We Use Getting Better?

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

This week a Jonathan Blow video titled "If AI solved coding, why is my software still the same?" landed on the Hacker News front page and pulled more than 150 comments.

The question stuck with me because I don't have a good answer.

If AI really makes developers several times faster, I'd expect to notice it as a user by now. Fewer bugs. Faster apps. Features I've wanted for years finally showing up. But the apps on my phone feel about the same as they did two years ago. Some feel worse.

Personally, I feel faster on small, boring tasks and not much faster on anything that needs real thinking. I'm honestly not sure that adds up to better software for the people using it.

One-line answers welcome. I'm especially curious to hear from people who think the improvement is real and I'm just not seeing it.
