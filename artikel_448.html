Nvidia Pares Gains on Blackwell Report; Altria Lower | Stock Movers

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Today's biggest winners and losers in the stock market. On this episode of Stock Movers: - Nvidia (NVDA) shares are higher even though news broke this morning that a pair of Chinese firms installed Nvidia Corp's powerful Blackwell semiconductors in a Shanghai data center linked to Alice Huang. The gear installed at the Yangtze River Delta Project included a GB300 NVL72 rack, among Nvidia's most powerful products, according to a serial number specified in documents submitted to Chinese regulators. - Altria (MO) shares are declining after the company issued a statement on Nov. 26 confirming early media speculation that it is considering an all cash offer for British cigarette maker Imperial Brands. - SAP (SAP) shares are moving in response to news it is preparing a plan to eliminate at least 10,000 positions to transform its software business for the artificial intelligence era. (Source: Bloomberg)
