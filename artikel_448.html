Amazon has cut the price of KPop Demon Hunters Collectible Trading Cards (Classic Edition)

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

SAVE $3.81: As of Nov. 4, the KPop Demon Hunters Collectible Trading Cards Classic Edition is on sale for $31.99 at Amazon. That's an 11% discount on the list price.

Rumi, Mira and Zoey have taken over screens, playlists, and pretty much every corner of pop culture, so it was only a matter of time before trading cards got in on the action. If this is up your street, or if you just love anything KPop Demon Hunters related, you'll currently find the Classic Edition set on sale at Amazon.

Normally $35.80, as of Nov. 4, they are discounted to $31.99.

The KPop Demon Hunters Classic Edition trading cards celebrate the film's anniversary with colorful artwork inspired by its characters, epic showdowns, and iconic scenes. Each box contains 20 packs, with five cards in each, featuring vibrant designs printed on premium card stock.

Look out for the special ZR cards inspired by “Honmoon” (the magical shield in the movie), which use fluorescent ink to bring the patterns to life and recreate the look of the film's full-powered showdown. If you're a true KPop Demon Hunters fan, these cards are a great addition to your collection, or a great place to start one.

You'll find this set available to buy on Amazon right now.
