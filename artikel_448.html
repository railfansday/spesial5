In The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, viewers will witness the first sparks of the revolution that eventually catch fire in The Hunger Games trilogy. But if Sunrise on the Reaping stars Mckenna Grace and Ben Wang had to lead their own revolution, which franchise characters would they want by their sides?

To find out, Grace and Wang, who play District 12 tributes Maysilee Donner and Wyatt Callow, stopped by Mashable's Say More studio to play a game of Choose Your Squad. They drafted their own revolutionary teams from a list of characters from the original Hunger Games trilogy, as well as Sunrise on the Reaping and The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes. That's right, everyone from Katniss Everdeen to Lucy Gray Baird is in play.

Which character will be the first draft pick? Which squad would pull off the best revolution? And will either Grace or Wang pull the ultimate wild card move and draft President Snow to their side?

Find out by watching the whole video above, and may the odds be ever in your favor.
