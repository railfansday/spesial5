Harry Styles Hits Festivals & Billie Eilish is Back: The Biggest Talking Points From Glastonbury’s 2027 Lineup Reveal

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The legendary festival will return to Somerset, south west England, next summer.

Few events hold quite the same place in the U.K.’s collective imagination as Glastonbury. Year after year, the legendary Somerset festival manages to dominate the musical conversation long before the gates to Worthy Farm even open, with speculation over its headline slots and potential surprise appearances keeping fans dissecting every detail for months on end.

On Wednesday (Nov. 18), the wait finally came to an end, with Glastonbury unveiling its 2027 bill – and, as always, there’s plenty to get people giddy. From Sam Fender, Harry Styles and Billie Eilish making up the festival’s youngest-ever crop of headliners to a handful of more unexpected – but welcome – additions (Underscores, Chase & Status) and other curios. There are bold, intriguing bookings to be found all across the slate, many of which will continue to spark debate among ticket-holders plotting their weekend itineraries.

What’s more, 2027 will mark Glastonbury’s eagerly anticipated return following its traditional fallow year, with the festival sitting out 2026 to give the site’s dairy farm fields a much-needed chance to recover. “We are so happy to be coming back after our fallow year and, as we head into winter after that peaceful period, we wanted to give you an early musical taster of what’s to come with this spread of incredible artists,” festival organiser Emily Eavis posted to Instagram.

“We will bring you even more names in the new year and can’t wait to see you down here on Worthy Farm in June for some much-needed good times!”

With revered names from the worlds of pop, rock, jazz, jungle, J-pop and beyond all set to converge on Worthy Farm next summer, here are the biggest talking points from Glastonbury’s first 2027 line-up announcement.

Some good news for Styles, then, just days after an almighty Grammys snub: he’s headlining one of the biggest festivals on the planet. This has been one of the U.K. industry’s worst-kept secrets for a while now, but there are still some unknowns. He’s never topped the bill at a U.K. festival before, and has generally swerved the festival scene for his own headline shows. Performing for casual observers is not something that Styles has much experience doing, having been closely followed by his fans since his One Direction days. He’ll need something special to keep everyone happy down on Worthy Farm given that it’s not “his crowd”; we suspect he’ll rise to the challenge. – THOMAS SMITH

Here he is, one of the U.K.’s most beloved modern talents, preparing to make the leap to Glastonbury headliner status at a festival he has described as “the best party on the planet.” In hindsight, the signs were there. Back in June, Fender shared footage of his emotional, golden-hour performance from the festival’s 2022 edition to Instagram, while social media chatter suggested the North Shields songwriter had spent the summer running riot in his adopted north London, so excited about the booking that he had already let neighbors and friends in on the news. Following a monumental 2026 – which has seen his Olivia Dean team-up “Rein Me In” smash chart records – and with a stadium tour on the horizon, and likely a new album, this promises to be a particularly special set. – SOPHIE WILLIAMS

Among the three main acts, Eilish is the only one who has previously headlined the festival. She topped the bill in 2022 alongside Paul McCartney and Kendrick Lamar, making history as the youngest solo artist to secure a Pyramid Stage headline slot. At the time, her catalog consisted of just two studio albums – most recently 2021’s Happier Than Ever. Her Sunday night set was impressive, though somewhat subdued, catering to an audience that was less familiar with her deeper album tracks compared to her hit singles.

Her return for 2027 carries much stronger momentum, following the massive global success of 2024’s Hit Me Hard and Soft and its accompanying world tour. With the potential for even more new material by next summer, her upcoming appearance promises a dynamic and refreshed show that will contrast significantly with her previous set. – T.S.

Now this is a booking. Few artists can lay claim to a career as enduring and downright extraordinary as Cher’s, making her appointment to Glastonbury’s coveted Sunday afternoon Legends Slot something of a coup – particularly given that the pop icon appeared to suggest she was ready to call time on performing back in 2024. Thankfully, it seems Worthy Farm has other plans. Get ready for the Pyramid Stage crowd to be awash with bouffant black wigs and sequins as the 80-year-old makes her inaugural trip to these fields, with an arsenal of hits spanning six decades ready to go. – S.W.

Sombr has undeniably established himself as a big dog in modern pop. Next summer, he will headline five sold-out shows at London’s 20,000-capacity O2 Arena as part of the venue’s 20th-anniversary celebration, building on a breakthrough 2026 tour across European festivals. Securing a spot on Glastonbury’s second row – just below the three headliners and Cher – marks another major milestone in his trajectory, and positions him as a strong candidate for a future headline slot. – T.S.

Over the years, Glastonbury has hosted several landmark moments in hip-hop, rap, and grime. Jay-Z made history as the festival’s first hip-hop headliner in 2008, Stormzy delivered an iconic headline set in 2019, and Kendrick Lamar gave an acclaimed performance in 2022. However, the initial 2027 announcement features a lighter representation from the genre, with Little Simz and De La Soul standing out as the primary acts. While more artists will likely join the lineup in the spring, this continues a trend where major international hip-hop acts are less prominent, reflecting potential shifts in international touring priorities or U.K. genre pipelines. – T.S.

In contrast, electronic and dance music remain heavily featured. Glastonbury has consistently supported the genre, and in 2027 reserved prominent slots for iconic acts like Massive Attack, Underworld, and Kraftwerk. A high-profile back-to-back set featuring Josh Baker, Kettama, and Prospa stands out as a major highlight following their three sold-out shows at London’s Finsbury Park. Although some anticipated Fred again.. could finally take on a main stage headline spot, the depth of the electronic lineup ensures ravers’ feet will keep on moving throughout the weekend. – T.S.

For a band that has delivered one of the year’s biggest records with Dopamine Chamber – and firmly established themselves as Glastonbury favourites along the way, having played the festival during every album campaign thus far – Fontaines D.C.’s absence from the 2027 bill is enough to raise an eyebrow (or several). Their omission feels notable given that the Dublin-formed five-piece will be touring U.K. and Ireland stadiums in the run-up to the festival – with their massive Slane Castle gigs happening the same weekend – and not least their current standing as one of the most electrifying live acts we have. Of course, there’s always the possibility of a late addition, or will fans have to wait another year for their return? – S.W.

Despite building serious momentum throughout 2026 following her Billboard Hot 100-charting hit “Die On This Hill,” London singer Spiro has kept a relatively low profile on the festival circuit, with Lorde’s All Points East show among her few U.K. festival appearances to date. Even internationally, her live schedule has offered relatively limited opportunities to catch her in a festival setting beyond a dazzling Montreux Jazz appearance this past July, and a string of U.S. sets. Yet having already been touted as a prime candidate for a few BRIT Awards next year, as Spiro’s profile continues to grow, a star-turn at Glastonbury could provide another major springboard towards pop superstardom. – S.W.
