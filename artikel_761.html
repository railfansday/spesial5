Ariana Grande Lands Four Grammy Nominations, Including Song of the Year Nod, After “Taking a Step Back From Visibility”

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The nominations come months after the 'Wicked' star released her eighth studio album, 'Petal,' and wrapped up her Eternal Sunshine Tour.

Ariana Grande has received four nominations for the 2027 Grammys.

Grande’s nominations, which were announced Monday morning, include a song of the year nod for “Hate That I Made You Love Me,” which also scored a best music video nod, as well as noms for best pop vocal album (Petal) and best traditional pop vocal performance for her “For Good” duet from Wicked: For Good alongside Cynthia Erivo.

In the song of the year category, Grande is nominated alongside Rosalía’s “Berghain,” Ella Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas,” Olivia Rodrigo’s “The Cure,” Taylor Swift’s “I Knew It, I Knew You,” Olivia Dean’s “Man I Need,” Bruno Mars’ “Risk It All” and Raye’s “Where Is My Husband!” Related Stories Music 2027 Grammy Nominations: Raye, Rosalía Lead a Women-Dominated General Field Music Grammy Nominations: Watch the Livestream

Most of Grande’s nominations come in support of her eighth studio album, Petal, released July 31. The 12-track album debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200.

The singer has previously won three Grammys. She earned her first in 2019 for best pop vocal album for Sweetener, followed by best pop duo/group performance for “Rain on Me” with Lady Gaga in 2021 and, earlier this year, for her and Erivo’s “Defying Gravity” from the first Wicked film.

The 2027 Grammy nominations come more than two months after Grande wrapped up her Eternal Sunshine Tour, with her final performance taking place in London on Sept. 1. Her 2024 album, Eternal Sunshine, earned three Grammy nominations but was shut out at the 67th Annual Grammy Awards.

In recent years, Grande has also returned to acting, starring as Glinda the Good Witch in Jon M. Chu’s Wicked (2024) and Wicked: For Good (2025). The role earned her an Oscar nomination for best supporting actress at the 97th Academy Awards.

In August, Grande’s representative shared a statement with The Hollywood Reporter saying that the singer would be “taking a step back from visibility” after finishing her Eternal Sunshine Tour. The statement came amid “ongoing public scrutiny” and a wave of online commentary surrounding her health and body. Grande also dropped out of the West End revival of Stephen Sondheim’s Sunday in the Park With George, which was set to feature her Wicked co-star Jonathan Bailey.

Last week, Grande attended the premiere of Focker-in-Law, where she posed for photos with her boyfriend, Ricky Alvarez. The film, which also stars Robert De Niro, Ben Stiller and Styler Gisondo, is set to hit theaters Nov. 25. Grande will then lend her voice to the upcoming animated adaptation of the Dr. Seuss book Oh, the Places You’ll Go!, from directors Jon M. Chu and Jill Culton, which is set to arrive in theaters March 17, 2028.

The 69th Annual Grammy Awards will take place Feb. 7 at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, with the music awards show airing live on ABC.

Raye leads this year’s Grammy nominations with 11 nods, followed by Rosalía with nine and Ella Langley, Olivia Rodrigo, Phoebe Bridgers and Miranda Lambert with seven nods apiece.
