NBC Celebrates 100th Anniversary With Amy Poehler, Jimmy Fallon and More Stars at Rainbow Room Soirée

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

NBC celebrated its 100th anniversary with a star-studded party at the Rainbow Room in Manhattan.

Amy Poehler, Jimmy Fallon, Andy Cohen, Hoda Kotb, Tracy Morgan, Al Roker and more celebs who work closely with the Peacock mingled in the luxury ballroom, located on the 65th floor of 30 Rock, where sushi, pasta and cocktails were served.

Wally Feresten, aka “Cue Card Wally,” who has worked at “Saturday Night Live” since 1990 and “Late Night With Seth Meyers” since 2014, was also in attendance, writing customized cue cards for party guests.

Top brass were also at the event, including Comcast co-CEO Mike Cavanagh and NBCUniversal execs Frances Berwick, Cesar Conde, Pearlena Igbokwe and more.

Raising a glass to the network’s history, Cavanagh said in a toast: “NBC has played a part in shaping American culture and society for 100 years. It’s an unbelievable legacy when you think about it. We’ve gone from radio to television, black and white to color, broadcast to streaming. All these technologies have changed. How we program has changed, and how we reach consumers and audiences is completely different than it once was. But what hasn’t changed is the strength of the people who make up our company, and their courage and willingness to take big swings. And that is a defining characteristic of NBC, and why I’m so proud to be a part of it. We look forward to an incredible next century.”

The cocktail party is one of many celebrations honoring NBC’s 100th anniversary. A primetime special will air live on NBC on Dec. 10, uniting legendary casts, talent and performers for musical performances, comedic tributes, and surprise reunions.
