KATSEYE Postpones Two More Shows Due to Illness Days After Delaying Detroit Concert

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

It's been a rocky week for the global girl group.

KATSEYE have postponed two more concerts.

On Thursday (Nov. 5), the official Instagram pages for Chicago’s United Center and Minneapolis’ Target Center shared statements announcing the postponement of the global girl group’s ongoing Wildworld Tour.

“We’re sorry to share that tonight’s KATSEYE show at the United Center has been postponed due to Megan’s illness,” read the United Center statement, which also confirmed that purchased tickets and VIP packages will be valid for the yet-to-be-announced rescheduled date. “Please do not travel to the venue, as doors will not open for tonight’s show.”

Around the same time, Target Center posted an identical statement — save for the guidance to not travel to the venue — regarding the group’s previously scheduled Nov. 7 show. On Tuesday (Nov. 3), the group postponed their show at Michigan’s Little Caesar’s arena, with sources at the show noting that KATSEYE did a VIP soundcheck without Megan present. At press time, none of the four touring KATSEYE members have posted on their personal Instagram profiles addressing the postponements. Manon Bannerman and Sophia Laforteza are currently still on hiatus.

KATSEYE’s Wildworld Tour commenced on Sept. 1 at Dublin’s 3Arena. The group completed a nine-date European leg before kicking off their North American leg on Oct. 13 at Miami’s Kaseya Center. When the group resumes their trek, KATSEYE will play arenas through the remainder of 2026, ending the year with two dates at Mexico City’s Palacio de los Deportes. After a two-month break, at the top of next year, the ladies will bring their tour to Asia and Australia for 14 more shows.

Promoted by Live Nation, the sprawling tour supports the group’s WILD EP, which debuted atop the Billboard 200 and spawned the Billboard Hot 100 top 40 hits “Animal” (No. 24), “Pinky Up” (No. 28) and “Hootie Frutti” (No. 31). WILD is the official follow-up to the group’s Beautiful Chaos EP, which soared to No. 4 on the Billboard 200 and earned the ladies two Grammy nominations: best new artist and best pop duo/group performance for “Gabriela.”
