Executive Assistant to the CEO at Supabase

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Supabase is the Postgres development platform, built by developers for developers. We provide a complete backend solution including Database, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions, Realtime, and Vector Search. All services are deeply integrated and designed for growth.

We’re looking for an Executive Assistant to support our CEO as Supabase continues to scale globally. This person will be a trusted partner to the CEO, helping manage time, communication, priorities, travel, follow-through, and key operating rhythms across a fast-moving, remote-first company.

This role must be based in an APJ / APAC timezone to provide strong timezone coverage for our CEO and leadership team. You’ll work closely with founders, executives, internal stakeholders, investors, and external partners, ensuring the CEO is prepared, focused, and able to spend time on the highest-impact work.

This role is ideal for someone who thrives in async, fast-paced environments, has strong judgment, and is excited about supporting a developer-focused company building tools that scale to millions.

Own the CEO’s calendar management, prioritization, and day-to-day operating rhythm.

Manage inbox triage and communications by monitoring emails, drafting responses, and keeping communication flowing across internal and external stakeholders.

Prepare briefings, agendas, decks, notes, and follow-up materials so the CEO is ready for every meeting.

Track follow-ups, decisions, and commitments to help projects move forward and keep stakeholders accountable.

Coordinate seamless domestic and international travel, logistics, scheduling changes, and last-minute adjustments.

Support investor meetings, board-related preparation, executive meetings, offsites, and company-wide moments.

Handle sensitive and confidential information with absolute discretion, professionalism, and integrity.

Anticipate needs, identify blockers early, and solve problems proactively before they escalate.

Partner across the company to create clarity, improve coordination, and help leadership operate effectively in a global, remote-first environment.

Have experience supporting a CEO, founder, or senior executive in a startup or scale-up environment.

Are based in an APJ / APAC timezone and can provide strong coverage for a globally distributed leadership team.

Communicate clearly across technical and non-technical audiences, with excellent written and verbal communication.

Have experience working in async, remote-first, or globally distributed teams.

Are tech savvy and comfortable using tools like Notion, Slack, Google Workspace, and AI-enabled workflows.

Bring strong judgment around prioritization, confidentiality, stakeholder management, and executive communication.

Are energized by organized chaos, fast-moving priorities, and solving problems before they become blockers.

Are comfortable navigating ambiguity, moving quickly, and staying several steps ahead.

We hire globally. We believe you can do your best work from anywhere. There are no Supabase offices, but we provide a WeWork membership or co-working allowance you can use anywhere in the world.

Every team member receives ESOP (equity ownership) in the company. We want everyone to share in the upside of what we’re building together.

Use this budget to set up your ideal work environment—laptop, monitor, headphones, or whatever helps you do your best work.

Supabase covers 100% of health insurance for employees and 80% for dependents, wherever you are. Your wellbeing and your family’s health are important to us.

Once a year, the entire company gathers in a new city for a week of connection, collaboration, and fun. It’s a highlight of our year.

We operate asynchronously and trust you to manage your own time. You know what needs to be done and when.

Every team member receives an annual education allowance to spend on learning—courses, books, conferences, or anything that supports your growth.

Supabase was born-remote and open-source-first. We believe our globally distributed team is our secret weapon in building tools developers love.

Over $1B raised (including our $500M Series F)

We move fast, build in public, and use what we ship. If it’s in your project, we probably use it in ours too. We believe deeply in the open-source ecosystem and strive to support—not replace—existing tools and communities.

New remote jobs delivered to your inbox, every week.

Location independence is the future of work. It serves companies with best talent from around the globe. And benefits employees with a greater work life balance and the ability to work in their pyjamas.- Nithur, founder.
