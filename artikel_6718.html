7 Celebrity-Approved Ways to Wear Flats With Jeans This Fall

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Stylish celebrities confirm that the timeless duo is also one of the coolest.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Today, the humble flat has become just as much of a wardrobe staple as a trusty pair of jeans. And styling them together? That’s practically a no-brainer. Everything from classic ballet flats and loafers to ornate Mary Janes looks especially sharp with structured, dark-wash denim and can instantly elevate baggy or light-wash jeans. With a seemingly infinite selection of chic designs in both categories, it’s an easy-to-repeat formula for all kinds of occasions: strolling through your city on the weekend, exploring a new one, heading into the office, or dressing for a night out.

Some of the most fashionable celebrities are making the combo look cooler than ever, which is why we’ve spotlighted a few of our favorite Hollywood-approved jeans-and-flats getups to inspire your upcoming outfits. From supermodels like Bella Hadid and Kendall Jenner to Zoë Kravitz, find seven impeccable flats-and-jeans ensembles worth copying this fall.

Offset simple black ballet flats and a matching cropped cardigan with a pair of no-waistband jeans, à la Zoë Kravitz. Topped off with a pear-cut tennis necklace, the look is the epitome of elegance with an edge.

A sleek leather loafer can work just as well outside the office with relaxed-fit straight-leg jeans and a patterned knit. Striped pullovers are trending this season, as are croc-embossed bags to top off the look. For a touch of elevated Y2K nostalgia, mirror Bella Hadid’s approach and add a grommeted belt in rich leather.

From bejeweled to feathered and embroidered, embellished shoes had a huge moment on fall 2026 runways. For milder fall days—and to wear again next spring—style a delicate, adorned sheer flat with a timeless, supermodel-worthy trio: a white button-down, bootcut jeans, and a timeless leather belt. Finish with a retro-inspired zigzag headband the It girls are also loving right now.

Designers, street style stars, and A-list celebrities agree: burgundy flats—a more versatile take on bright red styles—belong in your closet this autumn. During a recent press event for Verity, Dakota Johnson wore Khaite’s Jane glove flats with distressed light-wash jeans, a black top, and a cropped plaid Nili Lotan blazer—proving that, with the right styling, a jeans-and-flats combo can be an equally effortless alternative to a polished dress you might otherwise reach for. Reformation’s Blaine flat and A.L.C.’s Cass jacket offer somewhat more accessible ways to recreate Johnson’s look.

A mention of this season’s top flat trends wouldn’t be complete without ultra-popular jazz-inspired silhouettes. Eva Chen undoubtedly has a penchant for them, having been spotted wearing them with baggy jeans twice this year. Follow suit and style the pairing with a colorful sweater layered over a thin turtleneck for a look that can take you from lounging to a casual lunch.

Textured flats might actually be a bit groundbreaking for fall. Katie Holmes proves the traditionally summer-ready style can add eclectic flair to voluminous jeans and a chunky sweater, especially when teamed with thin crew socks for added warmth. Thrown over her shoulder? A spacious, sumptuous suede tote in a deep ink-blue shade.
