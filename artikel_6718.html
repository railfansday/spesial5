6 Stylish Ways to Wear a Blanket Coat This Fall and Winter

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Basically a blanket you can wear outside—and make it fashion.

Beating the cold has gotten that much easier with the rise of blanket coats. This winter, we’re staying bundled up, not only with stylish layering tricks like wraps, shawls, and capes, but by blending the two.

Blanket coats have become the cold-weather item fashion people are loving, and it was all over a lot of the fall/winter 2026 runways, too. So, it’s clear that the style is making the rounds, and there’s no need to wait for next winter to finally be tuned in. Blanket coats provide the coziness of at-home comfort, snuggled beneath a good quilt, to your everyday outfits.

And most serve a two-in-one purpose, also providing some neck protection, so you can opt out of scarves sometimes, or double up if you’re dealing with some really cold days. Below, I’ve gathered some stylish ways to lean into the popular blanket coat trend this fall and winter.

If you’re looking for something that feels a bit elevated, try a blanket coat similar to this style with draping that bunches and gathers flawlessly, giving an almost regal vibe.

If you’re not big on layering, let the coat do the layering for you. Leaning into a blanket jacket like this one that’s split open at the front makes it look like you’re wearing more pieces than you actually are.

The blanket coat serves as a literal wrap-around sweater that keeps you cozy and stylish. This black with brown trim one is perfect for your every day.

While some blanket coats wrap, others fall like trenches, but carry a bit thicker material like the one above. Pairing a simple neutral-colored piece with a monotone outfit is the easy way in.

The best part of a good blanket style coat is that it keeps you covered without having to deal with a zipper. If you want a more relaxed-fit coat then a style like this is great.

For those days when you wish you could carry around a big blanket with you, try instead pulling out your thickest coat that gives all the vibes.
