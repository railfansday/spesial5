Statement Scarves Are Fall’s Defining Accessory

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Puffed, twisted, and totally oversize: Scarves are taking over as new statements.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

The behemoth sculptural statement scarf is here, and it’s never looked better. Puffed, twisted, oversize, and opulent, these finishing pieces eclipse the coat and become a one-and-done look for winter weather. These are not your usual blanket scarves or faux fur creations. The new statement scarf is padded and sculpted, often done in fabrics like luscious leather. These XL scarves were seen on the fall/winter 2026 runway at Loewe, Celine, and Toteme. Elsewhere, Givenchy showcased the power of a smaller leather scarf done in brilliant cobalt blue. The main message with all of these outfit-defining scarves is that they do all the talking and create a strong visual impact with even the most simple of coats. Black leather jackets, neutral wool coats, basic blazers, and even beige trenches get a new look with a dramatic scarf. It’s no surprise the look is trending now. After season upon season of quiet luxury, we’re now firmly in the era of the statement coat.

But for those who don’t want to fully commit to the look, we have the statement scarf, which gives instant drama with ease. If you’re in need of styling inspiration, follow the runways’ lead. Keep it tonal and choose a scarf in the same color palette as your coat or jacket, or go the opposite way and pick up a primary hue like scarlet red or bright yellow to add contrast to a more subdued-hued jacket.

There’s also something beautifully haphazard about these pieces: On the runway, many of them are intentionally styled so that they look windswept or as if they were in motion and got frozen mid-moment. For designers like Michael Rider at Celine, that was part of it. The label’s fall/winter 2026 show notes presented the collection as “intuition over strategy. Feeling it rather than planning it,” and “contradiction, imperfection, character.” Adding to this, “I love when messy, complex, layered inner lives come through underneath great clothes,” Rider expressed in the show notes. “Thinking about people with style who wear beautiful clothes in a personal way.”

On a more practical level, the statement scarf adds a little bit of pizzazz with warmth. It acts as wearable sculpture. Tied tightly around the neck, styled with an over-the-top pair of sunglasses, it brings the focus to the face and creates an iconic look that is also strangely practical.

We’re all so bundled under layers in these cooler months that accessorizing (and wearing jewelry) can pose a challenge. Many of the big puffy scarves are editorial showpieces that may not have made it to the retail side of fashion, but the other side of statement scarves lies in embellishment. Think: embroidered wonders from Dries Van Noten, paillette triangle scarves from Zankov, or even just a classic scarf done with extra-long fringe. Just think of it as a warm version of a necklace.
