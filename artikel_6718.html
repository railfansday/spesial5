Drew Barrymore Winks to Her 2000s Style in a Black Newsboy Cap

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Anyone else thinking of that scene from Charlie’s Angels: Full Throttle?

Last night, the Museum of Modern Art held its annual film benefit in tandem with Chanel, which raises money for “the acquisition and preservation of key film works” in the MoMA’s movie department, with director Steven Spielberg serving as this year’s special honoree. Typically, the New York City event unites some of the starriest names in Hollywood, and the 2026 benefit was no exception, with attendees including the likes of Cate Blanchett, Zoe Saldaña, Emily Blunt, and Michelle Pfeiffer. But even at such an A-list event, one person managed to steal the show: Drew Barrymore.

The talk show host turned heads in a striking, Mod-inspired look, the star of which was a shaggy black-and-white jacket. Meanwhile, her choice of headwear injected the evening with a welcome dose of nostalgia.

Like most of the attendees, Barrymore dressed in Chanel for the occasion, pulling a statement piece of outerwear from the house’s Pre-Fall 2026 collection by Matthieu Blazy. The jacket featured a black-and-white windowpane pattern in a distorted tweed, with fringe tufts running all along the trim. Meanwhile, the lining featured a pattern of repeating faces in red and white, which only peeked out in brief moments.

Stylist Lee Harris left the jacket open to reveal a black turtleneck top and matching slacks underneath, while a long pearl necklace adorned with the house’s signature double-C pendant dangled in front.

However, it was Barrymore’s black newsboy cap that made the greatest statement. Over the years, the Emmy Award-winner has been a champion of all kinds of hat styles, from fedoras and beanies to baseball caps and berets. But you might recall that she went through a strong newsboy-cap phase in the early 2000s, especially when she was promoting the Charlie’s Angels films.

In fact, she event wears one during one of the most-memorable scenes in the sequel, Charlie’s Angels: Full Throttle (2003), when she and co-stars Cameron Diaz and Lucy Liu have an impromptu dance break to MC Hammer’s “U Can’t Touch This.” (You can revisit that incredible moment here.)

In honor of Barrymore, and in honor of cinema, it might just be about time for a rewatch.
