67 Unique Gifts for the Woman Who Has Everything

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Consider this your guide to shopping for the extremely chic women in your life.

Hello, sweet, lost soul that’s wandered here looking for some shopping help. Do you have a very cool person in your life whose general coolness makes it very difficult to shop for them? Perhaps an extremely chic and picky mother-in-law or a stylish work wife? Never fear, because as a longtime shopping editor, I’ve taken it upon myself to put together a list of the best gifts for women in 2026.

First things first: Think outside the box. I'm not saying a nice mug or pair of socks isn't a great gift, but I can almost guarantee you that the recipient has a couple of each that they already love. There should be something extra-unique about the gift, whether it's a new brand they haven't heard of or some sort of unique feature. Also, you've got to keep their personality in mind. That's a non-negotiable. Maybe they're really into luxurious showers, traveling in style, or decking out their handbags?

Look at you thinking ahead, you! Great question. To keep things short and sweet: It’s almost too late, but you can still beat the clock with a few methods. USPS recommends shipping by December 17 for its Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail services, December 18 for Priority Mail, and December 19 for Priority Mail Express. UPS and FedEx can get a package there in time for Christmas as long as you ship by December 23 at the very, very latest (and shell out some extra cash for overnight shipping). And then there’s always Amazon Prime: If you’re a member, you might be able to score two-day or even same-day shipping, depending on the item. But don’t wait!

Now if you're feeling stuck and crunched for time, below, you'll find 67 gift ideas for the woman who has everything. There's bound to be one (or two, or three) gifts that fits the bill.
