Shopping for men doesn’t have to be difficult. Even when he says he doesn’t want anything or shrugs and tells you to “surprise him,” we know (because this is what we do) that there are tons of thoughtful and really cool options out there, waiting to be discovered.

If you’re shopping for the holidays, stop wondering what to get him and look at this list already. Our gift guide for men spans everyone on your list, from the teen boy who’s into fitness and skincare to the hardworking dad who never treats himself to the polished guy who seems to already own everything worth owning.

While a gift card technically counts as a present, we think our picks are more memorable. Keep scrolling for unique, practical, and elevated ways to treat him, no matter the occasion.
