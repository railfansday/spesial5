Serious Choking Reports Spark Nationwide Recall Of Once Upon A Farm Toddler Bars

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The FDA is urging parents to stop serving the affected snacks immediately.

If Once Upon a Farm's Tractor Wheels are part of your toddler's regular snack rotation, it's time to take them off the menu. The company announced a voluntary recall of its Organic Toddler Soft-Baked Bars on October 30 after receiving three customer complaints involving choking.

The FDA is also urging parents not to buy or serve the snacks, citing several reports involving children between 18 months and 2½ years old, some of whom couldn't breathe after eating them.

The agency explained why these incidents have been especially dangerous, saying that “when chewed, the product reportedly forms a dry, sticky mass that standard choking interventions—including back blows and the Heimlich maneuver—may fail to dislodge." Some of the reported cases were serious enough that emergency personnel had to step in, with children also requiring monitoring at hospitals.

The recall includes three flavors: Apple, Sweet Potato & Spinach, Strawberry, Pumpkin & Beet, and Banana, Pumpkin & Cauliflower. No package sizes, production batches, or expiration dates are excluded, and the bars have been sold nationwide, including online and at retailers like Costco, Target, and Walmart.

In a statement, Once Upon a Farm explained its decision to remove the snacks from sale.

"We acted out of an abundance of caution, in the best interest of our customers and our commitment to quality. We are working with the U.S. Food & Drug Administration (FDA) to make sure affected products are removed and families have the information they need."

The company also acknowledged the seriousness of what happened.

"We know that parents place their trust in us every time they buy one of our products. We fell short of that, and that is why we are taking these aggressive actions."

No other Once Upon a Farm products are included in the recall. The FDA is telling families to discard any Tractor Wheels they have at home, while the company says customers can return unopened packages to the store where they purchased them for a full refund.
