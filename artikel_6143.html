<i>Combat</i> Took on America’s Socialism Fever

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The National Review newsletter captured a time in which revolutionary violence tried to join the mainstream.

Young men raise their fists and chant slogans during the “Days of Rage” demonstrations organized by the Weatherman to protest the Chicago Seven trial and “to bring the war home” in Chicago, Ill., in 1969.

Socialism looks in 2026 a lot like bell bottoms: tried on, worn out, and out of fashion.

Decades ago, Germany, Russia, Cambodia, Ethiopia, and points beyond tried socialism to disastrous results. Many American leftists, as judged by the election returns, believe in an American exceptionalism of sorts, too. They think that socialism in the United States will be different. We won’t need a world war, societal collapse, or the obligatory charitable single and accompanying global benefit show to save us from its consequences. Socialism, for its true believers, amounts to salvation and not that from which people living under it require deliverance.
