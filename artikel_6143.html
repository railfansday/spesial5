The Week: The Three Amigos Summit

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Plus: Ground stops, outages, and dirty bombs, oh my.

Russian President Vladimir Putin, Chinese President Xi Jinping, and U.S. President Donald Trump arrive for a trilateral meeting in Shenzen, China, November 19, 2026.

• We swear, it wasn’t our redesigned website that brought down email and the airlines.

• In an unprecedented trilateral summit in China this week, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin sat down with President Donald Trump. Ahead of the summit, Trump set no metrics for observers to judge its success. The summit cannot be said to have failed: It was not designed to achieve anything, and it didn’t. Of course, the president reportedly went into the diplomatic event of the year with some goals in mind. He reportedly sought commitments to nuclear disarmament from his Russian and Chinese counterparts, neither of whom had an incentive to agree to such limits. If Trump persuaded them to assist America’s efforts to isolate Iran economically, he has kept it a closely guarded secret. Putin sought to make the summit all about his war on Ukraine, but the Russian autocrat secured no meaningful promises on that front from Washington or Beijing. Only China, it seems, got what it wanted from the summit, which took place on its soil. The event allowed Xi to pretend to arbitrate the conflicts in which his great-power competitors are embroiled. The affair was not entirely unproductive, of course. It produced many joint statements of principle, which essentially state that all parties have agreed to keep talking.
