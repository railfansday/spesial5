GTA 6 is officially a record holder. With a truly funny-sounding 32.5 million copies sold pre-release driving $3.79 billion net sales as of launch day, November 19, Rockstar's latest open-world game is a caliber of commercial, critical, and cultural phenomenon never before seen, even beating out longtime record holders like... other Rockstar games.

Let's unpack that $3.79 billion figure. Sometimes numbers get so big that it can be hard to wrap your head around them. How does GTA 6 compare to other media juggernauts?

As we previously noted, 32.5 million copies sold before even launching sees GTA 6 eclipse Elden Ring's three-year sales of 30 million copies. Those pre-release sales also mean GTA 6 was, per the hastily updated Wikipedia entry for such records, one of the top 30 best-selling games of all time before it even launched.

What about GTA 5, the best-selling game of its kind? Well, way back in 2013, Grand Theft Auto 5 (as Variety noted at the time) earned $800 million in its first day. This was a record for Rockstar at the time.

As I reported in 2018 – holy crap, it's been a while – Red Dead Redemption 2 set the record for the biggest opening weekend with $725 million.

I'm gonna hand the microphone to Past Me for a moment, because I'm pretty sure he's correct: "That record comes with a small caveat: RDR2 released on a Friday and had the best opening weekend ever, not the best opening. The record for the best opening is still held by Rockstar's previous hit, GTA 5, which released on a Tuesday and sold $1 billion in its first three days."

In other words, GTA 6's launch is so big that, one day out of the gate, it has more than doubled the combined openings of GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2.

Here's a more recent comparison. Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey ended its box office run at $1.8 billion globally, making it one of the most profitable films ever. In one day, GTA 6 has more than doubled that.

Avengers: Endgame, meanwhile, ended a very long run around $2.88 billion. Again, after one day, GTA 6 is nearly $1 billion ahead.

Week-end figures from Rockstar will surely be even harder to swallow. There is nobody else in this weight class. By orders of magnitude, we are looking at the biggest entertainment release in history.
