Saliba has fight on his hands to usurp ‘unbelievable’ Konsa

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

From the moment Ezri Konsa pulled on an Arsenal shirt, he has moved with all the composure of a man who knows exactly where to be and when. The centre-back has required no adaptation period in north London, where he has filled the considerable shoes of William Saliba with such class that Arsenal have hardly missed the Frenchman at all.

The loss of Saliba to a long-term back injury was supposed to be a major problem for Arsenal. Instead, the situation has now reached a point where some fans are wondering how Saliba will get back into the side. That may prove a little extreme, but the fact there is even a discussion is proof of Konsa’s superb impact at the Emirates Stadium.

“Unbelievable,” Mikel Arteta, the Arsenal manager, said of his £55m signing. “He’s come to a huge club and to position yourself in that manner, and to perform at the level he is, it is very difficult to do. We are really lucky to have him.”

In Arsenal’s latest victory, their seventh from eight matches in the Premier League this season, Konsa offered all of his usual defensive security and calmness in possession. Even more excitingly, he also scored his first goal for the club, smashed in from close range after a corner had caused chaos in Everton’s penalty area. It was Arsenal’s first set-piece goal of the season.

“I have been waiting to score my first goal for a while,” Konsa said. “I spoke to the set-piece coach before the game and he told me he’s been waiting for my goal as well. We felt as players that it was coming, and today was the day.”

Some eyebrows were raised when Arsenal paid such a large fee to sign Konsa from Aston Villa. The Villa fans, though, were not among those who questioned the price or the player’s ability. They have known for years at Villa Park that Konsa is a player of the highest quality, and they will not be surprised by his exceptional start to his Arsenal career.

The same is true of the Arsenal players. Arteta asked the England internationals in his squad about Konsa before buying him, and the feedback was overwhelmingly positive. Konsa was already close friends with Declan Rice, Bukayo Saka and Eberechi Eze before completing his transfer from Villa.

Konsa’s start to life at Arsenal is all the more impressive because he did not even have a pre-season. He effectively came straight from the World Cup into the club, and has been starting games since early September. His first start was against Chelsea, when he endeared himself to the home fans with a crunching tackle on former team-mate Morgan Rogers.

Konsa, 29, has quickly built a strong relationship with Gabriel Magalhães, his defensive partner who scored Arsenal’s other goal here against Everton, from Ben White’s cross. For both centre-backs to find the net felt like a throwback to last year, when Arsenal were so dangerous from crosses, and underlined how Arteta’s team are capable of hurting their opponents in many different ways.

The nature of the goals might give a misleading impression of Arsenal’s performance, though. This was not a gritty win. Arteta’s side produced some excellent passing moves and circulated the ball with menace throughout the game. They were also a little open at times, especially in the first half when Everton created a handful of decent chances.

Thierno Barry missed the best of those opportunities, with the score at 1-0. It was the closest the visitors came to hurting the champions, aside from some of James Tarkowski’s full-blooded tackles. “I felt we didn’t really get to a level that was good enough to get near them,” admitted David Moyes, the Everton manager.

The victory, combined with Manchester City’s defeat earlier in the day, put Arsenal level on points at the top of the table. Aside from the extraordinary thrashing at Brighton, Arteta’s side have looked just as solid, if not more so, than last season. That speaks volumes of the quality in the team, of course, but also says plenty about the way Konsa has adjusted to his new surroundings. Thanks to him, Saliba’s absence has not hurt at all.

“Great feeling, it always is. A clean sheet, a goal – my first goal for the club – so a fantastic day overall.

“I’ve been waiting to score my first goal for a while. I spoke to the set piece coach before the game and he told me he’s been waiting for my goal as well, so it was only right that I got the goal today.

“I’ve come to this stadium for many seasons, I know how tough it is to play against us. It’s not surprising to me.

“We are champions and to be champions you’ve got to win games consistently. This is our home and we want to win as many games here as possible.”

All over at the Emirates and the champions go level on points with Manchester City at the top of the table.

They kept another clean sheet and scored from a set-piece again. All is right in Arsenal’s world!

One of the clearest signs a football club is in a good place is when the debate switches from whether the team are winning to how they are winning.

This is currently Arsenal and Mikel Arteta’s world. For years, Arsenal coaches and players were asked how and when they would start collecting major trophies again. With no concern about their capacity to challenge for top honours over the coming years, the style has become the main focus.

It’s a welcome “problem” – or some might argue a non-existent one – for Arteta while his side is so strong. Building a fortress is the expensive, time-consuming and difficult part. Once it’s constructed and established, how you decorate it is a matter of taste.

Whether neutrals will hold the current champions in the same regard as the great Arsène Wenger sides will be a trivial concern if Arteta does what no Arsenal manager has since 1935 – defend the league title – and then add a few more, which is a serious possibility based on the balance of power in England and the likely punishment heading Manchester City’s way.

Today there will be tributes to Ken Friar, who passed away at the age of 92 earlier this year. Advertising boards outside the Emirates Stadium have been taken over by images of Friar, who had a remarkable 70-year association with the club.

In today’s programme, captain Martin Odegaard has written the following: “It’s exceptional people like Ken Friar who have built the club into what it is today, and we as the current team are the ones privileged to continue his legacy.”

Hello and welcome to coverage from the Emirates as Premier League champions Arsenal host Everton.

Mikel Arteta’s men produced a good performance in midweek to draw with Bayern Munich in the Champions League while David Moyes’s squad have had the week off following their 2-0 defeat by Chelsea last weekend.

Arteta said: “We’re having three games in six days so we know what’s happening, especially when the games are so demanding and the necessity is to win. We know that, so we prepare the players for those scenarios and do the best we possibly can.

“I know David so well and how he sets the team, the quality they have in their organisation and individually. It’s no coincidence they didn’t lose before their last game. They have a really good team, which we know because in recent clashes, it’s always been a really difficult game for us.”

Both teams enter this game having been beaten just once this season but eight points separate them with Arsenal in second and Everton in eighth.

Moyes said: “It’s not an easy game and never has been over many years. We look forward to it; they had a game in midweek, and we have to try and capitalise on that. I thought we did really well last year against them; we played really well down at Arsenal, and I hope we can do the same.

“When I look back at the team we had out, we were pretty threadbare at that time, but we did a good job, and I hope we can do it again. Obviously Arsenal have some many different tools in their bag and people they can use in different positions, so we’re fully aware of the team we’re up against and the level.”

Former Arsenal players Ainsley Maitland-Niles and Christian Norgaard could feature for Everton this afternoon.
