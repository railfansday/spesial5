Jensen Huang offers to return as Nvidia CEO if the board asks, CNBC reports

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

In a statement obtained by CNBC, Jensen Huang apologised to Nvidia’s board for the problems he caused. The board is not considering his return and is searching for a new CEO.

NVIDIA founder and CEO Jensen Huang during his GTC 2026 keynote at the SAP Center in San Jose, 16 March 2026

Jensen Huang says he would return as Nvidia’s chief executive if the board decides it wants him back. He made the offer in a written statement obtained by David Faber of CNBC on Tuesday. It is his first public comment since he resigned as chief executive on 12 November. CNBC’s Kif Leswing published the statement in full.

“If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO,” Huang said.

The statement landed hours before Nvidia reports third-quarter results, its first without him.

Huang apologised to Nvidia’s directors and said he would cooperate with the board’s independent investigation.

“I did not live up to the standards expected of everyone at NVIDIA, and I have made serious mistakes in my personal life. I created problems for the Board that I deeply regret,” he said.

He said he had seen an outpouring of calls for his return from Nvidia staff, investors, business partners and customers. Many still contact him directly, he said. An open letter asking the board to reinstate him now has more than 180,000 signatures, CNBC reported. President Donald Trump said on Monday that he would not mind Huang coming back.

Huang also wrote about his own story. He came to the US 53 years ago. He called becoming an American citizen one of the proudest moments of his life. He described Nvidia as his life’s work.

Huang resigned days after admitting to an affair, CNBC reported. News reports alleged affairs with Nvidia employees and with Alice Huang, who worked for a customer and is alleged to have contact with Chinese intelligence.

Nvidia’s board is not considering bringing Huang back, CNBC reported on Monday. When he resigned, the board started a formal search for a permanent chief executive, according to Nvidia’s announcement. It will look at internal and external candidates. Colette Kress, the chief financial officer, is interim CEO. The board said on Friday it was choosing a search firm, CNBC reported.

Nvidia has hired the law firm Wachtell Lipton to lead its investigation, according to CNBC. The probe covers Huang’s conduct and whether it compromised national security or company secrets.

Huang has separately agreed to answer questions from three House committees on 1 December.
