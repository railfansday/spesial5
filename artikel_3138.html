Is This Cryptocurrency a Potential Millionaire Maker?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Hyperliquid has been skyrocketing in value, but does it have the long-term staying power required to mint new millionaires?

By Dominic Basulto – Nov 22, 2026 at 4:00AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Hyperliquid ›

One of the few bright spots in the crypto market has been Hyperliquid (HYPE +2.23%), up 140% for the year. Since its launch in late 2024, the HYPE token is up a remarkable 1,800%.

But can Hyperliquid hold on to these gains and surge even higher in the years ahead? If so, it could be a millionaire maker. But before you start shopping for luxury yachts and high-end vacation homes, here's what you need to know about Hyperliquid.

First and foremost, Hyperliquid still faces tremendous regulatory obstacles. That's because Hyperliquid is a decentralized crypto exchange based offshore, and it doesn't yet have regulatory permission to operate in the U.S. or to offer its services to U.S. retail investors. If you try to trade on the Hyperliquid platform, you will immediately get a red warning message that you're in a restricted jurisdiction.

Granted, President Donald Trump has given regulators the green light to bring Hyperliquid to the U.S. That's a huge positive. But the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) recently warned that Hyperliquid would be treated just like any other entity. Until it receives formal regulatory approval, Hyperliquid will need to partner with a registered exchange to offer financial derivatives to U.S. retail investors.

Meanwhile, the number of participants lining up to offer perpetual futures -- the financial product that led to Hyperliquid's meteoric rise -- seems to increase by the day. That's because the CFTC gave the go-ahead for crypto perps in May and for equity perps in November, which has dramatically changed the competitive landscape.

It's not just decentralized exchanges such as Hyperliquid that are potential rivals. Centralized crypto exchanges such as Coinbase Global (COIN -6.34%) and trading platforms like Robinhood Markets (HOOD -9.44%) are also potential rivals. So are prediction markets such as Kalshi and Polymarket. And what if legacy Wall Street financial exchanges decide to get into the act?

You get the idea. Competition could be off the charts, and that means Hyperliquid may start to account for a shrinking share of trading in perpetual futures. If that's the case, there's no way that it can grow at triple-digit percentage rates in coming years.

And here's the really scary fact: Hyperliquid is now down 37% from its all-time high of $97.98 in September. If roughly $100 were the top for HYPE, it wouldn't surprise me at all. In the crypto market, it's normal for highly volatile cryptos to skyrocket in price one year, only to collapse the next.

All of this is to say: Don't believe the hype about HYPE.

Given that almost all cryptocurrencies are down this year, it's easy to suspend disbelief and think that Hyperliquid might be a magic ticket to millionaire status. But I just don't think it has the staying power required to mint new millionaires. Hyperliquid might be beating the pants off Bitcoin (BTC -0.08%) this year, but I'd still rather buy Bitcoin.
