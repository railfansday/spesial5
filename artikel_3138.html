MDU Resources (MDU) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Net income slipped, but full-year guidance held as data center demand climbed.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

MDU Resources Group, Inc. (MDU -6.51%) reported a decrease in quarterly net income to $14 million while reaffirming its full-year earnings guidance. Management noted that the company reached a positive final investment decision for the Bakken East Pipeline, a multibillion-dollar project designed to transport natural gas across North Dakota. The utility operations saw growth in sales volumes, particularly within the electric segment serving data center loads. The company stated it is following a disciplined capital allocation strategy, which included the decision to exit a renewable natural gas project in Oregon due to shifting regulatory requirements and rising costs.
