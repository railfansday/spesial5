Lions owner/chair emeritus Martha Firestone Ford passes at 101

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Martha Firestone Ford, the owner and chair emeritus of the Detroit Lions, has died. She was 101.

The Lions announced her passing on Tuesday night.

Her husband, William Clay Ford, bought the Lions in 1963. When he died in 2014, Martha Firestone Ford became the owner of the team. In 2020, she relinquished control of the team to her daughter, Sheila Ford Hamp.

Mrs. Ford is survived by her four children, her 14 grandchildren, and 17 great-grandchildren.

Born in Akron, Ohio, Mrs. Ford was the granddaughter of Harvey S. Firestone, founder of the Firestone Tire and Rubber Company.

We extend our condolences to Mrs. Ford’s family, friends, and the entire Lions organization.
