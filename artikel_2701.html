Jaylen Robinson scores 21, hits go-ahead jumper in OT to lift Sacred Heart over New Hampshire 81-76

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

DURHAM, N.H. (AP) — Jaylen Robinson had 21 points and hit a go-ahead jumper in the final minute of overtime and Sacred Heart beat New Hampshire 81-76 on Sunday.

Robinson shot 7 of 9 from the field and 7 for 10 from the line for the Pioneers (2-1). Griffin Barrouk scored 15 points and added 11 rebounds. Jojo Wallace had 14 points.

Ryan Zan led the Wildcats (2-1) in scoring, finishing with 17 points, six rebounds and two steals. New Hampshire also got 17 points, five assists and two steals from Tyler Bike. Erik Oliver-Bush finished with 15 points.

Ryan Zan hit a 3-pointer for New Hampshire with 23 seconds left to force OT tied at 73. Robinson hit a jumper with 47 seconds left to put the Wildcats ahead 78-76.
