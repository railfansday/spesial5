Is Taylor Swift at the Chiefs game? Status for Thanksgiving vs. Bills

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Countless families will gather around the dinner table this Thanksgiving. Others will celebrate in the parking lot at an NFL stadium. Travis Kelce and the Kansas City Chiefs will be working this holiday, meaning those traditions will take on a different look this year.

And that family also includes Taylor Swift, who has been a fixture at Chiefs games all season long – at least at home. It's unclear whether the pop star will make the trip on the road to join her husband, Kelce, to celebrate Thanksgiving in Orchard Park, New York.

One thing is certain though. All eyes, or at least the cameras, will be pointed on the suite level at the new Highmark Stadium when the Chiefs battle the Buffalo Bills to see if Swift is in attendance.

Kansas City and Buffalo are set to renew their rivalry with the top spot in the AFC on the line. Will Swift be there to see the action in person?

Swift's attendance for the Chiefs' Thanksiving game hasn't been confirmed.

The pop star has only been in the building for Kansas City's home games this season, occupying her usual suite at Arrowhead Stadium. Plenty of big name celebrities have joined her to watch the Chiefs as well, ranging from Caitlin Clark to Tom Cruise and everyone in between.

Kelce's performance on Thanksgiving might rely on Swift's attendance, considering the tight end has played much better at home than on the road in 2026. He is coming off his best game of the season as well, scoring two touchdowns in Week 11 against the Arizona Cardinals.
