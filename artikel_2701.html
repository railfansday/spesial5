Where LSU WR Jackson Harris ranks among SEC receivers in 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

LSU loaded up on wide receivers in the transfer portal after head coach Lane Kiffin arrived. Jackson Harris emerged as the Tigers' top pass catcher and will finish as one of the best in the SEC.

Harris, a redshirt junior from Berkeley, CA, transferred to LSU after one year at Hawaii. He played his first two seasons at Stanford before breaking out with the Rainbow Warriors. Last year, he caught 49 passes for 962 yards and 12 touchdowns en route to earning first-team All-Mountain West honors.

In his first year of SEC football, Harris ranks among the conference's leaders in multiple statistical categories. His 871 total receiving yards rank fourth while his three 100-yard games are tied for fourth-most. Vanderbilt's Junior Sherrill, Missouri's Cayden Lee and Mississippi State's Anthony Evans rank ahead of or alongside him in both categories.

Harris' nine receiving touchdowns are tied for second with Mississippi State's Sanfrisco Magee. He first found the end zone in Week 4 against Texas A&M, scoring twice. Since then, he has tacked on three more multi-touchdown games.

Harris' 79.2 yards per game rank fifth, and his 57 total receptions are tied for seventh. He led the SEC in receiving yards during Week 10 when the Tigers faced Alabama. Against the Crimson Tide, Harris finished with 11 receptions for 166 yards.

Among 40 SEC wide receivers with at least 200 routes run, Harris ranks second in PFF offensive grade (84.4) and receiving grade (85.2), trailing only Missouri's Cayden Lee in both. He is fourth in yards per route run (2.61), second in first downs (43) and first in contested catch rate, hauling in 13 of 17 contested targets (76.5%).
