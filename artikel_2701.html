Texans CB Kamari Lassiter downgraded to a non-participant Tuesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Texans’ injury report on Tuesday had only one change from the estimated report on Monday.

Cornerback Kamari Lassiter (wrist) did not practice after being listed as a full participant a day earlier.

Lassiter has played all but three snaps this season, playing 2,361 plays this season.

The Texans listed wide receiver Marvin Harrison Jr. (hamstring), defensive end Danielle Hunter (hamstring), tight end Brevin Jordan (foot/illness) and running back Woody Marks (concussion) as non-participants again.

Linebacker Jake Hummel (groin) remained limited.

Running back David Montgomery (groin), safety Reed Blankenship (hand), wide receiver Tank Dell (hand/shoulder), offensive tackle Blake Fisher (thumb), offensive guard Febechi Nwaiwu (knee), offensive lineman Keylan Rutledge (shoulder), cornerback Jaylin Smith (hamstring) and offensive guard Wyatt Teller (back) were full participants a second consecutive day.

Montgomery is on track to return after missing the past four games.
