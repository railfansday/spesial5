Teng scores a career-high 24 in No. 2 Michigan State's victory over Detroit Mercy

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

EAST LANSING, Mich. (AP) — Kur Teng scored a career-high 24 points off the bench and Jeremy Fears Jr. had 17 points and 14 assists Saturday as five Spartans players scored in double figures in No. 2 Michigan State's 91-70 victory over Detroit Mercy.

Kaleb Glenn and Jasiah Jervis each contributed 11 points for the Spartans (5-1) and Cam Ward had 10 points and a career-high 12 rebounds. Michigan State bounced back from a 9-for-50 shooting performance Tuesday at Tennessee by making 29 of 55 shots from the field.

The Spartans were without starters Jordan Scott (shoulder) and Coen Carr (knee), with coach Tom Izzo electing to rest both players despite medical clearance.

Lance Stone scored 19 points and Tyler Spratt added 17 for the Titans (0-5). Detroit Mercy has lost its first five games for the first time since the 2023-24 season, when the Titans finished 1-31.

The Titans came out hot at the start of the second half, hitting 10 of their first 14 shots, to pull within 59-56 with 9:28 left. The Spartans responded with a 15-3 run to take control of the game and later built their lead to as many as 23 points. Teng had three of his five 3-pointers during the spurt.

PHILADELPHIA (AP) — Tyler Perkins had 23 points and Jake Fiegan hit four 3-pointers and scored 15 to help Villanova close out a much-needed win over Michigan.

Elijah Crawford added 13 points for the Wildcats (4-2) and Luigi Suigo, the 7-foot-3 freshman dubbed the Italian Wemby, had nine points and six rebounds.

Trey McKenney scored 22 for Michigan (3-2). Elliot Cadeau, the Most Outstanding Player at the Final Four last season, missed all six of his first-half shots but finished with 11 points.

Villanova topped Michigan in the 2018 national championship game and the banner to celebrate that title was raised in the arena rafters. The Wildcats are far removed from those glory years and missed three straight NCAA Tournaments before coach Kevin Willard got them back in the bracket last season.

BATON ROUGE, La. (AP) — Donovan Dent led six players in double-figure scoring for LSU in a victory over Queens at the Pete Maravich Assembly Center.

Dent finished with 20 points, 10 assists and six steals.

LSU (6-0) was playing without guard/forward RJ Luis Jr. The former St. John's star in the 2024-25 season, who scored 11 points last week for the Tigers in a victory over Louisiana-Monroe, was removed from the team the next day by LSU president Wade Rousse.

Luis had signed two NBA contracts in 2025, though he did not play. His LSU career lasted one game.

Center Brice Dessert added 19 points for LSU, forward Michael Ruzic scored 15, guard Skyy Clark had 14, including 4-of-6 shooting from 3-point range, guard Abdi Bashir had 12 and forward Mo Dioubate had 11.

Nasir Mann led Queens (3-3) with 19 points.

LSU led 52-40 at the half as Ruzic hit a 3-pointer with 31 seconds remaining and then two free throws with two seconds left to finish the period with 11 points. Dent also had 11 points in the first half with five assists.
