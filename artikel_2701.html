Preview: Wizards host Grizzlies

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

When: Sunday, November 16th at 7:00 p.m. ET

How to watch: Monumental Sports Network, League Pass

Where: Capital One Arena, Washington, D.C.

Wizards: Jamir Watkins – Out (Knee), Tristan Vukcevic – Out (Hip), Reece Beekman – Out (Shoulder), Justin Champagnie – Out (Foot), Tre Johnson – Out (Back)

Grizzlies: GG Jackson – Out (Foot), Isaiah Stewart – Game Time Decision (Illness), Ty Jerome – Out (Hip), Quinten Post – Game Time Decision (Concussion), Zach Edey – Game Time Decision (Ankle)

The Wizards host the Memphis Grizzlies on Monday night that will feature a lottery pick matchup between AJ Dybantsa and Cameron Boozer, and an opportunity for Washington to win its first game since Nov. 2.

Washington is playing the second half of its back-to-back after being blown out by the Houston Rockets on Sunday night — adding to its seven game losing streak.

Alex Sarr has seemed to find his shot over the past two outings against the Detroit Pistons and the Rockets, averaging 27.5 points, nine rebounds, two assists and two blocks during that period while shooting 77.45% from the field.

Now seven games into their skid, the Wizards rank 28th in three-point percentage (31.3%), 22nd in rebounds per game (42.5), 23rd in points per game (110.2) and 28th in assists per game (23.9) — despite Trae Young ranking fourth in the league in assists per game (8.7).

Washington hosts a 9-4 Grizzlies squad that is undefeated on the road (6-0).
