Suns vs. Pelicans injury report: Will Zion Williamson, Trey Murphy III play?

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Suns vs. Pelicans injury report: Will Zion Williamson, Trey Murphy III play? appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The New Orleans Pelicans could receive a significant offensive boost as they prepare to host the Phoenix Suns on Monday, November 23. Zion Williamson and Trey Murphy III are both listed as probable, putting them in a good position to return after missing Saturday's matchup against the San Antonio Spurs.

According to the NBA injury report, Williamson is dealing with a left thigh contusion, while Murphy is managing bilateral patellar tendinopathy. Both players were held out of the second game of New Orleans' back-to-back schedule. With Sunday providing an opportunity to rest, their probable designations suggest they are trending toward playing against Phoenix.

Williamson's potential return is particularly important for a Pelicans team looking to strengthen its offensive production. Murphy also provides valuable scoring and perimeter shooting, making their availability a welcome development for New Orleans.

The Pelicans could also have Bryce McGowens available after he received a probable designation due to a left elbow infection. However, Dejounte Murray (left ankle sprain), Bennedict Mathurin (left plantar fasciitis) and Trendon Watford (right ankle sprain) remain sidelined. Malik Dia and Julian Reese are questionable, while Jaron Pierre Jr. is probable. All three carry G League two-way designations.

Phoenix has a shorter injury list heading into Monday's contest. Ryan Dunn is questionable with right quad soreness, while Mark Williams remains out following left shoulder surgery.

For New Orleans, having Zion Williamson and Trey Murphy III back in the lineup would provide a significant boost. Williamson remains one of the Pelicans' primary scoring options with his ability to attack the basket and create offense inside, while Murphy adds perimeter shooting and versatility. Their presence could be crucial as New Orleans looks to secure a home victory against the Phoenix Suns.
