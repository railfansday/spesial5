Micah Parsons gets first sack of season, Packers and Rams tied 13-13 at halftime

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Packers defensive end Micah Parsons notched his first sack in nearly a year just before halftime in Los Angeles on Wednesday night.

Parsons made his return from a torn ACL against the Rams and had a couple of pressures early in the game. He got home for a sack of Matthew Stafford on the Rams’ first play after a Trey Smack field goal tied the score at 13-13 with 1:50 to play in the half.

The Rams ran out the clock from there and they will get the ball to start the second half at SoFi Stadium.

Parsons’s sack was part of an eventful final couple of minutes. Rams safety Kamren Kinchens was ejected for a hit to the head of Packers wide receiver Christian Watson and Watson is now being evaluated for a concussion. It would be a big loss for the Packers as Watson has four catches for 65 yards and a touchdown so far on Wednesday night.

Running back Kyren Williams scored the Rams’ only touchdown, but their offense slowed down after picking up points on each of their first three possessions. Stafford is 12-of-15 for 98 yards while Williams and Blake Corum have 35 yards on nine carries.

Packers quarterback Jordan Love is 12-of-18 for 154 yards. The Packers will be hoping for right guard Kevin Zeitler’s return from an ankle injury while waiting for more word on Watson and the Rams called tight end Davis Allen to return from an ankle injury.
