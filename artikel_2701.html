Watch every sack from Kansas City Chiefs' Week 9 win vs. New York Jets

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Kansas City Chiefs' pass rush put together an impressive performance in the team's victory over the New York Jets at Arrowhead Stadium.

After an early-season sack drought spoiled an otherwise respectable start to the Chiefs' defense's 2026 campaign, all of Kansas City's stars seemed to come alive in Week 9.

When the final whistle blew at the end of the fourth quarter, the Chiefs had tallied five sacks and countless pressures, bringing an end to the lackluster production that had earned Kansas City's front four significant criticism.

Super defensive linemen Chris Jones and George Karlaftis both sacked Jets quarterback Geno Smith twice, while second-year edge rusher Ashton Gillotte got home on the journeyman signal-caller once.
