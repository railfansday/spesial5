Rich Paul Debates LeBron James’ Impact on Lakers’ 2026-27 Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Support Local Media - Click to Add LA Sports Report as a Preferred Source on Google

The new-look Los Angeles Lakers are off to a flying start without LeBron James, and Max Kellerman argued the NBA's All-Time leading scorer's departure lit a fire under their current stars.

James left the Lakers in the offseason, joining the Philadelphia 76ers and headlining a busy offseason for the Eastern Conference squad. So far, though, the Lakers don't appear to be missing him. They currently have a 6-2 record on the year, and Luka Doncic leads the entire NBA in scoring with 38.3 points per game as well as 8.0 rebounds and 8.1 assists so far on the season.

"I think Luka was very motivated once LeBron got moved," Kellerman said. "…The whole was not greater than some of its parts with those two on the floor. Part of that happens when a guy needs the ball. The specter of LeBron, and Luka trying to do the right thing, LeBron trying to do the right thing, created a confusion on the team."

In response, James' agent Rich Paul argued the Lakers' start had nothing to do with LeBron leaving.

"What does LeBron have to do with it? LeBron doesn't have anything to do with that," Paul said. "…I think as media, we have to get out of the habit of mentioning LeBron. That man doesn't have anything to do with this year."

While the Lakers certainly look good thus far without James on their roster, comparing this year's Lakers with last year's team isn't exactly fair.

To start, the Lakers didn't just lose James and roll out the same team. They made several moves during the offseason to strengthen weak areas on their roster and fill the hole James left.

Walker Kessler joined the team in the offseason, filling the hole the Lakers had at center seemingly since the departure of Anthony Davis, and he is off to a great start with 14.4 points and 10.5 rebounds per game. Alongside Kessler, the Lakers also added defensive pieces like Ziaire Williams and Matisse Thybulle after struggling defensively last season.

Additionally, the Lakers have only played eight games so far this season, which is far too small of a sample size to compare to their full season with both Doncic and James last year. It's far too soon to compare the two Lakers teams, and their success will be graded by their future rather than their present.
