Second-Half Mistakes Cost Purdue, Losing 65 to 56 to DePaul

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Purdue's trip to the Fort Myers Tip-Off got off to a disappointing start Tuesday night, as the Boilermakers watched a halftime lead slip away in a 65-56 loss to the DePaul Blue Demons at Suncoast Credit Union Arena. After entering the matchup looking to build on an impressive 95-49 win over Oakland, Purdue struggled to find its offensive rhythm in the second half and suffered its third loss of the season.

The Boilermakers appeared to be in a good position heading into the locker room, taking a narrow 33-32 advantage after the opening 20 minutes. Purdue had controlled much of the rebounding battle and found enough offense to maintain the lead. However, whatever momentum Matt Painter's squad carried into halftime quickly disappeared as DePaul began to take control after the break.

The second half was a completely different story. DePaul outscored Purdue 33-23 over the final 20 minutes, taking advantage of a Boilermaker offense that simply could not get anything going. Purdue finished the night shooting just 35 percent from the field and an especially disappointing 2-of-15 from beyond the three-point line. The lack of perimeter shooting made it difficult for the Boilermakers to create offensive opportunities, particularly as DePaul continued to build its advantage.

One of the more frustrating aspects of the loss was that Purdue actually won the rebounding battle, pulling down 36 rebounds compared to just 27 for DePaul. The Boilermakers also collected 16 offensive rebounds, providing plenty of second-chance opportunities. Unfortunately, those extra possessions rarely translated into points. Purdue finished with just four assists as a team, another indication of how stagnant the offense became throughout the evening.

Defensively, Purdue had trouble slowing down a DePaul team that entered the matchup undefeated. The Blue Demons shot 49 percent from the field and did a much better job converting their opportunities. DePaul also took better care of the basketball, committing just eight turnovers compared to Purdue's 12. Gicarri Harris led the Boilermakers with 11 points, but Purdue struggled to find consistent scoring help as the game progressed.

This is a disappointing result for a Purdue team that appeared to be finding its footing following consecutive victories over Ohio, Lipscomb, and Oakland. The Boilermakers now fall to 4-3 on the season, while DePaul improves to 6-0. Purdue will have little time to dwell on Tuesday night's performance, as another opportunity awaits Thursday afternoon against Oklahoma in the Fort Myers Tip-Off. If the Boilermakers want to get back on track, finding some offensive consistency and getting their perimeter shooting going will need to be priorities.
