2026 Minnesota Golden Gophers in the NFL: Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Injuries and scratches continue to add up, but once again a stellar pair of defensive performances were recorded this time by Boye Mafe and Justin Walley. Hopefully the rest of the former Gophers can pick it up this week in Week 11. Below see just how the firmer Gophers faired in Week 10.

Gibbons played 36 defensive snaps and five on special teams in the Cardinals loss to the Rams. He recorded two tackles, one of them solo. He was targeted three times in the passing game and did not allow a reception.

Bateman led the Ravens wide outs with 63 offensive snaps. He was targeted five times and caught three passes for 42 yards.

Mafe played 42 defensive snaps in the win over the Steelers. He had three tackles, one of them solo, one for a loss and a QB sack of Aaron Rodgers. He added two more QB hits as well.

Span-Ford played 55 snaps on offense and just 2 on special teams as Dallas lost in a shootout to the 49ers. He was targeted three times and made one catch for 26 yards.

St. Juste played 12 special teams snaps in the loss to the Vikings. He did not record a stat.

Ersery played all 67 offensive snaps and four more on special teams in the loss to the Browns.

Walley returned to action and played 54 defensive snaps and six special teams snaps in the win. He had a great game making five tackles, three of them solo, two for loss, one a strip sack with a forced fumble and added a pair of pass defenses.

Faalele played eight special teams snaps.

Striggow played just two defensive snaps and six special teams snaps in the win. He recorded one solo tackle.

Lindenberg played 22 special teams snaps in the loss to Seattle. He did not record a stat.

Howden played 29 defensive snaps and 22 on special teams in the Saints loss to Carolina. He had three tackles, two of them solo.

Schmitz did not play a snap in the Giants loss.

Nubin played one defensive snap and 18 on special teams. He recorded one tackle.

The Steelers released Smith on Saturday. He is currently a free agent.

Eastern remained on the practice squad and did not play.

Kallerup played 26 offensive snaps and ten on special teams before leaving in the fourth quarter with what appeared to be an ankle injury. He did not record a stat.
