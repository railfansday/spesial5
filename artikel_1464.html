Lookman keen on leaving Atletico, Roma at the window

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Roma is looking for a dynamic winger who can bring pace and power to the Giallorossi’s attack.

Gian Piero Gasperini has plenty of quality up front with Dybala, Soulé, Mora, and Pellegrini, but lacks physical strength; he doesn’t have a Gervinho-like player.

This is why the club is monitoring the future of Ademola Lookman, currently playing for Atletico Madrid.

Lookman’s potential transfer to the capital would allow the Roma manager to reunite with one of the key players from his time at Atalanta.

Under the guidance of the general from Grugliasco, the Nigerian striker achieved particularly high levels of performance, establishing himself as one of the most effective wingers in Europe.

Ademola Lookman has played 735 minutes of football this season, scoring two goals and providing one assist for Atlético Madrid. His performances are significantly different from those he had in Bergamo under Gasperini.

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, the 1997-born player is dissatisfied with his experience with the Colchoneros, which is why his agent has announced he would like to leave Simeone’s side. This is a situation Roma is closely monitoring, although it remains a difficult move to finalize in January.
