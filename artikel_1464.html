Can you name every Barry in Premier League history?

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

We’ve devised some niche quizzes in our time, but this might be a new high. Can you name every Barry in Premier League history?

There are household names, cult favourites and some very forgotten footballers in the mix. We’ve listed their clubs and appearance totals to help you out.

One rule: they must have made a Premier League appearance. Apologies to the likes of Barry Douglas and Louie Barry, who’ve been part of squads but never made it onto the pitch. There are eight to get in total.

This would be a bit easy with our regular quiz rules, where simply typing ‘Barry’ would get you 100%. So this time around you need to get their surname if Barry is their first name, and their first name if Barry is their surname.
