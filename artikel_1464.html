Svilar expected to recover in time for Parma clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Svilar is back in his gloves and boots. And that’s already good news for Roma.

After more than a month out, the Belgian goalkeeper has finally abandoned his brace and returned to training at Trigoria.

The stress fracture to his right toe, sustained on October 2nd during a routine save in training, now appears to be behind him. His return to goal is getting closer.

As La Gazzetta dello Sport reports, Svilar has resumed individual training on the pitch, something he hasn’t done since his injury.

His rehabilitation program, undertaken in Belgium and Italy, has yielded encouraging results, and he could even return to the squad in the coming days. Parma, Roma’s opponents when the league resumes on November 21st, are in their sights.

The Belgian’s return would be important news for Gian Piero Gasperini, who has been forced to do without his starting goalkeeper for several weeks.

The first training session on the pitch represents a significant step, although it will take a few more days to determine when Svilar will be able to return to regular training with his teammates.

The international break comes at the right time to complete his recovery without rushing his schedule.

The Tardini remains the target, and the upcoming sessions at Trigoria will be crucial for assessing the goalkeeper’s condition. The hope is that Gasperini will be able to rejoin Svilar when the season resumes.
