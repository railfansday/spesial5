Sabrina Carpenter Swaps Espresso for Whisky in Damien Chazelle’s Johnnie Walker Ad

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Sabrina Carpenter makes her rounds on the dance floor with a drink, a wink, and a clink in the latest ad in Johnnie Walker’s “Keep Walking” campaign.

The fun, flirty, and festive ad dropped yesterday as part of Johnnie Walker’s ongoing partnership with Carpenter to reach the next generation of whisky drinkers and highlight the brand’s ethos of boldly moving toward the future.

The spot follows Carpenter in main-character mode as she walks through a swanky Jazz bar to order one of her signature Johnnie Walker cocktails: A Go Go Highball.
