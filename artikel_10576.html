Google Home Speaker gets first discount since June launch

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

In addition to the Pixel deals that started last week, Google is discounting the Home Speaker for the first time.

The Google Home Speaker finally launched on June 27. For the past four months, the price has remained at $99.99.
