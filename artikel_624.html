Magic: The Gathering Marvel Darkhold Destiny Booster Display preorders hit a record low on Amazon

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

TL;DR: The Magic: The Gathering Marvel: Darkhold Destiny Booster Display is available to preorder for $199.99 at Amazon, down from its $209.70 list price.

Magic: The Gathering's next Marvel trading card crossover isn't due until April, but in honor of early Black Friday season, Amazon is already dropping price cuts to preorders on the follow-up to the Super Heroes expansion: Darkhold Destiny.

As of Nov. 16, the 30-pack Marvel: Darkhold Destiny Booster Display box can be preordered for just $199.99 on Amazon. That's a $9.71 discount on its $209.70 list price. With that added 5% off, you’ll now only pay around $6.67 each — you’ll be all set for when the new set arrives on April 9, 2027.

This new offer is only $23.40 above its TCGplayer market price, and this box is coming directly from Amazon (rather than a third-party seller). It's also the lowest price this box has been since preorders dropped in October.

Darkhold Destiny essentially trades Marvel's familiar superhero clashes for an all-new supernatural battle involving Doctor Strange, Thor, Dormammu, and Hela. The display contains 30 booster packs with 14 cards apiece, aimed at Limited games, deck-building, or simply cracking open packs with friends.
