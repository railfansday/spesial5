Championship strugglers sack managerial duo as reality of second tier hits hard

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Lincoln City have sacked joint head coaches Chris Cohen and Tom Shaw after the club’s run of five straight Sky Bet Championship defeats.

Cohen and Shaw were promoted from their roles as assistant coaches in May, replacing Michael Skubala, who departed for Bristol City after guiding the Imps to the second tier for the first time in 65 years.

A club statement on Wednesday morning read: “Lincoln City can confirm Chris Cohen and Tom Shaw have been relieved of their duties as head coaches with immediate effect. We thank them for their contributions to the club over the last several years and wish them well for the future.

“We have worked incredibly hard to reach the Championship and will do everything in our power to retain our status in this division.

“We know how much this football club means to our supporters and are humbled by the level of support we’ve had home and away so far this season.”

First-team coach Shaun Pearson, supported by Paul McShane and goalkeeping coach David Preece, will take charge of Saturday’s home league game against Wolves.
