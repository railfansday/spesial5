CorSport: Milan table €5m renewal offer to Rabiot until 2030 – the next steps

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

This morning, some progress has been made by AC Milan in their attempt to renew Adrien Rabiot’s deal, it seems.

It’s been a season of big renewals so far for Milan, and they’re not yet done. In fact, there are a few core players who they are looking to tie down to longer-term deals and Rabiot is among them.

With five goals and as many assists already this season, he’s thriving for Ruben Amorim, who is keen to keep him as an essential player in his team.

In this morning’s edition of Corriere dello Sport (via MilanNews), the leading line is that Rabiot remains key, and that the Rossoneri aim to tie down his future by the end of the season. That much is not new information, but it’s a reminder that he’s not been forgotten.

With Christian Pulisic’s renewal finalised, the Frenchman is next in line, with his importance not unknown to the Diavolo. Indeed, they are ready to offer him what could be his final ‘big’ contract, which would see him extend his stay at San Siro until 2030.

Alongside this, a pay rise from €3.5 million to €5m per season is expected, another reminder of his importance.

The step here, though, is that the offer has now officially been made and is laying on the table. Rabiot played at San Siro last night, and he’ll now take some time to evaluate the offer from the club, which he now calls home.
