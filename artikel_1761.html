Opinion: Julian Ward will fix Richard Hughes mistake with €100m sensation

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Richard Hughes made a lot of mistakes when he was Liverpool's sporting director.

The Englishman spent well over £600 million on new players and, it's still unclear whether their team is any better off.

So, even though Hughes did quite well with some of the deals he made, he obviously had to make a few mistakes on the way.

Right now, Liverpool's entire team could still do with upgrading but, because they have a handful of players who's contract are going to expire in June 2027, they've taken priority.

The January transfer window is now just over a month away so, some transfers are going to have to take place soon, otherwise, the Reds will be left even further behind their title rivals than they currently find themselves.

Julian Ward can sign Luka Vuskovic after Richard Hughes chose against it in the summer

Liverpool's defence has been one of the main areas that Liverpool have tried to strengthen over the last few years.

Virgil van Dijk, Andy Robertson and Joe Gomez are just a few examples of the older players that they were using last season.

And, with Robertson gone and the other two having their contracts expire in June 2027, another defensive rebuild could be on the way.

Many different links with players have emerged recently with Giorgio Scalvini one of them.

However, a recent update from Florian Plettenberg on Luka Vuskovic's situation is something that should definitely pique the interest of Liverpool and their fanbase.

"Liverpool have Luka Vuskovic on their shortlist for next summer. The 19 y/o top defender is being monitored regularly," he shared via X.

"His contract at Brighton is valid until 2031. No release clause will be available next summer. Current price valuation: around €100m (+/-).

"Vuskovic is very happy under Fabian Hürzeler. However, his dream is to join Real Madrid one day.

Vuskovic has been a revelation since joining Brighton.

The Croatian had a spectacular season last term while on loan with Hamburger SV with most people thinking that Tottenham would welcome him back with open arms.

However, that didn't end up happening and, instead of keeping the 19-year-old around, Roberto de Zerbi chose to bring Jan Paul Van Hecke through the welcome doors instead.

And, while the Dutchman's performances have been impressive, Vuskovic has probably been the better player.

He's transformed Fabian Hurzeler's defence and, there's no wonder why the Seagulls want a record deal for the centre-back.

Obviously, it's a lot of money to spend on a defender who's only had one season of Premier League experience but, if they did manage to get a deal over the line, Ward would be instantly rectifying a problem that Hughes made for himself.

It would also be a sure-fire way for the Englishman to get fans on his side as, during the summer, most Liverpool fans wanted their club to sign him.
