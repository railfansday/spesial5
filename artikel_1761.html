Opinion: Julian Ward will sign €30m South American wonderkid for Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Julian Ward has been thrown into the deep end as Liverpool's sporting director.

Over the last couple of windows, the Reds' transfer regime has been handled by Richard Hughes.

The Englishman managed to spend well over £600 million during his time as the Reds' sporting director and, after managing to make the team worse despite this huge amount of spending, he chose to pack his bags and leave for pastures new.

This left Liverpool looking for a new person to take the reins with Fenway Sports Group eventually landing on Julian Ward as their preferred option.

Since then, the rumour mill has been in overdrive and now that we're edging closer to the transfer window opening back up, some of these links are going to have to turn concrete and actually get completed.

Liverpool want to sign Arthur Dias from Athletico Paranaense

One of the main transfer strategies that Liverpool have had over the last couple of years is signing young players for the future.

They've always had one of the best academies in the world but now, they're clearly looking for more ways to set themselves up.

So far, most of their transfers have worked out and, while the jury is still out for some of them such as Milos Kerkez, they're all still incredibly young and have a lot of their careers left to go.

But, as much as Liverpool have youngsters among their ranks, they also have some veterans who many need replacing in the future.

As mentioned, many different players have been linked with Liverpool but now, thanks to an update from FussballEuropa, Brazilian centre-back Arthur Dias has been linked.

"Liverpool FC has its eyes everywhere – including on the Brazilian market. The Reds' scouts are keeping a close eye on Arthur Dias. The race for the talented center-back is already on," they wrote.

"Athletico Paranaense could have made a significant sum from Arthur Dias last summer. Tottenham Hotspur showed concrete interest and reportedly submitted an offer of just over €20 million.

"However, this was too low for Paranaense, and consequently, no deal was reached.

"Dias has even earned two caps for the Brazilian national team. This automatically makes the rising star a target for other clubs. According to information from Fussballeuropa.com, Premier League side Liverpool are keeping a close eye on Dias.

"The Reds are regularly monitoring the young defender and keeping a close eye on his development.

"As Fussballeuropa.com has also learned, Paranaense are demanding a transfer fee of at least €30 million. The 2001 Brazilian champions will reportedly not even enter negotiations for anything below this magic number."

Dias is probably a player that hardly any Liverpool fans have heard of.

But, the fact that he's 21 years old and has already been given two international caps for Brazil proves how good he actually is.

The centre-back is one of the most highly-rated prospects in the Brazilian Serie A and, the fact that he's available for around £25 million is mad.

Obviously, the step up from the Brazilian Serie A to the Premier League will be a big one but, since Jeremy Jacquet managed it, there's no reason why Dias can't too.

Many transfers are likely to be made by Liverpool in 2027 but, if Dias isn't one of them, they'll be leaving themselves rather red-faced.

Here's hoping that Ward and Andoni Iraola can align their transfer values and bring Dias in.
