Stellar Lumens (XLM) currently trades near $0.145–$0.148 with a market capitalization around $5.2 billion, roughly one-thirteenth the size of XRP. However, Elon Musk’s Grok AI predicts that a single, highly improbable but not impossible catalyst could still ignite a parabolic advance capable of flipping XRP before January 1, 2027.

In this dramatic scenario, Grok AI predicts that a consortium of major global banks, payment giants, and several central banks will jointly announce that Stellar will serve as the primary public blockchain for a new generation of regulated cross-border settlements, tokenized deposits, and multi-currency clearing.

This initiative includes commitments to process substantial institutional volume on the network and to hold XLM as the preferred asset for liquidity and fees.

As a result, the narrative shifts from viewing Stellar as a “niche payments chain” to recognizing it as “the institutional standard that has finally surpassed its larger rival.” This shift could trigger a significant capital rotation, reminiscent of the major relative-value trades that have historically defined the cryptocurrency market.

XLM has been steadily declining in a broader downtrend, recently testing support around the $0.140–$0.145 range after failing to hold higher levels.

The Relative Strength Index (RSI) is currently in oversold territory on daily timeframes, suggesting potential for a sharp relief rally if buyers return. A sustained break and weekly close above the $0.17–$0.18 resistance cluster would represent the first significant structural improvement for XLM.

If this breakout is driven by high conviction and substantial volume—particularly during a landmark adoption event—it could lead to rapid expansion. This expansion could condense years of underperformance into a single parabolic move.Such a shift could enable Stellar’s market capitalization to challenge, and possibly surpass, that of XRP. However, current conditions remain cautious, and a complete flip of XRP would require explosive price appreciation and sustained capital inflows.

However, XLM’s growing real-world utility and the potential impact of transformative institutional mandates leave room for an extreme outcome.
