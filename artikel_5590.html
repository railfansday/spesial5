FlexFactor Wins Two Honors at The Card & Payments Awards US 2026 for Industry Innovation and the Future of Payments

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

After leading the inaugural U.S. awards as the only six-time finalist, FlexFactor earns double recognition for transforming when and how payment decline recovery happens.

NEW YORK, Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- FlexFactor, the AI-powered payments technology company pioneering real-time decline decisioning during authorization, today announced it won two awards at The Card & Payments Awards US 2026, held November 17 at Gotham Hall in New York City.

FlexFactor entered the inaugural U.S. ceremony as the only six-time finalist and finished the evening tied for the most awards won by any organization. The company took home two honors, Best Industry Innovation and Best Early-Stage or Future Payments Initiative, in recognition of both the innovation FlexFactor is delivering today and its potential to shape the future of payments tomorrow.

The awards highlight FlexFactor's fundamentally different approach to payment decline recovery. Instead of building a better retry or dunning tool, FlexFactor moved recovery into the authorization moment itself, using real-time decisioning to reverse eligible false declines before legitimate customers ever see a failed payment.

FlexFactor's proprietary AI-powered intelligence platform, AcceptIQ, evaluates declines in seconds to determine the optimal path to recovery. For recoverable transactions, FlexFactor purchases the merchant's invoice, guarantees the sale, and assumes the recovery risk. The result is more completed sales for merchants and a seamless customer experience, without the disruption of a declined payment.

Already live with more than 100 merchants, FlexFactor delivers cure rates ranging from 5% to 30% of eligible false declines, typically translating into a 0.5% to 3% uplift in gross sales. Results vary by merchant profile, decline mix, and payment environment. Beyond recovering otherwise lost revenue, FlexFactor helps merchants protect customer lifetime value, maximize the return on customer acquisition investments, and preserve the customer experience.

"At FlexFactor, we're obsessed with solving one problem: reversing false payment declines," said Philip McHugh, CEO of FlexFactor. "Our approach reduces negative customer moments and experiences while giving ecommerce merchants greater flexibility and certainty to drive incremental sales. These awards recognize the impact of solving a longstanding payments problem in a fundamentally different way."

"Being the only six-time finalist was a statement. Bringing home two awards makes an even bigger one," said Brennen Creer, CMO of FlexFactor. "Every transaction we recover is more than a sale saved. It's a customer who never knew they were almost turned away, and a merchant who keeps a relationship they worked hard to earn. We're excited to keep pushing the boundaries of what's possible alongside our partners and the merchants they serve."

The 2026 ceremony marked the inaugural U.S. edition of The Card & Payments Awards, an internationally established awards program recognizing excellence and innovation in the payments industry since 2005. Winners were selected through an independent judging process involving industry experts.

The two wins add to FlexFactor's growing list of 12+ industry honors, including the 2024 ETA Award for Most Innovative Product or Solution and the 2024 VIP Award for Best Payment Innovation.

FlexFactor is redefining payment decline recovery through AI-powered real-time decline decisioning during authorization. Powered by its proprietary AcceptIQ intelligence platform, FlexFactor identifies, evaluates, and reverses eligible false declines before legitimate customers experience a failed payment. For recoverable transactions, FlexFactor purchases the merchant's invoice, guarantees the sale, and assumes the recovery risk, turning otherwise lost transactions into completed sales while protecting the customer experience. Live with more than 100 merchants and backed by Bessemer Venture Partners, FlexFactor is on a mission to ensure every legitimate customer gets the yes they deserve. Learn more at flexfactor.io.
