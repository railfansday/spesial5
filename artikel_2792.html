Lakers hit their top-10 defensive goal — at least for one night

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Lakers have made one of their biggest goals very clear.

They want to be a top-10 rated defensive team.

Well, they skyrocketed from being rated 15th defensively Monday morning to 10th in the evening after a great defensive performance in their 109-94 win over the Jazz.

Los Angeles Lakers guard Collin Sexton, left, and forward Jake LaRavia defend Utah Jazz forward Brice Sensabaugh during an NBA game Monday, Nov. 23, 2026. AP Photo/Kyusung Gong

Sure, the Lakers have only played 18 games this season so a singular contest can wildly skew their defensive rating. And, they've found themselves in this position after a win over the Jazz, who have the second-worst record in the Western Conference at 3-16.

But, hey, at least for one night, they've reached their target.

"How many points did they score?" Walker Kessler inquired when he was asked about the team's defensive performance Monday.

After being told just 94, the Lakers' defensive anchor talked about how the team has made major strides on the less glamorous side of the court.

Ace Bailey (19) of the Utah Jazz shoots against Walker Kessler (14) and Luka Doncic (77) of the Los Angeles Lakers in Los Angeles, Monday, Nov. 23, 2026. Getty Images

"Yeah, you know, I would say that's pretty good defense," said Kessler, who had 10 points, 12 rebounds and two blocked shots against the Jazz. "I think our transition defense has gotten better, which is nice to see. Our limiting second chance points has gotten better. Obviously, we can still improve on that. We're kind of struggling right now with open 3s."

This is a positive development for the Lakers, who were obviously going to be a great offensive team behind Luka Doncic and Austin Reaves, but had a lot of questions on the defensive end.

But over the last five games without Reaves, who has been sidelined because of a left heel bruise, they've been forced to tighten things up on the defensive end while having a 25-point hole on offense.

Kyle Filipowski of the Utah Jazz drives against Jake LaRavia and Matisse Thybulle of the Los Angeles Lakers on Monday, Nov. 23, 2026, in Los Angeles. Getty Images

Funny enough, the Lakers have said since training camp that their defense is underrated. They've said their wings have been underestimated. They said that they could be good.

Well, they're showing signs that they weren't exaggerating.

We all knew that Kessler was an elite rebounder and rim defender. But now we're seeing that the Lakers are deeper than we may have thought on defense. From top to bottom, everyone is grinding and scrapping, including Doncic, who has received a lot of criticism throughout his career for only focusing on offense.

It's good news for the Lakers, who are tied for the best record in the NBA at 15-3. They have the No. 3-rated offense in the NBA. And at least for one night, they can say that they're in the top-10 on both sides of the court.

"I'm happy," Kessler said. "I think we're trending upwards, and that's all you want to see."
