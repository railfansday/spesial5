Quenton Nelson is not healthy enough to practice yet, Seahawks are unsure when he will be

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

When the Seahawks traded for Quenton Nelson, they added one of the best guards in the NFL — when he’s healthy. Unfortunately, so far, Nelson has only been healthy enough to play one game for Seattle.

After playing in Week 8 against the Bears, Nelson missed Week 9 and Week 10 with a knee injury. The Seahawks are on their bye in Week 11, but head coach Mike Macdonald said today that if they were practicing, Nelson would not be able to participate.

Asked when Nelson will practice, Macdonald said it’s too early to put a date on that.

“I know he really wants to play, but right now I think we’re too far out to put a practice conjecture on Q,” Macdonald said.

By trading for Nelson, the Seahawks were making an aggressive move to add a piece they hope can help them win another Super Bowl this year. The hope is that he’ll be ready to play down the stretch, but it’s unclear just how soon he’ll be back on the field.
