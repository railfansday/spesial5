Rhamondre Stevenson returns to practice, A.J. Brown remains limited

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Patriots had some positive news on their injury report from Thursday’s reportedly light practice in Munich.

Running back Rhamondre Stevenson (knee) was upgraded from a non-participant on Wednesday to a limited participant on Thursday.

Stevenson leads the Pats with 399 yards rushing with two touchdowns. He’s also caught 26 passes for 258 yards with two TDs.

Receiver A.J. Brown (ankle/knee) may also be back this week, as he was limited for the second practice in a row. He has not played since the Oct. 22 loss to the Bears.

Tight end Cameron Latu (back), offensive lineman Walter Rouse (concussion protocol), safety and Brenden Schooler (hamstring) were also upgraded from DNP to limited.

Safety Kevin Byard (chest), fullback Reggie Gilliam (knee), tight end Hunter Henry (foot), offensive tackle Morgan Moses (foot), offensive lineman Greg Van Roten (hamstring), and cornerback Charles Woods (hamstring) all remained limited.

Quarterback Drake Maye (right shoulder) is again listed as full.

New England previously ruled out offensive lineman Ben Brown (knee), cornerback Carlton Davis (knee), and running back TreVeyon Henderson (illness) who all did not travel this week.
