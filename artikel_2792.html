QB Jake Haener returns to the Giants' practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Giants signed quarterback Jake Haener back to the practice squad, the team announced Saturday.

Haener, 27, was cut from the active roster on Thursday.

J.J. McCarthy and Jameis Winston remain on the 53-player roster at the position.

The Giants signed wide receiver Jalin Hyatt to take the vacant spot on the active roster. His only game action this season came against the Texans when he played four offensive snaps and six on special teams.

The team also elevated running back Phil Mafah and outside linebacker Dawuane Smoot from the practice squad.
