Joe Flacco replaces Joe Burrow in 4th quarter of Bengals-Steelers rout

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Cincinnati Bengals backup quarterback Joe Flacco entered in the fourth quarter of the team's NFL Week 10 game against the Pittsburgh Steelers at Paycor Stadium on NBC's Sunday Night Football, as the Bengals sat starter Joe Burrow with a 38-18 lead.

Burrow left the game having completed 35-of-44 for 409 passing yards with four touchdowns. He had 302 passing yards in the first half.

When Flacco started for the Bengals in 2025 against Aaron Rodgers, ESPN Research reported that it was the second time in NFL history that two quarterbacks at least 40 years old face off in a regular-season or postseason game. The Steelers' Cam Heyward dubbed it the "Icy Hot" Bowl.

Rodgers is the only active NFL quarterback older than Flacco.

Rodgers' announcement in May that he'll retire after the 2026 season means the Bengals may not face the future Pro Football Hall of Famer again.

Flacco got to enter the team's NFL Week 7 rout of the Ravens in Baltimore during the fourth quarter and finish the game against his former team, but he did not attempt a pass.

Flacco entered Week 7 having appeared in one game for the Bengals this season but without attempting a pass.

Flacco told Burrow he thought Burrow's first touchdown pass to Ja'Marr Chase against the Houston Texans was "uncatchable."

Rodgers' announcement in May that he'll retire after the 2026 season means the Bengals may not face the future Pro Football Hall of Famer again.

Flacco, who turned 41 in January, played in nine games last season for the Bengals, making six starts after Burrow suffered a turf toe injury during a Week 2 win against Jacksonville. Flacco completed 158 of 254 pass attempts for 1,664 yards with 13 touchdowns.

In November 2025, Flacco played with a sprained AC joint in his throwing shoulder during a 47-42 loss to the Chicago Bears. At 40 years old, Flacco became the oldest quarterback in NFL history to pass for 470 yards in a game.
