Tresoldi on Bayern shortlist despite Kane extension

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Bayern Munich have previously been mentioned among the top clubs keeping an eye on Club Brugge and Germany U21 forward Nicolò Tresoldi.

According to local outlet TZ, the German champions continue to have the 22-year-old on their shortlist despite recently tying Harry Kane down to a new contract until 2029, as they consider adding a second "weapon" to their squad up front.

Bayern have reportedly sent scouts to Belgium several times to watch Tresoldi, with the report claiming that the German youth international was discussed within the club a few weeks ago.

The report estimates that the young forward, who is under contract until 2029, would cost around €40-50 million.
