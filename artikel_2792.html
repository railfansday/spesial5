Mavericks vs Clippers Recap: Late half runs seal the game for theDallas, 110-100

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Dallas Mavericks (9-7) defeated the Los Angeles Clippers at home on Saturday night, winning 110-100. Kyrie Irving was the high point man in Dallas, scoring 25 while grabbing six rebounds and four assists. Brook Lopez scored 21 in defeat for the Clippers.

The opening period felt like a rock fight, with neither team scoring well in the early going. The Clippers scored a flurry of points in the final minutes to take an early lead. The Mavericks played poorly into the second and it looked like another one of those games. After falling behind 33-24, the Mavericks came to life in a big way. Walter Clayton Jr. and Max Christie led the charge and Dallas scored 13 straight. They held court the rest of the quarter and took a 52-44 lead into the half.

The Clippers wouldn't go away though, and Dallas spent the second half trading baskets with Los Angeles. Though Dallas remained in control, the game felt up in the air right up until the 4:18 mark. With Dallas ahead 91-88, a Daniel Gafford dunk was followed by a Max Christie steal and dunk to give Dallas some cushion. Over the next minute, the lead grew to 14 before the Clippers would finally answer but by that time the damage was done. Dallas closed out the game with the final score 110-100.

The margin of this game also happens to be the same margin of the two teams' fast-break points: The Clippers had 12 and the Mavericks had 22. Coach Dusty May has mentioned since preseason that playing fast would be key for Dallas in wins and tonight it proved decisive.

It starts with defense, of course, and Dallas played with spots of frenetic activity that one can point to in what was a closer game than I expected.

It's safe to say that Max Christie has had a disappointing season, barring a few exceptions. Tonight against the Clippers is one and his 13 points and 24 minutes were absolutely needed. It's only the second double-digit minute performance for Christie this month as he's been dealing with injuries and poor play.

The Mavericks don't need Christie to be a star by any means, but they do need playable performances. He's got to have the confidence to take and make shots when presented with them and hopefully his play tonight will lead to more confidence in the coming weeks.

Past the fastbreak points, that the Mavericks were able to not turn the ball over contributed directly to the win. The Clippers committed 16 turnovers and it lead to 22 Dallas points. The Mavericks handed it over to Los Angeles just nine times and ceded 13 points in comparison.

The funny part is this season Dallas has about the same amount of turnoers in wins as they do in losses. They average about 15 per contest so most games it doesn't really make a difference. Tonight though, you can see it directly. They controlled the ball better and that small margin made the difference.
