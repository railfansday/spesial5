Binance raises RLUSD yield to 6.37% as XRP rewards surge 54%

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Binance’s higher RLUSD reward rate lifts XRP payouts by 54% as a new 600,000 XRP campaign seeks to attract stablecoin balances.

Binance’s base reward rate for holding Ripple USD (RLUSD) rose from 4.48% to an annualized 6.37% for the seven days through Nov. 20.

At a steady qualifying balance of 10,000 RLUSD, the estimated weekly payout rose from 8.25 XRP to 12.72 XRP as the exchange also used a lower price to convert the reward into XRP.

A separate 600,000 XRP rewards campaign began at 00:00 UTC on Nov. 20. The new round runs through Dec. 18 and gives eligible customers a reason to keep more RLUSD on Binance.

Binance’s distribution table shows base APRs of 4.84% on Oct. 30 and 5.29% on Nov. 6, with XRP conversion references of $1.2379 and $1.2072 for those payouts. The base rate fell to 4.48% on Nov. 13, with a $1.0413 reference, before climbing to 6.37% on Nov. 20 as the reference dropped to $0.9603.

On a constant qualifying balance of 10,000 RLUSD, Binance’s seven-day formula estimates rewards of $9.28, $10.15, $8.59, and $12.22 across the four weeks before conversion.

Dividing by each week’s reference and rounding down to two decimal places gives 7.49, 8.40, 8.25 and 12.72 XRP.

Each column is the annualized base APR Binance reported for the seven days ending on that distribution date, excluding reward multipliers.

The estimated payout rose about 54% in XRP from the third week to the fourth. The higher base rate increased the dollar value under the formula, and the lower conversion price turned each dollar of reward into more XRP.

Users qualify through net RLUSD balances in eligible Spot, Funding, Flexible Earn, or Margin accounts. For each day, Binance uses the lowest balance it finds in hourly snapshots, then averages those daily lows over seven days.

Eligible participants generally receive XRP in their main Spot account. The formula rewards maintaining a balance through the week because a low reading counts for the entire day.

The renewal also applies a 1.1x boost to eligible RLUSD in Flexible Earn and 1.2x to qualifying balance increases above a baseline from the prior two weeks. The portion that meets both tests can earn 1.3 times the base reward.

Funding records and any trades used to source the rewards could establish whether the program led to a fresh market purchase. Once the tokens were credited, recipient balances, trades, and withdrawals could show how much of the reward stayed on Binance.

To see whether the RLUSD balances lasted, the comparable figures would be qualifying holdings before, during, and after a rate change.

In a separate measure, XRPLDashboard’s signed snapshots show RLUSD outstanding on the XRP Ledger rose by 31.4 million, from about 714.4 million to 745.8 million, in the 24 hours through 02:00 UTC on Nov. 20.

The snapshots measure net issuance across the ledger and provide no breakdown of flows to Binance or the balances that qualified for rewards.

The new campaign’s first distribution is scheduled for Nov. 27, and the exchange plans to post the effective rate after that distribution. Aggregate payout and balance figures would show how much of the pool Binance used and how much RLUSD qualified for rewards.
