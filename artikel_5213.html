A $2.8 million XRP yield trade is about to mature, but the next two markets are 44 times smaller

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

XRP yield holders face a liquidity test as $2.8 million in Spectra pool liquidity nears maturity and rollover markets remain thin.

A Spectra pool tied to Firelight’s XRP yield strategy had roughly $2.8 million in liquidity on Nov. 19, one week before its principal tokens became redeemable on Nov. 26.

The two later maturities in the same Spectra overview had about $51,000 and $13,000, so holders seeking another fixed rate would have to trade in a much smaller pool.

The gap was visible in Spectra’s market overview on Nov. 19, which shows that its later pools mature on Dec. 31 and March 31, 2027. The first question for Spectra holders at maturity is which token the position can deliver on Flare.

Firelight takes FXRP, the representation of XRP on the Flare network, and issues stXRP shares in its vault. On Spectra, a buyer can purchase a principal token at a discount and redeem it at maturity.

This November series has its own market on Flare, which lists stXRP as the default input or output and FXRP as the underlying asset.

At maturity, Spectra holders can open their positions to see the available redemption token and estimated proceeds. Spectra’s redemption guide shows the underlying payout is normally close to one unit per principal token, with a lower rate possible if the strategy suffers a loss.

Spectra’s contracts also support redemption in the interest-bearing token. In that case, the output also depends on the conversion rate. For this series, the market labels identify the two assets, and the position’s redemption quote determines the available route and amount.

The pool’s $2.8 million counts the value on both sides of the market, stXRP and principal tokens. The amount redeemed at maturity depends on the principal token holders present and the rate the contract applies.

Spectra’s Nov. 19 overview at 12:29 UTC showed $2.77M, $51K and $13K in two-sided liquidity for the three listed Firelight stXRP pools.

Source: Spectra markets overview · Spectra Nov. 26 market · Spectra pool list

Spectra’s overview showed fixed rates of 5.21% for November, 7.56% for December, and 8.01% for March. The yield earned between purchase and maturity depends on the price paid and the time left. A rollover is a new trade, so its terms depend on what a later pool quotes for that order.

The December market held roughly $36,400 in principal tokens and $14,400 in stXRP as of Nov. 19. A buyer taking principal tokens from the pool would have to price that trade against the available inventory.

A holder who receives stXRP can swap the shares for FXRP at a market price. The amount received depends on the trade quote, which can differ from the Firelight vault’s share value.

Both the swap and the rollover depend on what the market will fill for a given amount of stXRP. Meanwhile, Firelight’s unstaking process records the share value in FXRP when the holder requests a withdrawal and ends reward accrual at that point.

The FXRP becomes withdrawable at the end of the next coverage period, currently 30 to 60 days after the request, and the holder must submit a second transaction to claim it. During the wait, claims tied to capital-backed coverage can still reduce the payout.

FAssets redemption burns FXRP on Flare and has an agent send native XRP to the holder’s XRP Ledger address.

The redemption request sets the payment deadline, and applicable fees reduce the amount the agent sends.
