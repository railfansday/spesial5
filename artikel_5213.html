Worldcoin is heading back toward a price level that could decide its next major move. The WLD price is trading around $0.3371 on the daily chart, approaching a highlighted support zone near $0.30. If buyers defend that area, the chart suggests room for a recovery. If they don’t, the bullish setup gets considerably weaker.

The chart shows WLD pulling back after reaching levels above $0.50 in recent months. It’s now approaching the green support area around $0.30 to $0.32, with $0.30 marked as a key horizontal level.

This zone matters because price previously found support around the same area. The current structure could indicate accumulation, as suggested by the chart analysis, but that interpretation still needs confirmation from price action.

A successful hold above $0.30 could provide the foundation for a rebound. However, support isn’t guaranteed just because the chart has reacted there before.

Traders will want to see buyers step in and establish a stronger recovery. A sustained move away from the support zone would make the bullish case more convincing.

The chart identifies several upside levels if a bounce is confirmed. The first target sits at $0.36, followed by $0.44 and $0.52. A stronger recovery could bring $0.66 into focus.

Those levels remain conditional, not promises. For now, the WLD price needs to defend the $0.30 support zone before traders can seriously discuss the higher targets. The next move depends on whether buyers finally show up.
