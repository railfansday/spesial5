Ole Miss Student Alaina Kunes Withdraws From School After Viral Doorbell Cam Video

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Alaina Kunes -- one of the University of Mississippi students making monkey noises and gestures in the controversial viral video -- has withdrawn from school ... TMZ has learned.

Sources close to Kunes tell TMZ she has not been kicked out or suspended from Ole Miss ... she voluntarily withdrew from the school.

We're told she withdrew in response to the harassment she's facing from other students and the local community.

Worth noting ... withdrawing is different than dropping out -- and leaves the door open for Kunes to return to school next semester. It's not clear if she'd even want to do so.

As we reported ... Kunes released a statement apologizing for the incident -- and insisting it wasn't an intentional racial attack, but a silly joke gone horribly wrong.

She says she had no idea people of color lived in the apartment, and she'd never make monkey noises or gestures at people of color on purpose.

We learned Kunes' family's business has also been at the center of a racial controversy ... after a Wisconsin-based woman claimed a worker at Kunes Buick GMC put a sticker with a racial slur on her windshield.
