Skyrim's Most Iconic Region Is Making a Major Comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Elder Scrolls Online is heading north in 2027, bringing a familiar snowy destination to its expanding map

Winter is coming to Tamriel, and The Elder Scrolls Online is preparing to take players somewhere particularly familiar. One of the most memorable regions from The Elder Scrolls 5: Skyrim is making its way to the MMORPG, giving fans another reason to revisit the icy northern reaches of the continent.

Bethesda has revealed that Winterhold is coming to The Elder Scrolls Online in early 2027 as part of Season Three. The new major zone and accompanying storyline will expand ESO's Second Era map, bringing the snow-covered region into the online RPG for the first time.

While Bethesda hasn't shared many details about what players can expect to do in Winterhold, it has confirmed that more information is on the way. A special livestream dedicated to Season Three and the new adventure is planned for January 2027, with further details expected in the developer's December community letter.

Winterhold is a remote, snow-covered region in the northern reaches of Skyrim, overlooking the Sea of Ghosts. Its namesake city was once a prosperous settlement, but much of it was destroyed during the Great Collapse, a mysterious disaster that caused large sections of the town to sink into the sea. The region is perhaps best known for the College of Winterhold, a prestigious institution dedicated to the study of magic.

In Skyrim, players can explore the College and join its ranks, embarking on a major questline involving mysterious artifacts, dangerous magical experiments, and the Eye of Magnus. But there's more to the region than its famous school of magic. The surrounding landscape includes icy coastlines, snow-covered mountains, ancient Nordic ruins, caves, and settlements inhabited by hunters and other hardy residents.

Winterhold is a particularly atmospheric corner of Skyrim, blending ancient Nordic history, magical intrigue, and some of the game's harshest wilderness.

Exactly which parts of the wider region will be included in The Elder Scrolls Online remain unclear. However, ESO takes place centuries before Skyrim, giving players an opportunity to explore Winterhold during a very different period in its history.
