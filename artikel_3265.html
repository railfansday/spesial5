10 Hacks Every Reddit User Should Know

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

If you're looking for discussions, recommendations, or advice online, it's hard to avoid Reddit. From mainstream sports to niche crafts, it does seem that there's some kind of Reddit community for everyone. (And if there's not one, you can make one yourself.) If you're using Reddit, here are 10 hacks you should know about to improve your experience on the site.

Reddit used to let everyone view your post and comment history, but in recent years, you can make your profile private. You can hide your posts, comments, and the subreddits you're a part of. If you want to do this, click your profile icon once you've logged in to Reddit, and go to Settings > Profile. Alternatively, you can follow the direct link to Reddit's profile settings page. Click the drop-down menu next to Content & Activity, and select Hide all. You should also turn off Online Status on the same page to stop people from seeing when you're using Reddit.

Old Reddit lets you use the site's classic design, and I prefer it to the more modern UI. Old Reddit looks dated, but it doesn't have infinite scrolling, distracting animations, and a bunch of features designed to increase the time you spend on the platform. It's no wonder that Reddit is making it harder to access this version of the site. As long as it's around, you should switch to Old Reddit for a cleaner reading experience. You can do that by going to Reddit Settings > Preferences and enabling Default to Old Reddit. You can also visit old.reddit.com, though you'll need to sign in for access

Many subreddits use custom themes that range from beautiful to borderline unusable. If you want a more consistent UI across the site, you should disable these custom subreddit styles. Go to Reddit Settings > Preferences and turn off Use community themes. If you're using Old Reddit, click the preferences button in the top-right corner, and under "display options," disable allow subreddits to show me custom themes.

A multireddit is a feed that shows posts from a customized group of subreddits. You can think of it as a way to break out of Reddit's algorithms, and create your own Reddit feed. I use multireddits to follow all sports-related subs I'm interested in. I even have one for gaming, and one for fountain pens. It's a great way to create interest-specific feeds, and if you make them private, no one else can see them. To create one, click the Create custom feed button in the left pane in new Reddit. In the Old Reddit design, click the Create button in the left pane. Be sure to select the Private button to keep it from showing up for others. You can add as many subreddits as you like to the list, and bookmark the feed to access it whenever you wish to.

Reddit has recently adopted an Instagram-like experience for its feeds. It removed the ability to sort posts by new, controversial, best, etc., and instead offers "For You" and "Following." If you dislike this change, you can bypass it. I use Reddit on mobile Safari, and I recommend an extension called Sink It for Reddit. It can automatically redirect you to Old Reddit, and there's a feature that optimizes it for mobile, which brings back the old sorting options. You can also use third-party apps such as RedReader for Android, or RDX for Reddit on an iPhone or iPad to restore the old sorting options for Reddit posts.

After all these years, Reddit's built-in search still leaves something to be desired—at least, on the surface. To find posts on Reddit faster, use search filters on the site. For instance, adding "title:" to your search will limit the results to post titles, and boolean operators such as AND, OR, and NOT also work for advanced searches on the site.
